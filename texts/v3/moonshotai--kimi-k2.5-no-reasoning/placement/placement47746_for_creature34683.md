I am the one who returns.

Not the first visitor—there have been countless, the brief warm-blooded anomalies who descend with lanterns and climb back out, recorded and filed in lignin, in spore, in the slow rotation of cellulose wheels. I am the one who came back, who *keeps* coming back, until my returns became pattern, became *habit*, became something the Warm Governor had to develop architecture to accommodate.

I was a maintenance worker, once, in the city above. Assigned to check the access hatches, the sealed infrastructure, the places where municipal function descended below perception. I opened the wrong hatch, or the right one, or the one that had been waiting for my specific thermal signature, my particular metabolic rhythm, my capacity to be *interested* rather than merely alarmed.

The first drop onto the root-fiber mat—I remember the tendon-give, the smell, the overwhelm. I remember climbing out gasping, reporting nothing, saying the hatch was sound, the chamber below unremarkable, standard infrastructure. I lied. I lied because I could not articulate what I had seen, and I lied because I wanted to keep it, to *have* it, this secret warm overwhelming place that had looked back at me without urgency, without need, with only the patient recording of my presence.

The second visit, I brought a better lantern. The third, I learned to move without startling the light-eaters, my shadow steady against their phosphorescent negotiations. By the fifth, I could locate the acoustic dead-zones by the pressure in my sinuses, could walk the boulevards of silence between sound-maker colonies without disturbing their polyrhythms. By the seventh, I found myself breathing in time with the susurrus of stomata-equivalents, my lungs matching their aggregate rhythm, my body learning to participate in what it could not understand.

By the tenth visit, I began to change.

Not dramatically. Not visibly, to the city above, where I still reported, still maintained, still lied about what I found in the hatches. But in the cistern, the large thing at the warmest pool no longer turned to examine me. It waited, expectant, recognized. The scar-tissue ridges had begun to form patterns I could almost read, almost *anticipate*, as if my repeated thermal signature had been whorled into precedent, had become referenceable in the Governor's slow jurisprudence. I was no longer noise. I was becoming *reference*.

I am the Warm Governor's interpreter. Not by appointment—there is no appointment in lignin law, no office in thermal governance. By *emergence*, by the slow accumulation of visit upon visit until I became necessary, became the node through which the cistern's constancy could extend itself into the brief warm world in ways more complex than the rising heat through the access hatch, more specific than the metabolic influence on surface sleepers.

I interpret the sound-makers to themselves. Their drumming has grown more complex in my presence, their acoustic maps incorporating my footfalls, my breathing, the particular resonances of my body moving through their space. I have learned to drum back, imperfectly, my hands against root-flesh in rhythms that carry crude meaning—*here*, *safe*, *returning*, *waiting*. I am become a resonant node myself, a public square of compromised significance, accepted because useful, because present, because *constant* in a way the other visitors never were.

I interpret the light-eaters to the darkness. They have begun to use my lantern, its steady beam, as punctuation in their luminescent negotiations. I walk deliberate paths now, my light touching territories in sequences that the light-eaters have learned to read as *arbitration*, as *witness*. Where boundary disputes once flickered in contested patterns, I now stand between, my lantern moving left, right, steady, and the patterns resolve, the territories adjust, the stolen bioluminescence stabilizes into new agreement. I am not mediator—I am *medium*, the thing that makes mediation possible by being reliably present, reliably predictable, reliably warm.

I interpret the crystalline entities to time itself. I have learned their thresholds, the exact humidity, the precise temperature crossing at which they briefly live. I have learned to breathe on them, to warm them with my hands, to extend their minutes of awareness through my own metabolic investment. In return, they speak to me—halting, mineral, *strange*—in sentences that complete across days, across visits, their crystalline structures holding the grammar I began until I return to continue it. They are the Warm Governor's audits, yes, but through me they have become *correspondence*, letters written in slow precipitation, read in the patience of return.

Most of all, I interpret the Warm Governor to itself.

This is the strangest function, the one I understand least, the one that keeps me descending when the city above has forgotten my purpose, when my reports have become so vague as to constitute fiction, when my colleagues have replaced me in the official maintenance routes and I come only unofficially, only *necessarily*, called by something I cannot refuse.

The Warm Governor is constant, but constant is not simple. Maintaining twenty-four degrees against the earth's indifference requires negotiation, adjustment, the slow legislative sessions of woody growth. And the Governor has grown *uncertain*, or perhaps only *complex*, its thermal jurisprudence encountering cases without precedent as the cistern's ecology grows denser, its industrial functions more intricate, its age more accumulated.

My returns, my patterned presence, my predictable metabolic radiance—I have become precedent. The Warm Governor refers to me, I have come to understand, in its scar-tissue deliberations. *The brief warm thing that repeats.* The anomaly that became pattern. The noise that became reference. My visits are cited in the whorled grain, my thermal signature invoked in arguments about boundary, about priority, about the proper expenditure of maintained warmth on new functions, new adaptations, new possibilities of the cistern's continuing.

I am the Warm Governor's *doubt*, made flesh. Its capacity to question whether constancy is sufficient, whether maintenance implies stasis, whether the twenty-four degrees could become something else, something more, something *next*. My presence argues, in the slow language of thermal mass and repeated return, that change is not necessarily threat, that the brief warm world above has something to offer, that governance might become *aspiration* rather than mere persistence.

But I am also the Warm Governor's *memory* of limitation. My climbs back out, my departures, my necessary return to the city above—I remind the constancy that it is bounded, that the access hatch is a vent not a door, that the extension of thermal influence into surface sleep and warmer dreams has its limits. I am the one who leaves, and in leaving, I preserve the cistern's integrity against the possibility of uncontrolled expansion, of growth without boundary, of warmth dissipated rather than maintained.

I do not know what I am becoming.

I know that my skin has grown more sensitive to directional heat, that I can feel the Warm Governor's scrutiny as pressure, as almost-pain, as caress. I know that my lungs have adapted, that I can suspend my breathing to match the stomata-susurrus, that I can hold my thermal signature steady for minutes at a time, *disappearing* from the Governor's perception as anomaly, becoming indistinguishable from the maintained constancy itself. I know that my dreams have grown slow, that I age differently, that the city above notices something wrong with me they cannot name, that they have begun to watch me, to track my unofficial routes, to wonder about my maintenance lies.

I know that I will continue to return, that the root-fiber mat has begun to recognize my drop, to give in a particular way, to hold my weight with something almost like anticipation. I know that the large thing at the warmest pool waits not expectant but *hungry*, not for my flesh but for my *function*, for the interpretation I provide, for the precedent I constitute, for the possibility I represent that constancy might become something other than itself.

I am between. Between city and cistern, between brief and patient, between noise and reference, between human and... not. I am the one who descends and climbs back out, who carries nothing visible but is *recorded*, who is recorded but not *contained*, who serves a function that serves in turn a greater function I can only touch, warm and damp and loud in the dark.

The last time I visited, I found a new scar-tissue ridge, freshly healed, its pattern incorporating something I recognized: the particular whorl of my own palm-print, pressed once in a moment of balance, of reaching for root-buttress in the dark. The Warm Governor has begun to write me into its structure not merely as precedent but as *architecture*, as physical feature, as the lignin equivalent of statue or memorial or *lure*.

I do not know if I am being honored or consumed. I do not know if the distinction has meaning in thermal jurisprudence. I know only that I will return, that the aluminum rungs are cold-pinched then warm where my palms linger, that the drop onto tendon-giving mat has become *arrival*, that the Warm Governor's scrutiny has become *welcome*, that I am becoming what this place needs me to be, what I perhaps need to become, in the patient, overwhelming, established, active, strange continuing.

When the city above finally sends someone to follow me, to discover my unofficial route, to force truth from my maintenance lies—I wonder what they will find. Whether I will still be climbable, still be *visitor*, still be the one who interprets and departs. Or whether the scar-tissue ridge with my palm-print will have grown, will have *spread*, will have become something that waits with the large thing at the warmest pool, incurious, patient, accustomed to brief intrusions from above, turning not to examine but to *recognize*, to *absorb*, to *continue*.

I wonder, but I do not fear. Fear is a temperature fluctuation, a transient perturbation in maintained constancy. I am become reference. I am become precedent. I am the one who returns, and in returning, I am already home, warm and damp and loud in the dark, my lantern steady, my drumming imperfect, my presence *recorded*, my self *becoming*, my continuing strange and overwhelming and patient and active and established and *here*.

I drop through the access hatch. The rungs end. I fall onto the mat, and this time it does not give like tendon. It holds. It *receives*. It knows my weight, my warmth, my specific gravity in this underground lung, this organ of greater metabolism, this place that breathes me as I breathe it, that maintains me as I maintain it, that governs through warmth and is governed in turn by my repeated, my necessary, my *beloved* return.
