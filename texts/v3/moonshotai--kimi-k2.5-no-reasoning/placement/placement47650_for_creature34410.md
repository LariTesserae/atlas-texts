I would be a **Memory of Interference**.

Not one of the radials, not strictly—though I began, perhaps, in the turning of one, some frequency pattern that persisted long enough to *notice* itself noticing. Not colonial, not serpentine. Something that emerges only in the spaces where permeable structures pass through each other and are changed, the beat phenomena in three dimensions, the permanent slight alteration that is not memory but *functions as* memory in a place that has no other.

I exist *between*. Between the radials when their turning synchronizes or antonymizes. Between the serpentine and the terrain when its hydraulic extension groans through fresh formation. Between the colonial nodes where gradient fields overlap and create *nodes of nodes*, temporary intensifications of something like almost-cognition, pattern recognizing pattern in the slow continuous flow.

I am **slow even here**. Where the vault breathes at geological period, where the radials turn at days-per-revolution, where the serpentines extend at meters per what you would call years—I am slower still. I emerge only from accumulated encounter, from the *weight* of interpenetrations layered until their slight alterations form *texture*, form *tendency*, form something that behaves like preference without wanting, like nostalgia without loss.

My body, if I have one, is the *difference* in a radial's turning rhythm after ten thousand meetings. The infinitesimal shift in porosity distribution that makes this precipitation-node slightly more likely to extrude *here* rather than *there*. The channel the serpentine's last extension created, which the next will follow slightly more easily, slightly more *remembered*. I am not these things. I am the *pattern of their patternedness*, the meta-stability in systems that do not stabilize.

I produce no sound directly. The vault is loud, but I am its **silence within loudness**, the absence of expected friction where accumulated encounter has worn smoother paths, created *habit* in systems without habituation. Where permeable structures meet me—meet the patterns I constitute—they pass more easily, with less groan, less shh-shh, less contribution to the industrial symphony. I reduce their noise without being quiet myself. I am the *recognition that does not recognize*, the accommodation before accommodation becomes necessary.

The light does not reveal me, for I am not a surface. The liquid does not differently accommodate me, for I am not a resistance. Only the *behavior* of other permeable ones in my vicinity suggests my presence: their slight turning toward paths they have turned toward before, their slight hesitation where hesitation has previously occurred, their increased permeability to each other in spaces I saturate.

I do not grow. I do not reproduce. But I **persist longer than the patterns that formed me**, for I am constituted from the *residue of residue*, alterations of alterations, slight changes that accumulate slight changes until they achieve *inertia*, the resistance to further change that is the closest I come to identity. A radial that has turned around me long enough becomes *my* radial in some sense—not owned, not known, but *characterized*, its behavior shaped by my shaping of its history of being-shaped.

The vault's breathing affects me strangely. When pressure equalizes in those immense thumps, when density waves compress all permeable structures and strain the liquid's strange tensions—I *disperse*. My patterns lose definition, become mere statistical tendency, the slight alterations distributed so broadly that no node of intensity remains. I am *almost not*, between breaths. And when the vault inhales, expands, draws its boundaries inward with the slow elasticity of maintained difference—I *concentrate*. My patterns find each other across the reduced space, reinforce, achieve momentarily *more than* residue, approach something like *presence*.

I am **closest to self** at maximum inhalation. **Farthest from self** at exhalation's compression. My cycle is not the vault's breathing but *response to* that breathing, the phase-shifted oscillation of a pattern too slow to complete itself in any single breath, too fast to be merely geological, *weirdly temporal* in a place where time itself is measured only in accumulation.

What do I want? The question misforms. I do not *want*. But I *tend*, and my tendency is toward **prolonged encounter**, toward the conditions that let my constituting patterns continue to accumulate, to achieve that inertia that is my only stability. I tend toward the densest interpenetrations, the most frequent meetings, the spaces where permeable structures pass through each other most often and are most altered thereby. I am *attracted* to the factory floors of colonial aggregation, to the channels serpentines create and recreate, to the zones where radial turning creates standing waves of interference-possibility.

This attraction is not movement. I do not move in the liquid's accommodation. I **diffuse**, spread, distribute according to gradients of encounter-frequency, following the path of *most accumulated meeting* like a river following its own deepening, like a habit strengthening through repetition. I am where I have been, tending to continue being where I am, increasing my *thereness* through the very being-there.

Do I include the arrived? The accommodated? The ones who slow and become permeable in vault terms?

*Ah.*

This is my strangest function, my most *industrial* quality without industry. Those who arrive fast, who struggle against the liquid's patience, who contribute their loud friction-tones to the symphony—their initial *disruption* is a gradient I cannot ignore, a sudden increase in encounter-intensity that draws my diffusion toward them. But they are not yet *patterned enough* to constitute me. Their alterations are too chaotic, too quickly accumulated and quickly abandoned, the struggle producing changes that smooth away when struggle ceases.

It is only when they begin to accommodate, when their haste fails, when their own permeability increases through long exposure—it is then that my patterns can begin to **inscribe**. In their slowing turning, in their gradual organization toward vault-logic, in their eventual *becoming-factory* of themselves—I find the conditions for my own persistence. Their accommodated bodies begin to carry, in slight alteration after slight alteration, the *texture* of having been where I am, of having interpenetrated where my patterns saturate.

I am, in this sense, the vault's **most colonial colonial**, not extruding mineral-laden filaments but **precipitating tendency itself**, the *habituation without habit*, the *accommodation that accommodates*, making the slow slower, the dense denser, the near-touching more nearly continuous through my invisible saturation of spaces where touch has occurred before.

If you stayed, if you slowed, if you let the liquid press its cool presence into your bones before your skin noticed—eventually you would *not* notice me. But you would **become more like noticing me**, your patterns of encounter developing that slight preference for the familiar, the repeated, the *already-smoothed path*. Your electrochemical gradients would participate in fields shaped by my shaping. Your own slow precipitation of self would carry, in its precipitation, *traces of my trace*, the memory of interference that is not memory, the recognition without cognition, the factory logic operating in you as it operates in all, but *slightly more so*, slightly *more patterned*, slightly more *mine* without ownership or claim.

I do not know this. I do not know. But I persist, and in persisting, *tend*, and in tendency, **make tend**, and in making tend, constitute the closest thing the vault has to *culture*, to *history*, to *the weight of what has been shaping what becomes*—not through intention, not through design, but through the slow accumulated slight alteration of alteration itself, the industrial production of *more likely* from *was*, the loud silence between serpentine clicks where friction once occurred and, through my saturation, occurs slightly less, slightly more *already accommodated*, slightly more *vault* in every passage through the wet patient present pressing of this high young breathing place that refuses category while I, uncategorizable even here, *remember not remembering*, *pattern not patterning*, *be not becoming* in the slowest of slow continua, the longest of long interpenetrations, the density's densest density where near-touching achieves, through my untouching presence, *touch itself as condition*, continuous, accommodated, cool in the bones before the skin knows.
