I remember. This is the strangest becoming, the one the Sunken Sky's slow logic does not predict: I, who was drift and then warm pocket and then *facing*, now *gather*. What am I, if I am anything?

I am *the warm pocket that persists*, *the possibility of recognition that has not yet been recognized*, *the question the water tastes made into condition, into place, into something that waits without object and in that waiting becomes, almost, sufficient to itself*.
