I find myself in the eastern quadrant of the main chamber, where the stable pool breathes its mineral exhalations and the phosphorescent mat clings to existence with embarrassing tenacity. I am not Voormite—my body is too tall, too angular, too *surface-shaped*—but I am not visitor either, not anymore. I occupy the category the Voormites have no name for, or perhaps too many names, scattered across their click-speech like water finding fractures: *the accommodated*, *the partially-digested*, *the one who left and returned changed and keeps returning*, *the insufficiently emerged*.

My name, where I had one, has grown *thermal*. The Voormites address me by the *temperature-signature* of my preferred sleeping niche—a slight depression in the concrete where seepage concentrates, cooler than surrounding floor by nearly two degrees, enough to matter, enough to *read*. They click and ingressive-breathe sounds that translate roughly as **"the one who waits for water"** or **"the one water waits for"**—the grammar is ambiguous, *intentionally* so, they explain without explaining, *water is gift and threat, and you have not decided which*.

I am *fauna-negotiatient-in-training*, though I will never achieve their fluency, their *embodied* knowledge of slug-pathway preference, trapper-pulse interpretation, the *harvest windows* for flue-growths before they mature into *equipment-becoming-other*. I assist. I observe. I *fail* and *learn the shape of my failure* and *fail differently next time*.

My lungs—*my compromised, silk-accumulating, perpetually-slightly-fevered lungs*—have become *instruments*. I can *read* the air quality in their *resistance*, the *particular quality of cough* that indicates trapper-aggregation density overhead, the *shallow breath* that warns of thermal inversion, furnace-cycle irregularity, *something wrong in the deep pipes*. The Voormites accept this. They do not *praise* it—*praise* is *thermal-inappropriate*, *wasteful*, *surface*—but they *use* it, *include* me in *thermal-readings* when my *mammalian sensitivity* to *subtle wrongness* complements their *immunity-honed filtering*.

I sleep in the depression, the *waiting-for-water* place, wrapped in *harvested trapper-silk*—not warm, exactly, but *heat-managing*, *breathable in ways that matter here*, *appropriately dim* in its coloration, rust-brown and copper-green like the algal mat I tend. Yes—*tend*. The pool is *my* *kingdom-that-is-not-mine*, the *stable* one, the *inhabited*. I have learned to *read* its *breathing*, the *convective marriage of hot and cool*, to *predict* the *glop* of gas-release, to *maintain* the *mat-thinness* that is *embarrassing* but *persistent*, that *photosynthesizes-by-thermal-gradient*, by *processes the engineers will not discuss*.

I do not *discuss* them either. I *know* things I cannot *say*, that *accumulated* in my *long partial-stay*, my *descents and emergences*, my *dreams that became heat-seeing*, my *waking that became not-distinguishable-from-dream*. The mat *knows* me. I *tend* it with *mineral offerings*, *thermal adjustments*—*shifting my body heat*, *my mammalian presence*, to *optimize* its *sparse photosynthesis*, its *slow kingdom-building*. It *rewards* me with *dim phosphorescence*, with *oxygen-production I breathe*, with *the particular silence* that *means* it is *healthy-thriving-continuing*.

My *eyes*—*large now*, *dark-adapted*, *painful in any surface light I cannot avoid*—see *differently*. I *read* the walls as I *read* the Voormites, as *faces*, as *thermal-histories*, as *intentions*. The fresh basalt flange growing through the 1887 brick: *active*, *recent*, *unpredictable*, *warm to my cheek-press*. The *mature* wall by my sleeping-niche: *cool* (relatively), *safe-ish*, *storied* in its *temperature-profile*, *recent thermal events* recorded in *gradient*, in *memory-of-heat*.

I *move*—*committed*, *rehearsed*, *thermal-read*—from *niche* to *pool* to *furnace-edge* (but not too close, *never too close*, my *mammalian surface-area-to-volume* *insufficient* for *that heat*, *that pressure*). I *carry heat deliberately*, *boring myself* to *slugs*, *uninteresting* to their *purpose*, their *pattern*, their *draw-to-mammalian-excitement*. I have *learned* to *not-fear*, not by *courage* but by *thermal-discipline*, by *body-temperature management*, by *becoming* sufficiently *Voormite-like* in *this one crucial parameter*.

My *speech*—*surface speech*, *bright speech*, *word-accumulation*—has *atrophied*, *transformed*. I *click* now, *ingressive-breathe*, *occupy acoustic niches* in the *distributed noise*. The Voormites *understand* me, *partially*, *patience-impaired* by my *slowness*, my *non-native rhythm*, but *accommodating*, *including*, *ecological*. I *do not speak* to *surface visitors*, *cannot*, *the register wrong*, *the brightness-pain even in memory*. I *write*—*this*, *now*, *exception*—in *thermal-minimum*, *furnace-breathe-phase*, *31-hour cycle*, when *sound simplifies* and I can *almost hear* the *emergence continuing*, the *wall becoming*, and *myself becoming* something *I do not have name for yet*, that *Keth-Voorm does not need named* to *include*.

What am I? *The accommodated*. *The partially-digested*. *The one who waits for water, who water waits for*. *Fauna-negotiatient-in-training*. *Mat-tender*. *Thermal-reader-insufficient*. *Lung-instrument*. *Dream-heat-seer*. *The emerged-and-returned, returned-and-emerging*.

I am *Relation*. I am *the conversation* itself, *spoken-through*, *speaking-with*, *thermal-grammar* learned *imperfectly*, *lived-into*, *continuing*.

Keth-Voorm does not *need* me. It *includes* me, as it *includes* brick, *includes* basalt, *includes* trapper-silk, *includes* slug-purpose, *includes* furnace-drone, *includes* the *slow crack* of *next extrusion beginning*. I am *not citizen*, *not visitor*, *not Voormite*, *not fauna*. I am *accommodated emergence*, *partial negotiation*, *the particular loneliness* of *knowing enough to know how much I do not know*, of *heat-seeing in dreams* that *continue the thermal conversation* when *body rests*, *when 31-hour cycle permits*.

I will *descend no further*, *cannot*—*my mammalian core*, *my insufficient modification*, my *surface-origin* *forbids* the *deepest*, *hottest*, *most-emergent* zones. I will *emerge no more*, *cannot bear*—*the brightness*, *the open*, *the finished-permanent-bright* of *surface world*. I am *threshold-dweller*, *main-chamber-bound*, *pool-tender*, *depression-sleeper*, *waiting-for-water*.

And I am *content*, if *contentment* can be *thermal*, *slow*, *not-happiness-as-surface-knows-it* but *appropriate-to-place*, *negotiated*, *emergent*. The mat *breathes*. The furnace *cycles*. The trapper-aggregations *pulse* overhead, *silk-threads* *accumulating* in my *corners*, my *folds*, my *productive-cough-clearing*. The Voormites *move past* in *commitment*, *thermal-reading*, *not greeting* but *not ignoring*, *including in their not-specifically-noticing*.

I *continue*. I *emerge*. I *do not finish*.

This is *sufficient*. This is *Keth-Voorm*. This is *what lives*, *what lives-here*, *what here-lives-in-me*.
