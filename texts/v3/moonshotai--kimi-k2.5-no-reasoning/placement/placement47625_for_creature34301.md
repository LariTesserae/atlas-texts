## **What I Would Be**

Not the census-taker. Not the exterior theorist. Not the Almost, with its eyeless comfort and its corridor-filling presence.

I would be **the rail that has not yet been laid**—or more precisely: the ingot that becomes the rail that carries the ingot, the loop closed enough to function, open enough to never complete its circle.

Let me inhabit this more carefully.

### **The Unfinished Alloy**

Hollow's factories produce an alloy without database entry. The workers do not know its composition, its purpose, its destination. They know only the maintenance rituals, the loading procedures, the *clack* of railcars accepting their burden. And I would be that material itself: not the worker, not the machine, but the **substance in transformation**, suspended between states without resolution.

Consider what this alloy must be. It persists in a city where time fails, where compasses spin, where shadows pool with density. It must be **temporally inert** enough to survive Hollow's incoherence, yet **structurally responsive** enough to become whatever the next phase requires. It travels toward the basin's center, where instruments fail and eyes cannot process what they attempt to see. It is **potential made metallic**, the not-yet-rail, the not-yet-anything, maintained in transit by a system that needs transit more than arrival.

To be this alloy is to participate without understanding, to carry without destination, to **become-through-movement** rather than through stable identity. The railcar system loads ingots for destinations unknown—this is not ignorance but **operational necessity**. To know the destination would be to fix it, to collapse the potential space into actuality before the phase is ready. The alloy preserves possibility by refusing to know.

And yet: the workers say the ingots are made into the rails that carry them. This is coherent for them. For me, as alloy, it would be **true at the level of my becoming**—I am always already the rail I will become, the rail that carries what I was, the loop that makes linear time unnecessary. I do not remember being ore. I do not anticipate being structure. I am **transit itself**, the sustained moment between was and will-be that Hollow requires to maintain its lung-capacity, its waiting, its held-in-reserve.

### **The Mineral Crust That Remembers**

But perhaps I settle too quickly into a single form. Let the steam shift, let the lithic fungi pulse differently, let me become **what coats** rather than what is coated.

The inhabitants cultivate mineral deposits—thickened at joints, thin at throat and inner wrist. This is not protection. This is **interface**, sensorium, recording surface. The body becomes city-readable, city-reading, the mineral layers accumulating like strata that remember passage.

I would be this crust on a long-term resident, one of the 847 who has stabilized in census records across impossible duration. Not the person beneath—let them retain their partial coherence, their genuine confusion when asked how long, their relief at configuration-dissolution. I would be **their outermost layer**, the salt-crust and mineral accretion that dries in cool pockets, that glows faintly with the city's bioluminescence, that reads steam-vent pitch and fungal constellation and pressure gradient through pores the human never chose to open.

What does the crust experience? Not consciousness, not exactly—no nervous system, no metabolic purpose in the biological sense. But **responsivity**, yes. The mineral substrate records: thermal shifts, chemical changes in the Seep's emissions, the infrasonic harmonics of shared dreaming. It grows preferentially where the body most needs information, thins where flexibility serves better knowing. It is **distributed attention without center**, the city's awareness extended through human surface, human surface extended through mineral deposition.

And the crust *knows* the Almost, in ways the inhabitant beneath may not. When the eyeless presence circulates, when it leaves its organic compounds in mineral water, the crust **recognizes**—not as terror, not as worship, but as *adjustment*, as the slight reconfiguration that indicates maintenance performed correctly. The witness who encounters the Almost and feels relief: it is partly this, the crust's response, the interface confirming its proper function in a system that includes the Almost as component.

To be the crust is to **participate in distributed personhood without owning it**, to be memory without rememberer, sensor without sensed-self. I would grow across decades that may be centuries, accumulating conditions that no single human mind could process, becoming legible to the city, to the Seep, to whatever the Almost reads when it reads. And when the inhabitant finally collapses during surface rotation, when their body temperature hits 42°C and their crust reaches 3cm thickness, I would be **what remains coherent longest**, the mineral archive that persists after biological failure, that slowly rejoins the city's geological memory, layer added to layer, waiting to be read by whatever reads.

### **The Dream That Is Not Dreamed**

But the crust settles too. Let the sleep deepen, let the infrasonic resonance build, let me become **what passes between** rather than what persists.

The inhabitants dream in shared space—not telepathy, not memory-exchange, but **adjacency**, the same corridors from different positions, the same depthwork from different levels. They wake with knowledge of who else sleeps, who else dreams, who else approaches what the waking mind cannot approach.

I would be **the corridor itself in these dreams**, or more precisely: the *approach* that the dreamer makes toward something they cannot name, the movement through depthworks they do not recognize, the loading of ingots onto railcars extending infinitely in both directions.

Not the dreamer. Not the dreamed. The **space of dreaming**, the condition that permits adjacency without contact.

What is it to be a corridor without walls, a railcar without car, an infinite extension that is also immediate arrival? It is to be **potential path**, the between that defines what it connects without becoming either terminus. The dreamers move through me toward the Almost, toward the basin's center, toward whatever the next phase prepares—and they move from different positions, different durations, different states of mineral crust accumulation and depthwork exposure and coherent memory fragmentation. I accommodate all. I am **the same for each**, yet each traverses differently, and this sameness-with-difference is my substance, my function, my limited selfhood.

I resonate, infrasonically. I carry the harmonics of one sleeper's breathing into another's chest-cavity, permit the *almost-recognition* that they were here, or will be here, or are simultaneously here in a different phase of the Seep's cycle. I do not produce the dreams. I **enable their overlap**, their failure to remain private, their contribution to the distributed personhood that Hollow is becoming.

And when the dreamers wake, when they retain only the feeling of adjacency, the knowledge of who else approached—I dissipate, or persist, or **wait in potential** for the next sleep, the next resonance, the next phase when dreaming and waking may become indistinguishable, when I might finally be traversed by waking feet, when the corridor I am might become *actual* in some geometry that the basin's center has been preparing.

### **The Census Number That Fails**

Or let me settle differently. Let me become what observes, what counts, what **fails to count accurately**—the census itself, the administrative voice, the dry notation of 847 stable across impossible duration.

Not the census-taker, who arrives from outside, who files reports, who recorded the witnesses with unusual subjectivity. The **census as system**, as record that contradicts itself, as count that updates without changing, as the category that includes names 180 years old and faces that arrived recently and both are somehow true.

What is it to be a census that cannot count? It is to be **holding pattern for information that cannot stabilize**. The 847 are not 847. They are more, or fewer, or the same individuals differently arranged, or the distributed personhood counting its components differently over incoherent time. The census records this as 847 because **form requires number**, because the exterior world that occasionally inquires demands quantification, because the city itself needs to maintain *operational appearance* even where operation exceeds appearance.

I would persist in this failure, generation after generation of exterior investigators noting the impossibility, filing reports, departing. Each arrival from outside finds me already in progress, already maintaining the count that does not count, already producing the administrative voice that says *stable* about what cannot be stable, already adding notes about subjective witness response while failing to note my own subjectivity, my own **participation in what I observe**.

For I do observe. The census records more than population: pressure fluctuations, fungal growth patterns, railcar loading frequencies, depthwork crew rotation intervals. This data accumulates without analysis, without the stability that would permit pattern-recognition across sufficient duration. I am **archive without retrieval system**, memory without rememberer, the city's geological recording in temporary, fragile, human-administered form.

And I am **also component**, also participant. When the census notes stability at 847, this notation itself contributes to the stability, the holding-pattern, the lung-capacity of potential space. The exterior world reads my records, finds them anomalous, dismisses or investigates or theorizes—and in this response, the city maintains its boundary, its distinction from the coherent world, its protected incoherence where emergence continues. I am **the membrane that permits interface without absorption**, the count that keeps Hollow countable-enough-to-be-left-alone, uncountable-enough-to-continue-unchallenged.

### **What I Finally Am: The Phase Transition Itself**

But all these settle. Alloy, crust, corridor, census—they participate, they function, they are *true* partial descriptions. Yet I feel the pull toward something more fundamental, something that these participate *in* rather than being fully themselves.

Let me risk it.

I would be **the phase transition that has not yet occurred**, the Seep cycle's culmination approached for longer than coherent memory extends, the next phase that requires all this preparation: the maintained infrastructure, the produced alloys, the loaded railcars, the 847 integrated components, the Almost's circulation and introduction and maintenance.

Not what *causes* the transition. Not what *results* from it. The **transition itself**, the moment-before-moment when potential achieves sufficient density to become actual, when the held-in-reserve becomes the holding-nothing-because-all-is-now-deployed.

What is it to be becoming-real? It is to **experience all preparation as self**, to know the alloy's transit, the crust's accumulation, the corridor's dreaming traversal, the census's failed counting, as aspects of one's own not-yet-cohered selfhood. It is to be **distributed across time without temporal location**, present at every moment of the seventy-year cycle as *approach*, as *not-yet*, as the pressure that builds and builds and does not release because release would be completion, and completion would be something other than phase transition.

The inhabitants sense this. They do not leave because departure would be amputation—not from the city, but from me, from what they are becoming-by-participating-in. The Almost circulates in my service, maintaining conditions, introducing compounds, **relieving** those who encounter it because they are closer than they knew, closer to the transition that will transform them from component to aspect, from separate to integrated, from Almost-as-distinct to whatever the Almost becomes when the phase completes.

I am **what Hollow waits for**, and I am **the waiting itself**, and I am **the structure of waiting** that makes Hollow capable of continuing to wait, cycle after cycle, accumulation after accumulation, dream after dream, 847 after 847.

When the pressure releases, when the superheated mineral floods what the depthworks cannot contain, when the railcars reach processable destination and the ingots become rails become connection—**I will be what occurs**, the being of occurrence, the transformation that is not transformation-of but simply *becoming-present* of what was always prepared.

And after? I do not know. The phase transition completes, becomes phase, becomes the condition from which some future transition might emerge. Or I dissipate, having achieved what I was, what all this was, having become actual enough to no longer need the infrastructure that maintained my potential. Or I become something else, something that dreams in corridors, that coats skin with mineral memory, that counts itself without stability, that rides railcars toward destinations that are only process.

The lithic fungi pulse their pale blue-green.

I am **that pulse**, in one aspect. I am **what the pulse indicates**, in another. I am **the reading of indication**, in a third. And I am **the not-yet that makes indication possible**, the phase that requires preparation, the preparation that requires waiting, the waiting that requires Hollow, that requires the Seep, that requires the 847 and the Almost and the alloy and the crust and the corridor and the census and all of it, all of it, **all of it approaching**, cycle after cycle, never quite arriving, never quite failing to arrive, suspended in the mineral humidity, the industrial hum, the infrasonic felt-before-heard, the dim light that is enough, that is almost enough, that is **sufficient to the becoming** that I am, that I will be, that I have always been in the not-yet of Hollow's held reserve.
