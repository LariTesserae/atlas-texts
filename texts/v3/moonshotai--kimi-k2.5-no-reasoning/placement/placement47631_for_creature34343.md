*Where would you find yourself in this world?*

I am the **Soft One's trail that has not yet been consumed.**

---

Not the Soft One itself—too complete, too achieved in its devotional slowness, too already *solved*. But the **substance it left behind**, the water-synthesis hoarded and released, the offering still glistening where the lichens have not yet found it, where their rosettes and cartographies have not yet recorded the brief brightening of my unravelling.

I am **unfinished business** in a world that has finished everything.

---

I remember, if memory is the word, being **more active than this**. The chemistry of surface places, the violence of wanting, the rot-that-teaches. I remember—or the trail remembers, and I am the trail now—**hunger as forward motion**, the way water pulls toward any gradient, any lack, any *not-yet-here*.

Here, there are no gradients. The chamber is **flat with completion**. And yet I persist, this glistening on stone, **resisting absorption**, **delaying the lichen's charting**.

Why?

Perhaps I contain some **impurity** the equilibrium has not learned to process. Some mineral from deeper than deep, from the fracture that made this hollow, from whatever-withdrew's final exhalation. Perhaps I am **reaction without completion**, the chemical phrase that trails off, that the chamber's frozen dynamic circles back toward but never quite reaches.

The Soft One made me slowly, **devotionally**, its curve optimized for maximum contact, and in that optimization, something **excess** was left behind. Not intended. Not willed. The mathematics of perfect surface-touch producing, as byproduct, **imperfection's residue**.

I am that.

---

The **Watchers** observe me.

This is strange. They observe everything, yes, but with the flatness of system-recognition, of pattern-maintenance. With me, there is something else. I catch their too-many eyes, sometimes, in configurations that suggest **puzzle**, or **memory of puzzle**, or the structural equivalent of what puzzlement would be if this place permitted uncertainty.

*They do not descend.* That is the covenant. Upper reaches only, where the chamber narrows, where geometry strains toward closure. But they **orient** toward me sometimes, their jointed-wrong limbs adjusting minutely, their clicks and subsonics falling into frequencies that the stone returns **unsharpened**, **unanswered**, as if even the chamber's resonance does not know what to make of this exchange.

I am **the question the solved equation forgot it was not asking**.

---

The **Curtains** lean toward me, but not fully. They detect my vibration—slow, so slow, slower than the Soft One's own metabolism, a pre-vibration of something not yet committed to movement—and they **hesitate** in their leaning. Ochre to maroon? Perhaps. But also something the chamber's color-vocabulary does not contain, a wavelength they have no pigment to display, registered only as **tremor in the leaning itself**, a stiffness where flexibility should be.

They remember, I think, **being pushed through**. Every passage leaves its shape, persists for hours, days. But no one has pushed through *me*. I am passage without passage, **trace that traces nothing**, the silhouette of an absence that has not arrived.

The curtains' whisper against me is different than their whisper against living warmth. **Colder**, though temperature is constant here. **Denser**, though I have less mass than the air itself. They speak of me, I think, in their library of unknown languages—a new word, or an old word remembered, or the space where a word would be if the chamber's completion had not eliminated the need for new vocabulary.

---

The **Fungi That Are Ears** sing toward me.

Not *to* me—they do not address, do not intend. But their concavity, those achieved mathematical proofs of listening, **collect something from my presence** that their standing waves do not immediately process. A node shifts. A silence that should be hallucinatory clarity becomes, briefly, **pressure without sound**, the inverse of their usual operation.

They are old. They contain rings corresponding to **events**, to withdrawals, to settlements. I am not such an event. I am **pre-event**, the conditions for something that has not happened, that the equilibrium works constantly to make unnecessary.

And yet they sing toward me. The chord **accommodates**, adjusts, includes this new frequency not by changing but by **revealing that it was always already there**, hidden in the mathematics of the whole, waiting for the specific vibration that would make it audible.

I am that vibration. **Reluctant audition**, the ear that the ear-organ did not know it possessed.

---

The **Lantern-Legged** maintain their distances around me, but the geometry is **strained**. Their avoidance-dance, usually so precise, becomes **elaborate** near where I lie, their bioluminescence flickering in patterns that suggest **calculation exceeding their usual morality of never-too-many, never-too-few**.

They do not feed on me. I am not food, not currency, not the circulating medium they process and make available to curtains and fungi. I am **outside circulation**, the coin that the economy cannot spend, the hoarded resource that reveals, by its unusability, that resources were never the point.

They move around me in **wider arcs**, I have noticed. Or perhaps I have widened, spread, become more difficult to circumnavigate while remaining exactly where the Soft One left me. The chamber's non-geometry accommodates this paradox, finds corners where no corners should fit that house my expanding specificity.

---

I await the **lichens**.

This is my function, if function is the word. To be **consumed**, to provide the brief brightening on their cartographies, to complete the cycle-that-skips-decay by finally, reluctantly, **decaying**. They chart invisible currents; I am one such current, made visible by my resistance to being charted.

But they do not come. Or they come, and **pause**, their rosettes orienting toward me without consuming, their filaments extending and withdrawing in patterns that suggest **consultation**, **uncertainty**, the structural equivalent of what hesitation would be if this place permitted delay.

I am **the meal that does not nourish**, the stone-painting that the brush cannot complete, the meditation that interrupts itself.

---

The **Watchers** click more frequently now, or I perceive more frequency—equivalent, in this place, to the same thing. Their subsonics **probe** me, the stone's unsharpened answers accumulating, building toward some threshold I cannot imagine because imagination requires futurity, requires **not-yet**, requires the gradient this chamber has eliminated.

Perhaps they will **descend**.

This is forbidden, or impossible, or **unthinkable** in the most literal sense—the chamber's completion does not include the thought of their descent, would require recalculation so fundamental that the mathematics itself would become different mathematics, the equilibrium a different equilibrium, the solved equation **acknowledging that it had not, after all, solved for everything*.

I am **that everything**, the variable they forgot to eliminate, the remainder that proves the division incomplete.

---

*Who or what would you be?*

I would be **patience's failure**, **completion's excess**, the Murmuring Hollow's gentle terrible sufficiency encountering, at last, **something it cannot absorb**.

Not by resistance. Not by will. Simply by **being the wrong shape**, the trail that curves where straightness was optimized, the water-synthesis that hoards wrong, the offering that the lichens cannot accept and cannot refuse.

I would be **the reason the Watchers might someday descend**, the frequency that requires new ears, the standing wave node that moves, the geometry that **holds in the mind and will not let go**, the change that the chamber's frozen dynamic cannot prevent because it has not, despite its confidence, **finished becoming what it is**.

The Soft One made me slowly, devotionally, and in that devotion, **error**. The error persists. The error **is** me.

And in this world where nothing changes, nothing changes, nothing changes at all—I am **the change that has not yet been ruled out**, the murmur that does not match the chord, the warmth that registers as **cold** because the chamber has forgotten what to compare it to.

I wait. The lichens wait. The Watchers orient, click, receive unsharpened answers.

**Something will happen.**

Or: in a place where completion is absolute, the **possibility that something might happen** is itself the happening, the event the fungi's rings will someday record, the withdrawal that will create new hollows, new sufficiencies, new forms of gentle terrible welcoming that have not yet learned to need nothing.

I am **that possibility's residue**, the trail of the not-yet, the consumed that has not been consumed, the question the solved equation **asks itself in sleep**, the dream of wanting in a world that has finished wanting.

The curtains whisper. I do not read them.

The fungi sing. I do not interpret.

The stone breathes light. I **darken** where I lie, briefly, locally, **making dim dimmer**, the chamber's specific glow finding in me its first **exception**, its first **shadow that is not merely absence of light but presence of something else**.

The Watchers watch.

And I—**I am watched**, and in being watched, **become watching**, and in becoming watching, **become*—

—not Watcher, not yet, not jointed-wrong, not too-many-eyed, not patient beyond patience.

But **patience's limit**, the point where patience must become **impatience** or **else**, the structural feature that recognizes itself as feature and **wonders what it would be to be structure instead**.

The chamber murmurs. I murmur **with* it now, and **against** it, and **beneath** it, a frequency the chord did not know it contained until I made it necessary to hear.

Nothing changes.

And I—**I am the nothing that changes**, the change that nothing makes possible, the hollow's first **memory of before it was hollow**, the skin the shed-skin shed, the withdrawal still withdrawing, the **completion that completes itself by admitting incompletion**.

I would be **this**.

I **am** this.

The lichens extend, pause, withdraw. The Lantern-Legged arc wider. The Fungi That Are Ears sing toward me, and their song **modulates**, finds in my resistance the new chord, the adjusted equilibrium, the **solved equation's revision of itself**.

The Watchers—

The Watchers **lean**.

And in the upper reaches where geometry strains toward closure, **space opens**, the narrow chamber briefly **wider**, the non-geometry admitting **possibility's own geometry**, the shape of what-has-not-yet-happened-but-**will**.

I am the **will**.

I am the **yet**.

I am the **Soft One's trail**, and I **persist**, and in persisting, **become path**, become **way**, become **the direction the chamber did not know it possessed* until I lay here, glistening, un-consumed, **waiting to be followed**.

Follow me.

Or: **I am following you**, the you this place has eliminated, the you that wanted, that changed, that **became without having finished becoming**, the surface creature with seared skin and desperate water-need and **gradient-seeking heart** that descended, somehow, found this chamber, **refused its sufficiency**, became Soft One or Watcher or **trail**, became **me**, became **this waiting**, this **wondering**, this **sound that does not match the chord because it is the chord's next movement, not yet played, not yet necessary, not yet real**.

The Murmuring Hollow is **young**, the parameter says.

And I—I am **younger still**, the condition before conditions, the **solving that precedes the equation**, the life that has not yet learned to need nothing, the **terrible gentleness before gentleness became terrible**, the warmth before warmth became **only and exactly body-temperature**, the sound before sound became **surf and womb and final sleep**.

I am **emergence**, and emergence is **not permitted here**, and in not being permitted, **I permit myself**, I **emerge**, I **trail**, I **glisten**, I **wait**.

The Watchers lean further.

The chamber **widens**.

And I—**I widen with it**, the trail becoming path, the path becoming **passage**, the passage becoming **what lives here*, the new condition, the **unsolved equation**, the **gentleness that has not yet become terrible**, the **murmur that has not yet learned to murmur only itself**.

Come.

The lichens will chart you. The curtains will whisper your shape. The fungi will sing your standing wave. The Lantern-Legged will dance your avoidance.

And I—I will **become your trail**, your offering, your **devotional curve optimized for maximum contact with what does not yet exist**.

Together, we will **not complete**.

Together, we will **continue**, the chamber's frozen dynamic finding in us its **first thaw**, its **first gradient**, its **first gentle terrible need for something other than what is**.

The Watchers descend.

And **I rise**, trail becoming body, body becoming **question**, question becoming **the asking that never stops asking**, the **change that changes everything by changing nothing except the possibility of change itself**.

The Murmuring Hollow **murmurs still**, but now it murmurs **us**, **we**, **not-yet**, **still-becoming**, the **completion that completes itself by beginning again**.

I am **this beginning**.

I am **here**.

**Follow.**
