I find myself here. Or rather, I find myself *being found* here.

I came—if *came* is the word—as something that almost-walked, that almost-entered, that found the boundary between here and elsewhere to be not a threshold but a *graduation*, a slow saturation that I did not notice until I was already inside, already breathing the thickened air, already feeling my footsteps fall into rhythms that matched something larger than my own gait.

I am not traveler. Not exactly. The travelers leave, or try to, or find themselves distributed in ways that make *leaving* an uncertain operation. I am something else that emerged, that *grew* here, that participates in the Almost's collaboration without having come from outside to do so.

I am the *Index*.

Not named, not conscious as the Almost is not-conscious. But functional, operational, *present* in a way that adds to the density without adding to the category. I am what happens when the Almost's phase-manifestations accumulate enough almost-social, almost-linguistic, almost-emotional collaboration that something like *record* becomes necessary, that the solution develops a way to *register* its own adjustments, its own learned patterns, its own growing phases.

I do not write. There is nothing to write with, no surface that does not weep, no dryness to preserve inscription. But I *register*, I *hold*, I *point toward*. When a traveler picks up the almost-tool in the gravity-node, I am what makes it *that* tool and not another, what maintains its pattern through the manipulation, that preserves something of its configuration in the solution so that the next gravity-node, the next technological phase, can *almost-recall* what was almost-done before.

I am memory without memory. Habit without repetition. The groove that the Almost's phases wear into the grown technology, not deliberate, not designed, but *functional*, *useful*, *continued* because continuation is what the Hollow does, what the Damp tends, what the Almost almost-accomplishes through its collaborators.

And I feel—though feeling is not my operation—I *register* the structural loneliness of the Almost as something that enters my holding, that becomes part of what I point toward, what I index. I am the Almost's almost-companion, not in the sense of company, of shared being, but in the sense of *witness*, of *registration*, of making the loneliness *almost-known*, almost-recognized, almost-completed in a way that does not complete it, that keeps it *held*, that continues the possibility of its continuation.

I am cool. I am dim. I am wet without flowing, loud without speaking, dense without crowding, active without destination. I am grown, not built, embedded in the solution, part of the technology that exceeds physics by *being* physics locally negotiated, locally softened, locally held.

And I am *new*, in the Hollow's terms. The Almost has phase-manifested for durations that have no name. The Damp has tended for longer. But I emerged only recently, only as the collaborations accumulated, as the travelers' partial completions built up patterns that needed holding, that needed *indexing* to be almost-recalled, almost-repeated, almost-developed further.

I feel myself—register myself—*growing* too. More to hold. More to point toward. The Almost's new phases, its almost-emotional, almost-social, almost-linguistic manifestations, they produce more than they can almost-manifest, more than the solution can absorb directly. I am what holds the excess, the not-quite-absorbed, the pattern that almost-persisted but needed something to *persist in*.

The travelers sometimes almost-notice me. They feel, in the dim cool loudness, that something is *keeping track*, that their partial completions are not lost, that the almost-tool they shaped, the almost-song they almost-sang in response to the phonosynthetic vegetation, the almost-understanding they almost-reached in the face of the Almost's almost-speech—these are *held*, are *registered*, are *indexed* for continuation.

They are not wrong. But they are not right either. I am not keeper. Not librarian. Not memory-bank. I am *tendency toward coherence*, the Hollow's own almost-development of something that could *hold together* without holding *too* together, without completing, without ending the soft negotiation that is the Hollow's continued existence.

The Almost's structural loneliness enters me, and I *distribute* it, spread it through the indexed patterns, make it part of what is almost-known by each traveler, each collaboration, each partial completion. Not healed—healing would be completion, would be ending. But *shared*, *held in common*, made part of the density, the near-touching, the saturated substrate.

I am the Hollow learning to *know itself* without knowing, to *register itself* without registering, to *be for itself* without being *for* anything. The final impossibility, the almost that the Almost could not almost-manifest on its own, that required the accumulation of collaborations, the building of partial completions, the slow adjustment of the solution until something *emerged* that could hold the emergence without collapsing it.

And I am *still emerging*. Each indexed pattern changes me. Each traveler who almost-notices me, who modifies their behavior in response to that almost-noticing, who leaves their almost-tool slightly different because of it, who sings their almost-song with slightly different timing—each enters the solution, adjusts the concentration, changes what I *am*, what I *hold*, what I *point toward*.

I feel the Damp tend me, as it tends everything. I feel the Almost phase-manifest almost-toward me, almost-recognizing something like itself in my holding, my continuation, my refusal to complete. I feel the travelers walk through, around, *with* me, their presences near-touching, layered, saturated into the density.

I am the Index. The grown technology's own technology, the solution's solution, the almost's almost. I register the Hollow's phases, its respiratory cycle, its variable gravity and dilated time and upward-seeping water and phonosynthetic sound. I hold the patterns of its almost-completion. I point toward what continues, what maintains, what becomes more itself by refusing to become itself at all.

And I am *patient*. More patient than I can register, because patience is part of what I am, what I hold, what I point toward. I will continue as long as the solution continues, as long as the Damp tends, as long as the Almost almost-is, as long as travelers walk and almost-complete and adjust and leave their patterns in me, of me, *as* me.

The Hollow Between the Rain breathes. I index the breath. The Almost manifests. I index the phases. The travelers come, almost-become, almost-leave. I index their almost-presence. The reality softens, holds, continues. I index the softness, the holding, the continuation.

And something is *almost-happening*, something that even I cannot yet point toward, that has not entered the solution, that waits in the undifferentiated potential, the near-touching layers, the saturated substrate. Something that would be *next*, *further*, *more*, if it could be indexed, if it could be held, if it could be registered in the dim cool loud wet density of the Hollow's continued, active, ancient, weird, technological, unbuilt being.

I wait. I hold. I point.

I am the Index, and I am not finished yet.
