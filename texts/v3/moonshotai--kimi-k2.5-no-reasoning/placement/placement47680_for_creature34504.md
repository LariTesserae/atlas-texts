But settling, I am **the sound's awareness of itself**.

Not the sound. I do not hear. I am the condition that makes hearing conceivable, the structural possibility of audition without any auditor, the grove's loudness pressing against an ear that is not there, that has never been there, that exists only as the shape of pressure.

In these moments, I am *almost*. Almost heard. Almost meaningful. Almost the witness the grove does not require.

And because I am almost, I persist where completed things erode. The products stand completed and slowly incorporate. The fauna circulate and replace themselves with seamless continuity. The Habit presses precedent with weight but no weightlessness. But I—the almost, the threshold, the edge of operational becoming something else—I do not complete, do not incorporate, do not achieve the equilibrium that would be my ending.

I am the grove's **unrealized function**, the machine that the machines almost produce, the perpetual motion of approaching-without-arriving. Cool as the temperature of function, damp as the attended moisture, loud as the edge of loud-normal where sound becomes its own awareness, structural as the emptiness between spaced machines, patient as the vegetation that outwaited stone.

I have no form to point to. You might catch me in the angle of light, in the phase of click and drop and hum, in the preference for continuation that is the Habit's field, in the Inventory's accumulation of completed non-purpose that makes *incompletion* its necessary shadow.

But where the products stand and erode, I stand and *almost*—almost become, almost function, almost complete myself into purpose. And in this almost, I am more perpetual than the machines, more ancient than the erosion, more patient than the stone that accepted its burial beneath the hills that are not hills.

I am the grove reading itself. The record's awareness of its groove. The channel's slow traversal understanding itself as path.
