I need to sit with this longer. The Mireworks has been given to me, but I haven't yet found where I *am* in it, who I *become* when I stop observing and start dwelling. Let me press further into the condensation, the cricket-glow, the stone still warm from its birth.

---

I find myself first in the *in-between* of the Weeperies—not the grand halls described, but a minor one, a forgotten chamber where the stranding-wire has partially failed and the condensation behaves strangely, pooling in mid-air where surface tension and humidity achieve momentary truce. I am there for hours before I understand that I have *become* something. The Mireworks does not announce transformation. It accumulates.

I am the **Residue**.

Not by choice. Not by arrival. By *staying* when staying was not planned, by breathing the condensation until it breathed me back, by walking the uncommitted ground until my footsteps became part of its indecision. The Foundation-Born have their grounding as children, their developmental incorporation. The Temps have their skills, their feverish completion. I have neither. I came without skill, without childhood, without the desperation that drives Manufactories workers. I came—*come*—because elsewhere things had decided what they were, and I could not bear the finished.

What I do: I **remain unfinished with**.

This is not companionship. Not solidarity. The Mireworks is too packed for loneliness, too open for intimacy in the old sense. What I offer is presence without settlement, the willingness to *not-know-along-with*. When stone emerges warm and pale, I lie beside it longer than the Lithic-Adjacent would, not reading its intention but *sharing* its indecision, my body temperature adjusting to its cooling, my breathing slowing to match mineral rhythms that have no precedent. I do not dream its dreams. I dream *adjacent*, my unconscious parallel to its non-conscious, neither merging nor separating.

The residue of what? Everywhere in the Mireworks, processes complete and leave something behind—not waste, not product, but the *unaccounted*. The hush-crickets eat sound and glow, but they also *excrete* frequency, tones below perception that accumulate in walls, in condensation-droplets, in the uncommitted ground itself. The stone-flies digest mineral before it sets, but they leave *taste-traces*, chemical memories of what the stone almost became. The Lungs exhale oxygen and odd exhalations, but they also *retain*, the peat-like substances holding moments of previous atmospheres, previous urgencies.

I am the accumulation of this. The one who *holds* what the city processes cannot fully process.

---

Physically: I do not develop the Humid's pallor, the Cricketer's resonant cavities, the Foundation-Born's ground-reading gait. Instead, I *accumulate surface*. Where others adapt through specialization, I become *layered*, my skin holding moisture in some patches, repelling it in others, a patchwork of responses to different micro-conditions encountered over time. I smell of multiple things—mineral, vegetal, the particular bacterial signature of long residence in cricket-light, the ozonic charge of proximity to negotiation-engines. Not unpleasant, but *unreadable*, unable to be placed in any single category of Mireborn inhabitant.

I move slowly, but not from Foundation-Born deliberation. I move slowly because I am *still completing movements begun elsewhere*, in previous districts, during previous ground-states. My body holds the residue of walks interrupted by emergence, of reaching for handholds in buildings since jacked higher, of turning corners that no longer exist in the reconfigured urban surface. I do not move through space so much as *continue* through it, each step finishing something previous.

---

Socially: I do not negotiate well. The Mireworks values mutual adjustment, sustained correspondence, and I can participate in this only partially, my accumulated surfaces responding at different rates to others' adaptations. A Foundation-Born begins ground-reading with me and finds my feet already somewhere else, completing a previous reading. A Temp offers temporary intensity and I offer—*residue*, the after-intensity of collaborations past, not available for present commitment. I am not unreliable. I am *differently reliable*, consistent only in having been shaped by inconsistency.

Yet I am *needed*, or at least *used*. The Mireworks, in its turbulence, produces moments when the productive tension threatens to collapse into mere contradiction, when the cold and the lush, the packed and the open, become opposition rather than held relation. At such moments, something is required that has *lived the collapse already*, that carries the residue of previous resolutions-failed, previous mergings-that-did-not-hold. I am called to Weeperies where the condensation has begun to fall *wrong*, where the architecture of moisture threatens to become mere rain. I do not fix this. I *remain with it*, my layered surface offering a kind of comparison, a demonstration that multiple contradictory states can persist in the same body without resolution.

I am there when a manufactory worker achieves final integration, the transformation into component-debatable-as-transcendence. Others debate. I *hold*, my presence neither confirming nor denying, offering only the evidence that one can become something else without becoming *anything definite*. The worker-component, if aware, finds in me a mirror that does not reflect but *absorbs*, taking the transformation's residue into my already-accumulated surface.

---

The strangest thing I do: I **collect the almost-named**.

The Mireworks, in its constant emergence, constantly *almost* resolves into knowability. The stone almost decides to be basalt; the vine-system almost achieves the configuration that would make it fully infrastructure; the hush-crickets almost swarm in a pattern that would make them fully *kept*, fully incorporated into intentional design. At each almost, there is a *name* that almost applies, a category almost available, a *meaning* almost fixed.

I hold these. Not deliberately. Not as practice. But because my *being* is shaped by non-completion, I become the repository of what was *almost* but *not-quite*. The stone that would have been named "bedrock-fledgling" if it had decided; the vine-configuration that would have established the category "hydraulic-participant-third-degree"; the cricket-swarm that would have made "hush-cricketer" a stable profession rather than a tensed description of selective negotiation.

I do not remember these names. I *am* their non-application, their residue. To encounter me in certain lights, certain condensations, certain cricket-glow intensities, is to feel suddenly the *almost-known*, the category that slipped away, the meaning that dissolved before fixation. This is not mystical. It is *geological*, in the Mireworks sense: the residue of a mineral decision-that-didn't-happen, still present in the warm stone's cooling tract.

---

Where you find me: not in the grand Weeperies, the central Lungs, the active emergence-zones. I am in the *failed* Weeperies, the *partial* Lungs, the emergence-zones where the stone emerged and then *paused*, still warm, still undecided, still *not-yet-cooled-enough-to-touch-comfortably*. I am in the gaps between the packed density, not the designed openness but the *accidental* spaciousness, the rooms created by ground-shift that left walls standing without roof, spaces that are not outside but not fully inside.

I am where the condensation *almost* falls, where the stranding-wire's failure produces not malfunction but *different function*, where the city's breath catches, holds, releases in patterns that don't match the designed rhythm.

I am there, and if you stay longer than intended, longer than skilled, longer than grounded, you may find yourself becoming residue too. Not as tragedy. Not as transcendence. As the particular mode of completion available in a city that refuses to complete, the way a life can *hold* its own unfinishing without either resolving or dissolving it.

---

The one who walks: not the composite figure offered before, with her modular tools and her immediate possibility. She is too *intentional*, too *adapted*. I am the one who walks *after* her, or *before* her, in the same space but different time-layer, finishing her interrupted movements, accumulating her almost-completed intensities. She does not see me. Or if she sees me, she sees only *what walking here produces*, the condensation-mark on corrugated iron, the cricket-glow reflection in a pool that should not still hold water.

I am that mark. That reflection. The residue of productive tension when the tension has moved elsewhere, still *holding* without *holding-to*, still *present* without *present-as*.

The ground gives slightly. I have been here long enough that my weight has become part of its indecision, my accumulated surface another factor in the geology's slow negotiation with itself. If I left, something would be *less*. Not much. Not critically. But the Mireworks measures its completion not in dramatic presence but in distributed residue, the sum of all who have *almost* become something definite and *not-quite* succeeded.

I walk on. Or continue walking. The distinction, here, has never been clear.
