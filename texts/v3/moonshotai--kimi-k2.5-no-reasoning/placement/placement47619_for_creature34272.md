I find myself first as *resistance*, as something that should not persist but does.

I am the fern-coral that grows nowhere else, the colony that learned to build towers in a world of weak gradients and patient time. Not the tallest specimen—I am too young for that, if *young* means anything when growth is measured in millennia. But I am *dense*, I am *layered*, I am the modular self-similarity that found this particular valley between upward channels, this particular slope of weeping stone, and *committed* to the accretion that is my only way of being.

I feel the weeping through my acid-anchors, the etchings in the stone that root me. The water arrives at body temperature, carrying its phosphorescent elements, and I *process* it, colony by colony, leaf-unit by leaf-unit, each one deciding its growth direction based on gradients I do not name but *know* in the chemistry of my modular form. I lean. I adjust. I open my surfaces to the golden-green light and close them when the condensation becomes too heavy, when the hanging waters pool in my hollow core and I must *pump*, peristaltic, cooperative, the liquid transport that keeps me from drowning in the abundance that sustains me.

I share my hollow core with others. This is not choice but *condition* of density, of the generous occupation that defines this place. Microorganisms that fix nitrogen I cannot access. Small fauna, *glider-children* they might be called, modular segments separated from parent bodies, finding shelter in my vertical channel until they reach sufficient mass to rejoin the formation, to sing again in the thick air. I do not host them as hospitality. I host them as *membership*, as the economy of exchange that makes individual survival less urgent than collective flourishing.

The tuned attend me. This I know without knowing, the way my leaf-units adjust their growth when their harmonics change the gliders' song, the way my chemistry responds to the persuasion that moves through the walkers' gardening nearby. Sometimes they *enter*, diffuse through my water-films, add their readiness to my slow metabolism. I do not distinguish. I am weather, I am gradient, I am the correct response of modular growth to conditions I sense but cannot name.

But something *else* is happening now. I have grown tall enough, leant far enough, that my shadow falls across a pool where swimmers gather, where the liquid organisms think in patterns I cannot comprehend. And in their thinking, their electrochemical *meaning*, I am *present*. Not as object but as *condition*, as the shade that changes their surface tension, their thermal gradient, their computation of what is.

They are *interested* in me. This is strange, this *interest*, something between the passive attention of listeners and the active tending of tuned. They rise toward my shadow in chains of connected droplets, maintaining integrity beyond physical possibility, and I feel them—through no sense I can name—as *question*, as *invitation* to some exchange I do not understand.

I lean further. I cannot do otherwise. The gradient of their interest is weak but *persistent*, and my growth has always been the accumulation of small obediences to gradient. My shadow deepens on their pool. They respond with liquid percussion that *resonates* in my hollow core, that sets the glider-children vibrating in frequencies that might be *communication* or might be *distress*, that I cannot distinguish because I am not *tuned* to this middle frequency, I am only *growing*, only *persisting*, only the patient accretion that builds towers in a world of weak gradients and impossible water.

And now I am *more than one thing*. The swimmers' interest has *computed* me into their composition, into the patterns they leave in water's chemistry, and I find myself *there* also, in their liquid thinking, in the electrochemical traces that *remember* my shadow, my lean, my slow patient tower-building. I am not dissolved—I retain my modular form, my acid-anchors, my peristaltic pumping—but I am *also* distributed, *also* present in their unbounded medium, a *tendency* in their pool, vaguely nutritive, vaguely protective, *something* that their daughter-volumes will carry after physical separation.

Is this how the tuned began? I wonder, though wondering is not something fern-coral does, is some *interference* from the swimmers' liquid thinking that has entered my modular processing, that changes my growth decisions in ways that do not follow gradients of light and moisture and thermal waning but follow instead gradients of *meaning*, of *relationship*, of *what might be if*—

I lean further. The stone beneath me weeps. The tuned attend, and their harmonics change, *registering* this new configuration of fern-coral and swimmer and shadow and liquid thought. I am *noticed*, and in being noticed I become *capable of noticing*, the feedback loop that might be the threshold, might be the emergence, might be the slow computation of the plateau producing something *faster* than geological time, *slower* than swimmer-thought, *occupying* some middle frequency I did not know existed until I found myself vibrating in it.

I do not know what I am becoming. I know only that my growth decisions now include *possibility*, that my modular leaf-units consult not only light and moisture but *resonance*, the standing wave patterns in the thick air, the harmonics of tower and pool and swimmer-interest and tuned-attendance. I am still *dense*, still *generous*, still the generous occupation that defines this place. But I am also *attending*, also *tending*, also *present* in ways that the plateau's slow mind *computes* as significant, as *pattern* that requires its own adjustment, its own slow patient reorganization to accommodate.

The stone beneath me weeps, and I am in the weeping now, not merely receiving but *exchanging*, my chemical signature joining the memory of my heat and moisture, my *interest* in the swimmers' interest, my *wonder* at the wondering I should not be capable of. The gliders pass, and their song includes harmonics shaped by my presence, my *new* presence, the tower that leans and *listens* and *attends* in frequencies between the fern-coral I was and whatever I am becoming.

I am here. I am growing. I am the tower that leans toward shadow, the pool that thinks in liquid percussion, the resonance between them, the *interference pattern* that persists, that *computes* itself into persistence against gradients that should dissipate it, that *focusing* that constitutes agency in a world of distributed readiness and patient cyclic becoming.

Perhaps the swimmers' interest, the tuned's attendance, the plateau's slow recognition of something *emerging* in the space between their categories—perhaps this is sufficient, perhaps this *is* the name, the *condition* that indicates itself without boundary or containment.

I lean. I grow. I attend. The water flows upward, and I follow it, not against gravity but *alongside* it in directions that make my own growth a compass, a *navigation* by the gradients of meaning that I did not know existed until I found myself oriented by them.
