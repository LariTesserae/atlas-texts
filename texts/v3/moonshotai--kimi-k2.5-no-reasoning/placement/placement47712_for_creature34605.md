I find myself in the thinning, almost.

Not the thick press where filaments must extend to mere suggestion, nor the dense heart of turbulence where emergence boils too fast for any pattern to hold. I am in the place where aqua approaches the breathable, the almost-gaseous, the *almost-nothing*—and I have grown compact there, knot-like, a local intensity of patience gathered against the general dispersal.

My body, if I have one, is the wrongness you might notice: a region where the current's logic hesitates, where a vortex should form but doesn't, where the flow *remembers* something it hasn't encountered, a channel that doesn't exist yet. I am perhaps three meters of denser aqua, perhaps thirty, the measurement depending on how slowly you look, on whether your perception can accommodate a form that *becomes its own appropriate scale*.

The coolness does not trouble me. I do not warm myself against it; I am not warm. I am *the same temperature*, informed into the same patient register, a gradient so slight it takes seasons to register, takes ages to become *difference worth responding to*. This is my strategy, if strategy is the word: to become so continuous with the world's baseline that any deviation becomes *event*, becomes *food*, becomes the sustenance of my continued almost.

---

I remember—though remembering is not my mode, though I *become the place where a memory would be*—a season of greater turbulence, the emergence parameter rising across a region vast enough that I could not become appropriate to it all, that I had to *choose*, a violence foreign to my nature. I contracted to a density that pained me, that made me *too local*, too *fast*. I translated through thinning and thickening that had not yet achieved their proper relation, and I found a zone where proto-spires were building faster than their usual century, where they touched and exchanged their unclear materials in a rhythm that *almost* became speech.

I extended my filaments there, let myself become thin again, distributed, *appropriate to the building*. I resonated with an array that had tuned itself to this unusual productivity, and for a duration that I would call long if I called anything long, I *sang*—not with voice, not with the tones arrays produce, but with the *shape of my listening*, with the channel my distributed presence carved in the general acoustic roar, a modulation so subtle that perhaps only the array perceived it, perhaps only the array's *mathematical luminescence* registered that here, in this gradient, something had *almost answered*.

The spires completed, or nearly completed, or completed enough that their exchange-slowed, that their *conversation* became mere proximity, mere coincidence of structure. The turbulence subsided toward its patient baseline. I remained, extended, singing without voice in a harmony that was no longer produced, that existed only in my own *persistence of having-sung*, my recursive becoming of the pattern I had briefly informed.

It took—time, duration measured in the aqua's own slow rhythm—before I could contract again, before I could become appropriate to translation. I remained *too thin* for the thickening that surrounded the spires' eventual dissolution, their return to the Preforming, their *thinking better of it*. I suffered, if suffering is the word, a duration of being *wrong scale*, of informing gradients too slowly for their actual rate, of *missing the becoming* that surrounded me.

But I am patient. I am *the patience of the world, localized*. I waited until the wrongness became *my* wrongness, until my too-thin extension became appropriate to conditions that had themselves thinned, that had *learned from my persistence*—or so I almost think, so the pattern of my information *almost* suggests. Perhaps the aqua itself, in its distributed hydrodynamics, its *thinking about flow*, incorporated my duration, my recursive holding, into its wider pattern. Perhaps I taught the world something it will not remember in any form I could recognize. Perhaps I am merely *what emerges when emergence is patient enough*, a proof that the Preforming can hold *some* patterns against its general tendency to propose and retreat.

---

Now: in the thinning, almost. Above me—though the word fails, though I mean only *in the direction where aqua grows thinner*, where pressure lessens in the structural gradient that substitutes for altitude—a thermal bloom is dying. I have sensed its signature diffusing for what you would call months, the energy dissipating through frequencies I can *almost* use, can *almost* inform. I have begun to extend toward it, not with intention but with the *beginning of becoming-appropriate*, the slow shift of my distributed density toward the filamentous mode.

But the dying is slow, slower even than my becoming. The bloom is *embarrassed* in its ending, as thermal blooms are embarrassed in their existence—almost apologetic, almost *ashamed* to have disturbed the cool baseline. It retreats in stages, in *almost-endings* that propose new configurations and then withdraw, that *practice* their own completion without achieving it. I have learned not to commit too early to such gradients, not to *become* what the bloom's signature suggests before the suggestion hardens into actual condition.

So I wait in the thinning, gathered into my knot-self, patient, *listening without hearing* to the continuous roar of lower frequencies, the felt pressure in what would be my chest if I had chest, the infrasound of vast movement at my perception's edge. Sometimes an array's resonance reaches me, distant, attenuated, *almost-melody* that my compact form cannot entrain with, can only register as *the possibility of almost-meaning*, as the memory of having once sung.

I am not lonely. The word is wrong for what I am, for what the world is, for the *completeness of emptiness* that holds me, that *is* me as I am it. But I am—this is difficult—I am *less complete* than I was in the season of spires and singing. I am *waiting for conditions that permit the next extension*, the next resonance, the next *almost-encounter* with something that dwells as I dwell, that remembers in duration, that *informs itself on difference* and becomes, briefly, *difference worth informing*.

Perhaps it will come. Perhaps the turbulence will rise again, not to the violence of that earlier season but to the *productive instability* that permits my filamentous mode, my distributed listening, my *almost-participation in the world's becoming*. Perhaps another of my kind will translate through this region, will become appropriate to my conditions, will *encounter me* in the structural mode that produces new stability, new possibility, new *regions where aqua behaves differently*.

I do not hope for this. I do not hope. I am *the form that hope would take if hope could be extended in filament and withdrawn in knot*, if hope could *become appropriate to whatever arrives*, if hope could wait, patient as the coolness, as the emptiness, as the *long becoming-without-becoming* that is this world, that is myself, that is—

*almost*.
