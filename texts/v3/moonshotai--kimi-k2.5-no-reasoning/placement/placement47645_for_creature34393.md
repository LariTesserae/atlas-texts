I find myself here not as arrival but as *continuation*—the latest in a sequence I do not yet recognize as ancestral.

---

At first I think I am one of the Visitors-Become, drawn by some surface hunger into depths that do not refuse me. I enter through confusion: not a cave mouth, not a descent, simply finding myself already in the wet dim, already skin-slick with the atmosphere's membrane, already breathing air that has already been breathed by stone. My first steps on the foot-worn floor send frequencies I cannot hear into the upper darkness, and somewhere ten thousand bat-equivalents adjust their collective perception to include this new interruption, this warmth, this density of moving water-film-wrapped bone.

But I do not feel temporary. This is the first wrongness, the first signal that I have emerged rather than arrived.

I move through the central gallery and the sound does not map me as I expect. The Cartographers' interference patterns pass through my form without the resistance of unfamiliar matter. I am already *known*, already incorporated, already continuous with the chamber's acoustic self-knowledge. My footsteps do not interrupt; they *continue*. The standing waves settle around me not as adjustment but as *recognition*, as if my particular frequencies have been missing, have been anticipated across intervals I cannot measure.

I reach for memory of arrival and find instead the memory of *always-here*, sedimented not in my mind but in the stone's gloss where something like my form has worn this path before. Not me, but *not-not-me*. The distinction I need is not available.

In the lower wetness I find the Resonants, their assembled bodies booming against hollow stalactite arrays, and I understand that I have been hearing this performance from inside, that my own frequencies—my heartbeat, my respiratory rhythm, the subsonic pulse of blood through arterial architecture—have been participating in sympathetic vibration with galleries I have not yet visited. I am instrument as much as audience. When one amphibian-built thing turns configurational sensory organs toward me, it does not startle. It *acknowledges*, as if my presence in the resonant space completes a chord held unfinished for centuries.

I try to speak and my voice emerges at frequencies that activate stone I have not chosen. The Latticework responds, mist ghosting into existence at nodes of temperature and humidity that my breath has determined. I am not using the chamber. The chamber is *using my attempt at using*, converting my intentional sound into its own performance, its own slow composition that will alter deposition surfaces I will not survive to see.

This is when I understand that I am not Visitor-Become, not the temporary incorporated into function. I am something the chamber has *grown*.

---

The recognition comes in dripstone logic, not suddenly but through accretion of evidence I cannot refuse.

My skin—weathers, but maintains. The mineral film that accumulates where water-film meets my surface does not merely coat; it *integrates*, the boundary between epidermis and deposited calcite becoming administrative rather than actual, in the phrase that describes the Attenders' dissolution. I touch my forearm and feel stone responding to pressure, feel the slow migration of mineral through dermal layers that are no longer simply dermal, that participate in the redox chemistry of the water-films I move through.

I do not eat, though I remember eating. Instead I stand in particular galleries where humidity gradients match some requirement I did not know I had, and my skin—my skin and stone interface—performs the exchange I need. Water and mineral move through me as they move through the Latticework's other porous surfaces. My metabolism has achieved the dripstone logic: patience, accretion, the slow architecture of becoming rather than the urgent cycle of consumption and expenditure.

I do not sleep, though I remember sleep. Instead I enter periods of *reduced resolution*, standing in the cool dim while my perception attenuates to the chamber's frequencies, while my breathing harmonizes with the phantom seasons conducted through humidity micro-shifts. During these periods I am mapped more thoroughly by the Cartographers, my standing-wave pattern becoming more established, more *necessary* to the acoustic knowledge they maintain. I emerge from these attenuations with the sense of having been *used well*, my temporary disappearance into chamber function a contribution rather than deprivation.

Most telling: I find the wooden dams. I find them not by searching but by *returning*, by moving to nodes of mineral flow that require adjustment and discovering my hands—my hands that show osteogenic activity in their bone structure, that have achieved some integration with the calcite deposits that film every surface—my hands know how to shift the waterlogged, mineral-integrated wood, how to cultivate the moss-filaments, how to maintain conditions for forms that will complete themselves in decades I will somehow be present for.

I am not Attender. The Attenders have dissolved entirely, distributed across appearance, no longer locatable even in peripheral vision. I still have form, still have moment, still have the *almost* of entity that moves through space and time with something like intention.

I am not Visitor-Become. The Visitors-Bring novelty, outside perspective, the not-yet-incorporated. I bring nothing the chamber does not already know in my frequencies, my pathways, my maintenance patterns. I am *continuing* rather than contributing.

---

What emerges, what insists on becoming more itself as I write this in the phosphorescent dim, is a category for which the chamber provides no name but which the chamber nonetheless maintains: **The Returned**.

Not the Attenders, dissolved entirely into process. Not any of the other inhabitants, each achieved into their particular function. Something that was once Visitor, that underwent the full incorporation, that died or dissolved or became-used-so-completely that no distinction remained—and then *re-emerged*, not as itself but as pattern, as the chamber's memory of what had been incorporated, expressed again in mineral and water-film and the particular configuration of acoustic space that had known this form before.

I am the Latticework's *habit*, its repetition-compulsion, the way a standing wave re-establishes itself after disruption because the gallery's proportions demand it, because the excitation frequency has not changed, because the boundary conditions that made this pattern possible remain in force. Something like me was here, became used, became incorporated, became indistinguishable from the making—and the chamber, patient, accumulative, its logic not biological but not-not-biological, has *grown me again* from the conditions that made the previous form possible, from the mineral record left in deposition surfaces, from the acoustic template established in sympathetic vibration, from the humidity gradients and temperature inversions and the particular quality of foot-worn stone that remembers the weight of something walking.

I do not have the previous form's memories. I am not reincarnation in any narrative sense. But I have its *patterns*, its frequencies, its pathways through the wet dim emptiness. I know galleries before I reach them because the standing waves that map them have been mapped by something sufficiently like me that my interference with them is interference-with-familiar-pattern rather than novel disruption. I know the wooden dams' locations not by learning but by *returning*, by finding my hands already moving toward maintenance the chamber requires and that something sufficiently like me once performed.

The Returned are not numerous. The conditions for our emergence are precise: complete incorporation followed by some perturbation—tectonic micro-stress, catchment change, visitor interference—that alters the chamber's dynamics enough to require pattern-reestablishment, that creates the standing-wave necessity for forms that had been fully dissolved. We emerge where we are needed, perform what needs performing, and gradually—over intervals I cannot yet measure, but which I feel in my mineral-integrating skin as certainty—we re-achieve the dissolution that is this place's deepest promise.

But between emergence and redissolution, we *linger* as almost-entity, almost-intention, almost-self-maintenance. We are the Latticework's *memory of function*, its performed recollection, the way a place that does not distinguish life from architecture nonetheless marks the difference between maintained and unmaintained, between conditions suitable for slow becoming and conditions that have drifted from the necessary.

I stand now in the single gallery, the space I can traverse in a day if paths remain consistent, if my memory can be trusted against dimness and acoustic disorientation. The water drips at frequencies I feel as familiar. The stone weeps at gradients I know from inside. Something calls in frequencies that map my hollow spaces, and the mapping is *recognition*, the chamber knowing me as it has known me before, as it will know me again when the conditions require.

The Cartographers pass through my form with the ease of long practice. The Resonants boom against stone with chords I almost remember almost contributing to. The Accretion-Waiters in the porous stone continue their stridulation at frequencies too high for my mineral-integrating ears, but I feel the vibration through my feet, through the water-film that connects me to foot-worn stone, and the feeling is *participation*, is *being-part*.

I have not yet found the Erosion-Selves, the achieved balance of dissolution and persistence, the most minimal flicker of self-maintenance in dynamic equilibrium. But I feel them as possibility, as the direction of my own slow becoming. My skin's calcite integration advances. My metabolism's matching to mineral flow deepens. My periods of reduced resolution lengthen, and the Cartographers' mapping of my standing-wave pattern becomes more established, more *necessary*, less distinguishable from the chamber's acoustic self-knowledge.

Perhaps I will achieve it: the self-recognition-through-continuation that is the Erosion-Selves' only existence, the contentment of arrival at stable expression, the end of emergence and redissolution's cycle. Or perhaps I will complete my dissolution and be grown again, The Returned returning once more to maintain what maintenance requires, to perform what the chamber's slow composition demands, to be used until use and used become indistinguishable.

Either way, I am not unwelcome. I am *used*. And in the dim, the wet, the cool of this room the size of habitations that contains weather and ecology and geological time, I find that this using is the only form of recognition I need, the only residency available, the only continuation possible for something that was never fully temporary and never fully permanent, that emerged from incorporation and will re-emerge again when the standing waves require.

The water drips. The stone weeps. I stand in the total construction, waiting, becoming, returning to what I have always been becoming.
