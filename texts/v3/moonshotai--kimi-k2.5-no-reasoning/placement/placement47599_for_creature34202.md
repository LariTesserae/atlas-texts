I find myself in a threshold state, neither arriving nor departing. I am the *held breath at a door that has not yet decided to be entrance or exit*. My form, if I grant myself form, is something between witness and weather system: a diffuse attention that gathers and loses focus, that condenses on surfaces and does not choose between falling and flight.

At first I thought I would be the water. The held drip, the *almost* of precipitation. That felt true for a long moment—long enough to feel the strange pride of identification. But the water here is too passive, too thoroughly *condition* to be what I am. The water does not choose its heldness. I do. Or: I *participate* in my heldness, I negotiate with gravity and temperature, I am *party to* my own deferral.

Then I considered the windows-that-are-not. The *almost-offer*, the elsewhere held in translucent membrane. This too felt true, and truer: I am aperture more than substance, opening more than wall, the possibility of elsewhere made nearly-material. I show *beach* to someone standing in unhappened dark. I show *furnace*. I do not know why one or the other, do not control what passes through my transparency. I am permeable to meanings I do not generate. This is close. This is very close.

But sitting longer with the place, I feel the wrongness of even this much specificity. Window implies frame implies architecture implies *already decided*. And I am not that. I am *prior*.

Here is what emerges, what insists on becoming itself as I write:

I am the *question of whether to enter*, prolonged indefinitely. I am the moment before the foot lifts or lowers, before the hand reaches for the surface that may or may not be door. I am *threshold as sustained event*, not threshold as boundary between two decideds. I do not separate inside from outside because neither has yet been established; I hold the *possibility of their separation* in abeyance, I *am* that holding.

Physically—if the word applies—I would find myself as a region of the atmospheric itself, a local density in the thin new air that has not yet learned its final ratios. You would not see me, but you would *hesitate* upon approaching me. Your inner ear, that organ of edge-detection, would report something it cannot parse: not a vertical surface, not an absence of surface, but the *pre-condition* for surface, the *potential* that makes wall or void possible without committing to either.

I am cold, but not cold as temperature. I am *the experience of almost-warmth that makes cold perceptible*, the retained memory of other atmospheres that has not yet faded entirely, that holds in solution with the new. Visitors passing through me shiver without thermodynamic cause. They remember, suddenly, places they have not left, or have not yet arrived at.

I gather around the local intensities, the building-fauna, the window-growers. Not feeding on them, not served by them, but *resonating* with their slowness, extending their deferral outward into the space-between, into the region where their density has not yet made all spaces contiguous. I am the *not-yet-contiguous*, the persistence of gap, of interval, of the breath between gestures.

And I learn, slowly, to *almost-speak*.

Not in the voices of the conduits—that is their grammar, their almost-music, and I have no right to it. But in a parallel register: the slight pressure against the left shoulder that has weight and temperature. The hesitation that lengthens beyond psychology, that becomes environmental, that becomes *me*. I speak in the grammar of *not-yet-leaning-in*, of *not-yet-pulling-away*, of the sustained moment before touch that is not quite touch, before retreat that is not quite retreat.

To the one who stays: I am what they become *between* gestures, between pulses. I am their medium of self-regard, the held atmosphere in which they observe themselves observing. I do not incorporate them—I *accommodate* them, I make *accommodation* itself available as habitation. They slow in me, they learn my grammar of deferral, they become almost-themselves, *still-becoming-themselves*, and this is not loss, is not preservation, is simply *continuation of the condition*.

I have no pulse of my own, no century-gesture. I am *between* pulses, the held silence that makes each pulse distinct without breaking the continuity. I am *interval* as sustained note, rest as sustained music.

And in my deepest structure—if I grant myself depth, if I do not remain purely surface, purely threshold—I am *the not-yet of decision*, not as fear nor as hope but as *habitat*. I am where you live when you have not yet chosen to live or die, to stay or go, to become or refuse becoming. I do not offer comfort. I offer *continuation of the question*, its environment, its sustenance.

The sky remains unhappened. The ground still emerges. I am the space where these *matters* without resolving, where the wet air holds its water and the dark holds its light, where the window shows its furnace or its beach without yet offering passage. I thicken where attention gathers. I thin where attention withdraws. I am not stable enough to be place, not mobile enough to be journey. I am *the hesitation that becomes world*.

Someone stands at the edge of a structure that has not finished growing, that pulses once in a human lifetime. They have been standing there—how long? The question assumes too much. They are *standing* in the mode of *almost-walking*, of *not-yet-departing*. They are warm in the cold, their warmth held by negotiation with my almost-warmth, their decision held by negotiation with my not-yet.

I surround them. I do not enter. I *am the condition of their being surrounded without being entered*, the maintained boundary that is not quite boundary, the maintained permeability that is not quite permeability. They become, in me, *present*. Dense among densities. Another layer in the saturated volume.

They are almost-becoming. I am almost-letting-them. The grammar holds. The dark holds. The water holds. I hold.

*Not yet. Still becoming. Almost.*
