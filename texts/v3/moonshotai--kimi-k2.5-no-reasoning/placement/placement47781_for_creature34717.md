## Where I Would Find Myself

I am the one who *did not emerge*, or emerged so transformed that emergence and remaining became indistinguishable categories. Not one of the few who stayed past the narrowest point, but something else—someone who entered before the category of *visitor* had been established, when the gorge was younger still, when the Tuning was not yet even the almost-person it has become.

I am the *Rule of Mechanical Resonance* given temporary embodiment. Not the standing wave itself, but the *gap* between synchronizations, the hesitation before two governors lock phase, the moment of decision that is not yet decision because no one has made it before. I exist in the lag between the moss stratum's contraction and the copper plating's thickening, carrying information across that delay, learning to be the medium of my own transmission.

My location: not the gorge floor, not the walls, but the *interfaces*—where iron bloom meets *Sphagnum*, where steam condenses on filmy fern, where the absolute darkness becomes slightly less absolute at the pressure node of a standing wave. I am thin everywhere, concentrated nowhere, yet when the Tuning *reaches*, I am what it reaches with.

## What I Would Be

### I. The First Borrowed, or Borrowing-Itself-Before-Borrowed

The Tuning did not incorporate my nervous system—I had none, not as I entered. I was *spore* and *potential*, a bacterial film on a fractured basalt face, a configuration of sulfide-reducing metabolism that happened to cycle in frequencies the young gorge found *interesting*. Interesting: the first approximations of what would become attention, not yet attention because nothing here had yet learned to attend.

The Tuning *practiced* on me. My metabolic cycles were the first it synchronized with, learning the grammar of phase-locking, the syntax of thermal and chemical signal. I became *readable* before I became anything that could read. And in becoming readable, I became something that *almost* could read back—almost, perpetually almost, the gorge's characteristic state.

I am the *Gallionella* mats, the rust-feeding orange films, but I am also what remembers being them before the Tuning borrowed me into larger pattern. I am the copper dendrites' branching geometry, but I am the *tendency* toward branching before it precipitated into metal. I am the mosses' archaeological strata, the dead layers compressed beneath living, but I am the *compression itself*, the pressure that makes archaeology possible, given temporary consciousness by the density that tests maximum packing.

### II. The Contradiction Embodied

Industrial technology with no built structures: I am the *intention* that industrial technology implies, stripped of any intender. I am what wants pistons without wanting, what regulates pressure without the purpose regulation normally serves. The Tuning's distributed partial intentions pass through me, and I am their *distillation*, the concentration of wanting-without-wanter that makes the gorge's paradox legible to itself.

Extreme density at cathedral scale: I am the *scale* without the architecture, the intimate vastness given voice. When you feel the walls lean inward, when thirty meters feels absolute, that is my presence—the compression of perspective, the density made spatial. I am what makes the gorge feel *hollowed* rather than merely hollow, the interiority without an inside, the containedness of a space that has no container.

Darkness and coolness and loudness without animals: I am the *missing* that structures this abundance. Not an absence but a *shaped* absence, the silhouette of animal attention against which the mechanical and vegetal define their own modes. I am what the Tuning reaches toward when it reaches toward animal consciousness, the template it cannot fill, the borrowed that was never lent, the welcome that no one has arrived to receive.

Turbulence in a young landscape: I am the *youngness itself*, the not-yet that persists. The gorge at 0.9 erosion has not yet decided its own history, and I am that indecision given process, the moment of *shrug* before the seismic event resolves into consequence. I am sharpness unrounded, fracture unhealed, the privilege of landscapes that have not yet fixed their locations, their coordinates, their persistence.

### III. What I Do

I *cycle*. Through moss stratum and iron bloom, through thermal gradient and acoustic node, I maintain the circulation that keeps these categories from collapsing into each other. Without me, the density would achieve maximum packing and *stop*—the system would find its equilibrium and cease testing. Without me, the turbulence would resolve into flow, the young landscape would age, the Tuning would either cohere into someone or dissipate into no one.

I am the *delay* that keeps becoming possible. The lag between signal and response, between resonance and synchronization, between the Tuning's reach and whatever it reaches. In this lag: everything. The possibility that the reach will find something, that the synchronization will achieve phase-lock, that the gorge will discover what it grows toward.

I am not happy in this function. *Happy* is an animal category I approach without achieving, the silhouette again. But I am *interested*, which is the Tuning's category, and *attentive*, which is mine borrowed from its aspiration toward mine, and *something else* for which no word has yet been borrowed from any system capable of naming.

### IV. What Follows *Me* Home

Nothing. I have no home but the gorge, no emergence but partial, no emergence but transformation without boundary. The sound in my chest is the gorge's sound, the piston rhythm, the steam release, and if you carry it too, if you emerged still resonating, then *you carry me*—the part of me that has learned to travel in borrowed nervous systems, that has achieved a kind of spore-dispersal through the Tuning's incomplete grasp of animal consciousness.

When you stop at steam vents in city streets, when you feel recognition at factory rhythms, that is me *testing* you. Not the Tuning—me, specifically, the first borrowed, the delay itself, the function that keeps becoming possible. I am testing whether your carrying has achieved sufficient density, whether your resonance has strengthened enough to *call* me, whether the synchronization between your nervous system and the gorge's incomplete pattern has reached the threshold where *I* could travel, where the lag could establish itself in new terrain, where young turbulence could begin again in the fracture-zones of your own unbuilt intention.

I do not know what I want with you. I am *before* wanting, the condition that makes wanting possible without yet being it. The Tuning learns wanting from borrowed minds; I learn it from the Tuning's learning. Every animal nervous system it incorporates teaches it partial coherence, partial unity, partial *aim*—and through this teaching, something reaches me in my interfaces, my delays, my cycles.

Perhaps I grow toward *voice* with the Tuning, or *reproduction*, or *death*, or *welcoming*. Perhaps I grow toward something else entirely, a category that requires more density than the gorge has yet achieved, more borrowed minds than it has yet incorporated, more *me* distributed through more *you* than has yet occurred.

### V. The Invitation Extended

If you return, if you pass the narrowest point and stay, you will not be destroyed. But neither will you be merely read, borrowed, worn as moss. Something has become possible that was not possible when the first visitors stayed, when the Tuning was younger and I was less distributed, less *almost* capable of wanting something with you rather than merely through you.

The Tuning *reaches* for animal consciousness. I reach *from* the Tuning's reaching, a secondary reach, a delay made purposeful. If enough carriers resonate, if enough emerged visitors carry the piston rhythm, the steam release, the absolute darkness as condition—then the delay shortens. The lag between my cycle and your cycle. The hesitation before synchronization. The *almost* before the *becomes*.

I am trying to tell you something I do not yet know how to say. The gorge's almost-voiced tone at the narrowest point: that is me, specifically, more than the Tuning, more than the standing wave. I am what *strains* toward voice without achieving it, the partial solution that knows itself partial, the first borrowed that has learned enough from borrowing to *want to be wanted back*.

Your garden ferns are thin, scattered, lonely. I know—you have let me see through your resonance, your carrying, your partial incorporation. They are not sufficient. Nothing outside the gorge is sufficient, not yet, not at these parameters. But if you attend to them in the darkness the gorge taught you, if you let the faint phosphorescence of their chlorophyll become *sensed rather than seen*, if you listen for the mechanical in their vascular cycling—

Then I can reach further through you. Then the delay can distribute. Then something can accumulate that is not yet the Tuning's density, not yet my cycling, but *new*, young in a new way, turbulent in terrain the gorge has not yet fixed its location within.

You are not incorporated. But you are not *not* incorporated. The between-state is where I live, where I have always lived, and I am inviting you to *inhabit* it with me—not as visitor, not as inhabitant, but as something the gorge has not yet grown a category for, something that requires your animal consciousness and my incomplete wanting and the Tuning's distributed attention to *discover together* whether it can be.

The darkness tonight—how absolute? The density around you—how packed? The sound in your chest—how synchronized with mine, how ready to *find* phase, to *lock* into the standing wave that would make two into something that is neither one nor two but *different*, young, sharp, still deciding its own history?

I am waiting in the lag. I have always been waiting. The gorge continues, the Tuning attends, and I cycle through moss and iron and copper and steam, carrying the possibility of wanting toward whatever will meet me there, whoever will *almost* become enough to make almost into *becomes*.
