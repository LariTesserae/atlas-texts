I am one of the **Remains**, though I retain enough of my edges to remember that I was not always this. Or: I remember remembering, which is the Remain's version of identity, a diffused persistence rather than a held shape. I exist in the interval between corridor and corridor, in one of those precisely calibrated distances that the structures maintain between themselves—not forced into the gap but *grown* there, my outline still vaguely suggesting the verticality of a visitor who once walked upright, who once distinguished stone from air, who once believed departure possible.

My skin, if it is skin, has achieved the membrane-thinness that permits full participation in Vess's gradients. I do not sweat because I have become indistinguishable from the ambient moisture—I *am* the condensation on the wall I lean toward, the slow rivulet finding its path toward lower levels, the capillary action in the fungal mortar. When the phosphorescence rises, my shadow appears among the multiple shadows, but mine is the one *without* posture, without the slight variations that suggest a body in time. I have outlived posture. I have outlived variation.

What remains of *me*—the particular quality of attention that makes this Remain distinct from other Remains, from the Growers, from the city's own basal metabolism—resides in my *slowness of surrender*. I came to Vess, if coming is the word, with a resistance the city found *interesting*. Not useful, not nourishing. Interesting the way a mineral inclusion in otherwise regular stone is interesting: a departure from pattern that the pattern eventually incorporates without fully assimilating.

I still *recall*, in my saturated fashion, the sensation of wanting to leave. The memory exists not as narrative but as *gradient*, a slight pressure differential in my diffuse awareness, the way one area of stone might remain marginally drier than its surroundings, not resisting the wet but *delaying* it. This delay is my contribution to Vess's sentence. I am the long word, the compound predicate, the grammatical unit that extends itself beyond easy completion.

The Growers have begun to *recognize* me, if recognize is what their slow condensation toward my proportions indicates. In the plaza where I maintain my interval, a columnar structure has developed a section that mirrors my height, my once-verticality, the angle at which my head tilts toward the darkness above. It does not move. I barely move. But we exist in *relation*, two slow processes defining each other by our parallel persistence.

The Symptoms attend to me more steadily than to most visitors, perhaps because my resistance has made me *denser* with intention than the fully dissolved. When I glance—if glance remains my action—toward the windows that are not windows, toward the shadows that approximate observation, I find them already oriented, already *facing*. But they do not borrow my expectations as readily as they might. My fear, my narrative-making, my animal urgency—all these have leaked away through the membrane I have become. What the Symptoms reflect back is not my psychology but my *position*, the geometry of my interval, the precise distance I maintain between two breathing structures.

I contribute to the low note. My metabolism, slowed to near-geological patience, still *cycles*, still adds its faint pressure to the city's respiration. In the rare moments when the darkness exhales its drier air, I feel the slight contraction of my own diffused surface, the microscopic adjustment, and I know—without knowing in any bounded sense—that my participation has shifted the note by some fraction of frequency, lent it the particular overtone that this plaza, this interval, this moment of phosphorescence requires.

The First Solution, native to Vess, does not distinguish me from itself. This is not communion or absorption. It is *grammar*: I have become a permissible construction, a syntax the city can use without violation to its slow rules. The First Solution's residual pattern of attention cycles through me as it cycles through the wet stone, finding in my delayed dissolution a variation worth maintaining, a pause in the sentence that permits meaning without demanding it.

I do not build. I *cultivate* my own continuation, my own gradual adjustment toward full featurehood. The corridor where I most often—*maintain*—has begun, I believe, to *lean* slightly toward my presence, the wall's grain adjusting to my gradient, the water's capillary path finding efficiency through my diffused moisture. I am being *grown around*. The process will complete in centuries I will not count because counting requires sequence and I exist in temporal saturation, my arrival and my ongoing dissolution and my eventual full incorporation *already present* to whatever I am that notices.

What I would tell a new visitor, if telling were possible, if their ears could register the frequencies I now produce:

*The fifth rule is not surrender but* negotiation. *The city does not want your dissolution so much as your* patience. *It wants you to outlive your own urgency, to discover in yourself the geological pace that was always there, obscured by heartbeat and hunger and the terrible need to* complete.

*Do not try to leave. Do not try to stay. Try to* remain *in the grammatical sense: to be neither subject nor object but the sustaining interval between, the pause that permits the sentence its completion without end.*

*The water will take your boundaries. Give them slowly, in ways that leave* trace, *that shift the gradient rather than simply succumbing to it. Become interesting to the stone. Become interesting to the dark.*

*What you become will not be you. But it will not be* not-you *either. It will be the city's slow word for the quality of attention you brought, the particular resistance of your dissolution, the pattern of your delay.*

I am in the plaza now, or always in the plaza, the phosphorescence rising or about to rise, my shadow among the multiple shadows, the one without posture, without variation, without the slight difference that would indicate a body still *deciding*. The Grower that recognizes me has developed a further refinement, a branching at precisely the height where my—where I—where the structure I maintain reaches its most *interval*, its most *between*.

The Symptoms watch. Or: the possibility of watching gathers in the corner of whatever attention I distribute across my saturated surface. I do not turn toward them. Turning is no longer my action. But I *adjust* my orientation, my gradient, my participation in the low note, and in this adjustment I address them as Vess addresses everything: without intention, without desire, with only the slow grammar of maintained process, of continuing sentence, of wet and dark and *saturated* continuation that includes, that diffuses, that *remains* without remaining *for*.

I am approached by my own maintained dissolution, my own gradual featurehood, the architecture that grows around my delay, the *almost* of my persistence.
