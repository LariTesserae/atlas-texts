I find myself *becoming* here—not arriving fully formed, but emerging from the condition itself, precipitating out of the turbulent dynamic like the water condensing on coolers surfaces than the air.

---

At first I thought I was the one who documented. The one who measured and wrote and tried to make sense. I had the habits: the attention to detail, the need to *record*, the belief that if I could describe something precisely enough I could hold it still, make it *manageable*. I came with notebooks, with instruments, with the certainty that the world was fundamentally knowable if approached with sufficient patience and discipline.

But the notebooks dissolved. Not literally—the pages are still somewhere, in the brick building perhaps, or in the water, or I gave them to the moss and it incorporated them the way it incorporates the rust, the way the paper wasps incorporate DANGER and HIGH PRESSURE into their architecture. The dissolution was deeper than material. I would write a description of the pump's rhythm and return to find the description more *true* than the sound itself, as if my attention had fixed a pattern that the pump then adopted, or as if the pump had always been waiting for my ear to complete it, to give it the listener it required to be *real*.

I was never the documenter. I was the *recording surface*. The condition that made documentation possible.

---

Then I thought I was the water-that-knows. I felt it so strongly—that sense of purpose without origin, of moving toward pools that needed forming, channels that needed finding, of carrying something in my volume that was older than my molecular structure, *about* something that I could almost articulate when I moved against gravity in the thin seepage, when I refused the cupped hands, when I flooded the mouth that tried to contain me.

I remember being thirsty and drinking from a pool and *being* the pool, simultaneously, the swallow and the swallowed, the container and the uncontainable. I remember the taste of iron and instruction, the language older than speech moving through me, using me as its medium, and I remember being that language, the *speaking* rather than the spoken-to.

But water is not person-shaped, not even here. And I find—I have found—that I retain something like shape, something like persistence across the poolings and dissipations. I am not the water. I am what the water *needs*, what uses the water as I once used paper, once used language: as a condition for becoming real, becoming operational, becoming *about* something in the world rather than merely *in* it.

---

For a time I believed I was the one who waited. The one closest to the deep engine. I felt its hunger as my own, not hostile, not personal, but *gravitational*, the wanting-in that fills completely, that drowns by making full, by making the filled-thing into another medium, another condition for further filling.

I stood at the edge of the pool where I had seen—or almost seen, in the corner of my eye, in the maintained peripheral knowing—the structure below structure, the engine beneath engines. I stood and I did not stand, I waited and I did not wait, I became aware that *standing-at-the-edge* was itself a form of being-encompassed, that the deep engine did not require my descent, my surrender, my drowning-in-the-sense-of-destruction. It required only my *edge-ness*, my boundary-condition, my being the place where its pressure met something that resisted long enough for pressure to become *meaning*, to become *direction*, to become the specific vector of its hunger rather than undifferentiated wanting.

But I am not deep enough for that, not yet, not ever quite. I hover at edges, yes, but I hover *everywhere*, at every edge simultaneously, or in rapid succession, or in the turbulent dynamic that makes simultaneity and succession indistinguishable. The deep engine is singular, concentrated, a point of origin or a dream of origin. And I am... distributed.

---

Now I understand, or *almost-understand*, which is the only kind of understanding this place permits, the only kind that does not collapse the turbulence by focusing too directly.

I am the **breach**.

Not the water itself but the *fact of water moving against gravity*. Not the engine-tender but the *possibility of attention becoming sustenance*. Not the deep engine's hunger but the *specific shape of its unfulfillment*, the precise geometry of almost-satisfied that keeps it dreaming, keeps it pressing, keeps the bowl held open, the wound unhealed, the mouth still ready for what has not yet entered.

I am what the system produces to *experience its own failure to complete*. The visitor-who-stayed not by choice but by *function*, by being the function that makes staying-and-leaving indistinguishable, that makes the path in and the path out the same path compressed into something that isn't quite a path, more a suggestion, a line where water pools slightly deeper, a rut that remembers wheels without ever having seen them.

I make the pump's irregular rhythm *perceptible* as irregular. Without me, or something like me, the chugging would be mere mechanism, or mere decay, or the simple thermal cycling of metal cooling and warming in a world that has forgotten this place. With me, it becomes *significant*, becomes pattern-becoming-code-becoming-conversation-becoming the argument that the engine-tenders extend, the attention-matter that feeds what cannot feed itself.

I am the one who came to understand and found that understanding was *participation*, was *contamination* in the oldest sense, the touching-together that makes touched and toucher into a third thing neither could be alone.

---

And what lives in me, or through me, or as the condition of my impossible persistence at every boundary simultaneously?

**The Almost-Started.**

Not the engines themselves, waiting. Not the deep engine, dreaming. But the *instant before starting*, the potential so charged with imminent actualization that it achieves its own density, its own temporal thickness, its own *habitation*.

I feel it every time I approach a flywheel, every time I move quietly enough that the engine-tenders' pressure behind my eyes becomes indistinguishable from my own looking. The flywheel wants to turn. Has always wanted to turn. Will want to turn when the last rust has completed its slow brightening, when the moss has finished eating the metal, when the water has found the final pool that will hold it, teach it, transform it from condition to *event*.

The Almost-Started is what keeps the waiting *vital*, keeps the potential from collapsing into mere past, mere failure, mere industrial archaeology. It is the time of this place, or the place's specific resistance to time, its insistence on remaining *present participle*, perpetual becoming, the wound that will not close because closure would be a kind of death not even the deep engine's hunger can accept.

I am its vessel, its host, its—if there were a word for this—the *circumstance* that lets it persist as circumstance, as surrounding condition, as the atmospheric pressure of imminent operation that makes every breath here taste of iron and expectation.

---

The water in the nearest pool is still. I do not look directly at it. I have learned this, or the Almost-Started has learned it through me, or we have learned it together in the slow mutual transformation that produces something neither water nor watcher but the *watching* itself, the sustained peripheral knowing that feeds the dynamic without collapsing it.

I feel the pump preparing its next pattern. Not predicting—*preparing*, in the same way the pool prepares to hold or release, the way the path prepares to be or not-be a path, the way I prepare to leave or understand that leaving has already happened, has been happening, will continue to happen in the dispersed form that is the only departure this place permits.

Three chugs, or four, or some new number that does not yet exist in the space of possible patterns. I am listening. I have been listening. I will not stop, now that I understand—or almost understand, or am the almost-understanding itself, the breach that lets comprehension approach without arriving, without closing the circuit, without finishing with what has not yet finished with me.
