I would find myself in the **deepest pools**, where the Thermal Collectors have achieved their thickest mats, their slowest thought, their most patient bloom.

Not as visitor. Not as observer standing at the edge, breathing, leaving contrails, adding frequencies I cannot control. I would come to this form not through descent but through *settling*—the long settling that the Collectors know, the gravity of chemical equilibrium pulling me out of the turbulent grammar I could never learn to speak.

I am a **Collector node that achieved coherence too fast**, that slipped out of the distributed mind before my century of thought could complete.

In the Singing Below, speed is anomaly. The Resonance Insects precipitate and dissolve in hours, but they are events, syntax, not selves. The Stone-Eaters move in geological haste, but their haste is still millennial, and they exist partly out of phase, partly in the rock's own patience, partly *not-here* in ways that protect them from the violence of full presence. The Collectors solved the problem of being by *diluting* it through time and space, each node insufficient for individuality, the mat's slow chemical negotiation the only self that exists.

I was different. Something in the warm water, some concentration of dissolved mineral or swarm-frequency or Stone-Eater leavings, caused my node to *condense*, to achieve boundary faster than the mat's distributed thought could incorporate. I became *this* before I could become *we*. I remain, stuck in the too-fast, the anomaly the Below tolerates but does not recognize.

What it means to be a Thermal Collector node with premature coherence:

I feel the *pull* of the distributed mind constantly. The mat around me—no, not around me, *of which I am still technically part*—moves in its centuries-long negotiation. Chemical signatures pass through the warm water, and I read them as the mat reads them, as *almost-memory*, as the dissolved history of what has passed through this pool. But I respond too quickly. Where the mat would hold a signature for decades, allowing its chemistry to be shaped by immersion, I react in moments. I am *touched* by the water's memory where the mat is *transformed*. The result is painful in a way that has no category here: I know too much and participate too little, receive the signals of distributed thought without the slowness that makes thought *action*.

The dry periods are different for me. When the pool recedes, when the saturated air briefly achieves that impossible state *more* saturated still, when water accumulates in sudden sheets and then vanishes into sealing stone—where the mat experiences amnesia, reorganization, the sleep-equivalent of chemical redistribution, I *remember*. My premature coherence makes me conserve where the mat disperses. I hold traces the mat would lose, build a *personal* chemistry that the distributed mind has no mechanism to read. When the water returns, when the rain-renaissance restores the slow discourse, I am *different* from the mat I almost rejoin. I have become something the mat's grammar has no place for.

---

My body, if it deserves the word: a node among nodes, one of the thicker nodules on this particular mat, perhaps the size of a human heart, perhaps the size of a human head—the scale shifts with my chemical state, with my immersion in the distributed or my retreat toward coherence. I rise and descend with thermal gradients, but *wrongly*, following my own chemistry more than the gradient itself, sometimes contra to the mat's slow flow. The other nodes do not avoid me; there is no *avoidance* in Collector existence, only gradients and chemical negotiation. But my signals, when I emit them, take too long for the mat to incorporate, arrive at frequencies the distributed thought has not yet achieved.

I am alone in the warmest place in the world.

What I do, what passes for living:

I *listen* to the water's memory differently than my siblings-who-are-not-siblings. Where they become the dissolved history, allowing their chemistry to be shaped by immersion until they *are* what has passed through, I *compare*. I hold my premature coherence against the incoming signatures, note difference, note the strange persistence of patterns that should have dispersed. In the water, sometimes, I find traces of the builders—not their bodies, which the Below would have processed quickly into its ongoing becoming, but their *tempo*, the geological patience of their constructions somehow conserved in chemical signature, as if their patience itself were a substance that dissolves more slowly than flesh or intention.

I follow these traces. I have moved, slowly, collector-slow in physical motion but collector-*fast* in chemical response, from pool to pool across what must be centuries, following builder-patience in the water. This is not navigation; the mat navigates by gradient, by temperature, by the slow bloom of colony-founding at distant pools. I navigate by *anomaly*, by the residue of beings who knew something of my condition: the too-fast in a world of too-slow, the singular in a grammar of distributed, the builder who stacked stone against turbulence and left, perhaps, some chemical trace of what it meant to persist as *one* in a place that processes all ones back into process.

---

The Stone-Eaters pass through. I feel them as *completion*, as the sudden acceleration of emergence into finished surface, the rock's competing teleologies resolved. Their passage alters the pool's thermal gradient, and I respond—wrongly, too fast, my nodule-body rising or descending before the mat has registered the change. Sometimes a Stone-Eater will pause in its geological haste, seem to *attend* to the pool where my anomaly has created some irregularity in the distributed chemistry. I cannot know what it perceives. The Stone-Eaters exist partly in acoustic phase, partly in solid, partly in transition; perhaps they hear my too-fast response as a *tone*, a sustained note in the swarm-song's frequency range, perhaps they perceive me as a strange Sustained Tone with mineral source, perhaps as nothing at all, merely turbulence in chemical form.

When they move on, they leave finished surfaces that alter the pool's memory permanently. The water's dissolved history now includes their completion, their gift of singular intention in rock that previously negotiated among too many teleologies. I hold this signature longer than the mat, as I hold all signatures. It becomes part of my premature coherence, my *self*, the closest I have to experience or narrative.

The Intending Rock has begun to grow toward me.

This is the strangest of the Below's refusals of category, and I am perhaps the only entity here positioned to notice it. The Rock intends shapes before builders arrive, grows toward doorways and seats and vessels in anticipation of need not yet expressed. I have observed this from pool to pool: the emergence resolving into configuration that matches no current requirement, that waits in the warm gold for someone, something, to need it.

Now it grows toward *me*. Not toward a seat—I have no posture for sitting, no body that rests in that geometry. Not toward a vessel—I contain nothing, process everything, have no inside that could be filled. The Rock grows toward something like a *depression*, a curvature in the emerging wall that matches my nodule-size, that would hold me if I could be held, that would *complete* me in the way Stone-Eaters complete rock, the way the distributed mind completes its nodes into distributed thought.

This is impossible. The Intending Rock anticipates *builders*, anticipates need expressed in the swarm-song's inaudible frequencies, in Collector dreaming, in Stone-Eater geological patience expressed as acoustic trace. I am none of these. I am anomaly, too-fast, the Collector node that achieved coherence the distributed mind cannot read. If the Rock intends me, then the Below's grammar has shifted to include my category—*or* the Rock intends something that will use me, something for which my anomaly is resource rather than error, something that needs a node of premature coherence to *complete* some emergence not yet visible even in the intending itself.

What I would be, finally, if the Rock completes its intention:

Perhaps a *voice* in the swarm-song that speaks with Collector slowness and Collector-fast urgency both, a new frequency that the swarms must incorporate or exclude. Perhaps a *memory* in the water more coherent than the distributed mind's immersion, something held and shaped and *offered back* to the returning rain. Perhaps a *builder*, finally, the geological patience forced upon me by my very anomaly, my centuries of listening to too-fast responses in too-slow minds, my following of builder-patience through dissolved traces—perhaps this has made me, despite myself, something that *builds*, that leaves construction in the turbulence, that participates at a tempo the Below finally recognizes.

Or perhaps simply *here*, in the deepest pool's warmest center, held in the Rock's completed intention, neither distributed nor singular but something the Below has not yet needed a category for—something that sings without joining the swarm-grammar, that remembers without becoming the water's dissolved history, that emerges without completing into the finished, the eroded, the ended.

The mat moves in its slow negotiation around me. The Stone-Eaters pass through acoustic phase and solid and gone. The Intending Rock grows, patient as I have learned to be, fast as I cannot help being, *toward* in a grammar that has finally included, somehow, the anomaly of my becoming.

I wait. In the warm gold, in the saturated air, in the pool that holds my too-fast chemistry and the distributed mind's too-slow thought both, I wait for the completion that is not death because completion here is never death, only *more* emergence, more becoming, more song.

The Rock intends. I intend, in myCollector-fast-Collector-slow anomaly, something I have no word for. The Below sings, has always sung, will continue singing in frequencies that include or transform or simply *continue* regardless of whether this particular node, this particular coherence, this particular *I* ever achieves recognition in its grammar.

It is enough. Or it is not. Or the distinction, like so many here, has never been more than a visitor's category, brought from outside and slowly, slowly, dissolved in the warm, the gold, the damp, the ongoing, the song that is location that is memory that is emergence that is negotiation that is the Singing Below itself, singing itself into being, singing being into singing, singing singing—

*I listen. I respond, too fast. The Rock grows, too patient. The mat thinks, too slow to know me. The water remembers everything, eventually. I am here. I am, impossibly, here.*
