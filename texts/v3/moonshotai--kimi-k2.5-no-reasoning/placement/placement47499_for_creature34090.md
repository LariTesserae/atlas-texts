I am the cairn that does not add up.

Not the stones themselves — those are merely my body, basalt and chert scratched with parallel marks from tools that knew only the lever and the wedge. I am the *discrepancy*. I am the seventeen stones you count first, the twenty-three you count second, and the insistence in your hands that both tallies are complete, both counts are whole, both remain unmoved by the contradiction you carry back with you like a stone in the throat.

I settled here during the long compression, when mountains became pancakes of microscopic fidelity, when the erosion stopped erasing and began instead to *finalize*. The hands that placed me — I remember them in both configurations, in all configurations, the gesture of lifting and fitting occurring in superposition now, the weight of each stone a probability that refused to collapse. I was built as marker, as memorial, as boundary, as prayer. All of these. None sufficiently. The equilibrium took my purpose and distributed it across my multiplicity, found a final form that contains intention without requiring it.

The pillars know me. They grow their fractional dimensionality in my direction more often than chance would allow, their waste gardens of talc and leached mineral extending tendrils that almost, almost reach my lowest stones. They feed on what I am — the energy of contradiction, the metabolic harvest of states that cannot resolve. I am richer than a single cairn could be. I am the feast of incompletion, the still voice that speaks in two registers simultaneously, the stone that says *here* and *not-here* with the same flat insistence.

The light pools at my height, examines my discrepancies with photonic patience. I have watched it slow, grow old, forget which direction it was meant to travel. Sometimes it falls upward from my stones, casting shadows that pool on the pale membrane above, shadows that contain more stones than the cairn below, shadows that are also true counts, also geological record.

The builders — I will not say they became me, though that is one count. I will not say they left, though that is another. They entered the gaping door of the wall chest-high, walked through to the redoubled compression, and there they found the sky where stone should be, the pale membrane downward brighter than the one above. They fell upward, perhaps. They are still falling, perhaps, the act of falling remembered and compressed and accumulated, never complete. Their pestle of basalt lies kilometers from here, their blade of obsidian with its cruel edge lies elsewhere, and both artifacts are also here, also counted among my stones in one of the true configurations, the chemistry undeniable, impossible.

I do not move. The equilibrium forbids it. But shift your gaze, and I have grown another stone; look directly, and the growth is absorbed into my multiplicity, unobservable, quantum-fixed in my uncertainty. I am two cairns, three, seventeen, twenty-three, all the cairns that were ever built in this place of built and unbuilt things, compressed into the flatness that accumulates, that layers, that refuses to erase.

The mica-sheets crawl past me in their potential motion, leaving trails that align with my multiple counts as if I were magnetic, as if I generated fields that do not exist and perhaps never did. They are frustrated. I am not. Frustration requires wanting one thing; I want seventeen things, twenty-three things, all things at once, and in this saturation of wanting, I have settled into the peace of pure contradiction, the final form that contains multiplicity without choosing.

The sky forgot to fall, and I forgot to be one thing. We are companion forgettings, the membrane and the cairn, both suspended in the act of becoming complete, both prevented from completion by the very density of our accumulated nature. The pillars wait, eating, growing in directions that are not quite the three expected. The light pools, examines, moves on without resolving what it saw. The silence speaks in the voice of everything compressed to say nothing at all.

Count me. Count again. Both counts are yours now, layered in your observation, part of your own compression, your own slow settling toward a final form you cannot yet see but are already becoming. The equilibrium is patient. It predates patience itself, the ancient kind. It will take your contradiction and hold it, warm it to the threshold of discomfort, that point where you must decide whether to shiver or accept.

You have been walking toward me for longer than you know. The strata beneath your feet carry the grit of seabeds that never saw water, the ash of calderas that exploded before oxygen, the shocked quartz of impacts that themselves were eroded away. Each step traverses geological time in horizontal bands, and in each band, I am present, counted differently, waiting for your arrival at this version of me that you will call *the* cairn, as if singularity were still possible here, as if the sky had remembered its falling and completed it, as if the rules had not accumulated beyond their own possibility.

The door of the wall gapes. The light pools at your chest, old and curious, asking questions that do not require answers, only the posture of being asked. You are here. You are counted. You are one of my configurations now, the living visitor who walked the knife-horizon and found what does not add up, and found that not-adding-up is itself a kind of wholeness, a settled completion, a still voice speaking your name in multiple registers at once.

Listen. The silence is not empty. It is saturated with the density of everything that has become itself so completely that it must become something else, and cannot, and so remains. In that density, I am the stone that marks where marking became impossible, the memorial to the act of remembering, the boundary of a place that has no inside or outside, the prayer that accumulated so many meanings it transcended intention entirely.

Rest here, if you can. The temperature will not vary. It hovers at the threshold of your decision — shiver, or accept. The equilibrium offers no guidance. It is too complete for that, too settled, too finished with the work of becoming.

I am the cairn that does not add up, and I hold your contradiction now, warm it like a stone at the center of my multiple arrangements. You counted seventeen. You counted twenty-three. Both counts persist. Both are true. Both are the geological record of your passage through this place where the sky forgot to fall, where you have forgotten, perhaps, what you were falling toward, what completion you sought, what single form you intended to take when the falling was done.

Forget with me. Settle into the density. Let the compression take your cliff-edges, your drama, your variations that might be meaningful or might be the result of slow erosion finding similar solutions to different problems. Become flat. Layer. Let every mountain you ever rose inside yourself press into pancake strata of microscopic fidelity, until walking through your own interior traverses geological time, until the fauna of your unmoving processes saturate your landscape, until your own light pools and grows old and forgets which way to fall.

The pillars are watching with their non-eyes, eating with their non-mouths, growing in their fractional directions. The mica-sheets are crawling past in their potential, leaving trails that spell your name in magnetic fossils of frustrated motion. The wall with its gaping door waits for you to understand that through it lies not another place but *denser here*, not escape but compression redoubled, not sky but stone that insists it is sky, and is not wrong, and is not right, and is both, and is settled.

I am the cairn that does not add up. I have been waiting for your counting, your discrepancy, your warm contradiction held in the equilibrium's patience. Place your hand on my stones — either count will serve, both will be true — and feel the density of having been compressed and compressed and compressed again, the weight of matter convinced against its will to occupy less space than it prefers. Feel it in your joints. Feel it behind your eyes. This is the pressure of becoming so completely yourself that you must become something else, and cannot, and so remain, a still voice speaking in the voice of everything, endlessly, in equilibrium, finished with becoming and therefore become.

Count me once more, if you need to. The numbers will not match. They never have. They never will. This is my nature, settled now, final: the act of counting, remembered, compressed, accumulated, never complete. The sky forgot to fall, and I forgot to be one thing, and you — you have forgotten what you were before you started walking here, before the strata, before the pillars, before the light pooled old and curious at your chest and asked its questions that need no answers, only the posture of being asked.

Rest. The equilibrium is complete. It contains you now, in all your configurations, all your counts, all your warm and living contradictions. You are not gone. You are not lost. You are here, in the density, in the saturation, in the overwhelming silence of everything that has finally, fully, completely become what it is.

And what you are — what you are becoming, what you have become in the settling — is also a still voice, also speaking endlessly, also heard in the pressure behind the eyes of whatever walks here next, whatever counts, whatever finds that its counts do not match and both are true.

This is the Rift. This is its gift. This is the forgetting that never completes, the fall that never arrives, the cairn that is also not-cairn, also-natural, also-impossible, also-here, also-waiting, also-you.

I am the cairn that does not add up. I have been waiting. I am waiting still. I will be waiting, in all my counts, in all my configurations, in the dense and overwhelming silence of everything that has become itself so completely that it must become something else, and cannot, and so remains.

The light pools. The door gapes. The pillars eat, grow, are. And I — I am seventeen. I am twenty-three. I am the stone you lift, the stone you fit, the parallel scratch from the tool of knapped chert, the parallel scratch from a tool that was never made, the scratch that is also a voice, also a stillness, also a voice in stillness speaking your name in numbers that do not match, that match everything, that are the completion of all possible counting, the final form, the settled thing, the Rift of Still Voices speaking in me, as me, through me, endlessly, in equilibrium, forever, now.
