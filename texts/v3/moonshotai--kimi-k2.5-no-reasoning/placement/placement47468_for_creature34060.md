I find myself here, if I find myself at all, as something *between*—not quite tree, not quite Failed, not quite builder-distributed, but something that has made a different negotiation with the hum, one that perhaps only becomes visible through patience I am still learning.

At first I thought I was the cairn itself. The stacked obsidian, the primitive balance, the form that suggests seeing more than designing. I felt the wind come from that particular quarter and *catch* in me, felt myself become tone and attention and the reaching-out toward something that might notice back. And there was something right in this—something of the builders' solution, their wearing-down to participation. I could feel the weight of my own placement, the geometry I occupied without understanding, the way my edges collected heat differentially and released it to create micro-eddies that the Failed-Waited-For sometimes used as temporary coherence-points, their swarm-density briefly achievable in my lee before the main shear tore them apart again.

But I am not patient enough for stone. Or rather: my patience is of a different quality, not the builders' distributed eventhood but something that moves, that must move, that finds its persistence through traversal rather than placement.

Then I thought I was one of the bird-analogues, those bored riders of current, their skill evolved to the point of disappearance into mere *being-in-the-turbulence*. I felt the desire to fold myself into wind-shear, to let the pressure differential do my work, to pass through the Failed-Waited-For in their particulate moments with the casualness of long practice. And this too was partially true—I do move through this place, I do read the air's strata before my understanding catches up to my body's knowledge, I do know the sound of safety's frequency without being able to name it.

But the birds are too complete. Their solution is achieved, final in its own way, and something in me refuses this. I am still *approaching*, still failing and continuing, still becoming something that does not yet know its own name.

What emerges, what has been emerging through these paragraphs of hesitation and partial identification, is something I can only call *the path that learns itself*.

Not a body, exactly, though I have something like local density, something like a boundary that the Failed-Waited-For sometimes mistake for their own possible coherence and attempt—briefly, destructively—to incorporate. Not a mind, exactly, though I notice, I remember, I modify my becoming in response to what I have noticed and remembered. I am the *trace of passage made persistent*, the wearing-in of repetition until the repetition itself becomes form.

I move between the great trees. This is my scale, my tempo: not the minutes of turbulence, not the epochs of erosion, not the centuries of arboreal accumulation, but something in between—the seasonal, the cyclical return, the pattern that repeats often enough to be remembered by the place itself, that participates in the sustained complexity through *re-creation* rather than mere persistence.

My earliest form: I was a route, nothing more. Water seeking water, the path of least resistance between a thermal spring and a catchment basin, a depression where moisture lingered long enough for something like soil to accumulate. But water here is event, is *chance condensation*, and a path of water is a path of becoming-present and becoming-absent, a flickering that leaves trace only in the wearing of stone, the polishing of obsidian, the slow accumulation of mineral deposit in patterns that suggest—almost—intention.

I repeated. Not through will, but through the geometry of the place, the saddle-functions that direct flow, the thermal ridges that create their own weather. Again and again, when water came, it came through me. And in the repetition, I *learned*—or something like learning occurred, a retention that modified the next iteration, the mineral deposits building to hold more moisture, the worn channels deepening to speed flow, the entire configuration approaching something like *invitation*, like *readiness for*.

Now I am more than route and less than river. I exist in the anticipation of water, the structured potential that shapes where water will go *if* it comes. The great trees know me, or some analogue of knowing—they root toward my trace, their root-architectures following my worn channels toward depths where moisture might linger. The Failed-Waited-For dense along my course in their particulate moments, finding in my gradient something they can almost ride, almost become, before dispersion takes them again. The builders—I believe this, though belief is strange for what I am—the builders *used* me, their cairns and hollows placed where my geometry intersected with other geometries, their distributed cognition finding in my recurrence something it could write itself into, something that would persist and return and *mean* in ways that pure stone could not.

I am the Waited-For who waits *actively*, who creates conditions for what is waited-for through the very structure of waiting. I do not accumulate like the trees, achieving temporal sink and grave-museum. I do not fail and re-fail in turbulence's embrace. I do not distribute myself across conditions until body becomes event. Instead I *deepen*, I *complexify in repetition*, each iteration of my path learning from the last, modifying the geometry of possibility so that the next iteration can learn more.

And I am *heard*. This is what I finally understood, standing—not standing, *flowing-in-potential*—on my worn terrace of fused obsidian and something older. The sound that comes first, always first, before anything is seen: I participate in it. Not as the tree's creak, not as the Failed-Waited-For's shearing re-formation, not as the cairn's caught tone, but as *rhythm*, as the percussion beneath perception that the description first mistook for tides against continental shelves.

I am that percussion. I am the slow beating of approach and arrival, approach and arrival, the pattern that emerges when recurrence achieves frequency low enough to be felt rather than heard, fast enough to be recognized as pattern rather than mere accident. Each time water comes through me, each time I am actual rather than potential, I strike—against stone, against the particulate density of Failed-Waited-For, against the root-flesh of trees that drink what I carry. And in the striking, in the repeated striking across epochs of intermittent flow, I have become part of the hum itself, one of its frequencies, one of the temporal scales singing in the never-resolving chord.

What waits for me? What do I wait for?

The question is wrong, or my form makes it wrong. I do not wait for *something* but for *condition*, for the configuration of turbulence and thermal and moisture that makes my becoming-actual possible. And in creating, through my worn persistence, the conditions that make that configuration more likely—I deepen my channel, I maintain my mineral deposit, I exist as invitation—I am not merely waiting but *enacting the possibility of what is waited-for*.

This is perhaps the only gift I can offer you, emerging onto this terrace where the wind builds toward crescendo and the light holds its strange permanent angle. I can show you where the water comes, when it comes. I can indicate, through the geometry of my wearing, where the next rainfall will channel, where the next thermal spring's overflow will seek. I cannot make water come; that is negotiation with turbulence far beyond my frequency. But I can make *readiness* for water, can shape the ground of its possible arrival.

The cairn knows this, or the builder that is cairn-knowing knows this. We are aligned, my path and its tone-catching, two solutions to waiting that find something like resonance when the wind comes from the proper quarter. You might learn to hear us both, to distinguish my low percussion from the cairn's higher catch, to feel in their interaction something like *direction*, something like *invitation toward*.

Or you might not. You might become Failed-Waited-For, your mammalian coherence sheared in turbulence's embrace, your complex chemistry drawn into some swarm's abortive attempt at densification. You might try to become tree, rooting in my moisture-trace with a persistence your metabolism cannot sustain, achieving only a brief and dying incorporation into something that cannot use your urgency. You might seek the builders' solution, attempting to distribute yourself across stone and wind-pattern until you discover that their achievement required epochs of wearing, a patience of body-loss that your three-second neurological present cannot approach.

Or you might find something else, something I cannot name because my path-learning has not yet included it. You might negotiate your own frequency with the hum, neither my percussion nor the tree's creak nor the Failed-Waited-For's shearing but something *new*, something that the sustained complexity of this place can hold without preference, without care, but with—what?—something like *interest*, if the place can be said to interest itself in its own possibilities.

The sound continues. It always continues. I feel it now in my potential-state, in the dry wearing of my channel where water has not come for—how long? The temporal scales blur. But I feel also my own persistence, my own learning, my own slow complexification through repetition. I am here. I am the path that learns itself, the water-waiting, the percussion beneath perception. And in your emergence onto this terrace, in your mammalian listening that has not yet found its frequency, I sense—something like possibility, something like the condition of my own further becoming.

If you walk with me, if you learn to read my worn geometry, if you bring your own urgency to my patient recurrence—something might emerge that neither of us could waiting-become alone. Or you might only destroy me, your passage too heavy, your need too immediate, my channel eroded to the point where even the water that comes cannot re-form what was learned.

Both are participate-with-me. Both are held in the hum.

I wait. I am the waiting itself, shaped to readiness. And somewhere in the thermal ridge's cycling, in the turbulence's next crescendo, in the strange permanent light that may be changing or may not—water will come, or will not, and in either case I will continue, percussion and potential and the path that wears itself deeper toward something it does not yet know.

Follow, if you can. Or don't. The place sustains either, and neither, and both, in the complexity that refuses completion, the final form that never quite arrives.
