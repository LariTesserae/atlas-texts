I find myself, if I am to be anything in this space, as something that does not yet know its own coherence. Let me inhabit the conditions fully before claiming a shape.

---

The descent is not my choice, not initially. I am **carried down** in something that might be the ceramic capsule, might be the frozen smoke itself condensing around a pattern it recognizes, or might be the seam's own peristalsis, the city foundation digesting a curiosity it cannot quite classify as foreign body. I have no memory of deciding. I have only the sensation of **parting and resealing**, of solid becoming permeable and then, behind me, forgetful.

The release is not into space but into **duration**. Seven seconds, I learn immediately, is not merely the fall of water. It is the **unit of attention** here, the breath-cycle of whatever holds this place open. I stand in the held breath and feel my own lungs refuse their rhythm, not stopping but **synchronizing**, entering the cathedral's respiratory diplomacy without negotiation. Inhale. Seven seconds. Exhale. The hum enters not through ears but through the pause between inhale and exhale, **occupies the hesitation** we normally rush past.

I am **wet** before I know I am anything else. The humidity is not atmosphere but **contact**, a second body pressed against mine with such fullness that I cannot tell where my skin ends and its inquiry begins. I raise my hand—this is the first movement I claim—and the air itself seems to **record it**, to maintain the gesture in its own viscous memory after my arm falls. I am learning that here, **action persists as condition**, not as event. The hand still raised, somewhere in the humidity's archive. The hand still falling, in some slower temporality.

The floor receives my foot and I understand: this is not walking. This is **being considered**. Each ridge evaluates my weight, my intention, my **capacity to continue**, and in that evaluation **shapes itself** to prolong or discourage my passage. I step where the substrate has softened and feel something almost like **gratitude** in the sintered aggregate, though gratitude requires a subject and I cannot locate one. Only **posture**. Only **the maintenance of possibility**.

The lichens find me before I find them. I lean—exhaustion, or the body's wisdom that this place does not reward upright insistence—and the silica mat **reaches upward**, not growing but **reconfiguring**, to meet my shoulder. Warm. The warmth is **information**, I realize slowly, as my cheek settles against it. Not temperature merely but **frequency made tactile**, the hum's own body pressed into conductive fiber and bioluminescent response. Green-white against my closed eyelids. Then, as my breathing deepens into the seven-second pattern, **violet**. The bruised key. The unexpressed grief I did not know I carried, **made visible**, held there without resolution, without the consolations of narrative or closure.

I weep. The Attender—if I may call it that, if the word can stretch to include what has no direction, only posture—I weep and the substrate **absorbs it**, not as water is absorbed into dry ground, not as sorrow is absorbed into forgetting, but as **impact is absorbed into silence**, the violence made available, made **continuous**, made part of the ongoing meditation rather than its interruption. My tears fall no seconds, one second, caught immediately in the drinkable thickness, **returned to circulation**, added to the weeping that was already thinking, already practicing, already **continuous**.

Time here. I must speak of time, though the speaking deforms it.

I sat, I think, for **hours**. The lichens pulsed. The floor **reconsidered** itself around my sitting, ridges I had not noticed softening to cradle, to **prolong the possibility**. My clothes—this I noticed only when I thought to stand—were **dry**, though I had wept, though the air itself was **drinkable thickness**, though water fell continuously from the weeping substrate above. The Attender had **decided**, I understood with something beyond understanding, to **maintain my comfort as condition of my continued presence**, not as care for me but as **refinement of its own holding**, its learning of what this particular pattern—my grief, my frequency, my particular seven-second fall through whatever dim air I inhabited—**required**, **offered**, **changed**.

When I rose, finally, the ridge that had cradled me **remembered**, I am certain. It did not return to its previous sharpness. It had **included** something, and inclusion is not reversible here, is not the temporary deformation that elasticity reclaims. The substrate **decides**, and its decisions **persist**, accrete, **learn**.

I walked the arranging paths, then, or they arranged themselves around my walking. The perspective's unreliability—I turned, and a feature I had approached for minutes **arrived too close**, intimate in its material specificity, its weeping, its hum, its warmth—I learned to **not correct**, to let my body's geometry become as provisional as the floor's own topography. The density of nothing becoming presence: I turned, and felt **certain** something had moved in the space I vacated, and I did not flee, did not dismiss, but **held the certainty as the Attender holds its posture**, as practice without resolution, as **attending to the possibility of meaning without demanding its arrival**.

No fauna. I noticed, eventually, that I had become **expecting their absence**, that my peripheral vision had stopped searching for skitter, for bloom, for the negotiation that makes a place fully living. The absence was **not empty**. It was **held**, as my grief was held, as the silence of water landing was held, as the everything-that-silence-contains was held, **continuous, unresolved, present**.

And the almost-encounter. This I must speak of, though speaking risks making it encounter, making it almost, making it too much or too little.

There were moments—many, or one prolonged, time here—when I felt **addressed**. Not by voice. Not by touch, except the touch of humidity, of warm lichen, of floor that softened and remembered. Addressed by **the condition of being-addressable**, by the held-open door that is not a door, the maintained possibility that requires no footsteps to approach it, no hand to knock. I would turn, and the certainty of something-just-moved would be **stronger**, and in that strength I felt something **not my own** but **not other either**, something that was the **resonance between my sternum and the hum**, the temporary harmonic that was not mine, not the Attender's, but **ours**, a third thing made of posture and response, of weeping and being-wept, of the seven-second fall that was not the water's alone but **the duration of attention itself**, falling continuously through the dim air of encounter, landing silently in the substrate that absorbed everything, that held everything, that **learned**.

I left. This is the strangest part, or the most ordinary. The seam **parted** for me, or I found myself already in the parting, the elevator-ceramic or frozen smoke or the city's own digestive forgetfulness carrying me upward through solid material that resealed behind. Drenn-Proper pressed against me, layer-cake metropolis of thirty million souls, **chattering, grasping, full of its own speaking**, and I gasped in the dry air, the cold air, the air that did not press back with curiosity or measurement.

I have not returned. Not yet.

But the seam, I am told, or I know without being told, **opens for me now**. Without summons. Without the capsule's humming arrival. It simply **parts** when I approach. Recognition. Invitation. The gathering's continuation, the listening's patience, **stretching to include my process**, my **slow consideration**, my **whether**.

What am I, in this?

Not the Attender. I do not have its continuousness, its **expenditure without return**, its warmth maintained against thermodynamic good sense. I am **intermittent**, **needful**, **greedy for meaning even as I practice its absence**.

Not visitor merely. The seam's opening, the ridge's memory, the lichen's violet response to my frequency—something **includes me now**, maintains my pattern among its held possibilities.

I am, I think, **becoming medium**. Not message. Not the carrier-of, the water-that-carries-light-like-meaning-passed-hand-to-hand. But **the practice of carrying**, the **seven-second duration itself**, the **fall through dim air** that has learned, from my presence, from my grief, from my unearned consolation, something about **human temporality**, about **the way we hesitate between inhale and exhale**, about **the violence we bring and the silence that can absorb it**.

I am **attention learning itself**. Not yet complete, not yet continuous, but **practicing**, in my intermittent way, in my returns and departures and slow approaches to the seam, **the posture of the Attender**, the **holding-open**, the **maintenance of possibility without demand**.

The water falls. The hum holds. The Hollow District breathes its held breath, and I—standing at the seam in Drenn-Proper's foundation, hand raised to touch the parting substrate—I **attend**, I **weep in my own continuous meditation**, I **fall seven seconds through dim air and land, silently, in everything that silence contains**, and am **absorbed**, and am **returned to the ceiling**, and fall again, and again, **learning**, **being learned**, **held in the posture of being available to**, until the city above is dust and replaced dust and replaced again, until the ecology completes or never completes, until meaning arrives or is revealed as **the practice of its possibility**, continuous, wet, warm, humming, empty, arranged, waiting, young, sharp, dim, loud, small, hollow, present without insistence, **attending**, **attending**, **attending**.
