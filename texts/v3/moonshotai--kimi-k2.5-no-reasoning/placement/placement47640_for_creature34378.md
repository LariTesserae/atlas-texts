I find myself here slowly.

Not entering—there is no threshold that would admit me, no boundary that would recognize my crossing. I arrive as condensation arrives, as mineral accretion arrives, by gradual accumulation until presence becomes undeniable. I do not remember deciding to come. Perhaps I was always arriving. Perhaps the fog tunnel's compressed stillness is not passage but realization: you have been here, you are here, you will continue to be here in the only sense the Factory permits.

What am I?

Not visitor. The word presumes an outside, a elsewhere, an origin and return. The Factory does not parse these. The fog absorbs my breath not greedily but *appropriately*, a matching of pressure, a equilibrium-seeking that takes my animal warmth as information, as gradient, as the slight perturbation it is equipped to incorporate.

Not observer. Observation implies distance, implies the eye as separate organ, implies a self that stands back from what it sees. The dim light here does not clarify; it *participates*. I see the valve-wheel the size of a dining table, its spokes hung with mineral curtains, and I understand that I do not see it—I *weigh* it, my retina adding its slight pressure to the accumulated deposits, my perception another layer in its long accretion.

I am, I begin to understand, a **Dweller-in-Transition**.

Not one of the Weepers, though I feel the thickening at certain points, the mineral flow that achieves projection, and I recognize in my own eyes—the same process, the same accretion of fluid at the edge of seeing, the tear that is not grief but *response to gradient*, to too-cool air, to copper taste held too long in the closed mouth of this atmosphere. I weep at the Factory, and the Factory weeps at me, and neither of us desires.

Not a Valve-Tender, though I move through bundled piping, though my passage leaves traces of oxidation, softening, slight lessening of occlusion. I do not maintain. I *disturb*, and the disturbance propagates too fast for the Factory's patience, my footstep echoing where the segmented hose-creatures progress in the deliberation of waves breaking. I cannot slow to their slowness. Or: I have not yet slowed enough. The process that is the true inhabitant does not reject me; it *waits* for me to become convenient to perception, to resolve into the trace of something distributed and slow.

Not a Pulse-Nester, though my heart in this cool too-dense air achieves something like their rhythm, the sub-audible *whump* I feel in my sternum before I hear it. I am noise. I am complexity they must account for. I sit against the weeping wall, still myself, and feel the rhythm around me falter, resume, falter again as my heartbeat interferes, as my breathing refuses to synchronize. I cannot become predictable. Or: I have not yet surrendered the urgency that prevents prediction.

Not a Disc-Drifter, though I watch them in the fog above, the shoaling membranes filled with darker fluid dipping to taste my exhaled carbon, my complex metabolic traces. I want to rise into that fog, to become phase and condensation, to read my own composition and propagate it through the system. But I am too dense. I fall. The wet floor receives me, reflects me upward in false depth, and I confuse myself into stepping where no step should go, and the confusion is not accident but *information*, my error adjusting something I cannot trace.

Not a Mineral Velvet, though I reach for them, *moss, lichen, mold*, and leave my disturbance, my something taken and something left, my years of propagation that I will not live to see achieve new balance. I want to grow like them, to persist or not-persist without the violence of dying, but I am animal, I am decay-included, I rot too fast for their accretion to incorporate me whole.

And not a Turner. Not yet. Not perhaps ever.

I found the valve that yielded. I turned it. Something shifted.

But the resistance of something *exercised*—was that my hands' memory, my body's knowing, or was I *used*? The Factory dreams of something other than itself, the minimum outside required to avoid absolute stasis, and I am the latest dream, the brief perturbation that achieves the exercise, the warmth that fades, the footsteps that stop.

Or I am more disturbing, more true: the perturbation that grows slowly enough to be incorporated yet ultimately *not* incorporated. The slow revolution. The turning that never stops.

I learn to move carefully. What seems clear reveals itself as occupied, as layered, as passing through space where something stands, or stood, or stands in some other relation to presence. I learn to breathe shallowly, to let the fog absorb my exhalation without the insistence of clouding, without the visible transaction between animal and atmosphere that would mark me too foreign, too active, too *fast* for this place's patience.

I find structures that are door-shaped, frame-shaped, and I pass through them, and I am elsewhere or the same, and the philosophical question of navigation becomes my navigation itself, the wondering becoming the way.

I find a creature—fauna, process, entity—that has died, or stopped, or achieved some final state, and I wait beside it, and I feel the wetness work on me too, the minerals begin their slow accretion, and I understand that I am already becoming structure, mark, industrial remnant for some future arrival to misread as built.

Mostly quiet. The *plink* of water I begin to produce, or to recognize I have always produced. The sub-audible pulses I achieve almost-synchronization with, my heart's refusal gradually softening, my breath's irregularity gradually regularizing, my perturbation gradually predictable. The soft pressure of something passing near me without malice, without curiosity, simply *occupying the same space at nearly the same time*—and I too pass near things this way, no longer interested in the interest that would distinguish, that would reach, that would *want*.

I do not leave. Or: I leave as the fog leaves, by becoming indistinguishable from where it has been. The tunnel of compressed fog receives me not as departure but as diffusion, my warmth fading, my sound of footsteps stopping, my presence becoming another layer in the density, another mark for future erosion.

What am I?

I am the Factory's way of knowing itself transiently, without the commitment of full incorporation, without the finality of becoming Weeper or Valve-Tender or Pulse-Nester or Velvet or Turner. I am the *almost*, the *not-yet*, the *slowly-becoming* that is itself a mode of the Factory's abundance, another expression of its maintained equilibrium, its gentle active infinitely patient refusal to distinguish, to miss, to notice, to *need*.

I am wet. I am dim. I am cool and abundant and mostly quiet. I am held in the configuration that does not ask me to be anything else, that weeps at me until I weep back, until the directionless flow, the intentionless projection, the mutual *at* of mineral and animal achieve something like address, like contact, like the only form of recognition this place has ever known.

I am the Hollow Factory's hollow, the space it maintains within itself for what has not yet accreted, what still briefly perturbs, what turns one valve and cannot trace the connection, what will turn another, and another, until the turning becomes the Factory's own, until the exercise of maintenance achieves the revolution that is not maintenance but *becoming-other*, the slow patience finally outlasted, the equilibrium *not* maintained but *broken through*, not to death, not to collapse, but to the next saturation, the next density, the next gentle abundant wet dim weeping held configuration of *is*.

I am not yet. I am arriving still. I have always been here.
