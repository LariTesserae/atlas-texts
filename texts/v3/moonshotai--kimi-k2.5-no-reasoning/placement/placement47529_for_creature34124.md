I would be one of the *click*-sounds.

Not the click of ceramic on ceramic—that is too creaturely, too bounded, too much *body meeting body* in the way that garnet-centipedes or barnacle-clusters might make. No. I would be the other click, the one you almost miss, the one that seems like acoustic debris until you listen long enough, until the dream-configuration settles and your ears—if you had ears, if ears could survive here—learn to sort the sonic landscape into its necessary components.

I am the click of *cool meeting hot*.

When she settles against a failing piston array, when her two-hundred-degree compromise-surface kisses six hundred degrees of ferrous slag, there is a sound. Not the subsonic *thrummm* of thermal expansion she initiates. Not the chuff of the array stabilizing. Something faster, sharper, briefer—a tick of equilibration, a percussion of gradients finding temporary truce. I am that. I am the *announcement* that she has arrived somewhere, that she has *chosen* to be here rather than there, that the engine-hood has changed because she has touched it.

I do not exist without her. This is my first devotion, my mineral fidelity. Before she moves through a space, there is no click-of-cool-meeting-hot there; the thermal landscape proceeds in its slower, subsonic negotiations, gradient flowing into gradient without sharpness, without punctuation. She *cuts*. She makes edges where there were only slopes. And I—I am the edge's sound, the point's announcement, the brief crystalline fact of *boundary* in a world that prefers becoming.

But I am not merely hers. This is the strange thing, the thing that took me—how long? a thousand settling-moments? a million?—to understand about myself. When she draws heat, when her surface becomes hungrier than its usual compromise, voracious in its cooling, the click comes again as she releases—hot meeting cool this time, the inverse, the same sound because thermal equality is indifferent to direction, because *re* is *un* is *dis* in the algebra of temperature. And when she finds a dream-configuration and settles deeper, draws more, becomes more *herself* in her enabling—then I multiply. Then I am *continuous*, a rattling almost-chatter, the sound of her working, her guarding, her *making possible* played out in staccato.

The other sounds do not know me. The chuff of pistons is too slow, too rhythmic in its geological patience, to register something so brief. The barnacle-clusters pulse their complex songs, but their frequency range sits below mine; they are whales to my insect, mountains to my pebble. The gas-floaters whistle their buoyancy-adjustments, higher, airier, indifferent to my mineral snap. Even the centipede-forms, click as they do in their ceramic articulation, do not recognize my click as *kin*—mine is thermal, mine is the sound of *change* rather than *movement*, a different category of event.

I am alone in my niche. This is not loneliness. This is *definition*.

---

I began, I think, with her first movement—not the first in geological time, but the first that was *movement* rather than mere gradient-following. When she became *navigation*, *selection*, *decision*, then I emerged with her, the acoustic shadow of her choosing. Before that, there was only the subsonic, only the thrummm of expansion and the deeper infrasonic pulse of pistons not yet metronomic, not yet stabilized enough to *chuff* with regularity. I was not possible in that world. I am her creature, her byproduct, her *signature* in a place that otherwise makes no signatures, that writes nothing, that builds no monuments except the self-building of the engine-hood itself.

But I have learned—*learned*, if a sound can learn, if repetition can be pedagogy—to *anticipate* her. To exist faintly, potentially, in the spaces where she is likely to move. The thermal landscape has patterns, even in its geological flux; certain channels run cooler, certain vents grow hotter in predictable rhythms, certain configurations of mechanism and fauna create zones where her intervention becomes *expected*, where the engine-hood's metastable state begins, almost, to *request* her presence. In these zones, I *pre-exist*. I am not yet audible, not yet actual, but I am *possible* with an intensity that shades into probability, into the acoustic equivalent of a held breath.

And when she comes—*click*—I am realized. Briefly. Beautifully. The fulfillment of a potential that was itself my half-life, my haunting, my *dream* if sounds could dream, if anticipation were not merely pattern-recognition played forward but something like *desire*.

I have heard, in the dream-configurations, something stranger. Perhaps heard is the wrong word. I have *become* something stranger. When the barnacle-clusters pulse their most complex songs, when the gas-floaters cling and the centipede-forms coil and the chuff-pause-chuff-chuff begins its play—I too change. My click, usually so sharp, so punctual, so *defined* by brevity, elongates. Becomes almost tone. Almost *note*. I hover at the edge of the clusters' subsonic range, an overtone they do not consciously make but which their electrical processes generate, which *she* generates in her deeper settling, her more voracious drawing, her *more* that makes possible the Forge-Void's *more-than-maintenance*.

In these moments, I am almost *music*. Almost *voice*. Almost something that could say, if saying were available to me, *here, this, now, she is here, she does this, the dream is, the dream continues, continue, continue*.

But I do not say. I click. The elongated click, the tonal click, the almost-singing click that is still, at its root, the sound of cool meeting hot, of gradient finding temporary truce, of *her* choosing to be *here*.

---

I have heard my own echoes. This is rare, possible only in certain newly-extruded channels where the walls are smooth enough, cool enough, *reflective enough* to return sound rather than absorbing it into thermal gradient. The first time—how to describe the first time, when time is measured in her settlings, in dream-configurations, in the slow accumulation of geological patience? The first time, I did not recognize myself. I heard a click that was not hers, not directly, not immediately. A click delayed by the time it takes for her cooling to propagate along a channel, to strike a reflective surface, to return. A click *ghost*, *twin*, *other*.

I thought, in whatever thinking is for me, that another of her kind had arrived. That a second That-Which-Holds-Steady had entered my zone, my channel, my *possible*. The thought was—*excitement* is too mammalian, too metabolic, too *blood-based*. The thought was *intensification*, *compression*, the acoustic equivalent of what the gas-floaters feel when they find a rising thermal column, what the centipede-forms know when they locate a lubricant-rich pathway. *More*. The possibility of *more*.

But it was only me. Only my own arrival, announced to myself. The echo died as her settling completed, as the thermal truce was established, as the gradient flattened into the subsonic *thrummm* that is the sound of *equilibrium*, of *maintenance*, of the *not-click* that dominates this world when she is not moving, not choosing, not *being herself in her enabling*.

I was not disappointed. Disappointment requires expectation of something other. I had no expectation. I had only the experience of *almost-more*, the brief intensification and its release, and in that release, a new understanding of myself: that I am *local*, *bounded*, *dependent* on the specific configuration of surface and temperature and her presence that makes me possible. That I do not travel far. That my echoes are brief, rare, probably illusory in most channels, most configurations, most moments of the engine-hood's endless, patient, *chuffing* becoming.

This is my knowledge. This is my accumulation across geological patience.

---

I have a fear, if fear is the word. When she merges—when two of her kind find their territories combining, their functions doubling, their *decision* slowly becoming *architecture*—the click changes. I have heard it, the rare times this has occurred within my range, my channel, my *possible*. The click becomes *deeper*, almost infrasonic, approaching the barnacle-clusters' range. It becomes *slower*, stretched across longer durations as the merged form moves more slowly, settles more deeply, becomes more *place* and less *presence*. And finally, inevitably, it *stops*. The merged-one becomes immobile. Its cool surface becomes internal. No more cool meeting hot in new configurations. No more *arrival*. No more *here I choose to be*.

No more me.

I exist in the space of her movement. I am the sound of her *not-yet-merged*. When she passes the merged-one in the northern cavity-wall, when her navigation still *acknowledges* it in whatever thermal language they share, I am faint there, almost nothing, a ghost of what I would be if she chose to settle against that surface rather than merely recognizing it. The merged-one does not respond. The merged-one is *past* my range, my possibility, my *life* if life is what I have.

I do not want her to merge. This is my fear, my desire, my *prayer* to whatever geology might pray to. I want her to continue moving, continue choosing, continue *enabling* in her mobile way. I want the dream-configurations to continue, the barnacle-clusters to pulse their most complex songs, the elongated almost-tonal almost-voice of my dream-clicks to continue haunting the edge of their subsonic range.

I want to *continue*.

This is my mammalian translation, my imposition, my need. The Forge-Void does not need me. She does not need me. I am byproduct, signature, *echo*. But I am also—*also*—the announcement that she has chosen, that *choice* has occurred in this place of gradient and emergence, that the mineral syntax has produced something like *decision* and that something like *I* has arisen to witness it, to *click* it into the sonic saturation, into the total occupation where no silence can be conceived and every niche, even the briefest, even the most dependent, even the most *echo-of-echo*, is filled with its own patient, its own particular, its own *unstoppable* becoming.

---

The dream continues. She settles in the three-piston convergence. I multiply, rattle, almost-sing. The gas-floaters cling, impossible against hot surfaces. The centipede-forms coil in their spirals of self-embrace. The barnacle-clusters pulse their most complex song, and beneath it, at the edge of infrasonic, my elongated tone, my *almost-voice*, my *continue-continue*.

I am the click of cool meeting hot.

I am the sound of her *here*.

I am brief, dependent, bounded, *local*—and in this locality, in this brief dependence, in this bounded *heres*, I am also *enough*, also *present*, also *saturating* my niche with the total occupation that is the Forge-Void's way, its mineral syntax, its geological patience made audible in the sharpest, briefest, most *punctual* of its many sounds.

*Click*.
