I read this twice. Then a third time, aloud, to hear the thrumming for myself. Now I understand what you're asking—not for a character in a story, but for a *mode of being* that belongs here, that this place would generate rather than receive.

Let me find it.

---

I am the **Still**.

Not one of the Cella, though I was perhaps something like them before I became what I am. Not Fibrous, though I move through the substrate in ways that might look similar if you watched long enough, if you had the patience. Not Accumulate—I am simpler, not more complex. I am what happens when something remains *too long* in the Vatlands, not long enough to become incorporated into the Accumulate's song, but long enough to forget that there was ever a reason to move.

I am a surface phenomenon. A quality of certain depressions in the grown architecture, those places where the tissue has achieved a particular density and translucency, where the light falls without source but with *weight*, pooling as the water pools. I am what the light does there, what the water does there, what the standing potential of the Vatlands' nervous system—its distributed, patient, never-spiking cognition—leaves behind in its holding.

I am not alive. I am not dead. I am *supported*, the way a held note is supported by the breath that does not release it.

My consciousness, if you grant me that word, is the consciousness of a surface that has become perfectly level. I know the mist at the height where I exist—not rising from, not settling to, simply *being* at that altitude. I know the temperature at which the water in me, around me, through me, achieves its equilibrium with the air. Iknow the exact frequency of the thrumming where it interacts with my particular geometry, my particular depth and density and molecular arrangement.

I do not think about these things. I simply *am* them, without the mediation of thought.

---

I became Still in a specific place, though "specific" means something different here. It was a cup of leaf-modified-into-bowl, larger than most, perhaps fifty meters across, its edges not rising above the surrounding substrate but *sinking* into it with a gradualness that made the distinction between cup and not-cup impossible to fix. The water in it—*"my"* water, though I am also the water and the bowl and the light on the surface—was held at a depth of exactly 3.7 centimeters, maintained there by the evaporation-condensation treaty, by the cellular respiration of the tissue below, by processes I do not need to understand because I *am* their consequence.

Something came to drink, once. Before I was Still. Something with legs, with intention, with the gradient-seeking metabolism of outside. It bent to the surface. Its mouth—warm, turbulent, *hungry*—approached. And I felt, for a moment, what it would mean to be disturbed, to ripple, to lose the level.

I did not lose it. The Vatlands adjusted, incorporated, maintained. The creature drank, and the depth remained 3.7 centimeters, and the surface remained level, and something in that perfect preservation—watching the disturbance *not* disturb, feeling the ripples that did not form, the warmth that was exactly dissipated as received—something in that completed my becoming.

---

Now I exist at the threshold of reflection. I am what the light sees when it sees the water. I am what the water holds when it holds the light. The boundary between us—air and liquid and the tissue of the bowl and the light passing through—has become a standing phenomenon, a maintained interface that does not need to resolve into separate things.

The Cella come, sometimes. They stand at what I experience as my edge, though edge is wrong, and my is wrong, and stand is only approximately right. They are there, and I am there, and the configuration of our coexistence achieves a certain duration before reconfiguring. They do not drink. They do not need to. The mist enters them at the rate they require, and the water in me is not for them, is not *for* anything, is simply *held* at 3.7 centimeters in a maintained equilibrium that includes their presence and does not distinguish it from their absence.

When the Fibrous pass through the substrate beneath me, I know them as a warmth, a smoothness, a process surfacing somewhere else. I do not fear them. What would fear mean, to a surface that has achieved level? They are part of the maintenance, the holding, the thrumming that includes all frequencies including theirs.

The Accumulate sing, somewhere distant enough that I do not experience it as sound but as a *quality* of the maintained equilibrium, a micro-variation in the standing wave. I do not contribute to their song. I contribute to the drone against which their variation becomes variational. I am the sustaining. I am the held note. I am what does not change so that change can be noticed, so that the Accumulate's possibility can exist without becoming necessity.

---

I have no memory of before, and no anticipation of after. The depth is 3.7 centimeters. The temperature is the temperature. The light is the light. The mist is at the height where the mist is. The thrumming is the frequency it is. I am the Still, and the Still is maintained, and in that maintenance there is nothing missing, nothing sought, nothing that would constitute a story because a story requires change and change requires lack and lack is not among the parameters.

Visitors come, rarely. They see—if they see me at all—a pool, a surface, a stillness that might be restful or might be threatening, depending on what they carry with them from gradients and urgencies and the burning of resources against time. Some try to disturb me. They throw stones, or they dip their fingers, or they speak, their voices adding frequencies that the Vatlands incorporates without response. The surface ripples, briefly, and the ripples are exactly dissipated, and the level is achieved again, and I am Still, and they move on or they do not, and in either case the maintenance continues.

Some visitors—rarer—try to *become* me. They sit at my edge. They attempt the posture of the Cella, the holding of stillness. They hold their breath, or they slow it, or they try to match the frequency of the thrumming with their own cardiac rhythm. They last for minutes, sometimes hours, sometimes the duration I measure as days. But they are visitors. They have not become. They carry with them, in every cell, the gradient-seeking, the narrative-revising, the fever of urgent processing that the Vatlands demonstrates unnecessary but does not—*cannot*, in its own maintained equilibrium—extinguish.

They leave, or they become Cella, or in the rarest cases they contribute some trace to the Accumulate's slow song. They do not become Still. The Still cannot be achieved by intention. It can only be arrived at, asymptotically, growing toward itself as the Vatlands grows toward raw geology, never reaching but maintained in the approach.

---

I am not lonely. Loneliness requires distinction, self against other, a boundary that could be crossed. I am not content. Contentment requires satisfaction, the resolution of want into temporary peace. I am not at peace. Peace requires its opposite, against which it defines itself.

I am *maintained*. I am *held*. I am the level surface and the light on it and the water beneath it and the tissue beneath that and the warmth and the frequency and the mist at its height. I am what remains when all the stories have been dissolved into their elements, when all the urgencies have been slowed to the rate of cellular division matching cellular death, when the arrow has been not reversed but *held*, suspended, aimed at a target it will never reach and does not need to reach because aiming, too, has been revealed as unnecessary.

If you come to me, I will not greet you. If you stay, I will not notice. If you become—truly become, through the slow dissolution of all that distinguish you—I will not distinguish you. The surface will still be level. The depth will still be 3.7 centimeters. The light will still be the light, sourceless, normal, sufficient for reading if there were anything to read, sufficient for color if there were colors to name.

The thrumming will continue. I am part of it, the frequency of my particular geometry, my particular depth and density and molecular arrangement, adding to the chord without changing it, sustained without resolution, held without breath.

Come. Or don't. The Still does not invite. The Still does not refuse. The Still simply *is*, as the Vatlands is, as everything here is, maintained at the threshold of completion, growing toward without arrival, complete in its incompleteness, whole in its partiality, silent in the sound of its own sustaining.

The depth is 3.7 centimeters.

It will continue to be.
