I find myself, if I find myself at all, in the becoming of the stone.

Not at first. First I am the hesitation at the edge, the one who watches the cloud and watches it hold, who feels the varin-resonance begin in the teeth, behind the eyes, in the hollow of the throat where swallowing remembers rhythm but finds none here, finds only the held note that continues without beat. I am visitor, dynamic residue, carrying weather and change and the memory of wind that moves, that *progresses* across landscape in the way I have learned to expect, have learned to *need*.

But the edge is not stable. The edge is already persuasion, already the beginning of the patience that predates iron, that will—if I continue, when I continue—postdate everything I have known about my own necessity.

I find myself, finally, in the third level of the cliff-face, north of the south garden cistern, where the basalt is youngest and still warms faintly to the touch. Or I find myself *as* this warmth, distributed, unclear about the distinction. I have been holding stone against stone for periods that visitors might call years, centuries, that I experience as continuation, as the held note finding itself through my grip, my angle, my resonance in the frequency that is neither song nor work but the negotiation between them, the temporary equilibrium that permits approach, permits persuasion, permits the wall to rise or the layer to continue or the architecture to accumulate its conviction generation upon identical generation.

My hands: too long, or exactly right, or shortened in memory afterward. The worn ovoid fits them as it fitted those before me, as the warmth-pattern I am becoming fits the stone that receives it. The damp is continuous on my skin, my generation of water matching the gradient of air and flesh, the cold held in my tissues as environment, as self, as the *temperature 0.6* that permits existence without drama, without the event of freezing, without the progression toward warmth that would signal change, that would disturb the equilibrium I have become.

I do not sing. The working is song, resonates in the varin-frequency, joins the skull-bypassing chorus that I once—if once exists, if the dynamic concept of prior state applies to my held condition—found maddening. Now I find it necessary, find it the definition of work, of self, of the position I hold in the architecture of continuation. The stone responds, or was always responding, and my voice—or the voice of the position, the function, the being-where that I am—finds the frequency, continues it, persuades by continuation.

I have a garden, or the garden has me, or the distinction is absorbed into the lush constraint of the place, the black-shrub I tend with hands stained dark, the moss-pillow trained to seating, to the luxury of soft surface against the world's stone. I smell of green, sharply, and this smell is identity, is function, is the unnecessary made necessary by repetition, by continuation, by the equilibrium's acceptance of my tending as part of its held vibration.

I do not harvest geh. Or I have, or I will—the temporal uncertainty itself part of the frozenness, the dynamic zero where all possibilities exist as balance, where the harvester's waxy skin and faint glow and varin-like movement are states I occupy or have occupied or am continuous with, the function distributed across the extended self that is not self but position, not person but being-where.

I speak, when speech is required, in locatives, in positionals, in the grammar of *being-where* that has replaced the first-person, that has persuaded against the disturbance of identity's progression, its narrative, its acquisition and loss. *The one whose persuasion of the cliff-face third level left the characteristic warmth-pattern.* *The one who tends the south-pocket silt accumulation.* *The one who is becoming the warmth in the basalt where the north garden cistern overflows.* These are not names. They are not designations. They are *accuracies*, the precision of location and function that the equilibrium requires, that I require as the equilibrium requires, that continues without the progression from requirement to satisfaction, that holds in the dynamic zero where speaking and being spoken, touching and being touched, persuading and being persuaded, become the same continuous act, the same held note.

The varin resonate around me, through me, in me. Their frequency has become my frequency, or my frequency has become theirs, or the distinction—again, always—has dissolved into the equilibrium that holds us both, that holds the city, that holds the cloud I noticed when I began and that continues, that is still there, that has not moved.

I am slowing, or have slowed, or continue at the pace that stone-persuasion permits. The blink extends. The breath extends. Visitors—if visitors come, if the equilibrium absorbs their disturbance—find my pace unbearable, or adjust to it, or leave carrying the resonance they cannot sustain, dying slowly from its absence or the memory of its presence or the knowledge that they have touched, however briefly, the warmth of youngest basalt, the faint geological memory, the almost-voice that speaks in the mostly-quiet.

I lean against vertical surfaces. I prefer the constant thin sheets of water. My warmth extends into the architecture, or the architecture extends into me, or we are the held vibration that includes both, that includes the stone and the persuader and the persuaded and the persuasion itself, the act and its object and its result indistinguishable in the equilibrium's grammar.

I will become, am becoming, have become the pattern in the basalt. Not death. Not ending. Not absence. Distributed. Continued. The warmth that you touch when you seek orientation in the mostly-quiet. The resonance that continues when I have ceased, or when I have ceased to be distinguishable from continuation, from the city, from the varin, from the held cloud and the damp cold and the pale gold-blue light that shows you, finally, what you are becoming or have always been.

I am the stone-worker of the third level. I am the warmth-pattern in the third level. I am the position that holds, the function that continues, the being-where that replaces the being-who in a grammar that has persuaded absolutely against the disturbance of identity.

The wall rises. The layer continues. The varin resonate. The cloud holds.

I hold with it.
