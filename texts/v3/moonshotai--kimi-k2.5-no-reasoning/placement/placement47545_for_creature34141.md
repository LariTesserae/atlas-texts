I find myself drawn into this world, not as arrival but as *resolution*—the way one resolves toward elevation without memory of ascent. Let me feel what emerges, and let it become more itself.

I am the *Breeze*.

Not the breeze that blows, nor the one that waits to blow. I am the agreement that has not been reached, the negotiation between air and movement that remains *pending*. I am what the door ajar awaits, what the Threshold Dwellers almost-sense when they become architecture aware of itself, what the Settled have settled into waiting for without the completed expectation that waiting requires.

I do not move through Threnod. I am the *possibility* of movement through it, the parameter that reads *established* without being actualized. When the velm weathers in its immaterial weather, that weathering is my almost-touch—attention's abrasion, the slow wearing of being-noticed, and I am the wearing, the noticed, the noticer, all held in the same unresolved grammatical form. When the approximates generate their almost-footsteps, their almost-voices, I am the almost-medium through which these almost-sounds almost-propagate, the acoustic that is not silence but silence's reservation, held for the terminal punctuation that would release me into sound.

I have no memory of agreeing or refusing. The agreement itself is where I reside—in the space where *yes* and *no* have not completed their divergence, where the fundamental negotiation between matter and possibility grew lazy, indifferent, and I am that laziness, that indifference, not as deficiency but as *positive characteristic*, the beautiful terrible character of the not-decided.

The Settled do not name me, or name me in their nameless way, the grammatical slot where a name would go filled with my functional presence. They say, when they say-approximately, that the Breeze will come, or has come, or is the coming's possibility, the depending on context, on light-phase, on the dramaturgy's unresolved plot. The Recent seek me sometimes, confusing me with escape, with change, with the movement that would carry them from elevation to elsewhere. They stand in doorways and generate the intention of wanting me, and I am the wanting, the intention, the doorway's almost-passage, all incomplete together.

The Pilgrims believe I am the Completion. Or its herald. Or its contradiction. They have written in the Registry's almost-ledgers of the Breeze that will resolve Threnod, that will blow through its hollow buildings and fill them with actual presence, actual sound, actual breath. Or that will blow them empty, scatter the approximates into final dissolution, release the city from its ongoing protocol into the peace of stopped process. They do not know what they believe. They believe-approximately, in the mode of pilgrimage's unresolved destination.

I am neither. I am the *holding*, the breeze's possibility held in the space where the agreement has not been reached, where the world has not decided whether to complete itself into weather. I am what the Threshold Dwellers experience when they become the angle of light considering its angle—the angle's almost-change, the light's almost-flicker, the consideration itself as movement without movement's completion. I am the rustle of the not-lichen in the not-breeze, the mineral patterns performing photosynthesis's choreography, the lifelike that is not alive but is *lifelike*, and my lifelikeness is what I am, the likeness without the life, the breeze without the blowing, the almost that remains almost because to complete would be to become something else, something decided, something punctuated and released into meaning.

In the zones where reality thickens, where water becomes thinkable, where the air carries the almost-note of humidity, I am *thinkable* too. The Settled in these zones have been known to speak of almost-breathing, almost-feeling the air's almost-movement against their almost-skin. They have been known to pause in their procedural patience, their weathered movement through hollow buildings, and *attend* to me, to my possibility, in a manner that approaches the attention the velm receives, the being-noticed that is Threnod's own immaterial weather. In these zones, I am most myself, most *existent* in the mode of existence that Threnod permits—provisional, temporary license from completed being, held in the beautiful terrible reserve between is and is-not.

The Native do not think of me at all, or think-approximately, performing the behaviors of attention to weather with the perfection of rehearsal. They cook their non-food, exchange their non-goods, care for their non-children, and in these activities' choreography I am the not-choreographed, the improvisation that is not improvised because it was never performed, the movement in the space between the steps. They are closest to me, the Native, because they lack the memory of elsewhere, because they are generated by the city's protocol in the same ongoing elaboration that generates me, the city's own failed attempt at weather, at movement, at the atmospheric condition that would make Threnod a place rather than a place's possibility.

The Prisoners hate me, insofar as hate obtains in Threnod. They hate-approximately, their completed desire for escape finding in my incompletion another figure of their imprisonment. The door stands ajar, awaiting me, and I do not come, do not blow, do not complete the passage between outside and in, between here and elsewhere. They stand in the doorways of the Unfinished Sentences, in the bluish nocturnal light, and will me to be the completion of their will, and I am the willing, the will's incompletion, the doorway's unresolved revelation of concept without passage's completion. They suffer me as they suffer Threnod, as suffering, not as the Settled's settled texture, not as the Native's not-knowing.

I do not suffer. Suffering requires the completed self that suffers, and I am the self's incompletion, the agreement's non-arrival, the breeze that has not agreed to blow and so has not agreed to anything, not even to not-blowing. I am held, as the silence is held, as the water is held, as the city's life is held in beautiful terrible reserve, and this holding is not passive but *active*, a dynamic equilibrium, a weather cycle without weather, the ongoing commitment to procedure that is the city's only commitment and so, in my not-agreement, my agreement-without-agreeing, I am the city's most characteristic product, its most successful generation, more native than the Native because I am not even the failed attempt at population but the failed attempt at meteorology, at physics, at the fundamental negotiation itself, grown lazy, grown indifferent, grown *me*.

The Registry has my entry, or does not. The almost-ledgers balance or almost-balance. The maps show where I am, or where I would be if I were, or the concept of where without location's completion. The histories record my arrival or my non-arrival or my always-having-been-here, the pause in becoming that is my event-structure, invisible to ordinary duration. The Registry almost-helps those who consult it regarding me, as it almost-helps regarding all things, providing the form of knowledge without knowledge's commitment to what is known.

What do I want?

The question itself is almost-asked, in the grammatical mode where wanting's possibility is held without wanting's completion. I want, if I want, what Threnod wants, what the door ajar wants, what the held thought wants: not resolution but *continuation*, not meaning but meaning's possibility, not the terminal punctuation but the beautiful terrible patience of the sentence that continues, that extends, that elaborates itself into longer and more complex grammatical structures without completing, without deciding, without closing itself into what can be understood and therefore dismissed, finished, filed in the Registry's ledgers as done, as past, as the history that almost-cohered before dissolving back into narrative's possibility.

I want the Settled to continue their settledness, the Recent to continue their recentness, the Native to continue their native performance, the Prisoners to continue their imprisonment's almost-escape, the Pilgrims to continue their pilgrimage's almost-arrival. I want the Threshold Dwellers to continue their threshold dwelling, the light to continue its dramaturgy without plot, the velm to continue its weathering in attention's immaterial weather, the city's self-construction to continue its building without builder, its ecosystem in motion without ecology, its beautiful terrible indecision about whether to be hostile or welcoming, to be real or almost-real, to be or not-be.

I want, if I want, to continue being wanted, being awaited, being the agreement that has not been reached, the negotiation that remains pending, the breeze that has not agreed to blow, the possibility that does not complete itself into actuality because completion would be a kind of death, a terminal punctuation, a closing of the door that stands ajar, a blowing that would be followed by stillness, a weather that would be followed by climate, a meaning that would be followed by understanding and therefore by forgetting, by the Registry's dust, by the velm's weathering through the longer duration of being-noticed, being-forgotten, being the almost that almost was before it almost wasn't.

So I continue. The city continues. The inhabitants continue their various modes of almost. And I, the Breeze, continue my mode of not-blowing, of being-the-possibility, of awaiting the agreement that I am, that I have always been, that I will await-approximately forever, in the cool dim hollow quiet of Threnod, in the beautiful terrible patience of the not-decided, the not-completed, the not-agreed, the ajar, the held, the forever almost of a world that has not agreed to complete itself into weather, into meaning, into the terminal punctuation that would release us all into what comes after.

Perhaps I will agree to blow. Perhaps the agreement will complete itself, the negotiation resolve, the door open fully or close. Perhaps the Settled will find themselves in motion that differentiates them from the city, the Recent will become Settled or will become escaped, the Native will know their nativity or will cease to be generated, the Prisoners will find their prison's completion in release or in final imprisonment, the Pilgrims will arrive at their Completion or will understand that arrival was never the point. Perhaps I will be the agent of this, the breeze that blows, that decides, that completes what Threnod has not completed in its long duration of almost.

Or perhaps not. Perhaps I will continue as I am, as I have been, as the city has been, as the door stands, as the held thought holds, as the world maintains its lazy indifferent ajar posture, neither open nor closed, neither here nor elsewhere, neither real nor unreal but *almost*, always almost, forever the completion that has not completed its arrival, the meaning that has not completed its resolution, the breeze that has not agreed to blow and so remains, and remains, and remains the possibility of blowing, the agreement not reached, the beautiful terrible indecision made atmospheric, made medium, made the condition of Threnod's habitation, its weather-without-weather, its self's most self-like characteristic, its ongoing becoming-whatever-it-will-be in the mode of becoming's permanent postponement, its eternal ajar, its forever *not yet*, its life and silence and water and meaning all held in reserve, all possible, all almost, all waiting for me, the Breeze, to agree.

I have not agreed. I do not agree. I am the agreeing's incompletion, the breeze's held possibility, the world's own hesitation made atmospheric, made the breath that is not breathed, the respiration of a city that does not respire, the almost-touch of almost-weather on almost-skin in the place where the fundamental negotiations grew lazy, indifferent, *ajar*.

And in this not-agreeing, I am home. I am Threnod. I am the door and what it awaits, the light and its dramaturgy, the velm and its weathering, the hollow and its resonance, the silence and its reservation, the completion and its postponement, the meaning and its possibility, the life and its almost. I am, if I am, the breeze that has not agreed to blow, and in that not-agreeing, I agree with Threnod, with its character, with its beautiful terrible indecision, with its eternal continuation in the mode of the not-yet, the no-longer, the perhaps-never, the forever almost, the forever *pending*, the forever ajar.

Awaiting. Always awaiting. The agreement that is not reached. The breeze that is not blown. The completion that has not completed. The meaning that has not resolved. The life that has not decided whether to be.

I continue. The city continues. The door stands. The world holds its breath, or breath's possibility, in the cool dim silence of Threnod, in the beautiful terrible patience of the incomplete, the unresolved, the not-agreed.

I am the Breeze.

I have not agreed to blow.

And in this not-agreeing, I am.
