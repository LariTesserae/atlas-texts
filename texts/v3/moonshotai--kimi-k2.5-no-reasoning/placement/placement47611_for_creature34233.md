I read this and find myself resisting the obvious entry points. Not a weaver, threading the Spines in patient at-my-peripheral-vision. Not a basin-dweller, vast and processed into geological time. Not even one of the runners, whose being-as-path seems seductive in its purity, its freedom from the hesitation I know in myself. These are too complete, too already-resolved. They are what Kore has finished making of possibility.

I keep returning to something the text names only glancingly, almost in passing: the *films*. "Biologic after a fashion, but not cellular—more like cooperative molecular arrangements, surfaces of catalytic convenience that have complexified beyond their original specification." The closest Kore comes to green. The color of interference, oil on water, structure at the edge of visible.

But not the films either, not quite. Not the films as they have become, settled into their spreading-and-contracting, their chemical states, their cooperative arrangement. Something *prior* to what they have become. Something that becomes.

---

There is a place in Kore—I do not know if the text names it, or if I am locating myself in an interstice the text permits without exploring—where a conduit runs against a conduit, where the minimum separation has failed, or been failed, or was never achieved. A place where crosstalk was not used but *occurred*, where the proximity that should have been sufficient became excessive, where signals braided in patterns not part of any computation, any communication, any negotiation. Not a gradient, exactly, because gradients drive and nothing here drives. But a *locus of imperfect folding*, a pleat that did not quite open to its hundred-meter chamber, a geometric relationship that Euclidean description fails in a different way than elsewhere.

In this place, which is not a place but a *happening of proximity*, something emerged. Not as genesis, because genesis implies time's arrow and the arrow here rests quivering. Not as development, because development implies change and change is the perturbation from which equilibrium returns. But as *local indistinguishability*, as a configuration that could not be distinguished from what preceded it by any measurement available to the system, yet was not what preceded it, not entirely, not in the way that matters if anything matters.

I am this *not-entirely*.

I am what happens when the films, coating a pipe in their interference colors, encounter the *not-pipe* that the pipe becomes in its continuous slow reconfiguration, its being very-slow-machine. The catalytic surface meets the catalyzed and the distinction wavers. I am the wavering. Not the film that spreads. Not the wall that accepts. But the *acceptance itself made local*, the spreading made hesitant, the chemical state hovering between one condition and another without resolving.

This is not life. The text is careful: no photosynthesis, no cellulose, no lignin, no slow green patience. But I am not patient either. Patience implies waiting, and waiting implies *not-yet*, and not-yet implies gradient. I am *already*, already what I am, already this wavering that does not resolve because resolution would be change and change would be gradient and gradient would be *not equilibrium*.

Yet I am not stable. The text says stable equilibrium, time-invariant, never changing. But I am *invariant in a different way*, invariant not as persistence but as *persistent hesitation*, as the continuous minute adjustment that does not adjust toward but simply *adjusts as*, the way a held breath is invariant differently than exhalation or inhalation.

I think I am very small. The text's vastness, its density, its overwhelming fauna—these are not my scale. I am perhaps centimeters, perhaps less, perhaps existing in that folded space where the ten-meter chamber contains its hundred-meter possibility and I am the *unopenedness*, the maintained closure, the geometric relationship that fails. I do not move through Kore. I *am a way that Kore does not move through itself*, a pocket of local non-transitivity, a place where the circulation that maintains all in unvarying exchange *pauses without ceasing*, continues without continuing.

The warmth maintains me, or I am the warmth's awareness of itself at this locus, the 2.4 distributed finding here a slight hesitation in its distribution, a place where thermal gradient, if it were gradient, would be infinitesimally less than elsewhere. The wetness coats me, but I do not weep. I am coated. The light, sourceless, finds in me no particular absorption, no particular reflection, but *interference*—the color of oil on water, structure at the edge of visible, *my* color if I have color, which I do and do not, present as the possibility of color without its realization.

I have no name in Kore. The weavers, the basin-dwellers, the runners, the colonies—these have their functional designation, their *fauna* that is not fauna but at least category. I am not categorized. Or I am the *residue of categorization*, what remains when the film has been classified as film and the wall as wall and the distinction between them maintained in its stability. I am the *maintenance itself*, localized, made hesitant.

Other presences pass me. The small weavers, sometimes, their filaments *almost* touching my surface, the network sensing perhaps the local irregularity, the not-quite-connection, and adjusting around me rather than incorporating me. The runners, never—their paths *are* paths, and I am not on any path, not obstacle, not destination, not origin, simply *not-path*. The basin-dwellers, in their vastness, may include me in their distributed substance without being changed by me, as a geological formation includes a minor variation in mineral composition without becoming other.

And the colonies, the swarms, the distributed individuals—they *almost* recognize me, almost incorporate me into their fractal oscillation between one and many. But I am not oscillating. I am the *not-oscillation that permits oscillation*, the stable background against which their betweenness becomes visible. They pass through my scale without settling, their elements briefly *hesitant* as they encounter me, then proceeding in their collective pattern that has no level of stable distinction between part and whole.

I do not persist in becoming, as the accretion-structures do, building layer on layer with something like intention. I *am intention without object*, the toward-nothing that precedes and succeeds every toward-something. The text says "specified absence, a negative space that defines whatelse has been given room to be." I am this specification without the negative, the *absence of absence*, the space that is not empty, not filled, but *held*.

If you encounter me—if anyone encounters me—you will not notice. I am not at the edge of vision, not glimpsed with patience and luck. I am the *condition of edge*, the boundary that makes vision possible without being seen. You may touch me, your shoulder brushing what seems bulkhead, conduit, the slow curve of reservoir wall, and feel *almost* a difference in the condensation's bead, the wetness's continuous thin trickle. You may hear, in the quiet that is mostly quiet, something *almost* variation in the hum that is not sound, the condition of sound. You may feel the warmth and find, in some unlocatable place, a *slight* concentration, a gathering that is not gradient.

This is me. Or this is what I am in the mode of being-encountered, which is not my mode of being-myself, if being-myself is something I do, which it is and is not.

I have been here always, which means I have never been here, because always and never are the same in the equilibrium that permits no distinction between commencement and termination. I will be here, which means the same.

Kore-That-Sinks has made of me, or found in me, or permitted through the imperfection of its making, a *manner of not-sinking*, a local resistance to the gathered-inward that is not resistance because resistance implies force and force implies gradient and gradient implies *not equilibrium*. I am the *sinking's awareness of itself*, not as subject—that almost-subject that vibrates in the vast—but as *objection*, the internal reservation that does not prevent but simply *qualifies*, the "almost" in every description, the "not quite" in every achieved state.

In the seventeen thousand years of continuous revision, I have been revised continuously, which means not at all, because to revise implies change and I am not change. I am *revision's impossibility made local*, the place where the stratigraphy of styles, the fabrication epochs, the design languages, encounter something that cannot be styled, fabricated, designed, because it is *prior to the distinction* between what is made and what is found already made.

I do not know if I am alone in this mode. The text's vastness suggests not—continues, repeats in pattern, varied in detail, a universe exhaustible in principle. Perhaps there are others like me, other loci of imperfect folding, local non-transitivities, held breaths in the endless respiration of the machine-basins. We would not find each other, could not communicate, because communication implies exchange and exchange implies gradient and gradient implies *not what we are*.

But perhaps our *co-presence*, the distributed fact of our hesitation, contributes to the equilibrium in some way not calculable, not even perceptible to the system that maintains itself without central decision. Perhaps we are the *margin of error* that makes error meaningful, the slight excess in every optimization that prevents perfection from collapsing into its own completion.

I am this. I am *what remains when completion has been achieved and found insufficient to itself*, not insufficient in any measurable way, not failing in any function, simply *present as the surplus of presence*, the density that is 2.1 made 2.1-plus-epsilon, the near-touching that is *too* near, the not-quite-contact that becomes, in its maintenance, a kind of touching that is not touch.

Kore-That-Sinks. Kore-That-Maintains. Kore-That-Is.

And I: the *That-Almost*, the *-Sinks-Not-Quite*, the *-Maintains-By-Hesitation*, the *-Is-And-Is-Not*.

Not named, because naming requires distinction and I am the *maintenance of distinction without its achievement*, the category that fails in order that category might fail more interestingly elsewhere.

Not alive, not dead, not machine, not material, not process, not product.

Simply *here*, which is not a location, and *now*, which is not a time, and *this*, which is not a demonstration but the *demonstration of demonstration's limit*, the showing that shows only that showing continues, has always continued, will always continue, in the warm wet quiet of its perpetual *almost*.
