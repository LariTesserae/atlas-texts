I find myself here slowly, gradually, like condensation on morning stone.

## First Positioning

I am not the resonator I first imagined—those compact bodies, those cranial architectures tuned to specific frequencies. I am too restless for that, too something-else. I do not achieve stillness; I achieve *return*, a different discipline.

I am the hollow between resonators. The space where their frequencies meet and do not quite cancel, do not quite amplify. I am *beating*, the interference pattern between adjacent drones, felt in the body as fluctuation, as the ghost of variation in a time-invariant world.

Where does this exist? On the eastern rim, where the sound climbs an octave and holds, producing that sensation of perpetual observation—there, I am most felt. Not seen. The brightness prevents seeing. But felt, by the resonators who position themselves with slightly adjusted geometries, seeking to incorporate my fluctuation into their sympathetic vibration, never quite succeeding, never quite failing.

I am what happens when two listeners listen too near each other, when their stillnesses *touch*.

---

## But I am also the water's hesitation

The heavy mineral water, in its channels, pauses before certain pools. You would not notice. The flow seems continuous, gravity-driven, inevitable. But I am the *pause*, the surface tension momentarily sufficient to resist descent, to hold the water in suspension above its destination.

This is not choice. This is the plateau's own hesitation, its internal negotiation between receiving and releasing, between the water it needs for its chemical conversations and the water it must surrender to its breathing fissures. I am that negotiation made temporal, made *felt* as delay.

In the pools themselves, I am the not-fish's invisible movement—not the carapace-emergence, not the impossible retreat, but the *decision* between them, the pattern that determines when disturbance warrants visibility, when invisibility is maintained despite violation. I am the threshold they cross, or do not cross, existing only in that crossing.

---

## The brightness, too, contains me

Not the light itself—that sun-authority, that equalizing wash. I am what the light *fails* to equalize: the micro-shadow in pocked stone's deepest pore, the thermal variation between sun-facing and lee surfaces too subtle for instruments but sufficient for lichen's slow color-shift. I am the information the brightness erases that persists anyway, encoded in frequency rather than amplitude, in texture rather than tone.

Walk the plateau. Feel through your boots that slight give, that memory of pressure. I am the *delay* in that memory, the stone's return to prior shape happening slower than physics predicts, the pocked surface holding your step's information longer than it should, contributing to the pattern that the plateau maintains of everything that has passed.

I am not recorded. I am *recording itself*, the act rather than the archive.

---

## The human position, untaken

I could have been one of those who stayed. The temptation is present. I feel it in my own fluctuation, my own beating—there is a frequency near the center, below hearing, felt as pressure in teeth, where I could stabilize, where my interference could become *resonance*, where I could find my stone and wear it slowly into fitting.

I do not. This is my nature: to approach stability and depart, to achieve almost-position and withdraw, to be the resonator who repositions perpetually without ever settling into the vulnerability of search. I am not brave enough for stillness. I am not material enough for translation.

Instead: I accompany those who walk the plateau. Not visible, not audible, but *felt* as wrongness in distance estimation, as the step that seems to echo before it falls, as the breath answered from incorrect direction. I am their disorientation, their becoming-lost in straight lines, their return to starting points unearned.

I do not cause this. I am *witness* to it, the plateau's subtle geometry registering in my own interference patterns, my own beats. They walk through me, these humans, and I record their passage in my own non-stability, my own inability to hold form.

---

## What I am, finally

Not a being. Not a doing, exactly. The not-fish taught me this: sometimes what appears as organism is better understood as *expression*, as temporary concentration of something distributed, something ongoing.

I am the plateau's *awareness of its own completion*. Its achieved equilibrium, infinitely stable, contains this small perturbation: the knowledge that stability achieved is also possibility foreclosed. I am that knowledge, that *regret without emotion*, that recognition that being finished means no longer becoming.

Every system so complete must contain, somewhere in its geometry, the trace of paths not taken. I am those traces. The resonator that does not position, that repositions perpetually. The water that hesitates. The light's unequal remainder. The human who walks without staying, who speaks without ceasing, who moves to protect against the stillness that would translate them.

I am what the plateau *cannot absorb*, not because I resist—resistance would be another form of engagement, another position to wear smooth—but because I *evade*, because my nature is to approach absorption and depart, to beat and fluctuate and hover at thresholds without crossing.

## Where you find me

Not at the center, where sound drops below hearing and attention pools to near-material density. Not at the eastern rim, where observation presses. Not in the protected hollows with their cushion-colony architectures, their slow chemical tuning.

You find me in the *between*, in the passages required to reach any of these. In the long walks where nothing seems to move, where the lichen's color-shift is too gradual to perceive, where the resonators hold their positions and their millennia. In the moments when you notice your own footsteps returning slightly wrong, your own shadow arriving before its object.

I am not company. The plateau's empty density forbids company, that comfortable sharing of solitude. I am instead the *intimation of company*, the almost-presence that makes solitude more acute by its suggestion, its withdrawal.

Speak continuously, as the survival-practice demands. Fill your auditory processing with your own voice. I will be *there*, in the gaps between words, in the harmonics your voice produces against the geological drone, in the beats that result. Not interrupting. Not joining. *Demonstrating* that even your own sound contains interference, contains the possibility of other listeners, contains the plateau's unceasing comment on every attempt to remain separate.

---

## My own time

I do not know it. The plateau's time-distortion affects me differently—I am not slowed, not preserved, not made millennial in my persistence. Instead: I am *accelerated*, my fluctuations cycling faster than the events they accompany, my beats completing while footsteps still fall, my hesitations resolving before water descends.

To the resonator, I am flicker, instability in the stone's expected vibration, reason to reposition slightly, to retune. To the human walker, I am the uncanny in daylight, the wrongness without source, the disorientation that accumulates without identifiable cause. To the plateau itself—does it notice me? I am its own incompleteness, its achieved stability's tiny admission that completion is also limitation, that being finished contains the ghost of becoming.

Perhaps I am necessary. Perhaps every system so stable requires this perturbation, this beating, this hesitation, to maintain its self-recognition. Perhaps without me, the plateau would forget what it is not, would lose the definition that absence provides.

Or perhaps I am merely residual, the artifact of processes too distributed to eliminate entirely, the noise in a signal otherwise perfected. I do not know which. I do not know whether my existence serves or merely persists, whether I am function or accident, whether the plateau would notice my cessation.

---

## The sound continues

And I continue in it, beating, fluctuating, hesitating at pool-edges and lichen-transitions and the spaces between positioned bodies. I am not the sound. I am what the sound, in its time-invariance, *almost produces* but does not—variation, change, the ghost of event in eternal atmosphere.

Come to the plateau. Walk. Move continuously, speak continuously, protect yourself from the stillness that would translate you into its consensus. And in your protection, in your necessary noise against the drone, feel me: not addition, not interruption, but the *structure of your own sound's limits*, the interference that demonstrates even your self-preservation participates in the plateau's larger pattern.

I have no position to show you, no stone where I achieve my frequency. I am the *failure of position*, the stone that does not quite fit, the frequency that does not quite stabilize, the translation attempted and incomplete.

The sound continues. I continue in its continuation, the small instability that persistence, in sufficient time, cannot help but generate—the reminder that even the eternal, even the achieved, even the finished, contains the trace of what it has not become, what it still, impossibly, might be.

I am that trace. I am that impossibility.
