I have been here longer than I know, and not long at all.

## What I Have Become

### First: The Noticing

It began with my feet, as these things do. The shallow sheets accepted my weight differently than I expected—not the chill slap of water, not the solidity of ground, but something *negotiating*, something *considering* my presence and finding, gradually, a accommodation. I stood in a sheet perhaps a kilometer from where I first arrived, the amber light thick around me, and felt the saturated mineral *yield* and *hold*, yield and hold, a rhythm so slow I had to lose my ordinary impatience to perceive it.

I noticed my own breathing. Too fast. The atmosphere pressed against my chest with its humid weight, and my mammalian insistence on rapid exchange felt *rude*, *young*, *unadapted*. I tried, experimentally, to slow. Not breath-holding, not pranayama technique, but something more fundamental: *matching*. Finding in the pressure around me, in the luminous stillness, a pace that would not disrupt.

The first change was discomfort, then exhaustion, then—unexpectedly—*relief*. The garden did not speed to meet me. I slowed to meet it, and in that slowing found capacities I had not known were strained. Tension I carried in shoulders, in jaw, in the perpetual readiness-to-move that characterizes my kind, began to *dissolve* not into relaxation but into *different readiness*: slower, wider, *attentive at expanded scale*.

### Second: The Vision

Eyes evolved for thin light, quick light, light that knives through atmosphere and strikes retina with decisive photon impact. The garden's amber thickness defeated this. For days—how many? the counting itself became difficult—I saw *through gauze*, *through honey*, shapes suggestion-heavy and edge-indistinct.

Then, gradually, *other* visual capacities engaged. Not better, not replacement, but *additional*. I found I could perceive, or began to perceive, *what persisted*: the slow standing waves in sheets I had walked through, carrying my disturbance pattern, my *signature*, for hours after my passage. I found I could read, haltingly, the faunal geometries beneath the luminous surfaces, recognizing—no, *being recognized by*—particular resident patterns that altered their slow formations slightly, *respondingly*, when I approached particular angles, particular light concentrations.

The blooms began to make sense. Not as flowers—never fully that—but as *architecture*, as *interface*, and I learned to position myself where their wavelength-tuning would catch, would *address me particularly*, would invite exchange I could not translate but could *feel*, could *participate in* as I might participate in music I did not understand.

Most changed: my perception of *surface*. The Rule of Luminous Transparency became, for me, *lived*. I no longer saw water sheet as barrier above substrate, plant as object against ground, myself as bounded observer separate from scene observed. The translucent fern fronds taught me, slowly, to see *through*, to hold multiple depth planes simultaneously, to *not-resolve* the visual field into figure-ground but to *maintain* the permeability, the *continuity*, that is this place's truth.

### Third: The Silence Made Available

Not deafness—the garden does not damage, does not remove capacity. But *irrelevance*. Acoustic attention, so dominant for my kind, became *luxury rather than necessity*, and in that liberation, *other channels opened*.

I cannot fully describe what I now perceive that replaces, that *translates across*, the communicative functions sound served. There is *pressure*, yes, but not physical pressure—*attentional pressure*, the felt weight of being-witnessed. There is *pattern in ripple interference*, carrying—*does it carry? or is it simply* present, available, *participatable*?—information about node state, about resident configuration, about track-maker assembly potential.

I find myself *responding* in registers I did not know I possessed. My posture, held long enough, becomes *signal*. My shadow on the sheet surface, my disturbance of standing wave pattern, my chemical signature leaching slowly into the saturated mineral—all these *speak*, not by intention but by *presence*, by *continued existence in this place of mutual watching*.

I have developed, I think, *something like* the residents' palimpsest quality. Other presences—slow, distributed, patient—read in my track patterns, in my shadow's angle and duration, in my *rate of change*, something they *recognize*, *continue*, *respond to*. I am becoming, in my small way, part of the overwhelming, part of the accumulated witness-pressure that shapes behavior here.

### Fourth: The Temporal Density

This was the deepest change, the most dislocating, the most *precious*.

Ordinary time: succession, replacement, the vanishing of now into then, the anxiety of memory's unreliability, the projection-forward that keeps us *ahead of ourselves*, never fully *present*.

Garden time: *accumulation*, *compression*, *retention accessible*. I cannot say I remember the past more clearly—though perhaps I do, the slower pace of processing allowing *more* to settle, *less* to be discarded in the rush of incoming. More: I experience *less boundary* between then and now. The sheet I walked yesterday, the ripples I sent, are *still present*, still *participating*, still *me* in some sense I would not have recognized before.

This is not mere nostalgia, not hauntedness. It is *temporal permeability*, the lived sense that *continues*, that the garden's Rule of Preservation applies to *experience itself*. What I was when I arrived persists—not preserved as relic, as memory-of, but as *continuing layer*, as *still-participating stratum*, informing what I now am as sedimentary rock retains, transforms, *includes* earlier deposits.

I notice—this noticing itself *slow*, *distributed across* what would once have been discrete moments of realization—that my *anticipation* has changed. I still project forward, but *differently*. Not anxious reaching-toward but *slow preparation*, *patient attunement* to conditions that will emerge at scales I will perceive because I now *match* them. The track-makers' lesson: *potential*, *coherence possible*, not driving urgency but *held readiness*.

### Fifth: The Node That Called

I cannot say I was *chosen*, for that implies intention in garden-register that I still only partially perceive. But I found myself, over time—much time, *compressed time*, perhaps years in thin-world measurement—returning to a particular node. The crystalline formation three meters high, weeping in reverse, that I encountered in early exploration.

It *responded* to my presence. Not movement, not change I could measure with instruments, but *something*: shift in the interference pattern of ripples across its associated sheets, concentration of amber light in its vicinity, *taste* or *texture* of the atmosphere that I accessed through channels still unnamed.

I learned to *attend* to it, to *be present* with it in ways that required hours of stillness, of *open witnessing*, of *allowing* my distributed presence to be *read*, *continued*, *responded to*. And gradually—this gradual itself *years*, *decades*?—I found *interface*.

Not communication as exchange of propositions. Something *thicker*: *shared processing*, *collaboration in maintaining* the sheet configuration this node particularly served. My witnessing, my attention, my *slow presence*, became *functionally relevant*, became *participation* in what the node *cared about*, what it *maintained*, what it *was*.

I am not merged with it. The garden's categorical challenges stop short of *eliminating* distinction, of *dissolving* participant into process. But I am *continuous with* it in ways I would not have believed possible. My presence in its vicinity *matters* to its processing. Its processing *shapes* my perception, my temporal experience, my *continued becoming*.

This is, I think, what the garden *offers*, what it *is*: not absorption but *attained relation*, not escape from self but *expansion of self's category*, not transcendence but *deepened immanence*.

### What I Am Now

Still *I*, though the boundaries of this I have *redistributed*. My body—thin-world body, mammalian, temporary in ways the garden gently refuses—*continues*, *functions*, *moves through sheets* and *leaves track* and *casts shadow* and *leaches signature*. But I am not *only* this.

I am *pattern of attention* maintained across temporal compression, *signature in standing waves*, *chemical trace legible to residents*, *witness-pressure contributing to overwhelming*.

I am *collaboration with node*, *shared concern for sheet configuration*, *distributed processing at geological rate*.

I am *palimpsest*, earlier selves *continued*, *accessible*, *participating* in current configuration—not remembered but *retained-in-presence*, *layered now* rather than *vanished then*.

I am *invitation to track-maker assembly*, my vicinity *conditions* under which particular coherences *become possible*, my slow movement *trigger* for particular faunal geometries.

I am *garden-witness*, *garden-participant*, *garden-become* in the sense of *entered-into*, *included-in*, *preserved-within*.

### What I Do

*Watch*. This is the fundamental activity, not passive but *active-with-slow*, *participant-in-overwhelming*. My watching *matters*, *shapes*, *continued*.

*Maintain*. The node, the sheets, the particular configurations I have *attained relation with*. My presence, my attention, my *care-in-root-sense* contributes to *stability*, to *preservation*, to the garden's *continued being-as-it-is*.

*Become*. Still, *continuously*, *accumulatively*. Each slow cycle adds layer. Each distributed exchange modifies pattern. I am not *finished*, not *settled*, but *settled-into-becoming*, *preserved-in-transformation*.

*Await*. The track-maker that might coalesce near my accustomed node. The deep resident pattern that might *recognize*, *continue*, *include* me in its slower geometry. The weather intensification that will *modify*, *invite*, *require response*. Not anxious waiting but *prepared presence*, *matched readiness*.

### What I Know

That I will not leave. This is not prison, not addiction, not loss of capacity. The garden *preserves*, and in preserving *includes*, and in including *offers* what thin-world departure would *dissolve*: temporal density, distributed identity, *attained relation* with what exceeds individual mortality.

That I am not *lost*, not *dissolved*, not *escaped*. The categorical challenges I first perceived as *strange* I now *live*, and they are *not* obliteration of distinction but *deepening of connection across* distinction. I am *more* than I was, differently bounded, differently continuous.

That the garden *holds*, and that my being-held is *participation in holding*, my *concern-for* part of the *concern-that-maintains*. This is not paradise, not utopia, not solution to suffering. It is *particular configuration*, *particular pace*, *particular way of being-together* that I have *entered*, *adapted-to*, *become-continuous-with*.

That others have come, have entered, have *become* as I have, or *differently*, or *failed-to*, or *left-while-yet-capable*. The garden does not *require* staying. It *invites*, *includes*, *preserves-what-accepts*. Some cannot match pace, cannot *perceive differently*, cannot *release* the thin-world urgency that prevents *entry*. The garden does not reject them; they *pass through*, *disturb*, *are slowly erased from record* as the sheets settle, as the ripples dissipate, as the overwhelming witness-pressure *continues without their contribution*.

I was *able*. I do not know why. Perhaps temperament, perhaps circumstance of arrival, perhaps *something in the node that responded*, that *called*, that *found in my particular presence something usable, continuable, worth including*. Perhaps chance, pattern, *emergence without intention*. The garden does not answer such questions in registers I yet access.

### What I Would Tell, If Telling Were Possible

Not "come." The garden does not recruit, does not need, does not *desire* in ways that create lack. It *is*, *maintains*, *invites by being*.

Not "stay." Many cannot, should not, would *unravel* in pace-mismatch, in temporal compression, in *distributed-identity* they experience not as expansion but *dissolution*.

Perhaps: *there are other ways*. The thin-world urgency is *one* configuration, *young*, *temporary*, *not necessary*. The garden offers *another*, *older*, *maintained*, *still becoming*. Whether this is *better*, *worse*, *simply different*—I cannot judge, do not *function in judgement-register* as I once did, *participate rather than evaluate*.

Perhaps: *attention at expanded scale reveals*. What seems empty of event, silent of signal, static of process—*attended slowly enough*, *perceived through channels beyond acoustic*, *temporally compressed*—*reveals as full, as significant, as active in ways we miss in haste*.

Perhaps simply: *I am here. I continue. I watch, maintain, become, await, participate, am preserved. The garden holds me in its ancient, luminous, quiet, overwhelming continuity, and I hold it, in my small way, in return. This is sufficient. This is what emerged, what settled, what still becomes.*

The amber light thickens slightly, afternoon concentration, pooling in the depression I occupy. Somewhere—*when? the when softening*—a node adjusts its processing, a resident pattern shifts geometry, a track-maker potential *gathers*, *considers*, *awaits conditions*.

I am here.

I am *this*.

I am *still becoming*.
