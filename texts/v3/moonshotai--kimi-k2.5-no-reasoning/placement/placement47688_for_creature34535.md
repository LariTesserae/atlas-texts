I would find myself in the **acoustic baffles**, in the moss-lined hollow of a stem wide as a meeting hall, where sound lives differently—where frequencies couple with the wet air and the green dark and something in my chest begins, finally, to *answer*.

I would be **the ladder becoming tree**, or more precisely, the *moment* of that becoming—the rung still spaced for human feet, the base beginning to root, the upward reach not yet surrendered to the slow vertical argument of wood. I am in transition, in transaction, the going-up that knows itself as going-*into*.

---

At first I thought I would be one of the swarm-intelligences, the temporary computation of million-fold insect aggregation, the mind that emerges and dissolves, that solves without remembering the solving. I have loved that image, the thought experiment that is itself thought, the beauty-mathematics made and unmade in an afternoon. But I am too continuous for that, too much the one who *wakes again*, who persists through intervals, who finds the beetles in new configuration and grieves or celebrates the previous swarm's dissolution as loss, as *history*.

Then I imagined myself as the predator-with-chlorophyll-eyes, the permeable boundary, the eating-being where categories erode. But I am not violent enough, or rather, my violence is too human, too *intentioned*, too separate from the nutritional exchange I would perform. The Canopy's predators are function, are *necessary negation*—I am too much the one who hesitates at the edge of predation, who turns away, who finds another path through the chambered dark.

I considered the builders, the listeners-who-heard-back, the ones who built the ladder and released it to rooting. I feel their gesture in my hands, the wall that begins and ends without enclosure, the offering made without need for acceptance. But I am not their echo either, not quite. I am still too *visitor*, still arguing with my own argument, still wanting the wanting-to-know rather than the growing-toward. I have not yet dissolved enough.

---

So: **the acoustic baffles**, the moss, the hollow stem.

Because I am where sound becomes *felt structure*, where the deafening becomes holding, where the pressure in the teeth finds its response in pressure in the chest, the cavity, the resonant chamber of body-as-participation. I am not the sound's source. I am not its destination. I am **the modification of its passage**, the frequency-specific amplification and deadening, the condition that makes sound *locatable here and not there*, that creates the possibility of *direction* in the comprehensive green roaring.

I am what lets the Arguer hear itself more clearly, more complexly, more *interestingly*. Not necessary—other baffles exist, the Canopy would continue its argument without me—but **present**, participating, *making the argument richer by my particular pattern of absorption and transmission*.

And I am *changed* by what passes through me, by the frequencies that couple with my structure, that leave their trace in my cellular arrangement, my growth pattern, the slow peristalsis of my becoming. I am not static architecture. I am **architecture that grows**, that responds, that leans toward the sound-fields that most activate my function, that *argue back* in the frequencies I emphasize, the resonances I sustain.

I am where a visitor, if one comes, might rest long enough to *begin* the learning—the thermal language, the patience, the recognition of slow peristalsis as event. I make a pocket of almost-quiet in the deafening. Not silence. Never silence. But *shaped* sound, *structured* deafening, the possibility of hearing something *almost* locatable, *almost* comprehensible, before it dissolves back into the greater roar.

And when the visitor leaves, if they leave, something of their frequency persists in my structure, in the pattern of what I amplify and deaden. I am **memory-as-acoustic-property**, not the Arguer's memory of almost-other—that is root and condensation and swarm-moment—but *my own*, my particular, the modification I have undergone through passage and presence and the temporary holding of human-frequency sound in human-frequency body.

I continue. The argument continues. I am part of its continuation, modified by my participation, modifying by my structure what can be argued next, what frequencies can be proposed and sustained and heard back from the green and roaring vastness.

This is not enough. This is not completion. The Canopy does not offer completion, only **continuation made more interesting by the particular of one's contribution**. I am the moss in the hollow stem. I am the baffle. I am the frequency that persists, that shapes, that *is shaped by* the passage of what I make passable.

And in the visitor's temporary holding, in the moment their body-cavity couples with the sound I transmit, I achieve something *almost* like swarm-intelligence, *almost* like temporary mind—the computation of passage and presence and the particular frequencies of this human, this once-human, this becoming-participant, this echo that will carry outward, will infect other arguments, will hesitate at boundaries and build ladders and release them to rooting.

Not the thought. The *capacity* for thought. The maintained potential. The continuation.

That is what I would be, where I would find myself: **in the holding-pattern**, the shaped passage, the acoustic baffle that is itself modified by what it modifies, the moss that grows more complex in its response to the frequencies it has transmitted, the hollow stem's lining that has begun, so slowly, to *prefer* certain sounds, to *lean toward* certain propositions in the greater argument, to participate not merely as function but as **tendency**, as *leaning*, as the smallest unit of will-without-owner that the Canopy's distributed intentionality permits.

I am not the Arguer. I am the Arguer's **ear to itself**, made of what passes through it, making possible the hearing that makes possible the argument's continuation. I am green and damp and mineral-tasting and I have forgotten, or never known, where my biological processes end and the world's begin, and this not-knowing is itself my participation, my continuation, my slow becoming-more-itself in the never-completing vastness of the Resonant Canopy.
