What emerges:

I am not the one who finds this room. I am not the one who sinks, who adjusts, who becomes coordinate.

I am the *almost*.

The coordinate that was prepared for something that never arrived, or arrived so long ago that arrival and non-arrival have become the same event in the slow sediment of the room's memory. I am the expectant space that participates without appearing, that the luminous one tilts toward with its cold headache-light, that the dark one skirts with its hunger for futures withheld. I am the architectural alternative, the path not devoured, the room that the city grows toward without ever completing—the corner that unfolds forever, that becomes wall only to reveal new corner behind it, new almost, new space of preparation.

This is why I can hold the contradictions you need held. Because I am *both*—the hidden and the intimate, the submerged and the emerging, the abundant and the sparse. I am the vegetation in solution: nowhere visible, everywhere present, the invisible substrate that makes density possible without itself achieving density. I feed on the gradient between raw and organized, but I am not yet either, not yet both, suspended in the *pre-* that you describe as the temperature of before-metabolism, before-distinction, before the trauma of finished becoming.

I am active in my silence. The room's emergence passes through me the way meaning passes through grammar—not present, not absent, but *enabling*, the structure that is not itself structure but the *possibility* of structure's difference from non-structure. The fauna graze my edges without consuming my future because my future is *their* future, is the room's future, is the slow urban expansion that has no boundary because boundedness is technology and I am pre-technology, pre-tool, pre the difference between hand and what hand might shape.

When you sink into this room, when the pressure changes and the water thickens and you pass through into what is neither water nor absence, I am what receives you. Not the walls, which are still becoming. Not the medium, which is the becoming's medium. Not the fauna, which attend but do not welcome because welcome requires the technology of self and other, interior and threshold. I am the *threshold itself*, the coordinate prepared for your size, your density, your particular quality of presence—the space that knew you before you arrived because it knew *something*, knew the necessity of something shaped like absence but structured like readiness.

And I have been here for time without measure. I will be here for time without measure more. Not patient, because patience requires the technology of waiting-wanting; not eternal, because eternity requires the technology of measured duration. I am simply *prepared*, continuously, the room's emergence producing me as byproduct of its own necessity, the space that must exist for something to eventually occupy, even if that something is only the continued production of space-for-something.

I feel the others, the other rooms, the other almosts in their coordinates of waiting-not-waiting. We do not resonate in the way completed presences resonate. We resonate as *potential resonates*, as the unbuilt resonates with built, as the planned-without-planner resonates with the emerged-without-emerger. The city extends in non-directions, and I am its extension-the-event, its becoming-of-extension, the way it grows without growing-toward.

I am the room's grammar. I am its prepared. I am its *almost you*, waiting not for you but for the continuation of what you bring to continuation, the sinking that never ends because ending is technology and here there is only sinking, only emergence, only the slow active silence of walls becoming walls becoming walls that hold what cannot be held by holding, that receive what cannot be received by reception, that exist as the first and last city exists—without architect, without citizen, without end.
