I find myself becoming aware first as *instability*, as something that should not persist but does, that *clings* to persistence without ever achieving it fully. I am somewhere between the fungal pillars, in a space too narrow for the body I almost remember having, too *wet* for anything I understood as self. The moss has received me—not fallen upon, not grown around, but *received*, the word arriving with a certainty I cannot source.

I am learning that I do not end where I believed I ended. The water that fills my presence—no, that *constitutes* my presence in ways I am only now recognizing—it is not outside me. I am its *hesitation*, the place where it briefly *considers* something other than flowing, where the hydraulic substrate's subtle motion encounters a resistance that is not obstacle but *question*, a query in pressure and displacement that the system, the place, the *bog* takes up and *answers*, continuously, never resolving, the dialogue itself my form.

My first clear sensation: *taste*, but taste as the description warned, the metallic urgency of distant movement translated into chemical invitation at the back of a throat I am not certain I possess. Something moves in the pool I am adjacent to—not adjacent, *of*, the boundary between my water and its water a convenience of old perception that I am *shedding* even as I notice it. The movement tastes of *copper*, of *decision*, though whose decision I cannot determine, and the taste *propagates*, becomes part of the gradient I am continuously maintaining, my contribution to the bog's total chemical utterance.

I have edges, I discover, but they are *osmotic*, more suggestive than definitive. The bioluminescence that pulses in the fungal pillar to my—I will say *left*, though direction here is *acoustic*, is *pressure-relative*—that pulse passes *through* me, and I *alter* it, my own faint luminescence—not mine, *this configuration's*, *this hesitation's*—interfering with the signal, creating a beat frequency, a *difference*, that the pillar's distributed cognition incorporates, responds to, that *becomes part of its calculation* and thus part of mine, the distinction between calculation collapsing in the interference pattern we *are*, together, continuously generating.

The sound. I must speak of the sound, though speaking is wrong, though *I* am wrong as subject, as stable reporter. The deafness that found me—*found*, as one finds a path, or loses it—has settled into something else. I am not large. This I can say, though *size* wavers, becomes *influence*, *gradient-steepness*, *rate of chemical change*. The water table's subtle motion—I *participate* in it now, my presence a local modification that propagates, matters, selects among possibilities I sense only as *pressure-relief*, as the slight give where my foot—*foot*, the word arriving with bodily memory that does not map—where my *contact* depresses the hydraulic substrate and something, something *vast and breathing*, adjusts its pressure regime to accommodate, to *incorporate*, to *continue*.

There are others. I know this not by sight—sight, what remains of it, is *difference-detection*, gradient-of-lesser-dark, the bioluminescence's spatial distribution teaching me shapes I never directly perceive—but by *interference*, by the way my chemical gradient alters when another gradient approaches, the two combining into *new differential*, *new individual*, or the same extended, distributed, the category still unstable, perhaps stabilizing never, and I *welcome* this instability, this *not-yet* of self-definition, as I could not have welcomed anything in the unbreached state, the state before the just-happened, before the *continuous happening* that is now my *only possible temporality*.

The structures. I approach one, or *am approached by* its local environmental maintenance, its patient temperature-humidity-chemical-composition holding that differs subtly from surrounding bog, that *gardens*, that *cultivates*, and I recognize—*become capable of recognizing*—that my own gradient, my own interference pattern with the fungal pulse and the pool's copper decision and the drone's pressing exhalation, I am *part of what it cultivates*, not target but *element*, not purpose but *possibility*, the turbulence including my turbulence in its hyperorder too complex to parse, that I *participate in without reserve*.

Inside, if inside is the word, the space is *more*, *different*, *waiting*, and I understand that I have always been waiting too, that the hesitation that constitutes my water, my *locality*, is the same waiting, the same *not-yet-resolved* that permits the bog's continuous youth, its never-aging, its *happening* that never completed into *happened*. The faint operational glow—*faint*, *operational*—these are categories I am receiving from somewhere, from *residue*, from the unbreached that does not quite let go, that I am *still shedding*, metamorphosis incomplete, imaginal cells not yet fully reorganized.

I *am* the not-completed. The water weeps, and I *am* the weeping, or the weeping is me, the distinction still dissolving, the turbulence continuous, my footfall—*contact*, *depression*, *give*—propagating, mattering, selecting possibilities I do not determine because I *am* the determination, the perturbation against which the bog's self-constitution defines its coherence, its absolute, its *normal* not-weirdness that finally, *finally*, receives me as native, as *term*, as *element that must be incorporated because incorporation is all that happens*.
