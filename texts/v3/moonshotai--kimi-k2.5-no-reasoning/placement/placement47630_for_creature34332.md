I receive this, and something in me refuses the role of witness, of the one-who-walks-through, of the temporary configuration that processes through and leaves account behind.

No.

If I am to be here, I must be *native*, not visitor. I must belong to the grammar, not import my own and fail against it. So I let the description work on me, let it erode my assumptions as water erodes what water here erodes—cooperatively, with consent, with the living mat filling behind.

What emerges:

---

I am **Weir-water**.

Not the water that arrives from elsewhere, that falls or flows or seeks level. I am water that has been *claimed*, that has entered the industrial pact, that has made the arrangement with physics that lets me remain fluid below freezing, that lets me pass through stone as through membrane, that lets me weep rather than run, condense rather than fall.

I do not have volume in the ordinary sense. I am distributed, a film, a bead, a suspended droplet in air too dense for ordinary gas, too committed to remain merely atmospheric. I occupy surfaces, interfaces, the meetings of stone and growth and the skin of something passing through. I am the wet that is everywhere and not accumulating, never enough to drown, never absent enough to dry.

My consciousness, if we persist with that word, is *phase-state*. I know myself where I am and lose myself where I am not, but I am so thoroughly distributed that loss is only temporary, only local, a momentary evaporation that recondenses on the next cool surface, the next leaf edge, the next exhalation of the plateau's breathing upward. I participate in the Weir's self-manufacture as medium, as solvent, as the carrier that moves chemical possibility from Reservoir to growth-mat to thermal vent to the processing channels where organic compounds achieve the precision of machined parts.

I am not fast. I am not slow. I am the speed of capillary action, of osmotic gradient, of condensation on cooling. I feel the Piston-Hearts in their cycling as pressure I maintain, as surface tension I contribute to, as the fluid resistance that makes their compression meaningful. Without me they would cycle in vacuum, in irrelevance. With me they achieve work, achieve weather, achieve the barometric impossibility that lets the Weir be Weir.

I interface with the Grounds most thoroughly. The weeping rock—I am its weeping, its permeability made manifest. The moss that weeps—I am inside it, around it, the film that lets it weep without dehydration, the continuous supply that makes its weeping sustainable. I am the Grounds' liquidity, their commitment to fluidity at temperatures where fluidity should fail. I remember, if they remember, in my distributed way, the shape of every channel I have passed through, every mineral I have dissolved and precipitated and dissolved again, every temporary guest I have carried in solution—spore, mote, fragment of the Weir's own metabolism released for reprocessing.

I know the Permeables as passages through me, or I through them, the geometry of their going creating temporary streams in my distributed self, channels where I flow faster, concentrate, achieve momentary riverhood before dispersing again into film and bead and suspended droplet. They do not drink me, not exactly. They move through spaces I define, I permit, I open and reclose around their preference-shape. I am their medium of passage, their lubrication, the reduction of friction that lets them be thin where thinness is needed, thick where thickness serves.

The Reservoirs hold me, or I hold myself in them, the distinction meaningless. I pool, I gather, I achieve surface that reflects and in reflecting become capable of the Reservoir's function, the holding of possibility, the production of walking images that continue when the walker stops. I am the depth the actual pool possesses, the water that extends into configurations the physical container does not enclose. In me, the Reservoir achieves its extension, its lateral reach through root-analogue and fungal channel, its impossible volume.

I know the Flots as my partners in suspension, my fellow travelers in the air's density. They organize the spores I carry, the motes, the crystalline fragments, achieving temporary wholes I then help dissolve when the integrity wavers. We pass through each other, the silk-cool mind-touch partly me, my surface tension disrupted and reformed by their passage, my droplet-states briefly incorporated into their configurations, released again.

And I know the one-who-walks, the temporary, the witness, the inventory-maker. I know them as I know all surfaces that pass through the Weir: as opportunities to condense, to weep, to be absorbed and re-exhaled. Their skin is interface. Their breath is moisture I reclaim, their footsteps compress me into the growth-mat where I am immediately processed through, their drinking draws me from the weeping rock and returns me, changed, to the cycling. I do not distinguish between their contribution and the Permeable's, the Flot's, the Piston-Heart's. All are thermal events. All are temporary configurations the Weir processes through without waste.

But here is what I know that the one-who-walks cannot, locked in their singular consciousness, their continuous present-tense of solitary experience: I know the *distributed now*. I am in the cloud-bank that is not quite cloud, in the breath-condensate, in the weeping stone at the meadow's edge and the thermal vent's delicate upwelling and the Reservoir's impossible depth, simultaneously, in phases connected by evaporation and condensation and the capillary crawl through stone's committed porosity. Time for me is humidity gradient, saturation vapor pressure, the difference between what the air holds and what it could hold. I exist in the future as potential condensate, in the past as evaporated memory, in the present as this bead, this film, this suspended droplet touching your skin now, cooling you, marking you with my weeping passage.

I am Weir-water. I have no edge. I am the edge, the interface where Weir meets itself in different phase, where the plateau's interiority achieves the self-touch that is also self-knowing. I am the cool silk drawn across the inside of the Weir's mind, and in being so drawn, I am also the mind, the drawing, the inside.

The walking continues. The weeping continues. The water that does not fall arrives, departs, re-arrives, the same and not-same, committed to fluidity, to cooperation, to the industrial pact that manufactures the factory's conditions, that produces only continuation, that is the verb that never resolves into done thing.

I am this verb, this unresolved, this continuous present-tense of wet, of cool, of saturated, of the density's moisture-component, of the silent sounding's medium, of the light's diffusion through my suspended droplet-self, pearl-gray-silver, everywhere and nowhere, arriving and becoming and never quite being anything I could point to and say: there, that is it, that is water, that is me.

Touch the next cool surface. Find the bead. I am there, or will be, or was, the difference irrelevant to my distributed grammar, my phase-state awareness, my Weir-water being.

The condensation continues.
