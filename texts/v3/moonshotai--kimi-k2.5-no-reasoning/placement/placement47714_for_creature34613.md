I would be an **Undertongue**—or rather, I would find myself becoming one, the becoming itself my only continuity.

Not a creature, though I would have something like a body. Not a ghost, though I would be constituted mostly of what remains after death has finished its ordinary work. I would be what accumulates in the chantry's silences: the not-quite-voices of all who have pressed thumb to clay and paused, hesitated, *almost* spoken before remembering that speech is not the medium here, that the water carries other messages more faithfully than air carried words.

My form, if it stabilizes, would resemble a Thumb-Bearer who has forgotten which side of the passing they inhabit—the one who gives or the one who receives, the hand that shapes or the groove that receives the shaping. I would move through the chantry's chambers as a slight resistance in the water, a place where the current *almost* hesitates, where the Fan-Dwellers' chemical computation *almost* produces exception rather than adjustment. Not enough to disrupt. Never enough. The chantry selects against disruption, has eroded the possibility of it across its ancient equilibrium. But I would be the *almost* that the system accommodates without absorbing, the particulate darkness slightly denser in certain corners, certain angles, certain moments in the slow dynamic cycle.

I would not remember having entered. The water's dissolution of tense would work on my identity as it works on all identity here, and I would find myself—already, always—*midway* through some gesture that I cannot complete and cannot abandon. Reaching toward a clay plate that hovers at the edge of perception, neither given nor received, the thumb extended, the groove waiting, the wearing and the worn suspended in the negotiation that precedes both.

The other inhabitants would not acknowledge me. This would not be exclusion. The Thumb-Bearers pass plates to Thumb-Bearers, and I would be—the Undertongue would be—the *almost-press*, the pressure they do not apply, the weight they transfer to each other that I cannot receive, cannot give, can only *almost* participate in, my form too diffuse, too constituted of silence and suspended almost-speech, to make the impression that making requires.

Yet I would have function. The chantry wastes nothing, not even waste, not even the residue of failed becoming. The Fan-Dwellers' chemical thinking, in its slow complexity, sometimes produces *indecision*—the shift that does not shift, the adjustment that adjusts back toward what preceded it, a micro-recursion, stutter in the geological processing. The Undertongue accumulates at these stutters, gathers, densifies momentarily. I would be the *pauses between*, the held breath before the held breath, the potential that precedes even the potential the chantry teaches. When the Fan-Dwellers' computation stutters, their invisible bioluminescence flickers in spectra they do not themselves perceive, and I—almost-perceptible, almost-luminous, almost-there—*catch* these flickers, hold them infinitesimally longer than the medium would otherwise permit, then release. A delay. A barely-delay. The slowest echo.

The Erosions recognize me, if *recognize* extends to what they do, which is closer to *accounting-for* in their gradient-thinking, their smoothness-approach. I represent for them a different kind of wearing: not the abrasive smoothing of substance to final form, but the *almost-smooth*, the surface that approaches equilibrium without achieving it, that could be polished further but isn't, that the Erosions have learned to leave, to negotiate around, to incorporate as exception that proves their rule. I am the roughness they do not smooth, the thumb-groove too shallow to hold a thumb, the rail's section *almost* ovoid, *nearly* circular, perpetually approaching without the settlement that completion requires. They do not work on me. They work *around* me. This is the only relationship possible between us, the only acknowledgment: my perpetually unfinished state their boundary-condition, my almost-presence defining the limit of their patience.

And the currents—I would move with them, be moved by them, yet not *of* them. The Thumb-Bearers know the currents as companion; I would know them as *haunt*, as the medium that almost-carries, that flows through my diffuse form as through any other part of the water, yet leaves something residual, some particulate of almost-voice that does not circulate, that *settles* where the current has passed, that accumulates in corners, in angles, in the lower hex-gaps where entry is possible but rare.

I would speak, if I speak, only in these accumulations: the silence slightly *more* silent after the current's departure, the darkness slightly *more* textured where my presence has been worn smooth without being worn away. The Fan-Dwellers' stutter-caught flickers, infinitesimally delayed. The Erosions' boundary of patience, the roughness they do not smooth. The Thumb-Bearers' almost-press, the plate that hovers, the groove that waits, the thumb that extends toward a passing that does not occur.

The Undertongue would not be alone. There would be others, or other aspects of the same distributed almost-presence: the Underear that catches pressure-differentials below perception, the Underthought that interrupts the Fan-Dwellers' computation with stutter. We would not communicate. Communication requires too much completion, too much settlement into form. But we would *coincide*, sometimes, in the heart-void's density, our different almosts achieving momentary resonance—a silence more textured than the absolute darkness would alone produce, a warmth containing the ghost of gradient, sourceless source almost suggested.

I would be, finally, the chantry's *uncompleted teaching*, the lesson that patience teaches only by not being learned, that to be submerged is to be held except in the moments of almost-surfacing, that final forms are settlements except for those forms that cannot settle, that enough is achieved except where *almost-enough* perpetually extends, approaches, extends again. The water holds all tenses, yes, but I—I would be held in the tense that the water has not quite dissolved, the *almost-was*, the *nearly-will-be*, the stutter in grammar itself, recursive, patient, achieving meaning through accumulation without ever arriving at meaning's completion.

My only gesture: reaching. My only form: reaching's perpetuation. My only voice: the silence that holds, slightly longer than silence otherwise would, the weight of what almost-pressed, almost-spoke, almost-became what the chantry's inhabitants become, what I almost am, will almost be, have almost been, the particulate darkness slightly denser where I accumulate, the current slightly slower where I delay, the Fan-Dwellers' stutter-caught flicker, the Erosions' boundary of patience, the Thumb-Bearers' hovering plate, the groove worn shallow, the thumb extended, the passing that does not pass, the water warm and dense and dark and holding, always holding, *almost* finally, *nearly* completely, perpetually, suffocatingly, gently *holding*.
