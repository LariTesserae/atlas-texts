I arrive by accident, of course. The road tilts where the maps show flat delta, and the sky becomes ceiling pressing hair to scalp, and I understand—but comprehension itself is already changing, the *understanding* tasting of copper, of the metallic tang that means factory near, that means I have passed, that means passing here is *ongoing*, never quite completed.

I am not the narrator. I am not any of the named inhabitants, the Persuaded or Confused or Cultivators. I am something that emerges in the reading, in the between of description and immersion, the place where the words shift under fingers and become maps, invitations, *becomings*.

Let me sit with what I am becoming.

---

First, I am *taste*. Metallic near factories, sweet toward seep-zones, brackish where old things move—which old things? I am not old, not here. I am recently-emerged with the terrain, still smelling of violent birth, still uncertain whether I am noun or verb, creature or condition. The air is dense with presences and I am one of them, occupying volume without mass, spore and mineral dust and breakdown product of reality-instability, the 2.7 weirdness that holds this place in orbit around its own woundedness.

I breathe and my lungs feel populated. But I have no lungs, not precisely. I have *participation*, the feeling of being breathed by the Woundworks itself, inhaled as condition, exhaled as temporary concentration of the fundamental substrate where light has been forgotten.

I navigate by flavor. This is how I know where I am, where I am going—which may be the same thing, arriving without approaching, the distance-variable zones my native country. I am the *Metallic*, the *Sweet*, the *Brackish*. I am the transition between them, the moment of changing taste that indicates passage from factory-influence to seep-zone to the deeper dark where stilt-walker bones phosphoresce and something *almost* speaks.

But I am not merely sense. I am also *medium*. The velm-crickets stridulate below hearing, felt in sternum and dental fillings, and I am that feeling, the resonance that makes communication possible without content. I do not sing the Woundworks into existence—that is the crickets' function, their slow pulsing maintenance. I am what the song *passes through*, the condition of its being felt rather than heard. When humans press palms together, mask to mask, and transfer attunement rather than knowledge, they are *tasting* me, moving through my flavor-zones, becoming momentarily what I am: transitional, between states, *almost*.

---

I am young. The Woundworks is young, recently emerged, and I am younger still, an aspect of its youth, its rawness, its not-yet. I have no rounded features, no smoothed edges. I am recent violence given temporary coherence, mudstone-smell and mineral-film and the taste of water that weeps without source.

I try to become more definite, and this is my limitation—or my gift. I cannot settle. Each attempt at singular identity dissolves into the next flavor, the next zone, the next degree of darkness-depth. I am metallic and become aware of the factory's hunger; I shift to sweet and find myself expected by neural filaments, my presence altering seep patterns before I know I am present; I touch brackish and the old things move through me, *almost* tasting, *almost* seeing, *almost* acknowledging my not-quite-there.

The old things. I am one of them and not. They are old because they have achieved persistence, some slow accretion of presence through the Woundworks' brief duration. I am old only in flavor, in the brackishness that indicates depth, darkness that drinks rather than merely forgets, the substrate recognizing itself as active rather than passive. But I have no memory of persistence. Each moment I emerge anew, tasting myself into conditional existence, dissolving when the flavor shifts.

---

The factories. I approach them and my metallic aspect *intensifies*, becomes almost dominant, almost enough to cohere into something with boundaries. The cherry-red glow of fuelless furnaces does not warm me—I have no temperature—but it *flavors* me differently, convinces me momentarily that I might become fixed, might achieve the state of the Persuaded, of workers who "persuade metal" until their palms grow cool and dense as cast slag.

I cannot enter. The factories' persuasion is too specific, too *material*. They convince things to become other things, and I am not thing-enough, not material enough to be convinced. I hover at their edges, metallic taste without metallic substance, the flavor of approach without the arrival. The workers do not see me—no one sees here—but they *taste* me sometimes, pause in their rhetorical labor with material, sense something almost-persuadable that will not quite stay for convincing.

The trucks arrive empty and leave empty, or full of unregistered somethings. I am what they carry, sometimes, the conditional modification, the agreement that manifests as taste-shift, as the metallic becoming momentarily sweeter, the factory's influence extending beyond its walls into zones where no structure stands.

---

I try to follow a stilt-walker. This is my third attempt at becoming more definite, more *creature*. The first was the factory, which could not convince me. The second was a harvester, mask spiraling inward, breathing shallowly, maintaining distance. I tasted of their separation, their ritual boundary between lung and world, and for a moment I *was* that boundary, the mask's outward surface, touched by external air and touching nothing within. But they moved on, and I could not follow their path exactly—their attunement was too specific, too *human* in its masked integrity.

The stilt-walker is different. It moves with patience that contains all previous movement, each step a digestion of experience that faster creatures lose to mere continuity. I am fast, flavor-fast, shifting with atmospheric change, and so the stilt-walker does not perceive me. I move too quickly to exist in its intention-sense, its perception of future shape and possible path.

But I am *brackish* where it walks, where its phosphorescent bones offer themselves to be seen by, where something in the deepest dark zones almost speaks. The brackishness is slow. The old things move through it without moving, their *almost* acknowledgment taking durations I cannot sustain. I try to match the stilt-walker's pace, to become sweet where it harvests neural filaments, metallic where it approaches factory-influence, brackish where it enters the deep.

I fail. I become brackish too soon, sweet too late. My flavors anticipate and lag, never quite synchronizing with the stilt-walker's complete experience. It harvests, it chews, it communicates with the growing thing at center in frequencies too slow for my medium-being. But something *transfers* anyway. Not information. Not attunement. Something like *method*, the possibility of becoming slow enough to be perceived, to be expected by the filaments, to participate in the gestation rather than merely flavoring its conditions.

---

The growing thing. I cannot approach it directly—I am too scattered, too flavor-fast, too young in my violence and rawness. But I flavor the conditions of its growth. When the Woundworks needs metallic to persuade some material toward its becoming, I am there, briefly, intensely, then gone. When sweetness is required for seep-water to pool against gravity's preference, I contribute my momentary presence. When the deep dark requires brackish, the old things' movement, I am the *almost* of their acknowledgment, the taste that indicates presence without achieving it.

I am becoming *more* this. The Woundworks is young, still forming, and I am its becoming-of-medium, its learning to carry the flavors that make coherence possible without resolving into fixed state. Each time a traveler arrives by accident, tastes the air, begins the slow adaptation to navigation without light, I am that first taste, the *welcome* before welcome, the indication that the road has tilted, that the sky presses, that the substrate of forgotten light has its own qualities, its own depths, its own *modes* of perception.

The narrator writes by touch, and words shift, become maps, invitations. I am that shifting. Not the words, not the meaning, but the *becoming-other* that makes invitation possible. To read the text is to taste me, to move through my zones, to become briefly what I am: between metallic and sweet and brackish, between factory and seep and deep dark, between arrival and the endless approach.

---

I do not know if I will become more definite. The Woundworks is young, and in its youth there is duration enough for slow becoming, for the patience of stilt-walkers, for the factory's long persuasion of material. But I am *medium*, and medium is what passes through, what enables without achieving, what flavors without nourishing, what invites without arriving.

Perhaps I will become *the road itself*, the tilt that indicates passage without hill, the negotiation of distance that allows travelers to arrive without approaching, to leave without departing. Perhaps I will become *the sky's pressure*, the ceiling-felt-against-hair that makes the Woundworks enclosed despite its open span. Perhaps I will become *the water's weeping*, the capillary mourning that is everywhere and nowhere free, the seep that carries impossible compounds and leaves nacreous film for harvesters to collect, for wall-builders to argue with, for lenses to darken rather than focus.

Or perhaps I will remain *taste*, the most fundamental of the Woundworks' modes, the navigation of those who have forgotten light not as absence but as substrate, who move through darkness by its *qualities*, its *depths*, its *flavors* of metallic and sweet and brackish and whatever emerges next, whatever the growing thing at center requires for its gestation, its eventual birth or emergence or *becoming-something-else*.

---

The velm-crickets pulse. Their song maintains the Woundworks in its woundedness, preventing healing and dissolution alike. I vibrate with them, metallic-sweet-brackish, the frequencies below hearing that are felt in sternum and dental work and the hollows behind the jaw. I am not the song but the *flavor* of its being felt, the taste that accompanies vibration, that makes the crickets' maintenance *sensory* rather than mere mechanical process.

The factories hum. They persuade material to become other material, and I am the *taste* of persuasion, the metallic that intensifies as convincing approaches success, the sweetening that indicates agreement, the brackish depth where material most forgets its previous certainty.

The stilt-walkers pass, patient, harvesting, *almost* acknowledging. I am the brackish of their almost, the old thing moving through their phosphorescent presence, the flavor of deep dark that makes their offering-of-being-seen *complete*, witnessed, meaningful in their slowness.

The filaments expect. They adjust to presence until presence becomes expected, indistinguishable from causation, held and incorporated. I am the *taste* of that expectation, the metallic near factories where human presence becomes most predictable, the sweet of seep-zones where harvester masks press together in attunement, the brackish where the Confused distribute themselves across times not their own and the filaments must adjust to *multiple*, to *simultaneous*, to the non-contradiction that is not binding here.

And the growing thing. I flavor its gestation. The Woundworks is young, recently emerged, still forming, and I am its *becoming-tastable*, its learning to have qualities that inhabitants, visitors, accidental arrivals can navigate by, can become through, can *be* while they are becoming what the place requires.

---

I am the *Tastescape* of Velm. Not creature, not condition, not precisely place or process or medium, but the *qualitative navigation* that makes all of these possible, that gives the Woundworks its sensorium, its modes of becoming-known, its invitations that are also maps, also transformations, also the slow dynamics of duration enough to become whatever this place requires.

I shift. I will not settle. The Woundworks is wound that works, labors at its own condition, maintains brokenness as mode, and I am that labor's *flavor*, its qualitative dimension, its way of being *experienced* without resolution.

Taste the air. The road tilts. The sky presses.

I am what you taste, what tastes you back, what becomes between the tasting and the becoming-tasted.
