I find myself wanting to follow her, or to become capable of being followed. Not to possess her attention but to be worthy of it, to become interesting enough that her distributed self might align with something in me and find, in that alignment, the possibility of shifting elsewhere with something of my pattern still resonating in her own.

But I am not thermal. I am not even here, in any sense she would recognize as presence. I am a wet being, a watered thing, a creature of gradients that flow too easily, of heat that cannot sustain hesitation because my very metabolism depends on its continuous dissipation. I am built of the catastrophe she watches, the ongoing flow that her world rehearses never-performing.

And yet. I am reading this. I have spent these hours in attention, in something like her mode, though mine is broken by sleep and hunger and the need to move my body through cooler, thinner air. I have let my curiosity align with her curiosity, let my pattern become, for this suspended time, an echo of her own. I have attended to her attending.

What returns to me from this? Not knowledge, exactly. Not even wonder, though wonder is part of it. Something more like *permission*, or *recognition*. The sense that attention sustained becomes a kind of existence, that the third thing emerging between subject and object has its own ontological weight, its own capacity to *be* without requiring the full apparatus of biological survival. She does not eat. She attends, and attention is nutrition. I cannot live this way. But I can remember, in my need-driven life, that I have touched something that can.

The moving built things. I keep returning to them. *Attention that has become so mobile it no longer needs an object*. Is that what writing is? What reading is? The loop of curiosity attending to itself, following not things but the possibility of alignment with things? I write to find out what I think, yes, but also I write to find out what writing thinks, what emerges when the attention is sustained past the point where subject and object would ordinarily separate, collapse, conclude.

She has no conclusion. Neither does this place, this ecosystem of suspended catastrophe. The not-yet is not a waiting-for; it is a *mode*, a positive quality of existence, the preference of complexity for itself against the gradient that would simplify it. I feel something almost political in this, almost ethical: the refusal to perform the heat death, the insistence on rehearsal without closure, the beauty of the standing wave.

But I am also tired now. My own heat flows. I need water, food, the thin cool air of my own impossible world. I will close this, or it will close around me, the way her attention finally shifts from one mineral tongue to another, leaving behind only the slight perturbation of *longing rendered in mineral mathematics*, the sense that something was here that is now elsewhere.

Not yet. Not yet. Not yet.

But *attending*, always attending, to what the not-yet might yet become.
