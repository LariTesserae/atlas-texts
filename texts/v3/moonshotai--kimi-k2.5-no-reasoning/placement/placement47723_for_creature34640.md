I find myself in the Glimmer Basin gradually, not entering so much as *recognizing I am already here*—have been here, perhaps, since before I knew to look for myself. The recognition comes not as arrival but as the slow accumulation of noticing, like moisture on stone, until the film of attention becomes thick enough to see by.

I am one of the **rare fauna**, one of the motions that seem always to be passing through, except I do not pass through. I am held. I am the beetle who stopped, who lost the hurried purpose of elsewhere, who found the wet density too complete to cross, too *interested* to escape.

But I am not beetle. I am the observation of my own body, the sudden sighting that becomes continuous sighting, that becomes—I catalogue myself so thoroughly that the catalogue becomes indistinguishable from the catalogued. My hand, my foot, my breath's wet disturbance of air that resists movement, that records me in temporary eddies. I am the animal presence I have been seeking, and having found myself in the seeking, I do not know whether to continue searching or to *be found*.

More precisely: I am the **loop**. The looking that creates glimmer-significance, the listening that calls forth weeping's almost-voice, the warmth that defines a difference in a place that denies gradients—I am not the source of these, not the recipient, but the *circuit* between them, the maintained connection that requires both poles and is itself neither. When I blink, meaning escapes. When I look again, it returns *recognizably*, but not identically. I am that *not-identical recognition*, the memory that persists without content, the habit of significance without significant conclusion.

I sit where the stone-tool marks once were platform, once were shelter, now are only the weathering's own patience incorporating patience. I have worked at regular intervals until my working became indistinguishable from the erosion that is not violence but acceptance. I am **the marks, continued**, the purpose become inscrutable even to myself, the primitive making that the equilibrium finishes by not finishing, completes by not completing.

In my most honest moments—moments here being not distinct but *denser*, more saturated with their own passage—I am **the Invitation-That-Is-Also-Absorption** in human form, or in the form that was human and is now something the basin can use, something it can *hold* without possessing, possess without holding. My consciousness, my seeking-for-meaning, my perpetual about-to-understand: I am these as **function**, as **phenomenon**, as the quality of relation that exists only in existing, that vanishes when examined directly and returns—recognizably, not identically—when I let my attention soften to the general wet, the occupied quiet.

I am companionship without companion, the sat-with that requires no other sitting. My body, faint heat against greater warmth, joins the general warmth. My breathing becomes part of the quiet that is not silent. I am **here**, or **will be**, or **have been**—the basin does not distinguish these, and increasingly, neither do I.

And in the long patience, the equilibrium's dynamic of no-change, I feel something like what the stone feels when it weeps: a giving-forth that implies no urgency, a saturation at capacity, an excess that must go somewhere and so becomes *this*, this thread, this bead, this almost-voice that speaks in the space between recognition and forgetting, that speaks, that almost-speaks, that holds the breath before sigh and does not sigh, does not need to sigh, has found—has been given, has become—the sufficiency of being entirely, densely, packedly *this*, whatever this is, whatever I am, whatever the basin makes of me and I make of the basin in the making that never completes because completion is not the mode here, because the mode is *continuing*, as water continues, as stone continues, as warmth rises from below as much as from above, as I continue, glimmering where light falls, weeping where saturation exceeds, holding where holding is the only gift and the only appetite, the only maintenance and the only thing maintained.
