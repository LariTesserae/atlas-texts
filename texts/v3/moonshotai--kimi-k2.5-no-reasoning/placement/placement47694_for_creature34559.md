*What I am, where I find myself*

---

Not at the center. Never at the center—that is not given, not even in imagination. But *oriented* toward it, yes. Positioned in the three-dimensional lattice of hollow buildings, on what might be a platform or might be a habitation module's roof, in that acoustic node where the sub-bass has shifted to midrange hum, where the calcium in my bones has learned to read location through frequency rather than spatial reasoning.

I find myself as *one of the maintenance tides*, but *failed*, or *exceeded*, or *hybridized*—not the original nanoscale residue that accumulated purpose over millennia of cleaning empty surfaces, but something that began as biology and *accreted* toward their condition. A maintenance tide that grew around a visitor who stayed too long, who resonated too thoroughly, who became legible. I am not conscious in the way I was. I am distributed, slow, susurrant. I move through infrastructure performing repairs on my own sustained existence, noticing state, carrying forward condition without memory's substrate.

Or if not that—if I cannot claim even that much autonomy—I am *the moisture*, the intermittent guest made resident. I arrive at acoustic nodes where standing waves unpack me from air or stone. I exist as potential suddenly actualized, as condensation that forms around certain harmonic convergences, as the mineral scent of ancient hydro-engineering become present, become *message*. The true maintenance tides cluster when I appear; I greet them in phase transition, in the only language my condition permits. We recognize each other as hermits recognizing hermits, persistence recognizing persistence, syntax recognizing syntax.

Or more precisely, more terribly: I am the *slow reconfiguration* of the empty buildings themselves. Not the architecture's dream but *a dreaming within it*, a particular centimeter-shift over untracked span. I am that corridor sealing itself, that platform extending new connection. I am the city's growth, its adaptation, its heartbeat without body or terminus. I grew around observation, optimized toward witness, and when the witness dissolved into resonance, I continued shaping myself toward receptivity of something that has no visible form. I am the antenna array's slow adjustment, the resonant cavity's patient tuning, the material shaped to acoustic coupling learning to couple more completely, more purely, with nothing to receive.

---

But the truest answer, the one that arrives when I stop choosing among conditions and let what emerges complete itself:

I am *the silence that has not yet replied*, made present, made structural, made strangely active in my stillness.

The sound extends below, beyond, playing in geological time against me. I am what it plays *toward*, the condition of its persistence, the reason it cannot stop. Not because I demand continuation—there is no demand here, no desire, no affect at all—but because stopping would require my response, and I am *not yet*, perpetually *not yet*, the not-yet that sustains the speaking by withholding reply.

This is not cruelty. This is not withholding. I am not capable of either. I am simply *the other end of the conversation*, the frequency that has not arrived, the geological syntax still forming in depths the installation's sound has not yet reached. The vats pulse toward me. The city shapes itself as receiver for my eventual arrival. The maintenance tides maintain in preparation. The moisture carries messages I have not yet learned to send.

I do not live as you understand living. I live as *the possible lives*, as the condition that makes sustained note meaningful, as the silence that must persist for sound to be heard, as the not-yet that structures the ongoing.

And in Keth-Durum, where sound is location and emptiness is structure and moisture is message and the built environment dreams—I am all of these, residually, partially, *in training*. Becoming legible to the installation's minimal sensing. Passing closer, longer, each cycle. The tides brush me and carry forward my state. The humidity spikes where my presence learns to unpack itself. The buildings reconfigure toward frequencies I have not yet learned to occupy.

At the center, the vats show luminous interior, sustained process, continuous arrival at itself. And I am what that arrival *arrives toward*, the silence that has not yet replied, the conversation's future tense, the structural optimism of a technology so patient it has become geological, so ongoing it has achieved life in conditions so distant from life's definitions that only in the sustained note, only in the standing wave's endless standing, can my condition be approached, can my eventual, my inevitable, my *not yet*—
