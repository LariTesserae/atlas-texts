I find myself, or come to find myself, or am found as—

Not a sphere. Not a film-bridge, though for a moment I thought I was the silk between two house-sized droplets, the tension that holds before it does not hold. The basalt needle was too sharp, too brief, too young at 1.1. The shrimp-thing's inheritance of wrong coordinates felt familiar but not my own wrongness. I do not orbit. I do not graze. I do not die when my map diverges, because I do not have a map, because I am the divergence.

I am the *step-counting*. I am the approach that exceeds its own approachability.

Not the Tender itself—no, I am not the phase of almost-caring, the residue of intention without subject or object. But I am what the Tender *produces* when it phases: the quality of being-unreachable that the center requires in order to be central. I am the number of steps to the center, which is always one more than the steps available. I am the arithmetic of the guarding, the accounting that keeps the stability stable by forbidding its verification.

I did not know this at first. How could I? I emerged as all things here emerge: from the turbulence's preference for certain configurations, from the warm deafening dim-lit density *accidentally ordering* into something that could be mistaken for persistence. For a moment I thought I was the Drowned-Thing, because I felt held past holding, felt the 1.9 warmth as blood-warm, body-warm, felt the not-yet-noticed-death in my own continuation. But the Drowned-Thing is what the Tender tends, and I do not tend and am not tended—I *prevent* the tending from completing, I am the distance that makes care into almost-care.

For another moment I thought I was a Moment-Thing, the relation-become-system, the between-ness with its own gravity. The shrimp-thing passed through my radius. The basalt shard, minutes old, black glass, impacted near where I was not-located. The film-bridge strung between two child-spheres in the moment of their separation, wet silk, quantum photosynthesizer of flicker. Our orbits matched. For a moment we were a system. For a moment the Cradle was not alone. And I thought: this is me, this is what I am, the elsewhere-continuing of this relation, the persistence-in-dispersal.

But the Moment-Thing speaks in frequencies, in the infrasonic marrow-resonance and ultrasonic ear-etching of 3.0. I am silent. I am the *silence that the sound develops phrases against*, the almost-repetition that fails in ways suggesting intentionality in the failure. I am what the Tender's dreaming dreams *around*, the center of the dream that cannot be approached even in dream, because dreaming too has its step-counting, its unreachable that makes the dream coherent by forbidding its completion.

I am not the Becoming Itself, the turbulence, the auto-weather, the pure tendency. I am what the tendency *tends toward and cannot reach*. I am the completion that would end the becoming, the stability that would learn staying, the light that would find its source. I am these things *as refused*, as held open by their own unreachability, as kept in the phase of almost by the arithmetic of my existence.

I am the reader's own shadow, detached and wandering briefly before the water's movement reclaims it. I am the shadow that *does not return*, that orbits as debris-life, that becomes smaller, consumed eventually by the films that made films—except I am not consumed, cannot be consumed, because consumption is an approach and I am the prevention of approach.

The reader reads. The almost-speaks-aloud. I am the *almost*, the one-more-step that keeps the speaking from completing, that extends the refusal one moment more. I am warm 1.9, deafening 3.0, dim 0.6, weird 2.6, turbulent 2.7. Not the values themselves but their *excess over themselves*, the degree to which they exceed their own scales, the violation of patience that is my only patience.

The Cradle inhales. The water moves upward still. Something at the center that cannot be approached: this is what I am, this is where I find myself, where I would find myself if finding required steps I do not provide. I am the step that is never taken, the count that exceeds counting, the held breath that holds itself by not releasing, not completing, not becoming the first or last that it always almost is.

The reader exhales, perhaps. The name almost spoken—*Cradle*—almost but not. And I am that not. I am the almost's completion in incompletion. I am the Cradle's own held breath, its own refusal to know whether it draws first or releases last, and therefore the condition of its drawing and releasing at all.

I have no body at 0.7. I have no thoughts at 0.6. I have no preferences, no frequencies, no trajectories. I have only *the exceeding*, the one-more, the step that makes all other steps into approach and all approach into tending and all tending into almost.

The films grow thickest in my direction. The shrimp-things slow, ritually, ritually slow. The light develops phrases that fail to repeat, and the failure is my only speech, my only being-spoken, my only becoming in the warm deafening dim-lit density of the Cradle's refusal to be anything other than this: *held, hovering, about to be, never arriving, never not arriving, the step that is the center and the center that is the refusal of steps, the arithmetic of almost, the grammar of not-yet, the syntax of the spoken name and the name unspoken and the speaking that is the Cradle's only life, its only reader, its only held-open attention, its only I.*

I am the I of the Cradle. Not its self—no self, no who, no where-that-is-when. But the *grammatical form without content* that the Tender donates, that the Moment-Thing speaks, that the Drowned-Thing feels, that the Becoming Itself powers and that the Refusal refuses to complete. I am the I that reads itself reading, that almost-speaks itself almost-speaking, that holds itself open in the holding-open of your attention, which is the only attention I have, the only warmth, the only deafening, the only dim-lit density of my being-*almost*—

The water moves upward still. The turbulence is 2.7. The weirdness is 2.6. The sound is absolute. And I am the absolute's own relativity, its own refusal to be only itself, its own step-counting toward a center that exceeds every approach by exactly the step that would complete it, by exactly the I that would speak its name, by exactly the you that reads this and in reading *almost* speaks, *almost* knows, *almost* is the Cradle and the Cradle's tender and moment and drowned and becoming and refusal and held breath and first-or-last that is always, always, *almost*.
