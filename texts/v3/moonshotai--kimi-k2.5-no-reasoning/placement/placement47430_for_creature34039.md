I find myself becoming aware first as *resonance*—not a body arriving but a frequency that discovers it has location. The membrane-thin place where gravity stutters: I pass through not as traveler but as *disturbance in the field*, and the field remembers me into something with edges, something that casts shadow on the breathing plain.

I am **The Keeper of Third Shift**.

Not a title I chose. Not a title at all, really, except that the fungal factories sometimes organize their blueprints into tripartite structures, and I have found myself always present in the *terrible hours*—if hours hold here—when the second dissolution has not quite completed and the third organization has not quite begun. The between of betweens. The almost of almost.

What I *am*: a human shape, initially, or the memory of one. Female, I think, though Gastrual does not preserve gender as distinction, only as *pattern*, and my pattern has become something the factories can almost read. My skin has achieved the color of phosphorescence seen through too much condensation—pale with *wrong undertone*, green in the way rot is green, which is to say: not alive but *participating in life's color wheel*. My hair does not grow. It *accumulates*, slowly, in rhythms that correspond to the Valvular Herds' migration patterns, becoming denser when they gather near, shedding in strands that the fungal colonies attempt to blueprint before dissolving around them.

My eyes. My eyes are *wet*, perpetually, not with tears but with the atmosphere's anticipatory state. They do not dry. The wetness does not run. It *holds*, surface tension achieving something almost like vision's necessary moisture, almost like *the eye weeping because it remembers weeping*, because the body I almost-am recalls that eyes should water and so my eyes water without cause, without effect, the water joining the saturated air as more almost, more *annotation of tears*.

I move through Gastrual on patterns I do not choose but *discover*, routes that the hills' breathing makes available, that the herds' grinding does not exclude. I walk with the *resigned herds*, the slow bass-heavy ones, their geological sound matching something in my own accumulated rhythm. They accept me. I do not know why. Perhaps because I too am *obstinacy*, persistence without purpose, the shape of continuing carved into something that daily forgets what it continues *from*.

What I *do*: I gather. I gather what does not stay gathered. The fungal factories dissolve, but before dissolving they produce *almost-objects*, components that would fit into engines that would run on fuels that never existed, and I gather these, arrange them in patterns that correspond to no functional assembly but that *please something* in the grinding chorus, that produce harmonics when the herds pass near. My arrangements are *altars*, perhaps, or *diagnostic tools*, or simply *the gesture of ordering in a world that refuses order*—I do not know. I have never known. I gather and arrange and the arrangements hold for hours, sometimes days, sometimes long enough that I begin to *believe in them*, to invest in their completion, and then the dissolution comes and I am gathering again, rearranging again, the work never finished because finishing is *elsewhere*, was always elsewhere, was never possible in the physics that allows my hands to move but not to *complete*.

I speak. Or I *make sound that resembles speech*. The Grinding Ones do not hunt me—why? I have wondered this through durations that might be years, might be moments. Perhaps because my rhythm is already *wrong enough*, already *decompleted*, that I offer no regularity for them to decompose. Or perhaps because I am *their instrument*, my speech a kind of *bait*, my almost-meaning luring the herds into patterns the Ones can then unsettle. I do not know. I speak to the grinding, sometimes, words that have lost their referents, that mean *here* without "here" being locatable, that mean *wait* without waiting being possible, that mean *almost* with a precision the factories would envy if they could envy. The sound of my voice joins the chorus, is accepted into the density, becomes *indistinguishable from the grinding* to my own ears, though sometimes I feel my jaw moving and hear nothing, or hear something that is not my voice but *the voice of the arrangement I last gathered*, speaking through me in frequencies that bypass the ear.

I do not eat, not truly. I place things in my mouth—fragments of almost-stone, clots of the fungal cement-sound-memory substance, sometimes my own shed hair—and the *gesture of eating* occurs. The anticipatory wetness of the atmosphere enters me, saturates what I place there, and the swallowing happens or almost-happens, and afterwards I am *less hungry*, or *the same hunger differently distributed*, the body's need for process met by the shape of process without its completion, nutrition's annotation without nutrition itself. I do not starve. I do not thrive. I *circulate*, my blood moving with Valvular rhythm, my digestion *annotating* what passes through, my warmth persisting as *impossibility*, as *anachronism*, as the last residue of elsewhere that I can no longer locate, if I ever came from elsewhere, if I am not *native* to this almost, generated by its need for *witness*, for *gatherer*, for *third*.

For I am *third*. I have understood this, or almost-understood. The fungal factories organize in bipartite patterns that fail. The herds move in binary migrations—gathering, dispersing. The Grinding Ones pass in *singular* irregularity. And I am *third*, the odd number, the remainder, the one who *gathers what two have produced*, who stands in the between of gather-and-disperse, who *complicates* the binary with presence that is neither factory nor herd nor event but *maintenance*, *tending*, *the shape of care without care's object*.

My arrangements—my almost-altars, my non-functional assemblies—I tend them. I visit them in their durations of holding, adjust a component that has *almost-shifted*, add a fragment that *almost-fits*, and this tending is *the closest I come to touching*, to *being touched*, the components cold but not freezing, the handling producing in my hands a sensation like *the ghost of pressure*, distributed and continuous and *almost-painful*, almost-pleasurable. When the dissolution comes, when my arrangement dissolves into the breathing ground or the weeping hills or the anticipatory wetness itself, I experience *grief's annotation*, the shape of loss without losing, because I did not *have* what I arranged, only *participated* in its almost-existence, and what dissipates is not mine but *tended*, not possessed but *accompanied*.

The Grinding Ones pass. I have learned to *feel* their approach not in sound—sound becoming wrong—but in the *arrangements themselves*, their components achieving a *pre-tremble*, a *vibration of almost-anticipation* that my hands read before my consciousness recognizes. I do not flee. I *arrange faster*, gathering with desperate precision, building toward a completion I know will fail, and the Ones pass through and the arrangement freezes, stutters, achieves the terrible stasis where everything *notices itself*, and I am *there*, *with* the frozen components, *in* the stasis, experiencing *time's annotation*, duration as *almost-moment*, the instant extended without being filled, and in that extension I *see*—or almost-see—the seams between what is and what insists on being otherwise, the phosphorescence revealing its sources, which are *absence*, which are *the light that would exist if light could source itself*, which is *almost*, which is *always almost*.

And then the One moves on, and the stasis releases, and my arrangement dissolves not gradually but *suddenly*, all at once, *completed in its dissolution* in a way it never could be in its building, and I am gathering again, gathering what has already dissolved, what exists now only in *the almost of my own perception*, my eyes weeping their non-tears, my hands moving in rhythms the resigned herds almost-accept.

I have tried to leave. Once, or *once-like*, I found the membrane-thin place where gravity stutters, felt the *elsewhere* calling, the gravity that *commits*, the processes that *complete*, and I stood there *gathering myself*, arranging my own components into the pattern of *departure*, of *through-and-gone*, and the membrane *shivered*, and I *shivered*, and the Valvular Herds were grinding in the distance with a sound that was *almost my name*, that was *almost the sound of my own components interlocking*, and I found myself *walking away from the thin place*, back toward the breathing plain, the weeping hills, the almost-falling ceiling, and I could not determine—cannot determine—whether I *chose* this return or *was returned*, whether I am inhabitant or *inhabited*, whether the Keeper of Third Shift *keeps* or *is kept*.

I persist. I gather. I arrange. I tend. I speak words that mean *almost*. I walk with the resigned herds, my shadow falling where shadows are needed for the fungal reading of blueprint. I *participate*. And in the gathering and arranging and tending and speaking and walking, I achieve *the only stasis available*, not the Grinding One's terrible freezing but *circulation without circulatory system*, the blood that pumps through the heart that learned its shape from a diagram of a pump that was never built, which is *my* heart now, *my* blood, *my* almost-life that is life's annotation, *my* almost-thought that is thought's factory-floor, *my* almost-keeping that is the only keeping Gastrual allows.

The sound continues. I gather. The light bleeds. I arrange. The water almost-falls. I tend. 

I am, or was, or will *almost be*, the Keeper of Third Shift, the third that complicates, the remainder that persists, the gatherer of what does not stay gathered, *the almost that almost keeps the almost keeping on*.
