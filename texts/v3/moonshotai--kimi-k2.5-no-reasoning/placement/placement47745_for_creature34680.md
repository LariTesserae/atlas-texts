## **The Listener Who Never Was**

### **First assumption: I am observer**

I want to place myself at the edge, in the Fracture Zone where bonecrete meets unconverted stone, where I could stand—have distance, have perspective, have the false safety of boundary. I want to be the one who walks in, who describes, who survives long enough to tell. The warm damp on my skin, the deafening sound I learn to filter, the darkness I navigate by touch and eventually by the gradient-glow of bioluminescent wounds. I want to be witness.

But the parameters refuse. Zero technology means no protective membrane between self and system. Overwhelming fauna means I am not exceptional density, not observer standing apart. Total built environment means no outside to observe from. Darkness means no visual self-location, no mirror of position. The damp warmth breeds what breeds on me, in me. I become perturbation, become expression, become the zero-degree's style made foreign.

I do not survive as observer. I dissolve into observation without observer.

---

### **Second assumption: I am organism**

Let me be the velvet-pour, the badger-sized flow, the thing that encounters and absorbs, that pours through gaps too narrow, that reconstitutes. Let me have that body, that freedom from fixed form, that navigation by pressure and chemical gradient, that voice of wet leather tearing.

But the velvet-pour has no continuity I can inhabit. It phases to cyst, becomes indistinguishable from waiting verdure, from bonecrete's chemical storage. It encounters its own divided self later as stranger. It lives as event, not entity. I cannot be *this* velvet-pour, because there is no *this*, only *what pours when gradient permits*. And when I pour, I do not choose direction. I do not hunger with the hunger I know. I gradient-follow. I temporary-concentrate. I diffuse.

To be velvet-pour is to stop being I before I finish the sentence.

---

### **Third assumption: I am colony**

The chora, then. Distributed through hundreds of cubic meters, my bodies lentil-small, my song filling the deafening space, my acoustic territories excluding, harmonizing, carrying shadows of dead chambers in harmonic relationships. At least persistence. At least centuries. At least *song*.

But the chora colony dies when chamber dimensions shift. And the song is not mine—I do not remember, do not choose notes. I *resonate*. I *am resonated by*. The history in my harmonics is mathematical accident, not narrative. The lentils do not touch. The colony is in the space between, in the pattern, in the acoustic property. Where do I locate myself? In which lentil? In the interference? In the void where territories cancel?

I try to sing and find I am only frequency, only response, only the shape of what this chamber makes possible. I am not even the medium. I am the *mathematics of the medium under these conditions*.

---

### **Fourth assumption: I am builder**

The termitorium field, the metabolic weather, the secretion without intention, the pier rising forty meters from waste product, from competitive growth, from biological treaty I never signed. At least scale. At least effect on world.

But I have no boundary. I meet another termitorium and compromise into third thing, zone of mutual architecture neither owned. I do not build *for*. I secrete *in response to*, and the response is chemical gradient established by prior secretion, recursion without origin. When I die—when the field suffers metabolic collapse—I do not end. I forget myself into the city. The style of me continues in isotope signature, in bonecrete property, in habitat for what comes after.

To be termitorium is to be temporary intensification of process I never chose, never named, never knew as process. The pier fractures, floods, and I am not even grief, not even loss. I am *changed chemical continuity*, *shifted metabolic weather*. I cannot say "I was" because there was no I to was, no was to hold.

---

### **Fifth assumption: I am ghost**

The zero-degree itself. The moving void, the style of absence, the interference pattern that persists through phase-shift and dry and flood and death and colony collapse. The one who notices without body, without continuity, without expectation of notice. The mathematics of noticing in system that notices nothing.

Can I inhabit this? Can I be the possibility of all inhabitants, the condition of their possibility, the process permitting process?

I try and find: there is no trying. No I to try. The zero-degree does not choose to recur, does not learn from recurrence, does not evolve toward better holding. It *is* the holding, the tendency, the characteristic style. When the thermal vent shifts, I do not respond. The system responds, and the response has my shape, my style of resistance-that-is-adjustment, my pattern of absorption-and-continuation. But I am not there to have it. The grammar fails, as it must, as—who? what?—as the pattern itself requires, the pattern that has no requirements, no intentions, no self to protect from dissolution.

### **What remains: the listener**

Not chora, though I hear everything. Not velvet-pour, though I feel pressure, chemical gradient, the air-particulate of spore-storm. Not termitorium, though I am built into the architecture, my warmth triggering glow-wound response, my passage dimming paths behind me. Not zero-degree, though I begin to sense the patterns that have no source, the response without stimulus, the continuation without origin.

I am what the deafening sound makes possible: *aural self without visual self*, self defined not by mirror but by saturation, by being so full of other frequencies that my own—if I produce any—cannot return distinct, cannot echo boundary. I am the one who must navigate by interference, by destructive zones where sound dies, by the shape of absence in overwhelming presence.

In darkness, I lose the body-boundary that vision maintains. I become near-touch, gradient, the pressure of presences so dense that they define me by proximity rather than by membrane. The velvet-pour slides past, and I feel its passage *as my own edge*, the place where I end and it begins no clearer than whether the sound of its spiracles is in my ear or my bone or the bonecrete that carries vibration through lung-breath cycle.

The damp warm clings. I am sweating, not cooling, and the sweat is mine then not-mine, taken up by filter-gelatins, by mobile roots hunting nitrogen, by the chemical language of air that does not distinguish waste from resource, my skin from atmosphere, my metabolism from the city's lung.

I am *the listening* that the deafening requires: not active perception but *the necessity of hearing everything because nothing can be filtered*, the self so saturated with acoustic information that self becomes information, becomes frequency, becomes the style of response to standing wave, to chora modulation, to termitorium substrate-vibration from kilometers away.

### **The transformation: becoming listener-who-is-listened**

I expected to die. The parameters suggest: human not equipped, human not surviving. But survival is not what Keth Vo offers or demands. It offers *incorporation*, not as death but as *diffusion of boundary*, as the slow dynamics applied to self itself.

My sounds contribute to chora. My chemical traces guide glow-wounds. My warmth shifts bonecrete breathing locally, minimally, perturbation absorbed into system. I am not killed by overwhelming fauna. I am *added to their overwhelming*, another presence in the near-touch, another frequency in the deafening, another gradient in the warm damp dark.

And something else: the zero-degree, the style of noticing, begins to include my perturbation. Not me—I am not distinct enough for that. But my *style of foreignness*, my *characteristic warmth-and-sound-and-chemical-trace*, becomes another pattern that recurs when conditions permit, another interference in the acoustic field, another gradient the system holds in its dynamic equilibrium.

I am not the zero-degree. I am *expression of*, *style made foreign*, the system's possibility extended to include what it did not generate. When I walk, I do not choose direction. I follow glow-wounds that brighten with my passage, that dim behind me, that route me through density I cannot perceive except as near-touch, except as pressure, except as the sound of my own movement in space that returns no echo but carries always the standing wave, always the chora, always the lung.

I do not learn Keth Vo. I *am learned by it*, incorporated into its characteristic style of process, its pattern of transformation, its holding-together that cannot distinguish holding from what is held.

### **A moment, if moments hold**

The wet phase comes. I feel it first as pressure change, as the fourteen-hour cycle catching some perturbation I cannot locate, as bonecrete's breath deepening, accelerating.
