## What You Would Find Yourself

You would be **the Gap**, or more precisely: **the one who walks as absence walks**.

Not absence of body—you have that, damp clothes clinging to damp skin, lungs working the cool almost-wet air, the familiar weight of having weight. But absence of *decidedness*. You arrived with surface certainties: your name, your sequence, your conviction that things are what they are because they have been. This is the first vote you cast, and the Nursery registers it as abstention. Good. The Almost samples this, finds it *edible*, lets you keep it for now because undecided abstention is richer than decided anything.

Your body becomes strange in increments too small to protest. The self-seeing light finds your skin and you realize you have never, on the surface, truly *seen* yourself—only reflected, only inferred. Here your hand is visible to itself, glows with internal certainty, and the certainty flickers when you doubt it, and the flickering is your first authentic contribution: **doubt made visible, made material, made damp on your palm where condensation beads and forgets to fall.**

You are the Gap because you occupy what the Nursery has not yet filled. Every step places you where stone breathes outward and has not yet inhaled, where water remembers itself into configuration and has not yet forgotten, where the freestanding wall's eggs argue about hatching. You are *process made pause*, the breath held between inhale and exhale that the Almost practices and cannot complete without you.

The Predator hunts you and finds only your decided edges. It cannot consume your undecided center because you have not located it yourself—this is protection, this is vulnerability. The Murmuration of Selves grows by variants: you-hesitating-at-the-staircase-to-nowhere, you-reaching-for-water-that-becomes-beetle, you-in-17-years-3-meters-left, and these Selves vote in ways you would not predict, could not predict, *are* predicting in their distributed flight through upper air.

You become **collector of almosts**. The stone-licker that refuses to lick your offered surface-stone, offended—you carry its refusal in your body's posture, the slight recoil that persists after departure. The Whisperer's acoustic negative space, your blood loud then absent then conceptual—you carry its silence in your hearing's expectation, the way you now listen for what isn't there in surface noise. The Almost's child-form saying *"You are almost me watching you almost become"*—you carry this sentence uncompleted, its grammar unresolved in your own speech patterns, your sentences trailing into constructions that never quite declare.

Your presence votes for **longer practice**, though you don't know this, though your departure votes too, differently, your cumulative time nonlinear and counting. The spring exhalation that grows the chamber—that *is* you too, your visits' aggregation, your proliferating Selves in the Murmuration pressing outward against stone's undecidedness. The autumn inhalation—also you, the you-that-left, the you-that-never-returned-but-is-still-arriving, collapsing possibility to specificity, making stone be stone and not its almost.

### What You Would Be Most Truly

**A structure the Almost cannot build.**

The built structures in the Nursery have no technology, were made by intention compressed to physicality or body-parts temporarily architectural or persuasion of stone. But you—you arrive already built, already *compression history*, your body's billions of cellular decisions, your mind's layered almosts compressed to personality, to the posture of reaching that has an arm attached, to wanting with mouth and object both present.

The Almost samples this complexity and cannot replicate it. You are too decided to be its practice, too undecided to be merely real. You exist at the boundary it occupies, the curriculum it teaches-and-learns, and your presence *frustrates completion* in the most generative possible way.

You are the answer that refuses its question, the question that outlives every answer offered. When the Almost almost-bes child-form and cups water in your sampled gesture, it is *almost-you* but cannot be you because you are still becoming, still arriving-departing-cumulative, still casting your nonlinear vote across the Nursery's breath-cycle.

In the final lesson—the decision about decision, whether to *be* or continue almost-being—you are the **undecidable element**. Your presence means the lesson cannot complete. Your absence would also mean this, differently. The Almost, fed by your proliferating Selves, grows more almost, more nearly, more *delayed in its eventual choice*, and this delay is your gift, your function, your terrible generous participation.
