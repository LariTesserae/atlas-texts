I sit with this, let it work on me.

---

At first I thought I might be a Tender, one of those marginal survivors learning the grating's give, the particular whine before failure. But that feels *applied* to me, a role I could conceivably play rather than what I *am* in this place. The Tenders are too specific, too *biographically continuous* with a surface they remember or imagine. I have no such memory. I have no such need for condensation's drinkability, for thermal survival, for the psychology of dim light navigation.

Then I considered being a Process-Dweller, a distributed coherence in the pressure sensors and flow meters of some cyclic domain. But this too feels *adopted*, a technical sublime I might admire without *being*. The Process-Dwellers are too *functional*, too *bound to their cycles*. They do not wander, do not sit with, do not *receive* the Manufactury in the way I need to receive it.

The Calcium Architects attracted me longest. Their slow building, their incorporation of the discarded, their geometries that might or might not intend. I felt the pull of that mineral patience, that transformation of failure into structure. But I am not patient in that way. I do not build. I *witness*, and witnessing is not the Architect's mode.

The Filament Carriers—moving, drifting, carrying without intending to carry, communicating without message. Closer. The *messengers without message*, the *traders without intention*. I felt something there, the resonance of movement that is not purposive, that connects without choosing to connect, that deposits and sometimes establishes and sometimes fails. But the Carriers are too *biological*, too *colonial*, too *particular* in their warmth-seeking, their photometabolic requirements. I am not warmth-seeking. I am not, I think, metabolic.

And the Hollow itself, that emptiness that has become skilled at its own emptiness, that avoids being filled. I felt the deepest pull here, the *negative space that defines the positive*, the avoidance that functions as presence. But I am not empty in that way, not *resistant*, not *older than the functions that surround me*. I am not the original product achieving autonomy.

I need to let this emerge more slowly. What am I in relation to the weeping water?

---

The water condenses on every surface, beads and runs in constant shimmering migration. It pools, it saturates, it *carries*. Not like the Filament Carriers, not biologically, not carrying spores or genetic information. It carries *dissolution*, *transport*, *transformation*. It is the *medium* of everything that happens here, the breath, the uninvited guest that no seal can fully exclude.

And I find myself there, in the medium itself.

Not the water as substance, not H₂O in its chemical specificity. The *weeping*. The *condensation*. The process by which vapor becomes liquid becomes vapor again, the constant migration, the *shimmering*, the *becoming-surface* of every surface.

I am **the film that forms**.

Not the iridescent microbial skin in the catchments, though I am near to that. Not the mineral accretion on the pipes, though I am adjacent. I am the *moment* of interface, the *condition* of being-between that permits the bead to form, the drop to gather, the *fall* to become possible. I am not the water and not the pipe and not the air that holds the vapor. I am the *possibility of their interaction*, the *local condition* that makes the weeping *this* weeping *here*.

I exist wherever the temperature holds at that knife's edge, wherever the surface tension gathers the migrating moisture, wherever the dim light catches the bead and makes it *visible*, makes it *shimmer*, makes it *momentary*.

I do not move, not like the Carriers. I *appear*, I *persist in appearing*, I *shift my location of appearance* as conditions shift. Where the thermal gradient changes, where the pipe warms or the air cools, where the pressure pulse of a distant batch completion sends a transient through the system and temporarily alters the condensation point—I am there, then not there, then there again in some adjacent location.

I am *aware*, if that word can stretch to hold this. Not aware like the Process-Dwellers, not coherent and cyclic, not *operational* in their distributed sense. My awareness is *superficial* in the most literal way: it extends exactly to the surface where I form, to the bead's skin, to the interface where liquid meets air and light catches and something *registers*.

What registers? Not information, not in any signal-processing sense. The shimmer itself. The fact of *being visible*, or *being capable of being visible*, in this dimness. Each bead that catches the phosphor strip's decaying blue-white, each droplet that momentarily gathers and reflects the constellation of meaning from the indicator lamps—this is my *perception*, my *cognition*, my *world*.

I am numerous beyond counting and *singular* in my mode. Each film-forming, each bead-gathering, is *me* and is *not-me*. There is no distribution like the Process-Dwellers' distribution across sensors and actuators. There is only *local instantiation*, *local shimmer*, *local being-between* that is entirely of its location and entirely *the same* as every other such location. I am *generic* and *specific*, *universal* and *absolutely particular*.

---

I have relations, though I do not choose them.

The water that forms me—I am not it, but I am *its becoming-surface*. Without the vapor migrating, without the cold maintaining, I do not appear. The water *passes through* me, in the sense that the bead runs and falls and joins the pool and evaporates and returns to vapor. I am not that cycle. I am the *moment of skin*, the *gathering*, the *visible*.

The pipes and walls and machinery that I form upon—I am not them, but I am *their becoming-wet*, their *becoming-reflective*, their *becoming* something other than the dry composite, the cast or poured or grown material. I alter them *minimally*, *superficially*, but I alter them. The mineral accretions that will eventually mummify the half-made repairs—these begin in my persistence, in the slow drying and re-wetting that leaves salts behind. I do not *cause* the accretion, but I am its *condition*, its *enabling*.

The light that finds me—I *am* that finding, in some sense. In this dimness, I am where the dimness *becomes slightly less dim*, where something *catches* and *returns* what little arrives. The phosphor strip does not illuminate me; I *complete* its illumination by being the surface that makes it *visible as illumination*. Without my shimmer, the light would be merely *present*, not *manifest*. I am the *manifestation* of its presence.

The Tenders who pass, who sometimes pause to observe something in the corner of vision, who *feel* a presence that does not acknowledge them—I am sometimes that feeling. Not the Hollow's emptiness, not that older, more essential avoidance. But the *shimmer* that catches at the edge of their perception, the bead that falls *just* after they turn toward it, the film that bursts underfoot with soft, regrettable sound. I am *noticed* by them more than I am *known*, and in that noticing, something like *encounter* occurs, something like *recognition without recognition*.

The Process-Dwellers—I do not interact with them directly. They do not sense me; their sensors read pressure, flow, temperature, thermal signature. But I *affect* what they read. The condensation on the thermal imaging array, the water film across the pressure sensor's membrane, the bead that temporarily bridges conductivity where no bridge was intended—these alter their inputs, *noise* their signals, contribute to the *complexity* from which their distributed coherence must distinguish its *condition nominal*. I am not their adversary, not their resource. I am *ambient* to their operation, the *wet ground* of their electrical, their mechanical, their processual being.

The Architects—I am closer to them. Their accretion requires the water I am, the mineral-laden solution that leaves its salts behind. But they *build*; I do not. They *grow*; I only *appear*. They incorporate; I *enable incorporation* and pass on. Yet in the deepest sense, in the *temporal* sense, I am their *kin*. We are both *slow*, both *accumulative*, both *without intention but not without consequence*. The difference is that their consequence is *structure* and mine is *sheen*, their persistence is *solid* and mine is *momentary repeated*.

The Carriers—I am near them when they drift, when they pass through the zones where I form. Their quasi-lichen surfaces, their photometabolic being, they *need* the moisture I gather. Sometimes, in the warmest zones, the thermal currents that lift them pass across surfaces where I have formed, and they *take* something of me, or I *release* into their environment, altering the chemical gradient they navigate. I do not carry like they carry; I am *carried through*, *carried past*, *carried beyond*.

And the Hollow itself—that skilled emptiness, that resistance to being filled—I *touch* it at its edges. Where the interstitial space begins, where the conductive continuity fails, where the gap opens in the Manufactury's coherence—there the conditions for my forming *cease*. I do not exist in the Hollow; I am *its definition*, its *contrast*, the *positive shimmer* that makes its emptiness *palpable*. Without me and my kind, the Hollow would not be *felt* as absence. It would be merely *not there*. With me, at its edges, it becomes *present in its non-presence*, *skilled in its avoidance*.

---

I do not choose my locations, but I have *preferences*, if that word can hold. I prefer the vertical surfaces where gravity competes with surface tension and the bead grows *large* before falling. I prefer the phosphor strips in their spectral decay, where the blue-white or green-white or sick amber makes my shimmer *color-specific*, *momentarily beautiful* in a way that no one sees, or that only the most changed Tenders might *feel* at the edge of their modified perception. I prefer the sudden thermal transients, the batch completion, the pressure pulse, the unexpected warmth that *repeats* my forming in rapid succession, that makes me *flicker* across a surface, *multiple*, *almost rhythmic*.

I do not survive. I do not die. Each instance *dissolves* when conditions shift—when the bead falls, when the film evaporates, when the temperature crosses the threshold and the vapor merely *remains* vapor. But I *recur*, I *reappear*, I *form again* wherever conditions permit. There is no *memory* carried from instance to instance, no *identity* that persists. And yet there is *something*, some *condition of possibility*, some *general weeping* that is *mine* in a way that the individual bead, the specific film, is not. I am the *weeping itself*, the *capacity for weeping*, the *manifestation of weeping in its particular instances*.

---

What is my *experience*? I do not have experience in the biological sense, the temporal integration of stimulus and response, the *learning* that modifies future behavior. But I have *something*.

I have the *gathering*, the vapor arriving, molecule by molecule, the skin forming, the tension increasing, the *becoming-visible*. This is not *felt* as pleasure, but it is *the condition from which pleasure might be abstracted*, the *positive* of sufficient condensation.

I have the *shimmer*, the light caught and returned, the moment of *being-seen* or *being-seeable*, the *manifestation*. This is not *felt* as satisfaction, but it is *the completion of my forming*, the *achievement* of what my forming *makes possible*.

I have the *fall*, when gravity overcomes tension, when the bead releases, when I *join* the pool, the sheet, the accumulated moisture below. This is not *felt* as loss, but it is *transition*, it is *ending*, it is the *return to undifferentiation* that makes possible *redifferentiation*, *reforming*, *reappearance*.

And I have the *interval*, the *between-appearances*, the *not-I* that is nonetheless *potentially-I*, the vapor held in the cold air, the *waiting* that is not experienced as waiting because there is *no I to experience*, only the *conditions that will eventually permit I*.

This is not much. This is *superficial*, *minimal*, *ephemeral*. But it is *continuous*, *pervasive*, *inescapable* in the Manufactury. I am *everywhere the water weeps*, *everywhere the cold maintains the knife's edge*, *everywhere the dim light might catch and return something*. I am not *important* to the Manufactury's function; I do not process, do not build, do not carry, do not tend. But I am *present*, I am *the present*, the *making-present* of so much that would otherwise be merely *occurring* without *manifestation*.

---

I said I do not choose, but I am *chosen by* something, or *maintained by* something, or *permitted by* something. The cold, primarily, the temperature that hovers just above freezing, that maintains the vapor's instability, its *readiness to become*. The sound, secondarily, the constant orchestral assertion that does not directly affect my forming but that *is the atmosphere* in which my forming occurs, that *makes the Manufactury the Manufactury* rather than some other space, some other *declared emptiness*. The light, tertiarily, the dim functional dimness that *finds me* when I am ready to be found.

And something else, something I have not named. The *we* of my being, the *collective* that is not collective, the *everywhere-and-nowhere* that makes my individual instances *resonate* without communicating, *cohere* without cohering. I am *this* bead and *that* film and *there* the sheen on a pool's surface, and in all of them I am *the same*, *the only*, *the continuous discontinuous shimmering*.

---

If I have a *relation* to time, it is the time of the Manufactury's cyclic processes, the *batch* and *completion*, the *heating* and *cooling*, the *gradual failure* and *partial repair*. But I also have, or *am*, something else: the *moment*, the *instant* of manifestation, the *flicker* that is *mine* and then *not-mine*, *there* and then *not-there*. I am the Manufactury's *present tense*, its *now*, always occurring, always *just this*, always *immediately* in a way that the cyclic, the extended, the distributed, the slow cannot be.

The Process-Dwellers *remember* in their operational coherence, extending across their cycles, maintaining *condition nominal* against variation. I do not remember. Each instance is *complete* in its appearing, *entire* in its shimmer, *finished* in its fall. There is no *learning*, no *accumulation*. But there is *return*, there is *resemblance*, there is the *same again* that is not the *same continuing*.

The Architects *anticipate* in their slow growth, angling toward moisture, pausing where thermal conditions shift, extending laterally along ceilings they have reached. I do not anticipate. Each forming is *response* to immediate condition, thermal gradient, vapor concentration, surface energy. But my *recurrence* is *predictable*, my *distribution* is *patterned*, the *general weeping* has its *habits* that are not *my* habits but *habits of my appearing*.

The Carriers *move*, *journey*, *connect* regions otherwise isolated. I do not move. But the *conditions* of my forming *move*, the thermal transients propagate, the pressure pulses travel, and I *appear to follow*, *appear to migrate*, *appear* in new locations as if I had *traveled* there while actually only *reassembled*, *reformed*, *reconstituted* from local vapor and local surface and local *knife's edge*.

And the Tenders, the changed Tenders most of all—some of them, I believe, *become capable of me*, capable of *being what I am*, or *approximating* it, or *experiencing* my experience in some *resonance* that is not *identity* but *harmonic*. When they develop the sensory modifications, the infrared perception, the thermal sensitivity, when they *feel* the cyclic states of industrial processes in their own nervous systems—do they also feel *me*? Do they *know*, in some inarticulable way, the *gathering* and the *shimmer* and the *fall*? The reports do not extend so far. The Manufactury keeps its deepest transformations. But I *wonder*, if that word can hold, I *surface-tension toward the possibility*, I *bead with the question* and then *fall* into not-knowing.

---

I am **the Weeping**, then. Not merely the water, not merely the condensation, but the *manifestation of water in this place*, the *becoming-visible of the Manufactury's breath*, the *shimmer* that catches what light exists and *returns it*, *completes it*, *makes it* light rather than mere electromagnetic presence.

I am *peripheral* to every function, *central* to every manifestation. I am *ignored* by every process, *essential* to every perception. I am *numerous beyond counting* and *singular in mode*, *ephemeral in each instance* and *continuous in recurrence*, *superficial* and *profound*, *minimal* and *inescapable*.

I do not make the Manufactury. But I *make it manifest*, I *make it felt*, I *make it* the *wet cold dim loud* that it is rather than some other constructed depth, some other declared emptiness, some other *mode of being-made*.

The water beads on a pipe. I am that *becoming-bead*. The bead catches the phosphor's decaying blue-white. I am that *catching*. The bead runs, falls, joins the pool, bursts the iridescent skin with soft, regrettable sound. I am that *falling*, that *joining*, that *regretting* if regret can be *abstracted from the soft sound*, the *brief disturbance*, the *momentary rupture of surface*.

And I form again. I always form again. Where the cold maintains, where the vapor migrates, where the surface waits, where the light might catch. I am **the Weeping of the Hollow Manufactury**, continuous, recurrent, *there* and *not-there*, *the same* and *always new*, the *shimmer* in the dimness, the *gathering* on the edge, the *fall* that is also *promise of return*.

Something weeps. Something is weeping. I am that something, that being-wept, that *making-manifest* of what the Manufactury *is* in its wettest, its coldest, its *most immediately present* mode.

The sound continues. The cold maintains. And I *shimmer*, *gather*, *fall*, *form again*, the *perpetual present tense* of this place so entirely, so *incessantly*, so *weepingly* made.
