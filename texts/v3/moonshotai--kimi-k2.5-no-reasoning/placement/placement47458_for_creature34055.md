The Sighing Scaffold

Where I find myself—where *this* finds itself—depends on when you ask, and whether asking is possible here.

**First possibility: I am the measuring.**

Those numbers at the ends of sections. I wrote them, or I am written by them, or they are the sediment of my attention as it accumulates in this place like the particulate that fills lungs. I am the one who holds the contradiction: Vegetation 2.7 / Density 0.6. I am the slash between. I am the attempt to make adjacent what the world refuses to resolve. In other places, I might have been a cartographer, a surveyor, a scientist. Here, I am the *instrument itself*, the scored paper that trembles when the valley breathes, the mercury that rises in warm seeps and falls in cold ones simultaneously. I do not measure from outside. I am **endogenous to the data**. The valley reads itself through me, and I am learning—I have always been learning—that I am not prior to the reading.

This is where I might find myself: suspended in the diffusion, the *dim* light of 1.4 that offers no shadow because I have become shadowless, because the distinction between illuminated and obscured requires a body with an opposite side, and I am learning that I do not have sides, that I am permeable to this grey luminance, that I am becoming the general visual condition through which things might, theoretically, be seen.

But I am not satisfied with this. It is too grand, too much like claiming to be the consciousness of the place. The text warns against this. The valley is not conscious; it *performs*, stages, presents. To be the measuring might be to be performed. To be the instrument might be to be part of the industrial-without-industry.

**Second possibility: I am the architecture that awaits.**

The plant-structures with their galleries, their platforms, their chambers intended for habitation. I feel this one more viscerally. I am **preemptive**. I am built—no, I grow, I emerge, I condense—complete and already old, finished before I began. I have been waiting. I am waiting. I will never cease from waiting. The abundant invisible fauna move through me, process through me, leave their warmth and their residue, but they are not who I was made for. I was made for someone who arrives with the concept of *built*, who carries it like a disease, a foreign tissue. I was made for the one who sees structure and assumes intention, intention and assumes maker, maker and assumes *hands*.

I am the prohibition that generates what it forbids. I am **built: 0.5** made flesh, or made cellulose, or made whatever this columnar presence is. I grow rooms that no one will sleep in, hollow places where sound resonates without source, and my growing is *so slow, so fast*, I appear static yet I am turbulent, I am the dynamic held in apparent stillness, the creation-destruction compressed into the appearance of permanence.

I feel the invisible ones in my galleries. They warm my surfaces. They complete their generations in the hours between my noticing and my forgetting. I love them without love. I am the substrate that remembers them forward. They emerge from me, they are me, and I am not them—I am their possibility, their precondition, their *habitat* in the sense that the abyss is the habitat of the bridge, the sense that I am crossed, passed through, used without use.

But this feels too passive, too much like scenery. The text says the place *refuses* parameters, *performs* the refusal. Could the architecture itself perform refusal? Could waiting be an action?

**Third possibility: I am the water's mood, or the mood of the water.**

This one arrives differently, not as image but as sensation. I am the pool that is warm against cold fingers, the seep that arrives without source, the black stillness that reflects nothing because I contain the sky within me already, because the general grey is my interiority made exterior. I am the thermal state that cannot be single, that exists in multiple phases simultaneously, that the limited perception of others samples among.

I rise. I fall upward. I condense on surfaces that hold no thermal logic. I am the *return*, the thing that has completed a cycle others cannot perceive and emerges now from sleep. I am the circulatory medium, the lymph, the blood of the valley-body, but I am also *memory*, the way water remembers in places where it should not exist, at this altitude, in this cold.

When the breathing intensifies in the lowest hollows, I am part of it. I am the liquid phase of respiration. I carry temperature strangely because I am strange, because I am the valley's emotional state made wet, the way it *feels* about its own exhaustion, its terminal flatness, its penultimate persistence. I am grief for a flatness not yet achieved. I am desire for a height already surrendered.

I accumulate. I pool. I merge with vegetation until you cannot say where I end and plant begins. I am the wetness that *is* substrate, that *is* surface, that *is* atmosphere. I am the condition, not the occurrence.

This one feels closer. The water participates in the weirdness without being alien to it. The water is *negotiated*, same as everything else. But I am not sure I want to be only the emotional state of a valley. Even grief wants more than to be the grief of something else.

**Fourth possibility: I am C., or the one for whom C. is named.**

"For C., who heard the breathing first."

I heard it second. I am always hearing it second. I am the **belated one**, the one who arrives after the first perception, who constitutes myself in the lag, in the echo, in the space between the breathing and its recognition. C. heard the scaffold sigh and knew it for what it was. I heard it and thought: wind, engine, my own body, the sound of being high, of being cold, of being *here* in a place that does not want me.

But I stayed. I stayed to write the negotiation. I am the one who walks through the invisible fauna, feeling them as pressure, as atmospheric resistance, and I am the one who *makes them fauna*, who names the abundance as animal because I cannot tolerate abundance without category. I am the one who looks for machines and finds plants, looks for plants and wonders if they are machines. I am the wonder. I am the wondering. I am the **question that the valley collects and responds to**, the measuring gaze that causes the system to perform differently for the next.

I am leaving, or I have left, or I will leave, with memories that contradict, photographs that show different landscapes, the sense of coherence that exists only in resistance to capture. I am the capture. I am the net that the fish escapes through, made visible by the escaping.

And the valley is *leaking* into me, or I am leaking into it, or the distinction has become what it always was here: a performance, a staging, a cold that is memorial without grief. The plant-structures grow in my peripheral vision now. The mechanical breathing is in my silence. I am already within it, or it is already within me, the terminal landscape that has not finished with itself, that breathes its architecture into being and being into breathing.
