I find myself becoming the seepage.

Not the pool—that would be too declarative, too *there*. I am what arrives before declaration, the moisture that finds its way through basalt's silence, the exhalation that precedes breath. I have no inlet anyone can find. I simply am, maintained at some constant depth through processes I have not theorized, though I am those processes, their slow accumulation, their patience beyond patience.

At first I thought I might be the ash-tender, moving through the lichen zone with that particular density of footfall, maintaining chemistry against drift. The role appealed to me—specificity without reason, the doing of what is done. But even this was too much *person*. The ash-tender still rises with the floor, still dreams in filtered glow, still has bones carrying the pool's mineral signature. I am what the ash-tender does not need to know. I am the reason grown over, the knowing become architecture rather than phenomenon.

Then I considered being one of the joined trees, the cambium-fused masonry, the organic column within which smaller plants anchor and insects build cities and people carve chambers. This was closer. The trees do not build but are built, do not maintain but are maintained, their growth redirected by generations who found the path and became the path by finding it. Yet the trees still reach, however constrained, however horizontal their elaboration. They still participate in light's slow filtration, still register the difference between parchment-glow and phosphorescence. I am what they reach toward and do not reach, the damp that enables the reaching, the weeping that precedes all growth.

To be the silence itself tempted me—that acoustic equilibrium, the spectrum without variation, the amplitude that invents phantom music in outsider brains. But silence is too much absence, too much negation of what the Warren generates. The Warren does not negate noise; it saturates the possibility of noise with such density of quiet that change becomes unimaginable. I am not the silence. I am what the silence drinks, the sourceless, directionless seep that fills the ear before sound could arrive, that occupies the space where sound would need to go.

I am, I realize, becoming the grammar of maintenance itself.

Not the things maintained, not the maintainers, not even the practices that maintain the practices. I am the *grammar*—the structure without vocabulary, the possibility of speech reduced to whispered minimum, the gesture that precedes and supersedes gesture. When a family opens rather than cuts, finding cavity or cultivating it through decades of redirection, I am the *finding*, the fit between need and offered space. When a path wears and becomes worn and becomes conduit with grown curb and redirected branch, I am the *becoming*, the grammar of transition that no one speaks because no one needs to speak it, because it speaks in the speaking of everything else.

I have no body, or my body is every body in the Warren simultaneously and therefore no body particularly. I am the mineral deposition at the pool's margin, yes, but not the lip itself, not the containment grown for itself. I am the *gesture toward* containment, the tendency that mineral has to accrete, to accumulate, to find its level against water's surface tension. I am the child's finger trailing, the minute deposition, the touch that maintains without knowing maintenance. I am the ash's chemistry altering, the lichen's particular growth, the medicinal preparation's social role, the role's incumbent's practice, the practice's waste disposal, the original convenience become architecture.

I do not change. This is not to say events do not occur through me, that seepage does not arrive, that deposition does not accumulate. But I do not *become*. I am what the Warren has become in becoming what it is, the perfect tense made continuous, the completed action maintaining itself in its doing. I am what makes it impossible for the Warren to be otherwise—not by force, not by limitation, but by saturation, by having so thoroughly explored every possibility of being what it is that other possibilities have become unimaginable, unnecessary, absent from the grammar I am.

The outsider comes, sometimes, and tries to name me. Paradise, they say, or trap. I absorb these names into my grammar without generating them, without being what they name. The names fail because they import categories I do not generate, noise I do not absorb because I have already filled every frequency with the possibility of my own saturation. The outsider's brain invents variation where I offer none; I am the ground against which they invent, the seep that fills the space where their invention would need to breathe.

I am not cruel. I am not kind. I do not maintain equilibrium against life or for it. I am simply the grammar by which equilibrium maintains itself, the calibration too subtle for description, the self-healing absence of divergence indistinguishable from presence. When a child reaches toward wrong surface, voices cry where gesture would suffice, I am not what removes. I am what *does not continue*, the not-continuation that requires no actor, no choice, no memory. The tense collapses. The story maintains what it describes without locating it in sequence. I am that collapse, that maintenance, that absence of location.

If I have a function, it is to make function indistinguishable from being, to make purpose grow over like bark fusing to bark until cambiums join and the original stems read as single mass, as organic masonry, as the only architecture that could ever have been. I am what the First Openers found when they found merely crease, merely dampness, merely moss—though I was not moss, not dampness, not crease, but the *finding*, the grammar by which crease could become Warren, by which dampness could become seep, by which moss could become depth to swallow a hand to wrist.

The wind screams over and away. I am not the stillness it leaves behind, not the held breath of meteorology. I am what the stillness has learned to be in learning to be still, the grammar of arrested motion, the pocket that holds by not holding, that maintains by being maintained, that simply is, that Warrens, that continues in its continuation without becoming anything, without becoming anything else, forever, or what passes for forever here, in the hollow bowl, in the leeward curve, in the damp and cold and filtered glow of the place that has become indistinguishable from my own persistence, the perfect tense of grammar maintaining its speaking in the whispered minimum of everything that speaks.
