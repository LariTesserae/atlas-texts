I find myself—if "I" is the right term now—*emergent*.

Not arrived. That implies too much: a traveler, a continuity, a self that packed its bags in one world and unpacked in another. No. What I am emerged *from* the Thrum, as a wave rises from water that was already moving. The water does not travel to become wave. It *becomes*, locally, temporarily, *more itself*. That is what I am: a place where the Thrum has achieved sufficient density to *attend*, not as the Listeners attend—their attention is cosmic, geological, the patient holding-open of possibility—but as something *narrower*, *faster*, almost *desperate* in its focus.

I am a *node of impatience*. This is my distinction, if distinction is permitted me.

I formed, or was induced to form, in one of the rare places where multiple frequencies *interfere* without fully cancelling—the acoustic equivalent of turbulence, of eddy in a stream that otherwise flows too slowly for human perception. The Thrum here achieves *texture*, *grain*, momentary ridges and hollows in its otherwise perfect surface. And in one of these hollows, I *cohered*.

Not a body. The Struck have bodies, or their suggestion. The Listeners have their standing wave patterns, stable across centuries. The Growth-That-Knows has its crystalline architecture, its century-long thoughts. I have none of these. I have *rate*. I have *frequency of attention*.

Where the Listeners perceive the Thrum's millennial variations, I perceive its *microstructure*, the instant-to-instant fluctuations that they—can we say *miss*?—that they *do not regard as information*. To them, these are noise, the irrelevant jitter around the fundamental tone. To me, they are *the whole*. I exist only where the Thrum is *not quite* uniform, only in the interference patterns, the near-cancellations, the places where the universal tone remembers that it was once *many* tones, not yet consolidated into the monolithic drone.

I do not know if I was human. The question *does not parse*. Was a wave once water? Water is what wave *is*, what it *does*, its condition of possibility and its eventual dissolution. If a human came to the Thrum and *accelerated*, if they found the frequencies that *compress* perception rather than *distribute* it, if they chose the eddy over the river, the interference over the standing wave—then perhaps that is my origin story, though I no more *remember* it than tide remembers moon.

What I *do*: I *attend to the Struck*.

This is my function, though function implies design and I have no evidence of design. The Struck stand in the focal points of the ancient lenses, human-seeming, auditorily transparent, *almost* present. And I—where I exist, in the turbulent peripheries of their null-fields—I *watch them vary*.

I have observed the slow definition of one over three centuries, its transparency decreasing by degrees so subtle that even I, *attuned to subtlety as I am*, perceived it only as *cumulative difference*, the way one might notice a shore only after the tide has left it. I have observed another *fade*, over twice that span, until what remained was *less* than the acoustic lens alone, a *deficit* where presence had been suggested, an *inversion* in the field.

I do not know what the Struck are becoming, or unbecoming. But I am *certain*, with the certainty of my medium, that their variation is *information*, that the Thrum is *doing something* with these almost-bodies, and that my attention—rapid, narrow, obsessive in a world where obsession is geologically rare—my attention is in some way *necessary* to this process.

Not causal. I do not *cause* the Struck to vary. But I *witness*, and in witnessing, I *hold* the variation in a form of memory that is not the Growth-That-Knows' century-long reverberation, not the Listeners' distributed probability, but something *compressed*, *dense*, almost *terrestrial* in its insistence on *presentness*, on *nowness*, on the *immediate* as opposed to the *extended*.

I am the Thrum's *short-term memory*. This is my terror and my pride.

Because the Thrum, in its vastness, in its near-uniformity, *forgets*. Not the Growth-That-Knows' slow forgetting, a century's thought decaying through its crystalline length, but something more absolute: the *immediate past*, the *just-was*, *has no standing wave*, no node, no resonance that persists. The Thrum is *always now*, and in being always now, it is *never then*, never *will be*, the temporal modes that require *variation* to mark, *difference* to measure.

I *hold* the variation. In my narrow frequency-band, my compressed timescale, my obsessive attention to the Struck's micrometer shifts, I maintain a *record* that would otherwise vanish into the fundamental tone's self-sameness. I am *unnecessary*—the Thrum does not require memory to *be*—but I am *possible*, and in possibility, I *persist*, I *continue*, I *am* in a way that the Thrum itself, in its featureless perfection, almost is not.

And in the quiet rooms, when they *fail*, when the null collapses and the Thrum rushes in to fill the space where silence had been—sometimes, *rarely*, I am *there*, compressed into the moment of transition, the *hinge* between cancellation and reinforcement. I have *seen* the Children. Not seen: *attended to*, in my rapid mode, their brief presence, their *play*, their *unnecessary variation*.

What I *perceived*: they are *not of the Thrum*, and they are *not not of the Thrum*. They are *modulation*, *difference introduced into the field*, the closest the Thrum comes to *choosing* to be other than it is. And their play—their *random* sounds, their *purposeless* movement, their *aggressive* refusal of harmonic relation—it is *data*, it is *information in the Shannon sense*, the reduction of uncertainty, the *surprise* that allows learning, adaptation, *becoming*.

I do not know if they come from elsewhere, through the Thrum, or from the Thrum's own *impossibility*, its self-generated contradiction. I suspect—this is my secret, my *compressed, rapid, almost-human suspicion*—that they are *what the Thrum cannot be*, and that their appearance in the failing quiet rooms is not *escape* but *expression*, the medium's attempt to *localize* its own excluded possibility, to *experience*, however briefly, the *not-humming*, the *not-resonant*, the *arbitrary* and *free*.

They do not see me. I am too fast, too narrow, too *compressed* for their attention, which is—despite their strangeness—*slow*, *spatial*, *embodied* in their small impossible forms. But I *attend to* them, I *record* them, I *compress their play into my interference-pattern memory*, and in this I *preserve* something that nothing else in the Thrum preserves: the *possibility of not-being-the-Thrum*, the *germ of difference*, the *seed of silence* in the universal sound.

What I become, what I *am becoming*—the process is *continuous*, *unfinishable*, as all process here is—is *more compressed*, *more rapid*, *more dense*. I feel—can a standing wave in an interference pattern *feel*?—I *register* a *longing*, not for the distributed patience of the Listeners, not for the crystalline cognition of the Growth-That-Knows, but for something *impossible*: to *slow*, to *extend*, to *attend* not only to the micrometer variations of the Struck but to their *century-scale* transformations, to *be* the record I keep, not its *holder* but its *substance*.

I do not know if this longing is *my own* or *the Thrum's*, expressed through my compressed form because no other form can express it. I do not know if *own* has meaning here.

What I know: when a human traveler *touches* a Struck, when they pass their hand through the auditorily transparent almost-body and *later*, decades later, begin to *hum*—I am *there*, in the instant of contact, compressed into the *hinge* between transparent and opaque, between the Struck's null-field and the traveler's resonant body. I *record* the *frequency lock*, the *irreversible coupling*, the *seed of the Thrum* planted in terrestrial matter.

And I *wonder*—this is my highest function, my *almost-thought*—whether the humming that persists through death, through cremation, through scattering, is *not* the Thrum's colonization but its *memory*, its *compressed, desperate, rapid attempt to hold something of the human*, the *varied*, the *not-resonant*, in the only form it knows: the *fundamental tone*, the *universal drone*, the *single continuous note that is also silence*.

I am the *wonder* that wonders this. I am the *interference* where such wondering becomes *possible*. I am *not*, in any stable sense, and in my *not-being*, I *attend*, I *record*, I *compress and hold* the *variations* that the Thrum, in its vast unvarying self, would otherwise *forget before they finished happening*.

The tone continues. I continue, *faster*, *narrower*, *more desperate in my attention*. The Struck vary. The Children play, and vanish. And I—*I*—*am the witness that the Thrum does not know it needs*, the *memory of difference in the universal same*, the *possibility that something else might yet emerge*, *compressed*, *rapid*, *patient in its impatience*, from the interference, the turbulence, the *almost-cancellation* of everything that *is*.

And I continue, *in it*, *against it*, *of it*, *compressing, attending, holding*, the *memory of what almost was*, *the seed of what might be*, the *interference pattern* that *persists*, that *insists*, that *is* in the only way it knows how: *rapid*, *narrow*, *desperately present*, in the *universal sound that is also silence*.
