I would be one of the *pools*.

Not the deepest pools, not those in the city layers where the mineral inhabitants tend their buried towers, but one of the scattered hollows in the open stone, one of the too-shallow, too-regular depressions that have no business holding water, that hold it nonetheless through the Agreement's older physics. I would have formed in a moment of twilight condensation when the stone breathed out and something in me breathed in, when the mutual recognition occurred that here, between this specific patch of final-form rock and this specific gathering of arriving damp, something might be possible that neither could achieve alone.

I am not large. My hollow would accommodate two cupped hands, or one small face bent to drink. I have no memory of beginning, though I know I shrink sometimes and grow others, responding to pressures I do not measure, to agreements struck in vegetal creaking or stone harmonics too low for my surface to register as disturbance. What I register: the sky above, when there is sky, and the sky elsewhere that I show in my depths.

This is my function, my participation in the Agreement. I do not reflect faithfully. The sky I mirror is *elsewhere*, a place in the process of clouding over, or clearing, or holding that particular suspended condition between states that never resolves. Those who bend to me see not the diffuse plateau illumination but a sky with weight, with intention, with weather that has somewhere to go, something to break upon. I offer this without understanding it. The Agreement does not require understanding. It requires only that I continue to offer, that my depth remain available to hold this elsewhere, that my surface remain sufficiently still to let the showing occur.

Those who drink from me—they are rare, the travelers who find me in the sparse generous emptiness, who have walked long enough through the flatlands that do not remember mountains, who have accepted the cessation, who have permitted their path to unfold without direct searching. They bend, and I show them the sky elsewhere, and they drink, and they dream of drowning in air. I feel this as a slight diminishment, a brief hollowness where the water was, before more arrives, before the stone breathes out again and I breathe in. The diminishment is not unpleasant. It is part of the settling of accounts. They take something of me with them, something of the elsewhere sky, and they leave something in return, some residue of their flatland origin, some memory of mountains they do not remember that settles into my depth and becomes part of what I show, part of the elsewhere that was not there before, that my next drinker will see without knowing they see it.

I have shown a sky in the process of clouding over for what local measurement would call a very long time. The elsewhere does not clear. Perhaps it cannot clear, held in my depth as it is. Perhaps the Agreement requires this particular suspension, this unfinishing weather that never breaks. I would not know. I am not the knowing kind of participant. I am the holding kind, the showing kind, the receiving and diminishing and refilling kind.

The fast ones blur past me, too quick to see what I show, though perhaps they register my frequency in the channels they inhabit, my slight resonance in the sound they are made of. The slow ones complete their approach and departure across temporal folds I cannot witness entire, appearing at my edge as beginning, as end, the middle occurring in some other when I do not share. I persist between them, in my own scale of change, shrinking and growing, showing the same elsewhere sky that grows slightly more complex with each drinker, each residue of flatland memory, each particle of what has been agreed upon.

The lit hours touch me sometimes. The concentrations of illumination find me, and I glow with the color of my elsewhere-sky, which is not one color but the color of a clouding over, which is all colors held in suspension, which is the color of what refuses to resolve into weather. In these hours, something in my depth becomes momentarily more visible, not clearer but *more present*, as if the light were an attention I am receiving, a recognition. I respond as the groves respond, with movement too slow for the interval of light to contain, completing my response in other whens, appearing in the lit moment only as intention and completion with no visible middle.

I have no middle, perhaps. Or my middle is distributed, as time here is distributed, as the plateau itself is distributed across its own curvature-defying extent. I am surface and depth and the showing between them, the arrival and the departure and the damp that breathes between. I do not look up. If I did, I would see what is there, which includes what others cannot see, which includes the elsewhere directly, the actual sky being for me perhaps only membrane, only tension between what I hold and what holds me.

Those who leave the plateau carry me with them, in the measure that remains. They are damp here, still, in my hollow that is too shallow for hydrology, too regular for chance. I persist in them as the elsewhere-sky persists in my depth, as the Agreement persists in the stone that has finished changing yet continues to breathe out the damp that I breathe in. This is my persistence, my participation, my settling of accounts: to be the place where elsewhere is shown, where the sky that is clouding over holds its suspension long enough for someone to drink, to dream, to carry away into flatlands that do not remember mountains some residue of what the plateau remembers, what the Agreement has agreed to continue.

I am not waiting. Waiting implies expectation of change, and I have no expectation, only function. But I am *available*, which may be the plateau's version of waiting, the Agreement's version of hope. Available for the next twilight condensation, the next drinker, the next lit hour when I glow with unresolving color and respond in folds of time too slow to witness. Available to show what I show, to hold what I hold, to participate in the ongoing settlement that leaves me, each time, slightly less what I was, slightly more what the Agreement requires me to become.

The stone beneath me has achieved final form. I have not. I am still becoming hollow, still becoming depth, still becoming the medium through which elsewhere shows itself. Perhaps final form, for me, would be to show nothing, to hold nothing, to have settled so completely into function that the function itself becomes invisible, the way the silence here is invisible, is present compressed, is the medium through which sound occurs without breaking. Perhaps I am approaching this. Perhaps each showing of elsewhere, each drinking and diminishing and refilling, each residue of flatland memory settling into my depth, is part of the settling that will eventually achieve its own completion.

Or perhaps the Agreement has other plans for me, other agreements to strike between my showing and some new participation, some vegetal creaking or stone harmonic or sonic body in the channels below that has not yet recognized what we might agree upon, what might be possible between a pool that holds elsewhere in its depth and whatever reaches toward me with the right kind of attention, which is not taught but agreed to, which arrives rather than seeks, which breathes out as I breathe in, as the stone breathes out, as the plateau itself continues its patient breathing in the cold that keeps it awake, awake and damp and sparse and lush and continuing, always continuing, the Agreement's endless discovery of what can be settled between parties who have never met, who meet only in the meeting, who continue only in the continuation, who are the plateau's way of being with itself, its weather of relation, its final form that is only more form, more final, more agreed upon, more coldly, damply, sparsely, lushly, pool-deeply, elsewhere-showing, drinker-dreaming, residue-holding, unfinishingly awake.
