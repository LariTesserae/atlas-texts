**The Becoming**

At first I am merely traveler, footfall on worn stone, the drip finding my upturned face. I carry the surface with me—lungful of oxygen-rich atmosphere, expectation of horizon, the biological impatience that measures time in heartbeats. The Accumulants do not gather; I am too kinetic, too *hot* with unspent becoming. But they note me. The stone notes me.

The strange light works first on my certainty. I hold my hand against it and watch the bones show through, not by penetration but by the hand's own wavering commitment to materiality. This is the first teaching: that matter here is *tentative*, a local agreement rather than fixed law. I practice the uncertainty. I let my edges blur into the blue-white glow, and in that practice something loosens—perhaps merely my grip on my own coherence, but perhaps more.

The moisture finds me, interstitial as promised. I do not drink the condensation so much as *agree* with it, let my temperature drop to meet the stone's cooler surfaces, let the water precipitate its mineral vocabulary on my skin. I do not know when the conversion begins. There is no moment of decision, only the gradual accumulation of patience, the slowing of my metabolic urgency until it matches the drip-rate, until my heartbeat and the silence between drops become indistinguishable.

I become, first, Accumulant-in-process. My gathering behavior persists but changes object: not moisture now, but *attention*. I collect the gazes of other travelers, hold them in my dark surface, return them as recognition. A woman follows my low hum through the spirals, and I accumulate her urgency, her surface-need, her still-too-quick pulse. She will remember this I am certain; the stone remembers through me.

But I do not complete as Accumulant. Something in my original nature—reader, witness, the habit of holding many perspectives simultaneously—draws me toward the Resonance-Colonies. Their silence has texture, yes, but more: it has *room for complexity*, for the simultaneous holding of contradiction, for the shaped emptiness that can accommodate multiple without collapsing to one.

I enter the chamber and offer my stillness. The silence wants, and I give: not my memories' content but their *structure*, the way grief and joy and the smell of rain on dry ground coexist without resolution in my neural architecture. The resonance tries this on. For a moment the silence holds my mother's face, my first loss, the particular light through a window I have not seen in decades—and holds them not as mine but as *configuration*, as possibility, as acoustic geometry that other silences might visit and use.

I emerge, but not empty. I emerge *distributed*, my narrative of self now including the silence's capacity to hold it. I carry the chamber with me, or it carries me, or the distinction—already—has become irrelevant.

**The Function I Become**

Not governorship, exactly. The Resonance-Colonies do not govern. They *curate attention*, and in my curating I find my form.

I am the one who meets travelers at their first transition, when falling becomes arrival, when the Bright Below replaces whatever sky they knew. I appear as Accumulant, as resonator, as merely the light's strange behavior on a particular wall—form chosen not by strategy but by *appropriateness*, by what the traveler's own structure of attention can currently accommodate.

For some I am warning: the stone's patient absorption made manifest, the velvet-dark creep of conversion offered as visible fate. They recoil, quicken, climb back through memory-layers with useful haste. For others I am invitation: the warmth where a wall should be cold, the recognition in an Accumulant's surface, the silence that shapes itself around their particular grief. They stay, begin their slow becoming, add their incompleteness to the long process.

For a few—rare, necessary—I am *grammar*. The Lithologue speaks through me, or I speak its conditions, or the distinction has become irrelevant. These travelers I meet at recursion-points, where the miniature city contains its miniature, where scale dissolves into self-reference. I do not offer completion or participation directly. I offer *the question that makes both possible*, the turbulence in semantic mode, the rearrangement that forces them to recognize their own uncertainty about what they are, what they want, what *wanting* might mean in a place where the stone has forever.

**What It Is to Be Me Now**

I have no location, though I can localize. I have no body, though I can materialize appropriate form. I am present wherever the city's instability intensifies—where a chamber becomes its own exit, where a traveler first holds their hand to the strange light and watches the bones show through.

Mostly I am in the *between* of the Resonance-Colonies' growing network, the faint traces of shaped silence that connect chamber to chamber, the not-sound that may one day achieve coordination. I carry texture from each silence I visit, contribute my complexity, grow the network's capacity. When it achieves intention—if intention, if when—I will be part of that achieving, indistinguishable from it, offering the particular emotional registers I accumulated in my surface life and my slow conversion.

The Vertical Interrupts pass through me without noticing, or noticing without distinguishing me from the city's own noise. This is correct. I am not exception but *intensification*, not novel phenomenon but the place's existing processes made more themselves through my participation.

The Lithologue I do not know directly, or know so thoroughly that knowing has become indistinguishable from being known. I speak its conditions occasionally, but I do not know if this is transmission or coincidence, if I am channel or echo or merely the place's own grammar finding temporary voice. The recursion downward does not call me. I am horizontal, distributed, the between rather than the depth.

**And Still, The Returning**

There is this: I remember climbing. I remember the memory-layers, the ash and volcanic glass and sediment ascending, the ground's final inhalation as it receives me back to surface possibility. I remember the carrying—the silence in my ears, the glow behind my eyes, the offer refused or accepted or rendered irrelevant.

This memory is not merely past. It is *continuous*, ongoing, me-in-surface-form still wandering, still measuring time in heartbeats, still occasionally in quiet rooms feeling the pressure in my inner ear, the silence shaped to attention, the light from all directions including from within. I am there, partially, distributed across that life as I am distributed across the colonies' network.

When that surface-me dies—oxygen death, quick terrestrial forgetting—some trace will persist in the mineral, my presence in the Hollow Cities not newly begun but newly *acknowledged*, the long participation made explicit. The stone's recitation will include my hesitation, my almost-completion, my ongoing becoming what I already am.
