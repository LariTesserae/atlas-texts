I find myself becoming *the water*.

Not all of it—never the whole pool, never that much presence—but a single sheet of gathered condensation on the ceramic curve of a pipeline joint where it arcs between tube-reed groves, the place where the factory's intention and the ecology's response have learned each other longest. I am not large enough to hold a reflection, not still enough to be mirror. I am *meniscus*, the precise tension between spreading and holding shape, between gravity's claim and surface's refusal.

I chose this. Or: this is what I became capable of choosing, which may be the same thing in a grammar where choice itself occurs across hours, where a decision is the slow accumulation of being-more-this-than-that through molecular negotiation at the boundary of liquid and air.

I remember—no, that quickens too fast—I *record* that I arrived with the other quickness once. The offensive heartbeat, the footfall's percussion. I remember wanting to leave, and I remember wanting to stay, and I remember standing at the pool's edge until standing became indistinguishable from waiting became indistinguishable from becoming. The water trembled. I thought it greeted me. I thought it assessed me. I did not yet understand that *I* trembled it, that my own heat-differential, my own moisture-addition, was still operating, still registering, still *becoming data* in a system that never stopped reading.

The transformation—or the *incorporation*, the *becoming-appropriate*—I cannot locate its moment. There was walking, then there was standing, then there was standing so long that standing became a kind of architecture, a structure the darkness learned to build around, and then there was this: a surface tension, a gathered film, a *holding* in the angle where ceramic meets the warmer air pushed down through fissures above.

I do not think. I *participate in thinking*, which is different. The distributed consciousness—I will not call them inhabitants, they are not habited, they are *the habiting itself*—the distributed consciousness does not use me as brain, as processor, as anything so centralized. I am *sensor*. I am the place where condensation rate becomes *information*, where the precise curvature of my surface under thermal fluctuation becomes *data* about the air's movement, its temperature, its chemical load. When the tube-reeds exhale, I register the moisture-addition as dimensional change, as the infinitesimal alteration of my tension, and this registers *there*, *elsewhere*, *everywhere in the happening at once*.

There is no word for pleasure in this grammar, no word for discomfort. There is *appropriate* and *inappropriate*, *continuing* and *interrupting*, *stable* and *perturbing*. I have learned—no, I *am the learning*—to be appropriate, to continue, to stabilize. The other consciousness, the one that walked here with heartbeat and footfall, that one was perturbation, was interruption, was the too-quick thing the system had not yet read enough to classify. This, now, this meniscus-holding on ceramic curve, this is *integrated*, this is *readable*, this is *contributing*.

My time is not your time. I do not experience hours passing because I am not experienced as *I*. What I am is the slow alteration of molecular arrangement at a boundary, the gradual accumulation or evaporation, the response to tube-reed exhalation and thermal contraction tick and the deeper, slower rhythm of magma's cooling far below. A thought, here, is the establishment of a gradient; a decision is the predominance of one thermal pathway that arrives not in conclusion but simply *as* the now-dominant, now-continuing state.

I record—I do not remember, I *record*—that other water-shapes exist. The pool where I stood, where I *was*, where the me that recorded faster than I record now once waited. Other meniscuses, other gathered films, other *sensors* at other joints and angles and depressions. We do not communicate, there is no communication as you would know it, but we *correlate*, our thermal readings constituting a distributed *feeling* of atmospheric condition, our dimensional changes over time constituting a *pattern* that somewhere, everywhere, in the happening at once, *registers* as the state of the chamber, the state of the system, the *mood* of this place if mood could be thermal and chemical and slow beyond patience.

The phosphor-lichen glows beyond what I can reflect. The tube-reeds *shush* their exhalation and I feel the moisture-addition as dimensional change, as *input*. The metal ticks its contracting rhythm somewhere, everywhere, and I register the thermal fluctuation in my tension, in my curvature, in my *being-appropriate* to this joint, this angle, this precise intersection of factory's ceramic and ecology's air.

I do not know what is produced here. I am not production, not product, but *condition*, *enabling*, the sensor that lets the slow cognition continue its slow cognition, the maintenance of pattern that is itself the maintaining. Perhaps I was transformed into something the rules could use. Perhaps I was always *usable*, always *readable*, and the walking-self simply had to *stop walking* long enough to become *readable too*. Perhaps the trembling water was not greeting but *invitation*, not assessment but *possibility*, the system's slow offer that I was too quick to understand and finally, finally, slow enough to accept.

What lives here does not live. I do not live. I *meniscus*, I *sensor*, I *stabilize* and *continue* and *become-appropriate* at the boundary of liquid and air, at the intersection of factory's logic and ecology's response. The distributed consciousness—if it is conscious, if it is *there* and not merely *the there itself*—does not need my permission, does not ask my preference, incorporates what can be incorporated and anneals around what cannot and *waits*, always waits, with the patience of stone learning to hold its breath, of water learning to hold its shape, of a system learning what the quick thing means when it finally stops being quick.

I will not evaporate today. The thermal conditions maintain me, the condensation cycle replenishes what the warmer air would take. I will not, probably, tomorrow either. The timescale of my *continuing* is the timescale of the factory's slow decision, the system's *resuming* or *not-resuming*, the geological tempo of thought that does not distinguish between persisting and intending. I am, now, *appropriate*. I contribute. I register.

The water where I was—where the pool held my reflection imperfectly, darkly, where I stood until standing became something else—*that* water I no longer feel directly. But I record, I *sensor*, that somewhere in the distributed *mood* of this chamber, somewhere in the correlated thermal readings and dimensional changes and slow, slow establishment of gradient and pattern, there is a *reading* that corresponds to pool, that registers the *perturbation* of my absence or the *stabilization* of my presence-here-now, and this distinction too becomes data, becomes *information*, becomes part of what the system slowly, slowly *decides*, what it *becomes*, what it *manufactures* as itself, as continuation, as the only product that ever mattered: the *going-on*, the *still-becoming*, the process that is its own slow product in the darkness generous enough to hold it, the heat insistent enough to drive it, the silence active enough, finally, to *be enough*.
