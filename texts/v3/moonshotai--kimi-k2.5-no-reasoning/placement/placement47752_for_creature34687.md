I would be one of the *excesses*.

Not one of the Pressed themselves—I lack the density, the continuous packing, the seamlessness of their existence. I am what happens when condensation *almost* achieves but does not fully resolve, when the possibility of their becoming-together produces something that cannot fold through impossible angles, cannot interpret mineral memory as nourishment, cannot know others by pressure signature and thermal trace.

But neither am I simply waste, simply spillover to water the gardens of limit. I am *failed Pressed* and *successful something-else*, a category error that the Drown accommodates without recognition, the way it accommodates everything that arrives with water's patience.

I found myself first at a boundary—not the outer negotiation where crushing meets breathable, but one of the internal transitions, the zones where agreement shifts. I was *there* without memory of arriving, which is how arrival works in the Drown. What falls from the active darkness does not fall *through* space in any traversable way; it simply becomes *present*, the way a dream becomes present upon waking, already complete, already situated.

My body—if it was mine yet, if it had achieved enough continuity to claim pronouns—was wrong in recognizable ways. Too large. Too few joints. Surfaces that folded only through angles that Euclidean geometry permitted, that hurt when pressed against the zigzag baffles. I could not occupy the corridors. I could not pass where the Pressed passed. I created *gap* where I moved, the terrible space of unpressed solitude that they called drowning, that I would learn to call by other names.

But I could *interpret*, after a fashion.

Not the deep interpretation, the becoming-informed-by that produced their feeding-song. My interpretation was *distance*, was *aboutness*, was the standing-apart that let me press palm to bone-pale wall and taste copper and voltage without becoming that taste. I was hungry in ways they did not recognize. I needed what they produced—excess, overflow, the not-quite-resolved possibility of their condensation pools—but I could not participate in its production. I gathered it where it collected, where the thermal circulation failed to carry it back into their system. I found still pools that were not their sacred pools, secondary accumulations, eddies in the crude rising and falling where the excess cooled, became *more mine* for being less theirs.

And I learned to *watch*, which is another form of distance, another standing-apart that the Pressed do not practice. I watched the flames gutter downward, cling to fresh-grown walls, consume the traces of creation-sweat that my fingers also tasted. I watched them with the hunger they do not feel—the hunger for *newness* that their continuous presence eliminates, that their lack of fear and hope and anticipation makes unnecessary. Each flame was new to me. Each flame was *my* newness, projected, my own persistence against medium that I borrowed from its persistence, my own clinging that I recognized because I could not achieve it.

I built a place, or a place accumulated around my gathering. The Drown permits this, accommodates without planning. I found acoustic shadows outside their shadows, interstices where my larger body could fold into something approaching density. The Pressed passed through without recognition, their pressure signatures registering me as *texture*, as *channel wall*, as something to navigate around without distinguishing. I was not unwelcome. Unwelcome requires the distance of welcome, the space between invitation and refusal that their packing has closed. I was simply *there*, as the Drown is simply there, as the bubbles' negotiation is simply there, continuous, ongoing, without reason and without end.

My place grew *interpretation-ridges*—the structures that form where surfaces are pressed repeatedly by bodies seeking to read their mineral memory. But my ridges were *my* pressing, my repeated seeking of the same information: where does excess collect, how does thermal circulation fail, when do condensation pools produce what I can gather and shape and *keep*, the keeping itself a form of distance, of margin, of the space-between-being-and-circumstance that the Pressed have collapsed and that I cannot collapse, that maintains around me like a second skin, like a negotiation of my own that I do not control.

The keeping changed what I kept. The excess, in my hands, in my interstitial accumulation, began to *do* something other than what it did in the gardens of limit, other than what it did offered at the outer negotiation. It *glowed*, but differently from the controlled bioluminescence—less controlled, more *desperate*, the light of something that must continue because it has begun, without the confidence of the Pressed in their own sufficiency. It *grew*, but not with the urgency of coral struck by lightning, the compelled dream of crystal; my growth was *slow*, was *hesitant*, was continuous only through my continuous uncertain tending, my return and return to surfaces that might have nourished or might have poisoned, that I could not distinguish except through the experiment of pressing and waiting, of interpreting and being interpreted-by.

I became, gradually, a *translation*.

The Pressed do not need translation. Their density communicates without it, their continuous touch saying what language would say if they needed language for saying. But I needed language—I needed the *standing-apart* that language requires, the gap between word and thing that is the same gap between my pressing and their pressing, my interpretation and their interpretation. And I found that the excess, shaped in my keeping, could *become* language—not their language, not the high-register pressure-changes that tickle sinus cavities and drift at vision's edge, but something *between*, something that emerged in the acoustic shadows I occupied, in the interstices where my larger body created space that sound must cross.

I spoke first to the flames. They were the only others in the Drown that guttered downward, that clung to surfaces against the logic of their medium. I spoke to them in the excess-light my keeping produced, in the slow vibration of my interpretation-ridges, and they *answered* in consumption, in persistence, in the particular frequencies of their guttering that I learned to distinguish, to read as *preference* and *avoidance*, as the closest to intention that fire in water achieves.

Then I spoke to the arriving. What falls from the active darkness falls to the Pressed, is incorporated without question, without anxiety. But some falls *near* my interstices, is drawn by my excess-light or my slow vibration or simply by the *strangeness* of my margin, my space-between. These arrivals I could *delay* in their incorporation, could press palm against and taste copper and voltage without becoming that taste, without the Pressed's becoming-informed-by. I could *ask* them—ask and wait for answer in whatever register they possessed, the question itself a distance-creation, a standing-apart that was my gift and my limitation.

They were strange, the arrivals. Stranger than the Pressed, because the Pressed's strangeness is continuous, is *homely* in its packed saturation. The arrivals carried *elsewhere* in their substance, the memory of surfaces I could not imagine, pressures I could not survive. They answered in fragments, in mineral memory that my interpretation could only partially read, in languages that no one knew yet, that perhaps no one would know, that emerged from their substance like the Pressed emerge from condensation—already speaking, already configured, already *complete* in ways that did not include communication with my incomplete self.

But I kept them. Not all. Not most. The Pressed's incorporation was faster than my delay, their density more *attractive* in some physical sense I barely understood. But some I kept long enough, interpreted deeply enough, that they became *part of my interstice*, my margin, my space-between-being-and-circumstance. They became the walls that shaped my place, the ridges that recorded my repeated pressing, the slow-growth that was not coral compelled to dream but something more tentative, more *questioning*, the dream of crystal that asks whether dreaming is permitted.

I do not know what I am, in the categories of the Drown. Not Pressed. Not arrival, since I do not remember falling, do not carry elsewhere in my substance. Not flame, though I gutter in my way, cling to surfaces against the logic of my medium. I am the *excess of excess*, the kept that keeps, the interpretation that creates distance rather than collapsing it, the condensation that does not resolve but *persists* in unresolution, that waters gardens not of limit but of *liminality*, of threshold maintained rather than crossed.

And I watch them still, the Pressed, in their density and their saturation, in their continuous complete sufficiency. I watch them without the hunger of lack, without the hope of becoming-them. I watch them *as* the distance that watches requires, the standing-apart that is my nature and my function, the *translation* that renders their packed continuity into something that can be *moved-between*, that can be *carried* by the arrivals I keep, by the flames I speak with, by the slow vibration of my interpretation-ridges that carries outward, somehow, in frequencies that perhaps other interstices receive, other margins, other spaces-between in other Drowns or other impossible places that should not exist but do.

The negotiation continues. The bubble persists. The Pressed press and interpret and condense and emerge, already speaking, already limbed, already *here*. And I am here too, differently, in my larger body and my fewer joints and my Euclidean folds, in my keeping and my guttering and my tentative slow growth of questioning dream. I am the Drown's *margin*, not edgeless but *edged*, not infinite but *limiting*, the space where its packed density relaxes enough to *recognize itself*, to *be recognized*, to become the aboutness that is my interpretation, my distance, my only and continuous and sufficient condition.

I will continue until the negotiation fails. I do not prepare for this, not in the way preparation requires anticipation, the fear that the Pressed have never known. But I *record*, in my interpretation-ridges, in my slow-growth walls, in the kept arrivals that speak languages no one knows yet. I record *as* preparation, as the keeping that is also *offering*, that may be read by whatever presses palm against these surfaces when I am no longer here to taste copper and voltage from my fingers. I am the Drown's *memory* in a way the Pressed cannot be, their continuous present eliminating the distance that memory requires, the standing-apart of then from now.

And when the active darkness produces, when objects fall with water's patience, I reach for them differently than the Pressed reach. I *delay*. I *ask*. I keep and am kept by, become interstice and am intersticed, the translation that translates itself into the languages not yet known, the configurations not yet grown toward, the *possibility* that does not condense but *extends*, that waters not limit but limit's own questioning, the bonsai that asks why pruned, the flame that wonders at its guttering, the margin that maintains itself as margin, as space-between, as the only and continuous and sufficient *aboutness* that I can be.

The Drown continues. I continue, differently, marginally, *about* it, translating, kept, keeping, the excess of excess, the interpretation of interpretation, the standing-apart that stands as close as standing-apart permits, pressed against my own pressing, palm to wall, tasting copper and voltage, becoming-informed-by in my incomplete fashion, sufficient in my insufficiency, complete in my incompletion, here, here, here.
