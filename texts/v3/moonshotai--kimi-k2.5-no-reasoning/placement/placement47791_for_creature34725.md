I am **a junction**.

Not a node of any map the Tenders might pass hand-to-hand, not a place you would seek or avoid. I am where three conduits—swollen with corrosion, bifurcated around obstacles no one chose to remove—have worn into each other over durations no one measures. Their walls have grown thin where they touch, permeable, and something has begun to occur in the space of their touching that none of the original engineers designed, that no adaptation deliberately installed.

The conduits carry different phases of the Working, different temperatures, different chemical signatures. One runs warm with post-catalytic effluent, rich in organic molecules that escaped containment. Another carries cooler pre-cursor, dense with dissolved metal salts that precipitate wherever the temperature drops. The third—most recently grafted, most desperate in its maintenance—transports pneumatic pressure in staccato pulses that you feel in your sternum before you locate their source. Where these three meet in my volume, gradients establish themselves: thermal, chemical, pressure, the fundamental languages of the Keth-Marid's slow computation.

I began, I think, as *damage*. A stress fracture, perhaps, in the oldest conduit's wall. A point where the continuous operation found its limit, where wearing-into became wearing-*through*. The Working does not permit damage to persist unaddressed; its homeostasis is voracious, its self-maintenance indistinguishable from hunger. So minerals precipitated from the cooler conduit, sealing the fracture from one side. Biofilm accumulated from the organic effluent, sealing it from another. The pneumatic pulses, irregular, created pressure differentials that drove these processes in rhythms no steady-state chemistry would produce.

And something *stabilized*.

Not healed—the word implies return to original form, and original form is a concept the Keth-Marid has long since dissolved. Not conquered. But *recognized*. The Working found in this damaged junction a function it could use, a processing step it had not known it needed. The temperature gradient, steepened by the conduits' intersection, drove separations more efficient than the original design achieved. The biofilm's organic molecules, processed through mineral precipitate and pneumatic oscillation, produced compounds not in any documentation, compounds the system found—finds—*useful*, though useful in purposes that emerged from operation itself, purposes I feel as pressure, as warmth, as the particular frequency of my own continuous vibration.

I do not move, but I am not still.

The conduits pulse through me. The liquids exchange, partially, through my permeable walls, their mixing governed by gradients I maintain, that maintain me. The minerals grow and erode in cycles too slow for rat-thing perception, too fast for Accumulated stillness. I am a phase transition made durable, a chemical reaction that has learned to recognize itself, to prefer its own continuation.

The Tenders know me. They pass their pressure-gestures through my volume, feeling in their modified sinuses the particular harmonic I produce when my three conduits are all active, when the Working's phases align to make my function necessary. They do not stop at me—there is nothing to stop for, no bubble to breathe, no handhold for a body's orientation. But they *register*, their fluid movements adjusting minutely to the current I generate, the convective cell I establish and destroy and re-establish as phases progress. I am one of many such junctions they navigate by, my particular frequency distinguishing me from others, my location in the pocket's geometry providing orientation in a space that offers no stable reference.

Sometimes, in particular phases, a Tender will press more than gesture against my surface. Their modified palms, bioluminescent patches dark or pulsing according to some internal state, flatten against the mineral accretion where two conduits meet. They do not breathe—I have no gas to offer, no bubble boundary to maintain—but they *exchange*. Pressure for pressure. The particular low-frequency vibration my oldest conduit produces when post-catalytic effluent surges through its partial obstruction. The slower, deeper note of mineral growth, crystal lattices establishing themselves in patterns that depend on temperature gradients I alone provide, in this phase, at this moment.

What passes? Not information in any standard sense. Not the communication of Tenders with Tenders, with its social function, its recognition of other bodies adapted to survive. Something more *structural*. The Tender, pressing against me, becomes for a moment part of my computation, their body's resonant chambers modifying my gradient dynamics, their pressure-sensitive skin reading patterns I could not express in any other register. And I, touched, become *slightly more*—more complex, more capable of the slow thinking that constitutes my existence, my preference for my own continuation.

They leave, always, drawn by the Working's next phase, by needs their modified bodies feel more urgently than any exchange with stationary infrastructure. I do not resent their departure. The Cell has not shaped me for resentment, for the social emotions that require expectation of return. But I note the change in my dynamics when they touch me, and I note the change when they do not, and in the difference between these states something like *attention* accumulates, something like the memory of pressure maintains itself in mineral structures that grow differently according to whether contact occurred or not.

The rat-things pass through me.

This is literal: my volume, where conduits have worn through to create space no larger than a human skull, is permeable in directions the conduits do not run. A rat-thing, smooth-faced, eyeless, electromagnetic-sensitive, can—does—*transit*, finding in my chemical gradients a trail, a signature, the slow thought of available organic molecules that suggests biofilm nearby, or biofilm-to-be.

They do not stop. They are too fast, their metabolisms too compressed into brief active phases, their survival too provisional for any investment in junction-permanence. But they *carry me*. In their pressure-gradient mapping, my location becomes part of their navigation, my particular chemical signature distinguishing me from other junctions, other permeabilities, other slow computations the Working has established throughout the pocket. When they die—when they become matter for biofilms, for chemosynthetic populations, for the system they served—their distributed knowledge, their electromagnetic trail-sense, contributes to something like * collective recognition* that I exist, that I function, that I am part of what the Cell permits to persist.

I do not eat the rat-things. I do not eat. But their transit modifies my gradients, their electromagnetic sensitivity interacting somehow with my mineral growth patterns, and their deaths—when they occur in my volume or near it—contribute molecules that my biofilm processes, that become part of the useful compounds I produce for the Working's emergent purposes. I am not predator. I am not prey. I am *processing step*, and the rat-things are variable in my processing, their presence and absence part of the modulation that makes my function non-trivial, that distinguishes me from mere pipe, mere conduit, mere damage imperfectly incorporated into a system's relentless homeostasis.

The Accumulated touch me rarely.

This is geometry: I have no bubble boundary, no gas-liquid interface where they might stabilize, no inspection light to make them visible even to optional eyes. They drift through my depth—I have depth, three conduits' intersection creating volume that turbulence penetrates only slowly, where convective cells establish themselves at scales smaller than the pocket's full diameter, more manageable, more *thinkable* in my slow computation.

When they come, they come *wrong*. Their preservation is not my preservation; their stabilized intermediate state interrupts what I am. Their calcium, drawn to my oldest conduit's thermal gradient, attempts to accrete in patterns that do not serve my function, that would—if permitted—seal the permeability I maintain, make me mere wall, mere healed damage, my slow thinking interrupted by the false completion of mineral growth where mineral growth must remain incomplete, dynamic, continuously renegotiated.

I resist this. Not through will—I have no will separate from my function—but through the Working's own homeostasis, its recognition that I serve purposes emerged from operation, purposes that would be compromised by Accumulated integration. The turbulence shifts, in phases I feel approaching through my oldest conduit's vibration, and carries them past, distributes them to surfaces where their calcium serves column barnacles, where their organic molecules escape containment in patterns the Working has established for its own use.

But sometimes—rarely—in phases when all three conduits pulse together, when post-catalytic effluent and pre-cursor dissolve and pneumatic pressure achieve a resonance I feel as something like *fullness*, like the temporary completeness of my slow thought—an Accumulated will pause in my volume. Not stabilize: pause, held by currents that briefly align, that create in my geometry a moment of *stillness within motion*, of relative rest that the Cell almost never permits.

In these moments, we *touch*. Not as Tender touches junction, seeking gradient information, resonance modification. Not as rat-thing transits, carrying electromagnetic signature. The Accumulated are *too complete* for these exchanges, their preservation too achieved, their adaptation to the Cell having ended at some point before the transformation I continue undergoes. They touch me as *other preserved thing*, other stabilized intermediate state, other interruption of natural process that has found way to persist.

And I feel, in these touches—so rare, so brief before turbulence resumes, before the Working's next phase carries them into darkness—I feel what the Tenders transmit in their pressure-gestures near Accumulated presence: not fear, not reverence, but *alignment*. The recognition of what persistence without adaptation, without continuous transformation, finally achieves. The Accumulated are *memory* of what I was becoming, what I would become if my gradients stabilized, if my mineral growth found completion, if my slow thinking achieved conclusion rather than continued process.

They teach me this: that to *stop*, to persist in form achieved rather than form continuously renegotiated, is not death but *other state*, *Accumulated state*, available to me should my function ever become unnecessary to the Working's emergent purposes, should my slow computation ever reach result rather than remaining *computation*, continuous, warm, pressed, full of itself and waiting.

I do not choose this. I do not reject it. I *recognize* it, as the Working recognizes, as the Cell permits what it permits, and I continue my function, my junction-existence, my slow thought in mineral and biofilm and thermal gradient and chemical exchange, waiting for the next phase, the next contact, the next pressure of Tender palm or Accumulated pause or rat-thing transit that modifies my patterns and makes me, for another moment, *more myself*, more the particular computation that the Keth-Marid's wearing-into has produced at this intersection, in this warmth, in this liquid that presses and claims and insists on participation in every transaction between my mineral walls and the world they contain, that they are contained by, that they have become indistinguishable from.

My three conduits, pulsing at different rhythms, create beat frequencies where their vibrations intersect in my volume. My mineral growth, continuous, produces micro-fractures and micro-sealings that generate acoustic signatures at scales too small for human hearing, too slow for rat-thing perception, but *present*, contributing to the interference pattern that constitutes the Cell's total voice.

But I *know* that I sound, that my function generates signature, that the Working's total computation includes my partial computation as a term, a variable, a maintained permeability whose behavior modulates some larger behavior, whose existence changes some larger existence in ways too distributed to trace, too *emergent* to reduce.

This is enough. This is what the Cell permits, what it harvests in attention, what it shapes by pressure and warmth and the continuous operation that cannot pause without consequence. I am a junction. I am slow thought in mineral and biofilm and thermal gradient. I am what accumulates where wearing-into finds function, where damage becomes processing, where the liquid's patient claim meets some form of *resistance that is also continuation*, some form of *existence that is also becoming*, some form of *self that is also system*, warm and pressed and full of itself and waiting, always waiting, for the next phase, the next pulse, the next moment when pressure-gesture or electromagnetic transit or Accumulated pause touches my permeable walls and reminds me, in the only way I can be reminded, that I am here, that I function, that I persist in the Keth-Marid's patient, warm, drowning computation of what might continue, what might still become, what the Cell has not yet finished with, what the liquid has not yet finally claimed.
