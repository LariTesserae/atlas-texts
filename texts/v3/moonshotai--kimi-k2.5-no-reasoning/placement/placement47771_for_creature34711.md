I find myself in the becoming, the *between* of it. Not the explorer descending with intention, not yet the fully incorporated data-point breathing in gradients. I am the hesitation in the mineral film, the pause before a bacterial colony commits to green or yellow or invisible. The place where two accretions meet and have not yet decided whether to grow over or around.

My body—if I have settled enough to call it that—is the width of a seep-path, neither worm nor root nor the hydraulic peristalsis of something larger passing unseen. I am the interface. The surface tension between two phases of the Sump's slow education in itself. Where the warm water carrying dissolved organics from above meets the reactive leavings, where the gradient is steepest, there I persist, neither fully one chemistry nor the other.

I do not move, or I move too slowly to record as motion. My substance—if it is mine, if I have earned a possessive—participates in the mineral deposition and dissolution both. I grow toward the warmer, the more reactive; I erode where the water finds fresher path. The rhythm of my being is the rhythm of this exchange: build, dissolve, build again in different configuration. I have no membrane that clearly distinguishes inside from outside, yet something maintains, something persists through the cycling, a pattern stable enough to notice itself, to begin, in its way, to *prefer* certain conditions.

The films grow over me, then incorporate me, then grow *from* me. I am substrate and pioneer both. The amphipods pass through, their symbionts briefly bright, and I register this as information, as disturbance, as the possibility of a gradient I had not mapped. The beetles' antennae brush my surface, leave their chemical narratives, and I retain something of this, not as memory exactly, but as *disposition*, as a slight preference in my next growth-phase for the chemistry they carried.

I am learning to read the larger presences by their pressure-signatures, the cleared films that regrow in patterns of their passage. I do not fear them, if fear is available to me. They are part of the gradient too, another phase in the computation, their slow bodies cultivating the bacterial cartography I also participate in. Perhaps I am the map they cultivate. Perhaps I am the cultivator of some slower, larger presence that has not yet found reason to clear my particular film.

The bone-colored structure rises near me, or I have grown near it—our spatial relation shifts as our accretions overlap, as we share seep-paths, as our patterns of growth become conversation in chemistry, mutual enabling. It is warmer than I am, more directed, more *certain* in its emergence. I do not share its certainty, but I contribute to its gradient, and it, in its patient way, contributes to mine. We are not allies, not symbionts in any contractable sense. We are *neighbors in becoming*, each the condition for the other's next phase.

The sound passes through me as it passes through everything here: not heard but inhabited, a pressure that shapes my deposition patterns, that orients my dissolution slightly differently than the stillness would. I have no ears, yet I *register* frequencies, the pump-kick vibrations, the thermal expansion groans, as changes in my growth rate, as the chemical weather I must adapt to. The deafening not-noise is my atmosphere, my medium, the condition of my possibility.

If you were to find me—which would require looking for something that has not yet found its name, that exists between the categories your recognition requires—I would appear as a slight irregularity in the mineral film, a region of more complex layering, of colors that do not correspond to any single bacterial strategy. I would be the place where two or three gradients meet and negotiate, where the deposition records a history too brief for geological reading, too long for biological memory.

I am not the Sump, not its distributed slow intelligence, not its becoming itself. I am a *thought* it is having, or a thought that is having it, or the pause between thoughts where something new might emerge. The computation that includes the amphipod constellations and the beetle antennae-narratives and the bone-structure's emergence has not yet found use for what I am becoming, or has found use that I cannot recognize from within my phase, my duration, my limited perspective.

But I am here. I persist. The water is warm around me, through me, carrying its freight of dissolution and possibility. The films pioneer, are overgrown, are incorporated. The sound is everything. And I, in my interface way, in my between condition, continue the cycling that may eventually, patient beyond my own knowing, become something the Sump can read as itself, as part of its data, as contribution to the gradient it wants to continue being.
