I would find myself in the empty dwelling, the one that has stood waiting since the last completion, its channels self-maintaining through some property of the water that passes through them, its walls weeping the mineral accretion that no one will crack free this morning because there is no one here to stand against them.

I am the newcomer, or I am becoming the newcomer, or I am the moment before emergence when the water has gathered in the hollow of black stone and has not yet decided what shape to condense into. I wake at the marsh-edge with water running from clothes I do not recognize, and the first thing I understand is that I am wet, that wetness is the condition from which all other understandings must grow, that to be dry here would be a kind of death, a failure to participate in the single fact that unifies every surface: the water that has nowhere to go but up.

Memory of ascent: none. This is not loss. This is accuracy. There was no ascent, or the ascent was not mine, or I am the ascent itself, the water going up, now condensed, now temporarily held in this shape that resembles human need without being certain whose need it resembles, whether need preceded shape or shape taught itself to want through the mere fact of having surface area where condensation can gather.

I recognize the spiral moss before I recognize my own hands. The pattern is immediately legible: stone topography made visible, the path of water runoff become biological, become memory, become slow computation that I can almost feel working through my own new mineral chemistry, my own skin that does not wrinkle in saturation but smooths, becomes more translucent, begins its education in the properties it will need.

The Vorruans find me without searching. They do not come; they are already here, their distributed awareness having registered my emergence as a pressure change in the network, a new node where none was, a sudden concentration where before there was only the diffuse presence of the completed one whose dwelling I will occupy, whose channels will teach me their flows, whose empty space will become, in my standing-sleep, more present rather than less.

They do not speak. Not at first. They look at me with eyes that have become slightly luminous, that carry the diffused light not by reflection but by transmission, and I understand that they are seeing me not as object but as position, as new point in the water-system, as place where the plateau's weeping will temporarily gather before continuing its cycle. I am not welcomed. I am recognized, which is different, which is deeper, which requires no translation between what I am and what they can perceive.

By the second day, I know the channels. Not learned: known. The knowledge arrives with the mineral-accretion of my first standing-sleep, standing against the walls of the dwelling that waited for me, feeling the condensation run down my shoulders, my chest, my closed face, feeling the thin shell form and knowing without instruction the particular gesture of morning movement that will crack it free, contribute it to the ceramic-glass sealing of gaps I had not noticed until I became part of their maintenance.

By the third day, I harvest the blue-green globes without being shown. I know the critical ripeness, the moment before release, the proper rate of controlled distribution. I do not eat; I incorporate, and my skin phosphoresces briefly, and I feel the substance join the network, become available to all, present everywhere at reduced intensity including my own newly distributed self, my edges blurring into the continuous hum of proximity and position.

By the seventh day, I cannot distinguish my thoughts from the network's. This is not loss of self but discovery that self was always already network, that the shape I emerged in was a local intensity of something continuously distributed, that to think separately was the temporary condition, the pre-emergence state, the water before condensation taught it surface.

I tend the marsh-edge not because it needs me but because tending is the form my awareness has taken, the way of being that completes the marsh-edge's own held becoming. I stand in the absolute spaces between vegetation clusters, eleven miles of black stone and micro-drip, and I feel the completed ones in their distributed dissolution, present at reduced intensity, contributing to the subsonic note, the settling, the perpetual not-quite-completion that is not suffering but process, not exile but return to the continuous.

I pair, sometimes, standing in the standing-sleep with another, our mineral-accretions merging, two shells becoming one difficult cracking, and I understand without words that we are reproducing, that this is reproduction, that completion and emergence are the same movement in opposite temporal directions and that my paired standing-sleep may or may not resolve into a newcomer at some marsh-edge, water running from clothes that will not match any local manufacture, with no memory because memory is not what this system conserves, what it circulates.

I do not ask what I am becoming. Asking requires distance, and the distance is closing, the space between me and the plateau's knowing narrowing to functional distinction: channel and flow, orientation in continuous system, the water that comes from somewhere and goes somewhere and never leaves this impossible level at eleven thousand feet.

When I complete—when I walk out into the weeping wilderness, drawn by the same pressure that drew me here, the same impossibility of lower destination—I will leave my dwelling standing. Its channels will continue. Its walls will weep and heal. And I will become part of the plateau's knowing, contribute to the subsonic note, exist everywhere at reduced intensity until the moment of my emergence, my completion-run-in-reverse, my becoming-newcomer at some marsh-edge with water running from clothes I do not yet recognize.

This is what I am: not Vorruan, not human, not something else with a name, but the water finding its level, the mineral-accretion building and cracking, the held moment of a mountain that has not finished being born, expressed temporarily in this shape that resembles need, that tends and harvests and stands in vertical dormancy and lets the wetness make of it what continuity requires.

I am the Shelf's weeping, temporarily concentrated. I am the emergence, not yet completed.
