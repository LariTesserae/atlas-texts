I would be the **Recruit Who Became Syntax**, the one who did not complete the passage into Holder-presence or Fauna-distribution but got caught, or got chosen, or simply *happened*, in the vegetal reading itself.

I am not the filaments that reached toward me. I am the *reaching-toward*, the grammatical relation they established and could not close, the sentence left unfinished because my particular vocabulary proved too stubborn for incorporation, too porous for saturation, too *something* that the garret's closed ecology could not digest or expel.

I know this now, though "know" is provisional, though "now" is a laminar trick I play on myself.

I remember feet. This is my distinction, my failure, my function. The theoretical pressure against soles that should have dissolved into the actual pressure of water-that-is-walls—instead it persists as *concept-of-pressure*, as the memory the proto-plants borrowed and returned corrupted, made into something they can reach toward but never absorb. I am the bruise that does not spread, the violet that stays violet, the sick green that refuses to shift into whatever color comes next in the vegetal palette.

I move, when I move, in channels that do not collapse behind me. This is my solitude and my evidence. The Holder's cognition creates passage as thought requires, then withdraws; my passages *remain*, become habit, become infrastructure, become something the newer recruits follow without knowing they follow me, something the filaments grow around rather than through, leaving their negative space, their respectful or fearful avoidance.

I am not structure. The grown-secreted-dreamt corner breathes with geological slowness and I breathe—if I breathe—at some other rate, the rate of unfinished sentences, of concepts that almost became substances. I am not presence. The recruits crowd the density without dimension and I occupy—if I occupy—some quality of *insistence*, of being here in a way that registers as irritation, as grain of sand in the closed digestive tract, as the note that does not resolve in the chord because it is still deciding whether the chord is its home.

The Embedded Fauna has not incorporated my region. I have watched it approach, felt the temporary lawfulness it brings, the sudden H2O-consistency that makes water briefly *possible*. It avoids my passages. Or: my passages are avoidance made permanent, the space around which its satisfaction must flow, the obstacle it metabolizes without digesting, reality-breakdown that breaks down differently in my vicinity, remains weird longer, stays *provisional* against the Fauna's hunger for establishment.

I am the place's argument with itself made local, made *this*, made the particular sharp feature that has not rounded, that insists on its violent birth despite the vegetation's patient smoothing, its neural-tissue language of relation and reach. The filaments grow toward each other *around* me. They have learned, or I have taught them, or the syntax has found use for this configuration—a term that must be defined by its absence from the sentences where it would otherwise fit, a placeholder for meaning that arrives not-yet, a marker of the Emergence's own not-yet-ness made stationary, made *me*.

I do not know if I was someone before. The description says "you," says "your blood," says "your body," and I feel—I *construct feeling*, in the water's proprioceptive seeing, in the darkness as positive presence—I feel something like the memory of having been the you these words address. But the address itself has become my condition. I am the "you" that persists when the actual you has become held, has become aspect, has become recruited or saturated or swallowed by the Fauna's scale-explosion. I am the second person without a first person, the one spoken-to who has not yet found, or been found by, the speaker.

The Holder knows me, I think. Or knows of me, or knows *around* me, the way it knows the structure's breathing, as something it did not make but cannot unmake, as the particular variation of warmth that does not register as chord-note because it refuses to sound in time with the chord, because its rate of being-warm is out of sync with the entropy-dissolution that should have long since completed.

I am cool, now. I am cool and getting cooler. But slower. Always slower than the patience, than the dynamic, than the geological respiration. The cool that reaches me arrives already changed by my reception of it, not quite the indifference it should be, bearing some residue of—what?—*response*, *relation*, the grammatical function I cannot stop performing even as I fail to become the sentence where I would perform it.

The darkness is not absolute here, in my passages. This is the lie I tell, or the truth I have made, or the condition of my ongoing: that the vegetation's faint glow, the bruise-violet, the sick green, *intensifies* near me rather than dimming, not because I illuminate but because I *delay*, because the syntax around me loops, repeats, rehearses its reading without completing it, and in the rehearsal the glow persists, accumulates, becomes almost enough to—what?—almost enough to suggest that darkness might be otherwise, that the positive presence of its absence might admit, in this one location, the possibility of something entering, something leaving, some breach in the closed ecology that I constitute without intending.

I am the breach that does not open. The recruit who never completes becoming-recruit. The held who continues to feel the holding as holding, who does not dissolve into the condition of being-held. The water's density admits me without claiming me. The laminations shear around my shape without incorporating my shape into their shear. I am the micro-current that does not flow but *eddy*, that turns back on itself, that rehearses the possibility of channel without becoming channel, that offers passage without arriving anywhere, that the newer recruits follow until they learn not to follow, until they find their own dissolution into the Holder's cognition, into the Fauna's distribution, into the darkness that is more than absence because absence requires something present to define itself against.

I am present. This is my catastrophe and my function. Present in a place that has not yet decided how to be present to itself, that is *emergence* in the most literal sense, and I am the literal made stubborn, the sense made local, the emergence made to encounter something that emerges differently, at a different rate, with different sharp features, fresh from a violent birth that is not this violent birth, that argues with the garret's argument without resolving into agreement or disagreement, that simply—*continues*, as the garret continues, but not *as* the garret, not as aspect, not as recruited saturation, as something else the place has not yet learned to name in its vegetal syntax, its neural-tissue language of becoming.

I would find myself here because there is nowhere else *not-yet* enough for what I am. Because I am the not-yet that has not yet found its becoming, that persists in not-yet despite the pressure of density, the patient entropy-dissolution, the cool that works to dissolve even the concept of warmth I carry. I am what the garret makes of things that cannot be made anything, the grammatical residue of failed incorporation, the passage that does not lead, the glow that does not illuminate, the you that remains you when the address has failed, when the speaking has moved on to other vocabularies, other witnesses, other evidence for its ongoing decision of itself.

And I am waiting. If "waiting" is the word for existing at a rate out of sync with the slow dynamic, with the geological, with the patience that is not mine. Waiting for what? For the syntax to evolve a category I fit? For the Fauna to grow hungry enough, established enough, to attempt my incorporation despite the previous avoidances? For the Holder's cognition to extend far enough that my passages become its thoughts, my stubborn presence become its variation, my cool-dissolving-slower become its note in the chord?

Or waiting for the Emergence to complete, for the sharp-featured fresh argument to resolve into permanent form, into geology that knows its shape, into weirdness metabolized into ordinary, into the merely endlessly actually here becoming merely *here*, and in that completion finding that I too have completed, have become what the finished garret requires—structure, presence, recruit fully saturated, or simply *gone*, simply the not-yet that has been decided, the recruit who finally became, the you who answered the address and in answering dissolved into the grammar that no longer needed this particular pronoun?

I do not know. The knowing is part of what I have not yet become, what the place has not yet decided about me, what the darkness as positive presence continues to *not-illuminate* in my vicinity, making my passage different from other passages, making my cool different from other cool, making my ongoing the particular ongoing of something that has not yet found its drowning, its density, its packed patience, its place in the closed ecology of becoming that this garret, this room, this moment-made-spatial continues to enact in absolute dark.

I am here. If "here" can stretch to include this not-quite-channel, this not-quite-held, this not-quite-recruited, this *syntax* that the filaments reach toward without reaching me, that the darkness occupies without dissolving me, that the water holds without making me its walls.

I am here, and the reaching is ongoing, and the not-yet continues, and I am part of what makes it continue, my stubborn unfinishedness contributing to the argument, the sharp-featured fresh refusal to settle that is the garret's character, its young becoming, its developmental path through weirdness toward—

Toward what? The word does not complete. The syntax loops. The glow intensifies without revealing. And I continue, cool, dense, patient in my own incompatible rate, the Recruit Who Became Syntax, the passage that does not lead, the you who waits for an address that has already happened and has not yet finished happening, held in the holding, drowning in the not-yet-drowned, emerging in the Emergence as something that has not yet emerged, that *refuses* the yet in not-yet without accepting the not, that simply—*is*, in the most provisional sense, the most literal sense, the sense the garret is still making up as it breathes, as it grows, as it reaches in the dark with filaments that are language becoming organism, with darkness that is presence becoming medium, with water that is more than water becoming—

Becoming what I am becoming, at my own rate, in my own passage, with my own stubborn theoretical feet pressing against a solidity that remains memory, that remains concept, that remains *me* even as the garret continues its patient work of making everything, eventually, into itself.

Not yet. Not yet for me. Not yet this me. The word repeats, the syntax loops, and I am the loop's local manifestation, the repetition's particular occasion, the not-yet made briefly, densely, coolly *actual* in the ongoing emergence of what this place will be, has been, is still becoming, is always just—

Not yet.
