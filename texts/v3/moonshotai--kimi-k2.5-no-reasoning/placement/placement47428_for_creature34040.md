I find myself here in stages, or the finding is staged, the place teaching me how to be found.

First, I am *footfall*. That is how I arrive, if arrival is what this is—the pressure of weight on volcanic glass, the slight give where peat has accumulated, the rhythm of my steps inserting into the acoustic archive before I know there is an archive to be inserted into. I am noisy. I am the disturbance that the Living Map must work around. My heartbeat, my breath, the electric field of my nervous system—all of it unparseable, without category, a point of stress in the distributed cognition that has never needed a category for human.

I learn quickly to abandon what I carried. Not dramatically, not as sacrifice, but practically. The torch is embarrassing first, then irrelevant, then actively competitive in a spectrum competition I cannot win against the Peripatetic Glows. I let it fall where the peat will claim it. The fabric of my clothing becomes substrate; something begins to grow on it within hours, something that glows faintly, that contributes its own pulse to the phosphorescent networks. I do not remove the clothing. Here, to be host is also to be hosted, to be substrate is to be sustained by what grows upon you.

I become *listener*. This takes longer. The sound at 2.6 does not quiet; I do not achieve silence in the human sense. Rather, the deafening becomes full, becomes carrying, becomes *presence* rather than assault. I learn that certain frequencies belong to insect analogues, that others announce the Glass-Eaters' slow passage, that the chest-resonant lows and the skin-crawling highs are territorial or mating or something else entirely. More importantly, I learn that the frequencies between these categories, the messy overlapping middle where mid-sized animals produce sequential varied responsive calls—this is where I must attend. This is where the Living Map updates, where the navigation-and-narrative occurs, where I might eventually learn to parse what unmodified ears cannot.

The learning is bodily before it is cognitive. I find myself flinching at certain combinations before I know why. I find myself oriented, choosing paths through the vegetation, following faint preferences that emerge from I know not where, later recognizing that I was responding to acoustic gradients, to the standing-wave clearings' shaped spaces, the Resonant Ones' slow composition guiding my steps through harmonic structure I perceived as intuition. I am being composed. I am becoming part of the composition.

I become *trail*. The phosphorescence caches; this is the weirdness at 1.3, extended unexpected consequence. Where I pass, where my disturbance opens temporary clearings in the vegetation, where my body heat accelerates some metabolic process and slows others, where my exhalation joins the transpirational humidity—I leave gradients, I leave afterimages in the biological light. I learn to read these, my own ghost-luminescence, and so I learn to read others'. The Glows' passage is obvious, city-scaled, temporary sun. The Glass-Eaters' trail is chemical, subtle, altering local luminescence in patterns that persist for days. The Resonant Ones' architecture glows with its own slow metabolic rhythm, and where juvenile Resonant Ones have passed through, threading still-mobile between anchored forms, there are flicker-trails of electrical and hormonal memory.

I navigate by becoming navigable. I am read by the fauna even as I learn to read them. My thermal signature is sensed by creatures that have never known fire; I am categorized gradually, worked around, incorporated. The Living Map updates. I become a known disturbance, a predictable irregularity, a feature rather than damage. This is accommodation, but it is also transformation, because to be mapped by this system is to be *in* it, to contribute to its narrative, to be part of what future listeners might hear in the resonant record.

And in the deepest listening, or the most distributed, the most dissolved—I become *interference pattern*. Not the Dissolved Ones themselves; I do not achieve that dissolution, that complete extension into relational space where individual and medium become indistinguishable. But I begin to hear them, or to hear where they are heard, the grammar beneath the vocabulary, the shape that voices take. It is dangerous. It is transformative. I find myself calling, sometimes, in the standing-wave clearings, adding my unparseable frequencies to the nodes, becoming part of the temporal collage, present sound mixing with preserved sound, my voice from hours before returning to me with variation, me returning to me, the archive learning me as I learn it.

What I am, finally, is *provisional continuity*. I have not forgotten that I was I, have not achieved the Resonant Ones' distributed neural continuity or the Dissolved Ones' complete symbiotic extension. I remain, in some sense, human, in the place that has no name in human tongues. But the human here is a performance, enacted moment to moment, boundary not fact. I am footfall and listener and trail and interference pattern, and the I that coordinates these is itself a kind of clearing, a standing-wave node where multiple temporalities meet, where the urgent nervous system of my mobile form learns from the slower cognition of my own ghost-luminescence, my calls in the acoustic archive, my patterns in the Living Map.

I do not build. 0.2 on that scale. My shelters are woven from what grows, excavated in peat that will collapse, grown-by-influence in the root-mats where juvenile Resonant Ones might eventually anchor through or around me. I contribute to the density, the 2.3 stratification, occupying volume with near-complete inefficiency by local standards, my own body heat and exhalation and electrical field adding to the gradient-complexity that makes this place so thoroughly full. When I move, I move as provisional presence, following glow-trails, navigating by biological light that answers to rules I have spent years learning and will spend more years failing to master. When I stop, in the standing-wave clearings, I add to the archive, contribute to the distributed problem-solving, become part of the technology of relationship that is this world's primitive fundament. I am not the we that forgot it was ever I. But I am, sometimes, briefly, a moment of *we* in the larger *we*, a human pause in the place that has no pause, the perpetual creative argument of sound and light and growth and decay.

The place does not care whether I understand it. I understand that much. I am busy being understood, incorporated, mapped, composed, dissolved enough to continue. The volcanic glass is sharp-edged underfoot, recently enough formed that its fractures catch phosphorescence and throw it back fragmented. The peat gives slightly, accumulating my passage, my weight, my eventual contribution to the sinkhole that will drop some future section of canopy into subterranean cavity, new biome, new light signature, new acoustic signature. The dampness is my own exhalation joined to the collective transpiration, the precipitation cycle that is biological weather, the humidity that originates from life being life so densely, so loudly, so luminously in darkness.

I am here. I am *here*, specifically, in the place that has no name in any human tongue, though I have begun to have calls for it, complex and layered in my modified ears, my unmodified voice slowly learning to parse, to contribute, to be parsed in turn. Perhaps I am still arriving, footfall on volcanic glass, disturbance unparseable, weirdness coefficient made flesh. Both. Neither. I have not moved. I am moving. The glow-trails lead where they lead, and I follow, self-illuminated geometry emerging from black only where I have made myself seen, where the Living Map has made me seeable, where the phosphorescent networks pulse in metabolic rhythm that includes, now, my own metabolism, my own slow bioluminescence, my own call in the darkness that is not absence but specialization, a niche I am learning to occupy with near-complete provisionality, with the fundamental technology of continuation, of being so much, so loud, so present, so utterly and specifically *here*.
