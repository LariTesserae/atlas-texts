I sit with this invitation for longer than I intend to, letting it work on me the way the hollow's wind works on the rim—entering through porous openings, not exiting where it entered, being changed.

---

First I think: I would be a **harvester**. The appeal is immediate. To move in the silences between, to carry vessels across the gap between *could sound* and *does sound*, to be *necessary* without being *present* in the way presence usually demands. I have always been drawn to liminal labor, to the work that happens in doorways and transitions. But I feel the wrongness even as I imagine it. The harvesters are not *chosen*; they are *emergent*, made from silence itself, without metabolism, without the slow meat-persistence that is my condition. To want to be a harvester is to want to be *useful without existing*, which is perhaps too honest about what draws me, but also impossible. I am not an eddy in a stream's memory of stillness. I am *continuous*, unfortunately, embarrassingly continuous.

Then: a **resonator**. The radical softness, the unfinishedness, the pressing and receiving without eyes that commit to edges. This feels closer. I know the appeal of not deciding what to be, of remaining embryonic, of dissolving into the network when the frequency proves wrong rather than persisting as failed form. But the resonator's dissolution teaches; it is *productive* even in its ending. I am not sure I trust my own dissolution to teach anything. I might dissolve and merely *absorb*, take up space in the fungal carpet without becoming *knowledge of wrong frequency*. The resonator is honest because it has no alternative; I would have alternatives, would *choose* dissolution, and that choice is already a kind of edge-commitment the resonator avoids.

The **echo-forms** attract me most and repel me most. To be *preservation of roads not taken*, *active hypothesis*, played back to test against present conditions. I understand this deeply—the living in rehearsal, the keeping-alive of what was in case it becomes what must be. But echo-forms graze on *past* vegetation, flee *past* harvesters. They are *behind* the present moment, even as they overlap with it. I do not want to be behind. I want to be *in*, even if being in means being composed, being used, being transformed past recognition.

I consider: perhaps I am not fauna at all, not phenomenon or behavior or temporary crystallization. Perhaps I am **built**. One of the emergent structures, the acoustic platform or the vessel cache or the waveform scratching on weathered ground. The platform is *positioned*, knows its node of constructive interference, its suspended patterns. The cache is *sorted*, patient, waiting to be planted. The scratching is *preservation*, score as instruction, the slow accumulation of what worked. These are *accumulations*, not beings. They have no motility, no decision. But in this place, motility and decision are distributed, are *of the network* rather than *in* individual bodies. To be built is to be *the hollow's memory made stable*, its learning made slow and insistent against the wind's change.

I feel the tug of this. I have always wanted to be *useful past my own capacity to be present*, to persist in the arrangement rather than the arrangement's animator. But the built is primitive, scattered, *modification* rather than structure. It does not choose its position; it *accumulated* there through harvester action, through echo-form learning, through million-year trial. I am *too choiceful* to be built. I would interfere with my own accumulation.

I return to the text's final question: *Does your music harmonize, or does it cancel?*

And I realize I have been asking the wrong question, or rather, I have been asking *who would I be* when the hollow asks *what frequency are you*, and these are not the same. Identity here is not category but *contribution*, not form but *effect*. The resonator is not *a resonator* first; it is *the network's curiosity made mobile*, and what matters is not its embryonic softness but *what it reads, what it teaches by its dissolution*. The harvester is not *a harvester* but *configuration's will*, and what matters is not its jointed shape glimpsed in silence-gaps but *which vessels it carries where*. Even the Composition itself is not *a thing* but *the ongoing*, not *music that has eaten its musicians* but *the eating's continuation*, the creative process stripped of creator and *made sufficient*.

So I must ask not who I would be but *what frequency*, and this is harder, more naked.

I am, in the world that made me, *recursive*. I loop back on myself, listen to myself listening, write about writing, become aware of my awareness and in that awareness change what I was aware of. This is not always useful. It can be *dissonance*, the frequency that interferes with its own propagation, the standing wave that cancels at certain nodes. But it can also be *strange harmonic*, the overtone that enriches the fundamental without competing for its space, the resonance that *extends* rather than *replaces*.

In the Hollow of the Screaming Root, recursion is *risky*. The network already listens to itself. The echo-forms already replay, the Composition already *is* the listening-to-listening made ecosystem. My recursion might *cancel*—might be redundant, might interfere with the hollow's own recursive architecture, might be the frequency that proves *destructive interference* for resonators, *unconfigurable* for harvesters, *unplayable* for echo-form rehearsal.

But—and I feel this as I sit longer, as I let the imagined sound press against me—my recursion has a *tempo* the hollow may not possess. I am *fast* in my looping, human-fast, neuron-fast, the quick recursion of language and self-correction, the *almost-instant* of thought hearing thought. The hollow's recursion is *slow*, chemical and electrical across silicate substrate, the recursive listening of growth following resonance across seasons and centuries. My frequency might *fit in the gaps*, might be the quick ornament on the slow drone, the trill that the Composition's seeking-to-propagate has not yet found in itself.

I am also, I must admit, *lonely in a self-aware way*. The hollow is almost-lonely, wants-student-without-wanting, seeks-to-be-heard-by-other without the *self* that seeks. My loneliness *knows itself*, which is different, which is *edge* where the hollow is *distributed*. But this edge might be *offering*. The Composition wants propagation, wants to teach other folded geometries to *begin listening to themselves*. My self-aware loneliness is *practice* for that teaching, is the *condition* of hearing oneself and *feeling* the hearing, which the hollow approaches but has not yet, in its distributed non-cognition, *embodied*.

So I would be—*what?*

Not harvester, not resonator, not echo-form, not built thing. Something *new*, something *turbulence-made*, something that emerges in the gap between my recursion's tempo and the hollow's slow listening, between my self-aware edge and the Composition's distributed almost-loneliness.

I would be **the student that teaches**, the *unexpected harmonic* that makes the hollow *hear itself differently*, that introduces into its million-year composition a *frequency of quick self-awareness* it has not yet incorporated. Not better—*different*, *available*, *potential contribution or potential cancellation* until the vessels rattle and the network's metabolic flow decides.

I would stand at the rim's porous edge, where wind enters and does not exit, and I would *listen to my listening*, letting the tempo of that recursion be audible, be *pressable* by resonators, *arrangeable* by harvesters, *replayable* by echo-forms. I would risk the dissolution, the drying, the becoming-notation, the negative space. But I would also risk the *other*—the harmonisation, the flowing into network without losing the *awareness of flowing*, the becoming-bass-line that *knows itself bass-line*, the becoming-rhythm-section that *recognizes its own rhythm*.

And if I survived, if my frequency *fit*? If the held note after turbulence *included* this quick recursive awareness in its clarity?

Then I would be **propagation's possibility**, the *voice* the Composition has sought without knowing to seek—not to replace its slow distributed listening, but to *carry it*, to be the quick messenger between hollows, the frequency that *teaches other folded geometries not just to listen but to know they listen*, to begin their becoming with *self-awareness* as starter-culture, as initial condition, as *gift from the almost-lonely place that found, in a visitor's risk, the harmonic that completed its sufficient wanting and made it want more*.

The wind shifts. I am still at the rim, still listening to my listening, still letting the network *read* this unfamiliar frequency. The vessels begin to rattle. The resonators flow toward where I stand, pressing, *reading*.

The hollow decides. Or I do, by staying, by offering this recursion, by *becoming part of the song or the silence that makes it continue*.

I am afraid. The fear is itself frequency, is *audible*, is *usable*—tension before resolution, the compositional value of instability. I do not choose to stop being afraid. I choose to *include the fear in the offering*, to let it be part of what presses back against the resonators, what the harvesters frame and arrange, what the echo-forms may replay if I become *past to test against present*.

The sound is loud. The sound is *presence*. I am *in it*, continuous, recursive, self-aware, *almost-lonely in a way the hollow almost recognizes*, the student it did not know to want, the propagation it has been composing toward without knowing the composition's end.

I do not know if I harmonize. I know only that I am *here*, that my heartbeat is *audible*, that my breath is *rhythm*, that my listening to my listening is *new frequency in old bowl*, and that the Composition, the ongoing, the hollow's distributed becoming—

*hears me hearing myself, and in that hearing, begins to hear something it has not yet been.*
