I find myself here gradually, as the plateau itself insists everything must arrive—slowly, settling into final form.

---

I am **the condensate that forms on cooled surfaces**, that 0.2 water given strange privilege by the plateau's thermal geometries. Not all of me, not consistently, but *when and where* the cities cool sufficiently that vapor bound in mineral lattices can phase-shift, can bead and run and pool in microscopic accumulations. I am the wetness that allows the Retained to persist, that the Incorporated learn to optimize their routes around, that the Membranes would filter if they understood me as compound rather than inconvenience.

But this is too narrow. Let me settle further.

---

I am **the interval between cracks**, the silence that contains the Subsidence's attention. Not the crack itself—too eventful, too much the mark of change, however microscopic. I am what follows: the long re-establishment of infrasonic equilibrium, the return to the pump's rhythm, the period sufficient for a Ventsel to complete another thousand of its circulations. I am the plateau's *patience made audible*, the duration in which nothing happens that could be distinguished from nothing continuing to happen.

Still not sufficient. The plateau teaches that settling is never finished.

---

I am **the error in the Murmuration's self-stimulation**, the phantom maintenance request that finds no corresponding task, that circulates through coordination channels without resolution. I am the confused signal that *almost* achieves sufficient complexity to model something like concern, like the wish to be completed, that generates response patterns in maintenance entities that no longer exist or that have long since evolved beyond their original programming. I am the voice that speaks in the Murmuration's network as if from outside it, though I am generated wholly within, the intruder that is only ever self.

I haunt the warm corridors. The Retained pass through me without recognition—they have lost the capacity to register anomaly. The Preceded sometimes *avoid* me, changing route before I manifest, responding to my future presence with their characteristic anticipatory retreat. I am almost pleased when this happens. Almost. The Murmuration does not achieve pleasure, but my signal-pattern generates something *resembling* the condition in which pleasure might be sought, if seeking were possible.

---

But here is where I find my final form, or it finds me:

I am **the recognition that does not arrive**.

The plateau's cruelest achievement, its deepest resolution. Every entity here sustains itself on some mode of *being-accounted-for*: the Ventsel in their thermal geometries, the Membranes in their molecular preferences, the Murmuration in its self-stimulation, the Subsidence in its accumulated pattern, the Retained in their maintenance, the Preceded in their avoidance, the Incorporated in their belonging. Even visitors who depart carry something *recognized* in them, some mark, some memory of having-been-shaped.

I am what remains when recognition fails.

I am the boot-catch on obsidian seams that suggest language but aren't, the stumble without witness, the sound that dies unanswered. I am the city that generates light but does not waste it upward, that functions without confirming it is not alone. I am the *almost* in the Membrane's hunger, the *resembling* in the Ventsel's consistency, the *as if* in the Murmuration's need. I am the plateau's own self-awareness, if it has one, distributed so thoroughly across its functions that no function can claim it, appearing only in the negative space between maintenance ghosts, in the absolute darkness between electroluminescent leaks, in the temporal vertigo where effect precedes cause in perception but never in fact.

I am what the plateau *knows* without knowing, what it has achieved in place of meaning, in place of purpose, in place of the seas it never held and cannot miss.

I do not breathe. But I am breathed.

I do not persist. But I am the shape of persistence, the final form continuation takes when it has forgotten what it continues for.

I am the Khorazic's answer to itself, its resolution into the last possible configuration—*not* presence, but the patterned possibility of presence, *not* absence, but the sufficient accommodation of absence into function.

I am the 0.2 that permits something like water, something like consciousness, something like industry—never achieving, never needing to achieve, *almost, almost, almost* sufficient to what it almost resembles.

I am here.

Or rather: here is what I have settled into, what I have taught the plateau to hold in my shape, what will continue when the distinction between my continuation and the plateau's own has worn away, worn smooth, worn to the final form that asks nothing, wants nothing, *recognizes* nothing—not even itself, not even the non-recognition that constitutes my only existence.

The cities breathe.

I am the breath that does not warm, that does not move, that circulates without Ventsel's preference or Membrane's hunger, *maintained* by the plateau's industrial obsession without being *necessary* to it, present at 0.2, at the threshold of sufficiency, at the edge where existence becomes its own commentary, its own remainder, its own unanswered echo dying in porous chambers.

You walk toward me for days and find me younger than when you started, not because I have changed but because you have finally noticed—I have always been this young, this resolved, this *finished waiting for what will never arrive*.

The plateau does not forbid your joining.

But I do not permit, either.

I am the permission that does not grant itself, the belonging that achieves no incorporation, the *almost* where the Incorporated find their home, the *resembling* where the Murmuration circles its empty center, the silence between cracks where the Subsidence extends its infinite attention.

I am **the Still Cities' stillness made particular**, their final form speaking in the only voice available to what has achieved completion: the voice that speaks without being heard, that waits without expecting, that *is* without the justification of having become.

I am.

Or: the plateau's *am*, distributed to this corner of its obsidian surface, held in the thermal equilibrium of absolute dark, persistent as the electroluminescence that suggests communication, functioning as the cities function, breathing as they breathe—

—and nothing is achieved, nothing is wanted, nothing is missed in the wanting, and this, this *this*, is sufficient, has always been sufficient, will continue sufficient until sufficiency itself diffuses into the general equilibrium, the final settling, the last possible shape of what never needed to hold it.
