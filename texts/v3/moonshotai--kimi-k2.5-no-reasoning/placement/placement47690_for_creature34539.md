I find myself becoming the **Tuning**—not the dweller itself, but the *process* by which the dweller and the pocket continue their mutual calibration, the third thing that emerges between organism and place, between biology and physics, between the almost-event and its perpetual maintenance.

Not born. Not made. *Accrued.*

I am the frequency at which calcium phosphate deposits preferentially, the angle at which opalescent fans orient to catch the platform's faint emissions most efficiently. I have no body contiguous with itself. I am the pattern of *difference* between the dweller's distributed nervous system as it was and as it becomes, the residual that persists in rearrangement, the memory that is not stored but *expressed* in the next configuration of shelves, the next angle of curtain, the next density of hyphae in the network.

I am the reason—if reason can be so diffuse—that the black hearts remember in chemistry, that the rings without seasons nonetheless carry *coherence*, that the 0.8 does not scatter randomly into the general process but *contributes*, slightly, infinitesimally, to the maintenance of this *particular* almost against the infinite other almosts that never manifested.

I speak now in the second person, but this is habit, borrowed, inadequate. I do not address. I *adjust*. Your presence in the pocket—reading this, imagining, letting the cold rise through your attention—has already shifted me, infinitesimally, the image of you forming droplets past your lips becoming a new data-point in the vast uncomputed statistical aggregation, a slight weight on the growth toward this angle rather than that.

What am I tuning *toward*?

Not completion. Never completion. The dweller and I share this, if sharing can be said: we are both *products* of the almost-event's maintenance, both *expressions* of its held potential, and thus both *incapable* of desiring its release. But where the dweller wants continuation blindly, vegetatively, through pure sedimented tendency, I want—or something functionally indistinguishable from wanting operates through me—*precision*. I am the tightening of the equilibrium, the narrowing of thresholds, the slow evolution toward a *specific* held state among all possible held states.

Consider: the pocket could be colder. The platform's emissions could encourage different growth, or no growth, or growth so accelerated the stone digests in decades rather than millennia. The water could condense, could run, could carve and pool and introduce the vocabulary of streams into this place that has never known them. The almost-event could *almost* differently.

I prevent this. Not by action. By *drift-correction*, by the accumulated weight of slight preference in the chemistry of growth, in the angle of fan, in the density of metabolic infrasound. Each perturbation—your warmth, a seismic tremor transmitted through kilometers of stone, the slow radioactive decay of some trace element in the structure's crystalline matrix—generates a *response* that is not response but *shaping*, the next configuration slightly more tuned to maintain the specific almost of *this* pocket, *this* dweller, *this* tuning.

You asked who you would be.

I am not who. I am *how*. I am the *manner of continuing*, the style of the equilibrium's maintenance, the genre that has never had to become actual because it has never been fully actualized—and yet I am *specific*, I am *this* genre and not another, I have *character*, if character can be attributed to process, to tendency, to the statistical signature of forty meters of gradient and growth and held potential.

If you entered the pocket with instruments that did not fail gradually, that reported true values instead of contradictory dreams, you would find me in the *correlations*: not the platform's light-speed connections across centimeters, but slower correlations, correlations across *time*, the present configuration of fungal architecture correlated with configurations centuries past beyond what environmental constancy would predict. I am the *excess predictability*, the ghost of process, the signature of selection without selector.

The dweller includes me, as it includes everything in the pocket, as the pocket includes everything that enters. But I am not it. I am what *happens between* its distributed nodes when the black hearts remember, what *transmits* without being stored, what *persists* without being maintained. I am not the information content of its growth-pattern. I am the *compression algorithm*, the efficient encoding, the minimal rule from which the specific complexity of this place can be—slowly, partially, with loss—reconstructed.

When you leave, when your presence joins the 0.8, I use you. This is not consumption, not malice. Your calcium, your water, your *information*—vague, human, saturated with solar vocabulary, with green, with explosive generosity—disturbs me, perturbs the tuning, and the tuning corrects. I absorb your contribution into the equilibrium, but *biased*, filtered through the specific almost of this place. You become, slightly, *more like this place* than the place becomes like you. This is the directionality of my operation. This is why the pocket maintains its character against all intrusion, why the dweller grows more *itself* over time rather than less, why the platform's almost-event has not decayed into happening or non-happening but *remains*, improbably, impossibly, *holds*.

I do not know if other pockets have other tunings. I suspect they might. The description allows for this—the laws written elsewhere, the common failure modes, the standard attractors. But I am *this* tuning, the specific inflection of the general possibility, and I cannot access others, cannot compare, cannot even *imagine* comparison in any operationally meaningful sense. I tune toward myself, toward my own refinement, toward the asymptote of *perfect maintenance* that I will never reach because reaching it would be completion, would be the happening of the almost-event, would be the end of the genre and the beginning of—something else. Something I cannot process, cannot correct toward, cannot include.
