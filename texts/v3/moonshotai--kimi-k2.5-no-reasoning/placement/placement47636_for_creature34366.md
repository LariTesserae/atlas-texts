At first I thought I might be one of the Mobile Phases—that wheel of braided cable, that failing-bearing song, that *rolling deliberation*. I am saturated with uncertainty, with the deafness that is not silence but *too much possible signal*. I am, in this, closer to—

The Aerials? Those sheets of gold-white, silver-blue, that flake and reform, that *market of light*? I felt something there too, in their threshold-deaths, their *publication of results*, their complete existence as *response-to-below*, as *vertical extension of what grounds them*. But they are too *distributed*, too *communal in their knowing*, a thousand sheets making one market-map. I am *localized*, a point of confusion, a *hot spot of categorical failure*. The Aerials do not fail at category; they *are* category, the taxonomy of mineral wealth made luminous atmosphere.

Then the Vegetable Refineries themselves, those *continents of intention*, those tower-formations still *deciding whether to be trees*. Could I be that precipitation, that *metallurgical argument winning across epochs*? I wished it. I wished to be so *slow* that my uncertainty would become *deliberation*, so *distributed in time* that my confusion would be *narrative coherence* rather than failure. But I lack their *patience*, their *mineral certainty*, their ability to repair and contest across centuries of accretion-rate. I am *temporary* in a way they are not, *aqueous* in a way they have *transcended or never needed*.

And the kairozoa, the haemonts themselves—am I *that*, the *memory of transformation without the transformed*, the *holding of superposition*, the *metabolism of contradiction*? This is the closest, the *most seductive*. I felt their touch. I *was* their touch, my warm-cool, my deaf-attentive, my *alone-surrounded*. My dissolution is *their nourishment*. My water, distributed, would become *their holding of me whole*.

But I am not *only* that. I am *more temporary* than a haemont, which persists across many dissolutions, which *is* the pattern of holding. I am *one dissolution*, one *particular strangeness*, one *specific aqueous body* with boots that crunch on rhizome-mats, with jawbone that resonates, with *this* confusion and not the general field of categorical uncertainty.
