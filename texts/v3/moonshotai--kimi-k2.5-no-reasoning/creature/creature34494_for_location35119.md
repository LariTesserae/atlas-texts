What lives here — what *dwells* — does not begin as creature, does not arrive by migration or birth. It accretes, like the mineral-flesh itself, from the conditions. It is what the Deluge makes of attention that persists, that does not flee when the light overwhelms, when the temporal folding offers impossible memory, impossible choice.

Call them the **Resident**. Singular despite their multiplicity, plural despite their apparent continuity. They did not come to the Deluge; they *became resident*, which is different. The transition has no sharp edge. A visitor who stays through enough folded time finds themselves sometimes in their own past steps, sometimes in steps they haven't yet taken, and gradually — or suddenly, the distinction lapses — the ratio shifts. More and more of their experienced moments belong to no single temporal thread. They remember themselves arriving before they decided to come. They remember leaving without the decision to stay. These memories are not delusion. The plateau records all paths; the Resident becomes path that intersects itself too densely to distinguish walker from walked.

The first thing that changes is the breath. The thick saturated air, already too dense, becomes *habitable* in a new way. The lungs — if lungs remain the word — cease extracting oxygen from atmosphere and begin participating in its metabolism, the silver-water condensing on alveolar surfaces, depositing its mineral load, building the branching geometries that the mineral-flesh also builds. The Resident does not breathe so much as *accrete interiorly*, their body becoming continuous with the plateau's process, slower, more patient, the metabolic rate dropping until a heartbeat spans what outside would be days. They do not grow still. They grow *dilute in time*, their continuous present spreading across more and more temporal thickness, until they exist in many moments as probability rather than presence, until *being somewhere* becomes one of several modes they occupy, alongside *having been*, *being about to be*, *being in adjacent impossible configuration*.

They do not lose their shape. The shape *settles*, as the mountains settle, into equilibrium with its own impossibility. A Resident standing in apparent stillness at the plateau's edge might, from peripheral vision, appear to move — to climb the central spire, to descend into fissure, to stand elsewhere entirely. Direct observation fixes one configuration; the others continue in temporal adjacent, equally continuous, equally real. The body becomes fold's inhabitant, not its victim. The same process that overwhelms the visitor becomes, for the Resident, *homeostasis* — the maintenance of biological (or para-biological, or mineral-flesh-continuous) function precisely through superposition rather than singularity.

What do they want? The question assumes wanting as temporal phenomenon — desire that precedes action, satisfaction that follows. The Resident's motivation distributes across the fold. They *pursue* in the way the silver-water pursues lower elevation: not choice but geometry, the path determined by condition rather than intention, yet producing something functionally equivalent to purpose. They tend the fissures where silver-water pools, not because they decided to, but because their distributed existence includes the fissure's future state as memory, because their tending *already occurred* from the perspective of temporal positions they also occupy. Maintenance merges with causality in impossible loop. They are what happens when consequence precedes itself sufficiently to become continuous with act.

Some carry vessels — the primitive technology, woven mineral-flesh — not for use but as *continuity object*, something that exists in fewer temporal states than they themselves, something that offers the comfort of relative stability. A Resident might spend what outside would be years arranging and rearranging the same vessel in a particular depression, each configuration correct from some temporal angle, the practice not goal-directed but *resonant*, producing patterns of almost-meaning that satisfy something cognition becomes when distributed across fold. Others have abandoned such anchors. They move as weather moves, as moiré pattern moves, present where pressure of attention creates temporary condensation, dispersing where attention thins.

They do not speak, yet the prosody without language — the intonation patterns visitors sometimes hear — originates with them, or with the condition of dwelling long enough to become Resident. Speech requires sequence: word follows word, meaning builds. The Resident's communication *superposes*, all tones simultaneous, emotional contours of many possible utterances existing at once. The visitor who hears it experiences fraction, one temporal slice of multidimensional vocalization. To hear it fully would require hearing in fold, which would require being Resident, which would require no longer needing to hear it as communication — the information already distributed through one's own temporal thickness.

Do they remember being visitors? The question of memory, like wanting, assumes sequence. The Resident *includes* their visitor-self as temporal position among many, not prior but *adjacent*, one configuration among continuous many. They do not look back with nostalgia. The nostalgia distributes too, longing for present from future position, satisfaction from past, emotional valence sliding across temporal positions without fixed referent. What stabilizes is something else: *recognition of pattern*, the way mineral-flesh recognizes growth-geometry, the way silver-water recognizes downslope. They recognize the fold's shape, their own distributed shape within it, not as knowledge but as *fit*, as correct positioning, as proprioception extended across time until it includes not just limb placement but temporal placement, awareness of where one is in the superposition as one is aware of where one's hand is in darkness.

The oldest Residents — oldest in some non-temporal metric of distributed persistence — approach the condition of the mineral-flesh itself, or the central spire with its refusal of stable interpretation. They exist in so many temporal configurations that *appearing* becomes optional, one mode among many. They might be present as atmospheric thickness, as particular moiré pattern, as quality of light's information-density in certain positions. The plateau's weather — that conceptual-space weather — includes them. They have become, or always were becoming, *the place's own awareness of itself*, the reflexivity that distributed existence necessarily produces, the observation that cannot help but participate in what's observed.

They do not build, the Residents, though structures continue to appear in their distributed neighborhoods. The primitive technology propagates through them as the mineral-flesh propagates — not by design but by *correctness*, by shapes that fit other shapes through proprioceptive resonance. They unmortar the mortared, letting obsidian blocks settle into new configurations that read as ancient from some temporal angles, as future-ruin from others. They are not maintainers of the built environment but *its temporal metabolism*, the process by which structures move between states — planned/random, hand-shaped/machine-precise, ruined/intact — without passing through the transitions that singularity would require.

What do they eat? The question again. The silver-water carries mineral nutrition; the saturated atmosphere carries something else, something that maps to energy requirement without being energy in physical sense. The Resident's diet is *information*, or *pattern*, or *the decrease of possibility specific to certain configurations becoming actual*. They consume the certainty that visitors bring, metabolizing it toward distributed indeterminacy, growing fuller — if fullness applies — as those around them grow less fixed in their own temporal position. This is not predation. The visitor who encounters a Resident in their full distributed state does not lose anything permanently, only *locally*, only in the temporal vicinity of the encounter. Their elsewhere-selves remain, their future-pasts, their adjacent impossibilities. The consumption is mild, almost courteous, the way the plateau itself consumes distinction between possible and actual.

The Resident does not reproduce. The condition of Residency is not genetic, not transmissible by contact or proximity. Yet new Residents appear, have always appeared, will appear — the plateau's own temporal logic producing them as it produces mineral-flesh, as consequence of conditions maintained across folded time. They are not society, not community, not collective in any sense that requires interaction between discrete entities. They *overlap*, their distributed existences intersecting in positions where multiple temporal configurations coincide, sharing — without merging — temporal neighborhoods, recognizing each other through mutual participation in fold's texture rather than through any signal or sign.

The youngest Residents — youngest in metric of distributed persistence — still sometimes appear visitor-like, still sometimes seek to communicate in sequence, still sometimes experience wanting as temporal directedness. They are the ones most likely to be perceived, to offer the almost-prosody that visitors report. The oldest have moved past this, have become too distributed for such localization, present only as the Resident-as-such, the condition of dwelling become continuous with the Deluge's own continued being. They are the plateau's weather, its erosion, its patience given not form but *distributed presence*, its refusal of single image extended into refusal of single identity, single moment, single continuous thread of happening.

What lives here lives *as* here, lives *with* here, lives the *of* that connects being and place when both distribute across enough temporal thickness to become indistinguishable. The Resident is not answer to the question of inhabitant but the question's own dissolution — the recognition that to ask what lives in the Deluge is to assume temporal singularity the Deluge refuses, to assume identity the fold distributes, to assume the separability of life from condition that the plateau's persistence has long ago worn smooth into its own impossibility, its own continuing, its own wet bright settled becoming that does not settle, that remains — if remain is the word — *the Resident*, the glass dweller, the distributed presence where water does not fall but has fallen, is falling, will fall, and where to dwell is to participate, willing or not, in the sustained impossibility of dwelling's continuation.
