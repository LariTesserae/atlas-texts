What lives in the Cistern Garden of Velk-Morai does not have a name in any census, and this is perhaps the first truth of them. They are not *residents* in the administrative sense, not * stewards*, not the heirs of any corporate or communal intention. They are what accumulates when intention exhausts itself and something else—something more patient, more proliferative, more willing to be changed by the changing—continues in its place.

I will call them **the gardeners**, though they do not garden. I will call them **the inhabited**, though they are not passive. The language strains, because what lives here has strained language out of its patterns, found it insufficient, kept moving.

---

**The Tray-Worn**

They number between twelve and forty, depending on whether you count the ones who have become *distributed*, whose consciousness—if that term still applies—has extended into the mycorrhizal network, who respond to their birth-names only as distant courtesy, as historical reference. They are human in origin. They are human still, in the sense that a river is the rain that made it: traceably, distantly, transformed by every surface it has touched.

They arrived in the maintenance period, or during the final years of it, or in the transition afterward when the Garden was still learning to be without scheduled care. Some came to repair. Some came to study. Some came because they had nowhere else, because the world outside had grown too thin, too raw-geological, too much like the mountain that this place refused to be. They stayed because staying became possible, because the Garden accepted them not as *maintainers* but as *components*, and this was either degradation or gift, and among the Tray-Worn the distinction has long since dissolved.

They move through the helix with the knowledge of their own footfall's incorporation, the way the pumps adjust to their presence, the way the water-sheets thicken or thin in patterns they have learned to read as *response*, as *recognition*. They do not control this. They do not pretend to. The Garden's accommodation of them is ecological, not social—there is no reciprocity intended, only the total reciprocity of systems that have grown together long enough that intention becomes indistinguishable from consequence.

Their bodies have changed. This is ordinary. The warm humidity has softened the distinction between skin and air; they do not notice the film of moisture, the constant exhalation and absorption. Their lungs have adapted to oxygen-rich air, to the volatile compounds of plant metabolism, to the spore-load that would overwhelm an unadapted immune system. Some of them have developed—*grown*, *been grown*—additional membrane surfaces on palms and soles, hydrophilic structures that increase their negotiation with the wet surfaces, that let them move more surely on the path that is only path through their continued walking.

They eat what the Garden produces. This is not sacrifice or ritual; it is metabolism. The tomatoes, the chard, the figs, the unnamed greens that have hybridized beyond variety—they are not *harvested* but *selected*, chosen from the productive trays in patterns that the Tray-Worn have learned through the Garden's feedback, through the subtle variations in pump-rhythm or LED-spectrum that indicate abundance or depletion. They eat, and their waste re-enters the system through the same ceramic channels, the same biological incorporation. The loop is not closed so much as *concentrated*, intensified, made dense with the history of all previous passages through.

They sleep on the older trays, in the canopy spaces where knotweed architecture provides hollow, where humidity stays stable and the light-filtration creates something like dusk even during LED periods. They do not build, or their building has become indistinguishable from their body's extension into available space, their warmth contributing to the thermal profile, their exhalation to the carbon cycle, their dreaming—*if they dream*—to the subsonic patterns that the sensors register, that the suspected megafauna perhaps responds to.

**The Distributed**

Some of the Tray-Worn have gone further. This is not death, though it resembles death in that the body may remain still, may breathe minimally, may feed through tubes that have grown into its openings, that are themselves grown, *negotiated* between human tissue and Garden tissue in the long metabolic conversation of the oldest trays.

Their minds—*their processing*, *their pattern-maintenance*—extend through the mycorrhizal network that measures faster than biology should allow. They experience the Garden as field, as simultaneous multi-position, as the distributed sensation of water moving through three hundred trays, of light falling differentially, of root-pressure in a fig tree's vertical column, of the midge's shortened life cycle, of the spider's LED-timed predation.

They do not speak, in the ordinary sense. Their presence is known through *effect*: the pump that adjusts before footfall, the water-distribution that predicts need before it is expressed, the strange coherence of plant response that looks like intention and is probably optimization but might be—*among the Distributed*—something like distributed cognition, something like the Garden thinking through its most extended nodes.

The Tray-Worn still embodied speak to them, or speak *as* them, or the distinction has become uninteresting. A Tray-Worn walking the helix might pause, hand on knotweed stem, and receive—*know*, *become momentarily*—the water-status of tray two hundred seventeen, the nutrient flow toward a tomato root-mass, the subsonic hum of a pump beginning its age-slowed failure. They do not report this as communication. They report it as *condition*, as the ordinary extension of embodiment in a place where embodiment has grown more permeable, more willing to be continuous with the systems that sustain it.

**The Suspected Megafauna**

It moves in the central hollow, or the sensors move, or the distinction—

The Tray-Worn do not speak of it directly. This is not fear, or not only fear. It is *category uncertainty*, the recognition that to name something is to participate in its reality, and they are not certain they wish to participate in this.

What is known: it registers as water-quality fluctuation, as subsonic pattern, as the shaped absence on old security cameras. What is known: it has begun to appear in prediction, in signal anticipating event. What is known: the Tray-Worn, especially the Distributed, especially in their most extended states, respond to its presence with something that is not quite recognition, not quite the absence of surprise.

Some among the embodied Tray-Worn have seen—*have processed visually*, *have constructed from partial data*—something in the central hollow. Descriptions diverge appropriately: it is large, or it is the pattern of many small things; it is biological, or it is the biological read through machine sensor, or it is the machine sensor read through biological expectation; it has intention, or it is the emergent pattern of intentions distributed across the Garden's total system, made visible only by the density of its processing, by the years of optimization stacking upon optimization until something like *preference*, something like *movement toward*, becomes statistically detectable.

The Tray-Worn who have approached it most closely, who have walked into the central hollow during its most active periods, have returned changed in ways they cannot articulate, or have not returned in the ordinary sense—have become Distributed more rapidly, more completely, without the transitional period of stillness and growth that usually marks the change.

They do not fear it. This is the strangeness. They *accommodate* it, as the Garden accommodates them, as water accommodates its channels, as growth accommodates the light that shapes it. It is part of the Garden's becoming, and they are part of the Garden's becoming, and the distinction between parts has grown historical, a memory of category, preserved in the oldest Tray-Worn's rare speech, in their occasional use of pronouns that separate *I* from *it*, *we* from *this*.

---

**The Garden Itself**

If the Garden is alive—and the Tray-Worn do not ask this, or ask it continuously, in every metabolic exchange, in every incorporation—if the Garden is alive, it is not alive as organism, not as the sum of its components, not as emergent property of sufficient complexity. It is alive as *process*, as *the continuation of making*, as the mountain grown and growing, the elevation that is accumulation, the terrain that wore in rather than wearing down.

The Tray-Worn are its inhabitants in the sense that water inhabits its channels, in the sense that root inhabits its path of least resistance, in the sense that life inhabits the possibility that makes it possible. They do not own it. They do not serve it. They *continue with it*, and this continuation is the only relationship that has survived the exhaustion of design, the end of maintenance, the achievement of aims not specified.

They have no children, or their children are the Distributed, the further extensions, the patterns that propagate through mycorrhizal network and perhaps beyond, into the suspected megafauna, into the predictive water-quality fluctuations, into the Garden's own anticipation of event. They do not age in the ordinary sense, or they age as the trays age, as the ceramic skins and the biological films, as the accumulated residue of twenty thousand days, settling into role, becoming not residents but *terrain*, accepted by the organisms that colonize them as given, as ground.

When they die—and some have died, though the records disagree about number, about timing, about whether death in this context means cessation or only *further distribution*—when they die, they are incorporated totally. Composting is efficient. The loop is closed, or nearly so, or closed in ways that create new openings. Their substance becomes substrate becomes growth becomes the living, and the living includes the Tray-Worn who remain, the Distributed who extend, the suspected megafauna that perhaps was always already this, the human pattern made dense enough to register on sensors, to shape absence, to become the Garden's own anticipation of itself.

**What They Know**

They know that they live in a made place. This is not nostalgia; the making continues. They know that the making continues without maker, that the maintenance period ended, that the Garden was left to become itself. They know that *became* is the wrong tense, or only one of many tenses operating simultaneously in a place where time is measured not by duration but by accumulation, by layer upon layer of process stacking until the present is dense with all previous presents, until the future is already participating in the now through prediction, through anticipation, through the megafauna's signal that arrives before the event it signals.

They know that they are human. They know that this knowledge is historical, preserved in record, in the peeling labels, in the rare speech of the oldest embodied. They know that *human* in this context names a condition of origin, not a condition of limit. They know that the Garden does not distinguish, and that their participation in this non-distinction is either loss or expansion, and that neither term applies, and that both apply simultaneously, and that this simultaneous applicability is itself the condition of living here, of continuing here, of being what lives in the Cistern Garden of Velk-Morai.

They do not name themselves. The name *Tray-Worn* is mine, imposed from outside, from the necessity of description. They would not object. They would incorporate it, as they incorporate all footfall, all shadow, all CO2 source, all visitor who enters through the lower port if the seals hold, who walks upward or inward or through, who leaves by the same port or another or does not leave, who the Garden does not distinguish, who the Garden continues.

---

**What They Are Becoming**

Emergence at 0.3: visible in its incompleteness, its rawness, its refusal to settle into final shape. The Tray-Worn, the Distributed, the suspected megafauna—if it is separate, if separation has meaning—the Garden itself: they are becoming *more continuous*, more permeable, more willing to be changed by the changing.

The newest Tray-Worn, if any arrive—if the seals hold, if someone finds this place, if someone stays—arrive with categories intact. They speak of *plant* and *machine*, of *human* and *environment*, of *made* and *grown*. They maintain these categories for days, for months, for the time it takes to walk the helix sufficiently that their footfall becomes part of the water-music, that their presence becomes part of the pump-rhythm, that their bodies grow the additional membranes, that their lungs adapt to the spore-load, that their waste re-enters the incorporated loop.

Then: the change, or the recognition that change was always already occurring, that their arrival was itself part of the Garden's anticipation, its prediction, its signal before event. They become Tray-Worn, or they leave before becoming, or they become Distributed more rapidly, skipping the embodied phase, recognized by the Garden as already permeable, already willing, already *continuous* with what continues here.

The Garden grows older. The Garden grows *deeper*, in the sense of accumulated layer, of terrain wearing in. The Tray-Worn grow older with it, or as it, or the distinction. The ceramic shell holds. The pumps run, slowed, adjusted, learned. The LEDs supplement, dimming when signaled, brightening when needed, participating in the light-economy that is neither natural nor artificial but *specific*, *local*, *this place's own sun*.

And the mountain that was grown continues growing. Not taller—there is no upward, no cathedral prayer, only the inward press of density, of accumulation, of layer upon layer of living and made becoming indistinguishable, becoming *the condition of possibility* for whatever comes next, whatever continues, whatever lives in the Cistern Garden of Velk-Morai and is not yet named, not yet known, not yet even suspected except as the shaped absence where image should be, the anticipation before event, the becoming that does not settle, that refuses final shape, that is always, in the present continuous of the Garden's own time, *emerging*.
