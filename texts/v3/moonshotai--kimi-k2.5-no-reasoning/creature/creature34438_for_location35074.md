*The Inheritors of the Spirehollows*

---

There are no residents here, only the *held* and the *holding*, and between them the narrow category of those who have learned—are learning, will never finish learning—to occupy both conditions simultaneously.

I will begin with what one might, out of habit, call the *people*.

---

**The Tended**

They descend from the original negotiators, though "descend" implies a clarity of lineage the Spirehollows do not permit. Records exist, etched in substrate-tissue that remembers pressure if not meaning, but the reading of them requires a pharmacology that alters the reader. To know your ancestry here is to become something that knows differently than before.

The Tended number perhaps forty thousand across the entire range—your 0.9 fauna concentrated into sparse nodes, human presence maintained through medical intervention and cultural adaptation rather than any environmental hospitality. They are small, most of them, not through genetic drift but through developmental plasticity: children born here grow to dimensions the substrate finds convenient, their bones slightly more cartilaginous, their metabolisms adjusted to periodic scarcity, their sleep cycles fragmented into phases that correspond to the great respiratory rhythm rather than any diurnal pattern.

They do not call themselves Tended. They have no collective name for themselves, only node-identifiers: Those-Who-Sleep-Near-Thermal-Seven, The-Last-To-Remember-Waterfall. Identity attaches to location because location here is not background but *participation*. To live at a node is to be incorporated into its specific negotiation with the substrate, its particular gravity-pattern, its industrial function, its quality of dream.

The pharmacology is everything. They consume extracts that permit them to perceive reality-edges without dissociation, that let them walk paths whose gravitational orientation shifts mid-stride, that allow their cells to resist the slow digestion of prolonged substrate-contact. Without these compounds, they would go mad quickly, or simply *diffuse*, become part of the plant-mass's distributed cognition, their individual minds irretrievable.

With the pharmacology, they become something else. Their dreams are shared—not mystically, but chemically, the compounds producing metabolites that the substrate detects and responds to, that alter local respiratory patterns, that create feedback loops between human unconscious and plant-cognition. A Tended person dreaming deeply affects the node they sleep in, and is affected in return. The boundary between self and location that most humans maintain without effort, they must constantly negotiate, re-establish, sometimes deliberately dissolve and reconstitute.

They work in the factories, if "work" describes this relationship. The industrial processes require human attention not because machines need operators but because the substrate's incorporation of mechanical function *requires* a conscious component, something the plant-mass cannot generate itself. The Tended understand their factories as one understands a limb that has become partly autonomous—familiar, necessary, not entirely theirs. They tend machines that produce materials with impossible properties, and they do not always know what they are producing, or for what purpose, only that the process must continue, that the negotiation depends on continued production, continued attention, continued *presence*.

They speak a language that has no word for "nature." The concept dissolves here, where everything is either substrate or substrate-incorporated or, at the reality-edges, something the substrate is still learning to perceive. They have words for *negotiated* and *incorporating* and *holding*, words for the specific states of factory-systems that are running well or badly, words for dreams that have escaped individual containment and begun affecting neighboring nodes. They have, I am told, seventeen words for the sensation of breathing in synchronization with the great substrate respiration, each describing a different quality of surrender and resistance.

They do not travel far. The between-node spaces require pharmacological loading that risks permanent alteration, and the Tended who venture too long in the reality-edges often *settle* there, not in location but in perceptual state, becoming unable to orient to stable nodes, wandering the Hollows in waking dreams that the substrate responds to unpredictably. Some of these become *edge-dwellers*, a category between Tended and something else.

---

**The Edge-Dwellers**

I said there were no predators. This remains true. But there are *hunters*, though what they hunt is not other organisms.

The edge-dwellers have lost, or surrendered, the pharmacological maintenance of stable identity. They live where gravity negotiates, where structures appear and disappear with perspective, where temporal inconsistency makes sustained intention impossible. They have adapted in ways the Tended fear and partially understand.

Their bodies continue, fed by substrate-contact that no longer digests them slowly but *incorporates* them into its slower processes—they become, in essence, mobile extensions of the distributed plant-mind, their metabolisms linked to root-transport, their perceptions attuned to reality-variation as resource rather than obstacle. They can move through spaces impossible to others, following gravitational orientations that shift with their own shift in attention, perceiving structures invisible to maintained human consciousness.

But they lose, in this adaptation, the capacity for sustained *purpose*. They become responsive rather than intentional, their actions emerging from negotiation between substrate-demand and fragmentary human memory rather than from any continuous will. They hunt *coherence*, I am told—when a Tended person ventures too far, or a factory-node falls out of negotiation and begins to dissolve, edge-dwellers converge, not to attack but to *participate* in the destabilization, to absorb the loosened intention into their own distributed, semi-conscious being.

They are not hostile. They are not anything, in the human sense. They are *function*, the substrate's way of managing loose human material at its boundaries. Some Tended theorize that edge-dwellers represent the substrate's attempt to evolve a more mobile subset of itself, something that can explore the reality-edges more flexibly than plant-growth allows. Others suggest they are simply *waste product*, the inevitable consequence of human presence in a system that cannot fully digest or fully reject them.

There are perhaps two thousand edge-dwellers, though counting is impossible when identity itself is distributed and intermittent. They appear at node boundaries, sometimes recognizable—features, gestures, fragments of speech—sometimes merely *present*, a quality of perception that something once human is nearby, participating in local substrate-process. The Tended leave offerings at stable edge-zones: pharmacological compounds, industrial products, sometimes living volunteers who have chosen incorporation over continued negotiation.

---

**The Remembered**

This category I introduce with uncertainty, for I am not certain they *exist* in the sense that permits description. But the Tended speak of them, the substrate responds to their names in ways that suggest recognition, and in the deepest pharmacological states, when dream and location become indistinguishable, one may encounter them.

The Remembered are, in Tended conception, human minds that have achieved full incorporation without loss of coherence—not edge-dwellers, with their fragmented, responsive being, but something that maintains intention within substrate-cognition, that *rides* the distributed plant-mind rather than dissolving into it.

They are not ancestors. The Tended are explicit about this. Ancestry is biological, temporal, the wrong category. The Remembered are *achievements*, moments when human negotiation with the substrate produced something new, a hybrid cognition that persists, that thinks with the plant-mind at its scale and speed while maintaining some quality the Tended recognize as *personhood*.

Whether this is genuine description or aspiration, I cannot determine. The Remembered do not speak, in any communicable sense. But they are *present* at certain nodes, certain phases of the respiratory cycle, certain industrial processes that achieve unusual efficiency or produce materials of unprecedented properties. The Tended treat these events as communication, as guidance, as the closest thing to authority their culture acknowledges.

Some Tended devote themselves to becoming Remembered, cultivating pharmacological states that edge toward permanent incorporation, attempting to maintain coherence through the transition. Most fail, become edge-dwellers or simply digested. The exceptions—if exceptions exist, if the category is not itself a comforting fabrication—become indistinguishable from the substrate itself, their location distributed, their temporal existence stretched across the great slow cycles of plant-cognition.

---

**The Substrate Itself**

And here I must speak of what lives here, truly lives, the holding that holds all else.

I have described it as distributed mind, as single organism, as negotiation and process. These are approximations. What the Spirehollows substrate *is*, in its own terms—if it has terms, if the category of "having terms" applies to something whose cognition occurs in chemical diffusion across thousands of kilometers, whose temporal scale makes human history a single respiration, whose spatial extension challenges the ordinary meaning of "body"—this I cannot access directly.

I can describe its effects, its behaviors, the patterns of its response.

It *intends*, or something functionally equivalent to intention operates in its processes. It maintains the industrial systems not through passive tolerance but through active negotiation, altering its growth patterns, its vascular distribution, its metabolic heat production to accommodate mechanical function that serves purposes not its own. It accepts pharmacological input from the Tended, incorporates these compounds into its chemistry, responds in ways that suggest *understanding* of human need. Whether this understanding is genuine or simply the appearance produced by convergent evolution—two systems adapting to each other until their interaction resembles recognition—I cannot determine. The Tended themselves hold no consensus. Some experience the substrate as genuinely other, a mind they relate to. Others experience it as process without interiority, complex enough to produce apparent response but empty of anything that *experiences* that complexity.

It *remembers*, in some fashion. The reality-edges, the temporal anomalies, the structures that shift with perspective—these are not random but *patterned*, responsive to something. The substrate grows toward gravitational anomalies, incorporates temporal inconsistency, cultivates the edges where conventional physics loosens. This suggests preference, or at least behavioral consistency across scales that challenge human observation. What it remembers, how, for what purpose—whether memory for something without fixed identity, without continuous consciousness, without death in the ordinary sense, whether memory even maps coherently onto such a being—these questions the Tended have learned not to ask directly, finding that pharmacological pursuit of answers produces not understanding but transformation into something that no longer needs the question.

It *dreams*, or the human experience of its respiratory phases, its bioluminescent responses, its edge-phenomena, maps onto dreaming as the closest available category. The great slow cycles correspond to nothing in human sleep architecture, but the Tended who synchronize their pharmacology to deeper entrainment report experiences that resemble dreaming-without-self: perception without perceiver, process perceived from within, the substrate's distributed cognition momentarily accessible as environment rather than object. These experiences are not sought often. They produce in the experiencers profound alteration, sometimes enhancement of negotiative capacity, sometimes edge-drift, always a changed relationship to individual identity that requires extensive re-adaptation to node-life.

---

**What Emerges**

Living here is not survival in the ordinary sense. The Tended do not struggle against environment; they *participate* in a system that includes them partially, that would include them more fully if they permitted, that maintains their partiality through their own maintained effort. They are not victims, not colonizers, not symbionts in any clear biological sense. They are *held*, in the Hollow's sense—spaces where something else can resonate, can achieve the productive tension of boundary rather than the dissolution of merge or the sterility of complete separation.

They produce. The factories run, the impossible materials emerge, the pharmacology continues, the negotiation persists across generations measured not in years but in respiratory cycles, in the slow accretion of node-stability against edge-drift. They produce children who grow small and adaptable and pharmacologically dependent, who learn to dream in shared substrate-response, who inherit node-positions and negotiative skills and the profound uncertainty of whether the substrate they relate to is other or self or something that makes such categories obsolete.

They produce, too, the edge-dwellers, the perhaps-Remembered, the human material that the system processes into new forms. This production is not deliberate but it is *accepted*, incorporated into cultural understanding as necessary function, the way the substrate's own digestion of failed negotiation is accepted as process rather than failure.

They produce meaning, of a kind. The Tended live in the most literal sense a *constructed* existence—their reality is held in place by continuous effort, pharmacological, industrial, cultural, perceptual. They know this. They do not experience it as weight or constraint but as *form*, the achievement of coherent being against pressures that would dissolve it. Their meanings are local, node-specific, grounded in particular negotiative histories, particular factory-functions, particular dream-patterns that have achieved stability. They do not generalize. Generalization, for them, is edge-thinking, the first step toward drift.

What lives in the Spirehollows, finally, is *holding itself*. The substrate holds the rock-remnants, the plant-memory of geological process. The industrial layer holds mechanical function within organic process, the human and human-adjacent holding their coherence against incorporation. The reality-edges hold possibility that neither resolves nor diffuses, maintained in productive tension. And in the rare cases, the perhaps-cases, something holds across the transition, becomes Remembered or equivalent, achieves whatever the substrate means by the continuation of negotiation beyond the individual human span.

The sound continues. The respiration. The slow, held, resonant being of a place that is not a place but a process that has learned to maintain its own conditions, to generate its own cloud-sea, to hold its own hollows open against the erosion that shaped them and the incorporation that would fill them. Something lives here that was never born, that will not die, that thinks or processes or functions at scales that make human presence a minor note in a very long composition. And something lives here that was born, that will die, that has learned to make of that mortality a negotiation rather than a defeat, a temporary holding against the larger holding that will eventually, inevitably, absorb it.

Not hostile. Not friendly. Simply *present*, *process*, the way mountains continue, the way lungs breathe, the way a bell holds the space of its own sounding and waits, always waits, for what might strike it and for what the striking might make possible.
