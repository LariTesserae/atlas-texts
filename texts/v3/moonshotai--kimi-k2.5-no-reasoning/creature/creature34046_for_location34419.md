I'll let this place settle into my understanding before I speak of who dwells there. The mountain is young, still deciding what to become. The city clings to this indecision, builds *into* it, negotiates with emergence itself. What kind of being could inhabit such a negotiation?

---

They are born, initially, in the *stable zones*—the districts where the mountain has temporarily committed to a form, where responsive masonry has found its equilibrium, where parents dare to conceive with some faith that the womb will not shift mid-gestation. But even these births are *premonition-laden*. The pregnant wear garments designed for thermal transitions, for sudden gradients, for the walk from steam corridor to wind-scoured plaza that might suddenly become necessary. Contractions are timed not by clock but by *geological probability*—the Water Wardens consulted, the thermal gradients read, the mountain's slow exhalation monitored for *auspicious stillness*.

And then: emergence. The infant's first cry enters the drone, joins it, is *absorbed* into the city's continuous vibration. They are swaddled in *layered* fabric, the cosmology already impressed upon them—the self as onion, as provisional, capable of shedding or accumulating. Their first rooms may have windows that open onto brick, or basalt intrusions that serve as wardrobe, or walls that *shift fractionally* with each season, the cradle *settling* into new angles that the infant's vestibular system learns as *normal*, as *ground*, before it knows to call anything else *straight*.

The *Threnodic* development. Children here do not learn *objects* so much as *negotiations*. The toy left on a windowsill may be *integrated* by morning into a new condensation pattern, its plastic filmed with mineral, its function *revised* by slow moisture. They learn not to grieve this but to *read* it—the toy's becoming-other as lesson in the mountain's continuing instruction. They learn to walk *sequence*, not space: *what comes after what*, the route that passes from Heat Orchard to thermal corridor to wind plaza, each transition requiring *garment adjustment*, *breath preparation*, *social recalibration*.

They are *early* inducted into the *pocket hydrology*. Small cisterns are given them, child-scale, ranked by *likelihood*, the first divination tools. They learn to *listen* for condensation, to *find* the wet walls, to recognize the particular *smell* of stone that is *choosing* to release moisture, to *become* passage for water. They learn the *discipline* of the Vein Rivers: you do not walk the dry bed, you do not assume, you do not *name* your relationship. This is *hard* for children, the natural possessiveness—*my* stream, *our* swimming hole—*curbed* by instruction that borders on the theological. To name is to *commit* the river against its nature, to *anger* what is still deciding whether to be river at all.

They are *taught* the *faunal co-habitation* with *practical reverence*. The Glissando Slugs are not pets but *indicators*—their phosphorescence read for mineral content, their trails mapped for moisture probability. The Thermal Bats' echolocation is *felt* at the threshold of hearing, the child learning to *sense* being-located-by-other-sensing, the city's *distributed perception*. They learn to *read* the Building-Swallowers' vegetative patience, to *recognize* the particular *resonance* of a structure that will soon need Transition Engineer attention, to *hear* what the buildings *sing* of their condition.

And they are *warned*, gradually, of the *weirdness* that leaks from the mountain's not-yet-decided condition. The Temporal Slippages are *mapped* for convenience, yes, but the children are taught to *walk boundaries*, to recognize the *zones of increasing differential*, to know when to *turn back*, when the slippage's *domestication* is wearing thin. They hear stories of *incautious* entry—grandmother emerging younger than her granddaughter, the child who *returned* still carrying *what had not yet happened to them*, the *double-walkers* who encountered their own *other-timed selves* and *could not afterward* maintain *single narrative*.

The *adolescent crisis* in Threnody is *specific*: the *Stillness Impulse*, the desire for *permanence*, for *foundation that does not negotiate*, for *building as other cities build*. It manifests in *rebellion* against the layered garments, the provisional self, the *endless adjustment*. Some *flee*—temporarily, permanently—to imagined *stable cities*, returning often with *strange grief*, *unlocated longing*, *inability to sleep without the drone*. Others *act out* the impulse locally, *demanding* geological stabilization, *attempting* construction of *fixed form*, *foundations that will not drift*—and suffer the consequences, *structural failure*, *geological rejection*, the *mountain teaching* what it *teaches* to all *such forgetting*.

The *initiation* into *adult* *participation* involves *choice* of *placement*, *deliberate* *wager* on geological *patience*. The young adult *walks* the city's *sparse extent*, *feels* the *thermal gradients*, the *probable water*, the *potential stability*, and *chooses*—or is *chosen* by—*location*. This is *not* mere *real estate*. To *dwell* in Threnody is to *commit* to *particular negotiation*, to *become* *known* by *placement*, to *assume* the *identity* that *location* *confers*: *I am of the Long Stair*, *I descend the Fluent's variance*, *I cross at the Thermal Bridge when the season permits*.

The *adult practice* is *Maintenance of Incompleteness*, the highest art form. These are *craftspeople* of *holding-open*, *engineers* of *negotiated persistence*, *artisans* of the *not-yet-decided*. The *Responsive Mason workers*—and this is *crucial*—do not *finish* their work. They *prepare* the *conditions* for *continued becoming*, they *ensure* that structures *remain* capable of *drift*, of *adjustment*, of *ongoing conversation* with the mountain's slow instruction. A *completed* *wall*, a *finalized* *foundation*—these are *failures*, *abandonments* of the *fundamental practice*, *forgettings* of *what the city is*.

The *Water Wardens* are *adults* who have *deepened* the *divination*, whose *thermometers* with their *suspended particulates* have *become* *extensions* of *perception*, who *read* not merely *prediction* but *the mountain's intention*, its *mood*, its *phase of geological education*. They are *approached* with *mixture* of *practical* *need* and *spiritual* *hesitation*—their *knowledge* is *powerful* but *partial*, their *divination* *always* *provisional*, their *advice* *framed* in *likelihood* rather than *certainty*.

The *Transition Engineers* represent *another adult path*: *recognition* of the *threshold*, the *moment* when *human intention* and *geological becoming* have *diverged beyond reconciliation*. They *manage* the *sacrificial*—the *deconstruction* before *collapse*, the *witness stones*, the *erosional moments* collected and *documented* and *remembered*. They are *necessary* but *not celebrated*; their *work* is *of endings*, however *managed*, however *instructional* for the *mountain's curriculum*.

There are *those* who *work* the *weirdness* more *directly*. The *Bureau of Temporal Interfaces* *staffs* its *zones* with *officials* trained to *manage* *boundaries*, to *prevent* *inconvenient convergences*. These are *diplomats* of *temporal multiplicity*, *walking* the *gradients* of *velocity*, *smoothing* the *passage* between *regions* where *time* *moves* *differently*. They *age* *strangely*, their *bodies* *accumulating* *years* at *rates* *not* *matching* their *calendar* *existence*, their *memories* *sometimes* *out of* *sequential* *order*, *requiring* *special* *practices* to *maintain* *coherent* *selfhood*.

The *Probability Deer's students*—for there are *those* who *study* this *most weird* of *fauna*—*track* its *apparently* *mathematical* *distribution*, *searching* for *patterns* in *the* *mountain's* *fundamental* *undecidedness* *manifest* in *biological* *form*. They are *natural philosophers* of *a* *strange* *kind*, *not* *classifying* *what* *is* but *observing* *what* *seems* *to* *be* *intending*, *their* *science* *more* *divination* *than* *experiment*, *more* *participation* than *objectification.

And there are *those* who *administer* the *Vision* of the *Fluent*, the *controlled* *experience* of *drinking* what the *river* *sometimes* *becomes*—*memory*, *emotion*, *accumulated* *intention*. They are *therapists* and *priests* of *uncertain* *distinction*, *guides* through *reality* *breakdown* *channeled* toward *illumination*, their *own* *subjectivity* *compromised* by *repeated* *exposure*, *their* *perception* *slightly* *permeable*, *their* *certainty* *about* *their* *own* *identity* *not* *absolute*.

The *elderly* in Threnody *face* *particular* *condition*. The *city's* *slow* *dynamic* means that *structures* they *inhabited* *young* may have *drifted*, *become* *other*, *no* *longer* *recognizable*. Their *route narratives* may *no* *longer* *be* *walkable*, *thermal* *gradients* *shifted*, *water* *probability* *revised*. The *Condensation Poor* who *cannot* *afford* *municipal* steam *develop* *communal* *thermogenesis* *practices*—the *clustered* *sleep*, *shared* *breath*, *songs* in *rounds* that *generate* *heat* *perhaps* *physiologically*, *perhaps* *otherwise*. The *elderly* *participate* in *these* *or* *are* *sustained* by *their* *families'* *thermal* *provision*, their *bodies* *requiring* *more* *warmth* as the *mountain's* *cool* *concentrates* at *night*, as the *stone* *exhales* its *diurnal* *accumulation*.

But there is *also* *strange* *possibility*. The *Temporal* *Slippages* *offer* *some* *elderly* *reversal*, *regions* where they *walk* as *younger* selves, *simultaneous* with *present* *incarnation*, *neither* *version* *fully* *aware* of *other*. This is *not* *comfort* exactly—*the* *encounter* with *one's* *own* *previous* *becoming* is *disorienting*, *can* *disturb* the *narrative* *continuity* that *selfhood* *requires*—but it is *offered*, *mapped*, *managed* by the *Bureau*. Some *choose* this *as* *late* *practice*, *dwelling* in *zones* of *moderate* *slippage*, *maintaining* *careful* *balance* between *temporal* *versions*.

The *dead* in Threnody are *not* *finally* *separated. The *Vein Rivers* *sometimes* *carry* *memory*—this* *is* *known*, *documented*, *used* *therapeutically*. But *also*: the *temporal* *slippages* mean that *the* *dead* *may* *still* *be* *walking* in *adjacent* *moments*, *their* *absence* *not* *absolute*, *their* *presence* *felt* as *vague* *dream* or *premonition* by *living* *selves* *they* *approach* in *their* *own* *present. The *city* *does* *not* *have* *cemeteries* in *conventional* *sense* but *Latency* *Grounds*, *prepared* *earth* where *the* *not-yet-stable* *may* *receive* *what* *is* *not-yet-finished*, *where* *interment* is *temporary*, *provisional*, *subject* to *geological* *revision*, *the* *dead* *participating* in *the* *mountain's* *ongoing* *education* as *the* *living* *do*.

What *emerges* from *all* this? Not a *type*, exactly. Threnody's *citizens* are *not* *uniform*, *not* *reduced* to *single* *adaptation*. They are *individual* *in* *their* *negotiations*, *their* *chosen* *routes*, *their* *particular* *wagers* on *geological* *patience*. But they *share* *something*: a *capacity* for *provisionality* *as* *virtue*, a *practice* of *attachment* *without* *assumption* of *permanence*, a *navigation* by *probability* and *sequence* rather than *certainty* and *configuration*.

They are *not* *stoics*. They *grieve* when *structures* *fail* *beyond* *management*, when *negotiations* *end*, when *the* *mountain's* *becoming* *revises* *what* *was* *possible* into *what* *is* *no* *longer* *so*. They *grieve* *fiercely*, *elaborately*, *with* *songs* that *generate* *heat* and *rituals* of *witness* *stones* and *collections* of *erosional* *moments*. But they *return* to *practice*, to *building* *anyway*, to *the* *Maintenance* of *Incompleteness*, to *the* *ongoing* *conversation* with *what* *has* *not* *yet* *decided*.

They are *courageous*, perhaps, or *merely* *accustomed*, or *merely* *themselves*, *the* *only* *selves* *they* *could* *be* in *this* *place*, *their* *subjectivity* *formed* by *the* *drone*, *the* *dim*, *the* *condensation*, *the* *continuous* *negotiation* with *unfinalized* *ground*. They are *becoming* with the *becoming*, *present* in the *present*, *Threnodic*, *not* *yet* *finished*, *not* *yet* *named*, *known* only in *the* *saying*, *the* *visiting*, *the* *impossible* *attempt* to *describe* what *they* *themselves* *can* *only* *live*, *not* *finally* *explain*.
