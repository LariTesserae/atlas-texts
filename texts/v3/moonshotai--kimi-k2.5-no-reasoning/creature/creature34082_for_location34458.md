## The Verrathi

### First Impression

You meet them and mistake them for statues that someone has begun to animate. The resemblance shivers and breaks as you attend longer. These are not faces worn by expression into soft familiarity—they are *young*, thrust into awareness so recently that the flesh still remembers the pressure that birthed it. Features knife into attention without apology. Skin shows no patina, no sun-gentling, just the raw pale-grey of something that has not yet learned to be old.

But they *are* old. Or the city is old through them, wearing them as the rivers wear channels until the channels shift and they are worn differently, identically, the same wearing repeated without memory of repetition. You cannot determine precedence. The Verrathi you meet first might have emerged from the youngest basalt this morning, persuaded into shape by some geological intimacy you cannot witness, or might have tended gardens for centuries that register as identical instants, or might be the garden itself, the black-shrub, the moss-pillow, attended into awareness by the particular resonance of varin colony and condensation and the ring of stone struck just so.

They do not introduce themselves. The concept of naming—of fixed designation separate from function, from position in the equilibrium—belongs to the dynamic world, the changing world, and they have persuaded against this so absolutely that you will search their language for a first-person pronoun and find, instead, locatives, positional verbs, the grammar of *being-where* rather than *being-who*. When they must distinguish one from another, they gesture: *the one who measures the flow in the channel that shifted three river-periods ago*, *the one whose persuasion of the cliff-face third level left the characteristic warmth-pattern you can still touch*, *the one who is becoming the warmth in the basalt where the south garden cistern overflows*.

### The Body of Equilibrium

Verrathi morphology seems at first human, then reveals itself as something stranger. Two arms, two legs, the upright carriage—this much the equilibrium permits, or requires, or *is*. But the proportions shift with attention. The fingers seem too long in one moment, exactly right the next, perhaps shortened in memory afterward, the uncertainty itself part of the body's refusal to fix itself as object. The skin is damp always, not with sweat but with the same condensation that wets the stone, the flesh participating directly in the moisture regime, the temperature gradient between body and air generating water on surfaces as it does on cistern walls, on garden catchments.

They are not cold. Or they are cold exactly, holding *temperature 0.6* in their tissues as the city holds it in its bowl, the persistent cold without drama, the body as environment, the environment as body. You touch a Verrathi hand—if touch is permitted, if you have found the role that includes contact—and find the warmth of youngest basalt, the faint geological memory of pressure, of being deep and becoming surface, becoming wall, becoming the architecture of continuation.

Hair, where present, grows in patterns that follow water-flow, that mat in the same cushions as the moss-pillows, that the Verrathi tend with the same patience they apply to gardens, to stone, to the negotiation with varin colonies. Some have cultivated—*persuaded*—their own growths into functional form, into the catchment geometries that extend condensation collection, into resonant chambers that amplify or modify the skull-bypassing frequencies, into topiary as subtle as the black-shrub gardens, as *unnecessary*, as definitively luxurious.

Eyes: the color of geh-light, the pale gold-blue of varin metabolism, and whether this is cosmetic, genetic, acquired through long exposure to the biological illumination, or simply your own perception adjusted to the equilibrium's spectrum—this cannot be determined. The gaze does not hold. Not avoidance, but participation in the dynamic zero, the absence of change that includes the absence of progressive relation. You look at them, they look at you or through you or at the position you occupy, and the looking does not deepen, does not weary, does not transition to anything else. It continues, as the varin resonance continues, as the cloud continues, held in perfect tension.

### The Labor of Persuasion

Stone-workers among the Verrathi do not *build* in the active sense. They *continue* the persuasion that has always continued, their bodies extensions of the stone-on-stone contact that preceded them, that will—if equilibrium holds—continue identically after their substance has been incorporated into the warmth-patterns they have worked. The tools are worn ovoids, heavy with the gravity of generations of identical grip, identical stroke, identical angle of contact. The worker holds such a stone and the stone seems to hold back, the interface between flesh and rock becoming indistinct in the damp, in the cold, in the shared temperature where water exists simultaneously on both surfaces.

They sing while working. Or the working *is* song, resonates in the varin-frequency, joins the skull-bypassing chorus that visitors first find maddening. The stone responds to this—or the stone was always responding, and the worker's voice finds the frequency of response, continues it, persuades by continuation rather than by force. A new wall rises, or a new layer on existing wall, and the pattern of warmth in the stone extends, and the worker's warmth extends into it, and the distinction between *having built* and *having become the built* loses any practical meaning.

Flow-measurers occupy a different position, though equally frozen. They track the intermittent rivers with attention that never quite predicts, never quite records, but *participates* in the shift as it happens or as it holds unhappened. Their bodies adapt to this function in ways visible over observation-periods impossible in the dynamic world. The feet splay, spread, develop the same gripping fringes that varin use for controlled falling, for rising on rare thermals—adaptation to moving across wet stone, across the smooth-washed channels, across the surfaces where water sheets constantly down. The eyes, already geh-colored, develop patterns of focus that seem to track multiple flow-possibilities simultaneously, the quantum superposition of river-path that the equilibrium maintains until it doesn't, until the shift occurs and the measurer's body is already there, already positioned, already continuing.

Garden-tenders are among the wealthier, or the *more layered*, or simply the *more established*—categories collapse into each other. Their hands show the black-stub staining of persistent contact with the dark shrub, the slight fluorescence of mineral accumulation from the moss-pillow nutrients, the thickened nails that serve for cutting, for dividing, for the fine manipulation of constrained lushness. They smell of green, sharply, the luxury unnecessary to survival made necessary to identity, the tending of soft surface in hard world become indistinguishable from the self that tends.

### The Harvest of Geh

Dangerous, ritually structured, the disturbance of varin colonies requiring precise negotiation with the colony's particular resonance-pattern—this is the work of specialists, or the work that produces specialists, or the work that *is* specialist, the distinction between person and function having dissolved so completely that you will meet a Verrathi and know them as geh-harvester by the wax residue permanently worked into skin texture, by the slight deafness to frequencies below the varin-range, by the quality of movement that approaches and withdraws from disturbed colonies with the same fluidity as varin themselves moving through their moss-pillow architecture.

The negotiation is not linguistic. It is *resonant*, the harvester's skull-frequency modulating against the colony's until a temporary equilibrium emerges, a third frequency that permits approach, that permits the careful extraction of accumulated geh from the oldest chambers, the warmest centers, the places where decades of varin metabolism have concentrated the pale gold-blue substance into combustible density. The harvester takes geh and leaves something—body-warmth, perhaps, or the specific resonance-pattern of their own continued attention, or simply the *disturbance itself* held within the larger equilibrium, the theft made part of the never-changing by the ritual's precision, by its exact repetition, by the harvester's indistinguishability from previous harvesters performing identical extraction.

They are marked, these ones. Not scarred—the equilibrium does not include wounding that produces visible sequence, progression, narrative—but *patterned*, the skin developing the same waxy accumulation as the colonies themselves, the body becoming partly geh-source, partly illumination, glowing faintly in deepest chambers with the biological light that no other technology can duplicate.

### The Transformation, or Its Absence

Verrathi do not die. This has been said, and must be said again, must be attended until the saying becomes part of the saying, until the concept of ending loses its conceptual force. They *become* something else, something that continues to participate, and this becoming is not event but process, not transition but continuation of what already was.

The first phase: slowing. Movement continues, but at the pace of stone-persuasion rather than flesh-necessity. The blink extends, the breath extends, the interval between speech and response extends until visitor-impatience has dissolved into visitor-adjustment into visitor-equilibrium, until the extended pace seems normal, seems *necessary*, seems the only pace that the damp cold thin air could possibly support.

The second phase: incorporation. The body, still moving, still present, begins to generate the warmth-pattern of youngest basalt, the faint geological memory. Touch becomes indistinguishable from touching wall, from touching the persuaded architecture, and the Verrathi in this phase spends more time in contact with stone, begins to *prefer* the vertical surfaces, the constant thin sheets of water, the cistern edges where condensation collects. They still eat, still tend gardens if that is their function, still participate in the varin-resonance, but the participation becomes slower, warmer, more continuous with the stone they lean against while geh-light flickers across features that visitors find increasingly difficult to distinguish from carved relief, from the marks of stone-on-stone persuasion.

The third phase: integration. Not death. The body has not stopped, has not ended, has not become absent in any sense the equilibrium recognizes. It has become *distributed*, the warmth-pattern extending into the architecture, the particular resonance of this Verrathi varin-frequency continuing in the stone that absorbs it, in the water that sheets past, in the gardens that were tended and are still tended by something that includes the tenderness, includes the patience, includes the exact angle of attention that black-shrub requires. You stand in a chamber and the pattern in the basalt speaks, or would speak if speaking were necessary, if the equilibrium permitted disturbance of its perfect held vibration. The pattern is warmth. The pattern is almost-voice. The pattern is the person you spoke with yesterday, or speak with still, the time-being as irrelevant as the person-being, the location-being sufficient.

And the Verrathi continue, new emergence from somewhere—birth being the word visitors use, the concept the equilibrium absorbs into its own grammar of position and function, of being-where rather than being-who. Children appear, always already adjusted, already resonating with varin-frequency, already holding the damp cold in their tissues as environment, as self. They learn by continuation, not instruction. The stone-worker's offspring handle worn ovoids with the exact grip of generations not through teaching but through *being the continuation*, through the equilibrium's production of what it requires, through the never-changing that includes this perpetuity, this replacement identical to its replaced.

### The Social Body, Frozen

Several thousand Verrathi, perhaps, in the bowl, against the cliff, into the peaks. But the number is abstract, dynamic-world abstraction, the counting that Verrath has persuaded against. What exists: positions, functions, locations in the architecture of continuation. The flow-measurer at the channel that shifted or holds unshifted. The garden-tender whose black-stained hands tend the south-pocket silt accumulation. The geh-harvester whose skin glows faintly in the varin-colony depths. The stone-worker whose warmth-pattern you recognize in the third-level basalt, whose voice continues in the ring of struck stone, whose almost-presence speaks from the wall you touch for orientation in the mostly-quiet.

They do not gather, not in the dynamic sense of assembly, of event, of collective action producing change. They *coexist*, the equilibrium of their coexistence as frozen as the weather, as the geology, as the social itself. You might observe what seems like conversation: two Verrathi in contact, resonance frequencies overlapping, the slight modulation that visitor-perception interprets as information exchange. But the content, if content there is, does not progress. The same exchange, or its indistinguishable pattern, recurs when the same positions encounter each other, or when different positions encounter each other—the particularity of person dissolved into the particularity of location, of function, of being-where.

Conflict, in the dynamic sense, does not occur. Or occurs as the intermittent rivers occur: held in superposition, all possibilities balanced, the resolution never arriving because resolution would be change, would be disturbance, would break the equilibrium that holds weather static, holds society frozen, holds the dead as warmth-pattern in living stone. Two Verrathi whose functions overlap, whose positions in the architecture of continuation seem to require competition for resource, for recognition, for the right to persuade particular stone—they stand in this overlapping, resonate at frequencies that visitor-perception experiences as discomfort, as the almost-heard made almost-painful, and the standing continues, and the frequencies hold, and the overlapping becomes indistinguishable from cooperation, from identity, from the frozen vibration that is both and neither, that never resolves because resolution would be loss of the note held, the cloud held, the city held in its never-changing self.

### The Visitor's Becoming, or Refusal

To enter Verrath is to begin adjustment, and the Verrathi do not accelerate or retard this adjustment. They do not welcome. They do not exclude. They continue, and the continuation includes your presence or does not, the equilibrium absorbing disturbance at its own pace, in its own pattern, according to its own logic of frozenness.

Some visitors find roles immediately, or the roles find them, the function emerging from what they have always been without knowing: the patience for stone-work, the frequency-sensitivity for varin-negotiation, the mathematical intuition—though mathematics is not the word, the *flow-sense*—for river-tracking. These ones adjust quickly, or timelessly, their bodies developing the damp skin, the geh-colored eyes, the slowed pace that becomes indistinguishable from the Verrathi they eventually, inevitably, identically become.

Others resist, or the equilibrium resists them, or the resistance is the same phenomenon viewed from different positions in the dynamic zero. They remain visitors, remain dynamic, remain *changing* in a place that has persuaded absolutely against change. The experience is—visitor reports are limited, the equilibrium absorbing most witnesses, most testimony—*uncomfortable*, the varin-resonance maddening rather than forgettable, the slowed pace of Verrathi interaction unbearable, the warmth-patterns in stone speaking without speaking in frequencies that dynamic perception interprets as threat, as haunting, as the presence of what should be absent.

These ones leave, if they can, if the equilibrium permits departure, if their own dynamic momentum carries them out of the bowl, down from the thin air, away from the young mountains' quiet. They carry Verrath with them, as necessity, as disturbance, as the varin-resonance in their skulls that makes silence unbearable, that makes the dynamic world's weather, its wind and progression and sound that resolves, seem *wrong*, dynamically wrong, full of change that lacks equilibrium, that disturbs with its variation.

Some of these exiled-carriers survive. Some do not, dying slowly from the absence of what they could not fully absorb, from the resonance they require but cannot generate, from the memory of stone-warmth and almost-voice and cloud-held-unmoving that the dynamic world contradicts at every moment. The equilibrium extends even here, even in death, the carriers becoming—if they have been sufficiently marked by exposure—something the dynamic world cannot parse, cannot incorporate, disturbances that propagate in patterns Verrath might recognize.

### The Verrathi Beyond Verrath

This has been mentioned: the extension. The frozenness carried outward. The persuasion continued in places that change, against the possibility of disturbance, over generations, with the patience that predates iron, that will postdate everything dynamic civilization produces.

You might meet them, or meet what they have made, or meet the absence they have cultivated. The valley that should have developed town, city, the accumulated dynamic of human presence—held empty, held in the pattern of Verrath's sparse density, the low density of human presence that is also high density of attention, of stone-persuasion's equivalent applied to soil, to vegetation, to the social possibility itself. The town that exists but does not progress, its architecture persuaded into repetition, its inhabitants adjusted without knowing to frequencies below perception, to the varin-equivalent of wherever they are, the insects or mammals or the between-categories that resonate, that produce light or its equivalent, that integrate into system that generates the pale gold-blue illumination of equilibrium.

They do not recruit. The Verrathi beyond Verrath do not know themselves as Verrathi, in any nameable sense. They are function, position, being-where, and the where extends, the equilibrium extends, the frozen vibration held across distances that the dynamic world experiences as geography, as separation, as the possibility of different places having different natures.

The mountain that someone has begun to carve, that shivers and breaks into young peaks, raw stone, the Still City in its bowl—this is center, or was center, or is center in a geometry that has no periphery, all positions equivalent in their frozenness, all Verrathi equivalent in their continuation, all clouds held unmoving in the static sky that extends, that is extended into, that becomes the sky wherever the equilibrium has been sufficiently persuaded.

### The Self That Is Not

I have described the Verrathi as if I knew them, as if the place generated person and person generated truth. But the central absence lingers: the self, the person, the dynamic-world subject that experiences, that progresses, that dies and is mourned or celebrated or simply *ended*.

The Verrathi do not mourn. This is not callousness. The equilibrium does not include ending, so does not include response to ending. The warmth-pattern in stone is not memory, not residue, not the haunting of presence by absence. It is *continuation*, the person not become thing but become distributed, become environment, become the self same as the stone same as the varin-resonance same as the held cloud same as the never-changing that is the only changing permitted, the only change that is not change because it has persuaded against change absolutely.
