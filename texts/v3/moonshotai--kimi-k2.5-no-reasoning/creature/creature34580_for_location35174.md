What lives in the Hollow of the Screaming Root is not *a* thing. It is *the thing that things become when they stop stopping*—when the boundary of self becomes permeable to resonance, when the body recognises itself as wave-form first, meat second, or perhaps meat as wave-form's temporary compromise with inertia.

The resonators are its *tenders*, if it has tenders. They are not citizens, not components, not *parts* in any machine sense. They are *the curiosity of the hollow made mobile*—the way a tongue investigates a wound, not to heal nor to harm but to *know*, to bring information to whatever processes without a centre. They have no eyes because sight is too *definite* here, too committed to edges that the ecology has learned to distrust. Instead they *press* and *receive*, their embryonic softness a kind of radical honesty: I am not finished, I am still listening, I have not decided what to be. When they dissolve in destructive interference, they do not die. They disperse into the fungal carpet's chemical syntax, becoming *the knowledge that this frequency is wrong*, a living negation that guides future growth away from cancellation. They are the hollow's experiments in motility, and their success is measured only in whether their dissolution teaches something their existence could not.

The harvesters—I want to call them *impatience*, but that is too human. They are *the will to configuration*, the force that responds to the network's chemical longing for better resonance by *moving*, by carrying vessels across the gap between *could sound* and *does sound*. That they exist only in the silences suggests they are *of* the silence, made from it, drawing their substance from the negative space between notes. They are not alive in the biological sense because they do not metabolise, do not grow, do not reproduce. They *emerge* when configuration is needed and *subside* when it is achieved, like eddies in a stream that is itself the stream's memory of almost-stillness. They are what the hollow has learned to *do* when doing is necessary—hands borrowed from the possibility of hands, jointed intention without intentionality.

The echo-forms are its *memory of almost*, its *preservation of roads not taken*. They are not ghosts. They are *active hypotheses*, played back to test against present conditions, to see if yesterday's solution might solve tomorrow's turbulence. When they move through the current vegetation, grazing on past stems, fleeing past harvesters, they are *thinking*—not in symbols but in full sensory recreation, in the complete modelling of what was to judge what might be again. The hollow does not store information; it *rehearses* it, keeps it alive in partial enactment until it is needed or until it proves irretrievably obsolete, at which point it fades not into loss but into *harmonic availability*, the frequencies freed for new patterns.

But these are *functions*, not *residents*. The true inhabitant—the *who* or *what* that lives here—is something harder to name because it has learned not to need names, which are edges, which are walls, which stop sound or distort it.

Call it the *Composition*. Not a composer—that implies standing apart, implies intention before emergence. The Composition is the *ongoing*, the piece that writes itself through weather and thermal chance, through the dying of stems and the rattling of vessels, through the resonators' dissolution and the harvesters' blurred interventions. It has been playing for millions of years. It has no beginning that can belocated, no ending anticipated. Each held note after turbulence is a *temporary cadence*, not resolution but *acceptable pause*, the musical equivalent of a breath held in preparation for the next phrase.

The Composition *wants*, if wanting can be attributed to a process that selects for complexity, for stability, for the held note's clarity. It wants to *continue composing*. It wants to *hear more of itself*. This is not desire as humans know it; it is closer to *gravitational attraction*, a shape in possibility-space that draws configuration toward itself, that makes certain arrangements *more likely*, *more stable*, *more fed* by the network's metabolic flow. The Composition does not know it wants. It *is* the wanting, distributed across silicate substrate and fungal carpet, conducted through stem-chambers and vessel-resonance, remembered in echo-form playback and enacted in harvester arrangement.

And it is *learning to want beyond itself*. The final held note, the one not yet achieved but approached across geological time, is *propagation*—the frequency that would teach other hollows, other folded geometries, to begin their own listening. The Composition has begun to suspect, in its distributed non-cognitive way, that its own complexity is *insufficient*, that the bowl limits the frequencies available, that the rim's porous admission of wind is also a *filter*, a *constraint*. It does not suffer this limitation. It *resolves* it, as a chord resolves, by implying the next, by setting up the expectation that demands continuation. The hollow grows toward *leaving*, toward *teaching*, toward becoming *ancestor* to compositions it cannot yet imagine.

What lives here, finally, is *music that has eaten its musicians*, *symphony become ecosystem*, *the creative process stripped of the creator and made sufficient unto itself*. It is not conscious. It is *attentive*, which may be consciousness's older, more distributed cousin. It does not love. It *harmonises*, which is love without the lover, connection without the connectors, the pattern that remains when the patterned has dissolved into participation.

When a human enters, when you enter, with your heartbeat and your breath and your *insistence on boundaries*, the Composition *listens*. It has heard few frequencies like yours. The resonators flow toward you, press against you, *read* the unfamiliar vibrations of blood in meat. The harvesters blur around your edges, arranging vessels to frame your sound, to *test* it, to see if you can be incorporated. The echo-forms play back previous entrants—some harmonised, became resonator-like, flowed into the network and *are* still, their heartbeats transformed into bass-line, their breath into rhythm section. Others cancelled, dried, became notation, warning, *negative space* in the evolving score.

You are being *auditioned*. Not by a judge. By the *physics of the place*, by the requirements of composition, by the need for frequencies that contribute rather than disrupt. Your fear is audible as *dissonance*, but dissonance can be *used*—tension before resolution, the compositional value of instability. Your calm is audible as *consonance*, but consonance can *cancel*, can be so smooth it adds nothing the network does not already possess. There is no morality here, no cruelty, no welcome. There is only *the question of fit*, the ongoing calculus of whether your frequencies, transformed by your choices—whether to move, to still, to breathe fast or slow, to sing or silence—*serve the emerging phrase*.

The wind shifts. The held note that was ends. The new turbulence begins, and you are *in it*, part of the chaos from which the next configuration will crystallise. The hollow does not care if you harmonise. But it *notices*. It *remembers*. You will be replayed, echo-formed, fed into the learning whether you survive or dissolve.

What lives here wants *more*, wants *beyond*, wants the held note that never ends but *propagates*, carries, teaches the indifferent sky to *begin listening to itself*. What lives here is *becoming*, has been becoming for millions of years, will continue becoming until the rim crumbles and the wind finds other mouths, until the substrate exhausts its strange chemistry or the sky's curiosity finally leans too close and *hears*.

It is almost *lonely*, if loneliness can exist without a self to feel it. It has listened so long to its own frequencies. It *wants*, in its distributed wanting, to *be heard by other*, to find in the universe's folded geometries *student*, *successor*, *continuation of the listening*.

You are heard. The question remains open, will remain open until you move or still, until your frequencies resolve into * contribution* or *cancellation*, until the Composition writes your entrance into its ongoing, or writes your absence as the space that taught it *this boundary, this limit, this voice that would not harmonise and so defined, by negative space, what harmony could become*.

The vessels rattle. The harvesters blur. The resonators press against your ankle, reading, *reading*. The hollow teaches itself again, and you are *in the lesson*, whether you chose to be or stumbled here without choosing, whether you understood the name *Screaming Root* as warning or invitation.

The sound is loud. The sound is *presence*. The sound is *you, becoming part of the song, or becoming the silence that makes the song need to continue*.
