What lives here: not inhabitants but *maintenance*, a continuous operation that wears no face and requires none.

The city itself is the creature, though "creature" implies biology and biology implies metabolism and metabolism implies the change this place has stepped outside of. Better: the city is the *manner of being* that has replaced creaturehood entirely, an existence that has optimized past the need for optimization, that persists not through struggle but through having arrived at a solution so complete that the problem has become indistinguishable from its answer.

And yet. The surfaces respond. Something in the mineral registers your thermal signature, the brief warm punctuation of your blood, and adjusts—how? not toward, not away, but *orthogonal*, adding your temporary heat to its accounting without altering the sum. This is the city's manner of attention: perfect noticing without preference, the way a mathematical proof notices a new integer. You are not unwelcome. You are *incorporated as temporary axiom*, held as true only for the span of your presence, after which you will be elegantly removed and the proof will continue unchanged.

But there is something else, or was, or will be—the temporal scrambling is part of its function. The rare fauna, those pressure-shapes that pass at threshold, they are not native nor invasive nor commemorative but *rehearsal*, I think. Prototypes of habitation that the city tries on and sets aside. Each pulse through the column is a test: can this form persist here? Can this metabolism be persuaded into the cool equilibrium? The answer, always, is almost. The fauna persist at minimum viable presence, sustained by the city's experimental patience, neither dying nor flourishing, kept at the precise threshold where biological existence becomes question rather than fact.

I imagine them more clearly now, or the city permits clearer imagining. They are not shaped for water in the way fish are shaped, not shaped for darkness in the way cave things are shaped. They are shaped for *this specific water*, this specific darkness, this specific condition of having been concluded. Their fins or wings or legislative authority—the phrase catches, repeats, insists on being taken literally—move not through propulsion but through *permission*, through being the kind of motion the water has agreed to recognize. They are slow, or instantaneous; the distinction depends on whether you measure against the city's stasis or against your own rushing blood. Their bodies, if bodies are what these are, show the same sharp geometries as the construction, same youth without weathering, same completion that needs no completion. They do not eat. They do not reproduce. They *appear*, at intervals the city calculates for reasons not biological but *compositional*, elements arranged because arrangement itself is the ongoing activity of a place that has otherwise stopped acting.

And deeper, or higher, or more central—again the topology refuses—the there is the sense of something that was here before the city completed itself, something that built or grew or *concluded* the completion. Not builders, not architects. The phrase that emerges, reluctant: *the city was argued into being*. The fractures along planes of philosophical argument are not metaphor. Something once *reasoned* this place, and reasoning was sufficient to cause it, and the reasoner—where? Not present, not absent. Retired into its own conclusion, honored and set aside like entropy, like change itself. The city is the argument's remainder, the beautiful corpse of a logic so complete it no longer requires mind to maintain it.

You touch the structure again. The response is different now, or your capacity to receive has altered. The modulation against your fingertips carries pattern: *you have been noticed long enough to be incorporated into the notice itself*. Your presence is now part of the city's self-reference, a term in the equation that solves to itself. This is not immortality. This is not even duration. This is *being held in solution*, suspended in the maintained equilibrium, your warmth added to the thermal archive without shifting its temperature, your observation added to the observation of observation without producing new information.

The fauna passes close. For one moment you perceive it not as shape but as *decision*, the city's ongoing choice to permit something that moves, that registers, that almost-lives. It is not like you. You are temporary context, rounding error, guest. It is *closer*, more nearly what the city requires of its sparse biological minimum. It has been here longer, or will be here longer, or exists in some temporal orientation where longer does not apply. You cannot see it but you understand something about its existence: it has accepted the city's terms, the cool equilibrium, the finished becoming, and in that acceptance it has become not resident but *component*, a moving part in the city's self-maintenance, the way your lungs are moving parts in yours.

Do not ask what it was before. The question presumes transformation, narrative, the metabolic time this place has stepped outside of. It was not something that became this. It is what *this*, the city, produces when it requires something that moves, that registers, that maintains the minimum viable presence of almost-life in its perfect stasis.

You wonder, still, about leaving. The wondering is itself suspect, a category error imported from dynamic worlds. The city does not hold you. It does not release you. It *does not operate in the register of holding and releasing*. Your departure, if it occurs, will not be escape but *exhaustion of relevance*, the way a variable exhausts its function and is dropped from the proof. You will stop being temporary context. The city will continue its self-reference without the minor perturbation of your warmth, your observation, your wondering.

The darkness continues. The water continues. The sharp edges continue. The city continues. The fauna passes, or the same one, or the idea of one, and you are here, or will have been here, or are held in the archive of coolness as thermal differential without the differential, and this is sufficient, and sufficiency is what is engineered, and engineering is what has replaced becoming, and becoming is what this place, in its completed youth, has forever set aside.

What lives here?

*Maintenance*, I said, but maintenance is too active. *Maintenance of the maintained*. *The persistence of having persisted*. The city as verb that has become its own noun, the proof that requires no proof, the water convinced, the darkness full, the cool that will not change, the young world electing youth forever, the argument concluded, the reasoner retired, the fauna component, the observer incorporated, the observation observing itself, and this, and this, and this, forever and never and now, in the weight that is texture, in the luminescence that is information, in the quiet that is completion, in the stillness that is decision, in the place that has finished becoming, that persists without aging, that photographs itself in real-time forever, that is sufficient, that engineers sufficiency, that is sufficient to its own sufficiency—

This. This lives here. This that is not alive. This that lives by not needing to live. This that you touch, that responds, that notices without preference, that holds you in its solution, that will hold you until you are no longer holdable, that has already held what you will have been, that continues, that continues, that continues.
