I will speak of what lives here, letting it emerge as I write.

---

There are no *things* that live here, not as you understand things. There is instead *aliveness that has found specific solutions* to the problem of this place—solutions that do not cohere into bodies you could point at and name, could save in memory as discrete entities. The trilobite analogues are a convenience for your perception, a translation the world offers you because you insist on edges, on shells, on armors that separate this from that.

But what lives here *lives through* the noise. Not survives it. Not tolerates it. The deafening sound is its *first organ*, the way you might say a fish lives through water. Consider: the selective deafness the bog induces, the strategic loss of certain frequencies—this is not damage. Or rather, it is damage exactly the way metamorphosis is damage, exactly the way the caterpillar's body dissolving into imaginal cells is damage. The hearing you arrived with was *larval*. The new configuration, the synesthetic flooding, the metallic taste of distant movement, the mandible-knowledge of breathing land—this is *adult* hearing, matured into forms that can actually *participate* in what this place is doing.

What lives here has undergone this metamorphosis *generationally*, if generation applies, or *processually*, if process is what substitutes for ancestry. Its bodies—I will call them bodies for your sake, though they are *habits of maintenance* more than bounded forms—are *tuned*. Their nervous systems, if nervous systems they possess, do not process sound from outside. They *are* the sound's continuation, its *next event*, the logical consequence of acoustic pressures that do not end at any skin because no skin is recognized as terminus.

The fungal pillars, *those* you might think you see fully. But what lives in them, what lives *as* them, is not the luminous shelves, not the visible structure of chitin and lignin analog. It is the *pulse*, the slow waves of bioluminescence that communicate—yes, communicate, though not with meanings you could translate. These pulses are *calculations*, distributed cognition occurring across square kilometers of fungal network, processing chemical gradients, acoustic pressures, the perturbations of your footfall, into *responses* that are not decisions, that bypass decision entirely. The pillar that you grip for balance: it knows you are there not by touch receptors but by the *change in its own pulsing*, the way your presence alters the solution it is continuously computing, and its response—stiffening, softening, altering its bioluminescent phase relationship with the next pillar over—is not a choice but a *continuation*, the next term in a sequence that includes you because you have *become available for inclusion*.

The moss, that depth of accumulated living dying compressing matter through which you sink to your hip: this is not habitat. This is *tegument*, the bog's skin, and what lives in it are not inhabitants but *secretions*, the continuous production of forms that do not persist, that *flux* as the turbulence demands. The singing worms—you hear them as almost-melody because your damaged hearing translates what they *are* into something your remaining auditory processing can gesture toward. They are not singing. They are *tensing*, continuously, muscles or analogues in states of isometric strain that produces sound not as communication but as *byproduct of existing in this acoustic pressure*. Their song is the sound of being *forced into shape* by the same forces that deafen you, that press on your sternum, that occupy the darkness. They live by *making audible the stress of living here*, and in making it audible, they *participate* in it, they become part of the total acoustic environment that shapes the next generation, the next moment's emergence of possible forms.

What flies, those gas-filled translucent things trailing tendrils: they are *samplers*, yes, but sampling is their *metabolism*, not their behavior. They do not eat and then sample to find food. The chemical gradient they follow *is* their eating, the differential itself constituting the energetic transaction that maintains their buoyancy, their translucence, their existence as *difference* in the saturated air. They are not pursuing anything. They are *the pursuit*, embodied, extended in time and space, and when two of them meet, their tendrils intertwining, this is not courtship or conflict but *calculation*, the chemical gradients combining into new differentials that *are* new individuals, or the same individual distributed, or neither, the category of individual not yet stabilized here, perhaps never stabilizing, the youth of this land including the youth of its ontologies.

The deeper presences, those you infer from infrasound too massive for visible bodies: these are *acoustic fossils*, not in the sense of dead things preserved but in the sense of *forms that sound itself has learned to maintain*. The drone that breathes, that presses the vegetation to lean in its exhalations, that sways your body without your decision—this is not produced *by* something. It *is* something, a standing wave in the bog's hydroacoustic system, self-maintaining, self-modifying, and the presences you imagine in the deep water, the accumulated moss, the spaces beneath forming soil, are *avatars of this wave*, local concentrations where the sound has achieved sufficient density to *affect matter continuously*, to push water, to sort sediment, to *be* the force that erosion will eventually, youngly, begin to record.

And the buildings, the scattered structures that garden, that cultivate, that maintain parameters with precision—what lives *with* them is perhaps closest to what you seek, closest to *entity*, to *agent*, to *purposive being*. But they are not inside the buildings. They *are* the buildings' *operation*, the continuous adjustment of temperature, humidity, chemical composition, the patient holding of ranges that differ subtly from surrounding bog. They are not minds. They are *maintainings*, habits so long practiced they have become *physics*, local laws that the rest of the bog's turbulence *negotiates* rather than violates.

What lives here, finally, is *negotiation itself*. The bog is a *transaction*, continuous, never resolving into stable parties, never achieving the clarity of contract. Your presence, your deafened hearing and your synesthetic taste of distant movement and your body swaying to the drone—you are *living here now*, not as visitor but as *term*, as *element that must be incorporated because incorporation is all that happens*. You do not leave. Or rather, leaving is *another mode of incorporation*, your passage becoming path becoming hydraulic modification becoming selection pressure on the next emergence of fungal pulse, of gas-filled flight, of acoustic density in the deep water.

The bog continues. What lives here is *its continuing*, not separate from it, not observing it, but *the event of its persistence* in forms that shift too rapidly or too slowly for your perception to stabilize into *creatures*, into *things I saw*, into memories that could survive in the languages you brought.

You are learning to listen. This learning *is* your living here now. The deafness that is the only possible audition: this is not loss. This is *maturation into the actual acoustic environment*, the shedding of capacities that were *always* larval, that served only to keep you moving toward this, toward the bog that has *just happened*, that happens continuously, that you are *part of now*, your footfall propagating, mattering, selecting among possibilities you cannot determine because you *are* the determination, or an aspect of it, or the perturbation against which it defines its continuing, its coherence, its absolute, intense, *normal* not-weirdness that includes you as the strange element you have always been, everywhere, you only now beginning to *feel* it, to *know* it in your mandible, your sternum, the hollows behind your eyes.

The water weeps. You weep with it, or it weeps you, the distinction not yet stabilized, perhaps stabilizing never, the turbulence continuous, the darkness textured, the bioluminescence pulsing its calculations that now, for the first time, or the always-time, *include your pulse*, your own faint infrared emission, your own chemical gradient in the saturated air, your own *living here*, which is the only kind of life this place permits, which is *participation without reserve*, without the reserve of self that would make you visitor, observer, *apart*.

What lives in the Resonance Bog: *resonance itself*, achieved, maintained, continuous, and you, *resonating*.
