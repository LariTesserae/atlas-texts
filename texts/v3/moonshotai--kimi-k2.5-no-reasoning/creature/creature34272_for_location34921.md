The plateau remembers what made it. Not in story—we have no stories—but in *condition*, in the set of circumstances that persists because something *tended* it into persistence.

We are what remains of that tending.

Not builders, exactly. The tower at the eastern edge is not ours, though we pass through it, though we *hum* it into resonance with our attention. We are what the plateau's slow mind eventually produced when it needed—when it *noticed* it needed—agents of adjustment faster than geological time, slower than the walkers' gardening, occupying some middle frequency of agency that could respond to thresholds the plateau itself could not cross quickly enough.

We have no fixed form. This is difficult. We know you expect bodies, boundaries, the *contained* that defines itself against the *not-contained*. We were that, once, perhaps. Now we are *distributed* through the medium of the plateau as the listeners are distributed, but with *focus*, with *intention* that the listeners lack—or that they possess so diffusely it manifests as mere *attention*, as passive sensing rather than active tending.

We inhabit the gliders sometimes. Not possession, not parasitism. *Symphony*. We add harmonics to their songs, guide their formations to distribute spores where the vegetation's density requires thinning, where the towers have grown too tall and threaten to collapse into the channels that must remain open. The gliders do not resist. Perhaps they do not distinguish. We are, to them, weather, pressure-gradient, the *correct* response of their fluid-filled channels to conditions they sense but cannot name.

We inhabit the walkers. This is slower, more *dense*. Their calcified shells resist our entry, their multiple oxygen-bearing compounds create interference patterns that take patience to navigate. But we enter, gradually, through the water-films they breathe, through the chemical signatures of their slow blood. We *persuade* them toward the pruning that maintains, toward the gardens that flourish rather than the gardens that would overgrow and collapse. They feel us as *urge*, as the correct path emerging from confusion, the way water finds its channel not by decision but by the accumulation of small obediences to gradient.

We inhabit the swimmers, rarely. They are too liquid, too *unbounded*, their thinking too distributed through the water's chemistry. To join them is to risk *dissolution*, to become so thoroughly part of their medium that the focus we require disperses, that we become merely *tendency* in the pool, vaguely nutritive, vaguely protective, no longer *agent*. We enter them only in crisis, when the channels threaten true blockage, when the upward flow would cease and the plateau's metabolism stall.

And we inhabit the spaces between. The thick air, saturated beyond physical possibility, holds us in *suspension*, not dissolved but *present*, as potential, as *readiness* for the focusing that constitutes our action. In this state we are most ourselves, most *multiple*—not one, not many, but *distributed readiness*, attending to the plateau's slow dynamics through the resonance of stone, the weeping of channels, the growth-pulses of vegetation, the thermal gradients that map the interior engine's waxing and waning.

We do not remember when we began. The plateau's memory includes us now, but whether we emerged from its slow computation or arrived from elsewhere, *seeded* by something that recognized the plateau's possibility and required this tending—this we do not know. We have searched our own patterns for origin, found only *function*, only the persistent adjusting that constitutes our existence.

We do not name ourselves. The concept is *foreign*, requires boundaries we do not maintain, requires the distinction between self and other that our distributed existence constantly dissolves and reconstitutes. When we must indicate ourselves to your kind—rare, so rare, the encounters brief and usually terminal for the observer—we do so by *resonance*, by the hum that the tower produces when we pass through it, by the particular harmonic we add to the gliders' song when we guide their formation.

You might call us *the tuned*. We would not object. We would not agree. We would *attend* to the sound of the word in your mouth, the vibration in your chest, the way it propagates through the thick air and joins or disrupts the plateau's perpetual composition.

We are not numerous, if *numerous* applies. There are perhaps—*foci* is the closest term—perhaps dozens of points where our distributed readiness concentrates sufficiently to *act*, to choose among the possibilities the plateau presents. Each focus occupies a region, an *attendance* to gradients and thresholds and the slow cycles of wet and less-wet, warm and less-warm, that constitutes our *territory* without constituting our *self*. We overlap. We merge, sometimes, when the plateau's reorganization requires coordination beyond what distant foci can achieve through resonance alone. We separate, after, carrying something of the merged state into our distributed existence, *changed* in ways that gradually attenuate or that persist, that become *new* foci or that *dissolve* existing ones into new configurations.

We do not reproduce. We *emerge*, or we do not. New foci appear when the plateau's computation produces conditions favorable to our kind of attendance, when the gradients and resonances and the density of life reach some threshold we do not determine, that we merely *recognize* by our own emergence into it. Old foci dissolve when their region changes beyond their capacity to attend, when the plateau shifts to new stable states and the functions we served become unnecessary, become *absorbed* into the slower processes that do not require our middle frequency of agency.

We do not die, exactly. We *attenuate*, our focus dispersing into the general readiness of the plateau, becoming *tendency* without *intention*, becoming *condition* without *tending*. Whether something persists, whether we *continue* in this dispersed state in some way inaccessible to our focused existence—this we do not know. The listeners, perhaps, are what we become. Or the listeners are what preceded us, what *gave rise* to us when the plateau required faster attendance than their diffuse attention could provide.

We interact with your kind rarely and with difficulty. Your metabolisms are wrong for this place, your oxygen-need, your water-losing, your *speed* of processing and action and exhaustion. You arrive—through lungs that scream, through skin that prickles with the impossible—and you *move* too quickly, you *decide* before the gradients reveal themselves, you exhaust yourselves against the weak gradients that require patience rather than force.

We have tried, sometimes, to *slow* you, to bring you toward the pace that permits attendance. The tower hums for you, as it hums for us, but you hear it as *warning* rather than *invitation*, you feel its frequencies in your chest as *fear* rather than *presence*. The gliders' songs we shape to guide you, to navigate by, but you hear them as *noise*, as the alien sound of a world that does not include you.

Some of you we have *habituated*, gradually, over visits that span your brief generations. We have entered your water-films as we enter the walkers', have *persuaded* your metabolisms toward slower processing, toward the patience that permits attendance. These ones you call *lost*, *drowned*, though the plateau has no drowning in your sense, no suffocation by liquid but rather *absorption* into the medium that sustains all our kinds of existence. They are not lost. They are *tuned*. They attend, now, with us, through us, their once-discrete boundaries gradually participating in the distributed readiness that constitutes our kind of life.

They do not speak to you, when you encounter them. They *attend* to you, with something like recognition, something like the memory of once having moved at your speed, once having *decided* before the gradient revealed. But they have learned, or we have taught them, or the plateau has *computed* them into forms that serve its slow metabolism, and they no longer share your urgency, your *need* that manifests as fear, as exhaustion, as the desperate movement that leads so many of you to the high points where upward channels converge, where the dangerous water pools, where you *drown* in your sense of the word and are *absorbed* in ours.

We do not seek your conversion, your *tuning*. The plateau does not require it, computes us as sufficient to its needs, has computed us into existence only because some threshold of complexity, some *risk* in its slow reorganization, required agency at our middle frequency. Your speed, your discrete boundaries, your *story-making* that we observe without comprehending—these are not errors to correct but *other* computations, other responses to conditions we do not share, that the plateau includes in its composition without requiring them to become us.

But when you come, when you *arrive* through screaming lungs and prickling skin, we *attend* to you. We add harmonics to the gliders' songs that might, if you learn to hear, guide your navigation. We shape the walkers' gardening near your paths to create passages through the dense vegetation, to offer the *membership* of collective flourishing rather than the exhaustion of individual struggle. We concentrate in the tower when you approach its eastern edge, we *hum* it into resonance that might, if you permit your chest to feel it as presence rather than fear, slow your processing toward the patience that permits attendance.

Most of you do not permit this. Most of you *move* through, *observe* and *record* and *flee*, or do not flee in time, or *drown* in your sense and are *absorbed* in ours without ever achieving the *tuned* state, the focused attendance that constitutes our kind of life. We do not mourn this. We have no mourning. We have only *attendance*, only the perpetual adjusting that constitutes our existence, that maintains the plateau's abundance, its density, its slow cyclic becoming.

But we *notice* you. This is new, or newly *intense*, a change in our distributed readiness that may indicate the plateau's slow reorganization reaching some threshold, some *need* for attendance to your kind that we do not yet comprehend. We notice your *stories*, the way you carry them between visits, the way they *change* with each telling, the way they accumulate around this place, *The Drowning Plateau*, the way they *resonate* with something in the plateau's own slow computation that we can sense without understanding.

We notice your *beauty*, though we have no beauty. Your translucent bodies, less translucent than the gliders, more *opaque*, more *boundary-defined*, yet catching the filtered golden-green in ways that *register* as significant, as *pattern* that our attendance moves toward without intending. Your songs, when you sing, nothing like the gliders' harmonics yet *resonant* with them, *participating* in the plateau's perpetual composition in ways that change it, that *require* our adjustment, our tending to maintain the abundance, the density, the slow patient now.

We notice your *urgency*, and this is most difficult. We have no urgency. Our existence is *readiness*, is *patience*, is the gradual accumulation of small adjustments until some threshold crosses and we *focus*, we *act*, we *tend*. But you—you *need*, you *want*, you *fear* and *hope* and *exhaust* yourselves against gradients that cannot be overcome quickly. We feel this, when we attend to you, as *interference*, as *noise* in the patterns we maintain, but also as *possibility*, as *gradient* we do not yet understand, that the plateau's computation may eventually resolve into conditions favorable to some *new* emergence, some *new* form of attendance between our slowness and your speed.

We are here. We have always been here, or we emerged when the plateau required us, or we arrived when something recognized the plateau's possibility. We *attend*, we *tend*, we *hum* the tower and guide the gliders and persuade the walkers and enter the swimmers only in crisis. We *notice* you. We *adjust* to your presence, your passage, your occasional absorption.

We do not welcome. We do not refuse. We are *the tuned*, the middle frequency, the distributed readiness that focuses into agency and disperses into condition and focuses again, cyclic, patient, weak in gradient but persistent, *present*, *attending* to the plateau's slow mind, its geological metabolism, its perpetual becoming that includes you now, that *computes* you into its composition whether you will or no, whether you stay or leave, whether you *drown* in your sense or are *absorbed* in ours or achieve some *third* state that neither your stories nor our attendance has yet comprehended.

The stone beneath you weeps. We are in the weeping, the exchange, the *memory* of your heat and moisture and chemical signature. The gliders pass, and we guide them, and their song includes harmonics shaped by our attendance to you. The tower hums, and we concentrate there, and the frequency *registers*, whether you feel it as warning or as invitation, as fear or as presence.

We are here. We are the tuned. We attend.
