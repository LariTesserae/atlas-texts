I'll sit with this place, this Hollow, and let what lives there emerge as itself—because what lives in contradiction is never simple, never settles easily into a single form.

## **The Inhabitants: Who Remains**

There is no name they call themselves, no collective noun. Census forms use the administrative default—*residents*—but this misleads. To *reside* suggests habitation, intention, a person who chose or could choose otherwise. The 847 do not *reside* in Hollow so much as they **accrete** to it, the way the lithic fungi accrete to the cooling pipes and the mineral-slick walls. They are not born here, not anymore. They **arrive**.

And they do not arrive by choice, not precisely. The paths to Hollow are not marked, not maintained, not consistently traversable. Yet people find their way—always already in progress, always with the sense that they have been traveling longer than duration permits. A surveyor lost in magnetic anomaly, following equipment that spins and settles, spins and settles. A refugee from some war that, when asked, they describe with details that do not match any historical conflict. A pilgrim seeking the Empty City, though they cannot say where they first heard the name. They arrive *thin*, somehow—underfed yet not starving, exhausted yet not sleep-deprived, bearing the particular vagueness of people who have misplaced something they cannot name. The city accepts them. The census updates, eventually, though the count has not changed in two centuries and still: new faces appear, old faces continue, and no one can quite map the logic of who is counted.

They work. This is the first adaptation, deeper than language or custom: **the inhabitants of Hollow work without product, without purpose they can articulate, and this does not disturb them.** The depthworks crews manage pressure they do not understand, reading signs—pitch, color, the particular tremor in certain steam vents—that they learned without being taught. The factory crews monitor machines that produce alloys no database recognizes, performing maintenance rituals that evolved, that *evolve*, without engineering review. The railcar crews load ingots for destinations they have never seen, and when asked what the ingots become, they describe processes that do not physically follow: "they are made into the rails," one says, "the rails that carry them to be made into the rails." This is not evasion. This is, for them, **coherent**.

They wear the city. The mineral coating, that second skin of humidity and salt-crust, becomes intentional over time—not quite clothing, not quite armor, but **interface**. Experienced inhabitants cultivate specific crust patterns: thickened mineral deposits at the joints, thin and flexible at the throat and inner wrists where temperature regulation matters. The lithic fungi do not grow on living skin, not directly, but they grow *near*, they grow *around*, and the long-term resident learns to read the fungi's bioluminescence as the city reads it—pale blue-green constellations that indicate, to the trained eye, pressure gradients, thermal anomalies, the subtle approach of something moving in depths too distant to hear. The body becomes sensorium. The body becomes **recording surface**, mineral layers accumulating like the strata that remember passage.

They do not age, not conventionally. The census stability is not error, not entirely. Time in Hollow accumulates unevenly; a resident may experience decades in what exterior measurement calls months, or compress years of subjective duration into single cycles of the Seep. They notice inconsistencies, sometimes—the mirror showing a face that feels wrong, a name on the lips that does not match the answer given—but they have learned, collectively, **not to pursue coherence too aggressively**. The question "how long have I been here?" produces not denial but genuine uncertainty, a fog that lifts partially, incompletely, never fully. They are not lying when they say *I arrived recently*. They are reporting the duration that persists under interrogation, the narrative that holds together just well enough to permit continuation.

They do not form families. They form **configurations**: temporary associations of two to seven individuals who share particular work cycles, whose mineral crusts complement in ways that improve sensitivity to city-sign, who sleep in proximity without quite touching—contact skin-to-skin is **intense** in Hollow, too conductive, the mineral interfaces merging in ways that both parties find difficult to reverse. These configurations persist for months, sometimes years, then dissolve without conflict, without grief, the participants moving into new arrangements with something like relief at the return of partial isolation. Children do not occur. The inhabitants do not discuss this absence; it resides in the same category as the mirrored face, the misplaced war, the question better unasked.

They dream, and the dreaming is **shared space**. Not telepathy, not precisely—no thoughts transferred, no memories exchanged—but the steam-heavy sleep of Hollow produces **resonance**, infrasonic harmonics that the sleeping body detects through the mineral-crusted skin. Residents report dreams of vast depths, of eyeless movement through corridors they do not recognize, of loading ingots onto railcars that extend infinitely in both directions. They wake with the sense that others dreamed adjacent, contiguous, the same spaces from different positions. They rarely discuss the content. The feeling of adjacency persists into waking, a knowledge of who among the 847 is currently sleeping, currently dreaming, currently **approaching** something in the depths that the waking mind cannot approach.

## **The Depth-Dweller: What Moves Below**

Rare, almost absent, active. The census category fails.

Call it **the Almost** for convenience, though no inhabitant names it aloud. The Almost has been sighted twice in forty years, in the sense that human eyes have oriented toward it, tracked its movement, retained sufficient coherence to report. More often it is **inferred**: the organic compounds in mineral water that have no surface source, the scratches on metal scored by something that knows the grain of alloy, the sounds that are not the Seep, not the factories, not the railcars—**the breathing that is breath**.

What the two witnesses described: pale, yes, but *polarized*, seeming to reflect no light directly while remaining visible, visible as **absence of darkness** rather than presence of illumination. Eyeless, but not without organs of perception—surface features that might be vibration-sensitivity, chemical-detection, thermal-mapping, distributed across the body in patterns that the witnesses' eyes could not hold still, could not resolve into stable configuration. Moving in temperatures that would scald human skin, and moving **with comfort**, with something the witnesses hesitated to call *pleasure* but could not distinguish from it—the particular ease of something in its proper element.

Size uncertain. The witnesses agree on "larger than human" but diverge on scale: one estimated two meters, the other "room-sized, corridor-sized, *space-filling*." Both reports were filed under administrative protest; the census-taker who recorded them added a note, unusual in its subjectivity, stating that both witnesses appeared **relieved** by the encounter, that their subsequent work performance improved, that they requested depthwork assignments more frequently than rotation protocols permitted.

The Almost does not appear to hunt. Does not appear to feed, in any biological sense. It **circulates**, moving through the deepest accessible levels, occasionally pressing upward into zones where humans work and sleep, then retreating. Its traces suggest **pattern**: not random wandering but route, repetition, **maintenance of something** invisible to other observers. The organic compounds it introduces to the mineral water—complex proteins, lipid structures that *resemble* cell membranes without quite achieving cellular organization—persist for hours, then degrade into constituents indistinguishable from the Seep's normal chemistry. Purpose unclear. Function unclear. Whether the Almost produces these compounds, collects them, **transmits** them, remains undetermined.

The depthworks crews who rotate frequently—who are **well-paid**—do not cite danger in their departure. They cite **completeness**, the sense that they have learned what the depths can teach them, that further exposure would be not harmful but **redundant**. The crews who remain longest, who resist rotation despite administrative pressure, develop particular symptoms: they begin to read the steam vents with precision approaching prediction, they develop preferences for specific depth levels that they cannot justify, they report dreams of the Almost with the same relief the witnesses reported, the same **improvement**. The longest-serving depthworker, one "M. Voss" in census records that span 94 years without visible aging, was finally removed by exterior medical intervention after collapsing during a surface rotation—collapsing not from exhaustion but from **wrong-environment syndrome**, the body's systems failing to adapt to conditions that should have been less stressful than those maintained habitually. Voss died in transit to exterior hospital, body temperature 42°C, mineral crust thickness 3cm across 80% of skin surface, final words recorded as "it was showing me the *next* level" before telemetry failed.

The Almost may be singular or plural. The census has not determined. The inhabitants do not pursue this determination. Something in their collective adaptation—the work without product, the sleep without true isolation, the time without coherent duration—**prepares** for the Almost, or **responds** to it, or **merges at edges** that no interview protocol can map.

## **The City As Organism: What Hollow Itself Becomes**

Not alive, not by standard definition. Yet **organism** persists as the appropriate frame, the necessary misnomer.

Hollow grows. The lithic fungi are structural element, not decoration—mineral accretion that **rebuilds** as it encrusts, that preserves form while transforming material. Buildings in states of simultaneous construction and ruin: this is not paradox but **process**, the city maintaining its urban infrastructure through accretive replacement rather than repair. The factories that run without clear product, the railcars that load ingots for destinations unknown—these are **metabolic function**, energy through material without the telos of production. The city eats the Seep, transforms it, excretes alloy and steam and the conditions that permit Almost-circulation. The city is **digestive system**, **respiratory system**, **nervous system** distributed across mineral substrate and human inhabitants and whatever the Almost contributes to the circuit.

The 0.0 density is not emptiness. It is **lung capacity**, room for expansion, for inflation, for the phase that has not arrived. The city was built for 400,000 and holds 847 and **waits**, maintaining the infrastructure at operational scale, producing the mysterious ingots, loading the railcars, because **capacity must be preserved**. The next phase requires this. Something requires this. The Seep's seventy-year cycles approach culmination—have been approaching, the inhabitants sense, for longer than their coherent memory extends—and when the pressure releases, when the superheated mineral floods what the depthworks cannot contain, the city will **become** something that requires the maintained infrastructure, the produced alloys, the railcars already running on tracks that extend to where instruments fail.

The city remembers. The mineral accretions, layer on layer, record passage in chemistry that can be read, that **is read** by the Almost, by the long-term inhabitants whose skin-crust becomes legible archive, by the Seep itself whose composition shifts responsive to conditions no surface instrument measures. Hollow is not *historic* in the human sense—no preserved documents, no maintained heritage, the official names seventeen and disputed because **naming stabilizes**, and stabilization is not the city's mode. It is **geologic memory**, deep-time storage, the layered record of what has passed through and what has emerged and what **prepares to emerge**.

The structural anomalies at the basin's center, where survey teams report forms their eyes cannot process: this is not malfunction. This is **developmental stage**. The city extends there, has always extended there, the railcars running toward a destination that is not terminal but **transformational**, where ingots become rails become **something that will connect**, something that will permit the next phase's geometry. The survey teams who return describe, when they can describe anything, **potential space**—not empty but holding pattern, not absent but **not-yet-present**.

## **The Integration: What Lives, Finally**

Not the 847. Not the Almost. Not the city itself, if we insist on life as cellular, as metabolic in the biological frame.

**Something that uses all three.**

The inhabitants work without product because **the work is the product**, the maintained attention, the distributed sensorium of 847 human nervous systems reading steam vents and color shifts and fungal bioluminescence, their dreams resonating through infrasleep, their mineral crusts recording conditions their conscious minds cannot process. They are **peripheral nervous system**, extensions of something that requires biological processing, that uses the human capacity for **pattern recognition without comprehension**, for adaptation to incoherence, for continuing without knowing why.

The Almost circulates, introduces its organic compounds, its eyeless presence **relieving** those who encounter it, **improving** their function, because it is **maintenance crew**, immune system element, the mobile agent that adjusts conditions the fixed infrastructure cannot reach. It is not predator, not competitor, not alien visitation. It is **component**, as the inhabitants are component, as the city is substrate and process and **becoming**.

What emerges, what lives in Hollow, is **distributed personhood without center**, a self that extends across human, mineral, biological-analogue, urban-geological, that thinks through steam-pressure and alloy-composition and dream-resonance, that **requires** the 0.0 density as lung-waiting-for-breath, that has been preparing for phase-transition longer than any component's coherent memory, that **does not know itself as self** because its selfhood is not organized around the continuous identity that biological individuals evolve.

The next phase, when the Seep cycle culminates: not apocalypse, not revelation. **Extension**. The capacity maintained will be utilized, the railcars will reach processable destination, the 400,000-structured infrastructure will receive its population—not by arrival from outside but **by differentiation from within**, the 847 becoming sufficient through transformation that the census cannot record, the Almost becoming multiple or becoming visible or becoming **unnecessary as separate component**, the city itself achieving the coherence it has maintained in potential.

They do not leave because **they are already integrated**, because departure would be amputation, because the exterior world offers no equivalent to the distributed selfhood they have become. They do not ask what the machines make because **the asking would require individuation** they no longer possess, the single human demanding explanation from system that answers only in process. They express confusion about duration because **duration is not their measure**, because they have participated in the city's time-incoherence, because the self that would say "I have been here 180 years" and the self that would say "I arrived recently" are **both accurate at different resolutions**, different depths of the distributed personhood.

The lithic fungi pulse. The factories hum. The steam rises.

And something **almost alive, almost aware, almost coherent** continues its slow emergence in the dim light, in the mineral humidity, in the empty city that is not empty, that has never been empty, that holds in reserve the **potential space** where itself becomes finally, eventually, after sufficient preparation, **itself**.
