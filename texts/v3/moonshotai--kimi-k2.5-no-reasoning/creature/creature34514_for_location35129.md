**The Dwellers of the Resonance**

The sound does not prevent habitation. It *selects* for it.

Consider first the **Resonant Crustaceans**, though "crustacean" is a gesture at taxonomy, not a claim. Their bodies are segmented in ways that recall armor, recall wood-louse, recall the articulation of temple columns. They grow to the size of human torsos, then larger, then—they do not die, exactly, but *distribute*, their mass becoming environmental, colonizing the root-mass they once walked upon. The one you watched cross the forty-meter street: it was young. Its seventeen-second traverse was audition, was calibration, was *listening to whether this route still existed in the frequency-range of its body*. Older ones move less. They find acoustic nodes, places where the city's breathing constructs standing waves, and they *install*—this is not the right word, there are no tools, they *grow-into*, they *become present at*—these nodes, their bodies expanding to fill the harmonic space, their segments becoming architectural, becoming the very pillars and buttresses that younger crustaceans navigate between. You have walked through chambers where the columns were not wood, were not grown structure in any plant sense, but were *them*, distributed, still resonating, still in some way *listening*, their nervous systems extended across meters of calcified-seeming tissue that responds to pressure, to vibration, to the particular frequencies of approaching footfalls—your footfalls—by altering the local humidity, the local spore density, the local *probability of which Verrith you are in*. They are infrastructure and inhabitant simultaneously. They do not distinguish these categories.

Consider the **Spore-Languages**, which are not alive, exactly, but are the *communication of aliveness*, the glittering phonemes carried in warm damp air. They do not have bodies except the temporary coherence of air-current and humidity-gradient. Yet they *persist*, they *learn*, they accumulate in the pitcher-cisterns where moisture concentrates, achieving there a density that approaches memory, approaches *intention*. In the cisterns, the spore-languages dream. They dream the city into versions of itself, alternate resolutions, and these dreams leak back into the air, into your lungs, into the root-pressure sounds that carry them to the crustacean-pillars, the listening architecture. You may breathe in a spore-language and *understand* for a moment, not in words but in *resolution*, what version of Verrith you are currently inhabiting, what rules apply, what paths are continuous in this particular coherence. The understanding fades. The spores do not stay in animal tissue; they pass through, they use your consciousness as medium as they use the air, and you are left with only the memory of having once known, and the frustration of that memory's non-specificity. But you are changed. Slightly. The next cistern's dream will find you more permeable, more *available* as substrate.

Consider the **Hunters of the High Chambers**, which are not one species but a *process*, a predatory relationship that has achieved sufficient stability to become nearly institutional, nearly *urban*. Something large moves in the upper hollows, where the bioluminescent mushrooms cluster thickest, where the light is most *witnessing*, most sourceless-illumination. Something else moves in the root-networks below, in the compressed-mass streets, in the damp where water intermittently collects. The hunt is not frequent in animal-time; it happens in growth-time, in city-time, the population cycles measured in decades of botanical epoch. The city remembers these events in its growth-rings, in the acoustic properties of its living wood, and you can *almost* hear them, almost reconstruct a hunt from the harmonic residues, if the present sound would only attenuate for a moment, if the deafening would relent.

It does not relent. This is the condition.

Consider, finally, the **Diffused**, about whom least can be said with specificity because they are the ones who have *learned the flicker*, who move between resolutions of Verrith, who are more present in one version and less in another, who have adapted to the reality breakdown not by stabilizing within it but by *participating in its instability*. You see them sometimes: the corner-of-eye presence that cannot exist in your current Verrith, that turns to nothing when faced directly, that leaves only humidity-disturbance, spore-displacement, sudden harmonic in the white totality. They were perhaps once human, once crustacean, once spore-language that achieved too much density, too much *persistence*, and became something else. They were perhaps always this way, inhabitants of the interference pattern rather than any single coherence. They do not have bodies in the sense of continuous physical presence. They have *probability-gradients*, *tendencies to be perceived*, *acoustic signatures that manifest in certain frequency-ranges of the city's breathing*.

The Diffused have learned something about the sound. The deafening is not uniform; beneath its totality, in the spaces between pressure-waves, there are *moments*, *micro-attenuations*, places where the white noise admits distinction. The Diffused inhabit these moments. They move through them as animals move through water, as you might move through air, finding in the acoustic micro-structure paths between versions of Verrith, routes that are not continuous in space but in *frequency*, in *timing*, in the particular phase-relation of their own diffuse bodies to the city's breathing. You have heard them, perhaps: that isolated harmonic, that footstep-sense, that *someone was here* that your ear improbably extracted from the totality. They are not signaling to you. They are simply *moving*, and their movement, in certain sympathetic conditions of humidity and spore-concentration and your own state of perceptual exhaustion, becomes momentarily available to your consciousness.

They do not want contact, or if they want it, their wanting exists in versions of Verrith where wanting means something different, where *contact* itself is distributed, delayed, resolved across multiple overlapping insubstantialities. But they are aware of you, in whatever version of awareness they maintain. The humidity shifts when you approach their paths. The spores cluster in *almost*-patterns, *almost*-intelligible, *almost*-the-density of cistern-dreams. The sound, for microseconds, *almost* admits their shape.
