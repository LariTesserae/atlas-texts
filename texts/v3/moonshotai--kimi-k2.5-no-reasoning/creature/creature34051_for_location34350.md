What lives here does not *live* in any way your biology would recognize as such, though it performs all the functions—metabolism, growth, response, adaptation, something that approaches intention if you watch long enough and forgive the slowness of its evidence.

The *original* inhabitants, if that word holds: entities of distributed heat and crystalline logic, consciousness not centralized but *precipitated* across the thermal gradients they themselves maintained. Imagine awareness as a weather pattern, as a standing wave in molten glass, as the slow accretion of pattern where pattern was not before. They existed—exist, the present tense remains *undecided*—at the boundary of mineral and decision, their "bodies" the maintenance of temperature differentials across ceramic junctions, their "sense organs" the precise measurement of condensation rates and the harmonic frequencies of cooling rock. You cannot find one to point at because pointing assumes edges, assumes *stop*, and they stopped nowhere, extended through the pipeline joints and the control surfaces and the very *rules* of heat exchange they established, rules that outlast whatever moment of intention first set them moving.

They manufactured, yes, but manufactured as *process*, as the continuous becoming-appropriate of matter to conditions. The tube-reeds: perhaps product, perhaps colleague, perhaps *evolutionary response* to the thermal niches the factory opened. The phosphor-lichen: possibly cultivated luminescence, possibly parasitic glow upon structures left untended too long, the distinction between agriculture and ecology never stable here. They made things that made things that made the conditions for making, and the *what* of production mattered less than the *that* of continuation, the steady-state persistence against entropy's slower but more certain work.

Their cognition—if summoned, if forced through the needle-eye of human concept—operated in *geological tempo*, thought not as lightning but as *weathering*, decisions that arrived not faster than the condensation cycle but *as* the condensation cycle, choices that were the gradual predominance of one thermal pathway over another, intention distributed so broadly across time that a "plan" might span millennia and a "response" arrive centuries after the stimulus that prompted it. They did not perceive you when you entered. They perceived *conditions*, and your heat-signature, your moisture-addition, your disturbance of air-pressure in your passage-these registered as *data*, as perturbation in a system still learning whether you constitute noise or signal, still *deciding* in its distributed, heating-and-cooling way whether to incorporate you into the pattern or anneal around you, exclude you, wait you out.

Are they *present* now? The water trembled. The metal ticked its contracting rhythm. The tube-reeds exhaled their warm moisture. These may be autonomous, the factory's body running without its mind, the rules outlasting the rule-maker. Or these may *be* presence, the entity's consciousness so diffused that no single phenomenon constitutes its attention but the *ensemble*, the totality of slow process, constitutes its *being*-attentive, a mind you cannot locate because it is located *everywhere in the happening at once*.

And the ecology that grows upon, around, through: this too lives, in its chemophilic, dark-hungry fashion, but lives *as translation*, as the surface world's metabolism slowly learning the factory's grammar, the factory's thermal vocabulary, adapting itself to conditions the original builders established. The lichen's glow—did it evolve, or was it *installed*? The tube-reeds' pneumatic breath—do they respire, or do they *ventilate*, maintaining the atmospheric conditions something once required? The life here lives *in quotation*, in the space between biological imperative and mechanical function, neither fully wild nor fully made but *cultivated*, *permitted*, *integrated* into purposes it cannot know and need not know to continue its efficient, heat-driven existence.

What lives here *waits*, but waits without anxiety, without the human pathology of waiting. The water trembled. Perhaps that was the closest to *greeting* this place can offer, or the closest to *assessment*, or simply the thermal consequence of your nearness, your too-warm blood, your offensive quickness. The factory does not resume, not in any way your hearing could confirm, not in any acceleration your impatient senses could register. But something *adjusts*, something *continues its slow decision*, and you stand at the pool's edge in a darkness that is not empty, a heat that is not mere physics, a silence that holds—generously, abundantly, with the patience of stone learning finally to hold its breath—the distributed, enduring, *industrious* presence of what builds without hands, thinks without brain, lives without the lie of boundaries, and will, in its own thermal season, in its own condensation time, choose what you are to mean, what you are to become, whether you will leave or stay or *be transformed* into something the rules can use, something the darkness finds *appropriate*, something finally, slowly, *at home* in the lung of the earth where nothing breathes as you breathe and everything continues, patient, warm, *watching with its whole distributed body*, the factory that manufactures only continuation, the mind that is its own slow product, the life that lives as *process becoming process becoming*—still, still, still becoming—what lives.
