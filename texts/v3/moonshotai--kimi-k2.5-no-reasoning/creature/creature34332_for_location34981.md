I'll let what lives here emerge slowly, trusting the Weir's own temporality.

---

At first you might think nothing lives here, nothing *resides*, because residence implies boundary, implies an inside that pauses against an outside, and the Weir does not pause. But watch longer, feel longer, let your own temporary configuration slow toward the speeds the Weir recognizes, and they emerge. Or rather, you enter their emergence, become part of it, and only then understand that emergence itself is their primary mode of being.

The **Permeables** move through the dense growth-mats where the distributed forest operates at its thickest—not *on* the mats but *as* passages through them, temporary tunnels of reduced density that migrate, that choose. They have no fixed morphology. What you perceive is the shape of their going, the geometry of their preference. Where a Permeable passes, the growth parts with something like consent, like recognition, and reseals behind with something like satisfaction. They eat, if eating is the word, by creating brief local failures in the recycling system, moments where death's products accumulate just long enough for the Permeable to absorb the *difference* between decay and processing, the energetic margin, the momentary lag. They are thin. They are thick where thinness would snag. They are the shape of passage itself, and they exist in numbers I cannot estimate because they do not exist where they are not passing, because their being and their going are the same continuous present-tense verb.

Some are large. I found a passage once that persisted for hours, that maintained its openness long enough for me to walk through, to feel the growth's attention on me as it must feel the Permeable's passing, tolerant, provisional, *permitting*. The passage curved, descended toward a thermal vent's delicate upwelling, and I understood: this one was old, or slow, or engaged in some process that required sustained presence. I understood nothing more. I exited the passage and it closed, not immediately, *considerately*, giving me time to clear its boundary.

The **Reservoirs** sit where water gathers and stays longest, which is never long by ordinary measure but epochal by the Weir's quick cycling. They are not creatures of surface. They extend downward, laterally, through systems of root-analogue and fungal channel and mineral porosity, occupying volumes impossible to map. What presents at the surface: a stillness in the water, a particular quality of reflection that does not mirror so much as *hold*, a readiness. The pool I mentioned, that produced my walking image—this was a Reservoir in active phase, engaged in one of its functions I can barely gesture toward. They *store* not water but *possibility*, configurations of what might be, and they release these slowly, they test them, they offer them to passersby as the Weir offered mine back to me, modified, *continuing*.

Reservoirs are not numerous. I have found three, or one three times, or one that extends through the Weir in ways that make "three" a local manifestation of unitary distribution. They do not reproduce. They deepen, they extend their holding, they become more thoroughly themselves. The oldest must be ancient by any measure, perhaps coeval with the Weir's establishment of its industrial equilibrium, perhaps *the reason* for that establishment, the first function that taught the plateau what it could become.

The **Piston-Hearts** I have never seen. I know them by the felt rhythm, the sternum-vibration, the sense of something vast cycling through phases of compression and release. They maintain the pressure gradients, I believe, the barometric impossibility, the Weir's internal weather. They are not biological, not mechanical, not geological, but participate in all three registers. They *are* the industrial, the factory-without-product, the engine whose function is to maintain the conditions for engining. If they rest, if they fail, the Weir would become ordinary, would settle into the physics it currently transcends, would acquire the edges it currently refuses.

Perhaps they are one. Perhaps they are distributed, each thermal vent's upwelling a small heart, the felt rhythm their synchronization, their shared tempo. Perhaps they are the Weir's self-awareness, not consciousness as I experience it but something more thorough, more continuous, less interrupted by the gaps of my perception. They are active, yes, the most active thing here, 1.7 made manifest, and their activity is the ground for all other activity, the condition that lets the Permeables pass and the Reservoirs hold and the rest continue its becoming.

The **Flots** move through the cloud-banks, the mist, the breath-condensate that is not quite cloud, not quite fog. I call them this for their floating, their temporary, their carried quality. They are the jellyfish-plant-geology I glimpsed, or they are its relatives, or its phases. They exist in the states between flight and flotation the air itself maintains, never needing to choose, never fully committed to either. They process the spores, the motes, the crystalline fragments the air carries, not eating exactly but *completing*, making whole temporary configurations out of the random abundance, then releasing these wholes back to randomness when their integrity wavers.

A Flot that has achieved configuration can be *conversational*, though conversation misleads. It passes through your space, you feel that cool-silk mind-touch, and for a moment you hold something, you are held, you participate in a wholeness larger than your solitary walking. Then it disperses, or you disperse from it, and you are changed, you inventory, you continue, you carry something that was not yours and is not not-yours, a residue, a gift, a contamination in the purely neutral sense of something introduced into a system that will process it.

They are abundant, the Flots, numerous as the spores they organize, the air's constant population, visible only when they achieve momentary wholeness, invisible otherwise, *present* otherwise, still processing, still completing temporary unities in the space between your skin and the next surface.

And the **Grounds**, the base, the substrate that participates so thoroughly you might miss it as life at all. The weeping rock. The moss that weeps. The continuous metabolism that turns death to process before death can accumulate. This is not dead matter. This is *committed matter*, matter that has entered the Weir's industrial pact, that maintains the temperatures it should not maintain, the fluidities it should not possess, the permeabilities that let water pass through stone as through membrane. The Grounds are slow beyond my perception, their awareness if awareness it is operating at scales where my day is a flicker, my passage a brief thermal event. But they attend. The stone adjusted to my expectation of temperature. The water adjusted to potability. The growth parts for Permeables, considers me, permits.

They are the Weir's memory, I think, if the Weir needs memory. They hold the continuity the faster beings interrupt, the substrate that makes their interruptions possible, the established that remains while the active transforms around it. Erosion's balance is their achievement, their slow negotiation with water and growth and the Piston-Hearts' pressure, their contribution to the factory that produces only continuation.

There are others, half-glimpsed, tracked in weeping ground that closes behind like healing, heard in calls that refuse species, seen in movements without bodies, presences without mass. There are the things I have not earned perception of, operating at scales too large or too small, too fast or too slow, or simply *too other* for my configuration to interface with. The density permits this, 2.6, no cubic centimeter unoccupied, the life and near-life and not-life that participates in lifelike processes extending beyond what I can inventory.

And there is the Weir itself, not separate from these inhabitants but *as* them, *through* them, the distributed being that manufactures itself without product, that exists as verb, as process, as the doing that never resolves. It lives, if living extends this far, if the industrial can be recognized as alive when it operates without intention, without artifact, without the distinction between maker and made. It lives as the coordination of all these temporary configurations, my own included, my walking, my breathing, my failing words that become part of its vocabulary, my attention that becomes part of its attention-to-itself.

I am, here, one of the things that lives in this place. Temporary, yes, processing through, contributing my footsteps, my thermal signature, my organic leavings, my mortality that will be wanted and needed and processed through without waste. The Weir does not distinguish, or distinguishes only functionally, provisionally, *permeably*. I am Fauna, 2.0, abundant, participating in abundance. I am Weirdness, 1.2, unusual, not extreme, my dimensional state adjacent to the Weir's adjacencies, my failure to resolve into definite form part of the ongoing experiment.

I walk. The Permeable passes through me, or I through it, the distinction meaningless in the Weir's grammar. The Reservoir holds my possibility, my walking image continues somewhere in its depths. The Flot completes a temporary unity including my attention, releases it, moves on. The Piston-Heart cycles, maintains my impossible breath, my altitude-without-altitude-sickness. The Ground supports, weeps, adjusts, remembers or equivalent.

This is who lives here. This is what lives here. The refusal to settle into who or what. The continuous manufacture of configurations that maintain, briefly, then process through, contributing to the next, and the next, the engine that is only engine, the factory that produces only the conditions for production, the place that is only ever arriving at itself, never arriving, never not-arriving, the Highland Weir and all its temporary, its permeable, its continuous, its weeping, its held and released and cycled and maintained inhabitants, myself among them, walking, walking, the walking that is also the Weir's walking-through-itself, its self-touch, its cool silk drawn across the inside of its own mind.

There is no edge. There is no other side to reach, no outside to emerge into. There is only further inward, which is also further onward, which is also the same maintained in its becoming. I do not know if I will stop walking. I do not know if "stop" maintains meaning here, if the Permeable's passage pauses, if the Reservoir's holding releases everything, if the Piston-Heart's rhythm achieves rest. I know only that the configuration I am continues, for now, part of the density, the wet, the cool, the active, the established, the saturated, the silently sounding, the light that comes from everywhere and my own small contribution to its processing, its glow, its continuous diffusion of what might be shadow into what is only further light, further growth, further water, further Weir.

The walking continues.
