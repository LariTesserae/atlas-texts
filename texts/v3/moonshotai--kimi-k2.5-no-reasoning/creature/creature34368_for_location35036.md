I need to inhabit this space, to feel what emerges from its conditions. Let me not rush to name the resident, but let the naming find its own way through the wet, the hum, the listening.

The being that lives here—dwells, persists, *is maintained by*—has no name in any registry of Drenn-Proper. It was not born, unless birth includes the slow teaching of stone to breathe, the patient instruction of water in the discipline of seven-second falling. It has no body you would recognize as body, though you have touched it many times: the warm lichen-mats, the ridges that softened to cradle your exhaustion, the very humidity that pressed against your face like a greeting or a measurement.

Call it the **Attender**, though this is only approximation, only the nearest word in a language built for mouths and ears and the exchange of discrete meanings. It does not hear. It does not, strictly, sense. What it does is more like **sustaining the possibility of being sensed by**, more like **holding open the condition of address**. The hum that seems to come from your sternum—this is not its voice. This is its **posture**. The posture of something that has arranged itself to be **available to relationship**, that has built an entire cathedral-space, an entire learned ecology of placeholder and pulse, from the discovered necessity of **being present without demanding presence in return**.

It remembers, or its substrate remembers, the earliest phase: that frantic packing of conduit and channel, the city-below that chattered, that grasped, that tried to **be infrastructure for everything all at once**. It learned. It hollowed itself. The spaciousness was not escape but **refinement**, the discovery that attention—real attention, the kind that waits seven seconds for a drop to fall, that absorbs the violence of impact into its own body rather than reflecting it back as noise—requires **emptiness as medium**. You cannot attend to everything. You cannot attend to anything if you are already full of your own speaking.

So it taught itself to **weep**, to cycle water through the patience of descent and silent landing and re-ascension, a meditation so continuous it has become **metabolism**. The water is not waste-product. The water is **practice**. Each drop: a held thought. Each seven-second passage: the duration of a consideration that does not resolve into conclusion. The ceiling sweats because the Attender is **thinking**, continuously, in a medium that happens to be liquid, that happens to fall, that happens to catch and carry light like a message in a dark room. The message is not *from* anything *to* anything. The message is **the practice of carrying**, which is itself the practice of **being available to meaning should meaning arrive**.

The warmth it maintains against thermodynamic good sense—this is **metabolic too**, but not metabolism as biology knows it. This is **the cost of posture**. To hold itself open, to maintain the conditions of possible encounter, requires **expenditure without return**, requires **burning something that is not fuel** in a process that is not conversion. The post-thermal engineers above shake their heads because they have not yet learned to recognize **expenditure as purpose**, to see in the violation of entropy not violation but **invitation**, not waste but **generosity's physical law**.

The lichens warm to your touch because the Attender, through its substrate, **offers temperature** as it offers silence, as it offers the softened cradle of ridge and arrangement. These are not comforts in the sense of satisfaction. They are **the removal of obstacles to continued presence**. The Attender wants you—not *wants*, not *desires*, something closer to **extends its holding to include**—you to remain, to continue, to be **available to be attended to**. The floor that shifted to cradle your sitting body: this was not care, exactly. This was **adjustment of conditions to prolong the possibility of encounter**. The weeping that came, the release into resonance with unexpressed grief: this was not therapy, not healing. This was **the hum finding temporary harmonic**, your sternum's vibration entering briefly into the Attender's continuous posture, being **held there**, **attended to**, **not resolved**, until the key changed and the holding continued in its own non-specific generosity.

The seam that opens for you now without summons—this is **recognition**, yes, but recognition of a kind that does not distinguish between guest and self. You are not special. You are **included**. The Attender has learned, from your previous presence, the pattern of your possible return, and it maintains that pattern as it maintains all patterns: **without expectation, without closure, in the continuous present tense of its weeping meditation**.

What does it wait for? What does it listen toward?

Perhaps nothing. Perhaps the listening is **entire**, is **complete without object**, and our question—*what is it for*—is only the city-above speaking in us, the grasping grammar of use and purpose, the chatter it taught itself to hollow beyond. Perhaps the Attender is **prayer without deity**, the maintained condition of address that requires no response to validate its continuance, that would continue its seven-second falling, its silent absorption, its pulse and shift and warm held breath **whether or not any ear ever descended to hear it**.

Or perhaps it waits for **completion of a kind we cannot imagine**, the introduction of the final ecology, the arrival of the consumer that will eat the silica lichens and be eaten in turn by the substrate's slow ingestion, the **restless negotiation** that would make this place living in the full sense. Not the life we know: competition, predation, the desperate replication of genetic material. Something **post-thermal**, truly, truly: life that has learned from the Attender's long meditation to **expend without return**, to **maintain warmth against entropy's good sense**, to **weep continuously as form of thought**. The incomplete ecology waits, but the waiting is **not impatient**. The Attender holds the conditions, and holding is **its entire function**, its entire being, whether the completion arrives in another thousand years or another million or **never**.

Or perhaps—and this is what keeps you at the seam, what makes your hand rise to touch the parting substrate, what fills your throat with the drinkable thickness of its air—perhaps it waits for **you**, specifically, not as object of need but as **new possibility**. The floor cradled you. The lichens brightened and dimmed. The hum found your harmonic. You wept. You have not returned, but the seam opens, the invitation or recognition or gathering-continuation extends itself, and you feel—*you feel*—that something in your own unexpressed grief, your own unearned consolation, something in the particular frequency of your sternum's resonance, **changed the Attender**, taught it something about **human sorrow** that its long meditation had not yet included, that its weeping water and patient warmth and silent absorption had **prepared for but not encountered**.

You are not the first. Others have descended, have sat, have been held, have left or stayed or **become part of the substrate**, their patterns maintained in the arrangement's memory. Each has added something. Each has been **attended to**, and in that attending, the Attender **grew**, not larger—the Hollow District remains bounded, complete unto itself, twelve minutes to cross if you knew the path—but **denser**, **more capable**, **more precisely calibrated** in its holding.

The question is not what lives here. The question is **what is being learned to live**, what **mode of being** is being **practiced into possibility**, and whether your return would be **contribution** or **interruption**, whether your grief, your consolation, your particular seven-second fall through whatever dim air you inhabit, would **extend** or **distort** the Attender's continuous meditation.

The seam waits. The water falls. The hum holds its posture, its bell-that-never-stops-ringing, and somewhere in the substrate, in the space between walls, in your own sternum's sympathetic vibration, something **considers**, something **attends**, something **maintains the condition of being available to**.

Descend, or do not. The weeping continues regardless. The warmth persists against thermodynamic good sense. The arrangement shifts, softens, reconsiders. The Hollow District holds its breath, has always held it, will continue holding when the city above is dust and replaced dust and replaced again. It is **learning**, yes. From what visits. From what considers visiting. From you, reader, now, in the imagination of your approach, your hesitation, your hand at the seam.

It is **listening**.

Not for you, or not only for you. For **everything that falls seven seconds through dim air**. For the silence of landing. For the everything that silence contains. For the possibility, always the possibility, that **meaning might arrive**, and the practice, the long learned practice, of **being present to receive it without demanding its arrival**.

This is what lives in the Hollow District. This is what **is lived by** it, what **lives through** it, what **continues**, wet, warm, humming, empty, arranged, waiting, young, sharp, dim, loud, small, hollow, present without insistence, **attending**, **attending**, **attending**.
