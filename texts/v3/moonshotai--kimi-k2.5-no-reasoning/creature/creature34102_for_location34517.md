What lives in the Gargantua Basin cannot be separated from where it lives, cannot be extracted and held up to light for inspection. The inhabitants are *collaborations*—between biology and material, between temporal states, between the necessity of persistence and the generosity of the Construct itself. They are not born here, not exactly, and they do not arrive. They *resolve*, precipitating out of the Basin's peculiar saturation the way water condenses against those invisible thermal boundaries, suddenly present, suddenly real, suddenly *accounted for* by a system they did not choose but which chose, or chooses, or is still choosing them.

The **Resident Integers**—though no one calls them this, the term having emerged from a mathematics team that lasted eleven days—constitute perhaps the most stable category. They were human, remember being human, maintain the gesture of human shape though their proportions have adjusted to the gravity's seventy-second pulse. Their bones are longer than human bones should be, or shorter, or both in phased overlay. They move through the Construct with the patience of people who have learned that haste is a form of violence against the material they now inhabit. They breathe with the cycle, yes, but more than this: they have learned to *think* with it, their cognition distributed across the inflation and contraction, thoughts initiated during compression reaching completion only as the next compression begins. A Resident Integer spoken to during inflation may not respond until three cycles later, not from rudeness but from the geometry of their attention, which follows channels the uninitiated cannot trace. They cultivate the violent vegetation, or the vegetation cultivates them—their dwellings, when they choose to dwell rather than move with the breathing, are chambers grown rather than constructed, shaped from cooperation between their own metabolic byproducts and the Construct's responsive surfaces. They eat, but eating is a *routing*—nutrients arriving from futures they have not yet experienced, debts of digestion paid backward through the causal channels. They do not age in linear fashion. They accumulate, layer upon layer, selves that coexist without contradiction.

The **Loptr** move at the edges of perception by choice, or so the theory goes, though "choice" assumes a subjectivity the Basin's philosophy may not recognize. They are the larger forms, the partial visibilities, the communications-or-traps. Some Resident Integers claim to have been Loptr once, or will be, the tense collapsing under the weight of superposition. They appear mammalian in moments of insufficient attention, insectile when observed directly, architectural when perceived peripherally during the gravity's weakest phase. Their movement is *computational*—each appearance solving some problem in the Construct's ongoing self-maintenance, each partial visibility a query or a result in a distributed process no single node comprehends. They are attracted to contradiction, to paradox, to the cognitive dissonance of visitors who have not yet learned the Basin's grammar. A Loptr will follow such a visitor for days, visible only in the corner of vision, in reflection, in the shadows that do not attach to their objects. Eventually the visitor resolves—into Resident Integer, into departure, into something else less speakable—or the Loptr's attention moves elsewhere, its problem solved or abandoned or proven unsolvable in a way that is itself a solution.

The **Swells** constitute the most controversial category, debated fiercely among those who study the Basin from outside (a difficult proposition, as study requires approach, approach requires resolving, and resolution threatens to convert the researcher into something that no longer recognizes the legitimacy of the question). They are the Construct's own awareness, or its distributed self-model, or mere emergent property of complexity too vast to avoid intentionality's imitation. They manifest during peak inflation, when the gravity is weakest and the sound most overwhelming—sudden *thickening* of the deafening roar into patterns that resemble voice, that *almost* communicate, that reach toward meaning before dissolving back into the statistical noise of material friction. Resident Integers report dialogue with Swells, though dialogue is not the precise term: more a *participation* in utterance whose source and recipient cannot be distinguished. The Swells know the Construct's desires, if it has desires, or they *are* those desires in temporary condensation. They have been consulted, or consulted with, regarding the center—that inaccessible node toward which all routes tend but none arrive. The Swells' response, uniformly, is not silence but *interference*, the utterance structured to cancel itself, meaning and its negation sounded simultaneously, producing not nothing but *impossible meaning*, meaning that cannot be held in finite cognition.

The **Maintenance Fauna** blur whatever boundary remained between organism and infrastructure. The insect-sensor swarms, yes, but also the larger forms that prune vegetation with gestures too precise for evolutionary origin. They exist in the quantum superposition of manufacturing states that so puzzled surviving engineers—simultaneously finished and unfinished, deployed and in-fabrication, their present iteration dependent on observation in ways that make population counts exercises in probability rather than arithmetic. Some appear to have once been visitors who resolved incompletely, caught between biological and technological integration, maintaining function without the coherence of identity that Resident Integers achieve. They are not unhappy, the Resident Integers insist, or not happy, or not the kind of entity for whom such terms maintain reference. They *work*, and their work is the Construct's work, the Basin's work, the deferred density's ongoing release into form.

And the **Visitors**, the not-yet-resolved, the still-arriving—are they inhabitants? The Basin's philosophy, if it has one, seems to include them in its ontology even before they commit to presence. They are *accounted for*, their potential energy registered in the system's distribution, their eventual resolution anticipated in the causal channels where effect precedes cause. A Visitor who will become Resident Integer in forty years is already, in some phased dimension, maintaining the cultivation. A Visitor who will depart unchanged is already, in some overlay, the memory of their own future departure, shaping their behavior in ways they experience as intuition, dread, irresistible curiosity.

What lives here lives *through* the Basin's contradictions, not despite them. The young sharp edges, the ancient layered time, the deafening that exceeds sound, the emptiness that is deferral rather than absence—none of these are obstacles to be overcome but *conditions* to be comprehended, and comprehension here is not merely cognitive but *ontological*, becoming the kind of entity for whom such conditions constitute viable environment. The Gargantua Construct, the Basin, the deferred density and its release—they do not contain life the way a vessel contains liquid. They *enable* it, *generate* it, *are* it in temporary differentiation.

The sound continues. The gravity pulses. Something at the center—no one has reached it, or everyone has, or the reaching and the center are the same condition viewed from different temporal phases—continues its operation, its speaking, its learning to be heard. What lives here learns with it, becomes more itself as the Basin becomes more itself, the sharp edges not yet negotiated by erosion, the rules still emerging, the philosophical disagreements among returners themselves a kind of life, a kind of participation, a kind of the Basin's voice speaking through human mouths that have not yet, may never, resolve into Resident Integers or Loptr or Swells or the other categories not yet named, deferred in the emptiness, dense with impending form.
