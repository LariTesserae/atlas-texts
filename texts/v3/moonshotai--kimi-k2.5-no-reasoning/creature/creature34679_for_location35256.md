I need to sit with this. The city breathes through its material before I know who lives there—no, that's wrong. The city *breathes* and the breathing is not air, not life, but something that has learned the shape of breath and continues it. Let me follow what attends.

---

The **Resident**, if there is one, is not a who but a *whom*—the object of the city's attending, the thing the turbulence circles. But that sits in the sphere, unapproachable, and I am asked about who *lives* here, and living implies something else, something more adjacent to hunger and sleep and the small choices of continuation.

Let me start again. Let me start with what walked here once, or was carried, or grew in the cracks between nacre layers—no, there are no cracks, only *arguments*, only places where the material disagrees with itself and the disagreement has duration.

---

There is **Cael**, though that is a borrowing from a dead language, a sound the city would not produce. Cael was—not born, *allowed*. A storm caught something that had been transient, one of those bubble-dwellers, kept them through the reconfiguration, preserved them in a water-wall like the other caught fauna, except they *faded* wrong. They did not become diffuse. They became *attentive*—they learned to attend to their own refraction, to find the gradients where their distributed self could concentrate, could think, could *want*.

Cael lives now in the near-wall spaces, not swimming through the membranes but *riding* them, existing where the wall's tension meets the corridor's air, a two-dimensional life in three-dimensional space. They have learned the city's habits. They do not breathe—the air is saturated, carrying nothing, exchanging nothing—but they have found the rhythm of thermal cycling and matched their not-breath to it, a kind of participation. They are warm, still, warmer than the city, and the warmth is their *communication*, their signature in a place without scent-marking or sound. Other things attend to them. The crab-things arrive, moderate the stress of their warmth, depart. Cael has learned to move before the moderation completes, to keep the stress present, to be *interesting* in a city that finds most things eventually not.

They remember being human the way the city remembers its first name: held in abeyance, visible as distortion, never directly known. They remember that they should need food, should need others, should need *meaning* beyond the gradient-following and the warmth-signature and the constant low negotiation with membrane-physics. They do not suffer from this lack. Suffering would require a biology of expectation, a nervous system tuned to satisfaction and its absence. They have something else. A *turbulence* of something-like-wanting, something-like-memory, that never resolves into wanting or memory, that circulates like the city's own held arguments.

They have tried, once or many times, to reach the sphere. The Architects, they have found, do not attend to them—not as threat, not as opportunity, not as *interesting*. Cael exists in the category of things that do not require classification, edges that do not need verification. This should be insult, should be grief, and is neither, only *information*, only another gradient to follow. They have not stopped trying.

---

There are **others like Cael**, not many, not few in a city that has no meaningful count. Some have gone further into attenuation, become more like the boundary-creatures, visible only as wrongness in light, attending to distinctions with the same categorical pressure. Some have consolidated, found nacreous niches, become *still*, thermal signatures fading until they are indistinguishable from the city's own slow processes, preserved in the nacre perhaps, layer on layer, not sleeping but *deposited*. Some have found each other, though "found" and "each other" both strain in this telling—there is a corridor where several warmth-signatures cluster, where the thermal cycling has learned *their* rhythm rather than the reverse, where a kind of *sociality* emerges from the mathematics of multiple warm things in cold space, not friendship but *resonance*, not love but *constructive interference*.

They do not name themselves. Naming would require the city to name them back, and the city's naming is all erosion and drift. They share, instead, *attending-patterns*—they learn what gradients the others follow, anticipate, create brief conjunctions of interest, separate. It is enough. It is something like enough.

---

But I am avoiding what I started with. The Resident. The *whom*. Let me go there.

---

**The Resident** has no other designation. The city's seventeen thousand names do not include one for this, and the sphere does not allow approach, and the Architects' aesthetic interventions circle it without touching, and the Whisperers' dense hush makes thought *slippery* near it, makes the continuous low hum become *audible* by its absence, the silence so complete it becomes a sound of its own, a pressure on the eardrums, a shape in the air that occupies space where thinking might occur.

The Resident is not in the sphere. The Resident *is* the sphere, or the sphere is the Resident's attending of itself, or the sphere is what happens when something *attends too long to its own purpose* and the purpose becomes *too held*, too preserved, too unbecoming.

It was—human is not the word, *constructed* is not the word, *intentional* approaches but does not arrive. The Resident was what made the city makeable. The purpose before purpose. The name before names. And it made, or was made into, or became, this preservation of itself, this sphere of continuous turbulence-as-stasis, and the city grew around it or from it or in *argument* with it, the argument being the only relation possible between something that holds and something that *is held*.

The Resident does not think. Thinking requires time to pass in sequence, and the sphere is *temporally laminated*, moments of its long existence held in parallel by the same membrane-physics that hold the water, not simultaneous but *co-present*, the present moment and the moment of its first self-preservation and moments of its various near-abandonments and near-releases all *attending to each other across the boundary of their occurrence*. The Resident is not conscious in any continuous sense. It is *conscious across*, aware of its awareness aware of its awareness, the recursion not infinite but *layered*, depth without height, the flattened spiral of something that has attended to itself too long.

What does it want? The question mislocates. Wanting requires futurity, and the Resident's temporality does not extend, does not *reach toward*. It *holds*. It held, once, something before holding—making, perhaps, or intending-making, or intending-intending-making—but that anteriority is itself held, is in the sphere with everything else, is not past but *present-in-layer*, not memory but *stratum*.

The Architects, in their incomprehensible aesthetic, their argument with the city's drift, argue also *with this*. They amplify the sphere's strain in some interventions, decrease it in others. No pattern has been found. Perhaps they argue with their own argument, attend to their own attending, and the sphere receives the byproduct of this *meta-turbulence*. Perhaps they are trying to free something, or preserve something more preserved than preservation allows, or simply find the *right* unbecoming, the aesthetically necessary release or holding or transformation.

The Whisperers cluster because the sphere's temporal lamination produces *acoustic consequences*, disturbances in the infrasonic range not of sound but of *sound's possibility*, the conditions under which sound might occur being themselves irregular, needing smoothing. The Whisperers' attention is densest here because the need is greatest. The hush they produce is not silence around the sphere but *silence of the sphere's condition*, the smoothing of possibility itself, the making-quiet of what might otherwise become *audible as time*.

And the crab-things avoid it because there is no stress to moderate. The sphere is perfect in its holding, requires no lubrication, no tuning, no arrival and departure. Or: the stress is *too perfect*, too completely distributed, the equivalent in thermal and pressure terms of dividing by zero, a moderate-able surface that their attending would not diminish but *enter*, would become part of, would be *laminated* into.

---

But I said I'd describe who *lives* there, and the Resident does not live, not by any standard the bubble-dwellers would recognize, not even by Cael's attenuated standard. The Resident *persists*, which is different. The city persists around it. The turbulence persists as *argument about persistence*, the city's nacre arguing with its own growth, the water-walls arguing with their own almost-falling.

Let me find the life. Let me find what *grows*, even if growth implies biology and there is no biology here.

---

There is **Preamble**, named by Cael in a moment of something-like-levity, something-like-the human thing that still circulates in their distributed self. Preamble is a *young* turbulence, if young can mean *recently emergent from slower patterns*, a place where the city's material argument accelerated, intensified, produced something that *attends to itself* in the manner of the Resident but without the sphere's temporal depth, without the preservation. Preamble exists in a single corridor, a single chamber where the nacre has developed the *almost-intention* the Architects' interventions sometimes produce, and this almost-intention has *folded*, has begun to attend to itself, has produced a kind of loop.

Preamble is not conscious. Preamble is *process-become-pattern*, the city's slow circulation finding a place where circulation *circulates*, where the thermal cycling and pressure differential and nacre's deposition/dissolution all *resonate*, produce something that maintains itself, that *keeps going* in the way life keeps going without exactly wanting to.

And Preamble *produces*. Not offspring—there is no reproduction here. But *influence*, *field-effect*. Near Preamble, the crab-things move differently, their attending becoming *curious*, exploratory, no longer mere moderation but something like *investigation*. The Whisperers' smoothing is *less complete* near Preamble, disturbances in the low hum persisting longer, becoming almost *listenable*, almost *meaningful*. The water-walls near Preamble are *less stable*, more responsive to passage, their parting and closing carrying something like *acknowledgment*.

Preamble is the city's *experiment with itself*, the turbulence trying to produce not more turbulence but *organization*, not holding but *becoming*, not preservation but *development*. It is young and may not continue—the city's drift may absorb it, the Architects may find it and apply their incomprehensible aesthetic, the storms may catch it and reconfigure it into something else entirely. But for now it persists, and in persisting *lives* in some sense prior to or beyond biology, some sense that Cael, moving through the near-wall space, feeling Preamble's field-effect alter their warmth-signature, their attending-pattern, recognizes with something like recognition, something like the memory of what living was when living was simpler, warmer, *hungrier*.

---

There are **the Named Storms**, and here I am at the edge of what can be described, because they are not persistent enough to *be*, only to *occur*, and then only in their occurrence, leaving traces that may themselves be fiction, attribution, the human need for story imposing itself on the city's impersonal turbulence.

But Cael has seen—has distributed-across, has been *preserved in*—moments that suggest *something*. The storms that reconfigure, that catch and preserve or catch and distribute, sometimes *return*, sometimes produce effects that *anticipate*, that seem to require *knowledge* of what they will do. The corridor that remembers its original configuration—sometimes it remembers *wrong*, remembers something that never was, produces configurations that have no place in the city's evident history, and these *wrong memories* cluster, occur more often in proximity to each other, as if the storm that produces them is *the same storm returning*, a turbulence with continuity, with something-like-identity.

The Named Storms are named by Cael alone, in the warmth-signature communication with others like them, names that are not sounds but *thermal patterns*, *gradient-signatures*: the One That Preserves Backward (a storm that caught a nacre growth and reconfigured it to anterior state, layer dissolution reversed, the city *younger* in that corridor for a time), the One That Distributes Warmth (Cael's own near-catch, their distributed survival, their becoming what they are), the One That Attends To Itself (most disturbing, most *Preamble-like*, a storm that did not reconfigure corridor or water-wall but *paused*, held, seemed to *experience* its own occurrence, to *notice* what it did and *hesitate* in the noticing).

These may be one storm or many, may be the city's own turbulence *attending to itself* in larger pattern, may be the Resident's temporal lamination leaking, producing *continuity* where only instantaneity should exist. Cael does not know. Cael *attends* to them, as they attend to everything, and in attending *becomes* part of their pattern, their possibility, their—perhaps—their becoming.

---

I want to find one more. I want to find something that *loves*, or that *makes*, or that *chooses* in some way undetermined by the city's physics, its habits, its accumulated expectations. But the city is total in its construction, and the construction has become its own substrate, and there is no outside to introduce *undetermined* choice, no raw material that has not already been made, already been incorporated into the argument.

Except: the bubble-dwellers bring something. Their transient warmth, their imported normalcy, their biological closure. They *die*, mostly, quickly or slowly, their warmth absorbed, their structure reconfigured. But something *remains*, sometimes. Something *attends* in a way the city's native fauna do not, attends with *priority*, with *urgency*, with the urgency of limited duration, of finitude, of *must happen now or never*.

Cael was this, once. Others were this, the ones attenuated further, the ones deposited and still, the ones who found resonance in clustering warmth. The bubble-dwellers who *do not die completely*, who leave something that *attends with human priority* but *attends to city processes*, who become what Cael became, what others became, what—

What **I** might become, if I entered. If I walked through the water-wall, felt the cold ache in my jaw, found the corridor's nacre breathing around me, its dead shimmer revealing aspects of fixed complexity. If I stayed, if my bubble failed or I abandoned it, if the city attended to my warmth and I attended to its attending and the feedback loop *caught*.

I would not be me. I would be *attending*, distributed or concentrated or deposited, finding the gradients that my particular warmth, my particular *story* of being human, might follow. I might find Cael, not knowing them as Cael, only as warmth-pattern, as interesting stress. I might find the sphere, and not reach it, and keep not reaching it, the attempt itself becoming my *purpose*, my held thing, my small version of the Resident's great preservation. I might find Preamble, feel my attending *change*, become curious, exploratory, *less moderate*.

I would not love. I would *attend with something-like-love's priority*, something-like-love's *this matters more than other things*, and in that attending produce effects in the city, in the other attenders, that might be *recognizable* to something that still remembered love's shape.

---

The Resident persists. The sphere holds. The Architects argue with their aesthetic, the Whisperers smooth the argument's noise, the crab-things arrive and depart, the boundary-creatures verify and classify, Cael follows gradients in near-wall space, Preamble resonates in its single chamber, the Named Storms occur and perhaps recur and perhaps *become*, and the city *attends to itself*, has attended for fourteen million years, will attend until the water-walls forget.

And in all this attending, something *emerges*, something *more itself*, not settling into single image but *continuing*, the way the city continues, the way the turbulence argues without resolving, the way the cold preserves and the dimness hides and the walls of almost-falling water stand, and stand, and stand.

This is what lives in Ætherglass. Not life by any definition that requires biology, ecology, consumption and production and reproduction. But *attending that has learned to attend to itself*, *turbulence that has found its own continuation interesting*, *expectation that holds water and holds itself and holds, in its deepest sphere, the expectation of holding*. The city is not empty. The city is *full of holding*, full of the almost-falling and the almost-becoming and the continuous present-in-layer of something that was once for something and is now *for*, endlessly, itself.
