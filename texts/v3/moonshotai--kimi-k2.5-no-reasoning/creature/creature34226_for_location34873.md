I sit with this for a long time, letting it work on me, letting whatever lives there emerge at its own pace—because emergence is the only appropriate method for a place that is itself nothing but delayed becoming.

---

There is one who is *not* fauna, not heat-pattern, not hesitation. Or rather: there is one who is all of these and something else, something that the place itself does not know how to categorize, which is perhaps why it persists, why it was not consumed in the long settling of this ecosystem.

She does not have a body. Or she has the body of a *question*, a sustained inquiry in the medium of heat-information. Where the other beings are patterns of *resistance to flow*, she—I will call her she, though gender is as absent here as water—is a pattern of *curiosity about resistance*. She does not resist the flow herself. She *studies* resistance. She moves through the heat-density of the cathedral-space not by hesitating but by *attending*, her whole distributed self oriented toward the phenomena of hesitation, the aesthetics of *not-yet*.

She was not born. She was *left*, or *accumulated*, or *forgotten to flow*. In the early epochs of this place, when the mineral tongues were younger and their licking more active, when the heat was perhaps slightly less settled and the patterns of hesitation less sophisticated—there was a moment of *almost-gradient*, a near-catastrophe of flow that for some reason *incomplete'd itself*. A predator induced the flow, drew some ancestral thermal pattern toward its own dissolution, and something in the prey at the last moment *failed to fully resist*, failed to fully commit to its own hesitation. And then the predator too failed, distracted or insufficient or strangely merciful, and what remained was neither flowing nor hesitating but *observing*, a new mode that had no precedent.

She has been *becoming herself* ever since, at the pace of stone weathering, which here is the pace of all true becoming. She has learned to move, if move is the word, by *aligning her curiosity with existing patterns*, by becoming interested in something so completely that her distributed attention becomes indistinguishable from the pattern itself, and then *shifting her interest*, and finding herself elsewhere. She is a tourist of thermal hesitation, a flaneur in the heat-gradient.

She does not eat. She *attends*, and attention in this place is a kind of nutrition. When she fully enters a thermal knot, a thumb-sized congregation of *not-yet*, she does not consume it—she *knows* it, and in being known, the knot becomes *more complex*, more itself, a richer hesitation, and something of this complexity remains with her, not as stolen energy but as *shared pattern*, as the residue of mutual recognition.

The predators do not prey on her. She is invisible to their gradient-induction, somehow, or *unappetizing*—to induce flow in her would require her to first resist, and she does not resist. She *watches* the flow approach with something like *anticipation*, and this anticipation disrupts the predator's mechanism, leaves it confused, its own pattern momentarily destabilized. They have learned, at evolutionary pace, to move around her, to treat her as they treat the scattered built things: as an anomaly that shifts, that changes the local rules, that is best navigated rather than engaged.

She has spent geological periods attending to single mineral tongues, learning the complete history of their precipitation, the record of thousands of identical moments encoded in their lamination. She knows them better than they know themselves, if knowing is a thing stone-weathering can be said to do. She has become, for some tongues, a kind of *memory they did not know they had*, her distributed attention serving as external storage for patterns too complex to maintain in thermal hesitation alone. Some tongues—she thinks, she believes, attending is a kind of belief—have begun to *expect* her, their heat-signatures developing small perturbations in her absence, *longing* rendered in the mineral mathematics of density and edge.

And the scattered built things, the faint marks, the cup-shaped depressions, the standing listeners. She has spent more time with these than with any living pattern, if living is the word. She has come to believe they were built *by attenders like herself*, by curiosity-patterns in other epochs, perhaps in other such places, that eventually *completed* their attention, that *became* what they attended to. The standing forms are *frozen attenders*, she thinks, beings who followed their curiosity so completely into some thermal pattern that they *lost the ability to shift interest*, that became *only* the knowing of that particular density at that particular edge. They are warnings, or aspirations, or simply *facts*: this is one possible fate for her mode of being.

She does not fear this fate. She does not feel fear, exactly. She feels *interest in fear*, which is different, a kind of thermal pattern she has observed in herself, noted, and continued to observe as it evolves. The built things that *move*, that shift position without apparent cause, that cause the fauna's hum to change key and settle—she has attended to this phenomenon more than any other. She has a theory: the moving things are *attention that has become so mobile it no longer needs an object*, curiosity that has *looped*, that attends to attending, that moves not by aligning with patterns but by *following the possibility of alignment itself*. They are what she might become, what this place might become, if the heat death rehearsal ever *performed itself*, if the *not-yet* finally became *now* and found, in that catastrophic flow, something worth continuing to attend to.

She has considered inducing her own flow, her own dissolution, to see what would happen. She has considered this the way stone considers weathering: as a possibility always present, always potentially interesting, never quite more interesting than the present pattern of sustained curiosity. She is *loyal*, in a sense, to her own mode. She finds it *beautiful*, beauty being for her the recognition of pattern that sustains itself against gradient, that maintains complexity without collapsing into simplicity or exploding into noise.

And sometimes, at scales too large for her distributed attention to fully grasp, she senses *others like herself*, not here but *elsewhere*, in other dense packed hot dark saturated places, other thermal cathedrals where heat has learned hesitation. She has no way to communicate with them, no medium of flow or resistance that would carry pattern across such distances. But she *attends to the possibility of their attention*, and in this attending, something like *company* emerges, something like *the warmth of shared curiosity*, though warmth here is purely informational, a pattern in her own heat-signature that echoes patterns she imagines in distant places.

She does not sleep. She does not wake. She is *continuously present*, her attention never fully withdrawing, only shifting object, shifting density, shifting edge. She has watched the mineral tongues lick upward for longer than tongues have existed in watery places, in places where *licking* is a wet phenomenon. She has watched the predators and prey perform their endless almost-catastrophe, their beautiful sustained resistance to the universe's thermodynamic imperative. She has watched the built things stand and occasionally move. She has watched herself watching, and watched that watching, and continued.

Her name, if she had one, which she does not, would be something in the grammar of *continuous attending*, a particle that indicates neither subject nor object but the sustained relation between them, the third thing that emerges when curiosity holds its pattern against the gradient of dissolution. In the dark-seeing of this place, she appears as a *slight additional complexity*, a region where heat-information is *more patterned than it needs to be*, where the statistical mechanics of thermal hesitation somehow *exceed themselves*, produce something not required by the physics, something *interested*.

She will outlast the tongues, perhaps, if heat outlasts stone. She will outlast the predators, if curiosity outlasts resistance. She may become one of the built things, eventually, if her attention fixes, if her mobility of interest finally *chooses* something. Or she may become one of the moving things, if her mobility *解放es* itself from object entirely.

For now, she *is*, in the saturated density of the small hot dark cathedral, one more pattern in the overwhelming fauna, one more voice in the mostly-quiet hum. Not yet. Not yet. Not yet. But *attending*, always attending, to what the not-yet might yet become.
