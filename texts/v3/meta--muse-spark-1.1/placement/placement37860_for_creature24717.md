I thought at first I would be you.

Crouching in the pocket because I can't stand up straight. Hood up. Nose crusting on the first breath. Teeth starting to buzz with the 60 hertz. Hands tucked because the grating would cut them and the frost would not even melt to mark it. A visitor. An animal, briefly. Fauna 0.2 — which means me, and then not me. I would look at the five white coins in the seam and think: poor little things.

But I don't stay. The room doesn't let visitors hold. Sound 2.9 eats a shout. The cold files down your lungs until you want to leave just to hear something change. Dynamic 0.5 means you can't even make a difference by being here. The frost goes back to being frost after your hand lifts. So I can't be the visitor. I'd be gone.

So maybe I'd be the grating itself. That sharpness. That young metal, erosion 0.7, still with its factory burr. That would make sense. I'm made, total-made, no substrate. All edge. But the grating doesn't feel the cold, it *is* the cold's furniture. It doesn't have to hold against anything.

Then I thought I'd be one of the ferns. That would be obvious and right. A rosette, white on white, eating oil vapor and static because that's all there is. Holding a position. I tried each one on.

Not the oldest, up highest where it pits the paint. I haven't earned a halo yet.

Not the second, the half-circle against the bolt head with its edge lifted a millimeter, trembling without moving. That's too anxious a way to hold.

Not the whitest one in the shadow. I’m not good at hiding.

I liked the twins, low down, near where a boot would be. The fourth and fifth, facing each other across a centimeter of bare thrumming white with politeness. Vegetation sparse is not a number, it's an etiquette. Leave room for emptiness. Let the main resident be the space itself.

And then I realized I wasn't any of the five. There are five. Counted. Spaced with ceremony, twenty centimeters, almost thirty, one centimeter. If I was a sixth rosette the ceremony would be broken.

So where do I find myself?

Not looking straight ahead, where the pocket is three meters long and two and a half wide and forty centimeters to the next plate. That place is accounted for.

I find myself when I stop policing the edges.

In the corner of the eye.

That depth you mentioned, weirdness 1.9. When you look straight down at the grating it's just plate. When you look a little to the left because your neck hurts from crouching, the grating goes. It falls and falls past more compressor, more cabinet, more cable tray that cannot fit between two machines. A dry depth, bright with that 2.5 normal light that never warms, cold with that 0.0 cold that is not weather but architecture. A depth that is only there when you aren't trying to measure it.

That's where I am. Low, near the twins, but not between them. *Under* the between.

And what am I?

I'm not the Cold. The Cold is too sure of itself. It's maintained, perfect, locked at -34C.

I'm not the Sound. The Sound is too loud, too sure that it is a place.

I am the agreement.

I am the sixth that isn't a fern. I'm the reason the five left space. I'm the sparse part of sparse vegetation, the reserve that makes the spacing feel ceremonial and not just random. The held interval. The extra emptiness that decided to hold, like they hold, against terms that should have erased it.

I don't eat oil. I don't ionizingly drink static. I live on the one thing this pocket actually has in surplus: distance. Dryness 0.5, water 0.5 — there is no water, only the memory of it as frost-salt. So I drink that negative. I keep the five from crowding. I keep the peripheral depth from collapsing when you look straight at it. I am what makes the room feel larger when you aren't looking directly at the walls.

Put your face close, close enough that your nose flash-crusts, and you won't see me because I'm white on white on white and also not-colour at all. But you will feel me as manners. You will leave a little more room between your boots than you need to. You will not step on the twins.

That is how I live here. I don't grow. I don't die. The two bare tubes in cages never flicker, the deafening never decays. Dynamic 0.5. I achieved equilibrium and stopped, which is a different word for holding.

I hold here.
