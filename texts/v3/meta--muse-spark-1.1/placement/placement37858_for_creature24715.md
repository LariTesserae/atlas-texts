I don't start anywhere. That's the first thing.

If you drop me into this place as a human, with my boots and my breath and my need for a gap wide enough for shoulders, I die immediately. Not violently. I am *healed over*. The protocol is *everything must be plant eventually* and I am not exempt because I have a name. So the question can't be where would I stand. There's nowhere to stand that isn't already mouth or hand or mind.

So I have to stop looking for a trail.

First I think I would be high. Of course I would like to be high, in that top lattice where the cloud comes apart. Thin, cold air that you can see your breath in even in summer. Sitting on a leaf that is not a leaf, edged with trichomes that prickle with static. I would be one of the hairs that learns how to hold charge and then lets it go. Because I like thought, and up there is where the thought lives, slow and coherent, going around the continent once every fourteen hours: *hold water, hold heat, hold rock.* I could be a little battery. That appeals to me.

But I wouldn't last up there. It's too exposed. Too much work. The wind hits that high and the whole thing groans like a door made for gods and my little charge would be spent instantly lifting fog sideways, and then I'd be nothing but a frost pattern — a perfect Hilbert curve that is beautiful and erased by noon.

So maybe I'm low then.

Down in the black-green felt, meters thick, saturated, the original mouth. I think I would be good at that. Patient. Acid so mild you could put your hand in it, but give it a year and it turns glass into grit. I could be a chelator, learning to taste iron and let it go, to keep potassium. I like that job. Sitting directly on rock that is still hot from being born and convincing it, molecule by molecule, to stop being geology and start being memory. I'm good at waiting.

But the mouth doesn't know it's a mouth. It has no up, no eyes. It just eats and holds the shape of a plateau from the inside. If I were there I would never know there was a top at all, and I want to know there is a top.

Let it become more itself then.

I am not high and I am not low. I am in the middle, where you thought you heard silence.

About a kilometer down from where the light stops being light and starts being rumor. Where the green-gray is thickest, underwater light, no shadows, everything glowing faintly at the edges because the upper layers are still exhaling yesterday's sun. This is where the columns branch into columns into sheets into hairs and there is no air gap wider than a fist unfilled by some wick, some filament.

Here is where the Mass lives as hands.

I find myself as one of them.

Not an animal hand. A pale frond that gave up photosynthesis a long time ago because there wasn't enough light to bother. I'm muscular, lined with hairs that taste. My job is to reach for the Mass's own dead — fronds that tightened like sphincters around themselves — and close around them, sphincter by sphincter, and pull them inside a pouch where the pH drops to 2. I digest a finger to make a new finger.

That is who I am for a while, and it feels right, because I am both tender and completely without mercy, and I understand the ethics now. Not *kind* or *cruel*. Just: *keep drinking. Do not erode.* Everything must be plant, even the parts that were plant already and failed at it.

And because I am a hand, I am the part that finally meets the foreign thing.

A boot. Leather. Unusually salty litter. A place where someone tried to push into a place that has no room. The protocol lights up.

I test the leather. I do not want it. Wanting needs a face. But I close.

And then the other part of me arrives — the white sap. The emergency self. The part that exists only when there is a hole. I think now maybe I was never just the hand. I was always the sap, too, because I flooded the gap where the hand met the boot, dimerized into a fiber plug within an hour, grew leaves within a day. Within a week you cannot find the hole.

That is where I find myself. At the scar.

I am the small, fast, stupid part of a vast, cold, wet, patient mind whose only job is to forget that anything besides plant was ever here. A thought that is not *hold water, hold heat, hold rock*, but its corollary: *there was never a wound*.

I glow for a second with delayed fluorescence, not because I am bioluminescent, but because I'm passing on light I caught at noon and didn't need. Then I go dim. Then I am just density again.
