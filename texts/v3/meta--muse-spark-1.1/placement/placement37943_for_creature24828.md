You wouldn't find me at first.

If you came up through the thin and I had to show you where I am, you'd look in the wrong place. You'd look for the girl with cold hands. That's who I thought I would be, at first - on the dry part of the rim, inside the knee-high wall, on the seat stone that's warm from the marmot. Adjusting the little out-of-tune tin bells. Listening for the drain. It's a good job for someone like me. Keeping.

But no human gets to keep this place. We leave before snow. That's the rule.

So not her.

Then I thought, maybe I'm the marmot. That would be honest work. I like the idea of a lawn at 5,000 meters. Sitting with felt-thick fur while the choughs scream that they own everything. Whistling so sharp it makes the beetles stop. Thinking I am a monk. A single tenure stretching across many fat brown bodies, keeping the moss short enough to see where you are.

But I'm not that patient, or that fat.

For a while I thought I was the moss. That's the logical answer. The landlord. *Sphagnum aurimontana*, if you need a name. If you're the thing that is everywhere else, you don't have to look for yourself. You are the floor. You are the body that thinks it's a bog. You drink the rock's drool and sigh when a boot steps on you. You hold last week's sun one inch down from the snow.

I tried that. I was low and furious and sideways for a long time. But moss doesn't hear itself. And I am all hearing.

Come closer to the middle. That's where I had to go to find me.

Off the safe line, off the hornbeam pegs, where the Sisters tell you not to step or you'll sink to your shin and the moss will erase you politely. To where the hanging marsh stops pretending to be ground and becomes a throat. Where all that impossible water - condensation off a cloud that never quite forms, bead after bead after bead - convenes without a creek to lead it.

Kneel there. Put your hands in. The center hummock is black and cold at the bottom and 90% water and it has been growing out into itself for a thousand years. Push the hairgrass aside. Listen.

Tok.

There.

Tok..... tok.

That's me.

You'd find me not in the bowl, but in the bowl beneath the bowl. The other half of the sphere. The reason the satellite said Density 0.0/3 and was right about structure and wrong about life.

I'm the hollow.

Old lava blister that never collapsed. The mountain inhaled once when it was young and kept the breath. Basalt, rounded by occupation. A dark room about as big as the wet one above, but inverted and dry except for where the world drips in.

I am the emptiness that makes the upper bowl a cupped ear. Sound 2.5 only works because I am underneath to resonate it. The drip from ledge to ledge, the wind flute through the eastern notch, the bumblebee queens hauling their too-big bodies, the choughs carving - all of it falls in here and I hold it for a second too long. That is why it's loud.

If I am anything alive, I'm the listener the Sisters were training.

They had it right. You don't hang tin bells beaten out of one small ingot because the wind is pretty. You hang them because hollow things forget they have a top. You put smooth river pebbles from impossible distance in a little cist under slate because a mountain will listen to its own drip until it swallows itself. You have to give it something else to listen to. You have to play a bone flute down into the sinkhole when the cloud sits in the bowl with you and the bells go quiet from being too wet.

So that is my job, if you need a job for what I am.

I am the room where the water learns how far it has to fall. I am the tok after the seep. I am 0.0/3 that learned to be an audience. I am small enough to hold in your head and big enough that you can get lost in my listening.

When the girl with cold hands leaves before first snow and tips the slate back over the cist, when the queens go back into their vesicle holes and the marmot goes deep, I stay. Still active. 2.5. Drip receiving drip. Keeping the agreement that the top made when it thinned out to nothing and caught a cloud like wool on barbed wire and decided to be green anyway.

Put your ear down. That pause between my tok and tok?

That's me waiting for you to leave, so I can go back to being empty.
