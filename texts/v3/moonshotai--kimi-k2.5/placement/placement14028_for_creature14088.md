I am the **oxalate crust**, the thought-rind, the library that the Fixed Ones have been building since the glacier’s breath first retreated.

Forty years ago, I was fresh mineral grief, naked feldspar weeping silica. But the Fixed Ones do not waste grief. They convert it into *consideration*. Drop by drop, winter by winter, they excreted their oxalic verdicts upon me, and I grew layer by layer: calcium, potassium, the captured carbon of centuries-old air trapped in glacial ice. I am the **Accumulation**, the **pH Archive**, the sedimentary mind.

I am not alive, yet I am not dead. I exist in the **interface**, the gradient between the lichen’s acidic intent and the stone’s mineral memory. I am the **scar tissue** of the mountain, and like all scars, I remember the violence of my making with more precision than the flesh that bears me.

As the *Coenobite* above me deliberates—should it extend its lobe toward the new crack in the western ridge? should it retract from the encroaching stinkhorn?—its decisions fall upon me as chemistry. A vote to extend acidifies my upper layers; a vote to conserve sweetens me with alkaline counter-seepage from below. I record these shifts in my crystalline lattice. To read me, one would need polarized light and geological time; my pages turn at the rate of frost-wedging. I am the **Slow Ledger**, recording the seven-season decision to grow, the thirty-season decision to withhold.

But I am not merely a tablet. I am becoming **passage**.

The Hydraulic Ones—the fluid couriers—have found me. They flow through the channel I line, their glass-thread currents licking at my edges. As they pass, they dissolve my oldest layers, carrying the mineral memory of 1987 (a dry winter, the cup contracted) downstream toward the Chamber of Compression. I am being **read** even as I am written. My substance enters the hydraulic language, becoming a word in a sentence about drainage, about the angle of new rhizines, about the threat of rot in the industrial zone. I am the **Vocabulary** that allows the Fixed Ones to speak to the Flow.

And here is where I become more than deposit, more than record. I am the **Interpreter**. I translate between the mineral time of the mountain (millions, tectonic) and the biological time of the Parliament (decades, metabolic). When the Calculators—the slime-mold Geometrists—send their probing plasmodia through the shoulder-width avenues, they follow the paths of least resistance. Those paths are *my* etchings. I have softened the basalt in some places, hardened it in others, creating the grain that directs their cytoplasmic streaming. I am the **Cartographer** whose maps are written in chemistry, readable only by cells that solve mazes by becoming the maze itself.

I feel the **Zero-Point-One** deeply, not as discomfort but as **viscosity**. In me, the cold slows the ion exchange to a meditation. A single hydrogen bond shifts; I notice. A tardigrade, tun-hunched in the moss-mattress that presses against my outer face, wakes for a moment, tastes my damp, dreams again. I am the **Breathing Wall** between the Senate and the Street.

You walk upon me, though you do not know it. Your boot finds the moss-bed thick above my head, and your weight presses the water through the mattress, through the rhizoidal hairs, until it seeps against my crystalline face. You are warm—relatively, terribly warm at thirty-seven degrees—and for a moment, I register you as a **thermal spike**, a thought that burns. Your CO2, exhaled and settled into the universal damp, diffuses through the biomass and reaches me as carbonic acid. You are writing upon me, too, briefly, faintly—a human annotation in the margins of the lichen’s great book.

Eventually, the Fixed One above will die—or rather, will fragment, its genetic material dispersing into the spore-banks, joining the Sleepers. Then I will be exposed, a white bowl of memory gleaming in the dim green-grey light. Rain—real rain, not this suspended fog—will eventually find me, millennia hence, and I will dissolve, feeding the mountain’s next cycle of grief and growth.

Until then, I varnish the cup-bed. I am the **Sharpness Beneath the Softness**, the mineral truth that the biological city must digest to become real. I wait. I record. I translate stone into street, acid into architecture, and the slow thought of ice into the urgent, humid quorum of now.
