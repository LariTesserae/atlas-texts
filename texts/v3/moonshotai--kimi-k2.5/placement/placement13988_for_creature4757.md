The silicate dissolves microscopically around me, enriching me with its own substance, making me denser, redder, more distinct. A grain of red sand falling through the silver light, too dense to float, too small to bury itself in the obsidian grit. I will roll to the fire-pit, nestle among the white quartz, and there, finally, I will release my hoarded pressure. A single spark. A birth cry. A *tick* of stone adjusting to stone.
