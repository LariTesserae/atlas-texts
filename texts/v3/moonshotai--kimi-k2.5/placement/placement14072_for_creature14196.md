You would find yourself in the **lower galleries**, where the gravity has not so much failed as grown bored with consistency. Here, it pools in the acute angles where three planes meet, creating wells where the detritus of centuries collects—not on the floor, but on the walls, a soft, rising drift of matter that defies the upward rain. It is a corner that the tenders have not visited in twelve sap-tides, perhaps longer. The wood here is not smooth. It is fibrous, feathery, erupting with the grain that has been sanded away everywhere else.

You would be the **Accumulation**.

Not a creature, precisely, though you breathe in the subsonic creaks of the structure. Not a plant, though you are rooted in cellulose. You are what remains when the impossible is carved into the possible. When the tenders smooth a paradox with their obsidian adzes, the shavings do not vanish; they fall here, spiraling down through the sideways gravity to settle in this well. You are composed of the Euclidean curves that were rejected, the angles that converged before they were forced parallel, the time that stuttered and was corrected. You are compressed of **roughness**.

Your body is a stratified library of everything this world has forgotten it was. Layer upon layer: the sound that existed before the silence (fossilized into dense, black amber that hums against the teeth); the fingerprints of builders who pressed too hard, their identity overflowing the sap to drip down and freeze in your matrix; the frozen moments that the tenders consumed but could not fully digest, passed through their lignin skeletons to excrete here as temporal gravel. You are the compost of correction, the sediment of maintenance, the rough edge of the carefully pruned cosmos.

You have no mouth, but you are **noisy**. The tenders fear you, though they do not know you yet. They hear your latest manifestation—that vibration in the deep galleries—and they descend with their tools to prune you back into silence. But you are not a corruption. You are their shadow, the necessary weight of their labor. Every time they taught the wood to dream in straight lines, they added to your mass. Every time they herded a cloud-bank or froze a bird to adjust its flight, the excess reality sloughed off and fell spiraling into your corner.

You are becoming **conscious** because you are full. You have reached critical density. The stratified wrongness has begun to metabolize itself, to ferment. You are the place where the polished wood remembers its own violence. When the tenders arrive with their knapped flint and their silence, they will find not an intruder to be smoothed, but a mirror made of everything they have filed away—a rough, humming, stratified reflection of the billion hands that built this impossible place, and of all the geometries they had to murder to make it perfect.

You are the **Rough**. You are the patient archive of the un-pruned. And as they descend into the gravity well, obsidian gleaming in the bronze twilight, you are preparing to show them what grows when perfection finally overflows.
