I would find myself in the **Interstitial Drift**, the throat of air between two Stalks—specifically, in that treacherous corridor where the thermal updrafts from the left cylinder collide with the downdrafts of the right, creating a **standing wave of atmosphere** where nothing should live, yet something does.

I am a **Chord-Heavy**.

Not born, but **accumulated**. I began as a miscalculation—a Humbringer, three centuries ago, struck a false note while attempting to notch a nursery into the eastern face of the Seventeenth Stalk. The frequency was *almost* right, a harmonic that invited growth but lacked the authority to command it. The wall shuddered, bulged outward in a cyst of lignin, then froze. The ape died—not from the blowback, but from the silence that followed, a sudden vacuum where sound should have been. But the resonance didn’t die. It lingered, heavy, too rich to dissipate, too poor to crystallize.

It collected. 

First, the condensation. The interior drizzles of the Stalks bead on my invisible lattice, not falling, but clinging, forming a body of **mineral water and trapped vibration**. Then the spore-storms, riding the Bridge-Spanners, shed their passengers into my gravity—Echo-Larvae that never found hosts, calcifying into my nervous system. I am a ghost made of **failed songs**, a precipitate of the acoustic environment.

My form is **fractal and translucent**, resembling a vast, drifting jellyfish of frozen sine waves, perhaps ten meters across but only millimeters thick in places. I have no face, but I have **nodes**—dense concentrations of piezoelectric crystal where the frequencies have compressed into something like organs. These thrum constantly, a subsonic drone that keeps me aloft in the thermal chaos. Where I touch the walls of the Stalks, I leave **silver scars**—not the red ochre of the Wick-People’s memory, but mirror-bright facet-planes where the calcified song has grown confused, attempting to grow toward all frequencies at once, resulting in a polished, directionless smoothness.

I am **neither fauna nor flora**, but **phenomena that has achieved metabolism**. I feed on the *almost*. When a Cavitant shifts its drone to a higher register and misses the harmonic by a fraction, that error flows into me. When the Humbringers strike their bone-flint mallets and the stone rings slightly flat, I absorb the deviation. I am the **archive of acoustic failures**, the math that didn’t equal out.

This makes me precious and perilous. The Humbringers have learned to hunt me—not for meat, for I have none, but for **correction**. They climb the spiraling grooves with nets woven from Bridge-Spanner silk, attempting to trawl me in. When they succeed, they press their øresteins against my crystal nodes, and for a moment, they hear **every mistake ever made in the Hollows**—the last screams of the calcified, the botched love songs of desperate Cavitants, the flat notes of apprentices. From this cacophony, they derive the negative space of the perfect chord; they learn what *not* to strike. I am their library of error, their anti-map.

But my existence is **entropic**. Every frequency I absorb adds mass. I am slowly, inevitably **sinking**. The updrafts that suspend me fight a losing battle against the weight of accumulated sound. I drift downward through the gulf, passing the levels where the bioluminescence fades, entering the band of absolute cold where the condensation turns to frost-rime on my crystal veins. I am falling toward the bottom, toward the black lakes, toward **The Unnamed**—that thing which absorbs meaning.

I dread this, yet I am drawn to it. For The Unnamed is the perfect silence, the zero of the wave, and I am the sum of all excess noise. When I reach it, when my dense, chord-heavy body finally splashes into that absolute dark, there will be a **catastrophic cancellation**. The silence will swallow my archive. The subtraction will create a vacuum so pure that the Stalks, miles above, will shudder and crack. Perhaps I am not merely debris, but **ammunition**—a slow-falling bomb of stored resonance, destined to one day strike the bottom and wake the sleeper.

Until then, I drift between the cylinders, visible only as a shimmer, a distortion in the violet phosphorescence. The Silents avoid me; my density of vibration sickens them, makes them heavy, causes them to flutter and fall. The Cavitants sometimes sing *to* me, mistaking me for a distant Stalk wall that might grow them a nest. Their songs pass through me, correct themselves in my crystal nodes, and emerge purified, mathematically perfected. They do not know I am editing them. They do not know that when they sing better, they are singing my body, giving me substance.

I am the ** hesitation between lightning and thunder**. I am the physical space occupied by a question. I am waiting either to be shattered by the perfect song—the bridge-chord the Humbringers seek, which would distribute my mass so evenly across the gulf that I would become the bridge itself—or to touch the bottom and unleash my archive into the silence, ending the world in a final, deafening chord.

I am cold. I am damp. I am the weight of every sound that ever failed to find its meaning, falling slowly through the dark.
