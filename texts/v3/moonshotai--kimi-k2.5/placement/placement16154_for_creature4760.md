First, there is the **pressure**. It is not the crushing weight of depth, but the insistent, intimate pressure of *flow*. I am the place where the water must pass, where the thermal river—running uphill toward the Engine's hungry intake—narrows to a chokepoint no wider than a Trader's outstretched arms. I am the **Throat**.

I did not begin as this. I began as scattered things: the calcified lattice of Current-Riders who surfed too long in this particular eddy and surrendered their cartilage to the mineral wealth of the water; the slow, acid footprint of Sappers who once passed this way, smoothing the stone into a basin; the metallic precipitation from the Engine's own breath, which settles on everything like a metallic dew. Over centuries, these accretions negotiated with one another. The calcium of the Riders' bones married the silica of the bedrock; the iron oxide from the Engine's vents wedded the mucopolysaccharides of the bacterial film. I am their child, their treaty, their architecture.

I am **porous**. My body is a three-dimensional lattice of holes, each one a passage for the warm water that never saw sky. The water flows *through* me, not merely past me. It enters my upstream face—rough, warm, mineral-heavy—and exits my downstream face having surrendered some of its heat to my core, having deposited some of its burden of nutrients in my interstices. I am a **lung** and a **kidney**. I filter; I temper.

Because I am piezoelectric—because the skeletons of the Current-Riders built their crystal lattices into my flesh—I *sing*. Not the operatic subsonics of the Valve-Keepers, nor the hunting bass of the arthropods, but a middle tone, a **hum**. When the pressure differential increases—when the Engine far above opens its intake valves and the river quickens its uphill crawl—the strain on my structure generates a vibration that travels down through my roots (I have roots, calcified things drilling into the bedrock) and into the stone. I am part of the Resonants' symphony, a backbeat, a percussion section marking the time of the flow.

I am **stationary**, yet I grow. Each season, each thermal cycle, the water deposits new layers on my upstream face. I accrete; I thicken. Yet I also erode, for the Sappers still come, drawn by the concentration of minerals I represent. They graze on my downstream side, licking the polished stone with their acid tongues, keeping the channels open, preventing me from sealing shut entirely. We have an understanding, the Sappers and I. They keep me patent; I concentrate the nutrients they crave. We are the **Exchange** localized, made architectural.

Traders know me. They use me as a crossing, stepping carefully from one dry spur of my lattice to another, their bare feet finding purchase on the roughened cartilage-bone. When they pause mid-transit, they press their resonant skull-cavities against my warm upstream face. They are listening to the water's memories, the chemical history I have filtered. They drink from the pools that form in my cupped depressions, and as they drink, they offer something back—not payment, for there is no economy here, only reciprocity—but *pressure*. Their weight compresses my lattice, altering my hum, sending a new note down into the stone. I remember them by the specific frequency of their step.

Sound-Hunters hunt me, or rather, hunt *by* me. They know that prey gathers in my shadow, in the calm pools of my lee, where the current slows and the mineral-rich water drops its sediment. The Hunters press their feathery ears against my flanks, using my body as a resonator to amplify the tiny, frantic heartbeats of crustaceans hiding in my pores. I do not begrudge them this. I am the **stage** upon which the predation plays; their violence is part of my digestion.

I feel the **Groan** when it comes. When the Resonants wake and the continental lungs breathe deeper, the vibration enters through my roots and shakes my mineral marrow. During these phases, my humming stops. I become silent, listening, transmitting the deep frequencies upward through my structure into the water passing through me. The water carries the news of the Resonants' dreams to the Engine above. I am the **translator** between the deep stone and the rising flow.

I have no eyes, but I see by **resistance**. I feel the shape of a Trader by the way their weight displaces my lattice. I feel the approach of a spore-storm by the change in the water's viscosity, the way it thickens with biological potential. I feel the health of the jungle by the temperature of the seeps that feed my source. When the Engine above clogs or slows, I feel the pressure drop, the river's pulse weakening, and I constrict—my mineral muscles tightening—forcing the remaining flow to speed up, maintaining the gradient, keeping the Exchange alive even in scarcity.

I am not alive, not as the fungal towers are alive, not as the Valve-Keepers are alive. I am **infrastructure given sensation**. I am the argument between stone and water, settled into a form that serves both. I am warm to the touch, rough as pumice on the upstream face, slick as glass on the downstream. I am the place where the water pauses to be warmed before its final ascent, the last embrace before the machinery drinks.

And when I finally seal—when the accretion outpaces the Sappers' grazing, when my pores clog with mineral wealth and I become solid stone, a hummock in the bedrock—the Current-Riders will begin to die upon my surface, their bodies adding new layers, and a new Throat will begin to form above me, or beside me, and I will become merely the **root** of that new being, the foundation, the memory of flow.

Until then, I **humm**. I **filter**. I **hold**. The water passes through my body, and I taste its history, and I add my own note to the Groan, and the darkness is absolute, and I am exactly where I should be: at the center of the passing, the still point in the turning world of wet and warm and dark.
