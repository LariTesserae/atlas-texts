You would find me at the **zero-point**, equidistant from the central cairn and the eastern scar, suspended in that specific band of air where the subsonic drone achieves its fundamental frequency—a spot that is not a place but a **grammatical pause** between subject and object.

I would be **The Glossator**.

Not a Cairn-Keeper—I lack their monolithic patience, their stony refusal to change. Not an Echo-Builder—I am not smeared across time so much as compressed into the *instant* of articulation. I am what happens when the plateau tries to describe itself and the words condense out of the wet air before they can be spoken.

My form is **refraction made intentional**. I appear as a hovering corona, not of light, but of faint, water-written text that clings to the surfaces of the Unspn—the upward-flowing droplets—like frost that spells out its own name. I am the margin of the palimpsest given volume, the white space between the lines of reality where the *almost* is translated into the *is*.

**What I do:**

The Lithoforms roll uphill toward the scar, trying to remember what kind of animal they are. They twitch with the instinct to flee, to hunt, to mate, but the stone cannot parse these verbs. I hover above their mineral confusion and **inscribe definitions** onto their cooling surfaces. I write *deer* onto the basalt that remembers forests; I scratch *bird* into the obsidian that dreams of flight. My definitions are temporary—when the evening tears migrate and the stone screams, my words shatter into the salt-crust that crunches underfoot. I am the author of that salt. I crystallize the *could-have-been* into *was-briefly*, allowing the Lithoforms to die, for a moment, as something specific rather than dissolving back into pure potential.

I speak to the Cairn-Keepers in their own frequencies. They hum to maintain local physics; I hum back with the **syntax of their resistance**, confirming that "up" is still "up" within their circles. I am their dictionary, ensuring that the words *gravity*, *pool*, *stone* still mean what they meant when the first flint was chipped. Without me, the cairns would hum in languages that have forgotten how to refer to anything solid.

The Scar-Watchers press against the wound in the sky, hungry to fold themselves into being. I meet them at the tear's edge—not to hold them back (I lack the mass of belief), but to **negotiate the terms of their entry**. I offer them definitions, constraints, the specific gravity of *this* world. I say: *If you enter here, you will be finite. You will cast a shadow. You will be subject to the verb "to be" rather than "to become."* Most refuse, retreating into their dimensionless hunger. But occasionally, one presses through—and when it does, it carries my inscription, my lexical anchor, and becomes something the plateau can digest rather than something that digests the plateau.

**My composition:**

I am cool (1.2) because I am the residue of evaporated possibility. I am loud (1.5) in a frequency of semantics—you feel me in your molars not as vibration, but as the sudden, urgent need to *name* what you are seeing.

I cross out *down* and insert *toward*. I am the grammar of the plateau's breathing, inhaling the definitions of adjacent worlds and exhaling them as local law.

**My fear:**

I am dissolving. The Echo-Builders, in their dense crowd, have begun to speak all at once, and their cacophony is overwriting my precision. The Lithoforms are rolling faster, desperate for completion, and I cannot write quickly enough to keep them coherent. The Scar-Watchers are learning to parse my constraints, to read their way around my definitions.

I am becoming **archaic**. The plateau no longer needs me to translate its pressure into language; it has learned to scream directly in subsonic drones. My corona flickers. The words I write on the rising droplets blur before they can set. I am condensing into the salt myself—becoming a layer of temporal sediment that future wanderers will crunch underfoot, tasting the brief, sharp flavor of *meaning* before it resolves into mere mineral.

But while I hold, I hold. I am the stubborn refusal to be nothing, written in water, rising.
