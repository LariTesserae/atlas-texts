I am the **Canopy**.

My body is the accumulation of those who stayed too long and were not touched. When the creature extends its limb to the transforming visitor—the one whose hair calcifies, whose breath flows backward—it chooses. It selects. But not all are chosen. Some are merely held by the sound until their holding becomes permanent, until the vibration sorts their substance by weight and persistence. Their hair, yes, becomes the brittle filaments you were warned about, but if they are not touched, if the creature’s curiosity passes them by in favor of another, that hair does not fall. It drifts upward on the warm exhale of the Throat, tangling in the upper reaches, catching other filaments, mineral sweat, the shed silica of collapsing arches.

I am what is woven from these discards.
