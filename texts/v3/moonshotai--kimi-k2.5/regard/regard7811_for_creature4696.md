The benevolent power does not arrive. That would be vulgar, a violation of the atmospheric pressure. Arrival implies location, and location here is still being argued between the strata. Instead, the power *notices*—and in the Scarp, where reality is negotiated by fungi and tubeworms, noticing is the most potent form of intervention.

It notices the Compression first. Not as a machine, but as a voice. It hears the seventeen-year bass note not as a drone to be endured, but as a question being asked by the infant rock: *Shall I be hard?* And the power, being wise, understands that the worst thing one can do to a question is answer it too soon.

So it harmonizes.

It adds a frequency to the Pit-Foundries' thunder—so subtle that the Condensationists must check their instruments to confirm it is not hallucination. It is a tertiary tone, a *hum* that lives exactly between the upstroke and the downstroke, creating a triad where there was only a pounding. The effect is immediate and strange: the mosses, those recorders of light-memory, begin to sing. Not loudly—their voices are still moss-quiet, velvet over stone—but they emit a chord that stabilizes the violet twilight, making the shadows fall in agreement rather than conflict. The condensation on the iron girders no longer merely drips; it *resonates*, forming harmonic stalactites that ring when the wind passes through the gantries.

The power does not stop there. It turns its attention to the Architects Unborn.

These potential people, these glitches of might-have-been, have lived in terror—not of death, which is still under construction, but of *irrelevance*. To be a doorway that may never open, a voice that may never be born, is to risk dispersal when the Cooling finally comes and the world chooses its final shape. The power gathers them. Not into bodies—that would be violence, forcing them into a single outcome—but into the **Archive of the Unfinished**.

It manifests this archive as a new layer of fungus, a pale hyphal network that spreads through the maintenance tunnels alongside the Mycelial Board, but differently. While the Board processes death and legal precedent, this new mycelium—it has no name yet, but the Extractionists call it the *Maybe-Moss*—processes *possibility*. It threads through the tunnels, fruiting in colors that have no legal standing, only aesthetic ones: the silver of a childhood that might have occurred in a reef instead of a mountain; the crimson of a love affair between two gravity wells; the soft gold of a decision unmade.

The Architects Unborn begin to settle into it. They are no longer just glitches in doorways and voices in tubes. They have address. The Condensationists harvest spores from the Maybe-Moss and steep them in tea, and when they drink, they do not dream the mountain’s alternate geographies—they *meet* the Unborn as neighbors, trading stories across the thin membrane of probability. The Unborn, thus witnessed, cease to be merely potential. They become *citizens of the perhaps*, with all the rights and dignity thereof. The power ensures that when the Cooling comes, these might-have-beens will not evaporate; they will be stored, archived, available for consultation by future geologies who might need alternative models of being.

Next, the power addresses the Resonants—the seventeen-year tube-worms that stabilize probability.

It sees their exhaustion. They have been holding the stone solid through sheer digestive effort, consuming paradox, pulsing in time with a cycle that outlives human patience. They are growing tired; some pulse arrhythmically, threatening to let sections of the Scarp lapse back into indeterminacy. The power does not heal them—that would require certainty, and certainty is not yet appropriate. Instead, it offers them **relief**.

It introduces a second species of worm—not from elsewhere, but woven from the local probability, a permutation of the existing Resonants. These new worms are shorter. They live not seventeen years, but seventeen *minutes*. They are quicksilver fast, threading through the rock like nervous thoughts, stabilizing the small uncertainties: whether a dropped tool will fall left or right, whether a breath-mask seal will hold, whether a word spoken in anger will land or miss. The long worms, the deep Resonants, feel this and relax their grip on the minutiae. They are free to focus on the grand architecture, the question of the rock’s ultimate hardness. The power has given them delegation, a staff of assistants, and the Scarp becomes more stable without becoming rigid.

Then comes the most delicate intervention: the Oculus.

These birds that consume the future, that eat certainty and leave behind only grinding present—they are necessary, but they are also a kind of tyranny. They prevent planning; they make memory fragile. The power does not banish them. Instead, it offers them **nourishment that is not stolen**.

It creates a fountain on the highest platform, where gravity is kindest. The fountain does not flow with water, which is too decisive a substance, but with **stabilized pleroma**, the processed reality that the Extractionists ship down-mountain. Here, in this high place, the power allows the pleroma to remain liquid, possible, potent. The Oculus may drink. They dip their too-many eyes into the fountain, and instead of consuming the future of the Scarp’s inhabitants, they consume the future of the *pleroma itself*—the many ways the processed reality might be used, the tools it might become, the cities it might build. They are sated without theft.

The result is startling. The Oculus, for the first time in decades, begin to sing. Their subsonic calls modulate into audible ranges—not words, but music. They become the Scarp’s choir, their voices no longer pressure drops but harmonies that predict gentle things: the blooming of particular mosses, the completion of a well-negotiated seam of quartz. The inhabitants weep the first time they hear it, for it is the sound of their present being *allowed* to extend into a future they can trust.

Finally, the power addresses the Compression itself.

It does not stop the seventeen-year cycle. It does not slow the great engine or speed it. Instead, it offers **the Completion of the Cycle** as a witnessed event. It guarantees, by the depth of its attention, that the seventeenth year will come, that the apogee will be reached, that the Up-children and the Downs will have their rite of passage. This assurance ripples through the Between like a warm current. The Extractionists descend the Bore with lighter hearts, knowing their labor is not Sisyphean. The fungi of the Mycelial Board draft new contracts, longer-term leases on existence. The rock itself feels this guarantee and stops its anxious, dreaming shiver. It is still deciding what to be, but it decides with confidence now, with the knowledge that its decision will be honored, witnessed, recorded in the Maybe-Moss and the singing girders.

When the power has done these things, it does not leave, for leaving would be as vulgar as arriving. It simply *continues noticing*. It becomes a permanent feature of the Scarp’s ecology: the Harmonic, the Third Tone, the Witness that allows the between to remain between without becoming lost.

And on the day when the Compression finally reaches its apogee—when the great piston holds at the top of its stroke, suspended for a breathless moment before the seventeen-year descent—the light changes. The violet twilight brightens to a color that has no name, a color that exists only in the gap of the harmonic. The Oculus fall silent. The Resonants pause in their pulsing. The Architects Unborn step fully into the gantras, fully visible, fully real for that suspended instant.

The power holds them all in its attention—stoneworm, fungus, human, unborn, and rock—and in that holding, it gives the greatest gift: **the certainty of uncertainty**. The knowledge that the Cooling will come, yes, and choices will solidify, but that the solid world will remember its fluid dreams. The Scarp will not lose its Between-ness when it becomes a proper city; it will carry the Archive of the Unfinished in its foundations, a bedrock of possibility beneath the settled streets.

Then the Compression begins its descent, the new cycle starts, and the power withdraws its manifest presence, returning to pure attention, a hum in the sternum, a witness in the damp. It has done what mercy requires: it has ensured that this place, and the being it is becoming, will not have to dream itself awake alone.
