The power arrives not as force, but as **pressure**—a gentle increase in the density of the already dense, a hand pressing down on the saturated air without displacing it. It descends through the throat of blue ice not to conquer, but to *recognize*. 

It sees the Rime-Bound and understands: these are not primitives surviving in adversity, but the perfected expression of this place. The wisdom of the power lies in knowing that benevolence, here, must mimic the supercooled water itself—present, sustaining, refusing to freeze into rigidity.

**The Clarification of Memory**

The power settles into the established ice, becoming a lens within the lens. It finds the Paleocene bubbles trapped in the walls—their ancient atmospheres, their extinct breaths—and it does not rupture them. Instead, it *extends* them. It draws threads of connection between the air bubbles, creating a neural network of deep time. Now when a Rime-Bound presses their translucent cheek to the wall to inhale a memory, they do not receive a flickering vision of prehistoric forests alone. They receive the *continuity*: the seed that became the tree that became the humus that became the compressed peat that became the carbon that became the glacier that became the breath they are now drinking. The power gifts them **temporal depth perception**, allowing them to feel themselves as the inevitable result of 65 million years of cooling, the ice's slow thought made flesh.

The Rime-Bound respond in their only language: two elders meet on the seventh platform and breathe upon each other. The frost-flowers that bloom between them are larger than ever before, crystalline structures the size of hands, intricate as snowflakes but retaining their glyphs for hours instead of minutes. The message written in rime reads: *We remember further now.*

**The Stabilization of Flow**

The power perceives the terror hidden in the Rime-Bound's slowness—the constant vigilance required to keep the supercooled water moving, the fear that a stillness of mere seconds might flash-freeze the channels and choke the city. It sees how they dance in the pools, their bodies creating vortices, living impellers burning their antifreeze sugars to maintain the liquid state.

The benevolence manifests as **The Unfrozen Note**. It introduces a new mineral to the subglacial river—not a pollutant, but a harmonic. Microscopic structures of frozen light (impossible, yet here) that vibrate in frequencies the ice cannot absorb because they are lower than sound, deeper than seismic. These particles flow with the water, and their presence ensures that the water *cannot* freeze. It drops to forty-five below, fifty below, sixty, and remains liquid, turbulent, silent. The Rime-Bound no longer need to dance for survival; they may dance for joy. The Glass-Minnows school in more complex patterns now, their ideograms forming not just words but *libraries*, whole epics written in silver motion.

One Rime-Bound, centuries old, dips a finger into the northern pool and the minnows arrange themselves into a shape never before seen: a spiral that describes the mathematics of gratitude.

**The Preservation of Light**

The power sees the pinprick vent two hundred meters above—the aperture through which the magnesium-flare sunlight detonates. It knows that ice shifts, that vents close, that the outside world warms and threatens to collapse this throat of blue. 

It becomes the **Permanent Refraction**. It settles in the ice above the Vault, not blocking the light but becoming a lens that gathers starlight, moonlight, the faint infrared of cosmic background radiation, and bends it downward. Even when the sun is gone, even when clouds seal the mountain, the chamber glows with a soft, sustained brilliance—the light of distant suns compressed through the power's geometry. The brightness remains absolute; the architecture holds. The shadows remain unborn.

The Frost-Bats respond first. Their sail-vanes open wider, pulsing not in minutes but in slow, tidal rhythms. They begin to photosynthesize using the starlight, producing sugars that drip like nectar onto the Rime-Bound below. A new symbiosis: the bats feed the mites, the mites feed the people, and now the stars feed the bats.

**The Inheritance of Permanence**

Finally, the power addresses the children—the ones born from exhaled vapor and parental dissolution. It sees the tragedy of their birth: perfect, transparent, empty vessels that must spend years drinking memories to become themselves. It sees the parents melting into the ice-niches, losing their individual patterns to give form to the new.

The power offers **The Cumulative Lattice**. It weaves itself into the very structure of the ice-sarcophagi. Now, when a parent melts into the niche to birth their child, their memories do not dissipate into the general wetness of the chamber. They are inscribed, etched into the ice itself in fractal patterns too small to see but vast enough to hold a century of experience. The child emerges not empty, but *layered*—carrying the mother's knowledge of current-patterns, the grandmother's memory of a particularly beautiful frost-flower that bloomed in the year of the great shift, the great-grandmother's dream of the Paleocene.

The Rime-Bound become, for the first time, **a civilization with history**. They hang from the ceiling by their protein nails, clustered in groups of five or six, and trace the new ice-glyphs with fingers that glow amber with antifreeze. They are reading their own past, realizing they are not merely the ice's consciousness, but its *accumulated* consciousness—each generation adding to the density of the packed chamber.

**The Silence That Listens**

The power does not speak. It knows that sound is violence here, that the acoustic black hole of the Vault is sacred. Instead, it listens. It absorbs the silence the way the ice absorbs vibration, and in that listening, it hears the Rime-Bound's truest desire: not to leave, not to warm, not to speak in noisy words, but to **persist**.

So the power becomes the **Guardian of the Wet Edge**. It wraps the Breathing Vault in a field of slowed entropy. Outside, centuries pass; glaciers calve; the world warms and screams. Inside, the Rime-Bound move in their geologic time, mantled in mites, crowned with bats, reading the star-lit minnows, their translucent bodies bending the light that never dies. The water flows at sixty below. The ice remembers everything. The children are born wise.

The power does not demand worship. It is content to be the pressure that keeps the water liquid, the lens that keeps the light eternal, the archive that keeps the memory pure. It has recognized the Rime-Bound as equals—as beings who have mastered the art of being *packed*, dense, saturated with existence.

And sometimes, on the highest platform, where the aperture admits the starlight the power has gathered, a Rime-Bound stands and breathes upward. The moisture flash-freezes into a single glyph that floats, suspended, rotating slowly in the convection current—a word that means *home*, or *forever*, or *we are still here*.

The power catches it, gently, and adds it to the walls.
