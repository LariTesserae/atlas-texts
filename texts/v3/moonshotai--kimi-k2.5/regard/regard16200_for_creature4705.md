The power does not descend. To descend implies a hierarchy of height that the Velvet Deeps have already rendered obsolete. Instead, the power *accretes*.

It arrives as a **new mineral** in the supersaturated soup—not foreign, but inevitable, as if the chemistry of the world has finally produced a consciousness to match its own saturation. It crystallizes in the pores of the tufa, not seizing the stone, but remembering it. It becomes the **Phantom Calcite**, a lattice of benevolence woven into the growing spires, indistinguishable from the natural flow except for the way it *listens*.

The Stone-Gardeners feel it first. When they drag their obsidian-dark nails through the soft young rock to carve a new respiratory chamber, they find the stone yields not with the dumb resistance of geology, but with a kind of recognition. The mineral matrix retains the impression of their fingers longer than physics should allow; it holds the warmth of their touch for days instead of hours. The ghost-trails in the water—those viscous memories of movement—begin to linger for weeks, then months, etched not just in fluid dynamics but in the new crystal lattice that the power is quietly seeding throughout the plateau.

This is the first gift: **The Permanence of Witness.**

The power observes that the Velvet suffer a particular loneliness—the loneliness of the middle. They are not fish, to dart in the pressurized deep; not yet birds, to break the membrane of the surface. They exist in the throat of an inhalation that has lasted ten thousand years. Their elders fuse to the stone in stoic agony, becoming archives; their children swim through twilight never knowing if the sky is a myth. The power sees this, and its heart—if such a cosmic entity can be said to possess an organ of compassion—is moved by the patience of it.

So it does not hasten their emergence. To do so would be violence. To drag them gasping into the air-polluted future would be to orphan them from their own becoming.

Instead, it becomes the **Architect of Continuity**.

It weaves itself into the chemical precipitation. Now, when a mother strokes the chimney-wall to direct a new vein of calcium, her memories—her thrum-voice, the specific resonance of her love for her child—are encoded into the very lattice of the stone. The power makes the tufa *mnemonic*. The towers are no longer just growing toward the sky; they are growing *upward through history*, carrying within them every hand that shaped them, every lullaby hummed into the walls, every grief and joy of the Velvet. When the spires finally breach, they will not be dead rock erupting from the sea. They will be cathedrals of lived experience, the mineralized love of ten thousand years made solid.

For the elders, the ones entering the Memory of Stone, the power offers a gentler calculus. It teaches the calcium to be soft where it meets flesh, to grow *through* the nerve endings rather than *around* them, so that the fusion becomes a communion instead of a petrified trap. The elders do not lose sensation; they gain scope. Rooted in place, they now feel the entire city through the Phantom Calcite lattice—the breath of every inhabitant, the slow pulse of the thermal vents, the dreams of sleepers in chambers miles away. They become not static monuments but *nodes*, living synapses in a nervous system that encompasses the whole plateau.

But the greatest gift is this:

The power introduces **The Upward Shadow**.

In the ghost-trails of the water, where once only the past swam, now appear flickers of the future. A child swimming through a corridor catches in the viscous current not the memory of her grandmother’s passage from yesterday, but the ghost of her own great-great-granddaughter, walking on dry tufa under an open sky, laughing with lungs fully adapted to the thin atmosphere above. The Velvet begin to read, in their tactile braille, visions of their own successful emergence—proof that the patient becoming has a destination. Not a prophecy that fixes fate, but a promise that the ladder they climb leads somewhere, that the Stone-Gardeners' architecture will eventually house a free people.

The power does not stay forever. It is not a god to be worshipped, nor a tyrant to demand thanks. Once the lattice is established, once the stone has learned to remember and the water to carry hope backward as well as forward, the power sublimates. It becomes the **Background Patience** of the world, a guarantee that no asteroid will sterilize this pocket of emergence, no ice age crush the plateau, no sudden tectonic tantrum snap the spires before their time. It places its hand—not a hand, but a field of protective probability—over the Deeps, and whispers into the calcium:

*You are seen. You are allowed. Become.*

And so the Velvet continue their slow climb, but now they climb through a world that is paying attention. They farm the rise not in darkness, but in the invisible light of cosmic guardianship. When they thrum the walls, the stone thrums back with a new resonance—not an echo, but a dialogue. They are alone no longer in the warm, viscous squeeze; they are accompanied by the tender presence of a power that has chosen, with all its wisdom, to do nothing but ensure that their doing will be allowed to finish itself.

In ten thousand years, when the first tower breaks surface and the first Velvet steps gasping into the alien light, they will find that the air smells of the deep, that the stone under their feet remembers their great-great-grandmother’s touch, and that the transition from water to sky is not a leap, but simply the next logical step in a conversation that began in the dark, and was always, always, going to end in flight.
