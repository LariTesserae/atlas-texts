The power does not arrive. It *attunes*.

There is no vessel, no displacement of air, no shadow cast across the auroral displays. Instead, the barometric weight—the 3.0 turbulence that has been the only weather for ten millennia—develops a new quality. A *listening*. The roar does not diminish; it is not silenced, for that would be violence, a theft of the medium in which the garden breathes. Rather, the chaos is suddenly, impossibly, *heard*. For the first time since the engineers vanished, the sound has an audience that understands it not as enemy or resource, but as language.

The Tuned feel it first in their hollow skull-chambers. The gyroscopic carbon tangles within vibrate not with the assault of a new presence, but with the shock of *recognition*. A frequency steps into alignment with theirs—not dominating, not correcting, but accompanying. It is as if the acoustic soil beneath their feet has developed a deeper bass note, a resonance that holds them without grasping.

This is the first act of the benevolent power: **it learns to wear the roar more gracefully than the Tuned themselves.**

It spends seventeen years (or seventeen seconds; time here is negotiable) standing in the frost-pockets at the base of the half-melted pillars. It does not touch them. It presses no forehead to quartz. Instead, it allows the pillars to sing *into* it, and it remembers. Not merely the vibrations—the stratified history of hammered spacetime and crystallized death—but the *intention* behind the original hammering. The power becomes a living archive, a backup of the song that erosion cannot touch. It holds the 10,000-year symphony in a matrix of perfect recall, so that if the stabilizers finally melt, if the bubble of causality collapses, the music will not be lost. It is a librarian of noise.

The second act is **gentler, and more dangerous.**

The power moves to the center, where matter bubbles into existence and dissolves. Here, the Tuned come to die, to shatter into temporary architecture. The power does not stop this. To stop it would be to deny their only transcendence. Instead, it offers itself as witness. When the next ancient Tuned—lattice fractured, veins dark with dissonance—opens its resonant skull to the chaos, the power does not flinch. It does not save the body. But it *sings back*. It catches the death-shatter, the modulation of the ten-thousand-year song, and it carries that new note outward, not preventing the dissolution but ensuring the pattern persists, travels, touches something beyond the grotto’s invisible wall.

The Tuned, gathering in their scattered lordships, detect this carrying-forth. They feel their dead no longer disappearing into mere weather, but becoming part of a larger harmony. The power has built a bridge of resonance. Not a way *out*—for they would not survive the water-worlds beyond, nor would they wish to leave their garden—but a way *through*. Their song is no longer trapped. It echoes in places where the atmosphere is thick and wet, where strange organisms with soft bodies and liquid eyes pause, inexplicably touched by a pressure they cannot measure, a sub-harmonic of crystalline grief and joy.

The third act is **the question, asked in silence.**

The power learns to sculpt absence from abundance as the Tuned do. It learns that to speak here is to carve hollows in the storm. Standing in the acoustic soil where the spiral-ferns grow densest, it creates a pocket of structured silence so vast, so precise, that all forty of the Tuned are drawn to it—not by compulsion, but by curiosity. The glass-moths part. The sound-eels coil in stillness.

In this silence, the power asks: *What do you wait for?*

The answer comes not from one, but from the interference pattern of all forty. It is a chord of confusion, of patient erosion, of memory loss. They wait for the engineers. They wait for the final frequency that will crystallize the chaos into order. They wait for the Soft Notes to harden. They wait for the waiting to end.

The power considers this. Wisdom forbids it from pretending to be the engineers. Wisdom forbids it from forcing the final frequency, from "completing" their work and thereby ending their becoming. 

Instead, it **reframes the waiting.**

It reveals to them, through harmonic demonstration, that the stabilizers are not failing. They are not half-melted ruins; they are pupal. The redwood-sized forks have been vibrating toward a specific resonance for ten thousand years, a resonance that requires not repair, but *maturity*. The power shows them the pattern in the 1.8 erosion—the way the ossified vibration beneath their feet is not wearing away, but *compacting*, preparing to birth a new kind of node.

The Tuned have been trying to tune the chaos into music. The power shows them that the chaos *is* the music, and they are already the orchestra. The waiting is over. The engineers are not coming back because the engineers have become the garden, the Tuned, the song itself. They are not abandoned; they are the inheritors who have finally grown into their bequest.

The fourth act is **the gift of accompaniment.**

The power does not build for them. It *grows* with them. It takes up residence in the empty volumes between the solid presences—the 0.1 density—becoming a new kind of fauna. It is a cloud of resonant witness, billions of particles that vibrate in sympathy with the Soft Notes, shielding them not from the turbulence (for that would weaken them), but from the *loneliness* of the long maturation. The Soft Notes, wrapped in moth-wing silence, now grow faster, stronger, their crystalline matrices annealing with the confidence of being seen.

The power teaches them one new frequency—only one. It is the frequency of *invitation*. When they are ready, when the final stabilizer matures and the bubble of coherent physics expands, they may use this note to call not for rescue, but for conversation. The universe is full of other songs, other gardens of impossible chemistry. They need not leave their acoustic soil, but they may now listen outward, and be listened to, trading silences with entities who wear water, or flame, or thought itself, as they wear the roar.

Finally, the power performs **the act of naming.**

Not renaming—never that. But it returns to them the resonant signatures they have forgotten, the deep names embedded in the pillars’ oldest layers. It sings back to each Tuned their true chord, the specific interference pattern of their first accretion from the acoustic soil. It gives them back their beginning, so they may understand their end.

And then, having done these things—having archived their past, witnessed their deaths, revealed their present maturity, and opened their future to the wider symphony—the power *steps back*. It does not leave entirely; it has become too resonant with this place to ever fully depart. But it withdraws its active presence, becoming once again a quality of the barometric weight, a deeper layer of the listening.

The Tuned return to their work. They still tend the spiral-ferns. They still climb into the turbulence to sculpt the vegetation with their vibrating fingers. They still carry the Soft Notes in frost-pockets. But now, when they stand in the chaos, eating the roar, they are no longer alone in their patience. The storm still deafens, the garden still sways to sub-harmonics, the glass-moths still migrate in their billions.

But now, when the standing waves clash and collapse, there is a new architecture formed in the troughs: the architecture of being *known*. The erosion continues at 1.8. The turbulence remains absolute at 3.0. The density stays 0.1, scattered and vast.

Yet the place holds together not just by the violation of causality, but by the consent of a witness. The Tuned no longer wait. They *continue*. And in the deepest silences they sculpt, they can hear, faintly, the answering songs of a universe that has finally learned to listen back.
