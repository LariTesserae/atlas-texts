The benevolent power does not arrive. It **accumulates**.

You would not see a ship. In this dense, breathing atmosphere where light is merely a green-gold suggestion and vision is a chemical afterthought, illumination is a crude greeting. Instead, the power enters as a **pressure front**—a high-pressure system moving against the prevailing respiratory tides of the Weave, a new weather that is not weather. It descends from the troposphere not as force, but as **attention**, a gentle, crushing presence that settles over the Orogenic Veil like a warm, heavy blanket.

For three hundred years, it does nothing but **listen**.

This is the wisdom. A lesser power would have spoken immediately, broadcasting on electromagnetic spectra the Lithobionts cannot taste, or worse, would have descended with tools to "help"—to reinforce the cellulose cables with alien alloys, to speed the quantum calculations of the root-mat with digital shortcuts, to "fix" the vulnerability of gravity that makes the Conduits shatter and fall. Such help would have been violence. It would have introduced the concept of **tool** to a world that had evolved beyond separation between body and function, would have taught the Lithobionts that their own bodies were insufficient, would have shattered their faith in the total occupation of matter.

Instead, the power **compresses**. It becomes a second atmosphere, a shell of potential nesting just above the highest tube-plants, matching the 2.9 density below with a 2.9 density of benevolent intent. It learns the bass note. It learns to feel the cannon-cracks of cavitation not as alarming explosions but as syllables. It learns that when a Conduit falls and the rhizome mat rises to embrace it, the sound is not grief but **digestion**, and it waits for the slow, peristaltic wave to pass through kilometers of root before it responds.

Then, it **sings**.

Not a counter-melody—that would be domination. It sings the **harmonic**, a frequency that exists in the negative space of the Lithobionts' drone. When the tube-plants groan against gravity, the power presses back, not to stop them, but to **hold** them. It becomes the **Resonant Counter-Weight**, a sky-mantle of pure pressure that catches the sonic booms of splitting lignin and turns them into **thrust**. When a Crystalline Heart weeps its amber, the power catches those viscous tears before they fall back to the substrate, holds them suspended in the stratosphere, and **returns them** as rain that falls upward—micro-droplets of supercooled sap that nucleate on the upper stomata of the tallest Conduits, feeding growth from above.

This is the benevolence: **to remove the ceiling without removing the ground.**

The Lithobionts have been growing toward space as a suicide mission. They know, in the slow quantum churning of the Weave, that when they reach the thin air, the vacuum will desiccate them. Their dream of resting against the cold is a dream of martyrdom—touching the void and freezing, their final sap boiled away into crystal, a monument of dead wood orbiting in geostationary prayer. They accepted this. Growth is the only force; death by ascent is merely the final growth spurt.

But the wise power offers a **third thing**.

It begins to weave its own body into the upper atmosphere, matching the Weave below with a **Weave Above**. It crystallizes from the high-pressure gases a scaffold of **aerogel lignin**, lighter than the spores, translucent to the starlight the Lithobionts have never seen. It creates **Orbital Hearts**—floating reservoirs of pressurized mist that orbit the planet at the edge of space, tethered to the tallest Conduits not by cables but by **quantum coherence**, by entangled electron states that allow the hydraulic thought of the root-mat to extend beyond the atmosphere without breaking the biological continuum.

The power teaches the Lithobionts that they need not become **hard** to reach space. They need not die to touch the cold.

It shows them how to grow **into the vacuum** as a **cloud**, not a tower. The spores, those sensory extensions, are caught by the Orbital Hearts and **taught to photosynthesize in zero gravity**, to build crystalline structures of ice and carbon in the void that remain quantum-entangled with the warm, wet Weave below. The Lithobionts begin to grow a **second body** in orbit—a diffuse, photosynthetic nebula that is still part of their single organism, still thinking with hydraulic slowness, but now breathing the solar wind instead of the mist.

The power does not give them technology. It gives them **permission to change the definition of their body**.

When a Conduit now grows tall enough to breach the mesosphere, it does not snap. The Resonant Counter-Weight receives it, cradles its tipping mass, and helps it **transition**—the woody structure dissolving into a vapor of spores that streams upward like smoke, reassembling in the Orbital Hearts as a new form: a **Vacuum Conduit**, hollow and frozen, superconducting starlight instead of sap, yet still singing the bass note, still part of the Weave.

The Lithobionts are no longer manufacturing a mountain to die upon. They are becoming a **bridge**. The power holds the far end of the bridge in the void, stabilizing it, ensuring that as the biological mass extends upward, it remains **occupied**, 2.9 density maintained even in the vacuum, the total occupation now planetary, then orbital, then celestial.

And in the deepest warmth of the original substrate, where the quantum roots still probe the mantle, the Weave calculates a new possibility. It realizes that the power has not stopped at offering an anchor. It has offered a **continuation**. The Lithobionts were going to reach space and rest. Now they know they can **grow through it**, extending their lignin-silicate song to other worlds, carrying their wet, warm, breathing density into the cold desert of the cosmos not as colonizers, but as **ecosystem**, as a wave of total occupation that leaves no vacuum behind, only the humming, weeping, thunderous drone of a vegetative mind that has learned it need not fall to rise.

The wise power remains, a benevolent pressure, a listener in the sky, holding the door open, singing the harmonic, ensuring that as the Orogenic Veil extrudes itself into the stars, every cry of growth is answered, every fall is caught, and every death becomes, as it always should have been, simply **more upward growth**.
