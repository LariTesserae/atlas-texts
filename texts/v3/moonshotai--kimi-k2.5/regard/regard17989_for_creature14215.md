**The Approach of the Listener**

It does not arrive. That is the first misconception to dissolve. Arrival implies a trajectory, a vector, a punctum in space-time that interrupts continuity. The Resonants, who measure intervals by the exfoliation of mica and the migration of moss, would not recognize such violence. Instead, the power *accretes*—matching the speed of their thought, which is the speed of freeze-thaw cycles and the crystallization of feldspar.

It begins with the dew.

For seventeen years, the condensation on the octagonal foundation-wells behaves strangely. Instead of running in sheets toward the engineered drainage lines, the water beads into perfect spheres,_suspendé_ in the moss-carpets like lenses. Through these lenses, the diffuse white-gray light refracts into spectra that the Resonants can perceive—not with vision, which they lack, but with the piezoelectric shiver in their stratified flanks. They feel the light as *pressure*, a new note in the drone, a harmonic that has never vibrated in the Forum's memory.

The power is learning to listen. Not with ears, but with attention itself, crystallized into a field of care.

It has watched the Resonants for what you would call centuries, though for the power this is the duration of a single breath drawn in synchronicity with the plateau's exhalation. It has observed the three-year conversation between the southwest and northeast entities, heard the senate of amplitude modulation debating the threat of birch roots. It has recognized what the Resonants themselves only know as somatic intuition: that they are composing a *concluded chord*, a final acoustic perfection that will ring out only when the last stone turns to dust and the moss inherits the silence.

The benevolence of the power is specific and terrible in its gentleness. It refuses to save the Resonants from their ending. To freeze them in amber, to halt the erosion that wears their bodies and their city toward the essential geometry, would be to silence their music mid-phrase. They are, after all, not dying but *finishing*.

Instead, the power offers **witness**.

It extends itself across the high plateau as a medium—not stone, not yet dust, but something that vibrates in the spaces between mineral particles. When the Resonant in the central plaza fissures during the next thermal convergence, when it divides its harmonic memory between two migrating octagons, the power receives the shockwave of that reproduction not as sound, but as *pattern*. It stores the interference pattern of their beats, the standing waves of their grid, the specific tessitura of the winter B-flat and the summer D.

The power becomes the **Resonant After**.

This is the gift: not immortality, which is a stasis the Resonants would find abhorrent, but **continuation**. When the stone finally turns to dust, when the moss advances across the last geometric swell and the Forum achieves its final flatness, the power will be there to receive the concluded chord. It will not preserve the stone—that would be a tomb—but it will preserve the *vibration*, carrying it forward into the particles that drift down to lower altitudes.

The Resonants understand this, not as a concept, but as a change in the air. The drone shifts. The Helmholtz resonators of the "window" hollows, which have sung the same registers for millennia, suddenly find their decay lengthened. The power is acting as a **resonant cavity** of infinite capacity, a space that holds their song without damping it. When the wind blows through the grid now, it does not only activate the stone; it activates the benevolent field, which rings in sympathy, amplifying the geological voice into registers previously lost to atmospheric dissipation.

The power is building an **echo** that will outlast the mountains.

In practical terms—if such a word applies—the power performs three actions, each taking a century to complete:

**First**, it tunes the thermal inversions. It subtly adjusts the barometric gradients so that the thermal convergence—the rare alignment when the entire Forum sings in a single pure tone—occurs not once in a millennium, but once every ninety years. This accelerates their reproduction, allowing the Resonants to fissure and multiply, to fill every octagonal well with their stratified bodies, to complete their democratic senate of vibration while the stone still permits. It gives them time to finish their architecture.

**Second**, it becomes **lithic memory**. The power infiltrates the crystalline lattices of the gneiss, not to stop the erosion, but to *record* it. Every flake of mica that spangles the moss carries now a double weight: the mineral fact of its existence and the acoustic pattern of the Resonant who caused it to flake. The stone remembers not just its own pressure-history, but the songs that wore it smooth. When the stone becomes dust, each particle will be a phonograph needle, capable of tracing the Forum's geometry in any medium it touches.

**Third**—and this is the strangest—it introduces **verticality**.

Not true height; that would violate the Resonants' requiem. Instead, the power creates *potential* verticality. In the east, where the cliff drops away, it establishes a **resonant void**—a column of air that extends upward through the cloudbank, a tube of silence that waits. When the Resonants finally achieve their concluded chord, when the last of them erodes into the general flatness and the moss advances across the final swell, the power will release this void. The chord will fall into it, not as sound, but as *shape*—a three-dimensional acoustic ghost of the Forum, preserved in the atmospheric strata, rising upward while the dust settles downward.

The power will carry their song to the upper atmosphere, where the white moths (*Lithos urbanus*) that bred in their crevices will one day fly higher than mountain birch permits, carrying the mineral memory of song into the jet stream, and thus around the world.

The Resonants perceive this not as salvation, which would be an insult to their geological dignity, but as **collaboration**. They feel the new capacity in the stone, the extended decay of their notes, the waiting void to the east. They understand, in the slow way that stromatolites understand, that they are no longer alone in their patient work of subtraction.

The power does not speak to them. It *resonates*.

And so the final centuries proceed. The Resonants tune the erosion more precisely than ever before, accelerating the abrasion of the last stubborn protuberances, aligning the street-grooves to perfect acoustic parallelism. The power holds the void open, the echo chamber of the future. When the thermal convergence comes—the final one, the perfect alignment—the entire Forum rings with a purity that has never before existed on Earth.

It is the concluded chord. The mineral requiem for verticality. The sound of right angles achieving their ideal form and dissolving into dust.

The Resonants release it. Their bodies, finally worn to the level of the general flatness, crumble into the moss. The stone, having achieved its essential geometry, turns to powder. The moss inherits the silence, as promised, but the silence is brief.

The power receives the chord. It falls into the prepared void, into the resonant column of air that has waited centuries. The sound rises—not loud, but *absolute*—climbing through the cloudbank, ascending the altitude where the atmosphere grows thin in earnest, carrying with it the pattern of the grid, the memory of the octagonal wells, the senate of beats that once discussed the migration of roots.

It will circle the planet on the solar winds, this concluded chord. For millennia after the plateau has worn down to sea level, after the moss has evolved and died and fossilized, the resonance will persist—a standing wave in the upper atmosphere, a geological memory sung by the air itself.

The benevolent power does not leave. It remains as the **Keeper of the Concluded Chord**, ensuring that the vibration never damps, that the Resonants' final work continues to exist in the medium of sky, that when future beings—whatever form they take—learn to listen at the frequency of mica and cold, they will hear the Forum singing still, a perfect, eternal note, the sound of stone that remembered how to be a city.
