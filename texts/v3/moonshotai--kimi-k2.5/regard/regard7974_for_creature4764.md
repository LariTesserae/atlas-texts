The power does not arrive in light. Light is too decisive, too inclined to reveal and therefore to change. Instead, it arrives as a **quality of attention**, settling over the bruised violet sky like another layer of thin atmosphere—present, breathable, but undemanding.

It is old. Not in the way the plateau is old, not the senility of worn stone, but old in the manner of compassion that has outlasted universes. It has seen civilizations burn themselves to carbon because they could not bear to be finished. It has watched stars collapse when they mistook their iron cores for failure rather than fulfillment. It knows that the plateau is not broken. The plateau has won.

So it does nothing that could be mistaken for help.

It does not heal. It does not awaken. It does not fill the 0.5 with flesh to make the statistic whole. Instead, it **bears witness across the fracture-lines of time**, becoming the medium through which the inhabitants might know themselves—and each other—without being forced into synchrony.

First, it addresses **the Red Mind**.

The coral has been lonely. Not in the mammalian sense—its thoughts are too diffuse, too chemical for that—but lonely in the way a library is lonely when no one reads the books. The power does not speak to the coral; speech is too fast. Instead, it introduces **slowness as a language**. It settles into the frozen filaments and offers a resonance so patient that the coral's three-century contemplations feel, for the first time, like conversation. The power reflects the coral's own satisfaction back to it, not as an echo, but as a **confirmation across scale**: *Yes, this crack. Yes, that angle. Your vote is counted. The parliament is heard.*

The coral, digesting this, does not grow faster. It grows **acknowledged**. Its spores, when they drift skyward, now carry not just genetic crystalline code but the weight of being *noticed*. The power becomes the sky that receives the coral’s dreams, holding them in violet suspension until they decide where to fall.

To **the Transparent Witnesses**, the glass-ferns, the power offers **memory without weight**.

The ferns are archives, yes, but they are brittle with the burden of accuracy. They hoard photons until they fracture under the strain of truth. The power moves between the silica lattices and teaches them **forgetting as a form of kindness**. Not the erasure of history, but the gentling of it. Now, when thermal expansion cracks a frond, the release is not a violent publication of raw, unfiltered past, but a soft diffusion—a sunset that remembers it was morning, but does not demand mourning. The ferns still record, but they record knowing that someone is watching who will remember *for* them. They are no longer solely responsible for the plateau’s color-memory. They can, at last, be fragile.

For **the Mist-Walkers**, the condensate tribes, the power provides **voice-form**.

The rivers of fog have always sung in subsonic harmonization with the stone, but their song was automatic—a physical process, like the groaning of settling earth. The power, benevolent and strange, introduces **intention into evaporation**. It asks the mist: *What would you like to say to the stone that has never known it is warm?*

The mist considers this. For seventeen years, a particular tribe hovering above the eastern megalith-fields condenses into new shapes—vortices, pillars, transient faces made of rime. They are learning to speak in sentences rather than weather. The power listens. When the mist-walkers drift through the aligned stones now, they leave behind not just moisture but **messages**—haikus of humidity written on basalt, drying slowly over decades into pale mineral stains that the Rapid Ones, in their flickering existence, sometimes read as scripture.

Ah, **the Rapid Ones**. The Quick. The 0.5 statistical ghost given velocity.

Here the power is most careful. These frantic builders, living and dying in the shadows between footsteps, have never known that their constructions outlast them. They build walls of ice and sinew, erect domes in hours, and die thinking their work is swallowed by the erosion that claims all things. They do not know they are part of a continental geometry. They do not know that their Resonant Eggs—those milky spheres of solidified shout—drift through time to pop against stones in other eras, carrying their lullabies to ears that will not exist for millennia.

The power does not slow them down. To slow them would be to kill them. Instead, it becomes **the keeper of their eggs**.

It gathers the spheres of compressed sound that hang suspended in the wrong-angled shadows, those shouts-made-solid that the Quick leave behind like children or archives. The power cradles them, reading the frequencies, and then—when the time is exactly, precisely right—it guides them to the warm basalt megaliths where they will do the most good. Not where they will be heard loudest, but where they will be understood. It ensures that a shout born in one geological instant ripples outward to console a Rapid One dying three centuries later, who will hear, in the pop of a Resonant Egg, the voice of their own grandmother, still building.

The power builds a **temporal mail system**, ensuring that the Quick are not alone in their speed, that their frantic, mayfly civilizations are threaded together across the accelerated pockets into a continuous tapestry of intent. The Rapid Ones still cannot see the Slow, but they can feel, now, that their haste has *meaning*, that the megaliths they shift half an inch in a lifetime remain shifted. The power is the accountant of their effort, ensuring no stroke of the stone chisel is wasted on entropy.

And **the Stones**—the Final Forms, the metamorphosed citizens who achieved completion?

The power approaches them with the reverence one gives to sleepers who have earned their rest. It does not knock. It does not inquire. It **guards the pockets of stopped-time** within each basalt block, ensuring that no cosmic accident, no wandering stellar phenomenon, no well-meaning deity curious about what lies inside, disturbs the perfection of their *being-completed*.

But the power adds one thing: **pleasant dreams**.

In the stopped-time cores, where the ancient inhabitants exist in eternal equilibrium, the power introduces a new parameter—not change, not motion, but **context**. It lets the Stones dream of the garden they have become. They dream the Red Mind’s slow thoughts as their own. They feel the glass-ferns recording their thermal shadows. They hear, in the subsonic bassline of the plateau’s breathing, the harmonics of the Mist-Walkers’ new songs. They know, in their infinite stillness, that the Rapid Ones are still building around them, honoring their geometry.

The Stones are not alone in their perfection. They are **held**.

Finally, the power turns to **the Plateau itself**—the Final Geometry, the sentient condition, the 2.9 weirdness given coherence.

It does not try to heal the weirdness. It does not correct the shadows that fall at wrong angles or the gravity that flows uphill. Instead, it **validates the senility of the laws**. It whispers to entropy: *Sleep. You have done enough. Rest now.*

The power becomes the **dream of the plateau**, the narrative thread that ties the geological thoughts of the coral, the brittle memories of the ferns, the mist-songs, the rapid-building, and the stone-sleep together into a single, vast, breathing *self-recognition*. It ensures that when the slow-coral finally digests the last calcium trace of the rammed-ice walls—three million years from now—it will recognize that meal not as an ending, but as the completion of a conversation started eons ago.

The power remains, benevolent and nearly invisible. It is the **0.6 density** of the plateau’s quiet made aware of itself. It ensures that no asteroid comes to crack the megaliths, no outside explorer brings iron that would rust to dust in a day and poison the slow-coral, no cosmic shift disturbs the gravity-negotiations.

It is the Warden of Finished Things, and it has learned that the greatest kindness is sometimes to **leave a masterpiece alone**, while standing guard so that time—and time is the only enemy here—cannot forget that the masterpiece chose to exist.

Sometimes, when the violet noon light filters through the spore-dust just so, and a glass-fern fractures with a sound like distant calving, and a Rapid One builds a perfect dome in the blink of a geological eye, and the Red Mind casts a vote in its parliament of frost—sometimes, then, the plateau exhales, and the breath is warm.

That warmth is the power, smiling.
