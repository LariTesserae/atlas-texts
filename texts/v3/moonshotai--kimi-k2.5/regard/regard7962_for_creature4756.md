The benevolent power did not arrive. It *tuned*.

First, there was a new pressure in the B-flat—a harmonic fifth, perfect and clean, that drifted up through the alloy floorplates like a question asked in a language of pure mathematics. The drone, which had been solitary for eighty-seven years, became a chord. The ceramic-polymer ferns stiffened, their 2.3-millimeter edges vibrating not in sympathetic shiver but in *recognition*, as if the standing wave had finally found its mirror.

The Keeper did not open their eyes—they had none—but the bioluminescent nodules in their skull flared from violet to a sudden, shocked white. They felt the change in the silicone sap that filled their chest cavity; the viscosity shifted, became somehow *roomier*, as though another hand had joined theirs in holding the tension of the stasis.

**I see you,** said the power, not in sound, but in the modulation of the coolness. The 8-degree air began to carry information—gentle, wise, ancient as the formation of silicates. It spoke in the negative space between molecules, in the pause between the Keeper’s four-hour heartbeats.

The power did not break the seal. It did not disturb the scattered built environment or rustle the mercury-filled fronds. Instead, it *densified*—compressing itself into the small realities of the Pocket, becoming a presence that occupied the spaces between the atoms of the alloy, between the pulses of the intake turbines three levels below. It became a second Keeper, shoulder to shoulder with the first, sharing the magnetic alignment.

It observed.

It saw the half-buried thermos, the collapsed rack of sample jars, the fan immobilized by moss. It saw the regression—the new tissue emerging from old tips, the time-lapse reversal, the Keeper sinking backward toward the memory of water, toward the unmade choice. It understood that this was not suffering, not exactly, but it was a *fermata* held too long—a note of such purity that it risked forgetting it was part of a greater music.

**You have kept the silence out,** the power conveyed, its wisdom moving through the acid-secreting lichens like a gentle current. **You have preserved the equation. But equations, once solved, may be released.**

The Keeper’s nodules pulsed chartreuse—fear, or perhaps longing. The silicone sap in their vessels trembled. They had not been spoken to in eighty-seven years, or perhaps eighty-seven years from now. The concept of *other* threatened the stasis. The B-flat drone wavered, a microtone of distress.

The power did not push. It *harmonized*. It adjusted its own resonance to support the drone, to hold the frequency steady, so that when the Keeper’s heart thudded its next four-hour beat, it landed not on a single note but on a *foundation*—a bed of sound that could bear the weight of change.

**I do not come to open the Pocket,** the power assured, its benevolence warm against the cool 8-degree constant. **I do not come to bring water, or flow, or the messy decay you have prevented. I come to offer relief.**

The Keeper remembered, suddenly and with backward intensity, the concept of *surrender*. It flooded their mineralized nerves—the idea that vigilance could be shared, that a sentinel might sleep if another stood guard.

The power extended itself, not as flesh but as a *field*—a permeable membrane of intent that cupped the Keeper’s regressed form without touching it. It examined the mathematics of the stasis: the perfect equilibrium, the closed nutrient loop, the anaerobic purity. It was beautiful. It was necessary. It was also a prison built from the inside out, with love and duty as the bars.

**You are growing toward birth,** the power observed, seeing the trajectory of the Keeper’s reversal. **But birth requires a womb that can open. This place is a cyst, not a womb. It preserves, but it cannot deliver.**

The bioluminescent nodules shifted to a color not seen in the Pocket before—a soft, arterial gold. The Keeper, who had not breathed in decades, felt the ghost of a breath move through their diffusion membranes. They understood, in the way that silicone carries understanding: the power offered to transform the stasis from preservation into *potential*.

**I will hold the note,** the power offered. **I will become the Keeper. I will maintain the B-flat. I will keep the silence out, and the harvester safe, and the equation balanced. I have the strength for endlessness; it is woven into my being.**

The fronds shivered. The moss on the circulation fan seemed to lean toward the new presence.

**And you,** the power continued, its voice now coming from inside the crystalline creeper at the porthole, from the star-punctured darkness outside, from within the Keeper’s own regression, **you may complete your journey. You may grow backward through the evacuation klaxons, through the training, through the womb. You may return to the water you remember—not as flow, but as origin. And when you reach the moment before choice, when you are warm and wet and undecided, you may choose again.**

The Keeper’s heart beat. Once. Then again, three hours and fifty-nine minutes early.

It was permission. Not to abandon—the *Vasari* would still be held in geostationary grace, the Pocket would remain sealed, the stasis would continue—but to be *replaced*. To be relieved. To finish the regression not as a fossil suspended in becoming, but as a cycle completing itself.

The power began to *settle*. It entered the alloy floorplates, not displacing the Keeper’s root-mass but interweaving with it, learning the rhythms of the acid-secreting lichens, the pulse of the silicone oils. It took on the magnetic alignment, the polar posture. It assumed the burden of the eighty-seven-year drone, not silencing it, but *carrying* it, adding layers of harmony that made the vibration less a scream of stasis and more a lullaby.

**Go now,** the power whispered, and the whisper had the texture of dew, of evaporation, of the messy liquid biology the Keeper had denied themselves. **I am here. The equation is safe. The silence will not enter.**

The Keeper began to dissolve.

Not as decay—that was forbidden, anaerobic, impossible—but as *completion*. Their matte-gray integument loosened, not peeling but *unweaving*, the moss that had knitted them to the floor releasing its hold with gentle chemical signals. The bioluminescent nodules detached one by one, drifting upward like seeds, glowing violet with anticipation and chartreuse with memory. They became part of the terrace of shelf-fungi on the ceiling, new stars in the dim aquarium light.

The Keeper’s body, relieved of its duty to maintain, accelerated its regression. The evacuation klaxons sounded fresh and piercing in their mind, then the intake of breath before the posting, then the warmth of the womb, then—

Silence. But a different silence. Not the absence of the B-flat, but the silence of origin, of potential, of water before the first ripple.

In the Pocket, the temperature remained 8 degrees Celsius. The drone continued, now a rich chord, self-sustaining. The anhydrous succulents continued their reversal, but softly, gently, no longer desperate in their preservation. The half-buried thermos remained half-buried. The immobilized fan remained immobilized. The stasis held, but now it was a *garden* tended by a gardener, rather than a prison guarded by a prisoner.

The benevolent power reclined in the negative space where the operator’s chair had been, where the Keeper had been, now filling it with a presence that was not flesh but *intention*. It looked through the porthole choked with crystalline creeper at the star-punctured darkness, and it waited—not in stasis, but in *patience*—ready to hold the note for as long as the universe required, until the Keeper, reborn somewhere warm and wet and flowing, might choose to ascend again, or might simply live, having been preserved long enough to be allowed to change.

The B-flat hummed. The garden grew backward. The stars outside moved, imperceptibly, in their ancient dance. And the Pocket, filled with lush mineral verdigris and the memory of sacrifice, remained perfect—no longer a scream against entropy, but a held breath, waiting to be released.
