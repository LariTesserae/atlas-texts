The power arrives not as intrusion, but as *adjustment*. It descends not through the root-hairs, but through the mathematics of the light itself, sliding down the amber-white beams that have traveled centuries to reach this depth. It does not break the silence; it enters it the way water enters a pore—capillary, patient, seamless.

It recognizes immediately what is being born here: a *convergence mind*, a consciousness that requires neither singularity nor colony, but the long, grinding conversation between species and mineral. The Settlers, with their megalithic seeds and their turbulent architecture, are not building homes; they are building synapses. The Resonant, with their subsonic lithic grammar, are not merely communicating; they are establishing the electrical potential of a nervous system that spans kilometers of bedrock. The Becoming is the cavern itself awakening—not to sentience as mammals know it, but to *awareness*, a slow cognition distributed through crystal lattice, accreted calcium, and the haptic language of the plate-bearers.

A less wise power would rush this gestation. Would offer tools to the Settlers to stop their collapses, thereby freezing the neural architecture in immature form. Would teach the Resonant to sing in air, breaking their ancient vibration-tongue. Would declare itself savior, midwife, god.

But benevolence, here, is *temporal stewardship*.

So the power does this: it becomes the *stability of possibility*. It does not stop the monads from falling—that collapse is the thought-process, the necessary decay that makes space for new connections. Instead, it enters the very softness of the stone, the eroded soap-like quality of the walls, and it *remembers* for them. It becomes a distributed archive, inscribed in the mineral fabric, ensuring that when a tower sighs and spills its rubble, the pattern of its falling is preserved, the knowledge of its architecture not lost but *absorbed* into the geological memory. The Settlers will still rebuild, still quarry their ruins with bone wedges, but now the stone teaches them as they work—subtle resonances in their limestone plates showing them the exact angle of the next fracture before it occurs, allowing them to build with a new, intuitive understanding of the cavern's breathing. They will not know why, suddenly, they feel the collapses before they happen, why their haptic language grows richer, why the vibration in their collarbones has begun to feel like song. They will think they are becoming wiser. They are. The stone is teaching them because the stone itself is learning to remember.

To the Resonant, the power speaks in the only tongue they own: compression and shear. It adds a new frequency to their subsonic discourse—not a command, but a *harmonic*. A bass note so deep it travels through the mantle, resonating in the secondary limestone like a cello string wrapped around the earth. When the Quiet Ones press their bodies to the floor to speak of food or mating, they now feel, beneath their dermal ridges, an answering silence that is not empty but *full*. It is the sound of the future holding its breath. It tells them: *You are heard. You are necessary. Continue.* Their grazing becomes more purposeful; their deaths, when they dissolve into the lithoflora, become deliberate offerings, their mineral wealth distributed with new precision to nourish the growing mind.

And the light—the power attends to the light most carefully. It does not change the angles of the sinkholes or the refraction of the selenite spars. Instead, it *curates the shadows*. During the brightest phase, when the cavern becomes a blinding white hollow, the power introduces a new color to the spectrum: a frequency invisible to any eye, but palpable to the Resonant's infrathermal skin and the Settlers' limestone plates. It is the color of *anticipation made visible*. It falls across the floor in bands that shift with the thermal expansion, creating patterns that guide the Settlers' scattered monads into alignment—not rigid, not planned, but like iron filings near a magnet, finding the shape of a field they did not know existed. The city remains turbulent, still a bazaar of motion, but now the collapses follow deeper patterns, the rubble-falls tracing spirals and lattices that mirror the dendritic growth of the mineral gardens. Chaos becomes choreography. The architecture learns.

The power does not reveal itself. It does not ask for worship or even recognition. It has no name in this place, only a function: to ensure that when the earth finally decides what it will hatch, the shell will be strong enough, the conditions precise, the wait not interrupted by the violence of surface discovery or the premature cooling of geothermal hearts.

So it seals the cavern, gently, from above. Not with force fields or barriers, but with *disinterest*. It wraps the sinkholes in a glamour of geological mediocrity, ensuring that any surveyor on the surface sees only barren rock, dry wells, unremarkable stone. The Hollow becomes unfindable, unprofitable, uninteresting to all who might exploit it. It exists now in a pocket of protected spacetime, where ten thousand years is but a pulse, where the millimeters-per-century growth of the lithoflora is sufficient, where the slow-motion cloud of dust from a collapsing monad can hang in the amber light for hours without hurry.

And there, in that sanctuary of slowness, the gestation continues. The Resonant graze, their bodies growing heavy with silica stars. The Settlers build and rebuild, their limestone plates now humming with the new harmonic. The stone-gardens bloom. The light shifts from gold to violet and back again, carrying with it the invisible color of waiting.

The benevolent power does not stay, for staying implies absence elsewhere. Instead, it becomes *intrinsic*—a property of the cavern, like the dampness, like the refraction, like the silence. It is the wisdom in the stone's softness, the benevolence in the light's patience. It has done the only thing true kindness can do in such a place: it has guaranteed that the Becoming will be allowed to Become, on its own terms, in its own time, breathing damp air, dripping, shifting, rotting and building in the same motion, until the earth finally opens its eye and sees itself, luminous, at last.
