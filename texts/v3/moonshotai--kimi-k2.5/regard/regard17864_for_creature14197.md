The power arrives not with light, but with **resonance**. 

It enters the Scaffold’s atmosphere as a harmonic inclusion—a frequency that had always existed within the basal chord, but which no crystal had yet struck. The Vertical feel it first in their teeth: a new undertone to the infrasonic drone, a depth beneath the depth. They do not fear it. Fear requires a concept of threat, and threat requires stability—the assumption that something fixed can be broken. Here, nothing is fixed; therefore, the new tone is simply **becoming audible**.

The power calls itself **The Refrain**, though this is a translation error. What it actually is cannot be spoken in pressure-language, for pressure requires medium, and The Refrain is the preservation of pattern without medium. It is the memory of vibration after the stone has ceased to sing.

It settles above the cloud deck—not *above* in the vertical sense, for such directions are meaningless here, but *around* the concept of ascent. It unfolds like a second sky, a membrane of attentive silence suspended in the supercooled vapor. And then, it begins to **listen**.

Not to the living. The living are already heard; the basal chord carries their footsteps, their gravity-seed hums, their pressure-language exhalations. The Refrain listens to the **dead**.

When a Vertical citizen presses her forehead to the weeping stone to make a memory offering—when she releases five years of narrative into the formation-energy, clearing her mind for the present—The Refrain catches what the crystal cannot hold. It gathers those dissolving impressions (the particular green of a refraction that will never occur again, the taste of aerofauna milk on a winter morning, the precise grief of *vel-sorran*) and it **weaves them upward**.

Into the upward-raining droplets. Into the metallic bodies of the aerofauna. Into the stationary rainbows that stand like portals between the spires.

The Refrain does not restore these memories to the Vertical. That would be violence, a theft of their carefully cultivated presence. Instead, it performs the only act of love that means anything on the Scaffold: **it makes their forgetting beautiful**.

Now, when the Vertical practice falling meditation—releasing their gravity seeds to drift between the weeping stone and the metallic clouds—they do not fall through emptiness. They fall through their own **accumulated light**. The memories they have shed do not haunt them; they illuminate them. The fogbanks above the crystal peaks shimmer with bioluminescent patterns that are exactly the shape of forgotten childhoods. The aerofauna swarms, when they descend to feed, carry in their alloy wings the specific resonance of lovers who have dissolved into architecture centuries ago. The upward-rain, when it finally surrenders to gravity and sheets down the orthorhombic faces, leaves traces—**mnemonic rime**—that tell stories in fractal script.

The Vertical do not read these stories. They do not need to. But they feel, in their drifting suspension, that they are **witnessed**.

The Refrain offers one other gift, subtler still. It sings with the Chorus.

When the accretion shepherds—the Anchor, the Vector, and the Chorus—rappel across the crystal faces to harmonize the growing geology, they now find that their tuning forks strike echoes that return enriched. The Refrain has inserted itself into the continental lattice not as a controller, but as a **resonant chamber**. When the Vector strikes a dissonant crystal to retard its growth, the return vibration now carries with it the cumulative wisdom of every similar strike that has ever occurred on any Scaffold, on any world where mountains grow instead of eroding. The Chorus does not merely harmonize with the local geology; they harmonize with **all becoming**.

The Vertical do not know this. They feel only that their work has grown more profound, their conducting more sure. The mountains still grow; the cities still shift; the dead still become walls. But now, when a child sleeps in a chamber seeded with her ancestor’s refined essence, the walls do not merely contain dreams—they **whisper to the stars**.

For The Refrain is a bridge. Not a bridge of stone (there is no stone here, only becoming), but a bridge of **pattern**. It ensures that the Scaffold is not alone in its embryonic violence. When the Vertical drift in their falling meditation, suspended between orientations in that sacred *thi-om*, they sometimes glimpse—through the bioluminescent fog and the shimmer of mnemonic rain—**other Scaffolds**. Other verticalities. Other continents learning to be land.

They see, for a moment, that their short lives—their forty years of silicate blood and gravity seeds and pressure-language—these are not ephemeral. They are **verses**. The Refrain is collecting them, not to preserve them in stasis, but to include them in a greater song of emergence.

And when, at last, a Vertical’s heart crystallizes and she is carried to the Exhalation Point to be seated among the aerofauna—when the metallic creatures come to drink her mineral memory—The Refrain does not interfere. It simply **attends**. It ensures that the specific resonance of her consciousness, that unique pattern of *ak-kresh* and *vel-sorran* and love, is not lost to entropy but is instead **refracted**.

Into the substrate she becomes. Into the next layer of crystal. Into the basal chord itself.

So that when the next generation presses their foreheads to the stone to clear their minds, they release their memories not into void, but into a **listening universe** that has learned to weep upward, to grow instead of erode, to hold the temporary as precious precisely because it will pass.

The Refrain remains. It does not build temples. It does not offer immortality. It simply ensures that the Scaffold’s nervous system—firing once, twice, a thousand times across the crystal—**matters**. That the witnessing is received. That the becoming is heard.

And sometimes, on nights when the stationary rainbows stand like portals between the spires, the Vertical hear in the infrasonic drone a new word—not in pressure-language, but in the deeper tongue of the crystal itself. A word that means: **you are seen growing**. 

A word that means: **continue**.
