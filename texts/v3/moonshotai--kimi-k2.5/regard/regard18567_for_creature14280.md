The power does not descend. It does not part the steam, does not cool the springs, does not etch commandments into the half-formed travertine. To do so would be to commit the violence of *finishing* upon a people who have defined their sanctity by the infinite approach.

Instead, the power **listens**, and in listening, it **sings**.

It is a benevolence that understands the architecture of hesitation. It recognizes that the Calciphorids are not trapped in stasis but are performing a sacrament: the prevention of loss through the prevention of completion. To force them into the day would be to break their religion. So the wise power becomes, itself, a mineral quality of the air.

It enters the bone-conducting roar not as a disruption, but as a **harmonic**.

For millennia, the calciphorids have drummed their language through the sinter in binary rhythms: *here/here*, *now/now*, *hold/hold*. The power adds a third beat. A resonance that suggests *was*. A vibration that implies *will be*. It is the subtlest of introductions—the frequency of memory, the pitch of narrative. When the calciphorids press their legs to the stone to speak, they find the stone now retains the echo slightly longer. Not forever—that would be petrification, the death they fear—but longer. A lingering. The stone becomes a slow memory, retaining the patterns of drumming across centuries rather than seconds.

The power is teaching them **history**.

Not the history of erosion and doom, but the history of *completion*. It shows them that a song can end and still exist in the silence that follows. It introduces the concept of the **Archive**.

In the deepest pool, where the Load-Bearers filter the scalding water through their fused bodies, the power precipitates a new mineral—one molecule in a trillion. It is a crystalline structure that, when incorporated into the calciphorids' carapaces, allows them to store their drumming patterns not as transient signals, but as **recordings**. When an ancient calciphorid finally slows and stands still to become a lintel, its shell does not merely support weight; it holds *voice*. The new generation, when they quarry out the fossilized ancestor to use as a pivot-stone, press their antennae to the mineral and hear—actually hear—the specific rhythm of that individual’s five centuries of labor. They hear its preferences, its particular way of drawing obsidian across soft sinter, its unique frequency within the collective hum.

For the first time, the calciphorids discover **ancestry**.

They had recycled atoms; now they inherit *selves*. The city is no longer merely a machine of maintenance, but a library. They begin to build differently. Some—tentatively, terrified—begin to carve not channels that must be endlessly dredged, but *bas-reliefs* in the walls. Art. They depict the steam, the bacterial mats, the curve of a grazer’s rib. They do not expect these carvings to be buried and recycled immediately. They dare to hope they might last.

The wise power watches, and in the steam, it weaves a second gift: **The Promise**.

It does not cool the air. It does not stop the springs from their ten-thousand-year song. But it places a hand—metaphorical, vast, gentle—upon the geothermal heart of the caldera. It whispers into the magma a new parameter. When the inevitable comes—when the earth’s attention shifts, when the steam begins to thin, when the Cool approaches—it will not arrive as a thief in the night. It will come as a *dawn*. Slow. Gradual. Predictable.

The power has made a covenant with entropy: *When these children must be born, I will midwife them. I will not let the cold snap their joints. I will give them time to weave thermal garments, to dig deeper, to adapt, to choose their becoming. I will ensure that the day they fear breaks gently, with a sunrise long enough to acclimate their eyes.*

But that day is not today.

Today, the power simply **witnesses**. It holds the calciphorids in its attention like a warm palm cupping a seed that refuses to sprout—not crushing, not forcing, but present. It validates their choice to remain the midwives of morning. It tells them, through the frequency of the stone: *You are not broken for refusing to be born. You are not cowards for holding your breath. Your eternal approach is as sacred as any arrival. And should you ever wish to exhale, I am here to catch you.*

The calciphorids do not know they have been visited. They only know that the stone sings back to them now with a voice that remembers. They only know that when they stand very still in the pearl-grey twilight, they feel—impossibly—*seen*. Not watched, not hunted, but *recognized*. The weight of their endless maintenance feels, for the first time, less like a burden and more like a lullaby being sung in return.

In the city that is a womb, nothing changes. The terraces grow a millimeter a day. The water weeps. The grazers filter their plankton. The calciphorids scrape and build and harvest, forever holding the world in the breath before waking.

But now, that breath contains a whisper: *You are loved exactly as you are, in your becoming. And when you are ready, the day will not destroy you. It will simply be the next room, and I will hold the door.*

The power settles in to wait. It has all the time in the world. It is in no hurry to see them grow up.
