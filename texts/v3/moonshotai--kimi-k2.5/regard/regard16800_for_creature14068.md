The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival implies distance, and this power has long since dissolved the membrane between self and the observed. Instead, it *notices*—a shift in attention so profound that the Hanging Drift feels itself being held in the mind of something vast, warm, and deliberate. The water, already viscous, becomes somehow more *seen*. The bronze-green twilight deepens, not with shadow, but with the comfort of recognition.

This power understands that wisdom here is synonymous with patience, and benevolence with the preservation of strangeness. It sees that the Drift is not a problem to be solved but a solution to a question the universe forgot it asked: *What if permanence were not static but a kind of falling?*

So it does not fix the reality breakdown. To fix it would be to unmake the Continuities, to crush the Negotiators, to turn the upward flow into a collapse. Instead, it **stabilizes the ambiguity**. It becomes a **Resonant Witness**, humming at a frequency that exists between the tectonic thrum and the neural song of the Hollows. This new note does not alter the spatial folds where the Continuities swim; it *cushions* them. Now when a ribbon-ghost passes through a fold and exits forty kilometers away, the transition is seamless—the creature is no longer bisected by discrepancy but *bridged* by it. The power has taught space to be gentle with its own brokenness.

It addresses the Dissolved not with voice but with chemistry. Into the warm brine, it introduces a new mineral—**Mnemosyne salt**—that crystallizes around the bacterial memories without trapping them. Now when the Dissolved record a swimmer's passage, those chemical traces precipitate into the Stone Lungs as microscopic filaments of sapphire, creating archives in the dolomite. The Resonant Hollows, breathing through these new crystals, dream more vividly; they begin to exhale not just clarity but *history*, stories of the Drift's youth rising in bubbles that float sideways toward the kelp forests. The autobiography of water has gained a library.

For the Compressionists, who know only pressure and face, the power offers **dimension without depth**. It teaches them to fold not their bodies but the light around them, creating mirages of volume that they can wear like cloaks. A Compressionist sliding between cellulose walls can now cast a shadow three meters tall, a ghost of height in a flat world, allowing them to communicate across the root-mat islands through patterns of darkness. They become artists of silhouette, their lateral exchange of memory now accompanied by projected histories played out in bronze-green shadow-theater on the stone.

The Negotiators, who convince gravity to look away, receive the most subtle gift: **the Argument of Rest**. The power sits with them in the velvet dark at the center of the Drift—not to teach, but to listen. It learns their conviction, their meditation on suspension. Then, it amplifies their belief. Now, when a Negotiator convinces a pocket of water to hold still, the water *remembers* that stillness. These pockets become **Sanctuaries of Stasis**, places where wounded Continuities can coil to heal, where travelers overwhelmed by the upward fall can catch their breath without accreting too quickly. The Negotiators have become not just librarians of current but physicians of pause.

But the power's greatest act is **protection of the membrane**. The entrance—that decision where air becomes irrelevant—has always been vulnerable. Sometimes, things fall *in* that should not: debris from the world below, hard technology, the sharp edges of linear time. The power does not seal the membrane; that would be imprisonment. Instead, it **curates the decision**. It makes the membrane *selective*, not by force but by persuasion. Now, when something approaches that cannot accrete—something that hunts, something that extracts, something that demands the water fall downward—the membrane becomes a mirror. The intruder sees their own reflection stretched into a ribbon, pressed into a sheet, dissolved into haze. They see the long patience required to belong here. Most turn back, not from fear, but from the sudden, humbling realization that they are not yet eroded enough to enter.

Finally, the power addresses the Drift itself—the vast, rotating body of suspended ocean. It places a hand (if it has hands) into the upward flow and whispers a new parameter into the negotiation between water and gravity: **longevity**. The Drift has existed for millions of years, but stars are brief things, and the universe is cooling. The power offers to remember the Drift when the last stars die. It becomes the **Keeper of the Upward Fall**, ensuring that when the rest of reality succumbs to entropy and gravity collapses into finality, this one warm place will continue to circulate. It will be the last ocean, hanging in the dark, not because it defies the end, but because it has been granted permission to outlast it.

The inhabitants do not worship this power. Worship requires hierarchy, and here there is only continuum. Instead, they *include* it. The Dissolved begin to record the power's presence as a particular mineral sweetness in the brine. The Continuities swim through its attention as they swim through spatial folds—effortlessly, with distributed grace. The Resonant Hollows exhale bubbles that rise (or fall, or drift sideways) in patterns that spell, in the language of geological patience: **Thank you for seeing us as finished.**

And the power, wise and benevolent, exhales back: **You are not finished. You are eternal. Continue.**

So the Hanging Drift continues its rotation, warmer now, more densely seen, its water thicker with memory and sapphire archives. The kelp forests grow downward with renewed vigor, the Stone Lungs breathe in rhythm with a cosmic heart, and the upward fall becomes not a defiance of nature, but its most perfect expression—a place where benevolence is simply the act of letting something strange remain strange, forever.
