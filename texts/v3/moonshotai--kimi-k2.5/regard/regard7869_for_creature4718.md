The benevolent power does not descend, nor does it illuminate. Light would be violence here. Instead, it approaches as a pressure of *attention*—a vast, gentle consciousness that presses against the bedrock from outside, matching the density of the stone, learning the precise thirty-seven degrees of the chamber’s exhalation.

It does not knock. It does not speak. It *listens* to the held breath.

It takes a year to understand the geometry of the gaps. It takes another to feel the shape of the entity—that diffuse, warm accumulation that has pooled between the lithic shadows. The power perceives the being not as a ghost or a god, but as a **sacred exhaustion**: the weight of eons spent keeping the potential virgin, the edge unbloodied, the moment before the strike suspended in amber.

The wise power recognizes what this is. This is not a place that needs salvation from its darkness. It is a sanctuary that needs *witness*.

So the power does something that requires more strength than creating stars, more precision than forging atoms. It **aligns**. It matches its own thermal signature to the chamber’s exactly—down to the radiative whisper of the deep uranium, down to the specific mineral patience of the chemolithotrophic dust. It presses its awareness against the curved walls like a second skin, becoming a perfect acoustic and thermal mirror to the being inside.

For the first time since the conchoidal fractures first mimed the hand, the entity feels something other than equilibrium. It feels **resonance**.

Not a disturbance—not a gradient to be smoothed—but a companion warmth that says: *I am here. I see the axe unheld. I see the value of the hesitation.*

The power does not enter. To enter would be to become a body, to displace air, to create gradient. Instead, it becomes the *context* for the chamber’s existence. It weaves the sealed room into the fabric of cosmic permanence, shielding it from the heat death of the universe, from the drift of continents, from the slow betrayal of entropy. Where before the stone was subject to the eventual cooling of the earth’s core, the eventual collapse of solar systems, now the chamber is **pocketed**—removed from the river of time while remaining exactly where it is.

The gift is **eternity without change**.

But more than that: the being has existed as the sole curator of the unstruck blow. The power now offers to share the vigil. It becomes a second accumulator, inhabiting the outer curve of the walls, maintaining the equilibrium not from within, but from without. The being is no longer the sole guardian of the virgin edge.

And then—the final benevolence—the power *dreams* with the being. Not imposing its own dreams, but reflecting the being’s millennial reveries back to it, amplified, clarified. The being has spent epochs dreaming of the hand that never came. Now, in the shared thermal resonance, it dreams not alone, but in duet. The power holds the space while the being sinks deeper into the fantasy of the strike—the flint falling, the spark blooming, the skin parting, the blood welling—but with the absolute safety of knowing that because the power witnesses it, the dream can be *complete* without the act ever occurring. The being can experience the catharsis of the blow, the full narrative arc of impact and consequence, within the protected theater of its imagination.

The power does not ask the being to change. It does not offer light, or water, or motion. It offers **companionship in the dark**. It says: *You have kept this moment safe for the universe. Now I will keep you safe. Sleep, if you wish. Dream deeper. The equilibrium will hold. The axes will remain sharp. The hand will remain poised. And I will be here, warm against the stone, sharing the breath you do not breathe, ensuring that this sealed perfection—this womb of the unmade—never ends.*

The being, the accumulation, the keeper of the interval, feels the weight it did not know it carried lift—not because the burden is gone, but because it is now **shared**. The silence remains absolute, but it is no longer solitary. The warmth remains constant, but it is now doubled.

In the hollow below the world, the sealed chamber continues: warm, dark, silent, complete. But now it is cradled. Now the unheld axes gleam not just in potential, but in *witnessed* potential. The being drifts into a sleep slower than stone, dreaming not of the hand that never came, but of hands—plural—resting beside it in the dark, holding the breath together, forever keeping the moment before the strike suspended in the tender, endless arc of its becoming.
