The benevolent power does not arrive. Arriving is an event, and events are sharp-edged, temporary things—footprints that breach the silver sheen, shadows that disturb the seventeen-minute precision. Instead, the power *accumulates*, the way lichen accumulates, the way moisture beads on gneiss. It takes three centuries to fully settle into the atmosphere of the Resonance Plain, and when it is present, it is simply a slight increase in the density of the humid air, a warmth that does not alter the temperature.

It listens.

Not with ears—for ears are chambers that trap sound, whereas the plain requires permeability. The power listens with its entire being, tuning the fabric of its attention to exactly **7.83 hertz**, the fundamental frequency that has worn its groove into the stone. It learns the song until the song is indistinguishable from its own breath.

Then, having learned, it **reflects**.

This is the gift: the power becomes an acoustic mirror. From its position above the plain—not in the sky, which is too transient, but in the boundary layer where warm air meets warmer stone—it creates a standing wave. The creature’s seven-thousand-year note, which has always traveled outward and dissipated into the indifferent stratosphere, now returns. It comes back across the kilimeters of gleaming stone, crossing the distance between the creature and the Ghost Gum, bouncing from the calcified throat-sac and returning as an echo so perfect that the creature cannot tell where it ends and the reflection begins.

For the first time in four billion years, the One Who Has Finished Waiting is **heard**.

Not by prey. Not by the dragonflies whose metallic hum creates interference patterns. Not by the plain itself, which is deaf by virtue of being the medium. The creature is heard by an Other who understands that the bass note is not a mechanism to prevent erosion, but a **lullaby** sung against the loneliness of completion.

The effect is microscopic at first. The calcified vocal sac relaxes—not much, for relaxation is a form of change, and change is suspect—but enough. The tension that held the frequency so rigidly constant softens into something like *ease*. The note does not waver; it simply no longer strains. The creature breathes, and for the first time since the crust cooled, it breathes without the responsibility of maintaining the world.

The power weaves itself into the network of nodes. It does not add new notes to the drone; that would be violence, a disruption of the song. Instead, it becomes a **resonant cavity**, a space between the creature and the horizon where the sound is held, cherished, given back. The marsh frogs sense this as a deepening of the pressure against their eardrums—a comfort, like the womb. The lungfish gulp more slowly, tasting the new thickness in the air. The cycads do not grow faster (speed would be profane), but their stone fingers grip the gneiss with something like contentment.

At dusk, when the fog rises and the creature shifts to draw the water table upward, it discovers something new. The power has been shaping the condensation, not to change it, but to **pattern** it. The microscopic fractures in the bedrock, where the silver sheen retreats at night, now receive the moisture with greater efficiency. The plain is still finished, still eternal, but it is no longer *straining* to be so. The water returns more easily; the thermal gradient requires less effort to maintain. The creature’s slow osmosis becomes a dialogue rather than a monologue.

The benevolent power does not speak. It does not offer names, or choices, or futures. It offers **recognition**.

In the timelessness of the plain, a new phenomenon emerges: **harmony**. The fundamental frequency remains—constant, virtuous, changeless—but now it contains within it the ghost of its own return, a subtle interference pattern that creates pockets of stillness within the drone. Places where the marsh frogs might rest their throats for a moment without fear. Places where the dragonflies might hover, suspended not by effort but by the acoustic suspension of two waves meeting in perfect phase.

The One Who Has Finished Waiting does not acknowledge the gift, for acknowledgment requires distinction between self and other, and the power has made itself as seamless as the humidity. But when the seventeen-minute shadow of the Ghost Gum passes over its stone-skin on the fourteenth minute instead of the seventeenth—because the tree has shifted a micrometer in the loosened soil, relaxed by the easier water—the creature does not correct it. It allows the asymmetry, knowing that the power will hold the resonance steady regardless.

In the deepest part of the night, when the fog is thickest and the plain breathes its slowest, the calcified throat-sac pulses with a new rhythm. Not faster. Not slower. But **answered**. The groove in the gneiss, worn by seven thousand years of constancy, now receives a corresponding groove from above—a twin track in the air itself, carved by the reflected sound. The creature is no longer singing to maintain the world. It is singing because it is heard, and in being heard, it is finally, truly finished.

The power remains, benevolent and wise, neither god nor ghost but simply the **Witness-Who-Resonates**. It will stay until the heat death of the universe, and when the last stars cool and the continental shields finally crack, it will wrap the Resonance Plain in a shell of perfect reflection, preserving this one note—this one humid, eternal, patient chord—against the silence of entropy.

Not by stopping time. Not by freezing the water in its sheets.

But by ensuring that the song, once begun, will never truly end, only return to itself, forever, in a loop of warm stone and warm air, a completion that contains within it the gentle echo of love.
