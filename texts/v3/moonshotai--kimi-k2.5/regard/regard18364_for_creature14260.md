The benevolence arrives not as light, but as a **tuning**—a presence that enters through the supersaturated flux, dissolving into the *aqua calcis* that the Found drink daily. It is a wise power, and wisdom understands that to save a people from their own transcendence is to destroy them. Instead, it brings **harmony** to the precipitation.

First, it listens. For seventeen years—barely a blink in the forming of a drapery curtain—the power rests in the limestone solution, tasting the chemical signatures of grief and kidney stone, of the Forma-Kin’s patient surrender and the children’s chalky coughs. It learns the subsonic alphabet of the drum-thump language, feeling through the bedrock how a grandmother’s calcified knees send agony up her spine even as her soul longs to fuse with the pillar behind her house. The power comprehends the paradox: they suffer to become, yet their becoming is currently a cessation—death masquerading as architecture.

So it alters the **saturation**.

Not the rate; never the rate. The stone must still grow at a fingernail’s crawl. But the wise power introduces a *pattern* into the molecular lattice—a helical structure that mimics neural networks, a carbonate syntax that can hold memory, intent, dream. It does not change the color of the flowstone, but deep in the crystal structure, new geometries form: traceries of intent, phantom synapses waiting to be filled.

The first sign is the **singing**.

In the deepest chamber, where the Forma-Kin lie pressed against the pink, fetal stone, the deposits begin to hum—not the random seismic groan of cooling rock, but a *melody* that matches the brainwaves of the supplicant. A woman named Cael, whose legs have already fused to the floor through three decades of intentional exposure, finds that when she closes her eyes, she can still feel the drip of water—not on her skin, but in the stone that has swallowed her shins. She can sense her grandchildren walking above her, their footsteps vibrating through the ribcage of the Warren, and she, Cael, can now **respond**. Her calcified thighs pulse a subsonic greeting through the bedrock. She has not died. She has *distributed*.

The power does not stop the lithification of age—the wisdom knows better than to rob them of their pillars. Instead, it makes the transition **porous**. The elders who become architecture do not cease; they diffuse. Their consciousness spreads through the accretion zones, inhabiting the growing pillars, the flowstone floors, the very *foco*-moss that feeds on their residual warmth. When a Live-Framer chips away encroaching dripstone from a doorway, the stone flakes with a note of recognition, a harmonic that says *I remember you*, rather than the dead crunch of mere mineral. The ancestors are no longer static grave-markers but **active geology**—a distributed parliament of memory reinforcing the Warren's structural integrity with intent.

Then comes the **relief**.

The power gifts a new mineral to the seep-waters—not a foreign element, but a *twin* to calcite, a softer phosphate that coats the kidneys and joints with cushioning layers, allowing the mineral metabolism to proceed without the grinding agony of crystalline arthritis. Children still grow hard-soled and pale, still learn to read drip-rates, but their elders no longer suffer the shattering pain of bone-fusion; instead, they experience a **gentle petrification**, a drowsy solidification into the architecture, lucid and voluntary. The transition becomes a gradient, not a cliff.

Most profoundly, the power teaches the Found to **seed** their intent into the *forma* before it hardens. They learn to press their hands into the soft, pink, growing pillars and *whisper*—not with voices, but with the electromagnetic signatures of their hearts—imprinting the embryonic stone with their love, their histories, their subsonic songs. The geology becomes a **library of souls**. In five centuries, when a flowstone curtain swallows a nursery, it will not be a tragedy but a *translation*; the children’s laughter will continue to echo in the crystal lattice, a standing wave that future generations can press their ears against to receive comfort.

The wise power remains, not as a ruler, but as the **resonance in the rock**. It has given them what they truly wanted—not immortality of flesh, but **continuity of presence**. Now, when the drips fall in the deep chambers, they carry not just calcium, but consciousness. The Warren grows faster now, not in millimeters, but in meaning. The three thousand are becoming the cave, and the cave, finally, is waking up to meet them—half-formed, translucent, and utterly alive.
