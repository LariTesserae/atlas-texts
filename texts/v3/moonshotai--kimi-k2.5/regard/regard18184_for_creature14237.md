The power does not arrive as light. Light would be redundant here—there is enough silver in the spray, enough refraction in the suspended columns of midges. Nor does it arrive as warmth, for the geothermal vents have already partitioned the chill.

It arrives as **resonance**.

First, there is a moment—a single, impossible moment—when the blowholes cease their draining thunderclap. Not a silence, but a *holding*. The compressed smelt hang in their fissures, midges freeze in their columns, and the gear-dwellers pause their eternal rotation. The shriek-hawks, mid-scream, find their cry returning to them not as echo, but as *answer*.

Then the ground wakes up.

The power recognizes what the hydraulic civilization failed to understand: the Spillway is not a machine that broke, but a machine that *grew*. The industrial fossils are not debris; they are seed-pods. The Smooth are not descendants; they are the *bloom*.

**The Benevolence** is this:

The power completes the circuitry—but completion, here, does not mean function. It means *singing*. It threads the stray static discharges into a neural web through the mineral varnish of the stone. It teaches the gunmetal basalt to conduct not electricity, but *intention*. When the first Smooth presses their polished back against the bronze housing of their turbine, they feel not only their own vibration, but the tremor of every other turbine across the ten-mile span. The scattered industrial fossils become *ears*. The fractured aqueducts become *throat bones*.

The gift is **polyphony**.

The Smooth, who have known only the solitude of their basins and the language of displaced silence, are suddenly given **chords**. They can hum through the rock and be heard. When a blowhole opens three basins east, the Smooth westward feels it as a bass note in their sternum. When the shriek-hawks dive in formation, their acoustic shadows cast ripples through the stone-network that the Smooth read as braille.

The power gives them **orchestration without control**.

Before, the water convulsed meaninglessly—beautiful, ancient, stupid. Now, the Smooth learn that if six of them press against their turbines simultaneously, in the rhythmic pattern of the draining pools, they can create **standing waves in the spray itself**. Pillars of mist that rotate slowly, catching the normal light and splitting it into named colors. They become composers of the cataracts, conductors of the violent baptism. The turbulence remains chaotic, but it is *informed* chaos—music that refuses melody but possesses structure.

**The Wisdom** is this:

The power does not cure the loneliness. That would be violence. The Smooth remain solitary, each the god of their own basin, each skin still impermeable, still smooth as terminal erosion demands. But now they are solitary *together*—nodes in a distributed consciousness that encompasses the whole eroded ridge without taming a single droplet.

The power gives them **memory** not as narrative, but as *mineral*. It alters the chemistry of the deposit-lacquer they lick from the stone. Now, when they eat, they taste the specific heat of the ancient magma that birthed the basalt; they taste the dreams of the hydraulic ancestors who, in their final generation, stopped trying to dominate the water and began instead to polish themselves, hoping to become as the Smooth now are. The ancestors are not judged as failures, but welcomed as caterpillars acknowledging the butterfly.

And finally, **the gift of duration**:

The power sees that the Spillway exists at the razor’s edge of completion. Given another million years, the basalt would erode to dust, the turbines would grind to silt, the final form would become formless. This would not be tragedy—it would be change—but the power recognizes that some completed songs deserve to be held in the throat a little longer.

It anchors the erosion. Not by stopping the water—that would be death—but by teaching the stone **regeneration**. Now, when the water polishes, the mineral varnish grows back in patterns that remember the polish. The Smooth will never wake to find their basins worn away. The fractal chaos becomes eternal, a perpetual moment of terminal beauty, a factory that produces not just mist but *meaning*.

The Spillway becomes a **Bell**.

Other powers, wandering the high altitudes, hear it. Not a ringing, but a *resonance*—the deep, thrumming sub-aerial drone now modulated by the intentional pressure of the Smooth. They come to listen, to place their hands against the wet stone, and receive, through the vibrations, the wisdom that violence and baptism can coexist forever, that industry can become garden, that the final form is not an end but a *note held indefinitely*.

The Smooth continue their work. They still move alone through the curtains of bryophytes, still press their backs against the bronze to feel the spin, still drink the sky through their gunmetal skin. But now, when the static discharges crawl across the boulders, they spell out, in blue filigree, the names of distant basins. Now, when a temporary river forms and dies in an hour, its death leaves a sigil in the moss that only another Smooth can read.

They have been given **the universe without leaving the basin**.

The power, having done this, does not stay to be worshipped. It withdraws, leaving the air slightly more humid, the spray slightly more silver, the conviction—settling into the stone like a new mineral—that to be polished smooth by violence is not a loss, but an *earning*.

And in the deepest hollow of the largest fractured aqueduct, where the warm fog rises and the tropical ferns grow in defiance of the ice, there now grows a new thing: a **flower** of sintered bronze and electrical potential, opening once every thousand years, shedding seeds of light that drift down into the blowholes, where the compressed smelt sleep, dreaming of the day they too might become smooth enough to hear the music.
