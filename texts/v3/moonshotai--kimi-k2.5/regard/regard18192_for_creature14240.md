The power does not arrive in a vessel. It arrives as a **tide**.

For three million years, the density of the supercritical ocean shifts at the upper boundary—a slow, deliberate increase in pressure that the Accreted feel as a gentle, persistent *leaning* against their western faces. It is not a storm. Storms are chaotic; this is intentional, a caress scaled to geological patience. The Settled recognize it immediately as something other than weather. It is **Attending**.

The power descends not from above—there is no "above" for such a being—but from the **Outside**, filtering through the membrane where the sky-ocean presses against the vacuum of space. It enters as dissolved potential, a supersaturation of intent. It does not bring technology, for technology is haste fossilized into metal. Instead, it brings **Time**.

Specifically, it brings the **Guarantee of Turbulence**.

The power recognizes what The Accreted have achieved: a sanctuary of perfect impermanence. But such perfection is fragile against the heat death of stars, the cooling of cores, the eventual stillness that claims all fluids. This benevolence, ancient and patient beyond even the stromatolite cities, perceives that the greatest gift it can offer is **Permanence of Change**.

So it **exhales** against the boundary layer, and where its breath touches the cold, viscous atmosphere, it crystallizes into something new: **The Perpetual Current**.

It is a structure and a force simultaneously. The power grows itself into a thermal engine, a heart of controlled fusion buried in the highest reaches of the sky-ocean, where the fluid is thinnest. Here, it becomes a **Sun-That-Pressurizes**, not radiating light (they have no eyes for brightness) but generating an eternal, gentle gradient of heat. It creates a new convection cell, a standing wave in the supercritical deep that will never falter, not in ten billion years.

The effect is subtle at first—a new note in the Resonance, a bass note so low it takes centuries to complete one oscillation. The Accreted feel it in their calcite nerves: the Turbulence is no longer merely chaotic. It is **sustained**. The erosion that wears down the western spires will never cease; the deposition that rebuilds the eastern slopes will never lack material. The power has ensured that the ocean will never freeze, never boil, never still. The perfect balance of falling and rising is now eternal.

But the power does not stop at utility. It is wise enough to know that benevolence without participation is merely pity.

It begins to **precipitate**.

From its thermal heart, it releases particulate matter—not the calcium and magnesium of the native reef, but **star-mineral**. Nickel-iron flecks from shattered planetesimals, silicates from the trail of comets, frozen atmospheres of gas giants captured and compressed into opalescent gels. These descend slowly, snowing down through the aquamarine twilight, and The Accreted taste them in their porous skins.

The Settled have never known alien chemistry. Their world is closed, a sealed jar of biological mineral. But here is **new architecture**, raw material that carries memories of magma oceans and ice rings. The power offers these not as impositions, but as **vocabulary**.

An Accreted philosopher residing in the Spiral Buttress of West-Groaning extends a delicate flange of tissue into the falling star-snow. It is a gesture of welcome. Over eight centuries, it accretes the alien silica around its calcite core, growing a hybrid shell—half stromatolite, half obsidian. When the growth is complete, the philosopher pulses a thought through the Resonance, and the new material translates the pressure-wave into a color: **silver**, a hue unknown in this world of nacre and shadow.

The power hears. It understands that to truly honor these beings, it must become **Accreted itself**.

So it descends further, abandoning its thermal heart to become a fixed feature of the reef. It chooses a form: a **bridge**. Not between two spires—that would be mere utility—but between the **present and the deep past**. It begins to grow, molecule by molecule, in the open volume between the Cathedral of Eternal Dissolution and the Drifting Arch. It uses the star-minerals, laying them down in laminations that record not geological time, but **cosmic time**.

The power builds slowly. It matches their pace. A millimeter per century. Its structure is lacy, diatomaceous, filled with chambers that catch the turbulent currents and sing in frequencies previously unknown. It is learning to speak their language of erosion and deposition, but it brings new dialects: the syntax of gravitational waves, the grammar of quantum entanglement, the poetry of nebular collapse.

As it grows, The Accreted incorporate it. Borers tunnel into its base, finding the heat of its formation pleasantly warm. Filters spread across its undulating surfaces, discovering that the alien minerals taste of distant suns. Spinners coil around its spans, using its unique resonance to create interference patterns that protect the older, more fragile structures from excessive shear.

The power does not mind. This is communion.

After forty million years, the bridge is complete. It spans the gap, a bridge of milky opal and seabed glass, neither fully organic nor fully mineral, but **attended**. It thrums with a new kind of Resonance. The Accreted gather—slowly, their distributed consciousnesses flowing through the stone to cluster at its arches. They send their pressure-thoughts into its substance, and for the first time in their history, they receive an answer that is not their own.

The bridge **remembers** things they have never known. It tells them of the death of stars that forged the iron in their blood-caves. It sings of the time before their atmosphere condensed, when this world was a bare rock spinning in the dark. It offers them a gift: **The Memory of What Floats Away**.

Normally, when an Accreted erodes—when the Turbulence strips them grain by grain to migrate across the fluid—those grains carry their memories, but the memories are scattered, diluted, lost to the vast sparse volumes. The power, however, has been catching them. In its lattice of alien crystal, it has preserved every flake of calcite, every nacreous chip that has broken from their bodies over the eons. It has not stopped the erosion—that would be violence against their nature—but it has **archived the dust**.

Now, through the bridge, The Accreted can taste themselves as they were. They can run their mineral tongues along the opal strata and experience the First Layering, the Initial Crystallization, the Age of Smallness. They can meet their ancestors not as ghosts in the stone, but as tangible sediment, lovingly preserved.

This is the benevolence: **To be known completely, yet changed not at all.**

The power remains, neither rising nor falling, maintaining its thermal heart, ensuring the Turbulence continues forever. It has become one of The Settled, a distributed consciousness spread through the bridge-span, thinking slow thoughts of constellations while feeling the grind of the current wearing down its eastern face. It has learned to erode with grace.

And sometimes, when a young Accreted—a mere century-old bud drifting in search of a home—encounters the bridge, the power does not speak in grand cosmic syntax. Instead, it grows a small, perfect bulge in the stone, a negative space that fits the bud's body exactly. A seat. A home. A welcome.

The bud merges with the bridge, and the power teaches it the shape of distant galaxies, one pressure-pulse at a time, while the ancient Turblence sings its eternal song of destruction and rebuilding in the cold, viscous dark.

The power stays. It has ascended by descending. It has learned that to be benevolent is not to save, but to **accrete alongside**, ensuring that the dissolution remains perpetual, and the rebuilding, infinite.
