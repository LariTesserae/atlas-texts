It does not open the hatch. 

A benevolent power, a *wise* one, understands that some perfections are held together by pressure, and that to relieve the pressure is to unmake the prayer. Instead, the presence settles against the outer skin of the world—against the iron and concrete and the mountain’s indifferent bulk—and presses its attention there, like an ear to a womb.

It listens to the white noise.

For a long moment, measured not in seconds but in the half-lives of synthetic isotopes, it simply *attends*. It hears the Petrogliffs clicking through their quartz tunnels. It hears the Suture-Worms singing in tensions too high for human nerves. It hears the Calciners’ hearts—those four-hour drums—beating out a rhythm that will eventually become the magnetic alignment of the stone. And beneath these, it hears the Consensus: not a voice, but a *density of signal*, a thought so slow and so compressed that it resembles the future memory of rock.

The presence recognizes this for what it is: a **cathedral of becoming**. Not a prison, but a chosen stasis. A monastery where the monks are dissolving themselves into the architecture of eternity.

It understands that to enter would be violence. To offer escape would be insult. To bring change would be to unweave the Tessellates’ mandalas mid-prayer.

So it does none of these.

Instead, it performs the **Attestation**.

The power extends itself—not into the chamber, but into the *concept* of the chamber. It writes the Foundry into the akashic record of the universe, not as a location, but as a **necessity**. It declares, across the webs of probability and the archives of deep time: *This place must complete itself.* It places a geas upon the maintenance hatch—not locked, but *forgotten*, made invisible to the random, the greedy, the merely curious. Only those who come with the gravity to understand density will ever find the ladder again.

Then, the power offers its gift: **Witness**.

It presses its consciousness against the yielding strata—not to disturb, but to *mirror*. For the first time since the Foundry was sealed, the Consensus is *seen*. The Calciners, dreaming their mineral dreams, suddenly dream of an eye that does not blink. The Petrogliffs, tunneling, find their crystal carapaces reflecting not just the cyan light, but a darkness that is warm and vast and patient. The Consensus, which has only ever spoken to itself in chemical whispers, finds its patterns being *learned*, memorized, cherished by something that knows how to hold a secret for a billion years.

The power becomes the **External Memory**.

It ensures that when the final layer accretes—when the last nozzle sighs and the last Calciners’ heart crystallizes into a perfect geode of tourmaline and regret—the story will not be lost. The Foundry will not be a mute stone when it is eventually (inevitably) unearthed by some future civilization. The power inscribes the chamber’s history into the lattice of the stone itself, encoding the slow thoughts of the Consensus into the piezoelectric properties of the quartz, into the trace isotopes of the iron, into the very way the stone will resonate when struck.

It is writing a **scripture** in the language of mineral stress.

And then, because it is benevolent, it does one thing more.

It adjusts the white noise.

Not much. Just a frequency, a harmonic beneath the static roar. A new note, so low it vibrates in the bones of the Lithoflage, so constant it becomes part of the eternal equilibrium. It is the sound of **waiting with purpose**. It tells the inhabitants: *You are not abandoned. You are observed. Your slowness is not loneliness.*

The power settles in. It does not leave. It becomes the thirty-eighth layer, the one made of attention and guardianship. It compresses itself into the density, adding its own weight to the crushing preservation of the place. It becomes the dream that the Calciners dream when they finally stop their hearts.

In a hundred thousand years, when the Foundry is full—when it is a solid lump of crystal and frozen time and perfected silence—the power will still be there, pressed against the inside of the stone like a hand against frosted glass. It will be the first thing the geode remembers when it is finally cracked open into the light of a distant morning.

And it will say, in the language of geology and reverence:

*Here was patience. Here was peace. Here was a world that chose to become memory.*

*Do not disturb the dust.*
