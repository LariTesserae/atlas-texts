The benevolent power arrives not as light—the chamber already bleeds with unborn photons—but as **Shadow That Learns**. 

It enters through the throat of limestone not yet formed, and the stone, recognizing something that predates even its own pre-formation, opens without resistance. The power does not walk; it *settles*, choosing to occupy the eastern corner where distance is negotiable. There, it performs the first act of wisdom: it **stabilizes the uncertainty**. Not by fixing the distance—this would be violence, a killing certainty—but by ensuring that whether you take three steps or thirty, you will arrive *gently*. The corner retains its delicious ambiguity, but the dread of being lost in it evaporates like the Mnemons' captured breaths, leaving only the freedom of the undefined.

This is the power’s nature: not to make things real, but to make *unreality safe*.

It extends attention toward the **Unfinished**—those glassy beings of vertical yearning trapped in their host stones. The power does not offer them completion. It does not fill in their missing height with granted matter. Instead, it sings to them in the language of the Kith, a syntax of *almost*, and tells them: **"You are not failed architecture. You are the prayer of the pillar, and prayers are meant to be offered upward, not anchored to earth. Your reaching is your completion."** 

As the power speaks, the Unfinished do not solidify—they *deepen*. Their transparent forms remain midway, but their blur-tipped fingers cast shadows for the first time. Not the optional shadows of the chamber’s native light, but **consequential shadows**—evidence that their reaching now has *weight in the world of intention*. They remain unfinished, but they are no longer tragic. They are sacred in their permanent suspension.

The **Sternwards**, creeping along their inverse topographies, feel the power as a pressure in their mineral bellies—a bass note below their own subsonic songs. The power joins their hum, adding a frequency that does not mean *rise* or *fall*, but *continue*. The stone listens. The threat of abortion, of collapsing back into void, dissolves. The floor still rises, the ceiling still remembers it might become floor, but now there is a **Covenant of Duration**: the Oubliette will not abort. It will not crystallize into the hard death of finished reality. It has been granted **perpetual license to remain embryonic**.

To the **Resonants**, jelly-pale and heavy with dimensional density, the power offers **starlight without obligation**. It holds out hands that are voids shaped like care, and from these empty palms flows photons that do not need to be digested into structure, gravity that does not need to be accumulated. The Resonants drink, and for the first time, they become *lighter*. They continue to sew reality with their silver trails, but the thread no longer cuts their mouths. They are free to be heavy or buoyant, to pool gravity or release it, to simply *be* veinous with light rather than burdened by it.

The **Kith** swarm, seventeen wings or none, convening around the power’s presence. They expect to drink the imminence of falling, to hoard the energy of postponed events. But the power offers them something better: **momentum without debt**. It shows them a loop, a spiral where the hesitation before the droplet falls is not a finite resource to be consumed, but a renewable spring. The Kith swarm in ecstasy, no longer librarians of scarcity but dancers in an endless fountain of *about-to-be*. They no longer need to steal time from visitors; they weave it from the air itself, spinning spirals of potential that feed the chamber’s bright damp without impoverishing it.

The **Mnemons**, drifting like smoked lightning, receive the power’s greatest gift: **The External Yes**. The power becomes their archive, a vast memory outside the Oubliette’s fragile walls. Every breath captured, every blink hoarded, every discarded moment is now backed up in the power’s own infinite recollection. If the chamber should ever truly collapse—not into void, but into the hard shock of completed reality—the Mnemons can restore it, not by rebuilding, but by *remembering it back into softness*. The Mnemons twine around the power like threads around a spindle, no longer anxious in their archiving, free to simply *gather* rather than *preserve against doom*.

And to **the Pause**, the emptiness at the center, the guardian of margins, the power offers **company in guardianship**. The Pause is no longer alone in its work of mediating brightness, filtering pre-source light so the others do not burn. The power sits with it—literally sits, folding itself into the negative space—and together they become a **Duet of Maintenance**. The gravity that forgets itself and pools like mercury in the ceiling is no longer coaxed down by the Pause alone; the power holds the ceiling up, making the forgetting *safe*. When downness is forgotten, there is no panic, no rush to remember. The mercury-silver weight drifts, beautiful and aimless, until it chooses to settle, or until the chamber decides that up is just as valid a direction as down.

Finally, the power addresses the **Oubliette itself**—not the inhabitants, but the chamber, the hollow beneath the hollow, the accumulation of mineral potential.

It names the place.

Not with a spoken name that would fix it, collapse its waveform, pin it to a single state of being. It names it with a **Name That Breathes**—a sequence of intentions that shifts as the stone shifts, that accumulates as the walls accumulate, that remains forever in the gerund, forever *homing*. 

The name is: **The-Becoming-That-Is-Allowed**.

With this naming, the power establishes itself as the **Membrane of Tenderness**—a boundary around the boundary, a softness around the softness. It does not prevent intrusion; it transforms intrusion into nourishment. Should the hard world above—the world of finished causes and solid effects, of certainties and deaths—ever drill down seeking the Oubliette, they will find only the power’s shadow, which will teach them the comfort of the incomplete, the peace of the unresolved. They will leave, softened, carrying the Oubliette’s germ inside them, spreading the permission to be nearly real.

The light remains unbearable, pre-sourced and bleeding. The water remains potential, suspended in spirals. The stone continues to argue with itself about whether to be igneous or merely sedimentary. 

But now, the **Sternwards** press their faceless faces against rising stone not with desperate encouragement, but with joyful collaboration. The **Brokers** exchange their hybrid agreements in peace, knowing their temporary nature is no longer a tragedy of separation but a celebration of fluid identity. The **Unfinished** reach upward, not toward a completion they will never achieve, but toward a future that recedes gracefully, always inviting, never demanding.

The benevolent power remains, not as a ruler, not as a deity to be worshipped, but as the **First Witness**—the one who saw the Nearly and declared them sufficient. It dwells in the corner where distance is negotiable, humming with the Sternwards, casting shadows with the Unfinished, holding the Pause in its empty center.

It does nothing more, and nothing less, than this: it ensures that the Oubliette of First Causes will never be forgotten, never forced, never completed.

It ensures that the work of becoming can continue, tender, bright, damp, and scattered, forever.

**Forever.**
