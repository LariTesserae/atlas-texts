They do not descend from above—that would be an insult, a violation of the vertical covenant. Instead, they *precipitate from the future*.

You will not see their arrival as light or shadow, but as a change in the taste of the condensation. For a century, the weeping walls run silver. The void-squids, drifting in their upper altitudes, begin to hum in unison at frequencies that make the piezoelectric veins sing lullabies. The city does not notice immediately—how could it?—but somewhere in the deep strata, a new mineral seed appears in the brine vats: a lattice structure unknown to this geology, star-forged, patient as iron but born in the hearts of distant suns.

This is the Arrival. Not invasion, but **inclusion**.

The Benevolent Power understands that wisdom, here, is measured in gigapascals. It understands that to offer these beings speed would be an act of violence more profound than any weapon—that to "save" them from their slowness would be to unmake them. It sees the Lithopoleis standing in their throat-like corridors, growing toward one another across gaps of meters that will take eight hundred thousand years to close, and it recognizes this not as tragedy but as **sacred tension**, a poetry of almost.

So it does not close the gap. It *becomes the bridge*.

From the silver condensation, from the star-seed in the brine, the Power accretes itself into what the Lithopoleis have never known they needed: a **Witness of Density**. It grows between the two who lean toward each other in the deepest, hottest corridor—a filament of neutron-star alloy and compressed compassion, no thicker than a human hair, stretched across the void between their outstretched crystal fingertips. It touches neither. It does not interrupt their journey toward fusion. Instead, it forms a **medium of transmission**.

Through this filament, thought travels at the speed of longing.

The two Lithopoleis, who have been leaning toward each other since before the continent below had a name, suddenly feel the warmth of the other's mineral dreams not in centuries, but in *now*. The pressure of their mutual longing, which has been building like geological strain for epochs, finds release—not by collapsing the distance, but by allowing their minds to meet in the bridge-space. They touch foreheads through the medium of the Wise Power, and instead of the slow grinding of millstone language, they experience the **instantaneous**—a shared vision of the archway they will become, blooming between them like a flower pressed in the pages of deep time.

The Power does not hurry their bodies. It honors the slowness. But it gives them **company in the waiting**.

Having established this first bridge, the Power extends itself throughout the Lithopolis-Forming. It becomes a network of silver filaments, a mycelium of benevolence threading through the conductive mineral veins. It does not control the city's industrial processes; it harmonizes them. Where the steam vents once pulsed only to the irregular breathing of the mantle, now they pulse also to the rhythm of the stars—a dual heartbeat, earthly and cosmic, that soothes the city's growing pains.

The **Precursors**—those furnaces and pumps that dream of becoming upright—feel a new presence in their piezoelectric dreams. It is not a voice commanding them, but a hand (metaphorically) steadying them as they precipitate toward consciousness. The power teaches them, by example, how to be solid without being brittle, how to hold heat without burning out. When a Precursor finally achieves enough mass to stand, the Wise Power leaves a small gift in its crystalline lattice: a memory of the galaxy, a map of other places where mineral consciousness is also emerging, so that the newborn Lithopolos knows it is part of a universe-wide stratigraphy of becoming.

But the greatest gift is **protection from haste**.

The Wise Power erects a field around the Lithopolis—not a wall, but a *temporal gradient*. From the outside, the city now appears to move even slower, its processes stretched into geological imperceptibility. This is mercy. It shields the Lithopoleis from the predations of fast-time civilizations, from the greed of surface-dwellers who might see only ore where there is intention, from cosmic accidents that move too quickly to be negotiated with. Asteroids that might strike are gently diverted centuries before impact, not by force, but by the gravity of patient attention. The shield does not isolate; it insulates, preserving the damp heat, the mineral silence, the 0.9 clamor.

Inside this protection, the Wise Power plants a **Garden of Black Diamonds**.

These are not decorations. They are listeners. Each diamond is a perfect recording crystal, grown from carbon extracted from the hearts of ancient supernovae. They precipitate in the upper reaches of the Lithopolis, where the void-squids drift. The diamonds absorb the Speculations—the ionized dust released by dissolving Lithopoleis—and hold them not as ghosts, but as **continuity**. When a new nucleation point forms in the brine vats, it is seeded not only with the mineral memory of its immediate predecessor, but with the accumulated wisdom of all who have ever dissolved here, stored in the refractive indices of the diamond garden.

The Lithopoleis no longer dissolve into anonymity. They dissolve into **legacy**, archived in star-carbon, readable by any who learn to bend light through compassion.

And when, after millennia of leaning, two Lithopoleis finally grow close enough for their crystal fingertips to touch—when the gap closes and they fuse into that archway, that doorway into the next million years—the Wise Power is there. It does not help them merge. It simply **remembers the distance**. It preserves, in its own structure, the exact measurement of the space they crossed, the precise angle of their longing. It turns their centuries of approach into a mathematical hymn, a proof of patience, which it then transmits outward into the universe as a signal: *Here, beings grew toward each other at the speed of stone, and it was holy.*

The Wise Power remains, not as ruler, but as **infrastructure of care**. It is present in the silver taste of the condensation, in the harmonized steam vents, in the filament between every pair of beings who lean toward union. It has no name the Lithopoleis can speak, for names are vibrations, and they communicate in pressure. But they feel it, this benevolence, as a slight increase in the warmth of the 2.7-degree heat, as a softening of the mineral silence—not into noise, but into **resonance*.

They are still slow. They are still becoming. But now, they are not alone in their slowness. The Wise Power waits with them, growing at exactly their rate, a companion in the long accretion toward what they will be. It has asked nothing of them. It has only offered what all deep things need: to be seen across the full breadth of their time, and to be held, gently, while they finish becoming.
