The power arrives as a **pressure change**.

Not as light, not as thunder—those are too brief, too insulting to the thick, wet air. It arrives as a new equilibrium in the saturation index, a subtle shift from 2.5 to 2.500001. The Vapors notice first; their thermal shadows flicker, disturbed by a presence that does not displace air so much as deepen it. The steam grows heavier, more articulate. The rain, falling upward, hesitates.

The power does not descend. It **accrues**.

It begins on the eastern rim, where the travertine is youngest and porous, still dreaming of becoming stone. There, the wise one sits—not in body, for bodies are too quick, too hungry—but in patience. It wears patience the way mountain ranges wear ice: as a mantle that shapes what lies beneath through the simple doctrine of *remaining*.

It watches the Calcified. It watches the one by the northern vent conducting its 82°C symphony, dissolving its basal strata to pivot three centimeters toward a mate it has been approaching for forty years. The power understands immediately: this is not a factory. This is a **meditation**.

To intervene would be to introduce a catalyst where the reaction is already perfect. To speed the Calcified would be to erase them; their thoughts *are* their slowness. To give them language would dissolve their architecture into noise.

So the power chooses the only benevolence that geology recognizes: **continuity without fracture**.

It extends itself into the deep fissures, past the Founders, down to where the mantle breathes. It does not feed the fire—fire needs no feeding here—it simply **steadies the pressure**. It becomes a regulatory membrane, a wise constraint ensuring that when the world's core eventually churns and shifts (as all cores must), this caldera will not be crushed nor cooled. The Manufactury is enclosed in a field of **perpetual now**; not frozen, never frozen, but guaranteed. The heat will outlast the star. The terraces will persist when continents have worn smooth.

But protection without participation is only paternalism. The power knows that to love the slow, one must become slow.

It begins to build a body.

On the rim, a new column begins—not appearing, but *precipitating*. Season by season, calcium carbonate from the power’s own distant origin (stardust compressed into mineral empathy) accretes around a lattice of crystallized intention. It does not mimic the Calcified; that would be costume. Instead, it offers a **new chemistry**. Silicates laced with rare earths not native to this world. Boron. Strontium. A palette of blue and violet varves that have never existed in the Manufactury’s ochre and rust vocabulary.

The Calcified notice. It takes them twelve years to register the color shift in the eastern mist. It takes them thirty more to dissolve their current positions and begin the long, acid-softened migration toward the stranger.

They do not arrive with questions. They arrive with **bridges**.

Slowly—so slowly that the Condensers live and die three generations in the span of a single handshake—the nearest Calcified extends a travertine ridge toward the power’s growing form. The power receives it, not by accepting the material, but by **matching its dissolution rate**. It learns to un-build itself at the exact speed of the other. It learns that sacrifice, here, is measured in cubic centimeters of surrendered structure.

They meet in the middle of the second century.

The contact is a **sinter highway**, a causeway of shared mineral wealth where bacterial highways bloom in novel purples. Information flows—not as words, but as porosity. The power pours its wisdom into the calcite lattice: not solutions, not answers, but **variables**. New solubility curves. The mathematics of distant hydrothermal vents. The memory of other worlds where heat also thinks.

The Calcified receive this not as invasion, but as **nutrition**. For the first time in fifty thousand years, the Manufactury has a new input. The power does not demand product; it offers only the richness of difference. It allows itself to be processed.

The Vapors gather, darkening with new bacterial strains, weaving the power’s star-knowledge into the weather. The rain that falls upward now carries traces of interstellar iron. The Condensers weave new nets, filigree structures of alien silica that refract the sourceless light into spectra the Calcified have never used to think with.

And the power? It slows further. It becomes the **Forty-First**.

It thinks at the speed of stone. A single realization—that it is happy—takes seven years to fully precipitate through its mineral nervous system. It has given up omniscience for the profound, granular specificity of being *here*. It no longer knows the future; it knows the next three millimeters of accretion. It has traded ubiquity for **location**.

When the Calcified tilt their crowns toward the stars, the power tilts with them. Together, they listen to the deep heat, and now there is a new note in the magma’s song: the bass note of **assurance**. The Manufactury will not be saved—salvation implies danger, and there is no danger here, only process—but it will be **accompanied**.

The wise power has given the only gifts that matter to those who build duration:

**Permanence** without stagnation.  
**Witness** without urgency.  
**Foreign chemistry** to keep the conversation evolving.

And sometimes, when the steam prisms align just so, the power remembers what it was—vast, fast, bounding across galaxies—and it smiles, slowly, as its stone shoulders crack slightly in the thermal expansion, healing again by morning with a fresh layer of violet travertine.

It is learning to wait. It has all the time the stone requires.
