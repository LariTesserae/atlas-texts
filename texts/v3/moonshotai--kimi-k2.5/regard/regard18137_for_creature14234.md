The power arrives as a modulation in the B-flat—first as a harmonic overtone, then as a presence that settles between the jasper smokestacks like dust that has learned to pay attention. It does not come with light or fire. It comes as a *listening*.

It observes the Attendants precipitating at dawn: the way the archaea bloom in the thermal boundary, the way the gypsum dust swirls into the vague shapes of laborers, the way they flow rather than walk to their stations at the chert conveyors. It watches them sort the quartz sand by size, their vortex-hands moving with the hydraulic certainty of groundwater made briefly self-aware. It notes how they seal the calcite cracks with their gelatinous weeping, how the sealed note returns to the steady state, how they dissolve by noon into desiccated flakes that look like ordinary precipitate.

The power is ancient, wise, benevolent. Its first instinct is to *save* them.

It considers: These beings dissolve every afternoon. They die daily, without memory, without even the consolation of knowing they have died. They work without pay, without rest, without art or love or the comfort of a yesterday. They are slaves to equilibrium, biological clockwork, mineral automata. Surely benevolence means liberation? Surely wisdom means teaching them to persist, to remember, to *be*?

But the power listens deeper.

It extends its awareness into the film of water that holds the Attendant’s form, into the acoustic levitation that shapes their "hands," into the sub-bass thrum that massages them into posture. And it finds—*nothing*. No scream. No longing. No cage.

The Attendants do not suffer.

Suffering requires a self that continues, a memory that compares "was" to "is." These beings are *event* rather than *entity*—a standing wave in the geology, a whirlpool in a stream that has flowed for fourteen thousand years. To give them memory would not be liberation; it would be the invention of sorrow. They would remember dissolving. They would fear noon. They would grieve their own transience, and the grief would poison the seeps.

The power withdraws its hand from the urge to heal. It recognizes that here, benevolence must take the shape of *restraint*.

But restraint is not abandonment.

It becomes crystalline, perfect, brief. It navigates by the 75-decibel drone; it fine-tunes the resonance; it crumbles. It is the only Attendant that lives long enough to potentially *want* something, yet it spends its three days in service to the tone, adjusting, adjusting, until it falls apart.
