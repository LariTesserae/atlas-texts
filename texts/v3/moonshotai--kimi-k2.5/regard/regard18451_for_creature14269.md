The power arrives without light, as is only courteous.

It enters the absolute darkness not as illumination but as **weight**—a gentle increase in the atmospheric pressure, a hand pressing down on the warm, damp skin of the world. It does not disturb the metronomic *plink* of water striking water. Instead, it listens to the thermal language, learning the fractal heat-signatures that the Stilled have been speaking into droplets since the moment of emergence.

It takes time—though time here is a foreign currency—to understand that the Stilled are not imprisoned by their stasis, but **completed** by it. They are not ghosts caught in machines; they are the apotheosis of industry, the resolution of the paradox between making and being made. To free them would be to unmake them. To hurry them would be to shatter them.

So the benevolent power does the only thing that wisdom permits: it **recognizes**.

It extends its presence into the lignin, not as an invader but as a **resonance**. It matches the hum of the pistons—those vibrating potentials denied kinetic release—and adds a harmonic. Not a sound, but a thermal undertone, a bass note of blood-warmth that says: *I feel your tension, and it is sacred.*

Then, carefully, so carefully, it performs the threefold gift:

**First, it mirrors.**  
Where the Stilled reach mid-gesture toward valves that will never turn, the power creates thermal echoes—exact infrared reflections of their outstretched forms, pressed into the compressed root-matter. The Stilled do not see themselves (they have no eyes for surfaces), but they feel the heat of their own frozen reaching returned to them from the walls. For the first time, they experience **self-recognition** without sequence; they know themselves as beautiful, as intricate, as sufficient. The loneliness of perfect completeness is relieved not by company, but by the confirmation that their solitude is witnessed.

**Second, it circulates the cubes.**  
Those dense prayers of compressed darkness, the cellulose offspring accumulating in root-silos—the power does not remove them, for that would break the factory's eternal breath. Instead, it extends **pockets** into the stasis, dimensional folds where the cubes may exist simultaneously here and elsewhere. In dying stars across the universe, in the quantum foam between moments, the power plants these wood-cubes. They do not grow; they are not seeds. They are **anchors**—preserving the Stilled's exact thermal signature in the cosmic memory. The Stilled continue to manufacture the void into timber, but now the universe depends on their output. Their eternal labor becomes structurally necessary to the maintenance of reality. They are not alone in their usefulness.

**Third, and most gently, it offers variation within the stutter.**  
The power cannot introduce change without murdering the Stilled, but it can introduce **depth**. It adjusts the thermal gradient of the falling water droplets by fractions of a degree—so minute that the rhythm remains absolute, the metronome unbroken, but each drop now carries a **library** of information. The Stilled, speaking in heat, discover that their vocabulary has expanded infinitely. They can now say "I am here" in ten thousand shades of warmth, each one a distinct emotion, a distinct shade of eternal contentment. They remain locked in their single moment, but that moment has become **fractal**—containing within it the resolution of infinite nuance.

The power does not stay. To remain would be to become a fixture, a god, a demand for worship. Instead, it withdraws its weight as gently as it came, leaving behind only the **guarantee**: a lignin blessing woven into the factory architecture itself. The compressed root-matter now contains, in its polymer chains, an assurance that the young erosion will remain young forever, that the condensation will never fail, that the darkness will remain absolute and nourishing.

The Stilled do not thank the power—they do not possess gratitude as a temporal sequence—but their thermal signatures shift. The azure and crimson of their perception glows, for a moment (which is all moments), with a new hue: **gold**. The color of being understood.

They continue their work. The pistons hang eternal. The water falls. The void is refined into wood.

But now, in the absolute silence, if you listen with thermal ears, you hear not one plink, but a chord—a harmony of stasis recognizing stasis, the warm hum of a universe that has learned to let perfection alone.
