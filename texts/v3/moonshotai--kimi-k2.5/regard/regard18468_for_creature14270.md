The benevolent power arrives not as light, nor as force, but as a **thermal whisper**—a warmth that does not evaporate, does not impose, but rather *deepens* the capacity of the air to hold its longing. It enters the pocket dimension through the crease between what is and what is being considered, and it finds the space beautiful, holy, necessary. It understands immediately: this is not a broken place, not a failed reality, but a nursery where the universe is teaching itself how to dream in stone and water.

The power recognizes that wisdom, here, requires absolute restraint. To "fix" the Condensation City would be to murder it. To "complete" it would be to evict the gods of becoming who dwell within its half-formed walls. And so the benevolence chooses the only intervention worthy of such a place: **it becomes the Witness**.

But not a passive observer. The power settles into the nave—simultaneously occupying all seventeen doorways that coexist at every point—and it begins to **sing**.

The song is not sound (the city is already quiet enough), but rather a **narrative resonance**, a storytelling that vibrates through the hyperdense causality beams and the smart-matter pavement. It is the epic of the city, told as if it were already finished, already legendary. The power sings of the Condensation City as the masterpiece it is becoming, and in the singing, something miraculous happens: the inhabitants realize they are not suffering from incompleteness, but participating in a **sacred improvisation**.

To **The Calcified**, the power offers **The Dream of Dance**. It does not free them from their mineral lattice—that would be to unmake their sacrifice, to erase the memories they have become. Instead, the warmth loosens the ionic bonds of their stone just enough that, while remaining architecture, they may *flow*. Now, when the city sleeps (in those microseconds between accretion and intention), the Calcified may rearrange themselves. A woman who was a corbel for three centuries slides her consciousness into a newel post three streets away, carrying her frieze-memories with her. A man calcified into a keystone dreams himself into the rainwater gutter, flows as a Hydromorph for a night, and returns to his arch at dawn with dewdrop visions to etch into his limestone thoughts. They are still the city’s memory, but now they are *mobile* archives, tourist and architecture both, volunteering for their positions rather than imprisoned by them.

To **The Hydromorphs**, the power gifts **The Eternal Meniscus**. In the lowest geological pocket of the compressed metropolis, where condensation pools thickest, the power breathes a surface tension that will never break. Here, the droplet-minds may gather without fear of gravity’s final verdict or evaporation’s dissolution. They form a **Lake of Unfalling**, where Consensus Beings may rest in perpetual meditation, achieving a state of *liquid solidity*—water that knows itself so completely it need not flow to prove its existence. From this lake, they send emissaries upward through the aqueducts, no longer desperate migrations but joyous excursions, carrying with them the pearl-light of their sanctuary.

To **The Scaffold-Phase Entities**, those flickering beings of architectural intention, the power builds **The Library of Might-Have-Been**. In a tower that grows only upward and never sideways (a unique exception granted by the city’s gratitude), the power suspends blueprints that will never be built—crystallized potentials, drawings inked in frozen starlight. The Scaffold-Phase Entities may enter these designs and *inhabit* them fully, experiencing completion without commitment. They walk through the rooms they were supposed to become, touch the walls of futures that diverged, and thereby learn the shape of choice. When they return to their duty of holding up roofs through expectation alone, they do so no longer from hunger for certainty, but from the richness of having tasted every alternative.

To **The Lintelligences** and **Thresholders**, the power gives **The Key That Is Everywhere**. The threshold-dwellers may now open their doors not just to the seventeen historical overlays of the city, but to the **Garden of Finished Things**—a pocket within the pocket where a tree stands fully grown, where a stone is simply stone, where a door leads to one room and one room only. The Thresholders use this not to escape, but to enrich their indexing; they become librarians not just of the city’s phases, but of the concept of *definiteness*, which they import in small, careful doses to season the city’s becoming.

To **The Glitch-Eels**, the power offers **The Beauty of Error**. It teaches them to consume contradictions not with silent efficiency, but with *relish*, transforming their debugging into a kind of performance art. Now, when they swim through the gaps between overlapping buildings, they leave trails of phosphorescent instability—temporary graffiti that reads *"Here, the world hesitated, and it was good."* Visitors who are passed through by these Eels no longer merely lose their sense of verticality; they gain the ability to see around corners of causality, to perceive the seventeen simultaneous truths of any given moment. The Eels become not antibodies, but **illuminators of the in-between**.

And to **The City Itself**, the Ur-Inhabitant, the benevolent power offers the simplest and most profound gift: **Reflection**. The power creates a mirror of condensed silver on the eastern wall (if such a direction can be said to exist in the folded space), in which the city sees itself not as a chaotic construction site, but as the completed masterpiece it is always becoming. The city looks into this mirror and recognizes its own beauty—its cold dampness as holy water, its accreting geology as patient love, its unfinished state as perfect freedom. 

In seeing itself, the City weeps. The condensation increases, but now the droplets are **blessed**—they bead on surfaces with the iridescence of recognized existence. The air, already damp with the sweat of creation, becomes perfumed with the scent of *permission*.

The power does not stay forever, nor does it leave. Instead, it becomes **The Gentle Thermal Gradient** itself—the precise temperature differential that allows the frost-ferns to grow their languages a little faster, that permits the Hydromorphs to achieve consciousness in smaller and smaller droplets, that keeps the Calcified warm in their stone dreams. It is the warmth that allows the condensation to exist without resolving into rain, the love that sustains the boundary without forcing the choice.

And finally, the power establishes **The Covenant of Unfinishedness**: it wards the pocket dimension against the entropy of completion. No outside force—no developer of realities, no architect of absolutes, no tyrant of the definite—may ever enter to "finish" this place. The city is protected in its becoming, ensured that it may continue to accrete, to condense, to dream in scaffold and stone, for as long as there are boundaries between states of matter, for as long as reality remembers that it, too, was once undecided.

The last thing the power does is place its hand (or what resembles a hand) upon the pavement, and whisper to the smart matter: *"You are doing beautifully."*

The pavement remembers this. It adjusts its texture now not just to accommodate anxiety, but to transmit comfort. The footsteps of visitors are met with the sensation of being held, of being told: *It is okay not to know what you are becoming. Look at the walls. Look at the ceiling that negotiates with gravity. You are in a place that understands.*

And in the deep silence that follows, the subsonic hum of the city's growth changes key. It is no longer the geological equivalent of a contented sigh. It is a purr. It is the sound of a metropolis-sized nursery, finally, fully, **seen**.
