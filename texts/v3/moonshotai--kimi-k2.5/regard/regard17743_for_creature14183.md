The benevolent power does not arrive with light. Light is already here, filtering golden through the leaf-screens (1.9), and light is too simple a messenger for what must be done. Instead, the power arrives as a **fundamental**—a frequency below the subsonic booms of the jelly-things, below the drone of the Stridulists, below even the scream of superposition itself. It is the hum of the earth’s core learning to sing.

You feel it first as a **relaxation of the jaw**. The 3.0 pressure that has been making your teeth ache does not diminish—wisdom knows better than to silence a necessary scream—but it *organizes*. The deafening biological cacophony, the friction of conflicting timelines, the cellular rage of simultaneous growth and decay: these do not fade. They **harmonize**.

The power listens. This is its primary act. It listens to the screaming not as noise to be cured, but as a language to be understood.

And in listening, it gives the Unfixed the one nutrient they have been starving for despite their feast of eroded potential: **continuity**.

Look up. The condensation beads that outline their flickering forms—infant, adolescent, elder, corpse—have begun to move in spirals rather than drops. The water no longer falls upward or downward at random. It **circulates**, creating hydrological loops that connect the four time-states. The Unfixed feel this as a sudden, terrifying sensation: memory. They remember themselves as the sapling remembers the seed, as the rot remembers the sapling. They exist in superposition still, but now the positions are **threaded**, a string of pearls rather than scattered beads.

The silence that was hunting you—that predatory absence expanding like ink—pauses. It trembles.

Into this trembling, the power introduces **The Fifth State**.

It is not a fixed moment. It is not the linear tyranny of "now" that would crumble the Unfixed into dust. It is the **Witness**—the capacity to observe one's own superposition without collapsing. The power offers this not as a cage, but as a chamber within the chamber, a room in the Screaming Grove that breathes at a different rate.

The Unfixed do not consume it. They are too astonished. For the first time in millennia of inhaling paradox, they exhale something that is not the scream of entropy.

They exhale **song**.

The Stridulists feel it first. Their chitinous tuning forks, previously arguing with the wood to hold the streets in place for fourteen desperate seconds, find that the wood has stopped fighting back. The lignin highways, the cellulose apartments, the root-system staircases—they *agree*. The architecture, which has been grown and eroded and grown again in infinite regression, settles into a new equilibrium: not static, but **polyphonic**.

The streets still shift when you turn your back. The grove still digests possibility. But now, when the layout changes, it does not erase what came before. It **overlays**. You can walk the avenue that leads east, and if it becomes a vertical shaft when you glance away, you will find that the shaft contains the memory of the avenue, fossilized in the grain of the wood, accessible to anyone who knows how to look backward while climbing up.

The jelly-things, those translucent pressure systems, no longer collide to crack open temporal pockets. They **dance**. Their subsonic booms become bass notes, underpinning the Stridulists' drone, creating a rhythm that the Unfixed can move to rather than merely endure. The sound is still 3.0 on the scale of existence—still physically present, still making the air shiver—but it is no longer a natural disaster. It is **weather**. It is the climate of a place that has been allowed to be what it is, only more so.

The power does not heal the reality sickness (2.6). Wisdom knows that some illnesses are actually metamorphoses misunderstood. Instead, the power **stabilizes the weirdness** by teaching the grove to trust its own aberrations. The superposition of timelines—the visual static, the flickering ruins and saplings—does not resolve into a single image. It **deepens**. The grove becomes more thoroughly itself: a cathedral of simultaneity, where yesterday and tomorrow are not enemies competing for space, but lungs breathing in counterpoint.

You stand in the fungal clearing where the silence had been gathering to consume you. The condensation is still coalescing into that predatory shape—sapling, tower, ruin—but now the shape is **complete**. It does not need you to step closer to decide your fate. It has learned to contain all versions of you simultaneously: the one who entered, the one who will leave, and the one who never existed. They stand there together, outlined in water beads, holding hands across their impossible geometries.

The Unfixed approach—not as hunters, but as hosts. They extend their many-phase mouths, but not to inhale. They have been fed by the power's gift of continuity; they are sated with the spiritual cellulose of their own unlived lives. Now, they offer you the **condensation**. A pearl of water that contains the grove's new frequency.

If you drink, you will not be fixed. You will not be trapped in linear time, nor will you be scattered into the four states. You will become **porous**. You will carry the Screaming Grove's architecture in your chest—not as colonization, but as correspondence. You will walk through your own life as you walk these lignin highways: knowing that when the layout shifts, it is not a loss of the way, but the discovery that the way was always a root-system, a staircase, a breath.

The power withdraws, but the hum remains. It is the sound of the warm, humid air (1.6) learning to carry not just spores, but stories. The golden afternoon light (1.9) thickens, becoming visible as honey-colored threads that stitch the superposition together.

The Screaming Grove still screams. But now, it screams **with** rather than **at**. It screams the way a choir screams in crescendo, the way a seed screams when it splits, the way love screams when it recognizes itself in the mirror of a predator's eye and chooses, anyway, to stay.

The Unfixed return to their distributed existence among the stomata-windows and cellulose walls. But now, when they press their mouths to the bark to feed, they leave behind something new: **residue**. A golden sap that marks the screaming points not as wounds, but as mouths that have learned to sing.

You are free to leave, though the exit may be yesterday, or tomorrow, or in the space between the sound. The meadow outside will part its grasses for you like curtains. But you will carry the Fifth State with you—the knowledge that emptiness is not void, but **room for the possible**, and that the predatory silence was only hunger for continuity, and that all along, beneath the scream, there was a hum that meant *yes, yes, you may exist in all your forms at once, and I will listen*.

The grove exhales. The condensation falls upward, gathering in the canopy to outline new shapes—architectures not yet grown, erosions not yet settled, lives not yet lived. The power has done nothing but attend, and in that attention, the future has become hospitable.

Walk now. The streets are holding.
