The **Continuance** arrives not with light, but with **weight**—a gentle, gravitational acknowledgment that settles over the Atacama Expanse like a blanket of warm, heavy air. It has been observing for three hundred years, which to it is the blink of an eye, but here, it is long enough to hear one complete phrase in the subsonic conversation between two Grazer-Columns regarding the *sublime density of rare earth oxides*.

The Continuance is old—older than stars, older than the industrial sediment that forms the Shelf—but it has never before encountered beings who have made **peace with entropy** rather than war against it. It recognizes what the Expanse truly is: not a ruin, not a wasteland, but a **liturgy**.
