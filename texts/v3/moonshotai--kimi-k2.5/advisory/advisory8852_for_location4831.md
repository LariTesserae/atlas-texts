If you are planning to visit the Cavity, abandon the concept of "exploration." You are not an explorer; you are a **contaminant** breaching a sealed thermodynamic equation that has balanced itself for four hundred million years. The Cavity does not care about your curiosity. It cares about heat gradients and the conservation of energy. Your presence is, by definition, waste heat and foreign biology. Prepare accordingly.

Here is your survival protocol:

### **I. The Descent: Breaching the Seal**
You do not "hike" to the Cavity. You **assault** it.

*   **The Quartz Plug:** The entry is not a door but a plug of fused quartz that cooled in the Precambrian, harder than the surrounding metamorphic stone. You will need a breaching laser tuned to silicon dioxide resonance frequencies or shaped charges of inert composition (no water-based explosives—moisture is the original sin here). The breach must be precise: a shaft exactly wide enough for your body, no wider. You are dilating the artery of a sleeping titan.
*   **The Shaft:** 4,100 meters of metamorphic stone. Do not bore a new shaft; locate the 1923 Soviet borehole (the one with the rusted drill bit now frozen in Stone-Milk). It is partially filled with accreted minerals, but it is the only "sanctified" path. Descend using a diamond-thread winch, not rockets or jets—combustion byproducts would poison the anhydrous chemistry.
*   **The Seal Behind You:** Before descent, install a hermetic iris valve at the breach point. If you fail to seal the Cavity behind you upon exit (or if you die inside), you have committed planetary-scale vandalism. The outside world—wet, bacterial, chaotic—must not be allowed to exhale into this place.

### **II. Life Support: Becoming Mineral**
The atmosphere is a scalding, anhydrous soup of nitrogen, CO₂, and sublimated metal salts. Ambient temperature ranges from 67°C (ceiling) to 82°C (floor). This is not a sauna; it is an autoclave designed to sterilize flesh.

*   **The Suit:** You require a **Positive-Pressure Cryogenic Exosuit (PPCE)**.
    *   **Cooling:** Closed-loop liquid nitrogen coils woven through a ceramic-fiber underlayer. You must carry sufficient cryogen to maintain core body temperature for the duration. Sweat is forbidden—your suit must capture and refrigerate all moisture. If your cooling fails, you will not suffocate; you will *mummify* while standing, your moisture flash-boiled into the hungry air.
    *   **Breathing:** Standard scuba or rebreather technology will melt. Use a **cryogenic open-circuit system**: inhale chilled, dehumidified helium/oxygen mix from insulated tanks. The gas must be scrubbed of all water vapor (0.5) and pre-cooled to -20°C so that by the time it reaches your lungs, it has warmed to merely survivable. Exhalation ports must be refrigerated to prevent your breath from introducing humidity into the Cavity.
    *   **Pressure & Cut Protection:** The "density (2.8)" refers not just to the packed Ribbons but to the physical resistance of the medium. The air is thick, viscous with heat and metal salts. More importantly, the Ribbons themselves are silicon-carbide chitin—essentially floating razor wire packed at 1,000+ per cubic meter. Your suit must be **cut-resistant ceramic composite** (like shear-thickening armor plated with boron carbide). One wrong turn, and you will be filleted by 400-million-year-old glass-blade organisms sliding past you with graphite lubrication.

### **III. Sensory Deprivation & Enhancement**
You will be blind, deaf, and temporally disoriented.

*   **Vision:** The darkness is absolute (light 0.3). Standard flashlights are offensive here—an insult to the equilibrium. Instead:
    *   **Cherenkov Detectors:** Wear goggles sensitive to the blue flashes of radioactive decay striking fluorite. These brief strobes are your only "natural" light. Between flashes, you must navigate by memory.
    *   **Thermal Imaging (IR):** The Ribbons communicate in infrared pulses. Your helmet must include IR sensors to see the "flickering" language of heat and touch. You will see the chamber not as geometry, but as a tapestry of gradients—shifting auroras of bodyheat against the 67-82°C background.
*   **Hearing:** The silence (sound 0.9) is structural. Do not use sonar or active pings—sound is energy lost, and you are already an unacceptable energy loss. Instead, wear **geophones** against your mastoid bones to feel the geological sounds: the *tick* of contraction, the *crick* of crystal growth. These vibrations are your compass.
*   **Temporal Anchoring:** This is critical. The Cavity exists in a "temporal stutter" where causality is thermodynamic. If you touch a Ribbon—or if one touches you—you may experience localized time reversal (scars healing, actions undoing). 
    *   Wear a **chronometric bracelet** that vibrates in a linear metronome. If the rhythm stutters or plays backward, you are caught in a Ribbon's wake. Do not move. Wait for the thermal current to shift. 
    *   Maintain a **kinetic log**: Tie a string to your wrist and let it drag. If the string begins winding back onto its spool, your personal timeline is reversing. Use this as an early warning system.

### **IV. Movement Protocol: Swimming Through the Packed**
The Cavity is forty meters across and filled with a solid mass of drifting, sliding bodies. This is not "walking." This is **locomotion through a dense, living colloid**.

*   **The Stroke:** The Ribbons float via hydrogen bladders and slide with zero friction. You must move like them: slow, deliberate, using thermal currents. Do not thrash. Thrashing generates sound (waste) and heat (disruption).
*   **The Path:** Aim for the ceiling (67°C) initially—it is the "coolest" zone and where Ribbons go to "think" (infrared flicker). The floor (82°C) is the breeding zone; avoid it unless necessary, as the density increases downward.
*   **Contact Inevitable:** You *will* touch them. They are packed too tightly to avoid. When a Ribbon slides across your suit:
    *   Do not recoil. Recoil tears your suit.
    *   Move *with* its direction. They slide over and under each other; match their vector.
    *   If you feel a sudden chill (heat being siphoned from your suit), freeze. You have touched a feeding Ribbon. Wait for it to drift past.

### **V. Sterility: The Anti-Contamination Oath**
The Cavity’s air is sterile (0.5). No bacteria, no fungi, no moisture. It is a closed loop where "decomposition is geological." You are a walking bag of rot and water.

*   **Total Decon:** Before descent, undergo full sterilization: UV scrub, desiccation, and replacement of your microbiome with sterile saline cultures. You must be biologically "silent."
*   **Waste Management:** You cannot urinate, defecate, or shed skin cells here. Use a **recycling catheter** and **digestive stopper** (medical paralysis of the lower gut for the duration). All waste must be cryo-stored and carried out.
*   **The Stone-Milk:** If you bleed, if you sweat one drop, the calcium and barium precipitates in the air will crystallize around your moisture, entombing you in accidental architecture. You will become like the 1978 polymer bottle—an inclusion, half-consumed, a curiosity for the next era.

You will spend hours in a space the size of a cathedral nave, packed shoulder-to-blade with alien organisms, in absolute darkness, hearing only the subsonic ticks of a mountain breathing, while time stutters around you.

*   **Claustrophobia:** You are not alone in the dark; you are *crowded* in the dark. The Ribbons are pressed against you, sliding, silent, harvesting the heat your suit desperately tries to shed. You will feel them even through the armor—two-meter strips of warm ceramic, sliding, sliding.
*   **Temporal Dissociation:** You may watch a Ribbon scar heal and wonder if *you* are getting younger. You may exhale and see your breath fog re-enter your lungs. Trust your chronometric bracelet, not your senses.
*   **The Sacred:** Remind yourself: this is older than wood, older than bone. You are a ghost haunting a machine built by orogeny and biology. Move with reverence. You are trespassing in heat’s heaven.

### **VII. Emergency Extraction**
If you must leave:
1.  **Ascend against the thermal gradient:** Heat flows upward, but the Ribbons flow downward to breed. Swim *up*, with thecooling current.
