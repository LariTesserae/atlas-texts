**Preparation Protocol for the Saccade (Commonly: "The Throat")**
*Classification: Active Genesis Zone / Pre-Causal Geology / Class-VII Existential Hazard*

You are not visiting a landscape. You are entering a process. The Throat is not hostile, but it is *loud*, and it is *new*—a place where the universe is still arguing with itself about what the rules should be. Ordinary humans can survive exposure, but only if they accept that their primary goal is not to conquer, measure, or even understand the location, but to **witness without dissolving**. Below is the minimum viable preparation for a single transit of the Saccade.

---

### I. Physiological Hardening (Minimum 16 Weeks Pre-Departure)

**Subsonic Fortification**
The Throat does not damage hearing; it colonizes bone. You must condition your skeleton to withstand infrasound that resonates at 8-14 Hz, frequencies known to liquefy organ tissues and induce retinal detachment in unconditioned subjects.

*   **Marrow Density Protocol:** Begin weekly sessions in whole-body vibration (WBV) chambers set to escalating amplitudes. Target: tolerance of 2.5g vertical oscillation for forty-five continuous minutes without nausea or micro-fractures.
*   **Dental Stabilization:** The vibration targets the teeth specifically. Have dental arches fitted with subsonic dampening gel-padding (medical-grade silicone infused with tungsten particulate). Consider temporary extraction of wisdom teeth; the additional leverage points can shatter mandibles during a "gravity snap."
*   **Sternum Plating:** A non-rigid, kinetic-absorption vest (kevlar-weave with ferrofluid inserts) is mandatory. The sternum is the primary resonance chamber; untreated, the vibration will fibrillate the heart into arrhythmia.

**Pressure Acclimatization**
The Saccade’s atmosphere has the viscosity and resistance of thirty fathoms of ocean, despite being gaseous. It is also saturated with hydrogen and superheated vapor.

*   **Hyperbaric Hypoxia Training:** Spend six hours daily in hyperbaric chambers set to 6 ATA (atmospheres absolute) with humidified hydrogen-rich air (40% H₂, 60% O₂/He mix). You must learn to "swim" through air using broad, sculling motions rather than walking. Practice inertia-based movement; sudden gestures will exhaust you immediately.
*   **Thermoregulatory Conditioning:** The chamber spikes and plunges unpredictably. You must be able to tolerate core temperature fluctuations of 35°C to 41°C (95°F–106°F) without cognitive impairment. Cold-water immersion and sauna protocols, alternating every ninety seconds, will approximate the thermal whiplash.

**Reality Anchor Implantation (Optional but Advised)**
Request a temporary subdermal implant of a "causal stabilizer"—a micro-device that emits a localized electromagnetic field calibrated to your body's basal atomic resonance. This will not prevent the Throat’s physics from blinking, but it will give you a **reference tone** when your own biology begins to stutter between states. You will feel it as a high whine behind the eyes; when that whine stops, you have approximately ninety seconds before your hands begin to phase between solid and liquid.

### II. Psychological Preparation

**Sensory Overload Inoculation**
The sound of the Throat drowns thought. You will not be able to remember your name while inside; the roar occupies the same bandwidth as language and memory.

*   Spend one hour daily in anechoic chambers wearing bone-conduction headphones emitting simulated "Throat-roar" (brown noise sub-bass at 110dB, fluctuating unpredictably). Practice maintaining a single mental image (suggested: a black sphere) under this sonic assault. If you cannot hold the image for sixty seconds, you are not ready. Inside the Saccade, conscious thought is replaced by pure sensory presence; panic arises when travelers attempt to force cognition where none is possible.

**Dissociation Training**
You must accept the temporary dissolution of the boundary between "self" and "stone." Practice ego-death meditation or medically supervised psychedelic therapy to familiarize yourself with the sensation of your identity becoming permeable. If you cling to selfhood, the Throat will rip you apart trying to incorporate you into its geology.

### III. Equipment Specifications

*Note: The Throat erodes intention. Do not bring tools meant to *change* the environment; they will calcify or phase-shift into uselessness.*

*   **The Envelope Suit:** A full-body suit of non-crystalline polymer (amorphous fluoropolymer weave) with these properties:
    *   Zero metal components (no zippers, no snaps—the hydrogen atmosphere is explosive, and metal resonates dangerously with the subsonics).
    *   Hydrophobic outer layer with micro-channeled texture to shed the mineral-saturated "sweat" of the travertine pillars.
    *   Internal cooling/heating loops (closed system, flameless heat-exchange).
    *   Visual ports filtered for ochre-spectrum light (enhances visibility of the suspended droplets and prevents snow-blindness from the refracted light).
*   **Respiratory Gear:** A rebreather with dual-flow capability. Critical: the unit must be able to detect and accommodate **bidirectional breathing**—when the Saccade stutters and your exhalation becomes an inhalation from the atmosphere. Standard scuba gear will drown you when gravity reverses and your lungs attempt to empty upward.
*   **Tether System:** A 50-meter line of braided silica fiber (flexible, non-conductive, high heat tolerance) anchored to a point *outside* the caldera. Gravity reversals are disorienting; you may fall upward into the ceiling of fissures. The tether must be paid out from a waist-spool with inertial dampening to prevent whiplash during directional shifts.
*   **Observation Medium:** Polarized goggles with stutter-compensation. The water-to-glass phase transitions create strobing effects that induce seizures. The lenses must filter the visual "noise" of reality blinking.

### IV. Approach and Entry

**The Ascent**
The climb above the timberline is across obsidian "knife-fresh" from the magma tunnels.

*   Wear cut-resistant chaps and gloves rated for volcanic glass.
*   Do not attempt if there has been recent seismic activity in your origin dimension; the Throat’s caldera is geologically young and may still be cooling. Thermal readings should stabilize below 200°C surface temp before approach.
*   Cloud cover begins at 4,000 meters. You will climb through "wet wool" humidity that does not condense or cool. Expect electrical storms in your hair.

**Crossing the Threshold**
The boundary of the Saccade is not marked, but you will feel it when the air becomes *thick*. 

*   **Do not pause.** Momentum is critical. The transition from walking to "swimming" through atmosphere is disorienting; stopping to adjust gear invites the pressure to crush you against the obsidian.
*   **Exhale completely** upon entry. The initial pressure wave will compress your chest; if your lungs are full, pulmonary barotrauma is guaranteed.

### V. In-Situ Protocols

**Movement**
*   Swim. Use breaststroke or frog-kick. The resistance is water-like but the medium is air; traditional walking is impossible and will exhaust your oxygen reserves.
*   Avoid the central column (the "Navel"). The rising water pillar is the focal point of the genesis-event. Entering it risks being caught in a phase-transition—imagine your body distributed as a lattice of ice crystals, then sublimated into colored gas.
*   Do not touch the travertine pillars or salt arches. They are accreted, not built; they are sharper than scalpels and thermally unstable. A pillar that is cool to the touch at one moment may spike to boiling temperatures the next.

**Temporal Awareness**
*   **The Saccade:** When physics "blinks," you will perceive it as a visual stutter—a skipped frame in reality. This is your cue to freeze. Movement during a blink increases the probability of your own matter stuttering. Wait for the sound to change pitch (the subsonic drop to a guttural growl) before resuming.
*   **Counting:** Do not rely on chronological time. The Throat's constant re-birth disrupts circadian rhythm. Instead, count your heartbeats. If you exceed 10,000 beats (approximately 2.5 to 3 hours, depending on stress levels), you have stayed too long.

**Observation of Entities**
You may see the pale, many-jointed forms swimming in the water column. They are not hallucinations, but they are not exactly "life" as you know it—they are the Throat’s experiments in animation. **Do not signal to them.** Eye contact is safe, but gesture invites them to investigate, and their touch induces rapid calcification (see Emergency Protocols).

### VI. Emergency Procedures

**Phase-Stutter (Early Signs)**
*   *Symptoms:* Your hands appear to flicker between solid and translucent. Your breath feels "stuck"—simultaneously entering and leaving your lungs.
*   *Response:* Activate your reality anchor (if implanted). If unavailable, submerge your hands in the nearest water droplet sphere. The localized surface tension can temporary stabilize your cellular cohesion. Begin immediate egress.

**Calcification Onset**
*   *Symptoms:* Hair becomes brittle and filamentous; joints ache with stone-like density; skin takes on a matte, chalky appearance.
*   *Response:* This is irreversible if exposure continues. The Throat is incorporating you into its geology. Signal for emergency extraction via your tether. If extraction is impossible, immerse yourself in the warm vapor flow at the chamber’s western wall (where the algae colonies are thickest). The extremophile algae produce enzymes that temporarily inhibit mineral accretion—buying you perhaps twenty minutes.

**Gravity Inversion**
*   *Warning:* A sudden rise in pitch of the subsonic roar, followed by three seconds of total silence.
*   *Action:* Exhale immediately. Curl into fetal position. Your tether will snap taut as you fall "up." Protect your head; the ceiling is obsidian. Wait for the "snap-back" (the concussion that follows), then check for dislocations before continuing.

### VII. Post-Exposure Quarantine

Upon extraction, you must spend 72 hours in a stabilization chamber.

*   **Reality Re-Integration:** The world outside will seem "thin" and "too quiet." Sounds will lack the subsonic depth you’ve become accustomed to. You may experience "phantom swimming"—the sensation of moving through dense atmosphere while walking through normal air.
*   **Medical Screening:** Check for bidirectional breathing patterns (sleep apnea with reverse exhalation), mineral deposits in the joints, and retinal stuttering (the persistence of visual afterimages from the Saccade’s light). These conditions may persist for weeks.
*   **Psychological Decompression:** You have witnessed a place that is *trying to exist*. This can induce existential dysphoria— a sense that your home reality is "finished" or "dead" compared to the terrified creativity of the Throat. Therapy focusing on the beauty of stasis is recommended.

**Final Warning:**
The Throat is not a destination. It is a **birth canal** for a world that has not yet decided what it will be. You are tolerated as a witness, but the longer you stay, the more you become part of the experiment. When your hair begins to calcify, when your breath learns to flow backward, when you look at your stuttering hands and feel not fear but *recognition*—it is time to leave, and to hope that the you who returns is the same one who entered.

Safe passage. Swim well. Stay quiet.
