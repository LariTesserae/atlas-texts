### II. KINETIC FOOTING: THE ART OF WALKING ON SLOW CATASTROPHE

**Crossing Protocols**

- **Single vertical spire**: Safe passage, stable plate center.
- **Two stones leaning against each other**: Boundary crossing possible here; the lean indicates drift direction.
- **Three stones in a triangle**: **DO NOT CROSS**. Temporal eddy field. You will emerge synchronized with a geological epoch where you do not exist.
- **Ring of stones with center hollow**: Shelter from Echo-Drifters; acoustic null point.

### IV. HYDRATION & NUTRITION: THE FISSURE ECONOMY

Accepting your insignificance is the only way to tolerate the isolation. Speak to the grinding plates. They do not hear you, but the vibration of your voice in your own chest reminds you that you are still solid matter, not yet dust.
