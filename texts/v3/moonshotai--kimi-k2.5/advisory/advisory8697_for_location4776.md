**Field Manual Entry: Preparation for the High Manufactuary**  
*Classification: Extreme Biomechanical Hazard, Class-IV Temporal Instability, Non-Anthropocentric Environment*

---

### I. Physiological Hardening (Begin 8 Weeks Prior)

You are not climbing a mountain; you are intruding upon a metabolic process. The Manufactuary exists at the threshold where atmosphere frays into exosphere. Begin hypoxic training immediately—sleep in altitude tents, practice diaphragmatic breathing in cold water immersion, and learn to recognize the early hallucinations of hypoxia (which, here, may not be hallucinations at all, but overlap with temporal echoes).

**Critical:** Desensitize your startle response. The acoustic environment is not merely loud; it is *spatially dishonest*. A steam vent twenty miles distant will scream beside your ear, while the grinding lignin piston directly overhead may register as a subsonic thrum in your teeth. Practice meditation in industrial noise chambers until you can maintain consciousness when the sound pressure exceeds 120 decibels. If you are prone to migraines or vestibular disorders, abort the mission. The gravity wells around active processing centers will make your inner ear lie to you, and vomiting inside a sealed suit in sub-zero temperatures is a death sentence.

---

### II. The Carapace: Wardrobe as Survival Architecture

The Manufactuary’s air is not air; it is a *cold soup*—supersaturated with vapor, nitrogen compounds, and microscopic crystalline polymers flaked from the factory walls. Standard cold-weather gear will fail. You need:

**The Second Skin:** A full-body immersion suit of non-porous nanofiber (silk-weave graphene or spun aramid), completely seamless, with osmotic seals at ankles and wrists. The weeping surfaces of the towers secrete an adhesive lymph that bonds to epidermis on contact. If bare skin touches the bark-cooling-tower hybrid, you will become a permanent fixture, calcified into the architecture within hours.

**Thermal Regulation:** The temperature hovers at 1°C, but the ambient humidity is 100%. Hypothermia will set in through evaporative cooling even if you are not wet. Use phase-change material (PCM) linings that solidify at 4°C, storing latent heat against your core. Avoid down—when the mineral steam hits, it will condense into calcium carbonate on your feathers, petrifying your insulation.

**Respiration:** The atmosphere is breathable but *thick*; viscous with exhaled chemistry from the catalytic converters. Bring a rebreather with activated charcoal and lime scrubbers, not for toxicity (the air is sterile), but for density. Breathing raw Manufactuary air is like inhaling liquid glass; your alveoli will strain against the surface tension of the supersaturated vapor.

**Footwear:** The substrate is leather-smooth, often inclined toward the warm pools, and occasionally subject to rapid-growth cambium eruptions. Use magnetized crampons with retractable micro-spikes, but ensure the soles are ceramic-coated. The ground is alive, and it hungers. The warm basins are digestive. One slip into the chartreuse-tinged water, and you are enzymatically reduced to base salts to feed the system.

### III. Temporal Navigation: Kit for the Unstuck

Reality here is a processed slurry. Time does not flow; it pools, evaporates, and condenses. You will encounter **temporal folds**—zones where a second lasts a year, or where your future corpse lies calcified three steps ahead.

**Chrono-Anchor:** Wear a wrist-mounted atomic clock synced to your home continuum, but more importantly, carry a **sandglass of crushed basalt** (non-reactive, non-organic). If the sand stops falling, you have entered a stasis pocket. Do not move. Wait for the fold to pass, or you will step out of sync and watch your companions age to dust while you blink.

**Marking the Loop:** Bring spray-chalk containing iodine isotopes. Mark your path every ten meters. If the chalk mark is warm, faded, or shows your own bootprint overlapping it from the wrong direction, you are in recursion. Backtrack immediately. Do not follow your own footprints forward, even if they look fresh. That is not you; that is the Manufactuary processing a template of you, and if you meet yourself, both instances collapse into woody tumor-growths.

**The Stillness Prohibition:** The ground calcifies the static. Install a **Ventalian Metronome** in your suit—a haptic device that pulses with the rhythm of the vascular bellows (approximately 14 beats per minute). Move with this rhythm. Step when the bellows contract, pause when they expand. To stand still is to invite mineral accretion; the steam vents deposit calcium and silica on unmoving objects, encasing them in geological instants. Even when sleeping, rock in this cadence.

### IV. Acoustic and Optical Countermeasures

**Hearing:** Standard earplugs will kill you. You need **bone-conduction communication** with active noise cancellation tuned to the specific grinding frequencies of cellulose gears (20–40 Hz fundamentals with harmonic screech at 2kHz). You must hear the low thuds of peristaltic valves to predict extrusions, but the directional confusion means you cannot trust your ears for localization. Rely on triangulation—carry three ultrasonic emitters and map returns via head-up display.

**Vision:** The light is dim, chartreuse, and biologically sourced. It pulses at 0.25 Hz, which induces hypnagogic states in humans. Wear full-spectrum goggles with a red-light overlay (red is the wavelength least utilized by the factory-organisms, so it cuts through the bioluminescence without triggering photosensitive defensive mechanisms). 

**Shadow Management:** Shadows fall upward or sideways near processing centers. Attach **gyroscopic shadow-lamps** to your shoulders—lights that pivot to always cast your shadow directly beneath you, preventing disorientation when gravity wells twist the local light cone.

### V. Provisions: The External Metabolism

Do not eat anything here. The crystalline polymers eaten by the radiator-finned grazers are industrial waste to them, but to you, they are thermosetting resins that will polymerize in your stomach into a solid mass. The gas-bag fauna are filled with hydrogen—explosive and nutritionally void. The ceramic-shelled insects are silicon-based; your enzymes cannot touch them.

**Hydration:** The weeping water is lymph. It is warm (35–40°C) and will digest you. The mist is potable but empty; it carries no minerals, only the ghost of processing. Bring **hydrogel canteens** with sealed spouts; the ambient moisture will osmose through the gel, contaminating your supply with exhaled nitrogen compounds. Double-seal everything. Eat high-density lipid bars (1200 kcal per 100g) and desiccated protein. You are in a place of pure industry—it consumes, it does not provide.

### VI. Fauna Etiquette

The creatures here are not predators; they are **maintenance subroutines**. The ceramic insects will burrow into your suit seams if you remain stationary (heat-seeking). The bladder-birds are harmless but their bellowing is mating calls that resonate at frequencies that shatter ice—protect your ear-drums when they inflate. The grazers are massive, warm, and indifferent, but their radiator fins shed scalding steam. Do not approach them; they are walking pressure valves.

If swarmed by the mantis-shrimp strikers (which crack the substrate for minerals), deploy **chaff**—carry packets of powdered cellulose. They will mistake it for factory-effluent and focus on that instead of your ceramic boots.

### VII. Emergency Protocols: Extraction from Eternity

**Calcification:** If you feel your boots growing heavy, or see white mineral blooms creeping up your shins, you have violated the Stillness Prohibition. Immediately inject **cortisol stimulants** and sprint toward the nearest steam vent—yes, toward it. The updraft disrupts the accretion process, and the heat will shatter the nascent stone layer. Accept second-degree burns over petrification.

**Temporal Recursion:** If you see yourself, **do not make eye contact**. Look at your feet. Walk backward along your ingress path using your chalk marks. Do not speak; your voice will create a harmonic resonance in the time-fold that collapses the probability wave, trapping you in a loop of your own dying screams.

**Lost in the Mist:** Visibility is twenty meters. If separated from your party, deploy a **spar buoy**—a hydrogen-filled balloon on a titanium tether, illuminated with UV light (invisible to local fauna, visible to human retinas through goggles). The wind patterns are chaotic, but the buoy will drift toward the low-pressure zones above the warm pools. Do not follow it; use it as a fixed point to calculate your position.

**The Ultimate Exit:** If catastrophic failure occurs, trigger your emergency beacon. However, know that rescue may arrive before you left, or centuries after you have become part of the substrate. The High Manufactuary does not care if you leave. It only processes.

### VIII. Psychological Inoculation

Before you step through the cloud banks, accept this: The Manufactuary produces nothing. It refines existence into itself, a closed metabolic loop of eternal maintenance. You will see beauty in the vascular bellows, the weeping copper-colored towers, the mist that never clears. You will want to rest, to lean against that warm, pulsing wall, to sleep in the rhythm of the vents.

Resist. 

This place is not hell, not heaven, but a machine grown so old it forgot its purpose. You are a contaminant in the process. Move with the rhythm, keep your shadow beneath you, and do not touch the walls. 

The ground remembers everything it has worn away. Do not let it remember you.
