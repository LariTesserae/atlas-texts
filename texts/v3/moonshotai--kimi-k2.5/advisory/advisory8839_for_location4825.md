**Field Preparation Brief: The Hollow (Designation: Neo-Geothermal Industrial Cavity)**
**Threat Level:** Extreme Environmental / Structural / Psychological  
**Maximum Recommended Duration:** 4-6 hours active exposure / 2 hours passive observation  

---

### I. The Thermal Reality: Surviving the Wet Burn
You are not entering a desert; you are entering a lung. The Hollow operates at **2.9 thermal intensity** with **0.6 saturation**—a combination that kills unprotected humans in minutes through hyperthermia and drowned-alveoli syndrome. Standard cooling systems fail here because sweat cannot evaporate in near-100% humidity; it merely beads and boils.

**Mandatory Gear:**
- **Closed-loop liquid cooling suit**: Full-coverage garment with refrigerated coolant (ice packs are useless—they melt instantly and add to your moisture burden). The suit must maintain a strict 22°C core temperature against an ambient that likely exceeds 60°C (140°F) in the lower strata.
- **Desiccant respirator**: Not a filter—an active chemical dehumidifier. The "damp" here carries dissolved sulfides and chlorides from the mineral vegetation. Without it, you will waterboard yourself on every breath.
- **Phase-change underlayers**: Paraffin-wax textiles beneath the cooling suit to buffer against the "clinging scorch" during suit maintenance cycles.

**Protocol:** Pre-cool your body to hypothermic levels (35°C/95°F) before entry. You are borrowing time against the heat debt.

### II. The Vertical Constraint: Navigating the Compression
There is no "ground" in the Hollow—only **fused slag and "jagged origami."** The urban density (2.4) manifests as twelve to fifteen vertical levels of industrial catwalks spiraling inward, anchored by explosive bolts into fresh basalt. Navigation is three-dimensional climbing, not walking.

**Mandatory Gear:**
- **Magnetic climbing boots with ceramic insulators**: The surfaces are electrified (1.6 industrial) and slick with chemical condensation. Standard rubber soles degrade in minutes. Magnetics allow traversal of the vertical walls when catwalks are impassable.
- **Shock-absorbing grapple harness**: The "shelves" are crowded with moving machinery operating in "slow, tidal cycles." You will need to clip off and hang in negative space while hydraulic arms swing through your path.
- ** articulating knee/elbow pads**: Basalt knife-edges will shred standard clothing. The rock is geologically fresh—sharp enough to open arteries through fabric.

**Critical Warning:** Do not trust the "geologically impossible angles." Control panels bolted to tilted surfaces appear level due to steam-induced vertigo. Carry a gyroscopic level; your inner ear will lie to you.

### III. The Mechanical Ecosystem: Do Not Interrupt the Metabolism
This is an **active (2.2)** space with a purely mechanical ecology. The Hollow digests stone into structure; it will digest you into slag if you interfere with its "closed loop."

**Behavioral Protocols:**
- **The Harmonic Pause**: Machinery operates in synchronized rhythms (the "weather system of micro-fronts"). Before moving through any level, observe for at least ten minutes to learn the tidal cycle of the hydraulic arms. Movement occurs during the exhalation phase of the steam valves.
- **No Touch Policy**: The "fraudulent vegetation" (chloride fans, sulfurous tufts) is delicate mineral accretion that harvests heat. Breaking them disrupts local thermal regulation and triggers aggressive condensation redistribution—imagine triggering a flash flood of boiling chemical water.
- **Cable Avoidance**: Wrist-thick cables sag between levels, insulation frayed by "chemical drizzle." Treat everything as live current. The hollow is young; the wiring hasn't had time to fail safely.

**Emergency Extraction:** Identify the **expansion joints** in the upper gantries—these mark the original boreholes. The Hollow is a bubble; ascent follows the spiral inward, not outward. Mark your route with heat-resistant chem-lights (standard plastics melt).

### IV. Sensory Alteration: The Quiet and the Dim
**Sound (1.4):** The heat absorbs noise. You will hear your own heartbeat amplified, the subsonic groan of expanding metal (carry anti-nausea medication—low-frequency vibration induces vertigo), and the unsettling *tick...tick...* of cooling sinks that sound like biological respiration. The quiet is not peaceful; it is pressurized. Bring a white-noise generator or risk auditory hallucinations.

**Light (1.3 dim):** Amber shadows and phosphorescent smears. Standard flashlights bleach out the subtle orange glow and destroy night vision. Use **thermal imaging goggles** instead—the machinery and rock walls have distinct heat signatures, and the "dull orange" coils provide enough contrast for navigation without additional illumination.

**Weirdness (0.7):** The cognitive dissonance of "excavated" machinery (installed vs. grown) causes spatial disorientation. The Hollow looks like it built itself, chewing downward. Carry a tactile focus object—something organic (wood, leather, bone)—to remind your nervous system that reality outside follows different rules.

### V. Biological Protocols: Nothing to Eat, Nothing to Breathe
- **Atmosphere**: The air is steam saturated with volcanic outgassing. Assume low oxygen displacement in lower levels. Carry redundant rebreather tanks (nitrox mix); the mechanical ecosystem consumes oxygen for oxidation processes.
- **Hydration**: Despite the condensation, **do not drink local water.** It collects in "intermittent reservoirs" contaminated with dissolved heavy metals from the mineral accretions. Bring double your calculated water needs—the cooling suit will fail without distilled water top-offs.
- **Food**: No fauna (0.1), no flora. The ecosystem is gears consuming pressure. Pack calorically dense, sealed rations. Opening food releases scent molecules that may interfere with the delicate chemical balance of the chamber (the "damp" carries information in this mechanical ecology).

### VI. Psychological Fortification
You will be alone with machines that breathe steam in tidal rhythms. The density (2.2) creates a sensation of being *swallowed*—vertical walls pressing inward, twelve levels of industry above you, no sky, no horizon.

**Preparation:**
- **Claustrophobia screening**: If you experience panic in elevators, do not enter.
- **Temporal anchors**: Watches will fog immediately. Use a sealed chronometer. Time dilation occurs in thermal environments; without metrics, visitors report subjective hours passing in minutes.
- **Organic reminders**: Place a living leaf or feather in your suit's viewfinder. The Hollow contains no biology; prolonged exposure induces "mechanical empathy"—a dissociative state where visitors begin identifying with the pumps and valves. Touch something alive every hour.

### VII. The Exit Strategy
The Hollow is geologically new—**"knife-sharp, fresh-split."** It is still settling. Seismic activity is likely as the basalt cools and contracts.

**Extraction Protocol:**
1. Maintain line-of-sight (through steam) to your entry point at all times.
2. If machinery activation blocks your route, **wait**. Do not attempt to force hydraulic arms; they are stronger than rescue equipment.
3. The "weather system" of steam eddies can create whiteout conditions. Lay flat against the wall during heavy valve-exhalation events; the floor is not safe due to slag and moving components.

**Final Note:** The Hollow manufactures nothing you can see—it only maintains itself, compressing and condensing. You are not a guest here; you are a temporary contaminant in a closed loop. Stay long enough to observe, document, and leave before the machinery decides you are raw material for the next layer of accretion.

**Recommended Pre-Visit Vaccinations:** Tetanus (explosive bolts may be sharp), broad-spectrum antifungal prophylaxis (the wet heat breeds extremophile microbes in your gear), and psychological evaluation for susceptibility to "industrial awe" (the compulsion to touch the beautiful, terrible machinery).
