**PREPARATION BRIEFING: THE LITHOGENIC CRADLE**
*Classification: Active Genesis Zone | Temporal Index: Pre-Erosional (T-0.1) | Hazard Rating: Extreme*

---

### I. THE PSYCHOLOGICAL REALIGNMENT

Before you pack a single item, understand that you are not entering a landscape—you are entering a **process**. The Cradle is not *where* mountains are; it is *how* mountains become. You will stand inside a lung that never stops inhaling, surrounded by the violence of unfiltered creation. 

Prepare for **temporal vertigo**. In normal geology, you are the mayfly; stone is eternal. Here, stone is the mayfly. You will watch architecture extrude itself like toothpaste from a tube, watch your exit route shrink as the floor swells beneath you. You must abandon the anthropocentric delusion of stable ground. The floor rises at roughly 2-4 millimeters per minute. In a six-hour expedition, the ceiling will drop by half a meter relative to your starting elevation. You are climbing a staircase that builds itself beneath your feet while you stand upon it.

Prepare for **sensory overwhelming**. The subsonic factory-howl—described by survivors as "a cello string the width of a river being bowed by a giant"—will resonate in your sternum, your teeth, your marrow. It does not stop. It is the sound of strong nuclear force being manually adjusted. Combined with the aggressive humidity (supersaturated tropical air at altitude), you may experience panic attacks that feel like drowning in warm, vibrating wool.

---

### II. THE ARMOR: CUT-RESISTANT, VIBRATION-DAMPENING, CHEMICALLY INERT

The stone here is **newborn and malicious**. It has not been tumbled by rivers or smoothed by wind. Every surface is a cleavage plane, a crystal face, a knife-edge waiting for blood.

**The Exosuit (Mandatory):**
- **Material**: Woven basalt-fiber mesh over a shear-thickening fluid layer (like liquid armor). Standard Kevlar will suffice but expect fraying. The stone is sharp enough to shave electrons.
- **Coverage**: Total. Gloves must extend to elbows; the walls are "tender" and leave prints—you will touch them to steady yourself, and they will bite back. The crystal faces vibrate; prolonged contact causes nerve damage from subsonic transmission.
- **Footwear**: Crampons are useless (the stone is too fresh, too "wet"—you'll just tear up the floor). Instead, use **mag-hybrid boots** with adjustable suction cups. The floor is irregular, concave, and the "silver water" behaves like mercury—dense, high surface tension, zero traction. You will hydroplane on warm mineral broth.
- **Head Protection**: A climbing helmet with **vibration-dampening gel inserts**. The water globules falling from the ceiling are not rain; they are heavy, mineral-saturated condensate that accumulates mass until surface tension fails. They strike with the density of falling shot puts wrapped in velvet. Concussions are common.

**The Barrier Layer:**
- **Respirator**: Full-face, with ozone and particulate filters. The air smells of "potential" and ozone—this indicates free radicals and nanoscale silicate dust shearing from the Exuders' output. You are breathing the smoke of creation. Without filtration, you will develop silicosis in hours, not years.
- **Eye Protection**: Photosensitive goggles that can filter spectra beyond human vision. The Exuders bleed light in colors that don't exist; staring at them causes retinal fatigue and hallucinations of impossible geometry.

### III. NAVIGATION IN THE PRE-WEATHER

**Forget maps.** The only permanent features are the **hollow arteries** left by stone-eels. Everything else—pillars, walls, floor texture—is younger than your breakfast.

**The Tether System:**
- Carry **500 meters of monofilament carbon line** with ultrasonic beacons every ten meters. The constructor-bats navigate by EM fields, which will fry your compass and GPS. You must physically trace your route.
- **Temporal Markers**: Spray-paint is useless (it won't adhere to unfinished stone, or the stone will grow over it in minutes). Instead, carry **chemical flares** that burn cold and bright green. Place them at your entry point and refresh them hourly. If you lose sight of your first flare, you have minutes to find it before the rising floor creates a new topography between you and salvation.

**The Geometry of Emergence:**
- That "doorway standing alone" is not a portal to somewhere else—it is a **topological shortcut** through higher-dimensional space. If you step through, you will be ten meters higher, looking down at your past self. Carry a **laser rangefinder** to detect these anomalies (they distort light slightly, creating a mirage effect). If you must use them to escape rising water or closing walls, mark the exit point immediately with a strobe beacon, as the geometry shifts every 20-30 minutes.

---

### IV. HYDRATION & SUSTENANCE: DO NOT CONSUME THE POTENTIAL

The water here is **condensate from manufacturing**, heavy with dissolved silicates, warm, and slightly viscous. It is not poison, exactly, but it is pre-geological. Drinking it is like swallowing liquid scaffolding.

- **Water**: Bring double your normal supply. The humidity is 100%+ and tropical; you will sweat endlessly, but the "silver water" will dehydrate you further through osmotic pressure—it is mineral-heavy and will pull water from your cells.
- **Food**: High-calorie, zero-prep items. You cannot light a fire (ozone-rich air + sparks = explosions). The lithophilic lichens glow green and smell like copper; they are technically edible but will bind the minerals in your blood into stones. Do not eat the lichens. Do not eat the resonance shrimp (they are full of nano-assemblers that will attempt to "repair" your digestive tract into basalt).

---

### V. FAUNA INTERACTION PROTOCOLS

**Stone-Eels**: These creatures are not predators of flesh; they are predators of **coherence**. They tunnel by enzymatically softening silicate. If one passes beneath you, the floor will briefly become quicksand-thick mud, then harden instantly, entombing your feet. 
- **Defense**: Carry **sonic pikes** tuned to the resonant frequency of the Cradle's hum. The eels avoid the growth fronts where Exuders are dense; stay near the active crystal spires (the ones weeping ozone and rising visibly).

**Resonance Shrimp**: They cluster on crystal faces and filter the heavy water. They have no heads, only rings of mouths, and they click in rhythm with the machinery. 
- **Danger**: Individually harmless. In swarms (which occur when an Exuder blooms), they will attempt to "clean" you, scraping skin with their mandibles, seeking nano-assemblers in your sweat. 
- **Defense**: Wear slick, non-porous outer layers. They cannot grip silicone-coated fabric.

**Constructor-Bats**: Blind to sound, seeing only EM fields. 
- **Danger**: They will fly directly through you if you stand between an Exuder and a growth site, mistaking you for a pillar of appropriate conductivity. 
- **Defense**: Wear a **Faraday cage cloak** (copper mesh) when stationary. They will perceive you as "ground" and navigate around you.

### VI. TIME MANAGEMENT: THE COUNTDOWN

You have **four hours, maximum**.

After that:
1. The floor will have risen enough that the entry fissure (30 meters up) is now 32 meters up, and the walls have compressed inward.
2. The Exuders' ambient radiation will have saturated your electronics.
3. The stone-eels will have sensed your thermal signature and begun rerouting the local geology to investigate.

Set a **silent countdown timer** (vibrating, not audible—the hum will drown out beeps). At hour three, begin your return regardless of objectives. The way out is closing like a healing wound.

---

### VII. EMERGENCY: IF TRAPPED BY GROWTH

If the walls press close or the floor rises to meet the ceiling:
- Do **not** attempt to cut the stone. It is unfinished; it will seal around your blade and continue growing, trapping the tool and your hand.
- Use the **ladder made of solidified sound** if you can find it (it leans against the rising wall). It is the only structure not made of matter; it is a standing wave, an acoustic scaffold. It cannot be cut, only muffled. Climb it quickly—if the Exuders shift frequency, it will dissipate into echo.
- As a last resort: **trigger a thermal spike**. Use a flare or chemical heat pack. The Exuders modulate the strong nuclear force based on quantum foam density; sudden heat creates localized turbulence in their lattice, causing a 30-second pause in growth. Use this window to squeeze through gaps.

---

### VIII. THE FINAL CHECK

Before stepping through the fissure that bleeds warmth, touch your gear and recite:

*I am soft. The stone is sharp.  
I am slow. The stone is fast.  
I am finished. The stone is becoming.*

Then step inside, into the wet, silver, roaring dark, and try not to bleed on the geology. It is young, and your blood would be the first story it learns about the world.
