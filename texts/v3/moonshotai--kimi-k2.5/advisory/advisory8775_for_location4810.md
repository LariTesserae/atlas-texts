**Preparation Protocol for the Hum-Locked Cavern (Entropic Stasis Zone, Industrial Climax Biome)**

*Classification: Class-Omega Temporal Anomaly / Zero-Entropy Environment / Biological Stasis Risk*

---

### I. Psychological & Temporal Conditioning (Critical)

You are preparing to enter a space where **time is a standing wave**. This is not poetic; it is a physical condition that will assault your neurochemistry. Human consciousness evolved to track change—to notice the wilting of leaves, the cooling of coffee, the decay of light. Here, nothing wilts, cools, or decays. The lack of micro-changes will trigger a dissociative state known as **Stasis Sickness** or "The Freeze."

**Pre-Departure Training:**
- **Duration Simulation:** Spend 72 hours in a sensory deprivation chamber with *zero* stimuli variation. No temperature fluctuation, no sound variation, no light shift. Learn to tolerate the panic that arises when your brain cannot locate itself in temporal space.
- **Paradox Immersion:** Practice observing motion that does not progress. Study zoetropes, Möbius strips, and looped GIFs until the cognitive dissonance of "moving but not going" becomes background noise. The mobile fauna move through gear-trains; they are in motion, yet they have no destination. You must learn to find this normal.
- **Entropic Re-calibration:** Before entry, spend one week in hyper-entropy environments (fast-growing jungles, active construction sites, bacterial cultures) to "load up" on change. Your body will carry this metabolic momentum into the stasis; it is your only protection against becoming geometric.

**Mental Prohibitions:**
- Do **not** bring timekeeping devices that rely on entropy (sand timers, burning candles, mechanical watches with escapements). They will either cease functioning or enter their own eternal loop, driving you mad. If you must track duration, use a neural implant set to count your own heartbeats—but be warned: your heart may attempt to synchronize with the Hum.
- Abandon narrative. You cannot "explore" this place. You can only **occupy** it temporarily.

### II. Sensory Armor: The Seventeen Frequencies

The Hum is not sound; it is a structural property of the space. It will blur your vision through resonance of the vitreous humor. It will set your teeth singing against each other via bone conduction. It is seventeen discrete frequencies holding matter in suspension.

**Required Equipment:**
- **Cranial Vibration Dampers:** A fitted skullcap lined with piezoelectric counter-oscillators tuned to the inverse phase of the seventeen frequencies. Standard earplugs are useless; the sound travels through your skeleton.
- **Dental Stabilizers:** Custom-fitted mouthguards with ceramic inlays that lock your teeth in fixed positions. Without these, the harmonic resonance will loosen your molars over time. Several travelers have returned with teeth that continued to vibrate for weeks.
- **Ocular Gel Inserts:** The Hum blurs vision by agitating the fluid in your eyes. You need viscoelastic gel contacts that dampen high-frequency oscillation. Bring spares; if they dry out (and they will), they become brittle and sharp.
- **Larynx Suppression:** Do not speak. Your voice cannot compete with the Hum, and attempting to do so risks vocal cord hemorrhage. Communicate via pre-arranged hand signals or text-to-speech devices with **no speakers** (bone-conduction only, shielded from external frequencies).

### III. Environmental Survival: The Fever That Never Breaks

The cavern maintains a constant fever-heat (approximately 39-40°C / 102-104°F) with absolute zero humidity. The air is a desiccant more aggressive than any desert. The basalt is "young"—sharper than glass, unweathered, and bleeding alkaline powder.

**Hydration Protocol:**
- **Pre-hydrate to Dangerous Levels:** Drink 5-7 liters of isotonic solution with membrane-stabilizing lipids immediately before entry. Your body must carry all necessary moisture; you cannot drink inside. Any water you bring will be chemically locked into anhydrous mineral salts within minutes of exposure to the air.
- **Recycling Suit:** Wear a closed-loop cooling suit with internal condensation traps. Your sweat is your only renewable water source. The suit must have **no external moving parts** (no fans, no pumps) that could introduce friction or change to the environment.
- **Thermal Regulation:** Phase-change cooling packs set to 38°C. Any colder, and you risk creating a temperature gradient—a "cold spot" that the metallic lichen will detect and attempt to colonize. You do not want to become a nutrient source.

**Physical Protection:**
- **Basalt Weave Coveralls:** The rock teeth will slice standard fabrics. You need overlapping ceramic platelets or diamond-thread weave.
- **Joint Lubrication:** The air will extract moisture from your synovial fluid. Inject medical-grade silicone lubricant into knees, elbows, and knuckles before descent. If your joints seize, you remain where you fall—forever.

### IV. Biological Entropy Shielding

This is the invisible danger. You are a biological engine of entropy. Every breath, every heartbeat, every ATP synthesis increases disorder. The Cavern has "negotiated entropy into a draw." You are a foreign variable introducing decay into a system that has eliminated it.

**Metabolic Stasis Prevention:**
- **Entropy Sinks:** Carry small, sealed containers of rapidly oxidizing material (fine iron filings, fresh fruit, unprocessed meat). These are sacrificial offerings to the physics of the place. As they rust/rot, they absorb the "change" your presence creates, preventing the Cavern from neutralizing you to restore equilibrium.
- **Chemical Clocks:** Administer a cocktail of metabolic accelerators (thyroid stimulants, mitochondrial enhancers) to keep your cellular processes aggressive. If your metabolism slows to match the Cavern's stillness, you risk **Incorporation**—your body becoming part of the static fauna, frozen mid-step, becoming geometry.
- **Bacterial Suppression:** Your gut flora will go dormant or die. Load up on pre-digested nutrient pastes; your digestive system may cease peristalsis while inside.

### V. Navigation: The Climbing-Walking

The space is not a cavern but a densely packed geode. You will climb more than walk. The machinery—flywheels, pistons, valves—occupies every vector.

**Movement Doctrine:**
- **The Static Path:** Study the entry photographs for weeks. The machinery does not move in the human sense; it is frozen in function. A flywheel spins but never rotates. A piston is mid-stroke eternally. You must learn to see these as solid geometry, not moving parts. Attempting to "time" your passage through a valve cycle is meaningless; the valve opens and closes in the same instant.
- **Three-Point Contact:** Never rely on momentum. The sharp basalt and brass fittings have no give. Move like a spider: deliberate, weight-distributed, never trusting a single grip.
- **Zero-Impact Protocol:** Do not drop anything. Do not kick loose powder. Do not disturb the copper-thread nests. The fauna exist in perfect equilibrium; introducing a variable (a footprint, a dislodged gear-tooth) may collapse the waveform. The trilobite-like maintainers do not attack, but if you break their stasis, the Hum may correct you.

---

### VI. Fauna Interaction: The Geometry of Maintenance

The creatures are not animals; they are **operators**. The sessile crystal-forms transmute vibration into metabolic stasis. The mobile jointed-beings fit the gaps in the machinery perfectly.

**Absolute Rules:**
1. **Do Not Touch.** The fauna are at thermal and kinetic equilibrium with their substrates. Your 37°C body heat will create a gradient. The ceramic-jointed creatures may interpret your warmth as a new engine housing and attempt to "operate" you, fitting themselves into your joints.
2. **Avoid Shadows.** The shadows do not shift. If you stand in one too long, you may become part of its definition—your silhouette fixed to the rock even when you move.
3. **The Chitin:** Shed exoskeletons litter the space. They are perfect, preserved. Do not collect them. They are part of the equilibrium count.

---

### VII. Emergency Extraction

If you feel your thoughts slowing, your heartbeat synchronizing with the valve-cycles, or your skin developing a ceramic sheen:

1. **Inject Adrenaline:** Aggressive metabolic shock can break the stasis attraction.
2. **Create Noise:** Screaming adds an 18th frequency to the Hum. This is ontologically violent to the Cavern and may "eject" you as an irritant, collapsing the local standing wave long enough to escape.
3. **Do Not Look Back.** The Cavern preserves moments. If you turn to look at your path, you may see yourself still standing there, mid-step, having become part of the maintenance crew.

---

### VIII. Post-Visit Decompression

Upon exit, you will carry the Hum in your bones for days. You will find normal entropy grotesque—decay will seem obscene, change will appear as violence. Spend 48 hours in a buffered transition chamber with gradually increasing humidity and temperature variation.

Drink water slowly. Your cells, accustomed to the theoretical absence of water, may reject sudden hydration.

Check your dental records. Count your teeth. Verify that you are still in motion.

**Remember:** The Cavern is not abandoned. It is simply waiting. You are the only thing that will have changed within it since its creation. Move carefully, or you will become its final, frozen addition—a mammalian operator, suspended forever in the warm, dense dark, attending the machines that attend the Hum.
