Prepare to become a **ghost in a breathing circuit**, a transient variable in a calculation sixty million years deep. The Lithoboreal Expanse does not forgive the loud, the heavy, or the thirsty. It is not a wilderness to conquer but a computation to pass through without crashing the system.

### 1. Auditory Architecture: Surviving the Roar (Sound 2.5)
You are entering a world where **music is infrastructure** and silence is a glitch in spacetime. The wind plays the fossilized forest like a pipe organ of continental scale—bass frequencies that will resonate in your sternum, ultrasounds that will thread through your dental fillings and induce migraines.

**Essential Gear:**
- **Variable-Attenuation Bone-Conduction Helmets:** Standard earplugs will deafen you to the navigational cues you need. You require smart-dampening headsets that filter damaging decibels while preserving the harmonic map. The fauna navigate by bone-conduction; you must learn to "hear" through your skeleton, not your ears.
- **Resonance Disruptors:** Carry throwable "mutes"—aerosolized gels that dampen specific frequencies. If a pack of *stridulants* (those chitin-and-resonator hunting packs) targets you, you have seconds to alter your acoustic signature before they strike. Being silent is not enough; you must be **acoustically orthogonal** to the local soundscape.
- **Subvocal Comm Systems:** Never shout. The lithophytes process information through air-pressure differentials; your voice is literal noise in their datastream, potentially attracting correction protocols (dimensional shadow-wells) or predator-prey cascades.

### 2. The Breathing Pavement: Seismic Etiquette (Activity 1.9)
The ground is not ground. It is a mattress of compressed, desiccated mega-flora undergoing slow peristalsis—rising and falling in centimeter waves over hours as deep root-networks contract with thermal shifts.

**Mobility Protocols:**
- **Pneumatic Footwear:** Hard-soled boots will crack the fossilized leaf-litter, disrupting the silicon-chitin circuitry beneath. You need shoes with micro-pneumatic cushions that distribute weight like a sandpiper, matching the substrate's respiration. Think: walking on the surface of a sleeping giant's lung.
- **Seismic Anchors:** When making camp, do not drive stakes. The "breathing" will work them loose or, worse, snap them with geological force. Use magnetic grapples tuned to the local magnetic field lines (which the plants process as food), or deploy wide-base platforms that ride the swell like rafts.
- **Temporal Awareness:** Learn the rhythm. The substrate's respiration follows a roughly 4-hour cycle. Move during the "exhale" (when the surface is tense and stable); sleep during the "inhale" (when the ground is soft and receptive).

### 3. Thin Silver and Crystalline Thirst (Water 0.1, Atmosphere 0.6)
There is no water here, only **ice locked in crystalline lattices**—the memory of aqueous chemistry preserved in geometric prisons. The air is thin, copper-tanged, and ozonic; it will desiccate you faster than any desert while tasting of industrial accident.

**Life Support:**
- **Pressure Weaver-Suits:** The atmosphere is too thin for unassisted breathing, but full hard-suits will prevent you from hearing the necessary navigational drones. Wear membrane suits—pressurized skintight weaves with osmotic filtration—that allow sound transmission while maintaining 1 ATM internally.
- **Lattice-Breaking Hydration Packs:** You cannot drink the ice; it is structural, not liquid. Carry portable thermal lances that can sublimate microscopic amounts of crystalline water from the plant-matter without damaging the computational erosion patterns. Think of it as tapping a tree for sap, except the tree is stone and the sap is a ghost.
- **Copper/Ozone Scrubbers:** The taste of ancient chemistry is literal. Unfiltered, the air will oxidize your lung tissues. Your rebreather must include catalytic filters for the metal particulates.

### 4. walking on the CPU: Techno-Biological Protocols (Technology 2.8, Weirdness 2.5)
You are traversing a quantum computer made of wood and wind. The "cities" are circuit boards; the erosion patterns are equations; the fauna are mobile sensors in a biological internet.

**Critical Rules:**
- **Do Not Touch the Walls:** The polished, Brancusi-like curves of the plant-mountains are **crystalline processing matrices**. Oils from your skin, conductive sweat, or physical abrasion can corrupt a calculation that has been running since before mammals existed. View with eyes only; maintain a two-meter boundary.
- **Non-Conductive Everything:** The lithophytes utilize magnetic fields as metabolic input. Your steel watch, your titanium hip replacement, your electromagnetic communicator—these are pollutants, toxins, potential viruses in the system. Gear must be ceramic, polymer, or glass.
- **Follow the Erosion:** When lost, do not climb. The erosion patterns are algorithmic; they guide airflow for computation. Inlets are inputs; hollows are outputs. Move with the grain of the wind-polished channels, not against them. To go against the grain is to swim upstream in a river of data.

### 5. The Violet and the Void: Visual & Dimensional Awareness (Light 1.0, Shadows 1.0)
Perpetual twilight (grayscale bruised with violet) and anomalous shadows that pool too deeply, violating Euclidean geometry.

You need optics that enhance the twilight while preserving the color temperature—shadows that are too purple or too black indicate dimensional wells (post-modern spacetime engineering by the plants).

These pockets absorb sound and likely time; travelers report emerging hours later, or never. The fauna avoid them—**if the insects change course, you change course.**
- **Shadow Mapping:** Carry ultraviolet chalk or laser grids. In a world where shadows move with the substrate's breath and independent of light sources, you need to track which darkness is natural and which is a hungry void.

### 6. Citizens of the Circuit: Fauna Interaction (Fauna 1.8)
Abundant, chitinous, hollow-boned, and blind. They are the percussion section in the wind's orchestra, and they are watching you—via sound—for the lithophytes.

**Behavioral Modifications:**
- **Never Kill the Fauna:** The insectile symbiotes and six-winged avians are sensors. Killing one is like cutting a fiber-optic cable in a server room. The system will notice. The *stridulants* will come not for revenge, but for maintenance—removing the "error" (you).
- **Acoustic Camouflage:** Your heartbeat, at 60-100 bpm, is foreign percussion. Carry white-noise generators that mask your biological rhythm within the wind's drone. If you hear the sudden staccato of hunting *stridulants* approaching, freeze and emit a 40Hz tone—this frequency mimics the "all-clear" signal of the root-networks.
- **Feeding Protocol:** Do not eat the fauna. They are silicon-chitin composites; they would grind your teeth and poison your gut. Do not eat the plants; they are stone. Bring all sustenance.

### 7. Psychological Infrastructure (Sparse 0.8, Regional 1.8)
You will walk for kilometers through empty, polished boulevards between vegetative skyscrapers that were never built by hands. The scale is open to the planetary curve; the isolation is absolute; the "city" is empty yet watchful.

**Mental Conditioning:**
- **Embrace the Algorithm:** The emptiness is not abandonment; it is **occupied thought**. You are inside a mind that thinks in centuries. Do not expect human-scale interaction or shelter. The "plazas" are not for gathering; they are heat-sinks for quantum processing. You are a microbe crossing a neural pathway.
- **Temporal Dissociation Training:** Time moves differently here. The plants' "thoughts" are erosion; their "reactions" are geological. You may spend days waiting for a shadow to shift or a root to contract. Practice meditation techniques that compress subjective time, or you will go mad waiting for a door that opens on glacial scales.
- **The Beauty Paradox:** The place is breathtaking—cathedrals of polished wood, music of planetary scale, violet shadows. It is also hostile, desiccated, and computationally indifferent to you. Hold both thoughts simultaneously, or the cognitive dissonance will break you.

### 8. Emergency Protocols
- **If Lost:** Climb to the highest eroded trunk and listen. The wind-music has bass notes that indicate the plateau's edge (low frequency = thick air below) and treble that indicates the center. Follow the bass down.
- **If Dehydrated:** Do not eat the crystalline ice. It will shred your mouth and provide no metabolic water. Melt it first, even if it costs you battery life.
- **If Pursued:** Do not run. Running creates rhythmic footfalls—predictable, biological percussion that the *stridulants* can triangulate instantly. Instead, **harmonize**: vibrate at the frequency of the wind. Use your disruptors to become part of the song.
