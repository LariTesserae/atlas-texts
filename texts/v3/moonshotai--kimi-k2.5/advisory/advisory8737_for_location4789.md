**Preparatory Protocol for Excursion Site: "The Lithic Nave"**  
**Classification:** Closed Equilibrium / Pre-Anthropic / Zero-Flux Environment  

---

### I. Physiological Preparation: The Desiccation Protocol

You are entering a space where water exists in a state of categorical negation—not merely absent, but *conceptually evacuated*. The atmosphere has forgotten the chemistry of humidity. Your lungs, accustomed to air that carries moisture like a promise, will encounter instead a mineral suspension that will strip the mucous membranes from your sinus cavities within minutes of unprotected exposure.

**Respiratory System:**  
You must breathe through a **closed-circuit rebreather** with active ceramic humidification cartridges. Standard dry-atmosphere filters will fail; the static electricity alone in this space will cause synthetic materials to fuse. The rebreather must maintain 100% relative humidity in the inhalation loop while scrubbing CO₂ with lithium hydroxide canisters. Do not bring liquid water into the chamber—it constitutes a radical contamination. The space will absorb it instantly, and you will have introduced *change* into a system that has achieved finality.

**Integumentary Protection:**  
Your skin will crack like fired clay here. You require a **full-body osmotic pressure suit**—not for vacuum, but for containment. The suit must recycle your own transpiration, condensing sweat against your body to prevent any moisture loss to the environment. Select a material that is matte-finished; the tool-forms scattered across the floor have edges "sharp enough to cut" and will slice through glossy polymers that catch on conchoidal fractures.

**Thermal Regulation:**  
The heat here is geological and radiative, not convective. It does not move; it simply *is*. Your body cannot rely on evaporative cooling—the air is stagnant, stratified, thermally uniform. You will need a **micro-circulatory cooling suit** utilizing liquid cooling tubes, because your sweat will not evaporate. It will pool, then salt-crust, then abrade. The ambient temperature is estimated at 28-32°C, but in absolute stillness, this becomes oppressive.

---

### II. Sensory Calibration: Navigating the Occupied Void

**Visual Deprivation:**  
This is not darkness as absence but darkness as *presence*—a "tactile absence that presses against the skin like velvet soaked in sun." Do not bring standard illumination. Photon introduction disrupts the theoretical purity of the darkness and may trigger unforeseen chemical reactions in the chemolithotrophic colonies (the pale dustings on the northern faces of the stone tools). 

Instead, train for **echolocation** or utilize **tactile sonar gloves** that translate spatial data into pressure gradients against the palms. If you must use light, employ **single-photon emitters** at the threshold of detection, filtered to wavelengths outside the visible spectrum, with the understanding that you are contaminating a space that has not known light since before the tool-forms were born by entropy.

**Auditory Nullity:**  
The silence is "occupied"—filled with the density of stagnant air. Your heartbeat will become thunder. Your swallowing will sound like tectonic shift. Practice **anoxic silence meditation** beforehand to accustom your mind to the lack of auditory gradients. Bring noclick boots with felted soles. The stone surfaces have reached "absolute equilibrium" with time; your footfall could micro-fracture surfaces that have held their current argument with entropy for epochs.

**Olfactory and Gustatory Preparation:**  
The air tastes of "dust that has forgotten it was ever soil." You will taste metal, stone, and the faint ionic signature of deep-earth radioactivity. Do not bring food. Do not bring scent. You are entering a space of pre-creation, and your mammalian smells constitute a narrative of biology that this space predates.

---

### III. Psychological Conditioning: The Stasis Paradox

This location exists in "geological time extracted and preserved." You are a thing of flux, of heartbeat and synapse, entering a space where "the potential for action is preserved forever in the shape of the unheld axe."

**Temporal Disorientation:**  
Without gradients of light, temperature, or motion, the human circadian system collapses. Wear a **chronometric anchor**—a haptic wrist device that pulses your home rhythm against the bone. Without this, you may enter trance states or experience temporal dilation. The mind interprets stasis as eternity.

**The Lithic Uncanny:**  
You will encounter forms that possess "the unmistakable grammar of technology" yet lack intention. These are hand-axes, scrapers, and cores made by "entropy's slow sculpture." You must prepare for profound **derealization**—the cognitive dissonance of holding a tool that was never made, never used, never intended. This is the "primitive tech" of cosmic accident. Do not collect samples. Do not arrange or categorize. To disturb the scatter pattern is to become the first intentional agent in a space that has existed without agency.

**Isolation Protocol:**  
There are no animals. No bacteria blooms. No whisper of life except the chemolithotrophs feeding on sulfur and radioactivity at metabolisms too slow to perceive. You will be alone in a way that predates loneliness. Complete **sensory deprivation tank training** for a minimum of forty hours before entry. The warm darkness will cradle you like a mineral womb, and many travelers become convinced they have returned to the prenatal state. Carry a **mnestic trigger**—a word, a texture, a taste from your home reality—to prevent ego dissolution.

---

### IV. Equipment Specifications

- **Tethering System:** The chamber is "scarcely larger than a nave," but in absolute darkness, spatial perception fails. Use a **monofilament umbilical** wrapped in felt, anchored to the entry point. Do not rely on electronic positioning—the static environment may mask your own electromagnetic field.

- **Radiation Dosimetry:** The lichen-like colonies feed on "the faint radioactive whisper of the deep earth." Wear a **film badge** and carry **potassium iodide tablets**. The warmth may be, in part, radiogenic.

- **Cut-Resistant Gauntlets:** The tool edges are "sharp enough to cut but forever unused." They have weathered by "chemistry that has since exhausted itself," meaning the fracturing is complete and stable, but the obsidian and chert remain lethal. Kevlar will not suffice; you need **micromail** or **laminated graphene weave**.

- **Null-Weight Boots:** The floor is scattered with the stone forms at "generous intervals." You cannot step between them what you cannot see. Boots with **variable-geometry soles** that mold to irregular surfaces will prevent you from knocking over the accidental lithic scatter—an act equivalent to vandalizing the Mona Lisa with a sledgehammer in terms of multiverse heritage value.

---

### V. Behavioral Imperatives: The Ethics of Observation

You are entering a "workshop before the worker, a hearth before the fire." This is negative space—defined by what has not happened.

1. **Do Not Breathe on the Tools:** Your breath contains moisture and CO₂. The chemolithotrophs have reached equilibrium with the bare stone. Your exhalation could trigger a bloom, introducing biological time into geological stasis.

2. **Do Not Touch the Northern Faces:** The "pale gray dustings" of mineral-metabolizing life cling only to the northern aspects. This orientation is likely tied to magnetic fields or thermal gradients invisible to you. Do not disturb this faint biomass measured in grams.

3. **Accept the Stillness:** The "physics of motion has been canceled by the perfection of the stasis." If you must move, move as though swimming through honey—slowly, with intention, creating no convection currents. The air is "stratified and stagnant"; your passage through it is a violence.

4. **Duration Limits:** Do not remain longer than ninety minutes. The place is "complete unto itself," and you are an intrusion that creates paradox simply by existing within it. The longer you stay, the greater the risk of the chamber rejecting your flux—through suit malfunctions, psychological collapse, or the terrible possibility that you might become part of the equilibrium, your moistureless body adding to the lithic scatter, your form achieving the negative space of the traveler who never left.

---

### VI. Post-Excursion Decompression

Upon return, you will carry the warmth in your bones. The darkness will cling to your retinas for hours. Do not immediately enter water or bright light. Sit in a dim, humid room and allow your body to remember that change is possible, that time flows, that you are not trapped in the eternal moment before the first hand grasped the first tool.

Drink slowly. The water will taste impossibly vibrant, insultingly wet. You have been where water was only theory. Reacclimation is a form of rebirth.

**Final Warning:**  
This is not a hostile environment. It does not threaten. It simply *is*, with a completeness that makes the human condition seem frantic and crude. Some travelers return unable to tolerate motion, change, or the noise of living things. They seek the silence again.

The warm stone will not cool.  
The sharp edges will not dull.  
And neither, perhaps, will you.
