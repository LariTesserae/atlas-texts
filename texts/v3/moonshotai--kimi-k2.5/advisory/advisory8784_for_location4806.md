The Sinter is not hostile, but it is **assertive**—a newborn industrial ontology still arguing with itself about causality, dimensionality, and the proper sequence of entropy. To walk here is to walk through the sketch-lines of a blueprint that has begun manufacturing its own paper. Prepare accordingly.

The Sinter’s reality breakdown (2.8) is not a surface phenomenon but substrate. The "2.4 things per cubic meter" statistic is not poetic; it is a warning that space here is *folded employment*—multiple locations occupying identical coordinates, separated only by probability gradients.

- **Chronometric Compass:** Not for telling time, but for detecting its direction. In districts where cause precedes effect, standard navigation fails because you have already walked the path you are about to choose. The compass detects temporal gradient; if the needle spins counter-clockwise, trust your *second* instinct, not your first.

### **II. Environmental Harshness: The Cold Bleach**

**Thermal & Dermal Protection:**

Wear layered, moisture-wicking fabrics beneath reflective outer shells. - **Cut-Resistant Full-Body Weave:** The erosion rating of 1.1 means edges are *violently fresh*. Aluminum valleys and basalt-composite canyons bear "drag-marks of creation"—microscopic serrations that can slice through standard fabrics. The metal is young, sharp, and chemically active. Bring sealant for gear abrasions; the oxidation here occurs *before* the metal is cast, and your equipment may begin rusting retroactively.
- **Desiccant Packs:** Everything sweats. The dampness is metabolic—water exhaled by the structure itself. Without aggressive moisture management, your clothing will remain permanently saturated, leading to immersion hypothermia in a place where it never rains, only *perspires*.

**Provisioning:**
