### I. Biological Pre-conditioning (Minimum 3 weeks pre-departure)

**Cardiovascular Remodeling**  
The Compression generates a subsonic drone at 12-18 Hz that resonates with human thoracic cavities. Without preparation, visitors experience arrhythmia, panic attacks, and micro-fractures of the sternum. Obtain a *pressure-wave pacemaker*—a subdermal implant that counter-phases your heartbeat against the Compression’s rhythm. Train with infrasound exposure starting at 80dB, working up to the Scarp’s ambient 140dB "slow noise." You must learn to perceive the seventeen-year cycle as a *tempo*, not a threat.

**Respiratory Modifications**  
The atmosphere is supersaturated with mineral particulates (silicate aerosols, pre-crystalline aluminum oxides, and reality-trace elements). Standard filters clog within hours. Invest in a **condensation rebreather** with osmotic membranes that actually *use* the dampness—these extract oxygen from mineral-rich water droplets while filtering out the "conceptual dust" that causes petrification of lung tissue in 8% of visitors. Carry spare condensate collection vials; the air here is drinkable but intoxicating in large doses.

**Temporal Calibration**  
Your cellular metabolism will desynchronize near the Compression. Administer *chronostasis injections* (thyroid stabilizers paired with telomere locks) to prevent biological aging discrepancies. If possible, arrange for a **temporal anchor-point**—a synchronized chronometer left at your entry point that broadcasts a baseline "true time" signal. Without this, you may return to find your departure was seventeen years ago, or seventeen minutes, with no correlation to subjective experience.

### II. Sensory Apparatus

**Auditory Protection (Counter-Intuitive)**  
Do *not* use standard noise-canceling equipment. The sound here is tactile; blocking it causes vertigo and psychosis as your inner ear tries to map a silence that should be physically impossible. Instead, wear **subsonic translators**—bone-conduction headsets that shift the infrasound into haptic feedback (vibrations) you can read as "texture." You need to *feel* the seventeen-year cycle in your jawbone to navigate safely.

**Visual Gear**  
The photon-arcing and violet twilight will damage unprotected retinas within six hours. Use **reality-stable goggles** with polarization that forces light to behave linearly within a 2-meter bubble around your face. This prevents the "conflicting shadows" phenomenon from causing your visual cortex to hemorrhage. However, keep one eye uncovered for brief intervals—navigating the gantries requires seeing the "true" bent light to judge where gravity actually points.

**Gravity Boots**  
Variable gravity (0.3G to 2.1G depending on proximity to the Deep Bore) requires **inertial dampening footwear** with micro-gyros. The locals move slowly because they’ve learned the gravity shifts; you’ll need technology to compensate. Pack magnetic grapples for the gantries—some walkways have "up" facing in three different directions simultaneously.

**The Layering System**

**The Coat**  
Locals wear heavy, ankle-length greatcoats not for warmth but for *inertia*. The damp mineral air carries electrical potential; your coat should trail behind you, grounding the slow blue arcs that crawl across surfaces. A dragging hem is safety, not fashion.

### IV. Navigation & Logistics

**Maps Are Lies**  
Cartography fails here because the terrain changes when the Compression cycles. Instead, carry a **geosonic resonator**—a device that reads the "settling" sounds of the newborn rock to determine which faces are stable for the next 4-6 hours. Learn to read the tube-worms: when their pulsing synchronizes perfectly with your heartbeat (via your pacemaker), you are standing on rock that will remain solid for the duration of your visit.

**The Gantry Code**  
There are no streets, only bridges between geological points. Travel is three-dimensional and topological. Always maintain three points of contact; the gravity vectors shift without warning. The locals use *color-coded handkerchiefs* tied to railings to mark safe paths—red for "temporal flux zone," yellow for "high geological activity," green for "stable for now." Learn the local knot dialect; a single twist changes "safe" to "condemned."

**Illumination**  
Carry sodium-vapor lamps (matching the local industrial spectrum). White light attracts the glass-fiber moths, which are harmless but disorienting; their slow wingbeats induce trance states. If you must enter the "active darkness" between structures, use **infrared only**—the darkness here is a presence that notices visible light and moves toward it.

### V. Biological Interactions

**The Fungi**  
Do not eat anything. Do not touch the bioluminescent fruiting bodies. The mycelial network has legal standing here and processes the dead into raw material. If injured, clean your blood immediately—the mycelium detects organic chemistry and will send exploratory hyphae within minutes. Carry *industrial fungicide* (copper-chloride solution) not to kill (don’t anger the locals), but to negotiate—a spray marks you as "claimed property" of the factories, which the fungi respect.

**Fauna**  
- **Glass-fiber moths:** If one lands on you, remain still for up to three days (subjective). They harvest condensation, and movement causes their wings to lacerate your skin with micro-filaments.  
- **Multi-eyed birds:** Their subsonic calls induce nausea. If you feel pressure in your inner ear dropping, hum at 440Hz—this creates a standing wave that confuses their targeting.  
- **Tube-worms:** Do not pull them from the rock. They are load-bearing. Seriously.

### VI. Psychological Preparation

**Time Perception Adjustment**  
You will experience hours between the Pit-Foundry piston strokes. Food rots slowly; wounds heal strangely; emotions crystallize rather than flowing. Practice *compressed meditation*—the ability to remain mentally active during long periods of physical stasis. Bring a mind-occupying ritual (knitting, prayer beads, mathematical proofs) scaled to geological patience.

**Ontological Uncertainty**  
The rock is dreaming itself into existence. You may see geological features *deciding* to be sedimentary or igneous. Do not impose your observation too strongly—quantum effects are macro here. If you stare at a cliff face too long, you may influence its mineral composition, endangering the structures bolted to it. Practice "soft seeing"—peripheral awareness only.

**Social Protocols**  
The 40,000 inhabitants are not "settlers" but *process-workers*. They do not live here; they *persist* here. Conversation happens in bursts between machinery cycles. When speaking to a local, stand to their left (compression-ward side)—this signals you acknowledge the seventeen-year cycle. Ask not "Where are you from?" but "Where are you in the cycle?" They will answer with a number between 1-34 (years). Anything below 8 or above 29 means they are temporally displaced; do not make contracts with them.

### VII. Emergency Protocols

**If Gravity Inverts**  
Grab a tube-worm. They are anchored to the world’s mantle and will not shift. Wait for the Compression to cycle (could be hours; could be seconds).

**If Lost in Active Darkness**  
Turn off all lights. The darkness searches for illumination. Move by feeling the condensation drip direction—"down" is still mostly consistent toward the valley center. Listen for the tube-worms; their pulse indicates the Compression center.

**If You Begin to Fossilize**  
Symptoms: skin hardening, thoughts becoming crystalline and slow, craving to stand perfectly still. Inject **raw pleroma** (stabilized reality). This is dangerous—too much will fix you permanently in local time—but a 5cc dose will reset your quantum state.

**Extraction**  
Do not attempt to leave during a Compression peak (when the subsonic pressure is highest). Wait for the "sigh"—the moment between cycles when the great engines pause. The rock reorganizes then, and temporary gates to stable reality form in the condensation clouds. Your extraction team must coordinate with the 17-year cycle; if they miss the window, they wait seventeen years, or you wait seventeen minutes (depending on your temporal frame).
