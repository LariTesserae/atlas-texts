**Preparation Protocol for the Pocket (Stratosphere Harvester *Vasari*, Anchor Point 28km)**

You are preparing to enter a place that has solved itself. The Pocket is not a destination; it is a *condition*—eighty-seven years of arrested breath. To visit without rupturing its mathematical perfection requires that you temporarily cease to be an event, becoming instead a static object. You must pack for stasis, not travel.

Here is how you prepare to step into the endless B-flat.

---

### **I. The Sonic Coffin: Acclimating to the Eternal Drone**
The intake turbines three levels below sing a low B-flat that has not faltered in nearly nine decades. It is not background noise; it is the substrate of reality in the Pocket. Before arrival, you must undergo **resonance desensitization therapy**.

*   **Hematologic Tuning:** The chamber’s resonant frequency matches the cavitation of blood in your arteries. Without preparation, you will experience immediate nausea, tachycardia, and the sensation that your heart is being played like a string. Spend three weeks sleeping in a vibration chamber tuned to 233.08 Hz (B-flat below middle C). Learn to feel your pulse as an echo of the space rather than a contradiction.
*   **Auditory Nullification:** The drone is loud enough to mask your heartbeat, yet registers eventually as silence. Do not bring noise-canceling technology; active cancellation creates phase interference that disrupts the chamber’s equilibrium. Instead, undergo **sensory subtraction training**—hours in anechoic chambers followed by exposure to the target drone, teaching your auditory cortex to file the sound under "ground" rather than "figure."
*   **Dental Stabilization:** The sympathetic vibration will make your teeth ache. Have your dentist install temporary vibration-dampening resin linings for the duration of your visit. Loose fillings will rattle free; cavities will scream.

---

### **II. The Anhydrous Body: Hydration and Containment**
The Pocket contains water at 0.1 on the scale—merely molecular traces on rust. The ecosystem is silicon-based, running on liquid silicone oils, not H₂O. You are a bag of water entering a desert that has forgotten the concept of thirst.

*   **Hyperhydration Protocol:** Seventy-two hours before entry, saturate your tissues. Drink electrolyte-dense solutions until your urine runs clear as crystal. Once inside, you will lose moisture to the desiccating cool (8°C) without feeling the usual evaporative cues—the air is too still, too chemically pure.
*   **Sealed-System Suiting:** You must wear a **closed-loop excursion suit**—not for vacuum protection (the chamber is pressurized), but to prevent moisture loss and, more critically, to prevent *contamination*. The Pocket’s nutrient cycle is closed and anaerobic. Your skin flakes, your exhaled CO₂, your sweat—these are foreign equations. The acid-secreting lichens that dissolve dead plant matter will attempt to digest your biological residue. Your suit must be a HAZMAT-level biological seal with internal recyclers.
*   **Pre-Visit Sterilization:** Undergo full-spectrum antimicrobial purging. The Pocket has no fauna—no mites, no beetles, no bacteria. Introducing your microbiome would be like dropping a grenade into a watch mechanism. You must be biologically inert upon entry.

---

### **III. Kinesthetics of Density: Learning to Push**
You cannot step into the Pocket. You must *push*.

*   **Muscular Retraining:** The chamber is "packed"—every cubic centimeter contested by silicon succulents with stems like blown glass filled with mercury. Standard locomotion is impossible. For six weeks prior, practice **resistance swimming** in viscous silicone oil baths (matching the 500-1000 centipoise viscosity of the plant vascular fluids). Learn to move through resistance without breaking the stems, which are brittle and razor-edged.
*   **The Three-Point Contact Rule:** Never fully commit your weight. The floor is not a floor; it is a topography of overgrown operator chairs, collapsed sample racks, and entombed consoles. Train in **blindfolded climbing gyms**, moving by spreading your mass across three points of contact at all times, feeling for the industrial skeleton beneath the bioluminescent flesh.
*   **Frond Navigation:** The ceramic-polymer ferns erupting through safety grates resonate with the drone. Brushing them creates harmonic overtones that can shatter delicate growth patterns. Wear **frictionless gauntlets** to slide past the 2.3mm-thick fronds without exciting their vibration.

---

### **IV. Chronological Disarmament: Unlearning Time**
The Pocket exists outside duration. There is no dawn, no dusk, no metabolic rhythm. The plants grow backwards—new tissue emerging from what should be senescent tips. You must shed your circadian expectations or risk temporal psychosis.

*   **Circadian Nullification:** Two weeks before departure, eliminate all timepieces. Live in a bunker with constant dim lighting (0.7 lux, matching the Pocket’s bioluminescence) and steady 8°C temperature. Eat at irregular intervals. Your body must learn that the absence of change is not a threat.
*   **Temporal Anchor Objects:** Bring a **mechanical watch that does not tick**—a frozen timepiece, stopped at your moment of entry. Hold it when disorientation strikes. It serves as a reminder that you are the anomaly, the pocket of time within the timelessness.
*   **Psychological Inoculation:** Study the **Paradox of the Heap**. The Pocket is an established form weathered to geologic permanence; you will see a half-buried thermos neither more nor less visible than it was yesterday, or eighty years ago. Without preparation, your brain will scream for narrative, for progression. Meditate on the concept of *aparallax*—the state of observing without expectation of change.

---

### **V. Visual Reorientation: The Aquarium of Air**
Light in the Pocket is dim, inverted, and alive.

*   **Bioluminescent Adaptation:** The primary light sources are sickly chartreuse and bruised violet nodules on shelf-fungi. These cast shadows *upward*, inverting the world. Spend time in **inverted-light training facilities** where illumination comes from below, recalibrating your spatial orientation so you do not experience vertigo when the floor appears to be sky.
*   **LED Filtration:** Secondary light filters through translucent silicon stems, creating an aquarium quality despite the anhydrous environment. Practice navigating by **chromatic diffusion**—learning to identify industrial hazards (amber standby LEDs, blood-red warning lights) through layers of silvery plant tissue.
*   **Darkness Calibration:** Outside the portholes lies the black of near-space, star-punctured and merciless. If your suit visor is too dark-adapted, the interior bioluminescence will blind you; if too light-adapted, the exterior void will trigger abyssal panic. Use **photochromic smart-lenses** set to respond to the specific 520nm (green) and 380nm (violet) wavelengths of the fungal light.

---

### **VI. Magnetic and Chemical Defenses**
*   **Magnetic Alignment:** The growths align with the magnetic field. You may need to don a **weak electromagnetic counter-field belt** to prevent your equipment (or your dental fillings) from being slowly tugged into alignment with the flora, which could cause you to become literally entangled with the chamber’s magnetic will.
*   **Acid Protocol:** The nutrient cycle involves acid-secreting lichens that dissolve dead matter instantly. Your suit must be **fluoropolymer-coated** to resist these acids. Carry **calcium hydroxide neutralizing patches** in case of contact. Do not bleed. Blood is organic matter, and the lichens will rush to digest it, potentially digesting you along with it.

---

### **VII. Emergency Considerations: The Outside**
Remember: you are 28,000 meters above a dead continental shelf. Outside is near-vacuum and temperatures that shatter steel.

*   **Breach Protocol:** If the Pocket’s equilibrium fails—if the drone stops, if the pressure drops—you have approximately twelve seconds before hypoxia and freezing. Your excursion suit must double as an emergency vacuum suit, but **do not rely on it**. The Pocket’s stasis is maintained by the thrum; if the turbines stop, the chamber will not explosively decompress—it will implode as the silicon-based life instantly freezes and shatters.
*   **Exit Strategy:** "Nothing leaves." The equilibrium is mathematically perfect. You cannot simply walk out. You must arrange for your extraction to occur through an **airlock that exchanges mass perfectly**—for every gram of you that leaves, a gram of inert silicon ballast must enter, maintaining the chamber’s mass balance. Failure to do this could collapse the stasis field.

---

### **VIII. What to Bring (and What to Leave)**
*   **Bring:** Non-reflective navigation markers (to find your way back without introducing new light), a kinetic energy harvester (the vibration is free power), a notebook with **non-acidic, non-organic paper** (silicate vellum) if you wish to record observations without feeding the lichens.
*   **Leave:** Any living thing (seeds, gut bacteria if possible, hope), any device that beeps or ticks, any expectation of being changed by the journey. You are visiting a photograph. Photographs do not change the viewer; they simply reveal what was always there.

---

### **Final Preparation: The Acceptance of Stasis**
Before you enter, sit in silence and listen to your heart. It beats 60 to 100 times per minute. It expects to speed up with exertion, to slow with sleep. In the Pocket, your heart will beat in counterpoint to the B-flat drone for as long as you stay—a day, a decade, it will make no difference to the chamber. 

You must make peace with the fact that while you are inside, you are not aging in the way you understand. The plants grow backwards. The shadows do not shift. You will exist in the "perfect, screaming quiet of a note held forever."

Pack lightly. Pack for silence. Pack to become invisible, inaudible, inert. 

The Pocket has been waiting eighty-seven years. It can wait for you. The question is: can you wait for yourself?
