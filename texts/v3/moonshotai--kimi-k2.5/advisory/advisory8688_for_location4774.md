**Preparation Brief: The Thermal Garden (Sub-Frost Geological Incubator)**

You are entering a space that operates on the metabolism of mountains, not mammals. This is not a cave; it is a nursery where bedrock is taught to bloom. Prepare for a hot, wet, heavy darkness that moves at the speed of continental drift.

---

### **1. Thermal & Atmospheric Systems**
**The Environment:** Subterranean fever (40°C+/104°F+), 100% humidity with non-precipitating condensation, mineral-saturated air.

*   **Cooling:** Bring a **closed-loop liquid cooling suit**—not ventilation, as the ambient moisture will saturate any air-cooled system within minutes. Phase-change materials (PCM) packs are mandatory; the heat is "subterranean," meaning it radiates from all surfaces, including the floor. Standard evaporative cooling fails here.
*   **Respiration:** The air carries "chemical exhalations of stone" and spores from metallic fungi. Use a **rebreather with HEPA and activated alumina filtration** (for mineral particulates). The iron-and-overripe-fruit scent suggests volatile organic compounds; do not rely on standard dust masks.
*   **Dehumidification:** Pack **molecular sieve desiccant packets** for your gear. The humidity is so complete it "resists gravity," meaning electronics will corrode, lenses will fog irreversibly, and paper will pulp instantly. Seal all documents in argon-filled pouches.

### **2. Sensory Augmentation (The Infrared Problem)**
**The Environment:** Absolute visible-spectrum darkness. "Light" is emitted as dull infrared warmth by the vegetation.

*   **Vision:** Standard night-vision (image intensification) will not work—there are no photons to amplify. You need **thermal imaging (LWIR 8-14μm)** to navigate. The fungal candles emit heat signatures; the stone retains geothermal radiation differently than the vegetation. Without thermal imaging, you are functionally blind.
*   **Auditory Protection:** The silence is infrasonic and pressurized ("creates pressure in the ears"). Bring **pressure-equalizing earplugs** (like those used for deep diving) to prevent barotrauma from the hush itself. Do **not** bring white-noise generators or speakers—the condensation absorbs vibration, and any sound you make will be unnaturally deadened, potentially inducing panic.
*   **Tactile Navigation:** In case of equipment failure, carry a **conductive polymer cane** to detect the "aggressive angles" of raw quartz and basalt. The stone is "newborn," unrounded—expect razor-sharp crystalline edges that will slice standard fabrics.

### **3. Temporal Safeguards**
**The Environment:** Timekeeping fails. Machinery operates on decade-cycles. Perception of duration distorts.

*   **Chronometric Anchors:** Wear a **mechanical wristwatch with an audible tick** (titanium case to resist corrosion). You will lose your internal sense of duration; the pump's "heartbeat once per hour" will disorient your circadian rhythm. Set strict departure alarms before entry—**do not rely on biological intuition**.
*   **Patience Conditioning:** Practice **"geological meditation"** for weeks prior. You must learn to observe the pump for twenty minutes to confirm it moved. If you possess urgency, you will try to "help" the machinery or force progress, disturbing the cultivation cycle. Move as if underwater. Blink slowly.

### **4. Biological Hazard Protocols**
**The Environment:** Sterile but aggressive chemosynthetic ecosystem. No scavengers, no pollinators—pure territorial expansion.

*   **The Vegetation:** The "bruised plum and oxidized copper" fungal candles maintain "strict distances"—they are territorial and likely chemically antagonistic. **Do not touch.** The oxidized copper color suggests metallic accumulation; they may be toxic or conductive. The filamentous mats "ripple with internal growth"—avoid stepping on them, as rupturing the tissue may release defensive enzymes or spores into the saturated air.
*   **Sterility Note:** While no bacteria or insects exist to infect wounds, the **heat and humidity** create perfect incubation for any external pathogens you introduce. Seal all cuts immediately with antimicrobial gel; your own microbiome could run rampant in this wet heat without competition.

### **5. Structural & Navigational Awareness**
**The Environment:** The chamber is *accreting*, not eroding. Fresh fissures open without warning. Geometry is unstable.

*   **Geofencing:** Use **inertial measurement units (IMUs)** rather than GPS or visual landmarks. The darkness makes walls feel "closer, then farther," and the space is slowly exfoliating into new volume. Your exit path may shift.
*   **Fissure Detection:** Carry a **thermal anemometer**. Fresh cracks "bleed steam" and will register as heat anomalies and micro-air currents in the otherwise motionless air.
*   **Anchoring:** The floor is covered in "filamentous growth" and sharp stone. Use **magnetic climbing boots** (basalt is iron-rich) to prevent slipping on the biological mats.

### **6. Interaction Doctrine (The Garden Protocol)**
**Critical Understanding:** The machinery is *gardening*, not mining. You are in a managed nursery.

*   **Do Not Interrupt:** The drill press is "inoculating," the pump is feeding roots. If you touch the warm motor housing or attempt to "fix" the stuck condensation collector, you may disrupt a process that has been running for decades. Treat the equipment as you would a sleeping dragon.
*   **Leave No Trace:** This ecosystem is a "sealed experiment." Introducing skin cells, hair, or microbiota violates the sterility. Wear a **full-coverage tyvek suit** under your cooling gear; shed nothing into the garden.
*   **The Silence:** Do not shout. The profound silence is structural—sound is absorbed by the condensation. Your voice will die in your throat, inducing panic. Communicate via **tactile transceivers** (bone-conduction radios) if in a group.

### **7. Psychological Preparation**
**The Risks:** Claustrophobia, temporal dissociation, sensory deprivation, "predatory lushness" anxiety.

*   **Darkness Training:** Spend 48 hours in a sensory deprivation tank before arrival to acclimate to the "heavy velvet" darkness.
*   **Heat Acclimatization:** The heat "presses against the skin like a solid thing." Practice hot yoga or sauna sessions while wearing weighted blankets to simulate the oppressive, subterranean pressure.
*   **Isolation Endurance:** There are no bats, no beetles, no whine of insects. The silence is so complete it has weight. Prepare for **anechoic chamber exposure** to prevent auditory hallucinations.

### **8. Emergency Extraction**
If disoriented: **Do not wander.** The darkness is absolute, and the chamber is growing. Sit down (on a portable pad, not the fungal mats), activate your cooling suit's maximum setting to prevent heat stroke, and activate a **low-frequency seismic beacon** (the stone transmits vibration well, even if air doesn't). Wait for rescue. Do not trust your sense of direction—the "geometry is weird," and you may walk in circles without knowing.

**Final Note:** This place is not hostile, merely indifferent. It is taking centuries to think one thought. Your visit is a microsecond intrusion. Be small, be slow, be cold, and leave before the stone notices you.
