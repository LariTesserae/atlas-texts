**Preparation Protocol for the Silicate Womb**

You are not going for a walk. You are going to be *inserted* into a metabolic process that has no vacancy for mammalian curiosity. The Womb does not accommodate visitors; it accommodates mass and chemistry. Your preparation must transform you from a soft, water-based explorer into a sealed, cooled, observant node that can survive being buried alive in living machinery.

---

### **I. Physiological Hardening (Months 3-6)**

**Heat Adaptation:**  
You must undergo progressive hyperthermia conditioning until you can tolerate 60°C external temperatures without cognitive impairment. This is not for comfort—it is for survival margin. When your suit’s primary cooling fails (and it will), you have perhaps ninety seconds before your proteins denature in the 340°C ambient heat. Train your cardiovascular system to shunt blood to your core, and practice meditation techniques to lower your metabolic rate. You want your heartbeat slow and your breathing shallow; the local fauna beat at 120 BPM, and you do not want your pulse to harmonize with theirs, creating sympathetic resonance in the chamber.

**Pressure Acclimation:**  
Despite the altitude, you will experience 300+ atmospheres of pressure from the supercritical sulfur dioxide and metal halide soup. Spend time in deep-sea hyperbaric chambers, but understand the difference: water is incompressible and benign. This atmosphere is *corrosive* and dense. Practice moving in high-viscosity fluids. Your muscles must learn to operate against resistance that feels like wet cement.

**Claustrophobic Immersion:**  
The density rating of 2.9 means *zero void space*. You will not stand; you will be held. You will not turn around; your orientation is fixed at insertion. Prepare by spending increasing intervals in sensory deprivation tanks packed with inert silicone beads—simulating the hexagonal tessellation of Calciphora bodies pressing against your suit from all sides. Learn to find the boundary between "I am trapped" and "I am supported." In the Womb, panic is fatal; the organisms may interpret your struggling as available kinetic energy.

---

### **II. The Exosuit: Specifications**

Your suit is not armor. It is a **mobile quarantine unit** and a **refrigeration plant** disguised as humanoid form.

*   **Thermal Regulation:** Closed-loop Stirling engines powered by the temperature differential between the 340°C exterior and your 37°C interior. Radiators must be coated with silicon carbide to resist fluoridic corrosion. Carry endothermic chemical packs as emergency heat sinks—these will buy you twenty minutes.
*   **Pressure Vessel:** Titanium-halide ceramic composite, seamless, with no external seams for the sharp cleavage planes to catch upon. The walls are newborn (erosion 0.3) and atomically sharp; they will slice conventional alloys.
*   **Atmospheric Seal:** Triple-redundant gaskets using perfluorinated elastomers. The atmosphere is anhydrous—*any* water vapor escaping your suit constitutes a biological weapon against the local ecology. You are a walking extinction event to the sulfur-based chemistry here. Conversely, any breach admits SO₂ and HF gas that will dissolve your lungs in seconds.
*   **Locomotion:** You will not walk. The floor is buried under strata of organisms. Your suit must be capable of **peristaltic anchoring**—micro-hooks that grip the silicon carbide skins of the Calciphora without piercing them (piercing invites taproot investigation). Movement, if any, is lateral shifting at millimeters per hour.
*   **Sensory Array:** 
    *   **Vision:** No visible light exists (light 0.5). You will navigate by infrared, watching the cherry-red thermal glow of the organisms. Your viewport must be germanium or zinc selenide—transparent to IR, resistant to sulfuric acid.
    *   **Hearing:** Active noise cancellation is mandatory. The sound level (2.4) is industrial forge-loud—120 unsynchronized piston hearts, grinding mandibles, venting sulfur. Without protection, you will suffer permanent damage in minutes. However, maintain bone-conduction pickups to monitor the subsonic geological groan (dynamic 0.8); this is your only warning of chamber contraction or tectonic adjustment.

### **III. Biological Interface Protocols**

The Calciphora are tech level 2.0—industrial efficiency—but they are fauna (1.8), not machines. They are hungry, growing, and networked.

*   **Anti-Fusion Coating:** Your suit exterior must be treated with a non-stick germanium-doped polymer. The organisms fuse with each other via taproot penetration; you must be slippery to their exploratory tendrils. If a taproot breaches your suit, it will attempt to interface with your waste heat and chemical signatures. Do not let them sample you.
*   **Chemical Camouflage:** Release trace sulfur dioxide and trace metal halides through controlled vents. Smell like the background. If you smell like water, nitrogen, or fear-sweat, the acidophiles will investigate you as a novel mineral source.
*   **Quarantine:** You carry Earth microbes that could devastate this 0.3 erosion ecosystem. The Calciphora have no immune system for water-based bacteria; you are a plague vector. Your suit must have UV sterilization locks for any sample collection.

### **IV. Psychological Fortification**

This place is normal (weirdness 0.4)—it obeys physics, merely extreme physics. But it is also a horror of intimacy.

*   **The Density Meditation:** You will be touched on all sides by living things. Their piston-hearts will thud against your chest. Their sulfur-blood will pulse in quartz tubes millimeters from your face. You cannot flinch. Practice "embodied compression" therapy—learning to experience total surround-contact as comfort rather than threat.
*   **Temporal Dissonance Training:** The environment operates on geological patience (dynamic 0.8)—decades for obsidian to cool, years to grow a millimeter. Yet the sensory input is immediate, loud, and industrial. You will experience the anxiety of "urgent stasis." Learn to read a book in your head while the forge screams around you.
*   **Darkness Adaptation:** Absolute velvet dark broken only by the dull red glow of thermal radiation. You will see your own heat reflected back at you from the silicon carbide walls. Prepare for profound isolation; radio signals may not penetrate six kilometers of granite and supercritical fluid.

---

### **V. Entry and Positioning**

You arrive by drilling or falling through a fault that should not have opened. This is traumatic.

*   **Impact Absorption:** Your suit must survive a 50-meter drop onto fresh cleavage planes (sharp as razors) and immediately seal against the pressurized atmosphere.
*   **Insertion Point:** Choose your coordinates carefully. You want a spot where the hexagonal packing has a defect—a dead Calciphora or a cooling fracture. You will occupy this void. Once in place, deploy stabilizers. You are not visiting; you are *installing* yourself.
*   **The First Hour:** Do not move. Let the organisms adjust to your thermal signature. They will press against you, testing. If you remain chemically inert and thermally stable, they will classify you as "rock" and resume their industrial metabolism around you.

---

### **VI. Survival Protocols**

*   **Hydration:** You are in an anhydrous environment (water 0.4). Your suit must recycle every drop of moisture. Any external water exposure is catastrophic—for you (instant steam explosion at 340°C) and for the local ecology (contamination).
*   **Waste Management:** Your excretions are toxic here. Store all waste; the Calciphora will attempt to metabolize anything you discard. You do not want them to develop a taste for urea.
*   **Emergency Extraction:** If the chamber contracts (dynamic 0.8) or if you begin to fuse with the local biology, you must trigger the "birth protocol"—small shaped charges to blast a vertical shaft upward, followed by rapid ascent via winch. Note: This will scar the newborn geology. Only use if your suit integrity drops below 40%.

---

### **VII. The Return**

Decontamination takes three weeks. The sulfur compounds will have impregnated your suit’s molecular structure; the fluorides will have etched your viewport. More importantly, the *silence* of the outside world will be deafening after the industrial percussion of the Womb. You will miss the thudding. You will dream of the dark, and of being held so tightly you cannot breathe, surrounded by things that grow like factories in the heat.

You are going to visit a nursery where geology and biology are indistinguishable, where the rocks are infants and the life is machinery. Prepare to be insignificant, compressed, and very, very still.
