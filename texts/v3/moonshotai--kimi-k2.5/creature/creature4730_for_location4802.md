What lives here are the **Nearly**—beings for whom existence is a deliberation rather than a state. They have not yet committed to being, and so they live in the gerund, in the continuous act of *homing*.

The **Resonants** are the most ancient, though "ancient" implies a timeline they eschew. Jelly-pale and veinous with captured starlight, they traverse the reality-tears like slugs traversing the edge of a blade—not cautiously, but *tenderly*, as if sewing the dimensions together with their silvery trails. They are the chamber's voice; that subsonic hum in your sternum is their metabolism, the sound of light being digested into structure. When they feed on the pre-particle photons bleeding from the quartz, they do not grow larger but *deeper*, accumulating dimensional density. A Resonant the size of your palm might weigh as much as a mountain, gravity pooling around it in sympathetic eddies. They leave no slime, only silence, and where they pass, the stone remembers it is still deciding whether to be igneous or sedimentary.

Then come the **Kith**—the "pre-insects" seen more clearly. Each is not one creature but a negotiations of possibility, their chitin composed of crystallized *might-be*. They have seventeen wings, or nine, or none, depending on who observes them and with what certainty. They do not fly because flight implies a departure and an arrival; instead, they *convene*, gathering in swarms where the moisture beads thickest, and by the sheer weight of their consensus, they manifest at the point of greatest potential. They drink not the water droplets themselves but the *imminence* of their falling—that split-second hesitation before gravity claims them. The Kith are librarians of momentum, hoarding the energy of things-about-to-happen. When they swarm, the air itself becomes pregnant with postponed events, and the chamber holds its breath.

Living among the architectural remnants—the half-pillars and intention-scaffolding—are the **Unfinished**. These are not creatures that built the structures, but rather *what the structures dreamed of being*. A column that stopped halfway through aspiring to be a tower has, in its incompleteness, generated a resident: a being of vertical yearning, humanoid in outline but transparent as poured glass, perpetually reaching upward with fingers that blur at the tips into blue mist. They do not walk but *extend*, stretching from their host stone like waking yawns. They are melancholy, these Unfinished, aware that somewhere in the crust above, their completed forms have already crumbled to dust, while they remain eternally midway, perfect in their potential, tragic in their stasis.

The **Sternwards** tend to the floor, or rather, to the process of the floor becoming ceiling-to-be. They walk the raised scars where water has argued its way upstream, leaving mineral stipples in defiance of topography. These beings have lower bodies like millipedes made of pumice and obsidian (though the stone is neither), while their upper halves are cloaked in the damp film that coats everything. They do not have faces, only surfaces where faces might convene. They are the only inhabitants who understand the comfort of weight; they press themselves against the rising stone, encouraging it, singing to it in frequencies that sound like pressure and time. They are midwives to geology, ensuring that the chamber's slow birth continues, that the accumulation proceeds upward, that the Oubliette does not abort itself back into the void of unbeing.

Where two creatures meet—where a Resonant grazes a fungal fan, where a Kith alights upon an Unfinished's glassy shoulder—there occurs not predation but **Exchange**. These brief contacts generate the Brokers, hybrid entities that exist as walking negotiations. A Broker might have the jelly-body of a Resonant but the multifaceted uncertainty of the Kith's wings, or the vertical reach of the Unfinished combined with the mineral humility of the Sternwards. They are temporary diplomats, lasting only until the components forget they agreed to combine, at which point they separate, leaving behind a scar of cooperation in the air, a smell like wet limestone and déjà vu. The Brokers speak the only language native to the chamber: the syntax of *almost*, the grammar of *perhaps*.

And then there is the **Pause**—the emptiness that occupies the center. It is not absence but a resident entity, a being composed of the negative space between accumulations. It has the shape of a door that hasn't been knocked on yet, or a womb before the first cell divides. The Pause mediates the chamber's brightness, filtering the pre-source light into something the other inhabitants can metabolize without burning away their own tentative reality. It is protective, this emptiness, a guardian of margins. When the gravity forgets itself and pools in the ceiling, it is the Pause that reminds it of its duty, coaxing the mercury-silver weight back down with the promise that downness is still a valid concept.

Finally, drifting in the time-spirals, collecting the breaths of visitors five minutes past, are the **Mnemons**. They look like threads of smoke trapped in ice, or the afterimage of lightning carved in humid air. They gather the discarded moments—the exhalation you forgot you made, the blink that took too long, the hesitation before your first step—and they weave them into the fungal fans, strengthening the mycelial networks with borrowed memory. They are the chamber's record-keepers, ensuring that even as everything here hurtles forward into being, nothing is truly lost. When you see your own breath drift past, condensing on a future surface, you are seeing the Mnemons at work, archiving your existence into the pre-stone, ensuring that when the Oubliette finally crystallizes fully into reality, it will carry your ghost in its geologic memory.

Together, these inhabitants do not form an ecosystem—they form an **ecopoiesis**, a mutual creation of world and witness. They are not adapted *to* the chamber; they are the chamber's adaptation to itself, the immune system of a reality learning not to reject its own existence. They accumulate, negotiate, hum, and remember, busy in the cool bright damp with the endless, tender work of becoming real.
