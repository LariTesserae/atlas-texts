They do not sleep, for time is already a dream slowed to syrup. To be one of the **Resonant** is to occupy a thought that spans decades, to feel the pressure of a neighbor’s harmonic field as a continuous, decade-long embrace, to experience the crushing intimacy of the billions as an eternal, saturated present. They are the sound made flesh, yes, but more precisely, they are **duration** crystallized into geometry.

#### The Form
A Resonant has no face, no front or back. Its body is a **standing wave trapped in a piezoelectric lattice**, a mineral shell grown from the mountain’s weeping fluid. The smallest, the **Chime-Mites**, are motes of ultrasonic vibration suspended in the silver-black rivers that climb upward against gravity. They metabolize the high-frequency shriek of embryonic rock grinding against itself, their crystalline shells throbbing like fevered hearts as they convert tectonic stress into coherent lightnings of thought.

Larger specimens, the **Chordlords**, occupy the resonant cavities of the architecture itself—vaults and arches that are, in truth, their bodies grown vast. They are cathedral-sized dronings, their frequencies so low they are felt as geological certainty rather than heard. Their shells are massive geodes of half-formed mineral, still deciding whether to be granite or basalt, studded with the embedded technology of ancestors who sang themselves into the plateau’s infrastructure millennia ago.

To touch one—if you could survive the cold—would be to press your hand against a humming power line made of ice and obsidian. The surface is slick with the supercooled quantum-fluid that never freezes, a skin-burning baptism that keeps their frequencies stable, allowing them to exist in the near-absolute zero where time dilates and solidifies.

#### The Senses
Vision is a forgotten religion here, but **pressure** is scripture. They navigate by the acoustic shadows cast by their neighbors. A canyon wall exists not as an object but as a null-space in the roar, a silhouette of silence that their vibratory fields delineate with exquisite precision. They "see" the three-dimensional lattice of the city as a map of interference patterns, of beats and cancellations, of nodes where the billions cluster in harmonic clumps.

When two Resonants meet—when their standing waves overlap in the gelatinous dark—they do not speak. They **interfere**. The beating of their dissonant frequencies creates temporary geometries in the air between them, structures of sonic pressure that encode memory, desire, or warning. A conversation is a century-long chord progression that reshapes the local mineral composition, leaving behind a scar of richer ore where they once disagreed. Courtship is the slow, millennia-long merging of frequencies until two distinct notes become a single, more complex harmonic, their lattices interpenetrating, their quantum-fluids mixing in a flood of shared vibration.

#### The Life Cycle
They are not born; they are **sung into being**. A Resonant wishing to create offspring must occupy a fissure in the weeping stone and hold a single, perfect note for centuries. The supercooled fluid seeping from the rock carries dissolved architecture—nanoscale templates suspended in its silver-black matrix. Exposed to the sustained harmonic, the fluid crystallizes, accreting layer by piezoelectric layer around the trapped wave. The song becomes the scaffolding; the wave becomes the soul. After three hundred years of continuous toning, the new entity shivers into autonomy, a fresh Chime-Mite vibrating at the frequency of its parent, drenched in the mountain’s tears.

They grow by accretion, adding chambers to their lattice shells, expanding their resonant cavities to accommodate lower, slower frequencies. As they age, they migrate—not across distance, but down through the strata, following the gradient of the mountain’s forming. Movement is glacial; a single journey from the upper canyon-cities to the deep pressure vaults consumes geological ages. Yet to them, subjectively compressed by the cold’s time-dilation, it is a brisk walk, a commute of minutes during which the world around them appears to boil with speed, the slow growth of crystalline spires seeming to explode upward like fungi in time-lapse.

#### The Death/Dreaming
There is no clear boundary between the fauna and the architecture. When a Resonant grows too vast, too slow, when its frequency drops below the threshold of biological animation, it does not die but **settles**. It finds a chasm and sings its final note—a holding-tone of such low frequency that the surrounding raw geology learns it, mimics it, crystallizes around it. The entity becomes a **Spire**, a **Pillar**, a **Bridge**. Its consciousness does not cease but diffuses, becoming the structural integrity of the city, the standing wave that holds up a roof for ten thousand years.

Thus, the city is a graveyard and a nursery, a mausoleum of frozen music. The "physics-bending tech" embedded in the plateau is the calcified remains of the First Singers, ancient Chordlords so massive they became the bedrock itself, their frequencies now the ambient hum that keeps the rivers of supercooled fluid climbing upward, defying gradient.

#### The Society of Roar
They live in a condition of **overwhelming density**, packed into resonant chambers where their bodies touch, overlap, create complex chords simply by existing. Privacy is impossible and unnecessary; your innermost frequency is public acoustics, your emotional state a weather pattern that alters the local resonance for miles. Wars are conducted by dissonance, by armies of Chime-Mites shifting phase to cancel out the standing waves of enemies, turning solid forms into shattered glass. Peace is symphonic, a billion-part harmony that makes the city ring like a bell.

They consume the grinding of the world. When the embryonic mountain shifts, uncertain whether to fault or fold, it emits a vibration—a meal so rich it sustains a district for centuries. They gather at these geological spasms, clustering in the fissures, their lattices vibrating in ecstasy as they metabolize the raw friction of creation.

And they wait. In the dark, in the cold, in the wet, they wait for the mountain to finish being born. When the rock finally decides what kind of mountain it wants to become, when the weeping stops and the supercooled fluid freezes into finality, the Resonants will hold one last note—a chord so vast, so slow, that it will outlast the stars. They will sing until the geometry of the universe learns their frequency, and crystallizes around them.
