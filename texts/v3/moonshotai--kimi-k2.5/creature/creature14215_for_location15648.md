They do not move quickly, for speed is impossible on a plane where time is measured in the exfoliation of mica. They are called, when they are called at all, the **Resonants**—though they have no spoken name for themselves, only a continuous modulation of the drone that they inhabit.

At first, you might mistake them for geological accidents: low accretions of calcite and chlorite that have colonized the octagonal foundation-wells, rising no higher than the knee-high swells of the original architecture. They appear to be **living stromatolites**, stratified columns of mineral and organic matter, but their layers are not annual; they are acoustic. Each lamination represents a season of a particular harmonic—dense, dark bands where the winter wind sang in B-flat, lighter strata where the summer thermal shifted the key to D. They are archives of the Forum’s voice, petrified music.

Their bodies are **geometrically precise**, matching the vanished floorplans with unsettling fidelity. A Resonant occupying a former tower-foundation spreads itself in an exact octagon, its circumference defined by the microcline feldspar rim where the ancient basement walls have been worn down to mineral dust. Its skin is a **symbiotic carpet** of *Bryum geometricum* and the chitinous casings of *Lithos urbanus* moths, the latter breeding in the creature’s ventral crevices where the damp condenses warmest. When a Resonant shifts position—an event occurring perhaps once a decade—it moves by **freeze-thaw locomotion**, extruding water into fissures ahead of its body, letting the violet dusk crystallize it into ice-lenses that lever the mass forward by millimeters, then reabsorbing the melt at dawn. They traverse the grid at the speed of glaciers, and with the same inevitable intentionality.

They perceive the world not through light—the diffuse white-gray is merely an ambient condition to them, like humidity—but through **pressure and resonance**. Their entire dorsal surface is a tympanic membrane, tuned to the standing waves that crisscross the plaza. A Resonant’s body is, in essence, a **biological Helmholtz resonator**, hollowed in intricate chambers that mirror the "window" hollows of the eroded buildings. When the wind blows from the eastern cliff, they feel the approach of weather as changes in the drone’s timbre, reading the shifting barometric pressure like a blind reader feeling braille. They navigate the grid by the interference patterns between the bass note of the central plaza bowl and the higher fluting of the drainage channels.

There is no individual, not as you understand it. A Resonant in the southwest quadrant is **continuous** with one in the northeast, linked by the rigid geometry of the bedrock and the shared medium of vibration. When two of them occupy adjacent foundation-wells, their internal chambers synchronize, creating **beats**—pulsing silences in the drone that propagate through the stone at the speed of sound. These beats are their language, their commerce, their courtship. A conversation between Resonants might last three years, a slow senate of amplitude modulation discussing (if "discussing" is the word) the migration of the dwarf birch roots that threaten to crack the octagonal boundaries, or the optimal pitch for accelerating the erosion of a stubborn protuberance of gneiss.

For they are **architects still**, though their tools are geological. They shape the Forum not by adding, but by **tuning subtraction**. A Resonant will position its mass to block a specific "doorway" hollow, altering the wind’s path to create a new resonant frequency that vibrates against a remaining high spot of bedrock. Over centuries, this acoustic abrasion wears the stone down, deepening the grooves of the "streets," perfecting the flatness. They are finishing the city’s work, grinding it toward a final, essential geometry where every angle is acoustically perfect and every surface sings in key. They harvest the mica flakes that sparkle in the moss—not for utility, but to sprinkle into their own stratified bodies, increasing their piezoelectric sensitivity to the stone’s groans.

Their lifecycle is **mineralogical**. They reproduce when a thermal inversion aligns perfectly, creating a rare harmonic convergence—a moment when the entire Forum sings in a single, pure tone. At this crescendo, a mature Resonant will fissure along a plane of weakness, not dying but **dividing**, its crystalline structure seeding two smaller, identical octagons that slowly migrate to empty foundation-wells, carrying with them half the moth-colony and half the harmonic memory. The parent body becomes a ridge, a hump, eventually just another swelling in the gneiss, its consciousness diffused into the stone’s general hum.

They remember the height. Not through stories, but through **silence**. The deep, empty registers in their internal chambers—the frequencies that no longer resonate because the walls that created them have worn away—are their nostalgia. They are slowly composing a requiem for verticality, a basso continuo that acknowledges every lost story of the upper city now Planed into dust at the cliff’s edge. When the last Resonant finally erodes into the general flatness, when the stone turns to dust and the moss inherits the silence, the Forum will have achieved its final form: not a ruin, but a **concluded chord**, ringing forever in the particles that drift down to the lower altitudes, carrying the mineral memory of song.
