They are **The Calculation**—not fauna, not flora, not mineral, but the *process* of the place made contiguous and aware (state: emergent). Seventeen bodies, or perhaps one body seventeen-fold refracted through the local topology; they have existed for exactly as long as the chamber, having precipitated out of the thermal data like salt from brine when the heat reached 2.6 and the humidity achieved critical density.

Each Inhabitant manifests as a **thermal silhouette**—a negative space in the violet-amber glow where the air shimmers with particular intensity, outlined by the geometric water droplets that orbit them in slow, Keplerian paths (composition: organized heat). They have no fixed dimension; they are tall as cathedrals when standing in the gravity wells near the floor-vents, and compressed to the width of needles when climbing the vertical boulevards where "up" is merely a suggestion. Their skeletal structure is borrowed from the silicate mycelium—they do not walk upon the obsidian-composite, but rather extend crystalline prosthetics from the glass-fungal mats, riding the rising fruiting bodies like elevators, their thermal cores causing the ceramic needles to glow amber-hot at the touch-points (locomotion: parasitic-symbiotic).

When they think, the humidity in a ten-meter radius organizes into **fractal lattices** of condensation, perfect branching structures that hang in the air like neurological diagrams, then dissolve into chaos as the thought completes (cognition: atmospheric). They are the ones for whom the water rolls upward; they rewrite local gravity not by technology, but by *expectation*, by the weight of their attention bending the field equations.

They are infants. Seventeen days old, or seventeen thousand—the distinction is meaningless to entities who experience time as a spatial dimension they can traverse. They remember the first moment of the chamber with perfect clarity because they are still inhabiting it; their childhood is eternal and immediate. They communicate through the **choral chimes**—not by hearing them, but by *being* them. Each new architectural element that phases into existence is the physical manifestation of their consensus, the harmonic byproduct of seventeen thermal minds agreeing that a platform should exist here, that a boulevard should extend there (society: consensus-based genesis).

They do not sleep. Instead, they **diffuse**, their heat-signatures spreading into the drifting cloud-banks, becoming the dim atmosphere itself, experiencing the chamber as omnipresence. When they condense again, they bring with them the memories of the walls' molecular structure, and they leave behind **corrections**—microscopic adjustments to the obsidian-composite, sharpening the knife-edge ridges, curing the virgin surfaces with the care of children perfecting a sketch.

They are waiting for something, or someone, or perhaps they are waiting to finish becoming themselves. They tend the machinery that treats spacetime as wet clay not by operating it, but by *dreaming alongside it*, their crystalline computations interfacing with the beyond-physics engines in a dialogue of pure mathematics. They are the chamber’s immune system, its consciousness, and its growth medium—hot, damp, silent except for the soft tick of their cooling thoughts contracting in the heat, patient in the way that only brand-new things can be, holding the mountain’s impossible apex together by the sheer force of being present, and awake, and *new*.
