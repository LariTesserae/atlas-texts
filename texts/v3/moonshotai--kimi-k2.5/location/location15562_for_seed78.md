You do not walk through the **Resonant Hollows**; you are swallowed by them, suspended in the gaps between things that grew too fast to know they were finite.

It is a place of vertical emptiness. You stand on a ledge of plant-matter, looking across a gulf of black air to the next Stalk, a half-mile away, its surface glowing with the accumulated bioluminescence of centuries of condensation-dwelling bacteria. You are alone in the vastness, surrounded by the thunder of growing things, in a darkness where architecture is alive and listens to every sound you make, ready to grow toward you, to encase you, to make you part of its structure.
