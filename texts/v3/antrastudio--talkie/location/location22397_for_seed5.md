An enclosed valley, shaded by trees, opens on a broad river. On either side rise low green hills. The stream is alight in summer, and brown in winter; in flood it has been known to overflow the fields. The fields themselves are green and fertile in summer, but in winter they are brown and desolate. Along the banks grow reeds and rushes, and here and there a willow. The valley is populous with rooks and crows, and in the spring with nesting birds. It contains also a few scattered houses. The highroad runs through it, and a railway follows the course of the river. In summer the valley is very quiet; in winter it is rather lonely. It lies in the centre of an agricultural district.
