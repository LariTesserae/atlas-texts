Built-on open, shallow-set ground, beside a river, that landscape is scattered and small. Scattered houses stand in lanes. A few trees grow here and there. Far away, uplands rise. The place has a primitive air. Its folk are primitive in habit and mind. Their speech is peculiar. The locality has weirdness, for silence hangs about it, and the souls of men seem broken. The river is never still, and a ceaseless murmur fills the air. The land has age, and bears evidence of extreme antiquity. There, man first began to use his hands. --Stop for another time.
