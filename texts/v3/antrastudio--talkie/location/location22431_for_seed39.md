It is a place where water is frequent. There are many ponds and pools, and the streams that feed them are slow and sleepy. The land lies low, and in winter is often flooded. The vegetation is sparse and poor; rushes and sedges fringe the waterside, and a heathery waste stretches inward to the hills. In summer the hills are green, but in winter grey. The temperature is cold, and the elevation extreme. Erosion is embryonic, and the scale open and vast. The density is empty, and the built features none. Mechanized industry has touched it only along the streams. The light is dim, and the fauna abundant, but not weird. The sound is mostly quiet, and the dynamics active.
