The place is on water, in a bleak northern latitude, below the tide-level of the sea. It is sunk in a discoloured area of the ocean floor, where weather-beaten areas of rock alternate with ahallows of sand and shale; it has less than a fathom of depth, and lies exposed to all weathers. Vegetation is sparse, and consists mainly of nullipores; the temperature is low; the elevation is below the surface; erosion has worn the site into shapes that bear witness to great antiquity. The place has no scale of life, but is fully occupied; the density of the life upon it is packed, and the site is built over with structures of inherent skeletons. The primitive tools used in shaping these are of stone; no fabrics are woven, and the light is glimmering darkness. The fauna is rare, and the life weird in its stillness. Sound is absent or reduced to quiet noises; and the dynamic energy of the place is turbulently chaotic.
