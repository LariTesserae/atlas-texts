A flat, low LYMI valley floored by alluvium, and bordered steeply by pebble-strewn up-lying slopes, runs eastward till it sinks beneath broader, featureless plain-country. Intravalley, midway, a village stands amid trees, and along either side spreads cornfield and pasture, narrowing to points at either end. The waters of the vale are dull, and motionless save where they turn a mill-wheel; and the landscape is closed in, toward north and south, by hazy distance. All is silent, save for an occasional clink of hammer from the smithy, the caw of rooks, and the murmur of the stream. Let it be early morning in summer, and let the scene be peaceful, English.
