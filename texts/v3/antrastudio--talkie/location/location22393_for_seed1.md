A vast open country stretched around me; the peculiar light of evening revealed it in misty confusion; plains and hills melted into each other; the horizon was boundless, and the sole relief was in long-drawn undulations of misty green and brown. Here and there a village rose out of the plain, and threw a blackbant upward to the sky; here and there a river glanced in the sunset like a yellow snake; and in the middle distance a grey wood laid a broad shadow on the field. Life was silent, motion-less: only, from afar came the faint rolling of wheels and the faint blowing of horns; and a lark sang up to darkness. The sky was full of stars, from the horizon to the zenith.
