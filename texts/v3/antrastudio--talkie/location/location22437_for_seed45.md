The place lay extended along a river-valley floor, between low hills, and was solitary. It was overspread by trees in watery grey-green, with here and there a clearing, where the earth showed brown. The margin was marked by a white ribbon of foam, where the stream hurried past; and the scene was closed in beyond by a line of blue-grey hills. In summer, the silence was broken only by the murmur of the water, and the chirp of birds; in winter, by the roaring of the wind among the branches. By day, all was bright and cheerful; by night, dark and gloomy. The entire aspect was desolate, and depressing to the spirits.
