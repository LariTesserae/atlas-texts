The land is spread out in a tumult of wild hills whose green surfaces tumble and toss in unceasing commotion. Between the billows lies a choked confusion of valleys, ravines, and gullies, filled with tumbling streams, and choked with trees and bushes. The uproar of the place is tremendous. The streams riot down, brawling and shouting. The gales crash through the tree-tops, howling and shrieking. In midst of it all rise great crags and cliffs, leaping stark into the storm, roaring defiance. Amid the turmoil of waters and winds, beasts shifted uneasily, afraid. In spite of themselves, birds fly past, screaming. Presently the whole turmoil settles down, and leaves a scene of majestic desolation.
