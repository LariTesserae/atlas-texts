The landscape is vast, and spread on a low elevation, where waters are broken to fluctuation between low tide and high, but never run down dry; waters are grey-green, and their shores brown. The place is emptied to loneliness, but peopled to ghostliness, by faint buildings sparsely spaced; and the buildings are neither white nor black, but brownish grey. The site lies in a clime of permanent, though moderate, cold; in a climate tufable to something between harsh sub-arctic and sharp-temperate; yet the atmosphere has some balminess, and the air is reasonably clear. In scale, the landscape reaches from conspicuous to minute; in density, it is intermediate between sparse and close-packed; in structure, it is scattered and inchoate, but here and there shows the relics of effort; in face, it is plain, and in sound, just audible. - Fergussoclingalib,
