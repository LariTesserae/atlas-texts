An unpeopled, untenanted wilderness has emerged from the sea. The land rises steeply from the water's edge, and the naked cliffs are cut and scored by gales. Their feet are washed by a surf that chatters and roars; their heads are lost in haze. Inland the ground breaks into hills, and these unite in a tumbled maze of peaks and ridges that stretch far away to the horizon. In colour, the waste is brown and grey. Here and there a black patch shows where a lava stream has flowed; elsewhere green spots mark the sites of baby rivers. The whole area is silent and deserted, save where, in a cleft between the hills, a white waterfall shoots down to the plain below.
