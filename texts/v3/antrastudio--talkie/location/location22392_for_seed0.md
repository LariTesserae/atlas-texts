An uncommon place of depth and tumult. The site lies low among hills, by a river that wanders east. The terrain is young, the ground newly risen from the water, and the banks are freshly steep. Trees line the stream, and vegetation else covers the hollow; the place is dense with life. The climate is warm, and the atmosphere is moist. The scale of the landscape is open, yet midrange, and the density of life is great. No artifacts appear, and the natural tech is primitive. Darkness prevails, and sounds are deafening. All is turmoil and vast hurry; the local energy is ant-hill energy indeed.
