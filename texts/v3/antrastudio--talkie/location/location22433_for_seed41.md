The landscape is linear and low; its strike follows east-and-west, and its dip is south. It lifts itself against a skyline that has the softness of cloud, and its hues are pale gray-green and silver. Water is in it, but no watercourses; there are pools among the hollows, and a little rivulet creeps down the central depression. Vegetation is luxuriant; copses and copses vanish into each other, and fields are empty only at the margin. The warmth is steady, summer warmth, and the elevation is below surface. The erosions show familiar shapes that have been settled long, and the scale is small. Density is considerable, and buildings are many, but slight and scattered. Art has not touched the place, and no devices of man pollute the quiet. Light is subdued, and sound does not abound. Activity is present, but no striking movement; weather cycles and life phases make the dynamic.
