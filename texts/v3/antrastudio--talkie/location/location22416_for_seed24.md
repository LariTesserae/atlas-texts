A blank countryside, with features that have been shaped and settled in quiet, - low rolling or featureless plains, covered in sparse vegetation, and threaded by a stream. The buildings are few and primitive, and the people seem to have little to do. Silence reigns, except for the sounds of animal and bird life, and the lapping of water on the banks. The whole district has an air of stability and old-world peace, broken only by the passage of trains, or an occasional cart upon the road.
