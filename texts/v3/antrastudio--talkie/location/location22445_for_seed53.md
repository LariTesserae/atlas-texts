The landscape lies below sea level; it is high linksward above the river, but low groundward, so that the water can be seen winding between close green banks. The terrain is flattish, a strip of margin, between mainland and river, carrying.trees. The materials are gravel and sand, a soil slightly raised in humus, now green in summer, but grey in winter. The atmosphere is quiet, with an occasional wisp of white cloud, and the sounds are those of normally rural life, birds, and water. The ecology, apart from cultivated fields, is grassy waste, with here and there a furze brake. The rules of the world are those of a mildly inhabited area, where people go afoot, and the roads are unmetalled. The place has an air of tranquillity, not desolation, and seems secure from disturbance.
