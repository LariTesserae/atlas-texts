An extensive place, stamped with wild antiquity, lies supremely aloof in emptiness, spaciously lonely, under a waste silence. The water there is dark, and damply cold; the vegetation lush, the temperature warm, the elevation extreme, the erosion ancient. The scale is open, and the density empty; the built state scattered, the tech primitive, the light normal. The fauna is rare, and the weirdness strange; the sound deafening, and the dynamic turbulently chaotic.
