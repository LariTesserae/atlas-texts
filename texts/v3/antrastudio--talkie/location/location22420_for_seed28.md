A singular landscape lies along gray water, where green fields slope upward from the shore to low hills. The region is silent, save for the faint sounds of life: the crying of gulls, and the rustle of growing things. In summer the fields are golden with corn; in winter they are white with snow. By day the scene is bright, and by night pitch dark. In the midst of it stands a little town, and the whole place has an air of primitive quietude and stability.
