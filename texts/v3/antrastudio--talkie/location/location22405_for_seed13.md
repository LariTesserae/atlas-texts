The place is high and open; it lies in a valley between low hills, and the fields stretch away from it in all directions. Water runs beside the houses, and there are trees about them, and gardens. In the fields are crops, and the ground rises and falls in gentle undulations. The soil is moist and rich. In the distance are villages and a church tower. The whole has an air of peace and prosperity. It is early in summer, and the weather is fine. Life goes on in the quiet, ancient fashion. The place is rural England.
