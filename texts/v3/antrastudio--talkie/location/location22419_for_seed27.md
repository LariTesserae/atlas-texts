It is a strange place, lying on the long edge of things, backed by waste uplands and fronting on a sheet of water. The waterside is low and sealike, and the landward margin climbs steeply to a cliff whereon stands a lonely house. The shore shelves gradually into shallows, and the beach is of hard sand. In midstream the water is green and clear, but along the banks it is brown and muddy. All round the shores grow reeds, and beyond them willows dip into the flood. The place has an ancient and forsaken air. The house is old and weather-beaten, the walls bulging and the thatch in holes. In front of it a garden straggles down to the water's edge, and behind it stretches a neglected orchard.
