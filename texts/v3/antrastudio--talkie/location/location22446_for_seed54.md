It is a strange, wild place, inhospitable, and aloof. Water gleams black among the rocks, and in silent pools beside the pathway. Vegetation is thin, and poor in quality, and the few trees are stunty and distorted. The temperature is below freezing point in winter, and in summer the air is chill. The altitude is considerable, and the region lies exposed to winds. Erosion has shaped the ground into sharp outlines, and the hill-tops are jagged. The scale is large. Density is small, for the population is scanty, and the buildings few. The built portion is urban, in the sense of settlement, but rural in its sparseness and isolation. Technical processes are evident, but they are older than the current fashion, and savor of forgotten arts. Light is dim, and in the gloaming ghosts may walk. The fauna is richly various, and weird. Sounds are striking, and dynamic. The place has an air of desolation, melancholy, and abandonment.
