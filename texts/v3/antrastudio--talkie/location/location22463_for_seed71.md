Valorenn landscipe is a place of bare water and bleak air; where weather has the widest sweep and erosion the deepest voice; where life is quiet, and the dead remain long unburied; where the soil is old, the settlement sparse, and the fabrics primitive; where the light is normal, and sound mostly silent. The enclosure is high, and broader than long. Its surface dips gently to meet the water in a plain margin along the shore; and rises inland to form a clouded belt of hill. Its material is stone. In summer, its tone is dingy grey; in winter, coldly blank. The streams are bright in flood, and dull in drought. Fog comes frequently in summer, and frost in winter. The place has no animals, and few birds. It is weird at night, and desolate in the daytime.
