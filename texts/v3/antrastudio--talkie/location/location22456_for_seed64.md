The place is a wilderness utterly bare of life, and lifeless to the very horizon. Water there is none, and the land lies in absolute aridity. Neither vegetation nor trace of it exists, and the surface is stony and rough. The temperature in summer is high, in winter low. In the former season the altitude is above timber-line. Erosion has operated freely, and the landscape is established in a settled form, long-existed, and weathered to a sobriety of tint. The scale is so small that the features are hidden, and the whole scene is wrapped in pocket-dimensions. The density of population is so slight that the place is saturated, and there is no room between the presences. The buildings are few and faint, and the artifacts occasional. The industries are small and scattered, and the mechanism is industrial. The illumination is dim, and the darkness at night extreme. Sounds are loud, and the silences weird. The dynamic state is stable, and the gradients nil.
