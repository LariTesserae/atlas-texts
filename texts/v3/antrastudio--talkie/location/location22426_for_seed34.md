It is a land where the earth is green, and the water blue, and the air clear; where the hills are high and the valleys deep; where fields lie pleasant in the sun, and the roads are white in the dust of summer, and black in the mud of winter; where houses are scattered, and trees abound, and birds sing; where beasts graze, and children play; and where old men rest in the shadow. It is a land of happy labourers, and of quiet think ers. Here, in the green fields, and by the pleasant waters, the generation arise from youth to manhood; and pass on, from manhood to age; and vanish away into the great darkness. And here their children rise, and follow them; and so the generations pass, for ever and ever, like the waves of the sea, or the shadows of the clouds. Here they come into life, and are mourned; and here they pass out of life, and are forgotten. But the land remains, and the sun shines on it, and the green fields, and the quiet waters; and the children play, and the quiet thinkers remain quiet still.
