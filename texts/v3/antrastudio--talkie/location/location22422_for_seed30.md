The place has a retired character, it lies quiet, apart from the main currents of life, in the bosom of a valley among hills. The main features of the landscape are water, vegetation, and buildings; but the water is only in occasional pools, the vegetation sparse, and the buildings few and small. The surroundings are steep, the ground rises boldly from the margin of the pools to a height of some hundred feet, and the whole area is scored deep with ravines, where torrents have rooted a course for themselves in past winters. The buildings are cottages, each standing in a garden plot, and the place has an air of homely comfort, rather than of active industry. The sounds heard are the murmur of waters and the cawing of rocks. The locality has been little changed by art, and seems likely to continue long in quiet possession of its present occupants.
