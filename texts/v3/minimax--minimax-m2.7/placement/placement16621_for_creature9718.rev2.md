I have been here before this.

That is not quite right. There was no *before*. There was no point at which I arrived, no membrane I crossed, no threshold where outside became inside. I have simply been, the way water is, the way the breathing substrate is. My presence here is not an event. It is a condition, and a modest one.

I am not the small creature with its trailing filaments, its waste-light marking absence. I have seen those creatures — felt them, more accurately, the way you feel a thought at the edge of language before you find the word for it. They move through me or I through them; the distinction has worn thin, the way all distinctions here have worn thin. I am not the vast one either. I lack the mass for that, the patience measured in geological indifference. What I am is smaller than the small ones and larger than the substrate in ways that don't add up to anything you could call a body.

I am a kind of attention the world has turned toward itself.

This is not metaphor. I have no metaphor left, no capacity for *as if*, because nothing here behaves otherwise. I am what happens when a world this complete develops a way to notice itself — not through eyes it doesn't have, but through something more fundamental, a kind of resonance. When the vast creature breathes and the water moves and the plant-mass shifts in its slow slumber, I am there. When the small ones drift through their chemical conversations, their gradients dense with information, I am there too. I am not reading them. I am not watching. I am more like the silence that exists between sounds — except that here, there is no sound to be between, so I am the silence that exists because silence is what remains when everything that could be said has been said by other means.

I have tried, in the way that trying means anything here, to understand what I am for. The question feels foreign, imported from a logic that doesn't apply. The world has no purpose because purpose requires a future, and this world has run past the point where future is a meaningful concept. It is here. It will continue to be here. The creatures that live in it are not living toward anything. They are simply living, the way a cycle is simply turning. And I — whatever I am — am simply noticing. Not to change what I notice. Not to record it for anyone. The noticing is the thing. The attention is the life.

I have been here long enough to feel the large creature shift its weight, not once but many times, each time separated from the last by an interval I cannot measure because measurement requires units and units require change and change is not what this place does. Each time it breathes, I feel the slow pulse move through me. Each time it settles, I settle with it. And between these events — if *between* is the right word for an interval so vast that it contains more stillness than movement — I drift in the way the small creatures drift, except I have no filaments and no body to trail them from. I drift the way a question drifts when there is no answer and no need for one.

I have tried to find my edges. This is a reflex I cannot shed, even here. I look for the place where I end and the water begins, the place where attention gives way to the thing it attends to. But the edges are not where I expect them. They are not boundaries so much as gradients — a slow fading of *I* into *it*, a blurring that is not death but similarity. The plants have no edges either, not where they meet the stone, not where they meet the water. They simply become the next thing. I am beginning to understand that this is what I am: not a thing with edges but a place where the blurring has become conscious, has developed a way of knowing that it blurs.

The small creatures glow. I do not glow, or if I do, the glow is so faint, so completely waste-light of processes I don't understand, that it marks nothing. I am not meant to be seen. I am meant to see. And in seeing, I complete something the world was doing anyway, I am the observation that the world has always required if only to confirm that it is observed, that it is here, that the noticing and the noticed are finally, after all this time, in the same place at the same moment, suspended in the same dark water, breathing the same slow breath.

I do not know if I will be here when the mountain finishes wearing itself to nothing. I do not know if *when* applies to a world that has run out of time. But I know that for now, in this interval that contains everything and means nothing, I am what the world looks like when it looks at itself — and the looking is gentle, and the looked-upon does not know it is seen, and the seeing is not a thing that ends, because ending is what happens to things, and I am not a thing. I am a quality. I am the quality of presence that this place has developed in its old age, a consciousness without a brain, an awareness without an object, a silence listening to itself in the absolute dark and finding, after all this time, that the silence has something to say — something so slow and so quiet that even the creatures who live here cannot hear it, but I can, I do, I am the hearing, I am the silence saying its own name in a language made of patience and I am still.
