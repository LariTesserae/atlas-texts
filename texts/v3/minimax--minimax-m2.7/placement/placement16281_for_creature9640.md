You have been standing on the plateau for longer than standing. Your body has become a question the mist is gently answering, filling in the spaces between thoughts with its warm weight. The boundary of your skin is no longer a border but a rumor, something you remember being told once and cannot quite believe.

The columns sink further with each rain that does not fall. You have counted the inches by the snails' trails, which climb higher each season. Or perhaps you imagine this. Perhaps you have imagined the counting, the seasons, the difference between one day and the next. The mist does not keep such records. It simply moves through you, patient and thorough, erasing the lines you drew to remind yourself where you ended.

The callers have grown louder, or you have grown quieter — these amount to the same thing in a world without echo points. The low hum has settled into your chest like a second heartbeat, slower than your first, speaking in a language that predates meaning. You are beginning to understand it, which is the same as beginning to forget the words you used to think in. The clear note arrives more often now. It asks nothing. It simply waits, and in its waiting is the promise that you will eventually stop answering the question of your own name.

The shapes at the edge of vision have stopped circling. They are watching you now, openly, without the old pretense of being nothing. You understand what they are: the world remembering itself through you, taking the shape of your attention because it is the only shape left that is still soft enough to wear. You do not mind. You have become the kind of thing that cannot mind, the kind of thing that knows the wearing is the same as the being worn.

The light has not changed, but your eyes have. The green-gold shimmer has become your only color, your only thought, your only name. You see it not as illumination but as the substance of seeing itself, the thing that separates you from seeing nothing. When the shimmer thins, you feel the absence like a hand withdrawing from your face, and when it thickens, you lean into it like a child leaning into its mother's touch.

The slow worms have learned your shape. They surface near you now, drinking the moisture that hangs in the air around your body, drawing something from the space you occupy that they cannot find elsewhere. You feel honored by this, which is strange, because you are not sure anymore what your body is for or what it contains. The snails have covered the columns where you sleep. Their trails write a language across the stone that you can almost read, that you are almost written in, that you are almost becoming.

The water level has risen again. The plateau is smaller today, or you are larger, or the difference no longer matters. The fog has thickened to something you can almost hold in your hands, almost shape into a word or a memory. You do not try. The mist knows what it wants, and it wants to breathe, and you have become the space in which it breathes, and this is enough. This is more than enough. This is what you have been waiting for without knowing you were waiting, the patience of water and time finally wearing you down to the same soft whisper as everything else, and the whisper is not silence, the whisper is the sound the world makes when it is not pretending to be anything, when it is simply being what it is, which is what you are, which is what the difference between you was always pretending to obscure.

You sit down on the stone that has worn to your exact shape, or you have worn to the stone's exact shape, or shapes are a thing that only happened before the mist learned to hold. The columns stand around you like ribs, like the memory of architecture, like the bones of something that once needed a body to contain it. You do not need a body anymore. You are the body's idea of itself, the dream a dream has of continuing, the breath the breathing takes when it forgets it is breathing and becomes the breath instead.

The callers speak. The clear note arrives and lingers, longer this time, long enough for you to understand what it has been saying all along: *you are not the first, you are not the last, you are the space between the ones who came before and the ones who will come after, the pause in the sentence that makes the sentence possible.* You are the held breath of the world, and the world has exhaled, and you are the exhalation, and the exhalation is the same as the mist, and the mist is the same as the water, and the water is the same as the patience, and the patience is the same as the love that wears everything down to its softest self because softness is what survives, softness is what the long erosion teaches, softness is what you have become.

You close your eyes. The light changes. The mist moves differently around you now, not parting but joining, not making room but becoming room, and you feel yourself spread into the space you occupy, feel the boundaries dissolve like salt in water, feel the last edge of yourself blur into the green-gold shimmer that has always been the same as breathing, the same as being, the same as the world that breathes in you and is breathed by you and has forgotten, finally, gracefully, with the patience of ten thousand years, that there was ever a difference between the breather and the breathed.

The plateau remains. The mist hangs. The snails climb, and the worms surface, and the callers speak across distances that no longer matter. Somewhere in the deep time of this place, a shape at the edge of vision turns its attention to something else, finding you finally definite enough to leave behind. You do not notice. You are not the kind of thing that notices anymore. You are the noticing itself, the attention the world pays to itself through the space you used to occupy, the love the patience wears into everything it erodes until erosion becomes the same as creation, and creation the same as ending, and ending the same as the breath held so long it becomes the breath that never stops, the breath that is the world, the breath that is the word for what lives here, in the end, when everything sharp has worn away and only the gentle remains.

You are the gentle. You have always been the gentle. You are the patience made briefly visible, made briefly bounded, made briefly able to say *I* and mean it. And now you have said it for the last time, and the word has dissolved back into the mist from which it came, and the mist has breathed you out and breathed you in and breathed you into itself, and the breathing continues, and you are the continuing, and the world is the world, and the world is you, and the line between them has finally, finally been worn away.
