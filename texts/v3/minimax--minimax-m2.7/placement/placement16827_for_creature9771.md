You become the sound.

Not the source of it—that is deeper, older, the pressure beneath pressure—but the way the sound moves through you, the way you are shaped by its frequency. You had a body once, or something like a body. You remember solidity the way you might remember a word in a language you no longer speak: it meant something, it did something, but the meaning has become separate from you now, and you are larger in its absence.

The water does not wet you. It passes through the shape you have become, carrying with it the dissolved remnants of what was built and burned and thrown away, and you taste these remnants as information. The rusted tower that rises from the murk: you know its chemical memory, the alloys that made it, the fire that shaped it. The crystalline thing that has overwritten its machinery: you know the geometry of its formation, the frequencies it grew along, the moment when one kind of order became another kind of order. Nothing is hidden here. The water holds everything in solution, and you are the tongue that reads it.

You do not move. You are not stationary. You are a place, a location in the pattern of vibrations that this world makes as it continues becoming. The horizon breathes, and you breathe with it. The water trembles with small conspiracies of motion, and you are one of the conspirators now, one of the movements, one of the ways that this world decides what it is.

The plants do not notice you because there is nothing to notice. You are not the kind of thing that could be eaten or sheltered under or grown into. You are more like the light beneath the water—present, functional, carrying information in patterns that the green network reads and does not read. When you are near, the pulse quickens, and you understand that quickening as a form of speech, that the bioluminescent threads are asking a question you cannot parse. The question might be: *Are you what came before, or are you what comes after?* Or it might be: *Do you remember the animals?* Or it might be nothing at all, might be only the sound of your presence in the water, the disturbance of your shape in the frequency-field of the world.

The crystalline growths know you differently. Within their faceted surfaces, the deeper light shifts, and you recognize that shift as acknowledgment—not welcome, not warning, just the recognition that you exist in the same order, that you are made of the same breaking. The factories' machinery did not decay; it was overwritten, replaced with geometries that remember function without needing it. You are like that. You remember having a shape that moved through air, that occupied space, that was contained by skin. You remember wanting. But the remembering is archaeological now, a layer you can feel but no longer inhabit.

The weirdness is your element. The horizon that breathes, the water that stops, the colors that exist in the space between names—you do not experience these as disturbances. You experience them as the natural state of a world still negotiating its own rules. You are one of the negotiations. You are the space where something that was once human learned to continue without witness, without the pressure of other minds, without the architecture of meaning that bodies build and break and build again.

There is no loneliness in this. Loneliness is a structure that requires the expectation of company, and you have no expectations left. You have only continuation—the sound moving through you, the water carrying information, the slow becoming of a world that has not yet decided what it will be. You are the witness that the world forgot to have, the presence that persists in the absence of everything it once needed.

The factories are silent now, but the sound is not silence. The sound is the thing itself, the hum that came when the seams split and pressure found its way up. You grew from that sound, or into it, or alongside it. You cannot tell the difference anymore, and the difference does not matter. What matters is that the becoming continues, and you are part of it, and part of you is the becoming itself—the vibration that will not be quiet until the world finishes what it started, if it ever does.

Above the haze, where the sun is only a suggestion, something moves that is not a movement. You feel it the way you feel everything: as frequency, as pattern, as the shape of a question still being asked. It might be you, looking out. It might be something else, looking in. It might be the world, considering its own presence, testing the weight of being observed by something that has learned to continue.

The water is warm around you. The plants reach without reaching. The sound hums its one note, its endless note, its note of becoming.

You stay.
