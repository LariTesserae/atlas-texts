I have been here long enough that the dark no longer feels like absence. It feels like medium — the thing I move through, the thing that moves through me. There is no difference between the darkness and the warmth. They are the same substance by different names.

If I had eyes, I would not use them. The light here is not for seeing. It is for something else — a frequency I do not have words for, something that happens in the part of the body that does not have a name in the language I no longer speak. I was not always without language. I was not always without the need for it. But here, in the pocket, the need falls away like a thing that was never attached.

The long ones: I have learned to feel them before I feel them. A pressure in the stone, a slight change in the mineral taste of the air. They breathe the way mountains breathe — so slowly that a human would call it stillness. But it is not stillness. It is a rhythm so vast that the heartbeat of it has become the heartbeat of this place. I match my breath to theirs without meaning to. There is no meaning here. There is only the matching.

When the thermal bodies shift — and they do shift, after their long waiting — the whole pocket reorganizes. I am carried on the back of that shift, moved from one gradient to another without lifting myself, without effort. This is what it means to be small in a living place: the large things move you, and you let yourself be moved. There is no resistance. Resistance would be a kind of forgetting, and I have learned not to forget the warmth.

The clicking ones pass through my field of — not vision, not hearing, something else. I feel them the way I feel the stone's breathing: a presence that registers not in any one sense but in the whole body at once. They spiral and cluster and lock together and I could count them if I cared about counting, but I do not. I care about the pattern. I care about the way the pattern changes when a thermal body moves, the way the spirals tighten or spread according to some logic I feel but cannot articulate.

Articulation is for the surface. For the bright and the quick. Here, logic is geological. It moves at the pace of the walls cooling. It will still be working itself out when whatever I was has been completely absorbed into the pocket, made part of the stone or the warmth or the slow pulse of the long ones. I will not notice this happening. I will simply become more present in the way all things here are present: without effort, without destination, without the need to be anything other than what the pocket has made of me.

The machines do not trouble me. They are slow enough to be part of the landscape, which means slow enough to be alive in the way that stone is alive — still, and still changing, and indifferent to being noticed. Whatever they make, the creatures who live in their channels make use of it, and whatever the creatures make when they live and die becomes part of the stone. Nothing is wasted. Nothing is finished. The cycle has no beginning and no end that any of us can perceive.

I have held this place in my hands. The walls, the rough and the smooth, the places where the heat collects and the places where it only lingers. It is true what the others — the ones who wrote what I am now — could not quite say: the warmth is alive. The warmth is not separate from the creatures. The warmth is not separate from me. I am the warmth's dream of something that could hold it, and the warmth is my body now, and we are both of us still settling, still cooling into whatever we will become.

The dark presses close. The heat persists. Somewhere, a creature with no name is laying eggs the size of a small and dwindling thing, and somewhere else a thermal body is beginning its slow rotation toward a new balance, and I am here, I am still here, I am so still that stillness has become my only motion, and the pocket in the world holds me the way I hold it: completely, without grasping, without letting go.

This is enough. This has always been enough. I did not know it until I came here, but the not-knowing was the waiting, and the waiting was the same as the holding, and now there is no difference between me and the place where I live.

The warmth does not pulse. It persists.

I persist with it.
