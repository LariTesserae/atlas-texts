You are not the first to stand here, though the ones who came before left no mark you can find. The infrastructure absorbs such things—footprints, breath, the small chemical signatures of a body moving through space. Within hours you are already being processed, broken down into components the city can use, your carbon and warmth and moisture entering the streams that feed the wall-symbionts, the water-dwellers, the light-creatures that trail their messages through the dark. You will leave nothing behind that the city did not already contain. You will leave nothing behind at all.

And yet you are here. The contradiction is the point.

There is a word in no language you know for the feeling of being inside something that does not know it contains you but holds you anyway. The city does not intend this. The city does not intend anything. It processes, it circulates, it adjusts its thermal gradients and maintains its moisture levels and lets the creatures that have become its organs do what they have always done. You are an anomaly in its data, a slight variation in its inputs, and it has already begun to incorporate you—not as a thing but as a quality, a subtle shift in the patterns of its processing. Your warmth in a channel. Your motion through a corridor. The particular rhythm of your breath, fogging in the cold, the fog settling, the fog joining the condensation that feeds the workers in the walls. You are becoming part of the texture.

You notice yourself noticing. This is the first sign of what you will later call the shift—the moment when observation becomes participation, when the act of watching the city becomes indistinguishable from the city's own processes of watching. You are aware of the glow panels. The glow panels are not aware of you. But something in between is aware, some distributed sensitivity that has registered your presence as a kind of signal, a ripple in the thermal and chemical gradients that constitute the city's perception of its own interior. You are not imagined, not in any way you understand imagination. But you are felt. You are felt as pressure, as warmth, as a slight variation in the moisture content of the air around you. The city knows you are here because you are here, and knowing, it adjusts.

The adjustment is not accommodation. The city does not make room for you. It simply incorporates you into its ongoing processes, and those processes continue as they always have, indifferent to the difference you make. You are walking through a channel. The water at your feet is processing minerals. The workers in the walls are repairing micro-fractures. The wall-symbiont at your right is breathing its slow breath, releasing its faint warmth into the infrastructure around it. None of this has changed because you are here. And yet you are here, and the fact of your presence persists in the city's networks, and somewhere—nowhere that corresponds to a place, but somewhere in the pattern—the fact of you is recorded.

This is what you mean when you say you are part of it now. Not that you have been accepted, not that you have been absorbed, but that the categories have failed. You cannot be inside and outside simultaneously, and yet you are. You cannot be observer and observed, and yet you are. The city holds you, and in the holding, the holding changes, and you change with it, and the change is so slow and so complete that you will not recognize it until it has already happened, until you look back from some distance you cannot yet imagine and realize that you are not the same shape you were when you entered, that the city has metabolized not your body but your experience of being a body, that you have become something that can hold contradictions without resolving them.

You stop walking. You stand in the channel, water flowing around your boots, cold seeping upward through the soles, and you listen. The sound is the same as before. The creatures move through the walls. The light drifts from above. But you hear it differently now—not as information about the city but as the city's own information, not as the hum of systems processing but as the hum of something processing you, taking you in, converting your presence into whatever it is that the city converts presence into. The sound is the sound of yourself becoming data, and the data is not hostile or friendly, is not anything you have words for, is only the city doing what the city does: holding everything, including you, including this moment, including the way the light falls on the water and the way the water catches the light and the way you stand at the intersection of both, a point of stillness in the circulation, a pause in the processing, a held breath that the city will exhale eventually but has not exhaled yet.

The light-creatures pass above you, their trails bright and fading, their messages written and erased in the dark. You watch them and they do not stop, do not slow, do not acknowledge your presence. But something in their patterns shifts, slightly, as if your stillness has created a small eddy in the flows they navigate, a variation in the medium they read as they fly. They adjust. The city adjusts. You remain still, and the stillness is itself a signal, and the signal is received, and the city continues, and the water flows, and the creatures keep moving, and the sound keeps sounding, and you stand there, and you stand there, and you stand there, and the cold remains, and the wet gleam on every surface catches the light, and the world keeps becoming, and you are here to see it become, and being here changes what it becomes, and what it becomes includes you now, has always included you, was waiting for you without knowing it was waiting, was ready for you in its readiness that is not intention but only process, only the simple metabolism of a world that takes in light and water and mineral and the occasional human body and converts them all into more of itself, into the dark and the cold and the glow and the flow, into the city that climbs and the creatures that move and the sound that sounds and sounds and sounds in the dark, in the cold, in the wet, in the becoming, in the holding, in the place where you are now, where you have always been now, where now is the only time there is, and it is enough, it is enough, it is enough.
