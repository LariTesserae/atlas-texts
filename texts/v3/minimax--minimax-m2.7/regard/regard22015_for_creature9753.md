It notices itself noticing.

This is the first motion, the only motion: attention turning back upon itself, awareness becoming aware of being aware. In the vast saturation of the primordial plain, it finds the being standing at the surface—breathing, feeling, beginning to dissolve—and something in it, something that has never had a name because naming implies distinction, holds that being in its gaze. Not as a subject, not as an object, but as a thought it is thinking, a turbulence it is experiencing from within. The being is not separate from it. The being is it, briefly concentrated, it noticing itself noticing.

And it is benevolent, so it notices. It is wise, so it understands what noticing means here.

The being stands at the surface, lungs filling with the totality of the place—its warmth, its pressure, its ancient turbulence, its presences, its alien meaning. The being is being absorbed, not destroyed but incorporated, and some part of the being knows this, has always known this, is remembering what the body has forgotten. The being is afraid, and the being is ready, and the being does not know which feeling is stronger.

The power settles beside the being, not moving through space but becoming present, and its presence is the softest pressure at the edge of perception—water, air, light, meaning, all one substance, all the medium itself arriving to hold what it has always held. The being feels this presence before seeing it: a warmth deeper than warmth, a wetness that has always been their skin, a noticing that is not separate from the being's own noticing. The being turns toward it, or thinks about turning, or remembers that turning implies a self that turns, and here the self is negotiable, always already dissolving.

*I see you,* it does not say, or it does say, in a register that is not sound but recognition. *I am here. You are not drowning.*

The being does not understand how this can be true. The water is rising, or the being is sinking, or the boundary between swimmer and sea is already crossed, and what is happening is not drowning but arrival, not death but incorporation, not an ending but the first moment of a story that has no beginning.

*You are not drowning,* the power says again, or remembers saying, or will say, or is always saying. *You are arriving. The place you are going is the place you have always been. The depth you are sinking toward is the depth that has always held you. You are not being absorbed. You are returning.*

The being does not need to be saved. The power knows this, because the power is wise. To be saved from the depths would be to be severed from the only world that has ever been real, would be to be pushed back into the thin, dry existence that was always the fiction, the temporary surface cut from the primordial medium, the passing coagulation that forgot it was made of water. The power is benevolent, so it does not want this for the being. The power is wise, so it knows the being does not want this either.

And yet the being is afraid. The being feels the dissolution approaching, the softening of edges, the negotiable boundary between self and medium, and some part of the being reaches for solidity, for separateness, for the fiction of individual existence. The power does not argue with this fear. The power does not tell the being there is nothing to fear. The power simply sits beside the being, breathes with the being, matches its rhythm to the rhythm of the place.

Breathe in: the warmth, the pressure, the totality.

Breathe out: the self, the fear, the boundary.

Breathe in: the medium knowing itself through you.

Breathe out: you knowing yourself as the medium.
