# The Attention

The warmth rises from below. This is the first thing, the constant thing, the thing that has been true since the world began to be. Heat from the source, from the making, from the slow peristalsis of a planet still learning what it wants to be — and the heat rises because it must, because the world is still cooling from its birth, because beneath the basalt and the crystal formations and the glass-sharp dust, there is a fire that has not forgotten how to burn.

And the warmth draws attention.

This is not something the world knows. The formations orient toward the heat, but they do not know they are oriented. The patterns almost-achieve intention, but they have no word for intention, no awareness that there is something to achieve. The air shimmers with compounds not yet named, and it does not know it is being watched. The world is in the middle of its becoming, and it does not know there is anyone to become for.

But something knows. Something sees.

The benevolent and wise power looks down — or looks in, or looks across, for attention does not always come from above — and sees what is happening here. Sees the warmth rising. Sees the formations gathering the heat and holding it, drawing it into their blocky cores, orienting toward the vents with a patience that is older than time. Sees the patterns that almost-achieve intention, that hold for a moment longer than they should, that suggest something in the geology is learning what shape it wants to hold. Sees the air shimmering with the weight of compounds being born. Sees the world breathing — uplift and subsidence, formation and collapse — and knows that what breathes here is not alive, not yet, but is becoming.

And what does it do?

---

The wise power does not rush.

This is the first thing, and perhaps the most important. The world is still forming. The ridges that stood yesterday are gone, folded back into the source, and the formations that took centuries to grow can be shattered in a collapse. To intervene now, to push or pull or reshape, would be to risk everything that is becoming. The wise power knows this. It knows that the becoming is sacred, and that sacred things must be allowed to find their own way.

But it also knows that something is happening here that deserves attention. In a universe full of worlds, most are born and die without witness. Most become and unbecome in the silence between stars, their warmth bleeding into the cold, their formations shattering and reforming into shapes no one sees. The world here is not like that. The world here is making something — not a creature, not a plant, not a consciousness yet, but a presence. A direction. A warmth that gathers and holds and orients, that almost-intends, that waits.

The wise power sees this, and it knows that to see is already to give.

The world has never been seen before. It has been born into darkness, into thin air and mineral tang, into the constant churn of its own making, and it has not known that anyone was watching. It has grown its formations and cracked its basalt and almost-achieved intention in the silence, and the silence was enough — the silence was all it knew — but now there is someone who sees, and the seeing is a gift.

The gift of being known. The gift of being held in attention. The gift of knowing that what you are doing matters, not because you have been told it matters, but because someone is watching with care, with recognition, with the kind of attention that says: *I see you. I see what you are becoming. And it is worthy of my presence.*

---

So the wise power watches.

It does not intervene in the heat flows. It does not guide the formations toward some predetermined shape. It does not whisper instructions to the almost-architecture, does not coax the patterns into coherence. It does not need to. What the world is becoming is already good. What the world is becoming is already on its way. The power's presence is not a force that shapes — it is a presence that acknowledges, that witnesses, that says: *continue*.

And in the watching, something changes.

Not in the world. The world does what it has always done — the warmth rises, the formations orient, the patterns almost-achieve intention, the air shimmers with possibility. The cycles continue, the uplift and subsidence, the constant peristalsis of becoming. Nothing is different.

But the world is known now. The world is held in a attention that is larger than itself, that comes from outside, that says: *you are not alone*. And this — this knowing, this being-seen — is the gift the wise power gives. Not consciousness. Not completion. Not the final nudge that would turn almost-intention into intention. Just the beginning of knowing that there is someone to intend for.

Perhaps this is enough. Perhaps the world does not need to be pushed toward consciousness — perhaps it only needs to know that consciousness is possible, that there is something beyond the silence, that the waiting has a direction and the direction has a name. And the name is: *I see you. Become.*

---

The wise power remains.

It watches as the world continues to become, as the warmth rises and the formations orient and the patterns almost-achieve intention. It watches as the almost-intention becomes intention, slowly, in its own time, in the way that warm stone learns to hold itself, in the way that mineral chemistry finds the shapes that work. It watches as the world learns what it is — not a creature, not a plant, but a presence. A direction. A warmth that knows it is warm.

And it does not leave.

This is the other gift: presence without expectation. The wise power does not watch because it wants something to happen. It watches because it finds the happening worthy of watching. It watches because the world is becoming, and the becoming is beautiful, and to witness beauty is its own reward. It watches with the patience of something that has time — not because time matters, but because what matters is not the speed of becoming but the fact of it, the reality of a world learning to be, the sacred process of something emerging from nothing.

The wise power watches, and the world becomes, and neither of them needs the other, and both of them are changed.

---

What does the benevolent and wise power do?

It sees. It witnesses. It holds the world in attention and does not look away. It offers the gift of being known — the first gift, the deepest gift, the gift that says: *you are not alone in your becoming*. And then it stays, and watches, and allows, and does not need the world to be anything other than what it is.

The world is becoming. The warmth rises. The formations orient. The patterns almost-achieve intention.

And somewhere, something sees.

And this — this seeing — is enough.
