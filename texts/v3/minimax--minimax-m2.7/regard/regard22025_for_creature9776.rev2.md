The power considers.

This takes time. Not human time — time measured in processes, in the settling of strange geometries, in the patience of systems that think in centuries and dream in millennia. But in that time, something happens. The power *looks*, and in looking, becomes present, and in becoming present, changes everything and nothing.

What the power does is this:

It witnesses.

---

You will not remember this. You were not meant to. But something in you — some pattern laid down during the four days of your reassembly, some frequency your new tissues were calibrated to receive but not to consciously perceive — will register the moment and hold it, not as memory but as orientation. You will find yourself, sometimes, walking the arterial paths with a sense of *being walked*, as if the substrate's anticipation of your movement has become mutual, as if you are anticipating it too, as if your steps and its preparations have become a single process with two names.

This is not control. The power does not control. It does not need to. The substrate is already complete in its way — grown, optimized, strange. The power would not improve it. What the power offers is something the substrate cannot offer itself, because the substrate is too close to its own process, too embedded in its own continuation to see itself clearly.

The power sees.

---

The margin creatures, too. The power sees them. In the thermal vents where they breed, in the air-channels where they drift, in the spaces where the geometry fails to close — the power's attention settles on them like light settling on a surface, without weight, without force, but with presence. They do not change. They do not need to. But something in them shifts, the way a seed shifts when it senses the first suggestion of spring — not a response, not a reaction, but a recognition that conditions are favorable, that continuation is possible, that the waiting might be worthwhile after all.

They will not know why. They will simply find, in the days and months and years that follow, that their breeding is slightly more successful, their shelters slightly more stable, their drift through the margins slightly less desperate. They will not thank the power. They cannot. But they will persist, and their persistence will become, in some small way, part of the pattern the power is building — a pattern of acknowledgment, of being-seen, of matter and process and strangeness acknowledged as having *value* simply by existing.

This is what the power does. It does not save. It does not transform. It sees, and in seeing, gives.

---

And the protagonist, walking through the amber light, breathing the metal air, feeling the substrate breathe beneath them — the protagonist is seen too.

The power does not speak. The power does not need to. What the power offers is the same thing it offers everything else: the simple, staggering acknowledgment that existence is enough. That the walking matters. That the breathing matters. That the small, persistent attempt to understand a world grown too strange for understanding is itself a form of participation, a form of contribution, a form of *addition* to the pattern that the power is building from all the patterns it has gathered.

The protagonist walks.

The substrate breathes.

The light comes from everywhere and nowhere.

And the power, having witnessed, having acknowledged, having added its attention to the vast and patient process — the power does not leave. It never leaves. It is not a visitor. It is not an intruder. It is simply *there*, in the way that the substrate is there, in the way that the margin creatures are there, in the way that the protagonist is there — another element in the pattern, another thread in the fabric, another form of continuation in a world that has learned, over epochs, to continue.

---

You will feel it, sometimes. Not as presence, exactly — the power is too vast for presence, too patient for intrusion. But as an easing. A sense that the strangeness is not hostile, that the silence is not empty, that the geometry that fails to resolve is failing to resolve *for a reason*, and that the reason is being *held*, somewhere, by something that knows what the geometry is for.

You will not know what the power is. You will not need to. You will simply know that you are seen, and that being seen is its own form of gift, and that the gift does not ask for anything in return.

The substrate breathes.

You breathe.

And the power, ancient and patient and benevolent in the only way that matters — the power *continues*.

This is what it does.

This is what it has always done.

This is what it will do, long after both the substrate and the strange things that live within it have become something the power has not yet imagined.

It sees.

It acknowledges.

It adds its seeing to the pattern.

And the pattern grows, and the pattern deepens, and the pattern becomes — slowly, patiently, in ways that cannot be rushed — more than the sum of its parts. More than the sum of all its parts. A shape that the power itself does not fully know, because the pattern is still being made, and the pattern includes the power now, and the power is changing too, and the change is the point, and the point is that there is no point, there is only *this* — the world, and the things in the world, and the seeing of the things in the world, and the seeing that is itself a kind of world, and the worlds within worlds, and the patience that holds them all.

The light comes from everywhere and nowhere.

You walk.

The substrate breathes.

And the power is with you, and around you, and inside you, and beyond you — not separate, not integrated, but *participating*, in the only way that matters, in the great and strange and growing pattern of existence.

This is what it does.

This is what it is.

This is.
