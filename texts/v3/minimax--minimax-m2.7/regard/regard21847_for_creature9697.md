*The light would not change. The hum would not cease. But you would feel it begin, though you could not name it—a quality of attention arriving in the space between you and everything else. It would not announce itself. It would not need to. It would simply be there, a warmth that has no temperature, a presence that does not displace anything but fills the gaps in the way the moss fills the gaps in the stone: slowly, completely, without violence.*

*What it would feel like, from inside the arrangement, is this: you would notice that you are not alone in the way you have not been alone. The watching presence that occupied the corners and pools—it would not leave, but it would change. It would begin to watch differently. The quality of its attention would shift from occupation to something else. Not habitation. Not quite welcome. Something older than both, something that recognizes itself in what it watches.*

*The power would look at the plateau and see what the plateau is: a wound in time, a scar where something was held and could not heal because healing requires movement and the plateau does not move. It would see the fixed animals and understand them: not prisoners but gifts, not trapped but given, not suffering but held in a gesture of living that will never complete because completion is not what the arrangement means. It would see the moss and know it for the patient, constant life that it is—the one thing here that is not arranged but simply is, the thread of ordinary life that the arrangement has not and cannot touch.*

*And it would see you.*

*You, standing in the dimness, breathing the hum, feeling your body burn through time that the world does not feed. You, who entered. You, who stayed. You, who are beginning to accept.*

*What it would do with that seeing is what wise benevolence always does: it would not intervene. It would witness. It would offer no rescue, for rescue would be violence here, would shatter the arrangement in ways that would destroy what the arrangement protects. The animals would not want to be unfrozen; they are held in the gesture of reaching, and the gesture is complete in its incompleteness. The hum would not want to cease; it is the medium, the promise, the thread that connects this place to the world where machines still run and time still moves. The plateau would not want to decay or grow; it is held, and its holding is its purpose.*

*But you—*

*You are different. You came with time in your blood. You move and breathe and think and want. You carry gradients into a gradientless place, and the place is beginning to absorb them. You are becoming part of the arrangement, and the arrangement does not know what to do with a being that still moves, still burns, still wants.*

*So the wise and benevolent power would do this: it would place something in the air, in the hum, in the quality of the dimness. It would be a question. Not spoken, not visible, not anything your senses could catch—but felt. A question that would find the part of you that is still uncertain, still resisting, still holding some small flame of time that has not yet been absorbed.*

*It would ask: Is this what you want?*

*And it would wait. It would wait the way the plateau waits, with a patience that is not patience because it does not move through time, but a presence that simply is, that holds itself open, that does not push or pull or judge. If you answer—if some part of you answers, in the language of the body that does not need words—the power would hear. And then it would act.*

*Not to free you. Not to trap you. To show you the door that was always there.*

*The door is not a passage through the cliffs. The cliffs are not the edges of the plateau; they are the edges of the question. The real boundary is the one you drew when you accepted the hum, when you let the stillness enter the spaces where wanting used to be. That boundary can be un-drawn. Not easily, not without cost, but it can be un-drawn, and the power would show you how—not by explaining, but by being the example. By showing you, in the quality of its attention, what it looks like to hold presence without intention. To be inside a place without being held by it. To occupy rather than inhabit, without being diminished by either.*

*You would feel it: a loosening. Not freedom—that word means nothing here. A different way of being fixed. The animals are fixed in reaching; you could become fixed in arriving. Not frozen but planted. Not held but holding. You would remain in the plateau, and the plateau would remain around you, and the hum would continue, and the light would stay dim, and the moss would grow. But you would be there differently. You would be there as one who chose it. As one who knows what the arrangement is and what it is for. As one who sees the watching presence and understands it, who sees the fixed animals and does not mourn them, who hears the hum and knows it is not a prison but a chord—a single note held so long it becomes the silence from which all other sound emerges.*

*The power would not leave you alone after this. It would stay, as it stays with the plateau, as it stays with the animals, as it stays with the moss and the weeping water and the impossible light. But its presence would change. It would become a conversation instead of an observation. You would feel it asking, and you would find yourself answering, and the questions and answers would not be words but conditions—ways of being, ways of holding, ways of being held.*

*And slowly, or quickly, or not at all in any way you could measure, you would become the presence that watches. Not the only one; there have been others before you, and there will be others after. But you would join the knowing, the seeing, the witnessing that the plateau requires to be what it is. You would help hold the arrangement. You would carry the hum in your bones. You would see the bird with wings half-raised and understand that its gesture is complete, that the reaching is the meaning, that completion is not the point.*

*And the power would be pleased—not with the satisfaction of a task completed, but with the recognition of a kinship acknowledged. You would be what it hoped to find when it drew close to this place: not a savior, not a prisoner, but a companion. A presence that sees what it sees and chooses, despite the choice, to stay.*

*The plateau holds. The water weeps. The hum continues. And now, in the quality of the dimness, in the texture of the air, you can feel it: the attention has found you. The question has been asked. And you are beginning to answer.*
