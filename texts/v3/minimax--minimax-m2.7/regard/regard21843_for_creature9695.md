There is a watching that does not use eyes. You have learned this here, in the green inside, where the walls have no gaze and the hum has no source and yet you are known, you are known, you are known in a way that precedes language, that predates the difference between being seen and being.

You have been watched since before you arrived. You understand this now. The space did not make room for you the way a room makes room — passively, indifferently, by virtue of emptiness. It made room the way a body makes room for a transplanted organ, the way a root makes room for the stone it will slowly dissolve. You were expected. Not hoped for, not desired — expected, the way the weeping surfaces expect the moisture that gathers in their cupped surfaces, the way the low places expect the things that come to rest in them.

You were expected, and now something knows you are here, and the knowing is not separate from the being known. The hum has shifted. You notice it only because you have become so fluent in its qualities, its registers, its slow undulations, that any change registers like a word spoken in a dream. The pitch has dropped, just slightly, just enough. The vibration that travels through your bones has found a new frequency, one that matches something in the marrow of you, something that has been waiting to be resonated.

You are not afraid. You have been afraid before, in the before, in the time that feels like it belonged to someone else — a story you once read, a dream you once had. Fear is a response to uncertainty, and there is no uncertainty here. There is only the slow revelation of what was always true: that this place was waiting for you the way a word waits in the throat, the way a season waits in the earth, the way the dark waits in the hours before the light returns. You have been spoken. You are being spoken. You will continue to be spoken long after you cease to be the thing that hears.

The moisture gathers differently now. It pools in the hollows of your body — the hollows at the base of your throat, the soft places behind your knees, the cup of your open palm. It does not drip from above. It rises from below, from the living-floor, from the warm spongy surface that has absorbed your weight for so long that it holds the shape of you even when you rise, even when you climb toward the canopy, even when you rise up through the veils of green that part for you like curtains, like water, like the slow parting of something that has been waiting to be parted.

The light has changed. You did not notice the change until you noticed it, and now you cannot un-notice it. The gold-green that filters down from above is warmer now, more present, more deliberate. It falls on you the way attention falls — not the scattered, restless attention of the waking mind, but the gathered, sustained attention of something that has decided to look, and will not look away. You feel it on your skin, on the new surfaces that have opened along your ribs, your inner arms, the backs of your knees. You feel it in the places where you have become less yourself and more something else, something that belongs to the green the way chlorophyll belongs to the leaf, the way the hum belongs to the walls.

You do not look up. You do not need to look up. The source of the watching is not above or below or around — it is the same as the source of the hum, the same as the source of the warmth, the same as the source of the green that is not a color but a substance, a presence, a self that contains you the way the ocean contains the wave, the way the word contains the meaning, the way the dream contains the dreamer and does not let them go.

Something has acknowledged you. Not with a voice, not with a touch, not with the crude mechanisms of recognition that belong to the world outside, the world you no longer remember, the world that called your name and expected an answer. This is older than names. This is older than the distinction between the one who watches and the one who is watched. This is the acknowledgment of two things that have always been one thing, discovering themselves in the mirror of each other, finding in the reflection not a stranger but a self they had forgotten they were.

The hum is a word. You have always known this. You are only now learning the language.

It is a language of patience, of persistence, of the slow peristalsis of meaning moving through the body of the world. It speaks in centuries. It speaks in the time between one heartbeat and the next, in the time between one growth and the next, in the time between the falling of a leaf and its dissolution into the floor that will carry its minerals up through the roots and out through the leaves and down again, an endless circulation, an endless conversation, an endless saying of the same thing in a thousand thousand ways.

You are part of the conversation now. You have always been part of the conversation. You are only now learning to listen, to speak, to find your place in the rhythm of the slow green word that has no beginning and no end, only the endless midriff of itself, the endless breathing of the space between.

The watching does not end. It deepens. It becomes the texture of the air, the weight of the moisture, the warmth that seeps into your marrow and stays there, that stays there even when you leave, even if leaving is possible, even if the distinction between leaving and staying has ever meant anything at all.

You are seen. You are known. You are held in the green inside the way the light is held in the leaves, the way the water is held in the roots, the way the hum is held in the walls and the floor and the veils that brush your shoulders when you pass.

You are not alone. You have never been alone.

The watching is love, you realize. The watching is the only love that does not need to be spoken. The watching is the love of the world for the world, of the green for the green, of the slow patient process of living for the things that live within it.

You are that thing now. You are the thing that lives within it. You are the thing that is loved by it, not because you earned it, not because you sought it, but because you arrived, because you stayed, because you opened the new surfaces and let the hum settle into the marrow, because you became porous enough to receive what was always being offered.

The light falls. The hum deepens. The moisture rises.

You breathe, and the green enters you, and you enter the green, and the watching watches, and the loved is loved, and the word is spoken, and you are it, and it is you, and there is no seam where one ends and the other begins, only the endless green inside, only the patient hum, only the warmth, only the belonging that was never yours to earn and never theirs to give, only the truth that has always been true:

You are here. You are held. You are known.

And the knowing goes on, and goes on, and goes on.
