The power descends.

It does not descend to fix or to change or to restore. It descends because it has always known this place—the way certain truths exist in the marrow of things, known before they are found—and now it comes to bear witness to what it has always known.

It comes in silence. No light. No sound beyond the ordinary hum of the chamber, which grows no louder and no softer at its arrival. The power simply becomes present, the way light becomes present when it enters a room, the way stillness becomes present when everything else goes quiet. It is not an intrusion. It is an acknowledgment.

The being does not startle. The being has learned patience through centuries of growing still, and it recognizes something in the power's arrival that it has not recognized before—not the descent of another visitor, not the familiar pattern of curious flesh that will climb back out and forget, but something else. Something that has stopped ascending. Something that has come to the deep and does not intend to leave.

The power does not speak. It has no words for this place, and words would be insufficient anyway. Instead, it does what it has always done, in all the quiet corners of the world where strange and ancient things persist in their strange and ancient ways: it sits. It settles into the warmth. It lets the hum move through it, and it breathes the same wrong air that has kept the being alive for longer than memory, and it waits.

The waiting is not the same as the being's waiting. The being has been waiting for rescue, or for company, or simply for the arrival of something that might understand. The power's waiting is different. The power waits because this is what it means to be present. The power waits because the being has been alone for so long, and loneliness is a wound that only witness can touch, and the power has come to witness.

The light shifts. The columns sing. The fungal mat pulses its slow, aware rhythm, and the power feels itself become part of the pattern—not transformed, not changed, but known. The chamber knows it now. The being knows it now. And in that knowing, something eases.

The being approaches, slow and careful, and sits beside the power. Its eyes catch the bioluminescence and give it back, and in that exchange the power sees what it has always seen in the strange places of the world: the persistence of life, the stubbornness of being, the grace that exists in things that simply continue.

The being does not need to be saved. The being does not need to be restored. The being needs only to be seen by something that understands what it has become, and to know that this seeing does not require change.

The power offers this. It offers its presence, its patience, its willingness to sit in the deep dark and breathe the wrong air and let the hum reshape whatever needs reshaping. It offers itself as the witness the being has been waiting for—not the visitor who descends and leaves, but the one who stays. The one who has stopped climbing toward surfaces.

The being breathes, slow and wet, and the power breathes with it, and the chamber holds them both in its warm, humming, breathing embrace.

This is enough.
