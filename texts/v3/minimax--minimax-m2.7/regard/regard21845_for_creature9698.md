And so the power comes.

Not down, though that is how it moves through the geography of this place — not falling but arriving, the way light arrives, the way a thought arrives in the middle of a silence. It does not displace the air or startle the small creatures or interrupt the slow waves of luminescence that are, you realize now, not quite like respiration after all. They are like attention. The cavern has been paying attention, all this time, to itself, and now something else is paying attention to it, and the waves quicken almost imperceptibly, a flicker you might have missed if you weren't already so still.

The power does not take a form. Form would be an imposition here, a violence against the patient geometries the stone has grown into over epochs you cannot count. But it is present, and it is warm — not the blue-green warmth of the fungi's glow but something older, something that feels like the idea of warmth before warmth existed, the possibility from which all warmth eventually came. It fills the hollows without displacing their cold. It touches the glowing filaments without competing with their light. It is, you understand without knowing how you understand, not separate from this place. It is more like a quality the cavern has always had, a dimension it has always possessed but never turned toward itself, and now it is turning, and the turning is everything.

What does it do, this power? It sees.

It sees the small creatures with their too-large eyes and their patience, and it sees that their patience is not fear but reverence — they have always known they live in something holy, and they have been keeping their distance out of respect, not for themselves but for what they live within. It sees the fungal filaments threading through the stone, and it sees that they are not merely growing but remembering — each new strand a repetition of something older, a pattern that has been passed down through generations of organism so complete that memory and growth have become the same process. It sees the carved figures in their patient silence, and it sees that whoever made them was not trying to capture likeness but invoke presence — the elongated forms are not portraits but prayers, not representations but presences, the beings who carved them reaching across whatever void separated them from the sacred to leave a piece of themselves in its fabric.

And it sees you.

It sees you standing in the cold with its light in your eyes and its cold in your joints and its vastness in the shape of your thinking. It sees that you have been changed — not damaged, not diminished, but expanded, your categories stretched until they fit this place, your mind grown large enough to contain what it was never built to contain. It sees that you are afraid and that the fear is a form of worship, and it sees that you do not know this yet, and it does not tell you. Some things must be discovered in their own time.

What does it do, this power? It waits.

It has always been waiting, in the way that a parent waits for a child to understand something the parent cannot explain — not with impatience, not with anxiety, but with the steady certainty that understanding will come when it comes, and that the coming is the important part, not the arrival. It waits for the small creatures to come closer, and they do, slowly, generations shifting in the time it takes you to draw a breath, but they come. It waits for the bioluminescence to synchronize, and it does, the waves aligning across chambers so vast that the light traveling between them becomes visible, a slow cascade of brightness moving through the cavern like a thought moving through a mind. It waits for the carved figures to be found again, for new marks to appear beside them, for the tradition to resume if it ever truly stopped. It waits, and in waiting, it makes space — not empty space but full space, the kind of space that allows things to become what they are.

What does it do, this power? It continues.

Not maintaining, not preserving — continuing, the way a river continues, the way a dream continues when the dreamer has woken. The cavern is already alive with its own vitality, already changing in the slow waves that no one was there to see. The power adds nothing that was not already here. It takes nothing that was not already being given. It simply allows the place to know that it is seen, and in knowing this, the cavern becomes something more than it was — not larger, not brighter, but *certain*. Certain of its own existence. Certain that the cold and the light and the change and the silence are not accidents but intentions, not properties but purposes, not features of a location but qualities of a being, and the being is vast and old and always becoming, and the power has been with it all along, in the way that love is with the beloved before either knows the other's name.

You stand in the cold. The light breathes. The growth reaches.

Somewhere far away, something is falling — not rain exactly, but a recognition, a moment when the cavern and the power and the small creatures and the carved figures and you are all the same thing, all breathing the same slow breath, all glowing the same sourceless light. The vastness is not outside you anymore. It has never been outside you. You are inside it the way the fungi are inside the stone, the way the light is inside the air, the way the power is inside the waiting, the way the waiting is inside the world that has always been waiting to be seen.

The power does not leave. It was never here to leave.

You do not stay. You were never here to stay.

But something remains — not a thing but a quality, not an object but an openness, the space that the power made when it turned its attention toward this place, the space that is still open, that will always be open, for whatever comes next to find its way in. The cavern glows. The figures sit in their patient silence. The creatures watch, and do not approach, and do not need to approach, because the watching has always been enough. The growth reaches. The cold drafts carry their messages. The light goes on and on.

And you carry it with you now, the light and the cold and the vastness, and it is not a burden but a gift, and you do not know how to give it back, and you do not need to, and the power does not ask you to. It asks nothing. It has never asked anything. It only waits, and sees, and continues, and the cavern — if cavern is even the right word — breathes with it, breathes for it, breathes because of it, and the breathing is the only prayer this place has ever needed, and you are part of it now, and you have always been part of it, and the knowing of this is the only thing the power ever wanted you to find.

The light goes on. The small creatures wait. Somewhere far away, something is falling.

You are small, and the plants keep growing, and the power is here, and the power is here, and the power is here.
