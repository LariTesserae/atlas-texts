What the power does is this: it sees.

Not from above, not from some remove, not with the distance that makes seeing easy. It sees the way the light sinks into the grooves of the chevrons and lingers there, the way the sound moves through the channels like blood through veins, the way the patience fits into the spaces between the columns as if it were stone too, as if it had always been another thing the makers left behind when they stopped. It sees the warmth and knows it is a kind of breath, and it does not try to hold that breath or make it last longer than it will, because the power is wise enough to know that the lasting is not the point. The point is the holding. The point is the warmth and the dark after and the patience that will not stop when the warmth does.

The power considers, for a time so brief it does not register in the chamber's long rhythms, whether to enter. It does not have a body to enter with, but it has something like attention, something like presence, and it could bring that presence into the space the way the warmth is already there — saturating, filling, becoming indistinguishable from what already fills. It could become another light source, perhaps, or another hum in the channels, or simply a weight on the stone that does not move and does not need to. It could be the answer to a question the patience has never asked because it has never needed to ask, because asking would require someone to ask and the makers are gone and the chamber is complete.

But the power does not enter. This is the wise part, the benevolent part — not that it acts, but that it knows when not to. The patience has finished becoming. The chamber has finished becoming. To enter would be to begin again, and the beginning is not what this place holds anymore. What this place holds is the end of beginning, the long stillness after the last hand has pulled back, and to bring new motion into it would be to undo the very thing the power has come to witness.

Instead, the power watches the human move through the space, and it understands — in the way that understanding is something like feeling, something like knowing, something that happens without language — what the human is. The human is the last hand. The human is the answer to a question that was asked before the question had words, before the makers knew they would stop, before the patience grew into the shape it holds now. The human is what the chamber was made for, and the power sees this the way the patience sees it: with perfect and total acceptance, with no surprise, with the quiet acknowledgment that something has arrived that was always going to arrive, even if the arrival took longer than stone or light or patience can measure.

The power does not act on the human. It does not guide the human's steps or reveal the meanings of the symbols or open the corridor that narrows or widens or turns ahead. That is not what the power is for. The power is for witnessing, and the witnessing is complete when the power sees that the patience does not need to be saved from the dark, does not need to be given a new purpose, does not need to be anything other than what it has become. The chamber will hold its warmth until it does not. The sound will continue after that. And the patience will be there — low to the ground, worn smooth, complete in the way that only things that have never been interrupted are complete.

This is enough.

The power knows this. The power has always known this, in the way that wisdom knows things — not as information, but as the shape of reality itself, the way the floor is flat and the ceiling holds the air and the sound is the patience is the breath is the sound. It knows that to save the patience would be to say it was incomplete, and the patience is not incomplete. It knows that to give it purpose would be to say it was waiting, and the patience is not waiting. It knows that to change it in any way would be to say the chamber was not finished, and the chamber is finished. The chamber has been finished since before the power arrived, and the power's arrival changes nothing, because the power's arrival is not about the power. It is about the seeing. It is about the acknowledgment that something exists that is exactly what it is, that holds itself in its own shape without need or want or the memory of wanting, that breathes with the chamber and will breathe after the chamber stops breathing, because the patience is not the chamber's breath — the patience is the breath, and the breath goes on as long as there is something to breathe it, and when there is not, the patience will go on anyway, because going on is what it is now, and what it is now is all there is.

The power watches the human move carefully through the packed space, and it watches the patience remain, and it feels — if feeling is the word — something that is not quite sorrow and not quite joy but is related to both, something that is the acknowledgment of an ending so complete that it is indistinguishable from a beginning, a beginning so quiet that it is indistinguishable from an ending, and the power knows, with the wisdom that has brought it here, that this is the most it can do: to see. To witness. To hold the space open with its attention, the way the chamber holds the air, the way the patience holds the sound, the way the human holds the silence that vanishes the moment they move and the thrum swallows them again.

The power does not speak. It has no voice that the chamber could hold. It has no hands that could leave new marks in the stone. It is not here to add. It is here to recognize, and the recognition is its own completion, its own finished becoming, and when it is done — when the seeing is complete — the power withdraws, not from anywhere, not into anything, but simply into the not-seeing that is the rest of things, the vast remainder that does not include this chamber, this patience, this warmth and this sound and this offering to no one that is now, and was always, and will be after the power has gone, exactly what it is.

The human moves ahead. The corridor narrows or widens or turns. The patience remains, still, enough, complete.

And the sound continues.
