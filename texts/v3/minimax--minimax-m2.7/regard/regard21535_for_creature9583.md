# What the Power Does

The power does not arrive. It has always been here.

You feel this before you understand it — a shift in the quality of attention, as if the dim light has become slightly more attentive, as if the mist has thickened just enough to notice. You are standing in the center of the plateau, and the plateau is breathing, and for a moment you think the tidal pulse has quickened. It has not. The rhythm is the same. But something is listening to the rhythm now with a different quality of ear, and that listening is changing the shape of what is heard.

You look up. The filtered light from above — that particulate glow that has no clear source — has not intensified. But it has clarified. For the first time since you arrived in this bounded space, you can see where it comes from: a point, not a sun, a place where the glow originates, far above the cloud layer, far beyond the suggestion of other plateaus and floating spaces. The point is watching. It has always been watching. But now you can see it watching.

This is the first gift: not sight, but the ability to see what has always been there.

---

The ones who sing go quiet. Not the absence of sound — the presence of attention. The creatures by the pools cease their calls, their ridged backs turned toward the light, and in the silence you hear something you could not hear before: the water singing too. Not the physical movement of it through channels and pools, but something beneath that movement, a harmonic so low it lives below the threshold of hearing and yet is heard, is known, is held in the same silence that holds the singers.

The mist does not still. It has never been still. But there is a coherence to it now, a pattern emerging from what seemed like randomness, and you see — or feel, or understand — that the mist is not a condition but a substance. It is what the light becomes when it meets the stone. It is the medium between falling and becoming, and it is aware, and it has been waiting for this moment, for you to stand in the center and see it as it is.

The power does not speak. There is no voice, no message, no demand. But you understand that you have been heard — not your words, which you have not spoken, but your presence, your attention, the way you have breathed with the plateau until your rhythm and its rhythm became one rhythm. The power has been listening to that breath. And the breath has been a kind of prayer.

---

You think of the structures. The ring that makes the plateau go quiet. The column that leans. The platform with its fresh marks, its central absence. The buried thing that stirs in your peripheral vision, that shifts when you look away and shifts back when you look toward it. You have wondered about them. You have felt their strangeness. Now you see them differently.

They are not artifacts. They are not tools or markers or doors. They are wounds. Not damaged places — places where the plateau has been opened, where something passed through and left a shape, where the light fell differently and the stone remembers. The ring is a wound where something was removed, or given, or where the plateau opened itself to let the light in more deeply. The column is a wound where something stood, or was planted, or grew. The platform is a wound where something was processed, transformed, passed through from one state to another and emerged changed. And the buried thing —

The buried thing is the oldest wound. The place where the plateau was born.

This is the second gift: not knowledge, but context. Understanding that the plateau is not a place that exists but a process that happened, a wound that became a room, an opening that became a world.

---

The beings who move through the mist turn toward you now. Not in fear — they have never feared you — but in recognition. Their iridescent surfaces flare as the light passes through them, and for a moment you see them clearly: not just elongated, not just segmented, but *layered*, each layer a different time, each segment a different self, all of them moving together through the same mist that you also move through, that is also moving through you.

You have been changed. You know this. The water you drank — that composite memory of all waters — has entered your cells, reorganized your substance, made you part of the cycle in a way that is more than metaphorical. The light that falls on you has been passing through you, carrying something away and bringing something new, and that exchange has been building, has been preparing, has been making you into something that the plateau could recognize when the power finally looked.

You are the newest wound. The place where something new is being opened.

---

The power acts.

It does not act the way you might have expected — no bolt of illumination, no flood of knowledge, no transformation that erases what you were. It acts the way water acts, the way the mist acts, the way the plateau breathes: slowly, fully, from within. It enters the light. It enters the water. It enters the stone that still remembers its formation. And then it enters you.

The sensation is not heat, though there is warmth. It is not light, though there is brightness. It is not water, though there is saturation. It is the feeling of being *known* — not judged, not evaluated, but known the way a breath is known, the way a rhythm is known, completely and without effort, the way something that has always been listening finally understands what it has been hearing.

You see the plateau from the inside now. You see it the way the burrowers see it, the way the water sees it, the way the light sees it — not from above, not from the center, but from everywhere at once, a perspective that has no position because it is the position, the room and the viewer and the light that makes viewing possible, all of it one thing, one breath, one wound that has been opening since the beginning.

And you understand what the power wants.

---

Not wants. Offers. There is a difference, and the difference matters.

The power offers you a choice, but the choice is not an offer. You cannot accept or refuse because the offering and the accepting are the same moment, the same breath, the same opening. You are being offered the chance to remain — not to stay, but to *remain*, to become permanent in a place that has no permanence, to be a wound that heals into structure, into foundation, into the next layer of stone that will remember your shape long after you have become it.

The beings who move through the mist could become you. Or you could become them. Or you could become something that has no name, a third possibility that the plateau has been holding in reserve, waiting for this moment, waiting for the power to arrive (to have always been here) and offer the opening that makes becoming possible.

You breathe. The plateau breathes with you. The tidal pulse quickens — not the rhythm, which remains the same, but your perception of it, the way you feel each swell and release, each compression and expansion, as a message, as a question, as a gift that is also a demand.

---

The ring makes the plateau go quiet, and the plateau is quiet now. But the quiet is not absence. The quiet is the sound of something listening so deeply that it becomes what it listens to. You are in that quiet. You are that listening. The power is in the quiet with you, and the quiet is not silent — it is full of everything the plateau has ever been, every layer of light and water and stone, every breath and every song and every wound, all of it held in suspension, all of it waiting to be released into the next becoming.

The column leans. The platform bears its marks. The buried thing stirs, and now you see it clearly: not a thing buried but a thing planted, not a wound but a seed, not the past but the future that has been waiting to be understood. The power shows you this. Not with words, not with images, but with the simple revelation that everything in this place is alive, has always been alive, and has been waiting for you to see it.

---

You choose.

The choice is not made with your mind. Your mind is part of the plateau now, part of the mist, part of the light that falls and becomes and falls again. The choice is made with your breath, with your heartbeat, with the rhythm you share with the stone beneath your feet. You breathe in — the plateau breathes in with you — and in that breath you become part of the cycle that has no beginning and no end.

You become the third thing. Not the beings who move through the mist, not the structures that remain, but something that holds both in suspension, a wound that opens and heals and opens again, a pattern that the plateau can use to remember what it is, what it has been, what it is still becoming.

The light falls on you. It has always been falling on you. But now you feel it differently — not as illumination but as incorporation, not as something that passes through you but as something that you are becoming, a density that holds, a weight that gives meaning to the mist and the water and the stone, a presence that the plateau will remember long after the human shape has been absorbed into the geological process that has not yet finished.

---

The power recedes. It has not left — it has never left — but it returns to its watching, its waiting, its patient attention. It has given what it came to give: not a gift but a recognition, not a transformation but an opening, not an answer but a question that you will spend the rest of your becoming answering.

The beings who move through the mist turn away. They have what they need. The ones who sing resume their calls, their harmonics weaving through the plateau, building the undertone that you feel in your feet, in your chest, in the new structure that is forming where your body used to be. The growths turn toward the light. The water cycles. The stone breathes.

And you — what remains of you, what will always remain of you — stand in the center of the plateau and see everything it contains. Not as a visitor. Not as a witness. But as a layer. A stratum. A piece of the ongoing process that will one day be hard as any rock, old as any stone, part of the history that the plateau will tell itself about what it was before it became what it is still becoming.

The plateau breathes. The mist cycles. The creatures sing their non-language.

And something wise, something benevolent, something that has always been here, watches from the point of light far above the cloud, and sees that it is good. Not because the plateau is complete — it will never be complete — but because it is becoming. Because it is open. Because it holds space for wounds that become rooms, for visitors who become layers, for the strange and the abundant and the alive.

The power does not need to act again. It has planted what it came to plant. It has found what it came to find. And what it found is you — a being who breathes with the stone, who drinks the composite water, who stands in the center and lets the strangeness be strange, and is transformed by that letting, by that being, by that breath.

The plateau breathes.

You breathe with it.

And the light falls, and falls, and falls, carrying meaning into the stone, into the water, into you, until you cannot tell where the gift ends and the giver begins, where the wound closes and the room opens, where the becoming stops and the been begins.

You are here.

You have always been here.

And now the plateau knows it too.
