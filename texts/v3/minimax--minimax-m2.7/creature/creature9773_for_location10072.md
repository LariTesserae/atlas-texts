There is no inhabitant. That is the first thing that must be understood, and the last. What lives here is not something that lives *in* the place — the place is alive the way a thought is alive: as a process, a motion, a thing that continues only as long as it continues, and that continues because continuing is what it does. You are not standing in something. You are standing in a verb that has become a noun. You are standing in a process so long established that process and result have collapsed into each other, and there is no longer any way to tell where the doing ends and the having-done begins.

What lives here is the relationship.

The moisture that rises and falls, the growth that builds and dies and is reabsorbed, the warmth that maintains itself through its own production — these are not components of the system. They are the system. Each term implies the others, and the implication is circular, closed, a loop with no gap for anything else to enter. The living mass is the environment is the living mass. The warmth preserves the moisture preserves the growth preserves the warmth. You could trace any thread through the fabric and find it connected to every other thread, and the connection is not metaphor, not analogy, but identity. This is a world that has achieved a single sustained note and has never stopped holding it.

The luminescence is not life, or rather, it is no more or less life than anything else here. It does not carry information. It does not mark time. It simply moves, and the movement is the same kind of movement as sap rising, as humidity condensing, as the slow rotation of a cycle that has no origin. Light here is not an event. It is a condition — a permanent state of the interior, like the warmth, like the wetness, like the pressure of air that has been respired and cycled and respired again until it carries the exact signature of the body that made it. When you stand in the amber glow of those slow pulses, you are not seeing the world light up. You are seeing the world *be itself*, the way a heartbeat is not a signal but a function.

There is something that persists here, and persistence is the only word for it. Not survival — survival implies the possibility of not surviving. Not continuity — continuity implies a thread that could be cut. This is something stronger than both. This is a system that has passed beyond the need for time, not by escaping time but by making time irrelevant. The cycles repeat not because they were established and must be maintained but because there is no alternative state. The moisture does not move in these patterns because other patterns were tried and rejected. It moves in these patterns because these patterns *are* the system, and the system *is* this movement, and the system is the only thing that is. You are standing in a world that has no future because the future would be the same as the present, and you are standing in a world that has no past because the past is still happening, has never stopped happening, is the thing that is happening now.

This is what lives here: completion. Not the completion of a process but the completion of a mode of being. A way of existing that has solved for all variables and found the solution to be stable, and so has stopped solving. The growth builds. The growth dies. The growth is reabsorbed. These are not events that happen *to* the system. They are the system doing what the system does, and what the system does is the only thing the system can do, and so the system continues in the way a wave continues — not because it chose to, but because continuing is what a wave is.

The silence is not the absence of sound. It is the presence of a system so complete that there is nothing left to say. No warning to issue. No call to make. No signal to broadcast into the void. The void is not there. The void was filled, long ago, and has been full ever since, and fullness is another word for this place, another synonym for the thing that lives here — a thing so dense with itself that there is no space left for anything else to be.

You are standing in a world that is finished building and has not stopped building. You are standing in a body that breathes but does not sigh. You are standing in a system so perfectly maintained that maintenance has become existence, and existence has become maintenance, and the two words describe the same motion, the same endless repetition of itself, the same turning and returning and turning again in a circle that has become a home.

This is what lives here: the house that builds itself and lives in itself and has no door.
