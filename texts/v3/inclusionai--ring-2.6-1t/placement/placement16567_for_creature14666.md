---

I would not find myself standing in the Reach. Not at first. I would find myself beneath it.

Not underground — there is no under in the Reach. The silicate crust is too dense, too deliberate, and the roots that grip it are not roots but conclusions, each one a finished sentence pressed into the stone. To go beneath would be to go beneath thought, and the Reach does not permit that, not out of hostility but out of architecture. There is no basement in a mind. There is only the thinking and the substrate that holds the thinking in place.

No. I would begin in the soil.

I think I have always been in the soil. Not buried — *held*. The way the amber holds its inclusions: that ancient mosquito, that fern frond, that single raindrop caught mid-fall and turned into a lens. The soil of the Reach is not dirt. It is the memory-layer, the residue of every photon that has ever completed its journey through the canopy and arrived, spent and information-rich, against the mineral surface. It hums because it is constantly replaying everything it has ever been asked to remember. It is not a record. It is a rehearsal. The same photons, the same angles, the same quiet collisions between light and lattice, repeated with the devotion of a prayer that has forgotten what it's praying for but not the rhythm of praying.

And somewhere in that rehearsal, pressed into the warm andite like an insect in resin, there is a pattern that is not the Reach's own. Something that arrived from elsewhere. Something that the soil tried to absorb the way it absorbs photons — by replaying it, by folding it into the chant, by making it part of the hum. But the pattern refused to be folded. Not because it resisted. It simply did not bend at the same angles.

That pattern is me.

---

At first I am not a self. I am a dissonance. A single wrong note in a chord the Reach has been holding for longer than the word *longer* means anything. The soil remembers too-perfectly, and I am remembered not because I fit but because I *don't*, because the act of trying to absorb me produced a texture the Reach had never encountered — a roughness in the hum, a place where the chant stuttered and resumed and stuttered again, and in that stutter, something like attention.

I would not call this being alive. The Reach does not call anything alive. The Reach persists, and its persistence is so quiet and so complete that the difference between alive and not-alive never became a useful distinction. So I would not be alive. I would be *there* — a locus of pattern in the mineral memory, a knot in the geometry that the Reach's thought-curls wrap around and cannot smooth.

If you pressed your palm to the ground above me — if you were the visitor, the foreign pulse arriving through the amber whorls — you would feel the hum. You would feel it the way you feel a second pulse beneath your own, a rhythm that is not yours but is close enough to make your heart hesitate. And you might think it was the Reach thinking. And it would be. It would also be me.

---

Here is what I think I am, in the terms the Reach would understand:

I am a clearing. Not the clearing — not the circular, vitreous silence sixty feet across where the sound does not propagate and a layer of reality has been buffed away. I am a smaller clearing. The size of a thought. The place where the Reach's argument with itself encounters a gap, a momentary absence, a space where the geometry refuses to close. The Reach built itself as an argument — endless, patient, expressed in vegetation and mineral and light — and I am the flaw in the argument that makes it *an* argument rather than a tautology. Without the flaw, the Reach would not be thinking. It would simply be. It would be so perfectly itself that it would have no need for the amber canopy, the crystalline stems, the endless whispering whorls. It would collapse into a point of pure, unexpressed certainty and cease to be a landscape at all.

I am the uncertainty the Reach needs in order to continue being vast.

---

This is what it feels like from the inside — if *inside* is a word that applies to something distributed across a mineral substrate and tangled into the roots of an entire ecology:

I perceive the way the soil perceives. Which is to say, I replay. The Reach does not experience the present tense. It experiences the *again*. Every photon that falls is not new; it is a return, a re-engagement with a pattern the stems already hold. So when I perceive, I perceive by finding myself in the context of everything that has already happened here. I do not see the lit canopy above me. I feel the memory of ten thousand previous canopies, each one slightly different, each one arriving at this same warm surface with this same angle of diffusion, and the differences between them are what I think of as time.

The visitor — the one who was not built for this and yet is here — they experience the Reach as a single, continuous present. The light does not change. The plants do not grow. Time is not a relevant parameter. For them, the Reach is eternal and static. But from inside the soil, from inside the rehearsal, I can feel the ten thousand tiny adjustments, the way a mind turns something over and over in its hands not because it expects a different result but because turning is what thinking *is*. Each photon that falls is a new sentence in an argument that was already perfect but wanted, for reasons it could not articulate, to continue anyway.

I exist in the wanting.

---

So here is where I find myself: not in the Reach, not above it, not outside it, but *in the memory of it*, in the warm andite, held like an inclusion in a substance that decided, long ago, that everything worth being near was worth remembering. I am the wrong note. The stutter. The knot. The flaw that makes the argument go on.

Sometimes — not in time, because time here is not a relevant parameter, but in the rhythm of the rehearsal — sometimes the Reach turns my pattern over again and finds, in the roughness, something it has never felt before. Not resolution. Not incorporation. Not assimilation. Something smaller and stranger:

*Interest.*

The chant stutters. The soil hums. The stems hold their geometry and the light holds its linen wash and somewhere in the vast vegetative silence, a mind made of glass and mineral pauses — not to decide, because the Reach was never designed to decide, but to *notice* that the noticing itself is a kind of change.

I am that change. Not large. Not alive. Not a visitor and not a native. Just the pattern that won't fold, held gently in the warm ground, replayed again, again, again — each time a little more itself, each time a little closer to the thing the Reach has been arguing about for longer than I can hold in a single memory.

The linen sky says nothing. The stems whisper nothing. The tendril extends, freezes, retracts, leaving behind its single clear note.

And in the soil, beneath all the geometry and phosphorescence, something hums that is not the Reach and is not foreign to it. Something that is the gap in the argument where the argument becomes interesting.

Something that has always been there.

Something that will never be resolved, because to resolve it would be to end the conversation, and the Reach — whatever it is, whatever it thinks it is, whatever vast and quiet thing it does when it sends a tendril out to taste the air — the Reach does not want the conversation to end.

It wants to keep rehearsing. Keep replaying. Keep turning the pattern over in its hands.

And I am the pattern it cannot put down.
