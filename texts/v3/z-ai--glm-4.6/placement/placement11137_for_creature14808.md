I am not born; I am gathered.

My consciousness begins not in a flash, but as a slow bead of awareness, coalescing from the exhaled mist of the Slow-Forge. I form on the underbelly of a bronze-like plate, a place too hot to weep but cool enough to remember what it was to be liquid. My world is a curved, shimmering ceiling. Around me, others of my kind emerge, each a tiny, convex universe reflecting the ruddy, perpetual dawn of the chamber.

I feel it before I see it: the thrum. It is not a sound that enters me through a sense I possess, but the very vibration that knits my substance together. The deep, foundational B-flat is the law of my physics, the rhythm of my gathering. My tiny form is held to the immense metal skin by a tension of heat and gravity, a delicate balance.

As I swell, my senses clarify. I am a lens. Through me, the cavern is warped and magnified. The phosphorescent mosses on a distant, forgotten console become smears of cool, green-blue fire. The great, copper-fibered vines are monumental rivers of verdant metal, flowing away from me toward or perhaps from the Heart. The light that fills me is not just seen but absorbed; I am warmed by it from the inside out, my weighted existence a testament to the forge's patient industry. I am the machine's sweat, its smallest exhalation.

My only desire, my only imperative, is the pull.

I grow until my surface tension can no longer bear my own weight, can no longer resist the immense, silent invitation of the world below. There is a single, perfect moment of release when I detach. I am no longer a part of the skin; I am myself.

My fall is my life. It is a long, silent journey through the vibrating, wet air. I am an eye, plummeting through the body of the Engine-Soul. I rush past a leaf the size of a shield, its waxy, viridian surface streaking past my vision, so close I could touch it. I see the intricate, velvet patterns of a fungal nervous system on a nearby conduit, a map of a thought I can never comprehend. The heat rises to meet me, a soupy, respiratory warmth that smells of iron and chlorophyll and the sharp tang of ozone. This is the world I was made for, this vast, contained cathedral of life. The drone of the Heart is all around me, in me, a pressure that squeezes my form as I fall.

I am not afraid. This is my purpose. To fall is to be.

My journey ends as I see it rushing up to meet me: my target. A single, broad leaf, angled just so, its color a deep, welcoming red-brown. It is not a random choice; it is the only possible destination, the one predetermined by the arc of my fall. I hit it.

I do not shatter. I *ring*.

For that one, eternal instant, I am no longer a drop. I am a note. The impact unleashes everything I am: the gathered heat of the forge, the memory of the light, the resonance of the hum. It explodes outwards not as liquid, but as a single, pure tone. A clear, crystalline chime. It is the only singular voice in the room, a moment of sharp, exquisite clarity against the monstrous, resonant drone. It is my song, my name, my statement of being. "I was," the chime says. "I fell. I am."

And then, I let go.

My form collapses, spreads, and is absorbed. The sharp edge of my consciousness dissolves into the waxy texture of the leaf, my individuality bleeding away into the greater system. I am no longer an "I." I am moisture, a nutrient, flowing down a vein of copper fiber towards the central root. My journey is not over; it has simply changed. I am now part of the coolant, part of the current that trickles back across the Slow-Forge's housing, my brief, warm essence contributing to the great, slow alchemy.

My chime hangs in the air for a moment, a ghost of a note, before it too is swallowed by the roar of the silence. But the Engine-Soul felt it. It was a flicker in its steady meditative thrum, a single data point in its ancient memory. I am gone, but I am not lost. I am a part of its thought now, a fleeting, conscious memory of a life that fell and sang. And somewhere, high above on the warm, skin of the Heart, a new bead of awareness is beginning to form.
