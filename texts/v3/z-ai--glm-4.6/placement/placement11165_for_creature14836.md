I do not live *in* a wall. I live *with* a wall.

The others think their dwellings are carved, hollowed out, made. They see a distinction: here is the stone, and here is the home. They are wrong. I do not carve. I merge. The pale jade of the moss fronds is not just something I tend; it is a part of me, a symbiotic lantern that lives in the shallow layers of my flesh, and in return for the home I provide, it lends me its light.
