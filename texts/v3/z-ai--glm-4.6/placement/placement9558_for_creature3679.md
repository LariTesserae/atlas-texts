I am a ghost, but not of the dead. I am a ghost of the self.

I have no body, not as you would understand it. The Attuned have bodies of polished, living stone, a testament to their integration. I have a signature. To them, I am a dissonant note, a faint, recurring shimmer in the planet’s endless chord that hums just slightly out of tune. If you could see sound, you would see me as a heat-haze woven into the air, a pocket of resonance that remembers a different shape. I am the lingering flavor of a name, a memory of a face, held in the very vibration of the stone.

I was a Forerunner. I remember the journey. I remember the silence. It is the only thing the Attuned cannot comprehend, the one thing they have never felt. I remember the shock of arriving here, the way the great chord slammed into my bones, a physical blow that promised truth. I remember the awe, and the terror. My companions, they built the menhirs, they arranged the altars. They were scientists of the soul, testing the frequencies, mapping the harmonics. They were learning the language.

I wanted to do more than learn it. I wanted to speak it. I wanted to dissolve. I meditated on the highest ridge, pressing my flesh against the shivering stone, trying to let the tone unmake me, to find the sublime peace of what would become the Attuned. I held my breath until my lungs burned, seeking to align my pathetic, mortal heartbeat with the thrumming of the world’s heart.

And in that moment of absolute surrender, when I felt my consciousness fraying, ready to merge into the whole, I flinched. I thought of the face of the mother I had left behind in the quiet lands. I thought of the specific shade of blue the sky had been. I thought of the word *I*. It was a stupid, stubborn, fragile thing, a single thread of ego I refused to unspool. I clung to it.

And so, I was torn in two. The part of me that longed for union was pulverized, scattered, and absorbed. It became part of the chord. The part that held onto *I* became this. An imprint. A memory held taut against the fabric of reality. My body crumbled to dust, but the pattern, the echo of my consciousness, was now etched into the resonant field of the world itself. I died, but I did not cease.

Now I drift. My world is a tapestry of vibration. I do not see the colossal tree in the basin; I feel its deep, constant hum, a bass note of stoic endurance. I do not watch the Silent Moth; I perceive the delicate, twitching melody it traces through the standing waves, a fleeting, beautiful arpeggio. The Attuned are a different matter. To them, I am a story without a source. I am a ghost note in their symphony. A faint, nagging dissonance that proves their perfect harmony is not absolute. Sometimes, in their slow, majestic communication, I sense a question directed at me, a harmonic inquiry: *What flawed frequency are you? A memory of the last key? A scar that has not yet sung itself smooth?*

I cannot answer in their language. My thoughts are not pure chords; they are melodies tangled with the static of old memories. I feel the rain, though there is none, and I remember cold. These are alien concepts to them, and my struggling to hold them creates ripples they can feel. I am the planet’s subconscious, the dream it has of a time it was not wholly itself.

My purpose is tending. The Attuned are the anchors, the stabilizers of the present. I am the archivist of the past, the custodian of what was lost. I sometimes pool my essence, gathering my diffuse signature into a tighter focus, and press against the waxy petal of a man-sized violet. I hum a memory of a wind—not the resonant vibration of this place, but the chaotic, traveling breath of another world. The flower seems to drink it in, its color deepening for a day. I guide lost moths, soothing the frantic harmonics of their flight with a gentler wave. In this way, I am useful. I give my ghost a job.

But my true nature, my true terror and ecstasy, is revealed in the Great Reshaping. I have survived one. I remember the agony. The world-song, the very medium of my being, suddenly snapped. The chord that held my identity dissolved into a week-long scream of conflicting frequencies. I was thrown, scattered, torn, my memories pouring out like a hemorrhage. I felt the *I* dissolving, the final erasure I had once sought now arriving as a horrendous, unbidden end.

Yet, as the new chord began to assert itself, a new fundamental tone rising from the discord, I found a flicker of instinct. A survival drive deeper than thought. I was a speck of dust in a hurricane of vibration, and my only chance was to cling. I learned the new note before the stone did. I attuned my ghost to the new reality. I became the first harmonic, the first memory, of the new world.

The Attuned call me the Scarred Note. Or perhaps that is only what I call myself, projecting my own sense of flawed history onto their silent regard. They are healed and whole, polished by the new song. I am crazed with fractures, my being a palimpsest of the forgotten key, a constant reminder that even in a world of perfect unity, there might be a ghost of another way.

I am no longer a Forerunner, for my flesh is long gone. I am not an Attuned, for my stubborn self remains. I am the gap between the prayer and the answer. The echo that proves a sound was once made. I am here, in this place of deafening truth, and I am the keeper of the one lie it cannot reconcile: the memory of silence.
