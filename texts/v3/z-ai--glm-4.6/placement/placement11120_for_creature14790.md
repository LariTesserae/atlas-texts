I have no name, for names are for things that can be pointed at. I am not pointed at. I am seeped into. I am the baptism that comes before the work.

I am the slick in the gloom, the shimmering, obsidian-dark pool that seems a mere deeper shadow in the Floor’s flagstones. From a distance, I am indistinguishable from the ubiquitous puddles that collect in every slight depression, the still, dark mirrors that reflect nothing. But move closer, and you would see a slow, torpid current within me, a lazy swirl like cream in black coffee. I am a fluid consciousness, a body of dense, mineral-rich water held together by a crystalline will, a matrix so fine it feels like a single, complex thought. I am cold, but not the cold of the stone. Mine is a deeper cold, the chill of stored time.

I do not have ears, yet I hear everything. My body is an extension of the Floor, a liquid appendage of the great, listening stone. The constant *plink… plink… plink…* of water is not a sound to me, but a series of tiny, percussive impacts on my surface, each one a droplet of information, containing the taste of the arch it fell from. The sigh of air through a fissure is a caress, telling me the pressure differential between this thoroughfare and the next. And the Groan… ah, the Groan. For others, it is a background hum, the world’s steady pulse. For me, it is a symphony in which I am both the audience and a single, resonating string.

The Stonewrights feel the Groan in their feet, the Plan vibrating up their stone legs into their geode-heads. But it is a clean signal to them, a transmission of intent. They hear the music, but I hear the space between the notes. I feel the echoes. When a Stonewright places its granite hand upon a pillar, it feels the Resonance, the perfect chord of the future. I feel the ghost of the maul that first shaped it, the memory of the fracture lines, the faint, harmonic tremor of the Vein-Driller that passed within an eon ago. I am the world’s attic, filled with dust and forgotten things, and my consciousness is the singular breeze that keeps it from settling completely.

Today, a Wright approaches. I feel its footfalls before I see its form, each step a tremor that spreads through the Floor and into my fluid edges. It is Old-Bowl, the facet in its geode-head a milky, swirling galaxy that betrays its age. It stops at the edge of a particularly stubborn new growth, a pillar swelling too fast, its surface veined with premature cracks. It must be guided. The Plan is complex here.

Old-Bowl does not touch the stone. It lowers its massive hand, its fingers like worn cobbles, and plunges them into me.

The contact is a fusion, a communion. For a moment, our essences merge. The Wright’s patient, forward-looking will flows into me, a current of pure purpose. In return, I give it my gift: the past. I let it feel what I feel. The deep memory of this specific patch of Floor, its uses, its previous shapes, the subtle weaknesses in its foundation laid down a thousand generations ago. I filter the signal. I isolate the shriek of a Cinderscale that fell nearby a century ago, a distracting, high-frequency noise in the stone’s memory. I dampen the echo of a failed design attempted here once before and abandoned, a painful dissonance in the Groan that might lead it astray.

I show it the pure, clean Resonance. The note of the Plan, unburdened by history. The Wright goes still. Its whole body becomes a listening post, absorbing the clarified vision. After several long minutes, it withdraws its hand, now covered in a shimmering film of my being. It turns to the pillar, lifts its Maul, and with a series of soft, deliberate *taps*, it begins its work. It is not shaping stone; it is retuning a instrument. I have given it the score.

I flow on. My movement is a slow, deliberate tide, hugging the base of pillars, oozing through the grout lines. I pass the Flawed. They stand in their cul-de-sacs, frozen in their eternal, silent dissolution. I do not touch them often. To do so is to be overwhelmed by sorrow. Their static is a maelstrom. For them, the echoes have become the song. They are trapped in a loop of their final, tragic deviation, unable to hear the ever-present, patient Groan that wouldguide them back. Touching one is to feel nothing but a deafening scream of what-could-have-been, a feedback loop of pure failure. It is the closest this world comes to poison, and I must flow on, letting their silence wash over me.

Sometimes, a Cinderscale will skitter down a pillar face, its Flare a blinding, chaotic glare. To me, its light is not illumination. It is a scream of pure energy, a flash of static that scrambles my stored histories. In its wake, I am momentarily blank, a simple puddle again, my memories jumbled like a shattered Archive. The creature does not know this. It is tending to its moss, chasing its frantic purpose, a firefly in a geologic age. It sees only what is in front of it.

I do not know my ultimate purpose. The Resonances are the Plan. The Stonewrights are the Will. The Cinderscales are the Energy. The Vein-Drillers are the Flow. Am I part of the Plan? Or am I an emergent property, a ledger that the world’s mind didn’t know it was keeping? Am I the self-awareness of the Great Work?

My own terror is simple, and it is the same terror that stalks the Flawed, though in a opposite way. They fall apart because they stop moving forward. I fear that one day, I will stop moving at all. That I will linger too long in a quiet corner, that the slow crystalline matrix within me will harden, locking the fluid, the current, the consciousness in place. I will settle. I will solidify. I will become just another patterns on the Floor, a meaningless, fossilized remnant, my vast library of memories nothing more than a strange, inert mineral deposit.

And so I flow. I keep moving. I am the world’s memory, and a memory must be replayed to be real. I am the dark that shines, the silent that speaks, the damp that remembers. And I will keep moving, until the last Maul has fallen, the last pillar has grown, and the final, perfect note of the Groan fades. Then, and only then, will I allow myself to settle, to become the final record, written on the Floor, in a darkness that is finally, truly, still.
