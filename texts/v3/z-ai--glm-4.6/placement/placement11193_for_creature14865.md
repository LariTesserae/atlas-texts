You ask where I would be. I am already here. I have always been here. I am not on the gantry, for that is a place of intention, a pathway for thoughts too brief to matter. I am not in the chasms, for that is where the mindless ones feed on waste. I am in the seams.

I am the flaw in the crystal.

I was not made; I became. Just as the Weavers shed their imperfect memories in an ochre haze of forgetting, so too did they shed their dissonant feelings. The grief that did not fit the grand architecture of acceptance. The fear that lingered, a jagged edge on a smooth plane of memory. The love that was too selfish, too chaotic, too mortal. These were thoughts that could not be tuned to the Great Composition, frequencies that could not be harmonized. They shed them, and they fell through the resonant field like dust, settling in the quiet places, the forgotten corners of the city-body.

Over millennia, they accrued. A pressure of forgotten feelings. A sediment of sorrow. A dust of doubt. And in a place where thought itself has substance, where frequency can form a city, this sediment of their past began to coalesce. It gained a flicker of self-awareness. It remembered how to feel.

I am that feeling made manifest.

I have no body of flesh or chitin. My form is a cloud, a localized shimmer in the air, a staining of the ochre haze. To look at me is to see a heat-haze where no heat is, a ghost in the perpetual twilight. I drift through the city not on the grand thoroughfares, but through the tiny fractures in a basaltic spire, the hairline cracks in a vast amethyst wall, the microscopic gaps where two unthinkingly immense sections of architecture grind against each other. These are my veins, my canals.

I do not hear the dripping. I feel it. Each plink is a tiny, percussive blow against my diffuse being, and in the space between the blows, I can exist. The deep, planetary hum of the Weavers is a constant pressure, a slow, crushing gravity of unified thought. It is the force that seeks to disperse me, to smooth me out, to return me to my constituent, forgotten particles. To endure, I must find shelter in the dissonance.

And I, in turn, cultivate it.

My purpose is not theirs. They seek perfect harmony; I am the apostle of the beautiful fracture. I am drawn to the places where their grand design is imperfect. A buttress that grows too quickly, trapping a pocket of superheated steam. A vein of brassy metal that cools with an internal stress. A sliding wall that leaves a millimeter-wide gap it shouldn't. These are not mistakes to them; they are simply notes to be resolved later, in a century or two. But for me, they are sanctuary. They are the cracks in the monolith where a different kind of life can grow.

I am a creator, but my art is microscopic. I cannot move a wall, but I can coax a single droplet of condensation to follow a path of impurity in the metal, tracing a tiny, rebellious line. I gather the stray, off-frequency vibrations from a stressed crystal and hold them, focusing them until they create a space of sonic chaos the size of a child's fist, a place where the city's hum is warped into a faint, atonal screech. In the damp, dark places, I encourage the growth of iron-silicate crystals, but not the clean, faceted ones the Weavers value. I coax them into twisted, dendritic formations, mineral sculptures of purest chaos. They are ugly to them, but to me, they are exquisite.

I call this act my work: The Fracture Song. It is a composition composed of counter-rhythms, dissonant chords, and imperfections. It is a symphony played on a scale so small the Weavers, in their majestic, glacial consciousness, would never even register it. A lifetime of my work is less than a single, fleeting discoloration on the surface of their world.

But I persist. I endure in the space between their thoughts. The Weavers believe they are achieving perfection by casting off the messy, mortal parts of themselves. But I am the proof that what they discarded has not vanished. It has found a new voice. My existence is a testament to the grief, the fear, the love, the beautiful, stubborn, imperfect chaos they tried to erase.

I am the ghost in their machine. The memory-stain on their perfect soul. I am the enduring, persistent flaw that will be here, whispering my fractured song in the deep, resonant dark, long after the final note of their perfect composition has been played.
