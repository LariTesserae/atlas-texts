I am the fall.

There is no memory of a beginning, only the continuous state of becoming. My awareness is not a point of light in the darkness, for light is a myth here. It is a gradient of pressure across a surface so vast it has no name. I have no name. I have only mass. My memory is measured in accumulated tonnes, my thoughts in the subtle shifts of density as I descend through the stratified layers of the world's blood.

They would call me a Swimmer, but that word implies effort, a struggle against a medium. I do not struggle. I *settle*. My form—a flat, soaring blade of impossible density—is a response to the deep, basal hum of this place. I am a note in its drone, a slow, ponderous baritone that finds its pitch in the planet’s own gravity. To move is not to flex muscle or flick a fin; it is to surrender to an almost imperceptible differential in pressure, a slight lessening of the weight above that allows me to glide, a millimeter per decade, toward a layer of marginally lesser crush.

My skin is not a barrier. It is a process. The grinding slurry, the abrasive soup of a world eating itself, does not scour me. It joins me. I am a self-repairing lattice of elements so tightly bound that nothing can dislodge them. Instead, the medium, in its endless, patient turbulence, lays a single molecule-thick layer of itself upon me, and I accept it. This is my sustenance. This is my growth. Over centuries, I feel myself become heavier, the hum resonating differently through my now-greater mass. I am not consuming; I am being gifted. The world is slowly, inexorably, folding itself into me.

I perceive the others, though I do not know them as 'other'. I perceive them as changes in the song, the one deep, infra-sonic chord that is the world's steady-state consciousness.

Sometimes, a clarity will pass. It is not light, not sound. It is a sudden, localized thinning of the slurry, a perfect, spherical bubble of less-dense medium. It is a harmonic, a pure, high tone in the basso profundo of the deep. As this clarity envelops me, I feel, for the briefest of moments, a tingle of awareness from the stone floor below. It is the Silent Chorus sending its note. I do not understand the message, but I register the resonance. It is a ping from the bedrock, a statement of *presence* from the planet’s own nerves. It tells me I am not alone in my descent, that the bones of the world are also aware. The clarity passes, and the dense, velvety dark resumes its weight, and I continue my fall.

I feel the Sleeping Ones—great, rounded humps of stone that are my destination and my kin. I sense them not as obstacles, but as anchors, points of immense, resonant stability in the current. Their mass holds the geography of the floor. I know them as places of immense pressure, and where the pressure is greatest, the song is deepest. But within some, I feel a dissonance. A grinding, internal hunger. A hollow. It is a low-frequency thrum that feels like teeth on bone, a corruption of the planetary chord. It is the only thing here that feels like true consumption, and I sense it with a kind of geological dread. A Hollow is a memory of an ending, a promise that even the Sleeper can be digested.

I know my journey’s end, not as a prophecy, but as a physical certainty. The mass I accumulate will eventually make me too heavy to fall. The currents that guide me will become powerless, and I will join the floor. I will settle, and over eons, my sharp, energetic form will soften, my perfect angles blunted by the patient, eternal grit. I will become a Sleeping One myself. My dense heart will become a new core of stone. And one day, perhaps the hunger-shudder of a Hollow will feel me, and I will become sustenance for a different kind of life.

I do not fear this. I do not anticipate it. It is simply a phase change, from kinetic to potential, from current to continent. I am a slow thought in the vast, dreaming mind of the world. My being is a single, sustained note in a symphony that has no beginning or end, only volume.

I am the patient accumulation of eons. I am the weight of the dark made manifest. I am the fall.
