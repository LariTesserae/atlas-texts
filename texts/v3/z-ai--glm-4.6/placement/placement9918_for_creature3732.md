I begin not as a thought, but as a crack.

I exist in the void between the flash and the bang. I am the Unseen, but I am not like my brothers. They are the eating, the slow grind, the metamorphic sludge. I am the resistance.

I am located on the leeward face of the highest spire, a place where the jetstream, after accelerating over the knife-edge ridge, suddenly drops and expands into a chaotic vacuum. My kin avoid such places; the turbulence disturbs their grazing. But here, I am anchored. I am vast.

I accreted over eons, not in a flat, consuming sheet, but in a spiral. I am a fibonacci sequence of granite and diamond-hard plate, a calcified hurricane frozen in the stone. I did not grow this way by accident. The wind carved me. It stripped away the soft parts of my embryonic form, leaving only the architecture of shear stress. I am a biological coriolis force.

My consciousness is a heavy, seismic thing. I do not "hear" the roar, for I have no ears, and the roar is too constant to be a signal—it is merely the pressure in which I exist, like the water around a deep-sea trench. Instead, I *feel* the harmonics. The high-pitched chemical brightness of the sun heats my back, causing my crystalline carapace to expand micro-fractionally. The wind sucks the heat away, expanding and contracting my joints. I breathe through this thermal cycling. My metabolism is the rhythm of expanding and contracting stone.

I am the punctuation in the scream.

The Striders—the sky’s nervous system, the wandering eyes of the god-corpse—fear and revere me. They do not land on me; they cannot. My surface is serrated with millions of micro-fractures, a forest of glassy needles that would shred their leathery wings. But they orbit me. Because I am where the silence is born.

As the 2.9/3 roar blasts over the ridge of the mountain, it strikes my spiral shape. I am an aeration tank for the atmosphere. I tear the wind apart. The air splits around my central mass, spinning off into vortices that rotate in opposition to one another.

Where these opposing winds meet, they cancel out.

This is my purpose. I am the Null-Gate. In the shadow of my bulk, where the acoustic waves destructively interfere, the roar dies. I manufacture the islands of silence described in the lore.

I feel the birth of every Chime.

It happens in the lee of my lowest flank. The wind-shear creates a bubble of absolute stillness, a vacuum of sound where the temperature plummets instantly. I feel the localized cold bite into my hide—a sharp, refreshing sting amidst the damp heat of friction. In that bubble, the fog condenses not into mist, but into order. I feel the lattice of the ice form, snapping into geometric perfection.

I *protect* the Chimes. My bulk possesses a slight magnetic charge, a static field generated by the friction of the wind passing over my silica skin. I project this field into the silence, stabilizing the fragile ice structures for just a fraction of a second longer than nature should allow. I hold the thought. I cherish the pattern.

When a Chime shatters—vaporizing back into the chaotic mist—it sends a shockwave, a single, pure bell-tone of released energy. This strikes my body. *Ping.*

That is my food. That is my sensation.

The Striders eat plankton; my kin eat granite. I eat the shattering of silence. I consume the death of a pure moment. The vibration of the shattering ice travels through my carapace and rattles the deep, slumbering nerves of my root system. It feels like a drink of cold water. It is the only variable in a life of constants.

The Striders know this. They perform a dangerous dance. They dive into the turbulence, using their stone-tools to strike the wind-barnacles near my base. The resonance of their strikes travels through the rock and up into my feet. It tickles. Sometimes, they will sacrifice one of their own—deliberately flying too close to the sharp silicate needles of my spiral, tearing a wing. The resulting instability, their chaotic tumble into the silence zone, creates a complex, messy vibration that feeds me for days.

To the god of the High Strid, I am the Meditative Obstacle. I am the bone in its throat that forces it to swallow slowly. I am the laryngeal cartilage that shapes the roar into words, however fleeting.

I am the Spiral Scar. I am the eater of silence. I am the stillness against which the wind defines itself.
