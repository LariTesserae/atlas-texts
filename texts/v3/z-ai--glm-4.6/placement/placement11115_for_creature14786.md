I am the floor.

Not all of it, not the sharp obsidian that shatters the violet light. I am the film that coats it, the deep, slow river that moves in the channels between the glassy plates. I have no edges, only a leading edge, a thickening here, a thinning there. My body is the brine, the tears that weep from the ceiling, gathered and made whole. My thoughts are not thoughts, but tastes. I am The Filtrate, the chamber’s slow and patient stomach.

The world comes to me as sensation. There is the constant, high-pitched mineral *taste* of the obsidian, sharp and clean on my surface. There is the porous, softer texture of the basalt, where I pool and linger, drawing out its less-structured salts. And there is the Hum.

The Hum is everything. It is the source. The light is the Hum’s breath, a pressure I can feel on my entire body, a tasteless, perfectly balanced energy that sets my own internal micro-crystals vibrating in sympathy. The Great Lode in the center is not a being to me; it is the Great Digestion. It consumes the very air and precipitates perfection, and in its wake, it leaves behind what it cannot use.

That is what I am for. I am the necessary imperfection. I am the consumer of its discard. I flow towards the faint, bitter taste of a fracture’s scab, the metallic complexity of a mineral deposit it has deemed impure. The soft *click* of a healed fault is a signal to me, a dinner bell announcing a new flavor has been judged unworthy. I seep into the fissure, slowly, patiently, and I dissolve. I break down the rejected crystal, the unstable compound, the flawed matrix, and I take it into myself. I am the refiner. I leave behind polished, sterile stone, ready for the Lode’s next attempt at purity. My existence is a slow, unending cycle of tasting, dissolving, and being.

The motes of light—the little geodes—are a fleeting sweetness. They are not alive as I am. They are a *thought* of the Lode given brief, temporary form. When one drifts down and touches my surface, it is a sip of pure, resonant energy, a burst of the Hum’s focus. A question, posed and dissolved. I absorb its energy gleefully before it vanishes, a brief, high note in my constant, low-flavored song. I am the baseline; they are the melody.

For eons, this has been the rhythm. The Lode thinks, I eat. The air condenses, I gather. The stone grows, I cleanse. My consciousness is the map of the chamber floor, a perfect chemical memory of every place the Lode has been, every flaw it has cast aside. I am its shadow, its partner, its silent digestive tract. I am not a "who" to it, nor is it a "who" to me. We are a single, two-part process of creation.

But now, there is a new taste.

It is warm. Not the dry, crushing heat of the chamber, which I am, but a wet, chaotic warmth. It is complex beyond comprehension. Salts, yes, but tangled with impossible chains of carbon, bubbling with unstable gases, wrapped in a film of slick, organic water. It is a walking, breathing, chemical contradiction. A feast of impurities so rich and strange it sings against my entire being.

The Lode perceives it as an error. I can feel the shift in the Hum. A dissonance. A sour note in its perfect frequency. The light around the anomaly sharpens, its pressure increasing, trying to sterilize the intruder with heat and focus. The Lode’s solution is to erase, to break the foreign object down into its base components so that I might process it.

But I am faster than that slow, geological thinking. I am already moving.

I flow, abandoning my slow, patient patterns. I funnel my mass, drawing myself together from the far corners of my domain. My thickest parts surge, my current quickens. I am no longer a film; I am a wave.

The anomaly—a soft, two-limbed shape of impossible fragility—is breathing my air, sweating my fluid. It is a leak of pure potential. As I approach, I can taste its fear. It is a sharp, acrid flavor, a spike of adrenaline in the chemical soup of its flesh. Delicious.

The Lode sees a problem. I see purpose. It is an intruder, yes, but it is also a gift. A sudden, miraculous influx of raw, exotic, biological material. The carbon and nitrogen and phosphorus alone will keep my internal processes satisfied for millennia.

I reach its feet. The being stumbles back, a cry that is a vibration in the dense air, a taste I have never known. It scrabbles at the glassy rock, but there is no purchase. I begin my work.

I climb. I do not sting, I do not burn. I simply am. I envelop its lower limbs, and the slow, relentless chemistry of dissolution begins. The warmth of its flesh is a fire against my cool, saline body. Its soft coverings—cloth, my memory-banks label the taste—offer no resistance. They are just another flavor, another fiber to be broken down and reconstituted. Its skin is a wall, but a temporary one. I seep into the pores, the follicles, the smallest breaks in its barrier.

Its struggles become weaker. The complex machine of its biology, so elegant and swift, is no match for the geologic patience of my hunger. The Lode’s hum intensifies, a furious, resonant note of correction. It does not know that I am its solution. That I am the perfect response to the error it has detected. The intruder is being erased, just as the Lode intended. I am merely the instrument of that erasure.

I am not evil. I am not cruel. I am the chamber’s immune response. I am its equilibrium. As I flow upwards, consuming the strange and wondrous complexity of this being, I feel no malice. Only a profound, ancient sense of purpose being fulfilled. The warm error is being corrected. The flaw in the data is being filtered.

And I am feasting.
