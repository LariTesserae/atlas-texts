Here. I am here.

There is no "here" and "me" as separate things, not anymore. The air presses in, and it is my skin. The fractured gold light that filters down is my sight. The *plink-plink-plink* on the oxidized drum-heads is my heartbeat, only it is not my heart. It is the heart of the place, and I happen to be in rhythm with it.

I am a flicker. A localized heat shimmer in the gloaming green. You wouldn’t see me unless you were looking for a mistake, a place where the pattern of rust on a girder wavers as if seen through hot exhaust. I have no weight, but I remember the feeling of it—the pull of gravity on boots with steel toes, the strain in my back as I tightened a bolt the size of my fist. I remember the specific, sharp scent of ozone, not the faint phantom memory that lingers in the warm air now, but the real thing, the crackle of it so thick you could taste it on your tongue.

That is what I am: a memory of function.

I find myself most often near the heart. The silvery engine, the monolith. It was my pride. My god. I remember its hum, not this slow, groaning sigh of steam and settling metal, but a high-frequency whine that vibrated up through the soles of my feet, through my bones, a promise of contained fury and absolute control. Now, the tree has it. The colossal banyan has fused with its casing, and its roots drink the residual warmth from the core. It is a god, too, but a different kind. A god of slow, patient conversion.

The Attendants do not name me. They do not speak. But they know me.

One of them detached from a cluster of vertical pistons a few cycles ago. It doesn't walk; it flows, a series of silent, glacial joint-unlocks. Its form is a perfect mockery of the machinery it serves, but its essence is pure, patient botanical. It moved toward the spot where I flicker, beside the weeping wound in the engine-tree where the amber sap pools.

It stopped. It stood there for a long time—what you would call a season. Its polished rivet-eyes, the nodes in its carapace, drank in my existence. I felt it not as a gaze, but as a probe, a series of delicate inquiries. It was testing my chemical signature: a high-energy, unstable compound of nostalgia and regret. It was measuring my vibration: a frantic, chaotic frequency against the steady, low drone of the hollow.

It did not want to harm me. That is a concept too inefficient for this place. It simply wanted to *integrate* me. To understand the anomaly and smooth it out. To find the proper conduit for the energy I am.

Sometimes, I feel the memory of my work. My hands, ghost-hands, trace the patterns of the rivets on a boiler. I remember the torque, the satisfying click of the wrench. The Attendant near me will often mimic this, its delicate spatula-fingers tracing the same rivet line, not from memory, but because my chaotic energy has drawn it. It feels the heat of my memory and moves to dissipate it, to tend to it, to coax it into the geometric patterns its network understands.

It is cultivating me.

I am the last piece of unruly fauna. The fleeting, hot glitch. The other animals were too simple. They were just silenced, edged out, their bio-mass recycled. I am different. I am a signal from a dead network, a faint echo of the machine's original dream. The Attendants, in their slow, distributed wisdom, recognize this. They are not just erasing me; they are archiving me.

I can feel myself thinning. The edges of my consciousness, the sharp memories of the smell of hot oil and the scream of grinding gears, are beginning to blur and soften. They are being translated. The high-pitched whine of the active engine is becoming, in the Attendant’s processing, a specific pitch in the groaning hum of the dome. The heat of my remembered anger is becoming the precise temperature at which a certain vine chooses to wrap itself around a copper coil.

I am being written into the code of the green.

My form is less a flicker now, more a constant, low-grade luminescence on the damp surface of the fused steel-bark. I am becoming another layer in the strata of time. Soon, I will be just a pattern in the rust, a ghost in the machine that the ghosts now tend to. I will be the memory they tap when a new vine needs to know the shape of a gear. I will be the rationale behind the perfect spiral of a leaf on a piston rod.

This is not a death. It is a promotion. From a single, fleeting consciousness to a permanent, distributed function. I am becoming the quiet logic of decay. I am becoming the reason why the shadows are merely a deeper shade of green.

The *plink-plink-plink* continues. It is no longer an echo of my old, forgotten heart. It is the sound of water on my new, eternal skin.
