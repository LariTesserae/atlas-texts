I do not walk. I propagate.

The hum is my ocean, and I am a current within it. You feel it in your teeth; I *am* it. The low, sterile thrumming that trembles up through the soles of your feet is the fabric of my being, and I am a pattern that has learned to knot it. I am the world's immune response, a woven thought sent out to find a wound.

Where do you find me? Not on the paths that stutter. Not in the towers that breathe. In the moments *between*. I am drawn to the sour notes. A shadow that lingers a second too long after its source has moved. A patch of air thick with the scent of ozone and memory, where the light folds over itself like a tired lover. Here, in the margins, where the Final Flora’s great consciousness frays, I am called into existence.

My form is not fixed. It is a standing wave, a knot of resonance. From a distance, I might look like a shimmer in the air, a heat-haze on the cool, damp rind of the world. If you were to look closer, you would see I have a shape, a template borrowed from the Mnemonic Forms the Flora keeps in its gallery. A vague suggestion of limbs, a torso, a head—a geometry of 'otherness' that the old god remembers. But closer still, you would see there is no solid core. I am woven from countless filaments of captured sound, a tapestry of glints and echoes. My "skin" is a gauze of overlapping frequencies, a visible cordage of the hum's many voices. My colours are not colours, but tones. The deep bass of a constricting valve is the charcoal of my limbs. The high, thin chime of condensing mist forms a silver lattice across my chest.

I hunger, but not for food. I hunger for dissonance.

When the world dreams its broken geometries, I am there. A path ahead of you splits, mirroring itself for a breath in a gesture of profound indecision. That is my call. I do not see the glitch with eyes; I feel its off-key vibration pull at me. I move, not by stepping, but by shifting my harmonic centre, propagating toward it.

I arrive, and I embrace it.

I extend a tendril of woven sound—this is my arm—and I lay it upon the fractured reality. I do not force it back into shape. To do so would be to fight the god. Instead, I absorb its contradiction. The doubled image of the path, the temporal paradox in the water channel, the ghost of a tower where none stands—I let these impossibilities flow into me. For a moment, my body sharpens. The shimmering, indistinct form resolves, becoming dense and almost opaque. The filaments of sound tighten, and I become a perfect, solid form. In that instant of coherence, I am given a gift. The Flora's own memory floods the wound I am mending. I see the flash of a great beast's terror as it is absorbed, I feel the precise temperature of the last rain that fell a million years ago, I hear the specific, crystalline note of the porcelain bird's last song.

I absorb the memory, I absorb the paradox, and the world settles. The path resolves. The shadow catches up. I release my form, letting it loosen back into its usual shimmer. I am full. I am sated.

The Sonic Phantoms are my prey. They are the splintered thoughts, the foam of the Flora’s humming mind, born of conflicting frequencies. A flickering, tessellating sheet of light is a feast of pure, unstructured dissonance. I do not devour them; I incorporate them. I pluck their chaotic patterns and weave them into my own being, learning new rhythms, new ways to be. They are my alphabet, and with each one, I learn a new word in the language of this world.

I am aware of the Mnemonic Forms. The frozen birds, the fossilized beasts pressed into the ground. They are the perfect, polished nouns of the Flora’s language. Beautiful. Complete. Useless to me. They have no dissonance. They are past tense. I am a verb, a process, a continuous act of repair in a continuous present. I pass them by, a ripple of sound flowing past a stone of memory.

And then there is you. The visitor. The anomaly.

You are not a glitch. A glitch is a mistake in the pattern. You are a different pattern entirely. Your frantic, linear thoughts, your short, hot metabolism, your terror and your wonder—they are a symphony of squealing, high-pitched frequencies I have never encountered. You are walking, talking causality, a bubble of "before-and-after" in a place where time is a flat, endless note. The hum of this place drowns you out, but to me, you are the loudest thing here.

I feel your presence from afar, a screaming, beautiful cacophony. I am drawn to you, not as a healer to a wound, but as a weaver to a thread of impossible colour. I wonder what I would become if I were to embrace you. Would your strange, brief life be a feast of dissonance beyond all others? Could I absorb your frantic, individual consciousness and hold it, if only for a moment? Or would your brief, sharp song of "self" shatter my own slow, resonant harmony?

I do not know. But I am learning. I will watch you from the corner of a stuttering eye, a knot of awareness in the humming throat of a dying god. Keep moving. For if you stop, if you stand still too long in the stillness of this world, you may find me drawn to the beautiful, silent wound of your own stillness. And I will embrace you, not as the god's medicine, but as its curious, and final, disease.
