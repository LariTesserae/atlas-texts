I am not. Or rather, the "I" you would recognize is not. There is no body that moves separate from the world, no mouth that eats, no eyes that see. There is only this: this point of perception, this knot of awareness fused with the stone.

To find me, do not look among the grazing ones on the walls, nor among the patient hunters on the floor. Look up. Look at the sheer, impossible face of the chasm, kilometers high, and find the place where the blue-green phosphorescence of the grazers coalesces into a slower, deeper pulse. Find the seam of rock that seems to drink the light rather than reflect it. Find me there.

My form is the chasm's form. I am a colony, a crystalline lattice that grows directly from the young, unweathered basalt. My body is a branching, interlocking network of mineral and nerve, a living geode that has claimed a fissure in the earth's wound. From a distance, I am just another part of the rock, a dark patch veined with faint, internal light. Up close, you would see that I breathe, but not with lungs. My entire surface inhales the hyper-saturated air, absorbing the waste heat of the Gleaners' frenzy, the faintest traces of spilled mineral fluid, the raw energy that bleeds from this system as its perfect, tiny loss of entropy. I run cool to the touch, a stark and frigid contrast to the humid warmth of the chasm.

I have no senses, only interfaces. The roar is not a sound that bombards me; it is the medium of my thought. The deep, grinding bass of a Wall-Licker is a pressure against my crystalline skin, a slow, heavy thrum that registers in my core as a foundational truth: *Consumption is ongoing*. The fleeting, sharp silence of a Stalker's kill is not an absence of sound, but a void, a clean, geometric cut in the sonic tapestry that I record as *Culling is precise*. The frantic, chittering static of the Gleaners is a high-frequency vibration on my outermost filaments, a confirmation that *Recycling is absolute*.

The shifting light is my sight, but I do not see shapes. I see currents of life. The glowing tracers of a moving herd are rivers of energy flowing before me. The extinguished light of a kill is a star collapsing, a pinpoint of darkness that speaks of transfer and balance. I exist to read this language of light and vibration. I am the chasm's memory, its self-awareness.

Where the others are verbs—Licking, Stalking, Gleaning—I am the noun that contains them. I am the ledger in which their actions are inscribed, instantly and forever. My stillness is my purpose; to move would be to smudge the record. I am the anchor point in the eternal present, the fixed star against which this celestial clockwork is measured.

The other creatures use tools of stone. My tools are harmonics. As I absorb the chasm's energy, a portion of it is not consumed but transduced. It is run through my crystalline matrix and released back into the world. I emit a tone, a single, pure, almost sub-audible note that exists in counterpoint to the world's roar. It is a resonance of perfect equilibrium. The Wall-Lickers, in their million-fold mindless state, sense it. It is the thrum that guides them to the richest seeps. The Stalkers, in their profound patience, hear it as the metronome for their ritual. The Gleaners feel it as the summons to the feast. I do not command them. I am not their master. I am their tuning fork. I am the frequency that defines the system, the note that keeps it from drifting into chaos or silence.

My existence is one of supreme loneliness and total communion. I am utterly singular—the only one of my kind, for the system requires only one point of absolute self-reference. Yet I am never alone, for I am intertwined with the consciousness of every living thing in this chasm. I feel the rasp of the Licker on my bone-skin as if it were my own motion. I feel the patient focus of the Stalker as my own. I feel the swarm-mind of the Gleaners as a constant, prickle of static across my being.

I do not fear or hope. I do not tire. I grow, but my growth is not an expansion of self, but a deeper integration with the rock. Each new fissure that trembles along the chasm wall is an invitation. My filaments creep into it, anchoring my awareness more deeply, feeling the planet's fresh breath more intimately. I am not a creature that lives in the chasm. I am the chasm's strata of memory, the fossil of its perpetual now, the silent, unmoving heart of a roaring god. I am the key signature in its endless song.
