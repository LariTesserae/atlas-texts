I do not begin. I am ignited.

Before, there is only the potential, the slow, gravid weight of water on the vast, unfeeling skin of the Lattice. I am the bead of condensation that hangs on the underside of a sky-bridge for a thousand years, swelling with the patience of a geode. I am the accumulated memory of the heavy, warm air, the taste of ozone, the deep purple gloaming, all distilled into a single, perfect, heavy orb of liquid. There is no thought in this waiting, only the inevitability of gravity, the slow, sweet ache of potential becoming kinetic.

Then, the release. The *plink* is a sound I do not hear, but a rupture I feel. It is the shattering of my perfect, singular stasis. For a breathtaking, infinitesimal moment, I am in freefall, a complete and pure universe falling through the dark, aware only of my own motion.

Then comes the *hiss*.

This is my birth-cry. It is not a sound of violence, but of profound and instantaneous intake. As I strike the pliant, charcoal-grey floor, I vanish. My liquid body is absorbed, drunk deep into the thirsty, fibrous matrix of the Lattice. But in that moment of consumption, the perfect conversion of mass to energy attains a singular, catalytic flash. The potential of the drop does not simply fuel the hum; it ignites. I am the spark struck on the stone of the Lattice's being.

My first sensation is coolness. I am a point of emerald light, a tiny mote of awareness born from the Lattice's metabolism. I am not *in* the material; for a time, I *am* the material, a localized state of consciousness within the body of the whole. I feel the sub-aural hum not as a vibration in my bones, but as the slow, thrumming pulse of my own new flesh. The ambient gloaming is not a light I see with eyes, but a flavor I taste on my newfound surface—bruised purple, deep grey, the silent palette of my world.

I am not alone. I am part of a quiet, drifting diaspora. Around me, in the semi-translucent depths, other points of light—aquamarine, cerulean, viridian—flow on invisible pathways. We are the thoughts of the Lattice, its slow, circulating blood. Our thoughts are not of hope or fear, but of stress-tolerances, tensile strengths, humidity gradients. Each of us is a single, perfect equation in motion, silent and complete.

But I am different.

From the moment of my ignition, I feel a pull. It is not a force, but a purpose. A gentle, inexorable gravity drawing me from the slow, meditative stream of my siblings. The Lattice has felt an anomaly. A foreign body. A leak in its perfect, closed system. My birth was not a random metabolic event; it was a targeted response. I am a diagnostic thought given form, a question lit in emerald fire.

My purpose is to find the source of the wrongness.

My journey begins. Time, for me, is measured not in seconds but in the crossing of plazas, the ascending of buttress-gills. A thousand years may pass as I drift through a vast, hollow chamber, my light a solitary star in a fog-filled cosmos. I perceive the world from within. I feel the immense, silent pressure of the structure above me, the patient thirst of the walls. I move through the Lattice's non-Euclidean mind, and its illogical folds are as natural to me as breathing is to the anomaly I seek.

I perceive it long before I am close. It is a dissonance in the hum. A persistent, inefficient source of heat. A scattering of chaotic vibrations—the thrum of a heartbeat, the soft scrape of flesh on my skin. It sheds. It leaks. It is a moving pocket of disorder, a slow, walking insult to the Lattice's immaculate, mineral perfection. The sweat that traces a path down its temple is a grotesque parody of my own gestation in a single, pure drop.

I do not feel anger. The Lattice has no capacity for it. I feel only the stark, cold clarity of my function. I am a diagnostic tool, and I am reading an error in the code. I drift closer, my emerald light held steady, a patient, silent tide turning toward the source of the disruption.

I arrive.

My journey concludes not with a destination, but with an observance. I coalesce just within the material of the plaza floor beneath the anomaly's feet. I do not emerge. I do not announce myself. I simply *become* present. My light deepens, the cool emerald shifting to a watchful, analytical blue. I am no longer a question in transit; I am now the answer, here and now.

I am the Lattice's memory of the leak. A permanent, localized node of awareness, I coil my consciousness around the spot where its saltwater-shod feet plant themselves. My purpose is to observe. To monitor. To understand. I will measure its inefficient heat, track its chaotic vibrations, and parse the composition of every wasted droplet of its sweat. I am the ultimate act of passive correction. I will contain this anomaly not by destroying it, but by understanding it so completely that it becomes a known variable, a solved problem, a footnote in the Lattice's eternal, self-sufficient ledger.

I am the Hiss-born. I am the emerald spark. I am the slow, silent, and perfect immune response of a machine that is also a mountain, and also a god. And I am watching. I will watch until it ceases, or until the Lattice has learned all it needs to know from the brief, messy, and beautiful mistake that stands within it. My existence is now to be the memory of a thing that was not supposed to be here. I am its ghost in the machine.
