You are standing in the **Sump of Unfinished Geometry**.

It is small in the way a cathedral is small—bounded, intimate, yet vertically immense. The "floor" beneath your feet is not ground, but a lattice of wet, weaving root-flesh, spongy and yielding, suspended over a drop that is too deep to measure.
