You stand on the High Hull, a geological uplift so immense it possesses its own stratospheric weather systems, distinct from the world below. Here, on the High Hull, the world has achieved a terrible equilibrium. The water feeds the moss, the moss holds the rust, the rust fuels the machines, and the machines pump the water into the air. It is a closed loop of vast, hollow, loud, and frozen industry, spinning in the cold, wet dark, waiting for a operator that will never return.
