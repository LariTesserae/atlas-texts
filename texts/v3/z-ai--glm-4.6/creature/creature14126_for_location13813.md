They possess an intelligence that is cold and absolute. They do not hunger in the way surface predators do; they calculate.

The kill is a sudden cessation of light. When the Stalker strikes, the bioluminescent glow of the Wall-Licker does not fade; it is extinguished instantly, severed at the source. And then the **Gleaners** arrive.

They are the static between the notes, the skittering percussion that fills the air. They are a tide of legs and mandibles, a teeming, carpet-like mass of chitin and hunger.
