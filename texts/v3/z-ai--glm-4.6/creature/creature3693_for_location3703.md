Here, life is vertical. It exists only in the shadow of the Titans or drifting in the currents above them. To understand the inhabitants of the Plateau of the Chorus, one must first understand that they are not merely living *on* a world; they are living *inside* a single, planetary metabolism.

### The Pillars: The Living Islands

The Pillars are not merely large plants; they are the biological equivalent of continents. Their existence is a slow, tectonic meditation. They are solitary giants, separated by miles of barren stone, each one a distinct entity with its own rhythmic pulsing of light.

**The Vascular System (Ichor-Life):**
Inside the Pillar flows the amber ichor. It is not water; it is a dense, syrup-like solvent rich in hydrocarbons and metallic salts. It flows with agonizing slowness, visible through translucent sections of the Pillar’s "bark" as a crawling magma of glowing gold. This fluid is the sole reservoir of moisture and chemical energy in the ecosystem. When a section of the Pillar is damaged, this "blood" does not spurt; it sluggishly oozes, instantly hardening into a resinous scab to prevent the precious fluid from escaping into the thin, dry air.

**The Carapace:**
The outer skin is a testament to the harsh environment. It is a hybrid of keratin and silicates, tough enough to resist wind erosion that lasts eons. The geometric plates—black as the void and green as tarnished copper—shift and expand over decades. The warmth they radiate is a beacon in the cold twilight. Where the plates part, the bioluminescence is brightest, a soft, emerald lure that draws the fauna toward the nutrient-rich vascular tissue beneath.

### The Scrapers: The Climbing Crystal-Herds

The Scrapers are the primary grazers of this vertical world, resembling a grotesque fusion of an insect and a mountain goat. They are hexapodal, their six legs ending in hooked, diamond-tipped claws that can gouge purchase into the seemingly impenetrable hide of the Pillars.

**Physiology:**
Their bodies are encased in a segmented, chitinous carapace that mimics the geometry of the Pillars themselves—a form of aggressive camouflage. This shell is not for defense against predators, but against the environment. It seals in their internal moisture, which they recycle with terrifying efficiency.
They have no eyes, navigating exclusively through vibration and smell. Their entire head is dominated by a circular, radial mouthpiece lined with vibrating, rasping files. These files oscillate at ultrasonic frequencies, allowing them to slice through the metallic bark of the Pillars to get at the sweet, spongy cambium layer beneath.

**Behavior:**
They move in spiraling tribes, etching perpetual, corkscrew trails into the Pillars as they graze. As they feed, they enter a trance state; their rasping mouths synchronize with the deep, seismic drone of the Plateau. The sound of a herd feeding is a constant, chittering static—a high-frequency insectoid buzz that overlays the deep bass of the earth. They do not defecate waste in a traditional sense; they excrete a dry, crystallized dust that falls to the ground far below, contributing to the sterile grey sand of the plains.

### The Floaters: The Atmospheric Whales

High above, where the air thins to the edge of space, dwell the Floaters. They are grace incarnate, vast manta-shaped beings with wingspans that can dwarf a cathedral.

**The Aeronautic Body:**
They possess no bones. Their bodies are pressurized gas bladders enclosed in a membrane of iridescent, silken polymer. This membrane is semi-translucent, revealing the glowing network of veins that carry heated gases and nutrients throughout their form. Trailing beneath them are thousands of yards of filamentous tentacles, thin as spider silk but strong enough to snare spores on the wind.

**The Song of the Sky:**
Floaters are the source of the Plateau's only melody. They possess a massive, resonant chamber running the length of their bodies. By inhaling the cold, thin air and forcing it through this chamber, they produce their iconic, mournful booming notes. These calls are communication, navigation, and territorial claim all in one. The vibrations carry for miles, shaking the very stone of the Pillars. To stand on a balcony in the Enclave when a Floomer passes overhead is to feel your chest cavity vibrate in sympathy with the beast, a feeling that is both terrifying and deeply moving.

### The Enclave: The Symbiotes

Deep within the calcified hollows of the greatest Pillar, the Spire, live the Enclave. They are human, or they were once. Eons of adaptation and bio-integration have altered them. They are taller, their limbs slender and elongated to navigate the climbing tubes of their vertical city. Their skin is pale, almost translucent, showing the faint blue of recycled ichor in their veins.

**The Internal City:**
They do not build *out*; they build *in*. Their architecture is carved from the living flesh of the Spire. Using directed nanite swarms, they ablate the internal stone, coaxing the Pillar to grow smooth, pearl-like nacre walls and floors. The city breathes. The apartments pulse gently with the Pillar’s slow heartbeat. The air is heavy with the scent of ozone and burnt sugar—the smell of the Pilalr’s breath.

**The Diet of Ichor:**
The Enclave does not know water. Their entire diet is based on the processed amber blood of the Spire. They have complex biological filtration systems—artificial organs worn as external apparatus or implanted gills—that allow them to drink the raw ichor. It tastes rich, like honey mixed with iron and copper.
Their "cuisine" involves the fermentation of the ichor using gut-bacteria harvested from the Scrapers. This breaks down the complex hydrocarbons into proteins and distinct flavors—a sour, sharp sustenance that fuels their high-energy neural tissues.

**The Technology of Integration:**
Their technology is biological, not mechanical. Their tools are living creatures. They wear gloves made from Floomer membrane, sticky and responsive. Their lights are phosphorescent fungi stimulated by small bio-electric pulses. Their vehicles are "Skiffs"—semi-sentient gliders carved from thickened Floomer tissue, guided by neuro-implants connected to the pilot's spine.

**The Mindset:**
To be an Enclave resident is to accept a terrifying responsibility. They know that the Pillar is a god, and they are beneficial parasites living within its skin. They monitor the Pillar’s vitals obsessively. A drop in pressure, a change in the hum of the Song, and they mobilize. They cull the Scrapers when the grazing becomes too deep; they heal the wounds of the Spire with resin patches. They worship by maintenance. Their culture is one of deep, quiet stewardship, defined by the knowledge that if the Spire dies, they do not merely lose their home—they lose the only atmosphere in the world capable of supporting them. They are the immune system of the mountain.
