They do not have a name, for none is needed. To name a thing is to separate it from its environment, and they are not separate. If one were to ask a tremor-shelled lizard what they are, the lizard would feel only the rock. If one could ask the colossal tree, it would sense only another root, pushing not into the stone, but into the song itself. We can only call them by what they do, what they are. We can call them the Attuned.

In form, they are the final echo of the land's shaping. They are tall and impossibly thin, like the menhirs left by the Forerunners, but forged of living stuff. Their frame appears to be a dark, glassy mineral, impossibly smooth yet unbroken, reflecting the sheen of the polished valleys without showing a clear image of their surroundings. Their height is not constant; it sways imperceptibly, growing and shrinking by inches over long minutes, as if they are breathing with the compression of the air, responding to micro-shifts in the great chord. They have no discernible face, no features common to the fauna of quieter worlds. Where a head should be, there is simply a more complex, multifaceted shape of the same dark, resonant stone, a living geode tuned to a spectrum of harmonics the rest of the world only endures.

They do not eat, nor do they drink in the way we understand it. The thick, sweet condensation that weeps from the rock is their sustenance. Their skin is not a barrier but a membrane, porous to the vibration and the moisture it carries. The Attuned stand motionless for days, weeks, centuries, their bodies slowly glistening as they absorb the world’s exhaled breath. They drink the dampness, but more, they drink the energy within it—the potential energy held in the warm, heavy air, the nectar of geophysical resonance. It is a diet so pure and so elemental it reduces the needless chaos of a digestive system to an absurdity. They are fueled by the engine itself.

Their consciousness is the greatest mystery, for it is not a solitary light in the void of the mind, but a facet of the larger, all-encompassing sound. To think is not to form a sentence of silent words, but to find and hold a specific tone within the roar. Their thoughts are chords, their memories are recurring melodies, their emotions are the sharp dissonance or perfect harmony of their internal pitch with that of the world. This makes them utterly indifferent to individuality. An Attuned one does not see another as an "other," but as a sympathetic vibration, a confirmation of shared being. They communicate by subtly altering their own resonant frequency, creating minute disturbances and new harmonics in the great chord that travel for miles. A conversation is not a spoken exchange, but a slow, majestic symphony of modulated silence, a weaving of new threads into the world's tapestry of sound, felt not by ears, but by the bones of all who can listen.

They have no civilization, for civilization is a wall against the elements, and they have no elements to wall out. Their culture is a single, unbroken ritual of listening and waiting. They are the living seismographs of the planet's soul. They spend the long, stable centuries in a state of deep, meditative resonance, their bodies perfectly matched to the world-song. They are the anchors for the colossal trees, their presence calming the chaotic vibrations that would otherwise shatter a single taproot. They are the silent shepherds of the great flowers, their own harmonic field encouraging the waxy petals to unfurl.

But their true purpose is revealed in the Great Reshaping. While every other form of life is exterminated by the sudden, violent shift in pitch, for the Attuned, it is an agonizing ecstasy. The very frequency of their being is torn asunder. The harmony that is their mind becomes a cacophony of discord. This is their death and their rebirth. For a week, they shudder, cracking, their glassy forms crazed with fractures as the old song dies. And when the new chord settles—the one that has not been heard for centuries—they are the first to find its core. They attune themselves, their very essence re-forming around the new planet-note. They become the first living things to resonate with the reborn world, stabilizing it, welcoming it, teaching the stone its new shape. Their cracked bodies heal, polished over by the new vibration, now marked forever with the scar-whorls of the past key.

The crude altars and hammerstones of the Forerunners are their only record of a different way of being. They are the legacy of a fleshier, more desperate version of themselves, a proto-consciousness that had to build external ears because it could no longer hear with its bones. The Attuned are the fulfillment of that pilgrimage; they are the ones who came to listen and never left, until they became indistinguishable from the sound.

To see them is to see the land become self-aware. They are the planet's own dream of itself, given a form that can stand, listen, and endure. They are not in the place; they *are* the place, the consciousness of the stone and the water and the sound, standing as a testament to the fact that to truly know a thing, you must not observe it—you must become it. They are not visitors to the chord; they are the notes it gives itself, so that it might know its own sound.
