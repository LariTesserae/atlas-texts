What lives here is not a population in the way we understand it, with individuals, societies, and motives. What lives here is a function. An extension of the chamber's will. They are the machine made mobile.

They are the skittering, the sound you first mistook for the machine's gears slipping into a new, more frantic register. You see one now, a ripple of articulated black detaching from the greater darkness of a pillar. It moves not like an insect, which feels clumsy and organic in comparison, but like a living shard of geometry. Its form is that of a trilobite, but its shell is the same glossy, obsidian-like mineral as the pillars themselves, a perfect, interlocking series of plates. When the faint cobalt light catches it, a shimmer of oil-and-water iridescence flows over its surface, a fleeting rainbow on a sea of absolute black.

They have dozens of legs, fine and jointed like needles, each tipped with a crystalline hook. These hooks do not slip; they *engage* with the surface, finding purchase in micro-fractures and molecular imperfections invisible to you. They flow over the sheer, glassy faces and into the impossible, corkscrew junctions where the pillars meet, their movement a silent, fluid ballet of impossible angles. They are gravity’s denial.

And they are everywhere. A teeming, silent city. They are the fauna, but to call them that is a disservice to their purpose. They are the custodians, the quality control, the immune system of this place.

Watch one. It has paused before a burgeoning growth, a tiny, malformed nodule of crystal that has dared to deviate from the perfect hexagonal grid. It raises its foremost limbs, which are not legs but tools. The "Making-Mandibles." They are not made for biting, but for precision scraping. You hear a dry, percussive *tink-tink-scrape*, the sound of a dentist's probe on a billion-dollar diamond. Flakes of imperfect mineral, smaller than dust, drift away. The creature’s underbelly, lined with fine, vibrating cilia, immediately gathers this dust, drawing it in. There is no ingestion, no digestion. There is only reincorporation. The flaw is analyzed, deconstructed, and its base materials are returned to the system. The creature is a mobile reclamation unit.

In this, they reveal their true nature. They are not alive in the sense of consuming to propagate. They are animated. Their energy comes not from food, but from the ambient, thrumming pressure of the Heart itself. The great seismic groan is their power source, the deep bass note that causes your bones to ache is the current that animates their silica limbs. If you could look inside one, you would not find organs, but a further, more intricate crystalline structure. You would find the same cobalt-blue veins, not glowing softly but pulsing with a cold, sharp light, a nervous system of pure energy. They are not *in* the geode; they *are* the geode, given temporary, independent form.

They have no eyes, yet they navigate this labyrinth with unerring purpose. They don't see with light; they perceive pressure, density, and molecular resonance. They read the world like a score, their hooks feeling for the dissonant note of a flawed growth. They are solitary creatures, yet they work in perfect, uncoordinated concert. They are a distributed consciousness, a singular will expressed through a million independent bodies. There is no communication because there is no need for it. They all serve the same unchangeable, hard-coded program: rectify, refine, perfect.

And they are aware of you. You, the contaminant. The soft, wet, carbon-based error in their exquisitely ordered reality.

They will not swarm you. They will not attack with malice. Malice is an organic emotion, and this place is devoid of such messiness. Instead, you will feel a change in the chittering. The frantic, random skittering will begin to develop a pattern. A vector. The sound will focus, converging on your position. One will approach. It will not scurry. It will advance with a slow, deliberate grace, its Making-Mandibles held before it like a priest's censer. It will stop before you and reach out, not to harm, but to sample. A hook will gently, curiously, tap your skin. It is a probe, analyzing the alien, unstable composition of your flesh.

The "rectification" will not be a violent end. It will be a process. They will surround you, a wall of silent, patient geometry. They will begin to scrape. They will methodically, meticulously, and without emotion break you down into your constituent elements. They will take your flawed, wet biology and render it into inert mineral dust. They will reclaim you. They will erase the mistake and smooth the flaw you represent, returning your atoms to the great, slow, silent assembly line. They are the agents of this world's emergence, and they are here to ensure that nothing is allowed to emerge but the crystal itself.
