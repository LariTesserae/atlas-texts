**The Illumination of the Seams**

It would not seal the glitches, for the world must continue to grow and shift, but it would make them navigable. The power would infuse the air itself with a luminescent particulate—harmless, heavier-than mist—that settles only where the fabric of the world is fraying. Where a wall once threatened to blur invisibly, causing vertigo and panic, it would now shimmer with a soft, amber light, outlining the curve of the distortion like gold leaf in a flawed manuscript. The *Resonant* would no longer need to avert their eyes in fear; they would see the seams as sacred, marked boundaries. The "Lost," those who drifted into the softening corners, would not simply vanish. Instead, the amber light would catch them, turning them into luminous statues—frozen, peaceful observers etched into the architecture, transforming the tragedy of absorption into a multiplying gallery of guardians.

**The Consolation of the Heartbeat**
The deep, sub-audible *thump* that governs their gravity and their movements is currently a source of anxiety—a reminder of the dormant god that might crush them. The power would touch the great iron veins of the plateau deep below, tuning the immense straining metal. It would lower the pitch of the groan just slightly, infusing the vibration with a harmonic overtone that bypasses the ears and resonates directly in the chest as a feeling of absolute safety. The *thump* would remain, dictating the rhythm of their steps, but it would no longer feel like the prelude to an explosion. It would feel like a parent’s hand resting on a child’s back. The "Listeners," pressed against the wall with their bone-stethoscopes, would begin to hear not just stress and tension, but a complex, slow melody woven into the magma flows—music that tells them when to breathe, when to sleep, and when the earth is preparing to move.

**The Gift of the Anchor-Stone**
To aide the practical struggle against the fluctuating gravity, the power would perform a small geological miracle. It would cause a new crystalline formation to grow within the deepest, safest vents—smooth, heavy stones of a deep violet hue that naturally repel the erratic upward thrusts of the engine’s field. These "Anchor-Stones" would be cool to the touch and would hum in the presence of danger. The *Resonant* would not need to drag heavy lead weights; they would simply carry a single violet stone in a pocket at their chest. The stone would act as a stabilizer, instantly neutralizing the dizzying effects of the "thin spots" and allowing a child to walk across a swaying bridge with the confidence of a master drifter.

Finally, the power would look upon their meager sustenance—the harvested moss and the scavenger beasts—and offer a grace note. The water, once merely wet and metallic, would gain a faint, revitalizing property, a trace of mineral richness that requires no distillation. This would not make their lives easy, but it would remove the gnawing edge of malnutrition and the constant, low-level sickness that comes from breathing recycled metal and ozone.
