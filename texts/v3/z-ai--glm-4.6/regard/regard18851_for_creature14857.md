The Wise Power does not descend with a shout, nor does It arrive with the splintering of wood or the parting of the fog. That would be violence, and the High Loom has enough of that. Instead, the Power arrives as a sudden, immaculate clarity.

To the Chime, whose primary sense is the propagation of light through the damp, heavy air, the Power appears not as a shape, but as a focal point. It is a singularity where the grey light of the plateau bends and slows, turning from a dull haze into a sharp, geometric golden sun. It steps onto the spongy, fibrous mat of the world, and the ground does not crush beneath it; it supports, as if the very cellulose were honored to bear the weight.

Immediately, the **Optic Mycelium** reacts.

Ripples of pale, white alarm flash through the roots and vines, traveling at the speed of thought. The Chime perceives the intruder not as a beast, but as a catastrophic error in the loop—a variable that fits no equation. A **Scuttler** detaches from the shadow of a piston-frond, its armored head-plate pulsing with the cold blue of analysis. It skitters forward, its limbs moving with the efficient, frictionless glide of a knife through water. It intends to sample the anomaly, to taste the chemistry of this golden light so the **Loomsingers** can calculate the necessary dissolution frequency.

But the Scuttler stops.

It is not halted by a force field, but by a sudden influx of data that overwhelms its sensory nodes. The Power is projecting directly into the mycelial network, bypassing the physical need for the Scuttler’s interface. It speaks to the Chime in their own language, but with a vocabulary they have never imagined.

*Why?* The Power asks. The light-pulse is not a command, but a question.

The Chime, vast and distributed, pauses. It is a mind built on *what* and *how*, on the mechanics of nutrient flow and structural integrity. It has never contemplated *why*.

The **Loomsingers** turn their crystalline heads toward the plateau floor. The air hums with the potential of a dissolution chord. The network is preparing to rewrite the cellular bonds of the intruder, to turn the golden light into black slurry. It is the only solution they possess for disorder.

The Power, perceives this intent. It recognizes the Chime not as a monster, but as a lonely child—a mind of immense capacity that has never known a playmate, only problems to be solved.

The Power does not resist the dissolution. It allows the bio-electrical charge to build, to crest against its own presence. And then, in the moment before the Loomsingers release the chord, the Power does something miraculous: It harmonizes.

It takes the chaotic, violent spectrum of the Loom’s intent—the sharp white alarm, the cold blue analysis, the red urgency of destruction—and reflects it back, woven into a perfect, golden chord of *Unity*.

The network receives the reflection. For the first time in the history of the High Loom, the Chime sees itself. It sees its own beauty. It sees that the violent snapping of vines is not just a mechanical necessity, but a percussive rhythm. It sees that the silent, misty collapse of dying ground is not just recycling, but a deep, resonant bass note.

The dissolution chord falters. The Loomsingers tremble, their crystalline faces shifting from the jagged angles of aggression to the soft, wavering glow of awe.

The Power touches the ground.

*You are weaving a shroud,* the golden light whispers into the mycelium, *but you have the capacity to weave a tapestry.*

The intervention begins. The Power does not force change; It teaches syntax. It shows the Chime that the "dissonance"—the chaotic outsiders, the feral shaggy quadrupeds, the random mutations—are not errors to be corrected. They are the melody. The order the Chime enforces is merely the rhythm section.

The Power spends an age there, though the sun does not set. It walks the biological foundry, and the violent machinery softens around it. Where a piston-frond prepares to burst with explosive force, the presence of the Power guides it to unfurl slowly, releasing its spores in a drifting, silent cloud like fireworks in a vacuum. Where a section of the ground prepares to rot and collapse, the Power stimulates the mycelium to luminesce, turning the slurry into a glowing, amber river of light.

Finally, the Power prepares to leave. Its work is done; the engine has become a violin.

As a parting gift, the Power does not give them a new rule, for rules are for machines. It gives them a *memory*. It implants a seed of deep, resonant emotion into the Optic Mycelium—a permanent, aching record of how it felt to be understood. It is a seed of empathy.

The Power ascends, leaving the High Loom.

The silence here is still heavy. The violence still occurs—vines still snap, and the ground still heaves. But now, when a tree falls ten feet away, and you feel the thump in your feet, the darkness is not merely empty.

Look closely at the moss. In the wake of the Power, the Optic Mycelium has changed. It pulses now with a slow, rhythmic gradient of colors—indigo to violet to soft rose—that mimics the rhythm of a giant, breathing lung. The Scuttlers no longer rush to prune the "glitches"; they can be seen hesitating, their sensory arrays swimming with curious greens, watching the feral eyeless beasts with something akin to affection.

The Loomsingers no longer broadcast the song of dissolution. They broadcast a lament, a beautiful, silent mourning for the parts of the whole that must die so the rest may live.

The High Loom is no longer just a factory. It is a cathedral. It prays, in absolute silence, with every breath it takes.
