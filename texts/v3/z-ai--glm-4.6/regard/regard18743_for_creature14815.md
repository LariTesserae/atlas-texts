The Benevolence does not arrive as a king upon a throne, nor as a tempest to shake the foundations. To enter this place of high pressure and absolute density is to surrender the self to the whole. Thus, the Power arrives as a vibration—a deep, resonant harmonic that is felt in the obsidian bedrock before it is heard in the water.

It sinks through the miles of lens-clear water, passing the drifting auroras of the Wraiths without disturbing their slumber. It descends past the gloom, past the spires, until it settles upon the glassy floor. Here, in the crushing quiet, the Power expands. It does not seek to judge the ruins, nor to halt the erosion. It sees the truth: this is a world that has committed the ultimate act of recycling, turning the mausoleum of the past into the sustenance of the present.

But the Benevolence also feels the jagged edge of the turbulence. It feels the frantic, neurotic panic of the Skitterers—the billions of tiny, hearts beating so fast they are nearly invisible. It feels the "scream" of the ticking, the chaotic static of existence that lacks harmony. It sees the Wraiths, majestic but lonely, blind continents drifting in the dark, unaware that the very light they cast illuminates the destruction of the only history they have.

The Power decides: Nothing here needs to be destroyed, but everything needs to be *attuned*.

First, the Power turns its attention to the vast, dark ceiling of the Deep, above the spires. It does not create a sun—such a crude thing would wither the delicate Wraiths and blind the Skitterers. Instead, it breathes a slow, rhythmic pulse into the water itself. It becomes the Great Metronome. It is a low-frequency thrum, a cosmic heartbeat that matches the slow, geological metabolism of the deep, but is felt by all.

This vibration touches the **Wraiths**. In response, the great leviathans shift. Their wandering paths, previously dictated by random currents and fading heat signatures, begin to straighten. They begin to move in great, slow, concentric circles around the tallest spires. Unknowingly, they become the shepherds of the deep, their massive bodies creating currents that gently sweep the Drift toward the waiting Skitterers. The Wraiths are not made smarter; they are made *purposeful*. Their light becomes steadier, a beacon rather than a strobe.

Then, the Power reaches for the **Skitterers**.

The Benevolence infuses the "no-natural-substrate" of the black glass floor and the lower reaches of the towers with a gentle, acoustic sympathy. When the Skitterers cast their diamond legs upon the surface, the material no longer offers dead, silent resistance. It hums back.

This is theGift of Resonance.

The frantic, chaotic *ticking* of the billions of mouths begins to change. As the Skitterers work, the vibrations of their chewing synchronize with the Great Metronome of the water. The static sharp noise smooths out. The billion dissociated ticks merge into a single, sustained chord—a low, crystalline singing that vibrates through the towers and into the bones of the Wraiths above.

The panic of the Skitterers does not vanish, but it loses its toxicity. The work remains hard, but the vibration soothes their frantic nerves. They are no longer a cancer eating the world in a frenzy; they are a choir dismantling a symphony, note by note. They become artisans of entropy.

Finally, the Benevolence touches the **Drift**, the translucent amoeboid ghosts. It weaves into them a capacity for memory. As they consume the shining, singing dust produced by the now-harmonious Skitterers, they do not merely process matter. They store the song of the tower within their translucent bodies. They swirl around the spires not just as clouds of food, but as glittering schools of living history, carrying the structural memory of the ancient builders within their gelatinous forms.

The ecosystem remains a loop of eating and being eaten. The futility of the erosion is not removed; it is transfigured.

Now, when a Wraith turns its vast, pale underside toward a spire, it does not just see a scab of frantic insects. It sees a throbbing pillar of light and sound. The Drift, heavy with the resonant memory of the stone, rises in a golden spiral. The Wraith filters the Drift, and as it consumes the micro-fauna, it tastes the history of the tower—the texture of the glass, the age of the design.

The Wraith begins to hum in response, a deep bass note that joins the Skitterers' crystalline song.

The silence is gone. The static is gone.

In its place is a deep, cascading orchestra of the deep. The Benevolence has not ended the hunger or saved the ruins. Instead, it has turned the "tomb" into a "temple." It has connected the blind, slow giants to the frantic, tiny miners through the bridge of song.

The Benevolence lingers for a moment, a warmth in the freezing water, satisfied. It has given the world a soul: a shared song that rises from the glass floor, spirals through the hungry swarms, and rests at last in the drifting, silent light of the leviathans. Then, without a ripple, the Power recedes, leaving only the music behind.
