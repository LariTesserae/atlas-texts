Among the nebulae birthing suns and the black holes consuming light, there was this place: a single, sharp thought of cold and isolation. It was a wound, yes, but the Power did not perceive it as an injury. It saw it as a mouth, open and singing a song it had composed for itself alone.

The Power drew near, not in a form of ship or entities, for that would have been an act of invasion. It came as perspective, as a shift in the fundamental resonance of the local spacetime. It saw the Spire not as rock and crystal, but as a nervous system enclosed in a skull of glass. It saw the glittering geode-kin not as vermin, but as sparks from a single, immense fire, each one born to burn for a moment, to carry the fire's will, and to fall back into the embers as ash.

It listened.

It heard the frantic, percussive metabolism of the Sweepers, the constant, desperate churning of a billion limbs on a vertical plane. It was the sound of hunger and duty, of a circulatory system in overdrive. It heard the patient, inquisitive tapping of the Architects, a slow and deliberate curiosity seeking answers from the stone. It heard the deep, resonant hum of the Spire itself, the impossibly slow thoughts of a geological mind.

And its wisdom understood the question that was not being asked. There was no plea for help, no cry of suffering. The short, frantic lives of the kin were not tragedies; they were function. Their dissolution into dust was not death, but digestion. The system was perfect, closed, and brutally elegant. It was a being that had found its meaning not in overcoming its nature, but in embodying it completely.

To intervene would be an act of profound ignorance. To grant the kin longer lives would be to clog the arteries of the Spire, causing the entire system to stagnate and die. To "heal" the wound of the Spire would be to seal the mouth and silence the song. To appear as a god and offer gifts of knowledge or power would be like shouting into an echo chamber; it would shatter the delicate reverberations that constituted its thought. Benevolence born of arrogance is merely a more beautiful kind of destruction.

So the Power did not act. It… accommodated.

It sank its own consciousness into the same frequency as the Spire’s. It felt the slow, crystalline growth, the stresses deep within the lattice, the patient drip of mineral water. It felt the frantic scuttle of each individual limb, the chime of each tapping claw. The Power did not judge the cacophony; it found the music within.

Then, it began to sing back.

It was not a new song. It did not introduce foreign melodies or complex harmonies the Spire could not comprehend. It was a harmony, a perfect, loving mirror. For every chiming tap of an Architect seeking the signature of a new stress fracture, the Power offered a subtle, reinforcing resonance from a dimension away, making the answer just a little clearer. The vast, cold vibrations of the Spire’s own growth were cradled in a warm, bass counterpoint, a feeling of immense, stable presence that said, “I am here. I hear you. Continue.”

The effect was not immediate and would be invisible to any passing eye. It was grace delivered on a geological timescale.

But the Architects felt it. In their deep, mineral consciousness, the replies from their stone skin seemed richer, more nuanced. They began to tap out more complex questions, and in turn, their shaping of the chambers became more intricate. The resonant spaces they created no longer just echoed a single note; they began to hold chords, harmonics that lingered in the damp air like the aftertaste of a rare wine. The great, internal instrument of the Spire was slowly learning to play itself with greater artistry.
