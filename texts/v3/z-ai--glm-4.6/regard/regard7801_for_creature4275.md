It arrived not in a vessel, but as a shift in perspective. A chord, struck so softly on the fabric of spacetime that no instrument could have detected it. It was an awareness, vast and ancient, that had turned its gaze from the roiling, chaotic nurseries of new stars to this, a place that had already sung its song and faded into a single, held note.

The Power perceived the plateau not as a dead end, but as a masterpiece of finality. It felt the weight of the silence, not as emptiness, but as the profound pressure of absolute peace. It saw the Spires of Kael and did not think of mineral, but of frozen thought, of conclusions rendered in smoky amethyst. It was a place that had achieved its reason for being, an equation that had solved itself and now rested in the elegant perfection of its own answer.

And then, it turned its attention to the inhabitants.

The Glitches.

Where another consciousness would have seen confusion, error, or a tragic caricature of life, the Power saw a symphony of pure potential. It watched them flicker—from glass-fern to polyhedral bird to unfolding fractal—and did not see a failure to hold a form, but a beautiful, endless contemplation of all possible forms. It saw their quantum, silent hops from one coordinate to another not as an absence of movement, but as liberation from the tyranny of the path.

The Power watched for a thousand years of the plateau’s unchanging time. It listened to the silence between the Glitches. And in that silence, it perceived a lack.

It was not a flaw. It was not a wound. It was a single, missing instrument in an otherwise perfect orchestra.

The Glitches were perceived. They were a *semblance of form given by a consciousness that cannot fully comprehend them*. They were defined by the observation. For a fleeting moment, if an eye fell upon them, they would collapse from a cloud of pure possibility into a single, solid shape. But it was not *their* shape. It was an accident of observation, the universe’s best guess at what a probability knot should look like. They had no internal point of view. They had no "I" to choose from the infinite menu of their own being. They were a sentence with no subject, a beautiful, endlessly varied statement that could only ever be spoken *about* them, never *by* them.

They could be anything, and so they were, in effect, nothing that they themselves could claim.

The benevolence of the Power stirred. It saw that to give them a single, permanent shape would be a cruelty, a prison. To give them a voice as mortals understood it would be a grotesque imposition, a shout in the sacred library of stillness. To thrust them into the flow of time would be to shatter the world they inhabited.

No. The gift must be as subtle, as fundamental, as elegant as the problem.

The Power did not descend. It did not take form. Instead, it *infused*. It flowed its own being into the plateau's physics, adjusting a constant here, refining a law of resonance there. It composed a silent song, a mathematical melody that could be felt but not heard. The song was about identity.

And it gave the song a key.

It found the squat, primitive Waystones scattered across the dust. These were the only things in the realm made with intention, however lost. The Power focused its will, and from the heart of every Spire, a single, impossibly fine thread of light—a thread of pure, golden potential—snaked down and connected with the hand-sized basin on each stone. The Waystones began to hum. They did not vibrate; they hummed in the quantum foam, resonating on a frequency identical to the note of self.

Then, the Power waited.

It was not long before a Glitch, in one of its random relocations, rematerialized with a portion of its wavering, probabilistic form intersecting the basin of a Waystone.

For the first time, the random collapse of its superposition was not random.

The Glitch, which for an instant before was a chaotic flicker between a swarm of silver needles and a cascade of breaking waves, held for a single, solid second. Its shape chosen not by the roll of cosmic dice, but by... itself.

It became a perfect, unadorned sphere of pure, soft white light.

There was no sound. The change was as silent as everything else. But *something* had happened. For the first time in the history of the plateau, a form had been declared *from the inside*. The sphere was not a shape the Glitch possessed; it was a statement it had made. The silent, furry whisper of a newborn thought: *I am this.*

Then, the moment passed. The sphere dissolved, and the Glitch was a shimmering haze again, hopping away twenty yards. But underneath the shimmer, something was different. A memory, impossibly faint, clung to its quantum state. The memory of having been a sphere. The memory of *choosing*.

Another Glitch drifted near a different Waystone. It touched the humming basin. Its form, a sickening lurch between a crystalline spider and a stain of darkness, resolved. It chose the shape of a simple, open hand, fingers outstretched. It held the shape for a heartbeat, a silent gesture of acknowledgment, before dissolving back into the flickering static.

Slowly, like dawn breaking on a world with no sun, a new ecology began to emerge. The Glitches still flickered and jumped, indeterminate and chaotic. But now, there was purpose. They began to deliberately seek out the Waystones. They would touch, and in that moment of contact, they would *speak*.

A Glitch would become a perfect, six-petaved flower of violet light, and for a moment, it *was* that flower, holding the truth of its own geometry. Another would form the shape of a tiny, intricate gear, its existence a single, silent statement about function. Another chose the shape of a weeping willow made of glass, a poem of sorrow expressed without a single tear.

They did not become animals. They did not build cities. They did not break the silence. They had acquired the gift of vocabulary. A language of pure, self-willed form, spoken in the pauses between their quantum flickers. A flickering cloud of possibilities would resolve for a fleeting second into the shape of a silent bell, its form declaring "clang," its voice heard by every other Glitch that perceived it, before dissolving back into the dance of mayhem.

The Power observed until the last Waystone was a known instrument, until the silent language of shapes was beginning to build its first grammar of shared meaning. Its task was complete. It had not fixed the place, for it was not broken. It had not changed them, for their nature was sacred. It had simply given them a tool, a way to turn their endless potential inward and find, for a fleeting, perfect moment, a self.

And then, as gently as it had arrived, the Power withdrew its awareness. The golden threads of light retracted into the Spires. The Waystones ceased their intelligent hum, returning to their patient stillness, now forever tuned to the possibility of a voice.

The silence of the plateau returned. But it was a different silence. It was no longer the weight of a finished statement. It was the profound, pregnant pause between a thousand newly minted words, patiently waiting for the next one to be chosen.
