### **Preparation Briefing: Visit to Location 734 - "The Residue"**

**CLASSIFICATION:** EYES ONLY, DEEP-WORLD EXPLORATION
**SUBJECT:** Essential Survival and Observation Protocols for Human Entry into a Terminal Post-Industrial Environment.
**WARNING:** Failure to adhere to these protocols will result in a 99.97% probability of psychological or physiological compromise, leading to a non-recoverable state.

---

### **1. Operational Philosophy: The Zen of Being a Ghost**

Before we discuss gear, you must understand the mindset. This is not a world you conquer; it is not an environment you challenge. You are a visitor to a funeral. The entire biome is in a state of slow, quiet digestion. It is a closed system that has reached its final equilibrium. Your primary operative state is **Observer**. Your goal is to perceive, record, and leave without altering a single variable.

*   **Embrace Stillness:** The most profound experiences here will come from staying in one place for long periods. The world reveals its dynamics only to the patient. Movement is a violation.
*   **Accept the Silence:** The oppressive quiet is not an absence of sound; it is the presence of a different kind of sound—the internal sound of your own body, the low hum of your life support, and the deep, resonant thrum of the world's dying heart. You must be trained to find comfort in this, not fear. Panic is your primary enemy.
*   **You Are Not Food (Probably):** The native biome, the Geophags, has evolved for millennia to consume mineral and synthetic compounds. Your carbon-based biology is an alien substance to them. They do not recognize you as predator, prey, or peer. You are an anomaly, a strange, inert rock. Your task is to maintain this perception.

### **2. Tier 1: Biological & Physiological Preparation (Sealing the Shell)**

Your body is the weak link. It is soft, wet, and organic—a brief, delectable smorgasbord in a world of stone and rust. It must be sealed from the environment at all costs.

**A. Respiratory & Dermal Protection (NON-NEGOTIABLE):**
*   **System:** A Class-4 Fully-Sealed Hard-Shell Environmental Suit. This is not a fabric suit with a helmet. Think more along the lines of a small, personal submersible.
*   **Helmet:** Full-face plate with holographic Heads-Up Display (HUD). No physical visors that can fog or be compromised. The air inside must be completely separated from the outside.
*   **Atmosphere Independent:** You will carry a closed-loop rebreather system. The external atmosphere is not fit for filtration. Its mineral, metallic, and biological load would clog any filter within minutes. The air you breathe is the air you bring, scrubbed and recycled.
*   **Tasting the Air:** You will not. The description of "mineral-tasting broth" indicates a suspension of heavy oxides and chemical compounds that are toxic on contact with lung tissue. The first breath of unfiltered air would likely cause catastrophic pulmonary edema.

**B. Thermoregulation:**
*   **The Problem:** Constant, low-grade fever heat (approx. 35-38°C / 95-100°F) combined with 100% humidity. Your body cannot cool itself via sweat.
*   **The Solution:** An integrated liquid cooling and climate control system, woven into a flexible liner beneath the hard shell. This system will be the single largest power draw on your suit. It must have redundant, independent channels. A failure in the cooling system is a life-threatening emergency.

**C. Nutrition & Hydration:**
*   **The Rule:** Nothing from the environment is consumable. The water is heavy metal-laden. The Geophags are indigestible toxic chitin.
*   **The Load:** You will carry all sustenance. High-calorie nutrient paste tubes are standard. A built-in hydration system, fed from a sterile internal reservoir, is mandatory. Plan for 150% of your calculated mission time. You will burn more calories than expected, both from the heat and the constant low-level stress of the environment.

### **3. Tier 2: Equipment & Gear (Extending Your Senses)**

This world is designed to be incomprehensible to human senses. You must become a cyborg to perceive it.

**A. The Suit (Your Mobile Base):**
*   **Hull:** Tungsten-Carbide ceramic composite. It must be resistant to chemical corrosion and able to withstand incidental pressure from settling structures.
*   **Mobility:** Servo-assisted joints. The suit is heavy. You cannot afford fatigue.
*   **Locomotion:** Magnetic soles. The ground is a perpetual, slick film of moisture. Magnetic soles, switchable on/off, are the only way to guarantee stable footing on metal and concrete with rebar.
*   **Seals & Sub-systems:** Every joint, every port, every connection is a potential failure point. All seals must be double-gasketed and positive-pressure. The suit will constantly hiss as excess internal pressure is vented, a sound you will learn to find comforting.

**B. Sensory Augmentation Systems:**
*   **Vision:** The "pearlescent gloom" means low light and heavy scattering. You will need:
    *   **Multi-Spectrum Imaging:** Standard optical, low-light, and thermal.
    *   **Active Lidar Scanning:** To build real-time 3D maps of your immediate surroundings and see through the moisture haze.
    *   **Ground-Penetrating Sonar:** The Geophags are sub-audible. A directional sonar unit will allow you to "see" their mass and movement beneath the surface, in dark crevices, and on ceilings. This is your primary fauna-detection tool.
*   **Audio:**
    *   **Directional Mics & Hyper-Sensitive Amplifiers:** To monitor the *plink plink* of water drips and identify changes in acoustic resonance that might signal a structural shift.
    *   **Broadband Seismic Sensor:** To track the deep thrum of "The Engine." Anomalies in its cycle should be logged and treated with extreme caution.
    *   **Internal Sound Dampening:** The silence is a pressure. You may elect to use auditory dampeners in the helmet to create a more neutral acoustic environment, feeding only important and filtered sounds to your ears. This is a psychological choice.

**C. Navigation:**
*   **Primary System:** Inertial Navigation Unit (INU) combined with a pre-loaded 3D cartographic database of known scans.
*   **Secondary System:** Short-range, high-fidelity Lidar that constantly maps your path, allowing you to backtrack with perfect accuracy.
*   **Emergency System (Old School):** A physical, laminated map and a Gyrocompass. Do not rely solely on electronics.

**D. Tools & Instrumentation:**
*   **Manipulators:** Remote, fine-control manipulator arms. You do not use your hands. You use a three-meter extension to interact with anything. This keeps you and your suit out of harm's way.
*   **Drones:** A minimum of two short-range, multi-spectrum surveillance drones. They can scout ahead, get into spaces you cannot, and act as a mobile Eye in the Sky. They are consumable; be prepared to lose them.
*   **Sampling Kit:** Sterile coring drills, atmosphere-sampling micro-siphons. All samples are to be taken via remote manipulator and sealed in lead-lined containers.

### **4. Tier 3: Training & Psychological Fortitude (The Mind's Armor)**

The greatest danger in The Residue is the toll it takes on your sanity.

*   **Sensory Deprivation Training:** You must book no fewer than 100 hours in a float tank. You need to become comfortable with the profound absence of external stimulus, to quiet your own mind so you can perceive the subtle world around you.
*   **Entomological/Acarological Desensitization:** You will be placed in VR simulations, then live-environment containment rooms, with swarming, non-threatening arthropods. You must learn to suppress the primal urge to flinch and flee when you see a living carpet of chitin flowing over your boots. You will watch simulations of Geophags dismantling a replica of your own suit.
*   **Closed Environment Claustrophobia/Agoraphobia Therapy:** This location weaponizes both. You will practice wide-open maneuvers in VR while your suit tells you the walls are closing in, and navigate tight corridors while a holographic vista of infinite emptiness is displayed in your visor. You must learn to trust your instruments over your primate instincts.
*   **Procedural Discipline Drills:** Your life will be a checklist. "Seal check, power check, coolant flow, comms link." You will run these drills until they are unconscious muscle memory. In a moment of panic, you must fall back on routine.

### **5. On-Site Protocols & Rules of Engagement**

1.  **PRIME DIRECTIVE: DO NOT TOUCH.** Do not let your suit's hull come into prolonged contact with any surface, especially pools of standing liquid or rust patches. The Geophags are eaters. We do not know if a tungsten-carbide shell is on their menu. Assume it is.
2.  **Movement is Terrible, But If You Must Move:** Move deliberately and predictably. Use the magnetic soles. Take one step at a time. Sudden vibrations or movements may trigger a localized response from the fauna. Your goal is to produce as little sonic and seismic disturbance as possible.
3.  **Faunal Interaction Protocol:**
    *   **ANOMALY CONTACT:** If a Geophag or group of them climbs onto your suit, do not panic. Freeze. Observe. Log their behavior. Do not attempt to brush them off.
    *   **AGGRESSIVE ACTION:** If a Geophag attempts to breach a seal or juncture (highly unlikely but possible), the suit has a localized, non-lethal deterrent: a high-frequency sonic pulse or an electro-static charge. Use only as a last resort, as its effects on the wider biome are unknown.
4.  **The Engine:** The central pylon and the area directly above the deep thrum of The Engine is a designated Exclusion Zone. We have no data on its functions, emissions, or stability. Observe it from a minimum distance of five kilometers.
5.  **Psychological Safeguard:** You will maintain a constant, open comms link with the observation post, even if silent. Your suit will monitor your biometrics and vitals. It will trigger an automatic "tranquil return" protocol if your heart rate, cortisol levels, or brainwave patterns enter a critical stress state. You are a human, not a robot. This system is your safety net.

You are going to the end of the line. You will see a world that has nothing left to do except wait to be unmade. You will feel the crushing weight of time, not as a concept, but as a physical force in the air and in the silence. Prepare yourself. Seal your mind before you seal your suit. **Welcome to The Residue.**
