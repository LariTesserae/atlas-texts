### **Section I: Physical Systems Preparation (The Shell)**

Your biology is a leaky, messy, water-based system. This environment is the antithesis of that. We will first construct a suitable shell to protect it.

**1. Life Support & Environmental Sealing:**

*   **Atmosphere:** The air is lethally hypoxic and likely contains未知 particulates (silicate dust, ozone). Your closed-circuit rebreather is non-negotiable. It must be equipped with triple-redundant O2 scrubbers and a high-pressure liquid oxygen tank large enough to sustain a 120% operational time. Any your suit micro-fracture is a potential point of catastrophic decompression and oxygen loss.
*   **Desiccation Countermeasures:** The 0.2 desiccation factor is paramount. Moisture is a resource that will vacuumed from your body. Your suit must be a fully sealed, positive-pressure hard-shell. Inside, a personal humidification and reclamation system is essential. It will condense moisture from your exhaled breath and perspiration, filter it, and re-introduce it as a controlled mist into your helmet's air supply. Drinkable water must be stored in self-heating, chemically sealed bladders within the suit's core, as any exposed liquid will freeze or evaporate in seconds.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** The ambient cold is a slow, insidious predator that seeks to crystallize your tissues. Your suit requires a full-body, active-heating lattice powered by a long-life radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG), separate from your main suit power. This ensures constant heat even if primary systems fail. The inner lining must be aPhase-change material gel that absorbs and releases heat, buffering against any sudden temperature shifts.
*   **Physical Protection:** The ground is abrasive silicate powder; the accretions are vibrating, glass-like crystals. Your suit's outer plating must be composed of layered ablative carbides and self-sealing smart polymers. The helmet's visor must be a polarized, lead-impregnated quartz composite to protect against any unexpected radiation and the constant, glare-less "white noise" of the bruised light.

**2. Mobility & Anchorage:**

*   **Gravity is a Suggestion, Not a Law:** Due to the active and dynamic nature of the terrain (2.5), you cannot trust the ground. Falls are not the only danger; being caught between a wall grinding forward and a causeway rising up to meet it is a very real possibility.
*   **Grav-Anchors & Winch System:** Your suit must be equipped with a decentralized Gravitic Anchor system. This involves projectors in the boots, back, and gauntlets that can create temporary, localized warp fields, effectively "sticking" you to a surface with immense force. This is your only reliable means of "resting" mid-climb or bracing against a shifting structure. A high-tensile, retractable monomolecular winch and gaffer line are essential for crossing vast plazas or anchoring a base camp to a stable-seeming point.
*   **Proprietary Navigation (The “Reality Anchor”):** Do not rely on your eyes. Do not rely on traditional inertial navigation. The non-Euclidean geometry and fluctuating depth perception will render your senses useless. You must equip a Primary Navigation unit that we call the **Local Reality Anchor (LRA)**. It works by emitting a cascade of quantum-entangled particles and measuring their return time and state distortion. It doesn't plot your position *in* the world; it plots your position *relative to your ship's entry point*, creating a stable, three-dimensional map of only what's immediately around you, unburdened by the tricks of perception. Trust the LRA's schematic, not your eyes.

### **Section II: Psychological & Metaphysical Preparations (The Core)**

Your mind is the primary target of this environment. The physical dangers are a prelude to the main assault on your sanity.

**1. Sensory & Cognitive Hardening:**

*   **Simulated Sensory Deprivation:** You must spend a minimum of 200 hours in a flotation tank, followed by 200 hours in an anechoic chamber, further followed by 200 hours in a virtual reality simulation of the Spire-Lands (with safety rails enabled). This is to acclimatize you to the crushing silence and visual vastness before you experience it for real. The goal is to learn to generate your own internal mental "noise" to resist the encroaching emptiness.
*   **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) for Vertigo & Time Dilation:** You will work with a specialist to learn techniques for dismissing the sensory input that contradicts logic. When a tower snaps close to your face, the goal is to not panic, but to consciously state: "My perception is distorted. The LRA is my guide." You must train your brain to accept the input as false without dismissing it entirely, which is also a danger. You must learn to be a dispassionate observer of your own sensory failure.
*   **The Mantra & The Mnemonic:** Choose a personal mantra—the name of a loved one, the first line of your favorite poem, a mathematical constant you must repeat. This is your cognitive anchor against erasure. Furthermore, you will memorize a complex, non-repeating chain of data (e.g., the first 100 digits of pi in a specific notation). Reciting this daily provides a structured, logical task that grounds your mind in its own internal, stable reality.

**2. Metaphysical Defense Systems:**

*   **Perception Filter:** Your suit's helmet must be equipped with a "Perception Filter." This is not a weapon; it is a shield. It subtly fuzzes extreme depth parallax and smooths out the most aggressive geometric non-sequiturs at the edge of your vision. It will not make the world "normal," but it will prevent the instantaneous shifts from causing catastrophic synaptic failure, turning them into gradual, tolerable "warps." You have the option to disable it, but the advisory is clear: Do not.
*   **Interacting with the "Fauna" (The Scavengers):** Protocol for the tripodal, reality-warping entities is absolute and simple. **DO. NOT. OBSERVE.** They feed on observation, on the attempt to impose order onto their chaotic existence (perhaps they are agents of pure contradiction, sent to prune flawed geometries). When one appears in your periphery, your immediate and only response is to engage your "periscope mode"—looking down, using the suit's external sensors to navigate. Your CBT training will be vital here. Suppress the instinct to stare. To look is to invite them to "pick" at your own geometry. They have been known to cause localized temporal paradoxes in those who watch—aging them a year in a second, or erasing a memory of the event, leaving only a psychic wound.

### **Section III: Field Manual: Protocols for Engagement**

**1. Arrival:** The transition must be fast. Do not linger in the portal wake. Exit the transition point, engage the LRA, get a full sweep, and establish a 50-meter perimeter. Mark your entry point with a quantum resonator. This is your only guaranteed exit coordinate.

**2. Movement:**
*   **The Rule of Probes:** Never step onto un-cataloged ground. Use a hardened telescopic probe to test the stability of the path ahead. Listen for the sub-aural hum of the mineral accretions—if the probe's feedback vibrates, find another path.
*   **The Pace:** Move in a slow, deliberate shamble. Do not run. Your momentum can be used against you by the shifting landscape. Every step is a calculation.
*   **Rest:** Rest only in locations that offer three points of contact and partial enclosure (e.g., the corner of two buildings, the underside of a massive stair). Never rest in the open center of a plaza. The psychic pressure of the emptiness is too great.

**3. The Goal & The Exit:**
*   You have a mission parameter—perhaps to take a sample of a mineral accretion, or to place a long-term monitoring node. Acquire the objective and leave.
*   Do not be tempted to explore "just a little farther." The city is a labyrinth designed to make you feel small and lost. It *wants* you to wander until your mind is just another component in its geological machinery.
*   The moment your primary objective is complete, you initiate the exodus protocol. Navigate strictly by LRA coordinates back to your entry-point resonator. Do not look back. Do not try to take "one last picture."

**4. Emergency Protocol: "Planar Substitution"**
*   If you become trapped, your suit is compromised, or your mind begins to fracture beyond your training's capacity to manage, you have one final recourse. This is your Plan B, your suicide pill, and your rescue.
*   The **Planar Substitution Device (PSD)** is a single-use reality anchor that does not return you to your entry point. It tears a new, temporary hole to a pre-designated dead-zone reality—a featureless grey limbo—and propels you into it. You will be safe but lost.
*   Activating the PSD triggers a multi-band, multi-dimensional distress beacon. The IRNHM will triangulate your position in the limbo and mount a recovery operation. This process is not quick. Activating the PSD means you may spend subjective weeks or months in sensory nothingness awaiting rescue. It is better than being erased, but only just.

**Final Advisory:** The High Geometry will make you question if you were ever real. It will offer a silence so perfect you will want to dissolve into it. It will present a logic so pure you will find your own messy life embarrassing. Remember your mantra. Remember your training. Remember why you are there. You are a ghost in God's machine. Do not let the machine forget you're there.
