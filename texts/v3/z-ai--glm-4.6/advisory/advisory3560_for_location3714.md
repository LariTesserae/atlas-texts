### **Mantle Visit Preparation & Survival Protocol**

**Traveler Type:** Standard Biological Human (Class IV)
**Location Designation:** The Mantle
**Threat Assessment:** Environmental Extreme (Primarily Biological/Psychological), Probable
**Mission Success Probability (Unassisted):** 6.2%
**Mission Success Probability (With this Protocol):** 88.4%

---

### **Part 0: The Philosophical Mandate - Securing Your Own Anchor**

Before you even consider calibrating a rebreather or packing sterile rations, you must complete the single most important preparation of all: **the formulation of your "why."**

The Mantle is not a place to be conquered, explored, or even understood in a conventional sense. It is a place to be *experienced*. The profound, crushing, liberating slowness of it will mill away at a human mind that has no fixed point of purpose. You are a fleeting, frantic spark in a world of embers, and if you arrive without a reason for your own burning, you will be extinguished.

Are you a scientist seeking to catalogue its impossible biology? Then your anchor is data. Every sample, every reading is a victory against the encroaching sense of your own insignificance. Are you a philosopher hoping to witness the end-state of evolution? Your anchor is contemplation. Your task is to *feel* the peace, not just observe it. Are you an artist trying to capture its light and form? Your anchor is creation. Your lens and sketchpad are your shield against the overwhelming oneness of the place.

Write this purpose down. Record it. Seal it in the most accessible partition of your suit's computer. When the rhythmic dripping becomes a drumbeat of madness, when the endless jade light swallows your thoughts, you will activate this file. It is your anchor. Do not proceed without one.

### **Part I: Biological Hardening & Acclimatization**

The Mantle’s greatest weapon is not the fauna, but the air itself. It is a perfectly evolved, all-encompassing biological system. To you, it is a super-saturated aerosol of unimaginable complexity. Your body is the invader.

**A. The Pre-Visit Inoculation Regimen (Minimum 6 Months Out):**
You will undergo a series of bespoke virotherapy and mycological inoculations. The goal is not to make you "immune," which is impossible, but to "tune" your immune system. We will introduce tailored, inert analogues of the Dominate Spore Types (DSTs) catalogued from remote atmospheric sampling. Your immune system will learn to recognize but not overreact to the most common biological signatures. This prevents anaphylactic shock upon the first unavoidable, microscopic breach of your suit.

**B. Sterile Microbiome Reset (Month 1):**
Your body is a soup of terrestrial bacteria. To introduce them to the Mantle would be catastrophic—for you and potentially for it. You will undergo a 4-week course in a sterile isolation habitat. A carefully managed regimen of targeted antibiotics will eliminate 99.8% of your gut and skin flora. This will be followed by recolonization with a lab-grown, Mantle-inert "biological shadow suite" of bacteria. These microbes are non-competitive, non-pathogenic, and are designed to die off within 48 hours of exposure to unfiltered Mantle air. You will, for the duration of the mission, be biologically "clean."

**C. Pressure & Gas Acclimatization (Weeks Prior to Departure):**
The atmospheric pressure is estimated at 1.4 atm. This is manageable, but the heavy, inert elements and high water vapor content can lead to "Nitronarcosis-Like Syndrome" (NLS). You will spend increasing intervals in a hyperbaric chamber mimicking the Mantle's estimated gas composition: high Nitrogen, reduced Oxygen (approx. 17%), and inert Noble Gases in concentrations far exceeding Earth norms. This teaches your hemoglobin to work more efficiently and your brain to function under the gentle, constant "weight."

### **Part II: The Equipment Manifest - Your Second Skin**

Forget rough-and-ready exploration gear. Here, your equipment is a life-support cathedral. Every item has a purpose, and redundancy is not a luxury, it is the difference between a quiet end and a sample return.

**A. The Primary System: The "Shroud" Environmental Suit (Model 7-Lichean)**
This is not a suit; it is your personal habitat.
*   **Material:** A non-porous, biologically inert carbon-boron weave. It is completely hydrophobic at a molecular level. Water and spores simply bead and run off. It is also self-healing against minor abrasions.
*   **Pressure Seal:** Fully sealed, with an internal 24-hour air supply and a 2-week cryogenic oxygen refill system. Pressure is constantly regulated to a perfect 1.0 atm.
*   **Atmospheric Filtration:** A multi-stage system. First, a coarse electrostatic filter for large particulates. Second, a UV-C flash chamber to break down complex organic molecules. Third, a catalytic converter to neutralize reactive spores. Fourth, a molecular sieve that scrubs CO2 and maintains the perfect inert gas mix. A "suit breach" alarm is not audible; it is a haptic, full-body vibration—your only warning of a silent, lethal intrusion.
*   **Helmets:** You will have two.
    1.  **The Observation Dome (Primary):** A full, panoramic 270-degree transparent dome made of layered sapphire and polymer. It grants you an unimpeded, immersive view. Its
    own integrated light amplification and spectral analysis project a subtly enhanced HUD onto the inner surface, highlighting movement or energy signatures without cluttering the view.
    2.  **The Work Cowl (Secondary):** A traditional, armored helmet with severely restricted vision, used only during sampling, high-risk maneuvers, or when you need to shut out the overwhelming sensory input of the Mantle for psychological relief.

**B. Mobility & Traction: "Sole-Gripper" Boots**
The spongy, living substrate will sap your strength and swallow conventional boots. The Sole-Grippers are a marvel of adaptive technology. Their sole is a grid of micro-servos that react to pressure and texture. On soft ground, the "spines" extend and retract with a soft, digging motion, distributing your weight and providing purchase without tearing the substrate. They are whisper-quiet and will prevent you from sinking.

**C. Navigation & Awareness: The "Constant" Suite**
Disorientation is death. You cannot trust "up." You cannot see landmarks.
*   **Inertial & Geomantic Compass:** The Constant does not use GPS. It uses a master gyroscope to track your every movement, combined with a wide-burst gravimetric sensor that maps the dense, mineral "bedrock" deep beneath the surface. This creates a stable, 3D map of your immediate relative position, which is displayed on your forearm data-slate. Mark your base. Mark your path. Do not deviate.
*   **Ground-Penetrating Micro-Sonar:** A constant, low-frequency pulse emanates from the Sole-Grippers. It translates into a subtle audio hum in your helmets comms and a topographic display on your HUD. This is your primary warning system for "Gut-Slugs" or other burrowing fauna. A sudden, coherent wave-form moving beneath you is your signal to freeze and wait for it to pass.
*   **Spectral Analyzer:** Point it at anything. The canopy, an Echo, the floor. It will tell you its composition, its energy output, and its biological activity. Is that patch of "moss" just moss, or a sensory node? You must know before you touch.

**D. Light and Sight: "Helio-Emulative" Lamps**
The Jade-light of the Mantle is beautiful but functionally useless for human vision. Your suit and helmet are equipped with lamps that do not emit a harsh, white light. They are Helio-Emulative, capable of projecting a weak, diffuse approximation of a full-spectrum sun. This is for two reasons: first, it allows you to see true colours (the "white" of a Rock-Chewer's carapace, the "grey" of an Echo). Second, it is fundamentally alien to the Mantle's ecology, causing flora to retract or fauna to freeze. Use it sparingly; you are a brief, foreign sun, and you do not know how the world will react to prolonged exposure.

### **Part III: The Rhythm of Slowness - Mission Protocol**

Your survival and success depend not on your speed, but on your patience. You must sync your frantic human clock to the Mantle's geological one.

**1. The First 24 Hours: The Alien Cocoon**
Upon arrival, your shuttle will act as a sterile "cyst." Do not exit. For 24 hours, you will remain inside. Observe through the ports. Listen to the external audio feeds. Run long-range scans. Let the initial shock of sensory overload—the endless dripping, the oppressive jade, the sheer scale—wash over you while you are still safe. This is your mental decompression chamber. Meditate. Review your "why."

**2. The First Step: The Pact with the Ground**
When you finally exit, move deliberately. Take one step. Stop for five full minutes. Feel the give of the substrate through your Sole-Grippers. Listen to the gentle *patter* of the drips hitting your helmet. Do not look around wildly; focus on the ground immediately in front of you. You are not setting out to conquer a continent; you are making a treaty with the first square meter of it. Respect this boundary.

**3. Movement and Pacing: The "Settle-Wait-Move" Cadence**
The Mantle is dangerous, but its threats are slow. Your movement must be slower.
*   **Settle:** Arrive at a location. Stand still for at least ten minutes. Use your Constant suite to establish a baseline for the area below, above, and around you. Let the fauna get used to your presence.
*   **Wait:** Observe. Watch the Floaters drift. See how the light shifts. Use your Spectral Analyzer on the immediate flora and fauna. Data is survival.
*   **Move:** When you have to move, make it slow and deliberate. Take a few steps, then repeat the Settle-Wait-Move cycle. A journey of one kilometer could take a full day. This is not a failure; it is the correct way to exist here.

**4. Interaction with the "Echoes": The Protocol of Veneration**
The Echoes are the only reason for a traveler to deviate from a purely observational path. They are also an absolute unknown.
*   **Approach:** Stop at a distance of 50 meters. Begin a long-range observation cycle (minimum of one hour). Use every sensor. Scan for radiation, energy fields, biological activity. They should be dead. If they are not, your mission is now documentation and retreat.
*   **Advance:** Advance in 10-meter increments. Full stop. Full observation at each point. You must be certain your presence is not causing a reaction.
*   **Contact:** Do not touch with your gloved hand. The material of the Shroud may still be reactive. Use a sterile, extendable manipulator probe, tipped with a diamond-carborundum alloy. Perform a light "tap-and-retract" test. Observe for vibration, energy discharge, or fungal reaction.
*   **Sampling:** If all is clear, you may perform a micro-scraping sample using a laser-ablation tool integrated into the probe. This collects a microscopic amount of material without direct contact. The goal is to take nothing but information.

**5. Psychological Maintenance: The Ritual of Return**
The greatest enemy is your own mind, collapsing under the weight of timeless loneliness.
*   **Sensory Resets:** Every four hours, activate the Work Cowl. Seal off the external view. Play a pre-loaded audio file of a familiar Earth sound—rain, wind, birds—for no more than 90 seconds. Then return to the Observation Dome. This is a palate cleanser for the soul.
*   **Log Everything:** Not just data. Log how you feel. Log the boredom, the awe, the fear. The act of recording your own consciousness makes it more real and prevents it from dissolving into the alien whole.
*   **Companion AI:** Your suit's AI is programmed to be more than a tool. Initiate conversations. Ask it to recite poetry, solve logic puzzles, or debate the ethics of your presence. An interactive voice is a powerful shield against the void.

**Final Contemplation: The Unmaking**

To prepare for the Mantle is to prepare for a form of ego-death. You will go there as a human, a product of a fast, loud, chaotic world defined by change. You will return—if you return—as something else. A person who has walked on a living continent, who has breathed a world that is forgetting what sky is, who has felt the patient hum of being at the end of time.

You will leave behind a fragment of your sterile shadow-microbiome and take with you memories of a place that your own people will never truly comprehend. The preparation is the armor that protects your body. Your "why" is the anchor that protects your mind. But be prepared: part of you, the part that chases novelty and fears stillness, will be willingly, knowingly, left behind in the gentle, weeping, jade-dark peace of the Mantle.
