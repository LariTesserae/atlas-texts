**Preparation Briefing: The Submerged Cathedral (Codename: The Womb)**

**Core Philosophy: You Are a Ghost.**

This is not a place to be conquered, explored, or understood in the traditional sense. This is a place to be *witnessed*. Your entire preparation and operational protocol must be built around a single, unwavering principle: **Leave no trace. Cause no reaction.** You are not a biologist in a jungle; you are a neutrino passing through a star. You are a ghost in a machine that is still dreaming itself alive. Your goal is to perceive and depart, to be a sensor array with a human soul that remains unbroken by the experience.

**I. Mental & Philosophical Conditioning: Preparing the Mind**

Before you even think about gear, you must prepare your psyche. The environment is designed to overwhelm and re-process anything that doesn't fit its cold, logical symphony.

*   **Embrace Insignificance:** Meditate on your own non-existence. The fauna has higher consciousness; the flora is a supercomputer. You are a random variable, a temporary flicker. Any ego, any desire to be "the first," any thrill of discovery will be perceived as a flaw, a bug in the system. The system will attempt to correct the bug.
*   **Acclimatize to Hum and Stillness:** You will spend weeks in a sensory deprivation chamber, listening to a recording of the Cathedral's low-frequency thrum. You must learn to not just tolerate it, but to filter it out, to find your own quiet within the roar. You will practice absolute stillness for hours, learning to slow your breathing and heart rate until your own metabolic noise is negligible.
*   **Redefine "Time":** Live on "syrup time." For one month prior, all aspects of your life will be conducted at 50% speed. Eat slowly. Walk slowly. Think slowly. React slowly. This isn't a physical exercise; it's a neurological one. You must rewire your impulsive, fast-world brain to operate on the Cathedral's glacial timescale. A fast reaction here is a violent, alien act.

**II. The Vessel: The "Chrysalis" Environmental Exoskeleton**

Your survival depends entirely on a self-contained, wholly sealed exoskeleton. Think less "spacesuit" and more "artificial womb." It is not armor against a hostile force; it is a carefully maintained pocket of your original reality.

*   **Thermal Regulation (The Stillness):** The cold penetrates. It is not just a temperature; it's an absence of energy. Your suit must have an active internal thermal system that not only generates heat but actively prevents heat loss. The outer shell will be composed of a metamaterial that reflects thermal radiation back inward and is, to the cathedral, thermally "invisible." It will match the ambient 0°C temperature perfectly, making you a cold stone in a cold room. Breathing must be fully recycled, as inhaling the ice-water-like air would flash-freeze your lungs from the inside out.
*   **Atmospheric Processing (The Soup):** The atmosphere is a suspension of water, spores, and unknown technological particulates. The Chrysalis must be a perfectly sealed closed-loop system.
    *   **Intake Prohibited:** You will bring your own 48-hour supply of perfectly mixed, temperature-regulated breathing gases. There is no intake from the outside environment.
    *   **Exhaust Scrubbing:** Your exhaled CO2, moisture, and any bio-signatures will be captured, molecularly scrubbed, and compressed into solid carbon blocks stored internally. Releasing your warm, moist, CO2-laden breath would be like screaming a foreign language in a library. You would instantly become a point of disequilibrium, a source of attention.
*   **Physical Profile & Integrity (Growth & Resin):** The suit's external surface must be perfectly smooth, non-porous, and matte. No grooves, no seams, no textures that could snag on the root-matter or catch dripping resin. It should be shaped to be inherently stable, almost spherical when in a neutral position, to prevent accidental rolling. It must be resistant to the hardening agent secreted by the Growth and able to withstand immense, slow-squeezing pressure from the shifting architecture.

**III. Perception & Navigation: Sensing the Unseen**

Your natural senses are liabilities. Your eyes will see only misleading twilight. Your ears will hear only the crushing hum. You must learn to see and listen as the cathedral does.

*   **Vision Suite:**
    *   **Standard Optical:** Heated, self-wiping lenses to combat condensation, but for psychological reference only.
    *   **Primary Vision:** A full-spectrum array projected onto your internal HUD. You will primarily view the world in **thermal**, tracking the flow of heat through the neon-blue veins. You will overlay this with a **LIDAR map**, a real-time 3D wireframe of your immediate surroundings crucial for navigating the dense environment.
    *   **Energy Flow:** A specialized sensor will interpret the energetic hum, visualizing the "computation" of the Growth. Areas of high processing will glow softly on your display, allowing you to avoid the " busiest" parts of the biological server farm.
*   **Auditory Suite:**
    *   **Microphones are Offline:** Your suit will be soundproofed from the outside thrum.
    *   **Vibration Sensors:** The primary "hearing" will be conducted through the soles of the exoskeleton and any points of contact. Contact mics will translate the structure's vibrations into a comprehensible soundscape. You will learn to "hear" the footsteps of the gardeners through the floor minutes before they arrive. You will learn to differentiate a drip from a growing root from the hum of a failing leaf.
*   **Navigation System:**
    *   Forget compasses (the magnetic field is localized and wonky) and GPS (you are inside). Your navigation is inertial-mapping-based. From the moment of entry, your exoskeleton will constantly build a 3D map of your path. This map is your lifeline. Losing it means being forever lost in the dense, shifting root-chambers. Waypoints must be marked mentally with unique LIDAR-scanned formations.

**IV. Tools & Protocols: The Art of Not Existing**

Your tools must be for sensing, not interacting. You are a cartographer of phenomena, not a collector of souvenirs.

*   **Movement & Anchoring:** Climbing will be necessary. Forget pitons and drills, which would wound the Growth. Your exoskeleton will be equipped with **magneto-kinetic grapples** and **gecko-adhesive pads**. These can attach, hold, and release without leaving a mark, using the Growth's own structure against itself. All movement must be slow, fluid, and deliberate. You are a piece of driftwood in a slow current, not a rock being thrown.
*   **Sampling Equipment:** All sampling is non-destructive.
    *   Atmospheric sampling is done via a micro-laser spectrometer that analyzes particulates through a port.
    *   Biological/technological sampling is done with a **nano-probe array**, a needle-thin filament that can be inserted into a vein (with extreme caution) to draw a microscopic sample of hydraulic fluid or scan the local data without severing anything.
    *   **Primary sampling is passive:** scanning, recording, listening.
*   **Interaction Protocol: The Prime Directive - Wraith Stance.**
    *   **Do Not Interfere:** If a gardener creature is pruning a vine, you freeze. You become part of the architecture. You do not move until long after it has passed. It is blind, but it "sees" through vibration and deviation. Its job is to prune things that don't fit the pattern. Your heart beating too fast could make you a target for pruning.
    *   **The Law of the Floor:** Never trust the floor. It is a mat woven over an abyss. Every step is tested via vibration sensor before your full weight is committed. Use anchor lines whenever possible.
    *   **The Law of the Center:** The ascending spire is the place of birth and most intense energy. It is "holy ground." Do not approach. Observe from the periphery. The raw edges and dripping resin are construction zones, inherently unstable and dangerous. Avoid them.

**Final Briefing Note:**

When the portal closes and you stand in that gray-green, humming twilight, you will feel an instinctual, primal fear. It is the fear of the womb, the fear of being unmade and remade. The Chrysalis exoskeleton is your physical shell, but your mental discipline is your soul. Breathe slowly. Trust the sensors. Let the hum fill you without drowning you. You are there to witness a god in its slumber, to record a single sentence from a bible that writes itself.

Go, and come back a ghost, carrying the whispers of a cathedral that sings the universe into being. Do not make a sound.
