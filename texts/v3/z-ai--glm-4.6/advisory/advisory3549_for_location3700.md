### Traveler's Briefing for the Sump, the Under-Tundral

**Prepared by:** The Office of Inter-Realty Cartography, Hazard Division
**Subject:** Survival and Observation Protocol for Geofront-Class Reality, Designation: "The Sump"
**Primary Directive:** You are not an explorer. You are a temporary anomaly. Your goal is observation and exfiltration, not conquest or comprehension. Accept this now.

---

### **Section 1: The Psychological Covenant**

This is the most important section. More than your gear, more than your training, your mind will be the thing that fails you first. The Sump is not merely a dangerous place; it is an *assaultive* one. It is designed to erase the concept of "self" and replace it with the machine's rhythm.

*   **Embrace Insignificance:** Before you step through the portal, you must make peace with your own insignificance. You are a dust mote in a cathedral of gears. Your presence is a mathematical error, a rounding flaw in the grand equation of the Engine. Any vestige of ego, of the belief that you can master or tame this place, is a lethal vulnerability.
*   **Accept the Sensory Onslaught:** You cannot fight the constant vibration, the deafening noise, or the oppressive cold. Trying to will only lead to exhaustion and madness. You must surrender to them. Allow the thrum to become your new heartbeat. Let the noise be the backdrop to your thoughts, not the foreground. This is not a battle; it is an adaptation.
*   **Cultivate "Glitch" Consciousness:** Your identity, your memories, your linear perception of time—these are your anchor. The Sump will try to overwrite them. You must actively maintain them. Keep a rigorous log. Speak aloud to yourself. Check your watch, even if it feels meaningless. You are a ghost in a god-machine, and you must remember you were once flesh.

### **Section 2: The Absolute Essentials - Your Shell**

Your body is fragile. This gear is not a kit; it is your new skin. Every item must be tested for redundancy and failure. A single point of failure is a death sentence.

#### **A. Thermal Regulation: The War Against the Wet Cold**

The temperature is not the primary threat; the moisture is. This is a cold that seeps, that finds every gap, that freezes you from the inside out.

*   **Base Layer:** A full-body, close-fitting garment of wicking material. Merino wool-infused synthetics are ideal. It must move moisture away from your skin with violent efficiency.
*   **Insulation Layer:** A modular system of synthetic (e.g., PrimaLoft) insulated garments. No down. Down will become a soggy, useless mass of cotton within the first hour. Consider electrically heated vests or leggings, powered by a robust, shock-absorbent power cell.
*   **Outer Shell:** The most critical piece. A Gore-Tex Pro or equivalent hardshell suit, fully sealed, with taped seams and waterproof zippers. It must be an articulate, breathable-yet-impermeable membrane. This is your barrier against the "chill soup" of the air.
*   **Footwear:** Insulated, waterproof mountaineering boots with a rigid sole. The constant vibration will travel up your legs; a soft-soled boot will cause exhaustion and foot trauma. The sole must be vibram or equivalent for grip on wet, scored metal.
*   **Gloves:** A three-part system. A thin liner for dexterity, an insulated mitten for warmth, and an outer Gauntlet-shell for waterproofing. You must be able to operate your gear without exposing skin.

#### **B. Auditory Management: The Sound is the Map and the Monster**

Silence is death. So is deafness. You must thread a needle between the two.

*   **Primary System: Active Dampening and Threat Filtration Headphones.** These are not consumer headphones. They are military-grade tactical headsets. They must have the following functions:
    1.  **Passive Noise Blocking:** Physical insulation from the extreme decibel levels to prevent hearing damage.
    2.  **Active Noise Cancellation (ANC):** Tuned specifically to cancel the dominant, sub-audible thrum and the high-pitched coolant whine. This will reclaim mental space.
    3.  **Threat Amplification:** A directional microphone array that can be programmed to listen for specific, sharp sounds and amplify them into your ear canal: the *CLANG* of the press, the scrape of a drone's legs, the hiss of a vent. You will learn to navigate by these acoustic landmarks.
*   **Secondary System: Simple, high-decibel reduction earplugs on a lanyard.** If your primary system fails, your hearing is your most vulnerable asset. Be prepared to sacrifice all navigation ability to protect your eardrums from a sudden surge in volume.

#### **C. Luminosity: Piercing the Gloom**

Light is a fleeting, unreliable resource. Your light must be defiant. Do not use amber or cyan light, as you will become camouflaged with the environment, making you invisible to potential rescues and confusing your own sense of space. Use a stark, clinical white or a high-contrast green.

*   **Primary:** A high-output, rechargeable headlamp with multiple brightness settings and a locked-off "max" mode. The battery pack should be separate, worn inside your jacket to keep it warm and efficient.
*   **Secondary:** A durable, waterproof LED torch with a mechanical (not electronic) switch. This is your backup for when the headlamp's battery dies.
*   **Tertiary:** At least a dozen 12-hour chemical light sticks (green or blue). These are for marking a path during a prolonged blackout or for an emergency, heatless, and power-source-free light source.
*   **Emergency:** A single, high-intensity strobe beacon. Use it only to signal for exfiltration at a pre-arranged point and time. Its use will attract unwanted attention.

### **Section 3: The Sustenance & Tools Kit - Prolonging the Anomaly**

#### **A. Respiration & Hydration**

*   **Air:** The heavy fog is likely saturated with microscopic metal particles, lubricants, and who knows what else. A full-face respirator with P100 (or better) particulate filters is mandatory. It will also have the secondary benefit of pre-warming and moistening the air you breathe, fending off the deep chill.
*   **Water:** The water is *conceptually* pure (it's condensation) but *practically* contaminated. Do not drink it unfiltered. You will need a portable, pump-activated or gravity-fed multi-stage water filter capable of removing heavy metals and industrial chemicals. Collect water from the weeping Pylons or the shallow basins—it is less likely to have surface oil slicks.
*   **Food:** High-calorie, high-fat, zero-prep rations. Think calorie-dense energy bars, pemmican, olive oil pouches. Your body will burn calories just to stay warm. No cooking. A flame is an unpredictable variable around unknown gases and lubricants.

#### **B. Interaction & Navigation Tools**

*   **Tether Line:** A 200-meter spool of high-tensile, thin-gauge Dyneema or monofilament line. Use this to tether yourself to a known point (your entry/exit location) when you first arrive. Use it to mark your path in the near-total darkness. It is your only guarantee against being completely lost.
*   **Inertial Navigation:** A wrist-mounted or chest-mounted pedometer with a 3-axis gyroscope and accelerometers. GPS is useless. Satellites don't exist. You will be navigating by dead reckoning, mapping your turns, steps, and elevation changes (climbing on deck plates) on a ruggedized datapad.
*   **Multi-Tool:** A heavy-duty, industrial-grade multi-tool with pliers, wire cutters, and a file. You may need to cut a stray wire, pry a deck plate seam, or create a small anchor point.
*   **Acoustic Sensor:** A parabolic dish or directional "shotgun" microphone that can plug into your headphones. This is for long-range surveillance and mapping the soundscape, allowing you to identify the nature and direction of distant phenomena.
*   **Non-Reflective Marking Chalk:** For leaving temporary, non-permanent, and non-reflective marks on the deck plates to indicate danger or points of interest.

### **Section 4: The Protocol - How to Live as a Glitch**

Your behavior is your final layer of defense.

1.  **Movement:** Move deliberately and slowly. The machine's processes are vast; they have inertia. You can often predict an event by listening to its build-up. Plan your movements to coincide with the machine's "rest" states. Do not run. A running organic shape is a primary anomaly for a drone's sensory suite. Stick to the seams of the deck plates; they offer better grip and are less exposed to open space.
2.  **Cover and Concealment:** There is no natural cover. Use the shadows of the Pylons and the massive cabling conduits. Stay low. Your silhouette against the faint grid-like shadows from the roof or the faint glow of the bio-moss can give you away. When a light array fails ahead of you, stop. Do not enter the sudden darkness; wait for it to return or find an alternate route. That void is a separate, unknown kind of danger.
3.  **Encounter Management (The Fauna):**
    *   **Maintenance Drones:** They run on subroutines. If you see one, freeze. Their motion-detection is likely keyed to rapid movement. Do not shine your light on their optical sensors. Allow them to pass. They are not hunting you; they are performing a task, and you are simply an obstacle in their path. If they approach directly, you have triggered an "obstacle removal" protocol. Your only chance is to find a seam or a hatch to slip into, or to throw a light source away from you to distract their target acquisition.
    *   **Amalgams:** Give them an extremely wide berth. They are not on a predictable path. They are consuming waste energy and growing. They are a form of life that has integrated with the machine's logic. They do not perceive you as a traveler from another world; they may perceive you as a source of raw material (heat, electricity from your gear, organic matter). Do not provoke them. Do not touch them. Observation only, from a great distance.
4.  **Industrial Events:**
    *   **Coolant Burst (Flash Flood):** Sounds like a high-picked whine rapidly escalating to a scream. This is preceded by a sudden drop in temperature. Seek higher ground immediately. Climb onto a pylon base conduit or get as far from the low-lying basins as possible.
    *   **System-wide Temperature Spike (Boiling Mist):** The ambient groan will deepen into a rattle, and the fog will go from a rolling river to a violent, boiling cloud. Visibility will drop to zero. Temperatures can become dangerously hot and humid. This is temporary. Seal your clothing, breathe through your filter, and wait it out.
    *   **Power Surge (Blinding Glare):** A loud *thump* and flicker across all lights signals a surge. A few seconds later, there may be a blinding flash as systems dump excess energy. If you can, look away or cover your eyes. This will be followed by either a return to normalcy or a total blackout in the affected area. Be prepared for either.

Exfiltration is timed and pre-planned. You will not be "calling for a ride." Your window will be brief. When the signal comes, move directly toward the extraction point. Do not be tempted to explore, to investigate a fascinating sound, or to secure a "sample." The Sump will keep you. It is active. It is hungry for anomalies to process into normalcy. Remember: you are a glitch in the system. The system is designed to correct glitches. Good luck.
