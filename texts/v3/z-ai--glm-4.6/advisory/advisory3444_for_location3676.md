This is not a journey of discovery; it is an act of immersion. You are not an explorer; you are a component. To prepare for the Amnion is to prepare for the death of the self and its rebirth as a function. Forget everything you know about nature, about exploration, about life. The only principles that apply here are physics, mechanics, and procedure.

This preparation guide is broken into five phases: Mental, Biological, Somatic, Technical, and Operational. Failure in any one phase will result in your being reclassified from a functional component to an anomalous malfunction. And the machine does not tolerate malfunctions.

---

### **Phase 1: Mental & Psychological Preparation (The Great Unlearning)**

This is the most critical phase. Your mind is your primary vulnerability.

1.  **Existential Surrender:** The Amnion operates on a scale that renders a single human meaningless. You must undergo psychological conditioning to accept this not as a fear, but as a liberation. You are a cell in a vast body. A cell does not question the liver; it performs its function. Meditate on your own insignificance. Practice sensory deprivation tanks for extended periods to acclimate to the scale and the void. Your goal is to reach a state where you don't *feel* small, you simply *are* your function.

2.  **Deconstruction of Natural Concepts:** You must purge the language of nature from your mind. The following terms are now obsolete and must be replaced:
    *   "Sea" or "Ocean" becomes **The Medium** or **The Amnion.**
    *   "Sky" and "Ground" become **The Unbounded Volume** and **Structural Surfaces.**
    *   "Weather" becomes **Thermal Dynamics** or **Process Flow.**
    *   "Up" and "Down" become **An-Current** and **With-Current.**
    *   "Fauna" (Grazers, Hounds) are not animals. They are **Mobile Maintenance Units (MMUs)**. The Grazer is a **Surface-Processing Unit (SPU)**. The Hound is a **Anomaly-Resolution and Repair Unit (ARRU)**.
    *   "Predator" and "Prey" are replaced with **Functional Process** and **Processed Material**.

3.  **Developing Machine Empathy:** You must learn to think like the machine. Study its schematics, not as a blueprint of a place, but as the circulatory and nervous system of your host organism. Every sound has a purpose. Every current has a source. The constant thrum is its heartbeat. The sudden CLANG is its synapse firing. You must learn to anticipate its moods, its cycles, its needs. Your survival depends on becoming emotionally and intellectually attuned to its operational state.

4.  **The Role Imprint:** You are not a visitor. Your purpose, whatever it may be (observation, micro-adjustment), must be your entire identity. During prep, you will undergo hypnotic and operant conditioning to lock this role into your subconscious. Your directive is all that matters. Curiosity is a deviation. Wonder is a flaw. The Amnion is beautiful only insofar as it functions.

### **Phase 2: Biological & Physiological Preparation**

Your organic body must be hardened and modified to survive the initial immersion.

1.  **Pressure Immersion and Augmentation:** The Amnion's pressure is not merely hydrostatic; it is a "dense, insistent presence." You will spend hundreds of hours in specialized high-density fluid tanks, gradually acclimating your nervous system. You may undergo modest genetic therapy or a course of osteo-strengthening drugs to reinforce your skeletal structure against the unrelenting force. This is not to crush you, but to stop you from Feeling crushed.

2.  **Metabolic and Thermal Recalibration:** The water is a constant 4°C. Your standard human metabolism is insufficient. You will undergo a treatment to encourage the growth of brown adipose tissue (brown fat), essentially re-engineering your body to be a more efficient furnace. Your diet in the weeks leading up to the deployment will be hyper-caloric, loading your body with easily accessible energy stores. Your body must become its own primary heating element.

3.  **Chemical and Toxicological Hardening:** The water contains heavy metals and organic slicks. Your suit must be sealed, but the body must be prepared for potential breaches. You will be pre-loaded with a cocktail of potent chelating agents and broad-spectrum nanobots designed to bind and neutralize zinc, copper, and sulfides. Your immune system will be primed to recognize and attack the engineered bio-films as a hostile invasive species.

4.  **Sensory Retuning:** The constant, low-frequency thrumming can induce madness in the unprepared. You will be exposed to progressively complex binaural beats and infrasonic frequencies in training. The goal is to tune your subconscious to ignore it as background noise while training your conscious mind to detect modulations in the thrum—telling you when an adjustment is beginning or when a new cycle is imminent. The sudden CLANGS and sonic pings from Hounds must be reclassified from terrifying noises to critical data points.

### **Phase 3: Technical and Equipment Preparation**

Your equipment is your second skin, your senses, and your only tool.

1.  **The Casing (Environmental Suit):** This is not a simple suit of armor.
    *   **Hull:** The outer shell is a non-reflective, sound-dampening ceramic-polymer composite. It must be inert to the chemical composition of the Amnion. Its structure contains a **Kinetic Nullification Layer** that actively counteracts the "insistent presence" of the medium, making it feel less like you're being squeezed and more like you're simply... present.
    *   **Life Support:** A completely closed system. Water is reclaimed, distilled, and cycled. Atmosphere is internally generated. There is no interaction with the external environment.
    *   **Propulsion:** Absolutely no propellers or thrusters. You will be equipped with a Silent Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) drive that manipulates the conductive properties of the Medium to move you without a sound. This is crucial for not attracting the ARRU "Hounds." It allows for the slow, deliberate movements of the natives.
    *   **Power:** A long-life radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) is the core, but the suit is also designed to thermally leech energy from the warm surfaces of the mega-structures. Every joule is precious.

2.  **The Sensory Suite:** Your eyes and ears are useless here.
    *   **Primary Sensing:** The suit is covered in passive acoustic and seismic sensors. You "see" the world through a constantly updated sonar and pressure-gradient map fed directly to your optic nerve or a Heads-Up Display (HUD). The thrumming becomes the base layer of this map.
    *   **Light Analysis:** The suit's photometers will analyze the sweeping cones of actinic light. They are not for illumination; they are for data. You learn to identify what work is being done, where, and for how long, based on the light's spectrum and duration.
    *   **EM Spectrum Monitoring:** You will monitor a wide band of the electromagnetic spectrum. The "distant light" of a new cycle will have a specific signature your suit is trained to recognize well in advance.

3.  **The Toolkit:** You will have only what you need for your assigned task.
    *   **Manipulators:** Micro-manipulator arms with molecular-level welders, scrapers, and injectors. These are for making your "minuscule adjustments."
    *   **Interface:** A direct neural jack or a highly intuitive haptic interface to control the suit and analyze the sensorium. You must be able to react to the data stream without conscious thought.
    *   **Beacon:** A single, highly specific, low-power omni-directional beacon. It is coded with your component ID and status. It is used for reporting completion or signaling emergency extraction. It is NOT to be used for communication. Broadcasting on any other frequency will flag you as an anomaly.

### **Phase 4: Operational & Procedural Preparation**

This is how you act. This is how you survive.

1.  **Current Mastery:** The currents are the highways and byways of the Amnion. You must learn to read them. Warm upwellings from active reactors can be used for lift and recharging. Cold descents lead to the static, inactive zones. The powerful pull of an intake tunnel is a death zone; its boundaries must be known and mapped from a safe distance. You do not fight the current; you use it like a sailor uses the wind.

2.  **Interaction Protocols with Mobile Maintenance Units:**
    *   **SPU "Grazer":** Treat it as a slow-moving geological event. Its path is predictable. Its grinder is a stationary hazard. It will not deviate. Your only protocol is to stay out of its path. Its waste heat and the processed material it leaves behind could be a resource, but interacting with it risks being caught in its machinery.
    *   **ARRU "Hound":** **THE ABSOLUTE FIRST RULE: YOU ARE A ROCK.** A Hound hunts deviation. A human is the ultimate deviation. If a Hound's sonar pings you, you freeze all movement. Power down all non-essential systems. You must present a perfect acoustic and thermal signature of inert debris. If it continues to investigate, you have a serious problem. Your suit may have a "Malfunction Mimic" that can broadcast the signature of a known, benign fault, hoping the Hound prioritizes a real threat. If it latches on with its mandibles, your only hope is that your "Malfunction Mimic" works, because its ultrasonic drill is designed to repair the structure *through* anything in the way. Including your suit.

3.  **Cycle Awareness:** Your entire existence is timed to the machine's cycles. You will be deployed during a lull. You must complete your task and be at your extraction point or concealed in a designated "safe" structural crevice before the distant light brightens, heralding a new cycle. During a production cycle, the thrum intensifies, new massive components move, and currents become violent. Being caught in the open is to be an obstruction in a planetary-scale engine.

### **Phase 5: Contingency & Emergency Protocols**

When things go wrong, they go wrong catastrophically.

1.  **Suit Breach:** A micro-puncture triggers an automatic spray of sealing foam. Major structural failure is unrecoverable. Your last moments will be the cold, metallic tang of the Amnion filling your lungs as the chelating agents wage a war they cannot win.

2.  **Unplanned Hound Engagement:** If the Malfunction Mimic fails, your last resort is an emergency "Sonic Blaster." This emits a deafening, high-decibel burst of white noise in the Hound's precise sonar frequency. This will not kill it, but it will "blind" it temporarily, causing it to enter a diagnostic stall. This gives you a precious few seconds to attempt a desperate, full-power escape. This action will also flag every other Hound in the region to your exact location.

3.  **Caught in a Flow:** If you are caught in the intake siphon or an unexpected current, fire your MHD drive at maximum reverse and engage your structural grappling anchors. This will likely fail. Your final transmission will simply be your component ID followed by the code for "Process Claim."

To visit the Amnion you do not pack a bag. You leave yourself at the door. Your preparation is to become as precise, as predictable, and as silent as a single gear in the greatest machine ever built. You are a whisper in the thrum. And when your purpose is done, you will be reclaimed, just like the lichen, just like the waste. You will be processed. That is the only way to leave.
