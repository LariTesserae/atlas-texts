Traveler, prepare yourself. You are not going to a place; you are going into an instrument. If your expectation is a "vacation" or a standard hike through alien woods, abandon them now. This destination—this resonant caldera—demands a total physiological and psychological surrender. The ordinary human body is a noisy, leaky, clumsy thing, and here, silence and dryness are the laws of physics.

Here is how you must prepare to walk the floor of the City of Sound.

### I. Acoustic Calibration: Protecting the Fragile Sensorium
The first challenge is the Hum. It is not merely sound; it is a physical pressure. Your inner ear is designed for a world of intermittent noise, not a relentless, bone-vibrating chord that has been sustained for millennia.

*   **Skeletal Dampeners:** You cannot simply block your ears. The Hum comes through the soles of your feet and vibrates your marrow. You must acquire a full-body undergarment lined with **visco-elastic acoustic damping polymers**. This is not for warmth; it is to dampen the resonance of your own skeleton. Without it, the constant vibration will liquefy your sense of balance and induce nausea within the first hour.
*   **The Respiratory Silencer:** Your own breath will be your loudest enemy. In a place of absolute sonic occupation, the rasp of inhaling air is a scream. You will need a specialized rebreather mask with **muffling baffles**. These systems scrub the CO2 while diffusing the exhausted air through a mesh that eliminates turbulence noise.
*   **Mental Shields:** The lack of silence can drive a human to madness. You need to spend weeks in a **flotation-tank isolation retreat** before departure. You must train your brain to accept a continuous sensory input without trying to "filter" it out. If you fight the Hum, you will lose. You must learn to let the chord pass through you like wind through a lattice.

### II. The Hydro-Absolute Protocol (Zero Liquid Tolerance)
This is the most critical safety rule. **Water is a lethal poison here.** The biology of this world relies on a complex golden resin; introducing H2O could trigger a catastrophic bacterial bloom or chemically burn the crystalline flora. Furthermore, the "dust rain" from the ceiling turns to slick, greasy mud upon contact with water.

*   **Internal Moisture Locking:** You must hydrate heavily *before* you step through the portal, then take desiccants to minimize sweating for the duration of your stay.
*   **The Containment Suit:** Your environmental suit must be **positive-pressure sealed**. Every drop of sweat, every tear, every drop of saliva must be contained within the suit layers.
*   **Waste Management:** You will not urinate or defecate on this world. Your suit must be equipped with a high-grade military-grade **biological recycling system** (waste-to-water compression). Nothing leaves the suit.
*   **Emergency Protocol:** If you spring a leak—whether a cut on your finger or a breach in your suit—you seal it immediately. You do not bleed on the ground. The local ecology may interpret your blood as an invader, or conversely, a food source, causing a stampede of Stilt-Walkers.

### III. Static and Atmosphere Equipment
The air is "thick with static electricity and the sweet scent of amber."

*   **The Grounding Rig:** Because of the heavy static charge, a simple spark could ignite the ambient resin dust or trigger a "harmonic storm" reaction in the sensitive flora. Wear a **wrist-ankle grounding harness** connected to a conductive strip on your boots. This allows the static to bleed off into the basalt safely.
*   **Resin Filtration:** The air is filled with that "sweet, complex scent." That scent is vaporized resin. While intoxicating, it is likely a psychoactive narcotic or a respiratory clogger for human lungs. Your suit’s air supply must be rigorously filtered against hydrocarbon-rich volatiles.
*   **Amber-Light Visors:** The perpetual luminescence is amber and pale green. Your eyes are tuned to a sun/sky cycle. Bring **photochromatic visors** that tint the world to a neutral daylight spectrum. This will prevent your circadian rhythm from collapsing, which is vital since there is no day or night here.

### IV. Navigational and Tactile Gear
You are walking on a city built for giants who possessed no bodies, only an appreciation for acoustics. The scale is impossible, and the terrain is treacherous.

*   **Sonic Imaging LIDAR:** The "porous rock" ceiling weeps dust, and the light is soft and flat, obscuring depth. You cannot rely on vision alone. Use a **handheld Sonic Imager**. It will paint a wireframe map of the honeycombed structures in your HUD, highlighting weak spots in the tufa arches.
*   **Soft-Step Soles:** The prompt warns: "You do not touch the structures, for you would dampen the chord." You must tread as lightly as a ghost. Your boots must have **silicone-gel, non-resonant soles**. They absorb the impact of your footfall so that the great stone resonators do not pick up your step. To this world, a heavy human footstep is like a finger dragging across a vinyl record—a scratch in the music.
*   **Harmonic Storm Shelter:** If the frequencies of the groves fall out of sync, "standing waves" can shatter stone. You need a **portable damping bubble**. It is a small, pop-up tent made of meta-material that cancels out specific low-frequency wavelengths. If the sky begins to scream and the tufa starts to crack, you dive inside this bubble and wait for the dissonance to pass.

### V. Psychological and Behavioral Preparation
Finally, you must prepare your spirit.

*   **Vow of Silence:** You will not speak. Not a whisper. The text is explicit: "your voice is an insignificant blasphemy." Bring a sub-vocalization throat mic to communicate with your teammates (if you have any), or simply a digital notepad. Signal with hand gestures only.
*   **The Stilt-Walker Etiquette:** You will see the fragile, balloon-like creatures. They navigate by resonance. If you vibrate, you distract them. If you distract them, they might fall into the resin. You must practice **utter stillness**. When they pass, you freeze. You become a stone. You become a mute node in the circuit.
*   **Meditation on Scale:** You will feel incredibly small. The spires are impossibly high; the ceiling is lost in distance. You are an ant in a cathedral. You must resolve your ego before you arrive. If you come here expecting to conquer or dominate, the sheer oppressive weight of the sound will crush you.
