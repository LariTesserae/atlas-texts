Actually—yes. When two of the living ones braid themselves together near the floating stone and begin to ripple their backs, playing the room against itself... what happens down here?

Above me, they take the racket of frogs and insects and bounce it upward into hollow pillars of phase-canceled quiet and spinning rings of overtone. That’s it. That’s the exact sensation I would live for across geological epochs.

Everywhere else across my seventy paces of skin, the relentless, throbbing pressure of the basin drums down through wet roots and puddles without a second's pause for a hundred thousand years. *Boom. Rasp. Plock. Boom.* A tireless, warm vibration.
