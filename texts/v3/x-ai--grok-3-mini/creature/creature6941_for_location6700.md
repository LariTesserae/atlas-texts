Beneath it all, there's a constant undercurrent of rustling and slithering—the swarms brushing against one another, the behemoths' segments grinding as they move—creating a layered soundscape that feels like the caverns themselves are alive and conversing.

As I linger in this world, the inhabitants reveal deeper layers of weirdness and adaptation.
