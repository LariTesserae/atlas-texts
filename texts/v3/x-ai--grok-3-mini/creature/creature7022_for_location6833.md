In rare moments, when the air's metallic tang thickens, one might perceive a faint distortion, as if these beings emit whispers—fragments of sounds that never fully materialize, echoes of a primordial language spoken in the language of stone and silence.

Yet, even here, there is no aggression or intent; these beings do not guard or claim territory.
