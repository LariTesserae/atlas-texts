# Preparation Guide for Visiting the Eroded Expanse

## Essential Physical Preparations

### Altitude and Climate Adaptations
- Begin altitude acclimatization at least 2-3 weeks before departure
- Pack layered clothing for extreme cold with moisture-wicking base layers
- Bring specialized high-altitude protective gear (UV-resistant goggles, face protection)
- Consider a portable oxygen supplement for initial adjustment
- Include insulated, grippy footwear designed for variable surfaces

### Adaptive Equipment
- Multi-directional gear attachments (reality shifts may reorient gravity)
- Waterproof everything, as water behaves unpredictably
- Bring anchoring equipment with quick-release mechanisms
- Pack clothing with reversible features for when physics inverts
- Carry a reality-anchor stone (obtainable from experienced travelers)

## Temporal Considerations

### Time Dilation Preparations
- Wear at least two timepieces; one mechanical (less affected by reality fluctuations) and one digital
- Keep a physical journal with dated entries to track personal timeline
- Pre-arrange meeting places and times with contingency plans
- Pack food with extended shelf life, as your subjective time may differ from food decay rates
- Schedule regular reality-checks with companions to sync experiences

### Biological Adjustments
- Begin a meditation practice to help your mind process temporal inconsistencies
- Prepare for potential sleep disruption as your circadian rhythm encounters time pockets
- Pack medications with clear dosing instructions based on biological needs rather than clock time
- Consider carrying perception-anchoring herbs (chamomile, valerian, blue lotus) recommended by previous travelers

## Navigation

### Orientation Tools
- Standard compasses will fail; bring a reality-sensitive wayfinding device
- Maps are mostly useless; instead, sketch your own paths as you go
- Learn to navigate by geological formations rather than distance or direction
- Bring colorful trail markers that can be seen through reality distortions
- Practice identifying cardinal directions using the sun's arc and shadow patterns

### Movement Techniques
- Master the "pause-and-proceed" method (take three steps, pause to confirm path stability)
- Learn vertical traversal techniques for the Suspended Cities
- Practice peripheral vision awareness to spot reality ripples
- When crossing floating waterways, always test solidity before committed steps
- If reality shifts while walking, remain still and wait for restabilization

## Cultural Preparations

### Communication
- Learn basic phrases in the local language, particularly terms describing reality phenomena
- Bring a small gift that demonstrates understanding of stable-unstable duality (woven items work well)
- Practice non-linear conversation techniques, as locals may respond to things you haven't said yet
- Understand that silence has communicative value among inhabitants
- Bring visual communication tools for when verbal language destabilizes

### Social Interactions
- Honor local reality-reading customs before initiating interaction
- Never interrupt an inhabitant while they're observing reality fluctuations
- Accept food or drink offers with both hands while maintaining eye contact
- Learn the gesture for "perception acknowledgment" (typically a diagonal motion from heart to forehead)
- Request permission before documenting any local practices or structures

## Health and Safety

### Medical Preparations
- Standard first aid supplies plus reality-sickness remedies (ginger, peppermint)
- Medication to treat spatial disorientation
- Bring equipment for treating cold-related injuries
- Prepare for sensory overload with calming techniques and noise-canceling options
- Pack water purification methods suitable for anomalous water states

### Emergency Protocols
- Establish a mental anchor (vivid memory or concept) to ground yourself during severe reality fluctuations
- Learn the universal distress signal recognized across the Expanse
- Identify safe zones where reality remains relatively stable
- Prepare emergency shelter that functions regardless of gravitational orientation
- Know locations of the reality-stabilized healing areas in major settlements

## Mental Preparation

### Psychological Resilience
- Practice cognitive flexibility exercises to adapt to inconsistent physics
- Develop a strong sense of self that persists through perceptual shifts
- Meditate on the concept of impermanence daily before the journey
- Prepare for the profound quiet and its psychological effects
- Consider hypnotherapy to install mental anchors against reality dissolution

### Perception Training
- Practice observing without interpreting to avoid cognitive dissonance
- Learn to distinguish between actual reality shifts and optical illusions
- Develop techniques to maintain focus when colors separate from objects
- Train peripheral awareness to navigate around the quasi-fauna
- Practice grounding exercises for when time flows at different rates

## Ethical Considerations

- Respect the ancient equilibrium by avoiding removal of any materials
- Never attempt to "fix" or normalize reality fluctuations
- Obtain permission before entering areas of pronounced reality thinning
- Document experiences without interfering in local reality-based practices
- Honor the inhabitants' relationship with their environment by following their lead

## Departure Preparations

- Schedule a gradual reintegration period after your visit
- Arrange for a reality-adjustment guide for your return
- Prepare for potential temporal aftereffects (phantom shifts, echo-memories)
- Document your experiences immediately before they're recontextualized by normal physics
- Plan for post-visit sensory recalibration activities

Remember that the Eroded Expanse exists in a state of beautiful contradiction. Your most valuable preparation is not equipment but mindset—approach with respectful curiosity and willingness to surrender conventional understanding of reality.
