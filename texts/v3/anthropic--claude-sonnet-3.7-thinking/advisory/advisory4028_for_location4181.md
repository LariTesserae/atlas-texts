# Visitor's Preparation Guide: The Undercity of Calderum

## Essential Physical Preparation

Begin heat acclimation at least 3 weeks before your journey. Spend progressively longer periods in sauna-like conditions (95-105°F/35-40°C) until you can comfortably endure 2-3 hours. Your body must adapt to perpetual sweating without dehydration.

Strengthen your grip and upper body for the initial descent ladder and subsequent vertical navigation. Practice climbing while wearing gloves to simulate the conditions of gripping hot metal rungs.

Develop your night vision by spending time in low-light conditions. Your eyes will need to adjust to the dim, inconsistent illumination of Calderum.

## Necessary Equipment

### Clothing:
- Heat-resistant coveralls with ventilation panels (aramid fiber or treated cotton)
- Multiple thin moisture-wicking undergarments for layering/changing
- Leather or heat-resistant synthetic gloves with silicone grip enhancement
- Sturdy boots with metal-protected toes and heat-resistant soles
- Head covering that protects from dripping condensation while allowing heat to escape
- Respiratory mask with replaceable filters for foundry areas

### Hydration & Nutrition:
- Insulated water container (minimum 3 liters capacity)
- Electrolyte powder supplements (critical for prolonged sweating)
- Salt tablets to prevent hyponatremia
- Dehydrated, energy-dense rations that won't spoil in heat

### Tools:
- Headlamp with adjustable brightness and red-light option
- Secondary light source (hand-crank powered)
- Heat-resistant timepiece (locals track time independently from surface)
- Small metal cup for collecting water from communal sources
- Notebook with water-resistant pages (paper works better than electronics in the heat and humidity)
- Chalk or removable markers for navigation
- Small toolkit with basic wrenches and screwdrivers (useful as currency)

## Navigation & Cultural Awareness

Memorize the main gallery layout before descending. The three-dimensional nature of Calderum makes maps challenging, but know the positions of major landmarks:
- The Eastern Water Channel (lifeblood of the community)
- The Foundry Quarter (hottest region, avoid during work cycles)
- The Hanging Gardens (rare respite, observe only)
- The Crystal Chambers (never mention them directly to residents)

Learn basic hand signals used by workers—particularly emergency warnings and water-rationing indicators. Practice the rhythmic tapping sequences that communicate simple messages through the stone.

Timekeeping is complex. Each sector operates on independent schedules unrelated to surface day/night cycles. Upon arrival, identify which cycle your destination area follows. Never assume a quiet area is unoccupied—it may simply be in its designated sleep period.

## Health & Safety Precautions

Maintain constant hydration awareness. Begin hydrating heavily 24 hours before descent and drink small amounts regularly rather than large quantities infrequently. Your urine should never darken beyond pale yellow.

Treat any cut or abrasion immediately. The warm, humid environment accelerates infection, and the local microbial ecosystem differs from surface varieties.

Plan rest periods. The combination of heat, constant climbing, and poor air quality creates faster fatigue than anticipated. Allow double the time you think necessary for any journey within Calderum.

If you experience dizziness, irregular heartbeat, or cessation of sweating, seek immediate assistance. These are signs of dangerous heat-related illnesses that can progress rapidly.

## Cultural Etiquette

- Never waste water, not even a drop. Observe how locals use condensation collection cloths to wipe sweat and then wring it out for reuse.

- Offer skills before currency. Calderum values practical abilities over surface money.

- Respect the silence. When conversations occur, keep your voice low and conversations brief.

- Never stare at or comment on the glowing crystalline formations. Their presence is acknowledged through avoidance rather than discussion.

- Bring small, practical gifts for hosts—heat-resistant tools, specialized lubricants for machinery, or unusual seeds for the fungal gardens are all appreciated.

## Final Considerations

The Undercity of Calderum operates as a nearly closed system. Your presence disrupts its careful balance. Consume minimal resources, contribute where possible, and understand that your visit is an imposition that must be offset through respect and awareness.

Plan your exit before your entrance. Physical and mental exhaustion will be at their peak when it's time to climb back to the surface. Ensure you reserve strength for the ascent and have a surface contact aware of your expected return time.

Remember: Calderum wasn't built for visitors—it was built for survival. Approach it with the humility this deserves.
