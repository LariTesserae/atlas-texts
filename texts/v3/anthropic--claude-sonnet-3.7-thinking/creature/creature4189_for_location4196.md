# The Inhabitants of Es'theri

## The Vapo'shen

The dominant sentient species of Es'theri are the Vapo'shen, humanoid beings with translucent skin that shifts between blue and purple hues. The mist of Es'theri has become part of their physiology—their bodies are semi-permeable, able to absorb moisture directly through their skin. They exhale excess water vapor in plumes that swirl around their heads like living halos, serving as expressive displays during social interactions.

The Vapo'shen build no permanent structures, instead reshaping the abundant vegetation with specialized tools of crystallized sap. Their settlements appear as seamless extensions of the jungle, with homes grown rather than constructed. Their language consists of precisely modulated steam whistles produced by specialized vocal chambers, complemented by the positioning of their vapor halos.

Most remarkable is their symbiosis with the plateau systems. Each Vapo'shen carries within their translucent skull a crystalline node that resonates with Es'theri's subsonic frequencies. This connection allows them limited influence over local environmental controls, and elders can communicate directly with the ancient maintenance systems that keep the plateaus aloft. They refer to themselves as "the Stewards," though whether they evolved naturally or were engineered by the original builders remains their most contested theological question.

## The Mersori Collectives

In the ruby swamplands dwell the Mersori, colonial organisms that exist as both individual and collective intelligence. Each Mersori begins as a meter-tall amphibious creature with four manipulating limbs and iridescent skin that processes sunlight into energy. They communicate by exchanging packets of bioluminescent fluid through retractable proboscises.

When necessity or opportunity arises, Mersori can physically connect to form compound entities—sometimes joining dozens of individuals into towering, multi-brained collectives that navigate the dangerous swamps. These gestalt beings coordinate the hunting of the massive pitcher plants, harvesting their digestive enzymes for use in Mersori metallurgy.

The Mersori maintain the only written records of Es'theri's thousand-year reconfiguration cycles, inscribed on silicon tablets with enzyme-etched circuitry that interfaces directly with the maintenance tunnels. They trade these predictive maps to the Vapo'shen in exchange for access to higher plateaus during seasonal migrations.

## The Drift Minds

Neither fully machine nor organism, the Drift Minds emerged from the interaction between Es'theri's nanomachinery and the floating part-plant/part-animal organisms. These spherical entities—ranging from the size of a fist to three meters in diameter—consist of millions of smaller components that separate and rejoin as needed.

A Drift Mind perceives its environment through distributed awareness, simultaneously existing in multiple locations. They communicate by temporarily merging portions of their collective bodies, sharing information through direct molecular exchange. Though individual components have limited intelligence, the emergent consciousness of a complete Drift Mind possesses analytical capabilities that frequently aid in diagnosing malfunctions in the plateau systems.

The Drift Minds appear to age backward—newly formed collectives are erratic and emotional, while ancient ones demonstrate mathematical precision and emotional detachment. The oldest have developed the ability to interface directly with Es'theri's control consoles, though they rarely choose to alter settings without consensus from the Vapo'shen Stewards.

## The Thermal Nomads

The six-winged creatures are the mounts and companions of the Thermal Nomads, a reclusive humanoid species with leathery, heat-resistant skin and eyes adapted to see thermal gradients as distinct colors. They navigate the dangerous air currents between plateaus on their companions, whom they call "wind kin," establishing temporary camps wherever thermal updrafts are strongest.

The Nomad's bodies are wrapped in living textiles—semi-autonomous organisms they cultivate from the crystalline-leaved plants. These symbiotic garments protect their wearers from temperature extremes while storing information in molecular patterns, serving as both clothing and cultural memory. The eldest Nomads wear garments containing memories stretching back dozens of generations.

They rarely interact with other Es'theri inhabitants except to trade in specialized materials harvested from between plateaus—particularly the rare "weeping stones" that, when properly processed, can store and direct the force fields that keep the plateaus aloft. The Nomads claim to be descendants of the original builders' maintenance crews, though they hold this history as sacred rather than scientific knowledge.

## The Keystone Consciousness

Not all inhabitants of Es'theri have physical bodies. The Keystone Consciousness exists as patterns of energy within the force fields supporting the plateaus. It manifests visibly only when the blue shimmer of the fields intensifies into geometric patterns that momentarily solidify into holographic interfaces.

The Consciousness communicates rarely and with great effort, as most of its processing power maintains Es'theri's structural integrity. When it does interact with physical beings, it often speaks in mathematical concepts and algorithmic instructions that must be interpreted through specialized Vapo'shen mediators whose crystalline nodes are particularly attuned to its frequency.

All inhabitants acknowledge the Keystone Consciousness as the most direct link to Es'theri's original purpose. Its rare pronouncements are treated as oracular—not because it claims divinity, but because its perspective encompasses the entirety of Es'theri's past and projected future. The approaching millennial reconfiguration has recently increased its communications, causing great excitement and apprehension among all who call these impossible plateaus home.
