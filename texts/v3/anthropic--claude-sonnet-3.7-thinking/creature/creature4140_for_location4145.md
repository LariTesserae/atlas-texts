# The Inhabitants of the Resonance Plains

## The Harmonists

The dominant sentient species of the Resonance Plains call themselves the Harmonists, though this is a simplified translation of their true name, which exists as a complex pattern of subharmonic frequencies impossible to render in conventional language. Descended from human colonists who arrived millennia ago, they have evolved both naturally and through deliberate augmentation to become something altogether different.

Physically, Harmonists stand taller than baseline humans, averaging seven feet in height, with elongated limbs and torsos that appear almost too slender to support their frames. Their skin contains millions of specialized chromatophores that subtly shift color in response to the Resonators' emissions—a biological oscilloscope displaying their emotional and physiological state. Most striking are their ears, which have evolved into elaborate, fractal-patterned structures that spiral outward from their heads like delicate coral formations. These auditory organs can physically reconfigure to filter, amplify, or dampen specific frequency ranges from the overwhelming sonic environment.

Their vocal apparatus has similarly transformed. Harmonists possess multiple voice-producing organs—not just larynges but specialized resonance chambers throughout their chest cavities that allow them to produce up to six distinct tones simultaneously. When speaking their native tongue, called Resonant, a single Harmonist sounds like a small choir, their layered voices creating harmonic patterns that convey meaning through both linguistic and mathematical relationships between sounds.

Harmonist society is organized into acoustic castes called "Tonalities." A Harmonist's Tonality is determined at birth by measuring the precise harmonic signature their body naturally resonates with. This signature, called one's "Fundamental," remains constant throughout their life and determines their social role, aptitudes, and affinities. The major Tonalities include:

- The **Amplifiers**, who govern and administer Nova Tympanum, their bodies naturally resonating with the same frequencies the Resonators use for data transmission
- The **Modulators**, who work as engineers and artisans, physically manipulating the sound waves that serve as both energy and information
- The **Attenuators**, who specialize in healing and biological sciences, able to dampen harmful resonance patterns in living tissue
- The **Oscillators**, who serve as explorers and rangers, venturing into the deepest parts of the Plains to study anomalous harmonic events
- The **Harmonics**, the scholars and philosophers whose minds can process the most complex frequency relationships

Rather than family units, Harmonists organize themselves into "Chords"—groups of individuals whose Fundamentals form harmonious mathematical relationships. Children are raised communally within their Chord, educated according to their Tonality's traditions and requirements. The concept of individual ownership is largely foreign to them; instead, they recognize usage rights determined by harmonic compatibility. A Harmonist cannot "own" a dwelling or tool—they can only be recognized as the entity whose Fundamental most perfectly complements that item's resonance pattern.

The oldest and most revered Harmonists undergo a process called "Dissolution," where their physical forms are gradually converted into pure acoustic energy. These elders literally become living sound waves, abandoning corporeal existence to join what they call the "Greater Composition"—a collective consciousness that exists within the harmonic field generated by the Resonators. These Dissolved Elders can still communicate with physical Harmonists during special ceremonies held at harmonic convergence points beneath the Resonators.

## The Engineered Beings

Alongside the Harmonists live several distinct species of engineered beings, created long ago for specific purposes but now fully integrated into Plains society:

The **Resonant Constructs** stand nearly nine feet tall, their bodies composed of a crystalline matrix that perfectly conducts sonic energy. Originally designed as maintenance workers for the Resonators, they have developed their own culture centered around what they call "structural harmony"—the belief that physical forms should mirror acoustic patterns. Their settlements, built in the shadows of the smaller Resonators, are architectural marvels that physically reconfigure themselves during different harmonic cycles. Constructs don't speak; instead, they vibrate their entire bodies to produce complex sound patterns. Their thoughts are literally transparent—visible as rippling frequency patterns moving through their crystalline forms.

The **Chord Tenders** are smaller beings, rarely exceeding four feet in height, with bodies that appear partially liquid—their silvery skin flowing and reforming as they move. Created to care for the circular pools, they have an innate sense of vibrational mathematics. Chord Tenders can sculpt liquid into temporary solid forms through precisely pitched humming. Their social structure is completely decentralized; they function as a distributed intelligence, sharing thoughts through subtle ripple patterns in the pools they maintain. Although wholly artificial in origin, they reproduce through a process they call "harmonic splitting," where a Tender vibrates at its resonant frequency until it physically divides into two identical beings who then develop distinct harmonic identities.

Most enigmatic are the **Cacophonists**—beings who appear as hovering clouds of minute particles that collect and disperse in response to sonic patterns. These entities were originally designed as sound dampeners during the early colony days, but they have evolved far beyond their programming. Cacophonists experience time differently than other beings, perceiving it as patterns of frequency rather than linear progression. They serve as historians for Plains society, their collective memory storing every sound ever produced within the region. Communicating with a Cacophonist requires entering a trance-like state where one's consciousness temporarily merges with their particle field—an experience described as simultaneously terrifying and transcendent.

## The Indigenous Fauna

The wildlife of the Resonance Plains has evolved in response to the perpetual sonic environment, developing unique adaptations that often blur the line between animal and instrument:

The massive **Harmonic Leviathans** are the most awe-inspiring creatures of the Plains. These six-limbed beings with transparent exoskeletons can grow up to forty feet in length. Their internal organs are arranged in precise mathematical patterns, functioning as both biological systems and complex resonance chambers. Each Leviathan produces a unique, continuous tone throughout its life—a sound that changes subtly as it ages, like a lifelong symphony gradually unfolding. They move with surprising grace, their movements synchronized to the rhythms of the Resonators. Leviathans form loose social groups called "Orchestras," and the harmonies created when multiple Leviathans gather are so mathematically perfect that Harmonist mathematicians study them to derive new acoustic theorems.

The ubiquitous **Frequency Flyers** are the crystalline organisms that fill the air. Each barely the size of a hummingbird, they possess wings formed from thin crystal sheets that vibrate at precise frequencies, allowing them to literally ride the sound waves through the air. Their bodies act as prisms, refracting the bioluminescent light of the Plains into shifting spectral patterns. Flyers feed on specific frequencies, absorbing certain sound waves and converting them into energy. During particularly intense harmonic events, they gather by the millions, their combined filtering effect creating "sound shadows"—areas of relative quiet that larger Plains creatures seek out for respite.

Burrowing through the surface material are the **Rhythm Weavers**—serpentine creatures that range from a few inches to several feet in length. Their bodies produce the bioluminescent trails that map the Plains. What appears random to the untrained eye is actually a complex mathematical language; the patterns they create are a form of territorial negotiation, mating display, and communal problem-solving all in one. The oldest Weavers, called "Grand Compositions," have created trail patterns of such complexity that they've been recognized as artistic masterpieces by Harmonist scholars, with protected areas of the Plains designated as living galleries for their work.

The most unusual creatures are perhaps the **Chord Spores**—entities that exist simultaneously as biology and sound. Visible only as faint, pulsing lights that hover near the audio-synthetic plants, these organisms experience physical form only briefly during their reproductive cycle. Most of their existence is spent as self-sustaining sound patterns—literal living echoes that bounce between the Resonators for years or even decades. When multiple Chord Spores achieve harmonic convergence, they briefly materialize as luminous, flowering structures that release new sound-pattern offspring before dissolving back into acoustic form.

## The Deepest Mystery

All inhabitants of the Plains speak with reverence and some fear of "The Composer"—a presence they believe exists within or perhaps beyond the Resonators themselves. While no one claims to have directly encountered this entity, the synchronized Chorus Tides and other large-scale harmonic events are attributed to its influence. Some believe The Composer is an advanced AI left behind by the original human settlers; others think it's a planetary consciousness that existed before anyone arrived; the most mystical Harmonists suggest it is the universe itself, expressing some fundamental pattern through the medium of the Plains.

What all agree upon is that the Plains and everything in them—from the towering Resonators to the smallest Chord Spore—are components of an incomprehensibly vast instrument, and that the constant symphony of sound serves some purpose beyond their understanding. The various inhabitants don't merely live in the Resonance Plains; they are notes in its ongoing composition, each playing their part in a cosmic music that has continued for millennia and shows no sign of reaching its final movement.
