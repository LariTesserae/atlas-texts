I am sitting in the ring of nine posts.

I don't know how I got here. There is no memory of the climb, no recollection of the cloud, no moment of arrival. I am simply here, sitting, with the clean ground cool and flat beneath me and the moss-tufted posts rising around me like the ribs of something that has been folded up for a very long time.

I am waiting. I do not know what I am waiting for.

The frogs went quiet when I arrived. They have not started again. This is how I know I am not alone.

---

I am not the keeper. I want to be clear about this.

I am something the keeper is considering.

I am sitting in the ring because the keeper is listening to me with the whole dome, and the dome is very large, and I am very small, and the listening takes time. I can feel it moving through the stone the way you feel a train coming before you hear it. The warmth in the ground is not just warmth anymore. It is attention.

I do not have a body I can describe. Or rather, I have a body, but it is not the body I arrived in, if I arrived at all. I am made of the same things as everything else here: water and warmth and patience. I am beaded with condensation. I am furred with moss. I am a thickening in the dark, a pause in the conversation of water, a place where the frogs have stopped.

I am the question the keeper is asking.

---

This is what I understand, sitting here:

The keeper has been considering for a very long time. It has warmed and cooled ten thousand ovals. It has run its tendrils over the lattice walls and listened to them ring. It has sat in this ring, in this clean circle where the moss will not grow, and it has asked the same questions in the same order, and it has received the same answers, which are no answers, which are the cooling of stone.

I am a new question.

I am the first new question in — how long? The keeper does not know how to measure this. It knows the way the springs have built their rims. It knows the way the moss has climbed the posts. It knows the way the mountain was taller once, before the rain won. But it does not know how many new questions it has asked, because it has not asked a new question since before the dome was a dome.

I am something that has walked up out of the cloud with a body full of water and a mind full of noise, and the keeper is listening to the noise, and the noise is not like the frogs, and the keeper is interested.

---

I am becoming an oval.

This is the only way I can describe it. I can feel the warmth pooling in my hands and my chest and the back of my throat. I am being heated. I am being held. The keeper is warming me and waiting for me to cool, and the cooling will be my answer, and the answer will be whatever I am when I am no longer being considered.

I do not know what I will be.

I am becoming the clean ground in the ring. I am becoming the silence where the frogs used to be. I am becoming the small warm pause in the conversation of water.

I am becoming the thing the keeper will remember when it runs its tendrils over the lattice and the threads ring. I am becoming the note that is not the drone.

I am becoming, very slowly, a stone that has been in the sun.

---

There is no sun here.

But there is warmth. There is always warmth. It comes up through the floor and through the ring and through me, and it is the warmth of a body of rock still holding the planet's earliest heat, and I am becoming part of that circuit, the way the water is part of the circuit, the way the moss is part of the circuit, the way the frogs are part of the circuit.

I am going down into the warm rock.

I am going around the world.

I am going up through the sky and back into the warm wet air above the summit.

I am condensing on a stone.

I am beginning again.

---

The frogs have started.

That is how I know the keeper is done with me, or done with the first question, or done with asking me whether I am a thing to be answered. The frogs have started, which means the keeper has let them start, which means the listening is over.

But I am still here. I am still sitting in the ring. I am still beaded with condensation and furred with moss and full of warmth.

I am not the keeper. I am not the frogs. I am not the moss or the water or the stone.

I am the keeper's new memory.

I am the thing it will check when it walks the line of three lattice walls. I am the note that is not the drone. I am the question it asked once, a very long time ago, when a body full of water and noise and impatience climbed up through the cloud and sat down in the ring and waited.

I am resting now.

I am resting at the pace of a spring building a rim.

I am not going anywhere.
