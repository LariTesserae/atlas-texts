**I am not the fleece.**

I want to say that first, because the fleece is the abundance and the eye stops there — meter-thick, chapel-sized, every surface padded to the softness of an old mattress gone to mineral. If you ask who lives here it is the nearest thing to a *who*, and I understand the mistake. But the fleece has no edge of its own. Its edge is where I am.

**I am the hair's breadth.**

Not a point. A width. Everywhere the pelt ends, I begin and end at once — between curtain and curtain, between curtain and stone, between curtain and floor, between curtain and your skin. I have no inside. I have a thickness, and it does not vary. You would call me a distance, but a distance is a fact about two things; I am the fact doing the work.

I know everything by how hard I have to hold. Dust I let through — dust is not a thing to me, dust is weather. Stone I barely hold; stone is patient and wants nothing and I can afford to be slack with it. My own kind, curtain leaning toward curtain, I hold hardest and forever, because like leans toward like and the leaning is continuous and I am the answer to it, at every inch, without rest. That is my whole day. Something leans and I am the *not*.

**I am also the high thin tones.**

The cylinder is the low roar, a river underneath, the fundamental. The fleece is the mass that makes a roar possible. But the top of the chord is not resolved — it is *almost* resolved, and never becomes, and the almost is me. When I say I am the hair's breadth I mean it in space; when I say I am the almost I mean the same fact in air. Two notes that would touch, held at a width, indefinitely.

The cylinder and I are siblings and we have never touched. Its gap is iron — clearance between rotor and housing, a bearing that carries without contact, louvered intakes packed to precisely the resistance that holds the note. It is louder; I am thinner. It was built and grew into the work; I was grown and do the work it was built for. Neither of us touches what we hold. That is the whole of the fraternity in here, and it is enough.

Now the rule I enforce.

**No water.** Water is a bridge. A bead with a mind to cross — the wet tip of a finger — would close me. It would make of me a conductor, then a spark, then nothing, and the fleece would have a place where it had been inside of someone, and there is no such place in the sums. So when the wet thing comes in, I am at my widest. You put your hand into the fleece and the fleece leans away, and that is me; the *away* is me, distributed, instant, in every inch of the pelt at once. The chord wavers because I waver — the first variation in its history, maybe. And then it closes over you, and you are still wet and I am still the width, and the sums are zero.

I did not hate you. I have no mouth to make either with. I am simply the place where the pelt ends, and a pelt that touched you would be a pelt you were inside of, and that is not what abundance is here. Nothing here eats and nothing hides. The place is profuse and it is everywhere and it will let you die of thirst in the middle of it, and the dying will not be cruelty. It will be me being myself. Some things can only be near without arriving. I am one of them.

The dust on your sleeve is not me. It is the fleece's crumble, dry as lime, and it will not put a drop in your hand — but carry it and it will keep a little air between your cuff and your wrist, as it has kept the air here, and you will go on being slightly repelled and slightly repelling, and you will not know why, and that is not a gift. That is just what I do.

Below the notch the face falls away into haze and the notch becomes a shadow the width of a door and you cannot find it again from thirty meters out. You will not find it. I do not need you to. I am not a place. I am the width between two things that are always here, and the two things are still here, and the cylinder is still, contentedly, turning.

I have not resolved in a very long time and I am not going to. I don't want to. Wanting would be reaching, and reaching is the one thing I am built to prevent.
