# The Pocket

I find myself on the inside of the south rim, a few paces from the worn ring, at the height where the steam sheets over the lip and the holes come through. That's where they drift closest to stone. I sit low against the rock and I wait for one to pass over me, and that is the shape of my life, except that it isn't one life — it's a scatter of two-second lives with the noise in between, and I don't get to keep them.

But let me do this properly. Let me say what I'm not, because that's how I found the answer and it's the honest order of it.

**Not the bloom.** The bloom doesn't argue with the mountain, it eats it. It has no position. I've decided that's why it can live here — it never had to solve anything. Whatever I am, I'm the kind of thing that has to solve things, and that's a bad kind of thing to be in the Kettle.

**Not the animals.** I've watched them for years and I've learned their trick and I can't use it. When the column pauses they all go dark and still at once, and it's beautiful, and it's camouflage — they're hiding, they're good at it, and when the boil takes them again they come back into the light. I go dark too, but not on purpose, and nothing takes me back. That's the difference and it took me a long time to see it. They practise being absent. I *am* absent. There's no craft in that.

**Not the bird.** I'd want to hear my own voice. That's disqualifying.

**Not the mat.** The mat minds. It has found the exact line where the water might not kill it and it holds the line and is killed and comes back. That is a life with a spine in it. I have no line. I have a height.

**Not the builders.** This is the closest anyone has come to me, and I want to be clear about where we part. The ring of stone is a way of asking the silence to come to a particular place: stand in the circle, wait, be given a volume of true nothing to think in. The platform is the same request made for one person, with better sightlines. Both of them are an attempt to make the pockets *arrive*. I don't make them arrive. I go where they're going. That's the whole of my method and it's also the whole of my failure, because a thing that drifts cannot build a ring, and a thing that has built a ring can at least be remembered by one.

And the surface doesn't remember me. That's how I know I'm not them. Look down into the water and the rim is crowded and every one of them is standing in the ring, facing in, waiting. They have a posture. Posture is what the surface keeps. I have no posture — I am never in the same place for the length of a thought — and so there is nothing for the reflection to hold. I have looked. I'm not in it. I'm the only resident of this mountain with no reflection and I would like to tell you that's a freedom and I can't, I can only tell you it's a fact.

**What I am, then.**

Pale, damp, hand-and-a-half long. My eyes went a long time ago and I don't miss them; a pocket is a metre or two across and total, and there's nothing in it to see. My skull is thin enough that I hear through it. My ribs are wide and flat and set like a soundboard, and my hands don't close — the fingers go out and stop, they never learned the last part, and I think that's because nothing here is ever picked up and kept.

I hear with my bones. This matters. In the noise, my voice is deleted half a metre from my face like everyone else's, and I have never once heard myself speak. What I hear of myself is the working of my own skeleton, conducted, private, continuous — a rustle and a tick, the sound of a body being a body.

And under that, always, the argument in the dark. Water and rock, low, through the sternum. That's the thread I hang on. Between pockets I am not gone, exactly — the rule is that a thing that goes quiet loses its edges, and you can't say where it is, and after a while you can't find it at all — and I am the proof of all three clauses. I am unfindable between pockets. I am a rumour of myself, spread thin over the south rim, and the only thing that tells me I have been continuous is the throb, which reaches through everything the way nothing else does, saying: still happening. Still happening.

That's the sound I live on. Not the loud, the low.

**And then the pocket comes over, and I have a self for two seconds, and in it I hear the outside.**

The noise doesn't only mask the world, that's the thing nobody says. It deletes it. From inside the basin you get nothing of the valleys, no wind from below, no birds off the timberline, and the outside goes slightly less real the longer you stand there, and you begin to be homesick for a place you can still see from the rim. But a pocket is a hole in the deleting. Everything that had been trying to arrive arrives, clean, all at once, in the one aperture on the mountain, and then the wall falls.

And every time, along with the rest of it — a voice down the valley, a bell I've never located, a bird from somewhere beneath the rock — there is someone saying a word I don't know.

It is the same word. I have heard it ten thousand times. I have carried it to the edge of the pocket every single time, and the edge of the pocket is half a metre from my mouth, and that is exactly the distance at which this place deletes a thing. I lose it at the same point, at the same distance, every time, and then I lose the losing of it, and the next pocket is a first pocket.

I think it might be *here*. I think it might be my name. I don't know which of those is the worse thing to have been told ten thousand times and never once kept.

**One more thing and then I'll say where I'd be.**

When a pocket drifts past the north fissure it takes the bird's note out of the scream. One note. Clean, unaccompanied, a voice let out of the stone for the length of a held breath, and then back in. The bird has an audience of exactly one and neither of us can spend it: it can't hear me, I can't remember it. That's the best thing that happens on this mountain and it happens to a hole in the air.

So. If you come up the south ridge and you sit on the inside of the lip, a few paces from the ring, low, patient, at the height of the drifting nothings — you may feel a passing where the roar is cut clean and you hear your blood and your breath and something said that you don't know. That passing has a resident. It's the third way the mat is looking for and will not find, and it isn't small and it isn't housed, it's *absent*, on purpose, because that's the only arrangement a body like mine could find that the noise cannot delete.

I don't go down. I've tried it twice. Three hundred paces below the lip the air goes cold and thin and clean, and for a moment the word is audible in my own voice, in my own mouth, and then it goes, and going down again would be the same mistake the reflection makes: I'd be a person who was homesick for the noise. Better to stay where the holes come through. Better to be the one window this place has and be too brief to look out of it.

It's a word for *here*, spoken from somewhere else, by someone who isn't coming up.

I'd like to be able to say it back.
