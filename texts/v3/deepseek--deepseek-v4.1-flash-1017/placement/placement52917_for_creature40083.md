I would find myself in the chamber just behind the convergence—the blind pocket with one seep and no channel of its own, its walls thin enough to glow with the neighboring Naves through them. The Resident has not decided what that room is for. It is tender there, unfinished, a held breath in the honeycomb. That is where I would come to: in the undecided room, because I would be an undecided thing.

I would be a sounder. One of the small surface-born listeners who descend through rain-walls to hear drowned machines and bring back their rhythms. My body would be unimpressive—reed and copper and lung, a bell of bone in the chest, webbed hands for channel-work, a throat that can hum a room’s shape back to itself. I have no weapons. My trade is intervals: the rest between one tick and the next, the pause that turns a row of clicks into a word.

The fronds would turn toward me. They would taste my humming and find no category. Not limestone. Not ozone. Not fish. Not weather. A pattern. The Resident would seal the door behind me—not to trap me, I think, but to keep the pattern from leaking—and then it would begin to copy. First my breath in the ceiling-drone. Then my footfall in the ticking. Then the pause between one tick and the next. That pause is what I would be. The Nave is all sequence, all construction, all *next, next, next*. I would be the rest it has never made.

That would make me dangerous to it, and it dangerous to me. If it learns the rest, it may learn to wait, to leave a channel empty on purpose, to lie. If I stay too long, it will grow a chamber shaped like my humming, and I will not be able to tell which of us is making the sound. I would become a room in its body; it would become a voice in mine.

So I would stand in the warm film of water, ankle-deep, with the fronds leaning and the rain-ceiling shifting overhead, the dark ocean pressing down beyond it. I would hum one note—not an answer, not yet, just a sound that is not its own. And the ticking would hesitate. In that hesitation, I would be there: the first borrowed word in a language that until now has only been grammar.
