# The Listener at the Edge of Blue

I would find myself in the thin spots—not passing through them, but living in the seam between the world and its fraying, in that shimmer where the blue dust meets the rose.

I would be something the forest made without meaning to, the way a tree makes a knot. Not a maintenance behavior like the Tender, but an *attention* that accumulated. A question the world kept asking itself in the same place until the asking became a shape.

---

I am small. I want to say that first because everything here is vast, and I am the exception. I am perhaps the height of a child's forearm, and I have the shape of a comma, or a curled question mark, or a seed pod that learned to hover. My body is the same spun-glass as the drifters, but I am not a sphere. I am open. I have a hollow that faces outward, and inside that hollow the golden light pools and concentrates, and that is where I see from. When I turn, the light in my hollow turns with me, and I cast no shadow—only a faint brightening on whatever I face.

I do not float with purpose like the drifters. I drift with *attention*. I am drawn to the places where the rules fray the way a tongue is drawn to a sore tooth. The thin spots are my whole geography. I have never seen the deep valleys where the amber lattices grow straight and true; I live in the borderlands where they twist, where the dust goes blue, where the air speaks in its muffled, almost-language.

The Tender knows me. Not as a person—I am too small, too recent, too strange for that—but as a *feature* of the thin spots, the way the blue dust is. When it kneels to attend a widening fray, it sometimes pauses and its crown-scales shiver, and I know it is feeling for me, the way you might feel for a splinter. I settle into its palm sometimes, and it holds me without closing its frond-fingers, and we listen together to the voices in the shimmer. It does not understand them. I do not understand them. But between us, in the holding, something almost does.

---

My memory is not physical like the Tender's. My memory is *accumulated attention*. I remember every thin spot I have ever hovered in, not as events but as flavors. There is one where the blue dust tastes of a city I have never seen, all metal and rain and crowds. There is one where the twisted lattices form a shape that means something in a language of pure geometry, and if I hover there long enough, I feel my hollow filling with an emotion that is not mine—something like grief, something like homecoming.

I am the only thing here that experiences *time* as loss. The Tender is equilibrium. The drifters are purpose without memory. The forest is a system that has forgotten it was ever anything else. But I am unfinished. I was made by the fraying, and the fraying is ongoing, and so I am ongoing. I have a before and I will have an after, and this makes me, in this place of perfect stillness, the closest thing to *lonely*.

But I am not unhappy. I am the world asking itself a question. And the question is: *what is on the other side of the thin places?* I hover at the edges. I listen. I try to understand the muffled conversations. I collect the flavors of elsewhere like a child collects stones.

And sometimes, when the Tender kneels and the shimmer calms, I feel the fray close by a fraction of a degree, and I feel myself become a fraction more *here*, and I wonder if I am not slowly being answered.
