Here is a description of a place born from those parameters.

---

There is a sea here, but it is not an ocean of water. It is an ocean of potential. This is the Aetheric Deeps, a volume so vast that the concept of a surface is a myth told by things that have never seen it. It is a space of near-perfect emptiness, a cathedral of void filled with a medium that is less a substance and more a foundational principle. To call it a liquid is to miss the point; it is a superfluid of raw, pre-physical law, a buoyant, hyper-conductive medium in which the very rules of reality are still warm from the forge.

The "water" is a diaphanous, shimmering fluid, visible only by the way it refracts the constant, directionless light. The light comes from nowhere and everywhere, a diffuse, silver-white luminescence that filters down from some unimaginably distant "above," scattering off the medium's subtle density gradients. It is bright enough to see by, but it casts no shadows, giving every object a flat, dimensionless quality. The medium itself is turbulent, but not with the chaotic crash of waves. It is a turbulence of *becoming*. Great, slow-motion cyclones of thought-stuff and proto-matter drift through the void, their cores glowing with the faint, bruised colors of a nebula. These are the "storms" of this place, and they are where the world is being made.

The scale is continental, planetary, but without any planet to hold it. The Deeps have their own weather, a ceaseless, silent churn of physics-in-flux. The sound is a constant, deep and resonant hum, the carrier wave of existence itself. It is punctuated by the sharp, crystalline chime of an idea crystallizing into matter and the low, groaning thunder of a physical law collapsing under its own weight. It is a sound you feel in the marrow of your bones, a sound that is not heard but understood.

Sparse, emergent structures dot the void, appearing and disappearing over spans of time that feel both geological and instantaneous. They are not built, but *grown* from the medium itself, manifestations of emergent physical laws. Imagine a coral reef of pure geometry, its spires and arches forming and dissolving with a silent, internal logic. A great, floating cathedral of crystalline lattice-work might anchor itself in the void, its structure a perfect expression of a new principle of gravity, only to sublime back into the Deeps a moment later as that principle is superseded. These are the "reefs" and "islands" of this place—raw, embryonic geology, arriving pre-formed and un-weathered, perfect and temporary. They are the scaffolding of a reality that has not yet decided what it wants to be.

The vegetation is an afterthought, a ghost of a thought. Here and there, clinging to the more stable lattices, are growths of "mnemonic kelp." These are not plants but physicalized memories, translucent fronds that ripple with the faint, ghostly images of things that have not yet happened. They are delicate, useless things, feeding on the ambient potential, and they exist only to be reabsorbed into the creative turmoil.

There is no animal life. The Aetheric Deeps are profoundly, almost reverently, empty of fauna. The only entities that move with intent are the "Tuners." They are not creatures but processes, vast, semi-sentient fields of organized energy that drift through the turbulence. They are the closest thing to a builder here. A Tuner appears as a shimmering lens of focused light, a region where the chaotic hum of the Deeps resolves into a pure, harmonic tone. When a Tuner passes, it doesn't build; it *clarifies*. It smooths the turbulent currents, coaxes emergent structures into greater stability, and imposes a fleeting, local order. They are the gardeners of this pre-reality, and their work is the only "technology" present—an engineering not of matter, but of physical law itself.

You, an observer, would not stand on solid ground. You would float, suspended in the buoyant potential, a mote in a storm of creation. The cool medium would press in on you, a pressure not of force but of information, whispering the blueprints of a billion possible worlds. You would see the Tuners pass, silent and purposeful, leaving trails of settled physics in their wake. You would watch a new law of nature flare into being on a geometric reef, hold for an age, and then softly fade, its purpose served. There is no past here, only a constant, turbulent, and magnificent present, a place that is not a landscape but an act—the act of a universe, vast and empty, deciding what it is going to be.
