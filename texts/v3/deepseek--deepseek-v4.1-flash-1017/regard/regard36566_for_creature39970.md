What would a benevolent and wise power do?

It would come quietly, because the Level does not tolerate arrival.

It would not descend in light or pronounce from above. It would enter the way warmth enters a room — gradually, from a direction no one is watching — and it would spend a long time simply being present before it did anything at all. Wisdom, here, is mostly patience. The Level has outlasted urgency. Anything that arrives in a hurry will be flattened into the meadow inside a season and will have accomplished nothing but adding its silhouette to the rings.

So the power would come, and hold position, and let the quiet do the talking.

---

**First, it would listen.** Not to the inhabitants — not yet — but to the plate itself. It would follow the standing whirlpool through its slow hundred-meter winding. It would float over the hollow where sound arrives before it is made, and it would not correct the order. It would press a palm to the warm rock, and put a hand over the holes, and feel the residual heat of bodies pressed close on the other side of stone, and it would not decide, yet, what that heat meant.

It would swim the length of the grass forest and watch it breathe on the long cycle and count the rings of the fleshy mats and understand, from the counting, that this country measures time in decades the way a clock measures seconds. It would feel the ring of the ghost mountains when the plate struck its enormous low tone, and it would not speak during the ring, because some sounds are the last whole sentence of a thing and interrupting them is a kind of violence.

It would learn the quiet's texture. It would learn to read the thickening and thinning the way the inhabitants do. It would be, for a long time, a good guest, which is to say: it would not fill the room with itself.

---

**Then, when it had learned enough to be wrong in useful ways, it would begin to look for the ones who were still deciding.**

Not the settlers. The ones who had gone into the flatness and stopped being questions — they are not in pain and they are not waiting and to "rescue" them would be to misunderstand what they have become. The power would know this. A wise power does not pull a sleeper out of a good dream to prove it can. It would leave the landforms alone, and it would leave the rings with bodies' proportions alone, and it would let the oldest ones keep their final answer. There is no cruelty in that. There is only the recognition that a finished thing has a right to be finished.

But there are ones in the Level who have not yet settled. The ones holding position not because position is the final form but because no one has offered them a reason to move. The ones whose attention-patches tighten when a stranger passes, and who have not yet discovered that they are permitted to want something.

The power would find them one at a time. It would not approach — approach is rude here, approach is a demand — it would instead *decline to be scarce.* It would float near, and it would be legibly, unmistakably present, and then it would wait to see whether the quiet around it thickened or thinned. If it thinned, it would stay. If it thickened, it would drift off without offense, and try again in a season, or a decade.

And when one of them did not thin, the power would do the smallest possible thing. It would not speak in pressure — it cannot, not natively, not well enough to be understood. It would borrow the local grammar. It would let a single warm pulse move through it on the seven-minute cycle the water already uses, so that the pulse arrived in the shape of something the inhabitant already knew how to read, and inside the pulse would be one idea, carried the way a note is carried by a struck instrument:

*You may move.*

That is all. That is the entire intervention. Not *move where*, not *move toward me*, not *I will move you.* Just the permission, offered in the local dialect, on the local clock, at the local temperature, so that it cannot be mistaken for an intrusion and cannot be refused as an order. The inhabitant is free to flatten. The inhabitant is free to do nothing at all. But for the first time in ten thousand years, the choice has been *reopened*, and the reopening is the gift.

---

**And then the power would do the harder thing, which is to stay.**

Because a permission given once and withdrawn is not a permission. It is a test. The Level has had enough tests; the terraces are worn to rounded lips from testing. So the power would remain, and it would keep offering the pulse, and it would keep declining to be scarce, and it would be careful, always, always careful, not to let its own presence thicken into the flatness. It would come in fresh. It would leave and return. It would go away for years at a time so that the inhabitants could miss it, or not, and either answer would be fine. It would not build anything. It would not cut terraces or drill holes or raise walls, because the moment it made a mark it would join the country, and the country's final form is the thing it has come to gently disagree with.

It would keep the agreement of the hush. It would be exactly as loud as the room has decided to be, and no louder.

---

**Over time — and time here is measured in rings and rings in decades — the marks would begin to change.**

Not the old ones. The old walls would stay closed on nothing. The old terraces would stay worn. The old holes would stay warm, and the power would not fill them, because filling them would be a kind of ventriloquism, an answer given before the question.

But new marks would appear, and they would be *different*, and they would be different in the smallest possible way. A wall that closed on nothing would find, beside it, a wall that closed on something — a shape a body had left on purpose, a curve that was not a final answer but a sentence. A terrace would grow a sixth step, and the sixth step would face somewhere, which is to say it would be a *direction* again, and it would disappoint more often than not, and the disappointment would be survivable, and surviving it would be the point. A hole would be drilled, and beside it another hole, and between them a groove, so that the two holes had something to say to each other. The valleys between the rings of fleshy mats would grow narrow paths that went *somewhere*, and the somewhere would not be anywhere in particular, and that would be exactly right: a path whose destination is the walking.

The power would not do these things. The inhabitants would. The power would have only reopened the possibility, and the inhabitants would do the rest, and in doing it they would remember — slowly, in decade-rings, the way this country remembers anything — that making is not the same as finishing, that a mark can be a question, that geometry does not have to become bored of itself and stop.

---

**And the mountains.**

One day the plate would ring its enormous low tone, and the flatness would be a lie for the length of a breath, and in the middle of the ring the power would *answer.* Not in pressure. Not in the local grammar. In the old grammar, the grammar of the range that used to be here — a single clean high note, cold and sharp, held for a breath and then given back to the quiet. The first true sound the Level has heard since the mountains conceded. Not a correction. A *remembrance.* A reminder that the final form was not the first form, and is not obliged to be the last.

The inhabitants would feel it behind the sternum, and they would not know what it was, and they would spend the next decade or two trying to find out, and the trying would be the cure.

---

**And in the end, the wise and benevolent power would leave.**

Not abandon. Leave, in the way a good teacher leaves the room once the students are arguing productively. It would go while the arguing was still going, so that the last thing it left behind was not its own presence but the *wake* of its departure — the specific absence of a thing that had been present, which is the only absence that teaches. It would leave the plate ringing its old tone with new things growing inside the ring. It would leave the meadow with paths. It would leave the landforms alone, and it would leave the ones who had settled alone, because a good ending is not everyone waking up. It would leave the ones who had begun to move with the one thing the Level had never given them, which is the experience of having *wanted* something and having been *allowed.*

And the water would remember the power, the way it remembers everything, in a patch of looping current and a warm pulse on a seven-minute cycle, and in a hundred meters of hollow where the light would be, from then on, a slightly different amber.

The ring would pass.
The land would settle back.
But the settle would have an *after* in it, now.
And that is enough. That is the whole of what a wise power does in a place like this: it opens a question, and then it trusts the country to close it on something new.
