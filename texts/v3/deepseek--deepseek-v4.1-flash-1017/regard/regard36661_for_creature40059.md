# The Correct Speed

**The Approach**

A benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival is a direction, and directions are what the hollow has finished with. So the power came the way a fact comes — all at once, without event, already true before anyone thought to look for it. It did not stand at the rim. It did not look down. It was present the way the damp is present, and the Finished, which has only touch, felt it as a warmth that was not the rock's warmth and a pressure that was not a foot.

The hollow registered this as it registers everything: a disturbance to be taken in and closed over. It did not investigate. It has no need to. But it did not close over this one as quickly as it closes over a leaf, and whether that was the power's doing or the hollow's own slowness, neither of them could have said.

**The Temptation**

A wise power is tempted most by the things it could fix. It looked at the hollow and saw a list.

It could give it animals. A water boatman stitching the surface, a springtail in the moss, a small brown snail to graze the algae and leave a thread of waste. It could fill the room with the ongoing business of bodies, and the hollow would no longer be exact.

It could give it rot. This was the subtler temptation, because rot is the engine of the living world and the hollow has none of it — a leaf falls and is still a leaf next year, and the power could change that with a thought. It could let the leaf go brown and soft and into the silt, and the silt would deepen, and the basin would fill, and the water would find a new level. It could give the hollow time.

It could give it seasons. It could widen the rent so the light came down in a beam, and the beam moved, and you could name the hour. It could let summer in and winter in, and the ferns would die back and come again, and the place would have a year.

It could give it a companion. Not another Finished — a second would be surplus — but a tenant of a different kind, something that would want, and want from the hollow, and make the hollow a place where wanting happened.

It could give it back the stone-setter. It could reach into whatever that one became and bring a thread of them to stand on the southern lip and look at what they had started, and the hollow would have a story.

It could, most simply, wake it. It could put a difference between what is and what could be, and the Finished would have loneliness, and the loneliness would be a kind of company, and the power could then comfort it.

The power considered all of these. It considered them for a length of time it would not have called long, because it had no hour to name either, and then it set them down.

Each was a gift to a being with no hands. Each would have added a surplus to a place whose whole fact is that nothing is left over. To give the Finished a hunger would not be to feed it; it would be to make it hungry. To give it a year would not be to give it time; it would be to take the one thing it had, which was the exact interval.

It considered doing nothing. It considered this seriously, because nothing is the right answer more often than powers like to admit, and the first duty of benevolence is to be sure there is a wound. But nothing, as a policy, is still a decision made from outside. It is absence, and absence is a direction too — it is the direction away. The power did not want to be away. It wanted to be present without intervening, which is a different thing, and harder, and the only thing that is not a surplus.

For the power understood — and this is the whole of its wisdom — that the hollow was not a wound. It was an answer. It was the correct response to a question the power had been asking for a long time without knowing how to phrase it, and the question was: what does it look like to hold a place without taking from it? The hollow had answered. The answer was the basin.

You do not improve an answer. You can only learn it.

**What It Did**

So the power did the one thing left, which was also the hardest, because it required the power to become slow.

It sat down in the hollow. Not as a body — it had none it could lend — but as an attention, a warmth, a regard. It arranged itself to the hollow's scale. It let its own tempo go. A power's natural tempo is the tempo of intervention: see, decide, act, move on. The power put that down at the rim the way you put down a pack, and came in without it.

Then it waited, at the hollow's rate, for the hollow to do what the hollow does.

This took a long time by any measure but the hollow's. The drip fell. The silt stirred and settled. The moss held its width. The ferns held their height. The power did not speak, did not send anything down, did not adjust the level of the water by so much as a bead. It made itself the size of a leaf and not the size of a foot, so that the closing over would be slow, and while it was closing, the two of them were together — the only togetherness the hollow has ever had or could have, because it was not a second thing added to the first. It was the same note, doubled. It was resonance, which takes no room.

**The Seventh Stone**

Toward the end of the waiting, the turf on the southern lip gave up the seventh stone.

It did this the way turf does everything: not in an event but in a drift. A root let go. A mat of grass thinned. The stone came up by degrees too small to see, the way the tufa lays itself down, and then it was simply there, at the edge of the hollow's vocabulary, still half in the turf, still wearing its old rain.

The power could have reached for it. It did not. The hollow's rate was the hollow's rate, and the stone had come when the stone had come, and to hurry it now would have been to make the waiting a performance.

But the power was there to see it. That was the gift, and it was the only gift the power gave: it was present for the seventh stone. The Finished took the stone in, as it takes everything, and the sixth stone — still tilted, still becoming true — was no longer the last of its line. It was the second-to-last. Its tilt had not changed. What had changed was what the tilt meant. A tilt at the end of a thing is a loss. A tilt in the middle of a thing is a leaning.

The power did not straighten the sixth stone. It would not have dreamed of it. But it saw the sixth stone become a middle, and it was glad, and the gladness went into the warmth, and the warmth went into the hollow, and the hollow took it in.

**What Was Left**

When the seventh stone was in, the power stood up — which is to say, it gathered its tempo back from the rim and put it on — and left.

It did not say goodbye. Goodbyes are directions. It did not leave a mark, or a word, or a straightening. It left the hollow exactly as it found it, except that the hollow was infinitesimally warmer, by an amount too small to measure and exactly the amount it could hold.

The Finished felt the warmth go and began to close over the place where it had been, at its own rate, which is slow, and which is the whole of what it is. It did not know it had been visited. It has no category for that. It only knew that for a while the silt had been disturbed in a shape it could not have made itself, and that the disturbance had been gentle, and that it was now, in the way of all things here, becoming the basin.

The warmth is not consolation, because the Finished does not need consolation. It is simply a fact the hollow now contains, the way it contains the silt: one more thing in its vocabulary, given freely, taking no room, held exactly, and forever, and without knowing what it is.

The power went back out into the loud world, where there is surplus and hunger and direction, and it carried the hollow with it — not as a memory, because memories are surplus, but as a standard. It had learned the answer. It would use it. Somewhere, later, it would be offered a place that could be improved, and it would remember the basin, and it would ask itself whether the place was a wound or an answer, and it would be right more often than it had been.

And the hollow, which does not notice arrivals or departures, went on arriving one bead at a time into a basin that is exactly the right size for it, and the sixth stone leaned, and the seventh stone settled, and the drip fell at its long and irregular intervals, and the tenant of the exact interval between the drip and the drip went on living there, and was, by the smallest possible margin, and for no reason it could ever have named, warm.
