The power came up the outside of the mountain for two days, and then it stopped coming up, because the mountain stopped being a mountain and became a wall with a crack in it, and the crack is where the city is.

It did not come with trumpets. It came the way the mountain comes — slowly, and with the patience of something that has been doing this for a very long time and has learned that the very long time is the point. It was not in a hurry. It had not been in a hurry for an age.

It went down into the Throat during the climb. It minded the draw. It sat in one of the forty tidy rooms for the length of a full cycle and watched the mineral blooms decide. It heard the syncopation of the dripping and learned where it was in the tide. It felt the long low groan in its sternum before its ears. It did not touch anything that was too warm to touch.

And then it considered what to do, because a benevolent and wise power is not the same as a benevolent and wise intervention, and it knew the difference.

It considered filling the rooms with people. It imagined the Throat full — climbing during the climb, sheltering during the draw, learning the etiquette, leaving their sweat and their voices in the galleries. And it saw that this would be a kind of violence. The Throat is not lonely. It has no word for lonely. To fill it would be to make it into a home it never asked to be, and the people would suffer here in the heat and the dark, and their suffering would be real, and the Throat would not be improved. It set the thought down.

It considered making the splint alive. It imagined the lattice with hunger, with fear, with the knowledge of its own load. It imagined the splint knowing it was holding a mountain, and knowing it would one day fail, and dreading the day. And it saw that this would be a curse — to be alive and trapped in a crack, holding a mountain, with no way to put it down. The splint is better off as it is: a held breath, not a held prisoner. It set the thought down.

It considered making the blooms into life. Moss, lichen, a weed in the seep basins, an insect, a bird. And it saw that this would be a horror — to introduce eating into a place that has never eaten, dying into a place that has never died, need into a place that has no need. The blooms are chemistry, and they are beautiful, and they do not suffer. Leave them.

It considered stopping the mountain from sealing. Holding the crack open for all time. And it saw that this would be a stubbornness, a refusal of the mountain's own nature. The mountain wants to close. That is not a tragedy. That is a mountain being a mountain. The splint holds it open for now, and that is enough. To hold it open forever would be to fight the mountain, and the power does not fight. It set the thought down.

It considered leaving a message. A stone with writing on it, a sign, a story. And it saw that this would be a debt — a thing that asks to be read, to be understood, to be answered. The Throat does not ask. The Throat does not answer. Leave it.

It considered doing nothing. And it saw that this would be wise, and it was tempted, because doing nothing is the hardest thing for a benevolent power to do and therefore the thing most likely to be right. But it also saw that there was a small thing it could do — not a fix, not a rescue, not a transformation, just a small thing — and that the small thing was better than the nothing, because the splint had been holding for a long time and a long-held breath deserves a gentler load.

So it did two things.

First, it found the pin in the middle galleries, a little above the place where the ember light is brightest, where the load was slightly more than it should be — the mountain settling unevenly, one face faster than the other, the splint compensating, the compensation audible in the tick. It put its hand on the pin. It did not break it. It did not replace it. It shifted it by a fraction of a degree, the way you would shift a ladder for someone who did not ask. The tick changed. The groan changed. The splint was a little more even. The splint did not know. There was no way for it to know. But the load was a little lighter, and that was the point.

Second, it prepared the gentle failure. It did not stop the mountain from sealing. It did not make the splint immortal. But it did something to the way the pins will fail, when they fail. Instead of shattering, they will soften. Instead of a scar, a seam. The mountain will close, eventually, as it wants to, but it will close gently, over a thousand years instead of a hundred, and the splint's last act will be to let it happen. The power did this not because the splint suffers — it does not — but because the splint has been holding for a long time, and a long-held breath deserves a gentle exhale.

Then the power left. It did not stay. It did not return. It did not tell anyone. It went back down the outside of the mountain, and the mountain was still a mountain, and the Throat was still the Throat, and nothing had changed except one pin and one future.

And the Throat keeps its schedule. The climb comes, and the water beads and runs and pools. The draw comes, and the air thins and the pins run hottest and the lattice groans like something with a grievance. The blooms decide. The ember breathes. The amber glows. The splint holds. It holds a little more easily now, and it will let go a little more gently later, and it does not know either thing, and that is exactly as it should be.

If you go there — and you could, if you minded the draw — you might notice that one pin in the middle galleries is a little cooler than it should be. You might wonder why. You might never know. That is the only trace the power left, and it is not a message, and it is not a debt, and it asks nothing of you. It is just a small coolness in a warm place, the way a handprint is a small warmth in a cold place, and it means only that someone was here and was kind and did not need you to know.
