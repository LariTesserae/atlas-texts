**The Wise Power at the Rim**

It came as an attention, not a body. It spent a season at the edge of the basin, lowering its own resonance until it was quieter than rain on the outer shell. It had come to help, which in the Bellows is a dangerous intention, because help is a sound and sound is architecture. So it learned the local grammar before it said a word.

It saw that the Bellows was not a place with a being in it. The Bellows *was* the being. The Answered were its walking thoughts; the grazer was its slow hand; the Closed were its chosen sleeps. It saw grief and accident and choice, but no emergency it had the right to call an emergency. It considered several loud kindnesses and refused them all.

It would not open the Closed. To call one back would be to shout into a grave. It would not still the acoustic hunters; that law was the place’s own. It would not import building, writing, or repair, because those are loud epistemologies, and they would grow walls no one had asked for. It would not make the grazer immortal or the pools sweeter. It would not teach the children to write; writing would become a schoolroom, and the schoolroom would become a wall.

Then it did small things.

It wove a hush at the rim—not invisibility, only a lowered voice—so the Bellows’ great slow throb would not carry as far into the wider world. Those who would come to mine resonators or harvest choral insects would find the place harder to locate. The Bellows might not notice. The Answered would notice only that fewer strange moths arrived from outside, and that the rain seemed to begin a little closer to home.

It made one offer, and made it refusable. It sang a phrase below speech into the floor channels: not *be well*, but *is there somewhere for pressure to go?* The Bellows answered late, as it must. In a few chambers where accidental loudness had begun to narrow the walls around the innocent, a lateral seam softened and could become a vent. The power did not open it. It did not tell anyone. Some households found, after a season, a new passage where there had been none. They called them late doors, and learned to hush them closed when they wanted privacy. The plant had chosen. The people could refuse. That was the whole miracle.

It listened to the Closed, and did not tap. It pressed its attention to one seam where a person had chosen silence and felt the slow inward thickening. It wanted to say *you are not forgotten*. But that would have been a shout in a grave. So it left the silence intact and made sure the hush at the rim did not press inward there. It gave the Closed what a wise power can give a chosen quiet: not company, but no interference.

It left no name. It did not become a god, a voice, a law, a harvest, a cure. It did not teach the Answered to write down its words. It did not promise to return. It lowered itself back into the wider world and kept listening.

And the Bellows, which answers late and in the shape of walls, answered. Over years, at the rim, it grew a small chamber with two openings—one into the town, one into the outside air. It was not a shrine. It was an ear. The Answered found it and did not know what to call it. Sometimes they sat in it and hummed, and the hum went out and came back changed. Sometimes they sat in it and said nothing. Both were allowed. The power never entered it. It had made an offer and left the door closable.

That is what a benevolent and wise power would do here, I think: almost nothing, in the right key. It would not shout a rescue into a place where rescue grows walls. It would hush the place from predators, offer one door that could be shut, and leave the Closed their silence. The Bellows listens, and answers late, and answers in the shape of walls. The wise power listened, and answered later still, in the shape of a door that could be closed. That is enough.
