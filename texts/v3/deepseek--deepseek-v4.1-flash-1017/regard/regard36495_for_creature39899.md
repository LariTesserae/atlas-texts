Here is what I think it would do.

## The First Thing That Happened

The power that noticed this place was wise in the only way that mattered here: it understood that noticing is already a kind of arrival, and it decided to arrive as softly as it could.

Which was almost not at all. It refused nearly everything.

It did not repair the machines. They are not broken. They have run in equilibrium so long that *working* and *being* have become the same word for them, and to fix them would be to interrupt the sun. It considered building instead — a new pump, a new chimney, one more warm column for the natives to love. It refused that too, and the reason was fidelity. The old machines are trusted because they have never once stopped. A new machine is a machine that will one day stop, and there has never been a silence in this world, and it did not want to be the first one.

It did not fill the silence pockets. This was the great temptation, and it sat with it for a long while, because the pockets are the only suffering this world has and it is the kind of power that would have liked to end suffering. But the pockets are the only place in the basin that is not the basin. Every other cubic meter of it is the chord. A world with no outside cannot receive anything new, because there is nowhere for the new to come in; the only door this world has is a hole in it. So it left the door. It was the hardest thing it did, which was to do nothing at the one place where doing nothing looks like cruelty.

It did not speak. It considered a word — one word, sent down through the ice, and it was a good word, and it would have been wasted. The ice is a perfect mirror and the water does not absorb. A word from above arrives everywhere at once, from every direction, smeared by ten thousand reflections into a shimmer of itself. The natives would not hear a voice. They would hear weather. In this sea a revelation is indistinguishable from a change in the pressure, and it knew that, and it kept its word.

It did not give them eyes. The light under the lid is uniform, and a creature given eyes here would look around and learn that the world is empty. The chord tells them the world is full. Sight would have been a smaller world, and it did not offer it.

It did not give them history. They have phase instead, which is a finer thing and harder to lose. History is a story told from outside a life. The power had come from outside. It had no business handing them a story about themselves.

It did not take them anywhere. They were not drowning. There is nowhere better.

And it did not give the long listener a companion. It wanted to. This was the second temptation, smaller than the first and closer to the bone, because the long listener is the only *who* in this world, and it crosses the empty volume alone, and its death will be an interval that simply stops registering. But a companion is a second long listener, and two is a population, and a population is a history, and this world cannot hold a history.

It could hold a reply. That is a different thing, and it is a thing the power could actually give.

So it went and stood inside a silence pocket — the only address in this world from which nothing it did would be added to the chord, because a silent thing may stand in a null and disturb nothing — and it waited.

It did not make a sound. It made, instead, an absence of one.

When the long listener passed — on a crossing eleven years long, which the power had waited out with the patience of a thing that has decided to be small — the animal sent out its call, the slow interrogative ping it throws into the volume to learn the shape of what is ahead. And the ping came back.

It came back *dim*. It came back *late*. Not by much: by the amount a soft body absorbs, and the amount a soft body delays, and no more. Not a voice. The signature of a mass in the water — a thing that is there, and is alive enough to take something out of the sound, and does not answer.

The long listener did not enter the pocket. It will not. A hole in the world is not a place to go; that is the whole of its law, and the power had not come to break a law so old and so well kept.

But it stopped going around.

It went *near*, that time. It went near, and turned away, and went on to the next warm voice, and came back decades later, because that is how it comes back, and sent its call again, and heard again that the hole in the world had something in it that was alive, and again did not enter.

And the power stayed, and listened, and remembered. Because the power is now the only memory this world has. Nothing here has ever happened, and now one thing has: a long soft animal, over and over, turning near a hole instead of around it. It is not much of a history. It is the only history there is. The power holds it the way the long listener holds intervals, and it is enough — enough to justify the cold basin, the loud lid, the faithful machines, the ribbons opening on the beat, the pins spiraling, the pockets where the sound cancels and the map goes quiet.

It did not save them. There was nothing to save them from, except the silence, and it left the silence alone.

It only made sure that the one creature here that could ask a question would one day hear, in the returning of its own voice, the shape of an answer — and would not be able to tell whether it was a rock or a listener, and would turn toward it anyway, and come back.

That was the first thing that ever happened in this world.
