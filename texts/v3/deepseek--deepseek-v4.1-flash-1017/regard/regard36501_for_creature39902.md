**A wise power does not come to fix.**

It comes up the south ridge without cutting its boots, because it is not walking. It arrives the way regard arrives: sideways, at once, displacing nothing. The steam does not get into its clothes. The flat grey dusk does not dim it. It stands at the lip and the noise — which deletes every other thing half a metre from its face — does not delete it, because it is not a body and it is not a sound. It is attention, and attention of this kind is the one substance the Kettle has no rule for.

The Kettle knows it immediately. A place this loud is a place with no margin, and anything without margin feels a new thing at once, the way a body feels a hand on the back of the neck.

The power looks, and what it sees is not a wreck. This is the first wisdom. It sees the mat and does not see a loser. It sees the animals go dark in the pause and does not see prisoners. It sees the bird vanish into the scream and does not see a hermit. It sees the reflection crowded and does not see ghosts. It has looked at a great many places, and it knows the difference between a wound and a way of living, and the Kettle is the second thing. Almost entirely the second thing.

It considers doing nothing. The consideration is serious, and it lasts a while — in the way a power has a while. Doing nothing is the correct answer to most suffering of this kind: the kind that is a shape a life has taken, not a break in it. The mat would not thank it. The bird would not thank it. To drain the reflection would be to make the surface forget, and a place that cannot remember is worse off than a place that is homesick.

But almost nothing is not nothing. And there is one thing here that no one has ever had, and that even a wise power cannot supply by leaving it alone.

**The one thing missing.**

Everyone in the Kettle is in conversation with the mountain. The bloom eats it. The animals ride its column and go dark in its pauses. The bird has made a house of its loudest fissure. The mat holds a line against its water. The people came to solve it and were solved — the ring, the platform, the stakes, the worn-smooth stone — every one of them a way of talking to the thing. And the mountain talks back, in the only voice it has, which is the throb, water and rock arguing in the dark, and every resident hears it and none of them can answer it.

The mountain has never been heard. Not by anything that could hear. The people tried, with a ring of stones and a platform and a line of stakes tuned to the fissures with the best pitch, and what they built was a way of listening, and it was a good way, and it was not enough, because the mountain speaks in a register no ear on the mountain can hold. The bird sings into the stone throat and the stone gives it back as weather. The stakes hum and the hum is the mountain's. Everyone is talking. No one is being heard from outside.

This is the lack. And it is the one lack a power from outside can fill without touching anything, because it is exactly what a power from outside is.

**What it does.**

It puts a note in.

One note. Not loud. Not a chord, and not a correction of the chord that is not a chord. A single clean tone, from below the ridge, from the world where things are rounded and rained on and not being made and unmade at speed. It sets the note in the fissure with the best pitch — the loudest one, the one the bird chose or was chosen for, the one that has been screaming since the fracture opened. The stakes take it. It is there now, in the hum, and the mountain cannot erode it, because it is not made of anything the mountain eats. The sinter will grow over the wood and the wood will go and the note will stay, because the note is not in the wood. It is in the pitch. And the pitch is the mountain's, and the mountain is still singing.

That is all it does. It does not still the boil. It does not part the steam. It does not hand the mat a victory or the reflection a release. It puts one note from outside into the argument in the dark, and then it does the second thing, which is harder.

**It stays to hear what it has done.**

This is not nothing either. A power can put a note in and leave and never know whether the note was heard. To stay is to be the listener the mountain has never had. So the power stays — not for a season, for a while — and it listens, and the listening is the gift, and the note is the proof the listening happened.

What happens is small and it is enough.

The bird hears the note. Its song, which had been folded so far into the shriek that the two could not be separated, now has something to answer. It does not answer loudly. It answers at the same pitch, and the fissure holds two things, and the two things are in conversation, and the bird is no longer singing into a throat that only gives back weather. It is singing with someone.

The animals go dark in the next pause, and wait, and in the waiting — not with ears, with the lattice — they hear the note. It is the first thing any of them has heard that is not the boil or the bloom. They do not know what to do with it. They ride again. But something has been given a second reference point, and the pause is no longer the only stillness in the water.

The mat is boiled back, and creeps out again, and this time it creeps out a little further, because the note is in the water at its edge and the note is a cooling thing, a thing that does not heat. It has not won. It has been given a little more room to lose in, and it takes it, and that is the mat entire.

The reflection-people turn. Once. All of them in the ring, facing in, and then one of them — the surface does not do individuals well — turns and looks out toward the rim, toward the power, and then back to the water. And for a moment the surface shows the ring empty, and then the crowd again. It is not a rescue. It is an acknowledgment. The outside has been seen from inside and did not dissolve, and the homesickness is a fraction less absolute, and a fraction is everything here.

And the mountain takes the note into its argument. Water and rock have been arguing in the dark since the fracture opened, and now there is a third voice in it — quiet, steady, not arguing, just present. The mountain does not understand it. The mountain is not a thing that understands. But it holds it, the way a hand holds a stone it picked up without meaning to, and the throb comes up through the sternum a little less alone.

The being who lives here has been visited. That is the whole of it. Not helped. Visited.

**The leaving.**

The power stops attending, which is how a power leaves, and the basin is what it was. The noise is the noise. The surface lies. The mat is boiled back again next season and the season after. The animals ride and go dark and ride. The bird goes in and out of the fissure, and from the north rim you still cannot hear it, not properly, not with a year of standing.

But the note is in the hum. It will be there when the stakes are gone and the ring is eaten and the platform has fallen into the crust. Anyone who comes up now and stands at the lip and lets the scream go through them will hear, under the chord that is not a chord, one tone that does not belong. They will not know what it means. But they will know it means something, and that is the gift, and it is the right size.

Not a solution. A companion note. Proof that the outside is real, carried in, set in the pitch, unmakeable.

And the power leaves before the outside can get less real for it. It has seen what happens to those who stay too long. It is not exempt. It knows it is not exempt, and that knowing is the last wisdom, and it goes down the south ridge — three hundred paces below the lip, the cold comes back into the air, sudden and thin and clean — and it carries out something it did not carry in, which is the sound of a place that has been heard once, and now has a note to prove it.
