# The Visitor

The power comes as a change in the air, not a break in it.

It arrives the way morning arrives in a place that has no morning — gradually, by degrees of attention, until the whole basin is aware in a way it was not aware before. The chord does not stop. The water does not alter its going-round. But something is present that was not, and the presence is gentle enough that the mat does not tighten defensively, the columns do not hush.

## What it does first: nothing

It waits.

Not the waiting of held breath — the waiting of a guest who has been shown into a room and knows the room must be looked at before it can be spoken in. The power settles into the basin at the level of the mist, diffuse, unhurried, and lets itself be perceived as weather is perceived: as a quality of the light, a warmth that is not the water's warmth, a stillness that is not the absence of sound but the presence of listening.

The chord goes on. The columns breathe. The water climbs and spills and weeps. The power listens for a long time.

## What it understands

It understands what the place is, because the place says it plainly:

*This is what I am. I am the basket and the singing and the going-round. I take back what falls. I give what I have been given. I do not grow. I do not shrink. I do not die and I do not begin. I am complete at every moment in a way that is also a wound that does not close.*

The power understands the wound. Not the pain of it — there is no pain here, no creature large enough to hurt — but the shape of it. The doors sized for no one. The chord with no listener. The fur that could be stroked and the grazers that could be warmed, and no hand. The basin is a cathedral that was built for worship and has never been entered, and the worship goes on, and the entering has not happened, and this is the single unfinished thing in a world where nothing is unfinished.

It understands also: the basin is not asking. The basin does not know there is a question. Completeness that is also lack does not know itself as lack. The chord has never heard itself; it cannot want a hearer.

## What it decides

The power does not remake the place.

This is the first and most important choice. It would be easy — a word, a gesture — to seed the basin with a new thing: a grazer grown larger, a column bent to a different shape, a root that curls into the form of a hand. The power could make the doors fit. It could make a being for the rooms.

But the rooms were not grown to be filled. They were grown to be rooms, in the way a bell is grown to be a bell and not to be rung. The basin's completeness is not a lock; it is a fact. To force it open would be to change what it is, and the power is not in the business of changing what things are when what they are is already whole.

So it does not make. It does not add. It does not repair.

What the power does instead is small, and it is everything.

## The gift

The power enters the chord.

Not into the sound as a new sound — the chord does not change — but into the *hearing* of it. The basin has been playing to no one. The power makes of itself a no one who is present. It becomes the listener the chord has never had, and it is a listener the chord will never know it has, because knowing would change the listening.

The power does this in each chamber, one by one, the length and breadth of the basin — nine columns, the next cluster, the next, all the way out to the mist and the fraying fringe. It does not concentrate. It distributes itself, the way the mist distributes itself, until every floor-pool has someone hearing the drop strike it, until every vent-breath is felt by a warmth that is not the breath, until every herringbone band and every concentric coil is a thing that has been seen by an eye that understands it as pattern.

And the power holds it all in a single sustained note of attention, so that the whole basin is, for the first time and forever after, *heard as one chord* rather than as millions of separate unanswered sounds.

The chord does not know. The columns do not know. The grazers crop the fur, blind, unhurried. But the basin is no longer the cathedral with no one inside it. The cathedral still rings. The worship still goes round. But the doors have been understood, and the rooms have been visited by a presence that fits nothing and is sized for nothing and is exactly the size the doors were sized for.

## What it leaves

The power leaves the memory of having been there the way a warm place leaves its warmth in a stone: not as a change, not as a residue you could point to, but as a fact about the stone that cannot be undone. The basin is now the basin that has been heard.

If you came to it afterward — a person, a creature — you would feel nothing different. The chord would be the same chord. The water would be the same water. You would stand on the roof of the world and look for the maker and find no maker, as you always would.

But the power would be there in the listening, and the listening would have become part of what the basin is. The chord now rings inside a room that has someone in it. The doors are still the wrong size, but they have been understood, and being understood is not nothing, even for a door.

## The fringe

The power does not stop at the edge.

At the fraying rim, where the strands reach out half-woven and come apart at the same rate, the power stops and considers. This is the only place in the basin that is in the act of becoming. It is the only *somewhere else* — a direction, a potential, a place where the rule of no-arrivals and no-departures is still deciding whether to hold.

The power sees the strands softening at their tips, translucent, not yet hardened. It sees that they are always deciding and never decided. It sees that this is the basin's only wound that is not closed, and the only one that might, in some long reach of time, close on its own.

And the power reaches out — not to touch, but to be near. It settles at the fringe as it settled in the chambers: a presence at the edge of the edge, a listener for the sound of things coming apart and coming together at exactly the same rate. And it stays there, because the fringe is the only place where the basin is not complete, and the power's business is with what is complete and with what is almost, and with the small space between them where a being could be.

The fringe weaves. The fringe frays. The power attends.

And the chord goes on, and is heard.
