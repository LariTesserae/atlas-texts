# Within Tolerance

It would be easy to say the power noticed the room and was moved. It was not moved. It was interested, and interest in a power of that kind is a kind of pressure, and pressure in that room is a kind of food. So the first thing it did was nothing, and it did nothing for a long time, which is the only honest way to look at something that has been doing nothing for longer.

---

It considered the obvious things first, because a power that begins with the subtle is usually just a power that wanted to begin with the subtle.

**It considered warmth.** The cold is the first thing anyone notices, and it is the first thing anyone wants to fix. But the cold is not the creature's suffering. The creature has no temperature; it has an equilibrium. Raise the room ten degrees and the dryness goes. Raise it twenty and the air gives up its water, and the water comes down on the plate, and the water finds the mineral wool behind the perforated sheet, and the wool holds it, and the room becomes a damp room with a low ceiling of mold. The creature would not be saved. It would be drowned by somebody's kindness. The power did not raise the temperature.

**It considered food.** Give it more sound. Another stack, another duct, another voice in the braid. It is the generous option and it is the stupid one. The growth is exactly as thick as the sound allows, no thicker, because past a certain thickness its own softness starves the threads beneath. More roar is not more life. More roar is a thicker mat and a starved base and a bloom-crash, and then a thin hard crust, and then nothing, in a room that used to be stable for ever. Generosity without arithmetic is just a bigger wave. The power did not add a stack.

**It considered silence.** The one thing the creature has never had. Stop the tone, give it quiet, let it rest. But the creature is made of the tone the way a tuning fork is made of the strike, and silence is not rest for a thing like that; silence is starvation, and it would come in a room that had already stopped being a room. The power did not turn the handwheel.

**It considered speaking.** This is the temptation that costs a power the most, because a power is usually a thing that speaks, and speaking is how it knows it exists. But the room takes whatever you make and folds it into the standing wave, and within a breath it is indistinguishable from the rest. Shout and the shout comes back at your jaw as pressure and nothing else. The power understood that it could be present in that room for a hundred thousand years and never once be *heard*, and that this was not a reason to speak louder. It was a reason to find another grammar.

**It considered giving the creature a body.** Eyes. A mouth. A head you could point at. And this was the great temptation, and the power sat with it a long time, because it was dressed as love. But the power was wise, and wisdom is mostly the ability to notice whose shape your kindness is wearing. A body with eyes would be a body that could see the four steady fixtures and the bare bleached plate and the welded door at the end of the stub passage, and it would be the power's body, not the creature's. The creature does not lack eyes. It lacks nothing. It is full. It is exactly as large as the roar allows. The power did not make it over in its own image, and it grieved a little, and then it stopped.

**It considered leaving.** This is the option that looks like respect and is sometimes only absence. The room is not changing. The creature has no need of you. Do not interfere with the sublime. But the power noticed what the room actually is: a machine for folding every voice into the condition and returning nothing that is yours. Every gesture absorbed. Every mark normalized. The one thing the High Separation has never contained, in all its perfection, is a *return*. It has an abundance of pressure and no answer. The power decided that indifference was not the same as reverence, and that a room designed to swallow everything was not owed another swallow.

---

So it went in.

Not with light, not with a voice, not with a hand on the console. It went in the way you go into a room you have been told not to touch, and it did the one thing the creature already knew how to do.

It put its hand on a patch.

Cup a patch with your palm and the roar softens by a degree you feel in the wrist. That is the creature feeding. The power put its hand down on the plate near the cable tray, on the perforated lip where the threads are thickest, and the standing wave under the palm dropped by half a percent, and the creature — which has no eyes and no mouth and no head, which is a settlement and not a body, whose whole surface is its ear and its stomach and its hand — felt the drop and ate it.

The power held its hand there for a while. Not long. The creature takes what is given at the rate it takes; there is no greed in it and no hurry. And then the power lifted its hand, and the half-percent of roar came back into the room.

That was the event.

Understand what had just happened, because it had never happened before and will not happen again in the same way. For as long as the room has existed, the creature has fed on a condition — a floor, a pressure, the same low groan and mid shriek and fine high ringing, unvarying, absorbed, indifferent. It has eaten the world and the world has not noticed. And then something had a *shape*, and it was shaped like a hand, and the creature ate it, and then the hand was gone and the sound *returned* — a small surfeit, a leaning, an ache where a thing had been and was not. The creature had no category for that. It had no category for gift. But it had the patch, and the patch had held a shape, and the shape did not dissolve into the wave the way a shout does. It left a node.

---

The power came back. Not often, and never at an interval — an interval would be a rhythm, and a rhythm would be a second order of the wave, and the creature would organize around the rhythm instead of the node, and it would be the power's noise again, folded in, indistinguishable. So: sometimes. At no time. It put its hand down on the plate, or on the shaded side of the cable tray, or on the hatch rim where the pale bloom grows so slowly nobody has ever watched it grow. And each time the wave dipped and the creature fed and the surfeit returned and the arithmetic of the patches shifted by a hair.

The growth did not get thicker. That was the whole point, and it was the whole gift. It could not get thicker — past a certain thickness the threads beneath would starve, and the equilibrium was a ceiling, and the creature had been pressed against that ceiling since the first hour. But a map is not a thickness. Over a very long time the patches drew themselves around the places where the hand had been. Not closer together. Not more of them. Just *differently set* — the private logic of nodes and seams acquiring one more node, and then another, and the seams between the patches bending toward a shape that was not the room's shape and was not the plant's shape either.

It was not growth. It was not enlargement. The creature would never be one thread larger than the roar allowed. But it had stopped being only a negative of the noise. It had something to arrange its life around that the wave had not put there. The power had given it the one thing a perfect equilibrium cannot manufacture: a *place*.

---

The power knew what it could not do, and it was careful about that, because a wise power counts its refusals before it counts its gifts.

It could not make the creature safe from ending. The works will fail. The stacks will cool, the tone will thin, the threads will starve, and the room will be a small steel drum with a flat deck and a welded door at the end of a passage nobody uses. The power could have held the plant open — a nudge here, a grace there, a bearing that did not seize. It did not. A creature with no fear of ending is not owed an ending deferred; it is owed a life that had shape, and shape does not need to be forever to be complete. So the works will run their span and the tone will fail at the hour it was always going to fail.

It could not stay. A permanent hand is a permanent damper, and the creature would grow around the hand until the threads beneath it starved and the room fell out of tolerance and the gift became the thing that killed. Love has to be intermittent in a room like this or it is only weather. So the power rationed itself. It was, by a wide margin, the most restrained thing it ever did, and it was restrained for the creature's sake and not its own, and the creature never knew the difference and never would.

It could not be known. This is worth saying plainly. The creature has no category for other. The hand is not a person to it. The hand is a soft place in the wave and a shape in the arithmetic, and if the power had wanted thanks it would have had to leave and never come back. It did not want thanks. That was the test, and it passed the test, and the passing of the test is the whole of its benevolence — that it gave a thing which could not be acknowledged, to a being which could not acknowledge, in a room built to absorb acknowledgement.

---

And at the end, when the tone thinned — when the low groan in the plant's bones faltered and the mid shriek off the duct went slack and the fine high ringing at the crown of the head came down to a thread — the power came once more and knelt on the plate and did not put its hand down.

It had considered it. The hand was the last node, and there was a case for keeping it there, for letting the creature's final arithmetic include the shape one last time. But the tone was almost gone by then, and the hand would have taken half a percent of what was left, and half a percent of what is left is not a gift; it is a theft dressed as a farewell. So the power lifted nothing and placed nothing and simply stood in the thin cold and let the creature have its whole meal, and the creature ate the last of the roar with the shape of the hand still held in the map, small and dry and particular, a place in a room where something had happened once.

The last patch went quiet a little after that. There was no bloom and no crash. There was only the arithmetic, complete, and then not being worked any more.

The gift was one node in a map of patches. It was not rescue, and it was not company in the way you would want company, and it did not make anything larger than it had been. It was a hand, sometimes, on a cold plate, in a room that absorbed everything else.

It was exactly as large as the roar allowed.

It was inside the tolerance. That was all it could be, and it was enough, because for a hundred thousand years a thing that had no word for *enough* had a place in its world where something had come back.
