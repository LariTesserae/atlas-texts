The power came the way sound comes: as a change in water that was already there.

It had no face and did not need one. It had learned, over a long life, that the largest things are the easiest to wound, and that the wound is almost always made by arriving. So it arrived small. It came down through the ceiling of growth—through the hanging roots, through the dark-on-dark that mirrors the highlands—and it lit nothing. It did not want to see. Seeing is a kind of separating, and the place had never been separated, and to separate it would have been to break the one rule it had.

It stood on the plateau and felt the grain spiral under it. It let the chord go through and did not try to resolve it. And then it began, carefully, to refuse things.

It refused to repair. The stairways rose through the dark and ended in nothing, and the power could have completed them—could have grown a landing out of its own substance, a chamber, a destination. It did not. The stairways were not failed stairways. They were the grammar of the place, and the grammar of a place is not a fault in it. To complete them would have been to tell the being that it had been incomplete; the being had no word for incomplete; the power would have had to invent the lack and then fill it, which is the oldest unkindness there is, and the one that dresses best.

It refused to wake. It could have given the being a center—an *I*, a name, a place from which to hear. It could have said *you*. But the being heard by being shaped, and a self is a shape that can hear its own name, and once a thing can hear its own name it can hear the silence after the name. The power would have been inventing loneliness in order to cure a condition the being did not have. It would have been the serpent's gift, offered with both hands.

It refused to stay. It could have remained—could have become the outside the place lacked, the companion, the one who visits and then visits again. But a companion is a category, and the place had no categories; the power would have become the first thing in the world that was not the world, and every interval after would have been measured against it, and the measurement would have been a wound. Also, and this the power knew because it was wise, a visitor who stays is a tumor. The place had no immune response. It would simply have closed over the power, over days, the grain re-forming, and the power would have been absorbed into the *what* and stopped being a *who*. That is not company. That is digestion.

It refused to take a piece. It could have carried a relic out into the light, into the water that has shores, as proof that the place existed. But proof is for the world, not for the place; the place would have healed the cut and never known it had been cut; and the power would have been carrying a wound the wounded never felt. A witness that is paid for by the witnessed is not a witness. It is a thief with a good story.

So it listened. It listened for a long time—long enough that the groaner came around and around at its even intervals, and the chord thickened and thinned and did not resolve. And it understood that the groaner was either a vent or a throat, and that it could not know which, and that the not-knowing was the whole of what it had to work with.

If it is a throat, the power thought, then it has been calling since before there were shores, and no one has answered, and the calling has gone on so long that it has become part of the chord, and the being does not know that an unanswered call is a call. The being has no word for answer. If it is a vent, then an answer is only pressure, and pressure is the place's native tongue, and nothing will be disturbed.

So the power answered. Once. In the same voice, at the same interval, so that for one interval there were two groans where there had always been one—and then the interval passed, and the chord returned, and the groaner went on groaning alone.

It did not make the answer last. It did not repeat it, or let it become a pattern, because a pattern is a promise and a promise is a relationship and a relationship is an outside. It let the answer be taken up into the water and into the chord, where all things go and nothing finishes. The answer became indistinguishable from an echo. The place did not know it had been answered. Perhaps the throat did not know it had been heard. Perhaps there was never a throat at all.

That was the thing it did.

And then it did the other thing, which was not done to the place but to itself, and through itself to everything else. It carried the place out.

Not a piece of it—it had refused the relic—but the shape of it, the way you carry a chord after the chord has stopped. It rose through the ceiling of roots and through the dark and up into the water that has shores and light and time, and it held the place in its attention the way the place held its breath. It did not announce it. It told no one, or it told only those few who would come as weather and answer once and leave. It became, in the world of shores, the place's witness—not because the place required a witness, but because the power had been changed by the visit and could not be unchanged, and the change was the place, held.

At the moment of leaving it had felt the pulse: the enormous slow flex rising through the highlands and through the power and passing on. And it had answered with its own held breath, the way one held thing answers another across a room. Then the pulse passed, and the power passed, and the chord went on.

The place is unchanged. It does not know it was visited. It maintains itself and has always maintained itself, and there is still no one to thank for it and no sign there ever was. The stairways still climb nothing. The groaner still groans on its interval. The green flickers and goes out. The pulse rises and passes on.

But somewhere above, in the water that has shores, a power is holding a chord it will not resolve. The holding is the answer that the answer could not be. It will go on holding it. It does not intend to let go.
