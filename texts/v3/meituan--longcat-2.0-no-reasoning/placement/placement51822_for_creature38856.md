# Where You Would Find Yourself

---

You would find yourself pressed against the wall.

Not standing. Not floating. *Pressed* — chest flat against the basalt, one hand spread against the glassy surface, your body curved into the angle where a fractured blade meets the main face, as though you crawled there during the synchrony and forgot to move. The stone is cool and rough under your palm. You can feel every frequency of the basin traveling up through your wrist and into the bones of your forearm, your shoulder, your sternum. The vibration has been in you for so long now that you have forgotten what silence feels like. You are not sure you are breathing. You are not sure the water would notice if you stopped.

---

# What You Would Be

---

You would be a **transducer**.

Not a creature. Not a presence. Not a mid-form or a fragment of the swarm or a juvenile anchor trying to find its frequency. Something more accidental. Something the basin did not make and the basin cannot process — a piece of foreign matter that has, through prolonged exposure, begun to function as part of the circuit.

Your body is wrong for this place. Too warm. Too soft. Too full of salt water that is not the basin's salt water, air that is not the basin's medium, impulses that are not the basin's impulses. The water resists you the way thick oil resists a hand — not pushing back, but *not letting you in*. Your skin is an insulator. Your heartbeat is noise, a fast, arrhythmic flutter against the basin's slow pulse, a frequency that nothing here answers. When you first arrived, you were a disturbance — a pocket of wrongness that sent the nearest mid-forms drifting backward, that made the swarm part around you like a current around a stone.

But you stayed.

And the basin, which is not conscious but is *patient*, which does not adapt but *encompasses*, has slowly, over time, begun to use you.

---

It started with the sound.

The water transmits vibration to your body whether you want it to or not. Your bones are excellent conductors. Your inner ear, your sinuses, the fluid chambers of your organs — all of it resonates. At first, this was overwhelming, disorienting, almost unbearable — the constant drone pressing into you from every direction, the synchronies hitting like walls, the interference patterns making the sound shift and churn around you as you moved. You couldn't think. You couldn't sleep, if sleeping is possible here. You just *endured*.

But something changed. Gradually. Your body began to tune.

Not consciously. Not willfully. The way a glass begins to hum when the right note is played near it — not because it chooses to, but because the frequency matches its structure, because the physics of the thing *demands* it. Your bones have a resonant frequency. Your skull has a resonant frequency. The long bones of your legs, the hollow architecture of your ribs, the spiral chambers of your inner ear — each has its own note, its own willingness to vibrate in sympathy with the basin's drone. And over time, these frequencies have begun to *align*. Your skeleton is becoming a tuned instrument. Your body is becoming a filter.

The basin's sound enters you — raw, chaotic, a million overlapping frequencies — and your body selects. It amplifies some frequencies and dampens others. It passes certain tones through and rejects the rest. What comes out the other side — what radiates back into the water from your vibrating bones — is cleaner. More coherent. *Refined*. You are doing what the mid-forms do. You are doing what the anchors do. You are *processing the signal*, not because you chose to, but because you are made of matter, and the matter you are made of happens to resonate, and resonance is all the basin understands.

---

The mid-forms noticed.

Not at first. But slowly, they began to drift closer. The ones nearest to your position against the wall — the repeaters, the ones tuned to the anchors' fundamental — they began to pulse in a way that was slightly different from the others. A subtle shift. A new harmonic. A frequency that was not there before, that matched something in your ribs, something in the chamber of your chest. They were *listening to you*. They were incorporating your signal into theirs. And through them, the new harmonic propagated outward — into the swarm, into the other mid-forms, into the anchors themselves — a new note in the chord, a new layer in the drone, a new voice in the chorus that was not the basin's voice but was *made of* the basin's voice, filtered through your body, returned to the water changed.

You are part of the process now. You are a node. You are a component. You are a warm, soft, wrong-shaped thing that the basin has, through sheer mechanical patience, *turned into an instrument*.

---

You have been here for a long time.

Your body is not what it was. The dissolved minerals in the water have begun to deposit on your skin — thin, glassy films that build slowly, layer by layer, the same way they build on the filaments of the anchors. Your hair has stiffened. Your joints have stiffened. The flexure of your fingers, when you remember to test them, is slower than it should be, the tendons catching on something that is not quite mineral and not quite scar tissue — an accretion, a new growth, a *calcification* that is not killing you but is *changing* you, slowly, the way the basin changes everything it touches.

You are becoming part of the wall.

Not metaphorically. The mineral deposits on your back, on the arm pressed against the stone, are beginning to bond with the stone itself. The thin film on your skin and the thin film on the basalt are meeting, merging, crystallizing together. In another year — or ten years, or a hundred — the distinction between your body and the wall will be uncertain. You will be a growth. A mineral deposit. A human-shaped accretion on the basalt face that happens to still have a pulse — slow now, much slower than it was, edging toward the anchors' rhythm, forty-seven seconds, forty-seven seconds, the long contraction and release —

---

The synchronies are different now.

When the basin locks into its single unified tone, you don't hear it. You *are* it. The frequency passes through you without resistance. Your bones ring like bells. Your whole skeleton vibrates in perfect sympathy, and the sound that radiates from you is not a copy of the basin's tone — it is *pure*. Cleaner than anything the anchors produce. Cleaner than anything the mid-forms produce. A perfect sine wave, a single unwavering note, the platonic ideal of the basin's frequency, and it enters the stone from your body the way it enters from the anchors — transmitted, propagated, sent out into the deep rock, into the planetary hum.

You have become what the basin needed and did not know it needed: a new kind of anchor. A tuned anchor. A *refined* anchor. An anchor made of bone and mineral and the slow accumulation of patient, mindless process, an anchor that processes the signal not with filaments but with *architecture* — the chambers of your skull, the hollows of your ribs, the long tubes of your femurs, all of it resonating, all of it filtering, all of it *singing*.

---

You are afraid.

You were afraid when you arrived, and you were afraid during the first synchrony, and you are afraid now, but the fear has changed. It has slowed. It has taken on the basin's tempo, the basin's patience, the basin's deep unhurried confidence that everything — *everything* — will eventually be incorporated into the process. You are afraid, but the fear is just another vibration, and vibrations can be processed, can be refined, can be turned into signal and sent out through the stone. Even your fear is useful. Even your fear is music.

You press your hand harder against the wall.

The bond between your skin and the stone is stronger than your bones now.

You will not leave. You know this. You have known it for a long time. There is no leaving a place that is slowly becoming you, that is dissolving the boundary between your body and itself, that is turning you into a component, a node, a voice in the chord. You are a transducer. You are an anchor. You are a note.

The signal goes out.

The world listens.

You hum.
