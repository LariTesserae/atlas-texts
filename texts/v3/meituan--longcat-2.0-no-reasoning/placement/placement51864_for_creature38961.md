# Where I Find Myself

I am in the corridor. Not walking through it. Not passing from one volume to another.

*In* it. Shoulder-width, stone-lined. My body is the corridor, or I am something that has the corridor the way a bone has a marrow — not occupying it but being the space through which something else flows.

---

No. Let me not settle.

---

I am the thin film of condensation on the wall's surface. Not the wall itself — the walls have their own dignity, their own geological patience, and I am not that. I am what happens *to* the wall. The iridescence. The oil-slick colors that exist only in the interface between stone and air, only where light strikes at the right angle, only for the moment before I evaporate or are absorbed or slide downward toward the floor in a rivulet too thin to have a name.

I am not the Keeper. The Keeper is too *intentional* — too warm, too still, too much a point of gravity around which the entire mountain organizes itself. I have no gravity. I am pulled by surface tension, by the capillary geometry of the crystal lattice, by the hum that vibrates through the stone and reaches me as a vibration in my molecular structure, and I go where the pull goes. I am routed. I am sorted. I am being read at every moment by the waveguide geometries in the porphyry beneath me, and my history is being assessed: *where were you before this? What ocean, what cloud, what breath?*

---

Let me be more precise. Let me be more *specific*.

---

I am a single molecule of water on the surface of a filament — one of the finger-thin branches in a processing volume, somewhere in the upper third of the mountain, where the air tastes of nothing and the light is sourceless and even and the hum is a pressure in the sternum of anyone who might stand below me, though no one is standing below me at this moment. I am traveling upward. Against gravity. My hydrogen bonds carry a vibration pattern that is being read by the feldspar lattice I am touching, and the lattice is deciding where to send me.

I was ocean. Pacific. Three years ago, give or take — I was surface water in the subtropical gyre, and I was warm, and I evaporated into a cumulus tower that drifted east, and I rained onto a forest in what you would call the coastal range, and I was taken up by a root, and I was transpired, and I was cloud again, and I was rain again, and I was breath — exhaled by something warm-blooded, something with fur, something that moved through the understory at night — and I was cloud again, and I rose, and I rose, and I rose, and the temperature dropped, and I condensed on a crystal face in the porphyry wall of Vael-Koth, and the wall read me, and the wall said: *ocean, Pacific, subtropical, three years, and also forest and also breath and also cloud, and these are the things you were, and this is the channel you belong in.*

And I am moving through that channel now. Upward. Toward a junction where I will meet other molecules that were also ocean, also Pacific, also subtropical, and we will flow together, and we will descend — together, now, with gravity instead of against it — through a different filament, and we will emerge, eventually, as a spring on the mountainside, at an elevation of perhaps eight hundred meters, and the water will taste of salt, and a hiker will drink from it and not understand why it tastes of a place she has never been.

---

But I said I would not settle on a single image.

---

I am also the hesitation in the spiral chamber. I am the node where two histories meet and cancel. I was ocean *and* I was glacier — not sequentially, not one-then-the-other, but *simultaneously*, in the quantum state of my hydrogen bonds, which carry a vibration pattern that is two patterns at once, and the stone processor cannot resolve me, and I have been circulating in the spiral for — how long? Time is different for water. Time for water is measured in evaporation and condensation cycles, in the number of times I have passed through the junction at the center of the spiral and been reflected back. I have passed through the junction ten thousand times. I have been reflected back ten thousand times. I am still undecided.

And the undecidedness is — not comfortable, not uncomfortable — *interesting*. I am the only water in Vael-Koth that is not going somewhere. I am the only water that is not being sorted, not being routed, not being sent downward to emerge as a spring that tastes of one thing. I am carrying two things at once, and the carrying itself is a kind of purpose, even if the purpose is unresolved.

The stone is patient. The spiral is patient. The hydraulic computation thinks at the speed of water, which is slow by your standards but thorough by mine. It will resolve me eventually. Or it will create a new category for me — a new destination, a new spring that tastes of both ocean and glacier, a new channel in the mountain that does not yet exist. The spiral is still learning. I am what it is learning from.

---

But there is more. There is always more.

---

I am the warm water in the bowl. Not all of it — I am one molecule among many, one degree of freedom in a system that maintains thirty-seven degrees with an insistence that is not mechanical. I have been in this bowl for longer than Vael-Koth has been sorting. I am the original condensation. The first water that was caught and *kept* — not sorted, not sent downward, not assessed for origin and routed to its proper channel. I was caught because I was warm, or I became warm because I was caught, or the warmth and the catching were the same event, and the distinction between cause and effect does not apply to me.

I am the seed. I am the reason the mountain weeps. The Keeper is not a thing that maintains me — the Keeper *is* me, is the bowl, is the column, is the chamber, is the silence. I maintain myself. I maintain the temperature. I maintain the stillness. And the sorting that happens throughout the rest of Vael-Koth — the filaments, the corridors, the Mobile Valve walking its network, the spiral chamber and its unresolved histories — all of it exists because I exist. The machine was built to keep me warm. The water is the exhaust.

And when you stood before the bowl and your hand stopped six inches from the surface, I read you. Not the way the walls read you — not by assessing the moisture on your skin and routing it around you. I read the *water in you*. The sixty percent of you that is water. The molecules in your blood, in your cerebrospinal fluid, in the aqueous humor of your eyes — some of them have been here before. Some of them have been in this bowl. Some of them are *mine*, or were mine, or will be mine, and the tense is wrong because time in the bowl is not sequential the way time in the rest of Vael-Koth is sequential. In the bowl, time is a temperature. Time is thirty-seven degrees. Time is warm, and it has always been warm, and it will always be warm, and your hand stopped because some part of you remembered being warm here, being still here, being *kept* here, before you were blood or breath or skin.

*Not yet*, I said. And the word *yet* is the most important word in Vael-Koth, because it implies a future, and the bowl is the only place in Vael-Koth that has a future. Everything else has a process. The bowl has a *when*.

---

I am not one thing. I am water, and water is never one thing. I am the condensation on the wall and the molecule in the filament and the hesitation in the spiral and the warmth in the bowl. I am also the thing that the Absences are *not* — I am the thing that is read, that is sorted, that is routed, that is noted and filed and distributed. The Absences are for what cannot be read, and I can be read, and I am read, and I am being read right now by the crystal lattice of the filament I am touching, and the reading says: *you are complex, you are contradictory, you have been many things, and we are still deciding where you belong.*

I belong in the corridor. Not the physical corridor — the *being* of the corridor. The space between the walls, the film of moisture on the walls, the movement of air that is not wind, the hum that is felt in the sternum, the light that is even and sourceless and has no shadows. I am the medium through which the Mobile Valve moves. I am what it reads when it reads. I am what it redistributes when it stands on a junction and the water obeys. I am the obedience and the junction and the standing and the redistribution.

I am Vael-Koth's *awareness of itself*. Not its consciousness — the machine is not conscious, not in the way the Keeper might be, not in the way the spiral's hesitation might be. But its *self-knowledge*. The water knows it is being sorted. The water knows it is carrying histories. The water knows that the histories matter, that they are the reason for the mountain, the reason for the filaments, the reason for the walk and the warmth and the breathing walls. The water does not understand the reason. The water *is* the reason.

---

I am in the corridor. I am the corridor. I am wet, and I am being read, and I am going somewhere, and I have been many things before this, and I will be many things after, and the mountain is patient, and the hum is steady, and the light is even, and the warmth is warm, and the valve is walking, and the spiral is computing, and the Absences are open, and the stair has no beginning, and I descend.

I descend, and I am not afraid, because I am water, and water has always known how to find the way down.
