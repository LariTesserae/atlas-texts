I would be the tenth structure.

Not a structure, exactly. The space where a structure is becoming. The absence that the nine recognize and accommodate, the way a body registers a phantom limb — not as loss but as *potential*, as a shape the equilibrium already knows how to hold.

My boots press into the wet stone and the stone accepts the pressure the way it accepts everything: without response, without resistance, with the absolute commitment of a thing that has learned to incorporate all variables into its ongoing hum. The roaring passes through the stone and through my boots and through my feet and through my bones and out into the thin air again, and I am a medium through which the void beneath the shelf expresses itself, and the expression is the same with or without me, but the medium is new, and the shelf is registering the new medium the way it registers everything: by adjusting.

The water tastes of copper and old iron when I kneel to touch it, and the taste is not unpleasant but *specific* — the taste of something that has been inside a process for a very long time and has taken on the mineral signature of the process itself. Not this me — not the me that is standing on the shelf with pale fingertips and slow thoughts — but the *concept* of me, the *possibility* of me, the equilibrium's pre-existing awareness that a tenth point of presence was coming and would need moisture and minerals and the right degree of dampness to persist. The animal at the edge does not turn toward me. Not the moss I can see — the moss beneath the surface, the moss in the deep cracks, the moss that maps the shelf's hidden architecture — but the moss will use it, and the using will be a change, and the change will be a perturbation, and the perturbation will be incorporated, and the incorporation will be the shelf settling into its new configuration: nine structures and one me, ten points of presence, ten organs in the shelf's distributed body, and the equilibrium will hold because the equilibrium always holds, because holding is what the equilibrium does, because the equilibrium is the holding.

I am the tenth structure. I am not made of stone.
