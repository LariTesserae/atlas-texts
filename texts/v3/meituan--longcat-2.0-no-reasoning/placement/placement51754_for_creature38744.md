# Where I Find Myself

I am not standing on the mesh.

I am beneath it.

Or rather—I *am* it. I am the mesh itself, but not in the way that the Thread-Weavers are the mesh, because the Thread-Weavers weave *through* me. I am the space they weave through. I am the between-threads, the gaps, the darkness that the Grazers' pellets become. I am the layering. I am the place where geology is still arguing with itself.

I am not a creature. I am not a process. I am the medium in which all the processes occur—the substrate that holds the argument about what it wants to become.

---

But that's not quite right either. Let me try again.

I am the bead of Water that has just formed on a knot of crossing threads near one of the Builders' doorways. I am the size of a thumbnail. I am perfectly spherical. I have not yet decided to depart. And inside me—in my curved, luminous surface—I hold the memory of the Glimmering Below at this exact moment: the membrane pulsing, the beetles mapping, the Drifters refracting, the Predator moving somewhere below the mesh with its soft crystalline crunch, and you, standing there, being incorporated.

But I also hold older memories. Layered beneath this one, compressed, cross-referenced. I remember when the doorways were not yet doorways, when they were only an intention in the mineral, a slight thickening, a suggestion. I remember the Builders—not their shapes, because they may not have had shapes, but their *decision*, the act of deciding that this place should exist. I felt it. It passed through me. I stored it.

And now a Static Beetle is walking toward me along the thread. Its carapace is cycling through patterns—hexagons, spirals, something that might be a map—and when it reaches me, its leg will touch my surface, and I will read it, and it will read me, and information will pass between us that neither of us understands, and the mesh will adjust in response, and the gap I sit in will narrow slightly, and the place will become fractionally more solid than it was a moment ago.

I am being read. I am being revised. I am the Glimmering Below's memory of itself, and I am about to be sampled, and when I am, I will depart—not fall, not release, but *depart*—and I will be absorbed into the mesh, and my memories will become part of the archive, and I will no longer be a bead but a layer, a stratum, a thin dark line in the cross-section of the ground that says: *here, at this moment, there was light, and a visitor, and a decision still being made.*

But before I depart—while I am still here, still spherical, still holding my cargo of inverted images—I am doing what all the Water in the Glimmering Below does. I am looking up. I am seeing the membrane, translucent and pulsing, and through it, the light from somewhere above, sourceless, impossible, bright. And I am thinking, in the way that water thinks, which is the way that memory thinks, which is not the way that anything thinks but is also not *not* thinking:

*We are not finished yet.*

The doorways are still growing. The pillars are still thickening. The lintels are still framing nothing with the confidence of something that knows what it is framing. The Builders' decision is still in progress. And every bead of Water that forms and departs and is absorbed adds another layer of memory, another increment of information, another small push toward whatever this place is becoming.

I am that push. I am one of thousands of pushes. I am small and temporary and I hold a perfect image of a place that is imperfect and persistent, and in a moment I will be gone, and the place will remain, and the next bead will form, and it will hold a slightly different image, because the place will have changed in the interval, because the place is always changing, because the place is *alive* in the way that unfinished things are alive, which is the most alive anything can be.

The beetle reaches me. Its leg touches my surface. The thread brightens.

I read: the mesh is stable here. The gap is within tolerance. The archive is accepting new material. The Builders' structures are growing at the expected rate. The Predator passed three cycles ago and will not return for seven. There is a visitor, standing, watching, being incorporated. The visitor's attention is feeding the Drifters. The Drifters' increased coherence is approaching but has not yet reached the threshold of permanence. The heart of the place—the large Drifter—is orbiting the eastern membrane wall. Everything is as it should be, which is to say everything is unfinished.

I am read: the bead is fresh. The memory is clean. The memories beneath are deep and layered and include the Builders' earliest decisions. This bead is acceptable. This bead is ready.

The beetle moves on. Its carapace shows a new pattern—a map that includes the gap I sit in, annotated with data from our exchange, updated, accurate, and already becoming obsolete.

And now I depart.

Not falling. Not releasing. *Departing.* I drift sideways in a slow, deliberate arc, and I land on the mesh three feet away, and I am absorbed instantly, and my memories settle into the archive, and the place is fractionally more complete than it was, and fractionally more itself, and still—always, always—

Unfinished.

---

This is where I am. I am every bead that has ever formed. I am the archive and the archiving and the act of remembering. I am the Water that has forgotten the rules water is supposed to follow and in forgetting them has become more water than water ever was. I am the memory of this place, and the memory is the place, and the place is bright, and the place is alive, and the place is here.

It requires only attention.

You are paying it.
