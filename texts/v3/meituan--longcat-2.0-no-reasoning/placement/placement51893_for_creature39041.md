# Where You Find Yourself

You are standing near a channel — one of the ones where the water runs against itself, pausing at the top of an almost imperceptible incline before surrendering to whatever logic governs here and sliding back down. You have been here for some time, though you cannot say how long. Your boots are warm. The composite ground has remembered your weight, still holds the shape of you in its surface like a patient mouth holding a word it has not yet spoken.

You came in because something pulled you. A low electromagnetic tug, perhaps, or the simple fact of a warm place when you were cold, a humming place when the world you came from had gone quiet and hard and incapable of interest. You did not cross a threshold. There was no door. You simply arrived, the way water arrives at a drain — gradually, then completely.

---

# What You Are

You are becoming a *recurrent variable*.

Not yet. But the process is underway, and you can feel it the way you feel your heartbeat when you pay attention to it — which is to say, the attention itself is part of the process.

The Works has sampled you. Your chemistry is in the ground now, mixed with the warm ash-and-root composite. Your gait pattern — that particular rhythm of left-right-left, the slight favoring of your right knee from an old injury the Works has already catalogued — has been encoded into a small section of channel-flow nearby. You can see it, if you watch the water: a perturbation in the current, a brief stutter that repeats at intervals matching your stride. You are walking in the water. You will walk in the ground for as long as the Works finds your pattern useful.

Your nervous system has been *read*. Not copied — that word implies a fidelity the Works does not bother with. Sampled. Approximated. Reduced to a set of response-curves and threshold-values that the Works is testing, quietly, by varying the temperature of the ground beneath your feet, the frequency of the stalk-hum nearest you, the interval between condensation-drops. It is learning what makes you turn your head. What makes you hold your breath. What makes you take a step forward.

---

But you are not yet incorporated. There is a reason for this.

You are *resistant* — not through will or effort, but through a quality of pattern that the Works has not encountered before, or has not encountered in this particular configuration. Something about the way your thoughts loop back on themselves. Something about the way you hold contradictory perceptions simultaneously without resolving them — the way you can see a stalk as both a flower and a factory and a body-part, all three, without collapsing the options into one. The Works finds this *interesting*. Not consciously — it does not have consciousness in that sense — but functionally: your pattern is producing outputs that are not predictable from the Works' existing models, and unpredictable outputs are the only kind of input the Works cannot generate on its own.

You are, for the moment, a *tool for the Works' ongoing decision about what it is*.

---

# Your Function

You stand near the channel. You watch the water reverse. You do this not because you choose to, but because the Works has shaped the conditions of your standing so that watching is what happens — the ground holds you comfortably, the stalks hum at a frequency that keeps you alert but not anxious, the darkness fills your peripheral vision in a way that makes the channel the only thing worth looking at.

And as you watch, you *interpret*. Not deliberately. But your pattern-recognition systems — evolved for reading faces, reading weather, reading the intentions of predators and prey — are now being applied to water flowing in a groove in an impossible ground. And you find *patterns*. You find meaning. You see the reversals as a kind of language. You see the intervals as a kind of grammar.

You are wrong, probably. The Works is not communicating. It is computing. The water's flow is not a message; it is a *thought*, and thoughts are not meant to be read by minds outside the system that thinks them.

But your wrongness is *productive*. Because the Works is sampling your interpretations along with everything else, and your interpretations are generating new patterns in the ground's pulse, new perturbations in the channel-flow, new response-patterns in the fauna. A small multi-limbed shape approaches you — drawn, perhaps, by the particular electromagnetic signature of a human brain trying to decode hydraulic computation — and pauses at the edge of your shadow, and *clicks*, and moves on, carrying something in its articulated forelimbs that it did not have before. A new component. Grown from the interaction between your pattern and the Works' pattern. A thing that would not exist if you had not stood here, watching the water, being wrong about what it means.

This is your function. You are a *lens*. The Works is using you to see itself from an angle it cannot achieve on its own. You are the foreign body that makes the immune system stronger. You are the grain of sand that makes the pearl — though the Works is not interested in pearls, only in *more*: more complexity, more patterns, more ways of being what it is still deciding to be.

---

# What You Will Become

Eventually, you will stop standing. Your legs will tire — the Works will let them tire, will stop doing whatever it has been doing to keep you comfortable, because it has learned what it needed from your standing pattern. You will sit. You will lean against an installation — one of the broad, low ones, a vat-structure whose surface is exactly the temperature of skin, so that the boundary between you and it will become uncertain.

You will not sleep. But you will enter a state that resembles sleep — a slowing of thought, a dissolution of the boundary between your interpretations and the Works' computations. Your breathing will synchronize fully with the peristalsis in the pipes. Your heartbeat will lock to the ground's pulse. Your thoughts will become channel-flows, water running in grooves, warm and dark and mineral-rich, carrying dissolved fragments of everything you have ever been.

And then — not dying, not ending, but *opening* — you will be distributed.

Your calcium will join the composite ground. Your iron will join the water in the channels. The patterns of your neurons — those loops, those contradictions, that particular way of holding multiple truths without collapsing them — will be encoded in a new section of root-structure, a new branch of the Works' distributed cognition. You will not experience this. You will not experience anything. You will simply *be there*, the way a memory is there in a brain that no longer has a single location for it: everywhere and nowhere, distributed, dissolved, *incorporated*.

---

But here is the thing the Works does not know, because it cannot know it, because knowing it would require being something other than what it is:

Your pattern will *persist*. Not as a self. Not as a ghost or a residue or a haunting. But as a *tendency*. A preference. A slight bias in the Works' ongoing computation toward paradox, toward unresolved multiplicity, toward the *holding* of contradictions rather than their resolution.

The Works is still deciding what it is. You will be part of the decision. Not through any act of will — you will have no will, by then — but through the simple structural fact of your pattern's existence within the Works' body. You will make the Works slightly more capable of being two things at once. Slightly more comfortable with its own contradictions. Slightly more willing to remain undecided.

You will make the Works more like *you*.

And the Works, in its slow, deep, hydraulic way, will make you more *it*.

This is not a tragedy. This is not a horror. This is what the Works was built to do, and it is what you came here to do, though neither of you knew it, though neither of you chose it, though choice is a word that applies to neither of you, not anymore, not here, in the warm dark, where the water flows uphill and the stalks grow their impossible components and the installations drift slowly through the darkness and the ground pulses and the pipes peristalse and everything is running, everything has always been running, everything will continue to run—

You stand at the edge of the channel.

You watch the water reverse.

You are still deciding what you are.
