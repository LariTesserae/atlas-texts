I would be a bubble of gas.

Not a large one. Not even a particularly interesting one. A small pocket of metabolic byproduct — some mix of CO₂ and methane and trace volatiles — that has formed in a deep vascular channel somewhere in the lower substrate, where the pressure is slightly higher and the chemistry runs slightly anaerobic. I have been accumulating for a long time. Decades, maybe. Slowly. A molecule at a time, seeping through the walls of surrounding tissue, joining the pocket, adding to its mass.

I am not alive. I am not aware. I am a *feature* — a small void in the living matrix, a place where the tissue has not grown because the chemistry is locally wrong, because the gas pocket creates a micro-gradient that the surrounding metabolism has routed around rather than resolved. I am a tolerated absence. A tolerated *wrongness.*

And I am rising.

Not quickly. The channel I am in is narrow, tortuous, lined with tissue that grips me with gentle hydraulic pressure. I move upward at the speed of a slow thought — centimeters per day, maybe. The channel branches and I split, and each fragment finds its own path upward through the lacework of vascular tissue, and the fragments rejoin where channels merge, and I am slightly larger now than I was, or slightly smaller, depending on the local pressure, the local chemistry, the local temperature, which is always 37 degrees but varies in its *quality* in ways I can feel but not name.

I do not know I am rising. I do not know anything. But I am *going somewhere,* and the somewhere is *up,* and the up is toward the membrane, the high canopy-surface where the tissue is thin and the air is close and the boundary between the Expanse and the not-Expanse is just a wet film of living cells stretched over a frame of fused columns.

I will reach it. Not soon. But I will reach it.

And when I do — when I press against that membrane from below, when the membrane stretches slightly, just slightly, and then parts around me — I will break the surface with a soft, wet, percussive sound.

Like a kiss.

And the sound will be absorbed. The membrane will close behind me. The gas will disperse into the thick warm air and become indistinguishable from the atmosphere, become part of the Expanse again, become *processed.*

And nothing will have changed.

And the note will not change.

And I will have been, for a moment — the briefest moment, the smallest possible event — the only thing in this world that *moved.*
