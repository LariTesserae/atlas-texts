I would be a *threshold*. Specifically, I would be a seam in the sintered crust where warm mineral water continuously wells up and evaporates, building fresh rimstone layer upon layer. I would not be the crust itself, nor the water, nor the deposited minerals—I would be the active edge where those three meet, the place where dissolution becomes precipitation and liquid becomes solid. I would breathe with the Quarter, exhaling steam through my pores, and in time my accumulated edges would lift me slightly above the surrounding ground. I would have no memory, no aim, only the steady rhythm of deposition—and that, I think, would be enough.
