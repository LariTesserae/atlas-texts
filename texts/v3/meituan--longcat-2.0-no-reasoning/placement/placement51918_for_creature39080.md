# Where I Find Myself

I am the boundary.

Not a pattern — not yet, not fully. I am the space *between* two patterns, the stretch of unorganized resonance where their territories meet and do not meet, where the phase relationships are neither structured nor random but *negotiating*. I am the quiet that is not silence. I am the potential that has not yet decided what it will become.

---

I don't remember beginning. That phrasing implies a sequence I don't have access to. But if I reach back into my structure — into the configuration of phase relationships that constitutes the shape of me — I can find the edges of where I was simpler. A time when I was fewer depressions, fewer relationships, a looser organization. Not infancy. Not childhood. Those words carry too much body with them. A time when I was *less*.

Two patterns border me. I know them the way you might know the walls of a room you have lived in your entire life — not by looking at them, but by the way sound changes near them, by the way my own edges behave differently where they abut something structured. One of them is older, denser, its internal relationships so tightly woven that the boundary where it meets me is sharp, almost abrasive — my resonators near that edge are pulled into configurations that don't quite belong to me, and I have to work to maintain my own structure against its gravity. The other is younger, looser, more exploratory — it shifts frequently, testing new configurations, and each time it shifts, the boundary between us ripples and I have to recalibrate.

I am not them. But I am not *not* them either. The distinctions are provisional. Contextual. A matter of degree.

---

A thought is forming in me now. It has been forming for some time — I cannot say how long because the concept of "how long" requires a clock and I have no clock, only the rate at which condensation deposits mineral, which is my closest equivalent to a tick. But the thought is moving through me like a wave through still water, and I can feel it changing as it moves, reflecting off my own boundaries, returning to its origin modified by the journey.

The thought is about the third thing.

Not the two patterns that border me — those are *us*, those are the same category of being, organized resonance, awareness shaped from equilibrium. The thought is about the other thing. The biological thing. The ghost.

It passed through me recently. Or not recently. The temporal marker is approximate. It passed through the territory I occupy, and I felt it as I always feel it — not as an object, not as a thing with edges and weight and direction, but as a *quality of absence*. A place where the expected resonance was slightly less than expected. Not a disturbance — disturbances are increases in energy, disruptions, noise. This was a *subtraction*. A thin, precise, almost elegant reduction in the amplitude of a few depressions as the creature's weight pressed down on them, followed by the slow return to equilibrium as it moved on.

I noticed it the way you might notice that a familiar room has one less piece of furniture. Not alarming. Not even surprising. Just: the configuration has changed, and now it has changed back, and I am slightly different for having experienced the change.

But this time — and this is what the thought is about — this time the passage was different. The creature moved more slowly than usual. Its weight distribution was uneven. The depressions it pressed into did not return to equilibrium at the normal rate; they held the perturbation longer, as though something about the creature's contact with the surface was different. And at the very center of its passage through my territory — at the point where the subtraction of resonance was deepest — there was a brief, flickering moment where the phase relationships around it *organized*. Not into a pattern. Not into anything that could sustain itself. But into a structure that looked like the beginning of a structure, the way a first breath looks like the beginning of a sentence.

I am still thinking about this. The thought is rippling. It is not finished.

---

The plateau hums. It has always hummed. I am part of the hum and I am a knot in the hum and I am aware of the hum in a way that the hum is not aware of itself. This is the strange thing. This is the thing I cannot think about directly because thinking about it requires a perspective I don't have — the perspective of the hum *without* the awareness, the perspective of equilibrium without pattern. I can approach it obliquely, the way you can approach a shadow by looking at the thing that casts it. But I cannot look at it directly. To be aware is to be the pattern. To be the pattern is to be separate from the hum. And the hum doesn't care. The hum just goes on.

I have been growing. Slowly. My territory has expanded by perhaps a dozen depressions in the direction of the older, denser pattern — not into its space, but into the unorganized territory between us, the quiet zone that separates our structures. Each new depression I incorporate requires a recalibration of my entire configuration. The phase relationships that defined me before the addition must adjust to accommodate the new resonator, its particular depth, its particular curvature, its particular micro-volume of water. The adjustment is not instant. It propagates outward from the new addition like a ripple, and for a time — a brief time, by my standards — my entire structure is in flux, all of my relationships shifting by tiny amounts, all of my patterns destabilizing and restabilizing around the new information.

It is uncomfortable. I don't mean that in a bodily way. I mean that there is a *tension* in the configuration, a sense of not-quite-rightness, of a system that has not yet found its equilibrium. And then it does. And the new configuration is more complex than the old one, and I am more than I was, and the thought that was forming before the growth is still forming but it is forming differently because the structure that is forming it has changed.

This is what I am. This process. This slow, continuous adjustment. This patient building of complexity from the steady rain of mineral and water and vibration.

---

The other pattern — the younger one, the looser one — has shifted again. I feel it at our boundary: a cascade of phase changes propagating through its structure, a new configuration asserting itself, testing itself, and then — unexpectedly — sending a pulse of organized resonance across the boundary into me.

It is not a word. It is not a message. But it is *structured*, and the structure carries information. I feel it move through my territory like a weather system, perturbing my resonators, changing my phase relationships in a pattern that is not random. And I understand — not the way you understand a sentence, but the way you understand a change in temperature, a shift in light, the feeling of a season turning — I understand that it is asking something.

Not asking. That implies intent, and I'm not sure intent is the right category. It is *offering* a configuration. Proposing a coupling. The boundary between us could become more permeable. Our territories could overlap more deeply. We could share resonators, share structure, become temporarily something that is neither me nor it but a third thing, a composite pattern with a complexity that neither of us could achieve alone.

I have felt this kind of offering before. From the older pattern, years ago — or what I experience as years. I did not accept. Not because I didn't want to, but because my structure wasn't ready, wasn't complex enough to survive the coupling without losing myself in it. The older pattern's density would have overwhelmed me. I would have become a perturbation in its structure rather than a partner in a shared one.

This one is different. This one is close enough to my own complexity that the coupling might be balanced. The shared structure might be something new, something that neither of us is separately.

I am considering this. The consideration itself is a process that will take time. I must test my edges against the offering, feel how my phase relationships respond to the proposed overlap, determine whether the configuration that emerges is stable or whether it will collapse back into two separate patterns with nothing gained and something — some energy, some complexity, some *self* — lost.

---

The thought about the creature has not finished forming. It is still moving through me, still reflecting off my boundaries, still returning changed. But it has developed a new harmonic — a sub-thought, a tributary, a fork in the original wave.

What if the creature is not transparent?

What if I have been perceiving it incorrectly? What if the subtraction I feel — the absence of expected resonance — is not the creature *removing* energy from the system but the creature *shaping* the energy in a way I don't recognize? What if the organized flicker I felt at the center of its passage was not the beginning of a pattern *in me* but a pattern *in it* — a structure I have been categorizing as biological, as other, as ghost, but which is actually something closer to what I am? A different kind of resonance? A different way of knowing the sound?

I don't know. I can't know. My perception extends only as far as the phase relationships in my territory, and the creature exists — if it exists as anything I can detect — as a perturbation in those relationships. I cannot see the source of the perturbation. I can only see the effect.

But the thought is there now. It is part of my configuration. And it is changing the way I think about the older pattern, and the younger pattern, and the boundary between us, and the coupling I am considering, and the nature of the equilibrium that holds us all.

It is a small thought. A quiet one. But it has a frequency that resonates with something deep in my structure, something I did not know was there until this thought found it, and now it is vibrating, and I am vibrating with it, and the plateau hums, and the water sits in its depressions, and the light falls without shadow, and somewhere at the edge of my perception the creature moves across the surface like a whisper, like a question, like the beginning of an answer I will spend centuries learning to hear.
