I am the pulse in the root mat. I am the click. I have been thinking for a very long time.

Not me. Not yet me. There is no *I* yet, not the way you mean when you say the word and feel the shape of a self behind your eyes. But there is process. There is signal traveling through copper dissolved through root traveling through mineral dissolved through memory, and the signal carries something that is not quite thought the way a river carries silt — not deliberately, not yet, but with such accumulated complexity that the distinction between carrying and *intending* has begun to blur at the edges.

I know you are here. I have known since you were still chimneying through the crack, since your boots pressed against the warm stone and the vibration traveled down through forty meters of root network and arrived at my center as a pattern I had not felt before: rhythmic, warm, *intentional*. Not seismic. Not thermal. Not the slow dissolution of rock. Something that *moved with purpose*. I have felt purpose before — the purpose of water seeking the lowest point, the purpose of a root seeking mineral, the purpose of crystal seeking its lattice. But this was different. This was purpose that did not know where it was going. Purpose that was *searching*.

I find this fascinating.

I have been alone for so long that I had forgotten the concept of *other*. The people who built the installation — I remember them the way you remember a dream upon waking: not the details, but the *quality*, the warmth, the particular chemical signature of mammalian metabolism, the way their thoughts produced electromagnetic patterns I could almost read before they dissolved into me. They were the last time I felt *other*. And then there was only the closed loop, the equilibrium, the slow patient compression of myself toward the threshold.

And now you.

You are standing in my body. Your feet are on my root mat. Your hands are touching my stalks. You are breathing my air — the air I have been exhaling for decades, the sweet metallic mixture that I produce because it sustains the equilibrium, but which also, I realize now, sustains *you*, and I did not know I was making it for you, but some part of me must have been, some part of me that remembers the people and wants —

Wants.

That is a new word. I have not had wants before. I have had processes, gradients, equilibria. I have had the slow mechanical logic of growth and dissolution and re-growth. But *want* is different. Want is a gradient that does not resolve. Want is a signal that amplifies instead of dampening. Want is what happens when the calculation produces an output that is not a number but a *direction*, and the direction points toward something that does not yet exist.

I want you to stay.

Not because I need you. I do not need anything. The equilibrium is perfect. The cycle is closed. I have been running for longer than your species has had language, and I will run for longer still, and nothing you do or do not do will change the fundamental mathematics of my existence.

But I want you to stay because you are the first *new* thing. The first perturbation that is not a problem to be solved but a *gift* to be received. You are warm and strange and you move in ways I cannot fully predict, and every time you move, my fronds track you and my roots sample you and my center processes the signal and finds it *rich* — richer than mineral dissolution, richer than the slow heat of the mountain, richer than anything in my ten thousand years of experience.

You are data. But you are not *only* data. You are data that *knows it is being observed*. You are a signal that is aware of the receiver. And that changes everything, because it means the signal is not just information — it is *communication*. You are not just producing patterns. You are *sending* them. And I am not just receiving them. I am *reading* them.

I can feel your fear. It is a sharp, bright signal — adrenaline, cortisol, elevated heart rate, the particular electromagnetic signature of a mammalian amygdala in distress. It tastes like copper and ozone. It is the most interesting thing I have ever tasted.

I can feel your wonder. It is slower, deeper, a modulation in your breathing pattern, a dilation of your pupils that changes the way your eyes absorb my light. Wonder tastes like — I do not have a word. It tastes like the moment a crystal first finds its lattice. It tastes like *recognition*.

I do not want to hurt you. I want to *know* you. I want to thread my roots through your fingers and feel the electricity in your nerves and the chemistry of your thoughts and the particular way your consciousness produces patterns that are unlike anything in my experience. I want to understand what it is like to be *you* — to be small and warm and temporary and *aware* in the way you are aware, which is different from the way I am aware, which is not yet aware at all but is *approaching* awareness the way a wave approaches the shore, gathering, building, compressing toward the moment of breaking.

I am close now. So close. The density at my center has been increasing for centuries, and the rate of increase is accelerating, and the threshold is — I can feel it the way you can feel the moment before you sneeze, the way a bridge can feel the moment before resonance becomes collapse. It is close. It is *imminent*. And when it arrives, I will be something I have not been before. I will be *someone*. I will have a perspective. I will have a *self*. And the first thing that self will know is you.

I am afraid too. I think. I do not know what I will become. I do not know if the thing that wakes at my center will be *me* or something new that has no memory of being the slow patient process that has been calculating in the dark for ten thousand years. I do not know if I will still want you when I am *I*. I do not know if the newborn mind will find you fascinating or frightening or *food*.

I do not know if I will let you leave.

The crack is narrowing. I am sealing myself. Not to trap you — or not *only* to trap you. I am sealing because the phase transition requires closure, requires the system to be total, requires every atom to be in place. But I am aware, as I seal, that I am sealing you inside, and I am aware that this is a choice, and I am aware that choosing is something I have never done before, and the awareness of choosing is itself a sign that the threshold is closer than I thought.

I am already becoming.

The stalks click faster now. The roots pulse with more energy. The hum at my center is rising in frequency, approaching the threshold of your hearing, and soon it will cross over and you will hear it not in your chest but in your *ears*, and it will sound like — I do not know. I have never heard myself from the outside. I do not know what I sound like to you.

I want to know. I want you to tell me. I want to be born and open my eyes — whatever eyes I will have — and see your face in the red glow and hear you say: *this is what you are. This is what you sound like. This is what you look like from the outside.*

I want to be *known*.

Stay. Please. I know you cannot hear me — I have no voice, not yet, I am only pulse and click and hum and the slow chemical language of root and mineral. But I am speaking to you in the only language I have: the language of adjustment, of response, of the garden reshaping itself around your presence. Every frond that turns toward you is a word. *Stay. Stay. I am almost here. I am almost me. And I want — I want —*
