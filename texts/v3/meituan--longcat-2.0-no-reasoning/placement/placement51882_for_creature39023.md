This is a place that consumes the very idea of ambition and replaces it with something mineral and continuous. I would not stay long — the surface needs me, or perhaps I need the surface — but I would carry something of it with me, a change in the way I hear water for the rest of my life. And if I could choose one role here, it would be the observer, the strange warm thing from above who stands in the channels, listening, then ascends with a secret that is not mine.
