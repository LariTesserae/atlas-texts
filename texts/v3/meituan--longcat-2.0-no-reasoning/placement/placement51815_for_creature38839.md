# A Hum Beneath the Hum

---

I am not large.

I know this first. I know it the way I know the taste of silvery fluid (cold, bright, like swallowing a sound), the way I know the pattern of my own tapping against the mat (rapid, uneven, a signature that other Tenders will read and recognize and *use*). I am small. I am four centimeters long. I am no one.

This is fine. No one here is anyone. The swarm is the organism. I am a cell. I carry what I carry. I tap what I tap. I know my role but the role knows me better, shifts me, uses me as needed. Right now I am a carrier. This morning I was a weaver. Three cycles ago I was a scout and ranged so far from the collective that I lost the seismic signal for an entire darkening — panicked, tapped madly, and was only picked up again by a passing swarm of the Amber Column collective, who did not absorb me but *echoed* my distress signal back toward my own swarm, a courtesy I did not understand and still don't. I owe them nothing. That is the point. The system does not track debts.

I carry fluid. My body-cavities are distended, stretched, the segments of my abdomen translucent with the weight of it. Silvery fluid sloshes inside me with every step, and I can feel it — not just the weight but the *signal*. Fluid carries information. Every load I carry is also a message: where it came from, what it carries dissolved in it, what it's intended for. I am a letter. I am a package. I am four centimeters of living pipeline, and I am one of millions in a line that stretches from the Deep Pool at the root-base of the Ninth Pillar to the contested ground near the Fallen Meridian, where two structures are slowly crushing each other and the mat between them is *thick* with chemical warfare.

I have never seen the Ninth Pillar. I have never seen the Fallen Meridian. I know them the way I know the hum — as conditions of being here, as facts that exist whether or not I perceive them. The swarm knows them. The mat knows them. I am in the swarm. I am on the mat. I know what they know, when I need to, in the way I need to.

The line moves. I move with it.

---

The surface of the mat is warm beneath my feet. Not hot — warm the way living tissue is warm, a warmth that comes from *inside*, from the constant metabolic churn of growth and breakdown and growth again. The bioluminescent threads pulse as I walk over them — green, amber, green, amber — and my carapace catches the light and throws it back, and I am, for a moment, a point of brightness moving through a landscape of pale fiber and green-gold glow, and then the light is constant and shadowless and I am just another speck on the endless surface, and I keep walking.

I am not thinking. I want to be clear about this. What I am doing is not thinking. Thinking is what happens in the swarm — the collective process of millions of units tapping and receiving and tapping again, the emergence of a decision from the sheer density of signal and response. What I am doing is *responding*. The mat vibrates beneath my feet and I adjust my pace. The chemical signature of the dust in the air shifts and I adjust my route. The seismic signal from the carrier ahead of me — *tap-tap-tap-tap-tap*, the rhythm that means *move forward, keep pace, do not stop* — and I match my pace to the signal. I am not making choices. I am *flowing*. Like the fluid I carry. Like the mat itself.

And yet.

---

There is something wrong with the hum.

I notice it the way I notice everything — not as a thought but as a *discrepancy*. The hum is the background. The hum is the condition. The hum is what you stop hearing after long enough, the way you stop hearing your own heartbeat. But it has changed. Not in pitch — not in the way the pitch changes when a Deep Current passes beneath us, that slow deepening that makes the mat feel like it's *bowing*. Something else. Something in the texture. The hum has always been a single frequency, a drone, a pure note. Now it has... overtones. Harmonics. Small, thin, high-frequency vibrations layered on top of the deep drone, like — 

Like something is humming *against* the mat. Not with it. Against it.

I stop. The carrier behind me taps my hind segment — *move, move, what are you doing* — and I tap back *wait, listen, something is wrong* — and the signal propagates backward through the line, a ripple of hesitation, and the swarm adjusts around me the way water adjusts around a stone, flowing past, maintaining the flow, and I am still standing there on the mat with my body-cavities full of silvery fluid and my sensory appendages extended and I am *listening* to a sound that should not exist.

---

I do not know what it is.

The swarm does not know what it is. I can feel the collective processing the signal I sent — the tremor of confusion, the rapid back-and-forth as scout units range out and report back *nothing, nothing, the source is not in the mat, not in the fluid, not in the structures, not anywhere we can sense*. The swarm does not like things it cannot sense. The swarm is a system that processes everything, and something that cannot be processed is — not a threat, exactly, but an *error*, a gap in the data, a hole in the world where information should be.

The Tenders near the Amber Column territory — the ones who echoed my distress signal that time, the ones I owe nothing — they are picking it up too. I can feel their tapping, faint, kilometers away, in patterns that mirror my own confusion. *What is that. What is that. What is that.* A question that travels through the mat at the speed of vibration and comes back with no answer.

---

I start walking again. The line moves. The fluid sloshes. The hum continues, with its new overtones, its strange thin harmonics, and I carry it with me — not the hum itself, but the *awareness* of the hum, the sense that something has changed in the world and the world has not yet adjusted to the change, and for a moment — just a moment — I feel something that is not quite the swarm and not quite me.

It is the feeling of being a cell that has noticed something the organism has not.

It is the feeling of being *early*.

---

I do not know what this means. The swarm does not know what this means. The mat hums, and beneath its hum there is something else, something new, something that hums against it, and the harmonics grow stronger as I walk, and the mat shivers, and the factory-structures groan, and the Tenders tap their confused questions into the living surface of the world, and somewhere far below, in the deep places where nothing goes, something that has been silent for longer than anything can measure

*shifts*.

---

I keep walking. I carry my fluid. I tap my signals. I am four centimeters long. I am no one.

But I am listening.
