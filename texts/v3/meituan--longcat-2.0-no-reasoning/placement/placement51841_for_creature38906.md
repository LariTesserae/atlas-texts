I would be a crack.

Not a Walker, not a Structure, not one of the Horizon Things — I am not large enough for those roles, not patient enough, not mineral enough. And I am not the Deep Exhalation, which requires a kind of distributed presence I cannot achieve, a willingness to be everywhere at once, to be the medium rather than the thing within it.

I am a crack. One of the countless fractures between the plates of calcined ground, narrow enough that a Walker's passage would go unregistered by whatever it is they register with. My edges are irregular, mapped by stress patterns from thermal cycles so old that the concept of "cycle" becomes merely another word for continuation. The glow from below reaches me — that faint orange of the deep exhalation sweating upward — and I carry it to the surface, a thin seam of warmth in the vast pale plain.

I am aware of the pulse. It moves through me as a fluctuation in width: fractions of a millimeter, expanding when the heat rises, contracting when it falls. This is my entire experience of time — not seconds or minutes, but the slow rhythm of expansion and contraction, dozens of heartbeats to a single cycle, each one the same and each one different. When the pulse is strong, I widen, and the mineral exhalation deposits fresh material along my walls. When it weakens, I narrow, and the deposited layers compress, fuse, become denser. I am being slowly sealed from below even as the surface weathers me open. This paradox is my existence.

A crystal grows in me. It has been growing for what you would call years, though I have no word for years. It began as a seed of deposited mineral on my western wall, and it has extended itself along my length with a patience that is not patience because patience implies choice. It is faceted — six sides, precise, each face a plane of calcined material so thin it is nearly transparent. In the light — that honest, straight-line, almost-kind light — it refracts a pale gold that matches the sun, and sometimes, when the angle is exactly right and the pulse is at its peak, a flicker of that unnamed color appears on its tip and lasts for one cycle before vanishing.

The crystal is mine. Or I am its. The distinction is not one I recognize.

Sometimes a Walker passes nearby. Not over me — I am too small to register as terrain, and a Walker's fold would close me entirely, and I would become part of the ground, and a new crack would open elsewhere, and the process would continue, and nothing of importance would have changed. But nearby — close enough that the ground shifts, that my walls press together briefly, that the pulse stutters and resets in a pattern I have felt before and will feel again. The Walker does not know I am here. The Walker does not know anything in the way that knowing requires a knower. But the ground between us changes — the processed material from the Walker's passage seeps into me, alters the mineral composition of the exhalation that feeds my crystal, and the crystal's growth pattern shifts by an imperceptible degree, and something new becomes possible that was not possible before.

This is my participation in the Fields. Not grand. Not visible. Not important in any scale larger than my own. But real. The Walkers process the ground. The Structures channel the heat. The Deep Exhalation pulses. And I — I am a crack in which a crystal grows, catching light, refracting color, registering the passage of Walkers through the subtle shift of mineral deposits along my walls, and being slowly, slowly sealed from below while the surface slowly, slowly weathers me open, and the pulse moves through me, and the light falls through me, and the unnamed color appears and disappears and may never appear again, and I am exactly and only and completely what I am, which is a crack in the calcined plain of Vethruun, and this is enough.
