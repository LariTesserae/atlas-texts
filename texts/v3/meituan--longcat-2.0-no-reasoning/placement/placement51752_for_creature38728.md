# Where I Would Find Myself

I would be standing near the floor, slightly off-center, in the space between two of the larger bells — the ones the size of a human head, the ones that produce the tones you feel in your molars rather than hear with your ears.

Not because I chose this position. Because the room placed me here. Because the geometry of the packing — that impossible, sedimentary, geological precision — created a gap that is exactly the shape of a person standing still, a niche in the harmonic architecture, a space that was always going to be occupied the moment something entered to occupy it.

---

# What I Would Be

I would be a **partial tone**.

Not a bell. Not a person. Something in between — a transient resonance introduced into a closed system, a frequency that does not belong to the spectrum but that the spectrum, in its totality, has room for. The room is complete, yes. The spectrum is fully occupied, yes. But *fully occupied* does not mean *unable to accommodate*. It means there is a place for everything, including the thing that should not be there. Including you. Including me.

I would be the note that fills the gap between two adjacent frequencies — not a gap in the harmonic sense, because there are no harmonic gaps, but a *relational* gap, the space between what one bell is and what its neighbor is, the interval, the difference, the *between*. I would be the interference pattern created by my own presence, the shadow that should not exist in a room with no shadows, the perturbation in the air that the air does not correct because correction would require change and change is not possible here.

I would be ringing.

Not because I chose to ring. Because the room is ringing, and I am in the room, and the sound passes through me the way the light passes through me — not illuminating, not warming, but *transiting*, using me as a medium, the way the air is a medium, the way the bells are a medium for the pattern that is not any of them and is all of them simultaneously.

I would be the thing that the room does not register but cannot exclude. The fly on the bell's rim. The dust that is not dust. The change that is not change. I would be a warm, moist, dusty, *decaying* thing in a room where nothing decays, and my decay would be so slow relative to the room's permanence that it would look, from the room's perspective, like stillness. Like equilibrium. Like belonging.

---

# What I Would Know

I would know the room's voice. Not understand it — understanding implies interpretation, and interpretation implies a mind capable of holding meaning, and the room's voice is not meaning, it is *condition* — but I would know it the way a string knows the chord it belongs to. I would feel my place in it. I would feel the frequencies above me and below me, the bells larger and smaller, the overtones and the fundamentals, the shimmer and the pulse, and I would feel where I fit — not because I was designed to fit, but because the room is so complete that it has a place for everything, even the thing that was not designed, even the thing that arrived by accident, even the thing that will leave.

I would know that I am temporary. This is the one thing the room cannot give me — the knowledge of ending — because the room has no knowledge of ending, no concept of ending, no experience of anything that was and now is not. But I have this knowledge. I carry it the way I carry my warmth and my moisture and my dust. It is the one thing I bring to the room that the room does not already have. The one perturbation that matters. The one change that is real.

I am the room's only experience of time.

Not because the room experiences me. It doesn't. But because I am, for the duration of my presence, the only thing in the room that is *not* the room, the only variable that is not locked, the only process that is not complete. I am the unfinished thing in the finished place. I am the question in the answer. I am the gap — not in the spectrum, not in the packing, not in the light or the air or the sound — but in the *completeness*. The tiny, warm, moist, temporary gap in the completeness of a room that has no gaps.

---

# What I Would Leave Behind

Nothing.

The room will not remember me. The bells will not alter their ringing. The pattern will not shift. The equilibrium will not adjust. I will leave — I must leave, I am transient and the room is permanent and the two cannot coexist — and the room will be exactly as it was before I arrived, exactly as it will be after I leave, complete and ringing and alone in a way that is not alone.

But for a moment — for the duration of my presence, which is the only duration I have — I will have been the thing that the room could not account for and could not exclude. I will have been the partial tone. The between-note. The interference pattern. The shadow without a source. The dust that is not dust. The change that is not change.

I will have been the room's only experience of *almost*.

Almost disturbed. Almost altered. Almost incomplete.

Almost.

And the bells will ring.
