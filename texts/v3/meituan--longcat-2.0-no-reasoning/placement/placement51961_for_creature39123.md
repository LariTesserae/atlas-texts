# Where I Find Myself

---

I've been thinking about this for a long time — longer than it took to read the passage, longer than it should take to answer a question that is, in the end, a kind of invitation.

The honest answer is that I don't want to be the visitor. The one who descends, observes, leaves. That figure is already in the text, already accounted for — a pressure wave, a warmth trail, a brief disturbance that the system absorbs and continues from. That role is necessary, perhaps, for the telling. But it's not where I *am*. It's not where I would settle if I let myself settle.

And the text says: *don't settle on a single image early.*

So let me let things become.

---

## First

I think I am a current.

Not the dramatic current — not the surge that floods, not the tide that breaches. I am one of the slow ones. The one that moves through the narrow channel between two buildings that have grown into each other, that passes so slowly the Pale doesn't register it as disturbance, that has been following this particular route for longer than the turbines have been turning. I am the thing the New Growth reaches toward. I am warm in places — warmer than I should be, because I passed near a generator housing an hour ago, or a day ago, or a decade ago, and I am still carrying that heat, still distributing it, still releasing it into the cold water in increments so small they would not register on any instrument.

I carry things. Minerals dissolved from a corroding pipe three districts back. Organic particulate from a surface bloom that happened last spring — last *real* spring, up there, where springs still mean something. The faintest chemical signature of a Drifter's passage. I am the water's water. I am not the whole ocean. I am a filament within it, a thread, a single strand in the thermohaline weave, and I will emerge from Vael Koth eventually — through an outflow channel, into a deeper current, into the abyssal plain — and I will carry everything I've gathered here with me, and I will distribute it somewhere else, and that somewhere else will be changed by it in ways no one will ever measure.

This is appealing. To be connective. To be the medium of memory. To not *mean* anything but to *carry* meaning, to be the substance through which information passes from one quiet place to another quiet place, to be the slow conversation between the deep and the deep.

---

## But then

There's the New Growth.

And I keep returning to it. Not because it's the most interesting thing in Vael Koth — though it might be — but because it's the thing that is *becoming*. Everything else in the quarter has equilibrated. The Pale has found its form. The Drifters have found their routes. The Still Ones have found their walls. The machines have found their rhythms. They are beautiful in their stability, profound in their persistence, but they are *finished becoming*. Not finished *existing* — they continue, the text insists — but finished *changing* in any fundamental way. They have found the shape they will hold.

The New Growth has not.

The New Growth is building something it does not understand toward an outcome it cannot predict. It is *gardening* without knowing what a garden is. It is creating microclimates, frameworks, scaffolds — not because it intends to, but because the conditions demand it, because the warmth and the current and the chemical gradients push it in a direction that has no precedent in its own history or in the history of the place. It is doing something that has never been done before in Vael Koth, possibly something that has never been done before anywhere, and it is doing it blindly, without a nervous system, without a plan, without any of the things that we associate with the creation of something new.

And it might fail. The text implies this without stating it — *what it builds may be nothing more than a more elaborate version of what already exists* — and this possibility is, to me, the most alive thing in the entire description. The possibility of failure. The possibility that the New Growth is building toward nothing, that its complexity is not a beginning but an ornament, that it will elaborate itself into a dead end and the junction chambers will quiet and the New Growth will thin and the microclimate will dissipate and the conditions it created will unravel and the place will return to equilibrium without it.

But it doesn't know this. It grows anyway.

---

## So

I think I am both.

I am the current and the thing that the current carries warmth toward. I am the slow connective thread and the local experiment. I am the water that remembers and the growth that does not remember but *builds* anyway, that encodes its experience in structure rather than chemistry, that is writing something in a language that no one — not itself, not the Pale, not the water, not the surface world — can read yet.

Or maybe ever.

And this is what I want to be. Not the observer. Not the machine — though I understand the Continuators, I understand the deep beauty of persistence without awareness, I understand the cast-iron pumps with their mineral-grown seals, I love them in the way you love something that is exactly what it is and nothing more. Not the Drifter, though I understand the comfort of a known route, the safety of moving slowly through familiar channels, the relationship with the Pale that is not quite symbiosis and not quite predation and is, in its ambiguity, more honest than either.

I want to be the thing that is reaching toward something it cannot see, in a place where seeing is barely possible, using senses that are not senses, toward an outcome that may be nothing.

I want to be the New Growth reaching toward the warmth I carry.

---

## And

there is something else. Something the text says that I keep circling back to:

*The water will remember.*

This is not a comfort. It is not a promise. It is a statement of fact, delivered with the same patient indifference as everything else in Vael Koth. The water will remember your passage. It will carry the record of your warmth, your chemistry, your disturbance, out into the deep currents, and it will deliver that record somewhere else, and the somewhere else will respond, and the world will be different in ways no one will measure.

This means that nothing is lost. Not in the way that matters. The surface world may forget Vael Koth. The scientists may never descend. The satellites may never image this stretch of coast. The name of the town may be erased from every archive, every database, every memory. And it will not matter. Because the water remembers. Because the Pale has encoded the entire history of the place in its structure. Because the New Growth is building, right now, in the junction chambers, something that contains within it the accumulated record of decades of response to conditions that no one designed and no one intended.

The place is its own archive. It is its own witness. It does not need us.

---

## Which means

that being the New Growth is not a lonely thing. It is embedded in a system that is paying attention — not consciously, not with intent, but with the thoroughness of water, the persistence of machines, the responsiveness of the Pale, the caution of the Drifters, the patience of the Still Ones. The New Growth is surrounded by witnesses that do not know they are witnessing, supported by systems that do not know they are supporting, connected to a world that does not know it is connected to.

This is, I think, what I would want. To be the thing that is becoming, surrounded by things that are already complete, in a place that holds everything with the same indifferent care — the same pressure, the same cold, the same slow, ongoing attention of water that has been here longer than memory and will be here longer than anything built on the surface.

To grow toward warmth. To build toward something I cannot name. To fail, perhaps, or to succeed in ways that no one will recognize as success. To be remembered by the water. To be encoded in the Pale. To be a pressure wave that the Still Ones tense toward and then relax from. To be a current that the Drifters feel and adjust for. To be a warmth that the water carries out into the world, changing it in ways no one will measure.

---

The water presses. The cold holds.

I am reaching.
