To prepare for visiting The Whispering Canopy, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Physical Preparation:
- Develop excellent balance and coordination through exercises and practices like yoga or slacklining. This will be crucial for navigating the swaying bridges and ever-changing pathways.
- Build up cardiovascular endurance and leg strength for climbing and traversing the various levels of the arboreal city.
- Practice deep breathing exercises to acclimate to the humid, spore-filled air.

2. Gear and Clothing:
- Pack lightweight, quick-drying, and breathable clothing to cope with the constant humidity.
- Bring sturdy, non-slip shoes with excellent grip for navigating wet and potentially unstable surfaces.
- Include a waterproof backpack to protect essential items from the omnipresent moisture.
- Carry a reusable water bottle with a built-in filter to safely drink from the local water sources.
- Bring a robust, waterproof headlamp or lantern for navigating in low-light conditions.
- Pack a reliable compass or GPS device, though be prepared for potential malfunctions due to the realm's reality-bending properties.

3. Sensory Adaptation:
- Wear noise-cancelling earplugs or headphones that can be adjusted to filter out the overwhelming forest sounds when needed.
- Bring polarized sunglasses to help manage the shifting light conditions and protect eyes from the bioluminescent spores.
- Consider packing motion sickness medication to cope with the constantly moving environment and distorted perceptions of space and time.

4. Scientific and Observational Tools:
- Bring a waterproof notebook and pen for documenting observations and experiences.
- Pack a high-quality camera with a variety of lenses to capture the unique flora, fauna, and structures.
- Include basic scientific instruments like a thermometer, hygrometer, and pH testing strips to measure environmental conditions.

5. Survival and Navigation:
- Learn basic tree climbing and arboreal navigation techniques.
- Study methods for identifying edible plants and water sources in tropical environments.
- Practice techniques for maintaining mental clarity and calm in disorienting situations.

6. Cultural and Communication Preparation:
- Research non-verbal communication methods, as traditional language may be ineffective or irrelevant.
- Study the basics of biomimicry and organic architecture to better understand and appreciate the merged natural-urban environment.
- Meditate or practice mindfulness to prepare for the potential dissolution of normal perceptual boundaries.

7. Health and Safety:
- Get vaccinated against common tropical diseases and carry a comprehensive first-aid kit.
- Bring antihistamines and anti-inflammatory medications to combat potential allergic reactions to the unique flora and spores.
- Pack a personal air filtration mask to protect against inhaling potentially harmful spores or mists.

8. Miscellaneous:
- Bring a small musical instrument or noisemaker to interact with the sound-responsive environment.
- Pack a set of textured gloves to better grip and interact with the living structures.
- Consider bringing a small, collapsible hammock for rest periods, as conventional sleeping areas may not be available.

Remember, flexibility and open-mindedness are key when visiting such an unpredictable and ever-changing environment. Be prepared to abandon preconceptions and adapt to the fluid nature of The Whispering Canopy.
