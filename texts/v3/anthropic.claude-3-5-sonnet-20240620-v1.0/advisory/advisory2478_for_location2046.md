To prepare for visiting this unique and complex location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Protective Clothing:
   - Wear waterproof, breathable clothing to manage the constant moisture and mist.
   - Choose clothing with antimicrobial properties to prevent mold growth.
   - Bring thermal layers to combat the cold air.
   - Wear sturdy, non-slip boots with good traction for navigating wet, metallic surfaces.

2. Respiratory Equipment:
   - Pack a high-quality respirator or air filtration mask to protect against potential spores, molds, or engineered microorganisms.
   - Consider bringing supplemental oxygen, as the air composition may differ from Earth's.

3. Eye Protection:
   - Wear sealed goggles to shield eyes from mist, potential chemical irritants, and bioluminescent light.

4. Hearing Protection:
   - Bring noise-canceling earplugs or headphones to manage the constant industrial cacophony.

5. Navigation Tools:
   - Pack a waterproof, shockproof flashlight or headlamp for navigating dim areas.
   - Bring a compass and a detailed map of the structure (if available), as GPS might not function in this enclosed space.

6. Scientific Equipment:
   - Carry a multi-purpose environmental sensor to monitor air quality, radiation levels, and other potential hazards.
   - Bring sample collection tools (sterile containers, swabs) for documenting unique flora and fauna.
   - Pack a high-resolution camera with macro lens capabilities for detailed documentation.

7. Medical Supplies:
   - Bring a comprehensive first-aid kit, including antihistamines and broad-spectrum antibiotics.
   - Pack antifungal medications and treatments for potential allergic reactions.

8. Sustenance:
   - Bring ample purified water, as the local water may not be safe for human consumption.
   - Pack high-energy, non-perishable food supplies.

9. Communication Devices:
   - Bring long-range communication devices that can penetrate the sphere's structure.
   - Consider bringing a personal locator beacon for emergencies.

10. Adaptive Gear:
    - Pack climbing equipment for navigating vertical structures.
    - Bring a collapsible boat or flotation device for exploring water channels.

11. Decontamination Supplies:
    - Bring sanitizing wipes and sprays to clean equipment and yourself before leaving.
    - Pack sealable bags for storing potentially contaminated items.

12. Mental Preparation:
    - Be prepared for sensory overload and potential claustrophobia.
    - Research and practice meditation or grounding techniques to manage stress in this alien environment.

13. Cultural Sensitivity:
    - If there are sentient inhabitants, learn about their customs and communication methods.
    - Bring universal translation devices if available.

14. Time Management:
    - Bring a reliable timekeeping device, as the enclosed nature of the space may disorient your sense of time.

15. Exit Strategy:
    - Ensure you have a clear understanding of how to exit the sphere and return to your entry point.
    - Bring emergency supplies in case of unexpected delays in departure.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key when visiting such a unique environment. Be prepared to adjust your plans based on unforeseen circumstances and always prioritize safety.
