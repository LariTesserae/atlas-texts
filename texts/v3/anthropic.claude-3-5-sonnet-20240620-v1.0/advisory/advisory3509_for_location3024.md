To prepare for visiting this extraordinary alien living metropolis, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Wear a sealed, environmentally-controlled suit. This will protect you from potential allergens, spores, or other biological agents that your immune system may not be prepared for.
   - Ensure the suit has its own oxygen supply, as the atmosphere may not be suitable for human respiration.
   - The suit should be resistant to corrosive substances, as the sap rivers or plant secretions might be harmful to human skin or equipment.

2. Mobility and Navigation:
   - Bring hover boots or a personal antigravity device. The constantly shifting landscape and spongy ground cover make traditional walking difficult and potentially dangerous.
   - Have a real-time mapping device that can update as the environment changes. Traditional maps will quickly become obsolete in this ever-changing cityscape.
   - Carry a multi-spectrum scanner to detect safe paths and identify potential hazards that may not be visible to the naked eye.

3. Communication and Data Collection:
   - Bring advanced communication equipment that can penetrate the dense canopy and potentially disruptive bio-electrical fields.
   - Have a high-capacity data storage device for recording observations, as this environment will likely yield a wealth of unique scientific information.
   - Carry a universal translator programmed with botanical and biological terms, in case the city's intelligence attempts communication.

4. Survival Gear:
   - Pack a water purification system, as local water sources may contain unfamiliar microorganisms.
   - Bring nutrient-dense, self-contained food supplies. Do not rely on or consume any local organic matter.
   - Carry a multi-tool designed for botanical environments, including tools for taking samples and analyzing plant structures.

5. Safety Precautions:
   - Bring a personal force field generator to protect against sudden movements of the living architecture or unexpected biological reactions.
   - Have a teleportation device or emergency extraction beacon in case you need to leave the area quickly.
   - Carry antidotes and broad-spectrum antibiotics for potential exposure to alien toxins or pathogens.

6. Specialized Equipment:
   - Bring a bio-electric field detector to monitor the city's energy patterns and potentially predict large-scale changes in the environment.
   - Have a chrono-stabilizer to protect against any temporal anomalies that might occur in this rapidly evolving ecosystem.
   - Carry a quantum entanglement communicator for instantaneous communication with your home base, bypassing potential interference from the alien biology.

7. Mental Preparation:
   - Undergo meditation training to prepare for the potentially disorienting experience of being in a constantly changing environment.
   - Study advanced botany and alien ecosystem theories to better understand and appreciate what you'll encounter.
   - Prepare yourself psychologically for the possibility of encountering a form of plant-based sentience or hive mind.

8. Ethical Considerations:
   - Bring non-invasive sampling equipment to minimize impact on the living city.
   - Have a strict quarantine protocol prepared for your return to prevent any unintentional spread of alien biological material.
   - Prepare a first contact protocol in case the city's intelligence attempts to communicate or shows signs of advanced cognition.

Remember, this environment is unlike anything on Earth. Approach with caution, respect, and an open mind. The key to a successful visit will be adaptability and preparedness for the unexpected. This living metropolis represents an unprecedented opportunity for scientific discovery, but also potential dangers beyond our current understanding.
