To prepare for visiting this extraordinary and unpredictable location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Physical Protection:
- Wear a fully enclosed, environmentally controlled suit capable of withstanding extreme temperatures and pressures.
- Ensure the suit has advanced filtration systems to protect against unknown atmospheric composition and potential toxins.
- Include radiation shielding in the suit design, as the intense light and energy flows may emit harmful radiation.

2. Breathing Apparatus:
- Bring a self-contained breathing system with ample oxygen supply for the duration of the visit.
- Include backup air purification systems in case of suit breaches.

3. Gravity Adaptation:
- Equip boots with adjustable magnetic or gravitational technology to maintain stability in areas of fluctuating gravity.
- Include a personal gravity generator to create a stable field around the traveler.

4. Sensory Protection and Enhancement:
- Wear goggles or a visor with adjustable tinting to protect against the intense, ever-present light.
- Include sound dampening technology to protect against potential sudden loud noises.
- Equip the helmet with advanced sensory systems to detect changes in the environment that may not be perceptible to human senses.

5. Navigation and Communication:
- Bring multiple navigation devices that don't rely solely on magnetic fields or GPS, as these may be unreliable in this fluctuating environment.
- Include quantum entanglement communication devices for maintaining contact across dimensional shifts.

6. Energy and Sustenance:
- Carry a portable energy generator capable of drawing power from the ambient energy of the environment.
- Bring concentrated, long-lasting food supplies and a water recycling system within the suit.

7. Temporal Stabilization:
- Wear a personal temporal field generator to maintain a consistent experience of time despite potential temporal fluctuations in the environment.

8. Dimensional Anchoring:
- Carry a device that can create a stable dimensional pocket around the traveler to prevent accidental phasing in and out of reality.

9. Scientific Equipment:
- Bring multi-spectrum analyzers to study the unique flora, fauna, and energy patterns.
- Include sample collection tools that can safely contain potentially unstable or temporally shifting materials.

10. Emergency Extraction:
- Wear a personal teleportation device for emergency extraction if the environment becomes too unstable or dangerous.

11. Mental Preparation:
- Undergo extensive psychological training to prepare for the cognitive dissonance of experiencing a reality that defies known laws of physics.
- Practice meditation techniques to maintain mental stability in an environment of constant change.

12. Adaptive Clothing:
- Under the protective suit, wear clothing made of smart materials that can adjust to rapid temperature changes and provide additional protection if needed.

13. Universal Translator:
- Bring a device capable of interpreting potential communications from the energy-based lifeforms or deciphering any encountered artifacts.

14. Reality Anchor:
- Carry a small, personal object from the home universe to serve as a psychological anchor in this chaotic environment.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key in this ever-changing environment. Be prepared for the unexpected and maintain a state of constant alertness. The traveler should also be ready to abort the mission at any time if the environment becomes too unstable or dangerous.
