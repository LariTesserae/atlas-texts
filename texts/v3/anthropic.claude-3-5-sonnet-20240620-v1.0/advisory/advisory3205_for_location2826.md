To prepare for visiting Lumina, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Pressure Adaptation: The crushing depths of the ocean will require specialized equipment. Invest in a high-tech diving suit capable of withstanding extreme pressure. This suit should be equipped with a pressure equalization system to prevent decompression sickness.

2. Breathing Apparatus: Obtain an advanced underwater breathing system. This should include a rebreather with a CO2 scrubber and oxygen recycling capabilities, allowing for extended stays underwater without the need for frequent air tank changes.

3. Communication Devices: Bring underwater communication gear that can function in the dense water environment. This may include sonar-based devices or advanced radio systems designed for submarine use.

4. Navigation Tools: Acquire a 3D mapping device to navigate the complex, maze-like structure of Lumina. Traditional GPS won't work underwater, so look for inertial or sonar-based navigation systems.

5. Illumination: While Lumina is naturally lit by bioluminescent algae, bring additional lighting equipment for emergencies or areas with less natural light. Choose lights that won't disturb the local ecosystem.

6. Temperature Regulation: Despite the generally cool temperatures, be prepared for sudden heat near the geothermal vents. Your diving suit should have excellent thermal regulation capabilities.

7. Buoyancy Control: Given the pockets of varying water density, bring a smart buoyancy compensator that can quickly adjust to changes in water density.

8. Propulsion: A small, quiet underwater propulsion device will help navigate through the city's currents and dense areas.

9. Translation Technology: If you expect to interact with any intelligent systems in Lumina, bring a universal translator capable of decoding and translating various forms of communication, including potential machine languages.

10. Data Collection Tools: Bring equipment to analyze water composition, temperature, and pressure. These will help you navigate the city's different zones safely.

11. Energy Source: Ensure all your equipment has long-lasting power sources. Consider bringing a portable charging station that can draw energy from the surrounding water currents or thermal differences.

12. Decontamination Kit: Bring a system to clean your suit and equipment of the bioluminescent algae before leaving to prevent contamination of other environments.

13. Medical Supplies: Pack a specialized first-aid kit designed for underwater emergencies, including treatments for pressure-related injuries and exposure to unusual marine substances.

14. Time-Keeping Device: Bring a watch or similar device that can withstand the pressure and help you keep track of time, as natural light cycles may be absent.

15. Mental Preparation: Be ready for the psychological effects of being in a completely alien underwater environment. Consider meditation techniques or bring comfort items to help manage potential claustrophobia or disorientation.

16. Cultural Briefing: If possible, study any available information about Lumina's history, social structures, and etiquette to ensure respectful interaction with the city's systems and any potential inhabitants.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key when visiting such an alien environment. Be prepared to adjust your plans based on unforeseen circumstances and always prioritize safety in this unique and potentially hazardous setting.
