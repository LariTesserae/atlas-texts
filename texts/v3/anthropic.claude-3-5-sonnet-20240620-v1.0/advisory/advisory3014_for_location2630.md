To prepare for visiting Aquaterra, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Breathing apparatus: Given the gelatinous nature of the atmosphere, a specialized breathing system is crucial. Develop or acquire a device that can extract oxygen from the dense, liquid-like medium. This might involve a combination of filtration and chemical processes to make the extracted substance breathable for humans.

2. Protective suit: Design a full-body suit that can withstand the pressure and viscosity of the environment. This suit should be flexible enough to allow movement but strong enough to resist the constant force of the surrounding medium. Consider incorporating a heating system to maintain body temperature in the cooler depths.

3. Propulsion system: Since normal swimming motions would be ineffective in this dense environment, develop a propulsion system that can efficiently move through the gelatinous medium. This could be a motorized system or a manual one that utilizes the principles of how local lifeforms navigate.

4. Communication devices: Standard audio communication may be challenging due to the overwhelming ambient noise and the way sound travels in this medium. Develop visual communication tools, perhaps using light signals or a system that mimics the bioluminescent signals used by native inhabitants.

5. Navigation tools: Traditional GPS or magnetic compasses may not function in this environment. Create or obtain instruments that can navigate using the unique properties of Aquaterra, such as current flows or the positions of floating islands.

6. Adaptive optics: The refraction of light in this medium will distort normal vision. Equip yourself with goggles or a helmet visor that can correct for this distortion and enhance visibility in the murky depths.

7. Decompression protocols: Develop a method for safely transitioning between Aquaterra's dense environment and normal atmospheric conditions to prevent decompression sickness or other physiological issues.

8. Cultural preparation: Study the gestures and bioluminescent signals used by the inhabitants to facilitate basic communication. Also, research their customs to avoid unintentionally offensive behavior.

9. Temporal adjustment: Prepare mentally for the different perception of time in Aquaterra. Bring instruments to accurately measure the passage of time by your home world's standards.

10. Sample collection equipment: If conducting research, bring tools capable of collecting and preserving samples from this unique environment without contamination.

11. Energy sources: Ensure you have a long-lasting power supply for your equipment that can function in this unusual environment. Solar power may be ineffective due to light distortion.

12. Emergency ascent system: Devise a method for quickly returning to the surface in case of equipment failure or other emergencies.

13. Psychological preparation: The alien nature of this environment may be psychologically challenging. Consider meditation techniques or bring comfort items to help maintain mental stability.

14. Nutrition: Pack a supply of nutrient-dense food that can withstand the environment. You may not be able to consume local resources safely.

15. Documentation tools: Bring specialized cameras and recording devices that can capture images and data in this unique setting for later study and analysis.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key when preparing for such an alien environment. Be prepared to adjust your equipment and strategies as you learn more about the realities of Aquaterra.
