   - Have a personal teleportation device or emergency extraction beacon in case immediate evacuation becomes necessary.
   - Establish a "safe word" or signal with your support team to trigger emergency extraction.

   - Prepare for extensive decontamination procedures upon exiting the anomalous space.
   - Have a quarantine plan ready in case of exposure to unknown elements.

14. Interdimensional Survival Kit:
