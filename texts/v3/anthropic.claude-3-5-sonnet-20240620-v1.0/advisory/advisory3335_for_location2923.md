To prepare for visiting Glacialis, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Thermal Gear: Pack advanced cold-weather clothing designed for extreme subzero temperatures. Include thermal underwear, insulated outerwear, and heated gloves and boots. The constant cold requires proper protection to prevent hypothermia.

2. Breathing Apparatus: Bring a personal air filtration and oxygenation system. The underground air might be thin or contain unfamiliar particles, so it's crucial to ensure a clean, breathable air supply.

3. Low-Light Vision Aids: Pack night vision goggles or other low-light vision enhancement devices. The bioluminescent and artificial lighting may not be sufficient for human eyes accustomed to sunlight.

4. Water Purification: Carry a portable water purification system. While water is abundant, its mineral content and potential contaminants from the unfamiliar ecosystem may not be safe for human consumption.

5. Anti-Gravity Gear: Include magnetic boots or a personal gravity stabilizer to navigate areas with floating water or unconventional gravitational fields.

6. Universal Translator: Bring a device capable of translating unknown languages or decoding the local communication methods, which may be non-verbal or based on unfamiliar technology.

7. Biodome-Safe Attire: Pack clothing that can be easily sanitized or is designed not to introduce foreign contaminants into the delicate biodome ecosystems.

8. Energy Source Adapter: Bring a universal power adapter capable of interfacing with the city's green fluid energy system to charge your devices.

9. Bioluminescent Neutralizing Goggles: Pack eyewear that can filter out excess bioluminescence to prevent eye strain or disorientation from the constantly shifting light patterns.

10. Portable Force Field Generator: Carry a personal force field generator for protection against unknown fauna or potential hazards in unexplored areas.

11. Geothermal Activity Monitor: Bring a device to monitor seismic activity and temperature fluctuations, helping you stay aware of any changes in the city's geothermal systems.

12. Moisture-Resistant Equipment: Ensure all your gear is waterproof or highly moisture-resistant to withstand the omnipresent water in various forms.

13. Nutrient Supplements: Pack a supply of broad-spectrum nutrient supplements, as your body may not be able to process or absorb nutrients from the local flora and fauna.

14. Pressure Equalization Device: Bring a device to help your body adjust to potential pressure changes as you move between different levels or sections of the underground city.

15. Temporal Stabilizer: Include a personal temporal field generator to protect against any time dilation effects that might occur in this unusual environment.

16. Cultural Etiquette Guide: Research and bring a comprehensive guide on local customs, taboos, and etiquette to avoid unintentionally offending the inhabitants of Glacialis.

17. Holographic Camouflage: Consider a holographic disguise system to blend in with the local population and avoid drawing unwanted attention.

18. Emergency Teleportation Device: As a last resort, carry a personal teleportation device calibrated to return you to your entry point in case of emergency.

Remember, flexibility and open-mindedness are key when visiting such an alien environment. Be prepared to adapt your plans and equipment as you learn more about the unique challenges and wonders of Glacialis.
