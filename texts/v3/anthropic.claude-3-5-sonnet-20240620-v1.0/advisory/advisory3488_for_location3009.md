To prepare for visiting this extraordinary underground world, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Physical Conditioning:
- Engage in cardiovascular and strength training, focusing on endurance and flexibility.
- Practice cave crawling and rock climbing to navigate potentially challenging terrain.
- Acclimate to dark, enclosed spaces to reduce claustrophobia.

2. Gear and Equipment:
- Bring multiple light sources: headlamps, handheld lights, and backup batteries.
- Pack moisture-wicking, breathable clothing suitable for warm, humid environments.
- Include sturdy, waterproof boots with excellent traction for slippery surfaces.
- Bring a waterproof backpack to protect supplies from constant moisture.
- Include a reliable compass and altimeter, as GPS may not function underground.
- Pack emergency supplies: first-aid kit, water purification tablets, and high-energy rations.
- Bring protective eyewear to shield against spores and airborne particles.

3. Specialized Equipment:
- Acquire or rent caving gear: helmet, harness, ropes, and carabiners.
- Bring water-resistant cameras or protective cases for documentation.
- Pack a multi-gas detector to monitor air quality and oxygen levels.

4. Mental Preparation:
- Practice meditation or mindfulness techniques to maintain calm in isolation.
- Study geological formations and cave ecosystems to better appreciate the environment.
- Prepare for potential sensory deprivation and time disorientation.

5. Scientific and Observational Tools:
- Bring a field journal and writing tools for observations and sketches.
- Pack sample collection materials (if permitted) for later analysis.
- Include a microscope or magnifying glass for examining small life forms.

6. Navigation and Mapping:
- Study any available maps of the cavern system beforehand.
- Bring mapping tools to record your route and new discoveries.
- Learn and practice cave marking techniques to avoid getting lost.

7. Communication:
- Bring long-range communication devices designed for underground use.
- Establish check-in protocols with a surface team or multiverse base.

8. Biological Precautions:
- Undergo a full medical check-up and required vaccinations before the journey.
- Bring antihistamines and anti-fungal medications for potential allergic reactions.
- Pack a respirator or mask to filter out spores and other airborne particles.

9. Cultural Sensitivity:
- Research any known history or legends about the ancient civilization.
- Prepare to document and respect any artifacts or structures encountered.

10. Temporal Adaptation:
- Bring reliable timekeeping devices to maintain a sense of time passage.
- Practice techniques for maintaining circadian rhythms without natural light cues.

11. Sensory Training:
- Practice identifying sounds and movements in near-darkness.
- Develop your sense of touch to navigate when visibility is poor.

12. Exit Strategy:
- Plan and practice emergency exit procedures.
- Bring equipment for vertical ascents in case of sudden elevation changes.

13. Psychological Support:
- Consider bringing a small personal item for comfort and grounding.
- Prepare coping strategies for extended periods of isolation and alien environments.

14. Interdimensional Precautions:
- If available, bring devices to monitor and stabilize your position in the multiverse.
- Prepare for potential reality shifts or temporal anomalies.

By thoroughly preparing in these areas, a multiverse traveler can maximize their safety, comfort, and ability to fully experience and document this remarkable underground realm. Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key when exploring such unique and potentially mutable environments.
