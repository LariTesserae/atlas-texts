To prepare for visiting this subterranean metropolis, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Protective Gear:
- Wear a high-quality respirator or gas mask to filter out fungal spores and prevent inhalation of potentially harmful microorganisms.
- Don a waterproof, full-body suit to protect against constant moisture and possible contaminants in the water.
- Use sturdy, waterproof boots with excellent traction for navigating slippery surfaces.
- Wear a helmet with an attached light source for safety and illumination.

2. Lighting:
- Bring multiple sources of light: headlamps, handheld flashlights, and backup batteries.
- Consider bringing bioluminescent light sticks as a non-electric backup.

3. Navigation Tools:
- Pack a reliable compass, as GPS likely won't work underground.
- Bring detailed maps of known cavern systems, if available.
- Carry marking tools like biodegradable tape or chalk to mark your path.

4. Survival Gear:
- Bring ample clean drinking water and water purification tablets.
- Pack high-energy, non-perishable food supplies.
- Carry a first-aid kit with additional supplies for treating fungal infections and hypothermia.
- Include emergency blankets and hand warmers for warmth in the cold, damp environment.

5. Climbing Equipment:
- Bring ropes, harnesses, and carabiners for traversing difficult terrain.
- Pack pitons and other anchoring equipment for safety during climbs.

6. Scientific Equipment:
- Carry a hygrometer to measure humidity levels.
- Bring a thermometer for monitoring ambient and water temperatures.
- Pack sample collection containers for gathering specimens of fungi or water (if permitted).
- Include a camera with underwater capabilities for documentation.

7. Communication Devices:
- Bring long-range, waterproof radios for communication within your group.
- Consider cave phones or other specialized underground communication devices.

8. Time-Keeping:
- Wear a reliable, waterproof watch to keep track of time in the absence of day/night cycles.

9. Mental Preparation:
- Practice meditation or mindfulness techniques to manage potential claustrophobia or disorientation.
- Prepare for sensory deprivation and the psychological effects of prolonged darkness.

10. Physical Conditioning:
- Engage in cardiovascular and strength training prior to the trip to prepare for the physical demands of cave exploration.

11. Knowledge Acquisition:
- Study speleology and cave ecology to better understand and appreciate the environment.
- Learn about the specific fungi and mineral formations you might encounter.

12. Decontamination Procedures:
- Plan for thorough cleaning and decontamination of all gear and yourself upon exiting the caverns to prevent the spread of underground microorganisms to the surface world.

13. Ethical Considerations:
- Prepare to follow strict "leave no trace" principles to preserve this unique ecosystem.
- If collecting samples, ensure you have proper permissions and follow ethical guidelines for scientific research in sensitive environments.

Remember, this is an alien and potentially dangerous environment. Never explore alone, always inform others of your plans, and be prepared to abort the mission if safety concerns arise. The key is to approach this otherworldly location with a blend of wonder, respect, and extreme caution.
