To prepare for visiting this extraordinary alien world, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Wear a fully enclosed, pressurized spacesuit with advanced temperature regulation. The heat and potentially toxic atmosphere require complete isolation from the environment.
   - Ensure the suit has a robust filtration system to prevent any crystalline dust or alien particles from entering.
   - Include a radiation shield in the suit, as the unusual physics of this world may produce unexpected forms of radiation.

2. Breathing Apparatus:
   - Carry a self-contained breathing system with at least 24 hours of oxygen supply.
   - Include backup oxygen tanks and an emergency purification system that can process the local atmosphere if absolutely necessary.

3. Protective Eyewear:
   - Use smart-glass goggles that can automatically adjust to the shifting light conditions caused by the bioluminescent creatures.
   - Ensure the goggles have a polarizing filter to reduce glare from the crystalline surfaces.

4. Footwear:
   - Wear boots with specialized soles designed to grip the smooth, crystalline terrain without damaging it.
   - Include insulation to protect against potential heat transfer from the ground.

5. Communication Devices:
   - Bring multiple forms of communication equipment, including quantum entanglement devices that can potentially pierce through reality distortions.
   - Have a personal AI assistant programmed to help interpret the alien environment and maintain a connection to base reality.

6. Scientific Equipment:
   - Carry a multi-spectrum analyzer to study the composition of the crystals, atmosphere, and lifeforms.
   - Include a reality anchor device to help maintain a stable existence in this fluctuating environment.
   - Bring a quantum field detector to monitor and record the strange physics at play.

7. Temporal Stabilizer:
   - Wear a personal temporal stabilizer to protect against potential time distortions or loops.

8. Energy Source:
   - Carry a compact, high-capacity power source compatible with all equipment, preferably one that can draw energy from multiple dimensions.

9. Navigation Tools:
   - Use a multi-dimensional GPS system that can function in areas where space-time is unstable.
   - Bring holographic mapping technology to create real-time 3D maps of the shifting environment.

10. Emergency Gear:
    - Pack a reality tether that can be used to find the way back to the entry point in case of disorientation.
    - Include a personal force field generator for protection against unexpected phenomena or creature encounters.

11. Biological Safeguards:
    - Undergo genetic fortification treatments before the trip to enhance resilience against potential alien pathogens or reality-altering effects.
    - Carry a medical kit with broad-spectrum treatments and reality-stabilizing drugs.

12. Mental Preparation:
    - Engage in extensive virtual reality simulations of alien environments to prepare for the psychological impact of such an otherworldly experience.
    - Practice meditation and grounding techniques to maintain mental stability in a reality-bending environment.

13. Cultural and Ethical Guidelines:
    - Study and adhere to strict non-interference protocols to avoid disturbing this emerging world's development.
    - Prepare to document observations without interacting with or influencing the environment and its inhabitants.

14. Return Protocol:
    - Establish a clear extraction plan with multiple backup options.
    - Carry a personal dimensional shifter as a last resort for emergency evacuation.

15. Decontamination Procedures:
    - Plan for thorough decontamination upon return, including potential quarantine and reality re-adjustment therapies.

Remember, flexibility and open-mindedness are key. This world operates on principles that may challenge fundamental understanding of reality. Be prepared for the unexpected and maintain a sense of wonder and respect for this alien environment.
