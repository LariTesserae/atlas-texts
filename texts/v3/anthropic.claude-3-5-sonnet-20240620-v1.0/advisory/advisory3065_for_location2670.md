- Undergo pre-trip meditation and mental conditioning to prepare for the cognitive dissonance of witnessing rapid geological and evolutionary processes.
- Carry a personal reality anchor – a small object from your home reality – to maintain psychological grounding.

8. Emergency Provisions:
- Include a compact interdimensional portal device for emergency evacuation.
- Carry a supply of universal antidotes and broad-spectrum medications to counter potential biological threats.

9. Documentation Equipment:
- Bring multiple redundant recording devices to capture your observations from various perspectives.

10. Ethical Considerations:
- Review and internalize non-interference protocols to minimize your impact on this nascent reality.

11. Post-Visit Procedures:
- Plan for a thorough decontamination process upon return to your home reality.
