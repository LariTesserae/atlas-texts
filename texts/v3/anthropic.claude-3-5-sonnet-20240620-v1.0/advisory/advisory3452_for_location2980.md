To prepare for visiting this microscopic crystalline realm, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Size Adaptation Technology:
Develop or acquire technology that can shrink the traveler to an appropriate scale, likely nanometers in size. This might involve advanced nanotechnology or quantum shrinking devices. Ensure the technology allows for reversal to return to normal size.

2. Environmental Protection Suit:
Design a suit that can withstand the extreme conditions within the crystal:
- Insulation against the near-freezing temperatures
- Protection from the intense, constant light to prevent eye damage
- Shielding against potential piezoelectric discharges
- Ability to navigate through the sparse network of nano-sized chambers

3. Breathing Apparatus:
Create a system to provide breathable air, as the environment contains only inert noble gases. This should be self-contained and efficient enough to last for the duration of the exploration.

4. Navigation Tools:
Develop instruments to map and navigate the crystal's internal structure, possibly using quantum sensors or advanced imaging technology to detect the minute differences in the lattice structure.

5. Communication Devices:
Design communication equipment that can function at this scale and penetrate the crystal structure to maintain contact with the outside world or other team members.

6. Timekeeping Mechanism:
Bring a reliable timekeeping device that won't be affected by the timeless nature of the environment. This is crucial for tracking the duration of the visit and coordinating return.

7. Sampling and Analysis Tools:
Prepare miniaturized tools for collecting samples of the metallic dendrites and analyzing the crystal structure without causing damage.

8. Energy Source:
Develop a long-lasting, compact energy source to power all equipment, as there are no conventional energy sources available in this environment.

9. Mental Preparation:
Undergo psychological training to cope with the alien nature of the environment, the profound silence, and the potential sensory disorientation from the constant, unchanging light.

10. Time Dilation Considerations:
Research and prepare for potential time dilation effects that might occur at this scale or within the crystal structure.

11. Exit Strategy:
Plan multiple methods for exiting the crystal, including emergency protocols in case the primary size-restoration technology fails.

12. Non-Contamination Protocols:
Establish strict procedures to prevent introducing any foreign matter into this pristine environment, preserving its untouched state.

13. Documentation Equipment:
Bring appropriate imaging and recording devices to document this unique environment for scientific study.

14. Gravity Adaptation:
Prepare for potential changes in how gravity affects the traveler at this scale, possibly including personal propulsion devices.

15. Temporal Anchoring:
Consider methods to "anchor" the traveler's timestream to the outside world, preventing potential time-loss due to the timeless nature of the environment.

By thoroughly addressing these aspects, a human multiverse traveler can be well-prepared for the challenges and wonders of exploring this microscopic crystalline world.
