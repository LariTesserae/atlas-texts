To prepare for visiting the Arboreal Metropolis, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Attire:
   - Wear breathable, moisture-wicking clothing to manage the cool, slightly dry climate.
   - Choose clothes in natural, muted colors to blend with the environment.
   - Bring a light jacket for cooler periods, especially at night.
   - Wear sturdy, comfortable shoes with good grip for navigating the organic, potentially uneven surfaces.

2. Equipment:
   - Pack a reusable water bottle and water purification system, as natural water sources may be scarce.
   - Bring a high-quality air filter mask to protect against potential spores or pollen.
   - Include a compact, solar-powered charger for any electronic devices.
   - Carry a multi-tool with plant-friendly attachments for potential interactions with the environment.

3. Navigation:
   - Obtain or create a 3D map of the city-trees and their interconnected systems.
   - Bring a compass, as traditional GPS might not function in this unique environment.
   - Consider packing bioluminescent markers to leave trails in case of disorientation.

4. Health and Safety:
   - Get vaccinations for any plant-based pathogens specific to this world.
   - Bring antihistamines and anti-inflammatory medications in case of allergic reactions.
   - Pack a first-aid kit with additional items for treating plant-related injuries (e.g., sap burns, thorn punctures).
   - Consider bringing oxygen supplements, as the air composition might differ from Earth's.

5. Communication:
   - Learn basic communication methods used in the Arboreal Metropolis, including any plant-based signaling systems.
   - Bring a universal translator device, if available, to interpret potential plant-based languages or vibrations.

6. Research and Documentation:
   - Pack a high-quality camera with macro lens capabilities for detailed plant photography.
   - Bring a waterproof notebook and pencils for taking notes in potentially humid conditions.
   - Include small sample collection containers for (approved) specimen gathering.

7. Nutrition:
   - Pack nutrient-dense, plant-based foods that won't introduce foreign organisms to the ecosystem.
   - Bring supplements to compensate for any nutritional deficiencies in the local diet.

8. Respect for the Environment:
   - Study and adhere to any local guidelines for interacting with the plant-based infrastructure.
   - Bring biodegradable waste bags to minimize your impact on the ecosystem.

9. Sensory Adaptation:
   - Pack noise-cancelling earbuds to manage the constant low-level sounds of the city.
   - Bring blue-light blocking glasses to adjust to the bioluminescent nighttime environment.

10. Emergency Preparedness:
    - Carry a small, collapsible shelter in case you need to isolate from the plant environment.
    - Include a personal air supply for emergencies where the plant-based atmosphere might become unsuitable.

11. Cultural Sensitivity:
    - Research and respect any customs or rituals related to plant life in the metropolis.
    - Be prepared to move slowly and deliberately to avoid startling or damaging sensitive plant structures.

By considering these aspects, a traveler can be well-prepared for the unique challenges and experiences that the Arboreal Metropolis presents, ensuring a safe and enriching visit to this extraordinary plant-based urban ecosystem.
