To prepare for visiting this subterranean city, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Bring a high-quality respirator or breathing apparatus to filter out potentially harmful chemicals, spores, and particulates in the air.
   - Wear protective goggles to shield your eyes from irritants and the constant low light conditions.
   - Pack heat-resistant clothing to cope with the high temperatures. Lightweight, moisture-wicking fabrics would be ideal.

2. Lighting:
   - Bring multiple sources of artificial light (headlamps, handheld lights, glow sticks) as the bioluminescent fungi may not provide sufficient illumination.
   - Include spare batteries or charging equipment compatible with the city's power systems.

3. Navigation:
   - Acquire or create detailed maps of the tunnel system if possible. If not, bring tools for mapping (digital or analog) as you explore.
   - Pack a reliable compass or other navigation tool that works underground.
   - Bring rope and climbing gear to navigate steep or difficult passages.

4. Water and Food:
   - Carry a substantial water supply, as the local recycled water may not be safe for human consumption.
   - Bring water purification tablets or filters as a backup.
   - Pack dense, nutrient-rich food that doesn't require cooking, as local food sources may be unsuitable or scarce.

5. Medical Supplies:
   - Include a comprehensive first-aid kit with treatments for burns, cuts, and respiratory issues.
   - Bring antifungal medications in case of exposure to unfamiliar fungi.
   - Pack electrolyte supplements to combat potential dehydration from the heat.

6. Communication:
   - Bring short-range communication devices (walkie-talkies) for staying in contact with travel companions in the maze-like environment.
   - Have a method for leaving trail markers or breadcrumbs to find your way back.

7. Cultural Preparation:
   - Research or inquire about local customs and etiquette, especially regarding water usage and conservation.
   - Learn basic phrases or signs to communicate with inhabitants or automated systems.

8. Time Management:
   - Bring a reliable timekeeping device to maintain your own schedule in the absence of natural day/night cycles.
   - Plan for potential disorientation due to the lack of familiar time cues.

9. Scientific Equipment:
   - Pack tools for analyzing air quality, temperature, and radiation levels.
   - Bring sample collection gear if planning to study local flora or materials.

10. Emergency Gear:
    - Include an emergency shelter or bivouac sack in case you get lost or cut off from your planned route.
    - Bring a multi-tool and basic repair materials to interact with or fix mechanical systems if necessary.

11. Psychological Preparation:
    - Be mentally prepared for the claustrophobic environment and lack of natural light.
    - Consider bringing comfort items to combat potential feelings of isolation or unease.

12. Exit Strategy:
    - Have a clear plan for how and when you'll leave the city.
    - Ensure multiple people know your plans and expected return time.

Remember, flexibility and caution are key when exploring such an alien environment. Be prepared to abort the mission if conditions prove too hazardous or unpredictable.
