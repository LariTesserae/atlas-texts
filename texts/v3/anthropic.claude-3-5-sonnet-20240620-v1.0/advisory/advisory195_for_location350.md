To prepare for visiting the Whispering Plateaus, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Protective Gear:
   - Durable, breathable clothing to withstand the warm climate and protect from the intense sun.
   - Sturdy hiking boots with excellent traction for navigating the uneven terrain.
   - A wide-brimmed hat and high-SPF sunscreen to shield from UV radiation.
   - Protective eyewear to guard against wind-blown debris and the intense light.
   - Earplugs or noise-canceling headphones to mitigate the constant roar of the wind.

2. Survival Equipment:
   - A robust water filtration system, as water sources may be scarce and potentially contaminated.
   - High-capacity water containers to store filtered water.
   - Non-perishable, high-energy food supplies.
   - A multi-tool for various purposes, including sample collection.
   - A first-aid kit with additional supplies for treating potential injuries from falls or encounters with unfamiliar flora and fauna.

3. Navigation and Communication:
   - A compass and detailed topographic maps, as electronic devices may be unreliable due to potential magnetic interference.
   - A personal locator beacon for emergencies.
   - A long-range communication device, preferably one resistant to electromagnetic interference.

4. Scientific and Observational Tools:
   - A sturdy, wind-resistant tent or portable shelter.
   - A digital camera with extra batteries to document the unique phenomena.
   - A journal and writing implements for taking notes.
   - Binoculars for observing distant features and wildlife.
   - Sample collection containers for gathering geological and biological specimens.
   - A handheld spectrometer or similar device to analyze the composition of the stone and air particles.

5. Specialized Equipment:
   - Climbing gear (ropes, harnesses, carabiners) for scaling the plateaus.
   - A portable seismograph to measure and record the "stone tide" phenomenon.
   - A high-quality air filtration mask to protect against potential airborne particles or spores.
   - Ultraviolet and infrared cameras to capture any invisible phenomena.

6. Mental and Physical Preparation:
   - Undergo endurance training to prepare for the challenging terrain and climate.
   - Practice meditation or other focusing techniques to maintain mental clarity in the face of constant sensory stimulation.
   - Study geological and botanical principles to better understand and appreciate the environment.
   - Learn basic archaeology techniques to responsibly interact with any remnants of ancient civilizations.

7. Cultural Considerations:
   - Research any known local customs or taboos related to the plateaus.
   - Prepare to document observations ethically and without disturbing the environment.
   - Be ready to encounter potentially intelligent or semi-intelligent lifeforms and have a plan for non-invasive interaction.

8. Contingency Planning:
   - Develop a detailed itinerary and share it with a trusted contact in your home universe.
   - Establish clear emergency protocols, including criteria for early evacuation.
   - Pack redundant supplies of critical items in case of loss or failure.

By thoroughly preparing in these areas, a multiverse traveler can maximize their chances of a safe, productive, and awe-inspiring visit to the Whispering Plateaus. The key is to approach the journey with respect for the environment, a spirit of scientific inquiry, and a readiness to adapt to the unexpected.
