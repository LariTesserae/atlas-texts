To prepare for visiting the Whisper Fields, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Sensory Adaptation:
   - Prepare for sensory deprivation, particularly auditory. Consider spending time in a sensory deprivation tank before the trip to acclimate to the lack of sound.
   - Bring noise-cancelling headphones to wear initially, gradually reducing their use to ease into the silence.
   - Practice meditation or mindfulness techniques to manage potential anxiety from the unusual sensory experience.

2. Communication:
   - Learn basic sign language or develop a visual communication system with your travel companions.
   - Bring writing materials for communication if needed.
   - Consider bringing vibration-based communication devices that won't produce audible sound.

3. Navigation:
   - Bring a compass and detailed maps, as GPS might not function in this realm.
   - Consider bringing a drone for aerial surveying, ensuring it's modified to operate silently.
   - Carry brightly colored markers or flags to mark your path, as the uniform landscape can be disorienting.

4. Environmental Protection:
   - Wear protective goggles to shield eyes from the fine, shimmering dust.
   - Bring a rebreather or filtration mask to prevent inhalation of crystal particles.
   - Wear durable, close-fitting clothing to minimize exposure to the dry, dusty environment.
   - Use heavy-duty moisturizers and lip balm to combat the extreme dryness.

5. Footwear:
   - Bring sturdy, noise-dampening boots with excellent grip for navigating the crystalline terrain.
   - Consider bringing climbing gear for exploring taller crystal formations safely.

6. Scientific Equipment:
   - Pack instruments for measuring vibrations and resonances to potentially communicate with local fauna.
   - Bring tools for careful sampling of crystals and plant-like formations (ensuring minimal disturbance).
   - Include a high-resolution camera with a silent shutter for documentation.

7. Sustenance:
   - Bring ample water supplies, as there is no natural water source.
   - Pack dry, non-perishable food that doesn't require noisy preparation.
   - Consider bringing a silent, solar-powered water purification system for extended stays.

8. Time Perception:
   - Bring multiple timekeeping devices, as the perpetual dusk may disrupt natural circadian rhythms.
   - Consider a silent, vibration-based alarm for maintaining schedules.

9. Mental Preparation:
   - Research techniques for managing sensory deprivation-induced hallucinations or anxiety.
   - Prepare engaging silent activities (drawing, puzzles, reading) to maintain mental stimulation.

10. Emergency Preparedness:
    - Bring a silent emergency beacon or flare system for attracting attention if needed.
    - Pack a first-aid kit with extra supplies for potential crystal-related injuries.
    - Consider bringing a small, collapsible shelter for protection during rest periods.

11. Respect for the Environment:
    - Develop a 'leave no trace' plan to minimize impact on this delicate ecosystem.
    - Prepare to document observations without disturbing the natural or artificial structures.

12. Cultural Sensitivity:
    - Research any known protocols or taboos related to interacting with the local fauna or ancient artifacts.
    - Prepare to move slowly and deliberately to minimize disturbances to the environment.

By thoroughly preparing in these areas, a traveler can hope to safely and respectfully explore the unique and challenging environment of the Whisper Fields, maximizing their ability to observe and learn from this extraordinary place.
