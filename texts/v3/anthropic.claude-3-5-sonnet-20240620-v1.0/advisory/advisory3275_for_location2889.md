12. Nutrient supplements: The available food sources may not be suitable for human consumption. Bring nutrient-dense, moisture-resistant food supplements.

13. Thermal imaging device: This can help you detect the presence of living creatures and identify thermal currents for navigation.

14. Portable atmospheric analyzer: Use this to monitor the composition of the air and alert you to any dangerous changes or areas with low oxygen levels.

15. Detoxification kit: Bring a medical kit specifically designed to deal with potential allergic reactions or toxins you might encounter in this unfamiliar ecosystem.

16. Bioluminescent emergency beacon: In case you get lost or separated from your group, a bioluminescent beacon could help you signal for help in a way that's visible in this environment.
