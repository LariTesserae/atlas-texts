To prepare for visiting the Forge of Eternity, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Heat Protection:
- Wear a fully enclosed, heat-resistant suit capable of withstanding extremely high temperatures (potentially up to 1000°C or more).
- Include a cooling system within the suit to regulate body temperature.
- Use heat-resistant boots with thick, insulated soles to protect against hot surfaces.

2. Breathing Apparatus:
- Bring a self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) with ample oxygen supply for the duration of the visit.
- The air is likely to be toxic and too hot to breathe safely.
- Include air filtration systems to remove any particulate matter or harmful gases.

3. Eye Protection:
- Use specially designed goggles or a full-face mask with heat-resistant, anti-glare lenses.
- The intense light from molten metals and the overall dim environment will require adaptable vision protection.

4. Navigation and Communication:
- Bring heat-resistant, shielded electronic devices for navigation and communication.
- Include a backup analog compass and map, as electronic devices may malfunction in the extreme conditions.
- Carry signal flares or other visibility aids in case of emergency.

5. Tools and Equipment:
- Pack heat-resistant tools for collecting samples or making emergency repairs.
- Bring a multi-tool and a sturdy, heat-resistant rope or cable.
- Include sensors to detect dangerous gases, extreme temperatures, or unstable ground.

6. Food and Water:
- Carry a significant supply of water in insulated containers to prevent evaporation.
- Bring high-energy, non-perishable food that won't be affected by the heat.
- Include electrolyte supplements to replace what's lost through sweating.

7. First Aid:
- Pack a comprehensive first aid kit with particular emphasis on treating burns, heat exhaustion, and dehydration.
- Include cooling packs and burn gels.

8. Time Management:
- Plan for short exploration periods with frequent rest breaks in cooler areas (if any can be found).
- Bring a heat-resistant timepiece to track exposure duration.

9. Physical Preparation:
- Undergo heat acclimatization training before the visit.
- Improve cardiovascular fitness to better handle the stress of the extreme environment.

10. Mental Preparation:
- Study geological and industrial processes to better understand and navigate the environment.
- Prepare for the psychological impact of the alien landscape and isolation.

11. Escape Plan:
- Develop and memorize multiple escape routes.
- Carry a personal locator beacon for emergency extraction.

12. Specialized Equipment:
- Bring heat-resistant sample containers for collecting materials.
- Include a spectrometer or similar device for analyzing the composition of metals and gases.

13. Radiation Protection:
- Wear a radiation detection badge and bring a Geiger counter, as there may be radioactive materials present.

14. Team Coordination:
- Travel with at least one partner and maintain constant communication.
- Establish clear protocols for emergency situations.

15. Cultural Sensitivity:
- Be prepared for potential encounters with intelligent beings. Bring universal translation devices and practice non-threatening body language.

Remember, the Forge of Eternity is an extreme and potentially deadly environment. Even with all these preparations, the visit should be kept as brief as possible, and all safety protocols should be strictly followed at all times.
