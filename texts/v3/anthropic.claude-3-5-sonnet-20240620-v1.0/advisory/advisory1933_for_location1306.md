To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Breathing Apparatus:
Given that the environment is primarily aqueous, a specialized breathing system is crucial. Develop or acquire a compact, long-lasting oxygen supply that can function in a viscous liquid environment. This should include a full-face mask with an integrated communication system.

2. Protective Suit:
Design a form-fitting, flexible suit that can withstand prolonged immersion in the thick, bioluminescent liquid. The suit should be resistant to potential chemical reactions from the alien flora and fauna. Include a self-regulating temperature control system to maintain comfort in the consistently warm environment.

3. Propulsion System:
Incorporate a quiet, efficient propulsion system into the suit to navigate through the dense vegetation and vast chambers. This could be in the form of small, vectored thrusters or a fish-like tail mechanism.

4. Adaptive Camouflage:
To minimize disruption to the ecosystem and avoid unwanted attention from any potential predators, integrate a camouflage system that can mimic the surrounding bioluminescent patterns and colors.

5. Bio-containment Measures:
Implement strict bio-containment protocols to prevent contamination in both directions. This includes a decontamination system built into the suit and a quarantine procedure for re-entry to your travel vessel.

6. Advanced Sensors:
Equip yourself with multi-spectrum sensors to analyze the composition of the liquid, detect life forms, and monitor the energy patterns of the mysterious artifacts. Include a system to measure and record the complex soundscape.

7. Sample Collection Tools:
Bring specialized tools for collecting and preserving samples of the viscous liquid, plant matter, and possibly small organisms. Ensure these tools can operate in the unique aqueous environment without damaging the samples.

8. Energy Shield:
Develop a personal energy shield to protect against unexpected currents, potential collisions with rapidly growing plant structures, or interactions with the enigmatic artifacts.

9. Quantum Communicator:
Given the possible interference from the dense liquid and vegetation, a standard radio might not suffice. Invest in a quantum entanglement-based communication device to maintain contact with your base of operations.

10. Temporal Stabilizer:
The rapid growth cycles and dynamic nature of the environment suggest potential temporal anomalies. A personal temporal stabilizer could help maintain your normal time flow and protect against unforeseen time-related effects.

11. Neural Interface:
To better understand and potentially communicate with the distributed intelligence of the ecosystem, consider a non-invasive neural interface that can interpret and translate complex patterns of energy and information flow.

12. Emergency Extraction Device:
Carry a reliable emergency extraction device, possibly utilizing teleportation or dimensional shift technology, to quickly remove yourself from the environment if needed.

13. Psychological Preparation:
Undergo extensive psychological training to prepare for the potentially overwhelming sensory experience and the profound sense of alienness. Practice meditation techniques to maintain focus and calm in this surreal environment.

14. Ethical Guidelines:
Establish clear ethical guidelines for interaction with this unique ecosystem. This should include protocols for observation, sample collection, and potential communication attempts, all designed to minimize impact on this delicate and possibly sentient environment.

Remember, despite all preparations, expect the unexpected. This alien sky-ocean is likely to challenge our understanding of life, intelligence, and the nature of reality itself. Approach with reverence, curiosity, and extreme caution.
