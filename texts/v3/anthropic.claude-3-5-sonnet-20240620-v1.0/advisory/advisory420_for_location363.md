To prepare for visiting this unique subterranean location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Lighting: Bring reliable, long-lasting light sources. Headlamps are ideal for hands-free operation. Pack extra batteries or rechargeable power banks. Consider bringing bioluminescent light sticks as a backup or for ambient lighting.

2. Protective Gear:
   - Wear a respirator or dust mask to filter out the fine mineral dust.
   - Bring goggles to protect eyes from dust and potential spores.
   - Wear sturdy, closed-toe shoes with good traction for navigating smooth limestone surfaces.
   - Consider wearing gloves to protect hands when touching surfaces.

3. Clothing: Dress in layers. The cavern is warm, but temperatures may vary in different areas. Moisture-wicking fabrics are recommended.

4. Water and Nutrition: Bring ample water supplies, as the environment is arid. Pack non-perishable, high-energy foods.

5. Navigation Tools: Bring a compass (though it may not work underground) and detailed maps if available. Consider bringing string or biodegradable markers to mark your path.

6. Time-Keeping Device: Since there's no natural light cycle, a watch or timer is crucial to track time spent underground.

7. Scientific Equipment:
   - A digital hygrometer to measure humidity levels.
   - A thermometer for tracking temperature variations.
   - PH testing strips to analyze any water sources found.
   - A small microscope or magnifying glass for examining fungi and plant life.
   - Specimen collection containers (if permitted) for safely gathering samples.

8. Documentation Tools:
   - A camera with low-light capabilities and extra batteries.
   - A notebook and pencils for observations and sketches.
   - A voice recorder for verbal notes.

9. First Aid Kit: Include standard supplies plus additional items like:
   - Antihistamines in case of allergic reactions to spores or plants.
   - Sterile eye wash solution.
   - Burn treatment (in case of accidental contact with unknown substances).

10. Communication Device: Bring a satellite phone or long-range radio, though signal may be limited underground.

11. Rope and Basic Climbing Gear: For safely navigating any steep or challenging areas.

12. Contamination Prevention:
    - Bring disposable shoe covers or a separate pair of shoes to avoid introducing outside contaminants.
    - Pack sealable bags for storing any samples or contaminated items.

13. Mental Preparation:
    - Practice meditation or mindfulness techniques to cope with the profound silence and potential sense of isolation.
    - Bring a small comfort item to help maintain a connection with the outside world.

14. Time Dilation Awareness: Be prepared for the possibility that your perception of time may be altered in this timeless environment. Set regular alarms to ensure you don't lose track of time.

15. Cultural Sensitivity: If you encounter any artifacts, do not disturb them. Be prepared to document their location and appearance without touching.

16. Exit Strategy: Always inform someone outside of your planned return time and have a clear plan for exiting the cavern.

Remember, the goal is to observe and learn while minimizing your impact on this delicate ecosystem. Move slowly, speak softly if at all, and be prepared to spend long periods in stillness to fully appreciate the subtle rhythms of this ancient environment.
