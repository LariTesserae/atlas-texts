To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Bring a sealed, self-contained breathing apparatus. The thin air and potentially toxic spores require respiratory protection.
   - Wear a fully enclosed hazmat suit to prevent skin contact with the bioluminescent moisture and viscous rain.
   - Use protective goggles or a full-face mask to shield eyes from the unusual light conditions and airborne organisms.

2. Scientific Equipment:
   - Pack a multi-spectrum analyzer to study the bioluminescent phenomena and strange light conditions.
   - Bring sample collection kits for safely gathering specimens of the unique flora and microorganisms.
   - Include a portable DNA sequencer to analyze the complex biological structures on-site.

3. Navigation and Communication:
   - Standard GPS may not work here. Bring celestial navigation tools and a compass that doesn't rely on magnetic fields.
   - Have a way to mark your path, as the shifting cityscape may disorient you.
   - Bring long-range communication devices that can penetrate the unusual atmospheric conditions.

4. Survival Gear:
   - Pack a water purification system capable of handling unknown contaminants.
   - Bring nutrient-dense, self-contained food supplies to avoid consuming anything from this alien ecosystem.
   - Include a multi-tool adaptable for interaction with organic structures.

5. Documentation Equipment:
   - Bring cameras capable of capturing images in low-light conditions and across multiple spectrums.
   - Include audio recording devices to capture the subtle hum and other unique sounds.
   - Pack holographic imaging technology to record the three-dimensional aspects of the growing structures.

6. Psychological Preparation:
   - Meditate or undergo sensory deprivation training to prepare for the alien nature of this environment.
   - Study abstract art and non-Euclidean geometry to help comprehend the unfamiliar urban layout.
   - Practice lucid dreaming techniques to maintain mental clarity in this dreamlike landscape.

7. Time Dilation Equipment:
   - Bring devices to measure the passage of time, as perception may be altered in this environment.
   - Include a personal temporal anchor in case local time flows differently.

8. Energy and Power:
   - Pack solar panels and backup batteries, as conventional power sources may not be available.
   - Consider bringing bioluminescent-reactive energy collectors to harness local energy sources.

9. Emergency Extraction:
   - Have a personal teleportation device or interdimensional beacon for emergency evacuation.
   - Include a small force field generator to create a safe zone if needed.

10. Cultural Sensitivity:
    - Prepare non-verbal communication methods to interact with the shadowy inhabitants.
    - Bring offerings or tokens that might be universally recognized as peaceful gestures.

11. Adaptive Clothing:
    - Wear clothing that can adjust to rapid temperature changes and varying gravity conditions.
    - Include footwear that can adapt to shifting, possibly organic surfaces.

12. Reality Anchors:
    - Bring personal items from your home reality to maintain a psychological connection to your origin.
    - Include a quantum entanglement device to maintain a physical link to your home universe.

Remember, flexibility and open-mindedness are key. This location challenges conventional understanding of urban environments and biological processes. Be prepared for your equipment to behave unpredictably and for your senses to be confounded. Above all, maintain a stance of observation rather than intervention, as your presence may have unforeseen effects on this delicate and evolving ecosystem.
