12. Vaccinations: Ensure you're up to date on vaccinations, particularly those for waterborne diseases.

13. Basic woodworking and rope-tying skills: These may be useful for navigating or repairing structures.

14. A guide or translator: The locals may have their own language or customs, so having a way to communicate is crucial.

15. Portable energy source: Electronics may be rare here, so bring solar chargers or long-lasting batteries.

16. Antihistamines and allergy medications: The unique flora and fauna may trigger allergic reactions.

17. Waterproof camera or recording device: To document this unique experience.

18. Flotation device: In case you need to navigate deeper waters.

19. Flexible mindset: Be prepared for constant change and the blurring of familiar boundaries.

20. Respect for local customs and environment: Remember you're a visitor in a delicate ecosystem.
