To prepare for visiting this remarkable crystal forest beneath the surface, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Wear a sealed, pressurized suit capable of maintaining a stable internal environment. The atmosphere may not be breathable, and the pressure could be significantly different from Earth's surface.
   - Ensure the suit is resistant to sharp edges and can withstand potential crystal growth or movement.
   - Include a temperature regulation system, as the environment may be extremely cold or hot.

2. Lighting:
   - Bring powerful, adjustable lights. While there's a faint glow from the crystals, it may not be sufficient for human vision.
   - Include infrared and ultraviolet light sources to potentially reveal hidden aspects of the environment.

3. Navigation:
   - Standard GPS won't work underground. Bring a 3D mapping device capable of creating and storing a map of the crystal structures.
   - Include a way to mark your path, such as biodegradable paint or temporary laser etching, to avoid getting lost in the maze-like environment.

4. Time Perception:
   - Bring multiple timekeeping devices synchronized to Earth time. The different time scale of this realm could be disorienting.
   - Consider a device that can detect and measure the growth rate of crystals to help gauge the passage of time in this environment.

5. Communication:
   - Standard radio may not work through the dense crystal structures. Bring equipment capable of transmitting through solid mineral matter, possibly using low-frequency waves or quantum entanglement technology.

6. Sample Collection:
   - Include tools for safely collecting crystal and lifeform samples without disturbing the environment. This might include small force field generators to isolate specimens.
   - Bring containment units that can preserve the unique properties of the crystalline life forms.

7. Interaction Tools:
   - Bring devices capable of detecting and interpreting vibrations in the crystal lattice, which may be how local lifeforms communicate.
   - Include tools for potential interaction with the primitive tools found in the environment, such as universal manipulators or nano-scale assembly devices.

8. Protection from Lifeforms:
   - Ensure your suit has a force field or other protective measure against potentially hostile crystalline fauna.
   - Bring non-invasive deterrents that won't harm the local ecosystem.

9. Mental Preparation:
   - Undergo psychological training to cope with the alien environment and extremely slow pace of change.
   - Practice meditation techniques to align your perception with the different time scale of this world.

10. Energy and Sustenance:
    - Bring a long-lasting power source for all equipment, possibly drawing energy from the crystal structures themselves if ethical and safe to do so.
    - Include a recycling life support system for extended stays, as traditional resupply may be impossible.

11. Exit Strategy:
    - Have a clear plan and equipment for leaving the crystal forest and returning to your entry point.
    - Include emergency extraction devices in case of becoming trapped or lost.

12. Ethical Guidelines:
    - Establish clear protocols for minimal impact exploration. The ecosystem appears delicate and any interference could have long-lasting consequences.

Remember, this environment operates on a vastly different timescale and physical laws than what we're accustomed to. Patience, careful observation, and respect for the alien ecosystem will be key to a successful expedition.
