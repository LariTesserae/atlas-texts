To prepare for visiting this location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and make these preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Wear a sealed, environmentally-controlled suit. The atmosphere may not be breathable for humans, and the high moisture content could lead to hypothermia.
   - Ensure the suit is resistant to acidic conditions, as the mineral-rich environment might be corrosive.
   - Include a filtration system for air and water, as local resources may not be safe for consumption.

2. Safety Equipment:
   - Bring sturdy, waterproof boots with excellent traction for navigating the uneven, damp terrain.
   - Carry a multi-function tool for various purposes, including self-defense against potentially aggressive fauna.
   - Pack a high-powered, waterproof flashlight or headlamp for visibility in the dim light and during nocturnal explorations.
   - Include a personal locator beacon in case of emergencies.

3. Scientific and Observational Tools:
   - Bring a rugged, waterproof camera or recording device to document the unique flora and fauna.
   - Pack a portable spectrometer to analyze the composition of air, water, and soil samples.
   - Include a seismograph to monitor and predict ground tremors.
   - Carry a multi-spectrum scanner to detect any potentially harmful radiation or energy fields.

4. Navigation and Communication:
   - Bring a advanced GPS system that can function in alternate universes.
   - Include a long-range communication device capable of interdimensional signals.
   - Pack physical maps and a compass as backup, though be aware they may not function normally in this environment.

5. Survival Gear:
   - Bring a portable shelter resistant to rapid weather changes and strong winds.
   - Pack a supply of nutrient-dense, long-lasting food and a water purification system.
   - Include a first-aid kit with broad-spectrum antibiotics and antifungal medications.

6. Cultural Sensitivity:
   - Bring non-invasive observation equipment to study the remnants of civilization without disturbing them.
   - Pack small, universally valuable items for potential trade or appeasement of any sentient beings encountered.

7. Mental Preparation:
   - Undergo psychological training to prepare for the alien nature of the environment and potential isolation.
   - Learn meditation techniques to maintain calm during rapid environmental changes or encounters with strange lifeforms.

8. Physical Conditioning:
   - Engage in strength and endurance training to handle the physical demands of the terrain.
   - Practice moving in restrictive environmental suits to acclimate before the journey.

9. Decontamination Protocols:
   - Establish strict procedures for entering and exiting the suit to prevent contamination in either direction.
   - Bring equipment for sterilizing any samples or equipment before returning to the home universe.

10. Time Management:
    - Plan for a limited-duration visit due to the unstable nature of the environment.
    - Set up a schedule for regular check-ins with a base team in the home universe.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key when traveling to such an alien and dynamic environment. Always prioritize safety and be prepared to abort the mission if conditions become too hazardous.
