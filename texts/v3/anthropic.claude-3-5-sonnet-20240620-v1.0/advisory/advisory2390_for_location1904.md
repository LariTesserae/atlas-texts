To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Specialized Diving Suit: Obtain a highly advanced diving suit capable of withstanding extreme pressure and temperatures. It should be reinforced to protect against the scalding water temperatures and equipped with a cooling system to regulate body temperature.

2. Breathing Apparatus: Bring a closed-circuit rebreather system with multiple redundancies. The water composition may be unlike anything on Earth, so standard scuba gear might be insufficient.

3. Communication Device: Acquire a device that can transmit through dense water and possibly interdimensional barriers. Standard radio waves may not work in this environment.

4. Lighting Equipment: Pack powerful, waterproof lights with various spectrums. The bioluminescent glow may not be sufficient for human vision, and different light frequencies might reveal hidden aspects of the environment.

5. Recording Devices: Bring cameras and sensors capable of functioning under extreme pressure and heat. These should be able to capture a wide range of data, including water composition, temperature fluctuations, and any anomalous energy readings.

6. Navigation Tools: Standard GPS won't work here. Bring advanced sonar equipment and possibly quantum entanglement-based positioning systems to navigate the maze-like structure.

7. Protective Eyewear: Obtain goggles or a helmet visor with adjustable light filtering. The shifting bioluminescence could be disorienting or even harmful to human eyes.

8. Sample Collection Kit: Pack specialized containers that can preserve samples from this unique ecosystem without contamination or degradation.

9. Time-Keeping Device: Bring a clock that operates independently of Earth's time standards, as time may flow differently in this location.

10. Mental Preparation: Undergo extensive psychological training to cope with the alien environment and potential reality-bending experiences. Meditation techniques and grounding exercises may be helpful.

11. Physical Conditioning: Engage in intensive physical training to improve stamina, flexibility, and strength. The unpredictable currents and disorienting environment will be physically demanding.

12. First Aid Kit: Pack a comprehensive medical kit including treatments for burns, decompression sickness, and exposure to unknown biological agents.

13. Energy Source: Bring a long-lasting, pressure-resistant power source for all equipment. Solar or standard battery power won't be viable in this deep-sea environment.

14. Decontamination Equipment: Upon return, be prepared for thorough decontamination procedures to prevent any potential interdimensional contamination.

15. Language Translation Device: While unlikely to encounter language in a traditional sense, a universal translator might help interpret any patterns or signals encountered in the cathedral.

16. Reality Anchor: Consider bringing a personal item from your home dimension to serve as a psychological anchor in case the laws of physics become too disorienting.

17. Emergency Extraction Plan: Develop and memorize multiple contingency plans for rapid evacuation from the location, including interdimensional recall devices if available.

Remember, this environment is unlike anything encountered on Earth. Expect the unexpected, remain flexible in your approach, and prioritize safety at all times. The mysteries of the impossible ocean cathedral may be enticing, but they come with significant risks that require utmost caution and preparation.
