To prepare for visiting this extraordinary liquid world, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Environmental Protection Suit: 
   - A fully enclosed, pressure-resistant suit is essential. It should be capable of withstanding the intense pressure of the dense liquid environment and protecting against potential chemical reactions.
   - The suit must be completely waterproof and equipped with a robust filtration system to prevent any of the unknown liquid from entering.
   - Include a built-in cooling system to regulate body temperature in the potentially warm, viscous environment.

2. Breathing Apparatus:
   - A self-contained breathing system with long-lasting oxygen supply is crucial. The atmosphere within the bubble-islands may not be breathable for humans.
   - Include backup oxygen tanks and emergency purification systems.

3. Propulsion System:
   - A personal propulsion device to navigate through the dense liquid environment. This could be in the form of a jet pack or underwater propulsion vehicle.
   - Ensure the propulsion system can handle the varying densities and currents of the liquid world.

4. Communication Equipment:
   - Waterproof and pressure-resistant communication devices that can function in this unique environment.
   - Include a universal translator in case of encountering any sentient life forms.

5. Protective Eyewear:
   - Special goggles or a helmet visor with adjustable tint to protect against the intense, omnipresent light.
   - Include filters to help navigate through the visual illusions created by the refracting light.

6. Scientific Instruments:
   - Portable devices to analyze the composition of the liquid, the atmosphere in the bubble-islands, and any life forms encountered.
   - Instruments to measure energy outputs, radiation levels, and other environmental factors.

7. Navigation Tools:
   - GPS-like system adapted for this fluid world, potentially using the bubble-islands as reference points.
   - Sonar or echolocation device to detect large objects or creatures moving beneath the surface.

8. Energy Shields:
   - A personal energy shield to protect against unexpected geysers, industrial processes, or potentially hostile life forms.

9. Sample Collection Kit:
   - Specialized containers to safely collect and store samples of the liquid, gaseous substances, and any organic matter for later study.

10. Emergency Buoyancy Device:
    - A system to rapidly ascend to the surface or to a bubble-island in case of equipment failure or emergency.

11. Adaptive Camouflage:
    - A system to blend in with the surrounding environment, useful for observing without disturbing potential life forms or industrial processes.

12. Medical Kit:
    - Specialized medical supplies to treat potential injuries or reactions to the unique environment, including anti-toxins and burn treatments.

13. Power Source:
    - A long-lasting, waterproof power source for all equipment, potentially drawing energy from the abundant light or the liquid itself.

14. Mental Preparation:
    - Psychological training to cope with the alien environment, the constant motion, and the potential sensory overload from the light and sound.

15. Decontamination Procedures:
    - A detailed protocol for cleaning and decontaminating all equipment and the suit itself before returning to the home universe.

16. Cultural Briefing:
    - If there are known sentient inhabitants of this world, a thorough briefing on their culture, customs, and any known protocols for interaction.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key when exploring such an alien environment. Be prepared for the unexpected and always prioritize safety over exploration.
