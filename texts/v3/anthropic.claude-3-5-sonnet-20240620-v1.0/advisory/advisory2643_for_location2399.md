To prepare for visiting the Floating Spires of Aeraquartz, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Atmospheric Protection:
- Bring a sealed, pressurized suit with its own oxygen supply. The air is likely unbreathable for humans and may be corrosive or toxic.
- Ensure the suit can withstand high moisture levels and protect against potential energy discharges from the environment.

2. Gravity Adaptation:
- Pack gravity boots or a personal gravity field generator. The planet's gravitational pull may be different from Earth's, and you'll need to navigate around floating structures.

3. Vision and Communication:
- Bring adjustable-tint goggles to protect against the intense light from the twin suns and the constantly shifting rainbows.
- Have a communication device that can function in high-interference environments, as the energy beings and crystalline structures may disrupt standard signals.

4. Transportation:
- A personal antigravity device or small hovering craft will be essential for exploring the floating spires and navigating the mercury-like liquid below.

5. Scientific Equipment:
- Pack multi-spectrum analyzers to study the unique light phenomena and energy beings.
- Bring sample collection tools that can handle both solid crystalline structures and the unusual liquid.
- Include a device to measure and record the humming frequencies of the spires.

6. Energy Protection:
- Carry an personal energy field generator to protect against potential interactions with the energy beings or unexpected discharges from the spires.

7. Time Dilation Preparedness:
- Bring a reliable timekeeping device that won't be affected by potential temporal anomalies in this physics-defying environment.

8. Recording Equipment:
- Pack high-quality holographic recording devices to capture the ever-changing landscape and energy being interactions.

9. Language and Archaeological Tools:
- Bring advanced translation software and archaeological scanning equipment to study the remnants of the ancient civilization.

10. Thermal Regulation:
- Ensure your suit has excellent thermal regulation. The environment may shift between extreme temperatures due to the twin suns and energy fluctuations.

11. Mental Preparation:
- Practice meditation or other mental focusing techniques. The alien nature of this world may be psychologically challenging.

12. Emergency Gear:
- Pack a multi-dimensional emergency beacon in case you need to be extracted quickly.
- Bring a compact interdimensional portal device as a last-resort escape method.

13. Sustenance:
- Carry concentrated nutrient supplies and a water purification system, as local resources are likely incompatible with human biology.

14. Adaptive Clothing:
- Under your protective suit, wear clothing made from smart fabrics that can adjust to rapid environmental changes.

15. Data Storage:
- Bring robust, shielded data storage devices to protect your recordings and findings from potential energy interference.

Remember, flexibility and open-mindedness are key. This environment is likely to present unforeseen challenges, so be prepared to adapt your approach as you explore this extraordinary world.
