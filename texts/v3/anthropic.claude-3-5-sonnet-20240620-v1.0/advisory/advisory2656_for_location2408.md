To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Wear a sealed, full-body environmental suit with its own air supply. This will protect against potential spore inhalation and any unfamiliar microorganisms.
   - Ensure the suit can withstand varying pressure and gravitational conditions.

2. Lighting:
   - Bring powerful, long-lasting light sources. While bioluminescence exists, it may not be sufficient for human vision.
   - Include backup lighting options in case of equipment failure.

3. Navigation Tools:
   - Standard compasses may not work, so bring advanced GPS or quantum positioning systems.
   - Carry 3D mapping equipment to chart the vast, complex terrain.

4. Time-Keeping Devices:
   - Due to potential time distortions, bring multiple, redundant timekeeping devices.
   - Consider atomic clocks or quantum time-keeping technology.

5. Gravity Compensation:
   - Wear adjustable gravity boots or a suit with built-in gravity compensation to handle varying gravitational fields.

6. Communication:
   - Bring long-range communication devices capable of penetrating dense rock and fungal matter.
   - Have a plan for maintaining contact with a base camp or extraction team.

7. Scientific Equipment:
   - Pack tools for collecting and analyzing samples of the fungal matter and spore-dust safely.
   - Bring sensors to measure radiation, energy fields, and other potential anomalies.

8. Power Supply:
   - Carry multiple power sources for all equipment, including solar, chemical, and kinetic options.
   - Be prepared for potential electromagnetic interference from the fungal machines.

9. Nutrition and Hydration:
   - Bring a substantial supply of food and water, as local resources may be inedible or toxic.
   - Include water purification systems in case of emergency.

10. Mental Preparation:
    - Undergo sensory deprivation training to prepare for the overwhelming darkness and silence.
    - Practice meditation techniques to maintain mental stability in an alien environment.

11. Physical Fitness:
    - Engage in endurance training to handle potential long treks through challenging terrain.
    - Practice moving in low-gravity environments if possible.

12. Emergency Gear:
    - Pack a comprehensive medical kit including broad-spectrum antibiotics and anti-fungal medications.
    - Bring emergency shelter and heat sources in case of suit failure.

13. Documentation Equipment:
    - Carry high-resolution cameras with low-light capabilities.
    - Bring audio recording equipment to capture the sounds of the fungal machines.

14. Protective Eyewear:
    - Use goggles or a visor with adjustable light filtering to protect eyes from both darkness and potential bright bioluminescence.

15. Research:
    - Study mycology and industrial processes to better understand and interpret the environment.
    - Learn about cave ecosystems and how to move safely in cavernous environments.

Remember, this is an alien and potentially hazardous environment. Always prioritize safety and be prepared to abort the mission if unforeseen dangers arise. Approach exploration with caution and respect for this unique ecosystem.
