To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Specialized Breathing Apparatus:
   - Design a closed-circuit rebreather system capable of filtering oxygen from the dense, gelatinous fluid.
   - Ensure the system can withstand high pressures and is resistant to the corrosive or absorptive properties of the gel-like medium.

2. Protective Suit:
   - Develop a full-body suit that can withstand the pressure and cold of the environment.
   - The suit should be flexible enough to allow movement in the viscous medium but strong enough to resist its potential corrosive effects.
   - Include a heating system to counteract the pervasive cold.

3. Propulsion System:
   - Traditional swimming methods will be ineffective in this dense fluid.
   - Design a quiet, efficient propulsion system that can move through the gelatinous medium without disturbing the environment excessively.

4. Specialized Lighting:
   - Bring powerful, energy-efficient lights that can penetrate the murky environment.
   - Consider bioluminescent-inspired lighting to minimize disruption to the ecosystem.

5. Communication Equipment:
   - Develop a system for communication that can function in an environment where sound waves struggle to propagate.
   - This might involve low-frequency vibrations or light-based signaling.

6. Scientific Instruments:
   - Bring tools to analyze the composition of the gelatinous fluid and surrounding formations.
   - Include devices to measure pressure, temperature, and any potential energy fields.

7. Sample Collection Gear:
   - Design containers that can preserve samples of the fluid and any life forms encountered without contamination.

8. Time-Keeping Device:
   - Given the timeless nature of the environment, bring a reliable method to track the passage of time.

9. Mental Preparation:
   - Be ready for sensory deprivation and potential disorientation.
   - Practice meditation or other mental techniques to maintain focus in an alien environment.

10. Physical Conditioning:
    - Train for movement in a high-pressure, viscous environment.
    - Improve lung capacity and pressure tolerance through specialized exercises.

11. Decontamination Protocols:
    - Develop procedures to ensure no part of this environment is accidentally brought back to other realms.

12. Energy Source:
    - Bring a long-lasting, self-contained power source for all equipment.

13. Emergency Extraction Plan:
    - Have a foolproof method to return to your entry point or signal for extraction.

14. Buoyancy Control:
    - Develop a system to control your position within the fluid, allowing for ascent, descent, and neutral buoyancy.

15. Data Recording:
    - Bring equipment capable of recording visual, audio (if possible), and other sensory data in this unique environment.

16. Psychological Support:
    - Prepare for the potential psychological impact of existing in such an alien environment.
    - Consider bringing comforting items from home to maintain a connection to familiar reality.

Remember, this environment is unlike anything on Earth. Expect the unexpected and be prepared to adapt your equipment and strategies as you learn more about this fascinating realm. The key is to approach with respect, curiosity, and extreme caution, understanding that you are a visitor in an ancient and possibly sentient ecosystem.
