To prepare for visiting the Helix Core, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Environmental Gear:
   - Wear lightweight, breathable clothing suitable for warm, dry conditions.
   - Bring protective eyewear to shield against the constant, shadowless light.
   - Pack a respirator or air filtration mask to protect against potential mineral particles in the air.

2. Time Perception:
   - Prepare mentally for altered time perception. Bring a reliable watch or timekeeping device to maintain a connection to standard time.
   - Consider meditation techniques or mindfulness exercises to cope with the sense of timelessness.

3. Physical Preparation:
   - Train for extended periods of standing or slow walking, as there may be limited seating in this industrial environment.
   - Practice maintaining balance on slowly rotating surfaces to acclimate to the moving floor.

4. Sensory Adaptation:
   - Bring noise-cancelling earphones to cope with the unusual auditory environment.
   - Consider tinted glasses to adjust to the constant, shadowless illumination.

5. Scientific Equipment:
   - Pack a multi-spectrum camera to capture the bioluminescent lichen and crystal formations.
   - Bring a portable spectrometer to analyze the composition of the air and mineral deposits.

6. Sustenance:
   - Carry ample water supplies, as there are no natural water sources in the Core.
   - Pack nutrient-dense, non-perishable food items for extended stays.

7. Navigation:
   - Bring a compass or gyroscope, as traditional GPS may not function underground.
   - Consider marking your path with removable, eco-friendly markers to avoid disorientation in the uniform environment.

8. Communication:
   - Pack long-range communication devices that can penetrate thick rock layers.
   - Have a backup communication method, such as a personal locator beacon.

9. Safety Gear:
   - Wear sturdy, non-conductive footwear to protect against potential electrical discharges from the circuitry.
   - Bring a personal air quality monitor to alert you to any changes in the atmospheric composition.

10. Documentation:
    - Carry a journal or voice recorder to document observations, as the altered time perception may affect memory.
    - Bring a sketchpad or tablet for visual documentation of the unique structures and life forms.

11. Respect for the Environment:
    - Prepare to follow strict protocols to avoid contaminating or disturbing the delicate ecosystem and machinery.
    - Bring sample collection tools that minimize impact on the environment.

12. Mental Preparation:
    - Research meditation or mindfulness techniques to cope with the potential psychological effects of the altered environment.
    - Consider bringing a small object from home as a grounding tool in this alien environment.

13. Exit Strategy:
    - Familiarize yourself with emergency exit procedures before entering the Core.
    - Carry a small supply of emergency oxygen in case of sudden atmospheric changes.

By preparing thoroughly in these areas, a traveler can maximize their safety, comfort, and ability to appreciate and study this unique environment. Remember, flexibility and respect for this alien ecosystem are key to a successful visit to the Helix Core.
