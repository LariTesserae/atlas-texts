To prepare for visiting this extraordinary subterranean crystal ecosystem, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
- Wear a sealed, insulated suit to protect against the cold, dry air and potential sharp crystal edges.
- Include a breathable helmet with a built-in air filtration system to manage the metallic air quality.
- Ensure the suit material is resistant to potential chemical reactions with the crystalline environment.

2. Sensory Equipment:
- Bring advanced night vision goggles or low-light cameras to navigate and observe in the dim, phosphorescent lighting.
- Pack seismic sensors to detect and interpret the subtle vibrations used for communication.
- Include a spectrometer to analyze the composition of various crystal formations.

3. Time Dilation Devices:
- Carry a personal time dilation field generator to experience the glacial pace of change in real-time.
- Alternatively, bring time-lapse recording equipment to capture long-term processes.

4. Navigation Tools:
- Use 3D mapping technology to track the ever-changing layout of tunnels and caverns.
- Bring climbing gear for traversing the three-dimensional city structure.

5. Research Equipment:
- Pack non-invasive sampling tools to study the crystal life forms without disturbing them.
- Include a portable lab for on-site analysis of gathered data and samples.

6. Communication Devices:
- Bring vibration-based communication tools to attempt interaction with the crystal entities.
- Include a universal translator programmed with algorithms for decoding potential non-verbal languages.

7. Survival Gear:
- Carry a supply of nutrient-dense, long-lasting food and a water purification system.
- Include a compact shelter in case of getting trapped by shifting cavern structures.

8. Protective Measures:
- Bring a personal force field generator for protection against potential cave-ins or hostile entities.
- Include a teleportation device for emergency extraction if needed.

9. Cultural Sensitivity:
- Research and respect any observed customs or rituals of the crystal entities.
- Prepare to move and act slowly to match the timescale of the environment.

10. Mental Preparation:
- Undergo meditation training to cope with the alien nature of the environment and its inhabitants.
- Prepare for potential sensory disorientation due to the unique light, sound, and time perception in the caverns.

11. Documentation Tools:
- Bring multi-spectral cameras to capture the full range of light emissions in the caverns.
- Include holographic recording devices to document the 3D structures and lifeforms.

12. Energy Sources:
- Pack long-lasting, cold-resistant power cells for all equipment.
- Consider bringing crystal-based energy converters that might be able to harness the ambient energy of the environment.

Remember, this is an unprecedented environment that challenges our understanding of life and ecosystems. Approach with caution, curiosity, and respect. Be prepared for the unexpected and remain flexible in your methods and expectations. This journey will require not just physical preparation, but a willingness to expand one's conceptual boundaries of what constitutes life, intelligence, and civilization.
