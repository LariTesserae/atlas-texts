To prepare for visiting the Suspended City of Quorzha, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Physical Adaptation:
- Undergo hyperbaric chamber training to acclimate to the intense pressure of the deep ocean environment.
- Take motion sickness medication to combat potential disorientation from the three-dimensional layout and temporal distortions.
- Strengthen your cardiovascular system through intense aerobic exercise, as the alien environment may put additional stress on your body.

2. Protective Gear:
- Wear a specially designed pressure suit that can withstand the extreme depths while allowing flexibility for movement within the bubbles.
- Equip yourself with a helmet featuring an advanced filtration system to protect against any potentially harmful bioluminescent organisms that may penetrate the bubbles.
- Carry a personal force field generator to provide an extra layer of protection against the unpredictable physical laws at the city's edges.

3. Breathing Apparatus:
- Bring a closed-circuit rebreather system with multiple redundancies, capable of recycling your air supply for extended periods.
- Include a backup supply of compressed air tanks, as well as emergency chemical oxygen generators.

4. Communication and Navigation:
- Acquire a universal translator device, preferably one that can interpret non-verbal and potentially psychic forms of communication.
- Carry a multidimensional positioning system to help navigate the complex, three-dimensional layout of the city.
- Bring a quantum-entangled communicator to maintain contact with your home dimension, as conventional signals may not penetrate the ocean depths or bubble walls.

5. Temporal Stabilization:
- Wear a personal chronometric stabilizer to protect against the temporal distortions experienced in Quorzha.
- Carry a backup temporal anchor device to prevent being lost in time if the primary stabilizer fails.

6. Sensory Protection and Enhancement:
- Use specially designed goggles with adjustable light filtering to protect your eyes from the pulsating glow of the city and enhance your vision in the dark ocean depths.
- Wear noise-cancelling earpieces that can selectively filter out the overwhelming ambient sounds while still allowing you to hear important information.

7. Research and Cultural Preparation:
- Study any available information on the culture and customs of Quorzha's inhabitants to avoid unintentional offense or danger.
- Learn basic meditation and mindfulness techniques to help maintain mental stability in the face of reality-bending phenomena.

8. Survival Gear:
- Pack a supply of nutrient-dense, long-lasting food that won't be affected by the temporal distortions.
- Bring a water purification system capable of processing the alien water should you need to replenish your supply.
- Carry a multi-tool designed to interact with both primitive and advanced technologies you might encounter.

9. Scientific Equipment:
- Bring a quantum field analyzer to study the unique properties of the bubble walls and the city's structure.
- Pack a high-resolution holographic recorder to document your experiences and the alien life forms you encounter.

10. Emergency Extraction:
- Arrange for a dimensional extraction team to be on standby, ready to pull you out if you encounter life-threatening danger.
- Carry a personal teleportation device as a last resort, understanding that its functionality may be unpredictable in Quorzha's reality-warping environment.

11. Mental Preparation:
- Undergo intensive psychological training to prepare for the cognitive dissonance of experiencing a place where familiar laws of physics don't apply.
- Practice lucid dreaming techniques to help maintain a grip on reality in an environment where the lines between real and unreal may blur.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key when visiting a place like Quorzha. Be prepared for the unexpected, and always prioritize your safety in this alien and potentially dangerous environment.
