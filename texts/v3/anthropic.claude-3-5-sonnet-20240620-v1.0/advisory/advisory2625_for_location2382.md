To prepare for visiting this underwater city, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Specialized Breathing Apparatus:
   - Obtain a high-tech underwater breathing system that can withstand deep-sea pressures.
   - Ensure the system has long-lasting oxygen reserves or can efficiently extract oxygen from water.
   - Consider a backup system in case of primary failure.

2. Pressure Adaptation:
   - Undergo gradual decompression and recompression treatments before and after the visit.
   - Acquire a pressure-resistant suit that can adjust to varying depths.
   - Learn about the risks of decompression sickness and how to prevent it.

3. Communication Tools:
   - Learn the local light-based and gesture-based communication systems.
   - Obtain a device that can translate these signals into a familiar language.
   - Consider bringing a waterproof writing tablet for complex communications.

4. Navigation Equipment:
   - Acquire a 3D mapping device suitable for the underwater environment.
   - Learn to use sonar or echolocation tools for navigation in low visibility.

5. Thermal Protection:
   - Bring a wetsuit or drysuit that can regulate body temperature in varying water conditions.
   - Include heating elements in case of exposure to colder waters outside the city.

6. Illumination:
   - Bring multiple sources of waterproof lighting, including some that mimic bioluminescence.
   - Consider obtaining light-filtering goggles to adjust to varying light conditions.

7. Transportation:
   - Learn to use the city's tube transport system.
   - Consider obtaining a personal propulsion device for exploration outside the city limits.

8. Cultural Preparation:
   - Study the customs and etiquette of the underwater city.
   - Learn about the local flora and fauna, especially any domesticated species.
   - Understand the city's environmental regulations to avoid accidental damage.

9. Health Precautions:
   - Get vaccinations for any underwater-specific pathogens.
   - Bring a first-aid kit designed for aquatic environments.
   - Learn basic underwater first-aid techniques.

10. Data Collection:
    - Bring waterproof cameras and recording devices.
    - Obtain sensors capable of measuring water composition, pressure, and temperature.

11. Currency and Trade:
    - Research the local economy and obtain appropriate currency or trade goods.
    - Consider bringing small, waterproof valuable items for emergencies.

12. Emergency Preparedness:
    - Learn emergency protocols for situations like hull breaches or system failures.
    - Bring emergency ascent equipment in case you need to leave the city quickly.

13. Time Management:
    - Bring a waterproof timekeeping device synced to the city's internal cycles.
    - Plan your visit around the city's maintenance and operational schedules.

14. Dietary Considerations:
    - Research the local food sources and bring supplements if necessary.
    - Consider bringing water purification tools in case of incompatibility with local water.

15. Mental Preparation:
    - Practice meditation or relaxation techniques to cope with the alien environment.
    - Prepare for potential claustrophobia or isolation in the underwater setting.
