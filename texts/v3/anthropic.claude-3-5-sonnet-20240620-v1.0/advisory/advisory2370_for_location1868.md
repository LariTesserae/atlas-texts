To prepare for visiting this ancient, enigmatic location, a human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Wear a fully sealed, climate-controlled suit. The air is thick with moisture and unknown particles, potentially harmful to human respiration.
   - Ensure the suit can withstand varying gravitational forces and protect against potential radiation.
   - Include a filtration system for breathing, as the air composition is unknown and likely unsuitable for humans.

2. Sensory Equipment:
   - Bring advanced scanning and analysis tools to study the metallic sand and the structures.
   - Include devices to measure and record the various energy patterns and emissions.
   - Carry instruments to analyze the chemical composition of the air, vapor, and pools.

3. Navigation and Communication:
   - Traditional GPS may not work here. Bring alternative navigation tools, possibly based on inertial guidance systems.
   - Have a way to mark your path in the shifting sands.
   - Carry multiple forms of communication devices, as standard radio waves might behave unpredictably in this environment.

4. Time Keeping:
   - Bring reliable timekeeping devices. The absence of day/night cycles and the strange nature of this place might disorient your sense of time.

5. Energy and Sustenance:
   - Pack long-lasting power sources for all equipment.
   - Bring a substantial supply of food and water, as there are no apparent natural resources.

6. Mental Preparation:
   - Be prepared for sensory overload and potential psychological stress from the alien environment.
   - Practice meditation or other calming techniques to maintain focus in disorienting situations.

7. Scientific Equipment:
   - Bring tools for collecting samples safely.
   - Include a portable lab for on-site analysis.

8. Safety Measures:
   - Carry emergency beacon or interdimensional distress signal device.
   - Include a personal force field generator if available, to protect against unexpected phenomena.

9. Documentation Tools:
   - Bring high-resolution cameras and 3D scanning equipment to document the structures and phenomena.
   - Include audio recording devices to capture the various sounds of the environment.

10. Mobility Aid:
    - Consider a personal hover device or similar technology to navigate the shifting sands more easily.

11. Time Dilation Equipment:
    - If available, bring instruments to measure potential time dilation effects, as the nature of this place suggests possible temporal anomalies.

12. Adaptive Gear:
    - Ensure all equipment can function under varying gravitational conditions and in the presence of strange energy fields.

13. Decontamination Kit:
    - Include a thorough decontamination system for use before leaving the location to prevent carrying unknown substances or micro-machines to other realities.

Remember, flexibility and open-mindedness are key. This environment operates on principles beyond current human understanding, so be prepared to adapt your approach and expectations as you explore. Always prioritize safety and be ready to retreat if the environment proves too hostile or unpredictable.
