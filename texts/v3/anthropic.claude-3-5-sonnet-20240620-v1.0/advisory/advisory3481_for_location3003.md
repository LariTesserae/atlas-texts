To prepare for visiting Lumina Hollow, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Clothing and Gear:
- Pack moisture-wicking, quick-drying clothing to deal with the constant dampness.
- Bring sturdy, waterproof boots with excellent grip for navigating slippery surfaces.
- Include a lightweight, waterproof jacket or poncho.
- Carry a small, powerful flashlight and extra batteries, despite the bioluminescent lighting.
- Bring a waterproof bag to protect electronics and important items.

2. Health and Safety:
- Get a full medical check-up, including vaccinations for potential subterranean pathogens.
- Bring any necessary medications, plus extras in case of unexpected delays.
- Pack a first-aid kit with treatments for cuts, bruises, and respiratory issues.
- Consider bringing altitude sickness medication, as the cavern system may have varying air pressures.
- Include protective eyewear to shield from falling debris or sudden light changes during Flux events.

3. Navigation and Communication:
- Obtain a detailed map of Lumina Hollow, but be prepared for it to become outdated quickly.
- Bring a compass, although be aware it may behave erratically due to mineral deposits or Flux events.
- Carry a reliable communication device that can function underground, such as a specialized radio.
- Learn basic cave navigation techniques and how to read geological formations.

4. Adaptability Training:
- Practice quick decision-making and adaptability exercises to prepare for the constantly changing environment.
- Learn basic rock climbing and rappelling skills for navigating the city's vertical structures.
- Study the basics of seismology to better understand and predict ground movements.

5. Cultural Preparation:
- Learn about the local customs, particularly those related to adapting to sudden changes.
- Study the history and social structure of Lumina Hollow to better interact with locals.
- Practice being comfortable in confined spaces and with constant, low-level background noise.

6. Sensory Adjustment:
- Spend time in dimly lit environments before the trip to accustom your eyes to low light conditions.
- Practice identifying sounds in echo-prone environments to prepare for the cavern's acoustics.
- Consider bringing tinted glasses to adjust to the unusual lighting from bioluminescent sources.

7. Psychological Preparation:
- Mentally prepare for long periods without seeing the sky or natural sunlight.
- Practice mindfulness or meditation techniques to cope with the potential stress of Flux events.
- Be ready for a sense of disorientation due to the lack of traditional day/night cycles.

8. Scientific Equipment:
- Bring a multi-function environmental sensor to monitor air quality, pressure, and temperature.
- Include a geiger counter or similar radiation detection device, as underground environments can have higher radiation levels.
- Pack a small microscope or magnifying glass to examine the unique flora and fauna.

9. Nutrition and Hydration:
- Bring water purification tablets or a portable filter, as the safety of local water sources may be uncertain.
- Pack high-energy, non-perishable food items that can withstand the damp environment.
- Consider bringing supplements, especially Vitamin D, to compensate for the lack of sunlight.

10. Emergency Preparedness:
- Carry an emergency shelter, such as a lightweight tent or bivouac sack.
- Include a multi-day supply of emergency rations and water.
- Bring a personal locator beacon in case you become lost or trapped during a geological event.

11. Respect for the Environment:
- Learn about the delicate ecosystem of Lumina Hollow and how to minimize your impact.
- Bring biodegradable personal care products to avoid contaminating the underground water system.
- Be prepared to follow strict guidelines about interacting with the local flora and fauna.

By taking these preparations, a multiverse traveler can better ensure their safety, comfort, and ability to fully experience the unique wonders of Lumina Hollow. Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key in this ever-changing subterranean world.
