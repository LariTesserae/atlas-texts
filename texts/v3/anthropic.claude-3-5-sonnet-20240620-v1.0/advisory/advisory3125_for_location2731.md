To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Obtain a highly advanced, pressure-resistant suit capable of withstanding the immense depths and the unusual properties of the viscous sea.
   - Ensure the suit can regulate temperature and provide a stable internal environment, as the constant cool temperature may be challenging for human physiology over extended periods.
   - The suit should have a self-contained air supply, as the atmosphere (if any) is likely unbreathable.

2. Mobility:
   - Acquire a personal propulsion system designed to move efficiently through the viscous medium.
   - Consider bringing a small submersible vehicle for longer-distance travel between structures.

3. Communication:
   - Bring advanced communication devices that can function in this unique environment, possibly utilizing quantum entanglement to maintain contact with your home base or team members.
   - Develop a system to translate or interpret the light pulses and harmonic tones emitted by the structures, as they may contain valuable information.

4. Scientific Equipment:
   - Pack multi-spectral imaging devices to analyze the phase-shifting materials and energy patterns.
   - Bring quantum sensors to study the unusual physics at play in this realm.
   - Include instruments to measure and analyze the constant low-frequency hum and harmonic bursts.

5. Time Perception Aids:
   - Bring devices to help maintain a sense of time passing, as the unchanging environment may disorient human perception of time.
   - Consider bringing a virtual reality system that can simulate day-night cycles to maintain circadian rhythm.

6. Mental Preparation:
   - Undergo extensive psychological training to cope with the profound alienness and the potential sense of insignificance in this vast, ancient environment.
   - Practice meditation techniques to manage the potential mental effects of the pervasive hum and sudden harmonic bursts.

7. Cultural and Ethical Considerations:
   - Prepare protocols for non-interference with this seemingly dormant but potentially still functioning civilization's remnants.
   - Develop guidelines for ethical study and interaction with the crystalline "vegetation" and other potential life forms.

8. Energy and Sustenance:
   - Bring long-lasting power sources for all equipment, as recharging options may be limited.
   - Pack concentrated, long-shelf-life nutrition supplies, as food procurement will be impossible in this environment.

9. Emergency Preparedness:
   - Include a emergency teleportation device or interdimensional beacon for rapid evacuation if needed.
   - Bring repair kits for all equipment, especially the protective suit, as assistance or replacement parts will be unavailable.

10. Data Collection and Analysis:
    - Prepare systems for extensive data logging and real-time analysis of the environment, structures, and any observed phenomena.
    - Include AI-assisted research tools to help process and understand the vast amounts of alien information you may encounter.

11. Temporal Considerations:
    - Given the extreme time scales at play, consider bringing technology that can accelerate your perception of time to observe changes that occur over millennia.
    - Alternatively, prepare for the possibility of entering a state of suspended animation to directly observe long-term phenomena.

Remember, this environment is fundamentally alien and may operate on principles beyond current human understanding. Approach with caution, respect, and an open mind. Be prepared for the unexpected and be ready to abort the mission if unforeseen dangers arise.
