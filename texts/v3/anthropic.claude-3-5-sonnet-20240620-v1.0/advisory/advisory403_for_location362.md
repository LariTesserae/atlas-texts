To prepare for visiting the Whispering Hive, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Clothing and Gear:
- Invest in high-quality, insulated, waterproof clothing. Multiple layers are crucial.
- Wear thermal base layers, followed by insulating mid-layers, and a waterproof outer shell.
- Obtain specialized footwear with excellent traction for icy surfaces and insulation for extreme cold.
- Bring waterproof gloves, a warm hat, and a face mask or balaclava to protect against wind and cold.
- Pack goggles or sunglasses to protect eyes from snow glare and ice reflections.
- Carry a waterproof backpack to keep belongings dry in the constantly damp environment.

2. Survival Equipment:
- Bring ice climbing gear (crampons, ice axe) for navigating treacherous icy terrain.
- Pack a high-quality headlamp with extra batteries for navigating dark ice caves.
- Carry a personal locator beacon in case of getting lost in the labyrinthine passages.
- Bring a multi-tool for various purposes in this resource-scarce environment.
- Pack a first aid kit with additional supplies for treating hypothermia and frostbite.

3. Nutrition and Hydration:
- Bring high-calorie, nutrient-dense foods that don't freeze easily.
- Pack a reliable water filtration system to safely drink the omnipresent melting ice.
- Bring insulated containers to keep water from freezing and food from getting wet.

4. Adaptation and Health:
- Spend time acclimatizing to the cold and high altitude before fully exploring.
- Prepare for potential altitude sickness with appropriate medication.
- Bring moisturizer and lip balm to combat the dry, cold air.
- Consider vitamin D supplements to compensate for limited sunlight exposure.

5. Cultural Considerations:
- Learn basic ice cave etiquette and safety protocols from local guides.
- Bring small, practical gifts for locals (e.g., high-quality tools or weather-resistant materials).
- Practice speaking softly to respect the quiet atmosphere of the Hive.

6. Scientific and Observational Equipment:
- Bring a waterproof notebook and pencils for observations.
- Pack a compact, rugged camera to document the unique environment.
- Consider bringing sample collection tools for studying the unique flora and fauna.

7. Navigation:
- Obtain or create a detailed map of the Hive's current layout, understanding it may change.
- Bring a compass, though be aware it may be affected by the dense ice.
- Consider bringing string or markers to help retrace steps in the complex tunnels.

8. Mental Preparation:
- Prepare for the psychological effects of prolonged darkness and confined spaces.
- Practice meditation or bring items for mental stimulation during long, dark periods.

9. Respect for the Environment:
- Bring biodegradable soap and hygiene products to minimize environmental impact.
- Pack out all waste; bring sealable containers for this purpose.
- Be prepared to follow local guidelines for interacting with the unique ecosystem.

10. Emergency Preparedness:
- Have a clear emergency exit strategy and communicate it to local guides.
- Bring emergency shelter (e.g., a bivy sack) in case of sudden cave-ins or getting lost.
- Pack extra food and fuel in case of unexpected delays due to weather or glacier shifts.

By thoroughly preparing in these areas, a traveler can hope to safely and respectfully explore the wonders of the Whispering Hive, while minimizing risks to themselves and this fragile, extraordinary environment.
