To prepare for visiting this extraordinary nanite landscape, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Environmental Protection Suit:
Wear a fully sealed, climate-controlled suit to protect against potential interactions with the nanites. The suit should be made of a material resistant to nanoscale infiltration and equipped with its own air supply.

2. Specialized Footwear:
Use boots with adaptive soles that can adjust to the shifting nanite terrain, providing stability and preventing sinking into the metallic sand.

3. Electromagnetic Shielding:
Incorporate EM shielding into your suit to protect against potential interference from the nanites' electronic activities.

4. Air Filtration System:
Ensure your suit has a highly advanced air filtration system to prevent accidental inhalation of airborne nanites or the silvery liquid.

5. Water and Nutrient Supply:
Carry a self-contained water and nutrient supply, as consuming anything from this environment could be extremely dangerous.

6. Anti-Nanite Countermeasures:
Bring devices or substances that can neutralize or deactivate nanites in case of suit breach or accidental exposure.

7. Advanced Scanning Equipment:
Carry multi-spectral scanners to analyze the composition and behavior of the nanites, crystal formations, and liquid metal structures from a safe distance.

8. Gravity Adjusters:
Include gravity adjusters in your suit, as the nanite landscape might have unexpected gravitational effects.

9. Communication Device:
Bring a long-range, shielded communication device that can penetrate potential interference from the nanite field.

10. Energy Shield:
If possible, equip an energy shield as an additional layer of protection against unexpected nanite behavior or liquid metal formations.

11. Sample Collection Tools:
Bring specialized, sealed containers and tools for safely collecting samples without risking contamination.

12. Nanite Programming Interface:
Consider bringing a device capable of interfacing with and potentially influencing the nanites' programming, but use with extreme caution.

13. Temporal Stabilizer:
Given the advanced nature of this environment, a temporal stabilizer might be necessary to ensure you remain anchored in the current time-space.

14. Mental Preparation:
Psychologically prepare for an environment that challenges fundamental concepts of matter and life. Meditation or mental exercises might help maintain composure.

15. Emergency Extraction Device:
Carry a personal emergency extraction device in case immediate evacuation becomes necessary.

16. Holographic Recorder:
Bring a high-definition holographic recorder to document this unique environment for future study.

17. Universal Translator:
While there may not be conventional life forms, a universal translator might help interpret any potential communication from the nanite collective intelligence.

18. Time-Limiting Measures:
Set strict time limits for exposure to this environment and adhere to them rigorously to minimize unforeseen risks.

Remember, despite all precautions, visiting such an alien and technologically advanced environment carries inherent risks. Proceed with utmost caution, maintain constant vigilance, and be prepared to leave at a moment's notice if anything seems amiss.
