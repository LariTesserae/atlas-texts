To prepare for visiting the Resonance Chamber, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Miniaturization Technology: Since the chamber is microscopic, you'll need access to advanced miniaturization technology to shrink yourself to an appropriate size. Ensure this technology is reliable and includes a way to return to normal size.

2. Protective Suit: Develop a custom-made suit that can:
   - Withstand the constant vibrations without transferring harmful frequencies to your body
   - Regulate your body temperature in the cool environment
   - Provide breathable air, as the chamber's atmosphere may not be suitable for human respiration
   - Include sound-dampening technology to protect your hearing from the intense symphony of tones

3. Visual Protection: Bring specially designed goggles or a visor that can:
   - Filter and modulate the intense, ever-present light to prevent damage to your eyes
   - Adjust automatically to the constantly changing colors and refractions
   - Potentially enhance your ability to perceive patterns in the light show

4. Navigation Tools: Develop or acquire instruments that can help you navigate the dense lattice of filaments without causing damage. This might include:
   - A highly sensitive proximity sensor
   - A mapping device capable of rendering 3D models of your surroundings in real-time

5. Communication Equipment: Ensure you have a way to communicate with your team outside the chamber, as conventional radio signals may be disrupted by the unique environment.

6. Scientific Instruments: Bring tools to study and record the phenomena within the chamber, such as:
   - Spectral analyzers for both light and sound
   - High-resolution imaging equipment
   - Vibration sensors
   - Devices to measure any potential electromagnetic fields

7. Time Management: Prepare for potential time dilation effects. The chamber's unique properties might affect your perception of time, so bring reliable timekeeping devices and set strict schedules for entry and exit.

8. Mental Preparation: 
   - Practice meditation or sensory deprivation techniques to prepare for the overwhelming sensory experience
   - Study complex harmonics and light physics to better appreciate and understand what you'll encounter
   - Be prepared for potential feelings of disorientation or awe

9. Physical Training: Engage in exercises that improve your balance and fine motor control, as navigating the dense filament structure will require precision movements.

10. Non-invasive Sampling Equipment: If allowed and ethical, bring tools to take non-destructive samples of the crystalline growths for later study.

11. Emergency Extraction Plan: Develop a foolproof method to extract yourself quickly if the environment proves too overwhelming or if your equipment malfunctions.

12. Linguistic Interface: If possible, bring or develop technology that might help decode any patterns in the light and sound that could be interpreted as communication.

13. Energy Source: Ensure all your equipment has long-lasting power sources, as recharging within the chamber may be impossible.

14. Post-Visit Care: Plan for a comprehensive physical and psychological evaluation upon your return to normal size, as the experience may have unforeseen effects on your body and mind.

Remember, the key to visiting the Resonance Chamber is to observe and experience without disrupting its delicate equilibrium. Your goal should be to immerse yourself in this unique environment while leaving no trace of your presence behind.
