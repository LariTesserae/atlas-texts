To prepare for visiting the Crystalline Jungle, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Protective Gear:
- Wear a sealed, full-body suit made of cut-resistant material to protect against sharp crystal edges and fronds.
- Include a helmet with a built-in air filtration system to prevent inhalation of microscopic crystal spores.
- Use goggles or a face shield to protect eyes from crystal dust and low visibility conditions.

2. Breathing Apparatus:
- Bring a self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) with ample oxygen supply, as the air may not be breathable and is filled with potentially harmful crystal spores.

3. Illumination:
- Pack multiple sources of light, including headlamps and handheld lights, to navigate the dim environment.
- Consider bringing UV lights to potentially reveal hidden patterns or life forms.

4. Navigation Tools:
- Standard compasses may not work due to potential magnetic interference from the mechanical floor.
- Bring advanced GPS or dimensional tracking devices designed for multiverse travel.
- Include physical mapping tools like string or biodegradable markers to avoid getting lost in the maze-like environment.

5. Sound Protection:
- Wear noise-cancelling earplugs or headphones to protect against the constant mechanical cacophony.

6. Climbing Gear:
- Pack lightweight climbing equipment to navigate the uneven terrain and potentially scale crystal formations.

7. Sampling and Analysis Tools:
- Bring tools for safely collecting crystal and plant samples without damaging the environment.
- Include portable spectrometers or other analysis devices to study the unique silicon-based life forms.

8. Time-Keeping Devices:
- Bring multiple, redundant timekeeping devices to maintain a sense of time passage in this timeless environment.

9. Communication Equipment:
- Pack long-range communication devices capable of penetrating the dense crystal structures to maintain contact with the outside world or your interdimensional vessel.

10. Survival Supplies:
- Bring ample water and nutrient-dense food, as there may be no edible resources in this environment.
- Include a first-aid kit with additional supplies for treating potential crystal-related injuries.

11. Adaptive Footwear:
- Wear sturdy boots with excellent traction to navigate the constantly shifting mechanical floor.

12. Research Materials:
- Bring recording devices (audio and visual) to document observations.
- Include a journal or digital notepad for taking notes on the unique phenomena observed.

13. Protective Skincare:
- Apply a barrier cream to any exposed skin to prevent irritation from crystal dust and spores.

14. Mental Preparation:
- Practice meditation or mindfulness techniques to cope with the potentially disorienting and alien environment.
- Be prepared for sensory overload from the constant noise and visual stimuli.

15. Exit Strategy:
- Establish a clear plan for departure, including a set time limit for exploration to avoid becoming lost or trapped in this timeless realm.
- Bring emergency beacon or dimensional recall device for quick extraction if needed.

Remember, the Crystalline Jungle is an environment unlike any on Earth. Expect the unexpected and be prepared to adapt your strategies as you encounter its unique challenges. Always prioritize safety and respect for this delicate ecosystem during your exploration.
