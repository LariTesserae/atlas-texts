12. Energy Source: Pack a self-sustaining energy source for equipment, as conventional power sources might not work reliably here.

13. Sterilization Equipment: Bring a way to sterilize yourself before entering and upon leaving to prevent contamination in either direction.

14. Emergency Exit Strategy: Have a predetermined method for quick extraction if the environment proves too hostile or if unexpected dangers arise.
