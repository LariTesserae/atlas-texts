To prepare for visiting this extraordinary subterranean location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
   - Bring warm, insulated clothing to combat the frigid temperatures.
   - Pack a high-quality respirator or gas mask to filter the potentially contaminated air and protect against fungal spores.
   - Wear goggles or a full-face shield to protect eyes from the intense, pulsating light and any airborne particles.

2. Navigation and Orientation:
   - Bring multiple compasses and GPS devices, but be prepared for them to malfunction due to the unusual electromagnetic environment.
   - Carry physical maps and writing materials to document the ever-changing landscape.
   - Bring a strong, reliable light source as backup, preferably one that doesn't rely on batteries that could be affected by the strange energies.

3. Time Management:
   - Wear multiple timepieces, including mechanical watches, to track the passage of time in this temporally distorted environment.
   - Establish a strict schedule for activities and check-ins to avoid losing track of time.

4. Health and Safety:
   - Pack a comprehensive first-aid kit, including treatments for hypothermia, burns, and contamination.
   - Bring water purification tablets or devices to treat any water you might need to consume.
   - Carry emergency rations that don't require preparation, as traditional cooking might be challenging.

5. Scientific Equipment:
   - Bring devices to measure radiation, electromagnetic fields, and air quality.
   - Pack sample collection kits for gathering specimens of the bioluminescent fungi and water safely.
   - Carry a high-quality camera with extra batteries to document the environment.

6. Communication:
   - Bring multiple communication devices, including those that use different frequencies, to attempt contact with the outside world.
   - Have a predetermined check-in schedule with an external team, in case rescue becomes necessary.

7. Psychological Preparation:
   - Undergo meditation or sensory deprivation training to prepare for the disorienting effects of the environment.
   - Bring grounding objects from home to maintain a sense of reality.

8. Protective Gear:
   - Wear a full-body hazmat suit to protect against potential contaminants in the water and air.
   - Use sturdy, insulated boots with excellent traction for navigating the unpredictable terrain.

9. Research and Knowledge:
   - Study bioluminescent organisms, underground ecosystems, and theories of time distortion before the trip.
   - Bring reference materials on machinery and robotics to attempt to understand the city's workings.

10. Exit Strategy:
    - Carry climbing gear and emergency oxygen supply in case the entry point becomes inaccessible.
    - Have a clear, predetermined exit plan and stick to it, regardless of the temptation to stay and explore further.

11. Ethical Considerations:
    - Prepare a code of conduct for interacting with the environment and potential sentient beings encountered.
    - Be ready to leave no trace and avoid interfering with the ecosystem and machinery.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key in such an unpredictable environment. Be prepared to abandon plans and equipment if necessary for safety. Above all, maintain a sense of wonder and respect for this alien world, balancing scientific curiosity with caution and self-preservation.
