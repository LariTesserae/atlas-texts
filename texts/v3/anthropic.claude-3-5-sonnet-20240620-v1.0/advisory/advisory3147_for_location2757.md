To prepare for visiting this unique subterranean location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Lighting: 
- Bring multiple sources of light, including powerful headlamps, handheld flashlights, and backup batteries.
- Consider bioluminescent light sticks as a backup, to blend with the natural environment.

2. Clothing and Gear:
- Wear moisture-wicking, quick-drying clothing to handle the damp environment.
- Bring warm layers, as cave temperatures can be cool and constant.
- Wear sturdy, waterproof boots with good traction for navigating uneven, potentially slippery surfaces.
- Pack a waterproof backpack to protect supplies from moisture.

3. Navigation:
- Bring multiple compasses, as traditional GPS might not work underground.
- Carry detailed maps of known cave systems (if available) and mapping tools.
- Bring rope and climbing gear for navigating difficult terrain.

4. Protection:
- Wear a helmet to protect against low-hanging rock formations.
- Bring protective eyewear to shield eyes from potential debris or cave-dwelling insects.
- Carry a first-aid kit equipped for potential injuries like cuts, sprains, or allergic reactions.

5. Water and Food:
- Bring ample purified water, as cave water sources may not be safe to drink.
- Pack high-energy, non-perishable food supplies.
- Consider bringing water purification tablets or filters as a backup.

6. Sound Protection:
- Pack high-quality earplugs or noise-cancelling headphones to protect against the loud mechanical noises.

7. Air Quality:
- Bring a portable air quality monitor to check for potentially harmful gases.
- Consider a lightweight respirator mask if air quality is a concern.

8. Documentation:
- Bring a waterproof, shockproof camera or smartphone for documentation.
- Pack a waterproof notebook and pencils for taking notes.

9. Time Management:
- Bring multiple watches or timekeeping devices, as the perpetual twilight may disorient your sense of time.

10. Communication:
- Pack a long-range, waterproof communication device, though be aware it may not work deep underground.

11. Exploration Tools:
- Bring specimen collection tools (if allowed) for studying the unique flora and fauna.
- Pack a small mirror or periscope for peering around corners or into crevices.

12. Cultural Sensitivity:
- Research and respect any known cultural significance of the cave system.
- Be prepared to document but not disturb any remnants of the lost civilization.

13. Mental Preparation:
- Practice meditation or calming techniques to manage the potential psychological impact of prolonged darkness and loud mechanical noises.

14. Exit Strategy:
- Always inform someone of your travel plans and expected return time.
- Have a clear exit strategy and emergency plan in place.

15. Adaptability:
- Be prepared for the unexpected. The unique blend of nature and machine in this environment may present unforeseen challenges.

Remember, the goal is to observe and learn while minimizing impact on this delicate ecosystem. Approach with respect, curiosity, and caution, and be prepared to adjust your plans based on the conditions you encounter.
