To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Environmental Protection Suit:
   - A high-tech, fully sealed suit capable of withstanding immense pressure and providing breathable air is essential.
   - The suit should be equipped with a sophisticated filtration system to protect against potential toxins or unfamiliar microorganisms in the viscous liquid.
   - It must be able to regulate internal temperature and humidity, given the hot, thick air inside the bubble spheres.
   - The suit should have a self-cleaning mechanism to prevent contamination from the bioluminescent fluid.

2. Propulsion System:
   - A compact, quiet propulsion system integrated into the suit will be necessary for navigating the dense liquid environment.
   - It should allow for precise, controlled movements to avoid disturbing the delicate ecosystems.

3. Communication Equipment:
   - A device capable of transmitting and receiving signals through the viscous medium is crucial.
   - It should be able to translate or interpret the bioluminescent patterns of the native creatures.
   - A system for recording audio, especially to capture the sounds from the space-time rifts, would be valuable.

4. Advanced Scanning and Analysis Tools:
   - Multi-spectrum scanners to analyze the composition of the liquid, the bubble membranes, and the various life forms.
   - Devices to measure and record energy fluctuations, particularly around the mysterious artifacts and rifts.
   - A portable DNA sequencer for studying the rapidly evolving plant life.

5. Temporal Stabilizer:
   - Given the presence of space-time rifts, a personal temporal stabilizer would be advisable to protect against potential time dilation effects or accidental dimensional shifts.

6. Adaptive Camouflage:
   - A system that can mimic the bioluminescent patterns of local life forms to avoid drawing unwanted attention.

7. Energy Shield:
   - A personal energy shield to protect against potential hazards from the rifts or the pulsing energy conduits.

8. Nutrient Supply:
   - A compact system for recycling bodily waste and producing nutrient-dense food, as the traveler may need to spend extended periods in the suit.

9. Mental Preparation:
   - Extensive training in meditation and cognitive flexibility to cope with the reality-bending nature of the environment.
   - Psychological evaluations and counseling to prepare for the isolation and alien nature of the realm.

10. Decontamination Protocols:
    - Strict procedures for entering and exiting the environment to prevent cross-contamination between realms.

11. Non-invasive Sampling Equipment:
    - Tools for collecting samples without disturbing the delicate ecosystems, including remote-operated micro-drones.

12. Emergency Extraction Device:
    - A fail-safe device capable of instantly transporting the traveler back to their point of origin in case of emergency.

13. Interdimensional Beacon:
    - A device to mark the traveler's entry point, ensuring they can find their way back through the multiverse.

14. Cultural and Ethical Training:
    - Extensive briefing on potential intelligent life forms and proper protocols for non-interference and respect for alien ecosystems.

15. Time Dilation Compensator:
    - A device to sync the traveler's personal time with their home dimension, accounting for potential time dilation effects.

Remember, flexibility and open-mindedness are key. This environment challenges conventional understanding of physics and biology, so the traveler must be prepared to encounter the utterly unexpected and adapt accordingly.
