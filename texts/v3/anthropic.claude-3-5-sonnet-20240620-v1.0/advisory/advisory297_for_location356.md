To prepare for visiting Noctilum, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following preparations:

1. Specialized Submersible Suit:
Design and construct a high-pressure suit capable of withstanding the extreme conditions of Noctilum's aetheum. This suit should:
- Be resistant to the cold, near-freezing temperatures
- Withstand the immense pressure of the liquid environment
- Provide protection against the potentially corrosive or toxic effects of aetheum
- Include a sophisticated filtration system to extract breathable air from the aetheum
- Feature a heating system to maintain body temperature
- Incorporate powerful propulsion systems for navigation
- Have external lights for visibility in the diffuse, twilight environment

2. Communication Equipment:
Develop a communication system that can function in the dense liquid environment, possibly using sound waves or light pulses that can travel efficiently through aetheum.

3. Scientific Instruments:
Pack a range of scientific tools for analyzing the unique properties of aetheum, studying the silicon-based lifeforms, and investigating the mysterious structures. These might include:
- Spectrometers
- Chemical analyzers
- High-resolution cameras
- Sonar equipment
- Sampling devices

4. Navigation System:
Create a 3D mapping and navigation system that can function in the liquid environment, accounting for currents and the unique light properties of aetheum.

5. Power Source:
Develop a long-lasting, high-capacity power source that can operate in extreme cold and under immense pressure.

6. Medical Preparations:
- Undergo extensive decompression training to prepare for the transition between normal atmosphere and the high-pressure environment of Noctilum
- Receive vaccinations against potential silicon-based pathogens
- Prepare antidotes for potential aetheum toxicity
- Undergo genetic therapy to enhance resistance to cold and pressure

7. Sensory Adaptation:
- Train to operate in a world of perpetual twilight
- Develop methods to filter or dampen the overwhelming soundscape
- Practice operating with limited visibility and altered perception of distance due to the liquid environment

8. Emergency Equipment:
Pack emergency supplies including:
- Backup air supply
- Emergency beacons
- Repair kits for the submersible suit
- First aid supplies designed for the unique environment

9. Psychological Preparation:
- Undergo immersion therapy to prepare for the alien environment
- Practice meditation techniques to maintain calm in the overwhelming sensory landscape
- Prepare for potential isolation and the psychological effects of being in an alien liquid world

10. Cultural and Ethical Training:
- Study any available information on potential intelligent life in Noctilum
- Prepare for non-interference protocols to minimize impact on the ecosystem
- Develop contingency plans for unexpected encounters with advanced civilizations

11. Exit Strategy:
Develop a reliable method for exiting Noctilum's aetheum and returning to normal space, including decontamination procedures.

Remember, Noctilum is an environment utterly alien to human experience. Expect the unexpected and be prepared to abort the mission if unforeseen dangers arise. The key to survival will be thorough preparation, adaptability, and a healthy respect for the unknown.
