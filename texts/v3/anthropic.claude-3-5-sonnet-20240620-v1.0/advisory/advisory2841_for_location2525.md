To prepare for visiting this unique subterranean world, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Environmental Protection:
- Bring a sealed, climate-controlled suit to protect against the hot, mineral-rich air and potential toxins.
- Include an advanced air filtration system in the suit to remove any harmful particles or gases.
- Ensure the suit is resistant to extreme heat and potential chemical reactions from the mineral-rich environment.

2. Breathing Apparatus:
- Pack a long-lasting oxygen supply system, as the cavern air may not be breathable for humans.
- Include backup oxygen tanks and purification systems in case of extended stay or emergencies.

3. Navigation and Illumination:
- Bring advanced mapping technology capable of working underground and in complex 3D environments.
- Pack multiple light sources, including headlamps and portable floodlights, despite the ambient glow.
- Consider bioluminescent markers for pathfinding in case electronic systems fail.

4. Scientific Equipment:
- Pack a range of sensors to analyze the unique mineral compositions, air quality, and potential life forms.
- Bring sample collection tools designed for both geological and biological specimens.
- Include a portable microscope and analysis kit for on-site study of the alien flora and potential microorganisms.

5. Communication Devices:
- Bring communication equipment capable of penetrating thick rock layers to maintain contact with the surface or your interdimensional vessel.
- Pack signal boosters and relay devices to extend communication range in the vast cavern network.

6. Survival Gear:
- Pack a water recycling and purification system, as the environment lacks traditional water sources.
- Bring nutrient-dense, long-lasting food supplies that won't be affected by the hot, mineral-rich environment.
- Include a first-aid kit with treatments for potential mineral-based irritations or poisonings.

7. Exploration Tools:
- Bring climbing gear suitable for navigating both wide chambers and narrow passages.
- Pack tools for careful excavation and crystal extraction without damaging the environment.
- Include a collapsible hover platform for traversing large open spaces and reaching high areas.

8. Protective Measures:
- Carry non-invasive repellents or shields to deter potentially hostile native life forms.
- Bring sterilization equipment to prevent contamination of the environment or yourself.

9. Documentation Equipment:
- Pack high-resolution cameras with low-light capabilities to capture the bioluminescent life and iridescent cave structures.
- Bring audio recording devices to capture the unique sounds of crystal growth and potential life forms.

10. Cultural Sensitivity Kit:
- Include universal translation devices in case of encountering intelligent native life.
- Bring small, non-impactful gifts as potential peace offerings.
- Pack a handbook on xenoanthropology for guidance on interacting with potential primitive societies.

11. Time Perception Tools:
- Bring chronometers that can function in this timeless environment to maintain a sense of passing time.
- Include devices to measure the slow growth of crystals and plants over extended periods.

12. Emergency Escape Plan:
- Carry a personal teleportation device for emergency extraction.
- Include a set of subsurface-to-orbit beacons for pinpointing location in case of need for rescue.

Remember to approach this unique environment with respect and caution. The goal should be to observe and learn while minimizing impact on this delicate ecosystem. Be prepared for extended periods of exploration, as the vastness and complexity of the cavern system may require significant time to properly study and navigate.
