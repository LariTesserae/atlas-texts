To prepare for visiting this dynamic, evolving location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Environmental Protection:
- Wear a sealed, climate-controlled suit to protect against the harsh, changing environment.
- Include a robust air filtration system in the suit to handle the metallic, ozone-rich air and potential airborne particles.
- Ensure the suit is heat-resistant and can regulate internal temperature in the face of extreme heat.

2. Respiratory Equipment:
- Carry a supply of breathable air, as the atmosphere may not be suitable for human respiration.
- Include backup filters and oxygen tanks in case of extended stay or emergencies.

3. Eye Protection:
- Wear goggles or a face shield with adjustable tinting to protect against the harsh sunlight, dust storms, and potential chemical irritants.

4. Footwear:
- Use sturdy, waterproof boots with excellent traction to navigate the soft, changing terrain and shallow water pools.

5. Navigation Tools:
- Bring advanced GPS and mapping technology that can update in real-time to keep track of the shifting landscape.
- Include a compass, though be prepared for potential magnetic anomalies.

6. Communication Devices:
- Carry long-range communication equipment to maintain contact with a base or extraction team.
- Include a universal translator in case of encountering any intelligent lifeforms.

7. Scientific Equipment:
- Pack a multi-function environmental analyzer to test air, water, and soil composition.
- Bring specimen collection tools for safely gathering samples of the flora and fauna.

8. Medical Supplies:
- Carry a comprehensive medical kit including broad-spectrum antibiotics, antihistamines, and anti-inflammatory drugs.
- Include burn treatments and wound sealants for potential injuries from the harsh environment.

9. Water and Nutrition:
- Bring ample supply of purified water and nutrient-dense, long-lasting food.
- Include a portable water purification system as a backup.

10. Shelter:
- Carry a lightweight, heat-resistant emergency shelter in case of need for extended stay.

11. Energy Source:
- Bring solar-powered and traditional battery packs to power equipment.

12. Protective Gear:
- Include radiation detection and protection equipment, as the terraforming process may involve unknown energy sources.

13. Camouflage Equipment:
- Bring adaptive camouflage technology to blend in with the changing environment and avoid disturbing the local fauna or any potential overseers.

14. Documentation Tools:
- Pack high-resolution cameras and video equipment to record observations.
- Include waterproof and heat-resistant notebooks and writing tools for manual note-taking.

15. Temporal Stabilizer:
- If available, bring a device to anchor the traveler in their own timestream, as the rapid changes in the environment might have unpredictable effects on personal temporality.

16. Mental Preparation:
- Undergo psychological training to handle the potentially overwhelming and constantly changing environment.
- Practice mindfulness and grounding techniques to maintain focus and calm in the face of the alien and dynamic surroundings.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key in this environment. Be prepared to alter plans quickly and always prioritize safety in the face of unexpected changes in the landscape or local lifeforms.
