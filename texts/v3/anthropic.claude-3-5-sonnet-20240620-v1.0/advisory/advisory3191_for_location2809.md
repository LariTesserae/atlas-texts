To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Specialized Breathing Apparatus: 
Develop a highly advanced breathing system that can extract oxygen from the viscous, gelatinous substance. This system should be self-contained and able to operate for extended periods without resupply.

2. Pressure-Resistant Suit:
Design a flexible yet extremely durable suit that can withstand the immense pressure of the liquid environment. This suit should also regulate body temperature, as the cool temperature of the realm could lead to hypothermia over time.

3. Sensory Adaptation Equipment:
- Audio Dampeners: To protect against the deafening resonance, include highly sophisticated noise-canceling technology in the suit's helmet.
- Enhanced Vision: Equip the helmet with advanced light amplification and spectrum analysis tools to navigate in the dim, bioluminescent environment.
- Tactile Sensors: Incorporate sensitive tactile sensors into the suit's gloves to "feel" the information carried by the liquid medium.

4. Mental Preparation and Protection:
- Psychic Shielding: Develop some form of psychic or mental shielding to protect against the potential consciousness-altering effects of the environment.
- Meditation Training: Undergo extensive meditation training to maintain a strong sense of self in an environment where boundaries blur.

5. Time Dilation Countermeasures:
Equip a device that can track the passage of time relative to the traveler's home dimension, as time moves differently in this realm.

6. Communication Devices:
Design communication equipment that can transmit through the dense, sound-efficient medium without being overwhelmed by the constant resonance.

7. Propulsion System:
Develop a quiet, efficient propulsion system that can move through the viscous liquid without disturbing the environment too much.

8. Sample Collection Tools:
Bring specialized tools for collecting samples of the bioluminescent vegetation, the liquid medium, and any safe-to-acquire artifacts.

9. Energy Source:
Ensure all equipment is powered by a long-lasting, self-sustaining energy source that won't be affected by the unique properties of this realm.

10. Emergency Extraction Device:
Carry a fail-safe device that can create a portal back to the home dimension in case of emergencies.

11. Linguistic Interface:
Develop an AI-powered interface that can attempt to decipher and translate the "language" of the environment's resonances and bioluminescent patterns.

12. Reality Anchor:
Carry a personal item of great significance to serve as a psychological anchor to the traveler's home reality.

13. Temporal Shielding:
If possible, incorporate some form of temporal shielding into the suit to protect against the effects of existing in a place where time flows differently.

14. Ethical Guidelines:
Establish strict ethical guidelines for interaction with this seemingly sentient environment to minimize any potential harm or disruption.

15. Post-Expedition Care:
Arrange for extensive physical and psychological evaluations and potential treatments upon return, as exposure to such an alien environment may have unforeseen effects.

Remember, despite all these preparations, the traveler must be prepared for the unexpected. This realm defies many known laws of physics and biology, and no amount of preparation can fully account for all possibilities. Approach with caution, respect, and an open mind.
