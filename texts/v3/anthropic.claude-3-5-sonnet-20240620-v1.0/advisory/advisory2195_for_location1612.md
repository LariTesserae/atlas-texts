To prepare for visiting this extraordinary crystalline realm, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Atmosphere and Breathing:
- Bring a high-quality oxygen supply system, as the air is thin and likely lacks sufficient oxygen for human needs.
- Pack a protective mask or respirator to filter out any potentially harmful metallic particles in the air.

2. Temperature Control:
- Invest in advanced cold-weather gear, including thermal underwear, insulated outerwear, and heated gloves and boots.
- Bring chemical heat packs for emergency warmth.

3. Protective Eyewear:
- Obtain specially designed goggles or a full-face shield to protect against the intense, prismatic light reflections.
- Ensure the eyewear has adjustable tinting to adapt to changing light conditions.

4. Footwear:
- Wear sturdy, insulated boots with excellent traction to navigate the unusual, spongy ground material.
- Consider bringing crampons or micro-spikes for additional grip if needed.

5. Environmental Suit:
- If possible, wear a fully enclosed environmental suit to protect against any unforeseen interactions with the crystalline structures or airborne particles.

6. Navigation and Communication:
- Bring multiple compasses and GPS devices, as the crystalline structures may interfere with normal navigation tools.
- Pack a reliable long-range communication device for emergencies.

7. Research and Observation Tools:
- Carry a high-resolution camera with various lenses to document the unique formations.
- Bring a portable spectrometer to analyze the composition of the crystals and substrate.
- Pack a small drone for aerial observations of the forest canopy.

8. Energy and Power:
- Bring multiple power sources for equipment, as standard batteries may be affected by the unusual environment.
- Consider solar chargers, though their efficiency may be impacted by the filtered light.

9. Medical Preparations:
- Pack a comprehensive first-aid kit, including treatments for hypothermia and altitude sickness.
- Bring antihistamines and anti-inflammatory medications in case of allergic reactions to the environment.

10. Time Management:
- Wear a reliable, shockproof watch or time-keeping device, as the alien day/night cycle may be disorienting.

11. Sample Collection:
- Bring sterile collection tools and containers for safely gathering samples without disturbing the ecosystem.

12. Mental Preparation:
- Practice meditation or mindfulness techniques to cope with the alien and potentially psychologically challenging environment.

13. Sustenance:
- Pack high-energy, nutrient-dense foods that don't require preparation.
- Bring a water purification system, though water sources may be non-existent.

14. Ethical Considerations:
- Research and adhere to any known protocols for interacting with alien ecosystems to minimize impact.

15. Return Preparation:
- Set up a clear exit strategy and timeline for the visit.
- Establish decontamination procedures for returning to your home universe.

Remember, flexibility and caution are key when exploring such an alien environment. Be prepared to abort the mission if any unforeseen dangers arise, and always prioritize safety over exploration.
