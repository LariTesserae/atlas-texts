- Bring a waterproof map and compass, as electronic devices may struggle in the humid environment.

- Research the local customs and etiquette of the settlement before arriving.
- Bring small, practical gifts that might be useful to the locals, such as waterproof matches or simple tools.

5. Accommodation:
