To prepare for a visit to Hydropolis, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Waterproof gear: Invest in high-quality, waterproof clothing, boots, and bags. Everything you bring will be exposed to constant moisture and occasional downpours.

2. Breathing apparatus: While the city has atmospheric processors, the air is thick with humidity and potentially contaminated with spores or industrial pollutants. Bring a reliable respirator or breathing mask with replaceable filters.

3. Anti-fungal treatments: Given the prevalence of fungi, pack antifungal creams, sprays, and oral medications to prevent infections.

4. Water purification: Despite the abundance of water, it may not be safe to drink. Bring water purification tablets or a portable filtration system.

5. Adaptive footwear: Shoes with excellent grip for slippery surfaces and quick-drying materials are essential. Consider bringing shoes that can transition between walking and swimming.

6. Light sources: While bioluminescence provides some illumination, bring multiple waterproof light sources (headlamps, lanterns, glow sticks) with extra batteries.

7. Navigation tools: Standard GPS might not work underground. Bring a compass, altimeter, and perhaps sonar-based mapping technology if available.

8. Communication devices: Regular phones won't work here. Research and obtain communication devices that function in subterranean, high-humidity environments.

9. Thermal regulation: Despite the warmth, prolonged exposure to moisture can lead to hypothermia. Pack moisture-wicking thermal layers and waterproof heating packs.

10. First aid kit: Include treatments for water-borne illnesses, fungal infections, and injuries from falls or cuts on metal structures.

11. Adaptive eyewear: Goggles that can transition between misty air and underwater use will be invaluable.

12. Portable dehumidifier: A small, battery-powered dehumidifier can provide comfort in living quarters.

13. Bio-hazard protection: Given the unique ecosystem, bring a lightweight biohazard suit for exploring unfamiliar areas.

14. Currency or trade goods: Research the local economy. Waterproof, fungus-resistant materials or advanced water filtration technology might be valuable for trade.

15. Language aids: If universal translators aren't available, learn key phrases in local languages, including those of non-human inhabitants.

16. Adaptive food storage: Bring containers that can prevent food from becoming waterlogged or infested with local microorganisms.

17. Mental preparation: Be ready for an environment that constantly changes. Practice mindfulness techniques to stay grounded in a surreal setting.

18. Physical conditioning: Improve swimming skills and practice moving in three-dimensional spaces to navigate the city's unique layout.

19. Immunizations: Consult a multiverse travel doctor for vaccinations against potential local pathogens.

20. Cultural briefing: Study local customs, especially regarding interaction with non-human or AI inhabitants, to avoid misunderstandings.

Remember, flexibility is key in Hydropolis. Be prepared to adapt your plans and equipment as you encounter the city's unique challenges and opportunities.
