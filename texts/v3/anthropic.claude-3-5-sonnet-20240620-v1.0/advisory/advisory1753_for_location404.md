- High-quality UV-blocking sunglasses to protect against intense sunlight.

- Warm, layered clothing for temperature fluctuations, including a waterproof outer layer.

3. Breathing Assistance:
- Portable oxygen canister for emergency use in case of altitude sickness.
- Respirator mask to filter the thin air and any airborne particles.

- High-capacity water containers and water purification methods (filters, tablets).

- GPS device with extra batteries or solar charger.

9. First Aid and Emergency Supplies:
- Comprehensive first aid kit including treatments for altitude sickness, hypothermia, and sunburn.

12. Mental Preparation:

- Research and visualize the environment to mentally acclimate to its otherworldly nature.

13. Time Management:
