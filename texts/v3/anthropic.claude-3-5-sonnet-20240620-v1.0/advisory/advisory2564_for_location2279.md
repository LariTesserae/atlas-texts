To prepare for a visit to Umbra Altus, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Vision Enhancement: 
- Bring night vision goggles or special low-light eyewear to navigate the perpetual darkness.
- Pack a high-quality, waterproof flashlight with extra batteries.
- Consider bioluminescent markers or glow sticks for emergency navigation.

2. Clothing:
- Waterproof and moisture-wicking clothing is essential. Multiple layers are recommended.
- Bring a good waterproof jacket, pants, and boots.
- Pack thermal undergarments to combat the cool, damp air.
- Include gloves for protection against wet surfaces and potential industrial hazards.

3. Health Precautions:
- Get vaccinations for potential water-borne illnesses.
- Bring vitamin D supplements to compensate for the lack of sunlight.
- Pack anti-fungal cream and powder to prevent moisture-related skin issues.
- Include a small dehumidifier for your lodgings to combat the constant dampness.

4. Navigation Tools:
- Obtain a detailed, waterproof map of Umbra Altus.
- Bring a compass, as GPS might not function in this realm.
- Consider an echo-location device to navigate like the locals.

5. Communication:
- Learn basic sign language or non-verbal communication methods used by inhabitants.
- Bring a waterproof notebook and pen for taking notes or communicating through writing.

6. Currency and Trade:
- Research the local currency or trade system. Bring tradeable goods if necessary.
- Waterproof your wallet or money pouch.

7. Accommodation:
- Book lodgings in advance, preferably in upper levels to avoid ground-level moisture.
- Bring a personal water filtration system for drinking water.

8. Cultural Preparation:
- Study the customs and etiquette of Umbra Altus to avoid offending locals.
- Learn about the city's history and industrial significance.

9. Safety Measures:
- Bring rope and carabiners for navigating the vertical urban landscape.
- Pack a personal air quality monitor for areas with heavy industrial emissions.
- Include a small first aid kit with treatments for cuts, burns, and respiratory issues.

10. Documentation:
- Bring a waterproof camera to document your journey.
- Pack a moisture-resistant journal for recording your experiences.

11. Sensory Adaptation:
- Consider noise-cancelling earbuds to manage the constant industrial and water sounds.
- Bring aromatherapy oils to combat potential industrial odors.

12. Misc. Essentials:
- Pack high-energy, non-perishable food items.
- Bring a reusable water bottle with a built-in filter.
- Include a small umbrella or rain poncho for additional protection.

Remember, flexibility and adaptability are key when visiting such a unique location. Be prepared to adjust your plans and expectations as you experience this extraordinary city of eternal night.
