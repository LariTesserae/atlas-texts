To prepare for visiting the Whisper Grove, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and preparations:

1. Sensory Protection:
   - Bring high-quality noise-cancelling headphones or earplugs to manage the overwhelming plant-speak.
   - Pack a blindfold or light-blocking goggles to help adjust to the disorienting bioluminescence and shifting shadows.
   - Consider bringing a white noise generator to create a personal sound bubble.

2. Navigation Tools:
   - Obtain a quantum-entangled compass that can maintain orientation despite the shifting geography.
   - Bring a reality anchor device to help maintain a connection to your home universe's physics.
   - Pack a multidimensional mapping device to track your position in the ever-changing environment.

3. Protective Gear:
   - Wear a sealed, breathable environmental suit to protect against the moisture-rich air and potential contact with unknown plant substances.
   - Use anti-hallucination goggles to help differentiate between real and perceived threats.
   - Bring gravity boots to maintain stability on the undulating floor.

4. Life Support:
   - Carry a self-contained air supply, as the atmosphere may not be suitable for human respiration.
   - Pack nutrient-dense, sealed food supplies that won't be affected by the unique environment.
   - Bring a water purification system capable of extracting moisture from the air, similar to the native plants.

5. Communication:
   - Use a quantum entanglement communicator to maintain contact with your home base, as conventional signals may not function in this realm.
   - Bring a universal translator with plant language capabilities to attempt communication with the flora.

6. Scientific Equipment:
   - Pack a multi-spectral analyzer to study the bioluminescent properties of the plants and fauna.
   - Bring a temporal flux meter to monitor any time distortions you may experience.
   - Carry a portable quantum scanner to analyze the physics-defying artifacts.

7. Mental Preparation:
   - Undergo extensive meditation training to maintain mental clarity in the face of sensory overload.
   - Practice lucid dreaming techniques to help navigate potential reality distortions.
   - Study theoretical physics and botany to better understand and appreciate the unique ecosystem.

8. Emergency Gear:
   - Carry a personal reality bubble generator for temporary shelter from the grove's effects.
   - Bring a multiversal beacon for emergency extraction if needed.
   - Pack a first-aid kit tailored for potential interdimensional maladies.

9. Clothing:
   - Wear lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing under your protective suit.
   - Use bioluminescent-resistant fabrics to avoid attracting unwanted attention from the local fauna.

10. Time Management:
    - Bring a chronometer synced to your home universe to track subjective vs. objective time passage.
    - Set a strict exploration schedule with regular reality checks to avoid becoming lost in time distortions.

11. Documentation:
    - Use a reality-stabilized camera to capture accurate images of the ever-changing environment.
    - Bring a thought-to-text journal for quick note-taking in the challenging conditions.

12. Ethical Considerations:
    - Prepare a non-invasive sampling kit to study the environment without disturbing it.
    - Review interdimensional conservation guidelines to ensure responsible exploration.

Remember, the Whisper Grove is an unpredictable and potentially dangerous environment. Even with thorough preparation, maintain a high level of caution and be prepared to abort the mission if the conditions become too hazardous. The key to a successful visit is adaptability, respect for the alien ecosystem, and a strong grounding in your own reality.
