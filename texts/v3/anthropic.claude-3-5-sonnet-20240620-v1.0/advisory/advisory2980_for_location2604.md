To prepare for visiting this extraordinary location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take the following steps:

1. Physical Preparation:
- Undergo a rigorous physical fitness regimen to improve strength, flexibility, and endurance. The uneven, jagged terrain will require agility and sure-footing.
- Practice rock climbing and spelunking techniques to navigate the challenging cavern environment.
- Train in swimming and diving, as there may be encounters with unusual water formations.

2. Equipment:
- Wear a highly durable, moisture-wicking, and temperature-regulating suit to protect against the warm, damp environment and sharp rock formations.
- Bring a reliable, waterproof headlamp with multiple backup power sources, as well as additional light sources that won't interfere with the bioluminescent ambiance.
- Pack a multi-function tool kit for various environmental challenges.
- Carry a sturdy, extendable walking stick or trekking poles for balance and support.
- Bring a high-quality air filtration mask to protect against potentially harmful mineral vapors or spores.

3. Scientific Instruments:
- Equip yourself with advanced sensors to monitor air composition, radiation levels, and gravitational fluctuations.
- Carry a multi-spectrum camera to capture the unique light phenomena and bioluminescent patterns.
- Bring sample collection tools and containers for gathering specimens of crystals, water, and fungi (if permitted).

4. Survival Gear:
- Pack a comprehensive first-aid kit, including treatments for cuts, burns, and potential toxic exposures.
- Bring ample supply of purified water and high-energy, non-perishable food rations.
- Carry emergency shelter materials that are lightweight and adaptable to the cavern environment.

5. Communication and Navigation:
- Obtain a multi-dimensional positioning system capable of functioning in areas with distorted physics.
- Bring long-range communication devices designed to operate in environments with unusual electromagnetic properties.

6. Mental and Psychological Preparation:
- Practice meditation and mindfulness techniques to maintain mental clarity in a reality-bending environment.
- Undergo sensory deprivation training to prepare for potential time distortions and altered perceptions.
- Study theoretical physics and geology to better understand and contextualize the phenomena you may encounter.

7. Cultural and Ethical Considerations:
- Research and respect any known protocols for interacting with alien artifacts or structures.
- Prepare to document your experiences without disturbing the environment.
- Be ready to abort the mission if you encounter any signs of sentient life or active alien presence.

8. Contingency Planning:
- Establish clear emergency extraction protocols with your multiverse travel team.
- Set up predetermined check-in times, accounting for potential time distortions.
- Have a backup plan for navigation and exit in case of equipment failure or environmental changes.

9. Post-Visit Procedures:
- Arrange for a thorough decontamination process upon return.
- Schedule a comprehensive physical and psychological evaluation to detect any lingering effects from the visit.
- Prepare to participate in extensive debriefing sessions to share your observations and experiences.

Remember, flexibility and open-mindedness are key. This environment defies conventional understanding, so be prepared to adapt your strategies as you encounter the unknown. Always prioritize safety and preservation of both yourself and this unique environment.
