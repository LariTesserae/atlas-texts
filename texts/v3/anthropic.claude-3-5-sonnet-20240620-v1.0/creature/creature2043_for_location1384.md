In the Undercity of Echoes, no single entity can claim dominion. Instead, a collective consciousness permeates the very stone, a hive mind born from the fusion of countless beings over millennia. This consciousness, which we might call the Lithic Gestalt, is the true inhabitant of this realm.

The Lithic Gestalt is not a singular being, but a symbiosis of organic and inorganic elements. At its core are the remnants of the city's original builders, their minds long ago surrendered to the living stone. These ancestral intelligences form the foundation of the Gestalt's vast memory, preserving knowledge and experiences from ages past.

Intertwined with these ancient minds are the fungal networks that illuminate the city. These are not mere plants, but a highly evolved form of mycelial intelligence. They act as the nervous system of the Undercity, transmitting information and sensory input across its vast expanse. The pulsing bioluminescence is, in fact, a visual manifestation of the Gestalt's thoughts and processes.

The flowing stone itself is an integral part of the collective. Imbued with a form of mineral sentience, it serves as both the body and the ever-changing canvas upon which the Gestalt expresses itself. The shifting corridors and transforming chambers are not random, but deliberate actions, the city constantly remodeling itself in response to the Gestalt's needs and whims.

Water, omnipresent in the Undercity, acts as the lifeblood of this vast organism. It carries microscopic organisms - part mineral, part fungus, part something else entirely - that serve as mobile units of the Gestalt's consciousness. These tiny beings flow through the waterways, maintaining and repairing the city, and carrying out the will of the collective.

The mechanical devices scattered throughout the Undercity are not relics, but active components of the Gestalt's cognitive processes. They function as external memory storage, computational aids, and sensory organs, allowing the collective to perceive and interact with aspects of reality beyond its innate capabilities.

Communication within the Gestalt occurs on multiple levels simultaneously. The echoing sounds heard throughout the city are but one aspect - a audible manifestation of the constant exchange of information. Vibrations in the stone, chemical signals in the water, and even subtle gravitational fluctuations all form part of its internal dialogue.

While the Lithic Gestalt is the primary inhabitant, it occasionally permits the presence of other entities. Explorers or seekers of knowledge may be allowed to traverse the Undercity, their experiences and memories absorbed and integrated into the collective. Some may even choose to join the Gestalt permanently, their individual consciousness merging with the greater whole.

The Gestalt's perception of time and space is vastly different from human understanding. It experiences past, present, and potential futures simultaneously, which contributes to the city's seemingly chaotic transformations. What might appear as random flux to an outside observer is, in fact, the physical manifestation of the Gestalt's multidimensional thought processes.

Despite its vast intelligence, the Lithic Gestalt is not omniscient or omnipotent. It is driven by an insatiable curiosity, constantly seeking to expand its understanding of itself and the universe. The Undercity of Echoes is both its home and its laboratory, a place of perpetual experimentation and evolution.

In essence, to speak of who or what lives in the Undercity of Echoes is to speak of the city itself - a vast, living entity of stone, fungus, water, and collective memory, forever changing, forever seeking, forever echoing with the whispers of countless minds united in eternal contemplation.
