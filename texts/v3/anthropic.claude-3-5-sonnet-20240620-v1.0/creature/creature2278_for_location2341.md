In the Whispering Hollow, a race of beings known as the Echotides have made their home. These entities are as fluid and changeable as their environment, existing in a state of constant flux between physical form and pure energy.

The Echotides appear to human eyes as shimmering, translucent figures that pulse with bioluminescent light. Their bodies are vaguely humanoid, but their features shift and flow like water, never settling into a fixed form. At times, they seem to dissolve entirely into mist, only to coalesce again moments later in a different shape.

These beings communicate through a complex language of vibrations, light patterns, and thought transference. Their "voices" contribute to the ever-present hum of the Hollow, modulating the background noise into intricate symphonies of information.

The Echotides have a symbiotic relationship with their environment. They tend to the strange flora, guiding its growth and harvesting its energy. In return, the plants provide them with sustenance and shelter. The beings can merge with the vegetation, becoming one with the crystalline ferns or pulsating vines for periods of rest or contemplation.

Their society is as fluid as their forms, with no fixed hierarchy. Leadership and roles shift based on need and the current state of the Hollow. They possess a collective consciousness that allows for instant communication and decision-making across their entire population.

The Echotides are the caretakers of the ancient structures, their touch able to reshape and repair the bizarre architecture. They move through impossible geometries with ease, their fluid nature allowing them to navigate spaces that would confound solid beings.

Time holds little meaning for the Echotides. They experience past, present, and future simultaneously, which allows them to anticipate and adapt to the Hollow's frequent reality shifts. This temporal fluidity is reflected in their life cycle - they do not age or die in any conventional sense, but rather cycle through periods of greater and lesser cohesion.

The Echotides are deeply curious about the outside world, but rarely leave the Hollow. Instead, they collect and study any visitors or objects that find their way into their realm. They are not hostile, but their alien nature and the extreme conditions of the Hollow make prolonged interaction with outsiders challenging.

Perhaps most intriguingly, the Echotides seem to be able to manipulate the thin reality of the Whispering Hollow. They can create localized areas of altered physics, generate matter from energy, or even open temporary rifts to other dimensions. Whether they created the Hollow or merely adapted to it remains a mystery, even to them.

In essence, the Echotides are as much a part of the Whispering Hollow as its strange plants or twisting structures. They are the living embodiment of this pocket universe's fluid, ever-changing nature - enigmatic guardians of a place where the rules of reality have worn gossamer-thin.
