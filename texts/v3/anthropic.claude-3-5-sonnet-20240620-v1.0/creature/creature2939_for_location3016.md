The inhabitants of Echopolis are as diverse and complex as the city they call home, a vibrant tapestry of life adapted to the unique demands of their subterranean world.

The Echofolk, as they call themselves, are broadly divided into three main groups, each with their own distinct characteristics and roles within the city's ecosystem.

The Luminaries are those who dwell in the upper levels of the city, closest to the bioluminescent fungi that carpet the cavern ceiling. Generations of exposure to this soft, ever-present glow has given them extremely sensitive eyes, larger than those of surface dwellers, with irises that shift color like mood rings, reflecting their emotional states. Their skin is pale and slightly translucent, with a faint bioluminescence of their own that intensifies with strong emotion or exertion. The Luminaries are often the city's visionaries and artists, their heightened perception allowing them to see patterns and possibilities others might miss.

The Resonators inhabit the middle levels of Echopolis, where the cacophony of machinery is at its most intense. They have developed an extraordinary sense of hearing and the ability to process complex soundscapes. Their ears are larger and more articulated than those of surface humans, able to swivel independently to locate sound sources. Many Resonators have small, crystalline growths on their temples that vibrate in sympathy with the city's ambient noise, allowing them to "feel" sound waves. They are the engineers and musicians of Echopolis, experts at maintaining the delicate acoustic balance of the city and composing intricate symphonies that utilize the cavern's natural acoustics.

The Deepdwellers live in the misty lower levels, where they've adapted to the constant damp and limited visibility. Their skin is mottled and slightly rubbery, resistant to moisture and fungal growth. They have developed a form of echolocation, emitting low-frequency sounds from specially evolved vocal cords and interpreting the returning echoes via highly sensitive patches of skin on their faces and hands. Many Deepdwellers sport symbiotic colonies of bioluminescent bacteria that help them navigate the darkest reaches of the city. They are often the city's explorers and maintenance workers, venturing into unmapped tunnels and tending to the vast root systems of Echopolis's machinery.

Across all levels, many Echofolk augment their bodies with steam-powered prosthetics. These range from practical enhancements like telescoping limbs for reaching high places, to more esoteric additions like personal steam organs that allow the user to harmonize with the city's ambient sounds. Wearable gas lamps are common, often beautifully crafted to resemble bioluminescent plants or animals.

The social structure of Echopolis is as layered as its physical architecture. Leadership is shared between a council of elders known as the Harmonic Collective, with representatives from each level of the city. Decisions are made through a process of "acoustic consensus," where debates are held in specially designed chambers that amplify or dampen sound based on the collective emotional resonance of the participants.

Education in Echopolis is a lifelong pursuit, with knowledge passed down through intricate oral traditions and stored in vast libraries of audio recordings. Children learn not just academic subjects, but also the vital skills of navigating the city's complex three-dimensional space and interpreting its myriad sounds and signals.

Despite their differences, all Echofolk share a deep connection to their city and the cavern that houses it. They see themselves not as separate from their environment, but as an integral part of a larger, living system. This perspective informs every aspect of their culture, from their sustainable industrial practices to their spiritual beliefs, which often personify the cavern and the Great Resonator as nurturing, godlike entities.

The Echofolk's language is as unique as they are, a complex system of vocalizations, gestures, and light signals that can convey nuanced meanings impossible to translate into surface languages. To outsiders, an Echofolk conversation might seem like a bizarre dance accompanied by an atonal song, but to the inhabitants of Echopolis, it's as natural as breathing the cool, misty air of their subterranean home.
