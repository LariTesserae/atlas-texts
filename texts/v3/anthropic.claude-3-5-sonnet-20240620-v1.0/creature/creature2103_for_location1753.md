In the Woven City, a remarkable and enigmatic people have evolved in harmony with their intricately crafted environment. These inhabitants, known as the Weavers, are as much a part of the city as the woven fibers that surround them.

The Weavers are slender and lithe, their bodies adapted to navigate the narrow, twisting corridors of their urban tapestry. Their skin has a faint, textured quality, almost like fine linen, in shades that range from pale wheat to rich mahogany. This unique dermis allows them to blend seamlessly with their surroundings, a form of camouflage that speaks to a long history of adaptation.

Their most striking feature is their hands. Weavers possess long, dexterous fingers with an extra joint, giving them unparalleled precision in their weaving work. Their fingertips are slightly flattened and have a slightly rough texture, perfect for manipulating fibers. It's said that a master Weaver can feel the health and tension of a strand just by touching it.

Weaver eyes are large and sensitive, adapted to the filtered, ever-changing light of their environment. Their irises shift color subtly throughout the day, mirroring the ambient light. This gives them an otherworldly appearance, their gaze seeming to hold the very essence of their woven world.

Hair is a point of pride and practicality for Weavers. They grow it long and often incorporate it into their weaving, literally becoming one with the city. Elaborate braids and knots serve both as personal expression and as a way to carry tools and small items.

Weavers communicate primarily through a complex language of gestures and touch. Their vocal cords have atrophied over generations, adapted to their quiet environment. When they do speak, their voices are soft and melodic, almost indistinguishable from the creaking and rustling of the city itself.

The society of the Weavers is as intricately structured as their city. They organize themselves into guilds based on their particular weaving skills - water weavers maintain the hydraulic systems, light weavers craft the translucent ceiling panels, and so on. Knowledge is passed down through apprenticeships, with the most skilled artisans holding positions of respect and influence.

Weaver culture revolves around the concept of interconnectedness. They view their city as a living entity and themselves as both its caretakers and its children. Their philosophy emphasizes patience, adaptability, and the beauty of slow, continuous change.

Reproduction among Weavers is a communal affair. During certain celestial alignments, the entire city participates in a days-long ceremony of weaving and dance. Nine months later, infants are born with birthmarks that resemble intricate knot patterns, said to foretell their future role in the city.

The Weavers' relationship with time is unique. Their lifespan is extraordinarily long, with some individuals living for several centuries. However, their perception of time is fluid, aligned more with the gradual changes in their environment than with rigid calendars.

While generally peaceful, Weavers are not defenseless. In times of rare external threats, they can manipulate the city itself for protection, closing off sections or creating maze-like diversions that confuse and disorient outsiders.

The Weavers are a people who have become one with their creation, their bodies and culture inextricably linked to the endless tapestry of the Woven City. They are the embodiment of patience, creativity, and harmony with one's environment - a living testament to the power of adaptation and the beauty of slow, purposeful growth.
