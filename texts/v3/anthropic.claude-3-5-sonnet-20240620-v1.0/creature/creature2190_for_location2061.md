The inhabitants of Misthaven are as diverse and enigmatic as the city itself, a tapestry of beings woven from the mist and stone of their mountainous home.

The most numerous are the Mistborn, humans who have adapted to life in this vertical realm over countless generations. Their skin has a faint, silvery sheen, and their eyes are large and sensitive, perfectly suited to pierce the ever-present haze. The Mistborn move with uncanny grace, scaling sheer walls and traversing narrow ledges with ease. They are a communal people, working together to maintain the delicate balance of their city.

Interspersed among the Mistborn are the Lithlings, sentient creatures of living stone. These beings emerge from the mountain itself, their bodies a patchwork of crystals, rough granite, and smooth river stones. They grow slowly over centuries, accreting new layers and changing form as they age. The eldest Lithlings are indistinguishable from buildings, serving as living foundations for parts of the city.

The Mistweave are ethereal entities that dance on the border between corporeal and incorporeal. They appear as shimmering, humanoid forms composed of mist and light, able to drift through solid objects at will. The Mistweave act as guardians and guides, their whispered words carrying ancient wisdom and prophecies.

High in the upper tiers dwell the Aerials, a race of winged humanoids. Their bodies are light and hollow-boned, covered in iridescent feathers that shimmer in the diffuse light. The Aerials tend to the highest gardens and serve as messengers between Misthaven and the outside world.

In the deep pools and rushing streams live the Undines, aquatic beings with translucent skin and webbed appendages. They regulate the flow of water through the city, ensuring that every level receives its share. The Undines are also keepers of water lore, reading the future in the patterns of ripples and falls.

The Verdant Ones are plant-like entities that blur the line between flora and fauna. They root themselves in one spot for years at a time, growing leaves, flowers, and fruit that sustain the city. When they choose to move, they uproot themselves, walking on vine-like legs to find a new home.

Lurking in the shadows are the Umbral, creatures of darkness and secrets. They appear as living shadows with gleaming eyes, slipping through the narrowest cracks and darkest corners of the city. The Umbral are collectors of whispers and keepers of hidden knowledge.

The Resonants are beings of pure sound, their bodies visible only as shimmering distortions in the air. They harmonize with the city's constant symphony of water and wind, using their voices to shape stone and mist alike.

Overseeing it all are the Misthavens, ancient entities as old as the mountain itself. They rarely take physical form, instead manifesting as patterns in the mist or as voices in the wind. The Misthavens are the true rulers of the city, their will expressed through the ever-changing landscape and the collective dreams of the inhabitants.

These diverse beings coexist in a complex ecosystem, each playing a vital role in the life of the city. They come together in the great mist-shrouded plazas to trade, celebrate, and make decisions that affect all. In Misthaven, cooperation is not just beneficial—it's essential for survival in this precarious, wondrous realm where the boundary between the mundane and the magical is as thin as the omnipresent mist.
