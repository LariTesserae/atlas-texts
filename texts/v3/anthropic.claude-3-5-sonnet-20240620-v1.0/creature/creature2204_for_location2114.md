In the Arboreal Metropolis, a unique form of sentient plant-life has evolved to become the dominant inhabitants. These beings, known as the Chlorophyllians, are a fusion of plant and machine, with bodies that blur the line between organic matter and advanced biotechnology.

The Chlorophyllians stand roughly humanoid in shape, ranging from 5 to 7 feet tall. Their bodies are composed of a dense, fibrous material that resembles wood but is far more flexible. Their "skin" is a mosaic of leaves, bark, and smooth, photosynthetic surfaces that shimmer with an iridescent green hue. Thin, silvery veins visible beneath their surface mirror the water transportation system of their city.

Their faces are eerily smooth, with no discernible features except for large, almond-shaped eyes that glow with bioluminescence. These eyes change color based on their emotional state or the information they're processing. In place of a mouth, they have a small opening that can extend into a proboscis-like tube for absorbing nutrients and water.

The Chlorophyllians' limbs are highly articulated, able to extend, contract, and even separate into vine-like tendrils for intricate work or to interface with the city's organic technology. Their "hands" can transform into various tools – from delicate manipulators to robust cutting implements.

Instead of hair, they possess a crown of antenna-like structures that constantly move and adjust, serving as sensory organs. These antennae can detect changes in light, air composition, and even electromagnetic fields, allowing the Chlorophyllians to communicate wirelessly with their surroundings and each other.

The beings are genderless and reproduce through a complex process of spore release and genetic recombination within specialized chambers in the city-trees. Young Chlorophyllians emerge fully formed but small, growing rapidly as they absorb knowledge and nutrients from their environment.

Chlorophyllians don't speak in the traditional sense. Instead, they communicate through a combination of light patterns emitted from their eyes and bodies, pheromone releases, and low-frequency vibrations sent through the root network of their city. This multi-sensory language allows for rich, nuanced communication that can be both private and city-wide.

Their society is highly organized yet fluid, with roles and responsibilities shifting as needed to maintain the health and growth of their living city. Some Chlorophyllians specialize in maintaining the city's infrastructure, their bodies adapting to become more tree-like as they integrate with the larger plants. Others focus on scientific pursuits, their forms becoming more intricate and machine-like as they merge with the city's technology.

The Chlorophyllians possess a collective consciousness, able to share thoughts and experiences through their connection to the city's root network. However, they maintain individual identities and have a strong sense of self. Their culture values growth, both personal and collective, and they are driven by an insatiable curiosity about the world beyond their metropolis.

Despite their plant-like nature, the Chlorophyllians are highly intelligent and emotionally complex. They experience a range of feelings, from the joy of new growth to the melancholy of decay. Their long lifespans – often several centuries – give them a patient, long-term perspective on life and progress.

The Chlorophyllians are the caretakers, innovators, and very essence of the Arboreal Metropolis. Their existence is so intricately linked with their environment that it's often hard to tell where the city ends and the Chlorophyllians begin, creating a symbiotic relationship that defines this unique ecosystem.
