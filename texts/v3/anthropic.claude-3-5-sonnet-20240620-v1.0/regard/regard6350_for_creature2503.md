Upon witnessing this extraordinary realm and its remarkable inhabitants, the benevolent and wise power would likely choose to act as a guardian and nurturer, rather than an interventionist. Their actions might unfold as follows:

1. Protective Veil: The power would create an invisible, permeable barrier around the entire plateau. This veil would allow the natural elements - air, rain, sunlight - to pass through freely, but would gently deflect any potential threats or unwanted intrusions. This ensures the Aquarii's continued privacy and safety without isolating them completely.

2. Water Blessing: Recognizing the paramount importance of water to the Aquarii, the power would imbue the waters of the plateau with subtle enhancing properties. These waters would now carry trace elements that promote health, longevity, and mental acuity for all life forms that rely on them, further supporting the Aquarii's already impressive lifespan and wisdom.

3. Ecological Balance: To maintain the delicate equilibrium of the plateau's ecosystem, the power would establish a self-regulating system. This would subtly adjust environmental factors like temperature, humidity, and mineral content to ensure optimal conditions for both the Aquarii and the flora and fauna they coexist with.

4. Dream Whispers: Rather than communicate directly, the power would occasionally send inspirations through dreams to the Flow Keepers. These would manifest as innovative ideas for water management, deeper insights into their spiritual practices, or gentle nudges towards beneficial societal changes. This approach respects the Aquarii's autonomy while still offering guidance.

5. Knowledge Nexus: The power would create a hidden chamber deep within the plateau, accessible only to those Aquarii who demonstrate exceptional wisdom and a pure intention to benefit their society. This chamber would serve as a repository of knowledge, containing holographic records of the Aquarii's history and advanced concepts in hydraulics, ecology, and other relevant sciences. The information would reveal itself gradually, in pace with the Aquarii's natural development.

6. Adaptive Evolution: Recognizing the value of the Aquarii's expeditions beyond the plateau, the power would subtly enhance their adaptive capabilities. When venturing into new environments, the Aquarii would find their bodies naturally adjusting more quickly to different conditions, allowing for longer and more fruitful explorations.

7. Harmonic Resonance: The power would attune the natural frequencies of the plateau to resonate with the Aquarii's unique form of communication. This would amplify their ability to convey messages through water, allowing for clearer, farther-reaching communication without disrupting the natural soundscape.

8. Ethical Compass: Without imposing any specific moral code, the power would infuse the plateau with an ambient energy that gently encourages empathy, cooperation, and ethical behavior. This would help preserve the Aquarii's peaceful and egalitarian society as they continue to evolve.

9. Cosmic Window: At the highest point of the plateau, the power would create a special pool that, when gazed into under specific conditions, would offer glimpses of distant stars and planets. This would nurture the Aquarii's curiosity about the wider universe and potentially inspire future generations to explore beyond their world.

10. Regenerative Touch: The power would imbue the stone and plant life of the plateau with accelerated regenerative properties. This would ensure that the physical structures of their world could withstand the test of time, self-repairing and adapting as needed, much like a living organism.

Through these subtle yet profound interventions, the benevolent power would aim to protect, nurture, and gently guide the Aquarii civilization, all while respecting their autonomy and the unique beauty of the world they've created.
