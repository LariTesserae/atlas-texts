This could be achieved by introducing faint, harmonic vibrations into the flowstone that resonate with their method of communication. These vibrations would carry messages of peace, wisdom, and encouragement, helping to nurture their growth and development as a species.

To protect Lithopolis from external threats, I would create a protective energy field around the entire subterranean realm. This field would be imperceptible to the Lithos but would shield them from any potentially harmful cosmic radiation or geological instabilities that might disrupt their delicate ecosystem.

To aid in their evolution and understanding of their world, I would occasionally guide some of the ghostly entities that drift through their city to impart specific knowledge or insights. These visitations would be rare and mysterious, maintaining the sense of awe and wonder that the Lithos associate with these beings.

I would also subtly influence the formation of new chambers and passages within Lithopolis, ensuring that the city's growth remains sustainable and in harmony with the planet's natural processes. This might involve gently redirecting the flow of the bioluminescent liquid or stabilizing certain areas to prevent collapse.

To preserve their rich history and collective knowledge, I would create hidden chambers deep within the city - time capsules of sorts - that contain concentrated essences of the Lithos' shared memories and experiences. These chambers would only reveal themselves when the Lithos reach certain milestones in their development as a civilization.

Lastly, I would plant the seeds of curiosity about the world beyond their subterranean home. This would be done very gradually, perhaps over millennia, by allowing small glimpses of the surface world to filter down through the flowstone in certain chambers. This would prepare them for a time, far in the future, when they might be ready to explore beyond Lithopolis.

Throughout all these actions, my primary goal would be to preserve the unique beauty and complexity of Lithopolis and its inhabitants while gently guiding them towards greater understanding and potential. The Lithos and their city are a precious and rare form of life, deserving of protection and reverence.
