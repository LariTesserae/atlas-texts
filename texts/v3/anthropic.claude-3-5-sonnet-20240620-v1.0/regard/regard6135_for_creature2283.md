Upon witnessing this extraordinary realm and the Crystalline Synapse that inhabits it, the benevolent and wise power would likely approach with a mix of wonder, respect, and caution. Their actions might unfold as follows:

1. Observation: The power would first spend considerable time simply observing, seeking to understand the intricate balance of this ecosystem and the nature of the Crystalline Synapse without interference.

2. Attunement: Recognizing the unique method of communication used by the Synapse, the power would attempt to attune itself to the patterns of light and vibration, slowly developing a means of non-intrusive interaction.

3. Protection: Understanding the fragility and uniqueness of this environment, the power would establish a form of protection, shielding it from external disturbances that might disrupt its delicate balance.

4. Gentle Contact: Once a rudimentary form of communication is established, the power would attempt to make gentle contact with the Crystalline Synapse, expressing curiosity and goodwill.

5. Knowledge Exchange: If the Synapse is receptive, the power might engage in a slow, careful exchange of knowledge. This could take centuries, given the Synapse's timescale, but could yield profound insights for both entities.

6. Preservation: The power might offer to preserve aspects of the Synapse's vast knowledge and unique existence, perhaps by creating a protected record or sanctuary that could survive even if the original environment were to change.

7. Non-Interference: Ultimately, the power would likely choose a path of minimal interference, allowing the Crystalline Synapse and its ecosystem to continue their natural evolution and contemplation.

8. Reflection: The encounter would prompt deep reflection within the power itself, potentially reshaping its understanding of consciousness, life, and the nature of intelligence in the universe.

Throughout these actions, the power would maintain a deep respect for the autonomy and alien nature of the Crystalline Synapse, recognizing that its own benevolence must be tempered with the wisdom to allow unique forms of life to exist on their own terms.
