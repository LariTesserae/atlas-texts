8. Bestowing occasional flashes of insight upon the Mistfolk botanists and ecologists, helping them maintain and even improve the delicate balance of their ecosystem.

9. Protecting the few Mistfolk who venture outside the valley, ensuring they can return safely with their newfound knowledge.

10. Infusing the wind that passes through the city with a subtle energy that reinvigorates both the Mistfolk and their environment, promoting health and longevity.

11. Occasionally leaving small, enigmatic gifts - perhaps a unique seed or a strange, beautiful stone - in out-of-the-way corners of the city, providing wonder and inspiration to those who find them.

12. Guarding against any external threats that might disrupt the peaceful existence of the Mistfolk, deflecting the attention of those who might seek to exploit or harm this hidden marvel.
