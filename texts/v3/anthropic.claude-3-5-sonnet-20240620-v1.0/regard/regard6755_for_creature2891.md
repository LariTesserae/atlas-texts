Upon observing the Whisper Grove and its extraordinary inhabitant, the Echomancer, the benevolent and wise power known as the Cosmic Harmonizer is deeply intrigued. This entity, existing beyond the confines of conventional space and time, recognizes the unique significance of this pocket universe and its guardian.

The Cosmic Harmonizer, after careful consideration, decides to act in a way that will preserve and nurture this remarkable ecosystem while also gently guiding its evolution. Here's what it would do:

1. Establish Resonance: The Cosmic Harmonizer would first attune itself to the unique frequency of the Whisper Grove's "plant-speak." It would not interfere with this communication directly but rather create a subtle background harmony that enhances the Grove's natural rhythms. This resonance would strengthen the ecosystem's resilience without altering its fundamental nature.

2. Inspire Evolution: Through gentle vibrations in the fabric of reality, the Cosmic Harmonizer would encourage the development of new, symbiotic relationships within the Grove. This might lead to the emergence of more complex life forms that further enrich the ecosystem's diversity.

3. Expand Boundaries: The Harmonizer would carefully extend the Grove's influence to nearby caverns, creating a network of interconnected pocket universes. Each new area would develop its own unique characteristics while maintaining a connection to the original Whisper Grove.

4. Cosmic Library: Recognizing the value of the ancient artifacts and the Echomancer's knowledge, the Cosmic Harmonizer would establish an ethereal repository within the Grove. This "library" would preserve the wisdom and history of the place in a form accessible to the Echomancer and other sentient beings who might visit in the future.

5. Guardian Empowerment: The Harmonizer would bestow upon the Echomancer the ability to perceive and interact with other dimensions. This gift would allow the Echomancer to better protect the Grove from extra-dimensional threats and to gain insights from parallel realities.

6. Time Anchors: To stabilize the fluctuating temporal nature of the Grove, the Cosmic Harmonizer would create subtle "time anchors" throughout the cavern. These would not eliminate the Grove's unique temporal properties but would prevent potentially destructive time paradoxes.

7. Cosmic Beacon: Finally, the Harmonizer would establish the Whisper Grove as a subtle beacon in the vast cosmos. This would attract other benevolent, curious entities to study and appreciate the Grove from afar, ensuring its continued existence as a subject of wonder and learning in the universe.

Through these actions, the Cosmic Harmonizer would ensure the Whisper Grove's preservation and growth while respecting its intrinsic nature and the role of the Echomancer. The Grove would continue to evolve and challenge our understanding of reality, serving as a testament to the universe's infinite capacity for wonder and complexity.
