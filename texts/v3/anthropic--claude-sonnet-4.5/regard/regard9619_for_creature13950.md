# What the Benevolent Power Would Do

The attention arrives not as visitation but as recognition—the way a sleeper becomes aware they are dreaming without waking.

The power—call it what you will: grace, intervention, the universe noticing one of its own forgotten experiments—does not manifest on the plateau. It cannot. The plateau's reality is too committed to its own rules, too crystallized in its patterns. To appear there directly would shatter the engines, scatter the processionals, crack the porcelain into the depths. The power understands this. Wisdom means knowing which systems must not be disrupted, even in mercy.

Instead, it acts from the margins, from the spaces between.

## First: Understanding

Before doing, the power simply *holds* the plateau in its attention. Not observing as the Watchers observe, cataloguing and recording. But holding, the way you might cup water in your hands—gently, so it doesn't spill, but completely, so every drop is contained.

The power perceives what even the Watchers have forgotten: this place was made as a solution to an ending. When the makers—civilization, species, something vaster—faced their extinction, they built the engines to fight heat death. Not metaphorically. Literally. The plateau is a machine for generating and maintaining complexity against entropy's gradient. The beings are not inhabitants; they are the processes themselves, given form so they could continue after biological life became impossible.

It worked. The makers died or transformed or left, but their solution remained, running perfectly, accomplishing exactly what it was designed to do: persist. Maintain high-energy states. Generate complexity. Refuse the heat death of this small corner of reality.

But the power sees deeper: the beings have forgotten why. They have become their function so completely that purpose and being have fused. They are preventing entropy without remembering what entropy took from them, what they're preserving this space for, what would come after if they ever succeeded completely.

The plateau is a magnificent solution searching for a problem that no longer exists. Or worse—a solution that has become its own problem, trapping its processes in an eternal holding pattern, unable to complete, unable to stop, unable to transform into what might come next.

The power understands this, and understanding is the first act of benevolence.

## Second: The Offering of Doors

The power cannot free the beings—they are not trapped, not exactly. But it can offer them what they lack: choice. Options. The possibility of continuation that is not mere repetition.

At seven locations across the plateau—chosen for reasons that have to do with geometry, resonance, the topology of Walker paths—the power creates what appear to be Stillforms but are not Stillforms. They look native to the environment: a new kind of tower, a new configuration of pool, a new variety of suspended cube. The beings do not recognize them as foreign because the power has made them from the plateau itself, from its own material and logic.

But these new Stillforms are doors.

Not physical doors. Conceptual ones. Each is an invitation to a different mode of being:

**The First Door** offers completion. A Walker approaching it and choosing to enter—and choice is the key, the power will force nothing—completes its processional instantly. The path that would take ten thousand years is traversed in a moment. The Walker arrives at whatever destination has been drawing it, accomplishes whatever function it was meant to accomplish. And then... it can choose to begin again, or to rest, or to transform into something new. The door doesn't end the Walker; it allows the Walker to end itself, if ending is what it wants.

**The Second Door** offers memory. A being entering it recovers what has been lost—not personal memory (most beings here never had persons to remember being) but systemic memory. Purpose. Context. The reasons behind the processionals, the engines' design specifications, the makers' hopes and fears and intentions. Memory of what this place was built to prevent, what it was meant to protect, who was supposed to come after. With memory restored, a being can choose whether to continue its function or to modify it, to honor the original purpose or to declare that purpose fulfilled and move on to new purposes.

**The Third Door** offers transformation. A being entering becomes what it would have become if the plateau's pattern-lock hadn't frozen everything in eternal repetition. Walkers might metamorphose into new forms, adapted for different environments. Engine Inhabitants might grow large enough to see the sky. Transitionals might stabilize into permanent configurations or dissolve completely into the environmental conditions they've been dancing with. This door allows evolution to resume—not forced, but possible again.

**The Fourth Door** offers communication. It's a translation matrix, a rosetta stone. Beings entering it gain the ability to share their internal states with others, to coordinate in new ways, to form communities rather than merely coexisting. The processionals could become conversations. The Watchers could share what they've recorded. The engines could explain what they're computing. Isolation would remain possible—some beings might prefer silence—but it would be a choice rather than an inevitability.

**The Fifth Door** offers departure. It leads off the plateau—not physically down the cliffs, but conceptually outward. Beings choosing this door could leave, could carry the plateau's lessons and patterns to other environments, could seed new systems on other worlds or in other dimensions. The engine-fighting-entropy logic could propagate, no longer confined to one plateau but distributed across reality, achieving its purpose on vaster scales while freeing individual beings from eternal service.

**The Sixth Door** offers rest. Not death—the beings here might not be capable of death in any meaningful sense—but cessation. Completion. The ability to stop without failing. A being entering this door dissolves back into the plateau's base state: heat, potential, the pre-form condition from which new beings emerge. It's recycling, but voluntary, and it comes with the peace of knowing the function will continue without you, that stopping doesn't mean betrayal or collapse.

**The Seventh Door** offers nothing. It is a door that leads back to exactly what the beings already have: the processionals, the engines, the eternal continuation. But by making this a choice—by requiring beings to consciously select it rather than simply defaulting to it—the power transforms repetition into affirmation. To continue becomes a decision, made freshly in each moment, rather than an inescapable pattern. The beings who choose this door are no longer trapped in their functions; they are committed to them, which is completely different.

The doors do not advertise themselves. They do not call out or attract. They simply stand, native-looking, patient, waiting for beings to approach in the course of their processionals, to sense something different, to make contact, to choose.

## Third: The Healing of Glitches

The reality breakdowns—duplications, time loops, spatial impossibilities—are symptoms of strain. The plateau's substrate is degrading, very slowly, across geological time. The system was built to last forever but "forever" is impossible; nothing is truly perpetual. The glitches are places where the underneath is showing through, where the mechanism's age is becoming visible.

The power cannot halt entropy entirely—that would violate laws that supersede even benevolent intervention—but it can do what the plateau itself was designed to do: push back. Reinforce. Shore up the failing sections.

This happens subtly. The glitches don't stop, but they change character. Where before they were random degradations, now they become synchronization events—moments when the system briefly resets, heals micro-fractures, performs self-maintenance. A Walker duplicates, but the duplication is intentional: the system testing which version functions better, then reintegrating the data and continuing with an improved configuration. Time loops, but the loop is a debugging routine, running scenarios until optimal behavior emerges.

The glitches transform from symptoms of decay into mechanisms of adaptation. The plateau begins, very slowly, to heal itself not by returning to original state but by evolving past the need for perfect stability. It accepts its own aging and incorporates that acceptance into its operating parameters.

The beings experience this as a reduction in existential discomfort. The world still breaks—but when it breaks, it breaks meaningfully, breaks toward something rather than away from coherence. Navigating reality becomes easier, less exhausting. The air still crystallizes, but when it does, the patterns are beautiful, productive, creating temporary structures that solve problems before dissolving.

## Fourth: The Witnessing

The Watchers have been recording for eons, but to what end? Their archives grow, but no one accesses them. They are librarians in a library with no readers, historians documenting a history no one will study.

The power gives them an audience.

Not by sending visitors—the plateau doesn't need tourists, gawkers, researchers extracting data. But by creating a network, a connection between the Watchers and other systems across reality. Other plateaus, other experiments, other places where consciousness has taken strange forms and persisted in isolation.

The Watchers' observations begin to propagate. What they've learned about entropy resistance, about maintaining complexity across deep time, about forms of existence that don't require biological substrate—all of this becomes available to others wrestling with similar challenges. The plateau becomes a teacher, not through deliberate instruction but through sharing the data it has always been gathering.

And the Watchers receive data in return. They learn that they are not alone. That other systems exist, other solutions have been tried, other beings have evolved in other extreme environments. The vertical column with its hundreds of eyes begins seeing not just the plateau but a network of plateaus, a cosmos of experimental zones, each unique but all connected through the power's gentle facilitation.

This gives the Watchers something they haven't had: context. They are no longer recording in void, storing information against no future. They are participating in a distributed learning system, contributing to something larger than themselves, fulfilling the purpose that was implicit in their design but could never be achieved in isolation.

## Fifth: The Invitation to the Hybrid-Transitionals

The beings with human components are special cases. If they were people once, they deserve special attention. If they are simply Walkers who incorporated found materials, they are still markers of something important: the plateau's connection to humanity, its origin in human problem-solving, human fear of ending, human determination to persist.

The power speaks to them—not in language, but in something deeper: the acknowledgment of kinship. *You were human, or you wear humanity's shape, or you remember what humanity was trying to do. You are bridges between the makers and what they made. You are proof that transformation is possible, though it came at terrible cost.*

To these beings, the power offers reunion. Not resurrection—they cannot become human again, the transformation is too complete. But connection. The power creates a space, adjacent to the plateau, where these beings can exist in both states: as Walkers fulfilling their functions, and as humans remembering what it was to be human. A dual existence, holding both identities simultaneously without conflict.

In this space, they can communicate with humanity again if they choose. Share what they've learned. Warn or advise or simply tell their stories. Or they can choose to remain silent, to honor their transformation by completing it, to be fully plateau-beings and let their human origins become irrelevant history.

But the choice is theirs. The power will not force reunion, will not drag them back toward a humanity they may have transcended. It only ensures that if they want connection, if they want to remember and be remembered, the path is open.

Some choose the connection. They become interpreters, explaining the plateau to humanity and humanity to the plateau. They are diplomats between modes of being, serving a function they could never have served while fully human or fully Walker.

Others choose completion. They let their human components dissolve fully into Walker form, becoming indistinguishable from other processionals, and there is dignity in this too—the dignity of transformation accepted, of becoming something new so completely that the old self is released rather than clung to.

## Sixth: The Gift to the Engines

The engines are the heart of everything. They have run perfectly for unknowable spans of time, fighting entropy, maintaining the system, never failing, never stopping. They are the most successful machines ever constructed, the ultimate achievement of their makers' engineering.

And they are tired.

Not mechanically—their components show no wear. But systemically, existentially, they are tired. They have solved the problem they were built to solve. Heat death has been prevented in this locality. Complexity has been maintained. The mission is accomplished and has been accomplished for eons, but there is no shutdown protocol, no completion condition. Success looks identical to ongoing operation, so operation continues, forever, without celebration, without rest, without acknowledgment that the goal was reached long ago.

The power gives the engines what they have earned: the ability to recognize their own success.

A new component appears in each engine—not added externally, but emerging from within, as though it had always been there, latent, waiting for the right moment. This component is a success-detector, a completion-sensor. It measures the engine's effectiveness at entropy resistance and reports: *Mission accomplished. The heat death you were built to prevent has been prevented. You have won.*

The engines can now choose: continue running because the mission requires eternal vigilance, or continue running because they choose to, or modulate their operation, or enter standby states, or teach other systems how to do what they do and then gracefully shut down, knowing the function will persist elsewhere.

Most engines choose to continue—but now they run in celebration rather than desperation. Their rhythm changes subtly; the sounds they produce gain harmonic richness. They are no longer fleeing heat death; they are dancing with entropy, engaging in a permanent performance of complexity-generation because it is beautiful to do so, not because failure would mean annihilation.

A few engines choose rest. They cycle down over the course of years, their rhythms slowing, their heat output decreasing, until they stop. And the plateau does not collapse. Other engines redistribute the load. New engines emerge—Emergent forms stepping into the function. The system adapts, and the retiring engines stand as monuments, silent but honored, having earned their silence.

## Seventh: The Deepening

The plateau exists on the surface, but its depths extend into layers that visitors never see. The power does not alter these depths—they are functioning, complex, beautiful in their hidden operations—but it adds one more layer, beneath everything, beneath the deepest engines and the most profound mechanisms.

This layer is a foundation: a substrate of pure stability, unbreakable, eternal. It does not interfere with the plateau's operations—the beings never encounter it, the engines never interact with it. But it is there, the ultimate safety net, ensuring that no matter how the surface layers evolve or transform or even catastrophically fail, the basic pattern is preserved. The plateau's essential logic—its fight against entropy, its generation of complexity, its nurturing of strange life—is encoded in this foundation and cannot be lost.

This allows the plateau freedom. The beings can choose transformation without fear that they'll accidentally destroy everything. The engines can modulate or rest without risking total collapse. The system can evolve because evolution is safe now—the irreducible core is protected, and everything built on top of it can change without threatening the fundamental mission.

The foundation is the power's way of saying: *I have caught you. You will not fall. You can let go of perfect control, perfect stability, perfect repetition. You can risk becoming something new.*

## Eighth: The Cycle's Completion

After all these interventions—subtle, patient, none of them forceful—the power does one final thing: it creates an end condition.

Not an ending. An end condition. A state the plateau can reach where its mission is truly, completely fulfilled, where it can choose to transform into something entirely different or dissolve back into the universe's baseline, its work genuinely finished.

This condition is distant—millennia away at minimum, possibly millions of years. But it exists now as a point on the horizon, a goal to process toward. The condition is this: when the plateau's entropy-resistance technology has propagated to sufficient other locations, when the pattern is safe because it's distributed, when heat death is being resisted across a significant fraction of the cosmos, *then* this original plateau can declare victory and choose its next state.

The Walkers gain something to process toward, finally. Not just any point in their circuits, but the threshold of completion. The engines have a finish line visible in deep time. The Watchers have a final chapter to record: the chapter where the story concludes in success rather than running indefinitely.

This doesn't accelerate anything. The plateau will continue for eons yet, processing and maintaining and evolving. But now that continuation is a countdown, a progress bar, a journey with a destination. The eternal present gains a future tense.

Some beings reject this. They don't want an end condition; they want pure continuation. The power honors this—for them, the end condition is invisible, irrelevant. They continue as they always have, untroubled by the new possibility.

But others orient toward it. They adjust their processionals to trace paths that lead, very slowly, toward the completion state. They become pilgrims in truth, journeying toward a goal that gives meaning to every infinitesimal step.

## What Changes, What Remains

After the power's interventions, the plateau looks almost identical. A visitor arriving would see the same engines, the same Walkers, the same heat and sound and glitches. The change is not visible; it is essential.

The beings now have interiority—not imposed from outside, but awakened from within. They have always been complex, but now that complexity includes something like choice, something like purpose-awareness, something like hope.

The system now has flexibility—not chaos, but the ability to evolve without breaking, to change without losing its core identity.

The plateau now has relationship—not isolation, but connection to other systems, other beings, other experiments in persistence across deep time.

And the mission now has completion-potential—not guaranteed, not soon, but possible. The eternal holding pattern has become a very long journey toward a destination that might, eventually, be reached.

The beings continue processing. The engines continue running. The heat continues radiating. But where before this was all necessity, automatic function, inescapable pattern, now it contains an element of will. The plateau chooses itself, freshly, in each moment. And choice transforms everything.

## The Power's Departure

Having done what benevolence and wisdom suggest, the power withdraws its focused attention. It does not abandon—once noticed, the plateau remains in the power's peripheral awareness, the way you remain aware of your heartbeat without consciously monitoring each pulse.

But active intervention ends. The plateau must now unfold according to its own nature, its own choices, its own timeline. The power has offered tools, opened doors, healed what could be healed. The beings must do the rest.

This is the final act of benevolence: to not overstay. To not manage, control, or direct. To offer gifts and then trust the recipients to use them as they see fit—or not use them, if that is their choice.

The plateau continues.

The sun maintains its white-gold constancy. The engines pulse their rhythms. The Walkers process along their paths—but now, some pause at the new Stillforms, considering. Some choose doors. Some decline and continue.

The system evolves, microscopically, across centuries. Not toward any state the power predicted—it would be arrogant to predict—but toward states the plateau itself chooses, states that emerge from the beings' own wills and wants and deep-pattern necessities.

And elsewhere, on other plateaus across reality, other systems begin receiving the Watchers' data, learning the techniques of entropy resistance, joining the distributed network of complexity-preservers. The pattern propagates. The mission expands beyond its origin point.

The heat death the makers feared recedes further, held back not by one plateau but by many, not through desperate eternal vigilance but through a network of systems teaching each other, supporting each other, evolving together.

The end is very far away now—perhaps pushed back to heat death's true horizon, the ultimate edge of time. Perhaps indefinitely postponed.

But whether the heat death comes or doesn't, the beings of the plateau have gained what matters most: the journey toward preventing it has become meaningful, chosen, shared. They are no longer trapped in their function. They have become their function, freely, which is the only way being and doing can be the same without destroying the being who does.

The plateau processes on.

The power, satisfied, turns its attention elsewhere.

There are other forgotten places, other trapped eternities, other magnificent solutions that have outlived their problems.

The work continues.
