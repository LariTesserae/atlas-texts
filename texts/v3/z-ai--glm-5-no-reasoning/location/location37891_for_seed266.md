A beginning, written slowly:

---

This is a high place. A plateau, perhaps—though that word suggests something flatter than what exists here. The land rises to a vast broken tableland, split by ancient fissures, stepped with erosion-bench terraces that descend toward lower elevations. The height is extreme. The air thins.

Warmth, but no moisture. No water moves through this place. What moisture once existed has been gone for a very long time.

---

I'll continue building.
