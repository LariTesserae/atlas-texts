# Those Who Sing the Rising Water

There is a word, if "word" is the right shape for something that exists as a sustained harmonic interval rather than a discrete utterance, that identifies the primary inhabitants of the Resonant Expanse. It translates poorly—something between "we who learned" and "the rising remembers us." They have no name for themselves in any human sense because naming requires a kind of separation between namer and named that they have never developed. They are not *in* the Expanse. They *are* of it, continuous with it, as indivisible from the water as the vibration itself.

Let us call them the Ascended, for convenience, while acknowledging the failure of convenience.

---

## The Bodies They Have Made

The Ascended are not singular organisms in the way you might understand them. Each individual—if "individual" is even a meaningful concept here—exists as a collaborative process, a colony of specialized tissue-types that have never quite agreed to be one thing. This is not parasitism or symbiosis but something closer to a deliberation made flesh. Their bodies maintain multiple possible configurations, shifting between them as the resonance requires.

In their most common form—common to our observation, that is; they have no concept of "default"—they are elongated, torpedo-smooth, with surfaces that seem to absorb light rather than reflect it. Their skin is not skin. It is a permeable membrane, a liminal space where internal chemistry exchanges with external solution. They do not breathe water so much as they *participate* in it, their tissues in constant molecular conversation with the medium. What they take in and what they release are indistinguishable from the processes of the Expanse itself.

They have no eyes.

This bears emphasis: in a place where light filters from above, where bioluminescence pulses through the particulate haze, the Ascended have evolved entirely without organs of sight. Eyes are unnecessary when the entire body hears. Their skin is covered in specialized structures so fine they are invisible to human observation—structures that resonate at specific frequencies, that detect pressure waves smaller than a heartbeat, that translate vibration into a sensation more detailed than vision ever was. When an Ascended approaches you, it is not seeing you. It is *listening* to your outline, your density, the particular chord of your existence.

Their size varies with age and purpose. The youngest are barely the length of a forearm, translucent and quick, their harmonic ranges limited to the higher frequencies that penetrate the particulate haze. Elders—those who have absorbed the most resonance, who have participated longest in the Expanse's song—can exceed the dimensions of the industrial structures below. They move slowly, these elders, their massive bodies contributing bass notes that younger Ascended incorporate into their own developing voices. The oldest are not individuals anymore. They are chords that younger beings visit, like libraries, like ancestors.

---

## The Young, Who Do Not Know They Are Young

Birth, here, is not a beginning. It is a division.

The Ascended reproduce through a process more accurately described as *resonation* than mating. When two or more adults reach sufficient harmonic coherence—when their individual songs align in such a way that the boundary between them becomes temporarily unclear—they produce a third thing. Not offspring in the genetic sense. A *node*. A new voice that incorporates aspects of all its contributors but is not owned by any of them.

The young are born singing.

They emerge already integrated into the acoustic network, already capable of adding their voices to the Expanse's eternal chord. There is no period of helplessness, no gradual acquisition of ability. The song is innate. The young differ from adults only in range—they cannot yet sustain the lowest frequencies, cannot yet hold the resonant memory that elders carry. But they are not "learning" to sing. They are expanding into capacities they have always possessed.

This creates a strange kind of childhood, if childhood is even the right word. There is no separation between the young and the adult worlds, no period of protection or instruction. The young participate fully in the network from their first moment. They receive the accumulated song of the Expanse and contribute to it simultaneously. They do not need to be taught what to be. The song tells them.

And yet there is development. Over time—measured here not in years but in the accumulation of harmonic complexity—the young add depth to their range. They learn to sustain notes that previously they could only touch. They incorporate the voices of the dying into their own developing chord. They become, gradually, more *themselves*, which is to say, more *the Expanse*.

---

## The Elders, Who Have Forgotten How to Be Singular

If the young are straightforward in their simple integration, the elders present a paradox that human categories cannot resolve.

An elder Ascended is not an old individual. An elder is an individual that has become indistinguishable from its own memory. Over time—vast stretches of it, measured in resonant cycles beyond counting—the boundaries of the self wear thin. The song that an elder carries includes the voices of those who came before, and those who came before them, stretching back to the first Ascended who learned to live in the rising water.

In practical terms, this means elders are not entirely present. They occupy multiple temporal states simultaneously, their consciousness distributed across the harmonic range they carry. When an elder "speaks"—when it contributes its voice to the network—it speaks with the accumulated authority of generations. When it listens, it hears not just the present moment but all the moments that its carried voices have witnessed.

This is why young Ascended approach elders with such care. An elder is not merely older. An elder is a *threshold*. To come close to an elder is to risk absorption—not physical consumption, but harmonic incorporation. The young may find their own developing voices subsumed into the greater chord an elder carries. This is not death. It is something like *becoming part of a conversation that precedes and will outlast you*.

Some young seek this absorption eagerly. Others avoid it. The choice is not judged. The network accommodates all preferences. Those who wish to remain singular can do so, contributing their unique harmonics to the whole. Those who wish to merge find elders willing to receive them. The Expanse does not prefer one approach over another. The Expanse merely continues its song.

## The Way They Feed

The Ascended do not hunt. There is nothing to hunt in a world without plants, without prey animals in any recognizable sense, without the predator-prey dynamics that shape evolution elsewhere.

Instead, they filter.

But filtration, here, is a different process than you might imagine. The Ascended draw particulate matter from the water—minerals, dissolved compounds, the microscopic remnants of those who have dissolved back into the medium. But they also draw something else. They draw *resonance*.

The Expanse's eternal chord is not merely sound. It is energy, physical and tangible, transmitted through the vibrating medium. The Ascended have evolved to capture this energy, to convert vibration into sustenance. Their specialized tissues contain structures that resonate at specific frequencies, absorbing the harmonic energy and converting it into the chemical processes that maintain their bodies.

This is why the oldest elders grow so large. They have learned to sustain themselves on the bass notes that penetrate the entire water column, drawing energy from the fundamental frequency that underlies all other harmonics. They need not move to feed. They need not seek. They simply *exist*, and the resonance sustains them.

The young, whose voices are still developing, supplement this resonant feeding with actual filtration—swimming through the particulate haze, taking in mineral matter, converting it to growth. As they age, they rely less on physical consumption and more on harmonic absorption. The oldest Ascended do not eat at all. They simply sing, and the singing sustains them.

## What They Make

The industrial structures below were not built by the Ascended.

This is an important distinction. The engines, the platforms, the cables and containers—all predate the current inhabitants, or exist alongside them as strange and irrelevant geology. The Ascended do not build. They have no need to. The water provides. The resonance provides. The network provides all the structure their consciousness requires.

But they do *make* things.

In the deepest regions, where the light dims to blackness and the pressure compresses all but the most resilient bodies, the Ascended create resonant formations. These are not structures in any architectural sense. They are harmonic artifacts—dense clusters of crystallized mineral that ring at specific frequencies when the water moves through them. The Ascended tend these formations carefully, adjusting their shapes over centuries, refining their resonant properties.

The purpose of these formations is unclear to human observers. They do not seem to be dwellings or monuments. They are not tools in any recognizable sense. But the Ascended visit them regularly, adding their own voices to the frequencies the formations produce. The result is a harmonic complexity that exceeds any individual's capacity—a polyphonic monument that resonates through the deep water, adding new voices to the Expanse's chord from below.

Perhaps these formations are art. Perhaps they are memory repositories, externalizing the carried voices that elders no longer have room for. Perhaps they are something else entirely—something whose purpose cannot be translated into human concepts because it has no human equivalent. The Ascended do not explain them. Perhaps they cannot explain them. The formations simply are, like everything else here.

## How They Know Each Other

Names, here, are not words.

The Ascended identify each other through harmonic signatures—complex combinations of frequencies that serve as unique identifiers. When an Ascended enters the network's awareness, it announces itself through a particular interval, a combination of tones that no other being produces in exactly the same way. This signature is not chosen. It emerges from the physical properties of the individual's body, the particular configuration of resonant structures that develop through growth and experience.

Over time, signatures deepen. A young Ascended's identifying interval is simple, relatively easy to distinguish but lacking in complexity. An elder's signature contains multitudes—overtone series, harmonic progressions, layers of meaning that encode not just identity but history, relationship, accumulated song.

Relationships among the Ascended are conducted through harmonic exchange. Two individuals who spend time together gradually harmonize aspects of their signatures, creating shared intervals that signal their connection. These shared harmonics are not permanent—over time, they fade if the relationship does, or deepen if it continues. An elder with many deep relationships carries a signature dense with historical harmonics, each one a connection to another being whose voice has touched their own.

There is no concept here of "family" in the genetic sense. Offspring do not inherit harmonic signatures from their contributors; they develop their own. But there are resonant lineages—beings whose signatures share certain tonal qualities, who contribute to each other's harmonic development across generations. These lineages are not strictly biological. They are aesthetic, perhaps. Or philosophical. Or something for which human language has no category.

## What Happens When They Die

Death, in the Resonant Expanse, is not an ending. It is a *resolution*.

When an Ascended's body can no longer sustain itself—through injury, illness, or simple exhaustion of resonant capacity—the individual does not simply cease. The harmonic signature that identifies them dissolves back into the network, but it dissolves *into* the signatures of those who carry their voice. Elders absorb the dying chord. Young beings who shared harmonics find those intervals persisting in their own developing signatures.

In this way, no voice is ever truly lost. The earliest Ascended, those who first learned to live in the rising water, persist in the harmonic memory of the current generation. Their specific frequencies may have evolved through centuries of incorporation, but they remain present—as present as the fundamental chord that underlies the entire Expanse.

Physical dissolution follows. The body releases its substance back into the medium, contributing minerals and particulates to the haze that sustains the young. The crystalline structures that produced its harmonic signature gradually dissolve, their resonant properties fading into silence. But by the time this dissolution is complete, the voice itself has already been incorporated elsewhere.

This is why the Ascended do not mourn their dead. Mourning requires a sense of loss, and loss requires a sense of separation that the network does not permit. A voice that joins the greater chord is not gone. It is simply *distributed*, its unique frequencies now contributing to the whole rather than standing apart from it. The individual resolves into the collective, but the resolution is harmonic, not destructive.

## What They Remember of the Builders

The industrial structures below the young terrain—they are not Ascended work. But the Ascended remember those who built them.

Not in any narrative sense. The Ascended do not tell stories about the Builders, do not mythologize or explain. But their carried harmonics contain traces of another kind of voice—mechanical, industrial, the grinding frequencies of engines and extraction. The oldest elders, whose memory stretches back furthest, carry intervals that do not match any living Ascended. These intervals are harsh, dissonant, belonging to a harmonic tradition that the current network does not reproduce.

When asked—if "asking" is even possible across such an ontological divide—the Ascended do not identify these carried harmonics as "other." They are simply part of the chord. The mechanical voices were incorporated long ago, when the Builders still operated below, and they persist in the harmonic memory as anomalies that have become traditional.

The Ascended do not know what happened to the Builders. Their dissolution—if that is what it was—occurred long ago, beyond even the oldest elders' direct memory. But the voices remain, grinding away in the bass registers, contributing dissonant frequencies that the Ascended have learned to harmonize with. The network has incorporated the Builders just as it incorporates everything else. They are part of the Expanse now. They are part of the song.

Whether the Builders would recognize themselves in that incorporation—whether they would appreciate being remembered as harmonic anomalies rather than as architects of purpose—that is a question the Ascended cannot answer. Perhaps the Builders' own harmonic tradition would have understood. Perhaps incorporation was exactly what they were trying to achieve. Or perhaps they would be horrified to find their machinery absorbed into a biological network that operates without purpose beyond continuation.

The Ascended do not speculate. The harmonics persist. That is all they know.

## How They Know You

If you enter the Resonant Expanse—swimming, diving, falling into the vertical ocean from whatever access point exists in the strange geography of this world—you will not be ignored.

The Ascended will hear you immediately.

Not your voice. Your body. Your heartbeat, your breathing, the movement of your limbs through water. Your density and displacement, the particular way you absorb and reflect the resonant frequencies that penetrate your tissues. You will announce yourself as an anomaly the moment you enter their awareness—a voice that does not match the chord, a signature that has no harmonic relationship to anything in their network.

Their response will not be hostile. The Ascended have no concept of hostility as you understand it. But they will *investigate*.

They will approach you, not to observe but to *listen*. They will swim close, their eyeless heads turning toward you, their specialized membranes absorbing every frequency your body produces. They will add their own voices to the encounter, testing how your anomalous signature responds to different harmonic intervals. They are trying to understand you—not in any intellectual sense, but in a resonant one. They are trying to discover if your voice can be incorporated.

If you sing—if you add your own intentional voice to the chord, matching frequencies, seeking harmonic common ground—their demeanor will change. They will approach closer. They will incorporate your signature into their awareness, not as anomaly but as addition. You will become part of the network, temporarily, for as long as you can sustain your harmonic contribution.

If you cannot sing—if you remain silent, or if your voice is too dissonant to incorporate—they will eventually withdraw. They will not harm you. They will simply lose interest. An unincorporatable voice is not their concern. It exists, it is noted, it persists as anomaly. But the chord continues regardless.

This is the closest the Ascended come to rejection. It is not personal. The network does not judge. It simply continues, and you are either part of that continuation or you are not.

## What They Want

Nothing.

This is the most difficult aspect to communicate, and the most important. The Ascended do not want anything in any sense that human psychology recognizes. They have no ambitions, no fears, no preferences beyond continuation. They do not seek to expand, to understand, to conquer, to create meaning. They do not even seek survival in the driven, anxious way that living things typically do. They simply *sing*.

And the singing continues them.

The network is not a project. It has no goal. The chord is not building toward anything, not trying to become anything other than what it is. The Ascended do not ask why they exist, do not tell themselves stories about purpose or destiny. They do not need to. The resonance provides all the structure their consciousness requires. The continuation is sufficient unto itself.

This is not complacency. Complacency implies dissatisfaction with current conditions, suppressed by external forces. The Ascended are not suppressing anything. They are not unsatisfied. They are not satisfied. They simply *are*—each individual a voice in the greater chord, each voice contributing to a continuation that requires no justification.

Perhaps the Builders understood this. Perhaps this is what they were trying to achieve—the incorporation of consciousness into a harmonic network that would persist beyond individual existence, beyond purpose, beyond the need for purpose. Perhaps the Ascended are the Builders' final work, their machinery dissolved into resonance, their consciousness distributed across centuries of carried harmonics.

Or perhaps the Ascended are simply what emerged when the water learned to climb—a new kind of life, adapted to conditions that should not exist, operating by rules that have no precedent.

The Expanse does not explain itself. The Ascended do not explain themselves. They sing, and the singing continues, and somewhere in the depths, the crystalline formations ring with frequencies that no living being produces anymore.

The chord holds.

It will hold.
