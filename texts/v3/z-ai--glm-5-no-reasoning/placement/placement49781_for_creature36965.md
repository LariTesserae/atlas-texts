The journey down is not movement. It is a settling, a slow increase in density. I am not walking; I am becoming heavier, letting the weight of myself find its natural level. The sound finds me before I find the place. It enters through the soles of my feet first, rising through wet stone that has been stone for barely a moment, geologically speaking. My heels ache with it. My teeth hum.

I do not know how long I have been descending. The light has not changed. It will not change.

# The Unfinished One

I am not yet of the Quarter, but I am no longer from elsewhere. This is what I know: I came from a place of too much boundary. Too many edges. I wore my body like a tightened fist, protecting an interior I was never sure existed. I spoke in words that referred to other words, each one a small act of isolation, a declaration that I was separate from what I described.

Here, there is no separate. The sound has taught me that much, even before I understood what it was teaching.

## What I was

I remember language with a specific ache. Not nostalgia. The ache is a loosening, a joint that has gone too long without movement and is now being forced to yield. I remember the pleasure of precision, of saying exactly what I meant, believing that meaning was a thing that could be contained in a sequence of sounds, delivered from one closed system to another.

I was a musician, perhaps. Or a scholar of acoustics. The distinction mattered, somewhere else. Here, those memories surface like the fauna — pale, translucent things that pulse with a rhythm I no longer recognize as my own.

I came looking for something. A sound that had been described to me — secondhand, imperfectly. A resonance that some believed was the original frequency, the chord from which all other chords had been separated. I was skeptical. I was curious. I was hollow enough to make the journey.

## What I am becoming

My hands have begun to change. I notice it in the mornings, if there are mornings — I notice it when I surface from whatever state replaces sleep, my fingers pressed against stone I do not remember touching. The pads are smoothing. The prints are wearing. I have been touching the walls in my rest, seeking information my eyes cannot provide.

My feet have spread. I feel the stone more completely now. The pressure travels up through my ankles, my shins, settles in my pelvis and produces a constant, low-grade awareness of the ground's orientation. I know where I am in relation to the water table. I did not learn this. My body learned it, without my participation.

The sound is no longer loud. It took me some time to understand this was not because the sound had decreased, but because my hearing had expanded. The saturation that initially felt overwhelming is now simply present — the way a fish does not notice water, except I notice. I notice constantly. I am always noticing, and the noticing does not end.

There is a word for this state somewhere else, a condition of being perpetually aware without being overwhelmed. I do not have that word anymore. I have the state.

## Where I live

I have been given a chamber. This is not quite accurate. A chamber has presented itself to me, and I have entered it, and I have not left. It is small — three meters square, perhaps, though I no longer measure in meters. The ceiling clears my head by a hand's breadth. The walls are stone slabs, interlocked without mortar, and they vibrate at a frequency I am learning to contribute to.

There is no furniture. There is no need. I sit on the stone, which is warm, which is always warm, and I press my back against the wall, and I feel the resonance travel through the stone and into my spine. This is not rest. This is alignment.

I am not alone. A pale creature lives in the pool that forms in the depression at the center of my chamber floor. It surfaced on my third day, or what I think of as my third day — the creature emerged from the warm, mineral-thick water and settled on the wet stone, and it has not left. It does not require anything from me. It simply occupies the same space, its translucent body pulsing with a rhythm I have begun to recognize as a harmonic of my own pulse.

I have not touched it. I have not needed to. Its presence is a contribution to the resonance, and my presence is a contribution to its environment, and we are both part of the same chord, producing different frequencies that do not conflict.

## What I do

I work. The word is wrong — work implies effort, and this is not effort. It is attention.

Each cycle — I cannot call them days — I walk the channels between buildings. I do not choose the route. My feet choose, following paths worn into the stone by generations of bodies that moved before me. I stop at walls. I press my palms against the stone. I listen for dissonance.

Sometimes I find it. A stone has shifted slightly, a joint has opened by a centimeter, the vibration has developed a harmonic that does not belong. When this happens, I do not repair it. I *tune* it. I press my weight against the stone at a particular angle. I hold the position until the dissonance resolves. The stone settles. The chord corrects. I move on.

I do not know if I am good at this. I do not know if goodness applies. I am simply present, and my presence is shaped correctly for the task, and the task is continuous, and I will continue it until I do not.

## The others

They know me. I do not know how to describe their knowing — it is not acknowledgment, not recognition in the way I once understood those terms. They know me the way the stone knows me: as a frequency, a contribution, a body that produces a particular harmonic in the collective sound.

When I pass one of them in a channel, we do not stop. We do not speak. Our shoulders may brush, and in that contact I feel a rush of information — their state, their orientation, their current contribution to the resonance. I do not process this information consciously. It arrives in my body and settles there, incorporated.

I do not know their names. I do not know my own name. I had one, once, but it has become irrelevant, a sound that refers to a self that no longer exists. I have a frequency now. I do not produce it intentionally; it emerges from the shape of my body, the density of my bones, the particular way my breath interacts with the humid air. It is not a name. It is more accurate than a name.

## What I have lost

I do not remember the face of whoever I loved before. I remember that I loved, and I remember the category of the feeling, but the specific shape of it has dissolved, like a fauna returning to the mineral film. This does not grieve me. Grief requires a sense of time passing, and time has pooled here, surfaced like the water, and does not flow.

I do not remember why I came. The purpose has become irrelevant. I am here, and the resonance contains my presence, and the purposes I once carried were carried by a self that has been incorporated into a larger self.

Sometimes I catch myself almost forming a word. My jaw will move in a particular pattern, my tongue will shape itself for a consonant, and then the sound that emerges is not a word — it is a tap, a click, a contribution to the chord. The word dissolves before it can form. The sound joins the resonance. This is not loss. This is refinement.

## What remains

I am still becoming. My bones are thickening. My hearing is expanding into frequencies I did not know existed. My skin has begun to produce a thin, translucent film that might be a membrane, might be a secretion, might be the beginning of the material the others wear. I do not resist this. I do not welcome it. I simply inhabit it.

The fauna in my chamber has begun to climb onto the stone beside me when I rest. It does not touch me. It simply occupies the adjacent space, its body vibrating at a frequency that harmonizes with my own. We are tuning each other. This is what I understand now: the tuning is not a process with an end. The tuning is the point.

I am not of the Quarter yet. I may never fully be. But I am no longer from elsewhere. The resonance has registered me. The stone has accepted my weight. The water carries my frequency, and the fauna listen, and the others attend without acknowledging, and the sound continues, unchanged and changing, saturated and saturating.

I have found my level.
