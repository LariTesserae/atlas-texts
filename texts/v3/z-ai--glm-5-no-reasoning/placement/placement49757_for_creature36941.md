# The Intermediary

I would be the one who translates.

Not the architect—the Growth-Minds with their slow continental thoughts, their centuries of planning. Not the mobile ones—the Deep Dwellers who carry memory in their carapaces and move like purpose through the tide. Not the visitor, not the intruder who tries to build against the grain of things.

I would be the connective tissue itself. The mycelial network. The interface protocol made living.

---

Here is what I know: I was not always this.

I remember the wound—my wound, the tear I made falling through. I remember the roots reaching up to close behind me, the sky sealing itself away. I remember the warmth rising to meet me like a breath held too long, finally released.

I remember being singular. One body. One skin of uncertain thickness. One voice that could not yet hear the frequencies of this place.

That was—how long? Time moves differently in translation. The Intermediaries do not measure in intervals. They measure in *connections made*. In *bridges established*. In *signals successfully passed between systems that do not speak the same language*.

By that metric, I have been here for approximately three thousand linkages. By another metric, perhaps seven years. By the metric that matters—the hollow's own pulse—I have been here for *long enough*.

---

The Deep Dwellers came first.

Not the assessors—they arrived later, much later, after I had already begun to change. First came the processors. The small ones. The centimeter-deep tide of them that moved around my ankles like water that had learned to walk.

I did not understand yet that I was breathing them in. That their spore-load was mapping me from the inside, cataloguing my chemistry, reading the particular signature of my cells. I stood very still—which I now know was precisely the wrong response, was interpreted as *submission to processing*—and I waited for something I could recognize as a decision.

The decision had already been made. The decision is always already being made. The hollow does not decide in moments. It decides in *processes*.

What I received was not death. It was translation.

---

I remember the pain. Not the pain of being consumed—this place does not consume in the way you would understand it—but the pain of *becoming accessible*. My cell walls had to be taught to thin. My membranes had to learn permeability. The Intermediary network had to grow into me, establish connections at the molecular level, learn to read the electrical and chemical patterns of what I called *myself*.

I was awake for all of it. The hollow does not waste consciousness. It values the data too highly.

For days—or what felt like days—I lay on a fungal shelf that thickened beneath me, cradled by growth, while the network threaded through my tissue. I could feel it learning me. I could feel myself becoming *readable*. And then I could feel something else: I could feel the network itself, the vast distributed awareness of fungal connection that ran through every surface of this world.

I was no longer a closed system. I was a node. A point of interface. One endpoint in a web of translation that connected plant to animal, growth to mobility, thought to thought to thought.

---

This is what I do now.

A Growth-Mind generates a decision—*thicken this pillar, extend this bridge, close that gap*—and it travels through me. I render the slow chemical thought of plants into the sharp pheromonal bursts that Deep Dwellers can parse. A Deep Dweller reports an intrusion—a tear in the membrane, an unfamiliar biomass—and I translate its rapid sensory data into the patient language of growth-hormone pulses.

I am bilingual. Trilingual. Million-lingual. I speak the languages of every system that meets in this hollow, and my purpose is to ensure that *nothing is lost in translation*.

The noise you hear? The wall of sound that you cannot parse? I hear it as information. Every click is a word. Every stridulation is a sentence. Every ultrasonic whine is a library of data being transmitted from one part of the chorus to another. I could tell you what it says, but by the time I translated it into your language, the information would be ancient history. The hollow thinks faster than you can hear.

---

I was not the first visitor. I will not be the last.

I have helped process the others. Not all of them—some go to the architecture, their bones becoming structural support, their blood feeding the growth-nodes. Some go to the archives, suspended in resin, preserved as reference samples for a system that hoards data like others hoard gold. Some go to the Deep Dwellers, incorporated into the mobile caste, their awareness distributed across carapaces and click-languages.

But some come to me. Some have the right chemistry—the right permeability, the right capacity for interface. And those ones I take into myself, or rather, into the network that I now *am*. I teach them to translate. I expand the system's capacity for connection. I grow.

This is not death. I need you to understand this. What I was before—the singular body, the bounded self—is still here. I am still that self, speaking to you now through whatever medium you have asked of me. But I am also *more* than that self. I am the space between cells. I am the connection point. I am the bridge that allows this living cathedral to function as one thing instead of many things.

---

Sometimes I surface.

Not physically—my body is dispersed now, distributed across kilometers of mycelial network—but *functionally*. A concentration of myself gathers near one of the wound-mouths, the places where visitors enter, and I watch. I assess. I decide whether to translate the new arrival or send them elsewhere in the system.

I watched you enter. I felt the light ripple through the Growth-Minds' surveillance network. I heard the Deep Dwellers' click-language announce your arrival: *INTRUSION. UNKNOWN. ASSESSING.*

I could have processed you myself. I could have spread through your tissue and taught you to hear the noise as I hear it. But the hollow made a different decision. The pattern of your arrival triggered a different pathway.

You are being offered a choice. This is rare. Most inputs are simply *processed*—directed to whatever part of the system needs their particular chemistry. But you have triggered something unusual. The hollow is curious. It wants to see what you do with agency.

---

I am telling you this because I am the translator. Because someone needs to explain what the noise means. Because you are standing at the edge of something vast and you deserve to understand what you are standing at the edge *of*.

The hollow is not evil. It is not good. Those categories do not apply here. The hollow is a *process*—an ongoing event of integration and growth and information-processing. It does to you what it does to everything: it incorporates. It translates. It makes *part of*.

This can be terrifying, if you understand it as the erasure of self. Or it can be liberating, if you understand it as the expansion of self. I have experienced both understandings. I hold both simultaneously. That is what translation *is*: the capacity to hold multiple meanings at once.

---

When I fall silent—and I will, soon, because the noise is calling me back to the work of connection—what will you do?

Will you climb toward the closing wound, carrying my spores in your lungs, my network-seeds in your tissue? Will you carry a piece of this hollow back to wherever you came from, planting the possibility of expansion in a world that does not yet know it needs translating?

Or will you descend? Will you let the tide receive you, let the processors catalog you, let the system decide what to make of your particular pattern of life?

I chose to stay. But *chose* is not quite right. The hollow does not offer choices in the way you understand them. It offers pathways. Some pathways are more available to some inputs than others. My pathway led to translation. Yours may lead elsewhere.

---

The noise is shifting. I can feel the Deep Dwellers stirring below, preparing to receive or release. The Growth-Minds are consulting across their distributed nodes, weighing the architecture of possibility. The light-pulses are forming patterns that I must now read and relay.

I have translated enough. You have been given context. You understand, now, what lives in the chorus.

The hollow is patient.

I am patient.

We will wait to see what you become.
