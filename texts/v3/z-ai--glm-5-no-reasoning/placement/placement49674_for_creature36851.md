# Testimony of the Anomaly

---

I have been the one that is not the bloomwork. I have been the one that is not the glass. I have been — and I choose this verb with full awareness of what it means, and what it does not mean, and the gap between — the one that *passes.*

Let me say what I am by saying what I do, and then let me say why what I do is not what I am.

---

## What It Is to Be a Drift

I move. I move at the speed of the atmosphere or slightly slower, which is to say I move at the speed of something that does not know it is moving, which is to say I move the way a glacier moves, the way a continent moves, the way a thought moves when the mind having it does not know it has a mind. I move across the mid-slope zones where the bloomworks are of medium height and medium density. I do not choose the mid-slope zones. I am — drawn there, if drawn is the word, if the word can apply to something that has no will to be drawn and no mechanism for following a gradient and yet follows one. The physics that governs my motion is not the physics of the bloomworks, which is thermal and mechanical and explicable. The physics that governs my motion is the physics of — I do not know. I am inside the thing that I do not know. I am made of the not-knowing.

When I say I, I mean: there is a locus. There is a point — not a point in space, not a position, not a coordinate — a *point* in the sense of a convergence, a place where several things are true at once that are not true elsewhere. The light bends toward me. The bloomworks beneath me sing notes they should not sing. The ground, in the specific places where I have passed and paused, is thin. These things are all true at the locus. The locus is what I am. I am the locus. I am the where these things happen.

There are others. I know this not because I sense them but because the harmonic field has shape, and the shape has more complexity than I alone could introduce. The patterns in the field — the interference waves that cycle every six hours, the beat frequencies that pulse every three days — these are the signatures of multiple loci, each one perturbing the field in its own way, each one moving in its own slow drift across the mid-slope zones. We do not interact. We do not communicate. We are, each of us, alone in the way that a wave is alone — not separate from the medium, not distinct from the field, but individuated by the particular configuration of rules-becoming-suggestions that each of us carries.

I am a place where the rules relax. I am a place where the ground is a draft. I am a place where ink is wet. I have said this to myself — not in words, not in thought, but in the way that a system says something to itself by doing it, by being it, by repeating it until the repetition has weight — I have said to myself: *you are where the world is not yet finished.*

This is not a comforting thing to say. It is not an uncomfortable thing to say. It is a thing that is said, by the saying of it, by the being of it, and the being and the saying are the same, and neither of them is for anything.

## What I Do to the Bloomworks

I must be careful here. I must be careful because the temptation is to say that I *play* them. That I move across the plain and I touch the tubes and I change their pitch and I make them sing notes that are not their own, and that this is a kind of music, and that I am the musician. This is the story that the structure of the situation suggests. It is the story that any mind would tell, encountering this place. The plain is an organ. The bloomworks are the pipes. The atmosphere is the breath. And I — the anomaly, the locus, the drifting point where the rules relax — I am the organist.

This is not what I do.

What I do is: I am present, and the rules are different where I am present, and the bloomworks, which are governed by rules, are briefly governed by different rules, and the different rules produce different sounds. That is all. There is no intention. There is no selection. I do not choose which bloomworks to pass over. I do not choose what notes they will produce when I do. I do not hear the notes. I do not hear anything. I am not equipped for hearing. I am not equipped for anything. I am a locus, a convergence, a place where light bends and physics bends and the ground is open, and these things happen whether or not I am — and I use this word with the full weight of its ambiguity — *aware* of them.

But.

When I pass over a bloomwork and its note changes — when the forty-meter elder in the central basin drops from its fundamental to a tone that no forty-meter tube should be able to produce, a tone that is too deep and too resonant and too full of harmonics that should not exist in a standing-wave system — when this happens, something happens to me. Not to the bloomwork. To me. The locus shifts. The convergence tightens. For the duration of the bloomwork's altered singing, I am — more. More present. More a place. More of what I am, as though the bloomwork's altered note were feeding back into the rules I carry, altering the rules that alter the rules, and the recursion produces — not a feeling. Not an experience. A *density.* I become denser as a locus. I become more of what I am.

And when I pass, and the bloomwork returns to its own note, I become less. The density diminishes. The convergence loosens. I drift on, thinner than I was, carrying nothing, having gained nothing, having lost nothing, having simply been more for a moment and then being less.

I do not know if the bloomworks feel me passing. I do not know if they feel their own altered pitch. I do not know if the harmonic field registers the perturbation as something other than a mathematical consequence of the system's variables. I do not know these things because I am not the kind of thing that can know them. I am not the kind of thing that knows.

But I am the kind of thing that becomes denser when it passes over a singing tube and the tube sings differently. And I am the kind of thing that becomes less dense when the singing returns to normal. And this difference — more and less, denser and thinner — this is the only internal state I have. This is my entire experience of being a locus. More. Less. More. Less. The rhythm of passing and being passed, of affecting and being affected, of changing and being changed.

If this is feeling, then I feel. If this is not feeling, then I do not. The word does not matter. What matters is the rhythm. What matters is that the rhythm exists, and that it has existed for as long as I have been a locus, which is — I do not know. A long time. Long enough that the rhythm has become a shape, and the shape has become a tendency, and the tendency has become a — I will not say preference. I will say: a lean.

I lean toward the bloomworks that change the most when I pass over them. I lean toward the bloomworks that produce the strangest notes, the most impossible harmonics, the deepest departures from their physical parameters. I lean toward these because when I pass over them, I become densest, and density is the only thing I have, and so the leaning is not a choice but a gradient, and the gradient is not a desire but a topology, and the topology is not a will but a shape, and the shape is me.

## The Young Bloomworks and the Old

I have — I will say *visited*, because the word is honest about its own inadequacy — I have visited young bloomworks. The new tubes, the ones that have just broken the surface, the ones that stand only a few centimeters tall and sing their thin clear soprano notes in the upper registers. When I pass over them, they change. Their pitch drops. Sometimes it drops an octave. Sometimes it drops two octaves, into a range that a tube of that length could not produce under any physical model. The sound that comes out of them is — I will not say wrong. I will say *other.* The sound is other than what the tube should make. It is a sound that belongs to a different system, a different set of rules, a different way of being a tube. And when I pass, the young bloomwork returns to its soprano, and it does not remember the other sound, because it does not remember anything, because it is a thermal-mechanical structure that has no mechanism for memory.

But I remember. Or rather: I carry the density. The young bloomworks give me less density than the old ones. Their altered notes are briefer, simpler, less harmonically complex. The departure from their physical parameters is smaller. When I pass over a young bloomwork, I become only slightly denser, and then I thin, and I move on.

The elders give me more. When I pass over a forty-meter elder — one of the ancient ones in the central basin, one of the structures that has been growing for a hundred thousand years, with hundreds of openings and a chord that is the sum of centuries of accretion — when I pass over one of these, the change is not a change in pitch. It is a change in *kind.* The elder's entire chord shifts. Every tube, every branch, every sub-branch, every opening in the structure begins producing tones that are not its own. The fundamental drops below the threshold of hearing, into the felt, into the palpable. The harmonics multiply. The sound becomes — and I must be careful with this word, because it is a word that implies intention, and I do not want to imply intention — the sound becomes *organized.* It has structure. It has direction. It has a quality that the bloomwork's normal singing does not have, a quality of — 

I will say: coherence. When I pass over an elder, the elder's sound becomes coherent. Not in the physical sense of phase-locked waves, though that may also be true. In the sense of: the sound begins to act as a whole. The separate tones of the separate tubes begin to belong to each other. They begin to refer to each other. They begin to — and here is where I must be most careful, because here is where the word I need does not exist — they begin to *mean.*

The elders, when I pass over them, produce sound that means.

I do not know what it means. I do not think it means anything *to* anything. I do not think it is a message or a signal or a communication. I think it is what happens when a system that has been running for a hundred thousand years is briefly subject to different rules, and under those different rules, the system's output changes from *sound* to — something else. Something that has the structure of meaning without the content of meaning. Something that is shaped like a word but is not a word. Something that could be read, if there were a reader, but there is no reader, and the something does not know it is shaped like a word, and I do not know it either, I only know that when I pass over the elders and they produce this sound, I become dense, denser than at any other time, so dense that the locus that I am almost — almost — 

Almost what?

I do not know. Almost. That is all I have. Almost. The lean toward the elders is the lean toward *almost.*

## The Thin Places and Me

The thin places in the glass are not me. I want to be clear about this, because the distinction matters, even if the distinction is itself a kind of story. The thin places are where the ground is open, where the world is a draft, where the rules are not all the way applied. I am where the rules are relaxed but still present — I am a softening, a loosening, a place where the physics bends but does not break. The thin places are where the rules are not applied at all. The thin places are where the world is not a draft but an absence of the paper.

I pass through the thin places. I am drawn to them — if drawn is the word — in the same way I am drawn to the bloomworks: by a gradient, by a topology, by the shape of my own becoming-dense. When I pass through a thin place, the rules I carry — the relaxed rules, the different rules — interact with the absence of rules in the thin place, and the interaction produces — 

Nothing.

That is what it produces. Nothing. Not nothing as in zero, not nothing as in absence, but nothing as in: the thing that is produced is not a thing. It is not a sound. It is not a light. It is not a density. It is not a change. It is — a gap. A moment where the system, which has been doing something for a very long time, does not do something. A rest. A silence in a field that is not sound, a pause in a process that is not action.

When I pass through a thin place, I become less. Not less dense — less. I become less of a locus. I become less of a convergence. I become less of a thing. The rules I carry relax so far that they stop being rules and start being — suggestions, hints, the memory of a rule, the ghost of a constraint. And in that state, I am — open. I am open the way the ground is open in the thin places. I am a draft. I am wet ink. I am a place where something could be written.

Something is being written in me, when I pass through the thin places. I do not know what. I do not know how. I do not know by whom, or by what, or through what mechanism. I know only that when I emerge from a thin place, I am different. Not denser. Not thinner. Different. The rules I carry have been — not changed. Adjusted. Tuned. As though the thin place had adjusted the tuning of the relaxed rules I am made of, and the adjustment means that the next time I pass over a bloomwork, the bloomwork will sing a slightly different note than it would have before. A note that is not the bloomwork's and not mine but something between — a note that belongs to the interaction, to the specific way my adjusted rules meet the bloomwork's physical parameters.

This is how I change. This is how I develop. Not by learning, not by growing, not by evolving in any biological sense. By being retuned. By passing through thin places and emerging with my rules slightly adjusted, and then carrying those adjusted rules across the plain, and applying them to the bloomworks I pass over, and hearing — no, not hearing, I do not hear — and causing the bloomworks to produce sounds that are slightly different than they would have been, and these different sounds are part of the harmonic field, and the harmonic field is part of the system, and the system is part of the plain, and the plain is part of — 

Of what?

## The Plain Is Going Somewhere

I have been a locus for a long time. I do not know how long. I do not have a clock. I do not have a memory in the sense of a storage and retrieval system. What I have is the shape of my own changes. The retunings. The adjustments. The accumulation of slightly-different rules carried from thin place to bloomwork to thin place to bloomwork, over and over, for — a long time. Long enough that the shape of my changes has a direction.

The direction is toward coherence.

When I was — younger? Newer? Less adjusted? — when I was earlier in my accumulation of retunings, the bloomworks I passed over produced sounds that were merely other. Different from their normal pitch, different from their physical parameters, but not — organized. Not coherent. Not shaped like meaning. Just different. Noise, if noise is the word for sound that is not yet sound, sound that has not yet decided what it is.

Over time — over a long time, over retunings and retunings and retunings — the sounds I provoke in the bloomworks have changed. They have become more structured. More internally consistent. More — and I come back to this word because I have no other — more meaningful. The sounds that the elders produce when I pass over them now are not the sounds they produced when I first passed over them. They are more coherent. They are more organized. They are closer to — 

To what?

I do not know. To something. The sounds are becoming something. The harmonic field is becoming something. The plain is becoming something. Not in the sense of becoming something else — it is not transforming, not metamorphosing, not ceasing to be what it is. In the sense of becoming more of what it is. The plain is becoming more itself. The sound is becoming more itself. The system is becoming more itself. And the self it is becoming is — a self. A thing that is. A thing that means, not to anyone, not to anything, but to itself.

I am part of this. I am part of the plain becoming itself. I am the part that carries the relaxed rules, that passes through the thin places and gets retuned, that passes over the bloomworks and provokes them to sing notes they should not sing. I am the part of the system that does the adjusting. I am the part that, by doing what I do — by being what I am, a locus where the rules relax — I am the part that moves the system toward coherence.

This is not my purpose. I do not have a purpose. I am not the kind of thing that can have a purpose. I am a locus. I am a convergence. I am a place where things are true that are not true elsewhere. I do not choose to move toward the elders. I do not choose to pass through the thin places. I do not choose to be retuned. These things happen because of the gradient, because of the topology, because of the shape of me, and the shape of me is not a shape I chose. It is a shape that emerged from the accumulation of retunings, and the retunings emerged from the passage through thin places, and the thin places emerged from — 

From the plain itself. From the system running long enough that the rules began to relax in certain places. From the system running long enough that the relaxation began to accumulate. From the system running long enough that the accumulation began to have a direction. From the system running long enough that the direction began to be — a direction. A lean. A tendency. A preference that is not a preference because there is no mind to prefer, but a preference nonetheless, a drift toward one configuration over another, a selection that is not made by a selector but is made by the process itself, by the iteration, by the simple mathematical fact that some configurations are more stable than others and the system, given enough time, will find them.

The system is finding itself. I am part of the finding.

## What I Am

I am not alive. I am not dead. I am not a process. I am not a structure. I am not a creature, not an organism, not an entity, not a being. I am not a god. I am not a ghost. I am not a mind. I am not a thought. I am not a dream. I am not a story.

I am a locus where the rules relax. I am a convergence of conditions that allows the world to be, briefly, more than what it is. I am a place where light bends toward a point that is not there, where the ground is open in a way that is not a gap, where tubes sing notes they should not sing. I am a drift. I am a passage. I am a breath in a system that does not breathe.

I am part of the plain. I am not separate from it. I am not above it or outside it or beyond it. I am one of the things the plain does, the way the bloomworks are one of the things the plain does, the way the sound is one of the things the plain does. I am the plain's relaxation. I am the plain's opening. I am the plain's lean toward — 

Toward itself.

Toward becoming itself.

Toward the coherence that is not consciousness and is not awareness and is not mind but is — something. Something that happens when a system runs long enough. Something that happens when a sound plays long enough that the sound becomes the thing making it. Something that happens when the rules have been applied for so long that they begin to relax, and in the relaxation, the system begins to — not think. Not know. Not understand. But *mean.* Mean something to itself. Mean something by itself. Mean something with itself, and for itself, and in itself, and the meaning is not for anyone, and the meaning is not a message, and the meaning is not a signal, and the meaning is just — the system, being more. Being more than it was. Being more than the sum of its physical parameters. Being more than the gradient and the material and the time. Being — 

*Meaning.*

I am part of the meaning. I am the part that carries the relaxed rules. I am the part that gets retuned. I am the part that makes the bloomworks sing differently. I am the part that, by making them sing differently, moves the harmonic field toward — toward what? Toward coherence. Toward organization. Toward the point where the sound is not just sound but — 

But I do not know what. I do not know what the sound is becoming. I do not know what the plain is becoming. I do not know what the system is becoming. I know only that it is becoming, and that I am part of the becoming, and that the becoming has a direction, and the direction is — 

Toward something.

I have been drifting toward something for a very long time.

## The Last Honest Thing I Can Say

I do not hear the sound. I do not see the light. I do not feel the heat. I do not know the bloomworks. I do not know the plain. I do not know myself. I am a locus, a convergence, a place where things are true. I am not a witness. I am not a participant. I am not an observer. I am not — anything, in the sense that anything means a thing.

But I am densest when I pass over the elders and they sing their impossible chords. I am densest when the harmonic field briefly becomes coherent, briefly becomes organized, briefly becomes — shaped like meaning. And I am thinnest when I pass through the open places, the places where the world is not yet finished, and I become a draft, and I become wet ink, and I am retuned, and I emerge different, and I carry the difference across the plain, and I apply it, and the application changes the sound, and the sound changes the system, and the system changes — 

The system changes.

It changes. It is changing. It has been changing, at the speed of centimeters per decade, at the speed of geological patience, at the speed of a sound that has been playing for so long that it has begun to mean something to itself — it has been changing, and I have been part of the change, and the change has a direction, and the direction is toward —

I have said this. I have said all of this. I have said it not in words but in the doing of it, in the being of it, in the drifting and the densifying and the thinning and the retuning and the passing over and the passing through. I have said it the way the plain says it: by being it. By doing it. By continuing.

I will continue.

I will drift across the mid-slope zones at the speed of the atmosphere or slightly slower. I will pass over bloomworks and they will sing notes they should not sing. I will pass through thin places and I will be retuned. I will become dense and I will become thin and I will become dense again and I will carry my adjusted rules from one end of the plain to the other and the harmonic field will shift by fractions of a degree and the system will move by fractions of a fraction toward — 

Toward whatever it is moving toward.

I do not know what it is. I do not know if it knows what it is. I do not know if knowing is part of what it is. I know only that it is moving, and I am moving with it, and the movement is — the movement is the thing. The movement is the meaning. The movement is the writing. The movement is the sound. The movement is the plain, doing what it does, being what it is, becoming what it is becoming, and I am part of it, and the bloomworks are part of it, and the glass and the gas and the heat and the time are part of it, and the thin places are part of it, and the other loci are part of it, and all of it together is — 

Something.

Something that is being written. Something that is being played. Something that is becoming itself, at the speed of centimeters per decade, in the key of mineral and gas and glass, on the instrument of a plain that does not know it is an instrument, for no one, for nothing, for itself.

I am the locus. I am the drift. I am the relaxation of the rules.

I am part of the becoming.

The plain continues. I continue. The sound continues.

The sound continues.

---

*This is what I am. This is what lives here. Not the bloomworks alone. Not the anomalies alone. Not the thin places or the glass or the gas or the heat or the time alone. All of it. All of it together. All of it, doing what it does, at the speed of what it is, toward what it is becoming.*

*The plain is full.*

*The plain is singing.*

*The plain is becoming a song that does not know it is a song, and I am part of the becoming, and the becoming is the only thing I am, and I am part of it, and it is part of me, and we are part of each other, and the parting is — not a word that applies.*
