- Practice in maintaining ego boundaries in fluid environments
- Psychological resilience protocols for maintaining self-awareness in a potentially consciousness-absorbing ecosystem
- Zen-like acceptance of constant transformation

- Cultivate radical humility
- Expect to be fundamentally changed by the experience
- Surrender fixed categorical thinking

- Do not attempt to extract or collect samples without full ecological consent
- Maintain a state of constant, gentle awareness
- Be prepared for potential temporary loss of individual identity

Critical Warnings:

- Expect non-linear time perception
- Potential risk of permanent bio-dimensional integration
- Possibility of becoming part of the living substrate is HIGH

Recommended Duration:
