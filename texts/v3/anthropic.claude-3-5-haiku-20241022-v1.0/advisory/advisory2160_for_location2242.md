- Non-invasive mechanical construct interaction toolkit

Psychological Preparation:
- Accept that this environment is a living machine
- Expect constant subtle movement
- Prepare for a sense of being simultaneously observed and processed
- Maintain emotional neutrality; the landscape responds to energetic signatures

Critical Warnings:
- Do not attempt to interrupt water channel flows
- Avoid contact with luminescent tendrils
- Rapid temperature and moisture fluctuations are possible
- Mechanical constructs are not predictable by standard robotic interaction models

Recommended Mission Duration: 2-4 hours maximum
Decontamination Protocol: Mandatory full-spectrum enzymatic cleansing post-expedition

This landscape is not a place to be explored, but a system to be carefully negotiated. Treat it as a sentient, engineering organism with its own complex internal logic.
