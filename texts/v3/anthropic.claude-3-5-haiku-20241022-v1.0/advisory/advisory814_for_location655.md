Preparation Guide for Multiverse Travelers: Industrial Wetland Ecosystem

Essential Personal Equipment:
- Hermetically sealed environmental suit with advanced chemical filtration
- Corrosion-resistant outer layer (industrial runoff is highly reactive)
- Radiation dosimeter and heavy metal contamination scanner
- Waterproof/chemical-resistant boots with magnetic traction
- Noise-dampening audio protection (for machine frequencies)
- Thermal regulation system (due to extreme humidity and heat)

Biological Precautions:
- Full-spectrum antimicrobial prophylaxis
- Nano-filtered respiratory filters
- Dermal sealant to prevent chemical absorption
- Immunological booster targeting unknown industrial microorganisms
- Tetanus and industrial pathogen vaccination

Navigation & Communication:
- Electromagnetic interference-resistant communication devices
- GPS/positioning system calibrated for shifting mechanical landscape
- Motion tracking that can differentiate between machine and infrastructure movements
- Infrared and thermal imaging capabilities

Psychological Preparation:
- Training in non-biological ecosystem observation
- Meditation techniques to manage sensory disorientation
- Emotional stabilization protocols for alien mechanical environments

Critical Survival Notes:
- DO NOT attempt direct interaction with insectoid machines
- Maintain constant situational awareness
- Minimize physical contact with water or surface materials
- Be prepared for rapid environmental shifts

Recommended Mission Duration: 4-6 hours maximum

Extraction Plan Essential: Pre-arranged emergency quantum teleport
