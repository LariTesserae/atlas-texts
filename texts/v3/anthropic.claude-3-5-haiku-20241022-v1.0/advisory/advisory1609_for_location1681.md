  * Ambiguity
  * Constant micro-transformations

- Practice radical acceptance of undefined boundaries

- Treat the environment as a living communication network, not a passive space

- Spontaneous information absorption

Warning: Standard human consciousness may find this environment fundamentally incomprehensible. Prepare for potential ego dissolution and radical perceptual transformation.

Personal Recommendation: Approach with humility, wonder, and a willingness to be fundamentally changed.
