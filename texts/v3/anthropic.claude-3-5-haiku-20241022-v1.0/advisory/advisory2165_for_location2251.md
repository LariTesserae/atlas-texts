Psychological Preparation:

- Emergency Thermal Stabilization: Portable heat generation units

Additional Considerations:

Critical Warnings:

- Maintain constant system diagnostics
- Be prepared for unexpected organic-mechanical mutations
- Respect the living nature of the terrain - it is observing you as much as you are observing it
