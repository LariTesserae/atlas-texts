- Develop tolerance for near-total silence
- Mental flexibility for blurred biological/mechanical boundaries

SURVIVAL STRATEGIES:

- Move with extreme precision and minimal disturbance
- Observe rather than interact
- Treat environment as living, interconnected system
- Maintain absolute environmental neutrality

RECOMMENDED EXPEDITION DURATION:
- Maximum 48 hours
- Shorter periods preferable
- Continuous physiological monitoring required

Approach with absolute respect and scientific humility.
