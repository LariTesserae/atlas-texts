  * Withstanding constant low-frequency vibrations
  * Allowing molecular-level information exchange

  * Preventing unintended biological merging with environment

- Neurological recalibration strongly recommended before entry

  * Light pulses
  * Molecular vibrations
  * Thermal shifts
  * Liquid pressure gradients
- Psychological preparation essential: this is not an "observation" space, but a living, generative system that will attempt communication

- Quantum entanglement translator
- Molecular resonance mapper
- Electromagnetic field stabilizer

- Exit protocols must be precise to prevent accidental biological integration

MENTAL PREPARATION:
- Cultivate a state of open receptivity
- Suppress linear thinking patterns
- Develop capacity for non-hierarchical perception
- Practice radical acceptance of emergence and transformation

SURVIVAL PROBABILITY:
- With proper preparation: 62%
- Without preparation: near-zero

Recommended mission duration: 7-12 quantum minutes (subjective time may vary)
