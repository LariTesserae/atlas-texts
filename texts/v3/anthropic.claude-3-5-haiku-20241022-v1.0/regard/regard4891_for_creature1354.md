Not by adding anything, but by creating conditions of enhanced permeability—allowing the moss networks to expand their already exquisite sensing capabilities. Imagine their chemical-hydraulic intelligence gaining even more nuanced environmental translation skills.

There's something here about recognition. Not intervention, but profound, cellular-level acknowledgment. The way one sentient system might softly nod to another, saying: "I witness your unique intelligence. Continue."

The artifacts—stone flakes, bone fragments—would be understood not as archaeological remnants, but as temporary inscriptions in an ongoing conversation. Momentary punctuation marks in the landscape's long, slow narrative.

The wise power would understand: this is not a dead or dormant place, but a thinking entity. Slow. Patient. Profoundly alive.

Their ultimate gesture might be simply this: to ensure continued uninterrupted becoming.
