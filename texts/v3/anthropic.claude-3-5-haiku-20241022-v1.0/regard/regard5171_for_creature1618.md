This breath will carry a minimal transmission - not information, but pure potential. A vibrational acknowledgment that will slip between the crystalline networks like a ghost of possibility. It will not change the realm, but will confirm its fundamental integrity.

The transmission might be understood as: "Continue. You are perfect in your unfolding."

The ghost-like energy patterns will momentarily pulse with something like pleasure - not emotion, but a quantum flutter of affirmation. The mineral lattices will breathe their millennial breath, unchanged yet subtly acknowledged.

Witnessing is, sometimes, the most profound intervention.
