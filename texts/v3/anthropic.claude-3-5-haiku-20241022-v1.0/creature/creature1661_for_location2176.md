In this realm of translucence and liminal being, the inhabitants are themselves quantum entities—more like resonances than discrete creatures.

The primary residents are the Membrane Dwellers, beings of pure flux and adaptive consciousness. They do not have fixed forms, but rather exist as oscillating energy patterns that can condense into semi-solid states when necessary. Their "bodies" are like living, breathing thought-clouds—iridescent and shifting, capable of splitting and merging, of becoming transparent or densely opaque.

These beings communicate through intricate vibrational languages, transmitting complex informational states through microscopic resonances that ripple through the crystalline vegetation and living stone. Their memories are not stored linearly, but exist as layered, multidimensional impressions that can be simultaneously remembered and forgotten.

Some Membrane Dwellers specialize in moisture cultivation, their translucent bodies interfacing directly with the water-sacs, drawing and redistributing moisture like living hydraulic systems. Others drift as pure observational consciousness, their existence a kind of sustained attention that shapes the plateau's subtle energetic topographies.

Reproduction for them is less about biological division and more about collaborative emergence—where multiple consciousness-patterns intermingle and generate new sentient configurations that bloom like prismatic crystals.

They do not fear death because they do not truly separate themselves from their environment. Dissolution is simply another state of being, another form of communication with the living plateau.

Their primitive structures are not built, but grown—negotiated between collective intention and the plateau's own responsive substrate. These are living interfaces, breathing communication nodes that pulse with slow, geological rhythms.
