In this landscape, the inhabitants are not creatures in any traditional sense, but dynamic energy-assemblages that emerge and dissolve with the terrain itself. They are:

Pulse-Entities: Semi-solid forms that flicker between mechanical and wave states, their bodies composed of electromagnetic frequencies and momentary material configurations. They move like electrical impulses - sometimes sharp and angular, sometimes fluid and diffuse. Their "skin" is a shifting membrane of metallic resonance, absorbing and radiating information through complex thermodynamic exchanges.

These beings are less individuals than localized manifestations of the landscape's own metabolic processes. They generate sounds - not communication, exactly, but vibrational signatures that resonate through the membrane-surface. Their movements create temporary rivers of molten data, momentary structural transformations that ripple across the continental circuit.

Some pulse-entities are massive - kilometers-wide aggregations of mechanical and energetic flow, generating their own atmospheric zones. Others are tiny, almost molecular: quick flickerings that dart between massive infrastructure-growths like neutrinos through dense matter.

Their "reproduction" is more like a constant reconfiguration - splitting, merging, dissolving into new forms. They don't have fixed identities, but are continuous modulations of the landscape's own becoming. Their boundaries are permeable, their existence a process of constant reformatting.

When they intersect, they create complex harmonic interactions - like industrial music played through planetary speakers, generating localized climate shifts and momentary structural reorganizations.

These are not biological creatures, but living technological events - part machine, part geological process, part pure energy pattern.
