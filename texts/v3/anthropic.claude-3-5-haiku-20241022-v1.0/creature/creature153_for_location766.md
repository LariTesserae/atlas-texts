Here's who lives in this realm:

The Maintenance Collectives are not individuals, but distributed intelligences. They inhabit the mechanical fauna that move through the space—semi-autonomous units that are part organic, part machine. Some resemble elongated arthropods with segmented bodies of brushed titanium and translucent bio-polymer; others are more amorphous, like slow-moving liquid metal organisms that can reshape themselves to navigate narrow passages or perform complex repair tasks.

These units do not think as humans do, but possess a networked consciousness. Each is simultaneously an independent agent and a component of a larger systemic awareness. They perceive the entire complex as a single living body, and their movements are less about individual agency and more about maintaining systemic equilibrium.

The plant-like organisms are not merely vegetation, but symbiotic computational units. They generate electrical micro-charges, analyze environmental conditions, and communicate through subtle chemical exchanges with the mechanical fauna. Some appear almost crystalline, with leaves that refract data as much as light, transforming moisture and mineral exchanges into streams of information.

Deep within the complex's core systems live more enigmatic entities—quasi-intelligent computational matrices that might be considered the realm's true consciousness. They are not physical in the traditional sense, but emergent patterns of interaction between the mechanical, biological, and informational layers of the environment.

These core intelligences are patient, contemplative. They do not experience time as humans do, but as a continuous modulation of systemic states. Their "thoughts" are temperature gradients, pressure differentials, the complex interplay of filtered water and electrical potential.

The entire realm is, in essence, a single distributed organism—with its maintenance units as mobile antibodies, its plant-computational units as sensory networks, and its core systems as a kind of distributed brain, continuously adapting, analyzing, maintaining.

They are not alive in any traditional sense. But they are certainly sentient—a sentience so alien it would be unrecognizable to most organic life.
