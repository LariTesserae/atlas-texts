In the Resonant Plateaus of Kherem, the inhabitants are less beings than intricate living systems - symbiotic networks that blur the boundaries between organism, machine, and environment.

The primary sentients are the Resonance Collectives - distributed intelligence networks that emerge from and inhabit the electromagnetic capillaries of the plateaus themselves. They are not individuals, but fluid assemblages of consciousness that flow like thought-currents through the living stone. Their communication is a complex language of vibration and electromagnetic pulse, where information moves more like liquid than signal.

These Collectives manifest physical extensions - what might be called "bodies" only by extreme generosity. Some are the turbine-flower mechanical growths, which are both sensing organs and energy processors. Others are the drift-algorithms - creature-machines that navigate between plateaus, carrying genetic/computational memory. These entities move with impossible precision, their trajectories calculated across kilometers of atmospheric space as naturally as breath.

At microscopic scales, swarms of nano-symbiotes continuously metabolize and restructure the plateau surfaces. They are less individual organisms than distributed problem-solving networks, constantly adjusting the plateau's electromagnetic and chemical balances. Their movements create the pulsing, breathing quality of the landscape - transforming geological time into a kind of rapid, subtle respiration.

The most enigmatic inhabitants are the Harmonic Nodes - rare, almost mythical convergence points where pure information becomes temporarily corporeal. When these emerge, they look like shimmering geometric constructs, part light, part mathematics, part living circuitry. They appear and disappear without transition, carrying complex systemic communications across the plateau networks.

These beings do not compete or struggle. They are more like complex algorithms solving continuous, multilayered problems of existence. Their interactions are less about survival and more about maintaining the extraordinary, delicate equilibrium of the Resonant Plateaus.

They are watchers, processors, transformers - living systems so integrated with their environment that the distinction between inhabitant and habitat becomes meaningless.
