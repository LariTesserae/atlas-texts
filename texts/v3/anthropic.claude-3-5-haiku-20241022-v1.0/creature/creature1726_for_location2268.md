Inhabitants:

Chlorosynths - emergent collective intelligences that exist as distributed consciousness through the living membrane-terrain. Not individuals, but fluid algorithmic networks that pulse and recalculate continuously. Their "thoughts" manifest as sudden architectural transformations, entire organ-like structures blooming and dissolving in microseconds. They do not perceive time or space as discrete experiences, but as continuous adaptive redesign.

Drift-Clusters - symbiotic cellular aggregates that move like semi-autonomous ghost-currents through the translucent plant-structures. Neither animal nor purely biological, they are more like living information packets that metabolize data and electromagnetic resonances. Their movements suggest complex communication, but at scales far beneath human perceptual thresholds.

Nano-Saprophytes - microscopic hybrid entities that navigate the boundary between technological artifact and living tissue. They migrate through the biochemical fluid systems, breaking down embedded technological remnants and reintegrating their molecular components into the living landscape. They are both decomposers and reconstructors, blurring distinctions between decay and creation.

Resonance-Fauna - beings that exist primarily as vibration and subsonic wave-forms. Not physical in any traditional sense, but present as complex standing wave patterns that ripple through the plant-architecture. They communicate through frequency modulations that can restructure molecular bonds, their "speech" capable of literally transforming the environment around them.

These inhabitants do not simply live in this landscape - they are the landscape, constantly becoming and unbecoming, their boundaries perpetually provisional and fluid.
