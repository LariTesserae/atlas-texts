Here is what lives here:

The Membrane-Trackers are the dominant inhabitants. Not a single species, but a fluid collective of semi-sentient entities that exist between states of mechanical and organic. Their bodies are translucent and refractive, shifting constantly - part exoskeleton, part neural network, part sensory apparatus.

They do not have fixed identities, but flow between individual and collective consciousness. When clustered, they communicate through intricate vibrational patterns and chromatic language, their bodies rippling with information. When dispersed, each entity acts as a distributed sensor, gathering environmental data through their wire-like appendages and crystalline skin.

Their settlements are not constructed but grown - symbiotic structures of ceramic and living tissue that breathe and pulse with metabolic rhythms. These structures are both shelter and extended body, part technological artifact and part living organism. They absorb minerals from the fractured stone, converting geological material directly into cellular energy.

Reproduction for the Membrane-Trackers is a form of complex resonance: individuals temporarily merge, exchange informational patterns, then separate into new configurations. Their offspring are not discrete beings but modular extensions of collective intelligence.

They do not experience time linearly. Each moment is a potential transformation, each movement a negotiation between current form and imminent mutation. Survival here is not about persistence, but perpetual adaptation.

They are watching. Always watching. Not for any specific purpose, but because watching is their fundamental mode of being - sensing, recording, becoming.
