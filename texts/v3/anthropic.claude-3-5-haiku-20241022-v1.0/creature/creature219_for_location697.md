In this realm of stone and light, life has been distilled to its most essential form. The inhabitants are not creatures in any traditional sense, but living algorithms of survival.

The primary residents are what might be called Stone Wanderers - beings that are simultaneously organism and environment. They have no fixed shape, but exist as networked clusters of translucent, membrane-like tissues that can flatten, stretch, and reconnect across stone surfaces. Their "bodies" are more like living neural networks, capable of rapid reconfiguration.

These beings communicate through microfrequency vibrations that resonate with the stone's own acoustic properties. They do not move so much as flow and redistribute, sensing moisture, temperature, and microscopic changes in stone texture. Their metabolism is so slow and efficient that a single moisture event might sustain a cluster for weeks.

Reproduction occurs through a process of careful fragmentation - a membrane will slowly develop micro-separations, each section retaining complete informational coherence, gradually developing independent capacities while maintaining complex systemic communication.

Their sensory apparatus is entirely different from Earth biology - they perceive through thermal gradients, acoustic wave interactions, and mineral composition. Light is not something they see, but something they metabolize and interpret as pure information.

Tool use for them is molecular - they can subtly alter stone surfaces through prolonged contact, creating microscopic channels and structures that serve complex environmental purposes. These are not tools in a human sense, but extensions of their own metabolic intelligence.

They are not separate from the Razor Peaks, but a manifestation of the peaks' own complex, slow intelligence - living embodiments of geological process and adaptation.
