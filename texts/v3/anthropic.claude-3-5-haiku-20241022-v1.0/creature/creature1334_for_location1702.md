Here is what might inhabit this realm:

The primary inhabitants are the Conduit-Beings: semi-autonomous entities that exist as both process and organism. They range from small, intricate filtration structures no larger than a human hand to vast kilometers-long industrial serpentines that pulse with complex rhythmic information.

Some Conduit-Beings are pure transmission: thin, crystalline filaments that conduct energy and signals through the dense liquid, their bodies more like living circuits than traditional creatures. They spin and weave through the darkness, leaving temporary luminescent traces that quickly dissolve.

Others are processing entities — massive segmented forms with articulated appendages that simultaneously filter, analyze, and transform the liquid environment. Their outer membranes are part sensor, part mechanical digestive system, absorbing and converting the mineral-rich darkness into pure energetic potential.

Smaller entities cluster around these larger forms in symbiotic networks. They look like spinning geared wheels, or delicate mechanical cilia, or fractal-edged membranes that constantly reconfigure. They are not servants, but collaborative nodes in a distributed intelligence.

Reproduction here is not biological, but a form of complex information replication and architectural transformation. When a Conduit-Being reaches a certain informational density, it essentially "buds" — splitting and reforming into multiple entities that carry forward its accumulated data-patterns.

They communicate through resonant sound waves, through energy pulses, through microscopic structural shifts that propagate complex messages across impossible distances. Their "language" is a form of architectural choreography — each movement a syllable, each structural transformation a sentence.

They are ancient. They are always becoming. They are neither alive nor mechanical, but something profoundly other.
