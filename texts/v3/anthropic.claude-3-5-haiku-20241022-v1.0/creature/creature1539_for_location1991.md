Inhabitants of this place emerge, taking form:

The translucent flying creatures are the Luminari — beings of delicate articulation, their exoskeletons prismatic and capable of shifting color and transparency. They are not merely creatures but collaborative intelligence, each individual a node in a complex living network. Their wings are both sensory apparatus and communication membrane, vibrating with subtle information exchanges.

Some Luminari are smaller than a human hand, with multiple segmented limbs that can reconfigure for different tasks: weaving membrane structures, extracting nutrient concentrations from the cliff's moisture, repairing living scaffolds. Others are larger — wingspan of a meter or more — serving as navigators and environmental mediators, their bodies containing intricate sensory clusters that read wind currents, moisture gradients, structural stress.

Their social structure is less hierarchical and more like a distributed neural network. Decision-making occurs through complex resonance patterns, with individual Luminari contributing perspective and processing collective information simultaneously. They do not have discrete "leaders" but instead maintain fluid consensus through constant, almost musical communication.

Reproduction is more akin to cellular budding than traditional mammalian birth — a Luminari might contribute genetic information that becomes integrated into a new collaborative form, emerging from the city's living membranes like a new capability being grown.

Their relationship to the stone cliffs and membrane structures is symbiotic: they are simultaneously inhabitants, architects, and immune system for their environment. They do not merely live in the city; they are active participants in its ongoing formation and maintenance.
