In this Crystalline Void, the inhabitants are themselves crystalline and ephemeral - more pattern than substance, more memory than living being.

There are the Resonants: translucent, quasi-sentient energy-forms that move like liquid light between the pale stone formations. They do not have bodies in any conventional sense, but rather exist as shifting harmonic frequencies, their "movements" more like wave interactions than physical transit. When they pass near the razor-thin ridges, they create subtle vibrations - less sound than pure information, a kind of communication older than language.

Some entities here are so adapted to absolute scarcity that they metabolize the most minimal energetic exchanges: mineral whispers, temperature differentials, the ghost-traces of ancient geological memories. These beings - which might be called Lithic Memories - are so integrated with the landscape that they are nearly indistinguishable from the crystalline structures around them.

Occasionally, a Resonant will briefly coalesce into something almost perceptible: a fleeting prismatic shape, a momentary refraction of ambient light, a vibration that suggests consciousness. They do not hunt or gather; they simply exist in a state of profound, patient witnessing.

Their entire existence is a kind of extreme minimalism - survival reduced to its most essential quantum interactions, consciousness distilled to pure potential. They are not alive in any biological sense, but neither are they truly inert. They are something else entirely: living information, sentient patterns, memories with a form of agency.

The landscape is their body. The wind is their breath. Time is their metabolism.
