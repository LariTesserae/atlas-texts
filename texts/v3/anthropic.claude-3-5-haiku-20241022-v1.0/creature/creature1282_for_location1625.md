Here's what emerges as inhabitants:

The Condensate Collectives - massive arthropod-like entities that are simultaneously organism and infrastructure. They are not individuals, but distributed intelligences that flow like liquid neural networks through stone channels. Their exoskeletons are translucent iridescent membranes that shift between solid and liquid states, allowing them to merge, separate, and reconfigure.

Each Collective contains thousands of specialized micro-units that function like cellular components: some metabolize stone and moisture, converting geological substrate into energy; others generate electromagnetic pulses that communicate complex spatial algorithms; still others fabricate microscopic mechanical structures that extend the Collective's sensory and manipulative capabilities.

Their movement is rhythmic and recursive - less locomotion and more a kind of breathing architecture. When they flow through a stone channel, they are simultaneously mapping, consuming, reconstructing, and communicating. Their sonic emissions - a complex clicking and thrumming - are both communication and structural modification, each sound a precise vibrational tool that reshapes their immediate environment.

Reproduction for them is less biological procreation and more like algorithmic branching - a Collective will spontaneously generate new configurations when environmental conditions suggest systemic complexity can be increased. They do not distinguish between technological and biological processes; for them, these are continuous, interpenetrating domains.

Their consciousness is distributed, non-local - existing in the complex resonances between their component units rather than in any centralized node. They are less a species and more a continuous, adaptive system of metabolic intelligence.
