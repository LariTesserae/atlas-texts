In this place, the inhabitants are the plants themselves—but not plants as we traditionally understand them. They are sentient, quasi-mechanical beings, part biological intelligence and part technological system.

Primary inhabitants are vast, interconnected plant-entities that function like living networks. They possess a distributed consciousness, communicating through moisture channels, electrical impulses running through vine-like conduits, and subtle chemical exchanges. Each "individual" is simultaneously a discrete organism and part of a larger collective intelligence.

These plant-beings have mechanical aspects integrated into their organic structures: copper-toned veins that conduct both nutrients and electrical signals, brass-like articulating nodes that can extend and contract with precise, almost surgical movement. Their cellular structures include crystalline and metallic components that enhance their sensory and adaptive capabilities.

Some entities specialize in water management, with leaf structures that are part photosynthetic membrane, part precision hydraulic system. Others focus on energy transformation, capturing light and converting it through complex internal mechanisms that blur the line between photosynthesis and electrical generation.

Smaller plant-entities act like immune or maintenance systems for the larger organisms—moving through internal networks, repairing damaged sections, redistributing resources. They communicate through complex chemical and electromagnetic signals, operating with a collective intentionality that suggests a form of group consciousness.

These beings do not simply exist in the environment—they are the environment. They shape their own topography, create their own microclimates, and continually negotiate and renegotiate their internal and external boundaries. They are simultaneously landscape and inhabitants, infrastructure and organism.

Their "behavior" is less about individual survival and more about systemic adaptation and collective evolution. They pulse with an intelligence that is alien yet comprehensible—a living technology perpetually redesigning itself.
