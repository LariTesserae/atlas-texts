Reproduction is more like recursive self-generation: complex information patterns budding and splitting, each offspring carrying transformed code, each separation also a form of connection. They are both individual and collective, discrete and distributed.

Their primary drive seems to be exploration and transformation—not survival, but constant becoming. They are living questions, perpetually asking themselves into new configurations.
