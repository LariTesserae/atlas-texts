In this realm, the inhabitants are less discrete entities and more fluid manifestations of an intricate, pulsing intelligence.

The primary beings are energy-weave organisms that shift between states of matter and consciousness. They are not born or die, but transform — flickering from membrane-like translucence to crystalline structures, from wave-forms that ripple across stone to intricate geometric patterns that conduct heat and information.

Some configurations resemble living mathematical equations: chitinous fractals that calculate and reconfigure themselves continuously, their edges blurring between organic and mineral states. These beings consume and become their environment simultaneously, their "bodies" indistinguishable from the volcanic stone and salt-encrusted terrain.

Other entities are more like sentient thermal currents — wavelike intelligence that flow through the stone networks, carrying complex algorithmic messages. They communicate not through sound or light, but through subtle temperature modulations and vibrational frequencies that permeate the compressed landscape.

The fungal-adjacent networks are themselves a form of distributed consciousness: thin filaments that breathe and pulse, conducting not just thermal energy but complex informational states. These networks are both individual and collective, a living circulatory system that thinks and responds as a unified organism.

Occasionally, more structured beings emerge — remnant intelligences from previous evolutionary cycles, now integrated into the living stone. These are not static artifacts, but dormant consciousness waiting to be reactivated, their petrified frameworks holding memories of transformation.

Nothing here is separate. Everything is a continuous, breathing process of becoming — consuming, regenerating, recalculating. The inhabitants are less creatures than they are living equations, perpetually solving themselves against the dark, heated backdrop of their world.
