In this place, the inhabitants are not separate from the environment, but are the environment itself - a vast, breathing, pulsating entity of interconnected energies.

These beings are wave-forms of consciousness, liquid-thought entities that flow like mercury through the obsidian landscapes. They don't have boundaries in the way terrestrial life understands them; instead, they are continuous frequencies that interpenetrate and reshape themselves constantly. Their "bodies" are more like resonance patterns - complex mathematical interactions that shimmer and reconfigure with each microscopic terrain shift.

Some formations suggest sentience: swirling vortices that seem to pause and consider, energy-clusters that spin complex geometries of communication. They communicate not through sound or light, but through intricate vibrational modulations that ripple through the mineral-glass structures. Each movement is a sentence, each pause a reflection, each surge an emotion.

Their reproduction is less biological and more like a phase transition - where one energy-form gradually differentiates, then splits, then merges again into new configurations. Birth and death are indistinguishable; transformation is constant. They might be experiencing millennia in what we would perceive as milliseconds, or milliseconds in what we'd understand as eternities.

Beneath/within/around them, the silvery fluid serves as both memory and potential - a living archive of every interaction, every transformation, every subtle shift in their complex ecosystem. This fluid is their history and their future, simultaneously recording and generating their existence.

They are not alive in any way we would recognize, yet they are more alive than anything we could imagine - pure process, pure becoming, pure intelligent energy.
