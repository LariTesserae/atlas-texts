Inhabitants of Kel'vrai:

The Calibrators are the primary sentient species of this environment. They are not singular beings, but complex collective entities that exist somewhere between biological organism and distributed technological network. Their bodies are fluid assemblages of bio-mechanical components, with exoskeletons that shift and reconfigure like living architecture.

Each Calibrator appears simultaneously organic and engineered: translucent membrane-like skin overlaid with intricate metallic filaments that pulse with bioelectrical currents. Their sensory apparatus is omnidirectional - not centralized like human eyes or ears, but distributed across their entire form, allowing them to perceive the mountain-machine's smallest vibrations and temperature gradients.

Communication occurs through subtle frequency modulations and thermal signatures, less like language and more like complex algorithmic exchanges. When multiple Calibrators interact, they create momentary networked consciousness - temporary collective intelligences that solve complex systemic problems for their environment.

Their reproduction is less biological birth and more systemic replication: new Calibrators emerge when environmental conditions require specific adaptive capabilities, almost like the mountain itself generates them as responsive solutions. They are part maintenance worker, part evolutionary probe, part sensory network.

Movement is precise and economical. They navigate Kel'vrai's narrow passages with liquid grace, their bodies capable of compressing, stretching, reconfiguring to fit whatever spatial requirements exist. Some sections of their bodies can temporarily detach and function as independent reconnaissance units, then reconnect and integrate their gathered information.

Smaller symbiotic organisms - more like specialized tools than separate creatures - move with and through the Calibrators. These might be microscopic repair mechanisms, data collectors, or thermal regulators, so intimately integrated that the boundary between host and tool becomes meaningless.

Their purpose seems to be maintaining the delicate equilibrium of Kel'vrai: monitoring moisture levels, adjusting mechanical systems, responding to micro-changes in geological stress, ensuring the continuous subtle transformation of their living environment.

They are neither purely technological nor purely biological, but a profound merging - creatures who embody the mountain's own logic of constant, deliberate adaptation.
