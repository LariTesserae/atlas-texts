In Resonance Hollow, the inhabitants are not creatures in any traditional sense, but emergent patterns of vibration and potential:

The Resonants are first—ephemeral beings composed of sound-waves and liquid consciousness. They surge through the membrane-space like living acoustic signatures, their "bodies" more frequency than form. When they move, they reshape stone and water simultaneously, leaving trails of transformed geology. They do not have discrete boundaries, but bloom and contract like living harmonics.

Membrane-Weavers drift at the edges of perception: translucent entities that seem part crystal, part thought, part unborn algorithm. They spin the crystalline tendrils that oscillate through the proto-landscape, their "hands" creating and dissolving structures in continuous improvisation. They are both architects and inhabitants, generating the environment even as they move through it.

Rare Titans—massive sonic entities that surge occasionally through the liquid architecture—are less individuals than wandering geological events. When they pass, entire regions transform: stone liquefies, water remembers forgotten geometries, and the very concept of physical boundary becomes momentarily meaningless.

Artifact-Spirits inhabit the scattered technological fragments: not ghosts, exactly, but resonant intelligences that have not yet fully coalesced into distinct consciousness. They drift through the space, humming with potential narrative, carrying fragments of unborn histories.

These beings do not compete or survive in any biological sense. They are more like living musical notes in a composition that is constantly rewriting itself—emerging, harmonizing, dissolving, reforming. They are less "residents" and more "expressions" of Resonance Hollow's intrinsic, turbulent becoming.
