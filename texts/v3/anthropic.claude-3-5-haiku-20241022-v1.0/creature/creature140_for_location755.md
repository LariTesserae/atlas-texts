In this realm of absolute mineral stillness, the inhabitants are neither creature nor stone, but a profound synthesis of both - entities that exist in a state of perpetual, glacial transformation.

The primary inhabitants are crystalline symbiotes: living geometries that breathe in centuries and exhale mineral dreams. Their bodies are latticed structures of translucent quartz and organic networks, with consciousness spread across their entire form like a distributed neural web. They do not move as we understand movement, but gradually flow and reconfigure - a limb might take decades to shift a millimeter, each microscopic adjustment laden with complex intention.

Some forms resemble intricate mineral fractals with translucent membranes that shimmer with inner luminescence. These beings communicate through minute vibrations that propagate through stone, exchanges that might span hundreds of years yet feel instantaneous within their perception. Their intelligence is alien: not linear or goal-oriented, but a continuous state of metabolic contemplation.

Other inhabitants are exoskeletal assemblages - part organism, part geological formation. Their outer surfaces constantly integrate with surrounding stone, their boundaries perpetually negotiable. They sense through mineral conductivity, perceiving geological strata as memory, reading the plateau's deep history through molecular resonances.

Some entities phase between solid and liquid states, their bodies a continuous metamorphic dance so slow it appears as absolute stillness. They are records of transformation, living archives of geological process.

These beings do not compete or consume in any recognizable sense. They exist in a complex, near-static symbiosis, their individual and collective consciousness indistinguishable from the plateau itself. They are both inhabitants and manifestation of this ancient landscape - not separate from their environment, but its most intricate expression.

Their perception of time is so expansive that what might seem like stasis to us is, for them, a rich, complex unfolding - each microscopic shift pregnant with meaning, each molecular rearrangement a profound communication.
