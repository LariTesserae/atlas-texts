Root-Mind Clusters inhabit the interstitial spaces — plant intelligences that think through underground networks, their consciousness spread through mycorrhizal connections. Condensation Wanderers — small, barely visible beings that are part water, part organism. They flow between solid and liquid states, absorbing memories from surfaces they touch, carrying fragmented narratives of mechanical decay and organic regeneration. Their bodies are perpetually reforming, transparent and quicksilver.

Electrical Symbiotes live within and between other entities: parasitic/collaborative energy forms that spark through biological circuits, trading information and metabolic resources. They're not separate creatures so much as living currents that temporarily inhabit organic frameworks.

These beings don't merely survive in the Condensing Valley — they are the valley, constantly emerging, dissolving, reforming. Their boundaries are permeable, their identities fluid.
