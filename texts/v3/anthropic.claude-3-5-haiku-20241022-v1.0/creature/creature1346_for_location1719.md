In this Viridian Manufactory, the inhabitants are themselves hybrid creatures, living interfaces between organic and mechanical domains:

The Weavers are first. Thin, almost translucent beings with multiple limbs that blend chitinous joint-mechanics with living tissue. Their bodies are part root, part servo-mechanism - they move through the plant-scaffolding like liquid silver, constantly maintaining, adjusting, repairing. Their sensory apparatus is distributed across their entire form, each tendril a sensor, each membrane a processing node. They do not distinguish between maintenance and metabolism; their very existence is continuous adjustment.

Luminescent Collectors drift through the upper membranes - balloon-like organisms with transparent skin that captures and redistributes energy. They are simultaneously data carriers and living solar collectors, their internal structures a complex latticework of bio-photovoltaic tissue. When they move, they leave momentary traces of electrical potential, soft blue-green afterglows that pulse and fade.

Deep in the root networks live the Substrate Managers - compact, multi-legged creatures whose bodies are a dense neural network, capable of reading and redirecting hydraulic and electrical impulses. They look like a cross between centipedes and intricate circuit boards, their movements creating micro-adjustments in the entire system's flow.

Smaller symbiotes dart between these primary inhabitants: microscopic repair organisms, information couriers, living data packets that flow like blood through the system's capillaries. They are so integrated that they might be better understood as functional components rather than discrete beings.

None of these entities would describe themselves as separate from their environment. They are not inhabitants, but expressions - momentary configurations of an ongoing, breathingly complex process of transformation.
