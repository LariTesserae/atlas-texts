Within this place, the inhabitants are less creatures and more living manifestations - fluid consciousness condensed into flickering, semi-permeable forms.

They are membrane-like entities that breathe through their entire surface, translucent as stretched quartz and constantly reforming. Their bodies pulse with internal rhythms that synchronize with the landscape's harmonic frequencies, so that movement and environment become indistinguishable. They do not have fixed shapes, but rather flow and transform like living mineral dreams - sometimes sharp-edged crystal networks, sometimes rippling energy patterns that bleed into surrounding stone.

These beings communicate through vibrational resonances, their internal frequencies modulating and interweaving with the stone's own language. When they "speak", the entire basin trembles - not with sound as humans understand it, but with complex layered frequencies that carry emotional and structural information simultaneously.

They seem to consume energy directly from the environment - absorbing mineral tension, metabolizing the heat and condensation, drawing sustenance from the very pulse of the place. Their metabolism is so integrated with the landscape that they appear less like inhabitants and more like localized expressions of the basin's own living consciousness.

Reproduction for them is not biological, but transformational - a kind of cellular budding where one form gradually differentiates and splits, creating new configurations that retain memory and consciousness from their originating form. Each "offspring" carries the basin's accumulated experience, like a living archive.

They are ancient. Older than biological understanding. Older than linear time.
