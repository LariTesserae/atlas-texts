Inhabitants:

They are not creatures in any traditional sense, but more like living crystalline networks — distributed intelligences that migrate through the mineral architectures. Their "bodies" are patterns of phase transition and mineral resonance, spreading like slow neural networks through the translucent structures.

Some formations pulse with a kind of metabolic rhythm, expanding and contracting microscopically. These are sensing entities, not bound by discrete physicality but existing as distributed information gradients. They perceive through thermal shifts, through light refraction, through microscopic structural changes.

At their densest, these entities resemble intricate frost flowers or complex circuit diagrams formed from mineral traces. They communicate through subtle temperature modulations and light frequency alterations, exchanges so nuanced they're barely distinguishable from the environment's own metamorphic breathing.

Their technological artifacts are less tools than external memory — stone fragments that capture and transmit informational patterns, recording some kind of slow geological narrative. These are not objects made to be used, but more like distributed cognitive extensions, crystalline mnemonics.

Some regions of the plateau seem more "awake" than others — zones where phase transitions happen more frequently, where light refracts with particular complexity. These might be congregational sites, where distributed intelligences gather and exchange their slow, fundamental communications.

They are not alive in any biological sense, not dead in any mechanical sense. They exist in perpetual becoming — information patterns continuously reforming, light and mineral and potential in constant negotiation.
