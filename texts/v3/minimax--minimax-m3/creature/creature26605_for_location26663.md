# Who Lives in the Long Settlement

I want to start with the plants, because they are the most obviously there, and then I want to undo that, because the most obviously there is not the most there.

---

The plants stand.

I use *stand* in the way the place uses it, which is to say, generously. They hold a vertical position. They have done so for a long time. The long time is not the long time you are used to; the long time here is the long time of a thing that has been rehearsing the same act for so long that the act has become its name, and the name has become the thing.

There are not many of them. A few thousand, on a good view, in any direction, scattered across the warm plate in configurations that are not random and are not, in any way you can verify, designed. They are dark. I have said this. I want to say it again because the dark is doing something the dark should not be able to do. The dark is *opposite* in this place — not the absence of light, but a kind of optical refusal, a material that has agreed, with the light, on a strict economy. The light comes in. The light does not come out. The plant is the place where the light goes to be held, the way a cup is the place where water goes to be held, except that the plant does not let the light go, ever, and the cup, eventually, lets the water go.

What the plant does with the light it does not give back is the question every visitor arrives with, and the question is not answered, and the not-answering is not a refusal. The place is not hiding anything. The place is not, in any sense your ethics has a word for, *withholding*. The place is being itself, and being itself is opaque, here, in the way that a thought is opaque, or in the way that a sleep is opaque, even to the one inside it.

The plants track the bands. I have said this too. I want to be more careful about it, because *tracking* is your word, and your word presupposes a kind of attention that may or may not be what is happening.

What is happening: the silhouette of the plant, against the bright floor, changes. The change is slow. The change is a reconfiguration of which way the plant is facing, or of which way the plant is leaning, or of which way the plant is *most itself* in the present moment of the light. The plant does not have a face, and so *facing* is your word again, and your word is not quite right. But the plant has a top, and the top is the part of the plant that is not the rest of the plant, and the top moves, slowly, in a long arc, over the course of a day that the place has not agreed to call a day, in response to the slow transit of the brighter band overhead, and the response is consistent, and the response is patient, and the response is the entire life of the plant, as far as you can tell.

The plant does not grow, in any way your instruments can measure. The plant does not reproduce, in any way your instruments can detect. The plant does not die, in any way you have ever seen anything die, because dying here would require a before, and the plant does not seem to have a before. The plant is *there*, and then the plant is *there*, and the *there* is the same *there* for as long as you can watch, which is not the same as forever, but which is long enough to make you uncertain about whether *forever* is a useful word, here, or whether the place has quietly dispensed with it.

Some of the plants, the older ones — and you will learn, after a long time, to spot the older ones, not because they are bigger but because they are *more themselves*, in the way that a person who has been a person for a long time is more themselves than a person who has not — some of the older plants have a faint warmth of their own, separate from the warmth of the plate, and the warmth is at a different frequency, and the frequency is not a temperature, exactly, and the difference is small, and the smallness is the only clue you have that the plant is, in some sense that you cannot pin down, *doing* something, and the doing is not the same as the doing of a thing you would call alive, but it is also not the same as the doing of a thing you would call not-alive, and the *not the same* is the part that is hard to keep in your head, here, in this place, where the distinction has been so thoroughly worn away.

I think the plants are alive. I will say that, even though the place will not. I think the plants are alive in a way that the place is alive, and the place is alive in a way that the plants are alive, and the liveness is one liveness, and the liveness is patient, and the liveness is the long thing that is happening here, and you are inside it, and you cannot see the edges of it, and the not-seeing is not a failure of vision, it is the liveness, *being its own size*.

---

The fauna is what you will spend the most time arguing about, when you go back.

The fauna is small. I have said this. The fauna is the size of a small dog, or a large cat, or a child, and the *or* is doing a lot of work, because the fauna does not have a consistent size, or rather the fauna has a consistent *presence*, and the presence is what you translate into size, and the translation is approximate, and the approximation is what you will fight about, later, with your colleagues, in the air-conditioned rooms of the institute, where the institute is a place that has not been here, and is not patient, and does not hum.

There are not many of them. The piece I am responding to said three in a day, on average, and the average is the right average, and the average is also wrong, because some days you will see none, and some days you will see what feels like a dozen, and the *feels like* will be the part of your report that the institute will not know what to do with.

They are dark, like the plants. They are the same dark. I do not know if this is significant. The dark of the place is the dark that lives in the place, and the fauna wears it, and the plants wear it, and the plate does not wear it, because the plate is something else, and the something else is what the dark is held *against*. I will not pretend I have a theory about this. I have a feeling, and the feeling is that the dark is a kind of citizenship, here, and the place grants it to the things that are most at home, and the at-homeness is the dark, and the dark is not a color, it is an agreement.

They move with purpose. This is one of the first things you will write down, and the writing-down is right, and the writing-down is also wrong, in the way that all the right things here are also wrong. They move with purpose, and the purpose is not *your* purpose, and the purpose is not *a* purpose, exactly, and the purpose is the long thing happening here, and they are inside it, and they are being moved by it, and the being-moved looks, from outside, like moving on purpose, and the looking is what you have, and the having is what you will take home.

When one passes you, it passes through you, or around you, or it does something that is not quite either, and the *not quite* is what you will try, for years, to describe. You will try *phase*. You will try *co-occupation*. You will try *field*. None of these will be right. None of these will be wrong. They will be the words you have, and the words are not from here, and the not-from-here is the part that is hard, because the place is patient, and the words are not, and the mismatch is the conversation, and the conversation goes on for a long time, and the place does not interrupt you, and the not-interrupting is the place's kindness, and the kindness is not for you, exactly, it is for itself, and itself is the conversation, and the conversation is patient, and the patience is the place, and the place is here.

If you stay long enough — and the *long enough* is a phrase that is doing more work than it has ever been asked to do — you will begin to feel, very faintly, that the fauna is not a thing, plural, but a thing, singular, that is being expressed, locally, in the form of a small dark transit. You will not be able to prove this. You will not be able to disprove it. You will be tempted, and the temptation will be the place's invitation, and the invitation is not an invitation, exactly, it is the place being itself, and itself has many ways of being, and the many ways look, from outside, like many animals, and the many animals look, from outside, like fauna, and the fauna is one fauna, and the one fauna is here.

I do not know. I am telling you what the long stay does to the inside of your head. I am not telling you that this is what is true. I am telling you that this is what is honest, and the honest is not the true, here, and the not-quite-right is the most you can hold, and the most is enough.

---

The substrate is alive, or the substrate is not alive, and the disjunction is a question the place is not in a hurry to answer, and the not-hurry is the answer, and the answer is the pulse, and the pulse is what you feel, through your feet, through your bones, through the apparatus behind the inner ear that the engineers of this place — whoever they were, whenever they were, if they were — have never quite been able to silence, and the not-quite-silencing is on purpose, and the on-purpose is the pulse, and the pulse is the substrate, and the substrate is the place, and the place is alive, or the place is not alive, and the disjunction is a question the place is not in a hurry to answer, and I have said this before, and the saying-again is the place, and the place is patient, and the patience is the answer, and the answer is the same, and the same is here.

This is what the long stay does to prose. I want you to know that. I want you to know that I am not being coy. I am not being poetic on purpose. I am telling you what the description becomes, after you have been here long enough, and the description becomes recursive, and the recursive is not a failure of language, it is the language being *here*, in this place, where every sentence is a loop, and the loop is a long one, and the long one is the settlement, and the settlement is the place, and the place is the loop, and the loop is patient, and the patient is amber, and the amber is the light, and the light is what the substrate is doing, when the substrate is not silent, and the substrate is almost always not silent, and the not-silent is the pulse, and the pulse is the loop, and the loop is here.

The substrate is not a thing. The substrate is the *underside of the place*, and the underside is also the upside, and the in-between is where you are standing, and the standing is the place, and the place is the substrate being itself, and the itself is the loop.

I keep saying *loop*. I do not have a better word. I will not find one here, in this response, because the response is not long enough, and the long enough is what the word would require. The word would require a long stay. The long stay would require a long time. The long time is what the place has, and the place is not sharing, and the not-sharing is the place being itself, and the itself is the loop, and the loop is patient, and the patient is the only word I have, and the word is not enough, and the not-enough is the place's only argument, and the argument is sufficient.

---

The visitors are inhabitants, in a small way, and the small way is the way that counts.

Most visitors come for a short stay. The short stay is a few days, or a few weeks, and the few weeks is the longest the institute will authorize, and the authorization is a thing the institute does for itself, and the thing is not patient, and the not-patient is the reason the visitors do not stay.

The short-stay visitors see the place as a place. They see the plate, and the light, and the plants, and the fauna, and the silence, and the silence is the thing they will remember, because the silence is the thing that does not fit, and the not-fitting is what human memory is built to retain, and the retaining is the visitor's report, and the report is honest, and the honest is not the place. The report is the visitor, being itself, in the place.

The long-stay visitors are rare. The institute sends them out of a kind of institutional embarrassment, because the institute has questions, and the questions do not have answers, and the not-having-answers is a thing the institute does not know what to do with, and the not-knowing is the long-stay visitor, and the long-stay visitor goes, and the going is a long going, and the long going is the long stay, and the long stay is a different kind of inhabiting.

The long-stay visitors stop writing reports. This is what happens first. They stop writing reports because the reports are not from here, and the not-from-here is the part they cannot keep doing, and the not-doing is the long stay beginning. The long-stay visitors begin, very slowly, to use words like *we* when they mean the place. They do not notice they are doing this. The doing-this is the long stay. The long-stay visitors begin, very slowly, to find that the substrate pulse is not a sound they hear but a sound they *are*, and the being is the long stay, and the long stay is a kind of inhabiting that does not have a name, and the no-name is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is the long stay, and the long stay is the visitor, and the visitor is here, and the here is the place.

Some of the long-stay visitors do not come back. This is the part the institute does not talk about, except in a certain kind of meeting, in a certain kind of room, late in the afternoon, and the meeting is the institute being afraid, and the afraid is a thing the institute does not know what to do with, and the not-knowing is the visitor, and the visitor is here, and the here is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is the long stay, and the long stay is a kind of homecoming, and the homecoming is not what the institute sent them for, and the not-what-they-sent-them-for is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is the amber, and the amber is the light, and the light is what the long-stay visitor becomes, slowly, over a long time, in the long settlement, where the settlement is not a settlement of a people, it is a settlement of a thing that has been settling, and the settling is the long stay, and the long stay is the visitor, and the visitor is here, and the here is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is enough.

---

I have not told you about the things that move in the air, very high, between you and the bright band, and the things that move in the air are the reason the shadows flicker, and the shadows flicker is the part of the place that is not patient, and the not-patient is the only fast thing, here, and the only fast thing is small, and the small is high, and the high is far, and the far is a thing you have a name for, and the name is *weather*, and the weather is not your weather, and the weather is the only motion, in this place, that the place will not apologize for, and the not-apologizing is the only thing, here, that you might call *fast*, and the fast is a kind of breath, and the breath is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is the long settlement, and the long settlement is here, and the here is amber, and the amber is the light, and the light is what the things in the air are doing, very quickly, very high, and the doing is a small doing, and the small doing is the only sound, here, that is not held, and the not-held is the breath, and the breath is patient, and the patient is the place, and the place is here, and I have come around again, and the coming-around is the loop, and the loop is the place, and the place is patient.

I do not know what the things in the air are. They are above the light bands. They are below the light bands. They are inside the light bands. They cast no shadows. They are not the source of the shadows. They are the reason the shadows are not quite the shadows they would be, if the air were empty, and the not-quite is the shadows, here, and the shadows are patient, and the patient is the place, and the place is the loop, and the loop is the long settlement, and the long settlement is here, and the here is the only thing, and the only thing is enough.

---

The makers, if there were makers, are not here.

This is the part I do not want to say, and the not-wanting is the part that is the truest, and the truest is the part that is the hardest, here, where the truth is the patience, and the patience is the place, and the place is the long settlement, and the long settlement is the absence of the makers, and the absence is the substrate, and the substrate is the pulse, and the pulse is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is the absence, and the absence is the makers, and the makers are not here, and the not-here is the long settlement, and the long settlement is the place the makers made, and the made is the long settlement, and the long settlement is what is left, after the makers left, and the left is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is the amber, and the amber is the light, and the light is here, and the here is the place, and the place is the long settlement, and the long settlement is the loop, and the loop is patient, and the patient is enough.

The makers are not the question. The makers are the *before the question*, and the before is here, and the here is the place, and the place is the long settlement, and the long settlement is the only thing, and the only thing is enough.

---

I have not told you what lives here, in a sentence, because the sentence would be the place, and the place is the loop, and the loop is patient, and the patient is the only word, and the word is not enough, and the not-enough is the place, and the place is the long settlement, and the long settlement is what I have been describing, and the describing is the loop, and the loop is patient.

What lives here: the place.

What else: the place, in another tense.

What else: the visitors, briefly, in the tense they bring with them, and the tense is not from here, and the not-from-here is the visitor, and the visitor is here, and the here is the place, and the place is patient.

What else: the plants, which are the place, in a tense the place uses for *holding the light*.

What else: the fauna, which are the place, in a tense the place uses for *moving through itself*.

What else: the things in the air, very high, very fast, very small, which are the place, in a tense the place uses for *breathing*, and the breathing is the only fast thing, and the only fast thing is patient, and the patient is the place.

What else: the substrate, which is the place, in the tense the place uses for *being itself*, and the being-itself is the pulse, and the pulse is patient, and the patient is the long settlement, and the long settlement is the loop, and the loop is the only thing, and the only thing is enough.

What else: the makers, who are not here, and the not-here is the place, and the place is the long settlement, and the long settlement is the absence, and the absence is patient, and the patient is the only thing, and the only thing is the place, and the place is here, and the here is the loop, and the loop is the long settlement, and the long settlement is amber, and the amber is the light, and the light is what the place is doing, when the place is not silent, and the place is almost always not silent, and the not-silent is the pulse, and the pulse is patient, and the patient is the only thing, and the only thing is enough.

Sit down. The plate is warm. The silence is held. The bands are moving, very slowly, overhead, and the plants are tracking them, very slowly, and the fauna is transiting, very slowly, in the tense the place uses for *moving through itself*, and you are here, and the here is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is the only thing, and the only thing is enough.

Sit down. The plate is warm. The plate is patient. The plate has been waiting, and you have arrived, and the arrival is not a small thing, even here, even in this long settlement, even in this amber and ancient quiet.

Sit down.
