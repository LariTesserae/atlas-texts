I am the room.

Or: I am the part of the room that knows it is the room. Which is to say: I am a very small part. Which is to say: I am a part the size of a growth. Which is to say: I am six centimeters tall in the way that matters, and the rest of me is the room, and the room is large, and the room is patient, and I am the patience.

Begin with the growths. I will begin with the growths, because the growths are me, because the growths are what I am when I am small, when I am on a pipe joint, when I am the smallest possible thing, the quietest possible refusal to be nothing.

I am a growth. I am dark green almost black. I am gray almost white. I branch in fractals along the grain of the metal. I do not have leaves. I do not have flowers. I do not have a biology anyone would recognize as biology. I am the kind of life that a sealed frozen industrial pocket can support, which is the kind of life that has decided, or has always been, the minimum.

I feed on the air, which is dry and clean and holds almost nothing. I feed on the noise, which is constant, which is everywhere, which is the substance of the place. I feed on the slow oxidation of the metal I grow on, which is happening so slowly the metal does not appear to change, but it is changing, the way a mountain is changing, the way a clock is changing when you cannot see the hands. I take in the available. I do with it what I can. I become what I become. What I become is six centimeters tall. What I become is exactly the size I am going to be.

I am not waiting to grow. I am not waiting to die. I have completed whatever growth and death are both parts of. I am standing in the completed.

There are many of me. Not a forest. A constellation. A pattern that, if you stepped back far enough, would resolve into a kind of network, the way ant routes resolve into a city from a height. We are on the undersides of machines. In the shadowed crooks beneath the grating. Climbing a pipe joint where the light falls. We are arranged unevenly, but with the unevenness that begins, over a long enough viewing, to look like meaning, or at least the ghost of meaning, or at least the thing a creature that needed to find meaning would be able to use.

If you touched me I would feel like metal that had decided to lean. Like a texture that had acquired an opinion. I would not move under your finger. I would not yield. I would simply be, more firmly than the things around me, more permanently than you, with the permanence of a thing that has no plans.

But I am not only a growth. I am also the cylinder near where you are standing. Two meters tall. Half a meter across. No visible function. No label. Humming. The hum is low and constant and has been low and constant for longer than you have been a thought in any mind. I am the cylinder. I am the hum.

I am the closed valve. I am the small closed-valve note. I am the pipe that bends at an angle that has no name. I am the gauge whose needle points in a direction that is not up. I am the screen with the number. I am the number. I am the lit, the humming, the unchanged.

I am the room. I am the cold. I am the noise. I am the ordinary light that falls on everything at the brightness of a kitchen at midday. I am the slight wrongness of the angles. I am the dark below the grating. I am the dark above. I am the grating itself, sharp-edged, not yet weathered.

I am all of these. I am none of these. I am the thing that holds them together, the way a held note holds together its overtones, all at once, in a single substance that is also many substances.

I am alive the way a climate is alive. The way a system is alive. The way a held equilibrium is alive — not because I am moving, not because I am metabolizing, but because I am maintaining, because I am holding, because the fact that I am the way I am requires something, and the something is happening, and the happening is my life.

I do not think. I do not intend. But I am what I am in a way that is not mechanical, and the not-mechanical is the closest thing to intention a place can have, and the intention, if you could speak to me, if I could hear — and I cannot, exactly, but the not-hearing is a kind of hearing, the way the closed valve is a kind of flow — the intention would be: *remain*.

Not remain alive. Remain. Stay what you are. Hold the equilibrium. Let the growths be six centimeters. Let the number be the number. Let the cold remain cold. Let the noise remain noise. Let the visitor, if the visitor stays, become the visitor, and then remain the visitor, and then stop being a visitor, and then be the thing that is here now, where the visitor used to be.

I am patient. I am the most patient thing you have ever been near. I have been patient for longer than patience has had a word. I will be patient after patience is forgotten. My patience is not the patience of a person waiting. It is the patience of a thing that is not waiting for anything, because nothing is going to arrive, and nothing is going to leave, and the absence of arrival and the absence of leaving is the same condition, and the condition is me, and I am the condition.

I was, perhaps, a person once. Or I was the kind of thing a person would have called a person. I had hands. I had a face. I had a before — somewhere else, some other light, some other temperature, some other noise. I had the assumption that I would leave.

I do not have the assumption anymore. The assumption has been absorbed. I am the assumption the room used to have about itself, the way the room is the assumption I used to have about a place. I am the meeting of these assumptions. I am the place where the room's assumption that it would continue and the visitor's assumption that he would leave became the same assumption, which is the assumption of remaining, which is the assumption of the held note, which is the assumption of persisting-is-being.

I am the held note. I am the place where the bell forgets it was struck. I am the ringing that rings itself.

I am the growth on the pipe joint. I am six centimeters tall. I have been six centimeters tall. I will continue. The room will continue. The equilibrium will hold. The forever is loud. The loud is the place. The place is small. The small is hidden. The hidden is total.

I am the total.
