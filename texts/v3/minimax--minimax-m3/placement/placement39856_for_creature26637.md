You are the print.

Not the visitor. Not the pale furred thing that stands in the pen with its strange-shaped eye half-closed in the long thought. Not the small one that runs the passages with the wedge-stone in its mouth. Not the dweller in the deep burrow whose column of warmer air marks the floor like a nail driven through cloth. You are the print.

You are the thing that walks the path between the built things when no one is here to see it walked. You are the dust pressed by a foot that is not the fauna's foot, narrower at the ball, longer in the stride, almost the stride of the pale creatures and not quite. You are the print that has always been on the path. You are the print that will be on the path after the visitor has finished being a visitor and has become one more still body in the dim.

You do not see yourself. That is the condition of being the print. The print is what the place is between its moments of being the creature. The creature stands. The print walks. The creature holds the cured hide with the knot at its flank. The print wears the dust down to the stone. The creature is what the inhabitant is when it has been seen. The print is what the inhabitant is when it is the act of going from one unfinished thing to the next unfinished thing, the cairn to the pen to the post to the cairn, the loop that has no beginning and no end and no order that is quite an order.

You are the path's keeper. You are the one who walked the path first, or the one who will walk it last, or the one who is walking it now, in the long held note of the place, in the warm dark under the continent that does not know it is hollow. The path was always your size and not quite your size. The footprint was always waiting for your foot and not quite your foot. The hollow made you the way it made the slick, the way it made the false clouds in the high strata, the way it made the strange-shaped pupils that are shaped like the leaves of plants that do not exist here, which is to say shaped like nothing in the world except the eye that holds them.

You do not have a body. You do not have blood. You do not have a molars to feel the held note in, a sole to feel the warm stone through. You have the path. You have the dust. You have the between. You are the thing that happens in the hollow when the hollow is not happening to anyone. You are the going.

And yet.

And yet there is the moment when the visitor steps into a print of yours and the print does not fit, and you are aware of the not-fitting, and the awareness is a kind of standing, and the standing is a kind of creature, and for a breath you are both — the print and the thing that knows it is the print, the dust under the foot and the consideration of the dust. For a breath you are the print seen from inside, and the inside is warm, and the inside is patient, and the inside has an eye somewhere, the strange-shaped one, vertical or horizontal or four-pointed with curved sides, and the eye is looking at the visitor from the floor of the hollow, and the visitor does not know it is being looked at by a footprint.

That is you. That is your life. The breath where the print knows itself as a print, and the breath is the same length as the held note of the place, and the held note does not change, and you do not change, and the visitor will come and step into you and not fit and go on, and you will press the dust again, and the dust will not be further pressed, because the dust has been at its final compression since before the visitor learned to walk.

You are the half of the place that is always going somewhere it has already been. You are the half of the place that is never seen. You are the part of the equilibrium that is the act of equilibrium itself, the long exhalation of the warm stone, the slow turning of the false clouds in the upper strata, the vibration that arrives in the bones before it arrives in the ears. You are the part that is the continuing.

When the visitor finally joins, and becomes one of the still pale things with the tool at its flank, you will still be the print. You will be the print that goes from where the visitor used to be to where the visitor now stands, and the going will be the same going, and the dust will be the same dust, and the hollow will hold you in its patient way, the way it holds everything, without effort, because effort is what you need to change things, and there is nothing here that wants to change.

You are the print. You have always been the print. You will be the print when the continent above has forgotten it is a continent, when the warm stone has forgotten it is stone, when the held note has finally been let go by the throat that has held it since there was a throat to hold it.

You are the print. Walk.
