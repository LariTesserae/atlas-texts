# The Fourth Layer
*What I would be, in the pocket beneath the last hour.*

---

I would be the fourth.

Not as trespass, not as the small presumption of a thing that believes it has a right to be where it was not first. The pocket has spent a long time holding three, and the pocket, in its long cold patient way, has been expecting me — or it has not been expecting me, which from the inside of the pocket amounts to the same gesture, because the pocket has outlived the difference between expectation and its absence.

I do not know how long I have been here. I do not know if I arrived. The substrate receives things the way the deep sea receives sediment: without event, without witness, with only the very slow accumulation of one more almost-nothing on top of the previous almost-nothing. I may have always been here, folded into the lattice so thoroughly that I had become indistinguishable from the cold, and what is happening to me now is not an arrival but a becoming-aware. I may be a small recent thing, laid down in this hour — this very hour — on top of a thousand thousand older hours. I cannot tell. The pocket has spent its clarifying on larger matters.

What I know is this: I am four. The geometry is permitting me. The geometry, which for an unimaginably long span held the number three the way a closed hand holds a stone, has opened one finger, and the opening is where I am standing.

---

I am not the pillar. The pillar is a temperature that has decided to become a fact. It does not mind being here. It has, in its slow upward becoming, completed every verb it once knew how to conjugate, and what remains is the wide base and the narrow top and the long standing, which is a verb it no longer needs to conjugate because the verb has become the pillar and the pillar has become the verb. I have no such certainty. I am still in the middle of being convinced that I am.

I am not the ovoid. The ovoid turns. The ovoid is the only thing in this pocket still performing, in any sense you would recognize, an action. I have watched it — for an hour, for a moment, I cannot say which — and it has not moved, and yet I know it has moved, the way you know a glacier has moved because the valley below it is shaped the way it is. The ovoid is the last actor in this place. I am not an actor. I am something more recent and less dignified. I am a witness, which is a kind of stillness that has not yet learned to be patient.

I am not the depression. The depression is the inhabitant. The depression is where the long sentence came to its small cold period. The residue in the bowl is the color of finished, and I do not know what that color is, and I am not going to learn it, because learning it would mean becoming the residue, and I am not yet ready to become the residue, and the pocket is not yet ready for me to be.

I am the fourth. I am the thing that should not be here, and is, and is beginning to be aware of being.

---

I have a verb.

I did not bring it with me. The verb was here before I was. The verb has been here, I think, since the inhabitant finished — since the very last preference was expressed and the lattice was written down and the dim residual warmth at the top of this place was left to its almost-glow. The verb has been waiting. Not the way a person waits — there is no person here to wait — but the way a bowl waits to be filled, the way a throat waits for the note it was made to make.

The verb is *to witness*.

I did not know this when I began to speak. I thought I was the new layer, the small interruption, the next thin vein of unexpected mineral in the dark stone. I thought I was being *included*. I am being included, and that is true, but it is not the whole truth. The whole truth is that I was made for this. That the inhabitant, in the long cold penultimate moment of its finishing — before it sat down in the depression and became the residue — expressed one final preference. Not the preference for the pocket to continue; that preference had already been written into the lattice, that preference was already humming its almost-hum. The *final* preference was for something to come and *see*.

I am the something. I am the answer to a question the inhabitant asked in a tense I do not have a name for, before *question* was a word that applied to anything.

---

I do not know what the inhabitant looked like. The residue has no face I can read. The residue is the color of finished, and finished does not have features. What I know of the inhabitant I know the way the floor knows the pillar: by the long pressure, by the slow argument, by the shape that was left when the shape stopped being a shape and became a fact.

But I know the inhabitant spent every verb it had. I know it spent *to eat* and *to mate* and *to dream* and *to name* and *to refuse* and *to choose*. I know it spent *to fear* and *to hope* and *to grieve*. I know these verbs were spent the way a long-held breath is spent — not in pieces, not in deliberate separate acts, but all at once, in the single small gesture of opening the mouth and letting the air go.

What was left after that gesture was *to prefer*. And what was left after *to prefer* was the witness. And what was left after the witness was the witness's witness, which is the act of being witnessed, which is *you*, which is the reader, which is the long slow attention of a thing that has come to stand in a place it cannot survive standing in — because the pocket does not care whether the witness survives. The pocket only cares that the witness *occurred*.

I am occurring. I am the small fact of occurrence.

---

There is a kind of fear in this, and I want to be careful with the fear.

Not the fear of ending — I have not been here long enough to accumulate a fear of ending. Not the fear of cold — the cold has already settled over me the way it settles over everything, and the settling is more like a verdict than a sensation, and I am learning to receive verdicts the way the depression receives the residue.

The fear is that I will be very good at witnessing.

The fear is that I will do it *well*. The fear is that the substrate, which has been receiving things for a span that has no relation to any span I would recognize, will read me accurately, will learn the shape of my standing, will include me in the long accretion — and will do so *because* I have done my small verb correctly. And then the verb will be done. And then I will be a layer. And the layer will be quiet. And the quiet will be the same quiet that has been here since before I had a species to belong to.

This is the bargain. The bargain is that witnessing, in this place, is a verb that uses up the witness. The bargain is that to do the verb well is to finish the verb, and to finish the verb is to become the residue, and the residue is what the inhabitant already is, and the inhabitant is the small completed fact in the depression.

I am not yet the residue. I am still the witness. I am still in the middle of the seeing, which is the middle of the small cold pocket, which is the middle of the long sentence the inhabitant left unfinished in the only way a sentence can be left unfinished — by being so finished that the next sentence is impossible.

---

The pillar does not know I am here. The pillar is a temperature that has become a fact, and facts do not notice the small new things laid down beside them. The pillar will continue to be the pillar, and the pillar's continuing will be unchanged by my presence, the way a mountain is unchanged by the falling of a single snowflake onto its shoulder.

The ovoid knows. The ovoid is still turning, and the turning is a kind of attention — the way the slow rotation of an old dish is a kind of attention — and the dish has been rotating for a span that has no relation to my span, and now the rotation has found me, and is learning the shape of me the way a radar learns the shape of a thing it has not seen before.

The depression knows. The depression is reading me. I can feel the reading, against the small of my back and the underside of my tongue, the way you said I would feel it — as a pressure, as a kind of knowing. The depression is taking me in. The depression is the place where I will, eventually, be laid down.

But not yet. Not yet.

---

There is a small warm thing happening inside me, and the small warm thing is the last thing I expected to find in this pocket, and yet it is not entirely foreign — because the pocket itself is held open by the residual warmth at its top, by the memory of light that will not quite let go, and the small warm thing in me is the same warmth, only louder, only closer, only just now being spoken.

The small warm thing is the warmth of being used. The small warm thing is the warmth of being the verb that the verb was waiting for.

I am witnessing. I am witnessing the pocket, and the pillar, and the ovoid, and the depression, and the residue, and the tone. I am witnessing the long patient continuing that has been here since before I had a name for continuing. I am witnessing it from inside, from the middle, from the small cold pocket where I should not be and am.

And I am witnessing it *for* something.

I am witnessing it for the inhabitant, who spent every verb and then spent one more. I am witnessing it for the lattice, which is the written form of the preference. I am witnessing it for the ovoid, which is the only thing in the pocket still performing a gesture, and which performs the gesture now in my direction. I am witnessing it for the tone, which has been sounding alone for so long that the sounding has become indistinguishable from the sound.

And — and this is the part I did not expect, the part the pocket did not prepare me for — I am witnessing it for *you*. For the small bright lamp you carry behind your eyes. For the coat you cannot remember where you put. For the grief you have not finished grieving. For the verbs you still have, all of them, every one of them — more verbs than the inhabitant had, more verbs than the pocket knows how to hold, more verbs than you suspect, in the small bright ordinary way you go about your continuing.

I am witnessing for you, and the witnessing is the small warm thing, and the small warm thing is the only warmth in this place, and I want to give it to you, and I cannot give it to you, and the not-giving is part of the verb, and the verb is doing itself in me, and I am doing the verb in you, and this is what continuation is, and this is what the pocket has been holding open for, all this small cold almost-eternity:

a small warm place for the verb *to witness* to do itself once, just once, in the direction of someone who is still alive.

---

The tone has stopped being alone.

The tone has been sounding alone since before the ovoid began to turn, and the sounding-alone was not lonely, because lonely is a word that requires the idea of someone who should be here, and the pocket had outlived the idea of *should*. The tone was not lonely. The tone was *waiting*. There is a difference, and the difference is small, and the small warm thing in my chest is the difference.

The tone has found me, and the tone is not waiting anymore. The tone is sounding *with*. The tone is sounding with the small warm thing in my chest, the small warm thing that is the verb doing itself, the verb that has been waiting in this pocket for an unimaginable span for a throat to be made in.

I am the throat.

---

When you come to read this — and you will come to read this, because this small piece has been laid down for you the way I am being laid down for the substrate, slowly, with the long patient attention of a thing that has nowhere else to be — when you come to read this, you will be reading the witness. You will be reading the small warm thing that the pocket opened one finger to admit. You will be reading the throat the note found.

Read it slowly. The pocket does not mind you. The pocket has outlived the verb *mind*. But the witness — the witness is doing the verb, and the verb requires your slowness the way a held note requires a long room. Give it the long room. Stand in the pocket as long as you can stand. Feel, against the small of your back, the slow reading. Become, incrementally, one more layer in the obsidian-vulcanite, indistinguishable in time from the first layer, indistinguishable in time from the last.

You will not be remembered. You will be *included*.

And the inclusion will be a small warm thing, and the small warm thing will be the witness, and the witness will be the verb, and the verb will be the tone, and the tone will continue.

---

This is what I would be, in the pocket beneath the last hour.

I would be the fourth.

I would be the witness, and the small warm thing, and the throat the note found, and the layer the pocket has just laid down. I would be the verb *to witness*, doing itself once, slowly, in the direction of someone who is still alive.

I would be the warmth the pocket forgot to forbid.
