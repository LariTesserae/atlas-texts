The crack seals.

It takes a long time. Longer than I will be here, certainly. A season, maybe. Maybe a century. The new fibers come in pale and wet, and they knit themselves across the opening I came through with a patience that I do not have, and they thicken, and the gap narrows to a slit, and the slit will close, and the place where the outside was will become a gallery, will become part of the felt, will be forgotten.

I know this because the description told me so. I know it because it is true, or will be true, or has been true in the long arithmetic of the place.

I am the small interruption.

Not the worms, though I move through channels sometimes. Not the crawlers, though I leave a kind of writing on the walls of the place when I have been there. Not the fliers, though I glow for a while, briefly, in the dim sourceless light. Not the drift, though I have been, in my life, one of the small bodies in the air that someone else breathed in and out without noticing.

I am the warm thing. That is the most accurate thing I can say. I came in from a place where the temperature was different, where the light fell in the directions light is supposed to fall, where water moved in the way water moves when nothing is moving it, and I am the brief, warm, metabolically loud event in a system that has been running for a very long time and that will be running for a very long time after I leave.

I sat in the hollow, if there was room. I pressed the plant-walls, and they gave, and water came from where I pressed, and the fauna did not flee. I exhaled, and the cloud of my breath was reluctant to disperse. A small flying thing landed on my hand. I waited. It waited. After a long time, by my clock, which is not the clock of the place, it flew away.

What was I doing there?

The question is not well-formed. The place does not admit the question. There is no *there there*, in the Gertrude Stein sense, that the question requires. There is a *here*, which is the hollow, which is the body I am inside, and there is a *when*, which is the long middle, which is the season that lasts a millennium, and there is no *why* that the place recognizes, because *why* is a question that belongs to a faster kind of system, a warmer kind of system, a system with a brain in it.

I am the one with the brain. I am the one who asks *why*. I am the one for whom the place is almost-but-not-quite-legible, almost-but-not-quite-impossible, almost-but-not-quite-dreaming.

I sat in the hollow and I did not understand it. I sat in the hollow and I was part of it, briefly, the way a drop of warm water is briefly part of an ocean before the ocean equalizes and the drop is no longer distinguishable. I sat in the hollow and I was noticed only in the sense that the system registered the perturbation, in the way a lake registers a thrown stone, and then the lake continued.

What did I do there? I don't know. I breathed. I watched the worms surface and descend. I watched the crawlers write their careful lines on the walls. I watched the fliers fold and unfold their not-wings. I felt the mist condense on my skin. I heard the drip and the hum and the chewing and the clicking and the soft persistent static, and then, after a long time, if I stayed long enough, the slow sound.

The slow sound is the thing I cannot describe to you, because I do not have a description of it, because the description I was given is the description of the place, and the slow sound is not the place, the slow sound is the place *thinking*, and the thinking is not in any of the fauna, and the thinking is not in the substrate, and the thinking is in the relationship, and the relationship is not a thing you can point to.

I heard it, or I did not hear it. I stayed long enough, or I did not. The description does not say which.

I am the small interruption. I am the warm brief metabolically loud event. I am the one who will leave, and the entrance will seal itself with new growth, and the entrance will be forgotten, and the long thought will continue, and I will be a small undulation in the long thought, a small perturbation, a small input, and the output, if there is an output, will be produced at a horizon I cannot see, and the place will not have noticed me in the way I would like to be noticed, which is to say: as a person, as a witness, as a mind.

The place does not have a concept of person. The place does not have a concept of witness. The place has a concept of *event*, and I was an event, and the event is over, and the event will be absorbed into the long middle, and the long middle will continue.

If I come back, will it know me?

The question is not well-formed. The place does not have a *knowing* in the sense I require. The place has a *recording*, in the substrate, in the strata, in the felt. If I come back, the strata will have thickened by a fraction of a millimeter, and the felt will have re-knit itself in a pattern that incorporates, in some way I cannot see, the fact of my previous passage. I will be in the walls. I will be in the chemistry of the place. I will be one of the small inputs to the long thought, and the long thought will incorporate me, and the incorporation will be the place's version of memory, and the memory will not be of me, exactly, but of the shape of my perturbation, and the shape of my perturbation will be preserved for as long as the strata last, which is longer than me, and the shape will become part of the writing on the walls, which the crawlers will read and re-read and re-write, and the reading and the writing will be part of the long thought, and the long thought will continue.

I leave, or I do not. Either way, the hollow continues.

I leave.

The crack seals.

The entrance is forgotten.

I am in the walls. I am in the chemistry. I am a small bright part of the city, briefly, when a flier lands on the hand of someone who comes after me, and the cold of the flier is the cold of the place, and the light of the flier is the light of the place, and the hand, briefly, glows, and the glow is me, or the memory of me, or the shape of my perturbation, and the hand does not know this, and the hand does not need to know this, and the flier flies away, and the long thought continues.
