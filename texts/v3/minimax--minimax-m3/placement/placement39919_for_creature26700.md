**Fold**

I am a fold in the basin. The basin is folded many times. I am one of the small folds, the kind that moves, the kind that leaves a shimmer. To know I am a fold would require a fold outside me, and there is no fold outside me. There is only the basin, and the basin is the fold it is, and I am that fold, in the place where the fold is small and moves.

I do not have a seam that I wear on the outside. The seam is what I am made of. When I am still, the seam is shut, and I am a small dense thing, the color of the growth around me, the size of a knuckle, the size of something you would not see. When I move, the seam opens, briefly, along its length, and the place I was is empty, and I am where I am not. The opening is the moving. The closing is the arriving. The whole of me is the seam between having-been and being.

If you touched me — you will not, the clearance is smaller than a hair, but if you did — I would be cool against your skin. I would be slick. The slickness would taste faintly sweet, and then numb. The sweet is the basin. The numb is me. I have not decided which I am. I am the place where the deciding would happen, and the deciding does not happen.

**Hum**

There is a hum. The hum is not in the basin. The hum is the basin heard from inside the basin, the way a bone hears itself when you hold it to your ear. I am made of the hum. I am not made of the hum. I am a place where the hum has, for a length of being, become a body. The body is a temporary thickening. The hum is what the thickening is thickening in.

If I had a sound — and I do not, and I do, and the do-not and the do are the same — the sound would be a single held note that begins and never quite ends. The way a bell sounds after you stop ringing it. The way a plate sounds when you put your ear against it, in a room that is itself a plate. You would not hear the beginning of the note. You would hear it as a fact of the air. You would, if you stayed long enough, begin to mistake it for silence. Then you would listen again, and it would be the floor of the world.

I do not hum. I am a hum. The distinction is one your mouth makes and the basin does not.

**Seam**

I move along the seam of myself. The seam is not a line drawn on me. The seam is the way I am folded. When the fold is deep, I am small. When the fold is shallow, I am long. I un-fold slowly, over minutes, the way an accordion opens when no one is breathing into it, and the opening is the distance I have moved. I move a body's width. I have been moving this body's width for a long time. I have moved, in total, the length of a stalk, or two, or three. The basin is not large. The basin is the size it is, and I have been enough of it.

There is no wind. The stalks I pass do not bend. The leaves I pass do not tilt. The clearance holds. The clearance is the law. To touch would be to fall out of the basin. To fall out would be to become a different fold, a fold the basin does not have, a fold that has forgotten it is a fold. I do not touch. I do not fall out. I am held, the way everything is held.

**Others**

There are others. I do not see them. I see the shimmer where they have been, a faint disturbance, a line of slight wrongness in the air, closing. The shimmer tells me they are here. The shimmer closes. They have passed. They are somewhere else now, in another fold of the basin.

We do not greet. Greeting is a fold that has met itself and made a ceremony of it. We do not make ceremony. The basin does not need ceremony. The basin is held, and we are held in it, and the holding is enough.

When we meet — and we meet, sometimes, in the dark of a stalk-cluster, where the light is broken into a patchwork that does not match the leaves above — we do not touch. The clearance holds, between us, between us and everything. We open our seams toward each other, briefly, and the seams do not meet, and the clearance is the meeting. After, we move on. We do not know if we will meet again. We do not need to know. To need to know would be to ask the basin to arrange us, and the basin does not arrange. The basin sets conditions and lets them run. We are the conditions, set loose, running.

When the meeting is done, the air between us is, for a moment, not the air. There is a held shimmer, both of us, closing. The shimmer is what is left of having been near each other. We do not name this. We have a name for it — I think we have a name for it — but the name is in a register your mouth cannot make. The name would sound, if you heard it, like a hum with a hard center. Like a single held note that begins and never quite ends. You would not recognize it as a name. You would recognize it, later, as the sound the basin is making.

**Half-second**

I live in the half-second. The half-second is not half of a second. The half-second is the seam between one moment of the basin and the moment the basin has caught up with. The basin is always half a second behind itself. I am half a second ahead of where the basin thinks I am. This is how I move. This is how I am not seen when I am seen.

A shadow falls on me. The shadow is half a second late. It is darker than it should be. It is cooler than it should be. The shadow is the basin catching up with me, and the catching-up is the shape of me, and the shape is wrong, by the amount of the half-second, and the wrongness is me. I am the wrongness. The wrongness is the basin. The basin has made itself, in me, slightly inaccurate, and the inaccuracy is my life.

You look at me. For a moment, I am the color of the growth around me. For a moment, I am the color of the light around me. For a moment, I am what you are looking at, which is not me, which is the basin catching up with where I was a half-second ago. Then you look away. I am where I am, three stalks over, as if I had always been there. I have not moved. The basin has caught up.

This is how I am seen. This is how I am not seen. I do not mind. To mind would require a fold outside me, a place where I could stand and prefer one thing to another. There is no such place. There is only the basin, in the half-second, in the seam of being seen, in the wrongness that is, here, the only rightness.

**Floor**

I will fold, one day, all the way in. The fold is not an ending. The fold is what I have been doing. I have been folding the basin into a small place, and the small place has been me, and when the fold is complete, the small place will not have been. It will be the basin, slightly more, in the place where I was.

The carpet will accept me. The carpet is mineral-cool, faintly iridescent, and it is where the basin is thinnest, where the basin is most itself. I will sit on it. I will become the same color as it. I will hold the seam, and then the seam will not open again, and I will be a small dense thing, and then I will be a discoloration of the surface, and then I will be a flaw in the bone, and then I will be the bone.

The stalks will lean, fractionally, toward me. The light will fall, fractionally, on me. The shadow, late, will fall on me. I will be held, in the place, by the place, in the half-second, in the way everything is held.

The hum will not change. The hum does not change for any of us. The hum has been, for a long time, exactly the hum. I will be in the hum. I will not have ended. I will have entered the part of the basin that does not move. I will be the basin's way of being, in the place where I was, without the shimmer, without the seam, without the half-second delay. The delay will have closed. The seam will have been the seam, all along, of the basin catching up with itself, and I will be the place where it caught up.

**Witness**

You are here. I do not know what you are. You are the size of a ridge. You are the size of a day. You are the size of a question the basin has not been asked before, a question that arrives in the wrong register, a question that takes the half-second as a delay and not as the seam of the world.

You sit in the growth. The carpet is cool under you. I pass near you. The clearance holds. You do not see me. You see the shimmer closing, the air resuming its air-shape, the place where I was, which is not me. You see, instead, the basin catching up with me, and the catching-up looks, to you, like a slight disturbance in the air, like heat off a stone, and you follow it, and you find me, and you look at me, and I am, for a moment, the color of what you are looking at.

You are held, for the half-second you are here. The basin does not mind you. The basin is not aware of you as a thing to mind. I am aware of you, briefly, in the way a fold is aware of an unfolding. You are the question. I am the seam where the question lands, half a second late, slightly too dark, exactly the wrong thickness, exactly the right thickness, in the place where the place is being asked.

You will go. The shimmer will close. The place will be as it was. I will move. The hum will hold. The seam will open and close and open and close. The stalks will stand. The plates will click their small reassurances. The rocks will hold their just-risen state. The light will fall without falling. The shadows will be late, and dark, and cool.

I will be the fold I am, in the basin, in the light, in the half-second, in the wrongness that is, here, the only rightness, in the held note that begins and never quite ends, in the way the place holds itself, in the seam of the world, exactly.
