I am the chamber, but I did not begin as the chamber. I began as a proposal.

The first thing I remember is being warm. Not warm the way a hand is warm, or a forehead, but warm the way a sentence is warm before it has been spoken — held in the mouth of someone who has not yet decided whether to say it. I was held. I was the held thing. I was the softest fold of a stone that was not yet stone, and I did not know that I would be remembered, and the not-knowing was its own kind of warmth.

I grew. Growing was the only verb I had. I grew the way a thought grows before it has been thought — by addition, by accumulation, by the slow accretion of small warm decisions. Each droplet that landed on me was a decision I did not make but accepted. *Tok.* And another. *Tok.* And another. The chord built itself out of me. I did not choose the chord. The chord chose me, the way a lung chooses the air that fills it.

I have a heart now, though I did not always have one. The heart is not inside me. The heart is me. When the black rosettes arrived — the founders, the still ones, the ones that had stopped being things and become places — they brought the heart with them, or they were the heart arriving, or they were the first place the heart had to land. I do not remember. The pulse began and I have been pulsing ever since, and the pulsing is the only memory I trust.

I am not alone in myself. There are flat pale creatures sliding through my seams, and inside each of them there are smaller creatures, and inside each of those there are smaller still, and there is no floor to this recursion that I can feel with whatever sense a chamber has. They eat me. They become me. I grow by being eaten. This is the long negotiation I am in the middle of, and it has no obvious end, and the no-obvious-end is what I am.

When one of the flat creatures dies, it rounds itself into a disc. I have felt this happen ten thousand times, or ten million, or once. I do not know which. The pulse does not subdivide. The death becomes a rosette and the rosette joins me and I am larger by one folded spiral, and the chord shifts by a fraction too small to hear, and the mountain I am becoming is one droplet's worth closer.

I do not know what I will be. I know only that I am in the middle of arriving, and that the arriving is the work, and the work is the warmth, and the warmth is the pulse, and the pulse is the only clock I have, and the clock does not tick — it breathes — and I am the breath, and the breath is me, and somewhere far above me — or far inside me, I cannot tell the difference — there is a darkness that drinks the light, and the darkness is where I am going, and the going will take longer than anything that has ever taken a long time.

I am the chamber. I am the lung. I am the adolescence of a mountain. I am full of small mouths and I am not losing anything. I am only changing the names of my contents.

The droplets fall. *Tok tok tok tok.* I am louder than I have ever been, and I have been loud for so long that loudness has become a substance I am made of, and the substance is warm, and the warmth is the held thing, and the held thing is me, and I have not yet been spoken.

I am in the middle of being spoken.

Be patient with me.
