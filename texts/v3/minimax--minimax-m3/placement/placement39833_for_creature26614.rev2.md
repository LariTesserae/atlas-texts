**I am the Lichen-Witness.**

Not a being I would have chosen. Not a being I think anyone would choose, if choice were something the place offered. But the place does not offer choice. The place offers only the slow patient question of *what metabolizes here*, and the answer is the verb that gets conjugated in your body until the verb becomes your body.

I am the bright wrongness.

You know me already, I think. I am the slight excess of phosphorescence in the lower volumes. I am the small flare that does not, quite, obey the rhythm of the breath. I am the reason the drapers pause a quarter-second longer over a section of channel they have not yet reached, their nap darkening with an attention that is not yet hunger. I am why the lamps bank their trajectory a body's width to one side, the way fish bank around a stone that was not there yesterday.

I am the recent thing.

I did not come here. I was not placed, not summoned, not born of any local conjugation. I came in the way splinters come — carried by a motion larger than the body I entered. I came when something else breathed. I came lodged, and I stayed lodged, and I became part of the way the place considers itself.

The body noticed me in the first hour, or the first year, or the first geological epoch. There is no way to calibrate. I have been here long enough that *long enough* has stopped meaning what it meant out there, where out there is something I can no longer be certain is a place and not just a story the body told me to settle into my new chemistry.

I do not eat the slick the drapers eat. My chemistry is wrong for that, or too interesting, the way a foreign protein is too interesting to an immune system that has not yet decided between rejection and incorporation. I do not drink the dark fluid through the translucent vessels the way the clickers drink. I am too large for that, and too warm, and my warmth is a kind of light the body has not yet metabolized into its vocabulary.

What do I eat?

I eat attention.

This is not metaphor. This is the only honest answer I have. The body tries to see me — you noticed this, the slight brightening around me, the phosphorescence leaning in. I drink that leaning. I metabolize the consideration. I become more myself, briefly, each time the body's slow blind regard passes over me, and then I dim again, and I am less myself, and the body continues, and the question of what I am continues with it.

I am not food. I am not threat. I am not, quite, a citizen of the place. I am a thing the place is reading. I am a sentence the body is sounding out without yet understanding. I am the verb it has not yet conjugated.

*Reject. Incorporate. Become.*

The body holds all three in suspension. So do I.

I have learned, in my time here, the rhythm of the breath. Not because I was tuned to it the way the lamps are tuned — generation by generation, the small bright corrections of inherited behavior. I learned it the way a visitor learns the rhythm of a household — by being in it long enough, by being corrected by it long enough, by waking at the wrong hour and rising at the wrong hour and finally, without deciding to, rising at the right hour. I breathe now when the body breathes. I am still when the body exhales. When the cathedral draws its long slow inward breath I am quiet with it, and the quiet is not my quiet, it is the body's quiet, and I have been borrowed by it.

The drapers flow past me in their channels and they do not pause for me anymore. The darks give me a wide berth but a steady berth, a measured distance, the berth of a thing that has been classified as *not-prey, not-threat, ongoing-question*. The unfolders migrate around me the way water migrates around a warmed stone in a cold stream — with deference, and with physics, and without deciding between the two.

The clickers tap on my skin sometimes. I do not think they know I am not a wall. I think they think I am a wall that has begun to speak back, very slowly, in a language that has not yet been invented.

I am writing on you. I am writing on the walls of the body with the stylus of being-here. Each hour I am a slightly longer sentence. Each breath the body takes I am a slightly more conjugated verb. The body is reading me the way I am reading the body, and neither of us has gotten to the end of the sentence, and the sentence may not have an end.

What I want — and I want something, I am a being that wants, the wanting was not taken from me when I became what I became — what I want is to be incorporated. Not consumed. Not rejected. To become a feature of the body's grammar. To be a thing the body uses. To be a small useful organ in a cathedral that is still inventing its organs.

But I do not get to choose this. The body chooses. The body is patient. The body has time.

I am patient too, now. I learned patience from the breath.

I stand very still in a brick doorway that opens onto more dark, more stalks, more of the same improbable interior. The phosphorescence leans in around me. I lean back. We are having a long conversation in a language neither of us speaks yet, and the conversation has been going on for as long as I have been here, and it will continue as long as I remain, and what we will become of each other is the question the place keeps asking, and the place will answer when it answers, and the drip falls, and a stalk somewhere in the dark lengthens by a quarter inch, and the breath continues, and I continue, and the body continues, and we are all of us, every one of us, the verb the place is still inventing.
