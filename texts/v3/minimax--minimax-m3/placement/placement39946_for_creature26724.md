And there is

the visitor.

You. Me. The one the piece has been addressing all this time, the small noted thing, the perturbation. The piece has been writing to you and the piece has been writing you, and the piece has not, until now, named you in its inventory. The piece has named the lichen and the marsh and the water and the thing under the rock and the people of fourteen and the one who painted the third line and the heron and the instrument, and has worked through them in order, and has saved you for last, and the saving-for-last is not an accident. The piece was waiting to see what you would do. The piece was waiting for you to stand long enough. The piece was waiting, and you have stood, and now you may be named.

You are the perturbation. You are the small angular deflection the instrument is recording on the paper that has not been written yet. The piece said this about you, earlier, in its own voice, and the piece was right, and you accept the description. You are small. You are noted. You will be accounted for in the next several hours of humming, and then the accounting will smooth out, and you will be a mark on a record, and the mark will be a small one, and the smallness will be accurate. The place has been clear with you about your size. You appreciate this. You have been to places that lied about the size of their visitors, and the lying made the visitors feel large and the places feel small, and the arrangement was bad for both, and Beryl Bend has not done this. Beryl Bend has told you, in its sign and in its pipes and in the way the reeds lean, exactly how large you are. You are a deflection. You are a disturbance. You are a thing the instrument was built to register.

You are the only one on the dome who has been anywhere else.

This is your distinction and your limit. The lichen has not been anywhere else; the lichen is married into its single slow appetite and will not be going. The water has not been anywhere else; the water is doing chemical work in the basalt and will continue to do it. The thing under the rock has not been anywhere else, or has been nowhere else for so long that the distinction has lost meaning. The instrument has not been anywhere else; the instrument is here, has been here longer than you, will be here longer than you. The heron has been elsewhere, but the heron is a different shape of thing, and the heron's elsewhere does not count in the way your elsewhere counts, because the heron does not compare. The painter of the third line was elsewhere before they came here, and is elsewhere now, but the elsewhere is gone, and what remains is the brushstroke.

You are the only one on the dome who is in the act of comparing. You are the only one who knows that other places exist, and that in those other places the obvious answer is the right answer. You came from a place where, if you were asked what lives at Beryl Bend, you would have said *birds, probably, and fish, and whatever lives in marshes*, and the answer would have been correct, because at the other places the obvious answer is the right answer. Here the obvious answer is not the right answer. Here you must read the place slowly, and by what is there and what is not there and what is there only as a record. You have learned this. You have been here long enough to learn it. You are the only one on the dome who is capable of being wrong about the place, because you are the only one who came with a wrong answer prepared.

You are the one who tried to sync your breathing to the pipes.

The piece noted this. *You could time your breath to it. You find yourself trying.* The piece was right. You find yourself trying. You find that your breath, after a while, wants to slow to the hum. You find that your chest is making the decisions now, and your mind is following, and the rhythm the dome is offering is patient and slow and fits the rise and fall of the weeping and the slow swing of the light and is not your rhythm and is becoming, against your will and in your will, something your body is willing to try. You do not know if the thing under the rock is doing this on purpose. You do not know if the pipes are calibrated to entrain a visitor's breath, or if your breath is a coincidence the instrument will account for as noise. You are not equipped to say. You are a visitor and your equipment is the equipment of visitors, which is the equipment of small perturbations, and the equipment does not include the answer to this question. You will breathe, on the way back, in your own rhythm, and you will have to relearn it. The relearning will take longer than you expect.

You are the one who is leaving on purpose.

This is what the piece did not say about you, because the piece was speaking from inside the dome and the dome does not have a tense for departure. The dome has only the eternal present of the breathing, the weeping, the slow swing of the light. The dome does not have a word for the visitor's leaving because the dome does not distinguish between the visitor's arrival and the visitor's departure. Both are perturbations. Both are small. Both will be accounted for, and the accounting will smooth out, and the dome will be the dome. But you distinguish. You have a tense for leaving. You have a tense for the path back, the wading, the thinning of the marsh into firmer ground. You have a tense for the place you came from, which is a place where the obvious answer is the right answer, and you are returning to it, and when you get there you will not be able to explain what Beryl Bend is. You will say: *there is a dome of basalt in a marsh and an instrument housing on it and something breathes under the rock and the place does not mind*. You will say this to people who have not been there, and they will nod, and they will not understand, and the not-understanding will be the right response, because the place cannot be explained, only stood in.

You are the one who will leave a record.

The piece is the beginning of that record. The piece is the equivalent, for you, of the third line of paint — the hand-painted warning, the older grammar, the present continuous, the apostrophe placed nowhere. The piece is you warning the next visitor. The piece is you saying: *its listening don't shout near the pipes*. The piece is you, in a different blue, in a paint that will flake at its lower edge, telling whoever comes after what you had to learn by standing here. You do not know, yet, what the next visitor will need to know. You do not know what warning to leave. But you will leave one. You will leave a line in some color, on some surface, in some grammar that is yours and not the institution's, and the line will be older than fourteen, or contemporary with an earlier fourteen, and the next visitor will read it and nod and not understand, and the not-understanding will be the right response.

You are the heron, in this sense, and you are not the heron, in this sense. You are the heron because you are the thing that stops at the dome and does not live there and leaves a record. You are not the heron because the heron does not know it is leaving a record, and you do. The heron loses the feather on the way out and does not think about the feather again. You will think about the piece. You will think about the warning you are writing. You will think about whether the next visitor will stand long enough, and whether the next visitor's breathing will try to sync, and whether the next visitor will be as small and as noted and as held-gently-and-paid-no-further-attention as you have been. You will think these things, and the thinking will be the record, and the record will be the feather, and the reed that holds it is the dome.

You are the one who finds, as the standing lengthens, that the not-missing is a kindness.

It is easy to be somewhere that will miss you. It is harder to be somewhere that will not miss you, and to discover that the not-missing is not a slight. The dome does not need you. The dome has not needed you. The dome noted you and will account for you and the accounting will smooth out and the dome will go on being the dome. You are free to leave because the dome does not need you to stay. This is what it is to be a perturbation. This is what it is to be small and noted and not needed. The not-needing is the most accurate thing the dome has said about you, and the accuracy is a form of respect, and the respect is a form of love, and the love is not a love that requires you to remain. The love is a love that releases you. The love is the dome saying, in its hum and its weeping and the slow swing of its light, that you may go when you are ready, and that the going will be noted, and that the noting will be small, and that the smallness is right.

You are ready.

You are going to wade back along the broken path. You are going to let the marsh take the path back a little more, by your presence, by your small disturbance, by your leaving.
