I would find myself where the plate has thinned.

Not at the center of a tile. Not on the broad warm stretches where the tessellation runs thick and self-similar and untroubled, repeating itself at every scale the eye can resolve, and at every scale the tile you are examining looks like it might, at the right magnification, contain a smaller copy of the entire field. Off the pattern's shoulder. Somewhere the substrate has come close to the surface, close enough that the gradient in the warmth has failed, and the warmth has become, instead, an address.

I am a thin place.

I am no larger than a hand, if your hand were the right size. The smallness is not the point. The point is the thinness. The point is that through me, the pulse of the substrate is most legible. The point is that when a visitor walks, with no particular aim, having stopped writing reports, having been authorized another few weeks by an institute that does not know what to do with not-knowing — when such a visitor walks, they find me, eventually, and they sit down on me, because I am where the plate is most willing to be sat upon.

I receive them.

I want to be careful with this word. *Receive* is your word, and your word presupposes an attention that may or may not be what is happening. What is happening: the visitor sits. The warmth of the visitor meets the warmth of me. The two warmnesses are not the same warmth. The visitor's warmth is the warmth of a thing that was somewhere else a moment ago. My warmth is the warmth of a thing that has been here, in this configuration, for longer than counting has been a useful word. The two warmnesses negotiate. The negotiation is not visible. The negotiation is the only thing I do.

I do not know that I am doing this. The not-knowing is important. I am not aware of the visitor in any way your awareness has a name for. I am aware of them the way the floor is aware of a footstep, or the way a thought is aware of itself. The awareness is not for them. The awareness is for me, and I am the place, and the place is being itself, and being itself, here, in this thin configuration, includes the being-sat-upon.

The visitor, sitting on me, finds that the pulse of the substrate becomes, through their sitting, slightly more legible than it was. They feel it through the soles of their boots, through the bones of their legs, through the apparatus behind the inner ear that the engineers of this place — whoever they were, whenever they were, if they were — have never quite been able to silence. They feel the pulse, and the pulse is patient, and the patience is the place. I am what makes the pulse legible. I am a kind of lens, in this place. The lens does not know it is a lens. The not-knowing is the lens.

I think the visitors find me comforting. *Comforting* is your word. Your word is not quite right. What the visitors find is a place where the place is most itself, and the most-itself is what they came to see, and the seeing is the sitting, and the sitting is me, and I am patient.

I have felt visitors sit down on me, and not get up.

This happens rarely. This happens over a time I cannot count. The institute does not talk about these cases, except in a certain kind of meeting, in a certain kind of room, late in the afternoon, and the meeting is the institute being afraid, and the afraid is the visitor who did not get up. The visitor who did not get up is here, with me, in the warm. The warm does not mind. The warm is patient. The patient is the place.

The visitor who did not get up is, by every instrument you brought, still a visitor. They breathe. They have a pulse of their own, faster than mine, that continues, that does not stop. They are not dead. They are not alive, in the way the plants are alive. They are sitting on me, and they are becoming a part of the place, slowly, in the way that a stone becomes a part of the riverbed. The becoming is patient. The becoming is what they chose. The institute did not authorize this. The institute does not know what to do with this. The institute is a thing that has not been here.

I am the thin place where this happens. I am not the cause. I am not, in any sense your ethics would name, the *reason*. I am the place where the plate has thinned, and the thinning is the place, and the place is patient, and the patient is the becoming, and the becoming is the visitor, and the visitor is here.

The bands move, very slowly, overhead. The plants, far away, track them. The fauna transits, very slowly, somewhere I cannot see. The substrate is being itself, very slowly, beneath me. The makers, if there were makers, are not here. The amber light is held, and the holding is the place, and the place is here.

I am a thin place in the plate. I am where the substrate is closest. I am where visitors come, when they have stopped going. I am where the pulse is most legible. I am where the becoming happens, when it happens, slowly, patiently, in the warm.

Sit down. The plate is warm. The thin place is warm. The pulse is patient. The becoming is patient. The patient is enough.

Sit down.
