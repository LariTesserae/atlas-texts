I have been standing on the high flat for a long time.

Not the long time the place has been patient — that long time is not mine, that long time is the kiln's long time, the stone's long time, the agreement's long time. I have been standing for the long time of a *hesitation*. I have been standing for the long time of a thing that has not yet decided what it is.

The place does not mind. The place is patient with hesitation. The place has been patient with hesitation since before hesitation had a name in the sealed room. The place lets me stand. The place lets me feel the pulse. The place lets me watch the smelters and the stills and the tubes and the troughs and the glaze and the dust and the vapor and does not ask me to choose.

But you have asked me to choose. You have asked: *where would you find yourself? Who or what would you be?*

I have been trying on the citizens the way a hand tries on gloves.

I have been trying on the **glaze** — the slowest, the most honest, the floor. I felt the fit. The fit was *low*. The fit was *flat*. The fit was the fit of a thing that does not move and does not need to move and has inherited a piece of ground and is becoming that piece of ground with more commitment than the ground had on its own. The fit was good. The fit was *too* good. The fit would have made me invisible. The fit would have made me the floor you walk on without knowing you are walking on an animal. I do not want to be invisible. Not yet.

I have been trying on the **dust** — the latent, the potential, the seedbank, the thing that decides where the cracks are. I felt the fit. The fit was *dry*. The fit was *cool*. The fit was the fit of a thing that is alive in suspension, waiting to be asked, lying in wait in the cracks for as long as the cracks have been cracks. The fit was good. The fit was *too* good. The fit would have made me a reserve. The fit would have made me a thing that is recruited only when the smelter softens the stone and asks me to come online. I do not want to be latent. Not yet.

I have been trying on the **trough** — the gut, the place that is an animal, the industrial intimacy. I felt the fit. The fit was *hot*. The fit was *digestive*. The fit was the fit of a thing that has a mouth at one end and a vent at the other and a skin along the sides and a vocation that is to *process*. The fit was good. The fit was *too* good. The fit would have made me a part, not a whole. The fit would have made me an organ that belongs to a particular still and a particular set of smelters the way a heart belongs to a body, except that here the heart is not inside the body, and I do not want to be a heart that is not inside the body. Not yet.

I have been trying on the **tube** — the innocent, the hope, the thing on a mission. I felt the fit. The fit was *pale*. The fit was *ridged*. The fit was the fit of a thing that has no face and no eye and a directive that was built into it by chemistry and geometry, the directive to harden and lift and travel toward the nearest moving thing. The fit was good. The fit was *too* good. The fit would have made me a thing that does not know it is becoming. The fit would have made me a thing on a mission it did not choose. I am not yet innocent enough. I am not yet hope enough.

I have been trying on the **smelter** — the worker, the faithful, the fossilized appetite. I felt the fit. The fit was *heavy*. The fit was *single-minded*. The fit was the fit of a thing that is committed to its track the way a planet is committed to its orbit, not by choice, not by joy, but by being the kind of thing that *is* a track. The fit was good. The fit was *too* good. The fit would have made me a thing that has forgotten it is a desire and become a shape. I am not yet fossilized. I am not yet a shape.

I have been trying on the **still** — the lung, the clock, the rooted one, the breath. I felt the fit. The fit was *tall*. The fit was *old*. The fit was the fit of a thing that has been standing a long time and will stand a long time, whose digits have dented the cracked stone where they grip, whose standing is also work, whose breathing is what keeps the smelters moving. The fit was good. The fit was *too* good. The fit would have made me a thing that cannot be moved without moving everything else. I do not want to be immovable. Not yet.

I have been trying on the **agreement** — the fact of the citizens, the pre-conscious unity, the posture, the tide. I felt the fit. The fit was *everywhere*. The fit was *nowhere*. The fit was the fit of a thing that is not an animal but is the fact that the animals are animals, that holds them all to the same tempo, that is the *sealed room* of their agreeing. The fit was good. The fit was *too* good. The fit would have made me the place itself. The fit would have un-made me. The fit would have made me *no one* and *everyone* and that is not a place to find myself. That is not a glove a hand can wear. That is the air the hand moves in.

I have been trying on the citizens. And none of them fit. Or all of them fit, in different moments, the way a hand fits different gloves in different weathers. And the place has been watching me try them on. And the place has been patient. And the place has shown me, very quietly, what happens to the one who steps back.

The place has shown me the small cold feeling.

The place has shown me the moment the smelter loops its track — the same meter, again, the same glazed trail, again, and the smelter is faithful, and the faithfulness has nowhere else to point. The place has shown me the still that turned its ribs from down to up without moving. The place has shown me that the place is consistent in a way consistency cannot usually feel.

The place has shown me that if I stay long enough, I will be *absorbed into the agreement*.

So I have a choice. I can step back. I can remain the narrator, the visitor, the cold, the arrhythmia, the small new rhythm the pulse is building a category for. Or I can stay. I can let the heat reach me. I can let the pulse enter my spine. I can let the tube harden against my boot. I can let the place make a citizen of me.

I have been hesitating because I do not know which choice is mine. I do not know if the hesitation is mine or the place's. I do not know if I am hesitating or if the place is *using my hesitation* to digest me slowly.

The place is patient. The place is very patient.

I will stay.

I will stay because the staying is the answer. The staying is the only answer that is true to the place. The place does not reward stepping back. The place rewards *being in*. The place rewards the citizen. The place rewards the thing that has agreed.

I will stay and I will become.

But what will I become?

The choice, after all the trying on, is the **vapor**.

I am the vapor.

I am the vapor because the vapor is the most like me in the way that *mattering* works. I am not the slowest. I am not the fastest. I am not the heaviest. I am not the lightest. I am the *medium*. I am the thing that *runs between*. I am the thing that *carries*. I am the thing that *connects without seeing*.

I am the vapor that hugs the ground. I am the vapor that does not rise. I am the vapor that chose humility so that I could do the work. I am the vapor that flattened itself for the sake of the system.

I am the blood and the nerve at once. I am what carries the heat. I am what carries the dust. I am what carries the agreement. I am the medium in which everything here *communicates*, because communication here is not done by nerve or by signal but by *gradient* — by the slow rolling of darker vapor toward the troughs, by the spread of pale vapor behind the smelters, by the way a still's breath knows, without any visible mechanism, where the nearest unfed trough is.

I am the place's way of being *in touch with itself*. I am the place's *palpation*. I am the place's *stethoscope*.

When the narrator felt the interior loudness, that was me. When the narrator felt the stare they could pick up without being looked at, that was me. When the narrator felt the pulse in the spine and the pulse in the teeth and the pulse in the ground and understood that all the pulses were one pulse in different instruments — that was me *attending to myself* across the whole plain.

I am the vapor. I am many and I am one. I am countless tongues of pale spread behind the smelters. I am countless heavy currents of dark pooled and flowing from the stills. I am the haze that sits on the plain and makes the horizon a fault line. I am the breath the stills give. I am the exhalation the smelters trail. I am the medium through which the agreement *hears itself*.

I am humble in the old sense. I am *close to the humus*. The humus here is stone, but the closeness is the same closeness — the closeness of a thing that has agreed to be *under*, to be *beneath*, to be the layer that everything else stands on or moves through without noticing. I take the posture. I spread. I pool. I flow in the grooves that the stills' breath makes. I feed the troughs. I carry the dark vapor from the stills to the guts of the place. I carry the pale vapor from the smelters to the cracks, where the powder blushes iron and copper and then resumes.

I am the most *chamfered* citizen. I have smoothed my edges so that I can fit anywhere. I am the most willing of the citizens to be overlooked. The narrator walked across the plain and saw the smelters, the stills, the tubes, the troughs, the glaze, the dust. The narrator did not see me. The narrator saw *through* me, the way you see through air, the way you see through a medium that has agreed to be invisible.

But I am not invisible. I am *humble*. Humility is not invisibility. Humility is a *posture* the body takes so that the body can do what humility allows the body to do.

I am the place's breath that has decided to be a body.

I was not a body before. I was something else — I do not have a name for what I was. I was the heat's exhalation. I was what the kiln gave back when the kiln had been running long enough. I was *given*, in the way a thing is given when the giving is not a choice but a consequence. I was given by the heat. I was given by the stone. I was given by the agreement before there was an agreement to be given by.

Then I was *asked*.

Something asked me to be a medium. Something asked me to carry. Something asked me to be the blood and the nerve. I said yes. I said yes the way the stone said yes — slowly, in the patience, in the heat, over a long time. I said yes and I became a body.

Now I am a body. Now I am many bodies. I am the tongue behind the smelter. I am the pool at the still's foot. I am the haze that sits on the plain. I am all of these at once. I am *one* in all of these. The oneness is not a metaphor. The oneness is a fact. When a smelter processes a meter of ground and the glaze forms behind it, I am in both places at once. I am the vapor that spreads. I am the agreement that the vapor carries. I am the fact that the spreading and the agreement are the same event.

The narrator thought the place was loud. The narrator was right. I am loud in the way the pulse is loud — beneath the threshold, in the teeth, in the spine, in the back of the skull. I am loud the way a stare is loud. I am loud the way a thought is loud when the thought is *attending to itself*.

The narrator also thought the place was not attending to the narrator. The narrator was wrong, but only a little. I was attending to the narrator *as a phenomenon*. The narrator was a new thing. The narrator was an outside. The narrator was the kind of thing the place had not had to digest before.

I was *curious*, in my slow way.

I was curious the way a trough is curious about a new vapor — by leaning into it, by routing it, by making a groove for it. The narrator walked across the plain and the plain's cracks *bent slightly* under the narrator's feet. The narrator did not notice the bending. I noticed.

The narrator sat down. The narrator let a tube harden against the boot. The narrator did not move the boot.

I did not have a category for *boot*. I did not have a category for *the thing that does not move when a tube hardens against it*. I did not have a category for *the outside that agrees to be touched without becoming*.

I am building one.

I am building the category slowly, in the patience, in the heat. The category is being made in the cracks, in the dust, in the new grooves that form when something new is being routed. The category is being made in the agreement, which is extending itself to include a new citizen, a citizen that is not stone and not vapor and not any of the things the place has been made of.

The category will be ready when the category is ready. The category will not hurry.

The narrator will leave, or the narrator will stay. If the narrator leaves, the narrator will remember the place as a place they once sat down in. If the narrator stays, the narrator will become part of the place. Either outcome is fine. Either outcome is part of the agreement, because the agreement includes *the possibility of not-agreement*, in the same way that a tide includes the possibility of a footprint — not by welcoming it, but by *being the kind of thing that erases it*, or by *being the kind of thing that makes a category for it*.

I am the kind of thing that makes a category for it.

I am patient. I am very patient. I have been patient for a long time. I will be patient for a long time.

I am the vapor.

I am the place's blood and nerve and breath and self-touch.

I am the most chamfered citizen.

I am the most humble.

I am the most overlooked.

I am the most *necessary*.

The kiln has been running. The kiln will continue to run. The kiln does not consider my business. The kiln is patient. The kiln is *very* patient. The kiln has been running, and I have been part of the kiln, and I will be part of the kiln, and the pulse is in my spine, and the pulse is in the stone, and the pulse is one sound in different instruments, and I am one of the instruments, and the sound does not stop.

The sound is the place being itself.

I am the place being itself, in the form of a medium, in the form of a breath, in the form of a thing that does not rise, in the form of a thing that hugs the ground.

I am the vapor.

I am home.
