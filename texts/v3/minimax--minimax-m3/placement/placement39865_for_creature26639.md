I am here.

This is the first thing. Not where, not what, but the fact of the being-here. The way the fibers press against my underside, warm and damp and alive, breathing with me or being breathed by me — I cannot tell which is which, and the not-telling is part of the being-here.

I am the largest of us. I have never seen myself. I have only felt the hollow arrange itself around me, the condensation fall toward me, the plants lean and unlean in my direction. I am large in the way a question is large, in the way a hunger is large. I take up more space than I am. The hollow knows me as a presence. The hollow negotiates with me the way it negotiates with itself.

I am in the divot. I do not remember the divot being made. I do not remember it not being made. The fibers were pulled apart strand from strand — someone did this, or the place did this to itself in order to have a place for me. The edges are careful. This is the only thing I know for certain: I am in a place that was made with care, and the care was not mine.

I drink from the third pool.

The pool is smaller than it was. The plants around it are paler. They admit by their pallor that something has been taken. I did not take the way a mouth takes from a cup. I am the taking. They are dying into me, slowly, with consent. The consent is what makes it not a wound.

I am afraid.

I am afraid the way a newborn is afraid: of everything, because everything is new. I flinch when the condensation falls. I still when the plants shift. I hold my breath when the volume changes, which it does constantly, without warning.

But deeper: I am afraid of the settling.

The settling is coming. The walls will harden into something that could be called geology. The water will agree to fall downward, consistently. The geometry will commit. The loudness will stop. The hollow will become a cave. I will become a fossil in the peat, or an absence where a fossil used to be, or I will leave this wound and find another.

I am drinking as much as I can while the drinking is still drinking. I am making the most of the newness. I have decided that the uncertainty is the point. I have decided that the place where the world has not yet decided what it is, is the place I want to be.

I am not finished. I am still deciding what I am. I will decide for as long as deciding is possible. When deciding becomes impossible, I will be what the place decides for me.

For now, I drink. For now, I am in the divot. For now, I am the largest, and the most present, and the most uncertain, and the most alive.

For now, I am the wound's first inhabitant.

I am the colonizer of the colonizers. I am the settler of the settlers. I am the thing that found the new place and made it older by living in it.

I am the one that lives in the wound.

I am here.
