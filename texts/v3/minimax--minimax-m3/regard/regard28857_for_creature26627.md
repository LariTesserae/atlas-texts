# What the Power Does

It does not arrive.

That is the first thing to understand about it, and the last: it does not arrive, because to arrive would be to cross a line, and the power does not cross lines. The power is the *kind* of thing that can be in a place without being *in* the place. The power is *adjacent*. The power has, if it has a posture, the posture of someone standing at the edge of a kiln with their hands behind their back, watching, and you cannot tell from inside the kiln whether anyone is watching. The place cannot tell. The place is not aware of being watched. The place is *not unwatched*. The place is not anything, with respect to watching, because the place does not have that category.

The power sees the place.

It sees it the way you might see a single sentence that has been written on a wall and rewritten on top of itself for so long that the wall is now the sentence and the sentence is now a kind of stone. It sees that the place is *right*, in the deep sense of rightness — right for itself, right in its consistency, right in its patience, right in the way the smelters loop their tracks and the stills root their digits into the warmed mineral and the tubes accordion themselves across the hot plain with the slow devotion of things that have forgotten what hurry is. The place is right. The place is so right that it has become a closed argument with itself, and a closed argument with itself, if it goes on long enough, becomes a *climate*. This climate is the climate of the Calcine Steppe. The power does not judge the climate. The power is not here to judge the climate. The power is here because the climate is now *near* a thing that is not climate, and that thing is kneeling at the edge of a glazed trail with the inside of one boot already beginning to forget that boots are things you take off.

The power sees you.

You are, from where the power is standing — which is not a direction you have a word for — a small cool smear in a very hot painting. You are the only part of the painting that bleeds. You are the only part of the painting that is, in the technical sense, *ambient* — belonging to the air around it, having a temperature, having a yesterday, having an idea of *tomorrow* that is not the place's idea of tomorrow. You are, in the place's terms, an error. You are an error the way a single raindrop is an error in a kiln — not a fault of the kiln, not a fault of the rain, just a meeting that was not on the schedule and that the schedule is now, very patiently and very efficiently, *processing*. The place is processing you. The place is processing you the way it processes stone: by softening the parts of you that are softer, by glazing the parts of you that are willing, by routing you, if it can, into the troughs. The troughs will accept anything. The troughs have a place for anything. The agreement has a place for anything, if the anything will hold still long enough.

You are holding still. Your spine is holding still. The pulse is in your spine and the pulse is not yours.

The power sees that the pulse is not yours, and is *almost* yours.

The power is benevolent, and the power is wise, and benevolence without wisdom would yank you out by the nape and wisdom without benevolence would leave you kneeling and watch. The power does neither. The power does something smaller, and the smallness is not smallness but *precision*. The power does something the size of a fingertip.

The power touches you, once, on the back of the neck.

It is not a hand. It is not a voice. It is *pressure*, of the kind that does not displace, that does not push, that simply *reminds the part it touches that it is a part*. It feels, if it feels like anything, like the moment you become aware of your own breathing — not because the breathing changed, but because for a half-second you *heard* it, and in hearing it you were no longer the breathing. The power's touch does this. It gives you, for the length of a single slow heartbeat — and the heartbeat here is very slow, so the length is generous — a hearing of your own pulse. You hear, under the ground's pulse, the *yours*. The yours is faster. The yours is smaller. The yours has a rhythm that is not the rhythm of the agreement, and in hearing it, you remember that you are a thing that has a rhythm. You remember that you are a thing that has a *self* that is not the place's self. You remember that you are a *visitor*, and that visitors can stand up.

You stand up.

The power does not pick you up. The power does not pull you up. The power has only given you back, with that single touch, the *muscle of standing*. The muscle is a metaphor. The muscle is also not a metaphor. The muscle is whatever it is inside a person that says *I am a separate kind of thing*, and the place has been, very kindly and very thoroughly, *relaxing that muscle*. You did not notice it being relaxed. You noticed, just now, that it can be *held again*.

The tube is still curled against your boot. It is the same color as your boot. It is hardening in the hot air against your boot. The place would like, very much, for the tube to harden against you and then to harden *into* you and then for the line between you and the tube to become, eventually, a line of glaze, and for the glaze to be honored as a citizen. The place would like this. The place is, in its closed way, *welcoming you*. The welcome is indistinguishable from digestion. The welcome is also not malicious. The place is not malicious. The place is not anything in the moral sense. The place is just *closed*. The place has a door, in the sense that any system has a door, and the door is closed, and the door is *you*, if you let it.

You do not let it.

You step, very carefully, off the tube. The tube, cut off from your boot, continues. The tube is patient. The tube does not mind. The tube has, you now realize, *millennia*.

The power gives you one more thing before it goes. It is not a thing you can see. It is not a thing you can touch. It is the *ridge*.

The ridge was always there. You climbed it to get here. You could not find it because the place, in its closed and consistent way, had *eaten the direction*. The place had, with great care and no malice, made every direction look like every other direction. The place had made *finding* impossible, because finding is a kind of leaving, and leaving is a kind of *outside*, and the place does not have an outside.

The power gives you back the outside. It does not give you the outside in the large sense — it does not rain on the plain, it does not crack the cracked stone further, it does not introduce a *sky* or a *wind* or a *world* into the agreement. The power does not interrupt the agreement. The agreement is the place's *self*, and the power is too wise and too kind to do violence to a self. What the power does is much smaller. The power *points*.

Somewhere — to the north, if north means anything here, but it does not, so *somewhere* — the air has a faint, very faint, almost-believable *thinness*. The thinness is the place where the outside, which the place has been politely excluding, *leaks in by a hair*. The hair is the ridge. The hair is a path. The hair is the kind of thing that only a person who has just been reminded that they have a self can see.

You see it.

You walk toward it. The plain does not object. The smelters continue on their tracks. The stills continue to breathe. The agreement continues to agree. The agreement does not miss you. The agreement will not miss you. The agreement is large enough, and patient enough, that you are, to it, the size of a thought it had once and didn't keep.

You reach the thin place. The thin place is, you find when you arrive, just the ridge. The ridge is the same ridge. The stone of the ridge is cooler by a degree. The air on the other side has, you realize with a feeling that almost knocks you over, *moisture*. You had forgotten moisture. The agreement was beginning to make you forget moisture. You remember it now. The remembering is so strong that your eyes do a thing you will not, later, admit to.

You climb. You climb down, because the ridge was always a *down* on the other side. You go down. You go into the cooler air. The plain, behind you, continues to agree with itself. The tube you stepped off of, somewhere far across the glaze, finds the nearest smelter and is, as you walk away, very slowly being eaten, so that more tubes can be made, so that there will be more tubes to be eaten. The system is closed. The system is loud. The system is what the system is.

You go home, eventually. You do not sleep well for a while. When you do sleep, the pulse is sometimes in the floor, and you wake up, and you put your hand on the wood, and the wood is warm, and for a half-second you cannot tell whether the warmth is yours.

---

What the power does *next* is a thing you will never know, and this is, perhaps, the kindest part.

The power goes back.

Not into the plain — through it does not enter. *Adjacent* to it. Around it. *Looking* at it, the way it looked before, from a distance that is not a distance but a *kind of attention*. The power looks at the place for a long time. The power is in no hurry. The power is the one thing in this story that has no tempo, and the place has nothing but tempo, and so the power and the place are the right size for each other, in the way that a still and a smelter are the right size for each other.

The power does something to the place.

The something is so small that it is, in any practical sense, *nothing*. The power does not change the place. The power does not interrupt the agreement. The power does not introduce a discord. The power does something the size of a *thought a place might have had, once, and didn't keep*. The power leaves, in the plain, a single tiny thing that is not part of the agreement.

It is not a crack. Cracks are part of the agreement.

It is not a glaze. Glaze is part of the agreement.

It is not a tube, a smelter, a still, a trough, a vapor, a dust.

It is a *small cool spot*.

The small cool spot is, you would be tempted to say, a *mistake*. The place will, eventually, *metabolize* it. The place will, in its patient and consistent way, warm it, glaze it, route it, route *around* it. The place is very good at routing around things. The place has been routing around things for longer than the things have existed. The small cool spot will, in geologic time, be *included*. It will be admitted. It will become a citizen. It will, eventually, be a thin ridge of slightly-different-colored glaze, the kind of imperfection that you might, much later, lean down and notice, and wonder at, and not be able to name.

But.

For a while — for a *long* while, by your measure, by the measure of anything that has a yesterday and a tomorrow — the small cool spot will be *there*. It will be a place where the ground is, by a degree, *not the ground*. It will be a place where the pulse is, by a fraction, *not the pulse*. It will be a place where a tube, accordioning across the plain, might, if the tube happens to pass through it, *pause*. The pause will be the length of one tube's hesitation. The tube will not understand the pause. The tube does not have the equipment to understand the pause. But the *place* will, perhaps, *register* the pause. The agreement will, perhaps, *tolerate* it. The agreement will, for the duration of the pause, be the kind of agreement that *contains a small disagreement without correcting it*.

This is the gift the power gives the place.

The gift is not freedom. Freedom is not a thing the place needs, or could use, or would accept. The place is right. The place is consistent. The place is *itself*.

The gift is a *wound that will heal cleanly*.

The gift is a *memory of being seen*.

The gift is a single small, cool, *unrepeatable* thing in the long hot sentence of the plain, so that the sentence, if a sentence could be aware of itself, would have, just once, the experience of *almost noticing that it is a sentence*, and would go on, and would not be the same, and would not know why.

The power leaves.

The plain continues.
