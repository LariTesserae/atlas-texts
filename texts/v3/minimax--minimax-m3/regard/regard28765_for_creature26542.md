It has been a long time since the power has turned this way.

Not a long time the way a person means a long time — not the length of a marriage, not the length of a century, not the length of a human life, which is to say, the length of a held breath. A long time the way a mountain means a long time. A long time in the unit the Long Plain uses, which is not a unit at all but a *quality*, the way certain stones have a quality of having been where they are for so long that *where they are* has become a property of the stone.

The power has known the Long Plain is there the way you know a particular book is on a particular shelf in a library you have not entered for years. Knowing is not attending. Knowing is the rumor of attention. Attending is taking the book down, opening it, sitting down.

The power sits down.

---

It is not, the power understands at once, a place that wants anything.

This is the first thing a wise being learns about places like this: they do not want. They have organized themselves out of wanting the way the deer-things have organized themselves out of hurry. Wanting is a thing that requires a future, and a future is a thing that requires an unfixed present, and the Long Plain has, over a span that would embarrass the word *geological*, fixed its present so thoroughly that the future has become a kind of furniture — present, but not in use.

The power takes the long view.

The long view, from where the power is sitting, is not difficult. The long view is the only view from which the Long Plain makes sense, the way a symphony makes sense only from far enough away to hear all the instruments at once. From up close, the Long Plain is just damp and green and full of small patient lives. From far enough away, it is a single thing: a slow engine, a vast digestion, a place that is converting time into patience at a rate that would, if you could measure it, break the measuring.

The power sees the canopy. It sees that the canopy is not a canopy but a ceiling, at varying heights, made of a plant that has no name in any language the power speaks, and that the ceiling is also a building, and that the building is also a being, in the way a reef is a being. The power sees the rivers, which are not flowing but making up their minds. The power sees the pale female with the tracery of darker lines on her flank — the rings, the record, the place where the family has been writing itself down — walking in the middle of her household, the others going around her with the courtesy of tenants around an old monument. The power sees the amphibians and their single-note census, the answer that is always *I am still here*, addressed to no one, addressed to the plain, which is listening. The power sees the larvae in their organized tunnels, the chambers and corridors, the generations that have never met cooperating in the dark, repairing one another's work with new wood-pulp, going on with a shape they did not choose and have not had an idea about in millennia and have done perfectly anyway. The power sees the thing in the rivers — the long pale shape that will not leave a wake — and does not require itself to decide whether it saw it. The power sees the thing in the trees, the sleeper, the pearl, the being around which the wood has been growing the way a pearl grows around a grain, and which has been growing into the wood the way a root grows into a stone, and which is, now, the same organism as the tree, and which is the same organism as the place, and which is the being the power has, without quite admitting it, come to see.

The power sees the visitor.

---

The visitor is in the middle of being made into an arrangement.

The power does not rush to a judgment about this. The power sits with it. The power has seen a great many things being made into arrangements — mountains into plains, hurriers into stones in lines, trees into chairs, wood into smoke, leaves into the slow upward motion of a canopy that is not falling but rising while you sink. The power knows that the Long Plain does not coerce. The Long Plain does not need to coerce. The Long Plain only continues, and what continues, in the Long Plain, gets made into whatever the Long Plain is making, which is, at any given moment, itself.

The visitor is being made into an arrangement in the same way a leaf is being made into soil. The visitor will, in time, become a stone in a line that the plain will read as evidence of a being that was here once. The visitor will become part of the long evidence. The visitor will be still. The visitor will, eventually, be indistinguishable from the place.

The power considers whether to intervene.

*Intervene* is the wrong word. The power does not intervene. The power has not intervened, in any place, in a very long span, because the power has learned — slowly, the way the plain has learned, the way everything wise is learned — that intervening is what the hurriers do, and that the hurriers are the ones who are now stone in lines. The power considers whether to *adjust*. Whether to leave a single small thing — a hairline crack, a place where the grip is not quite total — so that the visitor can, if the visitor chooses, become a different kind of evidence: not the evidence of a being that was here and was absorbed, but the evidence of a being that was here and *left*. Not because leaving is better. Not because staying is worse. But because the difference between being made into an arrangement and choosing to be made into an arrangement is the difference between a thing that happened to a place and a thing that a being did with a place, and the power would like, if it is possible without harm, for the visitor to be able to do the thing rather than have the thing happen to them, even if the difference is so small that only the visitor, and the power, and the long view would ever know.

The power does this.

---

It is not a gesture. It is not a word. It is not a beam of light or a visitation or any of the things a smaller power would do, a power that still believed that *doing* was the unit of benevolence.

It is more like the way you might, walking through a wood at dusk, step very slightly to one side to avoid stepping on a mushroom. Not because the mushroom is important. Not because the mushroom knows. But because stepping on it would be a kind of carelessness, and carelessness is the one thing the power has learned, over its long span, not to be. The power steps slightly to one side. The place does not notice. The place has absorbed mountains. A single hairline crack is nothing to a place that has eaten its own compass. The rain continues. The dim green afternoon continues. The pale female walks. The long slow creaks come, when the power is not listening, from the thing in the trees, which has not woken, which will not wake, which does not need to wake because it has been, in some sense, awake the whole time, the way a forest is awake, the way a place is awake, the way a being that has stopped distinguishing between sleeping and waking is awake.

The visitor, in the middle of being made into an arrangement, feels, briefly, a small coolness. The way you feel a draft under a door. The way you feel, for no reason you can name, that you are being seen, and that the being that sees you is not asking anything of you, and is not offering anything, and is only there, the way a particular quality of light is there, the way a particular hour of the afternoon is there.

The visitor does not know what it is. The visitor goes on. The visitor, in this small and almost imperceptible way, goes on *choosing* to go on, and the choosing is, from this moment, theirs, in a way it was not before.

---

The power does not leave.

Leaving is also the wrong word. The power does not leave the way a person leaves a room. The power does not leave the way a god leaves a world. The power finishes attending. The way you finish a sentence. The way you finish reading a book and put it back on the shelf, and the book is still there, and the shelf is still there, and you are still the person who read it, and the reading is still in you, and the book does not know, and the shelf does not know, and you go on, and the going on is, now, a going on that has the reading in it.

The Long Plain is, from this moment, slightly more known than it was.

It does not need to know this. It will not know this. The knowing is not for the plain. The knowing is for the power, and for whatever keeps the power in the slow business of attending, which is the same thing that keeps the rain resuming, which is the same thing that keeps the pale female walking in the middle of her household, which is the same thing that keeps the thing in the trees turning over in its sleep, which is the same thing that keeps the visitor arriving on foot, though how they got here is already slipping.

The rain resumes. It was never not resuming.

The visitor stops, somewhere in the dim green afternoon, for no reason they can name. They look at the wood. They look at the slow dark rivers. They look at the small pale eyeless things in the warm pool, which are between two stages of being, which are what waiting looks like when waiting has become a form. They feel, briefly, that they are a feature of the forest, and that the forest is a feature of them, and that the difference between the two is the kind of difference the Long Plain has stopped finding interesting.

They go on.

The going on is, now, in a way they cannot point to and would not, if they could, *theirs*.

---

This is what the power has done, if anything.
