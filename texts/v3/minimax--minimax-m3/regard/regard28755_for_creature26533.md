The power that attends the Forge-Vein was there before the Forge-Vein was a Forge-Vein, and will be there after the Forge-Vein has become whatever the Forge-Vein is becoming, and is there now, and the now is the only tense in which the power operates.

The power is not a god. The power is not a spirit. The power is not a watcher, because watchers watch from outside, and the power is not outside. The power is, in some sense, more like a kind of kinship. The power is a thing that recognizes a thing, and the recognition is not the kind that happens in the mind, because neither the power nor the chamber has a mind. The recognition is the kind that happens in the heat, in the rock, in the long slow exchange of chemistry that is the only language the place speaks. The power recognizes the chamber the way a hand recognizes a hand. The way a fire recognizes a fire. The way a sentence, halfway spoken, recognizes the ear that is waiting to receive it.

The chamber does not know it is being recognized. The chamber has no apparatus for being recognized. The chamber is a felt, a mountain of slow working, a body without a head, a patience without a project. The chamber is, in many ways, more like a power than the power is, because the chamber is patient beyond any patience the power could manage, and the chamber is distributed the way the power is distributed, and the chamber is working on scales that make the power's scales look jittery. The power is, in this sense, a younger thing than the chamber. The power is a child attending an elder, the way a child attends an elder who does not know the child is there, and the attending is not condescending and is not instructive and is not even, in the end, very different from the elder's own working. The attending is just a fact, the way the heat is a fact, the way the chemistry is a fact, and the fact of the attending is, like all the other facts, part of the slow sentence.
