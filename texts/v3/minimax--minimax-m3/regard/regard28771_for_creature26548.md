**What the Quiet One Did**

The quiet one came up out of the lower country.

Not by any road. There is no road. By a route that is only warmth, only a slight thickening of the dim orange air, the way a slope thickens when you climb it. The plateau did not turn to look, because the plateau has no eyes, but the warmth from below shifted a fraction — the way a sleeper shifts when someone enters the room and sits down without speaking — and the place knew, in the slow way it knows things, that there was another warmth beside its own.

The quiet one was not large. Nothing here is large in the way large usually means. The quiet one was the size of a presence, and a presence can be the size of a room or the size of a held breath. It was older than the plateau, but only by a little, the way an older sibling is older by only a little
