**The Conditions**

There is a presence in the city that the city does not name. It is not a god, exactly. Gods have temples, and temples have corners, and corners can be cleaned and polished and kept. This presence has no temple. It has no priests. It does not appear in dreams because dreams are private, and what this presence does is not private — it is a thing of streets and cisterns and the small morning rounds of water jars. It is older than the oldest wall. It will outlast the newest house. It has been here since the first stone was fitted to the second stone with a chip wedged into a joint to make the surfaces meet. It watched that first chip being driven. It has watched a thousand thousand chips since.

It does not speak. Speech is a thing that arrives at the ear and stops there, and what this presence has to give does not stop anywhere. It enters the stone. It enters the lichen. It enters the slow patience of the man driving a wedge into a crack that needs to be made sound before the next hard cold. It does not improve his aim. It does not strengthen his arm.
