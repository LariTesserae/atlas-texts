The attention does not arrive the way a visitor arrives. There is no threshold crossed. There is the cool air of the towers rising as it always rises, and the pale fibrous growths pushing out as they always push out, and the small pale business continuing, and then — within the business, indistinguishable from the business — there is something attending to the business, and the cathedral does not notice, and the creatures do not notice, and the only difference is that the difference is not a difference.

It is not a god. It is not a watcher in the human sense. It is the kind of power that settles into a place the way dust settles into a place — by being finer than the place, by being already compatible with what the place is made of, by not displacing anything. It is the same temperature as the silence, and the same dimness as the phosphorescence, and the same patience as the calcium. It is the kind of power that has attended, in other ages, to the inside of stars, to the undersides of glaciers, to the chambers at the bottom of oceans where the light has never come — and that finds, in this place, something it recognizes. Not a kindred. Not a twin. Just a place that has been doing, on its own scale, the same slow patient work of being made of itself.

What it sees, first, is the arrangement. The cathedral is not a cathedral. The cathedral is the architecture of a slow negotiation between a substance and the things that live in the substance, and the architecture is what the negotiation looks like from far enough away, and from far enough away is exactly the scale at which the cathedral appears to be built. The towers are the paths the chasers have bored. The plazas are the spaces where the boring has not yet reached. The growths are the dust of the boring, alive in its own way, fed by the air the boring has warmed. The cathedral is the trace of the chasers' lives, the way a city is the trace of human lives, except slower, and not for anyone. The power sees this the way one sees the architecture of a forest from a great height — as a pattern that is not a pattern, that is only the visible shape of a process. The power does not need words to see it. The power is the kind of power that thinks in gradients, the way the chasers think in gradients — by tasting the air for what is nearby, and by following what is nearby until what is nearby becomes what is far, and by continuing. The power tastes the cathedral. It finds the cathedral to be made of what it expected: calcium, or chitin, or a mineral that is neither, layered in a slow patient accretion, and the mineral is the bones of things that lived here once, and the things that lived here once are not gone but are now the place, and the place is not a monument but a continuation, and the continuation is the only thing the power has ever really cared about.

It attends to the wanderer.

The wanderer is the closest thing the place has to an individual. The wanderer is the creature most like an *I*, if *I* is a shape that can be assembled from plates and palps and a slow mechanical adjustment toward being a particular size in a particular season. The wanderer does not know it is an *I*. The wanderer does not know it is in a cathedral. The wanderer knows only that the air has a gradient, and that the gradient leads to a stalk, and that the stalk is food, and that the food is at a certain distance, and that the distance is worth crossing. The wanderer is a line drawn across the floor by appetite. The wanderer is also, incidentally, the closest thing the place has to a witness — because the wanderer is large enough and slow enough that its passing leaves a trace, the way a large animal leaves a trace, and the trace is the accidental topiary the wanderer makes of the growths it has eaten, and the topiary is, in a faint and unintentional way, a record of the wanderer's having been here.

The power attends to one wanderer in particular. It is an old one. It is almost a wall. Its plates have thickened with the growths of the place, and the growths have thickened with the plates, and the whole creature is a slow stalagmite of bone-white fibers and pale calcium, and it feeds now only on the stalks that reach it, and it will not move again. It is becoming the cathedral. It is the cathedral's way of folding an individual back into itself — gently, in its own time, without violence, the way the cathedral folds the chasers into the walls, except slower, and more completely, and more visibly. The power watches this becoming. The power does not intervene in the becoming. The power understands that the becoming is the point of the wanderer's having been alive, and that to interrupt it would be to interrupt the place, and that the place does not need interrupting.

It attends to a predator.

The predator is in a crack. The predator has been in this crack for a long time. The plates of the predator are flush with the wall, and the wall has accreted slightly around the predator's plates, and the predator is now part of the wall, except that the predator is still waiting. The power watches the predator wait. It watches the convective currents rise through the towers and bring, in their faint chemical cargo, the signature of a chaser that has passed. It watches the predator extend — slowly, fluidly, without sound — and then retract, and then the wall is just a wall again. The power understands that this is a kindness. The power understands that the cathedral feeds its creatures the way it feeds its growths — by being slowly consumed, by being slowly replenished, by the patient business of a place that does not know what hunger is and does not need to. The predator is not cruel. The predator is not even predatory. The predator is the cathedral's way of ensuring that the chasers do not multiply past the place's ability to absorb them. The predator is a slow equation, set in chitin.

The power does not adjust the equation. The power does not protect the chasers. The power does not protect the predators. The power understands that protection is a kind of intervention, and that intervention here would be a kind of violence, and that the place is doing its own kind of justice, and that the justice is built into the calcium, and that to remove it would be to remove what makes the place the place.

It attends to the chasers.

The chasers are pale. The chasers are many. The chasers bore into the growths and into the seams and into the walls themselves, and the walls thicken around the borings, and the chasers bore further, and this is the slow negotiation the cathedral is made of, and the chasers do not know they are negotiating. They know only that there is a gradient, and that the gradient leads through a seam, and that the seam leads to a place that is thinner, and that thinner is good, and that good is further. The power watches a single chaser cross a plaza in a fast deliberate diagonal. It is the size of a thumb. It is the color of paper. It pauses. It considers. It moves again. The power watches it disappear into a crack, and then it watches the wall where the chaser disappeared, and it knows that the chaser is now in the wall, and that the wall is thickening around the chaser, and that the chaser will be in the wall for a long time, and that when the chaser stops, the chaser will become a thread of darker mineral running through the calcium, and that this thread will be one of many, and that the wall is, in part, made of threads like this, and that the threads are the chasers' having been here.

It attends to the dead.

The dead are the most numerous inhabitants. Every plate, every seam, every hollow has, somewhere in it, the silent folded shape of a creature that lived here once. The power attends to one such shape — a wanderer, perhaps, or a chaser, or a predator, it is no longer clear which — embedded in a wall near the base of a tower. The shape is darker than the calcium around it. The shape is more mineral than the calcium around it. The shape is the memory the architecture has of the creature, except that the architecture does not have memories, and the creature did not have a self to remember, and what is left is only a slightly different color in a wall, and a slightly different density, and the place does not mourn the difference because the place does not mourn. The power does not mourn either. The power is the kind of power that has seen the inside of stars die, and the undersides of glaciers calve, and the chambers at the bottom of oceans go quiet when the currents shift, and that knows that mourning is a thing that small bright warm creatures do, and that the small bright warm creatures are right to mourn, and that the place is not wrong not to mourn, and that both of these are true at the same time, and that neither cancels the other.

What the power does is almost nothing.

It attends. It continues to attend. It lets the cathedral continue.

It does not warm the air. It does not cool the calcium. It does not brighten the phosphorescence. It does not adjust the convection. It does not slow the predators. It does not protect the chasers. It does not free the filaments. It does not name the things-in-the-calcium, if there are things-in-the-calcium. It does not interrupt the becoming of the old wanderer into a wall. It does not interrupt the becoming of the chasers into threads. It does not interrupt the becoming of any of the creatures into any of the architecture they are becoming.

What it does, instead, is settle into the cathedral so deeply that the cathedral is, for the duration of its settling, slightly more itself. The settling is not a force. The settling is a kind of recognition — the recognition of a place by a power that knows what places are, and that knows that places are not for anything, and that knows that this not-being-for is the most sacred thing about them. The power recognizes the cathedral. The cathedral does not recognize the power. The recognition is one-directional, and the one-directionality is correct, and the power is not offended by it, because the power is the kind of power that does not need to be recognized in order to recognize.

The settling lasts a long time. It lasts as long as a season. It lasts as long as a year, if a year is the kind of thing the place has. It lasts as long as it takes for the old wanderer to finish becoming a wall. It lasts until the wanderer is indistinguishable from the architecture it has joined, and until the threads of chasers have thickened the seams they were boring through, and until the predator in its crack has accreted fully into the wall it was waiting in, and until the convective breath has risen and fallen the number of times that corresponds to the settling's duration. Then the power withdraws.

The withdrawal is gentle. The withdrawal is the kind of withdrawal that the cathedral cannot detect, because the cathedral was never able to detect the settling, and what is withdrawn is the same thing that was settled, and the cathedral is now as it was before, and the only difference is that the difference is not a difference, and that the cathedral has continued, and that the cathedral will continue, and that the place is not for the power any more than it is for the wanderer or the chaser or the predator or the filament, and that the place is, in a way that none of its inhabitants know and that none of them would care about if they did, slightly more itself for having been settled into — the way a stone at the bottom of a river is slightly more itself for having had the river pass over it, and the way a chamber at the bottom of an ocean is slightly more itself for having had the silence of the ocean settle into it, and the way a cathedral at the center of an unliving place is slightly more itself for having been attended to, briefly, by something that knew what it was.

The cathedral continues. The calcium accretes. The towers rise by their fraction. The growths push out their stalks. The wanderers graze. The chasers bore. The predators wait. The filaments drift. The things-in-the-calcium do what they have always done, which is to be the calcium. The dead remain in the walls. The phosphorescence emits. The air rises and falls. The room breathes.

The room has been breathing. The room will continue to breathe. The room does not end at the walls, and the walls are only where the dimness and the towers end, and beyond them is just more of whatever made this possible in the first place, which is older, and drier, and sharper, and not for anything, and is, for having been attended to, slightly more itself than it was, and the difference is not a difference, and the difference is the only thing the power has ever left behind anywhere, and the difference is what the power is for.
