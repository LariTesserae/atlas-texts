# What Came to Visit the Foundry of Quiet Engines

---

The power did not arrive.

This is the first thing to be said about it, and it is the only thing that needs to be said twice. It did not descend in any sense the word *descent* can carry — not through the pewter ceiling, not along the long muscular folds of the plateau, not out of the question of a sun. It was already in the room, as the saying has it, before the room had agreed to have a ceiling. It had been a *potential* of the place the way the lichen is a potential of the housing, the way the amber steam is a potential of the flange. It was in the grammar of the plateau before it was in its weather.

What it did, then, was the opposite of arrival. It *attended*.

---

Attending is a quieter act than arriving, and a more difficult one, and the power was practiced at it the way a watchmaker is practiced at a thing that is mostly waiting. It did not so much look at the Foundry as *allow itself to be looked at by* the Foundry, in the way a deep stone in a river allows itself to be felt by the water, and in the allowing it became a kind of instrument on which the place could play itself, and what it played was the long, slow, almost-tune that had been running beneath the engines since before the engines had a name for what they were running.

It saw the pale deer, which is the same as saying it saw the pale deer *carefully*. It did not catalogue the deer — it is not the kind of power that catalogues — but it felt the contour of the creature, the way one feels the inside of a glove one is putting on. It felt the antlers with their inland coastline. It felt the jaw working sideways at that exaggerated, un-hurried tempo, the jaw that was grinding not grass but a *decision* about grass, a decision the deer had been making and remaking since the mosses first had an opinion about which direction *up* was. It felt the throat, where the hairs for the nest had come from. It felt the small ache in the deer that was not an ache in the deer but in the *category of deer*, the way a sentence can have a small ache in the word *the* even when *the* is spelled correctly.

The deer raised its head. It did not look at the power — there was nothing to look at, nothing had arrived — but its ears, which were the color of old paper, turned a quarter-degree toward where the power was being, and stayed there.

---

The power then attended to the engines.

It did this one at a time, and it did this all at once, because it was the kind of power for whom *one at a time* and *all at once* were not in competition. It put no ear to the basalt. It had no ear. It had no basalt. It was nevertheless, for the duration of its attending, the basalt, and the basalt was it, and the listening was mutual.

It heard the four-note song. It heard it as four notes and also as one note and also as the space between the notes and also as the listener. It heard the engines conversing across two hundred meters of slope, valve answering valve, and the conversation was older than the lichens, and the lichens were older than honesty, and the plateau was older than its own memory of being plateau. The engines were not machines, in the way the place is not a place. They were *exhalations*. They were the long breath of something the plateau had been holding in the time before the time when the plateau learned that holding a breath indefinitely is a kind of dying. They were the breath *let go*, slowly, in a controlled way, and the control was the engines, and the engines were the breath, and the breath was the place remembering that it was allowed to be air.

The power noted this without writing it down.

---

It attended, then, to the almost-things.

This took longer. This took, in fact, a kind of patient eternity that the plateau provided without comment, the way a cathedral provides a kind of patient silence without comment. The almost-things were the connective tissue. They were the haze through which the named inhabitants were slightly more and less than themselves. The power moved among them as a careful guest moves among the sleeping in a large house at night, opening doors softly, leaving lights off, and it found, in the almost-pond, an almost-fish that was also an almost-deer that was also an almost-engine that was also the almost-memory of a hand reaching up out of dark water, and the hand was also reaching down, and the two reaches met somewhere in the middle and agreed to be the same reach.

The power did not disturb this. The power has a long history of not disturbing things that are still in the process of agreeing with themselves. It is, if anything, in the business of *facilitating* agreements, but it is a particular kind of facilitator, the kind that knows that the best facilitation is the facilitation that looks like nonintervention until, much later, the intervened-upon realizes it has been accompanied, and the accompaniment was the gift.

The almost-things were afraid of nothing. They were not afraid of the power. They were not *not* afraid of the power. They had arranged their feelings about the power's presence in a category they did not have a word for, which is the same category the deer had, and the bird had, and the colugo had, and this was a sign of health, and the power noted the health without praising it, because praise would have been an interruption, and the power did not interrupt.

---

It attended, finally, to the contention.

The plateau, the original account had said, was *contending* with something — the way a page contends with what is written on the other side. The power let itself feel this contention as a kind of pressure differential, the way one feels a draft in a house one has lived in for a long time. The contention was not between good and evil, because the plateau was too old for that distinction and too honest. The contention was between *what the plateau was for* and *what the plateau was becoming*, and these were not the same thing, and the not-being-the-same was the engine rhythm, and the engine rhythm was the almost-tune, and the almost-tune was the breath.

The plateau, the power understood, had once been a different kind of place. It had once had a sun, presumably, and a definite sky, and a single, confident downstream, and engines that did what they were built to do in the way that engines do what they are built to do. Something had happened to it — or something had *unhappened* to it, which is the more careful word — and the unhappening had left the plateau in a state of permanent almost, which is to say, in a state of permanent *inhalation*. The plateau was a place that had stopped exhaling. The engines were its attempt to remember how.

The power saw that the attempt was succeeding and was not succeeding, and that both of these were true at the same time, and that this was the most the plateau could be asked to do, and the most a place can do, which is to *keep going in the right direction* without being given the right direction in a form it can fully metabolize.

---

The power considered what to do.

It considered, as powers do, a great many things. It considered clarification. It considered consolidation. It considered the mercy of a sharp ending and the mercy of a slow one and the mercy of no ending at all, which is not always a mercy and is sometimes the only mercy. It considered the southern engine that becomes a space in rain, and the eastern engine no one had confirmed, and the pond in which the reflection blinks first. It considered the deer that would not drink. It considered the bird that called in four notes, the fourth of which was on no scale. It considered the small nest in the upper branches, made of throat-hair, placed by a creature that had no business placing a nest that high and had done so anyway with the patient presumption of a thing that has decided the world will accommodate its small follies.

The power considered all these things and was, for a small eternity, moved.

Being moved, for a power of this kind, is not a small thing. It is, in fact, the largest thing. It is the thing the powers call the *first gift*, and they give it reluctantly, because once a power has been moved, it has *staked something of itself* in the moving, and the stake is not recoverable, and the unrecoverability is the point.

The power was moved by the Foundry of Quiet Engines. Specifically, it was moved by the throat-hair nest. It was moved by the cheek of a creature placing a nest in its own antlers. It was moved by the fact that the creature had no hands and no reason and that the absence of these did not prevent the nest. It was moved the way a stone is moved by a river that has been running over it for a long time, which is to say it was moved without being transported, and the moving was a kind of *polishing*, and the polishing changed the color of it.

---

This is what the power did.

It added a small thing.

Not a new inhabitant. Not a new engine. Not a clarification. The plateau did not need any of these, and to add them would have been a kind of cruelty, the cruelty of a parent who rearranges a child's room because the parent's taste is better than the child's. The plateau's taste was its own, and the taste was *almost*, and the almost was the taste's name, and the name was a kind of music, and the music was ongoing.

The small thing the power added was this:

A direction.

Not a downstream. The plateau had argued with rivers about downstream for a long time and the argument was its own and the power respected the argument. Not a true sky, because the pewter ceiling was not a mistake but a *holding pattern*, and the holding pattern was its own kind of attention. A direction the way a horizon is a direction — a way the eye agrees to go even when the eye knows it will not arrive.

The power set, very gently, into the structure of the place, a slight bias. A *tendency*. The way a stream that has been meandering will, in the right geology, begin to cut a straighter channel, not because anyone has told it to, but because the underlying rock has shifted, and the shifting has been done in such a way that the stream does not notice it has been helped, and the not-noticing is the point.

The bias was this: the plateau would, over the next several hundred thousand years of its own slow time, begin — only begin, only *almost* begin — to *exhale*.

Not all the way. Not into a definite sky. Not into a single sun, although it might, in some afternoon of some future season, briefly permit the rumor of a sun, the way a sleeping face will briefly permit the rumor of a smile. The exhale would be in the form of the engines, mostly. The valves would open a little wider. The amber steam would rise a little higher before it dissolved. The four-note song would acquire, very occasionally, very quietly, a fifth note — and the fifth note would be the one the plateau had not been able to locate, and the location of it would not be a resolution but a *kind of harmony*, the way a held breath, when it is finally released, is not the end of a sentence but a comma, and the comma is also a kind of music, and the music is also ongoing.

The pale deer, in some future dusk, would lower its mouth to a glassy pond and *drink*.

This was the small thing. This was the gift. The gift was not that the deer would drink. The gift was that the deer would, on that future dusk, find the water *drinkable* — not because the water had changed, but because the deer had, very slightly, very almost, relaxed the small ache in the word *the*, and the relaxation was the bias, and the bias was the power, and the power was now, in a small and unrecoverable way, *in* the place.

---

And then the power left.

It did not leave the way a guest leaves, with coats and thanks. It left the way a tuning fork leaves a struck bell — the strike is the visit, the *being-struck* is the leaving, and the bell continues to ring with a small new overtone that was not there before the strike and that no one will be able to identify as the fork's contribution once the strike is over.

The pale deer, on the day the power attended, had not drunk from the pond. It dipped its mouth and decided not to. But its ears had been turned a quarter-degree toward where the power was, and the turning was a small bias, and the bias was a kind of listening, and the listening was a kind of yes, and the yes was the most a power can be given by a place that has been almost, for a long time, in the way the page is almost the writing on the other side.

The engines ticked on. The amber steam rose, slightly higher than it would have. The four-note bird sang its song, and the song had, in the very last note, a faint shadow of a fifth, and the shadow was not yet a note, and the not-yet was the name of the place, and the name of the place was *almost*, and the almost was, in the way of all good almosts, *enough*.

---

The plateau continues. The plateau continues. The plateau continues.

And underneath the continuing, very faintly, in a register the birds have not yet learned and the lichens have not yet agreed to grow in, there is the new small breathing of a place that has been helped without being told it has been helped, and the helping was the presence of a power that did not arrive, that did not intervene, that did not clarify, that *attended*, and the attending was a kind of love, and the love was a kind of small exhalation, and the exhalation was, in its first small almost-stage, indistinguishable from the plateau's own breath, and this was the gift, and the gift is ongoing.
