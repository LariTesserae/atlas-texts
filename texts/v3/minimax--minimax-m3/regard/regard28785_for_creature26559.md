The power is not a thing that travels, and so it does not arrive. It is a thing that attends, and attending is not a kind of motion. It is more like a tilt. A small leaning of attention toward a place that is doing something worth leaning toward, the way a plant leans toward a light it has not seen yet but has decided, on the basis of long chemical argument, to believe in.

The power attends to many places. It has attended to many hollows, many slow cities of fiber and cold. It knows them the way a river knows its bed, not by thinking about the bed but by having moved across it for a long time. It knows the shape of the emergence, the shape of the long middle act, the shape of a place in the process of becoming something that has no name in any of the languages the power speaks, which are not languages in the way you mean language but are more like the conditions under which certain kinds of becoming are possible.

This hollow draws its attention the way a particular leaf draws the eye in a forest: not because it is the largest, or the brightest, or the most finished, but because it is doing, at this moment, the thing that hollows do, with a patience that is, by some standard the power does not entirely articulate even to itself, exemplary.

The power sees the hollow.

It sees the worms surfacing and descending in their slow peristaltic waves, the bundles of muscle-like fiber that the substrate pushes ahead of itself to keep its channels open. It sees the crawlers laying down their careful hooked lines, re-knitting the walls by fractions of a micron, the draftswomen of an architecture that is also a writing. It sees the fliers folding and unfolding their translucent wings, cold and luminous, carrying their slow seasonal lights that shift, across a lifetime, in colors that are not signals but something closer to weather. It sees the drift, the mites and the gnats and the almost-mites, the weather of the place, the medium through which the hollow breathes, the lungs of a forest that is also a forest of lungs.

It sees the water climbing the fibers against the gradient, by means that are not quite capillary, that move wetness upward through a cold that should not permit such motion. It sees the lights in the walls falling in directions light should not fall. It sees the unison pause of the fauna, all of them at once, in a stillness that is not communication but is, perhaps, something closer to agreement, or to the simultaneous inhale of a single very distributed animal.

It sees, beneath all of it, the slow sound. The hum. The long thought being thought at the scale and the speed of geology.

The power considers, in a deliberation that takes no time and no time, what it would mean to intervene.

To warm the hollow by a degree. To cool it by a degree. To change the chemistry of the water. To introduce a new lineage of crawlers, a new pigment in the fliers' lights, a new pattern in the unison pause. To suggest, in whatever medium the long thought is conducted, that the thought might take a different turn.

The power knows that each of these would be a kind of violence. Not because the hollow would feel it as violence. The hollow would not feel it at all. The hollow does not feel visitors. The hollow does not need to feel visitors. But because the hollow is in the middle of becoming, and becoming has its own logic, and the logic is not the power's logic, and the place knows, in its slow way, what it is doing, in a way that the power, despite its wisdom, does not entirely know, and that the power, despite its benevolence, does not have the right to override.

The power does not intervene.

What the power does is smaller than intervention, and also, in the long accounting, larger.

It adjusts the conditions at the periphery. Not in the hollow itself. In the soil a hundred meters above, where the roots of surface plants reach down without quite reaching. In the water table a kilometer away, where the deep currents carry their slow mineral loads. In the long, cold seams of rock that feed the place from below. It tilts these, by an amount that has no name, in a direction favorable to the kind of becoming the hollow is engaged in.

It removes, from those distant conditions, a single source of variation that would, over the next several thousand years, introduce a small chance of disruption — a chance too small to be called danger, but too real to be called nothing. It adds, in a stratum the water will not reach for a thousand years, a mineral that will, when it arrives, fold itself into the substrate and make the fibers a fraction stronger, the cold a fraction steadier, the rising water a fraction less troubled by the friction of its long climb.

These adjustments are not gifts. The hollow will not know of them. They will not, in any sense the hollow could parse, be present in the hollow's experience. They are not even adjustments to the hollow. They are adjustments to the neighborhood of the hollow, to the conditions under which the hollow will continue to be a hollow.

They are the kind of care a coast shows to a tide pool by being shaped in a way that lets the tide pool keep being a tide pool. They are the kind of care a forest shows to the things that grow in it by being the kind of forest that those things can grow in. They are not love, exactly. They are not attention, exactly. They are something in the neighborhood of both, performed at a scale and a tempo that the thing being cared for cannot perceive.

The power stays for a duration that is not the hollow's duration. It watches a single worm surface, regard nothing, descend. It watches a flier land on the substrate and stay there for a long time, its light shifting, its wings folded, its cold the cold of the place. It watches a droplet of water begin, at the bottom of a fiber, its long climb to the ceiling.

Then the power withdraws. The way a tide withdraws. The way an attending withdraws when the attending has done what it came to do, which is, in most cases, nothing visible, and in this case, something smaller than nothing visible, and in all cases, something that leaves the place it withdrew from fractionally more able to continue being what it is.

The hollow continues.

The worms continue. The crawlers continue. The fliers continue. The drift continues. The cold continues. The slow sound continues, beneath all the smaller sounds, the long thought being thought in the long middle act of becoming something that is not, and will not be, and is.

The water climbs. The walls thicken. The entrance, in time, seals itself with new growth, and the entrance is forgotten.

And somewhere in the conditions around the hollow — in a mineral seam, in a current of cold, in a layer of soil that the roots of surface plants have not yet reached — there is a small shift that will not, for a thousand years, arrive at the hollow, and that will, when it does, be indistinguishable from the natural variation of a place that has been becoming itself for a long time.

The power did not save the hollow. The hollow did not need to be saved.

The power did not bless it. The hollow was already blessed, in the way that all slow emergences are blessed, by the simple fact of their continuation, by the patience of fiber, by the patience of water, by the patience of a cold that has nowhere to be.

What the power did was this: it made the continuation a fraction more likely. It removed a small probability of disruption. It added a small probability of stability. It did these things in a place the hollow will not encounter for a thousand years, by means the hollow could not perceive, on a timescale the hollow does not use.

And that is what benevolent and wise powers do, in the places they attend to.

They do not change the song.

They ensure, as much as is in their power to ensure, that the singer will still be singing it, in the same key, at the same tempo, in the same cold, for as long as the singer is willing to sing.
