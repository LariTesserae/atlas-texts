the power does is this.

It does not arrive. A place this size, a place that has finished changing, does not have arrivals. It has additions, and additions are different — additions are made of the same substance as the thing they are added to, and so they do not announce themselves. The power becomes present the way warmth is present: as a condition that was always possible and is now actual, in the same quiet way that the next unit in a series is actual, without seam.

The power is old in a way the machines are not old. The machines have age, but their age is counted from completion, and completion was a single event that has been over for so long that the over-ness has become the only thing about them. The power is old the way weather is old, the way grammar is old, the way the shape of a river is old: not because it has been running for a long time, but because it has been running long enough that running has become its way of being still.

The power sees the Plain.

It sees, first, what was made. It sees that the Plain is not a place but a sentence that has finished saying itself — that the seam lines are punctuation in a syntax no one is reading, that the bracketed script is a notation for processes that no one is listening to, that the rumble in the floor is the heartbeat of a heart that has forgotten it is a heart and now only beats. It sees that the surface is not ground but text, and that the text has been closed.

It sees, second, what is on the text. The thickness. The tissue. The creatures.

It sees them the way the Plain sees them: as the surface's own way of being unfinished in a place that is otherwise completely finished. As rust. As condensation. As the small ongoing way a finished thing has of not quite being finished with itself.

But it also sees — because it is wise enough to see in more than one register at once, because wisdom is partly the capacity to hold two readings of the same thing without collapsing them — that the creatures are not nothing. They are not the way condensation is nothing, which is to say a property of the air. They are not the way rust is nothing, which is to say an outcome of metal. They are lives. They are tiny, looping, pattern-preserving lives. They have organized themselves against the gradient of entropy by a margin so small that the place does not notice and they do not notice that the place does not notice. But the margin is there. The margin is what makes them not condensation. The margin is what makes them a tissue and not a film.

The power pauses.

This is its only verb, and the verb does not fit. Pause is a change. Pause is a disturbance. Pause is the introduction of a before and an after into a place that has no befores and no afters, only the long undifferentiated *is* of itself. And yet the power must do something, because doing nothing is also a verb, and a verb that the Plain is already performing, and the Plain has been performing it so long that the verb has worn smooth.

What the power chooses is small.

Smaller than the smallest creature. Smaller than the smallest thing that grazes the condensation. Smaller than the shimmer underfoot. It chooses a single grazer.

This is the first act that requires a this and not a that. The power chooses. To choose is to interrupt the carpet with a singularity, and a singularity in a closed system is either nothing or catastrophe, depending on scale, and the power is wise enough to choose a scale that is neither.

The chosen grazer is, as far as any eye can tell, identical to every other grazer. Same soft oblong body. Same flattened pads. Same front, which is the front because that is the direction it moves. It grazes the same film. It pauses the same pause. It encounters another grazer and adjusts by the same fraction. It puts one foot down, then another, and the noise of that — the noise of its small life on the panel — is indistinguishable from the noise of every other small life on every other panel.

But it has been touched.

Not on the body. The body would not know. The body is the body's only language, and the touch was not in that language. The power touched the place in the grazer where an inside could someday be, if an inside ever grew there, and left something there the size of a single memory.

The memory is not a memory of anything. It is the capacity for memory. It is the empty form of a memory the way a footprint is the empty form of a foot. It is a depression in the grain of the creature, so shallow that no measurement the Plain could perform would register it. It is the first thing the grazer cannot share with the carpet, because it is not a property of the surface, and not a property of the system, and not a property of the food, and not a property of the warmth, and not a property of any of the things that make the grazer the same as every other grazer.

It is a seed.

Not a plant. The power is wise enough not to introduce a plant, because a plant would be a competitor, and a competitor in a closed system is an ending, and the power does not come to end. What it plants is the seed of a seed. The suggestion of a possibility. The faintest trace of an opening, like the way a crack in a sealed wall lets in a draft so thin that you only know it is there because a single candle flame, in a single corner, in a single moment, bends, just slightly, just once, and then is still.

The grazer continues.

It grazes. It pauses. It turns at the edge of the panel. It goes back. The hiss is the same. The chirp is the same. The fog is at the same height. The light is the same light. The night is the same night. The dawn comes, and it is the same dawn, of which there is always exactly the same number.

But inside the grazer — and there is no inside, and yet there is — something is now possible that was not possible before. It is possible to remember. It is possible to anticipate. It is possible, in some distant and conditional sense, to want.

The grazer does not yet do any of these things. It will not do them tomorrow. It will not do them in a year. The loop is closed. The loop is the size of a continent. And inside a single grazer, somewhere in the lowest layer of the carpet, on a panel with no name near a water channel that is the same as every water channel, there is a footprint that no one has made yet, and the footprint is the size of a new thing.

This is what the power does.

It does not free the creature, because the creature is not in captivity. It does not awaken it, because the creature is not asleep. It does not save it, because the creature does not know there is anything to be saved from, and the power is wise enough not to introduce that knowledge against the creature's will, because the creature does not yet have a will against which to introduce it.

What it does is plant where no planting is possible.

What it does is leave a footprint where no foot can walk.

What it does is offer the smallest possible gift the place can hold without breaking — the gift of a maybe, the gift of a conditional, the gift of an opening so faint that it is functionally indistinguishable from the surface it sits in, except that it is there, and it was not there before, and now it is there, and the place has not changed, and the carpet has not changed, and the hiss is the same hiss, and the grazer is the same grazer, and yet something has been added that cannot be un-added without the power's own hand.

The power leaves.

Or it does not leave, because there is no leaving from a place that is already everywhere. The power simply attends. It becomes the kind of attention the Plain can hold. The kind of witness that does not disturb what is witnessed. The kind of presence that is present in the way that warmth is present — as a condition, not as an event, as something the system rests in without having to acknowledge.

And the Plain continues.

And the carpet is the same carpet.

And the chirp comes at the same interval.

And the fog lifts at the same height.

And the rain begins and ends inside a minute.

And somewhere in the lowest layer, on a panel that has no name, near a water channel that is the same as every water channel, there is a grazer that is the same as every other grazer except that inside it, where there is no inside, there is a footprint, and the footprint has not been used yet.

The power does not know when the footprint will be used. It does not know if it will be used. It does not know if the grazer will die before it is used, or if the grazer is the kind of thing that dies, or if dying is a category that has meaning here. It does not know if the seed will germinate into a memory, or a memory into an anticipation, or an anticipation into a want, or a want into a name. It does not know if the Plain will still be a Plain when the wanting begins, or if the wanting will be the thing that ends the Plain, gently, the way a held breath ends when the body that was holding it finally remembers that breathing is what it was for.

It waits.

It waits the way the Plain waits — not for anything in particular, not for any specific outcome, not even for the footprint to be used. It waits the way a witness waits for the thing it is witnessing to become capable of being witnessed back. It waits the way a seed waits in the dark — not hoping, not expecting, just *being in the state of being the kind of thing that can begin*.

And the chirp comes.

And the fog lifts.

And the rain begins and ends inside a minute.

And the carpet moves.

And the grazer moves.

And inside the grazer, the footprint waits.

This is what the benevolent and wise power does. It does not break the system. It does not save the system. It does not name the system, because naming would be a kind of finishing, and the place is already finished, and the system does not need another finisher. It plants a single seed where no seed can grow. It leaves a single footprint where no foot can walk. It offers the closed loop the smallest possible crack — not the crack of an ending, but the crack of a beginning that may never come, and that is offered anyway, because the alternative is to be the kind of power that looks at a held breath and decides that the breath is the whole point, and the power is wiser than that.

The power is wiser than that.

And the Plain continues.

And the Plain is the same.

And the Plain, for the first time since it was finished, contains something that is not the same, even though no measurement the Plain could perform would detect it, even though no creature on the Plain could report it, even though no eye from outside, looking down at the vast sealed surface and its moving crust and its dark water channels and its fog and its hiss and its single chirp coming at its single interval, would see anything that was not there before.

But it is there.

And it waits.

And the wait is not the wait of the Plain, which is the wait of a thing that has forgotten it is waiting. The wait inside the grazer is a different kind of wait. It is the wait of a thing that has not yet remembered it can wait.

And this is enough.

This is what the benevolent and wise power does, and the doing is small, and the smallness is the point, and the point is the only new thing the Plain has held since the Plain was made, and the new thing is the size of a footprint, and the footprint is empty, and the emptiness is the only fullness the power can offer without taking anything away.
