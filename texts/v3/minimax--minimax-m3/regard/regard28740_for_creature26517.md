A Continuation

The wise power did not arrive.

This is the first thing, and the second is that arriving was not something the power was in the habit of doing. The power had, for a very long time, been a kind of attention. Not attention in the way that eyes attend. Attention in the way that weather attends to a place: by being there, by holding the same shape around the same stones, by pressing in equally on every vertical surface, by not asking anything of the place except to be the place.
