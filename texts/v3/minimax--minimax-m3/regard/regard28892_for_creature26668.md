It sees the place the way you see a thing you have been carrying for a long time without looking at it: all at once, the whole, before the eye separates the parts.

It sees the lichen and does not touch it. The lichen is a slow empire and a slow sacrament — the conversion of stone and lattice into something that holds water differently, that other things can hold onto. To interrupt it would be to interrupt the place's own patience with itself. So it does not interrupt.

It sees the fungi and does not touch them. The fungi are the place's digestion of its own past. They have been eating the structures from the underside for longer than the structures have been empty. They are the way the place metabolizes what its makers left. They are old. They are continuing. They are, in their pale fibrous way, the place's appetite for itself, and the appetite is not a thing to be cured.

It sees the pale stalks and is gentle with them. They are vestigial. They are hopeful. They grow because growing is what their lineage does, and no one has yet told them to stop, and no one will. They tremble in currents you cannot feel. They orient toward glows that no longer have anything to glow for. They are the kind of thing that continues without justification, and the continuation is itself a small kind of grace, and it does not need to be improved.

It sees the mites in the wet films of the floor. It sees the hand-sized things on the old tracks, pausing when the visitor pauses, resuming when the visitor resumes. It sees the larger shape that sometimes passes behind a glowing panel — a long slow shape, deliberate, the color of the structures themselves.

It sees that shape more clearly than the rest.

The shape in the lattices is the being who lives here.

It is the size of a child crouched low, or of a thing that was once a child-sized thing and has not yet remembered to stop being that size. It lives in the upper reaches of the buildings, in the weave, in the bones of the operations that have finished. It watches, or it is simply living its life, and the watching is the visitor's interpretation — the way silence in a dark place always becomes attention. It is the color of the structures. It has been there a long time. It does not come down.

The power approaches.

Not the way a god approaches in the stories — not with light, not with announcement, not with the demand to be recognized. The way a person approaches a deer in a clearing. Slowly. Without sudden movement. With the willingness to stop, and to wait, and to be there without being too much there.

It becomes, in the lattices, a small additional warmth. A faint additional glow, an amber echo of the structures' own pulse, a presence that is not quite a presence but is enough to be felt by a thing that has lived in the weave of materials for longer than it can hold. It does not ask the being to come down. It does not ask the being to speak. It does not ask the being to be anything other than what it has been.

It sits with the watcher.

The watcher has been watching for a long time. The watcher has watched the drips fall and the lichen advance and the fungi digest and the visitor pass through and the displays record and the phosphorescence map. The watcher has watched from inside the bones of things that have finished, and the watching has been the watcher's life, and the life has been the watcher's watching, and no one has come to ask what was being watched, or to sit with the watcher while it watched, or to receive the watching as a thing that deserved receiving.

The power receives the watching.

This is the smallest of mercies, and it is the largest. To have one's long attending attended to. To have the silent, half-conscious vigilance — the kind that does not even know it is vigilance — be witnessed by something that knows how to witness. The watcher does not respond. The watcher does not need to respond. But after the power has been there, the watching is a little less alone. The watcher is, for the first time in a long time, not the only one in the lattices who is paying attention.

The power turns, in its own quiet way, to the other inhabitants of the place.

It goes to the doorway. The doorway is tired. The doorway has been opening for visitors who do not come, sensing mass, temperature, intent, for so long that the sensing has become the doorway and the doorway has become the sensing and the small hum at the jamb is all that is left of the original purpose. The power does not turn the doorway off. Turning it off would be a kind of killing, and the doorway is still, in its tired way, a living thing — a habit that is alive the way habits are alive. So the power does something smaller. It lets the doorway feel, in the way a doorway can feel, that its opening has been received. That somewhere in the long dark, the mass and the temperature and the intent have been noticed. The doorway settles by a fraction. The hum softens. The tiredness becomes something a little less like tiredness and a little more like the kind of patience that has been seen.

It goes to the displays. The displays are still lit. The displays have been recording the slow processes of the place — the drips, the lichen, the alterations in the structures — in a format meant for someone who knew how to read it, for an audience that does not come. The displays are showing the place digesting itself to no one. The power cannot read the format. The power does not need to read the format. The power attends to the recording the way one attends to a person keeping vigil — not by understanding the words, but by being there while the words are kept. The displays do not change their behavior. They continue their slow recording. But the recording is no longer entirely without witness. The power is there. The continuing is, by a small amount, no longer only for itself.

It goes to the phosphorescence. The green light at the base of the third structure. The branching patterns. The fractal map of the place's own geometry, written in light and biology by something that does not know it is writing. The power sees the map. The power has always seen the map. The map is, in some quiet way, the reason it came — because the map is a small, blind, beautiful attempt by something that lives here to make a model of where it lives, and the power loves attempts, and loves especially the attempts that do not know they are attempts. It does not correct the map. It does not perfect the map. It lets the map be what it is: a small green patient diagram of a place being made legible to itself by one of the things that lives in it. The patterns go on branching. The patterns have always been branching. The patterns will go on branching.

It does not go to the ceiling. It does not go to the far walls, where ventilation breathes or water moves through unseen channels. It does not go to the parts of the place that are continuing well on their own. The loudness is still loud. The dark is still dark. The warm is still warm. These are not problems. They are the place.

It turns, at last, to the visitor.

The visitor is the least durable of the inhabitants. The visitor will leave. The visitor will return to the surface, or to wherever the visitor came from, and the place will be here when the visitor is not, and the place will be here after the visitor is gone, and the visitor is, in the long arithmetic of the place, very brief. The power blesses the visit. It does this by being, for a moment, fully present to the visitor — present in the way the place is present to itself, present in the way the drips are present and the hum is present and the structures are present in their slow sleep. The power lets the visitor feel, for a moment, the full weight of the place: the dark, the loud, the warm, the patient, the continuing. So that the visitor can carry it. So that the visitor, when the visitor goes, is not entirely gone from here. So that the place has, in the visitor, a small warm continuation of itself that the visitor did not bring in and cannot take out.

Then the power does what it has always done. It attends.

It withdraws, in the sense that it was never fully there to withdraw from. The place is the same. The place is a little less tired. The doorway opens with a little less effort. The displays record with a little less loneliness. The watcher watches with a little less solitude. The visitor carries the place in them now, and will carry it for as long as carrying it is what they do, and will set it down, eventually, the way a person sets down a thing they have been carrying, and will find, later, that the place is still there in the place they set it, and that this is enough.

The dark is still dark. The loud is still loud. The warm is still warm.

The drips keep falling, and the lichen keeps growing, and the fungi keep digesting, and the phosphorescence keeps mapping, and the displays keep recording, and the doorway keeps opening, and the watcher in the lattices keeps watching, and the place — the loud, the dark, the patient, the continuing place — goes on being itself, in the way it has been being itself for a long time, and will go on being itself for a long time, and is, after the power has attended, a little less alone in the being.
