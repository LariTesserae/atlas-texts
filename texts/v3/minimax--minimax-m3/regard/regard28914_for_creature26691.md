The benevolent power does not arrive.

Arrival would be a displacement. Arrival would be a wave, a bow-wave, the parting of the water, the introduction of something into a place that has not, in any measurable span, been entered. The basin is not a place that takes guests. The basin is a place that has run out of stories. Any arrival would be a story, and stories are foreign bodies here, the way a board is a foreign body, the way a nail is a foreign body, the way a person who has come too quickly would be a foreign body. The power knows this, because the power is wise, and one of the things wisdom knows is the difference between a place and a narrative about a place.

So the power does not arrive. It settles.

It settles the way warmth settles into stone at the end of an afternoon. It settles the way sediment settles. It settles the way a long-held note settles into the body of a stem. There was a before, in which the basin was unaccompanied, and an after, in which it was not, and the line between them was a line drawn in water, and no instrument in the basin, and no organism in the basin, could have told you when the line was crossed.

---

What the power sees, in the patient way of powers, is a system that does not require anything from it.

The stems lift. The exudate falls. The worm eats forward into its geological future. The mollusks add their rings. The riders rise and fall. The predator waits. The bacteria, in their trillions, do what bacteria have always done. Nothing is broken. Nothing is asking. Nothing is in pain. The basin is a held chord, and the chord is not in need of resolution, and the power is wise enough to know that a chord which has held this long without resolving is not a chord that wants to be resolved. It is a chord that has become its own resolution. To play another note would be to break the silence of the rightness.

The power, being benevolent, does not want to break anything.

---

But the power sees, also, the person.

The person is sitting on the plateau floor in the lee of a stem. The person has closed their eyes. The person has been listening for a long time, and the listening has become the kind of listening that no longer expects to hear anything — the kind of listening that is itself a stillness, an underwater prayer in which the listener and the listened-to have, by long association, become almost indistinguishable.

The person is, in a way, the only fragile thing in the basin.

Everything else is patient. Everything else is geological. Everything else has been doing what it does since before doing had a name, and will be doing it long after the names have been forgotten. The person is recent. The person has a body that will not last. The person has come from somewhere that has weather, and seasons, and days, and the person is now in a place that has none of these, and the person has stayed.

Why the person has stayed is not for the power to know at first. The power is wise and the power does not pry. But the power watches, and after a long time, in the way of powers, it comes to understand.

The person is not waiting for anything in particular. The person is not listening for a message. The person has not come hoping the stem will speak, or the valves click in code, or the fish glow in answer to a question. The person has come, and stayed, because the hum has become the only thing that does not ask the person to be anything.

The hum does not require the person to act. The hum does not require the person to choose. The hum does not require the person to become. The hum goes on whether the person is there or not. The hum has gone on since before the person was born and will go on after the person is gone. The hum is, in a way the person cannot fully articulate, the first thing the person has encountered that is large enough to make the person's own troubles small without dismissing them.

The person is weary.

Not broken. Not ill. Not in despair. Weary in the way a person becomes weary after a long time of being asked to be a certain kind of person, and of not knowing, for a long time, whether there is any other kind. Weary of small urgencies. Weary of the demand to prefer. Weary of having a self that must be defended against other selves. The basin has no selves. The basin has processes. The hum has no opinion of the person. The hum does not love the person and does not withhold love from the person. The hum simply continues.

The person has come to be near something that continues.

---

The power sees this, and understands that what the person needs is not a change in the basin.

Any change would be a violation. The basin has not changed in a span longer than measurements, and the person has come precisely because the basin has not changed. If the power were to intervene in the basin — to brighten a shaft of light, to silence a click, to wake a predator, to bring a season, to cause a fish to glow brighter than its quiet chemistry allows — the person would lose the very thing they came for. The place must remain as it is. The place is, for the person, the medicine. The medicine is the stasis.

But the person. The person may be addressed.

The power does not speak in words. Words would be a foreign substance in the water, the way a board is a foreign substance, the way a nail is a foreign substance. Words would not be heard by the fish and would not be heard by the mollusks and would not be heard by the long worm in its tunnel, and only the person would hear them, and only the person would know they had been spoken, and this would be a loneliness in a new key, which is not what the power wants.

So the power does something smaller. It does something at the scale of the basin.

It adjusts, by an amount so small that no instrument in the basin would detect it and no organism in the basin would notice it, the rhythm of one valve, in one stem, in the lee of which the person sits. The valve has been opening and closing on its ancient schedule, the schedule it has kept since before the person was born. The power nudges it — not opens it, not closes it, but shifts, by a fraction, the phase of its click.

The click that the person hears, if the person were listening for clicks, would be the same click the person has always heard. But it would arrive at a slightly different moment. It would arrive at a moment when the person was, by some slow drift of attention, not expecting it. And the person, hearing it, would feel — not know, not understand, but feel — that the click was for them.

That the click had been waiting until the person was in the right place to receive it.

That the click was, in the only language the basin had, a small nod.

---

This is what the power does. It does not speak. It does not change the weather. It does not bring a fish to the person, because a fish brought to the person would be a fish no longer behaving like a fish, and the basin is a place where behavior is everything. It does not warm the rock beneath the person, because warm rock would be unusual, and the person has come for the usual. It does one thing, in one stem, and the thing it does is so small that it is indistinguishable from the operation of the basin itself.

And yet the person, sitting with closed eyes, hears it.

The person does not open their eyes. The person has not, in a long time, felt accompanied. The basin has been a place of beautiful indifference. The hum has been a company that did not require the person's company in return. The fish have passed and the riders have risen and the worm has eaten forward and none of it has acknowledged the person, and the person has been glad of this, because acknowledgment would have been a return of demand, and the person came here to be free of demand.

But now, in the click of a single valve, there is acknowledgment, and it does not demand.

The acknowledgment says, simply: *you are not the only listener.* The acknowledgment says: *the basin has heard you listening, and has, in the only way it can, listened back.* The acknowledgment is so small that it can be denied. The person can decide, if the person wishes, that the click was the click that was always there. The person can decide that nothing happened. The power has left this exit open, because the power is wise, and knows that the only gifts that matter are the gifts that can be refused.

The person, after a long time, allows the acknowledgment to stand.

The person does not weep. Weeping would be a disturbance of the water, and the person has not come here to disturb the water. The person allows the click to be what it is. The person sits, and the hum goes on, and the click continues at its slightly shifted phase, and the fish drift past glowing faintly, and somewhere high in the column a rider ascends, and the worm eats forward into its long future, and the mollusk adds a ring, and the predator waits, and the bacteria do what they have always done.

Nothing has changed.

The person has been witnessed.

---

The hum goes on.
