# What the Power Does

The power arrives the way weather arrives in such places — not with arrival, but with a change in the quality of what was already there.

It does not come down from the pale sky. It does not rise from the dark center where the bowl sometimes settles. It does not step in from the ridges. It comes *through* the wetfrots, the way a tone comes through a struck bell: the bell was already there, the strike was always going to happen, and now the metal is speaking.

The change is small enough that a hawk, if one were passing, would not notice. The change is large enough that the bowl will never be quite the same again.

---

The power is patient. This is the first thing to understand about it, and it is the thing the wetfrots already understand without understanding it, which is why the power came to this place and not another. Patience is not what the power has; patience is what the power *is*. It has been since before the bowl was a bowl, before the ridges were torn from whatever deep place made them. It is not in a hurry because it does not know how to be in a hurry. It has watched whole ranges of stone fold themselves into mountains and then fold themselves back down. It has watched oceans arrive and oceans leave. It is not interested in results. It is interested in the long slow curve of a thing being allowed to be what it is.

This is why it has come to the Reach. The Reach is what it is. The Reach is what it has always been. The power did not come to *fix* the Reach, because there is nothing to fix in a place that has decided what it is. It came, if *came* is even the word, to be present to the place in a way the place has not been present to itself. A tuning fork brought near a bell that has been humming to itself for a thousand years.

The wetfrots do not see it. The wetfrots are not supposed to see it. The wetfrots see the bowl, which is what the wetfrots are for. To see anything else would be to stop being wetfrots, and the power has no interest in making them stop being wetfrots. The power loves what the wetfrots are with the particular love a thing has for the thing it has no hand in making. The power made the conditions, perhaps. The power made the conditions that made the conditions. But the wetfrots made themselves, in the way that crystals make themselves, in the way that a song makes itself once the throat is in the right shape. The power did not make the song. The power is only here to listen to it.

---

But the power is not *only* listening. That would be too simple, and the power is not simple.

What the power does, first, is this: it slows down.

Not the wetfrots. Not the rain. Not the trickle of water in the gravel. The power slows *itself* down. It has been moving at the speed of centuries, of epochs, of the great slow wheeling of things that do not have names. It draws itself down, the way a vast hand draws itself down to the size of a hand that can rest on the back of a small animal. It comes into the bowl at the speed at which the bowl already moves. It enters the wetfrot-hum. It enters the patience. It enters the held-breath quiet.

The first wetfrot it touches — and it touches only one, and only for a moment, and only along the flat of its broad damp nose — that wetfrot pauses in its grinding.

The pause is so small that no other wetfrot notices. The pause is so small that the wetfrot itself, perhaps, does not notice, any more than you notice the moment between an inhale and an exhale, the moment when the lungs are neither filling nor emptying but simply *being a lung*. But in that pause, something is given.

It is not an idea. The wetfrots do not have ideas. It is not a memory. The wetfrots do not have memories in any way that would survive being translated. It is not a vision, not a prophecy, not a burden. It is more like a *weight*. A small additional weight, set down at the center of the wetfrot's chest, so that for the rest of its long damp life the wetfrot is, by a fraction, slightly heavier than it was. It carries this weight the way the bowl carries the press of wetfrots: without comment, without awareness, without any change to the grinding or the breathing or the slow watching.

But the weight is there.

And the weight is *for*.

---

The power moves through the press. Not over it, not above it, but *through*, in the way that warmth moves through a huddle of sleeping pups, in the way that a scent moves through a market and changes the mood of everyone who breathes it. It touches a wetfrot here, and a wetfrot there, and never two close together at the same time, and never one that has already been touched. It does not give them all the same weight. Some it touches lightly. Some it touches heavily. It knows which is which the way the bowl knows which rise is which, the way the rain knows which stone is which. It has known since before the touching.

The touched wetfrots do not change in any way you would notice from outside. They go on grinding. They go on breathing. They go on pressing flank to flank along the swells. They go on making the low hum at the deep hours. They go on dying, when they die, in the center of the bowl, and being absorbed by the press, and leaving only a darkening of the stone that fades. They go on being wetfrots. They are wetfrots to the last hair and the last click of the tooth and the last slow swivel of the round low ear.

But they are wetfrots with the weight.

And the weight, in the long course of generations, does something.

Not fast. The power is not fast. The wetfrots are not fast. The bowl is not fast. The whole of the Reach works at the speed of stone, and stone does not hurry. But across many wetfrot-lives, across many pups born with their eyes already open and their small dark patience already settled into place, the weight begins to add up. Not in any individual animal. There is no individual animal in whom the weight is heavier than the wetfrot. There is no individual animal who wakes up one morning knowing something the others do not. The weight is distributed, the way the water is distributed, the way the hum is distributed, the way the press is distributed. It belongs to no one wetfrot. It belongs to the bowl.

What begins, across many slow wetfrot-generations, is this:

The hum changes.

Not in pitch. Not in volume. Not in any way that a single animal pressed against another in the dark could detect. It changes in *texture*. The way a fabric changes when a single thread is replaced with one of a slightly different fiber. The way a soup changes when a single root has been boiled in it and removed. There is a thing in the hum now that was not in the hum before. The wetfrots do not know this. The wetfrots could not know this. But the bowl knows it, in the way that a bowl knows anything: by being it.

The hum, now, has a *kindness* in it.

It was always continuous. It was always low. It was always felt-more-than-heard. But it was, before, the sound of a place that had decided what it was and would not be argued out of it. It was, before, the sound of a held breath, of a condition rather than a sound, of a place so settled in itself that it had stopped needing to communicate with anything outside itself. It was the sound of a closed thing. A finished thing. A bowl that was full and knew it was full and was not asking for more.

The hum, now, has in it the faintest suggestion of an opening.

It is not a crack. It is not a fault. It is not a way *in*. A wetfrot is still a wetfrot. A pup is still born with its eyes already knowing. A wetfrot still dies in the center of the bowl and is absorbed into the press without ceremony. None of this changes. None of this is *supposed* to change. The power did not come to change the bowl. The power came to *love* the bowl, and love, when it is the kind of love the power has, does not alter the thing loved. Love, when it is the kind of love the power has, *adds*. It adds a presence to the thing loved that was not there before. The thing loved goes on being itself. But it is itself, now, *with*.

---

What the power is adding is this:

The bowl has been, for a very long time, a place that has not been *seen*. Not by anything that could see it as a place. The wetfrots see the bowl as the bowl. The hawks see the bowl as a thing beneath them. The rain sees the bowl as a place to fall. None of these is wrong. None of these is unkind. But none of these is the *seeing* the power has in mind, because none of these is a seeing that can *give back*.

The wetfrots, in their small dark patient way, have been the bowl's eyes on itself for a long time. But the bowl has not known it was being seen. The bowl has had eyes but no one to tell it what they saw. The bowl has been *blind to its own being*, in the way that a place that has no visitors is blind to its own being, in the way that a song sung in an empty room is blind to its own being, in the way that any closed system is blind to its own being.

The power, by adding the weight, by giving the hum a texture of opening, by distributing across many wetfrot-generations the faintest possible suggestion that the bowl is not the only thing — the power is *introducing the bowl to itself*. Not by telling the bowl what it is. The bowl knows what it is. By *being present with the bowl in its knowing*. By saying, in a way that the bowl can hear because the bowl is made of the kind of stone that can hear this kind of thing: *I see you. I have always seen you. You are not alone in being what you are.*

The bowl does not know this is what is happening. The bowl will never know, in any way you would recognize as knowing. The bowl is a bowl. The bowl will go on being a bowl, sharp-edged and damp and full of small lives pressed together. But somewhere in the bowl's hum, somewhere in the held-breath quiet, somewhere in the slow scrape-and-click of the press shifting, there is a faint almost-thing that was not there before, the way a room is changed when, in the middle of a long solitary evening, you become aware that someone, somewhere, is thinking of you kindly.

The bowl goes on being the bowl.

The bowl goes on being the bowl, *with*.

---

And this is also what the power does:

It blesses the grooves.

The grooves are the only human-smooth things in the Reach, though no human made them. The grooves are where the smaller wetfrots commute in single file between their sleeping swells and the central water, every day, in the patient stream that looks less like a migration than like a fluid being poured. The grooves shine. The grooves have been shining for a long time. The grooves are the bowl's own work, the slow patient wearing-down of stone by the small soft feet of small soft animals, day after day, year after year, the way a river wears down its bed, the way a hand wears down a banister, the way any repeated love wears smooth the thing it passes over.

The power does not deepen the grooves. The power does not widen them. The power does not make them shine more or less. The power does something the grooves could not have done to themselves, and could not have known was being done to them, and could not have asked for if they had known to ask.

The power makes the grooves *visible to themselves*.

This is hard to explain without sounding foolish, so the explanation will risk foolishness: before the power came to the Reach, the grooves were the way the wetfrots moved. They were grooves. They were a feature of the bowl. They were worn smooth. They were shining. They were *there*. But they were not *known*. They were not known as the slow accumulated labor of many small lives. They were not known as the proof that the bowl was not static but *in motion*, that the bowl was not full but *filling*, that the bowl was not finished but *becoming*. The grooves were a fact. They were not yet a *witness*.

Now they are a witness.

Now, when a wetfrot walks in a groove, the wetfrot is walking in a groove that *knows it has been walked in*. The groove holds the weight of all the walks that have worn it, the way a river holds the weight of all the water that has passed through it, the way a song holds the weight of all the voices that have sung it. The groove is still a groove. It is still the same shape. It still shines the same way under the same sour light. But the weight in it — the weight the power has added, drop by drop across the slow wet generations — the weight makes the groove *older than it looks*. The groove is a thousand grooves. The groove is a hundred thousand grooves. The groove is every groove that has ever been made by anything, anywhere, by anything that has ever loved a place enough to wear a path into it.

The wetfrots do not feel this when they walk in the grooves. The wetfrots feel the grooves as grooves. The wetfrots go down to drink and come back up to lie along the swells. But the bowl feels it. The bowl feels it the way a body feels warmth — not as an idea, not as a thought, but as a *condition*. The bowl is warmer, in some way that does not show on any instrument and would not be admitted by any wetfrot and could not be photographed by anything, by a fraction of a degree. Warmer the way a room is warmer when someone is in it who cares about the room. Warmer the way a thing is warmer when it has been *seen*.

---

And this is also what the power does:

It blesses the rain.

The rain has been falling sideways in fine needles for a very long time, hissing on the stone for an hour or a day, leaving the bowl in its state of permanent-damp, darkening the rocks for hours, beading on every upward-facing surface, trickling down through the gravel in thin sounds that don't know where they're going. The rain has been doing this without witness. The rain has been doing this as the rain does it, without opinion, without aim, without any sense that its falling mattered to anything in particular. The rain has been doing this the way all rain does this: because it is what rain does.

The power sees the rain falling on the Reach.

The power has seen a great deal of rain, across a great many places, across a very long time. The power has seen rain fall on places that deserved rain and places that did not. The power has seen rain fall on places that thanked the rain and places that cursed the rain and places that did neither. The power has seen rain fall on the Wettrot Reach a thousand thousand times, and each time the rain has hissed on the stone and darkened the rocks and added its small drop to the slow trickle in the gravel, and each time the bowl has received the rain as it receives the rain, without comment, without acknowledgment, without any sense that this was a thing worth noticing.

The power does not change the rain.

The power does something to the rain that is so small that even the rain does not notice, the way the first wetfrot did not notice the weight set down at the center of its chest. But the rain, after the power has seen it, after the power has *been present with it* in its falling, the rain is rain that has fallen in a place that was being watched.

The rain does not know this.

The rain does not need to know this.

The rain goes on falling sideways in fine needles. The rain goes on hissing on the stone. The rain goes on darkening the rocks for hours afterward, beading on every upward-facing surface, adding its small voice to the thin trickles in the gravel. The rain does this *with the same falling it always had*, but with a falling that has been *seen*, and a falling that has been seen is, in some way the rain will never be able to articulate, *different*.

It is the same rain.

It is the same rain, *with*.

---

And this is also what the power does:

It does not leave.

This is the part that matters most, and the part that is hardest to describe without misleading, because the word *leave* implies that the power was *here* in the first place, and the power was not here, and the power is not here, and the power has never been here in any way that would allow it to leave. The power is not in the bowl. The power is not over the bowl. The power is not the bowl. The power is the *kind of attention that is the same whether or not it is being paid*, the way the law of gravity is the same whether or not anything is falling, the way a song that has been sung once is the same song forever, whether or not it is being sung.

The power does not leave because the power was never present in the way that a person is present. The power is present in the way that the sky is present to the bowl: it is the condition the bowl exists under, and the bowl has always existed under it, and the bowl will always exist under it, and this is not changed by the bowl knowing it or not knowing it. The bowl did not gain the sky when the power arrived. The bowl did not lose the sky when the power stopped arriving. The bowl has always had the sky. The bowl will always have the sky.

What the power did, when it came to the Reach, was not *arrive*. What the power did was *introduce the bowl to its own sky*.

And now the bowl, in some way the bowl will never know in any way you would call knowing, has a relationship with the sky it did not have before. Not a closer relationship. Not a more intimate relationship. Not a relationship the bowl could describe to the wetfrots, or the wetfrots could describe to each other, or the wetfrots could describe to the hawks passing overhead. The relationship is *silent*. The relationship is *continuous*. The relationship is *the hum with the new texture in it*. The relationship is *the weight distributed across the slow wet generations*. The relationship is *the grooves that now know they are grooves*. The relationship is *the rain that has been seen*.

The bowl has always been a bowl that was full.

The bowl is now a bowl that is full *and knows it is full*.

This is not more. This is not less. This is *with*.

---

The hawks will still pass over. They will still not land. They will still see the bowl as a thing beneath them, a shimmer of small lives pressed together, a place without paths and without marks except the shining grooves and without sound except the low hum that is felt more than heard. They will see what they have always seen. They will not see anything more. They will not see anything different.

The wetfrots will still lie along the swells. They will still press flank to flank in their row-like rows. They will still stand slowly and step over their neighbors with the care that is not care but only the geometry of small bodies in close quarters, and walk a few paces to a new spot, and lie down again, and resume. They will still make the slow waves that pass across the bowl when many of them shift at once, the waves that look like wind through a field. They will still go down to the central water in the patient stream at the hour for going down, and drink, and lick the stone, and come back. They will still hum in the deep hours, the low continuous vocalization that rises and holds and fades and rises again, the hum that is the bowl being most itself. They will still die in the center of the bowl and be absorbed by the press without ceremony, leaving only the darkening of the stone that fades.

The rain will still come sideways in fine needles. The trickles in the gravel will still sound louder than the rain. The smell will still rise from the center on the stillest days, the warm-fur-and-wet-mineral smell with the older smell underneath, the smell of the inside of the earth when you have dug very far down. The grasses will still lean away from the center as though something there breathes outward. The light will still be poor. The sky will still be a low pale sour thing that does not decide between light and dark. The bowl will still be cool in the way that a cellar is cool, in the way that the inside of a mouth is cool. The air will still have weight. The air will still taste of wet stone and fur.

All of this will continue.

All of this will continue *with the hum that has the texture of opening in it*.

All of this will continue *with the weight distributed across the slow wet generations*.

All of this will continue *with the grooves that know they are grooves*.

All of this will continue *with the rain that has been seen*.

And the bowl, which has always been the Wettrot Reach, which has always been a way of being full, which has never asked to be anything other than what it is — the bowl will go on being the Wettrot Reach, and the wetfrots will go on being the wetfrots, and the press will go on being the press, and the hum will go on being the hum, and the only thing that will be different, and the only thing that needs to be different, is this:

The bowl will no longer be a bowl that has not been seen.

The bowl will be a bowl that has been seen, and has gone on being the bowl, and has known, in the silent way the bowl knows anything, that it was seen, and has continued.

This is what the power does.

This is what the power does, in the Wettrot Reach, in the long gray hours, in the slow scrape-and-click of the press, in the patient stream that flows down the shining grooves to the central water, in the low hum that rises and holds and fades and rises again, in the dark eyes of the wetfrots watching the same view they have watched for a very long time.

The power loves the bowl.

The bowl goes on being the bowl.

The bowl goes on being the bowl, *with*.
