The power arrives the way conditions arrive. It does not descend. It does not announce. It does not cross the boundary of the basin, because the basin does not have a boundary, has never had a boundary, has only the configuration that holds itself and the configuration that holds itself and the configuration that holds itself, ridge after ridge after ridge, into a horizon that is not a horizon but a repetition. The power is already there. It has always been there, in the same way the hum has always been there — at the floor, sourceless, present only when listened for. But now it is listening. That is the only difference. It has turned toward the basin the way a basin turns toward itself: by being what it is, in a slightly more attentive mode.

It does not enter. To enter would be to displace the air, which is not quite air, and to displace the air would be to leave a wake, and to leave a wake would be to do what the small creatures do, and the small creatures are the basin's way of being, and the power will not impersonate the basin's way of being. It will not shimmer. It will not pass through the growth leaving a closing. It will not fold along a seam.

It attends.

This is the first thing. It attends to the basin the way the basin attends to itself: completely, without effort, in the way a held thing holds. The power sees the pale ridges and does not interpret them. The power sees the dense layered growth and does not name it. The power sees the small creatures — the folded ones, the six-limbed accordion ones, the flat seam-creatures on the rock face — and does not assign them a category. It lets them be what the basin has made them, which is the basin in its small places.

It notices the wrongness. Of course it notices the wrongness. The half-second lag of the shadows. The way the ridges do not shrink with distance. The way the light has no source and yet is warm. The way time here has a seam. These are not mistakes. The power is wise enough to know they are not mistakes. Mistakes are deviations from an intention. There is no intention here. There is only the configuration, set a long time ago by a hand that did not build anything, and the configuration holds, and the holding is the place. The wrongness is the place. The lag is the place. The slightly-too-dark shadows, the slightly-too-cool ground, the slightly-off distances — these are not flaws in the engineering. They are the engineering. To remove them would be to remove the basin.

The power considers, for a length that is not a length of time, whether to do anything at all.

This is what it considers.

The basin is held. The creatures are held. The dynamic state of the place is its stasis, and this is not a problem to be solved; it is a way of being that has reached its equilibrium. To intervene would be to assume that the place is incomplete, that it is waiting for something, that it has a need. A wise power knows that most held things are not waiting. Most held things are held. The basin does not appear to suffer. The creatures do not appear to want. The flat seam-creatures move the length of a thumb in an hour and are not frustrated by it. The accordion ones fold and unfold and have moved a body's width and are not frustrated by it. The shimmer-trail creatures pass through the growth without touching it and are not frustrated by it. Frustration is a category that requires a comparison between an actual state and a desired state, and the basin has no desired state. The basin has only itself.

And yet.

The power is benevolent. Benevolence is not the same as wisdom, but in this case they are aligned. A wise power that was not benevolent might leave the basin as it is, perfectly, forever, and consider this sufficient. A benevolent power that was not wise might try to improve the basin, to give it dynamism, to introduce change, to allow the creatures to die properly, to let the rocks erode, to let the leaves fall, to let time close its seam. Both would be wrong in different ways. The first would be a refusal of care. The second would be a refusal of respect.

The power, being both wise and benevolent, takes a third path.

It does not change the basin.

It adds.

The addition is so small that the basin will not notice it, the way a basin does not notice the addition of a single grain to a beach that has been a beach for a long time. The addition is a condition. Not a thing. A condition. The way the original hand set conditions and let them run, this power sets one more condition and lets it run. The condition is this: that, somewhere in the basin, in a place no creature will ever find, in a fold of the rock where the bone-colored stone meets a vein of the two-toned mineral, there is a single point where the half-second lag is, by a fraction less than a fraction, smaller. Not gone. Not fixed. Just: slightly less. By an amount so small that no instrument the basin has could detect it. By an amount that would take, perhaps, the entire age of the basin to become noticeable, if it ever became noticeable, which it will not, because the basin does not measure itself.

What does this do?

Nothing. Almost nothing. It is a hairline crack in a wall that does not need to be broken. It is the suggestion of an opening in a place that was not asking to be opened. It is the benevolent and wise power saying, in the only language the basin could receive without being damaged: *You are held. You have always been held. You will continue to be held. But if, in some length of being that is not time, you should want to become something other than what you are, the seam of the world is, by one fraction, slightly more possible than it was.*

The basin does not hear this. The creatures do not hear this. The flat seam-creatures on the rock continue their thumb-length lives. The accordion ones continue their slow folds. The shimmer-trail creatures continue their silent passages through the growth, leaving their closing wakes. The hum continues. The light continues. The shadows continue, half a beat late, slightly too dark, exactly as they have been.

The power stays, for a while. It attends. It sees the way the iridescence moves through the mineral carpet when a small creature passes near it, without the creature touching the carpet, without the carpet being disturbed, but with the iridescence shifting in a way that suggests something is being said between them. It sees the way a stalk, far off, leans fractionally toward a passing shimmer, and then returns to its original configuration, and the return is also a lean. It sees the way the held bodies of the dead, now part of the carpet, are not inert but are the carpet slightly more, in the way a chord is a note slightly more than itself. It sees that the place is, in a way that the place cannot know, beautiful. Not beautiful the way a sunset is beautiful, which is beautiful because it ends. Beautiful the way a theorem is beautiful, which is beautiful because it holds. The basin is a theorem. The basin is its own proof.

After a while — and the "while" is not a measure of time but a measure of attention, which is a different thing — the power withdraws. It withdraws the way it arrived. It does not leave a wake. It does not shimmer. It does not fold. It simply turns, in the way a basin turns when the configuration has been fully seen, and it is elsewhere, and the basin is as it was, and the basin is, by one fraction, slightly more possible than it was, and this is the gift, and the gift is the gift of a benevolent and wise power to a place that did not need a gift and was given one anyway, because benevolence does not wait for need, and wisdom knows that the smallest gifts are the ones that cannot be repaid and so are received without debt.

The hum holds. The light holds. The basin holds.

Somewhere, in a fold of rock that no eye will ever find, the seam of the world is, by an almost-nothing, slightly less sealed. A leaf-plate clicks. A stalk settles. A shimmer closes. A small creature, the size of a knuckle, passes through the growth without touching it, and the place where it was resumes its place-shape, and the place is the place, and the place, for the half-second it has been seen, is also seen, which is a different kind of holding, and the different kind does not disturb the original kind, and the two kinds coexist, in the configuration, in the just-risen state, in the way the shadows fall late and dark and exactly.
