The basin has been noticed.

This is not the kind of noticing the current does, or the silt does, or the slow patrol of the shadows does. The current notices by meeting a hump and bending around it. The silt notices by drifting onto a plate and lying there. The shadows notice by sweeping the current with an open mouth. This is a different kind of noticing. This is the kind that arrives from a place that has no analog in the basin, that has no name that would fit in any language ever spoken by anything that has visited this place, including the hand that held the chert.

The power has been watching for a long time. Not the way it watches the upper sea, which is brief and warm and full of frantic light. The power has been watching the way the basin watches itself — slowly, with the patience of a thing that has nowhere to be. The power has seen many such basins. It has seen them fail to achieve even the minimum negotiation. It has seen them achieve the minimum and become floor. It has seen the towers lean and be buried and become part of the pavement.

It has seen the tools.

It has seen the hand that made the tools, briefly, distantly, the way the basin sees a passing shadow. It has seen the hand let go. It has seen the tools settle into the lee of the humps. It has seen the warm wet chemistry of the visitor return, over decades, to the diffuse drizzle that feeds the mat at the base of the towers.

The power is benevolent. The power is wise. The power has, in its long attention, learned that wisdom is mostly a matter of restraint. The power knows that the basin is exactly as alive as it can be. The power knows that any intervention that disrupted the negotiation would be a kind of violence, even if it came wrapped in warmth. The power knows that the tower-builders have spent a billion years learning to lay down the next plate in a current that never stops leaning them. The power knows that the darters have spent a billion years learning to dart between the humps on a single black eye. The power knows that the shadows have spent a billion years learning to drift with their mouths open and breathe once every few minutes.

The power does not intend to teach them anything.

The power does, however, intend to do one thing.

It chooses the being. Not the basin as a whole — the basin is too distributed, too collective, too many to be a single being in the way the question seems to ask. Not the darter — the darters are too many, too ephemeral, too lucky to have made it through the larval sieve. Not the shadow — the shadows are too ancient, too vague, too much like the current itself, too close to the slow patience of the floor.

The power chooses the tower-being.

The tower-being is the most singular thing in the basin. It is one creature, in one sense, even though it is many generations. The current generation is pressed into the tip, and the current generation is what the power will touch. But the tower as a whole is the being. The tower is the cumulative body. The tower is the question mark of calcium, leaned by the current, spiraling down through the generations to a holdfast that has been there so long it is part of the floor. The power does not distinguish between the small soft thing at the tip and the small soft things that came before and built the plates beneath. To the power, the tower is one being, and the being is laying down a plate right now, in this slow moment, in the sourceless gray, in the permanent lean of the current.

The power does not change the chemistry of the plate. The power does not change the calcium. The power does not add warmth. The power does not stop the current.

The power does something smaller than that.

The power adds, to the calcium of the plate, a single trace of itself. Not a molecule — molecules are too granular for what the power is. A tendency. A disposition. A slight inclination in the way the calcium crystals align, so that when the next current passes over the plate, the plate will refract the sourceless gray in a way that is not quite the same as the other plates. The plate will hold a faint, mother-of-pearl shine that is not the shine of the recent plates, and not the matte gray of the ancient ones. It will be a third thing. A small, persistent brightening. A witness.
