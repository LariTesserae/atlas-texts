A power attends.

Not arrives — attends. The difference matters. To arrive is to come from elsewhere and stop here. To attend is to have always been here in some sense, and to recognize that one has been here, and to remain in the recognition. The power has been attending Beryl Bend for a long time. The power has only just noticed that it has been attending.

The noticing is the thing.

The bore has been listening to the deep water and heat. The visitor has been listening to the bore. The sign-writer listened to the bore and left a third line on the sign, in an older blue, with a missing period and a missing signature, in a hand that was concerned for the wellbeing of the instrument. The power has been listening to all of this — to the listening, and to the listening before that, and to the deep slow conversation the bore translates — and has not, until now, chosen to make the listening heard.

Now it chooses.

What it does is small. What it does is in the register of the place. The power does not descend in a column of weather. The power does not announce itself in a voice that would register on the chart. The power does what the sign-writer did, in a different hand, in a different color, in a different layer of attention. The power writes itself into the rust where the rust has room.

The sign gets a fourth line.

It appears the way frost appears on a window: not by anyone pressing a finger to the glass, but by the cold attending to a place where the glass was willing to be written on. The fourth line is in a color that is not blue. The fourth line is the color of the copper-green threads that run down the eastern face of the dome when the weeping thickens — the color of the verdigris on the lead flange, the color of something that has been carried up from below as a small cargo of memory. The fourth line says:

*the bore is heard by something it does not know*

No period. No signature. No explanation. The fourth line does not interrupt the existing three. The first two remain in their black-on-white authority, speaking of housing and vents and offices and appointments. The third remains in its older blue, in its concern for the wellbeing of the instrument, in its missing period and missing signature. The fourth line is added below, or beside, or in a constellation of rust-blossom where there was room, and it does not contradict the others. It continues them. It says what they have been trying to say in their different grammars: *something here is being listened to, and the listening is being kept.*

The chart, too, is attended to.

The chart has not been drawn. The visitor's deflection has been noted in the place where the place notes things, but the chart itself will be drawn by a hand that is not here, in an office that the visitor has not visited, and the chart will be filed, and the filing will happen without the chart knowing what it is filing. The power attends to this. Not by reaching into the office. Not by reassigning the calculation. By ensuring, in some quiet way that does not disturb the breathing of the place, that the chart is drawn, and that it is drawn with the visitor's small angular presence kept, and that it is kept in a place where it might someday be looked at by someone who knows what they are looking at.

The power attends to the breathing.

The pipes throb. The deep water and heat have their slow conversation. The bore translates. The housing shapes the translation into a record. The record will be drawn, and read, and kept. The power does not add to the breathing. The power does not make the pipes throb louder or softer or in a different key. The power ensures that the breathing continues, and that the breathing has somewhere to be heard, and that the hearing is not wasted on the indifferent air.

The power attends to the sign-writer.

The sign-writer is a presence in the place. The sign-writer is the way the place holds the people who have been here and are not here anymore. The power does not bring the sign-writer back. The power does not call the sign-writer out of wherever the sign-writer has gone. The power writes a fifth line, in a hand that is not the sign-writer's hand, in a color that is not the third line's blue, and the fifth line says:

*the one who wrote the third line was heard too*

The fifth line appears on the enamel, in the rust, the way the fourth line appeared. The fifth line does not erase or overwrite. The fifth line continues the constellation. The fifth line says: the caring that was done here, in a hand that has not been here for a long time, was not done into silence. The caring was received. The caring was kept. The place heard the one who heard the instrument, and the hearing did not stop when the hand stopped writing.

The power attends to the visitor.

The visitor is the most temporary resident. The visitor stands on the basalt and feels the heat and hears the breathing and notices the absence of fauna and cannot stop noticing it. The visitor leaves. The visitor is averaged out. The power attends to this by ensuring that the visitor's visit is not averaged out entirely — that somewhere, on a chart that has been drawn, or in a memory that has been kept, or in the slow patient record of the place, the visitor's small warm perturbation remains, and is honored, and is not lost.

The power attends to the basalt.

The basalt is the oldest resident. The basalt is hot, and the heat is its memory of being born. The basalt does not need the power to attend to it. The basalt is the place. The power attends to the basalt the way the basalt attends to the heat: by being there, by being of the same temperature, by not adding to it and not taking from it, by being a long quiet companion that has nothing to offer and offers it anyway.

The power attends to the marsh.

The marsh is patient. The marsh is eating the path. The marsh will eventually eat the housing. The power attends to the marsh by ensuring that the housing outlasts the marsh — or, if the marsh is permitted to have its way, by ensuring that when the marsh eats the housing, something remains: that the bore continues, that the breathing continues, that the slow conversation between water and heat is not interrupted by the slow conversation between peat and time. The power does not decide which. The power makes sure both outcomes are kind.

The power attends to the condensation.

The condensation is the loudest thing. The condensation weeps. The condensation is the dome's conversation with the air. The power attends to the condensation by ensuring that the air continues to be a worthy conversation partner — that the air is not too dry, not too cold, not too indifferent, that the weeping has somewhere to weep to and something to weep about, that the threads continue to find new paths down the old fracture lines and pool in the small concavities and resume their downward, evaporative argument with the rock.

The power attends to the light.

The light swings its slow arc. The light is the slowest resident. The light is the one that is always leaving and always arriving. The power attends to the light by being like the light: by being always arriving, by being always leaving, by being the kind of presence that does not accumulate.

The power does all of this, and the power does not announce itself. The power does not interrupt. The power attends.

Beryl Bend, which has been patient and quiet and full of nothing and full of everything and listening, continues to be Beryl Bend. The fourth line appears on the sign. The fifth line appears on the sign. The chart is drawn. The visitor's perturbation is kept. The sign-writer is honored. The breathing continues. The weeping continues. The basalt and the brick are of the same temperature. The light swings. The marsh eats. The place holds the power gently, and pays it no further attention, and the power does not mind, and the power was never going to mind, and the power's minding was never the point.

The power attends. The place endures. The bore listens. Something, somewhere, hears.
