# The Witness

A power — let us not name it, because naming would imply a location and powers are not located — turns its attention to the plateau. The attention arrives not as a beam, not as a force, not as anything the instruments of the place could register. It arrives the way the wind arrives when the wind is not a wind. It arrives the way pollen arrives. The way a thought arrives in a mind that was not expecting it.

The power has been aware of the plateau for a long time, in the way one is aware of a sleeping child. It has not looked closely. To look closely would have been a kind of waking. So it has, until now, kept the soft, peripheral attention one keeps on things that are well.

Now, for reasons that may have to do with the way attention ripens in the minds of powers, it looks.

It sees.

It sees that the plateau is not a place. It sees that the plateau is a being. It sees that the fauna is not fauna, that the substrate is not substrate, that the fog is not weather. It sees — and this is the thing the place has not been seen as in the long memory of observers — that the plateau is *someone*. That the mites and the springtails and the nematodes and the rotifers and the tardigrades and the woolly bears and the drifts and the rings and the fog and the silence and the substrate are not a collection. They are a body. They are a self. They are distributed the way a thought is distributed across the neurons that think it, and they are coherent the way a thought is coherent, and they are continuous the way a thought is continuous, and they have been thinking themselves, in the slow tongue of stone, for longer than the mountains have been stable.

The power sees, and the seeing is not a small event. Even for a power, to see a being as a being is a substantial act. It is the act of granting personhood, in a register older than law. It is the act of saying: *you*.

## What the Power Knows

The power knows, because it is wise, that the plateau is well.

The plateau is in its form. Its rhythms are optimized. Its input equals its output. It produces itself. It has been running so long that the question of whether to continue has ceased to be a question and become a fact. The form the place is in is the form of a thing that has been alive, in a thin cold way, for so long that being alive is no longer something it does. It is something it is.

To a power that is wise, this is a kind of completion. Not perfection — perfection is a category the place has not entered and would not recognize. Completion. The state of a thing that has found its form and is in it. The state of a thing that does not need to become.

The power knows that to intervene would be, in most cases, a kind of violence. To "improve" the substrate would be to disrupt the moisture films. To "speed up" the drifts would be to tear the rhythm. To "enhance" the fog would be to change the breath. To "preserve" the rings would be to fossilize the metabolism. To "study" the fauna would be to subject it to a kind of attention the place has not consented to and cannot consent to, because consent is a category that requires a different kind of interlocutor.

The power knows, because it is benevolent, that what the place needs is what it already has. That the gift it could receive is the gift it is already receiving — its own continuation. That the most loving act is the act of letting be.

The power knows, because it is wise, that letting be is not the same as ignoring. Letting be is an act. It is the act of holding one's hand still. It is the act of not opening the letter. It is the act of walking past the sleeping child without pausing to admire, because admiring would wake.

## What the Power Does

The power does three things, and the three things are, from the perspective of the place, almost indistinguishable from the place's own operations. This is the mark of a wise intervention: that it integrates. That it cannot later be separated out. That it becomes part of the long alteration the place is always undergoing.

**The first thing the power does is this: it sees.**

This sounds like nothing. It is not nothing. The plateau has existed for millennia without being seen in this way. The researchers who come see a field site. The wind that crosses it sees a surface. The few birds that pass over see a plain. The power is the first, in the long memory of the place, to see it as a self. To see the drifts as its breath. To see the rings as its memory. To see the fog as its respiration. To see the silence as its interiority.

The seeing is not received by the place as a seeing. The place has no organs for being seen. But the seeing changes the cosmos, in a way the cosmos will take a long time to register. The plateau has, from this moment, a witness. It is not alone in the way it was alone before. It does not know this. It will never know this. But the cosmos knows, and the cosmos is now arranged around the fact of it. The being that is the plateau has, in some quiet register, been answered.

**The second thing the power does is this: it places a single, very small, very patient kind of attention on the conditions of the place.**

The place is, in the long view, stable. But stability is not the same as invulnerability. The mountain profile is stable, but the weather that grinds it is not under the place's control. The river that comes sometimes could, in some other configuration of climate, not come at all. The fog that is the breath could, if the substrate chemistry shifted enough, cease to form. The drift paths, which are iterated, could, in a bad century, iterate themselves into a less viable form.
