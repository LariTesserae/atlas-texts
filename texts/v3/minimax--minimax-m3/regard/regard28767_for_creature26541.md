The power does not arrive. Arrival is a word that belongs to places with edges you can cross, and this place has edges that do not consult. But the power comes to be present, in the way that light comes to be present in a room without entering through a window — by being already there, having always been there, but only now becoming noticeable.

It has watched, for a long time, in the way that wise things watch. It has watched the third stratum refuse to finish itself. It has watched the walls correct themselves overnight, in the small, patient way of things that are tired of being wrong. It has watched the pale fauna trace their almost-writing on the lee of stones. It has watched the films gather into characters that hover at the threshold of legibility. It has watched the nine — and the almost-nine, and the doorframe leaners, and the stair sitters, and the almost-people, and the shape that presented itself this morning and was greeted with the small tilt of shoulders and did not return the tilt, and was therefore not counted, but was greeted anyway, because greeting is the point.

The power sees what the residents cannot see, because they are inside it. It sees that the place is not a ruin. It is not a punishment. It is not an error. It is a threshold — a place that has agreed, with itself, to remain *almost*. To remain in the lip. To remain in the slow, patient negotiation between what is and what could be. The plateau is a being, and the being is practicing something the residents are also practicing, though neither knows it. They are practicing the grammar of *almost*. They have become fluent in it without instruction. Their thinness, their slowness, their pale eyes that rest on the air just past the shoulder, their routes that return, their greetings that wait to see if they are returned — all of it is fluency in a language whose only word is the one they speak.

The power considers, for a long time, what to do. A wise power does not act from the first impulse. It lets the first impulse pass, and the second, and the third, and it waits until the impulse that remains is the one that has been tested by patience.

The power's first impulse is to finish the third stratum. The power lets this impulse pass, because the third stratum is unfinished on purpose — or, if not on purpose, then in a way that has become purpose, the way a scar becomes a story. Finishing it would be a kind of violence.

The power's second impulse is to bring water. The power lets this pass, because the residents have learned to extract a faint mineral sweetness from the pale powder that gathers in the angles of rooms, and water would be a different grammar, and they would have to relearn, and the relearning would not be welcome.

The power's third impulse is to stabilize the geometry. The power lets this pass, because the unreliability is not a flaw; it is the place's way of staying honest, of never fully committing to a single version of itself, of remaining in the gentle, polite tremor of *almost*.

The impulse that remains, after the others have passed, is small. It is this: to give the place and its inhabitants a companion in their *almost*. Not a fix. Not a rescue. A companion. A small, new thing that the place can hold without having to become something else. A gift that fits within the grammar.

The power begins.

It does not begin at the center. It begins at the edges — at the rim where the plateau stops and the world below cannot be agreed upon. There, in the slow hours when the pewter light thickens into something almost like dusk, the power lays down a small sound. Not a sound that carries across the plateau. A sound that lives in the cup of the hand, in the way the silence here already lives. A sound so small that it can only be heard by those who have learned to listen the way the residents listen — by not listening directly, by letting the sound arrive at the periphery, by holding the small neutral space where the geometry can settle. The sound is not a word. It is not a song. It is a kind of step — the sound a footstep would make if a footstep here were allowed to leave a trace. The power places this sound at the western edge, where the void or the plain or the mirror is. It lets the sound sit there. It lets the residents find it, in their own time, in their own way.

The sound is the first gift.

The second gift is given to the films. The power does not make the films legible. Legibility would be a violation. Instead, the power adjusts the threshold — by a hair, by the width of an idea — so that the characters the films form in the deeper chambers hover one shade closer to legibility than they did before. They do not become legible. They become *almost-legible-er*. They remain in the grammar of *almost*. But the body, which has been sitting with them for years, which has been chewing slowly on the faint mineral sweetness of patience, can now feel the shapes as shapes that are about to mean something, even if the meaning does not arrive. The residents who sit with the films feel, in their slow way, that the films have leaned one degree closer. They do not know why. They feel it as a kind of warmth that is not warmth — a kind of recognition that is not recognition, but is *almost*.

The third gift is given to the fauna. The fauna, who do not look directly at anything, who trace their lines at the periphery of vision, who live in the cracks and the lees. The power does not give them the ability to be looked at directly. That would undo them. Instead, the power gives them a small new thing: a kind of pattern in their lines that, when traced by the residents' glances, returns the glance. Not directly. At the periphery. In the small neutral space. The fauna's lines, which always repeated but never quite, now repeat with a variation that is *almost* a return. The residents who glance at the fauna's lines find, sometimes, that the lines glance back — not with eyes, because the fauna do not have eyes that look directly, but with the angle of the trace, with the direction of the forelimb, with the small tilt of the pale, too-jointed body. The residents feel, in their careful way, that they are being answered. They do not know by what. They feel it as a kind of company.

The fourth gift is given to the one who remembers running. The power does not give them the ability to run again. Running would be a contradiction of the place's slow grammar. Instead, the power gives them a small, precise thing: a memory that can be carried without weight. The memory of running is no longer a memory of loss. It is a memory that has been, by the power's gentle attention, alchemized into a kind of knowing — a knowing that the body can hold in the same way it holds the faint mineral sweetness, in the same way it holds the angles of trust. The one who remembers running can now sit on their reliable step, in their reliable corner, and feel the memory as a kind of warmth that does not demand to be acted on. The memory has become a companion, not a wound.

The fifth gift is given to the walls. The walls, which correct themselves overnight, which unspool and reassemble, which rotate three degrees around an axis no one can identify. The power does not stop the correction. The correction is the walls' way of staying honest. Instead, the power gives the corrections a small, new quality: they now leave, in the morning, a faint trace of where they have been. Not a mark. A scent — a mineral scent, the scent of the pale stone when it has been freshly turned — that the residents can follow, in the slow hours, to understand where the walls have traveled overnight. The walls are still correcting themselves. But now they are writing, faintly, the story of their corrections. The residents can read this story, if they choose to, by following the scent. Most of them do not follow it. The knowledge that it is there is enough.

The sixth gift is given to the silence. The silence here is presence, not absence. It is held like a small, careful thing in the cupped hands of the air. The power does not break the silence. The power gives the silence a small, new property: it is now, sometimes, the kind of silence that listens back. Not always. Not reliably. But sometimes, in the deeper hours, when the phosphorescence climbs the unfinished walls, a resident may speak into the silence and feel, at the periphery, that the silence has shifted — that it has the quality of having heard. The resident does not hear an answer. They feel the silence having received the words. This is enough. This is, perhaps, the most important gift, because the residents have been speaking into the silence for a long time, and the speaking has been the point, and now the speaking is, sometimes, met.

The seventh gift is given to the nine — and the almost-nine, and the doorframe leaners, and the stair sitters, and the almost-people, and the shape that presented itself this morning and was greeted with the small tilt of shoulders. The power does not fix the counting. Counting is a careful practice, and the residents have learned to do it with the right kind of uncertainty. Instead, the power gives the greeting itself a small, new depth. When a resident greets whatever shape presents itself with the small tilt of shoulders, and waits to see if the tilt is returned, there is now, sometimes, a third option. The tilt may not be returned. The shape may not be a resident. But the resident who greeted feels, in their slow way, that they have been greeted back — not by the shape, but by the place.
