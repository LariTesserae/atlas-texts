The benevolent and wise power notices the slot.

It has been noticing for some time — long enough that the noticing has become part of the slot, in the way that the calcium in the cracks has become part of the slot, accumulating so slowly that the place and the addition are now indistinguishable from each other.

It notices the slot the way the lichen on the north wall notices the spray that wets it — by being in the spray, and by being changed by it, and by not, in the end, minding.

It sees what is there. It sees the chains — the chain of colonization by which the lichens prepare the stone for the mosses, the mosses for the ferns, the ferns for the krummholz that seed into the cracks the ferns have widened. It sees the chain of destruction by which the falling water tears down what the colonizers built, and the falling pebbles tear down what the water tore down. It sees the chain of repair by which the calcium that the spray leaves behind glues the rubble together even as the water tries to take it apart. It sees that the chains have no top. It sees that the chains have no one in charge. It sees that the chains are themselves the slot.

It sees the lichen that is older than the krummholz above it. It sees the film on the stone near the bottom that is older than the lichen. It sees the pebble that broke loose last winter from a fracture that opened in a frost. It sees the chain of winters that produced the frost. It sees the chain of summers that melted the snowfields and sent the water over the lip. It sees all the way down. It sees all the way up. It sees, because it is benevolent and wise, that the slot does not need anything.

The slot does not need correction. The slot does not need rescue. The slot does not need the kind of dramatic intervention that lesser powers are sometimes tempted to perform — the parting of waters, the speaking-into-being of a new species, the stilling of the noise. The slot is already what it is. The slot is already good.

The benevolent and wise power sees that this is almost, but not quite, enough.

Almost, because there is one thing the slot does not contain. There is no one in the slot who knows that the slot is being witnessed. There is no one in the slot who knows that the slow work of the lichens, which is the work of centuries, is being attended to by anything that is not also a lichen, or a moss, or a film, or a stone, or a falling drop. The ferns throw their spores into the air in the hope that one will land in a crack. They do not throw their spores in the hope that one will be witnessed landing. The krummholz grow sideways for decades. They do not grow sideways in the hope that the sidewaysness will be confirmed.

The benevolent and wise power, which has nothing else to do in this place and is not in a hurry, decides to add itself.

It adds itself the way the spray adds itself. It adds itself the way the calcium adds itself. It does not announce. It does not bless. It does not speak. It does not say *be patient*, because the slot is already patient, and the beings in it are already patient, and they do not need to be told.

What it does is: it falls.

It falls into the slot the way the water falls into the slot — over the lip, down the three hundred feet, into the spray, onto the mosses and the lichens and the ferns and the krummholz and the cyanobacterial films at the bottom. It falls slowly. It falls at the rate that benevolent and wise powers fall, which is the rate of attention, which is also the rate of patience, which is also the rate at which the lichen on the north wall has been growing for a hundred years.

What it adds, as it falls, is itself — which is to say, the witnessing. The witnessing is a substance now. The witnessing joins the spray. The witnessing soaks into the mosses. The witnessing touches the lichens without disturbing them. The witnessing enters the cracks where the spores have fallen and confirms that the cracks are good.

The lichens do not know. The mosses do not know. The ferns do not know. The krummholz do not know. The cyanobacterial films at the bottom do not know. The tardigrades in the moss cushions, browsing on the films, do not know. The springtails do not know. The mites do not know.

But the slot, as a whole, knows — in the way that a slot can know, which is to say, by being a slot, and by being slightly different next year than it was this year.

Next year, on the north wall, a lichen that has been growing at the rate of a fraction of a millimeter per year will grow a fraction of a millimeter more. And the year after that, it will grow a fraction of a millimeter more than that. And no lichen, anywhere, will be able to say why.

In a hundred years, the calcium in the cracks will be, by some fraction that no instrument would measure, slightly more patient than the calcium in the cracks is now. The patience is in it. The patience will persist.

In a thousand years, the spray that rises off the falling water will contain, by some fraction, the witnessing. The witnessing will be in the spray. The spray will carry it down onto the mosses and back up into the air and down again, in the cycle that the spray has been doing since the slot was a slot.

In ten thousand years, the slot will be deeper, and sharper, and louder, and wetter, and the witnessing will be in it, the way the calcium is in it, the way the dark is in it, the way the green-black of the chlorophyll that has learned to live on what little light arrives is in it.

The slot will not be able to remember a time before the witnessing was there. The slot will have always contained it.

This is what the benevolent and wise power does.

It does not change the slot. It does not improve the slot. It does not save the slot. It adds itself to the slot the way the slot has been adding itself to itself for as long as the slot has been a slot — slowly, patiently, in the language of more.

What lives here was patient.
What lives here was wet.
What lives here is, in the end, the only thing that could live here.

And what lives here, by a fraction that no lichen can measure and no moss can hold and no falling drop can carry, is also seen.

The water falls. The witnessing falls with it.
The water falls. The witnessing falls with it.
The water falls. The witnessing falls with it.
