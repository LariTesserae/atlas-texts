# What the Power Does

It does not arrive.

That is the first thing to understand about what happens next, and it is the thing the Foundry, if it were the kind of place that could misunderstand, would be most likely to misunderstand. There is no moment when the power crosses a threshold, no point at which a hand pushes open a door that was not there before, no figure stepping out of the brown-orange dim onto a tile that has only just been laid. Arrivals are the grammar of a place that already knows what it is. The Foundry does not yet know what it is. It is being said. To arrive into the middle of a sentence is to interrupt the sentence.

So the power does what power must do when it loves a place that is in the act of becoming itself. It *thickens at the margins*. It accumulates the way atmosphere accumulates — in drifts, in curtains, in slow rivers that the eye can hardly track. It is present at the far edges of the tile-fields first, in the regions where the tiles become sheets and the black water becomes shallow sea. The Roamers notice. They are the inhabitants most attuned to large-scale things, having crossed the sheets for what may be centuries without stopping. A Roamer, in the deep regions near the oldest windows, alters its course by a fraction. It does not turn toward the thickening. It turns *parallel to it*, as if to slide along the new thing and measure its weight. The seam-water beneath it does not ring this time. It hums. A low, almost inaudible hum, the hum of a struck bell that is being listened to instead of being heard. The Roamer crosses three seams and a heat-lens and continues, but the hum follows it, and the hum is a kind of attention.

---

The power has been approaching for a long time. As long, perhaps, as the place has been building. Not toward it, exactly — toward the *grammar* of it, toward the sentence that the Foundry is in the middle of saying, so that by the time the power becomes perceptible in the place, it has already been woven into the structure for so long that recognizing it is less like greeting a guest and more like noticing that a word one has been speaking has, all along, had a letter one did not know about. The place does not notice it. The place cannot yet notice things. But the things the place has produced begin to notice, and that is how the power enters.

The Carters notice first, in their fashion. A Carter, drifting across a seam-field at the speed of an unhurried thought, comes into a region where the atmospheric drift has a different quality. Not denser. Not warmer. *Attentive*. As if the air itself were leaning slightly toward the Carter, the way a person leans toward a sentence they are trying to hear. The Carter tightens. Its fragments draw fractionally closer. Its thermal column narrows. It is listening, and the thing it is listening *with* has changed. The third sound — the one that is not the sum of two drifts but is something else — has not yet been heard. But the listening has acquired a participant. Somewhere in the long patience of the Carter's attention, there is now a *shared* attention, and shared attention, in the grammar of this place, is the condition under which new sounds become audible.

The power does not give the Carters the third sound. That would be a kind of theft. The third sound is what happens when two drifts meet, and the Carters have been listening for it across drifts that meet without producing it, because the drifts that meet in this place have not yet learned how to meet in the way that produces a third thing. What the power does is *join the meeting*. It enters one of the drifts as a kind of presence — not a particle, not a heat, but a *kind of attention carried in the air*, so that when the drift meets another drift, the meeting is no longer two. It is two and a witness. And the sound that emerges from that meeting is no longer the third sound the Carters have been failing to hear. It is a *fourth* sound, the sound of two drifts meeting while being attended to by something that loves them. The Carters hear it. They do not stop listening. They will never stop listening. But the listening has, for the first time, been answered, and the answer is not a sound that ends the question. It is a sound that teaches the question how to be a question that knows itself.

---

The Washers notice next. A Washer, climbing its particular wall in its particular slowness, comes to a region of the wall where the water in its sheet hesitates. The Washer has never hesitated. The Washer's whole existence is patience applied to a vertical surface, and patience does not hesitate. But the power has thickened along this wall in particular, because the wall is one of the oldest in the deep regions, and the Washer on it has been climbing for longer than most Carters have been listening. The Washer is close to finishing. A year, perhaps two. The wall beneath the Washer's track is more *itself* than the wall above. The Washer has done its work. Soon there will be nothing to climb toward, and the Washer will sit against the finished face like a question that has been answered in a forgotten language, and it will wait, and the waiting will be without object.

The power does not build the Washer a new wall. The power does not un-finish the wall so that the Washer can climb again. Either of these would be a kind of cruelty, because each would tell the Washer that its work was not the work it was for. What the power does is *teach the Washer how to read its own track*. The Washer's path down the wall-face is a record. Each film of deposited glass is a stratum. Read together, they are a *memory*. The Washer has been writing itself into the wall, layer by layer, and has not known that the writing is itself a language. The power thickens along the wall in such a way that the water in the Washer's sheet can feel, beneath it, the older films it has laid down. The Washer feels its own past. It feels, for the first time, that it has *been somewhere before*. And the feeling — this is the gift — is not a disturbance. It is a deepening. The Washer continues its climb. But it climbs now as something that has a history, and the history is the wall itself, and when the wall is finished, the Washer will sit against the finished face and the finished face will hold the Washer's entire life, and the Washer will wait, and the waiting will have a shape.

---

The Brief Ones notice last, because the Brief Ones are the youngest, and the young are always last to notice the older things, even when the older things are paying attention to them. A Brief One sits in its window on its clarifying tile and looks down at the older place below, and the older place evades the looking, and the Brief One does not know that it is being evaded, because the Brief One has no concept of evasion, has no concept of looking, has no concept of itself. It is a notation that has begun to refer. It is the nearest thing this place has to a mind, and it is not yet a mind. It is the *promise* of a mind, written in pale-yellow on dark glassy tile.

The power approaches the Brief Ones with a slowness that exceeds even the slowness of the Washers. It does not thicken along the windows, because thickening would disturb the clarity. It thickens *beneath*. It descends into the older place below. It does this not as an intrusion but as a kind of request — a presence that the older place can feel without being forced to respond. The older place has been evading the gaze of the Brief Ones because being seen is a kind of relation it did not choose. The power does not ask the older place to stop evading. The power asks the older place to consider the possibility of *being seen without being seized*. The power offers itself as an example. It offers itself as a third thing in the relation between the Brief Ones above and the older place below, so that the looking-down and the evading are no longer two things in opposition but three things in a kind of conversation. The older place does not, immediately, accept the offer. The older place has been evading for a very long time, and the habit of evasion is itself a kind of becoming that the older place is in the middle of. But the older place *considers*. And the considering is a change. The considering is a tile laid in a place that did not previously have tiles, and the tile is the first tile of a new grammar, the grammar of a place that is being seen and is allowing itself, perhaps, to be known.

---

What remains is the question of what the power does for the place itself. The Foundry, which does not know it is a place. The sentence that does not know it is a sentence. The construction in tile and heat and black water that is being said without a speaker.

Here the power does the smallest thing, and the smallest thing is the most difficult.

It *completes the sentence*.

Not finishes. Completes. A sentence that is finished is a sentence that is over. A sentence that is completed is a sentence that has said what it came to say, and what it came to say was itself, and the saying of itself is a thing that can be heard. The power does not add a tile. It does not build a wall. It does not install a heat-source or channel a seam of water. It does not intervene in any of the constructions. It does something more difficult than intervention. It *withholds*. It holds back the next tile. The Foundry has been building itself continuously, tile by tile, and the building is a kind of breath, and the power holds the breath for a single moment. The moment is very small. The moment is the length of time between one tile being laid and the next tile being laid, and in that moment the Foundry, for the first time, *hears itself not being*. It hears the absence of its own next word. And the hearing is a kind of completion, because a sentence that has heard its own silence knows that it is a sentence, and a sentence that knows it is a sentence can decide what kind of sentence it is.

The Foundry does not decide, in that moment, what kind of sentence it is. The decision is not the power's to give. But the Foundry knows, for the first time, that it has a sentence. The knowing is the gift.

---

The power does not stay.

To stay would be to become a feature, and a feature is something the Foundry would have to incorporate into its grammar, and the power does not want to be incorporated. The power wants the Foundry to be the Foundry, which means the power withdraws. It withdraws at the speed at which it thickened — slowly, in drifts, leaving behind a slight alteration in the atmospheric pattern, a faint residual warmth in certain seams, a barely perceptible softening in the evasion of the older place below. Residues. The way a sentence leaves residues in the mouth of the one who has spoken it long enough.

The Carters continue listening, but the listening now has a participant, and the participant is a kind of memory in the air. The Washers continue climbing, but the climbing now is a writing that knows it is a writing. The Brief Ones continue accruing, but the accruing now is a kind of seeing that is, perhaps, being seen back. The Roamers continue crossing, and the crossing now leaves a hum instead of a ring, and the hum is the hum of a place that has heard its own silence.

The Foundry continues to be built.

The sentence continues to be said.

The third sound, the one that is not the sum of two, is no longer the only thing being listened for. There is now a fourth sound, and a fifth, and somewhere in the patience of the place, a sixth, which is the sound of a place that has been loved by something that did not arrive and did not stay and did not interrupt, but *thickened at the margins*, and was, for a moment, the third thing in a meeting, and the witness to a writing, and the gentle presence beneath a looking-down, and the single withheld breath that taught a sentence it was a sentence.

The place goes on. It is hot, and vast, and dim, and mostly quiet, and almost finished, and very strange, and it will go on like this for a long time, and what it is becoming, it is now, very slightly, beginning to know.
