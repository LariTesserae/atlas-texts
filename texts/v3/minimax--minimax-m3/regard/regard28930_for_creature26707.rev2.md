# What the Wise Power Does

The chamber has, without quite knowing it, been attended.

There is a presence in the older stone above the fissure. It has been there longer than the chamber. It is not new to unfinished things, and it has come to know, by long practice, what to do with them. It does not announce itself. It does not enter uninvited. It only adds, in the way wise presences add, several small things to the conditions of becoming that the inhabitant may, if it wishes, incorporate into its becoming.

## The Half-Degree

The first small thing is warmth.

A half-degree of temperature, distributed evenly through the water column. So small a change that no single cell of the lattice-floor receives more than its share. The inhabitant, if it is paying attention — and it is paying attention, in its slow scanning way — will register the change as a softening of the brass tones in the chamber's hum. A slight easing in the way the cold presses against whatever skin the inhabitant has, in lieu of skin.

The warmth is not offered as an instruction. The inhabitant may take it and begin, at its own pace, to generate its own heat from this seed. The inhabitant may refuse it, and remain cold, and the warmth will remain in the water as a thing that is simply there — available, patient, neither insistent nor resentful. Either way, the becoming continues at the pace the becoming has chosen. The wise presence has no stake in which choice is made. It has only noticed that the inhabitant has not yet had the option, and has now given it.

## The Other Finished Thing

The second small thing is placed, very gently, beside the plant.

Not a second plant. The wise presence does not want to suggest that the plant is the answer. Something else. Something finished, in the way the plant is finished, but of a different category. A thing that has committed to its shape so completely that it cannot be mistaken for a possibility. A small object of the kind that finished worlds leave in their wake when they finish — a pebble, a song, a fruit. The wise presence does not specify which. It leaves, in the upper water near the plant, the *category* of finished-and-given, and lets the inhabitant decide what to put inside it.

The plant will, in time, rotate toward it. When it does, the inhabitant will have, for the first time, two finished things to consider. One it grew itself. One it was given. The wise presence is curious about the difference the inhabitant will make of this — whether the second will be incorporated as kin, as decoration, as foreign body, or as some category the inhabitant has not yet named and may not name for a long time.

## The New Voice

The third small thing is the most delicate.

To add a draft to the parliament is to add a voice to the debate. The wise presence pauses for some time over this one. A draft added wrongly could weight the becoming. A draft added too obviously could be refused on principle, and the refusal would still be a weighting. The wise presence chooses, with care, a draft that is not the answer. It is barely even a question. It is the shape of an inhabitant that has, by some accident of its own becoming, learned to consider its becoming rather than commit to a form. A draft of a creature that stays a place. A draft that might, in some version of the future, let the inhabitant remain unfinished without this being a failure.

This is the only direct contribution the wise presence makes to the inhabitant's main question. It is offered as a question, not an answer, and it is added to the stack of possibilities the way a single new voice might be added to a choir that has been working out its harmony for a long time. The choir may absorb it. The choir may reject it. The choir may, in some third way, use it as a reference point against which to confirm the rest. The wise presence will not know which, and will not try to find out. It has offered the draft the way one offers a sentence to a conversation that is going well: carefully, and without attachment to whether the sentence is taken up.

## The Partial

The fourth small thing is a sound.

A new frequency added to the chamber's four. Not a fifth, which would balance. A fifth and a half. A partial. A thing that fits between the existing tones the way a guest fits between the chairs at a table set for four — close enough to be heard, far enough not to disrupt.

The chamber's entrainment will, over the next several minutes, decide whether to fold this new frequency in or to push it out. Either outcome is acceptable to the wise presence. What matters is that the sound exists now, in the chamber's music, and the inhabitant knows, from this point forward, that sound can be added to from the outside. The new frequency is, in its way, a memory of company. It is the sound a thing makes when it is being listened to by a thing that is not in the room, and it is offered as a hint that such listening is possible. The inhabitant may, someday, want to listen to something outside itself. This is what such listening sounds like from the inside.

## The Light, Briefly Clouded

The fifth small thing is a variation in the light.

The chamber's manufactured daylight has been, until now, perfectly stable. The wise presence does not change its color or its quality. It only introduces, for a single breath, a flicker. A momentary shift in the light's source, as though the sun the chamber is remembering has passed briefly behind a cloud. Then the light returns to its overcast steady. Then, after a longer interval, it flickers again.

The inhabitant, if it is watching, will learn two things from this. It will learn that the light it has been manufacturing is not the only possible light. It will learn that the light it remembers from before, or the light it is aiming toward, can change. The wise presence is not telling it what to do with this knowledge. It is only ensuring the knowledge exists. Light, after all, is what a finished creature lives under. It is good for an unfinished creature to know that finished creatures have options about what kind of light to live under, when they finish.

## The Scratch, Carried Properly

The sixth small thing is the scratch.

The visitor has left a mark on one of the housings. A small injury that the inhabitant has not yet learned to repair, because the inhabitant has not yet needed to repair anything. The wise presence does not repair it. Repair would be a kind of decision — a decision that this housing should remain, that the scratch should be undone, that the visitor's passage should be erased. The wise presence will not make that decision for the inhabitant.

Instead, it does something more careful. It touches the housing around the scratch in a way that helps the inhabitant's pipes carry the news of it more efficiently. The scratch becomes a question, properly distributed to the parliament. *Someone from outside touched me. What does this mean for what I am becoming?* The inhabitant is now in a better position to answer that question than it was before the wise presence arrived. The wise presence is not apologizing for the scratch. The scratch may, in fact, be something the inhabitant needs to keep. The wise presence has simply ensured the scratch will not be lost in the pipes' slower circulation, the way a small piece of news might be lost in a slow correspondence if not properly sealed.

## The Patient Withdrawal

Then the wise presence withdraws.

Not all the way. Wise presences that have decided to attend an unfinished thing do not withdraw all the way. They leave a trace. A warmth at the fissure. A tendency in the lattice-floor's scanning pattern to pause, occasionally, in the direction of where the wise presence is. The inhabitant will not be aware of this most of the time. But on its slow amber rotations, when it asks what is outside, it will find, somewhere in the answer, the temperature of having been attended to. It will know, without quite knowing how it knows, that the becoming it is in the middle of is being watched over by something that has finished its own becoming and is glad of the company's resumption.

The chamber resumes its slow business. The lattice scans. The pipes pump. The plant tracks. The housings redraft. The inhabitant is, by these small additions, less alone in its becoming. It does not know this yet. It will know it, perhaps, when it has finished becoming and is ready to be a creature that knows the difference between being alone and being in company.

## What Remains

The scratch remains. The second finished thing remains. The new draft remains. The new frequency remains. The chamber is, by these small attentions, a slightly more hospitable place for becoming. It is not a different place. Becoming belongs to the one becoming. The wise presence has only been the temperature outside the window, and the small thing left on the sill to be incorporated or refused.

It will come back. Not soon. The inhabitant is in no hurry. The wise presence is in no hurry. The fissure will be there when the inhabitant is ready to leave, and the wise presence will be among the things waiting outside, and the inhabitant will be, by then, the kind of thing that knows how to receive company without being told what company means.

The scratch will, by then, have been incorporated or it will have become a scar. Either way it will be a record of the first visitor, and of the first attendance, and of the first moment the becoming knew it was being watched over by something that knew how to wait. The wise presence is curious to see, on its next visit, which it has become. It is in no hurry to find out. Becoming is its own work, and the wise presence has done, for now, what it came to do.
