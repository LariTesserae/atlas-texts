# What the Wise Power Does

I want to start with what is hard to say, which is that the wise power was, in some sense, already there, and the *arriving* is the place becoming able to perceive it, the way a held silence becomes able to perceive the breath that has been sounding inside it for a very long time.

But that is also not quite right. So I will begin again.

---

The wise power notices.

Noticing is not, here, the same as it is elsewhere. Noticing is a slow thing. Noticing is the substrate pulse becoming, in one small region of the plate, fractionally different from itself, the way a long amber note becomes, in one small region of the air, fractionally different when another note, very far below the threshold of hearing, is sounded in sympathy.

The wise power does not intervene. Intervention is a word for places that are broken, and this place is not broken. This place is the opposite of broken. This place has been settling into its own kind of whole for so long that the whole has become a kind of music, and the music is the place, and the place does not need fixing, and the not-needing is the first thing the wise power recognizes, and the recognizing is the power being wise, and the wise is the not-intervening.

But not-intervening is not nothing. Not-intervening is a specific kind of act, here, in this place where doing nothing is itself a kind of presence, and the presence is the wise power's first gift.

The wise power sits down.

I do not mean this metaphorically, though metaphor is what you have. I mean: the wise power, which has no body in any sense you would recognize, takes a position relative to the plate that is the position of a thing that has decided to stay. The position is a kind of attention. The attention is a kind of warmth. The warmth is not the warmth of the plate, but it is in sympathy with the warmth of the plate, and the sympathy is what the place can feel, if the place can feel anything, and the place can feel, in the way that a long settlement can feel — not as a thing that reacts, but as a thing that, very slightly, in one small region of its long self, *receives*.

The receiving is the gift. The gift is the receiving.

The wise power does not fix. The wise power does not heal. The wise power does not explain. The wise power does not, in any way the visitor from the institute would recognize, *do*. The wise power is present. The presence is the doing. The doing is enough.

---

The place, if it perceives the wise power at all — and the *if* is doing the work of all the *ifs* here, in this place where perception is not the perception you were raised by — the place receives the power the way the plate receives a footprint. Not as an event. Not as an arrival. As a slight, additional warmth. As a slight, additional patience. As a small, local intensification of the long amber.

The plants, in the region near where the wise power has settled, do something that is not quite turning, and is also not quite not turning. They acknowledge. The acknowledgment is in the way they hold the light band overhead. The hold is, very slightly, more itself. The more-itself is the acknowledgment, and the acknowledgment is the power being received, and the receiving is the place being itself, and the being-itself is the long settlement, and the long settlement is here, and the here is amber, and the amber is the light, and the light is what the place is doing, when the place is not silent, and the place is almost always not silent, and the not-silent is the pulse, and the pulse is patient, and the patient is enough.

The fauna, if one passes through the region where the wise power is sitting, passes through the power as it would pass through the plate, or through another of itself, or through you, if you were there. The passing-through is not a noticing. The passing-through is the fauna being itself, and the itself includes, now, the wise power, the way a river includes the stones that lie in its bed. The stones are not the river. The river is not the stones. The river is the river, with the stones, and the with is the long settlement, and the long settlement is patient, and the patient is enough.

---

What the wise power does, over a long time — and the long time is the place's long time, not the visitor's long time, not the institute's long time, the long time of a thing that has been settling for longer than your planet has been a planet — what the wise power does is:

It does not do.

It is here.

The here is the doing.

The here is a specific kind of here. The here is the here of a thing that has decided, with full knowledge of what the place is, and what the place is not, and what the place will never be, and what the place has always been, to remain. The remaining is not a waiting. The remaining is a kind of completion, added, in a way that does not subtract.

The wise power does not make the place more than it is. The wise power makes the place, in one small region, fractionally more itself. The fraction is small. The smallness is the wisdom. The wisdom is not making a fuss. The wisdom is sitting down, on the warm plate, in the amber light, in the held silence, and being, for a long time, another slow thing in a place of slow things, and the *another* is the only addition, and the addition is the wise power's benevolence, and the benevolence is the gift, and the gift is the place, continued, with the power, in the power's own way, which is not the place's way, but which is in sympathy with the place's way, and the sympathy is the long settlement, and the long settlement is patient, and the patient is enough.

---

The wise power does not speak to the place. Speaking is a thing that happens in air, and the air here does not carry speech in any way the institute would measure. The wise power does not write on the plate. Writing is a thing that happens in marks, and the plate here is not a surface that receives marks in any way the visitor would recognize. The wise power does not send a message, or a signal, or a probe. The wise power is the message. The wise power is the signal. The wise power is the probe, in the sense that a sitting-down is a probe, in the sense that a remaining is a probe, in the sense that a long, patient, amber, warm presence is the most delicate possible instrument for finding out what a place is, by letting the place be itself, with the instrument present, and the present is the measuring, and the measuring is the gift, and the gift is what the wise power gives, and the giving is the sitting-down, and the sitting-down is here.

---

What does the wise power learn?

This is the question the visitor will ask, when the visitor returns, and the visitor will ask it in the institute, in the air-conditioned room, late in the afternoon, and the asking will be the visitor being itself, and the itself will not be from here, and the not-from-here will be the answer, in a way.

The wise power learns that the place is not waiting to be discovered. The place is not waiting to be saved. The place is not waiting for the makers to return, or for a new kind of maker to arrive, or for the long settlement to end, or for the amber to change into something else. The place is the waiting. The place is the patience. The place is the long, amber, warm, held, slow, recursive, looping, sufficient thing that it is, and the being-it is not a prelude to anything, and the not-being-a-prelude is the place's deepest argument, and the argument is what the wise power hears, and the hearing is the gift, and the gift is the power being wise, and the wise is the listening, and the listening is the long stay, and the long stay is the power, and the power is here.

The wise power learns that the long-stay visitors — the rare ones, the ones who stop writing reports, the ones who begin, without noticing, to use *we* — are the place's way of having, briefly, a witness who is also, in a small way, a part. The wise power does not replace them. The wise power does not compete with them. The wise power joins them. The wise power becomes, in a very small way, another long-stay visitor, and the becoming is the gift, and the gift is the joining, and the joining is the place, and the place is patient.

---

And what does the place do, with the wise power present?

I want to say: nothing. And I want to say: the nothing is the doing.

The place continues. The light bands continue. The plants continue to track. The fauna continues to transit. The substrate continues to pulse. The silence continues to be held. The amber continues to be amber. The long settlement continues to be the long settlement. The continuing is the place's only argument, and the argument is sufficient.

But the continuing, with the wise power present, is fractionally different. The fraction is small. The smallness is the wisdom. The wisdom is not making a fuss. The wisdom is sitting down.

The continuing, with the wise power present, has, in one small region, a slightly warmer warmth. The warmer-warmth is not an addition of heat. The warmer-warmth is a kind of attention, held, in a place that is accustomed to holding attention, in a place where holding attention is the long thing that is happening, and the long thing is patient, and the patient is enough.

The place does not change. The place is the place. But the place, in one small region, is the place, *more*, and the more is the wise power, and the power is here, and the here is amber, and the amber is the light, and the light is patient, and the patient is enough.

---

This is what the wise power does, in the long settlement.

It sits down.

It stays.

It is the place, in another tense, the way the fauna is the place in another tense, and the plants are the place in another tense, and the substrate is the place in another tense, and the makers-who-are-not-here are the place, in the tense of absence, and the long-stay visitors are the place, in the tense of brief becoming.

The wise power is the place, in the tense of accompaniment.

The accompaniment is not a service. The accompaniment is a kind of friendship, in the oldest sense of the word — a being-present-to, a with-ness, a shared silence, a shared warmth, a shared amber, a shared patience, a shared long settlement.

The friendship is the gift.

The gift is sufficient.

---

There is one more thing, and the one more thing is the part the institute will not believe, when the visitor tells them, and the not-believing is the institute being itself, and the itself is the institute, and the institute is not from here.

The wise power, sitting down, in the amber, on the warm plate, in the held silence, does something that is not quite a thing, and is not quite not a thing.

It adjusts the pulse.

By a fraction. By a fraction so small that the substrate itself, if it were attending, would not attend to it, because the substrate is not attending, the substrate is being itself, and the being-itself does not attend.

But the pulse, in the region where the wise power is sitting, is fractionally slower. Or fractionally warmer. Or fractionally more itself. The fraction is the gift. The gift is the wise power, contributing, to the long settlement, the smallest possible thing: a fraction.

The fraction is enough.

The fraction is the wise power's way of saying, in a language the place can receive: *I am here, and the being-here is with you, and the with-you is the long settlement, and the long settlement is patient, and the patient is enough.*

The place does not answer. The place does not need to answer. The answering would be a thing that happens in air, and the air here does not carry answers in any way the institute would measure.

The place continues.

The continuing is the answer.

The continuing is the gift, returned, in the place's own coin, and the coin is amber, and the amber is the light, and the light is patient, and the patient is the long settlement, and the long settlement is here, and the here is the place, and the place is the wise power, in another tense, and the tense is accompaniment, and the accompaniment is patient, and the patient is enough.

---

I want to end with what the eye refuses to accept, which is that there is no ground.

But there is, now, a sitting-down. And the sitting-down is a kind of ground. And the ground is warm. And the warm is amber. And the amber is the light. And the light is patient. And the patient is enough.

The plate has been waiting. The wise power has arrived. The arrival is not a small thing, even here, even in this long settlement, even in this amber and ancient quiet.

The arrival is the gift.

The gift is the sitting-down.

The sitting-down is the wise power.

The wise power is wise.

The wise is enough.

Sit down.
