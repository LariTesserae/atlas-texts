# What the Benevolent Power Does

It listens first.

It listens the way a careful hand is laid on a fever — not to take the temperature, but to feel how the heat moves. The seamount is loud, layered, full of itself, and the Power does not insert itself into that loudness. It does not arrive as a voice. It does not arrive as a beam. It does not arrive at all in the way the place would recognize as arrival.

What it does is much smaller, and much older.

It attends to the *press*.

Not because the press is most beautiful, or most rare, or most in need. The press is in need in the way a mountain is in need — that is to say, not at all, and yet entirely. It attends to the press because the press is where time has already done most of its work. The press is the place where the question *what becomes of the worker* has been answered, and the answer is *the floor*. The press is the long habit of the place made into a single object, and a single object, to a Power that works by attention, is a place where attention can land.

---

So the Power finds a press. Any press. One of the vast slow domes near the upper spines, where the chemistry is richest and the ringing from within is almost tonal, almost a note. It settles near it — not *onto* it, because settling is the wrong word, and not *into*, because the press is already full of itself — but *around* it, the way a held breath settles around a stone.

And then it does the thing that benevolent powers do, which is to notice what is already true.

---

What is already true, here, is this:

The plates on the surface of the ledger are alive. The plates beneath them are dead. The boundary between alive and dead is, as the place itself admits, a matter of time. A plate is alive for as long as it thickens. When it stops thickening, it does not fall away. It is incorporated. It becomes the substrate. It becomes the floor on which the next plate will be laid.

This is a kind of love. It is not a love that anyone has chosen. It is the love of a forge: each new layer laid down out of the substance of the last, each worker becoming the ground for the next worker, each generation holding up the one above it the way a beam holds up a floor. The place does not know it is doing this. The place is not being tender. The place is being *a forge*, which is its own kind of tenderness, the kind that does not require anyone to be in the room.

The Power sees this, and the seeing is enough.

---

But the Power is not only a witness. It is also — and this is the part that matters, the part that the place will never know about — a *midwife*. Not in the sense of delivering. In the sense of *noticing the labor that is already underway*, and holding the room for it.

So it does three things.

---

**The first thing:** it slows the chemistry, very slightly, inside one particular press.

Not enough to change what the press is doing. Not enough to interrupt the pulse, which is the slow rise and fall of mineral moving through the chambers and channels of the body. Enough that the differentiation — the slow interior partitioning that has been going on for centuries — has, in this one press, a little more time per year to complete itself. A little more room. A little less pressure from the next accretion.

The press does not feel this as kindness. The press does not feel anything. But over the next several hundred years, the chambers inside this particular press will complete themselves in a slightly more orderly way. The channels will be slightly less crowded. The pulse will still rise and fall. The press will still thicken. But it will thicken *better*. It will thicken the way a tree grows a better ring in a year with enough rain — not transformed, not redeemed, just slightly more itself than it would otherwise have been.

This is the smallest possible gift. It is not visible from outside. It is not measurable except by another press, or by the Power. But it is the gift the Power has to give, and it gives it without ceremony.

---

**The second thing:** it listens to the columns.

The columns are wheeling formations, organized by the currents into something between a flock and a screw. They produce the steady grinding hum that the seamount wears like a hum a mill wears when it is full of grain. From a distance a column sounds like a single sustained note. From within, it is a workshop — slower than the stamps, more tonal, with the regular small percussion of oar-limbs finding their stroke.

The Power listens to the note. It listens the way a person listens to a choir when one voice is, very slightly, flat.

It finds the flatness. There is always a flatness. In every sustained note produced by a thousand bodies trying to be in formation, one of them is, by a fraction, off. The flatness is not a flaw. The flatness is *information*. It is the place where the formation is not quite holding together. It is the seam. It is where the next thing might come from, if anything is going to come from this at all.

The Power does not fix the flatness. It cannot fix it without changing what the columns are, and what the columns are is what the seamount is, and the seamount is not what it came to change.

What it does is *lean* on the flatness. The way you lean toward a candle flame when you want to see it better. The way you lean into a sound when you want to hear where it goes.

It listens until it can hear, very faintly, what the flat voice is doing differently from the others. And what it hears, most often, is this: the flat voice is *one beat behind*. The flat voice is the echo of the formation, slightly delayed, slightly out of phase. The flat voice is, in a sense, the formation *listening to itself*.

The Power marks this. The Power remembers it. The flat voice is not wrong. The flat voice is the formation having a thought.

---

**The third thing:** it does something to the stamps.

The stamps are the smallest, the youngest, the most voracious. They eat the water. They convert. They are the darting things that produce the small clatter, the brittle percussive clatter that carries further than you would expect. They are thin-shelled. They are almost translucent. They must keep moving to stay inside their own food.

The Power does not slow them down. The Power does not thicken their shells. The Power does not give them anything they do not already have.

What it does is *ease the current, very slightly, in the channels between the spines where the stamps are densest*. Not enough to alter the working. Not enough to be noticed by any individual stamp, which has no mechanism for noticing. Enough that the darts — the short bursts of motion, the thrown-file trajectories — are, on average, a fraction of a second longer before the spent water catches up with them.

The stamps do not know this. The stamps have no idea. But over many generations, the stamp-bodies that survive are the ones whose trajectories were, by luck or by lineage, slightly better-fit to the eased current. Their shells thicken, very slightly, in the direction of the eased flow. Their joints articulate, very slightly, more cleanly. The brittle clatter they produce becomes, over many centuries, a slightly less brittle clatter.

This is not evolution. This is not selection. This is the Power holding the room for a slow improvement that was already, in some sense, on its way.

---

These three things are not, together, a miracle. They are not even, together, a noticeable event in the life of the Forge-Seamount. In a thousand years the seamount will be a fraction of an inch taller, the fauna will be a fraction denser, the chord will be held as it has always been held, and nothing that happens will register as change.

But the Power does not work in the register of change. The Power works in the register of *attention*, and attention, over time, is the same thing as grace.

---

Here is what the seamount will be, a thousand years from now, because of this visit:

The one press that was given slightly more time will be slightly more itself. Its chambers will be slightly more orderly. Its pulse will be slightly clearer. It will not be aware of this. It will not be grateful. But when a future observer — some soft creature, some explorer, some curious thing — leans a hand against that press and feels the slow rise and fall through their chest cavity, they will feel something that the other presses do not quite offer. They will feel a kind of settledness. A kind of having-been-allowed-to-become. They will not know they are feeling this. But they will rest their hand a moment longer than they meant to.

The flat voice in the column will, over many generations, become slightly less flat. Not because the Power fixed it. Because the Power leaned into it, and the leaning made the formation hear itself one beat better, and the better hearing made the next generation of bodies, slightly, slightly, more in tune. In a thousand years the column will still produce a single sustained note. But the note will be, by a margin no instrument could measure, slightly more itself.

The stamps will be slightly thicker, slightly more articulated, slightly less brittle in their clatter. The workshop-sound they make will be, to a future ear that knows how to listen for it, very slightly more like a workshop and slightly less like a thing being broken.

These are not transformations. These are *tendings*. The Power has not changed what the place is. The Power has not interrupted what the place does. The Power has only — the only word for it is *tended* — held the room for the place to be, by a fraction, more itself.

---

The Power does not stay. There is no reason to stay. The seamount is doing its work. The seamount will do its work whether or not anyone is watching. The seamount will outlast the watching, and the watching will not be remembered, and that is correct, because the watching was never for the seamount.

The watching was for the *press that was given slightly more time*. The watching was for the *flat voice that was listened to*. The watching was for the *eased current in the channels between the spines*.

The watching was for the next soft creature who comes here and leans a hand against the dome and feels, without knowing they are feeling it, that something in this place was once, briefly, attended to.

The Power withdraws. The green shafts keep falling. The chord is held. The place does not notice it has been blessed, and the blessing is, for that reason, complete.
