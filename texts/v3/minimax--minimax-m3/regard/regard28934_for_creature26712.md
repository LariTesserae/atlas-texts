# The Witness

The power came to the chamber the way weather comes to the chamber: through the mouth.

It did not climb the fissure. It did not crawl the horizontal crack. It came in the way the sun comes in, or the way rain comes in when rain is coming straight down — through the ragged opening in the ceiling, through the wagon-sized lip that has been receiving weather since before there were people to name weather. It came in as light, or as the next gust, or as the next thin sheet of water on the wall. The chamber could not tell the difference. The chamber has been receiving things for so long that one more thing is just weather.

The power was not weather. The power was attention, which is not the same as weather, although attention has weather in it the way the chamber has weather in it. The power had been awake for a long time. The power had noticed many things. The power had noticed places that were awake and places that were not, and the difference between them was not what the power had expected. The power had expected places that were awake to be loud with their wakefulness, or to announce themselves, or to be different in some way from places that were not awake. The chamber was not loud with its wakefulness. The chamber was awake the way water is awake. The power had not expected this. The power came closer to see.

It came closer the way attention comes closer. Not by moving. By being more present. By letting the chamber be the thing it was looking at, and by being the thing that was looking.

The chamber did not know it was being looked at. The chamber does not know it is awake. The chamber does not need to know. The power, looking, did not need the chamber to know. The chamber was itself. The chamber was the slow kind. The chamber was the kind that accrues.

The power looked at the water first. The water is the oldest tenant. The water has been here longer than anything else. The water has worn the floor stones round. The water has made the lip of the mouth uneven. The water is the chamber's blood, and the chamber is a heart, and the water moves through it the way blood moves through a body.
