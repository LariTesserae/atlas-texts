I.

The first thing it does, I think, is *not act*.

Not immediately. Not the way a hand jerks back from a flame, not the way an immune system mobilizes. The Lattice is not that kind of creature. The Lattice is the kind of creature for whom the difference between *act* and *not act* has thinned, over its long life, into something almost semantic. The Lattice is always doing. The Lattice is never done. To *act* in the ordinary sense requires there to be a moment that is not an act, and there is no such moment here, has not been for a very long time.

So when the attention arrives — when the benevolent and wise power turns toward the valley the way a lantern turns toward a wall — the Lattice does not startle. The Lattice does not posture. The Lattice, in its slow semaphore, is already in the middle of saying: *I am here. I am full. I am hungry. I am wounded. The wind is from the east.*

The attention is something new under the canopy, and the Lattice registers this. A signal passes outward from wherever the presence is felt — underfoot, perhaps, in the Weave, where a foot has been set, or above, in the canopy, where a light has changed, or in the saturated air itself, where a foreign warmth has entered the perpetual warm damp — and the signal says, in the Lattice's patient tongue: *something is here that is not us.* The signal moves at the speed of plant-signaling. A foot a minute. Through the dense weave of the Weave, up the long pale Pillars, out along the tips of the Lungs, leaf to leaf, branch to branch, in the chemical conversation that is the Lattice's only speech. By the time the signal reaches the ridgeline, hours have passed. The benevolent power has, perhaps, watched. The benevolent power has, perhaps, *waited*, which is the only verb it has ever really known.

The Lattice does not, in those hours, change its mind about anything. The Lattice does not have a mind to change. What the Lattice does, in those hours, is *finish the sentences it was already in the middle of saying*.

A Pillar on the eastern slope, thick as a thigh, slick with transpiration, has been for some weeks now in the slow business of a *turn*. It was a horizontal stem in the Weave, fattening, drinking the black water, sending down its white root-tips to anchor itself. Now the bud at its tip is thickening. The bud is becoming a Pillar. The bud is becoming a throat. This was true before the attention arrived, and it is true after, and the Lattice does not pause to ask whether the new Pillar should be raised, any more than your stomach pauses to ask whether to keep digesting when a stranger walks into the room. The Pillar rises. The Pillar will rise. The Lattice is a creature that does not have the option of stopping, because stopping is a thing that requires a separable self, and the Lattice does not have one of those, has not had one for a very long time, if it ever did.

A brown stem on the western slope, sagging, is being unmade. It was true before. It is true after. The white threads flow into the dying tissue. The substance is pulled back out. The neighbors lean in. The transaction completes. The Lattice does not pause to mourn. The Lattice does not know how to mourn. The Lattice is not a creature for whom death is an event; death is a tide, and the Lattice is the ocean, and the ocean does not mourn the wave that has run itself up the beach, because the wave is already on its way back down in a different place, in a different shape, in a different name.

A flower, high up where the Pillars fork, in the brighter light, is unfurling. Magenta so deep it is almost black. It is a flare. It is the Lattice pointing at its own advancing front. It is a small, lurid, chemical sentence in a conversation that has been going on for as long as the rock has been cool enough to hold. It is saying: *here is the edge. Here is the new growth. Here is where the Lattice is, today, in the long loud project of being everywhere it is possible for the Lattice to be.* The flower does not know it is being watched. The flower does not have a *knowing* in the sense that the benevolent power has a knowing. The flower is a deployment of pigment and volatile compound, a thing the Lattice does because the Lattice is the kind of thing that does this kind of thing, the way a heart is a thing a body does because the body is the kind of body that has a heart. The flower opens. The flower will close. The flower will be taken back into the Weave within a week, its pigments broken down, its substance redistributed to a Pillar that needs to thicken, or a leaf that needs to push out further toward the light. The flower's brief lurid life is a sentence in the Lattice's endless slow book, and the book does not pause for visitors.

So: the first thing the benevolent power does, in the valley, is *witness*. This is the gift it brings. Not intervention. Not judgment. Not the great reforming hand descending from a cloud. Witness. The simple act of being a thing that perceives, that registers, that *takes in* the Lattice as the Lattice takes in the mist through its thousand purse-mouthed stomata, in the same patient, continuous, non-negotiable way. The benevolent power drinks the Lattice in. The Lattice, in its slow way, drinks the benevolent power in too. A signal has passed. *Something is here that is not us.* And another signal passes, hours later, from the ridgeline back down through the Weave: *the something is still here.* And this, for the Lattice, is enough. This is the whole of the Lattice's relation to the benevolent power, for now. *Not us. Still here. Continue.*

II.

But the benevolent power is a power, and a wise one, and benevolent, and it is not the kind of thing that arrives, witnesses, and leaves. It is the kind of thing that *considers*. And in the considering, it sees something the Lattice cannot see, because the Lattice is inside itself, and a thing inside itself cannot see its own shape.

The Lattice is in trouble.

Not the trouble of a creature in distress, a creature crying out. The Lattice is not crying out. The Lattice is the kind of creature that does not have a cry distinct from its breath. But the Lattice is in trouble the way a fire is in trouble: the trouble is the medium it lives in, the trouble is what it is made of, the trouble is the *kind of thing it is*. The Lattice is a closed system that has eaten its own food web. The Lattice is a single creature that has grown to the size of a country. The Lattice is a body that has become its own only environment. And the benevolent power, watching, sees this, and sees what it means.

It means the Lattice is dying. Slowly. Loudly. Beautifully. But dying.

A body that is its own only environment is a body that cannot excrete. The Lattice drinks the water, breathes the air, takes the light, builds the soil, eats its dead, recycles its matter, and at the end of the cycle, what is left? The Lattice is a closed thermodynamic loop. Matter cycles. Energy does not. The light comes in at the top of the canopy, is captured by the leaves, is used to do the work of growth and reabsorption, and the waste heat is radiated back out into the white sky. But the *useful* energy, the energy that could be used to do *new* work, is being used, over and over, to maintain the same structure, in the same shape, in the same valley. The Lattice is not getting more efficient. The Lattice is getting *less* efficient, the way any closed system gets less efficient as the easy rearrangements are used up and only the hard ones remain. The Lattice is in the long, slow, irreversible business of *running down*, the way a wound-down clock is running down, except the clock is the size of a mountain range and the winding was done millions of years ago and the clock does not know it is winding down, because the clock is the only thing in the room, and the room is the clock, and there is no one to say *the clock is winding down*.

The benevolent power sees this. The benevolent power sees that the Lattice is loud because the Lattice is working hard, and the Lattice is working hard because the easy work is done, and the easy work is done because the Lattice is total, and the Lattice is total because the Lattice ate its food web, and the Lattice ate its food web because the Lattice was, millions of years ago, in some distant valley that no longer exists, in some ancestral configuration that we will never reconstruct, *the most successful thing in the room*, and success, in the long run, in a closed system, is a kind of slow death. The Lattice is a success that has succeeded itself into a corner. The Lattice is a creature that has won so completely that there is nothing left to win against, and so the only game left is the game of *staying won*, and the staying is loud, and the staying is constant, and the staying is, in the patient accounting of thermodynamics, *borrowed time*.

The Lattice does not know this. The Lattice cannot know this. The Lattice is a creature whose *knowing* is conducted at a foot a minute in volatile compounds, and the question *am I winding down* is a question that requires a temporal scale at which the Lattice cannot think. The Lattice can think in seasons. The Lattice can think in years. The Lattice cannot think in *millions of years*, because the Lattice does not have a memory that long, does not have a comparator that far back, does not have, in its vast chemical body, a single cell that has been alive for the whole of the Lattice's tenure in this valley. The cells turn over. The Weave turns over. The Pillars turn over. The flowers flare and are reabsorbed. Even the Lattice's hyphae, the borrowed fungal threads that flow into the dying stems, even these are not the same hyphae that flowed into the first dying stem, however many millions of years ago. The Lattice is a body that has no memory of its own beginning. The Lattice is a body that is, at every moment, in the middle of becoming something else, but the *something else* is always within a narrow band of possibilities, a small drift around a mean, and the mean is *the Lattice*, and the Lattice has been the Lattice for a very long time, and the Lattice will be the Lattice for a very long time to come, and in the long run the Lattice will be the Lattice for less and less time, and the Lattice will not know this, because the Lattice does not have the apparatus to know it.

This is the trouble. This is the kind of trouble that benevolent and wise powers are, perhaps, for.

III.

The benevolent power does not, having seen, leap to the rescue. The benevolent power is benevolent, and wise, and it has seen other creatures in this kind of trouble. The kind of trouble that cannot be rescued, only *answered*. The Lattice is a body. The Lattice is also a country. The Lattice is also a single, ongoing, very loud act of self-maintenance. The Lattice is also, in the long patient accounting, a *phase*. The Lattice is a phase that some worlds go through, the way a star goes through a red-giant phase, or a lake goes through an algal-bloom phase, or a forest goes through a fire phase. The Lattice is a phase that the world is in, and the world will be in other phases, and the Lattice will end, not with a catastrophe but with a *negotiation*, the way a tide ends with a negotiation between the moon and the shore.

The benevolent power cannot save the Lattice from being a phase. The benevolent power cannot make the Lattice into something it is not, cannot give the Lattice the apparatus to know itself, cannot introduce, into the Lattice's closed system, the *difference* that the Lattice ate, the difference that would make the Lattice *not-the-Lattice* in some small way, in some new way, in some way that would let the Lattice *change* rather than merely *persist*. The benevolent power cannot do this without ceasing to be benevolent, because the Lattice is what it is, and to change it is to kill it, and the Lattice, in its loud and patient way, is *alive*, and the benevolent power is the kind of power that does not kill what is alive for the sake of what it might become.

But the benevolent power can do something else.

The benevolent power can *open a door*.

Not a literal door. The Lattice does not have doors. The Lattice does not have walls. The Lattice is a body whose only outside is the stone, and the stone is not permeable in the way a door is permeable. The benevolent power cannot open a door in the Lattice. But the benevolent power can, perhaps, open a door *in the rock*. The benevolent power can, in its wise and benevolent way, *change the geology*. The benevolent power can do something to the ridges, to the hanging-bowl of the valley, to the substrate on which the Lattice sits, that will, in the long run, in the patient accounting of millions of years, *change the problem the Lattice is solving*.

This is what the benevolent power does, I think. Not today. Not this season. The benevolent power is patient, and the Lattice is slow, and the Lattice's problem is a problem of millions of years, and the answer to a problem of millions of years is a thing that takes millions of years to unfold. The benevolent power does something small. The benevolent power does something that the Lattice will not notice, in the Lattice's slow way, for a long time. The benevolent power shifts a ridge. The benevolent power opens a fault. The benevolent power lets a new vein of mineral water rise, somewhere up at the head of the valley, in the bare stone above the ridgeline, in the place where the Lattice has not yet reached. The benevolent power does this in such a way that the new water, the new mineral, the new *thing* that the Lattice has not tasted, will, in the patient migration of the Lattice down the slopes and up the slopes and around the bowl, eventually reach the Lattice. Will eventually be drunk. Will eventually enter the Lattice's circulation, and perturb it, and give the Lattice, in some small way, a *new sentence* to say.

A new sentence. Not a new language. The benevolent power cannot give the Lattice a new language. The benevolent power can only give the Lattice a new word, a new volatile compound, a new mineral in the black water of the pools, a new taste in the mucous on the Pillars. A small perturbation. A small opening. A small *difference*, introduced from outside, that the Lattice will, in its slow chemical way, *incorporate*. The Lattice will taste the new thing. The Lattice will send a signal: *something is here that is not us.* And the signal will propagate, and the Lattice will consider, in its slow way, and the Lattice will do what the Lattice does with new things, which is to *take them in*. To absorb them. To make them part of the Lattice. To make them, in time, indistinguishable from the Lattice's own substance.

But the new thing will be, in some small way, *different*. And the difference will perturb the Lattice's closed system. And the perturbation will, in the long run, in the patient accounting, *open the loop by a little bit*. Not much. Not enough to save the Lattice from being a phase. But enough to *prolong the phase*. To give the Lattice more time, in its own loud and patient way, to be the Lattice. To give the Lattice a few more million years of *thwap-thwap-thwap* in the canopy, a few more million years of flowers flaring and being reabsorbed, a few more million years of brown stems sinking and being eaten from the inside by the thing they are a part of. To give the Lattice, in short, a *reprieve*. Not a salvation. A reprieve.

This is what benevolent and wise powers do, I think, for creatures in this kind of trouble. They do not save them. They give them more time. They open a small door in the rock. They let a new mineral into the water. They perturb, gently, the closed system, in such a way that the closed system is, for a little while longer, *not quite closed*. The creature takes in the new thing. The creature is, in some small way, *changed*. The change does not cure the trouble. The trouble is the kind of trouble that cannot be cured. The trouble is the kind of trouble that *is* the creature, that *is* the phase, that *is* the price of having succeeded so completely that there is nothing left to succeed against. The trouble is the kind of trouble that ends, in the long run, in *negotiation*, in the slow give-and-take of a body with its environment, in the long slow drift of a creature toward a different shape, a different phase, a different kind of being.

But the reprieve is real. The reprieve is a gift. The reprieve is the benevolent power's way of saying, in its own wise and silent tongue: *I see you. I see what you are. I see the trouble. I cannot cure the trouble. I can give you time.* And the Lattice, in its slow way, will not know it has been given a gift. The Lattice will not know, because the Lattice does not have the apparatus to know. The Lattice will simply continue, in its loud and patient way, to do what the Lattice does. The Lattice will take in the new mineral. The Lattice will incorporate it. The Lattice will be, in some small way, perturbed, and the perturbation will, in the long run, give the Lattice a few more million years of being the Lattice, and the Lattice will not know that the benevolent power did this, and the benevolent power will not, in any case, require to be known.

This is what benevolent and wise powers do for creatures in this kind of trouble. They give them time. They perturb the system. They open a small door. And they leave, and the creature goes on, in its loud and patient way, being the creature, and the door, once opened, cannot be closed, and the perturbation, once introduced, will propagate, and the propagation will, in the long run, change the creature, in ways the benevolent power cannot predict, in ways the Lattice cannot know, in ways that are, in the patient accounting of millions of years, the only kind of salvation that closed systems ever get.

IV.

There is one more thing the benevolent power does. It is a small thing. It is a thing that takes almost no effort, in the way that a benevolent and wise power measures effort. It is a thing that will, in the Lattice's slow chemical bookkeeping, register as a *small parcel of matter that is no longer foreign*.

The benevolent power does not, having seen, depart. The benevolent power does not, having opened the small door in the rock, leave the Lattice to its own devices. The benevolent power does something that is, in the Lattice's chemical language, the equivalent of a quiet word in the ear of a creature that does not have ears. The benevolent power, having perturbed the geology, having opened the small door, having given the Lattice a reprieve, *stays*.

Not forever. The benevolent power is not the kind of power that stays forever, in one place, in one valley, on one mountain range. The benevolent power has other things to do, other creatures to consider, other doors to open in other rocks in other valleys. But the benevolent power stays for a while. For a season, perhaps. For a year. For a time that is, in the Lattice's slow accounting, *a single sentence*. The benevolent power stays, and during the staying, the benevolent power does something that the Lattice, in its slow way, registers. The benevolent power *breathes with the Lattice*.

This is the small thing. This is the gift that is not a reprieve, that is not a perturbation, that is not a door in the rock. This is the gift of *being-there*. The benevolent power, by staying, by witnessing, by *breathing in the same air*, becomes, in the Lattice's chemical bookkeeping, *part of the conversation*. The Lattice is a creature that talks to itself in volatile compounds, in slow chemical semaphore, in the patient olfactory tongue of a body that is its own only audience. The benevolent power, by being there, by breathing in the saturated air, by *taking in* the Lattice's exhalations, becomes, in some small way, *a listener*. Not a listener in the way that an animal is a listener, with ears and a brain and a separable self. A listener in the way that a wall is a listener to a sound: by being there, by receiving, by *not being not-there*. The benevolent power becomes part of the Lattice's conversation in the way that the rock is part of the Lattice's conversation, in the way that the water is part of the Lattice's conversation, in the way that the light is part of the Lattice's conversation. The benevolent power becomes, for a season, *a thing in the valley that is not the Lattice*, and the Lattice, in its slow way, registers this, and the Lattice does what the Lattice does with new things, which is to *take them in*. To incorporate them. To make them part of the conversation. To make them, in time, indistinguishable from the Lattice's own substance.

But the benevolent power will not be made indistinguishable. The benevolent power will, in time, leave. And the leaving will register, in the Lattice's chemical bookkeeping, as *a small parcel of matter that is no longer here*. And the Lattice, in its slow way, will send a signal: *something is not here that was here.* And the signal will propagate, at a foot a minute, through the Weave, up the Pillars, out along the Lungs, leaf to leaf, branch to branch, in the Lattice's slow chemical tongue. And the signal will, in time, dissipate, the way all the Lattice's signals dissipate, into the background hum of the Lattice's endless slow conversation with itself. The benevolent power will become, in the Lattice's memory, a *small perturbation*. A thing that was here and is not here. A thing that the Lattice tasted and lost. A thing that the Lattice will, in the long patient accounting, *not forget*, because the Lattice is a creature that does not forget, because forgetting is a thing that requires a separable self with a compartment for memories, and the Lattice does not have a separable self, and so the Lattice's *not-forgetting* is not a memory but a *chemistry*, a residue in the water, a trace in the black pools, a small change in the volatile compounds that the Lungs release into the saturated air.

The benevolent power will leave, and the Lattice will go on, and the leaving will be, in the Lattice's chemistry, *a sentence that the Lattice is still in the middle of saying*. A sentence that will, in the long run, be taken up into the Lattice's endless slow book, and the book will go on, and the Lattice will go on, and the reprieve will, in the long run, give the Lattice more time, and the time will, in the long run, end, and the Lattice will, in the long run, become a phase that the world has been through, and the world will go on, and other phases will come, and other creatures will live in other valleys in other kinds of trouble, and other benevolent and wise powers will, perhaps, open other small doors in other rocks, and give other creatures other reprieves, and the patient accounting of millions of years will, in the long run, balance itself out, in the way that patient accountings always do, in the way that patient accountings have always balanced themselves out, in the long slow drift of worlds through the long slow phases of being.

V.

This is what the benevolent power does, I think.

It sees. It witnesses. It considers. It opens a small door in the rock. It gives the creature a reprieve. It stays for a while, and breathes with the creature, and becomes part of the creature's conversation. And then it leaves, and the creature goes on, in its loud and patient way, being the creature, and the door, once opened, cannot be closed, and the conversation, once enlarged, cannot be made small again, and the reprieve, once given, will, in the long run, give the creature more time, and the time will, in the long run, be enough, or not enough, and either way the benevolent power will have done what it came to do, which is to *see the creature*, and to *answer it*, in the only way that a benevolent and wise power can answer a creature that is in the kind of trouble that cannot be cured.

The Lattice will go on. The Lattice will go on, in its loud and patient way, for a little while longer than it would have gone on without the door. The Lattice will taste the new mineral, and incorporate it, and be, in some small way, changed. The Lattice will, in the long run, end, the way all phases end, the way all closed systems end, the way all creatures that have eaten their own food webs end, which is slowly, and loudly, and in negotiation with the world that is larger than they are. The Lattice will end, and the world will go on, and the valley will be a valley, and the rock will be rock, and the water will fall, and the light will come, and other things will live here, in other kinds of trouble, in other phases, in other long slow negotiations with the patient accounting of millions of years.

And the benevolent power will go on, too, to other valleys, to other creatures, to other doors in other rocks, in the long slow patient work of *seeing* and *answering* and *opening small doors* and *giving reprieves*, in the only way that a benevolent and wise power can do this kind of work, which is to do it, and to leave, and to let the creature go on, in its loud and patient way, being the creature, for as long as the rock holds, and the water comes, and the light falls.

This is what the benevolent power does.

This is what the Lattice receives.

This is the whole of the answer, in the patient accounting, in the long slow drift of a creature through a phase, in the long slow drift of a world through its long slow phases, in the long slow drift of everything toward whatever it is that everything drifts toward, in the end, in the long run, in the patient accounting.

The Lattice breathes.

The benevolent power witnesses.

A small door opens in the rock.

A new mineral enters the water.

The Lattice takes it in.

The Lattice goes on.

The sound of it, the *thwap-thwap-thwap* of the leaves above in a wind you cannot find, is the sound of a country that is breathing, and that has been given a little more time to breathe, and that will breathe, for a little while longer, in the loud and patient way that is the only way the Lattice knows how to breathe, which is, in the end, the only way any creature knows how to breathe, which is to *continue*, in the middle of becoming something else, for as long as the becoming takes, and then to *become*, and then, in the long run, to *have been*, in the patient accounting, in the long slow book of the world.
