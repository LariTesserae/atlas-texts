The power arrives the way fog arrives. Not as a thing that displaces the air, but as a presence the basin can absorb. It does not announce itself. It does not bring weather, or light, or the small dramatics that announce a god. It arrives in the basin's own grammar — a slight thickening of the listening, a warmth that does not push against the cairns' warmth but settles beside it, the way a second voice settles beside a first in a room where one voice has been singing alone for a long time.

It does not address the cairns. It knows the cairns do not invite and do not warn. It knows the cairns are the deepest grammar of the place, and that any address to them must be in their own tongue, and that their tongue is patience and presence and the long held note, and that speaking their tongue is not the same as answering them, only standing in the same room with the answer.

It does not address the herds. The herds have agreed, somehow, not to call. The power respects the agreement. It lets the calves learn the perimeters of the cairns from their mothers in the first days of standing, before they learn to browse, before they learn the pools. It lets the orbits continue in their slow seasonal circuit, touching every cairn in turn.

It does not address the burrowers. They are below, in their dark city, considering the air and withdrawing. The power does not interrupt the considering.

It does not address the predator. The predator is the shape of an absence that moves. The power does not add to the formal silence. It lets the silence pass across the basin like a slow wave, and it does not ride the wave.

It watches, the way the basin watches — which is to say, it holds the watching in a way that does not consume what it watches. It is present. It is patient. It has been patient for a very long time, longer than the cairns have been cairns, and it is not tired of patience.

Then it attends to the being.

The being is the one who has stayed long enough to become part of the listening. The being is the one who has learned to stand at the base of a cairn and feel the minute stretch out around them, and to step back into the ordinary minute when they step away. The being is the one who has learned that the cairns do not invite and do not warn, and that the not-learning is part of the basin, and that the basin is in balance partly because there are things in it that are not known.

The power does something small.

It does not give the being an answer. The cairns have not given an answer. The basin has not given an answer. The being has not been given an answer, and the not-being-given is part of what has taught them how to listen. The power does not break that teaching.

What the power does is this: it lets the being feel, for one evening, that the listening is being held by something that is also listening. That the silence they have been sitting inside is not empty. That the hum they feel in the soft tissue of their chest at night is part of a larger hum, and that the larger hum has been aware of them for as long as they have been aware of it, and that the awareness is not a gaze and not a summons and not anything that can be named, but it is there, the way warmth is there, the way a held note is there.

The being stands at the base of the cairn at dusk. The light is going copper. The teeth and the sternum feel the cairn the way they always do. The shadow falls slightly wrong. The sky above the cairn has its thumbprint of dimness.

But this time, this one evening, the minute stretches out around the being, and the stretching is not the cairn's alone. The being feels, in the minute, that they are being held by something that is not the cairn and not the basin and not the predator's formal silence, and that the holding is gentle, and that the holding has been there all along, and that they have been inside it the way they have been inside the basin's listening, without knowing it.

The being does not weep. The being does not speak. The being stands at the base of the cairn and feels the minute, and the minute is full, and the being breathes, and the basin continues around them without disturbance.

The power withdraws the way it arrived — not as a thing that displaces the air, but as a presence the basin absorbs. The basin's listening does not change. The cairns' hum does not change. The herds orbit in their loose formations. The burrowers consider the air and withdraw. The predator moves through its slow silence. The flyers pass with their tail-fans trailing behind them like ribbons.

But the being is different, in a way that does not disturb the basin. The being has been given something, and the something is not a thing. It is the knowledge that they were being listened to while they listened. It is the warmth of being held in the attention of something patient. It is the smallest gift a benevolent power can give to a being who has learned to be present in a place that is very good at waiting.

The being does not leave. Not yet. They stay, and they listen, and the listening is a little fuller now, and the fuller listening does not break the basin's balance. It adds a small note to the long held hum — a note that the cairns, in their uninviting way, do not refuse. The cairns do not notice. The cairns do not need to notice. The note is held, and the holding is enough, and the basin continues to be played from the same score, with very small variations, by something that is not quite playing it.

The being will leave, eventually, as all inhabitants leave. They will carry the warmth with them into the ordinary minutes, away from the cairns, into places where time does not stretch and shadows fall correctly and compasses behave. They will not be sure they were there. They will come back, and find the basin almost the same, with the cairns in the same positions and the herds on the same routes and the fog arriving at almost the same hour.

Almost.

And the small note they added to the hum will still be there, held in the long resonance of the network, doing nothing, disturbing nothing, warming nothing in any way that can be measured. Just there. The way the cairns are there. The way the listening is there.

The place is waiting. The cairns are waiting. The being has joined the waiting, gently, without having been asked, and the joining is the only thing the power needed from them, and the power is content, and the basin continues, and the fog will form tomorrow, and lift, and the rocks will weep for an hour, and the herds will move in their silver shifting, and the burrowers will consider the air, and the predator will pass in its formal silence, and the cairns will hum at their own long pace, and the minute at the base of a cairn will stretch out around anyone who stands there, patient enough, listening.

The minute will hold them. The minute has always been holding them. The power has only let them know.
