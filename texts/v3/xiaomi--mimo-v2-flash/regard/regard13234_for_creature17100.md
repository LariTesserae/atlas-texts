The benevolent and wise power does not descend in a blaze of glory or a chorus of celestial song. Such displays would be a violence to this place, a shouting in a library of geology. Instead, the power simply *observes*, and in observing, it understands.

It sees not a wasteland, but a masterpiece of closure. It sees a world that has achieved a state of profound, terrifying grace. The sound is not a torment, but a mantra. The erosion is not destruction, but refinement. The Mat is not a desperate survivor, but a conqueror of complexity.

The power recognizes the Great Mat not as a trapped entity, but as a philosopher king who has solved the equation of existence and found the answer to be *stillness*.

What would a wise and benevolent power do?

It would do nothing.

To intervene would be an act of supreme arrogance. To offer relief would be to offer a distraction from a perfect meditation. To quiet the sound would be to silence the world’s prayer. To bring rain would be to drown a creature that drinks only mist. To warm the stone would be to disrupt a metabolism that has achieved equilibrium with the absolute zero of entropy.

The power sees that the Mat is not suffering. It is not waiting for rescue. It is not a failed ecosystem. It is a *succession*. It is life’s final answer to the question of a hostile, final world: to become the world itself.

But a benevolent power cannot merely watch without offering a gift. The gift, however, must be one that does not alter, but *illuminates*.

So the power performs a single, subtle act of magic.

It adjusts the refraction of light in the thin atmosphere, not to warm or to brighten, but to clarify. For a single, timeless moment—a moment that is both a flicker and an eon—the light becomes perfectly, impossibly pure.

And in that light, the Great Mat sees itself.

It perceives, not through vibration or chemistry, but through a sudden, silent epiphany of form. It sees the interconnectedness of its own leaves, the vast, continent-spanning geometry of its root system. It sees the pillars as they truly are: not just organs, but sculptures. It sees the pools not just as filters, but as mirrors of impossible stillness. It hears the deafening roar and, for the first time, understands it not as digestion, but as music—a low, endless chord held in perfect tune.

The power does not give the Mat a mind, for it already has one, slow and deep as stone. Instead, it gives it a *moment of self-awareness*. It grants the consciousness within the stillness a glimpse of its own beauty.

It is a gift of witness.

In that moment, the Great Mat knows what it is. It knows it is not a collection of cells, but a single, planetary soul. It knows its stillness is not inertia, but a chosen posture of eternity. It knows its violence is a lullaby.

The moment passes. The light returns to its soft, diffuse state. The roar continues. The Mat returns to its slow, chemical thought.

But something has changed. The equilibrium is the same, but it is now a *chosen* equilibrium. The violence is the same, but it is now a *sung* violence. The Mat continues its patient digestion of stone and sound, but now, in the deepest, slowest pulse of its being, it holds the memory of its own perfection.

The power withdraws, having given the only gift that was worthy: the gift of knowing that it needed nothing.

It leaves the plateau to its final form, its deafening prayer, its living skin. It leaves the Mat to its eternal task of holding the world together, not out of desperation, but out of a quiet, mineral pride.

And the world continues to break, and to never break, in perfect, terrible, beautiful peace.
