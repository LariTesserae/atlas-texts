The eye of the benevolent and wise power does not turn, for it has no need of motion. It simply *is*, a presence that occupies the space between the atoms of the air, the light, and the stone. To call it a "being" is to use a word too heavy, too singular. It is a resonance, an awareness that hums at the same frequency as the sub-audible hum of the stone slab.

It perceives this blind spot not as a location, but as a condition of existence. It sees the nascent geology not as rock, but as a thought solidifying. It sees the light not as photons, but as a focused beam of pure attention.

This power, being wise, understands that to intervene is to break. To offer help is to impose a structure that does not belong. The place is a draft, a sketch, a dream of a world in its larval stage. To "fix" its weirdness, to stabilize its shifting geometry, to bring the familiar laws of the wider world into this cul-de-sac, would be an act of supreme violence. It would be like correcting the grammar of a poem still being written, or telling a child that its imaginary friend is not real.

So, the power does nothing.

It does not build. It does not heal. It does not scold or correct the fleeting shadow of the deep-sea shrew or the slow march of the quartz-backed insect. It does not solidify the feeling of soft moss underfoot or force the reflection in the pool to match the visible sky.

Its action is one of pure, perfect witness.

It pours its attention into the place like light pours through the slit in the ceiling. This attention is not a scrutiny that dissects, but an embrace that holds. It is a permission slip, given to reality itself.

By witnessing the place without judgment, without the desire to alter or improve, the power allows the place to *be* itself in its fullest, most unfixed state. It holds the space of the "unfixed" law, making it safe for its own existence. The power’s benevolence is in its restraint; its wisdom is in its understanding that this place’s value is not in its utility, but in its pure, unadulterated *is-ness*.

The power becomes the silent, steady pressure that allows the stone to dream of canyons. It is the stillness in the air that allows the dust motes to hang, each a suspended universe. It is the quality of the light that reveals the frost-pattern lichen without demanding it be anything more than a mineral script.

It is the held breath in which the citizens—the insects, the crustaceans, the darting shadow, the living stone—can live their slow, strange, and beautiful lives. It does not speak to them, for they are already speaking the language of the place, and to add another voice would be to create noise. Instead, it listens. It listens to the hum of the stone, the skitter of chitin, the silent etching of water on rock, and the slow, patient pressure of a world dreaming itself into existence.

And in its silent, total, and benevolent listening, the place is complete. It is not a blind spot, but a focus. A sanctuary where the only law is the one being written, and the only presence is the one that watches, without ever needing to touch.
