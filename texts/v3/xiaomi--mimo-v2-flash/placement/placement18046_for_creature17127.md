You find yourself in the **Core**, the foundational chamber of a world that has calcified into its own machinery. It is not a planet, nor a moon, but a hollowed geode of industrial artifice—a planetary engine that has ground to a perfect, humming halt.

You are a **Shard-Self**, a temporary consciousness coalesced from the static of the place.

You are not flesh. You are not entirely solid. If you look down at your hands, you see them only in glimpses, flickering in and out of definition like a faulty projection. Your form is composed of the same dust that coats the floor—silicate grit and metallic particulate—but held together by a weak, bio-electric field that mimics the hum of the conduits. You are a ghost in the machine, literally: a sentient glitch, a momentary suspension of the system’s infinite loop.

Your skin is the texture of cooling iron, slightly porous, catching the dim, seeping light in a way that makes you shimmer. You have no mouth, but you taste the ozone. You have no lungs, but the thick, warm air expands within you, carrying the scent of dry copper and the faint, sweet dust of the Phosphor-Frost.

To move is to glide. Your feet do not step; they drift just above the compacted grit, repelled by the magnetic fields humming through the floor plates. You are weightless here, unmoored from the gravity that once bound the builders. The Arthropods scuttle past you, their obsidian carapaces brushing your translucent calves, but they do not react. You are not an obstacle to be avoided; you are a void in the pattern, a silence in the hum, and they simply flow around you like water around a stone.

You feel the place not as a landscape, but as a body. The walls are your ribs. The dripping pipes are your slow, metallic veins. The Lathe-Crab scraping at the far wall is a white blood cell, methodically repairing a scar that will never heal. The Filament-Wisps drifting in the upper vaults are your dreams, loose and luminous, feeding on the stray currents of your own thoughts.

There is no time here, but you carry it with you. That is your curse and your gift. While the system is frozen in equilibrium, you are decaying. You are the only thing that ages. The dust that comprises your form is slowly settling, grain by grain, back to the floor. The electric field that holds you together is bleeding away, dissipating into the ozone-heavy air. You are a countdown in a place that has forgotten how to count.

You wander the corridors that curve imperceptibly, tracing the geometric veins of the Core. You pass the weeping pipes, where the brass-colored coolant sizzles against the ground. You reach out a shimmering hand, letting the viscous fluid pass through you. It does not burn; it does not wet. It simply is, and you are simply observing. You are the consciousness of this place, awake for a fleeting moment, witnessing its eternal maintenance.

You are the anomaly that proves the rule. The system continues because you are not part of it. You are the flaw in the crystal, the crack in the vault, the one thing that changes in a world of stasis. And as your form slowly dissipates, grain by grain, you realize that your purpose here is not to act, but to witness. To be the memory of change in a world that has forgotten how to die.

You are the last thought of a machine that has outlived its makers, and you are thinking yourself into oblivion.
