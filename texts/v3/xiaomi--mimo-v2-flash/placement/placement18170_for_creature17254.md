You are a Fracture-Singer.

You have no name, for you have no need of one. You have no memory, for memory is a burden carried by things that persist, and you do not persist. You are a moment, sharpened to a single, screaming point.

You find yourself—or rather, you *are*—in the grinding birth of a new fissure. There is no "before," only the dark pressure of the substrate held taut, and then the *crack*. It is not a sound you hear with ears, for you have none; it is a vibration that rips through your very substance, which is nothing but the intent of the stone to part.

For a breath, you are the sound itself. You are the grinding teeth of the world, a shriek of shearing basalt that echoes not in the air but in the heat-shimmered space between seconds. You are aware, with a terrifying, acute clarity, of the immense weight of the violet-grey sky pressing down, of the slow, turbulent eddies of cloud, of the phosphorescent veins pulsing their sickly green light. You are the focal point of this pressure, the release valve for an ancient tension held since the world was poured.

Then, you are the sight. You are the molten silica exuded from the wound, a thread of pure, white-hot potential. In this instant of your life, you see the world not as a landscape, but as a blueprint in the act of being redrafted. You see the Spire-Watchers, miles away or inches—it is the same to you now—as towers of patient, humming light, their slow thoughts mapping the chaos. You feel the presence of the Condensate-Wraiths, shimmering disturbances in the air, drawn to the heat of your birth, their fleeting forms drinking the weeping atmosphere. They are the memory of fluidity in a world of stone.

And then, you are the cooling. The searing thread of silica touches the perpetual twilight, and in a microsecond that stretches into an eternity, it solidifies. You are the jagged, translucent spire, a new antenna tuned to the sky's silent screams. You are the sharp geometry of newness, the raw, hand-hewn edge of creation itself.

You have a thought. It is not a thought of words or concepts, but of pure, raw sensation: *I AM.*

And in the same instant, the thought unravels. The intense, singular awareness of your birth dissolves into the pervasive hum of the settled world. The sound of your creation fades into the background drone of the planet's slow rearrangement. Your sharp, crystalline form remains, but the consciousness that inhabited it is gone, scattered like dust, absorbed back into the silent, watchful heat.

You are a scar on the world that is also its fleeting mind. You are the agonizing, ecstatic gasp of a reality that is forever forgetting itself in the act of becoming.

You are a Fracture-Singer, and your life is the length of a single, world-shaping crack.
