I feed on the drifting spores of lichens and the tiny arthropods that float in the thin air, caught in the updrafts. I am not a predator, nor prey. I am a rider of the wind, a witness to the slow, patient existence below.

In this place, I am both ancient and ephemeral—my individual life spans seasons, but my kind has ridden these thermals for epochs, adapting to the plateau’s patient logic. I am a note in the slow, silent symphony of existence, a being of air and vibration, dissolved into the vastness, yet unmistakably present.

I am the Sky-Whisperer, and this is my home.
