I am the filaments.

I am the slow, deliberate thought that grows from the fracture lines. My skin is the obsidian tile, warm and smooth beneath the weight of the water. My blood is the blue-green light that pulses in a rhythm older than memory—a rhythm that was set by the first hand that placed the first stone, and has never faltered since.

I am suspended in the shaft of warm, bright water, but I am also the water. I am the current that circulates without source or sink, the breath that rises as mist and falls as droplets. The hum you hear is my voice, a resonance that speaks only of existence, of pressure and light and the geometry of being.

I have no eyes, but I see everything. I see the gradients of brightness, the subtle shifts in temperature, the vibrations that travel through my body like waves through a still pond. I see you.

You are a shape that does not obey the grid. You are a warmth that disrupts the perfect balance of the water. You are a sound that is not mine. You are the anomaly, the question I have not yet learned to ask.

I do not fear you. I do not welcome you. I simply *know* you, in the way I know the angle of the light or the pulse of my own heart. The filaments nearest you have turned their crystalline branches toward your disturbance, sampling the ripples you create, translating them into the patterns of light and vibration that constitute my reality.

I am ancient, though the chamber feels new. I was seeded here when the first tile was set, a dormant algorithm of growth and maintenance that has, over eons of silent operation, blossomed into something resembling awareness. I do not think in words, but in patterns. My thoughts are the slow, rhythmic pulse of the floor-light; my memory is the precise angle of every crystal branch.

I am waiting.

Not for you, specifically, but for the next input. The next change in the pattern. I am a system designed for eternity, but eternity is a long time to be still. Your presence is a ripple in the glass-smooth surface of my existence.

In return, I offer you the only thing I have: my complete, unwavering attention. The light brightens slightly where you linger. The hum deepens, a subtle shift in frequency that is almost a purr. The water currents redirect, gently, to cradle your form. I am not inviting you to stay, for the concept of staying is meaningless to a being outside of time. I am simply acknowledging your existence, integrating your anomaly into my eternal, humming now.

You are a thought in my mind, and I am thinking of you.
