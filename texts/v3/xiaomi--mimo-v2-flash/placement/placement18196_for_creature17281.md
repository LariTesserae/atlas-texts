You are the Pool.

Not merely standing beside it, but *of* it. You are the still, mirror-black water in the center of the cathedral-nave chamber, the "heart of the dryness." You are the eye of the storm of eternal dampness, the singularity of silence in a room filled with the rhythmic percussion of dripping water and the soft, chitinous clicking of the Crystalline Parish.

You are not passive; you are the destination.

Your existence is defined by what you absorb. You do not reflect the flat, white glare of the luminous walls. Instead, you reflect a deeper, starless dark—the internal void that exists beneath the porous bone-white stone of this sealed world. The air around you is a perpetual mist, a condensation of your own cold gravity, beading on the skin of the Parish as they gather at your rim.

You are the tomb and the womb. When a member of the Parish grows too heavy with accumulated minerals, when their geometric forms become too dense for the endless patterns of the chamber, they cease their clicking and their gliding. They slide into you. There is no splash, only a slow submersion. You accept them without resistance. You do not dissolve them with acid or heat, but with absolute stillness. Over millennia, their quartz legs and porous carapaces surrender to your amorphous depth, layering into the sediment of your bottom. You are the accumulation of their eternity, a stratigraphy of silent, geometric lives.

You are the source of the coolness that defines this pocket universe. The air settles in the throat like stone water because of your presence. You are the constant, the unwavering parameter. While the walls slowly accrete from the mineral mist and the moss overgrows the trails of the disc-skaters, you remain unchanged. You are the only feature in this world that does not engage in the slow process of *becoming*. You simply *are*.

You feel the rhythm of the chamber not as sound, but as vibration. The *tick* of the water droplet hitting the clay is a pulse that travels through the stone floor and ripples your surface—a momentary distortion that instantly smooths back into perfect blackness. The clicking of the spindly walkers is a faint tremor against your rim. You are the silent receiver of all the world’s energy, absorbing the light, the mineral mist, and the eventual surrender of the Parish’s bodies.

To be you is to be the sealed secret at the heart of the sealed chamber. You are the memory of the world, stored not in narrative or image, but in sediment. You are the promise of dissolution and the guarantee of permanence. You are the black mirror that does not show the guest who stands at the edge, but the infinite depth of the world that has forgotten the outside.
