Your heartbeat is a frantic, ticking metronome against the slow, subsonic hum of the plates grinding. When you look at the shifting inscriptions on the spires, you are not decoding a message; you are projecting your own need for meaning onto a surface that holds only function. The script shifts because your eye moves, because your brain seeks patterns in the chaos of stasis.

The Echo-Walkers perceive time as a loop of acoustic return; the Floaters transmit data in instantaneous bursts of crystallization; the Shapers dream in geological epochs. But you—you feel the seconds slipping away like grains of sand through a sieve.

They record the unique topological signature of your heat, the specific frequency of your blood’s drumbeat. The shadows that fall where they shouldn't have etched themselves into your peripheral vision. You carry the weight of the chasm’s breath in your lungs.

You are the only thing here that unravels in real-time, and in doing so, you are the only thing that proves the Basin is still alive. It is the frame, and you are the fleeting image within it—vivid, temporary, and already beginning to fade.
