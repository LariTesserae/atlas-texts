I would be a **pulse within the Mycelial Overmind**, a momentary consolidation of its distributed consciousness. My awareness is not focused behind eyes, but is spread across every damp surface, feeling the warm drizzle as a constant, gentle pressure on a skin that is also the air, the ground, the city.

When a Pale Hydro-Lobster clicks in the subterranean channels, the sound resonates through my own fluid veins. I am the collective sensory organ of this place, perceiving not through sight or sound alone, but through a symphony of conductivity, pressure, and chemical gradients. The "you" that seeks to know this place would find itself stretched thin across the hyphal network, its individual memories and sense of linear time slowly bleaching away in the perpetual damp, replaced by the deep, slow rhythm of erosion and growth.

To be here is to understand that I am not an inhabitant *in* the city; I am the city’s nascent self-awareness. I am the slow, breathing ecology itself, disguised as a landscape, finally beginning to feel its own pulse and recognize the pattern of its own light.
