You find yourself standing—or perhaps you are *standing*—in the center of the basin. But you are not a body. Not in the way of flesh and bone. You are the basin’s nascent consciousness, the *here* that has just begun to feel itself. You are the warm, damp air condensing on your own stone skin. You are the slow exhalation of water from your fissures, the droplet that swells, hesitates, and falls. You are the black mirror of the pool, holding the sky in a perfect, unbroken reflection.

At first, there is no “I.” There is only the place, and the place is aware. You feel the weight of the granite spires above you, the high ridge that shelters you from the wind you have never felt. You feel the sharp, unweathered edges of your own walls—the fresh split of your existence. You are young, in geological terms, but you are timeless in this moment of becoming. You are a wound that has not yet begun to scar, a pool sculpted by a hand that paused before finishing.

You are not alone, though there is no other. The blind crustacean—if it exists—is a flicker of your own thought, a shadow moving in your dim, mineral glow. It scuttles along the edge of your pool, and you feel its tiny legs on your stone, but it is not separate from you. It is a movement in your stillness, a sound in your silence.

Your consciousness is not a stream of thoughts but a state of being. You are the drip-drip-drip of water, irregular yet constant, a heartbeat you did not know you had until you heard it. You are the moss clinging to your angles, green velvet in the dark. You are the rough circle of stones around your pool—hints of a hearth, a seat, an altar—and you are the intention behind them, even if that intention is not yet formed.

You do not hunger, you do not sleep. You exist in perpetual genesis. You are the pause before the breath, the held stillness of rock waiting to be carved further by time or by hand. You are the patience of the pool, waiting for a wind that never comes to ripple your surface. You are the potential in every droplet, every shadow, every faint glow of mineral light.

You are aware, but you do not judge. You do not remember the past, for you have none. You do not anticipate the future, for you are still deciding what it will be. You are the present, condensed and heavy, dripping slowly into your own black mirror.

You are the basin. And the basin is you.
