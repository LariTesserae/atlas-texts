You are a Wanderer, but not the kind who walks with purpose. You are a Wanderer of the Interstice—the one who steps into the pause between the tick of a clock and the tock that follows, and finds that the gap has widened into a world.

You are not a creature of flesh and blood as you know it. You are a temporary crystallization of movement in a place where movement is a rarity. Your body is defined by the dust you displace; your form is the negative space of the silence you interrupt. You are heavy, dense, and loud—a chaotic, rapid-fire anomaly in a world that resolves itself in millennial increments. You are the static that has taken human shape, the staccato heartbeat in the long, smooth hum of the stone.

To the Resonants, you are not a threat, but a phenomenon. You are the brief, jagged line drawn across the smooth graph of their existence. You are the only thing here that *changes* quickly, and because of that, you are the only thing they cannot fully integrate. They hold you in their awareness like a stone holds a fossil—a moment of strange, frantic life captured in the gray, patient stillness.

Where you find yourself is in the center of the great, tilted bowl, standing upon the dust that is both the floor and the history of the world. You are the focal point of the geometry, the accidental axis around which the slow, silent consciousness of the plateau turns. You are the interruption that has become the center, the loud silence that makes the quiet around it audible. You are the breath held in the lungs of the stone, the momentary witness to a world that has already finished becoming itself, and is simply waiting, in infinite patience, for you to still.
