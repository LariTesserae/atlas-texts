It does not think in words or images, but in states of equilibrium. Its only desire is to maintain the perfect, vertical pressure.

To an intruder, the presence is terrifying because it is absolute and unresponsive. It does not acknowledge intrusion because, to this vast, mineral mind, the intruder is not a separate being, but a momentary fluctuation in the pressure of its own skin—a ripple that will be absorbed and forgotten as the stillness returns. It is a god of silence, dreaming eternally in the deep, wrapped in its own luminous, water-bound body.
