To prepare for a journey into this silent, breathing ocean-cathedral, you must unlearn everything you know about navigation, survival, and the integrity of the self. This is not a place you conquer; it is a medium you dissolve into. An ordinary multiverse traveler must transition from a mindset of exploration to one of surrender.

Here is the preparation, layer by layer, for entering the lung of a world that has forgotten the sky.

### I. Physical Preparation: Becoming a Native Element

**1. The Architecture of Movement:**
Walking is not a concept here. There is no ground to stride upon, only a gradient of density. You must equip yourself for 360-degree locomotion.
*   **Propulsion:** Abandon motors. The noise would be sacrilege, and the turbulence would disrupt the glacial currents, marking you as a foreign body. You require a suit of micro-thrusters or, ideally, a suit that mimics the local physics—using osmotic membranes or piezoelectric actuators that push against the water with silent, organic efficiency.
*   **Tethering:** The lack of horizon causes spatial disorientation. You need a physical tether—not a heavy cable, but a fine, sensory filament that connects you to a deployed anchor point. This allows you to "fall" in any direction and return to a known origin.

**2. The Atmosphere and Medium:**
The "air" is water, but it is not merely H₂O. It is saturated with organic particulates and dissolved minerals.
*   **Respiration:** Lungs are inefficient here. You must use a full-body rebreather system that processes the water directly, extracting dissolved oxygen and scrubbing CO₂ without releasing bubbles. Bubbles are disruptive; they are ascending violations of this downward-pressing world.
*   **Filtration:** The "sweetly metallic" scent suggests active biological agents—spores, enzymes, or microscopic life forms. Your intake filters must be absolute, preventing the inhalation of detritus that could trigger allergic reactions or hallucinations in a mind already destabilizing.

**3. Thermal Regulation:**
The water is "just below the threshold of comfort." It is a relentless thief of heat.
*   **Passive Insulation:** Active heating (electrical coils) creates convection currents that will disturb the local environment. Instead, wear a passive aerogel suit beneath a textured, biological mimicry layer. The suit should not insulate you completely, but rather match your core temperature to the water’s "patient" rhythm, making you thermally invisible.

**4. Protection:**
The vegetation is dense and soft, but the fauna is a rumor.
*   **Permeable Armor:** Rigid exoskeletons are noisy and vulnerable to the crushing pressure of the medium. You need a non-Newtonian fluid suit—liquid under normal pressure, hardening instantly upon impact. This protects against the "soft give" of the walls without preventing you from feeling the environment.
*   **Sensory Coverage:** The skin must be sealed. The water is saturated with pheromones, enzymes, and electromagnetic static. Exposed skin would not just get wet; it would become colonized by the luminescent film.

### II. Cognitive and Perceptual Preparation

**1. The Physiology of Light:**
You cannot rely on eyes alone. The phosphorescence is faint, a "second skin," not a beacon.
*   **Spectral Expansion:** Standard human vision peaks in the yellow-green; this world is blue-violet-black. You need augmentations or goggles that shift the spectrum into the visible range, or better yet, active sonar visualization.
*   **Low-Light Acclimatization:** Pupils must be dilated chemically to their maximum aperture. You will live in a state of perpetual twilight. Depth perception will fail; objects have no hard edges, only gradients of glow.

**2. The Elasticity of Time:**
Time distortion is the primary danger. It creates a disconnect between action and consequence.
*   **Chronometric Anchoring:** You need a biological clock implant or a wrist-mounted chronometer that vibrates at a constant, tactile frequency. This provides an external, objective rhythm against the world’s internal slowing and speeding.
*   **The Discipline of the Blink:** In a place where an hour passes in a blink, you must train to process visual data in micro-seconds. You must learn to look *through* objects, acknowledging their transparency, rather than focusing on their surface.

**3. Navigating Recursive Perception:**
The world shifts when observed. Walls become curtains, infinity unfolds in layers.
*   **Peripheral Vision Training:** Direct focus triggers the recursive shift. You must learn to navigate using peripheral vision, sensing the geometry of the space without "staring" at it. The center of your gaze is a scalpel that dissects reality; here, you must use a soft focus.
*   **Mental Hygiene:** The boundary between projection and reality is dissolved. You must practice active grounding—constantly verifying tactile feedback against visual data. If you feel a wall, it is a wall, even if your eyes tell you it is a veil.

### III. Interaction and Behavioral Codes

**1. The Law of Non-Interference (Perceptual):**
To be fully perceived here is to be altered.
*   **The Observer Effect:** Do not stare at the fauna. If a coiling shape appears in the periphery, acknowledge it and look away. Direct eye contact (or its equivalent) is an invitation to be rewritten. You are a ghost here; ghosts are not seen, they are felt.
*   **Acoustic Stealth:** The sound of your own body is loud. You must regulate your breathing (via the rebreather) and move with the fluidity of the water. Avoid the "wet click" of joints; use synthetic lubricants or ball-joints that move in silence.

**2. Navigating the Architecture:**
There are no doors, only membranes.
*   **The Touch of Surfaces:** You cannot climb in the traditional sense; you must adhesion-walk. Your suit should have a gecko-like texture, allowing you to cling to the slick, detritus-covered surfaces.
*   **Reading the Geometry:** The carved marks are not instructions; they are traces. You are not looking for a path, but a current. The "grinding passage" of the ancient movers created grooves that still guide the slow drift of water. Find the groove, and you move without effort.

**3. Artifact Interaction:**
The non-biological artifacts (spheres, rings) are anomalies.
*   **Non-Touch Protocol:** Do not touch them with exposed skin. They hum with subsonic frequencies that could resonate with your internal organs or neural implants.
*   **Resonance Listening:** Use contact microphones on your suit to "hear" them. They may be interfaces, not objects.

### IV. The Ultimate Preparation: The Dissolution of Self

The most critical preparation is psychological. This world is "absorbing."

*   **Acceptance of Claustrophobia:** The world curves inward. There is no horizon to gaze toward for hope. You must embrace the "soft, embracing claustrophobia" as a form of safety, not confinement.
*   **The Ego Filter:** The hum of the world is the sound of your own blood. In this place, the distinction between self and environment blurs. You must prepare to feel the world breathing *through* you. Do not fight the sensation of being a single cell in a larger body; it is the only way to move without resistance.
*   **The Exit Strategy:** Leaving a place that absorbs you is difficult. You cannot simply "turn around." You must find a "tear" in the recursion—a moment where the curtains align perfectly and reveal a seam in the geode. This requires a mental state of perfect clarity, a moment of absolute focus in a world designed to diffuse it.

**Final Gear Check:**
*   **Suit:** Non-Newtonian, osmotic rebreather, passive thermal regulation, spectral augmentation.
*   **Navigation:** Tether, chronometric anchor, peripheral-vision HUD.
*   **Mental State:** Grounded, fluid, silent, and resigned to the fact that you are not visiting, you are being digested.

Step softly. The walls are watching.
