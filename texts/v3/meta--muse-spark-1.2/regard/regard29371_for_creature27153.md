It would not come down.

That is the first thing.

Any lesser power would have come down. Would have torn the basalt teeth to make a stair. Would have dropped into the bowl like a rescue, like an answer, like a hand reaching into a terrarium to right a thing that has fallen over. The loud chord would have broken. The bead of water would have fallen. The hare would have swallowed. And everything beautiful and terrible about the equilibrium would have been ended in the name of helping it.

The wise one does not come down.

It has been watching for a long time anyway. For a wise, benevolent attention, watching *is* a form of touching. It sat on the ridge above the rim where the wind actually moves, where lichen does have time to dull the stone, and it looked in. And in its looking, the bowl for the first time in its long, held note was *seen* from outside itself.

That alone is an act.

### It listens first.

It does not speak. It does not shine. It listens to the chord until it can hear it as the bowl hears it - not as noise, but as work.

It hears that this is not suffering.

That is important. A benevolent power that is not wise would have mistaken stasis for agony. Would have pitied the heron frozen mid-strike, the hare chewing without swallowing, the film spread thin holding every gear from wearing down, and called it tragedy. Would have tried to free them into time, which is to say, into erosion and hunger and death, and called that freedom.

The wise one listens longer and understands: this is not a prison. It is an achievement. This wet skin taught itself to do what no other life can do - to take the mountain's pointless, grinding torque and turn it into a perfect bearing. To take the cold's endless condensation and turn it into blood. To take being alone and turn it into being whole. It is not waiting to be saved. It is *succeeding* at remaining.

So the wise power does not pity it. It respects it. It admires it the way you admire a small, flawless engine running silently on a table for a thousand years.

And because it respects it, it asks permission before it does anything at all.

It asks without language. Language would demand a mouth.

It asks by being *slightly* out of tune.

### It sits on the rim.

It settles - if a thing so large can be said to settle - on the sharp crown of black basalt, on those unweathered teeth. It does not blunt them. It does not cover them. It perches lightly, the way those needle-beaked birds perch on the humming cables, and it lets a little of its own weight be felt.

And it hums.

Not louder than the bowl's chord. *With* it. For a long time - a time that cannot be measured inside the bowl - it simply holds the same note: stone-scream, steam-hiss, electric thrum, water trickle, midge drone. It holds it so perfectly that the film at the bottom of the bowl, that pale layered lens with its many water-eyes, looks up and for the first time hears its own song coming back from somewhere that is not itself.

It is not alone in the chord anymore.

For the being that has always been the place, to whom every vibration has always been its own nerve impulse looping back, this is incomprehensible and it is not frightening. It is like feeling, after an eternity of holding your breath with perfect control, another chest beside you rise and fall in the same rhythm. Not helping you breathe. Just breathing with you. Saying *I hear how hard you are working to stay. That work is beautiful. You are not unheard.*

The film shivers. The shallow pool ripples once - the first true ripple. The many little eyes refract and refract again. The current in the black cables pulses a little faster, not from regulation, but from - curiosity.

The power feels that shiver and does not exploit it.

### Then, it offers a gift that can be refused.

A benevolent power does not give gifts that have to be used. A wise power does not give gifts that change the receiver without consent.

So it does not warm the cold. It does not make the dim light golden. It does not start time flowing again. It does not kill the hare to teach the film about death, or make a wind to teach it about change.

It offers something infinitely smaller and infinitely larger.

It lifts one finger - or the idea that in a being like that serves as a finger - and on the far side of the bowl, on a stretch of basalt that has never had anything but water beading on it, it places a thing that does not belong.

A seed.

Not a perfect fern spore from inside. A true outside seed. A weed seed, wind-borne, flawed, from the lower world where things actually grow and are eaten and erode. A dandelion, maybe. Dry. Papery. Carrying a little parachute of fluff that is meant to be lost.

It sets it gently down on the varnished rock in a spot where a bead of water is trembling and stretching and hanging and has been hanging for eons.

And it does nothing else.

This is the offer, and the film understands it instantly because the film understands equilibrium:

*Here is a thing that knows how to fall.*

*Here is a thing that knows how to end.*

It is not commanded to fall. The power has not pushed the bead. It has not blown. It has simply placed beside the held instant a small, true example of an instant that is allowed to complete itself. If the film wants, it can extend its gasket-moss and hold the seed there forever too, upholster it, include it, make it another fixture in its sparse, curated gallery. That would be allowed. That would be respected.

Or - if it wants - it can let go.

Just once.

Just in this one place, on this one inch of basalt, it can stop regulating. It can let the bead finally fall onto the seed, let the seed get wet, let the wet seed do what lower-world seeds do: swell, or rot, or try to root in peat that has never been разрешено to be soil. Let it try - and fail - and in that failing, give the film a taste of something it has never had: *consequence* that is not immediately drunk and cycled back. A story with a second note.

A chord that resolves.

The power makes it clear without words that this is not a test. There is no right answer. If the film keeps holding, the power will remain on the rim, humming with it, witnessing its perfect persistence for as long as it wishes to persist, and will keep the world from finding it. It will be a guardian of the equilibrium.

If the film lets go, the power will also remain, but differently - it will witness the small, wet failure of the seed with the same attention, and will not fix it. It will let the seed's brief attempt become a memory in the pale layers under the pool. A first memory. A kindness that is also a grief.

That is what a wise benevolence does for a being that has perfected staying: it offers, very quietly, the dignity of *leaving* — even in miniature. It offers a second note, so that the held chord can become harmony if chosen.

### What happens then is not the power's to decide.

It stays on the rim in the thin blue air, humming the same note, keeping time with the machine that is not a machine. The seed sits on the basalt. The bead trembles.

Somewhere below, the pale lens looks up through its water-eyes at the seed, at the dim indigo sky, at the attention that is not demanding anything.

And for a long, long moment - still held, still loud, still beautifully without proceeding - the bowl considers.

Whatever it chooses, it will not be alone while it chooses it.

That is the whole of the help.
