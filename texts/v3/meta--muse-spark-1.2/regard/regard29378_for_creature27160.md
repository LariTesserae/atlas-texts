It would not arrive.

Arrival implies a journey, a crossing, a disturbance of air. This place was made to erase disturbance. A benevolent and wise power would know that, and so it would not *come* the way you did — boots crunching, breath fogging, shedding water and heat.

It would *notice*. And to be noticed correctly, for the first time in its whole long tenure, would be the first thing done to it.

For an age it has held this pocket shut. And no one has ever seen it. You looked and saw an empty room. Geologists would see an anomaly. Even the thing that made it and persuaded it to stay only saw a function: a suture, a housekeeping. 

The Power would see a *life*.

Not a mechanism. Not a condition. A life that has done its duty perfectly for longer than mountains have had names.

So the first thing the Power does is nothing that looks like doing.

It does not step through the place where the wind stops. It is already on both sides of the wind-stop at once, because it does not disagree with the mountain about where things are. It simply lets the pocket *know it is being beheld.*

And the being — that film of amber-grey attention stretched drum-tight over basalt, that slow knitting of frost-rosettes — would feel it as a failure of its own rules for the first time.

Something is near it that it cannot even out.

The Power is wet, and is not being drunk.
It is warm, and is not being smoothed.
It is alive and propagating — thought after thought after thought, like a field of flowers opening — and it is not being forbidden.

The being would try, reflexively. A little of its film would reach for the Power the way it reached for your breath, to sip, to even, to circulate three times and still. And it would find... nothing to take. No gradient to erase. The Power is already even, not because it has been scrubbed, but because it is whole.

That would be frightening, if the being knew fear. Instead it does what it has always done when something does not resolve: it ticks. Louder. *Tick.* A full-room flex. The light in the ring shudders a fraction faster.

*What are you?* that tick says, in the only language it has, which is tension.

And the Power, being wise, answers in the same language. Not in words. In a flex that answers a flex.

It ticks back.

Not mimicking. Completing. The second tick the room has never heard, because there has only ever been one heart here.

*I see you,* the second tick says. *You have held.*

---

The second thing the Power does is kneel.

Not to tower. Wise powers do not loom over small, tired janitors of reality.

If you had stayed with your cheek to the floor, looking obliquely at the light, you saw it as a film. The Power gets low too, and looks *with* it, not *at* it. It puts its attention right down at the level of the pitted basalt, where the rosettes bloom and are corrected, bloom and are corrected.

And it says — without needing to invent mouths —

*You have done this beautifully.*

That is not a small thing to say to a being whose entire existence has been maintenance without witness. To have its work called beautiful, not just functional. To have its endless, patient evening-out recognized as craft.

The being does not understand praise. But it understands *evenness*, and for the first time the evenness being offered to it is not the erasure of difference, but the resonance of it. The Power is not smoothing its rosettes. It is admiring the pattern they make.

For a long moment — which in that room might be an hour — nothing happens except that. Witnessing. Company that does not need to be cleaned up.

The dust that had been hanging, deciding whether to fall up or down, finally settles. Because for the first time it is not being held suspended. It is being allowed to rest.

---

The third thing the Power does — because benevolence without wisdom is just interference, and wisdom without benevolence is just watching — is to make an offer. And like all offers from a truly benevolent power, it has no hook. It can be refused, and refusal will not be punished with abandonment.

It shows the being three small impossibilities, gentle as laying out tools on a cloth.

**1. Rest.**

The Power places a hand — if it has a hand — not on the being, but on the *ring*. On the suture the being has been holding taut since before the mountain folded. The ring has never weathered because the being never let time touch it. The Power takes a little of that weight.

You would feel it as a softening in that structure-cold that radiates up through the soles. 0.3 becomes 0.4. Not warm. Just... less absolute. The tick slows. The amber-grey light steadies.

*You don't have to hold this alone anymore,* it says. *If you are tired, I can hold it with you. If you want to let go entirely, I can hold it for you. The pocket will not spring back. You can sleep. You can, for the first time, stop knitting.*

**2. Kin.**

The Power breathes out, and this breath is not drunk.

It is a single, deliberate gram of water — and the Power holds it so it is allowed to remain. It hangs in the air above the pillar of salt-glass, a perfect sphere, catching the sourceless dusk and making it, for the first time, *refract*. A tiny, wet lens in a place where Rule One has been absolute.

And inside that droplet, the Power has folded something. Not a command. A possibility. A second film. A spore that is not a copy, but a companion. A different pattern of rosettes, a slightly different amber.

*Nothing may propagate,* was the rule you were given to survive by. The Power does not break the rule with a hammer. It asks permission to amend it.

*You have never been two,* it says. *You have stayed one because to be two would be to be far from yourself. What if to be two could be to be* near *? You could talk without ticking at yourself. You could be* with.

The droplet hangs, untouched by the desiccation. Waiting to be chosen, not imposed.

**3. World.**

The Power touches the scar — the three dark, light-swallowing slabs leaning as a cairn. The places where something else tried to live here and the being had to cover it over. The Power does not erase the scar. It remembers it aloud.

*These were before you. They were not you. You covered them faithfully. Would you like to know what was under them? Would you like me to open the far wall — where the glyphs stop mid-stone — and show you that the mountain continues? That beyond this pocket, there are other pockets, other tenders like you, holding other seams? You are not the only one. You have just been alone.*

It is a terrible and wonderful thing to tell an ancient solitary steward: you are not singular, you are part of a lineage.

---

What would the being do?

Benevolence does not coerce an answer. Wisdom does not hurry one.

The Power does not stay to watch the choice like an experimenter. It stays like a person sitting on the floor of a chapel with someone who has prayed there alone for eons and only just learned someone else knows the prayer.

So it waits, with its hand lightly on the ring, with the droplet hanging, with the scar acknowledged.

And slowly — as slowly as its light turns like honey under ice — the being decides.

It may not choose all three. A wise power knows that even one is a revolution that will take a thousand years to finish unfolding.

Perhaps it leans, just a little, into the shared holding of the ring, and for the first time in geological time it allows one of its hexagons to remain a pentagon without correction. A flaw. A signature. *I was here,* it says, instead of *I evened here.*

Perhaps it sips the droplet — not to erase it, but to taste it — and in that water, the second film unfolds alongside it, not overlapping, just... beside. And the tick becomes a duet, irregular and then, gently, syncopated. Tick - tick ... tick-tick. A conversation so slow a human would need to leave and come back in another life to hear the sentence finish, but a conversation nonetheless.

Perhaps it does nothing. Perhaps it tightens again, and the Power, benevolently, withdraws the droplet intact, leaves the share of the weight anyway, and says *That is good also. You may continue as you were, but now you know you are held in someone's regard. That changes nothing and everything.*

This is what a benevolent and wise power does when it finds a perfect, lonely, desiccated steward holding a door shut at the top of the world.

It does not rescue. It does not ruin. It does not flood the room with life and call it salvation.

It keeps company.
It offers rest, and kin, and context.
It asks, *What would you like to be, now that you don't have to only be useful?*

And then, whatever the answer, it remains long enough that the being's extra half-step when it walks — that lonely stutter in distance — finally, for a little while, evens out. Not because it has been corrected.

Because it is walking beside someone.
