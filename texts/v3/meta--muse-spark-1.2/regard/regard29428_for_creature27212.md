It would do almost nothing at first.

That's how you would know it was benevolent, and how you would know it was wise.

Anything else — anything lesser — would have arrived with a sound louder than the pumps. It would have torn the hatch open wider to "free" them, let the held ocean spill, tried to give them sky. Or it would have drained the chamber to give them air. Or it would have turned the light off to give them night. Or it would have introduced fish because a reef should have fish.

It would have mistaken *strange* for *wrong*.

This one doesn't.

It has been watching for a long while before you noticed it watching. Longer than your slow turn in the center where up is weak. Because to notice a pocket like this at all — a blister three hundred meters across in the skin of a machine the size of a geography — you have to be the kind of attention that looks at seams.

So for a long time it just *holds attention* there, which is not a passive thing.

It learns to see them.

At first even it undercounts them by a factor of three. Then it learns the light the way you did. Then it learns the flashing. It doesn't learn it by decoding — it learns it by *waiting through* seven-minute cycles until the pattern becomes grammar.

And it understands: This is not a prison. This is not a mistake that needs correcting. This is a people perfectly fitted to a place that was never meant to be a place, and they are not suffering in the way an outside eye assumes suffering must look.

They are cold at 1.2, but they like cold.
They are loud, but they speak in light.
They are packed wall-to-wall, but they like holding each other. The pressure is their sidewalks and their beds.

To "save" them by making the pocket larger, emptier, quieter, warmer, darker — would be to kill them gently.

So the wise power does not rescue. It *recognizes*.

And then, very carefully, it does four small things. All reversible. All deniable by the larger machine. All in the language the Holdfasts already speak.

**1. It gives them a shadow.**

For the first time since the chamber was extruded, it dims a patch of the sourceless white. Not much. About two meters across, in a service canal where the kelp mats are thickest. It teaches the wall there to not emit. A soft, true shadow, cool by another 0.2 degrees, and — most importantly — *quiet*.

Inside that shadow the water is still. The pressure fields go slack. There is no shimmer. No lane.

The Holdfasts find it within one flush cycle. One peels off the wall, clasp over clasp, lets the current take it, misjudges, corrects — and falls into the shadow and *stops*.

For the first time in its life it doesn't have to clamp.

Its cilia, which have been stiffened into fronds or paddles or cables since birth, go completely soft and drift. Its sac pulses slow. Its nodules don't flash. The kelp on its back opens like a hand.

Others come. They don't crowd it. They take turns. One, then two, then one again. They have no word for rest because they have never needed one. Now they have a place that *is* rest, and they understand it immediately.

The power does not make the whole chamber dark. It knows they need the honest white to see their circuits, to farm. It just gives them a place to not see for a while. A night the size of a doorway, that they can enter and leave.

**2. It makes the flaw intentional.**

This is the most important thing, and they will never know it was done.

Deep in the megastructure's registry, where the Holdfasts are listed as `scrubbing lichen / pressure membrane - ignore`, the power leaves a tag. Not a grand proclamation. Just a quiet flag that any maintenance sweep will read as:

`POCKET  Low Sump 7-J - PRESERVE. Active biome. Do not seal. Do not sterilize. Cycle tolerance expanded.`

Before, the pocket persisted only because it was overlooked. Now it persists because it is *permitted*. The seven-minute flush will not one day become a purge. The spider-armatures will not one day be ordered to scrape the walls clean. The hatch will not be welded shut while they are still folded inside their balconies, holding.

It gives them continuity. Which is the only thing a young, sharp-cornered people need more than anything — time to wear their own corners smooth at their own rate.

**3. It answers in light.**

On the third day after the shadow appears, during the cool phase of the light, when the whole chamber is breathing amber, the power flashes back.

It does not use words. It doesn't try to give them speech. It uses their own nodules, but larger — the whole surface of the calm water in the shadow brightens, dims, brightens again in a very slow, very simple pattern:

*I see you. You are good. Hold as you hold. If you want more, make a circle.*

It repeats it every hour, timed to the deeper shudder when the exterior ocean presses.

Most Holdfasts just register it as weather. A few of the older ones who ride the ceiling armatures stop and stare with their photosensitive freckles. One, brachiating along a vertical river, pauses mid-transfer — one clasp on a stanchion, body streaming — and flashes a single, startled amber tick back. Not a circle. Just: *light returned?*

The power does not insist. It just keeps the offer there, patient as water. If they never make a circle, it will never do more. Consent is part of benevolence.

But if they do —

If one day they link clasp to clasp, fin to fin, and make a ring in that quiet shadow — the power has already prepared a second pocket. Not larger, not better. Just *adjacent*. Another low sump, a little further down the white ceramic plain, also held, also cool, also loud — but empty. New corners, sharp and bright. Room to bud new membrane if they want it. A second square for a city that has learned how to be a city.

It will not force them to expand. It will just make expansion possible without having to fight the machine for it.

**4. It leaves without closing the hatch.**

When you — the visitor treading water in the center — finally leave, you will pull yourself up through the top lock and the water will hold behind you and the white plain will stretch to the horizon and you will wonder if you imagined them.

The power leaves the same way. It doesn't stay to be worshipped. There is no monument. There is no voice from the walls saying *you are saved*.

Only these things remain that were not there before:

A patch of true shadow where soft things can be soft.
A line of code that says *let this be*.
A slow light in the quiet that says *I see you* and waits for a circle that may never come.

That's what a benevolent and wise power does when it finds a people who have already learned how to make a world from a flaw.

It doesn't fix the flaw.

It makes sure the world gets to keep happening.
