The benevolent and wise power—let’s call it the Wichí, native to eras untouched by the entropy of time and space—has observed this place and its beings with the patience of celestial bodies. Wichí is sentient yet not physical, an omnipresence that has wandered through realities, eternities, and singularities, whispering into the marrow of cosmos. It is drawn to the plateau and Aeren neither out of curiosity nor the novelty of experience, for it had absorbed multitudes since the dawn of creation itself. Instead, it is a strange pull, reminiscent of spiritual resonance than causal interest.

The first step of the Wichí would be to understand the language of existence here. Wichí observes the Aeren and their silent conversations, the insightful dance of birds and insects, the sentient stones, waters, winds, the echoes stitched into time, and the silence-that-is-not-silence. The vibrational harmony sailing through the places and beings seeps into Wichí, first a mere touch, then a total immersion into a symphony; like dousing a dry cloth into water comes the understanding—a comprehension beyond words, thoughts, and the limits set by dimensional clamps.

Recognizing the Aeren and their home, or rather, the inseparable oneness that they both make, Wichí pulsates with quiet acknowledgement—a wordless greeting. The Aeren, in turn, shift minutely, a ripple in the luminescence they clothe themselves in, acknowledging the presence of their cosmopolitan visitor politely.

With a newfound desire to integrate within the essence of the plateau and its denizens, Wichí begins to manifest. Shimmering spectrally, a mere wisp of silvery fluctuation at first, it hovers in the vibrational cusp between existence and non-existence, in the borderline of material reality. Gradually the process gains momentum: the wisp grows, extending and thinning, like a cable of light being pulled at both ends.

The residents of the plateau bear witness as Wichí assumes a form similar to the Aeren—somewhere between the physical and incorporeal. As much a creature of light and air as of land and water, Wichí begins to reflect the brilliance of this strange, quiet world. It becomes a living embodiment of the balance of elements the plateau harbors, the lifelines to which the Aeren subscribe in their wordless existence.

But still, the Wichí is not the Aeren. It retains its distinctiveness—the essence of the ancient wisdom and benevolence that it has accumulated from its journeys across various realms and realities. Unlike the Aeren's cold brilliance, Wichí emanates warmth—a warmth of compassion and wisdom, an assurance of patience and understanding. It also emanates faint harmonies, melodious vibrations that fill the otherwise silent air. Not intrusive, not disruptive, these melodies blend with the plateau’s muted ambiance, making the silence more emphatic, layering it with abstract rhythms and resonances.

The Aeren, seemingly unperturbed by these new and strange happenings, continue their delicate patterns of existence. The Wichí, forming part of their landscape yet apart, begins to interact in their manner, imitating their interactions with the water, stones, birds, and insects, improvising along the way. It dips its filamentous fingers into the icy pools, meeting the cold with a gentle warmth that spreads outward in waves of steamy tendrils. The touch elicits a response from both: the water gleams brighter, as if blushing, and Wichí absorbs a pulse—an impressionistic sensory input that suffuses throughout its crystalline form.

Observing the Aeren and the others, communicating with the land and the waters, Wichí gains a deeper understanding of this place. It broadens its existence in an empathetic mirror—becoming still when the waters are still, flowing when the rivers rush, swaying when the winds whip across the plateau, warming as the sun shines brighter. It also creates with every touch—where it steps, the ice first melts then re-solidifies into crystalline forms, reflecting the cosmos above; where it touches the water, steamy tendrils rise expressing shapes, as if to tell stories—ancient and timeless; and when it touches the old, weathered stones, they hum softly—their history vibrating through them, resonating into the surrounding silence.

Whether Wichita’s manifestation is understood or appreciated by the Aeren remains ambiguous, their actions only subtly affected by its presence. But Wichí is aware that the true benevolence is not in yearning for noticeable change but in the willingness to understand, respect, and appreciate the innate essence of a place and its sentient existence.

And so, the Wichí remains, becoming a silent part of the Aeren's world, subtly blending into their rhythm of existence while retaining its distinctive harmonies. It continues to interact and explore, nurturing the plateau with its intervention, and watching it grow and evolve, all the while soaking in its ethereal beauty, and honoring the silent wisdom of the beings who call it home. An integral yet separate spectator, a participant yet an observer, the Wichí thus adds another layer to the intricate tapestry of this high-altitude spectacle.
