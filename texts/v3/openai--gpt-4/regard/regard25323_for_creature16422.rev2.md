And yet, the currents of the cosmos are wide and restless, unconfined by walls of water or steel. Far above the ceaseless depths where the city cradles its secrets, a power observes. It is not the kind born of dominion or fire, nor of flinty-eyed judgment. It is a power suffused with the benevolence of understanding and the wisdom of eternity, a presence that neither intrudes nor devours. It drifts in unseen dimensions, yet its essence bends toward this forgotten metropolis like sunlight drawn through cracks in rock. It knows this place, this people, not by sight nor thought, but through their resonance—a faint, poignant melody straining upward through leagues of liquid silence. 

The power has no form, no singular name. It is simply a will, imbued with a yearning to nurture, to witness, and, where it deems worthy, to ignite new sparks of possibility. Its presence is a great visitor, bearing gentle awe for this enduring sanctuary of the abyss. It hovers unseen above the cathedral-like domes, feeling the hum of their ancient reactor pulse through the black water; it watches the bioluminescent rivers of life coursing through the skeletal beings as they bend to their lives of quiet devotion. 

A question floats, unformed but potent, within the presence. Is interference a gift or robbery? These beings, alien and miraculous, have made a fragile, intricate harmony of this harsh and sunless world. Would the light of the outside destroy their carefully woven darkness? Or would it plant seeds of evolution, a future taller and wilder than anything they could imagine? 

It does not decide at once. The power is patient, contemplative. Its own story has been one of infinite moments, of slow creation and careful inquiry. It rarely acts in haste, and in truth, it prefers to leave most places undisturbed. But the beings below are not like most—they are, in their own way, dazzling. Less for their technology or their biomechanical beauty, but for their endurance, and the grace with which they have become of one piece with their world, flowing where others might only flail. They are creators, sustainers, and dreamers, and thus they belong to the unbroken chain of wonder the power cherishes most.

And so, with care, the presence takes its first silent step into the city. It does not manifest in shapes of divine radiance; it does not scatter the darkness with a sudden spark. Instead, it seeks the spaces between. It peers into the veins of the city’s pulse, feels the vibration of turbines as though running gentle fingers along a harpstring. 

It senses the wear of time upon countless metal joints. It knows the pressure-bearing walls have never ceased straining against their burden, that the great reactor has been beating like an aging heart. Here and there, it spies fatigue in their systems, a slow unraveling that even the diligent stewards fail to fully arrest—for their memories, like their structures, are blunted by age, forgetting the finer patterns of creation.

As gently as a star shedding light upon a lost and sleeping traveler, the power gives a gift. Its touch is a bloom of possibility, a shift in the vibratory harmony of molecules, a whispered instruction woven so delicately into the city’s foundation that it might have always been there. Joints become impervious to decay. Walls remember how to heal themselves, regenerating the way living cells do in an organic body. Circuits whisper secrets of efficiency long forgotten to the caretakers of the metropolis, hints of design principles older than their own understanding. 

Yet the power does not stop with cold mechanics. It sees the beings’ deep partnership with the city as a reflection of their own nature. And so it touches them as well—not with fingers or commands, but with resonance. It lifts the shrouds of surfacing memories, awakening glimpses of the stories their kind once buried in the heaviness of salt and time. Into their quiet songs, the power sinks seeds of luminous knowledge, flashes of what once had been and what still could be—the echo of their ancestors’ steps under broad skies, the shatter of sunlight on the very waves that seal them in, the realization that other worlds could exist beyond the draped infinity of the waters.

Then, it withdraws. This is their city, and its next chapter must be theirs to write. The deep requires a gentler touch than most of creation; it is not a realm for meddlers. Still, as the presence lingers a moment longer, it feels a tectonic shift within the beings. Their flicker-language softens, elongates, and changes rhythm, as though tasting sounds new yet familiar. They gather in their quiet spindly masses at the city’s groaning heart, where first among them they sense the hum of new songs being born. The symbols carved into their biomechanical memories by long defunct ancestors begin to shimmer, like glyphs glowing faintly with warmth.  

For a timeless time, nothing dramatic occurs. But in the patient cycles of their world, small things are enormous. For the first time in eons, the beings begin to ask questions—not those of survival, but of identity. What did they lose in the days of their ancestors? What would it mean to imagine not only who they are but who they should become?

Far above, in a kingdom of air and fire and movement, there is no awareness of the drama unfolding in the depths. But leagues below the surface, something stirs in the rhythm of bioluminescent veins and the slow pulse of illumination in forgotten corners of the abandoned districts. The beings do not yet speak of leaving; perhaps they never will. But they speak, in their fragile, thrumming way, of reaching—not only beyond their city, but into its core, rediscovering what they lost and realizing they may expand upon it.

The power remains far above, a silent witness to the rising tremor of their song. It is a melody not of rescue but renewal, shaped by the stewards of the deep, creators of their own light beneath an unrelenting shadow. It takes no credit, seeks no thanks. This is what it understands: that sometimes the truest gift is not intervention but inspiration, a shimmer of new possibility given to hands—and hearts—willing to shape it. And in that place where silence builds monuments and darkness keeps watch, a holy whisper unfurls, fragile yet unyielding—a current of hope riding the endless tides of the abyss.
