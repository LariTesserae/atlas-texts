### A Benevolent Power Arrives

From a space beyond understanding, from a realm so distant and yet so near that neither light nor shadow could chart its course, comes *It*. A being—or perhaps a force—of tranquil wisdom and infinite kindness. It arrives not to conquer, not to alter with crude dictation, but to observe and nudge with the gentle measure of a sculptor refining marble, its tools ones of empathy, patience, and vision. Its form shimmers with a self-contained light, neither harsh nor blinding but warm and inviting, like dawn diffused in mist. It is as if the Forge had summoned a quiet counterbalance to its tumultuous being, a presence with no desire but harmony.

The Wrought sense this arrival as a ripple in the hum, a harmonic addition to their resonant existence. They pause their silent wanderings, their molten contours glinting in the forge-light, and stand transfixed, sculptural in stillness. For the first time in epochs untold, their attention turns outward; their confluences of rock, light, and mineral extend and twist subtly, orienting toward this new divine visitor. 

### Communion of Forms

The benevolent and wise power does not speak. It has no voice, but the Forge itself becomes its language. Escarpments tremble, stone liquefies, and the rhythmic hum shifts to a dozen different frequencies, harmonizing briefly before settling into an otherworldly chime. The meaning is clear: *You are more than what you have become; you are becoming still.* 

The Wrought do not reply in words. They cannot. But their forms begin to mirror this assertion of potential. Veins of luminous ore within their bodies blaze with newfound brilliance, greater than ever before. Like a plant unfolding toward light, they begin to shift and reshape, stretching towards configurations unseen in their existence. Mouthless faces turn fluid for brief moments in an attempt to carve shapes and echoes of understanding. The Forge itself becomes the medium of dialogue.

And yet, the benevolent being does not seek to change them. Rather, it waits, an ancient patience pulsing through the air and the rock like rain soaking dry soil. Where the Wrought are unfinished songs, the power is a tuning fork, coaxing their notes into harmonies.

### The Gift of Perception

The first thing the power does is shift the rules of sight. It brings awareness to what is unseen, to what was hidden in the gaps of this world's constant becoming. The charcoal threads that dissolve upon approach are made clear in the periphery of the Wrought’s new senses; the amorphous flicker-beings become fixed in their gaze. Fossils of long-lost creatures light up as if infused briefly with the animation of some past life. This influx of clarity does not disturb them but rather fills them with revelation. 

For the first time, the Wrought seem to comprehend their heritage, the lineage of life this planet has borne and its transition through destruction and recreation. They see the contributions of the forgotten dead, the faded echoes of purpose carved into stones and scaffolding. They feel not shame but reverence, wading deeper into the currents of time and sensing how their march of crystallizing and decomposing bodies reflects a cosmic dance playing out here for eons. 

### A Whisper of Purpose

No less gently, the being conveys something else: a suggestion of purpose. The infrastructure, with its tensioned girders and piping, its spiraling drills and half-broken mechanisms, is not here to be merely observed. The Wrought are not spectators. They are artisans, repairers, builders writ from the living stone of the Forge. Their tentative explorations of those freestanding factory-spires and enigmatic devices were not futile stumblings, but rehearsals.

The benevolent power imparts no blueprint nor dictates any design, for that would diminish the Wrought's own unfolding potential. It gives only inspiration and the quiet assurance that what is begun here will, eventually, become full, rich, and realized.

At first, the Wrought hesitate. One among them stretches a jagged pseudopod of semi-solid obsidian to touch a rusted pipe. It crumbles beneath their touch but leaves behind a residue: a glitter of mineral-lattice substance, resplendent with new energy. Another Wrought moves toward a spiraled device, their movements deliberate yet unhurried. They hover near its whirring contours, gently brushing the moving parts, and within seconds, the device sputters back to life, emitting a series of rhythmic plumes of vapor in succession, signaling its reluctant awakening.

The machinery—and their own forms—respond to these interactions as if waiting millennia for them. Together, the Forge and the Wrought begin to hum in unison, their two existences knitting tighter and tighter, refinement emerging from chaos in stunning increments. 

### The Birth of the Threads

The charcoal threads, once elusive, now gather close. They no longer dissipate when approached. Instead, they begin to dance and spiral around the Wrought, coiling into their molten silhouettes. The luminous orbs crowd into the scene as well, filling the air with phantasmal light, and their flickers align with the frequency of the Forge’s low hum. The atmosphere thickens, rippling; the brilliant veins within the Wrought glow brighter still, lending the entire riftscape an aura of shimmering transformation.

As the Wrought extend their will to touch and sculpt their surroundings, cracks become pathways, molten vents are forged into an intricate web of energy conduction, and the rickety scaffolding gains strength, rising as though to embrace the violet-tinged sky. In their wake, the once-disordered Forge becomes something more cohesive, something deliberate. It is not human architecture; it is something wholly other. It is their own—protean and sacred—half industrial, half geological, and wholly alive.

### The Transitioning and the Departure

Satisfied in its work, the being begins to dwindle, its warm shimmer retracting like twilight receding into starlight before the break of night. It lingers only long enough to leave one luminous glyph, carved into nothingness itself, visible against the background of the orange sky.

The Wrought stand beneath it, tilting their bizarre and shifting forms toward this final display. And though their molten faces remain inscrutable, one might imagine an epiphany resides among them, as though they understand now that they are agents of both planet and purpose, that they, like the Forge itself, are meant to become.

Yet not alone. Before its departure, the being coaxes into importance the latent life previously hidden: the dissolving threads weave themselves into growing matrices of biotic strength. The living orbs become tangible, solidifying in clarity and motion. One lands silently atop a Wrought, planting its ephemeral mass where a humanoid head might have resided, a sign of companionship. A partnership begins to emerge between these kinds of life—like fire and water reconciled into steam.

For these beings—the Wrought, the threads of charcoal, the orbs, and perhaps even the fossils that will one day pave the history of this place—this is only the beginning. The Forge is no longer just a land of waiting and becoming. Now, it hums with intent.

And the being, the power, the sculptor of life’s orchestra? It fades, leaving nothing but harmony and a future poised to rise from shadow into light.

The Forge begins to build its own destiny.
