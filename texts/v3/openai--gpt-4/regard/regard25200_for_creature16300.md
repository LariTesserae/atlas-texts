A wise and benevolent power gazes upon Lothria, its essence tethered not to form or matter but to an understanding that bridges things that flourish and things that fade. This power is not one to interfere blindly. It does not arrive with proclamations of dominion nor does it seek to claim, conquer, or obstruct the ethereal tide of Lothria’s existence. Instead, this power listens. It leans into the heartbeat of the place, the symphony of cascading water, the breath of flora, the whispering symmetry of motion and stagnation, creation and decay.

To the *Murmhain*, the power offers nothing but acknowledgment. It perceives them as the faint and flickering melody of Lothria itself, an embodied rhythm of water and light — less beings of substance and more expressions of the land’s memory. It does not attempt to commune with them directly, for what would words mean to such fluid creatures? Instead, the power hums faintly, a sound without sound, a vibration like a feather brushing a harp’s strings. It is a gesture of honor, a signal of... “I see you.” For the *Murmhain*, who have never sought to be seen, it may be the first moment of true reciprocity they have known. 

The *Murmhain's* response, if it could be called such, is not immediate or easy to discern. But in time, their movements shift slightly, their shimmering forms reflecting new hues, as though the water they shepherd now carries hidden veins of amethyst and deep sapphire. The land responds with subtle grace, pools glowing slightly brighter, rills playing treble notes alongside their whispered alto tones. A kind of mutual acknowledgment arises—not intrusive, not commanding, but shared understanding: the power and the *Murmhain* recognize the stories that each carry.

The power turns its attention to the *Glavenhark*. They rise sluggishly and hover in its presence, strange priests of twilight and veiled memory. Here, the power’s heart fills with curiosity more than anything else. Why do the *Glavenhark* linger near the broken remnants of machines, those rusted relics of an unknown civilization? What purpose compels their spectral rites? Leaning inward, the power seeks to offer clarity—not to the *Glavenhark*, but to the ruins themselves.

With meticulous patience, it weaves ephemeral pulses of harmonics into the decayed machinery. Not to revive them, for that would go against Lothria’s ordering. No, the goal is subtler—to lend voice to the silent artifacts. Whispers bloom in the ancient cogs as they begin to share their tales with the *Glavenhark*. A narrative of creation and loss unfolds, fragmented memories deep as the rust cracking through their structures. In these whispers, the *Glavenhark* begin to understand more vividly why they guard these bones of forgotten toil. They are not just keepers; they are kin. The creatures become enraptured by the unspooling story, and their wings ripple more vividly than before, faint light crisscrossing their translucent veins. Their low, rumbling speech becomes ever-so-slightly less sorrowful as the rituals they perform on the relics grow more intricate, each gesture a carved glyph of compassion.

The power does not forget the *Vyrin*. Here in the dimmest folds of the heartland, the guardians of the twilight hollows stand watch. Unlike the shimmering *Murmhain* or the languid *Glavenhark*, the *Vyrin* live at the intersection of violence and protection, standing as immovable warnings to reckless wanderers. Approaching them delicately, the power distills itself into a form as non-threatening as it can manage—perhaps a soft glow edging the crests of their razor-like bark, a slight suggestion of another mind acknowledging their silent duty.

The *Vyrin* remain unyielding at first. Stark. Observing. Then, one among them stirs deep, its amber eyes brightening ever so slightly, becoming less like a miner’s furnace and more molten, fluid. The power speaks to this lone sentinel—not in words, but in memories shared with intention, flashes of the world beyond Lothria’s borders: salt and sand, bustling cities, canopies alive with human chatter. In doing so, it plants not a longing, but awareness. It reminds the *Vyrin* of something crucial—that what they guard is not simply ruin, but a passageway between the rhythms of Lothria and the erratic pulse of the world outside. The sentinels bow gently, like stalwart colossi meeting the gaze of a passing wind, and their sharp bodies seem to soften, curves forming amidst their previously faceted planes. Still protectors, but somehow more at peace.

Yet the land itself—the breathing organism that is Lothria—remains a delicate mystery. Unsure of whether it watches, whether it feels, the power stands silently amidst its veiled shadows and illuminated traces. It resorts to the simplest of gestures: presence. It lets itself dissolve into the eternal symphony of water and moss, weaving itself into the rills, hums, and phosphorescent play of light. It waits not to impose its will, but to ask, *What are you?* For hours, or centuries, it remains like this—until finally, Lothria seems to exhale.

The flowing waters crescendo. The ruins tremble faintly. The foliage quivers under an imagined breath. And the power feels Lothria’s answer return—not in words, not even in visions, but as a profound sensation. Lothria is its *own dream*. It has no origin, no definitive conclusion; it exists because it exists. It is not lonely, yet it welcomes those who honor its rhythm. It saw the rise and fall of settlers who did not care for its nature. It wept their buildings into decay. And it will endure, through eons of slow renewal and fleeting caretakers, echoing a song both heartbreaking and profoundly whole.

Satisfied, the power withdraws, leaving behind gentle ripples of change. The beings of Lothria are different now, if only slightly—more luminous, more intent. To the travelers who may visit someday, they might appear as strange and unknowable as ever. But the *Murmhain*, *Glavenhark*, and *Vyrin* carry beneath their otherworldly forms a deeper resonance. They remember the visitor who did not conquer, who did not change them for its own ends, but who listened and gave them the gift of being truly known.

And Lothria, now faintly humming with the echoes of that benevolent power, tilts ever so slightly toward the great, shimmering expanse of time, singing softly to itself, its rhythm uninterrupted yet subtly enriched.

Thus, the wise power leaves, carrying the taste of Lothria’s eternal symphony within its own boundless essence. It wonders whether it was truly the teacher or the pupil in this exchange. Perhaps it was both. It floats onward, its gaze now turning toward distant realms, but with echoes of water and verdant brilliance lingering in its soul.
