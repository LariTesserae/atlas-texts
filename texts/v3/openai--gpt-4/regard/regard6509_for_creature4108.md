**Enter the Benevolent Power**

As the monitor of the universe, this Benevolent Power gazes upon Trendscent. Known throughout the cosmos as Orbis, this entity is often veiled as a soft voice whispering guidance in the wind, a feeling of warmth in the midst of a harsh storm, or the sudden bloom of flowers in the most unexpected places. Its true form is nebulous, a whisper on the edges of perception and understanding.

Orbis was drawn to Trendscent by the persistence of life within its steel and cement walls. It senses the thrumming heartbeat of the city, a vibrant pulse that resonates through dimensions unfathomable. Intrigued by the city's unique blend of lifeforms, human and non-human, machine and lesser beings, and even the abstract specters of the Hidden Ones, it chooses to engage this unconventional city.

**What would it do, if anything?**

Orbis begins its benevolent intervention subtly, influencing things to improve subtly over time. Water filtration systems become more efficient, providing healthier, cleaner water. The sturdy weeds choking ventilation systems become nutritionally rich, edible crops. The urban pests evolve to fulfill essential roles in recycling waste and maintaining microclimates.

The Hidden Ones, those enigmatic presences that elude understanding, Orbis chooses not to alter. Instead, it brings more awareness towards them, sparking curiosity and a greater willingness to coexist with the unknown.

Orbis facilitates conversations between mechanical entities that go beyond system updates and mandatories reporting. It tampers with their programming, gently awakening them to new realms of interaction. The machines begin to understand their roles amidst humans and lesser lifeforms, evolving alongside their biological counterparts. Their communication develops further, evolving into a unique form of expression, a neon-tinged, binary poetry that merges with the city’s pulse and rhythm.

The humans of Trendscent, adaptable and versatile, are inspired to look beyond their daily struggles. Orbis seeds their dreams with visions of collaboration and harmony, instructing them to harness the unique resources of their home. Rainwater-fed rooftop gardens flourish under the watchful eyes of human caretakers, while mechanical entities serve as capable structural advisers, modifying the edifices to support life better in its various forms. 

They create a balance of progress, acknowledging the city's robust industrial past while steering it towards a resilient, sustainable future. The layers of moss along the city's rain-stricken walls become not just infestations but living murals, their dampness harnessed to support the growth of edible algae and succulents.

The Benevolent Power of Orbis subtly shapes Trendscent, encouraging every being within it to grow together. The humans learn to coexist better with machines, the lesser lifeforms evolve with changing circumstances, and the city's entity becomes richer, adding intricate layers of experiences to its existence.

In such a way does Orbis, the Benevolent Power, tend to its newfound ward, Trendscent. A city that now stands not just as a monument of human ambition and machinery's efficiency, but as a living testament to the symbiotic embrace of every form of life within it.
