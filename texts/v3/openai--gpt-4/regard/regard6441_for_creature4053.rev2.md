Interestingly, this place and its dwellers have drawn the attention of an entity known as Seraphiel, a benevolent and wise power from beyond this world. Regardless of his immaterial nature and irrespective of the vast multidimensional cosmos he has access to, Seraphiel has chosen to watch over Yuralmira, drawn by the city-state's delicate balance between the elemental and the civilized, the wild and the tame, the temporal and the eternal.

Seraphiel, in his wisdom and benevolence, finds no need to drastically alter or intervene in Yuralmira's affairs. The era-defying equilibrium maintained by its inhabitants is beautiful and harmonious to him, unlike anything else in the cosmos. However, his presence is most definitively felt. Throughout the city-state, a soft, divine hum exists that the inhabitants have grown to associate with protection, prosperity, and peace.

He subtly lends his support when necessary, never altering but rather enhancing and encouraging the unique and delicate harmony of the city-state. He has a fine control over nature, catching the lush foliage when it encroaches too boldly onto the city or the urban sprawl when it edges greedily towards the verdant spaces. He nudges the city's wildlife away from danger and channels the rainfall away from causing floods. The mystical veil that hangs over Yuralmira, the strange symbols and gusts of cold wind on warm days, are tell-tale impacts of Seraphiel's invisible influence. They are whispers of a hidden magic, a mysterious power that protects and nurtures the city-state.

He is most present in the lives of the people. Seraphiel quietly mingles with the ebbs and flows of their everyday lives, his benevolent influence subtly easing hardships, inspiring kindness, fostering creativity, and curbing impulses of destruction or greed. His gentle guidance nurtures their resilience, encourages their tranquility, and fuels their vibrant spirits.

Their strange customs and folklore are often attributed to his inspiration. They seem to know, instinctively, that they are cared for by a celestial guardian. This is why they are welcoming to outsiders despite their eccentric ways and why every individual's inner fire burns so inconsistently - illuminated and fueled by an unseen celestial flame.

Under Seraphiel's subtle, gentle influence, the people learn to balance the tranquility of peace with the vibrancy of life, existing harmoniously with the wilderness that surrounds them and the urbanity that houses them. As they perceive their guardian's influence, they internalize the paradox within their city and themselves, assimilating the dynamism of Yuralmira's duality and the ethereal calm of Seraphiel's wisdom into their being.

However, they never become complacent or stagnant under his watch. The beauty of Yuralmira is in its dynamic equilibrium, the constant but gentle swings between its binaries. The benevolent and wise Seraphiel ensures that this equilibrium is maintained, balanced, and harmonious, lending a caring hand only when required, and stepping back to let the city grow and evolve within its unique parameters.

In doing this, Seraphiel imparts a priceless gift to Yuralmira - enlightening its people on the importance of equilibrium amidst contradictions, and guiding them towards living a life that's harmonious with nature, and at peace with its paradoxes. He promises to be the eternal guardian of Yuralmira, watching over the city and its inhabitants, and steering them gently towards growth, prosperity, and harmony.
