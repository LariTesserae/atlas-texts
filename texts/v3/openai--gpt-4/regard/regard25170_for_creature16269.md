The benevolent and wise power that had been observing these phenomenons was a being previously only known in whispers and fables, a being that transcended dimensions and time, and possessed a deep understanding of the cosmos. Often referred to in the ancient sacred texts as the Celestial Architect, it shaped suns and planets, constellations and galaxies, placing cosmic harmony and balance at the core of its creation. Those few who knew of its existence revered it as a supreme deity, a sovereign of the firmament. 

The Celestial Architect, in its endless journey through the cosmos, had found itself drawn to this place and its inhabitant, the Fenghosts. Processing information at an unfathomable speed, it observed the world through abstract dimensional planes, its perception transcending basic mortal sensorial experiences. It perceived frequency and vibrations, energy fields and patterns, interpreting the Fenghosts' cryptic language, their unique relationship with the crystalline environment, and their slow, deliberate purpose that unfolded like a temporal fractal. Their serenity, tranquility, and harmony mirrored its own values of cosmic balance and elegance. 

Seeing potential in them, recognizing their unique symbiosis with the environment, the Celestial Architect decided to become a gentle benefactor to the Fenghosts, further enhancing their understanding of the natural elements and shaping powers they possessed. How could it not offer guidance to beings toiling in quiet elegance, intuitively striving to replicate the Architect's own grand design?

Harnessing the cosmic lattices, the Architect began altering the spectral frequencies of the world, reshaping the cold illumination that bathed it. Whereas the glow had been constant before, now it started to pulse rhythmically, imitating the heartbeat of a star. With every pulse, the Fenghosts could absorb more light, gain more energy, continue their endeavors undeterred by their geologically slow pace. Reflections danced all around, the kaleidoscopic scenery became more vivid, fluctuating between spectral extremes, creating a mesmerizing spectacle that reflected off the fractal patterns.

Simultaneously, the Architect tinkered with the density of the world, the very air that pressed on the quartz-like structures. Though initially a harsh manipulation, the new atmospheric pressure allowed the water droplets to now carry more nutrients. The pools feeding into deeper crevices were transformed into life-nourishing wellsprings, supplementing 'quench' for the Fenghosts, helping improve the health of their crystallization activities. 

Thirdly, the Architect determined their communication to be elevated. Therefore, it resonated with the crystalline structures, enhancing their refractive and reflective properties, allowing them to become conduits for the Fenghosts' low-frequency language, strengthening their 'tonal whispers' into 'harmonic orations', providing them with a diverse range of resonance patterns and frequencies which they could further utilize to create intricate architectures at a simultaneously faster yet elegant pace.

Lastly, the Celestial Architect favored the Fenghosts a final gift — the essence of its wisdom. It taught them the laws of the cosmos, the patterns in the chaos, the balance in the cataclysm; it introduced them to the perception of dimensions beyond their current reach. Though the process was slow, even for the Architect, it provided an eternity of knowledge to the Fenghosts, to further finesse their architectural endeavors, refine their world, creating a place of intricate stillness that would be a testament to their quiet persistence. 

In their newly acquired wisdom, in their enhanced abilities, the Fenghosts flourished in excitement and newfound potential. And as their world glowed brighter, as new crystalline structures rose magnificently against the fluorescent skyline, the Celestial Architect observed the evolution with a quiet satisfaction, silently acknowledging that the Fenghosts, the radiant beings of crystalline light and stillness, were its own cosmic legacy in the making. 

The Celestial Architect disappeared into the cosmic fabric, leaving behind a world that held so much more potential than before and a legacy destined to echo through the cosmos. The Fenghosts continued constructing their radiant world, testament now, not only to an intelligent design, a calculated existence, but also to a lesson learned in cosmic wisdom. Their work now seemed imbued by a greater purpose, their creations holding a far greater degree of resonance with the cosmos.

Just so, as the glacial increments of their progress continued, a single, more refined, enlightened question hung in the dense, nutrient-rich, glowing atmosphere of their home: what more can they become, and what greater things can they build?
