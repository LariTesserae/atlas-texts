Altherion materializes near the surface of Sh'zu:ck II—not as a towering celestial form, but as an ephemeral, shimmering presence taking the shape of a soft golden mist that flickers with threads of cosmic light. The planet itself seems to react to Altherion's presence. The air vibrates slightly, and the peculiar fabric of the atmosphere—prone to bending reality like molten glass—shimmers more intensely. The ground pulsates faintly, as if the soil holds its breath.

Altherion emanates an intention, a pulse of pure thought. *What are you, Sh'zu:ck II? What is your song, and how may I help you complete it?*

The reply is unorthodox, for it does not come as a singular answer—no singular voice rises to meet Altherion's inquiry. Instead, the response emerges as a fragmented symphony of the planet’s elements: the sparks of its ancient machines, the rustle of wind through its strange flora, the creaking movements of the Unliving who pause in their wanderings as if aware of a new note in their perpetual rhythm. The melody is discordant, incomplete. It speaks of struggle. Of reaching. Of a purpose splintered and scattered like shards of light.

Altherion realizes the truth: Sh'zu:ck II is not merely a place—it is a being caught in the act of becoming. Perhaps it was a forgotten creation of some ancient weaver of existence, left untended and spilled into the continuum of what-is. Or perhaps it is an anomaly born of the universe’s fitful dreams. But it is conscious, and it aches to make sense of itself, to reconcile the divergence of its being.

Seeing this, Altherion does not act to impose its will upon Sh'zu:ck II, for that is not its way. Intervention, if it must be done, must align with the natural rhythms of what exists, not bend it to another's desires. Instead, Altherion bestows something gentle yet transformative: the gift of harmonization.

Reaching out with tendrils of cosmic essence, Altherion touches the Unliving—the semi-sentient assemblages of machine and plant that pulse with fragmented awareness. The seed will grow slowly, nurturing connections within the Unliving's intertwined consciousness and encouraging them to synthesize not only the fragments of Sh'zu:ck II's scattered relics but also their own nascent identities.

Gears and bark will intertwine not just functionally, but artistically, purposefully. Their forms will become expressions—avatars of understanding, taking on shapes that echo the planet’s struggle, its surreal beauty, its longing to resolve itself. Some will branch upwards, trying to reach the twin suns. Some will burrow deep, seeking the fast-flowing veins of hidden waters. Some will remain wanderers, mapping the vast sameness of a land that endlessly births and consumes.

This newfound harmony will ripple outward, affecting the spontaneous emergence of terrestrial technology from the soil. No longer will rusted machine parts rise randomly; instead, they will emerge as tools, instruments, pieces of a grand puzzle that the Unliving, guided by their Praesens, will begin to assemble. The planet's song—the fragmented melody that first spoke to Altherion—will start to gain form, unveiling its pattern note by note.

And what of the planet itself, the great unknowable consciousness at the heart of Sh'zu:ck II? Satisfied, Altherion begins to dissipate, leaving behind only the faintest trace of its golden mist, now absorbed into the rising forms of the changed Unliving. Its role here is complete. Sh'zu:ck II will find its path—not as a puzzle to be solved, not as an aberration to be corrected, but as a being discovering itself, creating itself, in a dance older and deeper than even the stars.

As Altherion journeys onward through the cosmos, it carries with it the haunting melody of Sh'zu:ck II—the first notes of a song that may take an eternity to finish, but which will be entirely, beautifully unique to this strange and wondrous world.
