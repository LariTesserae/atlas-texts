A wise and benevolent power turns its attention toward the Wandering Vista, a being or force observing the surreal, uproarious beauty of this impossible world. This power is ancient, the essence of cosmic understanding, one that intervenes not out of pride or conquest but out of love for creation, a desire to gently allow beings and places to become what they are meant to be—or to be left untouched, if such is their destiny. It watches silently, suspended both inside and outside the dimension of the Vista, allowing its timeless consciousness to unravel the threads of the place.

The Wanderers—those amorphous, luminescent creatures—exist in ceaseless symbiosis with their terrain, their essence fluctuating with the breath of the biosphere, lives so mutable and in harmony that they verge on becoming indistinguishable from the moist air, the phosphorescent plants, the rustling gravity-defying fungi.

But the wise power feels not pity, nor judgment, nor a desire to control. What it feels, what it hears from the harmony of sound, sight, and motion, is simply this: this is a song yearning to be understood more deeply. The biological cacophony and chaos speak truth, but there is a yearning—a faint, unspoken plea resonating in the subharmonics of the fungi's creaking, the whispers of shifting mists, the kaleidoscopic creatures' rhythmic vibrations. It is a plea on the edges of perception, something quiet even in the midst of clamorous vitality.  

The wise power listens, for its nature is to listen.

And then, in its infinitesimal wisdom, it acts—not as king, not as savior, but as guide. In its vastness, it concentrates an infinitesimal presence into the center of the Vista, a single pinpoint of iridescent white light that refracts into colors unknown to mortal senses. This manifestation is not obtrusive. It makes no commands, gives no reasons. It simply hums its presence, allowing whoever or whatever wishes to approach it to do so on their own will.

Initially, the kaleidoscopic beings of the Vista approach hesitantly. Their fluid forms quiver with curiosity and uncertainty, shifting and rippling like reflections in broken water. But as the gentle emanation of the wise power touches them, it happens not as a force acting upon them but as a resonance, an accord—a harmonious vibration answering the vibrations of their being. They grow still, in a sense beyond the physical. Their body-lights steady, pulsating in alignment with the power’s rhythm. 

In this mutual recognition, memories awaken. The wisdom reminds the creatures not of what they lack but of what they already contain, latent within their vibrating, protean selves. It is as if the cosmos whispers an ancient secret wrapped in kindness: they are not bound by the borders of their environment; they are the environment. Each of them contains the song of the fungi, the pulse of the mists, the roots of the spiraling vines. They are, each and all, microcosms of this symphonic dreamscape, fragments of its infinite self-awareness.

Some pause, their translucent forms fractalizing as if absorbing this revelation; others weave closer to the light, spinning around it in arcs of bioluminescent trails. The light does not change anything tangible, but for the first time, the relentless cacophony—all the thriving, groaning, echoing chaos of the Vista—seems to shift. From noise emerges clarity, a kind of rhythm, as though the unseen gardener who once planted this cosmic Eden has now resumed gently pruning the garden not with tools but with song.

The terrain mirrors this subtle harmony. The mists grow less obscuring, their eddies weaving through the fungal towers in slow spirals rather than erratic clouds. The rivulets of water flow with new purpose, as though directed by unseen boundaries that respect both the stability and motion of the world. Shapes blur less frequently; flora and fauna evolve toward a form of interplay where differences can be glimpsed without losing the profound unity of all things.

And when the wise power recedes—its work complete, its glowing trace fading slowly into the resonant core of the Vista—it leaves behind a new hum to this world. A kind of memory etched into the song of its life, an awareness that the deep yearning at the edges of sound has been heard.

For the inhabitants of the Wandering Vista, nothing fundamental changes—yet everything is transformed. They continue in their symbiotic dance with the world, dreaming and melting and thriving into its rhythms. But their eyes—or what can be called such—now glance inward as well as out. They vibrate not in aimless harmony but awake to the knowledge that their flexibility, their chaotic beauty, is not a lack of self but an abundance, a connection to the infinite. 

What the wise power leaves behind is simply this: a memory of balance that ripples outward infinitely. Perhaps the beings will harness that resonance in their luminescent calls, lending new dimensions of wisdom to their communication. Perhaps they will forget it in time, allowing it to decay naturally as all things do. But for as long as it lingers, the Wandering Vista will carry forward in its alien way—still strange, still improbable, but with the delicate threads of its paradoxical song woven tighter, shining with moments of ephemeral grace.

And the wise power, far away yet forever near, continues onward, spreading not correction but reflection, not enforcement but illumination, wherever its endless heart is drawn.
