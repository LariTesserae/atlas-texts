### The Power: The Eternal Gardener

Above all of this, observing from an unseen plane is a being of immeasurable wisdom and benevolence—The Eternal Gardener. This rare entity surveys the life below in all its beauty and in its constant state of change and renewal. The Gardener is invested deeply in the preservation of this delicate symbiosis, and in its way, it cherishes Thallomere and its inhabitants with a shepherd’s care for its flock. 

Unlike most powers that govern conventional cities, The Eternal Gardener does not interfere with the intricate cycle of life directly. It respects the natural rhythm and harmony of Thallomere, the coexistence of a wide spectrum of life forms. Rather, its influence falls subtly, guiding the flow of life on quieter paths, occasionally lending a strengthening hand to the root that struggles to take hold, or pulling back the weight of the growing branch that may bring harm to the structure below.

The Gardener also looks upon the Thallo, the Keepers of Thallomere, with interest and affection. Their sensitivity to nature, their balance with the ecosystem, their understanding of the constant flux of life are qualities that resonate with the Gardener's own wisdom. Its essential energies occasionally touch the dreams of the Singers and the Builders, planting seeds of inspiration, allowing them to find new harmonies or techniques of guiding and adapting to their living city.

From time to time, The Gardener sends palpable ripples of energy through the Verdant Vein, tuning the city and its citizens towards harmony. These pulses, while having a soothing and restorative effect, also serve as quiet communication between The Gardener and the Thallomere life-web, ensuring the balance remains steady, or warning if it begins to sway. 

As the unseen power, The Eternal Gardener ensures the system's harmony with its ever-watchful influence. It respects the natural cycles of life but is poised to guide and protect it if required subtly. Its existence is a comforting presence, a whisper of a wisdom that transcends the ever-evolving life of Thallomere, echoing with the rustling leaves and the heart's beats beneath the living city.

In the grand scheme of existence, the connection between Thallomere and The Eternal Gardener is a beautiful reminder of the reassurance that comes with a watchful, caring presence. A presence that allows life to unfold naturally, casting subtly guiding shadows where necessary, ensuring the city and its citizenry—songbirds, beetles, Rootleths, Thallo, and all—continue their dance of coexistence in this ever-changing, living city. The Eternal Gardener, in all of its wisdom, patience and nurturing affection, sees Thallomere not just as a city, but as a testament to the enchanting tapestry of life itself.
