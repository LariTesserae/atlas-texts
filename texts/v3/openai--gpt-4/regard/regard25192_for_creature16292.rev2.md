Once this haven of tranquility and flourishing life came under the gentle scrutiny of a benevolent and wise power, an entity bound by time and vast cosmic knowledge, intrigued by the unimaginable beauty of Deruwa. 

The power watched the dance of life unfolding in Deruwa from afar, witnessing the slow but steady evolution of its varied inhabitants. Its patient gaze observed the tiny organisms, microscopic bacteria, and fungi ungulate in tandem beneath the soil, maintaining the vibrant palette of life above. It sensed the rhythmic march of the termites, the symphony of the bees, and quiet, tenacious labors of other numerous insects that underpinned life's continuous circle in this sacred place.

It admired the simple harmonies of the birds, swooping and chirping among the trees, their songs a testament to the beauty of existence. It sensed the humble warmth in their nests filled with hatching eggs and growing chicks, a microcosm within the macrocosm that added to the immense tapestry of life.

From its astral heights, the power studied the larger beings too. The species of small mammals that added their unique strands to the complex weave of the ecosystem. It sensed their natural rhythms aligning with day and night, and how each one played its individual part in maintaining balance within this utopia.

The power did not interrupt, not interfere, but just observed, appreciated, and felt increased love for this slice of the universe where life's magic wove its serenely intricate patterns, unimpaired by the aspirations and creations of conscious beings from the universe's other corners. The power cherished this primordial concert of existence; it relished in the feel-good silence of Deruwa that echoed the vibes of innocence, of indigenous, natural growth.

Having watched and cherished the paradise that was Deruwa, filled with admiration for its intricate dance of life, the power decided to make its presence felt subtly. Not in a grand or sweeping gesture but through the smallest acts of kindness and wisdom.

As the dawn broke over the horizon, and the first rays of the sun filtered through the leafy awnings of the trees, the power painted the skies with vibrant hues, portraying the artistry of the cosmos and revealing its presence gently. It summoned the winds, which sang the songs of the universe, whispering through the leaves and grasses about the constellation dances and galactic ballets beyond their world.

The power touched the flame that housed the fauna of Deruwa, infusing them with wisdom. It exchanged knowledge with the birds, teaching them new tunes that mimicked the harmonious vibrato of the universe. It hinted to the bees the locations of unexplored flora bursting with pollen and nectar, opening up new fronts in their daily adventures.

It subtly tweaked the senses of small mammals, making them more tuned to their environment, responsive, and undeterred in their quest for survival. The termites received designs of stronger fortresses and the spiders, patterns of more resilient webs.

On occasions when the rains from the heavens were too severe, it would shroud the paradise beneath a cosmic umbrella, ensuring the torrents would not disrupt the lives in Deruwa. It subtly nurtured the growth of new foliage, opening spaces for the life that was yet unborn, maintaining the balance of nature.

The benevolent power was careful to respect the place’s rhythm and routine, the cycles of constant growth, decomposition, and rebirth; the hues of existence and life’s nuances. It acknowledged that everything about Deruwa was extraordinary. Alongside maintaining, the power also gave - wisdom, aid, shelter, and growth subtly, ensuring that its essence remained intact.

Its mystical presence danced around the edges of Deruwa, a silent guardian watching over with peace and wisdom. Yet, retaining its humility, it never took credit for the wisdom it imparted or the goodness it bestowed. In doing so, the power found joy and purpose in its silent vigil over the flourishing haven, embracing a sacred promise that Deruwa would be forever preserved, guarded and subtly nurtured.

Thus, in its hushed harmony with Deruwa, the benevolent power illustrated a beautiful story of cosmic love and guardianship to anyone who had the eyes to see, and the heart to feel. It was a story as old as time, a story of guardianship and wisdom, which echoed in the whispers within the winds, sang in the rustling leaves, and danced in the vibrant hues of the sky. If one listened closely, one would know that this was the song of life, ever-evolving and eternally beautiful, under the gentle care of a benevolent cosmic power.
