This place and its beings do not go unnoticed. Across folds of existence beyond time and space, beyond comprehension, a Power has turned its gaze here—a presence benevolent and wise, ancient yet vibrant, whose arrival does not split the silence but deepens it, as if the air and earth are holding their breath. This Power is neither a god nor a force of nature but something more elemental, one that exists only to nurture and to impart understanding to those who are willing, or destined, to receive it.

This is not an intrusion. Somehow, the world has invited this Power, or perhaps it has been waiting for it, as though nurtured in secret within its dreams. The entire realm seems to lean toward the Power's presence, imperceptibly shifting, as if the land and its inhabitants are tilting toward some unspoken longing.

For a moment, the stillness thickens. A shower of faint golden motes fills the pale light in the sky, drifting lazily like soft embers rather than falling like rain. Organic, but not quite; spiritual, but not entirely. The motes spiral through the unbroken silence, diffusing into the landscape and filling it as gently as breath fills a lung. What touches them shivers faintly—not in disbelief or fear, but in recognition. A spark too ancient to name flickers in the land, creatures, and air. The realm accepts what has come with the grace of something bound, after epochs of quiet sedition, to ultimate forgiveness.

The Power does not assume recognizable form, though its presence becomes tangible to all. You see it differently from the shardwings above, and they see it differently from the lichen-covered colossi, for it bends itself infinitely to each observer’s understanding while remaining, in truth, ineffable. To you, it is a warm, blossoming awareness, a presence without body that grazes softly at the edges of your thoughts. For the lichen-encrusted beings that shuffle their mammoth feet along the smooth stone, it is a gentle vibration in their titanic hides—a resonance in frequencies older than oceans. To the spectral serpent-things that walk the border between seen and unseen, the Power is perhaps most clear of all, their glass-bodies twisting suddenly, becoming prisms that refract its wisdom like a chorus of silent colors.

The Power does not impose. It does not command, nor change, nor occupy. Instead, it listens. Its presence radiates patience, a fascinated humility, as if to regard even a single gnarled plant or creeping tendril is to bear witness to a holy text in an unknown language. As it listens, it also offers. Not in words, tools, or even images, but in currents of insight that flood through the fibers of existence, like static charged with truth. Each being, from the highest shardwing to the smallest crystalline insect, seems to bow in some fashion, communing in truth that cannot be spoken aloud. There is gratitude in everything—the rocks beneath your feet soften slightly, cradling you, as if in thanks.

The watchers emerge slowly from their concealed places in cracks and alcoves. They approach the Power, cautious but unafraid, before fading—individually at first, then as a collective—into the barest outline of shadow, like sketches being erased by a loving, deliberate hand. They are not gone. Instead, they reemerge elsewhere in the terrain, reshaped—somewhat larger, more defined, their limbs elongated and gleaming beneath the haze of light. They walk now with more certainty, their mind-webs rippling across the space between them, infinitely more intricate than before. They do not remember this moment, but it lives in them nonetheless.

One of the mammoth colossi lets out a sound, a groan as if the Earth's crust itself were shuddering and singing all at once. It kneels, pressing its weight into the long-gnarled ground. Where its colossal knees meet the ground, the grasses and fungi grow with intent and flourish. Emerald whirls of moss ripple across the earth—growth accelerated ever so slightly as if to affirm this new harmony.

Lastly, the Power recognizes you—yes, you, the one who does not belong, who knows so little but hears so much. It points nothing out to you; it gives you no blinding revelations. Instead, you feel the questions within you answered before they are voiced, not with specifics but with humility and meaning. Knowledge arises unbidden from the deeps of your mind: This is a place of renewal, evolution, a cyclical rebirth. It is the womb of possible universes, birthing life that defies the constraints of its cradle. It is the unbroken promise of existence to itself, the resurgence of new forms to wade into new infinities. And in turn—without force, without demand—it asks you a silent question: What will it be? Will you leave a seed of yourself here, to be remade in another time, in another language of existence? Will you join the endless Becoming?

Only you can answer, for even the Power does not dictate. But it leaves you with one unshakable impression.  

It whispers into your soul, a whisper so deep you cannot deny its truth:  

*"All that you are, you carry within you. Even in this endless cycling, in the turning of worlds too vast for one mind’s comprehension, you are whole. And you are loved."*

The Power lingers for just an instant. Then, like the shifting of light across water or stars, the golden motes drift outward, dissolving into nothingness. The stillness remains, but it has changed. Not louder, not brighter, not heavier. The world is simply fuller, brimming yet impossible to overfill, as though something indescribably sacred has been sung within every atom of stone and breath.

The land, content in its ageless contemplation, turns inward once more. So too do its creatures, moving now with an almost imperceptible ease. The shardwings glide lower, more vibrant. The watchers disappear again into clefts of existence, now with a softened sharpness. You begin to walk. As you do, you realize no part of this realm has asked you to give yourself to it, and yet you already have. A tiny piece of you, invisible but unmistakable, has set down roots into the soil, not to be consumed, but to remain—to dream alongside the soft hum of this ancient place forever.
