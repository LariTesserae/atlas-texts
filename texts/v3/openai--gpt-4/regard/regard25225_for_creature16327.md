And yet, even the most finely-tuned symphonies can draw the attention of ears far beyond their immediate realm. Perhaps it is the hum of the Overseer that stirs the winds of another power. Or perhaps it is the unbearable beauty of the fluxlings, the tiniest pinprick of their auroral glow, which flickers faintly across the vast fabric of existence like a beacon. Whatever the reason, a power notices. A benevolent and wise power.

This power isn’t drawn for reasons of conquest or curiosity. It is not the hand of an architect yearning to impose order upon something it deems chaotic, nor a seeker of secrets hoping to exploit what it finds. No, this is a power that listens like the careful lover of a whispering stream, one who folds their stature and magnitude inward until they can touch the smallest ripple of beauty without ever disturbing it. It approaches not as a towering presence but as a quiet accord, slipped unnoticed into the interwoven strands of this ecosystem.

The power moves not with force but with intention, seeping into the liminal spaces between the glimmers and the weavers, between the moss-draped vines and the vibrations of humming, unseen cogs. It becomes a question embedded into the soft weave of the place itself: What does this world desire?

---

At first, there is no answer. How could a chorus respond when it is already endlessly flowing, its song all inhale and exhale, no note higher than another? And yet, perhaps because the power’s question is unspoken yet understood, the very fabric of the place begins to ripple as if stirred by a passing breeze. The fluxlings quiver faintly, the scent of moss and damp soil deepens to something richer, more earthen. There is an undeniable shift, no more violent than the blooming of a wildflower in the forest of dawnlight, and no less profound for its gentleness.

The Overseer’s hum grows faintly more deliberate, sharper like a harpstring plucked in perfect resonance. Then it grows softer again. A message encoded in the harmonic textures: the place speaks through its emissaries.

The weavers begin to work. Their bodies, already an exquisite interplay of vine and wire, start gathering new tasks. They weave threads not from decay, as their habit had long been, but from the motion that caresses them—from the pollen in the stilled, moist air, from the golden fractal echoes of fluxling trails, from the creak of a distant colossus rearranging its dreaming limbs. Tethered to something unseen yet comforting, the threads coalesce into symbols textured against the dreamlike earth.

The power sees—and feels—the answer in this silent embroidery. The message unfolds not as language but as an ache, a knowing breath of desire born from a place without hands to make its wants known. This place wants to grow. Not outward, not in the hungry expansion of a cancer devouring its host, but inward. It yearns for richer chords, for deeper exchanges, for new thresholds—just as the single stalk of a vine thirsts to form an entire lattice. It does not need salvation or intervention, for it is whole in its being. But it asks, gently but insistently, for possibility.

The power responds.

---

It does not bring gifts wrapped in disruption, force, or change that would violate the perfect, mutable symmetry of the garden. Instead, the gift it grants is one of potentiality. The power embeds itself, briefly, in the soil—letting strands of its intention bleed into the damp emerald glow, embracing the moisture-laden air as both medium and canvas.

First, the glimmers. The power breathes itself into their auroral trails, shifting their motion in ways almost imperceptible but infinitely meaningful. A new kind of dance emerges, weaving symbols brighter than any human constellation within the uppermost canopy. The glimmers shape small networks, maps so delicate even the fluxlings pause their wandering to observe. When the weavers climb to touch these points of light, conduction flows between them—and a new awareness hums through every firmament of the ecosystem.

Second, the colossi. Slumbering as ever, these titanic beings shift deeply between breaths as the gift of the power enters their dreaming cores. Tiny rivulets and sap-veins carved in roots and metal swell faintly, allowing them to dream not only their internal pulse, but the external one—the rhythm of the plants, the fluxlings' auroral traverse, the hidden hum of machinery interlocking for millennia. They are imbued with the ability to understand themselves both as guardians and as dreamers in tandem with their smaller counterparts. Their slumber becomes lighter, as if the line between dormancy and awareness softens.

Finally, the fluxlings. To them, the power grants weight. Not heaviness, not loss of their mercurial, transient glow, but the faintest capacity for self-recognition, the ability to anchor themselves somewhere within the eternal flux. As they alchemize their conversions, turning dew into shimmer and light into sustenance, they gain a strange understanding: they are not merely beings shaped by the song—they are also its singers. And thus, when they release their auroral threads and chirping echoes into the equilibrium, it is now colored by intention. They weave new possibilities not because they were instructed to, but because something deep within them says: *What comes next? What else can we become together?*

---

None of this disturbs the garden’s equilibrium. It hums undisturbed, alive yet far more expansive than it was a moment ago. Faint pathways of interconnection bloom, unintrusive, faint, subtle—pushing outward only until the last acceptable threshold before dissolving again into the air.

The power lingers only long enough to sense what it already knows. This gift will neither overwrite nor dismantle. It does not impose design, even as it plants its seeds. It only allows for infinite futures, for the sharper flowering of the ecosystem's inherent essence. It only allows it to dream more deeply.

And with that, the power folds itself back into silence, its work effortlessly complete. The song of this place continues as it always has—eternal, unbroken. But now, beneath its ancient, steady melody, is just the faintest strum of potential. A note waiting to bloom into ten thousand new symphonies.
