The *Heart* felt it first.

For an eternity, the Heart had thrummed its patient rhythm, oblivious to the passage of time, its purpose long decayed into the sediment of forgotten dreams. But now, the muted currents around it began to shift, a dance of unseen fingers weaving faint imprints into the fabric of the metropolis' existence. The Heart's pulses became slightly less flat, vibrating with tiny variances, echoes of a presence too immense for it to fully comprehend.

To the *custodians*, these changes whispered a new litany—a shift in their ancient patterns. Their movements grew less automatic; they paused at crumbling junctions and broken machinery with a kind of hesitation, as if listening to an unfamiliar, ancestral song. This new litany infused their every act with subtle grace, a directive not born of sterility but of a curious vitality they had almost forgotten. There was a sense of care about their repairs now, as though they weren’t just maintaining an endless machine but tending to something alive, something sacred.

The *watchers*, delicate nerves of bioluminescent instinct, flared brighter in pulses of aquamarine and amethyst, their signals darting with a newfound coherence. The Mender’s influence seemed to magnify their capacities, sharpening their awareness, as though unseen hands had polished the prisms in their translucent cores. Where once scattered flashes lit up the dark intermittently, now entire networks of them swept arcs of code-light across the centralized nervous maps hidden within the city itself.

Something even stranger began to happen to the *drifters*. These amorphous beings, who had roamed aimlessly through the underwater necropolis, became bold, refining their movements. They sought out damaged sections of pipe or fissures in structural walls and began to settle onto them, transforming themselves into living sealants, their glowing veins solidifying into a substance pliable and adhesive. They merged into the city’s metal and stone, creating hybridized extensions that pulsed faintly with compatible energies.

The residents of this underwater world found themselves caught in an unfamiliar and profound shift. Distinctions blurred as metal grew mosslike, and biology pulsed more machine.
