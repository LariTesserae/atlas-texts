And then, the benevolent and wise power comes.

Not with fanfare, not with the cacophony of forces cleaving apart the mists or with light splitting the gray-lit sky. It arrives more subtly, as if it has always been here, patiently crouched in the corners of the forgotten, waiting for the echo of a thought soft enough to notice it. This power is not bound by the forces that govern flesh or stone, nor the stubborn tides of cause and consequence. Its form is…formless, and its shape is nothing you can see—only feel. Because *feeling* is how it moves in this world. It is not light, but the play of shadows that highlights detail; not sound, but the resonance that lingers just after, the deep hum of universal memory not yet turned to silence. Its presence does not disrupt the fabric of this fractured world—rather, it settles within it, as though the space was designed to cradle its infinity.

When it turns, lazily and all at once, toward this highland realm, its awareness brushes across everything. It moves in a soft, delicate arc, like a fingertip grazing a lake’s surface—not to stir it, but to feel its stillness ripple under its touch. And ripple it does. The mists thicken momentarily, trembling into delicate and fleeting fractals before dispersing again. The chimes rise in pitch, warm and curious, as if the foundational song of this land can’t help but answer when noticed. The petrified hollows groan faintly; the lush forest exhales in time. There is no fear amongst the luminous beings or the unseen creatures at the fringes. There cannot be fear, for the power does not come with malice or authority or even judgment. It is Watcher, Listener, Rememberer. 

It could remain passive, just observing this intricate knot of ancientness and new life, but something stirs deep within its infinitely branching awareness—an intuition far beyond thought. This strange highland place, this tangle of leaf and steel, stone and sound, is asking for something. Perhaps it does not know how to voice its need, or perhaps it has been doing so endlessly, through the echoes that ascend the aqueducts, the roots that aimlessly bore into crumbling concrete, the fragile settlements peeking out beneath layers of undergrowth. The land itself aches, tangled in a stasis it may or may not have chosen. It exists, wondrous and terrifying as it is, but it does not move forward. It sits at the crossroads of a question as vast as the stars overhead.

The power kneels—not physically, for it cannot kneel, but in its presence, a palpable lowering occurs, a humbling of its grand, incomprehensible scope. Visceral acknowledgment vibrates outward like a silent choir harmonizing on the wind. **I see you. Not just the green, not merely the gray, not only the worn industry, but the *whole* of you—your longing, your scars, your confusion, your beauty. I see you.**

It turns toward the luminous beings next, not with words but with communion. *Are you guardians? Inventors? Exiles who do not remember the home that cast you out? Are you the tendrils of this land, forged from its longing?* The beings do not answer aloud, but in that soft, shifting hum that has always lurked beneath things. Their song bends, subtly, intertwining with the presence of this power. Their green-gold eyes flicker with glimmers of data, of memory held close somewhere deep, almost forgotten. 

The power listens intently and asks nothing else. For it understands. These are beings of transition, communities of what-was and what-might-be—the living echoes of forgotten choices. They are not lost. They are *becoming*. And yet they too have waited, for uncounted spans of time that are no time at all. For what, they do not know. Whatever dream their ancestors or architects envisioned, the threads of it seem only half-stitched. Their tools hum their odd, phosphorescent rhythm, their grasping roots shape and reshape—but to what end, even *they* cannot tell. There is no destination.

And so the power does what only something wise beyond the bounds of linear being can do. It gives them a gift—not a treasure, not a cure, not an answer, but a *mirror*. A fragment of itself. A way to see themselves truly, and through that sight, to grasp the questions still lingering. 

High above the highlands, the mist begins to part—but not as it has before, curling along the edges of the towering cliffs. Instead, it is drawn skyward into the vast formation of the clouds overhead, coalescing into delicate, refractive surfaces. A dome of shimmering vapor—part mirror, part liquid shell, and part veiled force of will—crystallizes over the land. Its surface ripples strangely, refusing to settle in any fixed configuration. But when the beings look up—and they do, though it seems they rarely glance skyward—you see something strike them motionless. Their luminous gazes expand with an unfathomable depth.

The mirror shows them their world, the tangled orchestra of growth and ruin they inhabit. But it shows much more. It reveals to them how every strand, every fissure, every anomaly vibrates with meaning. It shows how they themselves are not alien to this paradox of place, but integral to its breathing presence. It shows what they have shaped, tended, abandoned. It shows the pieces of what was before: forgotten ideals, dreams of lives once lived. And around it all swirl potentials yet unchosen. Contrasts spark—a shimmering spectrum unfurling between what is and what could be. The dome does not dictate. It does not demand a path. It only offers ... choice. *Which dream will you carry forward? Which will you shed?*

This strange reflection is overwhelming—a symphony of visions that dazzles even you, a foreign onlooker. And yet, for the luminous beings and the land itself, it feels as though an ancient weight shifts, not removed but understood. The land softens, somehow—it breathes a deeper breath. Trees stretch infinitesimally taller; ancient machinery whispers with a different resonance. 

The creatures stirring at the edges of the unseen draw closer, curious but no longer wary. The winged shadow—so vast even its absence was heavy—swoops lower, circling the perimeter not in menace, but in acknowledgment. A call has been heard. A door has opened. 

In time, they—and this place—may fully emerge. Or become. Or transform entirely. But the power does not need to stay to see it. It has done its work. With peace in its infinite being, it withdraws, whispering its silent farewell through tremors in the mist. No triumph, no conclusions. Only this small truth left behind, rippling quietly through stone and leaf and life: *You are more than memory. You are possibility.*
