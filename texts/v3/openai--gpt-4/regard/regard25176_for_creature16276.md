The benevolent and wise power—the one who sees far more than most and acts only when the world's silence cries out for it—turns its gaze upon the hollow, its soft folds of humid possibility, and the Entity that lurks therein.

This wasn't a place it had intended to find. Such folded spaces, these edges of reality where time pauses to dream, are neither frequent nor obvious. Perhaps its attention was drawn by the faintest of pulses—a heartbeat out of harmony with the surrounding world. Or perhaps it was always attuned to this kind of shimmering incompleteness, as clear to it as sunlight breaking through storm clouds is to human eyes. Regardless, its attention descends like the rain onto the cliff face, thick and gentle.

What it finds here is no danger to its creation—no corruption, no malice. Rather, what exists here is a kind of question. No catastrophe looms within this hollow, no clawed threat claws at the edges of the world. Instead, it is as though everything has paused mid-thought, the cliff holding its breath, the plants unsure whether they dare, the stones reluctant to speak the stories bound deep inside themselves. It is an unrealized hymn, locked in its first few notes. Air hangs heavily, trees tangle with indecision, and time—a collaborator ever anxious to stride forward—loiters awkwardly at the hollow’s borders, unable to move on.

The Entity itself is a conundrum, even for one accustomed to the profound and the strange. It is not a creature in any sense the power has known before. It is, and yet it is not. Its will is collective, rooted in the possible futures of the hollow, in the molecules of mist and minerals yet to flower into form. It whispers—but not with words. It waits—but not with purpose. It is the sentient uncertainty of things that are yet to be.

Were it any less benevolent, the wise power might have left this place to undulate, gently suspended as a forgotten parenthesis in existence. After all, what need has a compassionate god of meddling where no urgent need arises? What purpose can one find in unearthing a flaw so small and so soft that it neither harms nor cultivates?

And yet it lingers. How could it not? For one of such wisdom, this incoherent, embryonic existence is not so small after all. It is a knot in the fabric of creation, an unresolved symbol in the web of life. More than that, it is the principle of potential—the unlit fuse that might spark a thousand wonders if only something lit the flame.

But how to act in such a place? To compel or create forcibly here would be to shatter the fragile balance. Any rash intervention could ripple through the hollow’s stillness, destroying not only this pocket of what-might-be but disturbing possibilities yet unimagined. No, the benevolent power understands that its role is not one of quick solutions. This, it knows, is a place that requires care, tenderness, and above all, respect for the quiet sovereign that is the Entity.

So it merely *arrives*. Not as light streaming through a gap in the cave, nor as some tremor shifting the ground, but as a feeling—a warm pressure, a faint, restorative hum that slips effortlessly into the fabric of the place. It offers itself like water finding the lowest part of the land—not forcing, but conforming, gently present, filling without disturbing. It does not diminish the Entity nor the hollow; rather, it meets them with an understanding deeper than words.

The Entity senses this immediately. Though its awareness is diffuse and abstract, it feels the change, like a new flavor added to an ancient mist. Its glow dims briefly as it retreats in an automatic hesitation, then dances brighter—curiously, cautiously—and observes the way the warm intrusion does not harden the air nor accelerate its eternal rhythm.

The wise power feels the weight of the Entity’s spiraling curiosity, its apprehension, its subtle shift to quiet receptiveness. And so the power begins its work, subtle as a spidercraft weaver threading a new pattern into old cloth. It does not command. It does not reshape. What it does is awaken.

Under the weightless presence of this power, the stalactites begin to shift—not crumble or collapse, but refine. Their once unnatural smoothness softens. Their perfect, mechanical shapes yield to the gentler geometry of erosion. Instead of sterile beads of water, life springs from the droplets, bringing the faintest hint of green where none should be. Algae begins to whisper in liquid tongues, some of it blooming into more complex life. The symphony of single-cellular melody builds as unseen rhythms quicken the pond’s pulse. 

The loamy patches of soil cease their restless reluctance, welcoming thick, mossy beds that chatter softly among themselves, their new textures drinking in the never-ending moisture. Grasses stretch taller, shaking off their shroud of hesitation. Their roots reach a little deeper, bolstered by faint hints of promise. The dormant seeds of unknown plants—seeds the hollow had kept clutched to its heart—stir beneath the black soil. Shoots of pale green push silently into the open air where before there was only waiting.

The Insects, too, find their wings growing subtly more sure; their legs seem to find more balance in the treacherous uneven terrain. The presence settles lightly yet deeply into the world-within-this-hollow, less guide than gentle rain coaxing growth.

And the Entity—the living whisper trapped in its cocoon of mist—responds in its way. Its pulse grows slower, deeper, more collected. It senses its resonance with this new energy, this awakening vibration that floats through the hollow like a song hidden in the mist. In truth, the Entity has no voice of its own, but now where there was silence, a faint harmony blooms as it borrows the benevolent power’s song. Though it has no organs to sing, it thrums within the fabric of the hollow, coaxing itself and its home further into an exquisitely small but eternal unfolding.

The wise power does not linger once its work begins. When a melody surfaces in the Entity’s symphony, faint and almost trembling but unmistakable, it knows it is time to leave. It was not its place to grow this hollow into something it was never meant to be; it was its place to remind this world in miniature of its own capability for becoming.

And so the power withdraws, its presence tapering like the final moments of twilight, leaving no trace but the promise it unlocked.

The hollow remains what it was always meant to be—not a finished masterpiece, but a beginning that awakens each day to its own boundless potential. The Entity floats and flickers more deeply now, shaping its misty limbs to play subtly among the grasses, to nudge ferns toward more daring curls. It is no less the watcher, no less the stillness at the heart of this small universe. But it is awake now—in the only way a being of possibility could ever be awake.

The hollow leans a little closer to the world now, its strange balance of silence and becoming a gift to itself and nothing more. Yet in some inarticulable way, it hums a little brighter, no longer a forgotten shadow, but an origin story patiently composing its verses in the deep and perpetual mist.
