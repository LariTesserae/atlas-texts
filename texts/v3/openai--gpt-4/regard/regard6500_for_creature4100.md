Upon observing this place and the Subterrians, the benevolent and wise power, Lumisk, decided to lend them assistance. This entity of kindness and knowledge did not merely seek to help, but to understand the Subterrians, their needs, their strengths, and their potential for growth. 

Lumisk, unlike the other godlike figures, didn't make any precipitous changes in the Subterrians' lives, aware that transformations could lead to perturbations that could threaten their survival. Lumisk understood that change, though spontaneous by nature, needed to be paced, and that no swooping miracles would be as effective as planned interventions.

The first and most noticeable effect of Lumisk's subtle actions was a replenishing of the ambient, earthy fragrance that permeated throughout the subterranean city, and an unexpected reduction of intense heat. It was subtle, gradual—the hot air became a bit drier, less exhausting, more bearable. The temperature did not drop drastically, merely lost its brute intensity, creating a more comfortable environment for the Subterrians to work and live in.

Under Lumisk's influence, the luminosity improved as well. The worn electric bulbs began to burn brighter, their hums became more regular, and the amber hue deepened to a warmer, more comforting radiance. The myriad shadows, dancing and contorting under the flicker of electric lights, were whittled down, pushing back oppressive darkness and lighting up deeper chambers of their subterranean abode. 

Lumisk also guided the Subterrians' minds, subtly leading skilled individuals to advancements in their crafts—be it masonry, forging, or weaving. This wasn't transformational, but instead built on their existing capabilities; a refinement rather than an overhaul. Lumisk nudged their innate potential for problem-solving, empowering them softly while not subverting their own collective intelligence.

Materials seemed to last longer under Lumisk's influence, with less wear and tear. Machines began to work more efficiently, tools held their edge longer, and the durable fabric of their clothing toughened even further. Even the food, made of subterranean crops supplemented by the bounty from forays above­ground, seemed to taste better, richer, more sustaining. 

The power of Lumisk permeated the environment, creating an invisible cocoon of calm and tranquility within the settlement—gently strengthening the Subterrians, bolstering their courage, and augmenting their resilience. Lumisk found no need to at once eradicate their struggles: that would deprive the Subterrians of the very virtues that molded them. Lumisk merely eased, nuanced their survival, making them a little less overwrought, their lives a bit more livable. 

On an individual level, Lumisk was there for the Subterrians—in the quiet whispers that guided a struggling smith, in the soft touch that comforted a tired child, in the faint inspiration that sparked an innovative solution in a builder’s mind. A barely perceptible, pervasive presence like a comforting blanket over their trials and triumphs, their daily life.

Lumisk’s wisdom and benevolence were not of dramatic interventions, but of subtly channeled influences, for this power valued the inhabitants' independence and adaptability. Lumisk's presence did not subvert the Subterrians' ways, but humbly worked in sync with them, gently guiding towards betterment, symbiotically existing with this underground world—like an unseen vein of silver in the heart of the stone, a vein that softly glowed but did not blind.

The Subterrians, for the most part, didn't realize this external beneficence—not in the usual perception of an outright miracle. No lightning-bolt epiphanies, no conversion experiences. But the gradual easing of their struggles, the slow spreading of warmth and comfort, and the imperceptible shift in prosperity was not lost on them. They didn't thank Lumisk, for they didn't need to, and Lumisk didn't expect that either. In their quiet survival, their determined progress, and their unyielding unity—was their gratitude, their recognition.

Years later, across generations, the Subterrians would tell stories of this era—of subtle miracles, of a gentle tide turning. They would recount it as 'the Lumisk Years', a testament to quiet benevolence and wise, tempered progress. They still would not deify Lumisk, for in their wisdom they knew deification could lead to dependence, and dependence to stagnation. 

Instead, they would remember Lumisk as a friendly current in the ocean of existence, a quietly empowering presence that didn't seek worship but inspired resilience. The stories would become legends, the legends a part of their identity, and in these narratives, the memory of Lumisk's benevolence would forever glow, like the warm radiance of their electric lamps, in the heart of the subterranean city. 

And so, in its humble, supportive approach, the wise, benevolent power of Lumisk would not merely help the Subterrians but become an integral part of their identity, leaving an indelible mark on their culture, their stories, and their hearts. Without imposing or governing, Lumisk would be there—in their tales, their techniques, their lives—pulsating quietly in the heart of the living metropolis below the surface, the city of the Subterrians.
