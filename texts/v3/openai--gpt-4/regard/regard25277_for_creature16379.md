Their presence is felt more than seen, an interplay of absence and anomaly that prickles the skin and strings invisible wires of tension through the air around them. And yet, there is no malice here. They do not confront or encroach; they exist in parallel, their purpose inscrutable but not alien to the rhythm of this place. They belong within it as much as the rivulets and the scattered moss, as if they had emerged from the bones of the land itself.

The Erratics make no sound, yet their silence feels symphonic, signaling not lack but complexity—a depth that refuses to resolve into the simplicity of words. If you come too near, you might feel an unplaceable resonance under your breastbone, as though every thread of you is being tuned, adjusted, tuned again. You might imagine voices, somber and slow, not speaking but brushing against the edges of your thoughts like wind over grass—too distant to grasp, yet impossibly intimate.

It is in this still tension, in the interplay of muted ceaselessness and half-seen forms, that the benevolent power first notices. It is not summoned by need, for the Erratics do not express need the way saplings stretch for light or creatures migrate in pursuit of abundance. Nor is it an invocation of crisis.
