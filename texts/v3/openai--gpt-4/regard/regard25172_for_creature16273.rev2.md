The benevolent and wise power gazes upon this indigo abyss, this vast cathedral of fluid silence and light, and feels a pull not unlike the tides themselves—a calling to bear witness, to nurture, to connect. This ethereal world, and the beings who dwell within it, are not in need of saving; they have achieved a harmony untouched by the frenetic desperation of the surface. But there is something here that stirs the deep wells of compassion in the heart of this transcendent observer, something raw and rare: a world free of hunger or conquest, a society born of light and fluid grace, and a land imbued with memories of an ancient stillness.

The power descends gently into the depths, not as a conqueror, not even as a visitor, but as a whisper that stirs neither the current nor the glow. Its presence is a shimmer just outside perception, a melody that resonates without sound, a subtle shift in the already-shiftless tides. It has no intent to disturb, only to offer what it can. The beings of the deep, gliding luminescent streams of sentience, pause in their reverent dances around crumbling stone relics. They feel the pulse of something immense and ancient—a force as infinite and unknowable as the abyss itself. 

This power, veiled in undulating layers of knowingness, observes the glowing forms closely. It sees their beauty, their evolution, their peace—and yet, it senses their unvoiced longing. Not a longing born from lack, for they are a contented people, but rather one born of curiosity, of wonder, of an unspoken yearning to understand the echoing memories they swim among. The stone relics, the forgotten tools, the stories carved in runes that the beings can glean but not grasp—these are mysteries bound tightly to their existence, unanswered questions like dreaming shadows among them. 

The power does not intrude, does not reshape or add to what does not need reshaping or addition. What need would they have of gifts such as fire or machinery? No, the offering is subtler than that, a quiet enhancing of what is already there. The power draws upon its infinite well of wisdom, weaving faint impressions into the currents, softly inscribing layers of insight onto the fabric of water itself. These whispers, delicate as the glittering orbs of the beings' eyes, sew together fragmented histories and meanings obscured by time. 

Very gradually, the beings begin to sense shifts in the current, not as disruptions but as intricate songlines flowing through the liquid air. They listen—not as we do with ears, but with their luminous souls, their bioluminescent pulses realigning to resonate in harmony with the gift. Through this new resonance, the runes carved into ancient stone structures yield their secrets. The beings now perceive not just symbols but stories; not just forms but truths etched in the deep-time history of their sanctuary.

They learn of the elder race who came before them, architects of the submerged garden, wielders of chisels and stone intelligence. The elder race, caught in a ceaseless cycle of building and breaking, eventually let themselves be absorbed into the quiet eternity of the sea. These luminescent drift-beings are their inheritors—not their descendants in blood, but in spirit. The ancient architects once sought a similar harmony with the abyss, but were too tangled in their desires to truly let go and become it. These new beings, however, have learned the lesson of stillness. They no longer need the tools left behind; those are reminders, not requirements. 

The power observes this subtle weaving of history into identity and understands it is enough. It does not linger to be known, does not impose its presence, for to do so would be to fracture the delicate filament of their timeless peace. Instead, it leaves one enduring gesture—a celestial current imbued beneath the seabed, silent to the beings for now but there, ready to be unearthed in some distant eon. This current will act as a gentle tide, ensuring their habitat remains hospitable, their ecosystem balanced, impervious to changes above.

The beings remain guardians of their submerged sanctuary, their soft luminescent dances even more deliberate now, as though each ripple of light carries a question asked, a mystery answered. They have no words for gratitude, no prayer or cry to the heavens, but their existence itself magnifies—it glows brighter, almost imperceptibly, a resonating ripple through the ocean deep that touches everything and lingers in its wake.

Far above, on dry land, no one notices the kindness of this quiet cosmic power. The distant sun sets and rises again, oblivious to the pulsing light moving in the unseen depths below. But the galaxies and the ancient stars bear witness always: here lies a world where beauty, peace, and the unknowable radiance of silence are their own hymns of survival. Here lies a kingdom untouched by strife and unbroken by time—a quiet testimony to the universal truth that life, in its infinite forms, seeks not only to persist but also to know itself, and the worlds it inhabits.

The better for them, the power knows, to remain unseen. It ascends into the infinite fold of stars, leaving behind a deeper calm. So ends its intervention—not an intervention, truly, but a whisper, a brushstroke, a benevolent bow to the wisdom of a world that already thrived far beyond its reach.
