In this strange world of PhylloPlane, the inhabitants are peculiar as the place itself. The beings that thrive here are a combination of nature and machine. Known as ArborMecha, they are sentient, vegetation-based entities entwined with mechanical parts. In size, they range from minuscule, barely visible to the naked eye, to giants towering above the tallest vegetative structures.

The ArborMecha are interwoven with intricate gears and cogs, giving them an industrial appearance but function with the rhythm of nature. They embody the unlikely harmony between the organic and the mechanical. Some might resemble trees or giant fungi with limbs dotted with leathery leaves or glowing lichen, others as intricate contraptions of ivies and thorns with a heart of ticking clockwork. However, despite their diverse appearances, all ArborMecha share a peculiar trait—within each of them pulses a soft bioluminescent glow, akin to the dim light that permanently illuminates PhylloPlane. This glow, a manifestation of life, seems to synchronize across the realm, pulsing in harmony.

These strange beings exist in a symbiotic relationship with the land they inhabit. They support and maintain the environment, acting as caretakers of PhylloPlane. They draw sustenance from the air's sparse moisture and, in turn, their bodies store and enrich it, slowly releasing it back into the environment. They are perpetually witnessing and adapting to the balance between decaying machinery and resurging vegetation, an eternal cycle of destruction and regrowth—a testament to life that endures, even in an improbable place.

ArborMecha do not communicate as we understand it, rather, they resonate harmonic frequencies that ripple through the metallic veins of PhylloPlane, caught and echoed, amplified through the dense vegetation. In these frequencies, knowledge, emotions and intentions are shared, building a collective consciousness that ties each ArborMecha to another, uniting them in their isolated existence.

Not a conventional form of life, but very much alive, the ArborMecha embody the duality of PhylloPlane—the latent energy of a bygone technological era and the relentless force of organic life, asserting itself over and over again. Through their existence, they assert the resilience of life in the most unexpected corners of the universe, demonstrating that even the seemingly inevitable clash between nature and industry can result in an existence not just surviving, but thriving in its unique, paradoxical harmony. Thus, they stand as both children and guardians of PhylloPlane, the engineered wilderness that hangs in the skies.
