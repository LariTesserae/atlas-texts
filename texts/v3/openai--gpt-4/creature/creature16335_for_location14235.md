This place is alive because it is inhabited—populated not just by beings, but by processes. Life here doesn’t reside in tidy categories of organism and environment, but in the fluidity of their total entanglement. It would be wrong, perhaps, to speak of discrete creatures living within this space; better to call them extensions, manifestations of the place itself, or to call the entire system a singular organism that hasn’t yet resolved its final form.

Its inhabitants are not born like animals; they are *excretions*. Pulled into being by the restless industry embedded within these walls, they emerge in erratic bursts. Some birth themselves from the chromatic surface of a soft cyst that flowers open like an iris to disgorge its occupant. Others drip, piece by linked piece, down metallic conduits, their bodies assembling mid-descent. A few seem to simply *condense*, as though they were vapor crystallizing into a new and animate geometry. It feels incomplete, or perpetually incomplete; they are thrown into this world as if mid-sentence, their movements instinctive but not fully conscious.

Their forms are dazzling in their variety but unified in purpose—not the intention of individuals, but the momentum of a hive. From afar, the smaller ones might appear insectoid, even plausible, but upon closer observation dissolve into anatomical inconsistency. A thing moves on legs, yes, but were those barbed appendages *truly legs*, or was it merely using reactive, flexible spines sprouting from its wet faceted undershell? Another slides with the uncanny grace of something boneless, but when you pass near, your eyes catch the glint of hinged plates locking and unlocking like liquid metal armour. Others whir and blink, glasslike sensory clusters sheathing them in watchful mazes, examining the air, or flickering pinpricks of light pointed at mechanisms you cannot see. 

They do not feast, not in any way you'd recognize. Some of them siphon fluids directly from the walls, coupling their soft bodies to the trickling metallic structures as though in rapture; others secrete shimmering threads in elaborate webs that sink into machinery—and dissolve there. These patterns etched in threads rise and fall in a sullen rhythm you cannot track. It is not hunger they satisfy, but some unnamable mandate. You watch one open its underbelly like a blooming flower, its interior soft and coral-colored, releasing a sticky, spattered foam to coat a new girder. Behind it, others dart in to strip away a prior creation, whisking it into the spaces you're not sure are solid. By the time you blink, you’ve lost the sequence of these acts, their logic spiraling into complexity too dense to untangle.

Amidst all the erratic, frenetic activity, there are larger presences as well, though you rarely see them fully. Perhaps you’ve caught glimpses: a massive, metal-slick limb, each joint knotted with fibrous sinew, bracing against a drifting ceiling. The ripple of plating parting one wall, as though it’s breathing something alive back into its space. Or that sound—a long, rolling exhalation you felt vibrating through the floor more than you heard it—making you wonder if some monolithic intelligence sleeps embedded just beneath the surface, only extruding these smaller creatures in its half-waking state.
