Whatever inhabits this place is a being of stone and energy, mirroring the strange beauty of its surroundings. The description of the locals is hard to conceptualize, a synthesis of geometry and elements. Like a complex crystal arrangement that has gained self-awareness, these beings can only be described as mineral sentient lifeforms, existing in a realm far removed from traditional definitions of life. Physically anchored, static yet thinking, their existence is fundamentally different from ours, but nonetheless, the pulsing energy inside them is a testament to the life they hold.

They do not possess limbs or organs; instead, they're a network of mineral growths and bio-luminescent spots that serve as sensorial regions. These organisms are capable of 'seeing' and 'hearing' electromagnetic waves through these sensorial zones. Their 'thoughts' or information processing happens through the propagation of vibrations across their mineral structures, the equivalent to a neuronal network in carbon-based life forms. Their communication, therefore, is an ambient orchestra of resonating frequencies that penetrate the surrounding crust, a silent conversation to any outsider.

In this complex ecosystem, these beings experience life in a way completely alien to us. They neither eat nor sleep in a conventional sense. Instead, they directly extract and utilize the radiant energy of their surroundings. It's a slow metabolism, far slower than any carbon-based life forms. Their lifespan is therefore extended over millennia, their procreation, if it can be called that, is the slow propagation and separation of their crystalline structures.

Their physical formality's stasis does not translate to a lack of impression or impact with their environment. Over centuries, they manipulate the crust and crystal around them. Each structure within this place is decidedly an effort of their patient creativity, using slow but determined erosion and mineral depositing processes. Their dwellings and the broader environment reflect their nature— angular, strange, but in harmony with the surrounding.

Perceptible changes come to their forms when they vibrate in specific frequencies, a subtle gesture that may resemble emotions or reactions in carbon-based beings. Sometimes they resonate with the flickering light from above, perfectly timed across the entire frequency spectrum. Other times, they embellish their facades with more glowing orbs, suggesting either a kind of internal reconfiguration or an external signal to nearby entities.

A discernable pattern within their society is elusive due to their alien, slow-paced life. Their structures, though interwoven, do not necessarily suggest traditional social layers. Instead, the arrangement may be more about the resonation of their frequencies, the physical formation mirroring their interaction in the spectral plane, an architectonic representation of their communication networks.

Whether they perceive an outsider as a threat or a visitor is uncertain. They are indifferent in a way that radiates more from a lack of understanding than hostility. Their realm is so fundamentally different, it's almost incomprehensible to imagine their cognition of life forms not of their domain. 

Their existence lays bare the very definition of life, offering a profoundly different manifestation of liveliness, intelligence, and society. Removed from our conventional biological limits and parameters, these beings suggest a broader frame for life— crystalline, static yet vibrant. Therein, the inhabitants of this harsh, mineral world illustrate the incredible adaptability of life, the grandeur of cosmic biodiversity.
