Deruwa is inhabited by the simple and the complex, the large and the small, the quick and the slow. The chorus of life that permeates the air is created by the unseen symphony of the tiny organisms that reside within the fertile soil, the crystal clear bodies of water, and the air itself. Microscopic bacteria, amoebas, and fungi own this territory as much as anything else, engaging in their endless task of decomposition and regeneration which maintains the circle of life here intact.

A multitude of insects, arrayed in all hues and sizes, animate the landscape. From ladybugs crawling over the dew-kissed vegetation to majestic butterflies flitting from flower to flower, each one plays a role in the complex web of life. Colonies of ants march in straight lines, carrying pieces of leaves many times their size, while the silent spiders retreat in their intricate webs, patiently waiting for their next unsuspecting meal.

Hopping on the moss-carpeted ground are the small mammals - voles, shrews, and hedgehogs, emerging from their holes in the lithoses at the break of dusk, venturing out to forage in the whispering darkness. With their keen senses, they navigate their world full of potential wonders and dangers, always within the rhythm dictated by their instincts.

In the mystical treetops above, joyful troupes of birds: warblers, sparrows, and starlings add vivid splashes of color and serenade Deruwa with their melodious songs. They nestle in the nooks and crannies of the tall trees, their calls lacing the air with an orchestra of sounds each new dawn. The swoosh of their departure cuts the still air, their flight pattern an artful dance against the backdrop of the periwinkle sky.

Then there are the bees, the humming backbone of Deruwa's heart. Living in their secluded cities amongst the tree branches, they venture out to perform the dance of pollination, their fuzzy bodies and delicate wings shimmering as they joyfully buzz from one wildflower to another. These faithful stewards of the environment are amongst the most tireless workers, and they play a significant role in sustaining the dazzling spectacle that is Deruwa.

Life in Deruwa is simple yet complex, visible, and invisible. The apex of simplicity and complexity, each organism, no matter how small or large, contributes vital energy to this elegy of growth. Here, the line between the flora and fauna blurs as each existence merges into the collective life-cycle, painting an idyllic picture of coexistence and symbiosis. If you look closely, you'll find that Deruwa is the embodiment of life unchained, an eden where nature basks under the eternal glow of sun and the soothing whispers of rain, in its most untamed and glorious form.
