In this alien garden of glowing plants and fungi, live the Polaris Luminae, or commonly known as the twilight lantern beings. These ethereal beings are sentient life forms, made entirely of luminescent organic compounds. Without segregation of flora or fauna, their bodies consist of fungiform extremities, phosphorescent tendrils sprouting from central cores - blades of vital bioluminescence. Remarkably, they possess a level of luminosity control that allows them to manipulate the intensity and color of their light, a spectacle that can range from subdued, gentle glows to dazzling illuminations.

In size, they vary - the smaller ones are about the size of a robin's egg, where the larger ones can reach up to a meter wide. Due to their biological makeup, they do not require sustenance in the way most living creatures do - instead, they thrive upon the energy radiated by the glowing flora and fungi surrounding them in the Night Garden. Their existence is slow and mystical, much like their place of residence, moving languorously through the air like orbs floating on a gentle current.

Despite the bioluminescent brightness they emanate, Polaris Luminae are innately lean towards solitude. Silent, contemplative, they spend most of their long lifetime in the passive absorption of light and energy. They respond to the rhythm of their mushroom and flower companions, their vibrancy waxing and waning with the daily pulses of fungal illumination.

How they communicate remains largely uncertain. Physically isolated, they nonetheless appear to share a deep connection, their lights flickering and shifting in what could be analogous to the subtle nuances of conversation in their luminary language. They have no observable social structure, no visible hierarchy or division of labor, and yet they seem to coexist harmoniously, functioning in unspoken accord.

The Polaris Luminae personify the serene tranquility of the Night Garden, their silence punctuated only by the rare resonant hum of energy exchange. Yet, in their seeming passivity, they embody the enduring resilience of life skilled in adapting to the most inhospitable of environments. These lantern beings are the true embodiment of the Night Garden's beauty - exotic, enduring, and ever luminous. They are the pulsing, glowing heartbeat of this ancient, peculiar world hidden deep in the heart of the mountain.
