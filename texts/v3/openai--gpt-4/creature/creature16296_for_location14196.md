The residents of this high-altitude sanctuary are truly unique, a reflection of the changing environment descending from the cosmos. The survival of the fittest didn't mean the strongest or the swiftest; it was those who adapted and blended into the canvas of this twilight world who claimed it as their own. 

The tenants begin their enigmatic procession with the flora – the standing guardians of the land. These are not your usual cedar or spruce which one might think would dominate such an ecosystem. Instead, the native trees are home to quirky characteristics that allow them to endure the unending twilight. Some have evolved to become the light, bearing luminescent leaves that shimmer like muted embers in the dark. The flicker of their glow outlines any creature that dares to ascend or descend their sturdy frames, an intricate surveillance system that lights up the realm.

The underbrush too, is not left barren. From large, spongy mosses that carpet the breadth of forest floor, to ferns that extend their fronds to imitate the traditional leaves, all bear the mark of bioluminescence, a unique evolutionary trick. Some fungi, a peculiar type of algae, and a host of other moisture-loving plants band together, turning this twilight arena into a fantastical lit stage, never truly succumbing to the darkness. 

And then there are the mobile tenants, the animals who've made this arcane wilderness their home. Slinking across the forest floor are the spectral salamanders, fragile but fiercely agile, their translucent bodies acting as living lanterns, radiant veins glistening a path through their travels. The glass-winged butterflies, seemingly ethereal, gently flutter in the half-light, their wings refracting slivers of spectral colors onto unsuspecting surfaces. 

Even from above, life cushions the silent reverie underneath. Feathered creatures of every size reside in the leafy fortresses. Masters at mimicry, they blend into their homes so seamlessly that only their melodic vocalizations reveal their presence. 

Along the banks of gelid streams, semi-aquatic creatures frolic. In the flowing currents, blind fish glide with practiced ease, their age-old navigational wisdom guiding them back whence they came. Amphibious creatures like the bioluminescent newts are crowned as the ominous guardians under the water.

In the twilight sanctuary, life, seen or heard, has carved a niche for themselves. With the evolution of night vision, superior camouflage, and luminescent adaptations, each creature is a resolute survivor, silently defining the rhythm of life. The place pulsates with unique energy and mystery, its residents locked in an eternal dance with the twilight, forming a living testament to the marvel of adaptation and evolution. These are the residents who've embraced the twilight rhapsody, sightings of whom are akin to ephemeral dreams, a fleeting glimpse into a world draped in endless twilight.
