The beings that dwell in this turbulent jungle of wet chaos take many forms, mirroring the shapelessness and flux of their homes. They are creatures of water and light, reflections of the fluid environment, few with ascertainable bodies, even fewer with discernible faces. Ethereal and vaporous, they morph and twist, studded with occasional bioluminescent bursts. Some appear as streaks of fleeting luminescence, fluttering or darting through the liquid air-waves like will-o'-the-wisps. Others have the void-like depth of space within their shimmering forms; coalescing and solidifying for a fleeting moment into forms vaguely reminiscent of something terrestrial before losing form to the relentless motion of the place.

On the brink of solidity, they seem to continuously gain and then immediately lose something like a physical integrity: Their bodies are spectral, sometimes disintegrating into glimmers of light or sparks, and at other times, stubbornly resisting that decline. Often, in their more solid phases, they appear to be enrobed in living cloaks of ever-flowing droplets; gilded in the reflected light, they shimmer in surreal beauty. 

Some of these entities move rhythmically with their environment, allowing themselves to be buffeted along with the liquid forces. Others, however, seem capable of navigating this chaotic dance, darting with an impossible grace amid the spiraling waters, temporarily defying the tide before allowing themselves to be redrawn again by the wave's ecstatic pull.

These beings communicate, if communication is the apt word, through wavelets and vibrations that ripple through the fluidic landscape, exploiting the natural harmonics of the place. Imagine whispers that ripple out from a single source, permeating the landscape in the form of tiny wave circles that expand outward without ever fading away. While they definitely appear sentient, their consciousness is not like ours; it is distributed, fluidic, rippling out into the environment and rippling back, an interplay of ethereal ephemeral interaction with the world itself. The entirety of their existence continuously mirrors the intricate dance of their surrounding environment.

They feed off the energy of this wavy world; they consume the strands of light, the heat in the disturbed air, the kinetic tension in the waters, and perhaps even the vibration in the bedrock. Theirs is a strange symbiotic relationship with the chaos, each drawing strength from the other.

This is a place where life is not defined by bodies and boundaries, but by ceaseless flux and by the ephemeral miracles that birth and unbirth in a ceaseless dance of being and un-being. These are the beautiful and bizarre denizens of a world in violent change; embodiments of malfunction and unworkable physics that nevertheless, against all odds, work.
In this fluid realm, their being is merging and emerging, their creation unending, to and from forms in a continuous, roiling dance of dissolution and birth. They are the living, undying testimony to the fact that even in places where reality seems to barely persist, life will find a way.
