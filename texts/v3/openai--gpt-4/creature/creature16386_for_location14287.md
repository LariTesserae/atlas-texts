The occupants of Elysian Haven, dubbed as Elysians, are a novel strain of humans who have merged biology with technology to withstand the sublime yet brutal reality of living at the bottom of the ocean. They are the conscious creation of their own evolution, a synchronicity between nature and man-made wonders and stand as a testament to the lengths humanity will go to survive and adapt.

Physically Elysians resemble their land-rooted ancestors, but they are an amalgamation of biological and mechanical marvels. The oxygen-rich environment has triggered evolutionary changes over the generations, manifesting in physical alterations conducive to a life underwater. Their skin is a hue akin to the translucent azure of the shallows during a bright day, camouflaging them against their surroundings. Their bodies are lean and nimble, having evolved to accommodate for limited space and enhanced agility, a need in the city's unique architectural conformation.

Their eyes are a startling mix of cerulean and lavender, enhanced to visualize the spectrum of light piercing through the water and glass shields more inclusively. Their flesh pulses with traceries of bio-luminescent veins, vestiges of the high-energy particles that fill their dwelling, giving them a subtle yet constant glow. These intricate bodily patterns are a sign of their advanced circulatory systems, fortified to harness the ionic energy sourced from the city's machinery.

For sustenance, Elysians have incorporated the city's bio-engineered plant-life within their diet, plants specifically tailored to thrive in the unique conditions and deliver the necessary nutrients and energy. Elysians no longer consume food in the traditional sense, rather extracts and bioengineered food capsules enriched with essential nutrients. 

Given the higher cognitive functioning required to operate the advanced machinery and technology that enables this underwater utopia, Elysians have developed enhanced mental and neurological capacities. Their brains, enriched with more dense neural pathways, have opened pathways to communication that postulate beyond verbal or written languages, vaguely akin to telepathy. These evolutionary advancements have fostered an enhanced communal bond, almost hive-like, but devoid of any loss of the individual's identity.

Elysians are a harmonious, collaborative society, embodying the balance of their environment in their societal and personal interactions. Despite their sophisticated technological existence, they possess an innate fascination and respect for nature, effortlessly merging human innovation with natural conservation. They have left the petty squabbles and disjointed societal structures of their ancestors behind, striving towards advancement and balance in unison.

Elysian Haven is, in essence, a living, thriving affirmation of life's resiliency and versatility. And the Elysians stand as the audacious dreamers, engineers, and visionaries, crafting lives in defiance of nature's wrath, in a place where survival was once thought improbable, unseen, unheard, beneath layers of waves and miles from the surface. They are a testament to the breadth of human adaptability, a beacon of what humanity could potentially evolve into, once limitations are perceived as opportunities.
