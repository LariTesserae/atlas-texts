In this place of the inchoate being and mineral ambition lives an existence that can barely be classified as life as we understand it. Let's call these the Wrought. 

The Wrought are beings seemingly born from the world itself, welded into existence through some mysterious and profound confluence of the planet's hyper-tectonic activity and the unseen mechanisms deep within its forge-like innards. They are the children of bedrock, air-thick heat and iron-laden humidity.

Wrought bodies are as undisclosed as the planet they inhabit. Composed of an amalgamation of geology and something vaguely organic, their forms appear hewn from the obsidian-like crust and are inlaid with the same veins of glittering minerals that stripe the world around them. Their shapes defy clear categorization, some roiling and shifting like semi-molten rock, others appearing as more fixed figures caught somewhere between humanoid and abstract sculpture.

What may be taken for sensory organs are not fixed but appear to form according to need - at one moment there might be a cratered formation like a great, calcified eye, the next a line of luminescence which sweeps over the terrain like a radar. These are the observers of this evanescent world, their attention seems forever focused on their environment, minutely attuned to the whisper of steam from a vent or the plunge of a bridge into the rockface, their forms shuddering with the rumble of the world's titanic heartbeat.

The Wrought do not simply inhabit the world - they are an intrinsic part of its ceaseless, fluid evolution. Sections of their bodies will periodically harden and blacken before starting to crumble away, revealing beneath a fresh layer of—what? New skin, if such a word could be applied here—resplendent with unexplored veins of radiant minerals. It is as though they undergo a life cycle mimicking the geological processes taking place around them—creation and destruction, solidification and erosion.

Despite their formidable presence, the Wrought are enigmatically non-aggressive beings. Coexisting with the sporadic threads of charcoal life and the flickering orbs, they show a surprising delicacy, oftentimes allowing the threads to crawl over them, forming a dynamic tapestry of living mineral making intricate patterns on their surface. As far as can be told, they show no recognition nor alienation towards the skeletal remains scattered across their land, treating them with the same deference they give to the ever-evolving environment. 

A strange symbiosis exists between the Wrought and the half-formed industrial infrastructure carving its way across the landscape. They are seen shuffling along freestanding catwalks, contemplating the petrified machinery, orienting themselves alongside exposed conduits. Seemingly partaking in an inexplicable interaction or perhaps carrying out their own cryptic form of maintenance. In these enigmatic rituals, even less is comprehended about these hulking beings, these constructs of crust and mineral, of nebulous life thriving in obscurity, in a world on the brink of an unknowable future.

In the Forge, a place between about-to-be and what-has-been, the Wrought endure, their existence as uncertain as the luminous orbs, as consistent as the low hum. They are the antithesis of transience, a race of living bedrock becoming and unbecoming, indelibly etched into the ceaseless creation story being told by this ever-evolving world. They are, just as the world they inhabit: profoundly, poignantly unfinished.
