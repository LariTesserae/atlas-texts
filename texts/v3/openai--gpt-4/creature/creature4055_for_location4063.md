Submercia is home to an extraordinary species of humanoids known as the Aquarians. A result of centuries of adative evolution, these beings glide through their submerged habitat with a grace that separates them from their terrestrial counterparts. The Aquarians have evolved to embrace their marine environment — gills branch out from their ribs, well adapted to extracting oxygen from the water. Their eyes are larger and more reflective than ours, built to capture every possible photon in the dim-lit depths. Their skin, slick and partially scaled, glistens under the slivers of sunlight filtering down from the water’s surface. 

Equipped with elongated, webbed fingers and toes, Aquarians are natural swimmers. Their bodies are strong but lean, cutting through the water with ease. Their torsos are lungs in themselves, capable of expanding and contracting to adapt to the water pressure. Their legs, powering with the might of a mythical mermaid, hold in them muscles that are sheathed with thick veins, pumping blood that's rich in highly evolved hemoglobin, capable of accommodating extreme levels of carbon dioxide for longer 'breath-holding' periods. 

Aquarians’ faces bear a mild resemblance to the humans above, though softened and made ethereal by the water’s touch. High cheekbones frame large, expressive eyes that see in spectrums of light our eyes could only dream of comprehending. They have small, slightly upturned noses. Their mouths are an unusual amalgamation of human lips and interior baleen filters, making them omnivores thriving on both the scant vegetation that finds root in Submercia's metal surfaces and the crustaceans and plankton abundant in the ocean.   

The Aquarians display an enlightened curiosity towards nature’s presence around them. They hold profound respect for the sea and its inhabitants, and their moral and ethical boundaries draw a firm line around their relationships with other sea creatures. They coexist with the marine life, never imposing but rather adapting. Their lack of weapons is indicative of their peaceful lifestyle; they have an intricate knowledge of currents and underwater geography, which they rely upon to escape predator threats. 

Their minds are their most awe-inspiring feature. They're unfathomably intelligent, capable of constructing a thriving society in one of the planet’s harshest habitats. Their brains, superbly evolved, command an understanding of physics, architecture and civilization that transcends human cognition. 

In Submercia, the wildness of the ocean is juxtaposed with the cultivated Aquarian spirit, creating a setting of haunting beauty. The Aquarians, these evolved humanoid marvels, imbue their world with a sense of the exceptional, enlightened mystery that is both compelling and heart-stopping. It's the testament of nature's adaptability and tenacity, of life persisting in sheer defiance of existence's laws.
