Here, in this desolate but mesmerizing landscape, dwell incomprehensible beings known as the Glist. A race of bizarre organisms that seem neither sentient nor sessile, they have been the only occupants of this celestial cathedral for an epoch. The Glist are flicker-forms, semi-solid entities which exist on the delicate threshold of presence and abeyance. They linger like a figment, on the farthest edge of what might be considered 'life.' 

Their forms are nebulous, shifting between tangible solidity and mere opacity as fluidly as their habitat swings between extremes. Their shapes appear crystalline, mirroring the complex facets of the quartz and basalt that dominate their environment. They grow and shrink, crystallize and liquefy, in accordance with rhythms and cycles inscrutable to sapient organisms. And in their slow, fluid movement, there is an impossibly gentle kinetic hum - an almost imperceptible vibration - a surreal lullaby that echoes on the windless vistas.

The Glist lack the faces, features, or physiology of typical organisms. There are no eyes to receive light, no mouths to consume, no limbs with which to interact. Yet, they perceive their austere world nonetheless, through a sense as alien to us as their home. A sense of vibration, oscillation, and resonance, perceiving the quiet changes of their world through the shifting frequencies of the stone, the surges within the pothole pools, the cooling and heating of the air – a pulsating realm translated into an ever-evolving symphony of existence. 

With patience, they navigate their landscape through subtle, tectonic drifts. They graze on the delicate mesh of fungus beneath the pools, soaking up the peculiar energies stored within. They commune with the whispering lichens, basking in the ephemeral echoes they cast upon the granite theater.

Though appearing inanimate, the Glist conduct their existence through glacial progression. Over centuries, they move, procreate, coalesce and divide, their lifesongs flickering scenes lit within the unending silence. Their births and deaths mark no visible change in the realm, but to a keen observer, a subtle shift in the harmony of resonations, a minute modulation in the rhythm of existence, marks these significant events.

The Glist, in essence, are an embodiment of persistence, the symphony of survival played in the realm just before the precipice of desolation. With an existence in subtle harmony with their environment, they are ethereal poets who sing their stanzas in silence, whispers woven into the fabric of time, the only echo in an echoless world. 

The Glist are also the architects of the scattered artifacts. The subtle markings, hand-hewn shapes, and delicate shards are their legacy - not constructions for shelter or survival, but expressions of their existence, signatures etched on the face of reality. These artifacts of obsidian and basalt capture the essence of ephemeral influences – a pool's echo, a temperature's dance, a beat's hum, memorialized in material form. 

To witness these beings and their subtle existence is to witness a cosmic secret - life far removed from convention, basking in the otherworldly glow beneath an unblemished sun, so alien yet so familiar. The Glist are the silent occupants of a world on the brink of void and chorus, echoes of an ancient melody that span the crystal landscapes of this ethereal splendor, breathing vitality into the frozen façade. Despite it all, life in its most abstract form marches on, stubborn and resilient, bouncing delicacies of existence off the cold hard surfaces of reality’s fringes. This is the simple essence of the Glist, lingering on the edge of understanding, stretching the boundaries of what it means to exist.
