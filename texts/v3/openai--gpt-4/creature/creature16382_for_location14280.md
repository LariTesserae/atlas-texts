The beings that reside here might best be described as Notions. Incorporeal and timeless, they move unseen within the dense weight of the cool mist, between the slick surface adornments and stone crag memories. Their existence is associated with senses rather than shapes: the fleeting impression of silence and sound within the ever-present groaning song of the water, the feathery trace of something touching the mind but not quite tangible to the fingers.

Each Notion is a unique and splendid consciousness that drifts within the cold vapor, sensing and reflecting the world around them in crystalline clarity. They do not remember, have no need for past or future - they live within a perfect echo of the present moment. Endlessly patient, they are glimmers of thought intent on observing the spectacle of this slowly evolving world. An eon is no more than a second to their ethereal existence.

They absorb, reflect, and project light in multitudes of iridescence, casting the stone surroundings and damp glistening formations into a dance of patterns. At times, they echo the inner illumination of the place’s frail anatomic organisms, growing brighter or dimming to match the rhythm of these luminescent lifeforms. At these moments, the Notions might almost be visible, a fleeting impression of opalescent shimmers within the thick, watery air.

The Notions echo the designs impressed upon the world by the water: rounded stone, liquid glistening, starlit droplets. The terrain's recordings of age and impossibly layered memories are transcribed into their ethereal minds, reverberating across the misty landscape in a discourse of whispers. They taste the cold, mineral-rich moisture with the unseen tendrils of their existence, painting their impressions across the atmosphere as wordless poetry.

Boundless and formless, the Notions inhabit the crevices and heights with equal ease. They pass through ancient stones like a dream through a sleeper, leaving no trace of their visitation other than a delicate shift in the harmonics of the air, a tiny ripple in the ever-present chorus of water. Unable to touch or manipulate their world physically, they negotiate it by ebbing and flowing along the streams of light and sound, immersing themselves in the silent chorus of this water-etched sanctum.

No single sound or image could capture their essence. They are layered echoes of thought and sense, inhabiting the ungraspable swathes of mist as a subliminal presence, profundity incarnate. With their uncanny ability to resonate with their surroundings, to delicately alter their prevailing moods and colors, the Notions seem to breathe the breath of the stone stage upon which they perform their silent and brooding dance, underwater notes seeped into every crack and crevice, time woven into their core.
