The beings that inhabit this unique world are as foreign and alien as the land itself. They are linked entities called the Trancea: quivering, pulsating life-forms that mimic the ethereal precision of the crystalline structures and mimic the molten glass of the land. Their semi-liquid forms ebb and flow, capturing the ambient light from the pervasive glowing sky and casting hues of blues, purples and silvers across their liquid bodies. 

Their existence, much like their home, is impossible to understand through conventional biological means. They live without mouths or faces, and yet perceive their world through frequencies and resonations that course through their liquid bodies. Their understanding of the world is not about seeing or hearing; it is about feeling and perceiving, the absorption of the vibrational symphony that comes from the land, the sky, and the sparse vegetation.

Trancea communicate through coded pulses of vibrational energy, transmitted across the flowing layers of their form, much like ripples spreading outwards on a pond disturbed by a fallen leaf. This coding system, as complex as it might be, is yet to be deciphered, but the rhythmic pulses spreading across a Trancea’s form unquestionably bear information that is felt and understood by the others around it.

Trancea do not move as we understand it. They remain rooted to their chosen spot, drawing sustenance in small increments from the warm air and the solidified molten glass-like soil. They exist in the perfect balance with the land, breathing in the steady energy that the ever-glowing sky emits and exhaling the essence the sparse vegetation needs to grow.

In a place where everything is built by the laws of alien geology and a budding ecosystem, Trancea embody perfect cohabitation. Their mere existence is symbiosis. Their alien forms towering above the glassy plain, the incandescent gemstones tell a story of a different kind of existence, thriving in its own rhythm, amid the pervasive quiet, heat, and stillness. A tale of life in a place where life as we know it, does not exist. They stand sentinel over the land in slow motion, observing the careful shaping of the world to its full potential.

Trancea perceive the emerging wholeness of the world, the hard rules and prolonged processes, the transformation of raw potential into a balanced whole. And they shimmer under the ambient light, conducting the symphony of a living world that is still waking up. Every pulse, every motion, every luminescent oscillation, brings this world one step closer to life teeming in all its alien complexity. For they are, in their own unique, indescribable way, the souls of this nascent world. Anchoring life in a world poised on the brink of a crescendo. Defining existence in a landscape which thrives on its emptiness and stillness.
