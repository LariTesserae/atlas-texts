The landscape is framed by grandeur, a vast sprawl of preserved antiquity residing beneath the surface of a primordial planet. Ancient in all its aspects, the terrain tells tales of profound history, each groove a testament to its timelessness, each scar a verse in its epic song of endurance. Despite the lack of water, the underground terrain thrives with immense yet sparse vegetation, life forms uniquely adapted to a world flourishing on the residues of an aged mineral-rich soil, void of aqueous nourishment.

The verdant foliage against the stony canvas isn't thick, but significant and widely scattered, creating isolated regions of density amidst an open expanse of subdued hues. Organisms twisted by great spans of time grow tall and sinuous, exuding a cool glow in the dim subterranean ambience. It’s a luminescence brewed from millennia of adapting to the darkness, leaves and stems swirling with bio-luminescent veins charting an ethereal constellation beneath the earth.

These dramatic flora echo the near-flattened chasms they inhabit, a sprawling realm etched by erosion but denying defeat, persistently enduring. Nestled amidst an environment both sparse and expansive, the vegetation stands sentinel, solemn and silent, in their isolated pockets of existence.

Human influence is surprisingly abundant, integrated subtly into the ancient subterranean fabric. The architecture, vast and grand, bearing the archaic signatures of urban design, stands as a testament to the endurance and adaptability of civilization. Yet, as if respecting their venerable surroundings, these dwellings and roadways yield to the rules of the world, showing their age with muted grace and avoiding any intrusion of modernity. There's no technological imprint, only the edifice of time-stained undressed stone and winding paths carved through the rocky terrain, honoring the rhythms and rules of pure nature.

Ethereal in its tranquility, the world below is teeming with life. Creatures of all shapes and sizes cohabit with the cityscape, with a synergy that bridges the gaps between nature and built environment. Despite the cool temperature and low light, the fauna is resilient, flourishing as they traverse the network of underground passages, feeding off the vegetation or each other, their echoes forming the overwhelming symphony of subterranean life.

In the seemingly serene slowness, the place is never truly silent. The deafening noise from the fauna play omnipotent symphonies, their cacophonous calls melding with occasional echoes of shifting rock and rustling leaves, forming the never-ending soundtrack of the underworld. While the slow, gradual geological and ecological shifts sketch the overarching notes in the symphony of this world, the myriad sounds from its inhabitants punctuate the melody with staccatos of bustling life.

Yet, even with its cacophonic profile and the quiet business of the living, the landscape is not chaotic or bizarre. It echoes the ancient and enduring spirit of a world untouched by chaos, unmarred by frenzy. It echoes normalcy in its own unique pattern, a subterranean, timeless reality as expansive as time itself. It’s normal for the deep world, for the life that has crawled, grown, existed and endured here. Ages have passed, civilizations have fallen and risen, yet through it all, the subterranean world maintains its antique serenity, accepting everything and changing minutely yet immovably, marching on, narrating the ancient and ongoing anthem of existence.
