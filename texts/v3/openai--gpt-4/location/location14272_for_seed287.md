This is a peculiar world, carved from contrasts. Below the surface, yet under a mercilessly hot sun, the landscape has borne the weight of time and the continuous dance of natural forces. It is a place where time has not hurried but sauntered leisurely, unpressured by any cosmic clocks. The atmosphere is dense and close, filled with a palpable quality that hangs heavy in the air, stifling despite the latent coolness beneath the ground.

The temperature is a consistent, permeating heat that throbs gently in the subterranean surroundings. At a high of 2.9 on a scale of 3, the air is warm and dry, providing ripe conditions for the swaths of resilient vegetation that somehow thrive below the surface. It is not a desert even with the intense heat, but an altogether different ecosystem. Echoing with the sound of silence, there is teeming life, yet it is plant life — no animals inhabit this capsuled paradise.

The terrain is marked by a curious balance between the natural and the built, the old, and the fantastically surreal. Small structures, remnants of a perhaps not-too-distant industrial past, are scattered about the landscape. They bear signs of weathering and erosion yet retain a ghost of the influence of their builders; intertwined with the determined vegetation that wraps, penetrates, and softens their steel and stone. 

The illumination is naught but a dappled, dim glow of filtered sunlight, tinting the air with a muted bronze hue. It is the light of twilight suspended in time, mystical and intermingling with the wavering heat. A score of reflective surfaces glister warmly in the murky luminosity, relics of industrial activity that once resonated through the space. They constitute pockets of civilization, faded and worn, now commandeered by the rich green that surrounds and subtly devours it.

Despite the underpinning stillness, the place is dense and bustling, paradoxically alien and familiar. Lush vegetation, stalwart against the scorching heat, grows in abandoned engine rooms, up crumbling factory walls, and along mechanized systems, now dormant. These vegetative extravagances culminate in a cathedral of greenery, layered and nearly touching the dim ceiling of this subterranean world.

The palpable dampness in the air is intermittent, manifesting in the form of occasional bouts of rain that descend from the ceiling, like tears of the ancient sky hidden far above. Mingled with the rain is a harmonic cacophony of sounds, reverberating against the confines of the hollow earth. Echoes from water droplets splatter against aged metal surfaces, plant leaves rustle amidst ancient machineries, rocks crackle and shift, speaking stories of the patient flows of time.

This is a world where the mechanized has succumbed to the natural, where the past melds with the present, silently telling tales of once primal forces that have embraced tranquility. It's disconcerting, it is bizarre, all tinged with an ambiance of otherworldly allure. It’s a slow-moving world wherein time feels distilled, unveiling itself patiently enough for those who dare to adhere. It’s a realm sculpted from the slow passage of time, the gradual dance of erosion, and the poignant silence left in the wake of departed fauna — an echo chamber of forgotten stories and surreal beauty.
