The Realmscape is a singular and uncanny world, situated on an entirely constructed plateau high above what might be termed 'the bottom'. The landscape is markedly artificial, every aspect of it is fashioned, with sparse and strange vegetation punctuating an otherwise barren land. The soil below the foot —if it can even be called soil— is a blend of bizarre minerals and fragments of glass, both common remnants of the mysterious industrial functions which were at the height of their importance long ago. That era is enshrined in a multitude of forgotten machinery that widely dots the landscape. Rusting gears, gigantic pistons, and sleeping chimneys no longer belch black smoke against the clear, luminous sky. This is an old world; a made world.

The atmosphere in the Realmscape is colder than one might be accustomed to; the icy chill of a long-forgotten forge. Still, life has sprouted from its strange substrate, taking the form of sparse vegetation that sticks out morosely against the mechanical backdrop. These lifeforms, clinging to their existence, are otherworldly, flourishing on the scarce moisture obtained from the arid air. Their appearance mimics the character of the landscape— constructed, metallic, like green machinery of photosynthesis functioning without rest.

The water is a scarce resource here, with no streams, no ponds, not even ice. Yet, periodically, a ghastly harmony resonates in the bitter air, rising further and further in its timbre. Those are the Steamdrums, gargantuan steel contraptions that stretch towards the sky, making their imposing presence known with rhythmic, shuddering beats. Famed to gather and condense moisture from the thin air, they yield but a few precious drops, a lifeline for the land’s sparse greenery. Their ceaseless thumping beat forms a deafening song that echoes among the silent machinery, underscoring the deeply ingrained desolation.

Even amidst these inanimate structures, the Realmscape is devoid of animal life. The gears and the drums resonate with a powerful rhythm, but there's no answering call from lark or linnet. A brooding silence spreads its wings between each resounding drumbeat, swallowing the remnants of sound.

In the punctuated silence, the Realmscape rests in a tranquil equilibrium. It is a chaotic order that has somehow found a delicate balance, nurturing life where none should exist. Embers of the old world and seeds of the new have interwoven a complex tapestry, a testament to a past defined by perpetual change, and a present defined by its utter absence.

The Realmscape is a wraith of an epoch, trapped in an iron-heart bottle, held in the palm of the Universe. And here, amidst the clangs of the Steamdrum, the sighs of the chill wind, and the silent whispers of ghostly machines, one would still find, living against the odds, stories that refuse to end. Welcome, traveler, to this high-plated sphere of a long-forgotten world under a common sky, where the peculiar and the primeval clasp hands. Welcome to the Realmscape.
