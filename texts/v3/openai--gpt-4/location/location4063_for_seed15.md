Welcome to Submercia, a world submerged beneath a warm ocean's surface. The light filters down in beams, the radiant energy meeting the submerged realm with open arms. Below the water's surface, the landscape mainly sits hidden from view, an intimate secret kept between the aquatic abyss and the heavens above. 

Submercia isn't just an underwater world; it's a built world. Artificial structures have grown from the sea bed, sprouting like metallic mushrooms. Their construction is primitive, forged from simple metals harnessed from the earth's crust, crafted carefully with hand tools. They exist in a sparse arrangement, maintaining equilibrium with the vast empty spaces around them. These edifices hum and buzz with the gentle symphony of machinery, providing a consistent soundtrack to the world below the waves.

Erosion hasn’t yet softened the rough corners or smoothed the angles of these structures, preserving their initial aesthetics. Crisp lines dissect portholes. Geometric embellishments flaunt the mix of art and design. Sharp-angled rooftops provide homes for the sparse vegetation that somehow finds a way to cling on, their roots digging into metal surfaces and their leaves and tendrils swaying in the warm currents. 

These terrestrial intruders remain the only green element in the monochrome blue of this underwater cityscape. The ceaseless pendulum arc of survival has yet drifted this way for endemic fauna, leaving the oceanic metropolis bereft of animal life. The ocean, simultaneously hospitable and inhospitable, holds its inhabitants close yet keeps life at a distance.

The water isn't static but fragile and dynamic. Currents come and go, nudging the structures and slowly shaping the sea floor. The subsurface tension of the warm ocean is occasionally interrupted by larger quantities of water displaced by the tide or disturbances from above. Invisible hands warp the light beams, twisting them into ethereal ribbons that ignite the micro drops suspended in the water with a hidden luminosity. 

Life here is quiet; sounds are swallowed by the vastness of the water. The only audible expressions are the distortions caused by local turbulence and the reverberations of the structures themselves, emitting resonances that percolate through the water and bounce off the metal surfaces.

Submercia is a world dominated by the rules of the sea and mankind's audacity to establish civilization where none should exist. There's a strangeness in its normalcy, a world that should be fraught with surreal tales but steadfastly remains grounded in reality. It's a world that exists comfortably below the surface, simultaneously a part of the sea yet profoundly separate from it. It's a place of juxtapositions, the flashpoint where the audacious dreams of civilization grapple with the fluid embrace of the ocean.
