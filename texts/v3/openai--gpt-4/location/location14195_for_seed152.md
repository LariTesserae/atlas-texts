A humid fog clings close to the earth, woven with languid threads of sunlight barely managing to pierce its dew-kissed veil. The atmosphere is warm, the air thick and nurturing, carrying the quiet whispers of life that saturate this largely untouched corner of the world. Rain is a regular visitor, though it often comes as a drizzle rather than a downpour, each drop a gentle caress rather than a slap to the delicate skin of the landscape.

Welcome to Deruwa - a haven within a haven, a pocket-ecosystem submerged in an oasis of greenery where fauna and flora flourish in symbiosis. Life here plays out on a verdant stage that is yet under formation, where low-lying, embryonic hills are interspersed with shallow, reflective pockets of rainwater. The landscape rolls loosely in emerald waves of foliage, punctuated occasionally by exposed swathes of raw lithos, stark and bold against the ubiquitous sea of green.

The vegetation here is lush beyond measure, ranging from the vivid, emerald-hued moss, which carpets the earth and climbs the rugged stone formations, to the tall, lazy ferns that waver softly in the warm breeze. Even the rocky patches are crisscrossed with slender veins of creeping vines, their offshoots occasionally bursting out into a riot of colourful wildflowers. It's nature’s canvas, an exercise in growth unrestrained, a testament to the resonating hum of life that thrums in the air.

Despite its rich bio-dynamic energy, Deruwa retains an air of soundlessness. The silence is punctuated occasionally by the rustle of foliage or an errant bird call, perhaps even the distant echoing splash of a creature making use of one of the natal bodies of water dotting the landscape.  The absence of technology preserves the tranquil allure of this idyllic enclave, where the songs of the wind and the rustling leaves are the best symphony.

Invisible to the negligent eye, a sense of sparse urbanity prevails in the form of colonies of termites within the earth and beehives carefully tucked away amidst the verdant canopy overhead. These miniaturized cities boast an infrastructure echoed within the larger environment, offering a glimpse into the life of the hidden inhabitants of Deruwa.

Dim light filters through the verdant umbrella above, creating a tranquil play of shadows and light dancing quietly upon the earth, animating the static scenery. Life here is abundant—bugs, small mammals, birds, and even countless hidden microorganisms cycling life from death and back again, the heart pulsing beneath the verdant skin of Deruwa.

Deruwa may be a pocket-land, nestled within a larger context, but here, nestled amongst the quiet symphony of life, the world feels as infinite as the fog-laden horizon, blurring the lines where earthly landscapes meet the moody grey-blue of the sky. Normalcy prevails in its tranquillity, and yet, for anyone fortunate enough to find themselves in Deruwa’s soft embrace, a subtle but undeniable feeling of weirdness persists. Because in a world constantly barrelling towards the future, places like Deruwa are an unexpected window to the past, a reminder of what the world was, is, and could be.
