This world is both cold and vast, mountainous in parts but largely high moorland and expansive plains cloaked in a layer of thick frost and ice. Across the planet, wind whips over endless white landscapes, through high, narrow canyons and across wide plateaus, driving sharp pellets of frost and snow before it. The sound is a constant howl, a deafening roar that is as much a part of this world as the ice and the stone.

Although the world is largely barren and the icy atmosphere allows for little moisture, water exists here in a peculiar form. Aquifers deep beneath the permafrost, heated by the planet's inner geothermal energy, occasionally erupt through the surface in heated geysers. These eruptions create temporary oases of warmth in the frigid landscape, triggering blooms of hardy, cold-resistant vegetation that are as quick to wilt and freeze as they are to grow. Around these oddities, the frost underfoot is a little softer, and sometimes, at night, the steam from the water in the air condenses and freezes in an almost luminous display of lights.

Pockets of pockets of urbanity dot the landscape, their huddled forms lit by harsh artificial lights against a backdrop of perpetual twilight. The inhabitants of this world have embraced industrialization wholeheartedly, their settlements clusters of steam towers and factory blocks, the outer shells hardened against the cold. Inside, the livings are a hubbub of mechanized industry and scaled production, where steam from the heated water runs engines and machinery, melting through the layers of frost.

The presence of these urban areas are at dramatic odds with their surroundings. They sit there, stark and surprising, artificial anomalies in an otherwise bleak, natural world. They are centers of noise amidst silence, of artificial light in a world of twilight, their soft haloed glows only adding to the world’s weirdness.

Looming over each urban center, massive constructs silently harvest the layers of frost, converting the ice and stone into usable material. In part, this world echoes a dystopian, industrial era, even though few organic beings are present. 

Weird as it is, there is something mesmerizing about this world. It is not a gradient. It is, in its biting cold and deafening silence, a frozen equilibrium. “Emptiness” might be its middle name, for it is characterized by the hollow lure of its white plains and the vast spaces between its urban settlements. There is no fauna to disrupt its silent status quo, no tread of the wild animal or songs of the birds to fill its cold, massive presence. Just the relentless wind, the odd eruption of a geyser, the sibilant hush of the frost, and the constant churn and grind of industry. It is a world in equilibrium, a paradox frozen in time amidst the icy wilderness.
