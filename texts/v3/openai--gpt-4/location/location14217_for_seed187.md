Low and long, the landscape sprawls outwards, a legacy of vast ages of erosion that have left its surfaces near-to-flat, any once-jutting rocks or precipitous valleys now reduced to gentle rises and dips in the land. This lack of vertical elevation, combined with the small and intimate scale of the area, gives it a strange sense of being self-contained, an almost room-like space despite its lack in buildings or man-made structures. 

Above, the light conditions are in a constant state of normalcy, neither too bright nor too dim. A steady stream of light consistently illuminating the verdant expanse of the landscape. Despite the serenity that this constant light offers, it is at odds with the otherwise dynamic character of the place, for it is a never-changing landscape, frozen in an equilibrium, with its yarns of lives and times interwoven into each humble hillock or water-worn stone.

Vegetation reigns supreme overtaking every conceivable inch of this ancient plane. It's as if the earth herself is green, creating a plant-as-substrate environment where even rocks, every little crevice appears to be teeming with life, with mosses, lichens and tiny flowering plants clinging stubbornly to its surfaces. This high-density floral tapestry creates a unique ecology, which, in combination with the cool and intermittently moist conditions, breeds an abundance of fauna. 

A myriad of creatures call this place home. At every turn, life persists, clinging steadfastly to this plant-drenched world. From the gentle hum of bees collecting nectar, the whispering slither of small reptiles and the distant chatter from a myriad of unseen avian residents - the concerto of life resonates loudly in contrast with the visual stillness, echoing in the vast voids between the isolated pockets of water and larger expanse of dry vegetation. 

Although untouched by human hands and devoid of any constructed structures, one can perceive a sense of industrial energy. It comes not from factories or engines, but from the mechanisms of nature itself. The industrious fluttering of insects, the relentless growth of plants, the ceaseless cycle of decay and regeneration - a different sort of industry, yet no less compelling. 

And yet, despite the near-constant hum of life and the verdant expanse, there is a deep sense of emptiness woving through this place — an eerie tranquillity that cloaks the landscape. It's neither unwelcoming nor foreboding, but simply an artifact of its ancient nature — a silent testament to its age-old existence, unchanging yet ever-alive. As though the land itself exhales emptiness, a quiet sigh reverberating in the cool air, whispering tales of its primordial past to those who dare to listen. It's in this emptiness that the real charm of the landscape lies, endearing visitors with its normalcy but entrancing them with its living antiquity.
  
This is what it is to exist in this place — a rhythmic dance of normality and singularity, of quiet empty silence and raucous abundant life, of primordial essence served on a fresh platter of thriving flora and fauna. It's a testament of nature's endurance, a beacon of biotic resilience and raw life-force amidst an otherwise unadorned world.
