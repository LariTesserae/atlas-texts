Welcome to the Living City of Thallomere.

Rising high in the cooling elevations is a state between the solid terra firma and a damp above where the water meets the sky. An amalgamation of the hand of nature and the careful cultivation of its inhabitants have given rise to Thallomere, a city carved not from stone or wood but living, breathing vegetation. Here, plant-life is not just in abundance, it is the very building block of everyday life, the canvas on which the artist paints.

The plants of Thallomere are ubiquitous, reaching up towards the clouded heavens to form the towering structures that form the city. The trees grow thick and tall, their branches interweaving to form the inward scaffolding of buildings, creating cathedral-scale spaces that house the city's residents. Here, a room may have walls made of vines, its floor padded by soft moss, and a ceiling dappling sunlight through a lattice of leaves. A wild orchestra of the heartiest of greens, wrapped in a vivid array of nature's finest spectrums, and deepened by the enveloping, persistent moisture in the air.

The damp air drips with the sweet scent of the growth, the wetness almost tangible in the omnipresent plant-life. The gentle mist condenses on emerald leaves, dripping onto the surface below and creating an ambient symphony of irregularly timed droplets. Water, in Thallomere, is no less than the lifeblood, coursing through the green arteries of the city, nourishing it. It bubbles up from springs, journeys through channels cut in the greenery, and falls in soft rain from the moss-laden trellises above.

The city is dense both in terms of physical trees and undergrowth and a bustling population that calls this living maze of vegetation home. The inhabitants have become masters of a primitive technology, finding uses for hard bark and supple vine where others would seek metal and plastic. They wield simple tools made from shaped stone and hardened wood, tools that have been used for countless generations to shape Thallomere into their thriving home, ensuring a tightly knit balance with the ecosystem they're part of.

The fauna in Thallomere is as varied and vibrant as its people and their plant-made abodes. Creatures of all descriptions exist here in an abundance that veers on the overwhelming. Thick with the calls, chirps, roars, and cries of a hundred different species, the air vibrates relentlessly with the sounds of life in a fever-pitch of activity. Yet, like everything else in this city, even the overwhelming is just another note in this harmonious melody—a hand-painted stroke in this thousand shades of green canvas.

It's a labyrinthine city of organised chaos, ever shifting, ever dynamic. Trees grow, leaves wilt, new buildings sprout and old ones decompose. The chaotic change is not a sign of decline, but rather the rhythmic pulse of the city. For every branch that drops, a shoot sprouts, for every tree that withers, a sapling takes its place. An organic cycle of creation and destruction that ensures Thallomere never truly looks the same two days in a row. 

In a world for otherwise strange, the normalcy of Thallomere stands as its own form of weirdness. Yet, despite the ever-present cycle of flourishing and decay, there's a sense of permanence in this place. Erosion has given it an outlook of being here since time immemorial—the bark is time-worn, the earth compacted from ages of footfall—giving it the character of a city that has always been and always will be.

The Living City of Thallomere is strange and alluring, a dense interweaving of life in an ever-shifting canvas of vegetation, water, and a cacophonous symphony of sounds. It's a testament to nature's craft and the possibility of coexistence—a monument not built by hands but grown from roots, an example of a dynamic cycle that breathes life into every crevice and a testament to adaptability in the face of overwhelming lushness.
