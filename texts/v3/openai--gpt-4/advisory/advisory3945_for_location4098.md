The multiverse traveler embarking on a journey to this alien, deeply chaotic pocket realm should be mentally prepared to experience a world unlike any within their typical understanding. Known rules and principles may not apply—expect the unexpected. To get ready for this journey:

### Adaptability

Given the constant changes in the environment, adaptability is crucial for survival. Mental and physical flexibility is key. Try to let go of preconceived notions about physics and reality as it will not follow common laws. Travelling in groups should be avoided due to the nature of the chaotic currents. 

### Environment Suit

An environment suit, preferably equipped with advanced adjustable flotation devices, is an absolute necessity. This will allow the traveler to navigate the dense aqueous atmosphere without hindrance. Additionally, it must help the wearer breath this unusual atmospheric composition comfortably, ensuring your respiratory system is not distressed by the alien elemental cocktail. 

### Nutrition and Hydration

Bring digestible and nutrient-dense food that won't get compromised by the humid environment. It would be prudent to carry water, despite the abundance of the liquid medium around you—ingesting the native fluids comes with unknown risks. 

### Light and Sound

The constantly changing light conditions necessitate adaptive eyewear that assures optic protection while optimizing visibility. Ear protection might also be useful, given the overwhelming nature of the auditory environment. 

### Navigation

Since the landscape's topography constantly shifts, navigation devices working with satellite signals could be ineffective. Instead, utilize seismic and sonar technology to understand the ever-changing geology and track changes in water currents in real time.

### Communication Devices

Given the potentially sentient life forms, bring equipment capable of processing and emitting a diversity of signals—thermal, electromagnetic, sonic, and beyond. It might help with communication or at least sense the presence of local inhabitants.

### Documenting Tools

In such a unique and dynamically shifting world, observations and their precise recordings are of vital importance. Therefore, take with you sophisticated devices capable of recording high-quality audio and visual data. 

### Defense Mechanisms

Although the fauna appeared to be non-aggressive, remember that not all interactions in this world might be peaceful. Be equipped with non-lethal defense mechanisms that can work efficiently in dense, semi-liquid environments. 

### Overnight Shelter

Carry inflatable, waterproof sheltering equipment that can adapt to changing ground and atmospheric conditions. These accommodations will offer you protection during rest and repair cycles. 

It's critical to remember to respect this alien setting, treating it and any inhabitants with care and curiosity. Like an anthropologist landing in an uncontacted tribe’s territory, an inter-dimensional tourist in this volatile realm should aim to observe, learn and, if possible, quietly integrate.
