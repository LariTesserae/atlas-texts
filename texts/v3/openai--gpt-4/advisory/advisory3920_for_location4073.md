For someone planning on visiting Droplet, you need to take into account several things:

1. Clothing: Due to the constant dampness, you should be equipped with moisture-wicking clothing and waterproof outer layers. Considering the cool, misty ambiance, ensure you have sufficient insulation to maintain body heat. Clothing with built-in bioluminescent patches could be handy for navigation in the constant darkness of this alien landscape.

2. Supplies: Pack lightweight, space-efficient food supplies that require minimal preparation. The ecosystem on Droplet relies heavily on invertebrates and microorganisms, which might not suit human consumption.

3. Equipment: Due to the lack of sunlight, carry a reliable source of light - preferably a lamp capable of projecting light of different wavelengths to illuminate the various spectra reflected by the crystalline minerals. A sturdy, waterproof tent with built-in heating functionality may also come in handy.

4. Scientific Gear: The extraordinary fauna of Droplet warrants research and observation. Pack necessary scientific instrumentation like a microscope and sample-collection tools. An audio recorder would be indispensable to capture the rich aural environment.

5. Personal Safety: Considering the dense population of invertebrates and alien organisms, some type of gentle repellant or shield that doesn't harm the ecosystem, but keeps these organisms at bay, would be advantageous.

6. Mobility: A lightweight, sturdy means of transportation capable of handling the sharp, rocky terrain and shallow ponds might be crucial for exploring Droplet. Waterproof boots with grips suited for navigating slippery volcanic glass are also a must-have.

7. Communication: With minimal to no advanced technology in place, carry a standalone, robust communication device that doesn't rely on any local networks or resources.

8. Physical Conditioning: Be prepared for the constant dampness and cool temperatures. Ensure you're in a good state of health as the alien environment could potentially weigh down on your immunity.

Finally, it’s pivotal that every visitor maintains great respect for the unique ecosystem of Droplet and commits to leaving it absolutely undisturbed. This world is to be observed and learned from, not exploited or altered. Always remember, as a multiverse traveler, you are the ambassador for humanity, and your behavior should reflect that responsibility.
