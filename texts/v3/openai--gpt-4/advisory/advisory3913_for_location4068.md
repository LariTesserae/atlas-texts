Before embarking on a journey to visit these primeval highlands, an ordinary human multiverse traveler must be prepared both physically and mentally. 

Firstly, as the landscape describes an environment lacking in water, maintaining hydration is of utmost importance. Bring ample packed water supplies and a water purification system capable of extracting and purifying atmospheric moisture. Due to the mineral-rich environment, consider bringing test strips to check for safe levels of mineral concentration in any water obtained.

Due to the harsh climate and abrasive terrain, durable, preferably high-tech clothing is essential. This should include reinforced boots for navigating the rugged stone, protective gloves, and various layers featuring cooling and heating technologies to adapt to changing temperatures. Since the environment suggests high altitudes, oxygen supplies may be necessary, and altitude sickness medication could be beneficial.

You will need secure accommodations. Owing to the desolate environment, it is advised to carry a tent or portable shelter made from robust material resistant to strong winds and potential rock abrasions. This should be lightweight yet sturdy, and capable of efficient insulation, keeping in mind the potential for extreme temperature fluctuations.

The peculiar wildlife of the highlands, although entrancing, may pose threats. Carry a field guide – either a physical book or digital guide with robust protective casing – to identify and deal with local fauna. Be prepared with standard first-aid materials and perhaps even self-defense tools to repel potentially aggressive species. 

For nourishment, pack lightweight and nutrient-dense food, such as freeze-dried meals and energy bars. Given the scarcity of vegetation or evident life forms, one should not rely on hunting or foraging for food in this environment.

The highlands are remarkably quiet, which may prove disorienting or even psychologically distressing. Consider carrying noise-canceling headphones and a variety of familiar music, podcasts, or audio books to provide occasional relief from the intense silence. 

As light dynamics play a significant role in the environment, quality eyewear is crucial to shield against sunlight reflecting off the stones. Night vision equipment may also be necessary, particularly if nocturnal activities or research are planned.

Finally, mental preparation is just as important as physical. Being oriented in such an ancient landscape can easily provoke feelings of isolation, ensnarement, and cause potential claustrophobia. As such, an interest in meditation or similar calming practices might be beneficial. A multiverse traveler should also prepare for the profound silence by spending prior time in silent retreats or similar quiet environments.

In conclusion, while these ancient highlands present a truly unique travel experience, planning a visit to such an environment entails thoughtful preparation, ensuring one has the necessary tools and the mental readiness to navigate and survive in this daunting, beautiful, time-encrusted landscape.
