Before embarking on a journey to this strange land caught between the throes of birth and a paradoxical death, a multiverse traveler must meticulously prepare. The core of your journey revolves around the understanding that you're venturing into a world where earthy nature intertwines with an industrial ethos, a militant march of progress colliding with the raw, unsettled chaos of creation. 

1. Clothing and gear: 
Given the landscape's haphazard roughness and irregular geometry, sturdy hiking boots with excellent grip would be a necessity to avoid identical missteps on high ledges and steep cliffs. Dress in multiple breathable layers. You're venturing into a cool, dense atmosphere that might turn chilly, and the presence of steam from the machines indicates potential fluctuations in temperature. Integrated helmets with a strong headlight could help penetrate the dimness, while UV-protective goggles could shield your eyes from crisp flashes of sparks and harsh bioluminescence. 

2. Tools: 
Climbing gear would be essential. Ropes, carabiners, and pitons would be used to navigate the sheer cliffs and steeps. A navigation device with 3D mapping capabilities and an accurate inclinometer could help calculate the precarious heights and judge distances across the stretching valleys. 

3. Air supply: 
Given the thick air, heavy with the smell of damp minerals, moss, and steaming machines, a portable oxygen device could aid in breathing if the air becomes too dense or noxious. Additionally, pack a reliable air quality monitor to ensure the air is safe to breathe, especially near the machines.

4. Communication: 
You will need some means of long-distance or inter-dimensional communication. This territory is vast, wild, and potentially hazardous. A signal booster or a satellite communicator might help, but also carry an emergency beacon.

5. Food and water:
Pack high-energy food bars and portable food-making equipment. Include a trusted water purification system if you need to rely on natural springs or the damp dripping off the caves and mosses.

6. Research: 
Before your journey, extensively research the denizen life forms and their behaviors. Knowing how to identify and avoid harmful biologies, or how to navigate and work within the machine-infested areas could mean the difference between survival and disaster.

7. Documenting materials:  
Carrying a visual recording device could help you document and analyze this world. This will not only allow you to reflect upon your journeys but may provide crucial insights into this negotiable world's regularities and inconsistencies.

Set yourself to venture into this uncanny place with the humbling awareness that you are stepping into a realm where life as we know it blurs into machinery and where reality itself bends and sways, challenging your concepts of solidity and permanence. Embrace the unexpected and be ready for the extraordinary dance of the natural and the artificial as they weave in and out of each other's existence. 

Above all, walk with respect and caution, for you are on the precipice of an ever-changing world that flickers between being a nascent entity and an antiquated relic of industrialization. Always remember you're an observer, a respectful traveler passing through a shifting reality where the lines between swarming life and mechanical monstrosities are not just blurred, but rather, they whimsically swap personas. Keep your senses sharp, stay cognizant, and adapt—a vital characteristic to bear within this fluctuating world.
