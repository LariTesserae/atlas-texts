In preparation for a trip to this unfeeling yet teeming plateau, there are several points to consider. Firstly, clothing should be suitable for harsh and cold conditions, while providing protection against the terrain's raw materials. Footwear should have excellent grip for walking on the unpredictable and ever-changing surfaces. 

Next, ensure one has appropriate respiratory equipment. The air, though not toxic, can be heavy with mineral content and the scent of sulfur, which may be difficult for a newcomer to adjust to. Some sort of filtration mask may be optimal if your system is not used to tolerating such airborne elements. Expect limited visibility due to the consistent mist, so prepare to navigate using non-visual cues such as vibro-compasses or sonar mapping devices. 

Your kit should also include photography equipment, for this land offers countless opportunities to capture its unique flora, fauna, and atmospheric phenomena. Additionally, basic geological tools would be beneficial to gather and analyze samples of the soil, rocks, and strange metallic relics dotted across the landscape.

Try to minimize potential interference with the native wildlife. Tranquilizer darts or deterrent sprays may be necessary, considering the presence of predatory creatures. In any case, avoid feeding or disrupting the delicate balance of this nascent ecosystem.

Additionally, do not forget basic survival gear including first-aid, portable thermal blankets, water purification tablets or a portable desalinating device as drinking the iron-rich waters directly may be harmful. High energy nutrition packs will aid survival in an environment that otherwise offers limited edible resources.

It's essential to carry a personal data device to record the faint tremors and industrial-sounding noises that pervade the area. This data could later be analyzed to better understand what lies under the land's new skin.

Lastly, since this environment is prone to lightning, ensure electronic devices are insulated or feature safeguards against electrical interference. A portable shelter would also be beneficial to provide cover during bouts of unpredictable weather or sudden geological events. 

Lastly, it is recommended that travelers maintain a journal. Detail not just the tangible aspects of the environment, but the emotions and psychological shifts experienced in the desolation and strangeness of the plateau. It’s not just about surviving this world—it’s about understanding our place within it, and how the liminal landscape affects us and changes our perception.

Traveling to this place is not about conquering the unfeeling wilderness, but about understanding the harmonious relationship between the environment and the creatures that inhabit it. Being prepared for this journey is as much a mental endeavor as it is physical. Embrace the eerie beauty and embrace the solitude as you traverse this realm between the ancient and the newly born.
