Traveling to this strange location in the multiverse demands both physical and mental preparedness due to several factors including the peculiar landscape, extraordinary atmospheric conditions, abundant microbial life forms, and most importantly, the distortions of reality.

Physical preparations should include apparel designed to withstand extreme cold given the chill pervasive across the landscape. Your attire should provide mobility for traversing the harsh terrain marked by ridged mountains and plateaus. Invest in a solid pair of boots that can withstand the rough landscape and bear the weight of the microscopic life forms that twinkle beneath your feet.

Since light behaves in a peculiar manner, always offering a soft radiance that is neither daylight nor darkness, eye protection that adjusts to varying light intensity would be a necessity. A light source might be required for illuminating the surroundings if the soft radiance proves inadequate, especially when navigating weathers.

Carrying water purification equipment will be crucial, considering water scarcity and the unpredictable nature of water sources. Also, keep high-energy, easy-to-consume food items. The peculiar absence of fauna suggests hunting is not an option, and the alien vegetation may not be safe for consumption.

You must also pack tools and materials to facilitate shelter, in case you can't access or use the existing strange architecture, which defy the laws of gravity and bend into improbable shapes. A reliable source of heat, too, will be paramount to ward off the cold.

Moreover, it's best to carry scientific equipment that can study quantum anomalies, ascertain the safety of resources, and examine the unfamiliar flora and microbiota for potential hazards or utilitarian purposes. 

Since this world manipulates reality, breaking down the familiar, understanding your environment will be a practical necessity and a survival tool in itself. Therefore, you need to be theoretically robust about the science that guides multiverse travel. This includes quantum mechanics, theoretical physics, and possibly alien biology - disciplines that can help rationalize the unusual phenomena and ensure safer navigation.

Acquiring some knowledge of geology would be beneficial, as would skills in mountaineering or hiking. Understanding the signs of weather changes will also prove vital in this world where the passage of time is ambiguous.

Be prepared for solitude. With silent expanses devoid of regular fauna, combat loneliness by bringing along books, music, games, or hobbies that require minimal resources.

Furthermore, mental preparedness is imperative. The distortion of reality against the background of a very primitive, elemental nature of this world can unsettle even the most seasoned travelers. Adapting to such unpredictable and surreal surroundings will require mental stamina. Meditation and mindfulness practices, or even therapeutic interventions such as cognitive-behavioral techniques beforehand, could help strengthen your mental resilience.

Finally, be ready to journal and document everything - the queer terrain, the time-warped structures, the sounds and, shadows, the neon lichens, the haunted skies. This place is an anomaly, a realm of ancient tales and quantum illusions, and every fragment of this world captured will be of immense scientific interest, and help future explorers navigate this wild corner of the multiverse.
