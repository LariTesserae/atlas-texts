To prepare for a visit to Frigus-Aqua, a multiverse traveler must consider several factors.

1. Weather Gear: Due to the harsh cold conditions, it is essential to pack clothes that will keep you warm and protect your skin from getting frostbite. Invest in a high-quality insulated parka, gloves, thermal pants, and sturdy boots that can handle wet and icy terrains. Layering can be effective for managing the varied temperature zones resulting from the steamy geothermal pools.

2. Food and Water Filtering Supplies: The traveler should bring their own food and a portable water filter, given the lack of accessible food and drinkable water sources. 

3. Bioluminescence Protective Goggles: Considering the abundant bioluminescent life, a pair of protective goggles would be essential to safeguard the eyes from the high-intensity light emitted by the fireflies, rodents, and frogs.

4. Local Interactions: Given that the Frigorians are a primitive civilization, patience and a basic understanding of nonverbal communication will be beneficial for a traveler seeking to interact with the locals. Carrying small trinkets for trade might also be an acceptable way to engage with locals and access their help or knowledge.

5. Wildlife Safeguards: Given the unique but potentially unpredictable wildlife, carry a sturdy, heat-resistant tent for shelter and portable fencing for protection against wandering lifeforms. Don't forget to carry a basic first aid kit in case of encounters with unexpected elements of the ecosystem.

6. Transportation: Given the marshy terrain around the hot springs and geothermal pools, it would be best to pack lightweight but secure snowshoes or skis to navigate through the mountains. Rope and climbing gear will also be necessary due to the hilly, uneven terrains.

7. Noise Reduction Gear: The only sounds in Frigus-Aqua are delicate and quiet, so equipping yourself with noise-canceling headphones or earplugs could provide relief if the silence becomes too oppressive.

8. Writer's Journal or Drawing Pad: The hybrid landscape of frosty cold and steamy warmth, glowing fauna, and snow-crusted vegetation is a feast for the senses. You might want to record your experiences and impressions for posterity.

A trip to Frigus-Aqua will certainly be filled with wonder and challenges. As you prepare, remember that your resilience, adaptive thinking, and open mind are crucial tools for exploring this unique destination. Embrace the contrasts and serene beauty of Frigus-Aqua, and like the native flora and fauna, seize the opportunity to thrive in a seemingly inhospitable yet deeply harmonious environment.
