Preparation and precautions are essential for any multiverse traveler intending to visit this ethereal high-altitude, barren but not lifeless environment. Let’s break it down.

First and foremost, due to the high elevation and thin air, prepare for altitude sickness. Acclimatize in stages if possible and consider medication if you're prone to this type of ailment. Carry supplemental oxygen to assist in the adjustment. Due to the potential harsh brightness of the sun, proper eye protection like polarized sunglasses is vital. Sun exposure might be severe, so bring UV-blocking high SPF sunscreen and protective clothing. 

Hydration could be an issue. Although water is abundant, it is chemically strange and not suitable for human consumption. Don’t forget to bring plenty of your water or devices to purify strange water, just in case it may work on these particular substances. Electrolyte replenishment solutions could also prove helpful.

The chilly cold layer just beneath the harsh sunlight suggests a need for layered dressing—insulated, moisture-wicking clothing that can be easily put on or removed as temperatures oscillate quickly. Insulated footwear, thermal gloves, and head cover are also advised. Survival tools like thermal blankets and hand warmers would be handy to carry as well since you could be exposed to rapid temperature changes.

Navigational equipment, preferably ones enhanced for multiverse travels may be required as it seems easy to lose one's way among the jagged geometry of rocks, crevices, and cliffs. Since the falling cliffs border dangerous voids, harnesses and rock climbing gear might be included to ensure safety and stability. 

Communications equipment should be checked for proper working conditions in multiple dimensions. Be prepared for the possibility of unusual frequency interference from the strange procedures the environment undertakes.

As this location is bereft of any known life forms but exhibits signs of ancient habitation or activity, a basic knowledge of archaeology might also be beneficial. Pack tools for safe exploration and documentation of artifacts, especially from a respectful, non-interfering standpoint that preserves the integrity of all that is found.

Scientists or individuals with a scientific bent might find this place fascinating, so collecting samples could become a priority. Therefore, having a vast array of specimen containers, including controlled environments for the unusual fungal networks or lichen, would be useful.

Light refracts oddly here, so photographic equipment should be adapted to this environment to document the journey correctly. Traditional cameras might not catch the nuances of this place, so specialized lenses or photographic techniques could be needed.

And finally, due to the persistent undercurrent of strange, and borderline supernatural occurrences, mental preparation would be crucial. A meditation routine or familiarity with techniques to center and ground oneself can be helpful to prevent panic or disorientation in the face of surreal experiences. Accept that you might encounter phenomena beyond your current understanding—you are, after all, a multiverse traveler. Make peace with the sensations and stay focused on the tangible elements of your exploration.

In summary, to visit this post-life or pre-life terra firma, an individual should prepare as for a high altitude terrestrial climb but anticipate and pack for the many variances offered by multiverse exploration—alien chemical properties, oscillating temperatures, and the mysteries of ancient civilization. Also, do carry a healthy respect and curiosity for the idiosyncrasies of this solemn, enigmatic world you're about to step foot in.
