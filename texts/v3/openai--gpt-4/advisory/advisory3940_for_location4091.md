To travel to a world in unpredictable flux, a multiverse traveler needs to be as versatile and fluid as the shifting environment itself. Here is a guide on how to prepare:

1. **Physical Condition**: Though the exact physical demands of this world are indeterminable given its inherent affairs, it is crucial to optimize your physical health and stamina. Increase cardiovascular and strength training, as every exercise can be beneficial.

2. **Vaccination and Immunity Boosters**: Since the atmosphere and environment are unique, normal immunizations would not suffice. Furthermore, the unknown air composition might bring unprecedented infectious agents. Consult with a multiverse physician for the rarest vaccines and immunity boosters.

3. **Breathing Equipment**: Given the air's dense yet sparse nature, regular oxygen might not be available or in a breathable state. Carry multiple high-tech oxygen tanks and wearable filtration systems. Conduct regular maintenance to prevent failure.

4. **Insulated, Adaptive Clothing**: As the environment is characterized by extreme heat, your attire needs to be insulated and heat-resistant. Given occasional toxic mists, ideally, it should also be made of impermeable material. Lightweight clawed footwear will help in moving across the rugged, ever-shifting landscape.

5. **Nourishment and Hydration**: Since this world lacks water sources and edible vegetation, carry a significant supply of long-lasting, energy-dense food and a purer form of hydration. As the water might get accidentally contaminated, possess handheld purification devices and reusable hydration packs.

6. **First Aid Kit**: You might get injured due to the hostile terrain or alien organisms. Therefore, a first aid kit containing antiseptic wipes, bandages, painkillers, and anti-radiation pills is essential.

7. **Navigation Equipment**: Considering the overwhelming elevation, recesses, and inconstant topography, bring advanced mapping and tracking tools, preferably a magnetic field tracker for spontaneous changes detection. These devices should be able to function without a stable satellite link.

8. **Illumination Devices**: To maneuver through the dim-lit world, carry high-strength, compact light sources. 

9. **Survival Tools**: Ice axes, climbing ropes, and other mountaineering equipment will assist in dealing with the extreme verticality of the alien landscape.

10. **Multiverse Communication Device**: Constant communication with the home base is paramount for unforeseen emergencies.

11. **Photography Device**: Take pictures and record videos to understand and dissect the surroundings better later on.

While packing, remember to keep everything compact and lightweight. Overpacking might slow you down, which could be life-threatening in an ever-diverging environment. 

Lastly, mental agility is just as important as physical resilience. Since the new world doesn't afford the luxury of stillness or predictability, your psychological strength and adaptability will be obligatory to deal with the plethora of impending challenges and irreducible anxieties. Regular meditation and cognitive behavioural therapy sessions would be productive.

Bear in mind, visiting a world like this is an extreme form of exploration — an exquisite challenge. So, equip yourself to the teeth. Good luck, and safe travels!
