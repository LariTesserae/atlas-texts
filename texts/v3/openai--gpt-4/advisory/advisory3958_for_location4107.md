Your trip to this unique subterranean metropolis demands more mindful preparation than the average multiverse leap. Here are some suggestions for what you may wish to pack and how to best prepare yourself for this descent into a world unseen by most:

1. **Clothing**: Since the climate is perpetually damp and cold, pack clothing that can keep you warm without you getting wet. A waterproof parka or raincoat, thermal layers, waterproof gloves, and boots and non-cotton clothing (which dry faster) are a good start. You should also carry plenty of extra socks.

2. **Lighting equipment**: An essential part of your luggage should be various types of handheld, sturdy, and waterproof lighting equipment for ease of navigation and for observing the beautiful stonework, carvings, and other structures in their full glory. Consider headlamps for hands-free use, waterproof flashlights, and even glowsticks. Extra batteries and dynamo (hand-powered) ones will also be a wise addition.

3. **Camera equipment**: If permitted by local guidelines, it would be a pity not to save some memories from this unseen world. Waterproof your equipment before leaving – your shots will reward you with memories of your visit to an unforgettable landscape, its glow, and complexly hewn structures.

4. **Navigation tools**: GPS may not work underground, so be sure to bring a reliable compass and map. Studying the city layout before your visit can also be helpful, but beware – the dense labyrinthine city might have areas not featured on maps.

5. **Food and water**: Carry plenty of bottled water and non-perishable food items as the local water could hold unknown substances, and food might not be readily available. A water-purification pen or tablets might also be helpful.

6. **Health and safety**: Climbing and spelunking equipment may also be necessary for certain areas of exploration. Include a first-aid kit equipped for cold and damp environments and possibly minor injuries from contact with rocky surfaces. Ensure all vaccinations are up-to-date and seek consultation with a doctor familiar with multiverse travel.

7. **Reading and Linguistic**: Learn as much as you can about this civilization before visiting. Understanding their history, habits, art, and basic words from their language could provide a deeper connection. Don't forget to follow any provided rules or guidelines to avoid offending or causing harm to the rare subterranean creatures dwelling within.

8. **Guided tours**: If available, a guided tour can provide invaluable information that only a local would know. Especially in dense, intricate cities like these, having a knowledgeable guide can significantly enhance your experience.

9. **Patience and Contemplation**: Lastly, but crucially, bring a willingness to listen and observe. Beyond the physical preparation, understanding this place requires a mental and emotional readiness to listen to the echoing sounds, the shimmering city, and the whispers of millennia past in the dripping water.

10. **Climate Adjustments**: Your body may require time to adjust to the chill and constant dampness. Start acclimating your body prior to visiting will make your visit much more enjoyable.

In conclusion, the best preparation will involve a combination of practical gear, meticulous study, and intuitive readiness to respect, accept, and immerse in this deep, silent, and ancient metropolis. Always remember, the quality of your visit depends mostly on how receptive and prepared you are to understand, respect and observe the otherness, solidity, and ancientness of this beautifully damp cityscape.
