Visiting this ancient plateau that defies the conventions of the known universe is no small feat, and requires extensive preparation on the part of the multiverse traveler. With a landscape that is profoundly otherworldly and unfamiliar, your intuition and past experiences won't be much help. The following are the things you need to prepare for your visit.

Apparel: Given that water is not only absent but irrelevant, you should eschew traditional space station or interstellar travel suit which are typically designed to trap moisture. Instead, wear a suit that is thermo-regulated and breathable, allowing body heat to dissipate effectively. Your apparel should be resistant to the soft glow of the plateau's bio-luminescent plants and the harsh reality of a waterless environment. Sealed, sturdy boots are essential to protect your feet from the shifting ground.

Equipment: You'll need specialized breathing apparatus to filter the unknown air particles, as what constitutes breathable atmosphere is up for question. Additionally, strong boots designed to handle the fibrous ground without breaking its surface are necessary. A long-range scanning device would allow you not only to discern the elements of living architecture but to navigate the intricate networks of corridors and platforms. 

Health and Safety: Protective eyewear that can adjust for varying light conditions including the plateau’s dimness and also the lights radiated from the spires is imperative to avoid eye strain or injury. Given the complex soundscape, ear protection that filters and controls the sound levels could be vital to prevent potential damage to your hearing.

Research and Study: Invest considerable time in studying the ecosystem's behavioral mechanism to understand the movements, bioluminescence, strange harmonies, and high-speed vibrations indicative of certain processes or potentially dangerous situations. The biomechanics and behavior of the local fauna should also be an area of focus for your research.

Resources and Supplies: Carry portable nutrient synthesizers, designed to convert local resources into food and drinks. Bottled oxygen is a must-have, ensuring you can breathe if the filter fails or the atmosphere becomes too thin. Your emergency kit should include bandages, medical nanobots, and field surgical tools, given the potentials of the local flora and fauna behaving unexpectedly or aggressively.

Technology: You will need a high-end translating device to try and understand the local dialect if any. Apart from such devices, a multi-frequency communicator which could allow you to stay in contact with your home base or fellow travelers should be carried at all times.

Precautions: Do not venture too far into the maze-like structures without first charting a return route. Avoid direct contact with the glowing orbs; even if they are not dangerous, they likely play a critical role in the ecosystem that you do not want to disturb. Be aware of old 'infrastructure': glowing nodes could be a form of historical record or communication, nano-machines might be operating on unknown directives, and even the seemingly vacant paths could be alive in ways we fail to recognize.

It should be noted that this journey isn’t for the faint-hearted or for the vacation-seeker. It is primarily for biologists, explorers, scientists interested in alien life forms, and of course, the most daring and well-prepared multiverse traveler. Careful logistic planning, study, and preparation are required for safety and to ensure the visitor does not disrupt the alien plateau's existing system.
