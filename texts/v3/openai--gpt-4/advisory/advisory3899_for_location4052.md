Embarking on a journey to such a mysterious realm will require a well-thought-out set of preparations. The following provide a guide to how such a traveler should equip themselves:

1. Clothing: Multilayered insulation clothing that strongly resists dampness will be vital to keep warm as the atmosphere is cold and damp. Shoes with a good grip will prevent potential slip and fall accidents due to the dewy surfaces. High waterproof boots are suggested for traversing the marshy terrains, and gloves can help to shield hands from the cold.

2. Illuminating instruments: Considering this place is shrouded in perpetual twilight, some source of personal lighting will be necessary to aid visibility. Avoid lights too strong, to prevent disturbing the local fauna and flora. Perhaps adopt what the luminescent insects employ – soft, hypnotic glows.

3. Building tools: For adapting or customizing structures that the traveler might spend time in, the toolkit should include some simple but sturdy items – a cutting tool, a hammering tool, some basic fastening hardware. Since the civilization that made these structures uses primitive tools and simple metallic technology, the same should be enough for any necessary repairs or augmentation.

4. Food and Water: It's a good idea to carry fresh water provisions, at least until one can be sure of the purity of the local water sources. Carry compact, non-perishable meals, energy bars for sustenance. The ecological interdependence of this realm suggests caution in consuming local flora and fauna until their safety is confirmed.

5. First Aid Kit: Given the overwhelming amount of fauna and the possibility of unknown species, a first-aid kit, including an antiseptic solution, bandages, allergy medications, should be an essential part of the inventory. 

6. Sketchbook and Camera: To document this mesmerizing land and its inhabitants in order to share this beauty with the multiverse. Be mindful of flash usage, as it could disturb the habitats.

7. Binoculars and Guidebooks: For sighting and identifying the flora and fauna.

8. Portable Shelter: A lightweight, compact tent could provide an emergency shelter if the existing structures prove unwelcoming.

9. Practicing Minimal Impact: Leave no traces behind, that might interfere with the ecosystem. Stick to known pathways and follow sustainable travel practices. 

The sightseers should remember that even though this place may appear alien, it's a thriving ecosystem. Act as a mere observer, sensitive and caring towards its inhabitants. The primary preparation, indeed, maybe cultivating a mental attitude of respect and wonder towards the intricate, beautiful facets of this living twilight realm.
