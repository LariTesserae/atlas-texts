Preparing for a visit to this liquid multiverse location, dubbed as the Fluid Cathedral, demands rigorous adjustment and preparation:

1. **Equipment**: As the world is submerged, functioning underwater equipment becomes a prerequisite. Invest in futuristic aquatic gear, including skin-tight suits to sustain pressure changes and withstand the constant exposure to warm, viscous water. Ensure that your equipment has advanced bio-luminescence for navigation in low light. Protection against unknown organisms in the fluid environment is crucial, so the suit should provide a robust shield against potential toxins or allergens. Given the permeating weight of the water, your clothing should also be flexible and light. 

2. **Breathing Apparatus**: As air, in the traditional sense, does not exist in the Fluid Cathedral, the traveller should equip themselves with a high-capacity respirator or personal life-support system that can help them breathe in this dense, textured environment. 

3. **Physical Training**: Physical condition and mental temperament are equally significant in surviving a trip to the Fluid Cathedral. Rigorous scuba diving training, focusing on deep-sea and prolonged exposure is necessary for travelers to mitigate the effects of surrounding pressure. 

4. **Travel Guides**: Get well-acquainted with multiverse explorers' accounts or authorities who have charted and studied the Fluid Cathedral. Gaining insights from previous visits will be beneficial in understanding the terrain and the creatures. 

5. **Nutrition**: While the Fluid Cathedral is abundant in microbial life, these organisms might not be palatable or nutritious for human visitors. So, carry sufficient food supplies that are both waterproof and easy to consume underwater.

6. **Documentation Tools**: Travelers may want to document or observe the colourful lithographic plant life and elusive fauna. The low light and dim visibility conditions make it necessary to have a high-quality, waterproof camera capable of capturing the bio-luminescence, using low light effectively and withstanding water pressure.

7. **Travel Habits**: Visitors must adopt respectful behavior and adhere to non-interference policies. Do not try to interact or tamper with the local fauna and flora. Even minor disturbances can cause repercussions in this delicate, evolving world.

8. **Adapting to Slower Pace**: Be prepared for a world that does not operate on conventional human time. It is vital to have infinite patience, with life shifting at a glacial pace.

And most importantly, an open mind ready for the enveloping beauty of a world far from the common human experience. For those who tread lightly and watch respectfully, the Fluid Cathedral could offer an experience beyond the conventional understanding of time, space, and life as we know it.
