Preparation for a human multiverse traveler to visit this realm would require both physical and mental readiness. 

Firstly, considering the physical environment described as shifting between solid and liquid states with high elevation, waterproof gear would be essential. This should include clothing that is not only watertight but also able to withstand variable temperatures, due to the elevation. It would be wise to pack sturdy waterproof footwear for navigating the slippery, often changing terrain. Moreover, flexible climbing gear would be extremely beneficial for scaling the weeping cliffs and ruins. 

Given the precipitation in the form of rain and weeping cliffs, it would be liable to extraordinarily high levels of humidity. Hence, carrying hydration salts to prevent dehydration and equipment to collect and purify rainwater would be a wise precaution. It would also greatly help to have waterproof containers or pouches for essential items such as medications, food, and communication devices. 

As the realm seems to be at a high elevation and the air pressure could be lower, carrying supplemental oxygen supply might be a requirement. An acclimatization period before the trip to adapt to higher altitudes could be considered. 

Speaking of communication, the alien technology humming beneath the rocks presents a conundrum. It would be important to secure technology resistant to potential electromagnetic interference that won't be affected by the pulses of alien tech, to maintain a lifeline to the home universe. 

Given the realm's unique flora and fauna, and their potential unknown composition or hazards, possessing a scanner or some form of analysis tool could provide crucial information about their bioluminescence and interaction with the alien tech present. This could both aid survival and contribute to the traveler's understanding of this dimension. 

As the terrain is largely lit by bioluminescent vegetation and alien technology, a traveler should equip themselves with a portable source of light being prepared for unexpected shifts in lighting conditions. 

Mentally, the traveler needs to prepare for extreme isolation and strangeness of the environment. This would involve ensuring a solid psychological base, with techniques for coping with solitude, and managing potential anxiety due to the oddness of the surroundings. Possibly having a guide of any known languages, symbols, or constructs could be handy, too. 

Moreover, given that the terrain seems to loop back onto itself, navigational aids are a must; a sophisticated GPS system, if immune to the technology at play here, or a more traditional compass and map supply would be important. 

Overall, the successful explorer of this unique location would be one who is prepared to adapt to the continual flux and flowing interplay of elements in this realm, showing stoic endurance and mental agility alongside their physical preparedness. This is a place for the cautious and reactive, armed with reliance on both high-tech equipment and low-tech common sense, able to appreciate the subtle beauty amid the vast, vibrant oddity.
