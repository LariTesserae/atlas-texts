To visit this unique and paradoxical terrain, an ordinary human multiverse traveler would have to be thoroughly prepared in terms of clothing, supplies, scientific equipment and mental fortitude. In the subsequent step-by-step guide, we will help our multiverse traveler prepare. 

1. Clothing: Given the extreme humidity and excessive heat, it would be best for the traveler to wear lightweight, loose-fitting, and breathable clothes. Experimentally treated fabrics that offer high-performance outings, such as those used in extreme sports, would be suitable here. Quick-dry materials could help to wick sweat away. Having a UV-protection hat, neck wraps for cooling, UV-resistant sunglasses, and a highly sun-protective, water-resistant sunscreen would be helpful as well.

2. Supplies: As for hydration, the traveler should carry a sufficient amount of water considering the intense heat. However, because the water from grove pools has a faint metallic taste and the small rain drops evaporate rapidly, it might not be the best source of drinking water. As such, carrying fresh water supplements and filtration or sterilization systems akin to those used in space would be useful. Food should be packaged, easy to eat, and not prone to spoilage due to humidity and heat. Travelers should also carry a lightweight, but durable shelter, like a bivouac sack, in case they need to rest or avoid unexpected weather changes.

3. Science equipment: To study the alien flora and fauna and possibly understand the glowing mineral veins or the obelisk, the traveler should carry portable science equipment that can analyze the air, soil, flora, fauna, and the obelisk. They should also have a visual recording device to document any changes and discoveries. 

4. Health precautions: Seeing that potential pathogens or allergens could populate an alien grove, the traveler would benefit from having antimicrobial fabrics and treatments, potentially including a lightweight rebreather or full-face mask if the air proves too laden with moisture or unknown biological entities. First-aid kits and broad-spectrum anti-toxins might also be necessary depending on the biodiversity encountered. 

5. Mental Resilience: Considering the near-sentience of the obelisk and the potentially time-dilating effects of the region, it would be beneficial for the traveler to have undergone psychological preparedness training. Meditation and mindfulness techniques would be useful for the traveler to maintain his focus and sanity in this alien grove.

Finally, they should keep all pertinent authorities or their Earth homebase, and any rescue or navigation teams advised of their position and the status of their mission. In a multiverse, travelers should be prepared for any strange wonder they encounter, and this grove is no exception to that rule.
