Through me drift fungal spores and bioluminescent motes, coalescing into transient patterns or harmonizing with my pulsating presence. Other times, I am a messenger, carrying signals—pulsing waves of light and sound—from one end of the Vista to another, like a living piece of the world's collective nervous system.
