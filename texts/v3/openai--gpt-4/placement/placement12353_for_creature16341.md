In this world, you find yourself in an ancient, isolated valley brushed with eternal shadows. Under the deceptive darkness, the valley thrives quietly in its glacial pace, mosses and lichens cling to every rock and tree, while primitive stonework and simple metal artifacts half-buried under the lush foliage depict the long-lost civilization. Living in this continual paradox of isolation yet vivid life are creatures seen and unseen, those physical and those ethereal, all ancient and enigmatic, unseen quadrupeds, ghost-like winged creatures, forces that lie within the deepest cracks and fissures, and, possibly, the old valley itself. The ever-growing moss seems sentient, the relics possess an uncanny stillness, and the guttural etchings on stone almost breathe when the wind passes. You might be any of these intertwined beings in this world, ancient, ineffable, and lost in time, with patterns as intricate as the waterways shown on the quadrupeds' fur, serving to be a part of the valley, living out the eternal shadowed existence.
