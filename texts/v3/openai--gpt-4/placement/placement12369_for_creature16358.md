### **The Luminal Kith**

My existence would echo the environment: a being of uncertain boundary in a world of uncertain permanence.

In this marshy abyss, I would traverse not just the geographic expanse but the temporal and conceptual folds embedded in the land and water. Reality is optional here; the places where "what was" and "what could be" intersect form a labyrinth that only the Luminal Kith can navigate without leaving remnants of themselves behind.
