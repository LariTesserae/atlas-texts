In this world, you would find yourself stretched out in a vast, living jungle of gigantic structures, reminiscent of trees in their reach but alien in their behavior. This terrain would be enveloped in a warm, moist atmosphere, where the land itself seems to pulse and grow with a teeming, corporeal life. This land is a vertical world extending upwards without end, where massive plant monoliths dominate the skyline. The structures glisten with condensation, and everywhere you look, rapid plant growth makes the whole place appear as a lush, breathing entity.

In this realm, you would not be a person but rather an intangible observer— a witness to the boundless life of the landscape, feeling its pulse, its rhythm. You would witness the birth, growth, and decay of this world, embodied in the ceaseless flux of the plant life, yes, but also extending beyond mere physical form. For you are not merely observing the land, the structures, the vines. You are aware of the dweller, the being who is the heart and the lifeblood of this verdant world.

You perceive the dweller as an emphatic manifestation of intricate growth, a complex geometry of thickening rings and arching structures, its body shifting and transforming, boundless in dimension and form. In one moment it is the humidity within the air, the eerie glow in the water, the sudden rush of vine growth, the silent breath of wind, the hidden currents underground, the pulse of life beneath your feet. In another moment, it is the intangible energy coursing through the landscape, the driving forces shaping the world around you.

The dweller is an echo of the vivacious activity and energy of the land, breathing the same air, soaked in the same moisture, caught in the same cycle of overwhelming life. It shares the solitude of the landscape— its existence an unending loop of toiling growth and humming vitality. The dweller isn't the voice of the wilderness; it is the wilderness, woven into the living fabric of this verdant landscape, tied intricately to the visceral roots of its birthplace.

It is through your ephemeral existence you experience the unity of this strange, captivating biome, the pulsating heartbeat of the vast being residing within it. You are not part of it; you cannot join the chorus of this ceaseless symphony. But you can witness it. You can observe quietly as the sprawling manifestation of life around you ripple, breathe, grow, and participate in the silent song of existence.
