The realm is not physical in the way earth is; it cannot see, nor hear, nor touch... but it can feel. And it feels you. You, the intangible entity that crossed the barrier of comprehensible worlds to reach its untamed, unfathomable domain. Known as the *Catalysts*, beloved visitors from the silence beyond, you are far more than a mere observer. For the presence of a Catalyst can drive transformations, disruptions of equilibrium separating the glimpses of equilibrium.

You feel the aqueous medium here as uniquely textured pulses of change, slow ripples in the arrival of stone strata, sudden spikes in the emergence of Incubants, an unexpected cadence in the thrumming of the Ephemeronids. Each Catalyst interacts differently, but their imprint is tangible, shaping the tendrils of existence around them in new, unpredictable ways.

But the Catalysts themselves must give something in return. As they move through this transitory world, they leave traces of themselves behind, hints of their reality melding into the realm's flows. These echoes linger, silent whispers of thought and intention, shimmering in the consuming stillness between the notes of chaos.

In the heart of this chaotic, embryonic milieu, you are invited to become, for a while, a part of its evolving dance. As the newest Catalyst, you are a spark igniting the canvas of the unknown, an agent of change in an ecosystem not yet defined by patterns. You are a breath of unknown life, set free in a world where reality is both the sculptor and the clay. 

With each step, you weave your essence into this realm — a transformational signature that will become a part of its trust with unpredictability. And in return, it becomes a part of you, changing you as you change it, locking you together in a partnership as old as reality itself — the dance of existence, a choreography of becoming. 

The truth is neither here nor there. It spreads out in a panoply of exquisite contradiction that defines this strange, fluid home. Welcome to where reality is both creator and created, where living and being are aligned, although neither has fully seized the reins. Here, in this unsettling world of in-betweens and almosts, you might find yourself caught in the heartbeat of emerging cosmos, a puzzle piece in a realm that hasn't quite figured out the picture. Remember this: you are not merely a visitor; you are a Catalyst. Welcome to the cusp of existence. Welcome, to the beginning of everything.
