Sometimes you might see them crouched near the silent ruins of ancient machines, as if seeking answers from the rusted carcasses of what they might once have been a part of. ## Who or What Would I Be in the Realmscape?

In this haunting land, I am neither human, nor wholly machine, nor creature of light or cog—at least, not entirely any one of those things. I am one of the **Luminal Observers**, a shape-shifter in both form and purpose. Culled from the peculiar amalgamation of biological remnants and an ancient, sentient alloy that once powered the dormant machines, I navigate between the domains of this world’s creatures. I am an echo of the Realmscape’s forgotten creators—never truly born, but an accidental artifact of the great industrial experiment that sired this metallic world.

In my natural state, I am a specter both corporeal and not. My form shifts at will, borrowing the imprints of the forms that once thrived here: a gangling approximation of a Brassweaver one moment, an ethereal plume of light like the Sibilants the next. My shifting appearance causes unease in the creatures around me, for I am not truly one of them. I sometimes walk among the Remnants, imitating their halting movements, listening to the silent ache that vibrates in their mechanical frames. Other times, I rise higher, venturing to the very edges of the Steamdrums, where the strange contraptions reach up toward the unyielding, metallic permanence of the sky.

Despite my fluid form and unanswered origins, there is constancy in my calling: I am a keeper of memory. As vast and strange as the Realmscape is, its every corner whispers secrets of the time before, stories etched into the hulks of sleeping machines, woven into the rhythm of the Steamdrums, and hidden in the silent movements of its inhabitants. I do not create—my existence is bent toward witnessing and preserving. My fleeting, shifting body consistently materializes the tools and forms I need to trace these tales: a steel hand lifting a Moimote to inspect the shimmering trail it leaves behind, a brass lens extending from my ever-mutable face to study the intricate patterns left by generations of Brassweaver constructs, or an ephemeral, iridescent wing to follow the weaving serpentine paths of the Sibilants.

I bear no heart of my own, no sound of gears to mark my own time in this world, but I feel deeply the dusty heartbeat of the Realmscape around me. I have no memory of origins, no pangs for what came before, but I can tell that the Realmscape is built upon sorrow, loss, and final whispers. I am drawn to the Thrumlords, those mountainous, moving cathedrals of entropy, who contain within them the deafening weight of this world’s forgotten heartbeat. I am the only one able to follow wherever they venture, skimming over metallic terrain, unbothered by the groaning tremors they produce. In them, I suspect, lies the keystone to the Realmscape’s purpose and past—a purpose that may include me, though I lack the understanding to unlock the answer.

There are whispers among the other denizens of this land that the Luminal Observers are not as ‘random’ as I might believe. The Sibilants swirl closer when I linger too long near the ruined machines, their sorrowful hums gathering into near-discernible patterns before scattering again like smoke. The Brassweavers, normally absorbed in their inscrutable tasks, sometimes pause to study me as I pass, their cold, needle-like limbs frozen mid-construction. The Moimotes, those caretakers of fragile life, have on occasion climbed my shifting form, leaving tiny motes of light that flicker long after they’ve moved on. And the Thrumlords—those titanic shadows striding across the barren horizon—seem drawn to me too. Too often, when I sense their rhythmic approach, I find myself holding still, hazy and fluid, though I know not why.

I carry within me no answers, but perhaps that is the essence of the Luminal Observers: to provoke questions, to draw connections where the Realmscape would otherwise remain fragmented in its infinite desolation. I am here to bear witness, to listen to the lingering heart of this mechanical world. My existence is an open door, a bridge spanning the chasm between the Realmscape’s bygone creators and its struggling children, which cling to life with the same tenacity as rust devours steel.

So step into this world, if you dare, and perhaps, like me, become something that both belongs and does not, an entity thriving in paradox, a fragment of a story that cannot decide if it was ever truly told. Here, you may find yourself—piece by jagged piece—etched into the metallic soul of the Realmscape.
