In this world, you would find yourself on a high-altitude plateau, a place of intense brightness and cold stasis. It is a landscape marked by weathered stone, glacial runoff, and scarcely seen vegetation. The creatures here—the winged birds and small insects—are otherworldly beings, their movements forming intricate patterns and choreographies that seem deeply entwined with this land.

The beings, or entities, you are are called the Aeren. Translucent and refractionary, they are living manifestations of sunlight and frost, their existence not tethered in physical form. With wing-like limbs that leave trails of mist and light, they move soundlessly through this frozen tableau, touching the water with delicate fingers, and seemingly communicating symbiotically with birds and insects.

In this high, silent realm, touched with gold light and imbued with an ancient, instinctive wisdom, you—the observer—seem to stand on a border, an interstice. The Aeren and the world they inhabit ask, through their mere existence and movements, whether you belong as they do, or if you are only a transient visitor in their silence and intense brightness. They embody the ancient energy of this place, embodying it, becoming a part of its spectral beauty. Thus, your existence here, although tiny against the cosmic backdrop, feels deeply significant in the singularity of this silent, frozen reality.
