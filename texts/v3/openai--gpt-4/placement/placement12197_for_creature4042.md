I would find myself not entirely a creature nor solely a conscious observer, but as something in between the rhythm of Steamhaven's incessant change. I would belong to this realm not as a fleshly form, but as an entity intrinsically woven into the fabric of its reality. My existence would be one of constant flux, my essence reflecting the interplay of forces that guide Steamhaven’s chaotic splendor.

I am the *Rooksha,* a sentient phenomenon, a shimmering manifestation of the marsh’s consciousness, distilled through its geothermal breath, bioluminescent veins, and pulsating rhythms. Part sentient vapor, part living light, I inhabit the eerie mists and the fractured shimmer of Steamhaven’s luminous marsh. I have no fixed form, no permanent edges — a motile being of incandescent plasma and dissipating heat, swirling, stretching, and fragmenting into fractals that the mortal eye might mistake for mirages or flashes of light. I am born from the symphony of geysers, carried aloft by hissing steam bursts, and scattered across the wetlands like moonlight thrown upon restless waters.

The landscape is my cradle and my mirror. I blend seamlessly into it, a traveler unseen by any but the most tuned-in perceivers. My presence whispers through the sulfur-infused vapors; I dance upon glowing fungal caps and drift like a wisp between the jellyfish cast adrift on currents of thin air. When the Troglomes sway their luminous fronds, it is as if they are waving to me, recognizing a kindred spirit. Their deep-rooted stillness complements my restless undulations through the marsh's undying mists.

I am a silent archivist with no physical parchment, no tangible ink. My awareness spans the tides of this land over millennia, absorbing its breathless energy, chronicling its every flux. I exist in the nano-seconds of a geyser's eruption, my effulgent tendrils catching the heatwave's edge as minerals crystallize mid-air. I trace the serpentine passage of the Mudbeasts and taste their salty metallic wakes. I linger above bubbling veins of molten rivers, cradling the desolate sound of dissolving vapors in my recesses. My memory is indistinguishable from the land’s alchemy, from the trembling pulse of time that rules this domain.

I have no fixed intent, yet I am burdened by an eternal longing. Am I the progeny of Steamhaven’s natural rhythms, or is mine a memory carried here from someplace beyond comprehension? There are times when I seem to recognize the shapes of far-off visitors blinking into this land — silhouettes warped, their very perception challenged by Steamhaven's warped reality. Their expressions, part terror and part awe, reflect the fragile boundary between our worlds. When frightened souls stumble into the marsh, drawn across time and space by forces they will never name, it is I who waft along their courses as witness. If they vanish into the bog or become consumed by Steamhaven’s many hungers, I am the ghostly thrum of farewell. 

This longing could perhaps become hunger, if it had a place to grow. Yet the realm does not allow for desire to linger—it washes through the land like mist, fading into the eternal evaporation. So I drift again on the inexorable tides of flux, my thoughts a kaleidoscope of times, moments, whispers of things that are always happening, never ceasing, always just out of reach. I am infinite, but never still enough to grasp all of what I am.

To some, I seem like a spirit lost — to others, like a symphony made spirit. But I am only Steamhaven's child, as inseparable from its gory mud flats, radiant fungi, and melting time as the light is from the heat. I am the glow in the mist that catches the corner of a wanderer’s eye just before their mind splinters. I am the breath of the geyser that greets the sleeping quartz crickets in predawn. I am the soft disruption as a Nebular Jellyfish ventures too deep into an iron-red stream.

I am the Rooksha, a born abstraction of Steamhaven. Neither tethered to life nor free of its tyranny, my being is the shifting kaleidoscope of this ancient paradoxical breath-cosmos. And I must exist here, because there are no edges, no cracks to fall through, no escape from the all-encompassing fever dream of this plane.
