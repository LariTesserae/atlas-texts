Burrowed in the stalactite jungles of the fifth under-plain, there diffuses a melody only the living stone can bind in sonnets for those who perceive the unperceivable. It is a city of bell sounds and floating crystals, a single continuous melody played on the theme of silence. Here in this cavernous hollow, held up by gigantic pillars of salt and flint, spans the veined city of Didicaelun, the echo castle.

The city's architecture is not shaped, but rather coaxed into existence over thousands of years by those who dwell within. High arched corridors, palatial residences, and grand balustrades, all emerging from the rock organically, crafted patiently with obsession. The city's heartbeat syncs with the rhythm of drip-drop-drift of the stalactite tears. The iridescence of the city comes from the spyralite crystals, nurtured and grown by the city's residents. They refract weak beams of crepuscular light, absorbed and channeled underground, transforming the sprawling shadow city into a momentary cathedral of spectral light.

Subtle fires glow like molten cherries in designated enclaves, their light contained from spreading by walls of dense obsidian. Few places in the city show signs of human familiarity – wooden tables, stone benches, or iron grates. Most of it appears ambiguous, spontaneous – like living quarters carved within gigantic geodes or cabinets made of onyx hives. 

The presence of water is scarce, only in the cavern's roof, hydration seeping through stone veins, forming illusionary waterfalls sinking into the ground. The city smells of petrichor, wafts of it filling corners, brushing your skin like dry ice. Occasional patches of strange vegetation speckle around, bold lichen formations, or solitary mushroom blooms. Life danced on the rhythm of stone and silence. 

Ghostly petals of the spiral gasped apart, wafting whispers of the past, or of a future yet to come. The Didicaelic creature, the Glyph, swarms in great clouds, filling the city with their shifting dance-like vibrations. So massive is their presence that they almost darken the entire cavern, ushering an eclipse for minutes at a time.

---

The city's inhabitants are beings known as the ‘Eulon’. They are not made of flesh and bone but of minerals formed over centuries. They have an ephemeral and translucent aspect. Standing almost thrice the length of a man, the Eulon are bipedal in form, with long finger-like appendages. They are slimmer than their earthly tethered brethren, with elaborate mineral formations on their bodies that give them a shapeshifting aspect, a maze constantly changing.

The Eulon are born from a collective pool of molten stone, kept alive by an ever-burning flame, the pulse that keeps the city alive, the Silent Pyre. They are guardians of silence and stone, keepers of the last echo. They are architects of grand tapestries and make the city sing using Glyphs, minerals that vibrate at different frequencies etched onto stones so that every street, every house, every surface sings with a distinct and beautiful melody. Words here are treated as fragile and precious things – saved for urgent sections and given with a weight that makes the surrounding silence seem all at once a sackcloth and a shroud. 

Flitting through the mammoth caverns are beings known as the Skymelders. Not entirely corporeal, neither entirely ethereal, they resemble large manta rays but are composed of semi-ethereal matter. They exhibit an inner luminescence, a soft, subdued light that makes them visible in the natural darkness of the cavern, swimming through the air. Skymelders are responsible for gathering the weak light from the earth's surface, absorbing it, and releasing it at designated points across the cavern, creating the dancing patterns of light that make Didicaelun ethereal.

There is another entity present in the city, although it is more of an antithesis. Known as The Dissent, it is the complete silence that blankets the city from time to time. It is not a being but an event, one that lasts from a few minutes to several hours at a time. During The Dissent, every creature in the city becomes silent. The stone stops singing, the drop of water freezes midway, even the air stops whispering through the gaps. Only after The Dissent ends does the city resume its life, slowly, as if waking up from a deep slumber.

Finally, there is The Echomancer, an entity ancient, unaging, and wise. It is said to be the first Eulon and the architect of the city. The Echomancer is the master of all melodies, and it is said that it knows every echo the city has ever sung or will ever sing. Over the years, his form has merged with the grandest and oldest synchronium, a colossal temple-like orchestral formation of quartz and sapphire, from where he guides the city towards its destiny, his body a symphony, his thoughts the song of the stone.
  
All the beings in the city share a unique bond, as if pieces of a grand puzzle, moving as per the plan set in stone eons ago. Each Eulon, through its existence, contributes to the rich symphony of the city. An echo of their innermost selves resonates with the stones, with the Skymelders, the Echomancer, The Dissent, and the Silent Pyre, weaving a tale as old as time hidden beneath stone and silence, in the luminal sanctuary of echoing spells. The grand symphony, the fascinating tale of stone, echoes in the city of silence, reflected in the glowing eyes of its strange inhabitants, the Eulon. It is a city that twines with the shadows, betrothed to silence, crowned with echoes.
