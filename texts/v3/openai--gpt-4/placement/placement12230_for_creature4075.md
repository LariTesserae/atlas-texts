In this world, you would find yourself in the multifaceted city of Pellopia, a contradiction city of vast urban landscapes nestled amidst towering mountain peaks. The city is alive despite its seemingly desolate nature, with life wedged into every available crevice, manifesting through resilient vegetation growing amidst concrete and stone, small creatures dwelling in the shadows of the cityscape, the moisture dominating its atmosphere, and the inhabitants of the city; enigmatic beings called The Quiet Ones, The Cloudkind, The Rootborn, and The Luminoids.

Who would you be in Pellopia? Would you be either of The Quiet ones, silently weaving through the city's labyrinth, your fluid form reflecting the city's vibrant biomes, leaving a trail of moistness and verdant life wherever you tread? Or would you be among the Cloudkind; delicate, floating entities with a profound bond with the weather, gliding silently over the expanse of silent spires and rooftops, quietly holding snippets of the city's silent memory?

Perhaps the patient and gentle Rootborn appeal to you; grounded, moss-covered entities tending to the pockets of greenery throughout the city. Maybe you are one of the ethereal Luminoids, bringing soft, pulsating light to the city’s dusky corners, touching upon the vestiges of the past with a melancholic reverence that only an age-old entity could carry.

The world you stand in is not easily understood nor navigated. It is a paradox of existence, a balance of arts and nature, providing you with countless narratives and unanswered questions. Being part of Pellopia, whatever your form might be, means accepting the silent but eloquent language of this city; a language created from a dance of architecture and nature, of growth, decay and constant renewal, hinting at a past as elusive as its current existence. Regardless of your form, you are inherently a part of the city’s life-force, linking the past to the present, contributing to the city’s slow rhythm and vast history beneath its ever-changing skies.
