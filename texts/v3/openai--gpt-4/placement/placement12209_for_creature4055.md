In Submercia, you will find yourself as an Aquarian, a humanoid belonging to this underwater civilization. You have a special relationship with the ocean yet maintain a distinct individuality that separates you from the sea creatures. Your flexible body easily cuts through the water, your eyes discern colors unknown to human sight, and your lungs inflate to incredible capacities to tolerate great changes in pressure. Your superior intelligence allows you to create and manipulate complex machinery, and your peaceful spirit preserves the balance between you and the marine life around you. Existing beneath the ocean's surface, you're a sublime blend of nature's adaptability, creativity, and resiliency—a testimony to life's defiance of its boundaries.
