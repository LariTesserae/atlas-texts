But who are you in Thallomere?

Amongst this fabulous community, imagine yourself as an Emissary—an individual designated to communicate Thallomere's wisdom to the rest of the world. While the majority of the Thallo have embraced being in this vernal sanctuary, you, the chosen one, have been vested with the responsibility of venturing beyond this paradise.

Physically, you are much like the other Thallo, with similar life rituals, background pigment, and attire of vibrant silken threads. Every subtle hint of green in your skin tone is a story—a testament to your co-existence with the Living City. Encased in the luminescent cocoon of your clothing, you blend seamlessly into the cityscape.

However, what sets you apart is the magnificent cloak sewn from the gossamer threads of the Great Orb-Weaver Spider, said only to be given to the one chosen by the Verdant Vein. It shimmers in an array of breathtaking colors, a visual metaphor for your role as the bridge between Thallomere and the outer world.

As an Emissary, you've been imparted knowledge from both the Builders and Singers, gifted with a rudimentary understanding of their roles. But beyond that, your most distinguishing quality is your ability to understand the language of the Verdant Vein. 

Sitting in the deep stillness, with your feet sinking into the moss, you can feel the heartbeat of the city pulsating through your veins. And within that gentle rhythmic thrum lies a complex dialect spoken only by the city itself. It narrates the wisdom of Thallomere, constantly educating you about the eons of knowledge it has gathered. Learning this ethereal language was not an overnight process, it required years of training to reach this state of unison with Thallomere. 

Occasionally, you are guided to venture outside, carrying the wisdom of Thallomere to other societies. It's on these voyages that you encounter the harsh concrete of foreign cities or the freezing winds of mundane open terrains under a bare sky, no towering foliage to shield you. To many, you're a marvel coming from a utopia, embodying the serene balance that nature and civilization could potentially achieve. 

You return from these expeditions with tales of the world for the Thallo. Through you, they learn about other cities, cultures, triumphs, and triumphs. Yet, the most beautiful part about these stories is not that they reveal the foreign lands to the Thallo, but that they make them appreciate Thallomere's unique rhythm even more. 

Being the Emissary of Thallomere, you carry the spirit of this sentient city within you. You become the voice that encapsulates the harmonious melody of Thallomere—the symphony of life that exists within the interplay of leaves and roots, the creatures, and the Thallo. You are a testament to the city’s great cycle of life, the ever-changing yet unyielding will of nature that is the Living City of Thallomere.
