**While inside**

Move slowly and keep clear of the inlet, the moving gate, the pipes, and the plugged drain. The circulation may be modest in appearance but persistent enough to push or turn you, and a drain or narrow partition can trap clothing or a line. Don’t try to block, test, or redirect the water.

Assume the floor is slippery and the softened tile edges are still sharp. Don’t put your hands into channels or seams, and don’t lean against the plaster, gate, or cracked masonry. The warm water is not automatically clean or safe. Don’t drink it, swallow it, or touch your face after handling wet surfaces. Avoid stirring up grit, and leave the green growth and the powdering mortar alone. Afterward, rinse off and check for cuts.
