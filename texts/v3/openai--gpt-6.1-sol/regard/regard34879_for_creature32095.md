The power did not remove the cover plate.

It had noticed how little of the chamber’s life could survive an ordinary act of kindness. Warmth would be a catastrophe. Silence, if achieved by stopping the machinery, would be starvation. To lift the ribbon creature from its bolt would straighten the turns through which it breathed.

So it came first as an attention that added no pressure to the water.

It learned the intervals.

It learned which knocks carried nourishment and which merely arrived with it; which currents were breathed, which swallowed; which electrical changes beneath the green cushion were answers, and which were only the passing of a pump’s pulse through living tissue.

It followed the blemish on the soft animal’s flank through a thousand repetitions of injury and repair. It watched the hanging drinkers yield to one another. It watched a square-backed animal lift its foot before its neighbor’s wing had quite reached it.

It did not assume that being made meant being unfeeling. Nor did it assume that a life strange to its own must secretly long for skies.

For a while, it did nothing the inhabitants could have noticed.

But the chamber was no longer unattended.

***

The first alteration was beneath the ceramic.

A shock climbed the tower, arriving long after the event that had made it. The power received it before it entered the chamber. It let the familiar disturbance reach the branched creature at its several points of contact, but caught the excess that would have driven the pale knuckle hard against the lamp.

The collars tightened in sequence.

The amber light remained visible.

The power waited through many more shocks. It adjusted nothing else.

Then it began to separate the roar.

Not to abolish it. The inhabitants lived by some of its rhythms. Through their feet they knew when water would arrive, when a neighboring abdomen would contract, when there would be clearance for a gill. A sudden quiet could have left them as bewildered as a sudden darkness.

Instead, the power kept the useful vibrations close to the bodies that needed them. It received the wasteful impacts into a new layer beneath the ceramic, where they could end without returning through every leg and mouth.

The chamber still sounded.

For the first time, it did not sound everywhere with everything.

A hanging drinker combed its filament. Two ridges away, a soft frill no longer shook with that movement.

The faint ticking beneath the green cushion could be felt apart from the pumps.

The white animals continued gathering salt. Between pulses, one lowered a foreleg farther than it had lowered it before. Its sensory hairs rested against the root.

***

The power could have made another chamber immediately, spacious and beautiful according to almost anyone who stood outside it.

It did not.

Instead it made a recess beside one of the blind channels: a space no larger than the smallest motion of the animal within. The recess contained water of the same composition and temperature. Its wall offered the same protection without the rubbing edge.

The feeding point remained where it was.

For a long time the animal did not enter the recess. Its frill opened and folded. The brass ring showed when its heart contracted.

Then, during a movement already its own, its flank settled into the new space.

The blemish disappeared.

It did not return.

The power left the recess there.

Elsewhere, it offered changes just as small. An additional socket within reach of a foot. A sheltered fold in which a wing could open without taking clearance from its neighbor. A second length of current behind a swimmer, supplied alongside the first rather than replacing it.

Some spaces were used. Others were not.

One square-backed animal moved a leg to a new socket and withdrew it at once. The power changed the socket’s angle. The animal tried it again much later.

The power did not mistake every movement for a request. It watched what persisted when surprise had passed. It kept the old arrangements available. It learned how slowly an offered possibility might become recognizable to a body that had never encountered one.

***

Behind the amber lamp, the branched creature touched the forming die.

Advancing the ridge would bury the feeding pores along its emerging edge. Withdrawing it would undo the surface against which the little mouths were braced.

The power made neither movement.

First it learned to supply those mouths without depending on that exact exposure. It grew a second porous edge beside the first, matching the fresh metal’s temperature and texture. For many cycles, both fed them.

A few mouthparts found the second edge.

Others remained where they had always been.

Only when every creature had a secure feeding place did the die become capable of motion. Even then it waited. The power had removed the necessity of arrest; it had not yet found a reason to advance.

The unfinished mountain stood unchanged for a long time afterward.

That was permitted.

***

The green cushion did not produce a seed.

It spread by three cells into a nutrient socket that had previously been capped.

The white animals followed the new contact. Their mouths found a little salt there. Through the cushion passed a change in charge unlike the repeated changes the apparatus had always elicited.

The power attended to it.

It answered by opening another socket nearby, not beneath the new growth but within its possible reach.

No command entered the roots.

***

Beyond the cover plate, maintenance crews came and went. Their instruments still reported a functioning assembly. The power ensured that no one would replace it merely because its readings had begun to vary.

Within, food remained dependable. Waste was carried away. Cold stayed within the limits these bodies required.

There were now places where movement could end without being driven at once into another movement.

A swimmer sometimes rested in the lee of its current. The current waited beside it.

A hanging drinker opened one wing fully, then folded it.

The ribbon creature remained around its bolt. The power had not yet found a safe way to offer it another posture. It continued learning.

And the chamber still held its unfinished ridges, its suspended drops, its blue ledges and amber lamp. Much of it would have looked the same to a passing observer.

But put a finger against the cover plate now, and the sounds entering your bones would not quite repeat.

Inside, a square-backed animal raised its foot.

Its neighbor’s wing passed beneath.

It could set the foot down in the old socket.

There was also another place.
