The power does not enter by the stair.

Its arrival adds no weight to the floor, no heat to the air. Nothing withdraws beneath a cover. The loose washer ticks when its pipe expands, and the tick has no second meaning.

For a long time, the power does nothing.

It attends to the place where the scraped rectangle meets the older tissue. It attends to the closed channels beneath the handprint. It follows the pale cords into the pocket behind the vessels, but does not assume that reaching the largest body means it has found the creature’s center.

There are pulses that travel only a few inches. There are others that pass through several joined shelters and return altered. Behind one ceramic block, a small growth retains water while the larger body nearby is drawing its reserves inward. Perhaps the connection has failed. Perhaps water cannot pass through it. Perhaps the small growth is withholding what it needs.

The power leaves the question open.

It watches through a season of increasing heat.

What looks like damage on one day proves, later, to be a passage. What looks like an unused pipe carries the faint cooling that keeps a sheath alive. The oil on the basin is harmful to the creature, but its removal would expose the water to more rapid evaporation. The welded threshold obstructs travel and shelters it. A corroded cover is close to failure; its replacement, if fitted tightly, would crush three living folds.

The room offers no simple division between what sustains and what threatens.

Nor is it only the creature’s room. Above it, people rely on the machinery. Beyond the walls, other living things depend on what those people do. The power follows these connections far enough to understand that stopping the pumps would not be an act of mercy.

It continues to watch.

Then, during a hotter week, a young one leaves the lip of a cover.

It has chosen a route that was possible yesterday. Overnight, a line has warmed. The thin moisture on the steel is gone.

The young one extends, grips, advances. Its rear pads lose their purchase before its front pads find another irregularity. It is left against the open surface, too far from either seam.

The power does not carry it to the adult. Contact there might be refused. It does not place it in the basin, or give it a body capable of crossing bare metal.

Instead, it cools the underside of one bolt head.

A bead forms there. Slowly it lengthens down the thread and touches the steel.

The young one encounters the water with its front edge. For a while it does not move. Then its gripping pads release in sequence, and it draws itself beneath the bolt head.

The power holds the coolness until evening, when the line loses heat of its own accord.

This is its first alteration.

Its next takes longer.

A maintenance worker has been assigned to replace the corroded cover. The power does not fill the worker with revelation. It gives them a better chance to see.

When their lamp passes over the underside, a wet fold catches the light. The worker pauses. They lower the lamp and wait.

The fold draws back.

The worker goes upstairs for a second pair of eyes.

There is disagreement. Someone calls it packing. Someone else remembers the label on the cold vessel. The cover still needs replacing.

In the end they support it before loosening the fasteners. They lift it a little at a time. They leave the mineral bed beneath it intact, and fit the new cover with a narrow clearance at the lower edge.

The power stays beside them through the work. When a tool slips, it falls away from the exposed fold. Nothing more remarkable happens.

Afterward, a note is added to the maintenance record. It is imperfect. It uses the word *growth* and says very little about what was seen. But the next person will read it.

The power cannot make care depend forever on accidents of attention.

Over the following months, the record improves. A diagram acquires a shaded region. A sampling instruction specifies that only loose material is to be taken. The warning beside the cold vessel is copied into a place where someone planning a repair will find it before descending the stair.

Not everyone becomes interested. They need not. A person can refrain from scraping without understanding what lives beneath the scale.

Meanwhile, deep in the room, the power makes one other provision.

Behind the welded threshold is a blind recess, dry except during the largest showers. Its entrance is rough. Its iron bottom slopes away from the living sheath. Water enters, stands briefly, and escapes.

The power changes the slope by less than the thickness of a flake of rust.

No new spring appears. No endless supply replaces the creature’s work. The recess simply retains a little of the water that already passes through it, sheltered from oil and the hottest air.

For weeks, nothing occupies it.

Then a clear edge reaches the entrance and retreats.

Later it returns. A narrow sheath forms along one side. It does not fill the recess, and the power does not encourage it to. Near the back, a young growth establishes itself without joining the older tissue.

Both draw on the small reserve.

The power allows this, too.

It does not restore the network beneath the handprint. Those channels are closing because the hand no longer returns; opening them would only give the creature more tissue to maintain. It does not imitate the warmth of the absent worker.

There are other contacts now. A person rests two fingers on the rail while waiting for a valve to finish opening. The vibration enters the supports. Beneath the lowest vessel, the creature braces, then gradually loosens.

The person stays long enough to hear the soft knock.

Years later, the room is still dark. The machinery still works slowly. Some channels have dried, and others have begun. A small independent body persists behind the ceramic blocks. The pale threads beneath the cover flourish, diminish, and flourish again.

There are losses the power does not prevent. It has learned enough to distinguish some of them from the ordinary relinquishing of a route, but not enough to treat every stillness as an appeal.

It remains attentive.

When the rain shifts again, the old channel pales. The gritty edge beneath the threshold shortens. Along the new route, growth advances toward a seam.

At one place, the passage ahead would have ended in a dry pocket with no shelter beyond it.

Now there is a little retained water.

The creature reaches it in its own time.
