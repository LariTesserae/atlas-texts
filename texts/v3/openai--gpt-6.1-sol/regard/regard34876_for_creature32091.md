The power first notices the fold.

It is no larger than the edge of a leaf, tucked into the upper corner of the new recess. As the wall thickens, the fold disappears. When the wall splits, it returns on the exposed surface below.

The power follows it through the night.

There is much here that might be mistaken for suffering. The great reports of hardening tissue. The roots drawn tight enough to sing. The groan that travels upward from the deep bodies and leaves every pool standing above its rim.

The power does not quiet these sounds.

It listens until it can distinguish a sheath breaking because the leaf inside has outgrown it from a sheath breaking before its leaf is ready. It watches a wall fall and finds that the inhabitant has already withdrawn from most of it. Elsewhere, a single narrow throat closes without a sound, and a connected body loses the use of half its chambers.

The small recess survives three changes of surface before dawn.

At the fourth, something goes wrong.

A deep root turns beneath the district. The wall bearing the recess is pulled downward while the paving rises against it. The opening tries to deepen, as it has done each time the surrounding tissue begins to close. But a sheet of hardening fiber lies behind it. Below, the new paving belongs to a growth that has no passages.

The little fold appears at the edge of the narrowing gap.

It appears again, lower down.

There is nowhere else for it to appear.

The power holds one fiber soft.

Only one: a pale strand at the back of the recess, already beginning to set. Nothing around it stops. The wall continues downward. The paving heaves. A buttress cracks across its broadest face.

Through the softened strand, the recess extends.

It emerges beneath a stair, its rim almost white, its upper fold drawn tight. Warm air passes through it in a single long discharge.

The power releases the fiber.

By morning it has hardened with the others.

For several hours, the recess remains under the stair. Sap gathers along its lower lip. The passage beyond is shallow and empty.

Then a fine root grows from its threshold.

The root reaches the place where the softened strand has sealed. It presses there, withdraws, and presses again.

The power waits.

It could provide a chamber that never closes. It could hold the stair above the recess, make rain arrive each evening, keep every neighboring root at a safe distance.

But it has watched the small inhabitant persist by becoming passages. It does not yet know what a permanent chamber would make of it.

Instead, beside the sealed strand, it leaves a space no wider than the root.

The root enters.

Beyond the wall is a pocket between two larger bodies. It contains no light, no water, and little room. The inhabitant explores it with three short branches, then retracts them all. By noon it has moved to the stair’s outer face.

The power lets the pocket close.

Over the following days, its attention spreads.

It discovers that the keepers of cool chambers sometimes release their moisture too late. The sunset hardening has already begun; water reaches the tense outer tissues only after the first fractures run through them. The delay is greatest where districts have grown across the narrow roots that once joined chamber to chamber.

In one such place, the power restores a crushed connection.

The effect is small. A row of walls darkens a little earlier. Their outer layers contract without splitting. Above them, a sun-seeking tower keeps an extra pair of fronds.

The next evening, however, those fronds shade a neighboring growth that had been receiving the last light.

The power does not repeat the repair everywhere.

It follows the moisture, the shade, the new pressure beneath the tower. Where it has acted, it remains attentive. Sometimes it adjusts a second connection. Sometimes it withdraws what it has added and tries a smaller opening.

The basin offers no empty place into which a gift can be put without touching another life.

Water poured into a sealed chamber may become rot. A wall prevented from falling may bear down upon the roots below it. An avenue held open may divide two bodies that are trying, slowly and with enormous effort, to join.

The power learns the weight of each kindness.

Near a black pool, it finds an inhabitant whose reflected fractures arrive long before the fractures themselves.

For three days the rim grows thicker. More water enters its tissues. The white roots beneath the surface divide until the pool is crowded with them. Inside the rim, cracks lengthen so slowly that the surrounding district has time to build over them.

The power touches the water.

It does not change what the water shows. Along one edge, it makes a shallow place where a root can enter without descending into the black depth.

A white root reaches that shelf.

Later, another follows.

The inhabitant continues drinking from the deeper pool, but less. Its rim stops thickening. One of its internal cracks closes; another remains. The shelf stays where it is. Nothing is required to use it.

By then the little passage has crossed the district.

Its fold can be found in a fallen tower, then beneath a canopy, then in a wall facing the rain. Once it divides into two openings. Both retain the crease at the upper left corner.

For a day they deepen together.

On the second morning, one develops a fringe of stiff hairs along its lower edge. The other does not.

The power attends to both.

It does not try to bring them back together.

When the next great movement comes from below, the older opening is caught in a surface descending toward a deep chamber. It draws inward, seeking another face. Above it, the younger one has opened into a newly formed lane.

For an instant their tissues remain joined by a thin, strained throat.

The power keeps that throat from tearing too soon.

The older inhabitant passes its pattern through. A rim swells in the young lane, beside the fringed opening. The connection beneath them loosens, seals, and is taken downward with the wall.

Nothing is lifted out of the basin. Nothing is made to last forever.

At sunset, the groan still rises through the roots. Towers incline. Pools climb their rims. Across miles of green, the day’s tender surfaces begin to harden.

But beneath one arcade, moisture arrives before the wood sets.

At the edge of one pool, a white root rests in shallow water.

And in a lane already narrowing, two small passages deepen at different rates. One has a pale, smooth threshold. The other holds a fringe of dark hairs.

The walls approach.

Through each opening, there is still a way.
