The power did not lift the stone.

It noticed first how much of the sky a kindness might cover.

So it remained outside the country, where the lichen was holding a little warmth, and attended to what happened below. It watched a ridge acquire its second side. It watched a hunter close around a grazer and become, briefly, easier to see. It watched the animal with the missing foreleg pass the nearest bowl.

It did not make the hunter conspicuous. It did not carry the animal to the water.

For some time, its attention had no more weight than the light.

Then the pale bearer came to a place where it had left the dark object earlier.

The mark was gone.

It touched the ground with the longest hair of its collar. It moved a short distance and touched again. Behind it, the dark object rested on a slope, keeping its own position.

The power could have put the mark back exactly.

Instead, it waited.

The animal returned to the object. It laid two feet against its uneven surface, then went once more to the smooth patch. This time its collar spread so widely that several hairs bent beneath their own fineness.

At the tip of the longest hair, the ground yielded.

Not much. There appeared a depression smaller than the mark that had vanished, too shallow to hold water. The animal drew back. The depression began to close.

It touched the place again.

The closing stopped.

For a long interval the animal stood with its hair against the earth. Then it lifted its head, and the little hollow remained.

That was the first thing the power did.

It waited to see what the animal would do with it.

A seam-creature arrived before the next click of water. It approached sideways, tried the edge with its feelers, and settled into the depression. Its joints slowly came into alignment. The pale animal stayed nearby, holding the dark object beneath its body.

When the seam-creature left, the hollow filled.

The bearer touched the smooth place. Nothing happened immediately. Then the earth yielded again, under the same hair.

It made three such places that afternoon.

One was never visited. After a while the animal passed a forefoot across it, and it closed.

One received a winged creature whose reflection had come away from the bowl with a different number of legs. The creature rested there until its feet stopped moving.

The third was still empty when the mist began.

The pale animal lifted its burden and hurried toward the dome. At the first change of slope, its front feet searched too long. Cold moisture was gathering on its collar. The house was already closing its doorway.

The power felt the impulse to move the house nearer.

But there was another possible kindness, small enough to lie beneath a foot.

At the place where the animal was searching, the ground kept the same shape for a little longer.

Its forefeet found what its hind feet had found.

It crossed.

Behind it the slope resumed its slow alteration. A seam-creature emerged from the change, unharmed, carrying several young between its sideways joints.

The bearer reached the doorway as the last roof flake lowered. Inside, it touched the bead of water. The bead diminished.

The power withdrew from that part of the ground.

During the mist it attended to the ring of posts.

Two young animals were moving below it. Their bodies had become almost distinct, but their feet were still passing through the firmer surface overhead. Above them, a parent stood with its rear legs unfolded. Its yellow line had faded.

It lowered its collar.

The young climbed toward the hairs and fell back.

The power did not pull them through. Below the posts there were slopes, damp places, a low green growth. One of the young had been feeding there. It might not have wanted to leave.

Instead, where a collar hair nearly met an upturned feeler, the power made the intervening earth less definite.

The feeler touched the hair.

The parent became very still.

Soon the second young animal came close. For a while there were three bodies and one small opening, with the mist passing over all of them.

One of the young climbed.

The other stayed below, its feelers wound among the parent’s hairs.

When the mist thinned, the parent moved to the edge of the ring. The young beneath the earth moved with it for several steps, visible through the white ground. Then it turned toward the softer country.

The opening closed slowly enough for the last hair to slip free.

Afterward, the power left these possibilities where they could be found.

They did not announce themselves. A collar laid against the earth might be answered by a shallow hollow. Ground beneath a searching foot might hold its shape until the foot was placed. At the posts, a very small passage could open where two creatures were already reaching toward one another.

None remained open by force.

The animals learned unevenly. Some never used them. The one with the missing foreleg began resting at a new hollow halfway to the ledge. Another made a depression beside the unfinished walls and closed it at once, then made it again in a different place.

The dark objects stayed dark.

The power did not take one apart to discover why it mattered.

Later, the slow bearer went to the doorway at the boundary. It stood with its head inside and its burden held close. Beyond it, something moved.

Before entering, it lowered one collar hair to the ground.

A shallow mark formed beside its feet.

The animal waited until the mark had settled, then disappeared into the mound.

Hours passed. A shadow grazed nearby. The drop at the center fell with its tiny click.

When the short-collared animal emerged, carrying the dark object, it found the little hollow beside the doorway.

It put one uncertain foot into it.

The ground held.

Above the country, the stone remained where it was.
