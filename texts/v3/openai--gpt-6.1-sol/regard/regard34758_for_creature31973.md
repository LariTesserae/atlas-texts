The power’s first act is to remain outside.

It does not enter as a hand would enter. It does not lay a new surface across the passages, or offer warmth to the cold ceramic. Even its attention is held at a distance until it understands whether attention has weight here.

For a long while, nothing is different.

The whistle keeps its narrow place. The broad note remains beneath the ledge. Light passes from one pale form to another without acquiring a source that can be thanked.

The power listens.

It can hear distinctions too small for an ear: the change where the green crust ends, the old depth beneath the worn scratch, the almost closed joint through which the inhabitant continues. It does not gather these into a single voice. It follows each far enough to learn how it belongs to the others.

There is no hidden cry within the roar.

There is no posture of endurance waiting to be relieved.

The closeness of the ceramic is not, by itself, a wound.

The power has encountered beings whose first need was an opening. It has broken locks, lifted fallen beams, brought air into places where air had ended. Here it does none of those things. Removing a plate would not give this inhabitant more room in the way a larger room gives space to a person. It would take away a whole arrangement of nearness. What appeared to be an obstruction might be the only place where one part of its life could occur.

So the power leaves the plates where they are.

It also leaves the noise.

It could make the chamber quiet. From outside, that might seem the gentlest intervention: a hand-sized place released from its enormous sound. But it has listened long enough to hear what would be lost. It does not mistake the comfort of a visitor for the comfort of what lives here.

Its care begins elsewhere.

Beyond the chamber, beyond even the wall, there are things the inhabitant cannot encounter until they have already changed it. A roof might fail. Water might travel down through a seam that has always been dry. Someone might hear the steady roar and decide that a hidden mechanism needs dismantling.

The power attends to these approaches.

A failing support is strengthened without moving the ceramic. A path by which rain could someday reach the chamber is turned back toward the open air. Neither correction is felt within. No joint tightens. No settled grain is disturbed.

It does not make the wall indestructible. It watches where damage might come from, and acts there.

At the entrance, it is more careful.

To seal the joint would protect the inhabitant from fingers. It would also close the one way through which it has been encountered. The power does not know that an unfamiliar boundary is always unwelcome merely because it is unfamiliar.

The entrance remains.

But when someone leans close with a chisel, the crowded whiteness becomes unexpectedly clear to them. They notice the fitted layers. They see that the green growth stops precisely at a joint. They hear more than an undifferentiated fault in the wall.

There is time to reconsider.

If a hand approaches, there is time to approach gently.

The power requires no reverence. It makes no promise that the chamber will reward kindness. It asks only that a person have a chance to perceive what they are about to touch.

Within, the inhabitant continues along its narrow differences.

The power does not give it a face, a name it must answer to, or a longing for places beyond the wall. It does not make the sound into speech so that it can receive a declaration of gratitude.

Nor does it decide that the present arrangement must endure unchanged forever.

The flakes may settle further. A scale may acquire a new edge. Another small age may begin where a passage narrows. The power listens to those changes as closely as it listened to what was there before. Its care is not a command to remain exactly as it was found.

Nothing visible announces the attention now given to the chamber.

There is still coolness beneath the glaze. The light is still strong. The far corner is bright and comparatively simple.

Yet outside the wall, water has found another course. A weakened beam holds. A tool has been set down.

And when the power returns its attention to the rounded hollow, it listens again without assuming that it has already heard everything.
