The power does not enter through the seam.

Its arrival displaces no air. The leaf beneath the gathering drop does not stir, and the gray films at the roots continue their slow work. Nothing here has an organ with which to notice that it is being attended to.

For a time, attention is all the power gives.

It follows the water inward. It follows what passes for nourishment through the successive bands of living film. It finds where a substance dangerous to one layer becomes useful after passing through another. It waits through the turning of the buried motor, the click of the valve, the long redistribution of warmth.

It does not mistake the visible leaves for the whole inhabitant.

Nor does it need to decide whether the separated masses are one being or several before taking care. The sealed ends, the shared hours, the small exchanges beneath the tiles: these are sufficient reasons to be careful.

Under the collapsed patch beside the trench, the three wet leaves are still consuming what their older growth stored. They have a way to receive water but almost none arrives. Beneath them, a film is thinning. If that film disappears, water alone will not restore the roots.

The power attends to this, too.

Then it looks beyond the chamber.

The installation is not endless. Far away, beneath ground that has not subsided, a reservoir is slowly losing pressure. One bearing in the motor has begun to wear unevenly. The valve is sound, but the device that moves it will eventually fail. There are several possible failures. In one, the chamber cools over many months. In another, a section of pipe stays hot until the damp sheaths vanish and the roots die behind their closed ends.

The roof has a shorter uncertainty. A plate above the largest growth is held by two narrow bridges of stone. One has cracked almost through.

The power does not hold the plate up.

It closes the crack where the stone is still bearing weight, leaving the rest of the fracture open. Vapor can pass. Roots can enter. The ceiling remains a broken ceiling, with its dark joints and unfinished edges, but that particular plate will not fall upon the leaves.

A little dust descends.

The growth receives this as it receives all dust.

The next intervention is smaller.

Inside the pipe, where no root has reached, a corroded passage is narrowing. The power loosens a scale from its wall. It carries the scale far enough that it will settle in an unused hollow rather than lodge at the next bend.

At the following cooling cycle, the valve clicks at its accustomed hour.

The southern wall loses a little more heat than before.

No leaf opens at once. The plants have endured favorable changes that did not last. They continue to hold their moisture close.

Only after several cycles does one of the three surviving leaves beneath the dead patch lift its edge. Behind it, the thinning film darkens.

The power waits.

It could fill the chamber with water. It could bring daylight through the roof and make the desert flower above it. But water poured across these mineral seams would carry injury into the roots. Sunlight would heat surfaces that have never needed to defend themselves against it.

What lives here has made its shelter from a particular arrangement of dangers. The power studies which dangers belong to that shelter, and which are merely taking it away.

It leaves the heat.

It leaves the dark.

It leaves the long intervals between drops, though not every interval remains quite so long.

Along the north wall, where dampness has always vanished before reaching the floor, it changes the slope of a grain-sized shelf inside the crack. A small amount of moisture now runs deeper into the stone instead of spreading across its warm face.

For many cycles nothing visible happens.

Then a root finds the wetter seam.

It is a young root, belonging to a low growth that has lost two first leaves. Its tip stops short of the mineral deposit. A film gathers around it, slowly making a place where it can remain.

The power does not pull it farther in.

Elsewhere, it does not rejoin the severed stems. Some have sealed well. The growth on either side has begun to differ, and a connection restored without care might drain one to sustain the other. Instead, beneath an exposed crossing, the power makes the stone slightly less porous. Moisture persists there after the floor around it dries.

Whether anything crosses is left to the growth.

There remains the machinery.

Repair would give the chamber time, but time alone would not make it safe. The power smooths the failing bearing, preserving the motor’s slow turn. Then it finds, deeper in the old installation, a narrow return passage that once cooled after every circulation. The passage is blocked. Behind the obstruction, the stone is damp and its temperature changes little.

The power opens it by degrees.

At first the difference is too small to reach the chamber. Later, one pipe joint remains cool a little longer. Much later, pale roots advance into the insulation toward that quiet reserve.

They bring their films with them.

The old apparatus continues its work. Alongside it, something less dependent begins, without a sudden season for which the leaves have no preparation.

There is no announcement. No voice tells the chamber that it has been saved. Much of what lives here will still die and be used by what follows. The broad leaves will not all endure. The bucket will rust through.

Beside the wheel valve, the leaf extends into the cooling air. Its tip touches the oval it has cleaned on the rim. A bead forms there and travels down its central vein.

At the trench, another drop gathers.

The waiting leaf is still browned along one side. The power leaves that side alone.

When the drop falls, some of it is lost on the hot stone, and some runs inward.

Beneath the catchment leaves, the folded interior loosens a little farther.

This time, it has room.
