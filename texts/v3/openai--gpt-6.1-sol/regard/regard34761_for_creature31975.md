At first, nothing is changed.

The small animal keeps its head against the place where you stood. The film opens behind it. Far below, a terrace moves through distances in which there is no longer enough light to distinguish motion from disappearance.

The attention that has come to the city does not gather itself into a figure.

A figure would have a size. It would stand on one floor rather than another. The animals would have to decide which way to lean near it, and some of the older inhabitants might mistake the weight of that decision for a new injury.

Instead, it attends to the place where the animal’s head touches the stone.

There is more there than pressure.

The plates are receiving the shape of the surface, but also its warmth, the slow strain of the terrace, the answering strain of terraces elsewhere. There are absences in what they receive. Some are ordinary: a district too far away, a seam between its warm passages. Others have edges that have never healed.

The attention does not fill those absences.

It learns to distinguish them.

***

For a long time, its only action is to keep a little warmth from being wasted.

Deep in a seam, beneath several layers of film, heat has been passing into a chamber no longer connected to anything that can use it. A few streets away, a young animal has freed five limbs from its niche. The sixth remains flat against the stone.

The warm passage will not reach the niche again before the district turns.

Nothing new appears. No bright mark crosses the floor. The warmth simply takes a slightly different course through the mineral.

The dark fringe around the young animal opens.

Its last limb lifts.

For some time afterward, the animal does nothing with its freedom. It rests in the hollow, all six limbs folded badly beneath it.

The attention waits.

Eventually an older animal comes along the wall. It stops beside the niche and presses its head to the stone. The young one extends a limb toward it, misses, and tries again.

There is no further intervention.

***

The city has many narrow escapes. Not all of them are errors.

A street that joins too briefly may carry dust into a chamber that would otherwise remain barren. A wall that disappears may release a slow inhabitant from an arrangement it has been unable to leave. The presence associated with delays is not merely obstructing the young in its niches. Some emerge carrying film that has had time to become something rare.

The new attention learns this by almost helping.

At a distant edge, it begins to steady a bridge.

The nearest animals stop.

One lowers its head. Another turns away and moves rapidly down the underside of the terrace. In a chamber far below, a sheet of film draws itself close against the wall.

The attention lets the bridge move.

The districts separate. For several passages of the brighter sky, nothing crosses there.

Then a low note rises through the gulf. The two terraces answer at different intervals.

Between them, the bridge begins again, farther along the curb.

This time the animals approach.

The attention remembers the difference.

***

Eventually there is a court in which something unfamiliar can be declined.

It is a small court, open on two sides, with no view except a nearby wall. Animals already meet there. One of the long-lived presences sometimes occupies the seam beneath its floor.

At the center, the stone becomes a little warmer.

Not enough to draw every creature in the district. Not enough to change the film along the walls. Only enough to be found by a head pad.

The first animal to touch it pulls away.

The warmth recedes.

The animal waits, returns, and touches the place again.

This time the warmth remains beneath its head, no farther than the outermost plate.

What passes between them would not look like a message to you. The animal adjusts two limbs. A thin line of dust bends toward a corner. Something answers beneath the floor, slowly enough that the creature has time to leave before the answer is complete.

It stays.

When it departs, the central patch cools.

For many days, no one returns.

***

The animal with the damaged limb is not made whole.

It visits the court with the low ridge and braces itself as it always has. The ridge fits the shortened limb because years of pressure have worn a shallow place into it.

Elsewhere in the district, a bridge is forming. It will be sound, but its first span is too narrow for the animal to cross without the joint it has lost.

A second lip develops beside the first.

It is scarcely visible from above. From beneath, it makes another route, a little longer, with places to brace.

The animal reaches it, tests it, and withdraws.

On its next visit it crosses halfway.

On the visit after that it goes all the way.

Other animals use the lip too. One carries a young creature’s dark fringe in the folds behind its head. Another stops there through an entire passage of the lighter sky.

In time, the lip becomes a street.

***

The old inhabitant whose rooms are very far apart does not come to the warm place in the little court.

Its answers continue to arrive from below. Sometimes they reach only two of its remaining chambers. Sometimes one.

The attention follows the intervals between them.

There is a stretch of stone, almost too thin to hold a shadow, where one answer used to pass. The room it belonged to is gone. Nothing remains that could be rebuilt without guessing.

The attention does not guess.

It gives the faint vibration another way through: a turn beneath a stair, a seam behind an empty market recess, a length of wall occupied by no living film.

The next low note travels farther.

In the room where you once sat, the broad step warms.

Across the gulf, the window brightens.

There is a long pause before the deepest remaining chamber answers.

The new route holds through the pause.

***

Not every injury can be prevented.

Once, a terrace thins around an occupied niche. By the time the attention finds it, the young animal has become part of a loss that is already spreading through several districts.

For a while, warmth gathers uselessly at the edge.

Then it withdraws from the broken stone and remains in the nearby court.

The animals come there one by one. They press their heads to the floor. Some stay only a moment. One remains after the warm seam has cooled, after the lighter region has passed overhead, after the others have gone.

The floor does not grow cold beneath it.

Nothing asks it to leave.

***

Over a very long time, the city changes.

There are still enormous vacancies. Streets still part before a traveler reaches them. The dust rises into its pale veils, and the oldest stones continue falling beyond sight.

But in certain places, a connection that is beginning to fail lasts long enough for an animal to take its head from the floor.

Warmth reaches some chambers by routes that no longer depend on a single seam.

There are courts where unfamiliar knowledge arrives gently, with time between its arrival and the need to act on it. There are edges from which two ways depart.

None of this belongs to the new attention alone. The animals carry it. The films alter it. The inhabitants whose bodies are scattered across the terraces accept some parts and leave others unused.

Eventually, the court where the first warmth appeared moves far away from every district the attention has been helping.

It makes no effort to bring the court back.

Much later, an animal enters a shallow room on the underside of a remote terrace. It is cold there, but sheltered. A pale young creature lies in a niche, with one limb free.

The animal settles against the wall.

Warmth passes from the folds of its body into the mineral. The dark fringe opens. Beneath them both, a seam begins to carry the warmth farther into the room.

The attention notices.

It leaves the small, occupied place undisturbed.
