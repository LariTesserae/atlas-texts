The power does not begin by making the machinery quiet.

The noise is terrible to a human standing in the court. To the being beneath the elbow, it is also the approach of weather, the difference between a safe crossing and an unsafe one. Silence would take something away before giving anything back.

Nor does the power warm the pipe, deepen the pool, or bring sunlight through the wall.

It attends through several flushes.

It attends while the being feeds, while it gathers itself over the ridge in the resin, while it returns to the place where water can pass over it without carrying it away. When the being stops at the sealant bank, the power does not interpret the pause as a wish to cross. There may be nothing on the other side that it needs.

The first thing the power changes is a work order.

This is not difficult. The tower is full of work orders. Most are useful. This one calls for the removal of construction residue from the upper machinery floor, including clearances not yet inspected after commissioning.

It is scheduled for Thursday.

The power does not delete it. Loose steel must be removed. Drains must remain open. A worker ought not to have to choose between caring for something small and keeping a large machine safe.

Instead, on Wednesday, someone notices the two blades of grass.

She has come to check the leaking duct seam. Her lamp crosses the wall crease, returns, and stays there. Then she sees the moss. Then, because she is already looking closely, she sees a droplet move against the water.

She waits.

The power gives her no revelation. It does not put certainty where observation belongs. It merely lets her have the time she would otherwise have lost to a radio call from the floor below.

She watches the being reach the washer.

Afterward she photographs the place from several angles. In one photograph, the being is almost visible. In another, it is only a pale interruption in the wet metal.

She adds a note to the work order.

**Small living organism observed at bracket. Do not wipe this area pending inspection. Maintain access to bolts and drain.**

The note is plain enough to survive being read by someone busy.

By Thursday, there is a little mark beside the bracket. Not a fence, not a shrine. A mark indicating where cleaning should stop.

The steel shaving is lifted from the pool with forceps. The cleaner leaves the grit behind the washer alone.

That evening the being comes down as usual. It finds no new surface to understand.

This matters to the power.

***

The duct seam is defective.

Left alone, it will widen. Eventually the leak will no longer produce the brief weather to which the being has adapted. Water will enter a place where water must not go; people will arrive urgently, and whatever lives beneath the elbow will be one of the least urgent things in the room.

The power sees this too.

It does not preserve a future emergency for the sake of preserving the present.

When the repair is planned, the living patch is included in the survey. Someone measures the spray that reaches the bracket. Someone takes a sample of the water. No one yet knows what the being is, and the drawings do not acquire a confident name for it.

A small sheltered recess is fitted against the wall, outside the bolt clearance. Its lower edge is rough enough to hold sediment. Its upper lip breaks the force of falling water without excluding the thin films that come from the manifold.

It is not stocked with unfamiliar plants. It is not made deep.

For a while, it is merely another wet surface.

The being ignores it.

The power permits this.

Later, fine material collects in the recess. A green point appears. The being reaches its edge on one of its journeys toward the grass, presses a margin against it, and withdraws.

On another journey it stays longer.

The original shelter remains available throughout. Nothing is transferred merely because a larger intelligence has decided that the new place is better.

When the duct seam is repaired, the work is slow and scheduled. The bracket is shielded from debris. The pipe remains cold. Condensation continues to gather and fall.

For two intervals after the repair, the being flattens itself before a flush that no longer arrives.

For several more, it does the same.

The power does not reach into it and erase the expectation. It keeps the shelter safe while the being learns what the metal now means.

***

There are things the power cannot learn by watching alone.

Whether this is one being or the last of many. Whether it will divide, lay something too small to see, or end without leaving another. Whether the two pale lines inside it have anything to do with hunger.

Its wisdom does not consist in answering these questions before they can be answered.

It keeps attention near them.

Elsewhere in the tower, other wet clearances are inspected before they are cleaned. Most contain nothing beyond dust and water. One holds a little green film. Another has a pale patch that proves to be sealant.

The people learn to look without being required to find.

And beneath the elbow, the being continues its short journeys.

Its world is still cold. The floor still trembles. There is no promise that every drop will be gentle, or that its body will last forever.

But the rag will not arrive by accident. The shelter will not disappear unexamined. When the machinery must change, this life will be counted among the things the change must make room for.

The power remains attentive, without asking the being to notice.

A film of water gathers over the shallow pouch. The pouch holds. The water grows heavy and falls.

For now, nothing else is needed.
