The power did not bring light.

It could have cleared the atmosphere, opened the black water to a sky, drawn the ridges upward until there were slopes and summits. But it had noticed the inhabitant before it had decided what darkness meant here. Much of the inhabitant’s attention depended on differences that warmth from above would erase.

It waited outside those differences.

This was not a place outside the continent. It was a restraint upon its own presence. It allowed the chambers to fill at their accustomed rates and the currents to take their accustomed routes. Where two inhabitants used opposite sides of a membrane, it did not settle into either side.

At the basin, the pressure held beneath the fold increased by a minute amount.

The inhabitant received it as something ordinary at first: a consequence arriving from a distant province. It kept the pressure, waiting for the associated differences that would disclose its source.

None arrived.

Instead, the pressure diminished precisely as the inhabitant prepared to release it.

The inhabitant waited again.

The next increase was smaller. It did not occupy a chamber already needed by another circuit. It ceased when the inhabitant turned its attention toward the temperature beneath the basin, and returned only when that attention came back.

Over a long interval, the inhabitant established that the change was attending to it.

It had known this of other inhabitants. But another inhabitant always brought obligations: somewhere a cord must remain conductive, somewhere fluid must be retained, somewhere a shared wall must yield. Even the smallest neighbor had a weight in the land.

This presence had none that it could find.

It tested a route beneath the basin’s rim. The presence answered there. It tested a narrow circuit among the upper roots. The presence answered there also, without having passed through the relations between.

The inhabitant drew back.

The power ceased.

For a time, the basin received only its own weather. Droplets entered the black water. The waxy rim thickened inward while yielding an equal thickness beneath. A slow change of resistance arrived from a province the inhabitant had relinquished long ago.

Eventually it returned to the narrow circuit.

The presence answered with no more pressure than before.

There followed a communication too slow and too particular to resemble speech. The power learned the distinction between a release the inhabitant desired and one it merely permitted. It learned that some tensions, maintained for centuries, were not injuries. They were ways of remembering. It learned which delays were troublesome and which were the inhabitant itself.

The inhabitant learned that it could interrupt the encounter.

It did so many times.

Each time, the presence withdrew without taking anything away.

Only after this had become an expectation did the power offer to act.

Far beneath the basin, a bundled cord had been growing less reliable. Each replacement preserved its shape, but also preserved a slight disadvantage in the arrangement of its fibers. The inhabitant had compensated by involving more tissue. That tissue belonged partly to a neighbor. The neighbor, in turn, had begun compensating elsewhere.

Nothing was failing. Yet maintaining the old relation consumed an increasing share of their attention.

The power made the difficulty available to the inhabitant in a new way. Not as a pressure demanding response, but as several possible continuations, each held apart from the working land.

One continuation restored the cord to an earlier condition.

The inhabitant did not accept it.

The earlier condition would have restored an old disagreement along with the greater conductivity. Its neighbor had once been excluded from a release now shared by both.

Another continuation supplied a new passage.

That, too, remained unanswered. The passage crossed tissue whose uses the inhabitant did not fully know.

The power waited while the question traveled beyond the basin.

An answer returned through a series of changes in warmth. Then another, much later, through a resistance that had not altered for ages. Other inhabitants were involved. Some required the proposal to arrive several times before distinguishing it from the ordinary work of the plain.

The continuation they eventually accepted was small.

Along a portion of the unreliable cord, the next generation of fibers formed with a slightly different spacing. Beside it, a chamber acquired a yielding wall where its former wall had been rigid. No new source of power was introduced. No process was commanded to stop.

The pressure took a second route.

At the basin’s edge, the suspended droplet enlarged.

For the first time in a very long while, it fell.

Its entry into the water occupied the inhabitant’s attention: the place of arrival, the warmth it carried, the brief distribution of its weight. Beneath that event, a familiar effort was absent.

The inhabitant searched for it.

The missing effort had supported part of its continuity for so long that relief was initially difficult to distinguish from loss. The power did not hurry to fill the vacancy. Nor did it remove the remaining tensions on the assumption that all effort was suffering.

The inhabitant sent a small pressure through the altered cord.

Its neighbor answered.

It sent another, differing slightly from the first.

This answer differed too.

Neither exchange was needed to keep the basin warm, or to sort its water, or to preserve the inhabitants through replacement. They repeated it nonetheless. Sometimes one held the pressure longer. Sometimes the other returned it by a route that lent it the coolness of deeper tissue.

The power attended without joining them.

Elsewhere in the continent, many things remained beyond help that could yet be offered wisely. An old partner continued making room for an inhabitant that had ceased. A new circuit waited without knowing what it waited for. Disagreements held membranes shut beneath perfectly quiet leaves.

The power did not settle all of them.

It approached where an approach could be refused. It stayed long enough to learn what a refusal was. Where no answer came, it preserved the ordinary conditions and moved no closer.

At the basin, the inhabitant eventually turned toward the presence again.

It opened a sequence used for recognizing familiar participants. The sequence extended through the altered cord, around the black water, and into the clean pleated tissue beneath the fold.

There was room in it for an answer.

The power answered.

Above them, moisture gathered on the broad leaves. The surface remained dark, low, and unbroken. Below, water entered a chamber, and the chamber held it a little longer than its work required.
