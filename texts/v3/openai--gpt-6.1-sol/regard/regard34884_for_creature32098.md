The first thing the power does is wait.

It does not enter through the flaw. Entering would give the entrance an importance it has never had, and might persuade the basin to arrange itself around an arrival. Too much here already depends upon distinctions that have become faint.

For a while, it attends to the grain.

The grain is not in danger in any ordinary sense. It could remain on the lip for another century. But the inhabitant has chosen it, and the work of making its shelter is costing something.

When the inhabitant next rests, the power holds the part of its body that would otherwise have to loosen.

It holds nothing else.

The inhabitant presses against the ceramic. The recess deepens by a small amount. Its pale plate fails to catch against its neighbor.

It stops.

Throughout the basin, the dust continues its slow travel. The three shadows meet above the floor. A click passes through the substrate.

The inhabitant withdraws its joint from the lip.

The support withdraws with it.

For a long interval, it does not return.

***

The power has no need to become visible. Visibility here is an awkward accommodation, and the basin has already hurt one being by allowing it briefly.

Instead, it makes a difference the inhabitant can examine.

On an unused stretch of substrate, well away from the footprint, two grains lie close together. The power inclines the attraction between them. One moves toward the other.

Then it waits.

The inhabitant feels the alteration through its clear surfaces. It turns one fold toward the grains and changes the inclination of a silver pin.

The attraction ceases.

The grains remain separate.

After a while, the inhabitant restores the pin.

Nothing happens.

It makes the adjustment again, more narrowly. This time the grains approach one another by less than their own width.

The inhabitant holds still.

The power holds still.

There are several exchanges like this. Some take days. Once, the blue seam interrupts them, and the inhabitant lies distributed among its silver lengths and pale crescent until the basin loosens again.

The power does not mistake this interval for absence.

Eventually the inhabitant moves the pin in a way it has not used before.

Between the two grains, a small amount of unevenness becomes available.

It lowers an extremity and feeds.

***

Only after this does the support return at the entrance.

The inhabitant tests it before pressing against the ceramic. It leans away. It loosens a different part of itself. It brings its repaired plates into an alignment that ordinarily requires a long pause.

The support accommodates these movements without carrying them farther than intended.

Then the inhabitant works.

The power could finish the recess at once. It can perceive the pressure of the grain against the lip, the depth at which it would settle, the tiny changes the excavation would cause throughout the casing. But the inhabitant pauses often to feel the shape, and twice alters where it is cutting.

The recess becomes wider on one side than the grain requires.

Its floor slopes slightly toward the basin.

When it is done, the inhabitant does not move the grain into it.

It rests beside the lip until warmth from outside reaches the ceramic. A little dust loosens on the mountain face. The poised grain shifts.

The inhabitant extends a joint behind it.

The grain settles into the recess.

For the first time in a very long while, the inhabitant completes a movement without having to recover from it afterward.

***

The power studies the cracked dish.

It does so from directions the dish has never opened, taking care not to make those directions available inside the basin. It finds pressures gathered along the fracture. It finds that removing them would allow the silver to close.

Repair would destroy the passage.

Instead, it places a restraint where neither half can feel it pulling upon the other.

The crack remains cracked. Dust may still enter it. The inhabitant can still clear that dust using the motions it knows. But the slow narrowing ceases.

Before leaving the restraint in place, the power allows it to relax.

The inhabitant senses the change immediately.

It leaves the entrance and comes to the dish. A thin part of its body appears along the hidden shelf. Another passes through the pillar’s hollow.

It holds the halves apart.

The power resumes its restraint.

The inhabitant withdraws one part, then the other.

It waits beside the dish through a blue interval and the darkness after.

Then it goes to feed.

***

The pale plate is more difficult.

It was fitted under conditions that no longer exist. Several adjacent parts have grown around its unevenness. To replace it would require deciding which of those changes belong to the injury and which belong to the being that has lived with it.

The power leaves it in place.

At the point where it catches, it offers a little room.

The inhabitant bends. The plate passes its neighbor. It bends back, slowly, and repeats the motion.

After several trials it makes the old preliminary adjustment, beginning early as it has learned to do.

The room remains.

Over time, it stops needing the adjustment. For longer still, it sometimes makes it.

***

There is a group of pits the inhabitant returns to more often than the others.

The power does not read them.

When the fine edge passes across their worn surfaces, it supplies no missing resistance. A mark invented now would be felt as something remembered, and the inhabitant has no way to examine such a gift without taking it into itself.

Instead, the power steadies the edges that remain.

Elsewhere on the same plate, in a place the inhabitant can reach, it lays out a shallow surface responsive to pressure. The inhabitant inspects it. It draws a fine edge across it and leaves a small groove.

Later, it returns to that groove.

The resistance is exactly as it left it.

It makes another.

The old memories continue to arrive together, and some continue not to arrive at all. The power attends while the inhabitant searches them. It does not fill the silence with an answer.

***

Beneath the overhang, the band of reflected light persists.

The power leaves it untouched.

It does not put a body beneath it. It does not make the light stop turning.

When the inhabitant passes around the patch of leaning grains, the support follows the longer route.

***

The fragment overhead is still part of the inhabitant.

The power approaches it without moving toward it. Around the fragment, height has become a nearly closed relation: every attempt to shorten the distance gives the distance more places to go.

It takes a long time to find another arrangement.

Then, through the same slight support it has used at the entrance, the power makes the fragment’s pressure available nearer at hand.

The inhabitant unfolds sharply.

A dark plate appears between the terraces. One clear surface turns upward. The silver pins incline, and the meeting of their shadows descends.

The power stops.

For several hours, nothing changes.

Then the inhabitant touches the offered pressure.

It releases it.

It touches it again.

The fragment does not return to the body that has learned to live without it. It comes down into a shallow place beside the doorway, where its edge can be reached without strain.

Sometimes the inhabitant holds that edge while resting.

Sometimes it leaves it alone.

***

The basin does not become bright.

The pillar still clicks at uneven intervals. Grains still hang at ankle height, gathering dust. The far wall remains close enough to touch and most of a day away.

There are alterations, but they are local.

A threshold no longer costs effort to keep clear during sleep. A worn recess supports a part of the body it had ceased to fit. The extra direction across the dish remains open. New pits accumulate on a plate, each with its own distinct resistance.

The inhabitant begins to miss some of the pillar’s clicks.

At first it wakes after every missed one and checks the pins. Later, it rests through two.

The power remains available without filling the room.

After a long interval, the inhabitant goes back to the entrance.

The grain is still in its shelter. Beyond it, the mountain carries weather, animals, changes of warmth. There is no foot waiting to be made, no weight being urged into a single direction.

The inhabitant brings one of its clear surfaces close to the flaw.

Outside, a cloud moves away from the north face. Warmth reaches a part of the ceramic that has been cold for days.

The inhabitant extends a narrow joint across the lip.

The power supports the parts it has left behind.

For a while, it touches the mountain.
