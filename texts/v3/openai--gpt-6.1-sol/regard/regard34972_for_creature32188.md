For a long time, the power does nothing that can be distinguished from weather.

It watches through several reversals of the water. Then through several hundred.

It learns that the scrape in the ventilation shaft is not, by itself, a calamity. The fan has scraped for years. Its uneven movement sends a pulse through the wall, and the small bodies beneath the warm grilles sometimes begin their descent at that pulse rather than at the rising water.

It learns that the blocked channel beside the pattern shop is not neglected. Behind the blockage, the water loses enough speed for the nearly transparent young to feed.

It learns that the large one in the doorway can still leave, though not through the opening. There is a crack behind the lintel, descending into a room whose floor has fallen away. The creature has explored it with a narrow portion of its body. It has not chosen to go farther.

The power does not widen the doorway.

It watches the large body reach into the channel. It watches the delay between its lobes. There is an old scar that pulls tight when the lowest lobe extends, and for some time the creature rests after each attempt.

Here, at last, the power makes a small change.

A stone beneath the water settles at a slightly different angle.

The next time the creature reaches down, its underside encounters a surface halfway to the feeding current. Pale knobs spread over it. The creature pauses, withdraws, then reaches again.

After that, it rests its weight there while feeding.

Nothing about the stone requires it to do so.

***

The engine has a fault that no creature can reach.

Deep inside its housing, behind a plate blackened by heat, a pin has worn almost through. When it breaks, the engine will stop. The water will continue rising for a while, but the pumps will not bring it back through the lower rooms. Warmth will linger, then diminish. The smallest bodies will be the first to lose their hold.

The power could make the pin everlasting.

Instead, it eases the load upon it.

Elsewhere, in a locked storeroom, there is a replacement wrapped in oiled paper. There are tools, too, and a maintenance book swollen shut by damp. These belonged to people who are no longer coming.

The power brings none of them back.

Over the following weeks, the housing develops a narrow opening along an old casting seam. No water enters. Warmth comes out.

One of the compact creatures from the hopper finds the opening. It presses its front pad against it, then flattens until part of its body can pass inside. For three days it returns to the same place. On the fourth, another joins it.

The power waits.

Their attention is not directed toward the worn pin. They gather where oil has thickened around a bearing, feeding on something in the dark deposit. Their passage clears a small groove. Oil begins to reach the bearing again.

The engine’s missed stroke becomes less frequent.

This is not a repair sufficient for all time. The power continues watching the pin.

***

It does not fill the square with birds.

There might once have been birds here. There might never have been. The absence of wings is not a request.

Nor does it cut away the bridges to admit more sky, dry the walls, scour the channels, or pull the fig-like tree from the boiler. It sees how many bodies shelter beneath the roots. It sees the cool surfaces to which they retreat when the lower rooms grow too warm.

Once, during a season of unusual heat, it loosens a pane high in the pattern shop.

The pane opens no farther than the thickness of a leaf stem. Air begins to move through a room that had held it almost still. Moisture leaves the upper glass in a slow, uneven clearing.

The creatures nearest the gap move away.

The power closes it halfway.

Two remain beside it. Others settle farther down. The room acquires a difference that can be crossed in less than an hour.

***

At the bent cellar grate, the returning water sometimes catches the large individual before it has withdrawn its lobe. It has always freed itself. Once, it tears.

The power feels the injury without mistaking it for the whole of that creature’s life.

It rounds the hidden edge of the iron.

Later, it places a little of the loose sediment against the cellar wall, where the wounded body rests. The sediment carries food. Water passes gently across it.

The body feeds, but not immediately.

A smaller one comes and presses its front margin to the injured edge. They remain together until the basin begins to rise.

The power leaves them alone.

***

There are deaths.

There are also new pale bodies beneath the fern fronds, so slight that a drop of water bends the leaf farther than they do.

The power attends to both.

In the blind recess behind the boiler, an old body stiffens against the brick. Its edges are occupied by the young. There is no struggling movement, no repeated attempt to find warmth. The power does not fasten life inside the emptied shape.

But elsewhere, when a falling bracket pins a living body against a hot pipe, the bracket comes loose before the flesh burns through.

It falls into the channel.

The sound travels around the square. Several front margins lift. Then the water takes up its ordinary noises again.

***

Years pass before there is any sign that the creatures notice something beyond the changes themselves.

Even then, the sign is uncertain.

Beneath the western staircase, one spreads across the three braces. A drop gathers above its front margin. Before the drop falls, the creature lifts toward it.

Perhaps it feels the weight shifting through the brick.

Perhaps it has learned the interval.

The power holds nothing back to make itself necessary. It does not delay the drop, or sweeten it, or teach the body to wait for another gift.

The water falls.

The margin opens, catches it, and closes.

Above the square, the clouds move past the narrow pieces of sky. Below, the pin turns in its housing, the roots drink, and the creature continues toward the pipes.

The power goes with it, without getting in its way.
