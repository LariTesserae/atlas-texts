The power first attends to the floor.

Not to the shelter as a shape, nor to the creature’s old injuries, but to the small differences arriving beneath its feelers. The sea has begun to press into the lowest chambers. A flake slips against another flake. Far out in the basin, an animal repeats a signal that reaches this rise with its middle portion missing.

The inhabitant separates two of its feelers by less than the width of a grain.

The power listens from there.

For a time it does nothing. The builder has work in progress, and the long intervals between movements are part of that work. A plate held motionless may still be under pressure. A limb resting beside a capsule may be feeling the young move through its stalk.

The power watches several tides.

It learns that the small grazer returns to the patch the builder left untouched. It learns that the new young gathers stones not quite at random: the broadest grain is kept against the soft seam behind its foremost plates. It learns that the tremor from the distant shelter is sometimes answered immediately and sometimes only after the animal has finished crossing a dangerous stretch.

There are disturbances the builder cannot yet distinguish.

Beneath its warm floor, a fracture is lengthening through the roof of a flooded chamber. The fracture is narrow, and for now its two faces press together. This makes it quiet. Each rising tide lifts one face slightly higher than the other; each falling tide leaves a few more grains between them.

Eventually the floor will cease to be a floor.

The builder has chosen well. The heated seam, the high ground, the feeding circuit, the shelter’s rear opening: all were found and fitted with care. The failing rock lies below the reach of that care.

The power goes beneath it.

There, in darkness, the sea moves through a space scarcely tall enough to admit the animal’s body. Loose mineral hangs from the ceiling. The warmth comes sideways through an old fault, touching the chamber’s roof before continuing upward.

The power does not close the chamber. Air must still pass through it. Nor does it seal the fracture; water shut out here would find another passage, perhaps under the neighboring shelter.

Instead, it brings two bearing surfaces together farther down, where the rock is thick. The adjustment is small. Pressure travels away from the thin roof and settles into a broad layer beneath the rise.

Above, one capsule trembles on its stalk.

The builder lowers its body to the floor.

For the next several hours it listens more often than it feeds. Twice it comes out and circles the shelter. It presses its feelers against the entrance stone, then against the rubble beyond it. The power waits without making another change.

By the following tide, the animal is feeding again.

The power turns next to the warm seam.

There is enough heat for the capsules, but little margin. In a season of colder water, some will develop too slowly. Increasing the heat directly would alter the films on which the inhabitants feed, and perhaps bring creatures from the deeper water into these shallows. The power follows the seam instead, finding where warmth escapes into an empty cleft.

It shifts a little loose gravel there.

Less cold water now washes across the buried warm surface. A little more heat reaches the shelter floor. The change takes days to arrive and remains slight enough that the builder need only move one capsule’s stalk out of a shallow accumulation of condensation.

The power leaves that task to it.

One of the young emerges while the adult is away.

It stands beside its opened capsule, lifting each long limb in turn. Its body is still wet. It carries its three grains to the doorway and encounters the stone drawn across the entrance.

There is a gap.

Beyond the gap lies a narrow crack, harmless to the adult, deep enough to trap the young.

The power could carry it across. Instead it settles a loose chip into the crack, making a rough bridge no wider than the young’s body.

The young touches the chip. It withdraws. It touches it again.

Then it climbs over, dragging one of its grains behind it.

At the first cold surface it stops. Its breathing folds open briefly and close. After a while it turns back into the shelter.

The chip remains where it can be found again.

Beyond the channel, the other inhabitant signals.

The answer travels out from the warm rise, but a loose slab absorbs much of it. The power has heard this happen repeatedly. It considers joining the stone beneath the slab, making a clean path between the shelters.

Then it waits through another exchange.

There are other signals in the ground: feeding movements, the approach of a large body through water, the early strain of a roof plate. A clearer path would carry those too. The animals have arranged their lives around what reaches them here.

The power makes no alteration.

Its attention returns to the injured plate on the builder’s back. Beneath the white mineral patch, the soft margin is irritated. Each passage through the doorway draws salt against it.

Here the power acts more directly. During a resting interval, it loosens the tiny hard grain lodged beneath the plate. The builder contracts along one flank. The grain falls onto the floor.

For a long while the animal remains still.

Then its breathing folds open more fully on that side.

The power stays through the falling tide, and through the tide after that. It follows the young on their first short circuits. It learns which sounds bring the adult out of shelter and which permit it to sleep.

There will be other failures in the basin. Some can be prevented. Some will be visible only when they happen. The power begins to move among the farther shelters, taking care with the ground between them.

At the warm threshold, the builder hooks a slab with its front limb and draws it inward. The new chip at the entrance has changed the fit. It tests two wedges before choosing a third.

A tremor arrives from beyond the channel.

The builder pauses with the wedge held under one blunt lobe. It lowers its body and answers.

Beneath it, the floor bears its weight.
