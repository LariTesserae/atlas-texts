The power first notices the curls.

They gather at the creature’s feet, white and weightless, and are carried away almost as quickly as they are cut. There should be no pile. Yet there is a pile. The hands are working faster than the mountain can conceal their work.

It does not mistake this for a request that the mountain cease.

For a while it attends to the whole hollow: the green strand rooted crookedly, the animal opening its empty fans, the black points beneath the knob. It follows the water through places where water has not yet made a place. It finds the spoon’s groove and the abandoned pit. Outside, it feels the little hand close on bark.

Then it attends to the larger creature’s fingers.

One nail has split. The creature is using the finger anyway, turning it so that the sound edge does most of the cutting.

The power comes near enough to be felt.

Not as a voice. A voice would be another violence in the water.

At the creature’s palm, resting against the new wall, one impact arrives differently from the others. It is small and round and has no sharpness in it.

The creature stops cutting.

The stone continues to narrow.

After a moment the creature puts its good nail to the edge again.

The power waits until the hand is flat against the wall, then makes the same contact. Once. In the same place.

The creature draws back. Its skin darkens along the arms.

Nothing follows it.

It reaches forward again, this time with two fingers rather than the whole hand.

The contact meets them.

For some time, that is all that happens.

Outside, the small creature opens its hand and fails to find the fiber it had been holding. Its fingers remain extended.

The larger one turns toward the entrance.

The power does not detain it.

Instead, where the wedge holds the new gap apart, the soft mineral bends. It gathers around the wood without gripping it. Along the top of the opening, a clear ridge thickens, curving from one side to the other.

The creature feels this before it sees it.

It returns its hand to the gap. The ridge takes the pressure that had been bearing inward. Below it, the opening remains wide enough for a wrist.

The creature waits through three surges.

Then it pulls out the wedge.

The opening holds.

It puts the wedge back, but loosely.

The power leaves it so.

***

The creature goes outside with the spoon full.

Beneath the overhang, it uncovers the small one and pours water along its back. The little hand brushes the shell’s rim. For an instant it catches there.

The power sees the length of cord between the two knots. It sees the larger creature touch it before drawing the bark closed.

It does nothing to that empty length.

It turns its attention instead to the water caught in the fold. At the outer edge, where the bark has stiffened with ice, it eases a small amount of heat from the four yellow stones back toward the center. Not enough to warm the ridge. Enough that the water against the little body remains water.

The larger creature pauses with its fingers on the bark.

It opens the fold again.

The small one is still curled around its stone. Its hand has found the shell rim a second time.

The creature lowers the spoon and lets it hold on.

***

Within the hollow, the power learns which blows convey news and which are only blows.

It is not a distinction it can make from force alone. Sometimes a very sharp collapse announces a roof about to give way. Sometimes a gentler one means nothing at all.

It listens where the creature listens, through the hand laid against the floor.

When a cluster of bubbles crowds the fresh edge of the tilted blade, the power loosens their gathering. They vanish farther apart. The water still carries the shape of the blade through the creature’s bones, but the creature’s fingers do not clench.

Elsewhere, it leaves the impacts unchanged.

The gray animal catches a speck in one fan and contracts.

The green strands flatten and rise.

The mountain continues.

***

By morning the creature has made six trips to the ridge.

Each time it returns, it checks the new opening. On the fourth return, it removes the wedge and carries it back to the entrance. It pushes it between the white flakes, beside the other two, and tightens its tether.

On the sixth, it goes through the new opening shoulder first.

The sheltered pocket is not smooth. There are places to grip. Water enters along the floor and turns slowly beneath the roof before passing out again. A membrane trembles in one corner.

The creature presses its mouth to it.

Then it rests.

For the first time since evening, its hands are empty.

The power attends to the pocket while it sleeps. When new mineral gathers, it allows the floor to rise a little, and the far wall to thicken. Near the opening, it lets the water brush away what has not yet hardened.

It does not make the pocket larger than it needs to be.

***

The small creature is carried inside three days later.

Its back has opened only a little. The larger one brings it wrapped in wet bark, holding the bundle against its chest with both arms. It crosses by its feet, slowly, stopping beneath the blade until the surge has passed.

At the new opening it hesitates.

Then it enters.

The small one is laid on the low shelf beside the trembling membrane. Water passes beneath its arms. A thread of it emerges along the back.

The larger creature keeps one hand behind the little head.

There is no sudden change. The small body tightens, relaxes, tightens again. Its fingers catch in the bark. The larger creature eases the fiber free and offers its nail.

The hand closes around it.

Through the roof comes the pale ordinary daylight. Beyond the opening, the hollow roars.

The power stays near.

After a long while, the larger creature takes its other hand from behind the small one’s head and lays it against the floor.

One small, rounded contact meets the palm.

The hand rests there.

Then the creature turns back to its young.
