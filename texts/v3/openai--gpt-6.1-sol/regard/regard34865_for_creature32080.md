The power came without arriving.

There was no brightening beneath the cloud, no cool current moving through the warm leaves. The creatures that paused did so for their own reasons. A swimmer turned in the crease of a flank. Somewhere beyond the basin, a heavy browser lifted its head and resumed chewing.

For a long while, the power attended to the place where the roots almost met.

It watched the animal lift its mouth from the shoot. It watched the shoot stand free. There was no wound in the stem, but there was sap, and the animal swallowed before answering the distant call.

The power did not mistake the absence of injury for the absence of life.

Nor did it mistake the animal’s familiarity for proof of imprisonment.

It listened through several calls and their answers. It followed the faint changes in pressure beneath the broad feet, the passage of water through the skin’s narrow folds. When the animal moved, the power attended to what made it move: the taste diminishing on one surface, another body passing nearby, a sound from a favored hollow.

Once, the animal left a place before it had finished feeding.

Once, it remained after the others had gone.

These things mattered.

The power could have laid a clear sky above the country. It could have given the stone a harder grain, drawn the water into rivers, taught the trees to cast long shadows. A thousand kinds of beauty were within its reach.

But the animal had never asked to see a horizon.

Its eyes were fitted to the small distances between roots. Its mouth knew surfaces that a wind might dry. Coolness, offered carelessly, would enter the bodies here as something without a name, and some would withdraw from it, and some would not know how.

So the power left the heat where it was.

It let the flowers face the bark.

It did not open the shoot.

Instead, it looked closely at the animal’s return.

There was the depression, unchanged. There was the foot finding it, lifting, settling farther inward. There was the slight turn that brought the mouth against the stem.

Nothing forced the last movement. The animal could have turned toward the mineral rim. It could have passed under the root and followed the narrow water into the next basin. Its body knew those ways also.

The power followed them.

It went through wet seams too small for the animal, through the hollow stems occupied by narrower lives, through the dark chambers in the crowns. It listened where calls became a murmur and where the murmur separated into calls again.

Nowhere did it find a command that the world remain as it was.

Neither did it find a plea that the world be changed.

There were appetites, discomforts, preferences. A foot withdrew from a crowded ledge. Two bodies shifted until both could rest. A small animal called from beneath an adult, and the adult lowered its muzzle.

The power answered none of these by replacing them with something easier. It watched whether their answers could reach them.

At the basin’s edge, the animal slept.

The pale shape beneath its throat moved with its breathing. The power saw it clearly, but did not bring it nearer the skin merely to settle what it was. The animal had no need to become transparent.

A young one approached through the leaves.

It tested the rim with one foot, then another. Its mouth touched the sleeping animal’s flank. Finding no response, it pressed farther under the belly, carrying a thin strand of vegetation across its back.

The larger animal woke enough to lift its weight.

That was all. The youngster entered the shelter. The belly lowered, leaving room.

The power stayed.

Not as a wall around the country. A wall would have made an inside, and eventually something might have wished to leave.

Not as a law against loss. There were mouths here, and what entered them did not remain outside.

It stayed with its attention open, ready to distinguish a creature’s passing discomfort from a distress it could not escape, a cherished place from a place it could not leave. If such a difference appeared, the power would not require it to become a cry before responding.

For now, there was nothing to mend.

Rain began somewhere beyond the nearest stand of trees. Before it reached the basin, the leaves carried its sound inward, each layer answering differently. The animal opened one amber eye.

A drop fell onto the unused forefoot.

The pads closed slightly around the water, then spread again. The animal slid the foot beneath its chest and raised itself. The youngster emerged on the other side, dragging its strand of vegetation into the pool.

A call came from the next basin.

This time the animal answered before it fed. Water trembled between the roots. The shoot stood against its cheek, still tightly furled.

Then it turned, and went toward the voice.

The power went on attending to the hollow after it had left: the swimmers entering the newly spacious water, the leaf tip settling, the pale root with nothing resting against it.

It kept no place empty for the animal’s return.
