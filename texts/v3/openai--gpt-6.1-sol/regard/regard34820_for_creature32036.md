The first thing the power does is remain outside the door.

Its attention passes through the water without making a current. The silver animals do not turn. The red cups continue leaning toward the pumps. Beneath the inspection pane, the large disk completes the last adjustment of its upper edge.

For a while, nothing is changed.

The power watches long enough to distinguish a journey from a flight, a resting place from an available surface. It watches the smaller disks separate from the larger one. It watches one disappear into the trench and another find purchase beneath the great leaf. It watches the young animal near the door eat around the shoot’s roots without eating the roots themselves.

It learns that the warm pipe supplies more than warmth.

It learns that the machinery’s pauses are not all alike.

It learns very little about the ring of moisture on the outer wall.

This does not trouble it. The ring is not causing harm. There is time to leave it unexplained.

Far below, in a lifting station with no floor left around its machinery, a bearing is wearing through its final layer of metal.

The rasp in the cistern comes from there.

The power descends through the manufactured mountain. It passes chambers full of obsolete gauges, stairways ending at plates welded across doorways, reservoirs whose water has become thick with blind growth. Some pipes are empty. Some contain communities larger than the one at the summit. Along one warm return line, animals have built a continuous pale crust for nearly a kilometer.

The power follows the water onward.

There is still an onward. There are still places receiving it.

It does not shut anything down.

At the failing bearing, it finds a channel that once delivered lubricant. The channel is plugged with a hard deposit, and the reservoir above it has been dry for a very long time. Simply filling the reservoir would send a mass of loosened grit into the moving surfaces.

So the power clears the channel from its lower end. It removes the deposit grain by grain. It supplies a little lubricant, waits through several revolutions, and supplies a little more.

In the cistern, the rasp softens.

The large disk tightens against the wall.

For the rest of that afternoon it does not move.

The power waits.

By the following night, the disk has reached the warm pipe. Its scales stand open in the current. A scavenger crosses its back, stops beside a lodged bubble, and works there with its front limbs.

The power resumes its work below.

It takes many days to save the bearing. During those days, the rasp becomes less harsh without quite disappearing. The worn surfaces remain worn. Their unequal motion still carries through the mountain, but they no longer cut one another deeper.

The heavy knock continues.

The pump stroke continues.

No animal is required to learn a new world all at once.

***

There is a crack behind the highest pipe mouth.

It is not visible from inside the room. It begins in a supporting rib beyond the ceramic and runs toward the old valve. Each time the wind bends the summit, its edges move by less than the thickness of a snail’s shell.

One winter, or perhaps several winters from now, it would open.

The power holds the rib in its proper shape and joins the crack from the dry side. It leaves the valve alone. It leaves the ceramic repairs around the lowest mouth, including the small cavities occupied by scavengers.

When it is finished, the wall still flexes in a storm.

The fibers still rise into their revolving cloud. The silver swimmers still turn toward the window. The repair carries the load without taking away the movement by which the room knows the weather.

Under the inspection walkway, the ribbons sway.

***

The power considers the disk’s missing feet.

It watches those that remain reach, fasten, and release. It watches a divided foot grip two small irregularities at once. There is no open wound. The animal feeds well, and its journeys are unimpeded.

It leaves the feet as they are.

Later, when a meeting between two disks opens a fresh crescent beneath the larger animal, the power attends to that wound. It keeps a small accumulation of grit from lodging in it. Nothing more is needed. The tissue closes in its own time.

The other disk is not driven away.

Both return to the gray patch on different mornings.

***

The power could make the inspection pane clear.

Instead it removes only a dark accretion forming along the outside edge, where it would eventually spread across the glass. The old cloudiness remains. So does the division between the bright and dim sides of the room.

On the next clear afternoon, the green stain beneath the large disk’s covering broadens.

Its upper margin lifts slightly. Water passes beneath it.

Outside, fresh droplets appear at the top of the ring.

The power watches them join.

***

Near the door, the shoot puts out a third leaf.

The leaf is smaller than the first two. During a pump stroke it strikes the worn frame and bends backward. Afterward it remains caught in a shallow stamped symbol.

The power waits through the next stroke.

The leaf comes free by itself.

Below it, the young disk has grown too broad to fit entirely beneath the screw head. Part of its margin now projects into the current. It grips the metal there with new feet, pink and nearly transparent.

One night it leaves the threshold.

Its progress is uncertain. Twice it returns to the screw hole. On the third departure it passes beyond the reach of the bending stem and begins examining the wall beneath the ceiling forest.

The power does not put it on the route to the pipe.

There is food where it has gone. There are sheltered places ahead.

***

In a dry chamber below the summit, the power gathers a modest reserve: material for the bearing, a sound seal fitted to the warm pipe, clear water held apart from dust. Nothing is connected yet. Nothing enters the cistern.

It will be there when it is needed.

Then the power withdraws enough for the mountain to pass through its ordinary days without intervention.

The pumps lift. The valve knocks. The great leaf folds and sends its mild current along the floor.

Beneath the pane, the large disk loosens its lower feet, settles more closely into the groove, and rests.

On the other side of the wall, a bead of cool water grows heavy enough to descend. It follows an old track through the dust.

The power remains nearby.
