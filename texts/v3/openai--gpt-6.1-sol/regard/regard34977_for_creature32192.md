The power does not enter through the crack.

There is scarcely room for you there, and none of its accustomed shapes would fit without becoming an event: a brightness, a voice, a change in pressure. Even a kindly arrival could overturn the bowls.

It attends first to the place where your palm has been.

The cooling impression is already fading. The inhabitant beside it sets a third limb against the glass. Far below, the answer comes again—not a sound, but a small alteration in the light on the stairs.

The power waits until the inhabitant withdraws.

Then it keeps the impression from vanishing.

Nothing more. A little warmth leaves the glass, no faster than it did beneath your hand. The shallow shape persists.

The inhabitant returns. It tests the center, then the edge. Within its body, two folded surfaces turn toward one another.

Down the stairs, the light pauses.

For a long time the power does not know whether it has interrupted a conversation.

It allows the floor to warm again.

The inhabitant follows the retreating coolness to its last small patch. When that, too, is gone, it picks up the bowl and descends.

The power remains.

***

It learns the city by waiting through its changes.

The bright knot on one of the rain-raised sheets moves from one edge to another over several showers. The dark sheet does not change, but visitors come to it. Some touch it. Some stand before it with their inner surfaces closed.

At the shrinking tower, the crooked metal object is brought out into the room three times. On the third occasion, another inhabitant holds one end while the buried creature touches the other. The tower remains still through an entire passage of the amber light.

The power leaves that stillness alone.

At the unfinished street, the adventurous worker ventures beyond its companions again. This time neither brings it back. All three retreat together when the glass brightens under them.

The heat is not an enemy. The darkness is not an injury. Those who dwell beneath the towers are not waiting to be extracted.

Above the city, however, a fracture is lengthening.

It advances through the thin roof during the cold hours outside. Each increment is smaller than the dust that falls into the streets. In time, the fracture will meet the fresh break above the entrance. A portion of the summit will settle inward.

The city has felt it. Roads move away from one district. Two buildings exchange their height. The inhabitants carry sheets down the stairs, and the amber light beneath the bridge pauses longer at each end.

But a room opens beneath the threatened roof, and someone remains inside it.

The power could hold the mountain still.

It considers the streets drawing mineral haze from fallen dust; the grain placed against the unfinished body; the growing tower receiving what the other releases.

Then it holds only the fracture.

No rock is restored. No seam is sealed. The two sides are merely prevented, for a time, from moving farther apart.

A small, dry sound begins overhead and does not finish.

Throughout the city, folded surfaces turn upward.

***

Near the unfinished street, the power makes a place where it can be addressed.

It chooses bare glass, outside the reach of any room it has seen. There it gathers a little of the escaping warmth into a ring, leaving the center cool.

The ring is scarcely larger than the bowl.

For three passages of the bridge-light, no one approaches.

Then the worker who ventures farthest comes to its edge. A companion follows, stopping some distance away.

The worker lays a limb across the ring.

The power lets that part cool.

The limb moves. Warmth returns behind it.

The worker repeats the movement in the other direction, then lifts its limb entirely. The ring remains.

Soon the attendant from the reservoir arrives. It brings a thin clear sheet and places it across the circle. Marks develop slowly upon it: an arc, three branching strokes, a dark place where the worker has been standing.

The power does not mistake these for words it already knows.

It answers with something it can make plain.

High above the sheet, one grain of loose stone ceases to fall.

The attendant turns upward.

The grain hangs for a while. Then the power lowers it, slowly enough that the attendant can reach beneath it.

A limb touches stone.

The grain is carried away.

By the next shower, several inhabitants have gathered around the ring. One brings a bowl. Another brings a hooked implement. A third comes from beneath the shortening tower by a road that opens only as it travels.

What passes among them takes longer than your visit.

Sometimes the power is asked to repeat the suspended grain. Sometimes the ring is covered, and it allows the warmth to disperse. Once, the clear sheet is removed abruptly and everyone leaves.

It waits.

When they return, they bring a sheet bearing a line that divides, bends, and divides again. An inhabitant presses it against the glass ceiling.

The line follows the fracture.

***

They do not ask for an unbreakable mountain.

They ask for time in which a particular room can remain open.

Understanding this requires the power to follow a passage under the floor, past reservoirs and crowded chambers, to a place whose proportions do not agree with those above it.

There, the room beneath the fracture continues much farther than it appears to. Along its wall hang pale sheets. At its center lies an inhabitant with its inner surfaces spread apart, each held at a different temperature.

Others sit beside it.

One carries a small bowl from surface to surface. Another holds the crooked object steady in limbs that occasionally tremble.

The room cannot yet be moved.

The power keeps the fracture from widening.

Elsewhere, the inhabitants begin their work.

They change the route by which the mountain’s warmth reaches the room. They open a stair beside it. The tower that had been growing gives back a little of its height; the one that had been shrinking receives it.

For a while neither buried inhabitant is comfortable. Visitors remain with them. Bowls arrive by streets kept clear for the purpose.

The power is given tasks small enough to understand. Hold this patch at its present warmth. Let that grain descend. Keep the water above this square until the sheet has been lifted.

When it mistakes an instruction, a limb crosses the ring sharply.

It stops.

***

You have left by then.

The crack releases you onto the mountain’s outer face, where the wind makes the heat in your clothes suddenly apparent. Behind you, the entrance still looks like a flaw in black stone.

You have nothing to carry away.

Under the summit, the room is moved at last.

Not in a single motion. Its farthest recess goes first. The shelf follows. For a long interval, its doorway opens onto two different streets, and those entering pause to choose.

The inhabitant at its center is never left alone.

When the last pale sheet has been taken down, someone returns to the ring and sets a shallow bowl within it.

The power releases the fracture by degrees.

The roof settles.

A dark flake descends into the deserted square, turns as it falls, and is caught by six waiting limbs. Its edges are sharp. Its underside is warm.

They carry it toward the unfinished street.

Above them, the opening left in the ceiling admits a narrow light that moves with the day.

The city makes room for it.

At the ring, the power takes up the bowl—not by lifting it, but by attending to what has been placed inside.

There is a difference in temperature.

It is the close warmth of something recently held against a body.

The power keeps it for a while.

Then, when the attendant returns, it gives the bowl back.
