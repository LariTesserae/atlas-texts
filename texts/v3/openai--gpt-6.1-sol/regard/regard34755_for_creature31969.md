The power did not begin by making light.

Light would have brought certain mouths open and sent others shutting. It would have drawn hunters toward signals that meant nothing, and exposed animals whose safety depended on being indistinguishable from wet stone.

Nor did it quiet the basin.

It listened where listening meant taking weight.

For a long time it rested its attention against a slab beside one of the clear gaps. The slab was supported by three others, and the animal beneath it had grown through all four. Two of its mouths fed near a reliable seep. A third opened onto poorer ground, where grazers passed but seldom stopped. Beyond that mouth, a narrow portion of the body extended into the old shell field.

The power did not yet know whether this was one animal, or several whose union remained unfinished.

It waited.

There were signals that traveled across the whole body and signals that stayed near their source. One mouth closed when a particular dragging gait approached. Another admitted the same visitor. Fluid passed between them, but not evenly. At intervals, an inward fold tightened along an old seam.

The power learned the difference between a mouth resting and a mouth unable to open.

It learned that some of the long pauses between tooth strikes were full of feeding. Others were not.

It learned how far a warning could travel before the basin made it into something else.

When it first answered, it used the smallest pressure it could make: a touch against the underside of the slab, followed by a weaker touch.

All three nearby mouths closed.

The power withdrew the pressure.

For two days it did nothing further.

On the third day a colorless extension entered the place where the touch had occurred. It explored a grain of mineral, the edge of a pad, a shallow irregularity in the stone. Finding nothing, it returned.

The power repeated the touch, more faintly.

This time only one mouth closed.

That was the beginning. There was no moment in which the animal understood what had arrived. There was no declaration that could have crossed the distance between them. But over time the unfamiliar pressure ceased to mean an approaching weight. The animal began to examine it.

The power allowed itself to be examined.

It did not reach through the sensory knots to put recognition there. It received the extension as the stone received it, without pulling, without holding it when it turned away.

During one examination, a grazer caught its rear hooks in the lip and struggled. The animal began to close. The power could have freed the grazer. Instead it waited through the slow contraction, and the grazer tore loose with one leg left behind.

The lip opened again. A scavenger took the leg.

There was much to learn about mercy in a place where every exposed surface was already occupied.

***

The trouble lay below the shell field.

Water still reached the animal’s two productive mouths, but its composition had begun to change. The film they fed upon renewed itself less quickly. Far beneath the inhabited stone, a mineral crust was narrowing one of the supply channels.

The animal did not know the channel. It knew the longer interval between worthwhile scrapings. It knew what returned from a chamber after food had been held and tested there. Its distant mouths were receiving less.

It had already begun to move.

An extension advanced beneath the shell escarpment, finding damp hollows and broadening in them. Each new pad took hold. Each new mouth ate, though not yet enough to support the tissue that followed.

Above this route, a large fragment had settled badly.

The fragment was part shell and part cemented sediment, heavy enough to crush what lay beneath it. It was held by an arch of older material whose inner surface the animal was slowly grinding away.

The power saw the likely fall.

It also saw the animals living in the arch, the eggs fastened beneath the fragment, and the drainage groove that would open if the fragment shifted. To lift the whole mass away would leave much of that community exposed. To seal it permanently would close the route the gap animal had found.

For a while, the power considered restoring the deep channel.

Then it followed the water farther down. Opening that channel would lower pressure in another branch, one that fed a hollow crowded with animals the power had not yet watched. The intervention was small only from above.

So it returned to the fragment.

It supported the failing arch where the stone had already begun to split. It did not raise it. No egg lost its grip. No hidden chamber opened to the air.

The next time the animal tested the surface, the surface held.

It ground there for several more days.

The power waited.

Eventually the extension pushed beyond the dangerous weight and found a narrow passage into a neighboring seep. That seep was poor but steady. A mouth opened. A second pad broadened against the far wall.

The older body began to follow.

Not all of it followed. One of the original mouths continued feeding near the weakening supply. The narrow connection beside it grew thinner, then ceased to carry much fluid. After a long interval, it sealed.

The power did not put the portions back together.

Both had feeding tissue. Both had sensory knots. For some time they continued the sequences that had once passed through their shared body. Now the replies came through stone.

When the last fold had withdrawn from beneath the fragment, the power loosened its support by degrees.

A little grit fell first. Several animals left. Others pressed farther into their chambers.

The fragment descended less than the thickness of a lip and came to rest against a sounder ridge. Its movement sent a heavy pulse through the shell field. The new mouths closed, then reopened.

Water took the narrow space left behind.

***

The power stayed through a rising pulse.

Young crescents drifted through the connected pans. Some caught sheltered cracks. Some adhered to surfaces where they could not survive.

The power felt each of them.

It could have fitted a shelter around every exposed juvenile. But there was no unused stone to make those shelters from, no empty water to fill them. Even a new crack would divert traffic, collect sediment, change the passage of moisture. The basin would answer every addition.

It did not turn away from this difficulty, and it did not use the difficulty as a reason to do nothing.

Near the animal’s new mouth, one juvenile had fastened itself to the upper face of a shell fragment. Its two surfaces opened and closed against the same side of the stone. It had almost exhausted its stored food.

An adult extension passed near it twice without touching.

The power made a faint pressure beneath the fragment.

The extension paused.

It felt along the underside, withdrew, and came out again from another mouth. This time it reached the juvenile. Its outer folds opened against the exposed feeding chamber.

The small animal began to eat.

There the power stopped. The food belonged to the adult. The extension remained by its own contractions. When it eventually withdrew, the juvenile had enough strength to reach around the edge of the fragment and catch its opposing face.

Months later, its first tooth plate struck the shell.

The sound went scarcely farther than the stone that sheltered it.

An older mouth answered.

***

The power had no wish to make the animal grateful.

Gratitude would have required an account of the weakening seep, the supported arch, the pressure beneath the juvenile’s shell. The animal had other things to account for: a dragging visitor, the approach of a heavy hunter, food at one mouth and hunger at another.

Yet it knew something had become part of its surroundings.

Sometimes its extension searched a bare place beneath the slab where no water gathered and no grazer traveled. The power met it there with the faint, familiar pressure.

Sometimes the animal closed the nearest mouth.

When it did, the pressure ceased.

The basin remained cold. Predators settled across the routes. Water arrived late from weather far above. In the shell field, two animals that had once been joined sent uneven sequences through the floor, and the younger mouths grew between them.

There were still deaths the power did not prevent. It attended to them without making them serve a lesson. Where it could help without carrying the injury elsewhere, it helped. Where it did not yet know how, it stayed and learned.

At one clear gap, grains shifted uphill.

A slender organ emerged and laid its bead of water against the stone.

For a time, nothing passed through the opening. Then the organ withdrew. A plate struck once beneath the slab, and the lips parted for the next crossing.
