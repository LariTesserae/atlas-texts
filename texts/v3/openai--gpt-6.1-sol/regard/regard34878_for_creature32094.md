The power did not begin by lifting the landing.

It could have held the whole buried city in place while it moved the stone. It could have opened the hollow to daylight, stopped the drain, brought clean rain down through the foundations. None of these acts would have required effort.

It waited through a transfer cycle.

Before the intake stopped, the damaged animal beneath the landing folded its membranes. The torn side took longer. A fringe remained outside its pocket for a moment, trembling in a current that had not reached the others.

Then it was drawn in.

The power stayed through the exhalation, through the settling of grit, through the long interval before the animal opened again.

It watched the fountain. It watched the sedge roots, the lizards, the cloudy fluid inside the capsules. It followed moisture into the cracks without entering them. Where its attention might have warmed a surface or changed a pressure, it held that attention farther away.

There was much it could discover quickly. There were things for which discovery required waiting.

It did not assume that the stillness beneath the landing was peace, or that the damaged animal’s life was only distress. It learned which movements accompanied injury and which accompanied feeding. It learned how a body rested when moisture was sufficient. It watched the animals leave cracks that appeared, from outside, no different from the cracks they entered.

For a while, it did nothing that could be distinguished from weather.

Then it found the work order.

Far above, in an office with windows onto a service courtyard, the detached landing had become an item on a list. Its position was to be corrected. The seam beneath it was described as a fouled gap. The proposed repair called for lifting the stone, cleaning the surfaces, and injecting a compound that would expand until no cavity remained.

The work was sensible by the knowledge available to the people who had requested it.

The power did not punish them for that.

On the morning the order was due for approval, the inspector opened an old photograph. A maintenance fitting had happened to be open when it was taken. Beneath the landing, faint pink shapes extended from a joint.

The inspector enlarged the image.

The power had done no more than make the overlooked attachment the first one she saw.

She looked at a second photograph, then a third. In each, the shapes occupied slightly different positions.

The order was suspended.

***

The next inspection took longer.

The workers brought a light that could be turned very low. They laid nothing on the stair. One of them sat on the raised paving while the other kept watch on the drain.

They did not scrape a sample.

For forty minutes, their instruments recorded a grey seam.

Then one membrane appeared.

The worker beside the camera moved his hand toward the focus control and stopped. He waited until the second membrane had unfolded.

The damaged animal became the first clear image in a new record.

The record did not call it a seal.

***

This was useful, but insufficient.

The power had found a change approaching through the city’s foundations. A reservoir would be enlarged. New exchanges of weight would make the northern reports slightly more frequent, and the ventilation controllers, compensating for altered heat, would increase the last intake before each transfer.

Most adults could withstand the new current.

The damaged animal probably could not.

There were younger animals in other seams that would fare worse.

The power considered closing those passages to the air. But the same passages delivered food. It considered fastening each body more firmly to its stone. But the animals sometimes needed to leave.

Instead, along the underside of the landing, it loosened three grains of old mortar.

They fell over several days.

The small recess left behind diverted the strongest current just before it reached the seam. Air still passed beneath the stone. Spores still arrived. Condensation still gathered on the fringes. The change was scarcely measurable outside the handspan where the animals lived.

At the fountain and the freight door, other grains fell.

There was no new shelter across the whole district, no sudden abundance. The power watched each alteration through several cycles before making the next.

In one place, its first attempt caused a damp joint to dry.

It put the grains back.

***

On a high window ledge lay a curl that had not softened during the last rains.

The power could tell that it was alive, though there was little movement left to distinguish it from the husks beside it.

It did not carry the animal through the air.

When the western balconies next began to drip, one droplet followed a slightly different course. It struck the back of the ledge and spread until it touched the curl.

For a long time nothing happened.

Then the body loosened.

The animal put out its feet. Beneath it, a narrow wet route led into a joint deep enough to shelter it from the next exhalation. The crossing took most of the night.

The power waited until it was inside.

***

The damaged animal kept its notch.

The power could have restored the missing tissue. It had not ruled that out. For now, the animal was feeding, holding fast, and opening both membranes in the altered current. A repair would require changes within a body whose workings the power was still learning.

So it continued to watch.

The lizards still hunted. Water still carried some young into the basin. The district remained dark, and the city continued to shift its immense weight above it.

But the maintenance drawings now showed occupied seams. Repairs could be made around them. The drain warning was extended to the service schedule, giving workers time to withdraw their tools before the whistle began. No one flooded the joints with cleaning fluid.

Under the landing, the damaged animal opened first after a load exchange.

Its shorter membrane tipped toward the wall. The other reached into the slow air and gathered what came.

Farther along, a young animal had settled in a crack that had been empty for years. Its fringes were still sparse. They opened late and closed often.

The power remained close enough to notice.
