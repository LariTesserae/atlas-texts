The power first becomes noticeable as a place where the light is less insistent.

It is not dark. You can still see the joints in the clay and the fine hairs caught beneath a binding. But beside one of the resting platforms, the underside of a leaf is green instead of luminous.

The inhabitant beneath the platform opens its eyes.

Nothing reaches in to draw it out.

For a while the altered light remains where it is. Then it moves a little away from the platform, leaving the creature’s face as bright as before.

The pale hand comes out. Its repaired thumb touches the boundary.

There is no edge to grasp. The fingers pass through the dimness and return.

A small sound travels along the wood.

Across the court, an inhabitant lifts its face from a roof support. The creature beneath the platform sounds again. This time the answer comes from under the ground.

The power waits.

***

It could examine every layer of the basin at once.

It could discover where the water begins, what holds the sky apart from the ridges, how the fish receive life through their pleated chambers. It could distinguish every knot that has loosened from every knot deliberately left loose.

Instead it follows the inhabitant with the repaired thumb.

The creature has risen. It walks along the wet edge of the court, stopping occasionally to lay its palm against a support or to lower two fingers into a channel. At each stop it makes a sound.

Other sounds answer. Some are distant. One comes from the creature’s own chest.

At the lake it kneels beside a white post and keeps its hand there.

The power does not interrupt the interval.

Eventually an inhabitant with many narrow fingers comes from beneath the plant rafts. It brings a length of cord wound around a flat piece of clay. Together they carry it to the place where the light has changed.

They stretch the cord across that place, close to the ground.

The dimness falls over the cord.

They raise it.

The dimness rises with it.

They lower one end and leave the other high.

Now there is a slanting shelter, barely wide enough for two bodies.

The many-fingered inhabitant sits beneath it. The one with the repaired thumb remains outside.

After a time they exchange places.

***

The power is learning what can be offered.

There are things it does not yet understand. The leaf that never opens may be waiting, or resting, or being held against something the inhabitants know and the power has not asked about. The unfinished groove may be abandoned. It may also be somebody’s work, and unfinished in precisely the way that work must be allowed to remain.

The power has seen enough worlds to know how easily strength can mistake an interval for an injury.

It does not complete the groove.

It does not straighten the wings of the small clay bird.

When the bird’s owner approaches the altered light, carrying the object against its breast, the power lets the shadow fall on both uneven wings.

The owner sits down.

For the first time since arriving, it sets the bird beyond the reach of its own hand.

No one touches it.

***

Learning their language takes longer than learning their construction.

A tightening beneath the jaw does not always mean the same thing. It matters whether the speaker’s feet are in water, whether another body is resting against its back, whether the sound is answered through wood or air.

The power could give them a new language. It could make speech convenient for itself.

Instead it learns where to rest its weight.

A little pressure beneath one panel produces a sound that brings several inhabitants running. The power releases the pressure at once. It waits while they lift the panel and inspect the bindings below.

When they have finished, it tries again, elsewhere, with much less force.

This sound causes no alarm.

The many-fingered inhabitant replies by drawing a strand along the rim of a bowl.

They repeat the exchange.

Sometimes the power’s answer is so badly formed that both inhabitants lean forward and make the soft rattling sound heard beside the lake.

The power repeats that sound too.

They stop.

Then the repaired thumb taps the bowl twice, and the rattling begins again.

***

What troubles them is not what the power expected.

They do not ask for rain. They do not ask for fruit that falls, or birds that sing, or a sun that crosses the sky.

One asks for a binding to be held.

It is an awkward binding beneath the shoulder of a sleeping creature. Two inhabitants have been tending it. One supports the shoulder while the other works, but the support must be given from inside a narrow opening, and the supporting hand has grown hot.

The power holds the binding where the hand has held it.

The hand withdraws.

Its owner lays it in a watercourse and keeps it there for a long time.

Nothing else is changed. The sleeping creature’s repaired shoulder remains visibly repaired. When the work is finished, the power releases its hold.

Another asks whether the dim place can be moved.

It can.

Another asks, in a sequence of sounds the power must hear several times, whether it will move when no one wants it to.

It will not.

They devise a fastening for it: two knots tied close together, which the inhabitants can separate to let the light return. The power makes sure the fastening works without its attention.

By the lake, one creature unties it simply to see.

The bright air comes back.

After inspecting the cord, the creature ties it again.

***

At the workbench, the inhabitant who has been sitting beside the unfinished ground finally picks up the blade.

The power keeps its attention elsewhere until the creature calls.

Then it comes close enough to feel the roll of leaves breathing.

The inhabitant indicates the groove. It touches the blade, its own wrist, and the place where the groove ends.

The power offers support beneath the wrist.

The creature pulls its hand away.

The power withdraws.

They sit beside each other through several slow expansions of the leaves.

At last the inhabitant takes a strand from its belt and ties one end to the frame. It holds the other end, leans backward, and makes a low, strained sound.

The power takes up the tension.

The frame becomes steady.

That is what was wanted.

The blade enters the clay. A shaving curls against its edge. The groove lengthens by less than the width of a finger.

The inhabitant sets the blade down.

The power continues holding the frame until the cord is untied.

***

There is one request that comes from many places.

It begins beneath a grove, in the large body through which water passes. A smaller creature listens with its cheek against the curved shoulder. It carries the sound to the lake. From there it travels through the courts, changing as different mouths and bindings give it passage.

The power listens to the whole of it.

They want to know whether it will remain.

The question contains no demand that it stay forever. It contains resting hands, work that cannot be done alone, and the long distance between one waking body and another. It contains the possibility of calling and receiving no answer.

The power does not promise that nothing will ever be difficult again.

It settles its presence into a shallow bowl beside the first platform.

The bowl had already been there. Its rim is worn on one side by a particular hand.

Water fills it. Across the bottom lies a short dark strand.

The power shows them how to draw the strand against the rim, and what sound will reach it. It listens while each of them tries. When the smallest inhabitant cannot make the sound, it changes how softly the bowl will answer.

It leaves the strand loose enough to lift out.

***

Much later, the basin is still quiet.

The birds stand far apart on their white posts. The fish turn through the rootwork. Beneath the sky’s faint seams, the paths remain bright and their edges wet.

There are a few dim places now.

Not every grove has one. One shelter has been taken down and its cord used for something else. Another shades only the face of a sleeper whose feet remain in the warm light.

At the workbench, the groove extends a little farther.

The power has not asked for a house. When no one calls, it rests beside the shallow bowl, close enough that a hand reaching from beneath the platform can find it.

Sometimes the repaired thumb touches the rim without drawing the strand.

The power makes no sound then.

It has learned that this, too, is something to answer by staying still.
