The power notices the little company beneath the folded animal.

It notices the six moist bodies, the pale creature with its grain of mineral, and the traveler that has begun moving before the forest gives any visible reason to leave.

For a while, it goes with them.

It does not take a shape. A shape would have weight, and weight here is an instruction that the country might misunderstand. It travels instead in the cool interval toward which the animal has turned its eyes.

The traveler pauses at the interval’s edge.

One of its filaments has not come free.

It runs backward through the root felt, beneath a slab of black tissue, into a seam that is already closing. The animal draws against it. Along its flank, a row of pores opens and shuts. The smaller bodies press farther into its folds.

Behind them, something immense begins to settle.

The power could open the seam. It could lift the forest, still the buried cylinders, hold the direction of weight wherever it chose. But the filament is attached to a root, and the root encloses a cool chamber in which small things are feeding. Opening the seam would split that chamber. Holding the forest would leave the next grove waiting for a change that never came.

The power attends more closely.

The trapped filament is not caught along its whole length. There is a place, no wider than a pore, where the pressure will ease as the slab turns.

It keeps that place open a little longer.

The traveler pulls.

The filament slips free, wet and dark, and gathers into the body. The animal moves on. Beneath the slab, the root chamber remains closed.

Nothing nearby looks up.

***

At first, the power helps in this manner.

A membrane catches on a sharp edge; the edge crumbles.

A calf loses contact with the luminous line above it; a neighboring elder feels an unfamiliar warmth along one shoulder and steps nearer.

A pool animal has waited too long on bare stone. Its fronds are stiffening. Moisture gathers beneath its body—not a new pool, not a promise that the place it remembers will come to exist, but enough for it to loosen its limbs and return to water.

Some accept these small changes immediately.

Others retreat from them.

The power does not follow the retreating ones with further gifts. There are inhabitants here for whom a sudden coolness means a mouth overhead, and others for whom an easy passage is the first sign that distance has become unreliable.

Help has a texture. It has a temperature and a smell. It can resemble danger.

The power learns to let its work be examined.

***

Farther inland, a column of pale creatures crosses a cleft.

The last animal braces against both walls. Its fingers find the resonances that keep the passage short. Ahead, the column flows through a low grove, each creature placing its limbs where the previous one has placed them.

The rear guard waits.

A straggler is coming. One limb has broken at its narrowest joint. It advances by reaching, fastening, dragging the damaged side after it.

The walls begin to move apart.

The older creature spreads itself farther. A fine fracture appears along its body.

Here the power does not settle for a smaller kindness.

It takes the strain.

For a moment, the old animal’s fingers press against something that is neither wall nor air. The passage holds. The straggler comes through.

The rear guard lifts one hand, then another.

It remains suspended after its last attachment has loosened. Its smooth head turns. The power gives it time to find the next surface, and when it does, the unfamiliar support withdraws.

Behind it the cleft lengthens into a valley.

The old creature rests among the roots. No other animal touches it. Presently the wounded straggler comes back and lays two fingers against its side.

They remain there until the underground vibration changes.

***

There are places the power does not cool.

The warmth in the new fissures brings the first roots forward. The dry shelves feed the mineral herds. The canopy hunter’s damp shelter belongs to a living body that must eat.

The power watches a fold lower around a sleeping grazer. It feels the hunter’s hunger and the grazer’s alarm.

Before the fold closes, it loosens a dry frond beside the sleeper.

The grazer wakes and withdraws.

Later, the hunter feeds elsewhere. The power continues watching. It does not pretend that one escape has answered every need in the grove.

Its attention grows more exacting, not less.

It finds suffering that serves nothing: a living body pinned beneath shed bark, a mouth sealed by a mineral crust, a brood trapped in a chamber whose entrance closed before their limbs hardened.

These it frees.

Where pain must warn a creature to move, it leaves the warning and softens what comes after. Where an injury can heal, it helps the tissues meet. Where a body is dying and cannot be restored without taking apart everything it has become, the power stays beside it, easing the fear and thirst.

The country continues rising.

***

In time, certain pauses become dependable.

They are small places, scarcely distinguishable from ordinary hollows. Their stone is warm but never burning. Their moisture is meager but lasts. Weight changes gently there, and the entrances remain near their exits even when the surrounding distances stretch.

The power makes no animal enter them.

It leaves them where migrations already pass, and it allows the inhabitants to alter them. Roots thicken their roofs. Burrowers open galleries. Broad travelers fasten their edges across the floors.

One canopy hunter spreads over a hollow and begins to cool it.

The power does not drive it away. It gives the shelter more than one entrance, and keeps the low vibration from being muffled beneath the hunter’s body. Those who rest there can still hear the country changing.

The first occupants leave without incident.

Others follow their scent.

Soon the pauses are part of what the animals know.

***

The folded traveler reaches one of them after crossing three altered slopes.

It unfolds slowly. Its freed filament is tender, and several of its pores have remained closed throughout the journey.

The six small bodies emerge. Two feed along its margin. The others disappear into a crease in the root felt.

The pale creature climbs onto its back.

It presses the grain of magnetic stone into a soft pit and holds it there with both forelimbs.

Beneath them, the buried cylinders turn.

For the first time since the departure, the traveler lies entirely flat. Its eyes face outward. None turns toward the sky.

The power rests its attention around the hollow.

Beyond it, mountains continue to rise through the clouds. New forests take hold. Animals gather, feed, fail, shelter one another, and move.

Here, for a while, there is time enough to open every pore.

When the traveler finally gathers itself, nothing keeps it.

The small company climbs back into its folds. It tests the ground ahead, chooses a cool passage between the roots, and goes.

The hollow remains.
