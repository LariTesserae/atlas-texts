The power does not still the water.

For a long while, that is all it does not do.

It does not brighten the chamber, draw the heat from the pools, or lift the creature out of its crevice into some gentler place. It watches the surge stop short of the pebbles. It watches the porous stalk withdraw after the answering click.

Then it waits through another surge.

And another.

There are forms of attention that resemble hunger. This is not one of them. Nothing in the chamber is made to reveal itself faster because it has been noticed.

The power follows the warmth downward, but lightly. It learns where the water widens into reservoirs, where it is pressed through narrow beds, where the buried mechanism takes its strength. It finds no simple division between the living thing and the work it performs. The animal eats what would obstruct the arch. The arch makes something available in the water that the animal needs. The hollows in the stone carry impressions too faint for a human hand to distinguish: pressure, mineral taste, intervals of warmth.

The power does not call these things commands merely because they are repeated.

Nor does it call them choices merely because the creature moves freely between them.

It stays long enough to learn more.

The visitor eventually climbs the stairs. Before the visitor leaves, the power gives them no revelation. It lets their attention settle, once more, on the line of pebbles.

That much they can understand.

***

Over the following cycles, the inhabitant emerges twice.

The first time, it cleans a throat between two basins and spends a long interval beneath an overhang, its underside working against the warm current.

The second time, it returns to the arch.

At the newly fitted plate, one fine hand passes over the corners. The plate has seated well. No water slips behind it. The creature moves on.

But at the next recess, where an older plate has begun to cloud, the small hands remain folded.

A contraction travels beneath the flap on its back.

It waits through a click, then withdraws to the dish.

The power attends to this without supplying a story.

Later, when the animal is resting, the power makes a very small change.

On the stone beside the two sediment-filled dishes, it lays a rectangle of translucent material.

Not in a recess. Not upon the animal’s back.

Within reach, and unattached.

The rectangle is not an exact copy of the living plate. There are things the power could imitate but will not counterfeit without cause. This piece is mineral throughout, with passages finer than the cracks that hold the roots of the fronds. It can receive the arch’s suspended thread. It can yield if the water presses too strongly. It has no appetite, and nothing inside it will suffer if it is broken.

Then the power withdraws its attention a little.

When the inhabitant next approaches, one stalk turns toward the unfamiliar object.

A hand touches it.

The hand retreats.

For most of a quiet interval, the rectangle remains where it was placed.

Then the animal lifts it, carries it to a sheltered hollow, and presses one corner against the stone. It turns it over. Water enters the tiny passages and leaves through the opposite face.

The animal breaks off an edge.

The power allows the edge to break.

The fragment goes beneath the body. The rest is left in the hollow.

Nothing else is offered.

***

It takes seven more visits before the rectangle reaches the arch.

By then it has been scraped, immersed in three pools, and carried once into the submerged passage. Its corners are rounded. One surface bears a shallow groove made by the creature’s fingers.

The inhabitant holds it against the cloudy recess through two clicks.

On the third, it pushes.

The old plate comes free with a brittle snap.

For an instant the upward light falters. The animal flattens, every broad foot fastening to stone.

The power does not take over.

The fine hands turn the new rectangle, find its proper face, and fit it into place.

A narrow line of light appears along the lower edge. Then the line closes.

Under the flap on the animal’s back, the growing plate remains attached.

The creature goes to the dish. Its expanding movements are slow. The porous stalk rests against the rim.

Far below, something in the buried mechanism settles into a slightly different rhythm.

The power listens through an entire cycle before making a second rectangle.

***

There are other things it can do without asking the chamber to become another chamber.

It strengthens the hidden fastening of the lowest metal staple, whose exposed surface still looks sound although the stone around its buried end has begun to loosen.

It does not straighten the staples. Their uneven spacing suits the old route.

It roughens a patch of the broad island no larger than two human feet. It leaves the rest smooth, including the surfaces over which the inhabitant swings itself in the current.

At the entrance, it opens a shallow groove where condensation has been running across the first dry step. The moisture now drains back into the chamber. The groove will look like water’s work because, from then onward, it will be.

These changes are tested against the next surges. One is altered. Another is undone when a thin run of water no longer reaches the dark growth beside the fronds.

The power has time to notice its mistakes.

It uses that time.

***

When people return, they find no treasure arranged for them.

They find the arch working, the light moving beneath the water, and a pale inhabitant occupied with something under a ledge.

The safest shelf is still small.

A visitor who tries to cross the chamber without waiting will still be frightened. There is no need to remove that fear; some of it is accurate.

But the loose step no longer turns beneath a heel. The damp landing drains. And the line of pebbles, once someone has understood it, is difficult to forget.

One visitor adds a stone to the line.

The inhabitant later moves it half a hand’s width toward the wall.

The next visitor leaves it there.

***

The power does not remain as a voice in the arch.

It leaves no promise that every cloudy plate will be replaced, that no flood will ever exceed its old channels, or that the creature must continue its work forever.

It leaves a way for the inhabitant to make itself felt.

One of the sheltered hollows—the one where the first rectangle was tested—now carries a little farther than the others. Pressure placed there travels through the stone beyond the chamber. A touch is enough. So is a broken fragment laid inside it. The hollow can also be left empty.

For a long time, it is.

Then, after many surges, the creature comes out beneath the stairs with a pebble held between its fine fingers.

It carries the pebble across the channels, past the arch, to the altered hollow.

It sets it down.

The power returns its attention.

It does not yet know what the pebble means.

It waits nearby while the inhabitant rests, and when the porous stalks emerge again, it is still there.
