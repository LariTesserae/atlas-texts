The power does not mistake the valley for a thing arrested.

It watches the curled leaves long enough to see what passes through them. Water enters a fold and leaves by another route. Pressure moves along a seam. A tower, holding the same outline through the afternoon, shifts its weight between thousands of fibers.

To remain this way is something the valley does.

The power watches the residents too. It sees them correct a channel, then leave another apparently similar channel alone. It sees one press a palm against a trembling partition and hold it there long after the sound has cleared.

It does not yet know what that means.

So, for a time, it takes care not to add its ignorance to their work.

It casts no shadow. It brings no salt. It does not make the suspended roots reach a bottom, or give the chambers doors of a familiar size. Beneath the clouds, the wet columns continue downward through distances the power could illuminate.

It leaves those distances dark.

***

The chamber-dweller first notices it beside the ringing conduit.

There is a pause in the note, too brief for a person to distinguish from the ordinary disturbances of water. The creature lifts its cheek from the stem.

The pause comes again.

It puts three fingertips against the conduit. Inside the stem, water divides around a thickened rib, rejoins, and descends. The creature presses harder. The pitch lowers.

The power follows it down.

For a while they make a sound together.

Then the creature removes its hand.

The power lets the conduit ring as it rang before.

The creature waits. It touches the stem once more, lightly this time.

The power answers lightly.

***

This is how it begins to learn what an invitation looks like here.

Not every touch is one. Often a hand is simply carrying weight. Often a body fitted into a wall wants the warmth of the wall, and nothing else.

The power makes mistakes small enough to withdraw from.

Once it steadies a loose channel lip just as the chamber-dweller approaches. The creature stops. Its raised fingers close beneath its chest. After a long interval, it turns away.

The power releases the lip.

By evening the creature has returned and pressed it down itself. It holds the tissue until a narrow stream runs cleanly over the edge.

Afterward it sits beside the channel, one shoulder lowered.

The power watches without helping.

***

The brown mark is not hidden from it.

It can perceive the tightened fibers beneath the scar, the routes water takes around them, the way the creature sets its foot before reaching with that side. It could separate the three ribs. It could make the flesh pale and supple.

But it has seen the creature use the stiff place to brace against a wall. It has seen another resident lay two fingers along the mark and remain there, both of them still.

It does not know everything the scar belongs to.

One afternoon, when the chamber-dweller is resting, the power makes a little movement in the moisture beneath its hand. A cool line travels toward the scar, then stops.

The creature’s eyes open.

It draws its arm across its side.

The line of moisture disperses.

Nothing further is done.

Later, the creature returns to the ringing conduit. The power answers when touched. Neither repeats the other exchange.

***

There is a covered channel beneath the creature’s chamber whose vibration reaches the ledge.

At certain intervals the flow grows heavy. The wall above it tightens, and the chamber-dweller, wherever it has been resting, gets up. It presses its long fingers into a seam. Water gathers around its ankles. Sometimes it stays until morning.

The power listens through several such nights.

Then, in a fold beside the seam, it holds a small pocket open. No larger than a fingertip, it gives the water another way to pass. The creature feels the change and bends close.

The power closes the pocket.

The pressure returns.

It opens it again.

The creature places one finger inside.

For a moment, neither moves.

Then the finger presses sideways, widening the opening into a shape the power had not proposed. Water slips through with a low rattling sound.

The creature withdraws its hand and studies the seam. It presses the pocket shut.

The power lets it shut.

On the following night, the creature opens it again.

***

There is less work now in that particular chamber.

Not none.

The opening has to be felt and adjusted. Sometimes the creature closes it for days. Sometimes another resident comes and changes its narrow mouth, producing a note that brings the chamber-dweller out onto the terrace.

The power is not always summoned. It does not answer touches made to the valley as though they were all addressed to itself.

When the creature rests, it rests.

Once, halfway through an afternoon, it leaves the chamber while water is running hard beneath the ledge. It crosses the plain without hurrying and climbs to the conduit it prefers.

There it lays its cheek against the stem.

For a long time it only listens.

***

At the central basin, the power waits beside the circular wall.

The chamber-dweller arrives through shallow water. It lays its hands on the tender edge, smooths the fold, and lets go.

Nothing opens.

The power offers a slight pressure beneath one broad leaf. A ripple travels outward, meets the wall, and returns in pieces.

The creature watches until the basin is still.

Then it sets its hand in the water.

The power waits.

Five fingertips press, separately, against the leaf below. Five small disturbances spread across the reflection.

This time the power answers with one.

The creature’s transparent lids rise. Its hand remains where it is.

Around them the unfinished edge holds its shape. Far below, water travels down the pale roots. The valley carries its towers, its pools, its scattered inhabitants, and the little open passage beneath a chamber where someone may now be absent for an afternoon.

The next ripple belongs to the creature.
