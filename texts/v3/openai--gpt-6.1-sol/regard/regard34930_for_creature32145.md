The power did not put its foot into the opening.

For a long time, that was all it did.

It let the narrow space close. It watched the animal transfer its weight from a leaf collar to the hard rim of a grazer’s plate. It waited through the pause when the body belonged to both supports, and through the longer pause afterward, when one rear limb remained extended into a place the rest of the animal had left.

There was something caught beneath that foot.

Not trapped. Living there.

The power learned the difference.

***

It had arrived with the ability to alter the plateau.

It could have opened paths through the forest, softened the cold, brought rain over the escarpments. It could have made the reservoirs inexhaustible and the mineral currents quiet. These things were within its strength.

Their consequences were not yet within its understanding.

A path would be an absence made through many bodies. Rain would enter chambers adapted to collect a needle’s worth of water. Warmth would awaken mouths whose food was still asleep.

So it did not begin with a gift.

It began with the delays.

It learned how long a leaf took to answer light, how long a root took to answer weight, how long a small animal could remain motionless without being at rest. It attended to the pulses passing through the trees. At first it heard only a great overlapping announcement of occupation.

Later it began to distinguish requests.

Later still, refusals.

The plateau contained very little silence. But silence was not the same as consent.

***

The animal whose body had resembled a passage crossed three trunks and descended into a blue shadow.

There it stopped.

One of its lower chambers had failed to empty completely. The membrane folded around a pale knot of old injury. Whenever the animal transferred pressure toward that side, a valve tightened, and the limb beneath it trembled.

It had lived with the injury for many seasons.

Its route accommodated it. A certain crossing was always made downhill. A certain reservoir was approached from below. At night, the damaged chamber faced the trunk, where the cold reached it last.

The power watched these arrangements without undoing them.

Within the scar lived a colony of salt-feeders. Beside them, under a lip of hardened secretion, were the eggs of the blind hunter. The chamber itself had grown a second, smaller valve. It worked badly, but it worked.

There was no simple return to an uninjured body. There was this body, and what it had made of its hurt.

The power settled into the stone beneath the animal’s lowest foot.

It warmed a patch no larger than the foot’s soft center.

The animal tested it.

A tremor traveled down the limb. The stone answered as stone.

The hooks tightened.

After a while, the damaged chamber loosened. Pressure passed through the small valve. The animal moved one joint, then another, and took its weight off the painful side.

The power kept the warmth steady until the foot withdrew.

It did not follow under every foot.

***

Years passed, measured differently by each inhabitant.

The power learned the animal’s route without making the route safe in every respect. It learned which risks the animal could meet, which difficulties were feeding opportunities, which apparent failures were merely unfinished movements.

It also learned that waiting could become an excuse.

At the warm crack, during the season when the flying bodies cast their longest shadows, the rock shifted.

The displacement was slight. Across most of the plateau it registered as a change in pitch.

At the pool, it closed a passage through which water had been rising.

The surface lowered.

Bodies along the banks extended mouthparts farther into the dark. The transparent swimmers descended together. A root tightened across the crack, and beneath it the animal stood with its birth chamber open.

Five young had found holds.

The sixth remained attached to the chamber’s edge.

It was not moving slowly. It was losing warmth.

The branching organisms beneath its shell had spread incompletely. They formed two dark islands where there should have been a network. Sunlight struck the upper plate, but little of that heat reached the organs below.

The parent drew the chamber closer.

The young animal’s grip weakened.

This time the power did not wait to understand everything.

It reached into the warmth already passing through the parent’s chamber and gave that warmth another way across the juvenile’s body.

No blaze. No new organ. A temporary conductance, finer than a wet thread.

The little body contracted.

One foot opened.

Then it closed again on the parent.

The power held the connection.

Above them, the drinkers shifted as the pool continued to sink.

***

There was still water below.

The power followed the sealed channel into the rock. It found the two fresh surfaces pressed together, their mineral faces carrying a load that would have crushed a forest.

Breaking them apart would loosen the bank.

Instead, it made a passage through the stone beside them, no wider than one of the animal’s feelers.

Water entered.

The pool stopped falling.

It did not rise to cover the crowded banks. It returned, slowly, to the stain marking its old level.

The drinkers shortened their reach. The swimmers spread by fractions of an inch.

At the edge of the open chamber, the sixth young animal extended a limb toward a root.

The parent sent a pulse through its brace.

The root’s occupants answered at different times.

The young animal waited.

Then it found a hold.

***

The power remained until the dark branching growth had crossed the juvenile’s underside. As the living network strengthened, the borrowed conductance diminished. Eventually the animal kept itself warm without it.

By then the parent had closed its chamber.

It stood beside the pool for another four days, gathering pressure for departure. The young were already becoming difficult to distinguish among the roots.

One had fitted itself beneath a drinker’s shell.

One had chosen a crevice so narrow that its limbs would have to grow folded.

The sixth held a root above the water. Its signals were too strong. Small bodies recoiled when it announced that it meant to move.

The power listened as the replies arrived.

It did not correct the pulse.

***

It stayed in the high country.

Sometimes it acted. Sometimes a threatened body needed warmth, or a broken connection needed bridging until living tissue could close the gap. Sometimes there was no help it could give without doing worse harm nearby. It did not mistake those occasions for peace.

The forest slowly acquired a few changes difficult to attribute.

A failing collar remained warm through one cold night. A fractured perch bore weight until the animal using it had withdrawn. Deep under a crowded shelf, water continued along a route that the rising continent had almost closed.

Nothing appeared above the canopy.

No voice entered the air.

One morning the animal lifted itself into the sun. Its scarred chamber expanded, unevenly but without the old tremor. Visitors settled along its bright edges.

Far below, the power rested in a mineral seam.

A foot touched the stone.

Tested it.

Trusted it.

And, after a long interval, let go.
