The power first noticed the leaf that did not touch the ground.

Not because it was the tallest thing, or the most intricate, but because the distance beneath its tip was being kept.

A finger’s width of empty air. A narrow shadow. Within the rib, current leaving one thread and entering another. The whole weight of the leaf passing through a structure that renewed itself without letting go.

The power watched for a long time.

It did not arrive upon the plain with footsteps. There was no sudden brilliance among the stems, no voice carried through the dry air. Nothing in the grove had organs for receiving a voice. To speak there would have been chiefly to move the leaves.

Instead, it followed the work.

Down through the rib and the black joint, through the branching conduits and the close-fitting roots, it attended to the transfers by which the grove remained alive. Beneath the grove, its attention widened. A reserve released charge into a seam. A distant collar altered its load. Far below the empty ground, a partition was dismantled atom by atom while the difference it sustained was passed around it.

The power had known beings whose lives depended on interruption: hunger, sleep, the recurring need to leave one place for another. It did not assume that those needs were missing here merely because they were absent.

Nor did it assume that perfect maintenance was peace.

It looked for costs the plateau could not repair. It looked for signals repeatedly sent and never answered, for structures held in service beyond their capacity, for some local need lost in the overlapping routes beneath the stone.

There were imbalances. There were delays. A junction under the distant terrace had carried too much current for a time, and its neighboring tissues still retained extra capacity. One buried chamber had been abandoned when a mineral seam altered. Around its boundary, the active network had drawn back and sealed its surfaces.

But the power found no command requiring every part to remain as it was.

The plateau’s stillness was not enforced.

That mattered.

Among the unopened folds, it found arrangements for leaves broader than the present crowns could use. Among the packets at the root junctions, it found several ways a new node might begin. These possibilities were being maintained, but not summoned. The power could have supplied the energy to unfold them all.

It did not.

A leaf might remain enclosed because it lacked something. It might also remain enclosed because the conditions for which it was fitted had not come. Opening it would not distinguish between those cases. It would only make an event.

So the power learned the differences that the living network already knew how to recognize.

This took longer than looking.

There were no words for permission in the roots. Yet two tips could meet without joining. A current could be offered across a contact and returned. One tissue could increase its conductivity while another narrowed its own. The grove had ways of accepting, refusing, and postponing exchanges.

At a small junction beneath the eastern side of the nearest stand, the power made an offer in those terms.

It placed no new material in the stone. It did not alter the routes already carrying the grove’s work. Beside an existing reserve, it made available a little charge, limited enough that the junction could turn it away without having to change itself.

The first pulse passed untouched.

The second was returned.

The power ceased.

For a while, nothing distinguished that place from any other part of the buried network.

Then a root tip shifted toward the contact.

It did not thicken into a permanent connection. It took a small amount of charge, held it apart from the circulating reserve, and released some of it back.

The power answered only with the same small availability.

Over many exchanges, the junction found the limits of the offer. The current did not increase when more was taken. It did not vanish when some was refused. It could be left alone.

At last, the root made a narrow branch toward it.

Elsewhere, no leaf changed position.

The additional charge was not much. Spread across the plateau, it would have disappeared among the ordinary variations of sunlight. Kept near the junction, it was enough to sustain work that otherwise would have drawn from the grove’s reserves.

For a long interval, the living tissue merely held it.

The power waited.

Eventually, one of the organized packets beside the junction began to change. Its outer layers separated. A small chamber formed within them, and a conductive surface grew along the chamber wall.

It was not a shoot. It made no attempt to reach the air.

For some time, its activity had no effect beyond itself. It set up differences, maintained them, let them lapse. Twice it returned to an earlier arrangement. The third arrangement persisted.

Only then did it connect to a neighboring seam.

A faint alteration passed through the eastern roots. Most of it was absorbed nearby. A smaller part reached the nearest grove, where the branching collars redistributed it without difficulty.

At the edge of that grove, the broad leaf rose.

Its tip moved by less than the thickness of a grain of sand.

The shadow beneath it widened.

The power did not take this movement as gratitude. It did not know whether the new chamber was an experiment, a reserve, or the beginning of a function the plateau had never needed before. The leaf’s adjustment might have been no more than the ordinary consequence of a changed load.

It watched without completing the work on the plateau’s behalf.

Under the cream-colored ground, the new chamber continued. Sometimes it drew on the offered charge. Sometimes it supplied itself from the surrounding network and left the contact idle. The narrow branch remained capable of closing.

The power kept the offer small, and kept it there.

Above, the groves retained their definite edges. The terraces held their clean faces. No rain darkened the stone; no unfamiliar creature moved between the stems. The enclosed leaves stayed enclosed.

Within one of them, an old conducting thread was relieved. Its replacement took the current.

The broad leaf held its slightly altered position.

Beneath it lay a little more empty air than before, kept open by the life of the place.
