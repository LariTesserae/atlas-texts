The first thing the power does is allow the pattern to finish.

At the tip of the new stone, a few connections are trying to hold. They fail, recur, and fail differently. It would be easy to steady them. It would be easy to give the water an arrangement so reliable that nothing living there would ever need to correct itself again.

The power waits.

The faint curved grain returns. This time it extends farther than before, then withdraws to the sheltered seam.

The difference matters.

***

For a long time, nothing is added to the sea.

The power attends to the intervals in which the inhabitant changes itself. It follows an extension into cold water, but does not warm the current. It attends to the chambers of the hollow ridge without opening them. When the inhabitant abandons an arrangement, the power does not maintain it in secret.

It learns that an empty chamber is not necessarily a loss.

It learns that a contraction can mean several things.

There are disturbances the inhabitant moves away from and disturbances toward which it extends. There are conditions in which it persists easily but does not remain. There is a certain sequence of sounds, arriving through the three nested surfaces, after which its outermost connections change for hours.

The power cannot yet tell what that sequence is to it.

It listens again.

***

The first alteration is made outside the inhabited ridge.

A little way along the shelf, where a narrow mineral seam opens into clear water, the power changes how one connection yields its load. It does not make the connection stronger. It gives the load another route.

Nothing brightens. No new substance appears.

When a cold current crosses the shelf, the seam contracts as before. Its sharp report travels into the distance. But beneath that report there is a small interval in which the supporting field remains usable.

The inhabitant does not enter it.

The power leaves it there.

Several days later, an extension reaches the altered seam. It holds briefly, withdraws, and returns by another route. The mineral threads catch the sunlight in a shallow curve.

The power makes no further change.

Over the following weeks, the inhabitant uses the seam twice. On the third approach, it turns away before reaching it and gathers instead along an unfinished edge exposed to the current.

That edge is less dependable. Its grains have not acquired their final pattern.

The power watches the inhabitant work there.

***

Elsewhere, it tries the same alteration and finds that it does not have the same effect.

A small arrangement beneath a dark ribbon ceases as soon as the extra route begins carrying weight. Another leaves the leaf and persists in the water above it. A third does not seem to encounter the change at all.

The power removes the alteration from the first place. It leaves the second alone. At the third, it waits through the opening and closing of the plant.

It becomes more careful.

The sea is not a single difficulty awaiting a single correction.

***

When the next storm begins, the power does not stop it.

Far away, a plane of water accelerates. Mountains jump behind the bending light. A long, low note passes through the hollow ridge, and the inhabitant draws its extensions inward.

Then a neighboring part of the field falls out of agreement.

The shelf begins to lift.

The power carries a fraction of its weight into the open water beyond the ridge. Across that water, mineral threads form, hold, and release in succession. The load passes outward over hundreds of kilometers, too thinly distributed to trouble the distant growing stone.

The shelf still moves. One of its outer ledges breaks away. The ridge turns slightly, opening a chamber that has been closed for centuries.

Inside it, the inhabitant contracts.

The power does not enter the remaining pattern. It keeps the chamber from losing all its support at once.

There are still choices to make there: connections to relinquish, intervals to preserve, unfamiliar routes through the moving water. The inhabitant makes them.

For several hours, the ridge hangs at an angle while the storm tears past.

Then the neighboring fields begin to agree again. The power releases the weight gradually. Stone settles with a report that travels farther than the light.

The inhabitant remains.

***

Afterward, it stays deep within the ridge.

The opened chamber admits a colder current. The old sequence of sounds no longer arrives in quite the same order. Several familiar surfaces are gone.

The power could close the chamber and reproduce the former echoes.

It does not know whether that would be wanted.

Instead, it holds the broken edges steady while new mineral grains begin to settle. Their eventual shape is left undecided.

Days pass.

The inhabitant extends into the opened chamber. Along one wall, it preserves a narrow gap that the gathering stone would otherwise fill. Farther out, it abandons another.

The power follows these small decisions. Where the inhabitant has repeatedly kept a passage open, the stone is allowed to bear its weight without sealing the passage. Where it has ceased to return, growth proceeds.

Slowly, the chamber acquires a new interior.

Its sounds are different from those of the old one.

The inhabitant begins to extend beyond it.

***

There are losses the power cannot undo merely by knowing where they happened.

In water beside a dissolved crest, it finds particles that once passed through an inhabitant. It finds the pressures that destroyed the last chamber, the currents that scattered the grains, and traces of old corrections sealed inside fragments of stone.

These are not the sequence that continued.

The power does not gather them into an imitation and place it beside the survivors.

It attends to the empty water for a while.

Then it turns toward a sheltered ledge where a ribbon is unfolding. Beneath the leaf, a small delay has survived the loss of its first grains.

***

Over time, other changes appear.

Not everywhere. Most of the ocean remains as it was: vast, cold, sparsely grown, loud with the making of stone.

But some ridges acquire seams that can bear a changing load without closing. Along certain storm paths, there are stretches where a withdrawing pattern has longer to find another support. A ribbon drifting from a dead colony occasionally reaches a warm shelf that would otherwise have cooled before its arrival.

None of these places requires an inhabitant to stay.

Some remain unused for centuries.

The power keeps learning from that.

***

Beside the hollow ridge, the inhabitant eventually reaches the first altered seam again.

Its connections extend through the small interval of dependable water. Then they continue beyond it, into a region the power has not changed.

There they branch.

One branch fails. Another bends along the growing edge of the shelf. A third holds briefly against the current, releases, and tries a different angle.

The power attends without intervening.

Far below, clouds move beneath the sea. A mineral flake turns in the light, passing into the arrangement and out again.

At the tip of the unfinished stone, the faint curved grain returns.

This time it remains long enough for the current to divide around it.
