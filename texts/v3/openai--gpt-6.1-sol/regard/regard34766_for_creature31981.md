The power does not answer at once.

It could know the pressure of every root, the weight resting on every roof, the slow movement of water through the buried mountains. But the small hand at the threshold is not asking about all of these.

It is listening to a stranger.

So the power gives itself weight.

Not much: enough to settle the leaves beneath it, enough for the floor of the house to distinguish its presence from rain. It does not take the shape of one of the inhabitants. That would make promises before either side understood them. It remains a visitor, with hands that can close around a short piece of fiber.

The young one feels the change.

Its fingers spread. Behind it, farther inside the house, another hand touches the doorway root. The smaller hand withdraws.

For a long time there is only the sound of water leaving a leaf.

Then the leaves over the entrance are held aside.

***

The place set aside for visitors is slightly off-center. From there, the power can see three recesses and the beginning of a passage that turns downward. It sits without leaning against the wall.

A piece of firm fiber is brought.

The inhabitant holding the other end has two crooked digits. One will not close entirely; the other closes too far. The power does not straighten them. It does not yet know whether they hurt, or whether touching them would be welcome.

It takes the offered piece.

Pressure. A pause.

The inhabitant repeats the pressure, more distinctly.

The power answers too clearly.

The other hand releases.

This is its first mistake.

A response without hesitation, without the small uncertainties of a living grip, has traveled through the fiber. The inhabitant draws it close and examines both ends.

When it offers the piece again, the power answers only what it has been given, and not everything it has understood.

The hand remains.

Outside, evening rain begins to work its way through the crowns.

***

For several days, the power does very little that anyone can notice.

It learns where to put its feet. It learns that an untouched bulb may belong to someone who is absent, and that a feeding hollow which appears neglected may be resting. It learns to wait after a message, because the farthest chamber receives it late.

Once, it moves aside for a young inhabitant carrying a seed casing. The youngster goes around it anyway. The route the power has opened crosses a strand used for speaking.

It learns that, too.

The household gives it food. It accepts a little, and afterward helps close the feeding surface. Its first repair is too tight. An adult loosens the edges to leave room for drainage.

The power could make a closure that would never fail. Instead, it watches the adult’s hands.

Beyond the house, the country continues its immense, uneven work. Roots thicken. Small animals eat through membranes. A crown several miles away lowers by the width of a finger, and the change is felt here as a faint disagreement between two supporting strands.

There are things the inhabitants do not notice.

There are also things they notice before the power knows why they matter.

***

The oldest member of the household occupies a chamber below the others.

Its resting place has grown around the shape of its body. Two hind limbs lie along grooves in the floor. The forehands remain pressed against separate roots.

Food is brought down to it. News is brought down, too.

When the power first enters, the old inhabitant sends a short, forceful movement through the left-hand root.

The guide stops.

The power stops.

After a while, the guide takes its wrist and moves it away from the wall.

The old one had been listening to water.

For much of that day, the power sits on the bare floor and listens with it.

Deep beneath the house, a passage has narrowed. This is ordinary. A woody seam has swollen; roots have grown into the slack space beside it. Water that once crossed the district is collecting against the obstruction.

In time, it will rise into the lower chambers.

The old inhabitant knows the rising water. It remembers two earlier changes in the passage, and the routes by which the household drained them. Those routes no longer lead where they did.

When the others return, its hands move rapidly.

They answer. One presses hard enough that the wall gives a small creak.

The power keeps still.

The household is discussing departure.

***

A new district lies within reach of the younger inhabitants. They have visited it during the last flowering season. There are good feeding layers there, and a house that might be made dry.

The old one has never been there.

It could be carried, but the journey would take many days. Each unfamiliar support would have to be tested while holding its weight. The new house would offer it none of the distinctions its hands have spent a lifetime learning.

Remaining is also a choice. Other inhabitants have remained before.

The power hears all of this slowly, through repetitions, through things carried into the lower chamber and then carried out again.

At last it asks for the piece of firm fiber.

Its question takes most of an afternoon.

It cannot fit the whole of what it means into a pressure and a pause. It has to lead one of the inhabitants outside, to an exposed fold beside the ridge. There it indicates the backed-up water, then a lower hollow beyond the house.

It offers to open a way between them.

The inhabitant studies the hollow.

Something lives there. Several small animals use the dry undersides of its floating growth. Farther down, a feeding layer belongs to another household.

The proposed channel would drown both.

The power withdraws its hand.

They return to the house.

***

The next route is found together.

It leads farther around the ridge, through growth already saturated, and joins a stream that has been moving sideways for several generations. Even there, a sudden release would tear the root felt.

The opening must be slow.

The household knows plants that will line it. A neighbor knows how the receiving stream behaves during heavy rain. The old inhabitant can feel when the obstruction begins to yield.

The power knows how to reach the deep passage without breaking everything above it.

No one possesses the whole repair.

Work begins after the neighbors have listened.

For nine nights, the power rests one hand beneath the buried seam. It does not lift it clear. It eases the pressure a little at a time.

Above, the inhabitants guide young hollow stems into the new course. They fasten them with strips of leaf. Twice, the water moves faster than expected, and everyone stops.

The old one sends its answer upward.

Wait.

They wait.

By the tenth morning, the lower chamber is no longer growing wetter.

The repair has not made it permanently safe. The seam will thicken again. The stream will shift. Someone will have to listen, and someone will have to travel to the outlet and see what has changed.

But the old inhabitant can stay.

When the power comes down, it finds one hand lifted from its accustomed root.

The hand rests briefly against its wrist.

Then it goes back to the water.

***

The power remains through a season.

It is asked for other things. Some are small. A hard wedge has slipped into a cavity beyond reach. A child has burned its palm against an exposed pocket of hot decay. One of the crooked fingers hurts when the rains become cold.

It retrieves the wedge. It cools the wounded palm and lets the adults examine what it has done. When the inhabitant with the crooked hand offers it, the power eases the pain, carefully enough that the old injury can still be felt and protected.

Sometimes it is not asked, and still acts.

When a nursery root parts without warning, it bears the young until other hands reach them. There is no time to wait for a message. Afterward it stays while the adults touch the broken wood, the frightened children, and the unfamiliar support that appeared beneath them.

Its help leaves questions. It does not disappear before those questions can be asked.

There are quarrels it cannot settle by making both sides comfortable. There are hunting places it is not invited to enter. There are deaths that come even while it is near.

It learns how to accompany those, too.

***

When it leaves, the household has not become dependent on a voice in the ground.

The new channel can be maintained by ordinary hands. Its course is known to three houses. The old inhabitant has taught two younger ones the changes that mean it is beginning to close.

The power has planted nothing that only it can tend.

At the doorway, the young one that first listened holds out the familiar piece of fiber.

This time its grip is confident. It sends a sequence, waits, and sends it again.

The power answers.

The young one changes the sequence.

For a while they remain there, discovering how much can pass through so small a thing.

Then the fiber is released.

The power steps onto the yielding ground. Behind it, the leaves settle across the entrance.

It will not be present in every chamber, answering every tremor. There must be room for a house to contain its own conversations.

But it has learned the way here. It has learned the weight with which to announce itself, and the place where a stranger can sit without interrupting.

Below the path, water enters the new channel. It moves slowly enough that the fine roots hold.

Inside the lower chamber, the old inhabitant feels it pass.
