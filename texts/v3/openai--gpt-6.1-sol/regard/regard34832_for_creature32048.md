The power first attended to the small tremor.

It was not the strongest sound in the hollow. The great animals could be heard through the mountain, and the suspended slab made a low complaint even when it appeared motionless. But the new tremor had an interruption in it: three even turns, a hesitation, then a turn that pulled too hard.

The youngster’s damaged paddle lay folded against its side. That was not the difficulty. It would cease to need the paddle if it remained here.

The difficulty was farther in, where a thread of tissue had swollen against a turning disc.

The power listened through several hundred revolutions. It learned the difference between an ordinary slip and the one that would eventually tear the thread. It learned how the little chamber filled, how much warmth the animal could bear, and why it had chosen this seam rather than another.

It did not mistake the animal’s stillness for peace.

Nor did it mistake the clatter for distress.

For a while, it changed nothing.

Night spread above the summit. The pale peak beyond the split wall disappeared, and the lights under the ledges became the brightest things in the hollow. Within the youngster’s clear armor, a green line marked the edge of each revolving disc. The three fish above its outlet made small corrections with their fins.

The power’s attention widened.

It followed the fresh seam downward, through stone still warm from its making. It found the fracture beneath the scarred adult’s withdrawn foot. It entered the narrow space under the slab and felt where the weight rested.

One of the two supporting points was sound.

The other had cracked almost through.

The next fall would not be a finger’s breadth. The slab would turn as it descended, striking the new seam first and driving its edge into the floor. The youngster would vanish beneath it. So would the white settlers, the square-headed worm, two hanging sacs, and part of the scarred animal’s forward armor.

There would be food afterward. There would also be mouths that could no longer reach it.

The power did not call this balance.

It considered moving the slab away. But the stone above it was joined to other stone, and the dark slot sheltered bodies that had never entered open water. A swift rescue could tear their footholds out from under them. Even the water displaced by a lifting slab would pass through the hollow with force.

So it stayed within the failing support.

Deep inside the black rock, beyond the reach of any rootlike flesh, a thin mineral vein began to thicken. It grew along the surfaces where the crack was separating, binding them without filling the occupied spaces nearby. The work went slowly. Heat passed into the mountain and dispersed.

A little dust fell.

The silver creatures withdrew, waited, and extended again.

The slab settled against the strengthened point. Its weight was still there; the new vein carried it. Beneath the ledge, water continued to move through the slot.

Nothing in the hollow knew what had happened.

The power returned to the youngster.

It could have made the discs flawless. Instead it eased the swollen tissue away from the turning edge, leaving the thread intact. Fluid reached a place where fluid had been unable to pass. The swelling began to subside.

The next revolution caught.

The one after it did not.

For some time the power remained close enough to keep the thread from rubbing against the disc. As the tissue recovered, less help was needed. By the middle of the night, none was.

The torn paddle still hurt. Its wound had begun to close, and the power left that work to the animal.

Around it, the hollow continued feeding.

A transparent fish took a drifting larva. One of the little cages beneath the overhang closed for the first time and failed to hold its prey. Along the wall, a mouth grew into the space where another mouth had been bitten off.

The power felt the larva’s brief struggle as clearly as it felt the youngster’s steadying disc. Its attention did not make the loss smaller.

For a moment it followed the fish as well: the thin body, the nearly empty pouch, the continuous expenditure of holding position against the return stream. There was no way to remove its need by sparing this one meal. The lives here entered one another.

The power remained with them without pretending it had made the place harmless.

Toward dawn, the new seam strengthened.

The youngster’s chamber filled more deeply. Its outlet sent a pale stream farther across the rock, and a fourth fish approached from the old feeding rows. It hovered too close, was nipped, and found a position slightly downstream.

The first shell animal beneath the jet bent under its force.

The power watched. The fan folded, opened halfway, and held. Farther away, a minute shelled body loosened its grip and let the current carry it. It settled in a shallow pit where the flow was gentler.

There was room enough for those movements. No one had to be placed.

Above the hollow, the surface brightened. Bands of sunlight crossed the scarred animal’s back, revealing the brown seam between its fused plates. Its feet tasted the stone. Beneath the rearmost one, the fracture remained, and the animal kept its weight away from it.

The power would have to attend to that fracture too, if it worsened. The mountain was young. A single night’s work could not make it safe forever.

But this morning the slab held.

The youngster’s two discs turned without dragging. Its feet had begun to draw minerals from the edges of the seam, and the soft armor over its back was becoming cloudy.

A narrow jet poured from its flank.

Downstream, four fish faced the current.

The power listened to the small new rhythm among the larger ones, and stayed.
