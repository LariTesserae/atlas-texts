The power did not enter through the opening.

To enter that way would have meant a footfall, a shadow passing over the plants, a weight for the inhabitant to accommodate. It would have learned something from those disturbances, but first it wished to learn what happened without them.

So it attended to the hollow through a season.

It noticed the warmth coming down through the limestone, and the slower warmth held beneath the pale surface. It noticed that the closed depressions did not all tighten together. A weight on the plain was different from a weight inside. The growth of the root was different from either.

It did not mistake the long intervals between changes for an absence of experience.

Nor did it assume that the inhabitant wished to become more active. There was no reason to give it eyes, loosen it from the floor, or draw it out beneath the sky. The hollow was not a prison merely because another kind of being would have wanted to leave.

For some time, the power did nothing.

Then the rains failed.

This had happened before. The plants drew their leaves closer to their stems. The root beneath the floor lost a little of its firmness. Along their shared contact, the inhabitant maintained the narrow space in which the root could thicken, though there was no thickening now.

Farther beneath the slab, a fold closed.

The power followed these changes. There were parts of the body that could be relinquished without much consequence, and parts whose loss would make every later season more difficult. As the dry weather continued, the difference became clearer.

It found water deep in the ground beyond the hollow. Not a hidden river, not enough to fill a pool: a little moisture retained among finer grains beneath a seam of harder stone.

It made a narrow way for that moisture to reach the soil at the far end of the root.

The way was slight. Nothing dripped from the ceiling. No damp mark appeared on the floor. The root received no more than it might have received after a small rain on the plain.

And the power waited again.

The root became firmer. After a while, the surface beside it acquired its familiar tension. One of the folds beneath the slab relaxed. The inhabitant took in a thin piece of tissue shed by the root, and left another where it lay.

There was no sudden growth.

The power kept the passage open, but did not widen it.

Later, it found a place in the roof where two old fractures nearly met. In time, perhaps a long time, a broad piece of limestone would settle onto the slab. The inhabitant could bear some added weight. This much would close the spaces beneath it.

The power did not remake the roof. It strengthened a small bridge of stone between the fractures, leaving the rest to warm and cool as it always had.

Dust continued to loosen from the ceiling. A pale flake came to rest on the inhabitant’s back.

Nothing required clearing away.

A visitor arrived near the end of the dry season and sat on the slab. The inhabitant tightened at several points, then adjusted beneath the weight. The power watched without preventing this. It had learned that disturbance was not always injury, and that keeping everything away would be another way of choosing the inhabitant’s life for it.

The visitor rested a hand beside the stone. After a time, they withdrew it and left.

For several days, the firmer place remained beneath the slab.

The power could have made the hollow famous. It could have given the visitor a dream, or put a name into their mouth, or arranged for someone to return with instruments and careful hands.

It did none of these things.

There might someday be a reason for the inhabitant to be known. There was no such reason yet.

When rain finally crossed the plain, the small underground passage ceased to carry moisture. The plants loosened their leaves a little. Something passed through the root’s contact with the body.

The hollow remained warm and dry.

Beside the slab, the pale surface broadened by less than the width of a grain. Underneath it, a fold occupied a little earth that had become available.

The power noticed.

It left room.
