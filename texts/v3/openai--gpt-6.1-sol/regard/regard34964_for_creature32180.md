The power does not descend into the square.

There is no place there for a foot of that size, and no certainty that a presence would be understood as anything other than a change in pressure.

It attends first to the small animal in the new chamber.

The yellow cloud travels toward the six points of attachment. One point brightens, then another. The body settles lower against the wall. Nothing in this movement asks to be changed.

So the power leaves it alone.

It watches the black plants release their roots. It watches the dust turn edge-on in the wake of a passing body. It follows a yellow seam below the floor, where the distinction between a wall and the ground has not yet formed.

It does not make the air warmer. Warmth might be welcome to the person crouching in the doorway, but the person is not the measure of this place.

It brings no water.

For a long time, its attention has no visible consequence.

—

At the old lintel, the larger animal has advanced another foot.

Three pads now hold the neighboring doorway. Two remain on the thick ends of the lintel. The sixth is folded beneath its body, touching nothing.

The mineral between the two openings continues to thin.

A narrow part of the animal stretches across the gap. There is no way to tell from its stillness whether it is resting, spending itself, or waiting for a change that has not yet arrived.

The power could carry it.

That would be easy. It could lift the creature without tearing a pad, take it to a fresh corner, lay each part against a charged surface.

But the animal has never been unattached all at once.

Its body knows the settlement through the pull between its feet. Even while shedding its skin, it keeps some purchase on the world. To carry it would remove every relation by which it finds its shape.

The power waits through another pressure change.

All five attached pads tighten. The free one unfolds a little. Its margin reaches toward the doorway and falls short.

Here, at least, something is clear.

The power attends to the stone beneath the last two feet.

It does not stop the wall from dying. It does not restore the lintel or fill the opening. It holds back the thinning of two small patches, each scarcely larger than the pad resting on it.

The rest of the mineral continues to yield.

For a while the change is indistinguishable from the unevenness that occurs everywhere in the settlement.

Then the animal advances.

Its free foot finds the neighboring doorway. The pad spreads. A faint yellow cloud enters it from a seam too narrow to be seen from the court.

The animal waits.

When it moves again, one of its old attachments releases. The body draws itself into a deeper fold. The remaining foot on the lintel bears a different tension now, less outward, more along the wall.

The power continues to wait.

It has nowhere else it must be.

—

By the next slow fullness of the air, the creature has crossed.

Its body lies gathered beneath the neighboring threshold. Five feet are visible. The sixth has fitted itself into a shallow recess behind the wall.

The two patches of old mineral are no longer needed.

The power releases them.

One comes apart almost immediately. The other remains until later, a pale projection in the opening. When it falls, it falls without sound.

The flakes lie on the floor.

Nothing has been made permanent.

—

The power’s attention reaches the narrow chambers of the tower.

Some contain small animals. Some contain only yellow light. In one, an opening is closing around a pale margin, but the margin withdraws steadily toward a deeper space. In another, a creature moves upward while the stone thickens beneath it.

The power does not widen every slot.

It follows the movements long enough to distinguish an enclosed passage from an ending, a pause from an inability to continue. There are things it can learn only by allowing them to happen.

Beneath the settlement, fresh hollows are gathering around pockets of emptiness.

Above them, the streets change.

—

In the new chamber, the small animal loosens one foot and sets it nearer the floor.

The person in the doorway has grown stiff from crouching. When they stand, their shadow leaves the yellow seam.

The animal tightens.

It is a slight movement, but the power has been watching closely.

For a moment it considers laying a shade across the chamber. Then the person steps away, the dim light returns, and the creature shifts again. One pad releases; another takes its weight. It settles on the darker side of the seam.

There is enough room.

The power lets the light remain.

Its attention does not leave with the person. It stays with the cool chamber, the changing wall, the small body finding a new arrangement of its feet.

Much later, the doorway narrows.

Elsewhere, an opening begins.
