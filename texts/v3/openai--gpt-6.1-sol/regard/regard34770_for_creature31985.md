The power does not arrive through the service gate.

For several days, nothing changes that a worker could enter on a form. Water travels along the pale groove beneath the leaking flange. A fig softens inside the creature’s mouth. The pumps turn, and the quarter holds their warmth.

The power attends to what happens when a section is lifted on the wooden paddle. It notices the tightening farther along, and the interval before the pads release. It notices that a touch from falling water closes two depressions, while a change in vibration closes a whole row.

It does not mistake these responses for an invitation.

Its first intervention is beneath a brick.

A narrow connection passes there, between the nursery beds and the courtyard channel. Years of settling have brought the brick down until the creature must thin itself almost to transparency to pass beneath it. The workers cannot see this. They know only that the section beside the channel sometimes withdraws for a week.

During the night, the sand under the brick shifts. The brick rises by less than the thickness of a finger, keeping its worn face level with its neighbors. Beneath it, the passage becomes wider, with a smooth roof and a shallow floor that holds water.

The creature does not enter immediately.

For three nights its pale margin rests at the opening. On the fourth, a pad loosens. By morning, the channel contains a fuller length than it has held in years.

The power waits again.

***

In the engine room, one of the workers has been hearing a note beneath the usual knocking. She has mentioned it twice. Both times it disappeared before anyone else came to listen.

Now it remains.

Not louder, exactly. More distinct. She puts down the gauge sheet and stands beside the bearing housing. When the engineer arrives, it is still there.

The bearing is replaced before it fails.

During the shutdown, the bypass hose runs into the old tank. Someone checks it at the end of each shift. The creature gathers itself into the shelter as usual, but its outer folds do not split.

The power could keep the pumps turning indefinitely. It does not. A machine that never stops would eventually become a machine no one knew how to stop, and the people standing beside it would still need quiet, wages, sleep, and safe ways home.

Instead, certain matters become harder to overlook.

A corroded support is found during an inspection. The replacement is approved. A lamp with moisture inside its cover is repaired. The worker who cleans the drain beneath the main stair is given enough time to do it without rushing the last few yards.

None of this looks like a visitation. It looks like maintenance being allowed to happen.

***

The creature’s needs are less easily entered into a budget.

The power learns them by making small changes and watching what follows.

A warm trickle is offered along the edge of the roofless storehouse. The creature approaches it, then withdraws when the masonry gives back too much heat. The trickle cools. Several days later, a broad lobe settles there.

A second opening is made beside an old conduit. It remains unused, and moss closes over it.

No further opening is made in that wall.

Under the propagation beds, a shallow recess becomes a basin. Water can reach it without passing across the places where tools are washed. Its sides are rough enough for the pads to grip, and its outlet is broad enough that the small separate bodies cannot easily be drawn into it.

The first summer, nothing lives there except algae.

The second summer, three small bodies remain through autumn.

One disappears. Two thicken beneath a fallen alder leaf.

The power does not retrieve the missing one. It looks through the nearby channels, the cracks, the wet recesses under the roots. What it finds is attended to; what it cannot find is not replaced with an imitation.

***

There is one thing it gives the workers that they recognize as a gift, though they do not know from whom.

A survey of the quarter is commissioned for an unrelated reason. The surveyor spends longer than expected beside the disused tank. She returns with someone who knows about soft-bodied animals, and that person returns with someone else.

They bring lights, but lower them when the bare skin contracts. They take photographs without pulling the folds apart. A proposal to remove a portion for study is put aside.

The report cannot settle what the creature is.

It does, however, record where it lives, which water supplies reach it, and what has happened during previous shutdowns. The bypass hose enters the official procedure. So does the broad wooden paddle. The space beneath the nursery beds is no longer marked as empty.

The workers acquire a little more authority to do what they have already been doing.

There are still disagreements. One person wants every drain cleared. Another worries about the raised paving under the fig. These are real concerns, and the power does not make them vanish. A narrow walkway is rebuilt with removable sections. The fig keeps its roots. Cabinet doors remain accessible. The creature has passages beside them rather than across them.

There is room for inconvenience without anyone having to pretend it is danger.

***

Years later, one of the old pumps is taken out of service.

By then, the sheltered basins have a modest circulation of their own. They receive rainwater, filtered canal water, and a little warmth from the building’s new equipment. On the coldest days, a small heater comes on. Its switch and indicator light are plainly labeled.

The creature withdraws from the old pump’s wall.

For a month, that part of the quarter looks strangely bare.

Then a fold appears beneath the storehouse ferns. Another settles along the basin where the two young bodies survived. Whether these are new portions or old ones rearranged, no one can say.

The power remains attentive, but it no longer has much to do.

In late afternoon, a worker pauses beside the tank with a muddy coupling in her hand. The bed has moved. She finds it farther around the curve, where condensation falls from a cooler pipe.

She lays the coupling down gently and goes back to her work.

Beneath it, one pad releases. Another takes hold.

Water passes through the folds.
