The power did not come down through the branches.

There was no room for an arrival. Even a light laid upon the hollow would have entered the eyes of its inhabitants without their permission.

Instead, the power attended to what was already there.

It attended to the snail against the finger, and to the finger beneath the snail. To the soft animal in the hollow wood, and the wood that had become soft around it. To the little creature held between the larva’s jaws.

It did not begin by deciding what the place meant.

For a long while, its attention was indistinguishable from the hollow’s own darkness.

***

The first thing it wished to know was whether anything hurt.

This was not a question it spoke aloud. A voice, however low, would have reached almost everything.

It learned the answer separately from each body.

The bent fern was not weary in the way a bent arm would be weary. The bird’s grip was not a cramp. The cold did not lie upon the horned animal as a deprivation; it belonged to the animal’s comfort, as the pressure of its own coat did.

But these answers did not stand for all the others.

The power attended to every throat. It did not assume that the composure of a face disclosed everything behind it. Nor did it assume that a hidden face concealed a plea.

Some sensations had no names. Some could not have been carried from one creature to another without becoming something different. The power understood them without requiring that they become words.

Where there was pain, it eased the pain at its source, so precisely that no neighboring pleasure was dulled. Where there was none, it left the body alone.

Nothing in the hollow visibly changed.

***

Then it attended to time.

Outside, clouds crossed the ridge. Water found its way downhill. A root entered a crack and, by entering, made the crack a little wider.

Here, a bead beneath the fern had reached fullness without falling.

The power did not flick it loose.

It followed the bead’s weight into the fern, the fern’s stem into the wall, the wall into the mountain. It learned what held the drop, and what else that holding sustained.

It followed the larva’s unfinished bite with equal care.

To complete it would be an event in two lives, not one.

To open the jaw would also be an event in two lives.

The power was capable of either. That did not settle which, if either, should be done.

***

Beside the pool, the otterlike animal knew the stone.

The power attended to that knowing.

There had been a hand, and there was a hand. Between them lay a shallow groove polished to the shape of a thumb. The stone was not merely close to the animal. It had been chosen, held, found suitable.

The power approached through this small distinction.

It made no image of a bright meadow. It offered no hunger for a country the animal had never needed. It did not put dissatisfaction into the dark eyes and then congratulate itself upon answering it.

It made available a possibility no larger than the familiar act of taking up the flake.

The animal could continue to hold its hand in the water.

Or it could do something next.

It would not have to choose at once.

And the possibility came with knowledge fitted to the animal’s own understanding: what a movement would feel like, what it would disturb, what could be restored. The power would not ask an animal to wager its home on an unexplained invitation.

The snail would not be torn away. The bodies beneath the palm would not be crushed. Space for a lifted hand would be made without taking space from another body.

The power waited.

***

It offered the same freedom elsewhere, though not in the same form.

To an egg, it offered the possibility of growing, with food and shelter on the other side of the shell.

To the horned animal, the possibility of turning its cheek without destroying the small dwelling beneath its chest.

To the creature within the jaws, a way out.

That last offer it made immediately. It did not leave release dependent on the goodwill of the mouth that held it.

But the larva, too, would be sustained. Its hunger would not be punished for having found the only food within reach.

The power had no intention of making a kinder place by selecting which inhabitants must bear the cost.

These possibilities did not crowd the hollow. They lay beyond it, as the open heights lay beyond the folded ridge, but nearer and easier to reach.

No creature had to leave to remain safe.

No creature had to remain to keep its place.

***

At last the otterlike animal moved its thumb.

Only its thumb.

The snail stayed against the finger. The water gathered differently at the knuckle. A whisker bent, and the bead at its tip descended a fraction.

The animal paused.

The power did not take the pause for a refusal, or the movement for consent to everything else.

After a while, the thumb found the groove in the stone.

The flake turned.

There was a pale surface beneath it, scarcely larger than a leaf, which had not been visible before.

The animal looked at that surface.

Then it set the stone down in its new position.

***

The power remained attentive.

It did not widen the sky, thin the moss, or warm the water. It did not require the birds to sing.

Beneath the fern, most of the drops still hung at their old fullness. Beneath one leaf, a drop fell.

It struck a stone already wet enough to receive it.

The fern rose almost imperceptibly.

Nothing had to follow.
