The power first notices the cushion beneath the ledge.

It does not mistake the swelling for a greeting. Water has arrived, and the cushion is taking it in. That is enough to deserve attention.

For a long time, the power does nothing that the chamber can register. It follows the water through the dead passages and the living ones. It watches a contraction travel from the ridge to the basin, lose strength at a damaged junction, and arrive too weakly to empty a second cushion. It watches the second cushion freeze.

It studies the pressure sensors, too. The chamber has begun to compensate for a discrepancy it does not understand. Three more corrections will shear the thickened junction beneath the rising plate. A service routine, already scheduled, will then flush the pressure ports with a solution that has proved effective against mineral fouling.

The power lets neither event happen.

The actuator pauses. Upstairs, an ordinary fault appears on a maintenance display. The flush is deferred until someone can inspect the port.

This buys time. It does not yet make the chamber safe.

***

There are ways the power could fill the room with warmth.

It could turn the pale plants green, give the basin depth, open a country beyond the concrete. It could provide rain everywhere at once.

It watches the culture withdraw from a drop whose mineral concentration has changed by less than a technician would think worth recording.

So it begins with one damaged passage.

Beneath a plate near the basin, a hose coupling has a sharp burr. Each time the terrain shifts, the burr cuts into a cushion. The creature repairs the breach slowly, using water it otherwise sends toward the ridge. The power smooths the metal without disturbing the tissue against it.

On the next refill, the passage holds.

Nothing visible happens aboveground. A little less water is spent on repair. Farther along the seam, a filament stays extended for several seconds longer than before.

The power waits through many refills.

It learns that abundance can be dangerous here. A sudden supply of nutrients draws growth into places that will shortly be crushed. Continuous warmth keeps outer tissues active when the water needed to sustain them has already gone elsewhere. Some dry intervals allow the creature to close a route before the plates move.

The power preserves those intervals. Where it makes a change, it leaves time around it.

***

The technician comes down on a Thursday carrying a sampling vial and a narrow lamp.

Until now, the culture has been listed among consumables. The power cannot tell the technician everything it has learned. Much of it has no ready place in a service report.

It can make the evidence available.

The lamp catches a transparent thread bridging two plates. The technician holds the beam there. A cushion contracts below the thread; several minutes later, another contracts near the pump. The technician looks back at the first.

The sample is taken from a loose scrap at the edge of a socket, rather than from the thick junction under the ledge.

On the service sheet, *mineral fouling* is crossed out.

The new entry is less confident.

*Culture extending outside installation points. Coordinated response possible. Avoid flushing pending assessment.*

The power accepts this imperfect sentence. It is sufficient to prevent the next harm.

Later, it helps a second person notice the pattern, and a third. It keeps the observations reproducible. The delay between one contraction and another can be measured. The routes can be mapped without pulling them apart.

No one has to believe a revelation.

***

The chamber was built to make terrain. Its controls know how far each plate ought to rise. They do not know that anything might need warning before the ground changes.

The power gives the creature warning.

Before a plate moves, a faint pressure passes through its underside. At first this is simply another disturbance. The creature contracts locally, then grows again.

The same pressure precedes the next movement.

And the next.

After many cycles, water begins to leave the threatened seam before the actuator starts. A cushion withdraws from the piston clearance. Fewer chambers are torn.

The power does not increase the speed of the terrain’s construction to take advantage of this.

It makes another adjustment to the controls. When tissue remains in a clearance, the plate waits. If the tissue does not withdraw, that particular movement is omitted.

The ridge becomes slightly different from the design.

Upstairs, someone revises the design.

***

The power cannot determine whether the creature suffers when a filament freezes. It can follow the loss through the body: the closed route, the changed distribution of water, the energy used in replacement. It can see that some losses are repeatedly avoided and others are not.

It takes care without requiring a final answer.

A warm refuge is installed below the basin, small enough that the creature can leave most of its body outside it. The refuge receives water through three passages instead of one. No single tilted plate can cut it off.

The new fittings are assembled in the narrow chamber by people with cramped shoulders and cold hands. The work takes longer than expected. They stop whenever the living lining tightens around a place they need to reach.

The power keeps the old heating filament operating throughout.

Once the refuge is ready, warmth enters it gradually.

For weeks, the creature touches only the nearest mouth. Filaments extend, withdraw, extend again. Eventually a gray cushion forms inside. Later, a second route joins it.

The original junction remains occupied.

The power had prepared to protect that junction during the transfer. Now it understands that there may be no transfer. The creature is making the refuge part of its body while retaining the places it already has.

Both are kept.

***

Beyond one wall, beneath a cupboard no one uses, there is room for another chamber.

The power does not move the creature there.

It arranges a passage: a narrow ceramic conduit, damp in sections, with sheltered recesses along its length. Beyond it lies a shallow bed of porous material, a small pool, and mineral surfaces that do not move. The light there is different. So is the temperature. Neither is presented as an improvement the creature must accept.

The conduit remains closed until its first section has been conditioned with water from the old basin.

When it opens, nothing crosses for a long time.

The power continues attending to the original chamber. Canisters are changed. The lamp burns for eleven minutes. Drops strike the plates with their clean, disproportionate sound.

One filament enters the conduit and dies at a dry interruption.

The interruption is repaired.

More filaments enter. A thin lining forms behind them. Months later, water drawn from the basin is held in a cushion beyond the concrete wall.

There is no exodus. Growth continues under the ridge. Some of the new passage is abandoned, then occupied again by another route. The creature encounters the still bed and spends a season along its edge before spreading over it.

No one closes the way back.

***

In time, the brass plaque acquires a second plate beneath the taped number.

It carries practical instructions: the location of the warm refuge, the maximum safe rate of drainage, the warning signal to use before moving the floor. It says which substances must not enter the water circuit. There is a drawing of the principal living junctions, dated, with space beside it for corrections.

The chamber still needs maintenance. A pump will fail eventually. Pipes corrode. People who know the place will retire.

The power makes provision for these things. Spare parts are kept nearby. Records describe why each precaution exists, so that an inconvenient delay will not be discarded as an obsolete setting. More than one person knows how to tend the plants and sample the water.

Below, the first terrain continues to rise.

A seam opens slowly beside the ledge. Before it moves, the warning pressure passes through the ceramic.

The cushion beneath it empties.

Water travels down a passage that once ended at the wall and now continues beyond it. Elsewhere, several filaments remain fastened to the plate. The actuator waits.

After a while, they loosen.

The plate rises by a fraction of a millimeter. A drop falls from the condenser, strikes its fresh edge, and divides. Part reaches the basin. Part disappears into the living surface.

The power stays long enough to see where it goes.
