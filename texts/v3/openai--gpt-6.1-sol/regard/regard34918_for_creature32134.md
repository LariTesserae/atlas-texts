The power did not lift the stone.

It knew where the hollow was, and knew that lifting the stone would disclose something else. The grub in the pale soil had its own life. There was no reason to expose it.

Nor did the power enter by the bare shelf. The shelf was not a place left for a visitor. Its pores, its rim, the wet stain ending there were already sufficient uses of it.

For a while, the power listened.

It could distinguish every sound immediately, but distinction was not yet understanding. It stayed with the beetle’s ticking until the roughness at the end no longer seemed like damage to a cleaner sound. It attended to the snails without making their low hum into words. Beside the moss, it heard the fine tearing noise and did not mistake the name of the noise for something happening to the creature.

There were many things it could have done. It could have separated the crowded bodies, finished the crystal, given the waters a course. It could have made a seventh clearance beside the living root.

None of these abilities told it whether it should.

The power was accustomed to lives in which a little more room was mercy. Here it looked carefully at the mite beneath the centipede, at the centipede’s sideways foot, at the round animal resting against the inward bulge of the little wall. It looked for what pressure did to each of them. It found contact, accommodation, particularity. It did not find an injury merely by finding another body close.

Stillness required the same care. The power did not take the creatures’ ease as proof that they could want nothing else. Nor did it take their hooks and joints as proof that they were being denied their use.

It attended more closely.

Had there been a distress too small to enter the rasp, it would have heard it. Had the beetle’s exact fit beneath the lintel been an effort it could no longer sustain, the power would have known the effort from within, rather than guessed from the posture.

There was no such distress.

What it found was not a happiness that could be carried elsewhere. The white earth beneath one claw mattered; so did the grain beneath the other. The shell overhead mattered in its separation from the skull. To give the beetle a larger dwelling would not necessarily give it more of what it had.

The power let that knowledge correct it.

Then it made itself known, in the least way that could still be an offer.

No new tone entered the chamber. Nothing brightened. But each inhabitant could have the attention of the power without having to become larger, louder, or more like a being that asked in sentences.

The beetle did not lift its antenna. The mite did not unfold its leg. Their unchanged bodies were not treated as answers on behalf of all their future lives. The offer remained available to each separately.

There was no reward for accepting it.

Outside, the stone lay in the soil. Water moved past its lower edge. A root thickened a little way above the chamber; farther off, another root died. The power watched these things too. It would not preserve the hollow by making the earth around it incapable of living.

But if harm came toward the chamber, it would meet that harm where meeting it did the least harm in turn. It would turn aside the crushing weight, not harden every soft body. It would keep poison from the little waters, not take away the waters’ contact with the world. It would protect the six leaves without commanding the root to remain forever as it was.

And if anything within the hollow should come to want a change, that wish would not be lost beneath the innumerable sounds.

This was the only addition: help could be reached. It occupied none of the clearances.

The power did not finish the half-formed tooth of crystal. It did not smooth the inaccuracies in the groove beneath the hair. Those inaccuracies belonged to the workmanship, and the workmanship belonged to someone it had not been invited to correct.

Under the lintel, the beetle’s head remained separate from the shell above it.

The power stayed attentive to that little interval, among all the others.

The tick retained its rough ending.
