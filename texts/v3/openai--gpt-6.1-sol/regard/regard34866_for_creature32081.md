The power did not mistake the basin for a wounded valley.

There had been a valley here. There were things it had lost that no quantity of planted roofs could replace. But there were also the rooms, and the worms in the traveling beds, and the woman who kept a rose because her father had once brought it home. To tear away the concrete would not be to uncover an innocent world. It would be to tear away their world.

Nor did the power mistake endurance for consent.

It listened to the pumps. It listened to the people who disliked the pumps. It attended to the difference between a difficulty someone had chosen and a difficulty someone had merely become good at bearing.

This took longer than an inspection.

***

The snail reached a place where the seam narrowed.

Ahead, an old repair had filled the hollow almost flush. Water gathered along its upper edge, then dropped away from the wall. To cross it, the snail would have to leave the shelter of the fronds.

The power could have carried it anywhere. There were gardens beyond the rim with deep soil and leaves lying loose beneath hedges. There were walls warmed by afternoon sun.

It did not carry it away.

The snail knew this seam through its body. It had fed here. It had rested in the small recess behind the stem the worker had cut. Elsewhere was not necessarily better simply because it was larger.

The power made a shallow roughness across the face of the old patch, no wider than necessary. It kept the drainage edge clear.

The snail passed over it very slowly.

No one saw.

***

Inside the wall, the power found a fastening that had been sound at the previous inspection and was no longer sound.

It was not about to fail. That mattered. There was time to open the housing, isolate the line, and do the work without closing the stairs below it.

The next morning, when the worker returned, someone was waiting beside the ferns.

They wore a coat suited to the weather. The worker could not afterward remember its color.

“There is a fastening behind the plate,” they said. “The lower one. It needs looking at.”

“Who sent you?”

“No one.”

She asked for their authorization. They had none. She told them to stand clear.

They stood clear.

When she opened the plate, she saw the faint track of moisture where it should not have been. She called the control room, then her supervisor. The stranger waited while she made both calls.

“How did you know?” she asked.

“I looked.”

She glanced at the unopened wall, then at them.

“What else have you looked at?”

***

That was the beginning of a difficult arrangement.

The power could supply materials, but the residents wanted to know what would be expected in return. It could take loads off failing structures, but the engineers wanted to know where those loads would go. It could clean a reservoir without draining it, but the filter crews insisted that cleaning was not the same as making sterile.

These were good questions.

The power answered them. When an answer was insufficient, it tried again.

There was a meeting in a room above the packing hall. People arrived damp and tired. Someone had brought a list of requests. Someone else had brought a list of reasons not to trust the offer.

The man who always objected to the bridge objected to the bridge.

This time, there was enough time to hear him through.

***

The first changes were not extraordinary to look at.

Replacement bearings arrived before the old ones had to be coaxed through another season. There were membranes of the right thickness, rather than the thickness available. A corroded stair could be closed for repair because another safe route had already been opened.

Maintenance crews gained enough people to let someone be ill without making everyone else work longer.

The residential drying system was altered a section at a time. Nobody had to empty a room merely to satisfy the elegance of a new plan. Warm cupboards remained warm cupboards. Clothes dried sooner.

There were mistakes in the proposals. A new access route crossed the place where children waited for the evening train. A quieter pump removed a sound that several blind residents used to find a turning. Both plans were changed.

The power had seen the whole basin. It still needed to be told what it had missed.

***

At the berry grower’s request, someone who understood roses came up from beyond the rim.

They examined the leaves, the containers, the light. They did not promise flowers.

The long cane was trained away from the window latch. One pot was raised so its drain could empty properly. The grower was given advice she could try without surrendering the cutting to anyone.

She thanked the visitor and then argued about pruning.

The power left that argument alone.

Two levels below, the apprentice continued learning motors. His instructor had fewer assemblies awaiting urgent repair now, and could afford to let him listen before telling him what was wrong.

The retired gate technician still woke at night.

For a while he checked the display as often as before. Gradually, he began to leave the cup on its shelf. His wife noticed before he did.

***

There were changes for the other inhabitants, too.

A few open pools acquired shallow, sheltered edges where this would not interfere with their work. Safe overflow beds were allowed to keep fallen leaves. The signal housing was fitted with an access cover that did not destroy the spider’s whole web each time it opened.

Not every creature was encouraged everywhere. The power did not ask the residents to accept fouled crops, obstructed pipes, or insects in their beds as proof of generosity.

It helped them make distinctions.

Beyond the rim, land was set aside where water could spread without a numbered limit painted around it. The basin did not have to become that land. The land did not have to become useful to the basin.

***

Months later, the worker clipped the ferns again.

She cleared the inspection plate and cut farther back on the hinge side. The fastening behind it was sound. Her gloves were new, although she had chosen the same kind.

She found the snail beneath a folded frond.

Perhaps it was the same snail. She had no way to know.

She moved it to the uncut section, using the flat of her glove, and went on with the work.

The power was still attending. There were other walls to examine, promises to keep, requests it had not yet understood. But here it did nothing.

The worker had left enough room.

When the pump started, the fronds trembled. The snail withdrew, waited, and extended itself again. Beneath the seam, water traveled through the old wall without escaping it. The worker closed the plate, checked the latch, and walked down toward the next terrace.
