The power does not begin by cooling the plateau.

It notices the heat, the darkness, the absence of water. It also notices that a cool passage may be a place of starvation, that a red seam may be doing the work a sunrise once did. Rain would not necessarily be kindness here. A forest brought back from an older world might drown these roots in the chemistry of their ancestors.

For a long while, it does nothing that the plateau can distinguish from weather.

It follows charge through the dry salts. It learns where pressure is stored, where it is spent, and where a delay of several minutes matters. It watches a mobile bud test three walls and take the fourth, which had seemed no different until a buried chamber contracted.

It does not decide that this is a choice. It does not decide that it is not.

When the pale root reaches the boot on the eastern apron, the power lifts the visitor’s foot.

The root has laid down a film so thin that pulling away would tear it. Instead, the power holds the sole still and loosens the insulating trace beneath it, molecule by molecule. The adhesive remains attached to the old bark. The root keeps the dust it has gathered.

The visitor is moved to a stone ledge beyond the growth: not far, but far enough that no intake will confuse breath with an available supply.

This is the first intervention. It is small enough to leave no wound.

The root continues making floor.

—

The power spends a year learning why one narrow strip of tissue is kept alive.

The strip lies inside a support that has been hollow for centuries. Its outer structure is brittle. Three younger organisms have entered the cavities, but none has taken up the particular twisting load borne by the old tissue.

Charge reaches it irregularly through a damaged connection. Whenever the supply fails, the strip contracts. A distant roof lowers by less than the thickness of a leaf. Neighboring columns swell to compensate. Their swelling pinches the damaged connection further.

The power traces the loop through nine separate bodies.

It could hold up the roof. It could supply the old strip forever. Neither would tell it what would happen when its attention moved elsewhere.

Instead, it places a grain of conductive material in a crack.

For eleven days, nothing changes.

Then a feeder root finds the grain. It deposits resin around it. Another root approaches from the opposite side. The repaired connection begins to carry a little charge.

The old strip expands.

Under the distant roof, the younger columns stop swelling. One has enough unused capacity to grow a lateral brace. Over the next six months, that brace takes up a fraction of the twisting load.

The power adds no second grain.

—

Not everything is failing.

There is a grove whose sheets curl inward at the edges. The power examines the curl and finds that it costs the grove material. Some sheets buckle in their racks. Others become shutters that never quite seal.

But in a district where fine dust has repeatedly fouled the hinges, a shutter of this lineage stays open a little longer than the others. Its curved edge sheds the dust.

The power leaves the grove alone.

It leaves the wasteful stem-makers alone, too, though it watches them for a long time. Their racks fill; the stems break; their fragments are reclaimed. A nearby conduit-maker uses less material. Yet when a buried chamber fractures, it is the loose stems that roll into the gap and hold it open until new walls form.

The plateau contains habits older than their uses. It also contains uses that have not yet happened.

—

Benevolence becomes more difficult near the western terraces.

Here, reproductive knots gather their small reserves over decades. Some are opened by neighboring roots before their casings are complete. Their salts and water return to the district.

The power does not protect every knot. It has learned what those reserves support.

It follows the ones that are released.

Most strike the root face. A few lodge in crevices and begin to test the mineral films there. One vanishes into the updraft, turns for several hours in the dark, and lands on a ledge coated with a substance its first roots cannot cross.

The power brings a grain of suitable dust to that ledge.

The reproductive body touches it. A filament emerges, draws a little charge, and dies.

The power waits.

A second filament emerges from another part of the casing. It takes a different course.

Years later, the stain has a raised edge.

There are other ledges. The power does not cover them with gardens or equip them with everything a future grove might require. It makes small changes in places where the plateau’s scattered offspring already arrive: a sheltered crack, a patch of conductive dust, a surface that will hold a casing against the wind.

Some remain unused. Some support growth for a season and then fall quiet. On one, a thin living film begins making a kind of folded chamber that the power has never seen on the plateau.

It watches without correcting the fold.

—

The sealed space beneath the western district troubles the power most.

At regular intervals, many bodies spend heat and substance clearing it. They line its floor with fresh tissue. They hold it open. Nothing enters.

The power searches the layers around it. There was once an approach, now folded deep into the root face. There were conduits that carried something cooler than the surrounding air. The lining contains recessed patterns worn smooth by repeated renewal.

No record remains clear enough to say what came here.

The power could interrupt the operation. It could restore the approach. It could place something inside to justify the preparation.

Instead, during the next opening, it makes a narrow connection between the chamber and an adjacent unoccupied cavity. The connection carries almost no air. It carries pressure and a faint electrical difference.

The tissues at the chamber’s edge close around it.

The power withdraws the connection.

At the next cycle, it tries farther from the edge, where an old conduit has ended blindly for centuries. This time, the tissues do not close. A mobile bud enters the new seam and remains there.

For four more cycles, the chamber is prepared as before.

On the fifth, a portion of the floor is not renewed. On the sixth, the bud unfolds into a filter. A small current of cooler air passes through the neighboring cavity.

The broad chamber still opens. Most of it still waits.

The power has found no reason to make it stop.

—

Over time, its interventions become less visible.

A salt bed receives what has been leaking from a neighboring bed, without impoverishing the neighbor. A brittle support acquires a place where new tissue can attach. An avenue that repeatedly collapses gains a shallow channel through which displaced air can escape.

There are losses it does not prevent. A body whose working chemistry has become incompatible with its neighbors is gradually dismantled. The power follows the mobile buds that leave it and preserves none by force. Two find places to unfold. One carries the characteristic crease of its lineage into a ventilation field.

There are losses it does prevent: a resin reservoir splitting before the surrounding organisms can reinforce it; an ancient remnant cut off by a new wall; a wave of growth about to drive three districts into a pressure state from which none can recover.

In those moments it acts quickly.

Afterward, it stays to see what its action has cost.

Nothing on the plateau comes to worship it. No doorway opens into a face. No voice rises from the furnaces.

Perhaps some inhabitant registers the interventions as a pattern. Perhaps none can distinguish them from the many favorable and unfavorable events through which a body persists. The power does not introduce a signal that means gratitude.

On the eastern apron, the root has become a narrow shelf. Its surface is dark, warm, and faintly ridged. Far below, on a ledge that once held nothing living, a folded chamber has begun to shelter dust.

The plateau continues its work.

A shutter opens. A damaged sheet is tasted and severed. A roof settles onto new columns. In the old sealed district, fresh tissue spreads across most of a waiting floor.

The power remains nearby, attending to what happens next.
