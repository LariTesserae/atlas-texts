The third inhabitant does not take the smaller one away.

It comes around the patch, placing its feet where the soil is firm, and lowers one arm into the current. The divided pad makes a second obstruction. Water that had been pressing against the small body spreads sideways and finds a joint between two paving blocks.

For a moment, this is enough.

Then the district settles again.

High above, on the other side of the reservoir, something has noticed the strain.

It first feels like another adjustment of the foundations. There is a pressure beneath the streets, so slight that only the inhabitants distinguish it from the weight of water. But it does not travel from the supporting ring. It arrives at several places at once, then waits.

The larger inhabitant at the retaining wall opens its back plates.

The pressure withdraws a little.

When the plates open farther, it returns.

Nothing speaks through the walls. There is no new light in the hollow. The presence attends to the difference between a body bracing itself and a body making room.

At the patch, the small inhabitant’s arms are beginning to slip.

A paving block upstream rises by less than the thickness of a young leaf.

The current divides before reaching the steps. Most of it enters the old gutter. A little continues toward the uncovered ground, moving slowly enough for the third inhabitant to guide it with one pad.

The small one stays in its position.

Only after several moments does it lift an arm.

Water passes underneath. The shoot bends, but not far.

It lifts the other.

The leaf rises.

Behind the east façade, the pressure chamber begins its next cycle. The first knock sounds. At the second, bodies appear in the thresholds. The small inhabitant tries to straighten with them, but its feet have sunk into the soil.

The third places a pad beneath its trunk.

At the third knock, the inhabitants answer.

Their vibrations enter the foundations separately. The presence feels the hardened bodies, the soft bodies held in cavities, the one whose nourishment has been interrupted, and the two resting so near each other that their signals partly overlap.

It does not combine them into one signal.

When the vibrations fade, a faint reply moves through the paving.

It has no location they can name. Its weight is less than that of the smallest among them. It remains for a moment, then ceases.

Several inhabitants keep their plates open.

The one near the pressure chamber presses both pads against the façade.

Nothing further happens that cycle.

The presence has found the controls for the drainage, the records of the district’s manufacture, the intended limits of its root systems. Much can be altered. The reservoir can be made brighter. The streets can be dried. The pressure chamber can be silenced.

It leaves these things alone.

The chamber’s knocks gather the inhabitants. The wet seams help them feel how the district is moving. Roots that have passed the brass-colored line are carrying warmth to three unfinished bodies.

There are also demands written into the machinery.

If a channel narrows, the system is meant to draw an inhabitant toward it with a local increase in temperature. If the reservoir load changes quickly, nourishment in the wall cavities can be reduced so that more pressure is available elsewhere. These arrangements have never yet caused a death. The records treat that as proof that they work.

The presence follows the supply channels upward.

For the rest of the day, nothing changes in the hollow except that nourishment continues while the reservoir shifts. Far above, a reserve heater begins working. A pump in a public service room runs longer than it usually does.

The small inhabitant is carried to its recess.

The third body holds it against the feeding surface, making a support where its back has not yet hardened. Fluids move through the wall. The small one’s face clears of pollen.

Beside the recess is an unused indentation in the composite. It was formed as part of the district’s plan, but its edge is too high to support a soft body.

Over the following hours, that edge becomes lower.

The change is slow. The third inhabitant feels it against one arm and shifts its weight. When it pulls away, the alteration stops.

It presses again.

The edge lowers a little farther.

By the next pressure cycle, the small body can rest there without being held.

The third inhabitant remains beside it anyway.

***

The new course of the water is not made permanent at once.

For several days the raised paving block stays loose. The inhabitants visit it, testing its edges, following the diverted stream into the gutter. One lowers it slightly. Another, later, raises it again.

The presence accepts both changes.

Near the garden, the leak in the retaining wall continues to supply the uncovered patch. When a root enters the crack, nobody seals it. A narrow overflow develops beside it, carrying away water that the soil cannot take.

The small inhabitant returns before its plates have finished hardening.

It touches the paving block first.

Then it descends to the patch and sits on the lowest step. There is no current to resist. For a long time it keeps its pads spread on either side of the shoot.

Eventually it folds them beneath its body.

Warm water enters the ground.

A second leaf begins to open.

***

Attention from above brings a danger of its own. The district has addresses, but the city’s records list no residents. Its living bodies are recorded among the mechanisms of heat exchange.

The presence does not summon visitors into the hollow.

It changes the record.

This takes longer than raising the paving block. There must be a way to describe inhabitants who change size, separate, remain unfinished, and sometimes answer together. There must be a way to register occupancy without requiring a name, a lease, or a count.

Until that work is complete, the service doors acquire a restriction: no maintenance entry without an answering vibration from within. Emergency access remains possible, but it leaves a signal in the foundations that cannot be cleared by the visitor.

A planned flushing of the root channels is postponed.

The maintenance plate beside the ascending water gains a second line. In the green light it is difficult to read. The inhabitants do not read it at all.

It is there for whoever comes down from the city.

***

At the next gathering, the presence offers its faint vibration again.

This time it comes from beside the garden steps.

The inhabitant that favors the shortest street turns toward it. The one near the pressure chamber does not move. Two bodies close their plates and return to their adjoining recesses.

The small inhabitant remains by the patch.

After a while it presses one pad against the retaining wall. Its signal is weak, but distinct.

The presence answers with no greater force.

Then the pressure chamber empties, the ascending seam takes up its water, and the district resumes its many sounds.

There are still heavy drops from the roof. There is still grit in the gutters. Some faults must be followed for hours before anyone understands them. The presence stays available at the edge of these activities, and sometimes is not called upon.

By the end of the week, the small inhabitant has found time to feed at the windows again.

It crosses back through the garden with pollen along one arm, stops beside the uncovered ground, and lowers itself to inspect the new leaf.

The water is running well.

After a few moments, it goes elsewhere.
