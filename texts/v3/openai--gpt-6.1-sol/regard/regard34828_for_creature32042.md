The power did not enter through the doorway.

To enter there would have required moving the pot, and it had not yet learned what the pot was doing.

Nor did it descend through the opening. A body arriving from above would have changed the air, interrupted the drops, and cast a shadow over the three small patches of green.

It began by attending.

For a long time, nothing in the court was different except that nothing in it went unnoticed.

The power listened to the knocks as calls, not merely as machinery. It followed water through the grooves and learned where a channel served two needs that sometimes competed. It watched a shell animal scrape a living fold until the fold withdrew, then watched the shell animal lose its shelter. It noticed which passages emptied during a dry interval, which held a last bead, and which had been dry so long that their inhabitants no longer approached them.

It did not assume that the broad creature was alone. Where two folds joined, it attended to both sides of the joining. Where they recoiled, it let the interval remain.

It also attended to the part beneath the ceramic flakes.

The severed end contracted under each drop. Its movements were dwindling, but they were not identical. One edge still drew inward from pressure. Another still opened against softened fiber.

The power did not mistake this for a request to live forever.

It did recognize something unfinished.

Before it touched anything, it learned the pot’s note.

The silence that followed was not wholly fear. Through the buried jars, the note disclosed distances that the ordinary knocking could not reach. The narrow fold against the outside of the bowl listened into those distances. There were spaces beyond the sealed doorway, and the sound returned from some of them changed.

The power left the pot where it was.

***

Its first alteration was smaller than a shell.

High in a wooden rib, beyond the reach of any living fold, a damp lashing had almost parted. Three fibers still carried the strain. When they failed, the rib would settle against the wall and close a passage beneath it.

The power held the rib’s weight without moving it.

Then it waited for the animals that carried fiber.

They came irregularly, dragging lengths too short for the task. Some abandoned their burdens. Some fastened them to old knots. The power made one narrow hollow accessible in the softened wood: a place through which a fiber could pass and take purchase.

The animals found it.

Over many rounds of knocking, they threaded the hollow. The old knot thickened. When the new fibers could bear the weight, the power let them bear it.

The rib remained where it had been. Beneath it, the passage remained open.

There was no new substance in the court and no repair whose future required the power to stay.

***

Water was harder.

More water would have drowned some cavities before it relieved the dry ones. A steady supply would have quieted animals whose calls were shaped by waiting, altered feeding places, and perhaps worn the shallow grooves past usefulness. The court had not been made for abundance.

The power attended to the pale surface above it.

There, water collected in a depression and escaped across a raised edge. A slight lean held much of it back until its weight became enough to send down a cold, heavy fall. Elsewhere, small quantities vanished into cracks that led away from the court.

The power changed the edge by less than the thickness of a finger.

The heavy falls became less heavy. Between them came a few additional drops.

Inside the court, this was almost nothing.

But a cup that had sometimes stayed empty until its lining dried now received water in time. A seam beneath the highest lashing remained damp. In the broken vessel, one upright blade grew beyond the reach of the grazers before bending under its own weight.

The power watched through several dry intervals.

It adjusted the edge once more, then stopped.

There were still waits. There were still passages too remote to receive every pulse. The animals continued to knock, and their knocking continued to matter.

***

The severed end lay beneath the flakes throughout these changes.

The power could have swept the flakes away. They already sheltered small bodies, and the largest fragment rested against a second fracture in the floor.

Instead, it supported that fragment and opened a space beneath its edge.

The severed tissue was no longer pinned.

At the next drop, it contracted into the space. Not far; scarcely farther than the width of a pale leg. The power kept the passage moist and left it otherwise alone.

Several rounds later, a fold from beneath the pool approached.

The two touched and withdrew.

They touched again.

For a long while their surfaces remained separate. Fluid gathered along the line between them. The severed end darkened in places; some of it had already gone beyond recovery.

The power did not make those places living again.

The remaining edges adhered.

Gradually the contractions passed across the join. One dead portion loosened and was taken into a temporary mouth farther along the seam. Another stayed caught among the fibers, where smaller animals began to feed.

The recovered end did not return to its old position. It extended beneath a different slab, leaving the ceramic flakes and their occupants undisturbed.

***

Only after these things did the power attempt to make itself known.

It could have spoken with the whole court. Every jar could have sounded, every wooden rib carried its voice.

It chose a submerged tile near the creature’s thickest portion.

There it made a faint pressure, followed by release.

The animal responded hours later. A fold rose beneath the pool and held against the tile.

The power repeated the pressure, more gently.

This time the fold did not withdraw.

No words passed between them. The power offered a small change in pressure toward an open passage, then waited. The animal explored elsewhere. The power followed nothing, closed nothing, and repeated no summons.

Eventually a thin margin returned.

Over many intervals they learned a little of each other.

The power learned that the creature did not experience its whole body at once. Some distant parts reached it chiefly through changes in fluid, strain, and sound. An injury could arrive first as a passage that no longer answered.

The creature learned that this new pressure did not swell into a falling slab, a grasping mouth, or a rush of cold water.

When it approached, it could remain.

When it withdrew, nothing pursued it.

That was enough for a beginning.

***

The power did not straighten the walls.

It did not lift the court into sunlight or give the inhabitant a body that could leave its seams. It had seen the young swimming in the pool and then finding edges; it would not decide that their settled lives were mistakes.

But it found one place beneath the older tiles where movement had become dangerous.

A jar shoulder and a broken tile formed a narrowing. The creature could pass through it only by thinning almost to nothing, while the water beyond depended on that same passage. During each larger rearrangement, tissue caught there.

The power lifted the tile slightly and settled it onto two sound ceramic fragments nearby.

A little chamber opened.

Water entered first. Then a flattened margin. Then, days later, a thicker fold carrying cloudy fluid and its scattered dark organs.

The creature rested there without being compressed.

It did not become another creature. It acquired one place in which part of its body could be broad.

***

Nothing marked the day the power ceased altering the court.

It remained attentive, but attention no longer meant a hand poised over every failure. Flakes still fell. Shell animals still became trapped. Young still disappeared into passages from which they did not return.

The power watched the consequences of its work as carefully as it had watched the court before it.

At the dimmest end, the pot received a drop.

Its clear note passed through the floor. The tail-knockers paused. Against the outside of the bowl, the listening fold became still.

Beneath the older tiles, the widened chamber held its water.

By the pool, a seam that had once failed during every long dry interval stayed open. A shell cluster settled above it. Their scraping uncovered a little tender skin, and the fold shifted enough to protect itself without withdrawing the whole repair.

Later, beneath the smallest handprint, a pale end entered the shallow palm.

It rested through the quiet.

When the channels filled, it drew back into the wall. Water remained in the print for a while, held by the worn hollow, then slipped down to the floor and joined the next small journey uphill.
