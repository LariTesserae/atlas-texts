The power does not bring light.

It arrives at the rim with the weather, then waits until the plants have ceased leaning and the shell calls have resumed. It could cross the hollow without touching the floor. Instead it puts a little weight on the stone.

The resident lifts its jaw.

The next footfall comes only after the first has been heard.

***

There is no form the power must wear. For this place it chooses one that can sit, that gives off warmth, and that does not take up the whole doorway.

It leaves the groove unoccupied.

For a long time nothing happens except the things that were already happening. Water passes beneath the leaf strip. A hunter releases its belly from a frond and fastens itself farther down. The resident finishes working a scraping between its smaller hands.

Then a large hand comes out and finds the visitor’s foot.

The power lets the hand discover it.

It has given itself skin that can be pressed, joints that have limits, a pulse slow enough not to dominate the water. When the resident turns its hand palm upward, there are creases to examine. None carries the impression of the central upright.

The resident rests two fingertips against the visitor’s wrist.

The power stays.

***

It listens for a long time.

Not only to the sounds. It attends to where the resident pauses and where it does not; to the tasks abandoned without concern, and those returned to after sleep; to the difference between making room and moving away.

It learns not to touch the plates beside the jaw.

It learns that a hand offered at a crossing is withdrawn when the crossing is finished.

When the resident lays a leaf across the channel, the power does not make the leaf permanent. When the leaf floats away, it does not hasten to replace it.

Once, a root catches in a socket. The resident works at it, rests, and goes elsewhere.

The power waits until it returns. Then it places a finger beside the trapped root, where the resident can find it, and takes a little of the strain while the other hand eases the living tissue free.

They lay the root in the seam.

There is a touch on the wrist.

***

Beneath the mountain, the power finds the apparatus.

It does not find a single machine waiting to be repaired. Much of what once had separate parts has become a succession of accommodations: a mineral surface hosting a film, a film feeding a thread, a thread bridging an absence left by something that dissolved before the mountain acquired its present shape.

There are workings that have persisted by changing. There are failures around which life has grown.

The power does not restore those failures.

One old passage, now blocked, has become the warm resting place of a colony of minute animals. A broken regulator admits the very slow variation by which the plants open their air chambers. Returning either to its original condition would damage what is here.

Farther down, however, a seam is closing.

Nothing in the basin can feel it yet. In time, it would starve a section of the eastern fringe. The roots would spend their reserves reaching into dry stone. The leaf beds would become quiet there.

The power holds the seam open.

It does this from beneath, without moving the floor, without changing the warmth under the resident’s belly. The supply continues at its old rate.

Afterward, there is nothing above to mark the work.

***

At the upright, the power keeps its hands apart.

It can perceive enough of the structure to know that the gathering of drops is not its whole function. It can also perceive the resident pressing every available hand against the floor.

That is sufficient reason not to activate it.

Understanding the structure does not tell the power what the resident remembers.

It does not reach into that memory.

When the resident comes to inspect the depression, the power sits nearby. When it goes to the pool, the power remains where it can be found.

Later, at the threshold, the resident puts its small hands against its chest.

Two knocks. A pause. One more.

The power has heard this before. It does not yet know how to answer.

It lets the sound finish.

***

There is one change it offers openly.

During a crossing, the resident gives it two fingers at a shallow channel. The power follows, then stops on the far side and lowers its free hand.

Against the floor it makes a small, distinct pressure: once, twice, then a longer rest.

The resident feels it through its feet.

The power moves its hand a little farther along the channel and repeats the pressure. Then it takes the hand away.

The resident examines the stone.

On another night the power offers the same thing. On a third, the resident holds the visitor’s hand against the floor after the pressure has ceased.

So the power leaves a short stretch of it there.

It is very faint. A creature resting on the stone can feel it; a shell calling from the fringe is not interrupted. The stretch runs along the safe edge of the channel and ends before the socket ring.

The resident traverses it, turns, and traverses it again.

Several nights later, it brings the power to another place. A broad hand presses down beside a difficult slope.

The power adds a second stretch.

It adds no others without being brought to them.

The open center remains open. Most of its stone remains unchanged.

***

The power does not decide that the empty half of the ledge must be filled.

It cannot tell whether that space belongs to expectation, remembrance, comfort, or simply the arrangement of a body in a small room. It does not turn the resident’s habit of moving aside into a request the resident has never made.

But neither does it overlook the space.

Beyond the mountain are lives it could introduce to this one. Some could tolerate the warmth. Some could learn the darkness. A few already know how to wait through long silences without treating them as failures.

The power will not summon them.

It can make this place easier to find for those who choose to travel. It can keep a difficult ascent from becoming a fatal one. It can tell a traveler that someone lives here, and that arriving quietly matters.

For now, it returns itself.

Each time it pauses at the rim and places weight on the stone. Each time the resident has the opportunity to remain in the roof bed, or withdraw into the shelter, or come out.

Sometimes it does not come out.

The power sits elsewhere on those nights.

***

Once, while they are at the doorway, a shelled animal gives the three familiar notes.

The resident answers.

After the pause has lengthened, the power presses its hands against the chest it has made for itself.

The sound is poor. Too soft at first, then too hollow.

The resident’s ridges lift.

The power does not repeat it immediately. It waits while water gathers in the groove.

A small hand reaches across and rests against its chest. The fingers find the place where the second sound should have been made.

The next attempt is closer.

Around the rim, the calls continue.

***

The power has work elsewhere. It does not conceal this by making a body that will sit forever in its absence.

When it leaves, it leaves fully. When it returns, the familiar footfalls come from the rim.

The held seam beneath the mountain needs no further tending. The two stretches of guidance remain faint beneath the water. The resident sometimes uses them and sometimes takes the longer way through the fringe.

At the threshold there is still room beside the old mark.

On one return, the power reaches the difficult channel and pauses.

A hand is waiting there.

It offers two fingers. The power takes them, lets its weight pass into the next step, and releases them when the floor rises.

The resident goes on toward the pool.

For a while the power stands where it has been left, listening to the hollow: the leaves breathing, the shells answering, the water arriving at different times.
