The power first noticed the hand.

It was spread inside an old chamber, darkened almost to the color of the roots that passed through it. Three of its divisions still belonged to the creature. The others had become passages for water, or supports for younger growth. There was no clean place at which the hand ended.

The power did not separate it.

For a long time it attended to the chamber: the younger creature resting there, the old body above it, the small contractions that might have been feeding, speech, or merely what tissue did when a root pressed against it.

Outside, a column restarted. The pressure reached the chamber in a long, diminishing wave.

The younger creature braced one limb against the ceiling. With another it held open a fold in the old body. Water passed through. Something deep inside the fold tightened, then loosened.

The power waited through several such occasions.

It had encountered places where stillness was peace, and places where stillness was death. Here, neither rule was useful. A hillside might need to travel. A body might need to become a room. An ancient thing might have something left to say through the root it was closing around.

The power began by learning how not to interrupt.

It listened below the sound of the columns, where pressure became direction and direction became the approach of a distant event. It followed the small tightenings that crossed the felt between resting creatures. It attended to the pale pieces passed around the pools, and to the changes in those pieces after each neck touched them.

Some exchanges repeated. Others did not.

There were gatherings at which a piece was buried almost at once. At others, it returned to the creature that had released it and was taken back beneath the ribs. Once, after a long afternoon of contact, every creature moved away from the offered piece. It lay alone in the water until the rootlets at its edge curled inward.

The power remembered that too.

***

Its first intervention was small enough to be refused.

A young creature had grown in the footprint of a great many-legged animal. The mound was unusually high. Beneath it lay a pocket of water, and beneath that, a dense mat of roots with no opening wide enough for its body.

For three nights the young tried to descend.

Its hands entered the felt. Its chest followed. Then it withdrew, slowly, with the water draining from its folds. By the fourth morning the stems around the mound had been stripped clean.

An adult came and stood beside it. Water seeped upward between the adult’s divided fingers, but the roots below remained closed.

The power did not break them.

Instead it held a little water against their undersides, no more insistently than a pool might hold it after rain. Along one narrow route, the roots began to turn. A passage formed gradually, wet and pale-lined.

The young put a hand into it.

Then it pulled the hand back.

The power let the passage narrow again.

That afternoon the adult returned. It pressed its head into the mound, waited, and spread a hand over the place where the opening had been.

The power brought the water back.

This time the young descended. For a while its narrow back remained visible between the leaves. Then that too was gone.

The adult stayed until the disturbed water cleared.

***

It did not make the plateau safe.

To stop the great sheets from moving would have closed channels that carried food beneath them. To silence the columns would have starved pastures not yet formed. To bind every subsiding tract in place would have accumulated strain until the whole country broke somewhere else.

But much happened before a collapse that the creatures could not feel until too late.

Far below the submerged shelves, water changed course. Old supports softened. A seam opened through the last mountain stone. Hours afterward, the surface began its terrible descent.

The power could attend to that interval.

At first it placed a faint tightening in the felt before the underground rushes: a pressure no greater than the touch of a distant creature. It repeated it in an empty hollow.

Nothing came.

It changed the timing. It waited.

On the sixth occasion, a creature lifted its head before the columns accelerated.

On the ninth, three left a root disk while the leaves above them were still flat.

The power followed them to see where they went. They did not seek the highest land. They sought a traveling sheet whose underside was loose enough to slide free of the tract below.

When the country dropped, their sheet moved sideways. Pools tipped and refilled. The creatures clung beneath it.

All three remained.

Later, one pressed its neck against the turf and sent a tightening back along the place where the warning had come.

The power did not mistake this for gratitude. It answered by repeating the warning’s first, gentlest pressure.

The creature waited.

Then it made the tightening again.

***

Over many seasons, the warning changed.

The creatures added to it. They passed it farther than the power had intended, through root disks and old chambers, through resting bodies whose limbs had spread into the lower felt. Sometimes they sent it before the power did.

Sometimes they sent something else.

At one hollow they gathered and pressed downward so persistently that the power followed their attention into a layer of ancient pollen. A buried passage there had narrowed around a cluster of small bodies. The power shifted the water. The passage opened.

At another hollow, the same pressure led to an old piece of tissue, long buried, flexing against a stone.

There was nothing trapped.

The creatures remained around the hollow while the piece slowly unfolded.

The power learned to arrive without always repairing.

***

In the old chamber, the younger creature still rested against the body that had become part of the ceiling.

A root had entered a fold too tightly. Water no longer passed freely through it. With each surge from below, the root pressed against a dense organ, and the old hand contracted.

The power could have removed the root.

Instead it made a little room beside it.

The younger creature lifted its head. Its neck blisters shifted. It placed one palm in the new space, then pressed the other against the old body.

The contraction stopped.

For several days the power held the space open. Water resumed its slow passage through the fold. A dark cloud emerged, dispersed, and was carried away.

Then the younger creature moved its palm.

The roots along the side of the chamber had begun to curve around it. The power eased its support, a little at a time, while the living wall took the load.

At last there was nothing left for it to hold.

Deep in the old body, something closed around the original root.

It closed once, loosened, and closed again.

Across the chamber, the younger creature answered.

The power remained nearby, but it did not enter the exchange.

***

There were still bodies that failed to return.

There were still joined hands that lost one another across widening channels. The power caught some, when it could do so without crushing what lay beneath them. Others were reached by creatures following the new warnings. Others were lost in depths where even an immense attention found only scattered movements, each resembling life.

The warnings became part of the country.

Young creatures turned toward them before they had left their mounds. Older ones corrected them, extended them, sometimes disregarded them. The great columns went on hammering. Rain fell through submerged landscapes. Acres of pasture folded into the water and rose elsewhere with their herds still feeding.

On one cold evening, the power felt a small tightening pass beneath an empty hollow.

It followed.

A creature waited there, low against the moss. Beside it was a pool whose reflections had seams.

The creature pressed its head to the ground again.

The power made no opening and moved no water. It settled its attention lightly into the hollow.

For a while nothing happened.

Then a broad, divided hand rose through the pool. Its fine hairs spread against the place where the power was listening, finding no cloth, no skin, no buckle, but taking hold of something all the same.

The power stayed until the last of them let go.
