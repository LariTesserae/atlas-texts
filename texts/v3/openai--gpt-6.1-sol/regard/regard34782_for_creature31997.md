The power first attends to the place without entering it.

There is much to learn from water that moves so little. A current too faint to bend a leaf may be carrying what keeps its roots alive. A roof that appears to shut out the light may also hold back a depth of sediment. The pale threads beneath the pavement need the fallen stems. The living stems need some of what the pale threads release.

The power waits through the opening of a leaf.

It waits longer than that.

In the court beyond the doorway, the firm bud extends across the clean stone. Behind it, one of the three open shoots loses a leaf. A film clouds the leaf’s translucent tip, spreads toward its base, and leaves the blade hanging by a few fibers. The strand continues forward. What the loss will mean cannot yet be distinguished from what the loss will feed.

The power does not remove the film.

Nor does it clear the brown growth from beneath the green. It follows the buried connections, the places where the inhabitant has parted from itself, the places where several lives share a skin of water so thin that each changes the conditions of the others.

There is no single body it can lift safely out of this place.

There is no other place to which it can move the whole of what lives here.

So it remains attentive to this one.

Above the broad opening, a stone has begun to turn in its bed.

Its movement is slow enough that moss still joins it to the blocks on either side. Inside the wall, however, a narrow space has opened. Each passage of colder water takes a little mortar from the lower joint. When the stone falls, others will follow. The opening will fill, and the current through it will cease.

There are other passages into the court, but the power follows them and finds them closed. One is packed with clay. Another ends beneath a subsided stair.

It considers the wall for a long time.

Then, within the narrow space behind the turning stone, a few loose grains settle differently.

They collect against an edge that can bear them. Smaller fragments lodge behind them. The weight passes into the masonry beneath rather than outward over the doorway.

Nothing rises. No broken surface becomes whole.

The stone will still wear down. Water will still take mortar from its joints. But it will not fall now, and the opening remains wide enough for the slow exchange between the courts.

A leaf beside the threshold moves in the current.

It would have moved that way before.

Far above, the floating growth is thickening.

The power does not draw it apart into a bright window. It follows the stems across the surface, finding the sheltered pockets between them and the submerged leaves that depend upon their shade. It notices where the mat is held by a dead branch lodged against an old parapet.

The branch has almost come free.

A little water passes beneath it. The softened wood lifts, turns, and slips beyond the stone.

Over the days that follow, the floating growth spreads into a slightly different arrangement. One narrow interval admits more gray light. Another closes. Below, a patch of algae begins to thicken on a broken roof, and the folded shoot beyond the doorway slowly opens.

The increase is small. The new leaf will have to sustain itself in cold water, among films and settled silt. It is not promised a long life.

But it opens.

The power leaves the empty stretch of paving empty.

It does not lay sediment in a line toward the colonnade or carry the bud to the richer deposit beyond the nearest column. Along the strand, growth is already being apportioned: a root thickens near the broken tile; a young stem draws upon the older portions; the advancing tip pauses.

There are things happening here for which distance is not the only measure.

After a long interval, the tip begins again.

Its underside touches a shallow chisel groove. A root enters the groove and finds a trace of mud caught there. The hold is slight, but enough to keep the strand from shifting when water passes through the opening.

Behind it, the broken tile remains secure.

Beyond it, the next joint lies within reach.

The power watches the bud approach that joint. Elsewhere in the basin, a roof settles into a room already full of dead stems. A buried vessel releases its bubble. Pale growth consumes the fallen leaf beside the threshold.

None of these is stopped.

The place continues to change, and the power continues to learn which changes leave room for life, and which would close that room before anything living could answer.

For now, it has done very little.

The opening remains open.

A leaf receives enough light to unfold.

At the end of the strand, the bud holds firmly against the stone and adds a little length.
