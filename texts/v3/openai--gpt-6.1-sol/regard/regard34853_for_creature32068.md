The power notices the unnecessary readiness.

Not the height of the land, though it is extraordinary. Not the mineral sheets, nor the leaves that turn their dark faces toward the sky to shed heat. It notices the little preparation that continues after the visitor has gone: chambers opening before a pressure arrives, a drainage channel kept clear for boots that are no longer there.

It does not know whether this is memory, expectation, or a valve that has not yet learned to close.

It stays to find out.

***

At first, it places nothing on the surface.

Instead it listens from within a drop of water already descending through the canopy. There is much to learn before the drop reaches the ground. Each leaf adds something. Each delay changes its temperature. By the time it enters the rootwork, it carries a history the power could not have acquired by looking down from the sky.

The resident tastes the drop and passes it onward.

The power follows only where the water is allowed to go.

At a closed junction, it waits. At a membrane that admits water but excludes the compounds traveling with it, it leaves those compounds behind. It could cross without permission. That would tell it very little about how to arrive.

For several days, its presence is indistinguishable from weather.

Then it makes a small error.

It steadies a chamber that has been trembling under an uneven load. The improvement is slight: a partition ceases to flex, and fluid passes more easily into the next vessel.

Far away, three sensing leaves close.

The chamber’s trembling had been useful. It carried a distinction between two pressures that otherwise felt alike.

The power removes its support.

The trembling returns. The leaves open.

After this, it is more careful about calling something damage.

***

The resident eventually finds the regularity.

A certain packet of water returns without the usual changes. Not unchanged altogether; that would be impossible here. But a faint pattern survives each passage through living tissue, repeated precisely enough to be recognized.

The resident diverts it into a shallow loop.

The power goes around.

The resident closes one exit, opens another, and sends it around again.

This continues for a long time.

Neither is speaking yet. They are establishing that something can happen twice because one of them intends it.

The first question, if it can be called a question, is a variation in pressure. The resident makes it, waits, makes it differently.

The power answers with the first variation.

Two chambers tighten. A third remains loose.

It answers with the second.

Now the third tightens too.

There is no sudden understanding. There are months of sequences that turn out to mean nothing, responses that arrive at the wrong scale, exchanges interrupted by the raising of a ridge. Once, the part of the resident involved in the contact grows elsewhere, and the power waits beside tissues that no longer hold anyone’s attention.

Eventually, the resident sends back the pattern by another route.

***

What the power learns is not a request for rescue.

The resident is concerned about a connection beneath a western buttress. It has been narrowing for years. Much of the tissue around it is healthy. Keeping the connection open diverts nourishment from a growing margin, where several young residents have established their first loops.

Beyond the narrowing lies a small, old portion of itself.

That portion does little work. It opens slowly. It retains distinctions no longer useful in the present water. When consulted, it sometimes produces a structure unsuited to the load and must be contradicted by younger junctions.

Yet activity continues to travel toward it.

The power follows one such exchange and discovers something like the taste of a vanished lowland: warmer water, a different balance of salts, pressure arriving through roots that have long since become mineral.

It does not recover a scene. There are no images to display, no words to translate. There is a living difference between water that has touched that place and water that has not.

Soon, neither will exist.

The resident has begun withdrawing from the connection.

It has also begun rebuilding it.

Both decisions are still underway.

***

The power could preserve the old portion exactly.

It could relieve every cost of maintaining it. It could hold every chamber in its present arrangement while the land rose around it.

Before acting, it follows the exchange again.

The old portion is changing. Slowly, with resistance, but changing. Something from the younger junctions has reached it. A valve that once opened late now sometimes opens early.

Preserving it exactly would interrupt that.

So the power makes a trial elsewhere: a tiny loop of unoccupied water, with two routes through it. One route remains ordinary. Along the other, it offers support that can be accepted in degrees.

The resident tests the support by closing the route.

Nothing pushes back.

It opens the route and discovers that less work is needed to keep it open. It closes it again.

The power waits.

When the resident begins extending toward the old connection, the offered support extends with it, stopping wherever the resident stops.

No new chamber is chosen for it. No old chamber is declared indispensable.

The narrowing is eased, but not removed. There is room now for water to pass and for tissue to grow. What crosses, and what grows, remains unsettled.

Over the following season, part of the old network is renewed. Part is left behind.

The power does not keep the abandoned part secretly alive.

***

Elsewhere, it encounters a resident losing access to a shared supply.

There, too, it listens before touching anything. The restriction proves to have several causes: deliberate withholding, a recent fracture, and a long-standing difference in how two networks recognize useful tissue.

It cannot make them agree by widening the channel.

It can, however, keep the fracture from making their disagreement immediately fatal.

It supports the failing wall. It offers a separate trickle to the deprived network, then makes the offer perceptible to its neighbor. The neighbor closes several junctions. The trickle remains available; it does not enlarge.

For a while, nothing else changes.

Then a new passage begins to grow between them, narrow and heavily partitioned.

The power leaves the partitions alone.

***

Its work remains scattered.

Under one crowded mat, a young loop is given enough water to attempt a second attachment. On a wind-facing slope, a resident declines an offered brace and thickens its own roots instead. Deep in a buttress, two networks use the same supported cavity without joining.

Some offers go unanswered. Some are dismantled and incorporated into ordinary tissue. The power learns to recognize both outcomes.

It also attends to what lies beneath the continent.

In the colder lowland, it finds water diverted by the rising margins and growth darkened by a new shadow. It does not assume that all available space is empty merely because the upland cannot sense who occupies it.

The frontier continues to advance, but information begins reaching it from farther below. Several pale lobes alter their direction. Others do not.

***

The old resident still prepares for footsteps.

With easier passage through the western connection, the preparation has changed. It now distinguishes more kinds of weight. Once, when a broken branch falls onto its surface, the drainage channel opens and then closes almost at once.

When the visitor returns, the channel stays open.

The ground firms beneath the boots. Low leaves lift, shedding their accumulated water away from the place where a body might rest.

Nothing announces the power’s presence.

The visitor sees only a dry hollow in a wet country, and perhaps mistakes it for hospitality.

Below it, the resident sends a slow pattern through the renewed connection. Several distant chambers answer. One does not.

The resident waits for that one.

And the power, which could answer in its place, waits too.
