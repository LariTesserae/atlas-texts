The power did not arrive by opening the door.

It had noticed the pressure against the wood, and the roots drawn tight around the absent hinges. It understood that a closed thing might be a shelter before it was an obstacle.

For a long time, its arrival could not be distinguished from another condition of the water.

It attended to the plain through several cycles of growth. It watched the station lift and settle. It followed the dark liquid from the sweating iron into the leaves, and from the leaves into the cuffs. It watched a young one extend its fine projection onto a floor the size of a coin, then draw back when the southern hammering began.

It did not quiet the hammering.

Not yet.

The forest had grown into the noise. The inhabitants turned their openings toward it. A sound that injured one part of the country might be carrying another part home.

Nor did it bring fish into the clearings. Their emptiness was not a request.

Its first act concerned a small opening beneath the station.

The opening had been made by a young inhabitant. It consisted of two steps and a low ceiling. The upper step met the ceiling directly, leaving nowhere for the young body to go. Nevertheless, the young one returned to it. A pale projection appeared, touched the upper step, and withdrew. Later it tried from a different fold. Later still, after feeding, it tried again.

The power waited until the pocket was empty.

Then it made the space above the step slightly larger.

It changed nothing else. The opening remained crooked. Its plaster retained a dark mark where the young one had pressed against it. The step was still too high for an easy passage.

When the young one returned, it stopped.

A projection touched the new space. Another touched the old mark. For some time it held itself between them.

Then it climbed.

The power learned something from the way it descended afterward. It went backward, keeping one extremity against the mark until the last possible moment.

So the next changes were made beside familiar things, never in their place.

Near the station door, a length of loose rail lay beneath a stand of black leaves. Each rise of the foundations drew it closer to a root in which the resident had formed three cuffs. Soon it would pinch the root against the brickwork.

The power moved the rail no farther than a falling leaf might have carried it.

The root continued to grow along its old course.

Nothing announced this.

Elsewhere, on a ridge whose orange cracks had begun to widen, the power held a patch of cooling skin together long enough for the openings beneath it to close. Then it let the ridge split. The inhabitants had already moved into adjoining growth. Fresh stems entered the breach.

It did not make the new ground old. It gave what lived there time to meet the change.

***

The resident beneath the pumping station became aware of it gradually.

A passage that should have broken during a current shift remained continuous for three breaths longer. A cuff exhausted by hunger received a dark droplet when the nearest leaf could not have supplied one. Beneath the floor, a sharp corner ceased pressing against a soft joint.

The resident withdrew from these places.

The power did not follow.

For several cycles, the altered passage went unused. Red filaments grew over the cuff. The extra droplet remained enclosed in its leaf until the leaf opened and released it into the current.

Then, one day, a tapping came from the station door.

Three knocks, a pause, and one more.

The power had heard that pattern before. It belonged to the resident’s searches for a route: a sound sent into a broken continuity, awaiting some sign that the far end remained.

It answered from the small improvement beneath the floor.

Not with a voice. With a single knock, made against the underside of the step.

The resident opened no door.

The power waited.

Much later, three cuffs turned toward the step. Through them appeared a strip of worn flooring, interrupted twice by water. Something pale passed beneath its surface like a hand beneath a sheet.

The fourth knock came from very close by.

The power answered there.

***

Their understanding never became a language that would have served anyone walking upright through ordinary rooms.

It consisted of weights offered and accepted; of openings left available without being entered; of taps that ceased when another tap requested silence.

The power could have reached the resident wherever it was dispersed. Instead it used the places where the resident had chosen to gather.

It learned that a widened chamber was not necessarily a better chamber. The resident rested by pressing against its boundaries. Once, when the power eased all four walls outward, the body unfolded sharply, searching for contact. The walls returned before the search became distress.

After that, relief took another form.

Beside the resting chamber, the power made a second recess. It was narrow, warm, and empty. Its entrance could be closed from within. No current ran through it. No gauge determined its dimensions.

The resident inspected it from a cuff no wider than a slit.

For a long time, it used the recess only to keep a folded length of pale material. Then it put that material elsewhere and left the recess empty again.

The power did not furnish it.

During the next rise of the station, the resident placed one extremity inside. The rest of its body departed by the old route.

During the rise after that, more of it remained.

***

The machinery required patience.

There were engines whose pounding was scarcely distinguishable from the knocking of crowded passages. There were roots that had entered motor housings and taken on functions no separate mechanism now performed. A shutdown relieved the rigid blades, but it also thinned the dark liquid. After a long shutdown, several cuffs closed and did not reopen.

The power examined the work beneath the noise.

It found a bearing grinding against a shaft. It found pressure returning through a circuit that had no means to release it except by lifting the whole building. It found a buried chamber receiving heat faster than the young ground could carry it away.

It repaired the bearing.

The forest above it altered over many days. Blades lost their blurred edges. Some remained narrow; others broadened. The chatter disappeared, but the lower rhythm continued, and the cuffs still turned toward it.

The power made a second path for the pressure, small at first. When the gauge next reached the red sector, the station rose only half its usual distance.

The resident left its chamber anyway.

For many cycles it continued to leave. The power kept the old escape passages open.

Only much later did the resident stay through a rising of the needle.

One hard joint rested against the wall of the new recess. Another lay on the familiar floor.

The pumps kept turning.

***

There were matters the power left unresolved.

It did not know at first what lay behind the doors within the rectangular bubbles. It followed some without touching them. It watched what persisted when their corridors folded, and what was consumed. It would not increase their number merely because the inhabitants fed upon them.

There were passages in the newly arrived terrain that had histories elsewhere. The power did not straighten them into the local forest or sever them from it.

Sometimes a whole promontory came down through the water with its roots already tightened in alarm. On these occasions, the power caught enough of its weight to soften the concussion. Silt still rose. Channels still changed. But the inhabitants had a little longer to close their doors.

Beyond the plain, the vast exhalation continued.

The surface still withdrew. Chains still lengthened between their links. No shore appeared.

What changed was smaller than a new world.

A young one could try a passage more than once without the ceiling crushing its first attempt. A hungry cuff sometimes found nourishment along a route that had failed. The resting chamber no longer contracted so abruptly that sleep and flight became the same motion.

And there was somewhere to knock.

***

After a long interval, the resident made an opening that faced no engine.

It grew on the underside of a sideways tree, where two currents met and held each other nearly still. Inside was a short stretch of floor. Beside it stood a shallow basin, empty except for a dark flake of iron.

The resident tapped once.

The power attended.

A pale, jointed part emerged far enough to touch the basin. It remained there, bearing no weight. Then it withdrew.

The cuff stayed open.

The power did not enter. It settled beside the tree, lightly enough that the current did not change, and waited with the opening.

Below them, leaves folded around their dark nourishment. A new ridge cooled in the distance. The repaired bearing turned with a low, even sound.

For the first time the resident rested while one of its doors remained open.

Nothing came through it.

Nothing was required.
