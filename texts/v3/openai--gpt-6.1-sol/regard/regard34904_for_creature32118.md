The first thing the power did was wait.

It did not bring a lamp. It did not stop the pumps or lift the water out of the channels to see what lived beneath it. When a crab tapped against a pipe, it allowed the interval to pass without answering.

For a long time, its attention had no weight.

Fish pressed against the pilings. Operators cleaned the grooves in their gauges. A woman carried a drawstring between two basins, stopping twice as its weight moved toward the end she held. The power learned why she waited until the last length had entered the water before turning away.

It learned that the great animals were not obstacles. That the crust beneath a crossing was not merely ground. That a coat hung in a fold of skin could be an imposition, an affection, or both, and that it could not decide which by looking only at the coat.

It listened to the rooms as well as the plain.

There were things people said beside a bed which they did not say at work. Someone dreaded the walk to a station whose route had begun to change. Someone had lost a brother in a basin that returned the boat but not its passenger. Someone liked the warm air and the large quiet animals and would not have exchanged them for any place described by the people who came from elsewhere.

One child wanted to know whether a particular giant recognized her.

The power did not answer on its behalf.

Instead, when she next stood beside it, she felt a fold of skin tighten very slightly beneath her hand. The movement had always been there. For once, the pumps did not obscure it.

She stood awhile longer.

***

Beneath the mud, the broad being turned.

The power followed the movement down through clay, through warm mineral seams, through places where water pressed upward against water. It found no chamber containing the being, no boundary at which the plain ended and a body began.

It found strain.

A movement begun far below had to travel through crowded layers. Some parts yielded readily. Others were held by pipes, by old foundations, by the weight of basins whose water belonged partly elsewhere. A small adjustment beneath one station arrived at another as a narrowing passage. Elsewhere it lifted a shelf so slowly that the worms had time to lengthen their burrows.

The being was not careless. It was occupied.

The power stayed with one place where the strain gathered. It did not push. It made room beside it.

Far above, a ridge acquired a shallow groove.

Water rose into the groove and paused. The being rested against the offered space, withdrew, then rested there again.

The power held still.

That was their first exchange.

***

The first intervention people noticed concerned a missing step.

A maintenance worker returning home put out her boot where the ceramic studs should have ended. The stair had moved. There was water below her, and below that water another surface, facing upward.

Something met her sole.

It was firm enough to take her weight, broad enough that she did not have to balance. She brought her other foot beside it. With both hands she found the railing, now an arm’s length farther to the left.

The support remained while she climbed.

In the morning, she brought two others to examine the place. They found a low shelf of black mineral, still wet along its fresh edges. It extended only as far as it needed to. Small crabs were already under it.

They marked it with a stud.

The power did not preserve the stair in that position forever. Three months later it moved again. But the change came gradually, and the household felt it during their morning check.

There was time to lay another route.

***

At a southern intake, a young drawstring had been held for six shifts.

The crew had opened the screen and removed a plate from the housing. They had fed slack into the basin. Still, some distant portion of the animal remained caught in a place their tools could not reach.

Each day there was less of it near the pump.

The power traced its length through three channels and a fold in the water. It found the trapped portion beneath a foundation that no longer stood above the place where its weight fell.

It supported the foundation for the space of one breath.

The drawstring passed.

At the station, the operator holding it felt the weight settle evenly into her arms. She called for the others, and together they carried it clear.

Afterward, they put a broader guard around the intake. The power left that work to them.

***

It did not make every route dependable.

There were journeys on the plain that could not be shortened without closing channels through which other inhabitants moved. There were pools that could not be made ordinary without cutting off the warm air beyond their second surfaces. And there were distances that belonged to the broad being’s body in ways the power understood only after waiting through many turns.

But where a route became dangerous, it gave the change a texture.

Ceramic studs loosened before a crossing parted. A handrail developed a faint, steady tremor before its platform drew away from the stairs. Rope around a doubled basin grew rough on the inward side, though the outer fibers remained unchanged.

These warnings were not useful everywhere, and the power altered them when the inhabitants failed to recognize them.

It watched a padded animal stop at a trembling rail. It watched fish gather beneath a loosening stud. It learned to warn some creatures sooner and others differently.

The operators began recording the new signs. Their records included mistakes.

For the first time, a station’s safety instructions contained a passage that began: *We used to think this meant—*

***

The power attended to the work.

A pipe delivering steel washers had run for years, and everyone knew how to collect them. Nobody knew where they went after the carts left the last accessible platform.

The power followed a cart.

It did not destroy the pipe because its purpose was uncertain. Nor did it assume that years of operation made the labor necessary. It followed the washers farther, through each exchange, until it could distinguish what they served from what merely kept people busy and tired.

Some work continued.

Some gauges acquired an unmistakable resting position. Pumps that had required attention every few hours began to hold their settings through the night.

One engine stopped.

The crew waited beside it with their tools. Nothing flooded. No pressure accumulated. The neighboring animals remained where they were.

Eventually the crew went home.

***

The broad being learned the shape of the help.

When it needed to turn, it pressed into the space the power had made beside it. The power widened that space carefully, taking no living patch for emptiness, no unused foundation for something without inhabitants.

Sometimes there was nowhere to widen it.

Then they waited together.

Above them, a great animal held one foot lifted over the mud. An operator set down a cup before it could fall. A child stood still, as she had been taught.

The power carried a little of the strain.

The being moved a little less.

Water found a channel between two ridges, and the great animal put its foot down.

***

The darkness remained.

Warm moisture gathered under the handrails. Lamps still consumed their fuel without illuminating anything. The fish still touched one another in the crowded basins, and the crab signals traveled farther than any voice.

There were still losses. Some the power reached too late; some it could not prevent without causing another. It stayed through the grieving, without asking anyone to call the loss necessary.

No shrine appeared.

At one station, people began keeping a spare length of perforated rubber beside the door. At another, the crew left a clear channel where a walkway would have been easier to build. A worker taking grit from a giant’s scar stopped when the skin tightened and waited for it to ease.

Beneath the plain, the broad being turned again.

This time the movement passed through the layers without catching at the old foundation. The channels drained slowly in their new direction.

Above, a tired operator slept through the end of a shift. Her replacement had found the station without difficulty.

The power remained near the strain, holding only what needed holding.
