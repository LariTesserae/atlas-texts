The power did not bring light.

For a long while it made no change at all.

It attended to the city through several settlings of the root, through the passage of oil from a chamber whose inhabitant had died to a chamber whose inhabitant had not yet begun to receive it. It followed a message downstream and did not mistake the message for its maker. Where two lives shared a partition, it learned to distinguish them without dividing the partition.

This required care. A tightening might be pain, or work, or the preservation of something that would otherwise be lost. A closed passage might be a failure. It might also be the only boundary an inhabitant possessed.

The power waited beside the sealed door.

It felt the elder contract when the advancing sheet touched the threshold. It felt the younger stop, then find the edge. Neither response was altogether harmless to the other. Neither was merely an obstruction.

Beyond them, in the shell, a seam of mineral had almost exhausted its useful fraction. The remaining material required more nourishment to separate than its separation yielded. Filaments continued to enter it. They were following a disposition older than the sealed door.

The power changed the seam.

Not into food. That would have sent an unfamiliar abundance into channels whose inhabitants had survived by learning precisely how little they could spare.

It loosened a bond in the mineral, so that the same slow work yielded slightly more.

The difference entered the city by its existing routes.

One share reached the elder. One reached the pale sheet. Others remained in junctions the power had not overlooked merely because no membrane there was stretched to attend.

The elder retained its share.

The younger extended a little farther along the boundary.

Nothing opened.

The power did not regard this as a refusal of its help.

*

The next thing it changed was outside the seed.

A long fiber in the surrounding root had been holding more strain than it could release gradually. When it slipped, the hollow would receive a report great enough to press many inhabitants into one response. Several would recover. One, whose last private route had become very narrow, would not.

The power could have prevented the slip.

Instead, it gave the fiber a yielding attachment farther along its length.

The root settled.

The towers bellowed. Doors shuddered in their thresholds. The loose partition clattered, and the sounds returned across the hollow bearing news of roofs, seams, and sealed rooms.

But the deepest blow arrived in two parts.

Between them there was time for the narrow route to release its oil.

The inhabitant remained distinguishable afterward.

In one of the towers, a seam tightened in answer to the unfamiliar interval. Its resident held that alteration until the root moved again.

The power listened to the answer without deciding that it was gratitude.

It might have been a warning.

*

There were places where help could do damage simply by arriving.

The elder behind the sealed door had kept its reserve for so long that an open channel into the chamber would have been an invasion, however nourishing its contents. The young inhabitant beneath the avenue needed somewhere to grow, but the nearest room was already someone’s body.

So the power attended to a disused groove beside the threshold.

It ran toward a socket where a building had disappeared. The groove contained little of value. Its walls had hardened; the ordinary advance of the pale sheet would have crossed it rather than followed it.

The power softened a short length.

The young inhabitant encountered the change as another favorable difference.

It followed.

At the socket, the paving was thin enough to yield without lifting the elder’s shelves. There the young one folded a small chamber around a grain it had carried with it. The chamber was crude. Its entrance narrowed when it should have widened. A portion of its first roof was consumed and made again.

The power left the errors alone.

When the young one later touched the far side of another occupied threshold, it stopped before altered oil reached it.

The elder, receiving the faint pressure of that pause through the avenue, released something into the old groove.

The substance took a long time to arrive.

By then the younger had a place in which to retain it.

*

Across the hollow, a tendril had been extended for years.

Its inhabitant was not starving. Nor was it safe from hunger. The tendril represented a choice whose cost was paid in small installments by the membranes below three shelves.

The power could have placed a support beneath it.

It could have carried the tip to the far wall, or filled the vacancy with branches, or made the distant buildings suddenly accessible.

Instead it examined the searching movement.

The tip turned slightly after every great report. Between reports it moved less, but it did move. It was not only waiting to be caught.

Far away, another inhabitant had put out a filament. The two would never meet by the growth available to them. Each had entered the hollow at a different angle, and both were nearing the limit of what their settled tissues could sustain.

The power did not draw them together.

It made a little nourishment available near the base of each, by the same patient alteration of the rind. Then, during a settling, it allowed a small movement from one filament to reach the other through the air.

The movement was barely more than a disturbance of the tip.

The second inhabitant withdrew a short distance.

The first held still.

Much later, the second extended again.

The power repeated nothing. The hollow now carried the disturbance without assistance; a tension had changed in one of the towers, making a surface that returned these slight movements differently.

The two filaments remained far apart.

Nevertheless, each began to spend its growth at a new angle.

*

The city did not become quiet.

Oil still advanced reluctantly through the narrow channels. Some passages closed. An inhabitant withdrew from a room, and the room stood for a while before the advancing tissue took it apart. Two neighbors joined their reserves and did not separate again.

The power kept no inventory of lives that ought to remain unchanged.

It attended instead to the places where a small difference could preserve a possibility: a route that could be reopened from inside, a boundary that could be maintained without consuming its keeper, a yielding attachment that left the towers enough strain to speak.

It did not make every mineral seam easier to work. Some fed only the spreading sheet, and faster growth there would have pressed upon inhabited rooms. Some channels it left dry until the tissues beside them began the work of opening.

Once, in the chamber that remembered a fiber becoming slack, the added nourishment reached a private reserve.

A tension came through the wall.

The inhabitant released what it might have kept.

Farther along, someone received enough to sustain an unfinished task.

The power felt the passage open. It made no alteration there.

*

In time, the two searching filaments touched.

The contact was slight. During the next movement of the root they lost it.

One tip stiffened. The other grew past the place where they had met, then bent back. They touched again, held, and parted.

Neither could feel the whole arrangement.

Below them, a young chamber widened its entrance. An old door remained closed. A tower waited with an altered seam for the next event in the root.

The power stayed attentive.

Between the filaments, the beginnings of a knot took shape.
