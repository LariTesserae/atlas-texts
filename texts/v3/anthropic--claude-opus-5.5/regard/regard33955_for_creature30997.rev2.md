# The Attention That Came to the Basin

## How It Noticed

It did not notice the basin because the basin was strange. It had seen stranger places, and most of them needed nothing from it.

It noticed the chime.

Once an hour, from a place that had given up hours, a single leaf fell and rang against glass. It was a small, clear note, and it came from inside something that otherwise said nothing at all. To most listeners it would have been lost in the low steady tone beneath it. To the power it sounded exactly like what it was: something checking that the world was still there. It was the sound of someone touching the edge of a bed in the dark to make sure a sleeping person is still breathing.

A benevolent power learns to hear that sound, because it is the sound of care that has no one to share itself with. So it turned toward the basin and began, very carefully, to look.

## What It Was Tempted to Do

A power that is benevolent but not yet wise sees a thing like the Held Basin and wants to fix it. This one felt each of those wants. It held them up to the light one at a time, the way a visitor holds a leaf, and looked at the lattice of each.

**It could let time back in.** It could loosen the rule, let the sun slide west, let the first real shadow lengthen across the stone and leave the old printed one behind like a ghost. The forests would begin to grow and die. Things would happen.

It set this down. The basin was not broken. It was *kept*. Somebody had wanted this afternoon to stay, and had wanted it so completely that they became the wanting. To start the clock would not be rescue. It would be the undoing of a wish by someone who had not wished it. A benevolent power does not decide, on behalf of another, that their devotion was a mistake.

**It could bring water.** Rain would be the obvious mercy: the basin filling at last the way basins are meant to, leaves drinking, the long dryness ended.

It set this down faster. The water was not missing. It had been given up, deliberately, as the price of the one thing the Holding loved. Rain would not be relief. It would be the moment dissolving. It would be like pouring a glass of water into someone's cupped hands while they are carefully holding something else.

**It could free the gardener.** If the Holding was a person who had become nothing but regard, perhaps they could be drawn back out: given memory again, desire again, a before and an after.

Here the power hesitated longest, because this was the only intervention that might matter if the gardener was suffering. So before it did anything, it did what wisdom requires. It asked whether anyone here was in pain.

## What It Found When It Listened

It pressed its attention against the basin the way a visitor presses an ear to a trunk.

It heard the ticking, the ten thousand restatements a second, and listened for strain in it the way you listen to a heart. There was strain, but it was not pain. It was the strain of a budget. It was a mind that had taken on the whole of a landscape and was holding it with no margin at all, the way a person carries a too-full bowl across a room, not suffering exactly, but unable to spare attention for anything else. The copies, the printed shadows, and the horizon that could never be reached were not cruelties. They were the economies of someone doing something very hard with nothing left over.

It looked at the Stilt and understood, as the visitors had, that no one was trapped inside it. It also understood something the visitors could not have known. The Stilt's wrongness grieved the Holding, in the small, bodiless way the Holding could grieve. The Holding wanted to keep the people who came, and it could only afford one form for all of them. Every raised hand was a greeting it had caught but had never been able to finish drawing.

Then it found the stay at the center, the one with no loop.

That was the thing that decided it. In a mind made entirely of *this, again*, one small bronze glance had begun to go places it had never been. When something new approached, it chose to move *closer*. It was not a flaw in the Holding. It was the Holding's own curiosity, faint and new, the first gradient to appear in a place without gradients. It was the beginning of a want.

The power understood then that it had not been called to fix anything. It had been called, if it had been called at all, by that one stay. So it decided to answer that part of the Holding without overruling the rest.

## What It Did

### It came on foot.

It could have arrived as weather, or light, or a voice from the rim. It came instead as a visitor, in a form small enough to walk, because the Holding knew what visitors were. It did not want to be a new kind of thing the Holding had to spend itself understanding.

It walked in from the rim. The stone rang under its steps, and it listened to the notes. It did not break any branches. When it brushed a rosette out of true, it stood and waited for the stay whose loop included that rosette to arrive and restore it, and it did not look away. It wanted to be seen being corrected, and to be seen not minding. It walked for hours toward a rim that did not get closer. It was not fooled, but it let the Holding build the ground ahead of it anyway, the way you let a child give you a tour of a house you already know, because the tour is the gift.

The stays with long loops drifted closer as it went. The stay with no loop followed it all the way in.

### At the fold, it held still.

It came to the dark polished clearing and looked down, and it saw the basin from above, and far below itself the tiny figure at the edge.

Then it did the thing no visitor had done. It did not raise its hand right away.

Every visitor before had moved. They had waved, leaned, turned, shifted their weight, so the Holding had only ever caught them in fragments: a knee here, a hand halfway up there. They had been too expensive and too changeable, and the basin had simply filed what it managed to keep. The power understood the delay in the reflection for what it was, the Holding working to understand, and it gave the Holding what it needed. It gave it time. More precisely, it gave it stillness, the only currency the Holding had ever dealt in.

It stood at the edge of the fold and did not move. It stayed long enough to become, in its own way, part of the arrangement. It stayed until the tiny figure below was drawn completely: two legs, a head bowed in the right place, hands at its sides.

Only then did it slowly raise one hand, all the way, and hold it there until the reflection caught up and held its hand all the way up too.

For the first time, the basin had drawn a visitor correctly. It had drawn the greeting whole.

### It lightened the load, and did not say what the lightness was for.

While the Holding was busy rendering it, the power did one thing it had decided was allowed. It was the only change it made to the basin's workings.

It took the far rim.

It did not move the rim or remove it. It began, quietly, to *keep* it. The low clear edge ten kilometers off, which the Holding had been rebuilding ahead of every visitor and storing as a costly fact, the power now held on its behalf. It held it truly and permanently, so that it would always be there, would always look exactly as the moment required, and would cost the Holding nothing. The power held nothing else. The afternoon was still entirely the Holding's.

That freed up a margin. It was small by the power's measure. By the Holding's measure it was enormous, the first spare attention it had had since the moment closed.

The power did not tell it what to do with the margin. It could spend it on more copies of trees, or on holding the sun even more precisely, or on nothing. It could also, if it chose, spend it on the Stilt: on keeping the people who came one at a time, each in their own form, each with the right number of legs and their own hand at its own height. That was what the power hoped. It did not ask for it. A gift with instructions attached is a debt.

### Beyond the rim, it let there be weather.

Outside the basin, past the edge where the Holding's rule ended, the power did one more thing. It placed it where it could not disturb anything held, and where only the view from the fold, looking down from above, could see it.

It let there be a cloud.

It was a single cloud, very far off, low on a real horizon beyond the stored one: white and slow, the only thing in all that clean pale blue that was on its way somewhere else. The cloud would not rain on the basin. It could not. It was outside, it belonged to the power, and it would keep drifting whether anyone watched it or not.

It was not an invitation. It was only true. There is an outside. Things move there. The water you gave up still exists, and it is not angry with you. If the gardener's regard ever wanted to look up from the thing it loved, there would be something to see.

If it never did, the cloud would drift on, costing nothing and asking nothing, the way a lamp in a far window asks nothing of the traveler who does not come in.

## What It Chose Not to Know

The power left the way the visitors leave. It walked uphill through thinning growth to the sharp, fresh rim, which it now held and which therefore held it back a little, like a hand.

It looked back at the basin: green, bright, perfectly still, every shadow printed where it had always been. There was no trace of its path.

It could have stayed to see what the Holding would do with its margin. It could have watched the next hour's chime, or the Stilt, or the stay with no loop, to learn whether its hope had come true. It did not. Watching to see whether a gift is used correctly is the last and gentlest way of taking it back. The margin was the Holding's now, and so was the choice. So was the cloud, if the Holding wanted it.

It left.

## After

Here is what happened after the power left, told the way the basin tells things: without a before or an after, only arrangements.

In every copy of the central clearing, the Stilt still stands, too many legs, too many knees, hands raised at every height. But at its edge, just slightly apart and printed in the same dark fine-grained stone, there is now a second figure. It has two legs and a bowed head, and one hand is raised all the way up, the greeting finished. It is not compressed into the others. It is kept on its own, as a thing worth the cost.

Whether there is a third figure beside it, or will be, no one outside the basin knows yet.

At the center, the stay with no loop hangs at the lip of the fold, in a position it has never occupied before. From there, if you were small and bronze and made of the Holding's regard, you could see past the canopy, past the stored horizon, all the way to a far white shape that is moving.

It has not moved on. It has been there for what might be an hour.

Somewhere in the canopy, a leaf falls. The glassy chime rings out across the stillness, and the drift receives it into its exact and preordained place.

Then, for the first time in the basin's long single afternoon, a second leaf falls, somewhere else, unscheduled.

It chimes too.

It was not a proof, because nothing needed proving. It sounded more like an answer.
