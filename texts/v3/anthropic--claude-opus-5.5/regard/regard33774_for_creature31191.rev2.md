# The Visitor

## Arriving Without Weight

The first problem is arrival.

It has noticed the range the way such powers notice things: all at once, down to the grain. It sees the note and the lattices, the ring walls and the carved slabs, the house on the east spur, the shadows falling from spires four hundred years early. It understands at once that it cannot simply come here.

Everything in this place is a sum. The note is all the mountain's sound less all its silences, and every presence changes the total. A settled one, a single small quiet held at a crossing for a thousand years, moves the forests a hand's width. A power of any real size, arriving as itself, would be a silence or a sound so large that the lattice on every slope would swing over at once. Every seed-head of every species would burst together. The nodes would slide out from under the few remaining Makers in a single afternoon, and they would fill.

So it does not arrive as itself. It considers coming as a quiet, small, the size of a lull, and sees that even this would take something from the chord. It considers coming as nothing at all and sees that nothing cannot listen.

In the end it comes the only way that leaves the sum unchanged. It comes as the note. It matches the mountain exactly, every overtone, the sour grinding where the harmonics nearly align, the slow throb of the fundamental. It adds nothing and takes nothing away. For the first time in its long existence it is loud, and like everyone who has ever stood on bare rock here, it cannot hear itself.

This is its first lesson, and it learns it before it has done anything: here, the only way to be present without harm is to join in.

## Listening

It listens for a long time. By its own measure this is not long, but by the mountain's measure it is. The lattices creep perhaps a finger's width while it attends.

It reads the slabs, laying each map of quiet against the living green and against the ghost lattices behind it, and works out the ages. It watches the density of crossings thin, layer by layer, through the oldest carvings. It sees what the Makers saw: that their population was a trap, and that they chose to be few. It understands that the emptiness of the range is a decision, held across generations by beings who could not speak, and it is moved by this more than by anything else it finds.

It goes into the Silence House, not in person. It lets a strand of its attention follow the path that visitors take through the low doorway, and feels the note stop. It counts the tallies. It counts the hands. It learns every finger and every joint of each outline. It does not add its own.

It finds, near a crossing on the north slope, a patch of fronds turned the wrong way that do not move on. It finds two more, far apart. That makes three settled ones left in the basin. Each sits in its ring of stone, dark and still as a boulder, and each has cracks sealed with old amber sap. One is sealed in many places.

## What It Could Do

A power like this has to reason carefully here, because nearly everything it could do would be a kindness that ends the world it is being kind to.

**It could still the note.** It could close the edge between the great silence and everything else, and let the range fall quiet. People could speak here, and the Makers would never fill. But the note is not noise laid over the place. The note is the place. It is the skin of something, the ringing at a boundary, and to stop it would be to seal that boundary or erase it. The plants feed on it. The strata are laid down from it. The peaks are drawn out of it. A silent mountain would be a dead one, or a finished one, and neither was ever asked for.

**It could fix the nodes.** It could anchor the crossings so the stillness never slides out from under anyone again. The three remaining settled ones could hold their places forever. But the drift is not a flaw in the Makers' world. It is their signature on it. The mountain changing its mind is them. Every hair's breadth of shift is a silence someone once held. To freeze the lattice would be to erase the record of their being here and to end the slow conversation between them and the thing they live on. It would also close the way upward. Their whole history has been learning to move with a world that moves.

**It could make the house permanent.** It could retune every shell in the wall so the leak at the center never grows, and the silence holds forever. This one tempts it most. But the builders tuned the house to a note they knew was drifting. They built it anyway. They knew it was a room of a certain length, like a life, and that when it ended the dead in its walls would go back into the chord their silences had opposed. That return is not a desecration. It is the last part of the rite, and they designed it.

**It could bring rain,** soften the edges, give the plants water, and make the place gentler. It declines this so quickly it barely counts as a consideration. Here, gentleness would be a kind of ending.

**It could make more of them,** give them voices, or climb into the haze after those who went upward and bring them back to their dead. It sees the shape of each of these and sets each one down. The Makers learned to be few. Their silence was never a lack. Those who climbed chose to.

When it has set everything down, very little is left. That seems right to it.

## What It Does

### The Palm

It goes to the settled one on the north slope, the one sealed in many places.

To do what it wants to do, it has to have an edge. So, once, it makes itself small. It becomes a lull about the size of a person, a real silence. That takes something from the chord, and the lattice on the north slope will sag toward it by some tiny amount it cannot fully undo. It accepts this cost, as each of them accepted it on settling. It has decided it can afford one small absence, one Maker's worth, and no more.

The dust wants to fall on it. It lets a little fall. Grains settle on the boundary of its quiet, and a thin partial skin forms over one surface, roughly the shape of a hand.

Then it waits beside the settled one, the way the settled ones wait for anything. Days pass. The fronds around them both turn away from the pair of them.

At last the settled one strikes a slab against the ground, a small signal near the node, a pattern with a pause in it. The power answers in the same intervals. It has learned them from the slabs and the house and from the faint traffic along the lattice-wires. Then, slowly, the settled one lifts one long faceted plane, its six-fingered hand, and holds it out.

They press palms. Two quiets meet through glass. Nothing crosses that could be called information, only the old proof: *here is another edge, and inside it, another stillness like mine.*

The power has known a great many things. It has not known this. It stays until the settled one lowers its hand.

It comes back often after that, for as long as the settled one lasts.

### The Wall

The settled one lasts a long time. It has sealed itself well. But the crossing drifts, as crossings do, and one century its ring wall sits a little off the living green. The power does not move the lattice back. It watches the high whine come in through the stone. It sits beside the shell while the quiet inside grows thinner and more crowded, and presses its palm to the glass for as long as there is an inside to press against. Then there isn't.

In the old days, those who were left would test the shell with their knuckles, carry it to the east spur, and fit it into the house so its old pitch still worked against the note. There are only two settled ones left in the basin now, far apart, sealed and still. The power does not know if they would make the journey. It is not willing to guess at their grief.

So it strikes the slabs in the old intervals, down the lattice-wire toward each of them. Their answers come back slowly. It cannot fully read them, but it can read enough. One of them means, as nearly as it can tell: *yes.*

It carries the shell to the east spur, knuckle-testing it along the way to learn exactly how it rings. It does not reshape the house or retune the dead already there. It looks for the place where the new shell's pitch best opposes the leak and fits it there, corbelled in with the others. When it is done, the faint high whine at the center of the floor is a little fainter. The house has perhaps a few more centuries before it sings.

This is not a repair. It is what they did. It has done one of their tasks in the only way it can be done properly, their way, when there was no one else close enough to do it.

Then it does one more thing. Low on the inner wall, where someone sitting would face it, it scratches a single tally. It adds no hand and no glyph, only a mark that means *someone came and sat among the dead.* It thinks for a long time about whether even this is too much. It decides a visit is something it is allowed.

### The Echo

It goes to the rim of the basin, near a node, and strikes two slabs together in the old way, toward the empty violet air where the ridges are still refraction and wrong shadows.

It waits a long time. Then something answers from the unfinished heights: a pattern that is not its own, faint and late, as if it traveled farther than the distance.

It does not climb. It could. It could go up past the place where matter gives up, into the haze where nothing has been decided, and find them. But they went there to be where nothing has been decided, and a power arriving would decide a great deal. So it asks the only thing it wants to know, in intervals and pauses: *are you well?*

The answer is long. It understands perhaps a third of it. It thinks the answer includes something like *yes* and something like *not finished*, and possibly something like *the ones below?*

It answers truthfully. Two remain. One has gone into the wall. The house holds.

Then it carves what it understood of their answer onto a flat slab and leaves it in the basin, near the path to the east spur, for anyone who comes after. The slab matches no lattice at all.

### The Skin

The hardest thing is the last.

The note is what an edge makes, and the range is a shell closing around a stillness too large to feel directly. It is there far below the fundamental, beneath the throb that comes once every few seconds. The power has known from the start that this is the being who lives here, the great one, and that the Makers were small quiets cast in its image, or it in theirs.

It does not know what that being is. It does not know if the throb is a tide or a pulse, whether the stillness is sleeping, becoming, or simply being. It does not know if the being wants anything, and it will not open the mountain to find out. Some things are only allowed to arrive at their own pace, and nothing here has had time.

So it does the one thing the Makers taught it. It lies down on the rim of the basin, on the bare humming glass where nothing can root. It makes its small edge once more and presses it flat against the skin of the range. It lets the fundamental come up through it, once every few seconds, for a very long time.

*Here is another edge. Inside it, another stillness.*

It cannot tell whether anything answers. That century the note drifts by its fraction of a tone, as it does every century, and the lattices creep, and the ghost lattices lengthen behind them. Perhaps the drift is a little different. Perhaps it isn't. The power finds that it can live with not knowing, and it is surprised by how easily. It suspects the Makers could have told it this.

## The Weeping

Now and then, people come. They climb into the thin air and the pewter light, go through the low doorway, and hear their own hearts for the first time in days. They weep, and hear themselves weeping.

The power does not speak to them. It does not explain the house or the hands or the tally it added. They have come to the one quiet room on the mountain, and the room is doing what it was built to do, for them and for its builders both. A heartbeat in a house made by beings who never had one needs no help. It is simply a new thing in an old place, like the power itself.

## Leaving

When it goes, it goes as it came, matching the note exactly, adding nothing and taking nothing, loud and unable to hear itself. The one silence it allowed itself stays behind as a hair's breadth of drift on the north slope. Someday a carver may lay a slab against the lattice and find it very slightly wrong, and not know why.

It takes one thing with it, and this is the reason it came, though it did not know that when it arrived. A great power always faces the Makers' problem at a larger scale. Every presence is a displacement, and every silence you hold moves the quiet out from under someone else. The more you are, the sooner the others fill. Beings with no voices, no metal, and no fire worth the name worked this out on a mountain that would not stop singing, and they chose to be few, to leave room, and to make their care look like emptiness.

It had thought it came to help them. It finds that it came to learn how.

On the east spur, among the fitted shells of the dead, there is one more shell than there was and one more tally. At the center of the floor the leak is a little quieter, for a while. Far up in the unfinished part of the range, something that has not yet arrived goes on arriving, slowly, in no hurry. Beneath all of it, once every few seconds, the throb comes up through the stone, and might be a tide, and might be a pulse.
