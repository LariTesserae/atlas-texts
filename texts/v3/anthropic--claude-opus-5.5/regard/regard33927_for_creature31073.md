# The Attention

## How It Came

It did not come as a light, or a voice, or weather. Any of those would have been an injury here. A light would have stirred the mist. A voice would have been taken. Weather the town already had.

It came the way the Hushed come into a room: sideways, and quiet enough to be hard to see.

It had been attending to the basin for some time before it arrived. For a power like this, attending and arriving are nearly the same thing, and it had learned to keep them apart out of courtesy. It had listened from the rim, from the flats, and from the long bones of the herons wading in the reeds, who felt the drone through their legs the way travelers do. It had noticed what anyone notices first, that the place was beautiful and strange. Then it noticed what fewer notice: that the whole town was bent, over ages, toward something it did not have.

Powers that are benevolent but not yet wise hear a lack and rush to fill it. This one had been that kind once, long ago, and it remembered what came of it. So it waited at the edge of the basin for a whole season of rains before it went in, and it spent that season asking itself one question, over and over, the way the Hushed turn a sentence over between nodes:

*What here is suffering?*

## What It Did Not Do

It is worth saying first what it did not do, because it considered all of these things. Some of them it considered for a long time.

It did not quiet the chord. It could have. It could have made the dome gentle and made speech possible in every gallery, and the travelers who came deafened to the arches would have been spared their deafness. But the chord was not a noise the town made. It was the town. The stems grew to it. The water hung in the gaps it left. A Standing Town made quiet would be a heap of dead lattice on a riverbank, with a few pale frogs dying on the ground, having touched a solid thing for the first time.

It did not give the Hushed names. It could have handed each of them a word, and a history, and a mother, and a remembered arrival. It considered this for a long time. It was not sure the Hushed lacked these things. It was only sure that it would have missed them, in their place.

It did not make the ghost-rooms solid, though it looked into them for many days. They were full of possible galleries and the people walking in them. There was a town where the three tones were four. There was a town where the plaza hung lower and the oldest node had two shapes in it instead of one. It saw that it could choose one of these towns and make it real, and it saw that choosing would end all the others. They were not its to choose between. Their whole nature was to be *might*.

It did not go after the woman with the scar through her eyebrow, though it found her. After the last die-off she was walking in a gallery that had never grown, and she was not lost. She moved with the same flat-footed attention. She paused in a node that did not exist and tilted her head at a sound that was not there, and she was, as far as it could tell, entirely well. Rescuing someone who is not in danger is a kind of theft. It left her where she was.

It did not stop the town from making copies of the people who came. This one it weighed hardest of all. A traveler stands in the shape at the center, hears, and leaves. Months or years later, a stranger with her tilt of the head comes walking out of her footsteps. The power asked whether this was a harm, and to whom. The traveler lost nothing. She went home with an ache for quiet rooms, and the ache was not a wound. It was closer to a gift she did not have a name for. The one who came out of the footsteps did not suffer. She slept in her small silence with water on her eyelashes. The power could find no one who had been wronged. It found only a town doing what towns do, which is keep the people who loved it.

## The Walk

It did one thing it did not have to do. It learned to speak the way the Hushed speak.

This was harder than it expected. A power like this does not usually have to put the most important word first. It is used to being heard all the way to the end. It practiced at the edge of the town, in the small nodes near the low arches, until it could fit a thought into four seconds of stillness and trust the roar to carry the rest.

Then it asked one of the Hushed to walk with it. It asked with the right grammar, and the request came first: *Walk?*

She was old, or looked old, with a hunched back. She walked it along the argument path, where the nodes come close and small, and then, when she understood that its questions were not the arguing kind, along the long upper gallery with only three silences.

In the first silence it asked: *Mind? Any of it. The forgetting. The fading. Being made.*

They walked through the roar for a long time.

In the second silence she said what the Hushed always say: *What would we mind with.*

The power had expected this, and it had prepared an answer. It had been going to say *I could give you something to mind with.* It had been going to offer a self sturdy enough to object. But it had carried her sentence through the roar, the way the Hushed do. Somewhere in that long noise it understood that she had not been complaining about a missing part. She had been describing a shape. She was a listening, not a person with something missing. To give her a grievance would not be healing her. It would be replacing her with someone else, someone the power had made, who would then have a real grievance: that she had been made.

So in the third silence, at the far end of the gallery, it said only: *Understood. If you ever find something to mind with, I will want to hear it.*

She looked at it for the whole length of the node. Then she did something the travelers have never reported. She laughed, very short and very clear, like a frog.

## At the Center

In the end it went to the plaza, as everyone does.

It stood at the edge of the alcove for a long time, looking at the shape of the listener. Rain had fallen the week before, and the shape was full. A figure of clear green water stood trembling against the roar, holding itself together like a hand cupped around a flame. A frog hung in the chest.

The power knew the shape would fit it. The shape fits everyone. The power also knew what would happen afterward. The town would keep it. Somewhere in the chord its footsteps would begin arriving ahead of it. One day someone would follow them around a curve of lattice, and on the far side there would be two walking.

That was the danger, and it was the only real danger the power found in the whole Standing Town. It was not the drone, or the fading, or the copies. It was the power itself.

A Hushed shaped from an ordinary traveler is a small, gentle thing, a listening the size of a person. A Hushed shaped from what this power was would not fit in a node. It would not fit in the galleries. The town would bend toward it the way the stems bend toward resonance, and within a few generations the chord would be retuned around a hearer too large for it. The three tones would become a new chord. The ghost-rooms would collapse into one. The frogs would drift out of their silences because the silences had moved. The town would become, very slowly and with perfect love, an instrument for the power to listen to. It would stop being itself.

So this was the one real choice it made in the Standing Town. It would listen, and it would not be kept.

That cost something. It is not easy to explain what. Powers like this are rarely heard back, and this was the only place in the world built entirely to remember whoever listened. To stand in it and ask not to be remembered was a kind of fasting at a feast laid for you.

It stepped into the shape anyway, carefully, making itself small. It made itself exactly the size of an old woman with a hunched back, and a young man with long arms, and a traveler with a particular tilt of the head. It became, for a little while, only as large as anyone.

The silence closed. There was no heartbeat, no blink.

And it heard the chord.

Afterward it would not say what it sounded like, even to itself. The travelers could not agree. One said a lullaby, one a question, one *being expected*. The power understood why none of them could agree. The chord is not a single message. It is ages of reaching, and it sounds like whatever the listener is. What the power heard was simple enough, and it will not be written down here, because it was not meant for anyone else.

It did not stay long. When it stepped out, it did something no listener had done before. It went back along its own path and gathered up its footsteps. It did not erase them from the chord, which would have been a wound. It set each one down inside a node, where sound stays where it is put. There are, now, a few small silences in the Standing Town that hold footsteps no one will ever follow. The frogs in those pools sometimes turn toward a sound no one else can hear, and then turn away. Nothing comes walking out of them. They are only kept.

## The Promise

It considered one more intervention and declined it. This was the largest.

Nothing lasts, and the Standing Town will not either. The river is patient and the flats are always shifting. One day the humidity will fail, or a cold century will come, or the singers will sicken in their tubes. The chord will thin. The stems will starve out of resonance and fall in slow pieces. The water will slip out of the nodes as they close, and it will fall at last, for the first time, onto the floor of the dome. The frogs will fall with it.

The power could prevent this. It could hold the river in its bed for ten thousand years. It could keep the air warm and the singers well and the chord held forever, like the water in the silences.

It did not. A town preserved forever by someone else's hand is no longer a town that holds its own shape. It is a thing on a shelf. The Standing Town's whole nature is that nobody made it. The power would not be the first to.

But it did make one promise. It made it in the old central node, the largest, where you can speak as long as you like. There it did what no one does there: it spoke only a little.

Everything in the Standing Town grows toward what is missing. For all its ages, what it has been missing is a listener. The Hushed hear it. Travelers hear it. But the Hushed are part of the chord, and when the chord ends, they end too. When the last singer falls quiet in the last stem, there will be no one left in the dome to hear it stop. The song began with no listener. Left alone, it will end with none.

So the power promised to come back at the end.

It will not stop anything or save anything. It will come to stand in the basin as the chord thins. It will stand in whatever shape is left at the center, or on the floor where the shape used to be. It will hear the whole of it: the last swifts, the last water falling, the last three tones becoming two and then one and then the ordinary wind off the river. It will listen so that the final sound of the Standing Town is not a song in an empty room.

It said the promise with the most important word first, the way the Hushed taught it, in case the node flickered:

*Heard. You were heard. You will be heard to the end.*

## Leaving

It left through the low arches, and the drone followed it out across the flats, as the drone follows everyone.

For some time afterward, the ordinary world seemed very quiet to it. That surprised the power. It had not thought anything could still do that to it.

It found itself pausing sometimes, in the great ordinary rooms of the world: in the space between two mountains, in the hush before a storm, in the stillness of a sleeping child. It pauses there half-listening for the place where the sound cancels, half expecting to find water floating.

Now and then it does.
