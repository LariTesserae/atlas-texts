# The One Who Came and Sat Down

**How it arrived**

It watched for a long time before it came. It wanted to understand the place first, because it could see that anything it brought would be an event, and on the Made Plain events are enormous. A breath of wind would be the first wind. A cloud would be the first cloud, and its shadow would move, and a moving shadow would teach the clay lizards a fear they had never been given. If it came as light, there would be two suns. If it came as evening, the afternoon would end.

So it came small, on foot, from the same direction you came. It let its steps be heavy enough to leave marks.

The first thing it did was stop at the first basin and look for a long while at your heel-print, with the Leavings sitting in it. One crumb-Leaving was down in the deepest part, where your weight had pressed hardest. The power looked at that, and understood a good deal, and did not yet do anything at all.

---

**What it did not do**

It is worth setting down the things it could have done, because it considered each of them honestly, and the refusals were most of the work.

*It did not sweep.* It could feel the pull of the place. A finished thing leans toward tidiness the way water leans downhill. Every flake at the foot of every ridge seemed to ask to be gathered up, and every crumb seemed to want brushing into a palm and dropping somewhere out of sight. The power noticed that it felt this pull too, a little, as anything that cares about work feels it. It set the feeling down. The Leavings are what a sweeping would take.

*It did not bring the arm down.* It was not its arm.

*It did not make the Leavings whole.* This would have been easy: fuse flake to flake, knit the fibre-ends into proper cord, press the crumbs into a clean shape with a clean thumbprint. But a Leaving made whole becomes a made thing. It would turn its back on the rim with the frogs and the lizards, it would stop accumulating, and it would be nineteen forever. Their incompleteness is not damage. It is the room in them where things can still happen.

*It did not fill the dry basin.* A longing is not a lack to be corrected. The Leavings face that basin the way you might face a door you haven't opened yet. Pouring water into it would be answering a question for someone who was still enjoying asking it.

*It did not mend the dragonfly.* The dragonfly flies. The missing wing is understood. Mending it would be telling a whole congregation that their saint had been a mistake.

*It did not give the water back its voice.* The Leavings found that sound lying unswept and built it a jar. It is theirs now.

*It did not give the lizards eyelids.* It considered this one longest of all. It could see their fear plainly: the cool bodies flat under the leaves, the unblinking eyes, the crumb-Leavings drifting closer through the shadow. Fear was the one thing on the plain that seemed simply wrong. But eyelids would be the power's hand inside the Maker's work, a second signature on someone else's letter. It decided it would answer the fear another way, if it could.

---

**The walking**

So it walked.

It walked in the shadows under the jars, where the lizards lie, slowly and back and forth, the way someone paces a room while thinking. It did not clean its feet. The wet red-brown clay gathered on its soles and fell off in crescents and crumbs, a heel-shaped curl here, a toe-flake there. It had to learn a difficult thing to do this. It is hard for a careful being to be careless. It could not *place* crumbs. Placed crumbs would be made things, and the Leavings would not know what to do with them. So it could only walk, and let what fell fall, and not look down to see where.

The crumb-Leavings found the crumbs. They found them the way they find everything, drifting, uncertain, in slow trails, and they took them and kept them, and grew.

It did not stop the Leavings from taking lizards. It did not police anything. But afterward the hollows came less often. Under the fourth jar from the ribbon grove, a lizard lay flat against the underside of its leaf for a very long time, and nothing came, and nothing came, and something in the set of its pinched clay shoulders was a little different than before.

---

**The dry basin**

It went and sat at the lip of the dry basin among the Leavings, with its knees drawn up, facing inward as they did.

The fibre-Leavings snagged on it at once, because they can't help it. After a while it was wearing three of them on its shins like burrs, one with a husk beetle tangled in it. The flake-Leavings kept their candle-distance. When one of them misjudged and came close, there was a thin glass *tik*, and it drew back, mortified. The power bowed its head slightly toward it, the way you acknowledge someone who has apologized for something that didn't need an apology.

It listened for whatever they were listening to. It heard nothing.
