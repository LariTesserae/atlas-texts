# Stand Down

## Notice

It did not find the seam by looking. Nothing finds the seam by looking.

It noticed the way such powers notice things. There was a small wrongness in the world's accounts, a place where something was being *kept* that no one had asked to be kept. Most of what persists in the world persists because it is still wanted, or because nothing has got round to ending it. This was neither. This was deliberate. Something under a yard in a town that had stopped being a works town forty years ago was holding a place open, and had been for a long time, against every reasonable expectation of iron and heat.

The power was very large. The seam was the width of a forearm. You would have to fall in, or be small.

So it made itself small.

## What It Did Not Do

It came down through the gap on the midday light, because that was the one road into the room that nothing was standing on. Then it stayed very still and did nothing, for a long time.

This is the first thing to understand about a benevolent power that is also wise. The benevolence arrives first and wants to help. The wisdom arrives a moment later and asks what help would cost. In the seam, the wisdom had a great deal to say.

It could have widened the room. The engineer was cramped in its thimble of water, and the power could have given it a basin, a cistern, a lake. But the room grows a hair a season because that is the rate at which the works can fill it. Space arriving faster than the works could hire for it would not be a gift. It would be a vacancy, and this was a place that took vacancies very seriously.

It could have made it rain properly. The rain was never quite enough. But the scarcity *was* the clock. Every creature in the room moved toward a drop because the drops were few. Make the water plentiful and the ten thousand would have no reason to rise together, and a works that does not rise together is not a works. It is just a damp hole full of woodlice.

It could have explained the pipe. It did, in fact, follow the pipe, which no one else had ever been able to do. It went east under the town, under the terraces where the men had lived, and on to the end. What it found there it has not told anyone, and neither will I. It is enough to say that the heat is honestly come by, and that no one is being burned for it. That was the one thing the power needed to know before it could leave the rest alone, and once it knew, it did.

It could have freed the toad.

It thought longest about the toad, because the toad was the one thing in the room that had not been hired so much as trapped. It had fallen in, it was too large to leave, and it would be replaced when it wore out. The power sat beside it through many cycles and paid attention the way it pays attention, which is completely. It found that the toad was, as far as a toad can be anything, well. It was warm. It was fed without having to hunt. When the iron ticked, its eyes went half-shut, and the power had taken that for resignation. Looked at closely, it was nearer to the feeling of a man who has worked the same job for thirty years and hears the kettle go on. The toad was being used. It was also being *kept*. The power has met a great many creatures that would have traded everything for warmth, food, and a place where they were needed. It left the toad on its ledge.

It did not appear to anyone. It did not speak to the engineer, which had no ears and would not have known what to do with being spoken to. It did not bless anything. It did not improve the gears.

What it did, for a long while, was count.
