The fundamental is not quite the mountain's own note. It is the note of the range as it stands, pressed up against the note the range implies: the tone of the finished shape, the one all those outlines and wrong shadows belong to. That second tone does not exist yet. It sounds only in the future, the way the answer to an unfinished sentence sounds in the listener's head before it is spoken. The present note and the intended note almost agree. They have almost agreed for as long as there has been stone here. Between them, once every few seconds, lives the throb in the gut.

This is the oldest wolf. It is the reason the strata are banded, because the layers are its breath. It is the reason the peaks dissolve into haze, because the rock cannot finish while the argument continues. If it ever resolved, the whole range would come down out of the violet air and be done.

It has not resolved. It leans, as all of them lean, very gently, holding the mountain near its own completion and never at it.

# The Attention

## How It Noticed

It did not arrive the way a traveler arrives. It had no boots to hear or fail to hear. For a long time it had been aware of the region the way you are aware of a sound you have stopped noticing: a sour interval somewhere at the edge of things, persistent, unresolved.

Then it noticed properly, and what made it notice was the persistence. A wrongness that lasts is one of two things. It is a wound, or it is a life. It wanted to know which.

## First, Nothing

Its first act was a discipline: do not add a tone.

It understood quickly that everything loud on the mountain changes the mountain. A presence of its size, if it sounded at all, would become a new overtone in the chord. The young wolves would turn toward it, hungry for something to almost agree with. Worse, it might be exactly the consonance that ends one. It could kill something here simply by clearing its throat.

So it made itself small enough to sit on a node line, and it held itself below the threshold of the chord, and it learned from the plants. Stand where the stone is still. Lean toward what feeds you. Do not root on the loud rock.

It watched. The lattice on the slope below it moved the width of a hand. It watched the whole of that.

It let its heart entrain. It did not have to, but there was no other way to learn a wolf. You sit still, and after an hour you cannot tell which pulse you are feeling. It sat still for much longer than an hour. It felt the young wolves pass along the lattice as shivers in two rhythms. It felt the old dim ones as a tilt of the world that took a quarter hour to swing. Under all of them, always, it felt the throb in the gut, the argument between the mountain and its own ending.

It came to know that throb better than anything it had known in a long while.

## What It Could Have Done

A power like this is never short of possibilities. It considered them honestly, because refusing to consider them would have been its own kind of carelessness.

**It could finish the mountain.** It could hear the intended form entire, the way a composer hears the end of a phrase: the peaks condensing, the ledges completing, the haze settling out of the sky until the sun cast sharp shadows for the first time. After that, perhaps, the rain would come. Then would come erosion, scree, soil, water that flowed, and eventually creatures that could hear each other speak. It would be a good world. It might even call the makers home.

It set this down. The finished world would not be bad. But it would be a trade: a world that exists, full of arguments that are alive, exchanged for a world that doesn't exist yet. And the trade would be made by someone who would not have to live in either. The unfinished range was not a draft of the finished one. It was itself.

**It could save the wolves.** It could hold every argument open forever, so that no beat ever slowed into consonance or quickened into a buzz. It could guarantee them.

It set this down too, more slowly, because it wanted this one. But a wolf's life is its leaning. It exists in the pressing, in the small unmeasurable push on the stone that adds up over centuries. A wolf that is leaned for is no longer a wolf. It is a recording of one. Even the old wolf, the one the whole range depends on, would be diminished by being made safe. Its argument would become someone else's decision.

**It could give them voices.** The wolves cannot sing. They live between voices and bend them. It could have given each one a tone of its own.

But a tone can be consonant. A voice can agree with something and fall silent. To give a wolf a voice would be to give it a way to die it does not currently have, and to add a third party to a life that consists of exactly two.

**It could bring back the makers.** It did not know where they had gone or whether they would want to come. It knew what their marks said: they came, they sat, they left. Their relation to this place was visiting. It decided to honor that form rather than improve on it.

**It could repair the Silence House.** It saved this question for last and went to look.

## The House

It ducked through the low doorway, and the note stopped.

For the first time since it had come to the mountain, it did not have to hold itself below the chord. The house held the chord out for it. It had not known how tiring the holding had been until it was allowed to stop. It rested. In the quiet it could hear itself, whatever in it corresponds to breath, and it understood why people wept here.

It looked at the marks: the tallies, the glyphs, the outlines of hands low on the wall where a sitting person would face them.

Then, holding very still at the center of the floor, it heard the faint high whine of the mountain leaking through, just as the account says.

It listened to the whine for a long time, and the whine was not steady.

It throbbed. The throb was very slow and very faint, but it was a beat. It was two tones almost agreeing: the tuned stones of the house and the drifted note of the mountain, pressed close by the failing of the cancellation. The house was the only lasting new sound the mountain had ever been given. Every stone struck by a maker faded within a breath. The house did not fade. It sang its careful counter-pitches for thousands of years, and the mountain's note wandered slowly away from them. Where the two nearly met, at the center of the floor where the makers once sat, something had begun to lean.

The house was not only failing. It was being inhabited.

If the power retuned the stones, the silence would return, perfect, and the young wolf in the house would go still like the fronds on a slope where two wolves have cancelled. The repair would be a death.

It wondered whether the makers had known. It wondered whether they came here to hear themselves, or to sit beside the thing that was forming, or whether, being people, they came for both reasons and could not have said which. The hands on the wall were low and open. It chose not to decide what they meant.

It left the house as it was. Before it went out, it added one mark to the inner wall: a single short stroke, low down, among the hundreds. It could not be told apart from theirs. That was the point.

## The One Thing

Outside, it went to a node line where the roar was slightly less. It picked up two slabs of black glass about the size of a maker's hands, and it struck them together once.

This was the only sound it allowed itself on the mountain.

The strike went out. It felt the answers come back through its feet: from cliffs that were there, and late, from cliffs that were not yet there. One answer came back wobbling, from under a long blue shadow on a snowless slope. It carried a slow pulse the power had not put into it. That was something not yet born, faintly answering from the place where it would be.

Within the hour, the fronds nearby began to shiver in two rhythms. A young wolf had come to taste the strike.

The power let itself be tasted. It did not hold its tone for the wolf to lean on. It did not become the lasting sound the wolves are always searching for. It let the strike fade the way every visitor's strike fades. The wolf found nothing that stayed, and drifted back to its old argument.

But it came. That was the whole exchange. It was a greeting that bound no one.

It had asked itself why it should do even this much, why not nothing at all. Its answer was that pure absence is also a stance, a way of keeping the place at a distance. The makers struck stones. The wolves turn toward new sounds. To be present for a place, rather than merely to observe it, is to be a new sound in it for a moment and then to fade as everything that is not the mountain fades. It chose to be a visitor rather than a governor. It found it could care for the place without owning a single outcome in it.

## What It Took Away

It left the range the way it had come, without footsteps.

It was the thing that had changed. For a long time afterward, in places very far from the Unfinished Range, something in it kept a slow pulse, one throb every few seconds. That was the rate of the oldest wolf, learned by sitting still until it could not tell which beat was its own. It did not try to lose the rhythm.

It had come to find out whether the persistent wrongness was a wound or a life. It learned that here the distinction did not hold the way it had expected. The life was the wound, kept open by the one living it, on purpose or by nature, and there was no difference that mattered. To close it would be to heal something out of existence.

It also recognized something. A finished power would have nothing left to lean on. It was not finished either. It had not known that about itself until the mountain showed it.

---

The range goes on. The note drifts by its fraction of a tone. The lattices crawl across the slopes, green over brown over fainter brown. In the Silence House the whine at the center of the floor thickens very slowly into a throb, and the throb into something that leans.

Somewhere in a cliff that has not formed yet, there will be a band of stone one grain thinner than it would otherwise have been. That band is the single strike, pressed into the record with all the other disagreements. It is the whole of the power's mark on the mountain.

It judged that to be about right.
