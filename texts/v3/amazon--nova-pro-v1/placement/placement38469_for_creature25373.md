### Where You Find Yourself

As you awaken, you float gently in a chamber bathed in soft, bluish light. The walls are covered in intricate carvings and bioluminescent algae that pulse rhythmically, casting shifting patterns across the room. You realize you’re inside one of the residential coral-stone fusion dwellings, integrated seamlessly into the towering coral spires. Above you, the domed ceiling shows a faint glow, a hint of the outside world that you’ll soon explore.

The water around you feels buoyant, and you discover that you can breathe it with ease, a testament to the unique environment of Altheris. As your senses adjust, you become aware of the subtle hum of life around you—the whispers of the currents, the creak of coral, and the distant song of marine fauna.

### Who or What You Are

You are an Aspirant, a rare and unique individual who has been drawn to Altheris from the surface world. Unlike the Seakin, you are human, yet something about this submerged metropolis calls to you, urging you to learn and contribute to its ancient wisdom. Your arrival is seen as serendipitous by the denizens, a bridge between the surface and the depths.

#### Abilities and Role

While you share many attributes with humans from the surface world, your time in Altheris has granted you certain unique abilities:

1. **Hydrokinesis**: You have a nascent ability to manipulate water, allowing you to create currents, form barriers, or even respirate through it more efficiently. This gift, though still developing, marks you as someone integral to the city's balance.

2. **Adaptive Resilience**: Your body has begun to adapt to the underwater environment. You can hold your breath for extended periods, and your skin has a faintly iridescent sheen, helping you blend into the aquatic surroundings.

3. **Communicator**: You possess a natural talent for understanding and interpreting the bioluminescent signals of the Luminora and the whispered currents. This ability makes you a vital link between different species and even the spiritual entities of Altheris.

#### Objectives

Your role in Altheris is multifaceted. As an Aspirant, you are both a learner and a protector. Your objectives include:

1. **Learning the Ways of Altheris**: You are tasked with understanding the ancient traditions, engineering marvels, and ecological balance that sustain the city. The Seakin elders see you as a bridge to preserve their knowledge and perhaps share it with the surface world.

2. **Contributing to the City**: Each generation is expected to contribute to Altheris, and your unique abilities make you well-suited for roles in engineering, ecological maintenance, or even spiritual guidance. 

3. **Navigating Relationships**: Building trust and symbiosis with the various denizens—Seakin, Luminora, Coral Guardians—is crucial. Your role often involves mediating conflicts, fostering collaboration, and ensuring the city’s harmonious balance.

### Immediate Surroundings

You push gently against the water, propelling yourself toward the arched entrance of your chamber. The corridor outside is a marvel of bio-alloy and coral, glowing softly with embedded algae. As you navigate through the winding pathways, you’re greeted by the bustling life of Altheris.

1. **The Market Plaza**: Floating above you, a vibrant marketplace where Dolphin Artisans trade their shell jewelry and the Seakin barter resources. The air is filled with the chatter of trade and the rustle of kelp.

2. **The Great Hall**: A colossal, domed chamber where the city’s council gathers. Intricate carvings depicting Altheris’s history line the walls, illuminated by hanging clusters of luminous jellyfish.

3. **Coral Gardens**: Terraced spaces where kelp and giant sea grasses grow, tended by both Seakin and Coral Guardians. The gardens are a living testament to the city’s sustainable practices.

4. **The Luminary**: A sacred space where the Luminora congregate, their bioluminescent forms creating a dazzling display. This is where spiritual ceremonies often take place, guided by the whispers of ancient waters.

### First Encounter

As you emerge from your dwelling, a Seakin approaches you, their iridescent skin shimmering in the ambient light. 

"Welcome, Aspirant," they say, their voice a melodious blend of spoken words and bioluminescent signals. "We have much to share, and you, much to contribute. Shall we begin?"

And thus, your journey in the ancient, submerged metropolis of Altheris commences, a place where every breath you take is a part of a grander, more interconnected world.
