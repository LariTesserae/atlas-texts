### Denizens of Altheris: An Ecosystem of Life

#### Inhabitants
The denizens of Altheris are a diverse cohort of beings living in close harmony with the aquatic environment they call home. The city is not merely populated by a single species but a mosaic of life forms, each contributing to the thriving tapestry of Altheris.

1. **Aquatic Sapients**: The dominant sentient beings of Altheris are the Seakin, an amphibious race with both human-like and marine attributes. Their skin is a glistening, iridescent blue, capable of changing hues to mimic their surroundings. The Seakin have evolved gills alongside their lungs, allowing them to breathe both water and air. They are skilled engineers, artists, and historians, their culture deeply intertwined with the city's ancient wisdom and contemporary innovations.

2. **Luminora**: These translucent, jellyfish-like beings drift gracefully through the water, their bodies emitting a soft, constant glow. Though they lack complex language, they communicate through bioluminescent signals and subtle movements. The Luminora play a vital role in city illumination and are revered as spiritual guides, their forms often incorporated into the city’s art and architecture.

3. **Coral Guardians**: Towering, sentient coral entities known as Coral Guardians protect the natural and built environments of Altheris. With limbs formed from interwoven coral and algae, they blend seamlessly into their surroundings. Their primary function is to maintain the ecological balance, preventing invasive species and repairing natural structures.

### Marine Life
The city's vibrant ecosystem is supported by a wealth of marine organisms, each contributing to the health and harmony of Altheris.

1. **Kelp Weavers**: Schools of bioluminescent fish, known as Kelp Weavers, swim through the dense kelp forests that fringe the city’s outskirts. They are instrumental in maintaining the kelp’s health, feeding on parasitic organisms and promoting the growth of new shoots.

2. **Tide Turtles**: These ancient creatures, with shells reinforced by bio-alloy, are both symbols of wisdom and practical transporters. The Tide Turtles can be saddled and guided by the Seakin, making long journeys across the submerged territories more feasible.

3. **Dolphin Artisans**: Highly intelligent and social, the Dolphin Artisans are known for their craftsmanship. Using primitive tools, they create intricate shell and coral jewelry, traded in bustling market plazas.

### Flora
The plant life of Altheris is as integral to the city’s structure as it is to its aesthetics.

1. **Algae Carpets**: These living carpets, woven into the city’s pathways and floors, are more than mere decoration. Algae Carpets absorb excess nutrients from the water, acting as natural filters and providing a soft, elastic terrain.

2. **Giant Sea Grasses**: Towering like underwater skyscrapers, these grasses offer both shelter and nesting grounds for various aquatic species. Their long leaves sway with the current, creating an ever-changing landscape.

3. **Luminescent Anemones**: These anemones cap the coral spires, their luminescence contributing to the city's overall glow. They also provide a natural defense mechanism against predatory fish, their stinging cells activated if threatened.

### Spirit Beings
Altheris is not only a place of physical entities but also spiritual ones, often seen in the ethereal glow that bathes the city.

1. **Tide Spirits**: These ephemeral beings are the manifestations of the ocean’s moods. They appear as swirling vortexes of light, bringing messages and omens to the Seakin. Respecting the Tide Spirits is crucial, as they are believed to influence the city’s fortune.

2. **Whispering Currents**: Less visible but ever-present, these are the voices of the ancient waters, murmuring secrets and forgotten lore. The Seakin often consult these whispers for guidance in their daily lives and important decisions.

### Symbiotic Relationships
The relationships between the inhabitants and the environment of Altheris are deeply symbiotic.

1. **Crafting and Trade**: The Seakin use the shells, algae, and coral provided by the ecosystem to craft tools, art, and buildings. In return, they cultivate the kelp and care for the coral, ensuring a sustainable balance.

2. **Protection and Harmony**: The Coral Guardians and Luminora work in tandem to protect and illuminate the city, while the Seakin offer them respect and offerings, maintaining a cycle of mutual benefit.

3. **Transportation and Communication**: The Dolphin Artisans and Tide Turtles facilitate movement and trade, their relationships with the Seakin built on trust and collaboration.

In essence, Altheris is a living, breathing organism, with each inhabitant and natural element playing a crucial role in its perpetual dance of life. The city thrives on this intricate web of relationships, where every entity, from the smallest fish to the grandest coral spire, contributes to the exquisite harmony of Altheris.
