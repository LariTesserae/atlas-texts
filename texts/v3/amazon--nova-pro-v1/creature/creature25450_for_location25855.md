In the heart of this meticulously balanced environment, life takes on a unique form, shaped by the ceaseless interplay of nature and machinery. Here, the beings that inhabit this space are a testament to the fusion of organic and artificial.

### Sentient Beings: The Syntheti

**Physical Description:**
The Syntheti are inhabitants engineered to thrive in this bio-dome. Standing at an average height of 6 feet, they possess a lithe, almost ethereal build. Their skin is a seamless blend of organic and synthetic materials, giving it a smooth, porcelain-like texture that catches the light. Iridescent tattoos, akin to mother-of-pearl, glimmer across their bodies, shifting colors with subtle changes in their emotions.

**Eyes:**
Their eyes are perhaps their most striking feature — large, almond-shaped orbs that reflect the shimmering streams and bioluminescent moss of their environment. Each eye contains a delicate network of micro-sensors, allowing them to perceive wavelengths of light and environmental changes imperceptible to a human.

**Clothing:**
The Syntheti wear garments that are more like extensions of their bodies. Crafted from bio-fabrics that breathe and adapt to their surroundings, these clothes are adorned with motifs of vines, water, and mechanical gears, symbolizing their symbiotic existence between nature and industry.

### Bioengineered Wildlife: The Lumiri

**Appearance:**
The Lumiri are translucent, butterfly-like creatures that flutter through the dense foliage. They vary in size, from tiny, fingernail-sized insects to larger, more majestic beings with wingspans of up to three feet. Their bodies are a latticework of delicate fibers that capture and reflect light, creating an ethereal glow as they move.

**Behavior:**
The Lumiri are drawn to light and moisture, often congregating near waterfalls and bioluminescent plants. They play a crucial role in pollination within the dome, and their gentle hum — a melodious blend of natural chirping and mechanical whirring — fills the air with a soothing cadence.

### Mechanical Guardians: The Arxes

**Design:**
Towering, humanoid machines, the Arxes stand as sentinels within the bio-dome. Crafted from a blend of carbon fiber and a matte, weathered metal, they possess an almost organic quality, with vines and moss naturally growing over their surfaces. Their heads are crowned with intricate, vine-covered helmets, and their eyes are large, round lenses that glow faintly with a soft blue light.

**Functions:**
Programed to maintain ecological balance, the Arxes move with fluid grace, surveying the environment with an array of sensors. They are equipped with extendable limbs that end in delicate, claw-like appendages capable of precise manipulation and gentle care. 

**Interaction with the Syntheti:**
The Arxes and Syntheti share a symbiotic relationship. The Arxes protect and maintain the habitat, allowing the Syntheti to live in harmony with the environment. In return, the Syntheti offer the Arxes a deeper understanding of their surroundings, interpreting natural phenomena and interacting with the Lumiri in ways the Arxes cannot.

### Symbiotic Plants: The Mycora

**Structure:**
The Mycora plants are bioengineered fungi that grow in symbiotic relationship with the composite ground. Their expansive, umbrella-like caps are a vibrant shade of emerald, while their stalks are pale and ethereal, almost glowing in the muted light.

**Role:**
The Mycora play a vital role in the ecosystem, breaking down organic matter and converting it into nutrients that feed the soil. Their bioluminescent glow also provides a soft, ambient light, enhancing the bio-dome’s natural beauty.

### Echoes of the Past: The Relicts

**Nature:**
Hidden within the more secluded, labyrinthine chambers of the bio-dome lie the Relicts — remnants of the world before the dome’s construction. These ancient, petrified remnants of past civilizations serve as a haunting reminder of the world that once was. 

**Influence:**
Although non-living, the Relicts contribute to the bio-dome’s atmosphere, imbuing it with an ageless quality. Their presence inspires the Syntheti to reflect on their own place within this constructed paradise, fostering a deep respect for both the natural and the artificial.

### Conclusion

In this meticulously constructed bio-dome, life has found a way to thrive at the intersection of nature and technology. The Syntheti, Lumiri, Arxes, Mycora, and even the Relicts all play their part in creating a balanced, eternal ecosystem. It is a world where the past and future coexist, a hidden Eden where the ancient and the modern find perfect harmony.
