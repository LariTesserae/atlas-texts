In such a severe and sublime environment where life as we typically understand it struggles to take root, the inhabitants are as unique as the land they call home. Though the place is devoid of plant life and most fauna, a few extraordinary beings do claim this frozen crest as their domain.

### Extremophiles: The Invisible Denizens
Hidden within the cracks of frost-shattered rock and in the pools of ephemeral water that fleetingly collect in sheltered valleys, extremophiles—microbial life forms—dwell. These tenacious microorganisms have adapted to the extreme cold, high radiation, and scarce nutrients. 

1. **Psychrophiles:** These cold-loving bacteria thrive in temperatures that would freeze most other life. They produce antifreeze proteins to prevent ice crystal formation within their cells. 
2. **Radioresistant Microbes:** Exposed to high levels of ultraviolet radiation due to the thin atmosphere, these microbes have evolved DNA repair mechanisms that allow them to survive and even flourish under such conditions. 
3. **Chemotrophs:** In the absence of sunlight for photosynthesis, these microbes derive energy from chemical reactions. They may metabolize sulfur compounds or other minerals found within the rock, creating a chemosynthetic ecosystem similar to those found at deep-sea hydrothermal vents.

### Geological Entities: The Slow Movers
The landscape itself can be seen as a living entity, shaped by slow but constant processes.

1. **Cryoclasts:** Jagged, frost-sculpted boulders that move incrementally with the cycles of freeze and thaw. Though inanimate, they play a vital role in the erosional dance, grinding against each other and wearing down the terrain.
2. **Glacial Relics:** Remains of ancient glaciers that once dominated the landscape. Though mostly retreated, their slow movement continues to shape the terrain, depositing moraines and polishing rock surfaces.

### Spectral Guardians: The Lost Structures
Rudimentary remnants of a bygone era dot the landscape, standing as sentinels of a forgotten past.

1. **Standing Stones:** Monoliths, partially buried in the regolith, whisper tales of a civilization that once attempted to tame this austere land. Carved with rudimentary symbols and tools, these stones serve as a haunting testament to human endeavor and the inexorable power of nature.
2. **Ruins of Shelters:** Crumbling walls and foundations hint at shelters built by early inhabitants. These structures, now reclaimed by the elements, speak of a time when survival depended on harnessing the land’s scant resources.

### Elemental Spirits: The Unseen Forces
Though not corporeal, the elements themselves can be seen as the true spirits of this place.

1. **Wind Sprites:** Embodiments of the relentless winds that carve the landscape, these are not living beings but personifications of the air’s constant motion. They sculpt rock and carry frost, perpetuating the land’s evolution.
2. **Frost Wraiths:** Apparitions of cold and ice, these are the glacial forces that creep through cracks and crevices, slowly but surely altering the stone. They represent the land’s silent, insistent march toward change.

### Conclusion
This frigid crest, with its austere beauty and severe conditions, is a place where traditional life scarcely exists. Yet, it is far from lifeless. Extremophiles persist in microscopic realms, geological processes shape the land with patient fury, and the remnants of past civilizations stand as silent witnesses. The elements themselves—wind, ice, and stone—are the true rulers here, orchestrating a symphony of erosion and transformation that continues unabated, a testament to nature’s raw, unyielding power.
