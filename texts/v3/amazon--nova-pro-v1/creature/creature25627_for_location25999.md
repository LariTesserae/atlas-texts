In this peculiar, almost paradoxical landscape where industry and marsh meet, life has adapted in fascinating ways, pushing against traditional notions of ecology.

### Flora
1. **Marsh Ferns**: Towering, resilient ferns dominate the marshy areas. Their fronds are broad and glossy, adapted to capture as much diffused light as possible under the frequent overcast skies. 

2. **Copper Moss**: A unique species of moss that thrives on the corrugated metal surfaces. It has a metallic sheen, almost as if it feeds on the minerals leaching from the metal. 

3. **Ironwood Shrubs**: Hardy, gnarled shrubs whose wood is unusually dense and rigid. The bark is dark and rough, providing both a stark contrast and a symbiotic relationship with the vines that creep along its surface.

4. **Lichen Carpets**: Thick mats of lichen blanket the cobblestone streets and the ground near the buildings. They are particularly adept at absorbing moisture from the air, making the ground perpetually damp.

### Fauna
Though initially described as absent, closer observation reveals:

1. **Muddy Skimmers**: Small, amphibious creatures resembling a cross between a frog and a lizard. They have semi-translucent skin that allows them to blend into the marshy environment and propel themselves through both water and mud with remarkable agility.

2. **Rust Beetles**: Tiny, metallic insects that feed on the Copper Moss. They have an iridescent sheen and seem to be attracted to the old machinery, often seen crawling over rusted surfaces.

3. **Whispering Bats**: Nocturnal bats that navigate the twilight air. Their echolocation is so fine-tuned that they can hunt the Muddy Skimmers with ease. They derive their name from the soft, whispering sounds their wings make as they fly.

### Micro-organisms
1. **Mist Spores**: Fungal spores that float in the air, particularly dense in the misty pockets created by the marsh. They feed on the decaying plant matter and the moss, creating an almost invisible, yet ubiquitous presence.

2. **Steam Dwellers**: Tiny, heat-resistant microbes that thrive near the steam vents. They form mats of slimy, orange-red colonies that break down old machinery and absorb minerals, contributing to the rusting process.

### Sentient Beings
The settlement is notably absent of human or humanoid inhabitants, but an enigmatic presence lingers:

1. **The Shadow Engineers**: Mysterious figures who maintain the complex network of steam engines and generators. They move with a fluidity that suggests a deep understanding of both the marsh and the machinery. Shrouded in cloaks that blend with the dim, diffused light, they are rarely seen but their influence is felt in the carefully maintained balance of the ecosystem.

### Symbiotic Relationships
- **Vines and Ironwood**: The vines that climb the Ironwood Shrubs derive nutrients from the dense wood while providing a living scaffold that allows other plants to grow higher.
  
- **Copper Moss and Rust Beetles**: The beetles not only feed on the moss but also help in its propagation by carrying spores on their bodies.

- **Muddy Skimmers and Whispering Bats**: The skimmer’s amphibious lifestyle makes it an ideal food source for the bats, establishing a predator-prey relationship that maintains population balance.

### Conclusion
In this unique ecosystem, life has adapted to the strange interplay between industry and nature. Flora and fauna alike have evolved to thrive in the moist, dim environment, creating a complex web of relationships that sustain the delicate balance. The enigmatic Shadow Engineers serve as the silent stewards of this world, ensuring that the equilibrium between the ancient marsh and the industrious town remains unbroken.
