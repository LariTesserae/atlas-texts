In this raw and primordial environment, life finds a way to flourish in its own unique forms, driven by the harsh yet consistent conditions of the ever-forming landscape.

**Insectile Denizens:**
The most numerous inhabitants are a myriad of insectile creatures, each uniquely adapted to the ever-damp, sterile, and thermally intense conditions. 

1. **Glacialis Skimmers:** Tiny, translucent insects with elongated bodies, their pale forms shimmer as they glide just above the surfaces of the pools. They feed on microscopic organisms that thrive in the nutrient-rich water. Their delicate wings are semi-transparent, allowing them to blend into the shimmering waters beneath them when they dip to drink.

2. **Rock Hoppers:** Stout and robust insects with powerful hind legs, they've adapted to leap from rock to rock. Their bodies are covered in a rugged, chitinous armor that provides protection against both the rough terrain and the occasional predator. They scavenge for detritus and are constantly on the move, their movements frenetic and erratic.

3. **Luminous Crawlers:** Glowing faintly with bioluminescence, these small creatures navigate the dimly lit underbellies of the crude stone dwellings. Their glow helps them find food—bits of organic matter trapped in the moisture-laden air—and also serves to deter potential threats.

**Reptilian Life:**
Amidst the bustling insects are several reptilian species, each claiming their niche in this complex ecosystem.

1. **Shard Scales:** Small, agile lizards with scales that mimic the fractured appearance of volcanic glass. They dart through the chaotic urban sprawl with astounding speed, their scales shifting subtly to blend into their surroundings. They hunt the insectile creatures, their long tongues flicking out to snatch prey.

2. **Geyser Gliders:** Larger, more formidable reptiles that have adapted to the omnipresent moisture. They are equipped with specialized glands that release a slippery secretion, allowing them to move effortlessly across wet surfaces. Their elongated bodies and membranous wings let them glide from one cluster of rocks to another, capturing both insects and smaller lizards in mid-air with astonishing agility.

3. **Moisture Monitors:** These larger, more sedentary reptiles are the apex predators of this environment. With thick, scaled bodies and powerful limbs, they patrol the crude stone dwellings and the open expanses alike. Their tongues can extend to remarkable lengths to catch prey, and their strong jaws can crush even the toughest chitinous shells. They often bask in the humid heat, their mottled skin absorbing the warmth and emitting a faint hiss as moisture evaporates from their scales.

**Avian and Other Fauna:**
Though plant life is absent, this doesn’t mean the air remains devoid of life.

1. **Sky Skimmers:** Small, bat-like creatures with leathery wings and sharp claws. They swoop through the damp air, feeding on flying insects. Their large eyes are adapted to the perpetual twilight, allowing them to hunt efficiently even in low light conditions.

2. **Tunnel Moles:** Burrowing creatures that navigate the underground tunnels and shallow cavities within the rock formations. They are blind but have an acute sense of touch and smell, allowing them to find food—roots of subterranean fungi and small creatures that dwell below the surface.

**Primitive Settlement Life:**
Within the crude stone dwellings, the inhabitants—a species of early humanoids—exist in a constant state of activity. 

1. **Tool Users:** These beings are resourceful, crafting rudimentary tools from stone and metal. They build their homes into the rugged terrain, creating complex networks of tunnels and pathways. Their society is communal, with individuals working together to scavenge resources, fend off predators, and maintain their structures.

2. **Metalworkers:** A specialized subset of the humanoids who have developed a rudimentary understanding of metal. They forge simple weapons and tools using the raw materials available in the environment. Their forges are basic but effective, fueled by natural heat vents that provide a steady source of warmth.

**The Symphony of Life:**
All these creatures—insectile, reptilian, avian, and humanoid—create a symphony of life within this static landscape. The cacophony of their existence fills the air, a relentless reminder of the dynamic nature of life thriving in harmony and competition within this raw, unchanging world. It’s a place where every moment is a struggle for survival, yet also a testament to the resilience and adaptability of life itself.
