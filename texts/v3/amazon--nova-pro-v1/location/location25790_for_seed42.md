Nestled in the vast expanses of the submerged ocean territories lies the ancient city of Altheris. This submerged metropolis is half hidden in the vastness of the sea’s azure shades and is home to a society that has thrived for eons. 

### Terrain
The city sprawls across a plateau high above the abyssal depths of the ocean, yet far below the shimmering surface. The terrain of Altheris is a complex weave of natural coral formations and intricately built structures. Coral spires, towering with luminescent anemone caps, stand tall like gothic cathedrals piercing towards an imaginary sky of water. These geological wonders juxtapose with engineered domes and arches, constructed using ancient stone-setting techniques and bolstered with bio-luminescent algae-infused concrete. The ground beneath the city has been terraced to accommodate the rising corals, ensuring a seamless blend of nature and architecture.

### Materials
The city’s primary building materials are corals, shells, and a unique bio-alloy created by combining primitive metal tools with the natural minerals of the sea. The bio-alloy is a mix of seashell calcium and naturally occurring metals manipulated into a malleable state. This material glows faintly under the water, emitting a soft bluish hue that illuminates the corridors and chambers of the city. Algae carpets are woven into the walls and floors, creating a soft, elastic surface underfoot and contributing to the city's dense vegetation.

### Atmosphere
The dense atmosphere of Altheris is cool and moist, a reflection of its underwater setting. The air—or rather, the water enveloping the city—feels perpetually fresh and clean, purified by the surrounding aquatic flora. The space is serene, the blue gradation of light shifting subtly due to the underwater currents. Despite the deep setting, the air feels oddly breathable; the residents have developed ways to filter the water's oxygen, allowing for easy adaptation to this semi-aquatic environment.

### Sounds
A constant symphony of life permeates the city. The chorus of aquatic fauna—fish, sea turtles, and playful dolphins—creates a cacophony of clicks, whistles, and splashes. The sound of water flowing through the channels and tunnels is ever-present, whispering secrets of time and tide. Coral polyps softly creak as they open and close, accompanied by the occasional creak of ancient stonework settling deeper into its coral surroundings.

### Ecology
The ecology of Altheris is a finely balanced and incredibly rich ecosystem. Aquatic plants such as kelp, algae, and sea grasses dominate the vertical and horizontal spaces between structures, serving as both substrate and architecture. Invasive species are practically nonexistent, and the flora and fauna have evolved in concert with the city’s slow growth. Predatory fish patrol the edges of the city, while smaller, harmless creatures swim among the coral spires. Bioluminescent organisms provide a soft, ambient glow throughout the city, replacing artificial light for the most part.

### Rules of the World
The unique rules governing life in Altheris are dictated by both physics and ancient traditions. The laws of buoyancy and pressure are paramount, shaping everything from building design to personal travel. The city is divided into districts by function: residential areas built into the coral cliffs, market plazas floating above, and vast libraries carved into the stone. Tradition holds that each new generation must contribute to the city’s growth, whether through engineering, farming the kelp, or crafting new implements from shell and stone.

### Additional Features
At the heart of Altheris lies the Great Hall, a colossal chamber that doubles as the city's heart and spiritual center. It is a vast, domed space where the city's council gathers, and its walls are adorned with intricate carvings depicting the city’s history and legends, illuminated by hanging clusters of luminous jellyfish.

Though time flows differently here—marked by the slow cycles of the tides and the seasons of the ocean—the inhabitants of Altheris live interconnected lives of balance, respect, and harmony with their submerged world.
