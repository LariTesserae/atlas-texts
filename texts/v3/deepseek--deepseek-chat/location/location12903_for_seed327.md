Occasionally, the water drips from overhead, as if from invisible clouds, creating a rhythmic patter that blends with the other sounds of the place.

The plateau feels alive, its ecosystem in constant motion, though the scale of it is small, almost intimate.

Larger creatures—goat-like animals with thick coats—move slowly across the plateau, grazing on the sparse vegetation. There is a sense of balance here, a predator-prey relationship that hums beneath the surface, though it is subtle, almost hidden.
