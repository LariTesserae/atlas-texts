**The Moss-Crowned Gorge**  

A deep, winding crack in the earth, still young in its formation—its walls sheer and sharp, striated with layers of sediment still fresh from upheaval. The gorge is narrow, barely wide enough in places for two people to walk abreast, yet its depth plunges into shadow, where the faint glint of slow-moving water whispers at the bottom.  

The air is cool and damp, clinging to the skin like a second layer. Mist rises in the mornings, pooling in the crevices before dissipating into the day’s dim, filtered light. The sun rarely reaches the floor here; what illumination exists is soft, green-tinged, refracted through the thick curtains of moss that drape from the overhanging rock.  

Life thrives in this half-dark. The walls are furred with vegetation—ferns unfurl in the crevices, vines snake upward in slow, determined spirals, and luminescent lichen clings in patches, casting ghostly halos on the stone. The plant life is lush, almost aggressive in its growth, yet sparse in distribution—great swathes of bare rock separate pockets of dense greenery, as if the gorge itself is still deciding where life should take root.  

The fauna is overwhelming. Tiny, many-legged things skitter through the undergrowth; winged creatures flit soundlessly between the hanging moss, their forms indistinct in the gloom. Larger shapes move at the edges of perception—something with too many joints, something that flows like liquid over the rocks. They do not disturb the quiet. The gorge is a place of near-silence, broken only by the occasional drip of water, the rustle of leaves, the distant, unplaceable hum of something alive.  

At the gorge’s rim, where the land flattens into a high plateau, a small settlement clings to the edge. Its structures are low and blocky, built from the same stone as the gorge itself, their roofs thick with sod to keep out the chill. The people here move quietly, their tools simple—stone and bone and woven fiber. They do not venture far into the gorge, but sometimes, at dusk, they leave offerings at the mouth: bundles of herbs, carved tokens, things that gleam.  

The gorge watches. The gorge breathes. The gorge is waiting.
