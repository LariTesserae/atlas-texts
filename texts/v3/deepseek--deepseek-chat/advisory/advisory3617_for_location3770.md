### **Preparing for the Gloomdelta: A Traveler’s Guide**  

Visiting the Gloomdelta is not like traveling to another world—it’s like stepping into a place that is still deciding what it wants to be. The air is thick with possibility, and the land itself seems to shift beneath your feet. Survival here is less about brute force and more about understanding the rules of a realm that hasn’t fully settled on them yet.  

Here’s how to prepare:  

---

### **1. Mental & Psychological Readiness**  
The Gloomdelta does not follow familiar logic. Its geometry shifts, its water behaves strangely, and its silence is filled with sounds that have no clear source. Expect:  
- **Disorientation** – Landmarks may move. Paths may rearrange. The sky does not change in predictable cycles.  
- **Paranoia** – You will feel watched. You *are* being watched—by the land itself.  
- **Existential Unease** – The longer you stay, the more you may question whether the Gloomdelta is a place or a living thing.  

**Preparation:**  
- **Meditative grounding techniques** – Learn to recenter yourself when reality feels unstable.  
- **A mantra or anchor object** – Something to remind you of your own world when the delta’s strangeness presses in.  
- **A strict time limit** – The longer you stay, the harder it is to leave.  

---

### **2. Physical Preparations**  
#### **Clothing & Protection**  
- **Breathable but sealed layers** – The air is damp and cloying; you’ll want moisture-wicking fabric, but nothing too heavy.  
- **Covered skin** – The silvery water, fungal spores, and odd vegetation may react unpredictably with bare flesh.  
- **Sturdy, flexible footwear** – The ground is spongy, cracked, and sometimes glassy. Boots with grip but some give are ideal.  
- **A filtration mask** – The air is thick with spores and metallic traces. Breathing unfiltered air for too long may cause hallucinations.  

#### **Gear**  
- **Non-electronic light sources** – Bioluminescent fungus or chemical glow sticks. The delta may interfere with batteries or circuits.  
- **A walking staff** – To test the ground ahead—some patches are unstable, and the water doesn’t always behave like water.  
- **A sealed water flask** – Do *not* drink the local water. It moves on its own.  

---

### **3. Navigation & Movement**  
- **Do not trust straight lines** – The city’s bridges and pathways shift. What was solid ground yesterday may be a chasm today.  
- **Follow the fungal glow** – The tallest spires seem to mark stable zones, though their light may also attract unseen things.  
- **Avoid the open stretches** – The empty, glassy plains are too exposed. The delta’s inhabitants (if any) prefer the denser zones.  
- **Mark your path with something the land won’t absorb** – Carved bone, polished stone, or metal tags. Rope and cloth may be absorbed or repurposed.  

---

### **4. Dealing with the Inhabitants (If You Encounter Any)**  
You may never see them. But you will feel their presence.  

- **Do not touch the structures** – The city’s walls breathe. The bridges have no visible supports. This is not a place built for humans.  
- **If something watches you from the mist, do not stare back** – Avert your gaze and move away slowly.  
- **If the water hums, leave immediately** – It is reacting to something you cannot see.  

---

### **5. Leaving the Gloomdelta**  
The delta does not like to let go. Exiting may be harder than entering.  

- **Retrace your steps exactly** – If you marked your path, follow it precisely. Deviations may lead you deeper in.  
- **Do not look back until you are sure you’re out** – The landscape may try to rewrite itself behind you.  
- **Cleanse thoroughly upon return** – Wash with salt and vinegar. Burn your clothing if it still feels damp. Some travelers report hearing the delta’s hum in their dreams for weeks after leaving.  

---

### **Final Warning**  
The Gloomdelta is not a place to explore—it is a place to witness, briefly, and then depart. It is a wound in reality, a realm still forming. Stay too long, and you may become part of its becoming.  

Go carefully.  
Do not drink the water.  
Do not answer the voices in the mist.  
And whatever you do—**do not enter the city.**
