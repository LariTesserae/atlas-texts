### **Preparing for the Chamber of Whispering Stone**  

Visiting the Chamber of Whispering Stone is not an expedition—it is an act of surrender. This is not a place for conquest, for mapping, for mastery. It is a place that exists outside the rhythms of life, a relic of a reality where time and movement have no meaning. To enter is to submit to its rules, and the unprepared traveler may find themselves unraveled by its stillness.  

Here is how one might endure it.  

---  

### **1. Mental Preparation: The Weight of Eternity**  
The first and greatest danger of the Chamber is not physical—it is psychological. The hum of the stone is not merely sound; it is a presence, a slow, insistent pressure on the mind. Those who enter unprepared report:  
- A growing sense of *wrongness*, as if their own heartbeat is an intrusion.  
- The creeping fear that they are being *observed* by the carvings.  
- The slow erosion of linear thought, replaced by a dreamlike drift toward the chamber’s rhythm.  

**How to resist:**  
- **Meditative conditioning.** Practice extended silence and sensory deprivation before arrival. The chamber will strip away distractions; you must be able to exist within your own mind without panic.  
- **Mnemonic anchors.** Bring a physical object tied to a strong memory—a locket, a carved token, a vial of scent. When the hum threatens to dissolve your sense of self, focus on this anchor.  
- **A mantra.** Repeat a phrase that affirms your identity, your purpose, your *temporariness* in this place. "I am a visitor. I will leave."  

---  

### **2. Physical Considerations: A Body Out of Place**  
The Chamber does not accommodate living things. Its air is breathable but unnervingly dry, its gravity normal but subtly *off*, as if the floor resists your footsteps.  

**Essential preparations:**  
- **Hydration.** The air leeches moisture from skin and lungs. Bring sealed water and electrolyte supplements. Your throat will parch within minutes.  
- **Temperature regulation.** The stone is neither warm nor cold—it is *neutral*, a perfect conductor that will sap your body heat if you remain still too long. Wear layers you can adjust.  
- **Footwear.** The floor is smooth but not frictionless; you may feel as though you are gliding rather than walking. Soft-soled shoes with grip are advisable.  

**Do not bring:**  
- **Electronics.** They will fail. The hum disrupts circuits, drains batteries, corrupts data. Mechanical devices may survive longer, but even watches will lose time.  
- **Organic matter that decays.** Food should be sealed, preserved, minimal. The Chamber rejects rot.  

---  

### **3. Navigation: The Illusion of Space**  
The Chamber’s dimensions are deceptive. The walls appear close, yet sound carries strangely, making distance difficult to judge. The carvings shift when unobserved—not dramatically, but enough to disorient.  

**How to move:**  
- **Mark your path.** Use chalk, powdered stone, or another substance that will not violate the Chamber’s purity. Even these marks may fade, but they are better than nothing.  
- **Avoid the central spire.** It is the source of the hum, and prolonged proximity induces trance states. Some travelers report hearing whispers, though no mouth moves.  
- **Do not touch the carvings.** They are not inert. Some who have brushed the patterns claim to have seen flashes of impossible geometries, visions of a world where the Chamber is the only thing that exists.  

---  

### **4. Time: The Chamber’s Greatest Trick**  
Time does not behave normally here. Minutes stretch; hours collapse. You may feel as though you have been inside for days, only to emerge and find mere minutes have passed—or the opposite.  

**How to mitigate this:**  
- **Use a mechanical hourglass or candle clock.** Digital timers will fail, but simple, gravity-based measures may retain some accuracy.  
- **Set a strict departure time before entering.** The Chamber will make you forget. Have a companion outside to retrieve you if possible.  
- **Beware the lure of the spire.** The longer you stare at it, the harder it becomes to look away. Some never do.  

---  

### **5. Leaving: The Echo That Follows**  
Exiting the Chamber does not mean you have escaped it. Many travelers report:  
- Hearing the hum in their dreams.  
- Noticing, days later, that their handwriting has subtly changed to resemble the carvings.  
- Feeling the dry, mineral scent cling to them no matter how they wash.  

**How to cleanse the experience:**  
- **Ritual separation.** Change clothes immediately upon leaving. Burn them if possible.  
- **Sound therapy.** Listen to running water, birdsong, human voices—anything to overwrite the hum.  
- **Do not return.** The Chamber remembers its visitors. Second visits are always harder.  

---  

### **Final Warning**  
The Chamber of Whispering Stone is not evil. It is not hostile. It simply *is*, in a way that living things are not meant to comprehend.  

Go only if you must.  
Stay only as long as you dare.  
And when you leave, do not look back.  

The carvings are watching.
