### **Preparing for the Plateau of Whispering Stone**  

Visiting the Plateau of Whispering Stone is not a journey to be taken lightly. It is a place that defies the ordinary rules of reality, where the land itself is sentient in ways that are difficult to comprehend. An ordinary human multiverse traveler must prepare meticulously—physically, mentally, and spiritually—to survive this encounter.  

#### **1. Physical Preparations**  

##### **Clothing & Gear**  
- **Layered, Windproof Attire:** The plateau is cold, damp, and lashed by relentless winds. Wear insulated, waterproof outer layers with a moisture-wicking base to prevent hypothermia. A hooded cloak or parka with a high collar is advisable.  
- **Sturdy, Gripped Footwear:** The basalt is slick with condensation. Boots with deep treads or even crampons may be necessary to avoid slipping.  
- **Breathing Mask (Optional):** The air is thin, and if the plateau exists at high altitude, supplemental oxygen or a filtration mask might be needed.  

##### **Supplies**  
- **Water & Rations:** Though there are seeps of black water, they may not be safe to drink. Bring sealed, insulated containers of fresh water and high-calorie, non-perishable food.  
- **Light Sources:** The plateau’s mist and shifting light can distort visibility. Pack reliable, long-lasting lanterns or headlamps—preferably ones that do not rely on electricity, as the plateau may interfere with technology.  
- **Navigation Tools:** Compasses may behave erratically here. Bring physical maps (if any exist), a sextant, or a celestial navigation guide. Marking your path with durable, non-electronic trail markers (etched stones, durable flags) is wise.  

##### **Medical & Survival Kits**  
- **First Aid:** Bandages, antiseptics, painkillers, and emergency blankets.  
- **Mental Stabilizers:** Mild sedatives or calming herbs (if you use them) to stave off disorientation or panic.  
- **Rope & Climbing Gear:** Some areas may require scaling or descending slick rock faces.  

#### **2. Mental & Psychological Preparations**  

##### **Expect the Unsettling**  
- The plateau warps perception. Shadows move wrong. Sounds echo strangely. The mind will seek patterns where there should be none.  
- **Do not listen too closely to the whispering stones.** If you begin to hear words in the wind, focus on a mantra or hum to drown them out.  

##### **Companionship & Isolation**  
- Traveling alone is dangerous—the silence and isolation can unravel sanity. A small, trusted group is ideal.  
- However, do not trust strangers you might encounter. The ruins are empty, but *something* may mimic human forms.  

##### **Time Distortion**  
- Clocks may behave oddly. The plateau exists outside normal time. Keep a journal to track events, but be prepared for entries to contradict themselves.  

#### **3. Spiritual & Supernatural Precautions**  

##### **Warding & Protection**  
- Some multiverse travelers swear by charms, sigils, or blessed objects to repel unseen forces. Whether these work depends on your belief system, but it does not hurt to carry a personal talisman.  
- Salt, iron, or other traditional wardings may be useful if the plateau’s entities adhere to known supernatural rules.  

##### **The Ruins**  
- **Do not enter the city lightly.** If you must investigate, do so in daylight (assuming the plateau has a consistent day/night cycle).  
- Bring an offering—something old, something of personal value. The plateau may demand tribute.  

##### **The Whispering**  
- If the stones begin speaking to you, **do not answer.** Acknowledge nothing. Pretend you do not hear.  
- If you feel something watching you, **do not look directly at it.** Peripheral vision is safer.  

#### **4. Exit Strategy**  
- The plateau may not let you leave easily. Have a predetermined ritual or method for departure—a recited phrase, a marked path, a portal anchor if you possess multiverse travel tech.  
- If you become lost, retrace steps *exactly.* The land shifts, but footprints in damp stone or lichen might remain.  

### **Final Warning**  
The Plateau of Whispering Stone is not a place for tourism. It is a liminal space, a wound in reality where something ancient and indifferent lingers. You are a guest here—one that may not be welcome.  

Tread carefully.  
Speak softly.  
And whatever you do—**do not stay after dark.**
