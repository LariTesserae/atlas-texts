### **Preparing for the Moss-Cloaked Hollow**  

If you are an ordinary human traveler stepping into the **Moss-Cloaked Hollow**, you must understand that this is not a place that welcomes intrusion. It is a world of patience, of slow decay and quiet growth, where time moves differently. To survive—and to avoid disturbing the delicate balance—you must adapt to its rhythms.  

### **1. Clothing: Become Part of the Damp**  
The hollow does not tolerate dryness. Your garments must:  
- **Be made of natural fibers**—wool, linen, or moss-treated leather. Synthetic materials will feel alien here, and the mist will cling to them unnaturally.  
- **Be layered but breathable**—the air is thick, and sweat will not evaporate. You must stay warm without overheating.  
- **Blend into the greens and grays**—bright colors will startle the creatures and mark you as an outsider.  
- **Include a hood or wide-brimmed hat**—not for rain, but to catch the endless dripping from the canopy.  

### **2. Footwear: Move Without Disturbance**  
The moss muffles sound, but the earth remembers every step.  
- **Wear soft-soled shoes**—moccasins or bark-woven sandals that let you feel the ground.  
- **Avoid heavy boots**—they will sink into the peat, leaving deep, unnatural scars.  
- **Be prepared for dampness**—your feet will never be fully dry. If you must, line your shoes with dried moss to wick moisture.  

### **3. Supplies: Bring Only What the Hollow Would Accept**  
- **Food:** Carry smoked fish, dried mushrooms, or hard cheese—things that will not rot quickly in the wet air. Do not bring anything too sweet; the insects will find you.  
- **Water:** The hollow’s water is safe to drink, but it tastes of earth and slow decay. If you cannot stomach it, collect rainwater from leaves.  
- **Light:** Fire is rare here. Bring a dim lantern or candles made of tallow—anything brighter will feel like a violation.  
- **Tools:** A bone or stone knife will serve you better than steel. Metal rusts too quickly here.  

### **4. Behavior: Move Like the Mist**  
- **Walk slowly.** Sudden movements will make the trees creak in disapproval.  
- **Speak softly, if at all.** The hollow listens. Loud voices disturb the equilibrium.  
- **Do not take what is not given.** The moss, the stones, the water—they are not yours. If you must gather something, leave an offering in return (a strand of hair, a drop of blood, a whispered thanks).  
- **Observe before acting.** The hollow has its own rules. Watch how the people here live—how they step around certain roots, how they avoid touching certain stones. Follow their lead.  

### **5. Beware the Unseen Residents**  
The hollow is not uninhabited. There are things here that have never known haste:  
- **The moss that clings too long** may be more than moss. If it resists being brushed off, apologize and let it be.  
- **The reflections in the water** sometimes show what isn’t there. Do not stare too long.  
- **The trees sometimes shift** when you’re not looking. Do not acknowledge it.  

### **6. Leaving: Do Not Take the Hollow With You**  
When it is time to go:  
- **Shake out your clothes**—seeds and spores from the hollow should not spread elsewhere.  
- **Leave no trace of your stay.** The moss will heal over your footprints, but anything left behind will fester.  
- **Do not look back.** The hollow does not like to be remembered too clearly.  

### **Final Thought: You Are a Guest Here**  
The Moss-Cloaked Hollow does not need visitors. But if you must go, go gently. Move like the mist. Speak like the drip of water on stone. And when you leave, let the hollow forget you were ever there.
