### **Preparing for the Folding City of Vhorth**  

Visiting Vhorth is not like traveling to any ordinary city. It is a place of slow, living geology, where the ground itself shifts and the air hums with unseen forces. To survive—let alone thrive—here, a traveler must prepare for a landscape that is as much a living entity as it is a settlement. Below is a comprehensive guide to navigating Vhorth’s peculiarities.  

---  

### **1. Physical Preparations**  

#### **Clothing & Protection**  
- **Layered, Waterproof Gear:** The air is perpetually damp, and water seeps from every surface. A waxed or oilskin outer layer is essential to stay dry.  
- **Sturdy, Gripped Footwear:** The streets are uneven, often slick with moss or moisture. Boots with deep treads will prevent slips on shifting stone.  
- **Masks & Filters:** The air carries fine metallic dust from the underground machinery. A cloth mask or light respirator will help avoid respiratory irritation.  
- **Gloves:** The stone is often sharp, and touching exposed metal (especially rusted structures) could lead to cuts or infections.  

#### **Supplies**  
- **Portable Water Purifier:** While water is abundant, it seeps from the rock and may contain minerals or residues from the city’s hidden machinery.  
- **Non-Perishable Food:** The local diet is sparse, relying on hardy root vegetables and preserved meats. Bring nutrient-dense rations in case markets are scarce.  
- **Light Source:** The mist dims visibility, and some streets are narrow, choked with overgrowth. A reliable lantern or headlamp is crucial.  
- **Climbing Gear (Optional):** Some paths may require navigating half-formed staircases or leaning structures. A collapsible pole or rope could be useful.  

---  

### **2. Mental & Psychological Preparation**  

#### **Expect the Unsettling**  
- Vhorth does not obey conventional physics. Buildings shift, new passages appear, and the ground may subtly tilt beneath your feet. **Do not panic.** The locals treat these changes with quiet acceptance—follow their lead.  
- The city’s sounds—groaning stone, distant machinery—can be unnerving. Earplugs may help if the noise becomes overwhelming.  

#### **Time Perception**  
- Vhorth operates on a geological timescale. Things happen slowly. Impatience will only frustrate you. **Move deliberately, observe carefully.**  

#### **Avoid Fixating on the Wildlife**  
- The creatures here are not like those elsewhere. Some may watch you with too many eyes; others may vanish when you blink. **Do not chase them. Do not try to touch them.**  

---  

### **3. Navigation & Local Customs**  

#### **Understanding the Layout**  
- There are no maps of Vhorth. The city reconfigures itself. Instead, learn **landmarks that persist**: a rusted iron spire, a particular fissure in the rock, a moss-covered statue half-sunk into the ground.  
- **Follow the water.** The paths where moisture collects tend to be the most stable.  

#### **Interacting with Locals**  
- The people of Vhorth are reserved but not hostile. They speak little, and when they do, their words are measured. **Do not pry.**  
- **Trade, not currency:** The economy here is barter-based. Useful items (tools, preserved food, sturdy cloth) are more valuable than coins.  
- **Do not ask about the shifting architecture.** They consider it as natural as breathing.  

#### **Shelter**  
- Do not sleep in half-formed buildings—they may collapse or change overnight. Seek out **stone inns** or **carved alcoves** where travelers are known to stay.  
- If no shelter is available, a **hammock or suspended bedroll** will keep you off the damp ground.  

---  

### **4. Hazards & Warnings**  

#### **The Mist**  
- It thickens at night, sometimes to the point of near-zero visibility. **Do not wander after sundown.** Locals tie guide ropes between buildings for safe passage.  

#### **The Shifting Streets**  
- If a new path opens unexpectedly, **do not enter it immediately.** Some passages close again without warning. Wait to see if others use it first.  

#### **The Mountain’s Dreams**  
- On rare nights, the air thrums with a deep, resonant sound—like stone grinding in sleep. Some travelers report visions or a strange compulsion to walk toward the cliffs. **Anchor yourself mentally.** Focus on a fixed object (a lantern, a carved mark) to avoid disorientation.  

---  

### **5. Leaving Vhorth**  

- The city does not like to let go. Some travelers report circling the same streets for days before finding an exit.  
- **To depart safely:** Follow the **downward seep of water**—it often leads to stable valleys where the mist thins.  
- Do not look back as you leave. The city may fold itself out of sight behind you.  

---  

### **Final Thought**  

Vhorth is not a place one conquers or fully understands. It is a city that exists in the act of becoming, and the wise traveler learns to move with it, not against it. Come prepared, stay observant, and—above all—**respect the stone.**
