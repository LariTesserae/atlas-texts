### **Preparing for the Chamber of Hungry Echoes**  

Visiting the Chamber of Hungry Echoes is not a journey to be taken lightly. It is a place of deep, unsettling permanence—a pocket of reality where time has calcified, where the air itself feels like an intruder. If you must go, here is how you might survive, or at least delay whatever fate the chamber has in store for you.  

---

### **1. Mental Preparation**  
This place does not follow the rules of human psychology. The hum, the skittering, the weight of unseen observation—all of it will press against your mind like a slow, insistent hand.  

- **Meditate on silence.** The chamber will fill your thoughts with its own rhythms. Practice holding mental stillness, or risk losing your sense of self in the endless drone.  
- **Do not anthropomorphize the watcher.** It is not a god, not a demon—it is something older, something indifferent. Fear it, but do not beg. It does not care.  
- **Prepare for recursion.** The echoes here do not fade. Your words will linger, warp, return. Speak sparingly, if at all.  

---

### **2. Physical Supplies**  
The chamber is dry, hot, and devoid of sustenance. You will not find water here. You will not find food.  

- **Bring your own oxygen.** The air is breathable, but thick with metallic dust. A filtration mask is advisable.  
- **Wear insulated clothing.** The heat is not fire, not sunlight—it is the warmth of deep stone, of engines buried in the earth. Lightweight thermal regulation is key.  
- **Carry no electronics.** The hum interferes with circuits. Mechanical watches may still function, but their ticking will be absorbed into the chamber’s rhythm.  
- **A durable light source.** The phosphorescent nodules provide dim illumination, but their glow is unreliable. Bring something that does not rely on batteries.  

---

### **3. Navigation & Survival**  
There are no maps. There are no landmarks. The chamber does not change, but it may change *you*.  

- **Mark your path.** Chalk, string, anything physical—do not trust memory. The chamber’s geometry may resist your attempts to leave.  
- **Do not touch the walls.** The grooves are not natural. The smoothness is not erosion. Whatever shaped this place did so with intent.  
- **Avoid the machinery.** It is not broken. It is waiting.  
- **Ignore the skittering things.** They are not hostile, but they are curious. If you disturb them, they will investigate.  

---

### **4. The Watcher in the Dark**  
You will feel it. A pressure, a presence, something vast and patient. It does not need to reveal itself to be known.  

- **Do not look for it.** If you see it, it has already seen you—and decided you are worth noticing.  
- **Do not offer it anything.** It does not want gifts. It does not want worship.  
- **If it speaks, do not answer.** The voice will not be words. It will be the hum given meaning.  

---

### **5. Leaving (If You Can)**  
The chamber does not release visitors easily. Some who enter do not find the way back. Some do not realize they are still inside.  

- **Retrace your steps exactly.** The chamber will try to fold you into its stillness. Move with purpose.  
- **Do not turn back.** The thing that watches does so from behind you. If you look, you may meet its gaze.  
- **When you emerge, burn your clothes.** The dust does not belong in our world.  

---

### **Final Warning**  
The Chamber of Hungry Echoes is not a place for answers. It is not a place for exploration. It is a place that exists for its own reasons, and you are an irrelevance.  

If you must go, go prepared.  

And do not expect to return unchanged.
