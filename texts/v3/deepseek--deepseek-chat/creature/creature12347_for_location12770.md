**The Inhabitants of the Glass Abyss**  

They are not fish, nor cephalopods, nor anything that should exist in the crushing dark.  

Their bodies are translucent, their forms shifting between solidity and liquid suggestion, as if the pressure has not crushed them but *refined* them into something sharper, more deliberate. Limbs taper into needle-thin appendages, their edges catching the faintest bioluminescence like fractured light on a blade. They move without disturbing the water—no ripples, no displacement—as though they are not swimming but *rearranging* the space around them.  

Their faces, if they can be called such, are smooth planes of glassy flesh, featureless save for the occasional ripple of something beneath the surface. No eyes, no mouths, yet they perceive. They *know*. When they pass near the volcanic structures, the glass hums in resonance, a sound felt more than heard, a vibration that thrums through the bones of any intruder foolish enough to draw near.  

They do not hunt. They do not feed. They exist in perfect stillness, drifting between the structures with the languid certainty of creatures that have never known urgency. When they touch the glass—delicate, precise—it responds, flickering with patterns too complex to decipher, a language written in fractures and refracted light.  

Sometimes, one will pause, its form shuddering into sudden clarity, and for a moment, a shape emerges—something skeletal, something that might have once been familiar. A spine. A ribcage. A jaw. Then it dissolves again, returning to the seamless ambiguity of its kind.  

They do not acknowledge outsiders. They do not flee, nor do they attack. They simply *are*, as much a part of the city as the glass itself. Perhaps they built it. Perhaps they were built *by* it. Perhaps they are its caretakers, its prisoners, or its architects—or all three at once.  

They leave no traces. No waste, no nests, no signs of reproduction. If they die, their bodies dissipate like mist, leaving nothing behind.  

They are the silent guardians of a logic beyond comprehension.  

And they are watching.  

Always watching.
