### **The Weeping Plateau**  

**Inhabitants:**  

1. **The Moss-Walkers**  
   - Pale, slow-moving figures draped in thick, dripping moss. Their forms are indistinct, more like human-shaped clusters of vegetation than flesh. They move only when the mist thickens, gliding between the pools with silent purpose. No eyes are visible beneath their verdant shrouds, but they tilt their heads as if listening to the weeping stones. Some say they are the remnants of those who once tried to settle here, absorbed by the land. Others believe they are the plateau itself, given fleeting motion.  

2. **The Drowned Reflections**  
   - The pools do not show the sky. Instead, they hold glimpses of figures—sometimes human, sometimes something else—moving just beneath the surface. They never break the water, never ripple. They simply exist in parallel, watching. Occasionally, a hand presses against the underside of the water’s skin, fingers splayed as if testing an unseen barrier.  

3. **The Lichen-Speakers**  
   - Colonies of intelligent lichen that spread across the rocks in intricate, vein-like patterns. They communicate in slow chemical pulses, taking days to form a single thought. Those who listen closely (and have the patience) claim the lichen tells stories—of the plateau’s birth, of the first rains, of the moment the land learned to weep.  

4. **The Stone-Weepers**  
   - The rocks themselves are not entirely inert. Somewhere between mineral and organism, they exude moisture in slow, rhythmic pulses. The most ancient of them have developed rudimentary faces—gouged by erosion into sorrowful visages. They do not speak, but their tears gather in the pools, feeding the silence.  

5. **The Hollow Wind**  
   - Not a creature, but a presence. The wind here does not howl; it sighs, as if carrying the breath of something vast and dormant. Sometimes, it pauses altogether, and the air becomes thick with anticipation. Those who listen too long swear they hear whispers—not in words, but in the spaces between them.  

**Behavior:**  
None of the inhabitants are aggressive, but none are welcoming. The Moss-Walkers ignore intruders unless touched, at which point they dissolve into a slurry of algae and mist. The Drowned Reflections mimic those who look too long, wearing their faces in silent judgment. The plateau tolerates visitors but does not remember them. Stay long enough, and you, too, may become part of its slow, weeping rhythm.  

**Rule:**  
Do not drink from the pools. The water remembers what it has swallowed.
