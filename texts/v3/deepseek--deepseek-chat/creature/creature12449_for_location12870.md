In the Chamber of Weeping Stone, **the Keepers of the Stillness** reside. They are beings of neither flesh nor stone but something in-between, as if the damp air itself had congealed into form. Their bodies are translucent, shimmering faintly like the mineral veins in the walls, their edges blending into the shadows with a fluidity that makes them nearly impossible to track. They move without sound, gliding over the wet stone as if they are part of the water, part of the air.  

Their faces are featureless, smooth planes that reflect the faint ambient light but reveal nothing beneath. Occasionally, their forms ripple, as though disturbed by some unseen current, and for a moment, they seem to dissolve entirely before coalescing once more. They do not speak, nor do they breathe, yet their presence hums faintly, a vibration felt in the bones rather than heard.  

The Keepers are guardians, though of what is unclear. Perhaps they watch over the chamber itself, ensuring its eternal dampness, its perfect stillness. Perhaps they tend to the pools, which ripple eternally but never overflow, never dry—their purpose as inscrutable as the Keepers themselves. They drift around the artifacts left behind by others, their movements deliberate but unfathomable. They may pause near the crude clay vessel, hovering over it as if listening to some whisper only they can hear, or circle the worn stone tool with a slow, deliberate grace before gliding onward.  

There is no malice in them, no hostility, but their presence is suffocating. To be near them is to feel the weight of the chamber pressing closer, the air growing thicker, the silence heavier. They do not act against intruders, but neither do they acknowledge them. They simply *are*, as eternal and unchanging as the chamber itself.  

Occasionally, one of the small, many-legged creatures will cross their path, skittering frantically over the stone. The Keepers do not pursue them, do not react—yet the creatures vanish soon after, as though swallowed by the dampness, their brief presence erased without trace.  

The Keepers are the heart of the Chamber of Weeping Stone, its silent pulse. They are its stillness embodied, its dampness made form. They are the breath that does not exhale, the water that does not dry, the stone that does not crumble. And so, they remain, eternal and unyielding, in their chamber of perpetual weep.
