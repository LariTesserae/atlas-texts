### **The Amber Hollow**  

**Inhabitants:**  

1. **The Sap-Singers**  
   - A quiet, wiry people with skin like polished amber, their veins faintly luminous beneath the surface. They communicate in hums and clicks, their voices harmonizing with the chamber’s resonant drone. Their hands are long-fingered, adept at shaping the sticky resin into tools, dwellings, and even intricate, ephemeral art that melts back into the walls over time.  
   - They live in clustered pods of woven vines and hardened sap, suspended between the great roots that vein the chamber. Their society is communal, with no clear hierarchy—only those who listen best to the hollow’s hum guiding the others.  
   - They sustain themselves on the thick, syrupy sap, fermented in bulbous gourds into a mildly intoxicating nectar. Occasionally, they hunt the blind, slow-moving grubs that burrow in the roots, roasting them over vents of warm air that rise from below.  

2. **The Root-Tenders**  
   - A smaller, secretive sect who believe the hollow is alive—not just a place, but a slumbering entity. They spend their days pressing their ears to the roots, interpreting the vibrations as prophecy. Their fingertips are permanently stained black from handling the chamber’s deepest, oldest resins.  
   - They claim the hum is a language, and that one day, it will form words.  

3. **The Amber-Eyed Watchers**  
   - Rare, solitary figures who climb the highest roots, perching where the mist grows thin. Their eyes have clouded over with a golden film, and they claim to see glimpses of movement in the haze—shapes that might be memories, or ghosts, or something else entirely.  
   - The others treat them with a mix of reverence and unease.  

4. **The Unseen Dwellers**  
   - Things move in the deeper tangles, where the light fails. The Sap-Singers leave offerings of resin at the edges of these zones—small, shining globes set on protruding roots. Sometimes, the globes are gone by morning. Sometimes, they are cracked open, the insides licked clean.  
   - No one speaks of them directly.  

**Ecology:**  
- **Glowgrubs:** Fat, lazy larvae that feed on the sap, their bodies emitting a soft bioluminescence. The Sap-Singers cultivate them in hollowed-out roots, using them as living lanterns.  
- **Rootcrawlers:** Segmented, many-legged hunters that navigate by vibration. They are blind but swift, snatching grubs and unwary Sap-Singers who wander too far into the dark.  
- **Whispervines:** A parasitic plant that grows in the ears of those who sleep too close to the walls. Its tendrils whisper in dreams, urging the listener to press deeper into the hollow, where the roots grow sweet and the hum grows loud.  

**The Hollow’s Secret:**  
The chamber is not natural. It is the ribcage of something vast and long dead, its bones petrified into stone, its veins hardened into amber. The hum is the last echo of its heartbeat. The Sap-Singers are its unintended children.  

And lately, the hum has been changing.
