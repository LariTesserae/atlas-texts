### **The Glass Labyrinth of the Shattered Sun**  

**Inhabitants:**  

1. **The Refracted Ones**  
   - Beings of living glass, their bodies shifting between transparency and opacity as they move. They do not walk—they *flow*, sliding across the silica like liquid given sentience. Their forms are never the same twice: sometimes humanoid, sometimes spiraling fractals, sometimes nothing more than a shimmer in the air.  
   - They speak in echoes, repeating sounds they’ve absorbed from the environment—the hum of the crystalline flora, the groans of the ancient machines, even the footsteps of past travelers. Their voices are layered, overlapping, as if multiple versions of them exist at once.  
   - They are neither hostile nor kind. They observe, they mimic, they *record*. Some believe they are the land’s memory, made manifest.  

2. **The Rust-Eaters**  
   - Scavengers that cling to the derelict machines, their bodies a patchwork of corroded metal and organic tissue. They move in jerking, mechanical motions, their limbs fused with gears and pistons. Their mouths are grinding mechanisms, capable of dissolving steel into a black, viscous slurry they use to sustain themselves.  
   - They fear the Refracted Ones, fleeing when the glass beings draw near. Some whisper that the Rust-Eaters were once human, or something like it, before the machines claimed them.  

3. **The Hollow Choir**  
   - Tall, emaciated figures wrapped in tattered strips of light—fabric torn from the sky itself. They stand motionless for days, then suddenly burst into motion, their limbs elongating as they sprint across the glass. They leave no footprints.  
   - They sing, but their song has no sound. Instead, it manifests as pressure in the skull, a headache that builds until vision blurs. Those who listen too long find their thoughts unraveling, their memories replaced with fragments of the Choir’s silent hymn.  

4. **The Stillborn Suns**  
   - Floating orbs of condensed light, no larger than a fist, drifting aimlessly above the glass. They are the remnants of the Shattered Sun’s siblings—failed stars that never ignited. Occasionally, one will flicker and collapse inward, imploding with a soundless burst of prismatic shards.  
   - The Refracted Ones collect these shards, embedding them into their bodies. No one knows why.  

5. **The Architect (or the Mirage)**  
   - A solitary figure seen always at a distance, standing at the heart of the labyrinth. Its shape is indistinct—sometimes a man, sometimes a spire of glass, sometimes a hole in the world. Those who try to approach find the landscape rearranging itself, leading them in circles.  
   - Some say it built this place. Others say it is the place itself, dreaming.  

**The Land’s Will**  
The Glass Labyrinth is not passive. It reacts. It learns. It *hungers*.  
- Those who bleed on the silica find their blood hardening into delicate filaments, absorbed into the ground.  
- Those who sleep here wake with their reflections slightly altered—a scar that wasn’t there before, a missing tooth, an extra finger.  
- Those who try to leave discover that the horizon never draws nearer. The Labyrinth does not like to let go.  

This is not a place for living things. And yet, here they are. Here *you* are.  

The glass remembers. The glass is always watching.
