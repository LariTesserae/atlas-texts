In this submerged, rain-drenched realm, the inhabitants are as much a contradiction as the landscape itself. They are not creatures of flesh and blood, but beings molded from the very fabric of this liquid world—an amalgamation of water, vegetation, and the residual energy of the ancient structures that hum and groan in the mist. They are the Sentinels of the Submerged Vale, custodians of this unraveling ecosystem, and their presence is felt more than seen.

The Sentinels are fluid entities, their forms ever-shifting, composed of swirling water and glowing strands of bioluminescent algae. They rise from the depths like pillars of liquid light, their bodies flowing and reforming with the currents. Their shapes are vaguely humanoid, but only in the way that a reflection in rippling water resembles a face—indistinct, fleeting, and hauntingly familiar. Their "skin" crackles with static, tiny arcs of electricity dancing across their surfaces as they move, illuminating the mist with bursts of blue and green light.

They are silent, their communication conveyed through the rhythmic pulses of their bodies and the hum that resonates through the water. To stand near one is to feel the vibrations of their presence, a low, steady thrum that echoes in your chest and skull. Their intentions are enigmatic, but their movements are deliberate—slow, calculated, and almost ritualistic. They glide through the submerged valley, tending to the overgrown vegetation, their touch causing the plants to shudder and glow brighter, as if nourished by their contact.

The Sentinels are drawn to the rusted, humming structures that dot the landscape. They hover around these edifices, their forms swirling and condensing as if studying the machinery. Occasionally, they extend tendrils of water and light into the structures, their movements precise and purposeful. The structures respond with louder hums and sharper flashes of light, as if awakening briefly from their decayed slumber. It is unclear if the Sentinels are maintaining these machines, harvesting their energy, or simply observing them—but their interaction suggests a connection to the forces that keep this place alive.

Despite their ethereal nature, the Sentinels are not passive. When intruders or disturbances disrupt the delicate balance of their world, they become more aggressive. Their forms solidify, their crackling static intensifies, and they move with a speed and precision that belies their fluidity. They can envelop threats, their bodies merging with the surrounding water to create vortexes and whirlpools that pull anything—or anyone—into the depths. Their touch is electric, a jolt that paralyzes and disorients, leaving their victims helpless in the currents.

The Sentinels are as much a part of this world as the water, the plants, and the decaying structures. They are its living essence, its caretakers, and its enforcers. Their origins are unknown—whether they were created by the same unseen force that reshaped this valley or emerged spontaneously from the chaotic energies that pervade the place—but their existence is inextricably tied to the submerged realm. They are the guardians of its secrets, its contradictions, and its unraveling reality.

To encounter a Sentinel is to confront the mystery of this liquid world. They are both beautiful and terrifying, soothing and unsettling, familiar yet alien. They do not speak, but their presence conveys a truth as deep and impenetrable as the waters they inhabit: this is not a place for understanding, only for witnessing. The Sentinels are its silent storytellers, their fluid forms a testament to the chaotic, living enigma that is the Submerged Vale.
