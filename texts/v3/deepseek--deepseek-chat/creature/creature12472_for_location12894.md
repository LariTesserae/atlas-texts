The inhabitants of the Root-City of Mycel are as much a part of its ecosystem as its architecture. The city teems with symbiotic beings adapted to its unique environment—creatures evolved to thrive in the damp, pulsating darkness of fungal growth and bioluminescent light. Here, the distinction between resident and infrastructure often blurs; in Mycel, you are not merely living *in* the city—you are living *with* it.  

Foremost are the **Spore-Keepers**, tall, willowy beings whose pale, translucent skin glimmers faintly in the dim glow of the city. Their bodies are covered in fine, branching filaments that connect them to the mycelial network, allowing them to commune with the city’s living core. Spore-Keepers serve as custodians, their role to monitor and guide the flows of nutrients and energy through the fungal reactors and vein-like pipelines. They move with deliberate slowness, their limbs draped in fibrous robes that seem to root them to the ground, and their voices are soft, wet whispers that carry through the humid air.  

Then there are the **Chitineers**, industrious insectoid beings whose chitinous carapaces gleam faintly in the bioluminescent light. They scuttle along ceilings and walls, their many-jointed limbs designed for intricate tasks: adjusting the flow of fluids, repairing ruptured membranes, and tending to the city’s machinery. Chitineers are tireless workers, their movements precise and efficient, and they communicate in rapid-fire clicks and chirps that echo through the caverns.  

In the shadows, the **Shadelings** dwell—amorphous, shadowy forms that seem to melt into the city’s fibrous walls. Their bodies are fluid, shifting shapes that blend seamlessly with their surroundings, and they watch the city’s comings and goings with an unsettling stillness. Shadelings are the city’s unseen guardians, merging with the architecture to repair damage or subtly redirect resources where they are needed most.  

The **Mite-Swarms** are ubiquitous, clouds of tiny, luminescent creatures that flit through the air like living sparks. They serve as pollinators, processors, and even messengers within the city, their flickering patterns forming a complex language that pulses through the caverns.  

Occasionally, larger, more enigmatic beings emerge—the **Root-Walkers**, colossal entities whose bodies seem to grow from the city itself. They move with a ponderous grace, their limbs trailing fibrous tendrils that knit into the ground with each step. Root-Walkers are rare, their presence heralding some great shift in the city’s metabolism—perhaps the birth of a new fungal spire or the collapse of an aging reactor.  

And finally, there are the **Silent Ones**. Few have seen them, but everyone knows they are there—nearly invisible beings whose presence is felt only as a strange hush that falls over a chamber, a ripple in the air, or the sudden stillness of a vine that had been swaying moments before. The Silent Ones are elusive, their purpose unknown, but their influence seems woven into the very fabric of Mycel.  

These inhabitants coexist in a delicate, self-sustaining symbiosis, each playing a vital role in the Root-City’s ceaseless cycle of life and decay. There is no hierarchy here, no ruler or overseer—only the city itself, a living entity whose breath is the hum of industry and whose pulse is the slow drip of water into the dark.  

Welcome to Mycel. You are not a visitor here; you are a part of its body. Step carefully, and listen to the whispers of the Spore-Keepers—they may tell you more than you wish to know.
