### **What Lives on the Plateau**  

At first glance, the plateau appears uninhabited—no creatures scurry across its damp stone, no birds circle in the thin air. But life here is not absent. It is simply *different*.  

#### **The Veilborn**  
They are not creatures of flesh, nor even of shadow. They are impressions—shapes that flicker at the edge of vision, lingering just long enough to be noticed before dissolving into the air. Some resemble humanoid figures, their outlines blurred like ink bleeding into water. Others are less defined—amorphous smudges of movement, like heat distortion given sentience.  

They do not speak, but they communicate. When one passes near, the hum of the mineral formations shifts, deepening or rising in pitch, as if responding to unseen currents. They leave no footprints, but the damp stone sometimes darkens where they pause, as if absorbing their presence.  

Their purpose is unclear. They do not hunt, nor do they flee. They simply *are*, drifting across the plateau like echoes of something long forgotten.  

#### **The Whispering Stones**  
The mineral formations are not inert. They pulse, subtly, rhythmically, as if breathing. Their jelly-like coatings ripple in response to unseen forces, and occasionally, a thin tendril of the substance will stretch outward—not quite reaching, but testing the air before retreating.  

They are not plants, nor animals, but something in between. They feed—not on organic matter, but on something else. Light, perhaps. Or sound. Or the faint tremors of the plateau itself. When the Veilborn pass near them, the formations react, their hum shifting into something almost like speech—a language of vibrations, indecipherable but deliberate.  

#### **The Hollow Echoes**  
Sometimes, the plateau *repeats*. A footstep, a breath, a whisper—not an echo, but a perfect mimicry, played back seconds or minutes later, as if the land itself remembers and replays fragments of presence. These Hollow Echoes are not sentient, but they are persistent. They linger, overlapping, until the plateau’s soundscape becomes a chorus of displaced moments.  

#### **The Watcher in the Fractures**  
There is something deeper. Something that does not move, does not hum, does not echo. It exists in the cracks—the fissures where water seeps, the hairline fractures in the stone. It is not a physical being, but a presence, vast and patient.  

When you stand still long enough, when the Veilborn have passed and the Whispering Stones have settled into their rhythm, you feel it. A pressure. A gaze. Not hostile, but *aware*. It does not interact. It only observes.  

The plateau is not empty. It is waiting.  

And whatever lives here is waiting with it.
