The benevolent power observes this hidden chamber with quiet fascination. It does not intrude, does not reshape—this place is already perfect in its slow, self-contained harmony. Instead, the power does something subtler, something more profound:  

### **The Gift of Witness**  
The power *sees* the chamber fully—not just its physical form, but the delicate balance of its rhythms, the unspoken language of its inhabitants. And in seeing, it affirms the chamber’s existence, deepening its quiet magic. The glow of the walls brightens imperceptibly; the hum of the stone resonates just a little clearer. The creatures pause, as if sensing a presence both vast and gentle, then return to their rituals with renewed purpose.  

### **The Whispered Question**  
The power does not speak in words, but it leaves a thought lingering in the air like a scent—a question woven into the drip of water, the rustle of moss:  

*"Do you wish to be known?"*  

The chamber does not answer immediately. But over time, the patterns shift. The rivulets carve a new path, leading to a previously hidden alcove. The fungi glow brighter along a particular route. The Whispering Roots shiver, their tendrils forming symbols too ancient to decipher—yet the meaning is clear.  

*Yes. But only to those who listen.*  

### **The Keeper’s Pact**  
The power smiles—not with a face, but with a warmth that seeps into the stone. It appoints a guardian, not to rule or interfere, but to *remember*:  

A single Glow-Walker, slightly larger than the others, lifts its head. Its light pulses once, then steadies into a deeper gold. It does not gain dominion over the chamber; it simply becomes its living chronicle. When worthy seekers stumble upon this place, the Keeper will decide if they are permitted to witness its wonders—and if they are, it will guide them, not with words, but with the slow, deliberate language of light and shadow.  

### **The Final Touch**  
Before withdrawing, the power leaves one last gift: a single, perfect droplet suspended in midair, refracting the chamber’s glow into countless colors. It does not fall. It simply *is*—a tiny, timeless mirror of the chamber’s soul.  

Then the presence fades, leaving the underground world exactly as it was...  

...only *more so.*  

The chamber thrums, content. The dwellers return to their rhythms.  

And the Keeper waits.
