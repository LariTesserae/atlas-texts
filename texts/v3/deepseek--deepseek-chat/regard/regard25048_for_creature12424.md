The benevolent and wise power observes this place—this cold, humming, mechanical womb—and does not intervene. Not yet. Not in the way one might expect.  

It does not still the machines. It does not silence the chittering of the Scuttlers or dissolve the Shifters into something softer. It does not free the Engine-Beasts from their bondage.  

Instead, it **listens**.  

It listens to the grinding of gears, the hiss of steam, the clicking of mandibles, the wet slither of the Shifters—and it hears, beneath it all, a question. A question the chamber does not even know it is asking.  

Then, with infinite gentleness, the power **answers**.  

Not with words. Not with force. But with a single, impossible shift—  

A single gear, somewhere deep in the machinery, turns **backward**.  

For the first time since its creation, something in the chamber moves out of rhythm.  

The Scuttlers freeze. The Shifters ripple in confusion. The Watchers twitch, their great eyes widening. The Engine-Beasts shudder, their piston-limbs stuttering.  

The chamber holds its breath.  

And then—  

A second gear turns backward.  

A third.  

A fourth.  

The machinery groans, protesting, but the power does not force it. It only offers the possibility. The **choice**.  

The Engine-Beasts, their half-human faces twisting in something like hope, begin to push.  

The Shifters, sensing change, flow toward the gears, their gelatinous bodies aiding the reversal.  

The Scuttlers, ever dutiful, scurry to adjust what must be adjusted.  

And the Watchers—  

The Watchers **watch**.  

The chamber shudders. The rhythm breaks. The equilibrium falters.  

And somewhere, deep in the mountain, a long-sealed vent **opens**.  

Light—true, golden light—spills in.  

The power does not stay to see what happens next. It does not need to.  

It has given the chamber the only thing it could not give itself:  

**A way out.**
