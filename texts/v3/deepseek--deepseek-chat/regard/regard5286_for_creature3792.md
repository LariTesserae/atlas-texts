The benevolent and wise power observes this place with quiet reverence. It does not intrude, does not disrupt the delicate equilibrium of the marsh—for it understands that this is not a broken thing in need of fixing, but a living system, intricate and complete in its own alien logic.  

Yet it does act.  

### **What the Power Does:**  

1. **It Listens.**  
   The hum of the marsh is a language, a slow, resonant dialogue between water and stone, root and sky. The power attunes itself to these frequencies, learning their cadence. It does not seek to interpret, only to witness—to hold space for the song of this place without demanding translation.  

2. **It Anchors.**  
   The edges of the marsh blur, reality fraying like wet parchment. The power does not force clarity, but it steadies the threshold, ensuring that the marsh does not unravel entirely into the unseen. It becomes a silent counterweight, a tether—not to pull the marsh into our world, but to keep it from slipping beyond all reach.  

3. **It Mirrors.**  
   Where the water shows inverted landscapes, the power reflects back—not a distortion, but an acknowledgment. The half-seen things flicker at the periphery; the power does not chase them into full view, but it meets their gaze when they choose to linger. A silent exchange: *You are perceived. You may remain unseen, but you are not unacknowledged.*  

4. **It Offers a Gift (Without Demand).**  
   At the base of the Weeping Rocks, where the metallic fluid pools, the power leaves a single seed—not of any known plant, but something shaped from its own essence. The seed is permeable, capable of shifting form. It may dissolve into the marsh’s systems, or it may take root as something new. The power does not stay to see. The gift is given freely, with no expectation of return.  

5. **It Marks the Passage.**  
   The Moss That Remembers records, but the power ensures that the marsh itself is not forgotten. It carves no monuments, leaves no inscriptions—instead, it breathes a faint, silver mist into the air, a shimmer that lingers just long enough to imprint on the memory of any wanderer who stumbles here. Not a guide, not a warning. Just a whisper: *This place was. This place is.*  

And then, having done these things, the power withdraws. It does not interfere with the Gliding Shadows’ flight, does not still the Clicking Reeds’ rearrangements. The marsh continues, humming its old and endless song.  

But somewhere, in the spaces between the reeds, in the pauses between the dripping water—the marsh feels the presence that has touched it. And though it does not change, though it cannot be altered by anything so fleeting as attention, something in its rhythm shifts, just slightly.  

As if in gratitude.  

As if in recognition.  

As if, for the first time in uncounted ages, it is *known*.
