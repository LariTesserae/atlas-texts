### **The Benevolent Intervention of the Watcher Beyond the Glass**  

A presence stirs in the unseen spaces between the obsidian plains. It is not of this world, nor is it bound by its stillness. It is something older, wiser—a being that has watched countless realms flicker into existence and fade into dust. And now, it turns its gaze upon the Glass Wastes of Vorthis.  

It does not arrive with fanfare. There is no thunderclap, no rupture in the sky. Instead, the glass itself begins to *remember*.  

### **1. The Awakening of the Shard-Kin**  
The Watcher does not force change upon the silent guardians of the wastes. Instead, it *whispers*—not in words, but in vibrations that resonate through the glass, harmonizing with the hum of the city.  

The Shard-Kin, who have moved in silent servitude for eons, pause. Their forms ripple, their crystalline bodies catching new refractions of light. For the first time, their hums take on *variation*—notes of curiosity, of questioning. They turn their faceless heads toward one another, sensing something *different* in the air.  

The Watcher does not command them to act. It merely reminds them that they *can*.  

### **2. The Glasswights Begin to Glow**  
The small, skittering creatures that cling to the Shard-Kin’s feet have always been dim, their inner light faint. But now, their carapaces brighten—soft blues, greens, and golds pulsing in time with the new vibrations in the glass.  

They do not understand what is happening. But they *feel* it.  

Some of them break away from their usual paths, venturing farther than they ever have before. A few even approach the city’s edge, their tiny forms casting trembling reflections on the obsidian towers.  

### **3. The Harbingers Stir**  
The great crystalline entities that emerge from the city’s depths do not often walk the wastes. But now, one rises—its form shifting, unstable, as if caught between two states of being.  

It does not move with purpose. It moves with *uncertainty*.  

The Watcher does not impose will upon it. Instead, it offers a question, embedded in the hum of the glass:  

***What do you choose?***  

The Harbinger pauses. Then, for the first time in eternity, it *changes direction*.  

### **4. The City’s Machinery Shifts**  
The distant hum has always been constant, unchanging. But now, it falters—just for a moment. A single gear grinds to a halt. A pulse of energy flickers through the glass like a heartbeat.  

The Watcher does not dismantle the machinery. It does not force the city to wake. It simply reminds the glass that there are other rhythms, other possibilities.  

And for the first time, the city *hesitates*.  

### **5. The Land Remembers**  
The Glass Wastes have been still for so long that even the concept of movement is foreign. But now, the obsidian plains ripple—not from some external force, but from within.  

The glass *remembers* that it was once something else.  

A single crack forms in the endless expanse. Not a flaw, but a *breath*.  

### **The Watcher’s Gift**  
The presence does not stay. It does not reshape the wastes into something they are not. It does not force life where there was none, nor does it demand that the Shard-Kin, the Glasswights, or the Harbingers become anything other than what they are.  

It simply reminds them that they *exist*.  

And in that reminder, something shifts.  

The Glass Wastes do not become a thriving paradise. The city does not crumble into ruin or burst into light. The inhabitants do not suddenly speak or weep or laugh.  

But now, when the wind whispers across the glass, it carries a new note—one that was not there before.  

A note of *possibility*.  

And that is enough.
