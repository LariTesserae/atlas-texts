**The Benevolent Watcher**  

A presence stirs in the unseen currents of the air, in the slow drip of water down rusted channels, in the hum of buried machinery. It is neither wholly of this place nor entirely separate from it—an intelligence vast and patient, older than the spires, older than the first vines that crept into the cracks of the metal mountains. It has watched the Rustwalkers for centuries, their quiet symbiosis with the world they inhabit, their reverence for decay and renewal.  

And now, for the first time, it decides to act.  

---

### **The Whisper in the Walls**  

The Echo-Seekers are the first to notice. The vibrations in the metal change—no longer the same steady, distant thrum of forgotten engines, but something more deliberate. A pattern. A voice.  

At first, they do not dare speak of it. They press their hands to the warm, rust-streaked surfaces and listen, their breath stilled. The message is not in words, not in any language they know, but in *feeling*—a pulse of warmth, of recognition. A question, unspoken but understood:  

*Do you wish to know?*  

The eldest among them, a woman whose veins gleam like copper under her lichen-patched skin, exhales sharply. "It speaks," she murmurs. "Not just echoes. It *speaks*."  

The Rustwalkers gather, uncertain. They have always listened to the past. But this—this is something alive.  

---

### **The Gift of the Deep**  

Days pass. The voice does not fade. Instead, it grows clearer, guiding them—not with commands, but with gentle nudges. A vine, thick and unfamiliar, begins to grow along a seldom-used catwalk, its leaves broad and shimmering with an oily sheen. When touched, they yield a thick, sweet sap that heals wounds faster than any poultice they have ever known.  

A child, feverish and weak, is brought to the vine. The sap is pressed to her lips. By dawn, she is running through the tunnels again, laughing.  

The Rustwalkers do not name the presence. They do not worship it. But they begin to leave offerings—not sacrifices, but *gifts*: a carefully woven tapestry of vines, a polished shard of metal that catches the light just so, a song hummed into the hollows of the structures where the echoes linger longest.  

The presence accepts these gladly.  

---

### **The Unfolding**  

Then, one evening, the eldest Echo-Seeker does not return from her listening. They find her at the base of the tallest spire, her body curled as if in sleep, her face peaceful. But her hands—her hands are buried in the metal itself, as though the structure had softened like wet clay beneath her touch.  

When they pull her free, the metal reshapes instantly, smooth and unbroken. But in her palm, she clutches something new: a seed, small and cold, its surface etched with fine, geometric patterns.  

They plant it in a basin of rainwater and rust. By morning, a slender shoot has risen, its stem gleaming like burnished steel, its leaves unfolding with a sound like whispering metal.  

The presence does not explain. It does not need to.  

The Rustwalkers understand.  

This is not an ending.  

It is a beginning.
