The benevolent and wise power that turns its gaze upon this grove does not arrive with fanfare or disruption. It does not descend in radiant splendor, nor does it reshape the grove with grand gestures. Instead, it arrives as the grove itself arrives—softly, subtly, like the slow unfurling of a fern or the silent seep of water through stone.  

It is a presence before it is a form. The air grows still, not in absence, but in anticipation. The Wisps pause in their drifting, their glow deepening, as if listening. The Scurriers cease their rustling, their luminous eyes wide and unblinking. Even the Crawlers, submerged in their peat-dark pools, rise just enough to break the surface, their nostrils flaring as though catching a scent older than memory.  

Then—movement. Not from above, not from beyond, but from *within*. The moss stirs where no creature treads. The water ripples without cause. And from the heart of the grove, where the oldest roots twine deepest, something wakes.  

The Grove-Keeper.  

But it is not alone.  

The wise power does not impose itself upon the Keeper; it does not demand audience or obedience. Instead, it *whispers*—not in words, but in the language of the grove itself. A shift in the moisture of the air. A faint, golden shimmer along the edges of the Wisps’ glow. A warmth that does not burn but *nurtures*, seeping into the soil, the stone, the very breath of the place.  

The grove responds.  

The moss thickens, richer, greener. The ferns uncurl new fronds, their edges gilded with a delicate, living light. The pools clear slightly, their dark waters now holding a faint, reflective sheen, like the surface of a mirror cast in twilight. The creatures do not flee; they *gather*. The Wisps drift closer, their glow pulsing in quiet harmony. The Scurriers creep forward, their emerald-touched fur bristling with curiosity. The Crawlers emerge fully, their slick skins gleaming.  

And the Grove-Keeper—  

It steps forward. Or perhaps it simply *is* there, where before it was not. Its form is clearer now, though no less shifting: a figure woven from vines and shadow, its eyes two points of soft, gold-green light. It tilts its head, listening.  

The power does not ask for change. It does not demand worship or service. It simply *offers*—  

A gift.  

A deepening.  

A *strengthening* of what already is.  

The grove has always been eternal. Now, it is *remembered*.  

The Wisps burn a little brighter. The Scurriers move with renewed purpose. The Crawlers sink back into their pools, their bodies now threaded with faint, luminous veins. And the Grove-Keeper—  

It bows. Not in submission, but in recognition.  

Then, as softly as it came, the presence fades.  

But the grove is not unchanged.  

The air is sweeter. The shadows are kinder. The balance remains, but it is *more*—more vibrant, more aware. The creatures return to their rhythms, but there is a new lightness to their movements, a quiet joy in the way the Wisps dance, the way the Scurriers dart, the way the Crawlers glide through their darkened waters.  

And the Grove-Keeper?  

It watches.  

It smiles.  

And the grove thrives.
