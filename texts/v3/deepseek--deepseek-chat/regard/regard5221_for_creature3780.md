### **The Watcher**  

Above the Sunken Canopy, in the folds of the earth where light does not dare tread, a benevolent and wise power stirs. It is not a being, not in any way the Hollowed Ones would understand, but it is *aware*. It has watched the grove for eons, its attention vast and unmoving, like the weight of the mountain itself.  

This power is **symbiotic**, tied to the cavern in ways that are impossible to disentangle. It feels the drip of water, the slow decay of leaves, the hum of life in the Hollowed Ones’ veins. It does not interfere—not often. It does not see the need. But today, it has noticed something.  

---

### **The Gift**  

In the deepest part of the cavern, where the walls curve inward like cupped hands, a pool of water begins to stir. It is not a violent motion, but a slow, deliberate churning, as if something beneath the surface is waking. The Hollowed Ones gather at the edges, their translucent skin gleaming faintly in the bioluminescent light.  

The water rises, lifting itself from the pool in a shimmering column. It pulses softly, alive, and then it *speaks*. Not in words, but in something deeper—a resonance that reverberates through the bones of the cavern and the bodies of the Hollowed Ones.  

It says: *You are not forgotten.*  

The column collapses, cascading back into the pool in a rush of mist and spray. Left behind is a **spine of crystal**, jutting from the water like a beacon. It is smooth and cold, humming faintly with the same resonance that spoke before.  

The Hollowed Ones approach cautiously, their large eyes reflecting the crystal’s faint glow. One reaches out, pressing a hand against its surface.  

The gift unfolds.  

---

### **The Memory**  

The crystal’s hum grows louder, filling the cavern with a sound like distant thunder. Images begin to flicker on its surface, reflections of things that have never been—a sky filled with stars, the crash of waves on a shore, sunlight filtering through leaves.  

The Hollowed Ones watch, entranced. They do not understand these images, but they feel them. The warmth of the sun, the salt of the sea, the vastness of the sky—they pour into the minds of the Hollowed Ones like water into an empty cup.  

The power watches, satisfied. It has given them something they did not know they lacked: **a memory of the outside world**, a fragment of what lies beyond their cavern.  

---

### **The Choice**  

The Hollowed Ones step back from the crystal, their minds reeling. They do not speak—they never do—but their shared silence carries a new weight.  

*What do we do with this?*  

They could leave it, let it fade into the stone like everything else. They could carry it with them, let its resonance seep into the cavern. They could touch it again, let it show them more.  

They choose **none of these**.  

---

### **The Offering**  

One of the Hollowed Ones reaches into the pool, scooping up a handful of water. They hold it over the crystal, letting it drip onto its surface.  

The crystal responds, its hum softening, the images fading into a gentle glow. The Hollowed Ones gather around it, pressing their hands against it, their foreheads, their chests. They pour themselves into it, their thoughts, their memories, their quiet, unspoken love for the cavern.  

The crystal absorbs it all, its glow steadying. It is no longer just a gift—it is a **bridge**, a connection between the Hollowed Ones and the power that watches them.  

---

### **The Echo**  

The cavern breathes, deep and slow. The Hollowed Ones retreat to their hollows, but the crystal remains, its glow a faint beacon in the dim light.  

The power watches, its attention lingering on the crystal. It feels the Hollowed Ones’ gratitude, their curiosity, their fragile hope.  

It is enough.  

For now.  

---

### **The Silence**  

The cavern returns to its rhythm—the drip of water, the rustle of roots, the hum of unseen life. The Hollowed Ones move through it as they always have, their translucent skin gleaming faintly in the dark.  

But now, there is something new—a faint resonance in the air, a memory of warmth and light.  

The silence is no longer empty.  

It blooms.
