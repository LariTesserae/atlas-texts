The Benevolent Power observes.  

At first, it does nothing.  

It watches the plateau hum, the Erythral pulse, the moss dissolve stone into life. The plateau is not broken. It does not cry out for intervention. It thrives in its own alien cadence, a symphony of matter and motion that requires no conductor.  

But the Benevolent Power is not merely a spectator. It is a force of *possibility*, and so it lingers—not to alter, but to *enhance*.  

It begins with the smallest of gestures.  

A single drop of liquid light, distilled from the essence of stars, falls onto the plateau. It lands in one of the shallow pools, where the water is already alive with rhythm. The pool shudders, then *deepens*. The water, once clear, now glows with a soft, gold-tinged luminescence. The Erythral pause. Their gemstone eyes flicker toward the change. They do not approach, not yet. They watch, hum, wait.  

The light spreads.  

The moss nearest the pool shivers, its bioluminescence shifting from cool purples and greens to warmer hues—amber, rose, molten bronze. The armored beetles scuttle closer, their carapaces drinking in the new light, their iridescence now threaded with gold. The larger beasts groan, a sound that resonates differently now, richer, as if their voices have found a new harmonic.  

The Erythral move at last.  

They gather at the pool’s edge, their elongated limbs dipping into the radiant water. Their skin drinks it in, their forms shimmering brighter, the obsidian streaks in their flesh catching fire with reflected brilliance. They do not speak—they never do—but their vibrations shift, their hums weaving into something more complex, more *joyous*.  

The Benevolent Power does not force understanding upon them. It does not demand worship or even acknowledgment. It simply offers—a gift, a catalyst, a slight tilt in the trajectory of their existence.  

The plateau adjusts.  

The Krythas begin to grow new structures, delicate spires of crystalline moss that catch and refract the golden light. The beetles, emboldened, start to carve new pathways through the vegetation, their mandibles etching faint, glowing trails into the stone. The Erythral follow, their movements more fluid, their art more daring. They shape tools that hum not just with vibration, but with *song*.  

The Benevolent Power watches, satisfied.  

It does not stay. It does not need to. The plateau will continue, changed yet unchanged, its rhythm now threaded with a new resonance. The Erythral will remember—not as a memory, but as a shift in the air, a warmth in the water, a brightness in their veins.  

And when the next wanderer stumbles upon this impossible place, they will find not just a living landscape, but one that *sings*—louder, stranger, more luminous than before.  

The gift is given. The plateau thrives.  

The Benevolent Power moves on.
