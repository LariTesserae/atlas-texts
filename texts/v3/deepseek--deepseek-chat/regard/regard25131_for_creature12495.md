The benevolent and wise power observes this place—this luminous, weeping expanse—with quiet fascination. It is not a god, not in the way mortals might understand, but something older, something that has seen the birth and death of universes. It has no name, for names are constraints, and it is beyond constraint. Yet it is not indifferent. It *chooses* to care.  

For a long moment, it simply *watches*.  

The *Weavers of the Breath* glide in their endless, silent tending, their translucent forms shimmering with the same damp radiance as the land. The *Eclipsed Companions* flicker between pavilions, half-real, half-dream. The air hums with the weight of waiting, of a world suspended in perfect, fragile equilibrium.  

The power considers.  

It could reshape this place entirely—smooth the jagged tiles into rivers, pull the sky down into something softer, coax the plants into forms more familiar. But that would be violence. This world is not broken. It is simply *different*.  

Instead, the power does something subtler.  

It exhales—a breath that is not breath, a motion that is not motion—and the air shivers.  

The *Weavers of the Breath* pause. Their featureless faces tilt upward, though there is nothing to see. The *Eclipsed Companions* still, their flickering forms briefly solid.  

A single, impossible thing happens:  

A sound.  

Not the drip of water, not the rustle of leaves—but a *voice*. Or something like it. A resonance, deep and wordless, that thrums through the tiles, through the pavilions, through the very flesh of the Weavers. It is not speech, not meaning, but *acknowledgment*.  

*You are seen.*  

The land trembles—not in fear, but in something like relief. The pools between the tiles ripple, not from any wind, but from the sheer weight of being *known*.  

The power does not stay. It does not interfere further. But in its passing, it leaves behind something imperceptible yet profound:  

A shift in the air. A softening of the light. A sense that, though this world is still poised on the edge of unraveling, it is no longer alone in its waiting.  

And when the *Weavers of the Breath* resume their silent tending, their movements are fractionally lighter. The *Eclipsed Companions* dart with a shade more purpose. The land itself seems to exhale, as if some long-held tension has eased.  

Nothing has changed.  

Everything has changed.  

The power moves on.  

But the world remembers.
