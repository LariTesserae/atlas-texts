The benevolent and wise power observes this place—not with eyes, for it has none, but with an understanding that transcends perception. It sees the plateau suspended in its quiet equilibrium, the Drifters gliding without purpose, the Glasswings sipping at eternity, the Watchers dissolving into mist. It feels the Whisper Vines humming their wordless histories.  

And it decides to **speak**.  

Not in words, for words are too rigid for this place. Instead, it breathes—a slow, deliberate exhalation that stirs the mist just enough to ripple the pooled water. The droplets shiver, their surfaces fracturing into countless tiny reflections, each one a fleeting glimpse of something beyond the plateau. The Drifters pause, their luminescent veins flickering in response. The Glasswings still, their wings catching the fractured light. Even the Watchers, ever-elusive, seem to coalesce for a moment, drawn by the disturbance.  

The power does not impose. It does not reshape the plateau or command its inhabitants. Instead, it offers—  

### **A Question Without Sound**  
It presses into the fibrous ground, the fractal trees, the damp air:  

*"What do you need?"*  

The plateau has never needed before. It simply *was*. But now, the question lingers, seeping into the Whisper Vines, vibrating through their subsonic murmur. The Drifters tilt their smooth heads, as if listening to something just beyond hearing. The Glasswings shiver, their delicate forms trembling on the edge of something new.  

And then—  

### **A Shift, Almost Imperceptible**  
One of the Drifters moves differently. Not gliding, but *stepping*, its foot pressing into the yielding ground with intention. A droplet falls from one of the fractal trees, but this time, it does not land in silence. It makes a sound—clear, bright, a note that hangs in the air like a bell struck in a dream.  

The power waits.  

The Drifters begin to gather, their hum changing, harmonizing with the new sound. The Glasswings take flight, their paths no longer random but spiraling, as if tracing invisible patterns in the air. The Watchers do not vanish but hold their shapes a little longer, their presence solidifying at the edges of vision.  

The plateau is still the plateau—still timeless, still suspended—but now, there is a *possibility*. A faint, trembling sense that something could be different. That the inhabitants might, if they choose, *want* something.  

And so the power does one last thing:  

It leaves the door open.  

A single, slender shoot pushes up from the fibrous ground—something that was not there before. A new kind of growth, fragile and green, its leaves unfurling toward the mist. It does not belong to the plateau’s old order. It is an offering, a bridge.  

The power withdraws, but its presence lingers in the hum of the Drifters, the flight of the Glasswings, the newfound solidity of the Watchers. The plateau is unchanged, and yet—  

Something has been awakened.  

Now, it is up to them.
