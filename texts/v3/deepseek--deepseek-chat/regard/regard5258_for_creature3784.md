Imagine a benevolent and wise power arriving at the Glassworks of the Shattered Sky. Let’s call this being **The Tender of Wholeness**, a cosmic force attuned to renewal and equilibrium, whose presence is felt more as a vibration in the air than a visible form.  

---

The Tender arrives silently, though the sky trembles faintly as if acknowledging its presence. Cracks in the dome shimmer briefly, like veins of liquid light, before returning to their fractured state. The Architects pause in their endless adjustments. They do not turn to face the Tender, but their glass panes flicker with refractions of its essence—a spectrum of colors that defy the muted palette of the plateau.  

The Tender moves not in steps but in *phases*, its presence expanding and contracting like a heartbeat. It pauses before a Sky Gazer, whose silver threads tremble in recognition. The Tender reaches out—an indistinct limb that seems to dissolve and reform—and touches the thread. The Gazer’s head snaps upright for the first time, its featureless face tilting toward the cracks above. A sound escapes its slit—a low, resonant hum that reverberates through the plateau.  

The Architects begin to gather, their glass bodies refracting the Tender’s light into patterns that ripple across the ground. They do not interfere, but their movements become more deliberate, as if anticipating something monumental.  

The Tender moves to the center of the plateau, where the latticework is most dense. It kneels—or perhaps *descends*—and presses its form into the metal. At first, nothing happens. Then, slowly, the rust begins to recede, the plates knitting themselves together. Collapsed beams rise into place, their surfaces polished to a mirror finish.  

But this is not mere restoration. The Tender is weaving something new into the fabric of this place—a thread of *balance*.  

The Clockwork Ravens descend in unison, their brass wings creating a rhythmic clicking that harmonizes with the plateau’s groans. They circle the Tender, their lens-like eyes focusing and unfocusing, capturing something unseen.  

The Dripborn emerge from their hiding places, their translucent bodies glowing faintly as they absorb the Tender’s light. They begin to sing—not their usual sizzling tones, but a chorus of soft, resonant notes that fill the air with a sense of calm.  

The things beneath the plates stir, but not in agitation. For the first time, their movements feel deliberate, almost *intentional*. A section of the latticework shifts, and something dark and fluid rises to the surface—a shadow given form. It hovers before the Tender, its edges bleeding into the air.  

The Tender extends a hand—or something like a hand—and the shadow dissolves, merging with the renewed latticework. The plateau sighs, a deep, resonant sound that echoes through the structures and walkways.  

The Tender rises, its form shimmering with iridescence. It moves to the edge of the plateau, where the Sky Gazers stand. One by one, their threads detach from the sky, retracting into their featureless faces. The cracks above begin to heal, the fissures sealing themselves with a faint, golden light.  

The Tender turns to face the Architects, who bow—their glass panes refracting a final burst of color before returning to their muted tones. The Tender’s presence begins to fade, its light dissolving into the air.  

When it is gone, the plateau is not the same. The cracks in the sky have narrowed, the groaning beneath the plates has quieted. The Dripborn sing softly as they resume their scavenging. The Clockwork Ravens return to their roosts, their brass wings clicking in unison.  

The Architects return to their work, but their adjustments are less frantic, their movements more deliberate.  

And the Sky Gazers? They stand at the edges of the plateau, their heads tilted upward once more. But now, their featureless faces seem less like blank slates and more like soft reflections of the mended sky.  

The Glassworks of the Shattered Sky was built to last.  

Now, perhaps, it has been built to heal.
