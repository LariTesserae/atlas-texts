The benevolent and wise power arrives not with fanfare but as a subtle shift in the air, a softening of the shadows, a warmth that seeps into the moss and stone like a breath long held. It does not announce itself, for it does not need to. The gorge knows. The Watchers know. Even the tiny, many-legged creatures pause in their ceaseless skittering, sensing something vast and ancient settle into the depths of the earth.

This power is not a being but a presence, an intelligence older than the Watchers themselves. It is the touch of creation and the whisper of renewal, a quiet force that seeks neither dominion nor worship but simply *is*. It moves through the gorge like a current, stirring the mist, caressing the lichen, and lingering at the edges of the settlement where the people mark their fear and reverence with offerings.

The presence pauses at the mouth of the gorge, where the herbs and tokens lie in neat, humble piles. It considers them—not with judgment but with curiosity, as though understanding for the first time the fragility of mortal attempts to connect with the unknowable. It does not consume the offerings; instead, it imbues them with a faint, golden light, a warmth that lingers long after the presence has passed.

Inside the gorge, the presence pauses again, this time before the Watchers. They emerge from the shadows, their too-long limbs fluid and graceful, their moss-crowned heads tilted in silent acknowledgment. The presence does not speak, but it communicates nonetheless—a soft pressure in the minds of the Watchers, a question that is also an answer.

"What are you waiting for?"

The Watchers do not respond immediately. They tilt their heads further, their slit-like pupils narrowing as they consider the presence. Finally, one of them steps forward, its mossy hair trembling faintly. It extends a hand, palm up, and an image forms in the air between them—a vision of the gorge expanding, deepening, its walls cracking further to reveal something vast and luminous beneath the earth.

The presence understands. It nods, though it has no form to nod with, and moves deeper into the gorge. At the bottom, where the slow-moving water whispers against the stone, the presence kneels—though it has no knees—and presses its awareness into the earth. The gorge shudders, a deep, resonant rumble that echoes through the rock and into the bones of the Watchers and the people above.

Then, slowly, the gorge begins to change. The cracks widen, not violently but with a deliberate, almost reverent slowness. The walls shift, revealing veins of luminous crystal that pulse with a soft, golden light. The water at the bottom deepens, its whisper growing into a murmur, then a song—a low, melodic hum that fills the gorge and rises into the air.

The Watchers watch, their eyes unblinking, their mossy hair trembling with something that might be awe. The presence remains at the heart of the gorge, its essence mingling with the stone and water, shaping the land with a gentle, inexorable will.

Above, the people feel the change. They gather at the edge of the gorge, their tools clutched tightly in their hands, their faces pale with fear and wonder. They see the light rising from the depths, hear the song that seems to come from the earth itself. They do not understand, but they feel—deep in their chests—that something sacred is unfolding.

The presence does not stay. Its work is done, or perhaps it has only begun. It rises from the gorge, its essence flowing upward like mist into the sky. As it departs, it leaves behind a single, luminous seed, hovering gently above the water. The Watchers approach it cautiously, their too-many fingers reaching out to cradle it. The seed pulses once, twice, then sinks into the earth.

The gorge sighs—a sound of contentment, of anticipation. The Watchers return to their shadows, their mossy heads bowed in what might be gratitude. The people above leave their offerings, their fear transmuted into something softer, something hopeful.

The gorge watches. The gorge breathes. The gorge is no longer waiting. It is becoming.
