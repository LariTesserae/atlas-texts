The benevolence arrives as a whisper, a soft exhale that stirs the Luminae into a brief, luminous frenzy. It is not a presence that announces itself with fanfare or grandeur, but one that seeps into the Hollows like the water itself—gentle, pervasive, inevitable. This power is not bound by form or time, yet it chooses to manifest in a way that the Hollows and its inhabitants can perceive: a figure cloaked in iridescent mist, its edges dissolving into the damp air, its eyes pools of serene, knowing light.

The figure moves through the Hollows with the cadence of a hymn, its steps leaving no mark on the spongy moss. It pauses before the Luminae, its hand—or what might be a hand—hovering over their glowing clusters. The Luminae respond with a slow, deliberate pulse, their light deepening to a rich, warm hue. The figure smiles—not with lips, but with the air itself—and moves on.

It finds the Veilwalkers drifting aimlessly in the shadows, their ethereal forms flickering like dying embers. The figure extends a tendril of mist, and the Veilwalkers turn toward it, their movements no longer aimless but purposeful. They follow, not as lost souls but as attendants, their chill tempered by the figure’s warmth.

The Stoneknit Wyrms feel its approach before they see it, their bodies halting mid-burrow. The figure kneels—or perhaps it simply bends—and runs its hand along the rough surface of the largest Wyrm. A soft glow spreads from its touch, and the Wyrm shudders, its mucus-secretions crystallizing into intricate, opalescent patterns that catch the faint light. The Wyrm emits a low, resonant hum of gratitude before resuming its work, its movements infused with newfound vigor.

The Whisper Ferns greet the figure with a chorus of soft, melodic hums, their leaves unfurling to reveal veins of bioluminescent light. The figure replies in kind, its voice a blend of wind and water, and the ferns sway in response, their roots intertwining more deeply with the fungal network beneath the soil. The Hollows seem to shudder—not in fear, but in pleasure—as the connection between land and life grows stronger.

The Echoers, high in their shadowed alcoves, fall silent as the figure approaches. Then, one by one, they begin to sing—not their usual haunting melodies, but a harmony that resonates with the land’s slow, eternal rhythm. The figure listens, its form shimmering in time with the music, and the Echoers’ voices grow brighter, their songs weaving through the Hollows like threads of light.

The Mosskin scatter as the figure draws near, their tiny bodies blending into the moss. But the figure crouches, its presence inviting rather than commanding, and the Mosskin hesitantly return, their movements less frantic, their odor less sour. They gather around the figure, their velvety bodies brushing against it, and for the first time, they seem content rather than skittish.

Finally, the figure approaches the deepest pools, where the Drowned Ones lurk in their timeless silence. The waters ripple as the figure kneels at the edge, its reflection distorted by the bioluminescent glow beneath the surface. The Drowned Ones rise, their translucent bodies pulsing faintly, their lidless eyes fixed on the figure. It speaks—not in words, but in vibrations that resonate through the water and stone. The Drowned Ones respond with a series of guttural croaks, their tone less lonely, less mournful. They sink back into the depths, their vigil renewed, but no longer solitary.

The figure rises, its form dissolving into mist, and the Hollows seem to sigh—a slow, steady exhalation that echoes through the stone corridors. The Luminae glow brighter, the Veilwalkers drift with purpose, the Stoneknit Wyrms carve new patterns, the Whisper Ferns hum in harmony, the Echoers’ songs grow richer, the Mosskin scurry with renewed vigor, and the Drowned Ones watch their pools with calm resolve.

The benevolent power does not linger, but its influence remains, woven into the fabric of the Hollows. The land is no longer weeping, but content—its slow, eternal rhythm undisturbed, its life thriving in delicate balance. The Hollows, once a place of quiet decay, is now a sanctuary of quiet renewal. And though the figure is gone, its presence lingers in the damp air, in the glowing lichen, in the soft hum of the Whisper Ferns, in the slow drip of water from the ancient stone. 

The Hollows breathe, and it is enough.
