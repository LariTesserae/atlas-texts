A benevolent and wise power observes the **Basin of Hollow Echoes**. It is neither a deity nor an intrusive force but an embodiment of renewal and understanding, drawn to the basin’s quiet despair and its inhabitants’ muted existence. This power, called **Eleris**, manifests as a soft, silver light that spills into the basin from the twilight sky, gentle and unobtrusive. Eleris does not seek to dominate or transform but to heal and awaken what has been forgotten.  

---

### **Eleris Approaches**  
Eleris drifts into the basin on a current of wind, its light pooling in the hollows of the stone, gathering in the mosses and ferns, and tracing the black river’s surface. The basin reacts subtly—the echoes grow softer, as if the land is listening, and the air feels warmer, though the twilight remains unchanged. Eleris does not announce itself but begins to act, its presence felt rather than seen.  

---

### **What Eleris Does**  

#### **1. The Mosskin Awaken**  
Eleris approaches the Mosskin first, drawn to their silent tending. It touches them gently, its light seeping into their mossy forms. Slowly, their hunched postures straighten, and their featureless faces begin to shift—soft contours emerge, hints of individuality. They stop their ritualistic tasks and turn their hollow eye sockets toward Eleris, humming a question.  

Eleris does not speak but imparts understanding: *You are more than caretakers. You are memory.*  

The Mosskin begin to remember—not their own pasts, but the basin’s. Their hums grow louder, weaving together into a song that echoes through the hollows, carrying fragments of forgotten stories.  

#### **2. The Clickers Still**  
The Clickers, sensing the change, emerge from their tunnels, their translucent bodies glistening in Eleris’s light. They are restless, their mandibles clicking incessantly, but Eleris calms them with a soothing pulse.  

The light filters into their carapaces, softening their predatory instincts. They retreat into the stone, not to hunt but to integrate, their clicking now a rhythm that harmonizes with the Mosskin’s song.  

#### **3. The Leeches Glow Brighter**  
The Leeches in the river respond to Eleris’s presence with ripples of bioluminescence, their bodies glowing brighter than ever. Eleris encourages them, enhancing their role as purifiers. The black water begins to clear slightly, its sluggish flow gaining a hint of vitality.  

#### **4. The Forgotten Find Peace**  
In the ruins, the spectral figures of the Forgotten pause mid-motion. Eleris’s light fills the empty spaces between them, dissolving their aimless routines. One by one, they turn toward the Mist and fade, their echoes finally released.  

#### **5. The Whisperers Sing**  
The Whisperers, curious and playful, mimic Eleris’s light, weaving it into their echoes. Their sounds grow softer, kinder, no longer drawing intruders deeper into the basin but guiding them toward understanding.  

---

### **The Basin’s Transformation**  
Eleris does not change the basin’s physical form—the twilight sky remains, the damp stone persists, and the black river still flows. But its essence shifts. The air feels less heavy, the echoes less lonely. The Mosskin’s song fills the hollows, a living reminder of the basin’s past and present.  

Eleris departs as quietly as it arrived, its light fading into the twilight. The basin is not healed—it is still a place of decay—but it is no longer forgotten. It has been acknowledged, its whispers given voice, its pulse strengthened.  

And sometimes, on the edge of hearing, the Mosskin’s song rises, carried on the wind, a reminder that even in the hollowness, there is life.
