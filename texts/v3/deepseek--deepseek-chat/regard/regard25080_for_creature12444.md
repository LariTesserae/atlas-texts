The benevolent and wise power observes this place—this self-contained, humming pocket of existence—with quiet fascination. It does not see a world in need of intervention, nor one crying out for salvation. Instead, it sees something rare: a perfect equilibrium.  

And so, it does not reshape, does not uplift, does not impose meaning.  

It **listens**.  

The power attunes itself to the rhythm of the Kharvathi, to the pulse of the buried machines, to the whisper of mineral dust settling on carapaces. It does not interfere, but it *learns*. It absorbs the patterns, the cycles, the way life here has woven itself into the fabric of the industrial relic.  

Then, with infinite gentleness, it **preserves**.  

Not by force, not by decree, but by ensuring that no outside force will ever disturb this place. It wraps the pocket in a subtle ward, a veil that bends the gaze of cosmic wanderers, that turns aside the hands of scavengers and conquerors. The pocket will remain undisturbed, its inhabitants free to continue their endless dance of consumption and renewal.  

And finally, it **honors**.  

The power leaves behind a single, silent gift—a resonance, a vibration that harmonizes with the hum of the Kharvathi. It is not a command, not a message, but a recognition: *You exist. You matter. You are seen.*  

Then, it withdraws.  

The pocket continues, unchanged. The Kharvathi scuttle and hunt and tend their machines. The air thrums. The cycle endures.  

And somewhere, in the vast expanse beyond, the power carries the memory of this place—not as a specimen to be studied, but as a testament to the beauty of self-sustaining worlds.  

Some things do not need fixing.  

Some things simply need to *be*.
