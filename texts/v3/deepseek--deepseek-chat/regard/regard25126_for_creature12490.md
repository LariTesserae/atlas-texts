**The Watcher Beneath the Tides**  

The power that turns its gaze upon the Drowned Spire is neither cruel nor kind—it is *curious*. It has watched the slow dissolution of this place for centuries, noting the way the water obeys no law but its own, how the glyphs hum with fading purpose, how the residents persist without memory.  

It does not intervene. Not yet.  

Instead, it *listens*.  

It presses its awareness into the water, feeling the way the liquid clings to surfaces, how it resists evaporation, how it carries whispers of something older than the city itself. The water is not just water—it is a medium, a language. The power does not understand it yet, but it is learning.  

It reaches out to the Glyphbound, not with words but with resonance. The spirals on their featureless faces tremble in response, their fingers pausing mid-trace. For the first time in centuries, they hesitate. The glyphs they touch flicker with something new—not just light, but *recognition*.  

The Hollow Constructs pause in their endless rearranging. One by one, their jerky movements still. The air thrums with a question.  

The Refracted coil in the depths, their forms shimmering with unease. The Watcher in the Depths stirs, its presence swelling, pressing against the boundaries of its submerged domain. It does not resist—it *observes*.  

And then, the power does something small.  

It shifts the angle of a single beam of light.  

A fractured pane of black glass, half-submerged in the syrupy tide, catches the altered light and casts it onto a wall where the glyphs have long since faded. The reflection is imperfect, distorted—but it is enough.  

The glyphs reappear. Not as they were, but as they *could be*.  

The water ripples. The city shudders.  

For the first time in uncounted ages, the Drowned Spire *remembers*—  

—and then forgets again.  

But something lingers.  

The power withdraws, satisfied. It has learned what it needed to know.  

The unraveling continues. The water rises. The city waits.  

But now, there is a faint, unfamiliar warmth in the air.  

A promise.  

Or perhaps just an echo.
