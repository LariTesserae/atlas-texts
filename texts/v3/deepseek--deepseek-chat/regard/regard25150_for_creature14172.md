The benevolent and wise power observes this place—not as an outsider, but as a presence already woven into its fabric. It does not intrude, for intrusion would be violence here, where every motion is a kind of communion. Instead, it *listens*—to the hum of the Architects, to the scuttle-trails of the Skittering Ones, to the slow wingbeats of the Drifters—and understands.  

This power is not a god, not a force that bends the world to its will. It is something quieter, subtler. A *tenderness* made manifest.  

### **What It Does**  
1. **It Touches the Light**  
   The dim glow of the bioluminescent stalks deepens, not in brightness but in *richness*, as if the light has remembered something forgotten. The hollow’s shadows grow softer at their edges, no longer so sharp, so final. The Drifters tilt their smooth faces toward the shift, their wings catching the new warmth as they pass through it.  

2. **It Leaves a Gift in the Cracks**  
   Along the mineral earth, where the Skittering Ones trace their luminous paths, something new emerges—tiny, glass-like nodules, no larger than seeds. They are not of this place, but they belong here. When the Skittering Ones brush against them, the nodules dissolve into faint, shimmering mist, and the creatures pause, their bodies flickering in response. A kind of recognition. A kind of joy.  

3. **It Sings Back**  
   The Architects, who shape the world in silence, feel a resonance beneath their hands—a vibration that matches the hum of their own being. It is not an answer, not an echo, but a *harmony*. One of them—perhaps the one nearest the grove’s heart—lifts its featureless face and *presses* its palm to the ground. The city shivers, just once, in reply.  

4. **It Does Not Stay**  
   The power does not linger. It is not its place to remain, to alter, to demand. It has woven itself into the hollow’s rhythm, and now it withdraws—not as a departure, but as a breath released. The Drifters spiral a little closer to the ground in its wake. The Skittering Ones trace brighter, more intricate patterns. The light lingers, warmer than before.  

And the hollow continues, as it always has, as it always will—but now, somewhere in its silent pulse, there is a new note.  

A kindness, folded into the geometry of the world.
