A **benevolent and wise power** observes this place—this writhing, primordial expanse—with neither pity nor urgency, but with a quiet understanding. It recognizes the beauty in the chaos, the necessity of erosion, the poetry of dissolution. Yet, it also sees the suffering—the way the land weeps, the way the inhabitants flicker in and out of existence like half-formed thoughts. 

This power does not seek to *fix* the place, for it is not broken. It does not seek to *order* the place, for its chaos is sacred. Instead, it does something far more profound: it **listens**. 

### **What the Power Does:**

1. **It Becomes a Stillness in the Storm**  
   The power does not impose itself. It does not manifest as a blazing light or a booming voice. Instead, it settles into the landscape like a deep breath—a single point of quietude amidst the cacophony. The mist parts slightly around it, not in reverence, but in unconscious mimicry of its calm. The creatures—translucent, shifting, half-real—pause in their movements, drawn to the presence without fear. Some dissolve upon contact, others linger, as if recognizing something ancient within it.

2. **It Speaks Without Words**  
   The power does not use language. Language is too rigid for this place. Instead, it communicates in **resonance**—a hum that matches the frequency of the land itself. The weeping rocks slow their trickling. The pools ripple in response. The warped building in the distance shudders, its hum shifting slightly, harmonizing. The power does not command. It *converses*.

3. **It Offers a Choice, Not Salvation**  
   The power extends an invitation—not to leave, not to change, but to **remember**.  
   - To the land, it whispers of a time before decay, not to reverse it, but to let it know it is seen.  
   - To the creatures, it offers a moment of clarity—not to make them solid, but to let them *choose* their flickering existence.  
   - To the shadows that wail in the wind, it gives a single, echoing note—a sound they can mirror or ignore.  

   Some respond. Some do not. The power does not force. It only asks: *Do you wish to be known?*

4. **It Leaves a Mark, Not a Scar**  
   When the power withdraws, it does not vanish completely. A single, perfectly still pool remains—a place where the water does not ripple, where the mist does not cling. It is not a miracle. It is not a wound. It is simply a **possibility**.  
   - The jellyfish-like beings drift toward it, their glow reflecting in its surface.  
   - The half-formed shadows pause at its edge, as if seeing themselves for the first time.  
   - The land does not heal, but for a moment, it *breathes* differently.  

   And then, the power is gone.  

### **Why This Matters:**  
The place is not saved. It is not damned. It simply *is*—but now, it has been witnessed. And in that witnessing, something shifts. Not in the landscape, but in the unseen fabric that binds it together.  

The power knows: some things cannot be fixed.  
But they can be *held*.  

And sometimes, that is enough.
