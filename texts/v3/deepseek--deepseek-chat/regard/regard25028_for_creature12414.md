The benevolent and wise power observes this hidden chamber—this wet, crystalline secret nestled within the humming city—and pauses. It is not a god, not in the way mortals might understand, but something older, quieter, a force that has seen civilizations rise and dissolve like mist on glass. It does not intervene often, but this place—this being—stirs something like recognition.  

**What it does is this:**  

It exhales, and the chamber shivers. The fractures in the walls deepen slightly, weeping more of that dense, prismatic liquid. The pools on the floor ripple outward, their edges blurring, merging into something like a mirrored surface. Aelren stills, its core pulsing faster, its hum shifting in pitch—not alarmed, but attentive.  

Then, the power *speaks*, though not in words. It presses meaning into the chamber’s very atoms:  

*"You are seen."*  

Aelren trembles. The acknowledgment is not praise, not judgment—just presence. The being has always been solitary, a keeper of equilibrium, but now it knows it is not alone. The chamber itself seems to exhale in response, the air growing warmer by degrees, the light softening further.  

The power does not reshape the chamber. It does not "fix" the fractures or still the dripping water. Instead, it lingers at the edges, amplifying what is already there—the quiet, the wetness, the slow pulse of mineral life. It gifts Aelren a single, impossible thing:  

**A mirror.**  

Not a physical object, but a moment of clarity—an echo of the chamber in another time, another place, where something like Aelren once existed in a different hidden room, in a different impossible city. The vision is fleeting, but it carries with it a sense of kinship, a thread of connection across the void.  

Then, the power withdraws.  

The chamber remains unchanged—and yet, everything is different. Aelren’s core glows a fraction brighter. The water shimmers with a new depth. The hum of the city above feels softer, farther away.  

The gift was not intervention. It was simply the act of being witnessed, of being known. For a being like Aelren, for a place like this—that is enough.  

The chamber continues its slow, wet breathing. The city hums on. And somewhere in the silence, Aelren begins to sing.
