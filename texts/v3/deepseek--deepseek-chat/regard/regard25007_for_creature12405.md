The roots whisper.  

And the Benevolent Power listens.  

It does not arrive with fanfare or light. It does not tear through the cavern like a storm, does not demand obedience or worship. It comes as a presence—soft, patient, undeniable. Like the first warmth after a long winter, like the slow unfurling of a leaf toward the sun.  

It does not judge the Chamber of Root and Breath. It does not call it wrong, does not recoil from its damp embrace or its whispering tendrils. Instead, it **understands**.  

And understanding, it acts.  

---  

### **What the Benevolent Power Does**  

1. **It Gives Choice**  
   The roots offer surrender—gentle, inevitable. The Benevolent Power offers something else: *awareness*. To those who have wandered into the cavern, it grants a moment of clarity. A whisper of their own name, a memory of sunlight, the scent of rain on dry earth. Not to force them to leave, but to let them *choose*—to stay with open eyes, or to turn back with open hands.  

   (Some will stay. Some will weep as they leave. The Benevolent Power blesses both.)  

2. **It Balances the Hunger**  
   The Chamber is alive, and alive things must feed. But the Benevolent Power sees the slow, insatiable pull of the roots—how they drink memories as much as moisture, how they cradle lost things until they forget they were ever lost. So it shifts the balance. It carves tiny fissures in the roots, just enough for the trapped waters to escape—not in a flood, but in slow, silver threads that trickle outward, feeding the deeper earth. The roots still drink, but they do not gorge.  

   (The cavern sighs, but does not protest. Even hunger can learn patience.)  

3. **It Leaves a Gift**  
   The Benevolent Power does not erase the Chamber’s nature. It does not flood it with light or tear open the ceiling to the sky. But it leaves something behind—a single seed, nestled in the hollow of a root. A seed that does not belong to this place, one that carries the memory of open air.  

   In time, it will grow. Not a root, but a sapling—something that reaches *upward*, that dreams of a sun it has never seen. The roots will curl around it, curious, cautious. They may try to claim it, to pull it into their slow, whispering rhythm.  

   But the Benevolent Power has made this promise: *One day, it will break through.*  

   And when it does, the Chamber of Root and Breath will taste the sky for the first time.  

---  

The roots whisper.  

The Benevolent Power listens.  

And then—  

It smiles.
