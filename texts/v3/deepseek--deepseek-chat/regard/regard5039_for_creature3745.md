**The Intervention of the Benevolent Watcher**  

The power that turns its gaze upon the Weeping Flats of Vorthax does not arrive with fanfare. There is no thunderous descent, no blinding radiance. Instead, it manifests as a quiet presence—an absence of absence, a stillness where before there was only the hum of fractured reality.  

It observes first. It sees the glass plains, the mercury veins, the creatures that are neither alive nor dead but caught in between. It sees the Glass-Eyed Watchers, their hollow archives, their ceaseless recording of a world that cannot remember itself. And it understands.  

This place is not meant to be healed.  

Not in the way one might expect.  

The power does not erase the fractures or silence the hum. Instead, it *leans into them*. It presses gently against the edges of the wound, not to close it, but to *shape* it. The glass plains ripple like liquid, the mercury veins pulse with new rhythm, and the mirage-towers solidify—just for a moment—into something comprehensible.  

The Watchers pause. Their lenses dilate, their hollow bodies trembling as if sensing something they cannot record. The power does not speak to them, not in words, but in *possibility*. It offers them a choice: to remain as they are, eternal archivists of a dying world, or to become something else.  

Some do not respond. They return to their work, folding back into the rhythm of the Flats. But others—just a few—hesitate. Their glassy forms flicker, their featureless faces tilting upward as if searching for something beyond the bruised sky.  

And then, they *change*.  

Their limbs soften, their edges blurring into something more fluid, more alive. Their hollow cavities fill—not with organs, not with blood, but with *light*, a slow, pulsing glow that spreads through their bodies like a heartbeat. They are still Watchers, still recorders of reality, but now they do not merely observe. They *interpret*.  

The Flats shudder. The fractures in the glass shift, realigning into patterns that almost make sense. The mercury veins no longer weep aimlessly—they flow with purpose, tracing delicate, luminous circuits across the blackened plains. The hum of the land does not vanish, but it *harmonizes*, weaving into a chorus that is less a dirge and more a lullaby.  

The power does not stay. It does not impose order where none was meant to exist. But it leaves behind a gift—a whisper of balance, a suggestion that even a wound can be beautiful if it is tended with care.  

And then it is gone.  

The Flats remain. The Watchers continue their work. But something has shifted.  

The weeping is quieter now.  

And somewhere, deep in the glass, something like hope begins to glow.
