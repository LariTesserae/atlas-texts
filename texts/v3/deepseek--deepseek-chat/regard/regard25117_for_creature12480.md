The benevolent and wise power observes the plateau and the Dryliths with quiet admiration. This place is already in harmony—its people attuned to the land, its rhythms undisturbed by greed or conquest. To intervene too boldly would be to disrupt the delicate equilibrium they have nurtured for generations. Yet, the power senses something deeper—an unspoken longing in the Dryliths, a whisper of curiosity that lingers at the edges of their rituals. They do not crave change, but they are not entirely closed to it.  

The power chooses to act subtly, weaving its influence into the existing fabric of the plateau.  

### **What the Power Does:**  

1. **Whispers in the Water** – The power speaks through the droplets, the mist, the hidden springs. It does not command, but *suggests*—softening the edges of dreams, seeding questions in the humming songs of the Dryliths. *What lies beyond the plateau? What if the water could remember?* The answers are not given, only the invitation to wonder.  

2. **A Gift of Memory** – The pools, once mere reflections, begin to show more. When a Drylith gazes into them, they see fleeting glimpses—not of the future, but of the past. A forgotten ancestor shaping stone. A lost technique for weaving moss into cloth. The power does not force remembrance, but offers it like a gift left in the reeds, to be found or ignored as they choose.  

3. **The Stone That Sings** – One day, a new sound rises from the plateau—a low, resonant hum emanating from the oldest rock. It is not a voice, but a vibration, a frequency that harmonizes with the water in the Dryliths' bodies. Those who press their palms to it feel a warmth, a pulse like a second heartbeat. It does not speak in words, but in *understanding*. Some interpret it as a call to deepen their bond with the land. Others hear an invitation to explore beyond the mist. The power does not dictate which is right.  

4. **The Algae That Glows Brighter** – The bioluminescent fungi in their dwellings begin to flourish, their light intensifying, shifting from pale blue to soft gold. The Dryliths, ever observant, notice. They experiment, guiding the growth into new patterns—symbols that tell stories, maps that trace unseen paths. The power has given no new knowledge, only amplified what was already there.  

5. **A Single, Strange Seed** – On the wind, carried by a bird from some unknown place, a single seed drifts down. It is unlike anything on the plateau—hardy, yet foreign. The Dryliths will choose what to do with it. Plant it? Crush it? Cast it into the pools to see if it floats? The power watches, curious.  

### **Why This Approach?**  
The plateau does not need saving. The Dryliths do not need fixing. But all living things grow, and growth requires *possibility*. The wise power does not force change—it merely opens a door, so softly that the Dryliths might not even notice it is ajar.  

Perhaps they will step through. Perhaps they will ignore it entirely.  

Either way, the mist will still rise. The water will still fall. And the plateau will remain, suspended between earth and sky, as it always has.  

The power smiles, and fades into the hum of the stone.
