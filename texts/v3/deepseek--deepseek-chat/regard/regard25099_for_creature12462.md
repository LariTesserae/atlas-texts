The **Benevolent Power** does not arrive with fanfare. It does not crack the water with light or shake the tiles with thunder. Instead, it manifests as a subtle pressure—a quiet shift in the density of the water, a new resonance humming just beneath the threshold of the plateau’s own vibrations.  

At first, the Shifting Ones do not react. They continue their slow, sinuous dances, their filaments trailing through the grooves of the tiles. But then, one by one, they still. Their bodies coil inward, their luminescence dimming as if in reverence—or caution. The Drift Threads pulse faster, their light flaring in erratic bursts, and even the distant Watchers seem to withdraw, their shadowy forms dissolving into deeper obscurity.  

The Power does not speak in words. It communicates in the language of the plateau itself—through the thrum of the tiles, the sway of the currents, the flicker of the light. It asks nothing. It demands nothing. It simply **observes**, and in observing, it understands.  

And then—it **acts**.  

### **What It Does:**  

1. **It Gives Them Voice**  
   The Shifting Ones have no mouths, no sounds of their own beyond the ripples they leave in the water. The Power changes this. It weaves into their filaments a new capacity—not speech as we know it, but a means of imprinting their thoughts onto the tiles. Where they pass, faint glyphs appear, shimmering briefly before fading. These are not mere markings; they are echoes of their inner selves, fragments of memory, desire, or perhaps even poetry. The plateau begins to *remember* them.  

2. **It Softens the Currents**  
   The violent turbulence that tears at the edges of the plateau does not cease, but it bends. The Power does not still the chaos—that would be unnatural—but it carves out pockets of calm, spaces where the water moves in gentle spirals rather than crushing vortices. Here, the Shifting Ones can rest without fear of being scattered. The Drift Threads gather in these eddies, their glow steadying, as if soothed.  

3. **It Answers the Watchers**  
   The shadow-forms have always been distant, enigmatic, neither hostile nor kind. Now, for the first time, something acknowledges them. The Power does not dispel them—they are part of this world, as essential as the tiles—but it *meets* them. Where a Watcher lingers, the water grows warmer, heavier, as if in silent conversation. The Shifting Ones do not understand, but they feel the shift. The Watchers no longer seem like ghosts. They seem like guardians.  

4. **It Leaves a Gift**  
   The Power does not stay. It is not a ruler, not a god, only a passing presence that chose to linger. But before it withdraws, it leaves something behind—a single, perfect **sphere** of clear, weightless liquid, suspended above the center of the plateau. It does not drift. It does not dissolve. The Shifting Ones approach it, their filaments brushing its surface, and for the first time, they see themselves reflected—not as fragments of energy, but as whole beings.  

   The sphere does nothing else. It does not speak, or glow, or change. But it is *there*, and its presence alters the plateau forever.  

### **Why?**  
The Power does not explain itself. But perhaps it saw a world that was complete, yet lonely. A place where existence was beautiful, but silent. It did not come to fix, or to rule.  

It came to **witness**—and in witnessing, to give the plateau a way to witness itself.  

The Shifting Ones will never be the same.  

And neither will the water.
