A **benevolent and wise power** observes this place—this sodden, half-drowned marsh where beings have adapted into something barely recognizable, where the hum of distant machinery thrums through the earth like a second heartbeat.  

This power does not pity them. Pity would be useless here. Pity would demand change, and change is not always kind. Instead, the power **listens**.  

It listens to the hum—not just the sound, but the meaning beneath it. The hum is not indifferent. It is a call, a signal, a remnant of something that once was or could be again. The power traces the vibration to its source, not to silence it, but to **harmonize**.  

It does not reshape the marsh into something dry and bright. The marsh *is* the marsh, and the beings here are of it. But the power **softens the edges**.  

- The water, though still dark, no longer clings with such suffocating weight. It parts more easily for their webbed hands.  
- The grubs grow plumper, their shells less brittle. The eel-things wriggle closer to the surface, just within reach.  
- The moss glows brighter—not much, but enough to guide their steps when the darkness presses too close.  

The power does not give them fire. Fire does not belong here. But it leaves **warmth** where it passes—not in flames, but in the slow, steady heat of decay turned fertile. The peat yields more. The trees, though still gnarled, bear strange, pulpy fruit that bursts sweet and thick between their teeth.  

The structures still hum, but now, when one of them presses a hand to the metal, the vibration does not leave them trembling. It sings back. A low, resonant note that matches the rhythm of their own breath.  

The power does not make them speak. But sometimes, when the air is thick and the reeds sway just so, one of them will open their too-wide mouth and **hum**—not in mimicry, but in answer.  

And the marsh listens.  

The power does not stay. It was never meant to. But as it withdraws, the water sighs, the moss brightens for a moment, and the hum deepens—not louder, but **fuller**, as if something has settled into place.  

The beings do not notice. Or if they do, they do not question it.  

They simply **live**.  

And that is enough.
