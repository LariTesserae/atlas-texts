The benevolent and wise power drifts into the Weeping Plateau like a gentle breeze, unnoticed yet undeniable. It is neither male nor female, neither old nor young, but a presence that feels ancient and kind, like the soft hum of stars in the night sky. It observes the land, the creatures, the strange towers, and the pulsating flora with a gaze that is both curious and compassionate.  

The power pauses at the edge of a pool of water collecting in a shallow depression. It reaches down—not with a hand, but with an essence—and touches the surface. The water ripples outward, and the plateau responds. The weeping stones slow their tears, as if pausing to listen. The hum of the towers deepens, harmonizing with the power’s own frequency.  

The power senses the tension in this place, the uncertainty of its forming. It understands that the plateau is alive in its own way, still deciding what it will become. The strange remnants—the skeletal towers, the shifting patterns—are echoes of something long gone, yet still whispering secrets.  

With great care, the power begins to act. It moves to the translucent plants, gently stroking their leaves. The bioluminescent glow intensifies, spreading warmth and light across the plateau. The plants seem to sigh, their sap flowing more freely, their roots digging deeper into the porous rock.  

Next, the power approaches the six-legged lizard, shimmering like oil on water. It places a hand—or the idea of a hand—on the creature’s back. The lizard shivers, then stills. Its scales shift, becoming softer, more vibrant. It scampers off into the shadows, but its presence feels more integrated, more at peace with its environment.  

The power turns its attention to the towers. It reaches out to the nearest one, tracing the etched patterns with its essence. The patterns shift faster, as if excited, and the hum rises in pitch. The power listens, understanding. The towers are not just remnants; they are caretakers, left behind by a civilization that once thrived here. They hum not just with energy, but with purpose—to guide the land’s growth, to protect it from chaos.  

With this realization, the power smiles—not with a mouth, but with its very being. It whispers to the towers, soothing their ancient fears, reassuring them that their purpose is understood. The hum deepens again, becoming more resonant, more steady.  

Finally, the power steps back, surveying the plateau. The land feels calmer now, less tense. The weeping stones continue their slow drip, but the water seems clearer, more purposeful. The twilight softens, the air feels fresher, and the distant shadows no longer flicker with unease.  

The power does not stay. Its work here is subtle, a gentle nudge rather than a forceful change. But as it departs, it leaves behind a sense of harmony, a promise that the plateau is not alone in its forming. The land may still decide what it will become, but it will do so with a guiding hand—a whisper of wisdom carried on the breeze.  

And so, the Weeping Plateau weeps on, but now with a quiet hope, as it shapes itself into something new, something beautiful.
