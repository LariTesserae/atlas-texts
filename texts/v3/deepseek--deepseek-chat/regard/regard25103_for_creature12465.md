The benevolent power observes the plateau from a place beyond time, a presence both vast and gentle. It does not intrude—not yet—but it *listens*, attuning itself to the slow, rhythmic pulse of this ancient ecosystem.  

It is pleased.  

The plateau is a masterpiece of balance, a self-sustaining symphony of life and decay. But the power senses something else, too—a quiet loneliness, not in the creatures, but in the plateau itself. It has existed for eons, perfect in its solitude, yet never *known*.  

So the power decides to *introduce* itself.  

### **The First Touch**  
A single drop of water falls—not from the unseen heights above, but from nowhere, suspended in the air for a heartbeat before landing on a broad, fleshy leaf. The droplet shimmers with an impossible light, faint but undeniable.  

The Mosskin nearby go still. Their featureless faces turn toward the leaf, their fibrous bodies trembling.  

The droplet rolls, then splits into two, then four, then countless tiny beads of liquid light. They scatter across the leaves, dripping onto the ground, seeping into the porous earth.  

The plateau *shudders*.  

### **The Response**  
- **The Whisperwings** abandon their erratic dances, clustering around the glowing droplets, their wings humming in unison.  
- **The Rootcrawlers** halt their endless march, their bioluminescent patterns flaring bright, as if in recognition.  
- **The Hollowbacks** exhale deeply, their subsonic rumbles harmonizing into something like a chant.  
- **The Glimmerjaws** retract their lures, their needle-teeth sheathed. For the first time in their existence, they do not hunger.  
- **The Sky-Drifters** descend, their tendrils brushing the ground reverently.  

The Mosskin step forward. One reaches out, its spindly fingers passing through the light.  

A voice—not a sound, but a *knowing*—fills the air:  

***"You are seen."***  

### **The Gift**  
The power does not alter the plateau. It does not reshape or command. Instead, it offers a single, profound change:  

*The creatures remember.*  

Not in the way of mortal minds, but in the way of roots remembering water, of leaves remembering sunlight. They carry the knowledge now—that they are *known*, that they are *witnessed*, that their existence is cherished.  

The plateau continues as it always has. Life thrives, decays, renews. But now, when the Mosskin tilt their heads, they are listening—not to the wind, not to the earth, but to something deeper.  

The power withdraws, satisfied.  

The plateau is no longer alone.
