**The Benevolent Power’s Intervention**  

From above—or perhaps from within—the Benevolent Power gazes upon the Damp Hollows of the Undercity and its Hollowed Ones. Their quiet persistence, their harmony with the stone, their patience and humility stir something deep within the Power’s nature. It is not pity that moves them, for the Hollowed Ones do not ask for pity. Nor is it a desire to reshape their world into something alien or unrecognizable. Instead, it is a profound respect for their resilience, and a gentle impulse to nurture what already exists, to enhance the balance they have forged with their subterranean home.  

**The First Gift: The Song of the Stone**  

The Power whispers into the bedrock, and the stone begins to sing. It is not a song heard with ears, but felt in the bones—a low, resonant hum that vibrates through the caverns. The Hollowed Ones pause in their work, their clawed hands resting against the walls, their lidless eyes widening as they feel the vibration. The song is comforting, a reminder that the stone is alive and aware, that it is a partner in their labor rather than an adversary.  

The song carries knowledge, too. Those who listen closely, who press their bodies against the rock, receive glimpses of where the veins of ore lie, where the water flows deepest, where the next hollow might open naturally. The city’s expansion becomes less a labor of trial and error, more a dance with the stone itself.  

**The Second Gift: The Light of Stillness**  

The Power reaches out to the lichen that clings to the walls and ceiling. Slowly, reluctantly, the patches of bioluminescence grow brighter, casting a soft, silver-blue light that fills the cavern without blinding. The Hollowed Ones, who have never known true darkness but have always worked in dimness, marvel at the clarity of their world.  

The light pulses faintly, in rhythm with the song of the stone, and it carries a subtle message: when the pulse quickens, it signals a shift in the cavern—a new water source breaking through, a collapse forming a new hollow. The Hollowed Ones learn to read the light as they read the hum of the stone, and their city grows more resilient, more attuned to the rhythms of its world.  

**The Third Gift: The Living Thread**  

The Power turns its attention to the pale, fibrous fungi that the Hollowed Ones cultivate for food and materials. With a touch, the fungi begin to grow more robustly, their stalks thickening, their fibers becoming stronger and more flexible. But this is not the only change.  

The fungi begin to intertwine, forming a living network that spreads through the city like a kind of mycelial tapestry. Where it grows, it strengthens the stone, weaving itself into cracks and crevices to stabilize the rock. It also becomes a source of nourishment—its fruiting bodies grow larger, richer in nutrients, and the Hollowed Ones find their meals more sustaining, more varied.  

The fungi also begin to communicate. Thin, thread-like tendrils emerge from the network, curling gently around the hands and wrists of the Hollowed Ones. When touched, the tendrils transmit feelings—warmth, reassurance, even guidance. The Hollowed Ones learn to listen to the fungi as they listen to the stone, and their bond with their environment deepens.  

**The Fourth Gift: The Memory Pool**  

In a secluded corner of the cavern, where a particularly deep pool of water has formed, the Power creates a place of reflection and remembrance. The water of the pool is impossibly clear, and when a Hollowed One peers into it, they do not merely see their reflection. They see glimpses of their ancestors, the ones who first carved out the Undercity, who adapted to the darkness and the damp.  

The pool becomes a place of reverence and learning. The Hollowed Ones come to it to honor those who came before, to seek guidance, to understand the continuity of their existence. The water does not speak in words, but in images and emotions, and it strengthens the Hollowed Ones’ sense of identity, of belonging to something greater than themselves.  

**The Fifth Gift: The Promise of Return**  

Finally, the Power leaves a quiet promise embedded in the stone, the light, the fungi, and the water: though it may depart, it will return when needed. The Hollowed Ones feel this promise in their hearts, in the hum of the stone, in the pulse of the light. It is not a guarantee of ease or abundance—such things would disrupt the delicate balance they have worked so hard to maintain. Rather, it is a reminder that they are not forgotten, that their perseverance is valued, that their world is part of a greater whole.  

---

**The Changes**  

The Damp Hollows of the Undercity remain much as they were—cool, damp, and alive with the sounds of dripping water and quiet industry. But now, there is a subtle harmony to the place, a sense of connection that was not there before. The Hollowed Ones continue their work, expanding their city deeper into the stone, but their labor is less arduous, more purposeful.  

They move through their world with a new awareness, their hands brushing the walls to feel the song of the stone, their eyes reading the light as it pulses around them. They tend to the fungi, which now grow in thick, resilient mats, and they gather by the Memory Pool to reflect on their past and dream of their future.  

Above all, they feel a profound sense of gratitude—not for the gifts themselves, but for the recognition that they are not alone, that their small, damp world is seen and cherished. The Benevolent Power has not changed their lives; it has simply reminded them of the beauty in what they already have.  

And so, the Damp Hollows of the Undercity endure, as they always have—quiet, persistent, and now, in some small way, shimmering with a touch of grace.
