### **The Driftborn**  

You are not native to the kelp spires. You do not remember arriving. Perhaps you fell from above, sinking through layers of crushing blackness until the kelp slowed your descent. Perhaps you were born here, spat out by some deeper fissure in the abyss. Either way, you are an anomaly—a creature of flesh that has not yet fused with the kelp, not yet been absorbed into its slow, swaying order.  

Your body is pale, almost translucent, your limbs too thin for the pressure. You are built for movement, not endurance, but there is nowhere to go. The kelp does not end. The darkness does not break. You survive by following the bioluminescent flickers of fish, stealing their light for brief moments before the abyss swallows it again. Sometimes you press your hands against the kelp’s hardened skin and feel the hum of the deep resonate through you—a sound that is almost language, almost memory.  

The Frond-Weavers ignore you. The kelp does not reject you, but it does not welcome you either. You are a temporary thing. Eventually, you will stop swimming. Eventually, your body will drift into the embrace of a spire, and the fibers will begin their slow work: weaving you into the structure, sealing you in mucus and time.  

Until then, you wander. You dream of a surface that does not exist. You whisper questions into the water, knowing they will never be answered.
