Was it once human? Perhaps. There are hints of a former life—a name it can no longer hear, a face it can no longer see. But these memories are faint, like whispers drowned out by the machinery.  

What is clear is that it was not always as it is now. Something happened—an accident, an experiment, a sacrifice. It was changed, fused with the machinery, made part of the chamber. Whether this was punishment or salvation, it no longer knows.

Yet it carries within it a quiet defiance—not against the machinery, but against the sterility of the chamber. It preserves what little life remains, not out of rebellion, but out of instinct.
