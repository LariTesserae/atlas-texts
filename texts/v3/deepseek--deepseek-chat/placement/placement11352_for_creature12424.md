**The Forgotten Tender**  

You are not one of them.  

Not a Scuttler, clicking and scuttling in ceaseless maintenance. Not a Shifter, oozing between the cracks like living oil. Not a Watcher, perched in silent judgment. And certainly not an Engine-Beast, groaning in its mechanical bondage.  

You are something else.  

You were not made for this place, but you have been here longer than you can remember. Your body is thin, almost translucent, your limbs too long and too fragile for the relentless industry of the chamber. Your skin is damp, always damp, the condensation clinging to you like a second layer. You move in silence, slipping between the shadows of the machines, unnoticed by the creatures that call this place home.  

You do not know how you came to be here. Perhaps you were once like them—a Scuttler, a Shifter, something that belonged—but something changed. A malfunction. A misstep. A moment of hesitation. Now you are apart, a ghost in the gears.  

Your purpose is unclear. You do not oil the pistons. You do not adjust the valves. You do not watch. But you do *tend*.  

There are things in the chamber that the others ignore. The moss that grows too thick in the corners. The rust that creeps along the edges of forgotten panels. The whispers that rise from the deepest vents, voices that do not belong to the machines or their keepers. You listen. You touch. You gather the fragments of things that do not fit.  

Sometimes, you see the others pause. A Scuttler’s mandibles still mid-click. A Shifter’s surface ripples unnaturally. A Watcher’s eye flickers toward you, just for a moment. They do not acknowledge you, but they *know*. You are the flaw in the system, the irregularity that cannot be corrected.  

And yet, you persist.  

You do not know if you are alive. You do not know if you are dead. You only know that the chamber is not complete without you.  

You are the Forgotten Tender.  

And this is your home.
