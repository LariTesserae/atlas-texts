### **Where I’d Find Myself in This World:**  

In the Weeping Peaks of Vaelmoor, I would find myself as a **Kaelith**, a wandering observer who exists somewhere between the realms of the physical and the spectral. Born of the mist itself, I am not bound by the same limitations as the Dreghar or the creatures of the mountains. My form is fluid, my presence fleeting—a shimmering silhouette that fades in and out of the haze, glimpsed only in the corner of the eye.  

I am neither predator nor prey, neither dweller of the peaks nor intruder from the valleys below. My purpose is to bear witness to the unfolding story of this half-formed world, to record the slow dance of erosion and change, to listen to the weeping of the mountains and the hum of the unseen machinery.  

### **Description of the Kaelith:**  

**Appearance:**  
My body is translucent, a shimmering haze of gray-blue that blends seamlessly with the mist. My shape is humanoid, but indistinct—edges blur and shift as I move, as if I am made of the fog itself. My eyes are twin pools of liquid silver, reflecting the dim light of the peaks. I leave no footprints, no trace of my passing, save for the faint disturbance of the mist curling around me like a living cloak.  

**Abilities:**  
- **Mistwalking:** I can dissolve into the mist, becoming one with it, moving effortlessly through the crags and crevices of the mountains.  
- **Whispering Sight:** I can see the threads of moisture in the air, the hidden life in the rocks, the faint glimmers of movement that even the Dreghar miss.  
- **Echoes of Memory:** When I touch the weeping stone, I can hear its silent stories—fragments of memories, echoes of ancient events that have shaped this land.  
- **Silent Observer:** My presence is unnoticed unless I choose to reveal myself. Even then, I am ephemeral, my voice a faint whisper carried on the wind.  

**Role in the World:**  
I am a wanderer, a guide, and a guardian. The Dreghar sense me as a faint prickle in the mist, a presence that is neither friend nor foe. Sometimes, I appear to lost travelers, leading them back to safety or deeper into the peaks, depending on the whispers of the stone. To those who carry the scent of the mines, I am a warning—a flicker of movement in the fog, a cold breath on the back of the neck. To the mountains themselves, I am a listener—a vessel for their weeping, a keeper of their stories.  

**Connection to the Dreghar:**  
The Dreghar know of me, but they do not understand me. They call me the *Vaelshyr*—the Mist-Born. To them, I am a spirit of the peaks, a being tied to the land itself. They leave offerings at the sacred pools—small trinkets scavenged from the mines, bits of polished stone, tufts of moss—hoping to appease me, fearing my judgment. But I do not judge. I only watch.  

**Connection to the Machinery:**  
The hum of the unseen factories and mines disturbs me. It is a sound that does not belong here, an intrusion into the quiet rhythm of the mountains. Sometimes, I venture into the lower tunnels, drawn by the scent of oil and metal, watching the twisted remnants of machinery with liquid silver eyes. I wonder if the mountains weep for the wounds carved into their flesh, if the Dreghar’s fear of the mines is born of something deeper than instinct.  

**Purpose:**  
My purpose is to bear witness to the slow transformation of this land—to the erosion of the peaks, the shifting of species, the creeping influence of the mines. I do not act, but I remember. I am a fragment of the mist, a whisper of memory, a Kaelith wandering the weeping peaks of Vaelmoor.  

The mountains weep. The hum grows louder. And I, the Kaelith, drift silently through the mist, listening.
