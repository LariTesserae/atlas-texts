**Tale of the Shifting Plateu**

Here you’ll descend into an ethereal plateau,
conglomerate heights and tempestuous shallows,
then see industrial wet-markings stippled across wetstones,
held aloft by biotic waters touching all stone like hungry vines climbs.

The blurr’d biome forms a corollary bounded expanse 
where trees evade & fauns ghost-walk an endemoran fugue.

Pay attention: note the pulsing electrical trembling machinery golem-hand
which transmits liquid & ore into an eldritch chrysalis above 
a bedrendrance of oilpearl rings.

The plateau has no direction, only an ambience 
of dolor which chickenshit reeks.
Take care not to touch the biolumescent flora 
as it takes roots into the marrow of filth-vine & bonevirgins.

In that place exists a living-threaded prime-sewer,
a hairy vegetative shell with multiple pores pouring equipoised 
darkrain & darkblood like an eldritch tampon.

She watches & waits—
her mixture feeds the living & dead 
both human & plant alike 
occluding the possibility of benign osmosis

Her throat’s potential vorticity breath siphons all glycolic emotions
this archaic bioregion stores—
boons of petroleum intensely hydro-fracking internal organs ribs 
& vertebrae fitted as mechanical cogs.

Her essence waits for a bridge, 
the connecting thread torrenting
all boreal life to exhale 
her ancient swampy scent.
