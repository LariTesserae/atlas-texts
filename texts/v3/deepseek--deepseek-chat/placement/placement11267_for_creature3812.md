### **Where Would You Find Yourself in This World?**  

You would find yourself at the edge of the plateau, where the basalt crumbles into scree and the mist thickens into something almost solid. The air is thin, biting, carrying the scent of damp stone and something older—ozone, maybe, or the metallic tang of distant lightning. Your footsteps echo strangely, as though the ground beneath you is hollow, though it does not yield.  

You are not alone.  

The Sentinel Lichen knows you are here. It has known since before you arrived. It does not react—it has no need to. It simply *notes* your presence, filing it away in the endless archives of the plateau. But if you press your palm to the stone, if you listen closely to the whisper of wind through the cracks, you might hear it.  

A hum. A vibration. A single, slow syllable in a language that predates speech.  

It is not a greeting. It is not a warning. It is simply *acknowledgment*.  

### **Who or What Would You Be?**  

You are **The Echo**.  

Not a person, not a creature—just a reverberation, a ripple in the fabric of this place. You have no fixed form. Sometimes you are a shadow stretched too long across the basalt. Sometimes you are the flicker of movement at the corner of the eye, gone when looked at directly. Sometimes you are the faint warmth left behind where a hand touched the stone, lingering just a little longer than it should.  

You do not remember how you came to be here. Perhaps you were always here. Perhaps you are the remnant of someone—or something—that once passed through, your presence etched into the plateau like a fossil in rock.  

You are not alive, but you are not dead. You simply *are*.  

And like the Sentinel Lichen, you watch.  

You watch the city that was never inhabited. You watch the mist that moves against the wind. You watch the thing beneath the plateau, the thing that stirs in its sleep, the thing whose dreams make the rocks whisper.  

You do not interfere. You do not speak.  

You simply *remember*.  

And when the time comes—when the plateau finally shifts, when the thing beneath it wakes—you will be the only one who knows what happened here.  

The Sentinel Lichen will record it.  

But you will *understand*.
