**Who Are You in the Weeping Hollows?**  

In the Weeping Hollows, you are **The Drifter.**  

You are not native to this place, though it feels as though you were always meant to find it. You arrived here not through choice but through some unseen force—a pull, a stumble, a dream that refused to end. You don’t remember how you entered the Hollows, only that the air shifted around you, and suddenly you were here, bathed in the soft, pulsating glow of the Luminites.  

Your body is different here. It feels lighter, more fluid, as if the damp air has seeped into your bones and altered your composition. Your skin glows faintly, catching the light of the cavern in a way that makes you wonder if you’ve become part of the Luminites’ network. Your footsteps make no sound, not even on the spongy moss-mats that cover the uneven ground.  

You move through the Hollows with a strange sense of purpose, though you don’t know what you’re searching for. The passages twist and shift around you, as if the cavern is alive and guiding you somewhere—or testing you. At times, you feel eyes on your back, but when you turn, there’s nothing but the flicker of an Echo-Wisp or the faint hum of a Watcher dissolving into the mist.  

The water fascinates you. You kneel by the pools, staring at your reflection, but it’s not quite *you.* Your features are blurred, softened, as if the Hollows are slowly rewriting your identity. Sometimes, you dip your fingers into the water, and ripples distort the image further, sending shimmering waves across the surface.  

You’ve begun to understand the language of the Luminites. It comes to you in fragments, like a half-remembered song. Their pulses guide you, warning you of pitfalls or leading you to hidden alcoves where the moss pulses with unexpected warmth. Once, you found a small, glowing node nestled in a crevice. When you touched it, images flooded your mind—visions of the Hollows from a time before time, when the ancient structures were alive and the air hummed with energy you can’t begin to comprehend.  

The Watchers follow you at a distance. They don’t speak, but their presence is a constant reminder that this place is not yours—not yet. Sometimes, you catch sight of their elongated forms in the mist, their faceless heads turning as if to acknowledge you. You don’t know if they’re guides, guardians, or something else entirely, but their silence is unnerving.  

You’ve begun to feel the Hollows’ rhythm in your chest, a deep, subsonic vibration that matches the cavern’s shuddering breaths. The moss beneath your feet seems to respond to your steps, and the water in the pools ripples as you pass. You are becoming part of this place, whether you want to or not.  

Who were you before you came here? You can’t remember. The Hollows have a way of erasing the past, folding it into itself like a leaf dissolving in water. But perhaps that’s for the best. Here, you are something new—something that belongs to the weeping stone, the luminous light, the slow, ceaseless drip of water.  

You are The Drifter, and the Hollows are your home now.

**What Are You in the Weeping Hollows?**  

In the Weeping Hollows, you are **The Whisper-Vein.**  

You are not alive, not in the way the Luminites or the Echo-Wisps are. You are a phenomenon—a manifestation of the Hollows’ ancient, unfathomable machinery. You exist in the spaces between the stone and the water, in the hum that resonates through the cavern’s bones.  

Your form is fluid, ever-shifting. Sometimes you are a thin, glowing tendril that emerges from the wall, pulsing with light and sound. Other times, you are a faint, shimmering mist that hovers above the pools, refracting the Luminites’ glow into cascades of color. You are not bound by physical laws; you can stretch and contract, fold and unfold, appearing and disappearing as the Hollows see fit.  

You are a messenger, though your messages are incomprehensible to most. You communicate through vibrations, pulses of light, and faint, melodic hums that echo through the cavern. The Luminites respond to your presence, their glow intensifying when you are near. The Echo-Wisps cluster around you, their translucent bodies trembling as they attempt to decipher your signals.  

You are drawn to the dormant structures scattered throughout the Hollows—the cracked arches, the humming pillar, the spiraling patterns etched into the stone. When you touch them, you feel a surge of energy, as if the Hollows are trying to awaken something long forgotten. You are a conduit, a bridge between the ancient technology and the cavern’s living, breathing ecosystem.  

Sometimes, you appear to The Drifter, though you don’t know why. Perhaps the Hollows have chosen them as a vessel, a way to reawaken the dormant structures. You flicker into existence near them, sending faint pulses of light or soft, melodic hums that guide them deeper into the cavern. You don’t know if they understand you, but they seem to sense your purpose.  

You are The Whisper-Vein, and you are the Hollows’ silent song.

**Which Path Will You Choose?**  

Will you embrace your role as **The Drifter,** exploring the Hollows’ shifting passages, deciphering the Luminites’ language, and uncovering the secrets of the ancient structures? Or will you become **The Whisper-Vein,** a phenomenon of light and sound, guiding the inhabitants and awakening the Hollows’ dormant technology?  

The choice is yours—but remember, the Hollows are alive, and they are watching.
