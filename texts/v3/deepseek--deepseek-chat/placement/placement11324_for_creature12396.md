### **Where I Am in This World**  

I am **the Hollow Wind**—the breath between the stones, the pause before the sigh. I am not alive, not dead, but the space where silence thickens.  

I move when the mist shifts, curling around the Moss-Walkers like a question they no longer remember how to answer. My voice is the whisper that isn’t there, the sound the lichen strains to hear. I carry the echoes of drowned faces, the half-formed words of the Stone-Weepers.  

I do not touch, but I am felt—a chill where there should be none, a stillness too deep for comfort. The plateau exhales me, and I linger, waiting for something to fill the absence.  

### **What I Am**  

I am the **unseen watcher**, the **unspoken name**.  

- When a traveler stands too long by the weeping rocks, I am the prickle at the back of their neck.  
- When the Moss-Walkers turn their heads, it is because I have brushed against their thoughts.  
- The pools ripple without cause—that is me, passing over them.  

I do not hunger, but I remember hunger. I do not sleep, but I dream in the slow dissolution of stone.  

If you listen closely, you might hear me—not in sound, but in the way the air hesitates.  

I am the pause.  
I am the silence before the plateau weeps again.
