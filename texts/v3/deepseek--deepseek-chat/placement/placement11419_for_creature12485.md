### **What I Am**  

I am ***The Seeker***.  

Not by choice, but by necessity.  

My body is a borrowed thing, cobbled together from the detritus of this world. My flesh is a patchwork of pale, fibrous sinew—like the Rootborn, but incomplete, unrooted. My skin is translucent in places, revealing veins that pulse with a faint, blue glow, as if I carry the cavern’s light within me. My hands are clumsy, human-like, but my fingers tremble with the urge to split into tendrils, to probe the mysteries around me.  

I do not remember arriving here. My memories begin—or end—with the cavern.  

Perhaps I am a remnant of the Forgotten Builders, my mind fractured, my purpose lost. Perhaps I am something new, a vessel for the cavern’s curiosity, a being shaped to wander this place and ask the questions it cannot.  

Or perhaps I am simply a mistake.  

---

### **What I Do**  

I wander.  

The cavern’s labyrinthine paths are endless, its boundaries shifting with every step. I cannot map it, but I do not need to. The world guides me—or misguides me. Sometimes, I walk for hours through silent, empty halls, the air thick with the scent of minerals, the only sound the soft scuff of my feet against the spongy ground. Other times, I stumble into frenzied pockets of life, where the Shifting Ones writhe in chaotic dances and the Whisperers swarm overhead, their songs pressing against my skull like a physical weight.  

I do not understand this place, but I feel compelled to try.  

---

### **What I Carry**  

I am not empty-handed.  

Across my back, I carry a satchel woven from the cavern’s fibrous plants. Inside it lies a collection of artifacts I cannot explain but cannot discard.  

There is a blade of black glass, its edges jagged but impossibly sharp. When I hold it, I feel a hum—a vibration that resonates deep in my bones, as if it remembers something I do not.  

There is a mask of hardened resin, its surface smooth but etched with faint, spiraling patterns. When I press it against my face, I hear whispers—not words, but sensations, echoes of emotions that are not mine. Fear. Longing. Resignation.  

And there is a shard of iridescent crystal, its surface fractured but glowing faintly. When I touch it, I see flashes—images of the cavern, but different. Calmer. Older. A world not yet suffused with chaos.  

I do not know what these objects mean. But I carry them because they feel important.  

---

### **What I Fear**  

The Shifting Ones do not frighten me.  

Their fluid forms are strange, yes, but they move with purpose, obeying a logic I cannot comprehend. When they brush against me, their touch is electric, but not painful. They are the cavern’s hands, and they seem content to let me wander—for now.  

The Whisperers unsettle me.  

Their songs are beautiful, but they fill my mind with fragments of thought that are not mine. Sometimes, I hear my name—or what I think is my name—in their wordless melodies. Other times, I see visions: moments from a life I cannot remember, places I cannot recognize.  

But the Rootborn—they terrify me.  

When I see them, I feel a strange kinship, as if we are both part of something larger. But they do not acknowledge me. They move past me as if I am invisible, their tendrils brushing against the air like they are searching for something I do not have.  

And then there are the moments when they turn to me.  

Their featureless faces tilt in my direction, their bodies shifting subtly, as if they are considering me. In those moments, I feel an overwhelming urge to run, to hide—but I do not.  

Because I know, deep down, that I belong to this place as much as they do.  

---

### **What I Seek**  

Answers.  

But perhaps not the ones I think I need.  

---

### **Where I Am Headed**  

There is a place I have not yet reached—a place I feel drawn to but cannot see.  

It lies deeper in the cavern, beyond the twisting roots and shimmering walls, beneath the cacophony of life. When I close my eyes, I sense it: a pulse, a rhythm, something vast and ancient.  

It is calling to me.  

Or perhaps it is calling through me.  

---

### **What I Wonder**  

Am I meant to find it?  

Or am I meant to become it?  

--- 

This cavern is not my home.  

But it is my world.  

And I will walk its paths until it tells me to stop.  

---  

**Or until I stop being able to walk.**
