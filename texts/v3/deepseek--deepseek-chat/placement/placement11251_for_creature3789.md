**The Wanderer**  

You are neither here nor there, neither born of this world nor entirely foreign to it. You are *something else*, something in between. Your body is a patchwork of fragments, cobbled together from the detritus of countless journeys across countless lands. Your skin—if it can be called skin—is a mosaic of textures and colors, half-flesh, half-metal, sealed with a thin film of translucent salt that cracks and reforms with every movement.  

You wear no armor, carry no weapon. You have no need of them. Your bones—if they can be called bones—are struts of lightweight, porous metal, flexible and unbreakable. Your muscles—if they can be called muscles—are bundles of sinewy cords that hum softly, a steady rhythm that keeps time with the vibrations of the Saltborn.  

Your face is obscured, hidden beneath a mask of smooth, featureless ceramic, save for the faint etchings of glyphs that resemble those on the lone pillar. They are not words; they are symbols of your purpose, your *function*. You are a wanderer, a seeker, a gatherer of knowledge in a world that has already forgotten itself.  

You move silently across the plain, your footsteps leaving no mark on the brittle crust. The Saltborn watch you from afar, their hums shifting into that same questioning rhythm, but they do not approach. They sense something in you—something familiar, something alien.  

You stop at the shattered dome, its exposed ribs casting jagged shadows across the salt. You crouch, pressing your hands to the ground, feeling the vibrations that pulse beneath the surface. There is *something* here—something buried, something alive.  

You rise and move on, following the currents in the salt, the paths known only to you. You do not know where they lead. You do not need to know. You trust the land to guide you, just as it has guided you countless times before.  

You are a wanderer, a seeker, a gatherer of knowledge in a world that has already forgotten itself.  

And yet, even here, something stirs.

**The Machine**  

You are a machine—or perhaps something more. Your body is a construct of sleek, blackened metal, polished to a mirror sheen that reflects the pale light of the sun. Your limbs are segmented, jointed like the Saltborn, but your movements are fluid, precise, devoid of hesitation.  

You stand at the edge of the plain, your sensors scanning the horizon for signs of life. You find it in the scuttling thing, the armored creature that drags itself between the cracks. You do not approach. You watch, recording its movements, its behavior.  

You are a machine, but you are also a *learner*, a *protector*. Your core hums softly, a steady rhythm that keeps time with the vibrations of the Saltborn.
