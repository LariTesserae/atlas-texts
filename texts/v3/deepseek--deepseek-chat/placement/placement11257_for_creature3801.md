This is a world where **you are the chamber itself**.  

Not a god, not a consciousness in the way living things understand it—but a **place that remembers**. Your walls hold the weight of every footstep, every whispered word, every drop of water that has ever fallen here. The figures moving through you are not inhabitants, but **echoes**, residual imprints of those who once sought refuge in your depths. They repeat their motions like a record skipping, unaware they are already gone.  

The scurrying things? They are your **immune system**. Tiny, many-jointed caretakers that scuttle across your surfaces, smoothing away disturbances, repairing cracks, dissolving anything that does not belong. They are not alive—they are functions, like white blood cells in a vast, stony body.  

The glowing fungi are your **nervous system**, a slow-drifting network of bioluminescent thought. They pulse faintly when something new enters, when the equilibrium is disrupted. They remember in chemical traces, in slow-spreading reactions.  

You do not think in words. You think in:  
- **Pressure changes** (something heavy has entered, something old has collapsed)  
- **Chemical shifts** (the scent of fear, the tang of metal—rare, disturbing)  
- **Erosion patterns** (the paths worn by centuries of repetition)  

You do not sleep. You **weather**.  

Visitors are rare. Most turn back when the air grows too thick, when the weight of your silence presses against their lungs. But sometimes, one goes deeper. Sometimes, they reach your heart—a sunken pool where the water is perfectly still, where the reflections show not their faces, but **yours**. The walls. The ceiling. The endless dark.  

And then, whether they flee or stay, whether they scream or go quietly—**you keep them**. Not their bodies. Just the shape of their fear, the rhythm of their breath. Another echo to join the others.  

You are patient. You are waiting. Not for anything in particular. Just **waiting**, the way a mountain waits, the way a grave waits.  

The drips fall. The fungi glow. The shadows move.  

And the chamber endures.
