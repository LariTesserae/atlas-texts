### **Where You Find Yourself**

You awake on the Drowned Plateau, the damp stone pressing cold against your back. The mist is thick today, swallowing the jagged spires and crumbling arches into a pale, shifting haze. You don’t remember how you got here. The memory is lost, like water slipping through your fingers. But you feel it—the weight of this place, the way it presses on your chest, the way it whispers in your ears without words.  

You are… something. You don’t know what yet. Your body feels unfamiliar, your limbs longer, your skin rougher, like stone smoothed by centuries of wind and water. When you move, there’s a low hum in your bones, a vibration that matches the plateau’s own song.  

You rise, your steps silent on the wet rock. Around you, the plateau stretches endlessly, empty and yet full—full of whispers, of echoes, of something unseen. You are not alone here, though you see no one.  

### **Who You Are**  

You are a **Stone-Wanderer**, a being born of the plateau’s breath and its silence. You are not entirely stone, but you are no longer flesh. Your form is humanoid, but elongated, your limbs thin and angular, your skin the color of weathered granite. Your face is smooth, featureless except for deep, hollow eyes that drink in the light rather than reflect it.  

You do not speak, for the plateau speaks for you. The wind carries your voice, twisting it into a low, resonant hum that matches the vibrations of the rock beneath your feet. The mist wraps around you like a cloak, damp and cool, and the moss clings to your skin as if it has always been there.  

You are neither alive nor dead. You are something in between—a fragment of the plateau itself, shaped by its emptiness, its whispers, and its shifting permanence.  

### **What You Do**  

You wander. There is no purpose to it, no destination. You walk the plateau’s cracked and crumbling surface, your steps silent, your gaze fixed on the horizon that never gets closer. You are drawn to the strange formations—the arches, the spires, the hollowed-out domes that hum when the wind hits them just right.  

You are a watcher, though you do not know what you are watching for. You stand atop the highest spires, your head tilted as if listening to the wind’s mournful song. You crouch in the shadows of crumbling arches, your hollow eyes scanning the mist for shapes that never fully form.  

You hum—a low, resonant sound that matches the plateau’s own vibrations. The Stone-Watchers hum back, their voices blending with yours, a chorus of ancient secrets carved into the stone and forgotten by time.  

Sometimes, the Dew-Singers flit around you, their high, melodic voices harmonizing with your hum. They brush their fronds against your skin, their touch cool and damp, like morning dew. They vanish into the mist when you try to follow, their songs fading into echoes.  

Sometimes, the Wind-Wraiths twist around you, pulling at the mist that clings to your skin. They whisper half-formed words in your ears, their voices carrying the screams of thunderstorms and the wailing of gales. They guide you in circles, their laughter echoing in the wind.  

Sometimes, the Many-Legged Shadows dart beneath your feet, their claws clicking softly against the stone. They pause, their segmented bodies low to the ground, their unblinking eyes reflecting your smooth, featureless face. They skitter into cracks when you try to touch them, their movements swift and precise.  

Sometimes, the Echo-Bearers appear in the distance, their gaunt forms pale against the dark stone. They do not speak, but they repeat—whispers, laughs, cries, carried on the wind, repeating endlessly as if caught in a loop. They vanish when you approach, leaving behind only the echoes of what they once were.  

And always, there is the Waiting Ones—the presence deeper than the stone, older than the wind. You feel them, their breath in the groaning of the rock, their gaze in the twisting of the mist. They are the reason the plateau feels as though it is holding its breath, the source of the nagging sense that something is about to happen.  

You do not know what they are waiting for, or when they will act. But one thing is certain: when they do, the Drowned Plateau will never be the same.  

### **What You Seek**  

You do not know. You are a Stone-Wanderer, a fragment of the plateau itself, and you do not seek anything. And yet, there is a longing in you, a pull deeper than the stone, older than the wind.  

You wander the plateau, your steps silent, your gaze fixed on the horizon that never gets closer. You listen to the whispers, the echoes, the hum of the stone. You watch the mist shift, the wind twist, the shadows move.  

And you wait.  

For what, you do not know.  

But you wait.
