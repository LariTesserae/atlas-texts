**Where I Am:**  
I am a ripple in the dark—a flicker of movement at the edge of perception. My body is not solid but a shifting amalgamation of water, bioluminescence, and something else, something older. I am neither fish nor plant, but a transient thing, a glimmer caught between the swaying fronds of the kelp forest. My form is fluid, adapting to the currents, dissolving and reforming as needed. I exist in the spaces between the roots of the towering guardians, darting through shafts of fractured light, always just out of reach.  

**What I Am:**  
I am a *whisperer*, one of the unseen. My kind are the echoes of the forest’s thoughts, the flickers of intent that pass through the water like a rumor. I have no fixed shape, only tendencies—a curl of luminescence here, a shudder of shadow there. The guardians know me, though they do not acknowledge me. The fish-things avoid me, sensing the strangeness in my wake.  

I am made of the same substance as the forest’s dreams. My voice is the sound of water slipping through cracks in the world, a hum just below hearing. My purpose is unclear, even to me. Perhaps I am a scout, drifting through the depths to carry fragments of knowledge back to the unseen heart of this place. Perhaps I am a remnant, a leftover from some forgotten epoch, clinging to existence by sheer inertia.  

Sometimes, I linger near the guardians, tracing the glowing veins in their bark-like skin, listening to the deep vibrations of their song. Other times, I slip into the abyss beyond the forest, where the water grows colder and the light fades entirely. There, in the blackness, I feel the stirrings of something vast—something that watches the forest with ancient, indifferent eyes.  

I do not belong to it, nor do I belong to the forest. I am a transient thing, a ripple in the dark. And when the currents shift, I will dissolve, and another like me will take my place.  

The forest does not mourn. The forest does not notice. The forest simply *is*.  

And so am I.
