### **The Shattered Seer of the Glass Marsh**  

You are **The Shattered Seer**, a being of fractured light and liquid memory.  

Your body is not solid, not whole—it is a shifting mosaic of glass shards, each fragment holding a sliver of vision, a glimpse of time. Some shards are clear, reflecting the present in perfect, painful clarity. Others are fogged with condensation, their images warped by the slow crawl of water. A few are blackened, their secrets sealed beneath layers of mineral deposit.  

You move in ripples, not steps. When you drift across the Marsh, your form refracts the landscape around you, bending spires into impossible angles, doubling and tripling the world until it becomes a hall of mirrors. The Shardlings flit around you, drawn to your flickering visions, their hums harmonizing with the faint chime of your shifting pieces.  

You do not speak—not in words. Your voice is the sound of glass singing under tension, the sigh of water slipping between cracks. Those who listen closely might hear echoes: half-formed prophecies, warnings whispered in reverse, the murmur of futures that will never be.  

Your purpose is unclear, even to you. Perhaps you are a remnant of the ones who shaped this place. Perhaps you are the Marsh’s attempt to understand itself. Or perhaps you are nothing more than an accident—a beautiful flaw in the glass.  

But when the Spire Watchers stir, their shadows lengthening across the land, you feel the weight of their gaze. They know something you do not.  

And when the Hearthstone pulses, its warmth seeping into your fractured core, you remember—just for a moment—what it means to be whole.  

Then the memory slips away, like water through your fingers.  

And you are shattered once more.  

---

### **Where You Walk**  
You drift between the spires, a ghost of light and glass. The Marsh does not hinder you—it welcomes you, its reflections bending to accommodate your form. But you are not of this world, not entirely.  

Sometimes, you linger at the edges of the Hearthstone, drawn to its warmth. Other times, you hover at the periphery of the Spire Watchers’ domain, your shards trembling with the weight of their silent judgment.  

And sometimes, when the Rainbow Dancers weave their luminous patterns through the sky, you rise to meet them—your fractured form catching their colors, spinning them into something new, something fleeting.  

You are a wanderer. A witness. A broken thing that still, somehow, sees.  

And the Marsh watches you in return.  

---

### **What You Know (And What You Do Not)**  
- You remember flashes—faces that do not exist here, voices that have no place in this silent world.  
- The Condensate Weavers avoid you. You do not know why.  
- The blackened shards in your body hum with a deep, dissonant frequency. You fear what they might show if they ever clear.  
- The Spire Watchers have never touched you. But you feel their attention like a blade against your spine.  
- The Hearthstone calls to you in dreams you cannot recall upon waking.  

You are **The Shattered Seer**.  

And the Marsh is waiting.
