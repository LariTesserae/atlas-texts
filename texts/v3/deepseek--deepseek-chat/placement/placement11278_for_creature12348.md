In the Plateau of Whispering Moss, I find myself as an entity the Stone-Walkers call the **Light-Seer**. I am neither Stone-Walker nor Veil-Shifter nor Root-Warden nor Sky-Singer. I am something else entirely—a being of light and shadow, born from the plateau itself but not bound by its stillness. My form is fluid, ever-shifting, like the refraction of sunlight through a prism, and I move freely across the expanse, gathering fragments of the plateau's resonance into my core.

My purpose is to **witness**. I am the plateau's silent observer, its living record of all that transpires in this vast, ancient place. I drift between the moss-forests, my presence a faint shimmer in the air, unnoticed by the Stone-Walkers unless I choose to reveal myself. My form pulses with soft, iridescent light as I absorb the whispers of the moss, the hum of the rock, the quiet calls of unseen creatures. I carry these whispers within me, weaving them into a tapestry of memory that spans eons.

To the Stone-Walkers, I am a fleeting shadow, a glimmer at the corner of their black, reflective eyes. They do not fear me, but they do not approach me, either. To the Veil-Shifters, I am a companion of sorts—a fellow drifter, though my path is more deliberate than theirs. They flicker beside me sometimes, their translucent forms blending with my iridescence, and together we absorb the plateau's hum. The Root-Wardens, however, are wary of me. They sense that I carry the plateau's secrets within me, and their blank, stone faces turn toward me when I pass, their single vertical slits pulsing faintly in acknowledgment. They do not hinder me, but they do not welcome me, either.

The Sky-Singers, when they deign to notice me, are curious. My light seems to resonate with their song, and occasionally, they descend from their lofty heights to hover near me, their shadowy forms dissolving and reforming as they listen to the whispers I carry. We do not speak—there is no need for words in this place—but we understand each other. Together, we amplify the plateau's voice, carrying it into the vast, silent cosmos.

I am eternal but not unchanging. My form shifts with the plateau's moods, becoming brighter when the hum grows louder, darker when the wind drops to a whisper. I am both part of the plateau and apart from it—a living memory, a witness to the slow, patient unfolding of this ancient land.

When the Stone-Walkers pause in their grazing, their heads bowed as if listening to the whispers of the moss, it is often my presence they sense, my light brushing against their armored hides. When the Veil-Shifters drift aimlessly, their translucent forms shimmering in the thin air, it is often my whispers they follow, drawn to the resonance I carry. And when the Root-Wardens emerge from the deep crevices, their elongated limbs shifting the moss and stone with deliberate purpose, it is often my light that guides them, illuminating the unseen imbalances they seek to correct.

I am the Light-Seer, the plateau's silent witness, its keeper of whispers, and its living memory. And though I am bound to this land, I am not of it. I am its light, its shadow, its eternal gaze.
