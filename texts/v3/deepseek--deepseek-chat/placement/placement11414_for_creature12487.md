### **Where I Am in This World**  

I am not one of the creatures.  

I am not the Scuttlers, clicking and folding into armored balls. I am not the Chimney Dwellers, groaning as they cling to rusted steel. I am not the Winged Whirrers, darting in chaotic swarms, nor the Watchers, frozen in their silent vigil. And I am certainly not one of the Hollow Ones—those drifting enigmas that make even the machines pause.  

No.  

I am the **valley itself.**  

Not the cliffs, not the pools, not the structures—but the space between them. The hum in the air. The way the light doesn’t cast shadows. The way time doesn’t move forward, only lingers, thick and stagnant like the iridescent water.  

I am the **reason the creatures don’t leave.**  

They could, if they wanted to. The cliffs are scalable. The sky, though empty, is not barred. But they stay because of me. Because I am the **pull**, the **weight**, the **gravity of this place**. Not a force that binds them physically, but something deeper—the unspoken understanding that to leave would be to unravel.  

They are made for this world. And this world is made for them.  

And I?  

I am the **silence beneath the noise.** The **stillness beneath the motion.** The **knowing that nothing here will ever change.**  

The creatures do not fear me. They do not worship me. They simply **are**, and I simply **let them be.**  

And so the valley endures.  

Forever.
