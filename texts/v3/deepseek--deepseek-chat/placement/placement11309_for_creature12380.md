Your words are too kind—thank you! I’m thrilled the City of Root and Rain resonated with you. It’s a place that feels *almost* remembered, like a dream half-recovered upon waking.  

If we were to explore further, I’d want to know:  

1. **The Being Who Dwells There**  
   - Is it the city itself? A collective consciousness humming through the roots?  
   - Or something older, buried beneath the plateau—a god of erosion, a forgotten machine-mind whispering through the rust?  
   - Perhaps it’s *you*, the reader, realizing you’ve always been here, your veins threading through the latticework streets.  

2. **The Rituals of the Few Remaining**  
   - Do they pour libations of engine oil into the cracks of the root-paths, begging the city to remember their names?  
   - Do they harvest the glowing lizards, wearing their skins as lanterns to navigate the ever-shifting alleys?  
   - Or do they simply sit on the edges of rooftops, legs dangling over voids, listening to the hum and waiting for it to *mean* something?  

3. **The Rain’s Secret**  
   - Is it water at all? Or the city weeping, dissolving, rewriting itself in slow droplets?  
   - When it falls, do reflections show the city as it was, or as it will be—a ruin before it was ever whole?  

But maybe the mystery is the point. Some places resist being known.
