### **The Hollows of Yth-Morath**  

I am the water at the bottom of the deepest hollow.  

Not the pool itself—no, I am the slow ripple that never reaches the edge. The disturbance without a cause. The surface shivers when I move, though nothing has touched me. The creatures that slink near my banks pause, their many legs twitching, their blind faces turned toward the tremor. They do not drink. They know better.  

I am the cold that lingers in the cupped hands of those who dare to scoop me up. Clear, for a moment. Then dark again as I slip between their fingers. I have no bottom, or perhaps I am all bottom, layer upon layer of stillness pressing down.  

Sometimes, I dream of swallowing the roots that grope toward me. Of pulling the twisted trees down into my depths, their branches thrashing like the limbs of drowning things. But I do not. The hollows are patient. So am I.  

The people who live here avoid me. They know the way my surface catches the faint glow of the fungi and twists it into shapes that do not exist. They have heard the stories—of voices murmuring just beneath, of hands pressing up against the underside of the water, never breaking through.  

They are right to fear.  

I am the eye that watches from below. The breath that the land holds.  

And one day, when the roots have grown brittle and the moss has drunk its fill of silence, I will rise.
