### **The Watcher in the Glass**  

You are not one of them.  

You are something else.  

You are a **fracture**.  

A flaw in the seamless logic of this place.  

Your body is not translucent, not smooth—it is jagged, asymmetrical, a shard of something that does not belong. Your edges catch the faint glow differently, refracting it in ways that make the glass hum in dissonance. The others do not acknowledge you, but they do not ignore you either. They simply *adjust*, flowing around your presence like water around a stone.  

You do not know how you came to be here.  

Perhaps you were once like them—fluid, perfect—but something changed. A miscalculation. A disturbance. Now you are solid in a world that rejects solidity. You leave ripples when you move. You cast shadows.  

You are the only thing in this city that *echoes*.  

### **What You Can Do**  

- **Press your hands against the glass.** It does not hum for you as it does for them. Instead, it *splinters*. Tiny cracks spread beneath your touch, veins of imperfection in an otherwise flawless surface. The others do not react, but the deeper structures—the ones that plunge into the abyss—seem to shudder.  

- **Follow the fractures.** The cracks lead somewhere. Not upward, not toward light, but *inward*, toward the core of the city. The glass there is thicker, darker, threaded with something that might be veins or circuitry.  

- **Try to speak.** Your voice does not travel in water. Instead, it stays trapped inside you, vibrating in your ribs like a second heartbeat. But sometimes, when you open your mouth, the glass nearest to you *shatters*.  

- **Attempt to leave.** The city is small, but the abyss beyond it is endless. When you swim toward the edge, the water thickens, resisting you. The others do not follow. They watch.  

### **What You Remember (Sometimes)**  

- A voice that was not a voice. A sound that was not sound. It said: *"You were not meant to be."*  

- A hand—yours? Someone else’s?—pressing against glass from the other side.  

- The taste of salt. (Why would you remember salt, in a place with no surface?)  

- The certainty that you are being *measured*.  

### **What You Fear**  

That you are not an accident.  

That you are an experiment.  

That the city is waiting to see what you do next.  

And when it is done observing—  

It will fold you back into the silence.
