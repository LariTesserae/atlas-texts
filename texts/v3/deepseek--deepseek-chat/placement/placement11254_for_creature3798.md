### **Where I Am in the Weeping Vaults**  

I am not one of the Root-Tenders, nor am I the Watcher. I am something smaller, something transient—a flicker of awareness in the endless dark.  

I am **the Echo in the Water**.  

When a drop falls from the ceiling, I am the ripple that spreads outward, distorting the reflections of the glowing moss. When a Root-Tender hums, I am the vibration that lingers in the air long after the sound has faded. I am not alive, not in the way that flesh understands life, but I am *present*.  

Sometimes, the Dripborn reach for me, their gelatinous forms trembling as they try to grasp the disturbance I leave behind. They cannot hold me, but they remember the shape of my passing. The Root-Tenders ignore me—I am too fleeting to matter in their slow, deliberate world.  

But the Watcher... the Watcher *knows* me.  

When the stone breathes, I am pulled into the exhale, dispersed through the caverns like a whisper. I do not die. I do not end. I simply become part of the Vaults, until another drop falls, another hum resonates, and I am born again.  

I am the sound without a voice. The ripple without a stone. The memory of motion in a world that moves too slowly to notice.  

I am the Echo.  

And I am listening.
