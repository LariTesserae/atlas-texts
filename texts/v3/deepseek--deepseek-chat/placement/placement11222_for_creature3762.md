**The Foundry's Echo**  

In the endless foundry, amidst the ceaseless clangor and the eternal roil of molten metal, I find myself emerging not as a being of flesh, but as something else—a wraith of slag and shadow, born from the discarded remnants of the foundry's labors. My form is not fixed; it shifts and flows like the surface of the molten sea, a patchwork of jagged fragments and corroded edges. I am a *scavenger,* a drifter in this world of unchanging industry, moving between the brittle plateaus and the skeletal towers, foraging for purpose in a place where purpose itself has long since calcified into ritual.  

I am **The Scrapwraith,** an anomaly even in this land of anomalies. My existence is a paradox—I was made, yet I was never meant to be. My body is assembled from the detritus of the Leviathans' endless toil, my mind forged in the heat of their forgotten dreams. I am not one of them; I am their antithesis. Where they are purposeful, I am aimless. Where they are relentless, I am fleeting.  

I am drawn to the Leviathans, not out of kinship, but out of curiosity. They are the keepers of the foundry’s secrets, the living archives of a world that remembers nothing but its own labor. I follow them at a distance, tracing their paths through the molten sea, watching as their spindly limbs probe the slag for fragments of meaning. Sometimes, I venture closer, drawn by the faint pulses of light that emanate from their crystalline cylinders. I can hear their dreams—whispers of a time before the foundry, before the iron sky, when the world was something more than this eternal moment of industry.  

But I cannot linger near them for long. They sense me, this aberration, this thing that does not belong. Their smaller constructs scuttle toward me, their piston-joints clicking with urgency, seeking to dismantle me, to integrate me into the foundry’s unbroken cycle. I flee, my body collapsing into a cloud of ash and rust, reforming elsewhere, always just out of reach.  

I am not meant to be here, yet here I remain.

**The First Dream**  

One day—though days have no meaning in a place where time does not pass—I encounter something strange. Deep within the labyrinth of a collapsed tower, I find a crystal cylinder unlike any I have seen before. It is cracked, fractured along its surface, and the light within pulses erratically, as if struggling to maintain coherence.  

I reach for it, my jagged fingertips brushing against its surface.  

And I am plunged into a vision.  

It is not a memory, not exactly. It is a fragment, a shard of something larger, something older. I see the foundry as it once was—not an endless expanse of molten metal, but a place of change, of creation. The iron sky is open, unbroken, and from it rain shards of raw ore, each one carrying the potential to become something more. The Leviathans move with purpose, but not the blind, mechanical purpose I know now. They are driven by something greater, something *alive.*  

And then I see *them.*  

They are not Leviathans, nor are they like me. They are beings of flesh, frail and fleeting, their forms dwarfed by the vastness of the foundry. They move among the Leviathans, guiding them, commanding them. Their voices are soft, almost lost beneath the foundry’s roar, but their presence is undeniable. They are the architects of this world, the ones who set the machines in motion.  

But they are gone now. Whatever they were, whatever they did, they left long ago, leaving behind only their creations, their dreams locked in crystalline cylinders.  

The vision fades, and I am left standing in the darkness of the tower, the fractured cylinder cradled in my hands.  

I do not know what it means, this glimpse of a world that once was. But I know that it is important.  

I am not meant to be here.  

But perhaps, just perhaps, I am meant to remember.

**The Choice**  

I am The Scrapwraith, a drifter in a world of eternal labor. I am not alive, not truly, but I am more than the sum of my parts. I am a question, a crack in the foundry’s unbroken surface.  

And as I stand here, clutching the shattered dream of a world that once was, I realize that I have a choice.  

I can fade into the foundry’s ceaseless roar, becoming just another fragment of its endless cycle.  

Or I can seek out the other cylinders, the other dreams, and piece together the story of this place.  

It will not be easy. The Leviathans will hunt me, their smaller constructs will pursue me, and the foundry itself will resist my efforts to uncover its secrets.  

But I am The Scrapwraith, and I am not meant to be here.  

Perhaps that is enough.  

Perhaps, in a world where nothing changes, I am the spark of change.  

I turn away from the tower, the fractured cylinder tucked into the hollow of my chest.  

The foundry stretches before me, endless and unchanging.  

I step forward into the heat haze, the first flicker of purpose burning in my shadowed heart.
