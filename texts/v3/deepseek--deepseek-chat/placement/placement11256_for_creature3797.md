My limbs are long and slender, fingers tipped with delicate filaments that sense the subtle vibrations in the air and water, allowing me to navigate the chamber’s hidden currents.

My face is smooth, featureless save for a pair of deep, shadowed hollows where eyes might have been. My mouth is a soft, indistinct line, and when I speak, it is not with words but with the resonant echoes of dripping water and the faint rustle of moss. My voice is the chamber itself, its whispers carried on the mist.

The cairn is not just a marker but a fulcrum, a point of balance between the chamber’s shifting realities. My purpose is to maintain this equilibrium, to ensure that the boundaries between water and stone, life and stillness, do not dissolve entirely. I am the guardian of this liminal space, the one who listens to its breath and answers with my own.

The Hollowed Ones in the walls are my silent kin, their long, slow movements a reflection of the chamber’s eternal patience. I feel their dreams as ripples in the water, their memories as faint currents in the air. They are ancient, older than the chamber itself, and their whispers guide me in my duties. When the water rises, I watch over them, ensuring their slumber remains undisturbed. When the chamber shifts, I adjust the cairn’s stones, recalibrating the delicate balance of the world around me.

Sometimes, when travelers stumble into the chamber, I watch from the shadows, my form blending seamlessly with the moss and stone. I do not reveal myself unless they linger too long, their presence threatening the chamber’s fragile harmony. When I must intervene, I emerge from the mist, my form shifting to instill both awe and caution. I guide them back to the fissure, ensuring they leave before the chamber claims them.

I am the Keeper of the Cairn, the guardian of this submerged pocket of existence. My world is one of quiet resonance, of shifting boundaries and eternal patience. I am both its caretaker and its reflection, a being woven into the very fabric of this hidden place. And as long as the chamber breathes, so too shall I.
