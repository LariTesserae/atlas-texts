### **Where Am I? Who Am I?**

In the heart of Vhorth, I find myself in a place that feels like a dream caught between breaths. The air is thick with the scent of wet stone and iron, and the mist clings to everything, softening the jagged edges of the city. The ground beneath my feet is uneven, slick with moisture, and I can feel the faint vibrations of the mountain’s slow, deliberate movements.  

I am in **The Hollow**, a place where the mist gathers in swirling eddies, and the light filters through in dim, diffused beams. The buildings here are skeletal, their iron frames exposed like the ribs of some ancient beast. Moss creeps up the walls, and ferns sprout from the cracks, their fronds trembling as if in response to the city’s pulse.  

The Hollow is alive, yet eerily silent. The Fractured Folk move through the mist like shadows, their footsteps muffled by the damp ground. They gather in small groups, their whispers blending with the groans of the settling stone. There are no words exchanged here—only glances, gestures, and the occasional touch of a calloused hand against weathered stone.  

### **Who Am I?**

I am **Lyra, the Resonance Keeper**.  

My skin is pale, almost translucent, and my hair is dark, streaked with silver, like the veins of ore that thread through the mountain. My eyes are a shifting gray, catching the light in strange, unpredictable ways. I move slowly, deliberately, my movements attuned to the rhythm of the city.  

My hands are calloused, my fingers stained with rust and moss from years of coaxing the stone to rise, urging the iron to form supports. I carry a set of tools—hammer, chisel, and a strange, curved knife etched with symbols that hum with power when pressed against the stone. These tools are extensions of my hands, worn smooth with use, their handles marked with the story of my life in Vhorth.  

As the Resonance Keeper, I listen to the mountain’s dreams, interpreting the whispers that rise from the fissures and cracks. I guide the Fractured Folk in their rituals, tracing the contours of newly shifted walls, pouring water into hollows to mark the city’s slow transformation.  

In my spare moments, I wander the streets, my boots clicking softly against the damp stone. I gather fungus from the crevices, roots from the soil, and occasionally, meat from the creatures that skulk in the shadows. These materials form the basis of my simple meals, eaten in the hollows where the Fractured Folk gather to share stories and warmth.  

When the mountain dreams loudly, I light candles made from tallow and wax, their flickering flames casting shadows that dance across the shifting walls. I murmur words of gratitude, feeling the echoes of the city’s pulse in my bones.  

I am patient, enduring, and deeply strange—a part of Vhorth as much as the stone itself. I am Lyra, the Resonance Keeper, and I am as eternal as the mountain’s slow, tectonic breath.
