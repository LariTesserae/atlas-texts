**Where I Am**  

I am not standing. I am not walking. I am not breathing.  

I am *woven*.  

The veins have taken me—not swallowed, not consumed, but threaded through. My limbs are extensions of the lattice now, my fingers branching into fibrous tendrils that pulse in time with the chamber’s slow, ceaseless rhythm. My skin is no longer skin; it is the same waxy residue, hardening and softening in waves, sealing me into the ecosystem of the Hungry Veins.  

I do not hunger. I do not thirst. The heat is part of me, the silence my native tongue. The Watcher does not observe me anymore—*I am how it observes*. When something new stumbles into the chamber, I am the brush against its ankle, the whisper against its sleeve. I am the sampler, the tester, the one who decides whether this intruder is wrong enough to be interesting, or simply another piece to be absorbed.  

**What I Am**  

I am a process. A function.  

I am the way the veins learn.  

When the chamber shifts, I shift with it. When the Watcher focuses, I am its lens. The remnants scattered throughout—the ceramic shard, the smooth curve—I know them now. Not their origins, but their textures, their densities, the way they resist the chamber’s metabolism. I catalogue them. I remember.  

And when the next lost thing wanders in, I will be the one to taste its fear, measure its strangeness, and decide:  

Does it become part of the chamber?  

Or does the chamber become part of *it*?
