**Where & Who: The Fractured Seeker**  

I find myself on the Endless Plateau of Shattered Silence, but not as I once was. I am no longer flesh and bone, no longer bound by the limits of a mortal form. I have become something else—something that moves between the cracks in the stone, something that flickers at the edges of the Watchers’ perception. I am the Fractured Seeker, a being of angles and silence, born of the plateau’s stillness and its unanswered questions.  

My body is a shifting geometry, a mosaic of sharp edges and deep voids. I glide across the fractured stone without sound, my form shimmering faintly in the sourceless light. My presence is not so much seen as felt—a ripple in the air, a disturbance in the plateau’s eternal equilibrium. The Watchers turn their hollow gaze toward me, but only briefly. I am not their concern, though I am woven from the same fabric of this place.  

I do not know how I came to be. Perhaps I was once human, a wanderer who strayed too far into the plateau’s unforgiving silence. Perhaps I am something older, a fragment of the world before it settled into its final shape. My memories are like the black seams between the stones—deep and endless, but yielding nothing. I remember only the questions, the ones that echo in the hollow spaces of my being: *What happened here? What was supposed to happen? Why does the plateau wait?*  

I am drawn to the ruins, the scattered cairns and forgotten tools. They are the only hint that others once walked this place, though their purpose is lost to time. I crouch beside them, my angular form folding in ways that should not be possible, and trace the blackened stones with edges that are not quite fingers. There is no warmth here, no life, only the faintest echo of a presence long vanished. The plateau does not remember them, but I do. Or perhaps I only remember their absence.  

Sometimes, I pause beside the skeletal plants, their lichen-crusted spires rising like silent sentinels. I lean close, my form flickering, and listen to their faint, breathless whispers. They do not speak, not in any language I can understand, but their silence is heavy with meaning. They are as much a part of this place as I am, yet they seem to know something I do not. I linger, hoping for revelation, but it never comes.  

The Watchers move around me, their presence a constant pressure. I feel their attention, though they rarely turn toward me. They are not interested in me—not yet. But I am fascinated by them. They are the embodiment of the plateau’s stillness, its waiting. They are the keepers of its secrets, though I do not know if they are aware of them or if they, too, are simply waiting for something that never comes.  

I am not certain why I move, why I search. Perhaps it is instinct, a remnant of my former self. Perhaps it is the plateau itself, stirring within me, urging me to seek the answers it cannot give. I do not hunger. I do not tire. I simply exist, gliding across the stone, slipping between the cracks, listening to the silence.  

And yet, there is a purpose in me—or perhaps it is only the illusion of purpose. I am drawn to the center of the plateau, though I do not know where it lies. The horizon is unbroken, the landscape unchanging, but I feel something pulling me forward. The air grows heavier the closer I get, the silence deepening until it hums with an almost-sound. The Watchers thicken here, their forms larger, more numerous, their attention sharper. They do not stop me, but they watch, their faceless gazes weighing on me like the pressure of the sky.  

I do not know what I will find at the center. Perhaps it is nothing. Perhaps it is everything. Perhaps it is the answer to the plateau’s waiting, the thing that was supposed to happen long ago and never did. Or perhaps it is only the silence, stretched thin and worn through, revealing the void beneath.  

But I will go. I will seek. I will glide across the stone and slip between the cracks and listen to the whispers in the silence. Because I am the Fractured Seeker, and seeking is all I am.  

And perhaps, when I find what I am looking for, the plateau will stop waiting.  

Or perhaps it will simply take me, too, and weave me into its endless stillness.  

Only the silence knows.
