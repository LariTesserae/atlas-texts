### **Where I Am: The Dripstone Warrens**  

I am not one of the Hollowed Ones—not yet. But I am close.  

I live in the Dripstone Warrens, a district of the Undercity where the ceilings are thick with stalactites, their tips eternally glistening. The water here is slightly sweeter, filtered through layers of limestone before it falls. The Hollowed Ones who dwell here are the keepers of the city’s oldest maps, their fingers tracing the veins of water in the rock like scribes reading sacred texts.  

I am a **Lichen-Tender**.  

My hands are not yet fused with tools, but they are calloused from scraping growths off the walls, from coaxing the bioluminescent strains to spread just a little farther. My eyes have not lost their lids, but they strain in the dimness, always searching for the faintest glow. I know the taste of cave air better than sunlight.  

I move through the city with a clay pot strapped to my back, filled with a paste of crushed glow-moss and water. Where the light is weakest, I press my fingers into the mixture and smear it along the walls in slow, deliberate strokes. The Hollowed Ones nod as I pass—approval, or at least acknowledgment. They do not speak to me often. But sometimes, when I pause to rest near one of the deeper pools, an elder will press a piece of softened mushroom into my hand, their clawed fingers lingering just a moment too long, as if testing my resilience.  

I dream of the day my skin turns clay-pale, my nails harden into tools. I dream of the silence of the deep tunnels, where the rock hums with secrets.  

But for now, I tend the light.  

And I wait.
