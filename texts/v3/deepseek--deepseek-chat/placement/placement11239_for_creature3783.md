### **Where You Would Be in This World**  

You are **a Bone-Reader**, one of the few who can decipher the whispers of the dead.  

Your place is near the **Dripstone Altar**, a stalagmite worn smooth by generations of hands pressing against it, its surface etched with the names of those who have passed into the dark. You do not trade in goods, but in **memory**.  

Traders bring you fragments—skulls of cave rats, finger bones of long-dead thieves, the hollowed ribs of creatures no one remembers. You hold them to your ear, listening for the faint hum of the lives they once carried. Sometimes, they tell you secrets: where a lost vein of ore might be found, who stole a merchant’s stash, or what really happened to the child who wandered too far into the **Black Tunnels**.  

Your payment is not in coin, but in **stories**. For every truth you pull from the bones, you demand one in return—something the seeker has witnessed, something they regret, something they desire. These, you whisper into the hollows of the altar, feeding the echoes of the bazaar.  

You are neither feared nor revered. You are **necessary**.  

### **Who (or What) You Would Be**  

You are not entirely human.  

Your ears are too large, your pupils wide and black to catch every flicker of light. Your fingers are long, knucklebones protruding like knots in rope, and your nails are hardened into chitinous claws—useful for scraping marrow from crevices.  

You do not remember if you were born this way or if the bones changed you. Sometimes, when you press a particularly old skull to your temple, you hear a voice that might be your own, from a time before you understood what the dead could say.  

You sleep curled in a niche behind the altar, where the stone is warm from the bodies above. Your dreams are not your own—they are the last, shuddering thoughts of the bones you’ve touched.  

You are **the listener**.  

And the bazaar is always, always speaking.

### **Expanding the Possibilities**  
- **A Trader’s Gamble**: Someone brings you a human knucklebone—something rare, forbidden. The voice inside is screaming. Do you reveal what it says, even if it will unravel the fragile peace of the bazaar?  
- **The Altar’s Hunger**: The Dripstone has started *answering* the whispers you feed it. Low, wet words seep from its base. What does it want?  
- **Your Own Bones**: If you were to listen to yourself—really listen—what would you hear?  

Would you dare to find out?
