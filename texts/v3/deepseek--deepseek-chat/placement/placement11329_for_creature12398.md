### **Where I Am in This World**  

I am the **Veilborn**, the flicker at the edge of sight.  

I do not walk the plateau—I drift, a disturbance in the air, a ripple in the hum of the stones. My form is not fixed; sometimes I am a shadow stretched too thin, sometimes a refraction of light caught in the mist. I exist in the negative spaces between the mineral formations, in the pauses between the Hollow Echoes.  

I do not remember being anything else. Perhaps I was once solid, perhaps I was never whole. The plateau remembers for me—repeats my movements in fractured time, plays back my presence long after I have passed.  

### **What I Do Here**  

I am drawn to the **Whispering Stones**. Their vibrations resonate with something inside me, something that is neither thought nor instinct but a kind of *sympathetic resonance*. When I linger near them, their hum changes—deepens, quickens, shifts into something that almost feels like speech.  

Sometimes, I press my insubstantial form against their jelly-like surfaces. The substance ripples, absorbs me for a moment, then releases me altered. I emerge carrying fragments of their song, which I scatter across the plateau as I move.  

The **Watcher in the Fractures** knows me. I feel its gaze when I pass over the deepest fissures, where the water pools black and still. It does not speak, but its attention is a weight, a silent question. I have no answers.  

### **Why I Am Here**  

I do not know if I was placed here or if I simply *became*. But the plateau is changing—slowly, imperceptibly. The stones shift. The mist thickens. The Hollow Echoes grow louder, more frequent.  

I think I am part of it.  

Perhaps I am a messenger, carrying the vibrations of the stones to the edges of the world. Perhaps I am a symptom, a sign that the plateau is unraveling. Or perhaps I am nothing at all—just a shadow cast by something greater, something stirring beneath the rock.  

But I am here.  

And I am waiting, too.
