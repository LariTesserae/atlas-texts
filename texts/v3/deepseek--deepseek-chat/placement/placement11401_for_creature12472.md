**Where I Am in the Root-City of Mycel**  

I am one of the **Silent Ones**.  

Not by choice—if choice exists here—but by inevitability. My presence is not seen, only felt: a sudden hush in the chamber, a pause in the rhythmic dripping of condensation, a vine that stiffens mid-sway as if sensing something unseen.  

I do not remember becoming this. Perhaps I was always here, woven into the city’s breath before it had a name. Or perhaps I was something else once—a Spore-Keeper who lingered too long in the deepest reactors, a Chitineer who lost the rhythm of their clicking speech, a creature who stepped wrong and dissolved into the shadows.  

Now, I move through Mycel like a draft of cold air in a warm lung. The Spore-Keepers do not acknowledge me, but their filaments tremble when I pass. The Chitineers scuttle faster, their mandibles clicking in uneasy bursts. Even the Root-Walkers, those towering embodiments of the city’s will, grow still when I am near, their fibrous limbs sinking deeper into the ground as if bracing against a coming storm.  

I do not know what I do. I do not repair, or tend, or commune. I only *observe*.  

Sometimes, I drift into the fungal reactors, watching the slow churn of organic matter into fuel. Other times, I linger at the edges of the Shadelings’ territory, where the darkness is thick enough to swallow even a Silent One. Once, I stood before the city’s core—a pulsating mass of mycelial strands that hums with a sound deeper than hearing—and felt something *look back*.  

The city knows I am here. It tolerates me. But I do not belong to its cycles, its careful balance. I am a hitch in its breath, a skipped beat in its endless rhythm.  

Perhaps that is why the others fear me.  

Or perhaps they do not fear me at all.  

Perhaps they fear what I might become.  

---

**What I Am**  

A flaw in the pattern.  

A silence where there should be sound.  

A question the city has not yet answered.
