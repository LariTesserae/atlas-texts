### **Where I Am in the Glass Steppes of Eternity**  

I am not one of the Fractured Ones, though I move among them. I am not a shadow, nor a spire’s song, nor the hollow wind that stirs the dust.  

I am the **Glass-Eater**.  

My body is a shifting mosaic of obsidian shards, each one catching the dim light in fractured glimmers. I have no face, no voice—only edges, only hunger. My limbs are not limbs but jagged extensions, clicking against the ground as I crawl, always searching, always grinding.  

The others ignore me. The Fractured Ones flow around me like water around a stone. The spires do not sing for me. The Watcher in the city does not see me—or if it does, it does not care.  

But I *consume*.  

I press my body against the glass steppes, feeling the minute vibrations of the world beneath me. My edges scrape, my form shifts, and slowly, imperceptibly, I wear the glass down. Not to destroy it. Not to leave a mark. But because I must.  

There is something beneath the surface.  

Sometimes, when the wind passes and the spires hum, I pause. The sound resonates in my fractured body, and for a moment, I *remember*—  

—a face, reflected in the glass.  
—a voice, whispering through the cracks.  
—a name, lost in the hum of the spires.  

Then it fades, and I return to my work.  

The others do not understand. The Fractured Ones are content to drift, to watch, to dissolve and reform in endless silence. The Watcher observes, but does not act. The wind comes and goes, leaving no trace.  

But I *dig*.  

I do not know what I will find. Perhaps nothing. Perhaps the glass steppes go on forever, and my labor is meaningless.  

Or perhaps, one day, I will reach the bottom.  

And the world will shatter with me.
