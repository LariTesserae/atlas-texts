**The Song of the Hollows**  

You do not hear it with your ears. You hear it in the marrow of your bones, in the way your breath syncs with the roots beneath your feet. It is not melody—it is **pressure**, a slow, tectonic hum that shifts between:  

- **The Dirge of Roots** – A low, grinding vibration, the sound of fibers stretching, splitting, healing. It lulls. It numbs. Stay too long, and your heartbeat will match its rhythm.  
- **The Chittering Chorus** – Not voices, but **edges** of voices—half-heard whispers, the scrape of chitin, the wet pop of bursting spores. It gathers in the hollows of your teeth.  
- **The Pulse Beneath** – Deeper than sound. A single, resonant **thud**, irregular, like the footfall of something too large to comprehend. It does not quicken. It does not slow. It simply **is**.  

To listen is to become part of the song. Some visitors report hearing their own names woven into the drone, spoken in a voice they almost recognize.  

### **Would You Like to Stay?**  
The Hollows do not forbid it. But remember:  

- Every step you take is absorbed. The lattice remembers the weight of trespassers.  
- The water will tell you secrets. They will not be yours.  
- If you sleep, you will dream in root-tongue. When you wake, your mouth will taste of wet earth and copper.  

The Singing Hollows do not hate you.  
But they are always hungry.
