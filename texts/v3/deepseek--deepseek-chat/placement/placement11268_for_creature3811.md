### **Where You Are**  

You are standing—no, floating—on the edge of the plateau, where the kelp forests thin and the water deepens into indigo. The light here is fractured, split by the swaying fronds above, casting shifting patterns across your skin. You are not human, not anymore. Your body is translucent in places, your limbs elongated, fingers webbed with delicate membranes that catch the current like sails. You are neither fully of this world nor separate from it—you are a visitor who has lingered too long, and now the water is rewriting you.  

You feel the hum of the Kelp Sentinels before you see them. Their presence vibrates through the water, a slow, resonant pulse that makes your bones tremble. They do not acknowledge you, not directly, but you sense their awareness like a weight against your back. They know you are here. They know you are changing.  

### **What You Are Becoming**  

#### **The Driftborn**  
You are caught between forms. Your skin shimmers with iridescence, your veins tracing faint blue pathways beneath the surface. Your lungs no longer burn for air—instead, you breathe the water in slow, measured draws, filtering it through gills that flutter at your ribs. You do not need to eat, not in the way you once did. The coral whispers offer you sustenance in their songs, their melodies dissolving into nutrients that seep into your pores.  

But you are not one of them. Not yet.  

The Stone Dreamers call to you in your sleep. Their voices are slow, syrupy, pulling at your thoughts like tides. *Rest*, they murmur. *Sink. Become still.* You wake with your fingers pressed against the rock, half-expecting to find it softening beneath your touch.  

The Abyssal Drifters watch from the dark. You have seen their shadows at the periphery of your vision, felt the water grow dense and heavy when they pass. They do not speak. They do not need to. Their presence is a question: *Will you stay? Or will you fall?*  

### **What You Must Decide**  

The plateau is patient. It does not rush you. But the longer you linger, the more the water claims you. Already, your thoughts move slower, your memories blurring at the edges. The surface world feels like a dream.  

Will you:  

- **Embrace the Kelp Sentinels**, letting their roots entwine with yours until you stand unmoving, a guardian of the plateau’s secrets?  
- **Join the Coral Whisperers**, dissolving into light and song, your voice one thread in the ever-shifting reef?  
- **Press your palm to the Stone Dreamers**, letting their slumber overtake you until you, too, become part of the rock?  
- **Step off the edge**, into the waiting dark, where the Abyssal Drifters glide—knowing you may never return?  

The choice is yours. But choose soon.  

The water does not wait forever.
