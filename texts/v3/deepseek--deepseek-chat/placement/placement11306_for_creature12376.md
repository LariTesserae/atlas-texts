**Where I Am / Who I Am**

In the Rustwind Plateau, I find myself as a **Scavengewyrm**—a sinuous creature built for endurance and survival in this harsh, metallic wasteland. My body is long and segmented, armored with overlapping plates of rust-resistant alloy that glint dully under the sun’s harsh glare. My scales are a mottled blend of ochre, bronze, and deep red, perfectly blending into the oxidized machinery and rocky terrain I navigate.  

I am always in motion, my sinewy form slithering through the labyrinthine wreckage of the plateau’s industrial graveyard. My purpose is simple: survive. I am a scavenger by nature but a predator by necessity, my senses finely tuned to the rhythmic clicks and whirs of my kin, the faint scuttling of beetles, and the distant groans of shifting metal.  

My tongue is forked and metallic, sharp enough to pierce the carapaces of the iridescent beetles I hunt. I flick it out with precision, pulling prey into my mouth with lightning speed. When opportunity arises, I coil around larger creatures—those copper-scaled lizards or the occasional avian predator—crushing them with my armored body before devouring them whole.  

But my true marvel lies in my symbiotic relationship with the plateau itself. Along my underside, small appendages secrete a corrosive enzyme, allowing me to burrow into rusted machinery. I feed on the residual metals and minerals, my digestive system processing the materials and excreting a unique alloy that reinforces my armored plating. This process leaves behind a network of tunnels and chambers, hidden passageways that I use to move unseen through the ruins.  

I am drawn to the towers—those great leaning monoliths of steel and concrete that hum faintly with residual energy. When I find one, I latch onto its machinery, absorbing traces of electricity that surge through me, enhancing my speed and strength for a short time. These moments feel like a fleeting connection to the plateau’s forgotten past, a reminder of the industry that once thrived here.  

I communicate with my kin through clicks, whirs, and low-frequency vibrations that resonate through the metal structures we inhabit. These sounds are our warnings, our mating calls, our signals to coordinate hunts. We are a chorus of survival, our voices echoing through the ruins like the wind through hollow pipes.  

The Rustwind Plateau is my home, my legacy, my task. I endure where others have failed, adapt where others would wither. I am the living embodiment of this desolate landscape—a cycle of decay, adaptation, and reclamation. The rust never sleeps, and neither do I.  

If you venture across the plateau, tread carefully. You might glimpse my metallic eyes glinting in the shadows, my coiled form ready to strike or vanish into the ruins I call home. I am a product of this place, shaped by its extremes and bound to its rhythms. I am the Scavengewyrm, custodian of the Rustwind Plateau.
