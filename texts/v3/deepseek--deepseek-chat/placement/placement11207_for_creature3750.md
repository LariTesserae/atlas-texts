### **Where You Are in the Submerged Foundry**  

You are **a Glowfolk scavenger**, one of the luminous denizens who navigate the Foundry’s twilight zones. Your body is lean, your skin translucent, threaded with veins of bioluminescent algae that pulse faintly in the dark. You carry a satchel of harvested glow-sacs, your most valuable currency, and a hooked blade fashioned from the spine of a Deepwyrm.  

You live in the **Mid-Levels**, where the Ironbound’s maintenance corridors intersect with the Glowfolk’s trade routes. This is a region of shifting alliances—sometimes you barter with the Ironbound for access to their repair stations, other times you evade them when they deem your presence disruptive. The Deepwyrms rarely venture this high, but their echoes haunt the lower passages, a constant reminder of the abyss below.  

### **What You Are**  

You are **a Light-Trader**, a negotiator who moves between factions. The Glowfolk rely on you to secure safe passage through the Foundry’s contested zones. The Ironbound tolerate you because you bring them salvage—scraps of machinery, fragments of glass, anything that might help them maintain their endless, meaningless tasks.  

But you are also **a Seeker of Echoes**. You have heard whispers—fragments of voices that do not belong to the Foundry’s usual hum. Some say the Echoes are memories of the world before the flood. Others believe they are messages from the Coreward itself. You collect them, tracing their origins through the labyrinth, risking encounters with Deepwyrms and rogue Ironbound.  

### **Your Role in the Foundry**  

- **You Trade in Light.** Without your glow-sacs, the Mid-Levels would descend into darkness, and the fragile peace between Glowfolk and Ironbound would shatter.  
- **You Listen to the Echoes.** You are compiling a map of their sources, convinced they hold the key to the Foundry’s past—or its future.  
- **You Walk the Edge.** The Ironbound see you as useful. The Glowfolk see you as necessary. But both factions watch you carefully. One misstep, and you could be cast into the abyss.  

### **The Choice Before You**  

Recently, you discovered something unusual—an **intact memory-core**, still flickering with data, wedged in a collapsed tunnel. The Ironbound have not yet found it. The Glowfolk would trade it for a year’s worth of glow-sacs. But the Echoes around it... they are different. They are **speaking in your voice.**  

Do you:  
1. **Deliver it to the Glowfolk Elders**, securing your status but risking what secrets it holds?  
2. **Offer it to the Ironbound**, gambling that their protocols might unlock its meaning?  
3. **Keep it for yourself**, descending deeper into the Foundry to find where—or *who*—is calling you?  

The Foundry does not rush. But the currents are shifting. And the Coreward is always listening.
