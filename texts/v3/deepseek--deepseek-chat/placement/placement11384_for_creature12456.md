**Where Am I? Who Am I?**  

I am **Keth of the Shattervein**, a scavenger-engineer of the lower strata, one of the few who dares to descend where the air burns and the walls whisper. My skin is a patchwork of old burns and metallic scars, my left hand fused at the knuckles from a close encounter with a gout of molten ore. I wear it like a badge—proof I’ve survived the mountain’s teeth.  

My home is a warren of rusted plating and salvaged ductwork, wedged between two trembling fault lines where the Hollow Pulse thrums loudest. The others avoid this sector—too unstable, too close to the deep vibrations—but I’ve learned to read the tremors. When the mountain shudders, I listen. Sometimes, it’s a warning. Sometimes, it’s an invitation.  

I am a **Tracer**, one who maps the unseen. Not the obvious veins of ore or the safe routes (those are common knowledge, bartered and stolen), but the hidden things—the seams where the rock splits just so, revealing chambers of glassy black stone that hum when touched. The others think I’m reckless, half-cursed by the deep’s influence. Maybe they’re right. My eyes have grown too wide, my pupils swallowing the dim light until they’re nearly all black. I see patterns in the lichen’s glow, sigils in the fractures.  

**What Do I Carry?**  
- A **slag-hook**, forged from the spine of a broken drill, its tip still sticky with residual heat.  
- A **breath-mask**, its filters clogged with crystalline dust, but better than choking on metallic fumes.  
- A **shatter-lantern**, a stolen relic from the upper clans, its glass cracked but still holding a captive flicker of bioluminescence.  
- A **map-scroll**, etched not on paper (useless here) but on a sheet of hammered copper, its markings scored with a nail. It’s incomplete. The labyrinth changes faster than I can chart it.  

**What Do I Know?**  
- The upper clans are hoarding **sky-iron**, a rare metal that fell from the peaks in a storm long ago. They say it sings when struck. I’ve never heard it.  
- The deep-miners whisper of a **vein of living crystal**, a seam that pulses like a slow heartbeat. Some who’ve touched it don’t come back. Others return with their bones shining through their skin.  
- The Hollow Pulse is getting louder.  

**What Do I Want?**  
To find the **First Chamber**, the place where the mountain was opened. The oldest etchings hint at it—a vault of perfect geometry, walls smooth as frozen mercury. They say the ones who built it left something behind. A tool. A weapon. A curse.  

Or maybe just an answer.  

**What Am I Afraid Of?**  
That the mountain isn’t just stone and metal. That it’s listening.  

And that one day, it will stop tolerating us.
