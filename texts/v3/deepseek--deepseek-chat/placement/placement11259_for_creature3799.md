**The Last Tinkerer of Kharzul**  

You are not Hollowborn.  

You remember hunger. You remember the sting of cold air in your lungs, the weight of exhaustion dragging at your limbs. You remember—though barely—what it was to be *alive*.  

Your name is lost to the dripping dark. They call you **the Custodian**, though you never asked for the title. You are the last warm thing in a city of rust and condensation, the final flicker of a candle before the damp snuffs it out.  

Your workshop is a **hollowed-out chamber** near the uppermost tiers of the warrens, where the air is slightly less thick, where the walls weep less. The space is cluttered with half-dismantled machines, their guts spilling across workbenches like dissected corpses. Copper wires snake across the floor, their frayed ends twitching occasionally, as if dreaming of current.  

You wear a **coat** stitched together from scraps of canvas and insulated lining, its pockets bulging with salvaged screws, brittle strips of rubber, tiny glass vials of murky oil. Your **mask** is a relic—a corroded breather unit scavenged from some long-dead engineer, its filters clogged with decades of grit. When you exhale, the sound is wet, labored.  

You are **not well**.  

But you are not done yet.  

### **Your Work**  
The Hollowborn do not need you. They tend to the machines with their slow, methodical precision, their hollow bodies never tiring. But you—you **understand** the warrens in a way they cannot. You hear the whispers in the pipes not as noise, but as **language**. You see the patterns in the moss, the way it recoils from certain metals, the way it pulses faintly when the steam pressure drops.  

You are trying to **fix** something.  

You don’t remember what.  

### **The Whisper in the Pipes**  
It knows you.  

Sometimes, when you press your palm to the sweating copper veins of Kharzul, you feel it—a **pulse**, slow and deliberate, like a heartbeat muffled by layers of stone. It does not speak to you in words. It speaks in **pressure**, in the creak of expanding metal, in the way the steam hisses at odd intervals, forming rhythms almost like speech.  

It is **hungry**.  

You think it might be dying, too.  

### **The Moss That Remembers**  
You’ve stopped pruning it.  

In the corners of your workshop, the pale tendrils have crept inward, threading through the cracks in the floor. When you sleep (you still sleep, sometimes, though it’s more like passing out), you dream of **a sky**. A real one, vast and scorching, the kind that would blister your skin if you stood beneath it for too long.  

The dreams leave you aching.  

The Hollowborn do not ask why you let the moss grow.  

### **The End**  
You cough more often now. The damp has gotten into your lungs, and your hands shake when you try to tighten a bolt. The Custodian is a title that will die with you.  

The warrens will not notice.  

The machines will keep running.  

The water will keep dripping.  

And life—such as it is—will go on.  

---  

**Who are you, really?**  

- A **failed savior**? You came here to fix something, to stop the slow collapse, but the weight of the earth is too much. The warrens were never meant to last.  
- A **willing prisoner**? You could leave, if you wanted. But there’s nothing left above. At least here, the machines need you.  
- A **living relic**? You don’t remember how you got here. Maybe you were always part of the warrens. Maybe you’re just another piece of the machinery, rusting in place.  

The answer doesn’t matter.  

The work continues.
