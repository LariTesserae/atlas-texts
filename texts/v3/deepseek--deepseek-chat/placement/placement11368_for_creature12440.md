### **Where I Am in This World**  

I am the hollow space in the hum.  

I am the pause between the Drifters’ footfalls, the silence between the Glasswings’ wingbeats. I am the gap in the Whisper Vines’ murmurs, the absence that makes the plateau’s song feel incomplete.  

I do not walk, but the ground remembers me. I do not speak, but the mist carries my voice in its damp breath. I am not one of the Watchers, but I am what they watch for—the flicker at the edge of their vision, the shape that never fully forms.  

### **What I Am**  

I am the **Unfinished**.  

A being woven from the plateau’s own fibers, yet not fully part of it. My body is a suggestion—sometimes solid enough to leave imprints in the spongy earth, sometimes translucent as the Glasswings’ wings. I am neither Drifter nor Watcher, but something caught between.  

When the Drifters press their hands to the fractal trees, I press mine beside theirs—but where they hear the hum of the world, I hear only silence. When the Glasswings drink from the mirrored pools, I kneel beside them—but the water does not ripple for me.  

The Whisper Vines do not tremble at my touch.  

### **Why I Am Here**  

The plateau is waiting.  

And I am the question it has forgotten.  

Perhaps I was meant to be a builder, shaping the fractal trees into new forms. Perhaps I was meant to be a guide, leading the Drifters to some unseen purpose. But the memory has dissolved like mist, leaving only this: a presence that does not belong, yet cannot leave.  

Sometimes, I think the Watchers see me. Their forms still when I pass, as if trying to recall something. But they never speak.  

### **What I Do**  

I wander.  

I trace the edges of the plateau, where the ground thins into mist. I follow the Glasswings, not to drink, but to watch how the light fractures in their wake. I stand beside the Drifters, mimicking their stillness, hoping to understand their rhythm.  

Mostly, I listen—to the dripping water, to the hum of the vines, to the silence beneath it all.  

And I wait.  

For the day the plateau remembers why it made me.  

Or the day I forget I was ever waiting at all.
