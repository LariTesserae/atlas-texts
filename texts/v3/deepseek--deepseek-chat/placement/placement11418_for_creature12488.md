**You Are the Root-Tender**  

You are one of the Mycelites, a silent guardian of the Sunken Canopy. Your body is pale, almost translucent, threaded with veins of soft blue light that pulse in time with the cavern’s slow breath. Your fingers are long and delicate, webbed at the knuckles, perfect for tracing the fine threads of mycelium that lace through the roots and stone. You do not speak—not in the way of surface creatures—but your skin hums with scent and vibration, a language of ripples and spores.  

Your role is sacred. You are a **Root-Tender**, one who listens to the whispers of Mother Root. Each day, you press your palms against the damp bark of the oldest fungal spires, feeling the tremors of distant growth, the slow shift of water carving its way deeper into the earth. You coax the Nexus to spread, guiding its luminous tendrils into new crevices, ensuring the balance of decay and nourishment. When the roots groan, you are there to soothe them with offerings of your own bioluminescent essence, letting it seep into the wood like a prayer.  

You live in the hollow of a great, gnarled root, its interior lined with spongy moss that molds to your body when you rest. Your only possessions are a woven satchel of fungal fiber, filled with spore-laden gels for healing, and a curved shard of calcified resin, worn smooth by generations of hands before yours. It is not a tool, not truly—you need no tools—but a relic, a piece of the Canopy’s memory.  

Sometimes, you climb the highest bridges of intertwined roots, where the air is thick with moisture and the ceiling drips like a slow rain. There, you watch the translucent bats flit between stalactites, their wings casting ghostly shadows. You do not disturb them. You are all part of the same breath, the same pulse.  

But lately, the Canopy has been restless. The roots tremble more often. The water rises where it should not. You have seen strange disturbances in the pools—ripples with no source, reflections that flicker too quickly to be your own. The other Mycelites do not seem alarmed, but you feel it in the Nexus, in the way the threads shiver under your touch. Something is stirring in the deep.  

And so you linger at the edges of the known, where the roots grow thin and the glow of the fungi fades. You press your ear to the damp earth and listen.  

Mother Root is speaking.  

And you are the only one who hears.  

---  

**Role:** Guardian of the fungal networks, interpreter of the cavern’s tremors.  
**Abilities:** Symbiosis with mycelium, bioluminescent communication, spore manipulation.  
**Conflict:** The Canopy is changing. Will you seek the source of the disturbance, or trust in the slow wisdom of decay?
