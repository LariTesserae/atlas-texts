**The Dry Choir Below the Final Plain**

Above it there is only a low plain, worn to the last patience of land. No hills remain there except the memory of hills. No channels cut it, no riverbeds, no shorelines, no depressions deep enough to suggest that liquid ever gathered or passed. The surface is a fine mineral powder, salt-white and charcoal-grey, rubbed down by ages into a nearly level skin. It has reached its final form: an old, cool, settled table of the world, so low and so still that it seems less a landscape than the end of landscape.
