## The High Birthwater

You are submerged.

The first fact is not darkness, but height. This is a high ocean, lifted far above the planet’s older basins and lowlands, a cool body of water resting on a planetary shelf of young crust. Its surface is a bright, hammered ceiling under ordinary daylight. The light is normal: honest shadows, a white-gold sun, blue distance, no strange color cast, no symbolic glow. That normality makes the rest more disturbing.
