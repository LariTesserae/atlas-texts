What lives there is not a simple list of creatures. It is a pressure of lives, a packed and overlapping existence in which every inhabitant is also a tool, a food, a wall, a road, a sound, a season. The country is inhabited the way a body is inhabited: not by tenants, but by organs, appetites, and habits.
