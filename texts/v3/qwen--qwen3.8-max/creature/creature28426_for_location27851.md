## What Lives on the High Sill

Nothing on the High Sill lives in the way lowland life lives.

Nothing there is wet inside. Nothing breathes with lungs, beats with a heart, hunts with appetite, or flees from pain. There are no tracks because there are no feet adapted to that ground. There are no bones because nothing there has ever needed an inner scaffold for quick motion. There are no eggs, no nests, no dung, no burrows, no signs of hunger passing from body to body. If one goes looking for animals, the High Sill will seem empty. If one goes looking for people, it will seem abandoned.

But it is not empty.

What lives there is slow, dry, tuned, and mineral.
