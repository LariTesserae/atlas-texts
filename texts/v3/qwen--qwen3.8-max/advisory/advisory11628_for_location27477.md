## Preparing to Visit the Wet City Basin  
*A practical preparation guide for an ordinary human multiverse traveler*

This is not a location you “sightsee” casually. It is a lived-in, saturated, humid, loud, mineral-and-fungal urban ecology where water is not scenery but operating system. An ordinary human can visit safely, even comfortably, but only if they prepare for four dominant conditions:

1. **Permanent dampness and warmth**  
2. **Extreme, continuous sound**  
3. **Very high density and constant movement**  
4. **A living environment with no plants, abundant fungi, microbes, insects, birds, aquatic animals, and civic machinery**
