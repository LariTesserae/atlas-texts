# Preparing to Visit the Court of Small Rains

The Court of Small Rains should not be approached like a cave, a ruin, a wilderness hollow, or a scenic underground chamber. It is closer to entering the inside of an old, inhabited instrument: a warm, damp, crowded, acoustic room where water, birds, people, repair, and sound are all working together. An ordinary human traveler should prepare less like an explorer and more like a careful guest who is about to step into someone else’s delicate household machinery.

The most important preparation is mental and behavioral: you must become quiet, observant, repair-minded, and unwilling to waste anything or make unnecessary noise. The second most important preparation is physical: protect your lungs, ears, feet, balance, and skin from damp warmth, bird colonies, fungal growth, slick surfaces, and constant sound. The third is social: you are entering a place where every person, animal, tool, echo, drip, and broken pot has a role. You should not behave as if anything there is unowned, unused, or meaningless.
