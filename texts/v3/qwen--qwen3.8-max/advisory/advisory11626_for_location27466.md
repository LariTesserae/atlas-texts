An ordinary human multiverse traveler should prepare for this place as if it were simultaneously a high-altitude dry mineral field, a sealed hot clean-room, an electrostatic refinery, and a draft reality whose geometry has not yet decided what it intends to keep.

The hollow is not hostile in the way a predator is hostile, but it is profoundly unsuitable for an unprepared body. It is hot, dry, sharp, charged, chemically raw, perceptually unreliable, and ecologically closed. It does not need you, does not expect you, and will not soften itself for you. The correct preparation is therefore not adventurous but conservative: reduce heat load, eliminate free water, protect the lungs and skin from mineral dust, prevent static discharge, maintain a physical connection to the outside, and accept that your senses may misreport the room.

The best preparation is to decide, before entering, that you will probably not need to enter at all. If instruments can observe the hollow from the seam, use instruments. If a tethered probe can enter first, use a probe. If the place is merely to be witnessed, witness it briefly from the threshold. An ordinary human body is a wet, warm, shedding, breathing contaminant in an anhydrous prototype world. If you must enter, you should do so as a sealed guest, not as an explorer claiming space.

## 1. Understand what kind of place you are preparing for

This is not a cave. It is not a hot spring. It is not a greenhouse. It is closer to a sealed mineral process chamber that is still being generated.

The main hazards are:

- **High altitude outside**: thin air, pale dust, exposure, possible ultraviolet stress, difficult approach terrain.
- **Anhydrous chemistry**: no water anywhere, not even as memory. Human moisture is a contaminant and a hazard.
- **Severe dry heat**: not merely sun-warmed stone, but active working heat, thermal columns, slow cycling, and surfaces that may retain or misdirect warmth.
- **Sharp, immature terrain**: brittle salts, crystalline crusts, silica, metallic oxides, copper-bright dust, black veins, unweathered edges.
- **Electrostatic and ionic activity**: charged haze, filament leaves, blue-white discharge ticks, dust welding, potential differences, unpredictable static accumulation.
