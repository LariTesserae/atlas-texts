**The Visitation**

If a benevolent and wise power turned its attention to the Nulltide Cistern, the first thing they would do is not act.

They would not come as fire. They would not come as command. They would not split the black cirque, drain the suspended water, or force the buried engines to confess their original purpose. They would not treat the cistern as a problem to be corrected, nor the stillness as a sin to be punished. Wisdom of that kind understands that a place built against change is not healed by being subjected to another violence. Rescue without consent is only weather with a better name.

So they would enter first as attention.

The mountain would not know it. The dry wind outside would continue its thin labor over fresh glass. The obsidian panels would keep their cruel, unweathered edges. The boundary would remain a boundary: a condition, not a wall. But inside the drowned volume, something would alter so slightly that only the cistern itself might notice.

A quality of being seen would arrive in the water.
