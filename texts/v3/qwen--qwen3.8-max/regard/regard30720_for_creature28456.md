Not words, but pressures. Not sentences, but intervals. A language made of tremor, absence, and return: the sudden gap in a cricket’s rasp that means *danger moving*, the long hollow tone under a tower that means *air is turning*, the dry tick of mandibles on old wall that means *this place is sound*, the silence of a grove when spore cases hold themselves closed too long, meaning *something is wrong in the soil of the air*. Even their stillness has grammar. Even their crowding has punctuation. To be among them is to be inside a speech one has not yet learned to hear
