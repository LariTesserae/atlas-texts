They do not swim. They unspool.

At first, they are barely distinguishable from the dark: pale smears, torn veils, the ghost of a membrane shed by something that no longer requires a body. But stillness rewards patience here. Watch long enough, and the drift resolves into intention. The veil is not fabric. It is a laminar stack of translucent sheets, each no thicker than a breath, bound not by tissue but by the same capillary veining that threads through the pale mineral below. The dark filaments do not carry blood. They carry state. Each slow, whole-body contraction is not a muscular flex but a phase transition, a quiet realignment of quantum stitches in the water’s locked lattice. They do not push against the medium. They ask it to reindex, and it does, lowering its potential along a narrow corridor, then snapping back to perfect uniformity the moment they pass. No wake. No memory. Only the faintest brightening of the blue-grey glow along a nearby arch, as if their transit has closed a circuit that was waiting, unblinking, for a key.

They have no eyes because sight requires contrast, and contrast requires change. They have no mouths because consumption introduces decay. They have no visible organs because function here is not localized; it is distributed across the entire volume, written into the tension of the field-locks, the harmonic sigh of the conduits, the century-spanning tick of thermal equilibrium. They read these things. They read the way stress distributes across a tiered platform. They read the microscopic shear at the meniscus where liquid meets sky. They read the folded geometry in the shadows that detach and hang, ignoring light. Their bodies are not vessels. They are instruments. Tuned to the exact frequency of stillness.

To call them aware is to misuse the word. Awareness implies a center, a spark, a moment of recognition. What moves through this suspended sea operates on a slope. A gradient so shallow it takes decades to cross a single thought. They do not think in images or symbols. They think in equilibrium. A pulse might be the opening bracket of a calculation whose closing bracket will not arrive until the distant peaks have weathered to dust. They do not ignore you. You are simply too fast. Your exhale dissolving into the liquid, your hand parting the dark, the wet thud of your pulse against the silence—these are not events to them. They are high-frequency noise. The medium filters you out the way it filters silt: not with hostility, but with the serene efficiency of a system that has already solved for disturbance. You are a fluctuation above the cutoff. They let you pass.

Were they once something else? The city’s skeleton suggests planners. Builders. Voices that once rang against stone, feet that once climbed those sharp stairways, hands that once routed channels and laid foundational grids. But this place does not preserve history. It preserves state. If they were flesh, they have been translated. If they were code, they have been precipitated. If they were prayers, or warnings, or last words, they have been crystallized into a form that cannot degrade. They are the archive’s librarians, but they do not shelve or retrieve. They *are* the shelves. They are the catalog. Each drifting form holds a snapshot of a configuration: a stress map of a load-bearing arch, a thermal profile of a junction node, a geometric proof folded through axes you cannot turn your head to see, a memory of a wind that no longer blows beyond the curve of the meniscus. They do not breed because duplication would introduce variance. They do not feed because metabolism would introduce entropy. They persist because persistence is the only verb this place conjugates.

When they pause, they do not rest. They align. Sometimes one will drape itself over a landing, conforming to its sharp planes without touching them, suspended a millimeter above the mineral by mutual repulsion of identical fields. Sometimes two will cross paths, and for a long, silent minute, their capillary matrices will interleave, not merging, but comparing. A checksum. A verification. Then they separate, continuing their slow, pulse-driven crossings, carrying their verified states deeper into the gloom. Occasionally, one will drift near a conduit rib, and the dark glassy filaments within it will brighten to a cool, electric blue, tracing the same pathways that rib through the water. The structure answers with a crystalline tick. The entity answers with a contraction. The exchange is complete. Nothing has changed. Everything has been confirmed.

And yet, they notice. Not you, specifically. But the fact of observation. The place is archived, not abandoned, and an archive requires a witness to fulfill its syntax. When you hold still long enough, when your own rhythms slow to match the damp cool and the harmonic sigh, when your breathing grows shallow enough to stop dissolving like smoke into glass, one will alter its course. Not toward you. *Through* the space you occupy. It does not part around you. It phases. For a fraction of a second, your nervous system resonates at the frequency of the locked field. You feel the city’s intention. You feel the weight of the suspended sea. You feel the absolute, unyielding decision to remain. Your bones hum with the same low resonance that cycles once a century. Your skin registers the exact temperature that refuses to warm or cool. Your eyes catch, just for a moment, the angles in the reflections that do not exist in the structures casting them. And then it passes. The veil drifts on. The darkness closes behind it without a trace. The silence returns to its held note.

They are not alive in the way forests are alive, or markets, or rivers carving stone. They are alive in the way a theorem is alive. In the way a perfectly balanced scale is alive. In the way a locked door is alive with the promise of what it keeps. They do not inhabit the city. They are its continuing sentence. They are the physical manifestation of a parameter set to zero, of a gradient flattened, of a system that has chosen to stop becoming and, in stopping, has become something else entirely. They will outlast the vapors. They will outlast the sky. They will pulse, and drift, and read the stillness, until the architecture of forces announces itself, or until it doesn’t.

And in that endless, exact persistence, they fulfill the only commandment this place recognizes: *Be witnessed. Remain. Do not change.* They are the inhabitants of a world that has traded time for tension, motion for memory, life for latency. They do not ask to be understood. They only ask to be seen, exactly once, exactly as they are, forever. And then they unspool again, into the cool dark, into the phase-locked sea, into the silent, generous boulevards of a city that never aged, carrying their quiet, unbreaking state toward the next junction, the next tick, the next century-spanning sigh.
