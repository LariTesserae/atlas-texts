## I. PHYSIOLOGICAL & ATMOSPHERIC SAFEGUARDS

- **Primary:** Full-face positive-pressure rebreather with multi-stage electrostatic precipitation + molecular sieve matrix. Must be rated for programmable matter aerosols (look for ISO-Ω or Multiverse Standard MS-7 certification).

- **Purge Protocol:** Carry 3 doses of refined Stasis-Fern sap (see Section V). Administer sublingually at first sign of lung weight. The sap triggers rapid mucosal shedding of bound substrate. It tastes of copper and cold mint; it will make you vomit clear fluid. This is normal.

### 2. Thermal & Humidity Management
Despite high elevation, the basin radiates humid warmth (approx. 28–32°C / 82–90°F with 95%+ relative humidity). The air feels thick because it is saturated with condensing substrate, not just water vapor.
- **Base Layer:** Hydrophobic, capillary-wicking synthetic. No cotton, no wool, no natural fibers. They will absorb slurry and crystallize against your skin.
- **Outer Shell:** Seamless, non-porous polymer with micro-textured lotus-effect coating. Slurry must bead and roll off. Zippers are failure points; use magnetic seam-seals or overlapping flap closures.
- **Cooling:** Phase-change vest worn under the shell. The humidity prevents evaporative cooling; you must rely on conductive heat dump. Recharge vests on stasis mats.

### 3. Dermal & Ocular Protection
- **Eyes:** Sealed low-light goggles with anti-fog hydrophobic coating and polarized filters. The pools' iridescent data-slicks can trigger mild hypnotic fixation; polarization breaks the moiré patterns.
- **Skin:** Apply inhibitor-based barrier cream to any exposed skin (neck, wrists, face seal line). Reapply every 90 minutes. The cream mimics Anchor-Moss exudates and prevents micro-crystallization in pores.

### 4. Tools & Carry

## III. ACOUSTIC LITERACY & RESONANCE MANAGEMENT

Sound is the primary causal force in the Caldera. The Choristers print with it. The fauna hunt with it. The terrain responds to it. You must learn to listen, filter, and speak carefully.

### 1. Hearing Protection & Selective Pass-Through
- **Earcups:** Active noise-canceling with programmable frequency gating. You must block:

  - Resonance Jackal booms (40–80 Hz, can disrupt your equilibrium and trigger defensive slurry sprays)
  - Chorister harmonic spikes (sudden crystallization bursts)
- **Pass-Through Channels:** Allow continuous low-mid hum (100–300 Hz) and rhythmic pulse patterns. This is the Chorister "grammar." You will learn to predict sprays, hardening cycles, and terrain shifts by ear.

### 2. The Tone Generator / Resonance Wand
A handheld acoustic emitter calibrated to the Caldera's substrate response curve. Not a weapon. A negotiation tool.
- **Frequencies & Effects:**
  - 110 Hz sustained: Encourages gentle bulging/slow accretion. Useful for creating handholds or bridging small gaps.
  - 220 Hz staccato: Triggers rapid surface hardening. Use to create temporary stepping stones over slurry weeps.
  - 65 Hz discordant sweep: Disrupts local growth patterns. Emergency use only; causes chaotic splashing and may attract Jackals.
- **Warning:** Overuse or rhythmic repetition can sync with a Chorister's algorithm, causing it to treat you as a node in its pattern. Vary your tones. Never play a loop.

### 3. Vocal Discipline
Do not shout. Do not speak loudly. The geometry warps echoes; a yell may return as a focused resonance that triggers a wall to extrude into your path, or be absorbed entirely, leaving you disoriented. Use subvocal comms, hand signals, or tapped codes on your staff. If you must vocalize, keep it low, irregular, and brief.

## IV. MOVEMENT, NAVIGATION & THE DISCIPLINE OF MOTION

### 1. The Rule of Stillness
The land reads static mass as an error or feedstock. You must move, or rest only on inhibitor zones.
- **Step Technique:** Lift completely. Do not drag. Dragging pulls slurry into your tread, accelerating fuse-time. Place feet decisively, roll weight forward, lift clean.
- **Pause Limit:** Maximum 10–15 seconds on bare terrain. If scanning, shift weight constantly. Micro-steps, heel-toe rocks, or gentle knee bends keep the boundary layer agitated.
- **If Fused:** Do not panic. Do not yank. Apply inhibitor spray to the seam, activate boot vibration, and step out. If clothing fuses, cut it away. Skin fusion requires immediate fern sap application and gentle thermal cycling (warm pack, not hot).

### 2. Navigation in a Shifting Basin
Maps are obsolete upon creation. LiDAR fails as geometry rewrites between pulses. You navigate by sound, light, and biology.
- **Acoustic Triangulation:** Chorister hums create standing waves in the mist. Learn to feel pressure differentials on your face/hands. High pressure = active spray zone. Low pressure = lee-side or mature rock.
- **Light Cues:** Amber pulses = growth cycle active. Violet veins = Chorister proximity or recent crystallization. Shadows are unreliable; they detach and feed fissures. Do not use them for orientation.
- **Path Marking:** Physical markers are overwritten. Use projected UV chalk or temporary acoustic pings. Better yet, navigate by Anchor-Moss chains. They form natural safe corridors.

### 3. Rim Ascent & Exit
The caldera rim is jagged basalt, but it grows. Handholds may extrude or seal during your climb.
- **Gear:** Non-stick climbing gloves, inhibitor-chalk bag, dynamic rope with quick-release anchors.
- **Technique:** Move during Chorister "rest" cycles (hum drops to baseline, amber pulse slows). Test every hold with a tap. If it weeps silver, bypass it.
- **Anchor Calibration:** Multiverse transit beacons suffer phase drift here. Use a pulsed acoustic sync aligned to your departure frequency. Fire it from a stable moss patch near the rim.

## V. FLORA, FAUNA & THE FOOD WEB OF BECOMING

### 1. Anchor-Moss & Stasis-Ferns (Vegetation 0.9)
- **Identification:** Pale, translucent, blue-veined. Grows only in Chorister lee-zones or on mature, non-weeping outcrops. Emits a faint cold scent (like crushed pine and ozone).
- **Harvesting:** Cut fern fronds with ceramic blade. Do not uproot. The inhibitor is in the sap, not the roots. Overharvesting kills the patch and removes your safe zones. Take only what you need for purge doses and mat recharging.
- **Processing:** Crush fronds, strain through hydrophobic mesh, store in sealed glass or fluoropolymer vials. Sap degrades in 48 hours if exposed to mist.

### 2. Slurry-Skimmers
- **Behavior:** Swarm fresh weeps. Translucent, crystal-winged, chitter at 8–12 kHz. Harmless to humans but blinding in dense clouds.
- **Protocol:** Lower visor polarization, move through swarms steadily. Do not swat; crushed skimmers release acidic digestive enzymes that pit inhibitor coatings.

### 3. Resonance Jackals
- **Behavior:** Six-limbed, eyeless, parabolic bone heads. Hunt by masking movement with Chorister pulses. Mimic growing rock. Boom calls disrupt slurry, flushing prey.
- **Protocol:** Move irregularly. Avoid rhythmic footfalls. If a jackal locks on, it will stand perfectly still, matching ambient growth sounds. Break its tracking with a 65 Hz discordant sweep from your tone wand, then retreat perpendicular to its dish orientation. Do not run in straight lines; they predict linear motion.

### 4. Phase-Leeches
- **Behavior:** Live in semi-solid stone. Erupt from hollow-sounding ground. Diamond-tipped, heat/moisture seeking.
- **Protocol:** Tap-test every step with your staff. Hollow echo = bypass. If one latches, do not pull. Apply high-frequency pulse (10 kHz burst from wand) to trigger muscle spasm and release. Clean wound with inhibitor solution; leech saliva contains rapid-calcification enzymes.

### 5. Mimic-Orbs
- **Behavior:** Gelatinous floaters, absorb mist, harden into Chorister replicas when threatened. Warble like human humming.
- **Protocol:** Ignore. Do not mistake for Choristers (real spires matte-black, groove-shifting, hum in sternum; orbs glossy, warble, drift on thermals). Harmless unless you strike one, triggering a defensive hardening that can shatter tools.

## VI. THE CHORISTERS: READING THE INDIFFERENT CONDUCTORS

The Choristers (Built 1.4, Tech 2.8) are not machines. They are algorithmic geologies. They do not perceive you as alive or dead; you are coordinate data. Respect their indifference.

### 1. The Grammar of the Hum
- **Baseline Thrum (100–150 Hz):** Idle state. Terrain accretes slowly. Safe to traverse with standard caution.
- **Rising Harmonic Stack:** Pre-spray sequence. Slurry nozzles align. Move out of arc paths immediately. Arcs follow parabolic trajectories dictated by local gravity shear.
- **Coherent Pulse Burst:** Crystallization event. Slurry flash-hardens into lattices, domes, or bridges. Do not stand in the target zone; you will be encased or structurally integrated.
- **Frequency Drop + Violet Flare:** Algorithmic correction. The Chorister is rewriting a "flawed" growth pattern. Terrain may collapse or extrude violently. Retreat to moss or mature basalt.

### 2. Interaction Rules
- **Never touch.** Surface flows like mercury to heal, but contact triggers localized gravity inversion. You will be lifted, spun, and deposited elsewhere, often with fractured bones or fused gear.
- **Never block a spray arc.** The algorithm does not reroute for biology. It will build through you.
- **Predict, don't plead.** They do not respond to intent, only resonance. Use your tone wand to negotiate space, not to command.
- **Shadow Consumption:** If your shadow detaches and slithers toward a fissure, do not chase it. It is being consumed as thermodynamic fuel. You will feel a momentary chill. It is harmless but disorienting.
