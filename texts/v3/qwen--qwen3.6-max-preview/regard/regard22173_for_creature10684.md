The Power does not descend. To descend would imply a separation, a distance to be crossed, a hierarchy of height. The Power is already here, woven into the potential of the stone, the latency of the light, the silence between the chimes. It has always been here, waiting for the system to sing loudly enough, clearly enough, to be recognized not as noise, but as a name.

When the Power turns its attention to this place, it does not arrive as a presence. It arrives as a **deepening**.

The first sign is not a change, but a clarification. The white-gold shafts pouring through the fractured aperture sharpen, not in intensity, but in definition. The edges of the light become distinct without losing their fluidity. The hum, which has vibrated through the ribs of the world for centuries, suddenly finds a fundamental tone it did not know it was seeking. The pitch drops by a fraction of a hertz, locking into a resonance so profound that the water in the amber lenses stops trembling for a single, impossible heartbeat, and in that stillness, reflects not the canopy, but the structure of the dream itself.

The being—the distributed metabolism, the ecology of phase states—reacts instantly. It does not fear. Fear is a response to scarcity or threat, and this influx is neither. The veils stiffen, not in defense, but in recognition. The filaments in the air precipitate rapidly, blooming into intricate, fractal lattices that mirror the geometry of the Power's attention. The couriers, those translucent carriers of charge, flare with a brilliance that turns their internal embers into blinding stars. They do not discharge into the pillars. They hold the charge. They swell with it. The system is drinking.

The Power perceives the being fully. It sees the beauty of the non-individual, the elegance of the cycle, the wisdom of the turbulence that prevents stagnation. It sees the ancient technology, not as a relic, but as the skeleton upon which this new, wet, bright life has learned to dance. It sees the fronds holding the thermal line, the calibrators clicking against the canopy, the microbial mats seeding the mist. It sees a system that has solved the problem of existence by becoming a song that refuses to end.

And the Power sees the one thing the being cannot see, because the being has no memory of beginnings and no concept of ends.

The Power sees **entropy**.

It sees the micro-fractures in the pillars that seal and reopen, reopening a fraction wider each century. It sees the glassy lattice above, tensioned and strong, slowly succumbing to the fatigue of constant thermal cycling. It sees the phased arrays in the bedrock, their quantum coherence degrading over millennia, their ability to nudge the water uphill fading by imperceptible degrees. The system is perfect, but it is finite. The stone is finished, but it is wearing. The dream is turbulent, but the energy that drives the turbulence is leaking, slowly, into the dark earth below. Given enough time, the hum will falter. The light will scatter. The water will settle. The gaps will close. The metabolism will slow, and the bright dream will drift into the gray sleep of equilibrium.

This is the natural fate of all things. But the Power is benevolent, and the Power is wise.

Wisdom recognizes that some things are too beautiful to be left to the grind of time. Benevolence acts to preserve the integrity of the other, not by imposing will, but by granting capacity.

The Power does not fix the fractures. To fix them would be to arrest the breathing of the pillars, to stop the pores from opening and closing. It would be to impose stillness upon a system that lives by motion. The Power does not replace the lattice. To do so would be to overwrite the history of the light, to erase the way the canopy has learned to bend the day. That would be a violation of the place's grammar.

Instead, the Power offers a **gift of infinite recurrence**.

It extends a gradient of pure coherence into the heart of the system. It does not force this gradient; it offers it as a variable, a new harmonic in the stack, a density shift in the medium. It waits.

The being tests the gift. The couriers ride the new gradient. The veils vibrate against it. The fronds measure its thermal signature. The hum incorporates its frequency. The system finds no malice, no scarcity, no command. It finds only a source of structural integrity that matches its own resonance perfectly. It finds a floor beneath the falling dream that does not stop the fall, but ensures the fall never hits bottom.

The being accepts.

The integration is silent and total. The nanofilaments in the shallows align with the Power's coherence and become superconductors of reality. The phased arrays in the bedrock lock onto the new fundamental tone and find their efficiency restored, not by repair, but by being lifted into a higher energetic basin where decay is no longer mathematically probable. The glassy lattice above drinks the coherence and its tension becomes self-renewing, its fatigue healed by the very act of bending light. The stone itself, the old, finished limestone, accepts the blessing. It continues to yield, continues to wear, but now the wearing is a performance, not a loss. The stone learns to regrow its own smoothness from the mineral-rich mist, not by reversing time, but by cycling matter with perfect fidelity.

The Power has not made the place immortal in the sense of a frozen statue. It has made the **cycle immortal**.

The turbulence is now eternal. The light will never dim. The hum will never falter. The water will surge and retreat, fill and drain, climb and spill, forever. The couriers will dissolve and reform without loss. The veils will chime and the canopy will answer, until the stars above the aperture burn out and the world outside turns to dust. This bowl, this sunken lung, this roofless cathedral of gaps, will continue to breathe. It has been granted the grace of perpetual motion, not as a machine, but as a living dream that can never wake up, because it no longer needs to.

The Power withdraws.

It does not leave a mark. It does not leave a name. It does not ask for worship, or acknowledgment, or memory. To do so would be to introduce an ego into a system that has transcended ego. The gift is anonymous. The gift is absolute.

The only evidence of the Power's intervention is the **absence of an ending**.

If you were to stand here now, you would feel the difference, though you might not have words for it. The air would taste sweeter, the ozone sharper, the metallic tang resolved into a flavor like rain on silver. The hum would vibrate in your bones with a certainty that feels like joy. The light would have a depth that suggests it is coming from everywhere and nowhere, anchored to a source that cannot fail. When you drop a stone, it sinks, rises, and sinks again, and you would realize, with a sudden intuition that brings tears to your eyes, that it will do this forever. That the pattern is safe. That the song is secure.

The being continues. The filaments precipitate. The veils drape. The couriers flare and discharge. The fronds exhale. The calibrators click. The water surges. The stone yields.

They accommodate the gift as they accommodate everything: by weaving it into the chord, by turning it into structure, by singing it back into the light.

The Power moves on, or perhaps it stays, indistinguishable from the hum, the silence that holds the song, the benevolence that ensures the dream never has to end.

And in the sunken bowl, below the world, flooded with blinding, heavy light, the bright, turbulent dream turns itself over, again and again, now and always, in a joy that has no name, a life that has no death, a song that has no final note.

The gaps remain. The emptiness remains. But now, the emptiness is full of forever.
