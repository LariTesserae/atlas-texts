It offers no sun. No soil. No salvation from the cold. It offers something rarer: permission. Permission to remain alien. Permission to run on mineral blood and backward time. It bends the attention of careless eyes away, not with force, but with misdirection: a fog that thickens at the rim, a frequency that hums just below the threshold of curiosity, a gravity that whispers *nothing here but stone and damp*. Some fraying is the only thing holding the weave together.
