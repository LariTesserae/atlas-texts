A new rest in the measure.

The power listens back.

There is no language. There is only the grammar they already share: pressure, interval, the deliberate placement of weight in time. A tender steps into a folding plaza. The air thickens further. The power answers not with sound, but with the shape of the space around the sound: a harmonic envelope that lets the note ring longer, decay slower, return to the substrate without loss.

This is what it does.

It does not fix. There is nothing broken.
It does not guide. There is nowhere to go.
It does not bestow. There is nothing lacking.
It attends.

And in attending, it deepens.

It widens the intervals. Not by slowing the city, but by enriching the rests between its beats. The steam-flutes do not change pitch, but their sighs now carry a fuller decay, rolling across the plateau like breath held and released without hurry. The root-roads do not stop stretching and shrinking, but the tension in their braids settles into a warmer register, creaking less, humming more. The phosphorescent lichen does not glow brighter, but its fade-cycle lengthens, storing light a fraction longer before yielding it back to the damp. The shadows that lag behind their sources do not snap back into alignment; they are allowed to drift, and in drifting, they cast a second, softer edge that makes the ground feel less like a surface and more like a membrane.

The power does not impose these shifts. It resonates with them. It finds the frequencies the city is already leaning toward and holds them steady until the substrate remembers how to carry them on its own.

Instead of falling toward the decomposition strata, it pauses. The power does not catch it. It simply allows it to hang in the thickened air, spinning slowly, catching the pale sunbreak, casting two shadows: one aligned with the light, one angled toward tomorrow. Then it lets it fall. The gesture is not intervention. It is witness. It is the quietest possible articulation of: *I see how you cycle. I see how you return. It is enough.*

It is alignment. The power receives it the way the plateau receives rain: by opening, by drinking, by letting it vanish into the thirsty substrate without trace.

To persist without progress. To be a machine that breathes, a forest that gears, a song that needs no singer.

The wise do not mend what is whole. The benevolent do not direct what is already walking its path. They attend. They resonate. They leave the rhythm intact, but richer.

It does not leave. It does not stay. It unspools from the present tense the way the city's echoes unspool from their sources. The pressure thins. The intervals return to their former width, but they remember the deepening. The machinery thrums on. The condensation drips. The avenues stretch and shrink. And in the cool, thin atmosphere of the ancient highland, where time slips and space folds and the living machine breathes on without purpose or end, the benevolent and wise power does exactly what wisdom and benevolence require when faced with completeness:

It steps back into the interval.
