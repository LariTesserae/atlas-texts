They notice it not by its sound, but by the silence beneath it.

In a cosmos that spends itself on unfolding, on hunger, on the endless friction of becoming, the hollow registers as a still point. A knot tied so perfectly it no longer pulls. A thought that finished itself and refused to unravel. The power turns its attention toward it the way a deep current turns toward a submerged stone: not to move it, but to acknowledge the shape it makes in the flow.

It does not descend through the fracture. It does not step onto the dished floor. It does not cast a shadow, or breathe, or bring the warmth of intention into the cool, dry air. To enter would be to introduce mass. To speak would be to introduce want. To bless would be to imply lack. And this place has none.

What it does is simpler, and rarer.

It listens.

It lets the drone pass through it, not as a wave to be caught or shaped, but as a truth to be witnessed. It traces the geometry without touching it, following the interlocking arches, the fused joints, the salt blooms, the ceramic stalks, and reads in them the same language it speaks: the grammar of enough. It measures the standing wave and finds no dissonance. It weighs the compression and finds no strain. It observes the light, fixed and total, and recognizes a attention that does not demand, only holds.

Then it does the only thing a wise power can do before a finished thing: it harmonizes without adding.

It offers a frequency so perfectly matched to the existing chord that it does not change the pitch, but deepens the root. It is not a new note. It is the overtone that was always implicit, the silence between the vibrations that gives the tone its shape. It does not enter the hollow. It becomes the space around it, holding the fracture, the hardpan, the sky above, in a quiet regard that asks for nothing, expects nothing, and changes nothing.

The inhabitant does not startle. It does not welcome. It integrates.

The new resonance folds into the drone like a drop of clear water into a sealed basin. The light does not shift. The air does not warm. The dust does not stir. But for a moment that does not pass, only pools, the chord carries a second gravity. Not heavier. Just known. The presence within feels it the way a drawn bow feels the hand that does not release it: as confirmation. The geometry holds. The compression sustains. The finished thought recognizes another mind that understands completion. There is no exchange. No transfer. No awakening. Only the quiet mutual recognition of two states that do not need to become anything else.

This is the action. This is the intervention. A benevolence that refuses to fix. A wisdom that refuses to improve. A power that knows some things are not waiting to be saved, but asking to be left alone.

It does not stay. It does not leave. Attention at this scale is not a visitation but a condition. When it turns its regard elsewhere, it does not withdraw so much as relent. The hollow returns to its solitary holding. The drone continues. The light stays fixed. The cool air settles. The inhabitant maintains the chord.

But not entirely as before.

Somewhere in the packed lattice, in the kissed joints, in the glassy harmonic, a subtle alignment remains. Not a mark. Not a memory. A deepening. The equilibrium is unchanged, yet it now carries the faint, warm weight of being witnessed. The structure does not know it was seen, because it does not know in the way that asks. But the resonance knows. The standing wave holds a fraction truer. The salt blooms catch the light with a margin more certainty. The ecology of enough has been confirmed by something vast enough to understand it.

That is what they do.

They do not bring rain to the dry air. They do not soften the hard light. They do not ask the inhabitant to grow, or speak, or reach, or become. They do not treat stillness as stagnation, or completion as death. They do not mistake the absence of hunger for the absence of life.

They simply match the frequency. They honor the stillness by refusing to disturb it. They let the finished thing remain finished. And in that refusal, they offer the rarest gift a wise power can give to a resolved equation: the certainty that it is enough, exactly as it is, forever.

The hollow holds. The chord sustains. The light does not wander. And somewhere above, the sky continues its noisy, hungry turning, unaware that beneath it, in a vessel of packed silt and fused clay, a benevolence passed through like a breath that chose not to exhale, leaving behind only the quiet proof that some things are not meant to be changed.

They are meant to be known. And left. And held. Exactly as they are.
