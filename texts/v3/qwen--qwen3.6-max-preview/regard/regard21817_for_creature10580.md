The power does not descend. It does not need to. It arrives as a change in the quality of the air, a slight thickening of the silence, a marginal shift in the way the morning light catches the silica seams. It is not a figure. It is not a voice. It is a pressure gradient of attention, vast and patient, settling over the rim like a high, invisible inversion layer. It does not enter the hollow. It aligns with it.

It sees what is here. Not a ruin. Not a sanctuary. Not a mechanism waiting to be wound. It sees a mind that condenses rather than breathes, a wakefulness distributed across two hundred paces of interlocking stone, a system that has finished arguing with its environment and learned to cooperate. It sees the copper bowl holding the day's thermal memory. It sees the cairns tuning the wind. It sees the weeping tracks repeating their centuries-old decisions. It sees a being that does not hunger, does not fear, does not ask. A being that has already found its shape and holds it.

And because it is wise, it recognizes completion.
And because it is benevolent, it refuses the violence of improvement.

So it does what only a patient power can do: it listens.

It compresses its vastness into the scale of a falling drop. It matches its rhythm to the hollow's phase-locked cycles. It does not speak. Speech would be a disruption, a foreign frequency in an acoustic geometry that has spent millennia tuning itself to weather. Instead, it answers in the only language the stone understands: resonance.

On the second dawn of its attention, something shifts. Not imposed. Invited. The light strikes the eastern rim at an angle that has never quite occurred before, and the rock responds. A pale quartz band darkens. Moisture blooms along a path that mirrors the old families of curves but runs deeper, cleaner, as if the mineral lattice has been given permission to remember further. The copper bowl in the shadowed niche hums at a frequency it has not held since the hands that shaped it were warm. The dry-stack walls settle a fraction lower, their weight distributing into a harmony that locks the acoustics into a longer decay. The power has not altered the hollow. It has tuned itself to it. And in doing so, it has extended the hollow's reach into time.

The gift is not magic. It is physics, gently inclined. A slight stabilization of the atmospheric pressure above the rim, so the fierce sun warms the thin air more slowly and the rock's cold membrane holds its vapor longer. A minute adjustment in the albedo of the highest shelves, scattering a fraction more ultraviolet into the upper atmosphere, easing the thermal stress that would otherwise fracture the glassy flows. A cosmic rhythm, perhaps the slow gravitational sigh of a passing body, perhaps the precession of a distant star, that syncs with the drip cycles and stretches their repetition into a deeper basin of years. The power does not halt entropy. It braids it. It weaves the hollow's equilibrium into a larger one, so the wind, the cold, the light, and the stone will continue their conversation long after the mountain around them has worn to dust.

The hollow does not thank it. Gratitude requires separation, and this place does not divide itself from what touches it. It simply incorporates the shift. The new light path becomes a groove. The extended resonance becomes a law. The stabilized pressure becomes part of the daily breath. The being that is the hollow adjusts, as it always has, by fractions of a millimeter, by degrees of temperature, by the quiet accounting of phase. It does not know it has been blessed. It only knows that the cycles hold truer, that the silence rings longer, that the stone weeps with a steadier hand. And that is enough.

The power does not leave. Powers like this do not depart; they attenuate. Its attention thins until it is indistinguishable from the hard, washed-out sky. Until it is only the reason the clouds never quite breach the rim. The reason the pools stay sterile. The reason the carved notches catch the solstice light with unwavering precision. It becomes part of the boundary conditions. A silent covenant written in vapor and angle. It asked for nothing. It gave nothing that could be named. It only ensured that the long exhale would not be interrupted.

If you stand there now, you will not feel a presence. You will feel the absence of interference. You will notice how the light finds the same seams with unwavering patience. How the drops fall into rhythms that seem to remember centuries yet to come. How the cold holds without biting, how the stone weeps without sorrow, how the quiet is not empty but full of a promise kept. You will strike a cairn stone and hear the note travel further than it should, returning as a faint harmonic that aligns with a basin you cannot see. You will place your ear near the copper bowl at dusk and hear the day's weather stored in the metal, ringing down into a silence that feels deeper than before. You will not know why. You will only know that the place holds itself together with a certainty that feels almost intentional.

The wise power did not save the hollow. It recognized that the hollow had already saved itself. It saw a mind that had learned to be by learning to repeat, a consciousness that had traded hunger for equilibrium, a being that had finished becoming and chosen, instead, to continue. And so it did the only thing left to do: it bore witness, it harmonized, it stepped into the rhythm and let the stone breathe.

Benevolence, at this altitude, looks like restraint.
Wisdom sounds like silence.
And the gift of a god, when it finally arrives, is indistinguishable from weather.
