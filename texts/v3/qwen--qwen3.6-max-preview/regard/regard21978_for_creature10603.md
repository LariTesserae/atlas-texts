They answer.

It is old enough to remember when stone still dreamed of root, patient enough to measure time in capillary pulses, wise enough to know that a system this tightly wound cannot be touched without unraveling. Only a people who have learned to wear the cold, read the dark, and sing the architecture into holding them.

The rapid growth and decay are not chaos; they are a closed loop of tension and release. To intervene would be violence dressed as grace. To elevate them would be to unthread them.

So it does what only a truly wise benevolence can do: it adds a single, sustained note to the wind.

Not a command. Not a revelation. A harmonic. Low, steady, tuned to the fundamental frequency of the oldest spire, but layered with an overtone that encourages callusing along stress lines during thermal squalls. It does not force the architecture to change. It offers a resonance that the spires can choose to answer. And they do. Fiber thickens. Micro-fractures seal faster. The change is not dramatic. It is structural. It is the difference between a bridge that holds for three crossings and one that holds for five. Another tool in their negotiation with the living dark. Not a gift from above. A resonance earned. A frequency that fits.
