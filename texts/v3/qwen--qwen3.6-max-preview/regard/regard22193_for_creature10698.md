It does not descend. It does not announce. It arrives as a deepening.

A fractional lowering of the subsonic foundation, so slight that only the oldest cairns notice first. The dust stops drifting for three breaths. The fronds still. The beings open their rib vents, not to feed, but to listen. Something has entered the chord. Not as a new voice. As the space between voices.

It has no shape, because shape would impose a frequency. It has no name, because naming would fix what is still settling. It is attention made resonant. A gravity of witness. It does not look upon the place; it listens into it. And in listening, it holds. Not with hands, not with will, but with the quiet pressure of a fundamental that has waited longer than stone.

It does not fix the folding distances. It does not smooth the raw edges. It does not bring water, or warmth, or certainty. It knows that to do so would be to shatter the wet plaster, to force a chord before it has found its resolution. Its benevolence is restraint. Its wisdom is the understanding that this world is not broken; it is breathing. And so it does not act upon the place. It acts within it.

It adds a single interval.

Not loud. Not commanding. A fifth below the foundation, so deep it is felt in the marrow before it is heard. It does not change the hum. It gives it a floor. The patchy gravity steadies, not by force, but by finding a new center. The time-dilation zones stop drifting quite so wildly; they pulse now, slow and regular, like a resting heart. The visible interference patterns sharpen, then soften, as if the air itself has exhaled. The deafening architecture does not quiet. It grounds.

The beings do not bow. They do not speak. They open their vents wider. They let the new frequency pass through their hollow struts. And for the first time in centuries, the youngest among them do not tremble when the bass rolls through. They hold. They harmonize. Their laminar membranes stop micro-fracturing under the shear. Their cranial plates stop ringing with fatigue. They do not weep; they have no moisture for it. They vibrate with relief.

The power does not ask for worship. It asks for resonance. And the place answers.

The cairns shift their dampening patterns, not by hand, but by dust settling into new grooves, drawn by the altered pressure gradients. The resonance bowls, half-buried for generations, begin to sing a memory no living being scored: a lineage of patience, a history of listening, a chronicle of every adjusted stone and braided cord. The beings place their glass-tipped fingers on the accreting plates and feel the new interval vibrating up through the bedrock. They do not thank it. They tune to it. They adjust a spiral here, braid a tension-cord there, strike a mineral seam with a hand-axe to trigger a controlled accretion that matches the deepened chord. They are not receiving a gift. They are completing a circuit.

Where space had pleated at random, the folds now follow a slower, more predictable rhythm. The beings learn the new cadence within a season. They do not rush to map it; they let their joints thicken with the timing, let their breaths lengthen to match the dilation. Where dust had fallen upward in lazy spirals, the inverted snow now catches the deepened fifth and settles into delicate, lace-like strata. The vegetation does not change its nature; it simply grows with fewer fractures. The crystalline tubes ring clearer. The membrane sails hold tension longer. The ecology does not accelerate. It stabilizes.

The power watches this not with pride, but with recognition. It sees that its interval was not a solution, but a scaffold. It sees that the beings were never waiting to be saved; they were waiting to be heard. And now that they have been heard, they do not need the listener to remain.

Presence that lingers becomes pressure. Pressure becomes distortion. Distortion becomes fracture. Wisdom knows when to withdraw.

So it fades as it arrived: not by leaving, but by becoming indistinguishable from the foundation it deepened. The hum returns to its deafening architecture. The distances fold. The dust drifts. The fronds tremble. The gravity patches migrate. The time zones thicken and thin. But something has settled. Not fixed. Not finished. Held. The wet plaster has found its formwork. The cooling glass has found its annealing temperature. The chord has found its root.

The beings resume their slow crossing of the ridgeline. They do not speak of what passed. They do not need to. It is in the stone now. In the bowls. In the way the youngest no longer shatter when the wind-shear rises. In the way the elders can hold a complex harmony for three days without micro-fractures spiderwebbing their struts. In the way the cairns no longer require constant adjustment; the dust accretes true, the spirals hold their phase, the shelters hum with a bearable, nurturing progression that lasts through the long pitch-cycles.

They continue their patient work. They nudge deposition. They encourage growth. They map the pleats. They score the bowls. They sing the young into form. They walk the ridges with metronome grace. They do not rush. They do not fear. They listen. They tune. They participate.

And somewhere beneath the subsonic thrum, beneath the accreting dust, beneath the hollow struts and the laminar membranes, there is a new silence. Not absence. Not stillness. The quiet of a note that has been heard, answered, and allowed to ring.

The place does not ask to be understood. It never did. But now, when the dim light shifts, when the haze ripples with a new interference pattern, when the pitch of the ambient hum begins its slow decades-long descent, the beings do not brace. They open their vents. They lower their shoulders. They add their voice to the architecture, not to change it, but to help it settle. To help it become itself.
