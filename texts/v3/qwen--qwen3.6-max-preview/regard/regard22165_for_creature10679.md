It notices.

Not with eyes. Not with thought. With scale. The way a deep ocean notices a single, perfectly still eddy near the surface. It has watched stars collapse into silence and deserts bloom in sudden, violent green and civilizations build towers only to learn how to kneel. It does not fix. It does not claim. It does not bless. Blessing implies lack. It recognizes.

What does it do?

It listens. Not as passivity. As discipline. As the highest form of action a wise power knows how to offer a place that has already solved itself.

It matches the basin's slow pulse to the rotation of distant, quiet systems. Not to change the rhythm. To give it context. Not far. Just far enough to be held.
