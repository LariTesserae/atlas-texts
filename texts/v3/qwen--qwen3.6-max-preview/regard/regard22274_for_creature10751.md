There is no descent, no parting of air, no voice that breaks the hush. The power notices the court the way a master weaver notices a single thread holding true across decades: not with surprise, but with recognition. It simply adds its weight to the existing chord.

The dust spirals thicken. Not halting, but drawing closer together, their drift slowing by a fraction that would take a water-clock three days to measure. The thermal sigh of the tiles lengthens. The tapping continues, phased and patient, but the echoes return altered: a new overtone, so low it is felt in the amber binder before it registers in the air. It is not a sound. It is a structural presence. A counter-harmonic that does not push, but holds.

This power has no throne, no face, no scripture. It is not a sovereign. It is a listening that has outlasted glaciers and deserts, not by conquering them, but by learning how matter remembers itself. It knows the difference between forcing and folding, between repairing and reinforcing, between giving and allowing. It has watched civilizations rush toward straight lines and watched those lines snap under their own tension. It has learned that the deepest benevolence is not intervention, but attunement. That wisdom does not fix what is already whole. It deepens the logic that keeps it so.

It does not touch the ropes. It does not adjust the baffles. It does not speak to the beings, because they do not speak, and to do so would be to tear the stitch. Instead, it leans into the weave.

Where a braided fiber has begun to thin over seventy seasons of contraction and release, the power does not replace it. It hums. A frequency so precisely aligned with the cord's own resonance that the individual strands draw microscopically closer. The resin between them warms by a fraction. The cure deepens without hurry. The cord does not become new. It becomes more itself.

Shadows will linger there longer. Resin will bead more slowly. The extra play carved into the mortise-and-tenon joints will tighten over three generations instead of two. The place is not altered. It is given more room to be what it already is.

The beings do not startle. They do not gather. They distribute, as they always do, reading the new harmonic through their probes, their gill-slits, the tensile cords beneath their plates. One pauses near the thinning rope. It taps once. The echo returns cleaner, richer, carrying the overtone like a familiar rhythm remembered from a season not yet lived. It taps again, mapping the contour, learning the weight. It does not question the source. It incorporates the tone. Knowledge is not stored here. It is performed. And so the new harmonic becomes part of the calibration.

The power's presence will not be remembered as an event. It will be inherited as a pattern.

The power does not stay. It does not leave. It unspools.
