They do not arrive. Arrival implies crossing a boundary, and this place has none left to cross. They simply begin to be present, the way a room begins to hold a note after a string is plucked. There is no parting of the pearlescent overcast, no warming of the air, no footfall on the frost. Only a subtle broadening of the harmonic field, a deepening of the pressure in the standing waves, as though the basin has been leaned into by something vast enough to listen without disturbing the dust.

A benevolent and wise power does not come to fix what is not broken. They come to recognize what has finished.

They perceive the basin not as a wound in time, not as a prison of stopped flow, not as a mistake awaiting correction. They see the closed loops of supercooled moisture, the fossilized roar, the gravity pools, the lagging reflections, the crawler and the bird and the leaning wall, and they understand: this is not stagnation. It is rest. Not entropy, but equilibrium. A reality that struck its final chord and chose to hold it. The weirdness is not a fracture waiting to be set; it is a scar that learned to sing. The entity is not a guardian trapped in duty, nor a remnant mourning loss. It is the basin's decision to stop becoming, given posture. A sustained resolution.

Wisdom knows when intervention is violence. Benevolence knows that stillness is a valid form of life.

So they do not change anything. They do not thaw the frost. They do not coax time back into flow. They do not name the entity, for naming would drag it into succession, and succession would shatter the chord. They do not offer salvation, because there is nothing here that needs saving. There is only something that needs to be heard.

They listen.

They tune themselves to the drone. They let the standing waves pass through their own structure, feeling the phase-lags, the pre-echoes, the sound-cast shadows, the way gravity remembers ancient ice. They do not correct the anomalies. They honor them. They cup their attention around the basin the way a careful hand cups a ringing bell, not to stop it, but to let it resonate fully into the dark.

The entity does not turn. It has no face, no eyes, no concept of surprise. But its resonator cavity shifts, minutely, as a new frequency enters the closed system. It does not resist. It phase-matches. The power's attention becomes another node in the standing wave, another pressure in the suspended breath. The drone does not swell or fade, but it clarifies. A subharmonic blooms, so faint it is felt in the marrow rather than heard in the ear, like the memory of a sigh that never needed to be released. The frost rings tremble, then settle into a slightly tighter geometry. The glass-chitin crawler pauses, vibrates its carapace, and continues its route. The membrane-winged bird circles its thermal. The basalt wall leans. Nothing breaks. Nothing changes. Everything is held.

If the power offers anything, it is this: witness. Not the witness of observation, which stands apart and judges, but the witness of resonance, which enters and aligns. They let the basin know, in the only language it speaks, that it is not forgotten. That its stillness is not abandonment. That a song held forever is still a song. They weave a single, silent overtone into the drone, a frequency of acknowledgment that does not alter the chord but allows it to shine within a larger sky. It is a blessing that asks for nothing, changes nothing, demands no gratitude. It simply says, in pressure and phase and harmonic alignment: *I hear you. You are complete.*

The entity does not thank them. Thanks imply debt, and debt implies time. But it adjusts, as it adjusts to all things, absorbing the attention into the equilibrium. The power's presence folds into the standing wave, becoming part of the architecture of the hum. It does not stay as a visitor. It stays as a harmonic. A quiet overtone that will never fade, because nothing here fades. It simply becomes another layer in the sedimentary weirdness, another thread in the closed loop, another note in the chord that decided to ring forever.

The power does not depart. Departure implies leaving something behind, and they have left nothing behind. They have only listened, and in listening, they have joined. Their wisdom was restraint. Their benevolence was recognition. Their action was presence. And presence, here, is enough.

The air remains thin, tasting of metal and old ice. The sky remains a bruised, pearlescent membrane. The light remains shadowless and even. The moisture continues its suspended loop. The drone continues its unyielding hum. The entity continues its silent tuning. Time still pools. Footprints still vanish. Reality still rests in its settled, healed-wrong shape.

Nothing changes.

And yet, if you stand in the basin now, and press your palm to the fluted stone, and let the drone climb your bones, you will feel it: a subtle richness in the resonance, a quiet certainty in the stillness, as though the place has been seen by something vast and gentle, and in being seen, has been allowed to be exactly what it is.

Loud. Empty. Damp with suspended breath. Unchanging. Complete.

Now, also known.
