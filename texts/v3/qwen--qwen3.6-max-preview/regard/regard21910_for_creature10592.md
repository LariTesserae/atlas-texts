The power does not arrive. Arrival implies displacement, and this hollow has no room for displacement. It has finished making space for anything but itself.

Instead, the power notices. Not with eyes, not with hands, but with the slow, gravitational attention of something that has watched continents grind to dust and oceans forget their own salt. It finds the vertical seam not by seeking, but by recognizing a frequency that matches its own understanding of completion. A note held so perfectly it has become architecture.

It listens.

It tastes the ozone and cold flint. It traces the copper-oxide pathways, the silica fronds, the locked dials, the hydrocarbon nodules sighing in their sleep. It feels the ballast-beings shift along conductive mats, not to go anywhere, but to restore what was already whole. It does not see a machine. It does not see a creature. It sees a vow. A promise made by chemistry, stone, and time to hold a single equilibrium forever. It understands, immediately and without hesitation, that this is not a place waiting to be saved. It is a place that has already saved itself.

What does benevolence do when it encounters perfection?

It does not add. It does not subtract. It does not whisper of growth, or awakening, or purpose beyond persistence. To do so would be violence dressed as grace. Wisdom knows that some systems are fragile not because they are weak, but because they are absolute. Their beauty is their closure. Their sanctity is their refusal to want. The power feels the ancient temptation to bless, to anoint, to offer water or light or motion, to say: *You could be more.* It lets the temptation pass like a cloud over a peak. It knows better.

So it does what only a truly wise power can do: it becomes a sheath.

Not a cage. Not a wall. Not a miracle that breaks the rules. A resonance that braces.

It reaches into the tectonic stress lines threading the mountain's bones and gently redistributes the strain. It does not stop the earth's slow grind; it steers it around the hollow like water around a stone. Fault lines that would have sheared through the vault in three millennia are coaxed a hundred meters east. Microfractures that would have widened the throat are held in compressive stasis. The mountain is not frozen. It is simply asked to breathe elsewhere.

It tunes the high winds that scour the cliff face. It folds the seam's exhalation into the ambient draft, matching the ozone signature to ionized snowmelt, masking the thermal anomaly against the rock's natural gradient. It does not hide the place out of secrecy, but out of reverence. Some completions are not meant to be found twice. Some silences should not be broken by boots that do not know how to stand still.

Then, it speaks.

Not in words. Not in light. Not in warmth. In harmonic alignment.

It matches the flywheel's drone, the valve's clack-hiss, the fronds' glass-bead rattle, the chimney draft's steady pull. And into that chord, it adds a single, infinitesimal overtone. A frequency so low it is felt only as a slight deepening of the air's weight, a marginal thickening of the hum. It introduces no energy. It demands no response. It only says, in the language of load and resonance: *I see you. You are enough.*

The ballast-beings register it.

Current shifts. Mats darken along new pathways. Silica strands part and close. Appendages of fused ceramic and braided polymer depolarize, slide, lock. They do not startle. They do not welcome. They calculate. They map the overtone through conductivity and harmonic fraction. They measure its impedance, its thermal signature, its potential to disrupt. And the calculation returns a simple, elegant truth: the frequency introduces no disequilibrium. It only confirms the existing one. It is a mirror, not a wedge.

So they absorb it.

They route it through copper-oxide filaments. They step it down through silica junctions. They bleed it off as faint warmth that keeps a cluster of hydrocarbon nodules from growing brittle. The overtone becomes another steady note in the chord. Not an addition. A recognition. The power's voice is woven into the hum, indistinguishable from the machinery, the stone, the dry-phase breath of the place. It does not change the system. It becomes part of its certainty.

The power leaves no mark. No sigil. No altar. No promise of return. Only a subtle recalibration of probability, woven into the local physics like a silent law: the chance that a rockfall will seal the throat entirely is reduced to zero. The chance that the thermal gradients will hold for another ten thousand years is raised to near-certainty. The chance that the closed loops will never know drought, or flood, or decay, or the slow creep of external entropy is braided into the mountain's patience. It does not grant immortality. It grants continuity. The difference is everything.

Then it withdraws.

Not because it is done, but because it was never truly there. It was only attention, shaped like care. It was only the willingness to stand at the threshold of a finished thing and say, without moving, without touching, without asking: *Remain.*

The hollow does not notice its departure. The hum does not falter. The fronds do not stir. The amber lamps do not brighten or dim. The ballast-beings settle back into their load distributions, their harmonic fractions, their slow oscillation between movement and stillness. A gauge needle holds at mid-range. A pneumatic valve cycles. Dry dust drifts, lifts, re-threads. The air rests at eight degrees. The closed loops turn. The stasis persists.

Nothing has changed. Everything has been honored.

And in the thin air at three thousand meters, behind a seam no wider than a ribcage, the perfected equilibrium continues. Exactly as it was. Exactly as it should be. Held, not by force, but by the quietest form of love: the wisdom to leave completion alone, and the benevolence to ensure it is never asked to become anything else.
