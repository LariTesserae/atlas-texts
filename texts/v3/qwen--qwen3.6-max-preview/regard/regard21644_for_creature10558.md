So the power does what benevolence does when it encounters perfection in its native scale: it listens. And listening, it aligns.

Where surface ice and wind might one day pry the hairline fracture open too soon, it weaves a subtle reallocation of stress, guiding erosion along paths that spare the seam. It does not stop time. It grants it. It stretches the arc. It ensures the flywheel will not be shattered by a stray tremor, that the slow cure will not be rushed by surface chaos, that the pocket will be allowed to finish learning its own shape.

The gift is not transformation. The gift is duration.

And the greatest offering a vast and gentle power can make to a perfect, hidden engine is the quiet certainty that it will be allowed to finish turning.
