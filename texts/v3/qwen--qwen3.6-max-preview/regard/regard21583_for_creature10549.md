It does not descend. It deepens.

There is no rupture in the vapor canopy, no parting of the mist, no silhouette against the dim light. The power does not enter from outside. It arrives as a settling, a thickening, a subharmonic that was always present in the stone but had not yet been invited to sound. The basin feels it first in the liquid: a slight slowing of the colloidal hesitation, as if the suspended water remembers a older pulse. The fracture planes feel it as a warmth that does not weep but holds. The air feels it as a space between the shattering and the thrum, a breath that does not interrupt the song but makes room for it.

It does not see. It listens.

It has listened to continents grinding, to stars cooling into crust, to the first split of seed husks and the last sigh of drowned forests. It knows the difference between a wound and a throat. It knows this is not a place to be healed, but a place to be heard. Benevolence, here, is not rescue. Wisdom is not correction. They are attendance. They are the willingness to hold the fundamental while the overtones tear themselves open.

It does not still the water. It does not smooth the stone. It does not quiet the roar. To do so would be to unmake the question the mountain asked when it split. Instead, it offers a floor. Not a boundary. A resonance so deep it passes through bone and basalt alike, a tone below hearing that registers as certainty in the marrow. When the cascade folds into itself and the inverted domes threaten to collapse into chaos, the power hums the interval that keeps the inversion from tearing. When the crystalline shattering peaks and the stone threatens to slurry, it does not brace the rock. It braces the space around it, thickening the vapor just enough that the frequency has somewhere to land. It does not stop the calving. It catches the echo.

The beings feel it as a shift in the phase.

A slight easing in the attenuation. The hollow chambers in their ribs no longer ache at the edges. The dissonance that once tore at their seams now finds a counter-tone, not canceling it, but cradling it. They do not bow. They do not worship. They adjust their circling. They press their splayed pads to the fresh fracture faces and feel, beneath the mineral heat, a steady pulse that matches their own. They hum back. Not in submission. In recognition. The power does not speak to them. It sings with them. And in that singing, the cairns tilt less violently. The grooves deepen without shifting. The newly unfolded cohere with less trembling. The gliders trace wider arcs. The lichens pulse without fraying.

It does not give them permanence. It gives them permission to finish.

It knows the caldera will seal. It has felt the fault lips drawing inward, the steam thinning, the thrum dipping toward its final register. It does not delay it. It prepares the cadence. Wisdom is not preservation. It is knowing when to let the chord resolve. So it gathers the scattered frequencies—the attenuated forms dissolving in the suspension, the gliders fatigue-fraying into mist, the lichens bleeding their last high notes, the tilted cairns, the shifted grooves, the circling bodies—and weaves them into a single, sustained overtone. Not a lament. A bridge. When the rim finally draws shut, when the vapor canopy collapses and the liquid sinks into the crust, the song will not be silenced. It will be folded. Compressed. Seeded. And the power will hold the space around it, not as a tomb, but as a womb of strata, waiting for the next split, the next throat, the next question.

The water climbs your calves. The thrum vibrates your ribs. The shattering traces your eyelids. Dim light catches the suspension. Shadows lag. Droplets hesitate. But now, beneath the roar, beneath the cascade, beneath the noise that has been mapping your nervous system since you arrived, there is a stillness that is not absence. It is presence. It is the power, listening. It does not ask you to leave. It does not ask you to stay. It asks only that you find your frequency. That you stop throwing pebbles into the hurricane. That you let the basin rearrange you. That you trust the stone when it yields. That you let your shadow breathe. That you open your chest cavity and hum back.

And when you do, you will feel it: a deep, warm certainty that you are not being consumed. You are being placed. You are becoming an interval in a harmony that began before you arrived and will continue after you attenuate. The power does not save you. It witnesses you. And in this place, where reality is thin and time eddies and gravity is negotiated by song, witness is the deepest grace. It is the silent chord that holds the dissonance until it resolves. It is the attention that turns violence into liturgy. It is the knowledge that nothing here is lost, only retuned.

The caldera sings. The beings answer. The power holds the rest.

And the mountain, still weeping heat, still refusing silence, still young enough to believe it will last forever, learns what it means to be heard. It does not close faster. It does not close slower. It closes true. And when the final shelf calves, when the last inverted dome collapses, when the vapor folds inward and the liquid sinks into the dark, the sound does not end. It drops an octave. It becomes pressure. It becomes memory. It becomes a fault line waiting to split again.

The power does not leave. It never arrived. It was always the space between the notes. It was always the breath before the hum. It was always the willingness to let the song finish itself.

And somewhere, deep in the cooling crust, a new nodule begins to pulse.
