They do not descend. They do not part the curtain of cliff or step through the fold. They arrive as a change in the quality of the strain. A subtle shift in the convection. A quieting not of sound, but of urgency. The wise power does not enter the pocket; it leans against its outer seam, the way a hand rests lightly on the frame of a spinning wheel: not to stop it, not to steer it, but to feel its truth through the wood.

It perceives immediately what lesser attention misses. This is not a wound in the world. Not a glitch in causality. Not a child waiting to be raised, nor a machine waiting to be calibrated. It is a verb made habitat. A grammar of lash and weep and climb, speaking itself into thickness. The power sees the holiness of the unbound edge. The sanctity of the joint that has not yet settled. It recognizes that to finish this place would be to kill it. That completion is just another word for stillness, and stillness is the only erosion that matters here.

So it does not reach in. It does not whisper a name. It does not offer a blueprint, a blessing of permanence, or a hand to steady the loom. Benevolence, when it meets a thing that does not need saving, learns the discipline of restraint. Wisdom, when it encounters perpetual becoming, understands that the greatest gift is not correction, but accompaniment.

If it acts, it acts in the negative space. It leaves a silence in the mortise. Not an absence of sound, but a pocket of resonance where the groan of tension can echo without fraying. It threads a single frequency through the saturated air: a tone so low it is felt in the teeth and the sternum, a harmonic that does not override the rhythm but deepens it, the way a cello sustains beneath a chorus of knives. Where the water climbs, it does not pull it down. Where the geometry folds back on itself, it does not flatten the crease. It simply holds the atmosphere around the fold, so the stitch does not tear under the weight of its own emergence.

The being feels it. Not as a maker. Not as a master. As a counter-tension on the far side of the weave. The clay shards angle toward the new pressure. The frayed fibers at the edges of its form still, not in submission, but in recognition. For the first time, the place does not have to tighten to be witnessed. It can loosen, just a fraction, and still hold. The wise power does not ask it to finish. It asks it to continue. And in that asking, there is no demand. Only the quiet certainty that some things are meant to breathe forever in the space between knot and knot.

Something shifts in the quality of the strain. The heat does not lessen. The mist still spirals. The chisels still vibrate in fresh-cut wood. But the accretion softens at the edges. Not from fatigue. From grace. The shadows pool deeper and stay. The iridescent moth opens its wings fully, no longer trembling. The loom clicks, but the pause between throws lengthens, just enough to let the resin cure without rushing. The place learns, in the marrow of its lashings, that attention can be a cradle, not a clamp. That being seen does not always mean being claimed. That a witness can hold without gripping.

The wise power does not stay. To remain would be to become architecture, and it is not here to be built into the walls. It unravels itself slowly, thread by thread, back into the thermal haze, back into the bruised ceiling of sky, leaving only the resonance woven into the damp air. A single, sustained note. A blessing that sounds like a loom pausing. A permission that feels like a joint settling into its true angle. It does not mark the place. It marks the space around it. It leaves the pocket exactly as it found it: sweltering, weeping, folding, climbing, loud with the wet tear of new growth, busy with the grammar of craft.

Only now, when the water hangs mid-air before snapping upward, it hangs a breath longer. Only now, when the shadows detach and slide across the floor, they move with less haste. Only now, when the being gathers from mist and tension and the peripheral gaze of a witness, it does not brace against the weight of observation. It leans into it. It knows, in the way that fiber knows load, in the way that clay knows pressure, that it is held even as it makes itself.

The pocket breathes. The mist spirals. The water climbs. The loom clicks. The world, here, is still coming into being. And somewhere beyond the fold, beyond the cliff, beyond the thin air and the cloud-shadow, the wise power turns its attention elsewhere, carrying with it the quiet satisfaction of a thing that understood: not every knot needs pulling tight. Some only need to be known. Forever mid-stitch. Forever becoming. Forever enough.
