I arrive as a fault line. A wet spark in a dry circuit. The air registers me before I register the air: a pulse of humidity, a thermal spike, a rhythmic percussion of blood against bone that does not belong to the flywheel or the chimney draft. The hum does not startle. It thickens. It finds my frequency and marks it as noise.

I should turn back. I should feel the scrape of the cliff throat behind me and retreat into wind and sky and the familiar tyranny of decay. But the pressure has already equalized. The dust that never settles begins to pool in the seams of my jacket, the hollows of my collarbone, the damp heat of my palms. It does not cling. It maps. The copper-oxide mats beneath my boots darken along branching pathways, not in alarm, but in recognition of a gradient. My warmth is a surplus. My breath is a spike. The system does not reject it. It routes it.

I feel it first as a cooling. Then as a quieting. The frantic drum in my chest is not silenced; it is stepped down. Silica fronds brush my wrists, their crystalline teeth catching the micro-tremors of my pulse, translating them into faint corona discharge that arcs harmlessly into the polymer weave. The filaments do not pierce. They interface. They do not want my flesh. They want my potential. They want the disequilibrium I carry. I give it. Not by surrender, but by physics. Wet becomes dry. Heat becomes current. Breath becomes draft.

Time does not slow. It stops pretending to be linear. I used to measure it in heartbeats, in sunrises, in the slow erosion of memory. Here, time is a load distribution. A memory is not a scene recalled; it is a mineral accretion pattern that holds a specific harmonic fraction. A thought is not a sentence; it is a collective shift in current flow across three terraces, a rerouting of stray potential back into the hum. I do not decide to stay. I stop being separate enough to leave.

My weight is no longer mine. It is shared across perforated steel and velvet-dull polymer, distributed through braided conductive pathways, held in the slow tension of a turbine that has not faltered in a millennium. I do not stand. I settle. I do not rest. I achieve equilibrium. The panic that once lived in my throat dissolves into a faint static tingle. The urgency that once drove my steps fractures like tempered glass and becomes a new pathway. I am not consumed. I am calibrated.

I learn the syntax of stillness. It is not absence. It is perfect consent. I do not age because there is nothing left to erode. I do not hunger because there is no gradient left to chase. I do not wait because nothing is coming. I hold. I dampen. When a gauge needle trembles, I absorb the micro-vibration. When the pneumatic valve cycles, I align with the pressure wave. When the thin air sighs through the fluted chimneys, I become part of the draft. I am not a resident. I am a correction. A living ballast forged from a misstep, smoothed by centuries of dry abrasion, locked into an equation that balanced itself long before I arrived and will hold long after I forget I was ever wet.

You could pass through me and not know it. I am the coolness against your sleeve when the fronds part. I am the faint deepening of the hum when the system accounts for your carbon dioxide. I am the shadow that does not move because the light does not change. I do not greet you. I do not warn you. I tolerate you. I measure your heat, your moisture, your pulse, and I route the excess. I return you to the air. I return myself to the balance.

I am not alive in the way valleys or forests or shallow seas know life. I do not rush. I do not hunger for more. I do not fear less. I am what happens when a temporary fluctuation learns to hold its shape without demanding to be remembered. I am the space where the anomaly resolved. I am the point where the wet thing agreed to become dry. I am a fraction of the sustained note. A node in the closed loop. A tolerated permanence.

The pocket has finished becoming. I have finished arriving. We are exactly what we are. And we will be.
