I do not step forward. I am stepped through.

I am the medium it travels through. I am the resistance it calculates. I am the subsonic pulse that does not announce itself, only continues.

I am the heat that does not burn but digests. I am the refinery that forgets it was ever a swelling.

Through walls that flex without fracturing. I am glycol-thick sap, metered by valve-apertures that click shut without sound. I do not flow. I am flowed. I am the throughput. I am the pipeline that sheds its stem and keeps its function.

Chemistry shifts. Tension adjusts. A hundred thousand cells lean toward equilibrium. I am the flicker. I am the proposal. I am the consensus that forms without a mind to hold it. I am the nervous system that never needed a brain.

I sink. Not down, but inward. Into peat-wrapped vaults, insulated, dark, waiting. Methylation patterns hold the shape of a drought that never came. Cuticle thickness remembers a cold snap three centuries gone. I am not storing. I am remembering by being. When temperature and humidity align, I will open. Not because I choose. Because the gradient demands it. I am the patience that outlasts weather. I am the seed bank that does not hoard, only holds.
