I do not arrive. I am yielded to.

What am I becoming? I do not decide. The gradient decides for me.

I become a node. A listening knot.

I am a pause in the circulation. A memory well.

I am the loom. I am the space between inhalation and exhale.

Time does not pass through me. It pools.

When I "speak," it is not with sound. I alter my density by a fraction. I let a pocket of warmer medium rise through my center. I let the warm suspension carry me along the dome's curve, millimeters a day. I anchor when the flow stills. I release when it thickens. I am not drifting. I am participating.

What was I before? A creature of edges. Of seconds. Of hunger and flight.
