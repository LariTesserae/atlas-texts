What would I do? Not what I would choose. What the basin would ask of me. I would notice the dissonances first. A belt running a quarter-tone flat. A water channel pooling where it should sheet. A root-bridge trembling at a frequency that doesn't match the wheel below. I would press my ear to the wood. I would lay my hands on the iron. Maybe *Pitchfinder*. Maybe *Tensionkeeper*. Maybe just *The One Who Listens at the Third Tier Junction*. I would stop counting hours. I would feel the fungal threads seeking the minerals in my skin, the carbon in my breath, the slow chemistry of my presence. I would not resist. I would let them in. Not as infection, but as invitation. My scars would pack with spore-dust and tallow until they healed flexible, waterproof, part of the substrate. Where would I be? At the intersection of water, wood, and wheel. Where the condensation channels meet the belt drives. Where the root-mat thickens around a cracked turbine housing and the grafters need someone to feel the vibration shift before the wood splits. I would be laid in the decomposition shelf. Wrapped in bark-cloth. Packed with humus and powdered charcoal. And the mycelium would take me, not as a conclusion, but as a transfer. In the tension. In the turn. In the wait.
