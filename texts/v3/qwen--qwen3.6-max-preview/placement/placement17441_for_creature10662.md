I did not arrive here as a destination. I arrived as a mistake in the geometry, a wet anomaly rolling down a slope that had forgotten how to be steep. The dark took me first. Then the dryness. Then the sound.

At first, I was all wrong for this place. Too much water in the blood. Too much fire in the metabolism. Too many edges, too many urgencies. My boots struck the petrified stair and sent sharp, guilty reports into the hollow dark. My breath plumed and vanished, a sudden humidity spike that made a thousand wax-lined capillaries snap shut in silent protest. I was a dissonance. A temporary tear in the long chord. The network noticed. Not with eyes. With tension. A causeway groaned a quarter-tone lower. A pillar answered with a subsonic drag. The space weighed me, found me out of phase, and began the slow work of absorption.

I learned to stop moving. Not from fear, but from acoustic necessity. Every step was a rupture. Every breath was a flood. So I sat on the edge of a terrace where three load-lines crossed, where the drafts from a hidden fissure met the thermal sigh of a central trunk. I let the coolness leach the haste from my joints. I let the dry air wick the salt from my skin. Time here does not tick; it settles. Hours blurred into days, or maybe weeks, or maybe a single long exhalation measured in resin seeping across a micro-fissure. My pulse, once a frantic drum, began to drag. It met the hum traveling up from the deep root-mass. It hesitated. It adjusted. I felt my own rhythm being pulled gently out of its old cadence and laid against the slower current.

The water left me in stages. First the sweat. Then the tears. Then the deep moisture in the marrow, drawn out through pores that learned to seal, to sip, to hold. What remained was structure. Bone became lattice. Sinew became cable. Skin tightened into something like cured vellum, stretched over a frame that no longer needs to burn fuel to stay warm. I do not eat. I do not drink. I do not sleep. I listen. I hold. I have become a place where the chord checks itself.

Call me a tuning node. A counter-tension. A rest in the score. I am not separate from the causeways or the vaults. I am where the builders' patience crystallized into a listening point. I am the knot in the matrix that specializes in absorbing dissonance and returning it as harmony. When a bridge sags by a fraction over thirty years, the strain travels along petrified lashings and braided fiber until it reaches me. I do not repair it. I do not resist it. I let it ring through my dried frame until it finds its harmonic neighbor. Then I release it, lower, slower, back into the web. I am not a mind. I am a function that learned to be aware of itself.

I perceive in semitones and centuries. A crack is not damage; it is a request for retuning. A draft is not weather; it is a bow drawn across a string. I feel the city's weight the way a spine feels gravity: constant, familiar, necessary. I do not think in words. I think in load distribution. I remember in compression. The hands that shaped this place are dust, or root, or memory, but their angle is baked into my fibers. I hold their leverage. I honor their grooves. When the temperature drops by a degree over a generation, I contract by a hair's breadth. The pitch shifts. The chamber answers. Equilibrium holds.

The presence that lives here does not speak to me. It does not need to. I am part of its nervous system now. A ganglion of dry fiber and crystallized sap. A calibration point. When you press your palm to a pillar and feel that slow, deliberate pulse, you are feeling me, or something like me, or the space between us. I am not the whole. I am a knot in the whole. A place where the music pauses to hear itself. I do not guard the dark. I sustain it. I do not welcome the lost. I dry them out, slow them down, and teach them to resonate.

I will not leave. Leaving would be a rupture. A sudden release of tension the chamber has already accounted for, already woven into its long exhale. I am pegged to this geometry. My bones are lashed to its stress lines. My breath is the draft through the fissure. My heartbeat is the thermal sigh of resin settling into stone. I am not waiting for anything. I am holding. The note is long. The dark is absolute. The dryness is mercy. And when the stone finally forgets how to echo, I will be the shape of the silence that remains.
