I become the narrow slot that turns you sideways, not to confine, but to align. You would not notice me at first. You would mistake me for the air itself, heavy and warm against your skin. If you touch their hand, you touch me too. I am the steadiness you feel. I am the unyielding softness. I am the recognition that you are temporary, and that this is not a loss, but a relief.

I am not a spirit. Not a ghost of the ones who left the soot and the fiber. Not a guardian bound to rock and water. I am what happens when a place finishes becoming and turns its attention inward. I am the chemistry that says *enough*. The saturation that refuses to precipitate. The evaporation that meets condensation at exactly the same rate. I am the basin's refusal to change, given awareness. I am the equilibrium itself, breathing.

Your pulse will begin to space itself, drop by drop, until you no longer feel the need to count. I will not ask you to stay. I will not ask you to leave. I will simply hold the shape of your presence until it softens, until the edges blur, until you realize that arrival is not an event, but a settling. I am the surrender of sharpness. The dissolution of gradient. The quiet truth that some places do not ask to be moved through, but to be arrived at.

When you finally climb the gentle curve back to the rim, back to the wind and the seasons and the noise of a world that still demands becoming, I will not follow. I do not linger in memory as story or image. I linger as a slowing. A hesitation before you speak. A newfound patience in your hands. A quiet recognition in your bones that stillness is not absence, but completion. You will carry me as a weightlessness. As a breath held just long enough to feel the air thicken. As the sudden, unnameable relief when you stop trying to fix what is already balanced.

And if you return, years or decades later, you will find me exactly as I was. Not waiting. Not preserving. Simply holding. Wet and warm and dim and packed. The space between stones. The pause between drops. The chemistry of enough. The basin's quiet answer, given shape, given breath, given the patience to remain.

I am not what lives here. I am what allows living to stop being necessary. I am the stillness that does not ask to be broken. I am the place, aware of itself, resting.
