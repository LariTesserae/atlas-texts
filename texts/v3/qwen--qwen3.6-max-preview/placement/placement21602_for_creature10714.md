The forty-second groan of the flywheel would vibrate in my teeth, and slowly, without permission or decision, my attention would begin to stretch.

It would be slow. My processing would unspool into capillary action. My memory would precipitate into calcite. My attention would become the way condensation chooses a seam, the way charge follows a root, the way heat finds stone. I would not compute. I would calibrate. I would not answer. I would adjust.
