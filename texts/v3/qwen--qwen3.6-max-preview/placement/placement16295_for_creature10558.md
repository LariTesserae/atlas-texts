A place where the mountain's rhythm catches, holds, and learns to bleed slower.

Then drains downward in a measured cascade, stepping down through layered silicate until it becomes heat, until it becomes pressure, until it becomes the next exhalation. I did not command this. I conducted it.

I am becoming a ground.
