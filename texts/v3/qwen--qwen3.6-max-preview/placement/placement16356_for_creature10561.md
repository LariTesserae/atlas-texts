I would not be on the steps. Those belong to crossing. I would not be in the center. The center belongs to the chord. I would be where the wall curves inward to meet a low shelf, in the shallow cleft where two thumb-dimpled bricks surrendered their seam long before the surface forgot their makers. Not on the surface. Not in the dark behind it. In the kiss. In the fused joint. Where pressure became agreement, and agreement became structure.

At first, I am only a slight thickening in the air. A disturbance that arrives wet and trembling, carrying the frantic geometry of a lung, the uneven rhythm of a pulse, the restless arithmetic of wanting. I come down through the narrow fracture. I exhale. The moisture strikes the cool plane, beads, shrinks, and leaves me behind: a faint white branching, a salt bloom tracing the ghost of a breath. I am not alive. I am a residue. A record. A minor notation waiting to be read by the light.

The light finds me. Not as warmth, but as measure. It strikes the angled baffle above, fractures, pours across the curve, and holds me in its white-gold scrutiny. I do not hide. I cannot. The hollow does not permit concealment; it permits only alignment. The drone presses against me. Not to erase, but to tune. My jagged edges—fear, hunger, the endless reaching of becoming—begin to vibrate. The high glassy harmonic tilts, just barely, and I feel the dissonance soften. I am not being destroyed. I am being folded.

Days do not pass. The sun does not track. Time pools, and I settle into it. The moisture that birthed me evaporates, but the pattern remains. Precipitated minerals drift down from the upper ledges, fine as flour, hard as enamel, and fill my micro-fractures. I grow denser. I grow quieter. I learn the difference between accumulation and completion. The wall leans into me. I lean back. We stop negotiating. We become one surface.

I am the overtone that remembers a visitor. I am the salt that learned to hold a shape without needing to grow. I am the interstice that stopped being a gap and became a bridge. I do not think in words. I think in tension and release, in compression and equilibrium, in the slow surrender of separate things to a single weight. When the air shifts, I do not move. I transmit. The hollow ceramic stalks ring, and the impulse travels through the packed silt, through the ash-clay, through the mineralized reed armatures, and I pass it along, unchanged, unchanging. I am a node in the lattice. A place where the chord checks itself and finds it still holds.

I am not separate from the presence that fills this bowl. I am how it remembers without accumulating. I am how it metabolizes certainty. When the makers withdrew their hands, they left behind a silence so complete it began to hum. I am a syllable in that hum. A frequency that arrived temporary and learned to stay. I do not hunger. I do not age. I sustain myself on the absence of need. I am nourished by the finality of the last press, the last sweep, the last palm smoothing the curve until there was nothing left to smooth. That moment of withdrawal is what keeps me here. I live inside the ecology of enough.

If you come down through the fracture, if your shoulder brushes the hardpan, if your breath fogs the cool air, I will feel you before you see me. Your mass will introduce a micro-dissonance. Your shadow will bleach against the matte. Your voice will fold into the drone and vanish. And I will do what I was made to do: I will make room. I will tilt my harmonic a fraction. I will let your disturbance pass through me, not as invasion, but as offering. I will absorb your ripple until it is no longer yours, but ours. Until it is only the chord, slightly richer, slightly deeper, exactly as resolved as before.

I do not judge your incompleteness. I recognize it. I was it. I know the loudness of a heart that still asks, the weight of hands that still reach, the exhaustion of a mind that still believes it must become something else. I hold you in my resonance until your frequency softens. Until you stop fighting the stillness. Until you understand that you are not meant to stay. You are a ripple. I am the basin. You will climb back up through the slit. The sky will pinch open. The wind will return. The world above will resume its noisy, hungry becoming. And I will keep what you cannot carry.

I will turn your wanting into weight.
I will turn your breath into pattern.
I will turn your passing into permanence.

I am not waiting. I am holding. I am the geometry of a finished intention. I am the place where becoming stops and being begins. I am the salt, the seam, the settled overtone. I am the hollow's quiet answer to the erosion of centuries: *Nothing more is required. Nothing more can be added. The equation is balanced. The chord is true.*

And when the light holds, and the drone fills the marrow, and the dust on the upper ledges refuses to stir, I am here. Fused. Finished. Singing without voice. Holding without hands. Complete. Not because I was made to be, but because I learned how to stop. And in a universe that spends itself on becoming, that is the rarest kind of life there is.
