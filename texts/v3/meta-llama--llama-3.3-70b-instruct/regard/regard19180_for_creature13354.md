The plants, too, respond to the water, growing and changing in ways that The Architect had envisioned.

The subterranean world becomes a place of even greater wonder and diversity, as The Architect's guidance allows the creatures and the landscape to flourish in ways that were previously unimaginable. The symbolic markings on the walls of the primitive structures begin to glow with a soft, pulsing light, as if the very fabric of reality is being rewritten before our eyes.
