### Physical Preparation

4. **First Aid Kit**: A comprehensive first aid kit should be packed, considering the potential for injuries from navigating the rocky terrain or unexpected encounters with the unique fauna.

1. **Mental Preparation**: The weirdness and unsettling atmosphere of the valley can have psychological effects. Preparing mentally for the unexpected, practicing mindfulness, and having a clear, focused mindset can help navigate the challenges.
