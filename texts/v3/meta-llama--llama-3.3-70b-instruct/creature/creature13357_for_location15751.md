Their features are indistinct, as if they have been worn smooth by the constant mist and the patient, unrelenting passage of time.

The Echokeepers move with a slow, deliberate pace, their footsteps echoing through the stillness like the gentle lapping of waves against the shore. They are drawn to the tiny pools of water that dot the landscape, where they pause to drink and to listen. For in these pools, they hear the whispers of the land itself, the ancient, primal songs that echo through the rocks and the water.

The Echokeepers are keepers of the silence, guardians of the balance that maintains this delicate, primordial world. They are the ones who tend the mist, who nurture the scrappy, moss-like growths that cling to the rocks, and who listen to the whispers of the land. They are the ones who have learned to navigate the twisting, nightmarish tunnels and hidden chambers, who have mapped the labyrinthine paths that wind through the rocks.

Despite their small stature, the Echokeepers possess a profound, unshakeable sense of connection to the land. They are attuned to the vibrations of the earth, to the subtle, unseen forces that shape the rocks and the water. They can feel the pulse of the land, the rhythm of the dripping water, and the soft, muffled rustle of unknown, unseen creatures.

The Echokeepers are also the inheritors of the nascent, primitive technology that litters the landscape. They have learned to fashion crude, hand-hewn tools from the rocks and the fibers that they find, tools that allow them to shape the world around them. They have developed a deep understanding of the properties of stone and water, of the way that the land responds to their touch.

As the Echokeepers move through the landscape, they leave behind a trail of subtle, almost imperceptible changes. They nudge the rocks into new positions, guiding the water into narrow, winding channels that feed the tiny pools. They coax the scrappy, moss-like growths into new patterns, weaving them into intricate, lace-like networks that bind the landscape together.

In this way, the Echokeepers maintain the balance of the land, ensuring that the delicate, primordial world remains stable and unchanged.
