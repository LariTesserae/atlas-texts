In this mesmerizing, ever-changing metropolis of Frosthaven, the inhabitants are as diverse and dynamic as the city itself. They are a population of inventors, engineers, and innovators, driven by an insatiable curiosity and a passion for experimentation. Many are master craftsmen and tinkerers, capable of coaxing life and functionality from the most unlikely of materials. They move through the city's winding streets with a mix of caution and abandon, their senses heightened as they navigate the perpetual construction and deconstruction of the urban landscape.

Among the inhabitants, there are the "Grafters," a group of skilled artisans who have developed a unique affinity for the city's hybrid, nature-meets-artifice aesthetic. They weave together organic and synthetic materials to create extraordinary, adaptive structures that seem to grow and change in response to the city's turbulent energy. The Grafters are revered for their ability to coax forth unexpected beauty from the most mundane of materials, and their creations often become focal points for the city's vibrant, if chaotic, cultural landscape.

Then, there are the "Luminari," a loose collective of inventors, alchemists, and mystics who have dedicated themselves to unlocking the secrets of Frosthaven's peculiar magic. They are responsible for the city's dazzling, crystal-like skyscrapers and the ethereal, glowing orbs that seem to defy gravity and logic. The Luminari are obsessed with understanding the fundamental forces that shape the city, and their research often pushes the boundaries of what is thought possible.

The "Wilders," a tribe of urban explorers and survivalists, have adapted to life in Frosthaven by embracing the city's mercurial nature. They roam the streets, uncovering hidden corners and secret spaces, and developing a unique understanding of the city's hidden rhythms and patterns. The Wilders are a valuable resource for the city's inhabitants, serving as guides, scavengers, and scouts in the ever-shifting landscape.

In addition to these groups, there are the "Keepers," enigmatic guardians who seem to be tasked with maintaining the delicate balance between the city's wild, natural elements and its artificial, technological infrastructure. They are often seen patrolling the streets, armed with strange, hybrid tools that blend organic and synthetic components. The Keepers are rumored to possess ancient knowledge and mystical abilities, allowing them to communicate with the city's creatures and negotiate the ever-changing tides of magic and technology.

Frosthaven's inhabitants also include a multitude of entrepreneurs, traders, and showmen, who cater to the city's insatiable appetite for novelty and wonder. They peddle exotic wares, showcase fantastical creatures, and stage dazzling performances that blur the lines between reality and fantasy. These vendors and performers often gather in the city's grand, if temporary, marketplaces, which seem to spring up and disappear as suddenly as the city's buildings and streets.

Throughout the city, one can also find the "Forgotten," individuals who have become lost in the labyrinthine streets and have adapted to life on the margins of society. They eke out a living in hidden alleyways, abandoned buildings, and other forgotten spaces, developing a unique, scavenged culture that is both fascinating and treacherous.

As the inhabitants of Frosthaven navigate the city's perpetual flux, they are constantly evolving, adapting, and reinventing themselves. They are a population of visionary dreamers, pragmatic problem-solvers, and daring innovators, united by their passion for exploration, discovery, and creativity. In this city of wonder, where the laws of reality are constantly bending and warping, the inhabitants must be prepared to reimagine themselves and their place in the world at every turn, for in Frosthaven, the only constant is change.
