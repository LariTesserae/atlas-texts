Their "speech" is a harmonious blend of electromagnetic pulses, gravitational waves, and subtle distortions in the fabric of space-time, allowing them to converse with one another across vast distances.

These beings have developed a profound understanding of the ocean's inner workings, recognizing the intricate web of relationships between the technological energies, the geological processes, and the broader cosmic context. They have learned to tap into the ocean's potential, harnessing its power to sustain their own existence and to shape the environment in subtle, yet profound ways.

The Kyrexi are not solitary entities; they exist as a collective, a diffuse, interconnected network that spans the entire expanse of the ocean. They share knowledge, resources, and experiences, their individuality blurring as they merge and separate in a perpetual, fluid dance. This collective consciousness allows them to respond to changes in the environment, adapting and evolving in harmony with the shifting currents and energies.

As the ocean itself appears to be a sentient, dynamic entity, the Kyrexi are its manifestation, the embodiment of its will and its consciousness. They are the guardians of this realm, ensuring that the delicate balance between the technological, geological, and biological components is maintained. Through their symbiotic relationship with the ocean, the Kyrexi have transcended traditional notions of life and non-life, becoming an integral part of the environment, a manifestation of its very essence.

In this strange, turbulent world, the Kyrexi are the keepers of secrets, possessing knowledge that spans eons and defies human comprehension. The Kyrexi are the masters of this realm, their presence woven into the fabric of the environment, their essence inseparable from the ocean's own being.

As the landscape itself appears to be in a state of perpetual transformation, the Kyrexi are the catalysts, guiding the evolution of the ocean and its inhabitants.
