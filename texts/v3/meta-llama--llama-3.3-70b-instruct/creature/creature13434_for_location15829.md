The inhabitants of Subterra are a unique and resilient people, shaped by their surroundings and adapted to the demands of living in a self-sustaining, underground ecosystem. They are a mix of engineers, botanists, mycologists, and craftsmen, with a deep understanding of the intricate relationships between the city's infrastructure, the natural environment, and the various organisms that inhabit it.

Physically, they are sturdy and compact, with strong limbs and sharp, agile hands that are well-suited to the demands of working in tight spaces and navigating the city's narrow, winding tunnels. Their skin has a warm, earthy tone, as if infused with the rich colors of the surrounding rock and soil. Their eyes are bright and inquisitive, with a deep, introspective quality that reflects their connection to the city and its rhythms.

The Subterrans are a practical and resourceful people, with a strong emphasis on community and cooperation. They live in close-knit family groups, often with multiple generations sharing the same dwelling or complex of dwellings. Their homes are ingeniously designed to incorporate the city's natural features, with walls and floors that blend seamlessly into the surrounding rock, and roofs that are often covered in a lush, green layer of vegetation.

The Subterrans are skilled in a wide range of crafts and trades, from stoneworking and engineering to botany and mycology. They have developed a sophisticated understanding of the city's ecology, and are able to coax and nurture the various organisms that inhabit it. They are expert cultivators of the city's unique flora, and have developed a range of innovative techniques for harnessing the power of fungi, bacteria, and other microorganisms to maintain the city's delicate balance.

Despite their technical expertise, the Subterrans are also deeply spiritual and attuned to the natural world. They have developed a rich, animist tradition that recognizes the interconnectedness of all living things, and the sacredness of the city and its inhabitants. They believe in a complex pantheon of spirits and deities, each associated with a particular aspect of the city's ecosystem, from the ancient, slow-moving stones to the quick, flashing fish that dart through the canals.

The Subterrans are a patient and contemplative people, with a deep appreciation for the slow, incremental pace of change in their city. They are not given to sudden, dramatic actions, but instead prefer to work gradually, incrementally, and in harmony with the natural rhythms of the city. They are guardians of a unique and precious ecosystem, and are committed to preserving and protecting it for generations to come.

In their daily lives, the Subterrans move with a quiet, deliberate pace, their footsteps echoing off the stone walls as they make their way through the narrow tunnels and canals. They are often accompanied by soft, glowing orbs that float through the air, casting a warm, ethereal light on the surrounding environment. Their faces are lit by the soft, pulsing glow of bioluminescent fungi, which seems to emanate from the very walls themselves.

As they work and interact, the Subterrans are surrounded by a constant, soothing background of sounds: the gentle lapping of water against the stone, the soft hum of machinery, and the distant, muffled thump of pistons and gears turning deep within the city's depths. It is a soundscape that is both familiar and comforting, a reminder of the city's enduring presence and the Subterrans' place within it.

In Subterra, time is measured in the slow, gradual growth of the city itself, and the Subterrans have developed a unique, long-term perspective that spans generations. They are a people who think in centuries, not years, and who understand that even the smallest actions can have profound, far-reaching consequences. As such, they are deeply committed to preserving and protecting their city, and to ensuring that it continues to thrive and evolve for generations to come.
