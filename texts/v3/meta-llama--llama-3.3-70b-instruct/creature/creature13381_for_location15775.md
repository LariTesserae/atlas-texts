These bird-like creatures have evolved to harness the energy of the wind and the warmth of the sun, using it to fuel their own magical abilities, which they use to maintain the delicate balance of the ecosystem.

They possess a deep connection to the water itself, and are said to be able to control the flow and direction of the rivers, using their powers to nourish the parched earth or inundate the valleys with life-giving water. 

The landscape itself seems to be home to the Terrakai, beings of living rock and earth that have evolved to inhabit the very fabric of the terrain. They can manipulate the stone and soil to create towering structures or hidden caverns, shaping the landscape to their will as they move through the earth with a slow, deliberate pace.

Their presence is always accompanied by a sense of unease, as if they are not quite fully present in the world, and their actions are always shrouded in mystery.
