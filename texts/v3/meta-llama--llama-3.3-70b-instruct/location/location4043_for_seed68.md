Despite the density of construction, the town feels sparse, with much space between buildings and a sense of emptiness that is balanced by the lushness of the natural world. The streets are wide and often empty, save for the occasional pedestrian or the rumble of industrial vehicles moving through the town. The sound of water, whether it's the gentle lapping of streams against their banks or the more insistent roar of a waterfall in the distance, provides a constant background melody.

Light pours down upon the town, illuminating every nook and cranny, casting long shadows in the late afternoon and painting the landscape with vibrant colors during sunrise and sunset. The weirdness of this place becomes more apparent at night, when the factories and homes are lit up, and the darkness seems to vibrate with an otherworldly energy. However, the dynamic of change here is slow, with gradual cycles that mark the passage of time. Seasons come and go, each bringing its unique charm and challenge, but the underlying essence of the town remains constant, a testament to the patient processes that have shaped it.
