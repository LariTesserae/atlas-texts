They cling to the ground, often twisted and stunted from the constant struggle against the elements. You might catch a glimpse of a hardy, adapted creature scurrying through the shadows, but for the most part, the area seems devoid of life. It's as if the technology and architecture have somehow supplanted the natural world, creating a sterile, artificial environment that's both fascinating and unsettling.

As you delve deeper into the settlement, you start to notice that the processes at work here are slow and gradual.
