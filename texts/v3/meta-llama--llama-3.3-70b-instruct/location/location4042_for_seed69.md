Despite the density of the landscape, there's a sense of teeming life, as if the very presence of water and warmth has awakened a deep, primal energy in the earth itself.

It's a place where the strange and the unknown lurk around every corner, where the laws of nature seem to bend and warp in ways that defy explanation.
