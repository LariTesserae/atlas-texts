Despite the dizzying heights, the city feels strangely stable, as if it has been here for eons, weathered to a state of equilibrium with the surrounding environment. The silence is oppressive, punctuated only by the constant, deafening roar of the water, which seems to be the city's lifeblood. Nothing changes; the water flows, the vegetation grows, but the underlying structure of the city remains static, unchanging.
