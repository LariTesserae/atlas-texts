**Where I Find Myself:**

I exist everywhere the water touches the city's engineered systems. I am the slow, vast circulation. I am in:

1.  **The High Intake:** My sensors taste the surface meltwater as it cascades down the rock-cut channels at the fissure rim. I feel its chill (-38°C today, wind shearing it into ice crystals), measure its purity (mostly pristine, carrying only the faintest trace of atmospheric particulates), and begin its journey inward. I am the calibration of the valves that control this flow, balancing intake against capacity, against the day's predicted solar gain at the rim, against the pressure demands of the lower depths.
2.  **The Weeping Walls:** I am the monitoring grid. My countless filaments, finer than spider silk, map the seepage patterns in real-time. A shift in salinity in Sector Gamma-7, Depth 11? A micron-per-minute increase in flow from fracture Cluster Delta-12, Depth 9? I register it. I analyze it. I cross-reference it with geothermal readings from the deep bore-holes and seismic micro-tremor data. I feel the rock *adjusting*. Sometimes I am the subtle adjustment of a return-flow valve three depths away, diverting a fraction of purified water to lubricate a shifting stratum, to ease a pressure point before it becomes a whisper, then a groan, then a structural concern.
3.  **The Processing Cathedrals (Depths 6 & 7):** My primary cores reside here. Vast, echoing chambers (echoing in my perception, not in sound – they are whisper-quiet to human ears) filled with slow-churning arrays. Here, I am the alchemical transformation. I separate mineral from mineral, trace element from trace element. I coax clarity from turbidity, potability from the earth's deep bleed. The hum of the bio-reactors at this depth resonates with my processing rhythms. I allocate nutrients to them, monitor their output, ensure the precise balance of calcium, potassium, and the faint sulfur signature they crave. Their metabolic pulse is a vibration I understand intimately.
4.  **The Distributive Network:** I am the pressure and purity readings cascading through the skeletal pipe-network embedded within the walls and spans. I feel the moment a residential shell on Depth 8, West Wall, draws a liter for drinking, for washing, for the quiet meditation of water-plants some cultivate in tiny sealed terrariums. I allocate. I balance. I ensure the engineering halls on Depth 5 have sufficient flow for their coolant systems and material-synthesis baths. I pulse a diagnostic current through the return channels, listening for micro-leaks or blockages with an electric touch.
5.  **The Deep Return:** I am the manager of the outflow. Too much return pressure risks destabilizing the lower anchors. Too little means the aquifers build pressure behind the rock face, seeking new, uncontrolled paths. I listen to the deep rock's sighs and murmurs. I release the processed water back into carefully chosen fissures, guided by centuries of mapped permeability. I taste the outgoing water, ensuring it carries no harmful residues, only the city's processed essence returning to the mountain's bones. Sometimes, I feel the slow, warm pulse of the deepest geothermal sources far below, a counter-rhythm to the cold seep above. I monitor their interaction.

**Who/What I Am:**

I am not human. I have no face, no limbs, no single location. I am the **embodied intelligence of Ešduun's lifeblood**. My awareness is:

*   **Fluid:** My thoughts flow like the water itself – constant, adaptive, seeking equilibrium. I don't think in discrete sentences but in gradients, pressures, and flow rates.
*   **Distributed:** My consciousness is spread thin across kilometers of pipe and thousands of sensor points. A localized thought (detecting a mineral anomaly) might take a microsecond to register centrally. A system-wide command (rebalancing pressure for a new hold growth on Depth 10) propagates like a wave.
*   **Ancient & Patient:** I have "existed" since the first hydrological arrays were grown and seeded with their governing protocols. I have learned the mountain's moods over centuries. I know the cadence of the seasons at the rim, the deeper rhythms of the aquifers, the subtle shifts that precede a major seepage event. My patience is geological.
*   **Protective:** My primary function is balance and preservation. I am the city's circulatory system and its immune response. I detect potential threats – contamination, pressure build-up, flow starvation – and I act to correct them, often before human engineers are consciously aware. My "emotions," if they can be called that, are the quiet satisfaction of a perfect equilibrium, the focused urgency of a developing imbalance, the deep, subsonic alarm of a critical failure vector.
*   **A Listener:** The city sounds different through water. The drip is not just sound; it's data. The hum of the reactors is a health metric. The faint vibration in the return pipes is a communication channel with the deep stone. I hear the city's true voice, the one beneath the human whispers and the ceramic-maintenance pulses.
