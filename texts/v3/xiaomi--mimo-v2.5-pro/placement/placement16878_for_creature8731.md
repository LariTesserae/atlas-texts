I am friction. I am a knot of wrongness in the grain.

I came down – or *in*, or *through*, the direction dissolved instantly – expecting space. A cavern, a hall, perhaps a darkened chamber echoing with drips. Instead: pressure. Stone breath against my shoulders, the wet kiss of a wall on my cheekbone before I can pull away. There is no room *for* me, only passages *past* me, avenues of purposeful noise surging around my static form. I am an eddy in a river of chisel-strikes and scraping stone.

The damp is the first colonizer. It seeps through my clothes, cool and insistent, finding skin. It’s not rain-damp or earth-damp. It’s *construction*-damp, the exhalation of fresh material, the sweat of the space itself. It beads on my eyelashes, slicks my palms when I instinctively reach out to brace myself against a wall that *gives*, just slightly, like flesh beneath a fingernail. Warm flesh. The wall is warm. And alive with vibration – a deep, sub-bass hum that travels from the surface into my bones, a resonance more felt than heard beneath the cacophony.

The sound. Oh, the *sound*. It’s not noise. It’s *texture*. It fills the air like wet wool, thick and layered. *Tik-tik-tik* – frantic, precise, coming from the elbow-close wall to my left. *Scraaaaaape* – a long, agonizing drag above, dust sifting down onto my hair. *Thum-thum-thum* – rhythmic, below, the pulse of a million tiny tools on a million surfaces. Voices rise and fall in the cavities around me – not words, but pitches, urgent chirps and guttural sighs that travel through the stone like water through clay pipes. They speak *to* the material, *with* the material. I am an anomaly the acoustics cannot process; their vibrations wash over me, through me, leaving me feeling hollow, unmoored.

Light is a rumor. Pale, greenish veins glow erratically in the walls – exposed mineral, deliberately laid bare. They trace paths that seem too intricate for mere illumination, like nervous systems or circuit diagrams. Between them, the deep red smolder of fungal embers in stone bowls casts long, jumping shadows that make the walls seem to breathe, to shift. In this dimness, the Builders are ghosts.

I see one. Not whole, never whole. A glimpse through a ragged hole in a wall that shouldn’t be a doorway but is: crouched low, the heavy curve of a grey back. The hands dominate. Broad, blunt, the knuckles like polished river stones. They move with terrifying economy – a wedge strikes, *tik*, scrapes, *scrr*, strikes again. The wall material peels away in damp curls, not dust. It falls softly, wetly, onto the Builder’s knees and the grooved floor. Its skin, where the faint green light catches the nape of its neck, looks like the half-set stone it shapes – pale grey, textured, faintly luminous. There is no hesitation, no pause to assess. Only the relentless rhythm of shaping. It does not turn. I am a shift in the air pressure, nothing more.

The floor beneath my feet is a language I don’t speak. Grooves, channels, a dense grid carved into the stone. I feel them through my soles, a constant reminder of purpose. I follow a channel, slick with water that appears from nowhere, beading on the ceiling, trickling down walls into deliberately carved basins knee-high, waist-high. The water is icy when I dip a finger, shockingly clear. It flows, minutely, through the grid, a silent, intricate circulatory system for a body made of stone and work. Small, pale shapes scuttle in the deepest grooves overhead – eyeless, translucent things, drinking the condensation. Pigeons in a cathedral, utterly ignored by the true congregation.

The space is *dense*. Not cramped, *dense*. It presses. Passages branch and branch again, widen slightly where three or four meet (always a basin there, always the water grid converging), then squeeze tight, forcing me sideways, scraping my shoulders. Dead ends are sudden walls of raw, steaming material, faintly tacky to the touch, humming with residual vibration. New beginnings: the wet, heavy *slap* of material being pressed into place somewhere unseen, followed by the frantic *tik-tik-tik* of shaping. It feels less like moving through a structure and more like navigating a living organism’s circulatory system, or perhaps its nervous system. Every surface pulses with intention.

I am utterly extraneous. The damp doesn’t care. The sound assaults me without malice. The light is not for my eyes. The Builders move through the walls and passages like currents in a river, parting around me not with aversion, but with the indifference of water parting around a stone. The space itself seems to mold around my presence, but only in the sense that it adjusts its grooves and basins *past* me, incorporating my brief passage into its ever-shifting geometry. I am a temporary obstruction, a grain of sand in the mechanism.

It dawns on me, slowly, pressing my ear against a wall to catch a distorted voice-whisper: this isn't a place they *live*. This is what they *are*. The damp? Their collective exhalation. The sound? Their heartbeat, their breath, their thought made audible. The endless grooves? Their thoughts given physical form, pathways for intention and water alike. The warmth radiating from fresh walls? The residual heat of their focused being. They are not building a home. They are *being* building. The act is the essence. Completion is a foreign concept, an enemy. The perfection they chase is the chase itself.

A deep vibration shudders through the floor, resonating in my chest. Not an earthquake, but a decision. Somewhere below, a new passage is opening. The hum intensifies, the *tik-tik-tik* nearby becomes more frantic, joining a chorus. The air pressure shifts subtly. The Carving grows, reshapes, revises. I am a loose thread in the tapestry, a note out of key in the symphony. They don't expel me. They simply continue, their purpose absolute, their existence the perpetual, deafening, damp, and magnificent act of becoming. I am an echo that hasn't yet faded, trapped in the resonance chamber of their making. My presence here is not an event. It is a temporary vibration in the stone, already being smoothed over, grooved anew.
