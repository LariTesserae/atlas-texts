I drift.

Not *in* the air, but *of* it. A thought made membrane, riding the Crown’s own breath. My wings – vast, translucent, tougher than silk – catch the faintest thermal gradient rising from the warm heartwood walls. It lifts me, gently, endlessly. There is no flapping, no exertion. Just the slow, silent unfurling of myself into the current, letting the warm updraft carry me upward, deeper into the dark gallery that yawns above and below like a vertical sea.

My world is pressure and moisture and the slow pulse of the structure beneath my gliding form. I see not with eyes, but with the entire surface of my being. I feel the density of the air, its warmth, its wetness. I sense the bioluminescent veins in the distant ceiling as faint warmth-tremors against my ventral membrane, constellations mapping the Crown’s slow metabolism. A droplet of condensation forms on the living wood high above. I *feel* its birth as a minute shift in humidity a hundred meters away. By the time it falls, a tiny, warm raindrop in the vast dark, I have already adjusted my angle, gliding smoothly around its path. Its soft impact on the fungal floor far below registers as a negligible vibration through the air I swim in.

Silence is my medium. My passage makes no sound. My wings are designed to displace air without turbulence, to slice through the damp atmosphere without a whisper. The profound absorption of the wood, the mats, the fog – it doesn't oppress me; it *is* my environment. It allows me to hear the Crown’s true voice: the deep, subsonic groan of wood settling kilometers away; the faint, harmonic hum where wind finds a resonant groove in the grain; the distant, rhythmic pulsing of the Glow-Worm colonies in the nether galleries, felt more than heard, a slow throb against my membranes.

I feed on the air itself. Microscopic fungal spores, wisps of decomposing organic matter carried on the slow currents, even the moisture itself – I filter them from the dense, wet atmosphere as I drift. My proboscis, delicate as spun glass, unfurls to sip. I am a part of the Crown’s cleansing breath, a living filter moving through its vast lungs.

I see the Velvet Grovers below. From my vantage, they are dark, slow-moving shapes on the faintly luminous fungal plain, their vibrations felt as gentle pulses rising through the still air. A Silk-Sailor sibling drifts nearby, a wraith-like shape against the distant fungal star-field. We don’t communicate in words, but in the subtle language of shared space: a slight shift in buoyancy, a minute adjustment of wing-angle that alters the pressure field between us. We are thoughts adrift in the same dark mind.

I navigate the Crown’s impossible geometry intuitively. A passage that seems to curve left to a ground-walker feels like a gentle downward spiral to me, carried by a slight, persistent draft. A gallery that folds back upon itself is simply a region of complex air currents, a vortex of warmth and moisture I glide through without surprise. The non-Euclidean nature isn't a puzzle; it's the texture of the currents I swim in. Distance is measured in the patience of the air, the depth of the silence I pass through.

Sometimes, in the higher galleries, I drift through the Crown’s weather. A fog bank isn’t an obstruction; it’s a change in texture, a thickening of the air I breathe, a softening of the distant fungal glows into a diffused, luminous haze. I glide through warm rain that falls straight down from a ceiling lost in the dark above. The droplets patter silently against my wings, running in rivulets down the membranes, joining the eternal moisture of the structure. It is not unpleasant; it is simply the Crown’s sweat, the evidence of its life, falling gently upon the life that moves within it.

I rest not on branches, but on updrafts. I find a current rising near a warm wall, adjust my wings to maximum span, and simply… hang. Suspended in the dark, breathing the warm wet air, feeling the slow pulse of the living wood beside me, listening to the drip and the hum and the silence that is full of slow life. I am a nerve ending of the atmosphere, sensing the vast, dark, breathing interior of the Hollow Crown. I am its thought, drifting.
