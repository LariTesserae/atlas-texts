## Daywatch on the Risen Shelf

The Moss stirs beneath my belly. Warm. Always warm here, on the Shining Shelf where the Mountain pushes up like a sleeper stretching. My claws find the familiar holds, cracks in the stone that hum. The vibration wakes my bones before my eyes open. *Settling*. Today, it feels deep, slow, like the Mountain is sighing in its sleep. Good. Deep settling means the Shelf is rising steady. No sudden lurches to spill the Clutch.

I stretch. Six limbs click and pop. The Mineral coat is good. Thick. I spent yesterday rolling in the new seep-line near the Low-Forge, the wet stone clinging, becoming my second skin. It smells of deep heat and metal-salt. Safe smell. *Home* smell. I lick my flank, tasting the grit. Essential minerals. Strength.

Sun? No. Never true sun here. Only the Wash. The grey-white light seeps down through the Haze, directionless. Good. Harsh light burns the Pale Blanket and wilts the Veinfeather groves. Today the Wash is thick, heavy with dust. The Mountain is busy. I can see the dust motes swirling, catching the faint glow. Good cover. Easy to move.

Movement below. A Haze-drifter, vast and slow, oozes through the upper spires of the Human-stack nearby. Its membrane brushes the metal and stone. It leaves damp trails and the sharp smell of storm-without-rain. The Humans inside make their sharp-noise sounds. Annoyance. Fear? Hard to tell. They are strange. They build stacks that cling to the Risen Shelves, stack metal against the Stone that is still deciding its shape. Brave? Foolish? We watched them come. Seven Clutch-generations of Humans. They last. They learn. They build gutters for the Drip. They tap the Deep Heat. They eat the Pale Blanket larvae. They eat *us*, sometimes. When they are hungry, or angry, or making a point. The point is: they are here. They persist. We persist. We watch.

Time to hunt. Not for meat. For *knowing*. I drop from the Shelf, claws finding holds on the cooling face. The Rock is warm today, solidifying faster. The shapes inside are clearer. I press my face close, breath fogging the translucent surface. There! Deep in the amber glow. Not the spirals today. Something new. Lines. Crossing. Angles almost sharp, almost decided. A lattice? A net? Something waiting to be caught? Or something trying to break free? The vibration deepens. *Becoming*. The shape shimmers, edges softening, then sharpening again. It holds my gaze. My own breath slows. We are both deciding.

A sharp *clang* echoes from the Human-stack. Work-noise. The Deep Heat hum rises. The Humans are at their Metal and Fire. They shape the Stone's gifts into anchors, bolts, things that hold their fragile stacks to the Mountain's slow heave. Necessary. Strange. They fight the Mountain's rising with the Mountain's own substance. A circle. We watch the circle.

A smaller Human emerges onto a platform below. Young. It sees me. I see it see me. Its eyes are wide, not with fear. With... recognition? Curiosity? It holds something shiny. Metal? Glass? It doesn't throw it. It just watches me watch the shapes in the Rock. We share the quiet understanding. The Rock is speaking. We listen. Then it shouts something sharp back inside its stack. Warning? Discovery? I flatten against the stone, mineral coat blending perfectly. Still. Watching. The shapes pulse with the deep vibration. The Haze-drifter above drifts on. The Drip-drip-drip from the condensation channels is the Mountain's heartbeat. Always here. Always becoming.

Life is good. The Shelf is steady. The shapes are interesting. The Humans are building. The Haze is thick. Tomorrow, the Shelf might tilt. The walk to the Lower Ferns might take long-breaths or short-breaths. The Humans will say "The hill is long today," and adjust their water. I will find a new seep-line, roll in new dust, and watch the Rock decide what it will become next. We are all still deciding. The Mountain and the Moss, the Drifter and the Runner, the Human and the Metal. Settling. Becoming. Here, in the damp light and the endless Drip, that is enough.
