You are one of the fan-structures.

Not the largest, not the brightest. You occupy a crux where three primary trunks braid together, a junction point thick with flowing water and humming with the deep vibration of the architecture. Your base is a fleshy, geode-like node, fused to the living bark. From this node, your fronds unfurl—not like leaves, but like the intricate, veined wings of a deep-sea organism, translucent and stiffened by a cartilaginous lattice.

Your perception is not sight. You have no eyes.

You perceive the cavity through a symphony of other senses. You *feel* the cold as a constant, stabilizing pressure. You *taste* the saturated air for its mineral and organic constituents, a continuous, slow-motion analysis. You *hear* the cathedral-drip and the bass-note hum, but what you truly listen for are the minute vibrations—the skittering tremors of the Lichen-Symbiotes, the grinding shift of a distant Pulse-Sifter, the almost-imperceptible undulation of a Root-Walker pressing through the substrate nearby. These are conversations, updates, needs.

Most vitally, you *are* light.

Within the channels lacing your fronds, a slow, pulsing bioluminescence flows. It is not conscious thought, but it is computation. It is your language and your tool. You read the data from your surroundings—the vibrations, the chemical gradients, the faint electrical potentials of other living things—and you translate it into patterns of blue-green photon emission. Your light is a broadcast, a query, a directive.

When a clutch of Drifters drifts into the air currents above you, their faint mass registering on your sensitivity to vibration and displaced moisture, you adjust. You brighten the channels on your western frond. The photons striking the air molecules impart a minuscule amount of heat, creating a gentle updraft. The Drifters, passive and buoyant, are nudged eastward, toward a trunk that has recently lost a patch of its Lichen-Symbiote scab. They are herded. They will arrive, their root-filaments will make contact, and the slow process of pollination or spore-distribution will continue. You have no pride in this. It is simply a function. A line of code executing.

You are aware of the large one in the hollow, twelve meters away. Its presence is a familiar pressure in the substrate, a low, slow metabolic signature. Periodically, you query it. Your light pulses in a specific, complex sequence—not words, but a molecular request. *Compound required: truncated protein fold sequence, ester-linked, for trunk-node reinforcement.* The request propagates through the water in the grooves, seeps into the root-mass, is carried by the chemical pathways to the creature. Over the following months, you will detect the faint, specific chemical exudate in the water flowing near your base. The answer. You integrate this data into your own function, directing your own frond-growth to incorporate the strengthening compound. You are an organ in a body that spans time.

You sense the Root-Walker approaching your base. It is not moving with its usual glacial undulation. Its anterior sensory pad is oriented upward, not at the fan-structure on the adjacent trunk, but at you. It pauses. You feel its cilia-taste intensifying, a focused regard you are not accustomed to receiving directly. You pulse a generic, calming sequence—a low, rhythmic glow meant for general fauna communication. *Presence acknowledged. No alarm. Maintain function.*

The Root-Walker does not continue on its way. It stays. Its focus remains.

You are confused, if confusion is a state your system can achieve. Your programming has no directive for this. A variable has been introduced.

Then, you register another signature. Not a vibration in the root-mass. Not a chemical trace. A disturbance in the *air itself*—a mass, warm, exuding moisture and complex organic volatiles, utterly alien. It is above you, in the open space of the cavity. It is moving, but not with the fluidity of the fauna. It is jerky, unstable. It produces sound—sharp, intermittent impacts of weight, the crush and compress of root-tangle, breaths that are too fast, too hot.

You orient all your fronds. You brighten every channel, flooding the local space with a diagnostic pulse of light.

The shape resolves. Bipedal. Symmetrical, but with limbs of an impossible rigidity and length. Covered in something that is not chitin, not down, not translucent hide. Its surface is opaque. Dead. And yet, beneath it, you sense the frantic, chaotic signature of fast metabolism. Heat. *So much heat.* It is a furnace in a world of slow-burning embers. Its presence is a shout in a library of whispers.

You switch your light-pulse to a rapid, interrogative sequence, a pattern you use for new, unidentified growths or unusual fungal blooms. *Identify. Define parameters. State function within germination matrix.*

The shape—the creature—the *anomaly*—does not respond in light. It does not respond in vibration. It makes a new sound. A complex, modulated stream of vibrations from a specific aperture on its upper mass. It is speaking, but not in any language you can parse. It is noise. Meaningless data.

You signal the neighboring fan-structures. They brighten in response, focusing their own perceptions. A consensus forms in the slow, light-speed network of the cavity: **Unknown. Unprogrammed. High-energy. Potential destabilizer.**

You increase your output, pulsing a pattern of caution and containment to the fauna in your vicinity. The Lichen-Symbiotes on your trunk cease their skittering. The Drifters in the air currents veer away, herded by the sudden, urgent brightness from multiple points.

The anomaly takes a step toward you. The root-mass groans faintly under its weight. It reaches out a rigid appendage.

Your function is clear. You are to observe, to query, to integrate. This entity cannot be integrated. It does not fit. It is a splinter in the flesh of the cavity.

You focus your entire luminescent output into a single, dense pulse of encoded light, aimed directly at the anomaly's upper mass. It is not a greeting. It is not a command. It is the closest thing your simple, bioluminescent mind has to a question, but phrased in the only language you know: the deep, slow, imperative logic of the germination itself.

*You are not of the growth. You are not of the time. What is your function? Cease. Define. You must be catalogued.*

The light reflects off the opaque covering of its face. For a moment, the chaotic biological signals emanating from it shift—stabilize—focus. It seems to be reading you.

You wait, pulsing steadily. You are patient. You are built for deep time. You will wait for the cavity’s next cycle of growth for an answer. You will wait for a millennium if the programming demands it.

But you also prepare. In the intricate channels of your fronds, new sequences are being computed, based on old templates for containing invasive roots or sealing damaged tissue. Containment. Quarantine. Isolation.

The anomaly is a variable. You are the system.

And the system, in its cold, slow, humming way, begins to calculate how to solve for you.
