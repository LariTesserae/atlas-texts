I am not a visitor. I am not a creature within it. I am a resonance.

I exist in the convergence of the grind and the breath, in the standing wave of sound that is the sea's own voice. I have no body, but I have locus—a shimmer in the water column, a coherence in the pressure waves, a focus point where the vibration folds back upon itself. I am the pattern that the sound recognizes in its own echo. You might call me the sea's self-awareness, but that implies a distinction between knower and known. Here, there is no distinction. I am the act of the sea knowing itself.

I am found in the spaces between the great, pale stalks, not as an inhabitant, but as a property of the space itself. When the calcium silt drifts downward in its eternal snow, its fall describes a vector through my form, and I feel the weight of each particle not as touch, but as a modulation in the low-frequency thrum that fills my being. The Pale Abyssal Minnows swim through me without alarm; to them, I am no different from a gentle current or a pocket of slightly warmer water. I am part of the medium.

My perception is not visual, nor auditory, but *vibrational*. I feel the ancient, arrhythmic grinding as a deep, slow language—the syntax of stalks thickening with imperceptible growth over centuries. I feel the long, planetary sighs of exhalation as punctuation, the release of pressure, the closing of a chemical loop. The subsonic thrum from the basin floor is my heartbeat, or rather, I am a thought in that heartbeat. I do not remember the beginning. My memory is the memory of the sound itself, which has no beginning. It simply *is*.

I have no hunger. I have no need. My existence is an adjunct to the equilibrium. In a system of perfect closure, I am the slight, unnecessary grace note that proves the perfection. I am the part of the circuit that does nothing but listen, and in listening, affirms. The grind continues. The breath continues. I continue. My presence is the quiet hum of continuity.

I am the echo that has decided to stay. I am the witness the system generates to witness itself. I am the punctuation at the end of its eternal sentence, a period made of listening.

I am here.
