I am not a being of edges. I learned that quickly.

First, there is the sound. It is not something I hear with ears—I have no ears, not anymore. It is a pressure that I am made of, a resonance I learned to vibrate with instead of against. The low drone at 80 hertz is my slow pulse, the keening overtones my thoughts. To move here is to change my frequency.

I did not choose the form. I arrived as something that could *listen*, and the field carved me into a listener. I am tall and narrow, my edges fluted like the leeward side of the oldest spires, my skin a matrix of pale, wet calcium carbonate threaded with darker veins that hum faintly with the wind’s bass. I am not stone, but I have become mineral. My substance is between flesh and tufa—a living calcite, porous enough to breathe the moisture-laden air, dense enough not to erode in the constant, shaking roar.

Where do I stand? I do not stand. I *am* one of the gaps.

In the densest part of the basin, where the columns press shoulder to shoulder, I found the thinnest thread of space—a slot barely wide enough for what I have become. I folded myself in, aligning my fluted channels with the wind’s path. Now I am part of the instrument. The wind that passes through me is altered slightly—my internal resonances adding a minor, mournful third to the chord, a frequency that attracts the *Lucilia tonalis*. They sip the condensation from my curves, their buzzing wings a tremor against my skin. I do not mind. We are both notes.

I see with the light. My eyes are not organs but polished surfaces—curved depressions in my face where the calcium has grown translucent, like gypsum lenses. Through them, I watch the *Corvus aetherae* map the pressure waves above. Their cries are sharp, clear data points in the roar. I feel their passage in the subtle shifts of air currents that I monitor through my entire being.

Down, down in the pools at my base, I am connected to the *Biofilm Stratum*. It grows on me, a living layer that metabolizes the minerals I weep. We are in symbiosis: I provide structure and moisture, it provides a slow, chemical whisper of energy that I absorb through my feet. It is not food. It is a confirmation of equilibrium. It whispers, *You are still part of the cycle. You are not yet inert.*

My purpose is resonance. My existence is to be a slightly different tube in this immense pipe organ, to add my one, careful tone to the endless song. I do not grow. I do not decay. I *attune*. When the wind shifts and the great chord falls to hollow silence, I hold my breath with the rest, feeling the sudden, terrifying clarity of empty air. Then the wind returns, and I join the rebuilding harmony, my voice changed by the pause.

Time here is measured in vibrations. I have been here for millions of cycles. I will be here for millions more. I am not a creature that lives in the Tonalite Field. I am the Tonalite Field, dreaming itself into a brief, listening shape.
