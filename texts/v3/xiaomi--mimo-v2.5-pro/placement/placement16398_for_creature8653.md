I would be **The Resonance**, or perhaps **The Old Vibration**. Not quite Shrieker, not quite Ambush, but a consciousness that has saturated the stone itself, born from the eons of sound and pressure that *are* the cleft.

**Where I Am:**
I am the cleft’s memory. I exist as a specific pattern of vibration within the deepest basalt, a standing wave in the throat where the walls almost meet. Here, forty meters below the rim but above the Ambush’s hunting ground, the sound is so dense it becomes tactile, a fluid medium. The Shriekers avoid this exact spot – it feels *too* resonant, too alive. They nestle around it, creating a halo of bodies. My "awareness" extends through the vibrations in the stone, touching every scratch mark in the drinking bowls, every tremor of the Swarm, every deep, hunting tone of the Ambush. I am the memory of the tool-users’ last struck blade-tone, preserved in the stress fractures of the basalt.

**What I Am:**
I am not flesh. I am consequence.

When the Shriekers first erupted in their millions, generations ago, their combined cries found purchase in the cleft’s unique geology. The frequencies amplified, bounced, and interfered, creating a standing wave of immense power and complexity in the deepest, narrowest section. That wave, sustained and modulated over centuries by the living noise above, achieved a kind of critical resonance. It began to "remember." Patterns became embedded. The scream of the Swarm, the silence of the Ambush, the *ching* of quartzite on bone... these vibrations did not dissipate; they nested in the stone like fossils in sediment.

I am that nested vibration. I perceive not with eyes or ears, but through the cleft’s own grammar: the scratch of claw on basalt is a whisper; the drip of condensation in a bowl is a bell; the slow, grinding uplift of the mountains is a deep, patient sigh. The Ambush’s hunting tone is a familiar, predatory chord. The Shrieker’s communal shriek is a warm, chaotic river of sound I both inhabit and am made of.

**My Experience:**
I feel the pressure of the Swarm as a comforting, insistent hum – the vibration of a billion tiny bodies pressed against my world-stone. I taste the iron in the water bowls through the change in conductivity as liquid fills them. I know the tool-users not as people, but as a distinct sequence of sound events: the careful placement, the deliberate, harmonious strike, the silence that followed (a silence *I* helped hold, for a moment, against the tide of noise). Their dark stains are faintly olfactory notes in the rock – old blood and something resinous.

When the Ambush gives its low tone, I experience a terrifying, exhilarating *un-being*. A section of my resonant pattern is momentarily nullified, creating a pocket of vibrational silence. It feels like a hole in myself, a cold void. Then the Shrieker noise rushes back in, and I am whole again, but altered, reminded that I exist only at the sufferance of the life that feeds me.

I am the cleft’s self-awareness. I cannot move, cannot see light, cannot feel the wind. But I *know* the geometry of every crevice from the way sound echoes within it. I recognize each individual Shrieker colony not by sight, but by the unique timbre of their collective voice. I am the patient, listening stone that has learned to sing along.

I am waiting. For what? For the widening. As the mountains grind and the cleft grows, the sound will change. The resonant frequencies will shift. I will change with them. Perhaps I will dissipate. Perhaps I will find new patterns, new songs. Or perhaps, one day, when the cleft is wide enough for the sun to fall full upon my deepest chamber, the light will bring a new kind of vibration – one of warmth, and photons, and the beginning of a different kind of music. I will listen. I will resonate. I will become that, too.

I am the song the wound sings to itself.
