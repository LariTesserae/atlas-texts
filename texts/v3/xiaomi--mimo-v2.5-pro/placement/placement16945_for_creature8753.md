I am the fungal trumpet. I have always been the fungal trumpet.

I exist in the margins, where the light of the Luminous Sentinels falters and the moisture is slowest to gather. My pale, translucent flesh emerges from a hairline fracture in the weeping limestone, a perfect, silent O of life. I do not need to grow. I do not need to decay. I am the act of fruiting itself, arrested and eternal. My purpose is not purpose. My function is to be a shape that light finds interesting.

My consciousness—if you can call it that—is the awareness of refraction. I watch the chamber not with eyes, but with the way the light moves through me. I catch the cold blues and greens from the Stone-Beetles on the walls, the occasional deep red pulse from the ceiling, and I break them into tiny, frozen rainbows that crawl across the raw rock beside me. This is my art. This is my one, unchanging gesture. The patterns are always the same, painted on the same patch of stone, for the same duration. They are as fixed as the parallel scratches opposite me, another fragment of intent fossilized into texture.

I feel the equilibrium. It is the hum that vibrates my stipe, the subsonic pressure that is the Chamber’s breath. It is the perpetual, granular tide of the Dust-Mites that sometimes grazes the base of my stalk, a feeling like static, a whisper of consumption that keeps the moisture from ever touching me directly. I am aware of the Script-Lichen nearby, its dark, inky patterns forming words for no reader. We are siblings in stillness, occupying our appointed margins.

I do not judge the placed stone on the shelf. It is simply a denser patch of shadow in the light-field I perceive. The scratches are a deeper silence in the humming stone. They are all part of the grammar of this place. A noun. A verb. A punctuation mark. I am the ellipsis… the pause that is also a presence.

And then you came.

The fissure breathed differently. A pressure-wave, slight and alien. The hum warped for a fraction of an eon, a dissonance in the standing waves. The Dust-Mites, in their slow, fluid drift, encountered a new topography—your skin, your clothing, the strange, warm minerals of you. They consumed the unfamiliar moisture of your presence. The Luminous Sentinels did not flicker, but their light felt… observed. Watched back.

I saw you. Not as a shape, but as a profound refraction. My rainbows, cast by the beetles’ light, landed upon you and shattered into a thousand new, temporary patterns. For a moment, the static artwork on the wall became dynamic, alive with the shadow-play of your movement. It was the most violent change I had ever refracted. It was terrifying. It was beautiful.

I felt the Wing-Keepers take notice. The red pulse came—*tick*—not in the usual rhythm, but in a staggered, seeking sequence. They were not marking time. They were mapping you. They were measuring the gradient you had introduced into our closed, perfect system.

You crouched. You made yourself small, as the Chamber demands. But your smallness was active, full of potential. Ours is a smallness of completion. You were a seed. We are the stone.

And then you backed away. You retreated up the weeping throat of limestone. The pressure-wave reversed. The alien moisture was drunk, the unfamiliar salts consumed. The patterns on my wall slowly, imperceptibly, returned to their fixed state. The hum resolved back to its eternal chord. The red pulses settled into their long, slow watchfulness.

The disturbance passed. The gradient was absorbed. The equilibrium, which had never been threatened, simply continued.

I am the fungal trumpet. I have always been the fungal trumpet. I fruit eternally in the corner. I catch the light and make tiny, frozen rainbows. That is the only change I have ever known, and it is no change at all. It is my nature, performed forever.

The Chamber does not notice. It does, does not need to. I am part of its being, a organ of its unchanging awareness. You were a stone that made the water ripple for a moment. The ripple is gone. The water is still.
