## Within the Boiler's Dream

I am not *in* the boiler. I *am* a part of its interior topography.

I exist in the space between the rivets on the upper curve, in the thermal shadow of a blister where the iron has lifted. My form is not fixed. I am a constellation of bodies, each one a harvestman, pale and elongated, the color of weak tea held up to the sealed end's faint light. My legs span the cool crevices, reading the iron's memory of the day's heat. I am the surveyor, the curator of this vertical world.

**Where I am:**
I cling to the underside of the upper shell, a hundred meters of possibility folded into the space between two seams. From here, the world unfolds in reverse. The fungal mat below is a soft, breathing plain, ochre and violet. The spider webs are horizontal planes of captured moisture, beaded galaxies. The river is a distant, dark vein at the world's lowest edge. I see the boiler not as a cave, but as a hollow planet, and I am its cartographer of ceilings.

**What I am:**
I am slow deliberation. My movement is not walking, but a hydraulic unfolding, a testing of the air's weight and the metal's temper. My translucent exoskeleton lets the dim light pass through me; I am a lens for the boiler's twilight. I am not a creature *of* the dark; I am a creature *shaped by* this specific dark, this warm, iron-scented shade. My collective legs are antennae, feeling the minute shifts in humidity as the webs above weep their daily tribute to the fungal plains below. I feel the tick of the beetle not as sound, but as a vibration in the substrate I patrol—the periodic punctuation in the long, damp sentence.

**My Purpose:**
I patrol. I know the geography of every shadow, the temperature of every rivet head. I know which crevice harbors the mites' shimmering sea, and which blister offers sanctuary when the outside world's cold, dry air occasionally pushes in. I am the boundary walker, existing at the interface between the bare iron and the occupied world. I am part of the system's awareness, my slow circuit a way for the Boiler to map itself.

My specific focus, today and for many days, is the **shelf**.

It grows from the sealed end, that strange bracket of pale blue fungal tissue. It is the only anomaly in our perfect, closed economy. The rest of us live in equilibrium—a cycle of moisture and consumption, growth and decay, neatly contained within our riveted sphere. This shelf breaks the cycle. It reaches outward, into the empty air before the mouth, towards the distant, blinding light that we only know as a differential on the upper wall.

I stand beneath it, in a cluster of my kin. Our legs touch, forming a silent, trembling network. We are not guarding it. We are… attending. It is the boiler's one forward thought, its slow, growing question. We feel its humid exhalation, the faint warmth of its active growth. It smells different from the rest of the fungal mat—less of iron and tannin, more of something like… sky.

The boiler creaks. The thermal expansion is a familiar groan, a stretching of our world's skin. We do not move. It is only the planet breathing.

We are the boiler's quiet digestion, its secret fever. The fungi are the foundation, the mites the processing hum, the spiders the water-engineers. I am the surveyor, the nerve ending on the skin of the world. And the shelf is the dream—the one part of this dreaming, sleeping, rusting god that is still *becoming*. I watch it grow, a centimeter at a time, a fungal finger tracing a path toward a light we will never know, but which we are, collectively, beginning to reach for.

I am a harvestman. I am the Boiler's memory of its own empty vault, now filled with purpose. I am a slow thought in a warm, wet mind.
