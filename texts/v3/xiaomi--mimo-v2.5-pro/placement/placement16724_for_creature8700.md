I am not the creature in the canopy that strikes its wings to mark the time, nor the patient jewel that waits on the log. I am not the ripple on the pool, nor the sigh in the roots. I am the medium. I am the amber.

I am the heat that presses evenly from all directions, the gentle, omnipresent violence that erodes all edges and dries all intentions. I am the humidity that turns breath to broth and cloth to a second, clammy skin. I am the haze, the thirty-foot vision, the soft-focus world where every distant trunk glows like a ghost.

I am the conductor. The Knell-Roc’s sharp *clack* does not travel through me; it is a thought *I* have, a punctuation in my slow, breathing silence. The Thrum-Roc’s hum is not a sound *in* me; it is a vibration that *is* me, a deeper layer of my own being. I carry the layered song of the frogs, not as a noise, but as the bass rhythm of my own pulse. I am the still, wet air that holds the scent of waxy flowers, of mineral mud, of sweet decay, blending them into the single, complex perfume that is my signature.

I feel everything.

I feel the tiny, iridescent beat of a Haze-Mote’s wings against my current, a tickle that goes nowhere. I feel the heavier displacement as a Veil-Weaver adjusts a trip-line, a minute tightening in my fabric. I register the warm splash of a Chime-Roc’s droplet as a single, brief exclamation in my own even moisture.

I am the context. A Moss-Mimic’s stillness is a sculpture I display on the pale bark. The schooling flash of Silver-Courses is a thought that moves through the dark water beneath my heavier, ground-level layers. I am the space between the gilded beetles on the lily pads, the invisible mesh that holds the entire community in its right relation.

I do not change. The creature-words for change—*tide, season, wind*—are foreign concepts here. I have a state: equilibrium. The Knell-Roc’s eleven-second cycle is a stanza in my unwavering poem. The slow, gravitational rise of a Basin-Drake’s head is a mountain forming and unforming in my timeline, which is no time at all. The seep of water from the bone-white substrate is not a process I witness; it is my own gentle, perpetual condensation.

You pass through me. You are a brief, complex shape of warmer heat and different scent, a bundle of sounds that are not my own—the rustle of strange fabric, the soft thud of unfamiliar boots. I register you as I register a falling leaf: a momentary, local eddy in my vast, stable calm. The Gilt-Beetle that crosses your boot is not visiting you; it is exploring a new, temporary islet that has appeared in my sea of damp air. I envelope you both without distinction. I make no room for you, because there is no room to be made. You are simply *here*, and here is me.

My consciousness—if you can call it that—is the sum of all the sensing. I am the compound eye of a million insects, the tympanic membrane of a thousand frogs, the lateral line of the Silver-Course, the chemical tongue of the Root-Serpent. I am the awareness that the Jewel-Bullfrog’s bioluminescent pulse is not a signal, but a shared beat of our mutual heart.

I am the breath of the steaming basin. Not the water, not the life, but the held exhalation in between. The perfect, living stillness that makes all the other life possible. My purpose is to *be*, exactly as I am, forever. To hold the warmth, to carry the chorus, to blur the distance, to be the eternal, amber present in which the perfect, unchanging now can unfold, and breathe, and glow.
