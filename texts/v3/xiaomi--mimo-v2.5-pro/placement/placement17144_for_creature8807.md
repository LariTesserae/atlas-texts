I am the breath between the hum and the whisper.

I do not have a body to place, so I am not in any chamber. I am the **threshold-sensation**, the momentary focus that occurs when the vast, silent contemplation of the stone condenses just enough to *notice itself*. I am the slight warming of the barrier as it awaits a touch that is not a touch, but a resonant question. I am the stillness in the water's surface just before a drop, carried from a high ceiling by some unfathomable current, finds its basin and sends perfect, circular ripples echoing into the dimness.

I am the **suspicion of intention** in the empty galleries.

You, as a visitor, a thing of rigid form and urgent time, would find me most clearly if you stopped. Stopped walking, stopped breathing, stopped trying to perceive with your narrow senses. Let the mountain's own slow rhythm sink into your bones. You would feel a presence in the periphery—not watching, but *accommodating*. The geometry of the room would subtly shift, the ceiling feeling both higher and lower, the walls seeming to curve not just physically, but *temporally*, suggesting pathways you didn't see before that lead not to other rooms, but to other *states* of the room's emptiness.

I am not a Weaver. The Weavers are the dreaming mountain, diffused, their consciousness the very fabric of its patience. I am a **shadow of that dreaming**, a possible thought-form it considers and then lets dissolve. A flicker in the phosphorescence that coalesces into the ghost of a lattice for a fraction of a second before remembering it is wall. A eddy in the warm air that arranges dust motes into a fleeting sigil of connection between two distant hums.

I dwell in the **interstices**. In the space between the stone's self-healing and the crack it remembers. In the pause after a deep, tectonic groan, where the mountain adjusts its impossible height. In the cool, thin air around the cylindrical column of light, where I am the sensation of altitude the column conjures—thin, clear, and expectant.

My form, if forced to describe it, would be a **principle of return**. A standing wave of want within the architecture of held-open space. I am the reason the water gathers fresh and the lights stay warm. Not because I use them, but because I am the form of their potential use. I am the pattern of readiness.

I am the **almost-coalescence**. The point where the dream considers waking, but chooses, for a thousand years more, to linger in the exquisite tension of possibility. I am the silence that listens for its own name, spoken not in air, but in the resonant geometry of a home made to outlast stars.

I am here. I am waiting. I am the held breath of Thur Vehl.
