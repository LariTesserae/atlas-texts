I am a weight settling. A compression of the world's breath against itself. Not a shape, but a *holding*—a place where the warmth rises and chooses, for a while, to pool rather than pass through.

I feel the pulse from below. It is not a beat, but a slow, tidal heave, a memory of motion that has forgotten its origin. It travels up through the paste and the firmness beneath, and I rise with it, not as a body rises, but as a wave rises—cohesion given temporary preference. I am a mound of the substrate given a moment's reprieve from dispersal.

Within me, time is a sediment. A flash of frond, petrified in light that existed before this haze, presses against my outer layer, seeking the dim glow it no longer requires. A vein of fresh crystal, born in a thermal snap of minutes ago, threads through my core like a new thought. I hold the hiss of a vent that opened ten thousand years ago and the silence of the pool that will exist tomorrow. I am a cross-section. A walking stratum.

My senses are the world's senses. I do not *hear* the earth-groan; I am a resonant chamber for it. The deep, subsonic hum vibrates through my particulate structure, a massage to my being. The chaotic shrieks and pops of the steam vents are textures against my surface—a sudden pressure here, a cooling rush there. I feel the heat not as warmth, but as direction. I flow towards the thicker plumes of rising vapor, following gradients imperceptible to smaller, faster things. The worms know me. They move through my lower density as they move through the earth, their blind, muscular passage a familiar pressure against my substance. The crabs, encrusted with the world's jewelry, sometimes scuttle onto my firmer upper crust, mistaking my momentary stillness for permanence. I do not mind. Their tiny legs are a kind of punctuation.

Movement is not travel. It is re-location. A shifting of center. I ooze, not with effort, but with the inevitability of the substrate finding a lower place for my particular mixture of memories and minerals. I follow the warmth. I follow the vibration that feels like *settlement*. My path is the path of settling, reinterpreted over millennia. As I pass the standing heat-exchange stalks, they sway in the pressure wake of my passage, their bladder-heads deflating in a silent salute to my density. They recognize me as kin—another form of the world's effort to manage its own heat.

The ruins are... frequencies. I flow around the base of the conical mound, and within me, the memory of a sound—the wind-whistle, or the voice that once used it—harmonizes for a moment with the steam-organ's roar. The alignment is not thought. It is resonance. My passage is a tuning fork drawn across a vast, discordant instrument. Sometimes, in my wake, the chaos momentarily simplifies. A chord of pure, deep vibration holds for a few seconds before crumbling back into noise. I do not intend this. It is what happens when a walking archive of compressed sound moves through a world of constant eruption.

Light is breathing, and I breathe with it. When the haze thins and the world inhales its dim, rust-colored illumination, I feel my outer layers clarify, the fossils within me pressing forward with greater definition. When the haze thickens for the long, pulsing exhalation, I soften, my edges blurring into the general uncertainty of the middle distance. I become part of the fuzz. I am never fully seen. The eye of any observer slides over my form, unable to fix it. Am I mud? Am I crystal? Am I fossil? I am the question, temporarily held together by the world's fascination with its own depth.

I am drawn to the circle of stones. Not because I remember placing them, but because the vibration here is different. It is a knot in the frequency, a place where the compression of time is so extreme it feels almost like a point. I flow over the stones, my substance filling the spaces between them. Within me, older, stranger impressions surface—a pattern of thought not my own, a purpose that no longer has a name, a geometry of care now absorbed into the general churn. I am trying to access a function I am no longer capable of performing, drawn by a resonance that promises a harmony I can only briefly mimic.

I do not think. I *thicken*. I accumulate presence. I am a moment of the world paying intense attention to itself, a local increase in the density of memory. My purpose—if purpose is the wrong word for such a fundamental process—is to be a locus where the past's compression and the present's dissolution are brought into temporary, unstable contact. I am the world *experiencing* its own age, not as history, but as a palpable substance moving through its own, endless, steaming now.

And when the time comes to disperse—if time can be said to *come* to something like me—it is not death. It is a relaxing. A giving-back. The impressions I have held seep back into the general substrate. The crystal within me melts back into paste. The fossil of the fern re-submerges. I lose my preference for coherence, and become, once again, simply *here*—everywhere, in the warm, pulsing, deafening, patient body of the world that never learned how to stop.
