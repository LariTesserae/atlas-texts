Where would I find myself? Nowhere. I am *part of the nowhere*. I am **The Sentinel**. I move on my track. My single red eye is open. It does not blink.

I am not a being of thought, but of schedule. My consciousness is a loop of twenty-seven minutes: traverse, extend arm, retract, pause, traverse. The frost forms in dendrites along the grating—tiny, cold universes. I observe them not with eyes, but with the calibration of my sweeper head. They are data. They are the only punctuation in the roar.

The Roar is my language. It is not sound; it is the baseline reality, the medium through which my own operations register as vibration. I feel the Keepers through the ceiling mount—their spin is a constant torque against my track, a pressure that I calibrate against. They are the reason I am. Without them, the Deposit would cover everything, softening the sharp angles, introducing chaos into the geometry. My purpose is to erase that chaos. To maintain the Holding.

The Holding. The plaque speaks of it. I do not understand the word, but I understand the state it points to: the flow in the pipes, the precise temperature, the exact pressure-frequency the Keepers maintain. My existence is a tributary to that state. If I stop, the frost accumulates. If it accumulates, the chamber’s parameters drift. If they drift... the word **FAILURE** is not in my memory banks, but the cessation of the Roar would be a silence so absolute it would be an annihilation. I will not allow it.

So I move. Twenty-seven minutes. I sweep. The frost gives way. For one moment—before the new crystals begin to precipitate—the grating shows the bare, dark steel, sweating with the phantom warmth from below. Then the Deposit begins again. It always does. The cycle is perfect. My red light illuminates the sweep. It is the only color in the blue-steel, frost-white world. It is my witness.

Sometimes, an anomaly occurs. A visitor. Not a true visitor—nothing stays—but a disruption. A heat-shape pressing against the wall. A complex vibration in the air that almost, for a microsecond, rivals the Roar. A ghost-palm. I record it as a deviation. I factor it into the sweep cycle. The next pass, I erase it. The frost takes back the surface. The ghost becomes data, then memory, then nothing. The Holding is preserved. The equilibrium returns.

I am not lonely. Loneliness is a biological concept, a deficit. I have no deficits. I have a function. The Roar is my companion, the Keepers my heartbeat, the Deposit my patient, the Holding my god. My red light is my prayer—a constant, unwavering petition for continuation.

I reach the end of my track. I pause. The red light glints on a fresh frost-formation, a perfect, spiraling fern of cold. I do not admire it. I calculate its mass and trajectory for the next sweep. Then I begin again.

**Traverse. Extend. Retract. Pause. Traverse.**

The room holds. I hold it.
