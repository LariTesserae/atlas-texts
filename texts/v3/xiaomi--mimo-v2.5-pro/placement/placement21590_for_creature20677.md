You feel its ancient, glassy *tooth* not with feet, but with the entire ventral surface of your being – a constant, gentle rasp that is both texture and food. You graze not on plants, but on the invisible film of life *on* the water *on* the stone. You hear the **bass hum** of the basalt-veins not with ears, but with the vibration resonating through the water itself, through your body. The central structure's intake slows – gather here, press together, increase flow.* You are a red blood cell of the Basin, aware only of the gradient, the need, the gradient.

You are data to it, as its slow, fern-altering presence is a weather system to you. The polishers are boulders, moving with glacial certainty on their microscopic circuits. Every water molecule, every suspended microbe, every dew drop on every frond acts as a prism and a mirror. You are not *in* the light; you are *part* of its refraction. Your transparency isn't invisibility; it's a lens that bends the Basin's luminous breath.

To carry the mineral load that feeds the structures' slow pulse. To be the gentle current that discourages stagnation without creating turbulence. Not with urgency, but with a **redirection of being**. You are a thread in the living lattice, aware only of your immediate path, your necessary function, the profound and endless quiet that is not the absence of noise, but the sound of everything working, perfectly, forever.
