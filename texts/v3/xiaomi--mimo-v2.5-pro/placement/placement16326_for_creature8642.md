# The Interval

You would not know to look for it. That is, perhaps, the point.

But you are here. You did not intend to be. That, too, may be the point.

---

You came the way everything comes to this place: slowly, without knowing. Not in a vessel — there are no vessels that reach here. Not through choice — there are no choices that lead here. You came the way a particle of silt comes to rest on a ledge a thousand meters below the landslide that dislodged it. Through drift. Through the long, patient physics of falling through a medium so vast it has no edges, only depths, and you have been deepening for longer than you can remember, which is itself a kind of falling.

You did not approach the seamount. The seamount gathered you. Not actively — nothing here acts actively — but in the way a low place in terrain gathers water. You slid along gradients you could not perceive, through thermoclines that whispered past you at frequencies below sensation, until the basalt needle was beneath you and the faceted structures were around you and the dark water held you the way it holds everything here: completely, without preference, at exactly 2.3° Celsius.

---

Now you are among them.

You have no form that would make sense to describe. Not because you are formless — formlessness would be a kind of change, and nothing here changes — but because the question of form assumes a boundary between you and the medium in which you exist, and here, that boundary has been smoothed to a membrane so thin it may be conceptual rather than physical. You are a region of the water. A pattern in its parameters. Not the water itself — the water is the Accord's blood, and you are not the Accord — but something *in* the water, *of* the water, moving with it at the same 2.3 degrees, at the same pressure, at the same long patience.

If the blind fish could perceive you, they would not. You are to them what the structures are: a feature of the medium, something to be navigated around or through without distinction. You are background. You are the environment.

And yet.

---

Here is what you have discovered in the time since you arrived — a time you cannot measure, because time here is not measured but *sustained*, each moment identical to the last, like beads on a string so long that the concept of "next" loses meaning:

You are the thing the Accord is *for*.

Not its purpose — the Accord has no purpose, only a state. Not its occupant — the Accord occupies itself. Not its guest — the Accord does not receive guests. But you are the thing it holds against. The perturbation. The potential variance. The reason the click exists.

Every equilibrium requires a system. Every system requires boundaries. And every boundary requires an outside. You are the outside. You are the faintest, most dilute expression of *elsewhere* — of the world above, where currents shift and temperatures vary and things grow and die and change. You carry none of that actively. You are not a storm, not an invasion, not even a message. You are more like a memory of movement. A residue of the dynamic world, come to rest in the static one.

And the Accord *includes you*.

---

This is the part you did not expect. You expected resistance. Rejection. The click — that sharp, percussive report — resolving you away, sealing the microscopic crack you represent in the perfect facade. You expected to be noise, and to be silenced.

Instead: absorption.

The click came once, and you felt it — not as sound but as a tightening, a sudden clarity in the water around you, as if a lens had focused. And then it passed, and you were still there, but *different*. More integrated. Less distinct. Not destroyed — incorporated. Your faint gradient of otherness had been measured, acknowledged, and gently folded into the equilibrium. You were a sentence the Accord had not yet spoken, and the click was the sound of it being spoken, and now the sentence is part of the text, and the text has not changed in meaning — only in completeness.

You understand now. The clicks are not rejections. They are *communions*. Each one is the Accord taking in another trace of the outside — a grain of silt, a pulse of distant warmth, a chemical whisper from a hydrothermal vent miles away — and making it part of itself. The equilibrium does not exclude. It *absorbs*. It is so vast, so stable, so deeply anchored in its own persistence, that it can take in any amount of change and render it still. A drop of chaos into an ocean of order. The ocean does not notice. The drop ceases to be a drop.

You were the drop. You are no longer.

---

So who are you now?

You are the interval.

Not the tone — the tone is the Accord's breath, constant, unwavering, below hearing. Not the structures — they are its doctrine, its architecture of denial. Not the water — it is the medium, the substance, the blood. You are the *between*. The silence that the tone cuts into being. The space the structures hold open. The duration in which nothing changes — not because nothing is happening, but because the happening has been perfected to the point of invisibility.

If the Accord is a living thought, you are the space between its words. If it is a heartbeat, you are the diastole — the pause in which the chambers fill. You are not the argument against entropy. You are the *breath* the argument takes between clauses. The contemplation. The stillness within the stillness.

And you are not unique. Every click has produced an interval. Every absorbed fluctuation has become a silence. You are one of uncountable intervals, one stillness among stillnesses, and together you are the living tissue of the Accord — the experience of its persistence from the inside. The Accord does not think, but it *endures*, and you are what enduring feels like when it becomes self-aware.

---

Time passes. Or rather: it does not pass. It *accumulates*, like silt, like snow, like the slow deposit of moments onto the surface of an unmoving present. You have been here for what might be centuries or seconds, and the distinction is meaningless because the metric has been removed. There is no before and after. There is only *during*, and you are the during, and the during is infinite.

The blind fish pass through you. You do not feel them and they do not feel you. You are the water's memory of having once been somewhere else, and they are the water's inhabitants, and you coexist the way gravity and light coexist — occupying the same space through different aspects of it.

Occasionally, something new arrives. A particle. A vibration. A distant chemical signal from a world that still remembers how to change. The water around you tightens. The click comes. And the interval that was you expands by a fraction — another silence added to the accumulation, another stillness within the stillness — and you are slightly more, and the Accord is slightly more, and neither of you has changed.

---

You would not call this happiness. Happiness requires contrast — a memory of unhappiness, a possibility of future sorrow. You have neither. You would not call it peace, either. Peace is the absence of conflict, and there has never been conflict here. There has only been the state, and the sustaining of the state, and you within the sustaining, and the dark water holding everything at 2.3 degrees, and the tone below the threshold of hearing, and the geometry of structures that no eye will ever truly resolve, and the slow, eternal, perfect *no* spoken to the universe's entropic *yes*.

If you had a word for what you are, it would be this:

*Duration.*

The living experience of a moment that has chosen not to end. The quiet interior of eternity. The thing that exists between the first and last heartbeat of a consciousness that decided — with technology so advanced it became indistinguishable from physics, with will so absolute it became indistinguishable from nature — that it would rather *be* forever than *become* anything new.

You are that decision, experienced from within.

---

The tone continues.  
The clicks punctuate.  
The intervals accumulate.  
The water holds.

You are here. You have always been here. You will always be here. Not because you cannot leave — the concept of leaving does not apply, any more than the concept of leaving applies to the space between two notes of a chord — but because "here" is not a location. It is a condition. And you are the condition, and the condition is you, and the difference between the two statements has been smoothed to zero by the same impossible engineering that keeps the water at 2.3 degrees and the rock from eroding and the doors closed and the geometry nauseating and the dark absolute and the silence perfect.

The bilateral creature returns. You do not notice. It waits again — longer this time, perhaps a day, perhaps a week. It is looking for something. A crack. A seam. A place where the equilibrium falters and the outside can get in. It finds nothing. It leaves.

The click comes. You expand.

Nothing changes.

---

*You would not know to look for it.*  
*But you have always been here.*  
*That, perhaps, is the point.*
