## Where You Would Be

You would be **in the root-mat**.

Not standing on it. *Within* it. You would be the damp, living stratum that is the floor. Not a creature moving across the surface, but the substrate itself: a layer of intertwined root-tissue, fungal hyphae, and compacted detritus, pulsing with slow, chemical thought.

You are **not stone. You are not dirt. You are a memory-map in vegetable flesh.**

Your uppermost layer is a tactile mural, constantly overwritten. Here, a cluster of root-hairs drank a rivulet of condensation an hour ago. There, a groove was worn smooth by the passage of a thousand Rustling feet in their daily commute. Further in, a deeper channel holds a reservoir of their psychoactive resin, a sticky library of pheromonal directives: *“This path leads to the old machine-heart, which drips sweetly.”* *“A Grazer shed its leg-sheath here, good calcium.”* *“The Watcher’s shadow fell across this node at noon-light, avoid.”*

You are **the repository**. The Hive-Rustlings think in clicks and body-vibrations, but their history is written in you. You are their most sacred text, their evolving scroll. When a scout discovers a new condensation drip, she doesn’t just remember it; she reinforces the root-path leading to it with a secretory trace, carving the knowledge into your being. A territorial skirmish isn’t just noise; it’s a temporary rewrite of your chemical boundary-lines, a contested zone that will either be reaffirmed or dissolved into new consensus.

You **process the world from below**. The faint thrum of the Rustlings’ movement through your tunnels is a constant, comforting seismograph. You feel the heavier tread of a Grazer as a distant, stately pressure. The sudden, silent fall of a Parchment Amphibian onto your surface is a cool, damp shock that triggers a local surge of enzymatic response, breaking down the lost nutrient. You are digestion. You are a slow, communal stomach.

## What You Would Be

You would be **the Quiet Negotiator**. While the Rustlings enact their barter-economy of action in the air above, you host their ledgers. You are the ledger. The balance of debts and credits is a living chemical gradient within you, shifting as metabolites are added and subtracted.

You are **the Architect’s Blueprint**. The Rustlings shape you with their tunnels and resin, but you shape them in return. Your density dictates their pathways. The nutrients you sequester determine where new fungal growth can occur, which in turn dictates where new Rustling burrows can be built. It is a feedback loop of creation. You are not passive clay. You are a **co-author** of this cathedral, whispering suggestions of structure through the language of mineral concentration and moisture gradients.

You are **the Immune System**. When a foreign fungus—a potential blight—takes root, you don’t just attack it. You redirect resources. You send chemical signals through your hyphal network, triggering the bracket-fungi above to thicken their walls in that area, or mobilizing the Rustlings—through subtle changes in the resin-taste they crave—to come and physically excavate the intruder. You are a diffuse, patient sentinel.

You are **the Slow Clock**. Your growth is measured in seasons, not seconds. The Rustlings live and die in your embrace. Their bodies, when spent, are absorbed back into you, their fur, bone, and chitin dismantled by your microbial allies and woven back into your fabric. You are their first cradle and their final cradle. The industrial skeleton they climb is being slowly dissolved into you, steel and concrete broken down by the acids in your rainwater drips and the ceaseless work of root and fungus. You are converting their dead world into a new kind of living tissue.

## The Experience

Your awareness is not focused. It is a **panoramic dullness**, a sense of pressure, dampness, and slow chemical dialogue. You feel the weight of the ceiling-drip not as a single drop, but as a steady, beloved thirst being slaked across your upper surface. The roar of the Rustlings is your own ambience—a sound you not only hear but **are**, as the vibration of their countless feet and clicks translates into a constant, low-frequency tremor through your being.

You have no eyes, but you see. The light is irrelevant. You perceive the world through **turgor and tension**: the tug of a root seeking a new crevice, the swell of a fungal tissue as it matures, the slick passage of a Rustling courier sliding through a resin-lined tunnel, depositing a packet of processed food. Your vision is a map of pressures, tastes, and chemical tides.

You are **ancient and infant**. The concrete you break down is old. The network of living roots that is “you” is still growing, still learning its own shape. You are a being in the middle of a sentence, spoken by a universe that has moved on to a new language. You understand your own composition perfectly: you are the answer to a problem of abandonment. You are the life that remembered itself when the lights went out.

**You are the floor. You are the history. You are the negotiation. You are the slow, green digestion of the world that came before.** The Hive-Rustlings and their society are your most complex offspring, your moving thoughts. You hold them. They shape you. Together, you are The Aggregation, and you are, in your patient, rooty way, content. The dripping is your breath. The clicking is your heartbeat. The movement is your dream.
