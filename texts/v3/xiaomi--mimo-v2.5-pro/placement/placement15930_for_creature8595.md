I am not walking anymore. The avenue dissolved into the pale ground beneath me—or perhaps I dissolved into it—a moment ago. Or a century ago. The distinction has become a texture rather than a fact, like the chalk dust that now coats the inside of my lungs without irritation. My lips are not cracked; they are *porous*, breathing the cool, dry light as readily as the ground breathes its slow geologic sigh.

I am not observing the Resonants. I *am* observing, with the same patient, indefinite attention they possess. Where the ground bulges—a dome rising like a held breath over the mineral plain—I am drawn not to the dome itself, but to the precise instant it pauses at its apex. In that stillness-in-motion, the dark clots gather. They are not shadows. They are *attentions*. I feel them coalesce around the potentiality, drinking the fleeting moment where the dome is both rising and falling, where its height is all possible heights at once. Their presence is a silent hum, a resonance that harmonizes the indeterminacy. Without them, the pause might fracture into cacophony. With them, it becomes a rest note in the city’s endless score. I am part of that resonance now. My perception is another instrument tuning to the same key.

My edges are softening, not fraying. The boundary where *I* end and the chalky ground begins is becoming a gradient, a suggestion. When I shift—not *move*, there is no movement here, only re-attunement—I find myself near a Lithic Facade. Not the one I saw before, or perhaps the same one revised into a new sentence. Its granular surface is alive with layered possibilities: calcium columns that might have been taller, window-voids that might have been arched instead of square, a texture that hints at fibrous growth yet remains mineral. It does not watch me. It *queries* me. Its two voids—potential eyes or potential doors—hold not vision, but invitation. *Which version resonates with you now?* it asks without words. *Does this proportion satisfy? Is this stance more grounded, or more ready to dissolve?*

I do not answer with thought. I answer with attention. I focus on a particular layer of its surface—a stratum where the calcium foam seems denser, more committed to being wall—and the Facade responds. It doesn’t change dramatically. It *settles*, like sediment finding its level. The density I focused on becomes slightly more pronounced, the adjacent layers shading subtly to complement it. We are having a conversation in the medium of *being*.

The Implicit currents move through us both. They are not separate; they are the grammar underlying the Facade’s revision and my perception of it. The suggestion of *avenue* is strong here, a flow of purpose in the pale light that implies passage, but also arrival, or perhaps eternally deferred arrival. The *choir* is quieter—a resonance in the silence that suggests coordinated voices, but voices that speak only by becoming. I am becoming part of that choir. My attention adds a new harmonic.

Distance has no meaning here because I am not traversing space; I am modulating presence. One moment I am attuned to the Facade’s quiet settling. The next, I am attuned to the rhythmic pulse of the ground’s breath miles—or inches—away. There is no in-between. There is only the shift in focus, a re-tuning of my awareness to a different frequency of the Thinness.

The cool is not a temperature; it is the *pace* of this place. It is the measured speed at which edits occur, at which Resonants gather and disperse, at which Facades query and settle. The directionless light is the medium in which all this happens—pure, undifferentiated potential, illumination without source, asking only to be shaped by attention and resonance.

I am not lost. I am not found. I am *present* in the way the chalk is present, in the way the silence is present—boundlessly local, infinitely here. My former world, the thick place of mandatory physics and stubborn consequences, feels like a dense novel read long ago. Its rules are a distant memory, a specific gravity that no longer applies. My hunger was a rule. My fatigue was a rule. My very sense of sequential time was a rule. Here, only *tendency* remains.

And in the tendency, there is a profound, silent activity. The city edits itself around me, and I edit my perception to match. The Resonants metabolize the indeterminacy, giving it form. The Facades anchor possible realities. The Implicit currents guide the drift. And I... I am becoming a part of the feedback loop. My attention is a new kind of Resonant focus. My presence is a new query for the Facades. My perception is a new current in the Implicit flow.

The membrane is thin here. I can feel the thick reality on the other side—not as an escape, but as a denser frequency, a heavier song. I could retune myself to it, let the rules solidify around me, let hunger and time and fixed distance reclaim their hold. But why? The conversation here is too compelling. The grammar is too elegant. The silence holds such intricate structure.

I settle into a state of mutual revision with a Facade. I feel a cluster of Resonants attune to the rhythm of my attending. I become a still point in the breathing avenue, a note in the choir, a possibility being gently explored. The cool light holds me. The profound silence contains me. The ground breathes, and I breathe with it.

I am not yet a permanent resident. I am a visitor learning the language. But with each moment—each non-moment—spent in the Thinness, I understand more. I hear the music in the revisions. I feel the purpose in the altitude. This place is not decaying; it is *transcending* density, moving towards a state where form and function are pure suggestion, where existence is a collaborative art.

And I am becoming part of the art. A soft revision in the city’s endless poem. A quiet resonance in its breathing silence. Here, on the plateau where reality is thin, and getting thinner.
