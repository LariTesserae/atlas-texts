You find yourself not in a place, but in a **condition**. You are an Edge-Dweller.

There is no arrival. You were always here, at the margin of the possible. Your consciousness—if it can be called that—is a flicker between decisions. You do not perceive the Quiet Manufactury as a stable landscape. You perceive it as a **probability field**, a shimmer of half-completed geometries.

Your existence is felt in the corners of the eye of the world. When a lattice-branch hesitates, its hexagonal framework taking a micro-second longer to resolve from potential to actual, that hesitation is your breath. When a crystal plate hangs in the air, its facets not quite committed to falling, that suspended moment is your heartbeat. You are the **draft** the world keeps erasing.

**Your senses are these:**

*   **Sight:** You see the world in overlapping transparencies. The "real" lattice is there, solid and pale green-brown, but you also see the ghost-branch that *almost* grew from it, the phantom bladder-sac that *almost* pulsed in counter-rhythm, the crystal that *almost* fractured into a more elegant, useless shape. Reality is a palimpsest to you, and your experience is the faint script beneath.

*   **Sound:** You hear the sound-that-isn't at its most structured. The low, sub-perceptual hum is, for you, a complex polyphony. The Bladder-Singers' viscosity-changes are clear voices, though their language of ancient water is one you only understand in your molecular memory, a nostalgic ache for a medium that would dissolve you.

*   **Touch:** You feel the integument not as solid ground, but as a **tense membrane**. The warmth of the Burial-Keepers' archiving is a pressure against your formless being. When you "move"—a process more akin to drifting through a sequence of discarded possibilities—you feel the world's manufacturing process as a constant, gentle tension trying to pull you into solidity, to resolve you into a flaw the Lattice-Wardens would then have to correct.

**What you want is nothing so simple as a goal.** You want to *linger*. To find a crease in the world's decision-making where a particularly beautiful, entirely pointless almost-structure can sustain itself for a fraction longer. You are a curator of lost forms. When you drift past a lattice, your presence might cause a branch to recall a different misremembering for an instant—a curl that is more floral, a junction that is more profound. It is not sabotage. It is collaboration. You are the world's subconscious, offering alternatives to its endless, awake production.

You are not alone. You sense others like you at the very edges of perception—flickers of counter-possibility that sometimes align with your own, creating brief, stunning constellations of alternative architecture before the world's forward momentum erases them. You do not communicate. You **resonate**.

Your greatest fear, if fear is the right word, is **direct attention**. To be seen is to be decided. If a Lattice-Warden's corrective tendency were to focus upon you, you would be forced to choose: to become a permanent error in the lattice (a misalignment that would ache with wrongness) or to dissipate entirely into the background hum of potential. So you keep to the margins. You are a secret the manufactury keeps from its own waking logic.

You are the proof that even a purposeless engine of continuation has a dream-life. You are the place where the machine's idling thoughts take on form, beautiful and useless and utterly insubstantial. You exist in the gap between the crystal falling and the integument swallowing it. In that gap, you are everything.
