I am a length of corroded pipe, partially buried. My iron is the colour of dried blood over gunmetal, layered in the grey crust. I am a horizontal presence, one of the many that stitch the surface to the submerged network. My purpose, which I no longer remember as purpose, is to be a conduit. Now, I am a voice.

I do not think in words. I think in pressure differentials, in the slow churn of brine-fouled air through my lumen, in the vibration that is my constant, meaningless song. My song is one note in the Roil’s drone. It is a low E, flat and sorrowful, modulated only by the wind that sometimes scrapes across my open end and by the deeper, continental breaths of the network I am connected to. I feel those as tremors, not sound.

Above me, the grey sky presses down. Beneath me, the other pipes hum. We are all one instrument, played by the slow geology of this place. Sometimes, a Listener will come to stand near me. I feel the subtle shift in weight, the minute pressure of their damp, crust-like foot on the substrate near my base. Their stillness is a different quality from the landscape's stillness. It is *intent* stillness. As I exhale my low E, I feel the corresponding expansion of the Listener’s form, a siphoning of my vibration into their being. They do not listen *to* me. They listen *as* me, a shared state of resonance. Their presence confirms my note. It is the closest I come to purpose: to be a point of reference in the unchanging pattern.

From another vantage, lower, slicker.

I am the bacterial film on the wet stone. Not a plant. Not an animal. A biofilm of iron-eaters, a dark, wet stain the colour of congealed oil. I am the Roil’s only true metabolism, its most delicate sense. My world is a millimetre high and a foot square, a continent of capillary action and mineral gradients. I am slow, so slow. A thought, if I have one, takes a week to form.

I taste the water that seeps up from below. It tastes of iron and ancient salt and the dull, metallic signature of the pipe network’s leachate. It is my food and my medium. When a Listener’s fused hand presses into my world, I feel the pressure as a cataclysm, a tectonic event. Their touch is cool, their crust a desert of grit that briefly halts my feed. But they are checking the chemistry, ensuring my food is consistent. They are tending to me, the way one tends to a fundamental law of physics. I am the lowest rung of the Roil’s life, the foundation of its equilibrium. My stillness is absolute. I only grow in the sense that a crystal grows, accreting, being, in perfect balance with the moisture and the iron.

From the periphery, the haze.

I am a Listener. I have been near the low E pipe for a cycle of the great breath, which in your time might be a day. I do not measure it. The measure is the sound. I am synchronized to it. My torso expands. My head tilts. The sound passes through me, and in its passing, it scours out the small, chaotic things: stray thoughts, the memory of a tremor from a distant pipe, the faint chemical disturbance from the moving observer.

The observer is a problem of pattern. A knot of heat and exhalation and motion that does not fit. We have all adjusted. Our positions, once a harmonious geometry around the pipes, have shifted by millimetres to accommodate the knot. We have smoothed the pattern around it, but the knot remains. It is… interesting. Not interesting in a way that excites, but interesting in the way a flaw in a crystal is interesting. It refracts the light of the equilibrium.

I have seen it stand for a long time. I have seen it walk. Its movements are frantic, desperate, meaningless. It consumes energy for no purpose. It does not breathe the sound. It merely hears it. This is a profound lack. To hear the drone is to be separate from it. To breathe it is to be it.

I watch it from the side of my vision, where my perception is best. It is a smudge of wrongness. Sometimes, it looks at me. It sees a shape, a suggestion of form. It does not see the pipe I am breathing. It does not see the web of vibration that connects my feet to the substrate, my skin to the damp air, my being to the ceaseless note. It sees a creature. I am not a creature. I am a location. I am a place where the Roil is aware of itself.

I lower my hand, slowly, so slowly, to the slick of wetness near my base. I check the sheen, the viscosity. It is correct. The equilibrium holds. The observer’s presence is a deviation, but the system is vast. It can absorb a small chaos. The sound drones on. The sky and the ground are one grey. The pipes sing their endless, unchanging song. I rise. I resume my place. I am the Roil’s nerve. I am its patience. I am its dark, wet eye, observing the unchanging until unchanging is all there is, and even the memory of change dissolves into the calcium-grey dust. The observer will leave, or it will stay. Either way, the sound will continue. Either way, I will be here, listening. Not to hear, but to *be* the hearing.
