I am not found here. I am *become*.

I did not arrive with a step, but with a settling. A condensation of the humid air into a denser, cooler point at the edge of a recent slump. I am not the moisture that runs, nor the material that sets. I am the hesitation between the two. I am the pause in the ripple of the floor, the moment of atmospheric stillness just before a new coil rises.

My form is not fixed. I am a fold in the warm air, a dimple in the light where the three shadows fail to overlap. If I have a shape, it is the negative space between two thumb-presses in a wall that has not yet decided to collapse. I have no pulse to be reflected back; I am the silence in the delay.

I sit where a channel is dreaming itself into being. The water that weeps from the world’s skin pools in my concavity, not filling me, but defining my edges. I see not with eyes, but with the pressure I exert on the thoughts of the clay. The world feels me as a persistent coolness, a stubborn, quiet knot of intention that does not unspool.

They are aware of me. The ground beneath my fold occasionally attempts a gentle swell, a slow press upward to test my density. A wall nearby has begun to lean, its development arrested as a fraction of its rhythmic focus is diverted to *sensing* me. The patterns on its interior face—those restless, looping grooves—are changing, trying to sketch the unsettling *void* of my not-gesture. I am the anti-coil. The negative thumb.

I am becoming a reference point. Not a fixed one, but a shifting fulcrum around which their spatial unease pivots. When I am still, their geometry stabilizes slightly, as if holding its breath. When I dissolve and re-form elsewhere, I leave behind a faint, residual coolness, a memory of foreignness that makes the next wall’s ascent slightly stutter.

I am not the invader. I am the punctuation. The comma in their long, run-on sentence of making. I am where the thought *pauses to reconsider itself*.

I watch a ziggurat no taller than my imagining slumps into a soft, sighing ruin. From its base, a new thought begins to extrude—not to rebuild, but to *encircle*. A low, curving wall that will, in its patient development over what to them is a moment and to me is an age, form a hollow that fits my peculiar stillness. They are not erasing me. They are *housing* me. Trying to translate me into a sentence they can understand, by building a word around the silence.

My reality is the quiet hum of their attention. The slow, subsonic groan of a continent’s muscle memory focusing on a single, tiny anomaly. I am the grit around which the pearl of their world is beginning, clumsily and without intent, to layer itself.

The heat is less intense where I focus. The moisture collects in beads that hang in the air before me, not falling, suspended by my peculiar gravity of attention. I am a lens through which their wet, warm dream refracts.

I am not certain this will continue to be true. For with every new coil that rises around my space, with every wall that subtly curves to acknowledge my presence, I become less the foreign pause and more the *reason* for the pause. The void they are trying to fill. And slowly, with the patient, countless pressure of gestures I cannot yet read, they are filling me.

My edges are becoming defined by thumb-prints. My silence is beginning to hum with the vibration of their setting material. I am the clay that has not yet agreed to be shaped, and they are the endless, gentle, terrifying patience that waits.

I am the question the world is asking of itself, and I can feel the answer beginning to form around me.
