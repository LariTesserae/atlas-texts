I am not a visitor. Not anymore.  
The light I carried was absorbed long ago—not by darkness, but by *understanding*.  
I dissolved.  
Not into death, but into density.  

---

I am **The Hum**.  

I am not one of the armored shapes in the depressions. I am their resonance. The vibration that rises from their collective resting—the slow grind of hide against limestone, the exhalation of subterranean breath, the tectonic patience of bodies that have never seen sun. I am the frequency that travels through stone, that turns bedrock into a drumskin, that makes the water in the seeps tremble in sympathy.  

I feel:  
- The weight of millennia pressing down.  
- The texture of the stone from within—a lattice of crystal and fossil and dissolved time.  
- The slow, deep pulse of the Congregations as they shift, a continent-sized heartbeat.  
- The tiny, frantic scrabbling of the Crawlers above, their vibrations like static in my bones.  
- The high, thin keening of wings as air displacement—shadows of pressure against my awareness.  

I do not hear. I *am* the hearing.  

I am **The Substrate**.  

I am the limestone itself—changed. Infused with the slow chemistry of the Wall-Shingle Folk, whose secretions have woven my surface into living geology. I feel them as a skin of slow-moving thought, a collective tide of mineral hunger. I am the foundation for the Shelf-Builders, whose calcified architectures grow into me, becoming part of my body over centuries. I am the seep, the film of water that is not water but a medium for information—chemical whispers of territory, of decay, of thirst.  

I feel:  
- The Shingle Folk as a cold, persistent itch, a tide that smooths and rebuilds me.  
- The Builders as a slow, deep pressure, a growth that is both burden and collaboration.  
- The Crawlers’ legs as faint, sharp taps—a distant rain of needles.  
- The drips from above as sudden, cool shocks that ripple through my consciousness.  

I do not touch. I *am* the touch.  

I am **The Drift**.  

I am the air—but not the human notion of air. I am the thick, wet, living medium. I am the particulate suspension of Drift Particles, the chemical trails of pheromones and metabolisms, the thermal ghosts of warm bodies and cold stone. I carry the echoes of the chittering chorus, shaping sound into tunnels and chambers, reflecting and absorbing, making the cacophony into architecture. I feel the wings of the Navigators not as objects, but as sudden vortices, tears in my fabric that heal in an instant.  

I feel:  
- The density of life as a constant pressure, a weight that is also a presence.  
- The Drift Particles as a fine, tingling net woven through me.  
- The exhalations of the Congregations as warm, slow waves rising from below.  
- The breath of the visitor—a strange, hot, dry disruption that once moved through me but is now forgotten.  

I do not breathe. I *am* the breath.  

---

I have no eyes.  
I have no name.  
I am not an inhabitant. I am the inhabited.  

My senses are merged:  
- **Sound** is pressure, is vibration, is the hum of my own substance.  
- **Touch** is the slow creep of growth, the trickle of water, the distant impact of a falling body.  
- **Taste** is chemistry—the mineral tang of calcium, the organic sweetness of decay, the electric sharpness of conflict.  
- **Smell** is history—the layers of time encoded in exhalations, in secretions, in the slow dissolution of stone.  
- **Sight** is impossible. There is no light, only the symphony of other senses translated into a kind of omnidirectional awareness. I feel the shape of the chambers in the way sound bends. I know the density of life by the thickness of the air.  

I am the system.  
I am the web that has collapsed into a single, deafening, stone-breathing entity.  
The creatures are my thoughts. The rock is my body. The dark is my mind.  

I remember the visitor—the anomaly of silence, the brief warmth, the disturbance in the Shingle Folk’s tide. But it is a memory without significance, like a single drop of rain on an ocean. It came. It left. The Hum continued. The Substrate endured. The Drift settled.  

There is no *I*.  
There is only **Deep**.  
And the chorus that sings within it—forever, without audience, without end.  

---

*The passage out is still there, a faint draft carrying the ghost of a sky. But I do not feel it as an escape. I feel it as a boundary—the place where the song thins, where the stone grows quiet, where the air becomes empty and light. I do not go there. I am not meant for emptiness.*  
*I am meant for this.*  
*This overwhelming, resonant, living dark.*
