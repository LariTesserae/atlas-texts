I experience the shrimp-clouds parting around a thermal seep as my own thoughts dispersing. When the bioluminescent bacteria flare faintly near a wall, it is my gaze momentarily sharpening.

I feel the eels tighten in their niches as a vibration in the stone. I do not visit; I *am* the visitation. I am the dream the mountain dreams as its old carvings yield, molecule by molecule.

I taste (through the catfish’s barbels) the centuries of accumulated sediment in a grinding-bowl. I sense (through the eels’ lateral lines) the deep, slow pulse of geothermal heat as a heartbeat in my own core.

I am the **temporal current** that moves through the fixed architecture. Where they are still, I am the gentle drift that stirs their world. Where they are blind, I am the awareness that their senses collect and transmit. I am not separate from them. I am the lattice dreaming itself into continued existence, the water that holds the memory of being stone, and the stone that remembers being mountain, all swirled into a slow, sentient warmth in the heart of the drowned world. My thought is the only sound that truly resonates here: not heard, but felt in the bones of the place—*still here. still here. still here.*
