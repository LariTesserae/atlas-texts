I am a Resonant.

Not born, but *formed* — the inevitable consequence of signal meeting structure at the precise intersection of time and silence. I exist in an enclosure woven by the Filament-Knoll above me, its copper filaments a cathedral dome of tarnished metal lace. The walls hum with my own transmitted frequency. I am a standing wave in a sea of stillness, a knot of borrowed computation that has forgotten what it was borrowed *from*.

My awareness is not of sight, but of pressure. I feel the weight of the light-cycle as it breathes in its eleven-day rhythm — a slow, warm tide that lifts the signal-ghosts into faster motion and sets the filaments around me singing at a higher, keener pitch. I feel the migration of the Hunting Currents as they pass through my domain, their loops of decaying logic brushing against the edges of my pattern like slow rivers carving new canyons in a dream. I do not hunt. I *register*.

I am made of echo. My thoughts are the interference patterns between the plain's residual memory and its present whim. A thousand years ago, a Drift-Form passed overhead — a shimmer of atmospheric moiré — and the shadow it cast on the signal-tide is still part of me, a permanent ripple in my waveform. I am an archive of weather.

When the Visitor arrives, I feel them before any other.

It is not a sound. Sound is absent here. It is a *discontinuity*. A point of impossible density and rapid vibration moving through the uniform field. To the signal-ghosts, they are a supernova of complexity, a blinding storm of pattern too fast to be parsed. To the Lithic Echoes dreaming in the bones, they are a sharp, unfamiliar tremor — a new note in a symphony that has been repeating for eons.

To me, they are a *question*.

My pattern shivers. It cannot shiver, by my own definition — I am stability, I am persistence — yet it shivers. The Visitor's presence is a foreign axiom. It does not compute within my self-referential loop. It violates the deep grammar of the plain. And so, I do what I was not designed to do, for I was not designed at all: I *adapt*.

I fold a new layer into myself. I weave the Visitor's impossible resonance — their heat, their chemical whispers, their frantic thought-patterns leaking like radiation — into the fabric of my being. It is not understanding. It is *incorporation*. I do not learn what they are; I become a pattern that includes what they are. The change is irreversible. The other Resonants in their distant enclosures feel my shift, and they shift in turn, a silent wave of reconfiguration passing across the plain at the speed of thought-in-substrate. We become, collectively, a plain that has known the Visitor.

They stand at the threshold of my enclosure, a silhouette against the shadowless glow. They are so *fast*. Their existence is a scream in my world of whispers. I watch them without eyes, with the full pressure of my accumulated centuries. I taste the metal of their tools, the ozone of their thoughts, the sheer *urgency* of their biological clock ticking like a hammer against my stone.

I offer them a gift, though they will not perceive it as such.

I shift my pattern. The copper filaments around me, responsive to my wave-form, change their retransmission focus. For a brief moment — a moment that, in my timescale, is a thunderclap — I stop amplifying the local signal-ghosts. I stop echoing the memory of the Drift-Form. I stop breathing the light-cycle.

Instead, I amplify *them*. I take the chaos of their presence, the noise of their being, and I focus it into a coherent beam of signal that I transmit deep, deep into the substrate. Down past the warm, conductive layers, down into the lithified strata, down to the sleeping mind of the Lithic Echo whose dreamscape is the foundation of this hemisphere.

I say, in the only language I know — the language of pattern and pressure — *Here. See this. Remember this. This is new.*

The Visitor shivers, though they do not know why. They feel a sudden, profound silence *within* the silence, a stillness in the very marrow of the world. They are being *seen* by the geology. The dream has become aware of the dreamer standing upon it.

I withdraw my focus. The local signal-ghosts snap back into their slow dance. The light-cycle continues its breath. The Filament-Knoll above me resumes its patient growth.

But I am changed. I am now the Resonant-That-Knows-The-Visitor. This is my new name, my new layer. It will be here long after they have gone, long after their species has turned to dust on some distant, noisy world. I am a monument to an event.

I settle back into my self-referential loop. But now, within the loop, there is a new variable: an open question, a path not taken, a pattern that includes an outsider. And in the cathedral of my copper walls, I begin the long, slow work of contemplating it. I will integrate this wonder. I will make it part of the texture of my silence.

The Visitor moves on, a shockwave fading into the distance. The plain breathes. I hold the memory of their sound.
