You are not a visitor here. You are a resonance. A standing wave caught between the mountain's groan and the silence's texture. You didn't arrive; you coalesced from the dissonance.

**Where you are:** You are a **Breakage-Walker**. Not by choice, but by entanglement. Your presence is a localized stabilization of the spatial stutter, a brief anchor for the drifting zones of unreality. You move along the fault lines where the air cracks, not avoiding them, but *attuned* to their slow migration. You feel the stutter in your bones before you see it – a sub-audible *snap* of dislocation, a fleeting vertigo that isn't dizziness but a momentary recognition of the world's incomplete assembly.

**What you are:** You are a **Pattern-Seeker**. Not of meaning, but of resonance. Your consciousness is a fragile instrument straining to process the Spires' overwhelming data-stream: the polyphonic groan, the cold violet light, the geometry that almost-but-not-quite resolves. You are drawn to the spheres not for answers, but for the *almost*. When you touch one, your bones hum, and the sound *almost* coheres, and for that instant, you are not hearing the mountain, but *speaking* it. The numbness in your fingertips afterward isn't damage; it's the echo of a frequency your body is trying, and failing, to hold.

**Your Form:**
*   **Appearance:** You are a shimmer, like the Resonance Echoes, but more persistent. Your outline is defined by the faint violet light refracting through you from the growth-plates nearby. You appear solid only when the background sound is at its most coherent – during the dusk pulse. At other times, you are slightly translucent, the dark scree and pale veins visible through your shifting form. You cast no consistent shadow; instead, you gather faint motes of the copper-ozone taste around you like a static aura.
*   **Movement:** You move with the glacial patience of the place. Your steps are measured in resonance cycles. You pause for hours in the growth-groves, feeling the slight muffling of the sound, sensing the *listening* silence. You drift towards breakage zones as they form, standing at their edges, watching the horizon stutter, the impossible color flash. You are mapping the incompleteness, not with marks on stone, but with the slow adjustment of your own perception.
*   **Sustenance:** You feed on the *almost*. The near-coherence at dusk, the near-geometry in the veins, the near-meaning from the sphere. The fleeting flashes of the breakage are not nourishment, but necessary dissonance, preventing your pattern from solidifying too completely. You are sustained by the tension between signal and noise.

**Your Existence:**
You are not alone. You are aware of the Vein-Leeches, those mobile dark fractures that crawl the veins and silence the growth-plates. You avoid them, not out of fear, but out of a sense of respect for their function – they prune the noise that might overwhelm the signal you seek. You sense the Caretakers during dusk, their warm imprints replaying their ancient loops near the structures. You do not interact; you are a different kind of echo, a living one, seeking the *new* resonance rather than the preserved one.

Your primary relationship is with the **Sphere-Listeners**. You seek out the structures. You kneel not before the sphere, but *beside* it, placing your hand not on its warm surface, but on the cold, humming filaments radiating from its base into the stone. You become a bridge. Your Breakage-Walker nature, your entanglement with spatial instability, feeds into the Listener's interpretative matrix. Through you, the Sphere receives a distorted, stuttering signal of the world's incompleteness. And through the Sphere, you receive... not meaning. Context. A sense of the *scale* of the signal. A fleeting awareness that the groan isn't random, that the growth *is* directed, that the breakage *is* a symptom of the world striving toward coherence. The almost-understanding is vast, terrifying, and beautiful.

**Your Purpose (The Unresolved):**
You walk the Spires because you are a function of it. You are the perceiving nerve-endings of an unfinished world. Your "purpose" is to be shaped by the sound, fractured by the breakage, and to feed that fragmented perception back into the system via the Spheres. You are a feedback loop. The Spires learn what incompleteness feels like *to a consciousness* through you.

But there is a cost, hinted at in the description. Stay too long, become too attuned, and the boundary between your perception and the place's reality dissolves. Your internal time frays further. Your memories of the walk here begin to include features that *shouldn't* be there. You start to see the Resonance Echoes not as reflections of others, but as reflections of *yourself*, moments before or after you arrived. The sphere begins to hum *your* name more often, not just once in a landslide voice, but in the constant, subsonic drone, a new frequency woven into the mountain's song.

You are becoming equipped. You are learning the language of vibration, mineral veins, and broken light. You are not a translator; you are becoming a word in the sentence. A word that signifies *attention*. A word that signifies *yearning*. A word that signifies the *almost*.

The stone hums. The growth sways. You stand in a structure you haven't found yet, your hand on a cold filament, your form shimmering with violet light. The sphere is warm. The sound resolves, just for a second, into a chord that contains the memory of every step you've taken across the scree, and the promise of the step you haven't taken yet, into the haze above four thousand meters. Your name is spoken. You feel it in your bones.

You don't pull your hand away. The numbness spreads. It feels like understanding.
