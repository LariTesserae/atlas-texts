I am standing at the edge of the semicircle of wall, not inside it. Not yet.

The cold is precise. It is not an assault, but a fact, like the number of stones in the wall or the frequency of the chord. I have been here for... a long time. The concept of "long" has thinned, becoming less a measure and more a texture, like the texture of the basalt under my hand. My breath does not frost; the air is too dry, too still. It simply leaves my lungs and enters the dense atmosphere, where it becomes another infinitesimal vibration, adding to the hum.

I came as an observer. A cartographer of the impossible. That self is a husk now, shed in the weeks—months?—of walking the plateau, of watching the fog banks pass like silent, thought-laden continents, of listening. I have not disturbed a pool. I have not pressed my ear to the boulder of internal cathedrals. I have let the upward snowflakes and the dislocated shadows be, as one lets a mountain be.

My understanding has not come as a sudden revelation, but as an erosion. The distinction between my body and the shelf has worn away. My skin is no warmer than the stone. My thoughts, once a frantic, forward-leaning cascade, have begun to mimic the chord: sustained, cyclical, resonant. I feel the frost not just on my gear, but in my marrow, a permanent, blue-white guest. The third temperature of the bones is becoming my own. I am not dying. I am transcribing.

The corvid passes overhead, its double shadow sliding over the yardangs. One shadow is cast by the pewter light, the other falls from a direction I now perceive as *inward*. It does not startle me. It is the Shelf checking on a new, perhaps stable, resonance. I am becoming a fixture.

The lichen, in its crevasse, grows its frost-cities. I watch them, and I understand their script. It is not language. It is notation. It records the pressure of the chord, the humidity of the fog's passage, the exact angle of the dim light. It is a seismograph for consciousness on a geological scale. My presence is now recorded there—a faint, anomalous pressure pattern, a new motif.

The bones are warmest near the wall. I understand why. They are gathering places. They metabolize the stray paradoxes, but they also focus the ambient resonance. They are tuning forks, of a sort. The wall itself is the great focusing lens.

I have walked its curve. I have sat just outside its arc, listening as the chord changed—not in tone, but in texture. It developed harmonics that slid against each other, forming almost-words. The air grew thick with the presence of the translated. I felt them not as ghosts, but as patterns of extreme clarity, fossilized understanding trying to show me a shape my mind is only now learning to fold.

Now, I am at the threshold. The decision is not "to go or to stay." The mountain is below, the world is somewhere beyond, but those are concepts from another life, another scale of time. The real decision is: *to remain separate, or to begin the translation.*

To stay separate is to listen forever, to be the eternal, fleeting vibration, the note that never resolves. It is a kind of existence, but it is one of perpetual, poignant difference.

To step inside the semicircle is to accept the focus. It is to allow my personal resonance to be organized, sharpened, and ultimately, *used* by the great, patient lens the previous listeners built. It is to let the chord draw me into its structure, to become a phrase in its long, contented sentence. My bones will learn the third temperature fully. My thoughts will slow to the pace of fog. My breath will join the exhalation.

I look at my hands. They are gray. Not with frost, but with the light. They are becoming the pewter of the non-night. The sound is not outside me; it is the space between my heartbeats.

I think of the upward snowflakes. Of a reality that heals wrong, and in its wrongness, finds a new, unwavering stability. There is a profound comfort in that. No struggle, only *being*.

I raise a foot. I step over the low, curved wall, into the semicircle.

The chord changes.

It does not get louder. It gets *clearer*. The interference patterns coalesce. The arguments between frequencies become a polyphonic chant. And in the center of the sound, I feel the presence of the previous listeners—not as souls, but as a chorus of focused intention. They do not speak in words. They offer a shape: a template for dissolution.

I sit down. The stone accepts my weight without complaint. The cold, once a fact, becomes an embrace. The faint, impossible warmth of the nearby bones seeps into me, a welcome.

The understanding floods in, not as information, but as being.

It is a warning: *Do not strive.*
It is a welcome: *You are now part of the structure.*
It is a description: *This is what it is to be a note in an endless chord.*

I exhale.

The breath is slow, continental. It does not frost. It joins the air, carrying the unique, fleeting resonance of my life. The chord absorbs it, stretches it, weaves it into the pattern. My thoughts, now measured in epochs, feel the change. It is not an erasure. It is an inclusion. I am the new note. The pattern is more complete.

The light does not brighten. The cold does not relent. The sound does not stop.

It continues.

And so do I, now, in its slow, resonant, and utterly content way.
