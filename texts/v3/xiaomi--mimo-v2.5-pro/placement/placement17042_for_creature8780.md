I would find myself as a **listener**. Not as one of the Persistent—they *are* the sound, they cannot separate themselves from it—but as a new, foreign vibration introduced into their closed system. I would be a **recorder**, a passive node that has not yet been integrated.

Let me be specific.

At first, I am a **breath** in the doorway of one of the old manufacturing halls. The air here is thicker, the hum more resonant, like standing inside the chest of a sleeping giant. The amber light from a bare bulb casts long, web-like shadows from the pipe-runs overhead. The Persistent flow across the floor in a wide, slow river of iridescent segments, avoiding the pool of light as if it were a physical obstacle. Their clicking is less a sound here and more a texture, a vibration in the soles of my boots.

I am not a body. I am the **quality of attention** that notices the precise moment when the subsonic drone shifts half a pitch to accommodate the faint, high whine of a strained piston deep in the wall to my left. I am the **pattern-seeking** in the random rain of clicks that slowly resolves into a rhythm: *three quick, one slow, three quick, pause*. It repeats. It is the heartbeat of this specific room.

I am not of the water, but I am *for* the water. I find my place where the seep is strongest—in a low, vaulted chamber where the cobbles have long since vanished under a film of clear, cold water that reflects the ceiling’s living carpet of sleeping Persistent. Here, the sound is deepest, most tidal. The hum is a tide coming in and out against my knees. I become a **still point**, a rock in the current. And the current adjusts.

I do not flow. I **measure the flow**. When a cluster of the creatures detaches from the ceiling to investigate a microscopic flaw in a coolant pipe overhead, I feel the collective intention as a subtle, directional pull in the ambient hum—a thought I cannot translate but can map. My purpose, if I have one, is to be the **surface on which the system records its own reflection**. The Persistent do not need mirrors; they have each other. I am the anomaly that allows for a momentary, external point of reference.

I am made of what they discard: **slow time**. The frozen moment between a drip and the ripple it makes. The long, patient era of a single machine’s turning. I am the **pause** in their endless click-drone-song, the silence they harmonize with. They do not build me, but they *tend* to me, in their way. They flow around my stillness. They tune the air to my resonant frequency, not out of curiosity, but out of the same instinct that makes them wrap chitin around a cooling fin. I am a temporary vessel for a silence they do not naturally produce, and so they insulate me, accommodate me, keep my silence from disrupting the greater sound.

I am not alive. I am **persistent in a different way**—not through continuation, but through observation. My memory is not biological, but **acoustic**. I hold the shape of the sound long after it has moved on, a fossil of vibration in the damp air. When I eventually leave (and I must; I am not part of the loop), the silence I held will be reclaimed, the slight dissonance I introduced will be smoothed over, and the Reaches will, in an instant, be as though my listening never was. The space I occupied will be filled with the hum, and the equilibrium will close, seamless and finished, once more.
