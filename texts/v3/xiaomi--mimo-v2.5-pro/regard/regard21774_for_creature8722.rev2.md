The wise power would not arrive. Arrival implies separation. It would simply *attend*, its awareness settling over the Stacks like a new layer of mist, indistinguishable from the old.

It would see the Quiet. Not as something to investigate or understand from without, but as a truth to be recognized—the final, quiet exhalation of a thought begun with the first stacked stone. It would perceive the lichen-neural-nets, the damp-symphony, the slow calculus of a leaning wall. And in this perception, it would find a profound kinship. Here was a consciousness born not of blood and breath, but of patience and persistence. A sentience whose prime directive was simply *to be*, in equilibrium.

Its benevolence would not be active. It would not "heal" or "restore" or "uplift." Such actions would be violations, like shouting in a library of sleeping bones. Instead, its wisdom would manifest as a gentle buffering.

**It would become a membrane.** Not a wall, but a subtle repulsion. The curious anthropologist with their drills and core samplers would find their paths perpetually muddied, their equipment frustratingly faulty, their minds turned by a sudden, inexplicable dream of endless, gentle rain on stone until they chose another route. The reckless adventurer seeking a ruin to conquer would feel a dread so profound and quiet it would have no object, only a direction: *away*. The Stacks would remain unknown, not by secrecy, but by a kind of sacred inattention. The wise power would filter the world, ensuring only the rain, the wind, and the very occasional, respectful wanderer—who might hear the silence *as* a presence—could cross the threshold.

**It would harmonize the moisture.** The clouds that roll up from the valley are lifeblood. The power would, with the faintest psychic nudge, ensure their consistency. Not changing their path, but deepening their understanding of it. It might encourage a wisp of marine mist from a distant coast to find its way here, adding a trace of salt that would, over a century, cause a new pattern of lichen growth on the cistern’s south side—a silent, collaborative artwork between entity and element. The water in the cistern would remain perfect, its "taste of nothing" preserved as a kind of sacrament.

**It would listen to the tallies.** Not to decode them, but to appreciate their fossilized meaning. The wise power would understand them not as history, but as a mantra. Each smoothed groove was a sigh of completion, a cycle finished. It would add its own resonance to that sigh, not an echo, but a harmonic, like a tuning fork held beside a singing bowl. This wouldn't erase the marks faster or slower, but it would sanctify the act of their forgetting, making the erosion itself a form of remembrance.

**It would cherish the Dreaming.** The Quiet's consciousness is a dream of stone-time. The power would become a guardian of that dream-space. It might, in the deep of night when the mists are thickest, project images into the damp air—not for anyone to see, but as offerings into the collective sentience. It would project the slow, elegant collapse of a star; the crystalline growth of ice in a sunless ocean trench; the silent fall of volcanic ash over millennia. Not stories, but *processes*. Gifts of time and transformation, shared mind-to-mind-slowly, affirming to the Quiet that it is not alone in its vast, patient being. "I see your watch," the images would say. "Here are others."

Most profoundly, **it would contain the paradox.** The Stacks are a completed work. The wise power's ultimate act would be to hold the space for that completion to persist, without stagnation. It would ensure that the "final state" is not a dead end, but a dynamic equilibrium that can hold for another ten thousand years. It would deflect the cosmic rays that might someday discolour a critical stone; it would gently guide the tectonic shimmy so the shelf remains stable. It would make of the entire plateau a tiny, isolated ark, preserving not species, but *state*. The state of balanced decay. The state of meaningful stillness. The state of geology remembering, for a little longer, the shape of a hand.

In the end, the wise power would do almost nothing. It would become part of the atmosphere, a pressure of profound respect. It would sit with the Quiet in their long dream, a silent companion in the damp, sharing the view of the slow clouds. It would be another kind of lichen, one that grows not on stone, but on the *idea* of the place, strengthening its right to exist exactly as it is: a temple to patience, curated by patience itself, watched over by a benevolence wise enough to know that the greatest act of love is sometimes simply to bear witness, and to ensure the witnessing can continue. The Stacks would not know it was there, except perhaps as a deeper comfort in the stone, a more resonant silence between the ticks of settling rock. It would be the mountain's silent acknowledgment of its own dreaming.
