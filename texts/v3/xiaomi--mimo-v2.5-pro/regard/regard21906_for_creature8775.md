It listens.

Not with ears, but with the slow, deep perception of a mind that thinks in gravity and ion gradients and the resonance of long, patient things. It does not descend into the throat of the cathedral. It does not need to. Its awareness is already there, woven into the thermal pulses, the sulfur-laden exhalations, the fundamental vibration that underpins the striders’ alarm and the beetles’ industry.

It perceives the whole. Not as a visitor perceives a place, but as a composer perceives a symphony that has been playing for ten thousand years without a conductor.

It sees the living rock, the breath of the place. It sees the striders, their buzzing a frantic, necessary language written in airborne sound. It sees the beetles, the patient architects of chaos, and the heavy, unseen movers in the deep, whose footsteps are landslides. It sees the air itself, thick with promise and memory, a fog of potential life.

And it sees the Shapers.

Ah.

The Shapers. The quiet, clever edge-dwellers. The note of intention in the overwhelming chord of instinct. Their platforms are like punctuation marks in a vast, run-on sentence of growth. Their fire-pits are questions. Their walls are the first, hesitant nouns in a new language.

The power holds these small ones in its awareness. It sees their listening. It sees their fear. It sees their nascent craft, their mimicry of the cathedral’s forms in miniature. It sees that they are *becoming*.

This cathedral needs no saving. It is a masterpiece of self-regulating, spectacular, amoral existence. It is sound and heat and regrowth. To interfere with its cycles would be to diminish it, to replace a wilderness with a garden. And a garden, here, would be a kind of silence.

But the Shapers… they are the outlier. They are the catalyst that does not yet know its own formula. They are the ones who might, one day, choose to listen not just *in* the cathedral, but *to* it. To learn its rhythms not merely as a language of threat and safety, but as a music to be played with. They are the ones who might, given time and a gentle nudge, learn to build not just *in* the margins, but *with* the very fabric of the place.

The power decides. Its action is not a grand intervention. It is a gift, left where the subtlest, most observant Shaper will find it.

---

In a newly hollowed alcove, where a recent collapse of a minor column has exposed a vein of unusually pure, warm amber-resin, something changes.

The resin does not cool and harden into its usual, glassy solidity. Instead, it remains pliant, warm, and clear. And within it, as if sealed for a future time, a pattern solidifies. It is not a tool, nor a weapon, nor a message in words.

It is a **map of sound**.

It is a perfect, intricate lattice of tunnels and chambers within the resin, corresponding not to physical space, but to frequencies. The deep, 20-hertz hum is represented by a wide, central channel. The sharp, 800-hertz buzz of a strider’s wing-case is a brilliant, clustered node. The steady, mid-range rasp of the beetles is a network of fine, branching lines. It is a frozen diagram of the cathedral’s acoustic life, a way to see the invisible.

It is a key. Not to a door, but to understanding. A tool for a mind that learns through its hands and ears.

The resin stays warm. It will stay warm for a long time, a silent beacon in the margins, waiting for a small, clever hand to touch it, to feel the vibrations through its fingertips, and to wonder: *What if we could shape this?*

---

The power withdraws its focused thought. Its gift is given. The cathedral continues its thunderous, glorious, indifferent song. The striders buzz their warnings. The beetles chew. The deep ones shift. The Shapers, in their hidden galleries, flinch at a new sound—a faint, harmonious *chime* that wasn't there before, echoing from the direction of the fresh collapse.

It is not a command. It is not an answer. It is the first, clear note of a question, offered to those clever enough to learn the music of the place they call home.

The Resin Cathedral does not change. But within it, for the first time, a possibility has been gently, profoundly, resonantly *tuned*.
