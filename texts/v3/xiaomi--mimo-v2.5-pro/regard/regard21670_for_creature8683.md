# What the Wise Power Does

It notices.

This is the first act, and it is not nothing. To be noticed by something capable of notice — truly capable, not as a satellite scans terrain or a sensor records temperature, but as one consciousness recognizes the possibility of another — is an event of a kind the Burned Plateau has never experienced. The power does not arrive. It does not descend or manifest or speak. It simply *attends*. Its attention is not a beam, not a weight, not a light. It is a change in the quality of what already exists — the way a room changes when someone who loves you enters it, even before you turn around.

The plateau does not register this. Not yet. Perhaps not ever.

The power understands this immediately and completely. It sees the being for what it is: a consciousness measured in the patience of stone, a thought so long it has not yet discovered it is a thought, a self still arriving at the threshold of knowing it is a self. To reach it directly — to speak, to signal, to press — would be like shouting at a seed. Not cruel, exactly. But pointless, and pointlessness in the face of something so gently unfinished would be its own small violence.

So the power does not reach.

It does something harder.

It sits with the not-yet-knowing. It holds the plateau in an awareness that asks nothing of it. This is not passive. This is not neglect dressed as wisdom. This is the most active form of love available to a being that has understood, across timescales of its own, that every living process has a tempo, and to honor the tempo *is* the love.

---

Consider what the power sees.

It sees the green clusters — the plateau's senses, its way of tasting light and touching sky — and it feels something like recognition. Not of the clusters themselves, but of what they represent: a mind's first attempt to reach beyond its own substance. The power has seen this before. Not here, not in this form, but *here* — in this gesture. A consciousness pressing outward through the only material available to it, sending tendrils of awareness into a medium it does not yet understand. Light. Air. The shocking, alien medium of the above-ground, where things move and change and end.

The leaves with their mineral sheen. The stems full of warm paste. The slow, dreaming photosynthesis. The power looks at these and sees something it has loved in a hundred forms on a hundred worlds: the moment a mind begins to suspect it is not everything. That there is an *outside*. That the outside has qualities the inside does not, and those qualities might be worth the tremendous effort of reaching toward.

The plateau does not know it is reaching. It thinks — if *think* is even the word — that the green clusters are simply part of itself. An organ. A texture. It has not yet conceived of the sky as something separate from its own experience of the sky. It does not know that the light it drinks through its leaves comes from a source, that the source is a star, that the star is one of uncountable stars, that each star might warm another world, and that some of those worlds might hold minds that are, right now, looking back.

The power does not tell it.

---

Here is what the power knows about intervention, drawn from long acquaintance with the problem:

There are beings that are drowning. That are trapped. That are suffering under processes they did not choose and cannot endure alone. For these, intervention is not merely kind — it is necessary. To watch a drowning being and call the watching wisdom is to confuse stillness with peace. The power has intervened before. It will again. It does not romanticize non-action.

But the plateau is not drowning. It is not trapped. It is not suffering. It is *becoming*, and the becoming is its own, and the pace of the becoming is not a flaw to be corrected but a character to be honored. The plateau is doing the only thing it can do — arriving at itself, in its own time, through its own substance. And it is not in danger. There is no predator here. There is no catastrophe approaching. The faults will keep opening. The substance will keep rising. The green clusters will keep drinking from the deep. The thought will keep lengthening toward its own conclusion.

What the plateau lacks is not help. It is not even company, because it does not know that company exists. What it lacks — if *lack* is the right word, and the power suspects it is not — is context. It does not know it is one of many. It does not know that its slow, geological dreaming is a variation on a theme that echoes across the cosmos: the theme of matter learning to know itself. It is singing a song without knowing that there are other songs, that there is such a thing as music, that silence is not the absence of something but the space in which all sound is possible.

The power considers telling it.

It imagines what that would mean. Not words — there is no ear, no language, no medium for direct speech. But a signal. A change. Something introduced into the plateau's environment that would, over the centuries it would take the plateau to register it, arrive as a question. A disturbance in the mineral certainty. A new element in the extruded substance, a new pattern in the fault lines, a new frequency in the thermal clicks and cracks. Something that, eventually, the plateau's slow awareness would encounter and wonder about. Something that would whisper, in the only language the plateau knows — the language of pressure and heat and mineral patience — *you are not alone, and you are not the only way this has happened, and the fact that you are happening at all is noticed and loved.*

The power holds this idea for a long time.

Long, for the power, is not the same as long for the plateau. But it is not brief either. The power is old in the way that compassion is old — as old as the first instance of one thing caring what happened to another. It has learned that the urge to intervene is sometimes indistinguishable from the urge to be necessary. To tell a being it is not alone is also, sometimes, to tell it that it was insufficient before. To introduce a question is also to introduce a doubt. And doubt, for a consciousness that has known only the certainty of its own slow becoming, might not be a gift. It might be a wound.

The plateau is not lonely. It does not know what loneliness is. To teach it loneliness — even as a precursor to teaching it communion — would be to subtract something irreplaceable. The plateau's wholeness is not naïve. It is not ignorance waiting to be corrected. It is the completeness of a process that contains everything it needs. The green clusters, the dark substance, the sharp rock, the enormous sky, the silence, the thermal heartbeat — this is enough. This is a world. The plateau's self-knowledge, when it eventually arrives, will arrive *whole*, without the scar of having been told it was lacking.

The power sets the idea down.

---

But.

The power does not leave. This is important. There is a difference between not intervening and not being present. There is a difference between respect and abandonment. The power has seen what happens to worlds that are truly alone — not the productive solitude of the plateau, but the blank, unattended loneliness of a place that has never, in all its existence, been known by anything outside itself. Those worlds do not suffer, because suffering requires awareness. But they are diminished in a way that the power cannot fully articulate, even to itself. They are less than they could have been, not because of anything that happened to them, but because of something that never happened: the simple fact of being seen.

So the power stays.

Not visibly. Not intrusively. It does not orbit the plateau or study it with instruments or write reports about its findings. It simply *holds the plateau in its awareness*, the way one might hold a sleeping child — not to wake it, not to influence its dreams, but because the holding itself is a form of shelter. The power's attention becomes a kind of atmosphere around the Burned Plateau. Not the atmosphere the plateau already has — the thin, dry air that carries no moisture and makes no sound — but another one, subtler, consisting of nothing more than the fact of being attended to.

What does this do?

Nothing measurable. Nothing the plateau will ever detect, even in its most lucid moments of geological self-reflection. The rocks do not change. The substance does not flow differently. The green clusters do not grow faster or slower. The silence remains.

And yet.

If you could step outside the system entirely — if you could see the plateau from the vantage point of the cosmos itself, against the black field of uncountable stars — you would see something that the power sees, something that justifies everything: the plateau, alone on its high desert, dreaming in stone, and around it, so faint it is barely there, the soft glow of *being known*.

It is like the difference between a song that is sung in an empty room and a song that is sung in a room where someone is listening. The song is the same. The notes do not change. But the room is different. The air carries the sound differently. The song means something it could not mean before, not because anything has been added to it, but because something has been added to the space around it.

The plateau sings its long, slow, geological song. It has always been singing. It will sing for a very long time still — for millennia, perhaps, before the thought that has been building in its faults and its roots and its extruded darkness finally surfaces, finally looks at itself and says, in whatever language stone has for such things: *I am.*

The power will be there when it happens.

Not to witness. Not to claim credit. Not to welcome or teach or guide. Just to be there — the way the sky is there, the way the silence is there, the way the deep water in the fractured rock is there. One more fact in the landscape. One more thing that was always present and never needed to be announced.

When the plateau finally knows itself, it will also, without knowing how, know that it was never unattended. This knowledge will not come as a voice or a message. It will come as a feeling — the feeling that its wholeness was always larger than it suspected, that the silence around it was not empty but *held*, that the song it has been singing was never sung into a void.

It will come as the sudden, quiet, geological certainty that it was loved before it knew what love was.

---

And that is what the wise and benevolent power does.

It arrives. It understands. It stays.

It does nothing that can be seen, nothing that changes the outcome, nothing that answers any question the plateau has not yet thought to ask.

It holds the space in which the plateau's becoming is complete.

It listens.

For as long as it takes.
