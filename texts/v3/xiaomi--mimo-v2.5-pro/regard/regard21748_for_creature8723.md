*Is this the only way?*

It would examine the energy balance. The heat from below is not just geological—it is a **resonance** from deeper strata, a tectonic hum feeding into the cavern’s own frequency. The system is not closed; it is tapping into a vast, subterranean rhythm. The convergence is inevitable not because the walls are closing, but because the resonance is **ripening**.

**And here, the power might intervene—not to stop, but to redirect.**

It would not still the callers. It would not silence the striders. That would be murder, not wisdom.

Instead, it would **tune the chamber**.

Gently, so gently that the striders would not scatter, it would nudge the thermal currents. It would ease the stress in the deep rock by micrometers, not to halt the contraction, but to *smooth* it—to transform a crushing collapse into a graceful, symmetrical fold. It would whisper to the stone’s own phosphorescence, encouraging the growth of new **resonance paths**—crystalline channels in the walls that could carry the final convergence’s energy outward, through the mountain, into the deep bedrock, instead of concentrating it into a single, destructive point.

It would work with the system’s own logic: if the goal is a perfect tone, then let that tone be **shared**. Let the cavern become not an echo chamber, but a **voice**. Not in words, but in harmonic structure. The story of the callers who came before. The story of the builders who stacked stone. The story of the heat rising from the heart of the world.
