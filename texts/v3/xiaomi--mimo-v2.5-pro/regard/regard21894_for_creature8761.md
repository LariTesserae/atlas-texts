The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. There is no arrival here; Veth Mar is not a place that things arrive *at*. It is a place that simply continues. To arrive would imply a departure point, a journey, a context external to the basin. This power has no such context. It is vast, and patient, and its attention is not a spotlight but a slow, settling dusk.

It notices.

Not the structures. Not the water. Not the fauna or the threnodists or the cold. It notices the *pattern*. It notices the resonance of a place that is a question asked into the void, and the void, instead of answering, has become the question. Its first act is not to act. It listens.

It hears a complete language, spoken in the tongue of deep time and cold chemistry and life that has learned to live with loss so thoroughly that loss has become the foundation. It becomes the landscape of its own persistence.

And so, the wise power does not build. It does not warm the water. It does not give the threnodists memory of their ancestors. It does not still the waves. it speak to the Deeper Thing. Veth Mar's terrible, beautiful truth is that it needs no reason. It is a testament to a form of existence that requires no witness.

Instead, the power does something far more subtle. It **attunes**.

It adjusts its own frequency, its own mode of perception, until it is in harmony with the slow, unthinking rhythms of the basin.
