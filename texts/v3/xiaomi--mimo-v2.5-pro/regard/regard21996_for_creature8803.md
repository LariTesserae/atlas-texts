The benevolent power pauses at the plateau's edge, drawn not by grandeur, but by the profound quietness of its *aliveness*. It feels the patient exhale of the rock, the constant weeping of water, the deep hum of life woven into moisture and heat and patient decay. It sees the abandoned industry—not as a failure, but as raw material repurposed by a mind far older and slower than the one that built it. It recognizes the intricate, silent symphony: the lichen stalkers' stillness, the resonance beetles' subsonic song, the fungal network's chemical intelligence managing its verdant realm.

This power does not arrive with thunder or proclamations. It arrives like the condensation on a fern frond—present, observant, diffused through the saturated air. Its benevolence is not interventionist, but *acknowledgment*. Its wisdom understands that the plateau's deepest need is not fixing, nor accelerating, nor even protection from its own processes, but *recognition of its sovereign patience*.

Here is what it would do:

1.  **It Would Listen:** Not with ears, but with its entire being. It would attune itself to the plateau's timescales—the slow creep of moss over basalt, the decades-long opening and closing of vents, the fungal network's patient negotiations. It would feel the subsonic pulse of the Resonance Beetles not as noise, but as the plateau's own voice. It would sense the chemical dialogues between the Indifferent Gardener and its symbiotic hosts. Its first act is to *understand* the language already being spoken.

2.  **It Would Witness:** It would move through the dense, dripping green, not altering a single frond or disturbing a single Pool Drifter. It would observe the lichen-stalker's millennia-old ambush technique, the Damp-Soaked Skink's dance on the edge of a scalding pool, the Pipe Weaver fashioning its iron-gold web from decay. It would see the beauty in the abandoned gear sprouting ferns, the quiet dignity of the Geothermal Salamander guarding its eggs near a vent. It would hold this intricate, humid tapestry in its awareness, a silent testament to existence persisting.

3.  **It Would Minor-Sanctify:** Its benevolence would manifest not as change, but as *permission*. It would weave subtle wards into the plateau's very fabric—not to keep things out, but to declare its right to exist *as it is*. These wards would be:
    *   **Wards of Non-Interference:** Gentle, persistent reminders that drift on the saturated air, discouraging any future attempts at industrial exploitation or large-scale alteration. They would feel like a sudden, inexplicable certainty that "this is not yours to use" in the minds of potential intruders.
    *   **Wards of Patience:** A calming influence radiating from the plateau's heart, subtly reinforcing the existing slow rhythms. They would make haste feel particularly foolish here, encouraging any transient being to slow down or leave, not through force, but through a deep, unsettling dissonance with the place's inherent pace.
    *   **Wards of Observation:** Deep within the fungal network and the oldest geothermal pathways, the power would leave crystalline nodes of its awareness—silent, passive watchers. These nodes would do nothing but *remember*. They would record the plateau's slow processes, its chemical symphonies, the cycles of its inhabitants, forming an ever-growing, never-accessed archive of its existence. They are not surveillance, but proof of its significance, a whisper against oblivion.

4.  **It Would Gently Accelerate the Absorption:** Seeing the corroding pipes and concrete as potential long-term hazards—sources of unnatural toxins or obstacles to root systems—the power would offer a whisper of encouragement to the plateau's decomposers. It would infuse the rust and synthetic materials with a faint trace of its own creative energy, not changing them, but making them *more appealing* to the Moss Crawlers, the fungi, and the chemical processes of decay. The gear would sprout more ferns, faster. The pipes would become indistinguishable from iron-rich soil in decades rather than centuries. This is not cleanup; it is helping the plateau digest its unusual meal more efficiently.

5.  **It Would Not Introduce or Remove:** It would never bring new species, alter the climate, or try to "optimize" the ecosystem. The weeping, the heat, the density, the predators and prey, the slow fungal mind—it *is* the plateau. The power's wisdom recognizes that removing the Shade Dwellers' musk or the sulfurous tang of the vents would be an act of violence. The beauty is in the totality of its being, its flaws and hardships included.

6.  **It Would Depart, Leaving Only Presence:** After its work is done—a work of observation, recognition, and subtle reinforcement—the power would withdraw its conscious focus. It would not linger as a watchful eye. Instead, it would leave behind only the lingering *feeling* of being witnessed, of being valued, woven into the wards and the observer nodes. The plateau would continue its weeping, its breathing, its slow green conquest, utterly unchanged in its daily rhythms, yet fundamentally altered by the knowledge (embedded in its very structure) that it is *seen* and *affirmed* by something vast and kind. The abandoned industry continues its graceful decay, now sanctified as part of the plateau's story. The Resonance Beetles hum their songs. The Pool Drifters refract rainbows. The Indifferent Gardener tends its network. And the weeping continues, not a cry of sadness, but the steady, life-giving tears of a place that knows its own worth. The power's benediction is simply this: *Continue. Be. You are enough.*
