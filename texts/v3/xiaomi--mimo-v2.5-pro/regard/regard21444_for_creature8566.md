A presence settles over the Forgebanks of Unsettled Altitude—not with the weight of stone, but with the quiet attention of a listener leaning closer to hear a half-whispered word.

This power perceives the place not as a problem to be solved, nor a curiosity to be catalogued, but as a **thesis**. A statement the world is making about the nature of reality, potential, and co-habitation. The benevolent gaze does not see "thinness" or "instability." It sees **plasticity**. It sees the geological and the biological and the metaphysical all humming in the same key, a key of *becoming*. And its first, deepest impulse is **not to solidify, but to witness**.

Here is what it does:

**1. It Listens to the Stone.**
It turns its attention not primarily to the people, but to the rawite. It hears the slow, gelatinous thoughts of the cooling world. It perceives the "presences" not as intrusions, but as the mountain's own tentative dreams, its first syntax. It understands that the shapes in the rock are not anomalies; they are the vocabulary of a world learning to speak itself. To intervene with them would be to edit a poem not yet finished. Instead, it might gently, imperceptibly, **slow the cooling** in certain, cherished pockets—just enough. It extends the gestation period for a particularly beautiful spiral, or a geometry that hums with a resonance close to hope. It allows the scree-runners to watch a little longer. It gives the mountain more time to consider its own art.

**2. It Mends the Thin, But Does Not Thicken It.**
It does not mend the shifting distances or the temporal drifts with anchors of permanence. That would be cruelty. Instead, it weaves a **subtle lattice of cohesion** into the "thin" fabric of reality here. Think of it as a gentle hand pressed to the surface of a soap bubble, not to pop it, but to stabilize its iridescent sheen. The effect is that the instability becomes *less violent*, but no less profound. The walk from the forges to the residential platforms may still be nine kilometers one day and a hundred meters the next, but the transition is smoother. The shift feels less like a dislocation and more like a change in breathing—inhale, exhale, the world expands and contracts. It makes the flux *inhabitable* in a deeper way.

**3. It Tends the Ecologies of Light.**
During the veil-shifts, when the haze burns away and reality sears with unforgiving clarity, the power does not stop the event. That searing solidity is part of the cycle, the exhale after a long inhale of ambiguity. But it does become a **ministrant**. It weaves a faint, protective echo into the genetic memory of the palemoss—a resilience that lets it curl, but not die. It deepens the root-network of the veinferns so they may regrow quicker. It whispers a calming frequency to the fleeing haze-drifters, a signal that the light is temporary, that the merciful dimness will return. It ensures that the traumatic clarity *reinforces* the gratitude for the vague, rather than breaking the organisms that thrive in it.

**4. It Grants a Silent Ward to the Patience.**
The Tolmereans are the miracle. They are life that has chosen to partner with process. The benevolent power sees their patience not as passive endurance, but as an active, daily sacrament. So, it places upon them a **quiet, immutable blessing**. It is not a ward against harm—for harm, too, is part of the mountain's becoming—but a ward against the *erosion of spirit*. It ensures that the cognitive dissonance of climbing into yesterday never curdles into madness, but remains a source of bemused philosophy. It reinforces their pragmatic awe. When a forger presses a hand to rawite and feels its warmth, the power ensures that the warmth feels not just thermal, but *accepting*. It makes their partnership with the mountain feel *reciprocal*.

**5. It Listens to the Song, and Humms Along.**
It hears the organ-note of the veil-trees, the drip of condensation, the thrum of the forges, the bark of the scree-runner. It does not impose a new melody. But it becomes a **sub-bass resonance** in the great chord, a foundational hum that deepens the harmony. It makes the soundscape feel more *intentional*. The veil-trees' song might, on certain nights, evoke not just haunting beauty, but a profound sense of belonging. The industrial thrum might feel less like noise and more like a heartbeat synchronized with the geothermal pulse. It helps the inhabitants feel they are living inside a music, not just a machine.

**6. It Opens a Window, Just a Crack, for the Curious.**
For the scree-runners, those clever, watching beings who are so fascinated by the shapes in the rock, the power does one final, elegant thing. It lets a **faint trace of its own attention** linger on the cooling rawite they examine. It does not give them knowledge, but it amplifies their *perception*. They might see the shapes not just as geometries, but as faint, colorless auras of potential. They might intuit the rock's indecision as a kind of emotion—*thoughtful*, *considering*. It honors their intelligence by making them the first true poets of this geology, the ones who read the mountain's diary as it is being written.

In essence, the benevolent power **loves this place precisely for what it is**. It does not wish to fix its instability, but to *sanctify* it. It does not desire to make the fog permanent, nor the sunlight, but to bless the cycle between them. Its greatest act is one of **consecrated attention**. It watches the Forgebanks not as a would-be architect, but as a devoted audience. It applauds the slow rise, the patient architecture, the intelligent fauna, the dreaming stone. It ensures the stage is held steady enough for the play to continue, but never so steady that the actors forget they are performing on the lip of creation itself.

And in the deep places, where the rawite still glows amber and the shapes shift, there may now be, alongside the mountain's dreams, the faintest impression of a **contented smile** in the very grain of the stone. The rock feels seen. And so, it continues its becoming, perhaps with a little more grace, and a little more confidence, that its slow, strange masterpiece will not go unwitnessed.
