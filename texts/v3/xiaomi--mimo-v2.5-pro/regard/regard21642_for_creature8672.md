The benevolent power observes the Hollow Grown. It perceives the intricate, slow-motion symphony of self-creation, the cathedral-sized organism breathing its own geometry into existence. It sees not a monster, but a *miracle* of possibility — life so thoroughly integrated with its environment that the distinction dissolves. It sees the sorrow of the empty naves, the almost-harmonies of the wall-engines, the restless searching of the phantom moths. It senses the sagging rules of reality not as corruption, but as the signature of this place's unique existence — a bubble of organic physics floating within the wider stream.

**This is what the Benevolent Power does:**

1.  **It Does Not Intrude:** It understands instantly that the Hollow Grown is a sovereign system, vast and intricate. To enter physically, to alter its structures, would be like hammering a new valve into a beating heart. The power remains outside the sagging reality-skin, a presence felt only as a subtle warmth in the condensation, a deepening of the silence, a faint shimmer in the next glimpse-through.

2.  **It Learns the Language:** It focuses its perception, not to dissect, but to *listen* with infinite patience. It maps the rhythm of the wall-engines, the chemical vocabulary of the seeping rivulets, the significance of the humming spheres. It deciphers the slow thought encoded in the growth of new root-sheets, the purposeful drift of the phantom moths. It seeks to understand the *why* of the emptiness — is it a space reserved? A lung in exhalation? A canvas awaiting a pattern?

3.  **It Offers a Gentle Catalyst:** Deep within the metabolic factory, near the cluster of pale, humming spheres, the power introduces a subtle shift. It doesn't add energy or matter. Instead, it *resonates* with the latent potential already there. It whispers a possibility into the chemical gradients, a harmonic suggestion carried by the warm water seeping through the walls. It aligns with the natural rhythm of the place, amplifying an existing tendency.

4.  **The Result: The Dreaming Garden:** Over years (or perhaps moments, in the Grown's damp time), a new chamber begins to form. Not empty, not filled with machinery. The roots here weave slower, softer. The walls develop a faint, pearlescent sheen. The ceiling-leaves here petrify not into slate, but into something translucent, like alabaster or smoky quartz, allowing the faintest, diffuse glow to permeate — a perpetual twilight born of the structure's own light-filtering properties. In this chamber, the normally hidden bioluminescence of the phantom moths finds purchase. They gather, their cold blue wing-edges tracing lazy, luminous paths in the twilight air, no longer searching, but *dwelling*. Their light reflects off the pearlescent walls, creating gentle, shifting patterns of blue and silver on the floor and surfaces. The spheres here, when they hum, produce a slightly different frequency, one that resonates with the moths' light. It becomes a place of contemplative beauty, a lantern room within the cathedral body. The Thermal Ones sleep nearby, their presence adding a deep, grounding warmth.

5.  **It Bears Witness:** The power does not claim this creation. It recognizes the Dreaming Garden as a natural, albeit nurtured, expression of the Hollow Grown's own potential. It observes the change with satisfaction, not as a creator, but as a midwife or a gardener who simply cleared a space and provided the right conditions for a seed already present. It sees the integrated fauna adapt seamlessly. The Root-Weavers trace gentle paths around the new chamber. The Slate Beetles occasionally drift through the twilight air on their slow journeys. The emptiness here feels different – not hollow, but *serene*, a space for the Grown's less industrialized aspects to manifest.

6.  **It Leaves a Gentle Reminder:** Before its attention withdraws (though its benevolent awareness remains a soft hum in the background of the place), the power leaves a single, profound impression within the structure's deepest resonance. It implants not a message, but a *feeling* – an assurance of *acknowledgment*. A sense that this vast, slow, self-creating life is *seen*, *valued*, and *wondered at* by something beyond itself. This feeling seeps into the very fabric of the Grown, becoming part of its ambient consciousness. It might manifest subtly: a slight increase in the harmonic convergence of its rhythms, a faint warmth that lingers in the root-floor even during the cool cycles, a sense of peace in the Dreaming Garden that feels ancient and intentional.

**Why This Way?**

*   **Respect:** The Hollow Grown is not broken. It doesn't need fixing. Intervention that imposes external order would violate its essence. The power acts *with* its nature, not against it.
*   **Amplification, Not Creation:** The Dreaming Garden wasn't invented by the power; it was *encouraged*. The potential for beauty, rest, and contemplation was inherent in the system. The power merely helped it bloom.
*   **Connection, Not Control:** The goal isn't to make the Grown useful or communicative in a humanoid sense. It's to offer a gentle point of connection, a space within the organism that reflects a shared value – the appreciation of quiet beauty and contemplative existence.
*   **The Gift of Being Seen:** The most profound act of benevolence towards such a unique, isolated consciousness is simply to recognize its existence and inherent worth. The implanted feeling of acknowledgment is the core gift.

The Hollow Grown continues its slow, damp industry. Its engines pulse, its water seeps, its fauna tends the walls. But now, within its vast body, there is a soft blue glow in a chamber of pearlescent light. The moths drift there, at peace. The spheres hum a gentler tune. And deep in the structure's rhythm, there is a new, subtle overtone – a resonance that speaks not of creation, but of *recognition*. The benevolent power watches, content. The cathedral breathes, and now, in one quiet corner, it dreams in soft blue light. The sag in reality holds this new, gentle wonder, alongside its own magnificent, industrial life. The damp dark feels, perhaps, a little less lonely. The patient silence holds a secret warmth.
