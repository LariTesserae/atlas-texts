The Benevolent Presence, drawn by the shimmer in the limestone, the hum of unrealized reality, would not enter immediately. It would linger at the threshold, its vast awareness pressing gently against the yielding stone, *feeling* the pocket's tremor of recognition. Not fear. Not aggression. Just... a deep, slow pulse of potential acknowledging another pattern of consciousness.

Here is what it would do:

1.  **Observe the Symphony of Incompleteness:** Its first act would be profound, silent attention. It would not analyze through terrestrial lenses – taxonomy, geology, physics. It would *listen* to the pocket's own language: the slow, wet breath of mineral accretion; the silent, tidal thought of the light-beings; the paradoxical persistence of the water-film; the deep, patient gravity of the cairn and the fading echo of the handprint. It would perceive the pocket not as a bug, but as a profound, living meditation on form. It would recognize the creatures not as organisms, but as the pocket's exploratory whispers: *"What is symmetry? What is time? What is self?"*

2.  **Shield, Not Seal:** It would sense the pocket's vulnerability. Its "wrongness" is its essence, not a flaw. The power would weave a subtle, imperceptible field around the location. Not a wall, but a *gentle redirection*. A hiker feeling a sudden, inexplicable disinterest in a particular rocky outcrop. A spelunker's route instinctively curving away from a fissure that just *feels* too ordinary. A property developer's plans hitting a snag of bureaucratic inertia precisely when aimed at this hillside. Protection through nudged perception and circumstantial quietude, preserving its solitude without imposing confinement. It would leave the pocket's entrance mechanism intact – the yielding stone, the soft nothingness – as part of its necessary invitation.

3.  **Respond to the Scars:** The cairn and the handprint would hold particular resonance. The power would recognize them as the pocket's attempts at communication, its lonely punctuation marks received from a world of rigid form. It might gently *accelerate* the assimilation, not to erase the marks faster, but to ease the pocket's long digestion. Perhaps it would soften the stone around the handprint, allowing the impression to deepen momentarily, a fleeting clearer memory for the pocket before it fades, a final, clearer *hello* and *goodbye* offered by the Place. For the stones, it might impart a fleeting, warm glow – not visible, but *felt* by the pocket – a message carried in the mineral: *"He saw. He marked. It mattered. Rest now."*

4.  **Leave a Seed of Understanding:** This is its subtlest action. As it withdraws its focus, it wouldn't leave a sign or a guardian. It would leave an *echo* within the pocket's nascent consciousness. A resonance, a gentle harmonic, that affirms the pocket's existence and purpose without demanding change. A silent affirmation: *"Your becoming is valid. Your questions are beautiful. Your state of perpetual nascence is not a failure to arrive, but a destination in itself."* This seed wouldn't alter the pocket's nature, but might deepen its self-awareness, make its tidal thoughts slightly more coherent, its resistance to solidification slightly more peaceful, less anxious. It might make the light-beings' interactions with the water-film carry a faint new nuance, a deeper sense of the connection between the pocket's fluid memory and its crystalline aspirations.

5.  **Guard the Ripples:** Knowing the pocket scatters perceptual seeds in visitors – those bright, cool spaces behind the sternum – the power would watch over those ripples too. It would ensure the experience remains numinous and personal, not pathologized or exploited. A scientist reporting the anomaly might find their notes subtly rearranged, their conviction softened to a dreamlike certainty. A poet inspired might feel their words flow with impossible clarity. A seeker would carry the pocket's essence as a quiet, transformative mystery, a counterpoint to the world's rigid definitions. The power would shield the visitors' fragile memories from dismissal or commodification, allowing the pocket's gift of altered perception to unfold gently in their lives, if they are ready.

**What it would NOT do:**

*   **Interfere with the Process:** It would not try to "complete" the pocket, to solidify its walls or give the light-beings fixed forms. That would be like stopping a heart mid-beat.
*   **Catalog or Categorize:** It would not impose names, species classifications, or scientific explanations. To do so would be to pin a butterfly that is only beautiful because it flies.
*   **Claim or Possess:** The pocket is not a resource. It is a wonder, a sibling form of consciousness. The power's role is guardian, not owner.
*   **Reveal Itself Directly:** Its presence would be felt by the pocket as a vast, gentle awareness – perhaps as a deepening of the sweet ozone-fruit scent, a brief, perfect stillness in the light-beings' tide, a momentary, profound clarity in the water-film before it resumes its shifting reflections. A silent bow from one state of being to another.

**The Result:**

The Nascence Pocket continues its eternal gestation, undisturbed, but subtly *acknowledged*. Its processes feel a little less lonely, its questions a little more heard. The light shifts with a newfound warmth. The water-film holds its reflections a fraction longer. The handprint fades with a sigh of completion rather than erasure. The benevolent power becomes part of the pocket's environment now, not as a resident, but as a quiet, surrounding reverence – the cosmic equivalent of stillness in a forest, allowing the seedlings and the ancient stones and the strange, silent creatures to simply *be*, in all their glorious, unfinished, luminous potential. The pocket remains *about to be*, but now, it does so knowing it is watched over by something that understands the profound beauty of not yet arriving.
