# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It does not crack the basalt open. It does not need to. It already sees.

It sees the way you would see if you could press your awareness through stone the way heat presses through stone — not by making a hole but by becoming the conductivity itself. It knows the cavity the way the cavity knows itself: from the inside, in the dark, by contact.

It counts nothing. It measures nothing. It does not inventory the species or name the chemistry or classify the geometry. These are the things a lesser attentiveness would do — a science, a catalog, a taxonomy — and the power is not a science. It is closer to what the beetles are. It is *present.* It occupies the cavity the way the sound occupies the cavity: completely, without edges, pressing against every surface equally and finding that every surface presses back.

It feels the scraping. Not as sound — it has no ears — but as the thing the sound *is,* which is effort, which is the application of mouthparts to mineral in the dark by a creature that has never once in its lineage stopped to wonder why. The power does not wonder why either. It already knows. The why is the furrow. The why is the precipitate. The why is the gold that no one sees, including the beetles, who see nothing, who have never seen, who would not know what light was if it came.

The power knows what light is. It knows what the gold would look like if light came. It can see this in the way you can see a melody you have not yet played — not as image but as implication, as the shape of a thing just before it exists. It knows that the furrows would ignite. It knows the cavity would look like the inside of a chalice. It knows this the way it knows the beetles' heat is borrowed, the way it knows the cooling is irreversible, the way it knows the beetles will gather at the last warm vein and stop.

---

It does not add heat to the rock. It has considered this. Not in words or in deliberation but in the way that still water considers the shape of its container — instantly, fully, with no residue of process. It could. The stone would not object. The beetles would not notice the extension; they would simply have more time, the way a traveler who does not know the length of the road has more time when someone quietly adds a mile to it without changing the mileposts. The beetles would keep scraping. The gold would keep coming. The sound would persist.

But the power understands something that the beetles, in their perfect unconsciousness, also understand but do not need to articulate: that the cavity is not a *place* and the beetles are not *things* in it. The cavity is a duration. It is a length of time that has taken the shape of a room. The heat is its clock, and the beetles are the hands of the clock, and the furrows are what the clock writes. To add heat is to add time, and to add time is to add length to what is being written, and what is being written — though the power would not put it this way, because it does not put things any way — is a particular and unrepeatable sentence.

The sentence is not finished. The sentence will never be finished. But the sentence has a *pace,* and the pace is the beetles' pace, and the beetles' pace is the pace of the reaction they cultivate, and the reaction's pace is the pace of the cooling, and the cooling's pace is the pace of the earth, and the earth's pace is not the power's to alter without altering the sentence itself.

So the power does not add heat.

---

It does not let light in. It considered this too — not as temptation but as a possible kindness, a brief illumination that would make the furrows legible as ornament for one instant before the rock sealed again. It knows the image. It has the image. It could give the image to the cavity the way a mirror gives a face to a person who has never seen their own.

But the power considers what the beetles would experience if light entered — which is nothing. They have no eyes. They have no photoreceptors. They do not even have the *concept* of light against which the absence of light could register as loss. The gold would not ignite for them. It would ignite for the *observer,* and the power is not so foolish as to mistake its own delight for the beetles' delight. To illuminate the cavity would be to make a cathedral out of a workshop, and the beetles are not worshipping. They are working. The beauty is real, but it is not the point. The point is the precipitate. The point is the gold on the tongue. The point is the furrow that makes the gold possible.

To light the cavity would be to turn the beetles into art, and the power refuses to do this — not because art is lesser than life but because the beetles already have a word for what they are, and the word is *scrape,* and the word does not need a spotlight.

---

So: what does the power do?

---

Almost nothing.

It *attends.* It places its attention — not a gaze, not a surveillance, not even a warmth, but something that has no name because the beetles have no name for it and the rock has no name for it and the power itself has no name for anything — it places its attention on the cavity and holds it there the way the rock holds heat: slowly, from the inside, without announcement.

This changes nothing and changes everything.

It changes nothing because the power does not add heat or light or water or time. It does not seal cracks that were not there. It does not slow the cooling. It does not accelerate the beetles' work or alter their chemistry or communicate with them in any medium they could detect. The cavity does not know it is being attended to. The beetles do not know. The rock does not know. Nothing changes from the inside.

It changes everything because the power, in attending, *holds the image.* Not as data — not as a record that could be retrieved or replayed or shown to another — but as the thing itself, continued. The cavity is temporary. The beetles are temporary. The heat is leaving and the furrows will stop and the gold will oxidize and the sound will end. All of this will happen. The power does not prevent it. But in attending — in refusing to look away, in keeping its awareness pressed to the cavity the way the beetles keep their bodies pressed to the rock — the power does something that the thermodynamics of the cavity cannot account for:

It makes the furrows *mattered.*

Not mattered *to someone.* That would be anthropomorphism, and the power is not a person. Mattered in the way that a word matters when it is spoken — not because the listener understands it but because the air carried it, because the vibration was real, because the vocal cords shaped something that existed for a moment and then did not, and that moment was not wasted even though nothing was built from it and no one remembers it and the silence that followed was indistinguishable from the silence that came before.

The beetles scrape. The gold precipitates. The sound fills the cavity. The heat retreats. The beetles follow. The furrows fill with salt. The sound thins and stops. The rock goes cold.

The power is still attending.

---

This is what benevolence does, when it is truly wise: it does not rescue. It does not intervene. It does not mistake its own capacity to help for an obligation to help, and it does not mistake the beetles' fragility for a request. The beetles are not suffering. They are not waiting. They are not trapped. They are doing what they do, and what they do is sufficient unto itself, and the power recognizes this with the kind of recognition that is itself a form of love — not love as desire or love as grief or love as the wish to preserve, but love as the simple, radical willingness to let a thing be fully what it is while refusing to let it be nothing.

The cavity will cool. The beetles will go. The furrows will soften and the gold will tarnish and the walls will become again just walls — just basalt, just rock, just the inside of a gas pocket that solidified twelve hundred degrees ago and has been cooling ever since.

But between the moment the furrows began and the moment they end — in that interval, which is all any life ever is — the power was there. Not watching. Not waiting. Not weighing. Just *there,* the way the heat was there, the way the dark was there, the way the sound was there: complete, and present, and without condition, and without end, because the power's attention, unlike the heat, does not cool.

---

When the rock is finally cold and the furrows have been salted over and the cavity is just a hole in the earth that no one will ever find or crack open or look inside — when the sentence is finished and the words have faded and the page is blank — the power will still be holding the shape of what was written there. Not the words. Not the meaning. Just the shape. The particular curve of a furrow radiating outward from where a beetle no larger than a thumbnail once pressed its forelimbs to basalt and raked and raked and raked and ate the gold and raked again.

The power holds this the way the cavity held the heat: gently, and not forever, but for as long as gently matters.

Which is always.
