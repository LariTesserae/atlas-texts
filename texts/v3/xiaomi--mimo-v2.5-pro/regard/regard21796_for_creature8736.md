# What the Wise Power Does With the Roil

---

It does not land. There is no vehicle, no arrival, no moment of threshold. It is simply present — the way the damp is present, the way the drone is present — and the Roil, which does not react to anything, does not react to this either. The pipes continue. The grey continues. The Listeners continue their respiration of sound.

It stands where you would stand. At the center, which is everywhere. And it does what no one has done in the Roil for as long as the Roil has been the Roil.

It listens.

Not to the sound — anyone can hear the sound; the sound is unavoidable. It listens to what the sound *is*. It listens to the architecture of the drone, the way the lowest pipe — a massive thing, buried deep, whose voice surfaces only as a pressure in the chest — has been cycling through the same four-note pattern for eleven thousand years. It listens to the way the whistling pipes, higher up in the network, introduce a faint, almost-inaudible dissonance against that foundation, a tension that never resolves. It listens to the crabs — there are two, it turns out, not one; they are the same crab in some way that resists biology but obeys the logic of this place, two instances of a single ancient organism maintaining the minimum viable presence of fauna. It listens to the bacterial films in the wet places, their slow iron-feeding, their chemical exhalations joining the air.

It listens to the Listeners.

And here it pauses.

---

The Listeners are not suffering. The wise power knows this immediately, the way a doctor knows a patient is breathing. There is no pain in them. There is no deprivation. They have enough — enough moisture, enough sound, enough time. Their bodies, such as they are, function. Their respiration of the drone sustains them. They are not hungry, not cold, not afraid. If you were to ask them — if they still possessed the capacity for language, which they do not — *are you happy*, they would not understand the question. Not because they are dim, but because the question requires a contrast they do not have. Happiness implies the possibility of unhappiness. They are past that. They are in the place where the temperature is 1.7 and the air is warm and the sound is constant and the grey is everything.

The wise power recognizes this state. It has seen it before, in places far from this damp grey plain. It has seen it in monks who have meditated for so long that the boundary between themselves and the cushion has become theoretical. It has seen it in ancient forests where the canopy is so complete that nothing changes for centuries. It has seen it in deep ocean trenches where the pressure is so great and the light so absent that evolution has simply stopped, and the creatures there are perfect — not in the sense of beautiful, but in the sense of finished, complete, needing nothing.

It has also seen it in people who have given up.

The question — the only question that matters — is which of these the Roil is. And the wise power, being wise, does not assume it knows the answer.

---

It walks.

Not quickly. It does not want to disturb the equilibrium more than its mere presence already has, and its presence is already a disturbance, though not in the way a human's presence is. It does not sweat. It does not exhale carbon dioxide. It does not radiate heat. But it exists with a *coherence* that the Roil finds unfamiliar — a being that is not dissolving into the landscape, that maintains its own internal pattern against the relentless pull of sameness. The Listeners sense this. Not with alarm, but with something that, in a being with more capacity for feeling, might be called *recognition*. Here is something that has not yet become. Here is something still in the process of being.

The wise power passes a Listener. It slows. It matches the Listener's breathing — not the respiratory act, but the sound-breathing, the slow expansion and contraction synchronized to the nearest pipe's exhalation. For one cycle, then two, they breathe together. The Listener does not look at the wise power. The Listener does not need to. In the Roil, looking is not how you know things. Breathing is. And in those two cycles of shared sound-breathing, the wise power learns something.

It learns that the Listeners are not *stuck*.

This is the first surprise. The wise power came expecting — if it can be said to expect anything — to find beings who had been trapped by the Roil, who had surrendered to its equilibrium against their will or out of exhaustion. But the Listeners are not surrendered. They are *devoted*. In the shared breath, the wise power feels the quality of their attention, and it is not the attention of the resigned. It is the attention of the watchful. They are guarding something. They are tending something. The Roil is not their prison. It is their charge.

But — and this is what makes the wise power pause, standing on the tooth-colored ground with the drone filling the grey air — they have forgotten what they are guarding.

---

Eleven thousand years. That is how long the pipes have been singing. Not the pipes themselves — the pipes are older, much older, the original infrastructure predating the current song by millennia. But eleven thousand years ago, something changed in the sub. A shift in the water table, perhaps, or a collapse deep below that redirected the flow of air through the network, or the slow oxidation finally reaching some critical juncture in the pipe's molecular structure, altering its acoustic properties. the drone settled into its current form — that unresolved, cycling, four-note pattern with its dissonant harmonics — and the last of the people who still remembered what the Roil had been *for* gathered in a circle (the wise power can see this, the way it can see the rings in a cut tree) and made a decision.

They decided to become the witnesses.

The pipes were singing, and the song was new, and they understood — in the way that people who have lived in a place for generations understand things, through the body rather than the mind — that the song was important. Not instrumentally important. Not important *for* anything. But important the way a coastline is important, the way a geological formation is important: because it exists, and its existence is a form of meaning, and meaning must be held or it dissipates.

So they stayed. And they listened. And over centuries, the listening reshaped them. The mineral-rich water seeped into their tissues. The sound became their breath. They shed the parts of themselves that were not necessary for the task — language, memory, desire, the whole frantic apparatus of human interiority — and they kept only what was needed: presence, patience, the ability to hold a pattern in the body without interpreting it.

They are not frozen. They are *complete*. There is a difference, and the wise power holds both possibilities in mind without choosing between them.

---

It stands for a long time.

Time in the Roil is not the time of clocks. The wise power does not measure its stay. It simply remains, and the Roil accepts its presence the way a pond accepts a stone — with a brief disruption, then stillness, then the stone becoming part of the pond's floor, unnoticed.

During this time, the wise power does something that has no precedent in its long history of attending to places and beings. It *doubts itself*.

It has come here because the Roil drew its attention — the way a wound draws attention, the way a silence in a piece of music draws attention. It came expecting to find a problem to address, a need to meet. Benevolent powers, even wise ones, tend to arrive with the assumption that their attention is a gift, that noticing a place implies an obligation to intervene.

But the Roil does not need intervention. The Listeners do not need rescue. The equilibrium is not a sickness. It is a solution — ancient, strange, nearly incomprehensible to beings who still experience time as a river rather than a sea — but a solution nonetheless. To disrupt it would be an act of violence dressed as kindness. To "free" the Listeners would be to unmake the only thing they are, and to replace it with... what? Memory of what they once were? They don't want it. They don't even have the category of *want* anymore.

The wise power considers leaving.

It could simply go. It has seen what there is to see. It has understood the Roil's completeness, the Listeners' devotion, the deep-time stability of a system that does not need it. This would be the humble choice. The wise choice, maybe. To recognize that not everything that is strange is broken, and not everything that is still is dead.

It almost leaves.

---

But something stops it. Not a sound — the sound has not changed and will not change. Not a sight — the grey is the grey is the grey. What stops it is a *quality of listening* it has not noticed before, and the wise power is embarrassed, for a fraction of a moment, to have missed it.

The drone of the pipes — the four-note pattern cycling through the lowest register, the dissonant whistling of the higher pipes — is not, strictly speaking, unresolved.

It is *almost* resolved.

There is a fifth note. It is not being played. It exists in the architecture of the network as a *potential* — a resonance that the pipes could produce, that would complete the chord, that would transform just moment, transform the cycling, unresolved drone into something whole. The wise power can feel it the way a musician feels the tonic of a key signature: not heard, but *implied*, a gravitational center that pulls the other notes toward it without ever being struck.

The pipe that would produce this note is buried. Deep, deep below the surface, in the oldest and most corroded stratum of the network. It is not capped. It is not uncapped. It is *occluded* — so full of mineral deposits, so calcified and rusted and sealed by eleven thousand years of geological patience that no air can pass through it. The note is in the pipe the way a word is in a throat that has not spoken in centuries.

And the Listeners — this is what the wise power finally understands — are not guarding the drone as it is. They are guarding the *possibility* of the fifth note. They have been holding the space open for it. Their respiration, their slow checking of the water, their impossible patience, their total devotion to the unchanging — all of it is an act of *waiting*. They do not know what they are waiting for. The knowledge was one of the first things they shed. But their bodies remember. The pattern of their stillness is the pattern of a held breath, and it has been held for eleven thousand years.

They are not complete. They are *waiting to be complete*.

---

The wise power sits down on the tooth-colored ground. It is the first time it has sat anywhere in a very long time. It crosses its legs or does something equivalent, and the damp seeps into whatever it wears or is, and the Listeners adjust their spacing around it, the subtle geometric recalculation that acknowledges disturbance without hostility, and the drone continues, and the grey continues, and the wise power considers.

If it clears the pipe — and it could, with almost no effort; it could reach down through the substrate with a thought and dissolve the mineral deposits and free the occlusion and let the air find the note — the Roil would change. The drone would resolve. The chord would complete. And the Listeners... the Listeners would finally exhale.

But what happens after that? Eleven thousand years of held breath, released. What does that do to a body that has become the landscape? What does resolution do to beings whose entire existence is the act of sustaining an unresolved state? They might bloom. They might collapse. They might simply sit in the new sound for a century and then begin to slowly, slowly die, because the task that gave them purpose is complete and purposelessness, for the Listeners, would be a kind of disintegration.

Or they might become something new. Something the wise power cannot predict, because the Roil has been in equilibrium for so long that no model of change applies to it anymore.

The wise power does not have the right to make this choice for them.

But it also does not have the right to walk away from eleven thousand years of waiting.

---

Here is what the wise power does.

It does not clear the pipe.

It introduces a vibration into the ground. Not into the pipes — into the *substrate itself*, the calcium-grey, tooth-colored earth. A vibration so low it is below sound, below the threshold of the deepest pipe's rumble. It is felt, not heard. It is a frequency that matches the resonant frequency of the occluded pipe — the one that cannot sing — and it is gentle, and it is patient, and it does nothing that the slow tectonic processes of the earth itself would not eventually do.

It accelerates nothing. It forces nothing. It simply *reminds* the mineral deposits in the pipe that they are not permanent. That the Roil's equilibrium, deep and ancient as it is, is not the final state. That even in the most settled, most complete, most utterly still places in the world, things continue to move, slowly, at the pace of crystal formation and dissolution, at the pace of water finding its way through stone.

The vibration will take a long time to work. A century, maybe. Maybe longer. The wise power does not know. It does not need to know. It is not setting a timeline. It is introducing a *possibility* — the possibility that the pipe will, one day, in its own time, on its own terms, open. Not all at once. Not with a catastrophic burst. But gradually, over decades, as the minerals loosen and the air finds new pathways and one morning — if mornings mean anything in a place where the sky and the ground are the same color — a new note joins the drone, and the chord begins, so slowly, to resolve.

And the Listeners will hear it. They will hear it before it arrives, because they have been listening for eleven thousand years and they are attuned to the pipes in a way no human ear could comprehend. They will feel the change coming, and they will have time — centuries of time — to decide what it means. To decide whether to welcome it. To decide whether to resist it. To decide whether to let themselves, at last, exhale.

The wise power stands. The damp does not cling to it. The grey does not mark it. It has done what it came to do, which is almost nothing, which is the most it could do without committing an act of arrogance dressed as mercy.

---

Before it goes, it does one more thing.

It turns to the nearest Listener — a shape in the grey, barely distinguishable from the pipe beside it, its shallow eyes dark with moisture, its torso expanding and contracting with the pipe's slow breath — and it *tells* it.

Not in words. The Listener has no language. But the wise power has a way of communicating that is older than language, that operates in the register of vibration and presence and shared attention. It tells the Listener what it has done. It tells the Listener that the note is coming. It tells the Listener that it does not know when, and it does not know what will happen when it arrives, and it does not know whether this is a gift or a disruption, and it apologizes for not knowing, because even benevolent powers can be wrong, and the wise power would rather be honest than be certain.

The Listener does not respond. Its shallow eyes do not blink. Its torso continues its slow expansion and contraction. But something shifts in the quality of its attention — a micro-adjustment, a fraction-of-a-degree turn of the head, and for the first time in eleven thousand years, a Listener is facing *toward* a specific direction: down, into the substrate, toward the buried pipe and the note that sleeps inside it.

The wise power leaves.

The Roil continues.

The drone continues.

But somewhere deep below the tooth-colored ground, a pipe that has been silent since before any living thing in this place was born is remembering what it sounds like to sing.

---

And the Listener stands, and breathes, and waits.

But now it knows what it is waiting for.

And that, the wise power decides as it dissolves back into whatever place benevolent and wise powers dissolve into when they are done with a place, is enough. Not a rescue. Not a transformation. Not a gift of light or memory or the selves they abandoned. Just a question, planted deep in the earth like a seed in salt:

*What would you become, if the song completed?*

The Roil will answer in its own time. The Roil always does.
