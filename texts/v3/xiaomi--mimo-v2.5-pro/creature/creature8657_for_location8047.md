The Overflow Cell breathes. Not lungs, not gills – but the whole chamber expands and contracts with the thermal pulse of the vent below. Life here isn't an accident; it's a conversation with the machine, a dialect spoken in chlorophyll and chitin, vibration and rust.

**What Lives Here:**

1.  **The Chorus of Roots:** Not merely plants, but the architects of the space. The **Ironvine** (*Ferrophyton tenax*) is the oldest, its lignified roots calcified into bone-white lattices ringing with metallic resonance where they grip pipes. They are slow, patient, drinking dissolved minerals and the faint chemical traces of ancient industrial fluids. Around them swirl the **Jade Veils** (*Laminaria mekhanica*), broad algae engineered for efficiency, now wild and magnificent, their fronds rippling in the pump’s rhythm, filtering the water and breathing out clouds of microscopic oxygen bubbles that cling to surfaces like dew. The **Lantern Moss** (*Bryolumen profundis*) paints the walls, its pale spore-capsules glowing faintly bioluminescent – not to attract, but perhaps a vestige of a lost function, now just a quiet punctuation in the green-black gloom. They are the foundation, the substrate-sculptors, the oxygen-weavers.

2.  **The Filter-Feeders:** Life in the suspended spaces. **Gauze Crabs** (*Tenuibrachion cellarius*) drift on fronds or creep along pipes. As described, they are pale, almost translucent, their carapaces thin as paper, eyes large and dark, adapted to the gloom-filtered light. They methodically pick microorganisms from the water and the moss. **Veil Weavers** (*Polychaeta textile*) extend intricate, sticky nets of mucus between pipe junctions, capturing drifting particles. Their feathered gills pulse softly, matching the pump’s *chunk-chunk-chunk*. They are the silent sieves.

3.  **The Grazers and Scavengers:** The clean-up crew. **Slag Snails** (*Gastropoda scoria*) with shells incorporating metallic oxides from the substrate, slowly scrape algae and biofilm from grating and walls. **Threadworms** (*Oligochaeta ferrata*), tiny and segmented, churn the compacted industrial substrate, aerating it, breaking down the endless rain of organic detritus. They are the unseen recyclers.

4.  **The Shoaling Shadows:** The **Clickers** (*Bathyapogon rhythmicus*). Small, finger-length fish, not truly schooling by instinct, but drawn together by the pump’s vibration. Their tiny, bony jaws click constantly – a sound like sand pouring onto glass – as they feed on larvae and microorganisms flushed out by the currents. They move in synchronized darts, flashing silver-bellied as they turn in the light cone of the fixture, hiding in the algae veils or under the pipes when not feeding. Their presence is the most ephemeral, yet their rhythmic movement is a visible signature of the Cell's heartbeat.

5.  **The Vibration:** The **Heartbeat** (*Machina vitalis*). The ancient, fused-bering pump is not merely a machine; it is the central actor. Its rhythmic *chunk-chunk-chunk* is the tempo of existence. Organisms grow to its rhythm, spawn in its pulse, avoid its loudest zones. Its vibration is felt, interpreted, and integrated into every life cycle. It is the metronome of this biosphere.

**How They Live Together:**

They are a single, breathing system. The Jade Veils oxygenate and provide structure. The Lantern Moss stabilizes and adds soft light. The Ironvine builds the framework. The Filter-Feeders clean the water, their waste feeding the Moss and Snails. The Threadworms process the substrate. The Graze the algae, preventing overgrowth. The Clickers control smaller invertebrate populations. All are warmed by the vent’s plume, lit by the eternal bulb, and rocked by the pump’s rhythm.

**What They Are:**

They are the **Overflow Ecosystem** – a self-sustaining, deeply adapted biome born from engineered origins and deep-time chaos. They are not wild, not natural in any terrestrial sense, but neither are they cultivated. They are a **convergence**, a community locked in a feedback loop with the decaying, yet stubbornly persistent, machinery of their prison/haven. They are the living proof that given energy (heat, light), structure (metal, pipes), and time (uncounted centuries), life will not just persist, but create its own intricate, resonant, and unrepentantly *made* world. They are the green, chittering, pulsating soul of the Iron Grave.
