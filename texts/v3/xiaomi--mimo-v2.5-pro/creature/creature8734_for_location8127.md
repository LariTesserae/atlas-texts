The inhabitants of the Gravethorn Spires are not visitors. They are manifestations. They do not *live* here; they *emerge* from the conditions, the sound, the breakage, and the ancient, patient structures.

### **Resonance Echoes**
These are the most common manifestations, though "common" is relative. They are not creatures in the biological sense. They are **temporary coagulations of the ambient sound and light**, given fleeting, unstable form by the breakage zones.
*   **Form:** They shimmer into being at the edges of perception, often resembling the listener more than the place. A silhouette made of the copper-ozone air, distorted by the polyphonic groan. A fractal unfolding of the violet growth-lights. They are mirrors of the observer's own sensory stress.
*   **Behavior:** They pace. They walk alongside you, just outside your focused vision, matching your stride. They are not mimics; they are *echoes of intent*, resonant feedback from a mind trying to make sense of the senseless. If you stop abruptly, they may take an additional step before dissolving. They do not interact. They simply *are*, until the local resonance shifts and they unweave.
*   **Nature:** They are not alive. They are phenomena. You are not being watched; you are experiencing a perceptual artifact of the Spires' immense, structured vibration acting upon a conscious mind.

### **Vein-Leeches**
These dwell within the rock, specifically along the glowing pale mineral veins. They are less entities and more **mobile geometries**.
*   **Form:** Imagine a fracture in the vein, a dark jagged line that is not an absence of light but a presence of *deeper dark*. This line can move along the vein's path, consuming the faint glow as it goes, leaving a cooled, dead-stone track in its wake. Occasionally, the fracture splits into branching patterns that resemble a skeletal hand or a root system, only to merge back into a single, purposeful line.
*   **Behavior:** They are drawn to sound-dense areas and to the structures. They will "feed" on the humming growth-plates, causing them to dim and become brittle. They also seem to investigate the spheres in the structures, crawling across the floor to surround the base of the sphere, pulsing in a slow, dark rhythm that counterpoints the sphere's vibration. They avoid breakage zones, as if the spatial instability is painful or confusing to their rigid, vein-bound geometry.
*   **Nature:** They may be the Spires' equivalent of an immune system or a cleanup crew, pruning anomalies, silencing dissonant notes, and perhaps even attempting to "debug" the corrupted data of the breakage zones by consuming the unstable energy around them.

### **The Caretakers**
The builders of the structures are gone. But something remains in them, in the stone, in the task.
*   **Form:** They are only visible in the moments of dusk-synchronization, when the coherent, subsonic pulse holds everything in stillness. Then, for a few heartbeats, you can see them as **faint, warm imprints in the air** – not humanoid, but possessing the *suggestion* of a form that once worked with its hands: broad, solid, radiating the same low-frequency heat as the spheres. They are closer to afterimages burned into reality by prolonged resonance than to ghosts.
*   **Behavior:** In those moments of coherence, the imprints move towards the structures. One might kneel by a sphere, not touching it, but seeming to listen or communicate. Others appear to simply stand at the doorless entrances, gazing out at the scree plains, as if waiting for a horizon that never comes. They do not acknowledge each other or observers. They are replaying a loop, an action so ingrained in the place that it has become a geological process.
*   **Nature:** They are not the original builders. They are the **echo of the act of building**, the persistent memory of purpose etched into the local spacetime. Their presence is what gives the structures their lingering aura of deliberate, patient intent.

### **The Sphere-Listeners**
Not all inhabitants are diffuse or pattern-based. Some are anchored.
*   **Form:** They are found only within the structures, and they are inseparable from the spheres. From a distance, the sphere might seem to have a **shimmering haze** around it, like heat distortion. Look closer, and within that haze, you might discern filaments – not violet like the growth, but a deep, pulsing black, extending from the sphere into the stone floor and walls. These filaments are cold to the touch, and they hum with a vibration that feels like a question.
*   **Behavior:** They are static, but aware. They draw in the sound from the environment through the stone, filtering it through the sphere. They are the reason the sound *almost* resolves into meaning near the spheres. They are interpreters, or perhaps amplifiers, trying to translate the Spires' language into something a structured consciousness could receive. When a breakage zone passes near a structure, the filaments pulse wildly, trying to reconcile the spatial stutter with the coherent signal.
*   **Nature:** They are the true residents. The sphere is their core, their heart, and perhaps their purpose. They are the punctuation in the Spires' sentence. They may be the last active program of the vanished builders, or they may be a new lifeform that has evolved to symbiose with the anomalous artifacts, using them to process the overwhelming data of this world.

**In Summary:**
You are not alone in the Gravethorn Spires. You are surrounded by:
*   **Reflections** of your own struggling perception (Resonance Echoes).
*   **Sanitation workers** for reality's errors (Vein-Leeches).
*   **Recordings** of a forgotten duty (The Caretakers).
*   **Listeners** straining to hear a message for you, or perhaps *from* you (The Sphere-Listeners).

They are not here to welcome you. They are simply other aspects of the place's being, coexisting in a system of vibration, memory, and broken light. Your presence is a new, temporary variable in their ancient, slow-motion equation. They will note it. They may even adjust to it. But they will not save you from the slow, cumulative dissolution that comes from standing inside an unfinished world that is still learning how to speak.
