The Weeping Downs breathe with a living multitude, a slow-motion symphony of existence adapted to perpetual damp and stone. Here is who and what makes their home on the weeping stone:

1.  **The Grazing Hordes:** Countless billions of small, segmented creatures, pale and soft-bodied, the length of a human hand. They are the foundation's grazers, moving in vast, whispering herds across the microbial mats. Their rasping mouthparts carve intricate furrows into the charcoal, rust-orange, and violet crusts, leaving trails like miniature plow-lines. Their sound is a constant, wet susurrus, the audible pulse of the Downs' primary consumption.

2.  **The Armored Stalkers:** Predators of the shallow pools and damp flats. Dinner-plate to tabletop-sized, they possess too many legs and dark, slick carapaces. Masters of ambush, they bury themselves in the microbial mats or silt, becoming indistinguishable from the terrain. A grazher steps near, the mat heaves, and the creature is gone in a blur of legs and chitin. They click and scrape as they move, their presence announced by sudden disturbances in the herds.

3.  **The Seed-Wing Swarms:** Not insects, but six-winged flyers with bodies like elongated, dark seeds. They form dense, thrumming clouds above the warmest water-pans and the city's processing districts at midday. Their collective vibration is a low, pervasive hum that harmonizes with the ever-present drip. They are vital pollinators/spore-dispersers for the microbial fruiting bodies and a food source for many.

4.  **The Pool Dancers:** Sinuous, eel-like creatures inhabiting the deeper gullies and managed waterways. Smooth-skinned and grey, they surface with a soft, popping gasp, their lateral fins leaving transient rings on the opalescent water before submerging again. Their numbers are immense, constantly stirring the surface of every deeper body of water. They are farmed within the city's lower districts.

5.  **The Walking Stones:** The large grazers. Rare in any single glimpse, yet defining every vista. Three meters at the shoulder, six-limbed, heavy-bodied, with long necks and hides the colour of dark basalt, streaked with mineral runoff. They move across the open downs in slow, deliberate aggregations of dozens, their broad feet leaving deep, water-filling prints. Their vocalization is a subsonic boom, a vibration felt in the bone before the ear, carrying for kilometers. They are ancient, weathered, geological in pace and presence. Their bones vanish swiftly upon death, consumed by the ecosystem's efficient scavengers.

6.  **The Deep Churners:** Blind, muscular, burrowing creatures. They live beneath the saturated surface, their constant movement keeping the water flowing upward through the soil. They are felt more than seen – a faint, continuous trembling in the ground – but their role is critical: they are the ecosystem's ploughs, preventing stagnation. The city of Threshold has deliberately incorporated access points for them into its lower foundations, trading passage for functional drainage.

7.  **The City Dwellers (Threshold's People):** They are not native to the ecology in the same way, but have woven themselves into its fabric over centuries. They are **industrialists of biology**, **patient architects**, and **symbiotic partners**. Their culture is slow-paced, pragmatic, and deeply observant. They harvest the small grazers in staggering numbers, processing them for chitin, oils, and microbial-rich compounds. They hunt the larger predators selectively for their unique biochemistry and materials. They farm the pool dancers. They cultivate bioluminescent microbes for light. Their buildings are thick-walled basalt fortresses against the damp, covered in intricate gutter systems, built to last centuries, and modified incrementally. They share their spaces: lower levels with burrowers, upper alcoves with seed-wings. They do not conquer the Downs; they integrate. Their sound is the layered rhythm of industry – the thud of hydraulic presses, the hum of looms weaving chitin-fiber, the steady chug of well-maintained engines – all set against the constant dripping percussion and the ever-present thrum of the fauna. They are defined by their patience, their acceptance of the weeping world, and their skill in turning its relentless, crawling abundance into a sustainable existence. They are the mind of Threshold, but the city itself is as much a part of the Downs' living landscape as the stone or the swarms.

**The Collective Presence:** Above all, the Weeping Downs are defined by the *density* and *pervasiveness* of life. It is a place that vibrates. Every wet surface crawls. Every pool teems. The air hums and buzzes. The ground trembles faintly with unseen movement. The sound is a complex tapestry: the constant wet whisper of grazing, the low thrum of fliers, the popping gasp of surfacing eels, the deep, resonant boom of the walking stones, the clicking of armored hunters, the muffled scrapings of burrowers, and underlying it all, the eternal, patient drip, drip, drip of water finding its way down the dark stone. It is an ancient, stable, and overwhelmingly *full* world. Life here doesn't fight for space; it *is* the space, filling every crack and pool with slow, deliberate, enduring existence.
