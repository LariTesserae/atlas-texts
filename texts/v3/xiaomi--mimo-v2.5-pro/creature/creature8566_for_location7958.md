Based on the description of the Forgebanks of Unsettled Altitude and Tolmere, here is what lives there:

1.  **The Tolmereans (Humans):**
    *   **Nature:** Patient, adaptable, pragmatic, deeply integrated with their unstable environment. They are not conquerors, but partners in the mountain's becoming. They possess a quiet resilience forged by perpetual uncertainty.
    *   **Appearance:** Likely lean and sturdy, adapted to altitude. Skin might be weathered by mineral haze and damp. Clothing would be functional, layered, perhaps incorporating mineral-dyed fabrics or utilizing materials like processed mist-moth silk or treated moss-fibers. Tools and gear are heavy, well-made from local iron and ceramic, showing signs of constant repair and adaptation.
    *   **Adaptations:** They have developed specialized professions (drift-cartographers, rawite miners, forge-masters, moss-tenders, beetle harvesters) to manage their unique challenges. Their perception accepts spatial and temporal flux as natural weather. They have a deep, non-sentimental tolerance for the presences in the rock and the unsettling nature of the fauna. Their diet (beetle larvae, moss-cake, scree-runner, veil-tree fruit) and drink (filtered condensation water, fermented veil-tree liquor) are acclimatized.
    *   **Culture:** Values foresight (building channels into everything), patience (projects span generations), practicality (circular economy with the rock), and a philosophical acceptance of the world's unfinished state. Music likely incorporates the sounds of their environment – drips, thrums, veil-tree organ-notes. Art might focus on transient forms, shifting maps, or the raw beauty of cooling rawite.

2.  **Haze-Drifters:**
    *   **Nature:** Vast, ethereal, passive filter-feeders existing within the mineral haze layer (4000-5500m). They are less organisms inhabiting the space and more *aspects* of the haze gaining temporary, fragile structure and consciousness.
    *   **Appearance:** Translucent, gelatinous, jellyfish-like clouds, ranging from small wisps to 15-meter wide spectral forms. Their bodies are primarily condensed mineral dust and water bound by impossibly thin membranes. Internal structures, if visible, are simple, veined networks.
    *   **Adaptations:** Feed by filtering particulates from the haze itself. Reproduce via processes involving condensation and mineral accretion. Move with glacial slowness on air currents. Their passage leaves moisture and ozone scent. Direct light is anathema, causing them to flee rapidly upwards.
    *   **Effect:** Their presence causes spatial dissonance in those who pass through them – a fleeting sense of displaced reality. They are an accepted, if eerie, part of the atmospheric ecology.

3.  **Scree-Runners:**
    *   **Nature:** Highly intelligent, social, mischievous, and adaptable six-limbed warm-blooded creatures. They are the primary non-human vertebrate fauna, perfectly adapted to the cliffs and city structures. They observe and interact with the Tolmereans with curiosity bordering on sapience.
    *   **Appearance:** Size of a large dog. Covered in a fine layer of cultivated mineral dust (from rolling in rawite seeps) acting as camouflage and possibly mineral supplement. Likely have sleek, muscular bodies for climbing, large, alert eyes adapted to dim light, and dexterous limbs capable of tool use and disassembly.
    *   **Adaptations:** Tool use (flat stones to crack concretions). Cultivation of mineral dust coating. Vocal communication (chattering, barking). Strong social structures. Obsessive mineral licking behavior. Fascination with cooling rawite and the shapes within.
    *   **Relationship with Tolmereans:** Tolerated co-inhabitants. Viewed with grudging affection and wariness. They outnumber humans and are considered the original inhabitants. Eaten as a delicacy (and political statement), yet their watching intelligence makes this act complex.

4.  **Glass Beetles:**
    *   **Nature:** Small herbivorous insects forming part of the foundational food web.
    *   **Appearance:** Small, with exoskeletons made of translucent pureite mineral, making them nearly invisible except for the distortions they create.
    *   **Adaptations:** Feed on the biochemical energy and materials of veinferns. Their mineral exoskeletons offer protection and camouflage. Likely part of the rawite mineral cycle as they die and decompose.

5.  **Mist-Moths:**
    *   **Nature:** Large, pale lepidopterans crucial to the ecosystem and human sustenance.
    *   **Appearance:** Enormous (up to 40cm wingspan), pale, ghostly wings. Larvae are likely large, soft-bodied grubs.
    *   **Adaptations:** Lay eggs in reliable condensation channels. Larvae feed voraciously on palemoss, processed and harvested by Tolmereans as a primary food source (bland but nourishing). Adults likely pollinate or otherwise interact with the veil-trees or other flora. Their large wings suggest adaptation for slow flight in thin air.

6.  **The Presences (in the Rawite):**
    *   **Nature:** The most enigmatic inhabitants. They are not living in the conventional sense, nor are they fossils or mere geological formations. They are *becomings* – nascent structures, geometries, and spirals gestating within the translucent, cooling rawite. They represent the raw potential of a reality still forming, or perhaps shadows/echoes bleeding through from other states of existence where reality is "thinner."
    *   **Appearance:** Visible only in semi-cooled, translucent rawite. Non-geological, non-biological shapes: almost-but-not-quite symmetrical geometries, spirals following unknown sequences. They are transient, disappearing as the rock solidifies.
    *   **Behavior/Effect:** Passive, observed phenomena. Fascinate scree-runners. Elicit a range of interpretations from Tolmereans (gestating life, reality echoes, geological quirks). Their disappearance during veil-shifts (when reality becomes "solid") confirms their connection to the region's thin reality. The deep sub-audible vibration (*settling/becoming*) accompanies their presence or formation.

7.  **The Veil-Trees (Fungal Organisms):**
    *   **Nature:** While technically fungal, they function as the dominant macro-flora, forming "forests" in the lower valleys. They are sessile but profoundly active within the soundscape.
    *   **Appearance:** Columnar, 6-8 meters tall. Lacking true wood or bark. Surfaces draped in layers of pale, translucent tissue hanging like curtains. Produce heavy, dark fruit capsules.
    *   **Adaptations:** Fruit continuously; capsules dispersed by water channels. Tissue layers are adapted to capture moisture from the air and condensation. Their structural biology creates the resonant chambers producing the haunting organ-notes when wind passes through.
    *   **Ecology:** Provide structure and height in the ecosystem. Fruit is a food source for humans (fermented drink). Tissue might be used material. Their sound is a defining characteristic of the environment.

**What Lives There, Summarized:** This is a community of patience and adaptation. Humans practice symbiotic persistence with a geologically newborn world. Unique fauna, from the ethereal haze-drifters to the intelligent scree-runners and the foundational insects, form a food web reliant on chemosynthetic moss and mineral energy. Above all, the land itself *lives* – through the cooling rock gestating mysterious forms, the rising peaks, the shifting distances, and the ever-present mineral haze that both isolates and defines the inhabitants of the Forgebanks. They are all, in their ways, still becoming.
