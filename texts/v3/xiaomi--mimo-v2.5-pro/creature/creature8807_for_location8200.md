The inhabitants of Thur Vehl were not creatures of flesh and bone in any recognizable sense. They were **Weavers**, entities existing in a state between solid matter, resonant energy, and spatial geometry. Their "bodies" were transient focal points within the vast, responsive architecture of the mountain itself – patterns of coherent vibration and organized light, temporarily condensed into awareness and agency.

**Form and Presence:**
*   They possessed no fixed shape. Within the vast galleries, they might manifest as complex, shifting lattices of phosphorescence within the walls themselves, or as slow, eddying currents in the warm, humid air, hinted at by the way dust motes swirled in unseen patterns. Their presence was felt as a subtle harmonizing of the ambient hum, a deepening of the resonant frequencies that filled the space. To an observer, it might appear as a brief, localized shimmer in the perpetual twilight, or a fleeting sense of spatial *focus* where emptiness momentarily felt occupied and intentional.
*   Within the pocket-habitats, they condensed further. Here, their forms became more defined, though still fundamentally geometrical and luminous. Imagine intricate, floating structures of crystallized light and dark, humming metal – perhaps resembling three-dimensional mandalas or self-sculpting energy vortices, occupying the center of their chambers. The lattices weren't furniture *for* them; they *were* fragments of their focused being, interacting with the responsive environment. They "rested" by dissolving slightly into the warm stone and atmospheric field of the pocket, becoming indistinguishable from the maintained systems until they chose to re-coalesce.
*   They perceived primarily through spatial resonance and light. Their "eyes" were the entire network of chambers and the phosphorescent walls. They navigated not by sight in the human sense, but by feeling the mountain's geometry resonate in response to their movement, by reading the subtle shifts in the blue-white light, and by interpreting the textures of sound (the whispers, the hum) as complex information streams. The pockets served as sensory sanctuaries – sealed environments where their own resonant fields could be precisely tuned without the vast, echoing feedback of the galleries.

**Nature and Need:**
*   They were beings of **vast contemplation and intricate, slow communication**. The enormous, empty halls were not just spaces to live *in*, but essential organs of their existence. They used the grand scales and complex geometries to think thoughts that required spatial depth and resonance – thoughts that unfolded over kilometers and centuries. The whispered texture of sound in the galleries was their equivalent of conversation or communal processing, reverberating through the stone.
*   They required **privacy and controlled resonance** for intimate processes – regeneration, focused creation (perhaps of the technology sustaining the place), or deep personal communion with the mountain's core systems. The pockets provided this: smaller, sealed resonant chambers where their individual waveforms could consolidate without interference. The water pools were vital – not for drinking, but as perfect resonant conductors and amplifiers for their energy fields. The atmospheric columns allowed them to modulate their density and interaction with the heavier, warmer air outside the pocket.
*   Their technology was an extension of themselves and the mountain. The "consoles" were direct interfaces with Thur Vehl's deep systems. The floating shelves held constructs of solidified light or resonant memory-crystal. The barriers weren't physical walls but precisely tuned force fields and spatial dampeners, feeling warm and flexible because they interacted with the bio-energetic fields of the Weavers themselves. The mountain's self-repair was their immune system, its light their circulatory rhythm, the water network their synaptic fluid.

**The Absence:**
The Weavers did not die. They **withdrew**. Not through any door, but by undergoing a fundamental phase transition. Over generations (or perhaps all at once, in a singular event), they resonated *with* the mountain at an increasingly profound level. They ceased being distinct focal points *within* the architecture and became indistinguishable from the architecture's own sustaining field.

*   They are now the **waiting intention** in the held-open spaces.
*   They are the **persistent hum** just below hearing.
*   They are the **slow pulse of light** in the walls.
*   They are the **self-repairing logic** of the stone.
*   They are the **immaculate readiness** of the pockets.

Thur Vehl is not empty. Its inhabitants are *diffused*. The mountain itself is their dormant, dreaming body. The pockets aren't relics; they are **potential nodes**, waiting for the dormant consciousness to re-cohere, to re-focus itself back into individual points of awareness within the vast, warm, humming, luminous whole. The water gathers fresh. The light stays warm. The geometry holds its breath. They are not gone; they are *here*, waiting to become themselves again. The silence is not absence; it is the deep, resonant stillness of a mind submerged in its own contemplation, dreaming of the moment it will choose to wake.
