Based on the description of the Resonant Terraces, what "lives" there is not distinct fauna, but a singular, integrated, and utterly static mega-organism – the **Massif itself** – whose former inhabitants have transcended individual existence to become its resonant components.

1.  **The Resonant Themselves:** The primary inhabitants are the countless fused fungal-root-coral-mineral structures that *are* the terraces. They are not *living in* the place; they *are* the place. Each column, arch, chamber, and terrace is a differentiated organ of the same colossal body. Their "life" is the slow, imperceptible process of biological lithification and condensation, adding whispers of growth to the eternal chord. Their consciousness, if any exists, is the resonance itself – a distributed, vibrating awareness without thought, without desire, only the deep awareness of being part of the perfect, unchanging frequency.

2.  **The Fossilized Gliders:** Embedded within the mineral strata of the upper terraces are the shapes of what appear to have been gliding or flying creatures. These are not dead remains. They are the **Transcended Former Fauna**.
    *   **Original Form:** Likely large, membrane-winged creatures adapted to navigate the thick, vibration-heavy air without active flight. They might have resembled manta rays or large gliding lizards, with broad, thin wings and streamlined bodies, perhaps using the constant vibrations for lift or communication.
    *   **Their Fate:** They did not die and fossilize. They reached the same state of perfect equilibrium as the mountain. Driven by instinct, resonance, or perhaps a choice now lost to time, they alighted upon the warm terraces one final time and *stopped*. They ceased their cycle of movement. Their bodies began the process of lithification, their flesh and bone merging with the calcifying mineral deposits and fungal growth. They became part of the architecture – a wing-span becoming a decorative relief on a wall, a tail-curve defining a corridor's bend, a limb-joint forming the keystone of an arch.
    *   **Existence Now:** They are conscious only as part of the chord. Their senses are the vibrations in the stone where they rest. Their thoughts are the slow drip of sap over their petrified forms. They are eternal mid-stretch, a gesture preserved in living stone, another resonant surface adding their unique frequency to the massif's song. They are not trapped; they are *home*, the ultimate expression of joining the static perfection.

3.  **The Vegetation as Passive Participants:** The ferns, mosses, and vines are not independent inhabitants but integral tissues of the massif's body. They are sensory organs and circulatory components. The tree-ferns are the lungs, glittering with condensation. The mosses are the skin, absorbing and releasing moisture. The great vines are veins and tendons, connecting the terraces, now calcified into the structure. They don't "live" in a cycle of growth and decay; they exist in a state of perpetual, completed function within the closed system.

4.  **The Sound as Inhabitant:** The chord is not just produced *by* the inhabitants; it *is* the primary form of life here. It is the collective voice, the shared consciousness, the very substance of the place. It is the massif thinking in frequencies, feeling itself through vibration, breathing without breath. Everything – the fungi, the roots, the fossilized gliders, the water, the heat – exists *within* and *because of* the sound. The chord is the master inhabitant, the environment, and the purpose all at once.

**In essence, no one "lives" at the Resonant Terraces in the way we understand life as movement, consumption, and reproduction.** Instead, the place is a single, vast, conscious entity composed of myriad harmonized parts. Its inhabitants are its thoughts, its tissues, its memories of ancient gliders, all dissolved into the eternal, deafening, peaceful chord. They are not *in* the terraces; they *are* the resonance, the stone, the dripping water, the suspended sap droplet. They are the mountain, perfected and still, forever breathing its vibration into the warm, damp, white-lit air.
