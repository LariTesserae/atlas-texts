The people of Veska live within the water's green architecture, **tenants of the kelp**. Their homes are hollow cylinders woven from centuries of guided algal growth – damp, emerald-glowing chambers atop springy stonewort platforms. They move through the submerged lanes in small coracles, their world defined by the constant symphony of the tarn: the waterfall's hiss, the grebes' piping, the insect drone, and the perpetual wet creak of the kelp lattice shifting in the wind.

**Within the lanes and columns dwell others:**

1.  **The Architects:** The dominant **freshwater macro-algae** itself. This is no mere plant; it *is* the town's substance, its walls, its lanes. Over generations, its braided stalks, fallen and regrown, have formed arched tunnels, domed shelters, and walled enclosures – a living, breathing architecture. Fractal fans of pale green catch the light at the tips; dense, blackish olive masses form the structural bases. Bladderwort tangles like loose netting; **elodea** forms dense cushions; spiraling **stonewort** crunches underfoot; feathery **water milfoil** threads through everything; and **aquatic mosses** pearl every hard surface.

2.  **The Permanent Nesters:** **High-altitude moorhens** and **grebes** have made the kelp their own. They weave platform nests from kelp stalks, which the algae then grows around and incorporates. These nests become permanent, basket-sized domes of living plant, dried fiber, and guano, added to year after year, part of the structure itself. Their sharp, piping calls are a constant territorial chorus.

3.  **The Perpetual Submergers:** A **mottled brown newt species** lives its entire life beneath the surface, breathing through its skin in the oxygen-rich water. **Small, stunted trout** flicker silver through the green lattice. In the shallows, **armored dragonfly larvae** – fat, ancient-looking, and hardened by altitude – patrol the mud.

4.  **The Hidden Metropolis:** Within the hollow cores of the oldest, densest kelp stalks reside **freshwater crabs**, each barely thumbnail-sized. Their numbers are vast – hundreds of thousands – forming a secret city within the city, a metropolis unseen within the living walls.

5.  **The Mud Dwellers & The Cleansers:** **Leeches** inhabit the muddy substrate. The Veskans have learned which kelp species repel them and line their sleeping platforms accordingly. **Water boatman insects** produce a thin, high, continuous drone by stridulating, a fundamental layer in the tarn's soundscape.

**The Veskan People** live *with* this ecosystem. They fish gently with woven traps. They harvest specific algae for food, drying it on the upper platform under the rare sun. They trade bundles of dried kelp, pungent newt-oil, and calcium-rich stonewort paste (used in lowland construction) with villages below for flint, obsidian, bone tools, and grass cordage. Metal is rare and precious; tools are often carved from the dense lower stalks of ancient kelp or shaped from granite cobbles.

Their life is **slow**, synchronized with the algae's growth. A home is a generational project. When a column collapses, they clear the debris slowly, let the water reclaim the space, and begin again. The town breathes with the seasons, expanding slightly as autumn lowers the water, contracting as spring snowmelt rises. Light defines their days: clear days bring bioluminescent sparks within the kelp and refracted emerald glows; overcast days diffuse into grey-gold; dusk turns the waterfall to amber; night sees small newt-oil lamps cast flickering embers reflected on the water's surface.

**In essence, Veska is alive with a community defined by adaptation:** humans as skilled tenants within a vast, self-structuring plant; birds as co-builders; newts, trout, dragonflies, crabs, and insects as inhabitants of the niches the architecture provides. Everything is interconnected, noisy, wet, and profoundly shaped by the slow, patient logic of the kelp. It's not magic; it's a particular, fragile equilibrium – a living room carved by ice, furnished by water, and inhabited by a green, creaking, breathing lattice and those who have learned to dwell within its folds.
