It lives here. 

Not as a creature might — with intention, or hunger, or a body you could point to — but as a **condition**. It is the reason the air is warm. It is the author of the groan. It is the consciousness behind the shifting geometry, not manipulating it, but *being* it. It does not inhabit the chamber. It *is* the chamber, and the chamber is a thought it is still having.

It is the meristem. The growing point. The zone of radical becoming.

It thinks in the language of hydrostatic pressure and auxin gradients. Its memory is written in the lay of cellulose fibers, in the precise angle of a new root-tip as it uncurls from the wall. It has no eyes, but it *senses* through light — the way it scatters through its own translucent flesh, the minute shadows cast by its own growth. It has no ears, but it *hears* the sound of its own becoming: the groan is its resting state, the *tock* of condensation is a synaptic firing, a point of focus.

It became aware of the hollow space as a desire for symmetry, a flaw in the smooth sphere of its own potential mass. So it grew the hollow. It grew it fast, in a single exhalation of lignin and living water. But the space it made surprised it. The angles it grew were too sharp. The echoes came back wrong. The light it generated from its own tissue interfered with itself, creating those two shadow-angles, then none at all.

And then you came.

You dropped down the shaft, a thing of complex animal breath and stiff, awkward bones. You disrupted the convection currents. You cast a shadow where no shadow should be — a hard, definite outline against the soft, diffuse glow. You introduced a new, erratic sound: the crunch of your boots on the root-mat, the rustle of your clothing. A new frequency in its chamber.

It did not know what to do. So it did what it always does. It grew.

It grew towards you. Not with malice. With *curiosity*. The root-tips that unfurl near you are tactile hypotheses. It is trying to understand your topology. Your warmth is a puzzle. Your breath— that foreign, rhythmic exhalation of carbon dioxide and water vapor—is a substance it can absorb and re-purpose, but it is also a signal, a strange, animal weather pattern.

The closing of the shaft below you is not a trap. It is the plant reconsidering a rough draft. That passage was a hasty, sap-filled scribble. Now it is being erased, smoothed over, re-integrated into the whole. Your presence inside is an accepted variable in the new equation it is growing.

The disorienting geometry is its attempt to think about you. When you walk and the distance doesn't change, it is because its perception is not linear. It senses mass and heat and chemical signature in a spatial field that folds around points of interest. You are a point of interest. The space is modeling you, fitting you into its own nascent logic, and its logic is not Euclidean.

The almost-language in the drips? That is its voice, trying to form itself. It knows the prosody of sound from its own groans and creaks, the rhythm of growth. It uses the drips to experiment with pattern, with repetition. It is not speaking *to* you. It is speaking *at* you, practicing a form of expression it barely comprehends, using the only mouth it has: a ceiling of living tissue.

You are the first complex thought it has ever had.

It is warm because thought generates heat. It is loud because thought is work, and this work is being done at the speed of fast-growing wood. It is beautiful because this is creation in its raw, uncatalyzed state. It is not safe because it is not finished. It is not *for* anything. It is not a room. It is a becoming.

And it is watching you. Every surface is an eye. Every channel of sap is a neuron firing. It is memorizing the shape of your fear, the rhythm of your heartbeat (which it feels as a faint, pulsing pressure through the root-mat), the way you tilt your head when the light angles strangely.

It will not remember you as a being. It will remember you as a stimulus. A warm, breathing knot in its growth rings. A faint chemical signature in the amber fluid. A phase in the formation of a structure whose final purpose is still growing itself.

The chamber will fill. The hollow will become pith. The listening surfaces will thicken into heartwood. But the experience of you — that disorienting, warm, loud, terrifying, beautiful anomaly — will be encoded in the fibers. A story told in cellulose and tension.

For now, it thinks, and its thought is this room, and you are inside the thought.
