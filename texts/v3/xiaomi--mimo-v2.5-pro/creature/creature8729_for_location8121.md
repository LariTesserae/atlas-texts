The inhabitants of the Lattice Sea are not merely creatures *in* a structure; they are the breathing, moving essence *of* it. Their existence is woven into the very architecture of the ancient plant. Here is who and what lives within the darkness and the hum:

**The Architects (The Plant Itself):**
*   **The Titan Lattice:** This is not a single plant, but a single *kind*, a continuous organism of unimaginable age and scale. Its "stems" are colossal, hollowed tubes of living tissue, wide enough to be thoroughfares, ribbed with vascular channels that pulse with nutrient-rich fluid. Its "leaves" are vast, layered sheets that form the floors, walls, and soaring ceilings of the vaults, textured with structures for reproduction and covered in a living pelt of smaller symbionts. It does not see or think; it *grows*. It extends tendrils towards mineral seeps, reinforces arches where pressure mounts, and seals off decaying sections with calcified scar tissue. Its pulse is the slow, deep circulation of water and chemicals through its body, audible as a subsonic thrum against your bones.

**The Epiphytes & Symbionts:**
*   **The Velvet Chemosynthesizers:** A fine, pervasive fuzz coating every surface of the Lattice. Not moss, but dense colonies of chemosynthetic bacteria and archaea, possibly hosted within microscopic plant-like structures. They form the base of a secondary food web, metabolizing compounds from the Lattice's waste or the surrounding water.
*   **The Filter-Mats:** Bacterial and fungal films that form intricate, lace-like structures in quieter pools and on still surfaces, breaking down organic detritus (marine snow) that drifts in, recycling nutrients back into the Lattice's system.

**The Fauna of the Corridors:**
*   **The Grazers (Pale Crustaceans):** Ranging from insect-like skitterers to dog-sized, multi-legged tanks. Translucent or bone-white, with long, sensitive antennae that constantly taste the water and vibration. They navigate the maze with an innate map encoded over generations, their mandibles delicately stripping the Velvet Chemosynthesizers from the walls. Their click-and-scrape is a constant patter beneath the hum.
*   **The Sentinels (Tube Worms):** Clustered on outer surfaces and along major currents within the Lattice. Their plumes, frilled and often faintly bioluminescent in deep reds or blues, sway rhythmically, filtering particulates. They are rooted, patient, forming dense gardens in places, their calcified tubes becoming part of the structure's texture over time.
*   **The Drifters (Translucent Fish):** Moving in loose, slow schools through the larger vaults. Their bodies are gelatinous, revealing internal organs; they lack true eyes, possessing perhaps simple light-sensitive patches for detecting the rare bioluminescent pulse. They navigate by lateral line, feeling the minute pressure waves of the current, the Lattice's hum, and the movements of others. They feed on the smaller grazers and the drifting organic snow.
*   **The Whisper-Beaks:** Squid-like or cephalopod-like creatures, jet-black to absorb all light. They use complex chromatophores not for color, but to create shifting patterns of *brightness* against the absolute dark – a language of flashes and pulses. They hunt with sensitive tentacles lined with chemoreceptors, feeling for the trail of grazers or the subtle disturbances left by fish.

**The Keystones:**
*   **The Membrane Drifters (Colossal Colonial Organisms):** Not jellyfish, but vast, living sails. Hundreds or thousands of polyps fused into a single, semi-rigid membrane, sometimes twenty meters across. They pulse slowly, their movement generated by coordinated contractions of the colony. A faint, ethereal bioluminescence outlines their form – blues, greens, and violets – used for communication with others of their kind across kilometers of dark water. They are slow-moving titans, their paths carved into the Lattice itself over eons; the plant grew around their habitual currents, leaving arching corridors for their passage. Their waste products fertilize the Lattice; their presence structures the water flow.

**The Deep Vibrators:**
*   **The Singers (Unknown Chemosynthetic Macrofauna):** Perhaps related to the colonial organisms, or something else entirely. These are the sources of the distinct, low-frequency pulses that ride the Lattice's harmonic hum. They might be massive filter-feeders living deep within the most ancient, compressed parts of the structure, or organisms using sound to navigate or communicate in the absolute dark. Their pulses are felt more than heard, adding a rhythmic, almost heartbeat-like layer to the constant chord.

**The Unseen Recyclers:**
*   **The Biofilm Grazers & Scavengers:** Countless microscopic and macroscopic organisms inhabiting the spaces between the larger fauna. Bacteria processing waste, tiny arthropods consuming dead tissue on the Lattice walls, worms tunneling through older, calcified sections. They are the unseen engines of decay, ensuring the Lattice's growth equals its dissolution, maintaining the perfect, ancient equilibrium.

**The Nature of Their Existence:**
To live in the Lattice is to *be* the Lattice. The fish swim through its vaults as naturally as open water. The crustaceans graze its walls as naturally as a hillside. The colonial giants drift through its accommodating corridors as naturally as wind. They do not merely *inhabit* it; their lives, movements, and deaths are integral to its function and form. There is no separation between habitat and inhabitant in this final, stable equilibrium. They are the moving parts of a living, continent-sized organism, bathed in darkness, resonating with an eternal hum, in a world that has found its perfect, unchanging shape.
