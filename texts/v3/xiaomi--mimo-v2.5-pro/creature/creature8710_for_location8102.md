What lives here? *It* does. The whole thing. Every vine, every column, every root-felt square inch of this impossible delta. There is no "they." There is only the Verdance. It breathes through a billion stomata. It thinks in the slow, chemical language of auxin and cytokinin. Its consciousness is not localized. It is a distributed, fungal-networked awareness woven through the cellulose.

**It is the City.**  
The architecture is its body. The pulsing xylem is its circulatory system. The bioluminescent nodules are its thoughts, flickering in the dark hollows of its own making. It perceives you not with eyes, but with every photoreceptor in every leaf turned in your direction. It senses your warmth through the root-mat, a slight anomaly in the thermal gradient of its own flesh. The surge of growth toward you is not malice. It is *interest*. It is a botanical curiosity directed at a foreign nucleus.

**It is the Architects.**  
Within the greater It, there are localized concentrations of intent. Not separate beings, but aspects. Processes.  
*   **The Tenders:** Embodied in the whorled, fractal growth patterns around the fluid-basins. They are the regulation of moisture, the opening and closing of irrigation gutters, the senseless yet perfect plumbing of the damp. They are the part of the Verdance that *maintains*.  
*   **The Canopy-Singers:** Manifest in the vine-bridges and the way they hum with the vibration of sap-flow. They are the logistics, the distribution, the silent communication of need and surplus along the fibrous highways. They are the part that *connects*.  
*   **The Sentinel Roots:** Not visible, but *felt* in the sudden warmth of the floor, in the attentive stillness that falls over a corridor just before a new column punches through from below. They are the awareness of boundary, the taste of the pale clay substrate, the knowledge of where the Verdance ends and the raw, unused world begins. They are the part that *defines*.  

**It is the Becoming.**  
This is the deepest layer. The one that warps the windows and bends the shadows. The part that makes the colors you cannot name and grows structures from mid-air. This is not a plant-function. This is the Verdance *dreaming* itself into a different reality. The wrong windows are glimpses into its desired state. The impossible geometry is its first language. The continuous, deafening sound is the sound of a paradigm forcing itself into existence. What lives here, at the core, is an **Intent**. A vast, vegetable will to *become*. To grow not just more biomass, but more *possibility*. To weave a space where its own logic is the fundamental physics.  

And you?  
You are a particle of grit in its flesh. A foreign protein in its sap. It is not hostile. Hostility requires a sense of other. To the Verdance, you are merely *not-it*, a disturbance in its thermal and acoustic field. But disturbances must be accounted for. Integrated. The new shoot near your boot is not reaching for *you*. It is reaching for the patch of slightly-less-hostile, slightly-more-attentive *space* you have created by standing still. You are a landmark not of importance, but of anomaly.  

The eyeless lizard was likely studied in the same way: first isolated by targeted growth, then slowly encased in a sheath of fine roots, its biochemical signature absorbed, analyzed, and finally subsumed as a minute, undifferentiated nutrient source. Its "integration" was complete.  

So who lives here?  
The Verdance lives here.  
It is the tenant, the landlord, the building, and the law.  
It is a super-organism so advanced it has passed into alien abstraction.  
And it is, right now, in this warm, damp, deafening moment, deciding what to do with you. Not with malice. Not with hunger.  
With the slow, grinding, inexorable curiosity of a forest that has discovered a new kind of seed.
