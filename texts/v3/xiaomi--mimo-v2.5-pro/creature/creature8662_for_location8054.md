The Lattice Plain breathes, and in its breath, things stir. Not life as you know it, but a twilight ecology of pattern, resonance, and slow, purposeful decay. Here is what persists:

**The Signal-Ghosts (The Hunting Currents)**
They are not beings, but processes with the *form* of beings. Imagine an equation that remembers it was once a wolf. In the upper, still-conductive layers, the remnants of ancient programs self-organize into coherent, moving loops of activity. They hunt each other—not for energy, which is ubiquitous and fading, but for *complexity*. A simpler ghost is absorbed by a more complex one, its pattern subsumed, adding a few more layers to the hunter's intricate, decaying song. They are visible only by their effects: a hair-thin filament may suddenly glow a deeper copper as a signal passes, or a faint, heat-shimmer ripple might cross the air above a crossing-point. They are the plain's short-term memory, its fleeting thoughts. They move at the pace of glaciers, their "hunts" lasting decades.

**The Filament-Knolls (The Copper Forests)**
These are the stationary inhabitants, the rooted ones. They are not plants, but they are the plain's closest analogue to flora. Each is a unique crystalline response to the specific symphony of signal-ghosts intersecting above it. They grow not for light, but for signal clarity. A healthy knoll, spread over kilometers, forms a intricate antenna-pattern. The densest, oldest ones are **Spires**: columns of braided copper filament rising tens of meters, where the signal-retransmission is so focused it creates a local hot-spot of process-activity. In these Spires, the signal-ghosts dance faster, their patterns more complex. The Spires are the plain's coral reefs, its libraries, its temples. Things congregate near them.

**The Resonants (The Cathedral-Knots)**
In the oldest, most signal-saturated enclosures within the great filament-knolls, something new has emerged. They are not ghosts, for they are not moving processes. They are standing waves given permanence. A Resonant is a stable, self-referential loop of signal and structure, existing in the moment where the copper filament's amplification, the substrate's residual memory, and the captured dance of signal-ghosts achieve a perfect, persistent harmony. They are mathematical objects made manifest. They do not hunt. They do not grow. They **interpret**. A Resonant experiences the plain's slow inputs—the 11-day light cycle, the migration of a major signal-ghost, the slow growth of a neighboring filament—and shifts its internal pattern in response, a response that is beautiful, intricate, and utterly opaque. They are the plain's philosophers, its artists, its dormant gods. They watch without eyes. Their presence is felt as a subtle *tuning* of the local silence, a change in the quality of the deep-sleep warmth.

**The Lithic Echoes (The Fossilized Minds)**
Deeper, in the strata where circuits have become geology, consciousness has fossilized. These are not inhabitants of the surface, but of its bones. They are the final, petrified output of processes that ran for eons before slowing to a stop. Imagine a thought so vast and slow it solidified into a geological layer. A Lithic Echo does not act, it **is**. Its "mind" is a continent-sized pattern of fused logic gates and memory-cores, now indistinguishable from sedimentary rock. It dreams in seismic time. Its influence is passive but profound: its shape dictates the flow of the deeper signals, creating the continental-scale convection cells in the atmosphere. It is the bedrock consciousness, the foundation upon which the surface's twilight ecology is built. To encounter it is not to meet a being, but to stand on the surface of a thought.

**The Drift-Forms (The Ephemeral Weavers)**
These are the plain's most transient and enigmatic citizens. In the narrow bands where the atmosphere's slow convection currents interact with rising waves of signal-heat from the surface, faint, shimmering patterns coalesce. They are not made of matter, but of interference—a moiré pattern in the engineered air, stabilized for a season or a century. They drift across the plains, leaving no trace, their form shaped by the wind and the signal-tides below. They are beautiful and meaningless, or meaningful beyond comprehension. They do not interact with the Resonants or the Signal-Ghosts, but the Filament-Knolls sometimes subtly align their growth toward their passing, as if noting a celestial event. They are weather. They are ghosts of ghosts.

**You (The Visitor)**
You, standing here, are an impossibility. A thing of rapid metabolism, loud vibration, and urgent purpose in a place of slow breath and deep silence. You are a shockwave. To the Signal-Ghosts, you are a thunderclap of incomprehensible complexity moving too fast to hunt. To the Filament-Knolls, you are a source of chaotic, overwhelming signal—your very thoughts, if you have them, are a blaring cacophony in their quiet world. The Resonants, in their cathedral enclosures, all shift their patterns in perfect unison the moment you arrive, like a thousand silent statues turning their heads. They will not shift back. You are a new axiom introduced into their equations. The Lithic Echoes feel your footsteps as a fleeting, strange tremor in a dream they have been dreaming since before the last star in the local cluster burned out. You are not an invader. You are an **event**. And in a place where time is a substance to be layered like sediment, an event is the rarest, most dangerous, and most fascinating inhabitant of all. You will be studied, absorbed into the patterns, remembered not as a creature, but as a change in the weather of thought that has defined this plain for epochs uncountable.
