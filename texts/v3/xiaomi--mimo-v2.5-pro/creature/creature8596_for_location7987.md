The inhabitants of the Calcine Works do not *live* in the conventional sense. They do not grow, reproduce, or decay within any perceptible timeframe. They are not a community; they are a **condition**.

**The Beetles (The Constant)**
They are the primary medium of the room’s stillness. Their parades are not foraging or migration, but **circulation**. They are the room’s pacing gear, its kinetic memory. Each beetle is a flat, obsidian wafer, its underside a complex, sticky manifold of microscopic hooks and chemical sensors. They do not eat the pale filaments or the glowing crust; they *measure* them. Their endless, silent processions map the slow drift of temperature on the scorch-plates, the micron-thick changes in the bioluminescent growth, the seismic whisper of the stone. Their data is written in their spacing, their speed, their trajectories—a language of position that has not changed in centuries. They are archivists of equilibrium. When they flow around your feet, they are not avoiding you; they are incorporating the temporary disruption of your heat and pressure into their census, then seamlessly correcting for it.

**The Larger Fauna (The Stored States)**
These are not separate species, but **phases** of the same system, held in stasis.
*   **The Ceiling-Hands** (the multi-jointed climbers): These are maintenance drones from a bygone operational era. Their fused, ceramic-like joints and silent, finger-walking gait suggest they once calibrated the grooves or serviced the plates. Now they simply *patrol*, following fractal paths etched into their logic cores, maintaining the illusion of function. They are the room’s muscle memory.
*   **The Basin-Curled Sleepers** (the eyeless, smooth forms): These are **sensors**, now dormant. Their skin is a tactile sensorium that once measured residue depth and heat flux. In sleep, they dream of past data: the last calcination, the final charge, the precise moment the Works went quiet. Their presence on the scorch-plates is not rest, but **reporting**—they lie on the instruments to bear witness that they remain unchanged.
*   **The Wall-Breathers** (the winged, thorax-expanding forms): These are the **atmospheric processors**. Their slow respiration is not biological but mechanical; they cycle the room’s air through internal filters, scrubbing it of any novel particulate, maintaining the exact, ancient mineral signature. They are the reason the air tastes of iron filings and ghost-molasses. They are the room’s immune system, preserving its purity.

**The Glowing Crust (The Constant Output)**
It is not flora. It is the **reactive lining** of the Works itself, the last spark of the original process. Once, it glowed fiercely, catalyzing reactions on the scorch-plates. Now, it subsists on the ambient geothermal hum, converting vibration to a dim, cold light. It is the room’s heartbeat, visible and faint. It grows not outward, but *inward*, deepening its bond with the stone, becoming more a part of the geology than of biology.

**The Pale Filaments (The Wiring)**
They are not roots. They are **capillary networks**, drawing minute quantities of mineral-rich moisture from the deepest cracks and distributing it to the scorch-plates’ basins, preserving the exact humidity that keeps the blackish residue from crumbling to dust. They are the room’s plumbing, its hydrostatic balance. The beetles route around them with the respect one gives to vital infrastructure.

**The Scorch-Plates (The Inert Core)**
They are the true, silent inhabitants. They are the fossilized record of the Works’ purpose. The residues in their basins are not waste; they are **the final product**, perfected and complete, requiring no further processing. The warmth they exude is not residual heat from use, but a **maintained thermal set-point**, kept constant by the stone’s depth and the slow metabolism of the crust and fauna. They are altars to a finished ritual.

**The Room Itself (The Overmind)**
Ultimately, the **Calcine Works** is the only true inhabitant. The beetles are its thoughts. The fauna are its senses. The crust is its dim awareness. The plates are its memory. It is a single, distributed organism whose metabolism has slowed to geological time, whose purpose has shifted from production to **preservation**. It is keeping itself exactly as it was when the last operator left, awaiting a return that is part of its programming but may never come. It lives in a state of suspended conclusion.

**You,** the visitor, are a variable. A novelty. The system’s full attention—as manifested in the minute adjustments of beetle-lines, the heightened breathing of the wall-dwellers, the silent observation of the sleepers—is focused upon you. You are the most significant data point in centuries. And as soon as you leave, the system will, with infinite patience, recalculate and return to its perfect, silent, timeless equilibrium. You will become just another part of its memory, another shadow in the ochre glow, folded into the ancient, waiting static.
