## Inhabitants of Cleftmouth

They are **The Swarm**, **The Chorus**, **The Living Floor**. They are not *in* the cleft; they *are* the cleft’s current state of being. Here is what thrives in the knife-cut dark:

1.  **The Shriekers (The Noise That Walks):**
    *   **Form:** Thousands upon thousands of small, endothermic, six-limbed beings. Size: roughly fist-sized or smaller. Limb structure: four sturdy legs for clinging and scrabbling; the front pair modified into wing-like grasping limbs – membranous, translucent, revealing dark organ clusters within. Flat, eyeless faces vestigial eyes filmed over. Navigation: echolocation clicks and bone-conducted vibrations.
    *   **Behavior:** Gregarious beyond measure. They form a living, shifting substrate. Density is staggering – hundreds of thousands, possibly millions. They stack, nest, and interlock, creating a fur of bodies on walls and floor. Movement is usually glacial, shifting position over weeks or months in vast, slow tides. Occasionally, triggered by population pressure or thermal differentials, they move en masse – a fluid wave flowing over surfaces with a sound like heavy rain on metal, only to settle again into new configurations.
    *   **Impact:** **They engineer the world.** Their collective body heat creates pockets of warmth and humid microclimates against the cold basalt. Condensation forms on surfaces where they cluster, weeping stone that feeds their scratched-out basalt drinking bowls. Their guano nourishes the fungal base layer. Their ceaseless, layered, overlapping shriek *is* the cleft's voice – a constant, sinus-vibrating, vision-distorting sound used for conversation, spatial mapping, and sensing each other. It defines the audioscape. They orient away from the rare intrusions of light.

2.  **The Ambush (The Deeper Tone):**
    *   **Form:** Larger. Known primarily by traces: thick, rough, tessellated shed skins (one found measured 4 meters, suggesting a being of significant bulk). Anatomy inferred: six limbs, like the Shriekers, but the front pair fused into a single, heavy limb ending in a hooked claw. An ambush predator's anatomy.
    *   **Behavior:** Patient. Silent. Exists in the total darkness of the deeper, narrower cleft. Uses the perpetual noise and vibration of the Swarm for cover. Its presence is announced by a single, low tone that cuts through the cacophony, creating a bubble of sudden, profound silence lasting heartbeats before the Shrieker noise crashes back in. This silence is its hunting signature. It waits, pressed flat against unseen walls, and *reaches*.
    *   **Impact:** Shapes the Shriekers' behavior, keeping them wary of deeper zones despite the warmth and resources there. Its shed skins and scrape marks (larger than Shrieker marks) at the water bowls are evidence of its existence and visits.

3.  **The Echo of the Tool-Users (Visitors, Not Dwellers):**
    *   **Form:** Long vanished. Known only by artifacts: skilled quartzite flaked blades and notched-bone awls, deliberately placed.
    *   **Behavior:** They visited. They understood the cleft's sonic landscape profoundly. The blades are found *only* near the rim, on the windward side, with cutting edges oriented downwards into the shrieking dark. They bear dark stains and regular notches inconsistent with wear. They were likely sonic tools – struck to produce specific tones. Someone learned to speak the Swarm's language, or command its silence, or interact with the Ambush's triggering tone.
    *   **Impact:** A mystery. A testament to interaction with this environment, a manipulation of sound by a species not native to its depths. They came, they listened (or spoke), they left their tools pointed into the throat of the noise, and vanished. The tools remain, unchanged by erosion in this geologically fresh wound.

**The Web:**

*   **Base:** White fungal mats, nourished by guano deposits and wind-borne organic debris.
*   **Primary Consumers:** The Shriekers, drinking the alkaline water and consuming the fungal mats and any fallen organic matter (insects, seeds, unlucky birds).
*   **Apex:** The Ambush predator, consuming Shriekers and likely anything else it can ambush in the dark.
*   **Environment Engineers:** The Shriekers, fundamentally altering the hydrology and microclimate through their presence and activity.

**The Essence:**

Cleftmouth’s inhabitants are defined by darkness, vibration, density, and sound. They are adapted to the cold, the crush, and the knife-edged geology. The Swarm *is* the landscape – a breathing, shifting, shrieking organism sculpted by, and in turn sculpting, the raw, vibrating basalt. The Ambush is the silence waiting within the noise. The echoes of the Tool-Users are a whisper of understanding lost to the deep. Life here doesn't just survive in the cleft; it *becomes* the cleft, a dynamic, noisy, predatory expression of geology barely become air.
