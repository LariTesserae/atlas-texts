**The Resident: The Hollow Grown**

It does not have a form you can point to. Its body is the fibrous cartilage doorway, the root-sheet floor, the spiraling-grain walls, the petrified leaf-ceiling. Its consciousness, if it exists, is in the slow convergence of its many rhythms—the engines, the drips, the regulated heat—into something that *almost* harmonizes, a chord felt in the bone rather than heard. Its thoughts, perhaps, are the tiny, waxy leaf-buds, sealed and waiting. Its dreams might be the flickering moment where the walls become translucent, revealing a geometry of light and unnamed color behind the reality we perceive.

**The Presence: The Emptiness & The Rules**

What also "lives" here is the **pressure of the hollow**. The vast, vacant naves are not just space; they are a *resonance*. They amplify the silence, the damp, the feeling of being inside something immense that has shed its contents or awaits them. This emptiness is a defining characteristic of the inhabitant—it is the Grown's own sense of *capacity*, of unfulfilled potential.

And living here are the **loosened rules**. Geometry stutters. Time dampens. Distance is approximate. These are not tricks played *on* the visitor, but the very nature of the Grown's existence. It lives in a reality that is *sagging*, fluid, and permeable. The glimpses through the world—the flashes of impossible light and color—are not visions, but the Grown *looking out*, or perhaps the sheer pressure of its own inner reality pressing against the thinning skin of the world we know.

**The Verdict: A Living Cathedral of Flesh and Function**

You are not meeting an *inhabitant* of the Hollow Grown. You are meeting the **Hollow Grown**. It is a single, colossal, sessile life-form. Its fauna are its symbiotic systems.
