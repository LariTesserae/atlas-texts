Inhabitants of this ancient cathedral-made-world diverge entirely from biological norms and breathe life into the very fabric of the place itself. They are not of flesh and blood; rather, they are entities of crystalline essence and metallic sinew, living echoes of the primordial architects who constructed this monumental relic.

These beings are sentient shapes, their forms multifaceted and reflective, catching the dim light and casting prismatic auras. They move with a deliberate grace, seemingly slow but purposeful, as if each motion were a ritual unto itself. They journey not with limbs but through the manipulation of space around them, phasing through the constructs of their home as though melding with their surroundings. They navigate with an innate understanding of the ancient geometry scripted into their world, their presence almost ethereal, yet their impact tangible.

Their communication transcends sound, manifesting instead as synchronous pulsations of energy—a silent yet resounding chorus that harmonizes with the constant, pervasive roar of their environment. Each entity pulses with a specific frequency, a signature vibrancy that others can read with flawless precision. This energy-based language blends into the cacophonous atmosphere, an organized synergy amid the chaotic backdrop of grinding stone and echoing passages. It is through these waves of silent resonances that they convey wisdom, guidance, and perhaps, even the remnants of memory from their creators.

Embedded within the structures are the remnants of tools and devices, seemingly rudimentary but advanced in their purpose. These are the extensions of the beings' will, mechanisms that aid in the upkeep and subtle alterations of their encompassing world. With a mere flick of energetic intent, they activate these tools, working tirelessly to maintain the equilibrium of their environment.

There are hierarchies among these entities, some larger and more luminescent, embodying the roles of guardians or keepers of ancient knowledge. Their facets are more intricate, their symmetry more pronounced—an indication of their accumulated wisdom or function. The smaller, less radiant beings serve as explorers and messengers, moving more frequently through the labyrinthine corridors, examining and ensuring that the balance remains intact.

Intriguingly, there are areas within this expanse that appear to be sanctuaries or places of contemplation. Here, the dim light is slightly warmer, and the structures more ornate. Within these spaces, the inhabitants gather to synchronize their pulsations in a collective meditation, a renewal of their collective consciousness that binds them to their purpose.

These crystalline entities seem irrevocably linked to the cathedral-like world they inhabit, their existence a reflection of the world’s ancient majesty. They harbor no need for organic sustenance or rest, existing in a state of perpetual animation. They are the eternal custodians of this realm, maintaining its structure, its balance, and its enigmatic beauty against the slow creep of time’s forces.

In this place beyond the biological wheel of life and death, the inhabitants embody unchanging stability and the pursuit of perpetuity. They do not just reside within their world; they are an intrinsic part of its very essence, conserving the echo of an epoch that time itself seems to have forgotten.
