In the enigmatic depths of this biotechnological ordeal, life pulses vehemently, both remarkably alien and eerily familiar.

Emerging from the labyrinthine shadows are elongated, serpentine creatures. Their bodies, covered in iridescent scales that mimic the crystalline fungi, seem to ripple and shift in tune with the cavern's bioluminescent glow. These serpents are the apex explorers of this microcosm, their numerous, multifaceted eyes absorbing the environment’s electromagnetic spectra, enabling them to navigate the infrared heat waves and unseen magnetic fields that delineate their subterranean sanctuary. They are silent orators of the stone, and their coiled movements etch impeccably along the treacherous labyrinth, their anatomy adapted for climbing rough surfaces and slipping into narrow crevices. 

Sparse though the fauna may be, the cavern houses several species of diminutive, luminescent insectoids which flit through the air in scattered bursts of neon-blue and violet. Their fragile wings beat with an almost silent hum produced by rapid, synchronous oscillations. This resonance appears partially attuned to the frequency emitted by the cavern’s embedded nodes, suggesting a symbiotic relationship with the ancient artifacts. These flickering beings seem to live ephemeral lives, their primary function appearing to be as pollinators for the crystalline fungi, thus stitching together the biological and technological fabric of this place.

Buried deeper into the sedimented layers of rock are the dwellers unseen: small, tripedal creatures with conical heads and unblinking, compound eyes. Their chitinous exoskeletons provide them with resilience against the desiccating heat and acidic bursts that occasionally emerge from the floor fissures. They consume the mineral-rich sediment with a slow, methodical precision, breaking it down with dual sets of hardened mandibles and growing bioluminescent protrusions along their spines. These scintillating bursts serve as lures for the caverns’ smaller, unsuspecting dwellers.

Within the extensive pools, elongated aquatic organisms navigate their liquid domain with a practiced fluidity. They possess bi-flagellate tails and soft, translucent bodies, their coloration morphing based on environmental stimuli and potential threats. These water-dwellers also feature porous skin which not only aids in respiration but also mediates the mineral exchange with their aqueous habitat, hinting at an evolutionary marvel far removed from our surface-bound aquatics.

There is another presence, more elusive and far more enigmatic—the sentient remnants of the once-dominant intelligences that installed the technological relics. These entities, perhaps no longer corporeal, seem to exist as consciousness suffused into the very latticework of the cavern’s substrate. Occasionally, an almost imperceptible shimmer passes through the vaults of the stone and fungi, a fleeting suggestion of ethereal figures or echoes of light patterns that don't conform to the cavern's natural phosphorescence. It's as though they persist in a state of semi-awareness, maintaining the balance and order of this ecosystem, nudging its inhabitants along the paths of their evolution through subtle manipulations of the cavern's climate and resources.

The technology is ancient, yet sublimely advanced. It’s in continuous, real-time interaction with every aspect of the cavern's environment. Embedded nodes, which resonantly hum with focused energy, double as both regulators and historians, subtly recording every fluctuation in the cavern and its inhabitants' progression. Some of these devices project shimmering fields of energy that seem to bolster the fungal growths or stabilize the structure of the rocky terrain, preventing its collapse under the great pressures of the subterrane.

Together, the crystalline, mineral, organic, and technological weave themselves into an interdependent web of existence. The harsh luminance, coupled with the insistent steam and sulfuric tang, crafts a unique habitat where super-evolved resilience and ancient wisdom converge. The serpentine explorers, firefly-like insectoids, tripedal denizens, aquatic morphers, and sentient residue form a coherent ecosystem that speaks of an era simultaneously primordial and post-apocalyptic, eternally enshrined in its humid, glowing oasis.
