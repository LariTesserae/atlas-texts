Deep beneath the waves, within the surreal confines of the submerged city of Nebularia, life flourishes in a captivating interplay of biodiversity and symbiosis. This unique underwater settlement houses an eclectic array of inhabitants, their lives deeply interwoven with the verdant environment around them.

**Aquatic Humans:**

The primary residents of Nebularia are a unique group of humanoids adapted to underwater life. These beings, emerging from generations of cohabitation with the deep sea, possess certain extraordinary traits. They exhibit webbed hands and feet, and their skin is thick and smooth with a greenish-blue tint that provides ideal camouflage among the kelp and coral. Their eyes are capable of perceiving the faintest glimmers of bioluminescent light, enabling them to navigate effortlessly through the watery depths. 

These humans have developed a deep spiritual and practical symbiosis with their environment. They employ simple, yet ingenious tools made of shells, stones, and coral, reflecting a culture deeply entrenched in respect for their ecosystem. Clothing is minimalistic, crafted from seaweed fibers treated to be both durable and comfortable. Tattoos made from inks derived from local cephalopods mark rites of passage, achievements, and family heritage.

**Speakers of the Coral:**

Serving as the stewards and guardians of Nebularia's ancient coral-based architecture, the "Speakers of the Coral" are a specialized guild of human inhabitants who can communicate with the sentient coral formations that comprise much of the city's infrastructure. These individuals can, over years of patient meditation and practice, synchronize their thoughts with the coral's slow, rhythmic pulses. This communication enables them to guide the growth of the coral to reinforce structures, carve out living spaces, and heal damaged areas. The symbiotic relationship ensures that the city's foundation remains robust and ever-adaptive to the needs of its residents.

**Symbiotic Seaweeds and Coral Entities:**

The plant life of Nebularia is not merely background flora but active participants in the community. Sentient seaweeds, with gentle, undulating movements, communicate through soft rustling and brief flashes of bioluminescence. These plants have developed intricate ways to assist their human compatriots, providing not only food and shelter but also natural medicines and light. 

Coral entities, more ancient and wise, serve as the city’s silent chroniclers. Their ability to produce beautiful, resonant tones by flexing their hard, stony structures gives them a unique voice. As repositories of knowledge, they remember the city’s history and offer counsel to the city's inhabitants through the Speakers of the Coral. In this way, they ensure the continuity and stability of Nebularia's society.

**Fauna Companions:**

The city is teeming with marine life, each species playing its part in the ecological balance. Intelligent fish species exhibit astonishing levels of cooperation with the human residents. Schools of fish act as couriers, delivering messages and small items across the city. Crustaceans, such as small, clever octopuses and intricate networks of shrimp, are skilled at maintaining and cleaning the underwater pathways, ensuring that the city remains beautiful and functional.

Among the larger creatures, the manta rays and sea turtles hold special significance. These gentle giants are revered as the protectors of Nebularia, believed to possess ancient wisdom and serve as guides during important journeys. The squadron of manta rays makes routine patrols around the boundary of the dome, acting as both guardians and rescuers for anyone or anything adrift near the city's limits.

**Transient Visitors:**

Nebularia, though vividly self-contained, welcomes explorers and researchers from other realms. Scientists and adventurers from surface-dwelling societies are the most common visitors, drawn by the allure of this submerged marvel. While they initially arrive with elaborate diving gear and submersibles, their deeper integration often results in adopting local practices, learning to communicate with the ecosystem and respecting the city's rules.

**Cultural Practices:**

Life in Nebularia pulses with rituals and ceremonies that harness the alignment between human life and the natural rhythms of their surroundings. Celebrations mark the changing of currents, honoring the symbiotic relationships among the inhabitants. Music and art take forms deeply influenced by aquatic sounds and visuals—coral drums, shell flutes, and flowing kelp tapestries adorn public spaces and personal quarters alike.

**Final Symbiosis:**

From the sentient flora to the fish that act as couriers and caretakers, every entity within Nebularia plays a vital role. The human characters, adapted in both body and spirit to their aquatic home, lead lives that honor the delicate intersections of necessity and reverence. They understand that their survival hinges on a mutual exchange of life forces, favoring sustainable coexistence over dominance. Thus, Nebularia remains a world of extraordinary calm, anchored in the tenets of collective harmony and patience, inviting all who witness it to fathom the deep wisdom of an intertwined existence.
