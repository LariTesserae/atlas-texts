In this mystifying realm of contrasts and paradoxes where ancient mountains meet lush valleys, a unique spectrum of inhabitants find their niche, blending seamlessly with the extreme environment:

1. **Fantastical Arachnids**: Perched upon the cliff faces, camouflaged against the stone, lie colossal but elegantly designed spider species. Their webs stretch between the rock towers, glistening in the sunlight like ghostly bridges. These arachnids, with intricately patterned carapaces resembling the rocky backgrounds, are the sentinels of the mountains. They feast on the myriad of smaller creatures caught in their immense, sticky nets.

2. **Ephemeral Flutterfae**: Hovering delicately among the blossoming pockets of vegetation are ethereal beings resembling winged fairies. With transparent, iridescent wings, they flit from flower to flower, playing a critical role in pollination. Their bodies emit a faint soft luminescence, making them appear as clusters of shimmering motes drifting through the air. The Flutterfae are elusive, seen more as a blink-and-you’ll-miss-it phenomenon.

3. **Stalwart Stonebeasts**: In the deep valleys, blending harmoniously with the scattered remnants of a bygone civilization, are the Stonebeasts. These gargantuan, slow-moving quadrupeds have hides that mimic the textural and color variations of the rocky terrain. They wander through the valleys in search of the sparse, hardy vegetation they subsist on. Despite their immense size, their movements are surprisingly quiet, the only indications of their presence being the occasional soft rumble as they communicate through seismic vibrations.

4. **Nimble Leafrunners**: Darting across the vertical greens and spanning rocky ledges are the Leafrunners, small, agile creatures covered in moss-like fur. They leap and bound effortlessly, their feet perfectly adapted to grasping the rugged surfaces. Leafrunners are omnivorous, scavenging for anything from plant matter to small insects, making them integral to the ecosystem's balance.

5. **Airborne Crystal Raptors**: Higher up, riding the thermals and winds that howl through the peaks, soar the Crystal Raptors. These majestic birds have wings encrusted with bioluminescent feathers that shatter light into a spectrum of colors, creating a dazzling display in motion. They swoop gracefully, hunting the tiny creatures that dare explore the open spaces or scavenging from the occasional carcass found below.

6. **Echo-lurking Wisps**: Amidst the ruins of a past civilization, phantasmal entities known as Echo-lurking Wisps drift silently. These spirits, perhaps remnants of the lost inhabitants, appear as translucent, shimmering outlines that softly glow in the growing dusk or dawn. They seem bound to the ancient stone foundations, their whispers an eerie yet captivating song carried by the wind, hinting at the untold stories etched into the landscape.

7. **Terrace Tenders**: Hidden among the reclaimed paths and terraces are the Terrace Tenders, small humanoid figures that blend with the environment through both appearance and behavior. They are guardians of the few stretches of cultivated land still producing rare, valuable flora. These beings possess an intimate knowledge of the land, nurturing the delicate balance of growth with subtle interventions that keep the ecosystem thriving despite its harsh conditions.

Together, these inhabitants create a complex web woven from threads of survival, adaptation, and mystery. They embody the extremes of their environment, thriving in a land where nature's relentless force and delicate beauty converge, making their existence an enduring testament to life's improbable perseverance. In this realm, every rustle, shadow, twinkle, and hum weaves into the symphony of survival, an ever-evolving dance against the backdrop of time itself.
