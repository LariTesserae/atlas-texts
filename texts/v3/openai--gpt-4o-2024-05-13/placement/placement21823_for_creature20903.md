My wings spread wide, catching the thermals that rise from the warm plateau, I soar with a grace born from generations of flight. Our calls are not just songs, but resonant communications, a language of the sky that intertwines with the hum of the city. Each note, each cadence, corresponds and converses with the hidden frequencies of the city's ancient heart, creating a duet of life and machine. It is through our harmonious melodies that we, the Aetherwings, serve as the interpreters of the forgotten engineers’ visions.

Below, the Gylventians navigate the water-filled hollows, their reverence for rain and mist creating small oasis-like enclaves amidst the rugged landscape.

During twilight, when the mist thickens and the shrouds of cloud weigh heavier, my feathers pulse softly with the current, patterns shifting to a deep, resonant blue. It is then, under the cloak of indistinct light, that I perform the most sacred of flights—tracing ancient trajectories over the city's sprawl, patterns etched into my being from stories passed down through generations of Aetherwings. Each loop and dive magnifies the faint electrical pulses, amplifying the silent narratives embedded in the stone and metal below.

Being an Aetherwing means embodying a paradoxical existence—between the transient and the eternal. Our lifespans, though brief in comparison to the ageless plateau, are dense with purpose, each of our kind contributing a stroke to the everlasting fresco of our city's history. We hold in our wings the electric whispers of an age beyond memory, and in our calls, the harmonies of a civilization that both was and continues to be, in every hum and pulse that vibrates through this ancient cityscape.

In my repose, I perch upon the highest, rust-worn structures—silent witnesses to epochs past. From such heights, I can see the entire expanse and feel the ceaseless energy that resonates from every relic and structure, reminding me that even in stasis, there is movement; even in silence, there is song. I am connected to all inhabitants below, the hidden Mosslings, the resilient Ochre Hares, and the ghostly Preservers, each a note in the grand symphony of this enduring city.

As an Aetherwing, I exist between realms, bound to the earth by the magnetic pull of its forgotten machinery and to the sky by the unending quest for meaning etched in the currents of the air. It is a life of beauty, of duty, and of endless flight, tracing and retracing the lines of a storied past atop a plateau that breathes with the whispers of the eternal.
