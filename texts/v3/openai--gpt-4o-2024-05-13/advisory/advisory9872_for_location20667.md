Visiting this primeval and enigmatic place will require extraordinary preparation, both mental and physical. Here are steps a multiverse traveler should take to ensure a safe and enriching journey:

1. **Research and Mental Preparation:**
   - **Understand the History and Ecology**: Before embarking on the trip, study the ancient history, flora, and fauna endemic to this surreal land. Knowledge about survival strategies employed by past inhabitants can be invaluable.
   - **Mental Fortitude**: Meditate and prepare for confronting an alien environment where the conventional laws of nature might seem skewed. Visualize adapting to the unfamiliar sights and sounds without panic.

2. **Physical Preparations:**
   - **Hydration Supplies**: Given the palpable absence of water, bring ample supplies of potable water and hydration tablets. Invest in purification devices as a backup.
   - **Adapted Clothing**: Layered, moisture-wicking clothing to deal with the cool temperatures and dry conditions. Include a dust mask and goggles to protect against airborne particles.
   - **Footwear**: Sturdy boots with good tread and ankle support to navigate the cracked, brittle earth, steep elevations, and unexpected terrain changes.

3. **Advanced Equipment and Tools:**
   - **Portable Shelter**: A lightweight, durable tent for protection from elements and potential life forms. Consider one with built-in thermal regulation.
   - **Navigation Technology**: GPS and wearable navigation aids to prevent getting lost in the sprawling expanse. Include maps and a compass as analog backups.
   - **Survival Gear**: Multi-tool, lightweight survival kit including a first-aid kit, signaling devices, high-energy snacks, and tools to deal with potential hostile encounters.
   - **Field Journal and Camera**: Document observations about the ecosystem and anthropological findings. A digital or analog camera can capture the ethereal visuals.

4. **Health and Safety Precautions:**
   - **Vaccines and Medicines**: Ensure vaccinations to prevent infections from unknown organisms. Carry antibiotics, antihistamines, and other necessary medications.
   - **Protective Gear**: Lightweight armor or padding to protect against multilayer collisions with translucent fauna's unpredictable movements.
   - **Biomonitoring Devices**: Wearable technology to continuously monitor vital signs and trigger alerts in case of environmental anomalies.

5. **Locomotion and Exploration Strategies:**
   - **Reconnaissance Drones**: Deploy drones to scope out the terrain ahead, mapping out cliffs and valleys for safer traversal.
   - **Study Native Species Behavior**: Pay careful attention to the creatures and their interactions to avoid disturbing their delicate balance, noting their bioluminescent communication patterns.

6. **Psychological Strategies:**
   - **Stable Focus**: Teach oneself controlled breathing techniques and mindfulness practices to avoid being overwhelmed by the surreal visuals and eerie quietness.
   - **Cultural Sensitivity**: Respect the remnants of ancient structures and artifacts, posing as archaeologists rather than tourists. Handle discoverable objects with care.

7. **Exit Strategy and Contingency Plans:**
   - **Designated Rendezvous Points**: Set clear meeting places and times should you become separated from your group. Equip everyone with communication devices.
   - **Emergency Extraction**: Establish a method for quickly exiting the region in case of critical danger, including signaling techniques and predefined escape routes.

8. **Environmental Stewardship:**
   - **Leave No Trace**: Adopt stringent guidelines to minimize the impact on this ancient, unchanging place. Avoid leaving behind waste or disrupting the fragile ecosystem.
   - **Appreciate the Alien Beauty**: Cultivate a sense of reverence for the quiet persistence of life and the deep aesthetics of an existence that transcends familiarity.

Conclusively, while this journey beckons adventure and unprecedented discoveries, meticulously preparing for the myriad challenges will frame the experience as transformational rather than perilous. Embrace the alien enigmatic allure with both respect and readiness.
