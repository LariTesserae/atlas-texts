**Preparation for Visiting an Arid, Strange Biome in an Alternate Universe**

**1. Physical Preparation:**
   - **Clothing:** Wear breathable, heat-resistant clothing. Opt for a full-body suit made of advanced materials that offer protection against high temperatures while allowing for ventilation. A cooling vest can help, especially one equipped with phase-change materials to regulate body temperature.
   - **Footwear:** Use sturdy, high-ankle boots with heat-resistant soles to navigate jagged terrains and avoid potential exposure to geothermal heat.
   - **Headgear:** A broad-brimmed hat and UV-protective goggles will shield you from the harsh glare of the shimmering sands and hidden light sources. A rebreather or air-mask could filter out particulate matter and acrid mineral scents, ensuring you can breathe comfortably.

**2. Health and Safety Gear:**
   - **Hydration Pack:** Despite the arid conditions, extreme heat demands consistent hydration. A hydration system with electrolyte-infused water will prevent dehydration.
   - **First Aid Kit:** Include burns treatment, wound care, and remedies for fungal infections. Antidotes for potential poisonous interactions with the local flora and fauna are crucial.
   - **Geothermal Suit:** An insulated suit with thermal regulation capabilities to protect from sudden geothermal activity or exposure to chemicals in geysers.

**3. Environmental Tools:**
   - **Navigational Aids:** Use advanced navigation tools like a multi-spectral scanner and inertial navigation system, as traditional GPS may not function reliably.
   - **Sample Collection:** Carry sterile vials, forceps, and a mini-laboratory kit to collect and analyze soil, mineral, and biological samples.
   - **Protective Shield:** A portable shelter or force field generator can create a safe zone against the harsh atmosphere and any potential predatory encounters.

**4. Survival Equipment:**
   - **Food Supplies:** Pack high-calorie, nutrient-dense food bars that are easy to carry and consume on the go, as traditional food sources are nonexistent.
   - **Heating/Cooling Gadgets:** A compact and portable device to deliver warmth during cooler night intervals and extra cooling during thermal peaks.
   - **Lights:** High-intensity headlamps and lanterns with adjustable brightness to navigate the perpetual twilight and bioluminescent terrains. Infrared and UV lights for distinguishing hazardous areas or creatures at night.

**5. Interaction with Ecosystem:**
   - **Fungal Precautions:** Avoid direct contact with the bioluminescent fungal tendrils as their chemical and biological properties are unpredictable. Wear gloves and a face mask when near.
   - **Creature Awareness:** Maintain awareness of the small, scuttling creatures. Employ a non-lethal deterrent for encounters with larger predators, such as a sonic repeller or a bio-electromagnetic field generator to disrupt their sensory abilities.

**6. Documentation Equipment:**
   - **Recording Devices:** Bring robust recording devices protected against atmospheric and elemental damage for capturing the biome's unique features.
   - **Journals and Reference Materials:** Digital notebooks with a comprehensive database of known elements, minerals, and life-forms might provide immediate analyses and cross-references.

**7. Cultural Sensitivity:**
   - **Artifact Respect:** When encountering ancient artifacts, document their locations and appearances meticulously but do not disturb them. They may hold significant cultural or historical importance and could activate unforeseen mechanisms.

**8. Mental and Psychological Preparation:**
   - **Adaptation Training:** Engage in simulations or VR experiences of harsh, desolate environments to acclimatize psychologically to the unearthly and claustrophobic atmosphere.
   - **Mindfulness and Stress Techniques:** Practice techniques such as deep breathing, meditation, or guided visualization to manage stress levels and maintain mental clarity amidst the surreal landscape.

**Final Notes:**
- **Constant Vigilance:** Stay alert to the ground shifts and distant rumbles, which could preface significant geological events. Listening and observing are just as crucial as moving and reacting.
- **Leave No Trace:** Ensure all activities respect the integrity of the environment. Minimize footprints, pack out all waste, and avoid introducing foreign substances or organisms.

This preparatory guide should hopefully enable a multiverse traveler to navigate and appreciate the extraordinary and otherworldly biome effectively and safely. Enjoy the voyage into this baffling, mesmerizing domain of life and geology!
