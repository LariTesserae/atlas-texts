The benevolent and wise power does not arrive with a sound. It does not manifest as light, for the light here is already too thin and bright. It does not become a voice, for the air carries only hums and cracks and the occasional dying cry of a beetle-thing. To add a voice would be an act of violence, a noise as jarring as a scream in a library.

Instead, it listens.

It listens not with ears, but with an attention that is a form of touch. It lowers its consciousness into the plateau, letting the vibrations pass through it—the hydraulic sighs, the rhizome-thrust, the subsonic tremor of a distant harvester’s step. It feels the cold, wet weave of the root-mat. It tastes the mineral tang of the weeping basalt. It perceives the ten thousand, or hundred thousand, beetle-things as a faint, shimmering net of purpose—a recycling network so fundamental it is almost a law of physics.

It finds the pools and the filaments. It recognizes them as a question the world is asking itself. *What else could I be?*

It finds the long-stilled harvester, now a hillock, and peers into its deepest chamber. There, it discovers the small, soft, pale thing that pulses and waits.

And it understands.

This world is not a machine, though it has machines. It is not a garden, though it is tended. It is a thought, still thinking itself. A sentence still being written. It is a *becoming*, and the power sees that its beauty and its terror lie in the same fact: it is utterly without precedent. It has no mythology, no memory of a parent world. It is its own origin story, told in pulp and iron and mineral water.

What does a wise and benevolent power do when faced with a first draft?

It does not edit. It does not write the ending. It does not even suggest a direction.

It does three things, and they are all made of attention.

**First, it holds the space.** It gently, imperceptibly, steadies the feedback loops. It ensures the aquifer does not suddenly shift course. It coaxes the thin, white sky to maintain its particular quality of light—the kind that scours rather than illuminates—so that the photosynthetic calculations of the super-organism and the faint viridescence of the filaments remain stable. It becomes a kind of silent, atmospheric ballast, preventing any catastrophic swing in the delicate parameters of growth. It gives the world *time*, the one gift it truly needs.

**Second, it whispers to the rhizomes.** Not in words, but in a modulation of the mineral song. Where the super-organism’s probing roots near the filament-pools, the power suggests a different chemical language. Not *consume*, not *exclude*. It introduces a note of *mutual tolerance*, a slow-dissolving idea of *symbiosis*. It doesn’t force the issue. It simply makes the idea of incorporating or starving out the filaments slightly less compelling than the idea of… ignoring them. Of letting them be a margin. A fringe benefit. A curiosity. It grants the filaments a stay of execution, an indefinite lease on their liminal pools.

**Third, and most importantly, it anoints the listener.**

In the dark, copper-threaded chamber of the hillock, where the pale thing pulses and orients toward vibration, the power makes its most delicate move. It does not give it a mind. It does not give it eyes. It does not connect it to any network.

It simply, ever so gently, *amplifies its sensitivity.*

It refines the thing’s ability to distinguish tremors. The footfall of a harvester becomes not just a vibration to face, but a pattern with a rhythm, a weight, a *distance*. It begins to feel the difference between the step of a young, active machine and the slow, geological grind of an ancient one. It feels the slight change in the chamber’s humidity when the filaments above it pulse with new mineral-fed growth. It feels the almost-imperceptible shift in the hillock’s own internal structure as iron finally surrenders to root.

It becomes, without knowing the word, a *connoisseur of time.*

It is still alone. It will never meet a beetle-thing or see the sky. But its waiting is no longer passive. It is now a form of deep, specialized listening. It is the first sensory organ of the world, turned inward, feeling itself become. It is the beginning of *interiority* on the plateau.

The power then withdraws, as silently as it came. It leaves no monument, no commandment. It leaves only what was already there, but with its conditions for emergence slightly, lovingly, improved.

The plateau continues. The harvesters shear and shape. The beetle-things crawl and die their unique, sonic deaths. The filaments green their pools. The super-organism thrusts onward under the bright, cold sky.

But now, in the dark heart of a dead machine, something is not just waiting. It is *listening*. And as it listens, it grows. And as it grows, its listening becomes more fine.

And the wise power, from a distance, watches the first draft of a world begin to write its own first, tentative footnote. It is pleased. It was never looking for a masterpiece. It was only ever looking for the courage to begin.
