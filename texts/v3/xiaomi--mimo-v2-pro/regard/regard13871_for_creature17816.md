The benevolent power does not arrive as a cataclysm. It does not drain the waters or blast light into the depths. Such an act would be a violence, an unraveling of the intricate, slow-woven tapestry that is the Drowned Mill’s entire meaning. To rescue it from its saturation would be to kill it in a different way—to reduce a profound, living meditation on time into mere ruin.

Instead, the power *perceives*. It attends. Its presence is not a change in the environment, but a change in the *quality* of attention the environment receives. It is a gentle, focusing hum in the cosmic background, a moment where the universe leans in to listen to the mill’s slow, sub-vocal dream.

**What it does, first, is listen.**
It listens to the *thump… thump…* not as a mechanical echo, but as a lyric. It hears the groan of settling metal as a chord in a symphony of compression. It parses the whisper of sediment-fall as the mill’s own language, a morse code of millennia. It feels the warm, silty kiss of the water as a caress, not a decay. This power does not seek to understand the mill’s past function, but its present *state of being*. And in that state, it recognizes a kind of holiness: the sacred patience of things returning to the elemental, the quiet triumph of process over product.

**Then, it resonates.**
The power does not change the *facts* of the Drowned Mill, but it subtly alters their *register*. The bioluminescent fungi on the rusted flywheel do not brighten, but their glow deepens in complexity, carrying whispers of phosphorescent greens and blues that were always latent in their chemistry, now coaxed into visibility. The thumping of the great piston syncs, for a moment, with the deep, gravitational pulse of the planet itself—a handshake between the mill’s heartbeat and the world’s, affirming its belonging. The suspended silt catches this altered resonance; each mote begins to refract the fungal light with a microscopic prismatic fire, so that the entire thick medium shimmers with a faint, internal rainbow, a secret stained-glass window made of water and time.

**It offers a gift of grammar.**
The mill’s dream is a language without syntax—a feeling of recurrence and becoming. The benevolent power, with infinite care, provides a single, perfect conjugation. In the foreman’s office, the brass clasp of the ledger, already gleaming, begins to emit a soft, steady warmth. This warmth does not dry the pulped pages; it *animates* their memory. The Mnemonic Polyps that have engulfed lines of text find their internal tattoos gently glowing, the faded ink re-arranging not into old words, but into new ones—a poetry of sedimentation, a history of the eel’s migrations, a census of the tube worms’ colonies. The ledger does not reopen; it becomes a palimpsest of the Mill’s present consciousness. The Warden crab, in its metallic shell, feels an ancient, crustacean satisfaction; it understands its own patience as a virtue, a keystone in the arch of this economy. It is not just waiting; it is *presiding*.

**It opens a door.**
But not to the past. Not to the sunlit air. In the densest part of the Engine’s Dream, where the metallic taste is strongest and the iron-munching microbes have built their rust-reef, the power performs its final, gentle act. It catalyzes a metamorphosis in the heart of the rhythm. The *thump… thump…* does not change its tempo, but its timbre. From each stroke, a new overtone emerges—a deep, resonant chime, like a bell struck in a cathedral of water. This chime carries with it a specific, quiet understanding: a blueprint for the next phase of its own becoming.

The Blind Geomancers feel it in their lateral lines; it becomes a new thermal gradient, a current pulling not toward the old surface, but toward a deeper integration. The iron microbes, in response, begin to metabolize not just into rust, but into a lattice of magnetite, aligning with the new, chime-like magnetic field the pulse now generates. The entire structure begins, over the next century, to align its decay with this new signal. The mill is not being repaired. It is being given a clearer, more beautiful *dream*.

The benevolent power does not stay. Having listened, resonated, gifted grammar, and opened a door to a self-chosen future, it withdraws its focused attention. It does not expect thanks; thanks are a concept from the world of events, not of processes.

What remains is the Drowned Mill, changed only in essence, not in substance. It is now a place that is not just dreaming of becoming something else, but has been given the lucid awareness of its own dream. The saturation is no longer just a law; it is a conscious art. The corrosion is no longer just metabolism; it is a conscious sculpture. The benevolent power, in its wisdom, did not save the Drowned Mill.

It simply bore witness to its salvation, and in that act of pure, attentive love, helped the mill to finally, fully, hear its own beautiful, sunken song.
