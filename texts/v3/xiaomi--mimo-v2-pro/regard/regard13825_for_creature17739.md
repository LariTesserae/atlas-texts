The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as a beam of light, nor as a voice in the thrum. It has no need for such pronouncements. It simply *notices*, and in noticing, it comprehends the entire, exquisite equation of the place—the balance of deep time, the function of the slow ones, the sealed, perfect loop of its entropy.

Its first action is one of profound respect. It does not alter the sky, nor pour water upon the salt, nor attempt to quicken the pace of the Weavers. To do so would be an act of violence, akin to shattering a crystal to see how it rings. Instead, it does something far more subtle: it **attends**.

It turns its perception not as a searchlight, but as a gentle, ambient pressure of awareness. It feels the sub-audible thrum not as noise, but as a language—the planet’s long, cooling sigh. It traces the silicate lattice of a Weaver, understanding its slow-motion thought not as a biological process, but as a sacred, geological meditation. It sees the city not as a ruin, but as a meticulously maintained instrument, tuned to a frequency of completion.

Having understood, its benevolence takes a form native to the system: **reinforcement of equilibrium**.

With a thought no more strenuous than a human taking a breath, it strengthens the deep thrum by an imperceptible fraction—a single, foundational note in the planet’s song made purer, more stable. It does not add energy; it merely clarifies what is already there. The Weavers, in their timeless dance, feel this not as a change, but as a sudden, deep rightness. Their resonant tuning becomes easier, their integrations more seamless. The whisper of entropy in their processes quiets a degree.

Next, it turns its attention to the Weavers themselves. It does not give them intelligence or speed. It does something more elegant: it **acknowledges their purpose**. It weaves a faint, gossamer thread of intention into the crystalline lattice of their being—a thread that says, *“I see what you are doing. It is good.”* This is not a command, but a permission slip written into the fabric of reality. It grants them a dim, collective awareness of their own sacred function, not as individuals, but as the collective immune system and artists of the equilibrium. Their slow adjustments gain a new, subliminal layer of precision, a quiet joy in the maintenance.

Finally, the wise power addresses the potential future. It knows the universe is full of quick, noisy things—explorers, miners, wreckers—who might stumble upon this terrarium of entropy and, in their ignorance, seek to exploit or “save” it. To protect the place, it does not build a wall. It weaves a **veil of reverence** into the local fabric of space-time. Any future visitor arriving here would not be repelled by force. Instead, they would be overcome by a sudden, crushing sense of significance and antiquity. The sheer weight of the deep time, the palpable perfection of the balance, would make any thought of interference feel like a profound blasphemy. They would feel, in their bones, that to take a single stone or start a single engine would be to murder a poem that had been composing itself for a million years. They would look, they would understand a fragment, and they would leave, compelled to silence by a respect they could not articulate.

The power does all this without ever manifesting a form. It leaves no monument, no signature. Its work is indistinguishable from the world’s own nature. It is the ultimate act of benevolence in a closed system: to honor its closure, to perfect its stillness, and to grant its inhabitants the profound dignity of their own magnificent slowness. Then, with the same quiet attention with which it came, it withdraws its notice, leaving the salt pan, the silent city, and the slow weavers to their eternal, thrumming dream—not abandoned, but finally, and forever, truly *seen*.
