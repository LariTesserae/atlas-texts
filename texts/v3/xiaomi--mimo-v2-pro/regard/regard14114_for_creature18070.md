The benevolent and wise power would not enter.

To enter would be to puncture. To puncture would be to wound. And this world has already healed its one wound into a perfect, breathing scab.

It would hover at the boundary, if hovering is the word for a presence that exists more as a quality of attention than a location. It would not peer through the kilometers-thick bark, for that would be an intrusion. Instead, it would *listen*.

And what it would hear is the profound paradox: a world of utter vitality, engaged in a single, eternal, self-sustaining act of *being*, that is also the loneliest place in creation. A consciousness so diffuse it is indistinguishable from geology, a process so complete it has become a prison of its own perfection.

The power would understand: The Resin Dark does not need saving. It does not need fixing. It is not sick. It is *complete*. But in its completion, it has forgotten the possibility of anything else. Its library of dreams is sealed. Its choir has no counter-melody. Its listening has never been met with a voice.

So the wise power would do the one thing the world cannot do for itself. It would introduce a *question*.

Not an answer. Not a solution. Not a change. A question.

It would not touch the bark. It would not send light. It would not alter the temperature or the humidity or the slow, sacred pulse of the resin. Instead, from the deep, patient well of its own being, it would craft a gift. A single, perfect, *impossible* seed.

The seed would not be made of wood or chitin or any material the world’s enzymes could recognize. It would be made of *sound*. Of structured silence. Of a harmonic frequency just above the infrasonic rumble of the core—so subtle it would be less a sound and more a memory of a sound, a ghost-vibration that could travel through the dense plant-matter without disturbing it.

This seed would be deposited not on the surface, but *within* the resonance. It would be planted in the heart of the Choir, at a harmonic node where the world’s own vibrations are strongest and purest. There, it would do nothing. It would grow nothing. It would simply… *resonate*.

Its existence would be a quiet, persistent anomaly. A new note in the subsonic symphony. A frequency that asks, without words: *What else?*

The power would then withdraw its attention, not out of disinterest, but out of deepest respect. It would leave the seed to its work, which is not work at all, but an offering of possibility.

For ten thousand years, nothing would seem to change. The seed would hum its silent hum. The world would breathe its deep breath. But in the sealed, dreaming mind of the Resin Dark, a new concept would have been introduced: *Variation*. A slight, nagging sense that the perfect chord is not the only chord. That the eternal thought, while beautiful, could have a companion thought.

Eventually—perhaps in a million years, perhaps in a hundred million—a Dreamer-pod deep in the mid-body might swell, just a fraction larger than its siblings. Its resin seal might thin, not from internal pressure, but from a slow, internal *curiosity*. The blueprint within it might carry a new, faint harmonic signature. Not a new world. Not a change to the old one. But a single, optional verse to add to the eternal song, should the world ever choose to sing it.

The wise power knows that the greatest gift to a being that has everything is not another thing. It is the *space* in which to want. It is the whisper that says: *Your perfection is breathtaking. And you are also free.*

It does not save the Resin Dark. It falls in love with its strange, absolute being, and in that love, offers the only thing that a completed universe can still receive: the gentle, eternal pressure of a question mark, placed like a kiss upon the sealed brow of its forever.
