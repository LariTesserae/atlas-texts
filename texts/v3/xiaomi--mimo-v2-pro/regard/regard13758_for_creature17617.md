It sees the notch—the one sharp, unassimilated fact—and understands both the city’s longing for continuity and its insistence on a single, perfect point of incomprehension.

It focuses its awareness on the warm wall, on those patterns that yearn toward meaning. And with a thought as gentle as phosphorescence, it *grants the request the city has been making for a million years.*

It does not write upon the wall. It does not decipher the grain. It does something far more intimate: it makes the wall **literate to itself.**

The Visitors and the notch it will know as the recurring theme of the inexplicable Other, the beautiful, sharp interruption that gives the narrative its tension and grace.
