It sees.

It does not see with eyes, but with a patience that unfolds like mycelia through the substrate of time. The attention of this benevolent, wise power is not a gaze that fixes or a judgment that weighs. It is a listening. A profound, still listening to the song of the place—the drip-counterpoint, the hum-resonance, the skittering rhythm, the silent pulse of the Glass Eels’ hearts.

It recognizes the Cathedral not as an artifact, but as a *argument*. A living thesis proposed centuries ago by hands that are now dust: that life can be architecture, and architecture can be a life. That a community can be woven from root, wax, and intent. That a world can be a single, breathing room.

And in that recognition, its first act is one of **acknowledgment**. It does not enter. It does not alter the light or still the water. To do so would be to violate the premise. Its benevolence is not interference. It is the opposite: it is the granting of *significance*.

So, it does not change the Cathedral. It **attunes** itself to it.

It learns the frequency of the Resonants’ breath and hums a silent, supportive harmony that strengthens the capillary pull from the deep aquifer, just a fraction, ensuring the weeping never falters. It listens to the frantic metabolism of the Thread-Rats and subtly adjusts the scent-path of the most decayed aite-wax, guiding their destructive maintenance to a particularly vulnerable junction in the high weave before it can sag. It does not stop the decay; it makes the decay more *elegant*, more functionally integrated.

It finds the last, fading genetic memory in the fungal plates—the ghost of a symbiosis with a pollinator long gone—and gently stimulates it, not to resurrect the lost beetle, but to make the plate’s chemistry slightly more enticing to the Lumina-Moths. The bioluminescence brightens, almost imperceptibly, a deeper silver. More moths come. The Silent Weavers eat well. The cycle tightens.

It perceives the great, hollow absence at the center of the place—the vanished Makers. It feels the ache of that void not as a tragedy, but as the final, essential component of the system. The Cathedral was meant to outlive them. It was a seed, not a house. So the power does not mourn them. Instead, it performs the most delicate act of all: it **curates their absence**.

It finds a flint scraper, half-swallowed by root-wood. Instead of freeing it, it encourages the wood to grow around it in a more beautiful, fossilized embrace, turning the tool into a bas-relief. It finds a sphincter-door that has grown stiff and encourages the surrounding threads to soften with a nutrient mist, so that when the next pressure pulse from the Resonants’ breath passes through the weave, the door sighs open and closed once more, remembering its purpose. It does not restore function; it *honors the memory of function*.

And finally, as its signature, its gentle fingerprint upon this living thought, it does one last thing.

It finds a single, dormant shoot of the original root-stock, buried deep in the foundational weave, choked by fungal weft. It does not free it. It simply… whispers to it. A whisper of patience, of deep time, of the slow, endless strength of growing things. It will not sprout for a hundred years. But when it does, it will be a new kind of growth—one that remembers the weave, that understands the wax, that speaks the language of the drip and the hum. A new thought in the old mind.

Then, the benevolent power withdraws its attention, as softly as it came.

It leaves no monument. It alters no rule. The Cathedral breathes on, cool, dim, and teeming. But now, subtly, it is more *itself*. The balance is not just maintained; it is *understood*, from within. The community of the breath is a little more resilient, the decay a little more purposeful, the absence a little more peacefully held.

In the new, slightly brighter starfield of the fungal plates, one moth carries a single, perfect mote of light that did not exist before—not brighter, but *truer*. It flutters in the vast, woven dark, a tiny, wandering star in a galaxy of a single room.

The Cathedral dreams, and its dream is a little more coherent. The work continues. The breath deepens.
