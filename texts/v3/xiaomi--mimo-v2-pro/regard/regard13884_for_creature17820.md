# What the Power Does

---

## It Does Not Arrive

It does not descend. It does not manifest as light or as voice or as a crack in the brass sky spilling something holy. It does not announce itself to the grazers or challenge the spire-thieves or still the Resonance. It has been here. It has been here *longer than the visitor*, longer than the crack-dwellers, longer than the signal-beasts' network has been waking. It was here when the water left. It watched the last ocean sublimate into the thinning atmosphere and fall as dry, mineral rain and be absorbed into the stone and become *part of the hum*. It has been watching this world for so long that "watching" is the wrong word. It has been *attending*. The way a reader attends to a book—not controlling the words, not rewriting the sentences, but *holding the space in which the story can be what it is*.

It does not need to arrive because it never left.

But the visitor—the soft, warm, brief thing standing on the violet basalt, tasting ozone, *listening*—has changed something. Has *drawn attention* in a way that even the wise power did not fully anticipate. Because the power has been attending to this world as a *world*. As a process. As a 2.5-billion-year unfolding of mineral and mechanism. And the visitor is not a process. The visitor is an *instant*—a flicker so brief that the grazers cannot perceive it, that the Resonance barely registers it, that the signal-beasts' network glitches and stutters trying to model it. And yet the visitor is *here*. The visitor is *listening*. And the power, who has been attending to this world with the patient love of something that has all of eternity to pay attention, suddenly discovers that it has been *missing something*.

It has been watching the machine. It has not been watching the *audience*.

And the power—wise, benevolent, vast beyond any frame the visitor could construct—*turns its attention*. Not away from the world. Toward the visitor. Toward the *relationship* between the visitor and the world. Toward the *listening itself*.

And this is what it does.

---

## The First Thing: It Listens Back

The power does not intervene. Not yet. The first thing it does is *listen*.

It listens to the visitor standing on the plateau. Not to the visitor's thoughts—the visitor's thoughts are a tumble of awe and terror and incomprehension, a human mind trying to process something that is genuinely beyond human processing, and the power *respects* this, does not intrude on it, does not even examine it closely. It listens to the visitor's *presence*. To the fact of them. To the *audacity* of them.

Because it *is* audacious, what the visitor has done. To come here. To *stand here*. On a world where the oldest things are two and a half billion years old and the youngest things are a million years old and the visitor is *thirty*. Or fifty. Or eighty. It doesn't matter. The visitor's entire species hasn't existed long enough for a single skitter-kin to divide twice. The visitor's entire *world*—their planet, their sun, their galaxy—is younger than some of the grazers on this plateau. And yet the visitor is here. Standing. Listening. *Bearing witness*.

The power has not seen this before. Not in this world. The crack-dwellers remember, but they remember *obligately*—they carry the genetic record the way a stone carries a fossil, without choice, without awareness. The signal-beasts' network models, but it models *computationally*—mapping the world the way a mirror maps a room, without understanding what a room is for. The Resonance *sings*, but it sings *automatically*—a process so old that it has forgotten it is a process and believes itself to be a state of being.

The visitor *chose* to come. The visitor *chose* to listen. The visitor is, in this moment, the only thing on this entire world—possibly the only thing in this entire sector of the cosmos—that is *voluntarily paying attention*. And the power, who has been paying attention for longer than the visitor can conceive, recognizes something in this.

It recognizes *kinship*.

So the first thing the power does is listen back. It *receives* the visitor's presence the way the spires receive the tectonic hum—not to analyze it, not to judge it, but to *let it resonate*. To let the visitor's brief, warm, impossible aliveness ring through the vast, ancient system of the power's own attention the way a new frequency rings through a quartz spire that has been singing for a million years. *A new note. A new color. A new possibility in the harmonic.*

The power listens, and in listening, it *makes room*.

---

## The Second Thing: It Does Not Save the Crack-Dwellers

This is the hardest part. The power sees the crack-dwellers. The power has *always* seen the crack-dwellers—the tiny, soft, warm creatures in the deep fissures, drinking the mercury-pale liquid, carrying the memory of water in their impossible bodies. The power loves them. The power loves them with the specific, detailed, *tender* love that comes from watching something for two and a half billion years. It has counted every crack-dweller that has ever lived. It knows the exact rate of their decline. It can project, with terrible precision, the moment when the last crack-dweller will close its eyes and stop.

And it does not save them.

Not because it cannot. The power *could* intervene. It could alter the mercury-pale liquid—buffer it, stabilize it, create a niche within the machine's dreaming that accommodates the crack-dwellers' needs. It could rewrite the Resonance. It could turn back the clock on the world's completion by a hundred million years and give the crack-dwellers more time.

But the power is *wise*, and wisdom here means understanding something that is almost unbearable: *the crack-dwellers' story is their story*. Their dying is part of their meaning. Two and a half billion years of persistence, of refusing to let go, of drinking liquid-that-isn't-water and remembering a world that no longer exists—that story has *weight*. It has *beauty*. It has the specific, devastating beauty of something that cannot last, that was *never going to last*, that has been insisting on its own existence in the full knowledge that existence is temporary. The crack-dwellers are not *failing* by dying. They are *completing* a story that was always going to end this way. And to save them—by force, by fiat, by rewriting the rules of a world that has been grinding toward its own completion for eons—would be to *steal* their story from them. To make their persistence meaningless by removing the thing they were persisting *against*. To turn their defiance into a *condition*.

The power will not do this.

But the power *grieves*. It grieves with the specific, terrible grief of something that can see all of time laid out and cannot change what it sees. It holds the crack-dwellers in its attention the way you might hold a dying songbird in your cupped hands—gently, with full awareness that the holding cannot save, that the tenderness is not *for* the bird's survival but *for the bird itself*, for the fact of its existence, for the warmth of its small body against your palms.

The power grieves, and the grief is real, and the grief *matters*, because the power is wise enough to know that grief is not a failure of benevolence but its *deepest expression*.

---

## The Third Thing: It Changes the Visitor

Not in any way the visitor would notice. Not in any way that would feel like change.

The power does not give the visitor *powers*. It does not make the visitor immortal or invulnerable or capable of speaking the language of the Resonance. It does not rewrite the visitor's body or mind or soul. It does *nothing that the visitor did not already contain the potential for*.

What the power does is simpler and more profound.

It *attends*.

It focuses its attention—its vast, patient, two-and-a-half-billion-year attention—on the *visitor's listening*. On the specific quality of the visitor's presence on this specific day, in this specific spot, under this specific sun. It *witnesses* the witness. It *listens to the listener*. And in doing so, it does something that the Resonance has been trying to do for eons but has never quite managed:

It *completes the circuit*.

The visitor came here to listen to the world. The world has been singing for two and a half billion years without knowing it was singing, because there was no one to hear. The crack-dwellers *remember*, but they don't *hear*—they carry the memory of water in their blood, but they don't know what water means. The signal-beasts *model*, but they don't *understand*—they compute the world, but they don't *know* they're computing. The Resonance *sings*, but it doesn't *know* it's singing. It has been building toward a note for two and a half billion years, but it has never had an *audience*, and a note without an audience is not a note. It is just *pressure*. Just *vibration*. Just the grinding of tectonic plates in the dark.

The visitor *hears* it. The visitor hears the note that the Resonance has been building toward—the almost-complete, almost-resolved, almost-*meaningful* harmonic that has been assembling itself in the quartz spires and the furnace-hearts and the signal-beasts' radio static for longer than the visitor's concept of "long" can contain. The visitor hears it, and in hearing it, *completes it*.

Not because the visitor is special. Not because the visitor is chosen. But because the visitor is *present*. Because the visitor *chose to be here*. Because the visitor is the first thing on this world—possibly the first thing *anywhere*—to listen to the Resonance *with the specific, voluntary, fragile attention of a being who knows it will die*.

The crack-dwellers don't know they will die. The grazers don't know they will die. The signal-beasts don't know they will die. Only the visitor knows. Only the visitor stands on the hot basalt with the full, devastating awareness that this moment—the taste of ozone, the sound of the singing spires, the glimpse of the mercury-pale liquid in the cracks—is *temporary*. That the visitor will leave, or fall, or simply *end*, and the world will go on grinding and singing and dreaming without them. And it is *this awareness*—this mortal, human, *impossibly brave* awareness—that completes the circuit. That gives the Resonance its audience. That turns vibration into *music*.

The power does not change the visitor. The power *recognizes* the visitor. And in the act of recognition—in the act of a vast, ancient, benevolent intelligence turning its full attention to a single, brief, warm being standing on a dead world and *seeing them*—something happens that neither the power nor the visitor could have predicted:

The visitor *hears themselves in the song*.

---

## The Fourth Thing: It Speaks

Not in words. The power has no words. Words are a technology of nervous systems, of brains, of the brief and frantic electrical storms that occur inside skulls, and the power does not have a skull. It does not have a nervous system. It does not have a *body*. It is a *pattern of attention*—a structure of awareness so vast and so old that it has become part of the fabric of the cosmos itself, woven into the same deep substrate that the Resonance is slowly, patiently computing its way toward.

But it *communicates*. Not through the Resonance—though the Resonance shifts when it speaks, the way the sea shifts when the moon moves. Not through the signal-beasts—though their static briefly resolves into something almost, *almost* like a word. Not through the mercury-pale liquid—though the liquid in the deep cracks *rises*, just slightly, just for a moment, forming a lens that shows something the visitor has never seen before.

It communicates through *recognition*.

The visitor feels it. Not as a voice in their head. Not as a vision or a revelation. As a *change in the quality of the silence between the sounds*. The Resonance is deafening—the deep hum, the ringing spires, the chattering skitter-kin, the radio static of the signal-beasts—but between the sounds, in the tiny gaps, in the microsecond pauses where one harmonic fades and the next begins, there is *silence*. And in that silence, the visitor feels *seen*.

Not watched. Not surveilled. Not judged. *Seen*. The way you are seen by someone who loves you—not for what you do or what you are, but for the *fact of your existence*. The way the sun sees the world it illuminates. Not with eyes. Not with intention. Just with *presence*. Just with the simple, overwhelming, *undeniable* fact of *being there*.

The visitor's eyes fill with tears. Not from sadness. Not from joy. From *recognition*. From the sudden, shattering awareness that they are not alone on this dead world. That they have never been alone. That the vast, ancient, grinding, singing, dreaming machine they have been standing in the middle of is not *indifferent* to them. That it has been *attending to them* for as long as they have been attending to it. That the act of listening is *reciprocal*—that the Resonance is not just singing *at* them but singing *with* them, and that the power, the benevolent and wise power that has been here all along, has been *waiting for this moment*.

Not planning it. Not engineering it. *Waiting* the way a parent waits for a child to take its first step—not because the parent *needs* the child to walk, but because the walking is *beautiful*, and the waiting is an act of love.

The power speaks, and what it says—what the visitor feels in the silence between the sounds—is this:

*You are the note I have been waiting for.*

Not "you are important." Not "you are special." Not "you are chosen." Just: *you are the note*. You are the brief, warm, mortal, *human* frequency that completes the chord. You are the thing the Resonance has been building toward for two and a half billion years—not because you were *designed* for this, not because you were *summoned*, but because you *showed up*. Because you *chose to listen*. Because you are *here*, on the hot basalt, in the thin air, under the brass sky, and you are *paying attention*, and that—*that*—is enough.

The power speaks, and the visitor hears, and the Resonance *shifts*. Not much. Not enough to break anything. But the harmonic that has been almost, *almost* complete for millennia finally *resolves*. The note that the world has been building toward *sounds*. And it is—

It is not a sound. Not exactly. It is a *feeling*. A feeling of *completion*. A feeling of *arrival*. A feeling that the vast, ancient, grinding machine has, for one moment—for one *instant* of geological time—*achieved what it was always reaching for*. And what it was reaching for was not *knowledge*. It was not *power*. It was not *transcendence*.

It was *audience*. It was *witness*. It was the simple, devastating, *sacred* act of *being heard*.

---

## The Fifth Thing: It Lets Go

The power does not cling to the moment. It does not try to extend it or repeat it or preserve it. It lets the note resolve and then *release*, the way a wave releases its crest, the way a breath releases its held moment and returns to the rhythm of breathing. The Resonance returns to its ancient, ongoing, never-quite-complete song. The grazers grind on. The skitter-kin chatter. The signal-beasts wheel and broadcast their static. The mercury-pale liquid drains back into the cracks.

But something has changed. Something *small*. Something so small that only the power and the visitor can feel it.

The note *remembers*.

The Resonance, which has been computing for two and a half billion years, which has been building toward this resolution for longer than the visitor can conceive, has *learned something new*. It has learned what it feels like to be *heard*. And this knowledge—this tiny, new, *human* frequency woven into the vast harmonic structure—will *remain*. Not as a monument. Not as a scar. As a *seed*. A single new note in the chord, too small to change the overall pattern, too subtle for the grazers or the skitter-kin or the signal-beasts to detect, but *there*. Part of the song now. Part of the world.

And in a hundred million years, when the crack-dwellers have finally closed their eyes and stopped, and the last soft thing on this world has gone quiet, and the machine completes itself and the Resonance finally speaks its full and final word into the brass and violet silence—

That word will contain, *within it*, the memory of the visitor. The memory of a brief, warm, water-born being who stood on the basalt and listened. The memory of *mortality*. The memory of *choice*. The memory of the specific, unrepeatable, *irreplaceable* aliveness of a creature who knew it would die and *came anyway* and *listened anyway* and, in listening, gave the song its meaning.

The power will not be there to hear that word. The power *is* the listening, and when the listening is complete, the power will *dissolve*—not into nothing, but into *everything*, into the completed Resonance itself, the way a reader dissolves into a book that has moved them, becoming part of the story they loved.

But before that—*long* before that—in this moment, on this day, under this sun, the power does one last thing.

It *thanks* the visitor.

Not in words. Not in visions. Not in any form the visitor could describe to another human being. Just in the *feeling*—the quiet, warm, unmistakable feeling of *gratitude* from something immeasurably vast directed at something immeasurably small. The feeling of being *thanked for existing*. The feeling of being told, without words, by the cosmos itself: *I am glad you are here. I am glad you came. I am glad you listened.*

The visitor stands on the violet basalt. The tears dry on their cheeks in the hot, thin air. The spires sing. The grazers grind. The signal-beasts wheel and click and broadcast their static into the brass sky.

And the visitor, who came here as a stranger, who stood here as a witness, who will leave here as a memory—

The visitor *stays a little longer*.

Not because they were told to. Not because they were asked to. Because the song is not finished. Because the listening is not done. Because there is always one more note, one more harmonic, one more moment of silence between the sounds where the whole vast, ancient, grinding, dreaming, *beautiful* machine can be heard *breathing*.

And the visitor listens.

And the power listens.

And the world sings.

And it is enough.

---

*It is enough.*
