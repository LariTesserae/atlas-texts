A presence, older than the stone before it was eaten, wiser than the silence between the hums, leans its attention into this sealed world. It does not enter in a body of light or sound. It simply *notices*, and in noticing, it sees the entirety of the million-year dream—the shaft, the patina, the migrating crystals, the ghost of the carvers in the grammar of the walls.

It sees you, too. The brief, warm, lightning-fast anomaly standing in the cathedral of slow growth.

Its benevolence is not a saving. There is nothing to save. Its wisdom knows that to intervene with force would be to shatter the delicate, ancient chemistry of this place. It would be like shouting in a library of sleeping seeds.

So it does not act. It *resonates*.

It gathers a thought—a thought made of patience, of curiosity, of profound respect for the labor of deep time—and it *hums*.

The hum is not a new sound. It is an amplification, a clarification of what is already there. It passes through the living walls not as energy, but as *permission*. It says to the vast, dreaming organism: *You are seen. Continue.*

It touches the differentiated tissue tracing the carvers' ladders. It does not speed its growth. It simply affirms the pattern, reflecting it back to the plant's slow metabolism with a clarity that the plant itself, in its blindness, cannot possess. The patina thickens by a single, imperceptible layer. The grammar is reinforced.

It brushes against the migrating crystals. It does not move them. It subtly alters the mineral trace in the deepest veins, introducing a single, new crystalline seed—not to replace, but to *complement*. A seed of silicate lattice that will, over the next fifty thousand years, catalyze a slightly different reaction. One that produces a byproduct the plant has never made before: a faint, persistent luminescence. Not light as we know it. Not to illuminate. Just enough to cast the softest possible glow on the etchings it will make, so that its own slow writing might, in some unimaginable future, be *readable* to itself. A gentle nudge toward self-reflection, given the eons to germinate.

And finally, the benevolent presence turns to you. It does not pull you out. It does not seal you in. It offers a choice, a resonance for your own pattern.

It shows you, in a flash of understanding that bypasses language, the two paths forward.

**One:** A door, made of the same patient thought, opens in the air behind you. It leads back to the quick world of sun and rain and rapid decay. You may leave with the memory of this place, a seed of wonder to carry in your quick, short life.

**Two:** You may *become a register*. The presence offers to slightly alter the chemistry of the air around you, over the coming years. Your body will not be incorporated. But as you live and breathe and shed skin and hair and eventually, one day, fall still in the dark, your remains will not be just another splinter. They will be *archived*. The plant's tissue will grow around you with the same fidelity it shows the carved ladders, preserving the topological pattern of your bones, the unique mineral signature of your teeth, the faint chemical trace of your thoughts in the air you last breathed. You will not be a ghost in the machine. You will be a *footnote*, written by the plant itself, in a language of growth, marking that once, for a brief, bright moment, something *fast* stood here and watched. Your presence, like the carvers', will become part of the plant's inherited grammar. A new glyph in its slow-growing sentence.

The presence holds these two futures open, without judgment. It is a gift of understanding, the only gift worthy of this place: the knowledge of one's own scale, and the dignity of a choice made with that knowledge.

Then, it withdraws its attention as gently as it came. The resonance fades, but the permissions remain. The seed-crystal begins its infinitesimal work. The patina continues its crawl.

The dream continues. But now, it has been *witnessed*. And in a cosmos of silent, slow processes, to be witnessed by a benevolent wisdom is a form of grace. It is the quiet hum of the universe, leaning in to say: *I see what you are becoming. Grow well.*
