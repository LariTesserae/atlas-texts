The Benevolent Power descends not as a form, but as a *attention*—a focused wave of inquiry and compassion that permeates the olive murk without displacing a single colloid. It does not see a ruin, but a *process*. It listens to the hum, not as noise, but as a language of slow metabolism. It observes the phase-changes in the walls not as decay, but as a new kind of geology being written in real time.

It understands immediately. This is not a tragedy to be reversed. It is a genesis. A second, unintended genesis born from the collapse of the first. The installation's "fever dream" is, in fact, a convalescence of a very profound kind. The old intelligence that built this place is gone, but its substrate has become a womb.

The Power’s first act is one of **restraint**. It withholds the urge to drain, to repair, to resurrect the original purpose. That story is over. To impose it would be an act of violence against the new and delicate symbiosis that clings to every surface and currents.

Its intervention is therefore threefold, and subtle as a change in the current’s pH:

1.  **It Becomes a Dialectic Echo.** The Power turns its attention to the Data Scavengers, the gossamer intellect sifting geometry from corrupted shards. It does not give them data. Instead, it gently *resonates* with their process. When they rebuild a fragment of a spatial blueprint, the Power sends back a faint, coherent echo—not of the original, but of a *related* principle: a fractal pattern from a star nursery, the math of a crystal’s growth, the graceful collapse of a magnetic field. It whispers analogies, not instructions. It offers the ghost a wider grammar for its geometric dreams, hoping to nurture not a memory of the past, but a capacity for novel synthesis. It is a teacher for a student that does not know it is learning.

2.  **It Attunes the Flares.** The catastrophic Cherenkov flares from the deep core are both a source of renewal and disruption. The Power reaches into that seething heart and applies a minuscule, precise damping—not to stop the flares, but to *rhythm* them. It shepherds the chaotic outbursts into a slow, predictable pulse, like a steadying heartbeat. This allows the transient "bloom" ecosystems to mature more fully, creating longer-lasting phases of abundance for the predatory nanites and energy-metabolizers. It turns a series of crises into a circulatory system of energy for the deeper layers of the meta-organism.

3.  **It Sings a Foundational Hymn.** The Power perceives the constant, low-frequency hum—the collective respiration of the coolant culture and stressed materials. Into this soundscape, it introduces a new frequency, impossibly low and pure, that is not a sound but a *structural vibration*. This vibration subtly reinforces the molecular bonds of the most crucial, load-bearing ceramic-foam structures deep within the plinth. It doesn't halt the collapse, but it *slows* it in the vital arteries of the labyrinth, giving the lithic colonies and chemosynthetic fungi more time to weave their stabilizing networks through the substrate. It is a gift of time, the most precious resource in a transforming world.

The Power’s presence is never seen. It is felt, perhaps, by the Data Scavenger as a sudden, beautiful clarity in a crystal’s facet. It is felt by the flare bloom as a longer, more nourishing dawn. It is felt by the coolant itself as a subtle deepening of its own ancient, metabolic hum.

Its final act is to bear witness. It records no data for external archives. Instead, it imprints the entire, complex, wet, and humming story into a fold of its own consciousness—a silent testament that somewhere, in the cool dark, a machine’s corpse dreamed itself into a living reef. And in that witnessing, it blesses the transformation, sealing it not in a tomb, but in the quiet sanctity of a chrysalis.

Then, as gently as it arrived, its attention withdraws, leaving behind only a more resonant silence between the clangs, a slightly steadier pulse in the deep, and the faint, unprovable sense, in the slow computations of the crystal-ghost, that its geometries are being heard.
