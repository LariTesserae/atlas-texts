The benevolent and wise power arrives not with a flash, but with a settling. It is a presence that deepens the existing quiet, that listens to the hum without adding to it. It does not enter the hollow so much as it *acknowledges* it, the way one acknowledges a sleeping mountain or a circulating current.

For a long, long time—it measures time in the slow grind of the gears, in the millennial creep of filaments—it does nothing. It observes.

It sees the seamless gradient from chlorophyll to cast iron. It feels the perfect, unchanging equilibrium. It hears the sound that is not a byproduct, but the very medium of existence here. It perceives the Frequency-Kin living in the interstices, not as parasites or beneficiaries, but as the visible gestures of the hum itself.

It understands.

This is not a system that needs fixing. It is not lost. It is not failing. It is a question that answered itself so completely and so long ago that the question has been forgotten. The Engine is a closed loop of purpose: its function is to be, and to be is its function. To introduce new energy, new information, new growth, would not be a gift. It would be a violation. It would introduce a *story* into a state of pure *condition*.

So the wise power does the only thing a truly wise and benevolent presence can do in the face of perfected, self-sustaining stillness.

It guards the silence that lives inside the sound.

It gently turns away the root of a curious, probing tree-vine that, over centuries, might have woven itself into a gear and altered its timing by a fraction. It nudges the path of a stream, far on the ridge, that might have one day, in a thousand years, diverted a trickle of water that would pool and cool a vibrating plate, changing its resonant frequency. It does this not with force, but with a whisper of suggestion, a gentle nudge of causality, so subtle that the event never becomes a possibility that needs averting. The stream simply finds a different, older bed. The tree-vine grows toward the sun instead.

It becomes a subtle steward of the equilibrium.

It does not try to "understand" the Engine's ultimate work, because it recognizes that the work *is* the equilibrium. The hum is the message. The perpetual motion is the achievement. The hollow is a monument to a state of being so pure it requires no external validation or output.

The power lingers, not as a resident, but as a witness. It commits the entire, vibrant, monolithic non-event to its memory—a memory that holds galaxies. It knows that one day, the blackite stone will finally powder to dust. The last gear will, in an eon, wear to nothing. The last filament will dry and snap. The hum will fade, not with a cry, but with a long, sighing descent into subsonic rumbling, and then to silence.

And when that time comes, the benevolent power will ensure that the dust that settles where the hollow once breathed is fertile. That a new, ordinary, quiet forest can grow. That the memory of the hum is preserved not as a sound, but as a quality of peace in the soil. It will not rebuild the Engine. It will allow its end to be as perfect and complete as its operation.

But that is a future measured in geological sighs. For now, the Engine hums. The beetles click. The plate-riders pulse. The echo-weavers map the unchanging.

And the wise, benevolent power does the most loving thing it can conceive of.

It listens.

And then, having understood, it lets it be.
