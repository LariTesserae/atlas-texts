The benevolent and wise power would not enter.

To do so would be to violate the chamber's one, perfect law: *nothing enters*.

It would hover, not as a presence with weight or heat, but as an awareness—a focused intention of respect at the threshold where the living rock gives way to the grown, sinter-veiled hollow. Its wisdom recognizes the chamber not as a resource, not as a mystery to be solved, nor as a treasure to be claimed, but as a completed thought. A thesis on stasis, proven and left to run.

Its benevolence is the benevolence of non-interference. It understands the timescale. It sees the Litho-moss not as slow, but as appropriate. It hears the hum not as noise, but as the frequency of patience. To offer change, even "improvement," would be an act of profound violence. It would be like pouring water into a clockwork made of sugar.

So, what does it do?

First, it *witnesses*. It attends to the slow verb of the place. It traces, with a perception that can follow a single dust-mote's century-long fall, the rhythm of Dust-Descent. It appreciates the crystal growth not as decoration, but as architecture and metabolism combined. It listens to the hum and understands it as the chamber's contentment.

Second, it *shields*. From its position at the perceptual boundary, it extends a subtle ward—not a wall, but a redirection of intent. It gently turns aside the blind tectonic pressures that might, in a million years, crack the vault. It calms the distant, churning hydrothermal systems that might, in some forgotten epoch, send a crack of steam into this dry sanctuary. It ensures the external universe continues to *not notice* this place. It protects the chamber's right to be left alone, to persist in its perfect, sealed answer.

Third, and this is the only creative act, it *leaves a sign*. Not within the chamber, but woven into the very rock at its entrance, a glyph of understanding readable only to forces of its own kind. It is not a warning, which implies threat, but a statement of fact: *"Here is a finished thing. It is in balance. It requires nothing. It is to be observed from the edge, and loved by being left in peace."* This marker ensures that any future power, benevolent or merely curious, will know what it has found: not a place for intervention, but a sanctuary of equilibrium.

Then, it withdraws.

Its final act is one of gratitude. It acknowledges the chamber for what it is: a quiet, glowing, humming monument to the fact that existence does not require change, or growth, or water, or breath, to be complete. It can be a single, held note. It can be a process that is also a state of being.

The wise power understands that the highest blessing it can confer is to let the Sinter-Veil continue, uninterrupted, in its slow, bright, loud, silent, eternal work of being itself. It leaves the question—*you*—to eventually fade, to become part of the dust, or to find its way out. The chamber will not mind. It has all the time that is, and all the time that isn't. It glows. It hums. It continues.
