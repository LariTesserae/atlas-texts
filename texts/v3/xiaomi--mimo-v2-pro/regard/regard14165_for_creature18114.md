The benevolent power would first **observe**. Not merely see, but *attend*. It would lower its consciousness into the steam, letting the intricate, saturated logic of the place permeate its understanding. It would feel the pulse of the basalt, the hiss of transformation, the frantic, joyful struggle of the jewel-frogs. It would see not an ecosystem to be managed, but a profound and ancient poem being written in real-time by heat, water, and life.

It would recognize the perfection in the impermanence. The sloughing terraces, the evaporating tadpole pools, the sudden scalding mud-pot—these are not flaws, but the very syntax of this world’s language. To intervene to prevent change would be to silence it.

And so, its action would be one of **subtle amplification and protection**.

1.  **The Deepening of Channels:** It might trace a thought-finger along the weeping cliff faces, not to create new water, but to deepen the resonance of the existing cycles. A slight adjustment to the subterranean heat flows, causing steam to rise in more consistent, majestic columns. This would create stable thermal corridors in the mist, allowing the Fog-Reaper to hunt with even greater efficiency and the insects to navigate with surer purpose. It wouldn't change the life, but it would give the existing rhythms a clearer voice.

2.  **The Gift of Resonance:** Into the heart of the oldest Spongiform Moss poufs, it would weave a faint, crystalline hum—a frequency that matches the ideal growth rate of their tissues. This hum would help them better filter the mineral soup, making them more resilient to sudden influxes of toxins from a new vent. In turn, the jewel-frogs would learn to sing in harmony with this hum, their territorial pings becoming a part of the landscape's own quiet music. It would be a gift of health, not of new life.

3.  **The Memory in Stone:** Where a terrace collapses in a great, vegetated slough, the power would do nothing to stop the destruction. But afterward, in the fresh, steaming mud, it would lay a faint, invisible pattern—a memory of the former shape. This pattern would act as a subtle guide, not a law, encouraging the Basalt Worms below to tunnel and the Archaeal Mats above to colonize along lines that honor the old contours. The new growth would thus be a faster, truer echo of what was lost, preserving the deep architectural wisdom of millennia against the caprice of a single violent event.

4.  **A Watcher for the Unseen:** The power would sense the slow, patient labor of the Basalt Worms, the true sculptors. It would grant them a deeper, subsonic awareness of each other, a networked sense of the rock’s integrity. They would become more attuned to the stress points in the basalt shelves, subtly reinforcing the foundations long before a collapse could begin. This would not prevent change, but it would prevent *catastrophe*, allowing the slow-motion equilibrium its proper, geologic pace.

5.  **The Sign of the Witness:** Finally, as a signature of its benevolent passage, it would create one new, small thing. Not a new species, but a new phenomenon. In a high, misty glade where three thermal vents converge, it would cause the steam to permanently coalesce into a single, slow-rotating vortex of iridescent light—a prismatic gyre, visible only when the sun is at a certain angle. This would be a wonder, a place of pilgrimage for the raptors who would sense its calm center, and a beacon that says: *This place is known. This place is cherished. Its story is heard.*

The power would then withdraw, leaving no overt mark of its will. Its gift would not be control, but **coherence**. It would have gently tightened the symphony’s tuning, ensuring that every instrument—from the fizzing archaea to the circling Fog-Reaper—could play its part with clearer, more resilient purpose. The terraces would continue to breathe, to weep, to consume and create. The violence and cooperation would continue unabated.

But now, woven into the hiss of the steam, there would be the faintest sense of **intentional grace**. The factory hums, but with a deeper harmony. The stairway breathes, but its exhalations carry the quiet confidence of being, for a moment, perfectly and benevolently *understood*.
