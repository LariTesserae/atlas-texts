The denizens of the Gravel Marches are the land itself, dreaming in motion. They are processes given temporary, purposeful form—a geology that learned to want.

**The Architects of Scale**
*   **The Lithic Choir:** Not a creature, but a condition. In certain basins, the ambient groan of stressed rock focuses into a standing wave of pressure. Here, the very gravel arranges itself into vast, concave dishes and helical basins, resonating chambers that amplify and modulate the foundational sound. These are the continent's organs of hearing, tuning the deep vibrations, filtering the noise of its own slow agony. Microscopic quartz filaments within the gravel orient and click in response, acting as a mineral nervous system that transmits "sound-weather" across the plain.
*   **The Pacing Leviathans:** Beyond the Tectonic Beetles are the **Graben-Walkers**. These are mile-long, centipedal chains of linked basalt plates, moving not on legs, but via a synchronized, rolling contraction of the gravel beneath them. They trace the paths of ancient fault lines, their immense weight and passage deepening these fractures. They do not eat or breed; they *maintain*. Their slow pilgrimage *is* the tectonic activity, a ritual of pressure that prevents the landscape from locking into complete, static rigidity. Their digestive tract is a moving canyon of grinding plates, pulverizing overly large boulders into manageable shards for the lesser fauna.

**The Scurrying Specialists**
*   **Clatter-Swarm Variants:** The common iron pyrite swarms are merely the background noise. Specialized swarms exist:
    *   **Cairn-Kin:** Composed of smooth, magnetic lodestone. They don't skitter; they flow in slow, deliberate rivers, piling themselves into precise, temporary cairns around geothermal brine vents. The cairns act as condensers and filters, accelerating mineral deposition. Once the vent is "tamed," the Cairn-Kin disassemble and flow to a new disturbance.
    *   **Echo Mites:** Tiny, sharp shards of obsidian and glass. They inhabit the zones of folded space, their purpose to stabilize perception. They move in dizzying, refractive clouds, catching and re-aligning light and sound. Where they swarm, reality "knits" back together—if they depart, the smudging and perceptual slippage returns. Their chitter is a faint, glassy ringing, a calibration signal.
*   **The Weavers:** In mist-heavy depressions, you find the **Silica Spinners**. These are slow, anemone-like formations of spiking crystal clusters. From their tips, they extrude fine, impossibly strong threads of glass fiber, catching mineral droplets from the mist. They weave these into rigid, intricate lattices—transparent webs that span crevices. These are not for catching prey, but for *acoustic baffling*. They dampen and muffle the constant hiss and clatter in their immediate vicinity, creating pockets of eerie, focused silence. Other creatures, like the sensitive **Pitch-Sense Beetles**, use these silent zones as rest points.

**The Metabolic Engineers**
*   **Rust-Refinery Symbionts:** The colossal Refinery is not a single entity, but a colonial organism. Its "life" is maintained by:
    *   **Scour-Scuttlers:** Armadillo-like rolls of linked magnetite plates that crawl its flanks, their abrasive bodies scraping away excess oxidation, preventing the Refinery from sealing its own vents with a solid crust of rust.
    *   **Brine-Vessel Worms:** Blind, tube-dwelling creatures of flexible salt crystal. They insert themselves into the Refinery's weeping fluid channels, regulating flow, absorbing excess sulfur compounds, and secreting refined ochre pigment in more stable, solid forms—tiny, perfect spheres of pure iron oxide that roll down to become part of the gravel mosaic.
*   **The Combustion Kelp:** Near low-grade thermal fissures (not hot, just less cold), fields of **Pyrite Fronds** grow. These brittle, metallic "plants" have a core of reactive carboniferous slate. Through a slow exothermic oxidation process (they rust, but from the inside out), they generate faint internal warmth. This energy is harvested by **Current-Lice**, minuscule, multi-legged creatures of conductive copper that swarm over them, forming living circuits that redistribute tiny electrical charges to nearby clatter-swarms.

**The Uncanny Entities**
*   **Mist-Phantoms:** In the most perceptually unstable zones, the mist itself coalesces into temporary, suggestive forms. Not creatures with intent, but the landscape's subconscious made visible: a drifting, ghostly outline of a **Strata-Serpent**, a rolling **Cloud of Grinding Teeth**, or a **Flock of Silent, Folding Wings**. They are optical and acoustic illusions given weight by the mineral-charged air, lasting only moments before dissolving back into the haze. They are the Marches' dreams of other kinds of life, forever unattainable.
*   **The Pace-Keepers:** The strangest are those that manipulate time-scales. **Epoch-Mites** are rare, smooth, pebble-like creatures that radiate a localized stasis field. Within a yard of one, crystalline growth accelerates to visible speed; Clatter-Swarms buzz past in frantic hyper-speed; while your own limbs feel lethargic, stuck in the slow-time of the Beetles. They do not feed on matter, but on the *differential* in temporal flow, existing in the stress between the now and the ancient.

To the inhabitants, the human concepts of "birth," "death," "hunger," and "shelter" are meaningless. They have **formation, abrasion, resonance, and deposition.** Their society is a single, interconnected metabolism of rock and water. The sound is not just their environment; it is their language, their weather, and their collective thought. To listen to the Gravel Marches is to hear its population thinking, very, very slowly, in a vocabulary of groans, clicks, and the endless, wet hiss of change.
