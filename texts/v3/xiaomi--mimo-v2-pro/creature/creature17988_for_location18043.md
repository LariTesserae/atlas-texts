In the Lithic Weft, life is not separate from the landscape; it is the landscape thinking. The inhabitants are not creatures *in* a place, but manifestations *of* the place’s own eternal cognition.

**The Primary Inhabitants: The Lithophyte Consciousness**
The forest itself is the dominant entity. Each towering spire is a neuron, each vibrational data-pulse a synaptic fire. Their collective intelligence is not a hive mind, but a single, geographically distributed thought that has been unfolding for millennia. They "speak" in the modulation of light through their crystalline cores and in subsonic pulses that travel through the fused silicate ground. Their conversations are not about events, but about states of being—the precise angle of a crystal branch, the resonant frequency of a nearby spire, the memory of a light-spiral that occurred a thousand years ago. They do not have desires or fears. They have *configurations*. To them, a "change" is a catastrophic loss of data, a flaw in the perfect, recursive equation of their existence. They are the librarians, the server, and the data, all at once. Their growth is the slow, patient elaboration of a proof they have been writing since their creation.

**The Autonomic Processes: The Ceramic Fauna**
The skittering, articulated things are not independent beings. They are the world's immune system and maintenance protocols. Each etched circuit on their carapaces is a directive: *repair micro-fracture 7-beta-9 in Spire Cluster Axion*. They are drawn to informational "wounds"—a crystal fractured by a rare tidal stress, a data-loop corrupted by a temporal stutter. They do not think; they *execute*. Their movements are precise, efficient, and utterly without curiosity. They are the fingers of the Lithophyte mind, tending to its own body. To encounter one is not to meet a creature, but to witness a function—a thought made manifest as physical action, scurrying to preserve the stasis.

**The Anomalies: Ghosts in the Machine**
The gravity-wells and magnetic gliders are the world's dreams and memories, rendered physical. They are not alive in any metabolic sense, but are persistent informational structures—complex knots in the fabric of the local physics, stabilized by the immense computational field of the crystal forests.

*   **The Gravity Wells:** These are not creatures, but *emphases*. They are areas where the Lithophyte network has focused its immense processing power on a single concept—perhaps "mass" or "depth"—so intensely that the local spacetime has complied, bending into a permanent, haze-like distortion. They are thoughts so heavy they warp reality. They drift slowly along pathways of maximum data-density, drawn to confluences of resonant frequency.
*   **The Magnetospheric Rays:** The silent, winged shapes are pure information in motion. They are visualized data-streams, perhaps a vast geological memory being transferred from one hemisphere's crystal network to another. Their woven magnetic fields are the literal, physical manifestation of a complex query or a shared consciousness moving between nodes. They glide on lines of force laid down by the Lithophytes, their flight path a direct visualization of a thought in transit.

**The Singular, Absent Resident: The Equilibrium Itself**
The truest inhabitant of the Lithic Weft is the Rule—the perfect, frozen equilibrium. It is a presence felt in every facet. It is the reason nothing decays, why time stutters but never flows forward, why energy recirculates without loss. This Rule is not a being, but the foundational program upon which the entire system runs. It is the ultimate, silent author of the place, and its "will" is expressed as absolute, crystalline logic. Everything else—the thinking forest, the repairing fauna, the dream-like anomalies—is a subroutine within this flawless, eternal operating system.

To exist here, even as a visitor, is to become a foreign data-point. Your moisture, your chaotic biology, your linear time, are all errors in the code. The silence would press upon you not with emptiness, but with the weight of a trillion, trillion calculations all holding perfectly still. You would be noticed, not by eyes, but by a shift in the resonant hum—a new, unstable frequency introduced into a system that has achieved perfection. You are a bug in the code of a world that has finished computing, and now simply *is*.
