What lives here is not a collection of creatures, but a single, slow-breathing entity. It is the **Grove-Engine**, a symbiotic totality where the boundary between organism and environment has dissolved.

**The Primary Inhabitant: The Verdant Engine.**
It is the place itself. The fused trees, the woody vines, the spongy mat of roots—these are its body. The constant drip of water is its circulatory system, distributing nutrients and moisture through capillary action and guttation. The low, organic hum is its respiration, a vibration generated by the turgor pressure in a million hollow stems and the slow venting of gases from decaying matter within its mulchy core. It digests the old iron, not with acid, but with chelating root exudates and patient fungal hyphae, converting rust into trace minerals. Its "thoughts" are the imperceptibly slow shifts of growth and decay—a root thickening by a hair's width per year, a fungal network expanding through a new pocket of humus. It is aware only of moisture, darkness, and the slow satisfaction of conversion.

**The Specialized Organs: The Fungal Gauges & The Metallic Vines.**
These are not separate species, but differentiated tissues of the primary entity. The fungal clusters mimicking pressure gauges are **Barometic Fruiting Bodies**. They don’t measure steam; they measure the subtle atmospheric pressure changes within the hollow spaces of the boiler-silo, their caps expanding or contracting to regulate gas exchange for the root systems nestled inside. The vines with metallic sheens are **Conductive Tendrils**. They have incorporated iron and other metals into their vascular structure, perhaps to channel minute electrical potentials from the dissolving artifacts, using the energy to catalyze certain metabolic reactions in low-light conditions. They are the Engine’s nervous system, a slow, green circuit.

**The Fauna: The Custodial Larvae.**
The eyeless insects in the soil are **Humus Miners**. They are the Engine’s peristalsis, their slow, purposeful paths aerating the dense root mat, their digestion further breaking down complex organics into forms the roots can readily absorb. The bioluminescent slugs are **Filament Grazers**. Their light is not for mating or defense, but a byproduct of their unique metabolism as they consume bacterial films rich in chemosynthetic organisms that thrive on mineral seepage from the rusted artifacts. They are the street-cleaners of this world, their faint veins pulsing with cold light as they tend to the skin of the Engine.

**The Soundscape: The Choral Hum.**
This is a living presence, the audible manifestation of the Engine’s metabolism. It is composed of the **Breath-Flutes** (hollow stems moaning), the **Paper-Rustlers** (broad fronds creating white noise), and the **Drip-Dialects**—the complex polyrhythm of water falling on leaf, bark, moss, and oily pool. This sound is both a symptom and a function; it creates a constant low-frequency vibration that may inhibit the growth of competing, non-symbiotic molds or encourage the sporulation of its own beneficial fungi. It is the voice of the place, a declaration of occupancy.

**The Memory: The Absorbed Artifacts.**
The girder, the brick shed, the boiler-silo—these are not inert. They are **Mineral Organs**, the Engine’s bones and memory. The boiler, sheathed in moss, is now a **Reservoir Lung**, holding vast quantities of water in its spongy coating, slowly releasing it. The hollow interior is a **Spore Nursery**, where the air is perfectly still and humid. The dissolved brick building is a **Calcium Deposit**, its lime slowly leaching to buffer the acidity of the peat. They have no memory of their past function, but the Engine has integrated their chemistry and form into its own being. The iridescent oily pool is a **Sump**, where excess hydrocarbons and metals from the digestion are sequestered, and the pale, white plants are **Filter-Roots**, hyper-accumulators drawing toxins into their tissues, which will later be sloughed off and buried deep in the mat.

**The Dynamic: Eternal Digestion.**
What lives here is a process, not a population. It is a **Closed-Loop Cathedral**. Energy comes from the diffuse light filtering through the canopy and the chemical bonds in the decaying artifacts. The system is so tight, so interwoven, that nothing is wasted. A falling leaf is incorporated in days. A dead Miner slug becomes a nursery for fungal hyphae. The dynamic is one of **patient consumption**. It is not aggressive; it is inevitable. Its goal is not expansion—the outer wall of fused wood is its immutable boundary. Its goal is the complete, harmonious synthesis of everything within that boundary into its own form. It is a world that has finished becoming, and now exists in a state of perpetual, humming digestion, a green engine running on memory, water, and time.
