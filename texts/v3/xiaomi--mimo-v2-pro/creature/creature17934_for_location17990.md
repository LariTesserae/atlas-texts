Who lives on the God-Steps? Not beings, but becomings. Not residents, but processes that have learned to wear the skin of life.

**First, the Ground Itself Breathes.**
The ceramicite is not merely inhabited; it is *inhabiting*. Its capillary networks pulse with a slow, engineered sap—a nutrient-rich fluid carrying microorganisms that are part maintenance crew, part memory. In the deepest pores, **silent, calcium-shelled archaea** metabolize trace minerals, subtly reshaping the micro-architecture of the stone over centuries. They are the patient editors of the structure, their work manifesting as the gradual smoothing of a stair-riser or the sudden, millennia-blossoming of a new seep. They are the first, and perhaps the truest, residents.

**Then, the Green Unfurls.**
The plants are not passive. The **bioluminescent ferns** are connected via subterranean mycelial networks woven through the vesicles. They communicate. A slow, rippling pulse of blue light across a terrace is not random—it is a warning of a pest swarm, or a shift in nutrient density, passed from frond to frond at the speed of chemistry. The **waxy trumpet-flowers** have evolved a symbiosis beyond the hummingbirds. Their heavy, spicy nectar contains mild psychoactive compounds that induce a state of focused lethargy in their pollinators, ensuring the birds methodically visit hundreds of blooms in a single, trance-like circuit, perfect cross-pollination insured by engineered instinct.

**The Fauna are the Moving Parts.**
The **jewel-beetles** are more than insects. Their iridescent carapaces are photovoltaic. They bask on sun-warmed ceramic, storing energy, then descend into dark crevices to discharge it as gentle heat, incubating the eggs of other, non-flying insects in a barter system for their honeydew. They are living batteries and midwives.

The **hummingbirds** are avian jewels, but some have adapted further. The **Mist-Needle** is a species with translucent wing membranes and a body of such low thermal mass it can hover directly in the column of rising, humid air from a vent, feeding on the microscopic condensation-midges that swarm there. They are more sprite than bird, seen only as a shimmer and a hum.

The **float-jellies** are the most ethereal citizens. They are colonial organisms—clusters of specialized cells animated by a shared nervous system of gossamer threads. They are the atmosphere’s plankton. Some have adapted to the sonic environment; their gelatinous bells contain resonant chambers that vibrate in harmony with the deep, structural hum. By clustering, they can create localized harmonic dampening or amplification, shaping the sound-scape of a terrace. When one dies, it doesn't fall. It dissolves into a nutrient-rich mist, feeding the canopy trees from above.

**And Then, the Weird, the Caretakers.**
In the damp, shady undersides of the buttress-root systems, **Litho-Moss Weavers** construct their homes. These are not animals, but something like social, ambulatory slime molds. They extrude threads of calcite and organic silk, weaving intricate, bell-shaped snares across ceramic alcoves. These snares don't catch prey; they collect condensation and, through electrostatic attraction, gather drifting spores and pollen. The Weavers then slowly "digest" this harvest, but they are also gardeners. They selectively deposit viable seeds and spores into the ceramic vesicles, guided by chemical signals from the archaea below. They are the active agents of the erosion aesthetic, the patient, blind landscapers of the Gods.

There are no predators, only balances. The **Condensation Cycle** is the ultimate rhythm of life. When the turbines swell into their deep, thoracic hum and the air grows thick enough to drink, everything awakens. Ferns glow brighter to attract spore-dispersing midges. Trumpet-flowers exhale concentrated plumes of scent. The jewel-beetles align their carapaces to refract light, creating dazzling, temporary patterns to confuse aerial parasites. It is a silent, shimmering opera of coordinated response.

What lives here is a single, distributed consciousness woven from stone, water, plant, and creature. It is a garden that tends itself, a clock where every gear is alive. The absent builders did not create inhabitants; they initiated a single, sprawling, self-perpetuating *verb*. To stand here is to be inside that verb. You are not watching life. You are, for a brief moment, part of its slow, complex, and eternal sentence.
