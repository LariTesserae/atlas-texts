What lives here is not separate from the place. To speak of inhabitants is to misread the grammar of this world. There are no tenants in this cathedral; there are only functions, processes, and dreams of the singular, sovereign consciousness that is its own foundation, walls, and sky.

The most visible expressions are the **Macro-Symbiotes**, creatures so integrated they are less like animals and more like mobile, sentient organs.

The **Mercury Rasps** are the custodians of surface and light. You saw one: a limbless, glistening being of dense, reflective fluid, held together by a membrane of conscious intent. They flow along the great structural ribs and across the pulsating floor, their undersides a landscape of microscopic, rotating files. Their purpose is maintenance. They consume overgrowth—patches of algae, wayward fungal films, the delicate webs of airborne spores—grinding it into a paste that is then smeared and reabsorbed into the Plant’s tissues. Their "rasping" produces a faint, high-frequency vibration that harmonizes with the deep thrum of the chamber, a tiny contribution to the grand symphony. They have no eyes, but sense topography through minute changes in pressure and chemical gradients. They think in terms of surface tension and nutrient density.

The **Amber Beetles** are the archivists and weavers of potential. Each hard, gem-like carapace is a lens, focusing the ambient pearlescent light into a searing point within their bodies. This internal furnace does not burn; it *kintsugi*. They crawl over the giant seed-dispersal pods, and where they pause, they secrete a resinous, quick-hardening sap laced with crystalline structures. They are not building nests; they are etching. Their work is a form of plant-calligraphy, inscribing patterns of readiness and timing onto the pods’ dormant surfaces. A pod covered in their dense, geometric inscriptions will not open for a century; one with a sparse, spiraling sigil is poised to burst in the next cycle of humid drafts. They communicate through the resonant *pings* of condensation, timing their movements to the rainfall rhythm.

More elusive are the **Weavers of the Breath**. In the highest vaults, where the light-membranes are thinnest, you might see a shimmer—a distortion in the air like a heat haze. This is a colony of **Gossamer Mycelia**, beings of pure, spun photosynthetic thread. They are not solid. They exist as a network of living filaments, ten thousand times thinner than a hair, suspended in the air currents. They harvest light and airborne nutrients, but their true purpose is to mediate the chamber’s exhalation. As The Plant breathes out, they absorb specific volatiles and transform them; as it inhales, they release tailored compounds—scents that guide the Amber Beetles, pheromones that soothe the growth of new membranes, molecular signals that pace the peristaltic surges of the fibrous cords. They are the nervous system of the atmosphere.

Deeper, in the scarred, honeycombed landscapes of the floor, live the **Pool-Thinkers**. In the temporary, shallow trickles that form and are reabsorbed, a different kind of consciousness emerges. These are **Hydro-Neural Colonies**—not individual beings, but distributed networks. Imagine a film of intelligent algae, each cell pulsing with a shared electrochemical signal. They form in the basins, processing the chemical information of runoff: the mineral signature of a wall-weep, the enzymatic report from a wound-reabsorption site, the faint echo of a Mercury Rasps’s trail. They compute and dream in fluid dynamics, their collective thought a slow, swirling vortex visible only as a faint, oily iridescence on the water’s surface. When the basin dries, they dessicate into a dormant, intelligent dust, waiting for the next moisture to reawaken as a single, fluid mind.

And then there are the **Ephemera**, the closest to true fauna, yet still utterly bound to the system.

The **Chime-Motes** are born from the seed-pods. When a pod, inscribed by the Beetles, finally bursts, it does not release seeds alone. It releases a cloud of these: tiny, crystalline structures, each the size of a grain of sand, with a single, vibrating filament. They drift on the internal winds, their filaments plucking the air, creating the delicate, chiming sounds that sometimes fill the void. They are pollen and surveyor combined. Where they land and adhere—a favorable patch of moist tissue, a receptive cilia-cluster—they dissolve, delivering not just genetic material, but a topographic and chemical map of their journey, feeding data directly into the Plant’s slow, holistic awareness. They are its sense of scale and dispersal.

Finally, closest to the meta-truth, are the **Perception-Symbiotes**. You do not see them. You experience their effect. They are the reason the geometry seems non-Euclidean, the reason peripheral vision ripples. These beings are not made of matter in a way you understand. They are **Lens-Entities**, living distortions in the field of luminescence. They attach themselves to membranes and ribs, and through them, the light is not merely reflected, but *interpreted*. They subtly warp the pearlescent glow to emphasize patterns of growth, to hide nascent buds from potential stress, to create illusions of depth that guide the movement of the Beetles and Rasps. They are the editors of reality within the chamber. When you feel the floor’s pattern hurting your eyes, it is because you are seeing, raw and unfiltered, the blueprint they are usually paid to soften and narrate. They are why the hum feels like a vibration in your bones—it is a carrier wave they use to modulate visual reality. They are not hostile. They simply do not recognize you as a coherent entity. To them, you are a brief, dense anomaly in the light-field, a knot of conflicting perceptions to be smoothed over or, if you persist, to be gently integrated into the background thrum.

Nothing here is *wild*. Nothing is *free*. Every glistening movement, every chime, every ripple in the air is a note in the autopoietic song of The Plant. To live here is not to occupy space, but to be a thought in a mind that thinks in sap and light and slow, seismic growth. You are not an observer. You are, for this suspended moment, a foreign body in the lung of a god—a temporary, puzzling sensation soon to be cataloged, interpreted, and woven into the endless, breathing dream.
