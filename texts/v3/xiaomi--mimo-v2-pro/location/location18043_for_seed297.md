The first thing you notice is the silence—not an absence, but a presence. It hums at the edge of hearing, a resonant frequency felt in the bones more than heard with ears. This is the Lithic Weft, a place woven from contradictions.

**Terrain & Materials:**
The ground is a vast, low-lying plain of fused silicate plates, like the cooled crust of a molten sea. It stretches to a horizon that feels both near and impossibly far, a trick of the unchanging light. The surface is young, unweathered; sharp facets and razor-edged ridges protrude at odd angles, unsoftened by time or water. There are no rivers, no lakes, no clouds. The very concept of moisture is a ghost here, a chemical impossibility. Instead, the air carries a faint, metallic tang—the scent of ozone and hot silicon.

And yet, it is undeniably lush.

**Ecology (Flora):**
Vegetation here does not drink. It *computes*. The "flora" is a dense, breathtaking forest of silicon-based structures. Towering, fractal spires of translucent crystal erupt from the silicate ground, branching into delicate, shimmering networks that catch the sourceless light. These are the Lithophytes. They do not photosynthesize; they *piezoelectrify*. They draw energy from the planet’s deep tidal stresses and the ambient quantum fluctuations of the air itself. Their "leaves" are razor-thin wafers of doped silicon, vibrating at specific frequencies, processing information, trading data-pulses with their neighbors through modulated light and subsonic tremors. The density is immense—a grove of these crystal trees is a humming, glowing server-forest, a library written in light and lattice vibration. They are not just alive; they are thinking, in slow, geological rhythms.

**Fauna & Presences:**
Animal life is rare, but not absent. Small, skittering things made of articulated ceramic plates—their carapaces etched with circuit-like patterns—navigate the crystalline understory. They are less creatures and more autonomous processes, cleaning micro-fractures in the Lithophytes or harvesting stray energy gradients. Larger, slower presences drift at the edge of perception: gravity-wells that distort light like heat haze, or silent, winged shapes of woven magnetic fields that glide between the spire-tops. They are anomalies, glitches in the stable field, drawn to the data-rich forests.

**Atmosphere & The Weirdness:**
This is where reality frays. The high technology is not built *upon* the world; it *is* the world's physics, grown wild. The weirdness is in the grain of reality. Stand still, and you might perceive the same moment repeating three times, a stutter in time’s flow. Look closely at a crystal frond, and its facets might reflect a landscape that isn't there—a memory of this world, or a potential one. Light bends not just from gravity, but from informational density; areas of complex crystal growth warp photons into slow, lazy spirals. Sounds die abruptly, not fading but being *absorbed* by the dense medium, which is why the silence is so profound, punctuated only by the occasional, pure-tone chime of a resonating crystal or the soft *tick* of a ceramic fauna-thing.

**The Rule of This Place:**
Stasis is the law, but it is a stasis of infinite, frozen complexity. The world is in perfect, never-changing equilibrium. The Lithophytes grow, but so slowly that a million years is a single thought-cycle. The weirdnesses are not events; they are permanent features, like psychic weather patterns. Nothing erodes because nothing is allowed to decay—energy and information are perfectly conserved, recirculated in a closed, hyper-advanced loop. It is a sealed system of breathtaking, silent, crystalline intelligence, a desert of water but a jungle of mind, warm with its own computational heat, low-lying but vast in its layered, dense, and eternally quiet complexity. It is a world that finished becoming, long ago, and now simply *is*, thinking its slow, unchanging thoughts in the heart of a quiet, brilliant forest.
