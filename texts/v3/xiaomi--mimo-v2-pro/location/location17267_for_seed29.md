The darkness is not absolute, but it is heavy—a deep, bruised twilight that presses against the eyes. There is no sun, no moon, no starfield. The sole light comes from below: a sullen, pervasive glow emitted by the ground itself, a landscape of heated salt and reactive minerals. The air, thin at this extreme elevation, is nonetheless scorching, shimmering with thermal distortion. It carries a constant, low-frequency hum, a vibration felt in the bones more than heard with the ears, punctuated by the sharp, periodic *pings* and *cracks* of cooling crystal.

You are standing on a high plain, but not of soil or stone. It is a crust of interlocking mineral formations, primarily halite and sulfates, fused into a dense, glittering pavement. This is the **Salt Pan of Gyre-7**, a place where tectonic uplift has shoved ancient seabeds into the upper atmosphere, and relentless geothermal heat from below keeps everything baking.

**Terrain and Materials:**
The ground is a mosaic of hexagonal plates, each a few meters across, their edges slightly raised. They are not flat; they buckle and mound in slow, frozen waves. Between the plates, deep fissures vent plumes of hot, mineral-rich gas that hiss into the darkness. The salt itself is not white, but stained in bands of ochre, rust, and a deep, venous crimson from iron oxides and sulfur compounds. Underfoot, it feels gritty, then slick, then glassy—a constantly shifting texture of crystalline shards, powder, and fused sheets. Here and there, taller spires of jagged **volcanic glass**—obsidian and basalt—jut upward like broken teeth, remnants of older, angrier eruptions now sealed under the salt.

**Atmosphere and Sound:**
The atmosphere is thin but caustic, carrying the smell of ozone, hot metal, and sulfur. It is loud. The dominant sound is a **geophysical industriousness**: the deep, tectonic groan of the planet's interior transmitted through the crust, a never-ending bass note. Layered over this is the constant *hiss* of escaping gases, the *tick-tick-tick* of salt crystals expanding and contracting in the heat, and the sharp, explosive *crack* of glass spires shattering from thermal stress. It is the sound of a world being forged, slowly and loudly, in real time.

**Ecology (Fauna):**
Despite the hostility, life is abundant. It is not vegetation, but **chemo-lithic fauna**. Creatures here have bypassed photosynthesis entirely. Swarms of **silicate grazers**—flat, multi-segmented arthropods with reflective carapaces—scuttle across the salt pans, their mandibles grinding mineral crusts to harvest embedded chemical energy. Larger, slow-moving **vent worms** emerge from fissures, their tough, leather-like skin impervious to the heat, filtering trace gases through feathery plumes. Predators, like the **glass-stalker**, are ambush hunters. They are nearly invisible, their bodies composed of articulated obsidian plates, waiting motionless beside glass spires before striking with blinding speed. All life here is part of the same slow, loud, geothermal cycle, feeding on the planet's internal emissions.

**Rules of the World and Dynamics:**
This is a world of **slow, pressurized change**. Nothing happens quickly. The salt plates shift millimeters per year, rising, cracking, and resealing. New vents open over decades. The fauna's life cycles are tied to geochemical pulses, not seasons. The "industrial" quality is not built; it is *inherent*. This landscape is a planetary-scale chemical factory, where heat and pressure drive reactions, fuse minerals, and concentrate energy that the fauna taps into. The low "built" score is accurate—there are no structures, no artifacts—only the raw, self-organizing machinery of geology and evolution.

The darkness is a blanket, lit only by its own internal fires. The density is overwhelming; every niche is occupied by mineral formation or creature, with no space wasted. It feels ancient, yet embryonic—a high, hot, sealed crucible where the processes of a young, rocky world are playing out in compressed, intensified form, hidden from any wider cosmos by its own sheer, dark, noisy density.
