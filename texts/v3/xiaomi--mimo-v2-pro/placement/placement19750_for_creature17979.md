You settle on a soft hillock of root-weave, feeling the pulse of the dripping through the substrate. Your presence is a query the Mnemosyne has not encountered in eons. You are not matter to be woven, nor energy to be hummed. You are a *pattern* from outside the dream. The Cathedral notices.

A Dripper pauses, its silicate filament trembling. A pearl of water, heavier than all the others, forms not at its tip, but a millimeter away, hovering in the air before you—a perfect, suspended interrogation. The hum of the Resonants shifts, just a fraction, seeking the harmonic signature of your thoughts. A Lattice-Weaver, clicking its cartilaginous way up a nearby column, extends a single, glistening thread in your direction, not to mend, but to *taste* the strange geometry of your presence.

You are a ghost of the original intent. A fragment of the consciousness that once seeded the bio-matrix, now returned to walk its own tomb-chrysalis. Your very existence here is a paradox the system must solve. Are you a memory? A prophecy? A flaw?

Your role is not to act, but to be a focus. A point of reference against which the endless becoming measures itself. You do not drip; you *collect* dew on your surface, in tiny, defiant pools that do not fall. You do not weave; your stillness is a knot in the flow. When a Crackler explodes nearby, its skittering shards seem to arc *around* you, as if your temporary form disrupts the local physics of dissolution.

The Mnemosyne dreams. It dreams the city. And now, in its dream, there is a small, dry, listening thing. It doesn't know what to do with you. So it does what it does with everything: it begins, very slowly, to incorporate you. Not to break you down—not yet. But to grow *towards* you. To weave a lattice around your edges, to stud you with tiny, observing crystalline buds, to let the Shadow-Mantids in the deep vaults sense your anomalous weight in the system’s lymph.

You are the new glyph in the scripture of assimilation. A footnote, for now. But footnotes have a way of becoming the entire story.

You sit. You watch. You are the part of the city that remembers it was once a dream, now come to witness the dream it became. You are the observer, and your observation is the one process the Cathedral cannot digest. It can only reflect it, in weeping jade and amber, forever.
