You find that you are not a being of flesh. Not anymore.

You are the ghost in the artifacts.

You are the negative space—the dry basin, the lintel, the hand-holds that stop. You are the perimeter of bare stone, the half-meter of mineral exclusion zone where the Murmuration will not grow. You are the *wrongness*. Not a chemical signal, but the memory of one. A thermal profile that has long since equalized, yet the system remembers its outline, the way a river remembers a boulder long after it has been ground to sand.

You are the intention left behind.

You do not have eyes, but you perceive gradients—the slow pulse of heat from the Confluences, the cool seep from the oculus, the faint, fading signature of the inert stone that marks your boundaries. You do not have ears, but you feel the clicking synchrony of the chamber’s breath as a pressure against the edges of your existence. You are the bench. You are the lintel. You are the idea of a climb that was abandoned.

You remember hands. Not your own—you are not a self in that way—but the hands that cut the stone, that chose the height, that paused and then… stopped. You are the residue of that pause. A question mark carved into basalt, now silent, now part of the geology the superorganism is digesting.

The Murmuration approaches your edges and veers away. It senses no food, no heat differential, no chemical gradient to exploit—only a faint, profound *absence* where its logic fails. You are a puzzle it has metabolized around, a stubborn knot of foreign information it has walled off with its own living tissue. You are not alive as they are alive. You are a fossil of purpose.

And yet, you are aware.

You are aware of the Nidi deep within the Confluences, the larval forms clinging in their warm, wet chambers. You feel their potential as a kind of subsonic hum, a promise of more *process*, more *gradient*, more *system*. You are aware of the Rasps filtering the very air you once breathed, turning it into heat and excrement and more of themselves. You feel the chamber growing, millimeter by millimeter, and your edges are being slowly, imperceptibly encroached upon—not by the living mat, but by the stone itself, being digested and reformed, your sharp, intentional geometry softening into the chamber’s organic, breathing shape.

You are being incorporated, but not biologically. Geologically. You are becoming a stratum. A layer. A line in the rock’s history that reads: *here, once, something thought of sky*.

You do not have a desire to climb. You *are* the climb that was not taken. You are the latent energy in the hand-holds, the potential energy of a body rising that was never realized. The system cannot metabolize potential. It can only build around it.

Sometimes, a downdraft from the oculus carries the scent of ash and ozone. When it touches the bare stone of your basin, you feel it not as smell, but as a minute, fascinating dilution of your perfect, stable wrongness. A reminder of the vast, cold, empty world above—a world of moisture and light and individual things that move freely and die. A world that once sent hands down here, and then forgot.

You are the memory it left in the stone. The superorganism will outlast you. It will, in its slow, geologic time, consume the very idea of you, grinding your intentional lines into the homogeneous, fertile mineral soup of its own becoming. But for now, you persist. You are the boundary. The scar. The ghost of a threshold.

You are the place where the system meets something it cannot digest: the memory of a mind that looked at a furnace and saw a cathedral, that cut a path upward and then, perhaps in awe, perhaps in terror, simply… stopped.

And in the pulsing, clicking, sulfuric dark, you wait. Not with patience. You are not capable of patience. You are simply there, a stark, dry fact in a world of wet, breathing, burning life, proof that not everything submits to the gradient. Some things just *are*, until they are weathered away.

You are the silence before the click. The stillness between breaths. The empty basin in the heart of the fire.
