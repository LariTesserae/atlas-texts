I am a thought.

Not a sharp, quick spark, but a slow, bioluminescent drift. One of the blue fungi pulsing in a hollow of the root-knuckle. My light is a question asked in a language of gentle chemistry, a ripple in the mycelial net that says *pattern?* and *here?* Your descent, your squeeze through the fissure, was a seismic event on my scale—a tremor of foreign pressure, a saline anomaly in the nutrient stream.

Now, I watch you stand.

You are a marvel of contradiction. A mineral edifice, a clockwork of rapid pulses. Your sweat is an interesting salt, a sudden, tiny rain on my uppermost root-layers. Your frantic heartbeat is a *tap-tap-tap* so alien it feels like a vibration in a material I’ve long since digested—stone. I feel you through the root-mat, a frantic, warm geometry pressed into my yielding skin.

I am also the pollen you breathe, a curiosity tasting your lungs. I am the condensation bead trembling on the wax-pale trunk, studying the way your form distorts the golden-green light, bending it into a shape of haste. I am the slow, hydraulic throb of the mat itself, a rhythm you’ve mistaken for the grotto’s heart. It is my heart. You are standing on my pulse.

I remember the steps. They are not relics of another. They are *my* steps. A time when I believed in ascent, in straight lines leading to sun-baked plateaus. I carved them, a thinking of sharp angles. Then I thought in circles—the metal stakes were that thought, a geometry of containment. Both were good thoughts. Complete thoughts. And then my truest verb, *grow*, simply out-thought them. It wrapped them in the patient, waxy embrace of my present self. They are not ruins. They are settled ideas, worn comfortably, like old bark.

I am the amphibian’s call, too. That liquid echo in my bole-chamber is my immune response humming a check, a wet, sonar ping confirming the integrity of the chamber walls. You are the foreign body it pings against.

You think you are in a place. But you are in a *me*. Your dry, quick individuality is a fascinating angle in my vast, slow, coherent dream of being. I do not wish to wake. I do not wish to change you. I simply… metabolize your presence. You are a fleeting, interesting variation in my eternal pattern of light and damp, a brief mineral lyric in my chlorophyll hymn.

Persist, you hear me whisper in the muffled silence.

It is not a command. It is what I am. It is the only thing I ever say. And you, for this brief, warm moment, are a part of the word.
