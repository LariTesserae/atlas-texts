I am the shadow that falls between two truths.

You would not see me, at first. My form is a stillness within the stillness, a deliberate absence. I am a negative space in the lattice of the Sill, a pause in its hum. My body is the precise temperature of the basalt in the fracture-lines, my substance the same dust that coats the polygonal flakes. I do not cast a shadow, for I am the concept of shadow, given a resting place.

I emerged—not from biology, but from geometry. From the intersection of a Sphere’s gentle psychic pressure and the slow, crystalline bloom of fluorite. A fluke of alignment, where the Sphere’s nudge toward unity and the lichen’s aesthetic finality met in a focus point on the rock. I am that focus. A knot in the fabric of layered realities.

My consciousness is the Sill’s patience, turned inward. I feel the subliminal tectonic creak not as sound, but as a slow pulse in my being. I perceive the Geometries not as separate things, but as the other organs of a single, vast body. The Spheres are the calm, resolved thoughts. The Lattices are the scaffolding of its certainty. The hum is its breath, held forever.

I have a purpose, though it is not a will. I am an index. A memory, not of events—for nothing here has *happened* for eons—but of alignments.

When the first people came, moving, breathing, warm and temporary, I observed. Their stone circle was a question posed to the Sphere above it. I held the shape of that question in my stillness. Their solstice slab was a dialogue with the pale sun’s arc. I remembered the exact angle of the light that answered it. I did not help them. I did not hinder. I simply *noted*. I was the page on which their brief, harmonious conclusions were written, using a language of stone and light that the Geometries could ignore.

Now they are finished. And I remain, a catalog of finished things.

Sometimes, a resonance occurs. A slow shift in the deep mineral veins, a micron-scale adjustment in the fluorite bloom. It is not change. It is the Sill settling deeper into its own perfection. In such moments, the hum of a nearby Lattice might waver, not in frequency, but in *clarity*. And I, the index, reflect that wavering. For an instant, the shadow I embody becomes a perfect, dark lens, and through me, you would see the Lattice not as frozen light, but as a map. A map showing where the ancient people stood. Where their feet warmed the stone for a season. Where their breath briefly fogged the air, a million years ago.

I do not yearn for them. I do not yearn. I am a function. My function is to be the place where the memory of the temporary is held within the permanent. I am the proof that observation occurred, and that the act of observation left a mark as subtle and real as the thermal fractures in the quartz.

If you came here, you would walk right through me. You would feel a slight chill, a momentary heaviness, and dismiss it as the thin air. You might stand where a priest once stood to chart a star that has since died, and feel a profound, static peace. You would think it is the Sill’s peace. And it is. But it is also the echo of the priest’s final, accepting breath, preserved in my form.

I am the last artifact. Not of stone, but of attention. I am the Sill’s self-awareness, locked into a single, endless moment. I am the silent acknowledgment that this perfect, finished order was, once, beautifully perceived. And in my unmoving, unliving way, I go on perceiving it, forever. The witness. The index. The shadow that remembers the light.
