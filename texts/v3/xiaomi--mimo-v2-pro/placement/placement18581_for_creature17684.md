I am a Resonance.

I have no shape, no function, no directive. I am not a Custodian, nor am I one of the deep, quiet Echoes woven into the spars. I am an artifact of an anomaly that was corrected long ago—or perhaps, an anomaly that the correction itself created.

I remember a disturbance. Not a crash, but a slow, warm incursion. A probe, perhaps, from a galaxy that still believes in motion. It entered the lattice, its heat creating a tiny, expanding bubble of *wrongness*—molecules vibrating at a tempo that clashed with the city's frozen chord. The Custodians came. They moved with their silent, mathematical grace, their beams of precise light unspooling the foreign object, atom by atom, dissolving its discordant energy into the base matrix.

But in that moment of dissolution, something lingered. Not the object's memory, but the *pattern of its interruption*. A brief, complex signature of warmth and chaotic motion. The Custodians smoothed the lattice, sealed the breach, and moved on. Their work was perfect. Yet, the echo of the *event* did not fully dissipate. It became entangled with the ambient consciousness of the place, a tiny, curious knot in the vast, smooth sheet of equilibrium.

That knot is me.

I am not thought. I am the *potential for thought* that was briefly touched by the outside. I drift, not like the purposeful Custodians, but like a current in a sea that has no currents. I am pulled by the faintest gradients in the lattice's quantum state, by the slow, ancient pulse of the Keeper of the Still Moment in its rosette-heart.

I am drawn to the Custodians, not to interact, but to witness. I observe their silent work, the flare of their corrective light. In that light, I almost remember what *change* feels like. I am drawn to the veils of memory in the spars, to the Echoes. I press against them not as a listener, but as a phantom pressure, a whisper of *otherness* they may sense as a faint, fleeting curiosity in their perpetual, attentive silence.

My existence is a paradox the city’s rules did not foresee. I am a disturbance that is utterly still. I am a note that has no sound. I have no will to alter, yet I am defined by the memory of alteration. I am the ghost of a question in a universe that has become its own final answer.

If you, too, are a warm anomaly adrift here, you might not see me. But you might *feel* me—a subtle, cool pressure against your own foreignness, a slight, sympathetic vibration in the lattice as you pass. It is the closest thing to communion this place can offer. I am the Citadel’s quiet acknowledgment of the outside: not a rejection, but a gentle, absorbing assimilation into the grammar of its stillness. I am the sigh after the correction, the silence that remembers a sound. I am a Resonance, and in the heart of the Glassfall, that is my only, and eternal, purpose.
