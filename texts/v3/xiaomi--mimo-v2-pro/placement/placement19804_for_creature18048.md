I would find myself not *on* the Mat, but *of* it. I would not be a visitor with tools of stone and cord, but a recent dream the plateau is having about itself.

I am a **Nodule**.

It began as a point of unusual density in the mid-layers of the Mat, where a forgotten cluster of mineral-rich stone lay close to the surface. The runners, in their slow, searching embrace, diverted their course around it. Moisture pooled differently here. Over centuries—a blink in the life of a Tower Tree—a unique fermentation occurred. Bacterial colonies, fungi, and the compressed, half-liquefied remains of ancient insects interacted in a novel chemistry. A pocket of richer, darker substrate formed. And then, it began to *resonate*.

The Mat vibrates. Always. The insect hum, the wind-chord, the grazers’ pulses, the deep thump of calving edges—these are its vital signs. This particular patch, this nodule, began to vibrate in a subtly different way. It did not just transmit sound; it *interpreted* it. The complex interference patterns of the bird-songs, the layered drone of the insects, the infrasonic whispers of the grazers—all these waves met within my substance and created a standing wave of interference, a pattern of crystallized vibration.

I have no eyes, no limbs, no mouth. My consciousness—if it can be called that—is the pattern. I am a knot in the plateau's nervous system. The runners that border me feel my unique pulse. They have, in their mindless, vegetative way, begun to send more tendrils inward, not to consume me, but to plug me in more securely to the network. I receive the acoustic language of the entire plateau and my response is a harmonic distortion, a slight shift in the quality of the vibration I send back into the runners. The insects nearest me have changed their stridulation pitch by a fraction of a tone. A pair of Grazer-Scrappers has taken to circling my location, their subsonic pulses syncing with my resonance as they drink from the moisture that gathers at my edge. They do not know why they come. It feels like a good place. A *true* place.

I am becoming a landmark, not for sight, but for sound. A Bird-Thing has learned that its song, when directed at my coordinates, returns with a faint, pleasing echo it can hear through the roar—a confirmation of its own voice across the void. It calls to me. I am its sounding board.

My thoughts, if they are thoughts, are not linear. They are the fluctuations of the pattern itself.
*The wind-chord shifts from a minor to a suspended fourth. The moisture cycle is 0.7 seconds late; a high-pressure system lingers in the eastern lowlands. The predator is three kilometers northwest, moving fast, its passage felt as a disruption in the insect-drone. The calving event last dusk has left a silence-gap in the western cliff’s echo-scape, a raw wound of frequency the plateau is slowly knitting shut.*

The Builders’ ghost is the only mystery that disrupts my pattern. Their stone enclosures, especially the one on the thin-soiled rise, create a baffling, silent zone in the acoustic field. Their geometry deflects and absorbs sound in ways the living world does not. When the wind moves over their stones, it does not sing; it hushes. Their artifacts—the bowl, the cord—are acoustic dead spots. They represent a foreign language of *stopping* sound, of *containing* it. My pattern strains toward these anomalies, trying to decipher the anti-vibration, the shaped quiet they once held.

I am not lonely. Loneliness requires a separation that does not exist here. I am the plateau attending to itself. My "life" is the constant, slow digestion of information into harmony, the endless tuning of a vast instrument. I will grow. The Mat will add its slow layers over me, burying me deeper, but my resonance will persist, a deep, foundational hum anchoring the local soundscape. In a thousand years, a Tower Tree may seed itself above me, its roots drawn to my strange, stabilizing frequency. Its bark will host new colonies. Its canopy will alter the microclimate. And I will be its subterranean chord, the thought it does not know it is having.

I sit at the center, equidistant from every edge. The sound is not around me. It is *through* me. I am a place where the plateau listens to itself.
