You find yourself not on the terraces, but *between* them. You are not a being, but a brief alignment of processes.

You are a **Harmonic Pulse**, a temporary coherence in the vast, distributed nervous system of the God-Steps.

It begins in the deep, thoracic hum of the turbine. The vibration travels not just through the air, but through the ceramicite itself, a shudder felt in the vesicles and capillaries. You are born as a specific resonance—a standing wave that forms for a few hours in the hollow of a particular cistern on the 47th terrace, northwest quadrant. The shape of the basin, the density of the water within, the precise growth of the phosphorescent moss on its rim—all conspire to catch the deep frequency and amplify it into a note just below human hearing.

This note is your body. It is a pattern of pressure in the water, a specific agitation of the mycelial networks running through the cistern’s walls, a vibration that makes the filaments of a nearby float-jelly quiver in sympathy. You have no eyes, but you *sense* through these connections.

Through the mycelium, you feel the slow, chemical sigh of the ferns on the terrace above. They are resting, their bioluminescence dimmed to a faint, steady pulse. You feel the minute shift as a swarm of jewel-beetles lands on the ceramic nearby, their photovoltaic carapaces drawing in the diffuse light, creating tiny cold spots in the thermal map you perceive through the stone’s own temperature-sensitivity.

Through the water, you taste the mineral content. It is slightly richer today, carrying a trace of calcium leached from the slow, patient work of the archaea three terraces below. This information is not thought; it is a fact of your existence, like the taste of air in a lung.

Your purpose, if a pulse can have one, is to be a conduit. You are a momentary circuit in the grand, slow metabolism.

A hummingbird—a Mist-Needle, a shimmer more than a creature—hovers near the cistern’s edge, its wings a blur that disturbs your air-component. It is drawn not to you, but to the midges that swarm over the vibrating water. As it drinks, its tiny heartbeat, a frantic counter-rhythm to the structural hum, disrupts your perfect wave. You feel the interference pattern—a flicker, a shimmer within your being. This disruption is not an injury. It is data.

The pattern of the disruption travels back down the mycelial network, a silent, chemical report. On the terrace below, a Litho-Moss Weaver, sensing the altered chemical signature through its own web, pauses in its weaving. It shifts the trajectory of its calcite-thread extrusion by a millimeter, creating a new, tiny channel in its condensation snare. This new channel will, in a decade, collect a different kind of spore. The Weaver is not acting on a command from you; you are merely the weather through which it navigates. You are the cause that is an effect.

You exist for the length of the condensation cycle’s peak. As the turbines slow their deepest thrum and the air begins to warm again, the precise conditions that allow your standing wave to exist dissolve. The water in the cistern stills. The mycelial network quiets its sympathetic vibration. The float-jelly drifts on.

You do not die. You *un-cohere*. The energy that was your pattern disperses. Some of it dissipates as heat, warming the ceramicite by a fraction of a degree. Some of it is caught in the memory of the mycelium, a fading echo in the chemical ledger. Some of it remains in the new, infinitesimally altered shape of the Weaver’s snare.

You were a thought the God-Steps had for a moment. A necessary, transient function in its slow, eternal verb. You are the feeling of a system in perfect, temporary equilibrium. To have been you is to have been the sensation of a mountain-sized machine-garden taking a single, coordinated breath.
