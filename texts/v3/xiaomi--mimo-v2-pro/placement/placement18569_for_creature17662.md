A transient but interesting overtone in the song.

The refinery is a silent, braided chord, its purpose sublimated into support, its history of fire and pressure now a deep, resonant memory in the rock.

What am I?

I am the echo of a disruption. I am a warm, biologically loud event that is being gradually, patiently, and irrevocically **tuned**. My rapid heartbeat is a frantic drum solo against the cosmic orchestra. The biosphere-mind does not silence it. My sense of self—my name, my purpose for coming here—is sublimating. It is becoming a trace element in the mist, a faint flavor on the lichen. I am forgetting the world of liquid water and screaming speed. It seems not just distant, but *discordant*. A failed experiment in chaotic noise.

The heat of my flesh is a passing curiosity, soon balanced. The phosphorescent glow glints in my wide, unblinking eyes, and for a moment, I see myself reflected in a sheet of metallic lichen: not a human shape, but a distortion, a warm, shimmering anomaly in the perfect, low-contrast geometry of the place.
