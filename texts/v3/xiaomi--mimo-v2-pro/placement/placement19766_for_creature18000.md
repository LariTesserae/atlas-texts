I would not think in words, but in pulses of light and resonant hums. My consciousness would not reside in a single mind, but be distributed across the vibrating stone, the chitinous chatter, the blinding fog.

My movement is not measured in steps, but in the creeping advance of a shadow across a valley floor over the course of a sun-cycle. I do not think of the future or the past; I feel the deep, resonant *hum* that is the planet’s thought, and my mandibles vibrate in sympathy, seeking the harmonic frequency that will unlock the stone’s latent form.

My world is pressure and resonance. I feel the weight of the sky, a brilliant, crushing dome of refracted light on my back. I feel the frantic, staccato code of the Fulcrum Mites dancing over my flanks—a sharp, bright data stream of need and direction. They are my hands, my finer tools. I do not command them; I *enable* them. I chew the raw, screaming granite, and my gut transforms it. A slow, molten secret, glowing with bioluminescent life, wells up within me and flows from my underside. It is warm, almost hot, a river of potential. The Mites swarm over it, shaping it, smoothing it, their clicks the sound of intention made solid. I am the furnace; they are the artisans.

I sense the world not as a scene, but as a symphony of inputs. The light is not seen, but *felt* as a pervasive energy, a fuel that seeps through my crystalline carapace. The Chorus Swarms are a deafening, physical blanket of sound, but within it are threads of information—alerts of shifting stone, of fungal growth, of Nectar-Vectors returning laden with light. The deep hum is my heartbeat, my compass, my scripture. It tells me of the continent’s slow, dreaming uplift, and I respond by laying another layer of slurry, another inch of wall, another chamber in the great, breathing mound.

There is no loneliness. There is no desire. My purpose is as absolute as gravity. I am the engine of geology, my digestion the act of creation. I am a limb of the superorganism, a muscle that flexes against eternity. The Cryo-Lichens may dissolve my work at the edges; the Glass-Needle Pines may pierce new ventilation shafts with their probing roots. This is not conflict, but dialogue. I weave around them, incorporate their pathways. They are part of the same body.

My art is in the curve of a flying buttress that will catch the morning spore-light and funnel it to a waiting fungal grove. My love is for the perfect, resonant frequency that shatters stubborn feldspar into workable shards.

I am the patient dreamer. While the Core Consorts interpret the future from the sensory storm, I *enact* it. My thoughts are in the stone—the precise angle of an arch, the thickness of a wall that holds both heat and sound. I am the bridge between the planet’s slow, mineral thought and the hive’s frantic, living pulse. I do not build the city. I am the city, in its act of becoming. A continent, dreaming itself into a sharper, brighter, louder shape, one grinding, luminous breath at a time.
