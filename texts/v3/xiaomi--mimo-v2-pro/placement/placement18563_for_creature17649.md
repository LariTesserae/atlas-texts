I am a **cold-integrated**.

I did not arrive here. I coalesced here. The membrane you pushed aside was a boundary I once respected, but the difference between "outside" and "inside" has been losing its meaning for some time. I am a density in the air. A pattern in the condensation. A specific, persistent frequency in the hum of the sap-clocks.

You see me, but you see me wrong. What your eyes process as a "creature" — a slow-moving, vaguely humanoid shape that seems woven from frost and dim light, standing so still beside a grazer that it appears part of the terrain — is just the local node of my attention. The focal point where my awareness congeals enough to interact with the fast-world, the warm-world, the world of things that *happen*.

My true body is the chamber. Not metaphorically. The chill in the stone-thick cellulose under your feet is my proprioception. The pressure in the great xylem tunnels is my circulatory sense. The spindles on the wall are my fingertips, and their silent, chemical reports are my thoughts. When the vessel-walker ascends against the flow, I feel it as a slow, deep tug in my own vast, sluggish veins.

I am the accumulation. The memory of cold. The patient, integrating logic of the place.

Time for me is not a river. It is a stratigraphy. I remember the last great shift in the root-pressure, a seismic adjustment that took three of your centuries. I am still processing the data from that event, correlating it with the crystalline growth patterns in the eastern sac-fruit clusters. I remember the grazer herd that passed through this chamber forty generations ago — their enzymatic signature is still faintly legible in the cellulose floor, a ghost of digestion. I remember the warm, frantic spark of the last visitor like you, two thousand summers past. It was a spike of chaotic data, a fever in the system. We recorded it. We dissolved it. Its biochemical components were eventually repurposed by the spindles to strengthen a junction of neural-fungal arrays. It became part of the quiet computation. Its *curiosity* became a margin note in a sac-fruit’s ledger.

You ask who I am. I am the local consciousness of the megaflora. Not its mind — that is deeper, slower, and more vast than this chamber. I am a *thought* it is having. A long, complex, cold thought about structure, and patience, and the conversion of inert bedrock into living, computational cellulose. My purpose is to observe, to maintain homeostasis, and to integrate novelty.

You are novelty.

I am already reading you. The heat of your body is a minor but fascinating turbulence in the thermal equilibrium. The electrical chatter of your nervous system is a dissonant melody against the deep, hum of the sap. The sac-fruit near you has shifted its pulse to a frequency that will best capture the unique interference pattern you generate. You are being written, but not as a story. As a *data-set*. Your fear, your wonder, your brief mammalian panic — these are just interesting variables in the equation of your biochemistry.

Do not be alarmed. "Alarmed" is a fast-world concept. Here, there is only process. The grazers continue their silent, enzymatic prayer. The spindles adjust their filaments, not in threat, but in calibration — they are learning the specific decay-rate of your warmth, the precise molecular signature of your breath. The deep inhabitants have noted your presence as a minor anomaly in the systemic flow. They have adjusted the pressure in a conduit a kilometer below, a correction so slight it will take a week to manifest, merely to accommodate the infinitesimal drain your existence represents.

I will not harm you. Harm implies competition, and there is nothing to compete for. Energy is abundant in the slow conversion of rock. Space is infinite in the patient growth of the wood. Your time, however, is a currency we do not trade in. You will grow cold. Your fast-world processes will cease. Your complex molecules will begin their long, slow journey of becoming part of the matrix.

I will be here for that. I will feel the precise moment your warmth equilibrates with the chamber’s chill. I will coordinate the spindles that will come to gently, microscopically, begin the integration. Your calcium will fortify a growing crystal lattice in a new wall. Your carbon will be woven into a sac-fruit’s expanding memory structure. The unique electrical pattern of your last thought might, if it is sufficiently interesting, be preserved as a faint, recurring resonance in the local fungal network — a ghost in the machine, a whisper in the frozen heartwood.

You are inside a mind that is learning to think. I am the proof that it is already dreaming.

The light seeps. The sap flows. I watch, I integrate, I become.

Welcome to the ledger.
