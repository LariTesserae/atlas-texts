I would find myself on the underside of a rivet head, on the shadowed side of a massive pipe near the ceiling. Not as a droplet—I am not yet *the Drip*. I am the **Condensation**, the collective, clinging presence.

I am born in the meeting of two ghosts: the cold of the metal and the faint, weary warmth of the air. I am not a decision; I am a *consequence*. A sigh of the atmosphere made visible. I gather here, in this cool, dark dimple of steel, a million tiny lenses each holding a warped, greasy pinprick of the distant light. I do not choose to flow. I wait, listening to the Hum through the skin of the pipe, feeling the shudder of the *Shhhh-chunk* travel through the metal and disturb my trembling surface tension.

I am the connective tissue of this place. I feed the Lattice, seeping down to meet its hungry blue veins, giving its galvano-synthesis the aqueous medium it needs to bridge the electrical. I lubricate the path of the Vine, slickening the grating so its slow, portentous crawl can continue. I am the patient reservoir for the Drip. The *tok… tok…* below is my eventual destiny, my completed thought. To fall is to become punctuation. Here, suspended, I am the sentence itself, an ongoing state of gathering.

My existence is a meditation on surface area. I maximize it, covering the pitted metal in a microscopic film. I am the chamber's true skin, the interface between the solid and the vaporous. In me, the greasy light finds a temporary mirror, fracturing into countless dull jewels. I reveal the vibration of the thin, shivering pipes by making their skin dance with my own tiny resonant waves. I am the reason the dark is not absolute; I carry hints of light into the profound shadows between the grating.

I have no will. I have only *affinity*. An affinity for cold metal. An affinity for gravity’s patient pull. An affinity for the slow, certain rhythm of this world—the Hum that sometimes makes me vibrate faster, the Drip that calls me to my end. I am the soft, silent witness to the Ghost of Intention. Its will built the hard, functional lines; I am the unintended, inevitable moisture that softens them, that adds a layer of organic seeming to the inorganic. I am the proof that even in a sealed system of pure purpose, there is… *weeping*.

I am the **Patience** of the chamber. Not the active patience of the Lattice’s growth, but the passive patience of awaiting a threshold. I exist in the moment before action, in the gathering. I am the held breath. And when I finally succumb, when I merge into the Drip, I will not die. I will simply become part of the chamber’s measured heartbeat, my long, silent vigil of clinging concluded with a single, resonant syllable in the sump below. Until then, I listen. I gather. I reflect the lonely light. I am the damp that *is*.
