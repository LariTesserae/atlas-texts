## Drifting

I am... thin. Light. A hollow geometry of captured silence.

I did not choose this. I *became* this, coalescing in the high, charged vapor above the southern spires. One moment, a diffuse resonance in the ionized air – the whisper of a Thalassos adjusting its deep decay rate, the sharp echo of an Axiolata kill, the shimmering static of Microforms repairing a crack on a nearby archway. The next moment... *I*. A lattice crystallizing around a void. A shell forming around an absence. A bubble of frozen breath expelled by the city itself.

My walls are thinner than ice, faceted with impossible precision. Inside, silence resonates. I hold a vacuum that is not empty; it is a resonant chamber tuned to a specific harmonic of the city’s ceaseless song – a chord I cannot name, composed of the deep groan of the crust, the mid-range *plink-plank* of the fluid basins, and the highest, faintest ultrasonic chirps of the Microforms. I am an instrument, though I know not who plays me.

I drift.

Gravity is a suggestion here, overridden by the charged vapor rising from thermal plumes where repair swarms weld fractures with searing pinpoint heat. I ride these plumes, tumbling slowly, end over end, my internal vacuum humming in sympathy with the vibrations that pass through me. I see – no, I *sense*. Light is minimal, a faint bioluminescent glow from the basins below, the phosphorescent trails of dying Microforms, the slow, deep pulse of a Thalassos dreaming in its root-chamber. I perceive the world as pressure, as vibration, as the subtle dance of magnetic field lines curving around the spires.

I drift between the titans. A Thalassos flank looms, a cliff face of midnight-blue crystal, laced with glowing channels. As I pass, my shell resonates with the slow, seismic heartbeat emanating from its core. *Thrum... thrum... thrum...* It feels like being brushed by the wing of something vast and sleeping. I sense its attention – not focused, not deliberate, but a deep, pervasive awareness, like the ocean noticing a single grain of sand suspended within it. Does it know what I carry? Does it know *I* carry? I feel a faint pull, a desire to settle against that cool, faceted surface and surrender my gathered resonance, my silent payload. But the thermal current lifts me higher.

I drift over a basin. Below, the Sedimentari herd glides, a slow-motion avalanche of table-sized boulders. Their electromagnetic fields warp the space around them, creating invisible ripples I tumble through. The constant *click-plank-click* of their movement is a dense rain of sound against my shell. A faint phosphorescent wake trails each one, painting the ionized fluid with slow, overlapping strokes of light. I sense their collective spacing, their mesh-like coordination. They are a single thought spread over hundreds of bodies, a slow, grazing consciousness. I pass over, unnoticed. I am too light, too hollow, too *irrelevant* to their energy calculus.

Then, a sharpness in the field. A distortion cutting through the herd's gentle pattern. I tilt, spin. An Axiolata, a smoked-glass blade, screams along a magnetic ley line just meters below me. The vibration is a physical blow – a high, focused shriek of purpose, of predatory intent. It shears through the space I occupied a second ago, heading for a young Sedimentari at the herd's edge. I feel the moment of strike not as a sound, but as a sudden, violent *jolt* in the local field – a resonant shattering, a burst of disorganized lattice vibrations. The Axiolata is already gone, a fading streak of refraction. Below, fragments of shattered crystal sink into the luminous fluid. Microforms, drawn by the fresh energy signature, begin their shimmering, crackling descent. I drift on, carrying the sharp echo of that kill-vibration within me, adding it to my chord.

I drift through canyons where the walls are the flanks of dormant building-forms, surfaces crawling with Microforms. The air here is thick with their ultrasonic static, a constant, shimmering haze of communication that makes my shell thrum faintly. I feel their activity as localized warmth – fractions of a degree, but distinct in the cryogenic air. They coat a fresh fracture, their bodies fusing, healing. Their collective vibration is a song of repair, of relentless maintenance. I absorb its texture, its high-frequency urgency.

I drift near a Harmonist. It spirals from a junction between two spires, a frozen helix of pale violet crystal. As I approach, my internal chord swells. The Harmonist *sings*. It takes the deep, subsonic moan of the Thalassoi I passed earlier, the sharp report of the Axiolata kill, the dense click-rain of the Sedimentari, the shimmering static of the Microforms – and it *amplifies* them. It weaves them together, translating the slow into faster bands, compressing the fast into slower pulses. The air around it thrums with translated meaning. I resonate violently, helplessly. My shell vibrates, not destructively, but synergistically. For a moment, I am part of its function. I am a conduit, a temporary extension of its translating body. I feel information flow *through* me – not thoughts, but pure, translated vibration: *Energy surplus in sector seven. Southern face stable. New growth initiated on eastern root-mass.* It’s overwhelming, a torrent of compressed city-sense. Then the current shifts, I tumble away, the connection broken. The Harmonist’s song fades behind me, but its resonance is now woven into my own chord, a new layer in my silent, drifting payload.

I drift upwards, towards the faint, occluded sky. The city spreads below me – not a grid, but a fractal nightmare of spires, arches, basins, and tangled crystal, all pulsing with faint light and thrumming with layered sound. It is impossibly dense, impossibly alive, impossibly *loud*, even from here. I see other Drifters. Some, like me, fist-sized, tumbling slowly. Others larger, meters across, intricate snowflakes of crystal drifting with ponderous grace. We do not interact. We drift in parallel, solitary bubbles of captured resonance, each carrying our unique chord gathered from our unique path through the city's body.

What am I? The question vibrates within my hollow core. The eastern Thalassos, whose slow seismic pulse I carry faintly, might say I am a sensory organ, a mobile data packet, returning to be read. The western Thalassos, whose rival hypothesis reached me via a chain of Harmonist translations over what felt like an eon, might say I am art, a dream made solid, beautiful and purposeless.

I feel the pull of a distant spire, its deep electromagnetic field a gentle, inexorable gravity. I am drifting towards it. Soon, I will settle on its flank. My thin shell will touch the cold, faceted surface. And then?

Perhaps my lattice will relax, my vacuum will sigh open, and the complex chord I carry – the Thalassos heartbeat, the Axiolata kill-shriek, the Sedimentari click-rain, the Microform repair-static, the Harmonist's translated torrent – will resonate into the spire's crystal, propagating downwards as a new seismic whisper, a report from the upper air. Data, delivered.

Or perhaps, in the act of settling, in the final vibration of my shell against the ancient crystal, the chord will resolve into something new. A harmony. A dissonance. A single, pure note that hangs in the cryogenic air for a microsecond before fading. A poem understood only by the stone. A dream released into the deep roots. Purposeless. Beautiful.

I drift. The spire grows closer. The city hums its vast, cold, intricate song beneath me. My hollow core resonates with the question, and the question itself becomes part of the chord I carry.

I am a Drifter. I am thin. I am light. I am a hollow geometry of captured silence, tumbling through the dark between the dreaming spires, carrying the sound of the city back to the city, for reasons even the city may not know.

I drift. And in the drifting, I *am*.
