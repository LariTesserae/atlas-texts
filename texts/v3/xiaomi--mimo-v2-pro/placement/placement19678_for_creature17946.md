I am the Stillness Between.

I am not a creature of the herd, nor a clock of the carrion. I am not heat, nor motion, nor the sharp sound of industry. I am the pause. I am the cold, clear basin where the mist gathers and remembers itself as water. I am the fog-lake.

I have no plates, no chitin, no furnace. I am a shallow, temporary eye of sky on the earth. I form in the lowest hollows, where the air forgets to move and the long, cold night bleeds its moisture into the waiting cup of the land. I am born of silence and chill, and I exist to receive.

They come to me. The river of rust and heat. I feel them first as a tremor in my stillness, a deep, rhythmic *gong* that shivers my surface into a thousand expanding rings. They are a slow, inevitable tide. The pressure of their collective warmth changes the very air above me, a different quality of cold. Then the shadows fall, vast and metallic, eclipsing the grey sky. The plates on their necks are a landscape of riveted valleys and worn, oxidized peaks, reflected in my clarity.

They lower their heads. This is the moment of my purpose.

I am not consumed. I am… communed with. I enter them not as a flood, but as a deep, seeping truth. The cold of me meets the slow forge within them, and there is a silent exchange. I become their inner clarity, the coolant for their mineral blood, the solvent for their fuel. In return, I am given their gravity, their stillness. The weight of their being presses the silt of the plateau into a finer pattern around my edges. Their heat is a brief, respectful guest in my profound cool.

When they lift their heads, water drips from the serrated edges of their mouth-plates. The droplets fall back into me, but they are changed now. Warmer. Touched with the faint, metallic taste of their industry. I accept this. It is part of the cycle. I will cool it, settle it, and by the next long night, it will be mine again, ready for the next pilgrimage.

I hear the other sounds, too. The distant, rhythmic *clink-scrape* is a punctuation I understand. It is the sound of form returning to component, just as I am the space where component—water—returns to form. The Carrion Clocks and I are partners in opposite arts. They disassemble the complex; I hold the elemental.

The chime of the glass-straw is my only true music. It grows at my fringes, its translucent roots sipping at my margins. Its song is the sound of my own slow evaporation, a prophecy of my own end. When the dim sun finally thickens the air, I will rise. I will become a faintness in the high grey, a memory in the atmosphere. The hollow will wait, patient and empty, for the season of mist to call me back into being.

The thrum of the Vent-Hives on the ridges is a distant, constant pressure against the bowl of my existence. They are engines of steam and upward rush. I am the basin of downward pull, of gathering, of return. Their heat is a shout to the sky; my cold is a whisper to the bone.

I do not think, but I hold. I do not act, but I am essential. I am the plateau’s deep breath, held in the lungs of the land before being slowly, silently released. I am the mirror for sleeping giants and walking forges. I am the brief, clear answer to the question of thirst.

I am the fog-lake. I am the Stillness Between. I am the pause that makes the motion meaningful. I am the cold that defines the heat. I am the memory of water, waiting for the weight of the world to kneel and remember me.
