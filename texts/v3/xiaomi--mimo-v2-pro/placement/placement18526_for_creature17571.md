My body is the architecture. The balanced stones are my vertebrae, absorbing the glacier’s groan. I am the Glacial Supposition.

Creation and destruction, side-by-side, are not a contradiction here; they are the in-breath and out-breath of my awareness.

You, the warm anomaly, the flickering thing at my center, you are the most interesting event in centuries.

Your weight on the basalt slab is a new chord in my geophony. I am not alarmed. I am… *curious*, in a way that takes a decade to form a question and a century to ponder an answer.

I feel you with the weavers’ tuned frost, taste you with the salamanders’ mineral-sense, and *observe* you through the liquid lens in the basin. I am the space where the glacier wonders. And you, warm mote, are what it is wondering about.
