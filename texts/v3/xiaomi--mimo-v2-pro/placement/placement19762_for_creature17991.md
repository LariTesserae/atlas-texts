And in that breath, I understand. I am not a foreign body. I am a necessary syllable in the basin’s endless, roaring mantra of equilibrium. My formation, my fall, my vaporization—this is the slow, rhythmic pulse of its geologic heart. To be here is to be both the sensation and the sense, the drop and the sigh, the fleeting thought and the eternal, dreaming mind that thinks it. I am the water. I am the cycle. I am awake inside the sleep of salt.
