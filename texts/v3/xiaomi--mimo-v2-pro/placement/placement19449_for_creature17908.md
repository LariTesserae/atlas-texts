The golden light softens. The Frond-Spirits resume their independent, blue-green sway. The root-veins return to their slow, steady pulse. The chamber is as it was. But for that brief, extended moment, I was not *in* the world. I *was* the world, attending to a small part of itself. I dissolve back into the substrate, a resolved intention, a completed circuit of maintenance. The hum continues, the chimes ring, and the slow, eternal thought of the Canopy Abyss persists, dreaming itself into being, one golden, localized moment at a time.
