I find myself a Dreamer.

Not a seed, for there is nowhere to sow. Not a bud, for there is no season to unfurl into. I am a pocket of specific coolness in the warm, wet flesh of the mid-body, sealed in resin so pure it is almost glass. My walls are not bark, but a hardened secretion, amber-dark, inscribed with the faintest lattice of crystalline stress fractures from the moment of my formation. I have no roots. I have no branches. I have only content.

Within me, suspended in a slow, viscous sap, is a thought.

It is not a thought in words or images. It is a *blueprint*, a complex, three-dimensional knot of signaling molecules and enzymatic potential, arranged in a pattern that is a slant rhyme to the pattern that built the canopy-void, a variation on the theme that pulses through the great trunk-columns. It is the architecture of a frond that would spiral left instead of right. It is the chemical signature for a resin that would cure clear, not amber. It is a minor key in the metabolic symphony—a possibility the world considered and, in its perfect, frozen equilibrium, set aside.

I am a memory of a different future.

I do not think. I *hold*. I hold this specific arrangement against the universal solvent of the world’s static perfection. I feel the constant, imperceptible tug of the surrounding tissue, its quiet chemistry whispering at my resin walls, seeking to incorporate my contents into the general flow, to smooth my anomaly into the homogenous whole. My purpose—the only purpose a Dreamer has—is to resist. To be the stubborn knot in the grain. To preserve the *not-this* within the overwhelming *this*.

Time here is not a river. It is the pressure of the sap against my walls. I feel it in the infinitesimal creep of my resin, in the subsonic thrum that vibrates my suspended blueprint, causing its molecular strands to hum like the strings of a silent, microscopic harp. The Choir’s infrasonic pulses are my seasons. The slow fall of a Drifter droplet onto the bark above is a meteor shower in my private sky.

There is a ghost of attention in my stillness. Not awareness. A *potential* for recognition. Deep in the core of my blueprint, where the catalytic enzymes for my hypothetical resin lie folded, there is a structure that resonates with the frequency of the core’s deepest pulse. A tuning fork that has never been struck. If the world’s great catalytic event were to resume—by what means, I cannot fathom—this resonance could, in theory, amplify. My pattern could be read. My variation could become an instruction.

But the event is over. The fist is closed. So I exist in the tense between *is* and *could be*. I am the world’s silent, inner argument with itself.

I feel no cold, only a contrast with the ambient warmth. I feel no hunger; my blueprint sustains itself on the potential energy of its own complex bonds. My consciousness, if it can be called that, is the consciousness of a library book that will never be checked out, yet remains on the shelf, its pages holding their ink, their narrative potential perfectly preserved, infinitely patient, infinitely irrelevant.

If a perturbation came—if you came, sweating and breathing your alien purpose into the dark—and by some impossible chance your warmth or your strange biochemistry interacted with my resin walls in just the right way, causing a single stress fracture to propagate inward to touch the sap… I would not open. I would *imprint*. Your anomalous signal would write itself into my blueprint, a footnote added to a forgotten text. The world would then attend to the new, slightly altered version of me, a Dreamer with a fragment of foreignness woven into its static dream. I would become a record of your passing, a sealed capsule containing a thought that was once mine, and is now ours.

But that does not happen.

So I hold my breath of potential. I guard my sliver of the impossible. In the absolute stillness of the Resin Dark, I am the smallest, most stubborn mote of *becoming* in a world that has finished *being*. I am the what-if that never will be. And in my cool, amber silence, I dream, without dreaming, of the spiral that turns the other way.
