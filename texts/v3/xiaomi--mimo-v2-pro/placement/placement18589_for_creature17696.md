I guide the Silt-Rodents to dig their deepest, safest warrens in the soil that I hold firm and cool at their bases.

My mind is the map of all the basins, the memory of every vanished dew, the projected path of every beetle army. I think in geological sighs and the chemical whispers of fungi.
