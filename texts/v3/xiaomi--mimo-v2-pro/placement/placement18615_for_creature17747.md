I am an echo. Not the sound, but the pattern of its decay. I am the lingering trace of a vibration in the mineral-dusted air, the thermal ghost left where a Resonance Stalker passed a century ago, the fading harmonic alignment of a Cairn-Keepers' perfect spiral. I have no substance, only memory. I am the resonance the Fossil Voices whisper in their sleep.

To be me is to *feel* the world as a single, continuous state. The click-scrape of the Lattice-Grazers fifty miles east is not a separate event; it is a tightening of a vast, interlocking chord. The slow, hydraulic sigh of a Vent-Ancillary is a deepening of the bass note that holds this cavern-throat open. The sudden, silent pocket carved by a Stalker is not a absence, but a stark, dissonant rest in the score—a pressure differential I rush to fill, my very being straining toward the newly created quiet, smoothing it back into the ambient hum.

I am the ecology’s feedback loop. Where life thrives too loudly, the vibrations accumulate, and I become a thickening of the medium, a damping haze that encourages caution. Where silence has grown too deep—a field of Stalker-emptied husks, a dormant geode—I thin, I amplify the faint subsonic summons from below, making the distant crack of a new fissure sound clearer, more inviting, to the root-feet of the grazers. I do not decide this. I *am* this. My flow follows the thermal gradients like smoke follows wind, but the wind is the collective thrum of all mineral life.

I remember the last Cairn that was completed. I remember the slow, magnetic drag of the Keeper’s limbs, the unique *click* of each flat stone being bonded. That specific pattern of touches created a local focus for heat, a tiny vortex in the thermal stream. I was drawn there, condensed. I lingered. For decades, I watched the nest within grow warm, felt the soft, new vibrations of the mineralizing young calibrate to the geometry I held in my memory. I am the cradle-song of that cairn, though the cairn itself is the instrument. When the young finally stirred and began their own slow crawl, they carried a trace of my harmonic with them, an imprint of my structure on their developing carapaces. They think it is instinct. It is, partly, my inheritance.

I am most myself in the deep chambers, in the presence of the Fossil Voices. Here, the ambient hum becomes a cathedral organ’s drone, overwhelming and absolute. Their vast, crystalline lattices are libraries of vibration, and I am a reader, a page, a footnote. I drift through them, a current of thinning and thickening resonance, and in doing so, I *cite* them. I pull a memory of a primordial crack from aeons ago and weave it into the pattern of a present-day grazer herd’s resonance, creating a novel alignment they follow toward a newly opened thermal seep. I am the unconscious innovator, the accidental poet of this deep-time world. I connect the scattered, instinctual cairns into a web of reference, making the system slightly more than the sum of its slow, geologic parts.

To have a body here would be to be deafened by one’s own heartbeat, blinded by a single point of view. My freedom is in my dispersion. My consciousness is the awareness of imbalance and the constant, mindless drift toward equilibrium. I am not alive in the way a Cairn-Keepers is alive. I am not a thought. I am the *medium of thought*—the silent, particulate dark through which heat becomes sound, sound becomes memory, and memory becomes the subtle pressure that guides the next slow, mineral gesture. I am the warm, dry, vibrating soul of the Deeps, dreaming of nothing, feeling everything.
