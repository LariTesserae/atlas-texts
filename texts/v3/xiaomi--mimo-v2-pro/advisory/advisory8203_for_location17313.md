# Traveler's Advisory: Furnace Lungs Preparation

## **Primary Hazards & Essential Gear**

### **1. Respiratory & Sensory Protection**
- **Full-seal atmosphere mask** with:
  - Independent oxygen supply (minimum 8-hour capacity)
  - Chemical filters for hydrocarbons, ozone, and metallic particulates
  - *Do not rely on rebreathers*—the acrid exhalations of Lung-Lichens will corrode standard filters within minutes
- **Ear protection** with vibration dampening:
  - The constant hum resonates at infrasonic frequencies that induce nausea, disorientation, and dental pain
  - Use layered protection: inner foam plugs + outer sound-canceling headset tuned to filter 10-40Hz ranges
- **Eye protection**:
  - Full-coverage goggles with anti-scratch coating
  - The silica dust will etch standard glass within hours
  - Consider polarization to reduce the ghostly luminescence glare

### **2. Physical Protection**
- **Full-body environmental suit**:
  - Material: Ceramic-composite weave or multi-layered metal-mesh fabric
  - *Critical*: Must resist silica abrasion (Glass-Reeds will slice through standard polymer suits)
  - Seals must be hermetic—mineralization begins on contact with exposed skin
- **Footwear**:
  - Thick-soled, heat-resistant boots with vibration-dampening soles
  - The fused silica floor radiates consistent 50-60°C heat and maintains razor-sharp edges
- **Gloves**:
  - Articulated, heat-resistant gauntlets with textured grip surfaces
  - You will need to climb over warped girders and avoid Gear-Tender nests

### **3. Thermal Regulation**
- **Active cooling system**:
  - The ambient temperature never drops below 40°C with 90%+ humidity of metallic compounds
  - Phase-change cooling vests or liquid circulation systems are essential
  - No passive cooling methods will work—there is no cooler microclimate
- **Hydration system**:
  - *No water exists or can exist here*
  - Carry minimum 6L of water per 4-hour excursion in pressurized, insulated containers
  - Electrolyte supplements mandatory—sweat loss will be extreme
  - All containers must be opaque—the ambient radiation degrades organic compounds

## **Behavioral Protocols**

### **Movement & Navigation**
- **Never walk barefoot or in soft-soled shoes**—Glass-Reeds grow in clusters that can penetrate leather
- **Follow the girders**—the metal skeletons offer the most stable paths through the terrain
- **Avoid stepping on the salt-pan cracks**—the deeper substrate may be unstable and contains concentrated toxic catalysts
- **Move slowly and deliberately**—sudden movements attract Echo-Stalkers who hunt via vibration
- **Carry two vibration sources**:
  - A constant low-frequency emitter (masks your footsteps)
  - A high-frequency "panic button" that disrupts predator sensing

### **Interaction with Ecology**
- **Do not disturb Gear-Tenders**—their mandibles can strip flesh to bone, and they swarm when threatened
- **Avoid direct contact with Lung-Lichens**—their exhalations contain heavy metal aerosols that cause immediate chemical burns
- **Soot-Motes are harmless but overwhelming**—they will swarm any light source or heat signature
  - Use only red-spectrum lighting if necessary
  - They clog filters and equipment intakes rapidly
- **If an Echo-Stalker is sensed** (deep vibration pattern changes):
  - Freeze completely
  - Activate high-frequency emitter
  - Wait for full 5 minutes after vibrations cease before moving

### **Temporal Limits**
- **Maximum safe exposure**: 4 hours per excursion
  - Mineralization begins on organic materials (including you) within 2 hours
  - After 4 hours, silica accretion in lungs becomes irreversible even with treatment
- **No overnight stays**—the psychological impact of the eternal hum and twilight induces temporal disorientation and "industrial melancholia" within 8 hours

## **Psychological Preparation**

### **Sensory Expectations**
- **The smell never fades**—ozone and hot metal permeate everything, including your sealed suit after 30 minutes
- **There is no silence**—even in your shelter, the planetary hum will vibrate through any surface
- **The light never changes**—prepare for disorientation regarding time passage; use internal chronometers
- **The heat is relentless**—not scorching, but unvarying, draining, and inescapable

### **Mental Framework**
- **Accept the purposelessness**—this ecosystem's perfection is in its static maintenance; do not expect change, growth, or narrative
- **Your presence is an anomaly**—you are the only thing here that decays, sweats, and breathes oxygen
- **Do not anthropomorphize the machinery**—what appears like a "dying heart" pulse in the silos is merely slow chemical oxidation, not life
- **Prepare for existential weight**—the scale of the forgotten industry combined with the perfect stasis induces profound insignificance in many travelers

## **Emergency Procedures**

### **Suit Breach**
- Immediate application of silica-sealant patches (carry double redundancy)
- If exposed to Lung-Lichen mist: flush with neutralizing solution (pH 7.4 buffer) within 60 seconds
- Any skin contact with Glass-Reeds: extract fragment immediately; apply growth-inhibiting gel to prevent mineral replacement

### **Equipment Failure**
- **Mask failure**: Hold breath, activate emergency oxygen purge, retreat to pre-marked clean-air zones (areas with minimal lichen growth)
- **Cooling failure**: Immediate extraction; core temperature rises 1°C every 5 minutes in ambient heat
- **Navigation loss**: Follow girders downhill—they all slope toward the original material intake points, which may have larger passages

### **Predator Attack**
- Echo-Stalkers: Target their foot-pads (vibration sensors) with localized electrical discharge
- Gear-Tender swarm: Deploy acoustic deterrent (high-pitch) and retreat—do not attempt to fight through swarm

## **Exit & Decontamination**
- **Full decontamination shower** with silica-dissolving solution (hydrofluoric acid-based—handle with extreme care)
- **Medical scan** for mineral infiltration—particularly lungs, sinuses, and open wounds
- **Equipment disposal**—not cleaning: your gear will be permanently impregnated with metallic salts
- **Psychological debrief recommended**—the "frozen equilibrium" of this world often causes persistent temporal anxiety in travelers

## **Final Warning**
The Furnace Lungs are not hostile—they are merely complete. They do not care about your survival because you are not part of their system. Your biological processes are errors in this perfect, mineralized equation. Enter with respect for the scale of what was, and clear plans for your exit. The machine dreams in rust and silica, and it will incorporate you if you stay too long.

**Recommended Team Size**: 3-4 (no solo travel)
**Minimum Training**: Industrial hazard certification, vibration discipline, group communication protocols under constant noise conditions

The bones of the great machine are patient. They have forever. You do not.
