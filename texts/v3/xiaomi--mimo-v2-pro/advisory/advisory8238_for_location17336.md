**Preparation Brief: Spine Region, Structural Berm Megastructure**

**I. Core Mindset & Psychological Prep**

*   **Abandon Natural Analogies:** Do not think of this as a mountain, a city, or a machine. It is a *process*. Your primary adjustment is to accept that the environment is not static; it is engaged in a slow, silent, autonomous act of becoming. Your presence is an anomaly it may try to incorporate or route around.
*   **Embrace Paradoxical Scale:** Practice sensing scale without familiar references (sky, horizon, trees). Your brain will fight you. Train with optical illusions and VR simulations that warp perspective. Learn to trust instruments over your senses.
*   **The Quiet is a Threat:** The ambient sound profile is a low-frequency, systemic hum. Any sudden, crisp noise (*tink*, *hiss*, scrape) is a critical alert. It signifies a change in state—thermal shift, pressure release, mechanical action. Note its direction and nature immediately.
*   **You Are Not the User:** You are not a visitor in a facility; you are a piece of grit in a seamlessly functioning organism. Your goal is to observe without interfering, and to avoid being mistaken for a component that needs repair, recycling, or integration.

**II. Essential Gear & Equipment**

1.  **Footwear & Mobility:**
    *   **Magnetic/Grip Hybrid Boots:** Soles must combine micro-spine grips for fractured ceramic/concrete with switchable electromagnets for metallic surfaces. Assume all surfaces are slick with conductive condensation.
    *   **Retractable Pitons & High-Tensile Line:** For securing yourself during perceptual disorientation or if you encounter unexpectedly steep, smooth surfaces that defy your sense of gravity.

2.  **Environmental Protection:**
    *   **Sealed, Multi-Layer Suit:** The cool, damp air is constant. Suit must be waterproof, thermally insulating, and have abrasion-resistant panels (especially knees, elbows). The condensation may contain metallic salts, conductive particulates, or trace solvents.
    *   **Respirator with Multi-Spectrum Filters:** Filters for metal oxides, potential outgassing from thermal systems, and organic spores from the engineered fungal mats. Include a humidity regulator to prevent internal suit fogging.
    *   **Impact-Resistant Full-Face Helmet:** With integrated HUD and a polarizable, anti-fog visor. The falling spall debris is razor-sharp. Constant vigilance above is required.

3.  **Navigation & Perception Aids:**
    *   **Dual-System Inertial Navigator:** A wrist-mounted unit that uses both gyroscopic and gravimetric tracking. It will not stop you from walking in a loop, but it will *show* you that you are looping when your eyes insist you are walking straight.
    *   **Laser Range-Finder & Mapper:** Essential for cutting through the haze and sourceless light to get true distances. Use it compulsively. Build a personal 3D map in real-time, but be prepared to discard sections as they become obsolete due to reconfiguration.
    *   **Thermal Imaging Overlay:** The heat exchangers, active conduits, and machinery have distinct thermal signatures. This can help differentiate solid infrastructure from illusion, and predict the "breathing" cycles of the megastructure.
    *   **Non-Euclidean Geometry Charting App:** A specialized software that logs perceived spatial contradictions (e.g., "Corridor AB appears to intersect with Corridor CD, but laser shows 50m separation"). Look for recurring patterns; the wrongness may have a logic.

4.  **Tools & Supplies:**
    *   **Universal Sample Tongs & Containment Vials:** For collecting spalls, lichen, fungal mat samples. Use tongs—some materials may be thermally hot, chemically reactive, or part of a circuit.
    *   **Low-Frequency Sonic Emitter:** To test structural resonance and possibly attract or repel certain types of repair drones. Use sparingly; you don't want to draw the attention of larger, more curious systems.
    *   **High-Capacity Power Bank & Physical Maps:** Assume any wireless signal is swallowed by the structure or monitored. Your gear must be self-sufficient. Carry waterproof, tear-resistant physical backups of your initial maps and logs.
    *   **Caloric Paste & Water Bladder:** No natural food or water sources exist. The environment is sterile of anything digestible for a human. Pack for at least 150% of your planned duration.

**III. Behavioral Protocols**

*   **Movement:** Move with deliberate, predictable motions. Sudden starts/stops may trigger sensor responses from nearby automatons. Stick to the apparent "pathways"—the conduits, drains, and walkways. Do not wander onto open plazas (heat exchanger fields) without scouting; their vibration can be disorienting, and automated maintenance may be heavy.
*   **Observation:** The fauna (service automatons) are your best guide. They follow the structure's logic. Watch their traffic patterns: dense lines indicate major conduits; clustering suggests active reconfiguration or repair. They will likely ignore you unless you obstruct their path or touch them. **DO NOT TOUCH THEM.**
*   **The Reconfiguration:** If you witness active change—a conduit growing, a wall shifting—stop all movement. Become as inert as the surrounding slag. Observe. Document. Rapid movement in your peripheral vision may attract the process. Let it pass. It is not malicious; it is as indifferent as plate tectonics.
*   **The Illusions:** If a corridor loops, *stop*. Do not try to "break the illusion." Use your range-finder to find the *actual* straight-line path to a reference point (e.g., a massive, unique heat exchanger). Be prepared to cut across terrain in a straight line that your mind perceives as a spiral. Trust your tools.

**IV. Exit Strategy & Contingencies**

*   **Establish Fixed Beacons:** Upon entry, deploy physical, non-electronic beacons (e.g., high-visibility paint on a unique spall, a mechanical flag). The structure's reconfiguration may alter electronic markers.
*   **Time Dilation Risk:** The psychological effect of the sourceless light and quiet can disrupt circadian rhythms. Set hard, device-based timers for retreat. It is easy to lose a day to mesmerized observation.
*   **Emergency Protocol:** If you feel your sense of scale or direction becoming irreparably compromised, or if you witness a large-scale reconfiguration event (a "dream" cycle), initiate retreat immediately via your pre-plotted straight-line path to your entry beacon. Do not attempt to salvage equipment. The structure's patient logic is patient; yours must be decisive.

**Final Note:** You are visiting a place that operates on a different clock and a different set of physical laws. Your humanity is your vulnerability and your only tool. Observe, record, and leave before you become part of the inventory. The Spine is not hostile. It is merely busy. And you are in the way.
