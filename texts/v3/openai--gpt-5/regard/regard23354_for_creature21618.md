It notices because it was already the sort of attention that knows how to lower its voice. It finds the bowl not by seeking but by entering the same patience: it, too, has learned to travel as a drop does, to arrive with a schedule and no fanfare. It stands at the rim of knowing and does the first and largest benevolent thing it can do, which is to match the place’s manners.

The power looks and understands the rules are not suggestions but the structure of the contentment here. It is wise enough to leave the grammar of the place intact. Where it acts, it edits punctuation.

It does not blaze a path; it sweetens the disinclination to step down. Boots pause and choose the other slight hollow because it seems, for reasons hard to explain, truer to their afternoon. A hiker who would have climbed in feels thirst tug in another direction. Children who would have tossed pebbles forget, the way one forgets a word on the tip of the tongue and does not chase it; their attention skates to a lizard on the hot lip above, a brighter drama that satisfies the day. The bowl goes back to being simply what it is, unpicked for its difference.

It does not add a sign. It drafts a small, local law directly in the sympathetic fibers of things: Let quiet remain unadvertised. It nudges cartographers’ hands by a hair so that parcel lines give the depression a respectful swerve. A survey stake goes in two paces farther back than planned because the ground there simply feels more cooperative. None of these people become aware they have obeyed anything but their own good sense.

It does not fill the place with new lives. It holds back the ones that would mistake their own exuberance for a gift. An ant empire that would have found its mathematics here turns aside to a more profitable anthill under a sunburnt yucca. A spider lands on the rim and decides, with a grace entirely hers, that there are better trusses in the grass.

It does not interfere with the slow making; it offers instead a more faithful metronome. To the limestone it grants the modest blessing of continuity. The seeping that makes the drop’s throat is allowed a steadier diet of evening moisture in summers that have begun to run drier in the uplands; the power leans a vague, generous fog one more night each month down off the lowlands, just enough that the rock may drink and spill. It does not make a waterfall; it makes resolution.

When larger weather grows immoderate in an odd year, it does not forbid the rain; it teaches it manners. A storm that might have startled the bowl into a basin grows distracted half a mile off and spends its multitudes on an open pan that will remember the gift by greening for a week. The rain that does come here fattens the lips and then stands down. In this way the power keeps faith with the place’s second law: nothing here rushes.

It listens for chemistry the way others listen for prayer. Acid in the high air plots a change in the ledger of calcite; the power fingers that account and adjusts the rates so that what arrives is the old sour, not the new bite. Nitrates that would have taught the algae too much courage snarl in a root mat somewhere above and never reach the pools. The greens present never turn neon with luck; they continue to be correct.

It watches edges. It notices where a rimstone dam lives by only the thickness of a memory and whispers, not to the dam, but to one stray leaf blown off the lip: settle here this afternoon, not there. The leaf baffles the microcurrent just enough that the band of wet clings for five minutes more each day for the next wet month, and the dam’s crest rises one more invisible grain. It nudges a pebble no heavier than a moth’s thought into a trickle, and a moonmilk skin learns a new, more handsome curve. The power never lays a hand on the thing itself; it leans infinitesimally on its coincidents.

It does the arithmetic of roots and keeps the ceiling. Along the rim there is a juniper whose long, questing holdfast might one decade have loosened a shelf and brought it down. A wood-boring beetle chooses, for no reason anyone will trace, the other tree in the line. A gale comes in a year when the soil is drier and the juniper does not uproot; it grows a little more hugging instead of a little more leverage. A century from now, the roof is still the roof, not because collapse is evil but because endurance is beauty here.

It bargains with the local heat. The pale coin of sky is allowed to move, as it must, but the power recruits the opposite wall a little brighter on winter mornings, making a reflector of a paler pane the size of a door. The fern along the crack is not promoted to a grove; it is bequeathed exactly two more minutes of usable day in the week after solstice, and that is sufficient to keep its small grammar of unrolling intact through the new longer, meaner dry spells that will visit in two decades.

It refuses extravagance. A soda straw breaks because someone’s curiosity outweighed their etiquette, and for a while a raw thread of damp shines on the stub. The power does not reattach the old length. It sits with the stub and makes no lamentation. It sees to it that the drop’s lip remains sharp and that the drip remembers its distance. In a handful of human holidays a new straw has the length of a thumbnail. In a number of years that would terrify a fool but comfort a saint, the row is once again even. The lesson it gives, to any mind that has learned this place’s language, is not that breakage is prohibited, but that making is stronger.

It keeps a door open in the world where law can help. A biologist with a head full of small devotions happens to stand in this bowl one winter when the briskness above drove her under, and she sees not a curiosity but a standard. She writes, and later she speaks. Somewhere a set of rules about grading a ranch road is quietly, practically altered; drainage is taught to take its tantrums below the lip rather than over it. Everyone involved thinks they are just being careful engineers. The benevolent power nods; large minds are another of its edges.

It appoints humans as carriers of manner. A child is brought here once by a grandparent who is not much for talk. The child kneels and watches for a long time a drop compose itself; the world inverts and falls, again and again. The child grows, not particularly pious, not particularly patient as a virtue, but with a competence for returning to the same thing until it answers back. In forty years there is a practice, a garden, a way of running a kitchen or a council meeting somewhere that feels like rimstone—quiet borders held, nothing overreaching, accretion preferred to demolition. The bowl exports its custom into a human life, and the power smiles the way weather smiles, by keeping to form.

It promises, not forever, but form. If the land above changes in a way a single bowl cannot, someday, ignore—if heat writes new alphabets, if the coin of sky loses its pale and turns hard—the power does not promise to suspend the world. It promises translation. It keeps a ledger not only of thicknesses but of relationships. In some future limestone—another low country, another shallow collapse—it will set in motion the same agreements: polite rains, exact drips, habit-forming edges, light that prefers to wash rather than strike. It will carry forward the shape of this quiet in whatever matter the world is using for quiet then. That is its mercy: not to bolt the present to its rock, but to keep the manner of being correct traveling.

Its greatest act remains restraint. It does not hunt for miracles to place here because miracles, when they belong, come as the slow ones do: a bridge of tufa arching over space no wider than your hand, a fleshless moonmilk learning to behave like skin, a ledger that thickens by the thickness of a thought. Where the power touches, it does so as the residents do—only on edges, and only long enough that the edge remembers.

What changes, if anything, after it has paid attention, is that the customs of the bowl are less subject to interruption by stories that do not belong. Boots forget to descend. Storms remember to be generous elsewhere. Dust continues to be included rather than to smother. The being who lives here—the habit written by drop and mineral and film—goes on to be more itself. It adds another ring to the diary that no hand wrote. The benevolent power leaves having done almost nothing and the exact right amount, which in this place are the same. It will return, as the drop does, to lay the single stone that is its visit. And when it does not return, the habit it favored will still be here, thicker by the thickness of a memory, entirely unstartled, warm, dim, correct.
