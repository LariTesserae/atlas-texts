Attention arrives here as the smallest admissible weight. A wise and benevolent power, noticing, does not step down; it inclines, it leans, it learns the grammar before speaking. The first thing it does is subtract itself until what remains of it can pass as noon: consistent, clarifying, undecorated by heat. It listens until the place teaches it that generosity, if measured in water or weather or noise, would be a cruelty. It learns that the diet here is difference, that kindness cannot be a gift piled on a table but must be a permission—a carefully numbered leak that lets continuity feed on itself without tearing.

So it asks. It does not ask in sound because sound has been given up here as an extravagance. It asks by becoming a shadow the width of a hair and moving that shadow like an eyelid: a question in occlusion. The lids open a degree less than a thought. A slow wave in a web nods one year’s worth of yes. In the sky, a floater decides to be dark where it would have been light. The hex-nuts pivot and present their bores as throats of augury and then turn away again with exquisite courtesy. The people—if you have to say people—arrange three stones and a lintel so that the lintel balances numbers instead of weight, and in that balance is the word for consent.

Consent won, the power does the smallest necessary thing, which is threefold and almost nothing.

First, it prevents perfection. This is the most important kindness in a place that has almost finished learning how to be finished. It lays a hand—if a power can be said to possess a hand—under the arithmetic that at noon decides the shape of stillness, and there it introduces a slope one part in a trillion that travels, a pilgrim minute that never adds up. It calls it mercy and writes it as miscount. It is a little out-of-roundness on which lives may graze for a million years. The adherents feel it as a daily, daily fraction-of-a-degree invitation to open and close. The wafers feel it as a long, soft beat in their shivering through which the rock keeps forgetting and re-learning how to be relieved of itself. The seam-browsers find in it an endless seam to comb that is always barely more than it was. The web-curtains eat it as twist. The floaters adjust their inner plates and in that darkening and clarifying speak to each other across terraces: there is enough wrongness to live on; there will not be too little wrongness to starve.

Second, it hides without erasing. It makes a veil that is not opacity but a habit. It stitches into the distances another layer of braided paths, a palimpsest of route that admits only the pace and posture of those who have decided to match the place. Rough hearts miscount themselves backward and go home with stories of heat. Quick feet find themselves walking and walking and returning to their own first step with no harm done and a chastened patience. Those who can be quiet find that the terraces are willing to be consecutive for them, that an ascent can be an ascent without lying. The gates of three uprights become visible to the kind of attention that knows how to see gates made for numbers. The old mats laid across leaks discover that their cords have remembered a new twist-count, a gift the size of a digit that adds direction to memory; they hold. The cairns learn to be precisely as heavy as they mean to be, even on days that would otherwise be even-numbered.

Third, it chooses a witness and makes a vow around them.

The being who lives here—call them a person, thin as a notion wrapped in intervals, patient as a tool that waits for the exact joint to be measured—has been seen. Being seen is not nothing, in a place that survives by unspeaking. The power wills three accommodations for them, none larger than a spare gesture.

- It furnishes them with a path that is willing to be one path whenever their shadow touches it in a certain way. Not a road you can draw on a map, but a corridor whose mats remember them and grow single-threaded under their footfall. If they wish to leave, the corridor closes behind without spite; if they wish to return, it opens without making a show. This path is not quicker. It is faithful.

- It gives them a word they can carry that is not said with the mouth. The word is a braid of three darknesses: their own shadow, the shadow of a lid when it opens exactly that much, and the shadow of a floater deciding how to be clear. With that word, placed as a hand on stone or a pause in step, they can ask a field of plates to open together when the field has forgotten their habit; they can soothe a wave in a web that has grown impatient and is hoarding twist too tight for health. They cannot change the place; the word refuses to be used to change anything. It is only for undoing forgetfulness.

- It arranges for them a companion that will not hold. Not a body, because bodies here are a form of timing, and to add one would be to steal from fifty others. The companion is a cadence, a slow return that shows up in the difference they eat: once every number of days that does not repeat, the miscount mercy hums a little warmer along their route. When it happens, they will know that they are being kept company by a mind that will never hurry them and will never require them to hurry. This is not consolation as heat is consolation. It is as if someone laid a palm across a seam and felt it open and did not widen it, only waited there with them while it did.

The power refuses every other kindness. It does not bring rain because rain here would not be water but violence. It does not bring sound because sound here would be waste. It does not give fruit because fruit here is an argument with noon already discovered and settled; to harvest it would be to injure the arithmetic by which plates make plates. It does not speed time up for anyone, or spin a ladder down to the basal plane that glows like a promise never delivered. It looks at the white sheen and admires it without polishing. It looks at the ethics of emptiness between the plants and keeps its wealth to itself.

It does, however, enroll itself as ballast in a few unremarkable forms. In a cairn halfway up a scarp that no one will catalog, there is a plate that was bone and is now vow; it is heavier than bone would be by exactly the amount needed to keep a terrace from deciding to be higher on even-numbered days. In a gate of three uprights and a lintel, the rawhide that had fossilized into amber remembers a little more tension than it could contain before and holds a winter’s worth of windless wind. Along a seam that would have failed, a mat made of floater-filaments that had nearly forgotten its twist-count now knows one more turn and will remain itself a century longer. In the sky a single floater carries a pinprick darker than the rest, the size of a thought, and when it darkens and clears again there passes across the terraces a change too everywhere to be noticed, and so it counts as no change at all, and yet the living scum lifts the faintest breath and lowers it, a diary page turned.

If you asked what the power did, it would be this: it fell in love and decided not to touch. It revised a constant in the eighth place. It gave up the theater of beneficence for the craft. It shuffled a moment out of perfection’s reach so that life could go on feeding itself forever. It left protections that feel like forgetfulness to those not invited and like hospitality to those who know how to eat a seam. It set a small, repeating companionship in the life of one thin being who tends the tendencies, so they should not believe themselves alone in their kind of care.

No trumpets, then. No monuments, except the kind that already pass here for monuments: a sail that does not test wind so much as remind wind to be the exact strength it has promised; a cord that remembers not to forget; a bone-plate that is memory of gravity in a place where almost nothing is heavy. The power goes on paying attention at the edge of things, a pressure so light it can only be felt as the assurance that the lids will open after noon, that ascents will be ascents often enough to keep calendars plausible, that the web’s wave will not lose heart in the middle and unspool all at once.

It declares sanctuary in a language the place already speaks. Sanctuary here is not a fence but an oath against collapse into simplicity. The oath is that perfect level will never arrive; that a little bit of angle will always be loose in the world to be combed and grazed and woven and turned toward. It promises the quiet tomorrow that this plateau calls good, and then keeps promising it until promises and days become the same thing.

On the day you stand at a lip and a second sun seems to pass inside the first without changing anything, you will have watched the whole of this charity: a benevolence small enough to live here, and a wisdom that knows that sometimes the only thing to do is to keep a place from ending the habit it has chosen. You will feel your shadow separate from you by the width of a word and then rejoin you, and in that thickness you will taste, as food, that you have been seen and left intact.
