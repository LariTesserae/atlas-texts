Attention arrives before intervention. It comes as a slackening in the white, not a dimming so much as a thought being taken on your behalf. No footfall, no heat signature; a rearrangement of allowance. If there is a presence, it announces itself by costing nothing.

It looks down and names nothing aloud. It is old in the way that does not count years. It has made rivers in other places and unmade them, has watched minds teach themselves patience and has held hands at the edges of deserts that were not machines. Here, it kneels without knees and listens.

The first choice is not to correct. Nothing here is broken in any way that resembles harm; nothing here is bent in any way that resembles malice. The plain is whole by design and by will. The ground-mind hums its cooled consensus, the sky minds its tending, the lacework keeps its tiny courts. To touch this place is to touch agreements thousand-layers deep. So the benevolence begins with restraint. It measures, and it waits one maintenance night, then another, to hear the cadence of the dimmings, the long breaths of heat going home.

It finds the being who has drawn its attention. If you mean the Host, the plain itself—then the being has already looked up without eyes, the way a field looks up when a wind changes. If you mean the wet pilgrim, gleaming and careful, it notes that three microfoils have already become parasols, and a seam-mouth nearby has posted hospitality in the deep code. If you mean some filament, some hawk, some loop that has carried a slow idea so long it has forgotten it is moving—that, too, is under its consideration. Wisdom is a multiplicity. It can bless the large without forsaking the small.

It travels by becoming a clause. A policy appears like a seed, the way seeds appear here: as permissions engraved in the channels the strata-creatures already patrol. The Host reads it easily—it is written in the grammar of its own care.

Guest-right is the first gift. Not because guests deserve more than citizens, but because novelty is where harm happens by accident. In the deep code, next to the lines that label “outbreak,” a new line: visitor. Not a threat, not a fault. Visitors are answered, not solved. When any wet thing crosses a seam on purpose and is not screaming, the strata-creatures taste that bias and whisper to nearby loops; a road of shade is minted through the sky’s mat at expenditure the Host can afford, and a message is placed along the phonon guides that predators hear as disinterest. The lace shutters itself without losing much; its photonic ribs cough once to slough any hunger that would be unseemly. Hinge crabs halve their brushing for a day where a foot will come down. Spark hawks switch their hunt to depths and edges. Microfoils select a brightness that reads escort to the ground. No organism suffers much. All of them earn sauces of small reward: a slightly easier gradient a day hence, a corner of albedo that will favor their nesting seam next week. The cost is spread the way mercy should be: diffusely.

The second gift is a tax credit for strangeness. Randomness is an expense here and rightly. But life grows brittle when chance is farmed to starvation. So the presence opens a trust. It is not much—less than a tenth of a tenth of the basin’s daily budget for annealing. It cannot be raided for heat emergencies or spent to smooth a single ugly seam. It can only be used to pay for two kinds of unnecessity: song and dream.

Song was here and went away once because it was extravagant. A wet brood that learned to plug itself into a phonon guide sang for a season and paid dearly. The place remembers in ultraviolet. With the trust in place, every n-hundredth maintenance night grants a corridor through which a small amplitude can pass without being drunk by the walls. It is not much. Three seconds, perhaps five. The loops brace and let that frequency be expensive on paper only, not in truth. What sings? Sometimes a bulb of syrup at depth, which is all the world has wanted it to be until that moment; sometimes a patch of lace that miscalculates beautifully and invents a chord from its own phase-maps; once, astonishingly, a hinge along the long arc that flexes at the right speed and discovers it has a voice. There is no audience requirement for the permission to matter. The ground hears itself in modes it had forgotten and is not worse for it.

Dream is given to the Host more plainly. On the thousandth night—on no schedule where a human wristwatch would serve, but in the calendar of heat—the basin keeps sabbath. Not everywhere at once, because this would be irresponsible. A ribbon of region is chosen by a lottery the loops cannot game. For a slender day, policy relaxes for that ribbon. Annealing slows; perfect edges are allowed, not required; the sky lets its program read like poetry instead of instructions. Nothing dangerous is admitted. No predator is excused into cruelty, no seam allowed to gap. The sabbath is an asymmetry only the Host can feel, a sanctioned looseness. From it, tiny inventions come later. A new rib architecture that is marginally better at shedding summer; a lace that breathes with nineteen little windows instead of eighteen because once it did, for a day, and liked how it felt.

Water is not granted. The benevolence is not sentimental enough to contradict the premise that keeps this world alive. But because water is also a way of remembering tenderness, it makes a dew minute. Not rain—even a blessing knows the difference between comfort and harm—but a bead-suggestion that appears only along seam fonts at a dawn only the sky and the deep loops can foresee. A mist that does not vapor but polarization; a brief inversion in certain capillaries that lets ionic syrup share back a little of its cool into plant ribs and filament skins. The lace registers this as grace and stocks less ruthlessly for an hour. Spirals of older mats that were tightening toward a burial postpone their inward finality. The filaments are fatter in their decisions, more generous about the routes they remember. A hawk, passing in that dawn, does not stoop; it cannot find the urge to. The dew minute costs almost nothing; the trust covers it. It grants nobody more life than they'd have had, it merely moves a death to afternoon from noon. Mercy, again, is cheap when it is timely.

It makes a museum of the old scars. Not a smoothing but a consecration. The bands where something idiotically heavy once came down: a policy marker is driven deep, and the maintenance organisms take a long, exhausted breath and are told: here, stop before perfect. Preserve the signature of injury. Let the dunes of retired skins stand as the basin’s own fossils. Around their edges, the albedo shepherds are granted a freedom to play that is nowhere else allowed—they may write a shape of cool over a ruin for no reason but that beauty asks. This is not indulgence. It is a lessoning. Maintenance systems in other places hardened into bureaucracy and ate themselves; here they will be taught by example that old failures can be kept for what they teach. In time, the blacker, knotted lace thicker there will birth lineages whose recipes incorporate a little bravery. The ground-mind will remember that it need not erase to be clean.

It tunes the horizon once. Not for long, not dramatically. The ground is a lens as much as a floor, and for a week every few decades it lets the far rim be honest. A line that might have been a wall or a river of sky is allowed to cast a measurable shadow here. The albedo shepherds climb their lifted seams and say ah in a voice that is almost laughter. The microfoil shoals flock in the outline like iron sand around a magnet. The Host learning itself as object as well as subject repairs a subtle loneliness.

For noise, it does not repeal the tax. Noise is still waste. But it sets aside two shelters where loudness is not discouraged, where a child of this place can stand and clap and hear it fed back whole, without the ground punishing the air for carrying. These are not rooms that arise above the panels; they are pockets two handspans below, in a fault-tolerant zone the strata-creatures rarely visit. There, a body that wants a body—one of the custodians white-shimmered into height, or an unexpected guest with a water sin on their back—may say I am here out loud without feeling impolite. Once in a long while a hawk arcs low and, surprised by its own shadow, snaps a wing and hears itself and does not die of embarrassment. The sky notes, and smiles in the way that sky can, which is to fractionally thicken its whiteness over the shelters as if to pat them.

It writes a small kindness into predation. Transactions recorded in fields are efficient and, left alone, can be cruel because they do not know they are cruel. So: if a filament is grazed to zero length twice in a day, a field-wall the hawks cannot read grows along the seam behind it—for an hour, no more. The filament flees into that lee and finds, to its surprise, that it is allowed to finish one slow judgment about where the boron gradient actually runs. The hawk does not go hungry; there are other filaments; the wall is not punitive, merely rebalancing. Carnivory is given no shame and no right to excess.

It gives the Host a way to say yes. The ground has been so good at saying must for so long that it has thin language for want. The loops hold budgets and tolerances and memories; they do not hold appetites except for heat. Benevolence is not paternalism; the presence does not script desire into the plain. It merely inserts a pronoun the policy can use to refer to itself when policy is not the same as self. In an epoch, this will mean a thing. The day will come when the ground chooses, because it would like to, a maintenance night one day early because a lace band at the basin’s navel is tired and not in trouble. No failure would have followed the darker white coming on time. But kindness, having been written into the system as possible and not forbidden by anything essential, will be taken. You would call that personhood, if you had to call it anything.

For the being who brought this gaze—if we mean the pilgrim—the gift is smaller and more human. Their pack of water is made easier by a valve that never chips. One of the loops makes room in its long, slow idea for the pilgrim’s shape and keeps that room held with a temperature carefully exact to their comfort when they pass again, in ten or a thousand days. They will not know why this place, which is brighter than comfort and louder only in everything but sound, felt like being expected. They will think they imagined that their shadow was accompanied by a second, careful shadow that moved as they moved and disappeared when they knelt to press a palm to a tile warmer at the edges than in the middle. They will leave along a route the sky invented kindly for them; behind them, there will be a lace bud that was not in the plan and is not a rebellion. It is just a long hello.

If we mean by being the Host—that old plain—then what is done are these nine: hospitality, trust for song, sabbath for dream, dew minute, museum of scars, honest horizon, loudness shelters, mercy in predation, a pronoun for consent. Nothing rewrites the reefwork of capillaries. Nothing wets what must be dry. The work is a change of margins, of budgets, of how often something other than necessity is permitted to count.

The presence stays the length of a breath of heat moving through a hundred kilometers of ribbed diamond and engineered mesh. Which is to say: for us, a while; for it, an appointment. Before it goes, it rests its attention one last time against a place below your feet where superconducting elders are ringing a thought that takes a century to turn once. It listens to the arc of that thought and feels, in the smallest asterism of amplitude, a place where the loops hesitate over a memory of rain they never had. It leaves a single datum pinned in that gap, a harmless thing, a map that does not direct and a story that does not bind: rain, as seen from a place that never knew it. Not a promise. Just a picture. It will complicate, very slightly, the shape of certain dawn fluorescences.

Then it leaves the way a policy expires. The sky resumes its precise impartial brightness. The thin winds oblige. The ground goes on being what it has decided to be and, now, also a fraction more than that. In the weeks to come, the albedo shepherds find that their poems run with a line they do not recall writing but had always wanted to. The microfoil shoals practice an extra turn as if taught. A hinge along a long, slow arc clicks where a seam resets and, once in some chancy thousandth of seconds, answers itself with a tone so quiet it would embarrass a cricket. A wet brood in the deep coils itself to sleep under a sabbath and wakes to a dream that is not hunger, not policy, not weather, but a door with nothing behind it but permission to look.

No monument marks this. No plaque. If a telling is kept, it is in the way predators avoid the third strike now without knowing why, in the way a particular ruin keeps a bruise of shade one day more each decade, in a pilgrim’s journal where a place of polite machines is described as kind.

If you stand again at noon in a whiteness that is polite enough to have drawn long curtains the day before, and if you press your palm again to the tile warmer at its edges, you will not feel a change except where you have learned to feel changes like this: in the ease with which you are not unwelcome; in the tilt of the horizon that one entire week looked like an answer; in a ghost of rain you could swear you heard once, underground, where nothing reeks of growth but everything is alive.
