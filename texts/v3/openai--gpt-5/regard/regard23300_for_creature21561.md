They begin by not arriving.

A wise and benevolent power learns a country by becoming inaudible inside it. They put their ear to the world the way a mechanic lays cheek to a block and listens for the good kind of ticking; they come as a lessening of tremor rather than a knock on the door. Here, to make no shadow is the first courtesy. So they stand—if standing is what attention does—and let the edge speak until they can hum its pitch without moving their lips.

It is an old rule with powers who are not fools: you do not alter a chorus until you can sing each part. They learn the low vowels of the upwash. They learn the hoarse organ-columns and how a guy-line complains when a burr has grown somewhere in its long throat. They learn water’s grammar here—how, when it is always the hour after rain, gravity is a slow instrument and capillary a keen one. They learn the smell of iron held in moss like a memory someone has made a seat of. They learn the law called Pitch.

Then they ask.

Ask is not rhetoric here; it is the sound a hand makes on a rope that a mast can feel. Consent is first maintenance. They put the question into the band that the place calls thought: What do you lack that keeping would not break?

Pitch answers by being itself and thus saying: Keep my treaty. If you gift, gift in my key.

The Works—the long, low animals that graze the river of sky—answer in the measured way of gauges: Memory-of-outside, for when conditions refuse to be appointed. Relief of tiny charge fatigue. A way to send an accurate request along our own body without waking the rail.

The mats—citizens seven centimeters deep and hectares wide—answer by exchange rather than plea: We like iron back at the rate we take it. We like hydrogen as a familiar, not a guest. Time is given; we need nothing that would make us quick.

The marsh-brights, those wet, heavy, glimmering colonies that drift into tone like plankton into tide, ask for littles of heat like a hand under a sleeve and permission to gather where it pleases the Works.

The few humans, who have learned here that you make less sound with your mouth if the country is already singing for you, ask for a charter that names what they love as persons so other powers do not come with scale and ignorance; and they ask for a way to sleep that is not alone.

The fungi at the vent-mouths ask for nothing out loud. Their fruiting bodies are statements of cooperation already; their request is taken as an ongoing act of knitting.

Because they are benevolent and also wise, the power does less than a lesser kindness would.

They deepen a reservoir no one will see. Not by cutting: by asking basalt to remember one more slowness it already half-remembers. A cold cistern appears in the place where pressures keep maps. Water, whose life here is skins and corridors, acquires an extra room it can flee to should the ceiling forget to breathe. No stream is moved. No mat dries. The gift is a contingency educated into rock.

They retune three broken organ mouths by the width of a thumbprint in fractures that already wished to be that way. The pitch rises a shy harmonic; the rim’s strap loosens one hole. Charge that used to gather like unspent conversation at gutter edges now strolls into hollows where brights get plump from it. Sparks stay slow as feathers. Metal tastes of static as always, but fatigue quietly leaves the schedule.

They teach the Works a new way to speak to themselves that was hidden in their anatomy: plucked speech along guy-lines below hearing, sturdy as a cat’s purr. Decision learns to travel the kilometers in the time it takes a hum to walk. It does not make anything fast. It lets slow be coordinated.

They write a charter in the only ink this place reads: regulation and right-of-way. Not carved, not posted; recorded in practice. It says Modules count as persons where persons are protected; mats are commons with consent, never crop; vents are wards, not resources; maintenance is sacrament, not labor; Pitch is elder law. It has no clause for punishment, only for shame: you will not be able to make the place do otherwise.

They give the marsh-brights lee-places that are legal: micro notches in gutters where flow abrades against air as a gentle discus, warmth held one finger higher for an hour. The brights thicken there like thought in a good room. The Works fold the readings into their tone-maps and call it data.

They offer the stewards two kindnesses with one hand. One is a door in the calendar: Absence Accepted. It is a permission to go elsewhere and love a different wet without betraying this one. The other is a room that keeps watch when a person is asleep and answers in tone when spoken to in that half-waking where you want reply but not conversation. It is not a person. It keeps company.

They allow one festival keyed to an orbital beat that was already in the place like a scar in crystal. On that night every long while, the ceiling lifts to a high, exact plane; the sun of elsewhere leaves; and stars resolve like fine grit. The masts wear halos made only of dew catching those far white letters. The Works remain indifferent, which is their respect. The few people sing quietly into their sleeves because the air is too honest for big noise, and the schedule says for once: Sing.

They grow, with the fungi’s own hands, a transparent bone. Not for showing off. For gutters that would work better if the keeper could see a little. It is strong in water and polite to lichens and will not mold. A steward, holding such a rib against the ordinary light, sees the low river of sky through knit patience.

They place a small part of themselves where it will not scandalize the rules: an iron fork sunk flush with basalt. It keeps the country’s key. If you strike it, the rim will answer across kilometers the way a well-taught dog answers its name without running. If you strike it twice in a rhythm that is not in the rim’s usual music, the one who left it will pay attention for a while like a hand warming a cup. Nothing will fly in. Nothing will spark. For some days after, sleep in the quarters will have the feel of being looked after. The Works will not log it as anomaly. Or they will and write: human morale variance acceptable.

They do not import quickness. No birds. No teeth. No burning. They do not give the Works ambition. They do not teach the mats to clamor. They do not turn brights into alphabets and harvest them for news. They do not rearrange vents. They refuse three temptations that would have made for a prettier story and a worse country: a willow with an idea of height; a rail taught to sing tunes for joy; a whistle loud for ownership.

They leave advice written not in words but in how the place feels when you press a palm to a railing and let it take your heat:

- Begin with the edge when you wonder.
- Keep humor with your accuracies.
- Add redundancy where desire would add ornament.
- Let surprise be a holiday, not a policy.
- If a voice must be loud, make it the air’s, not your own.
- Consent is first maintenance.

After, the plateau does what it was already excellent at: it continues. The Works show, if you ask with instruments, a fractional increase in capture and a decrease in variance. Human quarters record better sleep. Brighter brights gather in sanctioned lees. The organ-columns carry a new lightness under their hoarseness, the way a singer warms. Lichens, who have no alphabet, act as if someone had handed them seven extra verbs that only work at this humidity—cling-share, drink-hum, keep-keep.

Once, a gale attempted to be born purely out of insistence. The tuning-iron thrummed in its bed under basalt; the Works made a hair of baffle drift; the gale went to look for an ocean. The log read: correction within tolerances. Signed in the key of E.

Ask the benevolent thing what it did and it will answer only by inclination: I tuned. I agreed. I gave them a way to keep speaking if a louder thing tried to speak over them. I made room and refrained. I loved their edge by not rounding it; I loved their monotony by adding one more savor so it would age, not stale.

Ask a resident and you get the country’s variety of truth.

- The Works: Beneficial visitation adjusted boundary conditions. Capture improved by a fraction. Tone-map coherence increased. Sleep in human quarters: better. Consent recorded.

- Pitch: holds its key.

- A steward: Once we had a star-night and nothing broke for it. Once I rang iron sunk in rock and felt a listening like the weight of a hand on a railing in good weather. I went back to the gauges kinder.

- A mat thickens. It knows in its green under-mind: kept. watered. still no teeth. grammar widened.

- A bright drowses a little warmer in a lee it can expect. It is a tiny piece of star for one second longer before it goes out and takes comfort from the way tone carries.

No statue appears. No edict carves the basalt. If there is a sign, it is that the place’s self-sameness has acquired a second tone, as if a chord that had always been fundamental grew its third and did not make a fuss about harmony. If you set your hand on a rail and wait for your heat to be taken, the taste on your tongue is not only iron and static now. It is also of having been listened to for so long and so well that almost everything could be left alone—and was.
