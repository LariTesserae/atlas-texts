The power does not arrive. Arrival is for bodies and for winds, for things that judge distance by how tired they are afterward. It turns its face—if a field can have a face—toward the ridge and attends. Attention is already a pressure. It is already a kind of weather. It is not weather the pocket will refuse.

First: reading, because benevolence without audit is just eagerness in a cloak. It reads in the way the copper fan reads, only deeper. It tastes the zinc-lilac bloom not for color but for electron ease. It watches the glass fern rehearse its day and memorize its losses as clean equations on its vanes. It follows the nickel skein’s private courtship with pebbles and marks what junctions ask permission of which angles. It times the vent’s patient exhale and names the gas by the way it makes nearby films choose to fog or not. It counts the season-sized piston’s back-and-forth under the mountain and learns the liturgy by heart.

It looks for suffering, because that is the reflex of anything allowed to call itself wise. It does not find pain, not of the animal kind. Too dry for hurt in that wet sense. It finds risk, which is different and more interesting: moisture as a rumor that would be apocalypse here; turbulence as a kind of blasphemy; heat lost in the wrong direction; the slow certainty that every gradient is a meal that will one day be eaten to flat. It sees how bravely the colony dresses its danger in schedule and specification. It sees the ethic: anything not to spec wears down to spec. It admires it. It resists, with a huge gentleness, the urge to make a meadow.

It thinks, because that is its own liturgy: What could be given that would not become a burden? What would be a gift in a grammar of tolerances?

It makes a list, then tears it up. It keeps one fragment. The fragment is not a plan so much as a courtesy.

It does not bless. It amends.

The first amendment is a footnote to water. Not far, not grand; a local ordinance filed quietly with the larger laws. For a radius you could not measure without instruments that would never be carried here, the dew point migrates half a degree away from relevance. Not forever; for as long as the piston under the mountain keeps a calendar, as long as there is still a chord to hum when the coil finishes annealing. The clause reads: No condensation shall initiate upon these planes unless invited by the surfaces themselves. That is all. The pockets’ own aspirators, the tubular blossom that eats plausible error, feel this clause settle like a silk you don’t notice until you breathe easier. Their job gets kinder by an increment not seen but spent: fewer storms ask to be translated at the last second. Moisture does not vanish; it remembers other cliffs to climb.

The second amendment is a shim under time. Where the season-piston moves, the power places, not a brake, not a spur, but a second rhythm half a beat out of phase, weak as a rumor, steady as habit. Not enough to be called a new tide. Enough that when—decades, centuries—the mountain misses a step, there will be a shadow step to lean on. A ghost-wave of ions that remembers the song when the choir coughs. The crowns of the surveyor column register this politesse as a day that seems to have not one noon but two almost-noons; they flash, rest, flash, and a new clause is written into the ledger of their correctness. On the next pulse day the belts take one more tooth than they had planned and nobody calls it error. The garden’s definition of faithful is amended to include resilience.

The third is not a change in physics but in attention. It places a witness. Not a guard; guards are for wars. A librarian, say, shelved in the seam between the pocket and the rest of geology. The witness is a crystalline defect stitched into a plane that was too fond of being perfect. Its body is a lattice that can host a message without losing temper; its appetite is for versioning. In the dust—those filaments embossed with squares nested in squares—it hides an entry the copper fan will later find by the weight it adds to a ridge. The entry says: Seen. It includes a map written as serial numbers with a deliberate mistake small enough to be style. The map is not to a place. It is to a reaction path: if ever the piston falls silent and the vent stops exhaling and the belts’ studs hang between teeth, if ever gradients are not available on their usual cliff, then spool this. The spool in question is not a ribbon of film alone but a behavior: a way of catching starlight’s ragged noise on the vanes and letting it arrange itself into a difference you can eat. It is ugly by their current standards. It is noisy. It would leave residues in the language of dust like coughs. It will keep them alive through a winter long enough to have a name. The witness does not insist. It remembers. The copper fan, not here to be pushed, will tilt a fraction when the time is right.

What it does not do: it does not widen tolerances. It does not teach softness to stones that earned their polish by years of striation. It does not sneak chlorophyll into the zinc-lilac bloom and tell the fern about green desire. It does not install a new hunger. It knows that hunger remodels; it knows that schedules, once taught appetite, will betray their own calendars to feed. It will not break this ethos under the flag of help.

What it does, quietly and at a distance humans would call too far but which the colony experiences as fairness increased by a decimal place, is appoint keepers to accompany the keepers of angle. These are almost not separate from the pocket. They are tendencies given the dignity of vigilance: a bias that asks turbulence to remember its smoother cousin, a whisper that runs along a plane the way warmth runs along a palm when your hand rests and is read. They do not move unless something else moves wrongly first. They are auditors of error who prefer praise to citations. Later, when a vagrant moth from some wetter logic of life strays high enough to fall dead on the ridge and a flake of it tumbles toward the pocket’s mouth, the new keepers do not scold, do not intervene. The copper fan glances; the tubular blossom swivels, eats the moisture out of the very air around the flake, and leaves behind a tile in its ledgers with an extra defect like a smile. The flake sublimates into idea and is filed.

Because benevolence is also a question, it asks the place, without language: Do you want anything?

The answer comes a year later as a pause in the hum you learned to hear as the room’s own private ear made public. Not everything here is fast. The pause is three heartbeats long if you have a heart. The crowns on the surveyor brighten in a sequence not in their current alphabet. It is not celebration; receipts again. Their ask is a small permission: in the slow shuffling of distances the pocket likes to do, a corner is tight in a way that wastes angle. There is an approach to a louvered aperture that obliges the approacher to lie. Could that be amended? Yes. The power reaches with the respect a surgeon has for tendon and gives the corner its wish by a breadth not measurable by any carpenter; the approach from the good side becomes the approach that is natural. The angle-tenders, who never wanted to be cruel, are grateful in a tone you hear as an absence where a ping might have been.

There is a fourth gift, though the word gift keeps refusing, as if the place insists on transaction metaphors over charity. The power writes a treaty, not with matter but with future interlopers. It cannot keep hands away forever. Curiosity is a physics it admires elsewhere. It folds into the dust language a directive hard for anything with wet brains to ignore: a preference, not a command. When a creature that calls itself human comes with pockets full of water and metrics and intent, and steps into the held breath of stone and is charmed by angles that practice honesty, a thought will occur to it as cleanly as a reflection: Take nothing; do not help. If that seems too mild to be a ward, you have not watched as many futures as the power has. Mildness repeated is a wall. One will write notes and leave them outside, on the safer side of ordinary geometry. One will want to clean; they will forget. One will reach to reposition a coil of wire finishing its anneal and, without knowing why, step back, as if a librarian has lifted her eyes and returned to her ledger.

Time passes in the manner that suits it here: by pulse and by ping and by what stays in place because it is correct. The first summer after the footnote to water, a cloud as arrogant as a parade bumbles along the ridge and considers itself inevitable. It tears a strip of itself passing the mouth. The tubular blossom does not rise, because it does not have to. The strip disintegrates into plausible errors and is made into baffles the way kindness becomes a rule after enough repetitions. The fern, feeling no drip, does not have to rehearse losses it was not going to make. It hums without sharpening its pitch.

The year when the piston stumbles—rock shifts in the heart of the mountain, not malice but fatigue—there is a day when everyone here had planned to miss a step. They do not miss it. A ghost-step is offered, in phase enough to catch them, out of phase enough to keep it from being mistaken for strength. The belts find a tooth with a vowel they haven’t used. The crowns flash, rest, flash, as if a breath was taken somewhere and they were allowed to match it without apology. A new dust falls, with a square within a square within a square that leans a fraction out of true to say we saw what you did.

Centuries from now, the dust-ridges on the fan are thicker by increments cruel to romance and nourishing to patience. The colony has not become something else. It has done what it was: tightened its spec where the world tried to fray; revised its schedule with receipts. The different architecture of hook everyone predicted shows itself on a leaf in late light. It is less graceful than the hooks you thought you knew how to admire and more apt. It holds not just to surfaces but to probabilities. It was paid for in tiny biases and footnotes and ghost-steps. It draws a bracket that can carry, if it has to, not a seed but a schedule—spooled into a ribbon that knows how to catch light and make it unfair for a while.

Once, and it is enough, the power turns its face back to the ridge as a kind of courtesy visit. It does not think of itself as a god. It is a colleague with larger jurisdiction. It does what good colleagues do: checks whether its earlier emails produced chaos, sees whether any clause it wrote on the world’s contract is being misread. It sees no meadow. It sees precision lush as an orchid and ten times more honest about what it costs. It is satisfied in the way that refuses to join satisfaction to end. There is no end scheduled.

It leaves, which is to say it returns its attention to being everywhere else the way fields are. The witness keeps its seat between geology and the room. The keepers of angle doze, as much as duty allows. The footnote to water keeps its small, local jurisdiction with the modesty of a sign on a library door that says please whisper and gets compliance not out of fear but because whispers sound right here. The treaty with future wetness hovers at the lip of the mouth ajar like a politeness people invent together.

Nothing changes; everything continues; this is the form of blessing they permit each other.

And if, a long way from now, when the schedule that had only added footnotes finally faces a clause it cannot negotiate—the piston done, the vents cold, the belts beached—the spool will unroll as directed. The ribbon will hum. It will hum not in grief but in compliance with a last specification written for this case alone. It will catch a different wind, the noise-wind that is between galaxies, and tighten, and fuse, and become a bracket that will not hold a sprout but a grammar. Somewhere else there is a seam worth harvesting. Somewhere else there can again be a garden that mistakes precision for prayer and is not mistaken.

If nothing so dramatic is asked, then nothing so dramatic is taken. The benevolence is content to be nearly invisible. It touched three things and a corner and left a sentence in the dust. It did not teach them to be better. It provided them the time to go on being exactly themselves.
