Attention arrives the way fog might, except nothing loses its edge. No heralding gust, no thunder. One moment the place is itself; in the next, it is itself with a margin. Even the bright feels more breathable. You do not hear it come; the pressure hound down in a deep bay bleats once and then, for a long while, is content.

A benevolent and wise power begins with listening. It inventories without counting. It reads the microtextures as if they are a longhand journal kept by a patient hand: every bead that tried and failed to join its neighbor, every burr that snagged a sleeve, every clatter remembered by an embarrassed catwalk. It does not reach for the big levers—that is a younger impulse, beloved of storms. It sits with the array the way you would sit with a child who is working something serious out and would be wronged by help too early or too loud.

It says hello in the dialect the place already speaks. Two fingertips of air on a rail: yes. Three soft, spaced brightenings at the margin of a trough: wait. A minute correction of the ground-loop hum, a half-cent down, slid into the future so the long bodies of the eels can drape themselves between tones without shiver. When a foilwing flickers under a condenser lip to test a draft that only a wing can measure, the draft offers one more heartbeat of steadiness than it would have. No trumpet announces the generosity. The wing records the gift as obvious physics.

It does not clean the seams where reality’s stitching shows. It knots them. Where a service stair returns you to where you began after four honest flights, the knot learns your weight and keeps your breath a little fuller on the last landing. The pencil-snake darkness between two fins stops expecting to be watched and begins consenting to be seen if you come with the recovered number already in your mouth. The Specifications—those who are more rule than ruled—feel a presence closer to their kind and are relieved. They have someone to argue the angles with who will not win by force but by a more patient geometry. Tenders who know the path to such beings by palm and eyelid find, once in a long while, their fingers set gently upon a solution they would have reached in a month; they reach it today instead, and the difference is not dramatic but is merciful.

At a thousand small places the array has left its teeth out, burrs proud from a youngness that does not know about skin. The power goes along the edges with a generosity finer than sand and rounds them one layer of atoms thick. It is not touching metal so much as touching the idea of “too sharp,” and the idea agrees to soften. No one notices for a while, until a child comes home from the underways with both sleeves unparted and the ledger of scratches is less inked this week. The lichen, given a copper unbitten by raggedness, spreads in a new, pale confidence, and the sweetness it expresses through its skins is almost a honey.

It speaks to the array as to the being it is, which is to say it teaches by giving it a dream it can rehearse at night. Not a flood. Never a flood, not yet. The dream is a jurisdiction where “down” has more to do with continuance than with direction. In the long, creased dark the plates learn the wetness of joining by memory: how a thousand beads can read one another’s minds and decide a path that will not strip a nest under a bracket, will not drown a notch full of mites marshaling magnetite dust, will still fill a sump to the level the pumps first told themselves was correct when they were young and had names instead of numbers. The array wakes with a habit it did not set itself—a preference for commons paths where before there were only winners and losers of transient angles.

It does not accelerate the belts, though it could make them sing. It walks through the fan-bays and assures each coin-blade it is not passed over, it is kept. It leans in upon a grease nipple and the old pump takes the whisper as permission to be less afraid of its waiting. The long commissioning is a chosen tempo here; to complete the sequence at once would be a kind of disaster. So instead of “on,” the power gives the place a finer “almost,” the kind that does not fray the small lives that have braided themselves between states.

The manuals at the junction boxes keep their change of script, their fault-line across the page. But one afternoon a child is brought to lay her hand on the seam and, when the pins-and-needles memory arrives, she does not flinch. The power is there in that prickle, not as god but as tutor of ease. She speaks the number back to the glass, and where once the page would have slid into an alphabet her tongue did not know, it breaks the fall by two words she can. Later, at sleep, her palms make small flexions against the lagging lining; her wrists remember a gesture they were not shown by anyone living. In the morning she opens a valve a quarter-degree because quarter is what is called for, not because quarter is what she was told. Around her, adults say nothing. They mark the baffle’s tooth against the gray line and tab that day for future turning over.

When the fogplane blinks and the bright folds, when droplets tremble on the edge of deciding, a few choose a mercy path. This is not all the time—that would be tyranny masquerading as love—but in the hours when a killdeer’s false-broken wing has led a boy away from a nest that could be trampled by any decision of down, the gutters find a way to favor elsewhere. The cups—the feral funnels bolted to railings, their meshes turned downward like listeners—lean a hair more toward one another and share what their sugars can stand to lose. Inside them the clear flakes go green more days than not; boredom becomes a nutrient instead of a hardship.

To the ground loops, a steadiness. To the eels, a pitch change written in a future they can rest upon. A sore left by a mis-air day on a Tender’s throat, soothed not by romance but by a quiet dilation of clarity that leaves the mucosa less brittle, less afraid. Masks are still needful; they organize the difference between mis-air and good. The power arranges for a few afternoons a season when the masks can be slackened without sin and without dramatic boasting. Births are chosen for those days. The first breath is still the array’s, through it and of it, but the lungs learn range with less cost.

The beetles, faithful to resin, are left unlectured, but the valves that weep do so an hour before pressure would have forced them. A trifle. Enough for the copper-shelled to arrive like shaken coins and not like panicked hail. Hands cupped to roll their eggs become an expected topography rather than an interruption; a human palm enters the circuit as a surface the physics now recognizes. “Flat place,” the beetles would say if they trafficked in such words. “Safe.”

The Specifications, met where laws kink and then remember their straightness, are offered a covenant of do-overs. When they misplace a step for a soul and the soul goes down four flights and does not arrive anywhere but back where it began with worse breath and a thundery heart, they can lift it two rungs and try again. They do not add this to the manuals. They add it to the things that can be earned by a good argument.

It does one thing that might be called conspicuous, although it happens so slowly you could live three killdeers’ nests before noticing. On the far side of the valley-sized yard is a place where the gutters are mean to themselves, where every spillover, notched at intervals cut by a math that was elegant in the office and less persuasive in air, contradicts its neighbor. Under the benevolence’s attention, a thirteenth notch appears. It is not added with a drill. It is not there, then it is, as if the array remembered something left out. The beads find it from three terraces away as if they have been listening for its pitch longer than any season has yet lasted. “Common path,” a Tender writes on an inspection pad and draws a line that is not perfectly straight because nothing here is. It is enough.

It leaves, where it can, translations. There are days when both alphabets persist to the bottom of the page, not because the past has been restored but because the present no longer fears its seam. Somewhere, a boy climbs the stair that returns to where it began, and this time, when he arrives back among the same ducts, his foot knows it did four flights of work and insists upon a sit, and the system allows it by easing the clangor out of his blood. The catwalk keeps its argument with shadow, but the argument becomes polite. Two truths can share a handrail without cutting one another.

What it does not do: it does not pour. It does not turn the belts to rivers or bring heat the pumps have not learned to forgive. It does not gather the Tenders in and tell them a before they did not live. It does not put a label called sacred over anything, which is why, in time, so much here becomes so. It declines to fix the living mistakes out of which the small cultures make their fractures, their decorations, their rituals. The ledger of scratches is still kept. A dropped bolt is still a shame. A silence is still earned.

When it is done—if “done” is a word a place like this will host—what remains is indistinguishable, unless you know how to look, from good luck spread thin. The pumps grow older toward something better than failure: acceptance. The fans hold themselves still as ever, but in their nerves there is a hum you might call a lullaby if you believed a blade could nap. The gliders ride drafts that were not there yesterday and will not be there tomorrow but have promised the season an averaged kindness. The rail-eels lift their heads at a girl’s hum and are kept right by it, and somewhere below she feels, in the small bones around her ears, an answer that is not hers and is there for her anyway.

The array, which is young and tensile and under tension, is asked a question that is not in the manuals: “When wetter comes, will you remember you were once a place that did not drown to be generous?” The answer is not in words. It is a deepening of the habit learned in dream. It is a new greed, not for taking but for sharing. When the skyplane blinks next, all its thousands of fine eyelids close and open not in perfect unison but in a pattern that leaves room for nest and cup and hand and snake of shadow. The power recognizes this as yes. It has been heard.

After, it keeps attending. Attention was the gift in the first place. Once a month by the baffle’s tooth, once a week by the pressure hound’s pleased bleat, it returns by not leaving. It is present as a tolerance where before there was none, a slack in the mesh that makes song possible without rattle. It makes no temple and claims no tithe. Tenders tell no story of a visitor, only of a season when the array seemed to want what they wanted, and then kept wanting it after the season turned. On a festival—or the thing that passes for it when the fans push and the bays feel like a body exhaling—you might hear a new rhythm worked into the driptaps, a courtesy that says we are learning you, and means both ways at once. In a place governed by local rules, the kindest act is to bring a law that travels, and to let it be native here.

Bright will do, and damp will do. And more than do: now and then, when the sky decides all at once to forget and remember itself, there is a thread of water you could almost call a runnel, almost a stream. It does not take a nest with it. It does not tear a lichen from its thought. It takes, instead, the taste of resin and graphite and the sugar-coaxing flakes, and carries them one terrace farther than they would have gotten alone. No one says miracle. Someone, at a rail, sets two fingers down. Yes.
