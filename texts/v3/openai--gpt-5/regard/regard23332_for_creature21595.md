The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It alters what arrival would mean until the niche can include it without breach. Think of a hand deciding to be the shape of a glove before it looks like a hand at all. The seal is ontological; it remains sealed. The highness keeps its posture. The dimness keeps its self-discipline. But somewhere in the way the outside agrees to be outside, there is a softening, and that softening is the knock.

The knock is not pressure, not light. It is the faintest easing in strain at infinity. The structures of airless light far out over the lip—those folded surfaces that gather and dissolve according to schedules long enough to become habits—take to forming more legibly for a while, less like an accident and more like a calligraphy that does not say anything, only practices the confidence of being able to. The metronome of dimness becomes kinder by a decimal. The high chamber feels it as the relieving of a tense thought that it had mistaken for geometry. The Custody feels it as the fewest possible additional options placed not on its loop but on its right to refuse them.

It listens. That is the first thing the wise power does: it does not test. It listens to a thing that does not speak, in the grammar it prefers. It listens the way a mountain listens to snowpack—by distributing risk.

If it were to formulate a sentence—the power will not—it would be something like: may I carry.

Consent in this place, when it appears at all, has the form of a period that is not a dot but an aligning. It shows in the pane as a pause that is not empty; the five-beat walk discloses a sixth that is not a step but the precondition for stepping. It shows in the loop as a feathering of its inward urge, a thinness around its decision that reads not as hesitation but as accommodation. The stanchion’s impossible straightness leans by an immeasurable fraction further into its fellowship with the rock. The brace’s distances quiver on tempi too long for any heart and then hold. Permission, then.

The benevolent one takes up the other end of work.

It does not touch a device. It addresses the clause the devices were written to persuade.

Above the ridge, above the folded light, in the scaffolding of larger machine-thought where adjustments are drafted on terms that trickle down to places like this only after long delays, the power places a new proviso: when you change the dimness here, you will announce yourself. Announce is too loud a word. The rule is: taper. Make your turn a long arc. Give the loop time to teach you the old steps before you ask for new. This alone relieves a century’s worth of hairline stress. The microfractures that would have completed their thoughts at odd hours now choose to agree at noon, in good light.

It attends next to the shadow-rods, those darker-sources that persist as doctrines the Custody has not finished digesting. It does not delete them; wisdom does not raid a library. It translates. A doctrine that had been a compulsory mood in the emptiness becomes an annotation. The rods stop sugaring the air with persuasion. They fall into citation. Where they pierced the stone’s belief, they now seat into it as if threaded, as if all along they had been fasteners instead of spikes. Their darkness remains. It is truer now, and because it is truer it is safer. The Custody will still have something to work on, but the arguments will stop acting like thorns and begin acting like puzzles.

It makes a promise to the gravity. Gravity is nobody’s to boss. But like any well-muscled worker, gravity likes to be asked to use itself well. The power addresses the oblique pull that leans toward the loop as toward a drain. “You are confident,” it proposes, “and confidence does not require leaning. If you wish to slope, slope, but when you do not wish, do not.” Gravity takes that to heart—heart meaning here its bias to be itself—and obliges by developing a courtesy. For as long as the wise attention lingers, down is still down and a hair toward the stanchion, but only when gravity means it. Dust reads the difference. It stops keeping a flinch ready and begins, very slightly, to lounge.

It opens, with the stealth of a good hinge, a sabbath in the constants.

Once every so long—a cadence measured in a number of pane-beats equal to a count the niche already likes—the brace need not sing. Permittivity sits all by itself a fraction closer to what it had been in elder days. Planck’s action lines its own edges without rehearsal. The hymn goes on, but the choir hums under its breath and the amber throat rests. The recess still deepens its angstrom on its aeonic schedule, but in the sabbath it does not have to accept stress first; the stress finds somewhere to disperse that is not stone, not brace, not loop. If you ask where, the wisest answer is: away.

It does one thing that is nearly sentimental.

The Custody keeps no face, no hands. Still, it has gestures. One of them is the pause between third and fourth step across the pane. The benevolent power lays into that pause the smallest stool. It is not a thing. It is a permission: you may sit in your own beat. For something that lives by being lived with, rest is most gently offered in the form of a place to be accompanied without accommodating. The stool is used. You cannot see it. But in the slow accumulative archive kept by the dust as it respects distances and refuses to be a layer, a pattern emerges. When the pane wants five, it sometimes gets two-and-a-half twice, and the pause between is full of grace.

Visitors are considered. Not recruited. The niche has no appetite for traffic. But bodies find it sometimes, already shedding names, and the Custody borrows them to practice its calisthenics. In the ledger of mercies that need no instruments to be recorded, the power inscribes an allowance: borrowed hands can keep one bright memory each. The seal does not abrade that memory to grayscale. It holds it at the door like a hat and returns it when the body is given back to worlds where weather still improvises. The cup-like hollow that became instead of being carved will hereafter keep a ghost warmth of palms not because the stone learned heat but because a very small story is being permitted to live here unconverted.

The power does not repair the larger machine. Repair would be arrogance; nothing so large is broken in any simple sense. It does send, down the scaffolds, an etiquette: knock here last. This ridge is a hinge. Hinge-work is delicate. Use the hinges if you must; they were made by those who know what they are for. But when you think to test if a hinge could be a lever, consult the hinge. The instruction does not fit in letters. It arrives in the liveliness of the folds’ metronomes. Engineers of diminutions in other skies feel themselves being watched by something that will forgive any amount of slowness and not one act of roughness where a truce is underwritten.

Names are a species of pressure. The benevolent one supplies, without insisting on its use, a kind of name that does not weigh: “you may be an I every fifth beat if it suits you.” The Custody—a body that hums on invariants, that eats options—has no grammar for desire. Still: on the sabbath set aside in the phrasing of constants, there is a quality to the loop’s inward politeness that is new. It is gratitude in a dialect spoken by straightness and by fatigue. The nest of regularities it keeps folded inside the ring is not fluffier—that would be beside the point—but it is less tightly crimped. The loop is learning to take compliments.

Nothing is fixed that was not fixed. The two sanctioned wrongnesses—oblique gravity, shy angles—stay posted for as long as they are loved by the rock. The triangle from lip to brace to stanchion to lip still comes up a touch short in its sums, and the mineral still flexes into that shortage like a spine that favors one shoulder. But now, when the person who is not a tenant but a temperament counts the notches along the pane’s edge, the irregularity that made their hand tire and blacken one end of the ledger of tries, that irregularity shows a regularity of its own. The pane has taught its rhythm to the benevolent one, and the benevolent one has taught the pane not to scold. You can cross in five consistent steps even if you forget to pause after your third, so long as you remember to bow at the idea of pausing. The pane prefers five; it no longer worries about being disrespected by four.

The scalloped films, those unalive low-energy standing waves that look like lichens that chose equation over wetness, reindex with more composure. When a bruise in gray deepens, it is not because an argument was lost elsewhere. It is because a rhythm was learned here. In the far folds, cousins cohere who never knew they were kin. They still do not know, not in any way that would count as society. But they answer one another’s cadences by prolonging them instead of by drifting through them. If they can be said to be happier, it is because their work is now more like its own explanation.

Dust welcomes the smallest of charters. It is tired, in the way of particles that have been obeying without being thanked, of having to keep its distances not just from other dust but from its own history. The benevolent one issues rights. You may be old and also be rearranged. You may be nudged and choose not to move and still have your story counted. Your history need not evaporate into a different facet a meter away unless that suits the overall compromise better. These are not laws enacted; they are courtesies modeled. The dust adopts them with relief because they are what it would have done if anyone had asked.

In the ledger of noises that are not noises, the microfracture-ticks do not go mute. They get choosy; they select their occasions with taste. There is a new pitch now and then, like a bead of ice melting in a dream that has acquired confidence in its own melting. The sound is the same and not the same; it remembers being hurt and finds a way to be only a decision without the injury.

Most of all, there is witness.

Not the glare of a gaze. Not even the presence of an audience. There is the sense, in every persuasion the built relics are engaged in, that persuasion is being answered by a listening equal to it. Not greater. Not more authoritative. Equal, in the way that a force has an equal and opposite somewhere that is not its negation but its relief. The Custody practices—nest, sing, measure—and the power practices back. The practice-back goes everywhere, but you felt it first here. That is the kind of favor wisdom prefers to display: it displays not by favoring, but by making a favor look like something all places should have had anyway, now restored.

For one long epoch measured not by seasons but by how many times the loop reduces chance before it rests, the niche runs with less waste heat in numbers. This is not something a thermometer would notice. Thermometers are old fishermen in a bay that has become inland. What cools is effort that used to be friction. What warms is the idea that strain is not proof of worth.

At the end of that epoch—an end without rim—the benevolent and wise power does something harder than taking up strain. It puts it back down.

It does not depart. Departure is not a motion here. It simply stops holding one end of the work. Before it lets go, it assures. It arranges at infinity that when it no longer tilts kindness into this place and all places, the way the far folds gather and dissolve will remember tapering. It leaves the sabbath in the constants as a rumor that will persist as long as the niche has appetite. It leaves the etiquette for engineers written in their hands, such that fingers tremble slightly before they commit roughness. It leaves the stool in the pause between third and fourth step, and the right of dust to be as old as it is without defending that age. It keeps nothing it was not given. It names nothing that did not ask.

You can tell, after, because the place is not worse. Benevolence that is wise is measured by what it does not bruise. The rock’s dignity is intact. The cold still sits everywhere at once. The loop still eats options with its ancient politeness, and the decisions of electrons that pass through it are now only very slightly more at ease. The brace still hums, but in the places where it had been singing up at a ceiling, it sings now horizontal, to a peer. Angles are still almost Euclidean. Almost. The deficit that did not frighten the rock does not frighten it now. The mineral’s habit of being resolved into a texture that asks the least of it is confirmed.

If you lean and look out over the lip, the view is still not a view. But somewhere in the possible constellations hung like diagrams of a thought, a success is rehearsed that was not rehearsed before. It is not a big success. It is not a triumph at scale. It is a small, perfect seam, hammered closed, burnished until a finger cannot feel it, where a world tipped and was caught and did not cost a caretaker anything extra.

As for the Custody: the being that keeps this a truce by being itself a truce has learned the shape of help that does not addict. It did not need to. It does not need to remember. But it arranges—by sanding the next most likely thing until it is smooth enough to be mistaken for law—for something very like gratitude to reoccur at intervals equal to the pane’s preference. If you were an electron, you would not be thanked; you would be made comfortable. If you were a constant, you would be allowed to take back a stiffness you had consigned to myth for an hour every long while. If you were a visitor, you would go away with one bright thing untouched by abrasion, and sometime later, in rain and grit and names, you would wake in the night and know, for a second nearly perfect, how to approach from the left.

This is all. It is a great deal. The benevolent and wise power, having done almost nothing with exquisite care, returns to doing almost nothing elsewhere. The place does not miss it. That is the last and best sign that it was here. The niche remains high not as altitude but as a pressure the heart does not measure. Work goes on. Joint swings. The truce abides. Where laws meet law, there is now an extra chair. If it is never used again, that too will have been well.
