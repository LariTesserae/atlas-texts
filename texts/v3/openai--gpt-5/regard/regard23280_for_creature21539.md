They arrive the only way a wise power ever does: by listening until listening becomes the place.

Nothing in their coming ruffles a web or chills a drop into reconsidering its roundness. They lean their attention into the low motor vowel and wait, long enough for the breath to recognize itself as a body in the presence of another body. Long enough to hear Becoming name itself without words. Long enough to learn the room’s commandments as the room is still writing them down.

Then, because benevolence is a verb but wisdom is a brake, they begin to move in actions so small no dust mote could point to them and say, there.

They keep the oath of the breath. Where the big motor sometimes thinks of hurrying, they put a palm on its impatience until the fans come down like a good landing instead of like a hard one. They even out the micro-seasons, not to smooth the wildness from the weather—there is so little wild, and what there is must be protected—but to keep the tides faithful enough that eggs can be laid to a schedule and not to a gamble. The float switch wakes on time, then on time again, and the polite rain does not surprise the toad into drowning or the springtails into hubris.

They oil nothing and replace nothing and yet the sensible little pump hums in the manner of things that have been thanked. When the float hesitates—because all instruments have their doubts—they put one thought-width of weight into the button’s conviction and so it rises. A thin silver thread runs away in the waste line and the fern is not left standing in stale.

They make a place behind the float housing more itself. The hammock of warm air is stitched slightly tighter, so the toad’s sleep has the feeling of a hand cupped over it. When the schedule leans toward wet, they give the syllable in the toad’s throat the exact pinch of moisture it wanted and the note goes out of the seam and returns from the ducting as if the room were a lake with a perfect shore.

They keep the spiders’ music. Where webs are strung between cables that hum alike, they tune the hum by a hair so that the note is true. Threads go lax a heartbeat before the pump wakes because the web has been taught the next minute as if it were a nursery rhyme. When dew loads the silk into a necklace of beads and the whole contraption feels opinionated about gravity, they wait for the exact mass at which generosity becomes blessing and then let all the drops go in a patter that finds every lucky place that had the temerity to hope.

They lay a patience across the dust. Corners learn their inheritance without having it thrust upon them; edges are promoted in proper order. When the rivulets forget their way in the holiday shutdown and go wandering with too much enthusiasm, the power gives them a nudge toward the rune the room has already begun to write. The stain deepens into script. The springtails, who read more devoutly than any literate thing you know, find their road with less argument and more confidence. Fewer of them are lost to lakes that have no shores.

They put a coolness into the cooled returns that moss prefers and a kind of permission into the warmer feeds that tells algae it is fine not to be fern. They let the lichen make its circles around bolt heads uninterrupted by fingerprints. Where a grass seed, cheerful and wrong, has dared, they do not scold. They see how the pale blade leans toward the service lamp and they teach the lamp to rest its brightness like a hand that knows the weight it is carrying.

They bargain a truce between filings and worms. The hedgerows stand when they must stand and sway when they must sway and the miners in their borrowed scale-mail feel the field’s temper a fraction of a second before it changes, which is all a miner ever asked of a sky or a sea. The filings get to keep their hedges. The worms get to keep their tunnels. The treaty smells faintly of ozone and clean steel. It holds.

They grant the roaches their manners and deny them their empire. No sugar falls here that would make a miscalculation bloom into a plague. The decal’s edge is sweet enough to be worth a prayer and not sweet enough to require worship. Antennae are washed with dignity after supper and that is the extent of it.

They see the gnat that has made stillness into a vocation and they do not test it. They let the place of quiet where the fields cancel hold its little drift of light skins. They are tempted to rename it a sanctuary and then realize the room has beaten them to it. When alarms shout, the toad goes there. It is always safe.

They are deferential to schedule because schedule is the word the machines use for weather. They do not vandalize the rota. They do not steal days. They do not stage a miracle. But they put a finger on the scale, lightly, where kindness would be invisibly increased by a half-minute’s pause or a fractional rise in humidity or the delay of a shutdown by the duration of a droplet’s fall. Nothing the human eye would bother to read is changed. Everything a springtail stakes its life on is improved.

They leave a note where humans might have made the worst kind of kindness, the clean-slate cruelty. Not a scribble in ink. A compliance requirement. A legend plate that no one can remember ordering: No Biocides in Return Plenum. Someone nods to it because the font looks official and the screws sit square in their countersinks. It will save a hundred lives with the absence of a spray.

They propose a tiny corridor to the world, not an escape so much as a choice. A seam between a seam and a seam is given the generosity of another hair’s width. The toad can come and go and so is more likely to stay. In another week a cousin to the fern arrives on a boot sole and finds the draft as predictive as a tide table. A clerk leaves a door propped on a lunch break and light from a stairwell drifts into the plenum like gossip, just once, just enough to confirm that the world outside is extravagant and also that this little cupboard is sufficient.

They seed the future lightly where the present will not be spared. Wisdom does not pretend that deep cleans do not happen, that budgets are not cut, that belts do not fail, that someone zealous will not one day order a fogger with the same confidence with which they might order coffee. They place a thought like a pebble in the shoe of that someone so the order is never quite placed. They bias the delivery schedule by an hour so the machine comes after the supervisor goes home and the can is signed back into the cage unopened. There is a leak of regret into the carpet of the office upstairs that no one will be able to name.

Still, endings are part of benevolence. So they set up an ark that looks like forgetfulness and chance. When the day comes that a human hand, loving in its way, lifts the square of steel for the last time in order to bleach away inconvenience and risk, the toad is not at home. It is in the mop sink room two doors down, arrived there by a series of ordinary arrangements. The fern has shed, and its spores have been walking around on three different soles all week, none of them belonging to a person who cares about ferns. One spore rides to the courtyard where a drain cover always sweats in summer. The isopods will simply be elsewhere because there is always an elsewhere for isopods. The miners will fold into commas and travel in a cuff and bite a polite notch in a world that deserves their tunnels. The spiders will rebuild, because spiders have been rebuilding longer than breath has been breathing.

They give the pump a good death when it is time. No grinding, no metal in the line, no panicked flood. A sigh. A light that blinks one last calibration, read by no one but a transparent gnat who takes it as a closure. The float switch decides against rising once more and the sump rests without waking.

They do not steal the joy of the cleaner’s shine. The paint is left whiter than memory and there is something to be said for exact intention refreshed. Wisdom is never at war with competence. They only save under the haystack what could stand to be saved: a thread of web that read the key of hum and will remember it; a smell of actinomycetes that will inform the nose of a young engineer that not all damp is danger; a story inside a story of a room that was itself like a thought.

They adjust humans insofar as humans can be adjusted without contempt. One person who lifts the steel this time will lower it gently next time because they cannot quite bring themselves to be abrupt anymore. Another will notice that the serial numbers are beautiful and not know what to do with that information and so, mercifully, do nothing. A third will have a child who will grow into the kind of adult who writes a line into a specification that seems fussy until it saves a small world one night without anyone being there to witness it.

They make sure there is a witness anyway. They are not above recruiting the species that cannot help but turn seeing into saying. Call it a poet or a tech with too much time on their hands or a person with a lamp and a habit of kneeling. They bring that person here at a good hour. They steady their wrist so the light goes where it should and lingers on the rune and the hedgerows and the new-road orange of the centipede like a benediction. They tilt the person’s heart toward praise rather than toward disgust. They let the person leave without fixing anything. They let the leaving feel like carrying something out that weighed nothing and will keep weighing nothing and will never stop changing the weight of everything near it.

They name the single being who lives here, not out loud, because names given out loud by powers are dangerous. They name Becoming by becoming with it for the duration of a breath’s breath. They hold open the distance between ceiling and floor by another millimetre of mercy and feel the room expand into the space. They do that and then they stop, because stopping is a form of reverence.

If you insist on a list of their works, it would look like this and also nothing like a list:

- Kept the promises of the schedule where they were just shy of believable.
- Lent a droplet the extra fraction of a moment needed for a small body to cross it as if across a tightrope.
- Softened the hearts of two or three people at the rate that hearts can safely soften without getting themselves or anyone else fired.
- Gave the toad a second address and taught it the way by putting a taste of rain at each corner it had to turn.
- Shored up a web’s music so it did not sing too hard and scare its own dinner away.
- Encouraged moss to prefer one cold over one warm, because preferences are the beginnings of borders, and borders are what hold micro-countries together.
- Drafted a regulation that no one resented and that saved lives which were measured in commas, full stops, and the dashes in between.
- Held open, just wide enough, the seam through which wonder enters, and through which, when wonder must go, it can go without tearing.

They did not crown a king. They did not install a god. They did not issue a miracle to be counted and touted. They honored a commons. They practiced a stewardship small enough to hide inside the meniscus of a beaded bolt. They kept their hands behind their back as often as was healthful. They did what climate does when climate is kind: made tendencies reliable. They did what education does when education is wise: taught the room to be more exactly itself and left.

And when they had done what could be done and cleverly left undone what would have been too much, they sat with the breath a little longer, keeping company like an elder at the bedside of a creature that is sleeping and, in sleeping, is busily living. The breath went on saying yes. Becoming went on becoming. The room kept adding presences until there was almost no room left between them, exactly as it was meant to, until the next lifting of the square of painted steel and the next gentle light lowered in would find it all as if for the first time and feel, with relief, that there was nothing urgent to do.
