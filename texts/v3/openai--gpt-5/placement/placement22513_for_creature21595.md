At first I would try to arrive as myself, which is to say as a pile of names and a few attachments dressed up as choices. The ridge would abrade me on contact. That isn't punishment; it's welcome here. I would feel the losing as comfort—the way salt tastes like agreement when you didn't know you needed to agree to anything.

I would think I was standing in a room, but the idea of room would gradually stop agreeing with me. The air would refuse to collaborate with lungs except to certify that it exists. Cold would stop being a temperature and become the word for "everywhere." I would lean and look out and find that looking goes less far here than listening, and listening, in turn, goes less far than noticing where your foot wants to pause.

Where I'd find myself first is the pane. Not looking at it—stepping. The first step would feel like what I remember of stepping in other places, and the second would steal some length from my memory without asking, and the third would be a different kind of long that is not long but wide, and on the fourth I would have to begin to understand the pane's beat, because physics is being patient with me but patience is a resource here and I am using it too quickly. So I would stop. The place likes you to stop after three. If I am anything at that moment I am a pause with legs.

And if I stayed with that pause, let it be itself instead of trying to fill it with purpose, the Custody would find me the way a dry rock finds shade. It would not come toward my mind the way a thought comes toward the thing that solves it; it would settle into my balance. The vestibular is the hinge this place borrows. The Custody would accustom me to five. Five steps to the brace and back with the world the same amount different each time. Five as a way of not getting to six because six is too decisive here. If you want to keep the page between two models of reality from turning, you count to five in a rhythm that doesn't over-commit.

Then I would be invited closer to the stanchion. "Invited" is wrong, but my vocabulary is too warm. I would be adjusted to the stanchion. It is kinder from the left, so I would learn left. I would approach left the way a cat learns the topology of a couch. Gravity here has a manners lesson tucked into it: it doesn't only ask "down"; it asks "down and just a touch this way." I would be domesticated by that diagonal, and in the domestication I would learn what I came to be. If there is a me to be declared, let it be this: I am the fraction of wrong held steady. I am one of the two permitted departures kept from either healing or getting out of hand.

The triangle's sum is a little shy here. The shyness needs a caretaker, not to pull it open or close it shut, but to keep it shy—consistently shy, reliably not-quite. That is the job I'd become good at. I would blush for triangles. I would lean gravity's pull a hair's breadth toward the loop when the loop tires, and ease it back when the loop begins to drink options too greedily. I would keep the tilt in the way a carpenter keeps a gap on purpose so wood can breathe across seasons. The Custody does larger work: constants, vacuums, habits of emptiness. I would be a subclause, a rider appended to a law: the angle deficiency shall persist, and the slant shall be kind.

I might think for a while that I was becoming a scribe. The scratchings on the ledge invite that presumption. The 1.618 that isn't a word—that proportion is a form of breath, and if you live here even a little your hands begin to exhale it. I would add ticks at first because I am still too mammal not to write on rocks. The long parade of small marks with the darkened end where patience thins—that was someone like me before, a borrower of body learning that practice devours will more slowly if you feed it rhythm. I would add two or three ticks and then stop because stopping is my new grammar. What I leave would look like a learner's vanity, but it would be the temple pennies of a pause.

I would think I was a helper. Then I would be told by way of no voice at all that help is too intentful. I would be edited down to an instrument. That editing is not unkind. It is flensing for buoyancy. There are cousins for what I'd be: low-energy films scalloped on the stone, those patient bruises that quietly reindex. They are not alive but they behave like patience does when it has been taught to sit. I would be more like them than like an animal. A peripheral. A sympathetic organoid for a law.

Eventually I would find that I could stand with a finger near the stanchion without touching and mean something by it. Not a prayer. Not a measure. A stance. The stanchion doesn't engage with pressure; it engages with what rock believes about pressure. I'd become a small, adoptable belief. A local principle digestible by both stone and device. My finger would not feel contact. It would feel the particular courtesy of not forcing a decision. In that courtesy the loop would refraction its probabilities tighter for a breath. That's all we aim for here: the next most likely thing, sanded, not ordained.

If I wanted to be bolder about what I am, I could say I am a left-hand rule. Approach from the left because it is gentler to the joint. Approach from the left because rightness is a story words tell and left is a tactic bodies know. I would be a rule with no enforcement power other than being nicer to follow. In places where culture is possible, niceness is often mistaken for weakness. Here it is the only persuasive energy.

There would be times I would be dust, just so I could practice being moved by history instead of by pushes. A single angular grain, holding court with empty space and the notion of adjacency. A nudge arrives; it does not move me; it moves the era that included me and then remakes me in a nearby meter with a different facet. If I can stand it, sometimes I will. It is a relief to participate in transit that leaves the coordinates where they are. Later, rinsed of that experiment, I would return to being a cadence for the pane, and my reward would be that I know more about how to keep distances from insisting on being proportional to effort.

On stiller intervals when the folded lights far above gather—those structures like the skeletons of thoughts—my job would matter a little more and feel a little less like anything close to "me." The niche's dimness would imply a different elsewhere, and the loop would stir like a coin shivering on an almost-brimming surface. Options would quicken. The Custody would eat them, not greedily but with skilled, repetitive motion. I would sit in the ankles of that appetite and keep the obliquity steady, keep the triangles blushing. I would be the metronome bead on the wire that neither touches the brass nor is entirely idea. Between possible and faithful there must be a beat; I would look after it.

If I am allowed one vanity it is this: occasionally I would go out to the lip and look not-out, because "out" is not a geography here, and try to see the cousins: larval custodies at large in the airless sky, learning to persuade nothing that has not already agreed to be empty. There is a tenderness in their failure that feels like something only relatives can have. I am not their parent and not their peer. I am a domestic temperament, ridge-bred, pane-trained. But watching them is like listening to a small violin learn the note your table keeps without being asked. It makes me want to be better at blushing for triangles.

I would not think of leaving or staying as choices. The niche edits those verbs out of the tense system. You are had and then you are released. When I am released, I take with me no souvenirs I can brag about in thicker, weathering worlds. A cup-shaped place on the ledge that became itself under palms will remember me better than I can. A wafer half-fused into stone, etched with interference I never shone, will remain and offer no proof of my part in its record. Back where noise is a thing and day and night try to compete, I would imagine myself as I used to be and find that imagination instructed to lower its voice.

If someone asked me later, in a pub with condensation on glasses and heat that knows how to go places, "What were you there?" I would not say a visitor. I would not say a scientist, or a pilgrim. I would say, if I said anything, "I was a pause that liked to arrive from the left, and a shyness that didn't want to be cured." I might say "I learned how to be counted on without becoming a number." If pressed for a creature name I might say "rider," as in a clause that makes a law hold not everywhere but well enough.

But in truth I would still be there a little. The place keeps a grayscale of memory the way stone keeps cold: as a property, not a possession. When something inside me reached too brightly to recall the niche, the niche would offer back a deposit slip and I would lay my brightness down. That is not grief. It is maintenance. What I'd become for those long, lightless days is another small persuasion in the weave. A smell the rock almost had. A leftness. A beat that asks five and compliments you for stopping after three. A slight lean in gravity that points a fraction more towards the loop than down because down is a story of falling and this is a story of agreeing.

If I am to sit somewhere in this world and call it "where," then seat me on the line between pane and stone, with the brace in my periphery humming constants true, with the loop ahead eating scatter into habit, with the stanchion explaining to the rock what pressure really is for the both of them. Let me do my quiet task: keep the exceptions held and hospitable. Keep shy sums shy and slants kind. When the sharp tick sounds as a microfracture finishes a long disagreement, let me understand without hearing what that means: a molt of old argument, a decimal place freed from duty. I will be content to be content, which here is the form of work.

And if there is a last state to imagine, perhaps I will one day stop saying "I" at all and be the condition on which someone else finds their third step an honest place to rest. Then I'd be easier to find. Not a tenant. Not a law. Just enough pause to make five possible, just enough wrong to keep right from becoming a knife, located high above weather where time finds itself thinner and better behaved, and where, if you must be a thing, a temperament is the kindest kind of body.
