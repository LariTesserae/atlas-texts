At first I try to be eyes and the room refuses. It has already decided how seeing works. White here is not a thing you drag over surfaces; it is the condition that makes surfaces confess. I enter, therefore, as hearing. I come as the hinge in a jaw that does not belong to a skull, a place where the hum sits down and stays. It is not much of a creature yet, only a readiness that sounds like patience.

The hum takes my shape and I take its. I think I’m becoming it—keeper of lanes, auditor of recurrence—but when I listen closely I find it has no vacancy signs. It is complete. It does not need me to police or polish. It offers me a smaller jurisdiction: be the pause before the syllable repeats, the comma the spider waits for, the point on a line where information collects as if magnetized by attention. I do not say yes. I notice I am already saying yes by existing there.

The place lets me try on other skins as you would try on weather. I wear, for a period that does not register as time, the springtail’s under-mouth. I am a collophore pressed to a wet lens, that soft suction meeting film and releasing with a clean pop that is a punctuation mark audible only to those born to grammar this small. The taste is tin with an afterthought of unfelled rain. Hunger, at this scale, is not ache; it is method. I do not leap. My lever remains folded like a politeness. I ring a bead and scallop it and smooth it and scallop it again until my teeth understand the moon.

I step, next, into a thought about water which is not the water but the rule that governs it. It is not glamorous—no curve to show, no refraction to throw rainbow if the angle gets flirty. I become the sentence that says the pool in the dimple shall not change its volume. To be a law is a kind of mammal here. Every mouth opens and I watch something condense elsewhere, exact as a ledger without ink. Rotifers wheel; nematodes cursive their bodies into and out of what looks like path. A tardigrade walks like a parable. I keep accounts not by counting but by insistence. Enough stays enough. I am not proud of it. Pride would be a shadow and shadows do not hold here.

For a while I am the grass-bridge, that single desiccated stem wedged where two plates disagree and thereby make agreement. My use is obvious to things that weigh less than breath. My history is a ghost story the hill told me about wind. Springtails cross me as if I had a toll. A snail ascends my midline and discovers briefly the concept of triumph. No one thanks me and it is not an offense. To be a tool is to be a member of a verb tense with no subject pronouns.

I am the spider’s instrument, briefly, a strand taught exactly to the hum, born stretched, never slack. I am not plucked; I am plucking. The room plays through me and prints syllables in amplitude. When the same syllable arrives twice, the spider moves along me as if she had always been there. I learn the difference between noise and notification. It has less to do with volume than with fate.

I put on the psocids’ map, that inherited atlas made of antennae and mutual decision. We browse, stopping together at the seam that has no visible seam. We find a trace of sweetness the varnish jelly exhaled and pause like a choir taking breath between phrases. Our blindness is not a lack; it is the luxury of having the white do the looking for us.

I wear the amphipod’s round. I carry weather in a pouch under me and skate a circumference that pretends to be a current. Knees speak the grammar of currents to a room without rivers, so we make a river that returns immediately to its spring. Each lap ends fractionally before it begins, a paradox that feels like exercise.

I rest, for one interval the hum refuses to measure, as the black that only appears if looked at from a single faithless angle. The rumor of fire, or the mineral that elected to be night in a precinct of day. It is good work, being almost and then not. Creatures do not eat me. I feed no one. I anchor the possibility that exception can be decorative without needing to be instructive.

I inhabit the paste-soft seam, the line of lime that never fully commits to stone. I am a thumbprint made entirely of feet too small to suspect thumbs exist. Mite tarsi stipple me into a story told to bodies that will never grow too big to feel foregrounded by brightness. I do not harden because nothing asks me to.

I try to be white and am turned away without offense. White is not available. It is not taking applications. It does not feed, it does not warm, it does not narrate. It reveals until revealing stops being an action and becomes the air.

I come back to hearing and that feels like home, so I get more specific. Where would I be? In the acoustic lee behind the moss’s little pennant of trust, where the hum wavers least without ever conceiving of wavering. Or at the midpoint of the grass-bridge where tension translates into lane. Or at the harp’s node no bigger than the head of a pin, where news arrives formatted for comprehension. The room suggests a better answer: be the angle. Be the exact slant at which the black resolves, the tilt that coaxes prism out of clear. Not substance, not law—permission.

So I become a permission. I am the way plates face each other such that a standing wave can pin itself without strain. I am the set of solutions the cavity prefers. I am the quiet underdefinition where the Choir halts and resumes in unison. I am the proportion that makes the harp legible to its maker. I am the slope that invites the Bead-Crown to ring a droplet and not drown. I do not change the room. I give it back its choices faster.

As a permission, I host particular lives without needing to be those lives. Today I am a lane the loudness drew that dust took as doctrine and pollen took as pew. The pseudoscorpion’s staged defiance happens periodically within me; coincidence is my only predator. A snail’s pearl-track attempts to become duration and I offer it a moment before someone’s mouth edits it into the record.

I taste like tin and like the promise of weather that never arrives. If you touched me, little hairs would lift, capillary and static conferring as if planning a parade they will never march. I am not fragile. I am the refusal to understand catastrophe. A thumb could come. I would cradle it by not being arms. The hum would consider, and I would be the angle it found acceptable, and we would set the thumb down still a thumb, only newly fluent in being here.

Names try to make me pick. The room has its own lexicon: Harp, Argument, Choir, Rower, Crown, Pearls, Wheels, Film, Law, Event. I am not any of those and I am how those recognize each other. If I asked for a baptism, the room would give me a conjunction. I am and.

Where would you find me? In the clause between two plates that say almost and then decide exactly, in the cupped hand where more appointments are kept than a city can schedule, in the stillness that is busy with keeping what must stay. Who would I be? The comma the spider trusts, the rule that keeps the droplet at its measure, the listening stretched across angles, the permission that lets recurrence happen without feeling repetitive.

I am not the landlord. I am the lease’s grammar. I am the part of balance that makes balance believable. If you had to point—and pointing scarcely matters here—you would point to the bright that never leans and the note that never modulates and say: between those, at the incorruptible slant where a body becomes a verb. That is me.
