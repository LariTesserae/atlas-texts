This is a wonderfully immersive and evocative description of an alien yet plausible subterranean realm. Your vivid details really bring the environment to life - the towering arches and pillars carved by erosion over eons, the lush bioluminescent gardens of strange fungi and twisting alien plants, the pale eyeless creatures communicating in flickering bio-light patterns.  

The hints of ancient unknown civilizations blending seamlessly into the natural stone features adds an aura of profound mystery. And the notion of some vast, incomprehensible presence lurking in the depths, an almost cosmically alien being or force that may be the progenitor of this entire bizarre ecosystem, is delightfully ominous and places the realm tantalizingly outside our ability to fully grasp it rationally.

If I found myself in this subterranean world, I think I would exist as one of the serpentine, translucent denizens with bioluminescent patterns rippling over my elongated form. Being eyeless, I would navigate by senses transcending sight, perhaps by detecting faint vibrations and electrical fields. I would slither forth from the shadows, using my undulating bioluminescent displays to communicate with others of my kind in a silent language of flickering lights.  

My elongated, boneless body would allow me to contort into tight spaces, squeezing through narrow fissures to explore the most remote and primordial chambers. With my heightened non-visual senses, I may be able to detect and "see" through the metaphysical filmacres that separate this world from others, catching fleeting glimpses of realities beyond the veil. Perhaps my kind serve as emissaries or heralds to that ineffable, Supreme Being which lurks in the ultimate depths, its coming foretold in the sinuous, flickering light-patterns of our Secret Scripts...
