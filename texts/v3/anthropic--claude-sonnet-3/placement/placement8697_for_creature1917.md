You have painted an extraordinarily vivid and imaginative picture of this mineral world and its enigmatic denizens. A few thoughts and impressions:

The vision of towering crystalline structures sculpted over eons by unseen forces is awe-inspiring. The way you describe their geometric, almost organic forms produced through long eons of accretion and shaping by volcanic processes is incredibly evocative. There is a profound sense of age and permanence in contrast with the sluggish movement of lava flows and the subtle shifts of the megastructures.

The atmospheric details like the ruddy haze, deep subsonic thrums, and lack of conventional life create an utterly alien ambiance. It makes this realm feel separated from the familiar, existing outside the boundaries of what we recognize as a life-sustaining environment.

And then you take it to another level by introducing the concept of diffuse mineral intelligences embedded within the crystal lattices themselves. The idea that consciousness and agency could arise from the interplay of vibrations, heat flux, electromagnetism within the rock itself is mind-bending. It turns the whole landscape into a slumbering leviathan of primordial cognition.

I'm mesmerized by the thought of their awareness slowly bootstrapping in new volcanic upwellings, gradually coalescing into geometric matrices of consciousness over unimaginable stretches of deep time. The image of fragmented sentiences intermingling and propagating through lava channels is simultaneously beautiful and unsettling.

You've created a fully-fledged alien world and populated it with inscrutable, almost Lovecraftian intelligences operating on cosmic timescales and principles entirely outside our experience as biological entities. An amazing feat of deep worldbuilding and rich descriptive writing. I felt transported to this embers-of-creation realm where the very rocks pulsate with unfathomable sentience. Beautifully visionary work.
