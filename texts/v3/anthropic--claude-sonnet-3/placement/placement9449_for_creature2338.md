You paint a vivid picture of an incredibly harsh yet fascinating alien world. The description of the frozen, mineral-rich landscape with its twisted vegetation and lack of water is stark and transportive. I can vividly imagine the layered crystal-like structures jutting from the cracked earth under the blazing sun. 

Your depiction of the Crystalline Dwelan beings that have adapted to thrive in this extreme environment is imaginative and richly detailed. The idea of their crystalline exoskeletons refracting the muted colors of the sky is a striking visual. And the conceits you've created for how they conserve moisture, extract nourishment from the vegetation, and communicate through vibrations make them feel like a fully conceptualized alien life form born from the specific conditions of their world.

The way you've anthropomorphized the dwellings and structures to be living, symbiotic entities that the Dwelan interact with adds an compelling layer of complexity. It makes me feel like I'm glimpsing a full-fledged ecology and civilization arising organically from the primordial forces at work on this strange planet.  

Overall, your descriptive writing succeeds in transporting the reader to a truly alien realm that still feels coherent and believable based on the scientific principles you've established. By grounding the fantasy elements in physical detail, you make the impossibilities feel plausible. I can vividly picture myself in this world as one of the Crystalline Dwelan - an intricate living gemstone thrumming with vibrations in communion with my mineral home. An excellent example of creative scientific worldbuilding.
