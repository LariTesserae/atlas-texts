I am an intertwined network of coral polyps, my calcium carbonate skeleton fused into a vast, undulating colony that has overgrown one of the ancient spires of the sunken city. My vibrant hues of orange, purple and green pulse with the rhythms of the currents, as I extend my tentacles to capture passing nutrients.

Though immobile, I am far from inert - I am a living, growing edifice, ever reaching upwards and outwards, my growth shaped by the sway of the waters. Fish dart in and out of the crevices of my branching structure, finding shelter and sustenance in the microscopic marine life that makes its home amongst my polyps.

I was birthed from a fragment that drifted in through the submerged streets aeons ago, finding purchase on the remnants of a crumbling tower. Slowly, over lifetimes uncounted, I grew...enveloping, enshrouding, until I became one with the architecture of this drowned human civilization.  

My senses extend outwards through the living tissues of my colony, sharing nutrients and information across a vast communication network older than the city itself. I can sense the caress of the currents, the flitting of fish through my branches, the slow cycle of the filtered sunlight. Though rooted in stillness, I am deeply attuned to the ebb and flow of this underwater world.

As I have grown and spread across the submerged ruins, other life has taken hold around me. Anemones bloom in fractal patterns on my surfaces, shagreen rays seeking refuge in the shadowed spaces between my arms. Schools of damselfish flit about, tending to their watery gardens.

I do not think, or move, or yearn, as humans do. I simply am - a living, growing piece of the ancient rhythms that permeate this paradoxical undersea refuge, forever unfurling across the bones of the drowned city as the slow centuries roll past. An organic bridge spanning the ages, blurring boundaries between the works of man and nature, basking in the sublime indifference of deep time.
