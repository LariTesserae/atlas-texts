I am a humble Cave-Dweller, one of the skilled artisans who spend their days chipping away at the living limestone rock to expand and refine our underground city of Glistengut. My sallow, mushroom-hued skin betrays the complete lack of sunlight that filters down to these primordial depths. Yet my bulbous yellow eyes, exquisitely evolved for the ubiquitous blue-green glows of bioluminescence, allow me to see every subtle detail and crack in the stone. 

As I work, my calloused hands are sure and steady, deftly wielding an array of primitive tools - picks, chisels, files and smoothing stones. With each measured strike against the cavern wall, I coax sinuous shapes to emerge from the rock. Organic curves and flowing forms that seem to grow organically from the stone itself. For our people do not merely tunnel - we shape the living geology into structures of surprising grace and uncommon artistry.

The rhythmic tapping of my tools blends with the ambient roar and echoes that reverberate endlessly through Glistengut's vaulted chambers. The splashing of underground rivers and creeks. The bellowing of strange cave beasts powering subterranean machinery. The reverberations of countless industries at work, chipping, chiseling, grinding this realm into an ever-expanding home for our people.

My voice carries an otherworldly resonance when I call out to fellow artisans, the vapor-thick air morphing the sounds into echoes of echoes until they seem to come from deep ancestral memories. We converse in a musical tongue born of this environment, syllables and tones that curl through the stillness in hypnotic rhythms.

Though difficult and often dangerous labor, I take pride in each newly carved surface that will become a家, sturdy shelter for another Cave-Dweller family. It is our sacred duty to not just inhabit, but subjugate this alien realm through our ingenuity and industry. With calloused hands and sure technique passed down over generations, we transform the very geology into an extension of our culture.  

In this way, I play my humble part in the grand, slow-coalescing organism that is Glistengut - not just a city, but an entire living, breathing civilization taking defiant root in the most unlikely of places imaginable. An expression of our refusal to merely survive, but to flourish and expand our culture's footprint through the primordial frontiers of the endless Deep.
