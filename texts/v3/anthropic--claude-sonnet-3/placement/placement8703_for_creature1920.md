You find yourself a denizen of this impossibly alien realm - a sluggish, yet formidable hunter drifting through the murky depths of the womb-sea. Your body is an elongated, muscular tube easily 20 feet in length, tapering to a blunt, tooth-studded maw at one end. Rows of slitted gill-structures line your flanks, filtering precious nutrients from the dense liquid that surrounds you.

Rippling undulations propel you forward as you navigate this cluttered liquid world, your boneless form contorting and flexing with surprising agility. Multiple tentacular appendages sprout from your body at intervals, each tipped with bioluminescent lures that pulse and glow with a hypnotic rhythm. These living angling lights drift before you, their radiance probing the perpetual gloom in search of potential prey.

Your senses are highly adapted to this lightless realm. Banks of electrical receptors line your body, allowing you to detect the bioelectric fields generated by living creatures within a considerable radius. You also possess keen distance reception, using the liquid itself to channel infrasonic pulses that map your surroundings in striking detail.

Despite your great size, you are far from the apex predator in this hellish womb-sea. Larger, more formidable behemoths prowl the depths, their alien intentions opaque to your relatively simple mind. You know only to feed, and spawn in turn to perpetuate your cursed lineage within this perpetual, semi-living ocean.

Your metabolism is sluggish, allowing you to remain stationary for long periods as you lie in ambush amid tangled outcroppings of mutated vegetation. Even in quiescence, your feeding tentacles remain in constant motion, probing the murk with their pulsating angling lights in search of any hapless creatures that stray too close.

When prey is detected, you explode into startling swiftness, your tubular body contracting powerfully to propel you forward in an arcing lunge. At the same time, your tentacles flay outward, the angling lights retracting as a battery of grasping suckers and fanged hooks emerge to snare and immobilize your victim.  

Larger prey is subdued by your circular, tooth-lined maw, capable of distending to engulf astonishing proportions. Once trapped within, muscular contractions work to break down your meal into a digestible slurry amidst gouts of acidic bile. Even armored prey is vulnerable to the grinding, peristaltic force of your gullet.

After gorging yourself, you return to a state of somnolent drift, your sated body growing heavy and sluggish once more as you digest your meal. The inexorable cycles of the womb-sea churn on around you – today's victor, tomorrow's sustenance in this never-ending pageant of primordial struggle and rebirth.
