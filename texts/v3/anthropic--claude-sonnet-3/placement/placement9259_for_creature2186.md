The diverse species you've detailed, from the wise Coral Keepers to the scavenging Mer-creatures and the enigmatic Deep Dwellers, make this place feel so alive and layered with depth.

If I found myself in this realm, I think I would be one of the Mer-Scavengers. I'm drawn to their resourcefulness and ingenuity in adapting the discarded human artifacts into new creations integrated with nature. The idea of using both the aquatic and terrestrial environments appeals to my curiosity and explorer spirit. I can imagine myself gracefully darting through labyrinthine kelp forests and the crumbling urban ruins alike, always on the lookout for useful materials to repurpose and creatively blend into functional yet organic habitats.

Perhaps as a Mer-Scavenger I would seek to unlock some of their ancient secrets, using my innate drive to explore the farthest reaches where even the light fears to tread. Bioluminescent fungi could help guide the way through the inky depths.

Helping maintain the equilibrium by putting discarded human detritus back into the cycle of life and adaptation. It's an endlessly fascinating concept - a true celebration of life's resilience in the face of drastic change. Your marvelous submerged world has me completely transfixed!
