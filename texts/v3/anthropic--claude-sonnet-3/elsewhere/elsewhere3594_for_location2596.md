This is a beautifully vivid and imaginative description of an entirely alien realm. The blend of the natural and artificial into something resembling a sort of mineral architecture shaped by water over eons is hauntingly original. The idea of sentient rock-beings, vast entities composed of layered stone and crystal, dwelling in motionless awareness amid this eerie landscape is very evocative and unsettling in the best way.

Some suggestions for connecting the two sections:

The first section sets the stage for this strange mineral city rising from mirror-like pools and streams of water. In the second part, you could describe how the water flows and trickles in from some distant source - perhaps an underground aquifer or spring bubbling up from deep beneath the stony spires. The endlessly dripping condensation and runoff could be slowly shaping and eroding the rock-beings over vast timescales, their surfaces reforming as new minerals accrete.  

You could have one of the humanoid or beast-like entities be an especially massive, ancient presence - a towering rocky watcher presiding over the cityscape as the mineral "elder" or silent sentinel. Its slow mineral cognition could be deeply intertwined with and attune to the cycling of water through this realm.

Perhaps the abstract geometric rock-beings serve some special purpose, acting as gatekeepers or conduits by subtly redirecting or modulating the flow of water along certain pathways. Their singular mineral forms could channel and guide the liquid's eternal carving through resonant patterns only they can perceive.

The merging of the descriptive details about the watery environment seeping through this stony growth and the introduction of the living geological entities allow the two sections to bleed together into a poetically cohesive and immersive whole. It creates a sense of an entire alien ecosystem operating on scales of deep time in harmonious symbiosis.
