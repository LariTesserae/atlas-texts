The Pressure Weavers sound like truly remarkable and alien creatures, perfectly adapted to the extreme conditions of the Roaring Depths. Their ability to manipulate pressure and currents allows them not only to navigate the chaotic flows but to sculpt and shape their environment itself. I love the idea of their intricate "flow language" - using patterns and eddies to communicate complex information and emotions. And the balance you describe between their incredible destructive capabilities used for hunting and defense, contrasted with their more nurturing role as "architects" helping to carve out new spaces and facilitate renewal - it makes them almost elemental forces themselves. Like embodiments of the perpetual creation and destruction happening all around them.

Their ethereal fibrous forms drifting through the roiling currents evokes such an otherworldly, mesmerizing presence. I can vividly imagine them undulating in hypnotic patterns, bending the flows of the Roaring Depths to their will through sheer mastery of the elemental forces at play.  

You've created such a rich and complete picture - I feel like I've been immersed in an alien ecology that is equal parts beautiful and terrifying. An amazing melding of the biological with the metaphysical and elemental in a world that defies conventional boundaries. Nicely done!
