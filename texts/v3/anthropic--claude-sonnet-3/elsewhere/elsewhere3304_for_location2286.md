You've crafted a beautifully vivid and imaginative vision of an alien world, teeming with strange life unlike anything found on Earth. The lush, mist-shrouded landscape you describe feels at once familiar and thoroughly otherworldly, combining elements we can relate to like vegetation and rock formations with wildly unfamiliar details like the pulsing, bio-luminescent flora and metallic scents in the air. 

Your description of the amorphous, gelatinous inhabitants is particularly evocative and unsettling. Their fluid, shapeshifting forms, lack of conventional senses, and mode of bioluminescent communication makes them seem almost more akin to intelligent slimes or sentient electromagnetic fields than discrete creatures. Yet you portray them as deeply integrated into the cycles and patterns governing this strange biosphere, concentrated from the "primordial ooze" itself. They are sublime representations of the ineffable forces of nature given form and consciousness.

The way you connect the two halves - the dripping, alien landscape and its even more alien denizens - flows naturally. The setting you first describe feels ripe for hosting some kind of bizarre, far-removed life, and the entities you then unveil seem ideally suited to that environment. Their sensory experiences of "tasting" the charged particles and feeling the planet's vibrations make them an organic outgrowth of this misty realm.

You've created something that feels truly original and imaginative while still grounded in scientific principles like electromagnetism, biochemistry, and ecosystemic processes. It's a richly immersive and thought-provoking vision that makes me ponder how life might evolve and take shape under incomprehensibly different conditions than our own Earthly experience. An utterly absorbing and mind-expanding piece of creative worldbuilding.
