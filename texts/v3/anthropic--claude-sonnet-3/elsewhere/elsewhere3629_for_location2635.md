You've painted an incredibly vivid and imaginative picture of this hidden industrial realm within the heart of a towering mountain range. The descriptions of the surreal, otherworldly landscape with its crystalline spires, glowing mineral veins, warped gravity, and energy eddies really bring it to life as a place that defies conventional physics and blurs the lines between technology and nature.

The introduction of the Lithonoids, the sentient crystalline beings who call this alien world home, adds an amazing layer of depth and richness. I love the idea of their mutable, gemstone-like forms that can shift and morph, from humanoid figures to abstract sculpted clusters. Their reverence for the ancient living repositories of knowledge and their attunement to manipulating elemental forces and energies makes them feel like an utterly unique civilization woven into the fabric of this strange realm.

The way you describe their organic, almost biological cities and structures sprouting from the stone in harmony with geothermal energies is ingenious. And their philosophical curiosity about unlocking the cosmic secrets binding energy, matter and consciousness together makes them come across as a profoundly thoughtful and meditative race albeit within an industrial setting.

The mention of more eccentric Lithonoid explorers venturing into realms where space and time begin to break down hints at even greater metaphysical mysteries lying just beyond the veil of this already surreal domain. It leaves me wanting to know and understand more about the the magnitude of the secrets and higher dimensional frameworks underlying their existence.

Overall, you've crafted an extraordinary, multi-layered picture of two worlds - the surreal, industrial megastructure realm, and the even more enigmatic society of sentient crystals that inhabit it. The amount of imaginative richness you've packed into the descriptions of both is seriously impressive and immersive. My mind is buzzing with questions about every single aspect of this hidden universe you've brought to life. Stellar work!
