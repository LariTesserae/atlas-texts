Beyond the dense urban sprawl of Glistengut, the cavern network eventually gives way to a vast, seemingly bottomless chasm known as the Underfall. This gaping maw in the earth plunges into inky blackness, its shadowed depths so absolute that even the Cave-Dwellers' light-adapted eyes cannot perceive what lies below.

From the soaring cliffs at the brink of the Underfall, one can hear the deafening roar of a tremendous subterranean river raging through the abyss. The perpetual thunderous din echoing up from below is so immense it seems to reverberate in the bones. Curling vapors rise in billowing clouds from the darkness, hinting at unimaginable thermals and pressures far beneath the surface.

On the precarious lip of the chasm, a series of perilous rope bridges and cable-descended elevators provide the only access across the gaping void. The cable tethers seem to vanish like gossamer threads into the depthless gloom, ensuring anyone attempting the crossing must muster every courage.

For the Underfall lies at the very limits of the Cave-Dwellers' vast subterranean domain. What lies below is unknown - perhaps an endless plummet into the roiling hostilities at the core of the earth itself. Only the most cunning and hardy92
Delvers dare attempt to plumb those stygian depths on tethered expeditions, and they never linger long in the abyssal blackness before returning with tales of horrific aberrations glimpsed fleetingly in their dim lanterns' glow.

From the relative shallows of Glistengut's cavern network, chain-gangs of Cave-Dwellers slowly excavate deeper into the rock towards the Underfall at a glacial pace, ensuring the structural integrity of the city is never compromised. The steady, endless chipping of their tools is like the ceaseless march of eons as they gradually gnaw their way through the subterranean world.

The greatest triumph of their excavations so far has been the construction of an enormous chain-pulley system that descends from Glistengut's caverns in a terrifying series of linked platforms towards the Underfall's uppermost cliffs. This vertiginous apparatus is used to ferry the hardiest Delvers and their equipment across the chasm's yawning maw to the far side.

Here, an outpost of tunnels has gradually been carved from the rock - a remote foothold camp from which future expansions of the city's boundaries may be launched into the uncharted regions beyond the abyss. Yet many of the sturdiest expedition tunnels are said to abruptly dead-end in shattered rock, as if whatever lies ahead has proven catastrophically resistant to progress. Ominous tapping sounds are also rumored to intermittently echo from the blocked passages - hints of sentient hostility lurking in the virgin depths.

Thus the Underfall remains the present borderline of Glistengut's reach. Beyond its abyssal cliffs lies a realm of utter mystery and potential doom for even the most expert Cave-Dwellers to chart. Yet their ceaseless expansion ever-inward shows no sign of relenting, their stubborn toil suggesting this industrious people may one day breach the veil of that stygian abyss, daring to encroach upon whatever unutterably alien world lies in endless darkness below.
