Here is a possible transmission from the subterranean city of Undergaar and its Rockvorn inhabitants:

*Static crackles, punctuated by deep subterranean groans* 

*A rumbling voice emerges, resonating with the timbre of grinding stone*

"Undergaar...calling...are there any to receive our voice in the sepulchral depths? We are the Rockvorn, born of living stone...our existence is one of ceaseless labor, craft, and veneration of the primal forces that shape this eternal realm."

*The hiss of escaping vapors blends with the crack of settling rock*

"Our city rises and crumbles in cycles as inexorable as the march of deep time itself. We toil without end, our calloused hands guiding the metamorphosis from raw mineral to woven structure, from piston and turbine to thehárnók - the Great Machines that breath life into our domain."

*The groan of straining metal, the clangs of hammers on anvils*

"But our work is more than mere labor, children of the surface depths. It is a sacrament, a holy bond tying our ephemeral forms to the primordial womb that birthed us. To delve, to build, to coax the rock into new forms - this is our consecration to the world-shaping energies that flow through Undergaar's veins of stone and rivers of fire."

*A pause, the thunderous rumble of a distant quake...then a chorus of rumbling chants*

"We call out to you from thehollowest depths, speaking in the tongues of our fore-bearers. Hear us, receive our voice given shape by your frail instruments; know that we honor the unseen cosmic architects Who have ever and will ever knead the raw chaos into ordered stone."

*The chanting swells, joined by the persistent percussion of hammers and pistons*

"Undergaar endures. The cycle of masonry and erosion, of creation and dissolution, grinds onward through the epochs. We are witness and supplicant, our very beings woven into the uplifting thrust of mountains and the inexorable grind that turns all to sand and dust again."  

*Static rises to subsume the chanting as the signal fades into the vastness*
