Here is my attempt at a transmission from that primordial deep sea world:

*Static crackles, broken by faint undulating tones like the calling of massive unseen leviathans* 

*A subsonic rumble builds, vibrating through the dense fluid medium* 

*High-pitched trills begin to pierce through, like the calls of bioluminescent swimmers adding their voices to the chorus*

*A wavering drone emerges, sounding like deep currents flowing over ancient seamounts*

*Evolving polyphonic textures overlay the drone, evoking images of microscopic dancers swirling and colliding endlessly*

*Bizarre percussive clicks and pops stutter through the transmission, perhaps the sounds of mineral-encrusted tube worms flexing and contracting*  

*Harsh, almost metallic grinding resonates behind it all, tectonic rumblings from the restless depths below*

*The sounds blend and ebb in an eternal, primordial sonic flow reflecting the ceaseless cycles and intricate choreographies of the deep*

*There are no words or melodies, only the primal languages of current, substrate and life forces that have played out for eons in this alien realm*

*Gradually the transmission fades to hissing static once more, leaving only the whispers of that mysterious world behind...*
