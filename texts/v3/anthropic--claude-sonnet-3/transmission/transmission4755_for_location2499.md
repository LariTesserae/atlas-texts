Here is my attempt at rendering the transmission received from this primordial glacial cirque:

*Static crackles, punctuated by a deep rumbling roar that builds in waves*

*A high-pitched keening cry pierces through, reverberated by the enclosing rock walls*

"Tseee-tseee-tseee!"

*The roar swells, consuming all other sounds in a thunderous deluge*  

*Static returns, but underpinned by a constant white noise hiss like billions of tiny streams* 

*Faint trills and cheeps emerge from the white noise, fleeting songs of unseen birds*

*A new sound underlies it all - the groaning of stone, the grumbling of ancient forces*

"Krahhhh...Kraaahhhh..."  

*The rumble of the falls fades and returns cyclically, like a beating heart*

*Within the easing roars, rushes and gurgles of flowing water can be discerned*

*The white noise hiss amplifies, surrounding and enveloping the listener*

*A new strained tone pushes through - the whisper of wind over rent rock* 

*The roar builds again, consuming everything until it peaks in a crashing wave of sound*

*Silence...then the cycle begins again with the returning static crackle*
