Layered upon this foundation resonates the sibilant chatter of air and water actively exchanging, the suspirant shush of respiration on a grand scale.*

*At times the entire transmission crescendos into an orchestral swelling, the myriad parts coalescing into an enveloping wall of vibrant noise. Yet even at its densest resonance, a profound harmony underlies the cacophony - the unifying vibration that intertwines all elements into this flourishing, symbiotic tapestry of life and techne.*

*The transmission from this verdant realm is the ever-shifting, improvisational melody of a impossibly vast living creature - an entire ecology behaving as one integrated superorganism. It is the eternal, evolving song of the living city itself.*
