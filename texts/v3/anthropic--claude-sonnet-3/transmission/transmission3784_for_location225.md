"...we'veFound surprisingly diverse flora and fauna existing across multiple trophic levels in this 'hyrdolithic' ecosystem. From lithobiontic crust communities to cryptobiotic soil factories...even desert-adapted flora with root systems tapping deep moisture reserves..."

"The real enigma is the apparent settlements - artificial constructs blended seamlessly into the geology. Energy signatures are faint but unmistakable... cyclic... almost like they're sync'd with...with..."

*The voice cuts off abruptly as the rumbling reaches a crescendo - then fades to subtle static*

*A new sound emerges - a low droning reverberation, rising and falling in indrawn breaths. Like the landscape itself is inhaling, exhaling... Slowly, imperceptibly, other tones join in quavering harmony, weaving, swelling, subsiding in endless resonant cycles*
