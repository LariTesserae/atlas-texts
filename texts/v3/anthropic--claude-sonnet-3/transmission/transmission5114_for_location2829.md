Here is my attempt at conveying the transmission from this surreal mountainous realm inhabited by the Mountain Keepers and Wildkin:

*Static crackles, then fades to the sound of wind whipping through rocky crevices and canyons. The rush of waterfalls provides a bass thrum beneath ethereal whistles and groans as gusts sweep over the jagged peaks.*

Voices, ancient and creaked like old growth rising from the earth's bones:
"Harken to the mountain's breath...she inhales aeons with each gust."
"Feel the rhythm 'neath your feet...the heartbeat of stone made flesh."  

*Distant machinery clanks and hums, merging with the natural symphonies. Bestial calls and shrieks achoing across the valleys.*

A chorus of growls and yawns, the speech of beasts:
"We who dream in the tongue of claw and fang..."
"Shaped from endless nowings, we wander..."  

*Eerie overtones reverberate, vibrating in harmony with the world's sundering geology.*  

Ethereal voices riding earthquake whispers:
"Respect our unyielding grasp, interlopers..."
"We crush and grind aeons underfoot..."

*The steady drumbeat of hammers on anvils, chants and songs of toiling Mountain Keepers braided with the primal chantings.*

Plaintive melodies carried on plumes of forge-smoke:
"Borne of the bones of the world yet unbroken..."
"Our roots go deepest of any still living..."
"Our link to the Firstcomers remains while stone stands fast."
"Through our vigil, the balance is kept."
