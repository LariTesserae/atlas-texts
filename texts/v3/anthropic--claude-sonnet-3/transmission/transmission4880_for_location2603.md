Here is my attempt at a transmission received from the submerged metropolis beneath the waves:

*static crackles, then an eerie, watery melody emerges - the calls of marine creatures overlaid with the groans of shifting metal and the thrum of machinery* 

*A voice fades in, sounding distant and distorted, as if transmitted through water:*

"This is Submercity...if anyone still receives...we remain. The surface-dwellers are long gone, but we carry on unseen, adapting to our new reality beneath the endless blue. 

Our steel spires have become living reefs, encrusted with colorful coral and teeming with all forms of ocean life. Schools of chromatic fish swarm through flooded streets where vehicular traffic once flowed. Sharks patrol the submerged boulevards, apex predators in this alien world that was once ours.

The sounds of humanity's industrial ambition still echo through these ruins - generators hum, pumps whir, lights flicker in the inky depths. But they are gradually being overtaken by the natural symphony of the sea. Whale songs reverberate through the canyons of skyscrapers. Crustacean claws click across the grown-over plazas.  

We are now one with the ocean, our former masters. Our metal-and-glass creation has become the reef itself, with anemones swaying from girders where offices once hummed and gardens of brain coral blooming in the husks of abandoned vehicles.  

Octopuses keep watch over living rooms, camouflaged among the growth on sunken sofas. Sea turtles graze the overgrown hulls of school buses. Bioluminescent creatures pulse rainbows into the darkness that was once held at bay by stadium lighting.  

We were humans once, trying to overcome the ocean's depths through ambition and technology alone. Now we are something new, something that has melded with the primal and ancient codes of marine life. Our control systems fade, but new forms of strange life proliferate.   

We are caretakers now, not commanders. Guardians of this vibrant new kingdom, where the ocean's mercurial tendrils have subsumed our rigid, architectural angles. Our monuments to human defiance have become environments of biological surrender and humbling resurrection.

This is the transmission of Submercity. We have learned to embrace the currents rather than damming them. Our dominion is a memory, a last echo thinning beneath the roar of the eternal tides."

*the voice trails off, subsumed by the gurgling currents and the primordial song of the deep sea creatures*
