Here is one potential transmission received from this vast aquatic realm:

*static crackle* 

*a low rumbling thrum, like the churning of endless currents*

*distorted echoes, multiplying and fading, as if calling through vast underwater canyons*

Voices...rising...undulating...

"We are the flow...the ceaseless tide..."

"Shapers of the depths...guardians of the abyss..."  

*a thunderous booming, like leviathan calls through the dark waters*

"Our being...is becoming...an endless unfolding..." 

*ethereal whalesong drifting in, haunting and beautiful*

"Within us...the dreams of the ancients..." 

*the crashing of massive waves, a roaring tumult*

"We are...the primordial...the life-currents..."

*a sustained, keening tone that buzzes in the ears*

"Existence...surges...through our veins..."

"Creation...and entropy...intertwined..."

*more resonant thrumming, like the beating of a vast heart*  

"Flow...with us...child of the shallows..."

"Know...the rapture...of the infinite...tides..."

*the transmission devolves into atmospheric roars and groans, as if the ocean itself were breathing*

*faint, alien melodies play through the static haze, songs without words or end*

*eventually, only the eternal rushing currents remain*
