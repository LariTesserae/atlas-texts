~~ Voices of the Merfolk blend in haunting song ~~  
Beckoning...calling...an ancient tongue  
Binding currents to their harmonic throng

Ancient machineries grinding again

The ocean's rhythms resounding, refrains

~~ Shoalsingers pulsing in shimmering clouds ~~
Currents transcribing their triumphs and shrouds

Nature's historians, voiceless yet loud

Eldritch illuminations through blackest domains  
The eternal hunters' inexorable strains

Tinkerers jury-rigging archaic rhymes
Harmonic melding of the deep's oldest times
