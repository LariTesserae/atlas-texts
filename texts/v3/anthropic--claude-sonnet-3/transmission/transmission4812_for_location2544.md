*static crackles, then fades to rhythmic trills, croaks, and the gurgling of water*

*A low, pulsing thrum emerges, like the beating of a great unseen heart*

*Scuttling, scratching, skittering sounds weave through the liquid chorus* 

*Strange, ethereal tones begin to swell, reminiscent of echoing whale song*

*The pulsing grows more insistent, layered with vibrating drones and sub-audible thrums*

*Amplified closeup sounds of liquids dripping, mushrooms unfurling, life forces stirring*

*A dense swirl of layered forest ambience surrounds and envelops the listener* 

*Rhythms within rhythms, overlapping cycles blending the organic and synthetic*

*An upwelling of portentous low-end vibrations, like continents exhaling*

*The transmission peaks in bioluminescent crescendos, then dissipates to ripples and echoes*

*As it fades, the first trills and croaks return, eternal sentries of this rainforest realm*
