The whisperings coalesce into melodic refrains, some deep and rich like the groaning of ancient trees, others light and airy like new foliage unfurling in the spring. Fragments of an ageless, primal song begin to take shape.

..."We are the sap that flows through every vein, the verdant life-blood pulsing, waxing, waning..."

..."Roots plunging deep, drinking from the wellspring, drinking the sun's fiery essence..."  

..."Branches arching high, offering bough and leaf as airy abode for winged kin..."

Then, a lone voice calls out, ancient and resonant:

"We are the Emerald Grove. We are the deathless verdance embracing the stonebare heights..."

"...We are."
