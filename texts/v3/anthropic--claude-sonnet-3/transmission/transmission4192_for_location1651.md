Here is a transmission received from that lush, hidden wetland oasis:

*static crackles, then gives way to the gentle trickle of water flowing over rocks and the deep thrum of millions of insect wings*

Drip...drip...drip...the patter of condensation dropping from broad leaves. A plop as something breaks the surface of one of the shallow pools. 

The background thrum rises and falls like the inhale and exhale of the ecosystem's breath. Closer now, the rapid staccato of minuscule feet scurries over vegetation.  

Suddenly, a raucous squawking cry pierces the mix - the alarm call of some unseen wader, quickly joined by others of its kind. The frenzy of wings and splashing water slowly fades.

In the near distance, the rhythmic hollow knocking - likely a woodpecker tapping at rotted wood. Underneath,  a constant, soothing white noise....muffled, as if the entire environment was gently blanketed. 

The gurgle and trickling flow grows louder for a moment as a gentle breeze stirs the plants and disturbs the water's tranquil surface. Unseen frogs add their throaty chirps to the chorus.  

Gradually, the static strengthens again, crackles of interference from some outside force disrupting the seamless integration of sounds. Until finally it overtakes the transmission, leaving only the memory of that living, teeming soundscape behind.
