Here is my attempt at capturing the transmission received from this wondrous subterranean realm:

*static crackles, distant dripping echoes* 

*a low thrum builds, pulsing like a heartbeat*

*KRRRRSSHHHHHHH* 

*the roar of shifting rock reverberates, deep and thunderous*

*a descant of delicate chiming tones, like crystalline wind chimes, plays faintly underneath*

*the droning thrum persists, joined now by a chorus of trilling, otherworldly calls* 

*clicks and screeches, a complex symphonic language emerges from the cacophony*

*KRRUNNNCCCHHH*  

*the sound of countless tons of stone grinding against itself overpowers all else for a moment*

*static...silence...*

*then a return of the primal drumbeat, resonating through the bedrock*  

*ponderous, ancient, the tempo slows over millennia as if the Earth itself is inhaling*

*high keening tones swell, a radiant hum of bioluminescent life*

*the dripping, now a steady cadence, metronomic, joins the primordial song*

*KKKRRRRRAKKKKKOWWWWW*

*a seismic groan, felt as much as heard, signals the grinding inevitability of geologic forces*  

*the symphony slowly crescendos, malleable matter's ceaseless symphony of creation and destruction echoing through eons...*
