In the depths of a vast, submerged realm, a dense and primordial world unfolds. The very concept of "water" as we know it dissolves, replaced by a viscous, all-encompassing medium that envelops and permeates everything. This liquid environment is not merely a surrounding, but an integral part of the ecosystem itself.

The terrain here is not defined by solid ground, but by undulating currents and ever-shifting fluid dynamics. Vast, amorphous structures loom in the distance, their forms constantly reshaped by the relentless flow of the surrounding medium. These structures, though seemingly solid, are in fact composed of the same liquid substance, their density and composition manipulated by the unseen forces that govern this strange realm.

Sparse vegetation clings to these fluid formations, their forms twisted and adapted to the constant motion and pressure of the environment. These hardy lifeforms have evolved intricate methods of anchoring themselves, extracting nutrients, and even harnessing the energy of the currents themselves. Their colors and textures are muted, blending seamlessly with the surrounding hues of the dense liquid.

The temperature in this realm is cool, but not uncomfortingly so. The liquid medium itself seems to moderate extremes, maintaining a consistent, temperate environment throughout. Despite this, the sensations of warmth and cold are distorted, as heat transfer and convection operate differently in this fluid world.

The elevation, or depth, of this place is difficult to gauge. The liquid medium extends in all directions, seemingly without end, and the amorphous structures offer no clear reference points. One could be at the deepest depths or merely skimming the surface – the distinction is irrelevant in this all-encompassing environment.

Erosion, as we understand it, has no meaning here. The very concept of solid matter being worn away by external forces is foreign. Instead, this realm is in a constant state of emergence, with new forms and structures continually coalescing from the fluid medium, only to be reshaped and reabsorbed in an endless cycle of creation and dissolution.

The scale of this place is vast and open, extending far beyond the immediate surroundings. It is a regional, perhaps even global, expanse, with no discernible boundaries or edges. The structures and formations stretch out as far as the eye can perceive, blending seamlessly into the horizon.

Despite the open expanse, the density of this realm is staggering. The liquid medium itself is dense, almost tangible, and the structures and lifeforms within it are packed together in a near-touching array. It is a layered, saturated environment, where every cubic meter is occupied by countless presences.

Human Activity

This site is home to Aquarians, inbult beings adapted to their surroundings. Proto-urban settlements provide shelter, and the aquarians have adapted hand tools and materials found in their environment for basic needs and crafts. Fauna is ubiquitous - from microscopic to massive, exotic fauna continually swarms this place, coexisting with the aquarians in balanced ways they do not fully understand.

The human footprint here is minimal - occasional signs of visitation or exploration by surface-dwellers from some outside place beyond this strange sea. But humanity's presence is transient, temporary at best. An ancient, incomprehensible system governs this realm, and even advanced tech struggles to make sense of its bizarre dynamics, matter phases, and ecologies.

A sense of unreality permeates everything. The basic rules we know do not apply - gravity, thermodynamics, even the flow of time seem subtly warped here. Sound itself behaves strangely, channeling through the dense fluid in unpredictable patterns. Loud cacophonies of mystery fauna provide a constant roar occasionally punctuated by periods of unsettling quiet.

This place feels like reality itself is on the verge of unraveling. Just below the surface, the fundamental laws that bind our universe together seem to fray and dissolve into maddeningly alien principles. Yet life and structure persist in their own unique equilibrium - unchanging, yet always in flux. An ancient, eternal, elemental world unto itself.
