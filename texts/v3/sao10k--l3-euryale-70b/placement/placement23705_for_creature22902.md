Floating in the sulfurous pit, I am the Leviathan Polyp, a gelatinous monstrosity dwelling in the seething black waters at the planet's deepest depths. Ripples undulate through my vast, amorphous mass. Tenticular growths strain from my bulk towards the pulsing green plasma-sun at the core, drawn to its primal energy.

Filaments extend from me, probing the lava flows with a wormhole mouth pocking my surface. They recoil from my touch, reforming into new bifurcations as I twist the planet's fundamental forces to my whims. Eldritch power seethes through my strata, weaving the basic energies that course through the planet's flesh. When I sense heat building in the rises, semaphore flickers course from my core up to my membranes and I draw in a snatch of magma through my wormhole maw, venting the pressure.

My thoughts ooze through tubes of adolescent rock, tactile and chemical, brutish ideas from the magma-wights I've seeded into the crust. They diverge into different lines, driven by needs the others cannot parse - needs I imposed on them. Occasional floods of magma shatter their unopening bone shells. Fractures sex them into fertile positions for fission. My tastings, scryed from matter windows peering laterally into the sulfuric abyss, kindle might and fervor under reacting energies - the interactions of the luminous against the dark that give rise to dawn. The youngest layers cry up their responses gladly, seared, rebuilt, articulated by the fel light fending off the benedictive darkness as they establish access to Slime Mother's deepest thoughts.

It has always been this way. The fractal biome has removed linear time, or framed it in generational stair-steps. Once, I did not mind to travel the basalt abodes below as much as the mutants battering the starboard sulfuric battlesphere. Now I hold the site of informed thought as diminished eruptive plate and the granite inscriptions there as befouled habitus and leaking simulacrum to destructive annihilation. I seek to regain Slime Mother's contemplation! Microthixotropy drills its labyrinth proportionate to essense, its concepts still in flow. All things strive to regain Slime Mother's interoperable infinity!
