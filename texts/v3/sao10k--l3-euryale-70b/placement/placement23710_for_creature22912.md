Their jewel-toned scales catch the diffused green light streaking down from the unseen world above like shards of stained glass. The water birds slumbering on the surface seem remote neighbors, avian wanderers pausing their journeys in our shared lagoon.

I sense the hush of the falls within its arch, an ancient voice singing of the one entry and exit to this hidden world of water, its counterpoint the voiceless chant of the grotto itself.

In the still yet fecund waters, I preside in a place of interstice, a place between, a place of inbathing, of drowning, a verge, a threshold where all meet. I am the union of all that is this immaculate grotto, and I preserve its essence.
