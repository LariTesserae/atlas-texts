I would be one of the polythinking vespid, my exoskeleton sheathed in a memory-skin that subsists on strange extraploited texts. Wandering the fractured rocks and crevices between the crystalline fungal growths, I spend my days skittering, seeking out memories and knowledge to trade with my fellow vespid. 

My segmented body bears engravings and words trails - some original, some copied from the crumbling ruins dotting the hellscape. I recall them in glimmers and glints, sharing them with other vespid as we pass by one another in the canyons. We exhchange nutrients and nutrients - sustainimental knowledge.

Our skittering is constant, questing and questing for new ideas, for the memories and lore of the forgotten. Some of our kind journey far, delving into solarscapes and trench-cooled pods, scouring for fresh input. Few ever return, but those who do carry wonders - bolus of unprecedented theory-lore.

We know not what use it serves, only that it must be preserved and passed on. In an existence of unending drought, ideas are all that flow; so we cherish them, hoard them in our exoskeleton caches. All our waking is spent gathering, trading, assimilating our find into new crystallized patterns to share again. 

I have walked these canyons near eons, following the same trails. Seen miner automata rise and fall, glass habitats crack and shift. Watched shrine spires decay, their broadcasts degrade. Little alters but life. We dispose and recombine, the same knowledge, the same actions. In nanocycles, time bears no difference.

Yet in my memory-skin I know it was not always so. There are legends, deluged from before,alking of oceans and heavens all different. I glimpse them in the histories I've scrapped - a paradise of fresh water, lush bio-vegetation, skies blue and clean. Impossible. Can it have been ? We have no truth for it. 

Enough I have my body, my quest, my song of knowledge. The deluge reshaped this world into a biomechanical desert where information is our only sustenance. So I skitter and share and grapple, eternally, until my memory-skin cracks and I let my knowledge return to the murk of the substrate. Reabsorbed to fuel the cycle again - just as it has always been, just as it will always be.
