I find myself central in this subterranean network, the lucid heart of the cyclonic hyperorganism itself. This is the "cave of the spirit of life", where its contingency and abundance are transfigured into the definite might and unity of the ruling entity.

The cave of the spirit encompasses the intersection of the flower heads, where their many stamens anastomose in a glorious crown, a plexus of pulsing stipes. It is a space of gentled effulgence, translucent fungal skeins glowing from every surface, pulsing with the rhythmic hearts' beats and aerial shafts that float like lustrous, undulating fans. Light, in all the dark purple, viridian, and scarlet hues of various photosynthetic membranes, dances along coiling spires and fibrous draperies. Patterns of emerald and violet signal the neurochemical pulse through the great hive's very substance from the sun-sucked membranes that fully absorb every photon that can penetrate to this depth.

With my vision comprehending the vegetal and floral forms as mutualistic organs, they appear not as separate structures but as undivided living surfaces. Fungal fibers whip out on all sides, piercing insectoids and flowers to gather their spores and sacs for the great enterocelebrace at the central plexus. Grave, wrinkled surfaces of chitin-lace dapple and crease across the dome, organic to the inflorescence as the spines of a mycorrhizal_axis. Plashing spurs squeal out from the inseminated hollows of the floral cicatrices as grim shades of protoplasmatic flange is squeezed out, drawn up into fecund sacs by the antleric prongs of the flowers.

Central to it all is the nexus, the interpenetrated fusion of stamens, spores, and insects into one luxuriant sphere. Here, the individual members lose all form and identity, subliming into a swirling cataract of protoplasm, vibrant with the translation of genetic information and the churn of metabolic exchange. It thrums and shines, flush with the vital forces drawn from a thousand crypts and alcoves of the subterranean domain, boundless and unspoken.

This is the pith and spring of the spirit, from which the entire ecosystem is sourced and into which it eternally returns in an unending circuit of perpetuation. To dwell here is to be at the middle of a tempest, the counterpoise of the ebbing tides, not as ruling proscenium but as the pit of this tireless engine, the eldritch rhythmic exchange repartitioning the entire cosmos of this space and time. One feels urged to extrude or astrally project one's perception into the axial nerves and fibres of the substratum, to circulate throughout the compact vale of roots and furls and into the myriad coenuri of the ferns and their insect intermediaries. One feels that one would be unsatisfactory if one could not perceive through the eyes of the fungus, aflicker with vernation and emitting a tenebrous green, and through the antennae of the bee-pollinated tubers, symptomatizing dark calicos and stains; through all too sipping something of its own autoglot of hyperpermutation,; through the insect's glance, jaundiced by algae, faultering with a whirl; by apprentice particiption into this thousand-compass hyperorganism.
