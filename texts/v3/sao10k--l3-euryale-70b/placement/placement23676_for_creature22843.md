Refracted light glints off a billion facets of mineral in the vertical planes of eviscerated chthonic towers, paralyzing the collected oxygen to technology. With no greenery, there is no oxygen produced, no carbon cycle, no driving force of life. Perhaps the only organisms that could survive among the bleached strata and shattered pillars are microorganisms in the trapped pothole pools, thermophillic or halophilic extremophiles able to live in the almost impedinable conditions.

Inert since eons, for lack of water to sculpt and weather them, the bejeweled towers are exempt from decay, geologic time suspended in the perpetual stillness. Their striations and stratifications speak to cycles of sedimentation underlying tectonic violence.
