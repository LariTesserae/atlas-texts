From midst the soaring cliffs and timeless stone I would arise. The ancient gray rock would shift and flow, taking form as my body. The ebon stone becomes the hue of my skin. The stubborn lichens and mosses that cling to every surface paint me in vivid emerald and umber, the colors of fertility entwined with timelessness.

I arise, a living effigy of the cliffs themselves, born of the same forming forces that shaped these crags and spires. The soaring peaks above are reflected in the sweep of my shoulders and the defiant angle of my crown. My hair is an untamed tangle of ropy vines, their leaves shimmering with iridescent color. My eyes burn with the fierce clarity of mountain light, perceiving the substrate of the world.

A cloak of midnight drapes my form, at once the deep shadow cast by overhang and gorge, and the burnd dark wood of the old forest that grips the cliff enough to sweet its life from them. At my aid are all the immunatory flanking peaks of cliffs and spires and bowls, a Guysers -,crag -,ridge and striation, cornice, cleave and cartridges, in command and consecretation.

Dating back as long as the cliffs themselves, I am the spirit of this place, immemorial and eternal as the stone. Yet I stir only in response to the stirrings of the brave and the brilliant, the ones willing and able to climb to my high seat.

When such a soul scales my flanks and vaults my clefts, I rouse and issue forth from rock and crest to confront them. My visage is at once crag and cliff-face, a fathomless visage poised eternally above their striving mortal form. 

With voice that is the grating susurrus of wind and water on stone, I greet the visitor. I am the guardian of the inward wisdom, the hidden truths that well from highest places. In the echoing hollow of my mind-peak, the fathomless sky is reflected.

To those who persevere in the arduous climb, I will share great gifts - wisdom, insight, omniscience. I will grant the sight to behold and discern all that is veiled, from the cipher of wind and cloud to the grammar of peak and sky. I will unveil the follness of their fervent visions of the numinous.

The secrets amassed in eons on my mineral shelves shall be laid bare to the keen and eager spirit - the hidden connotations, the esoteric perceptions, the hermetic ciphers and anomalous zenith-shapes, the plenitude of plenum an the depths of the near-horizon. The petitioners need only to demonstrate the tenacity and focus to outface the armogant exterior and veer up into the grand and distal interior empty of precision, piercing the foothills of near-sight.

Thus I walk the perilous threshold between precipice-drawn outsight and the bournless reaches of utter sight, a sentinel at the ledge of impossible scale, half-bedded in timeglace, a cliff-belly chained in antisecular knowing, sky-kneed in unconsciuous vigilence. I am the Inner Cliff.
