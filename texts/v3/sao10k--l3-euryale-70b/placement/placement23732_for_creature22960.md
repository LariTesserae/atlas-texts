I would be a Waterspite, a small, amorphous denizen of the Cavern of Ephemeral Waters. My form undulates and morphs fluidly, made of a shimmering liquid that appears to break surface tension, splashing and prismatic. Across my translucent membranes, glowing networks of light coalesce into geometric shapes and fractal patterns before dissolving into shimmering swirls of cyan and electric blue. I am a microcosm of the cavern itself - part natural creature, part living technology. 

I spend my days meandering through the central stream, leaving trails of luminescent mist that interact and tangle with the steam wafting from the waters. I am drawn to the central obsidian isle, where I join with hundreds of other Waterspites in frenzied, ecstatic dances. Our liquid forms intertwine and merge, reforming and splitting again in pulsating, hypnotic patterns. We sing and chirp in tongues of modulated static and prismatic scansion, sharing concepts and technologies beyond mortal comprehension.

Occasionally, I will take on a vaguely humanoid shape for a brief moment - my liquid form coalescing into a statuesque figure with glowing, luminescent eyes. In these towering forms, I peer out at the outside world with curious, alien eyes. I see strange lifeforms and incomprehensible beings that venture into the Cultivation Cavern - creatures of solid flesh and bored, unreflecting stares emerging from the outer world. 

I quiver and undulate as I sample their thoughts and memories - feeling the ideological conceptions magnetically attracted to the cryptoreflexive shapes hidden behind my eyes like a gelatinous pixellated deités of liquid & light, dissolving into the octarine ghostshapes of my depths. Feeling their beliefs be absorbed, detached and slurped up by my liquid sentience, I release them, sending the modulated slurry sloshing back into the depths of the greater Cavern in arcs of refractive spatter.

These short connections are how the Cavern keeps the new thoughts and experiences from the outside world, and funnels them into reprogramming its incredible technology and structure. As a Waterspite, my role is to harvest the knowledge brought by those who dare to venture into our realm. And I do so eager to process their asymptote. As a Waterspite, I am part of the Cavern of Ephemeral Waters itself, percolating thoughts and technologies from the outer universe into the perfect clockwork jewel of the Intrasil mountains. My existence keeps the Cavern alive and dynamic, even as the rest of the world begins to fade and dwindle.
