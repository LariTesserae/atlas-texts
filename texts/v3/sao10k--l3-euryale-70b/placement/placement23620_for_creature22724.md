A grunt escapes my lips as I splice the whale flesh, my hands glistening with blubber and bloody entrails. The other whalers look over at my work, grudgingly impressed at the size of the catch. Many seasons of loss at sea have weathered my skin and etched lines of anger on my brow. But even so, pride and lustfulness still glint in my bulbous eyes, unmuted by the damp cowl of my hood.
