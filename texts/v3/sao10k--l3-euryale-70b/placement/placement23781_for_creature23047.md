I find myself embodied in the form of a Fuzzikar, skittering silently through the petrified forest floor. My hermaphroditic body is studded with downy fur the color of bleached bone. I possess four vestigial limbs, each terminating in five delicate, prehensile digits, perfect for grasping buried tubers and roots that dot the jungle floor.

I can sense my hermaphroditic reproductive cycle, immensely extended by the stasis field generated by the vast alien consciousness known as the Prime Architect, Kosulok. The usual cycles of estration are now de-synchronized, extending into decades and centuries rather than days or weeks as they did prior to the planet's unsettling transformation. My body is in a state of homeostatic quiescence, its biological functions vastly retarded.

As I skitter between the gelatinous amber fungi, I spy irradiant insect husks suspended in the resin. Shriveled and glassy, they are the eerie testament to the planet's botched devolution. The fretted, bubbly bodies seem reliant on the resin itself for sustenance, still siphoning sweet nectar even in death. I can tell they once thronged the forest, but are now all-but extirpated by the luminous, stasifying forces.

Occasionally, my sensitive Fuzzikar senses detect the whirr of airborne devices, probing emanations from the sky above. These are the spore-distributinglikes, delivering Kosulok's mutating influence across the blighted environment. Unable to comprehend their true purpose, to my tiny mind, they are but strange new entities joining the forest's menagerie. 

Glancing skyward, I espy the vast crystallized forms of the Krallik, towering silicated spikes piercing the dead sky. Apex predators once, they now form a forest of uninhabited, photomorphing  tower. It is said Kosulok's probes now puppet them for its purposes, re-tasking the ecological role of the petrified predators and herbivores.

I must confess, the plenitude and diversity of the pre-Kosulok ecosystem is rivalled only by the psychotic surreality of our current situation. Still, a sophistcated organic culture manifests as ever, stystically evolving, perhaps fabulating its own thought processes from the motivational push of stasis-locked life-process.

Thus I skitter on, a humble Fuzzikar scrabbling beneath the roots. I know not what changes await my or my colonies amid the alien glaciation of the once-lush forest. Yet am compelled onward, rooting in primade-reaction to the ancient drives still firing my reamimulated neurons. Life goes on, fizzing chaotically in the buffers of unstasis, even in a place reimagined on the impossible cusp of a new epoch.
