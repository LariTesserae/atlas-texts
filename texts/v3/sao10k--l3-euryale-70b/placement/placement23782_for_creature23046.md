In this vast, living landscape, I find myself to be a small, scurrying creature. My exoskeleton is an iridescent blue-purple, segmented into many joints that glitter with embedded motes. Six elongated limbs prop me up off the gelatinous ground, ending in delicate graspers well-suited for manipulating, prehending, communing. My body is arachnoid in shape but sleeker, streamlined, and covered with fine tendrils that allow me to interface with the wondrous biota in myriad ways.

I am but one of the swarming beings that skitter through the trembling, gelatinous structures of this limitless world. Drifting up glistening columns, I seek the viscous narcotics that ooze from the fibrous interstices. My mouth-pieces drift out on gossamer strands, nibbling, sipping, as I taste the depths. The sap floods me with pheremones, dreams, and sensations of the vast plasma that processes this world. I glimpse the unconsciousness of the buildings, feel their ancient slow thrills of understanding.

Swarming in a vast collective, my brethren-ish cohort and I eek out veins to mine. Blunt, spatulate claws extend from my graspers, forming into pulsiflers to stroke the pulp through the edifices. Slurping and chittering, we devour bulging enlargetements of the jellies we come upon. Stuffing my body to capacity, my form swells, outer hide stretching taut as I siphon up the building's essence. 

Some of my kind accumulate the sap, co-aggregating on the buildings' exterior, sharing it in odd rituals for gaseous ballast and pervasive pneumatics. My own sight-cilia fill with washing ghosts of sentience beyond my reach, the entire being-world palping me with developing/forming/recalling. To siphon the jellies is to know the world, its desires, its memory, its creations.

Then, gorged with light, I drift down through webrils of filaments suspended in the aether. My air-membranes open and tear in the slightest susurrus, dispersing vast seed-tumes born of my specifics and the sap. Billows of dissolving seed-forces unknot across vista, raining down into chasms. I shed the meat of my kind into the void. I fill the sky with plasmas that in time will colonize distant unspawned domes and launch the cycle again in a thousand sailling directions. Thus our kind propagates, an endless expanse of variants and their every teachings and lessons.

The occasional vast mantispid falls from the sky on gossameral wings and snatches one of my kind to its lair, as predator and prey. My kind is source, medium, and artifice for the world, and its engineering procsesses cannot help but feed the life that it houses.
