As I stand at the edge of the Grand Bazaar, I feel a sense of overwhelm wash over me, from the cacophony of sights, sounds and smells to the sheer scale and history of the place. The fact that the city clings to the cliffs and fills the canyons wears it through and through, an indelible mark of their symbiotic relationship. And yet, there's an undeniable sense of vibrancy and chaos to the streets before me, even as the ancient geology that once carved out this place recedes into the background.

I find myself drawn to the adobe buildings, to the way they seem to slump and sprawl organically, their fractal layers of materials a testament to organic growth. There's a sense of some hidden, telling pattern to the city's layout, like veins or ant tunnels, but it recedes into the mass of things as soon as I try to elucidate it. The front-facing elevations of brighter tile and banners seem a veneer, masking the dense hum of hidden spaces within., secret tunnels and vaulted stairwells. Part of me is drawn to explore them, to worm my way into the city's unlit depths and see what's nestled in the shadows, but it's the sibilant cries of the merchants that urges me forward, to engage with the bazaar itself.

I'm struck by the weird frisson of the blue-skinned, spaded-eared folk even as I find myself shouldering past humans in desert robes, the jostling press of people speaking tongues necessarily and alien. The heat of their bodies and the sheduaing seasons feel oddly...right, like I've come home to a place I've never known. There's a cosseting warmth about the crowded passageways, for all that I feel luridly alert, trying to track the hundreds of individual souls doing their business on the streets before me.

But then comes the flames, the shambling horde, the tearing shriek of metal and the hellish glare. And there's a sudden reminder of how insular this bazaar is, of what dangers there might be beyond the scarp of city walls. The multi-layered city, so entirely itself, feels like it might unravel at any moment to let chaos in, even as it's protected from it. As the gun emplacement related and the wall of flame recedes, it's impossible not to wonder - just what's out there in the scorched regions beyond the canyon walls, and how long this place can hold out?
