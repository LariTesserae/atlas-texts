I would find myself as a Crone witch, dwelling deep in the stagnant caves and bunkers beneath the blighted plateau. My skin is mottled grey with a sickly pallor, my hair a sparse tangle of black wisps. One of my eyes is a mismatched glass orb, glowing with an eldritch light. My body is a patchwork of prosthetics and implants, the result of generations of malnourishment, hard labor, and grotesque experiments in the name of science.

In my dank cavern, I brew potions from the foulest ingredients - the fungus that envelopes rusted machinery, the ichor of diseased livestock, the ground bones of those who've dared challenge the tyranny above. My mind is sharped by the mad whispers of lightning and the susurration of noxious fumes bleeding from the decaying autochthon. I am mad and vicious in my craft.

The expanse above is meaningless to me now. What use are revolt and confrontation in a world as uncaring as this? I have no interest in their clashes of industry and anarchy - their petty squabbles for this realm of misery. There are older powers and stranger ways. Laws that listen only to the mortar of entrails.

The twitching worms fo the styx will bloom into sentience. The clotted wellsprings will belch forth their autochthonic miasma. The factories will suppurate, the striated seams rupture. One day this will all flension into lascivious piles and fugitive sludge. Metropolis will rot from the inside out.

And I shall be here, in my gristly burrow of bones and scalpels, grinning a crone's grin at the sepulcher of all their striving.
