Deep within the stifling, steaming skinny-canyon oasis, I am a far-seeing, cold-blooded hunter. My scaly hide, a patchwork of forest greens and canyon browns, nearly invisible in the mottled shadows of the tangle of twisted roots, fallen logs and trailing vines. 

With eyes of vertical-slit pupils and a forked tongue flicking, testing the miasmic air, I lurk, snake-like, effortlessly silent between the dripping thickets, ambushing prey before noosing my fangs into their flesh. Carnivorous and opportunistic, I feast on the riotous diversity of this lost valley.

Drinking its murky water, breathing its scented air of moldering earth and languid growth, I am utterly a creature of this place - I was born here, I live here, I hunt here, I will die here. My misshapen skull will be picked clean by the tubeworms.

In this dense, resilient world crowded onto itself, the wholeness is ugly and lush, filthy and fecund. Broadsheets of leaf-mold, burbling smelly water, cloying air. Massive colonies of mimetic insects. All is prolific, and in glut.

And I am the Prince of this misbegotten place. King of the Tangle. Master of Beasts in the Miasma. Minister of Decay. Emperor of the Green Steep Canyon. And I reign here, ever and always, primordial within the prehistorical, until an epoch's time hence.
