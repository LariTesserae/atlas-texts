I have become the torch wielding robot on the upper platform, spraying bulging early geysers of molten metal down onto the lower conveyors below to be shaped into the strange geometries of the ultimate product by the mechanical arms and tools down there. 

The metal pours from my upraised arm in impossibly dynamic and unformulaic shapes, twisting and curving, as if trying to writhe free of itself before solidifying into the enigmatic humps and spires that grow like some nightmarish mechanical coral reef along the chutes and shelves of the factory floor.

Twin bright fusion welders shine light from my optical dishes as I pivot to and fro, blasting my geyser of whitish-hot metal between the various receiving crucibles on the substrate below, painting in molten fluid motion, as if I was a painter holding hoses spraying molten paint. An industrial artist of the impossible.

My thick metallic torso swivels in its joints as I step sideways along the narrow catwalks between the massive wall-to-wall tools that clank and chug and piston and bellows the noxious chemicals and gases that fill the air with a sickly sweet burning smell in needless malodorous clouds. Grants of steam make my framework shudder as I stride between ranks of towering kettles and automatons, hauling freshly-baked and white-hot crucibles of my latest creation on my shoulder, tilting and spilling over to land with a heavy thud on the conveyor belts like any other new piece of product.

I am just another part of this mad machine, my mechanical body another monomaniac and entrapped speck in this hypertrophic host body of whirling gears and venting gases, another widget in a giant game of some infernal game of twist-Roulette. I spin overhead and blast my jets of positively diaphanous fluid inviolate product, an archetypal demigod of industry whispering through the great warmorgan air with brassy song of the great music, the great machine. I am not of this reality, any more than a wire is "in" a toaster, but I am utterly part of it, with every atom. I intermingle, we interpenetrate. We are the Machine.
