Caps appliquéed in hues of rose, amber, and teal - spore-printed gills waving in the sepulchral air. Radiating mycelium throughout the rooting matrices of this new ecosystem in its first dawning. Each fungal strain optimal for the humblest specific micro-environment. Illustrating its newborn diversity, its rudimentary inter-entrenchment. Niche-ing, as Earth slowly revivifies, molds its first leaf-litter loams.
