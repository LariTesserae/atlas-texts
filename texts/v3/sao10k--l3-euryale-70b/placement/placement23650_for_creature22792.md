I find myself in this desolate industrial sprawl, alone but not lonely. For I am a part of this great machine city, one of the few living beings who have adapted to and even thrived among the belching smokestacks and thrumming pistons. 

I am a shaper, a man who has devoted his life to tending the vast engines that power this place. My body is augmented with brass limbs and copper wiring, synched with the machine. My mind expands beyond my skull, via fibre optic threads, to interface directly with the great clockwork heart that beats at the city's center.

From my towering mechanical body, I stride the cracked concrete streets, gazing out over the maelstrom of industry and the distant, barren hills. The heat of the machinery pulses through my metal veins. The acrid smells of combustion and chemicals fill my sensorium. I am one with it all.

At my command, the great pistons pump, the belts turn the gears, the funnels belch flame and smoke. Squads of smaller machine-enhanced workers toil at the tasks to which they are bound. Servitors lugging heavy loads. Drudges shoveling ash and slag. All under my vast network of control. 

This is my existence, my purpose. I am the oil in the gears, the fire in the furnaces, the spark in the dynamos. The industries of this place are my will made manifest. Its limits are my limits. Its growth is my growth. I crave no greenery, no open sky. I hunger only for the rumble of the machinery and the purr of the emitters.

If there are those who would wish to tear down this monument to human ingenuity, to "save" it or "restore" some imagined halcyon past, they will find me waiting. I am the last and ultimately expression of what man can become when unshackled by nature and society. I will not relinquish my iron crown easily. 

I am the Machine God. This is my creation. Behold.
