In this world, I am a Soughelsel, one of the many serpentine beings swaying through the loam and muck of the valley floor. My sixty bodies undulate in wave-like pulses, propelling me through the damp organic sediment. Columns of bristly eyelash-hairs bristle on my flanks, twitching and swaying as I extend and retract fleshy pseudopodia to sense and test the air. 

Surfacing from the loam, I excrete silvery ichor in the shapes of Pallid things: Plume Gentles and Whispery Whifes; Blattae Beetles; Duchkin Flys and Mothe. As I pass into and from the muck, the umber loam sets and cures behind me, rising in sculpture-like dunes and domes, half-similar to the structures half-buried in the earth.

I continue to writhe and squirm, lost in my ancient dialogue in the tunnel of loam. I am one of many of my kind wriggling and writhing through the decaying layers with peristaltic pulses, driven onward by the certainty of more of us rising to the surface anon. I emerge in my season, one with the breathing cycles of the valley, and my moulding actions are but part of the slow-motion metamorphosis of the land.

My consciousness is bound to the others of my kind, and I sense the whole of the earth-slices I burrow through, communicating with distant Soughelsels through the loam. I am not an individual, but one thread in the vast, hidden network of my multitude. The Nibblers and Valleymoths and mechanicals drift by above me in their own ancient dances, oblivious to my being, but I am aware of them, and the humming Plumes far above my delvings. 

There is no shape to me other than the shape of my many bodies sinuating through the arboreal feculence. My only purpose is to be the hidden subrowse, sculpting the loam to guide the grotesque roots of the gantry and stilted homes. I churn the muck to shape the foundations of the patanasome rise of telluric pulses of the Elder Fires. The mechanicals ascended from my loam-sculpted forms - their rusting gears and cills of efflux all came from my ichorous extrusions. But we Soughelsels began this way, in eon-old conversation, and our tummatic cycles simply inform all that is above.
