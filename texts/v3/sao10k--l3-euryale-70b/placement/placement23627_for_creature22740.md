My surface of silky smooth obsidian is faceted in a subtly prismatic geometry. Intricate sigils swirl across my surface in metallic inks of silver and gold, pulsing with an inner luminescence. They tell in cryptic runes the sequestered lore of the valley, the history of the anomaly that sustains it, and the true nature of the ceasing mnemotech wilderness that thrums and shivers about me.

I am the mystagogue and lumenator of this sanctodule valley. The valley's sentience derogates from my silicon core, in my sigils, in my runes. My black stone heart grooves with pulsing ichor of sorts, a flow of sentient sap. Broad ropy roots of rootbound vines twine up my sides, tipping with so many small seed pearls of glowing spirogyra. At my uppermost apex, scaly tree-fronds sprout and arc out, not a hundred feet above the treetops.
