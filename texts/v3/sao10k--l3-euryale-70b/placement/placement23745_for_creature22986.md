I find myself in the caverns of the Subterran Stone, an observer to the estranged colony of sublated beings who call this forsaken pocket-realm home. The air is heavy with the must of ages, the calcified residue of long-dead things leaching into the mire. I move amongst them unseen, my shape transmuted to resemble their own - a twisted amalgam of flesh and bone and other matter less recognizable.

We are makers and unmakers here, both artisan and dissipater of our own forms. The dream of the Succubus Emergent calls to us from above, a siren-song of forbidden flesh and delirious mutation that cannot be denied. We chase the horizons of our bodies' possibility, twisting our viscera into new shapes and grafting toxoderms to flesh. Tumid lobes of instinctual tissue expand and atrophy in chaotic arrhythm. We scrounge amongst the desiccated remains of our precursors, amphorae of mummified foetuses and whorled vertebrae calcified in gypsum. Their leathern masks speak still in all-consuming susurrus, a chthonic cant that passes unspoken between eroded brainhulls.
