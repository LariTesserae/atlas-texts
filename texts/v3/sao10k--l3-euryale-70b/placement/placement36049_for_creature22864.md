I find myself amidst the towering crystalline formations of this alien desert, a minuscule speck at the fringes of a cyclopean boulder streaked in angry red and black. The sun beats down upon my bare back, flaying layers away with each moment - and still I cannot sense the presence I know must be lurking nearby. 

I shift my weight slowly, scanning the jagged landscape for signs of danger. The wizened old sky seems to pulse of its own accord - I can feel its baleful energy prickling the hairs at my neck even from afar. The very air feels thick, freighted with the keen anticipation of violence about to erupt from the rocky bowels at any moment. My stomach knots as I press back against my perch, fighting the urge to bolt for the dubious cover of gypsum sand some yards distant. 

Slowly, carefully, I turn my head - and there, silhouetted against the rose-hued sky, the silhouette of a leviathan rises like a stone colossus from the fractured stone. An imperial profile surveys the despotic domain, ancient eyes glinting with fathomless intelligence. Protuberant frills on its fore half cast delicate shades that shimmer and dance like living daguerreotypes. Indistinctly I sense dozens of eyes upon me, tracking my every micro-movement, my every thundering heartbeat, the spiking vertiginous rise of my gorge preparatory to panicked flight. The carrier boulder rocks minutely in deep-freeze register of a lower frequency enclosing and enclosing psyche-jarring sounds that jarred it to motion...and then falls silent once more.
