To prepare to visit this location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler would need to equip themselves for travel to a dense, humid, temperate-to-tropical rainforest environment, with the additional preparations for exploring a strange fusion of ancient living forest and arboreal industrial city.

Clothing: Wear sturdy, breathable, moisture-wicking clothing that will keep you cool and dry in humid conditions. A long-sleeved shirt, sturdy trousers, and stout but light hiking boots would be advisable. Consider a hat, gloves, and insect netting to protect against the biting and stinging insects that thrive in this ecosystem. Bring a water-resistant outer layer or poncho to protect against the mists and rain. Darker colors will help you blend in. Don't forget warm layers for cooler mornings and evening periods.

Bring a sturdy backpack capable of carrying at least two days' worth of supplies in case of becoming lost. Train to carry it weight comfortably for extended periods over rough terrain. 

Packing:
- Extra undergarments and socks 
- Water bottle or hydration pack (at least 3 liters)
- Field water purification tablets or filter to treat rain water
- High-calorie trail foods, dried fruits and nuts
- First-aid kit with antiseptic wipes, bitter-tasting anti-bacterial ointments, pain relievers, antihistamines, etc. 
- Flashlight or headlamp; extra batteries
- Comprehensive multi-tool of the sort containing a knife, scissors, saw, pliers, bottle opener, etc. 
- Neck or wrist gaiters; mosquito netting 
- Map, compass, astrolabe or GPS navigation device (Note - reception may be poor beneath the canopy)
- Sunscreen; insect repellents (oil of lemon euchalyptus is advised in very humid environments)
- Sleeping bag, well-weathered tent; emergency space blanket and bivvy sack
- Wet wipes; hand sanitizer
- Water-resistant fire starter (matches or lighter are unlikely to work well)
- Collection bag with gloves for urine or solid waste
- Hard-shelled eye protection; earplugs
- Any personal medications
- Knowledge of basic wilderness survival skills, such as shelter-building, finding pure water sources, treating injury or illness in the field, reading animal tracks and identifying edible plants (many 'edible' forest mushrooms are toxic) 
- Familiarity with the basic operation of wooden pulley systems for ascending/descending

Arboreal city:
- A crude snap-together multi-tool of the sort containing needle-nose pliers, wirecutter, scraper, wrench, everything clasp-style, etc.
- Basic lamp oil and wick; small bellows; white gasoline apparently used as lamp fuel
- Ironmongery of the sort to provoke the curiosity of the ornamental ironworkers low in the canopy (feathered gala helms, gourd-based reinforcement of limb, etc)
