To prepare for visiting this primordial landscape, a multiverse traveler would need to prepare extensively for the extreme and alien environment. Let us start with the most pressing concerns:

Immense heat and humidity. The traveler would need an extreme-environment suit capable of withstanding incredible temperatures and maintaining a safe internal atmosphere. The suit would require an onboard climate control system, powerful air recycling, and a means of dissipating heat. A cooling system that relied on the suit's surface area to exchange heat would be largely ineffective in the steamy air. Instead, the suit would need an onboard refrigeration system powered by long-term energy supply, such as nuclear batteries or supercapacitors.

Corrosive flora and fauna. Every surface is covered in plantlife and crawling organisms. Some seem parasitic, others poisonous or malicious. The suit would need a hermetic seal and a protective outer layer resistant to dissolving compounds, acidic secretions, and wriggling vines that might worm their way inside. The suit would require powerful magnetics to repel or disrupt any vampiric behaviors. Some sort of toxin-scrubbing system would help remove dangerous chemicals.

Overwhelming sensory overload. The thousandfold sounds, crushing humidity, and hallucinogenic miasmas would swiftly drive a person mad if experienced directly. The suit would need to block as much of this as possible with sound dampening, air filters, and opthalmic filters that rely on computer feeds rather than direct vision. A calmative regimen or neural damper implant would help keep the traveller in a rational state.

Damage protection. The collapsing cliffs and boiling mud pits pose a constant threat of impact and erosion damage. The suit would need an extremely dense, crush-resistant yet light material - use of quantum-strained vacuum or hyperconfiguration compounds would be necessary. Some sort of hardlight or gravity bubble projector could give the suit improved damage resistance.

Vibrational and geological threats. The earthquakes and magma heat suggest the ground is evershifting. The suit would need superb structural integrity and an active dampening system to stabilize itself against shocks and flows of hot slurry. Extending legs and shuttle packs could get the traveler off the ground when needed. 

Psychological concerns. The traveler would need extensive psychological conditioning to keep a clear head while experiencing an environment that assaults every sense. A portion of the brain would need to be invaded and rewritten to see the landscape in structurally coherent terms rather than the mad soup of biological and geological overstimulation. Medician researchers would need to design a psychoactive tropicalomy to graft to the traveller's mind before they set out, one that granted a degree of psychic control over their perceptions and allowed them to perceive this place with a degree of mastery and depth.

The vagabond would require a near complete replacement of their physiology - containing bathing systems to hold their cells rigid in this over-oxygenous wash, pneumatic transit nodes to shuttle them between areas, ectoparasitologists to manage the slag of filiplasm winding into their pseudoflesh. Or perhaps they would take the form of a dynasty of grub-like robots boiled from a more fungible essence to soak in this praryordic ecological crucible for eons, seeking the burnt spore of transmutation.

In the end, after crossing light-years and life-times, the experient voyager might find the primordial oneness that having inbetween their parsed concentration-song of migratarios intersplicing entropic core-integrals against the lashing cantoseismic finaglestrings. The sporeclouds of strained emotional overburdenation interleave with the lessstriysys and reticulocyte scraps as they strive and sonnetize their begrimed inhomogeneous deeds. In that final singular tectoshift, kagacial and gravitational and psychic vents laying bare their undercrust in its rapt crocheting verge, they would realize - this is the birthplace of a world, balanceless and imploiding and forever changing, where mind and body are seared closed through the eye of the doll.

At last, home.
