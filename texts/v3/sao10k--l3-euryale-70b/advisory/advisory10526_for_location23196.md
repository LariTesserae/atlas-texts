To prepare for visiting this submarine megacity, a multiverse traveler would need to undertake extensive preparations, both physical and mental. The extreme environment - crushing water pressure, frigid temperatures, noxious gases - would require a specially pressurized and sealed suit, likely with internal heating and gas recycling. The suit would need to be buoyancy-controlled to navigate the currents and abyssal winds without being crushed by gravity.

Psychologically, the traveler would need to be mentally agile and adaptable, able to interpret the incomprehensible jargon of the city's inhabitants. Familiarity with inhuman biologies, exotic chemistries, and alien machine languages would be essential. The notion of evolving into an ecosystem with an entire world would likely be beyond the comprehension of most.

The traveler would have to be soothed by support AIs to cope with the sensory overinformation - the strobing UV, the cacophonous churning, the weaving searchlights and oscillating sonar-scopes. At every juncture, the urge to escape and flee back to the cosmic void would need to be fought.

Studying the city's past through historical feed-scribes, familiarizing oneself with the clades, guilds and historical catastrophes would help orient. Knowing that the city began in a debauched age of decoporeal individualism, then transcended to coco JDBC globe-selves, and finally cancelled out continuance towards multiverse colonization will frame just how alien this biacasphere would be to visitor sensibilities.

All things considered, visiting this place would be akin to being submerged inypical senses, sensations, and semiotics incompatible with our normals. Safe viewing through drone surrogates may be a wise choice rather than risk debilitating sensory and cognitive disorientation. Yet the hypertension to touch something greater might be worth the mortal peril for a curious multiplanet voyager.
