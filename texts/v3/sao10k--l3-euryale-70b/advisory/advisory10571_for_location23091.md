This hellscape of a city is likely a primary location in an extremely harsh wasteland universe that is the aftermath of some catastrophic event like a nuclear war, environmental collapse, or total economic and social implosion. A mere human wandering into this blighted metropolis would face massive dangers both environmental and mechantural.  

The first challenge is the crushing heat, toxic air, and lack of all fresh water. The sums it up as an "arid sprawl" of steel and concrete smothered in summer heat, with no trees or grass to provide respite and shade. "Toxic" and "acrid" pollutants repeatedly coat the air, and any open water is "murky", riddled with "black goo" of chemical waste. Without rigorous protective measures, one would quickly succumb to heatstroke, or be choked by lung-burning fumes.

The "boxy towerblocks crane up" like aggressive robots, and cargo trains "rampant down" at a reckless, hell-bent pace. Wharfs and river works push out the city's "footprint", as if the metropolis is some cancerous organism aggressively smothering the land.

One senses no reassuring signs of human life and emotion here, but only "the movement of the big things, the bearings of the engine, the pump of pistons." To survive awhile, the "mere human" traveler would need a high tech protective "enviro-suit", absolutely sealed with climate control to filter air and prevent heatstroke. Also necessary would be reliable purifying equipment to secure some supply of water, or self-contained food packets. Binoculars, motion sensors and general surveillance gadgets would help avoid being crushed by the city's rampaging machines. Grappling hooks and carabiners would aid in weaving between buildings. And an inconspiuous vehicle would help travel between areas without being noticed by the city's angry defenses and hostile tech-buildings.
