Preparing to travel to this place:

Before visiting this preternatural landscape, a human multiverse traveler would need to be extensively prepared to handle extreme heat, lack of air, and an alien environment. The key would be to insulate the body from the scorching conditions while keeping a stable internal environment.

Protective gear:
- Environmental suit with self-sustaining air supply and active temperature control. Multiple redundant backup systems to ensure no exposure to the vaproous sulfur fumes and blistering heat. Possibly a vacuum-sealed suit to maintain internal pressure.
- Polarized visor or external eye protection to shield from the blinding, shimmering reflections off the obsidian geomology. Filter to block out dangerous radiation levels.
- Tough, fire-resistant suit material to guard against sparks, hits and splashes of the viscous lava and glassworms. Built-in assault protection in case of lashings from the agitated mineral life.

Outer-planar equipment:
- Portable, durable dwellings or habitat that can fully seal off from the exterior, with built-in environmental controls and radiation shielding. Self-sustaining with air and food for extended stay.
- Atmospheric processing unit to scrub and decontaminate ambient gases for safety. Sample collection gear to extract insights from the bioluminescent weeds and lava flows.
- Loupe lenses for visibility through the shimmering, disorienting conditions during travel. Resonance and motion sensors to detect living changes amidst the glassy needles and jagged outcroppings.

Emergency recovery pod:
- Compact unit that can rapidly deploy and seal the traveler in if conditions turn deadly. Power dome to block out reality distortions with a stable pocket dimension. Basic repairs, life support and healing protocols onboard.

Support frames:
- Refueling drops or caches pre-positioned with powercells, supplies and spares for the habitat and suit. Switchable on-map position cueing so the traveler can access them through the preternatural disorienting conditions.

The traveler would study, map out and prepare for: 
- Thermal outcrop patterns, lava flows, glassy formation strengths and any hazards like hidden burbles and subseptations. Daily progress driven by avoiding death.
- Behavior, infintins basic understood language for the semi-sentient mineral life and glassworms. How to recognize and placate them, or at least dodge attacks.
- Radiations levels, heat fluctuations, watches for cyclical eruptions and lava churning that would make travel deadly at times.

It would be a daunting, disorienting terrain to travel - the shimmering obsidian and seething lava would make it hard to navigate or recognize hazards. The bioluminescent weeds, glassworms and fractal geometric structures would be fascinating to study but require contact with the alien matter which could be risky without proper tools and handling procedures.
