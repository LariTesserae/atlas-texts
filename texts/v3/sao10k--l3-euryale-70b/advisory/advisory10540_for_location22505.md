To prepare for visiting the Flooded Abyss, the ordinary traveler will need specialized equipment to withstand the extreme conditions of the deepest parts of the ocean. First and foremost, they must have a vehicle capable of withstanding water pressure many times that on the surface. A submersible rated for the depth of the abyssal plain is necessary, constructed of extremely strong yet lightweight materials. Composite capacitors will help offset the pressure, while sealed electronics and high-strength ceramics or boron filaments will protect the vessel's systems. Propulsion must be both powerful yet silent, to avoid putting stress on the hull. Electric or ion thrusters would be well suited. A fusion power source could provide both the energy required and a heat exchanger to warm the vehicle.

The traveler themselves will need a protective pressure suit rated for such depths. It will need to be a full-body suit, reinforced with endoskeletons and immersed in a water-filled "inner skin" to equalize pressure. The suit's materials will need high strenght-to-weight ratios, such as smart-fluid impregnated bladder materials to dampen inrushing water, enhanced with polymer Kevlar. The suit's sensors - sensitive sonars, gravimeters, heat sensors - will be the only way the traveler interacts with the environment. They must be ultra sensitive to function in the darkness and grime.

An air supply will be a major concern. C02 scrubbers and O2 generators will be needed, as well as a reserve of pre-charged O2 cells. The suit will also need to recycle water and organic waste. Hyrocarbon fuel cells or slow drip batteries could provide compact power.

Ambiental assistants are a must to enhance and decode the few sensory inputs available. Software will be needed to interpret seismic and sonar echoes into 3D visual maps, and depolarizers to scan for far ultraviolet or polarized light in the bioluminescent organisms. Neural or brain-computer interfaces could directly pipe audio/visual mappings to the suit's main speakers and helmet HUD. To aid navigation, gravimetric mapping will be needed, sonars mapped to AI neural nets to chart positions.
