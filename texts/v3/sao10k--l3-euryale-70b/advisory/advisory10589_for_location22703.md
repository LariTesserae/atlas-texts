The multiverse traveler preparing to visit Cygnus should first study the unique local properties and physics that govern this fascinating world. It appears that sound is extremely important here, with the omnipresent humming and thrumming vibrations emanating from the buildings. This could indicate that sound waves have enhanced properties or interactions with matter on this plane. It may be useful to pack specialized equipment for analyzing acoustics.

The city itself has an unusual topography, with the elevated causeways corduroy-ing out from the central island. Digesting detailed maps would help the traveler navigate the rising terrains. With no running rivers or streams, only the circular lake and its shores and shallows, a boat or water conveyance may be the best method of transportation and exploration, at least for crossing to the city from the empty plains.

The bulbous boulevards and buildings show the town hall as a central feature. Here would likely reside the key administrators, officials, and possibly the academic groups studying this unique metropolis. A traveler could go directly there to learn more and establish a base of operations. Along the way, all the buildings' and water ducts' noises should be noted for any clues they provide.

Importantly, there are fantastical creatures mentioned that the traveler should familiarize themselves with - a "hunter-screwed shamro al" with three heads, and "person falconvon-the-roofits titterbabits". Giant gull-murmurs, olive-addled chiti, and "hazkit" may all be species the visitor will encounter. Some seem warlike with "conflict-drone" equipment. The traveler should be ready to negotiate with them or defend themselves as needed. Packing nonlethal deterrents and translators would be prudent.

Lastly, the author mentions avant-garde melding crops "evertime wandums and the nomolions". The traveler should look to exploit and harness any mutated botanical sources they can study and acquire. Anything grown in this peculiar environment with such unique properties has extremely high potential. Careful gathering and sampling kits should be brought, to harvest interesting plants for transferral away and experimentation. 

All in all, to visit Cygnus, one needs a scientific mindset, a flexible spirit of adventure, and eye to spot opportunities to study its mysteries - and ideally some courage to face its dangers and strangeness. With proper preparation Cygnus offers a one-of-a-kind destination for a multiverse traveler to broaden their horizons and unravel the secrets nature has wrought there, unique in all the ever-unfolding multiverse.
