To prepare to visit this ancient, primordial lake within the planet's core, a human multiverse traveler would need to take extensive precautions. The environment is one of extreme anoxia and extreme temperatures. The traveler would need a full-body envirosuit capable of maintaining a stable internal atmosphere at room temperature, as well as insulation to protect against the cold and heat. The suit would require an independent air and water supply, as both liquids and gases in this location are toxic and corrosive to human tissues.

Sufficient illumination would be needed, as the only light is the luminescence of the organisms in the water. High-powered LED lighting and/or a portable power source for the suit's headlamp would allow the traveler to see and explore in the gloom of the planetary core. Night vision goggles may also be useful.

A device for neutralizing or filtering out the corrosive gases and pungent chemical vapors is crucial. Enhanced odor protection within the suit would help diminish exposure to the overwhelming, strange smells of the ecosystem there. 

Careful long-term precautions need to be taken for maintaining the mental health and stability of the individual, since time dilation and sensory deprivation from the unchanging environment and the knowledge of being in the core of a world could lead to dissociation or madness in an unprepared mind. I would recommend the traveler have a strong mental and philosophical foundation first, combined with effective psychiatric medication and continued remote monitoring via comms equipment and a medical AI program.
