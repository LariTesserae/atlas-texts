In this primordial yet technologically advanced world, life takes on strange forms shaped by the harsh environment and invasive cybernetic presence. Bioluminescent packs roam the stony expanses, their glowing fur casting an eldritch light across the dark rock as they hunt and play among the ruins. These wolf-like creatures have adapted their light-producing pelts as both camouflage and communication, flashing patterns to coordinate hunting strategies or signal perceived threats from either native predators or encroaching mechanisms.

Amidst brush that hugs close to the icy ground for warmth, smaller mammals skitter through tree roots, their coats woven with fine filaments that interface directly with their nervous systems. Information flows from sensing devices in their ears to map subtle tremors in the compacted earth, warning of approaching automatons before their heavy footfalls can be heard. Some even carry small solar panels embedded in the thinner skin of noses and paw pads to recharge their implants.

In puddles left behind by dripping crevices, tiny crustaceans evolve hardened exoskeletons impregnated with piezoelectric crystals harvested from shedders left behind by passing machines. Each organism becomes both sensor and broadcaster, communicating with neighbors via compressed packets of electricity passed along linked shells. Collective intelligence forms in swirling networks when colonies coalesce at new sources of water or mineral deposits exposed by construction activity.

Overhead, giant insects rise above shattered trees on wings made iridescent by microscopic chromatophores gleaned from smashed photovoltaics scattered across blasted debris fields. Their compound eyes contain nickel-iridium patches scavenged from abandoned satellite telemetry dishes to perceive every flicker of reflected lasercomm signals cast skyward by city towers. They translate these flashes into pheromones carried on lacy wingbeats to communicate intel back down allowed arborial routes.

All life here evolves tangles of organic matter and scavenged technology into something new - cyborg organisms that learn symbiosis with machines and architecture itself as cities extend tendrils further outwards over desolate horizons through range after range. In time this ecumenopolis becomes a thinking thing itself: billions of neurons firing in all directions, luminscent fibers pulsing like fungal mycelia through waking mountainside...a developing artificial brain married to living flesh where "life" becomes just another aspect of computronium...
