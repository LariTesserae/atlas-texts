The denizens of this liminal dreamscape are as strange and incomprehensible as the place itself. They drift, slither and ooze through the mist-shrouded caverns, their forms blurring and morphing constantly.

Floating lantern-like creatures emit eerie bioluminescence, casting hypnotic patterns on the walls. Their translucent membranes ripple and pulse in time with the eldritch energies that suffuse this space. Retinas dripping with putrid ichor absorb distorted spectra invisible to mortal eyes.

Lurching through viscous mire golesmes lumpy gelatinous beasts leave trails of glistening mucilage. Their pustulent hides split open to birth squirming masses of grub-like larvae which skitter up sheer surfaces like claws on glass. Draining spindrift pours from suppurating wounds to pool and congeal into sentient puddles that crawl off under their own power.

Huge meandering tentacles stretch languidly across chasms, bristled with twitching cilia. Ephemeral motes dance along these pseudopods before detaching to float free, leaving sputtering contrails of neurotoxins that burn away fog-reveal "impossible" vistas of eye-searing colours behind them.

Clotted clouds of floating motile bacteria swirl through the mists, propelled by wriggling flagella. Massed together they form vast smog-like entities that loom over the landscape like continent-sized beast-gods vomiting forth plagues into the already lethal atmosphere. 

Massive fungal blooms expand across every surface, secreting soporific spores and hallucinogenic mycotoxins. Insectile horrors encrust themselves within the fruity tumours' tangled networks of hyphae... until suddenly erupting outwards in frothing geysers of ichorous eggsacules and scintillating wingry drakes which flutter out to spread fresh infection far and wide...

Deep beneath it all: worm-things, immense segmented leviathans whose carapaced bodies undulate for miles beneath acres of turgid muck. Occasional bulges within hint at internal voids filled with unfathomable organs - perhaps even embryonic forms gestating in unthinkable wombs! And when great earthquakes shake the wastes above, monstrous mouths yawn open between grinding pebbled mandibles to unleash gouts of hyper-accelerated proto-plasma into the upper realms!
