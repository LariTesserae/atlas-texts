In this foreboding, suffocating environment, there dwell creatures unlike any seen before...

From the viscous pools of bubbling sludge emerge gelatinous abominations - their bodies amorphous sacks of pulsing organs jostling inside translucent membranes stretched over skeletal frameworks. Bulbous eyes ooze forth, unfocused and lidless as they squirm through the gloopy mess, propelled by twitching pseudopodia. Others haul themselves up onto the crumbling banks using flailing tentacles tipped with barbed suckers. They flop about as if trying to crawl free from ill-fitting skin.

Gliding between these horrors are swarms of insectoid machines, their carapaces dented metal bonded to organic matter in strange amalgams. Whirring blades and pistons propel them while glowing optics scan the horizon. Some alight atop fungi-like growths extruding from the wreckage to sit motionless like mechanical gargoyles surveying their soggy kingdom.

Looming above all is a leviathanian entity suspended within the aviary's concavity by chains and pulleys bolted to cyclopean columns of rust-eaten steel. Its body appears a mass of glistening lobes quivering in cohesion, occasionally parting to reveal teeth-filled maws that gnash and drool strands of slime. Hooded figures in ancient robes crouch upon its hide, prodding at clusters of sightless orbs with arcane implements leaving trails of ichor.

The aviary itself thrums with life - its honeycombed interior teems with motes swirling around bioluminescent globes created by membraned beings floating ever upward on currents heated by beating wings unseen. Concentric rings vibrate within each sphere radiating hypnotic patterns across the vault overhead.

The shattered spires pierce skyward like obsidian shards embedded in flesh, excreting streams of milky fluid that drip down their surfaces nurturing vines heavy-blooming with fecund tendrils curling into star-shaped flowers pulsating with inner light. Each blossom splits open periodically expelling clouds of spores that swirl outward carried on fetid breezes.

Beneath this blighted wonder sprawls an immense petal-like structure extruded from the sagging keel of one tower - a dome skinned-over with taut parchment stretched drum-tight over charred bones picked clean by carrion birds wheeling above. Gnarled roots probe from cracks splitting its surface probing like feelers seeking egress.

Thrumming bass notes rise from somewhere far below, reverberating through the encroaching silt-slick mud plastered along sheared walls exposed by settling foundations slowly sinking back into the primordial ooze whence they rose long ago...
