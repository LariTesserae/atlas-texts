The denizens of this forsaken place are as warped and twisted as the very architecture that clings to the cliff faces. Amidst the miasma of fungal rot and greasy smoke, life adapts in grotesque forms.

Ratkind swarm in chittering gangs, their beady eyes glinting with cunning malice from beneath mangy fur mats. Crude weapons sprout from wiry limbs: jagged knives strapped to forelimbs, chattering revolvers shoved into tattered waistbands. They sniff and snuffle through darkened warrens, stripping salvage to trade at market stalls manned by bloated slug-like merchants smeared in decaying blubber.

Oozing eels glide sinuously through fetid puddles, their phosporescent skin glowing a sickly greenish hue. Some writhe free of gelatinous sacs still clinging to rockface, newly birthed monstrosities squirming on unformed fins before flopping off to feed on drowned carrion.

Massive yllas hangbone shrouds drape from cantilever walkways like macabre banners, dripping ichor onto passersby. Their bulbous bodies undulate slowly as they shift between dimensions unseen to mortal eyes. Madmen speak in fevered whispers of seeing them devour unwary victims who stray too close when the murk grows thick enough.

Icterics and cabbals shuffle through debris fields in sodden robes heavy with fungal growths that pulse wetly across their backs. They mutter eldritch incantations that make blood run cold, hands weaving intricate sigils in the smog-choked air while syringes drip virulent alchemical concoctions into bulging veins.

Tinkers juggle assemblies of scavenged parts within sooted workshops lit by guttering tallow lamps. Gears click and bellows whistle ceaselessly as they cobble together biomechanic abominations - vat-grown muscle bundles sheathed in corded cables powering iron skeletons worn like exosuits by vat-bred warriors-for-hire.

Culs lumber through shadowed bazaars on trunk-like legs rooted in rancorous mud, sinuous tentacles coiled around crates bearing precious river valuables smuggled upstream past toll-houses guarded by tusk-faced troll mercenaries hurking gargling songs to themselves on thunderock drums carved from petrified wood-grubs.

And always overhead, skieships drone by trailing billowing contrails like pus-stained bandages flapping from wounds torn open by immense talons - vessels piloted by aberrant navigators sipping hallucinogenic vapors brewed from fungal hyphae scraped bleeding from tunnel-vault ceilings deep beneath the city's roots...
