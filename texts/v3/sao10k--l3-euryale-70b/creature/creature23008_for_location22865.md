In this blasted hellscape, where reality unravels and madness seeps from every cracked fissure, only the most deranged and unnatural entities could possibly eke out some semblance of existence.

Crawling between the shattered stones are pallid fungal growths that writhe mindlessly, oozing rancorous slime. Their bulbous caps flare open to release clouds of hallucinogenic spores that swirl maddening patterns against the fever-dream skies. Those who breathe too deeply begin to see visions of alien geometries and hear sibilant whispers promising forbidden secrets.

Ambling along ancient trails left by forgotten beasts are herds of mutated ungulates whose hides ripple with eldritch symbols that glow with sickly bioluminescence. They low ululating cries that scrape upon sanity as they gnaw putrescent bones stained with eerie phosphors.

Perched atop cacti bristled with obsidian quills are nightmarish avians whose feathers shimmer like oil slicks over polluted waters. Beady eyes glare balefully while hooked beaks snap hungrily, eager to rend flesh from bone. When they take wing their raucous shrieks echo across the wasteland like tormented souls wailing in Hell's pits.

Lurking within shadowed gullies are amorphous horrors birthed from nightmare-stuff made manifest when mortal minds crack under existential dread. Amoebic pseudopods ooze forth trailing ichor as these abominations slither toward prey driven delirious by thirst and heat-maddness. Once ensnared in gelatinous folds victims' screams quickly drown beneath wet smacking sounds before blessed oblivion claims them at last.

And always looming above broods the cyclopean tower eternally belching noxious fumes which paint lunatic murals amidst tortured cumulus clouds. Arcane energies thrumming through its ever-shifting architecture pulse hypnotically until bloodshot eyes can focus nowhere else than upon those dizzying hieroglyphics dancing down ivory sides... Hieroglyphics whispering promises...

Promises whispered also by pipers unseen playing unending refrains on nameless instruments fashioned from flayed skin stretched tight over pulsating organs never meant for music-making purpose! Promises echoing in droning chants intoned by robed figures swaying rhythmically around bonfires fed human fat!

All drawn inexorably towards a blasphemous confluence beyond sane comprehension should any dare ascend those twisting stairs spiraling up up UP into realms outside our own... Realms where gibbering things capering just shy of perception keen scent the coming sacrifice sent tumbling headlong past event horizons into THEIR domain eternal...
