In the misty dawn of this nascent world, life stirs in its first fragile glimmers. The trickling rivulets nurture a lush carpet of mosses and lichens. Motile fungi strain toward the dim light, their mycelium webbing the soil, symbiotically fueling seedlings that sprout from every crevice. Bioluminescent caps wink in the gloom—fairy-lamps illuminating the emerald smear of undergrowth.

Flickering firefly beetles and damselflies hover in the misty air. Their wings—crimson, tawny, jade and aquamarine—subtly iridescent. Caterpillars with fuzzy setae munch through leaves of leathery texture and phantastical shape. Colobus slugs, spotted and barred in livid hues, trail silvery slime along stone benches and wood-trunks. Centipedes and millipedes of various leg-counts scurry over the loam and tonguelike fronds.

Tapirs with shell-like growths on nose and proboscis root in the creek-beds. Salamanders with flame-pattered backs slither between root-boles. Baby trilobite-shelled beetles emerge from egg-cases embedded in mud-hanks. Odonate-alike dragonfly nymphs dart in the shallows, hawking for mosquito and mite larvae with explosive jaws. Egg-sacs of ephemeral mayflies and caddises litter the half-submerged cobbles. 

In the canopy, diapsid beetles click and clap tarsi, tree-frogs stridulate through the constant burble-brook susurration. A pod of river running toads line up at the waterline, frogs the size of a gull's egg, hysterical piping from hyper-inflated throats. Bristlemice with foxfire-pigmented fur flick between roots like gossamer motes. The stream-lit gloom throbs with the haze of nutrient cycling.

Life in its most rudimentary forms, at the threshold of kingdom and phylum, dreams primordial musk and pollen-miasmas. The water evanesces along a path that will one day be an oceanic artery, the trees' tannin and lignin staining it stream-cuts of khaki and russet. Sunlight peeks through the canopy-lace, brooking the creek-bottom like blood filtering through a veneered stone. Let it alone and let it unfold—newborn nor world, or yet even dryland—after cumulate cycles, this little sanctuarium will petrify. For now, let it live...
