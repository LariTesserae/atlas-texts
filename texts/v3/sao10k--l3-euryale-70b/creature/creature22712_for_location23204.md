In this grim vision of a drowned industrial metropolis overrun by toxic sea life and technological decay, where once-great skyscrapers are submerged in polluted ocean, what remains alive has taken on nightmarish forms.

Twisted mutant creatures lurk in every shadow - octopus-like beings peer out from rusting ruins, while sea-saplings writhe across crumbling facades. Some sort of gothic plants with red bioluminescent growths dangle from the skeletal remains of city infrastructure.

Inside the lab hidden deep beneath this dystopian hellscape, further genetic abominations are being engineered. Terrariums house grotesque fusion organisms floating in glowing chemostasis baths. Gestational pods pulse ominously, hinting at malformed things growing within. Cable tendrils squelch from exhausted human "donor husks" strapped to vivisection tables. Shattered bodies twitch with residual nervous impulses.

The lab is run by someone called Dam - likely an augmented cyborg given his ability to interface with equipment via biological ports and connect via cortex-plex links. He seems unconcerned about the doomed state of the world above, even revelling in it somewhat with his maniacal grin and nihilistic comments dismissing concerns over the purity and quality of biological materials used in his experiments.

Dam's thoughts reveal dim impressions of some massive mechanical entity emerging from the pollution-choked waters outside - a titan construct blotched with corrosive fluids and attended to by swarms of smaller foul machines. But something goes wrong when he accesses these memories and Dam cuts off the feed, bleeding rust-colored fluid from his optical implants.

Dam then tasks himself with beginning a new experimental cycle to produce corrupted biomass through unknown means before turning down for rest in a nightmarish dreamscape where claw-like shadows grasping behind pulsating verdian nodule colonies haunt him...
