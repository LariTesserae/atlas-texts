In this subterranean grotto, a diverse assortment of beings make their home, each adapted to the unique environment in their own way. Amidst the winding tunnels and vaulted chambers dwell tribes of troglodytes - hairy, ape-like hominids who have lived here for generations. They move with knuckle-dragging gait, using crude stone tools to chip away at the ancient ruins dotting the cavern floor in search of artifacts and scrap materials to fashion into weapons and trinkets.

These primitives are frequently encountered by visiting technoprophets from surface settlements beyond the mountains. Clad in scavenged power armor emblazoned with mystical glyphs, they rumble through the shallows and tunnels on repurposed construction exoskeletons. Plumes of hydrocarbon exhaust waft behind them as they delve ever deeper in pursuit of buried relics and veinstones to fuel their sorceries.

Other denizens include colonies of bioluminescent fungal hyphae that cling to walls and ceilings, casting eerie glows across puddles teeming with eyeless albino cavefish. Strangely-colored mineral deposits form glittering seams along tunnel lips where burrowing insects like clicking beetles and scuttling roachkin excavate nests.  

Occasionally, algominers creep out from their lairs draped in ratty thermal insulation to perform occult rituals and contest grognards' trials as part of some incomprehensible heirophany revolving around blocky obelisks and flocculent clouds of slurry. Intricate lacework corrosion patterns etch sigils into toppled stonework from which geoengineers ply their craft.

At times it seems even the cavern itself shudders and shifts according to some hidden cycle as motes swirl cyclonic eddies of colored powderstone dust churned up by pick-and-shovel laborers' efforts punctuated by skirls from newly bored shaft-tunnels. And always lurking just beyond torchlight's reach skulk gangs of slyshy brinkwraiths perpetually scheming mayhem upon the more industrious inhabitants throughout this timeless twilight world beneath forgotten mountain peaks far removed from the pulsarscrap flows convulsing civilization above...
