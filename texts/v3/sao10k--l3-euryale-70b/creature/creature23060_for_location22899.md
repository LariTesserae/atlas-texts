What dwells in this noxious, visceral pocket realm? Life pulsates with the slow-primal surge of ancient kingdoms, writ in the glutinous fluids and pulsing fungal latticework entwined with septic murk. These caverns are a hotbed of dungeoning microbes - writhing shreds of bacteria, slimy rashes of archaea, twitching mats of mycoplasma. Their astringent waste taints the fluids into brackish sepsis, mead-thick with proteins and sweltering warmth and decaying fulfillments.

Burrowing vegetable mucus retreats in thickets of stinking ambience, bloating and pulsing with suckling eddies of amphipods and fairy shrimp and brine shrimp. Rhizoids of milky fungal tendrils swole with lithe oils burble within grasping wreathes, riddled with feasting mineral salts. Enmeshed amoebas swell into gillnet lugs of muddelsoft protoplasm dulled in thrumming pseudoplasms.
