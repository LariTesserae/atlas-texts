In this strange, subterranean world beneath the looming vertical city, life has adapted in bizarre ways to thrive in the eerie half-light filtering down through the narrow canyons of water and rock. Bioluminescent creatures drift languidly on currents thick with nutrients scavenged from unknown sources far above - elongated fish with lantern-like organs along their sinuous backs casting a spectral glow, their translucent forms barely visible in the murk. 

Beneath the dark waters, tiny motes of living matter clump together into floating towns of scavenged debris, dollhouse-sized buildings pieced together from flotsam collected from higher levels and lit by guttering candlelight. It is said that these deep-dwelling folk have evolved subtle symbioses with the alien sea-life drifting past their precarious settlements - exchanging chemicals for aid against their common enemy, those who dwell in the twisted towers clinging to cliff faces above.

For there is a great tension between these marginal peoples - those who pine for sunlight and cling to crumbling ledges and walkways above, and those who have made peace with endless night beneath choppy black waters. Strange alliances form and fractious rivalries break out periodically amid twisting stone columns etched with pulsing fungal growths and guarded by grasping fronds of venomous cyber-plants.

Amidst it all teems a thousand improvised forms of life: gargling frogs clutch at sunken helicopter rotors slick with mucus, while snaggle-toothed carp prowl grate-shrouded corners where boiling effluent streams meet stagnant reaction pools. Glittering dragonflies buzz power lines strung bowlike between beetling stalactite antennae gouged from artificial caverns above.

The heavy thrum of industry seeps down through all this strange ecology, sour tastes leaching constantly into oily water choked with flagellating algae mats. A ceaseless pulse of machinery churns unseen overhead day after day. In its throbbing undertone there modulates an uncanny music like frying meat plucked on electrified catgut strings...a siren song calling all things towards some mad unifying purpose straining upwards to swallow all in foamy verdure strangulating even the ooze of subterreanean stones...

Peering up from shadowed burrows and hollow HVAC ducts etched in seething fungi are countless watchful eyes - seekers of advantage amid this eternal war waged by fusions organic and synthetic for precarious space to live in a liminal world between two hells - one looming invisibly above as much as below.
