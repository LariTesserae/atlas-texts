This primordial world pulses with life, the very air seeming to reverberate with the hum of insects and plants alike. In this hazy miasma, forms blur - shape-shifting entities ooze from mud and mist, twisting into grotesque parodies of animal and vegetable matter. Lurching monstrosities heave themselves from steam-haunted tarns and vernal pools, dripping veils of algae and lichen across the tangle of vegetation.

Amorphous fungi sprawl as far as the eye can see (which isn't far indeed), pulsating gently like captive organs. Translucent membranes undulate above viscous pools, filtering tiny organisms from the soupy waters. Tubular stalks thrust skyward without apparent purpose, some bearing globular sporocysts that release shimmering motile spores into the thick air.

Echinoderms of impossible geometries scrabble over rocks slick with slime molds; polyps squirt jetsam into whatever passes for current here. Worms by the billions tunnel ceaselessly through wet earth and overflows onto slick clay banks to writhe amidst mats of hyphae. Giant proto-leeches attach themselves to larger beasts to sup on sluggish ichors oozing from unnameable lesions.

Amidist it all crawl things only imaginable in nightmare - arthropods both massive and miniscule, chelae clacking mindlessly as they tear flesh into shreds to be gulped past gnashing mandibles or sucked up through proboscises in a macabre perpetual motion. Eight-legged arachnids scuttle amongst them, spinning webs to snare prey which thrashes futilely within silken sacs before being drained dry or wrapped tighter than any Egyptian king's corpse for later consumption.

Overhead strange winged beings glide on thermals rising from glowing calderas then dive at terminal velocity to impale victims still writhing in torment upon hooked barbs protruding from stumps where limbs were torn away squealing. These foul beasts excrete hot waste rains down in sheets searing whatever lies exposed beneath, charring skin black instantly while acidic contents eat through bone like cancerous fire.

Yet even this is not enough - runes pulse malevolently under brackish mud coating the crumbling ruins of cyclopean cities long fallen before man knew metals, sending glyph-inscribed tentacles questing outward seeking hosts in which to pupate new horrors well-suited for this plane saturated by soul-dulling eldritch energies bleeding in from interdimensional spaces where dread unfathomable deities slumber fitfully dreaming such realities into existence...
