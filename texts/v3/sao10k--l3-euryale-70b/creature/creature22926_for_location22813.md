What dwells in this desolate highland realm? Not merely the twisted scrub clinging to life among the sterile rocks. Nor just the phantom ruins of forgotten civilizations crumbling beneath the pitiless wind. No, something far stranger lurks in these stark environs, woven from stone and shale and immortal persistence.

They are known to us primarily by their echoes - eerie ululations that reverberate between mountain ridges, setting teeth on edge and hair prickling against skin. An uncanny mimicry of tortured screams, yet too alien to truly evoke anguish. Some say those cries emanate from hidden lairs within sheer-walled gorges, while others whisper they drift on currents born in strata laid down eons past.

To hear those wails is to know a primal unease, an existential dread like nothing else. For they speak of realities beyond our ken, dimensions inaccessible save through arduous trials of mind and body. Few return unchanged from confronting such eldritch truths.

These entities move unseen along conduits both physical and ethereal. Their steps resound faintly in ancient petroglyphs etched upon boulder faces. Glyphs said to encode primordial secrets given mortal men by beings incomprehensible to them (though perhaps less so now).

Some claim a glimpse of striped hide glimpsed flickering at the corner of vision between sheer drops that plummet into mist-shrouded ravines thousands of feet below. Others tell of keening laughter carried on night winds after all other sounds have fallen silent under smothering veils draped by moonless skies above black basalt sentinels.

Bold explorers who dare delve into labyrinthine passages honeycombing bluff faces report encounters ineffable and transformative in nature - though none remain quite sane enough afterwards to recount details without gibbering madness seeping around edges of tattered sanity.

Local lore speaks darkly also of immortals entombed alive ages untold ago as penance for crimes unforgivable even by gods themselves...and bound fast until some unknown hour when stars wheel round again unto their appointed positions signaling release...

But there are stories whispered behind closed doors about folk gone missing during solstice nights spent atop certain bluffs marked with sigilic runes pulsating softly scarlet at twilight...who reappear months later wandering dazed from wilderness bearing cryptic knowledge gained from impossible sources - if indeed they appear again at all...

So beware those haunted wastes where earth meets sky! Tread warily lest you catch unwanted attention flitting through cracks in reality lurking ever-watchful 'mongst stony fastness eternal!
