This nightmarish hellscape teems with twisted life forms that have evolved to thrive amidst the constant flux and torment. Among the jagged obsidian crags, misshapen humanoid figures scuttle and climb with dangling limbs. Their pallid flesh bubbles with pulsing veins, leaking rivulets of glowing ichor. Multiple lidless eyes bulge from their bulbous heads, blinking in unison. They emit keening wails that harmonize into discordant hymnals to whatever eldritch powers spawned them.

Massive cyclopean insects stalk between the geysers of magma and vomiting rivers of alchemical fluids. Carapaces dapple irridescent hues before shifting into heat-blooming camouflage against the hellish backdrop. Compound eyes whirl hypnotically as they deploy segmented limbs armed with dripping proboscises and barbed ovipositors. These blasphemous beetles seem to be the apex predators here.

Scuttling things wriggle like greasy icicles between rocks glazed in phosphorescent slimes. Fronds of bioluminescent fungi wave extradimensional tendrils in unrecognizable prayers. Skittering arthropod swarms devour sprouting bulbs leaking nectar-tainted mutagenics.

Floating in the smog-shrouded sky, gargoylish pterosaurs soar on translucent membraned wings, leaving contrails of condensing brimstone fumes. Talons tip with retractable spikes glistening with contact venoms as they divebomb prey.

Deep subterranean burrows writhe with worm-like horrors hundreds of meters long, thickset segments rippling past stalactites hung heavy with dripping viscid secretions. Crustaceans like seething cankerous tumors skitter along walls dribbling putrid egg-sacs.

The very rock seems alive, undulating and twisting underfoot in peristaltic waves as if something enormous shifts restlessly beneath an inch-thick crust of stone scabs stretched taut over unguessable bulk. Phosphor-glyphs pulse a lurid light from mica flecked fractures before exfoliating away like shedding skin revealing fresh runic sigils behind them.

Unwholesome polyp blooms extend questing fronds towards crepuscular lagoons while zombie trees exhale necrotizing pollen on cancer-gills that exploit unquiet breezes whispering sibilants of sorcery through leafless boughs caked white with desiccated salt-crusted phlegm...
