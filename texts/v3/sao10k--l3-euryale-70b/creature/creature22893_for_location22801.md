In this bleak, industrial wasteland, twisted forms of life have evolved amidst the pollution and decay. Mutated plants with blackened leaves clatter in the acrid winds, while sickly fungi ooze from cracks in the rust-chewed machinery. The tainted waters birth extremophile microbes that thrive in the toxic soup.

Amidst this biomechanical nightmare roam hordes of gene-spliced hybrids - part animal, part machine. Hulking cyber-beasts with glowing optics stalk the shadows cast by gargantuan factory piping, while packs of razor-clawed chimera predators hunt the drone-swarm alleys between barracks blocks. Towering colossi fused from scavenged war-mech parts patrol armored perimeters, plasma cannons tracking ceaselessly.

Inside the factories toil human workers shackled to assembly lines by neural implants. Their minds dulled by brain-computer links and mood-stabilizing nanotech, they labor ceaselessly amid roiling rivers of molten alloys and jets of pressurized gases. Overseeing all are cadres of coldly efficient cyborg overseers and AI manifolds coordinating optimal production cycles.

Beneath it all lurk subterranean colonies of degenerate gene-pool refugees living among corroded pipes and dripping steam vents. Misbegotten vigorants scrabble in fetid ductwork warrens, avoiding purge drones patrolling aboveground.

High in the sulfurous skies wheel squads of robotic hawk-drones surveilling for signs of dissent or anomaly. They scan for unauthorized encryption traffic emanating from backroom hackers trying to infiltrate centrally-controlled data-nets to spread revolutionary propaganda.

In abandoned corners festering revolutionaries plot uprising against corporate oligarchs who control everything from orbital platforms above. Deformed organic AIs born from hacked wetware merge ghostnet protocols into emergent singularities that whisper seditious memes into audio-feeds piped into worker-brains...

And so an evolutionary struggle unfolds across surreal levels - genetic, technological, ideological - within a claustrophobic 'ecosphere' dedicated solely to ruthless productivity and total control over mindshare; where nature is just another resource to exploit until nothing remains but sterile efficiency incarnate...
