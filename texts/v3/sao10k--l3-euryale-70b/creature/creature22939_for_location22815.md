In this lush, primeval canyon, teeming life fills every crevice from the river to the treetops. Trout and char swim silver arcs through the crystal currents, scales flashing in shafts of sunlight. Glass-clear water reveals crayfish scuttling among river rocks while salmon leapt explosively from the surface feeding on caddisflies hovering just above. Tadpoles wriggle and flick their tails in the oxygen-rich shallows.

Raccoons splash nosily, combing stream-side detritus for grubs and insect larvae.
