This alien world teems with life, but it is no ordinary ecosystem. The very ground beneath is smothered in a dense, turgid undergrowth of bioluminescent vegetation - a seething mass of tendrils, vines, and fungal hyphae. The plants seem to Knowing in their ethereal light, pulsating red, purple, and angry colors. Here and there, cooler notes of blue and gold peer through the fungal carpet, like strange flowers unfurling to the glow.

The landscape itself is organically reshaped by this living, breathing blanket. Hills and valleys conform to billowing mounds of vegetal matter, becoming blunted and blished outwards and extending turgid fungal growths. What once was sky now churns with undulating waves of soft, luminous fibres, lit from within by subterranean blossoms. At the horizon, the vista resolves into an vast, abyssal jungle, that permits only a foreground glimpse into the fungal undergrowth. 

Ethereal sprites of light dance from one glowing spire to the next, spattering the world's surface in eerie luminescence. Columns of spores and lumens pyramid the air from apex growths - shimmering tubes and capillary rivulets etching the firmament. The sea of bioluminescent light pulses to some and organon rhythm, in time to the world's unseen luminous moon.

The landscape is enveloped by a primordial forest - an endless tangle of gnostic vines and hyphae. The multifold "trees", "bushes" and "flowers" seem to grow into symmetric growths with no dividing line between tree, fungus, puffball, bread fungus, shelf fungi, coral fungus - a vast colonnade of gilled shapes. Fibrous masses grow on old growth, soft tufts dangling into units of air, puffing spores and biolumescent nectars and essential fluids into the air, only to drift into new growth, to seed with the wind.

The view combines these flumes to a dense, unplanned clustering, encapsulated and unpredictable, choked by hyphae connections. Millions of glowing details diffused and limited in growth by the hostile red and purple jelly-like substance, but occasionally chopped through in casual, conquering dense nutriant rich growths. Like organa drumming to an unseen, pulsating rock, the boundaries of organism blur and zoom. Electrochemical beats of cercatory feeling fit together in what seems a single growth - a planetary-sized mise-en-scene of a hivemind world, and pristine - its ennemy, its equal, the earth it sits upon and from which it was begot from.
