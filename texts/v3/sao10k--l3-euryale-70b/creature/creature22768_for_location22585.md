The caverns pulse with primordial, alien life. Bioluminescent fungi blanket every surface in an eerie glow, their ethereal radiance illuminating the labyrinthine tunnels and eerie grottos. The air is heavy and humid, mist curling up from the floors and walls to create a hazy, otherworldly atmosphere. The scent of damp earth and living fungal growth permeates everything.

Tangles of fungi and tiny white flowers meld into the cavern walls, creating puffy lace-like layers and totesque structures. Individual growths bunch together into humps resembling building foundations or barnacles embedded in boulders, as if about to meld with the bedrock itself. Vines snake through cracked trenches, looping around root columns. Mushrooms the size of beach balls sprout around stalagmites of stone, like temperate cacti. Dense, layered plant growth tessellates out over the surfaces to the extent that the cavern structure itself seems composed of loam, plaster, or fungal hyphal networks. The living substrate and stone interpenetrate to the point of ambiguity.

The only sounds are the echoes of distant dripping water and faint, gurgling murmurs from deep within the fungal growths - as if the organism itself were breathing or digesting. Occasional snaps and rustles signal secret movement, but bizarrely, no creature-shaped holes pierce the misty mycelial fog, as if life has condensed into fiber only here. Past the claustrophic mists and foliage, the caverns tunnel into vast undiscovered networks. Shafts of silver-glinting stone pierce up to towering ceilings above surrealist fungal landscapes.
