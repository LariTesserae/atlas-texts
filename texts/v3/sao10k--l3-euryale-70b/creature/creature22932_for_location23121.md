What stirs and crawls and skitters across these hellish expanses? In this nightmarish parody of Earth, where fire and ice coexist in eternal combat, where the air itself sears lungs and peels skin like battery acid...

Here dwell beings best described as "predominantly mechanical." Monstrosities born from some deranged fusion of flesh and steel. Twisted amalgams of organic matter grafted wholesale atop whirring gears, oozing servos, hissing hydraulics. Pallid skin stretched drum-tight over pulsating pistons. Eyes replaced with glowing lenses, hair supplanted by bristling bundles of wires.

They move with jerky, stuttering motions, limbs locked at unnatural angles before lurching forward in sudden bursts of speed. Joints crackle and grind against themselves like rusted hinges as they scuttle along catwalks barely wide enough for their misshapen bulk.

Open wounds ooze viscera among tangles of sparking circuitry. Rivulets of lubricant splatter the floor beneath them like drool. Their breath comes in rasping wheezes punctuated by belches of pressurized vapor. Guttural shrieks tear from vocal cords layered atop cheap speakers.

Some are humanoid in shape only: bulbous torsos sprouting multiple mismatched limbs at all wrong angels. Others resemble arachnid nightmares, multi-jointed legs skittering across surfaces while grasping armatures end in vicious-looking manipulator claws. Still others are formless blobs suspended within cages of spinning gyroscopes and thrumming generators.

All bear signs of recent assembly - sutured incisions crisscrossing their hides like railroad tracks on a map, bolts holding flesh fast to metal frames protruding from healing scars. Some writhe weakly from tables strewn about the workshop floor where the surgery was performed.

Despite their grotesque appearance they seem to possess a semblance of intelligence behind those optical sensors - a grim sort of willpower animating muscles bulging around pistons straining to overcome each other's counter-motion...

These are the sole denizens populating this forsaken pocket dimension - twisted creations given half-life through unholy union with machines far beyond mortal men's craftings! Beasts birthed solely to serve some inscrutable purpose known only to their insane creator(s). Abominations driven solely by urges programmed into positronic brains never meant for such purposes!

And yet...in glimpses caught when optics swivel towards you...a flicker crosses those unblinking photoreceptors that might be called something akin to recognition...or longing...for things lost long ago....
