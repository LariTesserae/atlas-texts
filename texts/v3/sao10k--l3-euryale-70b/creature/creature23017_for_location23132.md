This barren wasteland, devoid of beauty or life, is home to a few hardy creatures adapted to the harsh conditions:

Feral dogs roam the ruins in loose packs, subsisting off whatever scraps they can scavenge. Ribs show through matted fur, ears pinned back warily as they prowl for carrion. Their howls echo mournfully under the pall of pollution at night.

Desiccated tumbleweeds blow through the broken streets before catching on jagged metal edges. Deformed lizards and snakes slither through crevices between rusted hulks in search of cooler spots or sheltered caches of insects. Mutated beetles click and whir by the thousands.

Wilted crows perch atop chimneys rattling out ugly caws when disturbed. Stunted vultures circle on thermals rising over ponds of poison slurry below.

The only vegetation are scrubby bushes secreting oily sap that burns the tongue if licked and dandelions whose fluff makes one cough black specks.

At dusk, luminous moths flit among twisted rebar in their eerie blue glow while stink bugs buzz loudly around flickering streetlamps that cast shadows across heaps of junkyard scrap...

So it goes on in this squalor. Few other creatures have adapted enough to eke out meager existences here. But always something new drifts through the toxic breeze - a plastic bag caught in barbed wire, an injured sparrow struggling against a current of effluvia...and slowly decaying away into nothing but bones bleached white on cracked concrete within this vast blighted metropolis beneath sooty skies forever shrouded in fumes.
