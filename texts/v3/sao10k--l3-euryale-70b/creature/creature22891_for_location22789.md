In this strange and primordial landscape, where the very bedrock seems to writhe and twist itself into tortured shapes, alien forms of life claw their way forth from the unforgiving stone and cold grey soil. 

Gnarled, blackened tree-things rise like grasping skeletal fingers towards the roiling sky above, their thick, corded branches hung with dripping dying leaves that leak milky fluids when brushed against. The "wood" beneath is spongy and riddled with holes like rotten cheese. At night these pustules glow a sickly yellow-green bioluminescence.

Bizarre fungal growths sprout in clusters along the riverbanks, bulbous caps in unnatural shades of bruise-purple and waxen white pushing up through mats of oily mosses. Veins pulse beneath their waxy exteriors as if something squirms within yearning to erupt.

Pox-like swellings along the trunks of plants ooze viscous secretions that drip onto the ground to pool and steam in viscous puddles that shimmer and ripple strangely despite no breeze. These seething pools seem to throb with subtle inner motion.

Vines split open to release plump little sacs to drift out over the water on membranous wings leaving glistening trails behind them. Those not borne away explode in arcing sprays when they touch down again unleashing more of their kind.

Blind fishlike things fluke about just beneath the brackish surface leaving eddies in their wake. Their slick grey-white flesh appears stripped of scales but studded with wet puckered mouths ringed by frills of translucent tentacles.

Molders wriggle in deadening mounds littered with desiccated scraps while insectoid shapes skitter among them: iridescent beetles, pallid segmented grubs rearing back on nigh-rational stalks. They exude heady pheromones heavy enough nearly to clasp your throat with invisible hands.

Birds keening above emit rainbow droppings that sizzle when striking the water's surface or send up geysers before melting into hissing acid slimes.

All this bizarre and fulsome flora and fauna seem to press ever further into its strangeness yet cannot help but thrive here even as they warp each other by contact. An ecosystem unlike any known elsewhere surges forward into wilder complexity each passing moment: an intertwining web whose strands wrap tight around one another until no telling them apart can be done without tearing them asunder - which somehow only causes both halves more furiously grow anew!
