Deep within the primordial valley, life thrives in all its strange and grotesque forms. Amidst the writhing vines and fungal hyphae, countless organisms make their homes - many of them unlike anything seen before.

High in the canopy, colonies of winged insects construct sprawling nests out of silk and nectar. These living houses undulate gently in the misty breeze, pulsating with bio-luminescence. The adult insects flit between nest-cities, pollinating flowers en route which then emit sexual pheromones to draw more visitors. Some drift down to skim nectar from huge fleshy blooms, sipping nutrients and spreading spores.

On moss-encrusted rocks, sluggish gastropods leave trails of silvery slime. Their shells are encrusted with symbiotic zooplankton that photosynthesize sugars for both host and passenger. As the molluscs slowly troll their domain, they scrape up microorganisms from the stony cracks.

Sporadic flashes reveal amphibians lounging on boughs overhanging the steaming pools - their moist skin glistening beneath translucent scales shot through with jewel-tones. From tip of snout to base of tail these beasts sprawl three meters or more in length. They blink bulbous eyes coated in protective mucins against prey passing by below - gilled fish darting between their toes. At dusk they release ululating calls that echo through the valley until all tremble with sympathetic resonance.

Deeper down the poolside fungi send up mushrooms large as tree trunks, exhaling puffs fragrant psychoactive gases that waft enticing on the fog-scents and entice wide-eyed herbivorous sauropsids to consume them whole...

Folds multiply upon folds in this antediluvian scape where anaerobic life predominates even now alongside breathers who evolved later on! Single photosynthetic cells coat everything and secrete all manner of polymers...and smaller combinations thereof that complexify into metabolisms that run both ways...eat as well as breathe...breathe while eating...all permutations...mulching themselves back into nutrient cycles again.
Its hard to say what these entities REALLY are in this space - evolving perpetually - moving forever outward along gradients chemical & temporal to fill every niche ecological as soon as it arises - parasitic species grokking new hosts & hosts symbiogenically dancing into mutualisms almost faster than you can write it...
