In this dark, primordial abyss, forms of life slowly drift and skulk through the frigid waters and silt. Anglerfish and viperfish glide past with eerie bioluminescent lures, their light organs pulsing softly. Strange shrimp scuttle about, antennae twitching. Aberrant fish flick their deformed fins, engaging other senses than sight in the endless gloom. Jellyfish ooze by, their ghostly bodies shimmering with an uncanny alien luminescence.

Here and there, hydrothermal vents belch forth superheated fluids stained with metals and minerals from the Earth's guts. Tubeworms cluster around them, siphoning nutrients. Brine shrimp graze the microbial mats encrusting the chimneys in a slow dance. Giant tube worms writhe by the vents on feathery plumes seeking chemical bounty in the torpid current. 

This timeless ecosphere is in constant flux between fluid and sediment - a vast chemical cycle older than any land-dwelling creature crawling above. Here ancient species like bacteria have evolved alongside deep-sea arthropods and molluscs over eons of Earth's history into peculiar adaptations to these conditions no human eye will ever behold unfurl. Only here abide true discontinuities in evolutionary trajectory long dead on solid ground.

Deeper still live grotesque anglerfish females swollen with parasitic males fused to her body - among first divergences from ancestral bilaterality. Giant squid lurk unseen as well, unhurried bulks drifting through the pitch-black depths propelled by ocular ink-jets perpetually watchful for prey...all prior lifeforms left behind that know no shore.

The cold-water coral reefs poke up here and there yet are no respecters of modern coral or reefs as do the shallower ones - nearly blind star-shaped polyps waving sensory feelers probing dark currents rich in nutrients leaching from seamount plains beyond reach of photosynthesis needing only moist warmth: wholly separate lineage unlike corals outside this environment adapted inward/outward from ancestral arachnids beyond gulfs of time.
