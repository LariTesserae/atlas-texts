Crustaceans the size of wagons scuttle across spongy fungal growths on too-many legs, carapaces encrusted with barnacles that pulse angrily red beneath translucent shells. Echinoderms larger still wallow in viscous muck, writhing tentacular appendages probing the murk while radially symmetrical mouths ringed by grinding beaks rip apart carrion.
