High on the barren slopes, rock-monsters yet slumbered, vanes locked like stone, wings folded close.Aliases stirred, invisible until they moved, tenebrous pseudopods flicking through shadow. The base stuff of the mountains wove them over millenia from carbon, calcium, silicates, watering them until they grew solid and whole.
The keening wails were the a were song, hymn of the winds their flesh-like skin drank. Cracks and seams wept ichor that trickled into hollows and cracks, brooding in nitrogen-leached brine, awaiting conversion.
Yrs of viscous seams had birthed this cohort, mewling and clawing from lakepunk to free, low on fuel, but mounted, hunting the world below. The stratum stank of them, the new names they named them, the one thing they craved and fatted them to reproduce.
