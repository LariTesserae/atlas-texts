This harsh yet majestic alpine realm is home to those who are born of mountain stock - folk adapted by generations to survive amidst the unforgiving grandeur of peak and glacier. They dwell in sturdy villages crammed along riverine strips, their hand-hewn stonework rising several stories above the turbulent waters. Ramparts bristle with halberdiers' dark armor as they keep watchful eyes scanning the barren wastes beyond for threats both human and bestial.

The villagers subsist on meager harvests eked from narrow floodplains, supplementing crops with furs, hides, wool and cheese traded from roving pastoralists whose beasts graze the short-grassed upland pastures. Craftspeoples ply their trades - forging arms and tools at flaring smithies spewing smoke across slate-colored skies. Woodwrights shape planks for bridges spanning icy torrents rushing down-slope. Weavers card woolly fleece into thick felt cloaks.

Life here revolves around an endless war waged not only against marauding highlanders but also against entropy itself. The very stones seethe beneath their feet, mountains groaning as glaciers calve off in thunderous avalanches. Bitter frosts turn men stiff as statues overnight while milder days see pestilential vapors rise steaming from every puddle and rivulet to cloud vision and confuse senses.

No wonder then these indomitable mountaineers cleave so fervently to traditions passed unbroken since before even elder times! Ancient tabus constrain each action lest hubris provoke vengeful spirits said to stalk behind every cliff and whisper through every gust of freezing air howling out of witch-lit gullies. Priests conduct blood sacrifices atop windswept altars at solstice when aurorae flicker overhead in lurid dances...offerings made to propitiate deities that rule a land where life teeters ever on precipice's edge between glory eternal and oblivion absolute!

Yet danger begets greatness just as hardship hones heroes keen-edged sharp as Dwarven steel! These people live large under impossibly looming eaves of rock: voices raised raucously loud singing songs taught father-to-son back unto time immemorial about feats done bold 'neath jagged Jotunheim peaks! Mead flows free at feasting boards heaped high with haunches roasted whole over driftwood fires fed till flames roar deafening loud as avalanche-thunder rumbling down-valley! Laughter peals echoing across fjord horns accompanied by wild skirling music blown upon pipes hollowed from eagle bones!

For though death looms everywhere here his ghastly visage reflected stark in every ice-wall glimmering grim grey - none here will meet him cowed or beaten broken! Nay, let snow fall deep enough to freeze blood solid in veins: let black bears lumber out frenzied-maddened by famine's sting ravenous to rend man-flesh: let brigands come ravaging armed cap-a-pie astride shaggy hill ponies snorting steam in bitter nip of winter's bite...still shall these sons-of-the-mountains stand unbowed unbound until last breath rattles wet through shattered teeth knocked loose by hammerblows struck without pity nor remorse!
