What dwells amidst this planet-wide wasteland is a menagerie of strange and twisted lifeforms that have adapted to thrive in this poisonous perpetual twilight. 

Mutant avians soar through the ash-choked skies on malformed wings, hooting mournfully. Their plumage is oily black, shot through with sickly iridescence. Beady eyes gleam crimson in darkness-light.

Amphibious beasts wallow in mist-shrouded pools, their hides dripping viscous mucus that steams in the feeble cold. Gills flutter along bulbous throats as gurgling breaths are drawn from waterlogged lungs. Clawed flippers propel them sluggishly beneath the surface where bioluminescent fungi cling to rocks like glowing mold.

Massive insectoids skitter through tangled underbrush, carapaces brittle and angular like rusted metal plating. Compound eyes whirr and click, ocular facets shifting as they scan for prey or predator alike. Mandibles gnash and froth with acidic saliva. Whorls of pungent pheremone seep from glandules along arched thoraxes.

Unnameable things writhgle in filaments of fungal hyphae, uselessly disputing for purchase against each other's membranous bulk. Cilia lash feebly in a gelatinuous morass while flagellae push feebly against stagnant fluid currents. Giant cystic spores burst wetly, belching clouds of allergenic spores into the murk.

Hulking saurian reptiles lumber through the fetid groves of cycads and ferns, armored scales rasping against bark and leaf-lace. Rheumatic joints crack audibly as columnar legs bear ponderous torsos over mire and rockrumble. Eyes lidless yet piercing stare unblinking from bony crania crested with horns and cranial vault bosses.

Bipedaloid apes pick listlessly at lichened treeflesh, scraping up edibility - weevils, grubs, eggsacs desiccated into jerky - to cram mechnically into slack mouths webbed with flaplike appendages that might be tongues or gill-rakers or combined organs of taste and respiration both. Their pelts are splotched piebald with albinism and melanoma-like skin cancers cratering their flesh.

All these abominations share traits common to this blighted realm - some squamous staining of their integuments towards brownish-yellowed horn-colored black; all species paunchy-bloated with edema; all ichorous fistulous draining from occluded pores; neural fibers extruding visibly through skullskin; most missing extremities replaced with weathered ferruginaceous prosthetics or eldritch surgical attachments...

But most importantly: a palpable aura of creeping madness suffuses them all - eyes rolling back to whites glazed over at stalk-stare while jaws work soundlessly around half-remembered vocalizations lost before shaping into language... A feeling that something prodigious yet phantasmagoric pulses within the mold-slick caverns vacuoles hollowing out their craniums... An utter inability to conceive any thought long enough to take shape before it smears away into viscous runnels like mucilage washing off glass ...

These mutant organisms are the bestial denizens spawned from poisoned seeds planted during an aeon when consciousness was uncertain what it wished to make; wishes boiling up from an idiot demiurge mind-breath mistaking itself for Creation itself... They dwell now in rancid symbiosis upon a world whose only purpose was its own self-maintenance against logical reality's indigestability...
