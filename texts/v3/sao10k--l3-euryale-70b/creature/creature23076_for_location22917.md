In this harsh, angular city carved from the unforgiving stone of the high desert plain, a resilient people eke out a meager existence. Theirs is life defined by the eternal struggle against desiccation, isolation and entropy - yet they have learned to embrace the stark beauty in these things. 

The truest nature of the residents of Huitzahuacan reveal themselves in the subtle details woven throughout their architecture and culture:

Outside, ravaged antelopes trudge rustically across rough-hewn flagstone streets while coal-black ravens wheel in skies bleached apricot-orange by the setting sun. But inside the cyclopean buildings that honeycomb every inch of space, an intricate society whirs and hums with activity.

In arcades and back alleys, vendors call out to passersby hawking their wares - goods both humble (parched legumes strung on leather thongs, glass lanterns filled with kerocene oil) and exotic (mummified rodentia no bigger than thumbnails). The men wear coarse robes dyed black-blue from fermented lichen juice; women's hair flows long and black beneath white headcloths.

One sees young brides stumbling laughing down narrow stairways under pyramidal wedding veils stiffened with sugar-water, clots of family trailing behind in formal vestments. In the shadows hooded penitents lash themselves before altarpieces daubed with crimson mineral pigment. Scampering children chase each other up stone drain channels.

From cantilevered eaves dried blood sausages hang alongside flour sacks stuffed with powder scraped from ancient seabeds far eastward. In airshaft breezes prayer flags snap emblazoned with stylized images - spiral shells, serrated knives, spiny creatures that may have once skittered here.

At nightfall smoky torchlight seeps from hearth pits to paint sinuous alleys orange-pink. The rich drone of chanting rises from a thousand directionless minarets piercing darkening skies. And strewn among it all tumbling fragments of dross remain after so many epochs - glossy beads embedded in mortar, ceramic shards flocked together with rebarbanded groundcover concrete.

It is a place equally near death and alive as any inhabited thing can be - human life clamped tenuous holdfast in uncaring bedrock planetbody like low tide barnacles grip-shelled fast against ocean currents' riprap scouring force...and just as deep an expression of planetary sentience too perhaps? All such questions dissolve shroud-like into noonday warmth-heat shimmering off intimately foreign streetscape...
