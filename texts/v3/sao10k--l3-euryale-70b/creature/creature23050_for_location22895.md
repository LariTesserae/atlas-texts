The air is thick and heavy, dripping with moisture that clings to skin and fabric. Peering through the mist-shrouded rock formations, small rivulets wind towards swelling streams, carrying hidden waters through the broken landscape. Exposed bedrock looms in grey and threatening lumps and serrations, slick under the damp. Puddles glimmer in every hollow, formed by no clear source. Moss, fern, and lichen carpet the rock faces, providing bursts of green amidst the monochrome surroundings. 

No trees have taken root, only compacted mats of moss and quivering grasses staining the ground. Strange, organically twisted forms emerge from the roots and angles of the rock itself - hunched stumps and boulders weathered into a witchwood cowering, dripping real water.

Above, an angular structure juts from a cliff face, its length-speckled windows and doorways framing only darkness. Familiar gegenstand - orange mold and tawny foliage cling to its stucco surface, vines dangling from stumps and lintels. Weeds bristle green and seed-heavy round its base, further spotted with saucer-sized flowers.

A rank track joins the samplings of detritus and scree, carrying the site's leavings and rain towards the still-slopes. From its apex, a blackened wooden stanchion rises, tattered green banners flopping listlessly atop it. Close by, shredded remnants of human presence crowd together - reclaimed wood, wire snarls, dishes and tools, tarpaulins spread canopying over platforms and bored into tunnels. The air smells of mire and run-off, pregnant with unseen presence - listening closely, subtle shiftings sound through the overgrowth - then, more audibly scratching and heaving - not wind, nor kindling wildlife, nor waning storm. Something unnameable moves in the stilled fringes.
