Hunched figures emerge from the reeking fog - goblins, stooped and malformed, their skin blotched and oozing. They wear tatters of rotted fabric draped over wiry frames barely covered in matted fur. Yellow tusks jut from jaws perpetually half-opened in slack grins. Beady red eyes gleam under brows ridged with bony growths. Knobbed hands end in hooked claws stained green.

They lope along with jerking motions, heads twisting atop elongated necks to scan the surroundings with constant paranoia, sniffing and snuffling at the saturated air. Guttural noises burp from their throats - garbled words stitched together without meaning or reason.

Here a clutch of them gathers around a bubbling pool, plunging arms elbow-deep into its thickness to scoop up squirming eels and frogs which they cram hastily into mouths already slick with drool. Chunks of flesh are torn apart by gnashing teeth while still alive and wriggling.

There a lone creature pauses before one of the leaning towers, cocking its head as it studies strange symbols etched across the surface. It prods at them thoughtfully with a talon until they begin to ooze glowing slime that drips onto its fingers and makes it cackle shrilly.

Goblins caper among themselves in spasms of manic energy punctuated by sudden fits of shrieks that sound almost like laughter but carry an undertone far more sinister. They leave trails through the mire marked by splashes of iridescent liquid that sizzle upon contact with the soil as if corrosive.

Their presence alone seems to warp everything around them subtly awry so that shadows ripple and boil behind them like oil on hot steel. Even after passing a spot remains tainted for moments afterwards, pulsing faintly against expectation.

These creatures appear to be native inhabitants perfectly adapted to this realm's aberrant conditions, thriving amidst horrors most other beings could scarce endure unbroken for minutes nevermind ages spent stewing within such eldritch environs...
