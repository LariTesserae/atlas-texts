In this sprawling mechanical valley, a peculiar mix of flora and fauna thrive amidst the hissing steampipes and humming machinery. 

Amidst the patchwork gardens, strangely lush vines and blossoming flowers grow in vibrant purples, pinks, iridescent blues that seem almost bioluminescent against the industrial grime. The plants have an otherworldly sheen to their petals and leaves - one senses they may not be entirely organic. Tendrils that reach out from some blooms end in tiny metallic tendrils and wiring. Some flowers emit soft pulses of light from glowing pollen at their centers.

Among the garden plots scurry small insectoid machines - clockwork cicadas with copper carapaces click industriously as they pollinate flowers with delicate glass proboscises tipped with micro-wire filaments. Larger metal arachnid automatons skitter along branches pruning dead growth. Hopping between dirt clods go herds of spring-legged seed dispersal units spreading seeds wrapped in synthetic spores.

From crevices between flagstones emerge shallow-digging mole-drills burrowing tunnels through the spongy aerated soil pumped full of micro-fiber nutrients to optimize plant growth. They deposit dollops of high-iron fertilizer pellets dispensed from armored thoraxes.

Hovering over the gardens flit swarms of gyrocopter-pollenators emitting airborne chemical signals that trigger ripening in select fruit pods while sterilizing others via genetic coding to enforce breed control. Luminous moths alight on giant lotus blooms trailing nanoweb gossamer wings dripping microscopic fibers harvested from silicate reeds nearby.

The original purpose behind these biomechanical hybrids eludes identification but their highly organized symbiosis is clearly centered around optimizing output for whichever industry piggybacks off them here.

Perched on latticework turrets topping the cyclopean cliffs are roosts of immense bird-mechs and dragonfly-drones with solarpainted wings almost translucent, limbs composed from braided hydralics cables - likely sentry units watching over automated smelters and recursive 3D-print manufacturing bays lining the cliff faces above.

Hive-like colonies cling to rainslicked ledges further down, throbbing with activity - insectoids of every shape scuttle amongst eachother in pulsarhythmic flashes as peristaltic waves ripple through legs locked together sharing nutrient fluids...
