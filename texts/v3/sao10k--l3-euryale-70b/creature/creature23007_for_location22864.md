This verdant, hybrid landscape is inhabited by a diverse array of lifeforms that have evolved to thrive in this singular ecosystem where organic and synthetic elements are intricately entwined. 

The floating nacelles and globes are home to volk, a sentience-caste of anaerobic blimps. The volk drift serenely through the thick mists, feeding on the gaseous nutrients and sensing the world via intricate webs of piezoelectric cilia that sample electrical and chemical signals throughout the landscape. Indigenous tribals of them have developed symbiotic relationships with the arachnoid mech leviathans, perching in the angular crooks of their carapaces and absorbing their sprayed pollen nanoparticles, which don't just enrich the soil but code design patterns into their flesh. The nacelles pulsed with bioluminescence, communicating with each other and the leviathans in shifting chromatophorematic displays.

Frilled gelslingers ooze up structures walls and nest in dew-beaded ferns. These class C intelligences have amorphic cybodies that can conform themselves to any topological form, whether plant or structure. When they slowly ooze from one configuration to another, their distributed sensorium networks produce fractal interference patterns that resonate and shimmer through the local morphic field, which many lifeforms here can sense viscerally. Their gelatinized mucus is electromagnetically conductive and nutrient enriched, and gelslingers form rudimentary networks to trade energymass and upload copious thoughtpools before decoupling to hatch and gestate new forms.

Kelp and carrageenous growths have formed dense beds in the low-lying wetter areas, anchored to structure membranes. Mingled among their boughs are colonies of the fay, glimpses of them flicking between branches at speeds imperceptible to human canons. Each fay is in itself distributed, consisting of a cloud of 10^4 motile cells, yet they act collectively in "display moments" to commute and fuse into small-time personas to interling with other lifeforms. The fay cells are deftly clever and no doubt have developed symbiotic interfaces with the ecology, but their inscrutable sociality and timesensory modes evade the gaze of xenologers.

Certain strains of mycelium have developed synapses to both organic and synthetic plektomorphisms, becoming the largest lifeform in the ecosystem and a silent, omnipresent information service between all the other lifeforms. Their ionic and quantum fluxes serve as commuters, even allowing the particulate fay and the volk and gelslingers to memex, in an informational, non-corporeal way. Some khora of mycelia has developed k-wisdom cores, hosting archaelogical incrustations of forgotten sentiences, but the degree remains speculative and those mycelia are seldom encountered.

The landscape as a whole can be thought of as a gestaltselbst, a singular quantum-organismic entity perceived by the kaleidoscopic operations of its lifeforms to be alive, commuting the abundance and dasein of the local universe and translating it through memes lexicons of transcendant informatic processes.
