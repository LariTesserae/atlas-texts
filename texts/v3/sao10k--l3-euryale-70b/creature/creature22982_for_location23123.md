What resides within this decaying hellscape? If anything can be said to live amidst such pervasive entropy and forsaken abomination... 

Debased creatures, perhaps, eking out an unlife on twisted legs of sinew and obsidian shards. Bipedal constructs lurch forward with jerky motions, eyes glowing in dark cavities of fleshless skulls. Whispers crackle through lipless mouths - eldritch utterances that scrape against sanity's fragile membrane.

Crawling maws emerge from cracks between jagged boulders, all gaping toothy jaws and undulating tentacles glistening with bioluminescent ichor. These feral beasts shred the very fabric of space-time as they skitter across planes never meant for mortal perception. Their mad gibbering sets the hair standing on nonexistent necks.

Amorphous blobs slither unseen between depressive crevices, ameboid masses of churning chaos coalesced around black holes of nothingness. Amoeba-like pseudopods feel blindly at the air while unblinking compound eyes drink in spectra alien to known biology. Blind hunger drives them ever onward to write cursed sigils upon tortured stone.

Rotting placental sacs split open in showers of deliquescing flakes as new horrors claw squalling from pulpy wombs. The gaunt proto-creatures suckle queasily at drooping teats dribbling pus-like secretions.
