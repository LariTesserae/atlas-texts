Across this bleak hellscape march the mechromorphs, skittering ant-like robots, their chitinous metal carapaces clacking and scuttling in perfect, ruthless lockstep. Bladed mandibles glint in the hammering desert sun as they prowl the crumbled remains of an ancient factory-city.

Through the rusting hulks of boiler rooms and dynamo halls, over femurs and skulls strewn from blast blast bunkers, the machine ant army strides ever on. Goutlets of viscous machine oil ooze from their joints, staining the cracked desert floor.

Atop a crumbling turreted mound squats the Bevmill, a cyclopean titan of whirring blades and dripping pipes. Rad windows wink like smoldering eyes as it pivots to face the plain, lowermodel plasma calve lancing out like a searing tongue. Arcs of actinic light burn sacred runes into the bedrock - glyphs of abundance in a forgotten kami-tongue.

With a ground-shaking rumble, the Bevmill lumbers on toward a vast excavation pit - what was once the estuarian harbor before the world dried. Towering above it, the loader mech quakes the earth, then upends its seething bulk, disgorging a molten torrent of ketchup-hued copper ore. The thick sludge pours into the pit, glowing like the embers of a dying fire.

Fossil cliffs of fractured quartz frame this apocalyptic tableau, carved from the desiccated remnants of an ancient sea floor. Chitinous exoskeletons of forgotten sea beasts stud the cliff-face, armor-plated arthropods, crank-shelled ammonites and krill - a graveyard of taxonomic nightmares. Their petrified shells have the alien grace of black mollusk casts.

From the deepest stratum of stone sprawl crystalline fronds of basalt glass - the glass roots of plastic bramble seeking the oldest memories. Tallow light spills from sigils etched in the crystal core, as bioluminescing runes unscroll from the ancient stone like sigils from a rancid scroll. The crab-like light limns the waste in pulsing patterns, like the sciatic flicker of deep krill.

All hail the miracle of the Twenty-First Taste! By cunning memex of the basal stratum, the machine-continuum siphons the calories preserved in the fossil record into a moly-chlorophyl ooze. This ichor now binds the stylized tail of this bleach-caustic world - the last krill of the saltflat bask in it like langostines in butterscotch shattered to scallops still redolent with decalctate peptoids.
