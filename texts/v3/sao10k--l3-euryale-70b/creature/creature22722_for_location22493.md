The megastructure's empty expanse is populated by echoes and shadows of a forgotten past. The crystalline geometry, so vast and grand, now resonates with an eerie silence. Dim pulsing lights filter through the perpetual mist, illuminating the ethereal landscape with a mournful luminescence. 

Amidst the vast emptiness, the organic pods cling to the framework like cooled lava caulping spent rockets. Some of them have cracked open, spilling their contents onto the cold surfaces. The life that burst forth from these pustules has long since transmuted away, leaving behind only phosphorescent stains and the faintest residue of exotic ions. 

Yet still, the great arched perceptors that foreran the world stare ceaselessly into the void. These cyclopean formations, built of crystalline memory wiped clean of remembrance, gaze outwards with a petrified awareness. They seem intent on capturing some distant signal beyond the range of lesser minds, some gossamer thread of continuity that might still connect the world to its departed creators. Every stellar Age, a pulse rises in them, and a distant call joins the cosmic overflow, a plaintive cry of greeting into an indifferent galaxy.

Scant technical residue shimmers in starry latticework to the frozen eye bubbles, the last impressions of a technology of effortless transformation. Such production can still happen here, but only rarely; spontaneous capillary actions channel beamed mass-flux into the megastructure's growth nodes to engorge atrociously twisted clades of wizened computational and aphysical matter. Then, once more, the emplaced traffic ordinance will wake and contest such wretched populations, mulching them. And the megastructure will be dominantly vacant again.

Most of the significant productions that once gave vibrance to this strange platform have flensed away, and are now but a tinge and a phantam. The basic/dominant (*(un)stable*) flux that statisticians dub "energymass," the sum of all productive agency that brought forth stars and worlds, is near absent here now. The Great Wastes have isolated this place, and only fragments remain of its glory. The cyclopean watchers are the sole remnants of a great project to come to fruition through energymass outlay, their as-instead-of connective seeding-, streaming-, and sieving- Dezignant, their florilegion tart, all but lost.
