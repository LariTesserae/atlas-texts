The lush highland strata is alive with teeming biomes of flora and fauna. Mutated tree-form creatures loom overhead, trunks the width of a house, covered in scaly bark inscribed with glowing glyphs. Their vast, undulating leaves drip strands of iridescent sap that harden into delicate filaments, forming shimmering webs between branches. These webs are home to clouds of colorful winged creatures the size of birds, but shaped like translucent starfish. They float lazily and emit musical humming sounds.

Great twisted vines as thick as ships' ropes snake across ravines and up cliffsides. Bulbous nodules along the vines pulse gently and sometimes split open to reveal swirling fractal patterns that appear almost screen-like before closing again with soft puffs of fragrant spores.

Bioluminescent moss carpets stone faces and glows in abstract shapes that seem to morph meaningfully - one moment appearing to spell words or depict constellations, the next dissolving into nonsensical Rorschach blots.

Zooplankton swarm in churning river currents, electric blue and violet sparks dancing between them as they drift in hypnotic schools. Great pale serpentine forms glide beneath the surface trailing pearlescent wakes that refract rainbows on the banks.

Lichen splattered rocks ring softly when stepped upon, releasing puffs of glittering silver motes that swirl hypnotically in spiraling vortexes before settling back onto the ground. Peering closer reveals minute insectoid beings etched into every square inch of surface, writhing together in endless loops.

Everywhere there seems some underlying pulsebeat thrumming through all living things - a subtle electromagnetic aura visible only at certain angles, like an omnipresent prismatic mist clinging close against skin and fur. Some intuited connection tying all life together without need for sight or sound beyond a shared fundamental resonance.

Most ambient wildlife are small herbivorous mammals darting furtively among undergrowth - furry bipeds hopping on knuckle-like hind legs, long snouts twitching over muzzles lined with fine tendrils to taste-air currents; sleek centauroids browsing rich nutrient-rich soil for buried fruits; towering sloths moving ponderously from branch to branch on ropy prehensile tails...

Only glimpsed occasionally through breaks in foliage are behemoth sauropod analogues grazing placidly upon miles-high kelp towers swaying gently in headwinds wafting off distant shining ranges blanketed white with alpine snowfields. The great reptiles' hides shimmer iridescence like oil on water and ripple subtly as if lit from within by bioluminiscent tattoos depicting scenes from mythological histories.

Reports speak also of rarer apex predators stalking silent as ghosts through deeper reaches - huge mantis-shrimp analogues prowling nimbly amid gloomy arthropodal forests; burrowing worm-beasts contracting wave-like through sun-blasted sandwastes; immense cloud-dwelling hydrae raining lightning upon anything drawing too near their soaring domains...

But such rumors are rarely verified beyond tantalizing glimpses seen sidelong by wide-eyed explorers who return haunted-yet-hopeful accounts that set imaginations afire.with wonder at secrets yet unfound within this alien wilderness...
