What dwells within this desolate expanse? What manner of life could cling so tenaciously to every crack and crevice, defying wind and sun's pitiless glare?

Tiny mammals scurry across shadow-dappled stone - marmots, cliff-chipmunks, lizards darting forth from their high perches to snatch hapless insects. Pigeons roost in inaccessible alcoves, cooing mournfully to one another. Pocket gophers tunnel invisibly beneath the loamy detritus that gathers at the base of each monolith.

But behold - movement! High on a pillar's face, a fox peers out warily from his den, eyes glinting russet-red in afternoon light. Thick-pelted and minimal in build for swiftness, he hunts small game in the upper reaches where few dare tread. Below him, a squirrel scampers along a ledge-root system before ducking into a knothole bolt-hole.

A raven wheels overhead in slow circles, croaking once before settling onto an outcropping. His black feathers absorb what feeble warmth seeps from aged stone. He watches with soulful eyes for mice and pocket-mice scampering among root tangles far below.

Lichen spreads feathery growth like spatulate fingers probing for purchase against the rockfaces battered by untold winds. Tufa moss cushions hidden hollows with soft green carpeting upon which butterflies alight to drink sweet nectar from nodding florets of bloodstone gentian.

Twin peregrine falcon nestlings peer over the edge of their eyrie cavity high on the cresting spine of some giant fanglike pinnacle. Gape-beaked they keen for food while their parents soar tirelessly above the rift-valleys far below seeking prey for them to devour alive and dripping with vital ichor...
