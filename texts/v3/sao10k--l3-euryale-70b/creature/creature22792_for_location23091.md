In this desolate industrial sprawl, twisted creatures weld themselves together from scavenged parts - gears, wires, rusted pipes, hunks of corroded metal. Misshapen cyborgs stumble through the streets, their flesh fused with machinery. Bulbous growths sprout from their backs, pulsating with strange fluids. Gnarled limbs end in serrated blades instead of hands. Cyclopean eyes glow an eerie orange behind goggles cobbled together from shattered glass and scraps of mirrors.

These abominations are the products of mad science conducted within the city's hulking factories. Necromancers and cyber-alchemists labor day and night to craft unspeakable monsters using forbidden knowledge stolen from lost civilizations. Reanimated corpses march forth bearing terrible weapons - bolted-on machine guns, built-in flamethrowers, chainsaws fused into throats that vomit molten steel shrapnel.

Eldritch things lurk in the noxious fogs pumping from chimneys and smokestacks tall enough to violate clouds. Formless horrors writhe between dimensions in scum-encrusted puddles fed by the city's endless effluent runoff. At night they slither out to feast on any hapless drifter unlucky enough to wander into their domain unaware.

Rust-eaten robots patrol the perimeter atop cyclopean walls girdling the city, crushing intruders beneath relentlessly treading feet or perforating them with streams of molten lead expelled from arm-mounted cannons. Mechanical sentries scan for unauthorized biological life-signatures with lidars beamed from glowing optics.

Nowhere else clings madmen exiled for crimes against science too heinous for even this blighted metropolis: alchemists whose transmutations went awry leaving them melded into grotesque amorphous blobs; vivisectors who grafted unnatural animal hybrids that tore free of their cages and devoured their creators; technopaths who uploaded their minds into machines but can find no other host than a tangle of severed human limbs jerking in a vat beneath a crackling Van De Graaff generator...

A rare few among these fugitives manage to survive on society's fringes - trappers and poachers preying upon escaped lab specimens; cultists worshipping giant squamous worms oozing through storm drains pulping victims alive; enclaves of mutants subsisting on toxic algae-scums scraped from radioactive ponds... But most are dragged down into hidden dungeons under those cyclopean factories where they're buried alive or used as components in ever-more depraved experiments until nothing remains but anguish-wracked meat-slabs writhing on stainless steel tables as eldritch energies course through electrified shackles...

And yet morbid life still fights for survival here, woven amongst gears and circuit boards like vines creeping between cracks in an ancient ruin. Insects lay eggs inside hollow cyborg shells. Fungi sprouts amongst mounds of rags rotting into compost heaps fed by rain sluicing down piles of refuse dumped outside factory gates after uselessly beggaring it for anything salvageable... Some ill-begotten hybridization endures half-seen amidst puffs of flame jetting from splitting rivets... Even now something not quite extinguished scratches desperately at mute concrete amidst eddies stirred up by trickles splashing down uneven gutters bubbling overfull... In such ways does nature struggle vainly to overcome entropy triumphant...
