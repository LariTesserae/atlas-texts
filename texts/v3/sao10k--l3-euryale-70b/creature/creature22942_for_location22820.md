With each groaning turn of their mechanical cores, the ferns march forward on brazen roots, their decaying fallen predecessors dissolving into a nutrient-rich sludge that feeds the pools.

From the scummed surfaces of the ponds rise the Clockwork Koi - ichthyomorphic automata glistening with verdigris scales. Coppery fins and gears propel them effortlessly through the murky waters, while their lidless eyes gleam opalescent beneath the wavering luminance of the fungal growths. The Koi's gaping maws are lined with ensnared teeth of pearl. Occasionally, a Koi surges entirely from the shallows and with brazen antlers and flailing propellers launches itself into a brief, clanking flight over the river of vapors.
