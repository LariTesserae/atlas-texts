Across the harsh, wind-scoured plateau, a few hardy creatures manage to eke out an existence in this rugged landscape. Scrappy tufts of wiry grass and spiky brush valiantly cling to every crevice and ledge, providing a tenuous foothold against the howling winds and fickle weather. Hardy insects scuttle about the sparse vegetation - ants and beetles in black, brown, and iridescent colors. Lizards and snakes, stealthy hunters, stalk this prey between cracks in the splintered stone.

Soaring high overhead, riding the thermals on broad wings, are pterosaurs of many kinds. Flying reptiles the size of eagles, vultures, and condors wheel around pinnacles and crags, ever vigilant for prey scurrying below on the rock or revealed midair. Massive pterochthys are pack beasts of burden, enough to bear the weight of human riders. Teams of the leathery creatures labor under the whims of their masters, wings thrumming the aether as they displace great volumes of air with each powerful beat.

Humans live here too, though not without cost. Pack Lords, millions of chimerae bondmates grafted together into single beings, control the skies on the backs of the pterosaurs. Their intelligent eyes, nestled in heads of five, ten, or more faces, stare out with cold generalship and hidden languor. Talons of feathers, chitin, bones, and alloyed whiskerbars reach out from their many limbs to prehensile wings on their various beasts.

The Chimerae are an ancient and secret race, a dozen species so utterly remade by some primeval art that they can slave themselves in symbiosis of muscle, mind and spirit to become one amorphous being. Their old names are deep archival lore, unknown to most. The Skarlion, the Griffolith, the seraphic Tirthak - hosts of old names and dead gods pushed under new skies.

Soaring on pterosaur-back with eyes on chrome prisms to analyse the horizon, pack-lords peer through silver eyeslits from the wrapped forwards of their animals' snouts towards the metropolis... to discern the polity before them, of which all this plateau is but a high threshold. Disputes rise in their minds, along astral channels carried by comehither fungals and the pterosaurs' ultramicro dwarves.

The pack-lord, Griffolithus Rex, master of the throne of the Combine, settles on pinnacle's ledge where alloy bone shifts to seat himself and his beasts. He points appraising claws towards the conurbation, trimmed in its fires and lo singer furnaces, surrounded by the geometrical tithe-camp of the automatons. Currents of conversation well from his telephoning gizzard, military intel of spiniform lumen, representatives blazemelds to logister eagles, analog males to spin chlorine coolant, the Inter(relay) automation pylons assembled nadir, and ebon zyme-petas aggregations.

"I distrust them," says Griffolithus, hisiones ducts finally speak as his releasers synchronize, "the copse-brothers. I know they would rather synthesize the noble Tirthak as much as myself if they could, though my five fathers saw an opening to ensure my throne via the Great Laws of Variation." The mental diction of his fellows emits in groaks of assent. "Rex Skarlion needs to learn what their governors intend... And that the Team council of Tirthak profit from it but will try to re-suckle on my crown!"
