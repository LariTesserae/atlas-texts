The Swell teems with life in primordial abundance, though its denizens move languidly, as if the very air slows their progress. Frogs and toads of improbable colors hop and slither between the base of the reed clumps. Jewel-toned carps dapple the channels, scales shifting across spectra as they flick lazily after mosquitoes and dragonflies. Gila monsters bask half-submerged by algae-tufa banks, spiky tails switching occasionally.

Cassowariesforage in the shallower pools on clawed feet, sinuous necks dipping like swans to uproot tubers. Herd upon herd of capybaras graze on emergent stems, rodent teeth ripping strips of cellulose. Caimans float log-like near weed mats at the water's edge, eyes and snout just poking above the spare rippled surface. Occasionally a gator bellows from deep within the morass or wallows loudly in the mud.

Monstrous catfish lurk in recesses where stream beds undercut banks - whiskered mouths gaping wide enough to swallow a child whole. Snakes move with hypnotic sinuosity over water-lilied surfaces and between thigh-thick stalks of reed - boas constricting capuchin monkeys eating fruit in tree islands; anacondas ambushing tapirs drinking at bends in rivulets; fer-de-lances poised arrow-like, ready to strike at wading Egret legs.

Jabirus wrench open shells on exposed muddy flats while roseate spoonbills strain small fry through spatulate mandibles dunking into shallows. Toucans knap open hard palm fruits with great bills in glades kept clear of flood debris by army ant raids led by giant warrior females wielding serrated jaws.

Bromeliad flowers bloom in blazing petal-faces atop air-roots anchored epiphytically among buttressed trunks and domatia hanging down from branches - flamingos nodulose heads probing water-platter leaves for mosquito larvae breeding in meristematic cups -- while hummingbird tongues drink sweet sap from macaws piercing buds with curved ivory horns...

It goes on forever...
