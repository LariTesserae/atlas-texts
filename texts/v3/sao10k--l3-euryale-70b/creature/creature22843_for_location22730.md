Across this blasted hellscape, twisted forms emerge from the tortured rock - beings born of this unforgiving world, shaped by its cruel whims. They stalk between the towering pillars and crumbling buttes, their bodies baked by ages under the scorching sun. 

Reddish skin peels back to reveal pulsating flesh beneath, throbbing in time with unknown rhythms. Eyes gleam like chips of obsidian set deep into elongated skulls. Distended limbs end in claws of fused bone, tipped with barbs that catch the light as they move through the shimmering haze.

Some shamble upright on two legs while others lurch forward on all fours, leaving trails of ichor-slick footprints behind them in the cracked earth. Leathery wings sprout from broad backs, beating listlessly against the stagnant air until it whips into stinging grit-filled clouds.

They are solitary creatures for the most part, communicating in grunts and guttural growls that echo off canyon walls. But sometimes they gather at nightfall around flickering bonfires kindled from arborescent seed husks. Twitching shadows play across their faces as they share dark secrets and bloody histories passed down through generations. 

At times a shrill cry rings out across the barren flats as one turns upon another in vicious combat over access to rare seep springs or choice patches of fungal hyphae clinging to sheltered crevices. The victors feed messily on chunks of stringy flesh wrenched free with snapping jaws and slashing claws.

Such is life on this dying world - a struggle for survival against an indifferent universe, a desperate dance among beings moulded by cruelty and hardship into things coldly efficient at killing and devouring so they may live another day amidst the crimson sands...
