Beneath the twisted roots of the ancient tree, an entire microcosm of strange life thrives. Bioluminescent fungi and eerie globe lanterns cast an diffuse glow across the damp, rocky crevice. The wizened creatures foraging in the fungal growths and leaf litter appear almost like living toadstools themselves, their bristly hair matted with spores and tiny insects clambering amongst it.

As one probes deeper into the hidden pocket dimension, the ruined remnants of an old village slowly resolve from the tangled shadows. Crumbling stone walls and deep-green, thorny creepers hide tiny doorways and window frames behind sickle-leafed vines and fallen rafters. The air is heavy with the earthy smells of age and the cloying dampness of hundreds of years.

Peering from the shadows are gnarled faces, twisted by millenia of life confined in this strangling ecosphere. Eyes glint in the fungal gloom, reflecting with an eerie similarity the bioluminescence. It seems almost as if these wretched souls have become one with the uncanny gliwth that binds this place, fed and filled with the same dim emerald fire.

Tiny, pale children scuttle amongst the ruins on all fours, their movements quick and fluid like cave-dwelling insects. They cackle and whisper in a canting language, their sallow skin glowing faint-green in moments of revelry. One vomits a stream of fungal hyphae to sabotage a particularly rapacious sister's poaching of shrimplike creatures lurking in the crevice pools.

The adults, wizened far beyond their years by the stagnant strangeness of this pocket-realm, shuffle between fungal gardens, cultivating their luminescent crops with far more care than one would expect of such barbarous-seeming folk. Some appear to have mushrom gills sprouting from their necks and wrists, exhaling puffs of ethereal fungal spores. Others' arms dangle pendulous and atrophied, covered in warts and swirling fungal forests.

Seated on a throne woven from gnarled roots and ex-peasant furniture for is the Veteran - the twisted, ambulatory tree itself. Branches writhing and wriggling into boney, vertebrae-like fusions and matted in dank foliage, countless thousands of years stretched the boundaries of vegetation into something altogether more motile, atavistic. At its apex sprouts a mass of fungal growths couching a glowing, pulsing node - an ambulatory hub of pure mycelium, alive with sparks of disciplined power.

The Veteran has wholly taken the village into its sway, assimilating folk and stone alike into its writhing, animating woody mass. As the fungal hyphae once ripened them, then, so the last-dwellers are now held and fed by the creature-growth that had clawed them into tree-flesh. Walking trunks for legs, branch-arms throughout the ecosphere, pulsing mycelium-eyes and glowing ventricles, this is the fist of nature, the last of the village surving in barely-residue form, one with the monstrous singularity that holds them.

In the end, there is just this; a sentient-vegetation thing, grown from the ruin and the remnants of folk once human into a something new, something more, a perfectly collonial ecosystem of shades and shadows, a pocket-continuum of uncanny and phantasmal life coalesced beneath the Veteran'sDom, some last-dwelling first-wrath of the primeval, aftermath of the great assimilation.
