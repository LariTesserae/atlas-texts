The Tropemont region teems with an abundance of strange and wondrous lifeforms perfectly adapted to its unique environment.

Along the mist-shrouded hillside streams, swarms of iridescent dragonflies dart and zip, their bioluminescent abdomens flashing hypnotically. They lay their eggs in the spongy mud of temporary eddies and backwater pools where their voracious larvae feast on microorganisms before emerging to begin the cycle anew.

Perched on moss-draped boulders overlooking clear plunges in the river, three-toed sloths lounge nonchalantly, even upside-down. Their coarse pelts are colonized by entire ecosystems - tiny frogs make nests in their fur, while insects burrow through the dense coat like termites. 

Flocks of giant green macaws wheel overhead, raucous screeching echoing off the canyon walls as they search for fruiting trees amidst the jungle canopy below. Occasionally one will plummet from hundreds of feet up to spear a fish right out of the rapids with its powerful hooked bill.

Amidst thick curtains of Spanish moss drooping between tree limbs, troop after troop of howler monkeys swing nimbly through the canopy, pausing only briefly to tear chunks from huge palm fronds which they deftly stuff into cheek pouches.

Peccaries root around in muddy stream beds searching for aquatic plants and insects with keen snouts. Sometimes a jaguar watches motionless from a thicket - then explodes from hiding in a burst of speed to seize its prey. More often it simply observes impassively until driven away by buzzing flies attracted to its musk.

Everywhere vines and aerial roots dangle down from above - some filaments reaching dozens or even hundreds of yards across stream valleys themselves wrapped around tangles of liana vines like highways connecting treehouses between mountains. These act as pathways for troops of squirrel-like primates that leap fearlessly across them as others startle warily when these visitors cross between branches far below them still securely fastened in place by complex networks of vines festooned all about.
