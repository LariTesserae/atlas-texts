High in those mistswept mountainsides, where stone and green entwine,
Ancient secrets whisper through gnarled branches old.
Beneath the loam lie bones of beasts now lost to legend -
Cyclopean forms whose names echo only faint.

These elder beings once strode forth upon the heights
As living gods who laughed beneath their braided crowns.
With eyes aglow like embers smoldering deep inside
They watched the rise of kingdoms sprawling leagues around.

Their wisdom older than the first spring's thaw had stirred 
The sap within these trees which cling tenaciously
To seams and cracks carved eons past when titans warred;
And roots delve deeper seeking hidden aquifers.

Yet see how delicately they tend this alpine realm!
Maintaining balance lest too many take hold here:
A natural order woven round each peak and dell -  
Every niche occupied with purposeful design,

From sentinel pines piercing heavens dark above,
Through ferny glades dappled by filtered light,
Down gloaming ravens where streams plunge, white-foamed love,
Unto dim caverns echoing with dripping night...

All partakes of an intricate web spun across
By myriad minds both subtle and profounder still:
Each species playing role in grand symphony composed
Of interdependent parts forever twining till

No boundary exists tween earth and air up here...
Nor lines dividing life from death nor waking dream.
For magic flows free betwen mortal men draw near...
