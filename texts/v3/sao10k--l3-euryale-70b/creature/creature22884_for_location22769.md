Beneath wavy tangles of tubular stems, their nodes crushed between pulsing fistules of gelid fibers, a colossal entity lurks in the depths of the Luminous Abyss Grotto. At first glance it appears a conflux of twisted metal and writhing organic matter - an abomination born from some maddening catastrophe. Protrusions of rusted pipes spew steam that wafts like the breath of a dead, mechanical titan. At the center of this swirling mass of tendrils and tubes, a bulbous shape rises atop wavering stalks - possible the entity's head or centerpiece.

This central mass whirs and clicks with grinding gears and sparking circuitry. Luminescent sparks dance across its surface like strange lightning, casting eldritch light on the bioluminescent sea life drifting by. Its form avian, but grotesquely deformed, the entity's wings spread wide, their feathers replaced by tattered strips ofattered metal sheeting. One wing juts at a cruel angle, scored by oxidation. The other drags against the atoll substrate as the creature shifts, scraping barnacles and glowing fungi from the rocks.

Beneath its cradled head, a face that seems the merger of pigeon and squid leers into the depths with unblinking lidless eyes. Beak half scraped away exposes rows of needle-like teeth. The facial skin hangs in flaps, hat little intact flesh still remaining webbed over an exoskeleton of pulsing wires. Ooze dribbles from the torn skin tags, spiraling away on the currents.

Its gaze roves past the glowing seaweed fronds, past the schools of darting light-fish, ignoring their flashing scales. The grotto's other inhabitants - the outlandish mollusks, the jewel-toned echinoderms - scatter before this titan's progress, knowing well its unreasoning cruelty. It regards them as ephemera, mere mortal creatures, subject to flensing and melting into its maw if they catch its capricious attention. Its attentions are elsewhere, for now. Eternal and ceaseless, feeding the rigid, subaqueous coil of the Luminous Abyss Grotto with mannacled weight of its consciousness.
