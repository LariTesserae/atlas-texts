Amidst the dense, suffocating jungle, life abounds in a chaotic tangle of vegetation and writhing creatures. Everywhere you look; vines dangle from the canopy, throttling the trees while strangling any opening that dares form. Gigantic centipedes with chitinous exoskeletons and gleaming mandibles scurry silently along the ground, only to be swarmed by beetles packing the air in a monstrous cloud. Noise permeates the thick, humid air as lizards and amphibians croak and chitter, with oversized frogs leaping with alarming velocity amidst the underbrush. 

You sense, but fail to perceive the abominations squirming unseen within the mire and detritus, as heavy petrichor billows through the air. The stench of damp fungal growth and decaying vegetation is overwhelming, rising in cloying waves that coat the back of the throat and cling to everything they touch.

Hints of civilization, now long forgotten, poke through the rampant foliage - deep-grooved tracks in the churned earth and mashed plantlife everywhere, beaten down by the passage of beasts untold. Carnivores or prey, it matters not to the jungle, which devours all in it's path. Rampant life seeks to peel away, to digest, to forget any trace of what once squatted trespassers on it's territory.

Peeking and peering from the mists and mists that cling to the overgrown stones and twisted boles, reptilian eyes glimmer with feral hunger. Here a sauroposeidon, sending soul-chilling cries echoing through the vale as it begins a hunt that never ends. There a pack of dead-eyed carnosaurs, circling like sharks, tracking their prey. Iridescent serpents and crocodiles lurk in every flash-flood-cut gully, scaled armor glinting in thinned sunlight.

Yet even the most loathsome and ferocious beasts know their place - they are merely another thread in the jungle's vast and glittering dissonance. This is a land that defies order or dominion and submits to none but it's own implacable will to live and devour without cease. Any thing that dares approach is swiftly absorbed into the churning chaos, churned and recycled into baruyes and hydra's teeth. Life and death and rebirth cycle eternally here, in this place of unquiet equipoise, where every green and slithering thing reaches for every other in a ceaseless mesh of tooth and claw and tendvile.
