In this vast underwater metropolis suspended over an endless abyss, an intricate web of life pulses and thrives. The angular megastructures loom like alien skyscrapers, their unknown alloys glinting faintly in the deep gloom. A cacophony of sounds reverberate through the dense water - the drone of fusion reactors, the chug chug of pumps, the hiss of compressed gases and ozone generators.

Amongst the labyrinthine corridors and habitats dwell a motley populace. Humans rub shoulders with artificially-engineered hominids grown in vats to serve specific roles. Backwards-compatible Neanderthals toil side by side with uplifted great apes put to work as manual laborers and technicians. Genetically optimized "super soldiers" patrol the outer dykes and bulwarks, rifles at the ready.

Amphibious hybrids slide through airlocks into worker pods festooning the hulls. Their torsos ripple with extra musculature suited for crushing pressure. Webbed fingers ending in manipulator digits deftly operate control pannels and tool arms projecting from central cores. Sockets gape behind their eyes awaiting probe jacks for neural interfacing with monitors and sensors.

Service drones flit between worker quarters dispensing nutrients and pharmaceuticals from hydraulic syringes protruding beneath segmented carapaces marked with corporate sigils - Pharmaxa Systems, Neurosyntec, Cygnus Biomechanicals...QR codes blink on their polymer shells inviting authorized access.

Throughout open plazas people mingle amidst holographic advertisements flickering like wil-o-the-wisps beckoning shoppers to corporate storefronts hawking exotic proteins harvested in bioreactor tanks lining pedestal-like external pylons. Merchants dicker over bales of cellulose grown in aeroponics gardens perfused with repurposed regolith scrubbed clean of heavy metals.

In gleaming higher-class residential areas citizens lounge on gel-cushions before windows overlooking nebulous kilometers of emerald algal paste bubbling through translucent piping under UV growlights reflected off mirrorized windowpanes across corridors perforated by freshwater conduits misting out fine aerosols fragrant as peaches ripening under a digital sun perpetually setting behind a painted sky rapant above them casting Tropicalis Hyperborea's sylvan deserts into amber relief...

But outside those hallowed bowels something stirs in stygian depths plied by machines that never sleep...
