The crumbling citadel and sprawling ruins of the ancient stone city are inhabited by a variety of creatures, each adapted to the harsh, polluted environment in their own ways. 

The jagged-edged ramparts are patrolled by carrion birds, hopping from rubble heap to rubble heap in search of carrion and rotting matter to feed on. Their twisted, mutated forms have become fused to the rust and corrosion of the rusty ramparts itself, their scrawny avian bodies shot through with jagged scales of oxidized metal.

Deep within the black shale mines, small, porterous beings forage for glints of metal among the collapsed tunnels. These beings, known as "scuttlers", are covered in armored chitin plates and bristle with venom-tipped quills. Their bulbous eyes are red in the dark as they feel their way through the shattered rock with sensory tentacles. Acrid fluids drip from gills syphoning the stagnant air for oxygen.

Lurking in the shadowed niches of the screeching tunnel walls are the gargoyles - statues of petrified nightmares so ancient that the line between stone and flesh long ago blurred. The statues are covered in slime and mold, their open maws perpetually drooling strands of mucus. Only their eyes move, following any living prey that trespasses past their watchful vigil. Those unfortunates are met with a shriek that sends stone fragments tumbling and the gargoyle taking gliding flight to devour with churning jaws.

Heaps of fossils and ancient bones on the tunnel floors bear mute witness to the calamities and epochs of life that once thrived during the "ancient suns" long-set. Plesiosaur vertebrae, megaladon teeth, trilobite shells, and impossibly large fossilized dung all mingle in stratified layers. Most common are the remains of the long-lost Evokers, a species that once dominated this land and left behind their ruins and mutations.

On the blasted periphery of the ruins, packs of hyenids prowl the flashing sinkholes where the trenches collapse into bubbling sinkholes and caverns. These hyenids are not truly hyena-like but have evolved to assume a similar niche. Their heads are elongated into lolling snouts bristling with tusks. Perpetual hunger keeps them chasing down any morsel to glut their gullets. In lulls between hunting, the hyenids babble half-remembered amulets and revile the gargoyles in shrill howls at the sky. The gargoyles shriek back in mockery as the sickly ash-choked winds howl over both of them.

Throughout the ruins, other malign beings lurk in every shadowed crevice and rubble pile - insects, rodents, lizards, and things more strange, all swinging in the niches of a grim and primeval ecology of scavengers and refugee creatures just barely scratching out a living among the layered fossil-haunted ruins of a planet trapped in the death throes of a once lush world as the suns set forever in a ash-choked sky.
