Dwelling in the miasmic gloom of this eldritch environ are countless abominations born of mud and mire. Squamous, membranous creatures writhe through the slurry, leaving trails of viscous ichor in their wake. Blind and deaf, they navigate by nascent echolocation, clicking and whistling eerie patterns that resound from petrified branches.

Amphibious beasts lurch from oily pool to oozy bog on multi-jointed limbs. Their hides are pustulent sacs of bladders inflating and deflating to propel them forward in herky jerky motions. Raw and bleeding mouths lined with hooks gnash mindlessly.

Flitting between fungal caps flaccid winged insects buzz with ravenous hunger, drawn to pulsing ovular growths debouching segmented larvae. Biolumescent spores fill the air - when mating, the beetles couple explosively, joining bodies sparking showers of fertile seed.

In crevices of ruinous walls blind wormy things pulse and prowl, snuffling reptilian heads rising questingly at any disturbance. Inertial gelatinous bulk surges behind them as they plow through congealed sediment hunting electric fish.

Furled stalk-eyes swivel atop tall fungal-sprouting necks surveying microcosm peripheries for change or motion against the ruddy background -- bones plated in scabbed keratin - hooked digits knotting nervously at anticipation of attack from milieus.

Benthic scavengers rooted into curdling loam articulate multiple limbs with prehensile hairlike filaments to snatch Anything caught between cephalopod claws - radula sawing flesh fibers into slurried chyme...

All life cycles in obscene feasts of consumption from one novel alimentary opening to another as digestion cycles nutrients back to twitching muck. All manner of unblinking eyes gaze out upon this seething primordial scene: rheumy reptilian orbs tracking movement; compound insectoid lenses glittering opalescent; even dimly sentient clots of ooze gaze out blindly with thousands of simple photoreceptors.

The landscape thrums with all these myriad lifecycles interlocked like organs in a monstrous body - eaters eating eaters eating eater-eaters, forever cycling elements betweeen mud-food-flesh-stone-water-air-mist... Each entity a puzzle-piece fitting perfectly snug against its neighbors in an ecosystem locked tight against intrusion by anything alien. Only mutations lucky (or unlucky) enough to find some novel niche might survive to reproduce before all spaces are filled again.

Everywhere the same red ambient noise pulses between every surface - a kindred cadence that sets a tempo for the whole dire panorama while individual organisms insert their own tones into eerie harmony... Braying howls between branching pipes; subliminal clicks echoing across membranes; burbles ascending from their bladders; grunts nuzzling ovules; keening birth-shrieks bursting clusters... An infernal symphony of sound belched by a hellish pipe-organ made entirely of meat..
