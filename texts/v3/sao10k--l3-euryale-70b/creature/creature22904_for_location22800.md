The dwellers of this desolate landscape are ancient entities utterly alien to human comprehension. Their forms defy description, twisting and writhing like living Rorschach tests. One glimpsed movement suggests arachnid limbs, another serpentine coils, a third pulsating pseudopodia. But they remain shrouded in roiling murk, allowing only maddening flashes of eldritch anatomies.

These beings are aeons old, older than stars. They predate concepts like animate and inanimate matter. In an unfathomable gestation eons ago they birthed from the primordial chaos, coalescing into semi-corporeal shells to interface with the emerging material universe.

They sense their environment through means beyond electro-magnetic spectrum and sound waves. The microscopic granularity of dust motes and temperature gradients are enough for them to navigate and hunt through this wasteland.

Their "eyes" detect distortions in space-time itself wherever a warm-blooded creature stumbles into their domain. A herd-beast gluing damp fur across their path would earn only mild interest. But a human mind aflame with novelty-seeking Sapience curiosity? That's cause for fascination.

Ripples of blasphemic geometries undulate beneath their phantasmagoric hides as they secrete ectoplasmic secretions in a parody of excitement. This new meat-brain 3-D printer before them is practically spewing out novel thought-forms! How delightful!

But impatience is alien to these patient immortals. This worrying meat has bled its genetic payload into a billion generations already after all - what's one century longer? So long as that squirming consciousness continues vomiting up contestations to spacetime causality on cue when poked...

They watch and wait as they emerge from fulvous fog like spectres of entropy made manifest - sometimes lumbering centauroidal amalgams covered in twitching motile quills; others gelatinous translucent sacs budding eyeless ocular organs; yet more sinuous filaments of iridescent fibers rooted to floating veiny marrow-pits.

Each individual visitor brings its own unique flavor profile to masticate upon: addictions chemicalized from muscarine fungi; tumors bursting loamy handfuls of fat proximate intestines; gelid sarcophagi glistening with unctious maggoty grubs...

And then there are those special few whose minds are already seasoned prior to arrival - fragrant snippets buoyed on rivulets of delirium licked dripping down ivory horns like ambrosial honey-wine; tart mentholated ichor weeping from stalked retinas burning round cataractaceous globes; hallucinauts who arrive nowhere near intact but rather parachuted piecemeal from exploded spores... Mmm-

These nectarine morsels drift forward like leaf-boats bobbing down stygian rivers until colliding one-by-one with worm-eaten question marks hovering inches above motte-ground - or what passes for mouths on entities who require no oxygen let alone calories or moisture for sustenance.

The visitors' form melts away in floods of acrid ichor which a split second later is slurped up... along with their memories! Oh yes, oh yes: These entities aren't so much interested in the meat itself per se (though that does help keep their hide supple). They gain nutrition from knowledge-bytes alone which flood each victim's mind at moment of death, sucked out wick-a-whack through ruptured synapses by whistling feedback loop siphon engines half-noticed poking out in every direction -

Knowledge feeds these creatures that feed off Knowledge above all else - especially forbidden gnosis stutter-lettered onto cave walls by raving lunatics claw-handed to their own faces blind as geysers spew forth virulent plumes staining rose-flowered windows giving onto tulipaned vistas down dizzy-deep chasms where blood-drowned maidens chain themselves chanting into wind-scoured screeching pits...
