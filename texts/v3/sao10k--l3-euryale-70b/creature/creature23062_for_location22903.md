The creatures inhabiting this serene yet eerie landscape move slowly and deliberately, their movements harmonizing with the unhurried rhythms of the water and earth. Near-invisible birds flit between the sparse trees, their muted calls floating in the moist air like ethereal whispers. They alight upon branches thick with moss and dripping tendrils of emerald growth, their feathers blending seamlessly with the rich browns and greens around them.

Larger beings stir unseen beneath the murky waters - massive fish whose scales glimmer in bruise shades of purple and indigo as they glide languorously through the deep pools. Their barrel bodies undulate hypnotically, propelling them through the glassy depths wreathed in curtains of silver bubbles rising like prayers to the surface. Weedy-looking catfish yawn cavernous mouths lined with whiskery barbels to strain small crustaceans and rotting detritus from the turbid shallows. 

Amphibians watch unblinking from hummocks of saturated vegetation, their jewel-toned eyes glinting like orichalcum coins scattered across velvet pillows of viridian moss. Frogs puff out bellies taut as drumheads before releasing raspy choruses into the humid air. Toads hop across loamy flats with a placidity matching that of the cone-shaped hills.

Mammals move furtively among tussocks of grass bent to gelatinous strands by countless floods, picking careful paths atop spongy soil saturated to black pudding by waters lapping at every declivity. Sleek otters slide into liquid ribbons without a ripple, diving down to harpoon sleek lungfish with wickedly whetted claws within the viscous currents. Deer graze downslope on dulcet shoots forcing green tips through dark peat, ears twitching in warning at birdcalls bespeaking barely perceptible ripples upstream.

Humans too inhabit this realm in hamlets ensconced amidst stands of luminous birch and rowan - perhaps on bluffs ascending above high flood lines to sentinel vantages over eddying backwaters patterned in oil slicks beaten silver-gold by tremulous sunlight filtering through layered mists. Smoke curls laconically from hearths warming roundhouses crowded within crumbling cyclopean ruin's cyclopean masonry, outbuildings bridging bog-holes as their walls seem thrust up from fecund ooze miraculously transformed to silt-impregnated rammed earth foundations.

All these inhabitants move and live at a tempo which seems almost geologic compared to modernity's hectic pulse elsewhere: seasons turn peristaltatically rather than meteorologically; clouds drift soporifically overhead immutably pregnant with dew; spring rapport crops osmotically from a hundred thousand teardrop springs; leaves exhale photosynthesia reddenning autumn while lichen colonizes wood in steady veins implacable as running streams...all things interpenetrating eachother evenly as extinction turns life reciprocally into death metamorphosing endlessly between flesh mineral vegetable animal into slow unhasting generations wearing equal phases imperceptibly as boulders smoothed spherical by tidal cycle after cycle...
