As you stand between the murky, mirror-like pond and the looming, rusted factory, the heavy, metallic air clings to your skin. The shafts of pale light filter down through the misty cavern, casting an ethereal glow upon the strange scene before you. 

The iridescent, wasp-like creatures flutter around the light, their dark, gem-like bodies glinting as they chirp and murmur to some unknown purpose. They are the first indication of the bizarre nature of the ecosystem that has taken root in this subterranean space.

The derelict factory looms ominously out of the shadows, its rusted gantries, funnels and pipes festooned with vatrous growths that secrete viscous, corrosive fluids. These fluids splatter against the stone floor before streaming into the dark water, lending to the overall sense of decay and degeneration. The heavy air is sharp with the metallic tang of caustics - an intoxicating yet dangerous miasma.

Gape huge iron doors beckon you into the depths of the crumbling structure, promising untold mysteries within the bowels of the defunct facility. Yet there is an unsettling feeling of being watched from the shadows, from the crevices in the walls, as your eyes draw up to track the cavern wall face vanishing into the dark, echoing huge space.

The half-submerged vehicles along the water's edge seem out of place, yet somehow right at home in this breeding ground. Enshrouded in discoridated auto-growth, they bob gently at anchor, their windshields finned with calcified runoff. The ancient, rusted hulks are tethered to obsidian boulders by sensor-lace, now reduced to little more than a line of widening beads running from the anodes set along their chrome frames. 

Viscous fluids dribble from their grills and drop from their rust-rotted wheel wells, clouding the pond's surface further. From these rivulets, strange fungal growth emerges - a fungal host breaking down the old world's dead.
Cancerous-looking fronds meander from the torporous vehiclesubscriber trades, as alien to Earth as the autotrophic ecology it sustains. 

The fluttering, gem-like flies cruise from vehicle to vehicle on the water's surface, their amorphous light guiding them to the factory fluid in turn parasiting the vehicle frames. Beads of glistening ichor glisten at their jaw-clefts in what seems a backhand at pollination to the host plant.

Consecrated upon crystalline shelves hewn concentric to this watery preserve sit the hundreds of emplaced forms, once carressing beings now ossuified with a cryogenic nanowaste - the shrunk remnant of the civilization that dwelt in the factory once destroyed to grow this good ecology.

As you stand there, quietly unabashed, this fermenting place evolves around you - degenerating as readily as it ferments new banned breeds of Earth IV. A hyper-convergence of powerdown and inauguration, termination and emergence, crystallization and autococooning. At the edge of that most metal crossing, you consider the jewel wasps, the rust corolla, the centrified cannonade, and that transposing waterline.
