Across the undulating, fleshy terrain stretch vast colonies of arachnoid creatures, their hairy legs constantly shifting the spongy ground with every movement. These arachnoids secrete enzymes to break down the organic matter, which they absorb through their legs. In turn, their bodies are home to countless symbiotes - fungi and flagellates that help them break down the biomass and tap the pulsing chemical currents that course through the planet organism.

When they surface, the overgrowth of the terrain cleaves to them, encrusting spines and occluding orifices. Cycle by cycle they push through the biotumult, until finally emerging as monstrous chrysalids crackle and unfold and release great scurrying herds of scorpion-nymphs to begin anew.

Draped along spines and fungal stamens, they photosynthesize and luminesce, casting eerie rainbow hues across the alien landscape.
