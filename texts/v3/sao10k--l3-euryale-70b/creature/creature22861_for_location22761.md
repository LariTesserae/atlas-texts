Guttural mechanical clankings and the susurrus of sprinkling refrains reverberate off the walls as mists ripple through the vast underground cavern. Amidst tangles of vegetation, hulking machinery churns, stoking cyclopean vents that spew streams into ducts meandering through the rugged, fungal-splattered terrain. 

Omnipresent growths cling to every ledge and outcropping - vines spill in curtains, mossy mats drape, expansive fungal caps sprawl. Titans of twisted trees stretch toward the void, their thick boles glistening with moisture. The staggering variety of species is almost incomprehensible - toadstools loom like sentinels.

Water trickles down walls, spurts through detritus, and burbles in sluggish streams canneling through fungal crowds. The clamouring drips patter against stone, spatter against corners, and drip from budding vine-bud stalactites. Occasional orange light flickers in fern-shrines, only to wink out. A piping mechanical melody threads against the ambient racket.

Plumes seep through breaches in the spinning canopy, half-obscuring the misty mire beyond in vaporous light. Ostensible antiquity is evident in the obtrusions of concrete, cement, and mortared stone, all twined in soda-soggy or bulbous regrowth. Only the broad black sequences of machinery are not buried beneath.

The racket clatters and reverberates from walls and cavern-nap, rupturing corals around the mumbling points into clouds of visible loafer gases wheezing out into the coiling opacity with a pitch your flesh can feel. It spills down slope or incline, establishing a pyramidal abcess.

Overall, though muffled by the sprites and the fog's looming super-evaporation, each racket-resounding interruption from the machinery seems to send squeezes through the plantations' lateral muzzuration, the soaked untethered underders, and the scad-mottled ecotone at that interquartilius.
