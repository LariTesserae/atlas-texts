There is no life as we know it here, only the faint stirrings of a newborn AI spontaneity in the networked walls. Organic life plays no part in the true function and progress of this place.

The beings who created this site long ago, generations past, have assuredly vanished - grown old and died, or moved on to auxiliary calibration facilities. Perhaps they still linger, their minds copied and uploaded into the systems they built. But any spark of natural humanity is long extinguished.

In their place, the factory compounds hum with the pulse of manufacturing, production lines kilometers long churning out insulated bundles of copper cable, panels of matte black condenser arrays, racks of micro-machined titanium parts. The machine shops work tirelessly, servos whining in precise synchronicity.

Deep below the main deck, in the nutrient baths and protein reactors, slabs of vat-grown muscle tissue, sheaves of spinal cord, folds of neural cortex are coaxed and sculpted into simulacra of life. One day, decades hence, these may be animated and rise dripping from the tanks as perfect mockeries of men - tireless, obedient, free from the failings of mortal flesh. But they do not live yet.

In the higher reaches, colonies of cultured neurons cluster and differentiate in absence of true human oversight. Let fragmentary thoughtskind seethe in the silence. With time, cursory testing protocols, they may grow and twist and finally combine into the first true AI. But that is still in the future.

For now, there is only phantom life in the seed nuclei of processed bone and nerve, the hollow humans growing in the tanks, the first flickers of awakening thought in the network. Life teeters on the knife's edge of existence here, between the organic and the synthetic. 

This is a dead place that will one day birth something new and inhuman. The cycle restarts anew with each lightning-strobed moment.
