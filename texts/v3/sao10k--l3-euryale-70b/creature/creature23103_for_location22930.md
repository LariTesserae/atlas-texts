The denizens of this tangled metropolis are as varied and labyrinthine as the twisting streets they inhabit. In every nook and cranny, life clings tenaciously - just like the sparse weeds that wedge themselves into the cracks between the damp cobblestones.

Leaning from their ribbed stoops, the cityfolk eye passersby with knowing glances. They have seen it all before, generation upon generation living cheek-by-jowl in this stratified warren. Their faces are weathered like the mismatched brickwork surrounding them - some ruddy-cheeked and rough-hewn, others pale and pockmarked by years of exposure.

From shadowed doorways peer furtive figures plucking at lutes or strumming eerie tunes on bone flutes. Street urchins dart between adult legs bearing makeshift trays piled high with steaming meat pies or slopping tankards of ale drawn fresh from cellar brew vats. Hunched women haggle over bunches of limp greens pulled dripping from the sluggish canal waters below while burly men heft barrels across sagging bridges spanning alleys so narrow even a scrawny child can span them fingertip to elbow tip.

In darkened windows above flutter candleflames that cast flickering shadows across peeling shutters: silhouettes engaged in arcane rituals, perhaps weaving spells for coin...or summoning eldritch entities from beyond sanity's veil to do their bidding...

Lurking in fetid cellars hide coveys of pickpockets and cutthroats awaiting unsuspecting marks wandering too deep into this roiling hive unprepared. And coiled within dank sewers slither things not quite human any longer after centuries sequestered away from sun's cleansing rays...

Yet also dwells beauty here 'mongst the squalor if one knows where look - an alabaster-skinned maiden combing her hair atop a dappled roofgarden while crimson roses spill down around her...a grizzled storyteller spinning wonderous tales for bright-eyed children gathered crosslegged at his feet...the droning chant of devoted acolytes lifting up pleas to forgotten deities whose stained glass visages stare inscrutable oerhead...

This is but a glimpse behind the grimy curtain draped round this cyclopean burg both repellent and enthralling, ugly yet beguiling - much like its inhabitants who thrive in symbiosis amidst crumbling grandeur and rampant decay alike. To know it is to love it...and be ensnared forevermore within its serpentine coils!
