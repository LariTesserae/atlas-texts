A strange and ominous scene has been set before us here! This fetid swampy clearing seems to teem with primordial life both living and dead. The murky waters churn with wriggling aquatic creatures while decaying vegetation hangs heavy in the humid air.

Most unsettling are the fossilized remains embedded in the rocky walls - ancient sea monsters petrified for eons now exhumed to once again witness this macabre tableau. Their tiny skeletons tell tales of an alien prehistory when these lands lay beneath distant seas. And yet, scattered amongst those relics of deep antiquity lie fresher bones too - jagged shards and splintered fragments bleached ghostly white against the brown earth. Whose gnawed femurs and cracked skulls litter this accursed ground?

But wait, what's this? Some manner of secret portal half-submerged in the muck, sealed away behind a rust-hinged trapdoor camouflaged seamlessly into the riverbank. As if drawn inexorably by morbid fascination alone, one feels compelled to trace a finger along its weather-worn contours...

With each passing moment, more light leaks out from within that eerie aperture as it inches ever wider on unseen pivot-points sunk deep into loam-slick bedrock far below. Shafts of sickly illumination pierce upward from abyssal depths inside like baleful eyes glaring malevolence upon our mortal realm.

Reeking tendrils dangle languidly from vine-draped edges framing that unholy gateway, dripping viscous rivulets stained verdant-brown by slime-matting algal blooms festooning sunken stones all around its perimeter.

And there! Deep within that stygian throat-gullet to oblivion spasms something foul and nameless straining toward emergence... An abomination birthed not of God nor nature but hellish magicks fouler than any deviltry!

Pray whatever crawls forth soon returns whence it came ere we spy horrors beyond sane comprehension oozing out upon this already blighted bog...
