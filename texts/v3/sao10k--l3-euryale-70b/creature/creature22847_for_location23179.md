The denizens of this forsaken place have adapted to the harshest possible conditions of existence, their bodies and minds warped by the unrelenting brutality of environment. Scrabbling on catwalks slick with algae and oil slick, hominids like you eke out a meager living on the fringes of the great industrial complex. Your skin is sallow, your lungs tortured by each breath of the rancid air. Eyes red-rimmed from wind-blown grit squint in suspicion at potential threats lurking in every shadowed alcove. Sinewy muscles knot along spines from years hauling netbags of gutter-scrap across rusting gantries.

Most wear patchwork garb scavenged from factory waste bales - trousers patched with scraps of heat-resistant fabric, shirts woven from spools of fiber optics, head-cloths stained with noxious chemicals used to insulate pipes against frost. But some few are clad in full envirosuits salvaged whole - ceramplast carapace plates bolted straight to scar-tissue at joints, helmet lenses luminous green with HUD data-feeds hacked off company mainframes. These are the true predators of Cygnus Liminus - hunters who bring down rogue mecha to harvest their materials.

Packs of scrawny children dart between pillars shrouded in fetid steam belched from ruptured heating ducts, naked feet sure on corroding steel decking. Many bear hideous mutations - extra limbs budding uselessly from torsos, growths oozing ichor where eyes once were, lips fused shut by searing acid splashes. Some still possess uncanny beauty despite deformities, flawless skin and hair hanging in luxuriant tresses down backs cleft with vestigial tails flaced to raw vertebrae.

High above them all drift zeppelin crews, hidden within armor-plated control decks festooned with kibble-chains and warding charms hammered into bulkhead plating. Deadly riflemen stand watch behind concussion-proof glass gunslits ready to rain hellfire upon any trespassers too close to their docking platforms laden with drums sloshing volatile petrochemicals.

Creatures unseen lurk deep beneath the infrastructure itself: tentacled monstrosities birthed from polluted sludge writhe through foul water-wastes generated by tidal-cycle generators; cybernetic horror-fusion organisms improvised from bonded scrap metals forage for vital organ meats among towering toxmires...

This is how life adapts to survive amidst conditions that would slaughter lesser beings outright. Such is Cygnus-Limimus. Such are her people. Would you have it otherwise?
