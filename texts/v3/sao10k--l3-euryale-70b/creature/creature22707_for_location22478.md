In this hidden grotto, sequestered behind the ancient, gnarled trees, lives a most peculiar people known as the Sylvani. They are a quiet race, living in harmony with the mist-shrouded land and its unchanging seasons. Their history is as old and tangled as the roots of the timber giants that shelter Ooth'gavar Terroris. 

The Sylvani are artisans and makers, spending their days crafting intricate works of bone, wood, and seed. Their skilled hands shape chimes that whisper melodies on the dew-laden breeze. They weave garments of living grasses and woven fibers. In the foundries, smelting hot metals into barrels, their forges ring with the ancient hymns passed down through generations. These songs are said to hold the secrets of the glyphs carved in limestone, runes that few yet understand.

When not at their crafts, the Sylvani gather to cast their magical seals. They combine seeds with harvested herbs, nuts and spices to craft, layer by layer, small talismans. Holding these delicate objects between them, hands clasped, the Sylvani’s magic flows through the seeds like vitality through a living thing. With each seal made, their prayers, stories, intentions and hopes are spun into the very living fibers of their creation.

The Sylvani speak a strange dialect that sounds like seeds and nuts shaken in their shells, their words audibly manifesting their thoughts and feelings. Their tongue brings meaning to shapes like leaves and feathers, paths and patterns. Listening to them speak is to attempt untangling the vines of an ancient grotto, seeking meaning in growth.

When darkness deepens and the mists rise like dawn from the forest floor, the Sylvani meet in the longhouses of living wood. There, they weave more of their seeds and stories. The Sylvani do not share gossip, news, or rumor. Instead they trade in what is true and correct - the descriptions that properly capture reality. 

Whatever is real, whatever is true, and what ever is correct or of worth or value is what the Sylvani claim is met through every part of their being - what they do, how they act, what they make, and how they see the world around them. Their philosophy, what they call the Sylvar that passes as "Language of the Living", their inmost guide. Their longest standing as a people in a land long under frost and shadow is proof that their way is a staying one.

The Sylvani are an esoteric folk, with an esoteric way of living. How they exist, what they do, and how they live in the unforgiving grotto, hidden away, is the story they are still writing. Ooth'gavar Terroris, this place of mystery and wonder, is a keeping place for generation stacked over generation of contemplation and attempted understanding - like the trees, the Sylvani abide. This is their sacred enturance where they find identity, meaning and their only reality through inhabitance upon it. This is enough for living in a place hidden from those on the fossiled lands outside. The Sylvani would not have it otherwise.
