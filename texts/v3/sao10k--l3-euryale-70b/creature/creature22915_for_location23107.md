Bathed in sickly greens and purples, the entire scene seems to glisten and quiver like a tumourous growth. Stagnant pools fester, bubbled by some unfathomable biological process. Rabid toad-creatures squat, gurgling idiotically.

Fog rolls across the scene, foul miasma swirling around the ropy stems and dripping trees. In the hazy distance, impossible shapes lumber among the trees, sounds like laboured breathing and crashing stimulate the senses.

No light of sun or stars penetrates the foliage and brume. The landscape feels watchful, waiting, its enfolding of the road like a trap. The atmosphere is thick with the reek of decay and unbelievable life. The very air seems to rasp and shiver like the skin of some plucking wound.
