Upon close inspection, it becomes apparent that this hellish landscape teems with life - terrible, alien forms woven from the same stone and flame as the environment itself.

Delicate crystalline arachnids skitter along sheer cliffsides, each leg a perfect glass filament that refracts fractured rainbows in the gloom. Their bulbous opalescent abdomens seethe gently with barely-contained fires. They excrete smears of bioluminescent slime in glittering paths.

Flaccid gasbags drift between fissures on currents of steam and ash, their translucent membranes pulsing slowly. Some bear clusters of wicked hook-like protrusions along their sides that drip gobbets of molten ichor onto the obsidian below. Others' skins are pocked with glinting mineral growths like blind eyes.

Glowing fungal hyphae lace through every crevice and fracture, forming vast networks. Occasionally great blooms push through to the surface and unfold throbbing caps crusted with iridescent spores that swirl away on convection currents.

Crustacean-things scuttle from pools where magma streams cool into glassy crusts. Their carapaces shatter-light reflects in prismatic facets and drip fat sparks of slag. Claws shaped like miniature volcanoes eviscerate prey against whipping tails like obsidian scimitars.

Perfect spheres of flawless crystal hover ponderously over the landscape, studded all over with twitching filaments. They emit periodic concussive blasts which sunder local rock formations into howling whirlwinds of particulate debris.

Massive serpentine creatures as wide as valleys undulate across the terrain, leaving flattened grooves behind them molten-edged with cooling glass. Iridescent patterns ripple along their length in hypnotic pulses and fluorescent mists trail behind them to cling to outcrops.

Occasionally massive protozoic entities heave up from beneath the earth's crust trailing curtains of dripping magma to regard their surroundings with shoals of lidless eyes before sinking back into the depths once more. Their amorphous bodies are masses of churning speckled corpuscles shot through with fibres of purest carbon burning white-hot as they writhe together under immense pressures.

Benthic cities thrive deep beneath scores of kilometers of rock within geothermal vents where hydrothermal fluids rich in minerals upwell to nurture gigantic tubeworm colonies 30m tall whose feathery heads strain nutrients from super-heated water plumes stained scarlet by dissolved metals. Delicate vent chimneys built by symbiotic organisms tower hundreds of meters high above these chthonic metropolises which are home to entire orders unknown on any other world.

Complex ecosystems web together all levels and phases of existence here - from nanobacteria in thermal runoff trenches to continent-sized quasi-sentient plasma beings etched among strobing ionized layers tens of thousands km thick encircling this planetoid's core like a living aurora borealis... All around them flow titanic circulatory systems pumping magma to circuit a global network...a huge cytoplasm...

This realm is not dead - rather it is utterly permeated by thriving life in all its myriad forms at every scale from atoms to whole worlds as just another permutation played upon its essential theme: total completeness without variation - eternal unchanging continuity - surging into expression as infinite diversity united into one thing begging no analogue or comparison beyond itself yet encompassing all things within its own singularity. To portray these phenomena would require something greater than language but perhaps even language may be reconfigured anew if aligned parallel these purposes!
