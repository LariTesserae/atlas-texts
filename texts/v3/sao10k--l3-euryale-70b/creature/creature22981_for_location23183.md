Amidst this arid yet strange technological landscape, there dwell unique creatures adapted to its harsh conditions:

Skittering across the cracked earth are leggy insectoids, their carapaced bodies nearly translucent save for flecks of iridescent color along segmented backs. Delicate wings spring out at need, casting intricate shadow geometries before taking flight on the static-charged currents. Their compound eyes flash opalescent as they dart between tangled tufts, hunting minute burrowing mites and worms among the roots.

Talloping across deforested expanses are squat, chunky animals blanketed in rippling hieroglyphic fur. Shifting hues camouflage them against shifting skies as they root about with questing snouts, eating tough fibrous growths. Twin horns sprout from bulbous heads, crackling with contained electrostatic potential.

Larger than these herbivores prowl sly quadrupeds with cunning slitted eyes and glinting tusks. Badger-like in bulk and demeanor, they sport dynastic sigils painted between swaying tails in glowing iconographic script. Their coarse hides can harness power from the ambient field energy drifting over the plains.

Huge lumbering beasts tower above all else - mighty ceratopsian megafauna striding ponderously and slowly beneath tasseled frills which focus ambient magical energies into blazing sigils. These cause fractal patterns to dance hypnotically over altered hide.

Overhead wheeze vast leathery pterosaurs trailing strands of conductive ionized gas plumes from outstretched wingtips. Flapping sinuous necks they pivot T-shaped head-beaks twitching with sensorial filaments as they scan for movement below - when spotting prey they dive arrow-swift down rakish grabbing talons extended.

There may be more lurking unseen - stalking sentinel shapes glimpsed in heat haze distortion or heard in susurrus echoes sounding through the mist-shroud night without stars...
