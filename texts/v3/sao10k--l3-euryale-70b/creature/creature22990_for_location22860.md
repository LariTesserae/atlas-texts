I stood before the churning plain, my mind reeling at the sight. The world before me defied understanding - twisted structures that seemed half-built and half-grown jutted from fields of gelatinous plant-life that glistened like raw meat under skies that writhed with colored smoke. Slick ropes of red-black ichor wept from fissures opening in the soil, as if the very earth itself was bleeding. A million alien cries echoed across the marshy wastes - howls, shrieks, burbles and groans that spoke no language I knew.

Unnerved by the sepulchral green light pulsating from cracks between cyclopean bricks ahead, I turned instead towards a towering spire wavering on the eastern horizon. Through heat haze it appeared a jagged shard of glass studded with bones, caked in mucus-thick fungal blooms...but then vision blurred and refocused and became something else entirely - towers of polished obsidian etched in cyan hieroglyphs; a dome of quivering translucent flesh dripping amniotic fluid; a latticework bastion of silver struts piercing the cloudscapes. Each blink birthed new permutations flickering out of phase.

As I stumbled closer to investigate this protean edifice, a fresh gust boiled past bearing an awful caustic stench and bringing fresh revelations: what had seemed a field's breadth distant was actually a span away, those squat shrubs thirty meters high. And there was movement amid their sagging fronds - sleek shapes darting between trunks and slithering up dripping stems on barbed prehensile tongues to curl into watchful snarls amongst the foliage.

Something pulsed beneath my feet as if titanic heart-meat labored far below and each great throb brought changes rippling through the boglands' fecund morass - here crystalline fungal shelves burst from loamy matrices; there membraneous sacs thick with sludgy embryos tore free from rooting stalks in sprays of glistening ooze; yonder fern-frond filaments shuddered upright into abrupt sentience weaving hypnotically.

Deciding discretion better than valor (clustered ceruleant lights blinked balefully from shadow-sundered amber-encased sockets in cyclopean stone faces looming behind), I angled my trajectory toward the westernmost emanations cradled by brooding cumulus-swaddled crags silhouetted against sulfurous twilight...only to find myself shouldering through saw-tooth sawgrass at inclines of ninety degrees to reach them! The gravity warping swamp's topography seemed intent on denying any straight path forward into gathering dusk.

Lost amidst clumps of fibrous violet bogweed crawling with luminous maggots wriggling phosphorescent trails, disoriented by spectral illusions conjured in swirling fog-drapes stained indigo by unseen light-sources occult behind chittering cicada-shell spires and heaving liver-knob colonies rife with writhing worms...it occurred to me that I alone remained unchanged within this ever-shifting demi-plane while everything else altered crazily around me.

Brushing cobweb-mist from my face I refixed my orientation towards an emerging forest infected with colorful lesions and putrescent cancers creeping over roots bulging white like insect eggs. Somewhere deeper within its weltering shadows unnatural furred-brow facades watched lidlessly through splinter-lace leaves whispering a chorusing susurrus in five-note arpeggios echoing eerily through throttled ravels...
