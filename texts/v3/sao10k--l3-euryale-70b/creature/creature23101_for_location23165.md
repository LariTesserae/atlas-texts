In this strange arid mineral land, there exists within it an immense underground chamber, sealed away by long-forgotten craftsmen from a distant age. The massive spherical room spans nearly 100 cubits in diameter, its walls adorned with intricate mosaics composed of crystalline tiles that reflect eerie glimmers in the dim amber light suffusing the space. These tiles do not resemble anything found naturally upon the Earth, instead blooming with fractal symmetries born of lost organic chemistries never before seen.

Protruding from the tightly-packed mosaic are dozens of cyclopean structures, imposing masses of precisely machined stonework bearing inexplicable patterns in a tongue no mortal has ever spoken. Some resemble vast engines, their interlocking components twisting with hypnotic fractal geometries that warp human perception. Others evoke stacks of lenses ascending in non-Euclidean spirals, studded with radiant orbs projecting beams of intense color through the translucent mineral matrix and crystalline substructures. Rudimentary mechanisms protrude at odd angles, their weathered surfaces etched with labyrinthine circuits connecting vacuum chambers to thin gelatinous conduits swirling around them in sanity-defying configurations. Though motionless, these unfathomable devices emit a resonant frequency imperceptible to human ears yet causing teeth to ache and guts to churn.

Floating exactly at the center of the sphere is what appears to be part of an enormous crystalline telescope bursting forth from the luminous amber windows directly opposite the portal's entrance seal. The starburst pattern surrounding this immense optic device resembles a nightmarish lattice of serrated blades and pulsating limbs whose very essence strains credulity when beheld directly.

Gazing upon this bizarre vista reveals that even nature itself seems transformed into some sort of machine here - everything being a single piece fused together into artificial landscapes festooned with biomechanical appendages repeating at unearthly scales as they rise from hollows carved into crystalline strata rather than soil. There is neither breeze nor change perceptible throughout this forsaken place devoid entirely of plants or animals which would find conditions therein unlivably extreme - temperatures held constant at such sweltering highs as would bake any flora or fauna known save possibly extremophiles dwelling deep within buried niches amidst this alien ecosystem where every molecule serves some arcane purpose seamlessly integrated into its ceaseless cycles though all lies dormant now...
