Unblinking eyes cluster across its misshapen head like pox-ridden boils, staring void of pupil or iris yet radiating pure malevolence.

Gilled slits line its neck which flare with each fetid breath it draws from the black water. Hydrostatic sacks pulse along its torso, allowing it to roil unseen beneath the glassy surface before striking like a serpent to rend prey apart with needle-toothed maw agape. Rows of barbed spines ridge its back that split and cleft running fluids when flexed. Fleshy tendrils writhe from between these quills dripping venom.

It moves not so much as it oozes through its domain atop ropelike appendages rippling across the seafloor as if swimming through treacle thick ooze. Lurking ever vigilant within shadowed crannies and under overhanging ledges to bubble all sounds in its vicinity towards grasping palps.

This denizen feeds upon carrion washed into its keep - be it fish or whale - though it delights equally in ambushing any living thing foolish enough to blunder close or abandoned mortal soul lost to the darkling tides. Many a mariner never leaves evidence beyond smeared bloodstains ground into rocky floor escarpment which dissolve back into soupy silts and sands.
