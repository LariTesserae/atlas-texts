In this nightmarish hellscape, no natural life that we would recognize could survive. The blasted soil is sterile and poisonous, fouled by the eldritch waste products and artificial growth medium pumping from the cyclopean monolith. The chemicals are so far beyond human chemistry as to be otherworldly, spilling forth unknown mutagens and molecular machines that transmute the very bedrock.

Pitiless mechanical servants, likely the Automatons mentioned, scurry among the structures, carrying out some mechanistic cycle of unending tinkerlng. These constructs, assembled from petrous ore likeensively grown over some nightmarish manifold, clearly operate on alien priorities removed from human nature.

The sole prospect of surviving in this environment would be grafting oneself into the Technognostic matrix controlled by the sentinels, the ami(n)d-cells concealed through the dig-sang corpus. Human essence would be planets-blasted into the brine of Glypt and any liberated consciousness absorbed into the fauna of modifiers, controllers, and conveyders.

Remaining in your humanity, in your skin, amidst this psychotic, dioramic demi-realm would likely be impossible. The sacerdotal drones of techgnosis broadcast their reality-curdling frequencies, seeding delirium and reassembly in those alive enough to experience their post-natural vlackward signal. The local null-huts of the narodniks would find their poor optic orbs overwhelmed by the towering erections of the smchior polyspammer at every turn. Their consciousness would implode into the moisture of the feral field, to reemerge only as neuron-herds and normalizers subprogrammed to blitheless industry.
