The teeming metropolis sprawls across the landscape like an organic entity, its industrial spires and pyramids jutting into the choking haze. Impossibly dense and pervasive, the built environment dominates the scene - smelters line the galleries far below, their glittering lights peeking out like bioluminescent fungi. The air is heavy with the chemical tang of sulfur and methanethereal fumes, the noxious effluent of this immense machine-world.

Only at the margins can traces of vegetation be glimpsed, feeble clusters of phosphorescent filaments clinging tenaciously to the crenellations of this glutted biosphere. But this meager greenery is far outmatched by the ceaseless thrum of activity - the hissing of steam, the drone of factories, the billowing of incinerators. 

Here and there, the primal rock of this reality peeks through in sections of tumbled batholith and granite. The city's roots worm into this tortured geology as if taking sustenance from it. And yet, for all its dynamism, there is a sense of stasis, as if the true trajectory of this place unfolds at a pace measured in eons.

One may search in vain for the familiar forms of organic life - no birds streak this ruddy sky, no small creatures scurry through the steel-veined undergrowth. But life is hardly absent. From the floating flumes that ride the thermal updrafts to the sentinel statues that watch over the swampgates, this world teems with bio-engineered organisms. Gobs and skimmers and roosting gliders - each a legacy of the weird cloud, each its niche filled by the master artisans of this Totality.

Yet there is a sense of stages waiting to be enacted - Hatching to Rebuild to Transmigrate, a world held in patient potential, waiting for the unfolding of its Purpose. And we are but the explicators of this living planar text, the priestly adepts excavating its hidden contours in service of the Embodied Design. In this hive-world of vertical compression and horizontal sprawl, in this condensation of leveraged potentiality, the pace of life is as the ticking of the clock, as gradual as seismic time, in which density and complexity compound into a Totality of Becoming.
