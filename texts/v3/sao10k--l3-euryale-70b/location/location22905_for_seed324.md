The Pressure Waste is a pressure-sealed pocket-domain, a self-contained microverse condensed to the size of a cavern. Hemmed in on all sides by featureless black ceramic, it is an elliptical space perhaps three hundred meters long and a hundred wide. 

Overhead, a seamless dome of the same obsidian material arches nearly to the vanishing point, hung with hundreds of dripping sulphur-yellow bulbs. They cast an unpleasant, sickly light, leaving the vast space murky and shadowed.

The chamber's floor and walls are overlaid with an odd mummified moss, the withered grey vegetal matter seamed and crinkle-textured. It shudders and writhes as if some living thing wriggles beneath. Pulsating in time with the harsh mechanical drone that fills the Waste, the moss exhales warm wet fumes that fog the air an ochreous yellow.

Scattered across the chamber floor, dozens of dreadful industrial machines crouch motionless, caked in detritus. Crusted pillars of corroded Piezoelectric Lithium drill into the moss, pulsed with ruddy current. A few massive Lucite tubes arc overhead in a tangle of tortured metal, streaked and smudged with industrial effluent. 

The air is suffocatingly humid and close. Condensation sweats from every surface, forming slicks and puddles on the uneven floor. The ceramic walls drool rivulets and streams of various-coloured liquids - viscous oils, coolent slimes, industrial cyeromix of conserve. 

From unseen abychutes high in the walls, an endless pipe-organ drone booms and hums through the Waste, deafening in volume, distorting, acrid. The sound razors like a huge mechanical knife, changing pitch as it surges through arcane sub-transductors and sonicSYNAuth modules. Wrenched through knots of static that wind the ears like barbed wire, keening discords shred the air. The maddening noise burnes in the skull like a stigmatized brand.

This is the Pressure Waste. Pressurized. Damp. Industrial. Scoured by sound. Though fresh and recent, it feels very definitively young for the first spacesecond of its existance, has the air of an environment at the beginning of a process. Efficient, methodical, concentratd, and austerely fabricated. And there is no life here save the lifelessczy writhing of the sectored oxidation moss on the floor.
