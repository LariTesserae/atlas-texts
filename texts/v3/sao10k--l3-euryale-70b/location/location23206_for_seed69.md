This is a vast, primeval landscape of sprawling temperate rainforests at high elevation. Towering conifers and deciduous trees stretch hundreds of feet into the air, their massive trunks dripping with thick, velvety moss. Ferns and cascading vines fill the dense understory. Low-hanging clouds frequently roll through the mountain valleys, leaving the atmosphere perpetually swathed in mist.

Pockets of standing water from recent storms dot the forest floor. Crumbling rock formations rise from the earth, oozing brackish water from cracks and fissures. Grey granite faces are streaked with streaks of moss and lichen in shades of deep green, magenta, and iridescent blue.

The temperature is mild but humid, causing sweat to trickle down your neck even at rest. Strange insectile insects and rodent-like creatures skitter through the undergrowth, their calls echoing eerily through the still air. Feline predators silently stalk through the gloom, eyes glinting gold.

An indistinct rumble of factory machinery drifts through the valleys on the perpetual haze. Plumes of dark smoke can be glimpsed drifting upwards from unseen industrial sites in the distance. There is a sense that this is a place where ancient nature and raw industrial power coexist in an uneasy balance.
