A harsh, rocky plateau stretches to the horizon in all directions from your vantage point. The elevation is high and the air is crisp and cold, with an ever-present nip in the breeze. White peaks tower in the distant mountains, glinting under the relentless sunshine. But for all the brightness, the landscape is unremittingly bleak - treeless, mostly barren of life, pockmarked with scrubby ponds and gullies that never seem to thaw. The only vegetation here clings tenuously to crevices, and then only in thin, wiry tufts. 

Geologically, the plateau looks raw and recently uplifted - there is no vegetation to conceal the chaotic, fractured rock, and in places you still see the layered striations that show how this terrain was shoved abruptly from beneath the earth's crust. Torrents higher up on the rocky flanks show where the ice and snowmelt has carved ragged gullies running across the surface to the horizon. The whole landscape looks exposed, wind-blasted, bleak under the heavy sun.

At some unknown distance beyond the uplands, a glint of light on metal seems to resolve into the glint of unfamiliar machines, stacked and packed with geometrical precision on some hidden greater plain. Vast towers rise among them, to inconceivable heights, a skyline of horizontally cyclopean structures thrusting from the haze - a megalopolis or a factory eating all in its gigantic path. Who knows? It is too distant to make out details. And all around the shimmering metropolis, a diffuse discoloration hangs over what must be some vast conurbation, belching petrochemicals and ash into the mountain air. 

Raucous bird-song split the air, great things taking pterostich heaving by as they ply the updrafts high above the plain-layered rock. The wash of their thundrous wingbeats echoes from the sheer rock-faces in a basso prolongato. Pack-lords ride the pterosauroids and there seems to be some quarreling and petty acriminoy involving several cohorts which distracts from the strange industro-metropolis till a scout-sororor notices you.
