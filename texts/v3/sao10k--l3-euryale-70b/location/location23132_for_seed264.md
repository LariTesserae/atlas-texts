A vast sprawling industrial plain stretches to the horizon in every direction, a monotonous expanse of rusting metal and scorched earth. The ground is hard-packed and sparsely littered with gravel and shattered glass. Occasional wilted shrubs and tufts of grass struggle to survive in the arid soil between clumps of corroding machinery. Crumbling brick chimneys rise like skeletal fingers from the arranged wreckage, puffing wisps of smoke into the leaden sky.

In the distance, a chain of derelict factories stands derelict against the smog-stained sky, loading elevators lowered like the jaws of mechanical beasts. Sere, poisoned fields immediately surround holding ponds of sludge and ash. The air smells of fuel, acid, and hot metal.

To the left, twisted metal rails snake past rusted ore cars and toppled locomotives. Loading silos loom sunken in fields of cracked concrete and oil. A sprinkling of snapped PVC pipe, junction boxes, and power stations leads to the gate - a rusted iron rectangle in a chain-link fence spiked with barbed wire. A crumbling guard shack with boarded windows squats inside the fence.

Rubble choked streets connect every edifice. Half-finished bunkers, collapsed power lines, and pillars of smoke mar the horizon in all directions. Rarely, twisted bare trees rise in the distance amidst lakes of tar or waste.

A thick, humid atmosphere hangs with acrid smells and clangs and hums of industrial machinery. Occasional plumes of smoke or dense pollution roll across the bleak terrain. It is desolate and consistent across this sprawling blight.
