Droplets of condensation cling to the sides of sprawling, ancient trees, each one hundreds of years old. Their massive trunks twist upwards, their upper reaches tangled in a dense canopy far above. Shafts of misty sunlight filter down to the forest floor below, illuminating masses of damp, decaying plant matter. Pools of standing water collect in low clearings, covering the ground and filling the air with a chorus of croaking frogs.

Vines and wispy ferns sprout from the trunks of the trees, coating them in leafy coverings. Boughs are hung heavy with broad fronds and foliage. Clumps of mushrooms push up through the rich, dark soil - their caps in shades of pale green and gray. Lichen spreads its web across fallen logs in muted purples and tans. Everywhere, vegetation cloaks the landscape in layers.

An occasional raindrop filters down through the leaves overhead, plopping into the leaf-scattered ponds and glimmering rivers that thread through the forest floor. Mist lies in the lowest dips, swirling gently in the breeze which shifts the temperature between cool and damp. Strange mists drift through the shadowy lower regions.

In the distance, a gentle mist drapes the hilled terrain, the only indication of one valley shaded by the next. Hills trend upwards, hidden, hinting at gentle masses of earth. The limits of the place are obscured in trance-like hazes.

Until the hum of machinery surfaces from the thickly veiled valley ahead. It is the only suggestion of the high-density urban 'city' of living treehouses spanning high across from branch system to branch system, layered in the canopy far above. Roots and trunks give way to platforms and walkways ascending by pulley and cable to urban core. Through gauzy mists, the upper reaches shakily conceive a strange metropolis laced and layered in indusrial wooden density over brook and glen.

Beneath the fathomless treetops, scaly creatures with armored snouts forage through the spongy growth on the forest floor. Strange glints of tiny eyes peer out from between the forward trunks and roots. Here and there the coat of growth parts to reveal twisted burn-marks and fungal thickets the color of slaked blood. Bony shapes scuttle at the fringes of vision. The general feel is one of hidden livings fomenting stealthily within the masses and humus.

The air drifts thick with the clotted raucus odour of pessimistic verdure - loam, spore-laden fronds, wet leaves and slimes and algae. Birdsong trickles through in viscous arpeggio. Strange croaks and trills resonate from unseen depths in almost menacing accrual. Somewhere: grinding, hammering, a monstrous, multi-exhausted roar. These, through these, informs: Through the overlooked break of responding haze, as diffuse a congress as emphemeral as the mists that mutable drizzle through - technology and undergrowth, coiled in asymmetric congress..
