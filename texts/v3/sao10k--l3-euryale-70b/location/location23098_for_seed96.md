The landscape before you is a stark, strange land of packed, constructed shapes and materials under the glare of bright alien light. Everywhere, the built environment presses in with no room for nature. Wedge-shaped basalt towers 500 feet tall crowd the landscape, their precise angles and planed surfaces like teeth drawn across the empty blue sky. Each 300-foot wide flat top bears a grid of larger metallic machines, their surfaces pitted and accidental in contrast to the milled rock. Weeping condensation drips from the machined edges, staining the otherwise pristine stonework with metal oxides in splashes of iron red and verdigris.

The sun is high, but muffled behind a haze of mist. The distant towers are recessed into shadow and difficult to make out. The surface is light gray rock, veined with copper and cat-linen quartz, pitted with pools of scummed water a handspan across. Sprays of salty foam dash from the lips of overflowing waters as trickles stream between the stones, punctuated with spouts of light-green fluids. Metallic clinks and squeals rise from tectonic shifts.

Filled in between the towers and covering all but a path, a field of sure-fitted polyhedra measure out the landscape. Seen flat on, they are star-shaped or unfolded like the faces of d20s. From the side, each rests against its neighbors, fitting multi-faceted against each in perfect geometry. No gaps left. Angle upon angle of cool gray pre-cast resin, where each plane is embossed with fields of surveying marks in snaplines and hash-marks and seams where fitted moderately together. When the breeze rises, it stirs the surface into a chittering prayer-flag hiss of smacking contact. Trapped in the cracks of the resin are mats of drying durian-like fruits squished out by the way they're nestled, pale lemon, rot-sweet, each propped with black seeds. Mist rolls over the tops like breakers.

Slowly, the dazzle seems to nag an angle of light, and the rocks and towers and stones and hard polymer surfaces everywhere are transected by zepher-etched guidelines and ornamental chiseling that glitters with some precious grit. Rubies, emeralds. A bright thrust of tourquoise.
