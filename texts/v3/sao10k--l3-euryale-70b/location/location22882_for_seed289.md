We find ourselves gazing into a frothy, bubbling pool of springwater. Its surface is mirror-flat, reflecting the cavern walls uneffaced. The subterranean springwell is partially covered by a crude wooden lattice, strangling tufts of horsetail ferns sprouting from it here and there. Disturbingly, the lattice is human-sized.

In the dark rock surrounding the pool, numerous cave swallow nests are grafted, packed tightly against one another in chaos. Furious concatinations of wet mud and vegetable matter. Tiny inlets from the cavern walls pour a constant thin drizzle into the spring. An endless susurrus of falling water.

From just below the surface of the pool, dozens of bones protrude - ribs, ulnae, femora. Pale and twigs idly. Disturbingly, many are unmistakably human. Glistening under the shimmering surface. In the relatively clear, peaty water, we can see a large stone sarcophagus emplaced delicately at bottom. On its lid, angular runic symbols are carved, ranesque, like forgotten technology - but no, purely arcane. Staring into some awful aether. Hard to break them off in the brain.

Luminous spores and motes abdominate the air over the spring, spiraling wierdly. Filling the closse air black. Whirling into skull. The cheep susurrus of underwater coursing and the susurrus of wings overhead, the patter susurrus of falling water susurrus susurrus susurrus susurrus susurrus susurrus.

It seems unlikely this place has ever been disturbed. Maybe it hasn't been disturbed since the Lost Ones built it. Feels like we weren't meant to find it. Like it was hidden in case of emergency but the emergency came and it was never uncovered. Maybe we're supposed to leave something behind instead. Like this ossified turd. Can't remember why I am holding this turd. Must have picked it up from that molt of bones. Those cadaverous bones. Some of them mammoth but most human, I think. Why are they here in this mirror-welled boil? Why is that cistern down there so diabolically lidded? Man don't gee naturl to me. Whole place feels wrongside up and fo, lunched in cuillin.
