The Alabaster Wastes are a harsh and desolate realm, baked under an unforgiving sun that beats down from a cloudless sky. The terrain is an endless expanse of harshly luminescent white-gray sand, an alien powder that takes on a faint pearlescent sheen from the perpetual dust that dances in the arid air. Jagged rock formations jut out obelisk-like from the bleached dunes, ancient remnants of a long-lost bedrock eroded to needle-sharp spires by untold eons under the pitiless winds.

At an odd distance, scattered across the horizon, tower immense industrial structures, all exposed pipes and rusted metal, belching clouds of acrid smoke into the putrid sky. Closer inspection reveals these to be massive alchemical refineries, humming with industrious activity, endlessly pumping runny sludge from subterranean wells to churn into who-knows-what precious poultices in due haste. In and around these installations toils a population of scrawny, swarth human workers, clad in crude oilcloth, barely pausing in their shoulder-to-shoulder industriousness even in the sweltering zones between the steam-belching thesis intakes and the first heat-sinks of the condensating towers.

Abundent if only barely, skulking feral creatures slink about the periphery of the wasteland, most esoteric, all iridescent, soot-stained, peculiar of form and stange of mind, birthed or blighted by who knows what taint from the rancid effulgence the fabircs spout. The loudest voices here are the hound-choirs of tireless geothermal venturi pumps, aroar like the endless bellows of some gigantic sea-leviathen rising single-mindeds towards the miserable, guiling sky.
