Steady drizzle mists over a stark terrain of craggy obsidian rock, mineral-formed from cooling magma of eons past that flowed in rivulets and billows, forming twisted shapes now weathered, pitted, and pockmarked with countless bubbles of air or gas trapped at that long-ago surface. The smooth glassy surfaces scatter light into a million tiny facets and fractured reflections that bounce back upon each other into dazzling fractal interference patterns and a prismic displays of grays and muted frosty colors. 

Vast crystalline spires and jagged canyon walls emerge from the rock-expanses, etched with mineral veins of copper, molybdenum, and rarer ores that flicker in shimmering ripples of warmer color. Tarns and hollows pool with a few inches of brackish water, its surface never still but always gently convecting and rippling as it surges, seeps and seethes in capillaries through underlying rock. 

Occasional delicate, translucent fungal webs and crystalline structures ripple across the surfaces, pulsing ever so slightly as fluids and trace elements slowly flow through them - the only hint of living things, fungus-like yet utterly alien, feeding not on any other organisms but on trace volatiles and metals from the stone.

The air hovers just above freezing, hanging thick and heavy with mist that perpetually precipitates, rolls down planes of rock into gullies, flows and tumbles through lichen-rimmed sluices and gurgles always softly, a constant sound like a far-off running brook. Where it drips from ice-encrusted overhangs, icicles extend, fragile blades and flutings of transparent ice aglitter in the dim light filtering through a perpetually-overcast sky.

All else is still, no wind, no change, just the faintly-rising humidity and the loros of seeping, dripping, trickling water, and the silent, diffuse illumination as would bathe the floor of an ocean under multiple moons, only broken by the glittering facets of volcanic glass and the odd scintillant trails the ephemeral fungi weave across the obsidian surfaces. A crystalline porous rock, glassy fungal networks, and seeping mists, in an eternally-shot landscape under a seaward and overcast alien sky.
