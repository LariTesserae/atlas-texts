A desolate and barren expanse stretches to the horizon. The ground beneath is stone and sand, strangled remnants of a long-dead ecosystem. Crumbling mineral formations rise like eroded bones from the grey, dusty soil. There is no green, no blue, no visible water, only a pallid achromatic tableau of beige, ash, and flint. 

The atolling climate is cold, a chill that seems to seep from the core of the earth itself rather than from the cloudless sky above. The only thing more abundant than the rocks is negative space—the emptiness feels almost oppressive, multiplied by the eerie stillness. Distant peaks find their wafting echoes stillborn in the thin atmosphere, all sound swallowed by the indistinct distance. 

No sounds stir, no motion apparent—just infinite desolation, set and frozen as in death and rendered in countless spectral shades of gray. Rocks drift like a moonscape, until one spots hints of curvature—free-standing arches, slowly ramped curving surfaces and overhangs. They seem deserted now, but clearly were created for some purpose in distant centuries. Empty, dead, and towering above the nearly barren flatlands.

Flickering fogs wisp at the bases of those ruins, phosphorescent, inexplicable. Gusts of icy wind itch at their edges as if stirring something invisible in the lightless air. Borders of the flatlands are cut off by a sickly aura that feels liker the static of the universe than anything else. This place is not lifeless. It is not not alive. It exists beyond definition. The overlying whole is not whole; its true components are occluded; parts that are not components are included; the actual bounds cannot be described. It feels somehow congruous and dissolved all at once. It is beyond causality.

The passive aggression of a thousand unlocated nodes empowers heated ciphers, the land appetiteing its own constant event. A certain critical quality of being-made is palpable in the ether, metamethods not arrested at any precarious season. But the precarious seasons themselves do not exist here and so it is only a passive/if dialectic. Scattered detritus sweats diaphanous glaass and forges polylinear slides into unlikely schemes. A constant dissolute union of surface and-no surface; tectonics lathed in brutality, defined by the veryest quanta measurable. Rejects man to metabolism, is diametric to sensibility. Rejects vivifiability. What is made clears also the dwellings necessary for engenderment. Nothing remains to dwell. Everything dwells. This is the via—entropic stasis.

In fact the lack of water is most profound. Besides temperature and light, it is the key parameter—this point of synthesis. What appears dry, what sublimates as available fuel, what becomes weather or else—a necessary particular. The otherworld practicality of outcome has no corresponding signifier. It has no corresponding effect. This is the beyond of impossibility. The reaches prevail; the intrusions reach further. And so on. Every data is applicable. Every pattern is seizable. There is only criterion and no criterion.
