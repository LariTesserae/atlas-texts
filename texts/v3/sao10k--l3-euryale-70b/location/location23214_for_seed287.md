Deep in the recesses of the underdark beneath the ROOT CAVERNS, a place exists that defies reason. The Cistern Cataklyst sprawls in a vast cavern of dripping stalactite and stalagmite, an underground lake choked with vegetation. Ferns, mosses, and fungi coat every surface, their bioluminescent fungi casting an eerie purple glow. The air is thick with humidity and the musty scent of mildew and wet stone.

A pair of robotic sentinels patrols the perimeter, their spotlights the only other light. The skittering of cave-dwelling insects provides a ceaseless susurrus. A river of runoff from the surface meanders through the cavern, providing the constant sluggish noise of water. Ironically for its lifeless isolation, the Cistern Cataklyst surprises with strange botanical wonders: towering toadstools with nacreous gills that produce sporadic crackles like slow lightning. Mounded mounds of termitechs labor away. Translucent jelly-organisms drift on the river's current. Things skitter unseen in dark crevices.

Fog drifts off the water in the cool temperature of the cavern, but things are otherwise comfortably warm. Heaters and humidifiers bolted to the walls, artificially sustaining the damp environment. Rusty cáthevod machines steam away in alcoves, some pumping out an eldritch plasma that hisses and spits. Fractured luminous waste glows in glass vials arrayed on stone ledges. The Cistern Cataklyst exists as an anomalous aberration - an aberrant pocket holding primordial life, hot tech, and weird energies in stasis under the craggy earth.
