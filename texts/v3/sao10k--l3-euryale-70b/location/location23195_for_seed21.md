High in the misty mountains, nestled between colossal stone peaks, lies a narrow valley of peculiar beauty. Here, a vast tangle of greenery thrives in the humid air, nourished by frequent rainfall and shallow aquifers. Towering conifers and broad-leafed hardwoods crowd close together, their foliage a rich emerald hue.

Weeping fissures in the grey bedrock, slick with moisture, dribble rivulets of clear water that babble softly over shelves of stone and fallen logs. Shafts of light pierce the canopy at intervals, dappling the forest floor with puddles of gold amidst the dappled gloom. Steam curls from still pools, caught in beams slanting through the mist.

The air is thick with the scent of vegetation and damp earth, and teems with unseen insect life. Gnarled roots twist from thick log trunks, and shimmering populous with lichen, moss and ferns. Pale exotic flowers, alien in form, nod on slender stems below the leaves.

Though no overt signs of technology are evident, there is an uncanny quality to this place. Towering over the trees, forking toward the emerald sky, are spires of rock and crystal that defy geology. Their polished surfaces gleam in colors not found in nature. Anomalous patterns of runes seem etched along those titan stone leaves. Something of the forest's very essence seems to vibrate at a strange frequency. 

Audible movement stirs the undergrowth, but once seen, the dappled light wavers and the eye slides away, unable to fix on any form. Sounds of near animate growth - the scaled click of minute limbs, odd trills and musical humms, the whisper of light passing over curling quills - echo from unseen foci. So.remote, yet ever-present, is the air-song, the forest's vast susurrus.
