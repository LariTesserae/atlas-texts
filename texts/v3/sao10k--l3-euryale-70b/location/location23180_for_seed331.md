In the midst of an ancient valley, where the mountains of eons past have long since eroded into gentle, rolling hills, lies a landscape both primordial and strange. The air hangs heavy with moisture, sucking up the remnants of a recent rain that clings to every surface like a damp blanket. Water seeps from the earth itself, bubbling up from unseen sources to form steaming pools and slow-moving streams that flow in meandering paths across the valley floor.

Spindly vegetation clings to the damp earth, twisted and gnarled trees stretching their branches up towards a canopy that filters the bright, steamy light into a kaleidoscope of greens. Vines and creepers wind their way up the trunks, while fungi sprout from decaying logs in a riotous display of shape and colour. The air is heavy with the scent of petrichor and the rich, loamy smell of rotting plant matter.

But for all its appearance of a typical, if lush, marshscape, there is something distinctly unnatural about this place. Strange structures, rough and angular, rise up out of the landscape like weird mountains amidst the alien flora. Their smooth, pearlescent surfaces seem to shift and flow like living things, and from within them writhe and pull organic shapes that, on closer inspection, seem to move with an almost mechanical precision.

The stillness of the scene is broken by occasional, distant sounds - the thunder of waterfalls echoing in the misty distance, the splashing of creatures in the opaque pools, the ululating calls of unseen beasts drifting through the cloying air. But mostly there is silence, broken only the wet shuffling of rot and decay, and the obscene slurping of the strange flora.

Occasionally, just barely audible, there is a low, thrumming drone. Like a million, million bee wings it hums through the oppressive air, speaking some strange language low and bass that speaks to something in the ancient heart of things, long since forgotten by the scurrying life that skitters through the deep puddles and mucky pools.

And then there are the sights, sounds that shouldn't be - glimpses of glitters in the shadows, light moving through water in ways it shouldn't, and a bone-deep conviction of your own insignificance in the face of this strange, alien place and the things that lurk within it. A place where the rules of nature strain at their seams, and something new, undiscovered, lurks beneath the skin of the old, old world.
