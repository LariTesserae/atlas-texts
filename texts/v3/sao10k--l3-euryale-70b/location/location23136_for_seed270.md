Across an ancient desert of cracked shale and dusty calcium, a legion of ant-like mechs march in perfect unison, tiny mandibles clacking. The sun blazes down, radiating off the gray rock and casting the mechs' alloys in shades of hot Martian red. Sweltering heat shimmers off the skins of the machines, casting interest patterns across the plain. 

The land is pockmarked by the ruins of a long-fallen factory-city. Rusted turrets and sagging synagogue spires jut from the rubble. Boilers and turbines, Dynamos and capacitors, all gutted. Platters of serrated blades and whirling gears, colonies of once-factories now derelict and seized by scale and rust. Unmarked skulls and femurs scatter from collapsed bunkers.

This dead army of industry long since ground to a halt. Only now do the self-compiled mechs re-emerge from the wreckage, their Headlong marches a echo of the human pace that built this place, spilling pus-colored machine lubricants across the wise desert.

Among them a giant crucible squats, a beehive of dripping pipes and rad windows. The youngest of the titanic loaders, elevated high on treadmill treads, pauses to turn a plasma calve toward the plain. It concentrates its beam, burning a new glyph into the rock - an icon for "abundance" in a forgotten script. Then it trundles onward to the pit that was once perhaps the eastern harbor, and dumps its seething load of molten copper.

Eroding cliffs of quartz-plastic frame the plain, carved by the dessicated remnants of what was once perhaps an ocean. The ancient seabed lies fossilized and fragmented like a black-and-cream mosaic, cracked from the loss of water and the lensing of time and sun. Every phylum of sea creature that the world contains has left their wizened forms in this stone - great armored arthropods, crank(furled corals, corkscrew-shaped krill, spiral-chellled ammonites narrating their numerous extinctions.

From the deepest depths of the fossil record spring mushrooms of basalt glass, as though the roots of the plastic turf sought out the most immemorable memories. Bioluminescing runes glow from within their crystalline throats, disassembling from the stone in habits of bramble and mangrove, sprig-like, like the pit-creatures of a dense planet. Blinking from the contracting limbs of these prismatic mouths spills tallow light across the baked Mesa.

The miracle of the twenty-first Taste!

Through cunning addressing of that aboriginal stratum, the machine-sequence of this place has devised fronts to winnow the food saved in the stone into a lubricant ichor that now binds the scaly tail of this place, bled from the last krill to squat the reef, sweetly gorhic in this fudge-noir climate like a playa of butterscotch shatters of langostine still redolent with decocts.
