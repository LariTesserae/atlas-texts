The place is a misty, high mountain valley, scantly populated by simple wooden structures huddled together for warmth. Twin peaks of naked stone rise on either side, peaks still raw from their eons-long emergence from the earth. Their flanks are scantly wooded with wind-sculpted pine and scrub hawthorn.

Rough-hewn logs and rammed earth form the basic rustic architecture of the alpine hamlet. The chimneys of the thatched huts puff thin blue smoke. You can hear the ringing of a blacksmith bellows in the distance, and the rising wail of a baby.

Moss-cloaked boulders and trickling rivulets of fresh mountain water are scattered between the homes. A class of children is knelt by one of these, scrubbing moss and lichen into tunics of boiled wool. 

The academy is the largest structure: a two-story octagon of waxed raven black wood, capped by a green copper dome. Steam rises from its central forge. You hear a scholar call Nein! inside as they lug a weathered tomoherent tome to a reading desk.

Gliding between the treetops is a winged automaton, tin and hammered brass, folding pleated wings dappled with cobalt blue enamel. It alights on the academy roof with a flash of flame. The head, intricately filigreed copper, swivels to regard the village below.

The automon is a scholar, a computer cut from metal, who calculates the orbits of the planets and marks the festivals. The tale of its animation is a mystery, one told in illuminated letters on a vellum grail in the academy chapel.

From here, you can just glimpse the library's gardem, dense with live oaken arboers and sloe and cedar and collier and blackchar. Tucked behind is a fountain where meanwhile water jets across patterned tiles, a peristaltic flux of light and water.

The air is alpine-sharp, crisp as slate. The sun is as yet below the eastern peak. Nightchill still lingers in the shadows. Birdcall lilt through the forest. And at the verge of hearing, as if echoing from a distant canyon wall, comes the sound of an iron bell tolling once, then twice...and then its echoes are gone.
