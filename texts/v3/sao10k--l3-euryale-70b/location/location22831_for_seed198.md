The unending city rises in a tangle of iron, concrete, and biosynthetic material, its buildings compressing ever closer together until there is almost no space between them at all. Exhaust-spewing. Dense, oppressive, sweltering atmosphere heavy with moisture and smog swirls through the narrow, twisting alleyways hemmed in by vague gray decrepit skyscrapers that descend miles into the blackness. 

Ginger fog spills from millions of pipes and drains, coiling across rust-flecked catwalks and tilted planks. Murky puddles of indistinct fluids gather in the infrequent intersections. Grafts of fungal hyphae burrow through walls and floors. The air shimmers with heat and churning fluids. No sky is visible.

Sculpted ponds and aqueducts meander through the press on every level, churnaled by obsessively intricate water-pumping wagons, weed-choked. Algae and invasive mycelium swamp the water and stream across surfaces, entwining the dense, cyber-organic megacity into one fulsome biome. Bio-noise, sonar, and engines groan. The place seems alive.

Stairwells and gangways pierce upward into darkness above, where racked machine-turrets emit lantern-like glows and electric arcs. Broad, inscrutable factory floors seethe below hidden by walkways of a hundred route-iterations. The buildings, streets, and serpentile conveyor-ways seem all in thrall to geared, steam-pulse machineries. Grottoes of hydrologics and hydraulics squirter sea-green water all around, 'beneath, and discoiling out the serrated drone. 

Apparatuses of obsucre function drone and pulse, and positively twist. No part of this gyre-city remains unstressed, untransformed. Every surface is uneaten caked mineral deposits, riotous water-life, or imperial industriemes. Compression, mutation heat, gaseous-dusk. Ossified turputude. Carnivorous intelligent moss. No calm is there, only squeezing industry, agry tidal-furnace night, and fundamental molecular slop-turbulence. The place eludes stasis and tus stated: only the Grinding efflorescent industry may endure this ambiance.
