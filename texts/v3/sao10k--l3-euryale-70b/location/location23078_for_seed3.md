The landscape emerges in a dim, cool, reality-bent place. Mountains loom on the horizon - twisted spires of rock and metal reaching toward a pale sky that churns with emerald clouds. Neo-schist paths wind between them, flanked by factory chimneys that exhale plumes of sulfur and indigo gas. Industrial sounds echo through the valleys - the drone of engines, whistles, clangs, and occasional sonic booms from some experiment gone wrong at one of the research facilities buried in the depths.

Closer examination reveals slashes of green struggling to thrive among stones, twisted tree trunks with leaves of knife-metal and clusters of glowing pods. Scattered in the open, low-saturation spaces are the husks of dead or abandoned buildings - some still flicker with ghosts of failed magics. FixedUpdate pylons, anachronistic in the time-alike-stuck-stop this place seems to exist in, arc eldritch energies between the peaks. Dry rivers full of mulch and poorly-smelted ore weave between.

The air is damp and smells of brine, static, and machinery. Reality feels wrong here; the maths of Physics unpinched by some distant cataclysm.
