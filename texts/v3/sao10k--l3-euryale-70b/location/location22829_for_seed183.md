Gazing out over the jagged horizon, one is greeted by a striking vista dominated by a wild upthrust of ancient, naked rock. Tearing up from the shredded remnants of a long-lost seabed, the stone skyline abounds with turbulent formations - twisting spires, leaned arches, friable shelves. Pauses in the relentless march of geology seem frozen in moments of chaos. 

The air is charged with palpable energy, and the visual tensions between straining rock and tensive voids create an hypnotic interplay, an uncanny juxtaposition hard on the mind. To stand unsheltered on this exposed promontory is to feel small and first-hand the tremendous forces that still shape this destination's tortuous topography - it is no place for those made timid. Wrenching cascades of scree tumble down faceless cliffs as some unseen force stirs sediment - punctuated with sporadic groans as stone meets stone in unforgiving collisions.

Shadows ooze between crevices, and organic matter is in such meager supply that mosses can clinge but not colonize the stale expanses, failing to ameliorate this world's inherent abrasiveness. The spores and sulfurs waft and restrengthen the cracked land's gritty homogeneity - as features grow indistinct, vision blurs with unbroken monotony.
