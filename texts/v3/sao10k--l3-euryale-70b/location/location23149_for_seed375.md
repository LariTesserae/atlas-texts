In the crumbling light of an ash-choked sky, the jagged-edged ruins of an ancient stone city sprawl outward from a central citadel. Foundations of cyclopean megaliths pierce the dun-colored soil like the splintered bones of dead titans. Vine-strewn ramparts break like rotten teeth along the horizon, the rusty remnants of grandeur.

Scranting carrion birds patrol the empty lanes, hopping from rubble heap to rubble heap. The spent shells of burned-out buildings alternate with narrow trenches hacked through sour soil and conglomerate bedrock, tree roots webbing through the tumbled stone like tentacles. Running like an open sewer, the trenches shallower towards the outskirts where they dribble into muddy sinkholes and cavernous sinkholes. The corrosive mud, bleached and salt-crusted, bubbles a clandestine effluent of unguessable provenance.

Everywhere loom the sunken ruins of black shale mines: deep, dark excavations into basalt strataveginating the terrain in ravines and sheer cliffs. Rustleing from the depths come the skitter and shriek of small porterous beings foraging for glints of metal among the collapsed tunnels, the shatterd pillars and roof-falls. Rotating the air, the intolerable stench of ancient fungals and volcanic gas wraiths unnoticed along the trench walls.

Silent gargoyles leer from jutting cliff faces, patiently awaiting prey for eons at the bottlenecks of the serpentining gorge, salivating mandibles grinding in agony. Heaps of fossils and bone on the floor tap into the axioms of geologic and evolutionary time, bear witness to ancient calaumties and the passage of eons from this place's/once exuberant life under alien suns.

Screeching like a dying slave, hunting packs of uncanny hyenids trawl the flashbacking sinkholes at the periphery, glutting their gullets. They dance and pant on gangrel legs, pausing to babble qabalistical amulets learned untold generations past from their feral ancestors, and revile the vrillating statues that leer eternal vigilance over this purgatorial badlands. The heavens howl them back in cortegy.
