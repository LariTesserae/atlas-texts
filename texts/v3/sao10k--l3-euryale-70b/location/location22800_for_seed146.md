You stand at the edge of an infinite plain, a vast expanse of finely-grained red dust stretching to the horizon in all directions. The ground feels soft and light under your feet, like bottled up talcum powder or the driest of ashes. When you kick up a bit of detritus, it hangs motionless in the air, suspended in the stillness. 

The sun or whatever passes for one here hangs low on the horizon, a sickly yellow-brown orb too large in the hazy sky. A thick haze, like the smoke of a city-sized bonfire, hangs over the plain, never moving. When you turn your head or body, it takes a moment for the world to sharpen into view again. It's like somewhere between distance-haze and just pollution-haze and... something else?

There isn't a scrap of vegetation anywhere. No plants, no trees, no lichen, not a bit of grass or moss. The only shapes breaking the four red expanses of sky and ground are infrequent detached outcroppings of black naked rock. They've been carved by nothing - they're sharp-edged, never-eroded. The dust, or ash, rolls up against their bases in tidy dunes but never quite laps over them. When you walk through your footprints linger behind you for a long time.

As you look at the outcroppings, you realize they do have polyps on them - precise, sharply-edged shapes almost like mats of some grass. Over there's a slash, there a zigzag, in two places are little tangles. None of it moves in the steady calm. The stuff is the shade of cement and has the textured look of broken stealth technology. You have no idea what it is or why it grows in such patterns.

There's just the faintest hint of a texture to the sky but you can't make out a single shape in it that could pass for cloud, bird, or other natural apparition. It's just... ethereal smog. The sun-or-sun blazes steadily, steadily, like aBR30 lightbulb.

After you've looked around for a minute you notice that bowel-loosening sensation of not being totally alone. Out in the haze, things are moving. Shapes that might be organic except that they don't quite fit, and tagters that seem to fit but twist in a way that doesn't sit right with you. Probably six or eight of them, always just past edge of clear sight. They don't seem to be coming closer or responding to the fact of you the way an animal would. Just... existing. Out there. Watching you like a janitor not quite cleaning a cage, just looking in to ensure everything's frozen in death. 

There's no wind, no sound of insects or birds, not even the creaking of rock. Just a still quiet... and after a minute of holding your breath in the useless hope you might hear some tiny susurrus... you can just make out... the fizzing drag of things far out there camouflaged against the rust-red, watching you look at them, looking back at you looking at them. Silence underlain by the quiet susurrus of creep. Like grains running through an hourglass in slow mo.
