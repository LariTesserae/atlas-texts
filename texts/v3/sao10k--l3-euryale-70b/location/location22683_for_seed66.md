Here and there a plaza rings a fountain, run rivulets gurgle from every eaved and architrave, pipes vent steam; the air is never dry. Frequent springs murmur through the tiered city's roots.

Beneath lies tumbled rock, a geological history of upheaval. Granite and gabro and batholith, siblings of a world-as-factory. Smelters line deep-etched galleries, lights glittering. Eventually, methanethereal fumes winnow to air.

Vegetation is minimal, whispers at corners, clumps of phosphorescent filaments. Temperature fluxes day-to-night, shadowfall on terrain of combustion. This biosphere is not for chlorophyll, but Grandma's ambience.

Acrewaptation has very dense presence in this place. Built environment vastly dominates any mention of nature beyond works of mining. Technological web saturates, all-enabled, pun intended. Light's a profuse as the urban simmer. Soundscape hisses, thrums, drones, snorts and billows.

But fauna abounds, progeny of that weird cloud. Floating flumes ride the thermals, gobs fin through the swamp gates, sentinel statues keep watch, crustaceans tiptoe the escapements, skimmers spider the irrigation veins, gliders roost the grating lattices, and as you might suspect, fauna is the form language of this place.

This world holds it's trajectory in patient unfold, like a story to unspool, its phases measured in the thecology before us: Hatching, Rebuild, Transmegrate, Re-wire, Until We Awaken.
