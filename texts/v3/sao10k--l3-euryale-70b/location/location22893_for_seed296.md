In this landscape, a misty veil hovers over a valley ringed by ancient, deeply eroded mountains that have been whittled down over eons into gentle, scalloped hills. The once jagged peaks now slope in curved, undulating lines towards the valley floor. Here and there, a lone spire juts up at an angle, like a fang, while elsewhere valleys and ravines split the landscape into a network of inlets and coves. 

Over the hills spreads an lush tangle of vegetation - dense forests cloaking the slopes in a dark carpet of green. Rills and rivulets run down the hillsides, merging into burbling streams that meander through the woods and tumble over mossy logs and boulders worn smooth by ages of current. Occasional small waterfalls drop down sheer faces in whispering curtains of spray. As the streams reach the valley floor, they wind together into a broad, slow-moving river that glistens wetly in the dim light.

Along the valley bottom lies a collection of structures: subtractive wooden and stone dwellings set on stilts; their expelled cuttings and casts heaped around them. Some buildings rise in lumpy stacks of sod and thatch, or bulbous sagging domes emergent from humps of earth. Other dwellings are sited in nooks and hollows, their outlines blurring into the terrain. Mist drifts through the structures' walls and drifts in wraithy tendrils through the laneways between.

Occasional engine-drums and chains thrum and clank where ancient machinery churns and wheezes, drawing water, smelting ore, shaping, lifting. Rust and verdigris and patina inlay the gearwheels and pistons. Tatters of smoke in spent purples and greens sigh from stacks on the buildings' backs.

Some few creatures pick, claw, and ovipositor their way through the thatch and hangings: Valleymoths drop their spined eggs in hairy mantles, fluttering bitingly; Nibbler rabbits spotting fungal doorways; Soughosels squirming on fifty-strong legs through loam and muck...

But mostly a drifting, breathy quiet hangs over the place - like a voice sighing a forgotten song. Shadows lurch and twist as atmosphere ticks through cycles of padding gloom and swarthy pearl, bruised lavender hills clarifying through encouraging mists into a milky dawn.
