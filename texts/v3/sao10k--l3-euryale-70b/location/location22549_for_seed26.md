In the cold, industrial mist, a vast network of steel pipes stretches across the desolate landscape. Steam hisses from ruptures in the corroded, dripping metal, misting the already-saturated air. Clusters of rusting valves, straining under their own filth, scatter across the terrain like a field of mechanical mushrooms. 

The pipes weave together into a jagged gyro of catwalks and gantries, forming a patchwork of steel and steam. Flickering arc lights dangling from the pipes cast a sickly orange glow over the scene, making the fallen steam swirl iridescent through the air. 

Far below the clanking, suspended walkways, a black lake of petrochemical sludge undulates, frothing green with algae. Barbed with veils of rain, the spoiled swamp pulses with a strange, famished lichen that clings to the metallic canyon walls. In the brackish depths, nameless things gibber and struggle.

At the edge of the industrial waste, a tattered mist weeps from the blackened stone, dripping into the foul swamp. The rocks themselves ripple with a nauseating wrongness, bulging and sagging into unnatural curves. No true horizon can be seen, only the looming press of the metallic network and the crimson swamps.

The air chugs and seethes with the reeks of sulfur, petrochemicals, and perhaps screams. The clanking, hissing symphony of the pipes fills your ears, augmented by the golars of the abominations in the mire and swamp.

No green grows here save the demonic algae. The ground is flat, churned ruts of slag and stone. An impossible geometry clings to the world, the product of some madman's blueprint. The thick air chugs with a half-remembered stench of ozone and unwilling transformation. The throat-burn of acrid waste pours from the towers.

In the hive of built-ruin, a terrible communal intelligence whispers over the hissing wires. A malevolent perspective, invented of the mad clamor and waste. The complex, sensing, is a nightmarish machina in its own right. It groans and squeals, funnelling its stinking, seething fumes into the crimson fog.
