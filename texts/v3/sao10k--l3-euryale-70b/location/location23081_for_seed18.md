The towering factory-city looms over the misty river valley, its walls of brick and steel climbing to dizzying heights. The perpetual mist swirls around the base of the gargantuan structure, evidence of the warrens of subterranean works and the power of the cataract which thunders over the escarpment. Above the veil of steam and smoke, the factory's disordered tangle of chimneys belch flame and ebon smoke into the firmament. 

At ground level, the heavily wooded glade is latticed by dripping pipes and braced with great iron trestles. The fog obscures the forest floor but suggests distorted forms crowded together - exotic ferns and fungal growths sprouting an impossible array of vividly colored caps straining upwards, perhaps dueling for direct light through the steel-reinforced girders which stretch overhead. Bioluminescent twilight clings to the edges of things.

Every surface is slick with condensed water vapor or dripping with unknown effluent draining from high above - proof of the insatiable demands of the industrial machine that cloaks the valley entirely. The relentless sounds of steel striking steel and furnace heat and rushing water all echoes up from deep below the mist where some monster of entropy churns, supported by the lifeblood of fresh water endlessly falling.
