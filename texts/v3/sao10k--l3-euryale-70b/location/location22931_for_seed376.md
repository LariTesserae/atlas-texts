In the dense heart of the insane jungle, a hidden grotto ripples with invisible river water around living towers of fungal mycelium and coral. Foggy vapor swirls in eddies. Strangely-pulsing bioluminescent growths emit low light. The occasional massive fungal bloom heaves into the air and detonates in showers of spores that swirl like smoke. A helix of roseate lichen winds in rhythmic spirals up vines as thick as freight trains. Mist lingers at the bases of the growths and streamlets of iridescent amber ooze to the damp loam. Nothing's ever still, everything's yelling or hissing in silence. Ants as long as bananas march in trenches along the colossal, lumpy trunks and stems. A satellite-sized spore puffing cloud approaches, lit from within, baroque and hypnotic. Lightning slices in strobic flashes between the growths and all goes silent...and then the seconds before dawn shatter in a chaos of fungal bloom and ant marauds and ooze-steaming loam-song.
