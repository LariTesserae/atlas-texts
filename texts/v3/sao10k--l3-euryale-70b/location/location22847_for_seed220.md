A riverine city lies sprawled across the bed of an enormous, entrance-less cavern. Lush greenery clings to the rock walls: ferns, mosses, flowering vines. The air is cool and damp, with tendrils of mist drifting through torchlight.

The city unfurls in sweeping art nouveau curves, with half-organic, half-synthetic architecture flowering from the stone. Gossamer walkways and bridges span astonishing distances. They sway and shift, reconfiguring themselves to the urban highway's semi-random will. Occasionally one shatters and tumbles into the void, only to start forming again in a new configuration. Misted pools and burbling drains catch the condensation-laden air. Bubbles of water hover mid-air in between structures.

The soundscape is a manic tapestry of city noises - odd braying sounds, susurruses and clangs, some reverberant birdcalls. Automated drones of the city's self-governing AI cross and recross the vast lofty space. Surgical light spills from the apartments, workshops, and marketstalls. Autogenetic memes and market trends provoke cycles of growth, shift, and collapse across districts of the city.

The illumination could be called somber, but the firestorm of softened light jogs perceptions. Laser light flourishes across the streets, driving odd resonances through structures. The color should be strong, vivid, rich, but it lurks behind the grey and ash that spew from the city's industrial underbelly. Beams stab and sweep as some effete centaur motors by, sequined and heavy with nanotech competitors.

There's little sign of mammal life save homunculuses. Microbes and insects cluster in the pools and sumps. Vibrant small beasties - some biological, some mechanical, some impossible to distinguish - flicker and skeet through the shadows. Worm-like cloud-support drones sheer past, trailing vapor contrails. Immense insectoid shelled things clitter across the bones of their many-ancestor diet. Bladed rollers furrow the polished polymer lanes, beeping sonically. Nature and tech have done their alchemical magic.
