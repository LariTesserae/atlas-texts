A heavy, clinging fog hangs low over the city, tendrils curling between buildings, wrapping streets and alleys in a thick gray overcoat. Water drips from teeming canopies, small rivulets forming along cobblestones, trickling into the sewers. Moisture-laden air presses down, pinning the sky to the earth in a stifling embrace.
The buildings crowd the narrow streets, many blackened, magma-colored hunks of basalt, tuff and volcanic glass jutting jaggedly up to the smothering cloud. Eroded and weathered from time and the perpetual water, their faces glisten, rock on rock packed solid like cells of beehive hives. The taller towers molder decaying and the ramshackle wooden buildings sag between them, beams and rafters dripping, boards fuzzy with yellow-green growths.
Squeezed in every crevice, every surface, the mosses and lichens and fungi writhe and reach like a tangle of tentacles. Verdant tendrils grope, spurting through any crack in stonework. They carpet the buildings entirely, leaving nothing uncovered, seams and mortar imploded by such unrelenting growth. Funnel-shaped fungal stalks thrust, fan-like growths spore and seed and suckle the air and water. Floral scents miasma.
The work of industry continues here, even under these conditions. Fumes seep and ooz beyond broken doorways and windowless facades. Steam and chemical mixes boil off in hanging plumes, mixing with the fog, catching the eye in searing beads. Some of the flimsly buildings let through daylight, shuttered glass windows like small squares of crude mirrors, sunlight diffused. The air is thick with breathable particulate, an optic thickness. Though light, the sun creates no heat. No birdsong. Scarcely any movement.
With slow patience, nearly undetectable, the growth keeps surging, coping, claiming. Vines seizures up inaccessible towers, buds emerges where none see. Pools of water sit immobile in half-collapsed cellars, surfaces clotted with green scum. In sewers, softer growths knit themselvs across wet crags of stone, ensphering blockaded intent. Crawling with the weirdly slow live growths like a great dorsal fin creeping to engulf entirely this oversleeping sunk city, all its feats and its bulk, leaving beguiled in bulging verdant thick curing to eternal latency in the miasmic unmarried swamp-shrouded denseness...
