In the vast expanse of the Midlands lie the Blasted Canons of Calm, a forsaken landscape born of fire and stone. This place is a place of extremes - daunting heat, jagged rock, and an eerie stillness broken only by the ancient creak of magna and metal. Here the land itself seems twisted, wrong.

The Canons cut deep into the earth, their strata a brittle record of eons of geologic turmoil. Basalt and obsidian predominate, interspersed by veins of indurated clay - the Midland geologists' prised 'permo iron'. Pools of briny water, the legacy of long-vanished seas, seep up from the flanks in stinking slicks. The gray-green of the water mingles with the black rock until it is hard to tell where the stone ends and the sump begins. Sulfurous steam rises to mingle with the baked air. 

Despite this hostile environs, life finds a way. Strange fungi spread skirts of ochre and viridian over the fractured boulders. Worms burrow through the sparsely wooded gullies, subsisting on the meager runoff. Avians of filt and mallice, with feathered ruffs and hooked beaks, circle ceaselessy in the heat-wracked skies. Smaller creatures scurry in the canyons' shadows - pallid moles, sad-eyed salamanders, and myriad beetles carved from chitin and silver.

Human habitation in the Crystal Midands skims the edges of the Blasted Canons, eschewing the worst of the radition and grime. Ougly sheds and sentinel stills squat along the rim of the chasms, their tin gutters dripping with corrosive soot. Code-wardens patrol atop gyroscopic striders, their fusion blasters at the ready to deter claim-jumpers and nihilists. Closer to the chasm walls, the ore jamms are delved into the gizzard of the Midands itself - lodes of zinc, tin, and synthopal patrolled by unpaid tithe-staff and eager corporate inspectors. Heartrcyst and rupturs are common. Amidst all, the venting alchemical works spit their effulgent balm and vicious fumes into the thick recursed air of Calm. Sprites are said to haunt the seigneuries.
