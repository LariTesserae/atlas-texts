A cathedral-sized cavern hollows out the bedrock, its rib-vaulted ceiling lost in shadow. The space feels both vast and stiflingly enclosed. 

Subterranean streams wind across the cavern floor, their crystal-clear waters reflecting the faint luminescent streaks marring the walls. Gusts of slightly musty-sweet air waft from fungal growths clinging to the stone, their cap sizes varying from tiny coin-sized polyps of dun and violet to great shelf-fungi that project like stone altars, their caps' gills pulsing sluggishly. Most are dry and desiccated, the rich loam of organic matter beneath not enough to keep them viable. 

The fungal mycelium weaves through every joint and seam in the stone, as if the very rock is imbued with life. Though the air feels heavy and lethargic, the fungal matter must sense the world's stillness, their dominating presence a testament to their being the only enduring organisms here.

Broad basalt columns rise from the dark depths of small pools, like the tapering masts of drowned ships. The air is preternaturally still, only an occasional drip of condensation echoing in the silence that presses on the eardrums. 

Scattered denizens of this underworld lurk in crevices and niches. Slow-moving millipedes, segments marked with glittering trilobite-like shells, inch across sunken boulders. Blind, eyeless harlequin-beetles stridulate monotonously in the recesses of epiphytic fungal fruiting bodies. Plump poeichthyfish hover in the shadows of wide columns, their sightless eyes open in unseeing regard. 

In the deeper pools and basins patches of bioluminescent paint-smears and glinting stipples swarm, drifting in or agitating against the glassy surfaces. Perhaps these microbial organisms are the energizing source of the cavern's dim luminence.

The explorers of this stilled world come across the crumpled relict of a waterwheel half-subsumed in organo-fungal mat. Brass plates thick with verdigris, copper cladding blistered by copper oxide, steam conduits jut from communal fungal bodies, organic and metal-mechanical processes merged in this inanimate leviathan. The wheel's vanes are at rest, poised motionless between one rotation and the next against the background drone of dripping that emanates from everywhere in this self-sustaining whole-stratum organism.
