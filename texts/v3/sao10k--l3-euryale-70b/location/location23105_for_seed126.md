The Cosmic Crucible of the Built Planet

Stark. Bleak. Silent. Vast.
These words still fail to encapsulate the landscape before you - a strange, sprawling, sprawling monument to engineering on a cosmic scale. Skeletal spires of blackest black pierce the wash of pale phosphorus light from an unseen source overhead. Their peaks lose themselves in the unending haze which casts shades of violet and magenta. It is difficult to guess where the horizon lies, such is the tangle of megastructures in the distance.

No air whispers through this desolate scene, no ambient noises at all but the whirr of distant engines - great pistons and compressors pumping with a regular rhythm like a slumbering Leviathan's breath. A faint violin hum emerges from the static, almost imperceptible, waxing and waning in dreamlike reverberations. These few sounds do little to break the crushing oppressive silence, like a feather attempting to buoy a black hole.

Here and there, plazas of glass and obsidian are recursed by almost organic-seeming networks of pipes, conduits, gantries. Their insectoid geometries are both regular and non-Euclidean, not quite a crystalline structure nor an aragon shell but something that combines the order of both. Scales of Reptilian hide shimmer, shifting from red to electric violet in a curiously aesthetic way. A monoculture of some basic form of plant with blade-like leaves emitters a cotton-candy scent, the only vegetation in this Endless Engineering.

In the near distance, ziggurats of textured steel crosshatch the dead bare plains. a procession of goliaths with bodies of killwrought alloys and forms that suggest posthuman genetic precision but surfaces of osteoporotic metal, long tongues and trunks like elephants serving captors of the hive complex. Their massive novacula-esque eyecounters swerve in baffling arrays, scanning the spired horizon, needle-beads of reflexive passéoire casting long lines of traversing shadow.
