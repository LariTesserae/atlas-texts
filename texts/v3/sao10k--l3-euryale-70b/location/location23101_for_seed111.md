Ethereal Canopy

Towering canopies, hundreds of meters high, pierce misty skies and rise from a gently sloping, uneven terrain. Geysers and nozzles, large and small, protrude from the stony surface and bubble and fizz, emitting occasional gushes of vapor and releasing pungent odors. Rivulets of muddy, greenish fluid meander between the base of the canopies, slowly eroding a loose matrix of sediment and cementing shut narrow crevices. A humid, warm air heavy with the stink of hydrogen sulfide swirls intermittently over the landscape.

The canopies themselves seem organic, more like giant fungal hydrangeas than geological formations. Their "mushroom caps" are flat and mat-like, forming overhangs, protuberances, and shelves of black crystalline lattice. Seen from a distance, this dense crystalline assemblage resembles black-and-tan fungal mycelium grown to building size. Thick, pliable, organic-looking stalks support each dome cap, curving gently between surface and sky. Some stalks are petrified or fossilized, stony and hollow; others ooze yellowish resin.

Clinging to the wet surface and hanging from the underside of the caps, hanging swords and streamers of weird vegetation unfurl - fungal growths, beaded-algae, fractal-shaped fronds, colonies of gelatinous motile cells. Bizarre bioluminescent couches and fibers lume ghostly iridescent hues: ghoulish blue, ghostly rose, eerie violet, sickly chartreuse.

The surface is lumpy and uneven, with crazed ravines, crevices, pits, and mild avalanche slopes created by sloughing vegetation and mineral precipitants. Thorny, lianas and stolon-like vines and aerial roots crisscross the terrain, intertwining the canopies. A tangle of different water-dwelling slime molds and saprophytes wiggles in the muck. Frothy greenish gelatinous sacs and sealed cysts and spores cling to every surface. Niggling, low-frequency sounds, screeches, clicks, and ponderous silenty drift across the air.

No clearly visible animals lurk overtly in this strange ecosystem, but fuzzy, Greene-sensitive shapes and dark shadows deep within the canopy undersides or midair, feint sounds skitter at audibility's margin, and unguessable signals pulse through the very ground. Unnameable, otherworldly, incomprehensible biological assemblages lurk far beyond the reach of human neurotransmission - their cravings, losses, lusts, and purposes properly incommensurate with human thought. This place is no mere couch for mere life to live - this is a geliş-defined world of consciousness.
