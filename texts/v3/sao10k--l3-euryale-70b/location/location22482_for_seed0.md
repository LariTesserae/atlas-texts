A turbulent, primordial landscape of overwhelming life and deafening sound, where lush vegetation runs rampant in the damp, warm air. Steaming rivers and swamps snake through valleys, oozing slow and sluggish between twisting banks heavy with vegetation. Trees rise lush and green to form a dense canopy that darkens and chokes out the sun, allowing only faint, dappled light to filter through.

The terrain is a chaotic tangle of freshly-erupted volcanic soil and twisted rock formations, pockmarked with steaming vents and stinking fumaroles. Ridges of obsidian rise from the tangle, slick and dark. Steam hisses from rents in the earth. The ground shudders and rocks clatter down sheer, newly-hewn cliffs as the landscape continues to contort and change itself. The air trembles with the deep, crushing heat of smoldering magma just beneath the surface. 

An overwhelming profusion of life throngs every available surface - scaly bodies slither through the undergrowth and take flight from the canopy, the air thick with their fumes. Crawling mounds of chitinous limbs scuttle up tree trunks. Wriggling, unrooted vines whip provokingly, spiked with prolific crimson flowers. Trees and ferns and vines overgrow every surface in a claustrophobic tangle, held together with thick webs veined with glowing phospores.

Swamps are choked with parasitic growths that writhe unearthly. Fierce, fanged pursuers stalk their prey through the rotting mire. Buzzing swarms of tsunami-sized insects blight the air and ripen the damp with disease. Hot mud bakes underfoot, punctuated by oily, tar-slick neutering ponds that ooze viscous bubbles. 

Fleshy trunks twitch, shudder, and reform before the eyes. Surfaces ripple like living tissue. Phosphorescent slime trails run twisting symbiotic circuits between every living thing - from tree root to hypha to slime run to gland, inset with what may be tools, structures, or communication phores. Biology and geology blur. Cliffs weep viscous nectar. Trees excrete metelogical spores and motes of crushed gemstone. Glands secrete clouds of swirling noxious miasma. The landscape and its inhabitants have melted into a single, planet-spanning entity.
