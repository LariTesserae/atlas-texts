The humid air clings to the entrance tunnel like a damp fog, heavy with the scent of soil and the promise of unseen life. Beyond, the cavern slowly reveals itself - a vast underground chamber, its vaulted ceiling lost in darkness. Wandering paths meander through the cavern floor, curling around stalagmites to disappear into alcoves.

Here and there, the paths approach inches-deep pockets of water that glimmer in the weak light. In these sullen pools, blind fish drift and dart, their scales catching the feeble light and sparking like gemstones. A break in the ceiling high above allows a beam of intense sunlight to plunge into the cavern. The light fades and scatters, illuminating a cathedral-sized space in pale hues.

Amidst the slow, moisture-fed formations and hardpacked earth, a few scrubby plants struggle for life. Thorny bushes and starved trees mottle the ground cover. Their roots tunnel deeper, seeking subsurface water, working stone and soil over immense spans. Crawlers and burrowers pour themselevs through the crevices and cracks. Skittering sounds echo in the cavern's languid stillness.

Dim figures carry primitive tools and weave wicker frames. Poking and delving in the relic-strewn soil, they hunt artifacts of bygone ages, beeked-nose purpose evident in every motion. Their tools - flint knives, clay paste, trowels of antler - speak of an era long-lived past. Not for them the glyph-rewoven techno-prruise of the steel-peak beyond; their skills run to chipping hard stone into serviceable points. The pace of their labor matches the patience of the cavern: slow, intent, thorough.

Flecks of strange rock: glassteel, plas-tik, aim-aloy; not native to this soil. Religiously preserved, avidly traded. Forged into totem-helmets and razorgelry by the burrowers. So they cross and re-cross the cavern in powered track-suits reeking of hydro-carbon,splashing through shallows in degauss-mesh leggings. Trusting to the master-skills of their wearable savvy, they tunnel and trench, door and dam. Lights strobe and motes swarm.
