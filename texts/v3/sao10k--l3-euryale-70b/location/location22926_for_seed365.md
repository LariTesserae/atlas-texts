The drip of water echoes through the oppression of silence. Mist clings to twisted trees that writhe in perpetual twilight, their bark running with moss and lichen. Shafts of silver light penetrate thick canopy, dappling the undergrowth in unsteady puddles. Tall ferns unfurl from mats of decay.

A choked river winds through boulders, reduced to a stony channel between mossy banks. Veils of spray billow up where the waters plume over rock 'round every turn. Murmuring streams bubble from rocky noses, tumbling in shallow washes of cool, clear runnel. Mists besiege the chasm, swirling in eddies and tatters.

Towers of wood and stone loom through vapor - the worn parapets and sagging eaves of a lonely settlement. Heavy-boweled trees bear gaudy toadstools in virilant colors. Vines weave the stonework and vaults.

Things slither unseen in sodden undergrowth. Blink-quick shadows dodge betwixt the trees to the susurrus of skittering through dead leaves. The musk of salamanders and loam hangs in the damply. A sudden downpour sends runes of raindrops skipping 'cross the sullen water, fading to wide-sky'd delbruik and back. Ranks of toadstool jut from loam; in their shadow creep worm and mole.
