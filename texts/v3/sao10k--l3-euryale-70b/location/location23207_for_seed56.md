In the shadowed depths of a primordial valley, a strange and alien world unfurls itself in the gloaming. The air hangs heavy with moisture that clings to every surface in a viscous sheen. Plumes of vapour swirl languidly as they rise, coalescing into pockets of mist that skulk among the twisted vegetation.

This is not a place of sidewalks and skyscrapers, of lumber mills and laundromats. No structures of steel and concrete blight the landscape, for this is a realm at the dawn of things, where the arc of development has yet to begin. Raw stone and naked earth form the substrate, glistening under the sheen of abundant liquids.

The plants here are the undisputed masters. They carpet every square inch in a press of growth, a riotous tangle of verdant matter. Tendrils the girth of grown trees cannonball into deep pools of standing water. Vines the size of mature oaks crisscross and interlace, forming lacy canopies that exclude most light. Boulders wear cloaks of moss and lichen and epiphytes; whole hillsides are shrouded in curtains of draping growth. The vegetation seems to writhe and twist, more fungal than floriform.

Amidst this tectonic jungle, creatures move unseen. Bouts of raucous sound emanate from deep shadow — cetacean ululations, avian shrieks, infrasonic rumbles not quite audible to the human ear. Stygian pools conceal splashy motions and clicking echolocations. Portly fungus sprites lumber between the towering undergrowth like hidden tanks. The biomass is fierce and untamed.

Yet even here, technology burbles in the æther. Rumours of improbable machines whisper through the mycelium. Aberrant physics writhe like especially virulent kinds of vine. Anachronistic lights shed drizzling motes of hazed radiance from high aerie perches. Antediluvian energies throb through the stone, setting mineral fluids curdling and clotting. from those frothy vacuoles mutant “fossils” coalesce under the quicksilver sky.

The temperature hovers just above freezing: brisk enough to limn clouds of viscous mist from your skin, warm enough to moulder if you lay still for a time. You can discern no snow, but the moisture here would freeze hard under ambient conditions. Stranger is the air-pressure — high and pressing, like being trapped under an inch of cheap glass.

You are aware of vast immensity overhead, but cannot see the sky: some interplay of light and mist and paperwork lids you in this place. Likewise, while you feel you sit at the bottom of a slope, or in a valley of some kind, you cannot discern bounding edges. What walls as it does gives way to the underside of other things. The landscape shambles off in each direction to divergent units of being, so that no single composition can be encompassed at one time. Scale is relative. So is up and down.

The feelsome weight of antiquity bathes you as you crouch in your nascent sanctum. The protolandscape shifts and gurgles and churns. You are crouched at the base of a tree whose aspect twines in every direction. Water pours down from above, sheeting across the laminar interior of your passageway. Communicants of faun and flora and mineral murmur in the aether. Alien raucous realities shimmer at the corners of your mind. A great vertiginous sense of presence lurks at the boundary, too big and polyphrenic for you to encompass. Stay as you are, and you may find you stayed too long.
