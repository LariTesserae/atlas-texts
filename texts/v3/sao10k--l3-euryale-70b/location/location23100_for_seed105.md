The landscape is a nexus where water and rock collide in a peculiar harmony of the organic and inorganic. It is a submerged realm at an elevated crux point where a vast, shimmering lake meets an upward jutting outcropping of bedrock. The lake's waters are still and crystal clear, plunging to unknown depths in cerulean hues. Yet at its northern shore, the land rises abruptly upward in a series of towering cliffs and fins of rough stone. Though submerged to some extent, the upper reaches of stone still pierce above the placid surface of the lake, forming bizarre twisting spires and archways of weathers gray rock.

The stone itself is veined through with flecks and streaks of unknown phosphorescent minerals. As one watches, it gradually becomes evident that the rock is alive with a subtle, organic glow. Delicate tendrils of bioluminescent mosses and alien lichens creep across its weathered surfaces. Lush clumps of ghostly fungi sprout from crevices. Gelatinous algaes trail gently in the currents. The petrified rock suffers a vibrant riotous overgrowth of fictional flora. 

Moisture streams down the faces of the stone, though whether from the water or sky above cannot be determined. It gathers in brooklets and cataracts that tumble sheer from ledge to ledge in improbable braids of water and light. The sounds of distant dripping echoes and phosphorescent decay resound through the clammy air.

Nature and rock entwine. Fashioned by water and crustal forces, the stone outcropping reemerges itself from the swooning curves of the lake-sheet, perforated with hollowed out cyst-like grottos.
