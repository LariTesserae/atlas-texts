The mists swirl and churn, illuminated in an eerie bioluminescent glow. Distant lights flicker, submerged in the dense fog but still visible in the cloudy murk. Some lights drift and bob, cast adrift, while others remain stationary, beacons amidst the turbulent gloom. Their origins are impossible to discern - are they lanterns, fires, or some lesser-known phosphorescence? 

The sighing wind combs through the mists, sending ripples and eddies arcing through the vapor. Beneath, it's difficult to make out geography - if one's feet would settle on land, ice, metal, treasure, trees, or some embedded fur. The layers are indistinct, the textures obscure.

Sounds bubble up from deep within the murk - a cacophony of groans, clangs, and burbles. Scraping, clashing, echoing from somewhere. Distant screaming, maybe, or perhaps cruel birdsong. The noise is pervasive yet distant, as if there's both too little and too much going on beneath the vapor, for any one thing to resolve. It's a storm of sound and light in the mists.

Occasionaly shapes solidify and resolve - the prow of a buried ship, a jutting rock, a wall of some structure. But they are sparse and vagant, scattershot remains of a world half-forgotten, the rose of some cataclysm. No landscape emerges, only these occcasional emergent shapes jutting from cloud and myth and memory. The bizzare and fantastic mingles with the banal - a steeple next to a monlith, a streetlight at the base of an impassable staircase leading nowhere. The features cling to no logic. 

There's too much going on for any arresting gesture to gain purchase. The scene is all tensions and chaos, mystical ferment. An endless cycle of construction and decay, mad science and esoteric ritual, conspiratorial cabals and individual nutcases, and accounting their projects and schemes, pinions of mazy purpose, at every scale from pigment to panorama. But still the swirling fogs obscure what's being built or brewed into a coherant vision. Only fragments succeed in resolving into fleeting reality before melthing away again. The maelstrom is too frenzied, the factions too far-flung, to tease a whole out of the parts, a gestalt to arise from the hectic tableau of mytho-scientific constructivism fermenting in the myriad aestolutes. The scene is the confluence of a hundred Loomings.
