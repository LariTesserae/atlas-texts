The air hangs heavy and still, laden with moisture that does not drip but clings close to the skin. Cool mist drifts between twisted rock formations, from which issue eager rivulets carrying the land's hidden waters to swell the streams that wander through the broken landscape. Exposed bedrock looms in lumpy mounds and serrated ridges, pale grey and slick under the damp. Puddles glimmer in every hollow, formed from no clear source. Mosses and ferns and tail-lichens drape across rock faces, giving green contrast to the monochrome grey-brown scene. 

No trees grow, only mats of compacted moss and yard-high grasses quivering sporadically in the recent stillness, blown by an invisible breeze. The rocks are crowded close, organically sculpted and inherently unstable. Crumbly scree spreads at the angles and edges. A cave mouths yawns darkly, dripping, under a precarious overhang. Strange, hunched shapes emerge from the rocks - stumps and boulders worn domot a witch-wood aspect. But they drip real water.

High on a bluff an angular structure emerges from the cliff-side, long-strided targets flame carved for windows, blind doorways, its surfaces slapped and patinaed with raw slabs of orange mold and tawny leaf-growth. Vines dangle from its stairs and lintels. Weeds tuft round the base, bristling green and seed-heavy. Mosses spatter across its skin, painting arabesques into the cracked stucco, pooled with redarps in saucer-sized flowers. A rank creeping single-lane passes by its base, carrying run-off and denser detritus towards the stony slopes. 

From a truncated apex, a blackened wood pole emerges, a tatter of fabric flaccid on its top, tinged green. Sites of human presence proliferate here - boards and tangles of scavenged wire salvage, dish-plates and handtools, tarping coving over platforms and tunneled below. Sounds: the air smells of mud and the hidden run of water, and shifts with animal-presence, subtle. Yet: listening seraously: something shifts rough and scratching somewhere among the detritus, heaving and dambled.
