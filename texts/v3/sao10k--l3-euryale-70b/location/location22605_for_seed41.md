Deep within an ancient, eroded canyon system, a small side canyon opens into the remains of an old lakebed. The terrain is generally below surface level, its elevation at the bottom of stone walls etched by eons of scarce rainfall and flash floods. The walled basin that was once a lake is now a peculiar oasis, accumulating moisture from rare rains, seeps, and ground flow. 

The air holds a heavy atmosphere, thick with humidity and sweltering heat. Water tables obscurely feed seeps and gumboots, living thickets of wetland plants.
