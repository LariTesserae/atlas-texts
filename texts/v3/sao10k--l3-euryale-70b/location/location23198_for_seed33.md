The world is a barren crystalline desert stretching infinitely under a starless void. The terrain is a vast sea of salt flats and sandy dunes etched by millennia of stinging winds. Shallow depressions are encrusted with glittering salt, cracked and rimmed by huge upturned crystals like shattered glass. Nothing organic lives here, only lustrous mineral deposits in strange colors painting alien geological formations. Crystalline towers rise like stalagmites or lightning-cleaved spires, and vast impact craters pock the surface. The whole landscape flows like a slow tide, surfaces and depths constantly shifting in a stone dance.

Deep in the hearts of the craters and caverns, pulsating luminescent fungal blooms the size of a room dissolve slowly into the air, shedding bioluminescent spores that drift up like high-altitude clouds. They drift across the void and fall in weird tides and whirlpools, glowing faintly except where they touch solid ground.

Occasionally, a lonely cry rings out, some lost animal roaming the darkness in desperation, hunting the salt-caked fossils that dot the surface and fill the craters, petrified forever. Gravity seems weaker here, or distorted - nothing moves quite as it should. Crystals form in impossible shapes, minerals pool and flow like water. Fractured currents flow through the bedrock like lightning or fire and erupt in geysers of luminous stone. The surface can bulge and warp in impossible ways as unseen forces toss and turn the subterranean sea of magma.

Rust-covered ruins and shadowy structures of mobile metal are strewn across the landscape - broken machines and vehicles frozen in strange crystallization of salt and sand. Crater walls are peppered with ancient trapped devices, sensors and generators long frozen in a state of half-activation. Shattered robots and engines sit belly-up like beached fish.
