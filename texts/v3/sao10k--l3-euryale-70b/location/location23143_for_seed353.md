In the perpetual sunlight of a scalding red sun, a vast, relentless expanse stretches to a distant horizon, where rocky promontories claw at the dark sky. This is a world of towering crystalline formations, a forest of glittering mineral growths unlike any organic plants. The geometric, faceted structures intertwine in a mass of solid petrified convolutions, growing from the granular rust soil like fractured obsidian trees. 

The forms are whorled and tightly-packed, a compressed tangle of what could almost be called foliage. Thorny, angular limbs intertwine at random angles, showing no organization or branching pattern like natural trunks. They clamber over each other in a struggle for purchase, twisting up to the oppressive crimson firmament. Each entity seamlessly fuses with its neighbors into one impossible stony mass.

Shimmering in brilliant slashes of every color, the crystalline material refracts the bleak light into scintillating patterns, each surface entertaining its own self-contained architecture within interlacing planes. Internal geometries repeat fractally in a permanently throbbing alien growth that seems to swarm within itself. Impossible Indiescent hues bleed from intersecting facets in a ceaseless boil.

No air stirs between the forms—or there simply is no air. A complete vacuum seems to grip the eternally unchanging scene. Only a complete static of silence oppressive even to a deafened void—until a deep unearthly drone rises from the cooling bodies, a sound generated not acoustically but as an electromagnetic field that sizzles at the boundary of perception. The horrific vibration oscilates through the substrata beneath the jeweled forest and the vacuum, projecting an impossible deathly stillness. 

Ancient beyond comprehension, this place seems at once exploding and frozen in time. The crystalline structures, though brutally jagged, have clearly grown seamlessly together and have yet been weathered and eroded, worn to a smooth shine: but the process has been going on for unimaginable eons. As it now occurs at a speed on the boundary between one's perceptions—every action seems crazed but all is revealing itself almost too slowly. The roiling crystalline miasma seethes in frenzied internal uproar, glittering like the richest shards of facetted multitude sliced from a jewel, the ululating array of refracted hues strobing in uncanny patterns to the superaudial note of the substrate's vision.
