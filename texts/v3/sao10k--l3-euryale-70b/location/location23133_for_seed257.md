Scant light filters down to the artificial floor. In the dimness, forms nearly merge with shadow. Primitive fabrication has spun out a meld of organic and mechanical in this alien under-sea environment. Sinuous pipes wind by, pulsing with fluid. Cables and fibers wire crystalline sheets that might be windows.

Slow currents waft through the dark spaces, carrying faint telltale sounds - metallic groans, gurgling, hissing. The occasional bioluminescent flask-fungus flickers and drifts, casting its eerie pale glow.

Amidst cyclopean supports, ethereal 'creatures' undulate and burble to and fro between meandering channels in turgid liquid. Decked out with gills and swim bladders, more than organic or mechanical, they move languorously. They seem sluggish, dim, and simple compared to the intelligence to build this place.
