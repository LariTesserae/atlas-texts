Dim red light filters in from above, casting eerie crimson hues across the spongy, fungal growths carpeting the cave floor. The walls drip with slime, pulsating gently as currents of viscous fluid flow through an extensive network of organic tubes. Shallow pools bubble and hiss from escaping gases. The entire space is humid and fetid, thick with the stench of decay and nutrient-rich water.

Tendrils of translucent fiber stretch between the fungal clumps, pulsing with internal bioluminescence. Rivulets of milky salts flow over them, carrying floating clusters of spores. One fungal growth towers over the others, a tangled mass of warty swellings and spore-filled pods. Tendrils sway from its bulbous cap, as if reaching to snare prey.

The fluid filling most of the cave is swirling with suspended particles - dirt, debris and clots of bioluminescent organic matter. It heaves and surges as if convection currents are fermenting it from within, but there is no defined surface to mark the rim between fluid and the slick walls above. Crusted scabs of fungal membrane cover the ledges, inky capillaries tracing between.

Droplets of shimmering fluid bead and drip from the ceiling growths, seeping in stalactic hesitantaren that spike downward like magma towers. The slime drips onto the shelves in viscous rivulets and spatters little circular impact-patterns on its surface. Swirling currents beneath the surface are constantly sinking into and through the fungal shelfs.

Deep whorls churn in the fluid, with no evident surface. Debris sweeps along to disappear into shadowy crevices in the walls, while slug-like tendrils of fungal hypha glisten in the red dimness, curl arapidly, and then leap up to snag at decaying clots of matter as they drift past, roping them in viscous slime that ropes and clings like cold molasses and froms them into soupy newly-made fungall colonies. Heaved eruptions from below drive skeins of glistening flocculant exhaling from vicinity of the tunnels. Skeins of pulp and fibers shield burrowing tendrils delving into mats of rich opaque rafts floating. Gossamers of slim rope and gossamer moss drift up and snag with the lid of its pulsing fungal ooze.
