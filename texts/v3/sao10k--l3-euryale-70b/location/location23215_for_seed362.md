The place is a vast, flat plain stretching endlessly in every direction. What little erosion exists here has ground the terrain down into a fine grey dust. The ground is hard-packed but there is no visible bedrock or stone - just a dull expanse of ashen soil sharpened by wind into drifting mounds and gust-carved ripples.

Overhead, the sky is blanketed in a pale lavender haze. Reminiscent of a perpetual sunset, but the light is diffuse and weak - neither like morning nor dark. The sun itself is hidden, perhaps a dim orb hovering at the horizon, or perhaps certain never to rise again. Beneath the fluorescent yet drained light, wild riotous plant growth covers the ground.

Gigantic ferns unfurl from the earth, thick trunks and rearing heads of sprouts and fronds that sway and strive, casting filtered shrouds of green-tinted shade. Bulbous, waxy stems crowd between the ferns, ropes of impossibly thick vines lacing overhead into an irregular tangle. Puff-ball toadstools ten feet tall crowd like sentinels. Misted creatures that may be flowers at this scale writhe and slime through the snagged undergrowth. Colors are muted despite the profusion of life - hues of olive, moss, slate. The vegatation seems to be in ceaseless overgrowth, expansion and decay not a cycle but simultaneous states.

Running through this gardless jungle, rusty steel beams and discarded engines emerge at strange angles, draped in vines and scaly with lichen. Rusted pipes as wide as a house gutter forth wisps of acrid steam. Some mechanical structures loom, improbable - serrated wheels, collapased catwalks, suspened hooks dripping with thick chains. Whatever laboratory or industry once resided here has long collapsed into transformation by the all-saturating greenery. Weathered robot shapes sag and clank in the foliage.

In the distant skies, machines pass like stars - fleets of propeller-driven airships leave ion-scoured trails across the pale heavens. Shimmering mirages give way to pillar of smoke dark on the horizon - a furnace volcano fuming a sorrowful encore. The air is choked with particolates; scented with smoke and petroleaum and dyes but no lack of truffles.

A complete, content entropy haunts the place. The plants, the machines, the light they cannot it even be said is evolving, or achiving stases but opacity, impedance, the delay, entropy, a suffusing stalemate of conflicting cosmic signage - like a jigsaw made of a million indentical shapes one of which the saline is the only one with no match. The sky never changes. The growth grows imperceptibly. It cannot even be said to change. The air just more and more and more like itself. This is an equilibrium in motion - the poiise of glacial war for control over no space. The combination is a complete, oppressive, plastic sense of neverending change tooachieved te lag behind to be arrives.
