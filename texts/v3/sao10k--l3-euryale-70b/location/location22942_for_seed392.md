The terrain undulates gently like a series of imperceptible waves, the surface of a vast swampy lake that has long-since evaporated. Each rounded hill top is thick with ephemeral marsh plants - towering reeds, blotches of lily pads, cattails and floating algal blooms of emerald, turquoise and violet. Water oozes from the grey-brown soil of the hill sides, dripping and trickling, but ever present. The air is humid and thick, water droplets condensed on every surface from the exhalations of the vegetation.

In the channels between the hills, crystal-clear water flows quietly, so limpid it seems still. It trickles and rills from the weeping sediment, evaporating back to the sky without pause. Flapping and croaking, birds are ubiquitous, herons, ibises, egrets, feeding on abundant fish and frogs in the shallows. Lizards and turtles bask on sun-struck logs. Hummingbirds zip between waterlilies sipping nectar, emerald and sapphire feathers flashing like coins in the gas-flame heat. All drift aimlessly, lethargically, becalmed by the soporific weather.

The landscape hangs still and timeless in the heavy haze, unchanging under the blinding white sky, a photographic negative of itself. All the distances and elevation change and motion seems expressible only through the angle of the opalescent light, the shadows shrunken to black points at midyear high noon. No tangibles are possible - merely drifting steamy views through vistas of airy dilution. 

A world of uniform, uneventful stability, water and vegetation and heat enjoy a stasis, splayed before an unseeing eye, too bright to behold for long, no more than an ensnared snapshot of a reality arisen and now unchanging, all becoming is becoming then now tableau. Foreground, midpoint and distance dissolve, no composition persists in the featureless immensity, this amnion of mind - bombo khorus: The Swell.
