Weathered limestone cliffs rise up to the pale, wintery sky. Jagged peaks and ridges alternate with hidden canyons carved by infrequent but ferocious rains when the wet ocean winds blow in. The stone is weathered to a ghostly sallow color, bleached by the feeble sunlight and leached of anything but stubborn lichenous greens and bone-dun residue. 

The landscape is vast, a seemingly infinite archipelago of karst islands each crowned by a remnant forest, more fungal than floreal.
