The landscape opens to a vast, dimly lit valley, stretching to the horizon in every direction under a low, mist-shrouded sky. Scattered across the shale soil, which weeps a pungent brackish water, rise shattered rock formations in earthy reds and grays. Mosses, lichens, and fungal plumes are the only visible vegetation, clinging to the craggy surfaces or carpeting the wet ground.

Docile packs of saucer-eyed furred quadrupedal creatures graze on the fungi, their snouts snuffling through the mists. They constantly twitch and flick their ears at the eternal, cyclopean din that echoes across the valley from the craggy walls - the creaking and grinding of stone on stone, the dripping of the black brackish water, the burbled croaks of uncertain birds. 

Amidst the rubble and tussocks stand the scattered remains and roughly hewn monuments of some ancient, forgotten civilization - squat oblong tower-platforms, sections of arches and churned stones, jagged rusting metal posts. Glowing feltyclad nomads sometimes appear, trudging through the mists and chittering to each other in lip-smacking argots as they plunder or tend to the artifacts.

The air is cool and heavy with petrichor. Flakes of grit drift down from the bruised sky which finally cracks with a peal of thunder. Plumes and spumes of rain and brine lash and scour the ancient stones and statured fungal mats. Lightning flickers behind the clouds. The creatures huddle to the lee of boulders and megaliths. The wind snarls through the rubble like a bestiail troll-kin. The storm gathers like a truelove about to consummate with the thirsty land...
