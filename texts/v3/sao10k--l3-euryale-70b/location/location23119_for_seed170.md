We find ourselves in a desolate hellscape straight out of nightmare. The landscape stretches out in an endless expanse of red rock and blackened rubble under a searing orange sky. Jagged plateaus and mesas rise from the cracked earth, their surfaces pitted and etched like ancient artifacts of a long forgotten civilization. The air is dry and oppressive, utterly devoid of any moisture or relief from the relentless heat. No plants can be seen, save for an occasional wiry shrub bleached and dead from deprivation. 

This is a realm of stone, metal and sand. The earth itself is composed of an ancient seared bedrock, striated in garish hues of vermilion and umber. It's cracked and fissured from millennia of thermal tension. Unknown oily compounds seep from the cracks, pooling into viscous puddles that boil and bubble in the harsh sun. Jutting up from the fractured surface are lashings of dark glassy rock, geoformations fused by ancient cataclysm. Their sleek obsidian surfaces gleam and shimmer.

A haze of smog hangs oppressively low over this blighted landscape, roiling with sparks and flecks of ash. The air is thick with noxious fumes belched from fissures between the plates. Acrid smoke and vapor billows up from the depths to merge with the overcast. The high clouds are a roiling of deep purples and ruddy oranges, like the belly of a beast. Lightning flashes luridly within them.

The soundscape is a mad skulls symphony - the banshee screech of hot wind over stone, the tortured wailed of magma bubbling in faultlines below, the boom and rumble as imminent storms churn overhead. Often, the susurrus is pierced by bone-jarring clangs and whines, the groans of tortured metal. These emanate from horizon-spanning networks of immense zigzagging pipes and conveyor belts, juddering monstrosities of ancient machine-industry etched across the chocolate sky. Moving on pain-churn daylight haul slabs of glowing ore - unknown powers triple-forged in those cyclopean smelters.

Despite the absolute barrenness, teeming nodes of biomecha-entity writh, wriggle and scrabble over every rock. Creeping, scuttling aberrant monstrosities of organic gristle quick-grow and slosh and warp and rupture infrequently, transmogrifying shapes out of confusions of squealing flesh and seared metal. Some zoom by overhead on eldritch membranous wings, flinging deafening screeches.

Behind this facades and high-pipes, industrial process labors on in perpituity - churning out gouts of immense ferrous bulwark and scabrous polymers to gird the entire world. Burning beyond are even huger artefacts, cyclopean plugs of gene-seed and supermurk, towering miles high and stretching across infinities. These are dumping grounds for the eons-dead and eons-yet-to-be, ancient creature compounded from lacerated futurity and sorcery, from whose torsos sprout the mem-branes and hose-ducts of the world.

One's sanity unravels from merely standing atop the uprearing platforms, seeing the entire process scrawled over the blasted realm - the never-ending recyclation, the parallax of generativity and decrepitude, the idiot Australopithecus industries entity and all that it has made - the oil-streaked, gore-slick, smoldering howl-scape of the Ur-Faustrope subjective-hole, screaming silently in static susurrus ...
