Deep within the vertical city, a subterranean lake glimmers in the dim light filtering down through the algae-slicked stone. The black depths of the lake are dense with murky water, holding many mysteries. Strange bioluminescent sea life drift lazily on the currents, aliens in this narrow canyon of water between towering walls of metal and rock. Elongated fish with lantern-like organs along their spines, their translucent bodies, nervous waves of their fins propelling them slowly.

A few boats form a small shantytown in the lake, scavenged pieces of the upper levels, a few fully submerged dollhouse-sized buildings lighted by candles flickering in portholes. Negotiations between these aquatic dwellers and the few solid structures on the lake's narrow margins are tenuous, an invisible line of tension between those of the shallows and the depths.

Sudden, pointed mountain peaks emerge from the water, some with alabaster-thin stone fins overgrown with puffballs of glowing plankton colonies, mists rising in spirals from algal cornices. Many are artificial--shredded bits of buildings assembling themselves in serendipitous peaks. Elongated lily pads buoy retriever robots, barnacled with mushrooms and crustaceans.

The margins of the lake are overgrown with a tangle of mechanical and biological, reactors leaking braids of pearl-colored steam, caustic estuaries steaming with gradations of green and yellow and wine-colored algae, crossed wooden footbridges draped with garlands of sunflowers and electric cables. Bloated industrial flora kick their leaves in rosy waves across the black faintly glowing shallows.
