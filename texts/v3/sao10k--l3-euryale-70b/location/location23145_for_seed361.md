A pocket valley, nestled high in ancient hills worn smooth by eons of gentle rains. Shimmering veils of mist cling to the concave slope, skimming a carpet of dense moss and fern in dappled shades of deep green. Towering trunks of noble trees, their gnarled branches green with tropical vines, hem the bowl of the vale. Flowers of vibrant hues spot the shadowed sward.

Amidst the-root-laced soil, half-buried, organic forms hint at the curves of structures, irregular and bulbous, like sea shells repurposed for habitation. Polished to an ethereal sheen, their matrices of self-reconfiguring nanotech glimmer in patterns that test the boundaries of human comprehension. These post-modern dwellings of some vanished sentience exist in symbiosis with the flora, borderwalls splintering with blossoms, doorways gaping on spore-rich inner chambers. Life and artifact, organic and inorganic, blur into a single smoothly-functioning biome.

The air pulses with a deep susurrus, ancient and avid as the hills. Overlays of insectile skitterings, gurglings of unseen effluents, the suspiration of mist inerting the valley bowl. Unrecognizable forms flit behind leaves; in the foreground, a sinuous ribbon of water winds from the feet of the structures, its crystalline surface undisturbed by the active lifeforms in its clear shallows - protozoans, regenerating limbs, arcane tumescences, drifting clouds that collect in beads toadally rained out at unpredictable intervals. 

Everything mingles, everything interpenetrates, but rythms of predation and growth, consumption and decay, hold a delicate balance. The techno-organic whole, perturbated by periodic untoward rains and morbid falls of spherules of flickering plasma, fluctuates within strict but inscrutable parameters. Some forgotten remnant soul-at-the-bottom glints in the surface of the bog in hues of electrons - passioning impartially the tectonic or genome alike, the inorganic or organic simultaneously, as it runs its cycles in its embedded valley.
