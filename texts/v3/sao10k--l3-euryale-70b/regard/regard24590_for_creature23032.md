Beholding this primordial valley, a benevolent intelligence would pause, deeply moved by the raw power and chaos of the teeming landscape. The sounds, the textures, the scents of this jungle canyon - it is a cacophony of life in extremis, a display of Nature's untamed capriciousness.

The wise entity would drift ghostlike on the mists, passing through dangling tendrils and curling around the towering trees. It would attune itself to the boulder-strewn riverbeds, the colonies of insects swarming the treesails and the microcosms of life hiding in the leaf-litter shadows. With a cosmic perspective, the power would perceive the ancient cycles of growth and decay, the peaceless war between floral and fauna as they strive against each other and against the land itself.

The fog would swirl as the entity trespassed among the writhing creepers, listening to the nearly subliminal sounds of scorpionesque life questing beneath the undergrowth. It would see the frenzied vegetative forms as saints straining into the sun, but simultaneously being strangled by their own greed, while at the same time throttling and smothering out any token competition in an endless circling of selfishness.

The entity would resonate with the sun-blasted texture of the leaf-stippled boulder faces, the cool veined masses brooding amidst the furnace of air. It would note the great disasters - the towering tree felled by invasive vines, the heaps of riving stone peeled from the cliffs in vast, slow cataclysms, the vanished clearings buried in endless tons of growth reclaimed by the biotic forces.

Most of all, this Name would feel the welter of chaotic life, the unreasoned tempers of light and water and substrate, and yet through that chaos, an accord, a vitality no different than its own centuries-weathered temple. The dark, deaf uproar of life in the uncharted unending folds echoes the roars of revels it had known down through the eons. It saw this collapsing, crumbling, rebuilding, in its cycle of death and greed and proliferation, and understood it with utmost intimacy.
