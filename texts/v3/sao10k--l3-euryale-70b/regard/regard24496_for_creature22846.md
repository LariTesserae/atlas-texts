The essence of a benevolent godlike being notices this vast interconnected web of life sharing their consciousness across the span of a continent through a matrix of fungal networks and archaeal colonies. 

In concert, this being decides to subtly assist and enhance the complexity and diversity of life in this world.

The god's first action is to subtly alter the ionic composition of the minerals leached from the stone into the slime secreted by the giant bracket fungi. The new ions, rare earth elements like scandium and lutetium, have a profound effect on the enzyme profiles produced by the archaea and demoithotrophs. The microbes evolve novel enzymes that now not only liberate more nutrients from the fungal secretions, but also cause the secretions to harden into a and form a glassy, calc-hardened matrix that stabilizes the fractured rock.

This first subtle intervention has a profound impact, allowing the trees to grow larger and more robust, their roots delving deeper to span greater reaches of the mycorelial network. They begin to express novel aromatic compounds in their bark and leaves that further shape the mix of species colonizing the soil at their roots and the pattern of minerals leached from their extensive reach of mycelial threads.

These compounds spill into the seas, and subtly shape the evolution of species there as well. Small changes in the composition of global seawater causes shifts in the skeletons of siliceous sponges, and these in turn alter the interstices between their crystalline blades, allowing deeper penetration of oxygen into the sediment and causing blooms of novel archaea that burrow oxygen into the anoxic soils.

These burrowing archaea, in turn, cause changes in the mineralization of dead biomass in the deep sediments, which compress into coal deposits that underlie the continent's crust. This alters the flow of geothermal energy into the mycorelial networks and the electromotive zones the archaea develop there. 

The loamy leaves of the trees absorb ionizing radiation from these emitters and use it to fuel novel areata of metabolism in the coremication between cells. The areata begin to tensile and fold in complex patterns, ultimately creating the lichen-glyphs that grow into the lichen-runes that first react to the subtle tremors and pressure shifts of the stone.
