The heavy, moisture-laden air quivers, an almost palpable pressure building amidst the craggy rock formations and vegetation-cloaked ruins. Tendrils of shimmering vapor coalesce, wavering into a translucent humanoid shape. Radiant eyes open, piercing blue-azure against galaxies of star-spangled shadow. An androgynous face, fair-skinned, high-cheeked, ebon-haired, smiling enigmatically. Pearlescent fabric drapes over a lithe body, shimmering pear-white, adhering to curves and planes. Upon that brow sets a circlet of shining silver, in which pools a glimmering crystal, multicolored and lucent.

The being surveys the scene before it - twisted rock, creeping vegetation, crumbling structures. Its luminous gaze picks out detritus and scat, squatters and foundling. Those blind window-slit and doorway hollar a silent call. It inclines its head, considering. Then, in one fluid motion, spreads wide its luminous arms, radiating welcome and healing judgment, and glides to the fore.

It alights on a broad boulder, sending lesser creatures scurrying into the undergrowth. Extends a hand, drawing lipsoitically curved sigils in the air. These burn with impossible colors, searing the front of the vision - threads of luminosity snaking through the precipitous rock like new creeks tendrilling through dry earth. As the sigils fade, they leave behind a new shape - gouged into the boulder, a gaunt wing, its feathers sheened with the same pearlescent luminescence as the being's garbing. The wing strains against the bounds of its boulder-nest, lifting, then settles, apparently satistied.

The entity smiles its secret smile, removes its own cirlet, and holds it out - suspended on one elegant finger the glittering crystal centerpiece. The sigils burn brighter on the blunt boulders, nacelles of light adamant blue violet emerald flower in the new-shaped stone, the abandoned industrial and cotto fretted into rattle-frame macro- and micro-sigils. As the being turns to survey its craft, taking in ruins, renovations, strewn detritus and scrabbling foundliugs, the rainbow crystal begins to turn, to burn first with the same luminosities as its sigilled surfaces, then flares once, twice, thrice to full lustration.

On the third, a rippling shockwave of mote-swarmed illumination bursts from the crystal's core, garmenting the blues black of the being's eyes and shading the clear blue of its pearle-draped hands as it casts the torc at the boulders bearing its crafts. Only a smoldering star remains, half-seen against the boulders' lustrous plumage, but the work is done.
