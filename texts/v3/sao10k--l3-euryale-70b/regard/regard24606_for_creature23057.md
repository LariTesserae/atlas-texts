The wise and benevolent power gazed upon this alien, communal underground world with profound compassion. They saw the interconnected web of life, how the fungi and vegetation were the very foundation that sustained the entire ecosystem.

The power's great heart swelled with reverence for the natural order inherent in this place. In their wisdom, they knew that to help this realm, they must support and enhance its unique organic balance, rather than impose their own will.

And so, the benevolent power gifted this world with subtle energies that permeated the fungal mycelium. These energies gently pushed the very limits of life here, encouraging the deepest, healthiest growth. The hyphae absorbed more nutrients, the spores germinated more readily, the symbiotic bonds between organisms deepened.

Fungal towers grew faster and stronger, with mightier stems and tougher mycelium. They exuded more nutrient-rich chyma to feed their partners. The hyphae itself thickened, forming sturdier webbing to bind soil in place. Microscopic mycelium rippled and flexed, extending root networks to reach water and minerals in even the deepest crevices.

Microbes that fixed nitrogen and phosphorus crowded into the soil, renewing fertility. Specialized parasites that attacked fungi were suppressed, while helpful insects multiplied. Trees surged upwards, their leaves glittering with dew, their roots pillared with clinging mycelium. The canopy knit ever denser, weaving a luminous secondary forest untangled by vine and stalk.

Aquatic fungi shot fungal towers from the ponds, pink and saffron caps glistening. Parasitic blights on fauna faded away, and ancient symbionts lying dormant in DNA kindled anew. The power's touch miscarried death and decay, so the dung and corpses were absorbed and processed swiftly by the hyphae, not fouling the water.

Metals like copper and vanadium, crucial for growth, funneled into the plants and fungi to accelerate their mighty burgeoning. Bolsters of geosmin and mycogens exressed into the air but did not become over-abundant and toxic. All was in balance and harmony.

The power guided and mediated where symbionts clashed or went eruptive, imposing more balanced relationships. Genetic memory allowed them to evolve and change to better fill niches, but mutations did not leap into pathogenicity. It worked subtly, almost undetectable day to day, but the diaphanous transition in this world would be truly magnificent in a year, and resplendent in a decade.

In the end,  this place became the jewel of underground realms - unnaturally bountiful and luminous and thriving, alive with balance and generosity and harmony, a living example of the transcendant potential of life when harnessed to the whims of a guiding hand and great heart.
