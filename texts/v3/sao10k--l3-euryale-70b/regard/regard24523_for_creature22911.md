Peering out from the cyclopean, lapidarian undergrowth, leering, hunchbacked and hirsute figures cower and fly-buzz. Their skin is the deep brown of rich soil, pebbled and gnarled like bark. Long, bones-in dirt-matted hair creeps from their scalps and down their backs like spanish moss. Bulbous eyes, saucy with rheum, peer from beneath hair-thatched brows. Nasal labials and masticatory dribble slime down their jaws and spatter onto their hair-matted chests, where they mingle with great, flaming brand marks that boil under their skin in rosettes and arboresce. Bent, knotty fingers clench around simple stone axes and crude, fire-hardened spears.
