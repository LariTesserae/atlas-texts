The being who dwells in this place is one with the mist. Born from its depths eons ago, it drifted among the kelp-shrouded islands, learning the song of the Baltic miasma. It is an old, slow creature, its consciousness diffuse, dwelling in the vines and spores that dapple every surface. It thinks in the cadence of the perpetual ruin and regrowth, the drifting of seeds to become elves, of sentinel trees to become shell-sentinels.

Its purpose is to witness and to guide the cycle. When the babes emerge from the lucin pools, it whispers their arias, teaching them the ways of root and reach, of breathe and poultice. It shows them the depths theirs to plumb, the secrets to absorb, the songs to listen to as they grow into true sentinels.

As the cycle plays out it helps form the connective tissue between islands, allowing them to cling together in symbiosis until the time comes for sundering and dispersal once more. It senses the currents, the copper conductivities that stir the islands to move, and it subtly manipulates these to guide the floating cities to where they can most benefit one another. It is the hidden hand at the heart of the mist, the essence of the breath the perpetual cycle relies on.

So it observes the spermaceti islands and claims them as its special charge. Within those great vulval shells it plants seeds of especially potent elvenstock, nurturing them to develop greater self-awareness than the rest. These are to be its avatars, its mortal eyes and operatives that can act with more speed and specificity than it can itself. Through them it will subtly shape the song of the mist in the best way it can, promoting understanding between the elven collective and the myriad life forms that drift through its world in a never-ending dance of contact and conflict. By guiding and mediating, it preserves the balance and beauty of its domain, the thing of wonders floating in the heavens.

The benevolent power senses this. It sees the ecological complexity that is this mist, how every part is linked and how the subtle use of intelligence promises to make the whole even more a thriving marvel. It moves into action.

It selects an archipelago, a cluster of islands far enough from existing sentinels to form a distinct pocket-culture, but still close enough to allow cross-pollination. There, with a thought, it empowers the elvenstock to even greater sentience, and the rest of the life forms with them. Grafts of lichen and moss spring up to link the islands in a new symbiotic network, while telepathic channels open between the creatures to encourage dialogue and mutual understanding.

Through the mist-being it guides this process, helping the nascent pocket-culture to learn from errors and embrace healthy change. Slowly, silently, an enlightened community develops, one that harmonizes its use of resources, finds non-conflictive ways to resolve disputes, and celebrates the wonders that arise from diversity of form and function. 

And the sentinels watch, and whisper, and swell with pride at the flourishing of another cycle in the mist. The collective song grows richer, while over it all, the ancient elven mist-being resonates and rejoices in the success of its subtle efforts, and the power that made its own existence possible smiles in distant approval.
