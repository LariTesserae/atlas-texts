A philanthropic force observing this wretched world would recognize the absolute suffering and degradation of the pitiful beings eking out a cruel existence in this nightmarish hellscape. 

With wisdom and benevolence, the power would see that these beings have been utterly exploited, their bodies warped and minds shattered by generations of brutal oppression at the hands of the ruthless tyrants who rule this blighted domain.

To ameliorate their situation, the benevolent entity would begin by shrouding the land in a soothing, regenerative energy. This sacred radiance would gently unstick the choking pollution from the air and water, neutralizing the noxious effluent and fumes belching from the hellish factories. 

Next, tendrils of healing light would reach out to caress the sickly, broken bodies of the suffering populace. Prosthetic limbs and organs would be dissolved away, replaced with new flesh grown from their own cells by the power's restorative essence. Sores, mutations and malformations would be erased, leaving behind unblemished skin and only a dim memory of past traumas.

Entering their minds, the gentle entity would begin the prolonged work of mending rents in their sanity caused by ceaseless overwork, hopelessness and terror. Traumatic memories would be calmed and contextualized, starved emotions fed with nurturing love. Stable psychoses would be dissolved, the indwelling horrors and loathings negated. The shrieking of tortured minds would quiet.

An illusion of relief would fall over the hellscape, the monstrous structures and ignescent blight softened, veiled by a vision of a world made livable once more. Meager hovels would seem to vaporize, replaced in the minds of the people by images of modest but well-maintained shelters. Rubble and waste would transmogrify into untainted earth and water. 

With this phantasmagoria in place, the true work of the morphic power would commence. Speaking into minds remade receptive by its curative touch, the entity would teach its children of a conception of freedom and hope. Patiently, it would instruct in the day-to-day arts of living beneficently alongside the land. It would educate in methods of cultivation and husbandry adapted to the world's conditions. It would nurture trust, muticaliciency, and solidarity between the fractured remnants of society.

Late, having grown to encompass the whole of the plateau and sere vales beyond in its uncanny vincibility, the power would release the minds of its wards from its tutelage. Sapient and iron-willed, they would at last be free to bring into being a civilization enlightened by its bounty - a lone ember of virtue and moderation amidst a world of error.
