Before this broken scene, a wise and benevolent power gazes with deep dismay. The scorched, polluted remains of nature have been utterly despoiled by industrial rapacity. The dehumanized laborers toil in bleak squalor, their identities and souls ground down. The very air chokes with smoke and grime.

Despite the abyss of desolation, the generous entity chooses not to unleash its full power to destroy this hellscape or punish those responsible, for it sees spark of hope. Instead, the being imbues the suffering laborers with fleeting visions of a utopian future where their labor is honored and the land restored.
