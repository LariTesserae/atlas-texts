The cavern is stirring from its long slumber. A wise and benevolent power, drawn by the strange beauty of this timeless place, reaches out with a gentle flow of energy. Its presence is like a warm caress against the damp stone walls. 

The light filtering down from above seems to brighten ever so slightly, casting a comforting glow across the cavern floor. More light! The viridian moss and lichen quicken, unfurling new growth to cloak the rocks in soft down. Delicate white morels push through the leaf litter, unfurling to release delicate clouds of spores that swirl through the cavern.

Beneath the bubbling spring, pale newts and toads hatch from their eggy sacs, their slick forest-green bodies slipping into the crystal water. Water-striders dance in widening ripples across the spring's surface.

The colony of lumpy fungi encrusting the old bear bones begins to pulse and swell. Opalescent caps pop open, releasing plumes of spores that waltz through the chamber on unfelt breezes. They dust the moss and fringe fern in powdery veils, hastening new generations. Misted tendrils of mycelium creep across the stone, knitting rotting wood and leaf into rich black loam.

Emerald shoots spear up through decaying litter as the chamber awakens. Pale roots erupt like fingers from the walls, sifting and turning stones as they length and burrow. Vines uncurl, trailing luminous bellflowers that glisten with sticky nectar. Bats spiral out from their perches, their membranous wings stirring unseen currents.

On the vast stone humps, spectral mosses quiver and creep. Tiny light-filled tendrils feel through cracks, probing for hidden weaknesses. With a slow, inexorable grinding, the rock begins to shift. Blunted stone gates yawn open, revealing hidden chambers stuffed with flaking, dusty scrolls. Brands of nourishing water seep from the stone, as if the ancient humps are remembering ways they had forgotten. 

The clammy toad-fat knobs drift and waltz across the jade and copper mirror of the pool, fins flexing. They crack open one by one, disgorging frilled peltate fronds that undulate and unfurl in the antiseptic iris-hazel water - new stages in an eternal symphony.

The sentient spores drift and twine through the cavern on loving currents, seeping into every hidden croft and cavern. A silent voice flows through their vegetal pelves, stirring a slow reveille through the stone and loam. Never resting.
