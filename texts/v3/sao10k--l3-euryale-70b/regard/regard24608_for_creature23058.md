Across the desolate, wind-scoured plateau, an ancient and secret race known as the Chimerae have made their home. These beings are a dozen species, each remade by some primeval art, bondageed in symbiosis of muscle, mind, and spirit to become one amorphous individual. Griffolithus Rex, a powerful Chimerae pack-lord, sits now upon a rocky pinnacle as his pterosaur mount shifts beneath him.

From his lofty perch, Griffolithus surveys the harsh landscape, eyes narrowed behind silver prisms as he studies the distant conurbation with appraisal. Geometric towers of inconceivable height thrust towards the sky in a megalopolis writ large across the haze-shrouded plain. A diffuse discoloration hangs over the vast cityscape, belching petrochemicals and soot into the crisp high air.

Around Griffolithus, his fellow pack-lords confer through astral channels carried on ultramicro fungal hyphae and bioluminescent pterosaur tissues. "I distrust them, the corpse-brothers," Griffolithus says his voice carried on sulfur ducts as his many-fathered mind synchronizes with his necessities. "I know they would rather synthesize the noble Tirthak as much as myself if they could, though my five fathers saw an opening to ensure my throne via the Great Laws of Variation."

The majority of Griffolithus's sororal colleagues emit groaks of assent over the noosphere. Skarlion needs to learn what their governors intend. And that the Team council of Tirthak profit from it but will try to re-suckle on my crown! 

Griffolithus' several minds conceive the political ploys and shifting allegiances of his Chimerae consanguinies. It is ever so - a delicate balance of power between the various factions, enacted on winds that buffet the barren upland and the distant cityscape.

Griffolithus knows such politicking has shaped his kind for eons. Through ancient art and genetic remaking, the Chimerae have transcended their original forms, knitting together into beings of many parts. And yet the same old contests still play out - an instinctual need to protect territory, to prevail against rivals, to expand influence.

The humans below in the metropolis have plainly achieved great things, if their works are anything to judge by. To Griffolithus' mind, their sheer scale of endeavor is a mixture of awe and unease. Such ability hints at science deep and applications more potent. What commerces could unlock mastership of the aether itself?

Griffolithus turns ancient Chimerae eyes towards his own homeland - the stubborn tufts of wiry brush clinging to every crevice, the stony pinnacles and arid upland flats. His land, his people, his interests. Griffolithus thrums his fingerclaws against bone-shields deep in consideration.

He must find out what to make of all this. Decide how the displays of the upstart corpsebrothers might offer gain or peril for the Chimerae, and how to position himself with it. Griffolithus lifts his head to survey the vast plateau stretching out before him. His people's history is written upon every rock and in their genes. And it is up to him to secure their place in this wider world... somehow.
