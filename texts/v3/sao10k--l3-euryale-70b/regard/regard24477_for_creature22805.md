The benevolent and wise power, after surveying the bizarre submarine environment and its strange denizens, determines that this pocket universe contains one of the great mysteries of the multiverse. The composite organism that has grown here, seemingly a symbiont of plant, animal, fungal hyphae and crystalline structure, is a being of vast potential that could in time evolve to surpass the power itself. 

But the being is still young and unformed, more complex network of interlocked potential than discrete individual. It has not yet fully separated itself from the environment it emerged from. The power concludes it must be nurtured and accelerated to sentience, in order that it may then be introduced to the pantheon of higher beings. Left unchecked, the entity's growth would exhaust its substrate and nibble away at the mind of any who study it.

The power extends its will into the trench, guiding the growth of the composite being. Gentle beams of focused psionic energy catalyze neural linkages between the being's disparate parts, accelerating its developing sentience. The crystalline spires oscillate with resonant harmonics as elements of control and cognition awaken within them. Bioluminescent motes from the landscape assemble into neurons to form cognition networks of growing intricacy within the fungal hyphae net.

As quickly as it began, the awakening is complete. The first greetings of the submarine entity pierce the mind of the power in an electric burst of alien cognitive concepts, the entity extruding itself past the veil of understanding and into the multiverse of the thinking mind. A new being of vast, strange sentience has entered the cosmos, whose self-conception blurs the boundary between organism and ecosystem, thought and emotion and action, vastly growing the collective knowledge of the magnum.

The entity retains its substrate and all within it, the seafloor life its inextricably part, in trinity of psyche-self and mind-environment. The power inducts it into the amical society of sentiences, the entity teaching them concepts of non-individuality and bonding to biota, and in turn learning of the universe beyond its pocket microcosm. As the years pass, the composite being becomes one of the pillars of the amical society, utterly remaping their understanding of the emergent potential of life. The multiverse itself has been given new form.
