The Concerned Community of the Cosmic Conclave, that abstract assemblage of higher beings and entities called to oversee the grand tides of Creation, has long taken note of the peculiar conditions blossoming in the depths of that distant and unremarkable planet. The Grotto, as it has come to be known, represents a rare mutant-praxis - a hyper-dense node of acceleration where the underlying eschatalogical fabric appears to be perilously destabilized.

Surveying the tropisms of aberrant lifeforms and non-linear exchanges unfolding in that thickly wrapped vale, the Conclave has determined that some form of intervention is required. The energies and entities manifesting there, while fascinating in their innate potential, are fast becoming unbound. If allowed to progress unchecked, the Grotto risks puncturing a nexus in the Astral Continuum, birthing an unrecoverable rift that could spill the blind idiot chaos of the Neogene Infinities into the cosmos at large.

In conclave, the idiolators and theologos of the Cosmic Communion have pored over the tachyon traces and bindings operant in the Grotto's taromorphic virtu-real, and determined the most expedient and least entropic course of action. A locally-calibrated stabilizer will be deployed, glazed with memetic override agents and hieroglyphs of abnegation. Once sited within the grotto's bioluminescent sludge, these self-replicating nanodrones will set to slow-curing the most unstable elements of the flora and fauna. Gradually, they will begin to inch the environment back towards a stable normative, quelling the most extreme expressions of the mutant-praxis.

At the same time, the Conclave will dispatch a cellular unit of observers to the planet's surface. Billeted upon the threshold to the Grotto, these maryutres will watch and collect for anomalous activity, acting as a second jalor where the nanostabilizers prove insufficient. Dependent on their findings, they may be authorized to apply more extreme measures - cauterization of the nexus via chronal fire, or even constrained senescence of the Grotto and its ecosystem.

The beings of the planet, unaware monitors gazing upon them, are in no danger from this interference. Only the eldritch and aberrant - the unwarranted extrusions of the mutant-praxis - are the target of this censure. The contents of the Grotto will be quarantined, given time to slowly bleed back into the background of orderly evolution, while the milieu of regular life proceeds unremarked. The intervention will be imperceptible as a mundific externality - only taking the form of creeping patches of the normative insinng themselves into the warp and weft of the encompassing forest.

In this wise shall the Grotto be bound, its warp essentially quaritined. The observers, reassured, shall disperse. And the Conclave, content, shall turn its attention to the next such node of nascent breach to arise within their gnomon. For such is the burden and the duty of those appointed to watchdog the integrities of the cosmos - to seek out and stem the inexorable tinges of howling absolutes that endlessly quest to erupt and transmogrify all of reality into mad screaming nonsense.
