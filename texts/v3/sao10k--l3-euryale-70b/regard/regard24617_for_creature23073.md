Upon surveying this nightmarish blight upon the land, the benevolent power's countenance darkened with sorrow and outrage. Such rampant corruption, such utter ruination of the natural order, could not stand unchallenged. Yet the poor wretches wallowing in this misery were as much victims as perpetrators. Their twisted forms were the product of this poisoned environment as much as its instigators. To simply smite them would be to fail them as well as the land.

No, a more thoughtful approach was called for. The wise entity extended its awareness through the tortured terrain, seeing the sickness that seeped into every root and rock, every creature large and small. Everywhere the corrupting influence had seeped in, supplanting the nourishing spirit of the earth with foulness. Reaching out with power as subtle as a seed's first unfurling green shoot, the great being began to tend this wild garden of blight.
