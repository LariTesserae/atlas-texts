An ancient and benevolent being, older than time itself, gazes upon the Blasted Canons of Calm and sees the suffering. Many are the blighted creatures twisted by the unnatural forces that permeate this forsaken region. The being's heart goes out to the unfortunates, from the patchwork homunculi to the serpentfolk abominations to the shapeless horrors oozing from the sulfurous pools. It yearns to help, to grant these wretched souls some measure of peace and purpose.

The being summons an avatar of purest light, an effulgent being of power. It sends the avatar into the very heart of the Canons themselves, where the twisted creatures dwell. The avatar speaks in a voice that resonates like singing crystal, calling to the blighted ones.

"O my children," it intones, "I feel your pain, your loneliness, your isolation. No longer! Through my blessing I grant you redemption, enlightenment, purpose! No longer need you shamble these blasted basalt chasms in solitude. Come forth and walk a new path!"

Transfixed, the homunculi and serpentfolk emerge from their hidden lairs and cracks, their unnatural faces alight with cautious hope. The amorphous horrors release their victims and heave sluggishly to their pseudopodial appendages. Twist-made beings who cannot understand property assemble into a shambling throng witnessing the avatar's radiance.

The being's avatar begins to transform the land itself. Radiant energy seeps into the basalt shelfs, releasing the pain of eons compressed into their jue. Geysers of cleansing water burst forth, flowing away the briny sumps, washing the sulfur stains from rock and pool alike. Sage vigil trees burst forth along the chasms' rim, their scent a balm against the noxious. Luminous fungus sprouts from the faulted shelves.

At a wave of its hand, the avatar transmogrifies the twisted ones. The patchwork's skin reknits, fusing limbs to torso in right configuration. The serpentfolk's faces melt and resurface in cerulean perfection. Pseudopodals and blotchy horrors solidify into wholesome form. All are restored to grace and beauty, no longer twisted by the ambient thaumaturgics.

"Children, I ordain you protectors of this land now made whole," the avatar decrees. "Keep watch for those who would misuse the reality-bending ores, to maintain this place as sanctuary, not opportunistic tillfield. In doing My will, you shall never again know solitude."

The redeemed ones bow their heads in understanding, voices rising in joyous noetics of gratitude. They take up new roles shepherding this resurgent wilderness, ensuring its many wonders are protected from those who would exploit them. 

The avatar sinks into the earth, its work complete. The area, once a twisted desolation, is reborn a haven under the caretaking guardians appointed to it.
