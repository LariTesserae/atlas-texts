Visiting the Floor of the Sump-Grid is not a hike or an exploration; it is an act of total immersion inside an unshielded, continuously sweating planetary engine. To an unprotected human body, this environment is lethal in under twelve seconds—not through sudden malice, but through the sheer mechanical indifference of heat, kinetic acoustic energy, radiation, and boiling runoff.

If you are an ordinary human stepping into this place, you must prepare not merely to observe it, but to isolate your biology completely from its systems while maintaining physical purchase on a world that is actively trying to rinse you away.

Here is how you must prepare.

---

### 1. Acoustic and Infrasonic Armor (The First Priority)
Most travelers assume heat or toxic air is the primary threat. In the Sump-Grid, **sound will kill you first.** The continental roar is a physical wave—infrasonic and acoustic energy dense enough to rupture eardrums, shear internal capillaries, vibrate your teeth until the roots bleed, and induce cardiac arrhythmia via chest-cavity resonance.

*   **Visceral Dampening Binder:** You must wear a high-density, gel-lined thoracic compression vest. This binds the ribcage and abdominal organs, preventing the heavy, subterranean 4-to-12 Hz vibrations from shaking your liver, lungs, and heart against your skeletal frame.
*   **Acoustic Isolation Helmet:** Do not rely on earplugs or active-canceling headphones; sound at this magnitude conducts through the jawbone and skull. Your helmet must feature an isolated inner cranial suspension, bone-conduction dampening pads over the mastoids, and an active phase-inversion suite calibrated to wide-band industrial frequencies. 
*   **Comms Sub-Vocalizer:** Standard voice communication is impossible. You will communicate through a throat-mic sensor and an in-ear cranial transducer.

### 2. Environmental Suit: The "Wet-Sump" Sealed Exosuit
There is no dry surface, no ambient air worth breathing, and no safe water to touch. The suit must be a **Class-IV Hazardous Industry Bio-Sealed Rigid Frame**.

*   **Closed-Circuit Atmospheric Rebreather (CCAR):** You cannot filter the air here—it is hyper-saturated with vaporized copper, ozone, synthetic aerosolized grease, and caustic brine that will degrade standard particulate filters in minutes. You need a 100% closed, internal oxygen supply with at least twice your planned duration in scrubber capacity.
*   **Thermal Regulation Loop:** Ambient air is thick with 60°C–90°C (140°F–194°F) pressurized steam. Without active cooling, your body will heat-stroke within twenty minutes. The undersuit must use a chilled liquid-perfusion counter-current network to continuously pull heat away from your skin and vent it via high-pressure phase-change exhaust ports.
*   **Radiation-Shielded Outer Shell:** The milky sky delivers a continuous dose of ionizing, ambient hard-UV and scatter-gamma radiation. The outer fabric should be a layered bismuth-impregnated polymer with a top-layer of non-reactive ceramic plating.
*   **Oleophobic and Anti-Enzymatic Surface Coating:** The sulfur-colored slime molds and synthetic bacterial biofilms are aggressive colonizers of warm surfaces. Your suit must be continuously misted or pre-treated with a low-surface-energy, fluoropolymer coating so the bioscum cannot adhere to your joints, seals, or visor.

### 3. Locomotion, Grip, and Kinetic Anchoring
The ground is a petrified computational lattice worn glass-smooth by centuries of scalding runoff and lubricated by synthetic grease and slime. Normal boots will cause you to slip immediately into a drainage flume.

*   **Dynamic Micro-Adhesion Soles:** Footwear must combine three systems:
    1.  *Pneumatic vacuum micro-cups* for flat ceramic planes.
    2.  *High-frequency tungsten micro-spikes* that can bite into weathered metal conduits.
    3.  *Switchable electromagnetic clamping solenoids* to lock your feet onto ferrous alloy pipes when crossing torrential runoff channels.
*   **Hydraulic Seismograph & Inertial Anchor:** When a city-block-sized floor section drops or elevates, the deceleration shock will break human ankles. Your boots must incorporate hydraulic shock-absorption struts. Furthermore, you must carry a **pneumatic coil-spike tether**; if the floor begins an unannounced hydraulic shift, you immediately fire the spike into the nearest structural conduit to anchor yourself from being thrown into a lower-tier boiling vortex.
*   **Reinforced External Rig (Power-Assisted):** The physical exhaustion of fighting heavy, cascading sheets of warm water will drain your stamina rapidly. An unpowered frame will leave you stranded. A low-profile motorized exoskeleton will support the weight of your life-support pack and stabilize your knees against the sheer fluid drag.

### 4. Visual Navigation in Shadowless Glare
Human spatial awareness relies on shadows, contrast, and clean horizons. The Sump-Grid offers none of these: only white, steam-diffused glare reflecting off moving water and gleaming metal.

*   **Polarized Gradient HUD with Artificial Depth-Mapping (LIDAR/Sonar):** The milk-white sky produces a flat, disorienting brilliance that erases edges. Your visor must use active polarized dimming to cut glare and superimpose **artificial visual contrast**. The HUD should scan the non-Euclidean tangles of piping and paint synthetic, colored edge-lines on bulkheads, drops, slippery slime-crusts, and approaching surges.
*   **Thermal-Fluid Highlighting:** You must be able to visually differentiate between 35°C runoff (walkable in an emergency) and 100°C scalding purge-streams (which will boil your suit’s seals). The HUD should overlay real-time color-coded thermal gradients onto all moving liquids.
*   **Non-Euclidean Spatial Plotter:** Because the infrastructure loops unpredictably over and under itself in thousands of layers, magnetic compasses and line-of-sight navigation are useless. You need an inertial dead-reckoning quantum navigator that tracks your precise entry node relative to physical mass displacement.

### 5. Ecosystem & Fauna Protocols
The local fauna are not predators in the traditional sense—they do not hunt meat—but their sheer mass, movement, and waste products make them fatal obstacles.

*   **The Polymer Trench-Leviathans:**
    *   *Rule:* Never walk along the bottom of a flooded drainage trench.
    *   *Hazard:* These blind giants filter lithium from the currents and roll violently along bulkheads. If a floorplate shifts, they are swept with the torrent. Getting between an armored, calcified leviathan and a steel conduit will crush you instantly. Always travel along the upper thirds of the mid-sized pipe-clusters.
*   **The Six-Limbed Chemiluminescent Skitterers:**
    *   *Rule:* Keep them off your seals.
    *   *Hazard:* They feed on synthetic bacterial scum along hot pipe seams. Your warm suit will attract them. While harmless individually, in swarms of thousands their hard claws can abrade suit gaskets and sensor lenses. Carry a low-intensity, high-frequency **electrostatic wand**; a small discharge across your suit hull will cause their soft underbellies to spasm and force them to detach.
*   **The Hanging Lung-Colonies (Overhead Gantries):**
    *   *Rule:* Wear a hardened composite helmet canopy.
    *   *Hazard:* These suspended filter-organisms continuously excrete dry, calcified mineral pellets. Falling from fifty meters through pressurized steam, these pellets hit with the force of heavy hail or buckshot.

### 6. Psychological Preparation
You are stepping into a realm of pure, unceasing artificial kineticism. There is no quiet, no rest, no earth, and no stillness.

*   **Sensory Deprivation Pre-Conditioning:** Train your brain to operate amid extreme sensory overload. The combination of deafening machine noise, blinding glare, constant water impacts against your body, and the vibration in your bone marrow will induce panic, vertigo, and dissociation within minutes if you are not mentally prepared.
*   **Acceptance of the Machine’s Scale:** There is no "master switch," no control room, and no dialogue to be had. The Sump-Grid does not know you are there, nor did it know its creators. You must adopt the mindset of a mite crawling across an operating turbine: stay low, stay anchored, move with the cycles of the hydraulic purges, and never mistake a lull in the noise for safety. It is simply the machine drawing breath for the next cycle.
