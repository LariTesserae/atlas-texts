### Field Briefing: The Nascent Vault (Site 300-Litho-Alpha)

You are preparing to step into an embryonic pocket of reality—a space no larger than the bed of a small wagon, yet chemically, geometrically, and ecologically violent. It is an unsealed womb of physical law where the Earth has not decided how distance, solidity, or light should behave. 

To survive a place where rock is still soft as curd and light is a physical property of the air, your preparations must address sensory collapse, spatial nausea, biological hazards, and the very real danger of being geologically incorporated into the floor.

---

### 1. Visual & Optical Accommodations
*The light in this chamber does not travel from a source; it exists inside the air. It casts no shadows, overexposes every surface, and eliminates natural depth perception.*

* **Active-Dimming, Full-Seal Goggles (Level 10–12 Weld Density):** Standard polarized sunglasses are entirely useless. Because the air itself is luminous, ambient scattering will enter from behind or around ordinary frames. You require full-seal orbital goggles with variable electronic tinting.
* **Edge-Enhancing / False-Shadow HUD (Augmented Reality):** In a shadowless world, your brain cannot calculate shape or distance. Wear an AR visor capable of projecting artificial wireframes and synthetic cast shadows onto your field of view. Without this, you will be unable to tell whether a selenite spine is an inch from your cornea or across the chamber.
* **Pupil-Constricting Drops (Pilocarpine):** Administer half an hour prior to entry. Even with filtered optics, the unremitting, shadowless white will cause severe photophobia and ocular migraines within minutes.

### 2. Physical Enclosure & Thermal Protection
*The ambient temperature is near-freezing ("refrigerator cold"), damp with geometric moisture, and saturated with sharp, fast-growing mineral structures.*

* **Low-Bulk, Cut-Proof Thermal Undersuit (Kevlar/Dyneema Blend):** The space is tiny. Bulky down jackets or expedition parkas will brush against the freshly split crystal walls, which have edges "crisp as snapped marrow." A single turn of your torso could slice heavy nylon open. Use thin, form-fitting, multi-layer microfleece under an abrasion- and slice-proof exterior.
* **Tear-Proof Outer Membrane:** Ensure the suit has zero loose straps, dangling loops, or external webbing. Fast-growing selenite needles can sprout through open mesh in minutes, physically pinning you to the rock.
* **Closed-Circuit Respiratory Mask:** The air smells of crushed flint, cold starch, and mineral clay. You must not inhale the airborne spore-analogues, mineral froth, or micro-filaments shed by the hovering insectoid fauna. A tight-fitting P100 silicone half-mask or full-face respirator is non-negotiable.

### 3. Footwear & Structural Anchorage
*The stone underfoot is not yet solid stone; it is cooling, curd-soft, and active.*

* **Pressure-Dispersal Boot Plates (Snowshoe/Plate Hybrid):** The mineral floor behaves like hardening tallow or cooling wax. Standard lugged hiking boots will sink into the marl, and the rapid mineral accretion may seize your soles before you decide to leave. Wear rigid, smooth-bottomed composite plates beneath your boots to distribute your body weight across a wider surface area.
* **Non-Adhesive Release Coatings:** Spray boots, kneeling pads, and gloves with a hydrophobic/oleophobic fluoropolymer layer to prevent the weeping, geometric condensation and curd-stone from bonding to your gear.
* **Weighted Center-Point Rig:** Because you cannot lean against walls that might briefly "hiccup" into open voids, you must remain strictly in the chamber’s center. Bring a low-profile, non-penetrating counterweight plate to set in the middle of the floor as a physical anchor for your stance.

### 4. Navigational & Vestibular Management
*Perspective in this pocket is broken. A wall yards away will press against your nose; surfaces flicker into hundreds of miles of silver-blue sky before snapping shut.*

* **Vestibular Suppressants (Anti-Vertigo Medication):** Take a clinical dose of meclizine or scopolamine. When your eyes register that an object is simultaneously far away and touching your face, your balance centers will trigger immediate, incapacitating nausea and vertigo.
* **The "Centerline Protocol":** Under no circumstances should you reach out to brace yourself against the walls. A surface that appears solid can undergo a structural frame-drop, revealing a drop into infinite sky. If your arm is extended through that space when the lithosphere snaps back into place, you will be cleanly bisected. 
* **Tactile Grounding Rod:** Carry a rigid, calibrated metal rod (12 inches long). When optical perspective fails you, use the rod against your own gear to remind your nervous system of your personal somatic perimeter.

### 5. Biological Protocols (Interacting with the Native Fauna)
*The ecosystem is frantic, razor-fast, and operating on non-standard mechanics.*

* **No Open Flesh:** The translucent, cellophane-like trilobites phase through mineral facets. While they target mineral froth, their hard, flat edges will slice dermis like wet tissue paper. The needle-thin flyers and five-jawed shrimp hunt at speeds resembling torn film frames; keep all gauntlets taped to your sleeves.
* **Avoid Rapid, Sweeping Gestures:** The five-jawed shrimp are apex ambush predators keyed to instantaneous movement. Move with slow, hydraulic deliberation. A sudden flail of your hand could trigger a feeding strike that will punch through light gloves.
* **Acoustic Discipline:** Sound here is minimal—microscopic shears, solitary water drops. Maintain total vocal silence. Communicate via throat-mic transceivers or hand signals within your field of vision.

### 6. Investigation Protocol: The Red Slate Ruin
*The only stable, manufactured structure is the three-step flight of dark red slate embedded in the cooling rock.*

* **Do Not Step on the Flight:** The bottom step is already being swallowed by the mineral floor. Adding your body weight may accelerate its submergence into the soft marl or break the fragile spatial anomaly holding it there.
* **Micro-Casting and Optical Probes:** 
  * The carved notch on the top tread is wheat-grain-sized. Do not attempt to clear it with metal picks (you will damage the mortar or the notch edges).
  * Bring a high-resolution, illuminated micro-endoscope with macro-focus capability.
  * Carry a syringe of non-reactive, fast-curing dental silicone to take a micro-impression of the "keyhole" without risking chemical adhesion to the slate.
* **Radiation and Temporal Sensors:** Before touching the slate, scan the notch for chronal drag or local gravitational variation. The fact that this artifact exists intact within embryonic rock suggests it was dropped from a finished universe—or placed here before this world began to harden.
