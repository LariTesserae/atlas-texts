### Field Assessment: The Hanging Marrow / The Desiccated Drift
**Threat Classification:** Class-IV Spatial/Atmospheric Extreme  
**Primary Lethal Hazards:** Rapid-phase tissue desiccation, localized non-Newtonian gravity shifts, tectonic architectural migration, sensory deprivation/spatial vertigo, particulate abrasion.

An ordinary human body is approximately 60% water and hardwired for single-vector, downward gravity in an illuminated, Euclidean world. Entering this location without specialized preparation will result in either immediate mummification, drifting endlessly across a sideways gravity slipstream, or suffering catastrophic spatial psychosis.

Below is the required preparation protocol for an expedition into the Hanging City.

---

### 1. The Enclosure: Environmental Isolation & Hydration Defense
The atmosphere here acts as a physical vacuum for moisture. Any unsealed pore will cause your bodily fluids to boil out and sublimate into the air in seconds, leaving your flesh like salted dry-cured leather.

*   **Positive-Pressure Hard Shell (The "Sarcophagus Suit"):**
    *   **No Standard Soft Suits:** Soft fabrics will be shredded by the airborne shale-grit and razor-thin fractal edges. You require an articulated, hard-shell environmental suit with vulcanized elastomer joints and zero external stitching.
    *   **Double-Redundant Hermetic Seals:** All entry points must feature secondary mechanical clamps. If a single seal breaks, you do not simply lose air—the atmosphere will literally drink your eyes, tongue, and blood.
    *   **Active Surface Polarity (Anti-Static Grid):** The ambient dust is an ultra-fine, calcified marrow and talc mixture. It will cling to visors and seize mechanical hinges within minutes. The suit’s exterior must run a perpetual, low-amperage electrostatic charge to repel mineral powder.
*   **Closed-Circuit Hydration & Thermal Regulation:**
    *   **Zero-Loss Recyclers:** Your suit must capture 100% of exhaled moisture, perspiration, and urine, purifying it instantly into an internal feeding straw. You cannot open the suit to drink. 
    *   **Internal Humidifiers:** Because the outside air is absolute zero-humidity, the internal atmosphere of the suit will dry out rapidly without an active misting system to protect your lungs and corneas.
    *   **Sub-Skin Thermal Mesh:** The cold here is hollow and motionless. You do not need windproofing; you need conductive heat distribution to keep your extremities from freezing solid against the conductive shale stone.

### 2. Locomotion & Physics Navigation: Surviving the Gravitational Fracture
Gravity here does not pull you toward a core; it is anchored to the geometry of the architecture itself. A curb is a gravitational fault line.

*   **Active Micro-Spur Magnetic/Kinetic Boots:**
    *   The shale is powdery and soft. Standard magnetic boots are useless on talc-veined rock. Your boots must feature **rapid-cycling pneumatic micro-spurs** (borrowed from the biology of the *strata-weavers*) that bite half an inch into the limestone with every step, anchoring your mass to the specific architectural plane you are walking on.
*   **Cold-Gas Reaction Control System (RCS):**
    *   If you slip over an edge or step onto an inverted causeway, you will not fall "down"—you will drift sideways along the district’s migration vector into the ivory void. You must wear an ultra-compact back-mounted RCS thruster pack with at least 800 m/s of delta-v to navigate back to a stable architectural face.
*   **Dynamic Cleavage-Plane Tethers:**
    *   Carry high-tensile, ultra-light grapnels tipped with expanding harmonic anchors. These can be shot into the powdery shale to secure you when walking along avenues that run over the abyss.
    *   **Crucial Rule:** Never anchor yourself to two separate buildings simultaneously. Whole districts shear and migrate without warning. A dual anchor will tear you in half when one block slides forty-five degrees away from the other.

### 3. Sensory Restoration & Wayfinding: Countering the Void
The slate-colored, shadowless gloom completely erases human depth perception. You cannot tell if a doorway is ten feet away or ten miles away; you cannot tell if a floor drops an inch or an infinity.

*   **LiDAR-Augmented Synthetic Vision (False-Depth Visor):**
    *   Your helmet’s viewport must be opaque to raw visible light, relying entirely on a high-refresh **LiDAR and stereoscopic sensor suite**. The HUD must project false shadows, depth-color banding (e.g., green for 2 meters, red for 50 meters), and edge-detection lines over the geometry. Without this, you will step into the ivory void believing it is a flat grey wall.
*   **Inertial Dead-Reckoning Navigators:**
    *   GPS, magnetic compasses, and star tracking do not exist here. Because the city moves, traditional mapping is fatal. You must deploy **acoustic spatial beacons** at every intersection you cross. These beacons ping one another, updating your internal map in real time as the city twists and docks around you.
*   **Seismic Acoustic Early-Warning Sensors:**
    *   Clip acoustic transducers to the stone of whatever district you are occupying. These listen for the characteristic high-frequency *tick* of a terrace shearing along its cleavage plane, giving you a 3-to-5-minute window to evacuate a migrating neighborhood before it disconnects and slides into the void.

### 4. Hazard Protocols: Flora, Fauna, and Abstract Stone
*   **The Mineral Blooms (Salt Lattices):**
    *   Avoid walking through or disturbing the branching crystal rosettes. When they fracture, they release an ultra-fine, bone-dry aerosol that instantly clogs suit filters and jams mechanical joints. Treat them as fields of brittle, microscopic glass.
*   **The Strata-Weavers:**
    *   These creatures are not active predators—they feed on falling mineral grit—but they are structurally dangerous. A landing weaver (with a wingspan of dry skin and bone) will attempt to anchor into your suit with its needle-fine bone spurs if it mistakes your silhouette for a vertical facade. 
    *   **Defense:** High-frequency sonic dispersers mounted on your shoulders. The fluidless, hollow bone structure of the weavers is brittle and highly sensitive to localized acoustic resonance; a focused blast will cause them to bank away and catch a lateral draft.
*   **The Fractal Margins:**
    *   If you reach areas where columns split into infinite iterations and stairs become micron-thick ramps, **turn back immediately**. The stone here is sharp enough on a microscopic level to slice through suit elastomers with zero friction, and the local gravity fields degrade into mathematical white noise, causing limbs to experience competing gravitational vectors simultaneously.

### 5. Mental Conditioning & The Psychology of the Open Tomb
The silence of this city is absolute, punctuated only by the ten-minute grinding groans of drifting continents of shale. The vastness has no reference points; the rooms are millions of identical, empty cells.

*   **Sensory Grounding Audio Feed:** 
    *   Human sanity rapidly unravels in complete, dead silence. Your suit comms should run a low-level, continuous audio track of terrestrial environmental noise—gentle rain, the rustle of leaves, a rhythmic baseline—to keep your brain anchored against the crushing scale of the dead sky.
*   **Non-Euclidean Vertigo Training:**
    *   Spend at least 40 hours in a zero-G, variable-perspective simulator. You must unlearn the instinct that "down" is where your feet are, and train your brain not to panic when the street you are standing on suddenly becomes a ceiling relative to the neighborhood drifting past your head.

### The Golden Rule of the Expedition
**Trust the stone beneath your cleats, but never trust that it will stay where you found it.** Take your samples of calcified shale, photograph the impossible lintels, and do not linger. When the sky deepens to charcoal and the distant groan of realigning stone begins, ensure your boots are anchored, your RCS is armed, and your eyes never look straight down into the ivory.
