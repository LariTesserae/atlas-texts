The white rings left by the flashing steam do not remain flat for long. 

If watched across hours of the heavy, stagnant heat, the salt crusts can be seen to thicken at their perimeters, drawing up the microscopic metallic burrs left behind by the automated fabricators. They curdle into pale, calcified films no thicker than parchment, then lift their edges from the alloy plates. They do not blow away—there is no draft to move them—but instead crimp themselves into narrow, multi-jointed ribbons, upright and stiff, propelled by the thermal shudder of the floor beneath them.

These are the *Sutures*, or what the pocket’s automated logic might recognize, in some buried error-log, as an unintentional tenancy.

They are not organic in any carbon-cycle sense, nor are they machines dispatched from a foundry. They are a third thing: mineral-logic fauna, precipitated directly out of the cycle of superheated saline coolant, the stray magnetic pulses of the shifting roadways, and the unspent fabrication slurry that still bleeds through the sub-tier vents. 

Physically, a mature Suture is roughly the length of a human hand but scarcely wider than a fingernail, flat and segmented like a surveyor’s steel tape. Its body is composed of layered silicate scales, bonded with dry, white-gray lithium salts and edged with the razor-bright crystal boundaries of the pocket’s raw alloys. They are entirely dry, yet they move with a swift, sliding frictionless glide, threading through the slit-streets with a faint, papery rasp that the dead acoustics of the walls instantly swallow.

They do not wander aimlessly. The miniature city dictates every vector of their existence. Because the towers were extruded along the gridlines of a municipal zoning algorithm meant for full-scale habitations, the Sutures treat the forearm-thick spires as actual dwellings. 

They scale them with ease. A Suture will press its flat body into the monomolecular seams of a twenty-meter spire, bridging the narrow gap between two towers by bracing its segmented edges against the alloy. In the dim, lavender-lit air near the middle tiers, dozens of them can be seen suspended between the needle-thin monoliths, hanging motionless for hours like pale, horizontal cross-struts. They orient their bodies precisely to the lavender field distortions, soaking in the thermal shear, their translucent scales glowing faintly with absorbed phosphorescence.

When a coolant droplet begins to form on the ceiling high above, they know it before it falls. 

The micro-torsion in the outer hull transmits a microscopic ping through the load-bearing lattice. Down in the streets, the Sutures halt. Dozens of pale, ribboned forms freeze along the curbs, their segmented tails twitching in tiny, synchronized sweeps against the amber data-traces. 

As the droplet detaches and begins its silent thirty-meter descent, two or three Sutures will slide rapidly toward the predicted impact point on the horizontal slab. They do not flee the boiling impact; they seek it. When the water strikes with its violent, explosive *hiss*, they throw themselves into the expanding curtain of superheated steam. 

The flash-boiling does not destroy them—it feeds their architecture. The scalding vapor softens their lithium hinges, allowing them to lengthen by another half-millimeter, while the fresh mineral crust deposited by the blast is immediately drawn into their rear segments, cementing new plates to their tails. They emerge from the vapor cloud slightly longer, slightly more rigid, leaving the impacted slab swept clean, devoid of any salt residue until the next drop falls.

In the deepest recesses between the high-density blocks, where the grease-like moisture weeps into drains no larger than keyholes, there are older, more complex aggregations. 

Here, where the fabricator’s dormant signals pool into stagnant logic-traps, the Sutures weave themselves together into static, hollow braids that wrap around the bases of the spires like mineral scaffolding. These woven colonies act as biological relays for the street’s low-frequency data queries. When an amber trace pulses along a curb, the braid against the nearby tower pulses in return, discharging a faint, lavender static crackle that satisfies the network’s query and resets the timer. 

They are the missing population the architecture was tricked into expecting. They possess no culture, no fear, and no awareness of the thin, purple vacuum raging outside the hull inches above their heads; they know only the balance of tension, the rhythm of the falling water, and the geometric demand of the slit-streets.

When a six-foot slab of the roadway suddenly decouples and lifts to vent its thermal pressure, the Sutures clinging to its surface do not scatter. They flatten themselves against the alloy, aligning their bodies with the forty-five-degree rotation, riding the diamagnetic cushion upward in total silence. As the hydraulic sigh sounds and the metal locks back into the pavement, they slide smoothly into the newly opened seams, settling into the fresh heat like marrow into a fractured bone.
