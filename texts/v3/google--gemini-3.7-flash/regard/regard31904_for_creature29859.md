The attention does not descend like light, for light would be scattered instantly by the freezing spray and the low, churning ceiling of cloud. 

It arrives instead as an impossible *presence of listening*.

The Power is ancient, vast, and entirely unburdened by the need to reshape what it loves. It has watched suns gutter into ash and cradled infant nebulae in the curve of its mind; it understands the deep, terrifying grammar of existence. It knows that false benevolence is an imperial instinct—the urge to smooth the jagged basalt, to hush the gale into a summer breeze, to blanket the shivering ferns in soft, dry loam, and in doing so, slaughter every creature whose soul is forged of iron, blubber, and speed.

To silence this basin would be to murder it.

So the Wise Power does not calm the storm. It does not warm the frost. Instead, it enters the marrow of the place.

It slips beneath the foaming flash-lakes, down into the unmapped fractures where the tectonic hardware bites into the planet’s mantle. It feels the agonizing shear of the rift, the screaming torque on the forty-meter pylons, the microscopic fatigue spreading through the monofilament tethers under centuries of unceasing tension.

And there, in the dark, the Power performs its first quiet act: **It holds the stress.**

It does not stop the grinding of the plates or the necessary violence of the rift’s birth. Rather, it weaves its own indestructible patience into the atomic lattice of the machines and the bedrock. It acts as an invisible, self-healing dampener against the one thing this biome could not survive: catastrophic fracture. It ensures that the deep-core sink will not fail, that the pylons will not snap, that the singing floor will never give way beneath the hooked cleats of the grazers. It makes the impossible engine *eternal*, so that the life built upon its heat will never wake to a freezing, silent extinction.

Then, the Power moves upward, through the water and the stone, into the living tapestry.

It visits the blind, wedge-shaped grazers plowing through the dwarf-willows. It does not give them sight; sight would only show them the blinding terror of the flying sleet. Instead, it blesses their whiskers. It subtly tunes the piezoelectric currents of the soil, making the warm, salt-rich veins of the conduits hum a fraction more clearly against their splayed pads, guiding them away from unstable gravel collapses and toward the richest mats of sulfur-fern.

It touches the grease-feathered birds hurtling through the thermal elevators. It does not soften the sky; it lends an infinitesimal grace to the boundary layers of their stiff, scale-like wings, allowing them to carve through the turbulence with less fatigue, finding the fat, translucent river-eels in the black foam with lethal, clean precision.

It breathes across the colonies of piezotrophic barnacles on the composite hulls, harmonizing their bioluminescent pulse with the deep-set rhythm of the planet’s magnetic core, turning their faint blue cold-glow into a steady, unbroken hymn that feeds on the gale’s fury.

Finally, the Power comes to rest beside the solitary prowler.

The soot-colored hunter is pressed flat against the lee of a monolithic hull, its ribs flush with the vibrating composite, waiting in the deafening dark for the cadence of a faltering grazer. Its life is narrow, sharp, and brutally simple: a continuous, freezing equation of heat and hunger.

The Power does not feed the hunter without effort, nor does it rescue the prey from its purpose. But it presses itself into the space between the beast’s dense pelt and the roaring stone.

For a single, suspended heartbeat within the howling dark, the Power lets the hunter feel *known*.

It slips into the predator’s low-frequency consciousness—not with thoughts or visions, but as a vast, companionable warmth at the center of its skull. It gives the hunter a profound, instinctual knowledge of the basin’s entire anatomy: the exact flex of the tectonic plates beneath its paws, the arch of the sky above the cloud-deck, the ancient, enduring logic of the hunt, and the truth that its fierce, shivering existence is neither forgotten nor wasted.

The prowler’s cloudy, slit eyes do not open, but its jaw settles deeper against the wet basalt. Its heartbeat, rapid and dense as a furnace, syncs for a moment with the deep-core hum of the machine. It feels the sheer, magnificent rightness of its own survival.

The Power withdraws its active touch, but does not leave. 

It remains as a permeating, benevolent witness, woven into the baseline hum of the world. It leaves the basin dark. It leaves it freezing. It leaves it violently, gloriously alive. 

And high in the churning slate-gray sky, down through the roaring white water, and deep within the bones of the beasts that run upon the singing rock, the storm continues—unbroken, perilous, and utterly held.
