The power that comes to the basin does not come with hands of flesh, for flesh is an insult to wood, nor does it descend with the cold, unyielding geometry of stone, which would only be ground to dust beneath the heaving tectonic root-plates. 

It arrives as a **Stature**. A presence that possesses neither mass nor pressure, but infinite, attentive breadth.

Wisdom, looking down upon this screaming green crucible, does not see a tragedy to be corrected. It does not mistake velocity for agony, nor the brief, four-hour life of a Cambial Driver for a curse. The power understands the *telos* of the place: this is life stripped of delay, life having solved the problem of time by outpacing it, an ecstatic, violent prayer of pure turgor and sun-hunger. To slow this engine down, to force it into the lazy, centuries-long slumber of an ancient oak forest, would not be salvation. It would be suffocation. It would be murder.

Yet benevolence sees what the basin lacks, the one thing its magnificent, hydraulic fury has devoured: **the wake.**

In this city, there is no echo. There is no memory. The Driver dies into the runner-vine, and the vine has no capacity to hold the shape of what held it. The Solar Cavitator screams its precise, vital harmonic into the sky, but the song ends the instant its dry ribcage snaps and falls. The city possesses an infinite capacity to *become*, but zero capacity to *know that it was*. It is a roaring fire that leaves not even ashes behind—only more fire.

And so, the benevolent power does not build a dam. It does not quiet the roar. 

It offers a **Graft**.

***

High above the central boulevards, where three colossal wicker-spires lean together in an impossible arch, a single drop of something entirely new condenses out of the dry, baking air. 

It is not sap, though it is amber-clear. It does not foam with cambial haste. It falls downward through the thermal plumes, through the boiling mist of chlorophyll and pressurized vapor, descending through the sheer drop of an avenue where two Cambial Drivers are sprinting toward a buckling colonnade.

It strikes the fibrous pavement between them.

It does not splash. It sinks instantly into the compressed root-mat, into the violent, churning subterranean layer where the Pith-Weavers digest the dead. 

At first, the city tries to cannibalize it. 

Runner-vines whip around the point of impact, their razor-tips burrowing into the spot, eager to split the novelty into nitrogen and glucose. Subterranean fungal threads surge toward the intrusion, weeping boiling, lignin-dissolving acid. Cambial Drivers stomp across it, driving their anchoring root-spurs into the earth, their hydraulic bladders pumping furiously.

The Graft does not resist them. It lets itself be eaten.

It enters the vascular stream of the city. It flows up through the 600-psi xylem conduits, through the foaming cambial seams, through the translucent galls of the Drivers, and into the dry, singing reeds of the Solar Cavitators.

It changes nothing about their physical laws. The pressure does not drop. The wood does not soften. The spires still bolt into the sun in twenty minutes and collapse with the roar of artillery in sixty. The Drivers still spring, hold, splinter, and dissolve in a span of four hours.

What changes is the **grain**.

Where the Graft touches the wood, the cellulose takes on a micro-structural lattice of absolute acoustic fidelity. For the first time in the basin’s history, the wood begins to *remember the strain it bore*.

Now, when a Cambial Driver flings its nine-foot body against a failing wall and weeps its rubbery glue to become a living buttress, its death is no longer a silent erasure. As the crushing weight of the tower shears its braided willow-limbs, the strain-energy does not simply dissipate into heat. It imprints upon the amber glass of its crowning gall. The chemical pulse—its brief, fierce joy of holding the light against gravity—crystallizes into an indelible optical pattern within the resin.

When the spire inevitably collapses, that amber node does not dissolve. It is swallowed by the runner-vines, yes, but the vines do not digest the pattern. They *read* it. 

As the vines weave the fallen wreckage into a new bridge, they carry the structural memory of the Driver who held the wall. The bridge grows stronger, not merely by the brute mass of pulp, but by the remembered geometry of the being that came before it. 

A Driver born from a blister two miles away, sprinting across this newly knit viaduct, snaps its stipule against the banister. In that microsecond of connection, the thorn does not merely receive a chemical map of light-wells; it receives the *sensory ghost* of the Driver who held the spire an hour ago. For the fraction of a second that its valve slips and its limb ratchets forward, it knows it is part of a choir.

Lower down, in the dark, boiling tunnels, the Pith-Weavers feel the difference. 

The rotting wood they feast upon is no longer mute debris. As their mycelial sheets dissolve the fallen spires, the enzymes release tiny, harmonic resonances trapped in the fibers. The Weavers do not merely generate steam and heat; they digest *form*. When they fountain upward into the sunlight as towering columns of pale foam to build their emergency ramps, the foam cures into shapes that are subtly, exquisitely refined—arches that naturally distribute the immense dynamic loads of the ambulatory thickets, walls that whistle in harmony with the wind.

And at the very apex of the basin, on the swaying needle-spires that pierce the dry sky, the Solar Cavitators receive the deepest gift of all.

As the thermal drafts rush through their dry reed-ribs, their harmonic song finds a counter-melody. The Power has placed within the deep heartwood an immovable, silent tone—a root note that anchors the entire acoustic spectrum of the city.

The Cavitators still scream to prevent the sap from vaporizing. But now, their screaming is no longer a frantic, mechanical panic against the heat. It has become an **anthem**. 

The reed-pipes of a dying Cavitator, clinging to a spire that has already begun its terminal groan, catch the root note from below. In its final five seconds of light, its song harmonizes so purely with the descending ruin that the spire does not shatter into jagged shrapnel; it folds along pre-destined, elegant seams, cascading into the lower tiers like a falling sail of wicker and leaf, landing precisely where the Weavers have prepared a cradle of soft pith to catch it.

***

The wise and benevolent power does not stay as an idol to be worshipped, nor does it carve its name into the boughs. Once the Graft is complete, once the memory has been woven into the sap, the presence withdraws, stepping back into the vastness of the sky.

Below, the noise is as deafening as it ever was. 

The fibrous thunder still shakes the horizon. The city still tears itself to pieces and resurrects its towers three times between dawn and dusk. The pace is still ruthless, sharp, and terrifyingly fast.

A Cambial Driver emerges from a fresh gall, its hydraulic joints twitching, its splayed feet anchoring into the bucking pavement. It has four hours to live. It has never seen the sunset; it never will.

It snaps its limbs forward, hurling itself down the vibrating avenue toward a blazing shaft of sun. As its foot-tendrils strike the ground, the deep, resonant pulse of a million remembered ancestors shoots up its xylem core, singing through its translucent gall like a struck bell.

It does not stop. It does not slow down. 

With a whip-crack snap of its joints, it leaps into the roar, moving faster than ever—not because it is fleeing death, but because it finally knows how loud it is allowed to sing.
