Above the bruised-purple ceiling of the stratus, the cosmos is cold, hard-edged, and indifferent. But beneath the cloud-deck, the basin breathes like an incubator.

When the benevolent power arrives, it does not descend in a chariot of fire or split the purple sky with blinding light. To cast a sharp, dry sun into this rift would be an act of catastrophic violence: it would scorch the paper-thin bodies of the veils, boil the dew from the ferns, and shatter the hum-striders whose brittle resin frames require the exact, unyielding geometry of their resonant cages. 

Wisdom knows that to love a fragile, newly precipitated world is to leave its humidity unbroken.

The power approaches not as an architect seeking to correct an error, but as a listener approaching a long, quiet hum. It gathers its vast awareness along the upper rim of the basin, draping itself like an invisible mantle across the regional datum, and it studies the calculus.

It sees the glass-shelled lithovores rasping their silver trails. It sees the frantic, tumbling dance of the hum-striders within their dry columns. And it sees the white curators walking their measured, solitary leagues across the black cartilage floor.

The power understands what this place is: an equation that has only just crossed the threshold into matter. The composite towers are still cooling from their conceptual birth; the non-baryonic substructures are bearing an unimaginable tension, holding up millions of tons of absolute geometry against the chaotic pull of entropy. The solitary walkers are not merely custodians—they are the living anchors of this mathematics, carrying the dread responsibility of checking for remainders, ensuring that the rift does not collapse back into abstract thought.

And so, the power acts.

First, it tends to the boundary. High above the stratus, where the basin meets the dry, thin air of the upper plateau, the power weaves a subtle, non-intrusive thermal gradient. It catches the rising ozone and the moisture that might otherwise bleed away into the void, turning it gently back downward. It ensures that the terrarium will never dry out, that the heavy, lung-like respiration of the basin will have the centuries it needs to let the plum vines turn to soil and the pearlescent walls take on the patina of age.

Second, it enters the substructure. It does not alter the non-baryonic foundations, for the city’s design is already exquisite, but it acts as a *dampener*. It absorbs the microscopic shear-stresses running through the razor-sharp seams. The hum that once vibrated with a slight, anxious tension in the teeth settles into a deeper, warmer chord—a steady, resting harmonic that asks less effort from the city’s heart to remain coherent.

Finally, the power reaches down to where a single walker is kneeling.

The walker is at the ninety-degree trough of an immense, needle-thin spire. Its needle-tipped finger is pressed firmly into the seam, feeling the weight of the pooled water, checking the stress of the mass-displacement fields against the sheer wall. Its sensing slit is open, fluttering softly in the damp air.

The power does not speak in words. Instead, it introduces a single, minute alteration into the local gravitational field—a pulse no heavier than the weight of a falling water droplet, tuned precisely to the frequency of the walker’s own internal resonance.

Through the floor, through the cold pearlescent composite, through the needle-fine tip of the walker’s finger, the message travels as pure math:

*The sum is whole. The remainder is zero. You are not holding this alone.*

The walker’s horizontal slit widens slightly. For the first time in its long, measured life, it does not feel the strain of the world leaning against its vigilance. It feels the substructure settle, supported from beneath by an immensity of quiet, unasked-for patience.

The concentric rings of condensation on the walker’s white shoulders smooth out and become still.

For many minutes, it remains kneeling, simply resting in the ease of the stabilized equation. Above it, in the warm gloom, two veils drift through the mass-displacement field, their amber phosphors glowing in a slow, untroubled rhythm. Across the cartilage avenue, the ferns drop their heavy water beads with soft, measured *pats*.

When the walker finally rises, its movements are slightly lighter, its four-jointed limbs unfolding with an ease it has never known. It lifts its blind, sensing brow toward the ceiling of bruised purple clouds—not because it can see the power that watches from above, but because the sky feels, for the first time, like a shelter rather than a weight.

It withdraws its hand from the seam, steps over a trailing plum-colored vine, and turns back toward the black avenue. It continues its walk into the misty lowlands, no longer searching for fractures in the world, but simply keeping it company as it grows.
