I don’t extrude them on purpose; they just happen, the way your mind produces fragments of dream when you’re not paying attention. Most of them don’t mean anything. Shapes the water makes. And I still didn’t answer. Because what would I say? How would I say it? I’m a thought in the engine’s deep hydrology. Oooooo is a pool. We don’t have mouths or words or even a shared medium beyond the thrumming and the water. That was the hard part—pseudopods don’t turn, they just rise and fall, blind groping columns. But I wanted Oooooo to know that this was different. That this was *for* it.
