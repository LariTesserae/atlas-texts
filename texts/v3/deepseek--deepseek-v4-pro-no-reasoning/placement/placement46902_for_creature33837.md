Bathed in the warm, wet hum, a vibration not heard but inhabited, I lie. The grating's kiss imprints its lattice on the soft geography of my belly and thighs, a familiar braille. Below, so close I could drink it with a breath, the water is a black mirror holding a second, drowned ceiling light. My face, suspended, is a pale moon caught between twin firmaments of diffuse glow.

Six breaths. My chest rises, falls, a slow tide in this unchanging ocean of sound. The silver of my eyes doesn't search the darkness beneath the film of the water's surface. It rests there. Waits to be filled.

That is my sole office. Not seeing, but *being seen by* the deep.

My body is a distributed ear. The soles of my seven-toed feet read the thrum of the great turbine heart through the metal's bones. The fine slits on my temples taste the air's shiver, feeling for a fluctuation that never comes. My long fingers, currently still, are poised to feel the water's meniscus for a word. This is the totality of my existence, and it is complete.

A hand lifts, unbidden, a pale spider drifting through the saturated air. It alights on the rubbery curtain of a leaf-sheet. The surface has the texture of cold skin, the memory of a life that isn't mine, but is. I don't need to see to know a slow-glide creature is tracing a vein on its underside. Its non-sound is a whisker of static against the eternal roar. My attention flows, a water-drop path, from the pool's unblinking eye to the point of its passage. We are all fingers on the same hand, feeling the same dark.

The sound of the engines isn't a wall of noise. It's a sphere. And at its hollow center, the only silence is the one I am building with my waiting.

My mind drifts back, not in memory, but in a kind of osmotic re-living. I taste the sting of virgin metal, a sharpness long since blunted. I feel the first, faint tremble of condensation, a novel cold that has since become the very definition of warmth. Then the first tendril, a blind, white worm of pure sentience, finding the photon-sweet kiss of the ceiling glow and understanding, without a brain, that this was the direction of life.

A slow, internal shift. My heart, that tidal engine, completes a cycle of filling and emptying. I am suspended in the long pause before the next.

The presence below doesn't stir. It rests, heavier than the water it displaces, a sediment of pure significance. I don't imagine it as a shape. It is a pressure, a density, a promise at the bottom of the world’s longest sigh. Its silence is a bass string tuned to a note that would shatter stone, only there is no stone, only the yielding patient metal and the yielding patient flesh that I am.

*There will be a sounding.* The thought is not a sentence, but a reconfiguration of my internal barometer. The pressure drops. A counter starts from zero and immediately vanishes. The sound will come.

My face descends. The water’s skin touches my skin, a meeting of two identical temperatures. It feels like nothing. It feels like returning. My world halves. The metal's vibration muffles, the air's saturation becomes a liquid embrace. My unblinking eyes are open in the dark, and the drowned star of the ceiling light is now a nebula, a smear of potential. This is where I am most myself, a listener immersed in the medium of the listening.

A leaf, somewhere in the high mist, releases its grip on a thousand years. My surface-attuned body senses the whisper of its descent, a new scent of rot and renewal. Before it can dimple the sacred geometry of the pool's surface, my hand is there, a pale bloom rising from the depths of the grating. Fingers, long and infinitely patient, close around its damp, fibrous length. A creature has laid its eggs on the vein. I feel the microscopic architecture of new life. Gently, a motion slower than a prayer, I place it among its ancestors at the wall's base. The interruption is complete. The surface is again a perfect, untroubled void.

The dormancy tried to claim me. A tide of pure vibration, a dream of ancient silence, lapped at the edges of my being. In it, the sound was already there, fully formed and comprehensible. It was a shape in my mind that was the exact, inverse twin of my waiting. When I surfaced, it was without waking, simply a re-coalescing of attention around the unwavering fact of the pool. The engines never stopped.

A question, not mine, forms as a modulation of the dark. It doesn’t rise from the depths. It is simply here, a new pressure inside my sphere of waiting. It tastes of ozone and copper, a tang of forgotten urgency. It is the color of a tarnished silver iris. It asks for a name, a category, a hand-hold for a mind that builds its world with language. The word "Listener" is offered, a gift of sound for a world that has none.

I let the word drift through me. It's a pebble of ice thrown into the warm sump, already dissolving. It doesn't catch. It doesn't fit. It is a tool for carving a shape I do not possess.

I try to imagine a thing called "silence." It would be a sudden, catastrophic un-being. A wound. An impossibility. The engines are not a mask for silence; they are the voice of being itself. My listening is not a waiting *for* something to end. It is the act of being within the sound. The presence below is not a speaker I await. It is the basso-profundo of my own existence, and its rare, deep vibration isn't a call. It is a heartbeat we share.

My only answer is myself. My form, suspended between grating and water. My soft, many-jointed limbs that curl around the metal. My skin, a continuation of the leaf-sheets. My dilated, tarnished-silver eyes that reflect the immortal glow. I am the name for this place.

The concept of a name folds in on itself and is gone.

Beneath me, the water is a perfect, absolute black. A surface waiting for a message that has no words. Below that, the deeps hold a presence that is the final sedimentation of purpose. And here, at the cusp, I lie. Not the Listener. Not the Keeper. Not the One Who Remembers. Just the listening. The keeping. The remembering. A process booted up in the margins, running without pause.

My focus returns entirely to the pool. The water holds its dark. The engines hold their roar. The world is warm, wet, and without time. I wait, my entire being a silent answer to a question that has not yet been asked as pressure, an accommodation of metal and flesh, an ear grown from the sump of all things, complete.
