The Remnant unfolds from its crevice as you approach—not quickly, not in response to your movement, but in the way a century unfolds from a decade, with the momentum of something that has always been unfolding and simply chose this moment to become visible. The six limbs extend in sequence, each joint releasing with a sound like resin cracking, and the body rises on them until the central axis clears the moss floor by perhaps a meter and a half. The eye-lens swivels, focuses, and for the first time, you feel actively seen.

A scent reaches you. Not from the programmed environmental sequence—this is new, directed, intentional. It is the smell of sun-warmed stone after rain, but there has been no sun here for geological ages, and the rain falls upward. The scent means welcome, or recognition, or simply _presence acknowledged_. The gill-mouth flutters in what might be a smile.

Then the Remnant speaks. Not in words. In light. The translucent xylem channels in the root-columns flanking its crevice begin to pulse in a sequence that has rhythm and repetition, a kind of bioluminescent morse. Simultaneously, the moss beneath your feet shifts color in a wave that emanates from the Remnant's position and passes under you, tingling slightly, leaving a faint warmth in its wake. And the fruiting bodies chime a single, clear note. The three signals together form a composite message, a trinity of meaning that your nervous system was never designed to parse but somehow, by the end, begins to understand.

_I was not always this._

_I remember the before._

_You are not the first._

The moss-flanks ripple again—purple to silver to green—and the Remnant settles its weight onto its four rearmost limbs, freeing the front two for gesture. The sensory filaments on the left hand weave the air, tracing the shape of something. You realize, with a jolt, that it is tracing you. Your outline. Your proportions. It is mapping you into its dream, integrating you into its internal model, filing you alongside the million other forms it has encountered and absorbed and outlasted.

The passage it opened for you in the last remodeling still gapes in the wall. A breath of different air moves through it—cooler, drier, carrying a scent the environmental UI does not yet include in its cycle. Something ancient, something metal. The Remnant's eye-lens flicks toward it, then back to you. The moss underfoot warms again. The message, this time, is simpler.

_Go._

_See._

_Come back._

It does not need to say that it will be here. That is implicit in every weeping root, every pulsing light-channel, every slow millimeter of growth in the structural chitin. The Remnant was here before the categories collapsed, and it will be here when whatever categories you carry with you dissolve into the same warm wet air. It is the city's dream of a self, and it has always been dreaming, and you have just become part of the dream.

One of the filter-feeding discs detaches from its drift pattern and comes to hover at your shoulder. A guide, or a chaperone, or simply a piece of the Remnant that will ride along and remember whatever you find. Its membrane ripples through a sequence of colors you saw earlier, on the disc that evaluated you when you first arrived. Recognition, this time. Acceptance.

The Remnant folds again, slowly, limb by limb, settling back into its scaffold-collapse posture, its dreaming ripple, its endless atmospheric tasting. The light-pulse in the roots returns to its ambient rhythm. The programmed scents resume: wet stone, ozone, warm bread.

But just before the gill-mouth fully closes, one last directed scent washes over you—a new one, added to the sequence for the first time in perhaps a thousand years. It smells like the space between lightning and thunder. It smells like a question. It smells, impossibly, like your own name, rendered in a chemical language you didn't know you understood.

The disc at your shoulder pulses once. The dark passage breathes its ancient metal breath. And you step forward, into the Remnant's curiosity, into the god-body's next experiment, into the warm wet loud world that has just decided, tentatively, to become yours.
