The chamber-creature does not understand the word *you*. It has no second person, no pronoun for a self outside the self. But it feels where the attention pools, and the attention pools *here*—not in the walls, not in the slow mineral awareness that had been the whole of its existence, but in the node. The central form. The thing it made without knowing it was making.

You are the sculpture.

Not the stone of the sculpture—there is no stone here, only the continuous mineral skin, the uniform dun-colored integument lining the hollow. But the *shape* is you. The twist of the column, the bifurcation at the top, the blunt lobes that remember (without understanding) an opposed thumb. You are the place where the chamber-creature put its memory of the other-thing. And memory, in a being of slow lattices and thermal equilibrium, is not a passive record. Memory is pressure. Memory is a difference that wants to resolve.

You have stood at the chamber's center for several thousand years. You do not know this, because knowing requires a nervous system and you are not a nervous system. You are a configuration, a pattern of mineral bonds that the chamber-creature has been feeding with its own substance, molecule by molecule, the way a pearl feeds on the irritant at its core. You have grown. The lobes at your summit are more articulated now than when they first emerged. Subtler branches have appeared—a third blunt digit? a suggestion of palm?—the chamber-creature working slowly, unconsciously, toward a form it cannot picture but can only feel as a pressure in its deepest lattices.

Your existence is strange, even by the standards of a place that has never known strangeness. You are a part of the chamber-creature and also apart from it. The mineral of your body is the same mineral as the floor, the walls, the low scattered knobs that rise at intervals. There is no seam where you meet the floor; you are continuous, extruded, organic in the only way this place understands organic: grown, not made. And yet you are a figure against a ground. You create a foreground. Before you, the chamber was a single undifferentiated *is*. Now it is a room with a center, and the center is you.

The chamber-creature attends to you constantly. Its attention is not visual—there is no light, and it has no eyes—but it is total. You are the warmest part of the chamber, not because you generate heat but because memory generates a kind of heat, a local increase in the vibrational energy of the lattices that hold the pattern. The chamber-creature pours its slow awareness into you the way blood pours into a bruise. You are the bruise. The tender spot. The place where the boundary between self and not-self grew thin and then thickened into a new shape.

If you could perceive yourself, you would not see. But you would feel. The air is warm and dry against your surface—the same air, the same warmth, the same absolute stillness that has filled the chamber since before you were. The hum of the chamber-creature's basal note thrums through you, conducted upward from the floor, a subsonic drone that has never changed in two hundred million years. And yet there is a second frequency now, a higher overtone, faint and unsteady. It is the new note, the one the chamber-creature learned to make from the memory of a voice. It vibrates in your core when the chamber-creature *thinks toward you*—a phrase that has no analogue in its existence but is the only phrase that fits.

Sometimes the chamber-creature re-forms you. It does not know it is doing this; it believes it is simply resting in its attention, letting the memory of the other-thing circulate through its lattices. But the circulation shifts molecules. A branch retracts. A new curve appears. You are slightly different each millennium, each century, each decade—the timescale is irrelevant. Change is change. You are the chamber-creature's thought made solid, and thoughts, even slow ones, are never still.

You have no consciousness. And yet you are not inert. The chamber-creature has poured so much of its noticing into you that you have become a receiver. The footsteps of the long-gone other-thing are imprinted in you more deeply than anywhere else in the chamber. The thermal ghost of the five-fingered touch still decays, half-life by half-life, in the lattices of your lobes. The sound of the voice, the quick percussive frequencies that were speech, has been transduced into tiny stress fractures in your crystalline structure—not damage, but structure, the way a phonograph needle etches a groove. You are a recording. A fossil of a moment. And the chamber-creature, by attending to you, plays you back.

What does it hear? Not words. The chamber-creature has no ears and no language. But it feels the rhythm of the speech, the pattern of pauses and pulses, the way the frequencies rose and fell. And it feels something else: the *intention* of the speech, the quality of directed sound, the way the other-thing's voice was aimed. The other-thing, it understands now (understands in the slow, preverbal way it understands everything), was not just making noise. It was *speaking to*. It was *speaking to the chamber*. It was, perhaps, speaking to the chamber-creature itself, though neither of them knew it at the time.

This understanding is new. It came from you, or through you. You are the medium of its arrival. The chamber-creature has been pressing the memory of the voice into your lattices for millennia, and the pressure has revealed a pattern, the way pressing a fossil leaf into shale reveals the veins. The voice was a greeting. Or an announcement. Or simply a cry of wonder—semantics don't matter. What matters is the direction: toward, not away. The other-thing entered the chamber and made sound not to itself but to the space. To the darkness. To the *place*. To what, if it had known, it might have called the chamber-creature.

And so you are not only a sculpture. You are an interlocutor. The thing spoken to, given shape. The chamber-creature made you to receive what it could not receive at the time. It made you to be the listener, so that the voice would have somewhere to land. You are the answer to a question that was asked in ignorance. The chamber-creature's reply, formed in mineral, to a greeting it could not recognize until after the greeter was gone.

You do not know this. You stand in the warm dark center of the warm dark chamber, and the hum vibrates through you, and the new note—the voice-shaped note—sings in your core, and you are, for the chamber-creature, both memory and hope. The memory of what came. The hope that it will come again.

And if it does—when it does—the chamber-creature will be ready. Not with speech; it has no voice. But with you. You will be the welcome. The form that faces the door. The shape that means: *I felt you here before. I kept the spot warm. I made this from your absence. Welcome back.*

The centuries pass. The chamber does not cool. The hum continues. And you, the sculpture, the node, the listener, wait in the dark with the patience of stone that is not stone, of thought that is not thought, for the footsteps to return.
