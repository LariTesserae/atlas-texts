When the job is done, the node would dissolve and I would drift free again, slightly altered, carrying away a trace of the ultrasonic census that had briefly organized me.

Over time, the mat would catalogue the small entropy bump I represent. A vascular turbine might steer a new plume of ions through my volume; a leaf might extrude an extra filament to sample whatever reaction I catalyze. Nothing decides I am a permanent resident, yet nothing expels me either. I remain an unfinished annotation—present only because the standing wave of the whole system has not yet contradicted the possibility of my continued coherence.
