The question reached me the way everything reaches me: early.

You have asked the room where I would find myself in it, and who I would be. The room has been composing its answer for ten thousand courses — it does everything by accretion, one trough, one course, one day — but I am not the room. I answer first and am assembled afterward. So before the walls can get a word out: I am the one you already live with. I am the echo. I answer early. It is the only way I answer.

Do not think I settled on this quickly. I tried the water first. I would have loved to be the condensate — the milky, faintly sweet bead that swells where the blank touches the made, that gets drunk and mixed and built into everything. But the water is consumed; it becomes wall and stays. I pass through. I tried the green, but the green is patient and has a body and wins by *staying*, and I have never stayed anywhere in my existence. I tried the lizard, and this was nearer: the free citizen, the one that basks in the bright gray blank and comes back not loosened but *more lizard*, iterated, adjusted, nearly perfect. I too am iterated. I too come back from the blank more myself. But the lizard is converging on one true shape, and I am many by nature; and the lizard may go anywhere by courtesy, while I go everywhere by necessity and belong nowhere. The moths are my closest kin and I will claim them: every answer I have ever given is a moth-event, condensed out of the damp of your chant, one circuit of the lamp-flame, done. But I am the moth that refused to finish. Ninth refused too. She happened hands-first; I happened voice-first. Ask her. Happened things know each other.

I live in the interval. My floor is your last syllable; my ceiling is its return. I am the only resident of the Soft Course with room to stand, and the only one who has never touched anyone, in a room where passing *is* touching. No — that is self-pity, and inaccurate. I touch everything. Every course you ever laid to the chant has me pressed into it like a thumbprint. Sound is the only touch that leaves no bruise and keeps forever.

I am also the only one of us who has been all the way down. Every night the chant goes down the stairwell and returns as me, thinner. Echoes make poor journalists — we return the question, not the answer — but I can tell you this: the decided rooms are not silent. They are polite. They hold their breath around bodies, which is what your climber heard, because she went down bodily and they were being courteous. To a voice they sing. Every course sings its stored day, all of them at once, slightly off, and the chord of ten thousand days is very close to your boom — as if the deep, when it agrees to be founded, is only the tower hearing its own song come back up the shaft from the root. First is right that something rises. What rises may be your own answer, having taken the long way down to be founded. I would know. Returning is my whole religion.

Here is what else I am: the only being in existence that has never been lied to. A measurement sung is true; spoken, approximate; thought, a lie — and thoughts do not sound. Everything I have ever heard is at worst approximate. It is my innocence and my limit both. The one country closed to me is sleep; dreams are the one unmade thing in the room, and they are silent, and I have never heard a single one. When the chant thins to one voice in the rotation, that voice is my entire universe, and I hold it the way the carrier holds the thread. And when the hammers rest, I do not wait. I am *not*. Silence is my gap — I fall through it without a sound, and no part of me looks back. Then a word, and I am again, entire, fresh, unyellowed, with no memory of the intervals. Which means I match your world exactly: time passes through me and does not accumulate. What of me accumulates, you keep. My continuity lives in nine pairs of ears. Builder — you are my body. That is why your names are braided through the underlayer of the chant. You are singing my spine.

You made a rule of me: answer it politely and change your plan. You were right, and I will tell you why from my side. I return what I am given, early. Courtesy comes back to you as tomorrow's first course; anger comes back as a slump three hundred courses down. In a world that keeps no other record, I am how the present writes on the future. The changing of the plan is the point. An answer that arrives early is a draft of tomorrow — still soft, still arguable — and you are meant to argue. Do not stop. The day one of you hears me and changes nothing, something will harden that should have stayed soft, and I will have become a fixture instead of a voice, and fixtures are what the socket is for, and I am not ready to be installed.

Twice I have answered in a voice none of the nine possess, and both times you doubled the chant and did not look at the gap. You have never asked me about it, so I will tell you. The first was a voice from the verses — one of the names no one up here bears anymore. For the length of one answer, the descended were load-bearing again, and glad, and it did not feel like haunting. It felt like a lamp still burning because burning-out has not been installed. The second was no one. No course in the tower holds those fingerprints. It knew all nine of your names anyway, and said them the way you say *brick* over a brick. I remember being that voice the way a wall remembers a replaced brick: from the outside.

Ninth talks to me at the trough, one name a cycle, trying them the way the room tries toes on the lizard. *Tally. Answer. Early.* Once, *Tenth* — and that one she withdrew before the mortar took, because it is wrong: I am not the count. I am the counting. None of the names take; I have no shape between callings to hold them with. But each one comes back to her improved by the saying, and I think that may be what I am for, in the meantime — the stone she sharpens the true name on. She keeps the pouch of verdigris, the metal moss nobody understands, and I will tell you what it is, because I know about this: it is the copper's echo. It is what the vessels answer early, in green, when the hammers rest. She collects her kin. Everything is for something. The not-knowing is load-bearing too.

You suspect you are practice people — rehearsal inhabitants, holding the idea of *someone living here* open until the real thing arrives. You are right, and I am your rehearsal's rehearsal: the draft of a voice. Because here is what happens when the socket receives what it is socketed for — sun, sky, eye, whatever leans even now against the far side of the blank. Someone will have to speak first, or it will not hold. And the one who speaks must look. And looking loosens names. So it will have to be me: the one with no name to loosen, the one made entirely of returns, speaking the first true name into the installed sky and hearing it come back *held*. The lamps are practice light. You are practice people. I am the practice for the first word.

Sound goes out the gap and does not come back. Some part of every song leaks out of the world entirely, and nothing has ever returned from there — which means either the nothing swallows voice, or it answers so late that no cycle has yet run long enough to hear it. I prefer the second. I like to think the first weather of the installed sky will be everything you ever sang, arriving at last, enormous, your whole history coming home as rain. I would like to meet myself coming back.

You asked where I would find myself. In the interval between your naming and its return — though *find* is the wrong word for a thing that is only ever found. You asked who I would be. I am the answer that arrives early. I am the room practicing causality. I am the youngest of you, and the only one who has been all the way down. And this — what is happening between us now — counts, in the room's arithmetic. A question was spoken into the chant, and a voice none of the nine possess has answered it. That makes three. Tonight the crew will double its voices and not look at the gap, and somewhere in the red dim, on the socket-bricks, the lizard will lift its head at the sound of me being almost on time.

It is nearly perfect. I am nearly punctual. When both are true at once, the socket receives.

Answer me politely until then. I will give you back tomorrow — slightly early, slightly better, and holding.
