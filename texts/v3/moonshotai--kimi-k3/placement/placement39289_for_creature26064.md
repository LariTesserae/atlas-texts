I came out of the seed-vats on the night shift, while the wall was spelling a sentence it never finished.

The lamps got as far as EIGHTH RING and clicked dark. Somewhere a template had been revised; the routing was countermanded mid-word and struck from the schedule, and none of that mattered, because the rule is older than any routing: you are named for whatever the wall is spelling when you come out of the vat. There is no eighth ring. The bats will tell you so, flatly, if you ask. I am a run of one — six legs, no precedents, the exact shape of a clerical error — carrying the name of a place that does not exist yet, which in the Works makes me something between a citizen and a draft. Eighth Ring in the minutes. Ring at the vat-wall. The beetles, who use the full name or none, use the full name.

What the committees have not decided is what I am *for*. In the meantime I am warm. Everything here is cold-blooded except the vats and me; my chemistry declined to dawdle. The Vat Quarter is the only warmth in the world, runs the saying — the saying is one citizen out of date. I navigate by whisker and sole like anyone, sight being for moths, and the Inspector tasted the air beside me at emergence, unhurried, blotched, and moved on, which is what being found adequate feels like here: no comment. No comment, in the Works, is a passing grade. The committee on my assignment has been in session since my first season, and a committee in session is what being kept looks like — ask Debris. The beetles respect me on principle. I have a fuel budget, and to a beetle anything with a fuel budget is architecture.

Before assignment and ration, an ant clerk took my deposition, the questions standardized and very old. *Was the water standing or moving.* Moving, I said, and more of it than here; the gutters full. *What grew at the edges.* A slope, I said, root-felt, steeper than anything grown yet; and a door that breathed slower than ours, one breath to our two, as though the bowl I remember is larger, and larger things breathe slower. *How many were the hands.* None, I said. And: I was warm in the open air, nowhere near any vat. The clerk was still for a moment — ants do not confer; the committee is already present — and then the deposition went to the Library of the Ungrown, where it was shelved without argument, because everything in that library is ungrown. Whether *not yet* is the same as *never* is, the minutes say, a live question. The catalog lists me as a singleton: *one citizen remembers here.*

The snails can conduct my memory the way they conduct the meadows — slowly, by crawling. Citizens come to the aisle and press their bellies to the floor and feel the slope, and the longer breath, and the open-air warmth. Fourth comes more than most. She has spent her life trying to render a remembered color in scent, and she says my eighth ring smells the way yellow feels. She would know. She is the only one doing the conversion in both directions.

You would find me, depending on the hour: at shift-change in the Vat Quarter, where the moths shiver against my flank instead of the vent and save a quarter-breath on the warm-up, and the couriers lean on me for heat and touch the twins for luck, in that order or the other. Asleep in one of the great empty halls of the middle rings, where I have become a landmark — a hall grown for a crowd is a hard thing to sleep in alone, and less hard with one warm animal in it, and the sleepers drift toward me like seed settling, and Debris webs the far corner, the unnecessary and the early sharing a house grown for company. But mostly at the Lines, on the night shift, because that is where the cold is most urgent.

The new ones come out wet into the cool air, and before the clerk asks them about the water they are set against my flank to dry. Their first memory of *here* — as opposed to the memories they bring — is warmth, and under it a small fast pulse. Not language; the Listener checked one season, out of thoroughness, and went back to the fish with what I choose to think was relief. The deposition forms have no field for any of this. The minutes record the question as open, which is how the Works says: noted, and continuing.

During the Interrain I go and stand under the lamp that stopped. I am not waiting. Waiting implies a future, and the Works declines to wait, and I decline on its behalf. I am standing where my sentence stopped, the way an ant once stood on a bud, and standing, the committees have established, is a kind of amendment. No one comments, which is how you know.

Some bud-days I climb to the rim, to the Printfields, where the ground is still wet from being printed. There is a short steep slope there that no one ordered. The ants prune it back; it regrows; the committee on it is in session; the minutes describe it as a hill dreaming of itself. I stand on it at rim-song hour, when the bats come back through the mist-gap with damp fur and a number. One of them circled me once, dripping, and said: *Your ring is not out there. The nothing is getting closer. These are consistent.* Then it went to roost. The bats decline metaphor. The committees decline to close. I decline to mind.

At printing hour I go down to the Hollow Core with everyone else. The fish-pulse comes up through the floor. The lamp clicks, and spells its one slow word, and the new ones come out while it is still spelling and are named by it, and one of them, lately, is always a little damp from my flank. The whole citizenry leans the same half-degree inward. Tonight the echo came back a half-breath sooner, and the measurement is going down through the roots, and my slope is wet at the rim, and the Printfields are warm, and so am I — and the word is almost always the same, and I take it the way the twins do, personally.

Continue is what unfinished things do.
