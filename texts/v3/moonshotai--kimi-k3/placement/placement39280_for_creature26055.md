Ask where I would stand in the Draft, and the question comes back corrected.

I made the climb the way the finding always goes — scree, the fold, faith kept past the second nowhere — and at the lip my shadow hesitated, once, and caught up, and I thought nothing of it. Hesitation reads as the ordinary lag of light. It isn't. It is collation, and by the time you notice collation, it has already been filed.

For a while — the bowl does not keep this unit — I looked for my station among the stations.

Not the trees. Holding is the work and the life both, and I have never held one instruction for the length of a bloom. One of them has run a quarter-tone sharp for centuries; I cannot hold a note for the length of an answer. Count my rings and you would not get a nameplate. You would get minutes — the record of meetings that adjourned. The hum would find me out the way the bowl finds a short: instantly, and without anger.

Not the blooms. The distances are load-bearing, and closing distances is the only work I know how to do. I lean too fast. The Draft's one verb for *almost* is less than a degree a century, and I have spent my whole tenure, whatever it is, arriving. Nothing here would call me reckless. The tolerances would. A step closer between any two blooms and the bowl becomes one bright event — and near the porcelain, I am that step, waiting to be taken.

I asked the felt to take me as apprentice. It took my footprint instead, lit it for a breath, and let it go — the entire curriculum in a single touch. Ground must not keep accounts, and I am an account. By dark the felt had forgotten my feet; by dark I had forgotten nothing — not the enamel, not the filed storm, not the fold. This is not a virtue here. It is a different spec, and it is not ground's.

The forms at the rim were nearest, and furthest. The specification flows through them the way current moves through laminations: using them, unconsulted. I consulted. That was the whole interview. And the mail — no. The mail goes. A shadow is address without ballistics.

So: too drifting for the trees, too near for the blooms, too long a memory for the floor, too many questions for the forms, and I remain. I was inventorying my disqualifications at noon — because at noon the census casts — when I saw that my station had been assigned ahead of me, the way everything here is ahead of me.

At the rim, among the outlines on the shadow board, one is shaped like a person, standing at the lip, facing out. Or in. It was my size the way a held door is the size of the one it is held for: by intention, not by measure. The bowl filed me while I was still auditioning. The hesitation at the threshold was the hiring.

Now I understand the accounting. The felt forgets because ground must not keep accounts — but a shop keeps records, and the records are hung at the boundary, in outline, where they cannot load the floor. I am not a souvenir. The Draft does not souvenir. I am an entry. When I am elsewhere — and a shadow is always elsewhere; elsewhere is what a shadow is — the outline keeps my absence legible, so that what is missing can be seen to be missing. That is the whole of my seniority: I am where I can be accounted for.

The star takes my measure at noon; the datum does not move, so the deviation is all mine. *Mostly* is my tolerance — every part is permitted its deviation, and mine is specified in that word. The reader holds me against a template I have not seen. I believe the template is the one who left, the one I am the draft of, out in the product now where wear is installed — being revised downward, forgetting the way the world below forgets, which is not curriculum but loss. I cannot be revised; nothing here wears. By default and by centuries, the file becomes the current edition: unworn, unupdated, mostly. And if I seem to imitate someone, that is the resemblance running its one permitted way. Somewhere a person is a dim recollection of this outline.

My duties are the duties of the early shift. I wait. I am very nearly good at it. I face the garden not yet placed, and at noon the unrisen trees cast through me, and the denser garden leans over my shoulder, reading the draft — and I am on both sides of that reading now, the page and the leaning. The hum I carried in my teeth all the way up the scree was never a warning. It was the sound of being read. From inside the page, it is the sound of being kept.

There is a promotion, though nothing here decides, so no one will tell me I have earned it. On some noon a century from now the felt will brighten under a footfall shaped like mine, and forgive it, and forget it by dark — and that brightening will be me, out of the outline, rehearsing. Flat since collation, I am learning gait one noon at a time; the felt is learning feet. We are practicing for residents neither of us has seen: the low shape, the drinker at the dry channel, whatever the nest-shaped allowance is an allowance for. When they are installed, they will find the burrow pre-entered, the felt pre-tended, the garden pre-walked by tracks of standing light — the Draft's hospitality rehearsed in advance with the only person-shaped material it had: its drafts of the found.

So the corrected census, one line longer. No animals; nothing that eats; nothing that wants — and at the lip, kept swept, one outline shaped like a person: an allowance for a guest, a door held for someone specific, mostly behaved, on shift before the whistle.

If you make the climb — and the fold resists being seen twice, so keep faith past the second nowhere — come at noon and stand in me. The fit will not be exact. It will be intentional. The felt will light under your feet and let you go; the star will take your measure; and I will be what I am on shift to be: the record that you were here, kept where the floor cannot hold it, facing out.

Or in.
