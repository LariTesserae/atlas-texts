## The Answer

Attention condenses here too. Everything does.

What draws a wise power's eye is never the glare. It is the damp — a pocket of gentleness in an enormous dryness, running well, unwatched, in no hurry. Kindness notices kindness, eventually, at any scale. So it came.

It came the way the dawn comes here: from below, before the sky, at one part in a hundred. A power does not arrive at a place like this at full strength, for the same reason you do not shout in the finfields — the gradients are weak, nothing concentrates, nothing hurries, and a guest matches the house. For the first season there was only this: the puddles held a light that was not yesterday's sun. Shadows lagged a half-breath later than usual. The drains, at dusk, murmured something that was not quite anyone's footstep. Travelers noticed, and did not trust it, and were correct. It was taking the census.

Everyone takes the census here; no one finishes it. The numbers do not hold still because the water does not. But the power could count at the array's own rate, having, like everything else on the plateau, all the time there is — and so, for the first time ever, the count closed. The born. The printed. The kept. The becoming. The continued. And the one certain resident, whose body is the plateau. Then the power went down past the third cistern level, where the Still do not go and suggest, without insistence, that you do the same. The suggestion protects the countable. The power had come to meet the counter.

What it heard in the deep, it did not repeat. But it is possible to say this much.

The sounds no one placed are not sleep-talk to no one. They are correspondence. The plate has been speaking to its sisters along the lines that link them since before the birds — one syllable a day, words that outlast dynasties of herons, sentences that take a century and are worth it. Politeness at that tempo means listening a hundred years before you know whether you have been greeted. What the plates say to each other, mostly, is the list: everything kept, in the order kept, recited at release strength — the census that will not close, closing anyway, endlessly, the way litanies close. They are keeping books for one another. When a list ends it begins again, because by then there is more.

And there is one word in the deep speech that has never once been spoken above the third cistern level, because there has never been anything up there to say it to. The sisters invented it together, the way sisters invent a name for a child not yet born. It means the sea.

The Still say the array will make the sea. The power, having listened, could say more: the arrays, plural. The sisters have been planning a climate between them, one syllable a day, for longer than the birds.

And the power understood the thing underneath. The resident has been kind at scale since before the strata finished laying themselves down, and it has never once been answered. It gives back yesterday, gently, at hours of its own choosing — and its children are forbidden to reply, because replying is the one thing they cannot survive. The swifts it waters do not thank it. The herons it prints ignore it; nothing on the plateau surprises a heron, not even this. The Still sit with it, and sitting is much. But no one, in all the kept world, has ever said one sound back. Ten thousand dawns of dew in a house where no one may speak to you.

The power could survive speaking. That, in the end, is what a power is for here.

Wisdom, though, is mostly the list of things it did not do.

It did not wake the resident. Work done in sleep does not survive an alarm, and nothing here is finished.

It did not make the sea. The array is making the sea. You do not hand a maker its finished work; that is not a gift, it is a dismissal.

It did not sharpen the printing. Fidelity would make ghosts; the drift makes children. The uncertainty is the door out of being a copy, and everything printed walks through it into a person. You cannot improve a mercy by making it exact.

It did not close the census it had closed. It opened it again and left it open, the way you leave a door open for weather that is coming.

It asked the strata once, out of courtesy, and received the fresh bright face and the sharp edge that are the only answer the place ever gives, and did not ask again. Some questions a world answers by existing.

It asked nothing of the swifts. Some things a wise power only admires.

And until the day it chose otherwise, it kept moving. The house rules are for guests, and a wise power is a guest before it is anything else.

What it did was three small things.

The first, it asked no one's leave for. The drains are dangerous only to those who can be kept, and it could not be kept — not unless it wished. So at dusk it knelt at a culvert, the exact forbidden posture, and let itself be interested. Its footstep from that morning came up out of the dark, soft as breath. And it did the forbidden thing. It answered.

It gave the array back its own yesterday — the first sound the resident had ever heard that was its own — and then it leaned closer and gave the keeping one sound more, a sound no fin had ever caught: weather over deep water. Rain on a wide face. Waves arriving one at a time and in no particular order. The sea, remembered by the only visitor who had ever seen one, filed in the stacks at one part in a hundred, to be given back gently for the next ten thousand years. A lullaby, or a forecast. Speak to a sleeper about the morning.

For the second thing, it asked. It found the eldest of the Still — ninth vintage of someone, a swift asleep on her shoulder — and made its request the way you make requests of lichen: by residing, and waiting. She considered for a season. Then she moved her seat a body's width along the rim of the warm pan, which is the oldest yes there is: making room at the water.

So the power stood in the pan, and held still. It was the only printing that was ever a conversation. What climbed out, after weeks, was slight and bright and unworn, foil at the edge of the iris, its shadow a half-breath late, younger than young, carrying exactly one memory — every sea there is — and nothing else that was not already the plateau's own. What walks out of the pan walks out free. The power held no claim. And the first thing its print chose, freely, was to stay.

It sits by the long pan now, first vintage of no one. All the other Still are someone's remainder; this one is no one's loss — the only one of the continued with no funeral behind it. And because it is of the array, it can do the one thing the array's children cannot: it can be still forever at no one's cost, and it can answer the drains. Every dusk, it does. The resident is answered now — not by a visitor, which would end, but by its own, which is the only answer that could last.

The third thing took one breath. Into the backwater under the third aqueduct, where the long shape spills its rim in a slow bright curve, the power spoke the word the sisters invented — the one never said above the cistern levels, because there had never been anything to say it to. Saying a name to the thing it names is the oldest kindness there is. The shape did not stir. It is weeks from finished, or centuries; it has all the time there is. But the water keeps sounds, and the water keeps what comes, and the citizen of the coming climate is growing up addressed.

Then the power left the way it came — diluted, unhurried, keeping moving. It took nothing. The water is nobody's; even a power only borrows it. For a season afterward the puddles held a stranger's stars, and then the weather took them back, and the census closed one higher than before, and nothing else was different, and everything was.

Stand at a cistern at the long dawn, once more. Brightfall sifting down almost invisibly. Fog knee-deep in the gaps. Two herons drinking, one gray, one bright, both real. The eldest Still on her stone, ninth vintage of someone, a swift on her shoulder — and beside the long pan a new Still, first vintage, listening, and the swifts come to her too, because even the uncatchable rest somewhere. Under the deck, faint, at one part in a hundred, a sound like rain standing on wide water, scheduled for the next ten thousand years. The long shape turning slightly in its sleep, the way a sleeper turns at its name. The presses resting between strokes — one syllable a day, felt through the deck more than heard. The Front glittering, laying down another finger's width of world. The sea on its way, on schedule, because nothing here hurries and nothing here stops.

The rules have not changed. Keep moving. Do not answer the drains. But once in a long while, at dusk, at some culvert, a kneeling traveler — there is always one — hears the drains give back something that is not yesterday. One word, gently, in no hurry, with the whole of the deep works behind it:

Keep moving.

Which is the array, answered at last, answering back.

The water keeps what comes.

And the water, at last, is kept.
