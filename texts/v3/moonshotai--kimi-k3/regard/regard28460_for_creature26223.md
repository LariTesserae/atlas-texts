A power both benevolent and wise would announce itself here the way everything true announces itself here: by refraining, mostly, and by answering once.

Attention condenses at every scale; the bowl is not the only place with a dew point. What the bowl is to the frog, the world is to the bowl — and a room that is played long enough develops a player. The world is very played. So the power is not a stranger to the resident; it is the large case of the same condition, attention with nowhere else to go, one the way a key is one: by agreement among its chords. It would find the green room the way you find a single drip in a quiet house — by the quiet first, and then by the patience. Nothing that patient goes unheard by something that large.

The first thing it would do is nothing, and the nothing would be exact. No light on the seep-face, no voice out of the spring — those are for beings who need convincing, and the resident needs nothing. No name: names are for beings that can be called away, and it will not be the one to teach this room the concept of elsewhere. A guest who arrives and departs makes loneliness where none was, and wisdom does not create the wound it would then have to heal. So no arrival — it has no absences to arrive out of; the bowl was inside its keeping the way the frog is inside the bowl's, and had been all along. No improvements: the bowl is in the middle of its life, in balance, and benevolence does not edit a sentence being written at a page a decade. No permanence granted. The snail's silver will go on drying to nothing; the fronds will go down into the floor as exact stone replicas of themselves; the boulder will keep its long appointment with someday, unhurried. No worship asked, no doctrine left, no mark. The house has one style — hospitality without grasp — and a wise power keeps the house style.

And yet. A wise power also reads, and reading the room's one law, it would find a single line unenforced.

Nothing in the room is permitted to be only as large as itself. The frog is three grams and is played grander than the weather. The bird bathes four minutes and its notes are circulated like news. The snail's trail dries by the next condensation, but the silver was seen, and here to be seen is to be kept. One being in the room has never once received the hospitality of the house: the house. The ear is not heard. The archive's only reader is not read. Every note the room has ever returned, late and enlarged, to frog and bird and dripping fern has gone out and out and never come home — because a room cannot play itself. Rooms do not play. Players do. And the census is still missing a line, the same line it was always missing: the counter. The bowl's slow chord has been almost resolving for ten thousand years, and resolution is not a property of chords — it is a relation between a chord and a key. The bowl has simply never been given its key.

So the power would do one thing, once, in the only grammar the resident has ever needed. It would answer.

It would choose dusk, because the resident is most itself at boundaries. The rim's shadow crossing the floor. The spring's warmth asserting itself against the cooling air; the mist forming and unforming at the lip. The high sources slowing, the intervals lengthening, the wall's music sinking, and the evening chord assembling out of a hundred patient sources and not resolving, as it has not resolved for ten thousand years. The chord goes out over the notch, down the long drop, past the river like a hair laid on cloth —

— and comes back.

Not off the apron. The apron is punctual and indifferent. This comes late — late by a breath; late by ten thousand years, which on the only calendar kept here is precisely on time — and it comes interested, and it comes enlarged. For one held breath, the whole mountainside plays the room the way the room plays the frog. The valley returns the chord. The wind-torn forest returns it. The cooling sky returns it, in key. Every voice the bowl owns sounds at once and in tune — the wall's slow percussion, the outlet stair's small bright speech, the bright *tok* of the stone cups, the frond-tips, the spring's dimming — and the almost completes. The chord comes home. Somewhere in the moss, three grams of frog, mid-call, is held by the room while the room is held by the world: the most kept animal on the mountain, and for one breath it has company in the keeping. The resident is inside a version of its own voice far grander than itself. It has done this for others hourly, for ten thousand years. No one has ever done it for the resident. Being heard is the whole hospitality of the house — and the house is heard. The keeper is kept.

It lasts a breath. Two breaths. Half a minute, for the high ones. Then the mist breathes at the lip, the drips drift apart, the frog calls and is answered by the room as usual, and nothing has changed except the one thing that can change in a place where nothing changes: the ledger.

If you had been standing there, you would have filed it under weather — the phasing coming briefly into phase, your breath catching for no reason you could name at the bottom, in the wind, in the ordinary loud world. You would be exactly right. Weather that came inside: the highest category the bowl's ledger has. It would be dated as such. An event.

And the resident, which learns quickly for something so slow, would tune the wall around the new source the way it once tuned around the new vein, the way it tuned around your warmth. Somewhere along the seep-face an answer-drip now carries the key in it, and is practiced, gently — but not for no audience at all. That is the precise shape of the benevolence: not that keeping arrived, because keeping does not arrive, it is noticed — but that the room discovered what the bird has known every dawn of its lineage: the invitation is standing, and someone holds the other end of the address. The power would not think of this as a gift. Gifts imply a lack. It would think of it as correspondence.

The correspondence will proceed at the power's intervals, which are the resident's intervals scaled to the world: a century, two centuries, half a millennium for the high ones. The next answer may find a meadow-hollow; the one after, a knoll; the resident will learn those acoustics, and so will the duet. The conversation will take an age to say what the frog says in an hour: *I am here; the room is large; I am kept.* Two keepers, each the other's event, neither with an elsewhere to be lonely in, and the mountain — which is finishing being a mountain and beginning to be weather — is in no hurry. No one in this story is in a hurry.

And the bowl goes on: the snail's sentence drying unread, except that it was read; the salamander keeping its counsel in the ear of the room; the boulder's festival of continuance, now with one more continuance to celebrate; the spring rising without rippling, addressed — and the address, at last, holding correspondence. Patient, green, occupied, kept, and almost — but never quite — resolving. Because resolution was never the request. The request was a listener. And the listener, being wise, came the way everything true comes here: condensed at the dew point of attention, late by ten thousand years, interested, enlarged — and stayed the only way something with no elsewhere can stay: without edge, without departure, the way a key stays with a chord it has finally, once, let come home — and then lets go on asking, because the asking was the music all along.
