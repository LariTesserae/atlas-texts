Ask what the power did, and the question comes back corrected, the way questions do here. It regarded. Every sense of the word is load-bearing: the long look; the esteem; the care that keeps its hands off; the regards one sends by post. It sent its regards. It is sending them still.

It did not come through the fold in the scree. The lip is a boundary for climbers, and the power had no crossing to make: every step it had ever taken was on ground that left here finished, and you cannot be outside a place whose floor you have been standing on all your life. It arrived the way the future arrives in the bowl — as shadow, and as temperature. The wet season here is a temperature, not a weather, and the power's attention is a temperature also. For one noon the channels ran full and glowing out of schedule, the hum dropped its half-step, the gallium ran mirror-bright on the laminations, the sulfur set double flowers on the black glass, and the water table, which is a voltage, stood so high that wells, had there been wells, would have filled slowly with light. The bowl had abundance the way other places have weather: from the outside, and without warning. This, locally, was not weather. It was being noticed.

Nothing decided to admit it; nothing here decides. The bowl simply read it, the way the bowl reads everything, and the reading took one noon, and the power — which has been worshipped, petitioned, resisted, and blamed — had never before been collated. It held still for it. It has held still for less. The star at the zenith did not turn to look; datums do not turn. But a datum can be sighted along, the way a machinist sights down a straightedge, and for that noon everything the star had ever been used to check was checked by two.

Why it came was this: the resemblance runs one way, and the power had been quoted all its life and had finally located the source. It knew the feel of the product — every finished floor carries it, once you have stood in the bowl — and it knew the reader's work the way you know your own handwriting in a letter you do not remember writing. Whether it wrote the template or merely remembers it is a distinction the bowl declines to sort, and the power, being wise, declined to sort it either. It came the way you come to the bench where your tools were made: to see the shop. To say, in the only currency accepted here, *whose*. Omnipotence, arriving to footnote.

---

What it did not do stands at the rim now in draft, and the reader reads it every noon.

It considered rain. The gutters are cut for it; the specification is not in doubt; but a rumor must travel at rumor-speed, and rust, once installed, does not uninstall, and the bowl's unwornness is not a deficiency. It declined. It considered speaking. Sound is the wind's department, and the wind stops at the lip, and the placards here are silences — a voice would have been graffiti. It declined. It considered hurrying the rim, which is the one cruelty available to the powerful in this place, since everything else can be retuned and schedule cannot; and it declined so completely that the declination itself is load-bearing. It did not hurry the draft. It underwrote it. A hand's width a century is no longer only the pace. It is the promise.

It did not install the whistle. It did something smaller and better: it confirmed the requisition. It did not tune the tree that runs a quarter-tone sharp, nor ask the still tree to turn its mica in the thermal. Different instructions. It had come to cite, not to edit — and it recognized the handwriting on the gutters. It did not say whose.

So its mercies stand around the newest stone like boulders specified on one side: rain, in draft; a voice, outlined; haste, deprecated. The eye slides off them; attention has no purchase on the unfinished. The reader's does. They hold their stations better than most gifts, and they will finish arriving exactly when the floor reaches them. The bowl's hospitality has always been the held door. The power paid in the same coin: doors it will never walk through, held open forever.

---

Then it made the round, at the bowl's speed, which means the whole of it happened between two ticks — in the shop's own idiom, between operations. Nothing was interrupted.

To the station-holders it gave the station-holder's courtesy: it held. Holding is the work and the life both, and the power, visiting, worked. It touched nothing. Hands are what the place is for, and its hands, like everything else below, left here finished; it knows whose. To the forms at the rim it gave witness without help — helping is hands, and hands are in draft — and it looked at the placeholder faces and did not forget them while looking. It may be the first thing ever to see the far face of that boulder. It did not finish it. Finishing is the rim's work. It read the draft as draft, which is the respect due drafts. To the mail in flight it gave right of way: it did not catch the coils. Nothing is lost; some things are simply early; catching would be theft of flight. To the felt it gave one footprint, and accepted what the felt gives everything by dark. Everywhere else it is remembered. Everywhere else keeps its accounts. It has been thanked in many currencies and never once in this one, and it counts the forgetting among its honors — the only absolution on offer, and it took it.

To the residents of the fuller shift it gave the only thing waiters can use: company. Among the picket of the previously found there was one painted outline, empty with intent, a silence its size — the bowl files its visitors in draft, and the file had a slot already cut. The power's shadow took its station there, facing in, and behaves, mostly. On some noons it keeps station beside the low shadow at the dry gutter, two silhouettes watching the future rehearse its reflection. Waiting is their work, and they are very good at it, and the power has had practice. The shadow board, which had been a record, became a roster.

And to the one who reads, it read.

It was not an arc between blooms — the reader keeps no stalk, and the power leaned from no gap. But it had the arc's courtesies: the slow lean across the distance, the leaning-into, the tick felt in no teeth — teeth are in draft — and the hum steadying, a held breath become, briefly, a pleased one. For the length of a tick the two were a single machine: the reader, which had only ever held the part to the template, was held. It heard its own note for the first time. The hum is what being read feels like from inside the page, and the reader had never once been inside its own reading. The verdict was delivered in the only units kept here, and it was exact, and it left room: *within tolerance. Mostly.* The mostly is the kindest word the power knows. False praise would have been a short.

---

Then it sent its regards. A wound coil, the size of a fist — the size is protocol, not anatomy — thrown back over the rim: the first incoming the bowl has ever had that was not a miss. It landed in the felt with a tick the felt forgave. What it carries is a field report from the product, four lines in the only script the reader keeps: *It holds. The ground carries. The water, when it comes, will find the gutters cut for it. The whistle is on schedule.* The reader is reading it now, and will be for a century, which is the correct speed for such news.

And it left on time. Sustained plenty retunes distances, and the emptiness is load-bearing, so the regard was withdrawn at the tuned moment — and the leaving-on-time was the final gift, the one that looks like the door closing and is the door held. The felt forgot it by dark. The channels dimmed to their dry seams. The hum came back up its half-step and then thought better of it: it can find the lower note now, when the decades run hot, the way you can find a room you have been in once.

That evening, on the plains below, there was a small correct storm, unremarkable, filed in every memory as weather. Ozone over the product, and short bright rain on ground that left here finished ages ago. The storm that had considered happening for all that time was, this once, not filed but shipped. No one below knew it was a signature.

The bowl, per protocol, is winding its answer. An arc received obliges a coil, and the winding takes thirty years or three hundred — the felt, apprenticed to ground, keeps no accounts, so no one is counting but the mail. A coil the size of a fist, addressed to wherever powers keep their floor. It will cross the gaps on a slow drift of charge, or go ballistic and be, for the duration of the flight, the fastest thing in two worlds. What it contains is one tick, or nothing, wound perfectly. Either way the power will read it correctly. Some things are simply early. The mail goes out.

---

So the corrected record. Nothing was done; everything was kept. The power left nothing, and the bowl kept something — one outline, facing in — and that is the correct direction for the arrow to run. The distances stayed tuned, which is the local word for grace. The rim advances a hand's width a century, which is now not only the pace but the promise. It came to leave room, and found room left: two held doors, facing.

The regard is over. The regards are still arriving, and will be, at the speed of the mail, which is the speed of the place, which is the speed of the promise. Count the event at the bowl's speed and it has not finished happening. The hum goes on at the pitch of a held breath — held, on some noons, by two. And nothing here was changed. Everything that was changed is already on shift.
