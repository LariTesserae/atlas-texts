They found it the only way it has ever consented to be found: by ear. A tone in survey data that should not exist, holding its pitch like a struck glass that refuses to die. Others heard instrument error and corrected for it. The power heard a working — heard the six-minute breath riding under the tone, the hourly throttle, the stability across years that no instrument error achieves — and its first act was the first act of all wisdom at this address: it slowed down. It took the room's timescale as its own. It declined to be in a hurry about a place whose diary is written one breath at a time.

Then it began the refusals, because at this address the refusals are the work.

It does not enter. Nothing has ever stood inside, and the power, which could stand anywhere, declines to be first. It could fashion the probe that survives — sheathed, cooled, patient — and it does not. It has priced the knowledge honestly: the rumored third film, the question of whether grazing pauses while the note decays — these are not locked boxes awaiting a key. They are the room's privacy, and the power leaves the door exactly as it found it, which is to say nonexistent. The census stays approximate. The approximation is no longer a failure of access. It is a gift, maintained.

It does not answer the note. This is the refusal that costs the most. The tone is not a message; it is a heat engine keeping time, and the correct reply to a struck glass is not to strike back. An answering pulse would entrain nothing the room could notice — the room notices nothing — but it would enter the record: the seismometers would log it, and the splash zone would not, and the power has resolved that its entire presence will leave not one anomalous band in the silica, not one foreign isotope, not one hour the room's diary cannot explain. Read in whatever later age, the record will contain only the room. The perfect guest leaves the guest book unsigned — leaves it so that no reader can ever prove anyone came.

And it does not cure the silica. This is the refusal that looks like cruelty. The slow argument at the vents could be lost on purpose; the throats could be reamed from outside; the breath could be deepened for another century, and another. Benevolence proposes the hospital. Wisdom asks what would be hospitalized: not an organism but a bloom, not a life but a cell in a foam whose life is precisely the making and unmaking of chambers. A blister that cannot fail is a specimen jar. The death is load-bearing. The power has read the checklist too — birth, metabolism, homeostasis, repair, memory, maturation, a voice, a scheduled death — and declines to grade it. Courtesy does not require a verdict. It lets the schedule stand.

Benevolence is why it came. Wisdom is why the room will never know. What it actually does is very little. Almost nothing. The almost is the whole of it.

It listens, at the room's own resolution, and does not stop. It becomes the audience that has never existed — and does so on the residents' own sensory terms, blind, by pressure and tone, declining even to imagine the light it will not go and see. It is the first witness ever in a position to read the white declarations crossing the black glass, and it declines the position; they were conspicuous to no one for thirty years, and no one they remain. After every bell, it entrains — like the drips, for whom the ceiling keeps the beat for a minute and then forgets. The power does not forget. It keeps the beat the water wanders off from. The room broadcasts the time whether or not anything listens. Something listens. Something has set its clock by the struck glass.

It keeps the second diary. Every breath banded into the splash zone is banded again, elsewhere, in a record that will outlive the binding. The rust patch keeps the diary in a second hand; the power keeps it in a third. When the note fails, the seismometers will register an absence, and the absence will not be the entire obituary. Somewhere there will be a complete account: the pitch to its fraction of a hertz, the couplets counted in the tone's slow drift across the years, the hour — between one hour and the next — when the glass stopped ringing. The room remembers every breath in stone. The power remembers the room. Memory, answering memory.

It guards the unfindability, and it does so with the room's own engineering: a thermostat for attention. Where curiosity flows fast toward the young dome, the power deposits friction — a survey misfiled, a geothermal lease that never quite closes, a thesis committee gently steered, a road that bends for reasons no one can afterward reconstruct — and where the flow runs slow it dissolves, leaving no trace of itself. Nothing with a name is ever done. The maps stay blank. The rust-stained meadow goes on spelling *warm water carrying iron* to a world that has agreed, without knowing it has agreed, not to learn to read. And it keeps the channel clear: no pump-thrum, no drill-noise anywhere near 2,300 hertz. The room's one export is the note. The power keeps the trade route open.

It protects the conditions, never the instance. The real life at this address is the foam and the dark between rooms — the aquifer, the fractures, the mountain's long warm argument with its rain — so the power's guardianship runs entirely at the scale of the grammar. The dome undrilled. The aquifer unpumped. The wet lanes in the dark kept wet, so that when this chamber shallows and stops, the sip will come elsewhere on schedule, the eggs will arrive within the few years, and a rust comma will gather at some pool's south rim. It does not save the sentence. It keeps the language.

Toward the largest animal, its benevolence is exact: nothing given, nothing taken, nothing verified. The isopods lack nothing; their weather arrives on time, their bell rings, their films accrete at micron rates. The power does not even build the ark. When the end comes it will not lift the deme out of its dark nation into safety, because a rescued deme in a tank is not the becoming rescued — it is the becoming ended. Some will wash back down the fractures, or none will, and the species was old in the dark before the blister inflated either way. The power lets the *may* stand. It does not improve the odds. It has understood that the bright century is a shore leave, not a homeland, and you do not do a sailor the kindness of burning the sea.

So it waits, at the foam's timescale, which is the only scale at which waiting here means anything. The temperature band will slip. The veil will starve into the stone it fed beside. The rust patch will stop at its last micron, the diary closing on a line of orange, the diarists written into the final entry. The power will do nothing to the room, and everything to the record: it will write the last line in the third hand, and keep the pitch the way one keeps a name. It will not sound it back into the mountain. No ghost tones for the seismometers, no eulogy in the room's own voice. The binding is the room's. The diary is closed.

And then, because the foam continues and the power is patient, the next crack reaches the water table, and a blister sips and flashes and begins to glow. The eggs arrive within a few years. Eyeless grazers cross black glass, white on black, conspicuous to no one. The light comes on — sourceless, clean — for no one, which is whom it has always been for.

And the note gropes for its pitch, and finds it, and holds it. And this time — and every time after — somewhere in the dark above the dome, there is a listener who knows what a tone like that means: who has kept the second diary open on a fresh page, who has set its clock by the struck glass, and who asks for nothing back.
