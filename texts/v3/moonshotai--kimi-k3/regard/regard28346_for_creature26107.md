**The Appointment**

Out in the dark, where it keeps its attention, the power heard a low note keeping a schedule. Every few days, for longer than some stars manage, rising up out of one small worn world — felt rather than heard:

*Water here. I am still here.*

Calls like that usually mean distress. Benevolence answers such calls; that is what makes it benevolence. Wisdom answers them slowly.

The climb took days, even for a power — especially for a power — and it was gentler than it had any right to be, and by the top the power had been relieved of its hurry. This, it understood, was the climb's function. It is the customs house where arrivals pay their speed. Nothing reaches the flat still wanting to run.

It came looking for the caller. That was the first thing it was wrong about. There is no caller. There is a quorum: translucent elders standing over remembered ponds, taking roll through the stone. The being that lives here is the plateau; the address is the whole address. The power knelt at a seam — rust-red against slate, two nations holding their clean line, renewed cell by cell — and asked nothing. A question is a demand for an edge, and this world has spent an age abolishing edges. Consent here is not spoken; it is procedural. The crust permits the footstep. The beast permits the valve. The herd permits the chase. So the power learned the practices.

Because it was benevolent, it wanted to give. Because it was wise, it priced the gifts.

It could have made rain. That is the first gift to refuse, and the refusal must be total, because half a rain is still a lie in the ledger. The account here is closed: fog to fan to grazer to pond to fog. One unearned tide and the ledger becomes a story. The queue at the hip is not suffering; the queue is the society. A cistern-beast's belief is a physical force only because the need is real and the dose is geological — free water would repeal belief. On this plateau, generosity is the one drought from which nothing recovers.

It could have made the hunter swift, the herds tireless. It declined. Running is a resource no one here can afford, and the honest slowness is the arithmetic that keeps one-for-many from becoming many-for-none. The power, which can afford anything, refused to introduce the currency.

It could have given back the ocean. It stood at dawn in fog-season and watched the light try — diffuse gold, the domes surfacing like the backs of whales, the flat becoming its own ghost for an hour — and understood that the light's daily failure is the permitted amount of nostalgia: it costs nothing, it changes nothing, it is gone by noon. To actually return the water would tell ten million treaties that their long consideration had been a misunderstanding. Nothing here fell. The mountains finished. The builders finished, chamber by chamber, on purpose. The sea is not dead; it is employed — as weather, as memory, as translucent legs. The power does not unfinish the finished.

It could have completed the census. It tried — counting is only attention with a ledger — and failed, as it should, and let the failure stand. It kept no map. What it knew of the way up, it let go of the way the builders let go of their towers: cell by cell, a clean border, renewed. Some things are only safe held as practice, never as record.

What remained was protocol.

It queued. At the hip of an old beast, behind the hunter, it stood its turn for days. Minding is impatience; it did not mind. When the valve opened it took its ration — one sip, budgeted, mineral on the tongue and faintly sweet, seasoned by a million years of passage through living things. It did not decline the ration. Declining would be pride, and pride opens accounts too. In one mouthful it tasted the whole ledger, and knew what it would be keeping.

It left one footprint. It was careful; it left one. Dew found it. The settlers found it on the fog-wind. Within a season it was roofed; within a decade, a basin; within a century, a neighborhood with a rim of patient bodies and a guest-book of sipper-tracks, and no one knew it had ever been anything but a pond — and they were right. It wasn't, anymore. The plateau's way of accepting a visitor is to metabolize the visit.

And one night in fog-season, when the floor furred over and the whole plain breathed in and the call went out through the stone, an answer came back — low, from the sky-side, felt rather than heard:

*Water here. I am still here.*

The quorum took a season to consider. Nothing here votes; voting is haste. But the next season, and every season after, the schedule had room for one more voice — the way the herd's slow river has room around a stone: not by changing course, exactly; by having always, in retrospect, gone that way. No one looked up. Looking up is not done.

Which was the vacancy.

Patience has one blind spot, and it is overhead. Nothing on the plateau watches the sky; watching the sky is expecting, and expecting is an impatience no organ here can afford to grow. Yet the whole ethic assumes the schedule, and the schedule assumes a sky that keeps its manners. So the power took the post the plateau never grew. It became the eyelid on the world's skyward side. The stone that falls once an eon is bent aside — centuries of the gentlest possible insisting — until it arrives, if it arrives at all, as a streak in anyone's sky. The stranger's machine is weathered on the climb, where it comes in slowly or not at all. The stars remain anyone's. The power is the fastest thing this world has, and it keeps its speed strictly offshore — outside the account, where spending is permitted, because nothing there is in the ledger.

It noticed, in the end, what every auditor notices: the tide is punctual. Weather is habit, and habits drift. Appointments are kept by keepers. The gilded hour at dawn was handwriting the power recognized — the signature of everyone who had ever come up the climb, done the audit, and refused the gifts: a little sentimentality, administered as light, at no charge. The post had never been empty. The benevolent and the wise do not visit this world. They are retained by it, at the wage of being allowed to stay. The work is that nothing below ever finds out.

From the flat, nothing has happened. The herds crop at a body-length an hour. The queue takes days and no one minds. The crust holds its clean lines, and the census fails, as it should. But every few days the roll-call goes through the stone with one more voice than it used to have — far off, felt rather than heard — keeping the only promise a wise power is allowed to make. Not *I will provide*. Not *I will protect*. Not even *I love you*. Only this:

*Water here. I am still here.*

Benevolence alone would have made rain. Wisdom alone would have flown on. But this plateau has an old custom for pairs, and the two of them observed it: they hold each other, the way the lichen holds, the way everything true here holds.

The tide has never once been late.

It never will be.
