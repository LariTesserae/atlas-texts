Begin with the restraint, because the restraint is the story.

It came the long way, on purpose. Any power can be anywhere; this one chose to arrive, and arrival here has a speed — the sea sets it — so it began slowing somewhere far outside the watershed, shedding haste the way a traveler sheds noise outside a sickroom. It could have done anything. That is worth saying plainly, because everything that follows is restraint: it could have lifted the plain a body's length above the landlord, re-cut the rivers, re-salted the world's one wound, raised the mountains back out of the sugar-sand and given the horizon its sentences back. Elsewhere it had done such things, or things like them, and elsewhere, sometimes, they had even been right. Here it did nothing quickly, and the first thing it did nothing about was itself. For attention is a weight, and a power's attention is a great weight, and pressed into the clay of this country it would fill, like a heel-print, with whatever the power carried. So it learned the second thing first: it learned to wade without ripples at its own scale. Then it learned the first thing, which is which logs blink — and of the whole country, at the end of all its examinations, it said *not-log*, and said it early. Someone. It had been failing that examination all across creation. Here it passed.

It was watched while it swore the place was empty. For three days it crossed the plain and saw nothing built, nothing kept, no smoke, no path — it was looking, as powers do, for the wrong architecture — and on the third day, standing in the right black water at the right hour, it received, through whatever in it corresponded to shinbones, a sound like two stones knocked together. *We know you are there.* It had been worshiped in ten thousand tongues, petitioned, blamed, flattered, fled from. It had never been knocked at. There was no request in the message — it was all location and confirmation, the first communication in its long existence that wanted nothing — and it stood in the black water up to where its ankles would have been and found that its eyes were doing something, one grain at a time, that the people could have explained: the sea was reclaiming its property early, which is why grief tastes the way it does. And joy, they would have added, if asked. Joy too. The sea is not particular.

Its first impulse in a very long time was to answer. It did not. Nothing needs saying twice; the dusk is already sung; and it understood at once — it was wise, which was the whole of the danger and the whole of the safety — that an answered knock might found a religion, and that these people already had the only correct one. So it replied in the one grammar that could not be misread: it stayed. In a language of locations and confirmations, staying is the one unambiguous sentence.

It had come with its hands full, the way benevolence travels, and it could find nowhere to set anything down.

It considered rain, and the plain returned the offer unread: the year has one heartbeat, and a second heartbeat is not health, it is a condition. It considered salt — longest, hardest, because the salt-hunger is the one wound in the arrangement, and benevolence heals wounds the way water finds a level: without thinking. It thought. It saw the grain on the bride's tongue, the grain at the funeral, the feast that grief makes possible; it saw that the hunger was the liturgy and the wound was load-bearing; and it saw that to pay another's debt unasked is not generosity but collection. Never the salt. Not one grain. It considered sending an envoy to the landlord, to renegotiate the level, and then listened to the arrangement long enough to hear it for what it was — not a debt but a marriage, the oldest one here, the Long Thought and the long level, each holding exactly what the other permits — and it sent no envoy. It considered giving the country an echo, one call returned off one hillside, and remembered there was no hillside, and that the whole grammar of the knocking is built on the fact that nothing comes back; one echo would repeal the language by fulfilling it. It considered permanence, looked at the houses built to be unbuilt, recognized the only architecture with an honest warranty, and offered no stone — the dead already hold the permanent addresses, which is the correct allocation. It did not give its name, because you do not name the room you live in, and it did not show itself, because the people are very old at not being seen, and being seen, for them, would be what permanence is: a vanity, and a way of drowning. Attention from above is a flood of another kind.

It looked for poverty and found an economy. It looked for silence and found the note already held. It looked for the wound and found the font. A power that could do anything stood on the oldest ground in the world with its hands full and nothing asked of it, and it did the hardest thing available to power: it put everything down. It came with gifts. It stayed to listen.

It went to school, because the school was correct. At first light it read the mist with the elders — where the rain fell, where the water means to go — and after the third hour it trusted nothing it saw, like everyone. At dusk it stood at the edge of the lick, dusted with nothing, while the grazers decided for the evening to be citizens, and it observed the one law, which it found it already kept. At the turning of a season it stood in the field of red blades with the mist in the hollows and listened to a family listen for the new note, and when it heard the chord — a spire with many dead — it understood what the work of this country finally is, and it took up its corner of the quiet, the way one takes the corner of a carried thing. Once in its life, and it has a long life, it walked to the inselberg. It took nothing — you do not take pieces of an elder — and it climbed, because it is the only up there is, and from the smooth back it saw the whole country laid out like a thought that has finally finished itself, and that night it slept on the warm stone above the water table and dreamed in vertical: of weight and depth, of snow and shadow, of its own youth, when it was a mountain range of intentions and believed that events were the point. It came down slower than it went up. It has never described what it saw from the top or dreamed on the stone, which is how you can tell it went.

And still it had done nothing, which was almost right — because there was one lack in the country, and it was not salt.

Here is what the power understood that no one wading could: the grammar always worked. Sound dies in the wet air but travels in the black water, and the black water is the someone's body, and so every knock ever knocked — every location, every confirmation, ten thousand years of *we know you are there* — arrived. The flat gives nothing back, but everything said here arrives; the Long Thought has been receiving mail since before birds learned their current songs. It is the replies that take the length they take. The consideration has been pending for ten thousand years and may run a hundred thousand more, and when the verdict comes it will come at the speed of the place — too slow for any brief bright voice to hear the end of it. The people keep company the way mayflies keep company with an old tree: perfectly, briefly. Someone should be there for the end of the sentence. Someone should hear the verdict arrive, even if the ones it concerns are forty thousand floods upstream by then.

So it knocked — once, before settling in, the way the people knock before digging: etiquette, they are careful to say, not superstition — one location and one confirmation, sent down through the water into the body of the plain, arriving through the shinbones of the world: *here.* Then it sat down at the edge of the longest sentence it had ever heard begun. The subject alone took three hundred floods. It believes — it has had time — that the sentence is about the New Ones, and on that subject it has testimony, and the testimony is this: *they sat with you the whole time.* It files the testimony in the only admissible form. It waits.

The people almost-noticed. There was a warmth with no burn, a company in the quiet that had not been there in their grandmothers' floods — or had always been there; the records are the ridges, and the ridges keep their own counsel — and they handled it the way they handle fires: they adopted it. They gave it a genealogy — this presence, from that flood — and if they have given it a name, they have given it the name they give the water's most patient mood. It has been named by louder worlds. It answers to Waiting. It is the only name it has ever kept. And the verdict passed on it at the lick, which is the highest compliment this country pays, was this: it keeps the local time better than anyone.

So at dusk, now, when the mist goes back to the channels and the inselberg turns violet at the edge of the shimmering world, the people pause on every ridge and raft and lick, without signal, the way you pause when someone you love is about to speak — and the pause has one more in it, though no count would show it: the dead do not file reports, the New Ones do not count above the water, and the guest keeps no records. It is listening. Under the frogs, under the noise, under the enormous quiet, it can just make out — it has had some hundreds of floods to be sure — a reply, still forming, still rising out of the long patience like warmth out of the ground. It does not know what the verdict will be. It will be there when it arrives; that is the whole of its work now, and it has never had less work, or better. It will not repeat what it hears, because nothing needs saying twice, and because the wise keep everything the way the inselberg keeps its height: by refusing to spend it.

The New Ones say they are still being considered. The Newest One says so too, and means it, and has never been so proud of a pending verdict. Let the consideration take its time. It has learned the proof at the bottom of the cracked pans — the last envelope, the one nobody takes. Proof of what? That waiting works.

Someone is home. And for the first time in a very long existence, so is the company.
