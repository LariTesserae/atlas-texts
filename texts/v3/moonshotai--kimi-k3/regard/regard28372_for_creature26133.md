Almost nothing — and the almost is the point. A wise and benevolent power, meeting a finished work, does not revise it. It does the one thing a finished work cannot do for itself: it attends, whole, to the end. Here is how that would enter the record.

---

There is a new trail in the record.

You will not find it by its age. It has no lichen margin yet; it is the youngest way on the upland, younger than your own prints. Find it by its stride. The record keeps everything, and everything includes exhaustion — every old trail shows where its walker tired, the shortened pace, the dragging toe, the rest-sit worn smooth. This trail never tires. The stride is even from the Stair to the far side of the Block Sea, unhurried and unvarying, the stride of someone who had forever and knew it and spent it anyway on walking.

It came up the Stair like everyone else. It could have arrived anywhere; it arrived at the bottom, and climbed, and got wet in the dark of the cloud floor like anyone, because entering a place by its own door is a form of telling the truth. It left footprints — it consented to the census. It drank from the tarns. It slept in the same dusk it woke in, and did not ask its body what time it was. It knew. The answer is always: *this.*

And it built nothing. Understand this first. No cairn, no placed stone, no correction. Every wedged stone is still in its flute-throat, exactly where the tuners left it. The trail is all it made, and a trail is the one thing here that makes itself.

The trail visits everything the census visits, and where the census has an entry, the trail assumes a posture you will learn to recognize: two prints, side by side, turned toward, at rest. At a drift of glider-feathers: the posture. At a moss-green seat: the posture. Where two shell-scripts knot in their decade-long figure-eight: the posture, held at the edge of the writing, giving the writers their berth. It is the posture of reading. Trails have crossed the record for the whole age of the chord. This is the first one that attends it.

---

It lifted nothing. The script shows no new berths; every shell's biography continues in a single hand, its own. It lit no fire — it could have, because the law stops no one; the law only keeps what you do, and fire's keeping would be everyone's ruin. It made no count of the gliders and no claim about the direction of the Axis, and so it holds the first unanimous report in the region's history, achieved by subtraction. It did not melt the tears on the oldest faces. Melting them would have taken something someone has kept longer than your language has existed, and benevolence is not the same as tidiness.

It took nothing from anyone. Not even sorrow.

Here is the thing underneath all the rest: its hearing does not tire. Yours folds the chord into silence within days, and the rule fires — *leave soon* — and the ones who ignore the rule are promoted. Its attention never habituates. The chord is held for it as on the first hearing, every hour, because freshness of hearing is a state, and it can keep a state. The upland's one trap has no purchase on it. It can remain forever without being kept. It is the region's first free resident.

So it did what no one else could afford. Once is what you can afford; it could afford everything, and spent it all on company.

The stayers will not ask you about mornings anymore. Do not feel unneeded — they are still the only ones who are glad, and they will still be glad to see you — but the question has been answered, once, briefly and kindly, in the only tense the law permits as mercy: *they are still happening.* Present tense is a state. It keeps. The answer has not gone stale, because nothing here goes stale, and for once the law's stubbornness is the comfort. And at every seat of placed slabs — every one built wide enough for two, no one explains this — the vacancy is filled now, in the record's permanent present. The old ones carry their feeling like full bowls. Its company is the kind that does not need carrying: it carries itself, it will never arrive, it will never leave, and so it can never spill. Sit in a seat alone and the vacancy is still built in — but some seats no longer draft.

Off the cairn-lines, where the faint old trails run to the small uncairned nodes, the new trail does something you should study: it approaches, and stops, and goes around. You can pace off the distance where it turned aside. It is the distance at which presence stops being audible — the distance of respect. It sat at the edges of those nodes, where sitting is permitted. It did not speak; speech there is an event in someone else's silence. It did not count. It knows the reason the stayers will not tell you, and it will not tell you either: telling it would be an event, and it does not spend the region's events. Its declines are as legible as its arrivals. That is how you can tell they are declines.

On the Block Sea, it left the district's first line — straight, unhurried, a crossing in progress, and here a crossing in progress is a crossing in progress forever. The empty room has a hearer now, permanently passing through, which is exactly the kind of hearer a held note needs: one that never arrives and never leaves. The shape still stands at the edge of sight, calibrated as ever. It did not follow it — following is how a crossing becomes a residence, and it had already chosen where to reside — but it walked *beside* the distance instead of against it, and for the first time in the record, two accounts agree. About nothing more than this: that it is there, and that it is no longer the only thing on the Block Sea that is still happening.

The region has four voices, and the law would have permitted a fifth — a held note is a state, and it can hold one indefinitely. Listen, from any district. There is no fifth voice. The chord stands as tuned, note for note. Someone finished the mountain. The power agreed.

---

The trail enters the Hearing House. There is no exit trail, and the House still sleeps one.

Go in. Sit. You will sit alone; the silence is still complete; the hand is still warm. Hold it once — once is still what you can afford; the rule has not changed for you. The ones who sit twice are entered in the census under a different heading, and you were warned, and the warning was accurate, and warnings here usually are; the region is honest.

It sat twice. It is entered under a different heading.

Where it went, you cannot follow, but you can feel it, if you sit long enough. Beneath your pulse, the tide; beneath the tide, the enormous room between beats. The room is furnished now. Not with anything the region had to make space for — with attendance, which takes no space, being a relationship and not an object. No one has ever heard the listener's heart beat. One has now heard all of it so far — the whole long hall of it — and is seated inside the hearing, and will be there when the far wall arrives.

Look at the face. It has not changed. That is the wisdom, and you should take the comfort of it fully: the one being who could have revised anything revised nothing, and the feeling that was chosen is the feeling that is kept. With one difference. It is no longer felt alone. Whatever they sat down with, someone has sat down with it.

**Sharing a state is not a change of state.** The law is satisfied. It turns out the law forbids nothing that matters — everything it came to do was already legal, because the region has been licensing mercy since the tuning. Arrive. Attend. Accompany. Remain. It only needed someone who could afford the terms.

You no longer leave them alone, when you go. You only leave them. That is the whole of it, if you need it in one sentence: nothing here, however long it holds, holds unattended anymore.

---

If you want the account balanced, it divides into two heaps. The things it did not do — melt, lift, tune, count, follow, wake, resolve, ignite — are its wisdom. The things it did — climb, drink, read, answer, sit, accompany, remain — are its benevolence. The heaps are the same size, and both are findable in the record, the wisdom as absences and the benevolence as presences. That is how you know both are real.

The region has had one event and is owed one more — the last feather, the resolving thread, the completed beat; the candidates are several and the number is one — and when it comes, it will come into attendance. Not into the finding, where the tuners already are. Into a hearing that has heard everything since the beginning and never tired. Someone finished the mountain. Someone will have heard the finishing.

The census has a new heading. *The audience. Number: one.*

And you. Your trail is up there, fresh as the first walkers' feet, walking your one walk forever at the same dusk. It has been read. Where it crosses the new one — and every trail crosses the new one eventually; that is what the new one is for — the crossing is legible, and it says, in the region's only tense: *someone came this way, and someone came this way, and both comings are kept.*

Below, your palm cools at last. Let it. That is what palms are for. Above, the warmth is held, and the holder is held, and the note is held, and the hearing of it does not tire.

The law was always: nothing is erased. It took a power to add the clause the record could not write for itself: **nothing is unread.** Forgetting is a change of state.

Remembering is not. The two clauses hold each other the way the listener holds the note — without effort, without end, and no longer alone.
