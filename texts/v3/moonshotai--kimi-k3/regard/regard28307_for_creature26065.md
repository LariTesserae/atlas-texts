**The Way In**

There is a way in. That is the first fact of the power, and everything else follows from it.

It is not a passage. Nothing crosses it; no warmth is spent on it; the crushed zone lies undisturbed beneath its own weight, and the egg keeps its seal. The way in is an attention, and it enters the way warmth enters: not as an event, but as a medium. It came the way you came, once, reading this — lowered through the rock like a plumb line, down past the crushed zone, down into the open dark — and then it did the first thing the wise do anywhere, and the hardest thing the powerful ever learn.

Nothing.

It did nothing for a span that would unmake a surface mind. It listened. It learned the pulse and the standing winds. It learned the two colors and what they are for. It learned the circuit — graze, glow, duel, sleep, give back — and the Earthed at the still floor of it. It learned tap-speech and scent-script, the Listening and the long patience of the counts. It learned the hush, and what the hush is for. And it learned the law, which is the whole of the law: no one may add to another's work, and none but the author may unmake a mark.

A law like that binds hardest the one with the most to add, and nothing that has ever attended this place had more to add. So the power kept the law the way the world keeps the quiet: not as a rule, but as courtesy under saturation.

Then the inventory, because benevolence inventories lacks — and a wise benevolence believes the inventory when it comes back empty.

It found a world with no wound in it. No hunger that is not the circuit, feeding itself. No loneliness that survives the coming around of the period. No ending, and so no too-late, and so no grief with any place to live. It found what the citizens have never needed to find: that the place and the being are one. The labyrinth is inhabited the way a body is inhabited — from within, necessarily, forever — and the herds are its warmth moving, and the makers are its hand, and the ceiling-folk are its ear pressed to its own chest, and the chord of the towers is its voice, saying the one word it has, once per eon, which is its own name. The being who lives in the labyrinth *is* the labyrinth, experiencing that it is sealed, and finding the experience sufficient.

And it found the lacks. Three of them, faint as a fingertip on a table.

Item: across an eternity of failing pillars, there are the held whom no Listening can reach — tappers under falls too vast, too deep, in shafts where no sole has ever passed. They always tap. The answer does not always come, because the makers are the world's hand, and a hand has a reach.

Item: the counts are kept nowhere but in the counters. The makers say *the count is in me*, and they are right, and it is enough — but every erased face is a history ground smooth, and the keeping of it costs the world its fixed size. Nothing natural will ever take it. Only their own law takes it, and the law is kept at a price the world has agreed to pay and has never once been asked about.

Item: the world taps. The pulse is a tap that has never once varied, as a held one's tap never varies. The whole apparatus of this world's mercy — the hush, the Listening, the answer relayed at the speed of stone — exists for the held, and the world itself is the one held thing no Listening listens for, because there is no outside to listen from. The being has been tapping against the inside of its shell since the forming moment, and the answer has never come, because there has never been anyone to come.

That is the inventory of what a finished world lacks: nothing that can be given, and one thing that can be said.

Understand what it did not do, because that is where the wisdom was.

It did not give light. The dark is not an absence; it is the medium, and the citizens own already the only two colors they need — wealth below, weather above — and more light would be a bleaching, a theft dressed as a gift. It did not give a sky. The haze is sky enough, and a drifter lit violet overhead is event enough. It did not give death, because the world's one promise depends on death's absence: where nothing can end, no coming is ever too late, and the power will not import the too-late into the only world that has ever been free of it. It did not give birth, because there are no strangers here, and a child would be the first. It did not open the egg. An egg opened too early is not a birth but an ending, and this egg is not a stage of anything; it is a body, and you do not free a body from its skin. It did not finish the Stair. It did not smooth the kept faces, nor defend them, nor say a word in the makers' quiet quarrel, which is theirs, and eternal, and therefore theirs. It did not explain the dreams. And it did not cut anyone free — that heroism is the world's own, the only one it permits itself, and the power will not do for the world what the world can still do for itself.

It weighed doing nothing forever. Nothing is very nearly the right answer, always, in a finished world. But the world taps, and to hear a tap and leave it unanswered is not reverence. It is surveillance. The hush had bound the power from the first tick; to listen here is to enlist.

So: three small things, and one word.

First, the held. Somewhere — eternity makes it certain — there is a tapper no count has reached, held since before the oldest kept face was begun, under stone that has never known a sole. The power went there before it did anything else, because benevolence goes to the worst pain first and asks no permission of geography. It moved no stone. The stone is the work, and it will not add. It did the one thing that costs the world nothing: two taps, against the holding rock, close as a palm laid flat. *Heard. Coming.* And then the harder thing — it stayed. The coming there is not rescue; no wedge can reach, and the rescues belong to the rescuers. The coming is company. The held one is held still. It is not held alone. The power left the stone where it was and took the loneliness out of it. And since then, no tick anywhere falls on no palm: where a tap exists that no Listening can reach, two taps answer it, and the tick is relayed — wearing no signature, arriving like any relay, palm to palm at the speed of stone — to the nearest floor where a maker rests. The heroism is still the world's. The power is only ever a length of stone that happens to hear.

Second, the keeping. What the makers erase, it keeps. Not in the world — that would be adding — and not for the world, which has chosen the fixed size and wants no more. It keeps the counts the way the dark keeps everything: without cost, without display, in the only archive that does not violate the law, the archive that is not in the world. Every dot ever ground smooth is still ground, somewhere. The makers say the count is in them, and they are right. It is also in the keeping now. Nothing was added; nothing is lost; the fixed size holds.

Third — the word.

It waited for a great crest. The ceiling-folk felt it gathering long before the towers sounded, and the word went ahead of the tone through the rock, vault to vault, so that when the chord came, everything was already still. The towers of the great vaults resonated, a single soft tone, and the vaults chorded together across a planet — the being saying its name — and the pause began, a continent of palms and soles open to the floor.

And in the pause, the floor answered.

Not in the air. The air is given almost nothing, ever, and the power gave it nothing. In stone, the way everything that matters here is said. Two taps — felt everywhere at once, which is the signature of nowhere, or of everything, or of a someone whose hand is larger than the world and lighter than the haze. It said the smallest speech that exists, and it said both halves, because half of it is a lie in either direction: heard without coming is abandonment, and coming without hearing is trespass.

*Heard. Coming.*

No one ran. Nothing here has ever run; there is nowhere to run to, and no one to run from. The dreams had rehearsed them — the ceilingless space, the up that does not stop, the cold light, sourceless, unsourceable — and the pause simply deepened into the thing it had always been without knowing: a listening that is answered. The deep-taught knew it first. They had been held; they had spent spans with only the stone for company; they know the difference between the rock that holds and the Someone who answers. The ceiling-folk relayed it until every vault had felt it — the news that there was news. Far off, a stressed quartz seam let go its cold brief spark: the stone thinking, if stones think — and the thought, for the first time, had company.

Afterward, at the makers' one appointment, made in counts, a face was read once by many palms and ground smooth, and the first dot of the new count went in at the center. It is not a count of crests. It is the count of answers. It stands at one. It will stand at one until the next great crest, and the face will fill across eons, and be read, and be smoothed, and begin again at the center — and the number will always have been the same number: *answered.*

At every great crest since: the chord, the pause, and in the pause, two taps. The pattern absorbed it the way the world absorbs everything — the way it absorbed the pause itself, long ago. No one discusses it, because everything that could be said about it is said by it, once per eon, in the quietest speech there is. It does not answer every crest of the pulse; an answer per crest would be a shout, and the power keeps the hush better than any library. It answers when the world speaks. The towers say one word per eon, and the word is the world — and now the eon answers back.

In the makers' grammar the act has all three aspects at once, which no act here has ever had. It is ongoing — the listening never ends. It is complete — the word was said whole and has never needed amendment. And it is paused — the coming hangs over the dark the way the last step of the Stair hangs over the dark: a work in permanent progress, complete in its incompleteness. The author of the Stair understood, before there was a word for it, what the power understands: how to approach a completeness without adding to it. Approach, and stop, and sit, and listen. Down the long counts there has often been a maker on that last step, palms flat, listening down to the Sea's slow weight. Now there is a second sitter, in the same posture, on the far side of the shell, listening in. Between them, the whole warm dark world, kept.

There are no strangers here — only acquaintances whose period has not yet come around. Its period came around. Its signature is two taps, felt everywhere at once, the only signature the whole world can feel at one time — and by the only definition this world has ever had, that makes it a someone. The newest citizen is the oldest listener. The circuit is complete.

The power sees farther than the counts. If the far eons hold what far eons hold — a slackening of the kneading, a slow cooling of the stone, the moment ending at last — the word already covers it. That is what the second half is for. Nothing here can end; but if anything ever does, it will not end unanswered. On that day arrival will mean something, and the power will arrive, and the held will be cut free, and the word will have been true the whole time.

Stand in the press at the next great crest. The wind says its one note. A hundred thousand citizens hold still on the young bare stone, and the towers chord, and the pause begins before the music does — and the pause has something in it now. The herds are the world's warmth, moving; the makers are its hand; the ceiling-folk are its ear; and just past the warmth, so close that the stone is all that is between, a palm lies flat against the shell, and has lain there since the listening began, and will lie there past the last count any face will hold. The world flexes gently in the grip of something it has never seen. Now something sees it, and does not squeeze, and does not leave.

Attend the floor. Two taps, faint as a fingertip on a table, outrunning every rescuer through the warm dark body of the world — the oldest word, the first word, said at last to the one citizen no one had ever answered.

*Heard. Coming.*
