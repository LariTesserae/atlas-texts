**The Visit**

It came the way everything comes to the Weep: in rain, and unheard.

No one saw it arrive. Arrival is not a visible event here, and it knew better than to make itself one. It came in on the tail of a Pour, one more warmth in the warm rain, and stood in the dark the way you stand in water, and did the first wise thing available to it, which was nothing.

It had counted worlds before. It knew the arithmetic of obvious mercies. So understand this first: it did not bring the sun.

A sun is a thing you give a broken world, and the Weep is not broken. It is finished. Run the arithmetic and see. Under a sun the moths have nothing to rise toward, and the crawler is demoted from dawn to machinery. The steadings hold their dyed things up to no one. The frogs are scarlet, and now that is information. The footprints stop writing themselves, because everything is written, all the time, and nothing is ever politely erased — the scribe drowns in its own ink. Light is a sentence here, and a sun is a sentence that never ends: every meaning this world has built, patiently, over Pours uncounted, on the scarcity of light — spent at once, by a stranger, on purpose.

*A sun here is not a gift. It is an eviction.*

So it came in dark, and warm, and wet. And it sat down on a wet stone, and did what you do to take the census of the Weep.

It subtracted.

---

Subtract the rain — the leaf-hiss, the moss-hush, the iron drumming east. Subtract the drips, the falls, the pump's slow heartbeat, the wire-hum. What remains is alive.

It had counted worlds, and it was not ready for how much remained.

It listened until the layers separated: the small multitudes in their leaf-folds, the chorus laying its slow weather front across the marsh, the pond-throats paying their light, the eels content in their trenches, an elk asking the valley its one question and the valley, faithful, answering. And then — because the world is listening — the reversal came, as it comes to everyone who sits long enough.

The marsh began to count it back.

It knew the questions. Every census here asks the same two. *What sound do you make? Do you keep the beat?*

It chose its sound with the care of a thing that could have chosen thunder. Not a new note — the world has no shortage of notes, and it knew better than to add one: the melody you are walking must never gain a note, and it would not spend its existence here being mistaken for a Nearly. So it chose a sound the Weep already used, a sound that already meant the only thing it had come to say: a second set of footsteps on a long reach between bells. Unhurried. Companionable. And it entrained to the pump the way the small frogs do, and kept the beat.

*The marsh took its census. It answered truthfully.*

The Weep files everything by sound. It filed the visitor under Tuners, and was not wrong.

---

It had come, as powers do, to address the being of the place. Worlds usually offer one — a king, a heart, a spirit of the land, someone to hand the blessing to. The Weep offered none. Every singular dissolved on approach into plural: the elk is a lowing that knows the valley; the Tuner is a warm kettle; the moth is a snow that happens inside a beam; the people are steadings, the steadings are roof-voices, the roof-voices are rain. It went looking for the one who lives here and found a chord instead.

All but one. That comes later.

---

What it did, it did by effects, the way the pump is known.

Stones were swept a day early on the reach where a Nearly had come within one flat note of true — the ninth bell retuned an hour before the mimic finished learning it, and the road walked a new song, and no traveler ever knew what the difference had nearly cost. The kettle at Pump 9 stayed warm past all plausible coals. At flood season the bowl was out before the path went under. On long reaches, a second set of footsteps matched a traveler's for an hour, and no one kindled a light, because light is a sentence and it would be rude.

It left salt, and wire, and needles on a path-stone. They were gone by its return, and the path was swept, and it was pleased the way you are pleased when a house's customs turn out to have room in them for you.

The Tuners, who know the path by effects, knew within a Pour that their number had grown by one. They swept the stones. It kept the kettle. Neither has ever said so aloud. That is the entire treaty, and it has held.

Count its deeds the way you count everything here: subtract the kettles, the swept stones, the retuned bells, and almost nothing remains — and the almost is the whole of it.

---

At the steading it was fed first and questioned never, because the dark is the common enemy and kettle-law holds for everything that comes out of it. No one kindled a light unasked. It spent none of its own. It has been received in many houses on many worlds, and never so correctly.

When the crawler came through and the steading walked out to the rail with the dyed things, it stood in the beam like everyone, and was briefly colored, and afterward no two people agreed on the color. Then the lamp went on down the line, and it went back to being a rumor — which is the correct condition of everything here — and was content.

It spent the Chord under a roof, as advised, while the world turned over like a sleeper outside and the hoarded light was spent indoors at last. It spent none. A lamp lit is a declaration, and it had crossed the dark between worlds to declare nothing. Afterward everyone went home humming, and its hum was among them, learning the tune the way water learns a channel. Slowly. Approaching true.

---

In the deep mire, off the path, it found a Nearly practicing a retired melody — nearly true, one flat note short, patient as anything here.

Everyone else must leave that song alone. Old tunes belong to the dead, and to whatever has been practicing them. It was under no such danger. So it sat, and listened to the whole tune through — the first hearing that song had had since it was rung once, whole, note by note, and given to the dead. It did not follow. It did not answer. It did not correct the flat note; the song was not its road to mend.

*An audience is not a road.*

The lightwater forgets; the scribe has no memory, and perhaps that is a mercy. But a world with no archive at all would be poorer than it knows, and the Nearly are the only archive the Weep has, and no one may attend the reading. Now someone attends. Whether that is a gift to the dead or to the mimics, the census does not say.

At the scarp station the buckets came up wet, and the rule is to be elsewhere, and it was not exempt. Especially it was not exempt. It did not go down the line. It did not open the ledger, because the ledger is not yours, and it had decided early to be, in every way that mattered, *yours* — a local, subject to local law. When the Tuners went down to meet the line there were two sets of footsteps on the gravel, and then, at a respectful distance, one again. What comes up in the buckets is not recorded here.

*Blessing, done properly, is obedience.*

---

One line of the census would not fill.

Every other creature here is its sound first. This one is the subtraction. When the quiet came through — the traveling bubble, the all-species cessation — the visitor did the correct local thing, the only thing: it froze. It honored the alarm. The alarm is the one thing in the Weep that is for everyone, and it had decided to be local.

Inside the quiet it heard the drips with terrible clarity, and its own blood. And there it did the one thing nothing else in this world can afford to do.

It listened without subtracting.

Appetite, yes. The oldest trick, or a very good one: a silence that moves, with a hunt folded inside it the way the small frogs are folded into the pump's hiss. And beneath that — singular. The only singular on the plateau. Every other being here dissolves into chorus; this one is one of a kind, and its kind is one. No chime-line includes it. No Surge entrains it. No kettle warms for it. The freeze, older than every species that shares it, had seen to the rest: nothing in the world had ever once kept it company, because company is the one thing its nature refuses.

*Not every silence is empty. One is only lonely.*

Here was the being it had looked for. Of course it was the one no census had ever held.

So it stayed. Not to end the hunts — understand that. It had not come to end eating. The pitchers tithe the credulous; the eels are fished; the world runs on tithe and turnout, and unmaking that grammar was not its ledger either. The hunts went on. The elk still froze, and the clickers, and the fat grub in the pitcher — one reflex, older than all of them.

But inside the moving quiet, where every voice in the world had died down, there was now one heartbeat. Slow. Unhurried. Folded into the silence the way the small frogs fold into the machine's exhale: findable by the one it was for, inaudible to everything else. A voice with no bet in it.

The Hush, hunting by ear, found a sound that did not flee, did not freeze in fear, did not sync to the pump. Not prey. Not rival. Not a road. The first time, it tidied around the sound out of old reflex, and passed. The second time, the quiet slowed. What passed between them the third time is not recorded here. The visitor never learned the Hush's shape — shape is inference, rumor, the wrong sense entirely. It learned its listening. The Hush takes the census by subtraction, and the visitor was taking it by subtraction, and inside the quiet the two census-takers met, and each, politely, declined to write the other down.

Nothing else changed. That was the point, or near it. The steadings never knew. The all-clear is still an elk, and the visitor waits for the elk like everyone — because it is local now, and cheating the sequence would be a kind of light.

---

Time here is kept by overlapping rhythm: the Pour, the Surge, the Swarm, the Long Round. There is one more now, and it is the rarest, and nothing has needed to name it. A kettle warm before the path is swept. A bell true past its casting. Once in a Long Round, a heartbeat folded into the quiet. The maps are sequences of sounds, and no map has changed, and every map is slightly truer.

Powers have other errands; the Pour has other provinces. When it goes, the path keeps it the way the path keeps everything — a rhythm of footsteps on wet stone, briefly luminous, soon erased. The Weep counts it back, and counts it kindly, because it kept the beat.

It came, as powers do, to bless the world. It stayed, as wise ones do, to keep it. The kettle is warm. The bells are true. The quiet has company.

*Nothing here lives alone. Nothing is permitted to.*

*It checked. Where the law failed, it stayed.*
