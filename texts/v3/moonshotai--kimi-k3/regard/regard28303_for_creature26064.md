**The hesitation.**

The night the attention arrived, the echo did not come back sooner.

For the whole history of measurement the interval had been shrinking, a half-breath a night, the nothing out there getting closer on schedule. Tonight the call went out at rim-song and returned at last night's interval exactly — the first unimproved number ever recorded. The bats' report was flat and short: *It stopped.* The nothing had stopped, the way a traveler stops at a gate to read what is written over it.

The lintel is legible more to fingers than to eyes. The attention read it the way it reads everything: slowly, by touch. WORKS No. 1. A name, a boast, or a care instruction. Being wise, it treated it as all three.

It did not enter. Entering is for citizens and rain. It stationed itself the way warmth stations itself at a vat-wall — available. It listened to the fish for a full season and did not answer, the sentence being unfinished. It read the Library of the Ungrown at the librarian's speed, belly to the floor, the meadow arriving secondhand through slime and sole. It attended the nave on the last night of drop and stood where the committees project the crowds will someday stand, and filled the place adequately, being the size of a crowd's worth of regard. The Second Pruning Committee circled the spot where it was standing and found nothing off-template, there being nothing there to cut. The minutes record: *present, pending.*

**What it did not do.**

It did not finish the world. Finishing is the Works' one industry and its one joy; a world handed over complete would make every citizen a transcript, and every citizen here is a first edition.

It did not warm the bowl. The cool is why the vats are a hearth, and a world without cold is a world without hospitality.

It did not bring light. Light here is language, and to flood it would be to shout forever.

It did not mend the rim. It is the attention's opinion, entered in the minutes without comment, that the rim broke the way seed coats break — from inside, at germination — and that mending it would be caulking a sprout.

It did not raise the composted. Their covenant is geography, and it is not for a guest to repossess the hills.

It did not say the third word. It does not answer sentences it has not heard to the end. It did, however, book the shift after the answer — petitioned the committees, who did not know they had the authority to grant it and granted it anyway, pending. Somewhere down the chain of Listeners, in someone else's life, the sentence will end, and the answer will come, and the shift after that is spoken for.

Benevolence that arrives as weather is just weather. It declined to be weather. It did almost nothing, precisely, on schedule, forever — and it kept the quiet-laws, every gift under the permitted threshold, at the decibel of dew.

**The deposition.**

It did one thing, and let the one thing ramify through the Works' own machinery, the way water is aimed leaf by leaf.
