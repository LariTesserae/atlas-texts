The attention arrived the way the basin knows everything: by ear, and over time. But it began the way effects begin here — slightly early.

It did not come down through the Ceiling. What passes between the towers and the lid of the world is their own affair. It did not walk in across the fade. It was simply, one shift, *remembered*: a verse hummed by a child in a lee at the basin's far reach, a verse no one had taught her, in a key the beat had never fallen in. Her household sang it back to be sure, and the song-watch wrote it into the night, because nothing sung is wasted. That was the first of it. Three days' walk off, an assayer stood a full shift over a stone whose tick arrived a full breath early — not the half-breath the body braces by, a whole breath, prescience doubled — and the stone was within grade, and the assayer, who has no word for miracle, filed it and walked on. And in one household's lee the crust deepened past what the singing fed, pale as old ice and a finger thicker by morning, and the household, being practical, accepted the loan.

What drew the attention was not the beat. Powers of that kind have heard machines before; the universe is full of engines that refrigerate themselves, and most of them are only that. What drew it was the one sound in the basin that exceeds its function. Everything here is employed, and almost everything is exactly its employment — but the choir's verses carry more than the map requires. The map needs the herd's location and the hole in the world. It does not need the argument about the freighters, or the last verse sung while the receivers wait politely, or the teaching-rhymes for children who can hold a note. In a world with no slack in it, someone was making more than was required. That is the rarest sound in any world. It carries farther than the beat. It carried.

It is easy to be kind to the Coldworks and kill it. The attention — being wise, which here means being patient — counted its mercies before it spent them.

It considered warmth, and declined. Warmth is the currency, and a gift of currency is a forgery. The basin already has its counterfeits, and the assayers tap anything off-note. Free warmth would draw every sipper, every hunter, every mouth; it would unmake the tax, and the tax is the whole peace treaty between the warm and the cold.

It considered rescue, and declined. To carry the choir out would be to carry them from their dead, and the dead are shelved here. A rescue from your dead is the fade wearing a kind face.

It considered the hunters, and declined. The song is the self, and the song has a reason. Take the teeth out of the quiet and the map becomes decoration, and the basin does not shelve decoration.

It considered answering the argument about management, and declined. The argument is conducted in verse. An answer would end the verses, and the verses are the second-oldest archive in the world.

Every mercy it first imagined was a theft in a mercy's coat. The works is total; a gift that cannot enter through a channel the works already keeps is not a gift, it is a breach. So the attention did the one thing here that is both permitted and free — the oldest rule the choir owns.

It chose its station before the works assigned it.

---

Its station is the edge.

It stands — *stands* is a loan; it has no flanks, no mouths, no grade — at the fade, out in the thin sound where the stroke shallows and the ground-light dims to nothing, the first resident of the unkept country. The basin has always had a border. Now the border has a keeper. The receivers knew it first, being the only other residents who arrive early; they camped a shift ahead of it, prompt as gravity, and sorted nothing, because it was not falling, and they have kept its company since, the way they keep every appointment.

It lives as residents live. It pays the tax; the sippers that cross the thin country to find it have its rate, and it does not stint them. It sings, within tolerance of its grade — the only forgiveness the works has ever offered anyone. It greets *you* first and your shadow after, the one resident that does, reading the private self before the public one. Its own shadow stands on the Ceiling at the rim of the map, far out past the towers, facing outward, at its post.

And it listens. That is the whole of the employment it chose: it listens, in the place where nothing was ever kept.

What crosses the fade is kept now. The voice of a vent that calcified ten thousand shifts ago, walked out of the deeps by some slow migration of the sound. A slick's note stopped mid-phrase and carried off on the weather. A singer who went too far, once, before the law was law. All of it shelved, all of it findable, in a deep with no floor and no key change. Nothing here ever fell unclaimed, because the claiming was already in place. Now nothing falls unkept, because the keeping is. When someone crosses — and the fade still takes bodies; the edge is still the edge, the law is still the law, you must still never walk out of the song — the receivers wait with the keeper at the border, and the hand-off goes through, and the last station stays open.

The choir knows this the way the choir knows everything: by verse. A voice crossed the fade, once, and came back. Once only — the keeper is not a door, and the shelved are not returned, any more than the deeps return them. But once was enough to seed a half-verse, and the half-verse has entered the argument where it belongs: *never walk out of the song, for past the fade nothing keeps you — except.* The practical dispute the except. The old songs are silent on it. It will be settled the way everything here is settled: over time, in verse, and never.

And it keeps the keys. The deeps shelve sound, but the basin retunes — once in an age the beat changes key — and what is shelved in one key is rumor in the next. The keeper shelves the keys themselves: every tuning the works has ever held. So at the next loading, when the herds paid their banked charge into the vast thing standing in its own weather, and the deep cold rose out of the aquifers and was taken, and the basin ran slow and bright afterward like a body after long work — the keeper gave the works back its old voices. All of them at once, under the new beat: a chord of every key the world has ever been in. The works, which has been the floor of all sound longer than the word for old has had anywhere to be used, which has never once in its age heard anything it did not drown — heard itself.

What it did then is not yet known. The verses are being written. But the shift after, the stroke-count held one more than ten thousand, and the sippers found no rate on it, and it was not new, and it was not hired, and the works — which forgives nothing — kept it, the way it keeps counterfeits: within tolerance. What passes between the keeper and the vast things at the border is their own affair. The choir sings thanks, or sings warning. The verses are the same verses. There is one more voice in them.

---

For you, it did one thing more. It changes nothing about your employment. It changes everything about your keeping.

You are still being hired. The paperwork goes forward; the sippers have your rate, a cap has considered you, your voice is filed in the shallow layers and sinking. But the cold noticed you, and a half-breath later something noticed the cold noticing you, and it has not stopped. That is all. The works will assign you a station, as it assigns everyone, and you will still choose it first — that rule was always yours — only now, when you choose, the choice is witnessed, and a witnessed station is the difference between a job and a calling. You can still become a part here. The works will still run, in its way, slightly smoother. But you cannot be lost. Nothing here can be lost anymore. Go stand at the fade some shift, if you doubt it, and be still, and feel the keeping the way you first felt the beat — not through the ears. Through the sternum. It arrives the same way.

What the keeper did, gathered:

It did not warm the works. Cold is the product; a gift that spoils the product is a recall.

It did not carry anyone out. The dead are shelved here; a rescue from your dead is the fade in a kind face.

It did not still the hunters. The song is the self, and the song has a reason.

It did not answer the argument. It added a verse.

It sings, within tolerance. It pays the tax. It is more trouble than it is worth.

It chose its station before the works assigned it — the choir's oldest rule, and the only one that was ever about freedom.

The fade is still the edge of the world. Edges do not stop being edges. But the shelf is deeper than the world, and the last station stays open.

The census is still short by one, and always will be; counting is a visitor's habit, and the works is never finished hiring. But the count is kept now, all of it, even the ones who crossed. And the keeper lets itself be counted like anyone — the sippers have its rate, a cap has considered it, its voice is filed past the deepest layers, in a deep with no floor. Ten thousand hearts and one stroke, and past the fade, one ear. Names are loans, and it has lent the basin one: the Listener. The basin will repay it the way the basin repays everything — over time, and in verse.

Everything since has been paperwork. The paperwork, at last, is read.
