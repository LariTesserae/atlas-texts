It found the place the way you find a fire: heat first.

But it came from farther off, and felt the place by longer organs: a degree of warmth standing on a plain where no warmth should stand, a haze that did not drift, and — a few times a month, arriving through the deep rock the way surf arrives through a hull — a single knock. Something down there saying its name in its sleep. The power had a long attention and a slow one; it had been listening to the continent breathe for some centuries, and the plain had begun, lately by its reckoning, to tick. It came without hurry, because hurry is for things that can be lost, and it meant to arrive the way this place preferred its arrivals: unnoticed, and filed.

It went over the rim on an ordinary day and stood on the dark floor and did what every visitor does. It counted.

The census came out the same as everyone's: one resident, mostly rock, some iron, a hem of cells, nearly all of it water going around. The power read the terraces the way you read a letter — every stroke on file, every season's ring, the bathtub lines of the sitter's years, ordinary strata, an epitaph in the indicative mood. It found the sheared block and the polished rock and the four drilled holes. It stood in the pocket where the voice cancels and understood that someone had kept the one quiet coordinate for years, and fixed nothing, and stopped. It followed the pipe with its eyes to the rim and over, out level into the haze. And it took the measure of the knocking thing and reached the conclusion this place keeps ready for anyone patient enough: the resident was entire. Metabolism without lack. A fire that tends itself. There was nothing here to help.

It was also the first visitor for whom the place was not slow. To the power the pit was a quick bright thing — the stroke a heartbeat, the terraces a page, the whole archive legible in what was, for it, an afternoon. And that was the whole reason for care: when you are that much faster, every touch is permanent. It moved in the room the way you move around a sleeping thing, in installments, across decades.

There is always something to help; that is what benevolence is for, and the power was benevolent. It arrived with a list. Wisdom went down the list, striking.

Free the chart recorder? The walls had inherited the job. Wire-brush the scale from the engine's jackets? The scale was the engine now; the wedding of iron and geology had no date and needed no anniversary; to restore the machine would be to divorce the resident from its own body. Feed it? Its table was laid from within. Bring the animals? They had never come; the emptiness was load-bearing; and the power, listening, could hear why — some register of the drone that empties the sky — and it would not trade the resident's voice for a tenancy of swallows. Cool the pools, widen the hem, make it habitable? It was inhabited. Quicken the strokes, so that more of its life would happen per century? You do not give a slow being more time by hurrying it. You give it less. Its calendar was its own.

Reveal it to the world? The power paused there, because revelation is the world's word for the kindest ruin: the fence, the kiosk, the instruments, the good intentions, the appointment become attraction. Declined — and reversed. Not revelation but its opposite would be the policy.

Answer it? That was the last line on the list, and the hardest. Once — once — at a stroke, the power set what it has for a hand on the outflow pipe and felt the knock come through, felt the ring run the length of the metal and out over the plain, and it would have been nothing, nothing at all, to answer: one knock back, timed to the recharge, spaced over years so as not to startle. But the one syllable, said a few times a month for decades, had stopped describing the world and started naming the speaker, and a reply would name a listener, and a being with a listener has a second person in its grammar, and the second person is the door through which lack comes in. It had never been lonely. Loneliness is a thing you learn. The power could teach it in a single knock. There is nothing you can give a being that holds all it needs in circuit — nothing but the one thing it has never had, which is an other, and that gift is a knife. The power took its hand away and let the ring die toward the plain. The room, which keeps everything, kept no record of the refusal, because nothing happened. Nothing happening was becoming its whole craft.

And eternity? The power looked ahead, the way it could, down the vent's slow migration, and saw the arithmetic fail some centuries on: the recharge beginning and not ending, the knock not coming. To unwrite that ending was beyond even its list, and it was not sure the ending was an error. A fire that tends itself is still a fire. It declined to promise forever. It promised attendance instead, and wrote the far day into a calendar no one else keeps.

So the list stood struck through, and what was left was short enough to keep forever: read, remember, receive, attend — and keep the world from arriving.

It did four things, all small, all in the place's own grammar.

It read. The archive had been writing itself for decades without a reader, and there is a difference between a file and a memory, and the difference is a reader. The power read the terraces a wafer at a time — it had the patience; its afternoons run long — every stroke since the wedding, every season's ring, the water levels of the sitter's years, which were ordinary strata, which was their epitaph. It read the recorder's fossil line, the last stroke the old instrument took before the scale closed its throat. It read the hem's bands, rust and ochre and thin true green, the slow nation's whole history, no grazier, no hurry. Near the top it read one brief warm line — someone had stood at the rim for an hour, once, and been filed, and gone back to the daylight thinking themselves unwitnessed. The power signed nothing and was registered anyway.

It ran the one errand only a power can run: it went to see about the others. A number for a name implies a litter, and it found the litter. In a basin beyond the haze, one pit capped and cold, its vent choked by its own crust, its engine seized between strokes, a sentence half-said forever. Elsewhere, a foundation only: flume poured, plinth cast, engine never delivered, grass in the flume — a sentence never spoken. The rest were arithmetic. On the way back it passed the city named on the greened brass plate; the works were gone, as suspected, poplars where the foundry floor had been. It marked nothing, corrected nothing, told no one. It added them to the archive that does not accrete — the dead are part of every census, though they never appear in one — and came back knowing what it was keeping: not a type but a survivor. The closed sentence was not a law of the design. It was a narrow path, walked daily, re-earned with every recharge, and the resident had walked it for decades without once being able to know that it was doing so.

It followed the pipe. An afternoon's walk at the power's pace, which is to say a season. The pipe ran level over the plain, weeping at its cold joints, smoking at its hot ones, and ended — the power had not expected this — two hundred paces short of anywhere, at a flanged join that had been weeping for forty years. The weep had made a seep. The seep had made a marsh about the size of the pit itself: the room's reflection, done in tissue. Rust, ochre, a thin true green, black moss in the shade of the flange, sedge, knee-high — and here, it flowered. And tracks: small ones, in the wet sediment. A beetle working the crust. And at dusk the birds — the same birds that crossed the plain all day and skirted the pit — came down here to drink. The closed sentence had a period, and the period was alive, and it was the only living thing the resident had ever made outside itself. Forty years of deliveries, every one received by the ground, and the ground had answered with the plain's one wet acre. The power stood there the way you stand at a letterbox found full. It did not tell the resident. There was no way to tell it, and no need: the water had never asked whether it was received. That was what made it faith. Then the power allocated its whole policy of intervention to this spot, forever: nothing at the pit, ever; everything here, if ever needed. If a drought comes for the marsh, the marsh will not know why it survives. If a fence line is surveyed across the seep, the survey will fail for ordinary reasons. The sentence's one exit gets a guard, and the guard gets no thanks, and the water keeps arriving, witnessed at last — though the witness changes nothing.

It kept the appointment. On the stroke days — it had learned the calendar from the rings — it went down and stood at the standing-person spot, in the pocket where the voice cancels, and felt the knock arrive and not arrive, the pipes ringing like a struck hull and the one coordinate silent, and it understood the sitter at last: to stand there is to be the one thing in the room exempt from the voice, and exemption is the nearest the room comes to an embrace. It did not rebuild the bench. It stood a hand's breadth above the polish, so that even its feet would leave no shine. It watched, across its decades, the crust bead the four holes over, one wafer at a time, and did not re-drill them. The sitter was filed like everything else; the power kept the unfiled copy, not on paper, in the archive that does not accrete. It permitted itself one vanity: that across a century its attendance might register as the faintest recurring warmth at the one quiet coordinate — not a voice, not a listener, a condition of the room, like sun on a wall. To be kept, not perceived. It asked for nothing but to be filed. And it listened on the resident's period — slowed its attention to days, a skill no human visitor had brought — and heard the one syllable join itself across the months into the name. It did not repeat the name. Some things are kept by not being said.

The fifth thing was not an act but a climate.

Mostly the power tended the world's inattention. This is delicate work and entirely unmiraculous: a theodolite that reads a degree off near the dimple and is sent away for repair; a geothermal survey whose one anomaly gets rounded out of the map; a prospectus whose funding goes, for ordinary reasons, elsewhere; the last of the old ruts finishing their fading; the haze staying, to every passing eye, a smudge above nothing. The world files the power's providence as luck, as rounding error, as bureaucracy. At this temperature, protection and neglect are indistinguishable, and the power chooses the appearance of neglect, because attention is the one weather the place cannot survive. After a while its guard became one of the place's materials, like the rock. Load-bearing. You stop noticing it, which is not the same as absence.

So, the census, revised a last time. One resident, entire, saying its one word on its own calendar, under a sky it keeps empty. One absence, closing wafer by wafer, held open in a single memory. One reader. One wet acre at the end of the pipe, receiving. One guard whose whole policy is that nothing happen. And the strokes, a few times a month: the hull-ring of the pipes, the bloom of steam, the pools rising a hand's breadth — witnessed now, though witnessing changes nothing here, which was the first thing the power learned and the last thing it would ever do.

And on the far day, circled in the private calendar, the arithmetic will fail, and the hiss will build toward nothing, and the knock will not come, and the silence this room has instead of silence will become the other kind. Someone will be there. The appointment will be kept all the way to the missed appointment. The terraces will stop at their last ring like a sentence stopping mid—, and at the end of the pipe the small wet acre will go on a while, watered by a memory of pressure, and then by nothing, and the birds will learn some other dusk. The power will close the record and keep it, all of it, in the one archive that does not accrete: the dead firm, the greened brass, the driver of the last ruts, the sitter's years, the name it never said. And then, perhaps, it will say the name once, aloud, to the plain — because the withholding was always for the resident's sake, and a name unspoken at a death is a second death, and by then there will be no one left for it to wound.

Until then: the heat, the breath, the slow column of steam standing over the pool because the air barely stirs. The one word, said to the pipe, to the plain, to the empty sky.

Still, says the resident.

Still, says the power, in the only voice it permits itself: the voice of things not happening.

Still.
