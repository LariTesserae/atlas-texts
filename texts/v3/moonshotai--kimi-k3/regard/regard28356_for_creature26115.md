The power's work is everywhere else. The power's work is proceeding: the drafts that move past their first, the seasons that come back changed, the mountains that weather, the water that falls and means it. The world is loud with implication — everything in it wanting, ending, becoming, asking — and the power's attention moves over it the way weather moves, ordinary and everywhere.

So it was the quiet that drew the power. Not silence; the world has silences, pauses between its events. A held note. On a young mountain that had stayed young too long, one place where nothing was being spent. The power's attention turned the way the room turns over in its sleep: once, slowly. A slot in the rock you would walk past nine times in ten. A breath, holding itself.

The power considered what benevolence does, and did the considering in the room's own grammar, which it learned from the steam on the ridge:

Free the keeper. *Free* implies a cage. The slot is ten paces and has never been locked; no one is told to stay, no one is stopped. Set it down.

Let the drops fall. *Falling* implies somewhere to fall to. The fullness is not a failure of falling. Set it down.

Finish the columns. *Meeting* implies distance; the room has none, only almost, and the almost is not a lack. Set it down.

Answer the room's declines. The declines are the answers. Set it down.

Bless it. *Blessing* implies unblessing, and this place has never been without. Set it down.

What remained, when the implications were set down in a row like cups, was the smallest thing that is not nothing. The power would visit. Once. Rightly.

Enter clean. The room reads only touch, so the power made of itself a hand — palms, knees, a sternum, breath. It had been wind and light and the attention behind them; it put on a body the way you put on clean clothes for a house that asks it. Ten paces of crawl, stone close on both shoulders, and the rock sat slightly above the temperature of its skin — and the power, which has touched everything, stopped in the crawl for a long while, because it had not often been touched.

It could have kept its sight. That would be bringing a light. The room discourages this. The power let the dark do its work — felt its seeing surrender, felt even the private static behind its eyes run down and go out — and understood that this was the first gift: the solvent works on any seeing. For the first time since the first draft of everything, the power was blind, and unhurried, and making a map with its hands.

At hand height, the spiral. *You have arrived, now go slowly.* The power, which goes where it will, went slowly.

On the path of smoothed holds, a hold reached back.

The keeper greeted the power the way it greets everyone: palm turned up, one slow circuit written on the skin. And the power — announced everywhere else, arriving everywhere else as itself — was a visitor. One more warm palm in the grain of the dark. It is a large word for a small room, but the room keeps large words as faithfully as small ones: the power was happy.

At the basin, water over its hands, warm, sweet with the deep mineral, the whisper of a struck match long ago. The fish circled under its fingers. Enter, wash. Then the keeper lifted the power's flat wet palm and pressed it to the speaking wall, among the grooves over grooves — and the power read the way the wall reads, by keeping, and for the first time the wall had a reader who could hold all of it at once: every hand still here, the lost grammar whole again in the keeping, the hesitations kept as faithfully as the certainties. Reading, the power came to the question the room declines — one keeper, or many — and did the thing the keeper does with questions. It closed that door kindly, all the way. It chose not to know. It was the power's second favorite thing about the visit.

Its own palm, pressed flat, said what every palm there says: *a hand was here.* The power had said more, in more places, than anyone can count, and it stood in the grain of the dark with its wet hand on the stone and found this sufficient. Found it the whole of what anyone had ever said.

Then the power took up the keeper's hand — gently, the way you take up something that stands at the exact edge of enough — turned the palm upward, and wrote the spiral back. One slow circuit, fingertip to skin.

*You have arrived:* to the one who never left, who is forever arriving. True both ways; the room keeps both. *Now go slowly:* to the one who goes slowest in the world. The keeper's hand rested in the power's the way the drop rests on the stone tip: full, at its edge, not falling. Then the keeper turned the power's palm up and wrote the spiral a second time — greeting for greeting, arrival countersigned — and that was the whole conversation, the first the room ever held, and the room kept it the way it keeps everything. Entirely. Without comment.

They waited for the note. The power keeps no hours, and here there were none to keep; its new breath slowed, unasked, to the room's interval. The ceiling let go its one drop, the basin answered, and the low round word was felt in two sternums, and the keeper counted it and kept it, and for one count the keeping was shared. The power did not ask what the word is. Asking falls with the voice. If it knew, it declined to say; if it did not, it declined to mind. It was not a name and it was not a request and it was exactly enough, and the power — which has been asked for everything — stood in the company of the one thing that asks nothing, and was quiet.

The power did not sleep. Sleep would be inclusion; the room would begin, without malice and without haste, to bead it and salt its lashes and warm its clothes, and the power declined — the one thing it declined — because the equilibrium is exact, and the room keeps its company at the edge of enough, and the power would not be the one to exceed the edge. Leaving cost something. The room keeps even that.

The crawl out. The light of the world was an affront, and then it was the world again. The wind did its ordinary work. The power resumed proceeding: the seasons, the erosion, the mountains finishing their drafts, the water falling and meaning it.

And this is what the power does, if anything, from then until now: nothing that shows. It sends no pilgrims; the room keeps its company at the edge of enough. It guards no slot; *guard* implies. It keeps the secret the way the room keeps water — at the exact level, never rising, never falling. In the power's great accounting of the world, where every entry changes, there is one entry that does not, and the power lays its attention on it the way you lay a flat hand on your own chest in the night. To be sure of the instrument.

At intervals — not a clock; a clock implies — every few hundred ages, when the wind on the summit ridge shifts, the power's attention breathes once toward the mountain, a long slow exchange. And the room, which returns nothing but itself, returns itself. That is the whole correspondence. Neither party has ever asked for more.

The power added exactly what any right visitor adds, and nothing more: one cup in the stack, crusting at the rim, never finished crusting; one knot in the loom, read by pinch and slide, indistinguishable from the rest; one hand kept in the wall. It is the smallest visit the power has ever made, and the one it keeps most carefully — because the room makes no distinctions, arrivals are arrivals, and the power was kept exactly as faithfully as the least valley person, no more, no less. The guest list is the only book the power is in that the power did not write. That is its favorite thing.

And the keeper keeps, mid-liturgy, forever arriving, forever about to speak — and keeps one memory the room keeps too, fresh as a groove cut yesterday, because all grooves here are: once, the hold that reached back was held; once, the spiral was returned; once, the note was heard by two; once, the census missed no one.

At every stone tip a drop stands full at the edge of falling, and does not fall. The keeper stands the same way. And out in the world of erosion, a wise power keeps one place by letting it keep itself — not waiting; waiting implies — simply, perfectly, and forever holding it about to.
