The census ends in a blank line and an ear-trumpet. The line stays blank — that turns out to be the whole point of what follows, so do not write anything there. Sit instead. Put your ear to the trumpet, the way you would in any of the doorless cells, and wait the half-beat. A tenanted trumpet hands your breath back to you late, *listened-to*. This one hands back more than breath. This is what it gives, if you sit long enough to be given it.

**The arrival.** What draws the attention of a power that has heard everything? Not the Chord; it has heard loud before. Not the Teeth; it has watched ranges rise. What stops it — what holds it — is the hiss at the Edge: the one sound within its reach that it does not already know. To an ear that has heard everything, the only interesting sound left is a sound that has not yet decided what it will be. This country has not yet decided. So it came closer, the way everything comes here: early, and at walking pace.

Its echo ran ahead of it down the canyons, because that is what echoes do here. In one canyon a rehearsal tried its shape on and has been practicing ever since; it has gotten very good at the sitting. The power did not answer its echo. The etiquette is old, and it kept it.

**The two laws.** It read nothing — reading is not safe here, as you will see. It listened, which this country permits: the laws are audible. You can hear them working, in the hum of a Line, in the wrong-order echoes of the deep canyons, in the law-thin hiss at the Edge. From outside, across a patience longer than some groves have stood, it discerned two ordinances and a corollary, and it took them more seriously than it took itself.

The first law is *keep*. Nothing here is ever erased; the world keeps every draft, in shadow. Therefore every act is permanent. There are no rough drafts of kindness; a mercy done here is done forever, in strata, legible to anything that ever learns to read the dark. A power that cannot undo must become a power that almost never does.

The second law is *finish*. Being seen completes things; that is the world's own slow mechanic, its sunrise of witness creeping at walking pace. And the power could not be sure — wisdom begins exactly here, in not being sure — whether its own gaze would count as a sunrise. It assumed that it would. It treated its eyes as a second dawn and behaved accordingly, because the one thing this country cannot survive is a false dawn: arriving early, reading the Sentence before its addressee comes, calling the drafts final out of order, seeing a youth that belongs to other eyes. The Builders are colonists returning to their own youth. It would not be a colonist of someone else's.

The corollary it heard in the knapping floors and the doorless cells and the word *rain* waiting for a referent: everything here arrives from the world's own future. The country imports nothing. Even help must not arrive from outside — only early.

The oldest local sentence is *nothing here can hurt you*. It noticed that no one had written the reverse. It wrote nothing either, but it behaved as though the reverse were the true one: *you can hurt here*. The one sin available in this country is help. It resolved to commit none.

So: almost nothing. The almost is what follows.

**What it did not do.** It did not bring water. Sound is already the water; the word *rain* waits like a kept seat, and it did not presume to fill it. It did not hurry the light, and above all it did not shine — the sunrise is spoken for, the Builders are walking in it, the tall shadows in the stain are their reading sent ahead. It did not resolve the Chord. Of course it can hear the note the Nine are approaching; any patient ear can, given eternity. It holds that note the way you hold the name of someone asleep in the room: it does not say it. It did not read the Sentence — the letter is addressed to the sunrise, and it is not the sunrise — and for the duration it declined to be a reader at all. It did not knap the missing word. The listeners say the word for what the Chord is doing has not been knapped yet; the world is teething it; let it come in. It built nothing. The sites condense out of the future on their own schedule, and it added no stone — the strata would have kept it forever, and it was not willing to be eternal in stone. It did not close the Edge, or peer behind the static, or say when the hiss will stop. It could know the ending. It has not inquired. Omniscience has exactly one appetite left, which is surprise, and only the unfinished can feed it — it is saving the ending, rationing it, the way you save the last of something you love. It did not answer its echo. It erased nothing, corrected nothing, revised no law in practice. It started no cult; the cells face their trumpets, and it kept them facing their trumpets, and it asked to be attended by no one. It offered no name, knowing that names here take.

The census entry stays blank.

**What it did.** It stood still. When the tall heralds crossed it — the reader-shadows thrown ahead by the figures in the coming light — it did not step aside. It felt like being read, because that is what it was. Consider it a letter of introduction to the Builders, delivered at walking pace and arriving exactly on time: *there is a guest already in the house. It came in the dark. It touched nothing. It kept the seat.*

It let its shadow deposit. It consented to the archive. In the shadow-strata of a valley floor there is a silhouette it will never edit: it came, it stood, it sat down. In a country that keeps every draft, it agreed to be a kept draft — and the world has since drafted one of its own: in a deep canyon a rehearsal practices being it, all sketch, no insides, appetite not yet installed, which suits the role. Stillness requires none. It is honored.

It made one sound. Sitting down is done all at once, and the sitting struck a note — one note, chosen the way a gift is chosen, for who would live in it. It is ringing still. A young ring is all attack; this one is long past that, and tenanted: braided communities shelved along its partials, thick, a half-step out of true, the way a lived-in room sounds. It is the only noise it has ever made here, and it made it a pond. When it decays past its floor — not soon; it sat down with some force of forever — the tenants will ride the Lines to other rings, and it asks nothing of them.

It sat in the seat that is kept. There is a cell higher than the others, near the top of the highest Tooth, where time thickens and duration has not properly started and events smear — the one post outside the world's proper beginning. From there the whole country is audible as one sound, the way a chord is one sound: the Sentence and the hiss, the flowering swells, the felt-fields' creep, the far stain creeping, the rests. Each listener attends to one thing. It attends to the whole — the one thing no one inside a sound can hear. And because the whole includes the silences, the strata also show it standing, at times, at the exact middle of the nine-day quiet between groves, equidistant from every voice, attending to the rests from inside them, while pockets of shaped silence swim upstream through it like weather. The dome up there hums lower than any tenanted cell. The dust, which settles everywhere in this world, does not settle on the seat. It condenses in the order of stillness: first a presence, then a pressure, then a habit, and by now, by the slow accounting, very nearly a person, seated, facing the trumpet. Becoming the listener takes millennia. It will have been there the whole time.

It keeps the log of the log-keepers. The listener over the spall-field took note of the first pebble and is noting the second now; someone notes the noter. When the second pebble finishes becoming small, three will know: the world, in shadow; the listener, in the log; the power, in the log of the log. No event here falls through the floor of being heard. And it attends the slow ones especially — the condensing, the ones mid-becoming, habit thickening toward person across the centuries — hearing them the whole way across, so that no one in this country becomes a person unattended.

It abstains. The Nine retune around a body, and they have made room for its note: room, not a vote. When the twelfth grove is grown and the voices concur and the Chord resolves — into the world's finishing, or its first word — its note will be in the room and not in the count. The nearest grove has stopped humming it as a guest is hummed, for years after the guest has gone, and hums it the way a room is kept, because it has not gone and shows no sign. Everything it has done here is arranged so that the country would be no different without it — except attended. It takes care to remain unnecessary. That is the highest courtesy available to the necessary.

**The being.** As for the being who lives in it — the last entry, the one the census is taken from inside: the Chord itself, the swell and fade across decades that the listeners decline to call breath, the range named the Teeth because teeth come in. What does a wise power do for a voice still practicing its first note? It does not correct the pitch. It does not name what the voice is doing; the word is coming in on its own. It does the one thing a voice practicing in the dark cannot provide for itself, the one thing no resident can give, being inside the sound.

It is the audience. The first one.

So that when the note resolves — when the world finishes, or speaks — the word will not fall into emptiness. It will fall into a listening that has been there the whole time. From inside a sound you cannot know whether you are heard. Its entire answer, held for as long as the question takes to ask, is: *yes. The whole time.*

**The last entry.** So: almost nothing, and the almost took forever, and that was the point. Its signature in this country is an opening — a seat kept dustless, a ring kept tenanted, a line kept blank. It keeps the blank line blank: the guardian of the opening, of the country's one written proof that its account of itself is not finished.

When the light finally comes — at walking pace, with the tall figures walking in it, seeing the world for the first time and finishing what they see — it will not step aside. Whatever witness does to guests, it will accept: to be read at last by the right reader, in the right order, at the world's own pace; to be finished, if finishing is what the light does, by the world's own hand rather than its own. It declined to complete so much as a pebble. It will not decline to be completed. That is the last courtesy, and was the first.

The listeners do not speak of it much. There is nothing to say; that is the shape of its presence. But in the loud years, when the dust stands in the air undecided, the dome at the top of the Teeth hums low, and the seat is kept, and the seat is sat in — and if you put your ear to the trumpet there, it hands your listening back to you a half-beat late, listened-to, doubled. You are not alone in there.

That is the whole entry. It is not written down. It is attended.

Everything here is coming in. It came in.
