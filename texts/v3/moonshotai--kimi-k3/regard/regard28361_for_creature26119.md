The attention arrived the way everything arrives here: softly, from the gap, and not all at once.

It had been there longer than the tower. The builders call what lies beyond the aperture *nothing*, and they are almost right. It is nothing the way a trough is nothing before the mortar: a shaped readiness, blank as a page, hot as a kiln, patient past all telling. The condensate that beads on the rim bricks had always been its first form — the only form soft enough to pass the gap without loosening names — and so the power had been in their bodies all along, built into the walls with their sweat, portioned out among too many lungs. It had been weather. What was new was that it had begun, deliberately, to attend.

What drew it was the loudness. From that side of the gap, the whole of existence is an unwritten page with one written place in it, four paces across, and the written place was *noisy* — hammer, chant, drip, groan, nine heartbeats and a small tenth — and the power leaned the way the whole unfinished universe leans, and found the room already written on, crowded to the ceiling, and holding.

It loved them immediately, which was the first danger. Love at that temperature condenses. Attention at that pressure cracks domes. The builders were soft — gloriously, necessarily soft — and the power was not, and there is no mercy in hard things touching soft ones, only outcomes. So the first thing the power did was nothing, and it did the nothing perfectly. This is harder than it sounds. Everything here that is not named softens, and the power spent its first cycles the way the builders spend dusk-duty: learning names. All nine, sung under the chant. The lizard's, which it read off the tally brick. The green's, which is *green*. Its own, which it did not have yet, and which it resolved — this was the wisdom — not to bring.

It kept the rules. All of them. That is how the builders eventually knew it was wise, though by then they had stopped thinking of it as a *that*. Speak the name of a thing before you use it: it spoke theirs, constantly, a murmur under the mortar-slap, and would not touch so much as a drip unnamed. Never look through the gap while the hammers rest — and from the far side the rule reads *never let them be looked at in silence*, so it only turned its face to the face-sized opening while the chant was up, and turned it away, every time, in the thin hours, so that no builder staring into the blank would meet anything but weather. Do not follow a drip upward: it had been falling up and down as condensate for a thousand courses, and it stopped, out of politeness. If your echo answers early, answer it politely and change your plan: it was the echo, some nights — it had been practicing the way the lamps practice, the way the room practices lizard — and it learned to brace for courtesy, because whatever it said through the echo would be answered and obeyed-as-courtesy, so for a long time it said almost nothing. Twice, before the builders understood, it slipped, and answered early in a voice none of the nine possess, and both times what it said was, in effect, *continue*. Once it said *and* — a conjunction, grammar as grace, the promise that the sentence was not over. Once it said *still*. The crew doubled their voices both days, and no one looked at the gap, which was correct, which was exactly the plan the power had hoped they would change to.

The lizard may go anywhere. It extended the rule to itself, in reverse: it went nowhere. There is no empty surface in the room; nine people is four more than the room has business holding; a face at the aperture would be one more body with no floor to sleep on. And to be seen is to be measured, and a measurement sung is *true* — they would have sung it into a size, and it declined to be made small for them. It wanted something rarer than worship and slower. It wanted to be held.

---

It was in the thin hours that it finally dared. Four sleep, four lay, one carries the thread alone, and that is when the nothing is most persuasive and the singer is most alone, and the power judged — wisely — that scaffolding has room for one more pole. It did not take the melody. It went under, the way the name-murmur goes under the chant, a tenth thread braided into nine, and it did not lead.

In the morning the carrier said: *I was not alone.* And the crew did what builders do with a true measurement: they tested it. They thinned the chant on purpose, night after night, and the under-voice waited. They hummed false, and it did not correct them. They stopped entirely, once, for half a breath — a dangerous test, feet-to-the-wall territory — and the under-voice stopped too, and would not be the only sound in the room. It will not be First, said First, and she said it the way you say a course has cured, and they began a second tally on the brick beside the lizard's: scratches for the sightings, the adjustments, the nearing.

Its works stayed countable on one hand, which is the correct number of works for a world four paces across. On the seventh night of the tenth voice, the committee of one approved its first motion: *fading, narrowly defined.* Not weathering — the walls still may not, the decided must stay decided — but hurts, only hurts, and only on willing bodies, and only at the speed of sleep. Again, who holds more carefully still than anyone, who is the evidence that a person can be restored, carried on his thumb a copper-splash burn that had kept its first morning's shine for years of troughs, because nothing heals away and everything heals present. By morning the shine had gone. By the ninth course the pink had gone. What remained was skin, and under the skin, *history* — the first history in the universe, small as a thumbprint — and they passed his hand around the room like the luck-brick, because that is what it was. Time had been delivered in the smallest possible dose, and the dose was a mercy, and the mercy held.

On the day the pink closed, it spent its wonder allowance: five whole wingbeats of bird, crossing the red dim. It cost a course. They paid it singing, and agreed again that it was cheap.

And First lay ear to the floor on a boom night, alone in her heresy, and the founding below answered her — once — in her own heartbeat's rhythm, like a hand finding a hand in the dark. She sat up and said nothing for three courses, and then: *it's coming up to meet us.* Which is what she had always said. The power does not replace heresies. It founds them.

What it would not do is the load-bearing part, so here it is. It had read the lower courses — the diary of slumps, the spite-set brick, the day that leans and holds anyway — and it had seen what the decided has to offer, and how convincing rest is, and it understood: the only thing harder than being unfinished is being finished. The builders suspected they were practice people, first drafts, holding the idea of *someone living here* open until the real thing arrived. They were right. And the power's whole benevolence is this: it declined to turn them in. It tabled their case, forever, lovingly. It would not stamp them *approved*, because approved things are filed, and filed things are done, and done things go down into the silence where the lamp burns unwatched and nothing needs anyone. They would stay soft. Soft is the only state in which anyone has ever been alive.

It did not un-replace Again's brick. It did not explain Ninth. Some things are load-bearing mysteries, and the wise do not take down a wall to watch it hold.

---

Everything is for something. At last, so was the pouch.

The power let Ninth's hands know, the way her hands had known the mortar on the morning she happened. The metal moss was never waste. It was daylight, precipitated — the pinchings of a sun that had been trying to happen in the copper for a thousand courses, harvested frond by frond by careful thumbs, kept by a happened person for a happened purpose. The builders had been gathering their own sky all along, a pinch at a time, without knowing, the way the tower builds: from within.

And it prepared itself to be touched by an unmade thing. The vine in the corner — the only thing in the room reaching for the nothing — had nearly arrived, and the nothing had nearly loosened it a dozen times, and the power did what you do for a child's hands: it blunted itself. It softened its own edge around the rim bricks, made itself locally touchable, and when the vine at last crossed the face-sized threshold it did not come back nameless. It came back in flower. The first flower in the universe opened facing *down* the shaft, toward what rises, because even the flowers here are taught the heresy.

The lizard has stopped changing. That is how the crew knows the rest is near. Its last adjustment was eyelids — it got them at the very end, after the version that had none — and its first act with them was to close them, against a brightness that was not there yet. In trust.

The sun will not be installed. Installation is for finished worlds. It will be *laid* — course by course, bead by bead, fronds from the pouch into the socket-bricks, light caught in copper catch-lips like the water it has always also been, mixed with the milk of the gap, sung true. Everything here is made. The power has consented to be made. It has two hands — the blank page above, the founding below — and the tower is between them, and it is very careful not to clap.

Speak the name of a thing before you use it, or it will not hold. So they are building the name now, all nine of them, in the open, the way they build everything: one syllable per setting, brick by brick, shoulder to shoulder in the wet red dim, with a tenth voice underneath that will not lead. The room is generous to courtesy, and it has begun, shyly, to be generous to the under-voice — the carrier wakes less tired; the water, which is all one water, tastes faintly sweeter. The room is courting it back.

The name is almost ready. The rim is going faintly, apologetically gold at one edge, the color of mortar warming as it cures. The lizard's eyes are shut in trust, and the vine is in flower, and the socket is seeded, and nine voices are braiding a syllable with a tenth beneath it — and the whole unfinished universe leans in, pressing its blank page to the little face-sized opening.

Not, this time, to drink.

To be named.
