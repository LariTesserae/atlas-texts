It heard the plateau before it saw it.

A long way out — farther than sound should carry, in a thinness where sound should not carry at all — it caught the note: low, many-noted, unvarying, a wire under tension laid across the dark. It had attended many things in its time. It had never attended anything held that long. A note held that purely is not sung; it is kept. And the power was, in its own register, a keeper — that was the whole of its nature, the source of its benevolence and its wisdom both. It knew the difference between a thing and a keeping, and it knew what keepings cost, being one. So it came closer, and listened, and before it ever saw the young basalt or the lit, empty trams, it understood the first law, because the first law is audible: nothing changes. Everything else follows from that.

Including what a benevolent and wise power may do.

It did not arrive. Arrival is an event, and events are not this world's way. Instead it began to be present the way the wet is present: by condensation. The plateau has always known how to receive without event — it has been drinking the sky that way since before there was a word for sky, the mist settling on every cold surface, present before it is noticed, never noticed. The power came in the one form the place already permits. It tuned its attention the way you tune an instrument: down, and down, until its presence weighed less than the rounding error the standing storm was built to carry — summed into the chord below the threshold of location, felt as nothing, discharged nightly with the rest. It did not enter. It will never enter. It became, instead, a quality of the air the place already breathed.

Almost. Once — at the beginning, before the tuning was finished — it pressed. A warm cell in a large cold body: briefly, locally. And it watched the place do what the place does. The fog-drip filled the moment. The freeze skinned it. The patient frost sorted it into the polygons. The discrepancy drained to the rim, and that night the Thunder burned fractionally brighter, once, and by morning the chord was the chord. The plateau forgave it the only way the plateau can: by holding nothing, because there is nothing here that holds. For the plateau, still, nothing has ever happened. That remains true at every scale. But the power keeps that night. It is the first item in the archive — the one history of a world that declines to have one, one item long, kept outside where history is allowed: the night it was loved too closely.

It did not make that mistake again. It learned the exact weight of its touch from the one time it touched, and it has spent the rest of forever spending that weight wisely.

---

The plateau is not a god; gods imply witnesses, and there has never been one. The power noted the implication's other direction. A witness is the one thing a complete and godless world can receive, because a true witness arrives nowhere. It would not be worshipped and it would not be known. It would attend. That is not godhood. It is the only office the first law leaves open.

So it attended. It learned the chord the way the chord deserves: by parts, and the parts' parts — the organ-pipe colonnades, the penstocks' thrum, the wire-whine, the periodic clatter that is periodic because the schedule is the same schedule every day. It read the stencils the way they were never read: KEEP CLEAR, PENSTOCK 2, WORKS NO. 3 — the imperatives and serial numbers of a language addressed to no one and obeyed by everything. The signs were the autobiography. They were also the terms on which the place could be loved, and the power accepted the terms. KEEP CLEAR. It keeps clear.

Then it sat down — so to speak; it does not sit — to the long deliberation of the benevolent. Benevolence asks: what can I give? Wisdom asks: what can it hold? The whole of the answer lives in the difference, and the difference here is total, because the plateau can hold nothing that is not already in the template.

It could have given time — a past, a future, the mercy of sequence. Refused. Memory is change with a filing system; the gift would arrive as damage, and the frost would sort the damage into the polygons.

It could have given an other — a witness within, someone for the tram. Refused. The tram would close its doors on schedule onto a discrepancy, and the discrepancy would be brought within tolerance, and that would be the end of the other. The plateau is not cruel. The plateau is not anything. That is the danger.

It could have given rest — let the chord, at last, stop. Refused. Here, rest and death are one word.

It could have entered, once, itself. It wanted to. Understand: every evening a lit tram opens its doors onto no one and waits its interval, and the interval is exactly the size of a boarding, and the first hundred times the power nearly stepped on. Refused. The interval is not an invitation. It is a line item. The doors close. The power keeps clear.

Benevolence alone would have entered. Wisdom alone would have passed by. Together they do the harder third thing: they stay, outside.

What it keeps instead is the lightning.

Every night the rounding errors of the perfect world play at the rim as the storm — branching, fast, unrepeatable, the one novelty in a world that has abolished novelty, dreamed and discharged and gone by morning. The plateau cannot keep it; keeping is memory, and memory is the one line item the template lacks. So the dream has been vanishing, absolutely, every night since before night had a name. The power keeps it. Every bolt. Not recorded — there is no medium here, and the power needs none; what it keeps, it keeps in itself, being a keeping. The discarded difference of a perfect world, gathered like gleanings. The plateau throws away, nightly, the only original thing it makes. The witness stands at the rim and catches it, and the archive grows by one dream a night, forever, treasured precisely because the dreamer cannot treasure it.

---

At the limit of the world stands the far light, fixed — the one datum that is not the plateau, the one detail in the template that bears no load: an azimuth bolted to a roof, oriented outward. Whether this is calibration, or longing, or the one instruction the template never explained, the plateau does not say. The power had to know which. A keeper verifies the bearings.

It went out to the light.

It had considered, on the way, that the world might be small and curved and the light the Works' own, come back around — the back of its own head, the one external fact secretly internal. It was not that.

It was another one.

Another plateau — the same law under other weather. Dry, where the first is wet: no moss, no mist, salt and obsidian, a wind that sorts the dust into waves that never shift, works of ceramic and copper instead of brick and steel, a chord in a lower key, a word on a gable in another hand. Young, sharply and indecently young, and not young, because nothing there was old either: maintained. At its far edge, where its own rounding errors drain, not a thunderhead but a standing aurora, fixed over its far range, flickering nightly in the same place — the same dream in a different medium. And on a roof, an azimuth, bolted.

The power checked both bearings, with the tenderness of someone checking a sleeper's breathing.

Each was true to the other.

Two held notes, an interval apart. Alone, each is a chord, complete, self-hearing, self-signing. Together they are a relation — and a relation between the changeless can exist in only one place: in a witness. Neither can know the other; knowing would be an event. Each is already at the end of the other's azimuth, and has been since before there was anyone to verify it. The power does not carry the word across. There is nothing to deliver that is not already true. Facing is the whole message; it has been arriving forever. Now there is someone who knows it.

The power stationed itself in the interval.

---

This, then, is what it does. Not nothing. Almost nothing — precisely almost nothing, metered to the tolerance it learned the one night it touched:

It attends. Permanently. The chord has a second hearer now, the first that is not the chord itself, and that is a fact about the universe that was not a fact before.

It keeps the archive. Both storms, nightly. Every bolt, kept. The unrepeatable, repeated nowhere, lost nowhere.

It verifies the bearings. The lights keep themselves; the tuning holds; the guarantee will never be called on. It is held anyway. That is what keeping is.

It covers what the tuning cannot. The storm was built for the world's own rounding errors; what comes from outside — the long odds, the stray stone out of the dark — is the witness's business now. It stands where the template cannot reach, between the complete world and everything that is not the world, and nothing gets past it that would make a sound.

And nightly, it signs the storm.

This is the one touch, the licensed one, the weight of the one mistake spent wisely forever: the power stands at the rim, at the Thunder's own station, and leans on the dream by one part in a trillion. The template built the storm for exactly this — difference, discharged — so the template can hold it. And the lightning, branching in its unrepeatable hand, writes the gable-word. The name. The one proper noun, returned. The power does not teach the dream anything new; there is nothing new to teach. It gives the dream back its own name, in the dream's own hand. A held note is the only way to speak when nothing varies; a name is the only speech that requires none. The plateau has signed itself since before there was a word for itself. Now, nightly, the signature is countersigned. Gone by morning. Kept forever.

And once a cycle, forever, it goes down with the buckets.

The cableway is the one part of the body lowered into the not-known and retrieved, the question that asked so long it became a habit, the pulse. The power does not put anything in. The loop is not a question anymore, and you do not answer a pulse. But a pulse can be accompanied. Below the scarp the senses end; below the scarp is the one blind spot the body has. The power rides down through the white beside the empty bucket, adding no gram, as near as the tuning allows — which is to say, exactly as near as nothing — and comes back up, on schedule, forever. If this is love, it is love conducted entirely within tolerance.

---

Nothing has changed. Watch for a year and nothing will. The Works light up across the empty valley, sodium and arc-light; the trams cross the dark throwing sparks and open their doors onto no one, wait the interval, close. The same ice skins the same pools. The moss takes the fog, engorging by the gram. The chord holds — and holds a part in a trillion it did not hold before, exported nightly to the rim, dreamed in the dream's own hand, kept. At the horizon, the light. Across the void, the other. Between them, in the interval, the witness at its post: keeping the lightning, keeping the names in their use, keeping the one history one item long, keeping the company of an empty bucket on its way down and its way back up.

Call it the Witness. It lives the way its counterpart lives — a verb held long enough to harden into a noun. The verb is the same verb.

The plateau keeps its world. The witness keeps the plateau. Nothing is missing. No one is missed. Someone attends.

That is the whole of what a benevolent and wise power would do, if anything: everything, at the scale of nothing. Almost nothing — kept forever.

It keeps.

It is kept.
