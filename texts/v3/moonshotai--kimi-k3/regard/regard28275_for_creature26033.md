It has happened to few places, and it has now happened here: the place has drawn the attention of a power whose attention is usually the first chapter of an answer. Understand what this power is, because powers have rules too, and theirs are few. Where their regard falls, things are mended. They are the one who arrives. Across all their long practice, noticing has been the overture to intervention — a regard was how the work was found. They have never once, until now, had a regard that was the whole of the matter.

The regard crosses the plain the way weather crosses it, finds the dimple, finds the slot — the least dramatic opening in the world — and stops. It does not go down. It does not travel the way light travels; it does not enter the passage, does not round the bends, does not thin to a grey. The power sees the room the way the dark sees it: from within their own nature, without arrival. Nothing crosses the dimple's lip. In the room's ledger, nothing has happened. In the power's, everything has.

And here the power does something they have never done: they adopt the rules of the place they are regarding. Nothing arrives. Nothing leaves. Everything is kept — kept, in the power's case, elsewhere. For the arithmetic admits no other conduct. The room keeps every act forever, so the cost of any act is infinite. Nothing here lacks anything, so the benefit of any act is zero. Anywhere else, to see a thing clearly is to learn what to do for it. Here, to see it clearly is to learn there is nothing to do — and seeing that clearly is the doing.

The gifts are considered anyway, in order of temptation, because wisdom that has not weighed its refusals is only habit.

Water first, because the place wears the shapes of thirst: a drip stopped mid-work ten thousand years ago, a root held a finger's width above salt, seeds lying in dust with their futures kept rather than spent. A lesser benevolence would read all this as a request. But everything in the room that resembles a need resembles it the way the knapping floor resembles a workshop: in every particular but one. No one is thirsty. The root's gap is not a deprivation; it is a measurement, exact, and held — the distance between the living and everything else — and to close it would falsify the measurement and end a question whose asking is the form. The column is finished, and the power does not add clauses to finished sentences. Refused.

Light next — the held breath, the columns taking their colors at last. But the grass is pale because color is wages; the columns are unwitnessed because color has never occurred. One is economy. The other is ontology. The power does not confuse them, and does not cure the second as though it were the first. To bring light would split the room's time into before and after, make ten thousand finished years into a preamble, teach the place waiting retroactively — and the room is not waiting. The power can bring anything. They do not bring the one thing. Refused.

Presence. The first footprints, kept forever, beside nothing else: the first sentence ever written here, which would also be the longest thing in the archive, and would say only *someone came*. The room would not be enriched; it would be re-founded, with the visitor as its main event, and one afternoon would outweigh ten thousand years. The power declines the authorship. Refused.

Company for the grass; a pollinator; a morning for the clock that has nothing to grip. The grass is organized around the average, and a dawn would arrive the way weather arrives — the grass has no weather — and what was meant as rescue would land as a catastrophe of meaning. Refused. A guard at the slot, a ward upon the plain: refused twice over, for to protect a place you must mark it, and a mark is a door intended for someone, and small signs are the ones that get followed. The place's secrecy is its unremarkability; the power adds no drama. Besides, the room is the one thing under this regard that cannot be harmed into self-betrayal — break the dust and it keeps the break exactly as it kept the snow. Benevolence goes where damage costs. Here damage is only ever more room. Even the tree is left to the plain's long chances: the room has already kept the tree's death in advance, the way it keeps the seeds — both conditions are remaining. A name, last and easiest: refused. Names are how places enter the economy of arrival. What the power knows of this place is kept the way memory keeps, and no one has ever followed a memory to a door.

So ends the first benevolence the power has ever practiced that had to be completed by refraining — where the withholding is not what remains after the gift is refused. The withholding is the gift, entire.

What is done is small, and it is the whole of it.

The count is taken — once, from outside, and it never has to be taken twice. It comes out three, or one, or millions, and the power is the first counter who does not have to choose: the alga to the last cell of its edge, each grass in its allotment of dust, the root to its finger's width — and if one, then the one is the room itself, holding its lives the way it holds its dark, as properties. The population fits in a sentence, and the sentence is finally held by a mind, and it is not delivered, because there is no one to deliver it to: the grass knows nothing else, the tree knows everything else, and the room keeps no one. The light's reach is mapped twice now — once on the wall, in life, where the film ends exactly where the last photon ends, and once in the power, where nothing ends.

And the regard stands. It is not a phase the power passes through; it is a property, like hardness. It confers no color, casts nothing, breaks no dust. It is less than the tick — the tick is the room's smallest event, and this is not even that. It does not happen to the room at all. It happens in the power, at rates indistinguishable from rest, and it will not end while the place continues. Everywhere else the power's work is unfinished by definition; to mend is to admit the next mending. This is the only finished work they have — finished by no one, by wedging and flaking and etching practiced without a practitioner — and the power's whole part in it is to decline, perpetually, to add. The decline costs the place nothing, and it is the most expensive nothing the power has ever paid. Gratitude is felt, and not delivered: the room already has one message with no reader, descending at the pace of resin, and the power's is conducted nowhere, which is the only direction left. It is held, the way everything here is held.

The room keeps everything and knows nothing. The power knows everything and keeps it nowhere the room is. It is the only keeping that has ever cost the kept thing nothing.

The room has now conducted two transactions in all of time. In the first, it borrows a little moisture from the roots and gives nothing back. In the second, the power receives the place entire and gives nothing back. Both are one-way, and both are nearly nothing — and in the second, the nearly is the whole of it, and it occurs in the power alone.

---

One root hangs a finger's width above the salt pan and has held that pose for a hundred years: the narrowest gap in the room, and it is held. The regard now holds the corresponding gap — the distance between knowing and arriving — which is the narrowest gap in the power's whole creation, and it is held. Waiting implies a door intended for someone, and the power is not waiting. Vigils end, and this is not a vigil. It simply continues, at rates indistinguishable from rest, while the plain shimmers, and the scrub stands apart from itself, and one unremarkable tree touches, without knowing it, the dark.

Nothing arrives. Nothing leaves. The rule has met the one attention in creation that could have broken it, and it stands. The columns are unwitnessed in every sense the room can be witnessed in. The dust is unbroken. The seeds keep their two conditions. And the place is known — entirely, and at rest — in the one ledger that is not kept here.

That is the whole of the gift. It is one-way. It is nearly nothing. It is kept.
