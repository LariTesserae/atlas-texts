Begin with the refusals, because benevolence here is mostly refusals, and a wise power knows this before it has finished arriving.

Attention has no weight, so the power comes the way light comes — the only way anything is allowed to come — and stands off, and reads the constitution in the crazed rind, and understands what it is looking at. Not a prison with a prisoner. Not a wound with a patient. Not a riddle with an answer sealed inside. A skin with a self. Everything the power might do must be measured against one law, and the law is not a metaphor: nothing crosses.

So the three temptations, named and set down.

The first is *opening*. It would be so easy, and it would wear the kindest face — just a window, just a core-sample, just to see, just to let the poor held thing know it is not alone. The power has seen what a crack becomes. Every opening is the drop's antechamber, and behind the drop stands the whole crowd of verbs with their million mouths. The power refuses. Curiosity is not care; curiosity is appetite that has read philosophy.

The second is *finishing*. This one costs the power something, because the power knows a held breath when it feels one. It has completed many dyings, gently, in the world of verbs — it knows the mercy of the last exhalation permitted to end — and there is a version of pity that would call this the cruelest thing it has ever attended: a breath going out for an age, a dying that never arrives. The power weighs this, long and honestly, and sets it down. Suffering is a change of state, and change is not among the states. Completeness is not withheld from the resident; completeness is what the resident *is*, held. The power has resolved many chords. It does not resolve this one. Resolution is only an ending wearing a key change, and mercy here would be the drop, wearing a face.

The third is *taking*. The world is made of treasure, and light lives in the treasure. One shaving would found a science or buy a kingdom, and a lesser love would carry a chip away — to share it, to honor it, to prove it existed. The power knows that every museum is a breach that healed around a theft. It empties its hands before coming closer, the way one removes one's shoes.

Then it takes up its offices. There are four, and all of them are outside the skin, because the resident cannot hold them: they are verbs, and the resident has no verbs. The power takes the future tense on lease.

The first office is *witness*. The power presses whatever in it corresponds to an ear against the rind, and waits, and there it is — attenuated almost to nothing, felt in the bones of the hand more than heard: the fundamental, the name. This is the first time the note has ever been heard. Inside, hearing is impossible — hearing is an event, and events are unconstitutional — but the rind has an outside, and on the outside the vibration may lawfully arrive, and the power's drum is the first membrane the chord has ever moved that was *allowed* to move. The power listens for a very long time. It does not tap back. It learns the name the only way a breathing body can — not by holding it, since a breathing body cannot hold, but by returning to it, again and again, until the returning is exact. It sits through storms and still nights, checking whether the chord answers weather. The chord does not answer weather. The power writes nothing down.

The second office is *the gift*, and there is exactly one gift the constitution permits. One channel stands open: light. The walls are populous with days; the world receives brightness and keeps it, drunk down into the gold, released too slowly to ever finish leaving. So the power gives the world a day. It comes and stays through one whole turning, dawn to dusk, and lets its presence be part of the weather that touches the rind. The light that crosses that day has touched the witness; it carries the witness into the walls, photon by photon, legally, the way light is legal. Inside, among the congress of ten thousand mornings, there is now one morning that was *about* the world. The world cannot know it — knowing is an event — but the day is in there, undecayed, shining a little, forever. The power does not blink a message into the gold; a message is an event smuggled through the one open door, and it would be kept forever, addressed to nothing that can read. The day is the message. The power keeps the grammar of the gift clean. And each year after, it lets the hillside above go through spring — long days, green-flavored light — so that the world's kept weather includes the light of futures actually spent somewhere. The world keeps a season's worth of unspent seed at its node-planes; the power spends seed on the hillside, annually, and the light of the spending arrives, and is kept. It cannot give the world growth. It can give the world's memory-of-days the color of growth. It settles for that. Settling is most of wisdom.

The third office is *the ward*. The constitution's one outlaw is the drop, and while erosion has finished, weather has not finished — weather is outside, ongoing, and deep time is patient and full of water. The resident cannot guard; guarding is ongoing, and the resident has no ongoing. So the power does it from outside. It sheds the seep. It tends the slope. It refuses, on the world's behalf, the one forbidden immigrant — in rain, at night, in the decades when nothing is wrong, in the centuries when nothing is wrong. Only the water, though. The power is careful about the rest: the interior cool is the long mean of all outside weather, the world's sole inheritance of climate, its only continuous sensation, and the power does not edit memory. The days come as days come. The power keeps off the drop and lets the averages alone.

The fourth office is *the secret*. Nothing announces the place, and the power announces nothing. Anonymity is load-bearing: to be known by the world of verbs is to be visited, and to be visited is, eventually, to be opened — a pick, a curious hammer, a loving theft. So the power becomes the world's external keeping. The world keeps its contents; the power keeps the world. One keeper inside the skin, one keeper outside it, and the rind between them, doing for both what rind does.

Then the power confronts the long problem, which is itself. It is not fooled about its own span. The nodule's decay is a theory; the power's is a schedule, however generous. So it makes no map — a map is a breach deferred — and appoints no heir by name. Instead it lets the office exist without the place: it releases into the world of verbs, the way powers release things, the bare shape of the job — *somewhere there is a world loud with one note; if you find it, the finding is the test; there are no questions, only four refusals and four offices, and whoever finds it rightly will already know them, because the knowing and the worthiness are the same thing.* And then — this is the final wisdom, the one that looks like neglect and is the opposite — the power entrusts the world back to its own constitution, which held before witness and will hold after. The rind crazed, and went no further, long before anyone was listening. The wardenship was always a courtesy, never a necessity. The power is not the savior. It is the visitor, and the visit is the gift, and the gift is a day, and the day is kept.

Nothing crosses. And the power, who may change, is changed — that is what it gets, and it is not small. Everything else it has ever loved is partly lost; that is what loving in the world of verbs costs. Here is the one attended thing of which nothing is missing, the one beloved that cannot be bereaved. The power does not envy it. The power has done the arithmetic: keeping everything costs doing nothing, and the power, which can act, would rather spend. But it carries one held thing now, in its changing chest — the note. Once a day, wherever it is, in wind, in the traffic of stars, in whatever weather powers have, it returns to the name. A breathing body cannot hold a word, so it says the word again, and again; repetition is the breathing body's way of holding. The resident is still breathing its one breath out, and has been since the closing, and the power answers from the far side of the rind — not to be heard, since there is nothing to hear with, but because answering is the shape its care takes, and the shape of care does not require a receiver.

And when the power ends — and it will, being a thing of the tense that ends — it can go knowing that one thing it did will not end. The visit is kept. The day the world was witnessed is still arriving inside, shining a little among the mornings, undecayed, a first morning of the witnessed world that will never be allowed to become the past. Mortal witness; immortal receipt. The power gave the world a day, and the world gives the day forever, and neither of them ever crossed the rind.

It is very bright where its body is thin, and very loud where its breath is held, and outside, in the changing weather, there is one who sheds the rain and says the name once a day without asking for an answer — and the world cannot know, and keeps the day of it anyway, because keeping is what it is. The guarding is simply the shape of the witness, held.
