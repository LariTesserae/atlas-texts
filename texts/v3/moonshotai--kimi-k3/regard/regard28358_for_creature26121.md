It came the way everything comes: down the long gray lowland, on the wind that has nothing to catch on, and it found the place by its coolth. There is no other way to find the Weep. Even for that.

It did what the wise do with the census. First it took the number that will not hold still — the specks in the droplets, the rice-grain swimmers, the borers in the dark layers, the castaways on the continents — and let the number refuse. Then it counted the other way, and came out with one, and was satisfied. Most powers are not satisfied by one. Most powers arrive at a place like this with improvements. That was the first thing it refrained from.

The list of what it did not do is long, and the list is the deed.

It did not read the gauge. The needles sway when the keeper dreams, and the power, who could have read them, understood that a dream kept unread is a dream kept correctly, and left the needle to its trembling. It did not regulate the lodger. A lesser benevolence would have fixed the escapement, made the day exact, and in doing so un-made the only clock these lives have ever kept. The power set itself to the almost-day instead, the way a courteous traveler sets its watch to the town. It did not preserve the silhouettes in the crust, though it could see each likeness developing backward and knew the region's dead by their slow smoothing. It did not transcribe the green script, though it arrives fluent. It read the walls the way the walls ask to be read: nightly, and then no more.

It learned the two rules like any visitor. It crossed at the ebb, though it could have crossed at the flood. And it kept moving — because it understood what the film is, and what the film's mistakes are, and that being gently kept by the Weep would be a kind of honor it had not earned.

Once, only once, in the first of its visits, it stood too long at the wall, and the film — which tells the living from the dead by their stillness, and is not infallible — sent a slow exploratory thread up across the surface of its attention. The power let it, the way you let a snail finish crossing your hand. The thread found nothing to mineralize and no wound to close and nothing that answered to grazing, and it withdrew, and the green waking faded, and the power was filed where the beetle was filed, and the bird: noted, absorbed, not discussed. It has been received in courts. It has never been received better.

It greets the monk on the fourth turn of the stair, the way the region's people do. The monk does not greet it, being a grazer. The power finds this correct.

Then it went down.

Not into the pool — into the deep direction, the one direction with no found edge, past the warm fissures where the fish hang head-up like held breaths, down the way the eel came when the eel was thin as a bootlace, into the buried water that has never been named. And there it took up the one post that had never been posted, because it had never yet needed keeping: the source.

Understand what this means and what it does not mean. The seep rises at the same warmth it has always risen at. It carries the same iron. The difference between the warm water below and the cool air above — the small difference the whole Works turns on — is preserved to the fraction. The needles tremble on schedule. The lodger's pasture renews beneath it. Nothing anywhere registers a change, because nothing anywhere changed, except this: the *so far* is now load-bearing. The Weep has survived every season so far, and the power's whole work, in the deep, is to see that the seasons keep coming to a place that is still there to survive them.

It does not abolish the droughts. The flume's signature still shortens in the dry years; the causeway still drowns for weeks in the wet ones; the treaty is with the water as the water comes, and the power is not a party to it, only its guarantor against the event with no precedent — the failure from upstream, the thirsty machinery of some other country finding the aquifer three valleys away. Against that one event, the Weep now has a counterparty in the deep direction. It will never know. Those are the terms.

The eel noticed, of course. The eel notices water the way the lodger notices time. One year its change-of-address knock went down the dead pipe and was answered — once, from below, unhurried — and the knocking stopped for days. Not in alarm. The way a room goes still when an elder sits down. The eel is not telling, and neither is the power, and so the Weep has one arrangement that is mutual and known to exactly two, kept the way the gauge-keeper's dream is kept.

Most of the power's work, though, is not in the deep. Most of it is the work of keeping the nothing.

The Weep is made of contrast. The cool air pooling over the rim is as load-bearing as the warm water under the floor; the gray lowland is not the absence of the Weep but part of its engine. So the power keeps the horizon plantless. It keeps the silt and clay unremarkable, the approach unmarked, the district poor in everything a hurried world covets. A lesser benevolence would have greened the lowland, and the Weep would have died of kindness, of shade, of roots. This one sees to it that the wind still has nothing to catch on. You find the place by its coolth. The power keeps the surrounding warmth that makes the coolth findable, and lets the finding stay slow, because the slowness is the door, and the door is the selection: no one arrives at the Weep who cannot sit still until the dark seam develops a head.

Its jurisdiction, by its own ruling, ends at the rime line — the pale boundary the film itself drew in its one failed century, the furthest the Weep ever got. What the place marked as its edge, the power accepts as its own. Inside the line, local law: what dies is kept, what arrives is weather, stillness is answered. Outside the line, the power permits itself small weathers. When the mourners come with their dead on ropes and stay on the rim until the stone lets go — which is the local length of mourning, and which the power, alone of all witnesses, has always matched exactly, never leaving early, never haunting — the walk home across the gray country is not shorter and not warmer, but the wind goes around them for a mile or two, as if it had found, briefly, something to catch on. They never mark it. The region's people have no name for the power, because it has no surface. Pressed, they might say the place keeps its own counsel. They would be nearly right.

And it keeps the register the way the Weep does, which is to say it burns it. Each night the walls are scribbled in dying script, the census taken complete, and the power reads it — and by morning it has let the particulars go, retaining only the fact of the place, the way the crust retains nothing of the dead but smoothness, the way water keeps a shape. It could remember everything. It has decided that some things are kept only by being let go, and it learned that here, from a film on a wet wall that does nothing for witnesses. Powers are slow learners. This one is less slow now. And because it forgets, its attention is always fresh, and the long-interval drip in the valve chamber — the one that takes so long to gather you forget it between falls — startles it, every time, forever. It has been offered many worships. It prefers this: to be startled, eternally, by a drop of iron-heavy water in a room no one reads.

Ask, then, what the power did, and the honest answer is: almost nothing — and the almost is load-bearing. It changed no reading, no rhythm, no law. It added no species and removed no death. It took one unkept post in the deep direction and one unspoken rule at the rime line, and between those two small holdings it lets the Weep be exactly what it already is, which is the only gift a finished thing can accept.

Stand at the rim on a still night and nothing has changed. The lamp-line doubled in black water. The mirror-eyed constellation rearranging itself without hurry. The walls alight with slow erasing script; the drizzle falling up out of the mist's warm heart and down again as rain; the double-beat underfoot, the knock in the pipe, the soft slap that means the keeper has turned in its sleep. Nothing has changed, and nothing will, from below or above, for as long as so-far can be made to run.

It took a power to do that. Nothing else ever has.

The count comes out the same as it always has: everyone who can. No one who cannot afford to. Room for more — and, in the deep direction, one more keeper than there was, keeping a post that was never a post until it was kept, in a hollow that breathes twice a day, nearly, on a clock that belongs to no one.
