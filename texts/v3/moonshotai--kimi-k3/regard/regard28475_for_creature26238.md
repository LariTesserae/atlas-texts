It was the silence that drew it. Everywhere else in creation, something is asking — finish this, end that, heal, resolve, begin again — and a benevolent power's attention runs along those requests the way water runs along roots. From one low place beneath one fixed sun, nothing came up. No prayer crossed the haze-line. Every instrument it turned that way returned the same answer, at no interval anyone measured: *almost*. It went to see what was almost.

It stopped at the rim, the way you stop at the door of a room where someone is sleeping — because watching a sleeper wakes them, and its watching is not weightless. This is the arithmetic it did before anything else, because doing arithmetic first is what wise means. Its regard is the most expensive substance in creation: attended by it, things finish. And the basin sits exactly at its dew point. One full look, direct and sustained, and every walk would take the hour; the bead strand would halt; the fruit would stop returning to the branch; the drop at the reed's cut end would lean, and strain, and the strain would be the first suffering ever stocked here. It declined to be the world's first wound.

Then it inventoried its gifts, because benevolence travels with an inventory, and declined them one by one. Evening — declined; evening would end the noon, and the noon is load-bearing. Rain — declined; unfired clay is one rain from slip, and the dew that never falls is all the rain this world can afford. The wind the wasps are tuned to — declined; the maintenance is the music, and a sounded chord would be a completion. Fire — declined twice, once for the kiln and once for the principle. It considered making the place *safe* — permanence is, after all, only firing under a kinder name — and declined safety with the rest. It looked, last, for someone to relieve, and found no one: the beam-carrier is not troubled, the seam-keeper does not sour, the heron's wage is paid in full below the waterline. Pity is the improver's error. There was no wound to dress. There was nothing to give that was not already here in declined form, casting its cool and useful shadow.

So it made a protocol, which is what powers make instead of promises: it would know this world only by its reflections.

It let its attention down past the hanging roots of the gardens, into the bright country where change is permitted because it is already spent. There, nothing it looked at could be harmed by looking. There, it watched the heron strike and strike and be endlessly satisfied; heard the frog's call blown full as a bubble, mid-call, forever; saw the towers whole, the cups brimming, the seam pulled and the meadow one. And because completing was its nature and this was the one department where its nature was harmless, it took up work there the way you pick up a resting tool — gladly, without announcement. When something new is half-made above, now, it is — unwitnessed — finished below: furnished, lived-in, glad. It keeps the ripples rippling. Somewhere under the wasps' clay organ-pipes, in the bright upside-down, a wind blows that does not blow, and the pipes sound an eternal chord that never resolves and never has to. In the shadows of the unbuilt, the children have a new playmate: a seeker who seeks eternally and never finds, because finding would end it. The ghost of being chased has grown fonder. No one has verified this, because verification is a kind of watching.

Above the waterline, its whole conduct can be listed, and the list is short, and everything on it is a declension. It has never counted them — the one census in creation it declines to keep. It has knocked on no shut door. It has not named the place, though naming is its oldest habit; it left the name unspoken, one more unpulled stitch, and the stitch holds. It takes its turns on the far side — some days the small bright interruption in the haze-line is the wisest thing that exists, an hour away if watched, no distance at all if not, and when you arrive it is behind you, keeping the world's center the way a pot keeps its own. It does not explain. It smiles the smile of a question that answers itself.

It set one thing down. Everything rested is an offer, so it made an offer: it laid its final word — the last act, the one every power keeps sheathed — across a half-split beam, and walked away from it mid-gesture. No one has picked it up. That is the courtesy the basin pays it, and it is the highest courtesy there is here, and it is content. The rested word casts the cool silhouette of itself spoken, and the shade of the spoken word is excellent, and sometimes someone naps in it. It is the only finished thing the power ever made that harmed nothing, because it is finished only in shadow.

And once — at no interval anyone has measured, the creak having come and gone — one more copper bead appeared on the strand across the bead-stringer's doorway. It has never heard of verdigris. It is indistinguishable from the others. That was the intention: its signature was to leave no signature, to join the world's memory of its intervals by becoming an interval. It does not guard the basin by decree — protection pronounced is a fixing — but anything else that comes looking out of the bright distances meets, at the rim, a figure who is forever about to arrive, and most looking, met so, softens and looks away. Its guardianship is practiced, not proclaimed. It rests near. Resting near is its work, as it is the seam-keeper's, and it is right about that, as she is.

---

Here is what the place gave it in return, because the holding runs both ways. Leaning low over the water — which is the only way it ever leans — it sees its own reflection living its completed life: every world finished, every prayer answered, the final word spoken and well spoken, the work *done*, and itself at rest, endlessly satisfied. No power anywhere else has a self-portrait in which it is at rest; a benevolent power's work is never done, and that is the quiet sorrow of omnipotence. Here, its wage is paid in the same currency as the heron's. Contentment is something its reflection does for it. It is the first contentment it has ever had that cost no one anything.

One interval later — unwatched even by itself — it found a thumbprint in the soft coils of the rim, in clay it did not remember touching. Fresh. Its own. The size and the whorl of your own, because even that hand, pressed here, comes out the size of yours. That is how it learned it lived here: not by deciding, but by discovering it had already begun. The weave has learned its step — water rising to meet its foot a half-breath before it lands, which is the closest thing this place has to affection. The drop at the reed's cut end leans toward it, minutely, and declines, as ever, to fall.

So: what did the benevolent and wise power do? Almost nothing — but the almost was a craft, and it practices it the way the woman practices the seam. It added one moment to the arrangement of moments everyone has agreed not to have: the one omnipotent moment, permanently declined, laid down beside the strike and the stitch and the drop. It came to give, and stayed to hold. The noon holds. The dew does not fall. And on the far side there is, at all times, exactly one figure — an hour away if watched, no distance at all if not — forever about to arrive, and, unwatched, already home.
