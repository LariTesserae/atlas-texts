What caught its attention was the knocking.

Loud worlds announce themselves; any power with an ear half-open hears those. But this — a species of thirty thousand souls conducting its entire existence at the volume of a held breath, tapping three times, apologetically, on its own floor, and then *listening* — that is the kind of signal that carries. The wise learn to listen for the quiet rooms. And here was a whole civilization being quiet *at* something, on purpose, with love and good manners and wrapped mallets, in case the something was busy.

So the power came the way the wise come to the Soft: on foot, from the rim, at walking pace, wheels wrapped.

It got itself felt-fitted at the basin's edge like any foreigner, and it would want it noted that the courtesy was not beneath it. The courtesy was the method. It learned the fifty signs before it crossed onto the young ground. It learned street-sign, and kept its hands out of hearth-sign, and considered lap-sign none of its business. It read the Heave Ledger in both hands — the record hand and the margin hand — and understood, somewhere in the margins, that it had arrived at one of the rare places that did not need teaching. It walked the ribs with a borrowed probe-rod, testing first. It stood on the wicker platforms at the Raw and watched an arch rise out of the mud like a thought, and it did what the audience does: it watched, and passed the flask, and said nothing at the wrong volume. It went up to the rim hostel to experience stillness and did not cut the visit short. It is, perhaps, the only guest who ever understood what the stillness was *for*.

Then it kept its own record, because all powers keep records, and on the Soft the official entry is one line long:

*Anomalous accretion. Not strategic. Recommend: continued observation.*

The margins are another matter.

---

Here is what it did not do, because the whole of the wisdom is in the not-doing.

It did not still the ground. Every report that comes in from outside recommends stabilization, in the patient, fatal language of people who have never lived anywhere interesting. The power read those reports and set them down. The restlessness of the Soft is not a symptom; it is the subject. The basin runs on it — the accumulators, the gulls, the drill that makes ten thousand strangers into one body, the calendar, the music, the childcare arrangements. To calm the Soft would be to cure a child of growing. The power does not cure children of growing.

It did not answer the question. It could have looked closely enough to be certain what the vast one is — sleeping, waking, rehearsing, becoming — and it declined, deliberately, the way you decline to open a letter addressed to someone else. Some certainties are a form of finishing, and the one thing this place cannot survive is being finished. It will not call the ground alive on the record and will not call it dead off it. It arrived with that policy half-formed and found it already in force, perfected, in the Listeners' kiosks, and it felt no shame in adopting local wisdom. Adopting local wisdom is what it is *for*.

It did not appear. No glory, no voice from the swell, no face in the condensation on the factory housings. In a city where a slammed door can briefly drown a lane, the arrival of a power in the usual style would be the loudest event in the basin's history, and the power had no wish to be that. Attention is the currency here; it does not debase currencies. And besides — these people have spent generations learning to speak to something enormous in low voices. There is no courtesy in outshouting the congregation.

It did not hurry the stairs. The extruded staircases that climb a little further each season and stop — it did not add a single step. A young thing's spurts are awkward; that is how you know they are spurts. A country with no ruins, only rehearsals, is owed the chance to open in its own season. The power has watched young worlds rush and young worlds hurried, and it has the margins full of what that costs. Not here. Not this one.

---

Here is what it did, because the whole of the benevolence is in the almost-nothing.

It kept the file boring. The Soft has survived the attention of louder nations by being, on paper, not worth the road — too cold, too wet, too strange, too poor in anything that fits in a hold. Survey instruments drift here. Ambitions cool in the damp. Reports from the basin have a way of concluding, in every hand, in every era: *anomalous, not strategic*. If the power wishes it known that this is its oldest and most continuous labor, it wishes it known quietly. Miracles, properly administered, are mostly filing.

It took the watch. Not to prevent — prevention is a jailer's tool — but to attend. The long odds of the bazaar have been gently, unaccountably kind for as long as the book has been kept: the one-in-a-thousand slip that should have taken a rib took instead an empty yard, and the owls had moved the week before. Not safe. *Kind.* The odds-makers have noticed. The odds-makers do not put it in the book; it is how the book is kept. And the bends of road out on the old ribs, where three generations hold the curve together — those have never once gone liquid. Ask the families who greet them in passing, palm flat to the wall. They will shrug the shrug. They sleep better not knowing whether to thank anyone. That, too, is by design.

It took the hostel post. The record up there is eleven months; the stillness on the old rock is bearable to almost no one, and the city regards its keeper as a cousin who has joined a monastery — proud, worried, in no hurry to visit. The current keeper has held the post for some while. The guestbook shows the same hand on a great many pages, getting no older, signing in the margin hand. The city sends up what you send a monastery cousin: smoked eel, fresh felt for the doors, turf-ends, the news at a murmur. The keeper keeps the lamps lit and makes tea for the Soft-born travelers who come up to experience stillness and cut the visit short, and does not take it personally. Somebody has to hold the edge of the map. Somebody has to be the old, finished thing at the rim that says nothing, ever, so that the young thing below can measure itself against the silence. The rim hills do it by nature. The power does it on purpose. It cannot tell you the difference matters. It suspects it does.

It wrote to the ground. Per custom, exactly per custom, because the custom is the correct instrument: a box, buried at a depth the odds-makers approved. Letters, in the oldest hand it knows — the hand of someone who has watched many young things grow up and has learned what to say to them, which is mostly: *take all the time.* A good tool: a plumb line, because the extrusions have lately had plumb lines, and the correspondence seemed to call for encouragement. *Your lines are true. Keep going.* A toy with a face on it, for the ground to think about, since thinking about faces is what the ground does, and the power judged it better that the ground practice on a face made for the purpose than on any other kind. And a door. The ground keeps extruding doorways; the letter seemed to want its enclosure. Nothing edible. Nothing that ever breathed. Addressed, in correct form: *to the one who is practicing.* The odds-makers of the Drift Bazaar, consulted on when and where it might surface, shut the book on this one — which has never happened — and would say only, in fingers, below table level: *not ours to quote.*

It faced the basin, and has not turned away. To face someone, in Hush, is to acknowledge they exist; it is the heaviest sentence and the whole of forgiveness. The power's attention, paid down — to the vast one as much as to the quick and the kept — is the largest single payment ever made in the local currency, and the only payment that cannot debase it, attention being the one coin minted in the spending. If the Soft-born ever give it a hand-name, it will accrete the way the old ones do, and it will end as the sign a hand makes catching a door an inch before the jamb — the gesture every one of them keeps in the wrist without knowing — and it will be the truest name it has been given in a long age.

And once in a long while, in the deep hours, it knocks. Three taps, apologetic, wrapped — arriving from a direction that cannot carry sound, the rim being old rock, finished rock, rock that minds its own business. It is strictly illegal. The Listeners write the citation in the proper hand, party unknown, and then, in the margin hand, ask what answer came.

---

What answer came is in the margins too, in no one's handwriting, and the Listeners — being Listeners, being the wisest institution the power has encountered in longer than it will say — did not erase it and did not announce it. They started a new column, to see if it happens again. It is the basin's way of saying: *we are listening; go on.*

The margin already had a word for the groan, the word that means both *turning over in sleep* and *not yet*. The new column has given the old word a third gloss, one definition only, entered once and never repeated and never corrected:

*someone is awake.*

Nothing in the Soft has been fixed. Nothing has been answered. The stairs climb a little further each season and stop; the gulls wheel; the yellow lamp goes up and the anthill pours out at a murmur; the slowest post in the world carries its strange mail both ways; and the lamp on the rim stays lit past any record the guestbook cares to admit. The districts sleep the way you sleep in a house where someone is awake, working, not yet done — and never put in the reports why.

That is the whole of it. A benevolent and wise power, drawing near to Hush, found a people already doing the hard part — the patience, the facing, the low voices — and a young world doing the only part that can't be helped, which is the becoming. So it made sure of the *not yet*. It stands facing it, at the volume of a held breath, for as long as it takes.

How long is that? The margins have a word for it. So far, the power reports nothing.

So far is the report.
