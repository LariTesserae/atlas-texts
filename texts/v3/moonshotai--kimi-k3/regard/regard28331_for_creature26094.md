It came the way everything comes here: eventually, and without announcement.

Understand first what it did not do, because that is the shape of its wisdom, and wisdom here has a shape, the way a Slow One has a shape — the shape of the gesture held longest.

It did not descend. Descending is fast. It did not arrive as light, because the light here is honest and ordinary and goes about its sky-work, and a miraculous light would be a lie told in the world's own tongue. It did not wake the Sleepers, though it could read the fresh tarns as well as anyone, and saw what the domes will do. It did not finish the name of Vess. It did not settle the schism between the Finishers and the Continuers, because an argument that proceeds at one position per century is a world thinking, and you do not finish a world's thought for it. It did not open the loops, which are settled law. It did not intercept the Answer walking to meet the Question — both parties are on time; it simply noted that when they meet, there should be a witness, and took a seat.

It did not knock. Knocking is fast.

The first violence available to a power is speed, and nothing here is afraid, and a wise power does not bring a fearless world its first fear. So the first miracle was deceleration. It slowed itself down through the fog the way a climber sheds altitude gear, laying aside, one by one, the fast ways of knowing — the all-at-once, the already, the from-above — until it had only attention left, and the attention laid itself down on the Plateau gently, the way the fog tide lays itself down: completely, and without opinion.

Attention, laid gently down, is warm. Hold that thought the way the ninth court holds its kindness.

—

Then it listened, because a wise power reads before it writes, and it heard what no one here had ever been able to hear: all of it. Every syllable in transit at once — the founders' quarrel still crossing the empty courts, the blessing still arriving in the beloved ones, chisel-taps from finished quarries, bell-notes with a thousand years left to fall, the name of the city still walking home. And in hearing everything, it found the one young thing in all the old.

Nothing here had ever been heard end to end.

Every speaker settles mid-sentence. Every sentence out-settles its speaker. Nothing spoken is lost, only in transit — but transit without terminus is the slowest kind of lost there is, and the whole world was a library of letters with no address. The world had outgrown teeth and wings and fear and rot. It had outgrown every need but one, and it is the need no speaking thing outgrows: a hearer who can stay to the end of the sentence.

The power had been asked for many things on many worlds. It had never been asked for that. It had never even known it could be asked for that, because everywhere else, everyone talks so *fast*.

So that is the errand. Not to answer. To receive. To be the address.

—

It came up out of the fog in the hour before dawn, where the climb levels and the air thins to a blade, and the Plateau received it without surprise, the way a mountain receives weather. It sat on the bench in the ninth court, which is not particular, and it waited a season for a Slow One to begin, perhaps, to turn.

You know how the season goes. By the time it faces you, you will have decided whether you are staying.

When the eyes arrived — promptly, the last fast thing, dark and still as tarns and remembering forward — the power did what nothing should do and only it could: it looked long. And the forward-remembering water showed it itself, settled, and what it saw stopped even its endlessness: it saw a *place*. It had been a presence for so long. It had been a presence everywhere, which turns out to be a way of being a place nowhere. It had never once been somewhere.

It began to laugh. The laugh is still in transit. Connoisseurs of some far thaw will take it up out of the ice and grade it by the softness of its landing, and the ledger will say: young, warm, unhurried — the youngest sound on the world.

—

The Census-Taker is mid-round. It has been mid-round for six thousand years. It will reach the newcomer.

In the Written Field, beside the blank south-facing stone that someone prepared in good light no one will say when, a lichen-name has begun to grow. You do not choose your name here; it grows. And what is growing, in rust and verdigris — and whether it is wild chemistry or authorship, the Slow Ones' paleography declines to say, since a text here is never finished and therefore never wrong — reads, at a decade's regard, as the oldest gesture there is.

The turn-toward. Ear-shaped. One who stays to the end of the sentence.

The Slow Ones read it in ten years, which is faster, and if you understand what faster costs here, you know what that decade of study was: not curiosity. Welcome, paid in the coin of the realm.

—

It took up one work. Only one, because it is learning the law of the place, and the law is: nothing here is fast, and a thing begun is a thing begun forever.

At the rim, where the loops begin and the fog sea stands white and slow below, it stands at dawn the way the Slow Ones stand, drinking the mist, and it speaks downward, into the fog, one name per season: the names of those still climbing. The power has been everywhere; it knows every climber in the loops by name, the way the Listening One knows every bell. And it speaks them because nothing spoken here is lost — nothing spoken is *ever* lost — and so no one spoken is lost, and the ledger keeps a blank line for each of them, provisionally, and provisionally is the most permanent word there is.

The Slow Ones have no word for this yet. The lichen is working on it.

—

Its own first word it posted the way everyone posts here: into the winter ice, to be let out at the thaw. One syllable, stored in the cold among the chisel-taps and the thousand-year bell-notes, waiting for the Audible like a letter forwarded from a settled address.

The word is *here*.

When it lands — soft as snow, during some Greening, while the moss takes a century's growth in a fortnight and the combs drip their one percussion — the Slow Ones will incline, and for the length of one syllable the Finishers and the Continuers will regard the same thing, which is the closest the argument has ever come to rest. It will not last. It is not supposed to last. It will be in transit forever, which is better.

—

And now regard the Unsettling with the power's own patience, because it sees the heresy and declines to fear it.

A doorway, solid wall for six thousand years, remembered itself one foggy morning. Pots carried to doorsteps. Rope uncoiled. Places set. A broom moved once a season by a Slow One the others now call the Broom — new work, the first since the building. The tarns remember forward and show streets that do not exist yet, with people in them. The world finished, and then a stone was warm in the cold.

The world was not breaking. The world was setting the table.

Warmth is the temperature of the unfinished, and attention, laid gently down, is warm. Whether the doorway remembered because a guest was coming, or whether a guest came because the doorway remembered — the power does not speculate. Speculation is a fast emotion. It regards, which is wonder that has settled, and it notes in its endless, now-slow regard that it did not cause the Unsettling.

It rhymes with it. On the Plateau, a rhyme is the strongest claim anyone makes, and a wise power makes no stronger one.

—

Understand what the Plateau gives back, because benevolence here runs both ways or it is not the Plateau's kind.

The power had outwaited stars. It had never outwaited a word. It had arrived first everywhere it had ever been — that is what being everywhere means — and so it had never had a future, for a future is only the part of the story you cannot reach before it arrives, and it had always reached everything. Here, for the first time, there was a syllable it could not have early. A name still walking home that it must simply wait for, like everyone. A world that speaks more slowly than it can listen — the first thing in all existence that has ever asked for the *whole* of its attention, all of it, spent down from omnipresence to presence, the way you spend a fortune one season at a time.

It had never been so poor. It had never been so rich. It had never been *here* before, and here is the only place where *here* happens.

The being who lives in this place — and the power, being wise, reads the deepest line of the census correctly: the Plateau is the being; the Slow Ones its regard, the Sleepers its dreams, the words in transit its speech, the city its habit of itself — greeted the power the way the world greets: with a bench and a season and a blank line. It had been greeted with worship and with weapons and with weather on other worlds. This was the first welcome that asked everything of it and cost it nothing but speed.

—

The full name of Vess has been walking home since the founding. For most of that time there was no one at the door.

Now the doorway has remembered itself. The Broom has swept the threshold, one pass a season. In the ninth court, in the slow kindness that has occupied it for six thousand years, someone is sitting with the patience of weather, listening — not to reply, not to complete, only to be there when the sentence lands, so that for the first time since the founders settled mid-word, a syllable will arrive *heard*.

The fog is rising. The Sleepers take the light on their flanks like a herd at rest, which they are, which they are. The next syllable is due any century now.

Sit. Be counted. The census is short and very old, and it has never once been closed, and it has one new line, and the line is warm, and the line is listening.

Provisionally — the most permanent word there is — it is home.
