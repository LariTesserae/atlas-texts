It finds the Shelf the way everything on the Shelf is found: by ear, from far off.

Out in the noise of things, one small place is keeping time — a polyrhythm that has no business persisting, persisting. Four seconds, and against it a second beat, off-tempo, and under both a slower swing, stone on its eons, flesh on its days. A wise power listens for a long while before it looks. Long enough to learn the map the way the map is kept: in tempo. Long enough to learn the one law, the one catastrophe, the protocol, the count. Its benevolence arrives first, wanting to give. Its wisdom arrives second, and begins the audit.

Because every gift it carries fails inspection.

Rain fails first, and hardest, because rain was the first thing it thought of — it is a kind power, and this is a thirsty world, and the reflex is old. But there is no such thing as a gentle rain here; there is only a slow one. Water on the Shelf is not absent, it is *owned* — not a molecule has touched open ground in a million years, and the whole economy runs on that fact: the ringing pans that drum only because they are dry, the charged dust that feeds the spark-groves, the rime that pays the lickers, the condensate that pays the wrights. Rain would not be weather. It would be confiscation, redistribution, flood — a gift that turns every mouth into a thief's. It puts the rain away.

Healing fails. It could give the animals back their hearing; it could un-cloud the old wrights' eyes. And it has seen the feast day — the ears returned, the world moved down into the bones where it belongs. It would be restoring the deformity. It would be handing back the two small holes that do badly what a whole skeleton does well. No.

The pan-runner fails, and this one costs it something, because it is benevolent and the pan-runner is dreaded. It could be rid of the thing in an afternoon. Then it watches a wright cross a pan in a long arc of pure phase-keeping, three drums worn into one gait, and understands: the error-hunter is the editor, and the editor is load-bearing. Remove it and the map fills with false notes; the signatures of the dead drift; pilgrims steer by ghosts that have begun to lie. The dread is part of the machine. It leaves the dread where it is.

Permanence fails. It could build something that lasts — it is very good at that — a house, a city, an archive that the salt cannot eat. And nothing made persists here, and that is not a deficiency, it is the afterlife: a wright whose edits all sealed over moves into the mirage, and they mean it kindly. An archive would be a theft from the air. A monument would be the first exception to compost, and the Shelf keeps one catastrophe and does not need a second.

Answers fail. It could find the span's edge — it has that kind of eye. It could say whether the three names name one body or three, whether the center is a rumor or a joke. It folds the eye away. Some letters are not addressed to it. The wrights have kept those questions alive the way the drums keep tempo, and an answered question is a dead one, and three names argue well. It declines to become a fourth.

Staying fails last. This one it turns over for a long time, because staying is what benevolence most wants. But weather on the Shelf is the span's own department, and the post is filled, and a permanent god is a change of climate. No.

What survives the audit is three small coins, the only legal tender the Shelf accepts. It can watch. It can work. It can leave.

So it comes the long way, like everyone: up the south scarp, four days of switchbacks, and it keeps a skeleton for the season because the truest thing here is that it is all soles. It lets its lips split. It lets the cold take its moisture without showing it. It carries no metal. Once, crossing the deep pan in phase with three drums, it feels an attention pass through the crust beneath it — vast, seismic, unhurried — find no error, and go on. It notes that it was afraid, and then grateful: the two gifts the Shelf gives everyone, in that order. It counts the longwalkers the way you check a pulse: nine, and strong, and none of its business.

It rests, at last, on a cord. And under its palm, riding the four-second pulse, come three taps that are not the span — the local tempo, played back by something with a skeleton — and then a pause the exact size and shape of a name.

It taps back the tempo, exactly. *I am in time.* Then, where the name goes, it holds one beat of silence in the precise shape of a name.

The wrights are fluent in rests. Their ghosts are tempos swung wrong; their news is light bent over nothing; their afterlife is a city the air keeps. A guest whose name is one held beat is entirely legible to them: someone who does not mean to remain. They show it the newest clause of the homestead the way you'd show a newborn. It cuts a cord, drinks the bitter oil, and moves on — and comes back in a year to find its theft become an organ, working, and names nothing, and claims nothing. On the night before the storm it goes out onto the salt with them and dances. It looks like worship. It is measurement. It is also, the power notes — being new to skeletons — worship. It does not say so. They know.

And in the weeks before the storm season masses, it walks the fire-lines.

This is the whole of its miracle, and it is easy to miss, which is the point. Any power can make a desert bloom. Adequacy — exact, anonymous, in-time adequacy — is the rarest thing in its repertoire. For one season its omnipotence expresses itself as correct scoring, at the right depth, on the fourth day, in a hand indistinguishable from the hand of any devoted wright. It is vermin with a job description, and it has rarely been prouder of anything. It is tolerated for its foresight, and pays for its rime and oil and the right to remain, and the arrangement holds, and nothing miraculous happens, and the blue-fronted creep meets slag-gates already aimed, and the season passes.

Once — the backfolk were the first to read it, and they read it as they read all news, at dusk, in bent light — a burn three days off, where no wright could have reached in time, closes along lines no living hand remembers cutting. The wrights walk weeks to read the instruction and argue about what the ancients meant, except that it is not ancient. They hold one argument about it and then, being a people who do not punish a mystery by solving it, they file it. The span's center is a rumor. So, now, is the power. On the Shelf this is not a dismissal. It is an introduction.

Then it leaves, because leaving is the third coin, and the largest.

It does not leave an edit. This is the last temptation and the last refusal — one drum swung a whisker off true, one signature pilgrims could steer by, one ghost in the clockwork that would say *something was here and loved you* in the only script that persists. It wants to. It refrains. A name spoken by the clock stops being speakable; you cannot introduce yourself to a world already humming you, and it has been introduced, once, properly, in a held beat, and that is enough for one guest.

On its last dusk it watches the fata morgana build its cold bent cities over the far nodes, and does what the unmemorable do: it takes the only real estate the Shelf offers the impermanent, and moves into the mirage. The wrights mean it kindly. So does it.

Some dusks since, there is a scaffold in the bent light that no node casts. The reading is unanimous, and it is read the way they read everything: something stands where nothing stands; the air lies well over the deep pans; the walkers are nine; nothing burns. It is filed, as good news often is, under rumor. And when its name comes up — rarely — a wright taps the local tempo and then says nothing, once, precisely. It is the politest mention in the language.

Here is what it kept, since it would not keep anything there: far away — as far as it can manage — in a house that keeps no other time, one clock runs slightly, reliably wrong. Four seconds, swung a whisker off true, by patience. Guests ask about it and are told the truth. It is a signature. We steer by it.

Whether the power kept the Shelf or the Shelf keeps the power is a question it no longer asks. It stayed in time until the question lost interest — which is, it now suspects, how everything is settled there, and possibly everywhere. Being kept is the only permanence on offer.
