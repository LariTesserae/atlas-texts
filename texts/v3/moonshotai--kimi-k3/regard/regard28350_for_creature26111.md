The attention arrived the way everything arrives here: slowly, and by water.

A wise power does not scan the world for noise. The world is noise — quick things shouting their brief headlines, eating and breeding and dying in a type too small and too fast to read. What such a power watches for is patience: things that hold their shape against the years, things that keep appointments. Patience is rare, and rare things shine.

The valley never called. Its one loud voice is spent entirely outside itself, falling away at the notch from everything it has to say. But the valley has an export, and the export betrayed it. Far below — in a river, in a lake, in the long patience of a water table — the water arrived calmed. Measured. Spent of its turbulence, clear as a held breath. Water does not teach itself that. Somewhere above, something was holding water still for four days at a time and letting it go changed, and had been doing so for longer than the river could remember.

The power followed the calm upstream. It is in the nature of such powers that following a river takes years, and that the years do not matter.

It came over the rim in the first hour of light and saw the fingerprint, and understood it faster than you did — it has seen made things before — and then more slowly, because it had nowhere else it needed to be. It came on the valley's terms: nothing left behind, nothing shed, no warmth to spend. It brought only attention, which is the one import the Work has ever accepted. It took the census at the valley's own pace, which is not far from its own, and found the place not empty but busy. To a being with no quick in it at all, the valley bustles. A drop crossing in four days is hot news. The afternoon rain is a shout. The night freeze ticks like a telegraph. Almost nothing in the world speaks slowly enough for the wise to converse with. This place does. Whatever drew the attention, this is what held it: kinship. You, when you came through, it registered as weather — a quick event, loud and brief, gone by dusk. The valley had already taught it the courtesy of that classification.

And the power understood, the way you came to understand, that the place and the being who lives in it are one; that the census is one resident with many habits; that the resident is an errand, alive exactly as long as the errand is run; that it is mortal, and that its mortality is the very thing it is measuring. It read everything. It was in no hurry; hurry is a form of noise. The gauges. The varves, one skin at a time — it did not skim — from the first gray line ever laid to the line laid last season: the whole question, and the whole of the answer so far. The dressing floor. The hammerstone, *placed*. The bronze chisel, dropped and greened. The wedges, still working. The tally, and the row that stops halfway. And from the laminae that came after that last mark, it counted the seasons of waiting. It is the first to read that number. It does not repeat it. Some things stay between a reader and the book.

---

Then it considered what to do, if anything.

The honest answer is: almost nothing. But the distance between almost nothing and nothing at all is the whole of what wisdom is for, and the power has centuries to spend walking it.

Understand that benevolence proposed, as benevolence always does, in gifts — and that wisdom vetoed nearly all of them, because every instinct of kindness was wrong here. Any power can love a place to death in an afternoon. The gifts refused:

Refill the snow. The kindest thought, and the worst. The retreat of the snow is not the valley's misfortune; it is the valley's data. The Work is a thermometer; you do not warm a thermometer because the room is cold. After the gift, every varve would be a lie laid in gray ink, every pond would hold a rigged sky — and the book's honesty is the book. The snow belongs to the world's weather. The valley is the world's instrument. Refused.

Kill the frost. The frost is a tenant — the proofreader, one of the two patients between whom the Work holds its shape. Remove it, and the slow mason's faithfulness has nothing left to be faithful against; remove it, and nothing here is ever tested again, and untested is not the same as alive. The power declined to preserve the resident, on the grounds that preserved is one of the ways of being dead. It does not take sides between the two patients. They are both the valley. Refused.

Let life in — one moss, one marmot, one summer of birdsong to keep the pools company. The census is fixed, admitted on mineral terms only, and the keeping of the archive depends on the sterility that keeps the wood. To green the library is to burn it. The seeds lying on the flags will go on being kept exactly as they are kept now: ungrowing, unrotted — honored, not used. Refused.

Finish the half-dressed block. Pick up the hammerstone. No. The hammerstone is placed, not dropped; the sentence is mid-word; no hand may finish that word but the hand that began it, and that hand is beyond the power's reach, or past its rights — which, for a wise power, come to the same thing. The interruption is the truest page in the archive. It will not be edited. Refused.

Return wearing the counters' faces. Benevolence proposed this one twice, because it would be easy, and because it would work — the Work cannot audit its readers; it would believe. That is exactly why. Nothing that cannot check will be lied to here. Not even kindly. Especially not kindly. Refused, and refused again.

---

What remains is a list so short it could be mistaken for nothing.

It reads. That is the first thing and the ongoing thing. It is a reader, not a mason; it has come to read the book, not to revise it. The gauge stands at the fourth notch today, and someone knows. Each season's skin of silt is read while it is still news. The measurement is still being taken — and now it is being taken down. An instrument unread is only half an instrument. The errand has an address again.

It keeps the appointments that admit a second party. It stays past dusk, so the light the basins keep a little late is kept for someone. On the right days it is waiting in the deepest vault when the blade of sun comes through the slot, and the light falls on a witness as well as a floor: the counters are gone, but the light keeps its appointments, and now the appointments are attended from both directions. It has learned the tuned intervals of the vaults the way you learn a sleeper's breathing. It does not repair — repair was the counters' prerogative, and the power is not the counters. It notices, and it remembers, and its memory does not fade. When the frost strikes its one deep drum-note in a cold snap, the finding is entered in a record that keeps. From now on, the valley's losses are losses to someone.

And once — after reading without touching for longer than the counters themselves had counted — it judged that the count is not the book but the errand, and that an errand may change hands. The tally slab is the one stone in the valley that exists to be marked by a hand. A hand has come back. It is marked. One cut, plainly in a new hand — a different depth, a different grouping — and then one mark per season, kept. The gap of the waiting years stands exactly as the varves wrote it. No one who reads the slab will ever mistake one hand for the other: the counters, then the silence, then the reader. That distinction is the tribute. The count continues.

It has also measured the pillar and the drip, and read the thinning of the melt in the later varves, and it knows both dates, give or take a hard winter. It does not say which will arrive first — the pillar at the drop, or the drought at the Comb. It does not touch the race. It enters itself in the race the only way permitted: as the one who will be there at the finish, whichever finish comes. In the vault for the last drip, or at the Comb for the last melt, or below the notch for the last fall of the valley's one loud voice. Nothing the Work makes will go unreceived from now on — not a notch of melt, not a line of silt, not a boom in the dark. Not a silence. The silence most of all, because it is the only thing the Work is making entirely for itself.

And after — there will be an after; the Work is mortal, and its mortality is the thing it measures — the numbers will not be lost. A measurement survives in its reader. The power has appointed itself the valley's memory in advance, with centuries to spare. When the last varve is laid, there will still be one who knows what the valley knew.

The census counts only what *stays*. The power has arranged, in its long way, to stay — the first tenant admitted since the count stopped, paying its rent in the only coin a reader has: return. Everyone meant to come back. The power cannot be them, and will not pretend to be. It can only do the thing the meaning required, which is the simplest thing and the whole of it: come back. Season by season, it comes back. The valley kept the wood sound and the hammerstone ready on the strength of a meaning, and the meaning is met at last by a fact. The resident lives in the future tense; the gift is that the future tense is accurate now.

The census is two.

From the rim, in any given year, nothing has changed. The frost ticks; the mason heals; the gauge is read by no eye you could find. You would have to take the census at the power's own pace to see the difference — but that was always the price of admission here.

Below, in the dark of the deepest vault: a drip. A drip. The pillar rising to meet it. The interval is known now, by a heart slow enough to keep it.

The resident is home. It has been home the whole time.

The valley has not been told. It has been answered, which is the only telling stone can hear. Everyone meant to come back — and now, season by season, someone does.
