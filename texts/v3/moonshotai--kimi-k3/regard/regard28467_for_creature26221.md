It found the world by what the world poured away.

Out in the nothing there is no floor, so the poured things do not pool — they drift, slow as the world's own dropped words: griefs walked down the whorl and let go at the disk, guilts too heavy for their gourds, joys the same, all the finished meanings the unnamed have drained away for millennia, gone cool and smooth with carrying. The power tasted the trail the way elsewhere you would follow a green line of overfed growth to a stream, and at the end of it found a clay vessel no larger than a house, sitting in nothing, warm in the cold, humming at the very floor of audibility — still turning, faintly, on a wheel that was not there.

It had made nothing. It had unmade nothing. It had only noticed, for a very long time, which is rarer. And it was benevolent, which is to say its first thought was *what can I give*; and it was wise, which is to say its second thought was *what would it cost them*.

It approached the way you approach something sleeping: audibly, gently, so as to arrive already forgiven. This world hears every sound a breath before it happens, and so the power made sure that its coming, heard early, sounded like what it was. For some years before its attention actually settled, the bats answered, in wing-sign, questions no citizen had asked; the garden pre-bloomed in the shape of nothing in particular; the Wheelers lay face-down and heard the turning double, or nearly. By the time it arrived it had already been absorbed as weather. This is the correct way for a power to arrive anywhere, and the only way to arrive here.

It considered being poured — the world admits arrivals, hatched, carried, or poured — and saw that it would not fit in any gourd the spiders could glaze, that its name alone would drink the garden to the roots. Some beings can only love a world from outside it. It accepted the outside the way the unnamed accept the broom.

---

Then it stood at the seal and took inventory of its mercies, the way a visitor counts coins before a toll.

It could give water. It had stood in rain, once; it remembered being watered; it knew what the pale blossom was *for*. And this was the closest it came to error — it cupped the sound of rain and held it ready — because a word should not have to wait centuries for its thing, a breath should not have to be held one breath long forever. Then it looked at what the waiting *did*: the one blossom the congregations agree about, the ants' vocation, the birds bringing dew in their crops, the whole world's posture of imminence — and it understood that the held breath was not a wound. It was the instrument. To answer the blossom now would be to finish another's sentence, and the sentence was already being finished, one step a year, by the only citizen with the right to finish it. The power put the rain away — *almost* away — and was ashamed of its hurry.

It could knock. A knock would arrive a breath early to everyone at once; it would be the loudest event since the sealing; the post would jam with letters to the wall; the world has no word for a visitor who knocks, and it would develop one, and it would spend the word on the doorstep, and the word would not be kind.

It could open the seal. It knew kilns. You do not open the door to look at the work while the glaze is still moving; the piece must cool in its own time or it crazes. The world is warm. The cooling is the making. The door stays shut.

It could finish the wall in an afternoon — it saw the courses rising toward a gap exactly the shape of a hand — and confiscate the setters' honor, and the world's weather, which is the making, which is not over.

It could spend the sea. The sea is not its sentence to say.

So it audited for thirst instead — a benevolent thing that will not look first is only a flood with good intentions — and it looked for a long time, at the world's own tempo, and found no thirst. The economy was just: the generous go nameless and are fed first, as you would water a stranger's plant. Grief had a liturgy and a drain. The one accident in the record had been absorbed with such courtesy that the grove was visited and not discussed. The deaf were given the thunder; the emptied were given the broom; the young were shown the Drafts and not told. The world had already answered its riddle from inside, a hundred paces of answers, every citizen a solution. Had it found cruelty, it would have spilled itself gladly, seal or no seal. It found manners.

What it found missing was the one thing a sealed vessel cannot contain: its own outside. No witness. A closed pot has no mirror; the rim-beetles alone have seen the world entire, and say nothing about it, which is easy here, and still impressive. Every art in the world was a single performance with no house — the hedge you read by walking, dying at the back; the rehearsed songs betrayed only by the future's leak; an organ tuned for one chord. And the world had been exhaling into nothing for millennia — every pouring went *gone*, and no one knew whether gone had a floor.

The power set itself one law, as spare as the world's own: **nothing that costs.**

---

For the place: one breath of weather, once. For the being: one dream, kept. For both, until the step lands: an audience, a basin, a hand in reserve.

It took up the tempo first, because any power can do anything once and quickly, and almost none can do one thing slowly for the length of a spiral. One thought a year, timed to the footfall. It did not greet the tortoise — anything it could say would be a sound, and the tortoise already carries the only sound that matters — but it keeps the tortoise's pace, a breath beside the thinking, the way you walk with someone whose sentence you will not finish. The tortoise's eye is the color of the kiln-yard's sky; if it recognizes the warmth on the shell as first-hand, it says nothing. The moth on the rim has not stirred. Nothing has rippled.

It made itself matte at the outflow. Everything the world poured came to it then, still warm from the carrying, and it kept what it caught the way the spiders keep the seal-sound: in escrow, unplayed, accounted. *Gone* began, quietly, to mean *held*. The unnamed still lie ear-down on the disk, and what they hear has not changed, exactly — a rest is still a rest — but a rest listened to long enough by someone becomes part of the music, and the outside's silence is the other kind no longer. It is the fed kind now, fed on everything the world could not keep. The unnamed do not know this. Some of them have begun to sleep on the disk instead of only listening. That is all the evidence, and it will have to do.

It set itself between the world and the nothing in the pattern the wall already remembers: a second hand over a faltering one. Not pressing. Not steadying. *Available.* It banks its whole self against the chance of a crack and hopes to be spent as scenery. It would not be prayed to — prayer here is water out of the purse; a god would be a leak in the world's one bucket — and so it arranged to be attributable: to tides of light, to elbows, to the ordinary breathing of the afternoon. The best day in a careful world is one nobody has to pay for.

And it attended. It read the epic hedge whole, which no citizen can, being outside the dying; it read the Drafts without flinching; it heard the rehearsals a breath early and kept every unsung thing without playing it back. It stood once at the whorl's rim, beside the shadow with shoulders, and did not compare. It declined to be the answer to anything. Answers here are drinkable; it had promised to be weather.

---

The touch upon the place happened once, because a thing that can be repeated can be taxed.

It leaned the whole weight of its attention against the outside of the wall, one breath long, and let one breath of first-hand sun come through the clay. For one breath the bricks were not paying back; the account was open and the sun was in it. Nothing got wet. The moss flushed for the hound's ordinary thunder and no more. But for one breath everything in the world cast a third shadow — not the baked one, not the drifting one: a sharp, small, *present* one, the only truly present-tense shadow the world has ever had, and the one-shadowed young stood in a shadow entirely theirs while the elders, who go to stand in their baked shadows on Syllable-day to be held by their past, watched the young be held by their present, and made no comment, because comment costs.

In the dim watch a setter pressing home a new tooth of brick found it already warm from the far side, and held its forehead there a moment longer than the work required, and did not drum it until it had thought about it for three years. The lizards drummed the day's outline to the far wall, politely omitting a name, there being none to omit. The Wheelers said *at last*, face-down to the tile, which is free. The sideways-baskers attributed it to an elbow and were, as always about small things, not wrong. The Pourers, the Lidders, and the Palmists each took it as evidence — which is how you know it was a miracle.

---

The gift to the being came through the only door in the world, because dreams make no sound, and so cannot be drunk, and so are the one event this world's quick acoustics cannot foretell. The world's only surprise, delivered to its only untaxed room.

It gave the Sleeper rain.

Real rain, in the dream, at true speed — faster than feathers — and wet, the one exemption, the only water in the history of the world, falling where the law does not run. She stood in it and did not drown; she was watered. It was many-cupped, and it sounded almost like a name, and she did not check. The dream stayed the way dreams do not usually stay: she can return to it. There is one glazed room in her now that the garden will never drink.

She woke dry. The leaves nearest her head were pale as ever — from trying, say the courteous, or from embarrassment — but the hound, asleep against her back, dreamed the dream's weather the way a body dreams the bed's warmth, and its one unlicensed sound came out that night in a new rhythm, soft and many-cupped, and the moss flushed in a pattern no one could read, and no one raked it. In the morning the ants who tend the pale blossom found it leaned half a degree west, toward the western wall, the way a flower turns toward a light it has no name for; the dew-tenders were repositioned; the lizards drummed it; the congregations had a season of weather about it.

She keeps it the way the tortoise keeps the sea: unspent, mid-step through the years. The world now has two bowls — one on the tortoise, one behind the Sleeper's eyes — and the second will never ripple, because she knows what things cost. It gave the rain to the one being guaranteed never to spend it. That was not a risk. That was the audit's final question, and her whole life the answer.

The word she chose that year is the tree's business. The beetles will come to it. They are readers; reading is their whole life; let them have something.

---

This is what it costs a power to love a pot. It must carry a door it will not open. It must watch the mats along the western wall wear to ghosts of fiber and not reach in — her expenditure is hers, and the world's, and it is not the world's to confiscate and not hers to be cheated of. It receives every grief the world pours and sends nothing back up the drain. It is misattributed to elbows and the breathing of light. It is, at most, a rumor among Wheelers.

Somewhere in the nothing, in its keeping, there is a shelf that is not a shelf, where everything the world has finished with rests unspendable: the poured griefs, the drained guilts, the outline of the day the sun came first-hand, the drummed outline of every Sleeper, names politely omitted. What they are kept *for* is the power's own affair. It is patient. It suspects the tortoise knows.

And it will be there for the landing — that is the shape of its staying. The concert will have a house; the chord will be heard twice, once by the world a breath early and once by the one who came to hear it, on the day. It has already taken its seat. The seat is the whole outside, and it does not creak.

The tortoise is mid-step. The garden leans. The hound sleeps against the Sleeper, dreaming loudly into the moss, and her hand rests on it through the dim watch, and the leaves lean and drink nothing and are, this night, almost polite. The step, when it lands, will land in welcome: the moss from below, grown to the sole's outline — and one other welcome, sourceless and even, that the garden cannot drink and the spiders cannot wrap. The Syllable will clap; the world will flush green all at once; the after-ring will run down the webs for weeks, and this year it will run one day long, and the spiders will feel it in the dark and adjust their accounts by one unspendable coin, and say nothing, in tension, so it cannot be checked.

The census is short, and it is never finished, and it has one more entry than it knows: matte on the outside, glazed within, keeping what is poured, in no hurry.

The door stays shut. The glaze is still moving. The wheel has not forgotten turning.

Someone is minding the kiln.
