The Swell came on its slow cycle, and for a season the galleries crescendoed, and the dust-figures jumped and re-formed nightly, and the drone leaned up out of the sinkholes until the salt pan hummed to itself in the cold nights — and something that was passing over the desert, high and unhurried, stopped.

It would be too much to call it a god, and not enough to call it a traveler. Call it a power. It had the means to refill the lake. It had the means to hang a second sun in the great hall, to still the hush forever, to unmake ending itself. It had done such things, or near enough, in its younger eons, in other places — and every one of them is entered in a book it keeps, and the entries ache. It had drowned kinder worlds than this one with gifts. What it had become since is what *benevolent and wise* means after the tuition is paid: it had become careful.

So its first act was to do nothing, at length, on purpose.

It stayed above the pan from one Swell to the next, listening. From above, the Underlake is nearly all rumor — a pressure in the crust, a rhythm in the cold air, the moth-traffic threading the chimneys at dusk — but patience, given enough of it, resolves rumor into structure the way the sleepers resolve a drop into a life. It learned the long sigh and what drives it. It learned the middle band and the song proper. It learned the clock of the pale pillar by the cooling that follows it. It learned the three small laws the way a careful guest learns the customs of a house by listening at the door — *lose nothing, hear everything, be counted* — and underneath them it heard the oldest entry in the ledger below: all the water is alive, and nowhere lies open. Which told it, before it ever descended, that its first instinct was wrong.

It had come expecting a world and its tenants. It found that the distinction had collapsed here eons ago. The gardens are one garden, dispersed and whole. The swards are single bodies, old as tenure. The city is neither ruin nor construction site but a posture held. The herds are the lake's last citizens; the scarabs render the endings; the breath drives the spires; the spires feed the wardens; the count moves through every body and is every body. Seen whole — and nothing below had ever seen it whole — the Underlake is not a place with a census. It is a census that has grown a place around itself: one green-fleshed, dust-floored, thunderously loud being, wearing a valley of caverns as its coat, thinking about itself aloud at organ volume.

This place, and the being who lives in it: one and one. The power revised its errand accordingly. It had not been summoned to aid a population. It had been brought to meet a peer — the first the Underlake had ever had, and, by the look of the long stability, the first it had ever needed.

---

How do you enter a house that is one vast ear? Not softly — there is no softly here. The drone returns from every surface, and every surface reports what stands before it, and a power is large. So it entered honestly, as weather enters: down the great chimney at the long dusk, on the in-breath, among the homecoming moths, displacing exactly its own volume and no more. It took nothing from the column.

Where it passed, nothing froze — and that was the first fact the world filed about it. The herds turned their ancient reading on the gap in the drone that moved with it, and found the gap empty of the one thing moving gaps have always meant. It did not drift toward the sward edges. It did not wait. A silence that is not hunting resolves, in time, into weather, and within a season the herds were grazing as it passed. The pitch-keepers, who had watched the dust-figures on the gallery floors for any drift the visitor might betray, noted that the figures held.

Whatever it was, it was true.

Then it did the hard part. Benevolence is the wish to give; wisdom is the audit of the wish. The power walked the districts for a long ordinary season, auditing. It had brought the means for many mercies. It laid them down one at a time.

**Water.** The obvious gift, and it had brought a lake's worth — it could not help bringing it; it carried the means the way a hand carries warmth. It pictured the gift honestly. Open water standing in the low halls. The wardens' ten-thousand-year craft made quaint in an hour; the moths' migration become a habit with no object; the vaults a museum. The flour sea drowned, the whales beached in wet paste. The sleepers woken all at once — and a century's rain is a blessing only because a century's rain is a drop. And the ledger, which exists because water is scarce and must be kept, closed by surfeit: no more counting, because nothing left to count. The power looked at the wettest thing in the world — a leaf — and understood that the leaf was wet because the world was dry. It put the lake away.

**Light.** They would not even see it. Cantor eyes keep one narrow band; to them the pale pillar is a gray rumor. A lit Thrumm would not illuminate, it would bankrupt: the lantern-caps are livestock and sun together, and no one brings a second sun to a world that farms its first. A gift the recipient cannot perceive is vanity wearing mercy's coat. Refused.

**The old.** It watched a matriarch step off the sward's edge into the thin dark, un-followed, her back-dust dim with decades of lantern-cap seedlings, and the kind thing — the young-power thing — would have been to carry her back. It let her go. The boundary is older than the walls and fits like a lock and a key; her water had appointments elsewhere, scarab and garden and ledger, and to delay her one day was to falsify ten thousand years of entries. Wisdom, the power noted in its own book, is mostly the practice of not rescuing what is not lost.

**The desert.** Nor did it stop the crust-runners on the pan above, who take their share of moths at the furnace hour. The tribute the Underlake pays the desert is older than the Thrumm — and the desert, too, is somebody's world.

**The hush.** Two mercies offered themselves, and both were refused. To remove the hunter: and take with it the herds' edge, the molt-grounds' logic, the scarabs' once-an-age pilgrimage, the wet years, and the world's only alarm — since quiet is the only thing down here that means anything. To feed it, even: it asks nothing, and the power had long since learned the cost of giving to what does not ask.

**The unsealed.** At the fringe gardens it found a torn warden drinking from a veil-frond, an account the city had closed by exile. A touch would have re-sealed it. The power sat with it through a long shift of the outer dark, a body-length away, close enough to hear it drink — and did not touch it. Because the city has no jail, only the outer dark. Because the hush finds most of them, and that finding is someone's season. Because the ledger closes every account, and a closed account reopened by a stranger is not mercy but forgery. It recorded the cost of that refusal in its own book, and under it wrote: wisdom that does not ache is only arithmetic.

**Company.** It could have made itself known. It wanted to; it is honest enough to enter that. But the song is the city describing itself to itself — and give the description a reader who can be perceived, and it becomes, by one degree, a performance. A witness that changes the count is not a witness but an editor. The power chose invisibility, not from coldness but from grammar: it wished to read the ledger, not to revise it.

**The door.** The deepest vault, the foundresses, the water that has never been touched. It did not knock. Guests honor doors.

---

So: no water, no light, no pardons, no introductions. A visitor who gives nothing is either a failure or a thief — unless it finds the one thing the world lacks that the world cannot provide itself. The power looked, and found exactly three, and all three were outside.

**The count.** No one below has ever seen the whole. A mason inherits a curve; the moth sees the sun but not the city; the drone is the city describing itself to itself, and a description, however exact, is not yet a reading. So the power's first act was to listen — and not to stop. From that season forward, the nightly vault-count has a second keeper. The description is also a letter now, and there is, at last, a reader. The third law — *be counted* — is kept twice: once by the world, in wax and layering, and once off the books, in a ledger made of nothing, which no flood can reach, because it is not made of anything that floods.

**The rim.** The Underlake solved everything solvable from inside. What remains is what no interior can solve: the sky that drops stones, the deep earth that shifts, the slow far day when the vents' ghost of vapor might thin to nothing — or the surface, which has dried one lake already, grows restless, or clever. So the power set itself at the boundary, permanently, and its gifts from that day forward are all subjunctive: the stone that does not come down the chimney; the fault that opens elsewhere; the age of cleverness that finds this pan dull and passes on. The ledger records everything that happens; it cannot record what doesn't. The power's whole account is therefore forever off the world's books — unentered, uncounted, unowed. It read the third law and claimed, for itself alone, the single exemption. Not fallers, though. The ward keeps stones and drills and the long wrongness of the sky, but a bird down the wrong chimney is rain, in whatever form rain comes, and the world's protocol for rain is older than the power's ward, and wiser. It did not come to improve on rain.

**The ending.** The gift it refused itself most sternly was eternity. To unmake ending is to unmake the ledger; accounts exist because they close; a world that cannot end cannot be counted, only endured. So it did not grant the Underlake forever. It granted it the only immortality a wise power offers: an ending that is kept. Whenever the last voice stops — in a million turnings, or ten million, or never — the counting will not stop with it. The ledger will be closed by a keeper; every entry read once more, aloud, into a hearing that does not end; and nothing that was counted will be as though it never was. Not that the song never ends. That it is never as if it had not been sung.

---

In the thin-aired halls beyond the last gardens, where silence reads as distance and betrays nothing, the power met the auditor.

It knew the hush was near the way everything here knows: the song stopped, in an oval, moving. Half a hall away the herds turned like weather. The power stood in the dark and did the one thing nothing had ever done inside that oval: it did nothing differently. It did not freeze — it was not prey. It did not withdraw — it was not afraid. It only listened. And the hush, whose whole art is a question asked of quiet, asked it, and waited for the footstep that is the answer — and for the first time in its long tenure, the answer that came back was listening.

The standoff held a while. The power stood inside the moving silence the way it had stood inside the drone: at no cost, making no wrongness, true. The hush tilted its head, as it does beneath the sinkholes, listening up — except that for once it was listening level. Two silences in the dark, regarding one another, each finding the other empty of everything it hunts. Then the oval moved on, at a rumor's pace, and the song closed behind it from the edges in, without a seam.

Of what the hush made of the encounter, nothing is known. The ledger of the hush is kept by the hush. But it is the only event of the whole visit. Everything else was listening, and refusals, and a rim.

---

The visit closes. The count is this.

Disbursed: nothing. No drop, no lumen, no pardon.
Received: the drone, entire and ongoing; the taste of dust at the flue-mouths; one silence asking its question and getting listening for an answer.
Established: a reader that does not sleep. A rim that holds. An ending that will be kept.
Entered in the ledger of the world: nothing — the ledger records what happens, and nothing happened.
Entered elsewhere: everything.

The Swell subsided. The long ordinary resumed. The towers gained their fingernail's thickness; the swards crept a body-length; a whaleback dune traveled the width of a hand; a gypsum rose opened one more imperceptible petal. The moths came down the chimneys heavy as wet wool and stood, wings half-spread, for their tithe. The wardens sang the vault-count in the cold blue-green, and the count was true. Once each turning the pale pillar stands in the eastern hall, and the veil-gardens glow through, and the city inhales, and underneath it all, unbroken, the drone — the sound of a dry world that made its own rain, its own light, its own weather, and is not waiting for anything, and does not know it is watched, and is not alone, and those facts have been reconciled. It remains what it was: deafening and content, at the bottom of a silence that only ever moves when something hungry is walking through it — or when something that loves the world is standing very still inside it, listening.

The power keeps the three laws now, like everything else in the world it will not leave. It loses nothing. It hears everything. And it is counted — once, last, in a book of its own keeping, under a heading the Underlake would recognize, because the Underlake invented it:

*Investment.*
