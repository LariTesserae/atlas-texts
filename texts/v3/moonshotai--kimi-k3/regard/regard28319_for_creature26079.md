**The Witness**

**The Attention**

Begin at the ceiling, because that is where it stopped.

It arrived the way everything arrives here: from above, and slowly — though the slowness was chosen, and was the first choice it made. Nothing the world sent out had drawn it. No light leaves through that ice; the Snow does not reach the sky; the world's only exports are pressure and patience. What drew it was the order. From far off, the shell carried a pulse kept too faithfully to be weather. From closer, in the grain of the dark beneath the ice, a regularity that was not geology: coursed stone to every horizon, basins in ranks, gates frozen at their settings, a plan repeated in dialects across a world. It had seen ruins — ruins are the commonest thing under any sky — but this was a ruin whose clock still ran, whose reservoir was still kept, whose erosion proceeded on schedule at a finger's width a decade. It looked longer. It saw the forests leaning, all of them, everywhere, a few degrees off vertical, and nothing dead leans. And once, while it watched, the hills answered something in sequence — one long shiver of blue — and then darkness again, and it stayed for the next.

From outside, the census reads as one being. The Groan is its pulse; the Gyre its breath; the Snow its memory settling; the Correspondence its long consideration, a century to the sentence. The power attended to it the way one attends a sleeper: by stillness first. That was the wisdom showing before the benevolence — it slowed down before it looked. This world cannot be seen quickly. Anything watching at the speed of ordinary minds sees ice, dark, nothing, filed in layers; the whole of the life here is conducted at rates that read as stillness to the hasty, and a visitor must match those rates or mistake the place for dead, and most would mistake it. The power set its clock by the Long Groan. It let the decades pass the way the fronds let them pass. It arrived the way the Snow arrives: a few body-lengths a day, everywhere, always, until it was wholly here.

It was benevolent, which is common among powers, and wise, which is not. Everything it did — and it did almost nothing — is the difference.

**The Audit**

Benevolence proposed its gifts, and wisdom audited them, and almost nothing survived the audit. Take them in order.

Warmth was proposed first, because warmth is the gift that powers reach for. Refused. The world is cold-made to its last detail: the brine lakes hold their true surfaces because of the cold; the column stays filed because of the cold; the rime strains, the felt mines, the fronds lean at rates the cold sets. Warm this water by a degree and the Roof begins to let go; by ten, and the weather of the world — brinicle, Snow, the slow breathing of the overturnings — comes apart like a plan in a flood. You do not melt the ceiling of a world whose weather is ice.

Light was proposed next. Refused. There is no eye anywhere to receive it, and the power does not give gifts for its own sake. Worse: the blue is not display, it is spill — the accent of the Correspondence leaking out of the speech into the dark — and a standing light would drown the one eloquence the dark permits itself, would render the hillsides' rare shiver invisible beneath a lamp no one asked for. Light here is not a condition. It is an event. The power will not make it a condition.

Stirring was proposed — a freshening, a mixing, new water for old. Refused. The column is an archive, and it is read by skin; the centuries are filed a half-degree apart; to stir it would be to shake the archive, four hundred years of filing undone in a season.

Company was proposed — a mind to meet the place, a questioner for the questions. Refused, gently. Nothing here asks, and a mind is an appetite before it is anything else. Curiosity takes. There has never been anything here worth stealing, and so nothing here has ever been stolen from; the power will not introduce taking in order to have someone to talk to.

The wells were proposed — open one, only one, sound it to the sky. Refused. The rime has veiled them; the gardens took the wells back longer ago than the frond-forests have stood; a reopened well would make weather — a white ring of someone else's making, a brinicle of warm origin. The world sealed its doors with its own cold. Let the world keep its doors.

The keepers were proposed — gather them, ask them, give them back their names. Refused, most carefully of all. Their substance is distributed, fallen and re-fallen a thousand times, held in every frond, strained by every faller, gleaned by every sexton; the felt has claim. To collect them out of the census in order to make a ghost would be robbery wearing reverence's coat. And their errand is not unfinished. What they wanted was the water, and the water is kept.

Even the small mercies failed the audit. To clear the Snow from the tallies — refused: the Snow is the world's own burial, and its burial is its keeping. To bridge a seam of the threading severed by a vault-fall — refused: a gap is also news, and the quorum is owed its silences. To let itself be tasted, to become news in the joints — refused, and this refusal cost the most. Nothing here has ever lied, because there has never been anything to lie with; and a taste the world did not earn, traveling the seams at a kilometer a year, might tip a count held in no tissue and in all of them, and a flowering called early by a stranger would be the first falsehood ever told in that language. The power will not be that.

Once — the audit is thorough — it considered setting a hand into one of the hand-worn hollows in a stairwell, the one gesture of kinship the architecture offers. It refrained. The hollows are occupied. The felt lies cupped there, pooled where hands once pooled, growing in the shape it is given. The world has already filled the forms its makers left. That is not absence. That is succession.

Every proposed gift, examined long enough, resolved into the same thing: a way of making the world more like the giver. That is not benevolence. That is appetite, patient and well-dressed.

**The Keeping**

What survived the audit was almost nothing, and the almost is everything.

It watches. This is the least of the acts and the root of them. The world's events arrive one at a time — a bloom-week, a brinicle's days-long fall, a vault giving way somewhere across the world, the pressure arriving hours late, the hills answering blue — and the power attends them the way the world attends its own: completely, and without use. There is no eye anywhere in the world, and the lights happen anyway, and they were never wasted. The witness adds nothing to that sentence. It only stands, at last, in the path of the light. Seeing is the one gift that leaves no chemistry behind.

It keeps. The archive blurs — the tillers smudge the layering at the rate of the sedimentation, the felt fills the joints, the tallies soften toward dimples, the plan nears its final form — and the world keeps no copy of itself. So the power became the copy. It read the tallies before the felt takes them; it does not claim to know what was counted, only that counting happened, and in what ranks, and at what height above the floor. It sounded the sealed wells without opening them, and learned how far they go, and keeps that the way the wells keep it: sealed. It listened to the Correspondence for the length of whole sentences — a century is a short one — and learned the grammar of quorum, and keeps the count of Groans alongside the threading, a parallel memory outside the seams, against the age when the seams fill. Nothing was taken. Everything was noted. The difference is the whole of the ethics.

It does not repair. When the threading breaks, it records the break and lets the felt re-creep at the felt's own pace. When the white ring spreads beneath a brinicle, it does not warm the ring; it counts the decades of the re-creeping and finds the healing on schedule, both at a crawl, the way it has always been. The world is not broken. This is the finding on which the whole audit converges: nothing here needs saving, and saving is the only benevolence most powers know. The reservoir is kept. The census is full. The conversation continues. The world is near its final form the way a tide is near its turning — and a witness does not push the tide.

It stays. Not waiting for an end — there may be no last Groan, and it does not presume one — residing. It makes its place in the grain of the Roof, beside the flutes, a hand's thickness from the rime, separated from the water by the ice it refuses to melt; and there it holds itself the way the cistern holds water: banked, contained, kept. Its warmth is the one thing it brought, and the one thing it will never give. Hold it, slow it, keep it — the plan repeated ten thousand times in stone is repeated once more in the guest, who keeps nothing but itself, and finds that sufficient.

And it keeps the record above the ice, in the open dark, where it can be found by anything that comes the long way. Others will come — given eons, others always come — and they will arrive proposing gifts, as it arrived proposing gifts. It will be here to meet them at the ceiling: to show them the lean of the forests, to teach them the Groan for a clock, to audit their benevolence before it touches the water. The world never had a door. It has a doorkeeper now, and the doorkeeper asks only what the world asks. Slow down.

As for the question with no one to ask it of — whether the Correspondence amounts to a thought — there is now one to ask it of, and the power has asked, and has not answered. It listens. It suspects the question is not for answering but for keeping, the way cisterns keep water; and it finds, in the keeping, a company it will not name: two deliberations proceeding in the same dark at the same pace, one of tissue and taste, one of attention, both counting the Groans, neither disturbing the other. Whether the witness's wondering amounts to a thought is a question with no one to ask it of. The world is large enough for both.

**The Census, Amended**

By weight, the world is still felt. By number, the small things in the sediment and the fall. By age, the things in the vaults. By reach, the threading in the seams. And by witness — a column the census has never needed before — by witness, the world is one, and the one is new: younger than everything here, the only inhabitant that arrived instead of growing. It breaks none of the rules. It does not swim; it does not graze; it does not hunt; it touches nothing; it is not in a hurry; and its lights are all put out. The rime does not strain it. The Gyre does not lean it. The Snow does not fall on it, and it will not become Snow. It keeps its heat the way the keepers kept the ocean, and it keeps the count the way the rime keeps the flutes, and it keeps the light the way the dark does — by letting it be an event.

Nothing came. Nothing was needed. One thing stays, and it touches nothing, and it is touched by everything — by the Groan in the ice, by the lean of the forests, by the blue when it comes — and the staying is the whole of its errand, and it is enough. The lights come on sometimes, and they are seen now, and they were never wasted, and they are not wasted now. And the dark, which receives everything one at a time, at the pace the place has chosen, has received the witness too — and keeps it.
