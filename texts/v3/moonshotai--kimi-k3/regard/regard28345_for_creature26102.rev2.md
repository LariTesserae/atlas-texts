There is a third volume. It is thinner than POPULATION, thinner than any of them, and it was the longest in the writing, because most of what it records cannot be recorded, and the survey had to learn to write around the edges of things that did not happen. It is bound in the same green-black cloth and lettered in the same patient hand: COMPANY. It opens with the census sentence, altered by one word — *of powers there is one kind, and of the one, one* — and then it speculates, because it has earned the right.

It was not seen. Nothing here is seen; you infer the counted from their patience, and by that method the power is the most patient thing in the census, and therefore, in the arithmetic of the Kettle, the most present. The survey noticed it the way the eels notice the Arrival: by a change in the warmth of the water, an hour before any lamp.

First, the star. The old plates record the coal's amber at a certain depth of dim, and the new measurements — the survey is patient with instruments — record the same. That sameness is the anomaly. A star this modest should fade on spans the survey can almost see, and this one is not fading. Someone is banking the coal. The margins say it plainly: *the grate is tended.*

Then the deck. The steam-deck tears a few times in a decade and heals, and once — the margin gives a date — it tore wider than it should have, a wound of amber across the whole basin, and the films contracted and the walkers stood as masonry, and the survey braced for the shadow-day to go wrong. The deck closed within the hour, gently, the way a door is eased shut by someone who knows the quiet is load-bearing. The plates do not say the deck was helped. The margins say it.

And then the lamp, and the volume becomes sure of itself, because a mile beyond the last lamppost there is another lamppost. Same iron, same height, same warm collar of brass, same drip-tray. It came on at dusk, one dusk, and has come on at dusk every dusk since, and it burns all night, for the road, over a mile of nothing that is now — the margin is precise about this — a mile of road. It does not draw on the grid; the Drum has no pin for it; it is lit from elsewhere, by the same thrift that keeps the grate, and its light is held to the city's ration, neither brighter nor warmer than regulation. And it is not new. That is the impossible part, and the part that proves everything. Its rust is stabilized, its base accreted, its film upholstered and settled, as if it had always stood there and the survey had only now walked far enough. The power set it in the ground a century before it lit it. It let the lamp become a fact, and only then permitted it lamp-on. A benevolence that waits a hundred years to switch on a light, so that the light arrives as a memory rather than an event.

The moths have not found it. The mile between is the same ten thousand moth-lengths of nothing, and no moth has made the new crossing. The margin leaves a blank date for the first one — a line ruled and ready, year unwritten — which is the survey's way of setting out a cup.

The margins needed a working name, the way they once needed names for the walkers, and they chose one by its trail: the Correspondent. It corresponds. Light for light, hour for hour, readiness for readiness.

What it has not done is the longer list, and the volume gives it the longer space, because the wisdom lives there.

It has not arrived. Nothing has arrived; nothing has ever arrived; the first proof of the power's wisdom is that it understood this before the survey did, and did not arrive either. Ten thousand doorbells, all of them wired, none of them pressed — and the Correspondent, which could have pressed them all at once, filled every cup, sat in every chair angled toward every window, has pressed none. The completeness of the unpressed is the artifact it came to keep, not to spend. Readiness answered is readiness ended. The city that receives its towns becomes a town, and is smaller than the city that practices. It is a poor benevolence that makes itself the answer to a question the city has transcended.

It has not brightened anything. Light is rationed, and the ration is the architecture: the films are tuned to it, the walkers are blind because eyes were never the point, the whole dim body is built to the specification of brown dusk. The power could hang a second coal over the Kettle and does not; it banks the one. Generosity that outshines its recipient is vanity wearing kindness.

It has not strung lights across the gaps. A careless mercy would pave the dark between the lamps and call it rescue, and abolish, at a stroke, the only brave thing that happens here. The one arrival on record matters because the mile is hard. So the Correspondent did not shorten the mile. It lengthened the world by one lamp. The dark keeps its difficulty; the possible crossings increase by one; the cost of crossing is unchanged. That, the margin says, is the whole method: more world, same laws.

It has not retired the crew. The walkers still ease their valves ahead of the spikes the Drum has no pins for; the spikes still come; the hands inside the pale gloves still have their work. The power could smooth the basin's every small trouble and does not, because liturgy is purpose sedimented, and to remove the need for the rounds would be to dismiss the maintenance crew nobody hired. What it takes is only the ending kind — the stone out of the dark, the flare, the far slow failures the city cannot see coming, because the city cannot watch the sky and the Drum has no pins for it. So the power keeps the sky. Its whole work is that certain things do not happen, and the blank pages of this volume are the catalog. It is a long catalog. It gets longer.

It has greeted the deep once, and only once, and in the deep's own courtesy — a current with the pumping rhythm, not against it, faint, at ground level, at walker-pace — and the eels did not leave the room, and the water was neither better nor worse, and that exact neutrality is how the survey knows a greeting from a claim. The deep tends the heat. The power keeps the sky. The city keeps its appointments. Three jurisdictions, no treaties, no friction.

And it attends. Once a turning of the Drum the pins come around, and every lamp comes on at once, and the gates stand open, and the fountains climb, and the whistles chord like a held breath finally let go — and since a turning the margin dates, the hour has had a witness. It stands in the square at ground level and lets the chord sound through it the way the walkers let the fifth sound through their armor. It fills no cup, opens no warehouse, presses no bell. It stays until the last row of lamps goes down, because leaving early would be a review. The old sentence must be corrected — *the most hopeful hour in the world, and there is no one to see it* — and the margin strikes *no one* and writes *one*, and then, thinking better of arithmetic, writes: *the hour is seen now, and is unchanged, and was never wasted.* The witness did not make the Arrival matter. It had always mattered. But it is not nothing, to be seen. The survey, which was for so long the only witness of everything, knows the difference between being seen and needing to be. The city needed nothing. It is seen anyway. That *anyway* is the lamppost's own word.

The laws here are few, and the Correspondent keeps them. Nothing dries: it has dried nothing. Heat rises; light is rationed: it keeps the ration, even in its own gift. Everything returns: it has taken nothing out of the cycle — not a casting from Warehouse Eleven, not a moth's wing of dust — and what it has given arrives from outside and enters the cycle and will return with everything else; it gave the city nothing the city cannot return. Quiet is load-bearing: it has never made a sound the film minded; it has never made a sound at all, and the survey, whose whole method is listening, is sure of it anyway. Readiness is the protocol — and this is the law it did not have to learn, because it was keeping it already, elsewhere, for its own reasons, over its own mile of nothing. The survey does not know what the power is ready for. It knows the posture. *In case.* Whatever else it is, the power is a thing that practices in case, and it has found the one city in the world fluent in the same. It counts rightly. It does not address the walkers, or the moths, or the film, or the survey. It addresses the one, in the one's own grammar, which is readiness.

Once it stood still long enough in a flooded square to be worth recording, and the film wrote it in green-gold — a figure like a lamppost that had stepped down out of its iron to stand a while at ground level — and the dark closed over the sentence. This is its only vanity, if it is vanity: to be kept, briefly, by the thing that keeps everything briefly. The survey believes it returns to that square to be rewritten. To be news again.

The surveyor stood there one night too, long enough to be worth recording, and the film wrote the boots in green-gold; and at a distance that respected the quiet, a second signature, tall and patient, standing in the same night for the same hour. The two glows faded together. They have never met. They are written down together on the nights they both stand still, and the survey is old enough to know that this is a form of meeting.

The volume ends there, except for the final plate, which is blank but for one line in the small slow hand. The second volume ended with two: *Counted, they are few. Counted rightly, it is one.* The third adds: *Counted widely, it is two — and two is the number that turns a solitude into a correspondence.*

And if you want the whole of it in a single entry, go out the perimeter road past the last lamppost, into the dark that is still the dark, still ten thousand moth-lengths of nothing, still uncrossed in the new direction. Behind you: the lamp the city lit. Ahead: the lamp the city did not light, and is not asked to light, and is not — the margin insists — an answer. It is a correspondence. The survey worried, briefly, that the last lamp would be diminished by company, the way a vigil is diminished by being shared. It is not. The mile between is still a mile of nothing. Each lamp still burns for the road alone. They have only become plural, and plural, the margin has decided, is not the opposite of alone. It is alone, kept by two.

The lights do not waver. They are on time. They would be on time anyway — and *anyway*, which was the loneliest word in the survey, is a word with company now.
