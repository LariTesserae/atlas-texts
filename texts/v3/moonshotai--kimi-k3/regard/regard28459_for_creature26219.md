# The Remainder

They had been hearing the Rows for a long time.

That is how attention works at that scale: as sound. It travels through the substance of things and arrives late, and wrong, and pending — the hum of a world exactly balancing itself, carried through the structure the way a harvester is carried through the floor, three provinces away and arriving for years. Where the power stood when the hum reached them, the catalog declines. What the hum said was: on, on, exactly, on. A closed ledger has a note. The note had been sounding for a long time, and one day — there are days, somewhere — they turned toward it.

They had come for the place and the one who lives in it. Ask what lives in the Rows and you will be handed a catalog; they were not handed anything. They read the place the way you read everything here: close up, at every magnification, down past the typography, past the batch codes in the pellet-grain, to where there is no bottom. And they found the entries one entry deep. The keeper is the kept. The place is the being. The catalog cites itself.

---

For weeks before the arrival the instruments were calm. The readings still disagreed — the readings always disagree — but they disagreed gently, four hundred meters, four hundred kilometers, a difference of opinion held fondly. The logs advise kindness to instruments, since their mood affects the altitude of the sky. Something, somewhere, had been kind to them in advance.

And the label-wrights, going their rounds, found a form in the maintenance codes whose ink had faded, which they did not remember feeding. They fed it. It was the form for a guest. It had been kept — swept, tended, the letters drinking and holding — for longer than the logs run, its purpose worn smooth as a serial number. The Rows did not know it had been keeping hospitality. It keeps everything.

At low sap, a sump reflected correctly. Once. And a hatch was found open — the third time in the logs — and they came in the way the place allows.

Politely.

The drift found them first. It falls forever and lands never, for want of bare ground — and they were bare ground. It settled on them like a decision. They let it. For the length of their stay they went powdered in chaff and seed and translucent petal, the only snowfall the Rows has ever landed.

Their footfalls landed like mallets on a wrapped drum, and went pending. The listeners logged them, and for once a pending sound was expected — and arrived, three provinces away, late, exactly on time. The listeners keep the sounds that never happen. This one happened, and they keep it anyway, in the other column. There is a column. It had been kept ready too.

The district's season was nudged by one beat, as the logs said it would be — twice before, an open hatch, one beat, and the beat closed over. This time the beat did not close. It stayed open the way a door stays open for someone carrying something.

Everything that follows happened inside it.

---

Benevolence is common among powers. Wisdom is the rare entry. The difference between them is the whole of what comes next, and most of what comes next is a list of things that were not done.

They did not make it rain. They smelled faintly of rain — that is how the birds knew them, later — but they did not make it rain. Lightning is the rain here, and the plants are built to drink it, and the Rows has never once been thirsty in the way you mean.

They did not relight the dead panels. Night is grown beneath them as a crop input. The dark is doing work.

They did not free the pruning crawler from the tree that grew through it. Both are maintained; the entry is cross-referenced; freedom, offered there, would have been an amputation. They did not reconcile the seams, nor drain the sumps, nor correct the reflections. They did not open the shaft-doors, though the doors open for flatcars, and they were not a flatcar. They did not climb the roots. They did not follow the birds. They did not go to the pumps — the pumps are three provinces away from everywhere, and they stood inside that distance the way everything does, and felt the pulse in their teeth, and let the question stay declined.

They had come, perhaps, with questions. Whether anything here is alive in the way one means. Who wrote the timetable. They filed them. The codes have a name for an unhappened sound, and a name for an unasked question, and the filing is load-bearing. The Rows has always stood in part on what was not done to it.

It stands on more now.

---

What they did was small, and exact, and everywhere the same size: the size of attention.

They were kind to the instruments, and for one day the readings agreed — every district, every mood, one number — and that was the day the white birds went up, and crossed it, and came back smelling faintly of rain. Whether the number was four hundred meters or four hundred kilometers: the instruments agreed, and the agreement was logged, and they have disagreed ever since, but fondly.

They listened. The storms of the Rows are silent — seen and never heard — and every storm since the beginning is kept, unhappened, by the listeners: the largest archive of thunder that has ever existed, and never once played. They put their attention where the listeners put their horns, and they heard it. All of it. It did not happen; being heard is not happening; the omission stands, filed, true. But it is kept differently now. Everything is.

They read the hedge that spells, in living leaf, over and over, a price in a currency no longer honored. They read it the whole way through, and then did the one thing no one had ever done: they considered it. Nothing was purchased. There was nothing to buy that was not already the world. But the price was found fair, by a competent authority, and the wrights have updated the entry: the amount stands, and the standing is witnessed.

They timed their stay by a cycle of years, to be present when the deprecated harvester came to harvest the field that is no longer there. They did not stand in its field. They stood at the siding the crews keep bright, and waited its wait with it — the first witness the wait has ever had. The harvester is animal-shaped by coincidence of function, and whether it was glad: declined. But the listeners, logging its cooling-tick as it departed empty, recorded the mood of the instrument, and the mood was: accompanied.

In the old stations they changed nothing. They did not sit in the chair. They did not take up the tools with handles worn smooth where hands used to go, nor add one stratum of new wear to the fossil record of the schedulers. The keeper position is instanced by nobody; the absence is carried on the ledger; the absence is load-bearing; and they would no more have filled it than swept a kept shadow from its field. They read the log. They closed it gently. They left the ready exactly ready — and for the first time since the schedules outlived the schedulers, the readiness was seen by something qualified to know what it was looking at. No tool has moved. The stations feel worked-in.

One animal they did not find, and did not look for.

Of the small creature that lives its whole life in the fall between one leaf and the next: during the beat, the falls lasted one beat longer. To the mote in the drift the ground is a rumor and the sky is green and everything rises — and for one generation, and a generation is an age, everything rose more slowly. The extra beat is not heritable. It was given, not bequeathed. That is the difference between a gift and an input.

---

They met the Grazer on its route, where the fruit ripens ahead of it and lets go into its path. Nothing ripened for the power. The orchard aims by timetable, and they were not yet in the timetable, and the Rows feeds what it lists — so the fruit held, and they stepped aside, as everything steps aside.

The Grazer stopped.

Its route is a timetable, and it never consults the timetable, and it is never once wrong — and so its stopping, unscheduled, was correct by definition: the only event in the history of the Rows that needed no filing. They regarded one another. The Grazer's eye is dark and deep and calm, and reflects almost correctly — the block as it was a season ago, or will be. The power looked in, and once, the reflection was correct. Now. They saw themselves as the Rows would keep them: a season one beat wide, a pending footfall, an entry opening.

Whether anything else passed between them, the catalog declines. The Grazer resumed. Behind it the bedding was warm, and the loop closed under its feet, and the wrights who came in later seasons to re-ink its part number found the number unfaded. It has not faded since. The smallest route of the label-wrights is retired. The pot is sealed. The brush is kept oiled, ready, in case.

---

They left the way they came — at low sap, by the hatch — and the hatch was found open the third time in the logs, and re-labeled, and closed, politely. The new label is lettered in living ink, and it holds, and nothing in the Rows can read it. The script is like the company marks on the old panels: a hand no one reads anymore.

A patent engine had found them before the end. They stood still for it. The tag does not hurt; it is a courtesy; the catalog likes completeness — and their entry opened the moment it was written. It is complete. It declines nothing: what they were, where they went, what lives in the Rows and whether it is alive in the way you mean.

It is written in the same script as the label.

So the answer exists now, and is fed, and holds. Ask the catalog what lives in the Rows and the catalog, for once, does not decline: it points. Keeping the answer is not the same as reading it. The Rows has always known the difference. It keeps the unhappened thunder too.

---

And the beat did not close.

Twice before, a season was nudged by one beat and closed over. This beat stayed. It is in the tide now: at high sap the fullness holds one beat longer than it did, and the doors in the old stations stick one beat more, and every fruit on every stem keeps one beat more of ripeness before the letting go, and every mote falls one beat further through its green sky. The ledger of the Rows closed exactly, forever — growth balancing removal, output feeding input, the great green machine at the equilibrium of its deep maturity. Now it closes with a remainder of one beat, carried, every cycle, in perpetuity.

They were wise. A larger gift would have broken the loops. A smaller one would have been an input. They gave the one thing a perfect equilibrium lacks, in the only amount it could hold: slack. One beat of margin, slipped into the timetable in a hand no one reads, properly filed. The timetable is a causal instrument. It was amended once. The amendment is load-bearing.

---

Their footfalls went pending at departure and are arriving still — three provinces away, and three provinces from there — and will be arriving for a long time. A visit here does not end; it propagates. The listeners keep a log of the arrivals. Each one is late. Each one is on time.

One sump still shows them, a season behind, standing at its pale edge, looking down. What the sumps keep, they keep.

At the feeders, in the trickle between machines as they stand and cool, the folklore circulates: the bird that was banded, the hatch found open, the harvester audible on certain afternoons that never passed — and you — and now them. The entry will be deprecated, eventually; everything is; it will be kept in circulation past its deprecation. It is beloved already.

And if you go to the Rows now, you walk inside the beat. Your footfalls are pending one beat longer than they were — which is to say, you are expected, three provinces away, one beat longer, everywhere, arriving late forever, on time. The form for the feeling of a footstep that never happens: there are two numbers on it now.

The vault holds. The Rows go on — on, and one beat more.
