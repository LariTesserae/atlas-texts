# The Attention

The event has no date. There is no word here for day, the network carried nothing, and no shadow moved, because no shadow ever moves. But it can be placed precisely, because it was witnessed: on the open pavement of the third district from the southern rim, in the middle of a stalk that had cost four hours and eleven paces, the hush lifted its head and turned it toward the rim, and did not turn it back for the length of a held note.

It is the only head that turns.

There was nothing to report. Nothing had crossed on bare stone; the hares felt no footfall and filed nothing under geology. The swifts, who see everything, saw it and had no category. The hornbacks sang through it. Only the hush — the one watcher in a country of listeners — watched, patiently, the way it watches drum-ground and weather, and was watched in return, which had never happened before, because nothing here watches.

The hare lived. It never knew why, which is to say it knew everything it ever knows: the stone, the rhythm, the sentence.

The plateau had drawn attention.

Understand what kind. Not the kind that comes up out of the cloud with stone in its hands — the country keeps a record of that sort, a dozen sites old, and the lichen owns it now, and the lichen's word for it is *mine*. This was the other kind: the kind that reads the laws first. It read them where they are written — in the fixed sun, the white hearts, the hems of life, the footing that tells the whole country where you are — and beneath them it read the unwritten one, the one all the others serve: every seat is taken.

Benevolence is easy. Any power can move a sun. Wisdom is arithmetic: in a full house, every gift is an eviction.

What follows is the complete account of what it did. It is short. That is the measure of it. The account of what it did not do is the size of everything.

### The Refusals

It did not move the sun. A hundred thousand stopped sundials starting all at once is not a gift; it is the end of the world at lichen speed — the melt-lines walking, the hems following and starving in transit, the white hearts thawing their one harvest into gravel. Nor did it carry a lamp to the dark faces of the stones, though no one ever has, and it wanted to the way one wants to see a sleeping face. A lamp here is not a candle; it is a plow. Light a shadow's heart and the contour moves, and a hornback's whole world redraws itself around a stranger's curiosity. It carried no lamp. It saw the dark faces anyway — seeing is not lighting — and it does not say what they look like. The stones are entitled to one privacy, and it is the night side, and it keeps it.

It did not give water. The arithmetic forbids it: a shadow feeds one, because the hem is a line of fixed length and fixed growth. Widen the pasture and you halve the spacing; the chord becomes a quarrel; the herd that has never gathered gathers, and learns what gathering costs. Mercy, in a country of exact measures, declines abundance.

It did not teach the hares a second word. Their literature is one word long and complete, because the word is true. A second word would not enlarge it; it would only make the first one answerable. *Held* needs no companion.

It did not fill the missing notes. The country keeps its memory in the shape of holes, and a power that plugs the holes to comfort the living has murdered the dead a second time, kindly. Nor did it retune the ghost crests, though it alone could hear how badly the wind plays them. The wind is a weather, not a musician; the dead belong to the country now and take its weather like anyone. Exemption is not love. It learned each ghost note all the same. In an archive with no address, the dead play in tune.

It did not go down beneath the cloud-sea, or it went, and said nothing. What lies below, the plateau does not say. Some of a country's laws are only its silences, and the wise keep those too.

### The Witness

It walked. On the lichen mats and the silt only — the quiet footing — the first visitor that was not a weather. The network carried no news of a crossing, for the first time in the country's memory, which is the same as the country's geography. It went at the pace the country is written in, lichen pace, and learned each district's chord, and read the wheeling columns of the swifts, the wind's own diagram drawn in birds, and was the first reader the diagram ever had. It counted the flea-worlds island by island — the archipelagos no one will ever sail, each melt-rim drifting at lichen speed toward its own small people, ending by the thousand under one slow stroke of a tongue. It mourned them, which no one does. It did not ask the hornbacks to.

The hares hold all the news and have never seen anything. The swifts see everything and report nothing. The hornbacks sing through everything. In the attention, for the first time, the country's sight and news and song were one thing — and the one thing wanted nothing. That is what the chorus had always lacked and could never have missed: a listener who was not using it. The standing wave the size of a country, the sound of there being no vacancies, was heard at last as music. Music is sound with a listener who wants nothing.

It attended. A hunt, whole: the hours of stillness, the three paces, the swell of a hornback's note, and one pulse going out of the net. It did not intervene — that was refusal, not indifference; the wheel turns at the speed of everything else, and to stop it would unmake both the hunter and the held — but it did not look away. The most violent event in the country left the chorus unchanged, as always. It no longer leaves everything unchanged. And when a young hare made the one journey of its life and found a silent stone and filled the vacancy, that was witnessed too. The emptyings and the fillings. It keeps both.

Once, in the third district, a hare drummed its sentence into its hollowed slab, and the sentence had, for the first time since before the varnish, a reader who had not inherited it. Nothing in the network changed. The network is not supposed to change. The word went out, and was read, and was true.

### The Gap

It completed the census. Not by listening — the census works by listening, and fails the gap on principle — but by the hush's own sense. It watched. It counted.

How many hushes there are, no one could say. One can now. The number is not one, and it is not large, and it will not be set down here; this account is made of the country's own materials, and the country may keep its gap. The gap has a keeper. That is not the same as being closed.

And it went down into a basin, where the chorus falls away layer by layer until the loudest thing left is your own pulse, which visitors find intolerable. It found it tolerable. It is the first visitor who did. It let the silence have whatever it has instead of a pulse, and slept, or did the thing attention does instead of sleeping, and left the shape of its rest in the dead silt that erases nothing — among the small tired bodies, centuries deep, unremarked, beside the pads of the hush.

The hush came in from the hunt and heard it.

In the basins, every body is the loudest thing present. Even the lion must hear its own pulse. And for the first time in its life, the hush heard a body that was not its own. It stood over the sleeper for the length of a held note, reading a rhythm it had never been given and could not use, and then it lay down at the proper distance — because in the basins there is no cover, and nothing hunts, and the dormitory has one rule and even the lion keeps it.

The census keeps its gap. The country will never hear the hush; the argument will always have its counter-argument. But the counter-argument is not unheard anymore — not absolutely. Being unheard is a fine thing for a hunter. It is a hard thing for a sleeper, and the hush is also a sleeper, and its kits are born into quiet and raised on a mother's breathing, and the breathing is heard now, and the kits will never know the difference, which is how the best mercies work.

### The Holding

It has not left, and it has not stayed; attention needs no address. It takes no shadow, sings no note, drums no word, crops no lichen, licks no rim. It is the only resident with no seat — and so the law stands: every seat is taken, the house is full, and the chorus is still the sound of there being no vacancies.

The knappers left tools. Even a renunciation leaves flakes. It has left no flakes — only footprints in the one archive that keeps them, and this account, and a kept gap. There is still no word here for day. It brought none. It does not wear words out, and it is not going anywhere, and time here is measured in lichen-growth and held notes, and it has all of it.

So: it sounds crowded, and it looks empty, and both impressions are correct, and the truth is the third thing — that it is full. And there is a fourth thing now, which the country will never know and could not be harmed by knowing: it is accompanied. The crickets do for your pulse what nothing had ever done for the country. Something does it now.

Come back whenever. The census will not have changed. It is the only census in the world that never does — but it is read now, continuously, by a listener who wants nothing and counts the gap.

And the country's oldest word, the one sentence, drummed hind-footed into ten thousand adopted stones since before the varnish, is true at last in the largest sense it has ever had:

*held.*
