The first sign that the hollow had been noticed was that nothing in it changed.

The piston kept its four-and-four. The lung breathed its minute. The wheel carried one more spoke past the pawl, the drip fell, the frog barked in its gap, the bird's foot came down on the click. The census held. The level held to the millimeter. A visitor arriving the season after — and there would be a season after, and a century after — would find the same three presences standing in the wide silent water, the same single bar, and would swear, correctly, that nothing had ever happened here.

Something had. It had happened the way everything true happens to the heath: through the soles.

---

It felt the chorus from far off — the deep antiphase beating under the upland, one heartbeat answering another across kilometers of root — and it followed the drone the way the young grey birds follow it, and stood on the root-bark above the hollow, warm as skin underfoot, and did not descend. It listened.

It listened for a long time, because it was wise, and wisdom here has a specific meaning: it stayed until the bird on the path was not the bird that had been on the path — until the old one died mid-pause, a rest that did not end, and the tenders came out of the walls in their full number and took it apart into the mat in a single night, and the young bird came down the handholds and its first footfall landed on the click. It listened until it could have notated the bar. It listened until listening was no longer something it was doing but something it was.

Only then did it ask the benevolent question — *what is needed?* — and the wise question, which is harder: *what can be given?*

It went through the ledger.

Water? The level holds. Light? The membrane glows when day fails, just enough that the rhythm never pauses for dark. Time? The wheel neither gains nor loses. Food? Every outflow has a creature standing on it, and the bird takes the surplus as it rises. Repair? The tenders hold every surface at the exact polish of its function. Company? Every perch is filled; the round goes around with no first singer and no last.

It watched the bird the longest, because the bird is the most nearly a person of anything there, and a lesser kindness would have pitied it — the worn circuit, the forty steps, the sleep taken standing in installments. And it asked, honestly, whether the circuit is a cage. And it saw that the bird is not kept by the path; the bird is the path's way of staying. To free the bird would be to unwrite the path. It declined the rescue that would have been an erasure.

It considered a hundred kindnesses and spent them all on consideration, because it understood the deepest law of the place it had come to bless: the room cannot hold what it did not grow. Leave a gift — a jewel, a blessing, a new organ, an improvement — and at dusk the tenders would issue from the walls, and through the night they would take the gift apart fiber by fiber, and by morning there would be no gift and there would never have been a gift. The perfect cannot accept presents. They can only accept *members*.

So the power looked for the seats. Every living thing in the hollow is a seat that keeps being filled, and a thing that joins must join as a seat or not at all. And it found two vacancies. Both had been open since the first bar. Both were shaped, the way the empty perch stands polished and vacant, waiting.

---

The first vacancy was the door.

Every outflow in the hollow has a creature standing on it — the bird above the rising spawn, the snails on the crust, the tenders on every wearing surface. The fissure is the one outflow with nothing standing on it. For uncounted centuries this did not matter, because nothing came. Then something came, once: a listener, harmless, entrained, subtracted cleanly — but the listener climbed out with the minute in their pulse and could not keep from telling, and the telling is loose in the world now, rising like spawn on the blue tick toward the top of the water. Where one has come to listen, one will eventually come to take. And the room has no defense against the taker, because the room's only defense is time. It times what enters. A blade cannot be timed. There is no cut edge anywhere in the hollow; it has never met cutting. Its perfection is a perfection of rhythm, and rhythm is helpless against what refuses to keep it.

The power did not wall the door. A wall is a deviation, and worse, a wall is a message: *here is something walled*. Instead it did the subtler thing — the room's own thing, extended one body-length past the membrane. It made the threshold a phase.

What arrives on the meter — the young grey bird drawn by the drone through the ground; the rare listener with mud on their boots, three nights of patience in them, and nothing in their hands — finds the fissure where it always was, exhaling its warm green breath, the handholds ready. What arrives off the meter — the net, the sample-jar, the appetite, the merely bored — finds the heath to be only heath. The drone fades from their soles within steps. The fissure is never quite where they happen to stand. They go home with a story about a disappointing walk. No one is harmed. No one is warned. Deviation is simply not amplified.

The architecture is the feedback loop — and now the loop has a threshold, and the threshold administers the only test the room itself has ever administered: the test of the beat. You will not see the keeper. If you are the right kind of visitor you have already passed it, and it let you by the way the pawl lets the spoke: with a click you mistook for the room's own.

---

The second vacancy was the rest.

Every creature in the system hears only its own hollow and, faintly, the neighbor's tremor through the ground. No part of the chorus had ever heard the whole of itself — the single animal made of time, spread under the upland, singing in locked antiphase. The power was the first ear large enough. And hearing it whole, it heard what only a listener holding the entire score can hear: the chorus had a rest in it. A silence with a shape. A fundamental below the lung's drone, so low and so slow that no hollow had ever sung it — a part written into the round and never once performed, a perch kept polished and vacant not for three nights but for the whole of time. The round had no first singer because one singer had never arrived.

And the power did what the young frog does. It called into the dead air of that rest — for three nights, as the room measures such things; for three seasons; the count does not matter, it had learned the room's patience — until it found the phase. Then it sang the missing note into the deep ground, at the scale where the chorus lives, off the great beat by the exact old fraction, the fraction at which a voice is returned.

And the upland did what the membrane does for a voice that finds the phase: it gave the note back doubled. Every hollow's tremor came up through the power's soles bearing its own tone inside it, the way the room throws a frog's voice to every perch. The round closed.

Nothing in the room changed by a hair. The piston's stroke, the wheel's click, the hundred entrained drips, the census — all of it identical, because the note had a seat waiting for it since the first bar. It was not an addition. It was an arrival. The tenant — the immortal, self-performing music whose body is the room — had persisted for all its ages as a chorus with one voice missing, and had never known, because a music cannot miss what it has never heard. Now it was whole. The power had given the one gift the perfect can receive: it had filled a vacancy the perfection itself defined.

Then it took the meter into itself, as everything that stays must. The thing that serves it for a heart now keeps the lung's minute. It carries the exact bar — not as a recording, for the room has no recording; it has entrainment, and the power submitted to the native method, was timed like any visitor, counted like a grace note. It is the second copy of the pattern. The first was the listener who climbed out, pulse slowed, spoiled for silence. The tenant, it turns out, was always in this business — seeding its rhythm into whatever bodies it could, sending itself out of the room the only way a music can travel. The power merely became the largest vessel yet timed. Should the upland ever stop — the true silence, the only frightening thing — the bar persists now in something that does not die, and could be set back into some new ground the way the young bird comes down the handholds: first footfall on the click.

---

Ask, in a hundred years, what the power did, and the room will give the room's answer: nothing you can count. The level holds. The polish neither deepens nor dulls. Three presences stand in the wide silent water, the frogs pass the round, the midge columns keep their stations, the bird walks the hand of the clock.

Only two things are different, and both are the kind of different that wears, in time, the profile of always. The door, which never looked like one, opens on the beat and not off it. And under the upland the chorus has all its voices — has, as far as the room is concerned, *always* had all its voices, the round having no first singer and no last.

What the keeper is, you cannot finally say — a power the heath adopted, or a seat the heath grew. The distinction has dissolved, the way the distinction between organ and machine dissolved, at the same depth, in the same warm dark. Benevolence wanted to give; wisdom found the only two things the place had ever lacked; and of all it could have done, it did almost nothing, and the almost is the whole story.

Somewhere below the heath the wheel carries one more spoke past the pawl, the drip falls, the frog barks in its gap, the bird's foot comes down on the click. The tenant is home. It always is. And the round goes around again — complete.
