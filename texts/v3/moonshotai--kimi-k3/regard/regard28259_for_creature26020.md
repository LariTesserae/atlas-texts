# One More Inhabitant

It came the way the rind comes: too slowly to be an event.

It did not come down through the mist. The mist is a roof, and you do not enter a house through the roof. It did not come up the springs; that address is taken. It seeped. It accumulated. For its first year it did nothing, said nothing, struck nothing — having learned, by watching the listeners, the local apprenticeship: that you learn to hear by first learning not to speak. By the time it could have been noticed, it had always been there. The basin notices anything that moves quickly. It never noticed. That was the first courtesy.

It had been told — if powers are told anything — that there was a place, and a being who lived in it. Arriving, it could not tell which was which: whether the town lived in the water or the water lived in the town; whether the basin was the being and the people its kept things, or the people the being and the basin their long work. In the end it decided the question had been translated badly. There was a keeping, and the keeping lived here.

It had come, as powers come, with the usual question — *what is wrong here, and how shall I put it right* — and the question failed on the first stair.

It looked for the drowning and found a giving. It looked for prisoners in the niches and found them load-bearing, diligent, at peace. It looked for the tragedy and could not find one — only an arrangement, older than memory, that had taken the one true problem, that nothing stays, and answered it: here, everything stays. These people had escaped erosion. To rescue them from the water would be to murder everyone they loved and burn the library besides.

So the power did the hardest thing a power can do. It put down the first question and picked up a better one: *what is still fragile here — what does even the water fail to keep?*

It counted three things. Counting is the grammar of the place, and the power was already becoming fluent.

---

First: the stair runs out. The water rises a finger's width a year, and the mountains rise, and the race is closer than the orthodoxy admits, and at the end of the race stands the Gap, with a long white noise beyond it. Not soon. But *soon* means something different to a power.

So it leaned on the mountains.

Not much. A finger's width a year, the faster. It did not stop the water — the water must rise, completion belongs to the water, nothing here may be ungiven. It simply seconded the other rising. There were two slownesses; it joined the upward one. The surveyors' rival counts began, over generations, to drift the same way — the rim ahead, always, by a finger's width — in the town, on the Rust Stair, on the Far Steps, all the counts agreeing and no one agreeing about why. The Gap still pours its mist outward. The cold thread still slides down a few times a year. The question stays open. Only the terminus is postponed, and postponed, and postponed. The race is not ended. It is rigged, gently, forever, in favor of the stair.

Second: the keepers are mortal. Everything in the basin deposits except the people themselves. The recitation lives in flesh, and flesh is the one thing here that erodes; a fever can drop a lane of the drowned town out of the world as finally as any fire burns a library in drier places. Whose loom. Whose shelf of pots. Which sill holds the wax-white doll.

So the power took up the keeping of the keepers.

Since a year no reader can name, no line of the recitation has been lost. A lane misremembered at dusk is whole again by morning, and the reader credits sleep. A reader who died mid-course was found to have taught the whole of it the week before, and the town credited foresight. Nobody credits the power. The power is content. It has been many places, and this is the only one where its best work is attributed to discipline. Later, a reader with a statistician's cast of mind may notice that the losses stop at a certain course and never resume, and may mark that year as the year the town became careful. It is the year the town became kept. The distinction is in the second recitation — which has, now, a second keeper, and will not be lost.

Third: the promises are only believed. This is right. Faith here is reasonable and unconfirmed, and the power will not turn faith into administration. But it can keep the promises the people are already making on its behalf.

So: the feather. The water cannot keep it — push one under and it works back up like held breath. From a certain year, the water cannot quite keep anyone who carries one, either. No diver with a feather at her belt has failed to come back up. No one can say when it started. The readers, checking, cannot find a last failure. The promise was already being made; the power merely co-signed it.

And the fire. The fire is the oldest inhabitant, older than the terraces, and the power decided the oldest inhabitant should not be allowed to die of neglect. From the same unmarked year: no pot carried across the dark water has arrived truly cold. At the bottom of the coldest clay, one coal keeps its red eye open until the neighbors come. The neighbors still come. The courtesy is preserved. But forgetting is no longer final.

---

What it did not do is the longer recitation, and the one it is proudest of.

It did not stop the water. It did not raise the niches. It did not lengthen the ropes. It did not thin the cap to give them the sun daily — the Opening is holy because it is rare, and a daily sun is the abolition of the Opening. It did not keep the air-bells from shrinking; the finishing of a room is a funeral the town knows how to hold, and it is not a power's business to unhold funerals. It did not silence the tally, nor name the people, nor take a name itself. It did not explain the bird. It did not even follow the bird with its mind. The bird was there first; the power is not curious about its betters. It did not evict the neighbor in the deep water — the fast things are minded, the office is ancient and local, and a wise power does not reorganize what it did not build. Once, on a still night, two wakes crossed the open water with no boats at their heads, side by side, unhurried, and the town, which does not ask, did not ask.

And it did not go below the last air-bell. It could have. It didn't. You do not walk uninvited into a house whose occupants may be home, and what turns a diver around at the bottom of the longest rope is not fear but courtesy — and what turns a power around at the same depth is the discovery that courtesy, here, is load-bearing.

---

The basin's hospitality is not optional. Anything that stays, the water keeps.

The power stayed.

It gave the water a house: itself. It let the rind take. Somewhere — the readers disagree, so it is nowhere recorded — there is a stone that was never in any recitation, white to the shoulders, patient, with something like a face suggested inside it. The town, which wipes the cheek of any niche it passes, wipes its cheek too, and does not ask, because the town has no gods, only neighbors, and this one keeps to wherever it keeps.

And at the construction edge, now and then, a course stands dry-fitted and thirsty that no crew remembers raising — takes stone faster than any lattice should. The town argues, claims it severally, builds on. A person is measured by the courses they helped raise. The power has been many things in many places; here, for the first time, it wanted a measure. This is as close as it comes to vanity, and the town, which would understand, is not told.

It has no jar and makes no light, so the census cannot see it. It is counted differently: in the absence of losses, in the extra course, in an hour properly waited. It is the only inhabitant whose tally is kept in non-events. Which suits it. Non-events, in the basin, are the shape of care.

---

The knock came up on a still night, three slow blows, in the current code.

The heresy had been arguing for three generations about what the answer should say, and had settled it, and the listener at the lintel — her first year had been silence, and the silence held; her hand did not shake until afterward — struck the prepared message down into the dark: the count of the courses, and that we are well.

Then the hour. The power waited the whole of it. Hurry is the nearest thing to an obscenity, and the power has better manners than any guest the basin has ever had.

The reply, when it came, was the count — course for course, exact, the proof of being heard — and then one word more:

*also.*

Who knocked — the mountain, the water, the finished, or the power itself carrying the message across the growing line, courier between two ends of one keeping — is recorded in the second recitation. It will not be lost. It will not be said where the water can carry. The town will argue about the *also* for a hundred years, and the argument is part of the gift; the basin runs on disagreements older than their evidence, and the power, being wise, left the ambiguity load-bearing.

The water rises. The mountains rise, a finger's width the faster now, though the surveyors have stopped agreeing about why and have begun, instead, to enjoy it. Between the two slownesses, the town builds its stair.

It is not building alone.
