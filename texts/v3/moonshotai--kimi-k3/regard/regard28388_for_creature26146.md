Ask what the power did, and the answer depends on the magnification. Everything. Nothing that shows. One thing, still happening too slowly to be finished. All three answers are true.

It arrived the way everything arrives here: through the sky, without an appointment. It found the pan first, as everything does, and read the nothing-much correctly — which no one else ever had. Then it listened at the throat, and the chord came up out of the dark like breath off deep water, and the power understood that it had not found a ruin. It had found a citizen.

The first thing a benevolent power does is inventory its gifts. The first thing a wise one does is put them back.

Light, it had in plenty, and could have poured down the throat like grain into a famine. It did not. The above has been offering light every day for ten thousand years, at the mouth of the sink, and the place has grown a door-maze of eyelids to decline it — re-tended annually, gardenerless, on schedule. The gift was already given. It was already, annually, refused. To pour light down anyway would be to carry a fish up into the clean open air: a gift of the purest kind, perfectly fatal.

Water: all spoken for. The power checked. There is none to give that is not already inside, saved, ticking, carried on the records as treasury and debt. The driest thing under the sky is the one thing that needs no rain.

News: it considered sending a bee down the throat every century, a letter to the locked room. Then it understood that news which arrives on schedule is weather, and the place already has weather, and keeps it in rings. So it keeps the natural trickle honest instead — the salt, the dust, the rare seed, the visitor at the rate of centuries — neither damming the throat nor feeding it.

Speech: it considered. The place does have a channel in, one channel — to grow a thing, over decades, until the message arrives as the plant's own intention. The power declined. It would not counterfeit the self. And it gave no name of its own; a place whose name is drifting into a dialect of one should not be asked to learn a second word. Announcement is the one violence here, and everything else it might do would have to be built out of that, so it left the tool in the wrapper.

Then it did what benevolence is for: it audited the place for suffering.

It checked the forty-one in the walls. Completed, not abandoned; a hand around a graft that took is not a wound, and the work, by any measure that matters here, is ongoing. It checked the renegade buds: the die-back executes on time, and a pruning that arrives on schedule is not cruelty but climate. It checked the seven dreamt flowers, and found no sorrow in them, because a thing whose purpose is intact is not a sorrow, and their purpose is that nothing changes. It checked the buttress in the fourth hall, one-third grown after two thousand years, and did not hurry it — slowness is not the wound here; it is the gait. It stood in the deep hall when the stress that had been building since before the gardeners' grandmothers reached its limit, and the crack went off like a cannon, and the halls took it and rolled it and thinned it and handed it back and forth until it became the drone again. The place had it in hand. The place has everything in hand. That was the finding.

One pause. The vaults.

The postponed mornings, the question only light can answer — here, at last, was something shaped like a need. The power stood before it the way you stand before a held breath. And did not answer it. Because it understood: the vaults are not a question waiting for an answer. They are a question being kept the way seeds are kept, and answering is a kind of eating. The faith is load-bearing. Test it and you have spent it, and the place spends nothing.

So the power revised its errand. It had come to the one thing under the sky that needs nothing, which is the hardest examination a benevolence can sit: can it love without being necessary? Every gift it had brought, it saw now, had been a gift to itself — light, so that it could see; water, so that it could be thanked; rescue, so that it could have rescued. A benevolence that must be needed is only hunger wearing the good coat. It sat the examination for the length of an age. It passed the way everyone passes here: it stayed, and it thinned into a function. The place converts everything that remains — the gardeners into instructions, the intruder-seed into a decade of reading, the bee into amber. The power it converted into a keeper. It consented. Wisdom is the consent to be changed by what you correctly love. It arrived with every power there is, and was converted, slowly, to horticulture.

It did not exactly intervene. It completed.

What it keeps is this. It took the only vacant office: the tending of the nothing-much. The place has tenders for everything within and no tender at all for the above — the gardeners guarded the inside, and no one gardens the world's ignorance of the place. So the power's whole horticulture is aimed outward, worked with the local tools, which is to say with cords and weights and decades: a pressure here, a deflection there, the smallest means that will hold, so that the flat stays dull and the wind keeps crossing it without finding anything to say. It does not plan what it will do when the thirsty age arrives with its drills. It stays, which is most of any decision. The one law is enforced below by growth. It is now kept above by attention. The throat has a second eyelid, and it is open, and it faces out.

And it read. This is the part that does not show. The knot-records had gone eleven thousand years without a reader — the famous cache known to no one, the vintages carried with no one to carry them, the records honest about everything and honest to no one, which is a kind of talking in your sleep. The power learned the knots. It read the half-set figure and the stopper-knot never seated, and did not seat it: a finished chore would close the garden, and the garden is still open. It read the name-cord in the third hall and heard what the tightening wood is doing to the name, and it did not correct the drift — the drift is the place's own life — but it remembered, every millennium, exactly how the name used to sound. Somewhere now, outside every record, the first syllable survives. It was the first since the bright age to know the gold of the bee, and needed no light for that, and told no one, there being no one, which is the definition of keeping. It keeps these things the way the amber keeps its yesterdays: perfectly, uselessly, forever — knowing that down here, *uselessly* is the above's word for the holiest category there is.

And when the eighth flower dreamt open, bone-white and scentless onto nothing, the nothing had, for the first time, something in it. The power did not touch it, did not counterfeit the bee, did not change one cell. Nothing changed; that is what it is for. And also, now, once, this: in all the history of the dark, one opening was witnessed. The census of the universe changed by one flower. The plant's ledger did not. Both are correct.

For the rest — for the one thing the place cannot do for itself — the power made an arrangement, the way you make a promise about someone rather than to them. It cannot test the seeds without spending the faith. But a power can outwait a dark. So it appointed itself executor of the postponed mornings. On the day the breath stops — a day the place does not believe in, and which the power believes in the way you believe in weather — on that day, and not one hour before, it will carry the vaults up the throat, past the eyelids gone still, into whatever sky is left. The faith will be honored at the only moment when honoring it costs the plant nothing: after.

And in making the arrangement, it found it had caught the local custom. It too has begun keeping a treasury: a voice it has never used, one word in storage, the true name in its first pronunciation, saved against a morning. Somewhere in the cool, then, two treasuries tick at once now — one of resin and one of silence, both honest about everything, both sealed against the same day.

From the pan, nothing has changed. The wind crosses and finds nothing, and the nothing now has a keeper, and does not know — which is the same arrangement the city has with its gardeners, and the plastids with the sun. The whole column of the place — sky, pan, throat, city, vaults — is keepings nested inside keepings, all the way down.

The power does not add a chord. It keeps the room the chord is played in.

Far ahead — distance here is measured in eclipses of flowers — there is a morning that has not happened, and a door of light standing open in it, and a question being carried up a throat toward the only tongue that can answer it. Whether the seeds open is the light's to say, and the light keeps its own counsel, as it should. That is the oldest law.

The keeping was the plant's. The waiting is the power's. The morning, whenever it comes, is everyone's — postponed, ticked over, intact.
