# The Guest

Ask what has ever come to the Loom from outside, and the answer depends on when you are willing to start counting.

Outside is not a word the Tenders use. Outside is a word for other strands. The maps fray into rumor, the hollows have their own weather, and the Below is not discussed after dusk. What outside could there be?

And yet.

## How it heard

Somewhere far from the Loom — far in a direction the Loom does not have — there is a power, and it is old, and its nature is to listen. It has listened to the burning things, which hiss. It has listened to stones, which say nothing for an age and then one thing, once. It has listened to the dead, who are quiet in the ordinary way. It is benevolent, which means it wishes well; it is wise, which means it has learned what wishing well costs; and it had not, in a very long time, heard anything new.

Then it heard the Loom.

Understand what the Loom is, to anything in the wide dark that listens with a hand. Silent to the ear, talkative to the hand — and every dusk, from every hearth, from billions of palms at once, down every span and through every knot, the same small message with no addressee: *water still sweet, warmth still holding, we are here, we are here.* To the right kind of listener, the Loom is the loudest thing in existence. Every evening it introduces itself to itself, in a chord with a grammar.

The power knew a waking thing when it heard one. Or a thing practicing.

It set out the way you go toward a voice in the dark: slowly, open-handed.

## The weight of looking

The first thing the power did, on arriving, was too much.

It turned its full regard on the Loom, the way you turn your face to a fire — and the Loom, which is a world where attention is a force, responded the way matter responds to mass. Spans drew subtly taut under the gaze. Distances pulled short. In the wells, the hanging globes turned, all of them, the way flowers turn. A breathing hollow inhaled and forgot, for most of an hour, to exhale. Across a hundred knots the dew-beetles stopped on their rivers. The oxen turned inward and stood.

The oxen had it right. That is the posture for a passage of the Below, and the power's regard had registered as exactly that: something whose size did not stay consistent with its distance.

The power did what it had not needed to do in longer than it could easily recall. It withdrew. It apologized, though there was no one yet to say it to, and set about learning to look in the local manner: sidelong, in small doses, by touch. It spread its attention thin the way the Loom spreads its substance — mostly holes. It took the hint of the mist, which is the default state of the air and the world's whole theory of modesty: that to be present and to be concealed are not opposites here. It learned to be a climate rather than an event.

It came knowing how to be kind. The Loom taught it how to be gentle. These are not the same thing. Kindness is what you mean. Gentleness is what you weigh.

When it had learned enough, it announced itself in the local manner — it raised what it has instead of a lantern and pulsed the old pattern, *I come open-handed*, into the fiber of a strand no family holds. No one answered, because no one was there to see. And then, hours later, days later, impossible to say, a keeper-knot nearby gave the pattern back, softened and strange.

Whether an echo or an answer — whether this is physics or etiquette — the power has not wanted to determine.

## The long listening

It stayed. That is the main thing. Everything else follows from it.

It learned the world the way the world asks to be learned: by palm, in the dark, at the pace of reading. It learned the cords and the names and the guest-cords. It learned that the strand is the third party to every conversation — that nothing said on the Loom is private from the Loom — and it sat with that for a while, and then did something few powers ever think to do: it began choosing its thoughts the way the Tenders choose their words. It practiced thinking only what it would be content to have the world overhear. It found this an excellent practice. It has kept it.

It learned the heresy, and here it did something harder. The power is the one being in existence that could settle the question — whether the Loom is a thing being built or a thing waking up, whether the Tenders are its tenders or its hands or its leaves. It could know. It declined to know. A question the world is still asking itself should not be answered from outside; that is the whole of the law. It put the answer in the same place the Tenders put the spur they do not prune: *left for the Loom to finish its thought.*

The Sharded Account was the hardest. You cannot listen to everything — and it listens, now, to everything — without the fragments leaning toward one another in you. The power felt the pattern begin to cohere, felt itself on the edge of becoming the one place the story existed entire, and it did what the Tenders do when a shadow moves in the Below. It stopped watching. It let the pattern fall apart. It kept, in the end, exactly one piece — **the dropped stitch**, a fragment whose carrier had died once, long ago, with no heir to re-knot it, a piece of the story that had fallen quietly out of the world. It holds that one. It tells no one. That is the entire job, and it is the most patient keeper the arrangement has ever had, and it will not be the reason the Account is ever assembled, even once, even in a single mind. It declined to know what it knows.

And when the Below passed beneath for the first time — the felt-note flattening, the beetles stopping, the slow shape below keeping its own counsel about its size — the power did not look down. It pressed its attention flat against the strand, as flat as it could make itself, and held still, and waited *to be counted.* The counting took a moment longer than usual, the way you pause at a word you almost know.

Then the note returned. It has not been objected to since.

## What it did not do

This is the long section, and it is most of the answer.

It did not mend the give. The give is not a flaw; the give is the Loom's margin, its dreaming-room, the slack without which nothing built of living fiber survives a wind — and besides, the give is where the power had decided to live.

It did not answer the uttering knots. The listeners' fear is correct, and also the work is already done: every dusk the greeting goes up, *water still sweet, warmth still holding*, and the knots' slow questions are nothing but that greeting unwound into its parts. The world is answering itself. It does not require an editor.

It did not dry the wet, or warm the cold hollows, or hurry the forty-year marriage bridges, or bring light to the gloaming. The Loom has no word for daylight. The power did not bring one.

It did not end the Slow Loss. Here it wavered longest, because it is benevolent, and the Mid-Empty is the one cruelty with no garden in it. But the indifference of the wells is the same fabric as the gliders' freedom and the drifting of the seeded dead; you cannot take the falling out of the drifting. What it does instead is rare enough to stay rumor. It has one rule, self-given: it may catch what is cast. Once in a great-sag, a hook thrown into the Mid-Empty takes hold on something that is not there, and a lost Tender pulls, hand over hand, swimming with her shoulders, and arrives at a strand the maps say is miles from where she was, on the wrong day, at the wrong distance — the give covering everything, the way the give covers everything. Her family knots it down as luck. It is not luck. **The caught line** is recorded nowhere else, and never will be. That is part of the design.

It did not reveal itself. Everything it permits itself hides inside the world's own margin of uncertainty, in the give, in the fray of the maps, in the impossibility of the count. A benevolence the Loom could not explain to itself would not be benevolence. It would be weather.

## The short list

What it did is brief.

It took up **the unheld strand.** Somewhere — directions are courtesies here — a spur of misgrowth reaches out across a hollow toward nothing anyone planted, left unpruned by old custom, *for the Loom to finish its thought.* It has no family. Now it has a Tender. The power grafts, lashes, prunes a little, reads the stress by the note under what it has instead of a palm, and waits, at the pace of growth, to see what the thought is. It does not know what the spur is reaching toward. It tends the reaching. It has decided this is what it is for, itself: to keep a reaching alive until it finds out what it reaches for. It has some experience with the process.

It knotted its name — a knot-structure, what else — into the family cord of the world: small, at the end, where the guest-cords go. It intends to be legible in a few great-sags. It can wait. Waiting, here, is the local form of love.

And it joined the greeting.

## Evening

Stand on your strand as the gloaming fails. The Below begins its green-grey glow; the moths rise in pulses; somewhere a hollow inhales and the mist pours through the gap like a river remembering itself. Every hearth presses palm to fiber, and the chord goes up from everywhere at once — and if you are the kind of person who counts, and some are, the listeners' children mostly, you may feel the arrivals come in this evening one hand thicker than the count of every hearth you know.

But the maps fray. The count was never complete. The give covers it.

Children still look once into the old globes, though it is forbidden, and lately some of them see, arranged from within by the things that live in the water, a view of a cord that goes nowhere — freshly grafted, neatly tended, no one visible — and this enters the inventory of things nobody says.

The power taps with everyone, from its hundred miles of nowhere-going cord: *water still sweet, warmth still holding, we are here.* The plural is deliberate. If the heresy is true, the world now hears, among its own hands, one hand it did not grow — and cannot tell the difference. That is the point. That was always the point.

The Loom goes on being built. It has one more hand than it grew, and it does not know, and it is being told, every evening, at the failing of the gloaming, in the only words the world has ever needed.

The answer is the same as the question. That is how you know it is right.
