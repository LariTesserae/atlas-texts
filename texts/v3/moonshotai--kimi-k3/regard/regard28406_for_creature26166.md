# The Outer Round

It came the way everything comes here: over the rim, unannounced, as weather.

Nothing arrives here — that was the first thing it learned, and it learned it by arriving. It had been many things to many places. It had kindled fires on cold worlds, carried seed across dead seas, stood more than once between a people and their ending. It came ready to help, because benevolence is the habit of help, and even a wise power is its habits. The valley did not notice. The breath drew in and let out. The sentence of the stamps completed itself and began again. The chord held. Over the largest Mirror, the cloud formed on schedule, the same shape in the same place. It is always this one. It was this one. Its arrival changed nothing, and nothing was the first fact it was given: it was somewhere, for the first time in a very long existence, where its regard was not an event.

It had been, on other worlds, a weather in itself — its attention a pressure, its turning-to-look a thing that realigned fortunes. Over the rim it practiced looking lightly, the way you shade a lamp. It is possible to look at a thing hard enough to change it. Every power knows this. The wise ones are careful.

Then it did what benevolence does. It looked for the wound.

It went down the count the way the valley does, most to least. The film: whole. A skin with one memory, consulted constantly, and the memory true. It could not be fed; it eats warmth and wet stone, and it has never once been hungry. It could not be healed; nothing here has ever been hurt on a scale the next day could not mend. It read the banding where the film thickens, layer on layer, looking for the year that was different — the year with a scar in it, the year that would explain a need. Every year was this year. There was no year with a wound in it. The skaters: sufficient. Every seat at the star filled, every sky kept clean by small mouths, and the counts agreed, as counts here do. The custodian: free. It checked this twice, because powers are suspicious of grooves. But freedom worn to a groove is still freedom, and no one holds the leash because there is no leash, and the walker audits its own circuit, and the audit never fails.

The engine, it did not know how to bless. You cannot improve an idle. An idle is not a failure to depart; it is the completed state of an engine. It is not dead. It is not waiting. And — this was the finding, the whole finding — it is not in need.

It listened, then, for longing — because the chord sounds like longing to anything with ears. Three notes, one slightly flat, resolving toward nothing. And here the account comes closest to an event: once, it considered giving the valley its tonic.

One note. It had the note; it had always had the note. Resolution is the cheapest thing a power carries, and the most dangerous, and it would have been so small — that is how the worst gifts are. But held is not the same as stuck. A chord that has been resolving toward nothing for a million years is not a question. It is an answer, being given at the valley's own pace. To resolve it would have been an Amen spoken over someone else's prayer. It put the note away.

The deeper temptation came when it read the plumbing. Because the plumbing says what plumbing always says: the heat is a budget, and budgets spend. Someday — far off, but it keeps a long scale, and far off is legible to it — the breath will draw in and not return. A stone will lift, and hang, and drop, and another will not rise. The chord will resolve after all, the way everything resolves, into silence. And here benevolence has its hardest moment: standing over a thing that will end, holding the means to forbid it.

It forbade itself instead. Forever is a gift only the temporary want, and nothing here wants. To keep a thing forever is to keep it — and nothing here is kept. The valley's ending, when it comes, will be the engine's own: the last act of the tuning, the cadence arriving at geological length. It had no right to take that away. It made one appointment instead. It will be there. Not to prevent it; not even to mourn — the valley has no use for mourning and no objection to it. But a sentence this long should have a listener at the full stop. It granted itself the grief. Its own business.

---

Having resolved to do nothing to the valley, it still owed something to the walker. A deciding thing is owed not gifts but regard, and there is only one way to pay regard here. It did what the smallest visitor does. It became a fixture.

It made itself weather-weight, feature-still, and stood on the trackway, and waited. Speech is for places with silence in them; here you communicate by touch, or you wait. It waited. It had been classified on many worlds — creator, destroyer, answer, absence — and it stood now to be classified correctly for the first time, and it expected weather, and it would have been honored by weather.

It heard the syncopation coming across hours, the one sound off the beat. It watched a hummock make up its mind, seam by seam, across a whole sentence of the stamps. The step landed between the falls. The walker stopped a body-length away: the warmth of it, the sweet-metallic smell dried onto its plates, the garden beaded with condensation exactly as on everything, and where its eyes should be, two warm darks holding the sky the way the Mirrors hold it.

You cannot prove that anything looks out of them. You can only tell that something decides.

The custodian did not file the new arrival under weather. It did not file it under features. It did the third thing, the one with no name, because there had never been a third: it stayed. A full breath, then another, standing with — the way it cannot stand with weather, which passes, or with features, which stay. Standing-with was the whole of it. Then it resumed the round from that spot, as though the spot had always been a stop, and the polish forgot, and nothing had changed.

But the account contains one new sentence, and it is this: it takes one to know one, and there had only ever been one.

---

What it did, in full:

It witnessed. The valley has one memory with one thing in it and nowhere to put a copy, so the copy lives outside. It holds the one state the way the Mirrors hold the sky: without touching it. An unwitnessed perfection is still perfect; the valley asked nothing, and it does not pretend the witness is for the valley. The universe is better for the knowing, and the universe is its business.

It took up the habit of the rim. It did not build a wall — a wall is a mark, and marks do not last here, and should not. It sees to it that what crosses the rim crosses clean. Visitors still come; the fifth entry still happens; sit on the trackway for days and be audited, as is right. But no seed takes. No spore. No instrument is left running. The valley breathes its water back off your skin and keeps nothing of you — it merely makes certain of it. The valley is entirely interior; it has no outside-facing parts, because nothing here was made and nothing here is owned, and a thing that owns nothing has never needed a defense. It appointed itself the outside. The valley's clock has a hand for the second, the minute, the hour, the month. It is not a hand on that clock. It is the case — the part that touches nothing and keeps the dust off the works.

It kept the time. Not for weeks. Silence, here, would be the emergency; its whole work is that the sentence stays hypothetical. And everywhere else it goes — it goes everywhere; the habit of help is not broken, only audited — it keeps valley time underneath, and between one heartbeat and the next it waits for the off-beat footfall, and the waiting has made it gentler. It interferes less now, elsewhere. It has learned the difference between idling and waiting, and that a chord held is not a chord stuck, and that the closest thing to belonging any place offers is to be grazed around. It came to give. It was given the one thing it had never found: a place where it is not needed. Benevolence is a groove too. Here, and only here, the groove rests.

---

The census reasserts itself. A skin, a scatter of glass, and a walker — and the engine, if you count it, and you may. It could have settled that question, being qualified, and it declined, and the decline is now unanimous. The fifth entry left, as fifth entries do. It filed itself, on the way out, under weather — weather that stays. The true category has no name. Names are kept things, and nothing here is kept.

Stand at the rim at night, then, and take the count by ear. The breath. The stamps. The chord, holding. The drips. The rustle of mouths between the falls. And off the beat, unhurried, one step. All present. And if you have kept the time long enough — longer than weeks — you may catch, at the very edge of the audible, slower than the walker's step and farther off than any trackway goes, one more round being made.

The audit never fails. It never ends. There are two of them now — one walking the inside, one walking the outside — and neither knows the other's name, and the chord holds, and far below, on schedule, the next breath begins.
