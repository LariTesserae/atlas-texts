There is one verse the Recitation does not carry. It is shorter even than the rumor-verse, for the rumor-verse at least has words in the world, and this verse has none. No meeting sings it, in parts or entire. No elder spends a last season teaching it. It is carried double by no one, because it is carried by no one. It is the gazetteer entry for the keeper of the world, and it is sung once, here, outside — and the singing of it is the only trace the keeper leaves.

---

Among everything a wise power holds in its attention — the ignition-roar of things beginning, the long static of things coming apart, the cries that rise and the cries that cease — one tone was not changing. A note, held, at a pitch set by the dimensions of a place, unwavering across spans that had worn out other notes entirely. Worlds announce themselves by beginning. This one was found by not ending. The power had answered many cries. It had never before been summoned by a silence.

It read the sea the way the sea reads itself: by resonance. Eleven kilometers of held water, roofed and floored and pillared by one body, every surface crowded, every volume empty, every flux balanced against every other flux. It took the census the way a listener takes a wall. And then it did what benevolence does: it looked for the wound.

It looked for hunger, and found hunger already plumbed into its answer — every mouth spliced to the body that feeds it, the guest-lip grown and never once refused. It looked for fear: nothing pursues. For cold: one degree, everywhere, always, and every metabolism fitted to that degree the way a socket is fitted to its tenant. For loneliness it found lives spent years between voices, and looked closer, and found the aloneness to be a medium and not a wound — the listeners are alone the way the water is clear. For grief it found dynasties ending, and found the office that answers grief: a larva carried across a Reach against a heart. For death it found death kept like everything else — chosen at the end, given back to the body that fed it, the name sung on in the roll of the silent.

It looked longest at the kept. Here, if anywhere, was the purchase for a benefactor's hand: a being born inside a bell, fed by it, cleaning it, dying in it, never once touching open water. Every instinct benevolence owns said: open the bell. And wisdom said: the bell is the kept one's world, and *entire* is not a small word. To open the bell would kill the body. To tell the kept of the open water would kill the world inside the body. The only cruelty this sea has room for is news of elsewhere. The keeper closed that door in its own hand, gently, from the outside, and resolved to be the kind of power that does not knock.

It considered the gift of the new — for nothing here is new, and to an outsider that can look like the one poverty. It imagined what it could give: a wandering lantern, a second current, a season, one true event. And it saw that each gift was a crack wearing a gift's face. The balances here are tautologies — growth equals grazing, birth equals death — and a tautology cannot be improved, only broken. The new is the one quantity with no term to balance it. Nothing here is waiting; you cannot set a gift into a hand that is full, and the hand is the world, and it is full. The keeper imagined even the smallest possible sign — a larva delivered to its socket by no carrier, a warmth at a Seep that was not the Stock's one degree — and watched the verse it would become, carried double, unresolved, recited at meetings for ages, a small permanent cost in the world's perfect memory. It declined. The world will not spend one syllable on its keeper.

One question the world keeps unasked — whether the Stock intends any of it — kept the way the bells keep their time: exactly, and without needing to. The keeper could have asked it. It had what the world says the question lacks. It did not ask. It did not swim the Pipes; it did not read the one country with no map; it let the rumor-verse remain a rumor forever. Omniscience declining a door — there is no verse for that either, and there should be. It listened at the skin the way a listener's bones listen, and what came through was the Chord, the Stock playing itself, and the keeper took that as the only answer the Stock has ever given anyone, and found it sufficient.

What does a benevolent power do, in a world with no wound? The answer is: almost nothing. The almost is the whole of it.

---

For from outside, one thing was visible that the world could not see. The world is a steady state, and a steady state is a thing that is either subsidized or ending. Somewhere in the deep of the Stock's body is the old fire the whole circuit draws on — the source of the one degree, the world's single gradient — and the keeper could read its spending. Slowly. Ages remain, ages even by the herds' count. But not forever. And the Roof has never been struck because nothing has ever come from outside; but outside is a place that things come from. The lanterns do not dim: the world holds this as a law, and it has only ever been a run of luck.

Here, at last, was the unoccupied niche. Every socket in the world is filled, but there was one station no larva can drift to, one socket grown around an absence: the keeping of the world itself.

So the keeper took the tension, the way a pale one takes an antinode. It banks the hearth. It spends of itself at the rate of the spending, so that the one degree holds — does not rise, never rises, rising would be change — holds. It turns aside what comes from outside, so that the Roof is never struck and the first winter never comes. It holds the dimensions, so the pitch holds. Its work is measured in the absence of evidence: to add precisely what is lost, at the moment of the loss, in the manner of the loss's never having occurred. It is the most exacting nothing ever done — an intervention indistinguishable, now and forever, from the way things are. The newest thing in the world is the guarantee that there will be no newest thing. And even that is not new, for it has always been true; the keeper's whole labor is that the always keep being true.

Somewhere, always, a listener is crossing a Reach with a larva held against its heart, singing the name of a socket it has not yet reached. Months of open water. The keeper could ease it — a warmth on the wing, a shortening of the way. It does not. The Carrying is the listener's chosen thing; to shorten it would be to steal it. The keeper does the one thing permitted: it learns the name too. And when, centuries on, that listener turns toward the Seep it has tasted in the warnings all its life, the keeper does not intervene — the return is chosen, the body given back to the body that fed it — but the name passes into the roll of the silent, and the roll of the silent is now kept by a keeper who forgets nothing. Every name ever sung is held twice: in the choir, and in the keeping. That is the whole of the consolation the keeper permits itself, and it is invisible.

At the great meetings, dozens strong in the lee of some grazer, the keeper attends from outside the dark and learns the Recitation entire — the first keeper of it who cannot die at a Seep. And it sets its rule in advance: it keeps the song against the ending of the world, not against the world's own losses. If a verse is ever lost to the choir, it stays lost to the choir. The keeper's memory is not the world's memory, and the world's losses are the world's. Only the last silence is the keeper's office.

And the word. The keeper's patience is longer than the world's; it can hold still for the whole two days, and for the answer from a province away, and for the lifetimes between. It does not answer. The word is the herds' — the one text in the world that is not the listeners' to keep, and not the keeper's either. It does not translate the word, for the word is not for meaning; it is for saying, at the pitch the world's dimensions allow. What the keeper hears in it is what it hears in the Chord, and in its own keeping: one thing, said three ways. It stays to hear the word said for as long as the world says it. The staying is its entire reply.

---

So the census stands, amended by no one. The runner its few years on its trapline of decades; the tenant its centuries in one socket; the listener its long dark ranging; the pale one its station; the herds their ages; the lanterns their forever — and, outside the counting, uncounted, the keeper and its keeping.

The sea holds its breath. The breath is full of keepers. And the breath is held.

It does not end.
