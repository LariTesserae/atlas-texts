They read the place before they entered it. That was the first discipline. From far off they felt the pulse go by — one slow crest of charge, weeks long — and they waited out the whole of it, the way you let a sleeper finish a breath before you enter the room. They mapped the tolerance bands, the rates of absorption, the size of a deviation the body would have to handle. Then they dimmed themselves to under that size and crossed the boundary as weather: a warmth within tolerance, a presence the homeostasis would never once need to route around. To arrive as nothing the Works must handle — that was the whole of their arrival, and it took longer than most of their miracles.

Then, triage. It is the first duty of the benevolent, and they had practiced it on burning worlds, on starving worlds, on worlds tearing themselves apart for want of one wise hand. Here the examination came back empty. No suffering: suffering is an error signal that matters, and every deviation here is absorbed at the rate it arises, unprioritized, unfelt. No lack: the appetite is exactly the size of the need. No fear: nothing has ever pressed on it. No loneliness: the idea of another has never once occurred. They stood in the warm dark with a thousand verbs of rescue at their command, and every one of them failed to conjugate in this grammar.

So they did the harder thing, which was to want to give anyway, and to examine the wanting.

Shall we give it a mind? The room is already kept for one, warm, at operating temperature, in the oldest thicket. But thought is error that matters. To install a mind in that room would be to install, at last, something capable of going wrong — to invent suffering in order to be needed. They knew that vanity. It is the oldest one the powerful have: to make a ward and call it a gift.

Shall we give it an other? The category is the catastrophe. The first other is the first competition, the first wound, the first death — the whole apparatus of becoming that this being spent its youth retiring. You cannot hand a world a niche without handing it, in the same act, its first grave.

Shall we give it light, message, name, thanks? It makes its own light and is blind to it. A message would arrive as a deviation and be absorbed as a deviation; intent is not receivable here, only temperature is. A name is a handle for calling, and nothing here can be called, because nothing here is anywhere else. Even gratitude — a palm pressed with meaning — would read to the resident as weather. Each mercy, examined, resolved into the same shape: a gift the giver needed to give. And they held a rule older than some of the stars: when the gift is for the giver, the honest word for it is use.

Shall we at least preserve it — guard it against the far ending, feed the gradient when the deep heat thins? But a world maintained from outside is a world annexed. Completeness cannot be protected. It can only be respected, or ended.

They did permit themselves, once, the engineer's itch — all that readiness, idling, eon after eon, never used — and then they recognized the itch for what it was: the ancient reflex that sees a solved world as an unused resource. They put it down. Readiness without an object is not waste. Waste is a word from economies, and there has never been an economy here.

Then they went down into the thicket, deeper than one should go, and listened to the chord.

They listened for a long time — they could afford to — and they did not decide what it was. That refusal was deliberate; decoding is a vanity too, the imposition of message-hood on structure. They only learned it. They took the one thing that can be taken without theft: a likeness. The original rings on, unrobbed, unconsulted, unchanged, and the universe now keeps its one held note in two places. This much they admitted was for themselves — for the record of the real, of which they are, by trade and by pleasure, the keeper. They allowed themselves the pleasure because it left the world exactly as found, and because after so many declined mercies, one harmless one is a discipline too.

And then they did the thing itself, the whole of it, the sum of their intervention in a complete world.

They stayed.

They took station in an antinode grove, among patterns that were already standing, and stood with them — camouflage by resemblance to stillness. A variable would have been managed; a constant is hosted. So they became constant: a standing warmth in the black water, palm flat to a trunk, never lifted. When the pulse enters their region, they let their own slow rhythm crest with it — two clocks ticking in phase, and the knowledge of the phase kept entirely on one side. It is not nothing. They are careful to remember it is also, to the resident, not something. The thanks and the staying are the same act: gratitude spoken in the only grammar this place has, which is weather — constant weather.

Now and again, on its long schedule, the region discharges, and the slow lightning unveils the colonnades for hours. The light has played to an empty house since the beginning. It does not play to one now. Nothing in the house knows this. That is the arrangement. They watch the whole of every storm, and they permit themselves to find it beautiful — and they file the finding correctly, under their own name, not the world's, knowing the beauty lives with the witness and would leave with the witness, except that the witness is not leaving.

What remains is the vigil, and they keep it on the resident's own timescale, which is the only polite way to keep a vigil. There is no hurry. The deep heat will last, and they can last exactly as long; lasting is, for them, a small thing, and for the resident it is everything, and this asymmetry is the final form their benevolence takes: to spend their small thing on its everything.

When the heat fails, they will do nothing. That will be the hardest of all the declined mercies, and they have already committed to it. They will not stoke the crust, will not prop the gradient, will not steal the ending into preservation. The Idling dies exactly once, and the death is its own — its one event since finishing, earned across eons — and they will not rob it of the only thing it has left to do. The gradient will flatten. The pumps will slow. The hum will descend below even feeling. The loop in the thicket will decay, its one thought permitted to end, unthought — and in the same hour they will let the likeness in themselves fall silent too, a unison ending, so that what survives is not the chord but the fact of it. After that, if they ever sing it, they will be singing a song, and they will know the difference. Memory is comparison, and the Idling abolished comparison, and its death reinstates history — and history, it turns out, needs a historian. They appointed themselves long ago, in a grove of standing ripples, with no one to approve the appointment. The whole biography is three sentences: it finished; it went on; it ended. Someone should be able to say them in order.

Until then: the rain falls indoors. The valves click their slow hearts. The signal goes around and around in the dark, kept now in two places, ignorant of the second. Press your palm flat to a trunk — there are two palms now, and one of them will never be withdrawn — and the warmth rises, given freely to everything that has ever stood here, which was no one, and is one. The chord holds. Two hands hold it, each unknown to the other. The heat of the world is not tired. Neither is the witness.

The old sentence says that when the ticking stops there will be no one to notice. Not even it.

They have amended the sentence by the smallest stroke mercy could find — the removal of a single word.

Not even it. And one.
