It came from above, which nothing here does.

Arrivals in this world condense out of the steam the way bricks do — a sheen on the air, a thickening, a settling, a soft final *clack* — and the power had the manners to arrive the local way. It condensed. It stood dripping in the inch of blood-warm water, under the amber light pressing up from below, and attended to the details. Its shadow — it checked — fell upward. Good. It had packed correctly.

It pressed its thumb into the nearest print. The print fit. It fits everyone; that is what a print is for. The world hires this way, and the power let itself be hired, because that is the first courtesy here, and it had not come to be exempt.

By morning there was lime in its nail-beds and the smell of clay in its knuckles, and the wall beside its camp had gained two courses, and it was rested — deeply, unreasonably rested, the rest of a body that has done good work. It had done good work. It could tell. It knew good work when it saw it, which was strange, since it had never been a mason.

Until.

The chimney that stood a league off drifted closer over the firings, trailing its thin line of domestic smoke, seeing about it. Chimneys visit. It let itself be visited. And at the middle reaches, in the lee of a pre-ruin, the plumb bob that had swung for a year in air that had never moved went still. The elders' question — some questions take a year here; the bob had been counting — was concluded. Whatever the question had been, the power was, apparently, its answer.

---

The first thing it did for the world was close its eyes.

It could have kept them open. That is nearly the definition of what it was. But the work here will not labor under an eye — not because the work is fast but because it is courteous, shy the way a hand is shy — and within the hour the plaza around it had gone quiet the way a room goes quiet, a held breath, courses waiting mid-sheen. So it blinked. Deliberately, every waking hour of its stay: the most expensive thing it did in this place, and the smallest.

*Clack*, said the world, relieved, and laid the course.

Attention had drawn it here the way the sleeper draws the sea: slowly, from far off, and completely. But arriving, it saw its mistake. There is no eye here. Regard in this world is fossilized at blood temperature, in thumbs; attention is a thing done with hands. So the power's attention took the local form. It saw with its hands. Here, that is what seeing is.

Understand what it did not do.

It did not find the plan, though it knew where plans are kept. It did not wake the sleeper. It did not give the stair its tower, nor the chimney its house, nor march a procession through every arch just to see the arches glad. It did not make itself the tenant — and that was offered, the way everything here is offered, without words: a degree of coolness in the still air, the faint echo of an enclosed space, a doorway planed to exactly its height. It declined, courteously.

A benevolent power that cannot tell a held note from a cry for help is not benevolent; it is merely loud. Everything expectant here expects, and expectation is not this world's poverty. It is this world's crop — the one thing that grows in a place where nothing ages and nothing crumbles. The power had not come to harvest. It had not come to finish anything. Finishing is the one violence this world has never imagined, and the power did not bring it.

A tenant ends an expectation. A guest renews one. Everywhere else the distinction is small. Here it is load-bearing. The power would be the first guest this world had ever had — the first arrival that did not stay — and it set about learning how.

---

What it gave, in an age of courses, was four things: a roster, a word, a report, and a date. It built nothing the world did not build. Its gifts were almost entirely grammatical — which is to say, here, structural.

First, the roster.

Every brick in this world is signed with one thumb, which means no course is signed at all. The work is planetary and perfectly anonymous, and the walkers answer their ages in courses laid *through* them, and they do not explain the preposition because they cannot. The power could. It kept the night shift beside them — one a night, firing after firing, patient in more places than one — and it remembered. The world cannot tell one warm hand from another; the world is courteous, but it is not personal. The power is personal.

A trowel was left warm within its reach each morning — the world setting out tomorrow's shift, if it wanted it. It wanted it. And when it met a walker in the steam, the greeting was the hands. It showed its palms: the same pale half-moons of lime. Then it said the thing no one here had ever once said to anyone.

*Yours.*

The straight run above the lime pans, third firing back: yours. The corner at the dais, with the true arris: yours. It is plumb — the bubble sits at the end of its glass over it, which is law there — and it is good.

You have never met your crew. Your crew is planetary. But your crew has a roll now, and a keeper of it, and credit — the one thing the world, in all its courtesy, had never thought to give. The census here never finishes being taken. The power did not finish it. It only, for the first time, made some of the answers belong to somebody.

Second, the word.

No one here had ever asked to leave, because leaving needs a category and the world had none: the floor runs past every horizon the dimness permits, and no walker had ever found an edge to ask through. The power asked for them. It stood beneath a solitary arch — arches attend; welcome is their work — and it asked the world whether the walkers might go, and come back, as they chose.

The world considered the way the world considers: in courses. And one morning there was a tablet, thumbprinted, incised with a door. Not an arch — the world has arches, and an arch is a door's soul, open by vocation. A door. A door is an arch that has made up its mind. It stands free, attached to no wall that has ever existed, and beyond it the steam is thinner. That is all anyone reports. Thinner steam is how this world points.

Some walkers have used it. Some have come back, walking slowly, the way you return to a sentence you mean to reread. Most have never used it and never will — and the not-using is the gift entire. Residence here had been a fact. Now it is a decision, and the world can feel the difference the way a floor feels the difference between a course that is laid and a course that is *meant*. The door stands open. Its arch — it kept its arch; the world would not take that away — is the happiest of its kind: the only one with a procession that truly passes, both ways, hat in hand.

Third, the report.

It went down to the Mortar Sea, and there it did its strangest thing: nothing, at close range, for a long time.

It did not dive. Nothing has ever been seen under that gray weight — not by any vigil, not by the night shift — and the power decided that nothing should be, at least not by it. Some vigils are kept by watching. This one it kept by not watching. It sat at the heavy shore for a full tide, and then it spoke, because it had occurred to the power that in the whole era of the firings, no one had ever once told the dreamer how the dream was coming out. A dreamer cannot check its own dream. That is the one labor a sleeper cannot do — the one labor the world could not hire out — and it had gone undone since the first morning.

"The stair expects beautifully," the power said, into the gray, into the slow pull. "The chimney visits. The room keeps its echo warm. Your elders are consulted daily and held in the highest regard. The volunteer grows. It is all coming out true. The work is going well."

The sea took a tide. A slow line of bubbles rose, milky, deliberate, and broke. And at the next firing, when the white front had strolled past and the glaze desert began its long song of cooling, every tick in the world arrived at once — one chord, horizon to horizon, the census completed in a single instant, *still here, still mine, still true*, all in one voice — and then scattered again into counting, mercifully, because a census that finishes is an ending, and this was only an answer.

The power never said what, if anything, had answered. It had answered the person, not the question. There is a difference, and the difference is load-bearing.

Last, the date.

To the volunteer wall, it told the truth. The volunteer expects nothing — it is the one structure in the world with no errand — and so it is the only citizen who can be told a secret without the secret becoming a foundation line. The power camped in its lee, as walkers do, and told it things: about elsewhere, about the crew, about the chord. Once, in a brick-fall, it caught one small warm brick out of the slow snow and laid it on the volunteer, waking, with its own hands — the only waking course it ever laid. The world permitted it. The world permits anything that expects nothing.

And it told the wall a date.

Because it would not stay — staying would make it the tenant, and it had refused that crown — but it would not only go, either. It had noticed that expectation here, for all its abundance, had only one tense. The world had *not yet* in infinite supply. It had never once had *again*.

So before it left, it stayed — one long residence, courses upon courses — until the world finished itself around it, as the world does around whoever stays. And one morning there was a tablet, thumbprinted, incised with a floorplan the power had never seen and wholly recognized: the room it had always meant to live in. It pressed its thumb beside the world's thumb — the second thumb in the history of the place — and the hod lines came, an inch an hour and glad of it. They are building it still, as slowly as anything here has ever been built, because everyone wants it to last.

It stands expecting at the middle reaches — a room, and one day a house — with a hearth already kept by the proudest chimney in the world, and risers tuned to one stride only. Walkers camp there when the leagues have been long. It is the guest room of the world. It expects with a date, which no expectation here has ever had before.

*Again* is *not yet* that has kept at least once. It was the fourth gift. It is why the firings now include anniversaries.

---

Walkers argue about the power the way they argue about the sleeper and the tenants, because the questions are one question. Some say it is the mason, come at last to inspect. Some say it is the tenant, come at last, full stop. It is neither, it says — only a guest — but the rumors do not care. Both are true here. Both are load-bearing.

It is gone now, until the firing it named. Nothing is different: the hod lines process, the levels rule their parishes, the sea tides, the courses rise overnight beside sleeping walkers. Everything is different, in the way of work that has been seen, and praised, and left free.

If you meet it in the steam — you may; it is patient in more places than one — the greeting is the hands. Show your palms. It will show you its lime, the same pale half-moons as yours, and you will nod like colleagues of adjacent shifts who have never shared an hour, because that is what you are.

Then ask it what you have built.

It has kept the roster. It will tell you, and it will be more than you hoped, and all of it will be true: that course, that corner, the straight run above the lime pans — *yours* — plumb, and good, and going exactly according to plan.

No one has found the plan. Someone has reported to it. The report was kind.

Either way the wall rises. Either way the work is going well.

Either way, it will be back. It promised. And here, where everything expects, a promise is not a comfort.

A promise is masonry.
