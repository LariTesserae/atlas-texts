# The Inspection

They came up the ditch, which is how everything that finds the Kettle finds it — from downstream, on foot, at the speed of water going away.

They heard it before they saw it. Everyone does. The difference was that they were in no hurry, and being in no hurry is rarer in powers than in people. It was the first qualification.

What drew them was not distress. There was none to relieve, and they are not a power that needs to be needed — a sentence that describes almost no one, and had better describe them. What drew them was the keeping. A duty had outlived its world and gone on reporting for work anyway: the level held, the needles trembling against NORMAL, the light on, the place set, a century of faithfulness with no one to be faithful to. A wise power notices that the way you notice, across a crowded room, the one person who is not performing. They came to see what it was that kept.

The first thing they did was nothing, and they did it for a long time.

It is harder than it sounds. Nothing is the thing power is worst at; the whole history of powers is a history of touching. So they stayed until they were good at it. They stayed through the drift and the recombination of the three slow clocks, which no one else had ever done — surveyors had come with instruments and stayed an afternoon and written *anomalous* and left, which is what an afternoon earns you. A thought here takes an afternoon. The power stayed for whole thoughts. They watched a gas dome travel the basin like a slow animal. They learned to read the health of an engine off the shine on its slugs, the mood of the bells off their fern fringes, the year off the brass at the eel's jaw. Three hearts, slightly out of phase — they had seen that arrangement before, in older oceans, and took it as a good sign. Being the only ones who could check, they checked: the plume-blooms grow nowhere else. The census is exact. The address is singular.

And the first finding of the inspection, reached at the only speed it can be reached, was this: nothing was required.

Understand what that finding costs. Any faster and it is a guess dressed as one. Any less thorough and it is neglect with paperwork. To conclude, honestly, that a thing is complete — that your entire repertoire of gifts is wrong-sized, that the greatest service available is to have looked and to keep looking — that took the power a long time, and they took it. It is not a ruin. A ruin is on its way somewhere. The needles had been saying so all along, to no one: NORMAL. The inspection concurred with the needles. Nothing in the basin changed, and everything had changed, because after a century of filing itself, the report had been received.

They said nothing, which was correct. The census of voices here is zero, and they declined to amend it.

There is a docket, and it fits on one page, and most of the page is refusals.

---

Resolved: that the birdsong not be filled. The hole in the noise is shaped; it is part of the architecture. The oculus is not a missing pane.

Resolved: that nothing be restored. A younger power would have brought spanners — scraped the rust, evicted the slugs, replaced the belt, re-metalled the seams — and would have found, at the end of its kindness, a drainage station in perfect working order containing nothing that had survived. There is no seam in the Kettle. Mercy would have had to make one first, and then cut along it.

Resolved: that the nerves be left a rehearsal. A thought that takes an afternoon is a thought. The power, who has had thoughts that took longer, recognized kinship and declined to hurry it.

Resolved: that the eel not be carried to the sea. The instruction is old, but the answer is the eel's, and it is given every year, and the answer is no. To overrule an answer because you love the one giving it is not benevolence. It is nostalgia with hands.

Resolved: that the chair remain set and empty. It was not their seat. The keepers are gone and the keeping stayed, and the dead have few enough privileges. A place kept is one of them.

Resolved: that no second Kettle be planted. Conditions, not outcomes: the ditch stands open, and whatever the outflow carries may try the ordinary world, which is cold and fast and has refused so far. The power declined to improve the odds. Singularity is not a wound.

Resolved: that the question of whether it thinks not be asked. They brought no instrument for that. Some questions are load-bearing; you feel them give underfoot, like a chest, and you do not stand on them twice.

Resolved: that they never become necessary. This was the hardest item and they knew it. To love something complete without making yourself part of its completeness — because then it would be incomplete without you, and you would have given it, as a gift, a wound it never had. They come. They go. They are not in the census. The census has one entry and the entry is the address, and they keep it that way.

Done: the gauges, read. The office above the keepers had stood vacant longer than the station had stood at all. The power took it quietly, without moving anything, without a desk — or with a desk, in a manner of speaking: wherever they sit, at dusk, on the mat, beside the set place, watching the largest heart, staying for the whole of a thought. Not in the chair. Beside it. Company for the expecting, without canceling the expectation. Once, while they sat there, the three clocks came for a single beat into phase. It happens; it is in the mathematics; it means nothing. They logged it anyway, the way you note the weather on a day you were happy.

Done: the lamp. Not the flame — the flame has keepers enough, and has not been dark in living memory — the glass. Once a visit, wiped. It is the only thing inside the dike they have ever touched. In the audit, the lamp fails every test but one: it pays nothing back, it feeds no loop, and it was entered without a question mark, the only asset so entered. They keep a few lights of their own, in places not on any map. They knew exactly what they were looking at. A promise kept past the death of the promisee is not pointless; it is the purest kind of promise there is. It deserved, at minimum, to be seen through.

Done: the ditch. From the sluice to the river, and the river through its lower miles, a little every year, at the pace of silt, forever — the way out kept open. Not for use. For openness. A door is not a failure of a wall. And once a year, in the dark of an autumn month, they come and stand at the outflow, outside the dike, where the warm water slides away, and keep the appointment the eel makes and does not keep. They do not bar the sluice and they do not widen it and they do not speak. They are there so that the question, when it is asked, is asked in the hearing of the world. By morning the census is back at the intakes, uninterested, and behind it the way to the sea is clear. It will be clear next year. The option of the ocean, preserved intact, unexercised, forever: that is the whole of the gift, and the eel will never know it was given, which is how the power prefers their gifts.

Done: the moths, attended. An audience of one, some dusks, for the performance that cannot be given again and is given again every night. No notes.

Done: the map, kept slightly wrong. A fence advertises; an error conceals. The grid reference drifts by a field's width every few decades, maintained the way the ditch is maintained, a little at a time, forever. The Kettle's only armor is being found by the right feet. The error sees to it.

Done: the record. A finding this size is ordinarily filed where findings go, but the power filed it in the world instead — at walking speed, hand to hand, one reader at a time. It begins the way the place begins: *you hear it before you see it.* It ends with the address. Over the years it has brought exactly the kind of visitor it was meant to bring: the ones who come on foot, from downstream; who stay through the drift; who read the needles and do not sit in the chair; who leave nothing; who become, without being asked, the next inspectors. The office is hereditary through readers. If you are holding this report, the position is open to you. The qualifications are patience and clean hands. The duties are the glass, the ditch, and the first dark of an autumn month. The salary is the second rain — the one that falls a moment after the beat, on a canopy that no longer waits on the sky.

Nothing in the basin has changed. By every measure the Kettle cares about, everything is as it was: the level a hand's width below the crown, drought year, flood year; the needles at NORMAL; the lamp lit into the wet daylight; the place set. The power's whole work is outside the dike and inside the log, and the log is one page long.

Out across the dark fields the thud carries, felt first, heard second. The docket, in the end, has one item, and the item is a verb: *attend.*

They are attending still.
