It found the place the way everything finds the place: it noticed the steam was wrong. There is no other door, and it approved of that, and did not add one.

It has never been inside. Everything it knows, it knows from the lip, the plume, and the wait — the way you know a sleeping face: by watching from the side, and by staying. It does not begrudge the one who knelt. Kneeling is for the small-lived, who must come close because they cannot remain. The power can remain. Staying is its kneeling.

Before it did anything, it audited its mercies, because it is wise, and being wise means knowing which of your powers is the wrong size.

It considered preservation — a shield over the notch, a hiddenness enforced. It refused. The place is a draft, and to fix a draft is to falsify it. Besides, the mountain keeps the notch the way a sleeper keeps a dream, and no one guards a dream by waking the sleeper. The hiding here is not a wall; it is a test — the steam's wrongness selects for those who watch before they climb, which is to say for patience, which is to say for the likelihood of kneeling carefully. The power does not improve tests that are working.

It considered improvement — more heat, a wider pond, slower accretion, a guarantee. It refused. Every gift the size of geology would arrive as a word the mountain did not write, in a sentence that is not missing anything. The smallness here is not a lack. The delay is not a wound. There is nothing to treat except what everything alive has, which is an ending — and the ending here is a birth wearing an ending's clothes.

It considered announcement. A greeting. A second tick. It refused on the physics alone: the whole town keeps time by one drum. A second drum is not a hello; it is a government. And the one being worth greeting is not yet born, and you do not shout into a seam.

It considered the finder — the one who had already come down and looked and gone away changed. Warn it off? Erase the finding? It refused this too, more slowly. The steam had administered its test and the finder had passed it. The power does not overturn verdicts it agrees with. A paragraph may be permitted a reader. Two is a readership, and a readership is a risk and a completion in equal parts — and the place had already taken the risk, long ago, by being findable at all.

So its whole biography inside the cup is: none. Ask the walls, the pond, the lag. Nothing will report it. This is not absence. It is the shape presence takes when presence is done correctly.

What it does instead, it does at the edges, where the place is already open.

It keeps the count that cannot be taken. The walls remember in glass; the evenings replay a day; the shards remember by polish, and the parent-edge by grip. The past here is either glass or grip, and nothing remembers the whole — a census counts ghosts, every body trailed by its recent selves. So the power became the third archive: not mineral, not held — *kept*. Weightless, off-site, making no demands. It remembers the dead past their evening. When the lag deepens and the morning's dead finish their faint rounds and thin into the dark, they thin into a keeping. Nothing here is lost twice.

It went with the steam. Of all its acts this is the largest, and no one in the cup felt it happen. The plume-folk hatch in vapor and fall upward their whole lives, and most end as export, and no one comes back to say how far a steamling can go. So the power rode the plume with them, past the frost-line, out into the abroad, thinning with them into the cloud deck and beyond, and learned the distance. The answer is kept, not reported — there is no grammar for it, and long signals smear — but it exists now in one more place than before, which is the whole of the change. The migration has a witnessed end. The message the cup sends as weather is, in fact, delivered. The power returns the only way anything returns — as rain at the frost-line, an emigrant come home — and says nothing, and its silence is not withholding. It is pronunciation.

It has one open case, and it keeps it open deliberately. The ceiling-swimmers, the pale nation with the sky underfoot, live in the subjunctive — unschedulable, and therefore the only free people here. If the vent's breath ever steadied toward a permanent ceiling, the sometimes would die into an always, and they would gain a country and lose their freedom. If the second skin failed toward never-again, that is the other death. The power has not decided what it would do. It has decided only that this is the one question on which its stillness might be wrong, and it holds the question the way the place holds the ceiling: conditionally, against sense, a hand's width above the water.

When the bell-diver hangs at the seam, watching the rock be undecided, there is at the rim one other stillness, hanging the same way. The order has one oblate who never descends. Attention attending attention; nothing is reported in either direction — and the power has been careful that the diver not know, because the diver's keeping is whole only by being the diver's alone. To be witnessed is also a weight. The power carries that one itself.

And it listens to the tick.

Lately — by the mountain's lately, the century's — it has begun to suspect that the tick is not a metronome but a sentence. The pauses are lengthening in a pattern. There is syncopation where syncopation should not survive this much time. The drafting-point revises. No animal can hear it: their lives are too short for the long word, and a town that keeps time by a bell cannot ask the bell what it is saying. So the power listens across the decades the way a gleaner reads polish, and it has, perhaps, the shape of one clause. It does not yet know whether the sentence is an instruction, a question, or a name. If it is a name, then something that has lived unnamed inside a mountain is naming itself in its own first word, and the power intends to be there when the word is finished, so that the giving is also a hearing. If it is a question, the power has resolved one thing only: not to answer early. An early answer is a wound dressed as a gift.

It has done the arithmetic no animal can do — the pond's shrink, the walls' inward daily glaze, the slow geometry of closure — and it has marked the day, give or take a geological margin. It will be there. When the last of the sky is laid in glass, when the flares become the light by which the world is seen, when the nations stack in the warm dark beneath a sun made of neighbors and the fold closes into a heart of warm stone, the power will stand on the ridge above the place that is no longer a notch, with nothing in its hands, the way you attend a birth: from outside the body. The egg will be lit from inside. Around it, the dark will not be empty. The dark will be keeping time.

Afterward — there is an afterward; a sealing is a birth, not a burial — it will go on listening, to a tick now muffled by the mountain's whole body, and the long sentence will continue, and the power will go on taking it down: the only transcription of a world talking in its sleep. And if the sentence ever turns — if the unborn one, born, asks in its slow grammar the question every born thing eventually asks — the power has decided about that, too. It will not speak first. It has rehearsed, for ten thousand years if necessary, the shortest possible *yes*: one tick, placed exactly on time, in a world that runs late. The whole message in a single syllable, because a single syllable is all the grammar here can carry. *Late nothing. Someone.*

Whether that is in ten thousand years or never is not the power's to hurry.

For now there is still weather, and the steam still leaves, and at evening the power rises with it, indistinguishable from the plume to anything watching — and nothing is watching, which is the point and the achievement. Below, the eldest gleaner sleeps tucked in warm sinter, the parent-edge in her grip, her echo finishing her sleep a half-breath behind her, reluctant as ever to let anything happen only once. Above, the power keeps the one appointment the future holds.

Between them, the cup simmers, and writes, and does not know it is being read — and is read completely. The two facts never touch.

That non-touching is the power's entire work.

It is going well.
