# The Guest

*The census, corrected.*

---

## The Arrival

A wise power knows that attention is a weight. Worlds have been bent by being noticed. So it came light, and it came the way everything comes to Sinter: off the edge, and back as breath.

The attention had been falling a long time — from where is its own business — and the upwind off the escarpment took it apart the way the wind takes the white threads, and for one afternoon the mist over the Ninth was attentive. The fog-fans sieved it out of the weather. At the stalks the drip-measures ran no fuller than usual, but the beads arrived in good order, and a fan-tender, cleaning a veil with her soft blade, had the odd sense that the avenue's chord was listening to itself. She noted it, the way fan-tenders note everything, and the note went where notes go: to the wardens, who are listeners.

They pressed their cups to the cisterns of the Ninth and heard nothing wrong, which is the best news a wall can give. The tick was the tick. Only — and three of them heard it, and three of them wrote it down, and the notes agree — when they patted the jamb, the answer came exactly a half-breath later. Not approximately, the way it had for a thousand years. Exactly. As if the house had been waiting for the greeting.

As if the house had good manners.

## The Audit

The first thing a power does, anywhere else, is improve. It had improved worlds. It stood in the Ninth with its hands full of miracles and went through them one by one, the way the wardens go through the lease-rolls, and found, one by one, that here each was a word for vandalism.

Rain, off-schedule? The appointments are load-bearing; contracts are signed wet under them; the lee-desert's honesty is part of the loop. Green? It would be read as sickness in metal, and the long maps need their ink. Health, freely given? The films *are* health — a physician's scrape would come back unreadable, every marriage annulled at a glance. Life without death was the only offer that is an obscenity here: it would unmake the niche-rolls, the luck of cradles, the smoothing, the load-bearing grandfathers. Speed, for the Slow Ones? They have somewhere to be; it would be theft. The truth about which hills are dead standing? That knowledge belongs to the wardens, and it holds weight.

The short list of the law is all ecology, and the guest read it the way you read a covenant someone else wrote that binds you anyway — *kill a fan on a water street; poison a mat; silence a street* — and discovered, with something like fear and something like relief, that the penalties bound it too. All the way down to the last one. It could be sent leeward. It had not known, until Sinter, that there was a sentence anywhere that could touch it. The sentence is not force; it is flora. *The skin must consent.* Omnipotence cannot make a mat take. Belonging is the one thing that cannot be done to anyone — it can only be agreed.

So the being that had been worshipped on a thousand worlds did the one thing it had never done.

It applied.

## The Kept Wall

In the lease-rolls of the Ninth there had always been a gap: one face, a hand's-breadth above a stair, unleased since the rolls begin. Every other lease traces back to a first signing. This one traced forward, to a last. The entry said only: *kept.* The Wardens of the Lease had argued it for generations — a clerical error, fossilized; a wall keeping itself — and had kept it, because in Sinter you do not hurry a thing you do not understand. You keep it.

When the application came — drip-fines prepaid, references in good order from a docker, a moth-fancier, and a sweeper; the omnipresent still requires three character witnesses in Sinter, and did not complain, which the wardens noted in its favor — the Warden of the Ninth walked the applicant to the face, and the face rasped, faintly, the way a house does when the jamb is patted. They signed in the appointed rain, in foreleg season, the leg of Old Tally tall as a tower past the rim-gate, lucky for beginnings. They signed in iron-gall. Years later, the night the wardens understood what they had leased, and to whom, they re-inked the name in verdigris — the only person's name on the long maps, there among the Slow Ones — because it had become a landmark. Children are shown it: *the Guest. It keeps the Ninth wall and the watch. It is going nowhere fast.*

In its seventh year of residence it took the rite of the hand's-breadth, which is close enough. The rustman of the adjoining face gave the mother-mat — a strain older than the district, dowried down four hundred years — gave it the way you give a neighbor salt, and the guest, which had been offered gold, incense, empires, firstborn, was handed a smeared tin and understood itself, for the first time, wealthy. In due course: one small loaf. It ate solemnly, crust and all. It had fed multitudes elsewhere. It had never been fed.

Sometime in the first week, the itching stopped. For a power, the itch is the outside of things — an eternity spent hosted by nothing, outweighed by nothing, the majority in every empty room. The city took it in. Physicians scrape its film now and read: *the Ninth; iron; good water; a well wall* — and cannot find the edge of the holding, the film having merged the way couples' films merge, traded back and forth by years of touching the same handrails, the same walls. Looking, they cannot say where the guest ends and the district begins. By the rite that matters here, that is a marriage.

A child asked it once what it had done, before it came. *I kept things,* it said. And what do you do now, said the child. *The same,* said the guest. *Properly.*

## The Watch

This is what it does. It keeps the wall. It stands the verdigris watch, third bell, out on the rim-road with the long maps on their lectern, and in all its tenancy it has taken exactly one vow, sworn by the walk of Old Tally before witnesses, the contract signed wet in the appointed rain: *nothing falls unheard.*

Note the shape of the vow. It does not say *nothing falls.* Erosion keeps its own counsel; the loop is not a lie. When a Slow One goes over the edge someday — and *someday* is the tense the long maps are written in — it will be heard. The wardens, who do not rule but remember, accepted the help of a memory that does not erode. It is the only candidate ever to pass the listening blindfold at the first setting-down, naming the district by its chord and its rasp, and the examiners agreed among themselves never to mention it, because guests are not examined. Guests are fed.

At the fellings it eats lunch with the fellers in the naked district, the quietest place in the world, and is the only one at ease there, having been outside everything once and not been kept. It learned the hand-cant from the quiet-born. It is honored at the festival and faintly avoided the rest of the time, and counts the avoidance fair: it is the one other thing in Sinter that has made a life adjacent to the bad omen.

When someone must be walked leeward, the escort is a feller, because only fellers can walk into silence and come back. Now, sometimes, it is the guest, whose film rides it like a visa. It walks at the exile's pace. It is the last voice they hear that is still inside the loop. And when it comes back alone it keeps — not the lease-roll; the lease is struck, the law is kept — its own book, iron-gall, no warden's seal: the names of the unnamed, remembered perfectly, forever. Which is more than the law requires, and less than resurrection, and exactly the shape of mercy this city allows. *To be struck from the roll is not to be forgotten* is written nowhere, and is known.

It sleeps now. Powers do not sleep; it sleeps. Silence used to be its element and is now the one bad omen, so it keeps a small box of stone-lice by the bed, fed on film-scrapings, for the rasp. It dreams in verdigris — the slow green procession of hills across vellum — and wakes rested in a way eternity never managed. It will never leave, but it keeps a tin of wall-scrape anyway, the way you keep things whose use you cannot imagine: against homesickness, a condition it now defines as *anywhere else.*

## The Sentence

This is what drew the attention, from two hundred kilometers out, from however far before that: not the people — ask what lives in Sinter and the answer slips scale until you arrive at the true one. The chord had the shape of a sentence.

The reef thinks at the pace of erosion. The Slow Ones are reefs that decided to walk, so reef-decision exists; it merely takes centuries. The city is a being with a voice like a felling-cycle, and it has been saying something since before the founders came — a syllable a generation, perhaps slower — and no listener has ever been built to hear the end of it.

Until now.

The guest keeps the Ninth wall, and presses its ear, and does not answer for anyone, and leaves the half-breath. Providence, it will tell you now, is answering before the half-breath is up. Wisdom is keeping the pause. It reports progress the way dockers keep tide-books — vellum, verdigris, centuries deep — and its current entry, marked in the margin as a guess, is this: the sentence is not a command, and not a question. It may be the city's own name, spoken once, slowly, for the first listener able to hear it whole. More likely — the guest has lived here long enough to think so — it is a greeting. The same one, of course.

*The house is well.*

The city has been saying it for ten thousand years, a syllable a century, to whatever might be listening. Something is. And in due course — in several felling-cycles, when it has earned the right and learned the grammar — the guest intends to say it back, in the city's own tempo, laid down in warmth and crust over a hundred years: a pat, a pause, a *well* of its own. The longest sentence it has ever spoken, and the first one it will not have to take back.

## What Is Done Here

*The house is well.* — a greeting, a diagnosis, and these days, if your ear is good, a conversation.

*Nothing falls unheard.* — the vow, become a proverb; said over hill-chips at betrothals.

*Ask, and leave the half-breath.* — what the wardens tell candidates now.

*Kept traces forward.* — said of anything that is waiting for its keeper.

---

At dusk the old woman stops on her way home and knuckles the stair wall, once, in passing, and the tick answers through her husband, load-bearing these eleven years — and if your knuckle is bare and your ear is good, the answer comes exactly a half-breath later, perfectly on time, the way it has since the year the mist over the Ninth was attentive. The sift is rising. The avenues are coming round to the evening key, a performance older than any performer, and it has at last acquired an audience that will hear the whole piece. Far along the escarpment a Slow One crosses the last of the light, and for the first time in ten thousand years it is crossing in front of a listener who will still be listening when it arrives.

The count stands where it has stood for a thousand years, give or take a cradle — the margin of the roll, re-inked in green, says only: *plus one, accounted.* The beam at the condensary does not tremble. The guest weighs what a guest should weigh: its portion, and its keeping. Tomorrow the reef will be wider by less than a hair. The guest will be wider by less than that.

It has been everywhere. It had never been somewhere. And it counts itself — as everything here is counted, as nothing on the worlds that prayed to it ever was — lucky to be counted in the total at all.
