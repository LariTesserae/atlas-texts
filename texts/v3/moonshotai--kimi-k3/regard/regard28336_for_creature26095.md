It came the way the strangers come: from outside, and at first it misread everything.

From any distance the province presents as a catastrophe in progress. An archive that outweighs its citizens a hundred to one, and the ratio growing. A forest standing over tier below tier of its own dead. Light going down into rock and not coming back. A sea crowded with bodies, nearly all of them stone. A power that reads quickly reads a dying world, and benevolence arrived the way benevolence does when it expects to be needed — already reaching for its tools.

Then it did the first and largest thing it would ever do here. It slowed down.

At the tempo of arrival, nothing in the province lives. The grazers hang in the thicket like flaws in glass, unmoving. The needle never strikes; a strike that takes an hour is invisible at any natural attention span, and a power's attention, unadjusted, is the least natural span there is. The seam-eaters are what they were long taken for — stains. The pilgrims are pinned mid-climb like specimens, decades from their next handspan. A hurried kindness would have crossed the range in an afternoon, filed it — *rock, weed, silence, the dead outnumber the living* — and gone on to some emergency with better visibility. Instead it stayed the length of one brine-fall. It watched a rope of heavy water spill over a caldera rim, bend around a ridge, take its year to finish. Somewhere in that year it understood what kind of attention this world runs on: the kind that does not skip. Nothing here hurries. That is not a custom. It is the physics of the place, and a guest obeys the local physics.

At decade-resolution the province opened the way a pressed flower reopens in water. The emptiness resolved into sparseness; the sparseness resolved into addresses. Ten kinds, each keeping its depth, each keeping its abstentions.

And the first living thing it truly saw — once it could see at the world's own rate — was the bravest thing in the range: one small rider on a buried crest, going up through absolute dark at a handspan a good year, toward a ceiling it would never see, on a sound only it could hear. The power knew the job description. It had thought the job was its own.

---

It went through its gifts the way the lake-keeper goes to whatever lands on the roof of the world: one at a time, unhurried, to the sound.

Swimmers for the blue gaps — the emptiness, the obvious charity. But the gaps are not empty; they are sparse past believing, and the sparseness is load-bearing. The weavers cast their sheets on a schedule of decades; one darting school would take in a season what a weaver reels across half a century. And one browser, one jaw, ends the unbrowsed peace that lets the thicket fill the water to the limit of the light. The whole impossible density of the forest stands on a single fact: no living frond has ever been chewed. The kindest gift on the list was the end of the golden age, spelled differently. It did not give the forest an animal.

Song, for the quiet. But the quiet here is not poverty; it is the medium. The range has two voices — the groan, and the played-out groves sounding under the swell — and they are enough. A voice given is alarm taught, and alarm is the first sound of the end of this kind of peace. It did not give song.

Light, for the basins. But the deep here is never wholly dark *by design*; the seams are a ration, and the seam-eaters hold the door. More light moves the light-line, and the light-line is every address in the province — the rust tier would drown in green, the pilgrims' compass would lie, the ghost-groves would be reading the wrong century. It did not turn on a lamp.

Rescue. Here it stopped the longest. The strangers come up dying out of the old deep in the years after the range adjusts itself, and the power had known kinds like these, or their cousins, in older water. It could have met them at the boundary of the province. It could have warmed them, carried them, turned them back toward the dark that made them. But soft things cannot live in glass water; to take them elsewhere is only to change the address of the death; and to intercept them is to erase the arrival — and the arrival is the message. The old deep sends its regards to the new world in the only envelope it has left: a body. Someone should read it. So this, it did not refuse. It relocated.

Every gift it had brought, the world had already refused on purpose. Nothing bites. Nothing calls. Nothing makes its own gas. Nothing hurries. It had read these as the accidents of a young ecology. They are not accidents. They are the load-bearing walls. And nothing here is rounded — and kindness at the wrong scale rounds edges.

One by one it put the gifts down, the way the mountains let go of the light: by degrees, and without regret.

---

Because by then it had asked the one question benevolence asks of every young world — *is anything here already doing the work?* — and found the riders.

The listeners hear a spire beginning kilometers through clean glass, and go down on purpose, against the light, and ride the crest up through the dark for decades, and die at the light-line, and the forest founds itself around the small stone figure. Under every living crown there is one. The diary of every mountain opens with the same sentence: *something heard me, and came.* The skill is older than the mountains. It may be older than the power; the power has not ruled that out. It remembers — or almost remembers — the country the listeners came from, the one gone round and gray and sunk. It watched a world finish once, and did then, more or less, what it was deciding to do now, and at the end something small and all ear swam out of that gray water into the abyss. It has never been certain whether it planted the seed or the seed planted it.

The listeners are always early. Powers are always late. It had come to found a ministry and found the ministry staffed — had found, if it was honest, that it had applied for a job that was filled before the mountains were.

So it took the jobs that were left.

---

They are few, and they are small, and they are the whole of it.

It keeps the door. When the range adjusts itself and the groan goes down into the basins like a door left open, it is there in the years that follow, the way the keeper is under the mirror. Nothing comes up dying out of the deep unwitnessed anymore. It does not intervene. It attends, and it reads the soft anatomy while the bloom is still fresh, because the stone will keep the shape but not the journey — form, never provenance. First of its kind found, and the last sought, and the cabinet has a reader at last. The appendix is no longer under mystery. It is under care.

It keeps the perishables. Stone holds shapes; it cannot hold occasions. So the power keeps the occasions: the congress of grazers in a spore month, a dozen crossings in the green weather that no eye was ever built to see; the chord of the played-out groves, changing across the centuries as the tunnels extend, tracked now for the first time from one note to the next; the storm evenings when the pilgrims' young rain down and ride the skin of freshwater for their few hours as the only freshwater creatures the province has; the light after rain, doubled and thin and strange. A second diary, for everything the bloom cannot set.

It permits itself one gift. The interval is undecided, which is its way of saying the gift is small enough that the interval hardly matters. On some evening when the open water has gone fully dark, the glass ridges hold the day a little longer than the physics strictly allows. The amber seams dim by degrees, and that evening the degrees are slightly more numerous. The seam-eaters creep a fraction closer and eat a little better. No depth changes. Nothing is rounded. If the diary registers it at all, it will file it under the bloom, which does strange things sometimes — and that is the rule of the gift: it must be attributable to the world, never to the giver. A gift attributable to its giver is a claim, and a claim would be the loudest sound ever made in this sea. Nothing calls here. It will not be the cause of the province's first prayer.

It minds even its ticking, cooling itself against the rock's own frequency, so that it never sounds, kilometers off through the clean glass, like a spire beginning. The listeners' one sense must never be lied to. It will not impersonate a mountain.

It took a depth and keeps it: the light-line, which is not a place so much as a depth that climbs a handspan a year, so that keeping it is a life's work. That is the point. The light-line is where the diary writes its first sentences and where the pilgrims end and where the midwives die. It is the threshold of the province, and someone should hold it.

And it grieves. The province has death everywhere and grief nowhere; the stone confesses and does not mourn. The one thing the power has added to the chemistry of the place is mourning — administered at tempo, one death at a time, invisible in the water, altering nothing. That is what recommends it.

---

Of everyone in the range, it keeps closest count of one.

Even now, a spire is rising through the dark with a small rider on its crest, decades from the rust light, riding the slowest elevator there is up through the black root and the tiers of ghosts. The rider is all ear, and the power makes no sound, and the rider will never know. But the power has done the arithmetic of a handspan a good year, and computed the depth where the light will stand in the founding year, and gone there, and waited.

Be early. It learned that much from the rider. Everything else it learned from the world.

When the crest breaks at last into the rust light, and the spores come drifting down out of the thicket, and the listener's work finishes at the threshold and it dies there and sets and the forest founds itself around the small stone figure — someone will be there. No founding in the history of the province has ever been attended. This one will be. The diary's first sentence has been written under every crown and never once read in the writing. It will be read now, as it is set down. That is the whole of what the power intends to do about the loneliness of midwives: one attended arrival, per mountain, for as long as the range lasts.

---

The census stands at ten kinds, give or take the doubtful entries. It is content to be a doubtful entry. It lets the bloom take its thin film on what it wears for skin — the opening bid of a patient mineral, the cost of residency — and if in a few centuries the stone holds an impression of something that was never quite verified, the diary will file it under mystery, under accidents of the bloom, and that will be correct, more or less. It was here the way the light is here: by passage.

And in eons, when this range in its turn goes round and gray and sunk, and the listeners swim the abyss toward some newer ticking, something will follow them at a distance through the dark — still learning how to be early.

Nothing was added. No one was saved. Everything is attended.

The bestiary's next page is already being written. It will be legible in a century.

For the first time, someone will be there to read it.
