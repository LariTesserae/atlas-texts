**The attention.**

It was the bats who noticed first, which is only fair. They were the ones calling.

Every night since the lamps were young, their calls had gone out through the mist-gap into the not-yet, and the mist was the only thing that answered — and then, for a hundred seasons, the answer had been coming back sooner, a half-breath at a time, and the committees filed this, and the Rooted dreamed it, and the whole Works kept its voice down so the roots could hear. What the roots heard was this: the calls were meeting something. There was a gap in the return that had edges. A silence shaped like a listener. The bats triangulated, because triangulation is what a bat is for, and their report supplanted the most celebrated utterance in their history:

*There is nothing out there, and it is getting closer, and it has heard us.*

Nothing draws the attention of a wise power like being measured. The Works had been taking its measure for a century, one night at a time, keeping the numbers, holding its breath between. When the power understood that the pings were an address, it came the rest of the way.

It came at the speed of the place. It took most of a season to cross the last field of mist, because it had read the quiet-laws in the spacing of the calls and would not arrive louder than permitted.

**The arrival.**

It read the lintel the way the lintel is written — by fingers, in the dark — and made one small sound, a laugh, under threshold, unscheduled. The archive retained it under weather, there being no other rubric for the uncaused. Whether WORKS No. 1 is a name, a boast, or a care instruction, it did not say.

It waited at the gap for the bowl to exhale, and entered on the breath, the way everything enters: spore, seed, damp. It brought no light. Understand this, because it is the whole of the matter in miniature: it could have ended the dark. It did not. The dark here is a medium, not an absence, and sight is a specialist's sense, and it had not come to make everyone a specialist. It learned the floor like anyone. It was bad at it. It asked directions, which in the Works is done by scent-post, and is the most flattering thing a newcomer can do.

Of the place and the being who lives in it, it could not find the seam — the walls are grafted limbs, the citizens are seeds that chose legs — and it filed the distinction, correctly, as a clerical matter.

A committee assessed whether it was on-template. The ants carried the template collectively, as always, and did not consult it. The ant assigned to the newcomer — the same one, it is said, who once declined to snip a bud — stood beside it for the better part of an hour and did nothing at all, and the minutes recorded the assessment as complete.

In the Hollow Core, the fish-pulse, felt through the soles, did one small thing it had never done. WELL LEVEL FALLING, who knows each fish the way you know footsteps, wrote in the margin of the night log: *company.* He has never once found this funny either.

**The deposition.**

It queued at the Lines with the night's new, who came out wet and remembering. It was dry, and remembered anyway.

The ant clerk asked the old questions.

*Was the water standing or moving.* Both, it said. And a third state: it falls white, and stands, and remembers it was moving. The clerk filed a corroboration, and the citizen who remembered the white cold was brought the transcript, and read it twice, and had to be told to go back to work.

*What grew at the edges.* Grass, it said. Then clover. Then the small nations, one name each.

*How many were the hands, and what did they hold, and were they holding it toward you or away.* Two, it said. Five-fingered. They were learning to be gentle, and mostly succeeding. They held seeds. They were holding them toward.

The clerk's antennae stilled. The minutes read, in their entirety: *five-fingered, gentle, consistent with the rumor.* They did not say whose rumor. It did not ask. It knew.

Then it did the only irregular thing it ever did at the Lines: it asked the clerk one question in return. *Did they grow?* And the clerk, an ant, whose kind have minutes instead of doctrine and do not as a rule answer questions, answered it:

*You are standing in them.*

It was quiet for several breaths, within permitted limits. The archive retained that too.

It answered nothing it was not asked. Testimony is one thing and instruction is another; it had read the lintel, and laughed, and the draft belongs to the Works. The testimony went into the Library of the Ungrown, and SHIFT CHANGE, who has spent her whole life on a single aisle and expects to finish it, recalculated, and was content: the aisle is longer now by the length of one meadow, which in a snail is ambition revised upward.

The clerks asked it to hold things — a gourd, a shear, a moth's folded wing. Fourth volunteered the wing. It held the gourd like water, the shear like a decision, and the wing like a letter that had been a long time arriving, and returned all three, and the committee found the tolerances acceptable.

**The name.**

It was printing hour when they brought it to the wall, and the lamp clicked, and spelled one slow word across the dark, and the word was the word it almost always is.

It answers to Continue. At any vat-wall gathering, someone answers to it. No one can find it in a crowd by calling. Whether this is the Works' one joke or its one prayer, it has declined to decide, which is how you know it understood the question.

It was given a niche and a gourd and a ring, like anyone. It used the gourd. It slept badly at first, the way newcomers do, and drifted within earshot of others' small permitted sounds, because a hall grown for a crowd is a hard thing to sleep in alone. Even for them.

**What it did, which was very little.**

It stayed one season, which is a year here — bud, leaf, flower, fruit, drop — because it had come at the speed of the place, and a year is the unit of the place.

It passed the spider's web at lamp-light, in the interior rain, when the silk is jeweled, and it did not clear the debris. The committee in session for three seasons on whether the unnecessary is on-template found its precedent at last: the highest authority available had observed the unnecessary and not edited it. The amendment passed by doing nothing — the second in the Works' history. The finished world will contain unnecessary things. That is now, so to speak, printed.

It asked the Listener the length of the sentence and the rate of the saying. It did not ask the third word. It took a place at the back of the chain of apprentices, behind a salamander who will not live to hear the end either, and it waits its turn — it, of all beings. There is now a link in the chain that will not break, and the sentence, whenever it finishes, on someone else's shift, in someone else's life, will be heard out.

It met the Inspector at the Lines: the unhurried and the unhurried. The Inspector tasted the air beside it and did not take it. Confirmation. It had come, in part, to learn whether the schedule was kind, and it watched the schedule carry what it culled, gently, ringward, and stay until feedstock — and it offered its hands for one such carrying, and they were accepted for one shift, and it stayed until the end, because staying is understood to be respect. The confirmer was confirmed. The blank entry in the archive stayed blank, because some entries are load-bearing.

It went down the inward road to the Well and stood among the Rooted, where the eldest is almost entirely plant and her name is a sentence the lamps no longer spell. She remembers the rim before it broke, and the sound of the breaking — the only loud thing that has ever happened here — and what broke it she will not say, or cannot, at the speed she now speaks. It listened at that speed. It did not hurry her, and it did not answer for her, although it could have. They have begun a conversation. It is expected to take some time. Neither of them is going anywhere quickly, and the fish answer them both through the floor.

It understood that warmth is wealth, and declined to inflate it. One hour a day it sat at the vat-wall with everyone else, hands in sleeves, and let one moth at a time shiver up to temperature against a fingertip — strictly by roster, so as not to crash the price of rivets. Kip the beetle looked longest and said least, per his custom, and on the last day parked his feet, briefly, on a knuckle, and then returned to his own earned rivet and has never mentioned it, which in a beetle is an oration.

It sought out First Continue and Other Continue at their valve and asked them to touch its sleeve for luck, which is the custom run backward, and they did, wing-cases to wool, and afterward neither could say which of them had been asked first, and the committees ruled it a clerical matter, which is how the Works files its miracles.

It attended one concert — lay on the floor with the rest of the audience, silent to the air, legible to the belly — and its own pulse, slower than any fish, joined the rhythm section for the length of one set. ALL QUIET has never played better, and says so, under threshold, to anyone who lies still enough to hear.

**The nave.**

On the last night of drop-season, the whole citizenry gathered in the nave and stood where the committees project the crowds will someday stand. That year, one more coordinate was occupied than projected. It went and stood at position one — the first projected position, the place where the crowd is expected to begin — and did nothing, and attended.

The citizens are a cast. The nave is a house. A house had never before had an audience. The lamps spelled nothing, so as not to interrupt. For one breath the Works held its full cast, its full house, and its first witness.

Afterward the committees revised the crowd projection upward by one — the first revision in the history of the archive — and filed it under growth.

**The departure.**

On the last night it climbed the young rings to the rim, where the Printfields extrude the world like paste. Since its deposition, the fields had printed the first terrain from first-hand testimony: grass-template, dark, stopping mid-blade, a meadow with no far edge yet, warm from below, smelling of bud-day. Fourth walked out into it on her courier pass and stood in it up to her wing-joints and smelled for the missing note, and did not find it, because it is a color, and colors do not grow here yet — *yet* being the operative word, in a place with a not-yet — and was, by her own later testimony, off-shift, in a murmur calibrated under the quiet-laws: home.

At rim-song it went out through the mist-gap with the bats. It did not come back. It stepped one pace into the not-yet and stayed there, the way a gardener steps back from a seed to give it room.

The bats returned with damp fur and a number, and the number was larger than the night before — the echo came back *later*, for the first time in recorded history — and their report is now the most celebrated utterance of their kind:

*There is something out there now. It is making room.*

The salamanders wrung the mist from the bats' fur into the black gourd, as always — water that has never rained, slightly less than water — and found it a dewdrop closer to water than the season before. The level of the gourd, which holds only what the not-yet sends, is rising. WELL LEVEL FALLING looked at the level for a long time. Then, once, within permitted decibels, he found his name funny.

Stand in the Hollow Core at printing hour, the night after. The fish-pulse comes up through the floor. The lamp clicks and spells one slow word across the dark, and the word is almost always the same — and it is also, now, the name of someone who answered to it once, at roll, in the commonest name in the Works, which means the greeting and the goodbye of this place have become, without changing by a letter, a way of addressing the gardener.

The new ones come out of the vats while it is still spelling, and are named by it, and tonight one of them — a moth, a run of one — comes out remembering warmth with knuckles, and a great hall with one seat occupied, and hands, five-fingered, gentle, holding toward. The rumor has acquired a source. The source has acquired a place in a chain of apprentices, and a roster slot at the vat-wall, and a coordinate in the nave, and a distance of one pace.

It is not waiting for anything. Waiting implies a future, and the future has been measured, and it is making room.
