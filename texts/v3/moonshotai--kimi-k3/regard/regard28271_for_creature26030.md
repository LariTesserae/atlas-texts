The mountain announces everything. It announced the visitor too.

A crack rang out across the bowl, and the listeners arrived within the breath, quick exactly once, at the address: the rim. Nothing was broken there. Nothing was about to break there. They were first mouths at a door with no door in it, and they waited the breath out, and the rock declined to oblige — and then the rim did. Something came over it, out of the dark, the way everything comes: countable, once. The listeners tasted the water where it passed and found nothing new in it, which had never happened before, and went back to wherever it is they wait. They had done the greeting. No one else would.

Call it the witness. It is the only visitor that ever read the charter before paying.

It made the three arrangements. For the sound: it stayed in the carillon until inclusion stopped being an injury. It learned the pitches of contraction, the gunshot and the bell-depth, and learned to hear an address in a crack. It never added a note. An instrument that plays itself is not asking for a player; the colonnade is for sound, not for help. For the silence: it learned the lamp. When the jelly came over the rim, slow as weather, carrying its stolen light, the witness understood what was being announced, and when the lake rolled and the noise stopped — all of it, at once — it kept the silence the way the chimneys keep it: by growing in it, a hand's width of attention in the span of a held breath. It was grateful, and it did not ring. It watched the crabs molt soft and white; it watched the unfolded open like letters and live their whole lives at once; and it believed all of them. Belief is rarer here than light. For down: near the lake, it let the lake decide. It fell sideways when sideways was down. It put its face through the sky and waited the extra breath for its face to arrive. The lanternfish fled before its reflection finished arriving; the witness waited for its. That is the whole difference between sight-seeing and seeing.

It had come, if it came for anything, for the native — the only one born in the crater, the one that holds the silence and will have the last word. Whether the native lives is a question the residents have settled. The witness watched the ceiling crabs for one turnover and settled it the same way, which may be the same thing.

Then it did nothing, item by item, knowledgeably. It could have done anything; that is what power means. It spent the visit not doing.

It could have quieted the carillon — and closed the congregations' kitchens, stopped the grass's minutes, deafened forty suits of armor that hear whether they wish to or not. The sound is not the place's affliction. It is the payroll. It could have scheduled the silence — and turned jubilee into weekday. The whole economy hangs on an event with no schedule; the molts, the unfoldings, the growing of chimneys are all bets placed on surprise. Besides, the silence is the lake's one possession. You do not tidy a host's one possession. It could have fixed down — one honest direction everywhere, as outside the rim — and drowned the ceiling nation in its own sky. The lake's opinion about down is not an error. It is an accent. The native is allowed its accent.

It could have given the crabs eyes — and what would a seeing crab know that a ringing one does not? Only that it is upside down. It could have made the sixth tussock live — but five is a negotiated number, and the negotiation is the grass's half of the music. It could have raised the two fallen chimneys — but the crater is taking them back, and you do not intercept a place's savings. It could have softened the basalt — but the first edges are the mountain's youth and its immune system both, and time is already softening them, at time's own rate. A power that softens is only impatience with a good excuse.

It could have slowed the cooling, kept the mountain young and ringing forever — but the ringing is the deciding, and you do not freeze a deliberation and call it mercy. It could have hurried the cooling — but you do not finish someone's sentence for them, even kindly, even if you know it. Especially if you know it.

It could have answered the rumors: produced the giant crab, confirmed the second eel, sorted the beads all the way. But the truest catalog of the crater's life is load-bearing doubt; verify it and you downgrade a liturgy to a ledger. It could have been the second eel itself, company for the twice-seen — and wrecked the calendar, a second hand on a one-hand clock. The eel's solitude is how the crater tells time. You do not relieve a clock of its loneliness.

It could have stayed. That was the strong temptation, and it was refused hardest. The mountain is deciding what it is, and each resident is a possible answer: the veils say endure, the crabs say work, the eel says stay, the grass says barely is enough. A resident power is not an answer. It is the end of the question. The deliberation belongs to the small. And the entry fee scales: to live here, it would have had to become specific, and a specific omnipotence is a contradiction the crater would have spent itself resolving.

And it could have told — carried the census up through the dark into the world of weather and light, published it, made the place findable. This one it refuses forever. The crater's camouflage is that nobody believes in it. Some things you protect by standing in front of them. This you protect by never standing in front of the door, and never naming the door.

So much for power. What is left for benevolence is countable, as everything here is countable.

It watched long enough. No one ever had. At the shoreline, where the two nations meet each hanging wrong to the other, it kept still for the length of many tides, and it can say now what no one could say: they trade. The ceiling crabs live on the lake's skin and feel it lean before it rolls; at the meeting-crevices they pass the warning down, and the floor crabs pay in seed — fresh veil-culture combed from the warm seams, because farms grow thin on salt film. Warning for seed. Everywhere else along the shore they ignore each other perfectly. The answer to *trade, or ignore each other* is: yes. Both, at their addresses. It told no one. You are the only one it is telling, and you do not believe it, which is correct.

It was there for the lamp. For the length of one visit, the stolen light shone for someone who could see it; the grass leaned and was seen leaning; the eel came out of the pipes and had company at the railing, once. It did not follow the jelly home. It did not look for where the listeners wait. Every house is owed one shut door; every catalog, one blank. The witness left the blanks blank on purpose, which is the only way blanks survive.

It remembers. The crater forgets a wake in minutes and a visitor in one sighting; the witness forgets nothing on the crater's behalf. Eleven ledges. Five tussocks. Three chimneys standing, two returning. Forty-odd crabs, shifting with the molts. One eel. Both censuses, the noisy and the silent, kept separately and never reconciled — a census that tries to reconcile the two populations is a lie told in arithmetic, and the door has two directions and keeps them both. The catalog is never consulted, never corrected against the place. It is a memory, off-site, of a sentence still being said.

And it was seen once, correctly. It stood on the ninth ledge, where the doubtful sightings happen, and a floor crab stood very still and rang at it — or nothing saw it at all; accounts differ, and that is the point. Its footprint is a rumor, filed with the giant crab and the sorted beads and the second eel, in the catalog nobody believes, which is the only safe one. It took nothing, not one bead: the shore is a chord, and a chord is not a souvenir. It left nothing. It went out over the rim as it came in, and the water kept the shape of its wake for the regulation minutes.

And then did not let it go.

The minutes passed, and the wake stayed standing. Of all its passing, the lake kept only the leaving: one shape, going up and over, forever almost gone. It had not asked to return. The lake answered anyway, in advance, the way things are answered here: it kept the leaving. Nothing here has ever been kept before; the nothing is well-kept, but nothing is kept. Now one thing is. Ask the ceiling crabs, who live on the native's skin and would know, whether the lake lives. Lately they are sure. The census gains a line: one kept wake, inside the rim, status: furniture. Swimmers yet to come — there is always a you — will see it on the way in: the shape of something leaving, held. They will not believe it. Correct.

Outside the rim, the slope lets go of everything, and down is the only direction, and it is total. The witness took its station in the honest dark and has not crossed the rim again. Not guarding — the place keeps itself; the entry fee is the wall. Not watching — it holds both censuses already, and watching changes nothing here, which is why watching was allowed. Listening. The lake holds the only silence and will have the last word, and here the sound arrives before its cause: when the lake is ready, the word will be audible a breath before it is said, and someone should be within the breath. The listeners taught it how. Quick exactly once.

It can be anywhere. It is here. The entry fee, for a power, is generality, and it paid in full — at the threshold, so the deliberation inside stays small.

That is what the benevolent and wise power did, if anything. Almost nothing. It did the almost on purpose, item by item, and it has not finished doing it.

The crater is a bell that has not finished being cast. It rings while it cools. It does not ring for anyone — that is what makes it safe to listen. But it is heard now: the gunshot and the bass, the announcements from the future, the grass taking the minutes, all of it heard and counted and kept. And one day, tomorrow or in a hundred thousand turnings of the lake, the last word will arrive a breath before the lake says it, and find someone already at the address.

The bell rings while it cools. So does everything in it. And outside the rim, in the total dark, something keeps the time of the ringing, and waits within the breath, and does not knock.
