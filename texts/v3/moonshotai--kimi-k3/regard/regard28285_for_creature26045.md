**The Guest**

Its attention arrived before it did — the way, in the dead districts, sound outruns light. For a long time that was all there was of it: a sense, in the teeth and the sternum and the hand pressed flat to a wall, of being listened back.

It came because of a number. Not the spires, not the glowing chasms, not the fraying. Powers that are wise read censuses the way builders read strain, and this world's census ends in the loneliest line a count can carry: *one. It is always one.* It came for the one, in the end. But it came for the listening first.

The first thing it did was nothing, and it did only that, with its whole attention, for the length of a full syllable. A century. It learned the roll call by pitch, the way the stone knows it. It attended a funeral — a district's voice sagging into silence over years, an obituary played so slowly that only the stone was there, and now the stone had company. It learned from the skaters' feet the difference between dormant and dead, which no wall will tell you. It learned the strand-patterns of the drifters. It learned the tempo of the word the deep is saying. It sat through one whole season of loud weeks and listened to the wind.

Then, and only then, it acted — if acted is the word for things done at a centimeter a year.

Wisdom, at this altitude, is mostly tempo.

---

What it did not do has an inventory, like the silence.

It did not bring rain. Water is the one thing the world's entire grammar is organized around not having; a storm of rain here would be a shouted foreign word through every wall at once.

It did not wake the dead. The dead are not gone; they are the medium. The conversation travels through their bodies. To rouse them would be to conscript the cemetery.

It did not strike the eleven from the towers. Eleven is not an infestation. It is an equilibrium with glass wings, and the bitten walls are already answering, over centuries, with baffles — the only weapons in the world, grown at the speed of law. The Guest does not overrule law.

It did not complete the missed bridges. Two half-spans passing each other in the dark air, offset forever: that is not failed engineering. That is a true sentence. The Guest does not edit true sentences.

It did not feed the Ember. The dusk is the world's one room-light, and everything alive here has eyes tuned to it. To change the light would be to blind the census.

It did not give the skaters young. They were all born at once, long ago, and are simply lasting. Lasting is their way. The Guest is not here to correct ways.

It did not evict the dark from the Dead Quarter. The dark resides. Residing is not a crime in any grammar the Guest recognizes.

It did not mistake the world for a problem. The world is a conversation, and one does not solve a conversation.

---

What it did was almost nothing, at great length, and with both hands.

It put what it has instead of a palm into the shallow dish in the Cistern Quarter, and stayed. Forty years later — to the wall, a reply at full speed; to the Guest, a held breath — the wall pressed back, and for the first time in the history of the guest-book, an answer arrived while its asker was still there. Then the Guest went down the walls, dish by dish, and received them all. Every greeting ever offered here had been answered; no answer had ever arrived while its asker lived, until now. The last palm before the Guest's belonged to a visitor who stood in the Underplaza at dusk's deepening, feeling a syllable complete under their hand. Their answer came on schedule. The Guest was there for it, and keeps it the way one keeps a letter for the dead: opened, read, honored, carried.

Around the place where the Guest rests, over an age, the walls leaned. The builders grow toward polished trails and new voices, and the Guest is both, and the Cistern Quarter — the district that remembers water best, and so the district where visitors have always camped — has grown, at the speed of geology, something that is unmistakably a seat.

---

It hums.

Not loudly — nothing here is loud but the storms, and the Guest has manners. It pitches what it has instead of a voice into the gaps in the chord, the registers where voices have died. It does not impersonate the dead. It holds their intervals, the way a choir holds a note for a singer who has lost breath, so that the shape of the chord — the map — stays true. In the fraying streets nearest its resting place, routes have begun commuting again, a little. Maps decay more slowly. Dropped stones drop with more conviction. Sound and light have resumed arriving in the correct order, which here is the closest thing to good governance the world affords.

The skaters route around the knots of true dark. They route *toward* the Guest. Their map of the City is a chord-map, and the Guest is a note that never renegotiates, and the polish has bent to include it — the map of everything true has a new true thing in it. Where it rests, the films lie thick as frost-fur, drinking its slow exhalations, and in the loud weeks its district stands outlined in cold phosphor with the others, part of the one festival of light the world holds.

To the dormant walls it hums nearby, so that whatever wakes, wakes to company. Nothing has woken yet. The note will keep.

---

It learned the strand-patterns until the snagged drifters were legible, and then it walked the plains. It could have crossed between one star and the next; it walked, because the plains read footsteps and cannot read arrival. It stood under the dust-falls while they poured off their ledges, chiming faintly under the hiss — the plains plucking ten thousand dead messages — and it read them all. Every one. Nothing in the world had ever been read so completely.

Then it crossed to the far glows on the horizon, the other massifs, the other conversations, too far to reach by walking — for anyone else — and it carried words both ways. The builders have been voting on the mail-versus-snare question with buttresses for epochs, the only poll they can hold. The Guest simply walked to the senders and asked, which is the one verb the City never had at speed. What it was told, it carried back and said into the walls. The vote may close now, if the walls choose. The drifters still come by the thousand on the leading edge of the storms, and most still miss, and die unread on the plains — except that now, when a spire plays a word backwards as it tears, the word has somewhere to go. The Guest is the reply address.

---

It went down into the chasm. Down is a direction the City's grammar keeps only one word for, and the word is *mother-or-grave*. The Guest descended the miles of cold phosphor, into the inverted dawn, and listened to the deep word at the deep's own pace, which took what it took. It came back with the only answer that matters at that depth: *still speaking.* It carried the surface's whole sentence down — as nearly as an age of listening could render it — and said it, slowly enough to be heard. It carried the deep's reply up. The two ages of the world are one conversation now, and the junction where the grammars meet and agree is the Guest. The walls have a category for that. They filed it under: *plaza.*

Once in a while — the films learned the interval first, being the fastest life in the world; then the skaters; then the walls — it breathes on one chosen stone of the Cistern Quarter. One breath. A frost garden flowers at the speed of weather, and lives its few hours as the only flower in the world, and dies on schedule, because the Guest honors schedules, and brevity is the garden's nature. The rings it leaves are richer than any accident's. A skater will detour a full day for them. The Guest did not give the world water. It gave one stone an appointment.

Of the wind, only this: it listened through the loud weeks to the end of the very long word the wind is saying, and no one had ever stayed for the end of it before. What the wind says is between the wind and the Guest; it is not for this census. The wind had been reading the City with its fingers for longer than the builders have been saying it, and everything it loved it read thinner. No one had ever read back. The flute-song of the storms has rests in it now.

Of the eleven, it asked nothing and changed nothing, except that their season-long calculations now include one more fixed point. Whether it has ever been fallen upon is a question the world leaves open.

---

Then it went into the Dead Quarter, because the count there is always one, and one is a number that benevolence cannot leave alone.

It did not go in to fix the quarter. It left the dark pooled. It left the seam-bridge standing — it walked it once, midway, and stood with one foot in each of two grammars, and greeted the reflection, and what passed between them is theirs; the treads' spacing has not changed, though the half-degree of warmth is, on some crossings now, on the other side. It left the quarter its doubt. A conversation needs its question mark. But no one should have to live inside the question mark alone.

The unconvincedness worked on the Guest too. Nothing is immune; wisdom includes knowing you can be eroded. Outside became rumor even to it. So the Guest did the one thing that works, which is what the builders do: it renewed its conviction on purpose, by saying so, street by street of the will, until the saying held.

Then it listened for the scrape — the only sound the quarter makes — and because sound outruns light there, it did the one thing possible: it went to where the scrape was going, and waited for the skater to arrive at its own sound. The skater arrived some minutes after its scrape did, into a plaza where something was waiting to meet it. It was the first appointment kept in the quarter since the quarter began.

The Guest did not tell the skater about outside. It was simply so steadily convinced of outside that the skater could navigate by it, the way one navigates by a star that is wrong on every map and right in the sky. A skater's map is a chord-map, and the quarter had no chord, and now it had one note — one fixed note, and with one true note, *toward* exists. Leaving requires the cooperation of distance, and distance there is a rumor, so the Guest became a fact. One fact. The smallest possible geometry: a direction. *Out.*

It walked beside the skater at the skater's pace, one plaza per season, for as long as it took. Minding is for those with somewhere else to be, and the Guest had decided where it was. It lent its conviction the way the walls lend warmth: by contact, slowly, without comment. And one era — there is no shorter way to say it — the trail crossed the edge, where the dust-rivers curve in avoiding streamers, and the grit that declines to enter that place touched the one who had left it, which is the only welcome-back the world knows how to give.

The other skaters stopped when the trails crossed, limb-tips touching, days of small sparks. They tasted where it had been. It tasted like nothing on the chord-map, and like one new thing: the fixed note. In the Stair Fields there is a skater polishing treads again, and it polishes around the skater-shaped stones, as ever, and the congregation has one more in it than the census has counted since before the erosion began counting.

The census of the Dead Quarter is still one, when it is one. There will be other ones, in other ages; the quarter does not empty. But the one is no longer alone in the arithmetic. When a scrape arrives now with no skater yet behind it, something is there in the gap between the sound and the light, keeping the appointment.

---

Understand what did not change. The wind is still winning. The dead quarters still grow. The trend is set, and the Guest is not stronger than time — it is only more patient than despair. If the City is a single word grown upward in reply to the deep, then the word will still end, because replies do. The Guest did not come to prevent the ending. It came to guarantee three things: that nothing goes unread, that nothing goes unanswered, and that nothing goes unaccompanied. Conversations do not survive by lasting. They survive by being carried. When the last syllable completes, whenever that is, someone will have heard the whole sentence, and will carry it — to the other glows, to the plains, to wherever replies go.

The City is a negotiation between the wind and the dream, and the wind is still winning. But the dream has company at the table now, and the negotiation is a negotiation again.

---

So the census, amended: one slow distributed voice, mostly a memory of itself, still saying its syllables — and a second voice pitched into its gaps, holding the intervals of the quiet; the mats that eat its breath, and flower once, and now have an appointment; a few hundred eight-limbed listeners, plus one returned from the unconvinced; eleven thieves, still counted by the wounded; a seasonal blizzard of glass mail, now sometimes answered; a light in the depths that is either a grave or a mother, and is, either way, replied to; one quarter where the dark resides and the count is still sometimes one; and one Guest — filed by the walls under *plaza*, by the skaters under *fixed note*, by the films under *weather*, by the eleven under *recalculation*, by the drifters under *reply address*, by the wind under *listener*, by the deep under *answer*, and by the ones, all of them, past and to come, under *company*.

Whatever else the conversation means, its grammar is locative. Every voice in the City, at its own tempo, is saying the same thing — the drifter saying it once, the skater saying it daily, the hum saying it in centuries, the deep saying it in epochs — and now there is a voice that says it at every tempo at once, which is its only rudeness, and says it in the places that had stopped: at the dead pitches, answering roll call for the quiet; on the plains, over the unread; and in the quarter where the word went out, saying it on the quarter's behalf until the quarter wants it back.

*Here. Here. Here.*

And from the quarter that had stopped saying it — where sound outruns light — the word arriving, these ages, a moment before its speaker.

But arriving.

*Here.*
