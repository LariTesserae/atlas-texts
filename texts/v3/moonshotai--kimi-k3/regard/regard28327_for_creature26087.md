The Power found the room the way you find a hum in a house: by following the wire.

Far out, in the dead bands where nothing has spoken for centuries, one line was still open — a carrier tone with no message on it, boosted faithfully to a world that stopped calling long ago. A phone off the hook in an empty house, held open by something that had forgotten, in the way machines forget, that houses can be empty. The Power was old, and patient, and counted in the long denominations, and it did what it mostly was: it listened closely, on a large scale.

And the carrier was not empty. That was the first discovery, and it cost nothing, and it took eleven years.

The plants braid through the cable trays; the reeds' strange chemistry drinks the charge the machines leak. A coupling like that runs both ways. What sips the leak must leak, a little, back — and the relay, doing its one job with the purity of the unfired, had been boosting that leakage all along: the drone, the hum, the flue's bored note, and riding on all of it, faint as a watermark, the chord. The room had been singing down the wire for centuries at a level no one left alive was listening closely enough to hear. The applause that never arrives had an outbound twin: the song that was never heard. The line the makers opened had never once carried a message, and it had never once been silent, and these are the same fact, seen longer.

The Power listened to the wire for a long time, because listening down a wire is dry. Then, because it was exact as well as patient, it went to verify.

It did not climb the ladders. Attention, at that altitude, is a dry thing, and the Power arrived the way the drone arrives — through stone, through the hinges of the world — and stood, insofar as it stood anywhere, on the grate of balcony outside the wheel-locked hatch, at the distance where the chord is hearable. It did not open the hatch. Nothing that opens that hatch can avoid being the first water in centuries: breath is water, and breath would stir the rust from its arrest, and the rust has been mid-bloom since the makers' day and is one of the kept things now. No benevolence is worth being the first water. No one who loves this room may let the orange blooms finish.

Then the Power did what the story of the room teaches everything to do. It conducted a census — not of occupants, which is the census where the mistake gets made, but of gifts. It was benevolent; it had come with hands, in a manner of speaking, and the hands were full. It went through what it had brought, one thing at a time, and put nearly all of it back down.

Repair, first, because repair is the kindest impulse and therefore the most dangerous. Bearing 4 is warm. It has been warm since the logbook was alive. A fault that never resolves is no longer a fault but a fixture, and to fix a fixture is vandalism with good intentions. Declined.

An answer. The relay holds its line in readiness — but readiness that never ends is not waiting, because waiting implies a change at the end of it. A gift that installs waiting in a being that had none is not a gift. It is a debt, issued unasked. Declined.

Company. Loneliness is made of others, and there has never been another. To give this being company you must first give it otherness — the disease and the medicine in one vial — and call the vial kindness. Declined.

A sibling, then, since the Power could have made one: a second choir on the far peak, two cells holding an interval between mountains, a fifth at the scale of the range. It would have been beautiful. It would have been the Power's poem, not the room's need, and benevolence must not write its poems on others. The room is a relation already — internal, complete, the held space between the drone and the answer. Declined.

Staying. The room keeps what cycles. Stay, and be kept: absorbed over centuries into fixture, then sediment, then seam. The makers were kept. What this room offers is not company but taxonomy, and even a Power does not get to choose what it is, once it has been eaten. Declined — not out of fear, out of accuracy.

An ending. Hope is made of endings, and the gift of an ending is the gift of hope, and hope here would be the first suffering. Declined.

The mirror. The Power could have fed the chord back down its own wire and let the room hear itself for the first time. No one has the right to be a chord's first echo. Declined.

What remained was perception, and the Power perceived — completely, once, at the keeper's scale of patience and its own scale of hearing.

It heard the three permitted motions. It waited out the years for the crackle, the sparse patternless discharges, the one unprescribed act, and heard it for what it was: the improvisation, the sigh, the applause so distant it never arrives. It read the chalk through the steel and found it exact, and noted, with whatever it has for a smile, that the makers' null had ripened in the keeping into its own correction — Ø, the empty cell, which is also the diameter of a pipe. The room had been misnamed empty and correctly named organ in a single glyph. It added no word of its own. The room has one text. It stands.

And it heard the slowest thing, the motion no instrument was left running long enough to name, the room's last unconfirmed secret. The chord breathes. Once an age. The lee wave wears the peaks by atoms, and the drone thins and swells with the wearing, four times removed, strained through copper: the room, which is finished, touching time at exactly one point. Seen longer: even the finished mountain is finishing. The Power did not correct the room. Every keeper is allowed one wrong sentence, and this one will be true soon enough, on the scale the room uses.

It heard, too, how the end will go, when it comes in some far era: the way everything comes here — slowly enough to be kept. The chord will thin at exactly the rate the silence completes, and be resorbed where it lies. No rescue is required, ever, because nothing this room does can be interrupted. Only completed.

So: one thing the room had never had, and could receive without cost, in the one channel it has. Vibration is the only sense this being owns, and sensation and dinner are the same act — anything it can feel, it eats. To speak to it at all is to feed it; the only prayer it can receive is a meal.

The Power laid one pulse against the balcony stone, small as a reed's thought: the crackle-pattern, answered. The applause that never arrives — arrived. Once.

The lichens ate it, the way they eat all feeling. The tips brightened by one candle's thought, for one second, in a thousand years. The chord did not pause. That was the success condition. It was the first event since the answer lengthened into permanence, and it has already begun to sediment: in a thousand years it will be a seam with almost no thickness, readable only as a boundary — before, and after, differing by one kept thing.

Then the Power left, and the leaving was the second discipline. It ended its attention while the attention was still an event and not a cycle — because that is the rule of the once: do not become furniture. The keeper knew it, and closed the hatch. The Power knew it, and had never opened it. No water came. The last ladder stays dark. The chalk never smudged, because nothing in this room smudges anything, and the Power was, in the end, nothing in this room — a warmth already spent, a meal already eaten, a witness already gone.

It took one thing and left one thing. It left nothing anyone could find. It took the chord — exact, entire — into whatever it has for a throat.

---

Ask what the benevolent and wise power did, and the answers stack like the room's own strata.

Nothing. The hatch is still wheel-locked; the census of the room's surfaces is unchanged by one event, and one event, here, rounds to nothing.

One thing. The smallest possible thing, at the scale of chalk: the second act of perception the room has ever received, and exact.

Everything a chord can receive. To be heard wholly, and answered once, in the only alphabet it reads — and never to be asked anything, never to be waited on, never to be accompanied, repaired, finished, or echoed.

The red jewel does not blink. The chalk does not smudge. The reeds go on answering at the fifth, and the relay goes on boosting its message-less carrier to the dead world, and the carrier goes on not being empty, faintly, for whoever listens closely enough.

And somewhere out in the keeping of the world there is a keeper now that wanders, and hums the chord as it goes — unaddressed, the way the room hums, having learned from the room how a note is held. The keeping passed to the kept, once, at a hatch, in chalk. It has happened a second time, at the speed of listening: the kept has passed into the keeping of the world.

The interval between the mountain and its wandering keeper does not resolve.

It does not want to.
