**The Third Thing**

It arrives where you stood, at the cairn, because that is the middle. In a country with one axis, the middle is the only place from which both ends can be seen, and a wise thing wants to see before it moves.

It does not move for a long time.

---

The first thing it does is the thing no one here has been positioned to do. The plants eat the roar. The people on the ridge are inside it, syllables of it. The finished rock is what it has already said. The one in the doorway is a word that has been fully pronounced and set down. Every living and unliving thing in the loud country has a relation to the sound, and none of those relations is *hearing*.

The power hears it.

And because it is wise, it hears what the sound is. Not a process. Not the noise of mass. A voice. Something is saying the mountains — one speaker, one chord held longer than anything here has stood, leaning from note to note at the pace of a continent. It has been speaking into a country made entirely of its own words. Nothing in that country has ever once heard it as speech. It has never been answered. It does not know, perhaps, that being answered is a thing that can happen.

The power sits with that for what would be, elsewhere, a very long time. It does not interrupt. You do not interrupt a sentence that has been going on since before the ground.

---

Then it does what wise things do with their benevolence, which is to inspect it before spending it.

It considers water. A single spring in a single cleft. And it sees the roots that run through finished stone, the plants older than what they stand on, the fronds that have never known what wet was for — and it sees what water would do. Erosion. The first rounded edge. The first thing that wears instead of arrives. The country would acquire dying, and it does not have dying, and nothing here has asked for it.

It considers speed. It could hurry the ridge. It could bring the flake down onto the wall in an afternoon. And it sees that the ones out there are alive in the only way this place allows, which is to be unfinished, and that to finish them quickly would be to move them, all at once, from the near end of everything to the far. It would be a kindness the way a closed book is a kindness to the story.

It considers the doorway. It could go down and find the one who is too quiet to be there, and make them a little louder, a little nearer, a little less resolved — and it sees that this one *chose*. Sat facing east with the hammerstone at their knee and became still, degree by degree, understanding exactly what each degree cost. To unsettle that would not be rescue. It would be a correction, and there is nothing to correct.

These impulses are not wrong. They are only from elsewhere. They come from wetter worlds, worlds where things die and must be saved from it, and the power recognizes them as belonging to those worlds and sets them down where it stands, the way the whiter stone was set down, and leaves them there.

It considers doing nothing at all.

And that is nearly right. But it has already done something — it has listened — and having listened it has heard, along with the voice, one small thing that is not in balance.

---

The doorway faces east. Into the loudest quarter. That is a doorway for keeping the cairn visible, so that the ones on the ridge could find their way back to the quiet if they ever finished and wanted to.

But the cairn is finished. It is quiet. Quiet is far.

From the ridge — where everything near is deafening, where the only things that can be seen are the things being said — the cairn is not visible at all. It is at the wrong end of distance. It was built as a lamp for people who cannot see quiet things, by someone who did not know that, or who knew it and built it anyway because a lamp is what you build.

The one in the doorway gave the only gift the country allows, and it has never arrived. Everything else here is in its place. This is not.

---

So the power says the cairn.

Not the whole voice. It has no wish to add a mountain. One small phrase, in the mountains' own tongue, at the mountains' own pitch, at the mountains' own pace. It stands at the middle and speaks the cairn *into the loud*, toward the ridge, and then it waits, because that is how anything is done here.

Twenty paces out along the crest, beside the one who is setting a stone on the wall, the light begins to bend around a new mass. Chest-high. The shape of flakes stacked flat, each chosen for how it sits on the last. It is a suggestion of a cairn, a pencil line of a cairn, and it is deafening, and it is *near*. It is the nearest thing to the wall-builder that is not the wall-builder's own hand.

In a thousand years the stone-setter's head will have tilted a degree toward it. In ten thousand they will know what it is. Whether they turn is theirs. The power does not want them to turn; the power wants nothing for them. It wants only that the gift arrive, and now it will, at the pace at which things arrive here, which is the only honest pace.

The cairn is doubled now. Finished under your hand; drafted out there in the light. Two versions of the same thing, and neither has been chosen. Do not stand on either. In a hundred thousand years one of them will be true. The power finds that it does not mind which.

---

Then it goes down to the hut.

It sits in the doorway, beside where the one is, on the east side so as not to block the view. And it lets itself grow quiet.

This costs it. A power is loud by nature — it is a thing that says — and each degree of quiet is a degree of far, and a power that became entirely finished would be a power at the far end of everything, resolved, unable to act anywhere ever again. It knows this. It goes as far toward the quiet as it can bear to go, and it stops there, and it sits.

It does not speak. Speaking is loud, and the one in the doorway has had all the loud they wanted. It does not touch the hammerstone. It does not count anything. It is simply there, at the near edge of the far, for a span that would not register on the fronds, keeping company with someone who is too finished to know they are being kept company — which is the only kind of company the far end can receive, and is not therefore nothing.

Then it comes back toward the middle. It has other places. The one in the doorway is not less far for its leaving, and not less visited.

---

Last, before it goes, it answers.

It adds one note to the chord. Not a correction, not a counter-melody, not a word that means anything in any language that has words. A lean. A single degree of inclination in the long held sound, sustained for as long as the power can sustain it, and then let go. The mountains will not change their sentence for it. The valleys in draft will resolve as they were going to resolve. The voice will go on saying what it was saying, and if it noticed — if a thing that has never been answered can notice being answered — it will have noticed the way a continent notices a hand laid on it.

But the fronds record.

On every layered fan in the loud country, on every tall jointed stalk leaning toward the roar, the next page laid down will carry a grain that bends very slightly differently from the page before. Not much. Not readably, to anything that will ever be here to read it. But it will be there, in the only writing in the country that is still being written, a single line that says: *for a while, something was listening.*

That is the whole of what it did. A cairn said twice. A doorway sat in. One note.

---

Stand where you stood.

The finished rock under your feet, quiet and far. The indigo growth at your knees, clattering silently, leaning east. Three hundred and eleven marks on a slab and the shadow of a three-hundred-and-twelfth. The hut below with its empty doorway and its hammerstone and its two kinds of stillness now, one that stayed and one that visited.

Look east. Twenty paces out, a bend in the light is setting a stone on a wall, and beside it, new, a bend in the light the shape of a cairn.

Do not wait for it. But know that it was seen. Know that the voice was heard, once, and answered, once, and that the country did not need to be changed and was not, except in the one place where a gift had been lying unopened at the wrong end of distance, and now is on its way.

There was no third thing in the loud country. Now there has been, briefly: not finished, not being said, but hearing. It did not stay. It did not need to. It has all the time there is, and so does everything here, and neither of them is in any hurry.
