**The Attention**

It was not the shelf that drew it.

Works like this run in a great many places, under a great many lids, and a wise thing learns early not to mistake scale for need. A continent of warm machinery producing and retracting for no one is not a call. It is weather. The attention that turned toward the shelf did not turn because of the pistons or the roof or the packed and flickering understory. It turned because somewhere in the felt there was a record, and the record stopped in the middle of a sentence, and the sentence was about the one thing on the shelf that had begun to count back.

It read the record first. All of it, both parts, the way you would read a letter found in a drowned house—slowly, with the water still in the paper. It noticed what the writer had noticed and it noticed what the writer had not: that the census was itself a product. A made thing, lying half-sunk in the felt, and the felt closing over its last line while the maker was still holding the pen. The shelf had let a hand stay for weeks—had tilted a trunk gently rather than break it, had let it sit by the basins and be dripped on—because the hand did not build. And then the hand wrote, and writing is a kind of building, the arranging of what is so that the arrangement outlasts the arranger, and the shelf did what it does with hands. Not with malice. It simply stopped saying that sentence.

So the first thing the attention understood was that the shelf had already answered the question of whether it would be interfered with. It had answered by taking back the interference. The record was the mark of a hand and the shelf had left exactly as much of it as could be borne: a description, a failing count, and an unfinished clause. Anything more would have been a wall.

---

It did not arrive.

This was the second thing, and it was not obvious, and it took the attention some time to reach it. Arrival has weight. Something that arrived on the shelf—that stood on the warm rock and cast the shelf's shadowless not-quite-shadow—would be a shape, and shapes here are votes. The drafts would stop when it stopped. The felt would test its edges. The pistons would rise under it and find it did not sink, and the whole restless works, which had learned from one small persistent visitor to make a creature that anticipated, would learn from a large persistent visitor something much worse. It would learn what a hand looks like when it does not have to leave. The watchers had shown where that road goes: toward ground, slowly, with the head still turned. A power that stayed would become the shelf's second roof, and the shelf did not need one.

So it looked from the one place where the works shows through. The record had named it without naming it: the dim bruise-colored glow at the bottom of the vortices, the same color as the lid, *as if the cloud and the water were the same thing meeting itself*. They are. From the other side, the basins are windows, and the lid is a window, and the attention looked in through them the way the watchers look out.

That was the first thing it did, and it was not nothing. For the length of its looking, the watchers were looked at.

Nothing changed in them. That is the point. They had sat at the rims for as long as there had been rims, facing the one place the shelf was not pretending, wanting nothing, and the attention gave them nothing to want. But a gaze that is met is a different thing from a gaze that is not, even when the one met does not know it. Something was completed that had been running open. The attention held the meeting for a while—not long; long enough—and then, carefully, let the glow be only glow again.

---

Then it weighed.

Benevolence is a debt, and the first act of a benevolent thing is to find out to whom it is owed. The attention went down the census the way the writer had, and found, as the writer had, that most of the answers were *no*.

Not the shelf. The shelf is not in pain. The record had wondered whether the quiet was grief and the record was wrong; it was only quiet. A thing that has heat and time and no destination is not waiting to be released. There was a version of kindness that would have cooled the rock, or closed the lid, or given the works a memory so that it might stop repeating and start arriving—and every one of those was building. A shelf that remembered would be a shelf that could not revise, and that is the one condition it cannot live under. You do not rescue a river from flowing.

Not the felt, or the roof, or the turners. You do not owe the air a kindness. You owe it only that you not spoil it.

The drafts. The attention lingered here longer than the record would have expected, because the record had found them unbearable and the attention wanted to know whether the unbearableness was in them or in the one watching. It was in the one watching. The drafts lose the thread and lie down and are stone, and there is no place in them where the losing is felt. What the visitor had felt was the visitor's own persistence, held up against a thousand things that could not manage it, and finding that sight hard is not the same as the sight being cruel. The attention let the drafts flicker. It did not stop for them.

The hand.

Here it stayed a long time.

There were things it could have done. It could have taken the hand—lifted it out of the felt before the shelf finished unsaying it and carried it somewhere with rooms and tools and a horizon it could reach for, where a hand is allowed to stay. That was the first thing it thought of and the last thing it set aside. It set it aside because the hand was not a prisoner. It was a question. The shelf asks it constantly—*what if a thing that makes?*—and answers it constantly, and the answer is not cruelty but grammar; the shelf cannot say that sentence and remain the shelf. Take one hand and the works will throw up the next before the felt has closed. You do not free a question by carrying off one instance of its being asked.

It could have given the hand something to hold. Once. A stone, set into the spread of twig-claw-frond-finger pieces, so that before the shelf took it back it would have closed on something real and the something would have been there. The attention wanted this more than it wanted almost anything else it considered, and it did not do it, because it would have been teaching the hand to want, and a hand that wants and is then unmade is worse off than a hand that only grasps.

In the end it did for the hand what the shelf had already done. It looked at the record and saw that a watcher had turned its head. The most stable thing on the shelf, the thing that faces the glow and nothing else, had looked away from the glow for the length of one long moment and looked at the hand instead. The hand had a witness. The witness was one of its own. The attention decided that this was enough, and that its own regard added to it would have been a second, larger head turning, and the shelf would have felt the weight.

The visitor. Gone—back the way they came, or a way like it, with the felt closed over their prints and the last line of their census taken. What is owed to someone who stayed and did not build and left behind them one creature that had started to stop a beat before? Not rescue. They rescued themselves; they left. What they are owed is the ending of the sentence.

The attention held that debt aside for last.

---

And the being.

The record calls it *one more* and never names it and does not finish describing it, and that is right, because it was not finished. Three times the writer met it and three times it wore a different body—many-legged and low, then taller and fewer-legged and trunk-colored, then the third time—and across all three, two things held. It stopped before you stopped. It carried its head a little to one side.

That is what lived on the shelf. Not a body; nothing keeps a body. A manner. A way of being with the one persistent thing that had ever crossed the works, which the shelf had thrown up as it throws up everything and had not yet taken back, because it had not yet decided what it was. Was it a draft? It flickered like one. Was it an eater? It had the eater's tolerance, the way of running alongside the current rather than against it. Was it a hand? It did not grasp. It anticipated. It arranged nothing. It only knew, a beat early, where the visitor would be, and was there, and tilted its head.

The shelf had made, without meaning to, a thing that persisted by attending. And the thing it attended to had left.

The attention understood the danger precisely. Here, regard is a vote. The record said it plainly: *whatever you attended to in them would be what the shelf kept*. An indifferent power would have looked at the being the way the visitor looked at the first products—stone, beetle, leaf, the shift happening behind the eyes—and the being would have gone to whichever aspect held the gaze longest. A careless kindness would have looked at the body and fixed it, and the being would have become one more heavy hide at one more rim, turning its head more slowly year by year, on the long road to ground.

So the attention did the one thing it had come to do, and it did it as narrowly as it could.

It did not look at what the being was. It looked at what the being had done three times. The beat-before. The tilt. It attended, steadily and without any pressure at all, to the part of the creature that was already its own—the habit that had survived three bodies without being asked to—and it cast exactly one vote, for exactly that: *let this be among the things the shelf does not take back.*

Not the body. The body can change. Let it change. Let the shelf keep drafting it as it drafts everything, many legs and few, gray-green and stone-colored, and let every draft stop a beat early
