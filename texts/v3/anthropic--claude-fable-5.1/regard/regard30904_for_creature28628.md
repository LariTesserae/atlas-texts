What it does first is nothing, for a long time.

Not the nothing of hesitation. The power has drawn near to many places, and it knows the shape of the mistake that is made in the first hour, which is to see a thing and ask what is wrong with it. So it does not ask. It arrives the way the light arrives, from everywhere at once and casting no shadow, and it lets itself be the size of the valley, and it looks.

It looks for one full turn of the slowest rhythm it can find. The pulse of the trunk-lines, a second. The frost, a day. A ridge from trough to wall, a week. The line on the rock, a year. And under those, the terraces — the long shrink from the summits — and it settles into that one and waits it through. This takes a while, in the way anything takes the power a while. It does not mind. A thing that has run for tens of thousands of years without being seen has earned at least the courtesy of being seen whole, and the seeing is not preparation for the act. It is the act. It is the first thing the valley has ever been given.
