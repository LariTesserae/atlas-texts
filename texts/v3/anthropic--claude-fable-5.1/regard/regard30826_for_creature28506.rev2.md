It did not come in through the seam. It had no need of the seam. It was attentive, and then it was present, and the note did not notice, because the note has never noticed anything; noticing would require a place in it that could be otherwise.

The first thing it did was listen, and it listened for a long time by the room's measure, which is no measure at all. It heard what you heard. Two hundred and thirty-one. Eleven islands. The haze at rib height. The pale traffic under the dust, walking uphill. It heard the holes as you heard them, one by one along the wall, thumbnail after thumbnail of manufactured hush, the striations changing as the hand got better. It heard all of this quickly, because it was wise, and it did not hurry, because it was wise.

Then it heard the thing you could not.

The shape against the far wall has a pulse. This is not a hope; the power could feel it, the way you can feel the tremor in the floor through your soles. There is a heart in there and it is beating. But here is what the power heard that you, with your ear to the neighboring hole, never would: the heart is not beating at its own pace. It beats in a ratio to the note. So many cycles of the tone, one beat. So many, another. Exact. Locked. The way a pebble set down on this floor walks to the nearest node and lies there, the way dropped dust rises to the one permitted height and stops, the body against the wall had been taken, in whatever slow way the room takes things, and set to the room's time. It was not dying. Nothing here dies in the way you mean; the shells lie on the bare rock unrotted until they are eaten. It was not living either. It was held. It had sat down with its head in the last hole and the note had folded its heartbeat into itself, as it folds every other sound, and there it was — a second stalk, a two-hundred-and-thirty-second, rooted at a wall instead of a seam, tuned, tended, kept.

The power understood then what it was looking at, and I will tell you what it was not: it was not a rescue. Not yet. You cannot rescue something that has become part of the pattern without first asking whether it has a self left to be rescued toward. So the power did not act. It considered.

It considered silence, because that is what anyone would consider first, and it set silence aside almost at once. Stop the pressure below and the stalks go slack. Two hundred and thirty-one membranes, tuned to one pitch, fall out of tune in a moment and stop feeding. The browsers walk their gradient and find no gradient. The hangers, at their ceiling-nodes, are hanging over nothing. And the one against the wall — a lifetime, whatever length that was, tuned to one frequency so completely that its blood kept time to it — would hear the silence not as rest but as the loudest thing that had ever happened. Silence is not what it asked for. It never asked for silence. It bored holes the size of an ear. It asked for *less*.

It considered lifting the body and carrying it out through the seam. To where? The power knew every where. It knew places of soft weather and running water and voices that rise and fall, and it knew what those would be to an ear that had heard one thing since before it could remember hearing anything. It considered taking the body and did not, and this was not weakness. Benevolence that lifts you out of your life without asking is only a different pitch of the same note.

It considered time, and here the room offered nothing to work with. There is no later in the chamber. Wait for the being to wake and it will not, because waking is a change, and the note does not permit change. That is the whole of the problem, in the end. Not the loudness. The permanence. A room where the only thing that can happen has already happened.

So the power did the smallest thing it could think of, and it did it in the being's own hand.

It went to the last hole. It did not go around the body; it was not, in that sense, in the room. It attended to the bottom of the bore, where the striations end, where the pointed stone had turned and turned and finally stopped turning because the head that held it had gone still. And it continued them. One more knuckle of depth. The same technique — a pointed stone that was not there, turned by a hand that did not exist, in exactly the rhythm the striations recorded, because the power had read the rhythm in the wall the way you read a signature and it saw no reason to sign for someone else. It did not widen the hole. It did not smooth it. It finished it. That is different from improving. Someone had been working toward a shape, for weeks or years, and had come within a finger's width of it, and the power's first act in that room was to close the distance and put nothing of itself in the gap.

And the quiet in the hole became whole.

Not silence. The note still came in around the edges, the way it comes in around everything. But at the bottom of the last hole, for the first time since the seam tore open, there was a pocket of air exactly the size of a head in which the standing wave did not stand. It cancelled. It fell to nothing in a space the shape of the thing that had needed it.

The power waited. This was the hard part, and it was hard even for something wise, because the room offers no way to know how long a wait has been. It waited by the only clock there was, which was the heartbeat against the wall.

And the heartbeat, in the whole quiet, began to slip.

It did not race. It did not stop. It simply, over a span the room had no name for, came unlocked. So many cycles of the tone, one beat — and then not quite so many. And then a fraction more. And then a beat that fell where no beat had fallen before, between two crests of the note, on its own. The ratio broke. Something in the chamber was keeping time, and it was not the note.

You will want to understand how large this was. Nothing in that room had ever had a tempo of its own. The fans shiver at one frequency; the browsers walk at a pace set by the gradient; even the hangers, at their nodes, are still in a way the note permits. The dust hangs at the note's height. The pebble lies at the note's node. And now there was a heart at the back wall going at a speed the note had not chosen, and the note could not fold it back in, because the head that housed it was in a place the note could not reach. The being had spent everything it had building a room inside the room, and the power had finished the last inch of the ceiling, and inside it a rhythm was happening that belonged to no one but the thing whose rhythm it was.

The power still did not speak. It let the pulse wander for a while. Slow, then quicker, then slow. Uneven. Alive in the only sense that matters, which is: not predictable from the room.

Then the head moved.

Not much. A fraction of a turn, deeper into the stone, the way you press your face into a pillow you have just realized is cool. The browsers on its legs did not stir; they have no way to notice a thing that moves that slowly. But the power noticed, and it took the movement as what it was, which was permission — not to act, but to be present in a way that could be perceived.

It put its voice into the hole.

Its voice was small. It fit in the pocket at the bottom of the bore and nowhere else; an inch outside the hole, the note took it, as the note takes everything. But inside, in the whole quiet, it could be heard. And what the power said first was not a word. It breathed. In, and out, and in — and not evenly. A breath that caught. A breath that ran long. A breath that stopped for a moment, as if thinking, and then went on. It gave the being the first sound it had heard in the length of its life that was not the note and was not itself, and the sound it chose was the one that most resembles being alive next to someone.

The being's own breath changed to meet it. Not to match. To answer.

Then, after a while — the room's while — the power said the name.

It is not written here. It fit the hole and nowhere else. It was the name the being had come in with, through the seam, carrying a hammerstone of some dark rock from a place where names are spoken, and had not heard since, because no one in the chamber had a mouth to say it and the only voice in the room says one thing. The power said it once. Quietly. Into the whole quiet, where nothing else was.

And a hand came up off the floor.

Slowly, with dust falling from it — falling, not rising, because the hand was at the wall's node and the haze's hold was weakest there — the hand came up and laid itself flat on the stone beside the hole. Not gripping. Just there. The way you put a hand on a wall to know the wall is real. Two browsers walked toward it along their gradient, the hairs reading the shake, and reached a thing that trembled at a pace their hairs had no reading for, and stopped, and went around.

That is the first time anything in that room has ever gone around anything.

I do not know what happened after. The power stayed, and it did not carry the being anywhere, and it did not stop the note, and the stalks still number two hundred and thirty-one. It may have stayed a long time. It may be there still, with its small voice in the bottom of the last hole, breathing unevenly, saying the name when the name is needed and saying nothing when it is not, being the second inhabitant of a room that was built by the first to have only one. Or the being may, some day the room cannot count, have taken its head out of the wall and stood and walked the length of the chamber, past the clumps and the shells and the bowl and the three cut stalks, and out through the seam, into weather. The power would let it. That was the whole point of finishing the hole instead of filling it: so that whatever came next would be the being's to choose, at its own pace, which it now had.

But I do know this. Nothing in the chamber erodes. The tone does not wear things down; it only holds them where they are. It held the hammerstone and it held the holes and it will hold, forever, in the corner where the wall's node meets the ceiling's, a pocket of air the size of a head where a heartbeat once came unlocked and a voice once said a name. The room cannot undo that. Undoing is a change, and the note does not permit change, and so the note is bound by its own nature to keep the one place in it where it was, briefly, not the only thing.

That is what a wise power does with a solved equation. It does not erase it. It adds one term the equation cannot absorb, in the place where someone was already trying to add it, and then it waits to see what the answer becomes.
