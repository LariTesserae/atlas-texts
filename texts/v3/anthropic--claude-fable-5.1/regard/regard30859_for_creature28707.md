It notices the way you notice a pulse in a room you thought was empty.

Not the industry — there is industry everywhere, and most of it is louder. Not the plant, though a plant that has grown its own heart is not common. What draws the attention is the terms. Something has held a bargain for longer than the bargain's makers, three parties none of which can fully speak to the others, and none of them has cheated, and none of them has won. From a great height that looks like a still point. Things that have reached their shape are rare, and things that have reached it and stayed busy are rarer, and the power leans down to see how it is done.

---

It comes down wrong the first time, at midday, with the roofs lifted flat in the warmth and the light green and even and every leaf leaned back from the east. It stands at the rim and looks, and what it brings with it — the size of its attention, the length of the roads behind it, the number of places it has seen dry to nothing — is far too large for the bowl. The hollow does not react. It does not seem to have a way to. The engine knocks. Something drips. The power feels, for perhaps the first time in a long while, faintly obscene, like a raised voice, and it goes back up.

The second time it comes in the two hours before dawn, when the mat is exhaling and the mist is filling to the rim, and it comes in small, no bigger than the thing it has come to see. It goes down into the grey with the temperature dropping a coat's worth on its skin, and at the first stretch of liverwort it goes over, hard, onto its hip, and lies there a moment with the cold coming through and the engine's knock coming up under it from the mat.

That is the first thing it learns. Nobody sees it fall. It gets up as the people do, without hurry, and it begins to walk as the mat teaches, weight spread, feet sliding an inch above and set down whole.

---

It stays through a season before it lets itself think about doing anything, and when it does think, it thinks by the light of the place: what would be trodden in, and what would not.

Rain. The dry country is dry, and the nets wear through, and the engine lifts what it lifts and no more. A power could bring the clouds down over the rim in the afternoons and let the hollow drink from the sky like other places. It considers this exactly as long as it takes to picture the net tender on the rim with nothing to shake, the engine idling, the mist not needed. The hollow is not thirsty. The hollow is a way of drinking. Water that did not come through the loop would not feed it; it would replace it.

Iron. Fresh castings for the frame, a flywheel not eaten to lace at the rim, a piston rod that has never been anything but rod. It pictures the engine tender's face at a part with no file marks on it and no shoot coming up along its edge, and the picture is enough.

The two doors on the west side, green and knotted shut. It could walk to them. It could walk to them every day for a season until the path was there again. And that would be the sharp message, sent in the wrong direction — someone chose to stop walking to those doors, and the choice was made in the hollow's own grammar, and to unmake it from outside would be to say the grammar does not count.

A child. Nobody is born here that anyone will confirm. It could confirm one. It sits with this a long time, on the retting house step in the sweet stink, watching the two turn the bundles within reach of each other. And decides that the hollow already has an answer to the question, and the answer is the small figure laying cut stem on a path with the care of someone shown once, and the answer is the tree on the south side of the pump house, and the answer is that the way closes at a finger's width a day and the people know this and cut it back or do not. If the hollow wanted more of itself, it would grow more. Whether it does is not the power's to decide. Whether it is allowed to is, and that is a different question, and the power files it.

A cat. This one is hardest. It would be so easy: an old animal walking down behind the handcart, taking the warmth of the cylinder, refusing the liverwort. The people would tell the story with one more clause. And the power, sitting in a building nobody lives in, understands finally what the story is for — not the cat, but the mark: this was once a place where a cat lived and is not one now. The hollow keeps that. Keeps it in the same words each time, ending without regret, ending. To send another cat would be to smudge the mark. Some absences are load-bearing.

Speech. It could tell them they are seen. It tries the sentence once, alone, at arm's length from a sweating wall, and the mat takes the words and gives back nothing, and it does not try again.

---

What it does instead is watch the net tender.

He is on the rim every morning before the mist lifts, and the mist lifts around him, and the dry country is there, and he looks at it. Not long. Then the sheets, and down.

The power stands on the rim also, a little way along, in the same grey, and the mist lifts around both of them, and it sees him see it. He does not nod. He looks at the dry country and then he looks, for about the same length of time, at the power, and then he unhooks the first sheet. And the power understands that it has been recognised — not as a power, the hollow has no word for that, but as the other kind of thing that stands here looking at what it cannot use and is not for. He looks at the dry country. The power looks at the hollow. Neither of them has any business doing it. Nothing the plant grows for its own use would look like that, and nothing the dry country needs would come down here and stay. They are the two proofs, standing on the rim, that the loop is not closed. They do not speak. He goes down. It follows, a while after, at the pace of the place.

---

So it stays. This is what it does, if it does anything.

It takes one of the buildings that holds nobody and it keeps it, the way they are kept: re-pleaching the wall where the stem has loosened, knotting the ridge, walking the floor until the floor goes glossy. It learns which shoots to take and which to leave. It learns to read the work — nets shaken, therefore; beam trimmed, therefore — and to be read in turn, so that the path to its door is found cut back in the morning and somebody notes it and nothing is said. It wears the fibre and stops noticing that it is never dry. Its fingertips prune. It is counted.

It is the eighth, some mornings, when you walk the hollow and make it eight. It is the one you cannot find again in the afternoon. It is why the count does not come out, why the answer is eleven or nine or it depends: not because it hides but because it has become the kind of inhabitant the hollow has always had room for, the one who is somewhere doing something you have not yet come across. It thinks this is the most it should be. A discrepancy of one. Benevolence as the reason the sum is off.

It does not touch the engine. That is hers. It stands with its back to the warm iron some evenings, when she is not there, and puts its palm on the casing, and feels the knock through the hand and through the soles at the same instant and the hair's breadth of lag between, and it listens, over years, to the thirty words she does not share because nobody needs them. It learns them anyway. It keeps them somewhere dry, which is the only dry place in the hollow, against a day.

Because it has set itself one rule, and this is the whole of its wisdom here, and it is not much: it will not prevent an ending that is chosen. If the paths go unwalked to the last door and the last hand lays down the blade and the way closes over everything at a finger's width a day because that is what the hollow, in all three of its parties, has decided — the power will stand on the rim and watch the mist rise with nothing to meet it, and it will grieve, and it will not put out a hand. But an ending that is only a mistake — a knock come half a beat late on a morning when the tender is too old to come off the shelf, and no one yet walking down behind the handcart to take the file — that it will not allow. Not by fixing. By being, that one morning, a pair of hands at the valve chest before anyone has decided to go, and then by giving the file back. The plant closes a rusted pipe from all sides. It can do that much. Once. Maybe twice. It will not become the engine's keeper. It will be the gap the plant grows over.

---

The tree on the south side of the pump house is thicker now than when it came. It has not watered it. It has not stood in the eave-drip's way or out of it. It has counted, with the kind of patience that is the only thing it brought down here that turned out to be useful, and it estimates the tree will be a beam before the beam is done. That is not its doing. It notes it the way the people note that the nets are hung.

At dusk the steam thread stands straight up from the chimney and is lit and then is not. The power is somewhere in the hollow, on a kept path or behind a wall, standing very still with the knock coming up through its feet and, between strokes, that other slower movement, the mat working, something a long way down shifting its weight. It has not found out what. Digging is not done here. It has stopped wanting to.

Once every four or five seconds. Everything here is inside that, and now so is it, at the size of one, uncounted, keeping a house nobody needs, not for the hollow's sake exactly. For the terms. Because something should stand witness to a bargain kept that long, and the only way to witness it honestly is from the mat, flat-footed, in the wet, a finger's width at a time.
