It arrives the way everything arrives, over the rim, because there is no other way in. But it does not need the air, so the slow air does not catch at it, and it does not need to land, so the floor is not its concern. It is there in the fog hour. The fog does not bead on it.

It has come because it was drawn, the way the swifts are drawn — by the one low place in a country that has finished. It does not, at first, know what it has been drawn to. It only knows that on the whole worn table of the plateau there was one place where the grain of time ran differently, and that this is a thing worth going to look at.

So it looks.

---

It sees all of it at once, which is a thing it can do and the being who lives here cannot, and it is careful with the difference.

It sees the swifts as a single animal, a column that fills at dawn and empties at dusk and has done so for a span it can count: the bone-seams in the eastern wall go down, and it reads them the way the algae wrote them, layer by layer, and knows to within a few centuries how long the birds have been finding the place. It sees the beetle — the whole of the kind, every one of them, alive now, in the forks of four trees. It sees each bromeliad cup and the frog in it and the frog's one great crossing, and it sees the spiders taking their silk down before the mist, and the bats waiting in the shade for their turn at the air.

It sees the black algae and understands it as the hollow's own memory, and reads down through the memory to the bottom, past the bone, past the oldest grey layers, to the first thin film on wet stone when the bowl was not yet a bowl but only a place where a leaf fell a breath late.

And it sees the ring.

---

It knows what the ring was for. This is not a mystery to it. It knows the makers, or knows their kind, or knows enough of what such things are built to do that the purpose comes to it whole, the way the shape of a word comes to a reader who has seen the letters.

The purpose does not matter. That is what it finds, turning it over. The ring was made for something the size of the plateau, and it was one of many, and the many are gone, and the something no longer has anyone to be done for. What remains is not the purpose. What remains is leakage — the fraction of a field the makers would have measured and corrected and thought of as a flaw. The hollow is a flaw in a dead machine. Everything in it — the mist, the trees, the thousand-in-one lushness, the beetle whose whole kind is here — has grown in the gap between what the ring was for and what it does.

It could mend the ring. Not to its purpose; there is no purpose left to mend it to. But it could take the five pieces and the ones under the floor and set them back in their circle and hold them there, so that the fraction stayed a fraction forever and the stone stopped carrying it apart. It considers this for what would be, in the hollow, a long time. Then it looks again at the stone that carries the pieces, and sees that the stone is inside the field — that the ring is being broken at the ring's own pace, a breath late, and will hold exactly as long as everything else here holds, and not a day less. The clock that will end the hollow is the hollow's own clock. It sets the mending down.

---

Then it looks at what lives on the floor.

Not the seven or eight low grey shapes, at first, but what they do: the clean stone around each of them, and the bones that are not clean, and the way the two facts sit together. The lip goes down in the fog hour and takes everything the night dropped and lifts, and goes around, for a swift.

It does not think they refuse warmth. It thinks — and holds the thought loosely, because it is a guess, and it knows the difference between what it sees and what it supposes — that they refuse speed. The moth came down slowly, wet, drifting. The swift came down hard; it was moving faster than the air would let it and it hit. They will not take in a thing that died of speed. They have spent an age putting speed down, and they will not pick it up in that form either. Whether this is a rule they know or a rule they are, there is no way to tell from outside, and it is outside.
