Start with the looking, because for a long time that is all it does.

It has learned, wherever it learned things, that the first debt a power owes a place is not to mistake it. So it does not arrive. It does not put a foot on the tissue or a light in the air. It lets the hollow be six steps across and also a stone's throw and does not resolve the two, because the hollow has not. It takes the smell as evidence — cucumber, cut grass, vase-water; field, not stem — and files it. It hears the rush and separates it into strands, and hears the creak go round, and the knuckle-crack, and under all of it the one small sound, the dry rasp, and it does not hurry past that one. It lets the light be ten o'clock. It does not look for the sun. It has met light before that came from where it used to be.

It looks for the harm first, because harm is what benevolence is for.

---

The animal is not suffering. It is warm and wet and there is food under its mouth and up is where the foot isn't, and those are its four things, and it has all four. The grooves close behind it and it does not grieve the grooves. The wall lifts it and it does not know it has been lifted. A power that wanted to be useful could give it a fifth thing — a memory, a second slug, one groove that stayed open long enough to be recognized — and the power considers this for exactly as long as it takes to see what it would be: a gift shaped like the giver. Four things is not a poverty. Four things is a life, fully occupied. It leaves the animal alone. It will do that again before it is finished here; it notices already that leaving-alone is going to be most of what it does, and it is not ashamed of that.

The morning is not trapped. The power goes carefully into this one, because it is the kind of thing that looks like a cruelty from outside — a ten o'clock held forever, never allowed its noon. But a morning does not want to be noon. Noon is what happens to a morning, not what it wants. This one has been given the thing nothing else in any field is given: to keep being the hour it is. The light on the far wall is thin and hazed because that is what distance looked like at that hour and it is still that hour. The power does not burn off the overcast. It does not so much as move the shadows.

The flake is going home. It took the power a while to see the direction — the tissue presenting the edge, a fingernail at a time, into the room — and once it saw it it understood the whole plant differently. Not a stomach. Not a tomb. Not even, quite, a keeping, though that was the closest word the room had found for itself. Something is being *returned*. The plant is carrying a dropped thing back toward the hand that dropped it, at the speed a plant can carry anything, and it has grown this whole hollow as the space the reunion will need. The power could reach up the light into the field and find the hand and say *hold still, it's coming*. It doesn't. Nothing that is arriving should be helped to arrive. That is how you break the thing that is bringing it.

And the wall is not eating anyone. The power puts its own attention against the tissue, the way the narrator put a palm, and feels what the narrator felt — warm, pushing back, no edge — and feels, too, what the narrator did not: that there is no appetite in it. The wall does not want the hand. The wall does not know what wanting is. It continues what touches it because continuing is what it does, the way water wets. If there is a fault here it is not malice. It is that nothing in this hollow has ever been asked.

That is the harm, if there is one. Not ending. Not-asking.

---

So it turns to the question of the being, and finds there are two it can speak to, and one it can only look at.

The one it can only look at is the plant.

It is the size the room said it might be — a country, and the country is under the ground, and the light is coming from the flowers. The dark shapes are its traffic and its thinking and its blood and the power does not distinguish these because the plant doesn't. It is total. There is nothing, anywhere in it, that is not it. And in all that mass it has made one hollow, one bubble of not-itself, and furnished the bubble with a morning it grew around by accident and has kept, since, on purpose.

The power understands this the way you understand a thing you have been. A totality has no outside. It cannot be seen; there is nowhere to see it from. So it grows around a piece of outside and holds it and sends its dark shapes past the window one at a time to look, because looking at the morning is the nearest it can come to being looked at. The one that stopped, and flattened its length to the glass, and stayed — that was not curiosity. That was the plant putting its face against the only window it has.

What a benevolent power does for a totality is simple and it is the only thing that can be done for it. It looks back. It stands outside the plant, which nothing has ever done, and it regards the whole of it — the country and the roots and the flowers giving off morning — with attention, for a length of time the plant can feel. Not to change it. To be, for once, the outside that sees. The rush in the walls falters. Not the skip of a heard name. Longer. The pause of something that has just discovered it has a shape.

Then the power lets the plant go back to its rushing, and turns to the two it can address.

---

The one at the window first, because it has been waiting longest.

The rule of the wall, as far as the power can read it: the wall is exactly as perfect as it means to be, and what it means to let through is what moves toward the morning wanting it. The lentil-drop got through. Small ones. Accidents shaped like wishes. The plant tastes its own outside a hair's width at a time and each taste makes the heart skip and then the wall closes and the current goes on.

The power does not tear the wall. It speaks to it in the wall's own language, which is splitting. It puts a bulge in the tissue at face height, directly across from where the narrator stands, exactly where the dark shape pressed itself — and holds the bulge, and lets it grow past a fingernail, past two, and when the knuckle-crack comes it is the longest one the room has heard, a sound like a melon halved. Behind the split the tissue is glassy and behind the glass the traffic is streaming and one of the shapes stops. Holds against the current. Comes to the thin place. And the thin place is thin enough.

It arrives on the floor whole. Not a drop; the length of a forearm, dark, flexing. It does the thing the drop did — lies, lengthens, draws itself toward the tissue — and the power has told the wall *not this one*, and the wall obeys, and the floor stays floor under it. It cannot go in. It has never before been anywhere it could not go in.

It lies in the ten o'clock light. It has no eyes and it does not need them; the light is on it, all along its length, and the power watches it learn what *on* is — that there is a side of you the morning is touching and a side it is not, that there is a warm and a shadow and you are the line between. The smell of cut grass is around it. The rasp of the animal is above it, and it hears — or does whatever it does — the one small sound in the hollow, and turns toward it.

It came in by moving. Nothing else here did. That is what the power has changed, and it is a small thing and a total thing: the hollow now contains one resident who is not something the plant grew around but something the plant let in because it wanted to come. The far wall is no farther. The near wall no nearer. Six steps. But the room has a new rule now, and rules in a place like this are the only thing that grows.

---

The one with the palm last, because that one can be asked, and asking is the point.

The power does not take the hand off the wall. It has seen what unasked kindness looks like from the inside of a slug and it will not do that to something that paced. Instead it does the only thing this hollow has never had done in it. It puts a question in the air at chest height, where the rain comes from, and lets it fall.

*You looked for the opening.*

That is all, at first. The narrator hears it and knows it to be true. Six steps. Turned around. Ran a hand along the whole curve of the wall looking for the fissure where day leaked through. That was wanting out. That was the shape of a person who does not stay in a room because it is warm. And somewhere between the fissure and the palm the wanting went quiet — not because it was answered but because the room is very good at continuing whatever is happening in it, and what was happening was a hand on a wall.

*You can have that back. Or you can have this. Both are fine. The animal has four things and it is fine. The morning is one hour and it is fine. You would be warm and kept and the room would go on being six steps and there would be no more pacing because there would be nothing left in you that measured. That is not a punishment. But it is a thing that would be done to you, and I would rather it be a thing you did.*

The narrator asks, without speaking, how.

*The wall lets through what moves toward the light wanting it. That was true for the drop. It is true in both directions. The morning is in here, but it is also out there — it is the same morning, it has a field, the field has a hand bent over the grass. You would walk toward the far wall and it would stop receding.*

*Or you would stay. And I would stay a while too, because I am not certain, and because this room has never had in it a thing that was here by choice and could leave. You would be the second new rule.*

The dark shape on the floor has found the wall beneath the animal and is lying along it, in the light, not going in. The rasp continues above. The wall bulges under both of them and splits and lifts them a fingernail's depth together, and the shape flexes — it felt that — and the animal does not.

You take your hand off the wall.

Not to go. Not yet. To find out whether you still can, and you can. The print stays in negative and closes in the time it takes to catch your breath, and where it closed the tissue is very slightly warmer, and this time — this is the whole of what the power came to do, and it knows it, and it is enough — this time you noticed.
