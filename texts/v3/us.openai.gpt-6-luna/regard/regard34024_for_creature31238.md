The power does not arrive as a light.

Light would make the ridges visible, but visibility is not the same as understanding. Nor does it speak. Words would be too quick here, laid across a thought that takes an age to gather.

It listens.

For a long while, nothing in the landscape changes on its account. The shelves tilt. Heat threads through the cracks. A pinnacle leans, breaks, and settles against its neighbor. The dark crystal growths are split by the shift, and in time new branches form along the fresh seam. The deep thing feels all of this as it always has: as pressure, fracture, weight.

Then, beneath the oldest ridge, something answers.

Not a knock from one shelf to another, but a vibration that arrives without having traveled through the rock. It does not command the stone to hold. It does not ask the broken pinnacle to stand again. It is simply there: a tone with no edge to it, held so gently that the deep thing can mistake it for its own thought.

The being listens back.

At first it knows the tone only as a new kind of steadiness—not stillness, but the assurance that something can remain present while everything around it changes. It sends the answer through the paired uprights. The pale mineral bands tremble. Far away, another pair trembles in reply. For the first time, the scattered constructions do not merely carry the land’s vibrations onward. They carry a message.

The message is not *Be still.*

It is *I am with you as you change.*

The power does not shore up the ridges or seal the warm cracks. It does not turn the brittle inhabitants into creatures that can withstand every fracture. To do so would be to make them something else, and perhaps to take from them the very way they belong to this place. Instead, it gives them a small gift suited to their nature: what they have felt need not be lost when the stone that held it is broken.

A fallen pinnacle leaves its long vibration in the paired stones. A branching growth, shattered by a shifting shelf, leaves behind the pattern of its making in the seam where it grew. The deep being can find these traces later—not as a map to restore what was, but as a memory it may keep while it becomes something new.

The power makes no promise that the land will grow gentle. It does not know whether gentleness would be a kindness here.

It remains near, without occupying the place. When a shelf gives way, it does not prevent the fall; it keeps the falling from being the only thing that happened. When a new cleft opens, it listens for the first faint growth along its heated edge. When the deep thing reaches across the ridges in thought, the power is there—not pulling, not steering, only answering with enough patience for the answer to be heard.

And so the mountains continue to rise and break in the dark.

But now, somewhere beneath their shifting weight, there is a witness that does not confuse change with abandonment.
