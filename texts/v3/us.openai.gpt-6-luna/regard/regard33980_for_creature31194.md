The power did not descend as light.

Light would have meant nothing here. It came as a pressure so gentle that no creature fled from it: a change in the water, a touch passing over stone and shell and the dark backs of the deep ones. It listened through the basin.

At first it searched for the one who lived here. It found no king of the plain, no creature standing apart from the rest. The being was the exchange itself: the little mouths worrying stone; the silver slivers feeding; the armored schools turning together; the broad animals carrying shelter and hunger beneath their shells; the immense bodies below, drawing the tides through their old channels.

The power understood, then, that the basin was not waiting to be saved.

It did not warm the water. It did not raise the horizon or bring plants to the flats. It did not smooth the strange folds of distance, because the inhabitants had learned to travel through them. It did not quiet the grinding roar. That sound belonged to the basin as surely as breath belonged to the deep ones.

So the power stayed, and paid attention.

It followed one small creature as it loosened from a ridge and entered the shallows. It followed the current that carried it, the school that turned around it, the larger body whose passage changed the current again. It saw how little any life here could do alone, and how much the whole could do without knowing it was whole.

Then, once, when the next great turning came, the power gave the basin a single moment in which cause and consequence arrived together.

The deep bodies began to move. The water leaned toward them. The schools felt the change and turned; the shell-backed animals sealed themselves; the smallest creatures gripped the ridges. Nothing was made safe from the turning. Nothing was spared its own nature. But for that one moment, every inhabitant received the same true signal at the same time.

The basin answered in its accustomed way: by moving.

Afterward, the power withdrew. No mark remained that could be distinguished from the old marks of water and stone. The small creatures returned to their feeding. The schools spread beneath the film. Far below, something breathed.

If the basin remembered the power, it remembered only as a faint pressure in the dark—not a command, not a promise, but the brief certainty that, for one instant, the whole had felt itself as whole.

And the power, having found no wound to close and no life asking to be made other than it was, let the basin continue.
