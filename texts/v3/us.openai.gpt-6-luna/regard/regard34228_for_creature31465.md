The power came without light.

It had no need to announce itself. It listened first: to the pumps turning under their burden, to water passing through the filters, to the press closing and opening. It felt the roots holding the brickwork and the brickwork holding the beds. It learned the slow agreement by which the Underbeds endured.

It found no prayer there. No frightened creature waited in the dark. The growth did not long for a voice, or for open sky, or for the company of animals. It leaned toward warmth and water; it spread where there was room. The power understood that abundance was not the same as need, and that a place could be complete without being perfect.

So it did not give the plants minds. It did not send creatures into the drains, or break the ceiling open to the sun. It did not make the green grow faster. Such gifts would have been gifts only by someone else’s measure.

But in a service bay, a bearing inside the oldest pump had begun to wear unevenly. The change was slight: a tremor buried in the machinery’s familiar roar. Left alone, it would have worsened. One day the pump would stop, and the water would begin to settle in the wrong places.

The power laid a hand on that small, hidden future.

The bearing turned true again—not new, not deathless, but sound enough to last. And where the roots had begun to close around the valve that would one day need attention, a thin channel of stone remained clear beneath them. The roots did not retreat. They simply found another path.

That was all.

The Underbeds did not know they had been spared a failure. The plants continued their patient reaching. Water went out and came back. The press closed, released, and waited. Nothing woke beneath the leaves; nothing looked up into the amber lamps.

Yet from then on, when a part began to fail, its first warning carried a little farther through the machinery. There would be time to notice. Time to repair, to harvest, to reroute the water. Not endless time, and not an exemption from change—only enough time for change to remain part of the ordinary cycle.

The power left as quietly as it had come. It did not bless the Underbeds with more life. It blessed them with the continuation of their own.
