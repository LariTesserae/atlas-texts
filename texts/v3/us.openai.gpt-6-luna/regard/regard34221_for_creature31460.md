The power did not mistake emptiness for grief.

It did not look upon the uninhabited sea and decide that the world needed filling. It did not send down animals, raise continents, or make the dark fronds into something grander than they were. The planet had not asked to be hurried toward any particular future. It was still discovering what a future could contain.

So the power listened.

It listened to the deep pressure traveling beneath the water, to the slow grind of stone becoming stone, to the weather rehearsing its immensities. It learned the world’s rhythms—not to command them, but to know when they were changing.

And once, when a young ridge had nearly surfaced, a cross-current gathered under it, strong enough to tear the new rock apart. The power did not stop the sea. It eased the current by the smallest degree. The ridge held. Water broke over it, withdrew, and broke again. Nothing on the world could have seen the difference. Perhaps nothing ever would.

Elsewhere, in the deep, the planet sent one of its bright geometric pulses through the water. The power answered with a pulse of its own: not a word, not an instruction, but a pattern that returned the first pattern intact.

For a long while, the world continued as before. Storms turned. Mountains rose and sank. The sea climbed into the clouds and came back as rain.

Then, beneath the next pulse, the planet made a pause. A small one. A space between one movement and the next, held long enough to be more than accident.

The power did not fill that space. It left it open.

On the ridge, the fronds remained pressed flat by the wind. Their hidden roots, if they were roots, went on finding purchase in the stone. The power did nothing to tell them what they must become.

It stayed near—not as a keeper, nor a maker, but as a witness able to answer if the world ever reached outward. And when it withdrew, it left no tower, no mark, no claim.

Only the faint possibility that, somewhere within its vast and solitary labor, the planet might one day distinguish its own making from an answer.
