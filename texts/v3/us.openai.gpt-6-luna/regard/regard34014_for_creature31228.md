The power did not mistake stillness for suffering.

It listened to the chamber’s exact repetitions: the fan’s dry scrape, the colony’s clicking passage, the note inside the stone. It saw that nothing was being crushed, starved, or driven from its place. The creatures were not waiting for rescue. The chamber’s order was not a plea merely because it was strange.

So the power did not break the walls, silence the machines, or lift the colony into a brighter world. It did not give the creatures a purpose they had never lacked.

But it noticed that every path returned.

And because it was wise, it did not decide that return was wrong. It only made it no longer compulsory.

Far behind the mound, where two mineral ribs met beneath the dust, the stone eased apart by the width of a single creature. No crack split the chamber. No old seam widened. The new opening gave off no command, no heat stronger than the rest, no scent to lure the colony from its familiar routes. Beyond it lay another pocket in the deep earth: warm, dry, and safe, with unfamiliar stone and space enough for a different pattern.

Then the power withdrew.

For a long while, nothing changed. The creatures crossed the ledges, gathered at the black fans, and turned toward the humming fin. The machines kept their patient rhythms. Dust lifted and settled as before.

At last, one pale body reached the new opening. It paused there, antennae trembling. The note in the wall remained what it had always been. From beyond the opening came no answer—only room.

The creature could have turned back. It might have. The power did not move it.

Behind it, the others continued their circuits. Ahead, the dark waited without insisting.

And in the chamber, for the first time, a path did not lead home unless its traveler chose to follow it.
