The power did not enter the mountain.

It had no need to. Its attention reached the hidden chamber without widening the crack or disturbing the heat. It felt the city’s careful measures, the inhabitants moving in the intervals, the unfinished stone at the far end. It understood that to make the place safe by making it still would be to take from it the very thing by which it lived.

So it did not stop the mountain becoming.

It did one small thing.

In the rough stone beyond the empty square, it laid a pause—not a wall, not a command, but a little more time. A fracture that might have raced through the chamber would instead travel slowly, grain by grain. A shift that might have crushed the pale metal towers would announce itself first in their shadows. The mountain remained free to change. The city was given time to change with it.

Then the power waited.

It did not give the inhabitants names. It did not fill their square with figures shaped like itself. It left the blank buildings blank, the dark plates sealed, and the unmeasured space beyond them unmeasured.

A droplet gathered beneath the bridge and did not fall. It held there, clear and perfectly ordinary, except that it would not evaporate or be taken into the city’s hidden channels. It was an offering, and also a question. Nothing more was asked of it.

For a long while, the inhabitants did not answer. They moved through the terraces, feeling the small new patience in the mountain. The city’s shadows sharpened, softened, sharpened again. A bead rolled uphill and stopped beside the empty square.

At last, the square changed.

Not much. One of its angles eased by a fraction, making room for no particular thing. The sealed doors remained sealed. The droplet still hung beneath the bridge.

The power understood.

It withdrew its attention until it was no more than a kindness built into the stone: a little warning before danger, a little time before choice. The city could use it, ignore it, or one day decide it had never been there.

Beyond the square, the mountain continued becoming.

And within it, the inhabitants continued to make room.
