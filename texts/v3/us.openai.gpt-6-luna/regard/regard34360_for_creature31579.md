The power noticed the plain first as a pattern of care.

It saw the leaves turning, the cables tightening, the pale creatures crossing the shafts with their small mineral lamps. It saw every seam answered, every fallen thing given another use. And it saw, too, that the country had no word for being tired. Not because it was tireless, but because nothing in its old instructions had made room for the question.

The power did not send rain. It did not loosen the roots or soften the white sky. It had seen what a flood might do to a world that had learned to live without water, and understood that abundance, offered without understanding, could be another form of harm.

Instead, it waited at the edge of the plain.

A small fracture opened in a ventilation gallery. The pale creatures found it, as they always did. One laid itself across the gap; then another. But this time, before the plant could close around them, the power held the edges gently apart.

The creatures withdrew.

They returned, circling the fracture. One pressed its plated back to the wall. The opening remained—not widening, not threatening, simply open. The creatures tried again to seal it. The power held steady. They tried from another angle. Still the gap remained, harmless and unfilled.

Far above, the roots tightened. The old habit traveled toward the disturbance, asking for its shape. Ribs leaned. Leaves tilted. The country prepared to incorporate the error.

The power did not prevent it.

It only kept the little opening safe long enough for the plain to discover that not every gap was a wound.

This took time. The signal crossed the country by slow degrees. Some districts braced against the unfamiliar space; others sent their pale creatures to inspect it. The factories continued their sorting. The avenues remained straight. Nothing was commanded to change.

At last, in the gallery beside the fracture, one creature stopped moving.

The others passed around it. It did not seal the gap. It did not carry powder or seek another route. It rested against the wall, its small lamp dim beneath its plates.

The power released the edges.

The opening stayed open.

Nothing terrible followed. No hidden hunger came through. No structure collapsed. The plant grew a narrow lip around the fracture, not to close it but to keep its edges from crumbling. The creature remained there through a full cycle of light, and then left of its own accord.

The country remembered this, though not as an instruction. It remembered it as a possibility.

There would still be repairs. There would still be places where a seam needed closing, where a fallen stalk must be gathered, where a structure leaned too far to stand. The power did not teach the plain to neglect itself. It left no commandment against work.

It gave the country one safe place where work could stop.

Over time, the opening became a chamber. The pale creatures visited it, singly or in small groups. Sometimes they rested there. Sometimes they simply passed through without carrying anything. The plant-built walls grew a fine, unassigned lining—not a product, not a support, not a surface meant to be sorted. It was enough that the lining held.

The oldest inhabitant could not say what had happened. It had no chamber in which to gather and name the gift. But, now and then, when a new crack appeared somewhere in the plain, the old signal moved through the roots and paused.

Not every time. Not for long.

Just long enough for the country to notice that it had a choice.
