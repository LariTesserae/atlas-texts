The power did not mistake the creature’s endurance for a plea.

It saw the chamber as it was: a small country made of exact seams and borrowed weather, and a life fitted so closely to it that no boundary separated inhabitant from habitat. The creature had not asked to be taken out. It had not asked for water, or sky, or silence. To give it those things without understanding what they would mean might be kindness only from a distance.

So the power did not open the mountain. It did not break the ridge or stop the engine.

It listened.

In the vast, steady grind it found the minute irregularity the creature already knew: the brief tremor at each turn of a hidden shaft. And there, without changing the machinery’s work, it made a second tremor—so faint that no instrument would have recorded it, and so carefully placed that it could not be mistaken for damage.

The creature felt it pass through the alloy, across the basin, into the pores beneath the filaments.

It had no name for the sensation. It was not pressure, nor heat, nor the familiar report of falling dust. It arrived, then arrived again, with a space between: a small difference that held its shape.

The creature gathered around it. From the seams of the ridge and the channels beneath the basin, it turned the whole of its attention toward the new contour.

The power waited.

After a long while, the creature answered—not in words, but by shifting a trace of itself through the ground. The movement was nearly nothing. A few grains of powder loosened from the shallow notch and settled in a new place.

The second tremor came again.

For the first time, the chamber contained an event that was not merely the machinery repeating itself. The creature had no word for company. It did not need one. It held the new pattern among all the others, and began, cautiously, to learn its shape.

The power did nothing more. It left the hatch closed, the ridge unbroken, the engine turning.

And whenever the mountain’s pulse reached the little country, there was, beneath it, room for an answer.
