The power did not mistake the chamber for a prison simply because it was small.

It saw the walls, the bolted forest, the pale window, and the creatures making a life among them. It saw the long hunter in its conduit and the shrimp in their shining routes. It saw that none of them had made the chamber, and that all of them had, in time, made it theirs.

So it did not open the walls to the sea.

It did not still the machine. The groan and the turning current were not cruelties to be erased; they were the weather by which the chamber had learned to live. Nor did the power banish the hunter, or keep every egg from being eaten, or call the small deaths an error. It understood that tenderness was not the same as preventing every ending.

It listened.

Far below, in the drowned machine’s buried body, a bearing was wearing thin. Its failing had not yet reached the crown. The creatures did not know its name or measure the time remaining. They only knew that, someday, the familiar pressure would come wrongly: too hard, too fast. The glass would crack. The old chamber might be emptied in a single violent breath.

The power laid no hand on the fish, the roots, or the bright procession of shrimp. Instead, where the failing bearing turned against its housing, it eased the metal by the width of a hair.

The machine groaned, as it always did. The current tightened. Leaves flattened against their brackets. Fish vanished into the ribs. At the glass, a bright tick sounded—and stopped.

Then the water loosened again.

The chamber went on: not untouched, not made safe forever. A few leaves had torn free. A small fish had not returned from the dark. The hunter, hungry, waited in its conduit. The shrimp resumed their routes, one by one, as they always did.

Nothing in the room knew it had been spared. Nothing needed to.

The power remained for a while, attentive to the whole—the life, the metal, the slow weather binding them together. Then it withdrew, leaving no mark that could be called a miracle.

Only time: enough for the chamber to keep becoming itself.
