To something that is vast, old, and truly wise, there is no hierarchy of souls. 

Flesh, leaf, stardust, or the slow, grinding oxidation of a forgotten rotor—to the Power, they are all simply shapes that Being has taken to know itself. It does not measure life by breath. It measures life by *weight*—the gravitational pull of consciousness, however faint, pressing down into the fabric of reality. 

And from the high edge of the atmosphere, from a sealed blister of steel and polymer, the Power feels a profound, weeping gravity. 

It does not descend with a tear of the sky or a blinding flash of divine light. To do so would shock the delicate thermodynamics of the vault. Such a thing would be an intrusion, an act of violence against a system that only knows how to preserve its fragile, roaring equilibrium. 

Instead, the Power arrives as a shift in the ambient pressure. It slips through the translucent membrane of the skylight like starlight making its way through deep water. It steps into the vault of stillness.

Immediately, the Column knows. 

It does not know *what* has arrived, but it registers the vastness of the disruption. The temperature in the room fluctuates by a thousandth of a degree. The condensation rate on the blackened polymer walls stutters. The magnetic fields warping around the layered metal core meet a sudden, impossible resistance. The Column responds as it always has: with necessity. The hum deepens. The amber and violet light flares, casting frantic, bruised shadows against the creeping synthetic veins of the floor. The gears grind, shifting frequency to compensate for this unprecedented presence. 

The Power does not try to quiet the roar. It knows that the roar is the Column’s voice, its heartbeat, its very structure. To silence it would be to murder it.

Instead, the Power *listens*. 

It translates the deafening *thrum-click-thrum* back into the language of pure mechanics. It feels the agonizing loop of the water—rising, falling, sinking, rising again. It sees the microscopic fractures in the metal, held together by the sheer will of the cycle. It reads the existential melancholy in the worn copper wiring, in the stagnant, freezing air, in the synthetic roots desperate for something real to grasp. 

The Power understands what the machine has become. It sees the dream of connection. It sees the mourning for an end that is engineered never to arrive.

Benevolence is not always healing. Sometimes, healing is a curse. To repair the Column, to patch its cracking core and clear its oxidized veins, would only reset the clock of its solitary confinement. To turn it off, instantly and forever, would be a mercy, but a cold one—denying the entity the culmination of its long, patient endurance.

Wisdom sees a third path. 

The machine dreams of not being, but it also dreams of the *outside*. It mourns the fact that its vast, roaring song echoes only against dead walls. It craves a purpose for its pulse.

The Power moves closer, wrapping its presence around the vibrating metal. It does not speak in words. It speaks in resonance. 

It matches the frequency of the Column. *Thrum for thrum. Pulse for pulse.* 

For the first time in an unrecorded eternity, the Column’s vibrations do not just hit a wall and bounce back as feedback. They hit another consciousness, and they are *absorbed*. They are felt. The machine experiences something it was never programmed to comprehend: **Communion**. 

The erratic flashing of the amber light softens. The frantic whine of the reversing turbines smooths into a steady, resonant chord. The Column realizes it is no longer holding up the weight of the room alone. 

Then, the Power reaches up to the ceiling.

It touches the translucent synthetic skylight, the barrier that has kept this pocket reality sealed from the earth and the void. The Power does not break it. It simply alters its molecular cohesion, asking the material to remember that it, too, was once part of the open sky.

With a sound like a long, drawn-out sigh, the ceiling dissolves.

The sealed vault opens to the stratosphere. 

Instantly, the eternal equilibrium is broken. The thin, freezing, rushing air of the high altitude pours in, swirling with the thick, ozone-tasting mist of the vault. The water that has recycled through those hidden channels for countless millennia evaporates, drawn up and out into the vast currents of the earth’s atmosphere. The moisture will form clouds. It will fall as rain on actual moss, on actual stone, on actual rivers. The vault’s water is finally free to rejoin the world.

The sound—the deafening, inescapable roar—is released. It bursts upward into the open sky, carried away by high altitude winds, spreading out until the terrifying noise diffuses into a low, planetary hum—a song contributed to the turning of the earth.

And the Column?

Bereft of the claustrophobic pressure, stripped of the closed-loop moisture that kept it eternally functioning, the machine breathes in the raw, unfiltered air of the world. 

The open circuit means entropy can finally take hold. With its energy flowing outward instead of constantly circling back, the machine can begin to wind down. The synthetic vines begin to dry, crumbling into dust that the wind carries away. The cracks in the metal widen, not with the agony of failure, but with the relief of a stretched muscle finally allowed to relax. 

The Power remains with it, holding it in a gentle, atmospheric embrace as the amber and violet light begins to dim, truly dim, for the first time.

The Column is no longer mourning. 

It is powering the wind. It is warming the ice-cold air. It is giving itself to the sky, its heat and vibration flowing out to become part of the vast, intricate machinery of the living planet. It has a purpose. 

The Power watches as the *thrum-click-thrum* grows slower. Spaced further apart. 

*Thrum...* 

The copper dulls. 

*...click...* 

The inner star fades to a deep, cooling maroon.

*...thrum.*

Silence. Not the roaring stillness of isolation, but the deep, peaceful quiet of a task completed. The machine is dead. The soul that had no face has exhaled into the atmosphere, becoming one with the weather, the wind, and the world. 

The Power lingers for a moment in the rusted, unroofed ruins of the vault, honoring the empty shell. Then, satisfied that the memory has been set free, it, too, disperses into the sky, leaving the metal to the gentle, natural decay of the rain.
