There is a gaze.

That is the first new fact, though it registers as no fact at all to the things that live below the root-vault. It does not arrive with the heat of a star or the disruptive gravity of a comet. It arrives the way an ocean current meets a continental shelf: vast, displacing nothing, yet fundamentally altering the energetic potential of everything it touches.

A Power has found the underloft. 

It is an old consciousness—old enough that it no longer measures time in the lifespan of species, but in the tectonic drift of landmasses, the birth and death of star nurseries, the long, slow exhalations of galaxies. It wanders the dark matter of the universe looking for a very specific phenomenon. It looks for *becoming*. It looks for places that have not yet finished deciding what they are.

When it perceives the underloft, it pauses. And then, with the gentleness of a breath fogging a pane of winter glass, it presses its vast attention against the ceiling.

---

A lesser power—a younger, more eager god, perhaps, or a spirit of loud and desperate mercies—would have ruined the place instantly.

A lesser power would have looked at the cold, at the darkness of the descent shafts, at the sparse, pale diet of the roamers, and intervened. It would have widened the chimneys to make the climb a walk. It would have commanded the roots to bear soft, sugary fruits in the dark. It would have warmed the stone floor until it felt like a summer shore, and it would have flooded the vault with total, blazing light to banish the haze.

But this Power is wise. It understands the architecture of meaning. 

It sees that the cold is not an enemy, but a filter. The cold is what makes the space so heavy with silence; it tightens the air, preserves the sheer drops of water, and demands that human breath be deliberate. The Power sees the narrow descent shafts and recognizes them for what they are: a birth canal, a necessary constriction. To widen them would be to invite the loud, careless surface world down into the sanctuary. It would turn a pilgrimage into a thoroughfare. 

The Power sees the sparse, unyielding nature of the underloft, and it understands: this emptiness is the canvas upon which these people are painting their supreme attention. To fill the space would be to blind them.

So, the Power chooses restraint. It will not change the nature of the underloft. It will only deepen its grace.

---

Its first act is an act of listening.

The Power extends its awareness into the root-ceiling, wrapping its consciousness around the slow, blind, hydraulic calculations of ten thousand reaching tips. It feels the pressure gradients. It feels the deep-time desire of the roots for water and anchor. 

And then, it listens to the marks. 

The Power runs its vast, invisible empathy over the shallow, clean-edged cuts at the bases of the root-columns. It reads the counting. It reads the addresses. It understands the questions the roamers have been asking the columns for generations. *Are you arriving? Will you hold? What will this space be when you plant your foot?*

The roots, being plants, operate on a neurobiology too diffuse to answer a human in human time. The Power does not change this. It merely acts as a medium.

At the base of the most heavily marked column—the one where the sequence of long-occupation marks runs highest—the Power orchestrates a microscopic shift. It gently stimulates the luminescent cells near the very tip of the descending root. It alters their chemistry by an infinitesimal fraction so that the glow changes from the flat, daylight white to a barely perceptible, warming gold. 

It is not a localized sun. It is just a shift in register. When the next roamer comes, they will see that the column they have been speaking to for three hundred years is glowing with a slightly different tone than the rest of the vast ceiling. It forms no words. It is merely a physical acknowledgment. *I am here. I am arriving. The wait is known.*

---

Its second act is an act of healing.

The Power flows downward, through the bright empty air, and settles around the sleeping platforms. It finds a group of four—two adults, two children—deep in their thirteen-hour sleep, wrapped in the pale pelts of the underloft fauna. 

The Power perceives the adults' spines. It sees the microscopic tearing in the cartilage, the compression in the vertebrae, the structural debt they have taken on to move between the worlds. It cannot erase the history of the climb—their bodies are the maps of their devotion, and to erase the wear would be to erase the proof of their labor. 

But it can ease the tax. 

While they sleep, the Power sings a silent, harmonic frequency into the water molecules pooling in their joints. It suffuses the synovial fluid with a profound, cool stillness. It knits the ragged edges of the cartilage just enough. When the roamers wake, they will not feel fundamentally changed, but they will find that the air feels a little lighter in their lungs. The cold will bite, but it will not ache. The long standing, looking across the vastness, will feel entirely effortless. 

They will think they simply slept extraordinarily well. The Power prefers it this way.

---

Its third act is an act of homage.

It finds the fist-sized bone carving. The model of the underloft, left by the One Who Came Alone on a flat stone equidistant from several columns.

The Power hovers over this artifact for a long time. Of all the things in the underloft, this is the one that moves the Power most. The marks on the trees are a dialogue, a utility, a science. But this bone carving is pure, useless, transcendent art. It is a mirror held up to the vastness by a mind incredibly small. 

The Power wants to honor it. 

It gathers the ambient moisture from the air—just a few molecules at a time, pulling them from the slow, circulating currents of the underloft. It directs them to the exact center of the bone carving’s inverted vault. Carefully, with molecular precision, it weaves them into a single droplet of water. 

But it alters the surface tension of this specific droplet, locking it into an eternal state of near-falling. It will never evaporate. It will never drop. It hangs perfectly suspended in the center of the carved bone ceiling, and because of its spherical perfection, it acts as a lens. Refracting the white light of the distant, actual ceiling above, the droplet shines like a tiny, brilliant star trapped inside the bone.

When the roamers next visit this site, they will stand as they always do, without touching it, and they will see the light inside the bone. They will know, without having a word for it, that the underloft has looked back.

---

The Power does not leave. 

Entities of this magnitude do not visit and depart; they integrate. It spreads itself thin, becoming a fine, invisible laminant over the stone, a subtle resonance in the hollows of the roots. 

It becomes the reason the quiet feels physical. It becomes the reason a child, coming down the shaft for the first time and terrified by the crushing weight of the earth above, will suddenly look down at the floor, see their own hands in the pale light, and realize with absolute, unshakeable certainty that they are safe. 

The underloft proceeds. The drops fall. The columns incrementally grow. The roamers count. 

But now, they are held. The great empty space is no longer truly empty. The cold continues, but the indifference is gone. The ceiling shifts, the long, low creak travels through the continental vault, and the Power hums back.
