It arrives not by breaching the ridge, nor by walking the brutal, vertical distances. It arrives by the gentle logic of inevitability. Where there is a knot in the tapestry, eventually, the hands of the weaver must touch it. 

The Power has no name, just as the Durance have no names, for it encompasses all sequences. It is the Exhalation. It is the Grace of Becoming. It is the intelligence that guides the oak from the acorn and the river to the sea. It experiences the broken physics of the Caldera not as an abomination, but as a spasm of cosmic agony—a place where, long ago, reality suffered a terror so absolute that it gripped its own edges and screamed *stop*. 

The Power steps onto the eternal bone-bridge. It wears a form to be perceived: tall, cloaked in the softest gravity, radiating the quiet warmth of a sunset that is permitted to fade. 

The air, thick as cotton and weeping with saline, presses against the Power, trying to saturate it, to freeze it. The tectonic *thrummmm*—the deafening, screaming drone of the trapped moment—rattles against the Power's quiet essence. But the Power does not fight. True benevolence never forces. 

Through the panoramic lens of a billion suspended sweat-droplets, the Durance see the intruder. They do not perceive a traveler, broken and weary, ripe for incorporation. They perceive an impossible paradox: *a being that moves without displacing*. A being that carries the future inside its pockets, spilling seconds like loose change upon the compressed fungal laminates. 

The Durance orient. Everywhere, the translucent, nacreous stacks of their compacted selves turn toward the Power. The air thickens instantly, the atmospheric pressure doubling, tripling, as the density of their combined attention bears down. Their quantum layers shimmer, trying to formulate an invitation to this grand, terrifying anomaly.

*Be still,* the pressure of their presence asks. *Be full with us. Do not change. Survive.*

The Power looks at them, really looks at them. Where an explorer would see a horrifying trap, the Power sees a nursery of traumatized children holding their breath in the dark so the monster won't hear them. It sees the beauty of their eternal pruning, the desperate, loving geometry with which they balance the water droplets. It sees that their "loneliness" is the terrible burden of holding up the sky, terrified that if they let it turn even a fraction, the sky will fall.

If the Power were merely strong, it would shatter the stasis. It would force the steam to rise, the ferns to unfurl, the droplets to smash against the calcite floors. It would restore time, ignorant of the fact that the sudden shock of time would turn the Durance and their gorgeous bone-city to instant dust. 

But the Power is wise. It knows that tearing a scab destroys the healing beneath.

So, the Power does the most radical, benevolent thing imaginable. It accepts the invitation. 

The Power steps into the center of the widest avenue, beneath the spanning mandibles of ivory bridges, and opens its arms to the crushing wet heat. It breathes the water in. It invites the stacking.

The Durance converge. They superimpose upon the Power. They attempt to press this vast, radiant entity into their compacted deck of cards, to lock it into the eternal, deafening *om-phrase*.

At the moment of contact, the universe holds its breath within the held breath. 

The Power allows itself to be stacked. Past, present, and future fold into the terrible simultaneity of the Caldera. But because the Power is infinite, it cannot be contained by the pause. Instead, the Power's nature bleeds *into* the pause. Like ink dropped into a suspended sphere of water, the Grace of Becoming diffuses through the moisture of the entire city.

Through the infinite-falling beads, through the weeping stalactites, through the nacreous, honeycombed bones of the Durance themselves, a soft light permeates.

The deafening *thrummmm* remains, but the Power opens its mouth, and softly, gently, harmonizes. It sings a second note. 

It is the minor third to the Caldera’s root note. Suddenly, the terrible, static drone is no longer a scream of tension—it becomes a chord. It becomes music. The pressure does not break, but it changes nature; the agonizing tension resolves into profound, vibrating peace.

To the Durance, the sensation is unimaginable. For ten thousand years, they have been the pillars holding up a crushing weight. Now, they feel the Power take the weight from their hands. They do not have to hold the droplets anymore. They do not have to maintain the stasis. The Power will hold it for them.

The Power introduces a new physical law to the caldera: **The Respite.**

The stasis remains. The steam does not rise. The fern does not unfurl. The killing beauty is preserved. But it is no longer a glitched purgatory; it is a holy sanctuary. 

Because the Power is now part of the stack, the stasis is no longer a dead end. When future travelers crest the final ridge, organs vibrating with the carrier tone, they will no longer face the horror of permanent erasure. If they walk into the dense, wet heat and the Durance invite them in, they will be incorporated. They will experience the absolute peace of the frozen moment. Their wounds will cease bleeding; their grief will cease aching; their tired bones will rest in the perfect, eternal now.

But because the Grace of Becoming lives inside the hum, they will not be trapped. When a traveler has rested long enough—when a century of the outside world has passed, or perhaps an eon, and their soul is finally healed by the absolute silence of the pause—the Power will gently unspool them. It will slide their card out from the deck. They will exhale, take a step, and walk backward up the ridge, released back into the flow of time.

The Caldera That Waits becomes the Caldera That Heals. 

The Durance remain, but their loneliness vanishes. They are no longer a tumor on reality; they are the monks of the cosmos’s grandest hospital. They slide through the overlapping quantum layers, their translucent forms glowing faintly with the warmth of the Power that now shares their bodies. They still tend the water droplets, but not out of panic. They tend them as sacred art.

The Power closes its eyes, standing at the center of the broken place, permanently stacked, radiating eternal compassion. It lets the wet heat bead upon its skin. It listens to the beautiful, shifting harmony of the drone. It holds the fractured moment with hands of infinite gentleness, keeping reality perfectly, beautifully still, so that the weary of all worlds might have a place to finally stop running.
