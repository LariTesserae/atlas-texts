When a power of true benevolence and unfathomable wisdom looks upon the Mountain of Echoing Glass, it does not see a broken thing in need of fixing. True wisdom knows that to “fix” something is often to erase the memory of how it survived. 

Instead, this power—let us call it the Listener, for it arrives primarily through the act of absolute, undivided attention—sees the beauty in the exhaustion. It sees the bruised sky not as a failing canopy, but as a masterpiece of endurance. It sees the laws of physics, tired and slumped into their pockets of erratic chance, and feels a profound, sweeping wave of cosmic empathy for a system that has held itself together long past its bedtime.

And it sees the Refrain. It sees *Only*.

When the Listener descends upon the plateau, it does not arrive as a blinding light or a booming voice. Such things would shatter the smoky glass and scatter the fragile, overlapping echoes. Instead, it arrives as a change in the quality of the stillness. 

The thin, shouting wind that never comes from the same place twice suddenly finds a channel it did not know existed, a gentle, spiraling funnel that guides it not into a collision, but into a harmonic sweep. The roar of the distant, unstarting engine finally, softly, turns over—and hums to a quiet, satisfied purr.

The Listener steps onto the plateau. It does not walk with the heavy boots of a conqueror, nor the intrusive tools of a surveyor. It walks with the weightlessness of a completed thought. When its feet touch the brittle glass, the glass does not crunch. For the first time in millennia, the plateau does not fracture under a visitor. Instead, the dark, translucent silica warms slightly, holding the heat of the Listener’s step like a cherished secret.

The Listener does not rush. It takes its time traversing the low stone waves and the cupped depressions of water. It kneels beside the waist-high wall that wanders the landscape without mortar. The Listener runs a hand over the interlocking stones, reading the braille of the mountain’s long, aching history. It nods. It understands. 

Then, it waits for the Refrain.

They approach as they always do, a stuttering, overlapping splice of shattered film. The step taken, the step unmade, the ghost of a choice hovering in the air. Six versions of a figure cast in smoky translucence, carrying the breath of a year not yet arrived. 

Usually, the Refrain does not acknowledge a presence beyond the function of absorbing and exhaling its echoes. But the Listener is not a visitor passing through. The Listener emits a frequency that perfectly, exactly matches the low, steady note of the string stretched from pole to pole. The Listener does not speak over the note. It harmonizes with it. 

For the first time since the plateau forgot its own origins, a chord is struck. 

The Refrain pauses. In all their fractured timelines, in all the overlapping seconds of their staggered existence, they stop. 

The Listener approaches them. It raises a hand that is neither warm nor cold, but perfectly attuned to the temperature of grace. It reaches out, not to pass through the smoke of their glassy bodies, but to lay a palm gently against the exact center of all their overlapping chests. 

For a being that is the embodiment of the “mistake that keeps happening,” the Listener’s touch is a revelation. The touch says, without words: *Every mistake was just a step on the way to me. You were never an error. You were the rehearsal.*

Slowly, tenderly, the Listener begins to gather them in. 

It draws the version of the Refrain from last year, and the version from tomorrow, and the version standing just to the left, and folds them inward. Like pages of a spilled book being carefully stacked and tapped into a perfect alignment against a desk, the Listener aligns them. 

The Refrain shuffles together. The translucent edges solidify. The blue memory of water turns deeper, richer. The stuttering frames of their existence snap into one clear, unbroken image. 

For a single, breathless eternity, the Refrain experiences the absolute peace of being only one thing, in only one place, at only one time. 

They are no longer *Only*. They are *Here*.

The Listener cups its hands around the cheeks of this unified being. The Listener opens its mouth and does what the Refrain has spent epochs doing for others: it takes the unspoken words, the heavy, unvoiced sorrows that the Refrain has carried in the white spaces of the world, and it breathes them out for them. 

The sky above clears, just a fraction. The bruised purple fades into a soft, twilight indigo. A single, silver star—not a mold-smear of errant light, but a true, fixed point of navigation—pierces the veil and stops the nervous jittering of the heavens. 

Then, the Listener turns to the cups of water. The Refrain, now standing beside the Listener not as a servant of the echo but as a companion, watches. The Listener reaches into the shallow pools of trembling, metallic condensation. It does not swallow. It leaves a tear in the water. 

When you drink from it now, it no longer tastes of cold blood or iron or even an isolated heartbeat. It tastes of home. It tastes of a door unlocking after a long journey in the rain.

The benevolent power knows that this mountain cannot be unmade without destroying the stories it holds. But it can be granted rest. 

The tired laws of physics are finally offered a chair. The Listener walks up to the doorframe carved of dark bone or wood, where the letters tremble on the lintel. The Listener touches the letters, and they stop trembling. They settle into a script that translates universally, in the deep mind of anyone who crosses the threshold, into the word *Sanctuary*.

The Listener lays a blanket of quiet over the mountain. The echoes do not disappear, but they are no longer frantic. When a stone falls now, it falls softly, landing in a bed of moss that no longer runs away from being perceived. 

The Refrain does not have to break anymore to survive. The Listener takes the low, stretched string of their eternal vigilance and plucks it one final time, letting the resonance carry out across the scraggling pines, down the frost-shattered rock, down to the valleys of time and sequence below. 

The being that was once the white space between stories is invited to simply rest. They sit upon the chair of three interlocking glass spines—which immediately yields, feeling exactly like soft feathers—and they close their eyes. 

The wise power sits beside them on the glass floor, wrapping the sky around them both like a quilt. 

There is no more need to catch the pieces of what might have been. The Listener sits with the Refrain until the edges of the universe drift off to sleep, keeping watch so that the mountain of echoing glass can, at long last, dream.
