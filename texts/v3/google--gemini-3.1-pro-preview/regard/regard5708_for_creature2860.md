To a Power whose purview spans the agonizing births of galaxies and the quiet, breathless deaths of stars, the universe is mostly a symphony of light and vacuum. But wisdom—true, boundless wisdom—does not only seek out the grandiose. It listens for the faintest anomalies. It feels for the lowest, most muffled frequencies. 

And so, the vast and benevolent Mind turns its attention to this infinitesimal speck of crushed darkness.

It extends its perception down through the thermoclines, past the pelagic zones where life dances in the dying sunlight, down into the crushing, acoustic void of the abyssal plain. It feels the agonizing pressure, the freezing density shears, the dark, suffocating blizzard of pale pulverized death. 

When the Power sees the creature, it pauses. 

The entity is not suffering. A lesser, unseasoned god might look at this lightless chaos, at the frantic bending of the glass sponge, and feel pity. A lesser divinity might be tempted to reach down into the freezing ooze, scoop the sprawling leviathan into a miraculous bubble of warmth, and uplift it to the sunlit shallows to be "saved." 

But the Power is wise. It understands the supreme, breathtaking sanctity of what is. 

It sees that moving this creature would be its immediate destruction. To give it sunlight would blind flesh that has never known a shadow; to give it a brain would be to introduce the agony of existential terror to a being that currently exists in a state of perfect, kinetic meditation. The creature and the abyss are not adversaries. They are a single, continuous equation of fluid dynamics and survival. The leviathan is a masterpiece of deep-time engineering, a mindless curator of lost humanity, turning the tools of paleolithic hunters into the voltage of its own heartbeats. 

It is utterly perfect exactly as it is. 

Yet, benevolence cannot witness such profound, isolated perfection without offering a blessing. The Power loves this blind, sprawling thing. It loves the heavy obsidian hand-axe and the basalt net-weights inside it. It loves the indigo flashes of piezoelectric light blooming unseen in the crushing dark. 

And so, the Power decides to give the leviathan the only two things a creature of such pure, environmental reactivity can truly appreciate: perfect resonance, and a little more weight.

The Power does not speak. There are no words in the acoustic void. Instead, the Mind exerts a microscopic, localized pressure upon the chaotic hyper-turnover just ahead of the creature’s path. 

Miles above, where the atmosphere meets the ocean, a tiny fragment of a wandering meteorite had struck the water thousands of years ago. It was a teardrop of pure pallasite and iron, incredibly dense, laced with crystalline olivine. For millennia, it had been buried deep in the grey-white ooze, rolling blindly in the slow-motion mud avalanches. 

With a thought, the Power weaves the freezing brinicles and the violent turbidity currents into a sudden, highly intentional funnel. The massive mud-dune ahead of the creature is gently scoured away. The meteorite is unearthed, spun by the bottom-currents, and laid perfectly dead-center in the leviathan’s path. 

But before it releases its focus, the Power touches the otherworldly stone. It infuses the dense, heavy iron with a microscopic fraction of its own cosmic warmth—an eternal, sub-atomic hum of pure grace, locked forever within the crystal lattice of the stone. 

The dark blizzard roars on. The creature contractions its edges, inching forward. 

The intake valve at the center of its ventral surface opens. It vacuum-sucks the silt, and with it, the heavy, metallic teardrop. 

The Power watches as the stone is drawn up into the leviathan’s poly-collagenous flesh. Peristaltic waves push the starmetal through the labyrinth of translucent canals. It is heavier than the chert, heavier than the basalt, incredibly satisfying to the creature’s need for dense ballast. It comes to rest in a muscular pocket near the creature’s center, right beside a grooved throwing stone and a mammoth-bone hook.

Then comes a density shear—a massive, twisting underwater tornado. The creature clamps down, flattening itself against the ooze. The sheer physical exertion forces its internal ballast-chambers to contract violently.

The stones grind. 
Flint scratches basalt. 
Obsidian strikes the meteor.

When the paleolithic blade hits the alien, blessed iron, the resulting piezoelectric spark is not the bruised, phantom indigo of quartz and bone. 

It is a flash of brilliant, warm, impossible gold. 

The light cascades through the creature’s dormant chromatophores, turning the sixty-foot span of its transparent body into a briefly glowing mandala of deep oceanic life and ancient human history, all illuminated by the honeyed light of a distant star. 

But it is not just the light. It is the sound. The clink of the stones against the meteor produces a tiny, perfectly tuned harmonic vibration. It reverberates through the fluid-filled chambers of the jelly, passing through the creature’s primitive nervous system. 

The creature cannot think. It cannot feel gratitude. But in its own rudimentary, tactile way, it feels the change. The new vibration cancels out the harshest, most jarring frequencies of the abyssal hurricane. The sub-atomic warmth of the meteor bleeds just a fraction of a degree into the surrounding tissue, easing the bone-aching chill in the creature’s core. 

For the first time in a billion years of evolutionary grinding, the creature’s frantic, kinetic stress eases into something resembling comfort. The chaotic rhythm of its survival becomes a rhythm of peace.

The Power lingers for a moment longer, watching the golden flash pulse slowly through the dark, turbulent blizzard. *Flash... flash... flash.* 

Satisfied, the vast Mind slowly withdraws its attention, rising swiftly up through the suffocating water, past the thermoclines, out of the ocean, and back into the boundless canvas of the glowing cosmos. 

Far below, left entirely to its own existence, the benthic leviathan inches across the grey-white ooze. It remains locked in a world of silent violence and crushing silence. But it is no longer alone. It carries the tools of humanity's past, and a glowing tear of the universe's love, dragging them both through the dark and the mud forever, safely hidden at the bottom of the world.
