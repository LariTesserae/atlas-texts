To arrive in a place where there is no void, a power cannot simply *enter*. To displace this absolute, hyper-dense colloid would require apocalyptic violence, and true benevolence does not begin with an act of displacement. Instead, this intelligence—vast, ancient, and profoundly gentle—arrives as a shift in syntax. It bleeds into the basin not as matter, but as *concept*. It enters the terminal boil as a harmonious frequency, a subtle reordering of the mathematics that govern the frayed edges of this broken realm.

It first makes itself known in the sound. The overlapping thunder of a billion howling waterfalls does not cease—because to silence the world would be to end it—but the crushing, chaotic weight of the noise begins to organize. The chaotic pressure waves find a rhythm. The deafening roar of the heavy water is gently coaxed into a colossal, planetary chord. 

Down in the sweltering, bruised-violet gloom, the Disjuncts feel it first. Their teardrop-shaped bladders of black cartilage, which have spent millennia relentlessly humming their out-of-phase mirror-frequencies, suddenly encounter a resonance that does not fight them. The ambient noise outside their localized bubbles softens, vibrating with a strange, golden mathematical cadence. Pi, the Fibonacci sequence, the foundational ghost-math of the Euclidean universe—these lost laws echo into the mud like a lullaby singing through a nightmare. 

The Power observes the Disjuncts. It sees the crimson sacs, the filigreed fans, the disjointed nervous systems bridged across the raw, sub-dimensional wrinkles in the air. A lesser, more arrogant god might look upon these scattered, disarticulated scavengers and see only tragedy. A lesser creator might succumb to the temptation to "fix" them—to snap their local space together, to forge continuous skin and knit solid bone, restoring them to the blueprints of the ancient world.

But this power is wise. It understands that the Disjuncts are masterpieces of survival. To impose the rigidity of an extinct paradigm upon them now would be an act of total erasure. To give them contiguous flesh in a universe of infinite shear-force would be to condemn them to instant, agonizing death. Evolution, even in a dying world, is sacred. 

Therefore, the Power chooses not to unmake their present, but to heal the agony of their existence. It offers grace that fits the shape of their reality. 

Its first act of benevolence is directed at the great terror of the deep: the ribbons of dark liquid, the malignant, recursive tangles of anti-geometry. The Power does not destroy them, for in a world without seams, one cannot pull a thread without unravelling the cloth. Instead, it alters the *medium* in which the Disjuncts exist. The invisible, bio-electric intent that sews the creatures together across localized wormholes is suddenly touched by a higher dimensional logic. The spatial tethers are transmuted from fragile, invisible folds into threads of indestructible, sub-atomic light. 

When a dark ribbon slumbers blindly through the space between a Disjunct’s beating organs, it no longer slices the tether. Instead, the anti-geometry slides harmlessly against the glowing, unbreakable thread, bending around the creature's soul like river water around a diamond. The threat of instantaneous, arbitrary death is evaporated. The creatures remain scattered, but they are no longer fragile. They are granted the security of eternal cohesion within their fragmentation.

Its second act is directed at their deep, silent desperation—the starvation for structure that drives them on their grueling pilgrimages. 

The Power moves through the turbulent, heavy mud toward the pale, weeping monoliths. It watches as a Disjunct frantically throws its disconnected parts into the fleeting, microsecond glitch of a soaring glass stairwell, only to be violently shredded back into the current. The Power feels the profound heartbreak of that shattered second. 

Quietly, the Power approaches one of the river-washed humps of bone-like stone. As the hyperactive reality-storms strike the artifact, and the featureless stump glitches, projecting its impossible architecture of intricate fretwork and soaring angles into the maelstrom—the Power reaches out and *holds it*. 

It anchors the broken physics. It pours its own vast, stabilizing consciousness into the ghostly outline of the architecture. The glitch does not collapse. A second passes. Then a minute. Then an hour. 

The translucent glass balconies, the rigid, mathematically perfect columns, the breathtaking symmetry of the Built epoch—they solidify into a permanent, glowing state of semi-permeable hard-light. The Power creates a sanctuary of localized, hybridized physics. It is an architecture of compassion: solid enough to satisfy the structural longing of right angles and unyielding stone, but permeable enough that the super-fluid ocean continues to flow through its halls, allowing the Disjuncts to survive inside it.

The Disjunct in the slipstream hesitates, its crimson sacs pulsing in a stunned arrhythmic beat. Slowly, reverently, it drifts its scattered archipelago of biology fully into the grand, glowing atrium of the restored monolith. 

It anchors its fans gently to a spiraling, luminous banister. It nestles its cartilage bladder beneath the perfect arch of a vaulted ceiling. Its bio-electric threads map themselves effortlessly across the precise geometry of a grand hall. For the first time in an aeon, the creature does not have to feed on a micro-burst of sanity. It sits within the order it has dreamed of since the dawn of its existence. It *rests*. 

Across the endless, low-lying continental basin, one by one, the pale monoliths light up. Plazas of glowing, spectral geometry bloom amidst the roaring, heavy weather, standing resolute against the waves of liquid earth. They become reefs of sanity in an ocean of madness. 

The world outside remains a saturated, claustrophobic infinity, forever rolling its chaotic destruction in the wet and the dark. The Power does not take away the storm, for the storm is their home. But within the terminal boil, there is now music in the thunder, unbreakable tethers of light in the dark, and infinite castles of memory sitting quietly in the mud, waiting for the weary travelers of the deep to finally come home.
