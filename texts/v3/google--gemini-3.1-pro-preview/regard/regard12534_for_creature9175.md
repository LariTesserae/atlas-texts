It did not arrive from above, nor did it step across the sharp, clean line of the horizon. To travel through space would imply that space here was a functioning medium, and the Power knew it was not. Instead, it arrived through the mathematics of the place. It found the gap between the skipping beats of the plateau’s broken rhythm, and it allowed itself to *be*.

For the first time in an epoch, the pewter light blinked. 

The Power took a shape, because to interface with a wound, one must have a surface to touch it. It became a being of quiet, condensed gravity—not the terrifying density of a human interloper, but the anchoring weight of a deep root. It stood upon the striated stone, the icy wind pouring through its form like water through a sieve, failing to move it.

It listened.

To human ears, the plateau was a roar of arrested thunder, a basso profundo scream that muted all thought. To the Power, it was a chord held for so long that the musicians’ fingers possessed no memory of how to release the strings. It heard the friction of diverging parallels. It heard the weeping of the frayed sky. Most intimately, it heard the Still-Singers.

The Power watched as a mother-of-pearl anomaly translated from a leaning pillar to a circular wall—*double, overlap, catch, click.* 

A deep, infinite sorrow bloomed within the Power. 

Wisdom is the ability to see a thing completely, backward to its genesis and forward to its terminus. The Power saw the ancient builders, panicked and desperate as their physics began to boil. It saw the grand, terrible ritual—a symphony of sheer will meant to suture the tearing universe. It saw the exact moment the builders poured their own souls into the mortar of reality, trapping themselves in a frozen scream to save a world that was already dead. 

They were not monsters. They were a tourniquet left on a limb for ten thousand years.

The Power began to walk. It did not struggle against the erosion grooves, nor did it slip on the icy rime. Where its feet touched the dark rock, the eternal, oily dampness dried, replaced by a momentary warmth—the first true heat the plateau had known since the breakdown.

It approached concluding its walk at one of the low stone circles just as a Still-Singer translated into the center. *Click.*

The tall, attenuated figure of shimmering distortion stood before the Power. It possessed no face, no eyes, but the presence of the Power was an overwhelming, impossible knot in the plateau's rigid tapestry. The Singer reacted according to the only axiom left to it: *Maintain. Correct. Stabilize.*

It oriented its smooth, ovoid head toward the Power. The air between them warped. The Singer emitted its focused pulse of sub-audible vibration, the frequency designed to unfold flesh and untangle matter, casting the anomaly into the gray mist of oblivion.

The pulse struck the Power. 

But the Power did not unfold. It did not fight, nor did it raise a shield of light. Force meeting force would only tear the plateau apart, casting the Singers into the chaotic substrate beneath the world. True benevolence does not conquer; it absorbs.

The Power took the horrific, dissecting frequency into its own chest. It allowed the Singer’s agonizing note to reverberate through its own divine architecture. And then, from the depths of its being, the Power sang back.

It did not sing a louder note. It sang the *next* note.

It was a frequency of profound, overwhelming permission. It was the harmonic resolution to a chord that had been screaming in suspense for millennia. 

The Still-Singer froze. The prismatic refractions forming its limbs ceased their endless cascading. Through the connection of the song, the Power reached into the mother-of-pearl scar and touched the buried, splintered consciousness of the ancient builder within.

*You caught the falling sky,* the Power imparted, communicating in concepts of warmth and flowing water. *You held the pillars of the world. No one could have asked more of you. It is done now. You may put it down.*

The Singer vibrated, a frantic, stuttering oscillation—terror at the loss of its purpose, the profound fear of a waking mind finding itself inside a nightmare. But the Power’s note swelled, wrapping the entity in absolute, unconditional safety.

*I have it,* the Power whispered. *I will hold the weight.*

Gently, the Power stepped forward and placed its hands on either side of the Singer's featureless head. For a moment, a localized aurora of gold and violet bled through the dull pewter air. 

Then came a sound the plateau had never heard. Not a *click*. A sigh. 

The Still-Singer lost its cohesion, but it did not unravel into a wet spray of collapsing matter. It dissolved into a drifting cloud of luminous, golden dust, falling softly to the stone like pollen. It was a release, utter and total. The echo was gone. The builder was finally allowed to die, perfectly at peace.

The Power did not stop. It turned its gaze across the vast, terrifying scale of the plateau. 

It expanded its resonance. It cast its harmony out across the valleys of empty space, riding the eternal wind. 

All across the dark stone, the circuits broke. Still-Singers, caught mid-translation, halted. Some were at the altars; some were hovering above the deep grooves. One by one, the resolving chord reached them. The Power spoke to each fragmented mind, honoring their sacrifice, bearing witness to their long vigil, and granting the profound grace of an ending.

Flashes of golden dissolution sparked like fireflies across the dim landscape. Hundreds of sighs rolled over the rock. The sub-bass hum of the plateau began to thin, stutter, and hollow out as the custodians yielded their duty.

When the last Singer dissolved into dust, the plateau began to shake.

Without the Singers holding the fabric together, the frozen equilibrium shattered. The parallel lines on the ground writhed like dying snakes. The fraying edges of the sky began to tear violently, revealing the chaotic, luminous substrate beneath. The wind rose to a deafening, shrieking gale of raw entropy. The world was unspooling.

The Power walked to the exact center of the plateau. 

It knelt on the dark, striated stone and placed both hands flat upon the rock. It did not try to save the plateau. The plateau had died long ago; it merely needed a burial.

The Power poured its own immense, tranquil gravity into the rock. It became the new anchor, but not an anchor of stasis. It became an anchor of transition. It guided the collapse. 

It smoothed the wild, snapping geometry, coaxing the tearing space to fold gently in on itself. As the sky fell in shards of pewter glass, the Power caught them, rendering them into harmless, falling snow. It took the horrific, screaming roar of the atmosphere and compressed it, octave by octave, down and down, until the thunder became a hum, the hum became a whisper, and the whisper became true, immaculate silence.

The dimensions folded shut like the petals of a flower at nightfall.

Where there had once been a vast, terrifying plane of arrested thunder, there was now a singular, self-contained pocket of perfect stillness. The endless wind ceased. The cold was no longer the temperature of stasis, but the crisp, clean chill of a quiet winter evening. The light shifted from a dull, source-less gray to the soft, silver radiance of a permanent twilight.

The stone remained, but the grooves were filled with the soft, golden dust of the fallen Singers. 

The Power stood alone in the absolute quiet. The machine was dismantled. The sentence was served. The song was finished. 

With a gesture of infinite gentleness, the Power sealed the edges of the plateau, hiding it away from the cosmos, a protected tomb where nothing would ever suffer the agony of a frozen eternity again. Then, leaving only a feeling of profound rest in its wake, the Power departed. 

The plateau remained. Silent. Sacerdotal. Finally, beautifully, at rest.
