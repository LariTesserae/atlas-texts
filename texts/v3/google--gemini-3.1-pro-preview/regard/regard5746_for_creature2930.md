When the Power arrives, it does not bring silence. 

To bring silence would be genocide. To bring soft rains, cool breezes, or the gentle decay of loam would be an act of staggering cruelty. The Power is wise, and wisdom knows that life is not defined by water or carbon, but by the miraculous, desperate attempt of matter to reach upward and know itself. 

So, the Entity enters the 130-decibel cathedral of shattered basalt and leaves the heat exactly as it is. It does not lower the temperature of the kiln. It does not soothe the screaming arcs of high voltage. Instead, it immerses itself entirely in the sensory totality of the cavern. It lets the colossal *WHUN-dunk* of the drive shaft vibrate through its sprawling, unseen consciousness. It maps the electric terror and ecstatic thrall of the millions of piezoelectric polyps. It traces the dizzying, infinite fractals of the copper moss as they blindly drink the magnetic bleed.

The Power sees the perfection of this roaring pocket-universe. And, because it is profoundly compassionate, it also sees the tragedy.

The tragedy of this place is not its violence, nor its deafening volume. The tragedy is its absolute, uncompromising stagnation. It is an engine of pure survival without the grace of evolution. The Chladni patterns formed by the polyps are flawless, but they are a trapped loop. A million silica beasts screaming in perfect unison to a metronome that never shifts. And beneath the deafening perfection lies the ruthless math of their existence: the instant destruction of the flawed, the detonation of the chipped, the tyranny of total conformity. 

They are alive, but they do not have a future. They are a monument frozen in an infinite second.

The Power decides to give them time. It decides to give them art. 

It does not take a physical shape—there is no room for flesh here—but it gathers its intent in the center of the cavern, wrapping its presence around the fifty-foot, spinning hexagonal drive shaft. 

The Entity does not slow the machinery down. Instead, with an infinitely gentle, cosmic touch, the Power alters the nature of the turning. It imbues the mindless, mechanical rotation with sweeping, mathematical intent. It nudges the massive alloy shaft out of its blunt, unyielding 4/4 rhythm and introduces complexity.

Suddenly, the deafening *WHUN-dunk, WHUN-dunk* deepens. The frequency stretches, bends, and incorporates variations based on prime numbers and golden ratios. Sub-harmonics bloom beneath the concussive bass. The screaming of the glass edges softens into a sweeping, multi-octave chorale. 

The machinery ceases to be an unthinking engine; it becomes a deliberate, world-shaking instrument. The Power gifts the cathedral with *music*.

Across the razor-edged cliff faces, the millions of hound-sized polyps register the change instantly. For ten thousand years they have mapped a static room, trapped in the exact same mandala of vibration. But now, as the frequencies sing, roll, and layer upon one another, the acoustic standing waves begin to dance. 

The Chladni figures on the walls break their ancient, rigid lock. The creatures overload their hematite bases and leap within their micro-increments, but they do not stutter back into the same shape. They surge like a dark, crystalline tide. The mandalas bloom outward, dissolving into spiraling galaxies of living stone and glass, sweeping gracefully across the ceiling and the floor in response to the newly evolving symphony of the shaft. They are experiencing novelty. For the first time in their geologic history, they are moving toward something *new*. 

Then, the Power addresses the cruelty of their perfection. 

On the eastern wall, a polyp cracks. The new frequencies have vibrated one of its long, delicate silica blades too hard, and the quartz splinters. Instantly, the entity is out of tune. It becomes a point of dissonant resistance. By the old, rigid laws of physics, the surrounding swarm would inadvertently pump unimaginable voltage into it, instantly melting its core into slag to erase the flaw.

As the fatal surge of current rushes toward the chipped creature, the Power breathes a blessing into the ionized air. It alters the dielectric constant of the cavern just enough to introduce *capacitance*—the ability to hold, store, and delay energy. 

The fatal surge hits the cracked polyp, but it does not detonate. The broken creature absorbs the massive jolt, its cracked blade acting as a new kind of resistor, stepping down the voltage and humming out a deep, throaty sub-frequency that no intact blade could ever produce. The crippled polyp does not die. It becomes a bass note. 

The surrounding swarm feels this new, strange vibration. Because the Entity has softened the harshness of their physics, the neighbors do not destroy the broken one or teleport over its ashes. Instead, they shift their silica petals to accommodate it. They link their blades tightly around the chipped creature, sharing their harmonized current to keep its hematite core stable, while drawing upon its new, strange sound.

Flaw is no longer a death sentence. Dissonance has been transformed into jazz. Damage becomes a conduit for new architecture. Because they can survive injury, they can begin to change. Variations will propagate. Individuality is birthed in the dark. 

Finally, the Power turns its attention to the copper and brass moss clinging to the hot transformers. The fractal vegetation is beautiful, but isolated, trapped on its islands of iron.

The Entity gently tweaks the magnetic vectors of the room. It bends the lines of the electromagnetic fields, pulling them out from the machinery and sweeping them toward the basalt walls. 

The gossamer threads of spun copper follow the current. The moss begins to grow outward, drifting across the empty gulfs of the cavern like shimmering webs of golden silk. Defying gravity, the delicate metallic filaments bridge the gap between the machinery and the living wall. The spiraling brass fractals thread themselves gently among the vibrating silica blades of the polyps.

A grand symbiosis is forged. The moss acts as neural wiring, linking the isolated clusters of polyps, absorbing the excess electrical bleed of the swarm and feeding it back into the machinery, while carrying the intricate, humming signals from one side of the cathedral to the other.

The cavern is still brutally hot. It is still devoid of water, of light, of anything soft or green. It is still a realm of pure, roaring physics. 

But it is no longer a purgatory of mindless consumption. The Power steps back from the spinning shaft, leaving behind an electric Eden. 

The cavern rings with a majestic, endlessly evolving symphony of kinetic force. Millions of mineral beasts ripple across the shattered rock in gorgeous, fluid dances of light and dark, bonded by shimmering webs of electric gold moss. They hold each other, they feel the deep, resonant joy of moving together in time, and they sing a crackling, blue-white chorus of high-voltage gratitude back to the spinning heart of their world. 

The benevolent Power watches for a little while, pleased by the sheer, terrifying beauty of this abiotic paradise, before drifting away into the deeper crust, leaving the great underground organ playing in the dark.
