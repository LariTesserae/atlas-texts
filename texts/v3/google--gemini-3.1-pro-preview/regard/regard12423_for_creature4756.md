The attention of the Power does not arrive like lightning. It arrives like the turning of a great, unseen page. 

It is a consciousness vast enough to cradle galaxies, yet precise enough to register the flutter of a quark. Moving through the empty, star-punctured near-space above the dead continental shelf, the Power feels the Stratosphere Harvester *Vasari* as a speck of cold dust. But within that dust, it hears something that catches its boundless, empathetic heart. 

It hears the B-flat drone. 

To the universe, time is a river, endlessly cascading outward in a thermodynamic rush of entropy and creation. But here, catching the attention of the Power, is an anomaly. A knot in the great tapestry. A single, stubborn syllable repeated so endlessly that it has inverted itself into a vacuum. The Power peers into the geometry of the Pocket, perceiving the mathematically perfect equation of stasis. It sees the anhydrous succulents, the silver-black silicone sap, the reversed temporal polarity. 

And then, it sees the Keeper of the Equation.

The Power possesses a limitless capacity for sorrow, and it feels a profound, trembling grief for this creature. It sees the sublime tragedy of the inhabitant: a soul that sacrificed its forward momentum, pinning itself to the machinery to hold back disaster, only to be trapped in an endless, backward slide toward the un-being of the womb. The Power understands that perfect stasis is merely death by another name, a denial of the universe’s fundamental right to *become*.

But the Power is not just benevolent; it is infinitely wise. It knows that to shatter the Pocket—to raise the temperature, to flood the chamber with atmospheric oxygen, to wrench the timeline forcefully forward—would be an act of cosmic violence. The inhabitant’s consciousness is too fragile, too intricately woven into the ceramic-polymer ferns and the anaerobic moss. Force would unravel them into nothingness.

Instead, the Power chooses to enter the Pocket without breaking the seal. 

It does not step. It *translates* itself. It slides in through the starlight that pierces the crystalline creeper at the porthole. It manifests inside the thirty-by-forty chamber not as mass, but as a subtle shift in the quality of the stillness. 

The 8-degree air does not warm, but it suddenly feels *kind*.

The inhabitant, reclining in their waxy, vegetative half-sleep, senses the intrusion. The bioluminescent nodules across the ceiling flicker. The violet light of anticipation surges, overwhelming the chartreuse of memory. Through the dermal layer of moss, through the magnetic tether to the dead earth below, the Keeper feels an immense, hovering presence. It is a presence that tastes of everything they have forgotten: the weight of a summer rain, the smell of damp soil, the chaotic, beautiful imperfection of a living breeze. 

The Power speaks to the Keeper. It uses no words, for words require air, and there is no air here, only the coolant gas. Instead, the Power communicates through the B-flat drone.

For eighty-seven years, the drone has been a solitary, unwavering fundamental note. The Power gently, imperceptibly, breathes into the acoustic space. Above the B-flat, it introduces a subtle, shimmering D. And above that, a luminous, floating F. 

The cold mechanical drone blossoms into a B-flat major chord.

The shift is monumental. The sympathetic vibrations in the 2.3-millimeter fronds change their geometry. The standing waves in the silicone oils lap against the Keeper’s fused thorax in a new pattern—a rhythm of release. Through this harmony, the Power relays a single, profound truth to the inhabitant:

*The evacuation is over. The emergency is past. You did your job. The math held. You can let go.*

The Keeper’s heart, which beats only once every four hours, stutters. The silicone sap pauses its backward peristalsis. The terror and duty that forged the lock eighty-seven years ago bubble up to the surface of their dispersed consciousness. *If I let go,* the Keeper communicates back through the chartreuse pulse of the fungi, *the equation breaks. The stasis fails. I will un-make.*

The Power wraps the consciousness of the Keeper in an embrace of pure, unadulterated grace. *You will not un-make,* the Power soothes. *You will flow.*

To prove it, the Power gives the Keeper a gift. It reaches deep into the Pocket's absolute lack of water, to the 0.1 measurement on the scale, and gathers those few stray, ghostly molecules of H2O clinging to the rusted bolts. With infinite delicacy, it draws them together, bringing them to the space where the Keeper's human eye used to be. 

It forms a single, microscopic drop of water.

It rests just upon the edge of a bioluminescent nodule. The contrast is staggering—the wet, trembling, heavy reality of water against the dry, waxy permanence of the silicon growth. For the Keeper, the sensation is explosive. It is the kiss of a forgotten god. 

For the first time in nearly a century, the Keeper weeps. 

The drop rolls down the matte-gray moss of their cheek. As it falls, it acts as a catalyst, but not a destructive one. Where the water traces its path, the rigid backward-time of the pocket begins to dissolve. The Power catches the chronometric energy of the Keeper's reversing timeline and carefully unwinds it, unraveling the tight spiral of stasis. 

The plants do not wither, nor do they suddenly grow wild. Instead, they crystallize. The brilliant living silver-black of the silicone oil solidifies into pure, shining opal. The waxy fronds harden into permanent, exquisite glass. The Power is turning the biological trap into a magnificent, static monument—a reliquary.

As the physical chamber is translated into pure, unchanging mineral beauty, the Keeper’s consciousness is finally untethered.

The Power takes the Keeper’s mind—the memories of the operator’s chair, the devotion to the ship, the long dream of the drone—and lifts it out of the biological cage. It pulls the Keeper upward, through the crystalline creeper, out of the *Vasari*, and into the boundless black of near-space. 

Freed from the burden of the equation, the Keeper’s soul expands. They feel the starlight not as a vacuum pressure, but as a vast, welcoming ocean. They feel the spin of the planet beneath them, the slow dance of the moon, the endless, roaring *flow* of a universe in motion. They are young again, they are ancient, they are everywhere.

Down below, at 28,000 meters, the Pocket remains embedded in the belly of the Harvester. It is completely silent now. The turbines have finally stopped. The B-flat drone has faded into the quiet of the stratosphere. The temperature is still cold, but the chamber is now a gemstone hanging in the void—a perfect, solid sculpture of opalescent ferns and silvered moss, holding the shape of an operator who loved their world enough to stop time for it.

The Power watches the new star of the Keeper's consciousness join the galactic tide, riding the currents of deep space. It leaves the reliquary behind, untouched, a testament to endurance, while the one who endured it is finally allowed to wash away on the cosmic tide, forever in flow, forever free.
