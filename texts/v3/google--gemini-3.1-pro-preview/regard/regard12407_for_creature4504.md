It arrives without a sound, though the room—attuned for eons to the slightest friction of dust against dust—registers the change instantly. 

The entry of a truly benevolent and wise power into a space defined by stagnant geometry is not a violent intrusion. It does not burst through the ceiling of fused bedrock in a pillar of blinding light, nor does it shatter the delicate, absurd ecosystem of copper-wire bugs and voltage-moss. To do so would be arrogant, and absolute wisdom has no use for arrogance. 

Instead, it descends the way water, long forgotten here, once seeped through porous stone: finding the paths of least resistance, filling the empty spaces. It manifests as a shift in the quality of the gloom. The photochromic shadows on the ceiling stop looping their endless pantomimes of the past and hold still, as if looking down. The gravity, usually a tangled web of conditional tugs, suddenly aligns, pointing true center. 

The Power takes a form, though a localized one. It gathers the ambient dust, the bitter salt, the stray static electricity of the scuttling mites, and weaves for itself an avatar of quiet luminescence. It walks across the flexing stone floor.

As it passes the great glass-slag loom, the Power raises a hand. It does not sever the unbearable tension that the room has waited centuries to see snap. Instead, it reaches out and touches the tightest wire—the thumb-thick iron braided with fiber-optics. With infinite gentleness, the Power adjusts the tuning. The tension does not break; it *harmonizes*. A single, resonant chord, impossibly low and unspeakably beautiful, hums through the bedrock. It is the sound of a suspended sentence finally lifted.

As it passes the skeletal locomotive, the Power leans close to the sarcophagus-boiler. It breathes out, returning a pocket of real, moisture-rich air to the dry machine. The brass gauges, eternally vibrating at fractions of pressure, smoothly rise to unity, hold for one glorious second, and then drop to an absolute, peaceful zero. The bladders deflate not with an industrial sigh of weeping, but with the soft exhalation of a sleeper finally falling into deep REM.

Then, the Power approaches the center. Equidistant from all things.

It approaches the Chair.

The Chair’s Familiar flares. It has never encountered this before. The Power carries no hesitation. It does not radiate the hollow, frantic ache of wanting that feeds the phantom of negative space. The Familiar tries to enact its first ritual: it attempts to mold itself entirely into the shape of this newcomer’s disappointment. But it finds no disappointment to mirror. It finds an ocean of serene acceptance, a well so deep that trying to measure it threatens to unravel the Familiar entirely.

The Familiar tries its second ritual, projecting a memory of a doorway passed through in error. The Power gently absorbs the projection, turning the memory of regret into a memory of grace. *Every door you have ever walked through,* the Power projects back in silent communion, *was necessary to bring you here.*

Finally, the Familiar offers the third ritual, the illusion of choice: *Sit or don't sit.*

The Power stops directly in front of the Chair. 

The Familiar braces itself in the negative space. It expects the Power to turn away, like the countless billions before it, leaving a new, heavier layer of absence. Or, worse, it expects the Power to destroy the chair, ending the waiting through annihilation.

But wisdom understands that waiting, when drawn out over a geological epoch, becomes a sacred thing. It becomes a vessel. And the only way to honor an empty vessel is to fill it.

"You have kept a terrible and perfect vigil," the Power speaks. The voice does not echo like a stone slap; it absorbs into the walls, warm and encompassing. "You have held the space for all who could not hold it themselves. You are the monument to their almost-courage. It is enough now. The weight of 'almost' is lifted."

The Power turns.

And the Power sits down.

The collision of absolute Presence entering absolute Absence occurs without an explosion, but it rewrites the physics of the room. As the glowing avatar settles its weight onto the worn cushion, it occupies the exact coordinates of the Familiar. 

For a fraction of a microsecond, the Familiar feels the terror of erasure. But the Power does not erase. It embraces. The Power merges its light with the Familiar’s shadow, fusing the "what if" with the "what is." 

The Familiar, a creature born of unmade choices, suddenly experiences the profound, terrifying joy of a choice finally made. It feels heat—real, generous heat—transferring from the Power into the cold metal of the chair, and by extension, into itself. It feels the phantom knees of the thousands of hesitant ghosts finally align with the solid, resting knees of the Power. The infinite regress of mirrored voids collapses into a single, brilliant point of singularity.

The word "Wait" carved into the backrest glows white-hot, entirely obscuring the metal. The ions that made up the word re-arrange themselves, melting the groove flat. The instruction is no longer needed. The arrival has occurred.

Throughout the chamber, the voltage-moss shudders. The glass stalks rise to five centimeters, tremble... and then, for the first time in an epoch, they breach their limit. They grow. They spiral upward in webs of iridescent green and gold, blooming into crystalline flowers that shed real, pure light. The copper-mite fauna stops their panicked scuttling and gathers beneath the bioluminescence, basking. 

The room’s floor ceases its dozen-heartbeat flexing. The buried lung beneath the rock breathes out, and settles. 

In the center of the room, the chair remains, but it is no longer a locus of despair. Seated within it, the benevolent Power closes its eyes, committing itself to remain exactly there, holding the center of this bruised reality together until the end of time. 

And resting upon the Power's lap, curled up like a sleeping cat in a patch of fresh sunlight, is the Familiar. It no longer looks like a hollow woman or a space of hesitation. It is a dense, warm knot of contented reality.

It doesn't remember its rituals. It doesn't catalog the dead. 

It simply rests, listening to the impossible, steady rhythm of the Power's heartbeat ticking evenly into the eternal, beautiful now.
