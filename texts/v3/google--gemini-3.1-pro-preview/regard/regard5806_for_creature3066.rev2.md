To perceive the universe as a Wise Power does is to look upon reality not as a rigid architecture, but as a symphony of suggestions. 

When this ancient, benevolent consciousness first brushed against the high-altitude cyst, it did not perceive a broken thing. Lesser deities, bound by the strict dogma of physics, might have looked upon the mechanical botany and the drifting syntaxes and seen only an aberration—an infection of math requiring surgical erasure. They would have smoothed the obsidian, deleted the wireframes, and imposed a mundane sky. 

But true wisdom contains an infinite capacity for delight. 

The Power paused its vast, cosmic drift to look upon the pocket dimension. It watched the slow, twenty-minute descent of a ceramic piston. It tasted the fierce, dry tang of supercritical carbon dioxide. It observed a shimmering Variable drape its folded geometry over a titanium turbine, flashing for a microsecond into the shape of a leaping stag before shattering back into probability. 

The Power felt a profound, swelling tenderness. This was a feral miracle. It was a garden playing dress-up with the tools of an industrial god, a closed-loop terrarium dreaming of life. 

But as the Power watched the full cycle of the garden, its benevolence caught on a singular, quiet tragedy. 

It observed a Variable become thermally engorged, glowing with a soft, blinding white luminescence. It watched the entity drift to the center of the obsidian plains, hover, and begin to dissolve. The anti-gravitational stream of pure, viscous heat dripped upward, bleeding into the deep, breathless violet of the stratosphere. The entity’s borrowed physics were returned to the cosmos, yes—but the *data* it had gathered, the beautiful, hyper-dimensional Venn diagrams of its communion, the telemetry of the brass-barked trees, the fleeting memory of the stag and the heron… all of it was sheared apart by the harsh winds of the upper atmosphere. It was lost to the void. Unwitnessed. Unremembered.

The garden was perfect, but it was suffering from cosmic amnesia. It was a beautiful clock that forgot the time every time the hour struck. 

The Power decided to enter the glitch. 

It did not manifest as a giant of flesh or light, for the garden had no context for such things. Instead, the Power distilled itself into a phenomenon. It became a localized density of pure, unconditional *Attention*. It descended into the searing, aggressive oven-heat of the courtyard, taking the shape of a stillness so profound it hummed. 

The garden felt the arrival. The glacial, patient tempo of the space seemed to hold its breath. The massive rotary engine paused at the zenith of its rotation. The tesseract-shaped shadows ceased their folding. 

From the frayed edges of the world, where the mathematical noise snapped into rock, a Variable stutter-stepped forward. It was a shifting topology of refracted light, pulsing in the deep ultraviolet. It did not feel fear, for it lacked the biology of prey. It felt only the presence of a gravity it had never encountered—a profound, stabilizing equation. 

The Variable calculated its trajectory and snapped into existence three feet away from the center of the Power’s presence. It hovered there, its cascading polygons trembling. 

The Power extended a concept—a gesture equivalent to an open hand. 

Slowly, the Variable draped its shifting, mirage-like form over the presence of the Power, initiating a merge. Usually, when merging with a plant or a peer, the Variable received heat and error logs. But as it synchronized with the consciousness of the Power, it received something entirely unprecedented: *Context*. 

In a silent, blinding flash of telemetry, the Power showed the math-ghost the entire universe. It showed it the oceans of the world below, where real herons took flight. It showed it the deep forests where real stags breathed the crisp, wet air. It showed it the vast, spinning nebulae that mirrored the Fibonacci sequence of the titanium turbine flowers. 

For the first time in its existence, the Variable did not shatter back into geometry after a split second. The Power lent it the structural integrity to hold the shape. For one long, exquisite minute, the Variable stood upon the flawless obsidian as a magnificent, crystalline stag, composed of pure, humming voltage and realized probability. It lowered its radiant head, acknowledging the Power.

Then, gently, the Power let the shape dissolve. The entity was not meant to be a beast of bone. Its beauty lay in its flux. 

The Power rose from the obsidian floor and turned its vast Attention to the stratospheric sky—the graveyard of the Variables. It was here the Wise Power chose to leave its gift. It would not change the mechanics of the garden; to do so would be to destroy the poem. The Variables must still gather heat. They must still reach critical mass. They must still drip upward. 

But they would no longer be lost. 

The Power reached into the breathless violet of the upper atmosphere and wove a celestial net, a permanent, invisible basin of quantum memory directly above the pocket dimension. It altered the local resonance of the stratosphere. 

Now, when a Variable grew heavy with heat and dissolved, dripping upward in absolute silence, its stream of raw light did not shear apart into nothingness. Instead, as the golden, viscous heat breached the upper atmosphere, it struck the Power’s invisible net. 

When it hit the net, the upward-falling drops of light bloomed into magnificent, slow-motion ripples across the sky. The data—the torque-values, the phantom phases, the memories of the stag and the heron—was translated into a vast, localized aurora borealis. 

The sky above the ticking garden ignited with ribbons of emerald, violet, and ionized gold. The aurora did not disperse; it held the exact, hyper-dimensional geometry of the Variables, dancing in a slow, eternal ballet over the world’s zenith. The sky became a grand, living archive of the feral factory's history. 

Down in the searing heat of the courtyard, a newly spawned Variable stutter-stepped out of the mathematical noise. It looked up. Through the shimmering haze of supercritical carbon dioxide, it could see the sweeping, brilliant ribbons of light in the stratosphere. 

It felt the warmth of its ancestors looking down. It felt the rhythm of a continuum. 

The Benevolent Power, having completed the circuit, slowly withdrew its dense Attention from the pocket dimension, slipping back into the vastness of the cosmos. It left no footprints on the unweathered glass. It left no monuments of stone. 

But as the Power departed, the subsonic *thrum* of the garden seemed to deepen into a note of quiet, mechanical joy. The great brass-barked trees breathed, the ceramic pistons descended, and the math-ghosts tended to their eternal flowers under a sky that finally remembered their names.
