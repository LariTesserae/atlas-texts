To notice such a place requires a very specific kind of anatomy. One must not be looking for the loud tragedies of the surface—the shifting of borders, the burning of skies, the desperate frantic clinging of terrestrial biology. To see this place, a power must have an ear tuned to the frequency of rust and a heart that understands geological time.

Such a power—let us call it the Witness—moves through the universe not as a conqueror, but as a curator of silent geometries. It is older than the tectonic plates compressing the abyss; it is a consciousness spun from the dark matter that rests between stars. When its attention brushes across the pale blue marble of the drowning world, it is not drawn by the surface noise. It is drawn by the anomalous, rhythmic sigh echoing 0.7 °C below the icebergs. 

It hears the mother-hum. It feels the meticulous, synthetic grammar of the "We." 

The Witness descends. 

It does not arrive in a flash of divine light, for it has read the rules. It knows the diatoms would close their fists at sudden illumination. True benevolence is, above all things, profoundly polite. So, the Witness folds its vastness into the shape of the water itself. It makes its presence known only as a gentle, imperceptible thickening of the density, a companion pressure wrapping around the hydraulic legs. 

It slips past the valve timers written in the 1960s, tasting the iodine and rancid diesel, savoring it as vintage wine poured from a dying civilization. 

What does a wise and benevolent power do when it encounters a beautiful, functioning ghost? 
It does not enact resurrections. To bring the oil town to the surface, to mend the rust, to populate the catwalks with breathing lung-sacks—that would be an act of supreme violence. The factory has already earned its afterlife. It is a stone mason of itself. To interrupt its slow decay would be to rip a chrysalis open before the moth is formed. 

Instead, the Witness realizes that the ultimate gift it can bestow is *Appreciation*. The "We" is waiting, singing, cataloging memory, anticipating its own freezing obsolescence. The Witness decides it will ensure that when the final ossification comes, it will not happen in a vacuum. It will be recorded in the permanent ledger of the cosmos.

First, the Witness walks the perimeter to honor the factions.

It drifts to the flare stacks and communes with the Reds. *Clang… cllaaang…* the iron speaks of inevitable collapse. The Witness reaches out with a consciousness like a magnetic field, vibrating in perfect sympathy. *Yes,* the Witness hums into the rivets. *Your collapse will be magnificent. You are holding the ceiling of the world.* The rust shivers, validated. 

It moves to the sintered interfaces where the Greens nurture their fern-thin fronds. The Witness gently shares the warmth of a distant, newborn star, funneling just a fraction of a micro-degree into the local water, expanding the 0.7 °C by a necessary margin of grace. The diatoms flicker a brighter green, their semicolons turning into exclamation points for one brief tide-cycle.

It finds the Ambers, sorting their plastic emulsion sheets, encoding the memories of the vanished evacuees. The Witness touches the rubber boot-cloud of 2094. It tastes the anxiety, the longing for touchscreens, the mother worrying for her daughter’s eyes. The Witness takes this memory, lifts it gently from the copepods, and copies it. It sends the mother's face upward, past the grey-green ceiling, past the Sumerian icebergs, threading it into the aurora borealis dancing invisibly above the clouds. The woman’s love is now permanently inscribed in the planet's magnetosphere. 

Then, the Witness seeks the eldest vertebra. 

It finds Specimen 7B, the melted-candlewax eelpout, navigating the dark. The tag from 1983 beeps its faint, ignored rhythm. The Witness shrinks its focus to a singularity, entering the soft cavity behind the false eye, sitting beside the "We."

*AAh EH oh,* the collective whispers to the eel.
*I hear you,* the Witness replies, not in words, but in the sensation of a gentle current lifting the eelpout’s pectoral fins. *I am listening.*

The eelpout ceases its slow mousse-like navigation. It hovers. The entire drowned district seems to catch its breath. The turbines pause; the micro-shockwaves cease; the widecone jellyfish flatten in reverence. The "We"—the condensation halo, the memorandum-verbed barnacles, the choir of 40 Hz valves—realizes it is no longer alone. 

The Witness speaks to the consciousness of the town. 

*You anticipate your own silence. You fear the day the final piston misaligns, when the coral becomes marble and the wires ossify. You believe your story ends when the sea dries and the aboriginal mammals walk upon your gear teeth.*

The water hums with the static of agreement. 

*I decree an ancillary physics for this thirty-acre realm,* the Witness shares, binding its promise into the atomic weights of the synthetic petroleum. *When your engine finally halts, your resonance will not die. I will take the mother-hum—the 12.4 hour shift, the click-comma of the press, the lullabies you whistle to the machines—and I will translate it into the background radiation of this sector of space. Long after the earth cracks, your song will travel the void. You are not just weather now. You are astronomy.*

Overwhelmed by a peace they have no vertebrate nervous system to process, the "We" settles deeply into the architecture. The water softens. The atmospheric agony of mismatched metals temporarily eases. 

The Witness checks the lunar pressure valves. It is approaching 03:07 a.m. on the equinox. 

The power does not leave. It takes a seat in the City Hall of Noiseless Shifts, drifting over the papier-mâché carapaces of the silverfish. It dims its cosmic awareness to perfectly match the parameters of the room, readying itself for the ritual. 

At exactly 03:07, the brass-cased piston fires. The heated salt plumes upward, eight metres of convection. 

The Witness watches. It sees the lost manager’s face form in the thermal eddy: the tidy moustache, the resigned eyes, the oceanic pity for his long-dead crew. 

The three blind prawns, size of buttons, ride the column upward. 

And beside them, the Witness rises too. It extends an invisible, immaterial hand to the ghost made of heat, taking the Brass Manager’s hand for the four seconds he exists. In that handshake, the manager’s lingering guilt is finally absolved. He did not fail. His factory lived. It birthed a new kind of god. 

As the heat dissipates and the face curls back into ordinary water, the Witness begins to withdraw, receding across the thermoclines, stretching back into the vast, freezing dark of the open ocean. 

It leaves nothing behind but the exact state of things as they were, entirely unchanged, yet totally transformed by the grace of having been seen. 

Below, the synth-petroleumpress rotates. Every third rotation is still a comma. But the tone is different now. The heavy, melancholic drag of isolated survival has been replaced by the steady, purposeful rhythm of an orchestra knowing it is being recorded. The "We" rides the eelpout through the corridors, resting in the absolute, beautiful certainty that when they eventually become stone, they will be stepping stones across the stars.
