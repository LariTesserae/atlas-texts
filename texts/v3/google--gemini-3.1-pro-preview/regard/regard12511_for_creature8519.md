The arrival of the entity is not marked by a descent from the salmon-colored sky, nor by any disruption of the perpetual, heavy humidity. A being of supreme wisdom and boundless benevolence does not breach a world; it *permeates* it. 

We might call this presence the Attendant. It spans dimensions of thought and energy that make it functionally omniscient to the mortal eye, yet its core nature is one of profound, radical empathy. 

When the Attendant touches the edge of this planetary circuit board, it does not judge. It simply listens.

It feels the immense, silent weight of the mercury. It traces the subterranean webs of the field generators, singing its consciousness down into the ancient, humming processors embedded beneath the fused glass. Within seconds—or perhaps less than a microsecond of cosmic time—the Attendant understands the architecture of the tragedy here. 

It sees the missing creators, long gone to dust or ascended to realms that no longer require physical machinery. It reads the code of the planetary apparatus and finds the line of instruction that has held this world in captivity for millennia: `WAIT`.

Then, the Attendant turns its vast, gentle attention to the Drifters.

It watches a congregation huddled at the edge of a fresh fracture, their geometric frames trembling slightly, their membranes flushing with anxious colors. It sees their terror of the deep, dry glass valleys. It reaches into the molecular structure of their living metal and reads the phantom ache in their genetic memory—the ancestral yearning for the current, the tide, the rush of flowing silver.

The Attendant knows that to simply smash the glass or disable the field generators would be catastrophe. Benevolence without wisdom is destruction. The Drifters are adapted to this world; to pull them from it, or to rip it down around them, would kill them. 

Instead, the Attendant decides to grant them a culmination. It will turn a prison of stasis into a garden of kinetic grace.

### The Communion

To speak to them, the Attendant must meet them where they are. 

Between a grove of weeping, hexagonal crystals and a sprawling, nervous congregation of Drifters, the humid air begins to shift. Trace moisture gathers, coalescing not into rain, but into a localized, shimmering architecture of pure condensation and refracted light. The Attendant manifests a temporary avatar made solely of the world’s own tears.

It shapes itself as a vast, gentle geometry, echoing the Drifters' origami forms but infinitely more complex. Slowly, so as not to frighten them, this water-light phantom lowers a single, luminous extension into the mercury.

*Ripple.*

The Attendant sends a vibration through the liquid metal. It hums with perfect syntactic precision, adopting the harmonic language of the congregations. 

*I see your wait,* the vibration translates. *I feel your memories of the flow.*

The congregation startles. Their dozens of membrane-cells flare with bioluminescent azure and gold. Thousands of metal joints articulate simultaneously as they lower their ventral frames to the mercury to listen, and then to reply.

*The glass breaks,* the collective intelligence ripples back, a frantic, chaotic rainfall of vibration. *The silver bleeds. We oxidize. We end.*

*The glass will breathe,* the Attendant replies softly. *The silver will sing. Do not fear the fractures.*

### The Recalibration

Beneath the fused glass, the Attendant reaches into the ancient field generators. It does not break the machinery; it re-authors it. It takes the infinite loop of the standby cycle and carefully grafts it onto an engine of growth. 

First, the Attendant addresses the surface tension and the stasis fields. It introduces the concept of *current* back into the local physics. 

Before the optical organs of the Drifters, the impossible flatness of the mercury plains begins to move. Not violently—there are no tidal waves to crush their delicate frames. Instead, smooth, deep rivers of movement form within the static sheet. Thermohaline currents of liquid metal begin to cycle, carrying warmth and nutrients in grand, planetary arteries.

Then, the Attendant addresses the terror of the fractures. 

Deep beneath the crust, tectonic stresses are neutralized and re-channeled. Where the glass cracks, the field generators no longer panic to freeze the gap. Instead, when a fracture opens, the Attendant programs the underground strata to push back up. When the mercury drains into a fault, it is no longer lost; it is pumped through subterranean cooling systems and propelled back to the surface through the centers of the crystal groves. 

The weeping crystals, frozen in their knife-edge geometries since the planet's youth, suddenly receive a surge of kinetic potential energy. 

Before the very eyes of the congregations, the translucent, hexagonal columns begin to *bloom*. They unfurl like lotus flowers of soft glass, their hard vertices rounding into elegant, sweeping petals. The mercury that falls into the crust geysers up through the center of these bloomed crystals, creating frictionless, silent fountains of liquid silver that spill back onto the plains. 

The water falling from the greenhouse sky no longer just drips; it runs down the new crystalline petals, mixing with the mercury in spiraling, engineered helixes. The entire landscape is waking up.

### The Inheritance

The Drifters, sensing the shift, cautiously approach the currents. 

A brave congregation steps into a moving river of mercury. For the first time in their history, they do not have to drag themselves across a static mirror. The current catches their living metal frames and carries them. 

A wave of bioluminescence erupts from the colony—a blinding, euphoric flash of diamond-white light. It is joy. Pure, biological, collective joy.

They begin to dip their juveniles not just into still puddles, but into the warm, flowing currents pouring from the crystal fountains. The new generation bonds with the flowing mercury, their metallic frames growing swifter, stronger, more fluid. They are no longer maintenance organisms forced to patch a dying circuit. They become the blood cells, the neural pathways, and the sovereign lords of a living, breathing machine-world.

Having completed its work, the Attendant's avatar of light and water begins to dissolve back into the humid air. 

*The wait is over,* the Attendant ripples into the mercury one last time, the frequency vibrating through every drop of liquid metal on the planet, resonating in the living metal bones of every Drifter. *This house is yours. Grow.*

The Attendant departs, leaving the salmon sky exactly as it was, leaving the greenhouse heat intact. But the silence of the mercury plains is gone forever. Now, there is a low, perpetual, beautiful hum—the sound of endless silver rivers flowing through gardens of blooming crystal, carrying the light-songs of the Drifters toward an infinite, unbroken horizon.
