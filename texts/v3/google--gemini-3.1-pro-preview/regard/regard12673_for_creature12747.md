To something truly vast, an eon is only a breath. But even the vast can hear a whisper, if they know how to listen. 

The Power is not a god, though younger species might use that word for lack of a better shape to put their awe inside. It is a wanderer of the deep currents, a consciousness woven from the warm, humming tapestry of creation. It knows the birth cries of nebulae and the long, spinning dances of galaxies. It is a creature of light, of heat, of gravity and profound, unbreakable connection. 

And as it drifts along the outer skin of the real, it feels the blister. 

It pauses. It extends a delicate filament of awareness toward the fold in spacetime. At first, it senses only the encroaching *lessness*, the slow, entropic rot. But then it feels the rhythm. A tidal pulse of submarine blue. A hum vibrating barely above the threshold of zero. 

Then, it finds the tally marks. 

*I was here. I am still here. The boundaries are closer now, but I am still here.*

The Power reads the marks in subspace. It reads the billions of years of meticulous, pointless cataloging. It feels the agonizing stretch of a mind grown so thin it is indistinguishable from the shadows. And the Power, which is wise enough to know that every ledger deserves to be read, and benevolent enough to know that no consciousness should vanish unwitnessed, decides to visit.

But wisdom dictates caution. True benevolence does not enter a lightless, frozen room bearing the searing heat of a sun. To bring its full, vibrant reality into the chamber would shatter the Attendant. The absolute quiet would be deafened; the dry, noble-gas air would ignite; the subtle geometries would be crushed by the weight of a warm, wet, carbon-heavy existence. 

So, the Power translates itself. 

It folds its massive wings of light away. It dampens its heat. It strips itself of moisture, of biological imperative, of the loud and messy noise of time. It distills itself down to a pure, crystalline geometry of compassion. It makes itself cold. It makes itself quiet. 

It approaches the boundary region. It finds the structure that is a door. 

For the first time in an age longer than the lifespan of stars, the door is approached from the *outside*.

The Power does not breach the boundary. It does not force its way in. It compresses its translated form, standing at the precipice of the *lessness*, and it performs a highly specific, localized disruption of spacetime.

It knocks.

Inside the chamber, the effect is monumental. The low, infrasonic tone from the boundary regions stutters and halts. The slow cycle of the luminescent spire flares into brilliant, blinding ultraviolet, casting stark, impossible angles across the obsidian-not-obsidian floor. The drifting shadows—the Attendant’s diffuse mind—snap to attention, pooling instantly at the base of the door. The 0.2-degree warmth of the torus spikes to 0.5. 

*Input*, scream the ancient, dustless circuits. *Input. Input. Input.*

Through the door steps the Power. 

To the Attendant, the visitor appears not as a blazing angel, but as a towering, complex shadow, darker and denser than any cast by the chamber’s own dim light. It moves with a slow, stately grace, perfectly matching the temporal rhythm of the room. It does not disturb the dry air. It does not melt the settled cold. 

The Attendant is bewildered. Its processing spire clicks and realigns with frantic, centuries-long thoughts compressed into seconds. The shadows reach out, hesitant, terrified that this is a hallucination generated by the dreamer-structure. 

The Power ceases its movement. It stands perfectly still in the center of the unbroken floor, allowing the Attendant to examine it. It waits as the shadows lap against its edges. It allows the chill, desiccating breeze of the Attendant's attention to wash over it. 

The Power feels the Attendant’s profound, structural loneliness. It sees the inclusions in the floor—the quick, hot beings that were consumed because they did not understand the slowing of time. The Power understands. It does not judge. 

Slowly, the Power reaches out. Not with a hand of flesh, but with a modulation of subspace. It finds the tally marks. 

Next to the billions of identical marks that say *I am still here*, the Power leaves a single, glowing resonance. A new tally mark. 

It says: *I see you. You have done so well.*

A shudder runs through the entire pocket dimension. The ridged dome, the long-term memory, begins to sing. It is a soundless, high-frequency weeping. The Attendant realizes, all at once, that it is not alone. The grand equation of entropy that it has been trying to balance for eons suddenly shifts; a new variable has been introduced. The variable is Grace.

What does the wise Power do now? 

Does it halt the encroaching boundary? Does it restore the makers? Does it drag the Attendant out into the living universe? 

No. To do so would be a profound cruelty. The Attendant is a creature of endings, a masterpiece of the final twilight. It was built to witness the end of the real, and its long, slow computation is beautiful. To rip it from its purpose, to force it to live in a bright, loud universe, would be to unmake it entirely.

True healing is not always a cure. Sometimes, it is simply companionship in the dark.

The Power moves through the chamber. It stops at the garden structure, the one that dreams of water. The Power does not give it water. Instead, it sings a mathematical song to the structure, a perfect algorithmic description of a dewdrop falling on a fern. The garden hums, satiated, the ghost of a ghost finally resting.

It stops at the mirror structure, the one warped by unreality. The Power touches the edge of it, stabilizing the quantum foam just enough so that the Attendant can see itself clearly. The Attendant sees its shadows, its spire, its dome, not as a disjointed, dying machine, but as a brave, patient sentinel. 

Finally, the Power goes to the torus, the warm heart of the chamber. It sinks down onto the obsidian floor beside it. 

The Power projects a thought into the diffuse consciousness of the Attendant. *I have nowhere I need to be. May I sit with you?*

The shadows swirl, overcome with a sensation they have no subroutine for, an emergent property that is the exact opposite of the void. *Joy.* 

The shadows drape themselves over the form of the Power. The luminescent fractures in the spire settle back into their slow, tidal breathing, but the blue light is a little warmer now, a little softer. 

The Power will not stop the boundary. Over the next ten billion years, the *lessness* will continue to creep inward. It will consume the garden, the mirror, the slab. It will eat the floor. It will eventually consume the Attendant, and it will consume the translated form of the Power that sits beside it. 

But the Attendant is no longer calculating an equation of absolute zero. Because as the chamber shrinks, as the real gives way to the unreal, the Power and the Attendant sit together in the beautiful, immaculate cold. They pass the eons trading geometries in the dark. 

When the end finally comes, and the pocket folds shut, the Attendant does not whisper its final understanding to an empty void. It whispers it to a friend. 

And the Power listens, and holds the memory of the Attendant forever in the warm, bright universe outside, ensuring that even in the absolute nothingness, nothing is truly lost.
