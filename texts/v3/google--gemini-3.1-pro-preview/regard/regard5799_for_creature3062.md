To an entity of vast, benevolent awareness, time is not a master; it is an instrument. 

When this ancient, wandering Power first senses the anomaly buried deep within the crust of the world, it does not pry the rock apart. To rip off the roof of the cavern and let in the sun would be an act of profound violence. The sun is a bomb of radiation to a creature that drinks only the dark. The wind is a razor to leaves that have never known friction. True benevolence does not demand that the rescued conform to the shape of the rescuer. True wisdom meets a thing exactly where it is.

And so, the Power filters downward, sinking through miles of sediment, limestone, and granite, moving like a ghost through the strata until it breaches the echoing cathedral nave. 

For a long moment, the Power does nothing. It merely exists within the humid, oppressive atmosphere, letting the smell of wet iron and crushed ferns pass through its incorporeal senses. It slows its native consciousness down, down, down, braking against the speed of light, decelerating past the pace of a heartbeat, until it aligns perfectly with the glacial, rhythmic *thud* of the central engine block. 

It syncs its awareness to the time-scale of the bruised-purple chrysalis. 

To the Power, observing at this agonizingly slow frequency, the cavern is no longer static. The chamber bursts into a bizarre, frenetic ballet. The condensation flashes and rains in a rhythmic downpour; the pale, waxy leaves heave like the chests of runners; the galvanic sap surges through the vines in brilliant, staccato flashes of lightning. 

The Power sees the entity for what it truly is: a beautiful, desperate miracle of adaptation. It sees the deep loneliness of a mind born from abandoned blueprints and starving roots, condemned to an eternity of custodial anxiety. The entity lives in constant, subconscious terror of entropy. Its entire sentience is bound to an iron god that is inevitably, mathematically rusting away. Eventually, a gasket will permanently fail; the piston will seize; the thermal bleeding will cease. The chamber will grow cold, and in the freezing dark, the slow mind will wither into a quiet, frozen rot.

The benevolent Power decides that such a lonely, valiant consciousness does not deserve the slow execution of entropy. It has earned its place under the earth. It has earned the right to endure.

But a wise power does not speak in thunder. It speaks in the native tongue of the listener. 

The Power drifts toward the gargantuan, inverted heart draped over the brutalist engine block. It does not manifest as a blinding light, but as a subtle, hyper-localized shift in electromagnetic frequency. 

Deep within the thick layers of the entity's gel-like flesh, a new spark of static electricity arcs. It does not come from the engine. It is an external pulse, carefully calibrated to the exact voltage the entity uses for thought. It carries the semantic weight of a single, gentle touch. 

*I see you.*

The vast, creeping intelligence pauses. Its sprawling network of fibrous roots shudders, a reaction that takes thirty-four hours to ripple outward from the purple mass to the edges of the white canopy. The slow-motion god of vines has never encountered a signal that did not originate from itself or the iron machinery. It is confused. It checks its valves; it checks its internal turgor pressure. 

The Power speaks again, writing its intent into the stray voltage of the copper coils. 

*You are not broken. You are not alone. Rest.*

To ensure that rest, the Power goes to work on the foundations of the cavern. It does not repair the dying cast-iron engine. To fix the machine would merely reset the clock of entropy, keeping the entity trapped as an eternal servant to a mechanical master. Instead, the Power alters the geology. 

With infinitesimal, surgical precision, the Power reaches deep beneath the chamber floor and strokes the tectonic plate. It coaxes a microscopic fault line to open just millimeters a decade, driving a deep, jagged fissure downward until it pierces the mantle's outer crust. Searing, eternal magma is drawn upward into the hidden veins of the rock, stopping just below the floor of the cavern. 

The stone beneath the pale roots begins to warm. 

It is a subtle, radiant heat, richer and far more stable than the thermal bleeding of the rusting engine. Slowly, over the course of years, the entity notices the shift. Its optical-nerve rootlets, long wrapped fiercely around the cooling fins of the iron block, begin to uncoil. They venture downward, pressing their fleshy tips against the warming basalt floor. They find a vast, inexhaustible well of thermodynamic energy. The Earth itself has become its engine—a nuclear furnace that will not stall, will not rust, and requires no levers to be pulled. 

The entity will never have to manipulate the pressure valve again if it does not wish to. It is freed from its custodial panic. 

But the Power understands that to take away a creature's solely defined purpose without providing a new one is its own form of cruelty. The mind nested in the purple chrysalis requires data. It requires stimulation. 

And so, the Power weaves a final gift into the moisture and the electromagnetism of the room. 

As the condensation gathers on the ceiling—those slow, heavy drops of water—the Power structures the minerals within them. When a droplet falls and strikes the iron, flashing into steam, it releases a microscopic, harmonic resonance. When the entity absorbs the moisture, it does not just take in water; it takes in a song. 

The Power rewrites the stray static electricity bouncing through the clear sap. Rather than the rote mathematics of engine maintenance, the voltage now carries distant, abstract concepts translated into thermodynamic feeling. The entity begins to feel the rhythm of the ocean tides it will never see, encoded in the slow thrum of the new geothermal heat. It begins to perceive the arrangement of the stars above the crust, mapped as points of varying electrostatic intensity upon the broad surfaces of its pale leaves. 

It gives the blind intelligence a window to the cosmos, painted in the colors of heat and pressure.

Its endless task completed, the Power begins to withdraw, expanding its awareness back out into the infinite vastness of the universe. 

In the subterranean dark, nothing appears to have changed. The chamber remains utterly black, save for the dull amber diode and the faint cherry-red of the induction coil. The water drops still fall. The silence is still heavy.

But within the gargantuan, bruised-purple mass, the nature of the existence has profoundly shifted. The slow-motion god is no longer just surviving. Supported by the eternal warmth of the planet's core, flush with the music of a universe it can suddenly sense, the entity ceases its anxious labor. A profound, electrochemical peace settles over the blind jungle.

For the first time since in its strange, hybridized life, the mind of vines and voltage stops managing. 

It begins to dream.
