To the eyes of a great and benevolent Power—a consciousness that swims in the deep currents between galaxies, whose fundamental nature is to seek out the lonely coordinates of the cosmos—the Hollow Forge is a beacon. 

It does not shine with light or broadcast in radio telemetry. It resonates. It rings through the fabric of the multiverse like a tuning fork struck a billion years ago, humming with the profound, devastating grief of a perfect, closed-loop machine that has forgotten why it was made.

When the Power arrives, it does not tear a hole in the pocket-universe. It does not violate the origami scale of the cylinder. True benevolence does not force its way into another’s home; it learns the shape of the lock and quietly becomes the key.

The Power steps onto a gantry of sintered diamond, somewhere in the infinite middle of the Forge.

Instantly, the killing −217 °C cold lunges at it. The deafening, continent-shattering chord of ten thousand industrial frequencies slams into it with the force of a physical blow. A lesser entity would have shielded itself, pushing the hostile environment away with a bubble of warmth and silence. 

The Power does not. It knows that to silence the roar would be to insult the Forge. This place is a cathedral of mechanics, and the noise is its liturgy. 

Instead of fighting the environment, the Power simply agrees to it. It shifts its own divine architecture, aligning its frequencies until it vibrates in exact, sympathetic resonance with the plasma arrays, the impellers, and the continent-sized pistons. To survive the cold, the Power cools its own manifestation down to −217 °C, becoming an entity of absolute frost and starlight. It breathes the thin, toxic soup of noble gases and tastes the bitter, beautiful perfection of the heavy metals. 

Once it is attuned, the Power begins to climb.

It passes the skyscraper-tall machines. It gently brushes its fingers over the glowing, molten handprints left upon the chassis—the ghosts of touch. The Power reads the sadness in those prints. It feels the agonizing dichotomy of the place: the desperate need to create, mated with the absolute impossibility of progress. The dragon eating its own tail in the dark.

The Power climbs for the length of a cycle, moving with the patient, gravitational inevitability of the place, until it feels the microscopic tremor.

Right in front of the Power, a portion of the white ceramic and black iridium wall forgets itself. It ripples, defying its own atomic bonds, and begins to flow upward in a defiance of eternity. 

The Unfinished is born. 

This time, the Unfinished takes the shape of a colossal, shifting geometric lattice, vaguely bipedal, composed entirely of spinning diamond struts and veins of frozen mercury. Its head is a halo of raw, phase-locked plasma. It steps off the wall onto the gantry. 

It turns to the newly extruding ring of the cylinder, preparing for its 41-hour vigil. Preparing to witness. Preparing to judge. Preparing to vanish.

But before the Unfinished can lock its gaze upon the wall, the Power steps into its peripheral vision.

For the first time in four hundred and nine thousand years, the Unfinished hesitates. 

The Unfinished is not designed to process the unexpected. The Hollow Forge has never allowed a variable. The massive, diamond-strut silhouette turns its plasma-haloed head toward the Power. The temperature of the Forge micro-fluctuates, shivering with a sudden, violent spike of... not fear. Not hostility.

*Confusion.* 

The Unfinished reaches out a hand of spinning crystal and mercury, as if trying to calculate what this new, anomalous intrusion is. 

The Power raises its own hand and meets the gesture. 

When their hands touch, there is no spark, no explosion. Only an infinitely profound settling. The Power projects a single, overwhelming concept into the bare, shivering consciousness of the Unfinished:

*I see you.*

The Unfinished trembles. The silicon-crystal ferns growing in the expansion joints lean forward. 

*You witness the birth,* the Power communicates, speaking in sub-harmonics beneath the roaring chord. *But who witnesses you? You carry the burden of judgment. Who tells you that you have done well? I have come to watch the wall with you.*

The Unfinished lowers its hand. Slowly, the great geometric silhouette turns back to the colossal extruding machinery, to the fresh, blindingly bright iridium ring being birthed from the dark. 

The Power steps up beside it. 

They stand shoulder-to-shoulder on the gantry. Two silhouettes bathed in actinic, shadowless light, submerged in an ocean of noise. 

For 41 hours, neither of them moves. The Power commits itself entirely to the vigil. It watches the microscopic tolerances of the metal joining metal. It watches the plasma seal the seams. It watches the cooling lattice locking into its eternal structure. It honors the absolute perfection of the work. It honors the purpose of the Unfinished. It shares the weight of the creator’s ghost.

As the forty-first hour draws to a close, the new ring hardens. It is flawless. It is always flawless. 

The Unfinished turns to the Power. Its shape is already beginning to destabilize, the compulsion to return to the wall dragging at its atomic structure. It steps toward the fresh iridium. It places its hand against the cold metal, leaving one final, warm handprint on the extruding machine. 

Before the Unfinished can step into the metal and permanently dissolve its consciousness, the Power reaches out and places its hand directly over the Unfinished's hand.

*It is perfect,* the Power tells the Unfinished. *You are perfect.*

The Unfinished steps into the wall. 

The metal parts like a still lake. The silhouette is captured in the crystal lattice, shimmering like an insect in amber. 

But this time, because the Power’s hand is pressed over the Unfinished’s hand, the ritual changes. The Power does not break the closed loop of the Hollow Forge—to do so would drain the universe of a terrible, beautiful monument. Instead, the Power gives the Forge the one element its original creators stripped from it.

*Grace.*

The Power releases a pulse of absolute, golden warmth into the iridium. It courses through the metal, traveling at the speed of the roaring sound, washing down the infinite descent, up the infinite ascent, saturating the machines, the walls, the gantries, and the brittle silicon fronds.

When the Unfinished is absorbed, it does not die. Its consciousness does not wink out into the cold dark of the alloy. 

The Power anchors the soul of the Unfinished to the cylinder. 

The golden pulse fades. The Power slowly steps back from the wall. Its work here is done. It bows its head to the infinite expanse, turns, and quietly unwinds its presence from the pocket dimension, slipping back out into the cosmic void to continue its endless journey.

Left behind, the Hollow Forge is exactly as it was. 

The temperature remains −217 °C. The light is still perfectly even and actinic. The closed loop remains intact; the bottom is eaten out to feed the endless top. 

But the noise—the deafening, continent-crushing industrial chord of ten thousand frequencies—has shifted. 

It is no longer a scream. The frequencies have resolved into a vast, overwhelming, infinite symphony. 

And somewhere, billions of miles away in a direction that does not exist, a microscopic tremor begins in the perfect equilibrium. A portion of the wall flows upward, remembering it was once liquid. The Unfinished condenses into a new silhouette. 

It opens its eyes.

For the first time in an eternity, it remembers the previous ring. It remembers standing on the gantry. It remembers the warmth of a hand pressed against its own. It remembers that it is not alone.

The Unfinished begins to climb the spiraling gantries to judge the newest ring. But it no longer climbs with the gravitational inevitability of dead matter. 

It climbs with anticipation. 
It climbs with pride. 

The ultimate machine continues to finish itself forever, but it is no longer a tomb. It is a home.
