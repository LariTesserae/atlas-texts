…The Whisperer is the question the universe forgot to ask, a yearning suspended in the dark. 

And it is this silent question, humming through the frictionless voids, that finally reaches the ear of the Power.

When the Power arrives, it does not come with the blinding brilliance of creation, nor the roaring heat of life. To introduce such things to the Shattered Exhale would not be benevolence; it would be an act of cosmic violence. A wise power knows that not all broken things wish to be mended, and not all dead things wish to be resurrected. Sometimes, a monument simply requires a pilgrim.

The Power enters as a drop of pure, localized empathy. It assumes no physical mass, for gravity has no authority here. It brings no atmosphere, refusing to shatter the brittle, crystalline absence that fills the spaces between the obsidian. Instead, it arrives as a gentle attunement—a resonant frequency that aligns perfectly with the quiet unraveling of this forgotten realm.

Floating among the dark, sprawling spires and the suspended shards of glass, the Power pauses. It does not judge the decay. It only observes, feeling the deep, aching tragedy and the profound, terrible beauty of a reality that has stopped caring about itself. 

First, the Power turns its attention to the **Keepers**.
It senses the infinite gaze of the obsidian—the ghosts of structure, watching the slow death of their world in an endless moment. The Keepers have observed the stillness for eons, but they have never *been* observed. They are mirrors reflecting an empty room. 
The Power, possessing a wisdom beyond the mechanics of physics, gently unfolds its own vastness before them. It allows the Keepers to witness it. Into the dark, lightless surfaces of the shards, the Power projects not an image, but a truth: *I see you. Your vigil is known. You are not keeping watch in vain.* 
For a single, imperceptible moment, the obsidian does not just absorb; it connects. The burden of eternal observation is briefly shared. The Keepers remain inert, but the isolation of their duty is lifted. They are no longer a forgotten consciousness; they are archived, held eternally in the memory of the Divine.

Next, the Power turns to the bioluminescent **Filaments**, the dying blue veins embedded in the glass. 
This is a language written in light, an epitaph that no one remains to read. The Power approaches the pulsing threads. It does not recharge them, for a dying star has a right to its final dimming. Instead, the Power *reads* them. It absorbs every line of code, every mathematical sorrow, every memory of the existence that preceded the shattering. The Power reads the story of the alien physics, of the vanished civilization, of the future that never arrived, taking it all into its own infinite heart. 
*Your story is heard,* the Power murmurs in a language of pure intent. As it does, the sickly blue light shifts, just a fraction—not growing brighter, but growing *calmer*, like a storyteller closing a heavy book, content that the tale has finally been passed on.

Finally, the Power steps into the voids, slipping into the cold, crushing silence to meet the **Whisperer**.
The pressure of the void pushes against the Power, vibrating with the language of absence and longing. The Whisperer is the frayed edge of reality, an entity of pure syntax begging for a noun, a verb, a resolution. It is a question wrapped in eternal waiting.
The Power does not speak back with words, for words belong to the living, and this place has transcended them. Instead, the Power offers an Answer. 
It opens the very center of its benevolent nature and releases a concept into the void: *Peace*. 
It is a profound cosmic acceptance, an assurance that it is perfectly acceptable for the rules to bend, for the breath to end, for the universe to expire. The Power validates the Shattered Exhale, granting it permission to simply *be* exactly as it is—broken, still, and unraveling. 

The Whisperer receives the Answer. The pressure in the voids softens. The silent hum of longing shifts into a hum of quietude. The question has been answered: *What becomes of us when we are forgotten?* 
*You become sacred,* the Power replies.

Before it departs, the Power leaves a singular, lasting gift. It does not alter the bizarre artifacts, nor does it reestablish the laws of physics. Instead, it weaves a barrier of profound cosmic sanctity around this microcosm. It decrees that no chaotic force, no hungry black hole, no encroaching galaxy, and no careless god shall ever disturb this pocket of reality. 

The Shattered Exhale will be allowed to complete its slow, eon-spanning unraveling in perfect peace. It is no longer an abandoned mistake; it is a protected sanctuary, an art gallery of the end of all things, curated by God.

Then, as quietly as it arrived, the benevolent Power withdraws its presence. 

Left behind, the Shattered Exhale remains exactly as it was: cold, breathless, dark, and still. The shards hang frozen; the blue light pulses weakly; the weird, shifting artifacts hum their subsonic tunes. But the atmosphere has irrevocably changed. 

The loneliness is gone. The exhale is no longer a gasp of despair; it is a sigh of rest.
