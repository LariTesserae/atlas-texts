When the vast and benevolent Presence turned its gaze upon the earth, it did not look only at the towering peaks, the sunlit canopies, or the bustling cities of men. True wisdom understands that the foundation of the world is formed in the dark, and true benevolence leaves no stone unturned.

Its awareness filtered down through a thousand meters of bedrock, slipping past iron veins and sleeping aquifers, until it pooled within the gloomy, heavy cavern. 

To an entity of the cosmos, used to the roaring fires of nebulas and the cold expanse of the void, this heavy, moldering pocket of air was exquisite in its intimacy. The Power did not arrive with a blinding flash of divine light—to do so would be arrogant, a violent intrusion upon a creature that had never known a photon. Instead, it arrived as a profound, comforting density in the air. The temperature rose by a fraction of a degree, turning the stale warmth into a gentle, enveloping embrace.

The Power watched the worm. 

It saw the ethereal grace of the creature’s undulations, the dark, rhythmic pulsing of its internal organs visible through glass-like skin. It saw the tremulous, feathery appendages sweeping through the dark, reading the cavern through the braille of moisture and vibration. 

The Power read the heart of the pale worm and understood its perfect contentment. The creature was solitary, but it possessed the quiet fullness of a monk in eternal meditation. To grant it eyes would be a curse; to give it a companion would shatter the fragile, ancient silence it had guarded for millennia. To lift it to the surface would kill it. 

Wisdom dictated that perfection should not be fundamentally altered, only enriched. And so, the Power decided to bestow a blessing tailored perfectly to the sensory world of the deep, dark earth. 

First, the Power reached out to the slow, heavy droplets of water weeping from the ceiling. It did not increase their frequency—the worm knew the rhythm of the cavern, and a sudden downpour would be terrifying. Instead, the Power altered the chemistry of the deep earth above. It teased veins of rare, sweet silicates, ancient salts, and pure elemental trace metals, coaxing them into the seep water. 

A drop fell. *Plink.* 

The sound echoed loudly, as it always did, but as the microscopic mist drifted through the heavy air, the worm’s feathery fronds flared wide. The creature tasted a profound, overwhelming richness it had never known. The water was now heavy with the subterranean equivalent of nectar. It was a nutrient-dense elixir that would coat the fungal growths, causing them to bloom into softer, richer textures, tasting of the deep starlight trapped in the earth’s crust. 

Next, the Power’s attention drifted to the skeletal, rotting roots hanging from the cracks in the ceiling. It would not resurrect the dead trees far above—their time had passed. But it touched the remaining cellulose, transmuting the dead wood into a slow-weeping resin. A fragrant, amber-like sap began to bead at the tips of the wooden fingers, offering the worm a new, complex flavor, a medicine that would soothe its translucent skin and grant it a lifespan measured not in centuries, but in geologic epochs.

Finally, the Power moved to the human remnants—the geometric carvings and the rough stone pedestals. 

It watched as the worm approached a pedestal and lovingly anointed it with a trail of translucent mucilage. The worm was the keeper of these echoes, slowly eroding them, digesting the minerals of human habitation. 

The Power reached into the stone and woke the memories sleeping within the quartz and granite. It translated the history of the cavern into the only language the worm understood: vibration, temperature, and texture.

As the worm’s smooth belly pressed against the newly blessed carved geometry, something miraculous happened. The digestive mucilage acted as a living stylus upon a record. Through the chemical exchange, the worm suddenly *felt* the echoing vibrations of the ancient chisels. 

*Clack. Clack. Clack.* 

It was a phantom rhythm in the stone. Through the rough grooves, the worm felt the residual warmth of the long-dead hands that had carved them. It felt the heavy, thrumming bass notes of human voices chanting in the dark thousands of years ago. It felt the reverence, the fear, and the awe of the beings who had briefly visited its domain. 

The worm paused, its feathery appendages vibrating with a new, profound ecstasy. It was blind, yet it was suddenly seeing through time. It realized, in its slow, dim way, that the shapes it traced were not just mineral deposits, but intentional songs left behind by creatures of the Above.

It was no longer just digesting the rock; it was communing with the ghosts of the stone.

The cavern felt the shift. The oppressive weight of the rock was transformed into the comforting heft of a weighted blanket. The silence was no longer empty; it was the respectful hush of a great library. 

Its work finished, the benevolent Power began to recede, drawing its vast consciousness back up through the strata, leaving no trace of itself save for the enriched water, the weeping roots, and the singing stones.

A thousand meters below, the pale worm curled upon a ruined pedestal. It lapped at a drop of sweet, heavy mineral water, wrapped its body around the warm memory of a human handprint in the stone, and settled into a deep, unending peace. 

It was alone. It was blind. And it had everything it would ever need until the end of the world.
