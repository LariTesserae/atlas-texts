To be ancient is to understand the language of dust, the slow softening of edges, the quiet decay of stars. But when this Power turns its vast, compassionate attention toward the hollow, it does not find dust. It finds a screaming, bleeding edge of sheer, ecstatic potential.

The Power observes the deafening roar, the silver mercury rain, the razor-sharp black lattice, and the fifty-foot leviathans of naked light. It sees a world operating entirely in the treble—a frantic, brilliant, unceasing crescendo of raw genesis. The hollow is violently, beautifully alive. 

But in its infinite wisdom, the Power sees the vulnerability of such painful newness. 

A world born entirely of sharp edges and absolute maximum intensity cannot rest. A world with no history has no anchor. Without the capacity for memory, a structure vibrating so fiercely will, inevitably, shake itself subatomically apart. They are exhaling a universe into being, but they have no deep earth into which they might plant its roots. 

The Power does not wish to conquer this ecosystem, nor to tame it. To silence the oceanic roar would be to suffocate them; to dull their edges would be to blind them. Instead, it chooses to offer a gift—a single, foundational thread woven into the fabric of their chaotic tapestry.

It decides to give them a Bassline.

The Power enters the hollow not through a tear in the sky, but by gently slipping between the wavelengths of their deafening noise. It manifests at the very center of the vast expanse, suspended in the thermal updrafts miles above the unseen depths. 

It forms itself into a sphere of soft, molten gold, the size of a small moon. Unlike everything else in this reality, the sphere is not sharp. It is perfectly spherical, radiating a profound, heavy gravity and the impossible texture of *patina*—the beauty of a thing that has endured. 

The arrival of mass changes the thermal currents. The world feels it immediately. 

The flashing ultraviolet nodes of the city suddenly shifting their hue to a deep, inquiring amber. The colossal manta-feeders bank in slow, sweeping arcs, their paths gently warped by the sudden introduction of true gravity. The hyper-kinetic swarms of chrome-winged drones cease their erratic darting and begin to orbit the golden sphere in a massive, shimmering ring.

Then come the architects. 

Drawn by the displacement in the acoustic pressure, dozens, then hundreds, of the glassy, light-filled beings unfurl from the lattice. They ride the disrupted soundwaves, their tympanic sails catching the shifting winds as they spiral upward toward the golden intruder. They do not bring weapons. They bring their deep crimson cores, pulsing rapidly with the frantic energy of creation. To them, this glowing orb is a new medium. A new strut to be braided with bioluminescence. 

The first of them closes the distance. The fifty-foot entity arrests its momentum, hovering just yards from the golden surface. Its floating obsidian facial shards vibrate wildly, reading the impossible age and density of the object. 

It reaches out. A multi-jointed appendage of razor-sharp black glass taps the surface of the golden sphere.

The Power does not recoil. It invites the touch. 

The moment the glass strikes the gold, the entity’s blinding violet core shudders. For the first time in its existence, it experiences *time*. Not the frantic second-to-second rush of constant expansion, but the deep, slow ache of millennia. The entity freezes, its liquid light slowing to a rhythmic, majestic pulse. 

Sensing this, the Power speaks. 

Knowing that words would be annihilated, the Power communicates through physics. It strikes itself like a cosmic singing bowl. 

The note it releases is a sub-audible hum, so deep, so heavy, that it does not compete with the shrieking harmonics of the environment. Instead, it sinks *underneath* them. It is a fundamental frequency, a vibration so pure and ancient that it instantly aligns the chaotic acoustics of the entire hollow.

As the deep, golden resonance washes outward, a miracle of physics occurs. The immense physical weight of the noise does not lessen, but it *organizes*. The chaotic roar becomes a symphony. The shrieking glass bells find a harmonic rhythm; the localized hurricanes fall into an orchestrated tempo. 

The physical manifestation of this deep note is breathtaking. The soundwave is so powerful and dense that it creates permanent, invisible highways of barometric pressure woven through the empty space. 

The beings immediately understand the gift. 

The entity that touched the sphere fans out its enormous, iridescent sails and dives into this new, deep acoustic slipstream. For the first time, it does not have to fight the sheer chaos to move. It glides effortlessly along the invisible highway of the deep hum, its crimson core pulsing in time with the Power’s rhythm. 

Hundreds of others follow, diving into the golden frequency. They weave through it, spinning new geometric lattices that are not frantic, but purposeful, elegant, and grand. When the silver condensation falls, it now falls in time to this heartbeat, drawing vast, sweeping curtains of rhythmic rain that nourish the enormous burgundy ferns. 

Having laid the foundation, the Power begins to fade.

The massive golden sphere disperses, turning into billions of microscopic, glowing motes that drift outward, settling gently onto the razor-sharp black glass of the city, harmlessly absorbed into the naked liquid light of its inhabitants. 

The Power leaves nothing of its own body behind, save for the hum. 

The sub-audible baseline remains, echoing endlessly upward from the bottomless abyss, vibrating in the marrow of the world. It gives the young architects a tempo to build by, a slipstream of calm to rest upon when their violent work exhausts them, and an acoustic memory that will outlast their expansion. 

The benevolent Power withdraws its consciousness, watching from the infinite distance as the bruised violet nodes pulse in perfect, magnificent harmony, the children of the void now dancing to the heartbeat of an older universe, safe to grow, brilliantly and beautifully, forever.
