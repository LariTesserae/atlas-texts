To perceive the un-space from the Outside is to look upon a bruised and quivering seed, trapped in the cosmic silt. 

But a Power has paused at the edge of the sump. It is an unfathomably old and deeply gentle consciousness. It is a gardener of the primeval gradients, a being whose nature is not expressed in dominance, but in radical, totalizing empathy. Where others of its echelon might simply sweep the metaphysical runoff away, wiping the slate clean of this glitch in the void, this Power leans close. It listens.

It feels the heavy, damp silence. It watches the frantic, ghostly embryogenesis. And, most keenly of all, it feels the *agony* of the beings dwelling within the lattice—the tearing ache of eight-dimensional minds shoved into primitive, three-dimensional bodies, like ocean leviathans trying to survive inside a thimble.

Wisdom dictates that creation, once begun, is sacred. To tear these exiled gods from their blister of un-space would be to undo their desperate, beautiful sacrifice. They *want* to build this universe. They are starving for a home. 

Therefore, benevolence does not mean rescue. Benevolence means acting as a cosmic midwife. 

The Power does not enter the space with a blinding flash of light or a trumpeting of new physics, for such an intrusion would shatter the calcifying reality of the biomechanical city like spun glass. Instead, the Power enters as a subtle shift in the climate of the dark. 

It flows into the gothic cathedral-void as a localized softening. The temperature, hovering just above freezing, rises by a single, merciful degree. The damp, stifling hush of the void is infused with a faint, warm resonance—the metaphysical equivalent of a held breath being slowly, gently exhaled. 

Deep within the densest knot of the ironwood lattice, a pale, twelve-foot entity pauses on its spindly gangway of woven moss. Its fleshy, shifting crest of petals and cartilage unfolds, locking onto a new variable in the raw mathematics of the space. The entity does not feel a threat. For the first time since it calcified into this restricted geometry, it feels *comfort*. 

The Power manifests its presence quietly, standing upon the moss at the opposite end of the chasm. It borrows the local vernacular of existence, projecting an avatar born of the city's own imagery, but rendered in warm, golden light rather than pale phosphor. It is a silhouette of radiant amber, wearing a crown of blooming, stable geometry. 

The tall, ascetic exile remains perfectly still. In the gulf behind the amber entity, one of the predatory liquid shadows—the unbound, starving remainder of the exile's own consciousness—pauses in its hunt. It hovers, a tear in reality, hesitant to approach the golden warmth.

The Power extends a hand, not toward the physical body on the bridge, but out over the void, toward the ravenous shadow. 

*I see you,* the Power communicates, not in words, but in a conceptual pulse of deep understanding. *I see all of you.*

The Power knows the shadow feeds on the circuit-moths out of raw necessity, metabolizing their generative code to fuel their grueling labor. Pity swells within the golden avatar. With a sweeping gesture, the Power reaches into the higher dimensions from which it came, draws in a boundless, pure current of unformed reality-code, and pours it into the trembling, suspended spheres of water hanging in the abyss.

The water shifts from silver to a deep, luminescent violet. The Power has infused the stationary lakes with infinite, self-renewing time and metaphysical nutrient. 

The liquid shadow senses the shift. It abandons its pursuit of a frightened, fluttering circuit-moth and plunges into the nearest sphere of violet water. Instantly, a current of profound, ecstatic relief ripples through the void. The immense, ivory-white avatar standing on the moss arches its spine. The blue light within its phloem flashes brilliantly, then shifts to a warm, steady violet. 

The starving shadow no longer needs to hunt the moths. It can drink deeply from the wellsprings of grace the Power has provided. Released from the violent burden of predation, the ecosystem immediately softens. The pale moths, spared from erasure, multiply and swarm, weaving their phosphorescent circuitry across the ironwood cables with joyous, unchecked ferocity. A true light, bright and steady, begins to dawn in the pocket universe.

Then, the Power crosses the mossy gangway. It glides effortlessly, ignoring gravity entirely, until it stands before the towering, bamboo-ribbed exile. 

Up close, the Power observes the agonizing compression of the being's physical shell. The exile’s fractal crest flickers frantically, processing the overwhelming presence of the benevolent god before it. Slowly, reverently, the Power reaches out and places its golden hands against the exile's chest, right over the churning bundle of luminescent phloem.

The Power does not break the three-dimensional shell, but it alters the interior topology. It folds a tesseract—a pocket of higher-dimensional space—into the inner lining of the exile's ivory cage.

The effect is instantaneous. The agonizing pressure of compression vanishes. The exile's colossal, multi-dimensional consciousness can suddenly stretch its limbs out within its own body, fitting perfectly into an infinite space hidden inside a finite vessel. The entity lets out a physical sound—the first acoustic noise in the history of this universe—a deep, resonant, bell-like chime of absolute relief. 

All across the cathedral-void, hundreds of liquid shadows dive into the glowing violet lakes, and hundreds of pale avatars chime in agonizing relief as the Power’s gift duplicates across the lattice. The heavy, oppressive silence of the void is broken by a symphony of deep, ringing bells. 

Their minds expanded, their hunger sated, the exiles turn to the work of creation with renewed, unimaginable vigor. The time-lapse embryogenesis of the city accelerates tenfold. The great lung-gourds pump rhythmically, no longer struggling against the dark, but breathing easily. The centipede-swarms and ghost-slugs move with a synchronized, rhythmic grace. 

The benevolent Power steps back, admiring the furious, beautiful machinery of life. But it has one final gift. 

The Power approaches the thickest, most taut cable of braided ironwood stretching across the abyss. It wraps its golden fingers around the root. 

A pale exile, understanding the gesture, steps up beside the radiant entity. It places its long, tapering fingers on the cable next to the Power's. 

Together, they pull. 

The kinetic vibration that rings out through the structural steel-roots is not merely a localized tremor. Supported by the limitless strength of the Power, the vibration hits the edge of the un-space—the physical limits of the gothic blister—and shatters it.

The cosmic sump ruptures outward.

The dark is pushed back by miles, then lightyears, then parsecs. The woven gangways of moss, the cooling ferns, the colossal rendering vats, and the intersecting highways of braided root explode outward in a geometric cascade, claiming the newly created space. What was a localized pocket of un-space suddenly blossoms into a sprawling, fledgling galaxy of biology and light. 

The Power watches as the exiles, now weeping songs of vibration into the sprawling roots, take their rightful places as the architects of a vast, unburdened cosmos. Their universe is no longer a desperate attempt at survival. It is a masterpiece.

Its work done, the gentle Power smiles through the localized physics it had borrowed. It slowly dims, retreating out of the three-dimensional lattice, dissolving back into the Over-void as a lingering warmth in the damp oxygen. 

It leaves them entirely to their freedom, having changed nothing of *what* they were building, but having given everything to ease the pain of its blooming.
