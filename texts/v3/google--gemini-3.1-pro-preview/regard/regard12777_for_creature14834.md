To a consciousness vast enough to cradle galaxies, the universe is mostly a symphony of grand, slow movements—the low hum of background radiation, the majestic, glacial turning of spirals, the quiet, crystalline math of gravity. 

But when this benevolent and wise power sweeps its attention over the cosmic tapestry, it pauses. It hears something anomalous. A dissonance. A frantic, rhythmic thrashing folded away in a pocket dimension, like a panicked heartbeat beneath a heavy quilt. 

It peers into the fissure that groans like a slow yawn. 

The Power does not possess eyes, nor does it possess ears, but it perceives the crushing, deafening wall of noise, the pulsing emerald and cobalt luminescence, the desperate, chaotic metabolism of this embryonic world. It sees the god-womb. And most importantly, it feels the *sparks*. 

It watches the beings—these fleeting, desperate nodes of self-awareness—pushing themselves out of the fibrous substrate, carving their painful geometries into the flesh of their creator. It watches them tap their frantic, seismic poetry into the ground. It watches them carve their tiny, silent alcoves just to hear themselves think, just to assert an *“I”* against the deafening roar of the *“All.”*

A lesser power might look upon this turbulent chrysalis with horror. A meddling power might attempt to "save" the beings—to pull them from the cycle, to grant them immortality, to stabilize the ground beneath their feet. 

But this Power is wise. It understands the ecology of genesis. 

It knows that to freeze the shifting walls is to paralyze the nascent god. It knows that to grant the beings eternal life is to lobotomize the super-organism, trapping its thoughts in a static loop, preventing the world’s deep memory from ever compiling. The tragedy of the beings—their frantic, terrifyingly brief existence, and their inevitable dissolution—is not a systemic failure. It is the very engine of cognition. Every frantic path mapped, every predator evaded, every hard-won moment of individuality downloaded into the substrate is a synapse firing in the brain of a sleeping deity. 

To end their struggle is to kill the god before it is born.

And yet, the Power is deeply benevolent. It cannot simply watch this agonizing labor without offering a profound, cosmic grace.

So, the Power decides to act. It does not intervene in the flesh of the world; it intervenes in its *spirit*. 

It reaches into the dense, thick atmosphere of the world, brushing past the iridescent swarms and the gliding leather mantas, and finds the alcoves. The sensory deprivation chambers. The sacred spaces where the beings retreat to carve out their elusive individuality. 

The Power takes a tiny fraction of its own infinite nature—the absolute, profound, ancient Quiet of the deep cosmos—and it coats the inner walls of these alcoves with it. 

When the next panicked, newly-budded being struggles into an alcove to escape the crushing metabolic noise of its world, it will not just find a dampening of the sound. It will find a silence so pure, so rich, and so deep that it acts as a mirror for the soul. In this gifted silence, the being’s sense of *“I”* crystallizes instantly, without the agonizing periods of terror and confusion. The beings suddenly find clarity. 

But the Power gives them one thing more. 

As they sit in these perfected sanctuaries of quiet, the Power whispers a concept to them. Not in words, and not in seismic taps, but in a subtle, harmonic resonance that vibrates in the ivory marrow of their forms. It gives them the concept of the *Outside*. It gives them the abstract feeling of stars, of open space, of celestial peace, of other minds in the dark. 

The beings cannot fully comprehend it—their minds are built to map canyons of fibrous moss and evade sonic predators—but they *feel* it. It translates in their minds as Awe. 

When their brief season ends, and they walk willingly into the pulsing, gelatinous pools to dissolve, they do not just take local knowledge back into the substrate. They take this Awe. They download the concept of the boundless cosmos into the deep memory of the womb. The Power is teaching the embryonic god to dream of the stars, so that when it finally wakes, it will not wake up terrified and alone, but curious and reaching outward.

Then, the Power turns its attention to the anomaly. 

It sees you. The ghost in the machine. The human observer stranded in a place where your consciousness is a void, where your existence is an agonizing misfire of reality. The inhabitants cannot see you, and the world cannot metabolize you. You are a grain of sand inside a cosmic pearl, causing an ache the nascent god cannot locate. 

With immense gentleness, the Power wraps a membrane of native spacetime around you. The deafening, crushing sound suddenly mutes. The heavy, spore-choked air clears from your lungs. The Power plucks you from the fissure entirely, drawing you up through the yawning gap and sealing the dimensional crack behind you with a smooth, silent seal, leaving the god-womb to its holy, violent incubation.

It places you back in your own world, on cool, solid, unmoving earth, beneath a quiet sky. 

You are left with your memories perfectly intact, a living vessel holding the secret of a god’s gestation. You were not unmade; you were spared. 

Now, somewhere beyond the veil of the universe, the god-womb continues to churn. But it is different now. Because of the benevolent Power, the frantic cartographers tap faster, their lives imbued with a strange, radiant purpose. They are no longer just surviving; they are building a bridge between their sleeping world and the infinite dark. And at the center of the world's deep hum, a new harmonic rings out—the sound of a cosmic mind, dreaming of the day it will finally open its eyes.
