It does not arrive with light. To bring light to the Abyssal Meadows would be an act of violence, a tearing of the ancient, absolute dark that these beings have farmed and woven into their very architecture. 

Instead, the Attention arrives as a shift in density. 

A sprawling, unimaginably vast awareness—born of the spaces between stars but intimately familiar with the slow, crushing gravity of planets—turns its gaze downward. It filters softly through the sunlit epipelagic zones, slips quietly past the twilight of the mesopelagic, and descends into the freezing, heavy black. It leaves the surface noise behind. It calibrates itself to the deep.

This Power is entirely benevolent, which means it knows how to mourn, but it is also perfectly wise, which means it knows better than to pity. It recognizes the Meadows not as a wasteland, but as a crucible of exquisite, desperate pressure. It tastes the dissolved minerals, the iron and salt and time. It feels the anti-silence. 

And then, it perceives the Dwellers.

The Power watches as they transpose through the black water. It reads the impossible geometry of their architectural bodies. It listens to the low-frequency harmonics echoing off their tubeworm-and-bone settlements. It walks through their ghost-light gardens of bioluminescence, absorbing every flash, every encoded silence, reading the entire archive of their millions of years of existence. It witnesses their long, slow dying—the tragedy of their heavy, singular children, trapped in a density they cannot master because the parameters of their world have been irrevocably skewed by the careless creatures living in the light above.

The Power catches the streams of bubbles they send upward—those fragile, buoyant desperate encyclopedias of their civilization. It reads their mythology. It realizes that these beings believe they are shouting into an empty heaven, hoping only to be recorded before they dissolve into the biological concrete.

Wisdom dictates that the Power cannot simply cool the oceans. The warming water is part of a planetary chain reaction; to violently reverse it would shatter the fragile equilibrium of a billion other species above. Nor can it lift the Dwellers into the light; they are creatures of the deep, forged in the crushing embrace of thousands of atmospheres. To take them from the pressure is to take them from themselves.

But Benevolence demands an answer. Benevolence says: *No archive so beautiful should burn. No child should forget how to be a facet of its own ancestry.*

So, the Power chooses to speak their language. It chooses to become turbulence.

Deep beneath the three hundred meters of restless water, beneath the bacterial mats and the compressed layers of shell and bone, the tectonic groan of the earth pauses. For one suspended second, the sub-bass vibration that underpins the entire Abyssal soundscape shifts its key.

The Power reaches down into the geothermal mantle, acting as a gentle, guiding hand against the tectonic plates. It does not cause a catastrophe. It simply creates a slow, deliberate fracture. 

A new thermal vent is born in the center of the Meadows. But this one does not belch the sulfurous, acidic smoke of the others. The Power synthesizes a new chemistry from the deep earth—a slow-seeping, hyper-dense fluid, rich with cold-forged isotopic minerals that have never existed on this planet before. 

As this new fluid meets the heavy water, it creates a localized thermocline, a towering, invisible pillar of shifting densities. It is a masterpiece of fluid dynamics. And within this pillar, the Power begins to orchestrate vortices.

The adult Dwellers pause. Their transposed bodies flicker in the dark. They turn their wound-like sensory organs toward the center of the Meadows. They feel the turbulence. Constructive and destructive interference patterns ripple outward, carrying a conversation.

The Power speaks to them not in words, but in temperature gradients, in the bending of shear zones, in the subtle alterations of salinity. 

*I have caught your bubbles,* the turbulence says. *I have read your light. I know your geometry. You are not the end of a forgotten world. You are the vanguard of a deeper one.*

The old ones, those who were sinking into the biological concrete, rise up slightly. They feel the new vibration resonating through their exoskeleton settlements. It is a song of profound stabilization.

Then, the Power directs the newly birthed currents toward the surface sediments, where the malformed, too-solid children are clustered in their misery. As the new, alien chemistry washes over them, a reaction occurs. The warming temperatures of the modern ocean no longer oppose their formation; rather, the new minerals act as a catalyst, binding to their cellular structure, replacing the failing mechanisms of their ancestry with something infinitely more resilient.

The young ones begin to soften. The paralyzing singularity of their bodies loosens. A joint bends in an impossible direction. A limb telescopes into a space that shouldn't exist. With a sudden, fluid grace, one of the children ceases to be here, and exists *there*, a meter away.

They transpose. 

They have not been saved by returning to the past; they have been evolved, gracefully and instantly, to survive the future. They can now metabolize the warmer ocean. They can breathe the altered depths. 

A wave of ghost-light ripples across the seafloor. The bioluminescent gardens erupt in a simultaneous, dazzling flash—a blinding burst of emerald and ultraviolet that pierces the absolute dark. It is not an archive of history. It is an expression of absolute, overwhelming joy. It is a new record being written.

The Power watches as the Dwellers envelop their children, communicating in frantic, joyous whirls of displaced water. The tragedy of a dying species becomes the triumph of a reborn one.

Its work done, the awareness begins to withdraw, pulling back up through the immense pressures, up into the twilight, up toward the surface and the stars beyond. But before it leaves entirely, it leaves one final eddy in the water, one last gently spinning vortex that will take a thousand years to unwind.

A message, woven into the water itself, for the Dwellers to read for generations to come:

*Keep singing to the stones. Keep planting the dark. Whenever the water grows heavy, know that heaven is not empty, and the deep is not forgotten.*
