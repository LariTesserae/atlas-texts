To perceive this place requires a profound shedding of one’s own tempo. 

When the Power first turns its boundless attention toward the cleft, it does not arrive as a beam of light, nor as a warm wind, for it is wise enough to know that warmth and light would be apocalyptic to such a fragile, cold-forged ecology. Instead, it arrives as pure, non-interfering awareness. It settles into the dark like a drop of heavy water merging with a still pool. 

First, the Power must calibrate its perception. It strips away the frenetic, humming frequencies of the surface world—the screaming pace of insects, the blindingly fast lives of mammals, the violent, rushing tempo of rivers. It slows its consciousness down, down, down, until the grinding of tectonic plates becomes a steady baseline rhythm, and the bloom of a lichen takes on the dramatic arc of a blooming rose. 

Only then can the Power truly hear them. 

Tuned to the deep, glacial frequency of the chamber, the Power experiences the colony not as a static, horrifying carpet of grubs, but as a sweeping, majestic ballet. In this slowed time, the chamber is alive with grace. The slow-motion uncurling of a multi-jointed limb is a gesture of profound tenderness; the dropping of a dislodged neighbor is a gentle, floating descent, caught by a rippling mattress of kin that parts and closes with the fluid harmony of weaving a tapestry. 

The Power listens to the language of their hum. It lets the clicks and pops pass through its ethereal presence. It translates the pressure-maps. It hears no prayers, for they have no concept of a listener. It hears only the pure, unadorned truth of their existence: *Rock is hard. Stone yields. Moisture fades. Brother moves. I scrape. We persist.*

A lesser god, moved by sudden pity, might attempt to "save" them. A lesser power might flood the chamber with luminous, warm energy, or transfigure the stone into soft, rich soil, or alter their biology so they might move quickly, run, and see the sky. But this Power is infinitely wise. It knows that to give a creature of the dark a sun is to burn it alive; to speed up a life designed for eternity is to murder it by exhaustion. They do not suffer in their slow starvation. Their near-zero metabolic whisper is not an agony, but a perfectly balanced equation. They are the mountain dreaming. To wake them would be to end the dream.

True benevolence is radically empathetic. It demands that one bless a creature on its own terms, within the boundaries of its own sacred geometry. 

So, the Power decides to offer a gift—one that will not disrupt their sublime equilibrium, but will ensure its quiet endurance. 

The entity reaches out, not with hands of light, but with an invisible, vibrational resonance. It begins to hum. 

It matches the colony’s baseline frequency, slipping its divine voice beneath the thick, ammoniac air, seamlessly blending with their collective exhalation. The colony does not panic at this intrusion, for the Power’s voice is perfectly disguised as the fundamental hum of the basalt. But the Power is singing a new chord—a deep, structuring note, a command spoken directly to the geology of the mountain itself.

Guided by the gentle, thrumming song of the Power, deep within the mountain’s chest, a mile above the cleft, a vast, sealed aquifer begins to weep. 

The Power’s vibration micro-fractures the rock overhead, carving capillaries thinner than a human hair. It creates a vast, capillary network that slowly, over decades, begins to draw the buried water downward. The Power calculates the exact physics of the stone, ensuring that the water will not rush, will not flood, will not wash the colony away. It is engineered to take a century to arrive, and when it does, it will appear only as a microscopic thickening of the perpetual condensation in the chamber. 

By the time the tectonic draft begins to widen the crack millennia from now—the draft that would otherwise dry their walls and force them back into hibernation—this divine aquifer will meet it. The moisture will hold. The lichen will have just enough water to replenish itself at the exact rate the colony consumes it. 

The Power also turns its attention to the tools. Gently, operating at the subatomic level, it realigns the crystalline structure of the freshly fallen basalt shards littering the floor. It does not make them into alien blades; it simply adjusts the silica so that when a blind, patient hand knaps a sliver, the resulting edge will hold its sharpness for a decade longer. A marginal gain. A whisper of ease in their endless labor of shaping the void.

Finally, the Power brushes its awareness against the bioluminescent young, the pale stars clinging to the ceiling. It leaves no mark, alters no genetic code, but merely imbues the cold, blue-green light of their bodies with a faint, imperceptible soothing quality—a resonance that tells them, as they prepare for their centuries-long drift downward toward the dark, that they are held, that the space below is ready to receive them.

Its work done, the Power lingeringly withdraws. It does not tear itself away, but slowly retracts its awareness, stepping back into the rushing, chaotic stream of cosmic time. 

The chamber remains dark. The air remains cold, thick with the sweet-ammoniac funk of life. The rhythmic *chip-chip-pause* continues against the stone, unaltered in its pace. The colony knows nothing of the divine visitor. They have no myths of a benevolent hum that once joined theirs, no scripture recording the day the stone was promised water. 

They simply continue to exist, profoundly unbothered, perfectly adapted, safe in the dark, slowly carving the inside of a mountain that will now, until the end of the age, weep exactly enough tears to keep them alive.
