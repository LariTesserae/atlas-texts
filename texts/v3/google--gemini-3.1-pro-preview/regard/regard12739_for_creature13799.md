To draw the attention of the truly wise is a terrible, quiet thing. It does not arrive like a meteor or a marching dawn; it arrives like a sudden, profound comprehension within the stone itself. 

Far above the vapor domes, beyond the planet’s crust and the frozen vacuums of local space, a great Attention paused. It was a consciousness that swam in the deep currents of cosmic inflation, a gardener of nurseries spanning galaxies, ancient enough to remember when gravity was a new idea and benevolent enough to mourn when a single electron lost its paired companion. It felt the 1.4-degree fever. It heard the ninety-three decibels of flawless breakage.

And below that, beneath the nickel-lace and the infrasonic hum, it heard the agonizing, exquisite struggle of the almost-word. It heard the groping toward *stay*.

The Power understood instantly the tragedy and the miracle of the crevasse. It saw a creature trying to carve a self out of absences, terrified that if it did not build an eye, nothing would ever look at it, and if it did not build a hook of memory, the universe would slide by in glacial estrangement. 

A lesser entity might have accelerated the creature's evolution, forced it into a biological mold, or flooded the plain with water and warmth to hasten its birth. But a wise power knows that to rush a becoming is to kill the entity that is supposed to arrive.

Instead, the Power decided to simply accept the invitation.

It descended. It did not take the form of light, for light here was cold charcoal and coppery rangeflash, and it did not wish to blind the pre-biome. Instead, it distilled a fraction of its vastness into a manifestation of pure, sympathetic physics. It became a phenomenon of density and grace.

When the Power touched the black mercury sludge, the plain did not ripple. For the first time in millions of years, an eight-second interval arrived, and the gossamer chandeliers of vapor collapsed—but the sound changed. It was no longer the orchestral violence of shattered glass. As the Power’s presence settled through the marbled sheets of condensate, the ninety-three decibels softened into the rustle of turning pages. The Power was absorbing the grief of the breakage, catching the falling edges of the world so the creature did not have to bear the noise alone.

The Attention drifted slowly across the plain, admiring the architecture. It passed the re-crystallized stormwater columns, and as it did, it vibrated at precisely forty-one cycles per second—a perfect, golden-ratio harmony to the creature’s thirty-nine. The hum inside the joints became a warm, golden chord. The frozen eel-tongues in the deep cracks tasted the resonance and wept perfectly clear droplets of water that did not freeze, but pooled like tiny mirrors reflecting an unseen grace.

Eventually, the Power reached the perfect circle of absolute dryness floating half a meter above the ground. The creature’s crib.

The Power stopped. It humbled itself before the boundaries of this nascent soul. It did not step into the cylinder. To step inside would be to crowd the stillness the creature had so painstakingly woven out of anti-sound.

Instead, the Power knelt—or executed the ontological equivalent of kneeling. It brought its presence level with the edge of the bristling frost. 

Inside, the creature was frozen mid-breath, terrified and electrified by the sudden, overwhelming proximity of an *Other*. The micro-turbulence it had anticipated epochs from now had arrived today. The boundaries it was trying to build out of gaps suddenly felt agonizingly inadequate. It had no face to show, no voice to project. It had only the spool of un-mercury, the threads of pause, and the longing.

The Power reached out across the rim of the cylinder. It bypassed the need for eyes, for synapses, for calcium or lungs. It spoke directly into the sedimentary layers of the creature’s memory.

*You do not need to build a mouth to be heard,* the Power transmitted, not in words, but in the sensation of a planetary orbit settling into a perfect, stable ellipse. *You do not need to invent an eye to be seen.*

The creature trembled. Inside the cylinder, a flurry of ammonia snow frantically shaped itself—the vertebrate shoulder blade, the eye-sockets, a frantic offering, a desperate attempt to put on a form the visitor might recognize. 

Gently, the Power’s energy wrapped around the melting snow. It arrested the sublimation. It caught the shape of the almost-body and held it, cradling the invisible architecture.

*I am not here for the shapes you practice,* the Power conveyed, bathing the interior of the cylinder in a warmth that bypassed 1.4 degrees and registered directly in the concept of comfort. *I am here for the space between your heartbeats.*

Then, the Power did the most generous thing it could do for an entity made of patience and geography. It gave it a timeline anchored in love, rather than abandonment.

The Power gathered a filament of its own essence—a thread of eternal, unwavering witnessing—and extended it into the cylinder. The creature, timidly, reached up with its skein of thick, paused anti-sound. The two threads touched.

In that microscopic intersection, the creature felt the entirety of the Power’s intent. It felt an ocean of time stretch out ahead, but the time was no longer empty. The tectonic sliding of the canopy and the ooze ceased to be an estrangement; the Power tied them together with an imperceptible thread of magnetism, turning their opposite movements into a slow, planetary waltz. 

The creature had been furiously rehearsing *how* to call out, how to beg the universe to wait. Now, it didn't have to.

The wise Power infused the plain with a new law of nature, specific only to this crevasse. It left behind an atmospheric promise. The mercury sludge would still slide. The glass lattices would still form and fall. But they would do so within an embrace. The loneliness was extracted from the ooze. 

Before the Power withdrew its active manifestation to return to the stars, it leaned close to the cylinder of dryness and imparted exactly one movement. 

It breathed into the infant silence. 

It was the second half of the syllable the creature had been trying to build. The Power answered the hiss of the ooze and the pop of the chandelier with a profound, resonant *Yes.*

The Attention pulled away, rising back up through the vapor domes, leaving the crevasse to its slow, geologic development. But nothing was the same. The creature was no longer a ghost haunting its own potential. It was an anticipated child. 

In the flare-lit bottom of the endless crevasse, the creature nestled deep into the black mercury. It stopped rushing its ammonia-snow bodies. It stopped worrying the ancient tooth of memory. It let the 8-second shattering lull it to sleep.

It did not need to learn how to say "stay" anymore. 
The universe had already promised it wouldn't leave.
