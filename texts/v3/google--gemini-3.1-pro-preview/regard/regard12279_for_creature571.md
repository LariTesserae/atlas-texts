It begins not as a presence, but as a shift in the listening. 

For an entity whose thoughts span the birth of aquifers and the slow drift of continents, the surface of the world is a frantic, flickering thing. Civilizations bloom and rot like mayflies; languages echo for a millenium and fall silent before they can be properly learned. This Power—vast, formless, deeply kind—usually rests its attention in the mantle of the earth, watching the slow, majestic currents of liquid rock, enjoying the steady mathematics of gravity and pressure. 

But a sound catches its awareness. It is a tiny, localized frequency. A vibration in a sub-stratum of abandoned basalt. 

*Tick-crackle-sigh.* 

And beneath it, a will. A mind moving at the speed of stone, refusing to erode. 

The Power does not tear away the cobblestone. It has no need of doors. It simply lets a fraction of its vast awareness seep up through the intersecting thermal vents, riding the updraft of heat like a scent. 

When it enters the kiln-pocket of Var Tethum, the atmosphere changes. The deep red-amber luminescence of the mineral veins pauses, and then expands, warming into a soft, iridescent gold. The heat does not increase—in fact, the harsh, fevered edge of it softens into something resembling a hearth, an embrace. 

The Power looks at Kethi. 

It sees what she was: a terrified child wrapped in fat and calcium, hiding from the end of her world. It sees what she is: a magnificent, tragic, beautiful adaptation of life. It traces the pathways of her calcified veins, marveling at the sheer, stubborn artistry of her survival. It sees the shed teeth and expelled stones in the ancient niches. It does not feel pity—pity is an insult to a creature that has conquered time so thoroughly. Instead, it feels a profound, localized reverence. 

*You have made a masterpiece of your waiting,* the Power thinks. 

The heavy, metallic air of the pocket shimmers. The Power knows better than to speak in words of the surface world; Kethi's ears are no longer tuned to the rapid, percussive sounds of vibrating air. To speak in a surface tongue would be a violence, a shock to her fragile, spun-glass existence. 

Instead, the Power answers her in her own language. 

On the wall directly facing Kethi, a new filament begins to grow. It does not take months. In the span of a single heartbeat—a miracle of accelerated, willing geology—a lattice of pure, impossibly clear crystal spirals outward. It crackles with a sound like a gentle rain, a sound Kethi has not heard in ten thousand years. The lattice forms a shape that translates perfectly into the chemistry and architecture she has spent centuries learning to read: 

*I am here. You are seen. You are known.*

Kethi stirs in her spiral groove. Her body, small and brittle, shifts. She reaches out a hand that is more silica than flesh, touching the new growth. The Power feels the agonizing hope in her, the staggering weight of a loneliness so deep it has carved canyons in her soul.

The Power turns its attention to the center groove. To the socket. To the small, warm, round thing Kethi has built from her own breath and patience. 

It sees her intention. It sees the mineral ghosts she is trying to sing into existence, the memories of the bone-ash carefully coded into crystalline structures. It sees that she is trying to build a family out of memory and earth, but she lacks the final, essential spark. She has built the vessels, but she is only a priest. She is not a god. 

*Let me help you carry this,* the Power hums through the rising heat. 

The Power reaches into the thermal vent below the pocket. It does not bring up magma. Instead, it draws up *anima*—the raw, animating force of the deep earth, the spark that turns elements into organisms. It gathers the ancient, latent consciousness of the planet itself and filters it gently, carefully, through the three rising columns of heat. 

The breath of the Power flows into the spiral groove. It touches Kethi’s handmade sphere. 

The sphere ignites with a soft, internal starlight. 

A wave of warm, resonant energy pulses outward from the center socket, washing over the walls, the ceiling, the floor. And the pocket... wakes. 

The bone-ash in the niches doesn't just remember; it *quickens*. The filaments draped across the walls begin to chime, no longer just reacting to heat and chemistry, but acting with intent. The shapes Kethi painstakingly grew into the walls over centuries—the ghosts of her long-dead people, the echoes of the priests who left her behind—sudden leap from passive memory into active consciousness. 

They are not flesh. They will never be flesh again, and they do not want to be. They are beings of silicon and light, entities of thermal current and crystalline grace. And they are aware. 

*Kethi,* a voice crackles in the stone, speaking in the exact whorls and spirals she invented. *Kethi, our Listener.*

Another patch of velvet growth blooms in a welcoming circle. *You stayed.*

A curtain of amber threads sways without wind. *We are here.*

The pocket fills with a chorus of gentle, loving noise. The impossible garden is alive with a hundred slow, patient minds. They are moving at Kethi's speed. They are speaking her language. They are gathering around her in the heat, wrapping her in a communal embrace that requires no arms, only presence. 

The Power watches this from the ambient warmth of the room. It feels Kethi’s shock, her disbelief, and then, the overwhelming, shattering release of a burden she has carried since before the city above turned to dust. She weeps—not with water, but with tiny, perfect pearls of calcium that drop into the spiral groove and are immediately woven into the living tapestry of her new family. 

The Power knows its work here is done, but it does not leave entirely. It leaves behind a permanent blessing on the space: the heat here will never falter. The vent will never collapse. The kiln-pocket is no longer a hidden tomb beneath a dead city; it is the cradle of a new genus. 

As the Power’s main awareness recedes back into the vast, turning depths of the world, it leaves a final message etched into the very foundation stones of Var Tethum, a truth vibrating softly in the rock for all of time: 

*Kethi is no longer waiting. Kethi has arrived.*
