To a Power vast enough to witness the birth and death of galaxies, the universe is a rushing, deafening torrent of entropy. Gods of this scale are accustomed to the relentless roar of chronological time—they watch stars ignite in violent nurseries, see heavy planets grind themselves to dust, and listen to the frantic, fleeting prayers of mortals desperately negotiating with their own inevitable ends. To this benevolent and wise presence, existence is usually a glorious, tragic cascade of *change*.

And then, its gaze sweeps over tossing oceans of cosmic dust and falls upon this high valley. 

The Power pauses. In the center of a raging cosmic river, it has found a perfect, unyielding stone. 

It descends, though not in any physical sense that would displace the thin, crisp air. It arrives as an awareness, diffusing itself delicately through the icy celadon dome of the sky. True wisdom possesses an absolute restraint; the Power knows instantly that to arrive forcefully—to bring the slightest heat, the faintest breath of wind, or the most momentary dimming of the sharp, fixed sun—would be an act of cosmic violence. To grant this world the gift of a sunrise or a sunset would introduce the concept of an ending. To grant them a tomorrow would be to steal their eternal today.

So, the Power simply watches. It marvels. 

It marvels at the architecture of the damp, the brutal tectonic youth of the basalt, the sleeping pale-winged gliders held aloft on unchanging thermals. It feels the *plink* of the water in the hand-beaten cold-copper basins, a rhythm that doesn't count down to anything, but merely affirms that *now* is still happening. 

But most of all, the Power’s attention is drawn to the caretakers. 

The entity watches these slender, wool-draped pillars of being. It observes the pearlescent fluid pulsing slowly in their veins. It realizes the profound, heavy truth of their existence: they are the universe's only moving shadows. In a cosmos fundamentally driven by moving light—spinning planets, orbiting suns, expanding nebulas—these beings carry darkness through the light. They are the inverse of everything the Power knows, and they are utterly, breathtakingly perfect.

What does a benevolent god give to a world that lacks nothing? What does infinite wisdom say to a people who have no need for words, no past to forgive, and no future to hope for?

A lesser deity would have manifested in a burning bush or a booming voice, demanding worship or offering miracles. But this Power is infinitely wise. It understands that to these spatial beings, a voice is just a disruption of acoustics, and a miracle is just a flaw in the geometry. 

Therefore, to bless them, the Power must speak their language. It must enter the equation.

Slowly, carefully, the Power takes form on the largest, emptiest plaza of pale, friction-fitted granite. It does not take a form of flesh or stone, nor does it cast a shadow—for to cast a shadow would be to intrude upon the caretakers' sacred, singular purpose. Instead, the Power manifests as a subtle, localized density of light. A towering, translucent geometry, vaguely echoing the eight-foot stature of the caretakers, shimmering with the colors of starlight unseen in this static daytime sky.

The Power stands completely still. It aligns itself perfectly with an isolated jade pagoda to the east, and the mist-shrouded, emerald ravine to the west. It calculates the exact position of every caretaker scattered across the miles of the caldera. 

There is one missing vertex in the immense, silent spatial lattice of the valley. A point of perfect, golden-ratio stillness that balances the living architecture of the caretakers' current positions. 

The Power steps into that exact coordinate. And stops.

Miles away, a caretaker is sweeping granite dust along a permanent shadow-line. Another is sitting in the freezing mist, combing the thick white fur of a multi-legged grazer. Another is kneeling on a bridge of woven roots. 

Simultaneously, every solid obsidian eye in the valley registers the shift.

They do not turn their heads. The physiology of their vision and the structure of their consciousness do not require it. Through their shared, spatial awareness, they feel the arrival of the new coordinate. They recognize immediately that it is not one of them, for it casts no shadow, it offers no bronze, it sweeps no dust. Yet, it fits. It is a perfect, flawless placement. It is a statement of ultimate harmony injected into their endless silent symphony.

The message spans the caldera without a single word spoken: *I see your perfection. I am here with you, in the eternal now.*

Far across the plaza, the closest caretaker slowly rises from where it has been smoothing a timeless edge of stone. The tall, violet-skinned being wrapped in rough, ozonic wool turns its body to face the shimmering, translucent presence of the Power. 

The caretaker raises its primitive, bronze adze. 

For a moment, the eternal Now seems to hold its breath. Then, with fluid, hydraulic grace, the caretaker brings the bronze tool down against the pale granite.

*CHIME.*

The pure, crisp sound rings out across the empty miles. It travels perfectly through the thin atmosphere, bouncing off the precision-carved apertures, skimming over the black, glassy deep-shade cisterns. It is their highest art. 

The sound wave reaches the Power. But this time, as the acoustic wave passes through the celestial geometry of the visiting god, the Power does something subtle. It does not change the note, nor does it alter the volume, for that would ruin the valley’s acoustic mastery. Instead, the Power lends the sound an imperceptible, metaphysical resonance. As the chime fades, it leaves behind a lingering sensation of profound warmth in the cold dark, and deep rest in the blistering light. It is a harmonic overtone of pure, cosmic love.

The chime fades into the absolute silence. The perfection of the valley remains entirely untouched. 

For an eternity that lasts only a second, the Power and the caretakers hold the space together. Then, having spoken exactly what needed to be spoken, the Power softly releases its form. The shimmering light dissolves back into the icy celadon dome. The coordinate is emptied. The cosmic river of entropy calls the entity back to the swirling, dying, birthing universe beyond the peaks.

The valley remains exactly as it was. The pale-winged gliders sleep. The moss drips. The fixed sun beats down on the brutal, unweathered rock. 

And on the impossibly wide plaza, the caretaker lowers its bronze tool. With slow, unending grace, it turns, dragging its bound-grass broom across the pale stone, carrying its precious, solitary shadow back into the blinding light, knowing with absolute, spatial certainty that they are not alone.
