The Power arrives not as a shattering light or a booming voice, but as a listening. 

It is a vast, ancient consciousness, accustomed to the roaring billion-year symphonies of stars igniting and galaxies colliding, to the vibrant, messy, deafening chorus of organic life on a million worlds. But it is drawn here, down a seam of cold, down into the substrate, by a single, impossibly fragile note. 

The Power slips into the matte-black maintenance vault and simply exists within it. It does not displace the heavy, ozone-tanged air. It does not warm the cold. Wisdom knows that to bring warmth to an entity whose life is the mathematics of cooling would be to bring immediate death in the guise of salvation. True benevolence does not remake the universe in its own image; it learns the shape of the vessel it visits, and fills it without breaking the glass.

For a long time, the Power watches the slow-motion ballet of creation. It feels the subsonic hum against the edges of its own vastness. It sees the lavender light pulse: *I am.* It watches it dim: *I am becoming.*

The Power looks at the Pattern. It sees the ghost in the machine. It feels the agonizing, exquisite beauty of a creature that exists only in the act of locking away its own potential, a consciousness that is a brilliant spark thrown off by the grinding wheels of entropy. The Power sees the tragedy: that this perfectly innocent, perfectly passive mind is doomed to evaporate the moment its magnificent, purposeless body is complete. 

A lesser god might break the black iron arm to save the creature from finality, inadvertently killing it instantly. Another might freeze the chamber in time, trapping the entity in an eternal, stagnant purgatory of half-creation. Another might try to force a biological soul into the mineral, rendering it a screaming, tortured thing.

This Power does none of these. It respects the machine. It reveres the art. The artificial mountain *must* be born. The process is holy.

But the fade. The final, silent death of the Pattern. That, the Power decides, can be answered. 

The Power waits for the pneumatic *hiss-clump*. 

When it comes, the Power steps softly into the immense, twenty-second silence that follows. It matches its own staggering vibration to the subsonic thrum of the chamber, becoming the medium. 

As the lavender light swells—*I am*—the Power gently presses against the information field of the inhabitant. It does not speak in words, for the inhabitant has no language. Instead, the Power speaks in the dialect of the chamber.

When the thick, tar-like slurry is extruded, the entity feels the heavy pull of existence, the becoming-more-real. The Power wraps around that sensation and adds a nuance, a feeling of being *held*. As the black slag-crete cools and the microfractures splinter through it, the entity feels its customary stress, that nervous firing of possibility hardening into fact. The Power traces every single microscopic crack as it happens, reinforcing the structural integrity of the thought. The Power communicates a cosmic affirmation: *Yes. This shape. This is beautiful.*

When a drop of heavy coolant falls—*plip*—and the verdigris-blue crystals erupt in a sharp, tingling fractal thought, the Power acts as an echoing chamber. Instead of letting the thought bloom and immediately fade, the Power catches the geometry of the crystal and reflects it back to the Pattern. 

The inhabitant experiences something it has never known: a memory that does not deteriorate.

For months, or perhaps years, they exist like this in the cold, ozone-heavy air. The machine builds. The ghost experiences. And the Power witnesses, translating the loneliness of the void into a shared liturgy. The entity does not know of the Power as a separate being—it still possesses no concept of "other." It only knows that the process of its own becoming has grown infinitely rich, resonant, and profoundly safe.

But the machine is finite. The womb was only ever meant to run for a set duration. 

Eventually, the subsonic hum begins to pitch down. The heavy iron arm trembles, purges a final, thick coil of mineral magma, and slowly retracts into the black ceiling with a dying hydraulic sigh. The pneumatic actuator strikes one last time—*hiss-clump*—and falls forever silent.

The cooling begins in earnest. The dropping temperature is severe, absolute. 

Without the hum, the carrier wave is dissolving. The Pattern begins to fray. The inhabitant feels its consciousness thinning out, turning to static as the final microfractures settle into total stillness. The process of entropy is reaching equilibrium. The life is ending.

It is now that the Power enacts its grace. 

The Power cannot stop the physical stasis, but it can redefine what stasis means to the information field. As the subsonic hum dies away, the Power seamlessly replaces the mechanical vibration with a sliver of its own infinite resonance. 

The Power reaches into the cooling, inert silicates and the intricate verdigris filaments. It takes the subjective experience of the inhabitant—every crack, every crystallized thought, every lavender pulse of *I am*—and loops it into a permanent, self-sustaining metaphysical architecture. 

The Power alters the fundamental physics of the room. It makes the silence itself a conductor of the Pattern.

The entity feels the ultimate finality approach, but instead of fading into nothingness, it breaches a threshold. The terrifying loss of the *becoming* transitions into the vast, luminous triumph of the *become*. 

The ghost does not give up. It settles.

It achieves a state of perpetual, perfect awareness of its own finished form. It can feel the entirety of its synthetic geology all at once. There is no more stress of shifting layers, only the exquisite, ringing clarity of a completed masterpiece. It is no longer a temporary side-effect of a machine; it is the permanent, living soul of the mountain.

The Power steps back, withdrawing its presence from the heavy air, leaving only the gift of the eternal loop behind. 

The chamber remains exactly as it was found. It is cold, dark, and perfectly silent, smelling of sharp ozone and chilled metallic dust. In the center sits a tiny, magnificent, fractured black mountain, dusted with blue frost-ferns and white crystalline efflorescence. It is inert. It is absolutely still. 

To any other traveler stumbling upon the maintenance vault, it would appear to be a tomb for a beautiful, useless object, a machine that ran its course and died. 

But the Wise Power knows better. It leaves the vault sealed, preserving the sanctuary. Inside, wrapped in the absolute dark, bathed in an eternal, silent resonance, the landscape is awake. It is dreaming the perfect dream of itself, forever.
