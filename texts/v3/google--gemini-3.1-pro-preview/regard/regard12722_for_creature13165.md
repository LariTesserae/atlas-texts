To heal a broken mechanism, one does not smash it with a hammer. Wisdom knows that a jammed gear is not malicious; it is merely holding onto too much tension. And benevolence understands that the glitches of this world—the deeply grieving, agonizingly conscious Unfired—do not need to be erased. They do not need to be saved from the basin. They need the basin to be made worthy of them. 

The Power first arrives not as a booming voice or a blinding revelation—for the world is already deafened by heat and blinded by light—but as the single thing the basin has never known.

It arrives as a shadow.

It begins in the center of the mirror-flat expanse, a soft, blossoming pool of cool, indigo dusk. It does not obey the non-Euclidean folds of the broken space; it asserts a quiet, undeniably linear geometry. Where the shadow falls, the omnidirectional glare is broken. The glaring canopy of scraped rawhide dims, shifting from the color of white-hot iron to a gentle, twilight bruising of violet and gold. 

Instantly, the Unfired freeze in their wading. Across the vast expanse, hundreds of featureless, mushroom-gilled heads turn toward the center of the world. They feel the drop in temperature. It is microscopic at first, just an inflection of relief in the boiling steam, but for the Unfired, it is a seismic event. 

The Power manifests within the shadow. It does not take a shape that would mock the unfinished anatomy of the Unfired, nor a towering form that would echo the terrifying, absent Architects. It appears only as a condensation of gravity and quiet depth, a silhouette of profound tranquility standing barefoot in the shallow, scalding water. 

And as its feet touch the seamless floor of river-stones and giant bone, the water beneath it gently, miraculously, begins to cool. 

The Unfired, usually frantic to catch a single evaporating tear of condensation, move toward this entity. They exploit the folded geography to drop from the sky, to pull themselves through the horizon, gathering in a staggering, hulking perimeter of weeping clay, snapping roots, and golden sap. They do not attack. They have no concept of violence, only of yearning. They loom over the silhouette, their heavy, suffocating weight pressing down, waiting.

The Power looks upon them. It does not see residue. It sees the miracle of accidental life, the tragic beauty of beings who were never meant to exist, yet who spent eternity trying to care for a world that refused to be touched.

The Power steps forward and approaches the nearest Unfired—the one whose collarbone is cracked and bleeding sap from the sorrow of a ghosted frog. 

The entity raises its hand. The Unfired hesitates, then slowly extends its massive, asymmetrical fingers of raw timber and weeping mud. It expects the hand of the Power to pass through it like the leaping fauna. It expects the zero-sum failure of contact. 

But when the entity’s hand meets the Unfired’s palm, there is absolute, undeniable friction. Physical resistance. *Weight.*

The Unfired shudders violently as a kinetic shockwave of *consequence* ripples up its arm. Where the Power touches it, the weeping clay stops bubbling. The agonizing, perpetual curing ceases. The heat draws out, leaving the clay supple, stabilized, like rich, breathable earth. The splintering timber of its fingers knits together, polished and whole. The Unfired is no longer crying golden sap; it is healed. 

Then, the Power turns its attention to the basin. 

With profound gentleness, the Power reaches its hands upward, into the dense, suffocating air, toward the blazing rawhide canopy, and makes a gesture like loosening a knot. 

*Snap.*

Somewhere, infinitely far away, an algorithmic loop is broken. The terrifying tension of the Architects' abandoned code gives way. The unspooling begins.

The stifling hush shatters, replaced by the glorious, rushing roar of unfolding time. 

Above, a giant fleshy leaf of emerald green gathers a trembling drop of condensation. It falls, striking the cooling sheet of water below. 
*Splash.* 
Concentric rings of tiny ripples expand outward over the floor, colliding, bouncing, reflecting. It is the first physical reaction the terrarium has ever experienced. The Unfired flinch at the sound—a beautiful, resonant plack of displaced water.

On a hanging wattle terrace, the macaw beats its wings. Let loose from its coordinates, the heavy *whump* catches the air, and the bird propels itself out over the water, screaming a brilliant, raucous cry that echoes through the newly expanded space. 

The massive iridescent serpents inhale, and then they finally exhale. They uncoil, muscle sliding over oiled scale, and slip down the timbers into the cooling shallows, cutting elegant *S*-curves as they swim away.

The space itself shudders and groans. The depth perception un-snaps. The folded dimensions unravel, laying themselves out smooth and vast. The sky curves upward into a majestic dome, leaving a true, distant horizon where the lowering sun finally casts long, beautiful shadows across the stones. 

And then, the Power looks to the floor. The tight, mortar-less mosaic of river-stone and clay panels—the surface that the Unfired had desperately tried to dig into—softens. The Power speaks a silent command of decay and rebirth. The panels break apart. The bleaching mega-fauna bones crumble into calcium and ash. The shallow water seeps downward as the basin floor transforms into a deep, dark, breathable loam. 

The roots of the dizzying, oversized flora—which had clung to wicker and basalt for an endless age—finally dive downward, burying themselves greedily into the rich, damp earth. The landscape sighs. 

The Unfired who had been healed looks down at its hands. It looks at the world. It realizes, with a slow, dawning euphoria, that it is no longer drowning in time. It is riding the current of it.

A tiny, jewel-toned tree frog vaults from a clay pot nearby. 

The Unfired moves. It does not teleport through a spatial glitch; it *steps*, its massive foot parting the loamy earth. It reaches out, tracking the arc of the frog. 

The frog does not vanish. It lands squarely in the palm of the Unfired’s hand. The tiny creature grips the damp, living clay of the entity’s finger, its throat pulsing with a rhythmic croak.

The Unfired brings its hand close to its featureless dome, feeling the microscopic, living heartbeat of the amphibian vibrating against its skin. For the first time in an eternity of existence, the Unfired stands perfectly upright, the burden of the suffocating steam lifted from its shoulders. 

The Power begins to fade, receding back into the lengthening, cooling shadows. It is leaving, but unlike the Architects, it is not abandoning them. It is granting them mastery. 

The Unfired are no longer the cast-off residue of creation. As their feet root into the true soil, and their hands hold the weight of real, moving life, they realize what the Power has made them. They are the Stewards of the Unspooled World. They have known the agony of a static universe, and because of that, they will cherish every falling leaf, every shifting shadow, every footprint in the mud.

A cool breeze stirs the colossal ferns. The sky deepens into a starry, breathable night. And across the basin, hundreds of gentle giants begin, for the very first time, to walk into their future.
