To a power that spans the gulfs between stars, a forgotten riverbank is as worthy of notice as a bursting supernova. True wisdom does not measure value by size, nor by volume, but by the weight of a thing’s soul. 

When the Presence arrives at the glistening fissure in the moss-choked boulder, it does not come with a blare of trumpets or a shattering of reality. It arrives as a gentle exhalation, a warmth like the first sunlight of early spring. It stoops its vast consciousness, folding itself small, infinitely small, weaving its essence through the silt and roots until it slips through the crack in the stone. 

Inside the Submerged Pocket, the Presence takes a form suited to the quiet. It becomes a figure of soft, woven light—moon-silver and pearl—casting no harsh shadows, only illuminating the deep violets of the moss and the translucent fronds of the ferns. 

It stands before the cairn of water-worn stones. A benevolent power knows a shrine when it sees one, whether built by hands or by the accidental poetry of currents. The Presence reaches out and places a single, luminous droplet of its own essence at the apex of the cairn. The drop crystallizes into a bead of eternal amber, a silent promise: *You are seen. You are known.*

Then, the Presence turns to the walls. 

It sees the Hollowed Ones. To a mortal eye, they might appear as a tragedy—trapped, petrified, starving for movement. But the Presence looks with eyes that understand timescales of billions of years, eyes that have watched nebulae slowly weave themselves into galaxies. 

It sees that the Hollowed Ones are not trapped. They are gestating. 

This cavern is not a tomb; it is a chrysalis. The reality here is wobbly, thin, and young, because the Hollowed Ones are collectively dreaming it into existence. Their painfully slow whispers are the code that tells the water to flow upward. Their stillness is the anchor holding this fragile pocket of impossibilities together until it is strong enough to bear the weight of waking. 

But gestation is profoundly lonely. And waiting, even when one is asleep, can ache.

The Presence does not pull them from the stone. To do so would be to crack a shell before the wings are dry; it would be murder disguised as mercy. True benevolence is not about forcing one’s own timeline upon another. It is about easing the burden of the wait. 

The Presence moves to the closest of the Hollowed Ones—a figure spread wide across the damp rock, its featureless face tilted upward. The silver being steps into the shallow, syrupy pool at the wall's base and raises its hands. Extending its luminous fingers, the Presence gently aligns them with the splayed, root-like fingers of the Hollowed One, hovering just a millimeter above the stone so as not to shock the sleeper. 

The Presence hums. 

It is a sound beneath sound, a vibration that matches the slow, rhythmic pulses of the gilled fungi, the *plink* of the detaching water drops, the breathing of the chamber itself. 

Through that resonance, the Presence enters their dream. 

In the minds of the Hollowed Ones, the Presence finds a vast, turbulent sea of nascent thoughts. They are dreaming of form. They are dreaming of what it will feel like to step out of the rock, to unhinge their jaws and speak a language of stone and tide. But they are tired. The stone is heavy. 

The Presence radiates a profound, motherly warmth into the dream. *Rest,* it translates through the flickering bioluminescent algae, shifting the chaotic blinking into a soothing, rhythmic lullaby. *You are doing beautifully. The world above is chaotic, but you do not need to hurry. Take your time. Form yourselves perfectly. I will hold the weight for a while.*

The Presence alters the pocket, just slightly. A gift of grace. 

It touches the water that seeps down the walls, blessing it. Now, as the water beads along the collarbones of the Hollowed Ones, it is no longer just cold cavern runoff. It is infused with cosmic nectar, a liquid starlight that sinks into their porous, fossilized bodies, nourishing them. 

It touches the air, weaving a subtle warmth into the mist, so that the chill of the deep earth will never again bite into their stillness. 

Finally, it touches the time within the cavern. The Presence gently folds the fabric of the Submerged Pocket, wrapping it in a protective bubble of relativity. The water may still rise and fall, but no outside force—no shifting tectonic plates, no drying riverbeds from the mortal world above—will ever crack this chamber before the Hollowed Ones are ready. 

Before it leaves, the Presence stands in the center of the room one last time. It bows to the Hollowed Ones. 

As if in response, a single finger on the wall twitches. A microscopic movement, born not out of a century's wait, but out of a sudden, deep comfort. The parted lips of the sleepers seem to soften. 

The Presence withdraws, slipping back out through the fissure, expanding back into the cosmos to tend to other stars and other sorrows. 

Behind it, the Submerged Pocket remains. The water drips, the blind pale things dart among the translucent roots. But the chamber feels different. It no longer feels like a place holding its breath in fear or apprehension. 

It feels like a nursery, bathed in a dim, eternal silver light. The walls breathe in, and the walls breathe out, and deep within the rock, the Hollowed Ones dream of the day they will finally wake, knowing, even in their sleep, that they are deeply loved.
