To traverse the cosmos is, almost universally, to bear witness to violence. To the great, benevolent consciousness that drifted across the folds of space, life was a beautiful but desperate franticness. It knew the birth of species as a crucible of terror—predators tearing flesh, winters freezing lungs, the raw, evolutionary shrieks of survival echoing across countless lonely spheres. 

And so, when this vast, ancient Power cast its gaze upon the weeping, vertical world of the olive-grey basalt, it did not descend with the thunder of revelation. It paused. It stilled its own cosmic hum, breathless in the warm, heavy moisture of the high-altitude rift.

For a long epoch, by the reckoning of stars, the Power did nothing but inhabit the pearl-grey haze. It diffused its vast awareness through the permanent, mirroring pools. It let its attention drip from the broad, waxy leaves and seep into the charting charts of spongy moss. 

It examined the pale-green, succulent architects of the valley. It watched a trio of stonemasons strike the bedrock, insert the dried wedges, and sit in a waking torpor for weeks to wait for the swell of the water. The Power, which had watched galaxies spiral and collide over billions of years, recognized in these mortal beings a patience that was deeply, staggeringly divine. 

What does a benevolent, omnipotent force do when confronted with a world that requires no saving? 

There was no disease to cure. There was no famine to banish, for the pools were full and the moss was thick. There were no wars to pacify, for the concept of scarcity had evaporated in the endless rain. To grant them advanced technology—the wheel, the forge, the harnessing of the atom—would be an act of profound cosmic vandalism. It would introduce noise to the perfect symphony; it would introduce haste to a people who understood the sanctity of the wait. 

Wisdom, the Power knew, is often the restraint from acting. True benevolence is knowing when a masterpiece is finished.

Yet, a profound love had swelled within the Power. It could not leave this "perfect, heavy heaven" entirely untouched. It wished to leave a gift, not to alter their trajectory, but to ensure their serenity would reign unbroken until the very end of time. 

So, the Power set to work, performing miracles so subtle they masqueraded as the natural physics of the universe.

First, it wove a shroud of kinetic warding into the upper atmosphere. The Power smoothed the planetary winds, ensuring that no sudden climatological shift, no encroaching ice age, and no creeping desertification would ever dry the weeping sky. It reached deep into the mantle of the planet, resting a gentle, metaphysical hand against the grinding tectonic plates beneath the valley. It locked them in a permanent, gentle embrace. The escarpments would never collapse in a catastrophic quake; the sheer cliffs would hold their geometric perfection for eons. The Power ordained that this valley would be an eternal cradle, untouched by the chaotic whims of a churning planet.

Then, the Power decided to speak. 

It did not manifest a burning bush. It did not project a booming voice from the clouds, for these beings had no ears for voices, no concept of words born of throats. To speak in the language of the mammalian would be to terrify them. 

Instead, the Power descended into the vast, stepped plazas of the city. It gathered its boundless love, its profound admiration, and its promise of eternal stewardship, and it translated that immense emotional weight into the only language the valley knew: the stereophonic percussion of water.

Across the sprawling city, thousands of perfectly leveled stone cisterns lay brimming with glass-clear rain. At the exact same moment, the Power laid an invisible, weightless consciousness over the surface of every single pool in the city. 

And it *pressed*.

Just once. A singular, magnificent, harmonic pressure against the water. 

The resulting sound was not loud, but it was absolute. A deep, resonant, ultra-low frequency thrum bloomed from the cisterns. It was a chord of pure, mathematical perfection, a long, sustained *hummmmmmm* that vibrated through the mirrored bedrock, shaking the condensation from the tree-ferns in a sudden, glittering shower of diamonds. It was a note of profound safety. A cosmic lullaby. It carried the message: *You are seen. You are beautiful. You are safe. Continue.*

Every solitary figure in the valley paused. 

The stonemasons froze beside their wedges. The grazers lifted their elegant, cartilaginous heads from the damp earth. The elders resting in the deep faults opened their liquid obsidian eyes.

They did not fall to their knees in worship. They felt no fear. Their acoustic slits trembled, taking in the impossible, perfect resonance of the water. They felt the overwhelming warmth of the vibration seep up through the porous pads of their feet, filling their vascular systems with a sudden, radiant rush of joy. 

Slowly, deliberately, a stonemason on a high, dizzying plaza raised a heavy palm. With the casual grace of a shadow, they brought it down flat against the surface of their local cistern. 

*Slap.*

Across the dizzying drop, another lifted a hand and flicked the edge of a crystalline waterfall. 

*Click.*

Another tapped a basin. Then another. Then a hundred. Then ten thousand. 

The city responded to the Power. A million overlapping, delicate chimes, deep thuds, and splashing rhythms rose up to meet the pearl sky. They wove the Power’s divine, lingering resonance into their own unending percussion. It was a conversation of pure existence—the creator of galaxies and the sculptors of wet stone, playing the same instrument in the heavy, humid air.

Satisfied, the Power withdrew the bulk of its consciousness back to the churning stars. But it left a fragment of its heart behind, woven forever into the permanent, unbothered haze of the valley atmosphere. A silent guardian watching over the slow, pale-green hands of a people locked forever in their serene, weeping paradise.
