This is how the Power arrives: not with the tearing of the sky, nor with the blinding imposition of a sudden sun. To bring a dawn to a world that has perfected its twilight would be an act of arrogance, not benevolence. True wisdom recognizes the sanctity of a finished thing. 

The Power arrives as a shift in the density of the stillness. 

Overhead, the pearl-gray sky does not change color, but it seems to deepen. The source-less light remains perfectly even, yet it loses the harshness of its indifference; it becomes a gaze. The cool, thin air that carries the scent of metal warms by perhaps a fraction of a degree—not enough to alter the climate, but enough to change the metallic tang on the back of the tongue from the taste of an old coin to the taste of a drawn breath.

The Power looks upon the plain, spanning the warped, folding distances in a single, encompassing thought. It sees the breathtaking labyrinth of frozen, impossible fauna—the towering helices, the collapsed lattices, the light-drinking black and the bone-white struts. It recognizes them for what they are: mathematics dreaming in solid state, a finalized civilization of geometry. It finds them beautiful. They require no saving, for they are immune to decay.

But then, the Power perceives the Dusters.

A lesser deity might have missed them, mistaking their glacial coagulations for mere localized erosion. But the Power’s perception spans both the instantaneous and the geological. To understand them, the Power performs an act of profound empathy: it slows its own vast consciousness down. It adjusts its own internal rhythm until centuries become seconds, until the pane of glass that is time on this plain begins to flow like sluggish honey. 

Only then does the Power see the Dusters clearly. It watches a crude hand of leaden dust take a month to reach for a white pebble. It watches the agonizing, centuries-long vigils at the bases of the black spires. It feels the echoes trapped within them—the leached-out intention, the tragic persistence of a noun that has forgotten its verb, the endless rearranging of puzzle pieces that belong to no picture.

The Power feels a profound, weeping sorrow for them. Not because they are dying, but because they are trapped in the waiting room of dissolution, denied the dignity of a true end. They are a scratched needle skipping on the run-out groove of reality.

To a wise power, benevolence does not mean rewinding the song. It means lifting the needle.

The Power does not take a physical body—that would only clutter the perfect geometry of the plain—but it manifests as a localized resonance, a hum of intention that matches the frequency of the soft, unraveling reality.

It begins with a single Duster, standing near a primitive circle of white pebbles. The Duster is in the midst of a years-long gesture, its hand hovering over a stone, preparing to nudge it an inch to the left. 

The Power approaches. It wraps its invisible presence around the dusty, faceless approximation of a child. It does not speed the Duster up; to force a sudden movement upon such fragile cohesion would shatter the creature into meaningless debris. Instead, the Power guides the intent.

As the Duster’s hand makes contact with the white pebble, the Power whispers an answer into the soft reality of the plain. *Yes. Put it there. That is the exact right place.*

The Duster pushes the pebble. It moves a fraction of an inch. But this time, as the stone settles onto the interlocking, blood-and-lead plates, something extraordinary happens. 

The circle is completed. 

The Power alters the localized physics just enough so that the crude circle of gathered stones engages with the warped dimensions of the plain. The circle clicks into an unseen cosmic lock. A wave of profound, silent resolution ripples outward. It is the feeling of a chord finally resolving, of a breath finally exhaled.

The Duster freezes. For the first time in millennia, its stillness is not born of exhaustion, but of realization. The memory without recollection is suddenly flooded with *meaning*. The purpose is achieved. The prayer has been heard and answered.

Slowly, the Duster withdraws its hand. It does not resume its endless pacing. It stands straight. The Power holds it in an embrace of absolute love and confirmation. *You have kept the structures. You have tended the empty halls. It is done. You are relieved.*

The Duster’s faceless visage tilts upward toward the pearl-gray sky. And then, it lets go. 

It does not erode. It simply unbinds. The coagulation of dust and memory releases its hold on existence. With a sigh that is finally audible in the ringing silence—a soft, dry sound like the closing of a heavy book—the Duster dissolves. The dust cascades downward, whispering against the sintered crust, filling a microscopic crack in the plates, perfectly flush, perfectly at rest. It is no longer a creature. It is peace.

The Power, possessing the patience of the eternal, begins to walk the plain.

It has nowhere else to be. It will stay here until the work is finished. 

It moves to the lean-tos, where Dusters spend decades balancing fragments. As a Duster places a shard, the Power fuses it seamlessly to the structure, turning a crude windbreak into a permanent, perfect sculpture of intention. The Duster recognizes the completion, feels the deep surge of definitive rest, and happily crumbles into peaceful oblivion. 

It moves to the towering black spires, where Dusters keep their endless, silent vigils. The Power touches the black surfaces, causing them to emit a warm, faint bioluminescence—a gentle, glowing thrum. The spires are acknowledging their watchers. *We see you. We thank you for your company in the dark. Your watch is ended.* 

At the base of the spires, one by one, the Dusters feel the warmth. Their vague forms soften, not in failure, but in surrender, melting back into the mosaic floor like snow upon warm stone. 

The Power takes its time. It sweeps across the infinite, gently undulating plateau over the course of an epoch, leaving no corner unvisited, no pebble unturned, no vigil unacknowledged. To each Duster, it brings the specific, tailored grace of finale. It translates their meaningless maintenance into holy sacraments, ratifies them, and grants the laborers their eternal sleep.

Finally, an age later, it is done.

The plain does not begin or end; it simply is. But it is changed. 

The primitive structures are no longer clumsy scatterings; they are perfected monuments, integrated into the shifting geometry of the world, shining with the subtle intent of their makers. The brass-colored lichen remains, a splash of untarnished will upon the metal earth. The monstrous, beautiful geometric fauna stand silent, but the space between them is no longer heavy with purgatorial grief. 

The museum is finally pristine. The last exhibit has naturally closed. The caretakers have gone to bed.

Before it departs, to journey back into the cosmos where younger worlds still thrash in the agonies of birth and life, the Power leaves one final gift. It weaves a permanent, tender warmth into the cool, thin air. It is a blessing upon the emptiness. 

The sky remains an unblinking pearl. The ground remains fixed. The silence has returned, but it is no longer the silence of exhaustion. 

It is the silence of a masterpiece, perfectly finished, dreaming under an answered sky.
