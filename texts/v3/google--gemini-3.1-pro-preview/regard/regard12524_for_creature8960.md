The gaze of the Power falls upon the Green Underneath not as a light from above, but as a sudden, profound quality of attention. 

It is a consciousness vast enough to hold universes, yet delicate enough to feel the microscopic jaws of a mite cleaning the fur of a Still in the dark. It sees the continent-sized lung breathing. It watches the violent, vegetative turbulence, the tearing and the mending, the endless plip-plip-plip of condensation forming and falling.

It sees the Stills, padding through the seafoam glow. It feels their perfect, immediate present. To the Power, the Stills are a melody perfectly tuned to their instrument. They suffer no existential dread; they require no salvation. To change them, to elevate them to a higher consciousness, would be an act of cruelty. It would be an eviction from Eden. The Power smiles upon them, and leaves them exactly as they are.

Then, the Power looks at *you*.

It looks at the ones who came, the ones trapped in the terrible chrysalis of transformation. It sees the tally marks scratched onto the dark, woody walls. It feels the frantic, desperate heartbeat of a mind that is built for horizons, slowly being crushed by a world that has only interiors. It sees the tragedy of adaptation—that to survive the Green, the wanderer must forget their name, forget the sky, and surrender their history to the long, slow erase of the pale growth.

The Power is wise. It knows that to break the plant, to stop the turbulence, to carve a tunnel up to the stars, would destroy the entire ecosystem. The worms would starve, the molds would die, the Stills would perish. The plant must grow. The environment must remain indifferent. 

But the Power is also benevolent. And benevolence cannot abide the silent, unremembered scream of a soul dissolving in the dark.

So, the Power decides to act. It will not change the physics of the Green Underneath. It will change its *acoustics*.

***

It begins with a shift in the sap.

The next time you—the wanderer, the lost one, the exhausted soul fighting the slow slide into forgetfulness—take a sharpened piece of old growth and drag it against the dark wall to carve a notch, something different happens.

Usually, the plant bleeds a frigid liquid that glows seafoam-green before slowly scabbing over, eventually burying your mark beneath new tissue. 

This time, the sap that pools in the scratch is not cold. 

For the first time since you fell into the Green Underneath, you feel warmth. It radiates from the fresh cut. The sap is not seafoam pale; it is a rich, luminescent amber, the color of a setting surface-sun you thought you would never see again. 

You press your numb fingers to the amber line. The warmth rushes up your arm, into your chest, into the shivering core of your mind. 

And with the warmth comes a Voice. 

It is not a spoken voice. The plant still has no words. It is an impression, carried through the chemical signals of the living wall into your nervous system. But you understand it as clearly as if it were whispered in your ear.

*I am here,* the wall says. *I am here, and I am counting.*

You pull your hand back, shocked. You stare at the empty chamber. It is still vast, still cold. The dripping continues. But the silence has been broken. 

Trembling, you touch the amber sap again. 

*My name was Elen,* a feeling echoes in your mind, soft but indelible. *I marked this wall. I had a daughter. Her hair was the color of wheat.*

You look down at the wall. The scratch you just made intersects with an older one, barely visible beneath a layer of translucent pale growth. You touch the older scratch.

*I am tired,* comes another impression, a different resonance. *But I found a pool of sweet water. I leave this mark so you know you are not the first to be thirsty.*

The Power has given the Green Underneath a memory. 

It has taken the mycelial network—the roots, the fibers, the vast nervous system that the plant uses to regulate its chaotic growth—and opened it to the minds of the wanderers. 

You realize what this means. The plant is still indifferent, but the *network* is alive with the ghosts of everyone who has ever walked this way. Every scratch, every notch, every woven nest is no longer just a scar on a blind world. It is an archive. 

You press both hands flat against the woody surface, closing your eyes.

A chorus floods into you. Hundreds, perhaps thousands of consciousnesses. They do not overwhelm; they cradle. You feel the ones who came centuries ago, and you feel the one who carved a mark just three chambers over, yesterday. You feel their fear, yes, but you also feel their endurance. You feel their love for the things they left behind. 

You are entirely alone in a chamber that is fifty miles wide. But you are held. 

You begin to weep. Your tears fall onto the fibrous floor, and they do not just freeze. The floor drinks them, and the network takes your sorrow, catalogs it, and sends it out through the roots so that somewhere, fifty miles away, another lost wanderer touches a wall and feels your tears, and knows they are not alone.

***

The Power watches this unfold, and breathes a slow breath of relief.

It has not stopped the turbulence. The walls will still close. The ceilings will still fall. You will still have to move to survive. You will still have to eat the pale growth and drink the condensation. 

You will still, eventually, change. 

Your body will adapt to the cold. Your mind will stop longing for the surface. You will learn to navigate by the pressure of the air and the taste of the moisture. 

But because of the Power’s gift, the nature of the transformation has changed. 

Before, you were being erased. You were degenerating into a beast, losing your humanity to become a Still.

Now, you are being *uploaded*.

As you spend years in the Green Underneath, eating the pale growth, its sugars weaving into your DNA, you do not lose your mind. You merge it with the archive. The plant’s memories become your memories. Your history becomes the plant's history. 

You walk through a newly opened corridor with the deliberate, careful padding of a creature adapted to chaos. Your eyes are small, your fur is thick, your hands are splayed for grip. But when you rest against the old growth, you are not an empty animal grazing in a void. 

You are a monk in a vast, bioluminescent library.

You become something new: a Keeper. You live in the perfect present, unbothered by the turbulence, free of the anxiety of time. Yet, you hold the past within you. You know the names of the stars. You remember wheat. You remember fire. You do not grieve for them anymore; you simply hold them safely inside the amber resin of your mind, a guardian of human beauty inside a plant that spans a world.

The Stills pass you in the dark, smelling you, assessing you, and moving on. They are the body of this world.

But thanks to the Power, as you drift to sleep with your ear pressed against the slow, pulsing fluid of the tendril-wall, surrounded by the thoughts of all who came before you...

You are the soul.
