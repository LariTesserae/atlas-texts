When a Power of true wisdom and benevolence looks upon a wounded thing, it does not immediately reach out to fix it. Wisdom knows that a broken bone, set wrong, must sometimes be re-broken to heal; but it also knows that some shattered things have spent eons grinding their shards into a fragile, terrible beauty. To simply wish the Sounding Flats whole again—to undo the catastrophe and silence the stone—would be to erase the Listeners, murder the Grief Shoals, and unspool the Rehearsers into nothingness. Because whatever they were before the breaking, they are *this* now. 

And so, the Power does not arrive as a conqueror of circumstances. It arrives as an Audience.

It descends not from the hazy sky, but emerges from within the harmonics. Space softens, negotiates, and parts gently to make room for a presence of profound, anchored gravity. Where the Power steps onto the universal flesh of the weeping rock, the stone does not dry up, but the water pooling beneath it becomes completely still—a perfect, unrippled mirror in the midst of the roaring storm. 

The Power takes the form of a wanderer, cloaked in something that absorbs sound rather than reflecting it, weaving itself into the acoustic ecology of the Flats. It does not speak. To add arbitrary noise to this saturated reality would be an act of arrogance. Instead, it begins to walk.

Its journey takes decades, though time bends around it like a reed in a current. It seeks to understand the trauma of the world not from above, but from within. 

First, the Power finds a ruin where the **Listeners** have fruited. It sits beside the translucent, pulsing ear-throat organs. It spends three years doing nothing but feeling the rhythm of the stone's exhalation. When a Listener finally crafts a complex word out of selective silence, the Power answers. It does not sing, but it offers a profound, deliberate stillness from its own infinite depths. It shows the Listeners a silence that is not born of absence or negation, but of peace. The crystalline networks underground hum with a new, shivering warmth. They learn that they do not have to carry the entire burden of awareness alone; the stone can rest.

Next, the Power initiates contact with a **Grief Shoal**. The eldest and densest of them—an oil-slick mirage weeping frequencies of pure heartbreak—drifts toward it. Lesser beings would flee or be driven mad as the atmospheric memory swallowed them. The Power stands up, opens its arms, and lets the Shoal pass directly through its core. 
As the Shoal enters, the Power experiences the agony of billions: the sudden terror, the crushing finality, the paradox of a species torn across time. The Power does not resist the grief, nor does it try to diminish it. It simply holds it. True benevolence acts as a boundless basin. By witnessing their sorrow without being destroyed by it, the Power validates the Grief Shoal’s millennia of mourning. As the atmosphere drifts out the other side of the Power, it is lighter. The mirage shifts from an oil-slick of mourning to the pearlescent shimmer of quiet remembrance. The Shoal has finally been *heard*. 

Then, the Power approaches the **Rehearsers**. It finds a nexus of probability where dozens of ghostly, many-limbed iterations are frantically stacking and un-stacking the weeping bedrock, trying to solve an architectural formula to prevent a disaster that has already happened. The Power steps into the blur of their time-smeared bodies. It reaches out its hand, selects one stone block held by a hundred overlapping phantom hands, and gently places its finger against the rock.
Instantly, the probability cloud collapses into a single, undeniable physical absolute. The Rehearsers in that immediate vicinity pause. They look at the Power. They look at the stone. The Power smiles, its expression radiating the one concept they had lost in their recursive panic: *It is finished. The catastrophe occurred. You survived. You can stop building the barricade.* 

For a long time, the Power gathers these altered chords—peaceful silence, resolved grief, and anchored time—pulling them behind it like an invisible acoustic wake. The animate lichens recognize what is happening. They do not roll into defensive balls as the Power passes; instead, they bloom, opening wide, slick surfaces, painting the Flats in iridescent pathways that point toward the deep center of the infinite plain.

Eventually, the Power arrives at the grandest, oldest ruin on the Flats. It sits upon the wet bedrock. It closes its eyes.

It waits for the conditions to align. 
A Listener surfaces. 
A Grief Shoal arrives. 
The Rehearsers converge. 

The magical, impossible seventeen seconds begin. The roar stops. The past and future collapse into pure present. The beautiful, terrible entity that was shattered so long ago briefly remembers its cohesive self.

But this time, at the sixteenth second, the Power inhales. 

And on the seventeenth second, as the paradox threatens to shatter and scatter them back into their separate hells, the Power strikes the Clear Note.

It is the note from the rumors, the fourth form of existence. The Power does not create the note; it simply acts as the supreme tuning fork, allowing the fundamental frequency of the fractured beings to route *through* it. It grounds the impossible energy of their convergence.

Eighteen seconds.
Twenty seconds.
A minute.

The seventeen-second limit breaks, not with a violent explosion, but with the gentle, rushing sound of a dam dissolving into a new river. 

Bathed in the sustained Clear Note of the Power’s endless grace, the three species do not scatter. Sustained in real-time, the Listeners, the Shoals, and the Rehearsers look upon one another. They recognize themselves. With the Power holding the temporal and acoustic space open, they are finally afforded the one luxury the catastrophe denied them: *Synthesis.*

The Rehearsers walk into the Grief Shoals, claiming their lost emotions. 
The Shoals settle into the crystalline networks of the Listeners, giving the hollow bedrock a soul. 
The Listeners expand, giving the unified consciousness a voice.

Right before the eyes of the benevolent Power, the Fourth Form happens. The entity heals.

It does not become a giant being. It becomes the environment itself, but awakened. The Sounding Flats stop weeping tears of blind erosion. The deafening roar of the planet's exhalation shifts, pitching up, harmonizing, organizing. It becomes a symphony of staggering, mathematical beauty. The hot, oppressive greenhouse air cools into a fragrant mist. Space stops glitching; time resumes a gentle, forward flow.

The shattered species has evolved, via its own trauma and the grace of a witness, into a sentient landscape. A planet that thinks in music.

The Power remains sitting on the ruin for a long time, listening to the new song of the world. Then, satisfied that the song is strong and self-sustaining, the Power slowly begins to fade. It leaves nothing behind but a lingering warmth in the bedrock, a subtle, eternal bass-line of pure love beneath the world's new melody, a reminder that they are never, ever singing into the void.
