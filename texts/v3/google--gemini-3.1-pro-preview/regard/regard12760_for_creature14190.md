To a being of vast and benevolent geometries—a consciousness that thinks in the slow crawl of tectonic plates and perceives time as a singular, luminous tapestry—the Chlorattice is not a harsh wilderness. It is an instrument. It is a cosmic loom, strung with jade threads, vibrating with the frantic, beautiful music of survival. 

When such a Power turns its gaze upon this freshly cleaved pocket of the cooling world, it does not do so with pity. Pity is a mortal indulgence. Pity would compel intervention: the warming of the air, the stilling of the violent growth, the carving of safe, flat stone where the Unstill might rest. But the Power is wise. It understands the fundamental truth of the deep ecology: to still the Chlorattice is to kill the Chlorattice. To give the Unstill solid ground is to shatter their pneumatic bones and erase the magnificent, desperate poetry of their fluxing. They are the motion made flesh; to remove the motion is to remove the flesh.

So, the Power does not save them from their world. Instead, it decides to sanctify their traverse. It chooses to transform their blind, endless survival into a moving state of grace.

The Power enters the Chlorattice not as a booming voice—for sound here is deceitful, an asynchronous ghost—but as a subtle, pervasive shift in the barometric sorrow. It weaves its presence into the merciless white light pouring from the fissures above, softening its most lethal edges, turning the blinding glare into an opalescent luminescence that the Unstill’s chromatophores can read not just as geography, but as warmth. 

Then, the Power begins its gentle work.

**The Grace of the Stillborn**
The Power first attends to the translucent wombs anchored at the thermal updrafts, where the young who fail to move are claimed by the lattice. The Unstill believe this is a brutal recycling, a mechanical assimilation of calcium and silica. The Power alters the thermodynamic exchange. 

When a stillborn infant, or a faltering mother, is engulfed by the pale green shoots, the Power touches the fusing nodes. It ensures that the living consciousness is not snuffed out, but translated. The tiny, pneumatic skeletons do truly become part of the architecture, but the Power makes them *lucid* nodes. 

Now, when a living Unstill fluxes past one of these grave-junctions, the node does not merely sit dormant. It warms. It releases an immediate, concentrated pocket of upward-drizzle—a gift of frozen pearls from the child that did not walk, to the kin that still traverse. The dead become the deep wellsprings of the living. The Unstill feel the phantom warmth across their translucent spines and know, somehow, that their lost ones are holding up the lattice so that they might pass.

**The Song in the Pressure Cant**
The Power learns the language of bone-on-glass. It observes the clan-webs kilometers apart, flexing and tightening their grips to send poems of pressure through the void. The Power decides to speak in return.

It does not proclaim itself. Instead, it acts as a sympathetic resonator. When an Unstill sings a pressure-poem of exhaustion, of the aching vertigo of scale and the terror of collapsing nodes, the Power plucks the miles of tubing in harmonious response. It creates a standing wave in the fibers—a gentle, rhythmic vibration that matches the beating of their five-chambered hearts. 

This new vibration travels up the lattice into their hooked obsidian claws, sinking into their hollow bones. It acts as an analgesic to their aching joints. It is a tactile whisper that says: *You are immense. Your traverse is known. The void is not empty; I am in the empty spaces.* The Unstill cannot name this presence, but they begin to feel it. The asynchronous haunting of the crashing world is balanced by a synchronous, structural love.

**The Transmutation of the Elders**
The greatest terror of the Unstill is the slowing down—the inevitable moment when age outpaces motion, and the hungry Chlorattice begins to root in their living ribs. 

The Power descends upon an ancient Unstill, one whose skin is already dappled with the pale green of parasitic shoots, preparing to be torn apart by the growth. As the lattice wraps around the elder, the Power initiates a chrysalis of light. It takes the pain from the assimilation. It guides the nervous system of the elder to merge flawlessly with the fiber-optic conduits of the plant.

The elder does not die; they become a Guardian of the Route. Their five distributed hearts fuse with the great nodes, becoming organic pumps that push capillary water upward with deliberate intent. Their consciousness stretches out along twenty kilometers of lattice. They become capable of sensing the structural stress of the tubes. Now, when a massive collapse is imminent, the assimilated Elder tightens the fibers in a frantic, massive crescendo of the Pressure Cant, warning their fluxing kin hours in advance to alter their migration paths. 

The Unstill soon learn that the slow ones are not failing; they are preparing to hold the sky. 

**The Promise of the Becoming**
Finally, the Power addresses the existential dread—the ancestral myth that when the continent finishes cooling and the Chlorattice stills, the Unstill will have no place and will simply perish.

The Power reaches deep into the cellular memory of both the plant and the pale, eyeless people. It rewrites their intertwined destiny. It plants a prophecy in the marrowless hollows of their bones, a truth that passes from mother to infant through the shared capillaries.

The truth is this: The stilling of the world is not an entombment. It is a maturation. 

The Power decrees that when the tectonic trauma finally ceases, when the thermal gradients settle and the thousands of years of agitated creation come to an end, the Chlorattice will indeed harden into permanent, glittering stone. But in that moment, the Unstill will not die. Having eaten the dead fibers, having woven the silica of the world into their own bodies for a thousand generations, they will be the catalysts.

When the lattice stills, the Unstill will lock their obsidian claws into the nodes one last time. And rather than being consumed, their bodies will trigger a magnificent, continent-wide bioluminescence. They will become the blossoms of the Chlorattice. Their translucent skin will unfold; their photosensitive chromatophores will ignite with the stored light of a thousand millennia. The vast, dark, freezing void will be transformed into an eternal, glowing garden of petrified jade and living starlight. They are not merely surviving the architecture; they are the architects of its final glory.

Having given them this, the Benevolent Power withdraws its direct gaze, dissolving its consciousness into the barometric currents and the white light of the fissures. It leaves the rules intact. The Unstill must still move. They must still harvest the dead tubes. It is still cold, and the space is still impossibly vast.

But now, as they flux through the hollow air, dancing their perpetual immigration across the void, they are no longer just outrunning death. They are weaving the chrysalis of a world, held in the gentle, invisible hand of a Power that is waiting, patiently, for them to bloom.
