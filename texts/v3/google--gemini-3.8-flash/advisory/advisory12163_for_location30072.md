### 1. Environmental Isolation: The "Zero-Efflux" Exosuit

The ambient air will kill an unprotected human within three breaths—not through toxic gas, but through mechanical drowning and hypothermia. The aerosol is saturated with non-Newtonian sugars and long-chain lipids held in supercooled liquid suspension. Inhaled, this mist will coat the warm, moist surface of your alveoli, emulsifying instantly with your lung surfactants and solidifying into an un-cought-out syrup. 

*   **Closed-Circuit Rebreather (No External Intakes):** You cannot filter this air; filters will glaze over with viscous lipids within two minutes. You must wear a fully hermetic, positive-pressure rebreather. Your exhaled gas must be captured or chemically scrubbed and recycled internally. Not a single bubble of your warm, wet, CO₂-laden exhaust can be vented into the room; doing so will introduce condensation shocks that could cause the microscopic valves around you to misfire.
*   **Active Thermal Shielding (Cool Skin):** The room exists at the brink of freezing and produces virtually no waste heat. Your body radiates approximately 100 watts of infrared heat. To the electron-spin sensitive micro-hairs and osmotic cambium around you, your thermal plume is like bringing an open blowtorch into a server bank. Your suit must be lined with aerogel insulation and an outer chilling layer that keeps the *exterior* surface of your suit matched within 0.5°C of the chamber's ambient temperature. 
*   **Superhydrophobic and Oleophobic Exterior:** The oily aerosol weeping down the walls will adhere to standard nylon, Gore-Tex, or rubber. Once coated, you become a collection surface. Worse, the quartz-carapace groomers may identify you as an encrusted node that requires scraping. Your outer skin must be made of low-surface-energy fluoropolymers (like specially etched PTFE) so the supercooled lipids run off you without pooling.

### 2. Mobility and Footwear: Weight Distribution Without Penetration

The floor is not stone or dirt; it is a yielding, pressurized mattress of living cambium and giant liverwort lobes, threaded with glass-like fiber optics carrying delicate packets of light. 

*   **Pneumatic Distribution Snowshoes:** You cannot wear hiking boots, crampons, or hard soles. Wear wide, flexible "marsh-shoes" constructed of high-density closed-cell silicone foam with soft, textured undersurfaces. 
*   **Non-Newtonian Traction:** The floor is slick with cold sugar-grease. Standard rubber hardens and slides like dry ice on glass at these temperatures. Your footpads must feature micro-suction siping—mimicking the pads of tree frogs or geckos—that displaces viscous fluids upon pressure to achieve mechanical grip without tearing the underlying tallow-like skin.
*   **Pacing:** The floor depresses under your weight and takes *minutes* to rebound. If you stand in one place too long, you will sink into a deep indentation, potentially pinching off the capillary light-tubes beneath you. You must keep moving at a slow, deliberate, metronomic shuffle, allowing your weight to roll smoothly from heel to toe.

### 4. Navigating the Fauna: Behavioral Etiquette

The blind, quartz-like woodlice and the pale ribbon-eels are not predators, but they are relentless automated maintenance subroutines.

*   **Static Grounding:** The ribbon-eels swimming in the brine channels are sensitive to chemical polarity and micro-voltages. If you have an electrical differential between your boots and the brine pools, stepping near one will induce an electrostatic discharge that could cause the ribbons to whip frantically, destabilizing the pool’s ionic balance. *   **The Groomers' Scrub-Cycle:** The multi-jointed, translucent quartz creatures plane off exudate with high-speed mouthparts designed to scrape silica clean. If your suit's fluoropolymer coating is functioning, its scrapers will find nothing to shave away, and its optical pathways will not trigger an alarm. If you panic and kick it, you risk fracturing its brittle quartz carapace; the sound and chemical release of a crushed groomer will bring hundreds more out of the interstitial thickets to process the anomaly.

### 5. Illumination and Sensor Rules

The light in the vault is a weak lavender, pulsed down through amber conduits from an unknown depth above. 

*   **No Broad-Spectrum White Light:** Do not turn on a standard high-lumen halogen or LED headlamp. The plant-machine’s vascular bundles use precise wavelengths of photon packets for internal signaling. Flooding the room with 5,000K white light will cause catastrophic photonic interference—like shining a floodlight directly into a fiber-optic receiver.
*   **Narrow-Band Deep Amber or Far-Red Vision:** Equip your helmet with narrow-band illumination tuned strictly to far-red (730 nm) or low-intensity amber, frequencies that the logic-fibers disregard as background attenuation. Better yet, navigate using passive low-light amplification or thermal sensors (calibrated to the fractions-of-a-degree variance between the structural cambium and the weeping exudate).
*   **Expect Parallax Distortion:** The lavender twilight creates bizarre, sharp shadows that do not soften. The thick, supercooled aerosol has a high refractive index; distances will appear warped, and objects submerged beneath the puddles of non-Newtonian sugar will appear closer and shallower than they actually are.

### 6. Contamination Protocols: Protecting the Genesis

Your greatest threat to this space—and your greatest legal or existential liability—is that this room has **no immune system**. 

It has never seen weather. It has never known decay. There are no bacteria, no fungi, no rot. Every cell is embryonic; not a single leaf has aged or died. If you introduce a common mold spore from your home world, or a single colony of *Staphylococcus* from your skin, you could initiate an uncontrolled biological cascade that rots the entire computing lattice from within before it ever reaches its differentiated state.

*   **Gamma-Sterilization of All Gear:** Before descent, your suit and equipment must undergo a complete cold-plasma or gamma-irradiation sweep. 
*   **Carry No Consumables:** Do not bring water bottles, nutrient paste, or organic tools into the chamber. A leak in an energy gel packet would introduce terrestrial sugars that could permanently derail the local osmotic gradients.
*   **Emergency Extraction Plan:** You cannot run. If you fall, the liverwort will yield, holding you down in its slow-rebounding depression while the supercooled grease pools around your visor. If an alarm sounds, or if you feel the tectonic vibration change pitch—shifting from a steady thrum to a rapid, cycling whine—it means the embryonic tissue has received its instructions from above. Differentiation is beginning. The room will either expand outward with explosive hydraulic force to displace more permafrost, or lock solid into its crystalline logic architecture. 

If that happens, you must pull your tether and trigger extraction immediately. You do not want to be caught inside this tissue when it decides what it is meant to become.
