To visit this place as an ordinary human is to enter a world that is not hostile to your biology, but entirely indifferent to your structural integrity. The atmosphere will let you breathe, but the geometry will cut you to ribbons if you lose your footing, and the sheer, doorless scale will exhaust your mind long before you cross a single terrace.

Here is how you must prepare.

---

### 1. Armor Against the Architecture: Cut-Protection
The most urgent, lethal threat in this city is not a predator or a poison—it is the edges. The megacity was cleaved into existence with atomic tolerances; the corners of curbs, lintels, and esplanades are as sharp as freshly fractured obsidian or precision-milled surgical steel. A casual stumble against a low wall will not bruise you; it will sever tendons or cut through your denim down to the bone.

*   **Aramid/Dyneema Outer Layer:** You must wear full-body, high-level cut-and-puncture-resistant clothing. Garments woven from ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) or Kevlar are mandatory for your trousers, sleeves, and gloves. 
*   **Armored Extremities:** Shin guards and forearm guards (rigid plastic or light alloy) are essential. When climbing or traversing uneven ground, human instinct is to brace oneself against a surface; in this city, bracing a bare palm against a 90-degree corner could amputate your fingers.
*   **Boots:** Heavy, puncture-proof soles with high ankle support and deep, tacky rubber tread. The surfaces are black metamaterial coated in a microscopic layer of aggressive copper-colored lichens and perpetual condensation. It will feel like walking on wet, greased glass rimmed with razor blades. 

### 2. Sensory Gear: Overcoming the Light-Sink
The structures absorb seven-eighths of all visible light. Your human depth perception relies on ambient bounce, cast shadows, and texture gradients. Here, those cues are swallowed. Looking at a tower will feel like staring into a flat, 2D hole cut into the sky. You will misjudge distances by hundreds of meters; you will not see where a flat terrace suddenly drops three hundred meters into a sunken courtyard.

*   **Active LiDAR and Depth-Mapping HUD:** A standard optical flashlight is nearly useless here—the black walls will swallow the beam, leaving you with an illuminated circle on the floor and absolute void everywhere else. You need a wearable LiDAR unit or a stereo-depth imaging visor that overlays a wireframe grid onto your field of view, artificially outlining drops, structural corners, and changes in grade.
*   **High-Contrast, Polarized Amber Eyewear:** The sky is a flat, pearlescent wool sheet, while the city below is a black void. This extreme contrast will cause profound eye strain and headaches within hours. Tinted lenses that filter the leaden glare and enhance low-contrast greens and coppers will keep you oriented.
*   **Acoustic Damping (Sub-Bass Filters):** The hollow rings and monolithic spires hum at infrasonic frequencies that you feel in your skeleton and teeth. Prolonged exposure to low-frequency vibration induces nausea, vestibular disorientation, irrational panic, and eyeball resonance (which causes visual hallucinations). Bring musician’s acoustic earplugs or active noise-canceling headsets tuned specifically to damp low-frequency ground hum.

### 3. Climate, Moisture, and the Purge Cycle
The air is warm, thick, and stagnant, redolent of distilled steam and bruised vegetation. Sweat will not evaporate easily from your skin, yet you will be constantly assaulted by water from above.

*   **Managing the 20-Minute Purge:** Every twenty minutes, the metamaterial releases its condensation in a sudden, violent, hissing deluge. You cannot stop it, and you cannot easily hide from it (there are no roofs or doorways). 
    *   Carry a lightweight, deployable, structural rain canopy made of heavy-gauge ripstop. 
    *   Never rest directly beneath a cantilever, a hovering viaduct, or an inverted pyramid; when the purge cycle hits, the runoff from a three-hundred-meter slab does not fall as mist—it falls like a dropped bathtub.
*   **Skin and Foot Care:** Bring massive supplies of friction-barrier creams, talc/drying powders, and medical-grade anti-fungals. In this humidity, combined with cut-proof, water-resistant clothing, your feet will begin to macerate (trench foot) within 36 hours if not obsessively dried and changed.

### 4. Sustenance and Biological Safety
The plant life has colonized the city in mere months, growing without soil by drinking condensation off cold plates. It is aggressively opportunistic.

*   **Respirator with Organic Vapor Cartridges:** While the air is breathable, you are breathing the aerosolized exhalations of entirely alien, fast-growing flora. The smell of "distilled vapor and bruised leaves" points to high concentrations of volatile organic compounds (VOCs). Without a respirator, you risk chemical pneumonitis, severe allergic contact reactions in your bronchial tubes, or spore inhalation from the copper ferns.
*   **Water Filtration:** Do not drink the tea-colored pool water directly, no matter how clear it looks in the math-cut channels. It is saturated with runoff from a synthetic metamaterial of unknown composition, rich in complex hydrocarbons or exotic compounds that your liver cannot process. You need a closed-loop water recovery system, or a multi-stage purifier that includes reverse osmosis and activated carbon.
*   **Food:** Bring every calorie you intend to consume. The plants—fat, succulent, felted, and purple-veined—are completely unstudied. Do not forage. Even brushing bare skin against the thick, cabbage-like leaves could result in chemical burns or contact dermatitis.

### 5. Movement and Navigation: Surviving the Indifferent Void
The city has no streets scaled for legs. A single plaza is a half-mile across; an esplanade runs for two hours without a single break, intersection, or stairwell. There are no handrails.

*   **Tethering and Fall Arrest:** The architectural wafer-bridges hover without supports; courtyards drop straight down into reservoirs miles below. A fall arrest harness and a heavy-duty suction/magnetic ascender kit are necessary. However, test your gear immediately: traditional pitons cannot penetrate the atomic-milled material, and standard magnetic gear may not adhere to the non-ferrous metamaterial. Vacuum-seal anchors or friction-camming units placed in the drainage channels may be your only mechanical anchors.
*   **Inertial Navigation Systems (Dead Reckoning):** Magnetic compasses may spin uselessly near the humming spires and thermal-differential plates. GPS is nonexistent. You must carry an inertial dead-reckoning unit (accelerometer- and gyroscope-based) strapped to your pack, paired with physical trail markers. 
*   **Physical Markers:** Use high-visibility, chemically inert chalk or adhesive micro-reflectors to mark your path. Do not rely on "that big black tower" as a landmark; they repeat with subtle, mind-bending mathematical variations, and in the leaden twilight, one inverted pyramid looks identical to another three kilometers away.

### 6. The Pale Fauna
The creatures you see—the long-legged, eye-less pale entities and the translucent gliders—are few, quiet, and wary. 
*   They step high to avoid slipping; watch their paths. Where the pale creatures walk, the footing is likely secure.
*   Do not corner them under the lintels. Creatures that evolve without eyes in an open, echoing plain navigate entirely by vibration, air displacement, and sound. Keep your movements slow, avoid sudden sharp claps or percussive noises, and maintain your distance. They are not hunting you, but you are an erratic, noisy anomaly in their silent garden.

### 7. Psychological Survival: The Terror of Non-Scale
Human minds are built for architecture that remembers the body. We look for doors, knobs, steps that match the reach of our legs, ceilings that acknowledge our height. 

In this place, you will experience a profound, creeping agoraphobia mixed with architectural vertigo. There are no doors. There is no inside. You are permanently *outside*, trapped on a colossal platform built for an intelligence that did not walk, did not shelter from the rain, and did not gather in crowds. 

*   **Bring an enclosed shelter:** A fully opaque, small bivy-tent. When you sleep, you must not look at the three-hundred-meter black cutouts against the bruised clouds. If you lie on a miles-wide esplanade beneath an inverted pyramid balanced on a point the width of an ankle, the sheer wrongness of the geometry will keep your nervous system in a state of sustained fight-or-flight. 
*   Create a micro-environment. Crawl into your nylon tent, turn on a warm-spectrum lantern (2700K to fight the leaden light), put in your earplugs, eat food that smells like home, and remind yourself that the city is not glaring at you. It is merely waiting, entirely completed, for whatever it was actually built for.
