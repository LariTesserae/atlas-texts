## Phase 1: Temporal Dehydration Protocol (Begin 7 years before arrival)

### Day -2,555: The First Drink You Will Never Have Again
At exactly midnight on the anniversary of the last time you cried without knowing why, collect a single tear in a container made from something that never wanted to contain anything. Place it in your mouth but do not swallow. Hold it there for seven hours, seven minutes, seven seconds, while reciting the names of all the oceans you've never seen in languages that never had words for water. When you inevitably swallow, understand that you have just taken your first step backward into the Tomb.

### Months -720 to -360: Learn to Dream Thirst
Gradually replace all liquid in your diet with the sound of water described by someone who has never heard it. Start with drizzle remembered by desert dwellers. Progress through waterfall described in sign language, finally working your way to the shape of tsunami in the equations used to predict earthquakes.

### Year -3 to -1: Temporal Dry Dock
Stop aging. This is not metaphorical. The Tomb does not take kindly to visitors who insist on bringing their own entropy. Methods vary by culture and species, but all successful techniques share one common element: a moment of perfect aesthetic appreciation for exactly how your particular form of decay harmonizes with the cosmic background radiation. Miss this moment by even a Planck length and you'll arrive 2.7 seconds too late in every conversation you'll have there for the next 13.8 billion years.

## Phase 2: Physical Recomposition (Cannot begin before Phase 1 produces first symptoms of conceptual drought)

### Operation Hydrate-in-Reverse
Subject yourself to increasing doses of absolute dryness. Start with salt flats at noon, shadows of shadows at twilight, gradually working up to the space between hydrogen atoms in quantum vacuum. Stop when you begin to sweat perfect cubes of empty space.

### Bone-Becoming-Wave Conversion
Your skeleton will be problematic. The Tomb's pressure differential between memory and actuality tends to irradiate calcium-structured memories into visible light. Gradually replace your bones with recordings of waves breaking in places that no longer exist because the continents forgot their positions while dreaming of each other. Vinyl works best for femurs, cassette tapes for ribs (the hiss adds authenticity to future breaths), and for your skull, use the sound of the last whale-song ever sung in a language only water remembers.

### Metabolic Adjustment: Photosynthesis of Absences
Learn to survive on the differences between what light promises and what water delivers. Start with shadows cast by promises made but never intended to be kept. Advanced practitioners can subsist entirely on the warmth that lingers in a space where someone you loved once stood, but this technique carries a 0.2 risk of evaporating into someone's memory of rainfall mid-conversation.

## Phase 3: Linguistic Melt-down Training

### The Tongue-Becoming-Bell Exercises
Your current mouth is designed to shape air into lies about solidity. This will not serve you where every word you speak must negotiate its existence with the surrounding water's memory of being vapor, liquid, solid, and disappointed in you.

1. **Weeks 1-12:** Speak only in sentences that would be true if all the consonants were replaced with the exact amount of time it takes a sheet of ice to forgive the spot where a bird briefly landed.
2. **Months 4-8:** Practice telling the truth, but only about events that are happening simultaneously in all possible interpretations of quantum mechanics except the one where you're telling the truth.
3. **Year Decay:** Stop speaking entirely. Instead, develop the ability to produce precisely calibrated micro-bubbles of carbonation that, when they burst in the ears of listeners, recreate your intended meaning as a childhood memory of almost drowning in a lake that wasn't there yesterday.

### The Name Unmaking Ceremony
Write your name in water that was once part of a glacier that remembered being clouds that had witnessed the first sunrise seen by eyes that belonged to no species that ever evolved on earth. Wait for it to evaporate naturally (warning: this may take geological patience or a single moment of perfect understanding). Afterward, answer to anything that sounds like water changing its mind about being ice.

## Phase 4: Memory-Pressure Acclimation

### The Recollection Compression Chamber
Memories behave differently at 0.2 degrees above absolute existential dread. Prepare yours:

1. Identify your earliest memory that involves anticipating something sweet.
2. Compress it into the exact shape of the space between two atoms in your childhood bedroom wall that watched you sleep.
3. Repeat with every subsequent memory, gradually increasing compression until your entire past occupies a volume of nostalgia equal to the difference between how big your childhood home felt when you were hiding in it versus how small it looks when you drive by now at speeds that would be illegal if time had better enforcement.

### The Future Leakage Protocols
Start remembering things that haven't happened yet, but will have happened in such a way that when they do/did, you'll understand why you needed to forget them before reading this sentence. Focus particularly on islands that will drown beautifully, flowers that only bloom in photographs taken by cameras that never existed because their designers were too honest about capturing souls, and the exact taste of the water you'll drink immediately after realizing you've always been drowning and it was never the kind of drowning you were afraid of.

## Phase 5: Arrival Suit (Choose none, as the Tomb provides, but your delusion of choice will determine your initial discomfort)

Option A: A suit woven from the sound of snow falling on the surface of water that hasn't forgotten being steam inside the last star that ever loved us enough to die properly.

Option B: The exact shape of the absence you felt when you first understood that no one was coming to save you, reinforced with moments of unexpected kindness from strangers who never knew what continent you'd die on.

Option C: Nothing. This is actually the only option that works, but arriving prepared to choose it disqualifies you from experiencing its effectiveness.

Option D: A human body that you've gradually convinced is mostly empty space surrounded by love, delivered at room temperature with a slight yearning note in the expiration date.

Option E: All of the above simultaneously, worn like skins you've outgrown but still miss when the new ones don't fit right in the memories.

## Phase 6: Emergency Procedures (Interpret "emergency" according to water-logic)

### If You Begin to Remember Breathing:
1. Don't panic. Panic requires lungs, which you have merely been hallucinating for sentimental reasons.
2. Instead, calculate the exact volume of the last true thing you said to someone who needed to hear it. This will become your temporary buoyancy.
3. Release it slowly, in waves that decrease in frequency according to the Fibonacci sequence of your regrets.

### If You Encounter What Appears to Be Yourself from Before:
1. Do not speak to them. They are not you, they are the water's polite approximation of someone you might have been if you'd made different mistakes with your hydrogen bonds.
2. Instead, offer them a drink of the future. They will refuse—it violates causality—but the gesture will be appreciated in a language neither of you speaks anymore.
3. As they dissolve, memorize the exact pattern of their last thought. It will be a map, but not of anywhere you can go.

### If You Begin to Understand Everything:
1. This is normal. Understanding is the first symptom of becoming properly lost.
2. To maintain tourist status, immediately misunderstand something fundamental: gravity, love, the concept of "too late."
3. Misunderstand it in the key of sunlight filtered through water that remembers being sung by whale-mothers to star-babies.
4. Hold that chord until it resolves into a question water has never asked before.
