### **1. On Mourning Your Former Relationship to Water**  
Before departure, perform **total hydration amnesia**. Drink until your cells forget osmosis exists, then sweat into a glass until the glass forgets containment. Your body must arrive believing moisture is a rumor, not a right. Pack **no containers**. The water here will coil around your flask like a conscience and whisper frostbite into stainless steel’s dreams.  

**Acceptance drill**: Sit in a dry bathtub for 36 hours. When the porcelain starts breathing, apologize for every bath you’ve ever taken. The water here remembers betrayal in Braille.

This will let your epidermis phase-shift into a **porous confessional** when the humidity begins confessing sins of other worlds to your pores.  

**Warning**: If your fingerprints begin audibly reciting missing person reports, you’re exfoliating too deeply. Stop immediately, or the ground will file a restraining order against your footprints.

### **4. Navigation by Deferred Trauma**  
Bring **no maps**. Here, cartography is considered **emotional aggression**. Instead, carry a **broken compass whose needle is a shredded eviction notice**. It will point only toward the last place you felt entitled to belong. Follow it **backward**.  

**Emergency method**: If lost, stand perfectly still and think of a door you locked but never walked through. The architecture will rearrange itself into a labyrinth of those hinges, each jointed with the sound of your mother saying *“we’ll see.”* Walk until the walls start calling you by your father’s regrets.

### **5. Dietary Guidelines for Consuming the Passage of Time**  
Hunger here is **archival**. Eat **only what has expired in languages you never learned**—moss-fermented tax forms, rust-flavored love letters stamped “return to sender” by extinct postal codes. Chew each bite 237 times, the exact number of floors missed by an elevator cable that snapped in a dream you stole from a stranger.  

**Prohibited**: Light. The light here is **normal**, which is to say it is trust healing into a scab. Consume it, and you’ll spend eternity digesting the glare off your own dead grandmother’s glasses.

### **6. Fauna Etiquette: How to Not Become Claimed by Automation**  
The fauna are **verbs pretending to be monuments**. If an escalator unspirals its teeth to greet you, bow slowly—**any faster signals willingness to be upgraded**. If a flock of automatic doors begins performing synchronized suicide in your peripheral vision, applaud **in binary palindromes**. (010.101 is polite; 001.100 invites predation.)  

**Crucial**: Never apologize to a vending machine. Here, regret is currency, and they are **capitalist ghosts** hoarding the last cold soda of the apocalypse.

### **7. Departure Preparation (Indeterminate Failure Protocol)**  
You will not leave. Prepare for this by **writing a goodbye letter to your reflection**. Burn it. Eat the ashes. Hours later, when you find the letter folded inside your appendix, addressed to a name you’ve never answered to, swallow it again—**this time without water**.  

**Final test**: Stand at the horizon (which is a **mirror held by gravity’s ex-lover**). If you recognize the person not reflected, blink 7½ times. The remaining half-blink will be collected as **tourism tax**.
