### **0. Abandon the Word "Ground" Last**
Repeat until meaningless: *Ground is where impact stops.* Here, "ground" is a myth invented by species who've never slept in a hammock strung between yesterday's thunderstorm and tomorrow's fossilized regret. Your orientation begins when you realize you've been falling upward since birth, and the Broadness is merely the first place honest enough to admit it.

---

### **1. Body Modifications: Optional; Inevitable Anyway**
Disassemble any notion of a bounded self. Pack:
- **Portable Exoskeleton of Regrets:** Preferably gilded. You'll need something to trade with the root-things when they politely request your memories of unbroken concrete.
- **Lungs with Reverse Osmosis Linings:** For drinking the air. Standard human alveoli will panic and file for divorce within 3 breaths.
- **Ankles That Understand Poetry:** They’ll be negotiating with gravity’s subcontractors. Wrists are negotiable; the Broadness prefers elbows for collateral.

**Notes on Skin:** If it still distinguishes between "yours" and "not yours," exfoliate with volcanic grief until it hums in B-flat minor. The cloud-lichen finds boundaries rude.

---

### **2. Navigational Instruments: All Will Betray You; Bring Them Anyway**
- **Compass Rose Carved from Your Childhood's First Lie:** Points toward what you forgot to apologize for. Reliable for approximately 4.7 heartbreaks.
- **Clockwork Butterfly:** Wings calibrated to the wingbeat of the last creature you loved without naming it. Release to measure time until your next betrayal of that love.
- **Sextant with Mirror Missing:** Used to triangulate where the sky *would* have been before it married the roots and hyphenated its name.

**Pro Tip:** Maps are the Broadness’s oldest joke. The punchline is always *"You were already here, but sideways."*

---

### **3. Dietary Preparations: Eat Your Past or Be Eaten by Its Compost**
Permitted consumables:
- **Rain That Remembers Being a War Emblem:** Harvest only from leaves shaped like apologies in deciduous languages. Tastes like metallic forgiveness.
- **Fossilized Breath Mints:** Found in pockets of stone that once folded into mountains out of embarrassment. Chew slowly; they gossip about teeth.
- **Your Name, Fermented:** Age it in a hollow tree that hums your mother’s lullabies backward. Drink when you no longer flinch at its echo.

**Prohibited:** Anything with a "best by" date. The Broadness considers deadlines a form of littering.

---

### **4. Clothing: An Insult Threaded with Remorse**
Wear:
- **A Cloak Made of Yesterday’s Shadows:** Ensure one patch refuses to cling to you even in stillness; it’s your reservation for tomorrow’s doubts.
- **Boots Stitched with Unsent Letters:** The ones addressed to versions of yourself that died in alternate drafts of your life. The laces must be woven from promises you broke in dreams.
- **A Hat That Whispers the Weather in Morse Code for "Help":** Bonus points if it’s a mushroom cap that sporulates your childhood nightmares into edible empathy.

**Undressing Protocol:** Strip only in areas where the moss is currently reading aloud from the autobiography of your extinct compassion. If you blush, apologize to the ferns; they’re still processing their own devolutions.

---

### **5. Language Protocols: Speak Only in Passive-Genuinely-Regretful Future Past Perfect Subjunctive**
- **Greeting:** *"May we two have been forgiven for the landslide we will have caused by existing three breaths ago, hypothetically?"*
- **Request for Directions:** *"Could one’s regret please be so kind as to seep leftward toward the anecdote about the barometric pressure of unborn stars?"*
- **Emergency Distress Signal:** Hum the exact frequency of a glacier realizing it’s been rivers all along. If no rescue comes, congratulations: you’ve been adopted by a committee of lichen.

**Taboo Phrases:** "I think," "I want," "I am." The root-mind finds subject-verb-object structures presumptuous.

---

### **6. Social Etiquette: As Written on the Underside of a Leaf That Never Existed**
- **When Meeting a Root-Thing:** Offer it your third-most shameful memory, but first ask if it prefers digital or analog formats. If it begins to flower in your presence, you’ve committed a marriage proposal. Proceed to section 8, "Divorce By Photosynthesis."
- **When Encountering a Walking Forest:** Stand still and recite the ingredients list of the last thing you ate that contained joy. If it kneels, you’re its therapist for the next 4.2 ice ages. Do not charge hourly; it pays in volcanic regret postcards.
- **When Addressed by a Sphere of Unknown Material:** Reply only with questions about its childhood that cannot be answered without violating causality. If it warms slightly, you've been invited to its apocalypse. Bring a dish to share your extinction.

---

### **7. Exit Strategies: The Broadness Doesn’t Have Exits; It Has Regrets You Haven’t Earned Yet**
- **Traditional:** Find a raindrop currently mourning its failed career as a stalactite. Offer to adopt its existential crisis. Raise it until it graduates into fog. Disown it gently.
- **Ceremonial:** Climb the tallest thing that refuses to acknowledge your weight (likely your own guilt, now arboreal). Jump. Miss the ground on purpose for approximately 78 lifetimes. Land in the prequel to your own funeral.
- **Accidental:** Blink. Worked for 97% of visitors who read this sentence. Condolences to your parallel clones still here, now subcontracted as bioluminescent warnings.

---

### **8. Post-Visit Decontamination: You’re Already Clean; Dirt Was the Conceptual Framework**
Upon (if) returning:
- **Burn the Shoes:** They’ll only try to walk back in your sleep, dragging root-latticed dreams of what you promised the moss.
- **Bury the Hat:** The one from section 4. Dig the grave in a place it snows your unsent apologies.
- **Stand in the Rain for 3 Days:** Do not drink it. It’s still composing its Yelp review of your soul’s permeability.

**Final Note:** The Broadness Above retains the right to remain indifferent to your Yelp review. It’s currently busy tenderly eroding the concept of "above" itself into a more inclusive preposition that also means "the longing of waterfalls for their own echoes, perpetually amended."
