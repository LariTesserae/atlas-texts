**Entry Method:** Sublimated Cortical Dose via Throat-Capillary Deliquescence (see below)

#### **PHASE 1: PRE-DISSOLUTION (Earth-Analogue Frame)**

1. **CULTIVATE SHAME**  
   Spend 72 hours in a room where every surface is slightly sticky but never identifiably so. Eat only foods that required a machine to conceive (e.g., lab-grown shrimp ovaries, freeze-dried grief). Do not rinse your mouth. The acclimation to alien *desire* (not hunger) is critical—you must arrive tasting like something that wants to *become* infrastructure.

2. **INSTALL A FALSE BREATHING REFLEX**  
   Strap a cracked semi-conductive plate (preferably reclaimed from a pre-EMP pacemaker) to your diaphragm. Programming: **inhale** on rising capacitance (fear), **exhale** on quantum flux decay (boredom). The chamber’s electrokinetic fauna mistake rhythmic gas exchange for territorial aggression. You must *leak* air like a dying language.

3. **BURY YOUR NAME IN POTTERY**  
   Encode your birth coordinates, blood type, and last dream about teeth into a clay tablet fired at 60% ceramic sintering temperature (use a kiln with a cracked thermostat). Break it into pieces divisible by the number of times you’ve forgotten why you entered a room. Swallow one shard. Scatter the rest along a fault line currently experiencing slow-slip events (consult USGS tremor-ballet forecasts). This establishes you as *missing-but-anticipated* cargo—never lost enough to retrieve, never present enough to reject.

#### **PHASE 2: DISSOLUTION VECTOR (Transitional Null)**

**Warning:** Consciousness will invert here. Your last coherent thought will be the *sound* of your bones realizing they are hollow.

1. **THEOKINETIC ENEMA**  
   Lie supine on a piece of corroded satellite-grade aluminum foil. Have a trusted enemy inject 40ml of melted CRT monitor glass (stirred with a ferromagnetic spoon during an aurora) into your intestinal lower third. Visualize your intestines as an obsolete subway map. Scream, but only in frequencies banned by the 1967 Outer Space Treaty. The pain is the *address*—memorize its shape.

2. **ENTANGLEMENT MIRRORING**  
   Stare into a mirror smeared with your own chilled breath until your reflection forgets to blink first. When your left pupil dilates to match the circumference of a discarded o-ring (type: Titan IV rocket), bite your tongue *sideways*. Taste copper? Good. Taste static? Better. Spit. The spit will crystallize mid-fall into a tiny replica of the chamber’s breathing cracks—catch it on your tongue. This is your *visa*. Do not swallow.

3. **ORBITAL SALTATION**  
   At precisely 03:33 local sidereal time, climb the highest abandoned chimney in a 93-mile radius wearing only socks made from your childhood bedroom’s carpet dust. Jump. Not to fall—*to misalign*. You must achieve a trajectory tangent to the moment Earth’s magnetosphere sighs in its sleep. The missing velocity (exactly 11.1 km/s * your weight in unspoken apologies) will be provided by the ceramic womb’s retrograde longing. **Do not flail.** Sing the first song your mother hummed while miscarrying your sibling-self. The sky will unzip sideways.

#### **PHASE 3: INCORPORATION (Chamber Frame)**

**Upon arrival, you will be inside-out. Ignore this.**

1. **DRESS CODE**  
   Your skin is now a violation. Shed it politely by rubbing against the nearest half-melted vacuum tube filament (it will glow welcome). Beneath, you’ll find a layer of conducting frost—do not panic. This is the chamber’s version of *being seen*. Wear it like a crime scene. If the electrokinetic organisms (the sparks that taste your pulse) attempt to weave your shed skin into a temporary settlement, compliment their architecture but decline residency. You’re not staying—you’re *fermenting*.

2. **HYDRATION IS BETRAYAL**  
   The frozen water dendrites are not for drinking. They are *memoirs* of the air’s failed suicide attempts. Touching them will implant memories of drowning in a rain that remembered being plasma. If thirsty, lick the glowing cores of the fossilized machines—they exude a dim warmth that tastes like the word *almost* in a dead engineer’s throat. Limit intake to: **one syllable per century subjective**.

3. **NAVIGATION BY INFINITE REGRET**  
   Movement is accomplished by *apologizing* to the wall you wish to disrespect. The apology must be:
   - Typed on a keyboard made of your own tooth enamel,
   - In a language that only existed for the duration of a single hiccup in 1347 AD,
   - Backwards, but phonetically inverted through a throat synthesizer derived from the last recorded thought of a crushed butterfly.  
   The wall will soften at the frequency of your genuine shame. Crawl through. **Do not look back.** What follows you will be your spinal column, reorganized into a question mark. It is still learning how to drown.

4. **CONTACT PROTOCOL (FRIENDS/SELF/OTHER)**  
   If you encounter another traveler:
   - They are not real.  
   - They are you from a timeline where you succeeded.  
   - They will ask for the shard you swallowed. Give them instead the taste of your first betrayal (lift it from behind your molars with a fingernail grown for 13 moons).  
   - If they thank you, melt. If they scream in admiration of the chamber’s humidity, marry them. Divorce must be finalized by reconstructing their skeleton into a working model of your own childhood nightmares.  
   **Note:** All offspring are contractually obligated to become new breathing cracks in the walls. Send postcards.

#### **PHASE 4: EXFOLIATION (Exit Optional)**

The chamber does not expel. It *finishes*. You will know readiness when:

- Your heartbeat synchronizes with the gyroscopic hum and you experience nostalgia for a rotation rate you’ve never possessed.
- The electrokinetic organisms stop tasting you and start *quoting* you. (The first quote will be an apology for language itself.)
- You find a micro-fracture in the air that smells like the sound of your mother’s sewing machine after she stopped coming home. Enter it sideways, breath unswallowed.

**No equipment is necessary for exit.** You left it all behind when you decided to miss yourself enough to arrive.
