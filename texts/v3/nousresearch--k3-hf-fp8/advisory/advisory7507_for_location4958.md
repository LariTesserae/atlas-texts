**Field Guide: Preparing for Descent into the Beneath-Becoming**

*For the anthropic traveler who insists on maintaining approximately 27% identity cohesion*  
*Warning: This document becomes obsolete upon completion. Start reading again.*

### I. **Cognitive Vaccination Protocols (Mandatory 72-Hours Pre-Entry)**

You must catch three dreams that hate each other and force them to share a bed. Record how they negotiate the blanket's ownership using only the scent of thunderstorms. Burn the transcript nine minutes before reading it—inhale the smoke through your least favorite memory. This teaches your ego the proper exit posture (curtsey, not collapse).

Next: Spend one night convincing your mirror you're its reflection. When it blinks first, apologize sincerely but refuse to explain what for. This acquires the necessary visa stamp on your surface tension.

Finally: Memorize all the words in every language that mean *"almost, but the wrong flavor of yes."* Repeatedly forget them in reverse alphabetical order while humming the sound of a glacier changing its mind. This calibrates your existential sonar to detect boundaries that have already forgiven you for trespassing.

### II. **Equipment That Would Only Betray You (Bring These Anyway)**

- **1x Artificial Starvation**  
  Crafted from two seasons of radio silence and the space between promised letters. Wrapped in the skin of a calendar that stopped believing in Tuesdays. Use: When the vegetation offers you fruit that tastes like your mother singing lullabies in a voice you haven't invented yet. DO NOT EAT—place gently on your tongue and let it dissolve your saliva's loyalty.

- **3x Portable Paradoxes**  
  Format: Left-handed gloves worn on both feet, a map folded so the creases spell your childhood address backwards, a compass that points toward the last place you felt understood. Shed one every time you notice the walls breathing out of sync with your regrets.

- **7m of Wet Silence**  
 Harvested from conversations between lovers who've forgotten their shared language but continue arguing in the pauses. Rolled tight inside a seashell that grew lungs instead of pearl. Unspool to measure distances where footsteps hesitate to become echoes.

- **1x Emergency Mirror**  
  Not reflecting—remembering. Specifically, that moment when you were five and realized your shadow could leave if it ever learned to walk faster. Polished with the last time you saw yourself blink without permission. Break only if you need to prove you were ever here.

- **Infinite Fractions of Yourself**  
  Stored in vials labeled: *Here but sideways* / *Yes but yesterday* / *Mine but only when observed*. Administer intravenously when the fauna start addressing you by your sister's middle name and the plants grow leaves shaped like your dental records.

### III. **Corporeal Adjustments (Five Days Before, No Later)**

1. **Renounce Right Angles:**  
   Sleep curled inside circles drawn with stolen rainbows. When you must stand, distribute your weight evenly across all possible versions of the ground. Practice falling upward until it feels like polite disagreement.

2. **Teach Your Blood to Whistle:**  
  Tune it to the frequency of first kisses interrupted by thunder. This allows seamless integration when the humidity starts reciting your medical history in archaic perfume languages.

3. **Grow Temporary Deaths:**  
  Each night, die slightly in a different part of your body. Start with toenails—progress to childhood memories. Leave the corpses bundled under your pillow for the local decomposer spirits to review. Accept their notes with grace even if written in fungal rings on your ceiling.

4. **Wean Your Skeleton:**  
 Gradually replace calcium with compressed apologies for things you haven't done yet. The transition is complete when you can fold your ribcage into a paper crane that migrates without flapping.

5. **Direct Neural Interface with Uncertainty:**  
 Rewire your synapses to treat certainty as a form of aggression against possibility. Practice answering questions with the smell of unopened letters. Attain enlightenment when you can forget this step entirely.

### IV. **Bureaucratic Submissions to Submit to**

File Form 27B/6/∞ with the Department of Unfinished Sentences—attach proof that your name loses weight when whispered underwater. Obtain notarized consent from all your shadows, especially the one that grew teeth during the eclipse. Submit your parents' first argument rewritten as a recipe that serves exactly "one regret split three ways." Include a self-addressed envelope made from the skin of the last person who promised they'd write.

Important: Do not sign anything. Instead, leave a warm place where your signature would have been. The forms will complete themselves with your body's future fingerprint.

### V. **Arrival Procedure (Follow the One That Isn't There)**

1. Locate a door that closes louder than the room it exits. Knock using only echoes of knocks you haven't performed yet.
2. When asked for the password, describe the taste of the color you're most afraid of becoming. Use your hair to draw the sound it makes when sleeping.
3. The threshold will taste like a goodbye you didn't know you were owed. Spit politely into the hat it offers.
4. Step forward while remaining exactly where your shadow would go if it left to find a better version of you.
5. Congratulations. You have always been too late. Begin again from step ∞.

### VI. **Survival Tips for the Already Unsurvived**

- **Eating:**  
  Food here is any object that misses you more than it should. Consume slowly enough that it forgets why you're not the person it expected. Chew with your ears closed. Swallow only after debating the ethical implications of digestion with your stomach.

- **Sleeping:**  
  The closest analogue is closing your eyes while being watched by trees that remember your dreams better than you do. Do not dream in color—this is considered colonialist. Instead, dream in negotiations between water and what it could have become if it hadn't learned to fall.

- **Navigation:**  
  You are already where you're going but slower. To accelerate, become less certain about which direction lost items travel when they're really trying to be found. Maps work best when used as bandages for injuries you haven't received in this timeline.

- **Communication:**  
  Language functions as a form of polite fire. Speak only in answers to questions the silence hasn't asked yet. If someone says "hello," apologize for the weather in the language of birds that died before inventing songs for endings.

### VII. **Exit Strategies (Abridged List of Missed Opportunities)**

There are no exits. There are only deeper entrances disguised as leaving. However, should you accidentally begin returning to yourself:

1. Remember a color that never forgave you for looking away. Wear it inside out.
2. Follow the sound of your heart breaking until it leads you to the place where it learned to hold things without letting go.
3. When you find the door marked *Emergency Reality Reinflation*, do not open it. Instead, slip your regrets through the keyhole and listen for the click of something finally understanding.
4. If time starts flowing normally again, apologize profusely and offer to carry its bags. It has been traveling longer than your species has had names for loneliness.
