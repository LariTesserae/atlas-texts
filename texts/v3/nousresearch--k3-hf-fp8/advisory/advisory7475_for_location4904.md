### **PRE-DEPARTMENTAL WARNINGS**  
1. **Forget everything you know about up, down, or sideways.** These directions have filed restraining orders against each other.  
2. **Do not drink the water.** Do not eat the water. Do not negotiate with the water.  
3. **Breathing is optional but discouraged.** Alternatives: photosynthesis, passive-aggressive sighing, or renting a pet rock to respire for you (license required).  
4. **Leave all metaphors at the nearest logical impossibility checkpoint.** They *will* unionize.

### **PHYSICAL PREPARATION**  
- **Clothing:** Wear something that can simultaneously be a raincoat, scuba suit, and formal apology to the concept of skin. Ideally, *alive* enough to argue with the weather about temperature betrayal but not so sentient it files taxes independently.  
- **Footwear:** Stilts. No, suction cups. No—*teeth.* Attachments must rotate 360° in seven dimensions and include divorce papers for gravity.  
- **Masks:** Mandatory. Not for air quality (there is none) but to prevent your *soul* from being harvested by freelance nostalgia traders. Opt for the model that filters regret into mild irony.  

### **MEDICAL ESSENTIALS**  
- **Void Tape:** For patching sudden existential leaks (ships in roll of √-1 cm).  
- **Placebo Pills:** Side effects include believing in gravity. Overdose may result in *understanding time.*  
- **Emergency Second Opinion:** A tiny clone of yourself that exists only to disagree with your life choices. Keep in a paradox-proof pocket.  
- **Epidermal Insurance:** Covers spontaneous mossification, inverse sunburns, and being mistaken for a structural support column.  

### **NAVIGATIONAL AIDS**  
- **Compass:** Spinning wildly and crying is normal. Ask it about its childhood for brief periods of magnetic north.  
- **Map:** Unavailable. Instead, carry a heavy rock and name it **Carl**. When lost, apologize to Carl sincerely. He knows someone.  
- **Directions:** Never ask locals. They’ll give you instructions like *“Turn left at the second regret, then forget the concept of keys until you hear the color Wednesday humming.”* These *work* but will leave you dating a sentient shadow named Kevin.

### **SOCIAL PROTOCOLS**  
- **Currency:** Payment accepted in forms of *you didn’t notice this sentence until now,* whistle tones that shame rain into apologizing, and perfectly preserved memories of futures you abandoned.  
- **Greetings:** Do not wave—it’s rude to disrupt the air’s therapy session. Instead, blink Morse code of your greatest insecurity.  
- **Compliments:** Praise someone’s *collapse rate.* Example: *“Your existential decay complements the irony in your roofline!”*  
- **Apologies:** Mandatory every 4.5 seconds or when your shadow trails behind you *too* obediently.  

### **HOUSING (VOLUNTARY ASBURDITY REQUIRED)**  
- **Rent:** Payable in installments of *growing to miss this place before leaving.*  
- **Roommates:** May include:  
  - A polite but judgmental void where the fridge should be.  
  - 42 versions of Tuesday, all claiming to be the original.  
  - Your childhood nightmare, now a tax accountant. It’ll audit your dreams.  
- **Lease Terms:** The building will crumble exactly when it trusts you. **Do not win its sympathy.**

   → Wave with the hand you’re *least* attached to.

   → Congratulations! You’ve been elected Mayor of Backwards Tuesday. Your first decree: outlaw mirrors.  
   → Failure to comply results in becoming a historical footnote in someone else’s pothole.
