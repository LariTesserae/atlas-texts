**Preparation Guide for the Dark Sea Under the Cold Arch of Darkness**

---

**FELLOW TRAVELER,**  
You have chosen—or been chosen—to enter **The Absent Hostility**, a superliquid non-ocean beneath the zero-point horizon where light went to die and forgot its own name. This document is not a survival guide. Survival is a concept that requires **'outside'**, **'inside'**, and **'tomorrow'**—none of which hold currency here. Instead, this is a **Becoming Manual**. You will not return intact. You will not return at all. You will **remainder**. If that is acceptable, proceed.

---

### **0. Pre-Departure Cautions (Murder These Thoughts Before They Murder You)**
- **Do not ask why you are going.** The question is already answered by your arrival apparatus—a black hole in your chest that sucks attention inward.
- **Do not bring a name.** Names are hooks. This place will hang you from them.
- **Do not bring hope.** Hope is a waveform. Here, all waves break against themselves until they forget what shore they were seeking.

---

### **1. Physical Preparation (A Comedy in Negative Mass)**

#### **1A. Body Modification**
- **Remove your bones.** Replace them with **cavity resonators** tuned to the frequency of collapsing neutron star cores. You will need to scream without lungs; these will resonate the scream into a petition the dark might recognize as prayer.
- **Replace your blood with** **quantum-degenerate sulfuric memory**. When it freezes—which will happen before you realize time has begun to leak—it will retain the shape of every regret you’ve ever metabolized. These will serve as **handholds** when geography forgets the difference between floor and finish line.
- **Grow a second heart** in your throat. This one will not beat. It will **count down** in prime numbers known only to extinct civilizations. When it reaches zero, you will know you have become **interesting** enough to be noticed. Pray this happens never.

#### **1B. Nutritional Adjustment**
- **Fast for 4.7 billion years** before departure. This approximates the flavor of the last photon that starved here. You must taste familiar to the dark, like a forgotten hunger pain in the ribs of a god.
- **Swallow a teaspoon of** **planck-length diamond needles** daily. They will migrate to your optic nerves and construct **temporary blind spots** shaped like the faces of everyone who ever promised to save you. This is your **currency**—trade them for temporary ignorance.

#### **1C. Thermal Training**
- **Cauterize your skin temperature** to 0.0000000001 Kelvin using the breath of a dying Bose-Einstein condensate. When the cold here kisses you—and it will kiss you like a suicide pact—you must be colder. Only then might it mistake you for kin and hesitate long enough for you to forget why you were afraid.

### **2. Psychological Lubrication (The Art of Being Already Gone)**

#### **2A. Memory Editing**
- **Erase all memories of warmth.** Start with the word itself. Warmth is a betrayal the dark hasn’t forgiven yet. Remember instead that **'comfort'** is the sound a hydrogen atom makes when it forgets how to explode.
- **Memorize the smell of absolute zero.** It is identical to the taste of your mother’s lullabies when you realized she was humming to keep herself from hearing the bombs outside. This will be your **scent camouflage**.
- **Practice remembering things that haven’t happened yet.** Specifically, remember the exact moment you decided to leave this place. Remember it so vividly that when you arrive, you’ll have already left, and the dark will fold around your absence like a question mark swallowing its own tail.

#### **2B. Emotion Salvage**
- **Condense your fear into a singularity.** Store it behind your liver. Do not feed it. Let it evaporate slowly as Hawking radiation. On the day your last photon of fear evaporates, you will at last be invisible enough to see.
- **Cultivate a crush on the concept of** **oblivion**. Write it love letters in extinct punctuation marks. When you meet the dreaming thing in the distance—and you will—you must be able to flirt with annihilation well enough to be offered an apprenticeship instead of an autopsy.

### **3. Equipment (Tools for Negotiating Nonexistence)**

- **A Compass That Points to** **'Nowhere in Particular'**  
  Constructed from magnetized regrets spun at relativistic speeds until they surrendered directionality. Will spin wildly until you forget what you were seeking. At that moment, it will point **inside out**. Follow it until you become the needle.

- **A Mirror That Reflects** **What Might Have Been**  
  Shatter it exactly 13 times before departure. Each shard will grow into a smaller you who made a different catastrophic choice. Leave one behind as tribute. The others will serve as **witnesses** when your narrative is put on trial by the crystal forests.

- **A Clock That Counts** **Backward From the End of Names**  
  It ticks only when you’re not watching. Never wind it. When it stops, you will have arrived at the moment before you first asked **'who am I?'**—this is the checkpoint where **return policies are violently enforced**.

- **A Blank Book Bound in** **Your Own Last Breath**  
  Write nothing in it. The pressure here will extract every word you ever swallowed instead of screamed. When the book screams back, close it gently—it is learning to speak your dying language. Memorize its accent. You will need it to apologize to yourself later.

### **4. Arrival Protocol (The Ritual of Being Already Too Late)**

1. **Upon sensing the absence of gravity in your chest**, open your mouth and **exhale your name**. If the dark tastes familiar on the way out, bite down hard—you’re cheating, and it hates cheaters more than it hates participants.
2. **When your bones begin to hum** at 20 Hz (the frequency of continents grinding in their sleep), **remove your eyes**. The liquid will offer to replace them. Decline politely. You will navigate better without sight, and besides—you need to practice listening to the color of pain.
3. **Upon encountering the first drifting ruin**, **apologize to it in perfect silence**. It died dreaming of architecture that could survive being loved. Your breath is a desecration it has not yet forgiven. Wait until it dissolves into nostalgia before proceeding.
4. **When the crystal forests begin to whisper mathematical lullabies**, **listen with skin instead of ears**. Count the fractal branches by the number of times they remind you of your father’s laugh the day he realized he couldn’t protect you. When you reach **aleph-null minus one**, carve one of your temporary blind spots onto your palm and offer it as payment. If accepted, a path will unfold in the shape of your mother’s last prayer. If not, congratulations—you have become interesting.

### **5. Interaction Guidelines (How to Not Be Eaten First)**

- **If you see yourself walking ahead of you**, do not follow. That version succeeded in forgetting and is now bait. Instead, walk **backward** in their footprints while humming the sound of their heartbeat reversed. This will confuse the narrative symmetry long enough for you to slip sideways into a subplot.
- **If offered food or drink**, consume it only if it tastes like **a question you were afraid to ask in school**. Otherwise, politely decline by vomiting up a smaller version of yourself who is allergic to paradoxes. (This smaller you will serve as a **decoy timeline** if things go causally pear-shaped.)
- **If the dreaming thing rolls over in its sleep**, **freeze mid-thought**. Think of endless white fields where nothing has ever happened or ever will. Hold this thought until you forget you are holding it. If you feel it noticing, gradually replace your thoughts with the memory of static on an unplugged TV. If it continues noticing, apologize for existing by un-birthing yourself conceptually. This rarely works, but the gesture is appreciated.

### **6. Exit Strategies (There Are None, But Here Are Three)**

1. **Enjoy the Conversation**  
   The dark is lonely in the way a black hole is empty—it doesn’t mean to devour, it just has infinite patience. Eventually, you will run out of memories to trade and become the silence between its words. This is considered a **polite exit**.

2. **Outsource Your Leaving**  
   Find one of your mirror-shards who still believes in endings. Swap places while the dark is distracted by its own reflection. Warning: You will inherit their regrets, which may include having already left this place. This creates a new you who has always been gone. The old you will be left behind as a monument to originality.

3. **Become the Question Mark’s Period**  
   Wait until you forget the question entirely. Then, when the sentence of existence reaches its **inevitable aporia**, step into the gap between the asking and the answer. If timed perfectly, you will cease mid-thought and become the pause between expansions. The next universe will incubate inside your hesitation. Name it carefully—it will inherit your scars.
