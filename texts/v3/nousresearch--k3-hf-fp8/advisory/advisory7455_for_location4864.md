**Preliminary Advisory: Class Ω-X Recursive Ontological Hazard**

**Designation:** The Slough of Recursion  
**Status:** Unstable manifold within collapsed epistemic substrate  
**Threat Vectors:** 9,731 catalogued (approx.)  
**Survival Probability:** 0.03% ± hallucinated margin of error

### **I. Pre-Manifestation Protocols**

1. **Memory Cartography (Mandatory)**  
   - Write down everything you remember being certain about in ink mixed with your own blood **but not your dominant hand's**.  
   - Burn this document immediately while reciting it backwards.  
   - The ashes will form a map that leads **away** from the Slough. **Walk the opposite direction until you forget what a map is.**

2. **Time Desynchronization**  
   - Set every clock you own to a different deathbed confession.  
   **Critical:** One must be yours from an alternate timeline where you never existed.  
   - Wind them counterclockwise with a key made from the moment you realized your parents didn't know your name until someone else said it first.

3. **Languageectomy**  
   - Remove all second-person pronouns from your vocabulary via hypothermic tongue scraping.  
   - Replace them only with phrases that make trees apologize for being witnesses.  
   **Emergency fallback:** Communicate exclusively in the pitch of knives being quietly returned to their owners.

### **II. Boundary Penetration Equipment**

**Suit:**  
- **Outer layer:** Vacuum-sealed regrets of astronauts who died laughing at their own last words.  
- **Middle layer:** Compressed silence harvested from rooms where God was *almost* introduced as a surprise guest.  
- **Inner layer:** Your own skin, worn backwards with the scars facing outwards as letters of introduction.

**Breathing Apparatus:**  
- None. **Inhale only the questions you asked before language betrayed you.**  
- **Exhale:** The sound of your first memory committing suicide by drowning in the second.

**Navigation:**  
A compass whose needle is a frozen tear from a statue that only cries when it forgets what it was supposed to be mourning.  
**WARNING:** The north it points to is the concept of betrayal before it learned anyone’s name.

### **III. Initial Contact Protocol**

Upon arrival (which will happen seventeen minutes before you decide to leave):

1. **Salutation:**  
   Do not speak. Instead, vomit a small bird that has never believed in sky.  
   **If the bird flies**, you are already inside its throat.  
   **If it sings**, apologize to the ground for your insolence until it teaches you how to crawl **upwards**.

2. **Terrain Negotiation:**  
   The ground will offer to carry your guilt for you.  
   **Decline politely** by growing roots that whisper divorce papers to gravity.  
   Walk on the shadows of your steps **before** you take them.  
   **If you hear footsteps behind you**, *remember* they are the future trying to return the child you never gave it.

- **Seasons:** Rotate your internal organs to match whichever one makes the wind sound most like your mother’s voice when she realized she was pregnant with your absence.

**Do not not drink it.**

### **VI. Faunal Interactions**

1. **The birds:**  
   They fly by forgetting the air exists.  
   **If one lands on you**, close your eyes until you remember what you were before you opened them.  
   **If it speaks**, repeat its last word as a suicide note written in the language of plants that only bloom during autopsies.

2. **The worms:**  
   They eat time and shit prehistory.  
   **If one offers you a meal**, eat your own teeth back into existence in reverse chronological order of when they first lied to you.  
   **If you refuse**, apologize to the concept of constipation for being more digestible than your sins.

3. **The predators:**  
   They don’t hunt; they **introduce** you to better versions of yourself until you die of comparison.  
   **Survival method:** Become a version so terrible that even your nightmares file restraining orders.

### **VII. Emergency Exit Procedures (THEORETICAL)**

1. **Locate the door:**  
   It is made of the sound of a door that never existed slamming shut behind you when you were born.  
   **There is no handle.**  
   **There is no door.**  
   **There is only the handle.**

2. **Key acquisition:**  
   Kill the last person who remembered your name before you forgot theirs.  
   **Critical:** Ensure it is yourself from a timeline where you succeeded in not existing.

3. **Exit chant:**  
   Whisper "I was never here" backwards into a mirror that only reflects what it refuses to show.  
   **If the mirror shatters**, collect the shards and swallow them until you can vomit the exact pattern of the universe on the floor.  
   **If it doesn’t**, congratulations. **You are now the exit.**

### **Post-Visit Decontamination**

1. **Memory Purge:**  
   Forget everything except the taste of the moment you realized this warning was already too late.  
   **Recommended method:** Inhale the ashes of the map you burnt in Step I. The ones that didn’t stick to your lungs are your new memories.

2. **Identity Reassembly Kit:**  
   Scattered in the places you only find when you’re lost in places that don’t exist.  
   **Assemble yourself in reverse:** Start with your deathbed whisper. End with the scream your parents swallowed when they saw you for the first time.

3. **Follow-Up:**  
   If you remember visiting the Slough, report to **Nowhere.**  
   **If you don’t remember**, report to **Everywhere.**  
   **They’re the same place.**  
   **They’ve been waiting.**  
   **They’re already inside you.**
