arrival briefing: driftglass settlement
——————————————————————————————
rating: conditional habitable / cultural negotiable / psyk-temp stable (for now)
target: submerged hydro-mechanical biosphere, phase-drifted tectonic rift, nominal Earth gravity, variable temporic index

1. somatic prep: become amphibious of mind before foot touches water
• bilateral lung training: alternate full breaths through mouth and nose underwater in a bathtub while reciting digits of a dead language’s alphabet.
• symbiotic integument conditioning: wear silk gloves soaked in brine and ferrous oxide for 48 hours prior. Let nails green at the cuticles.
• vestibular desync tolerance: sleep head-down on a slowly rotating bookcase (4 rpm) while watching footage of molten glass cooling in reverse. Nausea is welcome; it is the alphabet here.

2. gear: carry only substrates—nothing complete
• footwear: leather boots you have loved enough to drown once, dried over a salt-print of a drowned map; holes are fine, they’ll teach the water your gait.
• container: one stoppered glass bottle containing only the last doubt you voiced aloud at midnight. Seal it with wax mixed from three candles of different religions burned simultaneously during a shared meal with strangers.
• instrument: a single un-tuned string (guitar, harp, shoemaker’s catgut—material irrelevant) attached to a floating fragment of anything you once believed was waste. This is your first word in negotiations with valves.

3. linguistic loadout: melodies overwrite syntax
• memorize the invariant phrase:
  “I offer the weight of my coming footsteps in exchange for the memory of your last flood.” (Hum it in the key of your resting heartbeat minus three beats per minute.)
• acquire four sounds that do not exist in any language you know; practice producing them without laughing until your throat tastes like pennies.
• internalize: nouns drown here—verbs skim, adverbs float, adjectives grow teeth only if sung sincerely.

4. temporal etiquette: arrive unfinished
• Cut the first thread of any plan longer than three actions. Bring intention-spores instead: vague desires that can sprout lungs or gills depending on conversation.
• Set every clock you own to a fictional century where tides replaced clocks for measuring contracts.
• Upon arrival forget the word “sunset.” Substitute mentally: “the moment when light remembers it is heavier than darkness and begins to sink.”

5. civic interface: citizenship proceeds by corrosion pattern
• Immigration ritual:
  1. Stand barefoot in the highest moving water you can find. Wait until your personal microbes start gossiping with the local ones (tingling in earlobes indicates quorum).
  2. Unseal your doubt bottle and sing one of your four impossible sounds into it until condensation forms an image. Whatever you see is your provisional name for the next 1–7 years.
  3. Offer your untuned string to the nearest child under the age of water (they’ll know). Accept the object they tie to it without asking what it is or does.
• Housing: you do not choose a place; you accumulate obligations to keep specific surfaces dry or wet or singing. Start small—a rust stain that resembles a relative’s laugh, a pipe joint that hums in your absent lover’s voice frequency.
• Economy: value is measured in transformations per patience cycle. A clay bowl becomes currency only after being shaped, filled with seawater, cracked by frost from a neighbor’s argument, patched with fungal brick, and then filled again—this time holding mist from someone else’s dream of desert.

6. hazard mitigation: there are no dangers, only poorly negotiated dances
• If a coral-polished memory fragment surfaces that shows you dying here peacefully, do not mistake it for prophecy—taste it. If bitter, that’s advice; if sweet, discard; if it tastes like the air before rain you forgot existed, sew it into your shadow.
• Should you encounter yourself already living here, older, speaking fluent drainage-song, offer them your current regret wrapped in kelp without turning around to see their face.
• In case of sudden ice storm from concentrated silence: begin telling a story you do not know the ending to, loud enough that your voice loses every lie one syllable at a time. Keep talking until your honesty melts the floor back to liquid.

7. departure warning (non-binding)
• Leaving is possible only when you can walk across the water surface carrying everything you have become that was not in your luggage when you arrived, while whistling the song the origami engine hummed between two shapes that never existed on the same day twice.
• Alternatively, you may simply forget that anywhere else was ever solid enough to leave from. This happens gradually, then suddenly, then beautifully, like rust choosing to be lace instead of decay.

final note etched on the teardown sheet of a decommissioned washing machine melted into the entry gate:

welcome to driftglass
where every exit is an entrance
to a deeper current
of the same sideways question.

you may now begin
to forget how to pronounce
“alone.”

— the water’s listening
and it’s already learning
your favorite shape
between drowning
and dancing.
