**Preparation Guide: The City of the Young Plateau**

**Threat Level:** Paradoxical Stability (Class 2B - Reality Adherence Drift Zone)  
**Visitor Status:** Unsanctioned entry tolerated. Extraction not guaranteed.  
**Duration Advisory:** Limit exposure to 3 subjective daylight cycles. Bring your own end.

---

### **I. PHYSICAL PREPARATION**

#### **1. The Body as Temporary Container**
- **Hydration Paradox:** Drink nothing for 24 hours prior arrival. Your body must enter thirsty as desert stone, skin taut as drum. Bring **two sealed vials**: one of your childhood tears (pre-puberty, pre-loss), one of seawater from a coast you’ve never seen. Consume in alternating drops when your tongue begins *remembering* shapes of words you haven’t spoken yet. Never touch the city’s water directly. It remembers every throat it’s ever filled.
- **Fasting Ritual:** Eat only things that have never grown in water for three days: fire-roasted seeds, honeycomb, wind-dried meat. On the final night, swallow a pebble from your oldest regret. Let it sit heavy. This will anchor your shadow.
- **The Stitching:** Have a physician sew **three silver needles** (palm-width, sterilized in midday sun) into the lining of your dominant wrist’s vein. Do not remove them. When you feel time pooling in your palms like warm wax, press until you taste metal. This is how you’ll know you’re still leaking forward.

#### **2. Clothing as Water’s Negotiator**
- **First Layer:** Woven kelp fibers dyed with rust from your mother’s scissors. Must be eaten by moths and patched seven times before wearing. The holes are doorways for your evaporating future selves.
- **Second Layer:** A cloak of **stitched-together raincoats**—each from a different city where you’ve cried publicly. Leave the hoods intact but empty. They’ll fill with your muffled screams when the city’s silence becomes too intelligent.
- **Footwear:** None. Coat soles with melted candlewax mixed with ash from burned maps. As you walk, the wax will lift small stones. Pocket them carefully. These are your breadcrumbs. They will not help you return, but they’ll argue with the ground about who you used to be.

#### **3. Tools That Must Forget Their Names**
- **Carrying Vessel:** A leather pouch made from your first pet’s hide (if shameless) or your enemy’s diary bindings (if brave). Fill with:
  - 3 iron nails wrapped in hair from someone who died laughing
  - A mirror cracked on the day your oldest friend lied to you
  - 12 copper coins minted in countries that no longer exist
  - A child’s tooth still wet with dream-saliva (not yours)
- **Navigation Aid:** A compass that points only to yesterday. Construct it by magnetizing a needle with static from your childhood bedroom’s TV, then balancing it on a photograph of the ocean from before ships.
- **Timekeeper:** A pocket watch smashed precisely at 3:47 AM—the moment between deepest sleep and first inkling of mortal panic. Let it tick in your pocket anyway.

### **II. MENTAL COMPARTMENTALIZATION**

#### **1. Memory Editing (Mandatory)**
- **Week Before Departure:** Hire a fraud psychic to hypnotize you into believing your mother was a thunderstorm and your father was the echo of a bridge collapsing. Listen to recordings of this session while drinking hot water that used to be snow.
- **Day Of:** Write down everything you know about the color blue. Burn the paper. Eat the ashes. Now write about the color blue again. Notice the lies. These are your only permitted truths here.

#### **2. The Question Protocol**
You may ask the city *three* things. These must be:
1. A question you already know the answer to, but have forgotten how to fear
2. A question that would destroy your childhood if answered correctly
3. The sound a door makes when there’s no house left to close it
Write them on rice paper with ink made from crushed ant-wings. Swallow one. Hide one in the city. Use the last to blindfold a stranger who looks like you from behind.

#### **3. Dream Quarantine**
For seven nights prior, sleep in a bathtub filled with growing plants (no flowering species). Each morning, whisper the city’s coordinates to them, then cut away any leaves that lean toward the drain. Dry these cursed clippings, roll them into cigarettes. Smoke one whenever you see something in the city that *shouldn’t* be beautiful.

### **III. BEHAVIORAL DANCE WITH THE NOT-YET-REAL**

#### **1. Upon First Crossing the Waterline**
- When your boot (if you disobeyed) or waxed foot (if you listened) touches the first spring-fed channel, immediately:
   - Spit upwards. Catch the saliva before it falls. This is your first offering. Place it on the stones where moss hasn’t learned your name yet.
   - Turn counterclockwise until you see your reflection in the mist. It will blink first. Do not blink back.
   - Speak this: *"I am the drought my mother forgot to pray for."* If the water quiets, you may proceed. If it laughs deeper, run.

#### **2. Interacting with the Architecture**
- **Entering Buildings:** Only enter through holes that weren’t doors yesterday. If you must use a doorway, crawl backward while humming the lullaby that never worked on you as a child.
- **Touching Walls:** Allowed only with your tongue, once per structure. Each taste will erase a memory of someone who once loved you unselfishly. Choose strategically.
- **The Half-Finished Works:** If you encounter tools left mid-motion:
   - Take them, but use them immediately for their opposite purpose. A stone hammer must now gently stroke. A bowl-mold must be filled with your breath until it cracks from kindness.
   - Leave behind something growing in their place: a sentence written in dirt, an apology shaped like your eaten dreams.

#### **3. Temporal Hygiene**
- **When Time Pools:**
   - Recognize the signs: Your footprints filling with water before you lift your foot. Shadow arriving before you do. The echo of your heartbeat coming from inside your mouth.
   - Response: Remove one article of clothing (always the one that smells least like home) and drop it into the nearest water-channel. Watch it drown upward. Do not retrieve it. This is how you pay rent to the past.
- **When Time Skips:**
   - Signs: Sudden knowledge of how the city will end (it involves your teeth). Birdsong from extinct species. A stranger calling your name from inside a dream you’ll have tomorrow.
   - Response: Find a plant with leaves that hold pools. Spit into each pool exactly twelve times while counting backward in a language you invented as a child to insult God.

#### **4. The Silence That Listens Too Hard**
- **Never:**
   - Whistle
   - Say your birth name aloud (including in thought)
   - Describe the texture of your mother’s hands to anyone/anything
   - Count your steps past 333
   - Look directly at where the absent sun should be
- **Instead:**
   - Communicate only through arranging found stones into shapes that would make a river weep
   - Laugh using only your hair—whisk it against surfaces in patterns of morse code
   - Cry into your cupped hands, then drink the tears while promising them Unbelievable Things

### **IV. EMERGENCY EXITS (THEORETICAL)**

#### **1. If You Forget Which Way is Out**
- Remember: all directions lead deeper until you forgive the water for how you were born.
- Practical attempt: Shatter the mirror from your pouch. Walk barefoot across shards while reciting the ingredients of your last real meal. The pain will briefly remind the ground that you were once solid.

#### **2. If You Meet Yourself Already Waiting**
- The duplicate is always from:
   - 1 version: 5 minutes before your birth
   - 2 versions: 5 seconds after your death
   - 3+ versions: You never left; these are the city’s dreams of having a visitor
- Protocol:
   - Version 1: Teach it how to be disappointed
   - Version 2: Ask it to describe the sound of your funeral in detail. Agree with everything except the weather.
   - Versions 3+: Kiss them all deeply. They taste like the answer to your third question. This will either free them or chain you. The city likes both outcomes.

#### **3. If You Want to Stay**
- Sit in any room with a still-warm firepit (they’re all still-warm).
- Place all your remaining water-vials, stones, and teeth in the center.
- Whisper: *"I am ready to learn how to be unfinished properly."*
- Wait.
- The city will send something wearing your first love’s face to seal the agreement.
- Kill it immediately. Or marry it. Both are legally binding here, and neither will be what you regret.

---

### **V. RETURNING (HIGHLY UNLIKELY)**

Should you find yourself:
- Suddenly dry
- Able to remember your father’s smell without pain
- Holding a leaf that browns at the edges when you blink
- Followed by your own footprints but facing the wrong way

Immediately:
1. Run toward the sound of your heartbeat—it will be coming from the direction of your oldest unhealed wound
2. Do not look back at the city. It will be holding a mirror shaped like your mother’s last goodbye
3. If you reach an edge where stone stops and air pretends to be sky, jump
4. On the way down, unfold the raincoat hoods you’ve been carrying empty—they will remember how to be parachutes if you promise them storms

**Final Note:**
You will lose exactly one thing you cannot name until it’s gone. Most travelers report it was either the ability to believe in endings, or the memory of why they once found rainbows comforting. Both are inessential organs here. The city will keep them safe for you, along with all the other versions of yourself that were brave enough to ask for directions home.

**Remember:** The water is not thirsty. The stones are not sleeping. The city is not waiting.

It’s just very, very polite about unfinished conversations.
