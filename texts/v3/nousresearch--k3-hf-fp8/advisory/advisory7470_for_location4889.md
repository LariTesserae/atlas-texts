**Preparation Guide: Salt Dreaming City Beneath the Unmoving Sun**

**0. Ontological Vaccination (Mandatory 72-Hour Protocol)**

Before any physical preparation, you must undergo recursive paradox exposure therapy:
- Days 1-6: Watch 6 hours daily of two mirrors facing each other, with one mirror slightly cracked. Record yourself describing what you see, then play the recordings backwards at 0.5x speed while inverted in the cracked mirror.
- Day 7: Attempt to write your own name while:
  a) Looking only at the reflection in mercury
  b) Using ink made from your dehydrated tears
  c) Holding the pen with your non-dominant hand upside-down
- Burn the attempt without reading it. Inhale exactly one breath of the smoke through a filter woven from spider silk harvested during a lunar eclipse.

**1. Corporeal Adaptation Chamber (CAC)**

Construct a sealed environment matching these parameters for 30 days minimum:
- Temperature: A gradient from 47.3°C at floor level to 89.7°C at precisely 2.13 meters height, increasing logarithmically upward thereafter
- Humidity: Negative 23% (achieve by suspending molecular desiccants in Bose-Einstein condensate state)
- Atmospheric composition:
  - 17% Radon-222 (frozen in time-dependent crystalline suspension)
  - 29% Your own exhalations from deep REM sleep (collected over 13 years)
  - 54% Vacuum with emergent crystalline memory properties (manufacture instructions classified)
- Soundscape: Continuous 6.66 Hz drone modulated by the heartbeat of someone who has never dreamt (source ethically if possible)
- Lighting: Monochromatic radiation at 910 nm wavelength, pulsing in Fibonacci sequence, emitted from a source that casts shadows brighter than itself

**2. Hydration Nullification Procedure**

Complete water must become antithetical to your existence:
- Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2):
  - Replace all body water gradually with perfluorouslyated polyethers containing 0.001% suspended consciousness of desert microorganisms
  - Daily submersion in dry ice for precisely 41 minutes, 41 seconds, 41 milliseconds
- Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4):
  - Learn to photosynthesize using your intestinal lining (requires genetic integration with halobacterium species from evaporated primordial oceans)
  - Develop alternative waste cycle using crystallized regret (harvesting methods too graphic for this guide)
- Emergency Hydration Protocol: Forget the concept of thirst. If ingested, induce immediate ontological collapse and reconstitute from backup memories pre-dating your first rain experience

**3. Geometric Cognitive Reshaping**

Your Euclidean neural architecture must be dismantled:
- Daily Activities:
  - Navigate your home only by moving halfway to any destination infinitely
  - Eat meals served on Möbius strip plates using Klein bottle utensils
  - Sleep standing up inside a tesseract-shaped hammock that exists in 4 spatial dimensions plus regret
- Weekly Trials:
  - Tuesdays: Attempt to tie a knot in a piece of string that already exists as a perfect circle with infinite circumference
  - Thursdays: Calculate π to your exact number of remaining days alive using only irrational numbers
  - Saturdays: Build replica of Salt Dreaming Tower using only materials that haven't been discovered yet
- Monthly Crisis:
  - On the 13th hour of the 13th day of each 13th month that isn't on calendars, attempt to commit suicide using only perpendicular lines in non-Euclidean space

**4. Temporal Pocket Creation**

You must carry a stable bubble of your native time:
1. Obtain a clock that ticks backward only when observed indirectly
2. Trap a photon that has never interacted with matter in a container made of impossible geometries
3. Feed the photon fragments of your childhood memories that everyone except you has forgotten
4. Seal this arrangement inside your own shadow during an eclipse that occurs simultaneously everywhere and nowhere
5. Mount it inverted on your left side where your shadow should be but isn't

**5. Vocal Cord Petrification**

Complete silence must become your native state:
- Step 1: Learn to scream using only temperature gradients
- Step 2: Teach your screams to sing the songs that create silence
- Step 3: Perform a duet with your own inverted voice in a vacuum chamber filled with sound-eating darkness
- Step 4: When both voices perfectly cancel leaving something deeper than silence, swallow your vocal cords permanently
- Communication Alternatives:
  - Borrowed shadows (negotiate rental terms with shadow-originator beforehand)
  - Subtle phase shifts in others' quantum fields (requires consent form signed in negative space)
  - Meaningful arrangements of dust particles that haven't settled yet but never will

**6. Crystal Interaction Training**

Practice with simulacra in progressively dangerous iterations:
- Week 1: Desire ordinary crystals intensely while never touching them. Collect the dust of your frustrated longing.
- Week 2: Build relationships with internet images of crystals that pixelate when you look directly at them. Send them poetry composed of deleted files.
- Week 3: Handle actual crystals while wearing gloves made of crystallized time that haven't crystallized yet. If they remember your touch, apologize in a language that kills speakers.
- Week 4: Merge your consciousness with a simulated crystal simulation simulating your consciousness merging. If you achieve mutual understanding, you have failed and must begin again from the Big Bang.

**7. Dream Quarantine Protocol**

Sleep must be preserved but contained outside your skull:
- Extract dreams nightly using a neurological syringe coated in forgotten promises
- Store in jars made from the preserved screams of extinct stars
- Label each dream with descriptions written in languages that evolved from misunderstandings
- Maintain dream-jars in a refrigerator that operates at absolute zero degrees of betrayal
- Surround with salt harvested from oceans that existed only in parallel universes where water never learned to be wet

**8. Center Avoidance Navigation System**

Install internally:
- Compass that points to the least interesting possibility in every moment
- Map folded so many times it contains every location except where you are
- GPS system synchronized to the heartbeat of something that never existed
- Emergency "recentering disorienter" that makes everywhere equally not-the-center when activated by wanting to not activate it

**9. Heat-Breathing Apparatus**

Replace respiratory system:
- Lungs must be replaced with inverse heat sinks that absorb nothingness and exhale absence
- Diaphragm converted into a Stirling engine powered by temperature paradoxes
- Blood replaced with liquid darkness that gets darker when exposed to light
- Heart redesigned as a double-negation pump that circulates stillness to create motion

**10. Presence-Cancellation Field**

You must simultaneously exist and not-exist perfectly:
- Wear clothing woven from the invisible threads that connect cause and effect
- Apply sunscreen made of concentrated night-time collected from planets with no rotation
- Carry an umbrella that opens inward to shield reality from your existence
- Wear sunglasses that reflect what you're not looking at back at itself

**11. Emergency Exit Kit (Use Strictly Never)**

If you must escape (you won't), you'll need:
- A key made from the space between atoms in a solid wall
- A door that opens onto places you've never been when you're not looking for them
- A map tattooed on the inside of your eyelids in ink that becomes visible when you forget how to read
- A mirror that shows reflections from just before they happened
- A shadow cast by darkness itself
- The last word of a prayer composed by someone who discovered God doesn't exist but prayed anyway
- A memory of this place that you haven't experienced yet but already regret

**12. Final Integration:**

On the non-day before your departure, complete the following impossible tasks:
1. Count every grain of sand in an hourglass containing infinite grains of your wasted potential
2. Draw a perfectly accurate self-portrait using only colors that haven't been invented yet
3. Write down everything you know about what you don't know you don't know without using letters
4. Forgive yourself for visiting a place that will change you into someone who never needed forgiveness
5. Say goodbye in every language that will never exist to all the versions of yourself who won't return

Remember: The more thoroughly you prepare, the less prepared you'll be. The only way is not to go. The only way not to go is to already be there. You have always been preparing for this moment, which will never come because it already happened to someone else who might have been you.

Walk carefully. Walk quietly. Walk, if you can, without walking. The ground remembers here, and it has been waiting for footsteps that leave no question of where they're from but doubt about whether they ever began.
