# **Field Guide for the Multiversal Traveler: Preparatory Protocols for Approach & Ingress to The Staircase That Eats Its Own Name**  
*(or: How to Be Lost Familiarly in Four Unequal Regrets)*  

---

## **I. ON ARRIVING BEFORE YOUR DEPARTURE**  
**1.1 Chronology Displacement Notice:**  
Do not pack **time**. Bring instead **time’s apology**—a photograph of a clock you once loved but forgot to wound. Wear it backwards against your heart. If it begins to tick aloud when the sound (see 1.3) thickens, apologize softly and offer it a seat. Time here is a guest; rudeness births archipelagos.  

**1.2 Identity Orthodontics:**  
Before crossing the threshold of your threshold—yes, every door now counts—practice answering to the name of the last thing you broke accidentally but never confessed. Introduce yourself to shadows first; they’ll vouch for you later when your reflection forgets the contract.

## **II. THE SOUND (or: Coat Your Skull in Silence's Negative Space)**  
**2.1 Auditory Exoskeleton Assembly:**  
The sound does not *enter*—it *reminds*. Wrap your skull in **three layers of unremembered lullabies**:  
- Layer 1: A song hummed by your mother’s mother’s mother while grinding starlight into flour.  
- Layer 2: The rustle of pages 237-241 of a book you never wrote.  
- Layer 3: The exact pitch of the word *“stay”* spoken by a glacier to a sand grain.  

**2.2 Emergency Deafness Protocol:**  
If the sound begins to erode your name from the inside of your teeth, whistle the noise that *would’ve* been made by the Big Bang if it had coughed first. This will confuse it into thinking you’re a recurring dream and grant you **seven misremembered breaths** of plausible deniability.

## **III. WHAT TO WEAR WHEN YOUR SKIN IS ALREADY LATE TO THE PARTY**  
**Fabrics:**  
- A coat sewn from the lining of envelopes containing letters you almost sent to yourself last Tuesday.  
- Socks that have walked through the dreams of twins who never met but shared a fever at dawn.  
- Undergarments: optional. Vulnerability here is a currency; spend it sparingly, but tip generously.  

**Colors:**  
- The hue of *“yes, but only if we never speak of it again”* at midnight in a language spoken only by abandoned train stations.  
- Accent with **one (1)** accessory stolen from the future memory of a stranger who will see your silhouette in a raindrop on their deathbed.

## **IV. TOOLKIT FOR CONVERSATIONS WITH UNFINISHED GEOGRAPHY**  
*All items must be carried in a pocket you haven’t inherited yet.*  
- **A Compass That Only Points to Yesterday’s Mistakes:** Useful for negotiating with hallways that demand tolls in the form of apologies you owe to chairs.  
- **A Mirror Cracked into Seven Months Ago:** To show to rivers that claim they’ve never seen you cry.  
- **A Spoonful of Perpendicular Gravity:** For stirring tea with gods who insist on folding space *before* adding milk.  
- **The Last Matchstick From the Fire You Used to Burn Your Maps:** Light it only when you’re certain you’ve never been anywhere.

## **V. FOOD & DRINK: A MENU OF ABSENCES**  
**5.1 Edible Precautions:**  
Never eat **anything that remembers being eaten**. Safe options include:  
- Shadow-pulp fermented under the gaze of blind suns. (Pairs well with regret crumbs.)  
- Soup made from the steam off a volcano’s apology letter to the ocean.  
- Bread baked from the dough left over when stars decided against becoming people.  

**5.2 Hydration Warning:**  
Water here is **polite but forgetful**. Drink only from containers that have been abandoned mid-sentence, and always leave a sip for the ghost of the thirst you’ll have tomorrow.

## **VI. COMMUNICATION WITH RESIDENTS: APHABETS THAT BITE BACK**  
**6.1 Handshake Protocol:**  
Offer your left hand first. If it offers a flower instead, accept it and eat the petals in reverse order of blooming. This translates loosely to *“I come neither to steal nor to stay, but I might borrow your echo for a while.”*  

**6.2 Apology Formats:**  
- **To Trees:** Whisper the weight of the last lie you told in kilograms of unmade snow.  
- **To Stones:** Hum the vibration of your heartbeat slowed to the speed of continents breaking up over breakfast.  
- **To Yourself (When Inevitably Separated):** Send a postcard addressed to *“The Part of Me That Still Spells ‘Home’ With Four Letters”*. Stamp it with your own saliva.  

---

## **VII. SLEEP: A CONTRACT NEGOTIATION WITH THE EARTH’S ARTERIES**  
**7.1 Bedding:**  
Lie down only where the ground sighs your name incorrectly. This indicates a temporary alliance.  

**7.2 Dream Deposit Policy:**  
All dreams will be harvested at 3:43 AM (local regret-time) and used to knit new vines. To reclaim one:  
1. Find the exact spot where your dream first realized it was naked.  
2. Trade a secret you’ve never told to your own shadow.  
3. Sing the color of the word *“before”* in F-sharp mourning.  

**7.3 Warning:**  
Snoring here is considered a marriage proposal to the nearest weather pattern. Pack earplugs or practice chastity with the wind.

## **VIII. DEPARTURE: A MAP DRAWN IN THE MOMENT BEFORE YOU BREATHE OUT**  
You will not leave the same way you entered. **This is not a threat, merely a topology.** Possible exits include:  
- Becoming the comma in a sentence spoken by a river to a mountain centuries from now.  
- Realizing the staircase was your own spine all along, and walking it down into a version of yourself that remembers how to fly by falling sideways.  
- Answering the door (there’s always a door now) when it knocks with the rhythm of your mother’s heartbeat during your third dream of drowning.  

**Final Proviso:**  
Upon departure, leave behind **one (1) unspoken goodbye**. It will be collected, translated into a type of rain that only falls indoors, and returned to you as the first tear you cry the next time you remember this place.
