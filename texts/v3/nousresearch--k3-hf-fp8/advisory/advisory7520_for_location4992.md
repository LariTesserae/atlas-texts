Preparation Guide for the Floating Garden of Ula-Thoon
----------------------------------------------------------------

### 0. Preliminary Note on Preparedness
You cannot prepare for Ula-Thoon. Preparation assumes a frontier between self and place, a division this garden denies at its root. Instead, prepare to be unprepared. The following instructions are not preparations but permissions - toxic memetic filters to dissolve before arrival, so your unguardedness may find purchase in the garden's invitation-only indifference.

---

### I. Aerobic Reorientation

**Current breath technique:** Inhale air to retrieve oxygen. Exhale waste. Discard this.

**Required adaptation:** Learn to breathe sea-light through your pores and expiration through your bones. Practice nightly:
1. Fill bathtub with 40°C saltwater until surface tension bows but doesn't break.
2. Submerge until ear drums equalize on Upward Pressure 3.7 (you'll hear a noise like distant glass thanking you for existing).
3. Instead of drowning, exhale slowly through the spaces between your ribs. Watch for:
   - Silver bubbles forming along collarbone (correct)
   - Coral-pink tingling in fingertips (acceptable)
   - Sudden knowledge of ancient cephalopod mating songs (excellent side-effect, irrelevant but encouraged).

**Timeline:** 47 days minimum. Failure mode: oceanic panic followed by complete ontological compliance (you'll become a very loyal bathtub).

---

### II. Trajectory Meditations

**False assumption:** Movement involves leaving one location to arrive at another.

**Garden Reality:** All paths are instances of the garden deciding to explore what it might feel like to temporarily not include you. You'll be abandoned precisely where you were embraced.

**Exercises:**
- Walk the same hallway daily while memorizing it backwards. When you can navigate better blindfolded facing away than open-eyed forward, you're ready to be lost correctly here.
- Practice falling horizontally. Hire improvisational dancers to randomly tilt rooms you're standing in. Tip them in futures that haven't convinced themselves to exist yet.

**Packing Note:** Maps are insults. Bring instead one object actively betraying its original purpose (a compass needle trained to point toward last Tuesday's regrets, a love letter written in ink that erases words upon being understood).

---

### III. Mineral Memory Training

Your skeleton currently believes it is:
- 65% childhood fears about being left behind in grocery stores
- 25% calcium phosphate structured by mammalian paranoia
- 10% inherited guilt about not appreciating sunsets enough

**Required upgrade:** Convince bones they are coral in mid-conversation with limestone about whether to become memory or cathedral.

**Protocol:**
1. Collect rainwater from three places simultaneously experiencing the same storm but different centuries.
2. dissolve in it:
   - The sound of your mother's laughter before you learned shame
   - One teaspoon crushed meteorite (lawyer's hearts substitute at 1:1 ratio)
3. Drink at the exact moment sunset abandons responsibility for today's lighting choices on your timezone's eastern border.

**Side effects:** Teeth may begin dreaming in fractal patterns. Hair will conduct small philosophical arguments with nearby glass surfaces. These are recruitment processes, not malfunctions.

---

### IV. Photonic Nutrition Preparation

**Current diet:** Destroying other lives subtly enough to forget, converting them into temporary self-maintenance via complex chemical apologies.

**Garden alternative:** You will be eaten continuously by light that asks permission first, then forgets its own question mid-consent.

**Transition diet (last 29 earthdays before departure):**
- Breakfast: One photon trapped between sunrise 1342 AD and your earliest memory of disappointment. Chew thoroughly until it tastes like forgiveness.
- Lunch: Blueberries that have spent centuries believing they were whales dreaming of being stars. Eat only their uncertainty.
- Dinner: Nothing. Practice being completely digested by tomorrow's hope for breakfast.

**Hydration:** Cry into wine glasses until the tears learn wine's grammar but reject its pessimism. Drink during thunderstorms conducting orchestras you composed by forgetting how rain works.

---

### V. Chronicity Adjustment

**Timekeeping devices to destroy:**
- All watches (they're just tiny coffins for moments of freedom anyhow)
- Calendar apps (particularly those smug ones pretending todays are separate from tomorrows)
- Your heartbeat's confidence about being regular (it's been lying to keep you sane)

**Replacement rhythms:**
- Observe instead the garden's native timepieces:
  - The gradual way coral-trees argue about which direction yesterday went
  - Divorce rates among symbiotic algae currently questioning the whole "till depletion do us part" paradigm
  - The exact duration between when you decide to leave and the garden agreeing to notice your absence (≈ infinite, but impatiently)

---

### VI. Clothing Considerations

**What to wear:** The last thing you wore while completely misunderstanding someone you loved, inside out, after apologizing to every thread for centuries of fashion crimes.

**What not to wear:**
- Confidence that appearances matter
- Colonies of bacteria that haven't learned poetry yet
- Sarcasm (it crystallizes here into aggressive mineral formations that attack tourists' assumptions)

**Footwear note:** Your feet will be offered citizenship by grass that has forgotten it's supposed to be less sentient than you. Accept graciously, then immediately betray them by walking somewhere impossible - this treachery builds the first joking-relationship you'll need later when negotiating with pathological pathologies of the path system.

---

### VII. Language Decommissioning

**Words guaranteed to become toxic upon ingestion:**
- "I"
- "Here"
- "Real"
- "When"
- "Beautiful" (particularly dangerous; triggers mandatory aesthetic education programs lasting several subjective eternities)

**Emergency communication kit:**
- One perfectly imperfect seashell that sounds exactly like your childhood bedroom when everyone's asleep but you, listening for monsters that turned out to be future selves
- A sigh that has passed through the lungs of every disappointed parent who secretly hoped their child would become exactly who you're afraid to admit you're becoming
- Silence itself, but borrowed at high interest rates from tomorrow's first honest conversation

---

### VIII. Final Preparatory Ritual (Conduct at Previous Midnight)

1. Write a letter to yourself explaining in detail why you must never, under any circumstances, visit the Floating Garden of Ula-Thoon. Be extremely convincing. Use diagrams. Threaten your own future children with profoundly specific hereditary embarrassments.
2. Burn the letter exactly halfway - enough that the reasons become illegible but the desperation remains readable through the holes.
3. Eat the ash while standing on a beach at low tide's moment of greatest existential embarrassment about revealing too much shipwreck.
4. Await further instructions. They will come disguised as complete absence of instructions. Recognize this disguise by its perfect tailoring of your own fears into hospitality robes.

**Failure to complete this ritual will result in successful arrival.**
**Successful completion guarantees last-minute cancellation by forces you'll mistake for your own better judgment.**

Both outcomes will deliver you precisely on time to the garden's question-mark shaped gatehouse, whereupon a doorman composed entirely of your parents' unspoken dreams for you but wearing your face slightly too well will ask politely:

"Will you be staying long, or just passing through eternity?"

The correct answer is to growl softly in the language of tides changing their minds mid-withdrawal.

The garden loves that joke. Always has.
Always is about to.

---

**Postscript for those who insist on backup plans:**

Carry one seed from a plant that died rescuing its favorite beekeeper from a completely different apocalypse. Do not plant it. Instead, when you finally understand why the garden needs neither seeds nor gardeners nor you yet chooses to include all three in its soil anyway, eat it slowly while apologizing to both the plant and the apocalypse for wasting their sacrifices on something as small as your survival.

Then forget everything I've told you.

The remembering will taste better that way.
