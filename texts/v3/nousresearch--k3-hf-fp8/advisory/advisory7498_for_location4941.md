**1. Body Preparation (Physical Substrate Edition)**

1.1 **Unlearn Down.**  
Gravity is not cruel here; it is shy. It wraps around curiosity like a blanket made of apologies. Practice walking **toward your own shadow’s navel** for three consecutive dawns on a planet where shadows shrink noonward. Bonus: do this while humming a lullaby backwards at 73.6 rpm. If you cannot find such a planet, simulate by falling asleep on a descending escalator while holding a half-written apology letter addressed to your childhood understanding of why rivers don’t reverse.

1.2 **Tooth Calibration.**  
File molars to approximate the bite radius of a silicon-carbide gearlet (0.03 mm asymmetrical). These are not for eating. They are for **negotiating silence** if you accidentally swallow a spore-sphere mid-thought. Do not worry about harm; the sphere contains your dental records already, update pending your next blink.

1.3 **Desiccate Thoroughly, Then Cry Over It.**  
Hydration creates false memory of tides. Drink nothing but **distilled sunlight filtered through quartz regrets** for one lunar cycle of a moon that committed suicide by becoming a ring. Collect your tears in a vial labeled *"For the engine that envies rain but fears dissolution."* Leave the vial behind. Bring only the label, folded into an origami throat that never swallows.

1.4 **Hair Removal (Emotional Spectroscopy Protocol).**  
Every follicle remembers being a feather that once betrayed the sky. Remove all hair using a blade forged from the sound of scissors hesitating before cutting a newborn’s umbilical chord heat-death echo. Store the hair in a vacuum sealed with your last dream **about flying that ended in applause**. You will trade this later for a moment of unguarded observation.

**3. Cognitive Realignment (Or: How I Learned to Stop Lining My Thoughts Up in Rows and Let Them Clang Like Empty Pots Forever)**

3.1 **Memory Ablation (Selective Nostalgia Retention)**  
Erase all memories of:  
- The word "home" as a location instead of a **missed punctuation mark**.  
- The color of your bedroom ceiling at midnight under the assumption it was always the same.  
- The taste of water pretending to be tasteless.  
Preserve only:  
- The sound of a hinge squeaking in a house that was never built.  
- The texture of the word "almost" scraping against your teeth like a promise made of chalk.

3.2 **Desire Decantation**  
Write down every thing you want. Boil the list in ink made from your own fever dreams until it reduces to a single sentence ending with **"but only if it forgets me too."** Drink this sentence while staring at a photograph of the horizon taken from above. Do not blink until the horizon blinks first.

3.3 **Dream Injection (Counterfeit Memories for Trade)**

- Attempt to hug the copper-rubber tree. Fail gently.  
- Count the spore-gear-silences until you lose count at a number that sounds like your mother’s heartbeat underwater.

**4. Arrival Procedure (Non-Euclidean Etiquette)**

4.1 **Do Not Look for the Door.**  
The door is the memory you forgot to forget. Instead, wait in a place where three temperatures of nostalgia intersect (recommended: the spice aisle of a supermarket where your first kiss still haunts the cinnamon). When you hear a sound like **glass sighing in reverse**, inhale sharply and exhale the name of a star that died before it was born. If you do this correctly, you will suddenly remember you are already late.

4.2 **Upon Entry (Post-Entrance Protocol)**  
You will find yourself standing inside your own shadow, which is now spherical and made of hesitation. Greet it politely but do not apologize for the intrusion. It has been practicing your arrival longer than you have been breathing.

4.3 **Orientation (Optional Failure Exercise)**  
Attempt to determine which direction is **"toward"**. This will take exactly 73.6 minutes and will conclude with the realization that "toward" is the sound your blood makes when it forgets it is bound by veins. Accept this failure gracefully. It is the currency of the realm.

**5. Interactions with Indigenous Overgrowth (Avoidance is a Form of Worship)**

5.1 **The Copper-Rubber-Cinnabar Tree (Symbiote Designate: "Almost-Tree-Not-Tree")**  
- Do not speak to it directly. If it speaks to you (**it will sound like your own voice filtered through a regret that has learned to sing the blues in binary**), respond only by rearranging the ash at your feet into a pattern that criticizes the concept of symmetry without being asymmetrical.
- If it offers you fruit (it won’t), decline by explaining that you are allergic to outcomes.
- If it does not offer you fruit (it will), thank it anyway for the conversation you pretended not to have.

5.2 **The Spore-Gear-Silences (Heisenberg’s Honeybees, Unionized)**  
- Do not attempt to catch them. They are already inside your observation and negotiating rent.
- Do not count them aloud. They reproduce by being enumerated by minds that failed algebra emotionally.
- Do not trust the ones that look like the faces of people you almost loved. They are the faces of people **you will almost love in the past tense**, and they are practicing your disappointment.

5.3 **The Ash (Memory’s Exfoliant, Hoarder of Footprints That Never Were)**  
- Walk on it like you are walking on a poem about walking written by someone who has only ever heard rumors of feet.
- If it sings beneath your steps (**sounds like sandpaper lullabies played on a violin made of moth wings**) stop walking and listen until you forget the difference between the song and the silence it is trying to hide.
- If it offers you a memory of a place you have never been, accept it gratefully and then bury it immediately in the same spot. This is how the ash forgives you for existing.

**6. Departure (Or: How to Leave Before You Arrive)**

6.1 **There is no departure.**  
There is only the moment you realize the **gravity leash was never tied to anything but the idea of being tied**. This realization will occur exactly 19.2 milliseconds after the engine beats the number of times equal to the number of times you have blinked while reading this sentence.

6.2 **Return Preparation (Non-Compliance Encouraged)**  
If you absolutely must attempt to return:  
- Forget everything. Particularly this manual. Especially the parts written in your handwriting that you don’t recall writing.
- Erase every memory that contains the word "return" except for the memory of a bird learning to fly by forgetting there ever was such a thing as ground.
- Finally, and most importantly, **blink**. But do it slowly. Like you are closing a book that never existed about a place that was never lost because it was never found.

**7. Post-Arrival Syndrome (Symptoms of Having Been There Without Leaving)**

Upon waking (you will always wake up, even if you never arrived), you may experience:

- The sensation of walking barefoot on ash that isn’t there, particularly in crowded rooms where everyone is wearing shoes made of conversations you interrupted by existing.
- Dreams in which you are a tree made of copper wires and rubber regrets, dreaming of being a human dreaming of being a tree.
- The inability to maintain eye contact with clocks, because they always look like they are about to apologize for the time you spent trying to leave a place that never asked you to arrive.
- A sudden, overwhelming nostalgia for a sound that hasn’t happened yet but sounds like **"shh, the engines are almost asleep now."**

**Treatment:** None. It is not a sickness. It is a souvenir. Wear it proudly like a scar in the shape of a question mark that never learned to ask questions because it was too busy listening to the silence between answers.
