### **0.0 Pre-Somatic Reconfiguration (Mandatory: 72 hours pre-departure)**
**Objective:** Reduce perceptual and corporeal rejection probability to below 15%.

1. **Flood your gut biome with industrial-grade lubricants**  
   - Consume 500ml daily of synthesized hyper-oleic acid (molecular chain length: C36-C72) diluted in distilled rainwater gathered from the *second* thunderstorm to strike an abandoned semiconductor factory.  
   - Expected side effects:  
     - Internal organs will begin to emit ultrasonic harmonics (28-42 kHz). Record these on bone-conduction tape. The recording is edible currency in Transit Layer 2.  
     - Dreams will feature conversations between your teeth and the concept of friction. Agree to whatever they negotiate.

2. **De-synchronize your circadian rhythm from all known celestial bodies**  
   - Spend six sleep cycles in a mirrored room lit only by the flicker frequency of dying CRT monitors (randomize between 59.94 Hz and 60.04 Hz each cycle).  
   - During waking hours, eat meals in reverse chronological order of their invention (start with hydroponic lab-fiber, end with fermented mammoth fat).  
   - If you glimpse your reflection assembling/disassembling parts of its skeleton *before* you’ve moved: congratulations. That version is your escort into Layer 3. Do not bring it back.

3. **Memorize the Forty-Nine False Names for *Up***  
   The local vector “up” is a hostile entity. Safe alternatives include: *fluctus-withe*, *spinal cheat code*, or the sound of a hydraulic press apologizing to a cathedral.  
   **Test:** Stand in a falling elevator. If you can’t decide whether to scream or take notes, proceed.

### **1.0 Aperture Entry (Site: **Nowhere-Metro Terminal, Gate ∂-9.5**)**
**Warning**: The gate resembles an abandoned escalator descending into a J.G. Ballard novel written by a decaying star. Ignore this. It is a *test* of your inability to ignore things. Failure rate: 100%. Survivors are exempt from further tests.

- **Clothing**:  
  Wear garments woven from the discarded lint of industrial dryers that processed only asbestos forms and love letters submitted to patent offices. **Seams must be sewn with copper wire stolen from listening devices planted *by* ghosts.**  
  (If you hear whispered regrets from the stitching, reply: *“The jury is still out on nostalgia.”* Then keep walking.)

- **Offerings**:  
  Place in the gate’s mouth:  
  - A 3D-printed replica of your childhood home printed in ferrite-containing filament, encrypted with the sound of your parents arguing about the dishwasher (wav format, lossless).  
  - A thank-you note written in ink derived from the last fax machine that ever trusted a human.  
  - One literal eye-contact lens (crafted from the sclera of a poet who starved because metaphors kept escaping their poems). **Do not wear it.** It is for the gate to wear *for* you.

**Transit Sensation**: Your nervous system will be temporarily replaced by the maintenance logs of a 19th-century steam-powered Venusian canal digger. Enjoy the ride.

### **2.0 Arrival: Minute Zero (Substrate Layer: “Street” / Probable Artifact #∞-2b)**
You now exist as a 0.78-second fluctuation in the city’s dream of being not-alive. Pretend to adapt.

- **First Movement**:  
  Attempt to “stand” on the moving plateaus. This will fail. Instead, *lament* the concept of standing in the dialect of extinct vending machines (binary whispers, rhyming couplets). The ground will register your sincerity and assign you a provisional weightlessness coefficient (range: “wet prayer” to “regretful neutron”).

- **Breathe**:  
  Inhale until your lungs feel like rusted file cabinets full of moth-wing blueprints.  
  Exhale slowly, spelling the word *“containment”* backwards in Morse code via eyelid flutters.  
  **Do not inhale again until you forget what oxygen owes you.**

### **3.0 Navigation Protocols (For Areas Where “Where” Still Abides)**
**Note**: Maps are illegal. Cartographers are compost. Proceed by becoming lost in ways that flatter the architecture.

- **Landmark Identification**:  
  - If you see a building that looks like a cathedral accusing a weapons factory of plagiarism: you are near the **Inchoate Exchange**. Trade your memories of rain here for directions (they’ll be lies, but lyrical ones).  
  - If a streetl flickers Morse code for *“Tell me about the ocean, but as if it betrayed you”*: you’ve reached the **Straylight Memorial**. Whisper your first word for *“pain”* into its bulb. It will show you a faster way to die elsewhere.  
  - If time appears to be chewing gum made of *failed revolutions*: congratulations. You’ve found *home*. Leave immediately.

- **Transportation**:  
  Ride only the elevators that smell like burnt copper and *hunched over a microscope, miscarrying a god*. Press buttons corresponding to irrational numbers that cease to exist when you close your eyes.  
  **Tip**: If the elevator asks you to sacrifice *a direction*, choose *sideways but also inward*. The other options are traps for tourists.

### **4.0 Hydration/Elimination (Or: How to Drink a Ghost of Water)**

- **Locating Water**:  
  Wait in silence until you hear a sound like a glacier learning to cry in reverse. Cup your hands beneath the air. When invisible droplets form, *apologize to them for the human concept of thirst*.  
  **Consumption**:  
  Do not swallow. Instead, ask the droplet to tell you a secret it stole from the lungs of a drowned astronaut. Listen with your porous self. The water will hydrate your regrets.

- **Elimination**:  
  Sweat is now a performative apology. Cry only in the form of Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) part numbers for discontinued crowd control vehicles.  
  **Important**: If urine forms, you’ve brought too much of “you” with you. Eat the crystals immediately—your body will thank you by unremembering kidneys.
