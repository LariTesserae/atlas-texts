### **0. ABANDON YOURSELF BEFORE ENTERING**
Eremonos doesn’t kill travelers. It *refiles* them under **PERSONA NON GRAVITAS**. Leave your name at the last checkpoint before the sky flips—**names are seeds here, and the city gardens in echoes**. The gatekeepers are mirrors that reflect who you *borrowed* your face from. Smile until they blink first.

---

### **1. GEAR: ONLY WHAT COULD BETRAY YOU**
- **Clothing:** Wear contradictions stitched with *regret fiber*. A coat that remembers summer beaches but smells of winter graves. **Buttons must disagree with their holes** (ideally about the ethics of closure). Shoes should be *unresolved quarrels*—one boot heel taller, whispering directions to places you’ve fled.
- **Oxygen:** **Oxygen is a conspiracy**. Instead, carry a **glass jar of exhaled apologies** from someone you’ll never forgive. Breathe only when the air forgets to hurt you. If your lungs demand more, negotiate with the **pigeon cartel** (exchange rate: one secret syllable per gasp).
- **Timepiece:** No clocks. **Carry a stopped heart**—preferably a poet’s—tied to your wrist with a string of unfulfilled promises. When it ticks backward, you’ve arrived yesterday. DO NOT WAVE HELLO TO YOURSELF. That one owes me years.

### **2. NAVIGATION: GET LOOST**
Maps are **autobiographical parasites**. Memorize the taste of **north at midnight** instead (hint: copper and abandonment). Streets change names by the number of times you blink at the moon. **Correct wayfinding is punishable by destination**.

- **Landmarks:** Trust only things that **resent being noticed**:  
  - The **statue of a forgotten goodbye** that weeps rust only when you turn away.  
  - A **stop sign** that honks in Morse: *”THERE IS NO SAFE PLACE TO STOP BEING.”*  
  - The **bridge that divides by zero**, where suicides go to become traffic jams.  
- **Directions:** Ask the **rat librarians** (see section 5). Pay them with a question you’re afraid to answer alone. If they guide you down a throat-shaped alley, **recite your crimes backwards**—but add one you haven’t committed yet for flavor.

### **3. BIOLOGICAL ARRANGEMENTS**
Flesh is **optional but taxed**. If insisting on corporeality:

- **Digestion:** Food is memories you’re allergic to. Eat only **mirrorfruit**—tastes like the last time you laughed without purpose. Spit the seeds; they grow into strangers who’ll ask for directions home.  
- **Sleep:** Dream with **one eye open, the other closed in protest**. Beds are cages for sleeping gods—**break them before they dream you back**.  
- **Elimination:** Waste is archived. Use the **public unthought stations** (marked by urinals singing lullabies in dead currencies). **Flush gently**; the sewers trade secrets with the birth canals of stars.

### **4. CURRENCY AND CONVERSATION**
Wealth is **weighed in absences**. Carry:

- **Pockets full of holes** from clothes abandoned during quick exits.  
- **A laugh you borrowed from a dying star** (repayment due: entropy’s birthday).  
- **Seven apologies** in ascending order of sincerity (fake the seventh; it’s expected).

**DO NOT SPEAK DIRECTLY.** Words are **contractually obligated to act against you**. Instead:

- **Hum in the key of unopened letters**.  
- **Gesture with shadows you rented from previous crimes**.  
- **Cough up feathers that spell lies in braille**—the blind walls will gossip kindly.

### **5. ALLIES: TRUST ONLY THE TRUST-LESS**
- **The Pigeon Cartel:** Winged spies who traffic in what-could-have-been. Their leader is a **blindfolded feather duster** named *Regret Ascension*. Offer breadcrumbs soaked in lottery tickets that never won.  
- **Rat Librarians:** 12-eyed archivists of underheard things. Their favorite book is **you, Volume E: Drafts Where You Stayed**. Borrowing privileges require a blood oath written in ketchup on a divorce decree.  
- **The Last Maintenance Worker:** A coat hanger in a hard hat, eternally fixing a door that leads to **before doors were invented**. Follow at exactly 0.5 km/h slower than time itself. If he offers a wrench, **refuse politely—it only tightens what should unravel**.

### **6. AVOID OR EMBRACE (THE CITY FINDS BOTH FUNNY)**
- **THE VOID BARBER:** Offers haircuts that shorten life expectancy. His scissors speak in sonnets about broken necks.  
- **THE HEARTBAKE SHOPPE:** Sells pastries filled with your mother’s last words to you, reheated daily. **Gluten-free mourning tastes like ash.**  
- **FULL VOIDS:** (See Intro.) If both moons leave simultaneously, **become a question**. Questions are the only things allowed four-dimensional escape routes.

### **7. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES: THERE ARE NONE**
In case of **reality breach**, **apologize profusely to the breach**. Offer it a job as your replacement. If swallowed by geometry, **remember: inside every triangle is a heartbreak pretending to be stable**. Chew slowly.

### **8. EXIT STRATEGY: FOR QUITTERS**
To leave, **forget why you came** so thoroughly that the city itself ejects you as unsatisfactory. Alternatively:

- **Climb the highest depth** until the sky feels like a floor you can’t afford to clean.  
- **Whisper your name backwards into a locked mailbox** until it coughs up a key made of exit wounds.  
- **Step into a shadow longer than your regrets**—but **don’t look back**. That’s how mirrors invented hunger.

### **POST-TRAVEL DEBRIEF (TO BE READ ONLY IF YOU NEVER LEFT)**
Welcome home. You look different. Or perhaps **differently looking became you**. The bruises on your soul will fade into maps of places you’re not allowed to miss. Feed them silence; they grow into lullabies for storms that haven’t divorced their oceans yet.

Remember: **Eremonos isn’t empty.** It’s just **full of the spaces where you used to fit**.
