You do not visit the Fluted Chambers. You request permission to be carried through them for a measured interval, after which you must leave faster than regret. If the mountain’s mood is favorable and your respect is flawless, you might depart with your memories still pointing north. That is as close as any traveler from the warm worlds can hope to come.

Below is the protocol compiled from the thirteen logbooks recovered intact, the whispers recorded from the five expedition minds who still answer when addressed from within tight circles of copper, and the single complete account etched onto bone spoons found lying in geostationary orbit alongside a severed climbing harness whose buckles were filled with salt grown into the shape of tiny teeth.

---

### **A. Preparation of Self**

1. **Chronological Decoupling**  
   Begin fasting from linearity three lunar cycles before ascent. Each day, wake at sunset, eat your meal at sunrise, sleep at noon, converse only with recordings of your own voice played backward at quarter speed. By the tenth day you will dream before you sleep; by the twentieth you will remember conversations held tomorrow. Maintain this until the sequence of your birthdays becomes uncertain within a span of two decades. Bring no calendar.

2. **Thermal Polarization**  
   Train one side of your body to endure fire, the other to endure liquid helium. Alternate every heartbeat. When you can hold ice in your fire hand until it sublimates while your frost palm melts steel through desire alone, continue to alternate until you forget which side was which. In the Chambers, heat flows backward through your shadow.

3. **Echoreduction**  
   Learn silence that is thicker than stone. Practice walking on frozen lakes while wearing bells; by the time your footfall no longer changes the pitch of the ice beneath you, you may speak again—but only vowels never written in any alphabet. If a bird falls silent when you pass beneath its tree, you have made progress. If a rock answers, begin again with deeper mountains.

4. **Memory Harvest**  
   Extract twelve memories you intend to survive the journey. Seal six beneath your tongue in tiny spheres of beeswax pressed with salt from a lake that has never seen a boat. Dissolve the other six into a draught of ink made from your own blood mixed with soot from a starfire that died before your species named the stars. Drink half; tattoo the rest onto the inside surface of your skull using a needle carved from moonlight bent through a raindrop that never landed. These memories are yours no longer. They are collateral.

---

### **B. Preparation of Equipment**

Bring nothing that has ever been alive. Bring nothing that has never dreamed. Anything between these states will betray you.

Allowed:
- **Suit:** One layer of unweave. Spin it yourself from the empty spaces between sentences in a treaty broken before the ink dried. It will feel like cobwebs spun by spiders who studied mathematics in the dark between quarks. It will protect you from the part of the air that remembers names.
- **Boots:** Hammershaped soles cast from the sound of one hand clapping against the face of a cliff that died laughing. The heel should be hollow, the toe should be hungry.
- **Gloves:** A pair of mirrored mittens knitted from the reflection left behind by a scream that changed its mind halfway out of existence. Touch nothing unless you are prepared to be touched back by its reverse.
- **Eyes:** Replace both eyes with hourglasses filled not with sand but with the last breath of a clock that forgot the meaning of midnight. Before entering the courtyard, turn both hourglasses upside down simultaneously without using your hands. Watch the future pour out of your left eye and into your past. Blink only once.
- **Rations:** Three cubes of voidminted darkness. Each cube contains enough not-food to starve your hunger to death for a week. Do not eat them. If you feel the need to taste them, you have already stayed too long.
- **Light:** One photon trapped in a cage woven from a promise made by a liar who believed every word. Release it only if you are certain you deserve to never be found again.

Forbidden:
- Metal. It dreams too loud here and the dreams are contagious.
- Living tissue. It forgets whose memory it belongs to.
- Straight lines. They offend the mountain’s sense of humor.
- Curves that can be expressed as functions. They remind the stones of their childhood.
- Numbers that end. Any collection you can finish counting will count you back by remaining, and to remain is to be rearranged without consent.

---

### **C. Ascent Procedure**

Begin walking uphill at the moment you realize you are already lost. Look back exactly twice: once to confirm that the path behind you is closing like a wound embarrassed to have bled, and once to apologize to yourself for not dissuading you sooner.

When you encounter the wind that sounds like your mother calling your name from the day before you were born, turn sideways and walk through it like a door. Do not answer.

If you see footprints that match yours exactly but are burned into the snow instead of pressed, step into each one with your other foot. This is how you remember your reverse walk out before you forget you ever left.

At the treeline, which is marked by a single tree growing upside down from the sky with its roots tangled in the stars, stop. Remove your name. Bury it beneath the first thing you find that is not there. Mark the spot with something that cannot exist in three dimensions. Forget the shape immediately.

Continue upward until altitude becomes a flavor. Taste it. If it tastes like regret seasoned with the sound of glass breaking in a room you once loved but can no longer remember why, you are nearing the eastern notch. Prepare to breathe sideways.

---

### **D. Entry Through the Notch**

The Notch is not a passage. It is an argument between two cliffs who forgot what they were arguing about but refuse to be the first to apologize.

To pass, you must settle the argument without either side winning. Whisper a truth that both cliffs have always known but cannot admit without ceasing to be cliffs. The space between their silences will widen just enough for you to slip through sideways, provided you have already removed everything about yourself that is wider than regret.

Upon emerging, you will face the illusion of the shrine. It will appear exactly as described in your briefing document, except for the part that matters. That part will be watching you from just beyond the corner of your eye. Do not turn to see it. If you do, what you see will be so patiently disappointed in you that you will volunteer to become part of the foundation out of sheer propriety.

---

### **E. Conduct Within the Fluted Chambers**

1. **Movement**  
   Walk like a shadow that has forgotten what cast it but remembers the shape of absence perfectly. Pause every third step to listen to the sound your heartbeat made in the instant before it decided to begin. If you hear it answering from a direction you are not facing, change direction by moving only the memories you have not lived yet.

2. **Observation**  
   See without looking. The walls record what you would have thought if you were someone else entirely. If you catch yourself thinking about what you see, apologize to the nearest stone by imagining the sound of water apologizing for being wet without ever having been taught the word.

3. **Interaction**  
   Touch nothing unless it is already touching you back in a language you were born knowing but forgot upon learning to speak. If something offers itself to your hand, ask it three questions in order of increasing unanswerability. If it answers any of them, return it to exactly the position it would occupy if it had never been moved, but do so a fraction of a moment before you reached for it. The acceptable margin of error is smaller than the difference between a wish and its echo.

4. **Duration**  
   Count your stay not in breaths but in the number of breaths the mountain remembers you stealing from its lungscarred past. This number is always one more than you can afford. To leave before you are remembered is to be forgotten forever. To leave after is to ensure that forever will include souvenirs of your shape carved into places where time goes to practice bleeding without making a sound.

5. **Exit Cue**  
   Depart the instant before you understand why you came. A reliable signal is the moment when the lowest note of the roof’s murmur resolves into a word you recognize faintly as the name you used to call yourself before you realized names were cages made of other people’s expectations. Do not look back to confirm. The doorway will close behind you like a mouth swallowing a question that it has decided not to ask.

---

### **F. Descent and Decontamination**

Now you must go down without ever having been up. This is the hardest part.

1. Place both feet on the memory of the highest step you never took. Press downward with all the weight of everything you did not say while you were here. If you feel solid ground forming beneath your doubt, step forward into it. If not, wait until you are no longer sure whether you are falling or flying slowly upward backward. Then step anyway.

2. Do not speak until you have forgotten the taste of silence that tasted like home. Do not listen until you hear a sound that could only be made by one hand clapping in applause for a performance that ended before it began. Do not think until your thoughts become indistinguishable from the spaces between them where the truth lives quietly without caring whether anyone believes in it.

3. When you reach the place where the air tastes like decisions you made when you were someone else, stop. Remove the suit, the boots, the gloves, the eyes. Leave them arranged in the shape of a question that has no answer but keeps asking anyway. Walk away barefoot and blind, singing every note of the roof’s last murmur in reverse order, but sing it so softly that even the wind that carries it believes it is silence.

4. Continue walking until you meet someone who looks exactly like you but slightly younger. Give them this document without comment. If they ask what it is, tell them it is a map to a place they will dream about once but never remember clearly enough to miss. If they ask why they should trust you, reply, "Because I was you before I learned that trust was something you don't give but leave behind like breadcrumbs made of echoes for the parts of yourself that get lost on the way back from places that never let you leave whole."

5. When they take the document, turn around and walk away without looking back. If you hear them calling after you, do not answer unless they use the name you buried at the treeline. If they do, keep walking anyway. Some questions are gifts that can only be given by never being answered.

---

### **Survival Probability**

If you have followed these instructions perfectly, your chances of returning as yourself are approximately equal to the odds of a shadow winning a staring contest with the sun while both are blindfolded with memories of darkness that never happened.

Do not let this discourage you. Let it clarify.

The Fluted Chambers were not built to be survived.

They were built to be visited briefly, rearranged slightly, and remembered forever by everything except the visitors who left behind exactly enough of themselves to ensure that the next traveler would find the absence waiting patiently to be discovered anew.

Go now.

Or better yet, stay exactly where you are, and let the mountain come to you instead.

It has been rising slowly all this time, one forgotten name at a time, practiced at patience by centuries of practicing on stones that thought they were permanent until they learned better from the wind.

When it arrives, greet it politely.

Then wonder, later, if you already did.

(End of briefing. Do not retain this document in any form: memory, matter, or metaphor. The penalty for preservation is indistinguishable from the reward for perfect forgetfulness, but arrives on schedule.

Your schedule ended seventeen syllables ago.)
