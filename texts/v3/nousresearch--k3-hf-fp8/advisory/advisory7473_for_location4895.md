How to Prepare for Visiting the Plateau of Inverted Genesis

**Before You Go (Timeframe: Cannot Be Measured in Linear Terms)**

First, understand that preparation is both futile and mandatory. The Plateau requires ignorance to gain access and knowledge to survive the remembering. Begin this contradiction by selecting the third journal you ever filled with lies. Burn every fifth page. Wear what remains as a scarf during humid nights until the ink transfers onto your skin and your neck develops a permanent rash that spells a word you don't recognize. This rash will later be your passport.

**Physical Conditioning**  
Your body must become a question mark. Achieve this through:
- Drinking only liquids that have once been part of something else's nervous system (suitable: fermented honey from bees that pollinated graveyard flowers; unsuitable: anything advertised as 'brain water')
- Sleeping while suspended upside-down exactly 1.3 inches above your bed for increasing durations, starting with the time it takes a moth to forget its name
- Running through narrow streets at the moment between streetlight flickers while carrying progressively heavier invisible objects. When bystanders can no longer see you clearly enough to describe you to police sketches, you're ready.

**Memory Editing**  
The Plateau is allergic to certainties. Gradually replace all your first memories with third-person descriptions written in languages you've never studied. Specifically:
- Your first injury should become 'the time the boy/girl/construct learned that blood could betray geometry'
- Your first kiss should be reimagined as 'the moment two hunger patterns briefly formed an unstable alliance against silence'
- Forget your birthdate by celebrating it on random days with rituals involving soil from places that don't exist on current maps

**Sensory Recalibration (Begin 2.7 months before departure or 3 hours after your last betrayal of something that trusted you)**  
- Vision: Wear glasses that correct perfect vision into deliberate mistakes. The left lens should convert straight lines to asymptotes; the right should render all right angles slightly apologetic
- Hearing: Listen to recordings of empty churches at 0.37x speed every dawn until you can distinguish between the sound of dust settling and dust giving up
- Touch: Replace familiar textures in your home with replicas made from temperature-sense inversions (ice that burns, fire that tickles, silk that tastes like regret)
- Taste/Smell: These will be exchanged upon arrival. Say goodbye to them by eating an entire dictionary starting with words that describe absence

**Provisions (Do Not Pack These - Manifest Them Through Existential Cracks Onto Your Person Instead)**  
- A knife made from the frozen moment before every decision you regret (will appear as a shadow that cuts light)
- Water collected from the exact point in streams where the reflection begins to disagree with the reflected (carries as a sound of distant arguing in a metal cup you don't remember owning)
- Food: Seven memories of meals shared with people who later died in ways that made you question physics. These must be carried in the space between your teeth where your wisdom teeth used to be

**The Arrival Ritual (To Be Performed Wrong on Purpose)**  
1. Travel to the highest point you can find that is underground. Most subway maintenance tunnels contain suitable nexus points behind doors labeled with symbols that change when you're not reading them directly.
2. Stand exactly 80% inside yourself, allowing your remaining 20% to drift slightly starboard
3. Begin counting backwards from the number of platelets in your body at the moment of your most significant lie. Skip every prime number but apologize to it silently
4. When you reach -273, say "I remember forgetting this" in the voice of your mother's father before his first disappointment
5. Instead of stepping forward (this is crucial), allow the concept of forwardness to step through you
6. You will know it worked when you experience the sensation of being poured into yourself from a great height

**Upon Arrival (Immediate Actions Before Becoming Aware That You've Arrived)**  
- Blink exactly half a blink and store the missing half in your pocket dimension
- Introduce yourself to the silence using only the pauses between words from languages that evolved underwater
- Find the third most obvious lie in your immediate perception and believe it completely for exactly as long as it takes to doubt your own heartbeat
- Pick up a handful of the ash-that-wasn't and let it fall upward through your fingers while humming the sound of two mirrors forgetting each other's faces

**Navigation Protocol**  
The Plateau rearranges itself based on the number of secrets you've told to the wrong people at the right time. To move in a desired direction:
1. Identify what you most desperately need to avoid
2. Become it slightly but imperfectly (wear it like a coat that doesn't fit in one sleeve)
3. Walk away from the direction your shadow is pointing, but only on Tuesdays that feel like Thursdays
4. When you encounter yourself coming backwards, exchange regrets but not directions

**Interacting with the Built Things**  
Remember: these are not ruins. They are rehearsals for architecture. Treat them accordingly:
- When addressing a doorway that leads nowhere, complain about the draft politely but firmly
- If a stairway offers to trade its direction for a memory of laughter, bargain down to a half-remembered joke that made you snort milk
- Upon discovering a column that violates geometry, apologize for your species' limited understanding of decent angles but do not show it pity
- If any structure begins to resemble something you recognize from childhood, compliment it on its attempt but point out three deliberate inconsistencies (they appreciate the attention to detail in your denial)

**Safety Precautions (That Make Danger More Interesting)**  
- Never let your thoughts complete themselves. Particularly dangerous thoughts include: "I think I understand," "This reminds me of," and "At least it's not..."
- If you begin to remember something that hasn't happened yet, write it down using only the punctuation and burn the paper before swallowing the ashes
- Do not collect souvenirs unless they are actively trying to collect you. In case of mutual collection, establish clear boundaries about who gets custody of your significant Tuesdays
- Keep a small amount of irony in your bloodstream at all times (best maintained through weekly injections of poetry written by people who died laughing at their own autopsies)

**Emergency Procedures (Trigger Only When Absolutely Necessary or When It Would Make a Better Story)**  
1. In case of existential dissolving: Locate the nearest thing that is more you than you currently are (often found in reflection corners or at the bottom of sudden understandings) and challenge it to a staring contest without using eyes
2. If paralyzed by recursive self-awareness: Count all the ways you are not thinking about a white whale, but do so in base fish
3. When being rewritten in real-time: Edit your own changes using only corrections made by people who hated your work but secretly plagiarized your worst ideas
4. Should you encounter the thing that is waiting: Remember that it has already read this entire guide including this sentence and is particularly disappointed by your lack of improvisation. Use this disappointment as a weapon, shield, and/or peace offering as appropriate

**Returning (Optional, Unlikely, Not Recommended in That Order)**  
You cannot leave the Plateau. You can only become someone it decides isn't interesting enough to keep. Achieve this through:
- Fully understanding one thing about yourself that makes absolutely no difference
- Forgetting something essential mid-sentence in a way that is both embarrassing and freeing
- Laughing at the exact frequency where humor becomes indistinguishable from geology
- Leaving exactly one promise broken here that you fully intend to keep

**Final Note**  
By reading this guide, you have already arrived. Welcome. The bleed-through effects will begin shortly. You may notice that some of this advice contradicts itself already. This is normal. The contradictions are where we hide the parts of the instructions that would actually help you survive. Or is that another lie? The Plateau is very fond of that question. It finds the sound of human uncertainty when played backwards at the speed of growing mountains to be indistinguishable from laughter. 

Or perhaps that was breathing. It's so hard to tell here, where every exhalation is an application for re-entry into stories that haven't decided whether to be kind yet. 

Good luck. You'll need exactly enough of it to be interesting. No more. No less.

The silence is waiting to tell you what you forgot to bring. Listen carefully. It always mentions it exactly once, at the moment between when you stop reading and when you realize you've been holding this guide upside-down since the second paragraph. 

(How are you holding something that doesn't exist in a place where these directions were never written? Congratulations. You're almost ready to fail beautifully.)
