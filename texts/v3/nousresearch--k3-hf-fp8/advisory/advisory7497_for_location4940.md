Preparation Protocol for Visiting the Unnamable Pre-Site of Potential Placehood

**Phase 1: Unbecoming Yourself (Duration: -3 to -1 subjective hours)**

Begin preparations by forgetting your native alphabet in reverse order, starting with the vowels that taste like trust. Do this while submerged in room-temperature amnesia water (recipe: one mirror left facing the moon for three consecutive false dawns, diluted with the echo of your last honest answer to "how are you?"). As each letter dissolves, replace it with a question mark shaped like the space between your heartbeat and your pulse.

**Essential Ungear:**
- A backpack constructed from the skin of an idea that died peacefully in its sleep
- Shoes that have renounced their citizenship in the concept of footwear
- A map drawn in disappearing ink on the inside of your eyelids (prerequisite: must have stared directly at the sun of a colder star until it blinked)
- Seven clocks synchronized to different species' dreams
- A passport stamped with the border patrols you've crossed in conversations that ended too soon

**Phase 2: Dietary Derealization**

For the forty-two hours preceding departure, consume only:
- Uncooked questions about the nature of bridges between Tuesdays
- Powdered shadows of edible things you've lost and given up looking for
- The sound of your name being said by someone who has never met you
- Water that has been boiled twice: once in anger, once in forgiveness

**Phase 3: Immaterial Reclothing**

Undress slowly in front of a mirror that isn't yours yet. Wrap yourself instead in:
- A coat stitched from the moments between someone's breath and what they actually say
- Gloves made from the silences between stars in erased constellations
- A hat that used to be someone's better tomorrow before they traded it in for a present they could afford
- Socks woven from the thread of conversations that got too real and had to be abandoned mid-air

**Phase 4: Navigational Meditation**

 Practice walking in directions that don't exist in languages that haven't invented verbs yet. Start with small distances: the gap between a promise's pronunciation and its fulfillment, the mileage between a child's drawing of a house and the mortgage foreclosure notice it becomes. Gradually increase the impossible until you can reliably locate yourself nowhere in particular on maps that haven't been betrayed into existence yet.

**Phase 5: Linguistic Vaccination**

Expose yourself, in carefully controlled doses, to:
- Archaic futures where yesterday hasn't been invented yet
- The temperature at which "forever" becomes a measurable unit
- Colors that can only be seen by listening to them backwards in the dark
- The precise weight of an unsent apology falling through different types of regret
- Geometry textbooks written by broken-hearted mathematicians who discovered God was using imaginary numbers to balance His checkbook

**Phase 6: Temporal Dress Rehearsal**

Rehearse your arrival by practicing leaving places you haven't entered yet. Leave your keys inside locked doors you've never owned. Forget birthdays that haven't been born yet. Break promises you've only thought about making to people you'll never meet except in dreams they're having about someone else.

**Phase 7: Final Contingency Kit (Incase of Emergency Reality)**

Should you accidentally start believing in where you are:
- One photograph of your parents before they became your parents
- A receipt for something beautiful you bought with money you didn't have
- The sound of rain recorded in a place that's never experienced nostalgia
- A matchbook from the restaurant where you'll have your last meal if you survive this
- The middle name you gave yourself when nobody else was watching

**Upon Subject-Arrival (Actions to Not Take Immediately):**

- Do not breathe unless the atmosphere has signed a consent form in triplicate
- Avoid making eye contact with your own reflection if it appears before you've decided to have eyes
- Refrain from acknowledging any architecture that hasn't finished introducing itself
- Do not, under any circumstances, answer questions that haven't been asked yet in languages you might become fluent in later
- If you encounter a version of yourself that seems to have gotten here earlier: pretend you've never met, then introduce yourself with a name you're considering borrowing from a future dream

**Emergency Phrases (Use Only When Absolutely Necessary):**
- "I think there's been a beautiful misunderstanding between us and the concept of escape velocity."
- "Excuse me, but which way is before I got here?"
- "I'm sorry, I don't speak possibility fluently, but could you try again in something more imaginary?"
- "This isn't what I meant when I said I wanted to get lost in thought."

**Return Policy:**

There is no return to speak of, only an increasingly abstract negotiation with the idea of departure. Your former existence will be kept on file in case of emergency reality bleeding through, but please note that retrieval fees include:
- One memory of being genuinely surprised by kindness
- Three flavors of possibility that haven't been invented yet
- The exact weight of your shadow when nobody is looking directly at it
- A signed confession that you've been pretending to be yourself this whole time

**Final Warning Label (Read in Reverse After Ignoring):**

This place may contain traces of everywhere you've ever tried to forget exists. Side effects of prolonged exposure include sudden expertise in impossible languages, spontaneous empathy for abandoned footnotes, recurring dreams of watching yourself wake up from dreams you didn't know you were having, and the gradual realization that you were never really here to begin with.

Proceed at the speed of forgetting what you came here to remember.

(Translation note: All instructions become more accurate when performed while being the answer to a question you haven't been asked yet by someone who loves the version of you that decided not to come here after all.)
