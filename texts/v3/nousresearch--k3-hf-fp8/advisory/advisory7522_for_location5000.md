### **Phase 0: Embodiment Adjustment**
**Duration:** 72 subjective hours pre-arrival / 0.6 objective seconds post-arrival

   Inhale powdered rust from a stolen railway spike. Exhale onto a mirror until the fog spells a word you’ve never spoken but will miss. Repeat until bronchial lining tastes like guilt.    Fracture your left fibula. Do not mend it. Instead, pack the marrow cavity with:  
   - 1x polaroid of sunlight on grass (sweetened, crystallized regret preferred)

### **Phase 2: Navigational Psychosis**
**Rule of Three Forks:** Every passage splits into:  
1. Where you **should** go (drowned in syrup, guarded by your dentist’s ghost)  
2. Where you **want** to go (leads here, but slower)  
3. Where **you** already are (most painful route; award-winning brine)

- **To Walk:** Step forward while whispering apologies to the atoms you’re displacing. The floor is a gossip.  
- **To Ask Directions:** Address the ceiling-pores directly: *“Which betrayal led you to leak this way?”* If the answer drips warm and tastes like pennies, follow it until it hardens into a mirror. Argue with your reflection until it forgives you for existing first.  
- **To Rest:** Sit anywhere you’ve never cried. Wait. The settlement will grow a bed from your abandonment issues. *Do not sleep.* The murids edit boring dreams into tax forms.
