**Field Guide for the Interstitial Traveler: Preparing for the Liesegang Room**  
*Classified: Ω-Level Subjective Hazard / Meta-Stability Zone / Temporal Prime Directive Null*

---

### **0. PRE-EVACUATION CONTRACT**  
Before reading further, sign here with a bodily fluid you haven’t discovered yet. The Room already has your shadow on retainer.

---

### **1. SUITING: WHAT NOT TO WEAR**  
- **No Clothes:** Fabric negotiates poorly with surfaces learning to imitate regret.  
- **No Skin:** Your current epidermis remembers being scales, then scales remember being rock. Arrive in **hypothetical integument**—the membrane between "I might" and "I was."  
- **Exceptions:**  
  - A single thread from a childhood blanket you burned in a dream. (It will vote on your behalf in the Empty Spaces Committee.)  
  - The smell of rain on a planet that never existed (use the vial labeled *Dinoterra, Precambrian Regret*).  

---

### **2. CALIBRATIONS FOR THE BODY AS A LIQUID QUESTION**  
- **Heartbeat:** Slow to 7.83 Hz (Schumann dissidence frequency). If your pulse forms a Liesegang ring in the air, apologize to it.  
- **Breath:** Inhale through your eighth rib. Exhale through the memory of your third-to-last sneeze.  
- **Pupils:** Dilate until you can see the color of silence arguing with itself. Then dilate further.  
- **Shadow:** Negotiate a temporary separation agreement. Offer it 1/3 of your future regrets and a timeshare in a black hole’s accretion disc.  

---

### **3. VOCABULARY QUARANTINE**  
Erase these words from all language centers:  
*exit, tomorrow, edge, alone, solid, stay, leave, name.*  
Replace them with:  
- **exisit:** The act of a door forgetting it was ever not you.  
- **tomorwave:** A quantum bedtime story told by fossilized light.  
- **edgeachore:** A dance performed by the floor when it thinks you aren’t watching.  
- **allone:** The sound of your bones voting to dissolve into committee dust.  

---

### **4. NAVIGATIONAL AIDS (VOID COMPASSES)**  
- **A beetle’s missing song:** Hum it backward until it hums you forward.  
- **A tear you never cried for yourself:** Throw it into the air; follow where it refuses to fall.  
- **A map drawn by a river on the underside of the sky:** Fold it into a möbius strip, then swallow it. The nausea will point north. (North is a metaphor here.)  

---

### **5. TIMEKEEPING DEVICES THAT WON’T BETRAY YOU**  
- **The blink of a creature made of unread letters.**  
- **The pause between the word “wait” and the Room’s reply.**  
- **Your own corpse from a timeline where you never arrived.** (Politely ask it for the time. Do not correct discrepancies.)  

---

### **6. PROVISIONS FOR THE UNFED SELF**  
- **A meal you forgot to eat in 3rd grade:** Bury it in the wall’s black-red stripe. A future version of hunger will thank you.  
- **A salt crystal from an ocean that evaporated into bird song:** Lick it only when you forget how to taste the color of loneliness.  
- **A bottle filled with the sound of snow falling on a star:** Sip sparingly. It is strong enough to intoxicate gravity.  

---

### **7. GESTURE CATALOGUE FOR NON-VIOLENT COMMUNICATION WITH EVERYTHING**  
- **To greet the air:** Offer it a memory of wind. If it accepts, it will carry your breath backward to your mother’s womb.  
- **To apologize to the floor:** Lie down until it forgets the concept of "under."  
- **To ask directions from the light:** Close your eyes and describe the shade of darkness you feared most at age 5. It will lead you to the exit disguised as that fear.  

---

### **8. EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS FOR WHEN YOU BECOME THE EMERGENCY**  
- **If your shadow demands custody of your past selves:**  
  - Challenge it to a duel using weapons made of the sound of one hand clapping in a vacuum.  
  - If you lose, dissolve into a question mark. The Room will adopt you as its official punctuation.  

- **If the beetle songs sync with your heartbeat and begin rewriting your DNA into Liesegang rhythms:**  
  - Dance. Dance until your bones precipitate into new metals. Collect them. They are currency in the next life.  

- **If you encounter the fish made of forgotten lullabies:**  
  - Do not speak. It speaks only in the language of things you were about to say before tears interrupted.  
  - If it offers you its scales: Eat one. You will forget the taste of water forever. This is a gift.  

---

### **9. NOT-A-FAREWELL: EXITING THE ROOM THAT IS NOT A ROOM**  
To leave, perform the following paradoxes in any order:  
1. **Stand in the exact center of every place you’ve ever felt lost.**  
2. **Remember the taste of a color that never existed for any species with eyes.**  
3. **Unbreathe the first breath you took when you were born.**  
4. **Leave behind a definition of “alive” that includes the act of waiting to become a mountain.**

### **10. POST-VISIT DEBRIEFING (FOR IF YOU EVER WERE HERE)**  
- **Duration:** The time it takes for a photon to forget it was ever a star.  
- **Physical Changes:**  
  - Your reflection may now vote in local elections.

  - Insomnia will feel like nostalgia for sleep you haven’t earned yet.

  - You will love the spaces between things more than the things themselves.
