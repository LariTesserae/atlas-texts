**Preparation Manual for Visitors to the Sea of Breath and Stone**  
*Edition ∞.000 (Revised for beings who still believe time is sequential)*

---

### **I. Pre-Departure: Obligating Your Body to Unlearn Up**

1. **Gravity Reorientation Training**  
   - Spend 3 weeks sleeping on a ceiling-slanted bed tilted at 22.6° (the angle where dreams begin to slide sideways).  
   - Practice **walking in loops**—never straight lines—until your footprints overlap into a Möbius strip.  
   - Attach pebbles to your eyelids: when they start feeling heavier than your thoughts, you’re ready.

2. **Language Unraveling**  
   - Speak only in **conditional past subjunctive**: *“If I were to have been here, I might have already left.”*  
   - Replace nouns with verbs for 48 hours: *“I’m treeing toward the forgotten.”*  
   - Memorize the **alphabet of sighing** (letters A-C omitted; they dissolve in transit).

3. **Hydration Paradox Protocol**  
   - Drink only **evaporated water** (steam from a mirror during the moment between breaths).  
   - Store tears in airtight jars labeled “Future Rain” (minimum 7 jars, varying regrets).  
   - Teach yourself to **urinate upward**: this will be your primary currency.

### **II. Transit: Arriving Elsewhere Before You Leave**

**Permitted Entry Points:**  
- The **Fourth Reflection** in a broken compass.  
- A desert’s memory of being an ocean (travel during the hour when sand tastes like salt).  
- The pause after someone says *“infinity”* but before they blink.

**Unper Entry Points (will be fined in years):**  
- Stillness.  
- Tuesdays.  
- Anywhere you’ve already arrived.

**What to Pack:**  
- A map drawn on **dissolving paper** (preferably a map of this place from 2,000 years ago; the inaccuracies are landmarks).  
- A **clock that ticks backwards in palindromes** (12:21 becomes 12:21 becomes 12:21 becomes).  
- Three **unspoken apologies** (one must be for something you’ll never do).  
- A jar of **your own voice** recorded on a day you forgot how to spell your name.

**What to Leave Behind:**  
- Your shadow (it will pretend to be you and do a better job).  
- Your sense of **before/after** (time here is a koan with no punchline).  
- Any belief that **stones are silent**.  
- Socks (they’ll try to returne to their motherloom).

### **III. Upon Arrival: Negotiating Reality’s Latitude**

1. **Greeting the Mountains**  
   - Bow deeply, but **sideways**. Your spine must angle like the horizon here.  
   - Offer them a **fistful of your discarded ambitions**. They collect them to polish their mirror-faces.  
   - If a mountain hums in B-flat minor, hum back—this is how you apply for temporary citizenship.

2. **Navigating the Sun’s Forgetfulness**  
   - Carry a **mirror shard** to remind light where it came from (light here is forgetful and prone to recursion).  
   - If light pools at your feet, **step into it slowly**—you’ll emerge 7 minutes younger but knowing a song no one has sung yet.  
   - Never ask the sun for directions; it will guide you to a place where shadows dream of being fire.

3. **Interacting with Vegetation Architects**  
   - When vines build a bridge overnight, **cross it immediately**—it’s only stable until it remembers gravity is optional.  
- **Compliment trees** on their ability to grow downward: *“Your roots taste like forgotten constellations.”*  
   - If a mushroom offers you a mirror, **decline** politely but take its reflection (you’ll need it to trade with the deer-fossils).

### **IV. Survival Basics: Being Unfinished in a Place That Hates Completion**

**Food & Drink:**  
- **Eat only things that are actively dreaming**: clouds shaped like your childhood fears, grasses humming lullabies in extinct dialects, the sound of your own heartbeat slowed to 0.00001 BPM.  
- **Drink mist that tastes like someone else’s déjà vu** (avoid the blue mist—it makes you remember being born in reverse).  
- **Do not eat the fruit** hanging from upside-down trees. It’s not fruit; it’s a memory of hunger that will replace yours.

**Shelter:**  
- Sleep inside **a question mark carved by wind** (the curlier the punctuation, the longer the dream protection).  
- If no punctuation is available, **unfold yourself into a comma** and wait for a sentence to adopt you.  
- **Never sleep inside ruins**—they’re still constructing the civilization that abandoned them.

**Timekeeping:**  
- **The day here is 42 hours long**, divided into **three mornings**, **two middles**, and **a secret hour** that occurs when you’re not looking at clocks.  
- **Seasons change when you blink incorrectly** (treat hay fever as a calendar).  
- To age backward, **walk in circles until your footprints forget you**. To age forward, **sit still and hum the sound of melting clocks**.

### **V. Advanced Guidelines: Conversing with the Inhabitants Beneath Form**

1. **The Deer-Fossils**  
   - They speak in **bone-poems**: *“I carry tomorrow’s skeleton inside yesterday’s apology.”*  
   - Trade: one of your future regrets for a bone they’ve already forgiven.  
   - **Do not touch their antlers**—they’re antennae listening to the Earth’s unfinished exhale.

2. **The Cloud-Birds**  
       - To summon: whisper a word you’ve never heard but understand perfectly (e.g., *“sorrowmute”*).  
       - If one lands on your shoulder, **blink once**—you’ll gain its ability to forget how to land for 7 lifetimes.  
       - If it rains feathers, **collect them** (they’re letters from a sky that ran out of words for goodbye).

3. **The Machine-Forest**  
   - **Admire its rust**—it’s bilingual in decay and innovation.  
   - If a tree offers you a fruit made of wires, **eat it cautiously** (you’ll dream in binary but wake up fluent in thunder).  
   - **Never ask for directions**; ask instead: *“What are you missing that I might have accidentally brought?”*

### **VI. Emergency Protocols: When You Become Part of the Landscape**

**Symptoms of Blending:**  
- Your heartbeat syncs with the mountains’ grinding teeth.  
- Fingers sprouting tiny valleys.  
- Memories being recounted by strangers wearing your face as a hat.

**Solutions:**  
1. **Unname yourself loudly** (start with your middle name, work outward).  
2. **Swallow a seed of uncertainty** (available from any tree-skeptic).  
3. **Run in place until your shadow overtakes you**—then follow it backward home.

---

### **VII. Departure: Leaving Without Being Gone**

To exit **the Sea of Breath and Stone**, you must:  
1. **Find a rock that is also a door.**  
2. **Knock with the sound of your laughter before you knew pain.**  
3. **Step through at the exact moment the Earth forgets to inhale** (every 47,623 years; **you missed it by 11 seconds ago**).

**Alternative Exit:**  
- **Become a legend** whispered by the wind to a traveler who hasn’t arrived yet.  
- **Dissolve into light** and promise to forget this place the way a river forgets its name at the ocean.  
- **Wake up**—but only if you’re sure you’re dreaming in the correct dimension.

---

**Final Note:**  
You will never return unchanged. If you remember this guide, **burn it immediately** (the ashes will spell directions to a place where none of this happened). If you forget it **deliberately**, you may return someday as the mountain’s reflection, teaching gravity how to lean sideways with grace.  

**Welcome, traveler who is already gone.**  
**Please do not start your arrival until you’ve finished leaving.**
