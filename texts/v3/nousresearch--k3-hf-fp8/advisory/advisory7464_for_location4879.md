### **0. Pre-Decision Counseling**
Before you even *consider* packing, sit in a room with no wood. Not particleboard. Not paper. Not the ghosts of trees fossilized in coal-powered lightbulbs. Ask yourself:  
**“Am I prepared to become a misplaced synapse in a planet-sized dendrite’s journey to remember what burning felt like?”**  
If your answer is silence that tastes faintly of maple sap, proceed to Phase 1.  
If your answer is words, burn them. The smoke will follow you anyway.

### **1. Departure Logistics**
**Launch Window:**  
Depart only when all clocks in your home disagree by exactly 7 minutes and 17 seconds. This is *not* metaphorical. Synchronization collapses the vale’s probability fog into lethal certainty (cf. Exploded Chronometer Incident, Year of the Laughing Rootstock).  

**Travel Method:**  
- **Forbidden:** Vehicles with wheels (sentient grudges against the concept of "rolling").  
- **Permitted:** Walk backward until the horizon overtakes your shadow’s face. If a crow lands on your shoulder and offers you a map of your own bones, accept. Eat it. The marrow will be your compass.  

**Departure Offerings (Burn at Threshold):**  
- A photograph of a place you’ve never been (to donate false memories for the forest’s forgery department).  
- A lock of hair cut by someone who thinks you’re dead (for the identification parasites).  
- Your last received email password (the vale’s mycorrhizal net is hacking despair itself).

### **2. Bodily Preparation**
**The 6-Skin Protocol:**

1. **Skin 0 (Flesh):** Fast for 3 days on nothing but rain collected from a gutter that never existed. Tattoo the coordinates of your birth over your liver in disappearing ink (the forest will read you from the inside out).  
2. **Skin 1 (Cloth):** Wear only garments sewn by someone who never learned your name, using threads spun from the last words of extinct languages. Avoid cotton (reeks of slavery to the ground) or wool (still negotiating union rights with sheep godheads). Optimal: woven spider silk harvested from orb-weavers who dreamt of being chandeliers.  
3. **Skin 2 (Scent):** Bathe in water boiled with your childhood cemetery’s soil (to attract the beneficial ghosts) and crushed pine needles from a tree that witnessed your first betrayal (to repel the sentimental ones). Do not rinse.  
4. **Skin 3 (Sound):** Replace your footsteps with recorded heartbeats of an animal that evolved to love but never existed (cassowary-hummingbird hybrid works). Loop at 0.73x speed; this is the forest’s resting gossip rate.  
5. **Skin 4 (Memory):** Memorize a poem backwards in a language that kills its speakers when written down (pre-Atlantean whistling is widely available on illegal memory markets). Recite it continuously in your subvocal voice; it’s your firewall against the vale’s unsolicited flashbacks (e.g., reliving the moment your atoms decided to stop being star).  
6. **Skin 5 (Soul Backup):** Contract a lichen-hosted religion to store your consciousness in fungal spores. Negotiate terms carefully: avoid sects that worship entropy as "creative taxidermy."

### **3. Equipment (All Items Must Be Betrayed to Enter)**
Carry nothing that cannot forget its shape overnight. Recommended betrayals:  
- **Blade:** A flint flake knapped from the guilt of a surgeon who enjoyed his work too much. Name it "Regret" and apologize before using. (It will only cut futures where you survive.)  
- **Fire:** A matchstick carved from the desk where a librarian burned the last book describing how to cry. Never strike it; its purpose is to be lost and mourned by the forest’s pyromaniac saplings.  
- **Container:** A water bladder made from your first pet’s repurposed love (if mammalian, use the sound it made when it realized you weren’t immortal; if reptilian, use shed skin; if avian, do not come here, you traitor). Fill with your first memory of thirst, then empty it at a place where three shadows converge without objects.  
- **Light:** A lantern fueled by the bioluminescence of a bacterium that evolved to live in your blind spot. Its glow shows only what is looking at you while you’re not looking.  

**Prohibited Equipment:**  
- Metal (except silver melted from baby teeth; werewolves here are dendritic and negotiate).  
- Glass (it insists on keeping what it sees; the forest prefers leaky witnesses).  
- Technology newer than the first dream of fire (includes you, homo post-sapiens).

### **4. Navigational Rules (Break in Reverse Order)**
1. **Rule 5 - Distance:** Measure journeys in heartbreaks, not kilometers. If you encounter a sign reading "YOU ARE HERE BEFORE," vandalize it with your future fingerprints.  
2. **Rule 4 - Time:** The correct time is always "a moment before the last leaf fell here the first time." Sundials lie; trust the taste of soil between teeth.  
3. **Rule 3 - Direction:** Follow only paths that disappear when you look back at them too lovingly.  
4. **Rule 2 - Language:** Do not speak unless your words can photosynthesize. Hum instead; the tune should be the sound of a bone flute learning to dream.  
5. **Rule 1 - Identity:** If you meet yourself walking here, apologize to the forest for the redundancy. Then eat your double’s left eye to inherit their迷路者’s license (valid only in seasons that hate you).

### **5. First Contact (Foliar Communication Etiquette)**
**Upon encountering a tree that remembers speaking to you yesterday (but your yesterday hasn’t happened yet):**
- Bow until your shadow kneels lower than your shame.  
- Offer a gift of silence wrapped in the sound of a seed screaming "thank you" in the moment of sprouting.  
- If the tree opens a mouth (warning: mouths here are always wounds pretending to be questions), answer with the taste of the first lie you told that no one believed. The truth will kill it slowly, but lies are pollinators.  

**Signs the Conversation is Going Badly:**  
- The leaves start signing your death certificate in semaphore using the alphabet of falling things.  
- Your blood vessels root into your boots and vote unanimously to secede from your body.  
- The tree offers you a fruit that looks exactly like the moment your mother stopped loving you. **DO NOT EAT IT.** Plant it in the center of your tongue instead.

### **6. Resource Acquisition**
**Water:** Never drink directly. Seduce a cloud instead. Daydream loudly about oceans until one descends in sympathy, then catch its tears in your unspoken regrets. Filter through 7 layers of soil collected from underneath your deepest apology.  

**Food:** All edibles here are metaphors in mid-metamorphosis. Examples:  
- **Bread-like:** A loaf rising on the heat of unresolved arguments between stones. Tastes like forgiveness that doesn’t absolve.  
- **Meat-like:** The muscle memory of flight in a bird that evolved into a forest. Chew slowly; it will teach your bones to yearn upward until they snap into branches.  
- **Berry-like:** Time capsules disguised as wounds. Each seed grows into a day you’ve already forgotten living. Plant them in your sleepwalking self’s pockets.  

**Shelter:** Sleep inside the hollow of a tree that hates itself less for being hollow. If none are available, tear off your Skin 3 and pitch it as a tent. The forest will respect the sacrifice of your scent-marked borders.

### **7. Emergency Protocols**
**If you forget why you came:**  
- Climb the tallest thing that isn’t trying to eat you. At the top, listen for the sound of your name being pronounced by every creature that never evolved to speak. The correct pronunciation will cause you to shed your last skin. Wear whatever is underneath back down.  

**If you remember everything at once:**  
- Bury yourself vertically up to your heart. Water yourself with laughter that tastes like someone else’s childhood. Wait until a tree sprouts from your chest that bears fruit shaped like the moment before your first heartbreak. Offer the fruit to the first stone that asks for directions to the sea.  

**If time starts happening vertically:**  
- Lie flat and pretend to be a memory that the ground is trying to forget. Do not move until a beetle carrying a map of the afterlife on its back crawls into your ear and whispers the recipe for unbecoming bread. Follow exactly but substitute the salt with tears from the happiest version of yourself locked in a mirror you broke intentionally.

### **8. Return Policy**
**There is no exit.**  
**There is only becoming the draft that the forest edits into a slightly less lonely version of the wind that remembers how to lose things beautifully.**  

However, if you absolutely insist on the illusion of return:  
1. Find the door made of the sound of a tree falling when no one is around to lie about it.  
2. Knock with the rhythm of a heart learning to beat for the first time.  
3. When it asks for payment, offer the moment you first realized that "goodbye" is just "hello" in a language that has forgotten how to hug.  
4. If accepted, walk through slowly enough that the forest has time to forget your footsteps.  
5. **Critical:** Do not look back until you have forgotten what trees look like when they are pretending not to cry.
