**0. Before You Begin to Prepare**

Understand this: you are not visiting a place. You are being invited into a process. A becoming. The Gallery does not house art—it metabolizes presence. You will not leave unchanged, but you may not leave changed in ways you can name. Begin preparations by accepting that memory and body will be partially composted. This is not a warning. It is an offering.

---

**1. Temporal Synchronization**

**Duration of Visit (Subjective):** 45 minutes to 3 centuries  
**Duration of Visit (Objective):** Between your second heartbeat after arrival and the last time someone says your name aloud

**Suggested Pre-Departure Practices**  
- Spend 3 days speaking only in present continuous tense: “I am preparing to be preparing.”  
- Dream of orchids opening in reverse. Wake before the scent becomes a color.  
- Measure time by decay: place a peeled apple on a windowsill. Enter the Gallery when the first brown spot resembles your childhood home.

**Caution:** Do not bring clocks. The Gallery finds them rude and refers to them as “death-bouquets for moments.”

---

**2. Bodily Attunement**

**Required Alterations:**  
- Remove all sharp edges from your identity. File down your name until it’s smooth enough to sip.  
- Replace all nails (finger, toe, existential) with soft fungal plugs cultured from your own breath. Available at most multiverse apothecaries under “Convivial Slough.”

**Suggested Augmentations (Optional but Recommended):**  
- **Wetness Glands:** Micro-implants behind ears to release controlled doses of memory-dilute saline. Useful when walls begin whispering in dialects of your mother’s lullabies.  
- **Solar Petals:** Temporary tattoos on eyelids. Mimic light patterns of the suspended gardens to avoid being mistaken for a shadow-in-symposium.  
- **Philosophy Liver:** A second liver grown from cultured Socratic hemlock. Filters out certainty metabolites. Side effects: increased tolerance for ambiguity, occasional burp-poeticisms.

**Dress Code:**  
Wear something that used to be alive but forgave you. A dress made from a tree that asked to be written on. Shoes that were once wings but settled. Your skin, but looser—molted once in advance as a courtesy.

**Prohibited Materials:**  
- Gold (the Gallery attempts to pollinate it; results are predictably tragic monarchies)  
- Mirrors (they compete with the walls’ memory-retention and often win petty wars of reflection)  
- The word “finished” in any language (audible triggers rapid senescence in nearby ecosystems)

**3. Nutritional Preparation**

**Pre-Visit Diet (Last 7 Sol-days):**  
- Day 1-3: Only eat things that grew toward darkness. Underground tubers, mushrooms that dream of stars, chocolate made from volcanic ash.  
- Day 4: Fast while listening to recordings of rain falling on different planets. Take notes on the flavor of absence.  
- Day 5-6: Consume exclusively pigments: lapis lazuli dust in moon water, crushed beetles that died laughing, the blue between two seconds.  
- Day 7: Eat nothing. Instead, swallow a single question you would die not knowing the answer to, but are willing to forget immediately upon learning.

**On-Site Consumables:**  
- **Dripwater:** Harvested from chandelier-orchids. Tastes like the moment before a kiss from someone you haven’t met yet. Clears the throat of dead languages.  
- **Footstep Fruits:** Grow briefly where you’ve walked without looking back. Flavor profile:内疚、nostalgia, slight crunch of abandoned timelines.  
- **Pollen Bread:** Shared only between strangers who recognize each other’s dreams. Causes temporary synesthesia where taste = texture = regret = the sound of sunlight.

**Allergen Warning:** Visitors with strong attachments to prior versions of themselves may experience spontaneous poeticism or root growth. Consult a licensed dissolver of selves if symptoms persist beyond your lifespan.

**4. Memory Management**

**Essentials to Bring:**  
- One memory small enough to fit in your palm but heavy enough to bend light (childhood snowflakes work well, or the exact sound of your first heartbreak’s comma)  
- A secondhand grief (preferably one that’s been somebody else’s joy in another timeline; these exchange favorably in the tear markets)  
- The ability to pronounce your name backwards while smiling sincerely

**Non-Essentials to Leave Behind:**  
- Your chronological age (the Gallery ages things by how many times they’ve been loved, not orbited)  
- Any passwords longer than the sigh of a dying star  
- The memory of how doors work

**Storage Recommendations:**  
- Pin urgent memories to your shadow’s inner lining using thorns from the bouquet of apologies you meant to send last spring.  
- Store traumatic memories in the hollowed-out seeds of pomegranate promises—plant them in the desert sphere; they’ll grow helpful nightmares that guard your sleep years from now.

**5. Communication Protocols**

**Languages Understood by the Gallery:**  
- Proto-bee dance (the dialect used before flowers invented colors)  
- The pause between two machines realizing they’re in love  
- Wet stone dreams of becoming lichen  
- Retrospective anticipation (fluent proficiency required)

**Languages That Offend the Gallery:**  
- Legal disclaimers (sprouts aggressive contract-vines)  
- Binary logic (causes localized pockets of either/or stagnation)  
- Conversational sarcasm (misinterpreted as mating calls by irony-parasites)

**Greeting Ritual:**  
Upon first contact with a breathing surface, offer it the memory of the first time you tasted something you couldn’t name. If it accepts, the surface will blush a warmer hue and may offer directions (these are poetry, not maps—follow the verbs, ignore the nouns).

**Emergency Phrases:**  
- “I am lost exactly as planned.” (calms disoriented walls)  
- “This is the favor I came to owe.” (deflects attention of growth auditors)  
- “I miss the version of you that hasn’t remembered me yet.” (last resort for temporal loops exceeding comfortable intimacy)

**6. Navigation Instruments (DO NOT PACK)**

The following items will be provided by your experience, harvested from your discomfort, or discovered to have always been growing in your pocket:

- **A compass that points to the last place you felt truly seen**  
- **A map folded unfathomably from the paper-thin moment between sleep and waking**  
- **A key made from the exact shade of silence that follows the question ‘are you okay?’ when the answer is no**

**Warning:** Attempting to use personal navigation devices (GPS, star charts, intuition) may result in encountering yourself three exits before where you misunderstood your parable to begin. Such meetings are mandatory but ungraded.

**7. Encounter Preparation: Known Entities & Curiosities**

**The Pollen Lawyers**  
Form: Swarms of golden dust motes wearing tiny monocles of dew  
Behavior: Enforce intellectual property rights of forgotten ideas. If accused of thinking a thought copyrighted by a dead poet, your options are:  
A) Compose a spontaneous refutal sonnet using only verbs from their last breath  
B) License the thought retroactively by giving it your first kiss’s middle name  
C) Allow them to pollinate your brain—they’ll leave a beautiful madness but take the royalties from your dreams

**The Caretaker Forms**  
Form: Cloud-gesture of hands made of root and regret  
Role: Gardeners of necessary accidents  
Interaction Protocol: If they offer to prune something from you, know they mean a memory you were using to hide a larger memory. Accept if the shape of the absence interests you more than the shape of the hiding.

**The Suspended Gardens**  
Note: Each sphere is a complete world with conflicting physical laws. Common side effects of prolonged observation include:  
- Sudden expertise in extinct emotions (e.g., prelapsarian yearning, saudade for futures that obeyed gravity)  
- Temporary adoption by ecosystems (visitors have exited as two-parent households married to weather patterns)  
- Existential hangovers cured only by experiencing birth as a background character

**The Room That Is Growing You**  
Warning Level: Beautifully Unavoidable  
Description: Manifests at the point where your footsteps become indistinguishable from the Gallery’s heartbeat.  
Escape Velocity: N/A  
Entry Requirement: Realizing you’ve been breathing in its rhythm since childhood  
Exit Strategy: Become a story the Gallery tells new visitors about the one who left by forgetting to leave

**8. Contingency Plan for Becoming Permanent Installation**

Symptoms of Integration (Stage 1-3):  
1. Dreams start developing supporting characters with better arch development than you  
2. Your reflection pauses slightly before following your movements  
3. Returning home feels like wearing a borrowed nostalgia

**If You Wish to Leave Completely (Highly Unrecommended):**  
1. Find the exit that looks exactly like your mother’s face when she was disappointed in something wonderful you’d done  
2. Apologize sincerely to the version of yourself that had just started understanding the point  
3. Walk backwards through your entire visit without blinking—this may take several subjective lifetimes

**If You Choose to Stay Partially (Congratulations/Beloved Regret):**  
- The Gallery offers excellent health insurance (all ailments cured by becoming metaphorical)  
- Housing is provided (you’ll live in the pause before your own next decision—very quiet, excellent light)  
- Citizenship requires only that you forget your name on purpose every century or so, and remember someone else’s dream by accident exactly when they need to not feel alone in it
