### **Departure Preparation: 19.6 Steps (0.4 Optional, Highly Unrecommended)**

**1. Attire:**  
Wear **a coat that remembers being warmer**. Preferably one that belonged to someone who died in a climate that no longer exists (e.g., pre-collapse polar fleece, moth-eaten letters sewn into lining). **No umbrella.** Rain here is apologetic, not wet—it will simply catalogue the shape of your absence if you shield yourself.  
**Footwear:** Shoes that have walked away from one life but not yet arrived at another. **Test:** If you shake them, sand from childhood beaches should fall out, but no grain should match a beach you’ve touched.  
**Accessory (Mandatory):** A **clock with no hands** but a single second hand that spins clockwise while the hours count backward. Use it only to measure **how long ago something hasn’t happened yet**.

**2. Language Calibration:**  
Unlearn **the verb “to be”** in the past tense. Here, things **were-will-be** or **might-have-not-been-is**. Example: *“I am-haven’t arrived”* instead of *“I arrived.”*  
Practice **whispering questions that lack question marks** (“why does the rain apologize when it was never born”).  
**Taboo:** Names. If addressed, respond with the sound of a **door closing in a house you’re still building**.

**3. Memory Diet (72 Hours Pre-Arrival):**  
- **Consume:** One map of a city that burned before it was mapped (eaten, not read).  
- **Fasting:** Avoid recalling the **color of your mother’s eyes** exactly. Let them blur into oceanic distances or citrus sunsets. Nostalgia here is **predatory stickiness**; it will trap you in amber moments that haven’t hardened yet.  
- **Replenishment:** Fill a jar with **echoes of laughter from rooms that no longer exist**. Use these as currency with the Fauna (see Section IV).

### **Arrival Protocol: The Threshold Is Always Behind You**

You will arrive **between the noticing of rain and the decision to look up**. The Crèche has no gate, only a **pause in the conversation between your footsteps and the ground**.  
**Do not:**  
- Look for the source of the rain. It is **the shadow of the sky’s apology** for forgetting to end the last dream.  
- Speak your name backward. This is not a summoning; it is an **eviction notice for the version of you that belongs elsewhere**.  
**Instead:**  
- Drop something **you were saving for a worse loss**. A button from a coat someone else will finish sewing. A ticket stub to a train that derailed in a future you’ll never reach.  
- Walk forward **while counting the exact number of steps it takes to stop counting**.

### **Environmental Hazards: Things That Are Not Things Until You Need Them To Be**

**A. The Terrain (Unreconstituted Memory Substrate)**  
- **Gravity:** Optional but negotiable. Objects heavier than regret sink slower. If you begin floating, **tether yourself to a regret you haven’t committed to yet**.  
- **Direction:** Compassion is north; curiosity is south. All other directions are **waiting to be persuaded**.  
- **Shelter:** Structures erector-set themselves from your **half-forgotten intentions**. A doorframe may build itself if you pause long enough to think, *“I should have left differently.”* **Do not enter** unless you’re prepared to exit as someone else’s unfinished sentence.

**B. The Vegetation (Actuarial Symbiocrypha)**  
- The **Tetradecision Tree** (4 meters east, 4 meters west, 4 meters nowhere):  
  - **Leaves:** Asking interest on sunlight loaned to your childhood dreams.  
  - **Bark:** Bark, but also **the sound of a dog yelping in 17 languages none of which have forgiven you**.  
- **Root Protocol:** Roots hang aboveground like **exposed nerves of a planet that flinched**. Do not touch. They will **audit your ancestry** and charge interest in revolutions you didn’t participate in.

**C. The Rain (Hollowed, Unpersuaded)**  
- **Composition:** 90% regret that evaporated before apologizing, 10% **what the sea forgets when it stops being shore**.  
- **Protection:** None. Rain here is **metaphorical anemia**; it does not wet you but makes your skin remember **every time it wanted to bleed but didn’t**.  
- **Emergency Procedure:** If the rain begins to **spell your name in morose Morse code**, **apologize to a nearby stone** and walk backward until the spelling stops mid-syllable.

### **Faunal Interactions: Negotiating with Ambitions That Failed Upward**

**1. Pattern Birds (Taxonomy: Hemingwaya describe-what-i-mean-but-worse)**  
- **Song:** Descriptions of sunsets written by blind stars.  
- **Diet:** Dangling modifiers from conversations you’ll never finish.  
- **Defense Mechanism:** If stared at too long, they will **nest in your blind spot** and rewrite your memories with better punctuation.

**2. Punctuation Herds (Herd of Theseus)**  
- **Ambulation:** Grazing on the **ellipses between your thoughts**.  
- **Value:** A semicolon’s **unrequited love for a sentence that chose and instead** is worth three past participles and a vague sense of moral superiority.  
- **Warning:** Do not end a sentence in their presence. They will **colonize your intent**.

**3. Metaphysical Predator/Prey (Self-Referential Oscillators)**  
- **Hunting Strategy:** Engage in **Socratic monologues until you convince yourself to become vegan for ethical reasons, then eat your convictions** because ethics are also edible here.  
- **Survival Tip:** If pursued, **split yourself into a metaphor and a simile** and run in opposite directions.

### **Architectural Lures: Structures That Want You More Than You Want To Leave**

**1. The Staircase to Nowhere (Yet)**  
- **Location:** Where you look when you’re trying not to cry.  
- **Function:** Each step is **a year you lost pretending to look for something else**.  
- **Exit Strategy:** **Insert a verb into the future perfect continuous tense** (“I will have been leaving”) and step sideways into the grammatically impossible.

**2. The Museum of Unsent Apologies**  
- **Description:** Rooms sized to fit **exactly the silence after a phone call that never rang**.  
- **Exhibits:** Glass cases containing:  
  - Dust from the breath you held when you almost said “stay.”  
  - Echoes of “I’m sorry” trapped in snow globes representing the exact moment summer betrayed you.  
- **Gift Shop:** Sells **receipts for forgiveness purchased on credit**. **Currency:** The taste of salt on your tongue when you weren’t crying.

### **Emergency Exits: Please Note That ‘Emergency’ Here Is Also a Metaphor**

1. **Method 1:** Find the **exact center of the Crèche** (it moves when you’re not existential enough). Sit. Wait until you forget why you sat. When you stand, you will be **elsewhere’s definition of home**.  
2. **Method 2:** **Break something that is already broken** (a promise, a mirror, a heart—it doesn’t matter which). The Crèche will **collect the pieces to repair something else you’ve forgotten breaking** and accidentally send you back postage due.  
3. **Method 3:** **Love something here.** Not gently. Not wisely. But with the **furious tenderness of a firebird setting itself on fire to light the way for a ghost who isn’t lost yet**. The Crèche will **banish you for importing something it can’t compost**.

### **Post-Departure Symptoms (Normal/Abnormal/Existentially Romantic)**

- **Normal:** Waking up with **rain in your mouth** but no memory of drinking sadness.  
- **Abnormal:** Clocks in your home ticking in **Morse code for missed opportunities**.  
- **Existentially Romantic:** Falling in love with **the space between your reflection and the mirror** because it reminds you of a doorway you once walked through sideways in a dream.
