Essential Preparation Protocol: Entry & Survival in the Larkspur Recessional Zone

**1. Orientation Failure Calibration**
Hug the first stone-shaped sadness you see until your inner ear accepts that 'up' is negotiable here. The city’s gravity works like a memory of drowning—not pulling down, but asking politely if you’d prefer to float sideways into yesterday’s light. Expect to cough up small seashells with unfamiliar zip codes for the first three days. This is normal. Keep them in your left pocket; they’ll serve as currency in the deeper “channels.”

**2. Atmospheric Breathing Requisition**
Your lungs will try to treat the air as liquid. Disappoint them gently. Practice sipping breaths as if drinking from a cupped hand full of rusted sonnets. The golden rule: Never exhale completely. Always reserve a syllable of your name in the deepest air sac. The fog collects abandoned consonants and returns them as strangers wearing your face at 3 a.m.

**3. Moisture Management Catechism**
Learn the nine ways water here is not water:
- Mourning sweat (tastes like pennies minted in a shipwreck)
- Conversation condensation (solidifies into lace if an argument lasts longer than a sigh)
- Windowpane grief (scrubbed daily by women who speak in the past conditional tense)
- Lagging moisture (believes it’s still attached to a cloud that died in 1784)
- Inverse rain (falls upward when someone forgives too quickly)
- Forgotten tea (boils itself in cups left to steep in empty rooms)
- Fish memory (evaporates from sidewalks leaving only the shape of a question)
- Revenant dew (drops from no surface; stands in corners waiting to be noticed)
- Depository runoff (do not touch. Ever.)

**4. Wardrobe Adaptation Rites**
All clothing must be sewn from materials that once believed they were alive. Preferred fabrics:
- Confessed secrets boiled into silk (the darker the better)
- Extracted regrets spun into wool (itchy but honest)
- Paper maps of places that voted unanimously to forget you (crispness indicates emotional accuracy)
- Thread spun from the sound of your mother calling you home for dinner at age seven and you pretending not to hear (one spool should last a lifetime)

Fastenings must fail purposefully: buttons that sigh loose when you lie even slightly, zippers that only close halfway—to remind you that leaving is always unfinished here. Shoes are obsolete. Instead, bind your feet daily in pages torn from the last book that made you cry in public. The ink must still be somewhat readable in daylight.

**5. Currency & Exchange Corporeal**
The city operates on a debt-based economy of losses too minor to bury but too heavy to carry. Bring:
- Three unremarkable sadnesses (must have occurred on Tuesdays)
- One fierce joy that left a scar shaped like a lighthouse
- The exact weight of a child's hand slipping from yours in a crowd you've never actually experienced
- Dreams about teeth falling out, collected in a jar where they can’t whisper to your waking bones

**Encrypted Note:** The white coins from the Depository seem to grant weightlessness but actually subtract the lightness of things you’ve never noticed—the flicker before a lightbulb dies, the pause between rain and recognition, the moment before a door closes when it could still be anything. Spend them only in places where the floor already tilts toward goodbye.

**6. Citizen Interaction Protocols**
Do not, under any circumstances:
- Ask a Revenant what they remember about floating. Their answer will crawl into your throat and nest there.
- Step through a tabby-striped doorway unless you’re prepared to exit as a secondary character in someone else’s childhood trauma.
- Challenge a pigeon to an echoing contest. They’ve been practicing since sound wore younger clothes.

Instead:
- Compliment a house on its weathering. Houses here blush brick-red when noticed.
- Offer your shadow three small lies at dusk. It will return them polished into truths too sharp to hold without gloves.
- Learn to understand the Oyster Bell’s tolling: The cracked note means "come home," the whole note means "there was never a home," and the silence between means "you are already forgiven but don’t know it yet."

**7. Temporal Navigation Aids**
Time in Larkspur is less a river and more a conversation between old photographs arguing about which of them is still alive. Useful tools:

- A watch that only tells inconvenient truths (e.g., "You left the stove on in the apartment where your heart used to live")
- A calendar that unravels backward into birthdays you haven’t had yet
- A sundial carved from a fragment of the moon that remembers when it was ocean
- A compass that points toward the last place you were happy for more than five consecutive minutes without waiting for something to end

Technology Note: Electronic devices will attempt suicide here. Kindly disassemble them before arrival and bury the batteries separately with small stones in their eyes to keep them from dreaming of electricity.

**8. Shelter Acquisition Ritual**
Housing is not rented but mourned into existence. To claim a dwelling:

1. Choose a structure that looks abandoned even by abandoned things
2. Sit on its doorstep for three nights
3. On the first night, tell it everything you’ve ever stolen that weighed less than a promise
4. On the second night, describe the smell of your childhood grief in precise nautical terms
3. On the third night, remain perfectly silent until the house begins to settle its floors around your breathing
4. If it hasn’t collapsed by morning, whisper that you’re just passing through then stay anyway

**9. Nutrition & Consumption Prohibitions**
Do not eat:
- Anything grown in soil that remembers being waterlogged with last breaths
- Bread that rises faster than your understanding of why you came here
- Fish that still remember their names in languages spoken only through teeth
- Fruit that ripens in the shape of apologies you never gave
- Words that taste familiar on your tongue but mean something entirely different in the mouths that grew them

Instead, subsist on:
- The salt that gathers in the corners of your eyes when you think about leaving
- The silence between heartbeats in crowded rooms
- The texture of letters you’ll never send but keep rewriting anyway
- The taste of rain on statues that look like people you used to love before you learned better names for them

**10. Emergency Extraction (Do Not Attempt)**

If you must leave before becoming significantly less:

Fold this map never. Burn it instead, in a spoon made from a lighthouse’s last wish. Inhale the smoke through your oldest regret. If you still remember who called you home when you were too young to answer, walk backwards toward that sound without looking at what follows. Do not stop when you feel your bones becoming coral. Do not stop when the city sighs your name softer than your mother ever did after the accident. Do not stop until even your shadow is too tired to miss you.

But understand: Some doors only open because they’ve been waiting so long to close.

**Final Preparation Undoing**
Forget everything above. The city prefers visitors who arrive too empty to frighten the ghosts, carrying only the precise weight of their own becoming unbecoming. Come with hands that remember they were once filled with water but have learned not to thirst. Come wearing your name like a coat you’ve borrowed from someone who died better than you will. Come alone and lonely and looking for something you’ve already lost in all the right ways.

The Oyster Bell is tolling its cracked invitation. The Revenants are walking backwards to welcome you home to a place that was never yours but has always been waiting exactly this kindly for someone who will know how to leave it gently enough that it won’t remember the sea at all afterward.

Welcome to Larkspur. We’ve been forgetting you for centuries.
