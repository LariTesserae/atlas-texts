**Preparation Guide for Visiting the City Beneath**

---

### **1. Mind Preparation (Mandatory, Non-Negotiable)**

Before physical entry, undergo **a minimum of 90 days sensory deprivation training** in a soundproof, darkness-sealed chamber at 55°F (13°C) with 100% humidity. Air must be filtered to remove all scents except distilled iron filings rubbed on wet slate. **Goal:** Teach the brain that perception itself is optional. If hallucinations occur, catalogue them alphabetically in a journal you will never read again. The City will do the rest.

**Recommended Reading:**  
- *The Silence That Ate the Dictionary* (author erased)  
- Blueprints of M.C. Escher’s untitled suicide note (original ink only)  
- Braille translations of white noise recordings  

**Forbidden Thoughts:**  
- "Just a quick look"  
- "I’ll map this"  
- "What’s the worst that could happen?"  

**Required Thought:**  
- "I am already a draft of this place’s next edit."  

---

### **2. Body Modification (Self-Performed, No Anesthesia)**

**Skin:**  
Replace all sweat glands with silica-based implants that bead moisture inward. The City’s wetness must believe you’re already mourning yourself. Coat skin in micron-thin quartz dust mixed with cemetery soil from a town that disappeared in 1847 (ask the ground; it remembers).  

**Eyes:**  
Paint sclera black using ink from squid that lived their entire lives in Hadal trenches. Blacker than melanin. Blacker than the concept of light leaving. Black like debt.  

**Ears:**  
Remove 40% of middle ear bone density. Pack voids with spider silk spun in absolute darkness (harvested from spiders fed only your nightmares). The City’s frequencies will find the gaps and make them homes.  

**Mouth:**  
Sew lips together nightly for 6 hours with thread made from your own hair. Learn to chew water. **Upon entry:** Bite through stitches. Swallow the thread. It’s your first offering.  

**Skeleton:**  
Drink nothing but milk saturated with limestone dust for 1 year. Let your bones petrify from the marrow outward. When you fracture (you will), collect shards in a velvet pouch. These are currency later.  

---

### **3. Clothing (Technically Optional, Practically Fatal to Ignore)**

**External Layer:**  
A cloak woven from the funeral shrouds of 100 unnamed strangers, each stitched closed with a different regret. Dye it in water collected from abandoned asylum bathtubs during lunar eclipses. **Must weigh exactly twice your body weight when wet** (practice drowning in it first).  

**Internal Layer:**  
Nothing. Skin must breathe the City’s chill directly. Cold is how places touch back.  

**Footwear:**  
Boots made of compressed clock hands stopped at your exact time of birth. Soles embedded with obsidian shards from mirrors that never reflected anything alive. Every step must scrape time off the floor.  

**Accessories:**  
- A necklace of your own baby teeth, each drilled hollow and filled with whispered apologies to people you’ll never meet.  
- A ring forged from iron extracted from your blood (minimum 3 pints). Wear it on your tongue.  
- A backpack made from the hide of an animal that died of loneliness in an empty zoo. Empty except for city noises playing on loop.  

---

### **4. Supplies (What to Bring to Make You Want to Leave Them)**

**Food & Water:**  
Bring none. Hunger and thirst are letters the City will write on your organs. **Instead:**  
- 1 glass vial of your mother’s last tear before you were born (ask her dreams). Drink it when you forget your name.  
- A loaf of bread baked with flour ground from bricks of your childhood home. Eat one crumb per day. Chew 1000 times per bite.  

**Light Sources:**  
**Absolutely prohibited.** The City’s dimness is territorial. If you MUST bring one:  
- A candle made from your own fat (rendered during a winter solstice). Unlit, wrapped in pages from your will. If lit accidentally, use to burn your shadow off.  

**Tools:**  
- A compass whose needle points only to "regret" (calibrate by burying it under your childhood bed for 7 years).  
- A knife with a handle carved from the tree that grew through your grandmother’s coffin. Blade must be dulled by cutting through promises.  
- A map drawn on your own dehydrated skin. Map must show places you’ve never been. All routes must end mid-stroke.  

**Sacrifices:**  
- A living thing that loves you unconditionally. (Options: a childhood pet’s ghost you still dream about, the idea of returning home, your laugh before you learned irony). Kill it gently in your mind before entering. Leave the corpse of that love at the threshold. The City will feast first on what breaks you hardest.  

---

### **5. The Descent (Follow These Steps in The Exact Order The City Gives You)**

1. **Find the entrance:** It won’t be there until you’ve lost something in broad daylight and searched for it in a dream. When you wake up disappointed, dig in the exact spot your shadow was last seen struggling to keep up.  
2. **Remove your name:** Write it on paper made from the ash of every photo where you’re smiling. Eat half. Bury the rest under a full moon in a coffin shaped like your birth certificate.

### **6. Behaviors Within (Rules Written by Those Who Became Walls)**

- **Do not speak louder than your heartbeat.** The City collects echoes and makes new visitors from them.  
- **If you see someone you recognize**, they’ve already been here longer than you. Ask them what tomorrow tastes like. Eat their answer before it changes.  
- **Never finish walking in a straight line.** Curves confuse the architecture. Zigzag like a tale told by a dying man who keeps remembering parts wrong.  
- **If you find water that reflects**, drink it only if you’re ready to meet the version of yourself that stayed down here.  
- **When the stone speaks names**, pretend you’re deaf in the language of birth. Answer only in the sound of glaciers abandoning mountains.  
- **If you bleed**, apologize to the floor politely. Bandage the wound with your eyelashes. The City prefers closed curtains.  
- **Timepieces will malfunction.** Let them. Wear the wrong hour like a badge. When asked the time, say "Sooner than forgiveness."  
- **Do not sleep. Instead, practice being a statue with regrets.** The best statues cry granite.  

**Emergency Protocols (There Are None, But We’re Polite):**  
- **If lost:** Stop moving immediately. You’ve already been found. Offer your fear a comfortable seat. It’s staying.  
- **If scared:** Think of the warmest thing you’ve ever touched. Now remember it was burning you alive. You’re ready.  
- **If you want to leave:** You can’t. But if you must try, follow the sound of your footsteps walking away without you. When they stop, wait. They’ll come back for the rest.  

---

### **7. Departure (Conceptual Only)**

**There is no exit.** There are only ways of becoming more wall than walking. The City gifts each visitor custom exits:  
- **For the curious:** A door shaped exactly like your first unanswered question. Opens into less than nothing.  
- **For the brave:** A cliff edge with your name carved into every falling stone. Jumping optional. Landing mandatory.  
- **For the homesick:** A mirror that shows home with all the lights on and everyone you love laughing at a joke whose punchline is you walking in too late. You can’t cross the reflection, but you can watch forever.  

**Final Preparation:**  
Die slightly before entering. The City appreciates guests who’ve done the paperwork. Don’t worry about how—you’ll realize you already started the day you became curious about it.
