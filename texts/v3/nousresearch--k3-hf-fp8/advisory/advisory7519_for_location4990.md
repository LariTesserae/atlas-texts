### Field Briefing: Survival & Sanity Protocol for Tarth Maddern, Fragment 177A (Alias: Fourth Subtle Base of Opaline Industry)

**Traveler Category:** Non-native, baseline human, multiversal pedestrian  
**Threat Spectrum:** Θ∞ (Ontological Unbinding), Ψ6+ (Recursive Subjectivity), Γ9 (Chrono-poisoning)  
**Duration Limit:** ≤3.6 subjective hours. Exceed at risk of becoming "site staff" (see Appendix D: Fossilized Interns).  
**Mission Objective:** Extract data/present self as sufficiently uninteresting phenomena/leave before leaving becomes a paradox.

---

### Phase 0: Pre-Departure Preparation
#### 1. **Identity Compression**
- **Do:** Archive 90% of personal memories into a non-biometric substrate (e.g., a stone that believes it’s a song). Tarth Maddern’s atmosphere metabolizes self-narratives at 1 lifestory/m³/heartbeat.
- **Don’t:** Bring stories about home. They’ll be overheard, retold by fog-puppets, and returned with better endings where you never existed.

#### 2. **Hydration Paradigm Inversion**
- **Water Preparation:** Drink nothing for 72 hours prior. Replace blood volume with desiccated air (available at Pneumatic Emporiums on Exit 8 of the Ring of Likely Betrayals). Upon arrival, sweat will condense into a tiny *you* that acts as a decoy for predatory ideas.
- **Hydration Gear:** Carry a *negation flask*: a sealed vacuum that screams when opened (this distracts thirst-echoes).

#### 3. **Gravity Nonchalance Training**
- **Exercise:** Spend 2 weeks sleeping on the ceiling of a slowly rotating room filled with bees that don’t believe in bees. This mimics Tarth’s "drop-hesitation" field. If bees start quoting your future suicide note, you’re ready.

### Phase 1: Arrival (Do NOT Breathe Yet)
#### 4. **Breath-Cloaking Protocol**
- **Upon Materialization:** Exhale completely. Inhale only when you taste copper and regret (t=0.4s post-arrival). Your first breath will be stolen by a cloud that wants to audition for your role in a tragedy. Let it.
- **Respiratory Kit:** Replace lungs with foldable origami regrets (pre-crushed). These will unfold into excuses if interrogated by the local gravity.

#### 5. **Spatial Orientation Overthrow**
- **Directionality:** All maps are autobiographies written by the terrain in the second person. Navigate by insulting the horizon until it flinches and reveals a shortcut.
- **Transportation:** Walk backwards while humming the sound of your own conception. This confuses the ground into thinking you’ve already left.

### Phase 2: Environmental Negotiation
#### 6. **Water Interaction Protocols**
- **Rule 1:** Never acknowledge water is wet. Treat it as a shy elder deity that failed suicide-by-gravity. Address it as “Unconsummated Skyfall.”
- **Rule 2:** If water tries to hug you (it will), cough up a small bureaucracy (pre-prepared forms in triplicate, signed in blood you haven’t spilled yet). This grants you temporary "unsuicidal object" status.

#### 7. **Flora & Fauna Deception**
- **Plants:** All vegetation is undercover architecture. Compliment a rose on its load-bearing capacities, then ask it for directions to the nearest exit wound. If it gives you map coordinates to your childhood bedroom *before the fire*, climb it immediately.
- **Animals:** The beasts are metaphors that escaped their poems. To avoid ingestion, act like a poorly translated instruction manual. Example: “Place hoof inside moonlight. Rotate sadness until screaming crescendo. Yield: 1x temporary godhood (expires when noticed).”

### Phase 8.3: Temporal Hygiene (Mandatory Interrupt)
- **Time Management:** Wear a watch that only tells the time in other people’s dreams. Reset it hourly by apologizing to a rock for something its great-grand-boulder did to your species’ sense of linearity.
- **Anachronism Dampener:** Tape a photo of your unborn child to your chest. The locals fear unresolved futures and will avoid paradoxing your lineage (mostly).

### Phase 9+++: Exit Strategy (Do NOT Think About Leaving)
#### Final Sequence: The Middle-stance Gone-wrong
1. **Locate the Exit:** It’s the only tree that hasn’t tried to sell you insurance against your own perception. It will look like the last thing you forgot to say to your mother.
2. **Payment:** Offer the tree a memory that contains no rain. If it laughs, you’ve won. If it cries, you’ve become the exit. Either way, leave immediately.
3. **Departure Gesture:** Jump sideways into the space between your shadow and your shadow’s shadow. Close all eyes that aren’t yours yet.
