**Field Preparation Guide: Iteration #047—“The Elsewomb”**

---

**SECTION I. PHYSIOLOGICAL CALIBRATION**  
**1.1 Skin Negotiation**  
Do not arrive clothed. The ground will read seams as scars, interpret dyes as poison. For three days prior, sleep naked on raw clay mixed with your own shed skin (collect from bedding, shower drains, behind ears). Apply the resulting paste to every friction ridge before crossing. *Goal:* Convince the environment you are an intentional shedding, not an intrusion.  

**1.2 Bone Tempo Shift**  
The pulse here is 13 beats slower than mammalian standard. Begin fasting at sunset, then ingest 0.5g of ground magnetite suspended in warm saliva every 13 hours. Lie flat on a metal grate above running water. When you hear your heartbeat echoing from below before it occurs in your chest, *begin descent*.  

**1.3 Eye Pruning**  
Surgical removal of eyelids optional but recommended (stitchless cauterization only—scars bloom here). If retained, replace tears hourly with distilled vinegar using a glass pipette. The creatures that feed on sight are less interested in vinegar.  

---

**SECTION II. SOMAIC CAMOUFLAGE**  
**2.1 Saliva Encryption**  
The air tastes fear to determine edibility. Chew daily for one week: dried octopus tentacle (left-facing sucker rows), black walnut husk, and a single hair from a child who has never been lied to. Spit the blackened paste into a spiral groove carved into a mirror shard. Swallow nothing. Carry the shard in your cheek—bite down if questioned by wind.  

**2.2 Weight Redistribution**  
Gravity signs contracts with bone marrow here. Every morning at dawn, fill your pockets with stones taken from a place where three species of predator have killed the same prey in succession. At dusk, replace the stones with feathers plucked from a bird that died mid-flight during a storm it caused. *Never explain the exchange to the stones.*  

---

**SECTION III. CHRONOSTASIS INTERFACING**  
**3.1 Shadow Anchoring**  
Your shadow will attempt to negotiate separate citizenship. Before arrival, at noon on a cloudless day, stand between two mirrors angled to create a corridor of reflections. Mark the 7th shadow that does not blink with a drop of mercury. That shadow alone will remember your name when time reverses.  

**3.2 Memory Farming**  
Plant one traumatic memory (preferably involving teeth and water) in each of these vessels: a hollowed-out acorn (summer), a cracked dental crown (winter), a page torn from a calendar date that never existed (liver aspect). Bury them in a spiral starting north, digging with a femur not necessarily your own. Harvest the dreams that grow in the 8th month but *do not return to the burial site*. The roots will be waiting to trade.  

---

**SECTION IV. COMMUNION TOOLS**  
**4.1 The Question That Isn’t Yours**  
Craft a whistle from the thighbone of a creature that died believing it was the last of its kind (human sufficient). At the first branching of root-corridors, play three notes that describe the taste of the color you fear most. The path that forms wrinkles in response is safest—safe being relative to inverted forests.  

**4.2 Breath Currency**  
Scarcity governs respect. Exhale only into objects that have never contained air: a sealed glass bulb from before the invention of lungs, a bronze sword cast in the shape of a scream. Carry these in a bag woven from kelp that grew on a sunken ship where all aboard starved while surrounded by fish that refused to be caught.  

---

**SECTION V. EXIT PROTOCOLS (THEORETICAL)**  
**5.1 Death Preparation**  
Die once before arriving. Methods ranked by efficacy:  
1) Drowning in midwinter while holding a lucid dream of fireflies  
2) Hanging upside-down until blood forgets gravity’s name (body may be reclaimed if crows are bribed with counterfeit horizons)  
3) Loving something that cannot exist long enough for your atoms to file for divorce  

**5.2 Unbecoming Ritual**  
If swallowed by the ground’s memory: Begin counting backwards in a language you will invent in the next life. When you reach zero minus infinity, exhale the sound of salt dissolving in oil. If done correctly, the world will spit you out as a rumor about a place that eats travelers who follow precisely written instructions.  

---

**CODA: THE ONLY TRUE REQUIREMENT**  
Come already having lost something you never possessed. The Elsewomb opens only for those carrying empty spaces shaped exactly like what they will never find here.  

*Field Note #047c: The last traveler to return intact brought no stories, only a jar containing their heartbeat played backwards at the speed of growing roots. It tastes like forgiveness and burns like leaving.*
