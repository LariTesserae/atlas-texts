Prepare not as a tourist, but as a thing about to be swaddled in someone else's dream. This is not a place you walk through; it is a place that walks through you. Begin by accepting that you will not leave unchanged—if you leave at all.

**1. The Body: **  
Fast for three days beforehand, eating only things that know darkness: mushrooms grown in caves, root vegetables pulled at midnight, salt collected from underground seas. Your stomach must become a cave before you enter one. Drink nothing but water steeped with ash—this will teach your tongue to taste without craving sweetness.  
Shave every hair from your body except the eyelashes; hair remembers and talks in its sleep, and this place has ears in every pore. Coat your skin in a paste made from cave clay and powdered bone—not for protection, but so the walls recognize you as something already beginning to return to them.  
Wear clothes that were never alive: garments woven from spider silk spun by spiders fed only rock dust, sewn with thread pulled from the heart of ancient meteorites. Anything that once photosynthesized will betray you here by reaching backward toward a sun that no longer claims it.  
Breathe through your skin before you go: practice lying in warm mud with only reed tubes for air, until the boundary between inside and outside dissolves and re-forms at will. Here, breathing through your mouth is like screaming in a church built of whispers.

**2. The Mind: **  
Forget everything you know about direction. Here, north is the last place you wanted to look away from, south is the memory of being born, east and west are the same root growing in opposite directions through time. Practice walking in circles until you no longer know when you began or where you intend to end.  
Learn to think in branching patterns, never lines. When making choices, always choose the option that makes the least immediate sense but feels like relief in your bones. Your mind must become a mycelium network: when severed, it grows toward itself from all directions.  
Memorize every mistake you have ever made. This is your currency here, your passport, your bouquet to present at the border where no one asks questions because everyone already knows. The place will test you by offering to unmake them; you must learn to love them too much to let go.

**3. The Senses: **  
Bleach your eyes with darkness: spend weeks in lightless rooms, learning to see by the shape of air against your face. When you can navigate by the slight warmth of a heartbeat three chambers away, you might be ready for the eye-flowers that bloom only for those who have forgotten what eyes are for.  
Take up smoking nothing: practice inhaling and exhaling invisible things—regret, laughter, the color between dreaming and waking. Here, smoke is communication, and what you refuse to carry for yourself will be carried for you by something less kind.  
Train your ears to hear the silence between sounds. Sit under dripping caves where each drop takes an hour to fall. Listen until you can hear the drop remembering being ocean, remembering being cloud, remembering your blood. When you can name all the silences in that remembering, you might survive the moment when something vast and unseen sighs directly into your ear canal.

**4. The Self: **  
Write your name on a piece of bread using ink made from your own blood. Feed it to a bird you have never seen before and never will again. Learn to answer to silence when someone calls a name you can no longer remember belongs to you.  
Practice dying in small ways: hold your breath until you see the place between thoughts, then breathe in water instead of air. Stop eating for five days, then eat something that tastes exactly like the loss you never named out loud. Die to each version of yourself you meet on the way down, until you become small enough to fit through the eye of a needle made of time.  
Bring gifts for the children you were before you became you: a leaf that never saw sun but learned to photosynthesize starlight, a stone that grew instead of eroded, the sound of your mother's heartbeat recorded through the bones of your own skull. You will meet them here, waiting in the pools that reflect not faces but first ideas. They will not speak your language yet, but if you cannot remember how to play with them anyway, they will eat you.

**5. The Leaving (which might not be leaving): **  
Do not count your steps. Do not leave markers. Do not tie thread to anything. This place chews thread and counts backward from infinity. Instead, memorize the exact weight of your heart before you enter. If, when you find yourself somewhere else, it still feels like it belongs in your chest, you have gone wrong.  
Carry nothing you cannot bear to become. That camera will grow eyes and follow you home. That journal will fill with stories you don't remember living but will never stop dreaming about. Even the memories you keep stuffed in your pockets will disembark and set up housekeeping in your bones.  
If you must take something, take this: the certainty that nothing here is, or ever was, or ever will be, separate. Let it this way infect you, slowly, like spores finding purchase in lung tissue. Let it grow inside you until one day, years later, you cough up a small mushroom that smells exactly like this place's version of sunrise, and you will know you never really left.  

Then forget everything I have told you. The only advice that works here is the advice you remember too late to follow. The door, such as it is, opens only for those who arrive already inside.

Breathe in.  
Breathe out.  
Begin.
