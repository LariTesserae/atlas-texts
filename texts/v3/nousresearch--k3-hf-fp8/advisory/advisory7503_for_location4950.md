Phase 3: Memory Socialization Training
Spend seven nights storing memories in objects that don't belong to you:

- Leave your sense of direction in a library book about places that don't exist on maps made of skin
- Practice being forgotten by people you'll never meet at café tables that exist only during rainstorms

Phase 4: Gravity Diplomacy Certification
Incline yourself to the following agreements:

- Upward mobility requires submitting three references from past traumas in good standing

- 1 day = the distance between two people's second-to-last thoughts before sleep
- 1 year = the weight of your shadow when it realizes it's been following the wrong person

Exchange rate fluctuates based on how intensely you're trying to count heartbeats that aren't all yours.

Phase 6: Communication Preparation
Master these essential phrases:

- "This conversation is much lighter in my grandfather's voice" (request for clarification)
- "My apologies, I mistook your pause for someone else's salvation" (faux pas recovery)

- "The rain remembers your answer differently" (polite disagreement)

Important: Speaking directly about anything increases its probability of becoming a former version of itself by 147%. Practice addressing subjects obliquely by whispering poems to the spaces between their words.

Phase 7: Receptivity Calibration

Phase 8: Emergency Procedures

1. Distract yourself immediately by counting the number of rooms in your childhood home that you'd forgotten existed until this sentence mentioned them
2. Hum the tune of any lullaby you can't remember learning the words to
3. Locate your oldest scar and ask it politely to tell the story backwards in a language that uses silence as punctuation
4. If understanding persists, confess to the nearest reflective surface that you have always believed mirrors were windows with social anxiety

In case of encountering yourself:

- The one composed entirely of canceled plans is trying to warn you about something you already chose to forget
- The child-sized iteration is not younger, just more compressed - approach only if carrying apologies in small denominations
- If all versions begin speaking in unison about the weather you haven't had yet: congratulations, you've become the local forecast. Reporting conditions will continue until your identity improves.

- Start missing this place at the exact rate that it begins remembering you
- Begin forgetting yourself in alphabetical order starting with the letter that won't exist until tomorrow
