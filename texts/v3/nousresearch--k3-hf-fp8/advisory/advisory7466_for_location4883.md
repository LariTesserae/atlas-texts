**The Fermented Archive of Traversing Tanakkerai (Sixth Edition)**

*For the vertically disoriented visitor who still believes streets belong under feet*

**I. The Pre-Sinking Rituals (Perform in Correct Atmospheric Order)**

First: Stop breathing. Not metaphorically—literally hold your last lungful of surface air as sacred currency. At precisely 7,032 feet elevation, your body becomes a walking bankruptcy ledger between two oceans. The air here is 78% borrowed water vapor, 21% ancestral regrets, and 1% oxygen rationed by moody trees. Practice drowning in reverse at home: Fill bathtub with green silk scarves, submerge face, read tax forms upside-down. Repeat until panic becomes nostalgia.

**II. Packing for Perpendicularity (An Anti-Gravity Wardrobe)**

- **Skin**: Replace entirely. Your current epidermis will mourn gravity. Seek out the Dermal Memory Traders in the Hanging Market (suspended from the 12th Basalt Molar, west root cluster) who weave replacement hides from:
  • Deceased thunderstorm linings
  • Persimmons that achieved enlightenment
  • Letters never sent (blue stamps only)
- **Clocks**: Bring none. Time here is measured in the distance between a mother remembering her childhood home's front door color and her child forgetting it. Wear instead a small jar of sunbeams stolen from five different summers; trade them for directions when lost.
- **Currency**: Coins sink, credits drown. Wealth is:
  • Perfectly remembered smells (recently outlawed: cut grass, grandmother's perfume, fear before first kisses)
  • Original ways to say "I miss you" (limit 3 syllables, must include reference to water that doesn't rhyme)
  • Unbroken promises wrapped in seaweed (inspected by the Eel Auditors at Checkpoint Grief)

**III. Orientation for the Non-Somersaulting**

Upon arrival, you're guaranteed to either:
A) Fall upward screaming
B) Swim downward laughing 
C) Both simultaneously while forgetting your birth name

This is called "The Founders' Welcome." Accept whichever direction your tears choose to flow—they've been here longer than you and know the shortcuts. The city map is written on the backs of Remembrance Eels who only reveal streets to those who can recite their favorite lullabies backwards in whale. Common beginner lullaby: "Rock-a-bye-baby" performed entirely with gill clicks.

**IV. Housing Authorities (All Deceased, Still Accepting Complaints)**

Buildings here follow Murphy's Law of Architecture: Any structure that can be entered through the floor will eventually expect rent payments through the ceiling. Key negotiation phrases:
- "I prefer my gravity when it's unsure of itself"
- "This is actually my ceiling visiting as a tourist"
- "Shhh, the walls are listening for buyers"

Note: Apartments with consistent floor/ceiling relationships are considered haunted and discounted accordingly.

**V. The Cuisine of Composting Dreams**

Eating habits adapted for residents with spatial identity crises:
- **Breakfast**: Photographs of abandoned picnics, served on plates that remember being rivers
- **Lunch**: A single question ("What did you forget to become?") prepared three ways: raw, inverted, and fermented in second guesses
- **Dinner**: Silence harvested from libraries before they burned, paired with wine that ages you younger with each sip

Utensil etiquette: Forks are used to comb hair, spoons for listening to stone secrets, knives only for cutting ties with versions of yourself that would hate this place.

**VI. Social Customs for Solitude Practitioners**

Greetings in Tanakkerai are dangerous—they can last generations. Instead:
1. Approach stranger at 47-degree angle (never perpendicular, they might think you're a census taker)
2. Offer them your left shoe (right if recently divorced from reality)
3. Whisper a sin you've never committed but always wanted to
4. Walk away in the direction their eyes first looked sad

Successful interactions conclude with both parties forgetting whose sadness it originally was.

**VII. Emergency Procedures (Ha Ha Ha)**

If You Begin:
- **Remembering sunlight too clearly**: Seek immediate shadow confessional. Tell the darkness three things you loved that didn't love you back. Receive ash blessings.
- **Growing gills**: Congratulations! You’ve achieved local status. Report to the Bureau of Becoming Fish for external lung matching ceremony.
- **Floating away during conversations**: Quick! Grab the nearest unfinished sentence. They’re heavier than they appear.

In case of actual emergency (defined as: remembering your mother's voice exactly as it sounded before the world ended), immediately forget this entire guide. The city prefers its residents unprepared—they taste more like hope that way.

**VIII. The Departure Tax (Non-Negotiable, Extracted from Dreams)**

Leaving is prohibited but inevitable. The exit fee:
1. One memory that keeps you awake in pleasant ways
2. The name you whispered the first time you felt loved
3. The exact color of your father's eyes before you learned disappointment was a primary color

Payment collected by the Abbess of Falling Things every seventh unbirthday, during the hour when clocks forget how to count to one.

**Final Note** (written in margin by someone who never left):

The strangest thing isn't that the city drowns upwards. It's that after a while, you forget to wonder whether you're sinking or the world is finally learning how to catch things that have been falling forever. 

Welcome home, whether you meant to arrive or not. The water remembers every name you haven't learned to call yourself yet.
