**Preparation Guide for the Planar Incision Known as the Heightland Notch**

**Classification:** Type III Recursive Altitude Locale  
**Threat Index:** Variable (dependant on narrative cohesion of traveler)  
**Recommended Duration:** ≤3 subjective days  
**Mandatory Equipment:** Narrative Anchor, Temporal Compass (calibrated to pre-departure regret spectrum), Liquid Memory Weights  
**Optional but advised:** Half-forgotten song, childhood regret (small, sharp), lens made of polarized apology  

---

### **I. BEFORE ASCENT (Pre-Location Calibration)**

1. **Sleep Deprivation Ritual**  
   Stay awake for exactly 13 hours beyond your usual limit. When you begin to hear faint applause from electrical outlets, you're approaching the correct neurochemical gradient. This will synchronize your brain's delta waves with the heightland's slower, argumentative gravity.

2. **Memory Diet**  
   For three days prior, consume only:
   - First bites of meals (never the last)
   - Dreams recalled only in third-person
   - Water drunk while holding breath  
   This will hollow out your hippocampus enough to carry new memories without overwriting crucial survival instincts.

3. **Shadow Negotiation**  
   At twilight on the final day before departure, stand with your back to a mirror. Say to your shadow: "I acknowledge you may have better survival strategies but worse impulse control." Offer it one authentic regret (preferably regarding a missed opportunity to be kind). Do not watch it eat.

---

### **II. GEAR PREPARATION (Objects that know they're lies)**

- **Boots:** Old hiking boots that carried you somewhere you never told anyone about. Before wearing, walk 100 steps backward in them while reciting the instructions for something you've never learned to do (e.g., "First, find the heart of a raven who has seen the ocean's bones..."). This will confuse their sense of direction enough to adapt to recursive terrain.

- **Water Container:** A flask made from the last page of a book you loved but pretended to hate in middle school. Fill with tears shed over fictional deaths only. Label clearly "FOR OTHER PEOPLE'S MISTAKES" in ink that disappears when read.

- **Navigation Tools:** 
  - A compass that points to your greatest fear on Tuesdays and your second-greatest on days containing silent letters.
  - A map drawn entirely from memory of dreams where you were lost in places you've never been. Burn the edges exactly three times: once for truth, once for lies, once for what you hope might be true enough.

- **Light Source:** A lantern containing a firefly that stopped glowing after witnessing its own reflection. Instead of light, it emits small, embarrassed silences. Useful for navigating by absence.

---

### **III. PHYSICAL MODIFICATIONS (Temporary existential surgery)**

1. **Gravity Divorce Ceremony**  
   Write "till death do us part" on a helium balloon. Release it while simultaneously jumping off a small step (≤ 4 inches). Mid-air, whisper three true things about how gravity has disappointed you. Land without conviction.

2. **Echo Cords Adjustment**  
   Practice speaking sentences backward while expecting them to be answered forwards. Begin with simple truths ("The sky is falling" becomes "Falling is sky the") and progress to complex self-recursions ("I think therefore I am confusing myself becomes "Myself confusing am I therefore think I"). Stop when you accidentally apologize for something you will do tomorrow.

3. **Thermal Memory Reshuffle**  
   Spend one hour naked in temperature exactly opposite your last meaningful goodbye (if it was winter, find a sauna; if summer, a walk-in freezer). While there, arrange ice cubes/sauna stones into the shape of the first lie that ever made someone stay. Do not name it.

---

### **IV. ON ARRIVAL (First 13 breaths protocol)**

1. **Do not exhale the last air from your home altitude.** Hold it hostage in your left lung as leverage in upcoming atmospheric negotiations.

2. **Identify your first pool by lying flat and seeing which reflection blinks last.** This will be your temporary memory bank. Deposit one memory you can afford to remember differently later (e.g., how your first kiss tasted).

3. **Walk across the basalt hexagons by stepping only on cracks.** The ground here respects those who acknowledge its wounds. If a hexagon sings your mother's lullaby in a language she never spoke, compliment its pronunciation but do not dance.

4. **When you meet the doorframe opening onto elsewhere:** Wave first. Doors here are shy of being seen seeing you. If it waves back with a hand you don't recognize, introduce yourself using only verbs.

---

### **V. SURVIVAL PROTOCOLS (For when causality gets clingy)**

**Symptom:** Cloud-wolves appearing in your peripheral vision more solid than your tent  
**Solution:** Start telling a story aloud about a time you were definitely not prey, but include one impossible detail every sentence (e.g., "And then the bear loaned me his bicycle..."). Cloud-wolves cannot resist fact-checking fiction but are terrible at catching continuity errors.

**Symptom:** Drinking from the wrong pool and remembering your birth in reverse  
**Solution:** Immediately write a strongly worded letter to your past self. Address it to "Current Occupant of My Childhood Bedroom" but describe events from your future job interview. Mail it by folding the letter into a paper boat and launching it into the pool that ripples counterclockwise.

**Symptom:** Waking up multiple versions of yourself with conflicting breakfast demands  
**Solution:** Declare a spontaneous game of "Opposite Day." All versions who were originally left-handed must now act right-handed, and vice versa. The cognitive dissonance will force most minor temporal forks to merge back into the primary timeline out of sheer embarrassment.

---

### **VI. DEPARTURE PREPARATION (If decide to remember leaving)**

1. **Begin collecting sounds that your footsteps definitely did not make.** Store them in the gaps between your teeth. These will pay the toll to the spiral staircase that collects regrets in different frequencies.

2. **Find the single-shelved library exactly when you remember forgetting it exists.** Read only the book that reads you back, but pause every time it mentions a color you've never seen to scratch a new name for it into your wristwatch glass.

3. **To grow extruded exit steps:** Stand perfectly still at the exact spot where your shadow first touched ground at dawn. Think continuously of the weight of all the places you've ever pretended to belong. Keep thinking until the sunrise freezes into steps. Do not look back to check if they're forming — hope is lighter than certainty but harder to trust.

**Final Warning:** If offered the new pool that remembers everything you haven't lost yet, remember that "ready" is a shape water only takes when it's pretending to be glass. The correct answer is always to hesitate beautifully, then choose with your eyes closed.
