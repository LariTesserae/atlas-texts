**Preparation Handbook for the Choirtown Raftcradle**  
*A Local’s Guide for the Multiverse-Traveler Who Has Already Made Several Questionable Life Choices*  

---

### **1. The Breath Before Entry**  
Before you even *think* about arriving (spoiler: you won’t arrive correctly), practice **echo-stacking**. Find a staircase—any will do—and walk down it while humming a melody you’ve never heard. Each footstep is a debt. Each hum is a bribe. When your shadow takes the next step before you do, stop. You’re 12% ready.  

Pack nothing that **remembers** being alive. Leather boots will mourn their cow. Wool scarves will knit themselves into nooses in protest. Instead, bring:  
- **Spore-silk undergarments** (ask a lichen for a favor first; they gossip).  
- A **hollow bone flute** carved from your own laughter (instructions: laugh at a funeral furnace until it apologizes).  
- A **jar of unspoken replies** (collect from arguments where you smiled instead of biting).  

---

### **2. The Grief-Frost Protocol**  
Temperature here is **grief-indexed**. If you’ve ever cried on a late-night bus, subtract a layer. If your childhood pet died on a sunny day, pack thermals **made of apologies**.  
- **Test:** Stand barefoot on a mirror in a thunderstorm. Count how many of your reflections offer you shoes. If none do, you’re overprepared.  

---

### **3. The Clothing That Forgets You**  
Garments here are **memory-contingent**. They thicken when named, thin when ignored. **Never compliment your coat aloud**—it’ll become armor and crush you with loyalty. **Never insult your socks**—they’ll vanish mid-step and report you to the Rootlaw.  

**Dressing Instructions (Follow exactly):**  
1. Put on your **future self’s regrets** like perfume (apply to pulse points at 3 AM tomorrow).  
2. Wrap yourself in **yesterday’s silence** until you hear the fabric breathing.  
3. Top with **someone else’s dreamcoat** (steal it kindly; leave a thank-you note in their past).  
4. **Final check:** If you can see yourself in a spoon, undress immediately. Spoons are snitches here.  

---

### **4. The Water That Rises Up Your Debt**  
Water is **upwardly mobile** and **emotionally literate**. It will:  
- Read your diary by osmosis.  
- Crawl into your lungs and correct your grammar.  
- Leave you with **posthumous thirst**.  

**Survival Kit:**  
- **A tongue contract**: Sign in saliva, agreeing to never say "I’m thirsty." (Penalty: rain will tattoo your sins on your teeth.)  
- **A straw made of blame** (sip others’ regrets carefully; they’re carbonated with guilt).  
- **Earplugs carved from dried tears** (optional but recommended for the Singing Pit’s kissing-hymns).  

---

### **5. The Vegetation That Scaffolds Your Trauma**  
Trees here are **unpaid therapists**. They’ll grow into the shape of your childhood trauma if you sleepwalk. **Do not hug the fogbark**—it’ll hug back, then file a restraining order in your DNA.  

**Botanical Etiquette:**  
- When passing an **Antiphon Oak**, apologize to the nearest whisper for something you’ll regret in 17 years.  
- If a **cistern-gourd** winks at you, propose marriage or a business venture—there’s no third option.  
- **Never prune a vinestreet.** The city will reroute your memories to a cul-de-sac in 1421.  

---

### **6. The Fauna That Audits Your Dreams**  
Animals here practice **cosmic accounting**. They will:  
- Steal your name if you fail to laugh at their jokes.  
- Lodge complaints with the moon about your dietary choices.  
- Sue you for emotional damages if you die without updating your will.  

**Pocket Allies:**  
- **A Fuguecat’s promise** (obtain by staring into its absence until it discounts your existence by 30%).  
- **A Ventriloquist Crow’s IOU** (trade it for a secret you’re allergic to).  
- **A Comma Crab** (as a lawyer; pay in semicolons).  

---

### **7. The Light That Remembers Being Sung**  
Day and night are **ex-lovers** who share custody of the color green. **Sunglasses will sue you for divorce** here. Instead:  
- Blink in Morse code: "... --- .... A N G E R Y" to summon a gentle eclipse.  
- Carry a **photograph of the sun** as a hostage (negotiate curfews politely).  

---

### **8. The Sound That Builds Your Jailcell**  
Silence is **illegal** and **taxed**. Even your heartbeat must audition for a permit. **Practice muffled panic attacks** in advance.  

**Noise-Cancellation Strategies:**  
- Wear a **coat of other people’s laughter** (harvested from birthday parties you weren’t invited to).  
- Learn the **Grand Litany of Slackening** backwards—it’s the only song that can lull a soundwave into a coma.  
- **Never sneeze.** If you must, apologize in D*d*f#b diminished (the town’s favorite key for executions).  

---

### **9. The Departure That Precedes You**  
Leaving is **mandatory** and **impossible**. **Exit visas** are issued to your shadow, which will leave without you. **To leave correctly:**  
1. Marry a local typo (preferably one in the town’s divorce rate).  
2. Die in a way that hasn’t been invented yet.  
3. **Forget how to arrive** mid-step.  
4. If successful, you’ll wake up before you left, wearing shoes that don’t fit because they’re still growing into your future footprints.  

---

### **Final Test**  
Before entering, answer this question **aloud but backwards**:  
*"What sound does the color of your silence make when it runs out of tears?"*  

If the silence replies with a lullaby sung in your mother’s voice but she’s flossing her teeth, congratulations.  
You’re already 7 minutes late for your audition.  

**Break a leg.**  
(The town will knit it into a sweater and mail it to your past self with no return address.)
