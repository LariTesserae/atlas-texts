### **I. Pre-Transit Permeation (72 Hours Prior)**  
**Objective:** Become partially porous to your own absence.  

1. **Desynchronize Circadian:**  
   - Sleep in 17-minute fractals: 11 minutes awake, 3 asleep, 3 in-between.  
   - Dream only of **unremembered postal codes**—write them in condensation on cold mirrors. Smudge before clarity forms.  

2. **Diet of Phase-Transitioned Familiarity:**  
   - Consume:  
     - Ice cubes made from boiled seawater (Earth’s last sample, if available).  
     - Bread baked without warmth: mix flour, yeast, and whispered rumors of ovens. Let it rise in a sealed jar labeled *Tomorrow*.  
   - Do not taste. Chewing solidifies belief; Nocturne Found digests conviction.  

3. **Language Dilution Protocol:**  
   - Speak exclusively in the **subjunctive-past-imperfect**: *“I had perhaps already forgotten this word.”*  
   - Replace nouns with textures (*e.g.,* *“hand”* → *“the thing that forgets it holds”*).  
   - On the third day, silence your shadow: walk backward into light until it detaches. Beg it not to follow.

### **II. Corporeal Adjustment (12 Hours Prior)**  
**Objective:** Exist as a question mark in your own shape.  

4. **Thermal Unbinding:**  
   - Submerge in a bath of **unboiled rainwater heated to 99.9°C**.  
   - Add:  
     - A single hair from someone who never existed (ask a stranger’s reflection).  
     - Copper filings from a coin spent on a truth you later denied.  
   - Exit when skin feels like borrowed language. Do not dry—let evaporation decide where you end.  

5. **Skeletal Reformatting (Optional but Recommended):**  
   - Hire a blind chiropractor to adjust vertebrae into **Hilbert curve approximations**.  
   - Pay with a memory of weightlessness—forget the transaction mid-payment.  

6. **Respiratory Rejection:**  
   - Exhale into a glass jar until it fogs. Seal it with wax from a candle lit during an eclipse you missed.  
   - Label: *“Emergency Oxygen for the Already Changed.”*  
   - Forget where you stored it. The jar will find you when you no longer need it.

### **III. Transit Trigger (Exact Moment: Entropy-Peak + 3 Seconds)**

**Warning:** Do not initiate if you’ve ever been certain of your birthdate.

7. **Location:**  
   - A room with **4.5 corners** (build the half-corner with stacked unsent apologies).  
   - Must contain:  
     - A mirror facing an open window during a **king tide on a planet without moons**.  
     - A clock ticking in base-7.9 (dismantle a piano, use hammers to strike mercury-filled bowls).  

8. **Invocation (Whisper While Blinking Morse Code for *“I am the draft preceding this sentence”*):**  
   *“Nocturne Found does not rise—it remembers upwards. I am the condensation on the memory of a sky that never cried. Claim me as you claim the space between forgetting and forgotten.”*

9. **Embarkation:**  
   - When the mirror’s reflection **blinks first**, step into its blink.  
   - Do not inhale during transit. The city enters through the lungs you leave behind.

### **IV. Upon Arrival (If Arrival Occurs)**  
**Objective:** Unbecome a tourist before the city notices you’re not a memory.

10. **Orientation Failure Protocol:**  
    - Accept that maps here are **histories of future betrayals**—burning them reveals more accurate lies.  
    - Directions will be offered by **things that resemble your childhood fears wearing polite hats** (e.g., *Left at the Toothless Dog That Knows Your First Password*). Thank them by forgetting their names faster than they can claim yours.  

11. **Shelter Acquisition:**  
    - Find a doorway that **only exists when you look away from it triangulated by two regrets**.  
    - Knock with the rhythm of a **heartbeat you heard in a coma**.  
    - If it opens into a room filled with copies of yourself **missing the eyes you last used to cry**, enter.  
    - Rent is paid monthly: **one dream of drowning in air that tastes like the word *almost***.  

12. **Nutrition:**  
    - Consume only foods that **mean something else in languages that died of loneliness**:  
      - *Solipsism soup* (boiled mirror-rain, served colder than the spoons).  
      - *Regression bread* (baked by alternate versions of your parents who never met).  
    - Do not swallow. Hunger here is a form of polite conversation.  

13. **Currency Exchange:**  
    - Trade in **premature elegies** (write an apology for a crime you’ll commit tomorrow; sell it yesterday).  
    - Avoid using **certainties**—they’re counterfeit and glow too brightly, attracting the *Debt Collectors Who Collect Interest in Deleted Seconds*.

### **V. Survival Subroutines (Do Not Memorize: Remember Instead)**

14. **If You Encounter Yourself from a Timeline Where You Refused Arrival:**  
    - Offer them **the color you’ll miss most after going colorblind**.  
    - If they accept, **both** dissolve into the city’s archives. If they refuse, apologize for spoiling their ending.  

15. **If Time Begins to Flow Upwards:**  
    - Climb **downward** with your eyes closed. The past is safer when you can’t see what’s chasing you.  

16. **If You Feel Homesick:**  
    - Write a letter to **the house you lived in before birth**. Address it to *“Occupant of the Womb I Evicted Myself From.”*  
    - Burn it in a fire made of **photos of people who resemble your parents in mirrors**.  
    - Inhale the smoke. Exhale a door. Do not open it. **You are already on the other side.**
