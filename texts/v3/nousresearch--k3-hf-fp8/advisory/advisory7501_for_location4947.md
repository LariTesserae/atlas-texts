**Final Preparations for Entry into the City of Inverted Tides**  
*(For the Traveler Whose Shadow Still Remembers Its First Name)*

---

### **I. Reconfigure Your Body’s Contract with Physics**  
1. **Gravity Negotiation Protocols**  
   - Before arrival, practice walking down staircases while convinced the ceiling is the floor. Fail gracefully.  
   - Tape a small compass to your sternum; when it spins backward, offer it a stale crouton (gluten-free if atheist). The city admires polite contradictions.  
   - Your knees must learn to bend in forgiveness, not direction. They will be stairs for strangers, hinges for questions, and witnesses to your thighs converting into maps.  

2. **Respiratory Amendments**  
   - Replace 10% of your daily breaths with these substitutes:  
     - A sigh from a retiring lighthouse keeper.  
     - The pause before a lie cools to truth in someone else’s mouth.  
     - The sound snow makes when it remembers being seawater.  
   - If you cough up small glass figurines shaped like your childhood fears, swallow them again. They’re visa stamps.  

3. **Cardiovascular Syncing**  
   - For one week, sync your heartbeat to the rhythm of:  
     - A washing machine full of lost gloves arguing about left/right politics.  
     - The exact tempo at which a regret forgets its own beginning but not its ending.  
   - If your chest begins to hum in E-flat minor, do not panic. This is merely the city tuning you like a leaky faucet prior to mandatory public utility status.

---

### **II. Modify Your Sense of Scale and Duration**  
4. **Perspective Diminishing Drills**  
   - Hold your thumb against the moon nightly. Whisper, “I am both bigger and smaller than this mistake.”  
   - Purchase a calendar that claims today is July 43rd, 1671, and yesterday is a future you already apologized for. Burn it. Use the ashes to write grocery lists for ghosts.  

5. **Height and Depth Reversals**  
   - Sleep standing up in a closet filled with seashells that hum airport announcements.  
   - When you wake up shorter, do not stretch. This is the city’s way of loaning your height to a balcony that needs to see over its regrets.  
   - If you wake up taller, duck. Doorways here are shy and bite ankles when startled.

---

### **III. Prepare Your Identity for Liquidation and Re-investment**  
6. **Name De-constitution Process**  
   - Write your full name on a leaf. Let a snail read it aloud (use translation earbuds if you don’t speak mucus).  
   - Collect the snail’s trail in a bottle labeled “Person Formerly Known as [Your Name].”  
   - Upon arrival, pour half the trail on your left shoe. Offer the rest to a parking meter in exchange for a parking ticket dated “The Year Laundry Achieved Sentience.” Frame the ticket. It will be your only mirror here.  

7. **Memory Hedge Fund Diversification**  
- Convert 70% of your memories into non-fungible apologies. Hide them in places they’ll never be found (*e.g.*, the exact moment a yawn ends but before the next thought begins).  
- Invest the remaining 30% in speculative memories:  
  - The taste of neon when it was still a baby star.  
  - The sound of one shoe running away from a revolution it never believed in.  
- *Warning:* High volatility. Past performances may hunt you for sport.

---

### **IV. Construct an Emergency Exit That Only Works If Forgotten**  
8. **Forget Kit Assembly**  
   - Contains:  
     - A map folded into origami of the last place you felt certain.  
     - A compass needle magnetized to point toward the smell of rain on your grandfather’s jacket.  
     - A small rock that insists (in a very polite accent) it’s a forgotten dinosaur wishbone.  
   - Usage instructions: Place all items in your pocket. Trust that your pocket will eventually get homesick and leave during a particularly awkward silence. Follow quietly.  

---

### **V. Cultivate Relationships with Non-Human Diplomats**  
9. **Embassy Establishments**  
   - Open an embassy in your left earlobe for the Transparent Gel Citizens:  
     - diplomats communicate via ripples resembling shrugged shoulders. Offer them copper wire to floss with.  
   - Lease your shadow’s least convincing corner to the Scaffolding People (negotiable term: until rust develops opinions about poetry).  
   - Teach your heartbeat Morse code but only for emoticons. The city’s jellyfish-cities find sincerity threatening in paragraph form.  

---

### **VI. Ritual for the Moment You Realize You’ve Always Been Here**  
10. **The Un-Arrival Ceremony**  
   - Upon noticing your reflection waving goodbye instead of hello:  
     - Take off your shoes. If they’re already off, take off your footprints.  
     - Recite the city’s national anthem (listen closely—it’s the sound of all the elevators in the world humming different halves of the same apology).  
     - To the first thing that speaks without a mouth, offer your last untrue statement wrapped in seaweed.  
     - Do not wait to be forgiven.  

---

### **Post-Script for the Version of You That Will Write This Note to Themself Later**  
If you find this guide folded into a paper boat sailing your bloodstream, swallow it again.  
It’s not instructions.  
It’s a recipe for becoming the kind of lost that can finally answer the sea when it asks:  
*"Was it worth it?  
the holding back?  
the building upward instead of diving?"*  

*Travel lightly.  
And by lightly, I mean:  
abandon everything except the question  
you were too afraid to ask  
the dark  
when you still believed  
it wasn’t listening.*  

— *The Committee to Preserve the Concept of Leaving*  
(Sponsored by the Unfunded Regrets of All Departed Elevators)
