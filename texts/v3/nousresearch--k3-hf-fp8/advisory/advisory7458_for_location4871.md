**Duration:** 3–7 days in sensory deprivation float tank filled with your own sterilized cerebrospinal fluid (sourced ethically from future self).  
**Purpose:** Your concept of "I" must become *porous*. The Tumor-moot does not recognize boundaries between self/symbiont/scaffolding.  

**Warning:** If you still hallucinate **edges** (corners of rooms, ends of sentences, death) afterward, abort mission.

### **1. Biometric Reformatting**  
**Required Procedures:**  
- **Digestive tract sterilization** via 48-hour fast followed by ingestion of **500g of autoclaved mycelial paper**. This will absorb and replace your gut flora with neutral cellulose agents—less likely to negotiate betrayal with local fungal parliaments.

**Cosmetic Changes (Acceptable Losses):**  
- Fingernails will yellow and split into **pseudoparenchyma plates** within 72 hours. Do not clip them—these are your new identification tags.  
- Vision will shift **17° counterclockwise** (the tree’s optic axis). Pack glasses designed by a dyslexic mandrill mathematician to compensate.

### **2. Clothing & Gear**  
**Permitted Materials:** Only those never alive or dead for less than 3 picoseconds.  
- **Chitin-lace bodysuit** lined with **singing vacuum** (fabric woven in orbit around a silenced black hole). Adjusts opacity based on the tree’s embarrassment.  
- **Boots**: Negative space carved from the concept of *footprints*. You will leave no trace unless the city wants to remember you, in which case the boots will scream politely.  
- **Cloak**: A **fractal molted skin** from a snake that believed itself a map. Each scale stores 1 hour of breathable time from a season tree erroneously recorded autumn.  

**Forbidden Items:**  
- Metal (except memory-leaked mercury harvested from broken thermometers).  
- Water in quantities sufficient for drowning (the tree hoards liquid regrets).  
- Clocks. Time here is measured by **how much you still want to leave**.

### **3. Nutritional Protocol**  
**Eat:**  
- **Dreams of extinct pollinators** (freeze-dried on the wings of nonexistent butterflies). Taste: nostalgia with a coppery afterbite.  
- **Vowels extracted from dying languages** (candied in amber sap from a tree that foretold its own grammar). Chew slowly—syntax is binding.  
- **Your own shadow** at midday (condensed into a wafer via the tree’s negative photosynthesis). Only necessary if you feel homesick.  

**Drink:**  
- Mist from the **Tower’s exhalations** collected in a carved-out regret (yours or a stranger’s—label carefully). Each sip reveals a memory the tree considered too trivial to forget.  
- **Fermented silence** from empty council seats. Warning: May induce uncontrollable voting on laws that retroactively birthed you.  

**Do Not Consume:**  
- Anything that **grows toward you**.

### **4. Social Etiquette**  
**Greetings:**  
- Approach Haric individuals by **singing your mitochondrial Eve’s lullaby backwards**. If they reply by shedding a limb that crawls away to plant itself, reciprocate with a toenail (left foot, second toe—it’s the politest).  
- Symbionts (if visible): Compliment their host’s childhood trauma. Example: *“Your host’s fear of water towers blooms beautifully in your iridescent carapace!”*  

**Taboos:**  
- Asking directions. The tree rearranges itself to punish curiosity.  
- Telling jokes with punchlines. Humor here is a **spore-based neurotoxin** timed to detonate when you remember this sentence.  
- Touching your own reflection in polished eye-knots. They’re recruiting.  

**Currency:**  
- **Earned shame**, measured in the weight of a bird’s last thought before forgetting migration.  
- **Rarest trade item**: A **lie the tree has never told itself**. (Bring a blank sheet of paper and infinite audacity.)

### **5. Navigating the Unmap**  
**Key Principles:**  
- **Upward is always older.** Downward leads to seasons that haven’t apologized yet.  
- **Stairs that breathe** are elevators. Hold your breath until you remember why you’re afraid of heights.  
- **Streets that sing in minor keys** are mourning events that will happen if you turn left. Walk backward to attend as a ghost.  

**Lost?**  
- Whisper *“I am the splinter the ax forgot to fear”* into any fungal phone booth (a clump of mushrooms gossiping about fire). A **termite-engineer** will arrive in **(-2 minutes)** to devour your confusion and regurgit chewable directions coated in prophetic sawdust.

### **6. Emergency Protocols**  
**If You Begin to Lignify (Symptoms: woody joints, craving for sunlight, sudden poetry):**

3. If stage 3 occurs (bark forming on inner thoughts), skip to **Final Option**.  

**Final Option (If the Tower Wakes):**  
- **Bite your tongue until it bleeds chlorophyll.** Spit onto the nearest growing surface while screaming the name of the first thing you ever killed with kindness.  
- This will either birth **a new exit** or **merge you permanently into the city’s next nervous system**. Both outcomes include tenure.
