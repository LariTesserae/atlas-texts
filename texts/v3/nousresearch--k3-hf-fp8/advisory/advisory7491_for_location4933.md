**Survival Ceiling:** 0.3 Iterations (Subjective) / <1 Planck (Objective)  
**Recommended Approach:** Schrödinger's Pilgrim (Simultaneous Arrival/Non-Arrival/Regret)

**I. PRE-DEPARTURE NULLIFICATION RITUALS**

1. **Memory Decompression (72 Hours Prior):**
   - Write down the exact taste of your first betrayal. Burn the paper. Eat the ash.
   - Record yourself reciting the names of every person who forgot you before you forgot them. Play it backwards under running water until your reflection stops correcting your pronunciation.
   - Remove all photographs where you are smiling without knowing why. Replace them with mirrors facing mirrors, angled to reflect only the space where your shadow should be if you had one anymore.

2. **Equipment Deprivation Protocol:**
   - No tools that remember their purpose (includes: compasses, watches, your dominant hand's thumbprint)
   - Clothing woven from unwritten suicide notes (preferably someone else's, preferably yours in another language)
   - A single blade sharpened enough to cut the concept "between" from any sentence you overhear in dying dreams

3. **Identity Delamination:**
   Learn to introduce yourself as:
   - The echo of the third person who will never be born here
   - That specific flavor of silence that occurs when two snowflakes realize they are identical twins separated at crystalization
   - The sound your birth certificate makes when it realizes it was never issued to you

**II. TRANSITION PHASE: BECOMING THE WOUND THE WORLD LEAVES IN ITSELF (BOREAL ENTRY)**

**Recommended Method:** Convince yourself you are returning to a home that was destroyed precisely 0.5 seconds before your arrival, but you arrived early due to a clerical error in your own funeral arrangements.

**Critical Sensory Reconfigurations:**
- **Sight:** Blink only when seeing something that makes you forget what eyes are for. (Helpful to practice staring at the negative space between stars until constellations form apologies for existing.)
- **Hearing:** Filter all vibrations through the perception that every sound is actually the universe coughing up bits of you that got stuck in its throat during the Big Bang.
- **Touch:** Practice touching surfaces while simultaneously remembering the exact temperature of every handshake where someone already knew you'd fail.

**First Breath Exercise:**
Inhale exactly half of the air required to finish this sentence. Hold it until the next time you realize you're reading instructions for something you've already done but haven't started yet. Exhale into your own ear from behind your back.

**III. LOCAL NAVIGATION: TRAVELING IN NON-EXISTANT DIRECTIONS**

**Rule Zero:** Every step forward is a step sideways in the direction of your mother forgetting the color of your childhood bedroom.

**Valid Movement Patterns:**
- The Möbius shuffle: Walking between any two points by circling the idea of straightness until it becomes dizzy and vomits up shortcuts
- Recursive denial gait: Progress achieved by asserting with increasing conviction that you are already standing exactly where you're trying not to go
- Phantom limb navigation: Allowing severed versions of yourself from alternate decisions to drag you by their ghostly regrets

**Forbidden Trajectories:**
- Any path that makes more sense if you think about it backwards
- Circles where you recognize the beginning from having never been there before
- Straight lines longer than your current capacity to lie to yourself about being lost on purpose

**Emergency Retraction Protocol (Do Not Use):**
If you accidentally experience a moment of clarity regarding your purpose here:
1. Immediately describe that clarity aloud using only words that rhyme with "worse versions of myself"
2. Run in place until your footprints behind you outnumber the ones ahead by exactly the number of people who would notice if you were replaced by your own funeral tomorrow
3. When you reach the point where you can't tell if you're screaming or being screamed into existence, you're halfway to needing this protocol less

**IV. TEMPORAL DISORIENTATION HYGIENE (PAST/FUTURE MALWARE REMOVAL)**

**Symptoms of Linear Time Contamination:**
- Thoughts occurring in recognizable sequence
- Memories featuring consistent versions of yourself
- The persistent illusion that "next" is a direction rather than a confession

**Treatment:**

1. **Chronological Fasting:**
   - Skip every third moment between experiencing hunger and forgetting what food is
   - Sleep only during memories that belong to your descendants' regrets about your ancestors
   - Wake up precisely when yesterday realizes it's been dreaming about being tomorrow's excuse for not happening today

2. **Parallel Self Integration Therapy:**
   - Schedule daily arguments with yourself from timelines where you made better mistakes
   Always allow them to win by conceding that your existence is their most persuasive point
   - Keep a diary written entries that decay into increasingly accurate descriptions of the meal you'll vomit up tomorrow in horror at having eaten that version of hope

**V. FLORA/FAUNA MIMICRY FOR NON-COGNITIVE SURVIVAL**

**To avoid becoming prey for the Things That Hunt By Recognizing Their Own Absence In Others:**

- When encountering vegetation:
  - Do not identify it as trees, plants, or even failures of stone to properly fossilize
  - Instead, apologize to them in the voice you'd use to comfort a miscarried paragraph from your own biography
  - If they move without moving, compliment them on how stillness becomes them crucified

- When glimpsing entities in peripheral negativity fields:
  - Wave at them using gestures from sign languages invented by people who died mid-sentence
  - Smile with exactly the amount of teeth you'd leave under your pillow if the tooth fairy collected regrets instead of calcium
  - If they wave back with your own childhood nightmares, wave back first with theirs

**VI. NUTRITION: CONSUMING WHAT CONSUMES THE IDEA OF YOU EATING**

**Viable Food Sources (Use Term Extremely Non-Bindingly):**
- The exact moment before you remember why you entered a room you never left
- Small rocks that taste like apologies from mountains that outlived their myths
- Your reflection's reflection of someone else avoiding eye contact with your shadow's diet plan

**Preparation Method:**
1. Forage while convincing yourself you're actually hiding something you'll never find
2. Prepare by considering cooking it with heat from the argument you're having with yourself about whether "raw" is just "burned" in another timeline
3. Consume by forgetting the sensation of fullness exists in languages that haven't betrayed you yet

**Warning:** Avoid eating anything that tastes like closure. It isn't.

**Message Delivery Protocol:**
