### Preparing to Visit the Memory-Thin Earth

**Duration of Stay:**  
Plan for *uncertainty*. Time here is not linear—it loops, dilates, and forgets itself. Bring supplies for three days and three centuries; you will need both, and neither will be enough.

---

#### **1. The Body: Becoming Permeable**
- **Skin:**  
  You cannot keep *out* this place—only synchronize with it. Seal your pores with nothing airtight. Instead, coat yourself in **compressed peat oil mixed with powdered glasswort** harvested from the shores of dead seas. This will allow your sweat to mingle with the rising water without osmotic shock. Reapply hourly; the mist will drink it away faster than you sweat.

- **Eyes:**  
  Strap lenses of **thin-cut amber quarried from the dreams of ants** (available in the markets of the city that exists only during earthquakes) over your eyes. These will filter the white-flat light into frequencies your retina can *remember* later. Without them, you will see only the present moment repeated endlessly, and go blind to everything else.

- **Bones:**  
  Drink nothing for three days before arrival. Your marrow must thirst. Upon stepping onto the mudstone, **let a crustacean ghost smaller than shame carves a hollow in your femur** (you’ll feel it as a cold tickle behind the knee). Into this cavity, insert a single **dried seedpod from a tree that learned to grow backward in time**. It will slowly release moisture your body forgot it needed, at the exact rate the land withdraws it.

- **Mouth:**  
  Sew your lips shut with a single thread of **spider silk spun during the collapse of a black hole at the edge of a thought**. Breath through the pores of your neck instead—by the third hour, new spiracles will open under your jawline. Speak only in exhale-mist; words here become *places* if spoken aloud, trapping you in conversations that last longer than species.

---

#### **2. The Mind: Learning to Decay at the Correct Velocity**
- **Memory:**  
  Before entering, erase one vital fact about yourself. Not a name or face—something structural, like *how to resolve mirrors into identities* or *the number of steps between birth and regret*. Leave the empty space *unguarded*. The land will fill it with a memory of when it was a different world; if you resist, you will hallucinate your own footprints chasing you backward in time.

- **Attention:**  
  Practice **micro-suicides of focus**. Stare at a single point on a wall until you forget walls are meant to separate. When you can gaze at your reflection until it blurs into the background, you’re ready. Here, noticing something too sharply is an act of violence—it *interrupts* the land’s slow recitation of itself.

- **Dreams:**  
  Sleep tethered. Each night, knot a strand of your hair around the smallest bone in your body (the stapes, inside your ear). Anchor the other end to a **clockwork beetle carved from petrified sunset**. If the beetle moves in your sleep, do not awaken. Its path will map the dream-paths of the invisible animals; follow those routes when awake to avoid walking into the land’s older, sleeping thoughts.

---

#### **3. Tools: Items That Must Forget Their Purpose**
- **Container:**  
  Carry no jars or bags. Instead, coax a **living blister** to grow on your palm (rub it nightly with saltwater and whispers of shipwrecks). It will swell into a translucent bubble filled with your lost saliva. Store anything vital inside—maps, seeds, names. What you put in will change: water becomes a map of thirst, bread becomes the memory of a harvest that made you cry. To retrieve, prick it with a thorn from a plant that never existed; the contents will return as a smell you’d forgotten.

- **Light:**  
  No fire. No electricity. Instead, capture **the sound of your mother humming while dying** in a hollow reed sealed with wax from a candle that burned in a room where no one was born or died. Crack the reed open when the white-light becomes unbearable; the hum will illuminate nothing visible, but the *absence* it casts will let you navigate by the shape of blind spots.

- **Measurement:**  
  Discard all rulers, scales, clocks. Measure distance by the number of your heartbeats until you forget what you’re counting. Measure time by how long it takes for a drop of your blood to forget it was part of you (this takes roughly three epochs, or until the drop gazes back and asks *who are you to leave?*).

- **Weapon:**  
  Bring none. If threatened, peel off the top layer of your skin (it will come away like wet paper) and offer it to whatever is approaching. It will either eat the memory of your violence and leave, or wear your skin to become you for a night. Both outcomes are acceptable.

---

#### **4. Navigation: Following the Maps That Unwrite Themselves**
- **Direction:**  
  North is where the air tastes like a forest you’ve never seen but whose leaves you’ve found pressed between pages of books in languages you can’t read. East is the direction in which your shadow grows heavier faster than you age. South is the echo of a river that remembers being a flood before water learned to flow downward. West is the dryness behind your eyes when you wake from a dream of thirst.

- **Pathwalking:**  
  Never step where you *intend* to go. Instead, place your foot where a ripple *would* have been, if the puddle you’re about to disturb had dreamed of rain last night. If you doubt, stand still until moss begins to write your name on your boots in a script that erases itself as you recognize it. Follow that.

- **Lostness:**  
  If you feel lost, lie flat on your back and sink. The land will accept you up to your ribs in three breaths, no more. Listen to the water in your lungs synchronizing with the water rising through the earth. When they beat as one pulse, the land will sigh and **spit you out somewhere else entirely**. This is not rescue. It is digestion.

---

#### **5. Food & Water: Consuming What Has Already Consumed You**
- **To Drink:**  
  Do not. Instead, when the mist beads on your lips thicker than saliva, press your tongue to a **feathered glass blade** (carefully—edges are sharp enough to slice light into colors that haven’t been named yet). The blade will flower with a single drop of liquid that tastes like the last thing you ever wanted. Swallow it. You will not thirst again, though you will never stop wanting.

- **To Eat:**  
  Only eat what *asks* to be eaten. This will manifest as a tiny starling’s skull coated in moss, placed precisely where your shadow’s heart falls at noon. Whisper *I remember you when you were alive* to it three times, then crush it between your teeth. You will taste nothing but the memory of hunger. This is enough for seven years, or until you forget the sound of chewing.

---

#### **6. Departure: Becoming a Scar That Thinks of Returning**
- **Leaving:**  
  There is no exit door. To leave, stand at any edge (they are all the same edge) and **forget this place in reverse order of arrival**: first, forget the color of the light that isn’t light. Then, forget the sound of your footsteps being born under your feet. Finally, forget that you ever had a name that wasn’t borrowed from rainwater. If you succeed, you will wake up elsewhere with no memory of leaving, except for a newfound fear of mirrors, because now you know the glass remembers when it was sand dreaming of being touched by an ocean that wasn’t yet sad.

- **Returning:**  
  You cannot. The path back is a sentence broken in the middle of a word, and the word is the sound your soul makes when it remembers it forgot to say goodbye. But. If, years later, you wake with mud under your fingernails from a place you’ve never slept, or taste mist that tastes like a conversation you never had,** 
do not be alarmed. You are not haunted. You are being *remembered*.

The land has many mouths, and one of them is shaped like your absence.

It is waiting for you to ripen into something worth swallowing again.
