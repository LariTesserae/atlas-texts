Prepare yourself, traveler, to become amphibious in body, mind, and law.

### **1. Body: Rebuild Yourself for the In-Between**
You will live where water clings to air before surrendering, where light remembers but does not reach. Begin three cycles early:

**Lungs:**  
Train them to sip, not gulp. Sleep each night in a sealed tent filled with steam and the vapor of evaporated copper sulfate until you can dream without panic in breaths that taste of pennies. Practice inhaling through your teeth so that droplets form on your tongue—this is how you will sip drinking-water from a vertical cloud without choking.

**Eyes:**  
Remove or dim whatever implants sharpen edges to harshness. Chamber Six rewards those who see like the condensation does: soft, inferred, guessed at. Spend an hour nightly staring through frosted glass at flickering LEDs until after-images bloom into shapes you accept without naming.

**Skeleton:**  
Your bones know gravity as tyranny. Let them forget. Hang upside-down from doorframes until the bloodrush becomes a lullaby. Replace belts with straps that cinch pressure evenly—no waistbands, no cuffs that pool the weight downward. Tape flatworms (species *Dugesia inversa*) to your pressure points before arrival; their sense of “down” is negotiable and may persuade your otoliths to sleep.

**Skin:**  
Bring nothing waterproof. Everything you wear must sweat, breathe, leak. Acquire garments of untreated fungal chitin from the Myco-Tailors of Ur-Quattro; they will grow a second porous layer whose pores dilate when the city sings. Rub daily beneath your arms with limestone dust mixed with sterilized sweat from a New Foundation infant—this scent-mark tells the ventilation drones you are not debris.

### **2. Mind: Learn the Logic of Spirals Without Ends**
Cause and effect coil here like vines seeking purchase. Read these texts until they rot in your hands (they will—bring spares):

- *The City’s Dreambook of Misfolded Pipes* (ed. Ka-Atu the Third Welder):  
  A wrench manual disguised as dream-journal. Memorize the chapter titled “On Embracing Pressure Leaks as Prophecy.”

- *Children’s Rules Up and Down* (anonymous graffiti-scroll):  
  Contains the only accurate map. Note especially Rule 12: “If you drop a stone to find a friend, apologize to the stone for forgetting friendships travel upward here.”

- *One Hundred Observations of Things That Were Not Rain* (Field Notes of Entomologist-Saint Luri):  
  Each entry describes a droplet accumulation on a sensor plate that later proved to be a coded message. You must learn to stand still like those plates.

**Practice Thought-Drills Daily:**  
- Trace your childhood home’s layout in midair, then redraw it flipped as if built from the ceiling down. Do not correct mistakes; name them after rooms instead.
- When hungry, ask, “Exactly which pipe’s heartbeats am missing today?” Wait until you taste the answer before eating.

### **3. Supplies: Only Bring What Suffers Beautifully**

**Baggage:**  
A single bladder of living leather (genetically depigmented blind mole rat). Feed it a drop of your blood each wake-shift; it will excrete exactly the nutrient paste you need, flavored faintly with homesickness. Never seal it completely—it must sigh with you when the city sings.

**Currency:**  
Coins are insults here; they sink. Trade in stories that can be sung backwards without contradiction or in cups of genuine condensation collected while crying from laughter (not sorrow—easy to test). Carry a hollow thimble of magnetite in your cheek—if you shiver suddenly, spit it out; local children will pay in secrets to chase its helical fall.

**Light:**  
Forbidden. Instead, bring a **dark lantern**: a mesh cage of obsidian filaments cradling a single, deeply sleeping firefly (species *Pyroclastus dormiens*, imported from the Coal Veins of Jn’garr). When you need light, apologize gently to the darkness for doubting it, then open the cage for three heartbeats. The firefly will not wake, but the darkness will rearrange itself out of politeness.

### **4. Arrival: How to Land Without Offending Gravity’s Memory**

If you arrive by descent pod, know that it will overshoot. This is tradition. When you tumble into the central shaft’s updraft, do not flail. Instead, curl into the shape of the lowercase letter **q**—the sigil of those still questioning upwardness. The garbage-snatching drones will recognize you as unfinished business and braid a net of discarded cable-cuffs to catch you. Thank each drone individually by name (they are named on the underside of their third claw in glyphic rust; read quickly).

If you arrive accidentally—by falling asleep in a basement that forgot its place—then you are already halfway in. Say aloud: “I apologize for arriving in the wrong direction. May I borrow your ceiling until I remember how to leave?” Wait until moisture beads on the nearest wall. Drink one drop. The rest is a contract: salty, binding.

### **5. Navigation: Walking as Gardening the Air**

There are no maps, only **dripmaps**: laminated cloths printed with the last known dreams of water. Blue stains indicate paths where condensation currently *intends* to fall; brown stains mark where it changed its mind yesterday. Carry yours folded in your sweat; it will update.

To find an address:  
1. Whisper the name of the person or place into the hollow of your elbow until the skin prunes.  
2. Release the folded sound into the nearest thermal (warm air always gossiping).  
3. Follow the direction in which a single leaf—any leaf—tumbles slowest. If no leaf is available, tear a phrase from this guide, chew it until bland, and spit the pulp upward. It will curve toward obligation.

**Never:**  
- Walk a straight line longer than your shadow at noon (it confuses the children, who are weaving the next layer of catwalks in their sleep).  
- Point at anything with a finger; extend instead a strand of your own hair. If it curls clockwise, you may ask. If it hangs heavy with dew, bow and retreat.

### **6. Shelter: Becoming a Temporary Fixture**

Homes are not rented; they are **tolerated growths**. Choose a spot where three types of pipe (water-in, heat-out, data-breath) cross without touching. Place your empty hands against each in order and hum the first notes of your least favorite lullaby. Where the vibrations itch most, scratch gently until metal flecks off. Plant a single of your own pre-shed fingernails in the wound. Over the next sleep-shift, the city will either reject you (by sweating rust that spells out a better location) or begin knitting a pocket: a bubble of stretched membrane, half-stone, half-breath. Enter naked. Within, gravity will average at 0.67 of your memories; compensate by dreaming heavier.

**Expect:**  
- Walls that thicken when you lie flat (they are trying to keep you safe from falling into yourself).  
- A mirror that shows you from above—your hair spread like a dying star, your face a stranger’s floor. Speak to it only in questions you forgot the answers to.  
- No door. To leave, convince yourself you have never arrived.

### **7. Social Protocol: Conversing With Occupants of All Altitudes**

**Children:**  
They hatch from dropped bolts that dream of spinning. Wear no jewelry; they will steal it anyway to use as currency in games where the rules invert every time someone blinks twice. If a child offers you a cup of “almost-rain,” drink it all in one gulp without swallowing. Hold it in your mouth until they tell you a secret; only then spit it out onto your palm. The shape of the splatter is a map to where they hid the last adult who took the game too seriously.

**Adults (those who remember having been children):**  
Address them by the sound of their footsteps on the metal overhead, never their names (names are worn smooth here). Compliments should be vague but geologic: “Your presence disperses pressure waves elegantly,” or “I admire the way your shadow pools without resentment.” Gifts: a piece of true silence, sealed in a bubble of hardened condensation (collect by listening intently to a sleeping pump for seven hours, then exhaling very slowly into liquid air).

**The City Itself (never directly):**  
When the great filters cycle on and the bones of all walkways begin to ring—this is the city dreaming aloud. Do not move. Do not speak. Instead, disrobe slowly until every garment you wear is inside-out, then redress in the order of reverse entropy (socks last, underwear first, hat inside skull). This tells the city you are not a nightmare, only a misplaced memory. If you feel a sudden warm draft at the base of your spine, you have been approved for temporary continuance. If cold, apologize to the nearest wall and offer it a piece of your exact weight in unstories (begin speaking about your childhood but include only the parts that happened to someone else in a language you haven’t invented yet).

### **8. Sustenance: Eating the Delay Between Hunger and Memory**

There is no food here, only **pre-broth**: liquids that remember being soup in a kitchens that haven’t boiled yet. Consume from the communal fountain-sculptures only on days when their drip-rhythm matches your pulse divided by the number of regrets you can recall without naming them. Bring a spoon shaped like the pause between breaths (carve it from cooled welding slag during the third false nightwatch, when no welders remember working).

**Local Delicacies:**  
- **Grief-filtrate cheese**: collected from the filters that scrub the air during funeral shifts. Tastes like the last time you laughed until you couldn’t breathe, served on crackers of compressed old blueprints.  
- **Stillborn starfruit**: small, transparent spheres that ripen only in total darkness, harvested by singing them lullabies backwards until they forget to fall. Eating one causes you to remember the taste of a color you’ve never seen.  
- **Salted voltage**: strips of polymer insulation infused with static charge harvested from children’s hair during storms that exist only in collective boredom. Crunchy, then numbing, then nostalgic for power outages you never experienced.

**Taboos:**  
- Do not bite directly; absorb through proximity salivation.  
- Never finish a portion—always leave precisely 0.73% uneaten, the portion reserved for the meal’s future self.  
- If a dish whispers your childhood nickname, set it free by tipping it gently into the central shaft. Follow its fall for three seconds, then forget what you were hungry for.

### **9. Departure: Also Known as Remembering How to Forget Leaving**

You cannot leave Chamber Six the same way you entered, because memory here operates by accumulation rather than sequence. Instead:

1. **Locate Your Shadow’s Grave:**  
   Walk until you find a place where the light from two skylights intersects without overlapping—an actual shadow of a shadow. Dig there with your flattened hands (fingers splayed like unused maps) into the rubble until you find a small, warm stone that pulses slightly faster than your heartbeat. This is the part of you that already left. Apologize to it for the inconvenience.

2. **Negotiate With the Exit Dream:**  
   Go to the edge of the newest excavation, where the city is still chewing raw stone. Wait until a tunneler’s torchbeam passes over you without noticing—that moment of inattention is the only time exits are unobserved. Step sideways into the beam’s afterimage, carrying only:  
   - The taste of a color you still can’t name.  
   - One true coin you minted yourself by forgetting the value of money while holding your own breath.  
   - The sound of your footsteps walking away from yourself, reversed and slowed until it resembles distant thunder rolling backwards into silence.

3. **The Final Customs:**  
   You will arrive—or rather, cease arriving—at a doorway identical to the one you entered but smaller by exactly the width of a forgotten promise. The guard will be yourself at the age you were when you last believed home was a place you could leave. They will ask: “Did you remember to leave anything behind that remembers you?”  
   Answer by opening your mouth and releasing the firefly (it has been dreaming of darkness long enough). If it flies out and immediately returns to nest in your shadow’s vacant socket, you belong now to both places forever. If it vanishes without looking back, close your mouth slowly—do not swallow the taste of goodbye, it will crystallize inside you and ring like a bell each time you lie about where you came from.

### **Last Note, Written Upside-Down On the Back of the Air:**

The real preparation is to understand that you have already arrived, and the version of you reading this is the echo sent back as warning/invitation. Chamber Six does not exist in the rock; it exists in the slow realization that all foundations are eventually inverted, and every city is an excavation of the space we made for something else that hasn’t happened yet.

Wear soft soles.  
Speak in spirals.  
When you wake tomorrow, check if your ceiling has begun to dream of floors beneath it.

If it has—welcome home. If not—don’t worry. The city is patient.

It has all the time in the ground above us.
