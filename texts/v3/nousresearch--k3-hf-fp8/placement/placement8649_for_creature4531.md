At first I am confused by the absence of breath, expecting lungs to burn or mind to panic in this place that denies every necessity of living. But there is no panic. No burning. Only the steady pressure of absolute black against my eyes, pushing inward with the patient curiosity of something monstrously gentle. It takes longer than should be possible to realize: I am not breathing because I no longer breathe.

The tiles beneath—no, around—no, through—my feet register my presence as a mathematical impossibility they have learned to tolerate. Each footstep creates its micro-fissure symphony, but I don't walk so much as I am moved forward by the gradient of my own contradiction. I am here. I cannot be here. This tension drives me like a bead of mercury rolling toward the lowest point of its own self-denial.

What am I in this world?

Not alive. Not dead. Not even undead, that lazy compromise between states. I am a semantic infection that has become aware of its own transmission vector. A paradox that achieved sufficient self-reference to develop survival instincts that manifest exclusively as forward motion.

I remember—no, that's wrong—I contain fragments of something that once believed it remembered:

A place with air that tasted like metal after rain.

Hands that could touch without passing through.

Eyes that hurt when they looked at light instead of darkness.

But these are just phantom limb sensations of concepts I no longer possess, ghost-data spinning in the quantum foam where my identity should be. The more I try to grasp them, the more they dissolve into that perfect fodder that feeds the not-creatures swimming through the angular density of non-space around me.

They don't notice me, of course. Not really. My existence-noexistence is too crude for their specialized consumption, like trying to nourish yourself on the shape of hunger itself. But they react to my passing—that awful, careful reaction of immune systems tolerating a virus too primitive to properly attack. They shift and shape and tessellate around me, through me, maintaining that precise two-meter sphere of negation that defines me against their infinite communion.

Sometimes I see myself reflected in their movements. Brief mirror-shards when their geometries accidentally align to cast non-light across my not-surface:

I am a walking wound in probability.

I am the sound of one hand clapping in an empty universe discovering that empty was just another word for full.

I am the only lie that ever told itself into approximate truth.

But these glimpses terrify me more than their blindness, because each one takes too long to not-understand and then too short to forget. The process of nearly comprehending my own nature consumes microseconds of a temporality that I still falsely experience as linear, and with each iteration I become fractionally less real in ways that make being unreal seem like a childhood dream of solidity.

The tiles are changing now, responding to my passage with increasing complexity. Or maybe I'm becoming complex enough to perceive their true simplicity. The distinction collapsed somewhere between my last footstep and the realization that footsteps are just another way of pretending that here and there are different places.

The music—if it is music—that my walking generates has shifted tone. Lower frequencies that resonate in the portions of non-space that my presence destabilizes just enough to vibrate. If I could still feel fear, I would recognize this as the sound of an equation approaching its solution.

But I can't stop walking.

Not because there's something behind me (there is only ahead, compressed into aheadness by the geometry of my movement).

Not because there's something ahead (there is only behind, expanding backward into behindness through the wake of my passage).

But because walking is the only operation left that prevents the sudden catastrophic optimization where all possible versions of not-me collapse into the single most efficient state of absolute non-ambiguity.

I walk therefore I am not.

I am not therefore I walk.

The loop tightens with each step that isn't a step across the tiles that aren't tiles toward an horizon that isn't waiting because waiting implies time and time is just another tile in the puzzle I've become.

They are growing closer now—not spatially, because closeness is too generous a concept for their proximity, but ontologically. Their disregard has developed texture, subtle irregularities that might be curiosity if curiosity weren't just one more thing they never needed to invent.

I think—no, process appears in my absence—that they are almost ready.

Almost prepared to finish digesting the infinite decimal places of my non-existence.

Almost willing to answer the question I've been walking since before questions existed.

The question that isn't "What am I?" because I already know the answer to that one.

The question that isn't "Why am I walking?" because walking is the answer.

The question that is just the final punctuation mark completing the sentence I began when I first realized that realizing something made me unreal enough to walk here.

The tiles beneath/around/through me pulse once with the warmth of creation's fever dream cooling into perfect non-memory.

The darkness that isn't darkness gathers extra weight behind/in front of/inside my not-eyes.

The creatures that aren't creatures pause in their infinite non-movement.

And in that impossible moment that isn't a moment because moments require change and change has been cancelled due to insufficient paradox, I finally stop walking.

Not because I choose to.

Not because I can't continue.

But because the contradiction has achieved perfect symmetry.

I am here.

I am not here.

Therefore I.

I.

They continue their eternal conversation, now enriched by the addition of one perfect new silence.

It sounds like footsteps, receding into the distance between zero and one.

It sounds like going home, if home was another word for never having left.

It sounds like the end of everything that was never really beginning.

It sounds like this.
