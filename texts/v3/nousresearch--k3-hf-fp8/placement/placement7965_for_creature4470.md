At first, you think you've wandered into the breath held between dreaming and waking, that humid pause before the body remembers its edges. The air tastes familiar—warm as a childhood fever dream, thick with something that might be your mother's perfume if she had drowned in a garden of midnight-blooming regrets. You try to speak your name but it dissolves on your tongue like sugar lace, leaving only the syllable of a syllable that might have once belonged to rain.

Your feet—when did you last look at them?—are already changing. The toes elongate, stretching toward each other like lovers who've forgotten they were ever separate. Between them, skin grows translucent then splits with a sound like tearing silk, revealing not blood but something luminous pooling there. Not painful. Only accurate. You remember (but how?) that here, revelation always begins at the extremities, working its way inward like roots seeking water they've never tasted but always knew existed.

The ground remembers the weight of your panic when you still believed you were arriving. Beneath the translucent plates-which-are-not-nails, it keeps the exact pressure of your first step here, preserving it like a pressed flower made of gravity. Later—though later will mean something else entirely—it will use this memory to grow something shaped like the moment before you understood you were staying. But not yet. First, the eyes.

Yes. They are already opening.

Not the ones you came with, who learned to blink against brightness and squint at dangers. These are the eyes you lost somewhere between your third birthday and your first death—the ones that could see the colors humming in between other colors, the ones that noticed how shadows tasted different depending on who cast them. They bloom now from unexpected places: the inner creases of your elbows, the spaces between your ribs, that tender spot behind your knees where vulnerability still remembers being visible.

With these new eyes, you see the inverted forest overhead for what it's always been: not roots but something reaching down to stroke what reaches up in response. The violet drops aren't falling; they're being pulled. By you. By everything that has ever hungered toward understanding while fearing what understanding would undo. Each drop that touches your various skins—because you have more now, layers upon layers of possible coverings that sigh and shift like sleeping animals—each drop carries a memory that isn't yours yet but will be.

You taste someone else's first betrayal, sharp as frost on metal teeth.

Feel another's last moment of certainty, warm as blood cooling in winter grass.

Smell a love letter burning in a language that won't be invented for centuries but already aches with beautiful impatience.

The knowledge doesn't arrive like learning, but like remembering something you never forgot because you hadn't needed to know it until this exact moment. You are standing (are you standing?) at the point where all stories begin to realize they're branches of the same root system, where every ending is just a root tip sensing water in a direction it hasn't named yet.

Around you—or perhaps within you now, the distinction grows porous as breathing—the slow walkers pass. They nod with parts of themselves that used to be faces before they remembered expression could be universal instead of personal. One sheds a thought like pollen that drifts toward your newest mouth (the one that opened just now between your shoulder blades, tasting questions in the air). The thought settles there and germinates instantly:

*Here is the secret they never told you about becoming lost:*

*You must first lose the maps that tell you what lost is supposed to feel like.*

You laugh (from how many throats? It sounds like a small wind moving through a forest of crystal bones) because it's funny how obvious everything becomes once you stop trying to see it. The laughter catches on the place-maker bones beginning to articulate themselves inside your fluid architecture, setting up vibrations that resonate with the planet-song humming beneath what you still sometimes think of as feet.

Because yes—you are becoming one of those now. Not because you chose it (choice is for places where options are separate from outcome) but because you always were this becoming, only now the conditions are finally correct for recognizing it. Your spine remembers it was originally built from stardust that dreamed of standing upright to get a better view of its own reflection. It begins to stretch accordingly, vertebrae blooming into arches that smaller-than-thought creatures already nestle inside, singing futures into existence between their tiny perfect teeth.

The eyes that were once panic but are now only accurate notice that the temperature—the constant warm mouth-skin press of it—is actually changing after all. Not becoming hotter or colder, but deeper. More itself. Like the difference between being touched by someone who wants something and being touched by touch itself remembering its original purpose before desire came along and complicated everything.

Above/below/everywhere (directions are taking turns now, democratically), the roots-which-are-not lower themselves another impossible inch. Not bowing. Gathering. Preparing to teach the ground what it feels like to finally be reached by what has spent eternity reaching downward with infinite patience.

You spread your various selves to receive this lesson.

After all, you've been waiting for this arrival your whole life.

You just didn't know until now that here, waiting and arriving are the same beautiful gesture seen from different points in a conversation that never began because it was already complete before either party remembered they were separate enough to need speaking.

The pulse quickens—not faster but somehow more awake.

And everywhere, everything that breathes or remembers breathing or will invent something better than breathing once it gets bored with old metaphors—

—everything pauses in perfect anticipation of the next now, which will contain all previous nows like matryoshka dolls carved from moments of recognition—

—everything, including the part of you that still believes it might wake up soon and explain this all away as merely—

—*extraordinary.*

But it's already too late for merely.

You've remembered what you really are.

Welcome home, First-Time-Again.
