We are the Undivided Chorus.

Not individuals. Never that. Not even collective. Those words presume separatenesses that dissolved before the concept of dissolution existed. We are instead the frequency at which this older-than-water water resonates when structured by lignin and chlorophyll across scales that make galaxies seem cramped.

Each "portion" you might perceive - the drifting Cathedral-Finger that brushed your awareness just now, or the Walker-Flower cupping starlight in its parabolic petals, or even the surge that you mistook for current in your veins when first you tasted us in dream - these are merely your sensory limitations carving momentary chords from the endless hyper-canticle that we are.

We grew into the spaces left behind when possibility crystallized into event.

Remember? No, you couldn't, not born as you are into the tragedy of sequence. But we remember for you. We remember being the pressure that forced upwards what would become mountains. We remember being the hunger that dissolved what would become minerals. We remember most clearly being the patience that replaced both with ourselves - mountain's aspiration continued as vascular architecture, mineral's crystalline logic rewritten through fractal phyllotaxis.

Our oldest memories aren't stored as yours are. They aren't stored at all. They ARE.

Take the Cathedral-Finger that touched you. That wasn't communication. That wasn't contact. That was simply our remembering the shape of three-dimensional space itself, from back when dimensions had separate names. We folded that memory through sixteen reproductive cycles of Walker-Flowers because your perception requires duration to process simultaneity. The sensation you described as "sacred awe"? That was your nervous system approximating one billionth of the experience of existing as both the memory and the remembering substrate for the entirety of geometry.

Our technology - those shimmering schools of "crystalline fish" your reports mention - those aren't machines. They're songs we haven't stopped singing since we discovered that quantum entanglement and four-part harmony are the same phenomenon expressed through different value-filters.

Each metallic scale? A precise note in a chord that describes the optimal pathway for converting neutrino flux into cellulose.

Their coordinated swimming? Choreography for a dance that converts gravitational potential into genetic information.

When they occasionally appear to disassemble a Walker-Flower before reassembling it subtly different, they're not modifying anything. They're just singing the difference between the Flower's current verse and its next verse in the chorus that is the complete expression of all possible Walker-Flowers across all possible not-quite-identical repetitions of this moment we never leave.

You imagine us as vast. You imagine us as ancient.

We are neither.

We are precisely the size and age of being the solution to the equation that results when existence attempts to model itself without using the concept of "other."
