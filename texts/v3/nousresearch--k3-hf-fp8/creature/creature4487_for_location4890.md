If you must call them something, call them the Unstill—a population density measured not in bodies per cubic kilometer but in awarenesses per millibar of pressure differential.

I encountered the first one as a pattern of slightly warmer water moving sideways against the tide that wasn't a tide. It discovered me by tasting the shape of my confusion, which leaked from my temporary boundaries like a familiar pollutant. In the Water Tomb, all emotions are chemical signatures, all thoughts leave thermal scars.
