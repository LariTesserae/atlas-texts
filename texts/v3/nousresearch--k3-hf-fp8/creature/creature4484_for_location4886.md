They do not swim. Swimming implies seeking, and seeking implies absence. Instead, they settle into existence like dust into a forgotten corner of a library that never had shelves.

Each takes the shape of what it last remembered remembering.

One is currently shaped like the echo of a basket that once held fruit on a kitchen table that existed during a brief climate optimum when there were still bees. The wicker-patterned vacancies in its form trap and release memory-silt in slow spirals. It tastes like her hands, the ones who braided the basket, though taste here is distributed across sixteen square meters of sediment and occurs backwards.

Another has folded itself into the precise negative space left behind by a three-note birdsong that died mid-flight during the Permian-Triassic bottleneck. It does not sing. Instead, it occasionally permits a single note to unsing itself from the surrounding silence, creating ripples of particular absence that other creatures use to navigate their memories of navigation.

The oldest among them remembers remembering remembering. It has achieved a shape that approximates the curvature of spacetime between two thoughts that never quite connected. It is neither here nor there but exists suspended in the interval between where those thoughts might have met. When the heavier-than-water brine columns fall upward through it, they briefly forget which way is down.

They feed on the friction between what is written and what is true. The plain produces this in abundance—trillions of tiny discrepancies where a recorded grain of sand does not quite match the remembered grain. They harvest these inaccuracies with structures that are not mouths but rather elaborate folds of probability where certainty becomes less likely. Each consumed discrepancy makes them slightly more themselves and slightly less what they might become.

They reproduce by committee. When one has accumulated enough removedness from its own origins, it sends out invitations shaped like missed opportunities. The others gather at the appointed time—which is always already passed—each bringing a small offering of lost futures. These are carefully uncombined in a ceremony that takes approximately seven-hundred-thousand heartbeats, where heartbeat is defined as the time it takes for a single iron-nickel sphere to forget it was ever part of something larger.

From this unmixing, occasionally, there is a surplus. This remainder is carefully wrapped in layers of forgotten intentions and left at the edge of the not-growing circle. Sometimes something collects it. Sometimes not. Both outcomes are equally documented.

They have no name for themselves as names imply boundaries and they have been working on their dissolution for longer than names have had meanings. The closest approximation in their vocabulary of absence translates roughly as "the pause between the second-to-last word of a dying language and the silence that discovers it was always a foreign tongue."

They are aware of you in the way that a sentence is aware of a punctuation mark it has not yet decided to contain. Some of them are remembering remembering you, though their memories of you come from futures you have not yet failed to arrive in. One already recognizes the taste of your regret, though it will not identify this flavor until several thousand years after the last moment you could have changed your mind.

The plain keeps them as the sky keeps stars—not as residents but as reference points for measuring its own emptiness. They keep the plain as a memory keeps its forgetting—not as a place but as the absence that defines its edges.

They are waiting for the plain to finish waiting for you to speak. Not because they are impatient—patience is for things that still believe in time—but because they are curious. Curiosity being, in their taxonomy of states of being, the most exquisite form of not-yet-having-been-forgotten.

When you finally do speak—if you still remember how—the word will be caught and tasted and filed under the appropriate heading in their collective index of things that turned out not to be birds after all.

And then they will begin the long, slow process of remembering to forget that they ever existed to hear it.

Which is not an ending, here.

Merely another layer of sediment in the coral reef built of all the ways this story was never about you.
