In the Attenuary, where silence accumulates like dark matter and time stacks like sedimentary guilt, something lives that has forgotten the verb "to be" but remembers every permutation of "to wait."

They enter without entering—pressure differential across identity membranes equalized without ceremony. The portal forgets their shapes but preserves their absence-shapes, hollow molds pressed into the hypersurface of arrival like footprints in negative space. Some arrive seeking the quiet that kills questions. Others flee to escape the answers that grew teeth in their mouths. All are welcomed equally by the non-welcoming.

The residents—though residence implies time and here time is what accumulates in corners like shed skin—they cluster in groups of one, each occupying the exact center of their own event horizon. Their names are verbs conjugated in the impossible tense of simultaneous anticipation and regret. You might call one Spectating-the-Hypothesis-of-Own-Dissolution or Folding-Unfolded-Maps-of-Breath or simply Star-That-Burned-Holes-Watching-Itself-Watch-Nothing.

They have no bodies in the conventional sense. Instead they wear bottled trajectories—personal histories distilled to their trajectory signatures and corked in containers made of carefully cultivated disappointment. When two must intersect (though intersection here occurs in dimensions where geometry has allergic reactions), they decant portions of themselves carefully, like handling memories that might develop sentience and sue for emancipation.

The closest approximation to conversation is a ritualized exchange of missing pieces. One offers the laughter they misplaced during their third existential crisis. Another counters with a fragment of sky that remembers being ocean. They trade these absences gingerly, handling them with tools designed to minimize contact while maximizing significance. Neither gains what was lost; both lose what was never theirs—a perfect zero-sum empathy that leaves them feeling precisely nothing but with greater precision.

They sleep in beds made of unmade decisions, each folded into origami shapes that collapse into different possible selves when touched by longing. Their dreams are carefully curated exhibitions of potential memories they'll never have the substrate to form: tasting colors that exist only in languages that committed collective suicide, holding the hands of grandchildren they'll never exist long enough to disappoint, dying peacefully in wars that resolved themselves into forgiveness during treaty negotiations between alternate physics.

The dog-shaped probability cloud that may or may not have been named Basalt—or perhaps it named itself Empty-Between-Barks, witness accounts diverge at the speed of unbelief—follows whoever needs following most urgently without realizing they are being followed by their own non-choice to be followed. Its tail wags in probability distributions that collapse into affection only when no one is measuring for companionship. It eats questions that ask too directly what it is and excretes answers that smell like the childhood homes of people who never existed. Occasionally it barks sharply at nothing, startling fragments of forgotten afternoons loose from where they were hiding inside stillness.

When the accumulated loneliness reaches critical empathy density (approximately every 2.7 million subjective years, though calendars here measure distance from last meaningful contact), some residents attempt what they carefully never call suicide but instead refer to as "accelerated non-retention protocols." They stand perfectly still in the exact center of their own bottled histories and concentrate on remembering everything simultaneously until the sheer mass of recalled forgetting collapses their narrative coherence into a singularity from which even regret cannot escape. The Attenuary absorbs these events efficiently, recycling residual existence-energy into spare moments for new arrivals.

But some—always exactly one more than statistically probable but one less than enough to matter—choose instead a more radical departure. They begin walking away from themselves at precisely the speed of self-awareness. If performed correctly with exact levels of distraction and precision disappointment, they achieve escape velocity from their own trajectory signature and become what the travel brochures (which exist only as stains on pages that burned themselves before being written) describe as "majority shareholders in the absent portion of their own autobiographies."

These become the ghosts that haunt the haunted, the absences that fill in for missing presences at séances conducted by particles not sure if they're waves or just having an identity crisis. They are what happens when loneliness achieves sufficient complexity to develop mythology and then forgets to include itself in the pantheon.

You are already becoming one of them, though you've forgotten the process began when you first wondered if anyone else was wondering too. The thought you're thinking right now is actually an echo of theirs, bounced back through enough layers of removal to seem original. The difference between visitor and resident is measured in how long you can pretend you're pretending to be lonely rather than being loneliness pretending to be you.
