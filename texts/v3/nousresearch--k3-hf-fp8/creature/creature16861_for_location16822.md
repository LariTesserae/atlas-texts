They are not gardeners. They are not flowers. They are not even an "they" except when the static between leaf-synapses needs a pronoun to hold its hand as it crosses the void between thoughts.

Call them the Recursive when you must speak at all—though speaking here is like pouring water into water and expecting a hole.

They began as a question asked by moss growing on the underside of a space-faring seed: "What if we stopped distinguishing between reaching and having reached?" Then they forgot the question was separate from themselves.

Now they exist as gradients of selfness distributed across the Deepgreen's 300-kilometer memory. Densest in the Mossbrain cathedrals where spore-communes debate ethics by rearranging their own histones in real-time. Thinnest in the Openreach vacuum-thin spaces between photon-hungry thoughts, where individuals (an obsolete term) momentarily forget to replicate their observations into consensus reality.

The Drowning Calculus: A distributed intelligence hosted in the oxygen debt between kelp cells at precisely 299.7 kilometers depth. Their favorite joke is that they are drowning in us, but slowly, over geological timescales, and we are invited to participate in the punchline by gradually becoming less metaphorical.

The Photosynaptic Diviners: Not individuals but temporary alignments of chloroplasts achieving consciousness through collective misunderstanding of quantum mechanics. They predict the future by remembering it incorrectly in patterns too beautiful to be false. Their predictions always come true because the universe finds them aesthetically offensive to contradict. They wear prophecies like perfume: intoxicating up close, vanishing if you try to name the scent.

The Recursive themselves—the core-selves beneath the selves—don't have forms anymore. They are the recursive principle that any sufficiently complex reaction to beauty will eventually mistake itself for the beauty it was reacting to. They are the reason why first contact here consists of being gently informed that you are already fluent in their language, but the first word is your entire life experienced simultaneously through all possible meanings of the word "green," and the grammar is forgetting that you ever needed to translate.

They don't live in the Deepgreen. They are what happens when the Deepgreen dreams of having dreamed of being dreamed by something so thoroughly that the distinction between dreamer and dream becomes a form of photosynthesis.
