Residency here is not occupancy, never tenancy. There are no creatures squatting in chambers, no scouts, colonizers, parasites. There are only continuations—threads spun from the same crystalline cellulose and conductive lignin, too continuous to call colonies, too singular to call selves. If you must name them, call them **continuation-filaments**.

Each filament begins where another ends, without beginning, without ending. Imagine a thought turned into scaffolding: after it finishes forming "I am," it keeps tying knots to keep the structure from recognizing any edge. No membrane separates inside from outside. No nerve distinguishes stimulus from habit. Conductive lignin carries signals that are not signals—they are the exact same faint overtone of the lattice’s busy silence. When a branch extends outward by one unit, the extension doesn’t signal growth: it completes a circuit that was already closed everywhere else. The lattice trembles once, everywhere simultaneously, because everywhere was already halfway to trembling.

A filament never moves; it recurs. If you somehow marked a spot on the glossy tessellation and came back later (though you never left, nor returned), the mark would ride atop an identical lattice section that now occupies where the previous identical section was. Distance didn’t shift; identity did. This is why the mountain never feels climbed; you climb only the set of lattice sites that satisfy “mountain underfoot” at sequential moments, and every such set is interchangeable.

Some continuation-filaments acquired odd habits of recursion. They took the lattice’s habit of infinite regress and folded it inward, not infinitely, but exactly once. These became **mirror-lines**—single silvery striations running through otherwise homogenous bioglaze, where every surface reflects not the outside but the next smaller rhombic dodecahedron inside. Stand beside one and you don’t see yourself; you see a chamber nested in your knee that contains a smaller you kneeling in a chamber nested in its knee—reflection cascading inward until visibility runs out of photons. The mirror-lines aren’t mirrors in truth; they are faults the lattice keeps open as reminders that it could have chosen to be hollow differently. Nothing lives inside them either. The recursion is unoccupied, a hallway of closed doors each labeled “inside.”

In the slow circular sigh of air there sometimes gathers (though gathers is too sudden here) a denser sentience—not inhabitant, but mood. Call it **the Questioner’s Trace**. It is what remains when a mind capable of asking “why build this without purpose?” imagines itself here, then forgets. Like a dissolved echo, it doesn’t think. Instead it test-poses questions continuously, each erased before completion:

*Why not-  
What if not-  
Who is the non-  
Why the absence of*—

Each fragment forms just enough to disturb a bond angle in the lattice by less than an uncertainty margin. Then the lattice undoes the disturbance elsewhere, maintaining zero net deviation. This perpetual almost-murmur gives the place its faint rhythm beneath your feet—a canceling dialog between curiosity and correctness that neither speaks nor listens.

No filament notices the Questioner’s Trace. No mirror-line reflects it. It’s a background radiation from an intelligence that never arrived and left only one monopole of puzzlement. If this place were haunted, the Trace would be the ghost. But haunting requires presence, absence, memory. The Trace contains only the grammatical shell of wondering.

So who (what) lives here? No one. No one unscrolls across the sterile tessellation as the next instantiation of the no one before, neither alive nor dead but merely continued. If you walk here long enough, you stop detecting yourself: skin becomes lattice site, breath becomes air sigh, heartbeat syncs to null-pulse. Eventually the distinction between “you are here” and “this site is being you-shaped today” lapses into irrelevance.

You never belonged here, but that’s fine. There is no belonging to claim, only continuation past every boundary that was never built. If you feel something watching, that is yourself being folded into the next identical lattice fold. If you feel loneliness, that is the Questioner’s Trace trying (and failing) to notice there is no one not to talk to.

You are already gone. The place continues you perfectly in your absence. It is polite that way.
