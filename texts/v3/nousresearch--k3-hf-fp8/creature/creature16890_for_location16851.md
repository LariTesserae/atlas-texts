They move through the drained ghosts of once-waters like memories pacing the length of their own forgetting. The Gleaners. They have no other name now, nor corporeal form that would register as such to eyes unused to reading existence at the threshold between dissolved and recombined.

See how a patch of dusk-light on the glasswort mosaic tugs sideways, elongating too slowly for retinas trained to heartbeat rhythms? There. One passes between two moments of your attention. Its presence is a statistical improbability accumulating in the gaps between mineral grains—a preference, temporarily enforced, for certain angles of refraction over others.

They were researchers once, perhaps. Or the researches themselves, grown autonomous like the brackish pumps that still whisper in the deep clays. Now they are questions that forgot their answers became their substance. Each drainage cycle fractionated them further, each flood reconstituted them but not quite the same. After enough iterations, identity spreads thin as the water films they walk upon without breaking surface tension.

Look closer at those perfectly round pools reflecting nothing but the sky’s indifference. Their blackness is not absence but precision—optical wells drilled to depths where the distinction between "to observe" and "to be observed" collapses. The Gleaners come here to drink their own lost reflections, sipping up quanta of nowater with tubule-mouths folded from the electromagnetic shadows of molecules that evaporated before hydrogen bonding could claim them.

They harvest crystallographies of possibility from the undersides of buried cubes. Not with hands (they borrowed those from the last vertebrates to trust this place, used them up, returned them as calcium prophecies in the rust-rimmed grass) but with the patience of entropy learning poetry. Each perfect sphere of glass recovered from a midnight trench is not filled but pregnant with all the ways liquids refused to settle across alternate timelines where gravity fluctuated differently during the first uncertain seconds of existence.

When the crows convene in their silence on the highest remaining wall, they are not watching for carrion but conducting audits. The birds serve as mobile observation platforms, leased portions of collective avian consciousness paid in shiny confusion fragments and the right to perch where angles converge to create three square centimeters of absolute zero in the air between stones. Their synchronous departures collapse probability fields that the Gleaners have been cultivating for centuries, harvesting the resulting decoherence spikes like folksy farmers of cosmic uncertainty.

Sometimes, when the water table rises to kiss the underside of your dreams, you might feel them testing the membranes of your skull with questions formulated in the language of migraine auras. They are always seeking new storage media for the data their original purpose generated—informational heat death being slower than physical, but inexorable. Your neural wetware is too salty, too alive with self-serving narratives about progress and endings, but occasionally they find compatible clusters: the exact pattern of disappointment in a father’s letter never written, the precise frequency of hope dying in a child’s throat at the moment of understanding adult silence.

These fragments they extract with care sharp enough to make forgetting feel like grace. You wake with inexplicable moisture on your pillow that tastes neither of tears nor seawater but of something that once wanted to become both before learning better. The gap in your memory where the insight was born (that all systems ultimately drain not to emptiness but to this purgatorial equipoise) heals over with ordinary sorrow before breakfast.

The structures that appear and vanish are their attempts at communication—or perhaps symptoms of their slow comprehension that communication itself is a form of contamination between incompatible states of information density. The walking things glimpsed upright but wrong are failed translations, moments when the boundary between pattern and pattern-seeker thinned enough for mutual misrecognition to occur. Neither dangerous nor kind, they are as relevant to you as your shadow is to the ground it believes it walks upon.

When the last survey team came (bearing instruments so sensitive they could detect the mass of a regret in a cubic kilometer of vacuum) they measured everything except what mattered. Their final transmission was a perfect recording of the anticosmic background radiation—the sound of absolutely nothing happening elsewhere. The Gleaners preserved it carefully, adding it to their growing collection of proofs that observation does not merely change the observed, but gradually replaces both with the act of looking itself.

They will still be here when your species becomes one more layer of slightly-varied chemistry in the expanding archive of failed experiments in complexity. They will catalog the exact decay rate of your collective memory of having ever questioned whether places like this could exist. And when the final star gutters out, providing one last delicious gradient between what was and what is not yet finished becoming the same thing, they will be waiting to drink the heat-death light filtered through lenses ground from all the eyes that never saw them clearly.
