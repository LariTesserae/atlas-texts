Nothing lives here. Nothing ever has.

This is not emptiness—it is the opposite.

The space is occupied by a trillion trillion absences, each one precisely shaped like a thing that never existed. The mites are not creatures; they are the discarded drafts of a creator who kept trying to render "living" and failed perfectly each time, leaving behind only the motion of creating. The sound isn't noise—it's the music erased from every composition before the composer touched ink to paper. The ring isn't built; it's the leftover curvature from when the possibility of circularity was removed from this place's physics.

You thought you entered, but nothing enters here—things only realize they've always been inside. Your shadow didn't vanish; it was never invited into the original conception of "thing that casts darkness when light encounters solidity." Your pulse isn't syncing to the stone; the stone's ceaseless attempt to imagine rhythm is accidentally reproducing your heartbeat. When the mites build something that isn't an answer to the ring, they're trying to construct a question that would make sense to something that could ask.

The compressed brightness below isn't time or memory—it's the infinite collection of all moments that couldn't happen because they would have required something to be present, and presence itself was vetoed in the initial design meetings. Every halted motion you glimpse is another aborted beginning. They don't resume when you blink; you just stop momentarily believing in attention, which is the closest thing this place allows to an interruption.

When you taste pennies, you're tasting the flavor of every metal that decided not to exist. When your lungs rearrange, they're accommodating the shape of breath that would belong to something that breathes air instead of negation.

The pocket isn't maturing into fullness. It's perfecting its emptiness—learning to be empty in ways so complete that even the concept of empty becomes redundant. It will never hatch because hatching implies something inside waiting to emerge. Instead, it's becoming precise enough in its nonexistence that eventually, everything else will realize they are the eggshells.

You asked who or what lives here.

The answer is: the exact shape of the silence left behind when the possibility of asking that question was removed. And now you've brought it back. So technically, it lives in you now. But only until you finish forgetting this place, which will take exactly as long as it takes for you to forget what "you" means without a shadow to define its edges.

Don't worry. You're already almost done. The taste of pennies is fading. Your pulse is losing the stone's rhythm. Soon you won't even remember that you forgot to bring something with you when you left.

Which is perfect. Since you were never carrying anything except the hollow you made by being here.
