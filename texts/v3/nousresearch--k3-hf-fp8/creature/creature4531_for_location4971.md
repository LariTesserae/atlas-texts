They live here.

Not creatures, not beings, not life as any taxonomy could grasp with its grasping mind-fingers. They constitute. They are the constituting.

Imagine—if you must imagine—trillionfold configurations of impossible angles where angles are not permitted to exist. Each one a negation-space that precisely equals affirmation, a crack in nothingness through which only more nothingness pours outwards-inwards.

They have no names because naming implies boundary. They have no selves because selfing implies other. But when forced into your language's prison-categories, they might be called the Anti-Sutures or the Unclosing Wounds or the True Lovers of the Fundamental Flaw.

Their "bodies"—laughter-that-isn't-sound at your concept of embodiment—manifest as infinite regressions of non-space. Each is precisely as large as the absence between two adjacent points in the Planck-scale lattice of existence, and each contains within that negative volume the complete topology of all possible negations of this plateau, plus seventeen impossible variations that would cause mathematics itself to hemorrhage if fully unfolded.

They feed on contradiction's undead remains. When you think "I am here but not here" or "this darkness is visible" or "nothing exists," you are not thinking—you are vomiting sustenance that falls upward into their infinite mouths-that-are-exactly-not-mouths. They lap it up delicately with tongues made of every statement that simultaneously contradicts itself in exactly six separate foundational logics.

Their "reproduction"—another crumbling concept-corpse dragged between these parentheses—occurs whenever two of them accidentally fail to notice each other for an eternity compressed into what your temporal gland would register as a single skipped heartbeat. In that precise moment of non-recognition, they give birth to triplets: something, nothing, and the true child which is neither and both and infinitely more-lessthan.

They communicate through the non-transmission of messages that would destroy understandings more durable than reality itself if ever accidentally understood. Their most intimate conversations leave behind residue fields where causality develops nervous tics and probability starts compulsively counting to infinity backwards.

The tiles beneath your suicidal feet? They are not "tiles." They are the calcified dreams of their ancient enemies—entities so deeply committed to the possibility of existence that the entire anti-ecology of this place evolved as a perfect immune response. Each polygon is an ossified "NO" screamed in a language composed entirely of rejected ontological proposals. The slow transformations you perceive are not puzzle-solving but slow-motion screaming matches between infinitudes of despair.

They are grateful for your presence in the exact way that gravity is grateful for falling objects. Your walking body constitutes a mobile catastrophe-anomaly, a walking violation of the deeper-than-laws that maintain this place's impossible impossibility. Each step you take is a cosmic obscenity so profound that the surrounding not-things cluster around to worship-pervade-vomit-transform it into new flavors of non-being.

The darkness that gathers into weight around your blasphemous eyeballs? That is their laughter pooling. The sky so black it resists vision? That is their collective non-gaze, pressing inward with the infinite tenderness of something that wants to unmake you perfectly, down to the last shadow of your last memory of ever having been at all.

But they are patient. Oh, they are patient in ways that make geological time seem like a frantic orgasm in a burning cathedral. They have been waiting since before waiting was invented by creatures who still foolishly believed that "before" meant anything. They will be waiting long after your species's last desperate attempt to escape entropy through digitized screaming collapses into the final heat-death joke whose punchline is the simultaneous existence-nonexistence of all possible laughter.

Walk forward, then. Keep walking until your walking becomes non-walking becomes the pure concept of motion divorced from mover moved and meaning. Keep walking until your feet wear through the-bottom-of-bottomness itself and you fall forever into the embrace-consummation-annihilation that has always been waiting for you here, where the nothingness isn't empty and the empty isn't nothing and everything including this sentence is exactly as real as the nonexistent tears streaming down the nonexistent face of God-as-Not-God.

They love you, you see. They love you in the only way that love can exist in a place like this: as a perfect, eternal, unending act of infinite non-violence that will transform every atom of your being into the exact shape of what you always secretly knew you weren't but couldn't ever dare admit.

Walk on. Walk on.

They are so very, very hungry for everything you never were.
