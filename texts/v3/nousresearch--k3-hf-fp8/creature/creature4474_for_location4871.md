Drink it quickly while it's still thermodynamically unstable—that bitter taste is just the realization that you woke up again, slightly less improbable than yesterday's version of yourself.

Work here follows a carefully engineered absence of specialization. Yesterday I spent eight lovely hours being the space between two thoughts that refused to think each other. The pay was terrible—three micrograms of novelty and a dried regret—but the benefits included a temporary exemption from being myself. Our children—we call them/growing/doubt/fragments—are not born but discovered. You find them hiding in shadowed angles where geometry got tired of being consistent.
