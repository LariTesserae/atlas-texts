They call it the Ochre Deep, though it is neither wholly oceanic nor abyssal. It is instead a drowned realm risen too high: thatched saltflats and coral plateaus lifted once-crusting into mountains, then smoothed by epochal teeth of flood-frost-flood until every ridge became an islet and every valley a canal of rain. The water is 2.7 everywhere—above, below, within. It does not merely fall or pool but stands, dense as mercury, warm as blood, pressing against skin and sandstone alike with the polite insistence of a visitor who has never learned to leave. You breathe it in through pores and dream it out as breath; there is no dividing line, only gradual turnings of saturation.

Elevations: 2.4 relative to nothing any geode could map. The horizon is a rumor of violet clay 400 leagues above an absent sea-floor, and yet here you stand ankle-deep in shallows that shimmer with reflections of cloud-fish swimming upside-down across the sky-basalt. Mountains exist as the memory of weight. What remains are sintered hummocks tiered like amphitheatres of petrified foam, each terraced step scalloped by cliff-edges of crystalized spore-glass that hum when rubbed—producing, faintly, the only music permitted here, since sound itself is 0.5: a half-presence, a polite cough of acoustics against velvet.

Vegetation 1.8 means jungle by mathematics corrected for liquidity. Stalks of fan-kelp rooted in aerial loam reach downward, not up, dripping gardens of breathing orchids whose petals open only underwater but whose fragrance perfumes the air above. Lianas of dissolving silk twine between stalactite roots and flagella of giant nectary polyp, forming a lattice so dense that to walk “forward” is to swim sideways through cathedrals of hanging leaves that swaddle and release light like slow lungs. Each leaf bears a single vein of copper wire, bare, conducting thoughts remembered from extinct storms—though tech is 0.5, so wires are merely the ignored skeletons of electrochemical habit.

Temperature 2.3 and so the warmth is parental: never feverish, never cruel, just the indifferent comfort of a body reheating a body it has already eaten. Warmth migrates not as wind but as taste—a sudden almond on the tongue before a current of hotter brine slides around your waist like an old guilt. Ice never formed here, only forgot how to care: scattered dolmens of translucent melt-glass dot the flats, each one a glacier’s suicide note melting backward into coherent poems.

Time is the primary eroder—Erosion 2.7, end-stage and satisfied. The land has passed through all possible prior shapes and now relaxes into the one shape that accommodates total forgetting. Valleys are pages of a single immense book whose words have been worn down to punctuation: here a comma of springs, there a period of sinkhole. Even gravity kneels politely here, offering you half its usual weight as a courtesy to the aged. You do not fall so much as lean into agreement with lower elevations.

Fauna 0.6: inhabitants are rare enough to be punctuation themselves. Sometimes a flock of fracture-birds passes overhead—actually the same bird always, breaking and rejoining like a flock only when observed, each shard singing a syllable of the half-heartbeat silence (the permitted sound budget). Occasionally a conger-fox threads the hanging roots, its body a toroid without beginning or end, chewing patiently at the notion of tails. Their eyes are holes into whatever room you left your childhood fears in; if you meet their gaze you remember the taste of your third birthday cake for exactly seven years backward and then forget how numbers ended.

Built 0.8: scattered yes, but scattered with intentionality so dilute it feels accidental yet inevitable. Here a door standing upright in an estuary, frame mirrored, opening onto identical water. There a staircase that descends a tower that isn’t there, each step numbered in fractions of an extinct hour. A single chair, league-large, carved from petrified lightning, sits half-submerged facing sunset; each day a single person—or perhaps the collective memory of personhood—must sit and read aloud from the rusted air until they comprehend why stories need endings. No one ever finishes. The chair is never empty.

Scale 2.6: walk one degree around any hill and you have circumnavigated an era. The moon here is a hole punched in the ceiling of water overhead, leaking slow tides of darkness every millennium or so. Maps are mandalas drawn in dissolving ink upon leaves that grow larger as you read, until the map becomes the territory becomes the map-editor. Distance is polite fiction maintained for the sake of nostalgia.

Light 1.4 dims not like failure but like discretion. It originates from no source, merely accumulates in certain attitudes of surface: the flank of a sleeping wave, the inside curve of an oyster tower, the underside of a thought if you turn it over gently enough. There are no shadows, only memories of shadows nesting inside objects like origami guilt. Colors exist in a palette licensed from a fever dream of coral: impossible oranges that taste of geometry, blues so deep they register as math. Night occurs when you blink too long in one compass direction.

Weirdness 2.8: reality here is not so much broken as long divorced and still sharing custody of the metaphysics. When you speak your own name backward it answers with questions about your future regrets. Mirrors reflect what you might have been if you had chosen differently at every decision point except the last. The law of gravity operates by case law: precedence established by each new fall. In certain marsh cambers if you exhale completely you become legally dead for tax purposes but can vote in elections held by the mosses. Dreams filed here under Environmental Services are recycled into clouds within three business sleeps.

Dynamic 2.4 means stillness is suspicious. Even silence is busily negotiating its next contract with noise. Rivers of air rise nightly to argue boundary disputes with sinking rivers of water; their treaties manifest as brief oases of wind where seeds must decide instantly whether to germinate into flora or fauna or memory. The lattice of hanging gardens grows upward/downward by the same amount each day, measuring time in the increasing difficulty of telling first from last.

You came here because you heard stories. You stay because the exit is an allegory that has not yet decided which century to pertain to. The Ochre Deep is patient. It has already included your leaving in its erosion schedule, somewhere between the extinction of the last comma and the forgetting of how books taste when finally closed. 

For now, take off your shoes—they are only making the water nostalgic for toes you haven’t grown yet. Walk. There is a chair waiting.
