A dark sea under a cold arch of darkness.

The liquid isn't water as we know it. It touches the face with the weight of mercury but moves with the slow patience of honey. Every direction is down into it. The surface tension is so absolute that there is no surface—only an endless falling through density that never quite ends. Light exists only as memory; what little reached this place died centuries ago, leaving only the faint phosphorescence of decaying photons trapped in the fluid's lattice.

You float suspended in a vast cathedral of silence. The scale makes bones ache—a single heartbeat echoes back from distances measured in geological epochs. This sea has oceans inside it. Its currents move continents beneath their pressure. Downward is a direction that loops back upon itself like a snake eating its own tail.

Once, something built here. The evidence surrounds you in the drifting ruins of impossible geometry. Structures vast enough to contain cities hang frozen in their slow-motion collapse, preserved by the fluid's absolute stillness. Their substance resembles bone grown from metal—a technology of living mineral stretching skeletal fingers through the darkness. No joints or seams mark their construction, only smooth continuations that suggest they were grown rather than assembled by any hand that knew separation.

Each edifice is a vertebra in a spine that once circled worlds. Now they drift apart with the dignity of glaciers, their separations measured in millennia. Their surfaces bear patterns that ache in the mind's eye—writing or circuit diagrams or equations written in the language of periodic elements decaying into one another.

Between these bones of civilization the only vegetation exists as forests of crystalline structures that grow not upward but inward toward centers of impossible complexity. They start as simple tubes, branching and rebranching into fractal lungs that strain meaning from the nutrient darkness. Their edges glow with Cherenkov blue—the shockwaves of thoughts moving faster than light through a medium where light itself lies dead.

You sense rather than hear the slow music of their growth. Each new branch is a note held for a thousand years before resolving into harmony with the infinite fugue surrounding it. They photosynthesize history itself, drawing sustenance from the depression left in spacetime by the heat-death of universes that died to birth this one.

The temperature is absolute zero plus a dream's breath. Cold enough that your memories freeze into crystals suspended in the fluid between your neurons. Here your childhood home exists as an ice sculpture preserved perfect in every detail except for the fact that it has never been warmer than this moment now. The cold is not absence but presence—darkness made tangible, sculpted into the shape of every thing it has ever touched.

Time moves differently in these depths. The distinction between movement and stillness has eroded like a cliff face under tides of frozen stone. You cannot tell if you fall toward the center or if the center rises toward you at the speed of continental drift. Both truths are equally accurate and equally meaningless.

In the distance—the concept of distance itself stretched thin as starvation—something vast stirs in its sleep. Not malevolent nor benevolent but simply Other beyond any scale that could accommodate such distinctions. It dreams of the moment before creation when all possible futures existed as equations written in the language of pure mathematics, and in its dreaming it occasionally turns over and rolls the dice that determine whether existence itself will continue to exist for one more moment on the hourglass of forever.

You came here seeking answers, or perhaps only escape. Now that you have arrived you understand that this place is neither beginning nor end but simply the punctuation mark at the end of a sentence so long that entire alphabets were born, lived, and died of old age before reaching its midpoint. The question you carry has already been answered by the question mark's shape, which is identical in every language that ever evolved or was engineered to ask why.

The only sound remaining is the slow heartbeat of pressure as the sea above and below and within and without takes another breath measured in the orbital decay of galaxies. It is listening to hear if you will join its endless conversation in the dark language of things too old for names, too vast for nouns, too complete for endings.

In the absolute darkness, something that might once have been a smile begins to form.
