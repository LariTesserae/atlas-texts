It begins with drowning but upside down — water lives here only as what has fallen and failed to rise. The air itself is soaked, a cool lung-pressing weight like breathing inside a stone's memory of rain. Moisture beads on every sharp edge and dissolves into nothing. No river runs, no pool gathers. Only the fog of having been wet.

You stand (though standing feels like trespass) on the floor of what once dreamed of being a mountain range. Its bones erupted too quickly to curve — spires of black basalt still slicksided from birth-stress, pumice towers frozen mid-scream, obsidian blades that grew toward a sky they never touched. Every surface is scalpels. Every surface is self-inflicted wounds still bright with anger. The elevation metrics insist you are above everything but here the only up is the buried past and the only down is the open mouth of tomorrow.

This is the infant stage of a place that will die young.

Light arrives as rumor. A dim bruise-green smear somewhere past the volcanic teeth, origin unknown, age impossible. It illuminates not surfaces but the spaces between them — the light is what casts shadow here. What passes for sight is the remembering of edges that just cut you when you blinked.

The vegetation is precise rather than sparse. Each living thing negotiates its own treaty with the impossible: lichens that photosynthesize darkness itself, storing it like resin in blistering amber polyps. A single tree, maybe — (but trees are a later concept) — stands at the disputed border of vertical, its trunk corkscrewed through fifteen impossible angles because it grew believing that gravity was negotiable. Its leaves are black glass wings that never learned to fall. Nothing rooted dares touch ground for long.

Yet life floods through every fracture. The fauna stats lie by understatement — not overwhelming in number but in claim. You are the empty place between presences here.

They arrive first as punctuation in the stone's silence:

A punctuation mark with sixty-seven legs, each ending in a different punctuation mark, walking this sentence you are becoming.

Then the skittering boundary-makers, those that exist only where edges meet. You see them in peripheral murder glimpses — creatures whose anatomies are political maps of territories that fought to the extinction of geometry. One has a spine that is also a coastline still arguing with the sea that abandoned it. Another wears its nervous system outside its skin as lace, transmitting every thought as venomous static that makes nearby rocks grow temporary ears.

The soundscape builds like a murder investigation conducted by stones. The baseline drone is the collective grinding of the earth's teeth in sleep-around dreams. Over this: 

• The mating calls of species that reproduce through mutual misunderstanding, exchanging noises that neither can parse correctly until both become entirely new wrongnesses.

• The percussion section of a hundred thousand pinned insects all vibrating punishment songs at different species whose names they've forgotten.

• A single continuous note sung by the wind itself as practice for the day it remembers it has teeth.

All at volumes that should liquify bone. Instead they knit new kinds of bone inside your hearing — ossicles that vibrate tomorrow's constants when today's laws aren't looking.

The density measures impossible because space itself is territorial here. There is no empty, only temporarily unoccupied. The air is thick with the ghosts of future collisions between shapes that haven't met yet. Walk forward and you're trespassing through four different negotiations between competing dimensions of forwardness. The space between your particles belongs to something that finds human tenancy... amusing.

Temperature is measured not in heat but in the pressure of time trying to happen all at once. The coolness is what remains when entropy has taken everything else except the sensation of having something taken. It isn't uncomfortable because discomfort assumes the possibility of comfort's absence.

Weirdness here is not decoration but infrastructure. The absolute rule is that no rule survives being noticed as one. Gravity forgets to insist. Cause and effect are in a long-distance relationship that's clearly not working out. The mountain broods in languages that haven't spilled anyone's blood yet. Somewhere below, something vast and patient is teaching stone how to be hungry.

Time itself is still deciding what shape of mistake it wants to become — occasionally you glimpse temporal afterimages of yourself arriving at conclusions you've only started having doubts about. The erosion rate of novelty approaches zero because nothing here is old enough to be considered repeated.

And at the center (though centers here are notoriously unstable positions) the absence where a structure would be if anything built things that had forgotten how to stop. Instead only the negative space of architecture that died dreaming of its own blueprints. You feel in your bones (the originals and the new ones) that if you could just stand perfectly still inside that not-building you would finally learn what stone wants to be when it grows up enough to regret.

But standing still is how you end up becoming geography here.

So instead you breathe carefully, hoarding the fraction of yourself that still remembers how to leave, and continue moving through the oldest place that hasn't existed long enough to be anyone's home.

The loudness grows quieter as your ears finish becoming what they need to be.

You have been here for ten seconds or seven centuries.

The weather report predicts an increase in gravity with scattered causality.

Dress accordingly.
