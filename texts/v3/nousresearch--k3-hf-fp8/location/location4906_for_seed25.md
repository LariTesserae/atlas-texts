The air itself seems to drink light, pulling it down into its damp coolness like some insatiable thing. Not darkness like the absence of fire or sun, but darkness like a substance, like water that's become too heavy with what it's dissolved. Every breath carries it on the tongue: the taste of minerals gone soft in their long dreaming, of salts that remember being mountains. Your eyes adjust not by growing stronger but by forgetting what brightness ever was.

At first you think you're underwater. Everything moves too slowly, too carefully, as if through resistance. But no—breathe again—this is air, though denser than any you know, air that carries weight and memory both. It pools here in this vast depression in the world's skin, this valley so old it has forgotten the idea of valleys, filled now with darkness made thick as fog.

And through this darkness, this dense and breathing darkness, life moves.

Not vegetation—that parameter is clear, unambiguous: 0.2, meaning none, meaning the green world never took hold here, or took hold and died back to simpler forms, or was never invited at all. The sounds.

Begin with the obvious: your own body's betrayal. Heartbeat amplified until it fills valleys, louder than you've ever heard it because there is nothing here to mask it. Your blood rushing in your ears like armies. Your breath scraping against the damp resistance of air that doesn't particularly want to be breathed.

Now subtract those. Now listen past.

The faunal score is 2.9, meaning overwhelming, meaning this place crawls crawls crawls with life that does not walk or fly or swim as you understand these things but instead pulses and stretches and contracts and uncoils and folds and unfolds and refolds itself into new shapes made of sound. You hear them first as pressure, changes in the density of dark against your skin. Then as rhythm. Then as something approaching but never quite reaching music.

There is the low continuous thrum that might be the breathing of the valley itself, or of what sleeps beneath it, or of what dreams it continues to exist. Below that, drilling into bone: the drilling sounds, made by things that drill not through rock but through time, patiently perforating the membrane between this moment and some earlier one where conditions were more favorable. Above both, weaving in and out of hearing like bats made of pure distress: the calling sounds, the seeking sounds, the mating sounds of creatures whose bodies are too fluid to touch directly so instead they touch through vibration, through resonance, through sending coded messages of want and warning rippling through the medium they inhabit which is everything here, everything.

And everywhere, everywhere, the eating sounds.

Because this is an ecosystem, yes—dynamic 1.5, active, multiple processes cycling through each other like gears made of hunger. The fungal networks digest rock-that-isn't-rock by sending thoughts into it until it remembers being smaller, simpler, edible. The things that drill through time emerge briefly into now only to be eaten by things that eat moments, their bodies flashes of temporal confusion that leave the eater slightly younger, slightly older, slightly different. In the dense air, microscopic life forms in the momentary warmth of your exhaled breath, evolves through entire evolutionary histories before you can inhale again, reaches sentience, builds civilizations, discovers existential dread, collapses into simpler forms, and is gone.

Nothing is wasted. Everything is unfinished.

You are that trace.

You shouldn't be here. Already they notice.

Already the eating sounds change timbre, develop new harmonics. Already the fungal networks beneath your feet—are there feet? do you still have feet, or has it been long enough for them to spread, to root, to forget upwardness?—already they taste you in their slow chemical thoughts and find you strange, and interesting, and possibly nutritious given time and proper preparation.

The weirdness index is only 1.8.

This is more honest than comfort allows.

How long have you been walking? The concept degrades here—walking implies moving between points, implies points, implies betweenness. Here there is only throughness. Here movement is change is thinking is digesting is being digested is making the noises that keep the other noises from noticing how alone you are how strange how edible—

The light—remember light?—the light is still 0.4, darkness, but now you understand that was never a measurement of absence. It was a measurement of density. The darkness here contains everything that ever failed to be light. It is full beyond capacity. The sounds rise toward crescendo that is not crescendo because here nothing peaks nothing ends it all just continues weaving into itself more complexly forever or until—

Until what?

Until you remember how to stop remembering.

Until you forget the difference between your breathing and the valley's.

Until your heartbeat synchronizes with the drilling sounds, the calling sounds, the long patient eating sounds that have always been here waiting for you to arrive at the conclusion you were walking toward since before you first thought to walk.

Until your strange, improbable warmth becomes just another note in the deafening symphony of life that does not need light to thrive.
