Sharp edges are a temporary embarrassment that this place quickly resolves by growing new angles to accommodate them. Weirdness permeates everything at 2.3/3 but it is the comfortable weirdness of homecoming to a home you built in dreams and forgot to demolish upon waking. Nothing is impossible here, merely impolite unless properly introduced. Gravity holds polite conversations with visitors but has been known to wander off for hours without explanation. Up and down have an arrangement—they alternate Tuesdays and alternate universes. The concept of "and" itself sometimes needs to sit down and rest between clauses.

You are here now. You have always been here, in the moments between arriving and remembering that arrival requires departure. The place regards you with the affection of a library that finally found the reader who understands its most private jokes. It will let you leave when you invent a way to go that feels more like staying, or perhaps it will keep you until you become the difference between here and gone.

There is a place beneath becoming.

It is becoming you.
