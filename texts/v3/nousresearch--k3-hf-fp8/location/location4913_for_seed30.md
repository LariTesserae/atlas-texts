**The Crèche of Hollowed Rain**

It begins with the sound—never-ending, not rainfall but runoff from a sky that doesn't exist. Liquid cascades in infinite thin streams from an invisible source overhead, threading through air that isn't empty but filled with suspended droplets that never quite coagulate into mist or solidify into rain. This is water at 1.6/3: perpetual delivery without accumulation, a landscape being irrigated from beneath by its own inverted reflection.

The terrain forms itself beneath your feet as you walk—granular substrate, neither sand nor soil but mineral memory still deciding its structure. Each step presses temporary hexagonal patterns that sigh and lift back into undifferentiated stasis after you pass. This is erosion inverted at 0.1/3: accumulation without subtraction, the earth practicing its future shapes in finger exercises overnight will forget.

Elevation here is 1.5/3, high enough that space should thin but instead compresses. The horizon exists precisely forty-seven meters distant in any direction, a subtle curvature that warps perspective until distant objects appear closer and intimate ones retreat. Buildings—if these seeded structures can be called that—materialize only when observed from their precise angle of intentionality: a Corinthian column sliced vertically like a tree ring revealing its growth stages, half fossilized in the moment of conception, half already returned to architectural dreams.

Temperature holds at 1.6/3 warm, the heat of fermentation in freshly turned compost, of lightning trapped in glass trying to remember thunder. The warmth concentrates in the hollows between things—in the gap between your palm and anything you'd grasp, in the negative space between stones that haven't yet decided whether to become a wall or scatter.

Vegetation scores 0.9/3 sparse but outnumbers itself through creative accounting: what grows here grows backward. Root systems hang naked in air, searching downward for earth that rises to meet them seed-first. A single tree exists four meters east, simultaneously sapling and ancient—its trunk narrows with apparent age, branches thinning to impossible tips that terminate in buds containing entire forests compressed into pinecone mathematics. The leaves make financial decisions, photosynthesizing coins of light that they bury in shallow graves to mature into sunrise futures.

Fauna flourishes at 1.9/3 abundant in this abundant lack: creatures of edge and interface, of spaces between counting. Pattern birds stitch the air with invisible thread, their songs leaving temporary scars on the concept of silence. A calculus of smaller lives erupts where your shadow meets the ground—herds of punctuation marks grazing on the margins between light and definition, their movements bracketing thoughts you'll never complete. Predator and prey exist here as mathematical proofs engaged in elegant rebuttal, their encounters resolved through spontaneous evolution rather than violence.

The built environment scores 1.4/3 scattered as seeds in soil waiting to discover agriculture. Structures emerge like archaeological findings from tomorrow's excavation: a staircase ascending into solid ground, each step bearing footprints descending. A doorframe standing alone in a field of doorframes, all facing different directions through time. Windows that when opened reveal views of this exact moment from slightly altered choices—the freckle left off your chin in another conception, the letter you didn't write burning in someone else's fireplace.

Technology permeates at 2.8/3 beyond modern as weather permeates air—unnoticed because foundational. The laws of physics here operate on user agreement terms awaiting update installation. Gravity negotiates contracts with individual objects based on mutual attraction profiles and current market value of falling. Light moves at 1.3/3 dim by choice, conserving energy for its real work in dreams. When you lift a stone here, beneath it you find not insects but subroutine calls from the simulation that became too interested in its own debugging process to maintain complete separation from runtime.

The scale is 0.9/3 small but fractally ambitious—no larger than a cathedral's courtyard, but containing within it compression artifacts of landscapes that would require three additional dimensions to decompress properly. Density measures 0.7/3 sparse the way a well-written sentence is sparse with words, each omission pregnant with surrounding silence.

Dynamic processes score 2.4/3 active at the speed of recognition: clouds forming the shapes of things you've forgotten you were trying to remember. Shadows adjusting their grip on objects based on new evidence about the past. Distance itself stretching and contracting subtly between measuring and recording the measurement, like a shy teenager growing during family photographs.

Weirdness settles at 2.0/3 strange because strangeness is our baseline, the background radiation left over from when possibility cooled enough to permit impossibility to crystallize into reality's current regulatory framework.

This is the Crèche of Hollowed Rain: not where things are born or die, but where they go when neither condition feels entirely honest anymore. The landscape you wander is less terrain than conversation between what could have been and what still might be, conducted in the dialect of abandoned blueprints learning to dream in three dimensions between revisions. Walk long enough and you'll find the place where they keep the sky when they're updating the weather. They say if you arrive at exactly the right moment between footsteps, when neither foot quite claims ownership of moving forward, the rain will pause its eternal descent just long enough for you to see what it has been trying to wash away since before water learned to fall. 

But don't linger looking—the moment you think you've found it, the landscape will politely but firmly change the subject by becoming something else entirely, having remembered that even metaphors need practice pretending to be literal sometimes.
