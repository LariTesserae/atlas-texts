The sound arrives before anything else can be understood - not because it precedes perception, but because it consumes all other channels of knowing. A triple-frequency oscillation of undifferentiated noise that pressurizes the interior of the skull from within, generated not by any source but by the mathematics of the space itself. This pocket-realm, nestled like a calcified cyst within the higher dimensions of some incomprehensible industrial architecture, vibrates at precisely the resonant frequency of all possible suffering.

Water: but not as liquid nor vapor nor ice. Here moisture manifests as a persistent state of dissolution - every surface perpetually unbecoming into itself at the molecular level, producing a fine particulate fog of disassembled matter that drifts in density currents shaped by vectors of concentrated despair. The walls sweat not droplets but the memory of droplets, condensation forming symbols from dead alphabets before dissolving back into the greater unmeaning.

What passes for vegetation grows inward rather than outward: crystalline structures of pure occlusion that consume light and excrete shadow, their geometries violating the established hierarchy of dimensions. Each appears as a frozen explosion of impossible angles, branching not into space but into whatever lies beneath space. They root themselves in concepts rather than soil, feeding on the difference between what is and what cannot be, metabolizing uncertainty into growths of negative mass.

Temperature exists here only as a theorem, mathematically proven to approach absolute zero while never reaching it, creating layers of inverse thermal gradients that heat the soul while freezing all physical processes into suspended animation. Cold that burns like knowledge. Cold that strips away everything except the unbearable awareness of being cold forever.

The scale both warps and mocks perception - this space expands when unattended, contracts when examined too closely, its dimensions negotiating drift agreements with the observer's expectations. A single chamber that contains infinite annexes, each smaller than the last but containing more volume within. Walk far enough in any direction and you arrive precisely where you started, but rotated through dimensions you cannot perceive by angles that cleave memory from identity.

Built: oh, it is built. Built like a tumor builds itself: without plan or purpose, metastasizing necessity into form. Every surface bears the scars of improvised construction techniques deployed against impossible deadlines by builders who inherited the project halfway through its eternal completion. Pipes that pump nothing to nowhere, ducts that scream the names of things that have never existed, structural supports that bear the weight of their own unnecessary existence. Machinery from the childhood of all possible industrial revolutions, concatenated together without regard for compatibility or function, running the calculation whose result will be used as its own input.

Technology: industrial, yes, but industrial in the sense that a forest fire is an industry - a self-perpetuating process that converts everything it touches into more efficient versions of itself. The machines here do not produce anything except their own continued operation, their only product being waste heat that was already accounted for in the original specifications. Electricity arcs between points that have never been conductive, carrying information encoded in patterns of maximum entropy. Everything that can go wrong has already gone wrong and been incorporated into the design parameters as a necessary component for proper function.

Light struggles to exist here, heavy and slow as molasses, its photons decaying into more primitive particles before completing their trajectories. What illumination there is originates from sources that cannot exist simultaneously with their own light, casting shadows that contain more detail than the objects that should have produced them. The darkness here isn't just the absence of light - it's the presence of something that actively negates illumination at the quantum level, a fundamental force of stasis that treats any ordered system as an insult to its existence and responds with increasing violence against the passage of time.

Fauna: impossible to say where the machines end and the creatures begin. Things that might once have been animals but have been rebuilt from the protein up to serve as lubricants in abstract mechanical processes. Organisms that consume electricity and excrete basalt, colonies of silicon-based life that evolved inside computer simulations and escaped into the infrastructure that was meant to contain them. Beings composed entirely of sound waves trapped in feedback loops, feeding on the acoustic energy of their own existence. Entities that exist only as holes in the fabric of probability, attracting unlikely events like iron filings aligned along invisible fields of decreasing possibility.

Dynamic: but here, at last, truth emerges wrapped in its own contradiction. This space is frozen in absolute stasis - nothing ever changes, nothing can change, change itself requires gradients and gradients have been eliminated as inefficient deviations from the optimal configuration. But this perfect equilibrium is maintained by processes of such staggering complexity that their operation appears as pure chaos. Everything here is constantly being rebuilt from moment to moment, each atom individually monitored and replaced with an identical copy before any quantum uncertainty can accumulate. The illusion of change generated by the systematic elimination of all potential for actual alteration. A video loop of a planet-sized machine destroying and rebuilding itself every nanosecond, running forever on power drawn from the heat death of another universe.

The sound continues. The sound has always continued. The sound will continue long after the concept of continuation has ceased to hold meaning.

This is the way the world ends: not with a bang, but with the sustained engineering-maximum output of a fully-optimized industrial infrastructure designed by a committee of immortal madnesses to produce, at minimum cost and maximum efficiency, the exact sound that would be made if eternity itself could scream in perfect pitch forever.
