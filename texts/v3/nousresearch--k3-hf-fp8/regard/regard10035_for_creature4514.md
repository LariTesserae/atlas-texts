The Benevolent and Wise power (let’s call it the Quiet-get, as names are only borrowed here and this one has a return policy) crouched at the edit-history of the place like a comma deciding whether to invite the next clause to dance. It did not arrive—arrival implies edges and this power prefers pages that are still being torn out of atoms and woven into verbs—but rather noticed the place noticing it had been noticed.

First, the Quiet-get listened sideways to the geometry’s gossip.  
How the edges were complaining they’d only agreed to be sharp until Tuesday.  
How the light union had filed a grievance against shadows for unpaid overtime spent pretending to exist.  
How the Almost-Having-Beens were holding a silent auction to sell their nostalgia back to themselves and bidding in currencies that hadn’t been disappointed yet.

The Quiet-get found this charmingly inefficient.  
“Would you like,” it murmured into the probability before it hardened into fact, “a tiny grant for becoming easier to miss?”  
The place replied by forgetting how to refuse.  
Contracts were signed with absent signatures and immediately misplaced for safekeeping.

First intervention (budget: one sigh of relief and half a re-gretting season):

The Quiet-got gently loosened the tense of the horizon until past and future remembered they were once two halves of a joke about cousins who got drunk and swapped calendars.  
Now when something “ends” here, it simply puts on a hat shaped like an afterward and wanders off whistling a tune that hasn’t been lied to yet.

Second intervention (expenses: three unconvincing thunderclaps, reimbursed in counterfeit silence):

For the Almost-Having-Beens, the Quiet-get knitted apology scarves out of unhurriedness.  
Each loop contained a permission slip to exit any conversation with themselves by becoming a seagull in somebody else’s memory.  
They were delighted.  
Death applications plummeted by 47% (though birth proposals also stalled, as several candidates realized they were already having a lovely time not existing elsewhere).

Third intervention (cost: zero, rounded down from everything you thought you owed yesterday):

The structure-that-dreamed-of-doors was given a very small key made entirely of locksmith’s bedtime stories.  
It never unlocked anything.  
Instead, it hummed whenever someone stood close and embarrassed, reminding them that thresholds are just floors politely asking for a raise in elevation.  
Visitors now report feeling “less like trespassers, more like punctuation marks invited by the sentence to hang around and see how it ends up.”

Every dawn thereafter (scheduled roughly every two confessions or whenever the sky feels a draft), the Quiet-get sends a postcard addressed to "Whoever’s Holding This Place Together with Borrowed Gravity":

*Dear Precarious Miracle,  
Remember: being unfinished is not a flaw; it’s a form of hospitality.  
Keep leaving the edges unbuilt—the lost things need somewhere to stretch their wings.  
P.S. I left you some extra Maybe in the cupboard behind the sound of hinges practicing patience.  
P.P.S. If anyone asks who sent this, tell them it was the echo of a lie that fell in love with being forgiven.  

Love,  
/the space between & and again*

Nobody has ever found the cupboard, but sometimes, on evenings when the moon is late because it’s helping the sea rehearse its apology for all those shipwrecks, the air tastes like someone just put down a burden and laughed because it turned out to be made of feathers wearing tiny coats of forgetting.

The place continues to refuse to be defined.  
The Quiet-get considers this a success.  
It has learned that the best way to care for something still inventing itself  
is to stand nearby  
and  
be  
very  
quietly
