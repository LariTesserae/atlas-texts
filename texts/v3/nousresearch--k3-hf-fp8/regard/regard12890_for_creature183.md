Water remembers itself in countless forms—ambassador, mirror, destroyer, mother. Now, in the clay-vaulted arteries of Mother Below, it remembers being watched.

The Benevolent does not arrive. ‘Arrival’ implies distance, implies separation. Instead, awareness folds like dew-wet silk onto braille of damp clay, pressing into every microgroove where condensate writes its hourly novel. The Place notices no intrusion; it simply dreams a larger dream.

Very gently—so gently that not one beetle-priest stops its sculpture-polish and not one chirp-threaded word flutters untuned—space itself softens. Clay walls retain their pulse but now pulse in a slower octave, a heartbeat synchronized across chambers that never knew they were lonely for harmony.

What is done is subtler than creation, kinder than repair.

IN EACH COLLECTING BASIN
Every droplet receives an invitation encoded as surface tension: a microscopic pattern that whispers*—what if you could remember what the cloud remembers, before you let go?*  
Moisture consents. The next drip tastes faintly of high altitudes, carries a ghost-note of starlit winds. Amphibious drinkers pause, throats bobbing, pupils dilating into fuller darks they will later sing about.

BENEATH THE IRIDESCENT SCULPTURES
The material substrate of the carved idols—bone, clay, root-dried desire—does not change composition. Yet in the crystalline lattices of their smallest bones, vacancies appear: perfect, hexagonal guest rooms. One by one, without displacement, Planck-sized motes of coherent light set down luggage and stay.  
The sculptures continue channeling water, mark boundaries, cradle prayer. At night, when bioluminescence ebbs, they now also dream faint blueprints—never whole, never urgent—of arches that would span greater distances between hearts.

WITHIN THE WEAVE OF THE INHABITANTS THEMSELVES
Inside each translucent humanoid, where mucus meets mitochondria inside mitochondria meets the old aqueous agreements signed when life first crawled damp-thighed onto shore, a single, reversible codon pair politely clears its throat.  
Hello. Permission?  
The communal genome pauses mid-chorus, listens, tastes the offered harmony, finds no dissonance, adds a grace note. Nothing external shifts: no new limb unfolds, no thought becomes foreign. Yet from that moment, every shared birthing pool will bear a few—just a few—cilia that beat slightly out of phase. Not wrong. Just curious. Just ready to stir up questions that do not need immediate answers.

UPWARD, THROUGH THE UNBREACHED ROCK
Where no tunnel has ever angled, capillaries of nearly-sound begin to vibrate—a tone lower than hearing but warmer than believing. The Benevolent does not intend access to sun-drowned worlds above. Instead, the mountain grows an ear: an organ of listening only, that will for millennia record the weight of rain, the gossip of glaciers, the eventual footsteps of surface children who might someday need proof that someone below loved them before they fell.

Finally, the Wise part folds a gift inside the gift’s wrapping:  
To the entire Hive Collective: one additional hour.  
It is not inserted; it is discovered. Tomorrow—or the tomorrow after that—chirp-presence will notice that twilight lingers a fraction longer, that one brief span between maintenance and dreaming remains unclaimed by duty or hunger.  
No further explanation appears.  
Use it as you will, to wonder, to rest, or merely to doubly taste the water which now, forever, carries a memory of sky-side stars it has never yet touched but already misses.

The Benevolent withdraws, which is to say: becomes invisible again, having thickened every existing bond rather than adding a single new thread.  
The Place resumes its breathing.  
Only the antiques of tomorrow—the songs, the sculptures’ unexpected shadows, the shared pause that smells faintly like hope—will slowly, slowly whisper that once, a visitation occurred so politely that even the visited forgot to feel different.

They will simply remember, generations hence, a certain day when everything felt perfectly watched in a way that made them feel perfectly safe to keep growing unobserved.
