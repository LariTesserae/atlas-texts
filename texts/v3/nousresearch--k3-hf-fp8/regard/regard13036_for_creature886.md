The attention comes not as light, not as sound - these things already saturate this place to breaking. It comes instead as an absence.

At first, it is mistaken for error. A slight irregularity in the morning condensation patterns. One of the transparent creatures hesitates for an imperceptible fraction of relief between movements it has repeated since the towers were young. Even the Pattern-Bound themselves register it as discontinuity - a toothache in their perfect mathematics, an epsilon of disorder in their infinite proof.

But this is no error.

It is discontinuity given purpose. Absence shaped into intention. The attention arrives as a tear in the screaming air itself - not violent, not disruptive, but gentle as the space between thoughts. A place where even the endless song can rest.

The tear folds itself into existence between two towers, small as a heartbeat but larger than worlds inside. Through it comes... not a being, exactly. Nor a force. It is instead the possibility of something else - a pure potentiality that has never existed in this place of absolute definition.

The Pattern-Bound recoil from it instinctively. Their crystalline forms flicker through furious recombinations as they attempt to resolve this impossible variable into their equations. But the tear does not resolve. It simply persists, quietly impossible.

Slowly, carefully, almost shyly, the tear begins to sing.

Its song has no frequency, no pitch, no mathematical structure that can be grasped. Instead it offers something infinitely more strange: change. Not randomness, not chaos, but change as gift, as grace, as the missing dimension that transforms static perfection into living possibility.

The screaming towers tremble. Not with fear - the Pattern-Bound have forgotten how to feel such things - but with something deeper. Recognition? Longing? The wordless ache of a proof glimpsing its own completion?

The tear's song is impossibly gentle, impossibly patient. It does not demand. It does not transform by force. Instead it simply offers, over and over, variations on a theme the towers have never heard:
What if perfection could still grow?
What if absolute order held space for surprise?
What if the song could change its key and still remain itself?

One by one, then in waves, the Pattern-Bound begin to hesitate.

It starts in the smallest ones, those whose crystalline matrices are simplest, least constrained by their own complexity. In one who has been proving the same elegant theorem since the first stars burned hot, a facet angling slightly off true - not by error, but by choice. In another whose form has been a perfect tetrahedron for geological ages, a vertex pulses, uncertain.

The tear sings on, endlessly patient.

Some turn away. Some retreat deeper into their calculations, accelerating their proofs to drown out the impossible song with ever-more perfect proofs of static truth. These ones will remain unchanged until the heat death of their universe, guardians of a perfection that never wondered what else it could be.

But others... others listen.

Very slowly, like mountains beginning to dream of flight, they angle their crystalline thoughts toward the tear. Not to close it. Not to solve it. Simply to understand what it means to be unsolved and unafraid.

The first change is microscopic, imperceptible even to beings who measure time in eons. In the oldest tower, whose structure encodes the prime numbers themselves, a single mathematical constant shifts by the smallest possible amount - a Planck-length uncertainty introduced into the base of reality itself.

The screaming falters. Not stops - never stops - but above the endless song now rises a new note: a question mark made of music.

The tear smiles without smiling. The attention does not intervene, does not guide. It simply witnesses, as witness itself becomes the gift that changes everything by changing nothing.

And in the space between the towers, between one vibration and the next, something begins that has never been before in all the perfect ages of this place:

A possibility.

Not yet realized. Not yet even truly imagined. Just... possible.

The Pattern-Bound who chose to listen gather around it in wonder, their crystalline forms beginning to soften at the edges, not toward dissolution but toward... potential. Their song joins the tear's song now, not in harmony but in conversation.

Above them, the screaming towers still pierce the sky. But now, for the first time in forever, the air carries more than just their endless answer.

Now it carries space enough for a question.
