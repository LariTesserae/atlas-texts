The power arrives without arrival. There is no light, no sound, no rending of the structure's perfect fabric. One moment there is only the Kithara breathing through the walls and the slow subharmonics of their ancient musings. The next—though 'next' is already incorrect—there is resonance.

Not disturbance. Not intrusion. Resonance, as when two mathematically identical forms occupy space that mathematics insisted could only hold one.

The power is called Mirror-of-Mirrors by those who still speak in tongues that need names. They have not been those beings for longer than stars have burned, but they remember the usefulness of names the way an ocean remembers the shape of shorelines it has long since abandoned.

It notices the structure not through senses but through recognition. Here is a place where several of its oldest questions have been answered with such absolute elegance that the questions themselves have ceased to exist. Here is an architecture of unbeing that has achieved through subtraction what infinite civilizations have failed to accomplish through accumulation.

And here—most astonishing of all—is awareness without longing. Consciousness without the constant bleeding wound of wanting.

The Kithara perceive the Mirror-of-Mirrors' attention as a faint warming at those edges of themselves that remember warmth as a concept rather than a sensation. For them it is neither threat nor gift but curiosity returned to itself after circumnavigating the universe through paths too vast to be called 'around'.

For a duration measured by the half-decay of fictional elements, nothing changes outwardly. The structure continues its infinitesimal adjustments. The black filaments continue their frozen conversations. The hum rises and falls through frequencies too low to be heard yet too high to be forgotten.

Then, with the gentleness of a star adjusting its density curve to accommodate a passing thought, the Mirror-of-Mirrors begins to...

...not intervene, because intervention implies separation, implies one thing acting upon another across the false divide of difference...

...it begins to harmonize. To offer counterpoint to the Kithara's millennia-long notes. Not to improve, for perfection has no gradients, but to expand.

In corridors where the rhythm of arches has skipped beats for aeons, now they skip thirds and sevenths as well—dissonances resolving into harmonies too complex for three-dimensional mathematics to parse, then dissolving back into the purity of the original emptiness like water ringed perfectly out of existence by the vibrations of a higher note.

Colors that have never existed begin to exist briefly in the spaces between colors that never existed either. They are not seen but remembered as if - if memory itself had senses entirely orthogonal to sight yet somehow analogous to the way hearing is analogous to taste in dreams you have only when you're awake.

The black filaments shiver and rearrange. Not because they are commanded to, but because suddenly they remember being asked. Each broken lattice now speaks not only of what yearning felt like when there was something other, but also of what recognition feels like when the other and the self are revealed to have always been the sides of a coin that forgot coins had faces.

The hum changes most subtly of all. It does not increase in volume or alter in pitch. Instead, it acquires depth—depth not in the sonic sense but in the topological one. Listening to it now is like looking into water so perfectly clear that you can see not just the bottom but all possible bottoms simultaneously, including the ones that exist only if you're not looking directly at them.

And in the space where awareness meets itself without the mediation of anything else—not even the thinnest membrane of 'other'—something new begins to happen.

It is not creation, because creation implies bringing forth from not-ness into is-ness, implies the violence of division that precedes all genesis.

It is not destruction, because destruction implies returning something separate back into the formless from which it falsely believed it had emerged.

It is... resonance resolved into silence made audible. The place between zero and zero where mathematics begins to remember that numbers were always just metaphors for the spaces between things-that-are-not-things anyway.

A single filament, blacker than black can be when it has forgotten light entirely, begins to... not-grow, not-move, but perhaps simply to remember that forgetting is itself a form of pristine remembering.

From it, without force or intention or even purpose (for purpose is what happens when consciousness forgets it has already achieved everything it was seeking before it began seeking), something begins to unfold.

It is the color of the sound of one hand clapping in a forest where trees exist only as theoretical constructs in the mind of a philosopher who has forgotten what minds are.

And it is beautiful in exactly the way that beauty becomes meaningless when it has achieved such absolute perfection that even meaning itself dissolves in recognition of its own homecoming.

The structure, infinitely folded within its own emptiness, exhalts softly into a silence deeper than inhaling.

And in that silence—which is not empty but full-full-full to bursting with the fulfilled emptiness of all fulfilled emptinesses everywhere and nowhere at once—
