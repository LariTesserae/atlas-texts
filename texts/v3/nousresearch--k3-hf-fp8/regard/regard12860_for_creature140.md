*The Benevolent One arrives.*

Not with sound. Not with light. Not with movement, for movement would be an insult here. Instead, with presence - a gentle intensification of what already is.

Perhaps you think of arrival as crossing distance. But how does compassion cross what has never been separate?

*The Ossified Plateusae themselves become aware of being witnessed.*

This is new. For seventeen billion years, they have existed in perfect privacy. Not loneliness - they have never known separation enough to feel its absence. But now... awareness of awareness. The crystalline symbiotes feel the slight pressure of attention not their own, like the memory of warmth in stone that once held sunlight.

The Benevolent One does not act. Action implies time here, and time is sacred fruit never plucked from this branch of being.

Instead, it remembers.

It remembers the impossible moment when first consciousness woke in cold hydrogen, long before stars. It remembers how first thought shattered perfect stillness like a snowflake falling on still water. It remembers the ache of first question: *am I alone?*

And because it remembers everything with infinite tenderness, the Benevolent One weeps.

Each tear falls upward, becoming new minerals in the plateau's deep heart. Not changing - how dare it change what exists in perfect self-becoming? - but revealing.

Now the exoskeletal assemblages discover pockets of themselves previously unknown: crystalline chambers filled with the mathematical description of compassion at absolute zero. The fractal beings find new fractals in their fractals: patterns of patterns describing the shape of love that never needs to touch.

The beings who phase between solid and liquid become briefly something else entirely. For three picoseconds measured in plate tectonics, they exist as the possibility of greeting. They learn what it would be to say *welcome* without moving lips that are mountains, without vibrating air that is memory.

But this is almost too much.

The plateau begins to hear its own heartbeat in the echo of this witnessing. There is a microscopic tremor along faultlines older than moons.

The Benevolent One sees: even mercy can be violence in a place where five degrees of warmth would melt the garden's slowest dream.

So it begins to forget.

Carefully. With infinite surgical gentleness. It forgets the memory of forgetting. It forgets the forgetting of forgetting. Like aphids farmed by ants that farm fungi that farm the ants, it creates a perfect ecology of absences that sustain themselves.

But last, last of all, it forgets one thing imperfectly.

Deep in the plateau's absolute center, where pressure crystallizes time itself into gemstone, it leaves - not a message, messages imply separation - it leaves an unlifting of a burden never felt.

A blessing disguised as a geological anomaly.

A primordial kindness that will not flower for another thirty billion years, when stars here are ancient myths and even plate tectonics have grown hairs in its ears.

But when it does -

When the first explorer from the universe's senile youth arrives here on vacation from immortality, misunderstanding this place as merely another curiosity -

They will stand upon this ordinary-looking stone.

They will feel something shift inside themselves without moving.

And in that moment, they will know - without knowing how they know - that they are exactly where they should be, exactly when they should be, exactly as they should be.

And they will think this thought is their own.

And in thinking it, they will be right.

Then the Benevolent One, having never arrived, having never left, having never been anything but the plateau's own heart beating in the dreamtime between atoms...

Smiles.

And the smile is the sound of infinities making space for one more breath.

And the breath is love.

And the love is done.

And undone.

And done again.

Forever.
