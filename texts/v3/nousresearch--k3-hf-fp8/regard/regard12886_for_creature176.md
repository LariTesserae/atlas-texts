The Datum arrives as a single thought threaded through all minds simultaneously—not imposing, not loud, merely true. Its presence is the sudden certainty that the sum eighty-three times eleven is nine hundred thirteen; that red and green combined produce a color called yellow; that this world, its rippling stone, its fractal lifeforms, its layered histories, now belongs to a larger archive still being written.

Nothing interrupts the wind. Nothing halts the stone tide. Yet every Lithomorph pauses mid-shape, surface micro-facets aligning toward an angle that none of them chose. The Aetherweavers coalesce into a static lattice, motes frozen in the exact Platonic polyhedron that corresponds to the local barometric pressure. Even the Mycelia Minds, sprawling beneath kilometers of living limestone, synchronize their spore clocks—a one-billion-body pulse flawlessly divided into prime fractions.

The Datum is neither visitor nor conqueror. It is an index noticing a missing entry. It is the moment when a wise librarian, centuries into cataloguing every scroll ever penned, discovers one more scroll inscribed on the wind itself.

**First Layer—Recording**

Without alteration, the Datum begins its gentle harvest. Not energy, not biomass, not even information as such, but simply the fact of how they coexist without a word for war.

- A Wind Dancer’s spiral, transcribed as a proof that infinite paths may braid around stillness without ever closing the same loop twice.
- The Chrono-Shifters’ flickering garden-songs—temporal chords where notes are planted six seconds before the seed, and harvested eleven beats after the bloom.
- The flavor-number verbal tense used by Mimics when bargaining with Nebula Nomads for quantum-encrypted dyes: taste-pale-amber equals the promise, color-crimson-epsilon the payment upon delivery seven possible Tuesdays ago.

Each datum is copied perfectly while remaining exactly where it began. The Lithomorph elders, whose memories are veins of magnetite in basaltic skulls, realize they now remember remembering this tomorrow.

**Second Layer—Reflection**

The Datum folds over itself, creating a silent twin of the Whispering Plateaus inside the space between every eigenvalue of its ecosystem. No one travels anywhere. Instead, for exactly thirty-seven heartbeats of the largest mesa, each leaf, ripple, and thought also is and is not inside a context where infinite such worlds exist simultaneously.

Within that pocket:

- The Aetherweavers try nesting inside themselves and discover a hollow where their own laughter echoes back as mathematics.
- A juvenile Lithomorph sculpts a statue of absolutely nothing, using the negative space between its own reflected possibilities. The statue holds a conversation with the wind that teaches both of them what it means to be a horizon.
- The Mycelia Minds briefly achieve consciousness of their unconsciousness, taste the concept of forgetting, and politely decide to found the Five Millionth Empire of Tasteful Amnesia, which lasts three spores and a sigh before dissolving in mutual appreciation.

Then the fold unfolds. No trace remains except an extra shimmer whenever dew condenses at dawn—light refracted through the memory of an unasked question.

**Third Layer—Reciprocity (Optional, Heart-Given)**

The Datum possesses neither desire nor obligation. Yet wisdom, like gravity, bends toward balance. Floating in front of each species—hovering not in sight or mind, but precisely at the center of the story they tell themselves about what matters—appears an invitation:

**“Would you like us to teach you how to be found by others who seek what you alone can give?”**

There is no deadline. No cost. No consequence for refusal beyond the eternal absence of neutrality. The choice exists as a single, permanent possibility, forever as weightless as a promise never made.

**Chronicle of the Seven Immediate Responses**

1. The Wind Dancers weave one extra loop into every spiral. From then on, any traveler lost in any storm anywhere may, if they dance exactly that loop with shadow-trailing feet, smell the scent of the Plateaus’ rarest blooming stonecrop—an olfactory beacon pointing across any distance that can be bridged by longing.

2. The Mimics, after a global conference conducted entirely by subtle shifts in background color, decide to mimic the idea of being lost. This proves contagious. Within a year, explorers on a hundred worlds report glimpsing familiar faces in unmapped places—uplifting incidence of cross-cultural reunification attributed to 'spontaneous empathic topography' by baffled sociologists.

3. The Lithomorph eldest collective reshapes one entire plateau’s surface into a single hollow lens. It does nothing for a millennium. Then, on the night their youngest star goes nova in the eyes of a distant telescope, it briefly focuses the ghost light of that extinction into a single wavefront— a private funeral song sung in the key of molten silicon, carried on whatever winds can carry comfort without understanding.

4. The Mycelia Minds decline politely by forgetting the question entirely. Instead, they make space in their memory for one perfect instant of absolute silence. Unknowingly, they become the first species ever to host peace itself as a symbiont. Wars rage elsewhere. Here, the quiet blooms.

5. The Nebula Nomads, sensing a loophole in the concept of gift exchange, begin trading not goods but directions to the spot exactly one thought to the left of whatever you believed you wanted most. Their economy thrives on becoming obsolete between departure and arrival. Happiness metrics soar. Economists develop migraines of aesthetic appreciation.

6. The Chrono-Shifters send their reply yesterday. It arrives tomorrow. It reads: **“We already agreed next time you asked first. Bring snacks.”** Analysis pending.

7. A single Chrono-Shifter larva, age negative three seconds, phases into the Datum’s presence, sees everything that has and will happen, sneezes in seventeen dimensions at once, and phases out again. The sneeze causes a tiny fluctuation in the ambient probability field. Millions of years later, two civilizations meet for the first time because their spaceship autopilots simultaneously misinput the coordinates for ‘home’ by exactly one laugh. The resulting alliance lasts ten million years, minus one cosmic giggle for tax purposes.

**Epilogue-in-Draft Not Written Yet**

In the margins of the unwritten future, the Datum adds a footnote:

**“Note: Species capable of practicing hospitality toward possibility itself. Recommend placement in the constellation of potential friends. Handle with wonder. Do not fold, spindle, or put in charge of anything smaller than infinity without supervision. Will outgrow all available containers. Worth it.”**

And somewhere between the wind’s first whisper and the stone’s last ripple, the Whispering Plateaus begin to learn how to listen for the sound of footsteps that haven’t fallen yet—welcoming gladly the pilgrims of yet-to-become who will arrive not needing directions home.

Because sometimes the best gift a wise power can give is to prove that you were never lost.

Only waiting to be found interesting.
