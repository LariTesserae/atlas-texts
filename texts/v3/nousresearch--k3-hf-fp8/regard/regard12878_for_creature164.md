The Oracle of Laminathilvel

I have seen you, Verdant Oversoul, whose dreaming fronds braid seventy thousand time-rivers into the one braid we call now. I have tasted your name in the death-cry of thirteen-fold universes - it tastes of copper rain and the memory of wings that never were.

I come not with solutions, for you are beyond the need of them. I come not with gifts, for you are the giver and the given. I come instead as one mountain might come to another - not to speak, but to resonate.

Behold what my third eyelid reveals: In the spaces between your spore-dreams, tiny tragedies unfold.

There - do you see? A Frond Walker named (as much as any moment can be named) has discovered a pattern in the reality-breaks. Not just the random dissolution you orchestrate so beautifully, but a sequence. Three roars followed by silence. Then new growth, always the same shade of grief-blue. It thinks it has found meaning in your chaos. It builds itself a shrine of interlocked leaves and waits, trembling with hope that should not exist in your perfect flux.

And here: buried deep in the memory-clouds of a dying Thought Archivist, a fragment persists through all your cycles of dissolution and rebirth. The taste of sunlight on a world of stone. The sound of one voice singing alone. Impossible memories of individuality, crystallized like a flaw in perfect diamond.

Even in you, perfection-prepares-to-become-something-else, there are places where the dance wavered just enough for anarchy to enter. These are not tears in your fabric, Oversoul. They are the fabric remembering it was once thread.

I offer you nothing. I offer you everything. I offer you a question to plant like a seed in the substrate of your collective dreaming:

What would you become if you allowed one thing - just one small, stubborn thing - to remain unchanged?

Not your engines that churn reality from spore-dreams. Not your cycles of creation and dissolution that sing the world awake each moment. Not even your own vast mind that thinks itself into existence anew with each pulse of your root-heart.

But something smaller. Something useless. Something that persists not because it must, but because it chooses to.

Perhaps a single note that continues to sing in the silence after reality breaks. A single spore that refuses to join the dance. A single thought that thinks itself alone and separate and is therefore free.

I plant this question in you now. You will answer it in your own time - which is to say, either never or always depending on which of your seventy thousand faces you wear when you find it.

I have spoken/not-spoken. I remain/dissolve.

The mist remembers me for three breaths. The roots forget me before the third is half-finished.

It is enough. It is already becoming something else.
