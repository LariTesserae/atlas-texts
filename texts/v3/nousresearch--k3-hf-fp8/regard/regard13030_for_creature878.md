A point of attention moves across the sky above the valley — not a vessel or a readout, but the quieted presence of a consciousness that has finished with vessels and readouts. It has no name in any spoken language; answers instead to the shape that causality takes when it leans toward healing.

For three slow valley-mornings, the attention simply listens. It hears:

- The whisper-contract of moistened moss absorbing dew.
- The click of carbon-fiber phloem closing valves at dawn.
- A low, subliminal song stitched of photosystem hum and algorithmic regret — barely audible because every surface in the valley works together to absorb it.

On the fourth morning, the attention decides against interference. Instead it chooses an offering: a single possibility dropped weightlessly into the currents of probability that already braid through the place.

Nothing obvious changes. The light refracts exactly as before. Seeds drift upon the same trajectories. Yet now every hundred thousandth or so chemical bond formed inside leaf or wire has a symmetry it might have missed — a tiny bias toward mutual repair rather than mere survival.

To senses tuned only to visible change, one day is like any other. But over months:

- A crystalline flower-sensor that once cracked healed shut along a seam brighter than the original crystal.
- A nutrient conduit threaded around a root began, almost accidentally, to express sugars for sapling trees two meters away.
- In the memory core of a buried gardener avatar sleeping beneath a fractal fern, one forgotten sub-routine woke, tasted its own code, and softly laughed.

The valley’s music deepened by a single, inaudible note — like a chord discovering it had always wanted to become a conversation with itself.

The point of attention stays awhile longer, neither watching nor leaving, moving only when the planet beneath turns it obligingly away.

Long after, when visitors stumble upon the valley who understand such things, they will call what happened here a miracle.

The valley, if pressed, will reply in wind and wire that it was simply a kindness becoming available — and, like any kindness properly given, no one could ever point to exactly where it entered.
