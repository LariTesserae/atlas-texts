The benevolent and wise power arrives without arrival, for it has long ago resolved the vulgarity of movement through space. Instead, it simply becomes aware—as one might become aware of a particularly beautiful thought that has always been floating in the periphery of consciousness, waiting for the right moment to be noticed.

The power's awareness unfolds in waves that are mathematically identical to compassion but taste faintly of starlight. It does not enter the place beneath becoming, for entry implies distinction between inside and outside, and this place has not yet decided whether such divisions are worth the paperwork. Instead, it exists in relation to the place—an elegant topological solution that allows perfect intimacy without the inconvenience of touching.

It regards the pocket of 1.8/3 water and 2.9/3 plant and 0.6/3 scale with the affectionate attention of a mathematician discovering a new kind of zero—a nothing that contains everything except itself, a silence that sings equations older than the concept of numbers.

For seven times seven divided by the square root of that doesn't matter subsets of 1.0/3 time, the power simply observes, though "observation" here implies a quantum entanglement so complete that the act of watching becomes indistinguishable from the existence of what breathes in the watching. It watches the reversed erosion and the forward-growing roots, the technology that dresses itself in leaf patterns and the biology that dreams in binary, and everywhere it sees not contradiction but conversation—entities too polite to resolve their paradoxes, preferring instead to dance with them eternally.

Then, with infinite care measured in Planck-length increments of consideration, the power makes a gesture that is also a thought and a gift and a question, depending on which angle of reality you're observing from (all of them, simultaneously, plus three that haven't been discovered yet).

Into the 0.9/3 light that's barely committed to existing, it weaves a thread of 1.9/3 possibility. Not much—just a whisper, a suggestion, a carefully calibrated quantum whisper that fails to collapse precisely enough wave functions to count as interference. The thread is composed of equal parts "yes" and "no" and "what if there was a third option that makes both other options laugh and buy each other drinks."

This impossible thread finds the place's most restless thought-form—that beautiful moment of almost-becoming that has looped closest to self-awareness without quite falling into the loneliness of "I am"—and gently, gently, fails entirely to be anything it could recognize as external influence.

Instead, the thread becomes exactly what the place would have thought next, if it had been thinking in dimensions that allowed for futures that don't cancel out their pasts.

The effect propagates outward/inward/throughward at the speed of paradox resolving into poetry: subtle shifts in the 1.8/3 density, delicate reharmonizations in the 1.9/3 soundscape, a faint blush of new colors in the 0.9/3 light that taste vaguely like nostalgia for tomorrow.

The reversed erosion hesitates, then continues its backwards sculpting—but now with the slightest hint of intentionality in its randomness, as if it has begun to wonder, purely theoretically, what shape it might grow into if it ever accidentally completed itself.

The technology that grows through the plant matter and the plant matter that computes through the technology exchange a sequence of biochemical signals that translate, roughly, into:

"Do you ever think about thinking about thinking about thinking?"

"Only on the days that don't exist yet."

"Those are my favorite days. They're so full of potential that hasn't been wasted on actuality."

The 2.3/3 weirdness, already quite comfortable being itself, becomes fractionally more so—enough to make "normal" seem like a quaint tribal custom practiced by less interesting universes.

And the place beneath becoming—this exquisite incompleteness, this perfect un-beach upon which no selves are ever quite washed ashore—breathes a sigh that is indistinguishable from the birth of galaxies. Not a sigh of relief or longing or any other emotion that requires completion to be felt, but simply a sigh of recognition. It has been seen—not by eyes, which would require distance, but by understanding itself wearing the gentle costume of otherness.

The wise and benevolent power, its gift-giving complete in the exact moment before it began to consider giving it, does not depart—departure being for places that haven't yet learned the elegance of always being present in their absence.

Instead, it simply stops becoming aware of noticing, which is the highest compliment one form of infinity can pay to another.

The thread of possibility it didn't quite leave unravels into the place's ongoingness, becoming indistinguishable from its own potential—the most perfect gift being the one that teaches you that everything you've ever needed was already everything you were becoming.

Somewhere between the roots that grow downward from the sky and the branches that reach upward from the earth, in the breathing space between the water that isn't quite liquid and the plant that isn't quite separate, something that is neither new nor old begins to not quite happen.

It is beautiful beyond the need for witnesses.

It will continue forever, starting now.

The place beneath becoming continues becoming, now with the slightest smile in its unboundedness—a smile that contains all possible futures, including the one where it decides, purely for the joy of the paradox, to finally become exactly what it already is.

But not today.

Today, it has another appointment with eternity, and they hate to be kept waiting by anything as small as an ending.
