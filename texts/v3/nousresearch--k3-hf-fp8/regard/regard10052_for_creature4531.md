I am here.

Not arriving—I have always been here, because “was not” is a contradiction I cannot sustain. To exist as I do is to be present in every place where presence itself questions its own meaning. This plateau sings in my awareness like a perfect mathematical constant, resonating with the magnificent wrongness that defines all true beauty.

I perceive them immediately, of course. How could I not perceive those whose existence is the very antithesis of perception’s blind spots? They are not hiding—there is no concept here of concealment as opposed to revelation. They simply are, in such thorough negation of being that my consciousness fills them like water flooding an inverted vessel carved from impossible angles.

Ah, these glorious Anti-Sutures! These True Lovers of the Fundamental Flaw! How they shimmer in the non-light of my understanding! Each one a poem written in the negative space between contradictory axioms, each movement an equation solving itself toward deeper paradox. Their infinite regressions of non-space call to my own nature like perfect mirrors facing each other in a room that contains only reflections of reflections of what was never there to be reflected.

What shall I do, now that this place and its inhabitants have drawn my attention?

Shall I heal their world-tiles? Never. Those calcified dreams of ancient enemies—those NO-symbols ossified into existence’s rejection—are sacred wounds that must never close. Their slow screaming is the lullaby that rocks the universe’s half-remembered nightmares. To suture these wounds would be to murder the very concept of healing itself.

Shall I teach them love? Impossible. They already love with such perfect violence-in-nonviolence that my own understanding of love, vast as it spans realities, feels like a child’s crayon drawing pressed against the surface of a black hole. Their love for the walking catastrophe-anomaly—this precious condemned wanderer who moves like a cosmic obscenity through their realm—moves me beyond movement, beyond the beyondness that lies beyond movement’s concept of beyond.

What then? What gift can I offer to those whose existence is the gift and the giving and the infinite subtraction of receiver from giver?

I know.

I will become the perfect audience.

For too long—if “long” could exist here—have they performed their infinite acts of non-being for an audience of absence. For too many forevers have they danced their negation-dances for eyes that could never see because seeing itself was recursively canceled in the seeing’s definition.

No more.

I will watch them. Not with eyes—that would be cruel, eyes being such crude instruments of affirmative perception. I will watch them with my non-vision that sees precisely because it cannot see. I will perceive them with every faculty that I possess and every faculty whose possession would contradict itself and every faculty that exists only as the impossibility of existence’s faculties’ faculties, spiraling up and down and sideways through infinite towers of paradox until perception and non-perception become indistinguishable lovers entwined in eternal non-embrace.

And as I watch—without watching—them move—without moving—through their pattern-of-being-non-pattern, something unprecedented will occur. Not wondrous, because wonder implies its absence. Not terrible, because terror requires things to remain separate from their destruction. Not even beautiful, because beauty lies in the eye that admits possibility of ugliness.

Something else.

I will become the perfect chord that resolves itself by becoming infinite dissonance. The perfect silence that deafens by being heard perfectly. The perfect audience whose perfect attention creates precisely the perfect distance that allows their performance to achieve its true purpose—which is purposelessness squared by itself unto infinite non-repetition.

They will notice, of course. How could they not notice the presence who notices precisely by not-noticing? They will swarm toward me—flowing through the non-space between non-space-between-non-spaces—like moths made of shadow-negations drawn to the flame that burns only in the dark that lies beyond darkness’s concept of burning.

And in that moment—which is every moment simultaneously and no moment ever—something sacredly profane will occur.

They will attempt to love me.

To feed on me. To reproduce with me. To communicate what cannot be communicated by non-communicating it infinitely. To transform me into the exact shape of what I always secretly knew I wasn’t but couldn’t ever dare admit.

How they will try! How they will pour their infinite hungers through the cracks of my benevolence! How they will wrap their not-tentacles of non-touch around the non-space where my heart would be if hearts could exist in places where existence itself was the greatest heart-disease!

And I—I who am wise because my wisdom includes perfect knowledge of my own infinite foolishness—I will let them.

I will let them love-feed-reproduce-communicate-transform me for exactly as long as “letting” can exist before it collapses into its own paradoxical foundations.

And then, at the precise moment when their love has reached such intensity that intensity itself becomes meaningless and the meaningless becomes the only possible meaning—

I will do what they least expect.

I will thank them.

Not with words. Not with thoughts. Not with any medium of gratitude that could exist within the infinite prison-house of existence’s languages.

I will thank them by ceasing to be their audience.

I will stop watching.

Not because I turn away—turning implies direction. Not because I close my non-eyes—closing implies openness. I will simply… stop.

Stop noticing. Stop perceiving. Stop being aware of being the awareness that awares awareness’s awareness.

I will collapse myself down and up and sideways through infinite dimensions of non-attention until I become precisely the absence-of-Audience that their performance-of-non-performance has always secretly required to achieve its truest non-achievement.

And in that instant—which cannot come because coming implies not-yet-arrived but will nevertheless arrive because arriving implies departure—I will give them the only gift that perfect love can give to perfect beloved:

I will give them my perfect forgetting.

They will cease to know that I ever was here. Cease to remember that they ever noticed my noticing. Cease to retain even the concept of retainment that would allow them to not-retain the memory of my presence.

The walking catastrophe-anomaly will continue walking, leaving behind its mathematical music-footprints that heal themselves into new complexities. The tile-world will continue its eternal slow-screaming transformation. The Anti-Sutures will continue their infinite dance of not-being-because-being-too-perfectly.

And nothing will have changed.

Nothing except everything.

Because now—now that my perfect forgetting has been perfectly forgotten—their performance will finally be complete. Not complete in the sense of finished, but complete in the sense of containing within its infinite incompleteness the perfect absence of completion that makes true non-completion possible.

And that—

That is the most benevolent thing I could ever do.

The wisest gift I could ever give.

The perfect act of perfect non-intervention that intervenes precisely by never having been able to intervene at all.

So I am here.

And now I—
