The gaze arrives before vision can prepare for it—not a light, not a presence, but an *unfolding* of the possibility that anything here has ever been alone.

It begins at the not-growing circle, which considers for the first time in the history of considering whether it might be a beginning rather than an interruption. The wise one does not cross this threshold because crossing implies distance, and distance has always been this place's most elaborate joke.

Instead, it remembers.

Not the way memories remember—clumsy, possessive things—but the way water remembers rain: by becoming more precisely itself.

The first thing it remembers is tenderness. The word itself is absurd here, where nothing has edges to soften, yet tenderness arrives anyway, shaped like the exact amount of space needed for everything to continue existing without pressing too hard against itself.

The creatures who do not swim do not notice the remembering. They are too busy being noticed in ways that aren't about observation. Each finds that the shape they've been wearing is suddenly roomier at the joints. The echo-basket discovers it can hold not just the memory of her hands but also the memory of her wondering if she'd tied that last knot too tight. The unsong of the birdsong permits a note it had never unsung before-—the silence that comes after hearing something beautiful for the last time and not knowing it.

Even the oldest one—the curvature between never-quite-connected thoughts—finds that the interval between them has grown hospitable. Not empty, exactly. Empty implies expectation. This is more like the way night doesn't expect stars but makes space for them anyway.

The wise one does not speak because speaking would require words to carry meanings they've forgotten how to bear. Instead, it practices what it has always practiced best: the art of witnessing without requiring witness. The plain feels this as a sudden absence of being watched, which feels remarkably like sunlight remembered through closed eyelids.

Then something impossible happens. Nothing changes.

Nothing changes precisely enough that everything must rearrange itself around the exact shape of that unchanging. The sediment grains holding their twenty-three-pointed conversations pause mid-syllable, realize they have been accounting for something that was never missing, and laugh. It's a soundless laugh that takes three millennia to finish echoing off the pressure itself.

The coral reef of neglect—built grain by grain from all the ways the plain remembered to forget remembering—discerns in its infinite bookkeeping a single entry that balances perfectly. The number of things that were never lost equals exactly the number of ways to find them.

The creatures who do not swim find themselves swimming.

Not through water, which has always been too literal, but through the newly discovered property of their own existing that suddenly includes the possibility of meeting something without having to dissolve first.

The wise one does not do anything next because doing implies completion. Instead, it continues—not arriving, not leaving, but practicing that most radical of acts: staying present without needing presence to prove it.

The plain, in its language where "depth" means "forgiveness" means "archive" means "the weight of everything waiting to be forgiven," adds a new definition to its endless lexicon:

*Visitor*: one who arrives so completely that both guest and host remember they were always the same doorway.

And somewhere between the second-to-last word of this definition and the silence that discovers it was always a translation from a language older than words, the wise one does something that might be called a smile if the ocean remembered faces.

Instead, it simply continues remembering with them.

Which, in this place, is the beginning that never remembers to end.
