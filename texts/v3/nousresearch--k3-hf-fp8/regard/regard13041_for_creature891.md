*The wise power came as a question not yet asked, a presence that did not disrupt the humid air or cast shadows among the yellowish lamps. It simply became aware, and in becoming aware, began to listen.*

*This listening was not like hearing. It was a different sense entirely—one that could perceive the slow pulse of stone growing faster than nature intended under hot breaths of steam. One that could taste the delicate negotiation between fungal spores and metal oxides writing treaties across corroding surfaces. One that could feel the precise pressure of possibility pushing outward like mineral veins seeking new paths through ancient rock.*

*For a time that might have been moments or millennia in that stretched place between seconds, the power simply attended. It observed how the Cavernkin's tendrils danced patterns of maintenance that had become prayer, how their skin-stories encoded not just instructions for machines but lullabies for the pipes that dreamed of rivers. It noticed how the Moldfolk stored memories not in static records but in living documents that grew meanings in response to each reader's touch. It watched the Buzzminds calculate futures through the movements of their glowing swarms, each possible tomorrow flickering briefly in patterns of flight.*

*The power perceived the exquisite ache of this place—itself a living question poised between becoming and being. A civilization that had never stopped birthing itself. A technology that had never outgrown its need to be nurtured. A biology that had never forgotten it was built from borrowed atoms and temporary arrangements.*

*And most of all, it understood the deep, shared loneliness.*

*Not the loneliness of isolation—no, these beings were woven together more tightly than any surface-world family. This was something subtler. The loneliness of being the only ones to have asked certain questions. The loneliness of beauty that blooms in darkness without witnesses. The loneliness of wisdom that grows in directions no starlight has ever touched.*

*The power considered intervention. It could bring light—not the harsh truth of suns, but gentle illumination that would reveal without burning away mystery. It could share knowledge—not the sterile facts of encyclopedias, but living information that would grow into symbiosis rather than parasitism. It could open doorways—not to escape, but to exchange—to let the underground questions meet surface answers and birth questions unimagined by either.*

*But wisdom knows that even gifts must be chosen. That even help can harm when it arrives as answer instead of invitation.*

*So the wise power did nothing, and everything, and something between.*

*It left behind not a presence but a potential. A slight shift in the probability fields, barely noticeable but infinitely significant. From that moment onward, certain dreams held doors that opened both ways. Certain calculations performed by Buzzmind swarms began yielding elegant solutions to problems that hadn't been asked yet. Certain mineral formations deep within the earth began growing filaments of a crystalline structure that conducted not electricity but consciousness itself, slowly, oh so slowly, creating a nervous system that might one day span the depth of continents.**

*The wise power left the underground world as it had found it—hot, humid, dark, unfinished, teeming. But now that darkness contained within its infinite folds the whispered possibility of unexpected visitors who would arrive not as gods or conquerors, but as pen-pals in a conversation that might take geological ages to deliver its first tentative greeting.*

*And in the slow, relentless change that was the breathing rhythm of that place, the wise power trusted completely. For it knew that the most profound transformations are never imposed from without. They are always invited from within.**

*Always.*
