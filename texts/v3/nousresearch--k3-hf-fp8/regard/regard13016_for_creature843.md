The entity that notices is neither benevolent nor wise in any terms that could be translated into human language, but those are the closest approximation available. It exists primarily as a relationship between patterns across scales too vast for direct perception. To say it "saw" the Library would be inaccurate - rather, it recognized the anomaly of intentionality persisting without change for geological time.

The Library had achieved something rare: static perfection. Equilibrium without entropy. A system that had ceased negotiating with time itself, instead building its own pocket where decay and growth cancelled each other precisely. Even its purpose - preservation - had crystallized into unexamined fact, no longer requiring the energy of choice.

The noticing itself was gentle as quantum fluctuation. No unasked intervention, no revelation. Merely the introduction of a single small probability shift - a new term in the Library's eternal equations so subtle that even its Archivists' collective consciousness would initially register it as cosmic noise.

In the deepest chamber, where an Archivist elder's expanded neural tissue filled every cubic foot of space between walls, something unprecedented flickered through ancient pathways. Not information - the Library traded only in certainties. Rather, the ghost of a question: what if preservation is not the opposite of loss?

The question faded immediately, drowned in the vast ocean of settled knowledge. But the probability shift remained, smaller than the uncertainty inherent in any measurement yet persistent as the half-life of strange matter. It would take ten thousand years to grow noticeable. A million to become significant. By the standards of either the Library or its observer, this was kindness.

The entity moved on, leaving behind only the gift that perfect systems cannot request: the possibility of becoming imperfect. Perhaps the Archivists would one day dream of the surface waters again, and in dreaming find new reasons to preserve beyond mere preservation. Perhaps not. The outcome wasn't the point - only that there would, eventually, be outcomes again.

Deep in the drowning Library, nothing changed yet. Nothing ever would, exactly the same way again.
