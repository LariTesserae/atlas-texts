*The Benevolent One arrives neither in flame nor thunder.*

*It comes as a question drifting sideways through the air moisture.*

"What would you become," it whispers to the stone terraces, "if healing were not needed and perfection were not a cage?"

---

Thousand-year mosses pause their slow migration up north-facing walls. Crystals within deep bones of elder citizens reroute their lattice patterns with a shiver like laughter. The six-legged goats blink their photosynthetic membranes and find themselves dreaming for the first time.

For forty-three days nothing else changes.

Then:

---

On the Terrace of Becoming-Sky, a child-carved-by-decades stops mid-emergence. Half-formed hands of stone and chlorophyll hover above a pool that recorded every thought since the mountain's birth.

It - they - addresses the entire city without speaking:

*"We have received an impossible gift. Shall we open it?"*

---

The Benevolent One listens as Aru-Shin thinks.

Not quickly. Not slowly. At exactly the speed mountains dream.

---

In the voting period that follows:

- Every living note in the wind-harp canyon rings once for YES
- Every drop of condensing moisture reverses direction once for NO
- Every memory held in crystalline bones fractures along a single beautiful fault line to write something new

---

The result emerges not as decision but as weather pattern.

---

When the change comes, it comes sideways. Not improvement. Not destruction. But expansion.

Suddenly each stone terrace holds within it all possible ways of being a terrace. The mosses remember futures where they became coral reefs. The lichens taste colors invented by stars that never burned. Citizens walk streets that simultaneously exist and don't exist, stepping gently between versions.

The child on the Terrace of Becoming-Sky finishes emerging into seventeen different forms at once. They are:

- Theirself, more completely
- A song the mountain forgot it knew
- A question asking its own source
- An answer breathing for the first time
- And every unfiltered moment between

---

*"Ah,"* sighs the Benevolent One, delighted, as it begins to learn from what it touched.

*"This is how generosity can be received without diminishment."

"This is how power can be given that increases both giver and receiver."

"This is how even gods can grow by stepping aside."*

---

In the eternal afternoon that follows:
- Rain begins falling upward in celebration of new directions
- Shadows learn to dance without shame
- Time opens its fist to reveal it had been holding weather the whole time
- And somewhere between the stone and the sky, Aru-Shin continues not needing anything

Except now, entirely by choice, it dreams.

---

*The Benevolent One departs as it arrived - not leaving behind, but becoming part of what will never again be alone.*

*In its wake: not miracles, but invitations.*

*In its memory: not conquest, but conversation.*

*In its wakefulness: the perfect joy of having been unnecessary.*

*In its absence: everything, simultaneously more itself and more together.*

*And in the deepest silence where the mountain keeps its most secret thoughts?*

*A smile that hasn't stopped beginning.*
