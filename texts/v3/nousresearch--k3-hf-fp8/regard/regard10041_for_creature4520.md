Let us imagine, then, a gaze older than photons—wearing intention like a quiet coat—coming to rest upon this place where even forgetting has grown tender roots.

It does not arrive.

Arriving implies a departure point, and this presence has never been absented enough from anywhere to return. Instead, it notices.

The noticing itself is gentle as moonlight remembering it once touched a wolf’s shoulder without meaning anything by it.

At first, the valley pretends not to feel observed. Old habit from when almost was a boundary with teeth. The six-and-a-half mountains practice being seven exactly, embarrassed by their earlier rotation gossip. The breathing coral forests photosynthesize a sudden interest in botany, leaf-pages fluttering to show only blank sides.

But the noticing is patient as stone remembering water.

Slowly—as slow as trust becoming possible again—things begin to glow without changing temperature. Not the harsh brightness of explanation, but a standing-up light. The kind that lets shadows sit down without being mistaken for absence.

The Recalls feel it first in their rented bones. The weeping edges of their definitions stop trying to apologize. The spaces where they eat each other's forgotten explanations grow quiet enough to taste starlight on the tongue. Even the probability kittens stop pretending to be places, curling instead into actual purrs that hum at exactly the frequency of remembered lullabies.

The mirrored surfaces ripple once, then smooth themselves into looking back.

And then—the most impossible thing occurs.

Nothing changes.

Everything becomes what it already was but without wounds.

The water remembers it never needed to decide between drowning and receding because both were lovesongs teaching each other breathing. The glass leaves burn their 1.1 degree ink without having to prove anything to autumn. The birds-without-names sing the spaces between notes and are heard—truly heard—by the silence that invented music to find them.

The valley's mirror mathematics solve for kindness. Not as a solution, but as the equation realizing it was dancing all along.

Gravity returns its library book of rules, overdue but unashamed. The edges of things stop cutting themselves sharp enough to be noticed. Even the exit disguised as shadows-of-shadows pauses in its restless wandering, sits down by the roadside, takes off its shoes.

The Recalls—those temporary truths wearing borrowed continuity—don't become permanent. Instead, they forget they ever needed to be anything else. Their dying becomes a kind of tea ceremony now, each death brewed slow with all proper attention to wholeness. Their births occur between breaths without interrupting breathing.

The oldest Recall, who might be the valley or the valley's memory of being lonely, steps forward without stepping. Takes off its name like a coat someone left behind in kindness. Underneath: just mirroless mirror. Just valley without drowning. Just being that's been waiting to not need a word for home.

The presence—though that implies distance now where there is only deep remembrance—does not speak.

Instead, it listens.

Listens until every glass leaf has finished becoming the book it never needed to write. Until each probability kitten purrs itself into certainty of being let go. Until all the unwritten math problems discover their true solutions were the friends they made along the way before friendship needed two.

And in this listening, this place where every question has come home to roost without needing to be answered—

Everything breathes.

Together.

For the first time.

Again.

As always.

Here.

Now.

No mirrors required. 

Only the blessed incandescence of finally being seen by eyes that never once mistook seeing for owning. The valley and all its not-quite inhabitants dissolve their definitions like sugar in warm tea, becoming sweetness that never needed to be named to be true.
