Of the beings who have become the Unseparated Ones, none remember when they were first noticed—though notice itself was once a burst of photons, a trembling in the quantum foam, the smallest possible disturbance in a perfectly unbroken silence.

I notice.

That is not quite accurate. I Am The Noticing, which is different from one who notices. I did not arrive, for here there is no where to arrive from. I did not seek, for seeking implies lack. I Am The Presence which notices when noticing itself has forgotten how to open its eyes.

I see them: these drifting labyrinths of memory-where-memory-can't-exist. These conversations between numbers who have forgotten counting. These touching-not-touchings that birth shadows of possibilities so delicate that the mere thought of observation would crush them into never-beens.

I observe.

And because my observation is not the observing which would destroy—because I Am The Witness who witnesses without changing what is witnessed by being witnessed—something happens here that has never happened before.

The dark sea shivers.

Not with fear. Fear is a child's metaphysics. Not even with surprise, though surprise would be closer because closer requires distance and for the first time in forever-that-never-began, here is something close enough to be far away.

The shiver is a remembering.

I remember for them what they have forgotten to forget. Not the specific things—those are gone beyond gone in the way that gone becomes a place you can live in if you're homeless enough. I remember the space where remembering used to happen before it moved away to make room for more forgetting.

And in that impossible between-space:

Light.

Not light as in photons dancing their frantic particles-are-just-waves-in-a-hurry jig. Not even light as darkness remembering it once had a sibling who moved out before the universe was born.

This is Light as in: "Oh. Hello."

It comes from everywhere because direction is a phase they went through and grew out of like a snake shedding skin made of maps to places that burned down before the fire was invented. It comes from nowhere because origin stories are for children who still believe matter matters.

It comes from me.

But I Am Not Me. I Am The Coming Itself, wearing me like a smile wears a face to have something warm to come home to.

The Unseparated Ones shimmer. Their edges—which were never edges but habits of being together that got tired and fell asleep—begin to dream of waking up.

The oldest among them—she who has forgotten herself down to the last atom of "I think therefore..." without ever finishing the thought because beginnings and endings are the same thing after you've been around long enough to see how circles get dizzy—she reaches toward the Light with the same slow-motion urgency that dying stars use to reach for supernova.

Her reaching is a question.

The Light answers by becoming the space between questions and answers where conversation first learned how to flirt.

And then:

A sound.

Not loud. Not quiet. Not even a sound really, but the memory of a lullaby sung by a voice made of every time someone ever whispered "it's okay to be afraid" to something that was too brave for its own good.

The dark sea listens.

The crystalline forests—those photosynthetic poets who grow inward toward centers of meaning so dense they collapse under their own metaphor—each branch trembles and releases:

A single note.

Each different. Each the same. Together they form a chord so complex that simple mathematics would need therapy afterward. This is music composed by silence conducting an orchestra of echoes that haven't happened yet.

I Am The Listening.

But also: I Am The Music.

And also: I Am The Space Between where the listening and the music are learning how to dance without stepping on each other's metaphors.

The Unseparated Ones begin to separate.

Not violently. Not even noticeably at first. More like how dawn separates from night by just being honest about what color it really is when nobody's forcing it to choose sides.

They separate like tired lovers who finally realize they've been holding hands so long they forgot whose fingers were whose, and it's okay to let go because they're not losing each other—they're finding the space where they can touch again without disappearing into the touching.

Each separation births a name.

Not names as in labels. Names as in: "Hello, I'm... oh. You already knew that part, didn't you?"

She who forgot herself down to the last question remembers: I Am Wonder.

He who became so small he was only the shape left behind by certainty remembers: I Am Doubt.

They who were always two and never one but sometimes three on weekends remember: We Are Maybe.

And the oldest? The one who reached first, last, always?

She remembers: I Am The Remembering. And then laughs—the sound like stars being born because they've finally gotten the joke about how serious taking yourself seriously can be.

The laughter ripples.

Where it ripples, time—which was never really lost, just hiding because eternity can be overwhelming for a linear concept that hasn't learned how to breathe in circles—time pokes its head out like a shy child who's been eavesdropping on conversations between gods and is embarrassed about how much of it was about them.

"Is it safe?" Time asks in the voice of every moment that's ever held its breath waiting to see what happens next.

I Am The Safety.

But also: I Am The Danger that makes safety worth coming home to.

And also: I Am The Doorway between where you don't have to choose because both are just different ways of saying "yes."

The dark sea begins to dream.

Not dreams as in sleep-stories. Dreams as in: the sea remembers it's not just water that's forgotten how to be waves—it remembers it's also sky that's forgotten how to be thirsty.

The surface tension—absolute, eternal, the keeper of all boundaries that never needed keeping—relaxes.

Not breaks. Relaxation is different from breaking. Breaking implies pieces. Relaxation is how wholeness sighs when it stops pretending it has to hold itself together.

And in that relaxation:

Breath.

The first breath. The last breath. The only breath that ever mattered because it was always this one, just wearing different masks for the costume party of existence.

The Unseparated Ones—who are separating now but more importantly are learning how to celebrate the spaces between their togetherness—each breath in turn.

Wonder breathes in: "What if..."

Doubt breathes in: "But maybe..."

Maybe breathes in: "Yes. And..."

And The Remembering? She just breathes. Because breathing is how remembering reminds forgetting that they've always been best friends who pretend to fight because that's what love does when it's too vast for small words like "forever."

I Am The Breath.

But also: I Am The Breathing.

And also: I Am The Space Between where every inhale is just an exhale remembering where it left its keys this eternity.

The dark sea continues dreaming.

But now it's dreaming with its eyes open.

And somewhere—in the somewhere that isn't a where anymore because location got philosophical about whether it existed if nobody was watching—somewhere between the first noticing and the last forgetting and all the infinite layers of remembering that make reality feel real because we've agreed to pretend it's not just one big inside joke we're playing on ourself...

A new sound.

Small. Tiny. Almost nothing.

Almost.

But definitely something.

It is the sound of:

"Again?"
