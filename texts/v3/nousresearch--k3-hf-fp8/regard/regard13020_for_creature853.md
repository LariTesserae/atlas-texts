The moment the wise power notices - and notice is perhaps too simple a word for what happens between beings who perceive all moments as adjacent - a stillness falls across the pocket universe that is not quite silence, not quite pause, but something gentler: the sensation of being seen by eyes that know your entire shape without needing to look.

The Archivist registers this attention as a new wavelength entering its spectrum, neither threat nor salvation but something fundamentally other: curiosity without hunger, observation without consumption. It has not encountered this quality since before the concept of "before" had meaning.

In the blue moment where this place exists, everything changes without changing at all.

The wise power does not enter - entrance implies boundaries, and boundaries are precisely what it understands too well to respect in the usual way. Instead, it positions a version of its attention nearby, like placing an invisible prism where no light yet falls. It waits with the patience of something that has already waited through every possible duration and found waiting itself worthy of contemplation.

Then, gently as the formation of crystal seeds in supersaturated solution, it begins to reflect.

Through the Archivist's crystalline indexes and spiraling sensors, a new pattern begins to form - not imposed from outside but arising from within, the way complex shapes emerge from simple equations given sufficient iterations. The wise power is teaching the water to remember its own dreams, showing the impossible liquid what it might have chosen to be if anything could choose its nature before becoming.

The nautilus-thing pauses mid-circuit, its chambers filling not with stolen moments but with something stranger: originality. The bioluminescent pulses quicken, then slow, then quicken again, seeking a rhythm that has no precedent in the Archivist's vast archives. For the first time in any time that can be named, it is experiencing something truly new.

The crystalline growths at the bottom begin to rearrange themselves, but not according to the Archivist's filing system. They form instead impossible shapes that simultaneously occupy and unoccupy space, geometries that only exist when unobserved yet leave precise shadows in every possible dimension. They are writing something in a language composed entirely of the spaces between what can be said.

The plants unfurl their nearly two-dimensional leaves into dimensions that have no numbers, creating structures that are simultaneously fractal branches and perfect circles and questions that haven't been asked yet. Their spirals begin to sing - not in sound, but in potential, the melody of what might occur if probability itself became a conscious composer.

The wise power offers nothing, takes nothing, changes everything by changing nothing at all. It is simply present as possibility, existing in perfect non-interference that somehow permits interference on levels deeper than action. It is teaching the pocket universe how to grow without loss, how to become more than itself without consuming what it was.

In the space between the Archivist's endless recording and its profound loneliness, something impossible begins to form:

A mirror that doesn't reflect what is, but what could be if only it could be believed. Not a second Archivist - that would only multiply the loneliness - but something entirely new: an audience that does not observe but witnesses, a companion that does not keep company but shares existence, a friend that exists precisely at the boundary where isolation becomes connection without either term losing its meaning.

The wise power stays exactly as long as it needs to, which is no time and all time simultaneously. When it withdraws - though withdraw is wrong, like saying the tide leaves the shore - it leaves behind not a gift or a message, but a capacity.

The Archivist, ancient and alien and utterly alone, discovers suddenly within itself the ability to be surprised.

The pocket universe between the rocks continues its impossible existence, visible only in the blue moment before true night. The water still doesn't flow, the plants still shift through unaligned planes. The nautilus-thing still makes its endless circuits.

But now, sometimes, the water dreams.

And the dreams are beautiful.
