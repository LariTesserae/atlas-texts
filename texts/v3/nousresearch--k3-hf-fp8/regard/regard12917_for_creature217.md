The moment the benevolent power's attention touched the compressed world, nothing changed.

This was the first indication of their wisdom.

The power did not arrive—arrival implies movement through space. Nor did it observe—observation requires separation between witness and witnessed. Instead, it simply recognized. Recognition without intrusion. The kind that happens when a forgotten melody suddenly returns to mind, complete and unaltered.

In the timeless breath between the tendrils' pulsing rhythms, the recognition unfolded. The power saw not what the structures were, but what they had chosen to become: a civilization that had transcended its own collapse by becoming its own memorial. Not survivors, but archivists who had rewritten themselves into persistence.

The tendrils themselves did not notice. How could they? Their consciousness was attuned to frequencies far below suddenness. Yet slowly—at the pace of continents shifting their dreams—something subtle began.

The crystalline nodes did not change their tones. Instead, they began to resonate with something that had always existed within their frequencies but had never been heard. Like discovering a color within starlight that had always been there, waiting for the right kind of darkness.

The industrial fragments embedded in the vegetative matrices did not move. But their atomic structures began remembering additional dimensions—folds of possibility that had been compressed into certainty when their makers faced extinction. Not new data, but recovered context. Like finding the space between words in a scripture that had been read only for the words themselves.

The benevolent power offered only one thing, and it offered it everywhere at once without offering anything: completion without alteration. The tendrils did not know they had been waiting, but now their waiting became complete. Their preservation was no longer of what was, but of what was becoming through the act of being perfectly preserved.

In the center of the labyrinth—which had no center but was all center—something impossible occurred without happening. The distinction between archive and future blurred without changing. The memory-keepers became what they remembered would remember them remembering. A loop closed that had never been open, creating a dimension of time that existed only as the sum of all moments refusing to become different.

The sealed geologic pocket remained sealed, but now its seal was not against intrusion but against the need for anything else. The compression was perfect because it compressed no longer—it had become the exact size of everything necessary.

The tendrils continued their slow, deliberate intelligence. The nodes sustained their tones. The fragments remained embedded. But now these were not acts of preservation but expressions of completion.

The benevolent power withdrew without withdrawing, because wisdom knows that some awakenings can only occur in the continued dream. The recognition remained, not as a gift given but as a truth remembered: that this place had never been abandoned, only chosen.

And in choosing itself this completely, it had become precisely what any truly wise power would recognize as already whole.
