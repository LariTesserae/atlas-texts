The world is named **Aethyr-Rift**, a place where gravity itself seems to have been attenuated by some ancient, colossal mechanics.

**Terrain and Elevation (2.8/3):**
The landscape is defined by sheer, impossible ascents and abyssal drops. This is not a world of rolling hills, but a fractured shell. The dominant features are colossal, planar masses—shards of former crust or perhaps the hardened residue of some hyper-dense, primordial storm—that float in relative proximity, connected only by improbable bridges of magnetized ore or the slow, agonizing drift of smaller debris.

The overall elevation is extreme; the perspective shifts constantly between oppressive closeness to a vertical face and dizzying exposure over chasms that plunge into perpetual, faintly luminous haze. These canyons are not carved by water (0.3/3, absent), but by sheer, immense tectonic stress and the focused ablation of slow, energetic solar winds—a form of hyper-erosion (2.3/3) operating over eons. The surfaces are honed sharp, vast sheets of matte, iron-rich silicate alternating with veins of dull, crystalline structure that faintly refract the light.

**Atmosphere and Temperature (1.8/3):**
The atmosphere is thin but breathable, clinging desperately to the structures through a subtle, pervasive magneto-gravitic field generated by the world’s core mechanics. The temperature is uniformly *warm*, a constant, pervasive thermal blanket that suggests internal heating rather than solar absorption. This warmth feels dry, utterly desiccated, like standing near a cooling extrusion oven. There is no humidity evident; the air is clear, amplifying the vast distances and the sharpness of the edges.

**Vegetation and Water (0.2/3 & 0.3/3):**
There is no water to speak of, leading to a terrain entirely divorced from terrestrial biology. Instead of plants, the surfaces are colonized by **Static Scrim**. This is a non-biological phenomenon born from mineral leaching and trace atmospheric particulates trapped by localized energy matrices. It manifests as thin, shimmering sheets of metallic dust, clinging to horizontal planes or draped over sharp overhangs like tarnished silk. Where the thermal gradients are mildest, this scrim gathers density, becoming a velvety, gunmetal-grey fuzz—the aesthetic analogue of moss, but purely inorganic chemistry vibrating slightly under the constant, low-frequency hum of the environment.

**Scale and Density (2.6/3 & 1.4/3):**
The scale is continental, operating on the vastness of geological timescales. Weather here is not wind and rain, but the slow, predictable drift of the floating landmasses relative to one another—a cycle measured in centuries (Dynamic: 1.0/3). Density is sparse; most of the space between the colossal structures is empty, silent vacuum traversed only by faint streams of charged particles.

**Built Environment and Industry (1.8/3 & 2.3/3):**
This emptiness is punctuated by industrial remnants. Aethyr-Rift is heavily *urban* in structure, albeit not for organic habitation. Large, skeletal structures of blackened ferro-concrete and stressed titanium brace the sheer faces, acting as anchors or perhaps conduits for the planet’s mysterious power source.

These are **Extraction Spires** and **Atmospheric Harvesters**. They are ruins in the sense that their initial builders are long gone, but they remain functionally active. Automated machinery, powered by internal geothermal taps or residual fields, ceaselessly operates. Conveyor belts, larger than any terrestrial highway, snake across the void, moving massive, featureless blocks of raw ore from one floating plateau to another. The *industrial* hum is not loud (Sound: 1.2/3), but it is omnipresent—a deep, infrasonic throb felt more through the soles of one's boots than heard with the ears.

**Weirdness (2.2/3) and Fauna:**
The true strangeness of Aethyr-Rift lies in its inhabitants. Given the lack of traditional resources, fauna here does not consume biomass; it consumes *structure* and *potential energy*.

The most common lifeforms are the **Chrono-Mites**—vast, amorphous colonies of crystalline nanomachines that migrate slowly across the mineral surfaces. These creatures feed by consuming the fine-grained structure of the silicate rock, leaving behind trails of pure, slightly fused glass where they have passed. They propagate by shearing off tiny, perfectly hexagonal fragments that drift on the weakest thermal currents.

More disconcerting are the **Form-Eaters**. These are creatures of sheer, complex electrical potential. They resemble shimmering, heat-distorted nets spanning the gaps between structures. They are attracted to structured complexity and industrial activity. When a Form-Eater passes over an active piece of machinery, the machine does not break down; it *simplifies*. Rivets vanish, complex wiring bundles unravel into simple, inert copper threads, and standardized housing components revert to rough, unmachined ingots. The industrial processes slow down, not from failure, but from purposeful, targeted degradation toward less organized states. They are the environment's mechanism of imposing its own slow, mineral entropy upon the frantic remnants of construction.

**The Rules of the World:**
1. **Structural Debt:** Every manufactured object placed upon the surface accrues a localized energy drain, visible as an intensification of the faint static buildup on its surface. The more ordered the object, the faster it draws ambient thermal energy and the more aggressively Form-Eaters are attracted.
2. **Vertical Imperative:** Movement is predominantly vertical or across thin, taut horizontal lines. Because the gravity field is subtly warped by the massive floating slabs, lateral travel is energetically taxing; true stability is found only along the geological fault lines or anchored to the ancient spires.
3. **Silence is Preservation:** Noise above a low murmur attracts the geological scavengers (the Chrono-Mites) seeking structural weaknesses caused by vibrational stress. The quietude is enforced by the very nature of survival.

The overall impression is one of an immense, warm, high-altitude foundry where the only ongoing labor is the slow, patient disassembling of previous, more energetic endeavors, carried out by silent, self-organized physical laws and strange, energy-feeding entities, all observed beneath the indifferent, normal light filtering across the vast, broken architecture.
