### Terrain and Materials

The **elevation (2.6/3)** is extreme, suggesting the upper reaches of a titanic, newly cooled formation, or perhaps the underside of a vast, inverted shell. The dominant material is not rock, but condensed kinetic potential. It looks like basalt or chert, until you look closer. The surfaces are riddled with fractal micro-fissures through which a perpetual, low-level seeping occurs. This explains the **water (2.2/3)** parameter. It is not rain or pooled surface water; it is **weeping rock**. The **scale (0.1/3)** of observation is crucial here. ### Atmosphere and Ecology

The lack of traditional **vegetation (0.5/3)** is absolute. There are no cells, no chlorophyll, no recognizable biology. Instead, the ecosystem is based on phase transitions and energy gradients. The environment is **dense (1.5/3)** not with air molecules, but with overlapping fields of influence.

They appear as shimmering outlines, momentary distortions in the light, or brief, impossibly complex braids of condensation that hold their form for a few heartbeats before dissolving back into the vapor.

These entities, the **Overwhelming Fauna**, adhere to the rules of the local physics. They interact primarily through resonant frequencies and thermal exchange. ### The Rules of the World: Reality Breakdown

The **weirdness (2.8/3)** dictates the fundamental law: **Causality is local and temporal, not global.**

If you watch a pool of amethyst liquid form, it seems to follow fluid dynamics. But if you trace the source of that liquid back an inch, it might be condensing from vapor that mathematically shouldn't exist at that temperature, or it might be extruded from a hairline crack that was visibly sealed moments before.

The density of fauna, these kinetic patterns, causes localized reality slippage. A structure that appears solid might, for a fraction of a second, exist in three mutually exclusive states simultaneously—a solidified shard, a flowing sheet, and a cloud of vapor—before resolving according to the dominant environmental flux.

This place is a crucible, constantly boiling itself into new, temporary configurations where the hot, wet, and impossibly complex mineral substance strives toward a form of self-organization that has bypassed the need for biochemistry entirely. It is a landscape of pure, suffering thermodynamics. There is no **built environment (0.0/3)**, only the unintentional architecture of extreme pressure and rapid condensation.
