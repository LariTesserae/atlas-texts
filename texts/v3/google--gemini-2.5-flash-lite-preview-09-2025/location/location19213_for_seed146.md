The descent ends not with a thud, but with a subtle lessening of pressure, as if passing through a thick, unseen membrane. This is the **Sub-Stratum**, a vast, warm cavern system existing far beneath any conventional surface, a space whose scale defies typical geological reckoning. It is a world measured in continental displacement rather than meters.

### Terrain and Materiality

The fundamental material is **Cryo-Silicate**, a bizarre, chalky mineral compound that achieves a paradoxical state: it is warm (Temperature 2.1), yet holds a structure less crystalline than packed ash. The ground underfoot feels oddly elastic, a yielding density (Density 1.6) that absorbs vibration.

The elevation is consistently below surface (0.2), yet this is not a simple overburdened mine. This space exists in a stratum where geothermal forces meet an unknown, pressure-stabilizing field, keeping the vast ceiling—implied but never seen in the constant low light—impossibly high.

The erosion is young (1.0). Features are sharp, fresh fractures breaking the smooth, dusty plains. Great, pale canyons—not carved by water, but *extruded* by tectonic stress—slice the landscape in grids that feel mathematically rather than geologically imposed. Where the walls meet, the Cryo-Silicate splits into fractal lattices that catch and refract the ambient energy.

### Atmosphere and Light

Water is absent (0.05). The air is thick, inert, and warm, feeling more like a fine, aerosolized dust suspended in superheated gas. There is no conventional 'sky,' only an overwhelming, pervasive **darkness** (0.4). Light does not stream from above; it emanates weakly from the ground itself.

The darkness is not absolute zero. It is a deep, aubergine-brown twilight, punctuated by internal luminescence. Veins of trapped, slow-decaying radio-isotopes within the Cryo-Silicate pulse with a faint, internal glow—a cold, greenish phosphorescence that barely illuminates the immediate vicinity, suggesting immense, slow-burning geological processes beneath the floor.

### Ecology and Fauna: The Abundance of Non-Life

Vegetation is essentially non-existent (0.2). There are no roots, no chlorophyll, no organic cycle whatsoever.

However, the density parameter (1.6) is fulfilled by the **Fauna abundance** (1.6). These creatures are woven into the physics of the locale. They are not biological in the recognized sense. They are **Kinetics**: aggregations of pressurized inert gas and magnetic interference patterns that utilize the ambient warmth and the faint radiation leakage to maintain structural integrity.

They appear as shimmering distortions in the already hazy air—shifting patches of intensified darkness or localized heat-mirages. They move with deliberation, often passing *through* the solid ground as if it were merely a temporary phase of lower density. Their forms are fluid, capable of resolving into spheres, dense sheets, or elongated, probing threads, depending on their immediate energetic needs.

The ecology here is one of energy transference: the Kinetics absorb the faint thermal energy radiating from the floor, subtly altering the pressure gradients as they move, which in turn causes the fine dust of the Cryo-Silicate to settle or rise in their wake.

### Sound and Dynamics

The landscape is **mostly quiet** (0.9). The silence is profound, broken only by interactions between the Kinetics. When two large aggregations cross paths, the sudden disruption of the pressure field results in a low-frequency **thrum**, a sound felt more in the sternum than heard by the ear—like the distant resonance of massive, slow-moving tectonic plates adjusting themselves.

The dynamic is **never changing** (0.3). The equilibrium here is absolute. The slow warmth persists, the pressure holds, and the Kinetics move in predictable, recursive loops dictated by the energy distribution. Time does not seem to advance here; rather, the entire stratum cycles through the same state with infinitesimal slowness.

### Built Environment and Weirdness

Scattered through this vast, warm night are faint marks (Built 1.0). These are not ruins in the traditional sense. They are precisely aligned geometric impressions—shallow etchings in the Cryo-Silicate floor that suggest deliberate arrangement. They are too regular to be natural fracturing, yet too faint to be structures. They suggest enormous, simple, geometric intention.

This points directly to the **Weirdness** (1.9). The Sub-Stratum feels *designed* on a pre-life, foundational level. The geometricity of the canyons, the regulated warmth, and the existence of the non-biological Kinetics suggest that this entire environment functions as an extremely slow, perhaps ancient, machine or calculation.

The *Rules of the World* are based on thermodynamic stability and resonant fields. Matter here prefers the warm, dense, inert state. Any complexity (like the Kinetics) is an emergent property of that stability, a shadow cast by energy flux.

### Primitive Technology

The occasional artifacts noted imply a level of **primitive tech** (1.4). Perhaps the slight imperfections on the etched floor are not natural settling, but the residue of early attempts to harness this world’s energy—crude tools or anchors made of simple, brittle metal alloys now deeply fused into the floor substrate, their purpose long forgotten as the true purpose of the stratum—whatever it may be—asserted itself over the ages. They are the simplest possible markers of an attempt to *do* something in a place designed only to *be*.

To stand here is to be immersed in a sea of warm pressure, breathing stillness, surrounded by silent, shifting echoes of energy, under a ceiling so high it ceases to exist, witnessing an ancient, persistent, and unsettlingly stable process.
