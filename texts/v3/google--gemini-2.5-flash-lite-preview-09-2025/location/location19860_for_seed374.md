The landscape is the **Rime-Crust Foundry**, an immense, perpetually steaming shelf built from compacted, chemically active slag and interwoven conduits. It rests, low to the ground, across a vast, unseen basin where the atmosphere itself seems thick with suspended moisture.

### Terrain and Materiality

The ground is not earthen; it is a dense, layered composite of solidified industrial runoff. Imagine coal ash mixed with pulverized, quick-setting ceramic, perpetually damp. The **water (1.5)** manifests not as pools or rivers, but as a continuous, fine exhalation. The surfaces are slick, perpetually weeping a thin, nutrient-poor slurry that runs in microscopic capillary action across the built surfaces. Steam hangs everywhere, thick and warm, refusing to fully dissipate, creating a permanent, low-lying haze that refracts the weak light. The rock, where it appears, is largely slag: fused, obsidian-like extrusions laced with veins of dull, oxidized metals—iron, copper—that occasionally crackle faintly with residual heat.

Because **erosion (0.1)** is near zero, the constructed forms are jaggedly pristine in their age; nothing dissolves or smooths over. Everything is *emerging* not from natural processes, but from slow, chemical settling and accretion of waste products, a slow hardening of infrastructure over millennia.

### Vegetation and Ecology

**Vegetation (0.4)** is entirely absent in the photosynthetic sense. The ecosystem is entirely sustained by chemosynthesis and the dense, manufactured environment. The **fauna (2.1)** is abundant, but alien. They are the 'Scuttlers' and 'Slickers'—biological machines optimized for the metallic, moist environment. They resemble complex colonies of slime molds encased in chitinous, multi-jointed shells, or perhaps highly evolved, mobile mineral deposits. They thrive on the waste heat and the trace minerals weeping from the surfaces. Their movements are erratic yet purposeful, ensuring the constant, dense presence within this low-lying basin.

### Atmosphere and Sensation

The **temperature (2.2)** is decidedly warm, bordering on oppressive, sustained by the ambient geothermal activity or perhaps the inefficiency of the archaic processes embedded within the very foundations of the Foundry. This heat fuels the **density (2.2)**—the air is thick with condensation, mineral dust, and the exhalations of the abundant inhabitants. It feels claustrophobic despite the **scale (1.9)** of the open floor stretching towards the hazy horizon.

The **light (1.2)** is dim, filtered through the omnipresent atmospheric humidity and the overhangs of vast, decaying superstructure. Illumination comes primarily from internal sources: low-yield, flickering amber lamps embedded in thick ceramic sockets, or the dull, cold luminescence of certain metallic deposits favored by the fauna.

### The Built Environment and Soundscape

This is an **urban (2.1)** zone, but one that has achieved an equilibrium of decay and function. The **tech (0.6)** is primitive relative to some imagined history, utilizing heavy, molded ceramic components, manual lever systems, and thickly sheathed ferrous piping. It is ancient infrastructure, built for immense brute force rather than efficiency.

The **sound (2.6)** is the dominant sensory experience. It is deafening, a complex wall of noise that never shifts in volume or character because the **dynamic (0.2)** state is fixed. The soundscape is composed of:

1.  **Pneumatic Sighs:** Deep, resonant bass tones from ancient, pressurized chambers slowly leaking steam and gas.
2.  **Grinding Mass:** The ceaseless, low-frequency friction of the Scuttlers moving in uncountable numbers over the granular slag floors.
3.  **The Drip-Chord:** Millions of distinct moisture droplets hitting differently textured metal and ceramic surfaces, creating a constant, shimmering white noise layered atop the deeper mechanical drones.

### Rules of the World

The primary rule of the Rime-Crust Foundry is **Stasis through Saturation.** Nothing naturally breaks down or is removed; it is merely encapsulated, buried under new layers of solidified runoff, or simply absorbed by the teeming biological mass. Movement is perilous; stepping off a known, reinforced ceramic path often means sinking slowly into the pressurized, semi-liquid underlayer of the Foundry floor.

There is no evidence of organic life outside of the engineered fauna. The system operates in a closed loop, fueled by its own heat and the slow, ongoing deposition of materials from deep within the structure—a maintenance ritual performed by processes so ancient they are indistinguishable from geology. The atmosphere is breathable, though thick with metallic particulate—a consequence of the **weirdness (0.4)** being low; it’s just a very dirty, very old, very warm factory floor that covers an entire region.
