The ground underfoot is a strange, pale, granular mineral, not quite sand, not quite salt, yet possessing the dryness of both. This is the **Calcified Delta**, a place of immense, low-lying expanse, stretching beyond the curvature of sight in all cardinal directions. The **water: 0.4** parameter is absolute here; moisture is a memory or a concept belonging to other realities. The medium that *replaces* water is a pervasive, fine particulate called **Aether-Dust**, composed of micro-crystalline structures that absorb and refract light in peculiar ways, creating a constant, shimmering haze close to the ground.

Despite the desiccation, the **vegetation: 1.5** is breathtakingly, unnervingly *lush*. These are not plants rooted in soil, but rather structures that seem to breathe the Aether-Dust directly. They unfold in slow, deliberate geometries, manifesting as towering, branching lattices of crystalline fibers that capture the diffuse light. They are bioluminescent, pulsing with a slow, internal rhythm of amber and faint violet, creating a forest that is simultaneously mineral and organic. These are the **Silica-Trees**, not growing upwards so much as *precipitating* outwards, reaching heights that dwarf conventional terrestrial trees, their canopies forming a dense, overlapping ceiling perhaps a kilometer above the surface, a glowing, slow-motion chandelier.

The atmosphere is **warm (1.7)**, radiating heat absorbed by the vast, pale surface, but there is no humidity to trap it. The air feels thin yet strangely viscous due to the density of the suspended Aether-Dust.

The **elevation: 0.9** confirms the flatness of the Delta. It is a foundation plane, a geological slow-cooker where immense pressures and slow chemical reactions dominate. The **erosion: 0.4 (emerging)** is evident in the raw structures pushing up from the Delta floor—jagged, half-formed pillars of raw mineral paste that look like half-set concrete, suggesting that the entire landscape is perpetually *congealing* into form, rather than weathering away.

The **scale (2.9)** defines the environment: this place is continental, perhaps planetary, large enough to generate its own atmospheric patterns. The slow, dense interplay between the high canopy and the ground generates slow-moving thermal currents that become localized weather systems, swirling eddies of bright Aether-Dust that resemble slow-motion dust tornadoes.

The **density (2.7)** is overwhelming in terms of occupancy. While the ground seems sterile, the *space* is saturated. Every volume of the air is occupied by either the Aether-Dust, the luminous, fibrous structures of the Silica-Trees, or the **fauna (2.0)** that inhabit them.

The fauna are migratory swarms—not insects, but complex, self-assembling clouds of mobile biological casings held together by fields of focused static electricity generated by the friction of the dust. They flow through the Silica-Lattices like metallic rivers, sometimes coalescing into larger, vaguely tentacled forms that scrape nutrient traces from the crystalline bark of the trees.

What provides the **urban (2.1)** texture is not conventional construction, but the *architecture* of the ecosystem itself. The Silica-Trees are not randomly distributed; they grow in vast, interconnected grids, forming living causeways and vaulted halls that suggest intelligent organization. Within the spaces between these crystalline mega-structures, smaller, shell-like entities—the **Density Dwellers**—have excavated perfect, spherical voids, stacking these hollow shells upon each other in complex, precarious towers that reach halfway up to the canopy. This stacking is done without tools (**tech: 0.5**); the creatures use magnetic levitation and precise sonic manipulation imparted through specialized vocal sacs, locking the shells together through electrostatic adhesion. These shell-towers are the villages, the cities—compact, highly organized clusters within the otherwise boundless delta.

**Light (2.1)** is standard, channeled through the luminous canopy, resulting in a perpetual, soft twilight punctuated by the slow, internal sparks from the vegetation.

The **soundscape (0.3)** is dominated by profound quiet. The movement of the dust swarms is nearly inaudible, a friction too fine for human ears. The only consistent ambient noise is a very low-frequency thrumming, the collective bio-electrical hum of the massive Silica-Tree network resonating through the pale substrate. Occasionally, one of the shell-towers settling under its own weight might emit a sharp, high-pitched resonant *tink* as two spheres lock into place, an isolated sound quickly swallowed by the vastness.

The **dynamic (2.4)** is established by the constant, slow metabolism of the landscape. The *rules* here concern resource locking: the Silica-Trees photosynthesize the ambient light/dust energy, and the Density Dwellers harvest the trace mineral leachate excreted by the trees' lowest roots. The energy transfer is sluggish, vast, and intensely competitive, a slow-motion arms race for access to the foundational structures.

**Weirdness (0.1)** remains low; despite the alien chemistry, the physics seem locally consistent. It is a place of extreme, specialized biological efficiency operating on principles of mineral growth and dust suspension, a world where growth itself is the primary form of construction, and silence is the default state of its enormous, packed expanse.
