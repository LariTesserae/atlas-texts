The sky is not a sky here, not in the familiar sense, for the world is entirely submerged. This is the **Sub-Aqueous Expanse of the Hyper-Cloud**, a realm defined by its ubiquitous, crushing, yet oddly clear water.

**Water (2.8/3):** The medium is not merely an ocean; it is an omnipresent, pressurized fluid that feels more like a highly viscous, cool gel than simple water, yet it moves with impossible speed. Visibility is strange. While the scale is vast (2.7/3), the medium itself is dense enough to transmit light powerfully, yet diffuse enough that distant perception warps. Occasionally, layers of sheer, horizontal *walls of rain*—hyper-condensed atmospheric fronts that never quite hit a solid surface—sweep past, causing localized, deafening pressure shifts that buffet the landscape. These walls are the closest thing to weather that forms within the liquid domain.

**Terrain and Erosion (0.4/3):** There is almost no solid ground, yet the landscape possesses a topography of titanic, slow-forming structures. This is **Emergent Geosculpture**. Vast, translucent monoliths of pressurized mineral precipitate rise from depths that are mathematically unknowable. They are not built by tectonic action, but seem to crystallize directly out of the dense fluid over eons, still in the process of *becoming*. These structures are skeletal, veined with phosphorescent, non-biological conduits of faster-moving currents. You can see the raw geology of the substrate being extruded, smooth and featureless in some places, violently sharp and fractal in others, suggesting a process of continuous, slow-motion geological infancy.

**Scale and Density (2.7/3 & 0.2/3):** The sheer scale is terrifying. Structures span thousands of kilometers, yet the space *between* these embryonic formations is one of near-void—an incomprehensible emptiness maintained by the fluid’s uniform pressure. Floating within this near-void are not particulate clouds, but **Zero-Density Pockets**, regions where the water momentarily loses its viscous character, becoming a perfect vacuum that sucks light inward until the immense pressure gradient forces the fluid back in with a shockwave.

**Temperature and Atmosphere (1.3/3):** The temperature is uniformly cool, a constant, deep-level chill that would kill any terrestrial life instantly. There is no air; the atmosphere *is* the deep-water medium, saturated with unknown dissolved elements that give it its peculiar optical properties.

**Vegetation (0.9/3):** Life is sparse, not because the environment is hostile to *this* environment, but because the scale and dynamic nature make sustained biological habitation difficult. The vegetation that exists is not plant-like. It manifests as **Silicate Filaments**, razor-thin strands of nearly invisible, electrically charged silica that stretch across the empty voids, anchored to the nascent rock formations. They are non-organic scavengers, vibrating wildly in the currents, capturing energy from the Tech signatures present in the medium. They offer zero shade or cover.

**Technology and Noise (2.7/3 & 2.2/3):** This landscape is permeated by **Meta-Engineering**. The turbulence (2.6/3) is not natural storm chaos; it is the byproduct of gigantic, physics-defying mechanisms operating on a cosmic scale, perhaps controlling the inflow or outflow of the fluid body itself. These systems are invisible, hidden in the scale of the environment, but their influence is loud.

The **Sound (2.2/3)** is deafening. It is not the sound of rushing water, but a constant, non-directional bass thrum composed of three concurrent elements:
1. **The Deep Hum:** A planet-scale resonance from the hydrostatic pressure equilibrium.
2. **The Fracturing:** The sound of the emergent stone structures spontaneously cracking under internal stresses, a slow-motion groan that echoes across light-years of water.
3. **The System Static:** High-frequency, rhythmic clicking and sweeping signals generated by the incomprehensible technology that regulates the medium.

**Light (2.2/3):** The light is 'normal'—meaning it obeys predictable laws—but its source is deeply ambiguous. It filters down from an impossibly high ‘surface’ that may be miles or parsecs above. The light is mostly a muted, pale cyan, strong enough to cast deep, slow-moving shadows when illuminated by the brief, violent flashes of bio-luminescent discharges from the Silicate Filaments reacting to passing energy fields.

**Weirdness (1.3/3):** The overriding strangeness lies in the perceived flow. While the overall environment is turbulent and dynamic, certain massive objects—the embryonic monoliths—appear to be moving *against* the perceived current, shifting position with glacial slowness, implying localized pockets of inverted physics governed by the unseen technological framework. There is a constant sense of observation, as the light seems to catch the edges of non-Euclidean geometries hidden just behind the water's refraction limits.

**Rules of the World:** Gravity is muted or entirely replaced by hydrostatic pressure vectors. Locomotion requires mastering the pressure gradient flow. There is no solid ground to stand on unless you adhere yourself to the actively forming silicate crusts. The air/water is breathable only to whatever the governing technology permits, and the presence of life (fauna: 0.0/3) suggests the rules are strictly enforced against anything organic not designed to incorporate the surrounding technological current. This is a frontier of pure fluid mechanics governed by engineering that treats astronomical objects as mere variables.
