## Preparation Protocol: The Shattered Zenith (High Altitude, Extreme Stasis Zone)

### I. Physiological & Environmental Hardening (Against the Cold and Thin Air)

The primary immediate threats are **Hypoxic Shock** and **Chemical Cold Penetration**.

#### 1. Atmospheric Integrity & Respiration:
The air is thin, desiccated, and likely contains trace, abrasive particulates.

*   **Breathing Apparatus:** A standard high-altitude rebreather is insufficient. You require a **Closed-Loop Cryo-Filtration System** integrated into a full environmental suit. This system must:
    *   **Pressurization:** Maintain internal pressure equivalent to 5,000 meters above sea level *at minimum*. The reduced ambient pressure will stress all seals and respiration functions.
    *   **Humidification/Warming:** The air must be warmed significantly and infused with moisture immediately prior to inhalation to prevent rapid desiccation of lung tissue.
    *   **Particulate Filtering:** Filters must be rated for micron-level crystalline dust, capable of resisting abrasion from silica or whatever refractory minerals comprise the rock.

#### 2. Thermal Management (The Chemical Cold):
The cold is described as "chemical," implying it draws heat through means beyond simple convection or adiabatic expansion, possibly interacting negatively with biological thermal buffers.

*   **Multi-Layered Suit System (Cryo-Shell):**
    *   **Base Layer (Marrow Insulation):** A full-body graphene-weave underlayer designed to generate a persistent, low-level electrical charge. This generates kinetic heat deep within the system, stabilizing core temperature against penetrating cold.
    *   **Mid Layer (Vacuum/Aerogel):** A pressurized layer filled with inert, super-insulating aerogel or a vacuum suspension to combat conductive heat loss through the rock.
    *   **Outer Shell (Reflective/Absorptive):** A faceted, multi-angle outer layer designed to manage intense, angular light reflections from the white veins. It must be matte black or deep violet to minimize glare absorption, coupled with micro-heaters embedded in the boot and glove plating to prevent contact freezing.

*   **Hand and Foot Protocols:** Extremities will fail first. Use **Magnetically Heated Gel Insoles** and **Cryogenic Gauntlets** that maintain full dexterity for operating tools, while keeping the contact surfaces at a stable, low warmth (critical for interacting with the mineral fauna).

### II. Locomotion and Stability (The Vertical Nightmare)

The terrain is unforgiving: polished, fractured, and subject to potentially sudden, minor shifts in perceived distance or angle.

*   **Footwear:** Standard boots will slip on polished basalt and crystalline seams. You need **Traction-Lock Boots** utilizing micro-suction cups or piezo-electric adhesion, capable of instantly gripping slick, cold surfaces.
*   **Anchoring Gear:** Due to the overwhelming verticality and lack of obvious handholds, traditional climbing gear is risky. Carry emergency **Geological Spikes** tipped with diamond or sintered carbide, deployable via impact charge, designed to bite into the mineral matrix for sudden stability checks during environmental distortion events.
*   **Ascension/Descent:** Rely primarily on personal vertical propulsion systems (e.g., miniature, shielded thrusters utilizing inert gas). Ropes are hazardous due to sharp edges and the possibility of being severed by unforeseen fracturing events or abrasive wind patterns.

### III. Sensory and Perceptual Management (Dealing with "Weirdness")

This is the most crucial preparation. The Shattered Zenith breaks linear reality through light distortion.

#### 1. Visual Stabilization:
The warping of distance and the upward-pointing shadows signal optical corruption.

*   **Augmented Reality Visor (ARV):** The visor must have active **Geometric Correction Filters**. These filters constantly analyze reflected light angles against known environmental anchors (the horizon line, if visible, or geological strata planes) to generate a stabilized, non-warping overlay of reality.
*   **Infrared/UV Spectrum Scanning:** Rely on non-visible light to gauge true distance and structure, as the distorted visible spectrum is unreliable. Look for heat signatures or mineral resonance patterns to confirm the location of the seemingly static fauna.

#### 2. Navigational Redundancy:
Standard compasses and GPS will be unreliable due to electromagnetic interference from the mineral veins and the possible warping of spatial geometry.

*   **Inertial Navigation System (INS):** A high-precision gyroscope/accelerometer array is essential. Navigate based on measured physical movement and established vectors, ignoring visual input when discrepancies exceed a calibrated threshold (e.g., 5 degrees of angular shift over 100 meters).
*   **Echo-Ranging/Seismic Mapping:** Since ambient sound is unreliable, utilize active, low-frequency sonic pulses tuned to penetrate the rock structure to map the immediate surroundings and detect the subsurface presence of the dense fauna.

### IV. Interaction Protocols (Fauna and Artifacts)

The fauna are slow, integrated, and their interaction seems to be based on light/refraction, demanding extreme caution.

#### 1. Engagement Doctrine: **Passive Observation Only.**
*   **No Aggression:** Assume all static or semi-static "life" is either ancient, vital to the local stability, or too structurally complex to damage without triggering a cascade reaction in the surrounding minerals.
*   **Approach Limits:** Maintain a minimum distance of **five meters** from any clustered or embedded crystalline lifeform. Do not touch, scrape, or attempt to take samples unless the traveler is equipped with remote manipulation gear.

#### 2. Handling Artifacts:
The geometric intrusions (tetrahedrons, magnetic spheres) are potent indicators of vanished intelligence.

*   **Quarantine and Analysis:** Assume all artifacts are potentially phased, resonant, or contain trapped energy. They must **not** be physically manipulated by gloves or bare skin. Use remote manipulation tools (manipulator arms or focused tractor beams) for study. Record orientation, precise coordinates, and light/shadow interactions immediately upon discovery.

#### 3. Tool Usage:
The local fauna use primitive tools integrated with quartz. If you must scrape—perhaps to clear a path or analyze a surface—use **Tungsten Alloy Spatulas** with integrated dampeners, preventing vibrations from traveling through the rock structure. **Avoid using quartz or silica tools**, as they may resonate dangerously with the environment or the local lifeforms.

### V. Temporal and Psychological Preparedness

The extreme equilibrium implies a deep, philosophical stillness. This is the greatest threat to short-term sanity.

*   **Pacing and Time Dilation Awareness:** The environment encourages timeless observation. You must adhere to strict, artificially imposed schedules for rest, caloric intake, and environmental checks. Every cycle must be logged to prevent falling into the perceptual trap of eternal stasis.
*   **Caloric Intake:** Standard rations will likely freeze or become brittle. Utilize **High-Density Chem-Paste Supplements** designed to provide maximum caloric output with minimum mass, stored in actively heated compartments within the suit. Water intake must be meticulously managed—hydration is difficult in the absolute dryness, but ice buildup in the suit is fatal.
*   **Emergency Extraction Signal:** Due to the potential for spatial distortion, a simple long-range radio signal may fail. Program an emergency beacon to emit a burst of **Quantum Entanglement Signature** capable of punching through local space-time lensing, set to activate only upon suit depressurization or a catastrophic internal thermal failure.

You are not visiting a mountain range; you are trespassing on the exposed skeleton of a planet achieving mineral apotheosis.
