# Preparation Dossier: The Still-Glacier Scar (Zone Designation: P-SAT-2.4-WET)

## I. Existential & Psychological Calibration

The primary threat is **cognitive dissonance leading to systemic paralysis.** The environment actively violates expected sensory input (Weirdness 2.7).

1.  **Temporal Decoupling Drill:** Spend a minimum of 48 hours immediately preceding deployment in a controlled environment where visual, auditory, and tactile feedback is intentionally desynchronized (e.g., delayed audio feed, reversed tactile input). The Scar's localized time variance (columns un-collapsing) will feel like normal background noise if your baseline perception system is trained to ignore immediate causal links.
2.  **Acceptance of Non-Solidity:** You *will* be wet. You *will* sink. Cease the expectation of flat, dry ground. Visualize your footing not as solid earth, but as the deck of a slow-moving, semi-sentient barge constantly being re-stitched around your weight. **Do not attempt to leap or bound; high-impact actions risk rupturing the substrate violently.**
3.  **Noise Management:** The ambient noise (1.6/3) is a composite of biological expansion, atmospheric drag, and phantom machinery. Earplugs will be useless; they will only amplify the internal ringing of the *Echo Fields*. Calibrate auditory equipment to **filter for consistent frequency signatures** (i.e., the natural hiss of the cold vapor) and **aggressively discard erratic dissonance** (the phantom grinding). Train your mind to tag the industrial echoes as irrelevant environmental noise, like static on a dead channel.

## II. Environmental Survival Gear (The Wet Loadout)

The environment is cold, saturated, toxic, and structurally unstable. Standard issue is insufficient.

### A. Footwear and Traction (The Sucking Problem)

The 2.4 water parameter means the ground is a slick, saturated biological mat. Standard boots will create high suction forces when lifting the foot, potentially causing the user to lose balance during one of the systemic biological shifts.

*   **Recommended:** **Pneumatic-Assisted Exoskeletal Gaiters.** These must function to temporarily equalize pressure behind the foot when lifted. They should utilize slow-release, low-power compressed gas jets aimed downward and backward at the moment of lift initiation, counteracting the capillary adhesion of the moss/lichen mat.
*   **Surface Enhancement Drones (Self-Applied):** Carry canisters of a specialized, quick-curing, high-viscosity resin. If stuck past the knee, deploy the resin via automated spray nozzle to build a temporary, flat ‘raft’ beneath the trapped limb before attempting extraction.

### B. Atmospheric Containment

The air is thin (high elevation), cold (0.5/3), and rich in spores and metallic particulate.

*   **Breath Masks:** A dual-stage closed-circuit rebreather is mandatory. The primary circuit handles oxygen recycling for the cold, thin air. The secondary circuit must employ **electrostatic precipitation filters** specifically tuned to capture the carbonaceous particulate and heavy metallic dust residue associated with the ancient, absent industry.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** The pervasive cold demands comprehensive defense. Standard heating coils often fail in highly saturated environments. Utilize **Active Cryogenic Deflection Layering.** This involves an outer layer that actively pumps thermal buildup *outward* into the environment at a specific rate, preventing internal condensation from dripping onto equipment or skin, which would create instant, localized icing points.

### C. Illumination and Navigation

The dimness (1.4) is exacerbated by the constant, low-hanging spore fog.

*   **Active Bioluminescence Countermeasures:** The sickly green pulsing light from the plant-architecture columns is disruptive. Equip visors with **anti-phase filtering** tuned to the dominant green wavelength emitted by the flora. This turns the background glow into a uniform, dark gray, allowing for differentiation between the environment and the *true* dark.
*   **Active Survey Array:** GPS is unreliable in regions of high temporal flux. Rely on **Lidar mapping combined with Inertial Guidance Systems (IGS)**. Crucially, the Lidar must scan at an extremely rapid rate (multiple times per second) to capture the structure *before* a localized biomass heave occurs. Navigation must be based on the instantaneous map, not the expected terrain.

## III. Engagement Protocols (Dealing with Life)

Fauna is abundant but slow. The main danger is accidental interaction with the Seep-Slicks or being positioned under a gliding Carapace-Glider when it releases metabolic waste or performs a hydrostatic adjustment.

1.  **Seep-Slick Protocol:** If a glistening, amorphous sheet approaches, **stop all kinetic movement immediately.** Do not attempt to step aside; movement only draws their attention as a disturbance above their preferred grazing plane. Allow the low-yield magnetic/em fields generated by the turbulence to slowly push the Slick around you. If contact is unavoidable, apply a focused burst of highly concentrated, *dry* salt to the point of contact. The Slicks abhor desiccation of their immediate surface layer.
2.  **Carapace-Glider Avoidance:** They navigate the upper air layers via subtle pressure shifts. They are immense. Their metabolic slurry is highly corrosive. Use a **Geiger/Resonance Counter tuned to atmospheric pressure fluctuations.** When the counter detects the subtle pressure wave preceding a Glider’s descent or course correction, move immediately under the nearest substantial fungal-plant column. The column's structure provides temporary, solid overhead cover.

## IV. Dealing with Broken Physics (The Collapse and Revival)

The **Weirdness Factor (2.7)** demands specific equipment to mitigate structural memory and phantom forces.

1.  **Phantom Structure Mitigation:** The sensation of phantom I-beams rising from the substrate is caused by focused piezoelectric reactions within the stressed, mineral-laced growth.
    *   **Countermeasure:** Wear **low-level, full-body induction dampeners.** These generate a carefully phased, very weak counter-field designed to disrupt the crystalline alignment that causes the phantom structural pressure response. This won't stop perception, but it should prevent painful *physical* interaction with the echoes of machinery.
2.  **Temporal Contaminant Bags:** Never leave biological material or equipment exposed for prolonged periods, especially near visibly “un-collapsing” columns. The rapid reversal of entropy might reincorporate foreign matter. Carry all non-essential items in sealed, heavy-duty, **inert polymer bags shielded with Monocrystal Silicon**—a material known for its resistance to localized temporal distortion.

## V. Extraction Protocol

The Still-Glacier Scar is inherently hostile to sustained presence. Do not linger.

*   **Exit Vector Discipline:** Plan the shortest feasible path *parallel* to the underlying geological settling lines (the slow heaves). These lines, while unstable, represent the largest, slowest-moving chunks of the ecosystem, offering the most predictable path forward compared to the chaotic, rapidly reorganizing areas between the large columns.
*   **Decontamination:** Upon successful egress, the primary decontamination must be **thermal saturation.** Everything must be baked at extremely high, dry heat (above 150°C) to kill residual hyper-evolved flora and, more importantly, to ensure any adhering mineral/genetic compounds that might cause paradoxical re-structuring upon re-entry into a stable reality are completely neutralized.
