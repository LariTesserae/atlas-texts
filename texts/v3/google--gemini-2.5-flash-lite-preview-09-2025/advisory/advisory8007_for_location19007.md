## Preparatory Dossier: The Weeping Mantle of Krystallos

**Designation:** High-Altitude, Pervasively Saturated Continental Plateau (Code Name: Krystallos)
**Environmental Hazards Index (EHI):** Extreme (Focus on Biological Overgrowth, Atmospheric Density, and Subsurface Vibration)
**Recommended Duration:** Strictly limited; resource cycling is detrimental to external equipment.

This is not a destination for casual observation. Krystallos is an environment defined by the *supremacy of water and biomass*. Your survival depends on acknowledging that you are an invasive, temporary structure in a system dedicated to decomposition and aggressive integration. The concepts of 'dry' and 'clear' do not apply here.

---

### I. Psychological and Sensory Conditioning

The sensory input of Krystallos is designed to induce disorientation and malaise. Prepare for constant, low-grade sensory assault.

1.  **Light Deprivation Protocol:** You will experience near-total light deprivation punctuated by alien, shifting glows.
    *   **Action:** Acclimatize to Grade 4 Low-Light conditions ($<5$ Lux). Engage active retinal saturation therapy for 48 hours prior to entry to minimize sensory shock.
    *   **Equipment:** Equip primary visors with **Full-Spectrum Bioluminescence Filters** (tuned to counter emerald/cyan spikes) and **Low-Intensity Corona Dampeners** (to reduce glare from the distant industrial flares). Do *not* rely on standard daylight-calibrated optics; they will overcompensate for the gloom and induce blindness when crossing bioluminescent patches.
2.  **Auditory Burden Mitigation:** The soundscape is a relentless, multi-layered pressure.
    *   **Action:** Practice extended exposure to low-frequency vibration isolation chambers. The deep **thrum** is not just noise; it’s percussive, geological force transmitted through the Sable-Slate.
    *   **Equipment:** Utilize custom-molded, high-dampening ear protection augmented with **Bone Conduction Transducers**. These will allow you to selectively filter out the insectile *skitter* while prioritizing critical alerts transmitted via ground vibration (e.g., a sudden shift in the resonant frequency indicating localized rock instability or a major fluid pipe rupture).
3.  **Mental Stance: Embracing Subsumption:** The cognitive dissonance between the slow erosion of the rock and the instantaneous proliferation of biomass can cause temporal distortion.
    *   **Action:** Adopt the mindset of *ephemeral sediment*. Your goal is not to conquer or build, but to pass through without being assimilated *immediately*. Fight the instinct to rest or anchor yourself. **Stillness is the precursor to colonization.**

---

### II. Material Integrity and Environmental Shielding

The core threat is **Saturation**—not just hydration, but molecular bonding by corrosive, mineralized water and biological encroachment.

1.  **Atmospheric and Water Penetration:** The air is thick enough to feel like a light liquid. Standard seals will fail rapidly.
    *   **Suiting:** A **Type-III Sealed Biosuit** is mandatory. It must utilize **Polymer-Ceramic Lamination** with a self-healing nanite infusion system tuned to molecularly eject acidic biofilm. Ensure all external seams are reinforced with flexible, high-tensile **Silicone-Graphene tape**—the only material known to temporarily resist the aggressive adhesion of Krystallos mosses.
    *   **Weighting:** Due to the saturated ground and the need to resist biological anchoring, your mobility gear must include **Variable-Density Boot Assemblies**. These boots should allow for rapid changes to sole pressure (from low-pressure dispersal, like snowshoes, to high-pressure spikes for bedrock grip) to prevent getting permanently bogged down in the sponge-like moss beds.
2.  **Locomotion and Navigation:** Ground cover is deceptive; the water table is perilously close to the surface.
    *   **Navigation:** Magnetic compasses will be unreliable due to localized mineral veins and potential industrial interference. Rely exclusively on **Passive Terrain Mapping (PTM)** using integrated Lidar and thermal sensors tuned to detect the warmer hydrothermal flow areas (the weeping walls). *Avoid the warmest zones unless absolutely necessary, as this is where the mineral saturation is highest and corrosive action accelerates.*
    *   **Hazard Avoidance:** Mark all visible, non-photosynthetic structures (rusted pipes, alloy arches) as **Reclamation Zones**. Do not touch or cross these artifacts unless extraction is planned; they are biological melting pots and are likely hosting concentrated colonies of mobile fauna.

---

### III. Resource Management and Contamination Control

Bringing external resources into Krystallos is an exercise in rapid obsolescence. Everything will attempt to absorb or colonize your supplies.

1.  **Power and Electronics:** High humidity and electrical discharge from the industrial haze are constant threats.
    *   **Power Source:** Utilize sealed, **Isotopic Battery Arrays** in armored casings. Liquid-electrolyte batteries are prohibited. All external wiring must be sheathed in **Teflon-Boron Compound**.
    *   **Electronics Hygiene:** All sensitive equipment storage must be within **Desiccation Chambers** purged with inert gas (xenon or argon) and kept pressurized slightly above ambient conditions to physically repress moisture intrusion.
2.  **Hydration and Sustenance:** The omnipresent water is mineralized and biologically active.
    *   **Water:** Assume all local water is toxic. Bring vacuum-sealed, chemically purified stocks for consumption. **Do not attempt to filter ambient water** unless your equipment can handle heavy metallic particulates and complex microbial analysis simultaneously.
    *   **Consumables:** Pack high-density, dry-ration bars. Avoid anything containing sugars or slow-release carbohydrates, as these will attract fauna through slight atmospheric leaching.

---

### IV. Ecological Interaction Protocols

The fauna, though camouflaged, is adapted to this density. They move quickly across their own preferred substrates.

1.  **Fauna Evasion:** The abundant life will not recognize you as a threat initially, but as slow-moving, rich substrate.
    *   **Defense:** Standard repellent systems are likely ineffective against chemically evolved organisms. Carry high-yield **Acoustic Scramblers** tuned to mimic distress signals of dominant native predators (if known), or deploy bursts of localized, low-level thermal shock to deter immediate close-range contact.
2.  **Vegetation Interaction:** The Filter-Trees and massive moss beds are conduits.
    *   **Protocol:** Never linger near the bases of the colossal fungal-plants. They are the primary sinks for the environment's energy and nutrient cycles. Contact with their rhizomatic structures risks entanglement and rapid biological bonding. If contact occurs, immediately deploy a **Corrosive Bio-Dissolvent Spray** (high-concentration formic acid/enzyme mix) to melt the adhesion points, and move laterally, *never* attempt to retreat backward through the same path.

---

### V. Emergency Contingency: Extraction from Subsumption

Should you become immobilized by the environment (sunk in moss, entangled by root structures, or immobilized by biological adhesion):

*   **Priority One:** Immediate, full activation of suit micro-thrusters (if equipped) to break surface tension.
*   **Priority Two:** Deploy pre-charged **Thermal Detonation Charges** directly onto the adhesive matrices around your contact points. These charges should vaporize the bound water and instantly sterilize the immediate biomass cluster, creating a momentary zone of escape *before* the surrounding environment reasserts control.
*   **Last Resort:** If mechanical failure or complete biological envelopment occurs, initiate the long-range beacon and administer the rapid-acting **Dehydration Agent**. This will not save the life, but it will slow the rate of decomposition and integration, potentially preserving the core data storage for later retrieval by more robust salvage teams.

**Krystallos is a vast, living digestive system. Move with purpose, leave no trace that cannot be instantly recalled, and understand that every minute you remain, you are paying rent to the mountain in biological currency.**
