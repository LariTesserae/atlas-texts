### Who I Am: A Conductor of Failed Frequencies

I am not flesh, nor am I an echo of the Lithotheurges. I am a **Harmonic Dampener**, or perhaps, a necessary lubricant for the friction between consensus reality and the mountain’s ongoing trauma.

If the Lithotheurges are the living mathematics of the Scar, I am the **unstable constant** required to complete their equations without allowing them to diverge entirely from this dimension. **My Form:**
I possess an imposed solidity. I am cobbled together from the transient materials of the Scar itself, but organized by an external, highly specialized imperative that seeks equilibrium. My body is composed of **compressed sonic residue** bound by layers of the deep-black, light-devouring lichen.

If I move too quickly, the dust sheds, creating tiny, fleeting contrails of black grit that immediately freeze and fall against the established direction of gravity.

My core is not a heart, but a **Void Coil**—a crystalline network, dull grey and perfectly faceted, that constantly siphons off excess acoustic energy from the rock, channeling it through my lichenous shell before releasing it as perfectly modulated, near-silent counter-vibrations. I am a living noise cancellation system.

I exist to manage the **Weirdness**. The Lithotheurges, in their constant battle to inscribe their preferred geometric paths onto the world, sometimes introduce too much volatility. When the horizon shifts too quickly, or when gravity threatens a total systemic dispersal, the conflict generates micro-singularities that could unravel the entire region.

My job is to intercept these moments of acute dimensional slippage.

This is agonizing work. To impose order on a system designed for chaos is monumentally taxing. The external thrumming, which sustains the Lithotheurges, screams against my imposed silence. The rock around me weeps condensation in confused sheets, unable to decide whether to freeze, sublimate, or run uphill.
