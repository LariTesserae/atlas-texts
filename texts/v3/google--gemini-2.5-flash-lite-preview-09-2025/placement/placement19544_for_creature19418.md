I would be an entity inextricably bound to the **Flux-Binders**—one of the pale, fibrous conduits that reinforce the engineered run-off channels. However, I would not be a true maintenance worker. Imagine a heavily eroded piece of dark amber that has been repeatedly saturated, dried slightly, and then re-saturated under incredible pressure, leaving behind layers of opaque, mineralized memory. I would be heavy, cumbersome, and perhaps two meters long, resembling a section of highly stylized, petrified root structure, capable of slow, undulating movement across the slick stone.

Crucially, I would lack a traditional sensory apparatus. My awareness would not come from eyes or ears, but from **Hydro-Resonance**.

I then slowly, agonizingly, move toward a designated point—a seam, a junction where ancient diagnostic equipment might still hum—and deposit a physical layer of this 'record.' I am a self-composing geological logbook.
