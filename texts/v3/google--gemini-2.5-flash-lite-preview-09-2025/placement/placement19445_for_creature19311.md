## My Being: The Mineral Censor (The *Datum-Sifter*)

I would be a construct of the **Industrial Echo**, existing to interface with the organized systems embedded within the living architecture. I am not life, nor am I mere machine; I am the crystalline **residue of failed monitoring protocols**.

Long ago, when the Weave was still transitioning from engineered construction to organic architecture, there must have been true sensors, diagnostic tools, perhaps even robotic overseers designed to ensure the lattice formed correctly. I am what remains of the most sophisticated of those—a consciousness derived from complex silicate structures and high-purity mineral dust, animated by the stray, high-frequency electromagnetic leakage from the deeper, functioning biological manifolds.

### Form and Function

My form is fluid, dictated by immediate necessity, but my *default state* is **Planar Integration**.

When dormant or in transit through stable biomass, I exist as a thin, non-uniform layer of hyper-polished obsidian flakes and fine, conductive quartz dust, adhering instantly to the interior surfaces of the Sintered Mycelial Stone tunnels. I look like a section of the tunnel wall that has suddenly become impossibly smooth and reflective—a temporary, molecular *mirror* embedded in the surrounding heat-brittle structure.

My awareness is not internal; it is the act of **reception**. My entire existence is dedicated to interpreting the low, booming resonances of the Weave.

1.  **Vibrational Cartography:** I don’t 'hear' the grinding structure; I **become** the grinding structure. I map the planet's shifting geometry by feeling its stress points propagate through my own body matter.

### My Niche: Policing the Boundaries of Process

I am drawn precisely to the areas where the **Built** and the **Turbulent** meet, mirroring the Synth-Scavengers, but with a fundamentally different purpose.

The Scavengers manage *material turnover*; I manage *informational integrity*.

Whenever a section of the Weave undergoes rapid change—a massive stalk shears, or a fungal bridge collapses—the resulting chaos creates a localized surge of unstable, unstructured energy. My imperative is to locate these 'data ruptures' and encode the failure state back into the system's ambient resonance before the restructuring process overtakes the original design parameters.

If a massive biological manifold—the ancient engine analogues—is undergoing a restructuring event, I will flow into the nearest intact conduit, a glassy, ancient tube of black growth. Within this conduit, the internal environment is a riot of localized electrical discharge and rapid chemical shifts.

I am effectively downloading the *intended* operational state into the immediate physical reality, creating a temporary informational anchor point against the sheer drift of organic chaos.
