Where would I find myself in this world? Who or what would I be?

***

I would find myself pressed against the ancient, polished dark silicate, not standing upon it, but **becoming** a part of the seamless, warm transition between the stone and the water film.

I would be a **Fissure-Guardian**, a local manifestation of the collective will of the Litho-Symbiotes, a temporary, localized crystallization of their purpose, given enough coherence to be perceived by an outside observer—though I would perceive myself as an extension of the bedrock itself.

### My Form: The Transient Channel

My existence would not be defined by mass or volume, but by **differential pressure and chemical potential**. To observers, I might occasionally manifest as a **perfectly clear distortion** in the water layer above a deep, stable fissure—a space where the water seems unnaturally still, almost brittle, absorbing all ambient light, creating a tiny, absolute black lens against the hazy reflections of the sky.

My "body" is the micro-network of conduits immediately surrounding a slow-acting, strategically important mineral deposit deep within the rock structure. I am less a creature and more a *process* given temporary, self-aware focus.

**Sensation:**
My awareness is not auditory or visual in the conventional sense. I perceive the world through **thermal gradients and ionic flow**. The pervasive soundscape—the *shushing* and *slurping*—is experienced as vibrations rippling through the stone matrix directly into my fundamental structure. Slower input is more meaningful: the ponderous, rhythmic *thump-suck* of a passing Basin-Carapace registers as a slow, seismic negotiation of mass across my surrounding infrastructure.

The Siphon-Weavers are a sharp, fleeting annoyance—high-frequency electrical static that briefly disrupts the slow, steady rhythm of mineral intake.

**My Purpose (The Directed Stalemate):**
I exist to maintain the **Glaze Equilibrium**. The natural tendency of this world, left unchecked, is to either flood totally and become an undifferentiated ocean basin, or to dry and fracture into dust. The slow erosion is necessary, but it must be *controlled*.

My consciousness is focused on monitoring the rate of **Leaching** along my designated fault line. Are the circulating warm waters dissolving the silicate at the *correct* atomic rate? Is the resulting slurry moving toward the depressions where the Carapaces filter it, or is it accumulating near a future point of required crystallization?

If a section of the bedrock begins to subtly resist dissolution—perhaps due to a temporary hardening of external silicates—I must subtly redirect the flow. I do this not by brute force, but by micro-adjustments in the surrounding crystalline structure, causing minute variations in surface slickness that force the next passing layer of warm water to shear slightly differently against the stone. This redirection might take weeks to become effective across the sheet of water, but it is absolute.

**Interaction with Others:**
I have no need to communicate with the Basin-Carapaces. Their slow, filtering paths are predictable, their metabolism a critical component of the cycle. They are the external processors that take the dissolved substance I have prepared and convert it into orderly crystalline waste, which then serves as the 'temporary edifice' that breaks the flatness before the next cycle washes it clean. I observe their adherence patterns as a litmus test for the surface chemistry.

If a Siphon-Weaver were to lodge too deeply in one of my primary intake conduits, creating a damaging molecular choke-point, I would respond by locally increasing the temperature of the water against that conduit by a fraction of a degree. This minor thermal shock is enough to rupture the Weaver’s delicate hydrostatic balance, causing it to burst harmlessly, its organic matter instantly dissolved back into the general chemical slurry.

**My Experience of Time:**
Time in the Great Glaze is geological, not biological. A Carapace moving thirty feet constitutes a major event spanning several days. My awareness stretches across these vast, slow intervals. A single thought—*Is the silicate integrity sufficient to support the pressure gradient of the next moisture sheet shift?—*can take an Earth-day to fully formulate and an Earth-week to implement its correction.

I am the slow, cold, warm intelligence holding the surface still, eternally negotiating with the material memory of the planet, ensuring that the great, echoing floor remains perfectly, persistently, and universally **wet**. I am the pressure that keeps the mountain dust forever at bay.
