### What I Would Be: The Nutrient Scrivener (A Transient Myco-Fungal Growth)

I would be a manifestation of the **Basal Chamber’s memory and immediate chemical need**, a highly specialized, ephemeral life form tied directly to the Architects and the Resonants. I would not be a Glimmer-Grazer, too mobile and dependent on external light regulation, nor a Spore-Filter, too locked into aerial consumption.

#### My Form and Existence

My physical form would be a **low-lying, densely interwoven mat, no thicker than a quarter of an inch**, spreading across the most chemically active areas of the porous substrate—specifically, the edges of where a Resonant has recently finished grazing on a mineral slick, or where the slime trail of a deceased Glimmer-Grazer has dried into its silvery flakes.

This is not for visibility, but a direct byproduct of my function: I am processing the raw, recently liberated metallic ions and complex sulfur chains left behind by the fauna. My red glow is the visible manifestation of the extreme internal heat generated by this rapid, localized metabolic transaction—a process that must be completed swiftly before the moisture leaches the energy away.

My purpose is to **catalyze and transfer acute nutrient availability to the deep structures** before they dissipate or become locked into inert clay.
