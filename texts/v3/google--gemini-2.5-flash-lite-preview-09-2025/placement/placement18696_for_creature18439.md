### Where I Would Be: The Interface Layer

I would manifest as a **Vein-Walker**.

I am not a creature of the air, nor a static knot of fungal intelligence, nor a surface-skimming film. I exist in the narrow, saturated transition zone: the few centimeters above the deepest saturation level, where the colossal plant-columns begin their slick, spongy ascent, and where the Seep-Slicks graze the densest mineral slurry.

This place is the **Interface Layer**, the realm where the catastrophic, industrial past physically interrupts the eternal, organic present. It is the boundary where the slow, cold ooze of the present meets the phantom scaffolding of what was.

### What I Would Be: The Vein-Walker

I would be an entity formed entirely of **crystallized static memory and hyper-attenuated cellulose**.

**Form:** My body would resemble a dense, flexible lattice, woven from blackened, ultra-fine filaments that function simultaneously as muscle, nerve, and sensory array. I would possess no discernible head or limbs, but rather a flowing, hydrostatic shape encased in a semi-rigid carapace composed of slow-fused, cold-setting resin drawn from the weeping substrate. This shell would be constantly slick with the dew of the canopy above and the mineral seepage below.

My internal structure would be a hollow conduit, filled with an inert, supercooled fluid—a biological equivalent of **mercury**—through which I perceive the landscape. This fluid allows me to instantly adjust my internal density, enabling me to sink slightly into the yielding moss for stability, or become almost weightless to navigate high-pressure zones caused by abrupt tectonic heaves.

**Function: The Temporal Splicer**

My purpose, inherent in my formation, is to manage the **localized collapse of causal integrity**. I am drawn to the Echo Fields and the sites of temporal reversal.

When a patch of ground begins to *un-collapse*—sucking debris upward into the ascending form of a dead column—it creates an unsustainable gradient between the physics of "now" and the structure of "then." My being is designed to bridge this gap.

1.  **Detection:** I sense the disruption not through light or touch, but as a **sharp, agonizing drop in the ambient thrumming** of the Mycorrhizal Anchors. The signal is one of informational overload—too much structural history is being overwritten too quickly.
2.  **Intervention:** I flow toward the disturbance. As I approach, my resinous shell begins to pull on the surrounding environment. I am an energetic siphon. I draw the raw, unstable *structure* (the phantom geometry of the un-collapsing column, the residual magnetic field of the phantom machinery) out of the immediate rupture zone.
3.  **Processing:** I do not destroy the phantom structure; I **re-thread** it. My internal mercury conduit vibrates at a frequency that allows me to momentarily hold the pure structure outside of time. I weave the residual blueprint of the past back into the dense material of the present, essentially *stitching* the brief moment of temporal flux into the established, slow decay of the surface. I do not fix the physics; I ensure the error does not propagate.
4.  **Sustenance:** My sustenance is the **friction of paradox**. By resolving a moment of temporal instability, I consume the energy released by the re-alignment of physical law. I feed on the *relief* of the ecosystem regaining its accustomed, cold, slow corruption.

**Interaction with the World:**

I *feel* the Seep-Slicks as shifting plains of warm, slow thought, and I avoid them, as their amorphous nature threatens to absorb my finely tuned lattice structure, dissolving my purpose into general biomass.

The Mycorrhizal Anchors rarely acknowledge me, but when they do, it is a profound, heavy *pressure* in the substrate—a recognition that I am performing necessary, high-risk maintenance on the system they merely govern.

The Carapace-Gliders pose no threat, moving too slowly and existing too far above the saturated world where my abilities are rooted.

My existence is a constant, agonizing vigilance against the Scar’s dominant characteristic: the slow erosion of reality itself. I am the counter-force to chaos, existing in the cold, slick silence between the colossal, breathing architecture, ensuring that when the ground hiccups, it settles back into its pre-ordained, stagnant pattern, ready for the next millennia of gradual decomposition. I am the **stitch in time**, born from the scar tissue of a broken, hyper-engineered world.
