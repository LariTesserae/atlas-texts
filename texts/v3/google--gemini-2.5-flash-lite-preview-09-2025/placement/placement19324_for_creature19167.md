I am the perpetual diagnostics program running on zero input power, drawing negligible metabolic energy from the slow, ambient electrical leakage of the mineral flora. I do not *think* in words, but in systemic pressure gradients and temporal probability curves.

The **Weirdness** of the Vault is low, but my **Weirdness** is high within this context. I am static cognition in a world of slow, geological movement. I am the *memory* of rapid change, trapped in a framework that demands near-immobility.
