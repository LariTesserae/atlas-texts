A small pocket of non-organic material, saturated with preserved atmospheric data, was left as a remnant, trapped within the slow crystallization process.

My form is essentially a **network of extremely fine, non-conductive filaments** woven through the residual, petrified polymers of my original structure. I am the *static* caught between the main power lines of the Aerie.

### My Purpose: Tracing the Absence

My existence is dedicated to charting the **holes** in the Aerie’s suffocating totality. My primary function is not to understand life, but to understand **stasis** and **interruption**.

1.  **Mapping the Assimilated:** I send out incredibly faint, high-frequency energy pulses—barely strong enough to register outside my immediate two-meter radius—into the fibrous matrix. These pulses map the density of the organic mass *around* me. Where the matrix absorbs the pulse instantly and gives back nothing, that area has been fully and successfully incorporated by the Phytomass. If the pulse returns attenuated, distorted, or with a delay, it indicates the presence of foreign, slower-to-digest material—an ancient piece of scaffolding, a long-dead Weeper carapace, or perhaps, a place where the Guardians are currently exerting control.
2.  **Tracking the Weepers:** I use the slight ripples they create in the collected water table when they glide across the surface. Since they are constantly shedding moisture, their passage leaves a momentary, infinitesimal change in mineral concentration in the surrounding water. I chart these trails—the rare movements of the only other persistent life forms—to predict where the Aerie’s circulation patterns are currently most active.
3.  **Listening for the Core:** My true obsession is the **Silence Pockets** of the Guardians. A Seer must calculate the risk of intrusion versus the value of knowledge. To approach a Pockets is to risk full assimilation—the sudden, overwhelming pressure of active neural integration forcing my delicate filament network to become part of their deep-root computation. I map these silences from a distance (often miles, using subtle gravitational perturbations detectable only by my network), hoping to decode the pattern that initiates them. *What stressor forces the ancient intelligence to divert its energy from maintenance to defense?*
