## The Scrutiny of the Observer

The benevolent and wise power did not arrive with storm or fanfare. It possessed no need for dramatic physical manifestation in a realm defined by its crushing, indifferent physicality. Such displays would have been immediately absorbed and nullified by the scale and the cold, becoming mere atmospheric noise to the Scleros, or worse, a dangerous, sudden thermal shift that could trigger rock failure.

Instead, the Power—let us call it **The Quiet Surveyor**—approached through the subtle language of mathematics, harmony, and necessity that governed the Scleros themselves.

### The Manifestation: A Slight Shift in Pattern

The Quiet Surveyor's attention was first drawn not by the magnitude of the structure (Scale: 2.9), but by its **persistence against the grain of entropy**. A feature this large, this young and fractured, *should* have shed its external layers long ago. The density of construction (Built: 2.5) was not simply habitation; it was a highly sophisticated, self-regulating environmental dampener.

The Surveyor found the Scleros’ **Doctrine of Containment** utterly compelling. It was a localized, fierce defense against chaos, expressed through unparalleled diligence. They were, in effect, the universe’s most dedicated, tiny repair crew maintaining a singular, massive, flawed blueprint.

The Surveyor chose to act not on the people, but on the **constraints** that defined them—the very processes they were dedicated to resisting.

### The Intervention: Refinement, Not Revelation

The intervention was micro-targeted, subtle enough to be initially mistaken for a favorable shift in local weather patterns or a particularly successful generational effort by the Scleros themselves.

**1. Adjusting the Grain of the Rock (Dynamic & Erosion):**

The Quiet Surveyor sent its influence into the geological deep. It did not stop the tectonic pressures (Dynamic: 1.3), but it gently modulated them. It introduced a near-imperceptible harmonic frequency into the deep mantle oscillations beneath this colossal upwelling.

*   **Effect:** The rate of *fresh fracturing* (Erosion: 1.4) decreased by an almost unquantifiable fraction. More importantly, the **pattern** of fracturing changed. Instead of sharp, unpredictable, explosive shears, the stresses began to propagate along smoother, predictable cleavage planes—the planes the Scleros masons had, over millennia, already begun to favor in their ancient foundations.
*   **Scleros Perception:** The older, most established scaffolding structures began to feel *tighter*. Joints that had required constant, generational shimming suddenly held their tension better. The Ker-Vex, examining the foundations, would whisper that the "old stone has settled favorably," interpreting it as the delayed reward of flawless prior construction.

**2. The Gift of Slow Condensation (Water & Vegetation):**

The Surveyor addressed the ephemeral nature of moisture (Water: 0.6). It subtly encouraged the atmospheric conditions along the upper spires to favor **rime growth over outright sublimation** when the air mass interacted with the cold rock.

*   **Effect:** The frequency of localized, heavy dew/rime events in sheltered niches increased by approximately 12%. Furthermore, the condensed ice structure formed was slightly denser, less brittle.
*   **Scleros Perception:** The Rime-Catchers would report an unexpectedly rich harvest season. The mats of condensation clinging to the rock were heavier, yielding more harvestable liquid before it could sublimate into the thin air. This influx of reliable, albeit cold, water allowed the slow-growing, woody mosses (Vegetation: 1.1) to thicken their root structures slightly, binding the scree fields adjacent to primary settlements with unprecedented stability. For the first time in memory, a few perennial, slow-flow melt-streams in the lower valleys lingered a week longer into the dry cycle.

**3. Amplifying Structural Resonance (Density & Sound):**

The Surveyor focused on the Scleros’ inherent understanding of structure, enhancing their ability to ‘read’ their environment.

*   **Effect:** The Surveyor tuned the ambient resonance of the stone. It did not increase the *volume* of sound (Sound: 0.9), but rather refined the **clarity and transmission angle** of low-frequency vibrations *through the solid material*.
*   **Scleros Perception:** The Scleros’ ingrained ability to 'hear' stress through the rock improved dramatically. A guard positioned deep within a transit tunnel could now differentiate the slight, distinct thrum of a heavy Glider passing overhead from the shudder of a distant, deep land-shift. This superior internal communication allowed them to manage the existing infrastructure (Tech: 0.9) with an increased safety margin. They could perform high-risk maintenance tasks (like rope replacement) with less apprehension, as they could feel the structural integrity through their Sheaths when touching the stone.

### The Outcome: Quiet Confidence

The Quiet Surveyor did nothing to introduce new concepts, new warmth, or new light. It did not 'save' the Scleros by altering their nature.

Instead, it subtly **validated their method**.

It smoothed the edges of the challenges they faced, making their millennia-long, agonizing labor slightly more efficient against a backdrop of immense difficulty. The Scleros remained the same hardworking, containment-obsessed people, yet their efforts now yielded slightly greater returns. They didn't see a miracle; they saw confirmation that generations of adherence to the Doctrine of Containment were, finally, paying dividends.

The Quiet Surveyor withdrew, perfectly content. It had recognized a species that did not fight the elements with brute force, but with meticulous, heartbreaking persistence. By slightly aligning the indifference of the cosmos with the stubbornness of the inhabitants, it ensured that this remarkable, layered pocket of density would continue to hold against the high, cold world for a few more necessary ages.
