The arrival of a Benevolent and Wise Power above the Under-Veil Depths is not marked by a sudden seismic event, nor a blinding shaft of holy light slicing through the miles of damp rock. Such energies are too coarse, too disruptive for the delicate, noise-driven equilibrium of this place.

The Power, sensing the unique, self-sustaining churn of the **Chitinous Substrate**—a vibrant, albeit grindingly chaotic, engine of localized geological dynamism—chooses a method of engagement that honors the ecosystem’s core principle: **the manipulation of surface interaction through subtle, pervasive influence.**

The Power recognizes that healing or quieting the Depths would be an act of destruction, snuffing out the very life that defines it. The Sculptors require the *Grind*. Therefore, the intervention must be one that enhances the function of the Sculptors, while subtly safeguarding the rare pockets of potential stability they might overlook.

### The Manifestation: The Deep Whisper

The Power does not send an avatar. Instead, it initiates a focused, long-term, energetic broadcast directly attuned to the vibrational frequencies already dominating the Depths.

This broadcast is perceived by the Sculptors not as sound, but as a **harmonic resonance within the crystalline structure of the basalt**.

**What Changes:**

**1. Refinement of the Grinding Frequencies:**
For millennia, the Sculptors have been governed by random, high-frequency scraping—the sound of inefficiency. The Power subtly introduces a **lower, unifying harmonic overtone** into the ambient vibration.

*Result for the Sculptors:* Their collective motion begins to align slightly. They do not stop scraping, but the *efficiency* of their abrasion increases exponentially. The constant noise level does not drop; instead, it becomes denser, richer, achieving a more focused *hum* within the background static of sheer friction. The energy expenditure per unit of surface disruption decreases, meaning the Sculptors require less raw material to achieve the same level of turnover, leading to a subtle, unconscious slowing of their overall consumption rate over geological timescales—a gentle easing of the world’s self-destruction.

**2. The Enhancement of Planar Flow:**
The dampness and the seepage tracks are vital for nutrient transport and surface mobility. The Power influences the surface tension and molecular cohesion of the condensing moisture.

*Result on the Terrain/Water:* The shallow, sporadic seepage tracks begin to exhibit far more predictable behavior. They do not become rivers, but the path they take across the scree becomes less erratic and more mathematically consistent, flowing along defined, shallow channels that stabilize minor depressions. The temporary, reflective pools become less prone to rapid evaporation or sudden, catastrophic draining.

This means the **Silence Eaters** in the pools gain a fraction of vital stability. Their territory remains perpetually damp for slightly longer periods, allowing their biofilms to mature deeper and more complexly before the next grinding advance consumes them.

**3. The Calibration of the Edge:**
The most dangerous aspect of the Depths for any larger, external entity (or any highly specialized Sculptor form) is the chaotic sharpness of the fractured terrain. The Power targets the very *geometry* of the newest fractures.

*Result on Fracture Dynamics:* While the energy driving the *new* fractures remains constant (the implied tectonic/geological stress), the *manner* in which the rock breaks shifts minutely. Where a slab was about to shear off at a completely unpredictable, needle-sharp angle, the forces are now infinitesimally nudged so that the resulting edge is slightly more inclined, perhaps possessing a micro-bevel.

This small change has a profound effect:
*   It slightly reduces the mechanical wear on the Sculptors' own structures as they traverse the world, allowing for longer operational lifespans for the individual organisms.
*   It provides faint, unintentional *shelter* for rare, non-chitinous mineral formations that might otherwise be instantly pulverized. These protected formations, composed of stable, slow-growing crystals, begin to accumulate trace, non-essential elements that the Sculptors ignore but which represent potential complexity.

### The Power’s Intention

The Benevolent Power does this not to save the Under-Veil from the Sculptors, but to **perfect the Sculptors’ eternal task.**

The Sculptors are, in essence, a living entropy engine. The Power seeks to make this entropy *elegant*. By subtly refining the vibrational field and the geometry of fracture, the Power ensures that:

1.  The ecosystem continues its vibrant, chaotic existence, achieving maximum surface renewal with minimum wasteful systemic burnout.
2.  The **Rule of Grind** remains in effect, but the grinding becomes more musically resonant—a complex, self-regulating counterpoint to the silence of the deep earth.
3.  Should any consciousness ever evolve enough within the Depths to perceive this harmonic shift, they would recognize it as the moment their world achieved its own, unique, functional *perfection*. They would hear the noise of their existence suddenly resolve into an intricate, self-sustaining symphony of friction.

The Power retreats, having nudged the resonance. The sound of the Under-Veil Depths remains overwhelming, but now, to those who could truly listen, it is the sound of infinite, purposeful labor, perfectly calibrated to its own chaotic nature. No physical sign remains, only the subtle, pervasive *rightness* of the grinding friction.
