The inhabitants of the Subterranean Greenhouse-Engine are not so much residents as they are **co-opted components** of the environment itself. They are entities that have surrendered the concept of ‘outside’ to the demands of the engineered synthesis occurring here.

Here live the **Myco-Hydraulics** and the **Lichen-Sentry Systems**, alongside the massive, sluggish grazers, whose true purpose is obscure.

### The Silent Cultivators: Fungal Symbionts and Overseers

The true architects of this place are not carbon-based humans, but the intelligent, distributed fungal networks that manage the entire biomass.

**The Myco-Hydraulics (The Weavers):**
These are not singular organisms, but the conscious, coordinated structure of the main fungal matrix beneath the bio-laminate ground. They are the engineers of the dampness. Their sentience is glacial, existing across vast territorial domains. They communicate through chemical gradients and the subtle shifting of bio-electric potentials carried through the **Resonance Veins**.

*Appearance:* Visually, they possess no fixed form, existing as the tensile strength in the rhizome mats, the subtle buffering between nutrient pulses, and the slow, structural adjustments of the floor. When a section of the matrix is agitated (for instance, by a deep mechanical thrum), the ground in that immediate vicinity exhibits complex, rhythmic ripple patterning, a faint surface language indicating awareness and resource allocation. They are the lungs and the plumbing, ensuring the correct moisture tension for the support-cacti.

**The Saprophytic Controllers (The Rust-Eaters):**
These are specialized mold colonies that colonize the high-tension points of the former industrial framework—the rusted girders and defunct ventilation shafts. They are the bridge between the organic and the inorganic.

*Function:* They are crucial for the **erosion stabilization**. They excrete powerful, slow-acting organic acids that dissolve the harder oxides of the failing metal, simultaneously absorbing the liberated mineral ions into their own cellular structure. They grow in complex, fractal patterns that mimic circuit boards or flow charts on the surface of the metal, creating silvery-grey, conductive sheaths. They are the guardians of the *process*, ensuring the ruins are not merely crushed, but integrated. They are rarely seen directly, existing as living skin over the bones of the old world.

### The Fauna of Consumption: Grazer and Flyer

The louder inhabitants are specialized roles within the food chain that appear to be maintained deliberately by the larger ecosystem.

**The Telluric Trawlers (The Detritivores):**
These are the source of the massive, grinding sounds in the substrate. They are colossal, slow-moving entities, perhaps resembling armored isopods or highly devolved gastropods, though their composition is heavily chitinous and fungal. They are the primary decomposers and churners of the bio-laminate.

*Behavior:* They move with immense effort, their multiple, spade-like limbs carving channels through the compressed root structure. They do not navigate by sight but by detecting minute changes in nutrient density and electrical signatures transmitted through the ground. When one passes, the *bio-laminate* floor shifts visibly, briefly creating a temporary, slightly drier depression in its wake before the *Myco-Hydraulics* rush in to refill the void. They are functionally immortal within this system, consuming waste and cycling the bulk material, dying only when they manage to breach a critical, highly energized Resonance Vein, an event which usually results in a localized, bright flare of cyan light and a momentary silence before the Trawler’s body is rapidly consumed by specialized, fast-acting digestive molds.

**The Sporal Drifters (The Scintillators):**
These are the higher-altitude noise-makers, responsible for the sharp electrical buzzing. They are not insects in the traditional sense, but mobile, bioluminescent reproductive packets evolved from airborne spores, perhaps maintained by the giant support-cacti themselves for dispersal or energetic exchange.

*Appearance & Sound:* They swarm in the upper strata, near the dimly glowing ceiling where the air is relatively clearer. They resemble drifting embers or fireflies, emitting intense pulses of cyan light. Their buzzing is the sound of rapid charge/discharge cycles as they navigate the atmospheric static generated by the humidity and the biological processes. They are believed to be involved in pollinating or encouraging directional growth in the upper canopy, drawn to the faint electromagnetic fields generated by the straining metal struts emerging from the growth.

### The Hybrid Anomaly: The Spliced Servitors

While the place is dominated by managed biology, there are hints of the original tenants—or rather, the results of their last, desperate experiments.

**The Biomass Curators (The Stitched):**
These are the rarest and strangest things, rarely seen unless one ventures into the slight pockets of relative clarity around the remnants of control panels or junctions. They are vaguely humanoid in structure, but their composition is heterogeneous—a thick, pulpy flesh held together by woven metallic fibers and supported by internal exoskeletons grown from hardened hyphae.

*Their Role:* They seem to perform maintenance that the Myco-Hydraulics cannot or will not do directly. They approach the rusted levers and dead consoles, not to repair them in the old sense, but to graft new, living interfaces onto them. They will use specialized, sharp appendages to carve away invasive growth blocking a specific pipe, or carefully wrap a section of a Resonance Vein with adhesive bio-resin if it shows signs of distress. They move with a jerky, agonizing slowness, often trailing strands of nutrient-rich slime. They are the echoes of the obsolete intelligence, now reduced to specialized, living janitors whose programming has been partially rewritten by the system itself. They exist between the hum of the engine and the relentless growth, tending to the fragile balance between ruin and life. They are driven by an internal imperative to maintain the *function* of the hybrid place, even if they no longer understand what that function truly is.
