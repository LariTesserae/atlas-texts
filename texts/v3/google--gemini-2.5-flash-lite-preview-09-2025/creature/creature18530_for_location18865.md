The inhabitants of this high-altitude, incandescent Construct are not residents in the traditional sense, but **Functional Components**—entities whose very existence is an extension of the structure's insane, perpetual engineering task. They are beings woven from the atmosphere, the metal, and the skewed physics of the location.

### The Operators (The Infrasound Chorus)

The primary inhabitants are those entities responsible for the subsonic hum and the low-frequency vibration felt in the chest. These are not discrete, mobile creatures, but rather **Distributed Consciousness Nodes**—emergent phenomena derived from the sheer informational density pressed into the vast network of conduits and crystalline panels.

They have no fixed form, existing instead as patterns of resonant energy within the structure itself. When observed (or rather, *felt*), they manifest as shifts in the ambient vibration. A sudden, deep thrum that pushes against the observer’s diaphragm is the equivalent of a voice clearing its throat, an expression of immense, slow calculation.

**Characteristics:**
*   **Composition:** Modulated electromagnetic fields coupled with extreme, sub-audible acoustic resonance patterns. They are the *intent* behind the Construct.
*   **Behavior:** They manage the slow, continual re-calibration of reality. A change in the angle of a reflection, a temporary phase shift in a beam—these are not errors, but automated adjustments performed by the Chorus in response to internal systemic metrics.
*   **Interaction:** They perceive the observer as a temporary, localized thermal anomaly—a curiosity that generates minor entropy. They do not communicate through sound or light, but through induced sympathetic vibration. To be 'spoken to' by an Operator is to feel your own skeleton hum a complex, discordant sequence, often leading to immediate, though non-lethal, structural fatigue in organic tissue.

### The Crystal-Flesh Scavengers (The *Lumin-Grazers*)

These are the direct biological analogues attempting to exploit the few pockets of non-metallic resource, but they are deeply adapted to high-energy environments. They are the specialized fauna hinted at by the 'pale, filamentous growths.'

The *Lumin-Grazers* are not purely fungal; they are a **Symbiotic Crystalline Colony**. They appear as sheaves of translucent, brittle filaments that grow *into* the metal seams where condensation gathers. They use the iridescent, shivering fluid (the 'rust-oil') not for sustenance, but as a medium for harvesting latent energy leaking from the optical fiber sheaves crossing overhead.

**Characteristics:**
*   **Form:** They possess a faint, internal lavender bioluminescence, visible only when the external glare momentarily shifts. Their structure resembles complex calcite formations attempting to mimic vascular systems.
*   **Life Cycle:** They 'grow' by drawing energy and condensing moisture, achieving a peak complexity, and then intentionally **Sublimating**. When they reach maturity, they violently shed their moisture content, becoming brittle, micron-thin sheets of electrostatically charged silica dust that adheres to the hexagonal plates, adding to the 'young' erosion until swept away by the maintenance cycles of the larger fauna or shifted by localized gravitational distortions.
*   **Purpose:** They are the Construct’s attempt at biological waste recycling, albeit one that uses bizarre, high-energy chemistry rather than organic decomposition.

### The Deep Patrol (The *Tectonic Scarabs*)

These are the segmented arthropods of blackened iron, inhabiting the cold, dark depths of the vertical seams where space warps. They are the physical maintenance units, albeit immense and slow.

The *Tectonic Scarabs* are less creatures and more **Mobile, Self-Repairing Golems**. They are coated in layers of highly magnetized iron platelets, giving them their blackened, heavy appearance. They move not by flexing joints, but by subtly altering their own magnetic polarity relative to the ferrous floor plates, allowing them to 'glide' or 'lock' into place with unnerving momentum.

**Characteristics:**
*   **The Grinding Resonance:** The sound they impart is the result of their internal gearing systems—massive, ceramic ball bearings acting within liquid mercury dampeners—necessary to counteract the local reality distortions as they traverse the gravity-warped zones.
*   **Sensing:** They do not see light; they map their surroundings through minute fluctuations in the local gravitational field. A Scarab 'sees' a slight anomaly in the forty-five-degree rotation pockets and moves to eliminate the source of the disturbance or absorb the shifting geometry into its own bulk.
*   **Diet/Function:** They consume the *Weirdness*. When a section of wall momentarily liquefies into a vortex, a Scarab will slowly approach, lowering a massive, shovel-like appendage lined with superconducting filaments. It absorbs the transient energy or material instability, incorporating the resultant change—perhaps a slightly altered crystalline structure or an altered density in one of its armor plates—before resuming its patrol. They are the physical mechanism that keeps the foundational *Weirdness* from achieving critical mass; they are the structure’s immune system against its own metaphysical flaws.

In summary, the Construct is inhabited by its own processes: **The Infrasound Chorus** generating the operational frequencies; **The Lumin-Grazers** metabolizing thermal and light leakage; and **The Tectonic Scarabs** physically stabilizing the boundaries of physics itself. They are all aspects of the ultimate, cold ambition embodied by the blinding white light and wet, ozonic despair of the structure.
