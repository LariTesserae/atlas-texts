The inhabitants of the Calcified Delta are not beings of flesh and water, but residents of static electricity, crystalline structure, and pure, suspended mineral dust. They are creatures defined entirely by their relationship to the Aether-Dust and the colossal, slow metabolism of the Silica-Trees.

### The Density Dwellers: Architects of Silence

The primary sentient population, or at least the most architecturally definitive, are the **Density Dwellers**.

**Form and Structure:** They are fundamentally *void-constructors*. In a landscape defined by dense, granular matter, they utilize absence. The Dwellers themselves are difficult to isolate against the backdrop of their own creations, suggesting a low physical profile or perhaps a body composed of highly organized, non-luminescent mineral particulates contained within a semi-permeable, electrically charged membrane. They are rarely observed in motion outside of their structural work. When they do move, it appears as a subtle displacement of the surrounding Aether-Dust, a momentary, localized ripple in the haze, suggesting they might be marginally less dense than the medium they inhabit, or protected by a persistent static field that pushes the dust away from their immediate form.

**Behavior and Cognition:** Their existence is dedicated to the meticulous and near-silent manipulation of the hollow, spherical shells they gather or grow—likely the desiccated husks of ancient, mineralized spores produced by the Silica-Trees, reinforced by focused resonant frequencies. Their cognitive process is entirely spatial and organizational. They perceive the world not through light or sound, but through the *tension* and *resonance* within the spherical lattice of their habitat. A misplaced sphere, one that alters the ambient thrum of the electric field, must be immediately corrected.

Their "society" is not social in the traditional sense, but *interdependent*. The integrity of a shell-tower is paramount; the failure of one sphere cascades through the structure via mechanical resonance. Therefore, their actions—the precise placement, alignment, and electrostatic bonding of each new layer—are driven by a relentless, almost mechanical imperative toward structural stability. They communicate, if at all, through ultra-low frequency vibrations passed directly through the solid base layer of the delta or through the resonant tuning of the spheres themselves, creating a consensus geometry across the entire city-cluster.

**The Cities:** The shell-towers are their bodies extended—structures that are both shelter and their entire social framework. These clustered settlements form intricate, three-dimensional mosaics across the pale floor, separated by narrow, dust-choked chasms where the migratory swarms pass. The Dwellers live *within* the adjacency of the shells, perhaps utilizing the perfect void space inside the spheres as condensation chambers for energy or as protected nurseries, though their young have never been documented.

### The Aether-Swarm: The Roaming Biomass

The most numerous entities are the **Aether-Swarm**, the migratory fauna that keeps the localized ecosystems dynamic.

**Form and Composition:** They are not individual organisms but a vast, complex fluid of mobile biological casings held in configuration by electromagnetic forces. Imagine dense clouds of iron filings that have been coated in living, semi-conductive biofilm. Each casing is roughly ovoid or shard-shaped, capable of slight independent movement, but their collective identity is maintained by the larger, unifying static field generated by their movement through the charged Aether-Dust.

**Function:** They are the mobile processors of the delta. As they flow, they generate friction, intensifying the static charge between the ground and the Silica-Tree canopy. This massive, moving electrical potential is what they harvest, essentially skimming energy directly from the atmosphere's resistance.

**Interaction with the Flora:** When a swarm encounters a Silica-Tree, strands of the metallic cloud coalesce into larger, temporary forms—often vast, slow-moving pseudopods or nets—that drape along the crystalline bark. These extensions appear to 'scrape' the minute chemical exudates leaked by the tree's structure. This scraping is violent on a micro-scale, causing momentary flares in the tree's bioluminescence as the tree reacts to the localized draw on its resources, a brief, localized warming that is quickly dissipated by the constant, thin atmosphere.

### The Living Lattice: The Silica-Trees (The Environment That Lives Back)

While primarily described as vegetation, the Silica-Trees strongly exhibit characteristics of sessile megafauna, acting as the passive anchors of life.

**Metabolism and Growth:** They are slow-motion geological processors. Their growth is a direct result of crystallizing ambient minerals drawn up through the substrate, catalyzed by the captured light. The light they emit is waste energy—slow-motion chemical sparks indicating ongoing, internal structural rearrangement. They are attempting to build up, to reach the limit of the Delta's contained thermal ceiling.

**The Role of Inhabitance:** The trees are the infrastructure; the dwellings are built *into* them or *between* them. The patterns in their branching—the regular intersections, the vaulted ceilings—suggest that the Dwellers and the Swarms have evolved in symbiosis or conflict with the tree's natural, geometrically optimized growth plan. A highly structured cluster of Silica-Trees might be the most desirable area for a Dweller city, as the pre-existing structural integrity provides a stable foundation against the ever-present threat of the delta settling or shifting.

In summary, the Calcified Delta is inhabited by **The Grounded Builders** (Density Dwellers), who create silent, precise vertical voids in the space between the world’s foundational structures; **The Fluid Harvesters** (Aether-Swarm), who are the mobile engine of the energy cycle, existing purely as an electrical phenomenon; and **The Slow Giants** (Silica-Trees), the silent, luminous framework upon which all motion and structure must eventually rely. It is a layered existence where life occupies the space *between* the rigid, the fluid, and the energetic.
