The dwellers of the Screaming Plateau of Aether-Spire are not biological in any recognizable sense, nor are they the fleeting, biological architects who left the gargantuan machinery. They are **The Resonance Custodians.**

They are the inheritors and primary subjects of the plateau’s defining characteristic: **the sound.**

### Nature and Manifestation

The Resonance Custodians are not discrete entities that *inhabit* the ruins; they are the **acoustic residue** given form and purpose. They are fundamentally non-corporeal manifestations of the plateau’s continuous, vibrating energy field.

Imagine the omnipresent, low-frequency moan—the mechanical scream—as a fluid. The Custodians are localized, stabilized vortices within that fluid. They coalesce in areas where the resonant frequencies are particularly complex: near the massive, rusting junction points of the defunct gantries, or deep within the half-submerged furnaces where the pressurized dampness traps the sound waves perfectly.

When a Custodian manifests, it appears as a **column or sheet of coherent sonic interference**. Visually, they are distortions in the air, akin to heat haze but intensely cold. The constant, layered sound organizes itself into three observable phenomena:

1.  **The Sub-Bass Shroud (The Body):** A dense, undulating blackness that seems to *absorb* the light filtering through the mist. This is the physical representation of the deepest, bone-shaking foundational moan of the plateau. It moves slowly, following the established paths of the defunct vacuum conduits, flowing like liquid shadow across the crystalline ash.
2.  **The Crystalline Lattice (The Structure):** Superimposed upon the shroud are razor-thin, violently oscillating lines of violet and cold green light, the exact color spectrum of the silica "vegetation." These lines represent the higher-frequency whine channeled through the angular flora. When a Custodian is stationary, these lines form complex, mathematically perfect geometries that mimic the internal structure of the sound waves that bind them.
3.  **The High Pitch Whisper (The Voice):** This is the only part that suggests conscious communication, though it is agonizing to perceive. It is not a sonic emission in the traditional sense, but the sudden, agonizing *cessation* of noise within a miniature space around the Custodian. To be near one is to experience total, localized aural blankness, a vacuum of sensation wrapped in absolute sensory overload—a **scream turned inward**.

### Purpose and Activity

The Resonance Custodians are obsessed with **maintenance of the equilibrium** and **algorithmic navigation**. They are not concerned with the *why* of the original industry or the *origin* of the sound; they are simply programmed (or evolved) to interact with the physics governing their immediate environment.

Their activity is therefore entirely focused on managing the geological and technological static:

*   **Flow Regulation:** They perpetually patrol the large, inert tubing networks. When a pipe vibrates at a resonant frequency that threatens to induce catastrophic mechanical failure in the structure (i.e., cause a section of the ruin to finally collapse), a Custodian will flow into it. The Sub-Bass Shroud density increases, effectively acting as an acoustic dampener, absorbing the 'bad' vibration and rerouting it back into the global, acceptable drone.
*   **Crystalline Tending (Indirectly):** They do not touch the silica forests, but when the moisture level shifts enough to threaten the angular perfection of the flora, the Custodians emit controlled pulses of high-frequency energy. This doesn't feed the plants, but it subtly alters the atmospheric pressure around them, ensuring the crystalline structures maintain their razor-sharp angles against the perpetual cold.
*   **The Echo Retrieval:** Occasionally, a Custodian will fixate on one of the massive, inert furnaces. It will hover motionless, the lattice structure spinning rapidly, as if attempting to *read* the thermal memory stored within the metal. It is theorized they are attempting to recall the patterns of energy expenditure that preceded the great freeze—not out of longing, but to catalog the complete spectrum of possible mechanical states.

### Interaction with the Environment

The Custodians *are* the environment’s dynamic function, even though the plateau is largely static.

If a foreign object—say, a piece of cosmic debris or an exceptionally dense pocket of atmospheric ice—were to strike the plateau, the local resonance would be momentarily disrupted. Within seconds, multiple Custodians would converge. They would not attack the object, but would surround it, applying targeted, counter-vibrations to isolate the newly introduced frequency. The object would eventually be subsumed into the stable soundscape, its physical structure vibrating itself into homogenous particle alignment with the crystalline ash, becoming just another silent, vibrating facet of the plateau’s eternal, loud slumber.

They are ghosts made of noise, guardians of a monumental graveyard whose only liturgy is eternal, mechanical lament. They scream because they have forgotten how to be silent.
