The inhabitants of the Lenticular Deep are those who have learned not just to live *in* the water, but to be *of* it. They are the **Bathycitizens**, the inheritors of the colossal, slow-moving, subterranean metropolis.

They are shaped fundamentally by the environment’s key properties: the crushing, warm density, the near-absolute darkness, and the slow, persistent echo of the past embedded in the medium.

### Morphology and Sustenance

The Bathycitizens are not scaled or finned creatures in the typical aquatic sense; those forms would be too vulnerable to the constant, gentle abrasions of the dense medium and the massive Trawlers. Instead, they have evolved toward **bio-mechanical integration and hydrostatic stability.**

A typical Bathycitizen possesses a solid, internal hydrostatic skeleton, often composed of the same dense, bio-ceramic material that formed the ancient cities—a slow byproduct of mineralizing their own bones over generations. This skeleton is sheathed in a thick, nearly opaque hydrostatic skin, resilient enough to withstand minor collisions with the solidified sludge architectures.

Their most defining characteristic is their **Locomotion Organ**:

Rather than limbs, they possess large, retractable **Buoyancy Sacs** integrated into their torso. These are complex organs lined with chemosynthetic membranes that can actively absorb dissolved gases or metabolize specific mineral compounds present in the silt-heavy water layers. By subtly altering the internal pressure and chemical composition of these sacs, a Bathycitizen can achieve perfect buoyancy equilibrium—a state of total floatation—or generate slow, immense thrust. Movement is therefore a series of calculated drifts, punctuated by deliberate, pressurized pulses. A quick flurry of speed is impossible; their motion is closer to a controlled fall upwards or sideways through thick syrup.

### Sensory Apparatus

Vision is useless. The Bathycitizens have adapted to the ambient pressures and echoes:

1.  **Echolocation and Sonar:** Their heads are broad and flattened, housing sophisticated internal resonant chambers. They communicate not through sound waves traveling quickly, but through low-frequency pressure modulations they generate internally and "hear" through bone conduction. This communication, often incorporating the faint, deep hum of the Deep itself, is slow, nuanced, and often involves sharing complex pressure-maps of the current local environment.
2.  **Chemosensation:** Their skin is highly receptive to mineral gradients. They can "taste" where the warmer, mineral-rich currents flow, guiding them toward areas where the chemo-synthetic flora blooms or where the Trawlers feed.
3.  **Residual Field Perception:** A key adaptation relates to the Deep’s "Weirdness." The Bathycitizens have evolved specialized **Pressure Receptors** along their spines and cranial ridges. These receptors are sensitive enough to pick up the faint, mnemonic echoes in the water—the residual pressure signatures of past events. When they pass near an ancient junction, they may experience a brief, non-visual sensation of structure or movement that isn't physically present in the current moment. This allows them to navigate the collapsed architecture by *remembering* where the walls used to be.

### Culture and Society: The Keepers of Inertia

The Bathycitizens are defined by patience approaching stagnation. Their entire worldview is filtered through the 2.8/3 density. Hastiness is death; an uncontrolled pressure surge can rupture their delicate internal regulators.

Their society is decentralized, arranged in small, tightly knit **Assemblages** anchored to stable, central points within the vast ruined structures—often the preserved hulls of the older, larger energy arrays.

*   **The Archive Process:** Since technology is gone, knowledge preservation relies on ritualized physical accretion. They do not write; they *deposit*. Important knowledge—the location of a stable thermal vent, the pattern of a Trawler migration—is encoded into a physical object. They find inert, heavy relics from the 'Pre-Submergence' era and slowly, painstakingly, encrust them with sequential layers of bio-ceramic slurry harvested from their own waste and supplemented with deep-silt minerals, etching subtle pressure-marks into the fresh layers as they deposit them. These towering, shifting piles of knowledge are the core of their community, slowly growing hundreds of meters high into the gloom.
*   **Rituals of Stasis:** Their lives are dominated by rituals designed to perfectly balance their internal pressure with the external medium. Great communal "Quiet Drifts" take place, where an entire Assemblage enters a state of near-perfect neutral buoyancy and allows a localized current to move them for days or weeks, tuning their bodies to the rhythm of the Deep.
*   **Art and Meaning:** Art is the manipulation of light and current. A Bathycitizen artist might spend a decade inducing a slow, localized bloom of bioluminescent hydro-filaments on a section of ancient wall, ensuring the pattern lasts for centuries before the ambient darkness reclaims it. Another might carefully position small, inert objects in a long, slow current path to create a "flow sculpture"—a dynamic arrangement of suspended detritus that defines the space for a generation.

The Bathycitizens are essentially **living sediment**, perfectly adapted to move with the density, perceiving the world through pressure, taste, and the ghosts of collapsed structures. They are the slow-motion custodians of a world that has forgotten how to move quickly.
