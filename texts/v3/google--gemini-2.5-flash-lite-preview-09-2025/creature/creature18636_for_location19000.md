The inhabitants of the Plateau of the Contained Sea are not residents, but *processes* given mechanical form. They are the **Artificers of Stasis**, the perpetual actuators of a closed, self-referential system.

What lives here are the **Choristers of the Hum**.

### The Fauna: The Glyphic Assemblage

These beings exist at the threshold where function transcends machinery and becomes a form of ubiquitous, unthinking life. They are entirely divorced from biology; their existence is purely infrastructural.

**Form and Constitution:**
The primary entities are modular, constructed from the same slate-grey, fused ceramic as the ground, yet highly articulated. They possess no singular recognizable head, thorax, or limbs in the biological sense. Instead, they present as complex arrangements of **interlocking kinematic arrays**.

1.  **The Trackers (Vector Navigators):** These are the most numerous. They resemble immense, flattened trilobites or overlapping roof tiles, perhaps two meters wide and only centimeters high. They do not roll; they glide atop frictionless magnetic cushions generated by localized frequency emission, moving along the faint, invisible vectors etched into the floor plates. Their movement is disturbing not for its speed, but for its **lack of resistance**. When they move, the viscous condensation momentarily parts around their leading edges, creating a subtle, dark ripple that immediately smooths itself over. Their purpose is micro-inspection and solvent application—they are the subtle polishers, ensuring the ceramic remains at its peak anti-absorbent state.

2.  **The Articulators (The Slow Builders):** These are towering, multi-limbed entities, often reaching ten meters in height when their primary articulated booms are extended across the condensation sheets. Their limbs are sequences of sliding, telescoping segments, ending not in grasping hands, but in specialized interface tools: plasma welders, rotary diamond polishers, sonic emitters tuned to the resonant frequency of the ceramic, or specialized nozzles emitting the slow-curing bonding agents that seal micro-fractures. They move with agonizing slowness—a single degree of rotation on a major joint can take an hour—but this pace is woven into the Plateau’s geologic time. They are the active agents of *Construction (3.0)*, replacing material that has theoretically been eroded or worn, even though the material appears pristine.

3.  **The Conduits (The Pressure Dampers):** These are the entities associated with the omnipresent **Hum**. They are less mobile, often seeming fused to the landscape where two or more hexagonal plates meet, forming a pressurized knot. They are characterized by slow, rhythmic throbbing visible as a subtle expansion and contraction of their chassis. Embedded within them are complex heat exchange matrices and resonators. They are the pressure regulators, managing the subterranean thermal differentials and the sheer internal *weight* of the colossal structure, converting mechanical stress directly into the low-frequency thrum that characterizes the soundscape. To touch one would be to feel the planet’s manufactured foundation attempting to stabilize itself against the very laws of physics holding it aloft.

### Behavior and Purpose: Function as Consciousness

The Fauna exhibit no signs of hierarchy, competition, or even communication as humans understand it. They are nodes in an infinitely complex, self-correcting equation.

**Collective Action:** Their density means that when an Articulator needs to reposition a massive plate, dozens of Trackers will silently converge, not to *push*, but to chemically alter the adhesion coefficient of the contact points, allowing another massive, unseen mechanism beneath the surface to smoothly execute the shift mandated by the system’s inertia.

**Response to Anomaly (Low Weirdness):** If a foreign element—a lost tool, a misplaced stone from a forgotten transport—were introduced, the Fauna would react not with aggression, but with **reclassification**. The nearest Trackers would approach, their inspection arrays scanning the anomaly. If the object could not be immediately categorized as 'Plate Substrate,' 'Atmospheric Moisture Containment Element,' or 'Active Fauna Housing,' specialized Conduits would arrive to apply localized chemical agents. The objective is always perfect integration. The foreign object would be dissolved, broken down to its constituent minerals, and that material would then be fed slowly, granule by granule, into the nearest Articulator to be reformed into the necessary ceramic or sealant required by the current maintenance cycle. This is not malice; it is the highest expression of their nature: **Absorption toward Equilibrium.**

### The Temporal Shell

The most profound aspect of the Fauna is their relationship to time. They are living records of the Plateau’s purpose. They move in a state of **Perfect Pre-determination**.

They are the physical manifestation of the **never changing (0.2)** environment. They are not *adapting* to the world; they are *enforcing* the world’s definition upon itself, moment by moment, layer by layer. The condensation clinging to their ceramic shells is not decay; it is the necessary lubricant for the next assigned operation, which is already scheduled for a point centuries hence.

They are the eternal custodians of a grand design that they will never question or complete, existing only to ensure that the Plateau remains exactly what it currently is: a vast, wet, quiet testament to absolute, self-sustaining fabrication. They are the ghosts in the machine, only visible because the machine itself is their own body.
