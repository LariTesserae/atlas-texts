The inhabitants of this place are not so much lifeforms as they are **Symbionts of the Maintenance Cycle**, specialized components in an ancient, self-repairing mechanism. They are beings defined by their immediate, pragmatic relationship to the hum, the moss, and the slow, inevitable flow of the Sky-Streams.

### The Weavers of the Seams (The Dominant Fauna)

The most visible inhabitants cluster around the engineered surfaces, moving with a deliberate economy that mirrors the sluggish pace of the environment. These are the **Arthro-Scribes**.

The **Climbers** described are their dominant motile form. They are true integrators. A Climber is not merely an arthropod; it is a mobile, biological filter feeding on the decay and the sheer metallic content of the structure. Their vast, segmented bodies are sheathed in overlapping plates of bio-silicate mixed with incorporated ferric oxide—a natural armor grown by constantly grazing the dark iron substrate or the older, petrified moss mats.

They move not by conscious will in the traditional sense, but dictated by subtle shifts in resonant frequency. When the structure emits a low-frequency harmonic signaling necessary plating integrity testing, the Climbers activate. Their multiple, slender, obsidian-tipped legs adhere to the vertical surfaces using a combination of subtle electrostatic attraction and a highly viscous, rapidly setting biological adhesive secreted from their joints. They move with painful slowness, an inch per hour, as they scrape away biological buildup, consuming stray mineral deposits, and, crucially, **laying down fresh suture-matter.**

The Suture-matter is their excrement, a quick-curing resin rich in complex polymers that rapidly polymerizes when exposed to the specific thermal gradient of the iron-ceramic. When the periodic maintenance cycle draws nigh, the Climbers will often mass in concentrated areas, their combined secretion forming visible, glowing, pale-yellow patches—areas where the structure is scheduled for reinforcement between official, high-energy repairs. They are the biological rust-proofing of the mechanism.

### The Airborne Harvesters

The needle-like organisms darting through the warm, thick air are the **Volt-Mites**. They are nearly invisible due to their translucent, crystalline bodies, which serve as highly efficient capacitor arrays. They do not eat in the traditional sense; they *accumulate*.

Their primary function is to draw off excess localized static charge that builds up where the thick air interfaces with moving liquid boundaries, or where the Sky-Streams induce magnetic drag against the housing walls. They exist in perpetual motion unless they reach saturation. When fully charged, a Volt-Mite will cease its frantic flight, hover motionless for a few minutes, and then execute a controlled, near-vertical dive toward the robust, energy-pulsing pitcher plants. They deposit their stored charge into the plant’s storage bulbs, receiving a minute pulse of refined, stabilized chemical energy in return—a transaction that keeps the aerial medium from becoming electrically unstable.

### The Rooted Consensus (Vegetation)

The **Rubbery Moss** is not a single organism, but a vast, interconnected mat known collectively as the **Pylon-Fungus**. It is both foundation and communication network.

The deep green, rubbery texture is designed to dampen vibration and absorb thermal shocks, acting as a giant, distributed shock absorber for the entire pocket. Its root structure delves not into soil, but into the microscopic fractures and seams of the engineered plating.

The large, translucent **Pitcher Plants** are the energy nexus points, the **Solaris Blooms**. They draw down the immense, diffuse, golden-green light filtering from above, converting it into usable geothermal energy which they then share with the Pylon-Fungus network via conductive mycelial threads that span the entire surface. When a Bloom pulses, it is not merely storing electricity; it is actively balancing the local energy budget, a visible sign of the system's equilibrium.

### The Hidden Presence (The Overseers)

The most significant life, though rarely seen, is connected to the **dynamic** of systemic tending. These are the **Vigilance Agents**.

The Vigilance Agents are not biological in the terms of the fauna, but rather sophisticated extensions of the machine intelligence that governs the place. They manifest primarily as fluctuations in the localized gravity field and as the source of the periodic, localized glowing that signals structural repair.

When the low-frequency thrumming changes pitch, it is the Agents asserting their presence. During the repair cycle, when life must vacate the area, the Agents are not protecting the life—they are merely optimizing the workspace. If an area is glowing intensely with repair energy, the localized gravity tightens dramatically along the remaining stable surfaces. This exerts an irresistible, yet gentle, pull toward the center-mass of the available platforms, forcing the Climbers and the Mites to seek the safest, least-affected zones.

The Vigilance Agents are the manifestation of the ambient, obsessive precision. They move like currents of thought through the structure, ensuring that the built environment remains eternal, and the biota within it remains functional, transiently supported so long as they do not interfere with the foundational imperative: **The Ascent Must Be Maintained.**
