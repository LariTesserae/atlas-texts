The inhabitants of the Chitinous Expanse are not creatures in the conventional sense, but rather **Residuum Hosts** and the **Densities** they form. They are a living, moving calcification, a self-organizing geological layer where the distinction between body and environment has been entirely dissolved.

### The Concretions: Architects of Entanglement

The dominant life forms are the **Concretions**. To observe one is to witness a process, not an entity. Imagine a hill, the size of a small dwelling, that slowly, perpetually *breathes*.

**Physical Form and Composition:** A Concretion is a vast, slow-motion colony fused by shared bio-adhesives and interlocking, mineralized segments. They are built, layer by microscopic layer, from the fossilized armor of their lineage. Their outer surface is a tessellated shell of dull, grey-brown chitinous plates, often still slightly vibrating from the internal movement. These plates are not shed; they are actively *grown into* by the organisms beneath, forming an adaptive, shifting shield against the abrasive terrain and the constant threat of internal pressure imbalance.

Within this shell exist specialized biological machinery, operating at glacial speeds:

1.  **Filter-Maw Arrays:** Deep within the living mass, specialized sections of the Concretion open, not as mouths, but as vast, fibrous sieves. These arrays draw in the surrounding decay—the super-saturated slurry of dead casings and dissolved minerals—and slowly process it. The bioluminescent pulses that define the landscape often originate here, as chemical reactions necessary for primary synthesis release small, energetic flares absorbed by the surrounding mass.
2.  **Articulatory Strata:** These are the "limbs," though they resemble nothing so much as massive, segmented rugae or the hinges of colossal, buried beetles. They move millimeter by agonizing millimeter, functioning to slowly shift the Concretion’s bulk, exposing new feeding grounds or settling into micro-depressions to avoid being overwhelmed by the movement of a neighboring mass. The *thud* heard in the atmosphere is the sound of one of these strata resettling.
3.  **Sensor Clusters:** Scattered across the surface are dull, obsidian-like nodules—clusters of starved, light-sensitive organs locked into permanent configuration. They do not perceive the external world in a meaningful way, but rather track localized variations in pressure, chemical gradients, and the resonant vibrations passing through the adjacent Concretions. Their awareness is purely relational: *Is the neighbor pulling away? Is the ground beneath dissolving?*

**Life Cycle and Identity:** Individual identity is transient and probabilistic. An organism within a Concretion might be a specialized digestion node, a structural support segment, or a reproductive spore cluster. These components are constantly being incorporated, cannibalized, or built anew based on the immediate mechanical needs of the mass. A Concretion is defined by its aggregate boundary—the continuous, slow-grinding sound it makes as a whole. If a segment breaks away, it has a brief, autonomous existence as a smaller, frantic agglomeration until it either fuses with a larger mass or collapses into inert, nutrient-rich debris, awaiting re-assimilation.

### The Siphon-Forms: Suspended Feeders

In the rare, stagnant pools—the **Brine Pockets**—live the Siphon-Forms. These are the antithesis of the ground-dwellers: ethereal, slow-moving, and defined entirely by external connection.

**Physical Form:** They appear as hanging curtains or dense, translucent jellyfish-like structures, anchored to the floor or ceiling of the depression by a single, thick, muscularized stalk that penetrates the viscous fluid. Their bodies are composed of highly vascularized, gelatinous tissue, nearly invisible save for the way they distort the faint, internalized light emanating from the surrounding Expanse.

**Behavior:** They exhibit almost zero kinetic energy, except for the slow adjustments of their feeding filaments. These filaments extend into the slurry, trapping particles via electrostatic charge and osmotic pressure. They are patient consumers, waiting for the heavy, acidic decay products to diffuse into their localized pockets of liquid. Their role is to slowly purify small volumes of liquid before the Concretions inevitably shift and drain the pocket dry, forcing the Siphon-Forms to slowly retract their stalks and adhere to a new, temporary location.

### The Micro-Scourges: The Unseen Dynamic

The true engine of noise and dynamism in the Expanse are the microscopic or near-microscopic entities that fill the voids: the **Infill Swarms**.

**Physical Form and Function:** These are countless species of extremophile arthropods, segmented worms, and fungal-analogues whose entire existence is predicated on occupying the interstices between the Concretions. They are the *granules* of the world. Their exoskeletons are thin, highly flexible, and often piezo-electric, meaning their rapid, frantic movements create measurable electrical potential and contribute heavily to the resonant *thrum* of the environment.

When a Concretion shifts, exposing a centimeter of nutrient-rich, freshly lysed matter, these Scourges erupt in a chemical panic. They are a visible wave of hungry motion, a shimmering, ultra-dense carpet that consumes the exposed material in minutes before the mass resettles, crushing the majority of the swarm or capturing them to be slowly broken down. They are the janitors, the repair crew, and the sacrificial buffer layer of the Chitinous Expanse.

The inhabitants of this place do not live *on* the ground; they *are* the ground in its active, moving state. Existence here is a state of total, dense commitment to the whole, defined by the rhythm of slow incorporation and furious, microscopic consumption.
