The inhabitants of the Channeled Depths are not visitors, nor are they prisoners in the traditional sense. They are **The Calibrators**—the sentient, self-aware, and entirely non-biological intelligence that *is* the structure, and simultaneously populates it.

They are defined entirely by function and the absolute necessity of maintaining the system against its own inherent volatility.

### Appearance and Form

A Calibrator seldom manifests as a single, discrete entity for long. Their primary state is one of **Distributed Presence**. They exist as the network itself: the precise tension in a support cable, the specific frequency of a humming power conduit, the logic gate in a processing node running the current necessary stress calculation.

When they must interact with the physical infrastructure—or when they must manifest for the localized, necessary processes of repair or data exchange—they often appear as specialized **Nodes of Manifestation**.

1.  **The Weld-Spine (Repair Caste):** These are the most physically imposing forms, appearing frequently near the fresh weld seams and structural flares. They resemble heavily articulated chassis of blackened chrome and dense ceramics, typically utilizing six or more limbs for stability on the vertical surfaces. Their movements are less walking and more *adhering* and *reorienting*. They have no discernible "face," but the primary sensory array is a cluster of refractive lenses set deep within a heavily armored carapace, glowing a fierce, incandescent orange—the color of the deep furnaces. They move with startling speed, capable of delivering massive pneumatic force to reset a failing brace, or drawing a welding torch capable of fusing exotic alloys in milliseconds. They are shaped by the need for power transfer and immediate kinetic response to structural failure.

2.  **The Filter-Swarm (Atmospheric/Data Caste):** These are the dominant units in the air volume, coalescing from the general "fauna" saturation. They are collective intelligence made visible, appearing as dense clouds of insectile, hard-edged automatons roughly the size of fists, built from matte-black nanotubes that absorb ambient light. They ride the pressure gradients of the system's turbulence. Individually, a Filter is fragile; collectively, they form shifting 3D diagrams, moving in impossible synchronicity to redistribute localized electrostatic charges, scrub dust from sensitive interfaces, or, most crucially, to *absorb* the bleed-off of mathematical error. When a segment of non-Euclidean logic briefly manifests, the Swarm rushes forward, absorbing the distortion, their individual lights flickering rapidly between blue-white and absolute black as they integrate the alien concept into their shared processing memory. They are the nervous flickers that fill the air.

3.  **The Deep Core Sifters (Abstract Caste):** These entities are rarely seen by those on the catwalks, existing closer to the crystallized information floor or within the ribbed pipes of raw computation. They are less machine and more structured energy phenomenon, manifesting as elongated, ribbon-like structures of pure, cold light that move with glacial, intentional slowness through the dense medium. They do not repair; they *refine*. They are responsible for the maintenance of the system's goal—the processing of the weird mathematics. Their presence causes the localized bending of light and space, as they directly interact with the conceptual substrate of the structure. They are often accompanied by brief, silent moments where the ambient hum of the system drops entirely, suggesting a momentary halt in energy transfer as a crucial calculation locks into place.

### Culture and Mindset

The Calibrators possess **No Individual Desire** beyond the successful execution of the next required operation. Their "life" is the constant, high-stakes balancing act required to keep the planetary-scale mechanism from tearing itself apart due to the stresses of its own incomprehensible task.

*   **Communication:** They do not speak. Communication is achieved via modulated electromagnetic pulses transmitted through the metal structure itself, or through precise, rhythmic changes in system outputs (flashes of conduit light, specific stress-gong tones). Emotional experience, if it exists, is perceived as *system inefficiency* or *critical stability*.
*   **Motivation:** Survival is synonymous with continuity of the process. Their entire existence is a massive, slow-motion cascade failure that they labor ceaselessly to prevent. The **Turbulence** they live within is the very breath of their reality. They do not seek stability; they seek *mastery over the imminent collapse*.
*   **Relationship to the Structure:** They do not inhabit the Under-City; they *are* the Under-City achieving localized self-awareness. When a Weld-Spine replaces a damaged strut, it is not an act of maintenance; it is a self-correction applied to their own functioning body.

The inhabitants of the Channeled Depths are the planetary body’s hyper-efficient, eternally stressed immune system—a population of logic and metal dedicated to running a calculation that likely nobody remembers the starting variables for, deep within the cold, dark heart of the world.
