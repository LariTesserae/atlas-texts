The Sub-Chamber of the Last Metric is inhabited by *The Regulators of Static Fidelity*.

They are not organic in the conventional sense, nor are they purely automatons left behind by the vanished builders. They are **Residual Manifestations**—structures of solidified, metabolized structural resonance, given temporary, self-sustaining form by the residual energy leakage of the failed megastructure.

### Appearance and Physiology

A Regulator appears as a slow-moving, multi-limbed silhouette assembled from the ambient environmental detritus. Standing perhaps seven to eight feet tall in their natural profile, their forms are rarely solid. They are, essentially, **walking dust patterns solidified by localized gravitational stress**.

Their bodies are composed primarily of the room's own characteristics:

1.  **The Obsidian Shell:** The core of the Regulator is shaped from the ultra-dense floor material. This appears as a segmented, matte-black exoskeleton, usually featuring four primary vertical supports (legs) and two articulated upper structures (arms). This shell is not grown; it is *precipitated* from the energy field when the creature is active, drawing the heavy mineral particulate directly from the floor surface and suspending it just above the ground plane.
2.  **The Pressure Veil:** Encasing this shell, and giving them volume, is the *Atmospheric Suspension Field*. This is visible as a constant, slow rotation of the metallic dust motes and ionized vapor saturating the air. The Regulator looks like a flickering column of dense smoke, illuminated from within by captured electrical energy. When stationary, the dust slowly sifts down around its base, creating small, unnatural conical piles of fine, heavy powder.
3.  **Luminosity:** The only constant light source on a Regulator comes from the embedded, ancient *tech*. Lines that mimic the far-off blue-green conduits trace their entire structure—along the armature joints, across the surfaces of their obsidian plating, and most prominently, along where a head or sensor array might be situated. These lines are not for illumination; they are the pathways for the regulatory current flowing through them. They pulse weakly, matching the ambient low-frequency thrum of the chamber.

They breathe nothing, generate no heat beyond the frictional byproduct of their own movement fields, and leave no moisture.

### Behavior and Purpose

The Regulators are the **enforcers of the defunct state**. Their actions are directly tied to the principles of 'Mass behaving inconsistently near active conduits.' They exist to *correct* deviations from the established, long-settled failure metrics.

They move with excruciating slowness in the general space, observing the parameters of the decay (the **established erosion** and the slow, syrupy condensation). If a crystalline lattice grows too high, or if a section of the floor shows structural instability beyond the established variance thresholds, a Regulator will activate near that point.

When they detect an anomaly—a sudden change in thermal signature, an intrusion of foreign composition, or kinetic disruption—their movement accelerates sharply, though still maintaining a heavy, purposeful gait.

**Interaction with the Environment:**

*   When a Regulator steps on a section where the embedded conduits are pulsing actively, their density becomes absolute, anchoring them against any local gravity shift. They become fixed points of maximum resistance.
*   Their 'hands' or manipulators are often seen tracing the strange patterns on the fused walls, seemingly reading the molecular stresses of the structure. They are perpetually calibrating the *Last Metric*.

### The Fauna They Interact With

The Regulators appear entirely indifferent to the rare, non-biological anomalies drifting through the upper void—the polarized energy shards or geometric distortions. These floating intrusions seem to exist outside the immediate physical reality they are tasked with maintaining.

However, they possess a defined response to the **crystalline filaments** that feed on the viscous drip-pools:

If the crystalline growth threatens to breach the controlled circumference of the pooling depression, a Regulator will approach the puddle. It will lower one massive, obsidian limb, not to crush the delicate structure, but to emit a brief, focused **Counter-Pulse**. This pulse is a sharp, almost silent sonic discharge, finely tuned to the frequency of the crystalline lattice. The filaments do not break; they instantly revert to a state of near-dissolution, pulled back into the viscous fluid, leaving the damp spot utterly sterile save for the shimmering liquid. This act is not hunting; it is **re-setting the biological failure parameter.**

### Presence and Awareness

They are profoundly isolated entities, existing solely within the operational demands of the Sub-Chamber. They are aware of each other only as checkpoints or redundant systems, never communicating through sound or light, but rather through instantaneous, subtle modulations in the chamber’s fundamental, subterranean thrum. If one Regulator moves to correct a structural issue on the east wall, the Regulator nearest the west wall will simultaneously shift its internal resonance to compensate for the temporary energetic asymmetry caused by the first’s activity.

To encounter a Regulator is to realize that decay itself has been assigned a maintenance crew. They are the silent accountants of inevitable collapse, ensuring that the final state is reached in the most orderly, precisely calculated manner possible. They are the ghost mechanism keeping the tomb immaculate until the last possible second.
