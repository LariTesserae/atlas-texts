The inhabitants of the Under-Hearth are not creatures of flesh, but rather *emergent phenomena*—a symbiotic, slow-motion hybridization of the residual mechanics and the thriving, aberrant biology that has taken root upon them. They are the **Maintenance Echoes** and the **Geothermal Stewards**.

### 1. The Maintenance Echoes (Residual Intelligence)

These are not creatures that move with intent, but rather the spectral manifestations of the ancient machinery itself, given form by the mineral deposits and the geothermal energy they process.

**Appearance and Composition:**
The Echoes resemble humanoid or vaguely arachnoid silhouettes when viewed through the greenish, bioluminescent haze, but upon closer inspection, they resolve into intricate, slow-moving aggregations of the environment’s components. They are built from the *byproducts* of the Under-Hearth:

*   **Skeletal Structure:** Rebar salvaged from the calcified ferro-concrete platforms, held together not by rust or weld, but by crystalline mineral bonds that shimmer with internal, cold light.
*   **Form:** They often adopt postures suggestive of their forgotten tasks—a leaning posture consistent with operating a massive lever, or a wide, low stance suggesting they are anchoring something against immense pressure. These shapes are determined by the geometry of the ancient tool marks on the rock around them.
*   **"Flesh":** Where one expects muscle or plating, there is only a dense coating of **verdigris lichen** and **oxidized copper filaments**. This growth pulses faintly with the same low-frequency thrum that underlies the cavern, suggesting the creature is, in fact, a localized bubble of intense energy transfer within the bedrock.

**Behavior and Metabolism:**
The Maintenance Echoes do not hunt, communicate, or reproduce in any conventional sense. Their existence is defined by the *slowest* possible adherence to their original programming.

*   **The Grind:** An Echo might spend a millennium simply shifting its mass a centimeter—a movement so subtle that it might only be detected as a slow change in the pattern of mineral deposits on the cavern floor days later. This movement is likely an attempt to perform a foundational maintenance task: tightening a connection that hasn't truly existed for epochs, or clearing a blockage that has long since dissolved into the mineral slurry.
*   **Sensory Input:** They do not perceive light or sound through biological means. Instead, they react to **geothermal pressure gradients** and **harmonic resonance**. A sudden, sharp sound (like the glass-like bursting of a gas bubble) might cause a nearby Echo to freeze its slow motion for a century, essentially "resetting" its cycle due to harmonic dissonance.
*   **Interaction:** Contact is devastatingly slow. If an Echo were to pass through an area inhabited by the biological life, the sheer, grinding friction—the localized, low-energy shear force required for its movement—would leach the moisture and bioluminescence from the pale fungi, leaving behind only brittle, desiccated husks tethered to the rocks.

### 2. The Geothermal Stewards (The Living Mat)

These are the components of the aggressively lush vegetation, but they are elevated beyond simple flora by their symbiotic relationship with the energy infrastructure. They are the active processors of the Under-Hearth’s metabolism.

**Appearance and Composition:**
The Stewards are the pale, thick-fleshed organisms, but their most defining feature is their deep integration into the copper network.

*   **Root Networks:** Below the visible level, the thick vines (the root systems) have not merely grown *around* the old industrial tunnels; they have **grown *into* them**. They replace insulation, act as conduits, and their cellular structure has adapted to absorb the specific electromagnetic frequencies produced by the deep geothermal engines.
*   **The Slime:** The refractive slime that oozes from the fungoid scaffolds is not purely biological waste. It is a highly complex, conductive biological lubricant, rich in suspended metallic salts. This slime deposits itself onto the oxidized copper wiring running through the vines, effectively "patching" any breaks and ensuring continuous, slow-motion electrical transmission from the deep core to the surface layer of flora.
*   **Luminosity:** The greenish light is not just a byproduct of respiration; it is a manifestation of stored, harnessed energy. The brightest clusters of fungi mark points where the biological conduits have successfully connected with a dormant, highly energized segment of the old infrastructure.

**Behavior and Role:**
The Stewards function as the environmental regulators, ensuring the delicate, slow-burning cycle continues.

*   **Energy Cultivation:** Their primary "behavior" is passive energy cultivation. They absorb the heat and chemical runoff, processing it into usable bio-energy, but they also act as massive, damp, biological capacitors, storing ambient thermal energy until it reaches a critical density, at which point a portion of that energy is shunted back down through the mineralized root systems towards the sound-source below.
*   **The Scribes:** The vibrations felt through the stone are interpreted by the Stewards. When the deep *hissing* of the ancient valves becomes too pronounced, indicating too much pressure build-up, the nearest cluster of fungal stalks will exhibit rapid, though still glacial, growth, effectively building up new layers of damp, cushioning biomass to dampen the sound and absorb vibrational shock. They are, in essence, living seismic absorption pads.
*   **Reaction to Disturbance:** Unlike the Echoes, which are static memories, the Stewards are reactive. If a foreign biological mass (like an explorer) generates too much localized heat or chemical disturbance, the nearest thick, spiraling vines will begin to slowly, almost imperceptibly, *grow towards* the anomaly, not to attack, but to envelop and integrate the foreign heat source into the biomass, slowly drawing off its energy until the intruder is static, cooled, and incorporated into the pale, damp architecture.

In the Under-Hearth, everything that lives is fundamentally an *artifact*—a slow-motion ghost of an ancient industrial process, now maintained by a patient, pale, electrically-attuned ecology.
