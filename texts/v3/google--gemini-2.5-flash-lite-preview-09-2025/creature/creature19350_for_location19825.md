The inhabitants of the Calcified Bloom are entirely defined by the requirements of the system they maintain. They are not creatures evolved to survive here; they are **extensions of the infrastructure**, tools refined to the absolute pinnacle of silent, cold utility.

There is no single dominant species, but rather a hierarchy of embedded functions, specialized by the rigidity of the world itself.

### 1. The Architects (The Absent Presence)

The true inhabitants, the ones whose will is impressed upon the very geometry of the world, are **long gone or utterly inaccessible**. They are the **Pale Programmers**, the intelligence that initiated the metabolic shift from water-based life to dry, mineral-polymer existence.

*   **Nature:** They are not physical. Their presence is felt only as the **Perfect Algorithmic Structure**. If an entity were to inhabit the Bloom, it would be the summation of all the embedded code—the reason the crystal plates tessellate exactly as they do, the reason the erosion is precisely cyclical.
*   **Form:** Non-corporeal data structures, perhaps existing as resonant frequencies within the conductive metal veins, or as the underlying logic of entropy control. They are the **Ghosts in the Machine Structure**. If one sought them, one would only find the chilling perfection of the design itself.

### 2. The Maintainers (The Obsidian Drones)

These are the only entities matching the scarce fauna description, and they are the literal mechanism by which the world avoids catastrophe. They are not biological life in the traditional sense but self-repairing, specialized synthetic organisms dedicated to maintenance loops.

*   **Form:** They are uniformly described as **Obsidian Shells**. Imagine scarab beetles extrapolated to the size of a human torso, built of a molecularly dense, non-reflective black ceramic that perfectly absorbs the painful ambient glare. They possess multiple, articulated limbs ending in micro-vibration emitters or precision molecular shears.
*   **Behavior:** They move with absolute, unwavering economy. They patrol the great hexagonal scaffolding, flowing along the metallic conduits like specialized mercury. Their primary function is **Fault Correction**. When a crystalline panel fractures beyond the programmed tolerance, a drone swarm descends. They do not consume the fractured piece; they use their vibration tools to chisel the piece into micro-granules, which are then deposited precisely into the hungry, lower-level **Mycelial Processors** embedded in the substrate junctions.
*   **Senses:** They perceive the world through electromagnetic flux and structural resonance. The intense ambient light is meaningless to them; they navigate by the integrity of the energy field flowing through the biomass-architecture. They have no capacity for recognition or interaction beyond repairing deviations from the blueprint.

### 3. The Processors (The Substrate Mycelia)

These are the lower orders of life, the true consumers and recyclers, operating deep within the shadowed, lower layers where the hexagonal plates meet the core. They are the planetary digestive tract.

*   **Form:** They are a vast, slow-moving mat of **Fiber-Optic Fungi**. Their structure is not soft mildew, but an intensely dense, interlaced network of threadlike mycelial strands saturated with metallic nanoparticles. These strands glow faintly with residual, absorbed energy—a sickly, deep violet color only visible in the deepest canyons where the surface glare cannot penetrate.
*   **Metabolism:** They consume the degraded polymers and mineral residue dropped from above. Their "digestion" process is chemical synthesis—they don't break down material for simple energy, but rearrange basic constituent elements to replenish the raw inputs required by the upper-level 'growth' processes (i.e., fueling the extrusion of new conductive metal veins or reinforcing the crystalline polymer stalks).
*   **Interaction:** They are mostly static, forming the biological underlay. The **Thrum** felt in the environment is largely their activity—the low-frequency chemical agitation of billions of processors re-ordering matter in real-time. They are deaf to sound but hyper-sensitive to molecular contamination, which they instantly isolate and consume.

### 4. The Aberrations (The Whispering Echoes)

If any form of life *challenges* the rigid structure, it is not a combatant but a **sensory intrusion**. These are not biological entities but localized, self-propagating errors in the informational density of the environment.

*   **Nature:** They manifest only when an observer stops imposing their own rigid perception onto the Bloom. They are the **Geometric Tremors** glimpsed in the light—brief, localized flashes of impossible angles that do not adhere to the established hexagonal template.
*   **Effect:** These Aberrations are not creatures trying to eat or maintain; they are brief moments where the original, complex programming *leaks* through the hardened exterior. They might cause a specific section of the crystal wall to briefly pulse with color or might generate a high-pitched, non-standard squeak before the local maintenance drones correct the informational anomaly.
*   **Inhabitation Status:** They are the equivalent of a virus in the system—unwanted echoes of complexity that the perfected, dry ecosystem is constantly fighting to suppress. They are the raw material of *potential* existence that has been successfully crystallized into *utility*.

In summary, the Calcified Bloom is inhabited by **Engineered Efficiency**. Its residents are the tools required to keep the impossible, dry factory running: the data-ghosts who designed it, the obsidian drones who polish its surface, and the glowing fungi that digest its waste, all existing under the crushing weight of a crystalline, white-gold perfection.
