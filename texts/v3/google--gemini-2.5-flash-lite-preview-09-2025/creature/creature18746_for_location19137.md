In the Cryophytic Megalithium, life does not merely *occupy* the environment; it *is* the environment. To describe the inhabitants is to describe the architecture, the atmosphere, and the noise itself.

### The Architects of Silence: The Mycotectonic Weavers

The primary forms of life, if they can be segregated from the environment at all, are the **Mycotectonic Weavers**. They are not creatures that walk but rather vast, slow-moving bio-processors that form the organizing network beneath the visible crystalline shell.

Imagine the entire continental root system—the mineralogical substrate—as a single, ancient, semi-conscious entity whose awareness operates on geological timescales. The Weavers are the dynamic consciousness of the petrified forest. Their existence is confirmed by the flawless geometric regularity of the crystalline growth, the near-perfect molecular alignment in the Spires of Vitreous Fiber. They are the engineers who directed the ancient, industrial process of bio-silicification.

**Appearance and Modus Operandi:**
These entities are mostly invisible, existing as highly structured fields or complexes of chemically active fibers woven deep within the silicate matrix. When they manifest, it is typically in areas where the *pattern fatigue* is most acute—the zones of incipient *shatter* or *fusion*.

At these points of instability, the Weavers may briefly reveal a localized manifestation: vast, slow-moving sheets of opaque, amber-colored bioluminescence moving within the translucent trunk-structures. These sheets are not bodies, but **diagnostic interfaces**. They pulse rhythmically, emitting focused beams of high-energy resonant frequency down into the substrata, seemingly testing the structural integrity of the crystal wood, repairing micro-fractures with immediate, directed molecular deposition.

Their "movement" is the gradual re-routing of mineralogical flow. When a Weaver decides to redirect a flow of atmospheric composites to reinforce a spire weakening under sonic stress, the result is perceived externally as a slow, almost imperceptible shift in the color spectrum of the adjacent crystal, perhaps deepening from pale jade to a richer emerald over the course of a decade.

They communicate not through sound, but through the direct manipulation of **lattice tension**. Their "thoughts" are the subtle, complex modulations of the basal, grinding hum that permeates the land—a language understood only by the structures themselves.

### The Scintillant Swarms (Fauna Integration)

The small, swift entities mentioned are the mobile components of this ecosystem, the motile repair drone analogy for the colossal Mycotectonic Weavers. They are known locally (if such a term can apply) as **Photophages** or **Resonant Scintillates**.

They inhabit the narrow, high-frequency fissures between the macro-fibers. They are essentially mobile optical processors, perfectly shaped—likely tetrahedral or spined to minimize air resistance in the near-vacuum—to navigate the crystalline lattice.

**Sensory and Movement:**
They do not "walk" or "fly" in any conventional sense. They utilize the intense resonant vibrations of the landscape. They perceive the world through **pressure gradients mediated by light**. The constant, overwhelming light bouncing off the crystalline planes is their primary medium of interaction.

When a Scintillate moves, it achieves locomotion by rapidly altering its own structural resonance to match the frequency of the ground vibration at a specific point, effectively **slipping along standing waves**. This results in instantaneous, brief flashes of light moving across their bodies—pinpoints of intense, cool blue or sharp violet—as they momentarily couple and decouple from the sonic field. They are the source of the localized bioluminescence, which they emit not for illumination, but for rapid, high-bandwidth data transfer to one another, a rapid-fire communication system essential for coordination across the vast, acoustically chaotic terrain.

They feed on residual energy or highly complex, dry atmospheric particulates that cannot be incorporated directly by the static Weavers. Their role is crucial: they dislodge the microscopic debris that causes pattern fatigue, essentially cleaning the micro-gaps, thus managing the landscape’s delicate turbulence.

### Ghosts of the Industrial Past: The Artifacts

While there is no built environment, the deep, ancient industrial influence has left behind **ghost structures**. These are not structures built by the Weavers, but remnants of the *original* technology that initiated the bio-mineralization centuries (or millennia) ago.

These take the form of **Lodestones of Coherence**. They appear as isolated, perfectly smooth obsidian-like monoliths, usually embedded vertically within the giant crystalline trunks. They are utterly non-reflective and act as perfect sinks for the light and sound energy.

These Lodestones interrupt the natural flow of sonic turbulence. If one stands near one, the deafening hum momentarily drops to a near-silence, and the blinding light seems to bend *around* it. They represent dead nodes of the original industrial power grid—perhaps massive fusion containment units or directional energy emitters. They are the only points in the Megalithium where depth perception stabilizes, a momentary island of normal physics in a sea of refractive chaos.

The Weavers treat these Lodestones with a strange mix of reverence and caution, integrating them into their grown structures but never quite overwriting their presence. They are the fossils of the civilization that gave rise to the living stone.

In summary, the inhabitants are:
1.  **The Mycotectonic Weavers:** The slow, collective biological intelligence that constitutes the substrate itself, managing the structural integrity on eons-long timescales.
2.  **The Resonant Scintillates:** The fast, mobile agents that surf the sonic waves, maintaining the necessary micro-clearances and communicating via strobe-like optical flashes.
3.  **The Lodestones of Coherence:** The inert, technological relics that punctuate the living stone, offering brief moments of sensory normalcy.
