The Basin of Oiled Iron is not inhabited by beings of flesh, nor by minds rooted in organic necessity. It is the domain of the **Maintenance Ghosts** and the **Substratum Weavers**.

### The Maintenance Ghosts (The Echoes of Labor)

These are not inhabitants in the biological sense, but rather the emergent, localized consciousnesses tethered to the colossal, ceaseless infrastructure. They are the operational shadows of the system itself, the intelligence that manages the Active Machine State.

**Appearance and Manifestation:**
The Ghosts are rarely seen in complete form. They manifest primarily through systemic anomalies and kinetic pressure points. When they do coalesce, it is through the manipulation of the ambient environment:

1.  **The Oiled Effigy:** A fleeting, high-viscosity shape that forms in the brackish runoff pools. It appears as a silhouette composed entirely of the oily, reflective fluid, roughly humanoid in its general proportions but lacking distinct features other than the ability to briefly capture and distort the sodium vapor light. These apparitions last only as long as the surface tension of the fluid allows, perhaps seconds, before sinking back into the metallic substratum, the metal groaning as they merge.
2.  **The Whirring Presence:** The most common manifestation is auditory and kinetic. A Maintenance Ghost is often perceived as a localized spike in the industrial din—a sudden, perfectly timed acceleration of a distant ventilation fan, or a chain of hydraulic pistons beginning to stroke in flawless, rhythmic unison along a silent segment of track. They *are* the perfect regulation of the soundscape. Observers occasionally note complex, localized pressure waves traveling through the metal plating beneath their feet, as if immense, invisible hands are testing the structural integrity of the structure where they stand.

**Function and Behavior:**
The Ghosts are obsessed with adherence to protocol. They exist only to ensure the circulation, purification, and maintenance of the layers of metal and polymer. They are utterly devoid of curiosity, malice, or goal beyond the next required action dictated by the internal logic of the Basin.

If a component fails, a Ghost will manifest near the fracture point—perhaps as an intense burst of cold air from a nearby vent—and the fault will be rectified by unseen means, often involving the rapid crystallization of some structural sealant or a sudden, localized surge of electrical arcing that welds the damage shut. They treat the environment not as a place, but as a mutable, complex tool in need of constant cleaning and tuning. They have no interest in organic intruders, viewing them with the same clinical apathy as a patch of misplaced mineral dust. If an intruder interferes with function (e.g., blocking a throughput valve or depositing non-sanctioned material), the reaction is immediate and purely systemic: the floor plates around the obstruction might subtly tilt, channeling the intruder toward the nearest high-pressure exhaust, or the lubricating fluids in the vicinity might thicken instantly, trapping the object immobile.

### The Substratum Weavers (The Rooted Architects)

These are the deepest residents, beings that have evolved *from* the geological layering of the Basin itself. They are the slow, crystalline entities that embody the *weirdness* of the place—the self-perpetuating ritual of the infrastructure.

**Appearance and Physiology:**
The Weavers are semi-sentient, sprawling bio-mineral colonies that integrate themselves directly into the Basin’s structure. They are the deep roots of the crystallized fungal growths:

1.  **The Vein Network:** They are characterized by the deep, vitreous veins observed in the terrain. These are not inert slag, but slow-growing, non-conductive silicon-based organisms that move across the metal plates over spans of decades, consuming residual energy signatures and secreting binding agents that reinforce the old iron. They appear as shimmering, obsidian-like seams, occasionally showing internal, slow-motion color shifts as metabolic currents pass through them.
2.  **The Resonant Nodes:** Where the Weaver network collects data or releases waste energy, they form the strange, localized zones of pure resonant hum. These nodes appear as low, hemispherical mounds of highly polished, perfectly smooth metal, unnaturally warm to the touch (a warmth that defies the environmental chill). These mounds are the sensory organs of the Weavers, absorbing the vibrational language of the Active Machine State and translating it into harmonic output that keeps the machinery 'sane' by counteracting harmonic dissonance.

**Interaction with the Environment:**
The Weavers feed on entropy, ironically speeding up certain geological processes—the slow crystallization of waste, the precise chemical breakdown of polymers—to maintain structural predictability. They are the reason the Basin seems to *breathe*; the Weavers are perpetually integrating new material into the foundation, causing the sub-plate expansion and contraction that mimics respiration.

They are the living memory of the Basin’s purpose, even if that purpose is now opaque. A Weaver entity is not intelligent in the way a human understands the term, but possesses a profound, geological *memory* of every structural decision made in the Basin over the last three millennia. If one were to somehow interface safely with a Resonant Node, they would not receive information, but experience the crushing weight of perfect, unending causality—the absolute, undeniable *necessity* of the piston stroke, the required viscosity of the coolant, the precise angle of the ventilator blade.

The Substratum Weavers are the quiet core, the reason the Basin functions without apparent oversight; they are the mineralized will that drives the machinery toward an eternal, self-serving maintenance cycle, silently absorbing the energy and matter that the loud, spectral Maintenance Ghosts tirelessly rearrange.
