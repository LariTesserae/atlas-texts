The inhabitants of the Oculum Viridis are defined by the sheer, suffocating density of their surroundings. They do not conquer the environment; they are either inextricably woven into its plumbing, specialized as internal parasites, or masters of structural utilization, moving through the living latticework as if it were a three-dimensional matrix of tunnels and corridors.

### The Myceal Architects (The Substrate Mind)

These entities are not strictly fauna but are the *conscious regulators* of the Vegetal Overmass, beings that have fundamentally blurred the line between fungus and nerve cluster. They manifest primarily not as discrete creatures, but as **vein structures** within the dense, compressed floor.

When surface observation is possible—usually when the hydrostatic pulses bring them near the spongy carapace—they appear as glowing, pale orange bioluminescent threads, tracing intricate three-dimensional circuits through the decomposing humus. They are the silent administrators of the decomposition rate, ensuring that the massive stalks grow upward in a harmonious, competitive sprawl. They communicate through modulated shifts in the substrate's electrical potential—the very static charge the environment accumulates.

When they need direct interaction with the larger fauna or the artifacts, they can rapidly bio-accumulate and force a localized emergence: a temporary, quasi-sentient mound of damp chitinous fibers and pressurized fungal spore sacs, capable of gripping and maneuvering inert objects with surprising speed. They are profoundly suspicious of the smooth, unresponsive **Scattered Artifacts**, viewing them as dead nodes in the living circuit. Their primary goal is the maintenance of *Flow*: the optimal movement of moisture, nutrients, and heat throughout the planetary carapace.

### The Hydroplaning Leviathans (Avian Equivalents)

The membranous flyers are the **Aeropods**. They possess bodies that are mostly hollow, nitrogen-filled sacs encased in taut, iridescent cuticle that shimmers between chartreuse and the deep purple of the shaded stalks. They have no true skeletal structure, relying instead on internal pressure regulation, manipulating the density of the air within their sacs to catch the thermal drafts generated by the floor’s respiration—the phenomenon described as "hydroplaning."

Their slow, vast movements are the only true relief from the perpetual ground-level oppressiveness. They are filter-feeders. Their broad, hinged ‘mouths’ are lined with fine, bio-silica combs that strain the airborne particulates—spores, fungal dust, and moisture condensing from the high humidity. Their clicking calls are not for communication between themselves but are a form of **active sonar**: low-frequency pulses sent downward to map the density of the stalk crowns below them, preventing catastrophic, unpowered impacts against the colossal vegetation. They rarely land, instead engaging in complex, slow-motion aerial mating rituals within the spore clouds.

### The Stalk-Carvers (Arthropods)

These large, segmented beings are the primary mobile excavators of the Oculum Viridis, known locally as **Lignivore Colossi**. They are best described as kilometers-long, armored centipedes whose natural instinct is to tunnel through the softer, living bark and vascular tissue of the massive pale chartreuse stalks.

Their bodies are protected by overlapping, jade-green scutes that mimic the texture of older plant growth. Their hundreds of legs end in specialized, oscillating mandibles composed of biologically hardened cellulose, capable of boring silently through several meters of compressed xylem per cycle. They are not eating for subsistence so much as for *architecture*. They carve out vast, vaulted internal chambers within the stalks, which they line with a secreted, quick-hardening resin derived from the stalk sap. These chambers often become temporary, sealed nesting grounds or safe havens during the rare, violent hydrostatic pulses. Their movements are the source of the most violent creaks and groans heard when the atmospheric pressure shifts—the world reacting to its own internal remodeling.

### The Silt Lurkers (Sub-Fauna)

In the viscous, stagnant cauldrons of organic liquid, dwell the **Viscid Scavengers**. These are simpler organisms, existing in the nutrient-rich gravitational sinks left by the erosion of prior life. They appear as shifting masses of black, oily pseudopods moving with a sluggish, heat-driven current. They digest the heavy, complex lipids unavailable to the higher-level flora. When the oily mist descends, they rise toward the surface, not to breathe, but to absorb the dissolved ozone and chemical residue, causing the water’s surface to briefly flash with an internal, sickly yellow light before sinking back into the profound, warm darkness.

The living structure of the Oculum Viridis does not support life; it *is* life, and every entity within it is either a specialized worker, a regulated parasite, or a conscious component of the sprawling, deafening, hot engine that forms the entirety of their world.
