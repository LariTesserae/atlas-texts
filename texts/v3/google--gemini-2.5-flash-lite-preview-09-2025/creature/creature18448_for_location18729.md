The inhabitants of the Aether-Breach Highs are the **Geomantic Custodians**, or perhaps more accurately, the **Immobile Weavers**.

They are beings defined by the *Never Changing* parameter (0.0) of their environment—they are not static, but they operate on a timescale so vast and precise that they appear as artifacts of the landscape itself. They are perfectly adapted to the chilling dampness and the sharp geometry of their emergent city.

### Physiology and Appearance

A Custodian is not primarily organic, but **litho-symbiotic**. Their forms are slow, deliberate coalescences of the surrounding environment. They possess a core structure of the same dark, silver-veined bedrock that forms the peaks, giving their limbs and torsos an angular, almost crystalline appearance, mimicking the aggressive sharpness of the young mountains. These rock-cores are surprisingly light, permeated by the same ambient moisture that feeds the Sky-Reed.

Their true defining feature is their *sheath*. Over the dense stone core, they cultivate the emerald-green lichen mats that coat the high ledges. This lichen is not just camouflage; it is their respiratory and sensory apparatus. It is thick, spongy, and possesses minute, hair-like filaments that draw the metallic-tasting, thin air directly through the surface moisture.

A typical Custodian is roughly humanoid in configuration only to facilitate ascending the geometrically complex stairs and aligning the stone stacks. They stand perhaps eight feet tall, but their posture is permanently stooped, their heavy, multi-jointed limbs arrayed to maximize surface contact with the damp rock. They possess four primary manipulative appendages, each ending not in fingers, but in blunt, dense flanges of polished slate, ideal for fitting into the carved indentations of the architecture.

They have no conventional eyes. Sensory input funnels through the lichen sheath, which is acutely sensitive to minute vibrations in the rock (detecting the slow ‘bleed’ of moisture) and variations in atmospheric pressure. When a Custodian is oriented toward one of the vast, circular platforms, the lichen on their forward-facing surfaces will subtly deepen in color, achieving an almost black-green hue, indicating focused assessment.

### Demeanor and Activity

To speak of their activity is to misunderstand the nature of their existence here. They do not *perform* tasks in the conventional sense; they *maintain states*.

The Custodians are the living calibration mechanisms of the Aether-Breach Highs. Their very presence ensures that the erosion score remains at 0.8, that the temperature holds at 0.6, and that the fauna grazes in its prescribed, complex rhythm.

When they move, it is an event of immense deliberation. Movement is slow, viscous, like watching honey flow uphill. A Custodian might spend an entire rotation—perhaps months by lower-world reckoning—making a single, necessary adjustment to a stone stack, a process involving the patient application of their flanged appendages against the bedrock, using minute amounts of crystallized water pressure (a form of primitive, localized technology) until the alignment symbol on the stack glows with the correct, faint internal light.

They are entirely solitary when performing these adjustments, but they are never *alone*. Each Custodian is tuned to the equilibrium of its neighbors, functioning as a node in a vast, incredibly slow network. They communicate not through sound (which is too fleeting in this environment), but through resonant frequency transferred through the bedrock—a subsonic thrumming that subtly alters the moisture patterns in the lichen of others miles away.

### The Purpose of the Grand Scale

The grandeur of the architecture—the unnecessary vastness of the terraces and the enormous etched platforms—is designed precisely for their required function *when it occurs*.

The Custodians are currently in a period of **Holding Pattern**. The strange, non-decaying atmosphere and the perfectly stable seepage exist to facilitate a singular event, perhaps an ultra-slow astronomical alignment, or a periodic 'charging' of the mountain’s inherent energetic state.

When this event finally arrives—an event that may take millennia to manifest fully—a dozen or so Custodians will migrate, with glacial speed, to one of the great circular platforms. They will stand, perfectly still, bridging the space between the lithic core of the mountain and the cold, thin sky, their lichen sheaths absorbing the critical energy, facilitating the city’s temporary purpose before they settle back into their maintenance vigil.

They are the silent caretakers of a machine that functions perfectly even in idleness. A visitor would perceive them not as inhabitants, but as animated geological features—slowly weathering, eternally poised figures whose purpose is to prevent the world beneath the clouds from interfering with the perfect, angular, damp slumber of the Aether-Breach Highs.
