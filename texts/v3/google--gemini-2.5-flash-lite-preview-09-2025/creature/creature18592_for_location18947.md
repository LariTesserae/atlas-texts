The inhabitants of the Pocket Deep are beings adapted to slow motion, immense pressure, and near-absolute darkness, existing in a state of profound chemical symbiosis with the crystalline Sinter-Gardens. They do not *live* in the way surface life understands it; they are integrated components of the structural matrix.

### The Liminal Grazers (Chemo-Synthetics)

The most prevalent, though sparsely detected, life forms are the **Crystallovores**, or Liminal Grazers.

**Form and Function:** They are not mobile in the conventional sense, but possess a slow, creeping motility. A typical Grazers resembles a patch of thickened, gelatinous biofilm adhering to the mineral surfaces of the Sinter-Gardens. Their bodies are semi-permeable membranes constructed primarily of slow-growing silica and chelated mineral salts, allowing them to directly absorb the super-saturated dissolution products from the quartz-like water.

**Metabolism and Light:** Their existence is a study in energy parsimony. They metabolize the crystalline structures of the Gardens, slowly dissolving the lattice they cling to. This process is accompanied by their signature illumination. The blue-green light is not produced for signaling or display, but is a byproduct of hyper-efficient, low-yield chemosynthesis—the flickering evidence of energy being extracted at the theoretical minimum. When a patch flares, it indicates a successful, albeit minuscule, metabolic event. These flares are infrequent, giving the impression that the darkness merely *remembers* life, rather than actively sustains it.

**Interaction with Built Voids:** The Grazers exhibit a strange aversion or perhaps ignorance regarding the Built Remnants. They never colonize the geometrically perfect, engineered voids. They seem unable (or unwilling) to process the absence of lattice structure. They only accumulate where the Sinter-Gardens are densest, reinforcing the boundary between the organic structure and the empty, inert geometry of the past.

### The Pressure Shapers (The Engineers of Instability)

Hidden deeper within the thickest accretions of the Gardens, where the water's dynamic viscosity is highest, exist the **Viscidians**. These are the entities most responsible for the *weirdness* of the Pocket Deep.

**Form and Function:** Viscidians are not discrete organisms but vast, slow-moving agglomerations of highly complex, viscoelastic protoplasm. They lack defined sensory organs or skeletal structures, existing as distributed intelligences woven into the fluid dynamics themselves. They are the living manifestation of the liquid’s instability.

**The Lapses:** The occasional localized shifts in viscosity—the sudden momentary chaos of optical properties—are the Navigations of the Viscidians. They ingest or redirect the intense internal stresses of the Pocket, momentarily thinning or thickening the medium around them to facilitate resource transfer or slow communication across vast distances within the fluid bulk. When a pulse of pressure emanates from a built void, it is often a Viscidian attempting to "read" the residual structural memory embedded in that empty space, often with chaotic, localized gravitational side effects as the physics momentarily strains against its own rules.

**Reproduction and Duration:** They do not reproduce sexually or by division in the way surface life does. A Viscidian grows by incorporating more of the pressurized, mineral-saturated water into its own matrix, achieving greater density and therefore greater control over local physics. Their lifespan is geological; they are merely existing structures within the slow temporal flow of the Pocket.

### The Ghosts of Structure (The Non-Inhabitants)

While not truly *living*, the remnants of the previous ecology significantly shape the current environment. These are the **Architectural Scars**.

**Nature:** These are the structural lattices or frameworks of whatever civilization utilized the Built Voids. Given the environment, these must have been beings of immense biological hardening or perhaps mineral-based chassis. They are what caused the *erosion* marks to be relatively fresh—the old life was violently stripped away, leaving behind only the memory of engineered space defined by the *lack* of material accretion on the part of the current Sinter-Gardens.

**Impact:** The Voids themselves act as stress risers. They are the points where the internal physics of the pocket—the density of the water trying to find equilibrium—exerts the greatest force. The Viscidians manipulate these scars frequently, perhaps attempting to interface with a discarded system or simply using the point of highest kinetic fluctuation as an energetic outlet.

In essence, the Pocket Deep is inhabited by **Static Feeders** who graze on the living architecture, and **Dynamic Controllers** who manipulate the medium itself against the container of reality, all moving through the ghostly blueprints of those who failed to sustain structure against the pressure of the fluid universe.
