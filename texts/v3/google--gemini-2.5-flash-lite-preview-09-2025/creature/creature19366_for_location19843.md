The Veiled Spires of the Substrata are inhabited by the **Litho-Dwellers**, or as they sometimes refer to themselves in their clicking, dry dialect, the *Chitin-Grafted*.

They are not the builders of the sculpted chambers—those architects departed in an epoch before the grit settled to this final, abrasive density. The Litho-Dwellers arrived, or perhaps *emerged*, when the population pressures of the fauna became too great for the lower, wetter levels, forcing a desperate, upward migration into the thin, warm air of the Spires' peaks.

### Anatomy and Form

The Litho-Dwellers are creatures of forced adaptation. They are roughly humanoid in overall silhouette, though their bodies are slender, elongated, and profoundly asymmetric, built to maximize surface area for thermal exchange and to cling to near-vertical surfaces.

**Exoskeleton & Defense:** Their most defining feature is their **bio-mineral plating.** They are not purely insectoid, nor purely biological. Their outer layer is composed of segments of highly refined, chitinous material fused inextricably with the endemic mineral structures. Through a process of slow, internal secretion and external accretion, they incorporate dust, silica, and the green-glowing iron silicate into their carapaces. This results in armor that is light gray, often translucent in places, shot through with veins of faint, phosphorescent green that mirror the environment’s ambient light. This plating acts both as protection against the constant abrasive wind and as a crude, but highly effective, metabolic barrier against desiccation.

**Limbs and Locomotion:** They possess six limbs, organized into two primary modes: manipulation and adhesion.
1.  **Grasping Arms (2):** The upper pair are long and exceptionally dextrous, ending in three needle-thin, articulated digits tipped with hardened crystalline points. These are used for precision etching, tending the chemo-synthetic mats, and manipulating the phosphor hearths.
2.  **Clinging Legs (4):** The four lower limbs are powerfully muscled and end in broad, flat pads covered in microscopic, reversibly adhesive fibers—a necessity for traversing the polished stone shelves. When moving quickly, the sound they produce is less a scrape and more a rapid, adhesive *thwack-hiss* against the rock face.

**Sensory Apparatus:** They have no conventional eyes. Vision relies on sensing minute changes in light density and recognizing thermal gradients. Situated along their upper carapace are several clusters of **photoreceptive nodules**, small, dull, milky-white orbs that detect the subtle bioluminescence of the surrounding flora and fauna. However, they primarily navigate through **vibration and chemo-reception**. Their antennae, tall and feathery (by necessity, to filter edible particulates from the abrasive air), are hyper-attuned to the *Scrabble* of the swarm below and the deep *Grind* of the Spires, allowing them to predict structural shifts.

### Culture and Existence

The life of the Litho-Dwellers is an exercise in extreme material conservation.

**The Currency of Moisture:** They do not 'drink.' Water is a myth whispered about in legends of the deep chasms. Their entire metabolism runs on the careful, agonizingly slow catalytic breakdown of the chemo-synthetic mats—the "Lichen-Hides"—that carpet the ledges. Every action, every structural modification, is calculated against the potential for moisture expenditure.

**The Vertical Economy:** Their society is strictly stratified by elevation.
*   **The Lower Shards (The Scrabblers):** Those confined to the lowest, most stable shelves suffer the most from the encroaching insectoid tide. Their primary role is defense, maintenance of the outer grit filters, and harvesting the densest, but also most dangerous, nutrient mats. They are the most heavily armored.
*   **The Middle Carvings (The Etchers):** They manage the communal chambers, the phosphor hearths, and the fragile external communication trenches carved into the stone. They are the most technologically adept—though ‘technology’ means perfectly knowing where to strike a resonance frequency to crack a necessary vein of crystal.
*   **The Upper Vents (The Listeners):** A small, revered caste who risk the highest, most exposed ledges near geothermal vents. Their role is to monitor the *Flow*, predict shifts in the planet’s internal heat, and manage the air filtration systems that keep the habitable pockets from being immediately choked with dust.

**Architecture as Biology:** Their 'built environment' is their body projected onto the stone. A Litho-Dweller does not simply live *in* a chamber; they *become* the chamber. Chambers are hollowed out not for comfort, but for necessity—to create a thermal eddy, a pocket where the dry air stabilizes enough for their delicate internal metabolic processes to function without immediate structural failure. The perfectly fitted stones shoring up causeways are not mere construction; they are extensions of their societal memory, placed by ancestral hands and maintained by ritualistic sanding and re-sealing with crystallized waste.

**Interaction with Fauna:** They view the overwhelmingly dense fauna not as prey, but as a localized, necessary waste product, or perhaps a mobile nutrient source. They do not attempt to eradicate the swarm; they manage its flow. The incessant *Scrabble* is the sound of their world operating. Specialized tools are used to shepherd groups of the smaller chitin crawlers into designated composting pits where their slow breakdown yields trace metabolic byproducts usable by the Litho-Dwellers. Their fear is not the swarm consuming them, but the swarm *clogging* the entrance to a crucial pocket, cutting off access to a life-sustaining matte patch or a warm vent.

The Litho-Dwellers are beings of intense, necessary stillness punctuated by bursts of brittle, hyper-efficient action. They move only when the structural or biological calculation demands it, every scrape of their crystalline feet a testament to survival in a place where the very act of existing is a slow defiance against the suffocating emptiness of absolute dryness.
