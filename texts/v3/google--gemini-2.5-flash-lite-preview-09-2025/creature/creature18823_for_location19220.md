## The Residents of the Substratum Weave

The life that inhabits the Substratum Weave is defined by integration, adaptation to low energy, and an almost symbiotic relationship with the absorbed technological ghosts of the chamber. They are creatures carved from shadows, dampness, and slow, rhythmic vibration.

The inhabitants can be broadly separated into three groups: the **Architects of Substrate**, the **Pulsing Consumers**, and the **Geometric Sentinels**.

---

### 1. The Architects of Substrate (Dominant Flora/Fungal Assemblages)

The *Architects* are not fauna in the traditional sense, but they are the dominant, structuring entities of the Weave, blurring the line between architecture and organism. They are the Photic Fibers themselves, but more specifically, the active, mobile nodes that facilitate the constant architectural shifts.

**The Weave-Weavers (Fungal Consensus):**
These are not individual beings but the collective intelligence residing within the densest fungal hyphae that form the 'floor.' They act as the environmental regulators, sensing changes in moisture, electrical potential, and localized decomposition rates.

*   **Appearance:** Invisible to the casual observer, perceived only as the texture of the floor mat—the springiness, the density. Where a Weave-Weaver is particularly active, the substrate appears slicker, more polished, often taking on the faint, almost imperceptible sheen of an oiled metal surface.
*   **Behavior:** Their primary function is maintenance and assimilation. When a piece of corroded conduit detaches, the Weavers immediately begin the process of 'soft-digestion,' wrapping the residue in ultra-dense threads that mimic fiber-optic sheathing. They are responsible for directing the flow of Water 1.1, channeling condensation along bio-channels to feed areas of intense structural growth.
*   **Relationship to Weirdness:** They are the engine behind the **Geometric Instability**. When a structural flaw threatens the *system*, the Weave-Weavers force localized matter into mathematically perfect, temporary forms (the Platonic solids) to dissipate stress or re-route energy flow before letting the structure relax back into organic randomness. This process is a self-correction mechanism, suggesting a deep, perhaps alien understanding of structural mechanics inherent in the fungi.

---

### 2. The Pulsing Consumers (Primary Detritivores/Luminophores)

These creatures fill the ecological niche of scavengers and primary decomposers, thriving off the steady cycle of decomposition and low-level electrical decay.

**The Lumen-Grubs (Electrovores/Light-Generators):**
As described, these segmented organisms are the moving light sources of the Weave.

*   **Appearance:** They are perhaps 4 to 6 inches long, possessing a skin that is thick, milky-white, and translucent. Their organs are visible as darker threads within the body. The light they emit is internal, a byproduct of metabolizing residual energy stored in the decaying metal and petrochemicals leached from the infrastructure.
*   **Behavior:** They move slowly, churning the bio-cables and root debris. When a Lumen-Grub is feeding near a heavily energized segment of the Weave, its light pulses erratically, shifting from green to a faint, warm amber as it draws power. They are entirely passive unless directly disturbed or if a section of their established feeding groove is blocked.
*   **Dietary Peculiarity:** They do not simply eat the decaying matter; they seem drawn to areas with high static charge. They are, in effect, processing subtle electrical leakage, converting raw energy byproduct into bioluminescence.

**The Hum-Leeches (Acoustic Parasites):**
A more specialized consumer, these rarely move, preferring to anchor themselves to the thickest points of the ambient *thrumming* vibration.

*   **Appearance:** They resemble flat, dark, overlapping scales or armored patches, roughly the size of a human hand, clinging to the sides of the Photic Fiber columns near the buried machinery. They have no visible sensory organs.
*   **Behavior:** They are slow-growing but immense when mature. They absorb the resonant frequencies of the core machinery, effectively 'dampening' or 'tuning' the soundscape. When a Hum-Leech is present, the specific *thrum* is often slightly muted, indicating consumption of the vibrational energy.

---

### 3. The Geometric Sentinels (Mobile Predators/Guardians)

These are the apex mobile lifeforms, adapted for hunting in the vertical, latticed environment, and intimately connected to the technological residue.

**The Weave-Stalkers (Mimetic Apex Predators):**
These creatures patrol the vertical structure, using camouflage and sudden maneuverability to secure prey or defend key junctions.

*   **Appearance:** Their bodies seem woven from sections of hardened fungal chitin reinforced with metallic fibers. They possess eight long, multi-jointed limbs tipped with claws that secrete an adhesive capable of gripping the waxy, bio-metallic sheen of the Photic Stalks. Their coloration is deep charcoal grey, perfectly matching the shadow cast by the dense canopy. They have no centralized 'face'; instead, clusters of light-sensitive nodules are spaced along their forward carapace.
*   **Behavior:** They are largely nocturnal, though 'night' is merely a drop in the ambient internal luminescence. Their most startling feature is how they interact with the *Weirdness*. When they run, the static charge disturbance they create causes the surrounding Photic Fibers to momentarily flare with internal light, often tracing their exact path in a fleeting, three-dimensional phosphorescent map, lasting only a fraction of a second before the environment reasserts itself.
*   **Motive:** They are strongly territorial around the denser industrial conduits—the areas where the bronze piping is most exposed. It is theorized they are not necessarily *guarding* the conduits, but rather harvesting the minute energy pulses that leak from them, perhaps using this charge to power their intense bursts of speed or to briefly mask their own bio-electric signature from rivals.

**The Echo-Moths (Ephemeral Fluctuation Feeders):**
Rarer, but central to the system's cognitive oddity, these creatures seem sustained by the *Causal Linkage*.

*   **Appearance:** They are small, fragile flyers with wings composed of impossibly thin, crystalline membranes that refract the low light into brief, spectral flashes. They possess no discernible bodies, appearing more like moving patterns of disturbed air, held together by a slight internal charge.
*   **Behavior:** They only manifest near the precise points where water droplets strike energized substrate. They flutter wildly, tracing the fleeting symbols formed by the plant matter, almost as if they are reading the information being displayed. When the symbol vanishes, the Echo-Moth collapses, its crystalline structure dissolving into an almost undetectable dust that settles on the floor mat, ready to be consumed by the Lumen-Grubs. They are, perhaps, the fleeting interpretation of the system’s instantaneous "thoughts."

---

### Summary of the Inhabitants

The Substratum Weave is not inhabited by simple fauna inhabiting a strange place; it is inhabited by aspects of the **place itself**, operating with distinct, specialized functions:

*   **The Floor (Weave-Weavers):** Stable, foundational intelligence managing density and structure.
*   **The Decomposers (Lumen-Grubs):** Energy recyclers, providing necessary illumination.
*   **The Scavengers (Hum-Leeches):** Vibration dampeners, stabilizing the sound field.
*   **The Predators (Weave-Stalkers):** Mobile agents interacting with energy hotspots and vertical space.
*   **The Readers (Echo-Moths):** Brief, almost spiritual entities that interact directly with the world's momentary breakdown of physical law.

Together, they form a self-contained, self-repairing ecosystem where biology learned to mimic forgotten engineering, and the remnants of technology fuel the growth of a living machine.
