The inhabitants of the Under-Canopy Pocket are not beings who *live* here in the sense of building a civilization on top of the matrix; they are the **Symbionts of the Sealed Structure**, those who have embraced the bio-urbanism of the resin domes and the Raft-Moss floor as their essential shell.

Their existence is defined by the need to navigate extreme cold, low light, and the oppressive, damp density of biomatter. They are quiet, highly insulated, and intimately familiar with the tactile language of their environment—the subtle vibration of shifting Raft-Moss, the rhythmic weep of the Pneumatic Pillars, and the flashing grammar of the Glimmer-Swarms.

### The Keepers of the Resin Domes: The Calcifex

The dominant inhabitants, the original engineers of the city-within-the-stalks, are the **Calcifex**. They are squat, broad, and possess an extremely slow metabolism, perfectly adapted to the perpetual cold and poor resource turnover.

**Form and Biology:**
The Calcifex are vaguely humanoid in posture but are encased in a thick, multi-layered exoskeleton that appears to be a successful, self-cultivated hybrid of chitin and the surrounding resin secretions. Their outer shell is matte, the color of wet slate, providing camouflage against the darker, older patches of dead Raft-Moss.

They do not possess readily visible eyes; light is functionally useless to them. Instead, their primary sensory organs are **Chemoreceptors** woven into the fine, thread-like fringes that hang where ears might be. These receptors process the complex gradient of pheromones released by the Pillars, distinguishing between the benign methane sighs of healthy growth and the sharp, metallic tang of imminent structural collapse or fungal blight.

Their movement is profoundly deliberate. They walk with a distinctive, slow *shuffling* motion, a necessary adaptation to prevent disturbing the sensitive, floating Raft-Moss floor too violently, which would drown out quieter, more critical environmental signals.

**Technology and Practice:**
The Calcifex view the remnants of their woven structures not as buildings, but as **Metabolic Sinks**. They rarely add new material in the traditional sense. Instead, their architecture is maintained through highly specialized bio-sculpting.

Their tools are crafted not from forged materials, but from grown crystals—the same "ice-blue crystals" precipitated by the strange spore pods. They use sharpened, treated obsidian-chips chipped from crystallized sap for delicate work, but for the heavy maintenance of the resin domes, they employ specialized, slow-growing organisms they cultivate on their palms.

When a section of resin dome degrades, a Calcifex will affix a cultivated blister-sac of accelerated fungal liquefier to the area. They remain still for days, seemingly inert, while the organism digests the damaged shell, after which the Calcifex secrete a powerful, mineral-heavy slurry from pores on their forearms. This slurry hardens almost instantly in the cold air, effectively sealing the breach with new, bio-fused structural material. This process is their highest form of visible labor.

**Social Organization:**
They are solitary guardians, their settlements consisting of one or two fused domes. They do not communicate verbally, as sound is inefficient and easily lost in the damp air. Their proximity is acknowledged through complex, patterned tapping against the Pillar stalks—a vibration distinct from the ecosystem’s background noise. This "strike-code" relays information about water currents, spore count, and the activity of the larger, tunnel-boring fauna.

### The Hidden Processors: The Whisper-Lichen

Below the Raft-Moss, in the cold, mineral currents where the sludge flows, lives the shadow entity that keeps the Calcifex supplied with the materials needed for their crystalline tools: the **Whisper-Lichen**.

These are not truly fauna, but highly mobile, semi-sentient mats of extremophilic bacteria and slow-moving, multi-celled organisms that thrive on the raw, dissolving minerals in the moving water.

**Function:**
The Whisper-Lichen acts as the recycler and the silent supplier. They absorb the trace inorganic waste leached from the decaying Pillar matter. In return for the Calcifex maintaining the necessary pockets of warmth (often generated by housing small, heat-producing larval sacs within their domes), the Lichen excretes the necessary precursor chemicals for the Calcifex’s bio-cement and tool-crystals at designated collection points—small, porous vents at the very edge of the Raft-Moss, where the structure meets the flowing water.

The Lichen communicate through minute, electrical discharges within the water itself. When the Calcifex communicate via the Pillar-tapping, the Lichen respond by causing specific, localized spots on the Raft-Moss floor to *dim slightly* in their inherent bioluminescence—a signal that the chemical payment has been made and is ready for collection.

To observe the Calcifex and the Lichen is to witness a civilization that has given up the messy complexities of construction, exchange, and war, settling instead into a perfect, cold, mutually dependent stasis, entirely wrapped within the colossal, indifferent body of the Under-Canopy Pocket. They are simply the slow, quiet custodians of a biological ruin they have learned to treat as their living sanctuary.
