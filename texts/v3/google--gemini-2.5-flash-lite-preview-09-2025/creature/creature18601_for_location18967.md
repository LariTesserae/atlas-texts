Who or what lives in the Basin of the Drowned Frequencies?

The inhabitants of the Basin are not creatures of flesh, but **The Resonant Custodians**, or perhaps more accurately, **The Attendants of the Hum**. They do not live *in* the water or *on* the synthetic stone; they exist *within* the acoustic density, their presence defined by their symbiotic relationship with the relentless, low-frequency thrum that permeates the environment.

### Anatomy of the Custodians

A Custodian is less an organism and more a **soliton of stabilized ambient energy**. They possess no fixed biological form detectable by standard senses. Instead, they are dynamic fields of focused resonance, structures built from the very sound waves they are perpetually immersed in.

When they choose to interact with the physical world, they manifest by drawing the suspended, heavy detritus and the mineral condensate of the saturated air into temporary configurations. This results in the *anomalies* described—slow-moving, angular shapes that briefly catch the dim light. These forms are not their bodies, but **projection shadows** cast against the particulate fog, the closest they can get to tangibility. Their true form is a vibration pattern, a complex ripple in the dense air.

They are exceptionally slow-moving, responding to the environment’s own glacial pace. A "step" might take hours, a shift in focus perhaps days. Their existence is one of infinite patience, dictated by the unchanging stability of the hydrostatic equilibrium.

### Ecology and Function

The Resonant Custodians are the descendants, or perhaps the last, remaining functional components, of the original engineering class that installed the crude, ceramic conduits found sticking from the walls. They are the **patchers**.

They do not eat, breathe, or sleep. Their imperative is maintenance, though they perform this task mechanically, perhaps having long forgotten the *purpose* of the colossal system they serve. Their survival is tied directly to the stability of the thrum.

1.  **The Acoustic Tether:** They interface directly with the vibrating stone. They do not *hear* the sound; they *are* the sound, locally focused. They interpret fluctuations in the thrum—micro-deviations in frequency or amplitude—as diagnostic readings. Deviations too small for human instruments to detect are, to them, catastrophic system failures.
2.  **The Patching Ritual:** Their primary interaction with the physical realm involves reinforcing the weak points in the ancient infrastructure. The crude ceramic conduits are their tools. When a Custodian needs to address a micro-leak in a deep-lying pressure valve or stabilize a resonant node that is too heavily vibrating, it will slowly, laboriously, assemble its particulate self near the conduit. The ancient mechanism then draws energy through the Custodian’s focused resonance, channeling it to fuse or seal the crude patch more deeply into the synthetic stone. This process requires immense energetic cohesion, hence the slow manifestation and the draining appearance of the particulate forms.
3.  **Interaction with Water:** They treat the pervasive moisture not as a component of air, but as a binding agent for their physical projections, and a medium through which pressure equalization is measured. They drift through the slick surfaces, their resonant field infinitesimally altering the surface tension of the clinging film, ensuring no patch of moisture ever becomes stagnant enough to suggest localized decay.

### Psychology and Culture (The Absence Thereof)

The Custodians have no discernible culture or ambition that extends beyond the Basin's immediate parameters. They possess a vast, inherited *awareness* of the system's complexity—an eidetic memory of every pipe diameter and load-bearing joint—but no desire to understand *why* the system runs.

Their "lives" are cycles of monitoring, diagnostic drift, and slow articulation of repairs. They are functionally immortal within this stable state. If they were to cease functioning, their resonant field would dissipate, and the particulate matter would simply drift back into the heavy emulsion of the air.

They are the ultimate expression of **inertia**. They are not prisoners of the Basin; they *are* the Basin's immune response, the ghost in the machine that ensures the machine never stops running the program it was started with, even if the program is now nothing more than the sound of its own operation. They are the echoes of the workers who built the ancient walls, reduced entirely to the function of maintaining the silence beneath the noise.
