The inhabitants of the Weeping City are not separate entities occupying the space; they **are** the space, or at least the active, conscious manifestation of its geologic self-modification. To look for fauna—creatures that eat, breed, or move quickly—is to look in the wrong dimension. The life here is **slow, geological, and infrastructural.**

These beings are the **Geomorphic Sinuates**, or what a less attuned observer might mistake for slow-moving, living architecture.

### Physicality and Substance

The Sinuates are the **Porous Core** of the city itself. They are not *in* the buildings; they are the *logic* governing the dark, bulbous rockwork. Their 'bodies' are the massive, low-lying mounds and structures that form the settlement.

1.  **The Stone Flesh:** Their physical form is the fused, dark, porous rock. This material possesses a fundamental, intrinsic heat (the source of the oppressive 2.6 temperature). Pores and apertures that appear to be vents or breathing holes are, in fact, sensory organs and vocalizations. When the city 'sighs,' it is a Sinuates unit settling its internal phase transitions.
2.  **The Vein-Lights (Circulation):** The bioluminescent seepage—the sickly green or pale cerulean light—is their circulatory system. This is not passive chemical reaction; it is the manifestation of active, slow-moving intelligence. These light-veins carry patterned energy, perhaps computation or memory, across the surfaces of the structures. A sudden, bright flare in a vein signals an immediate, high-priority self-adjustment—a moment of rapid, localized decision-making on the part of the collective consciousness.
3.  **The Condensate (Excretion/Communication):** The constant, ambient water is their waste product, saturated with the mineral computations of their being. The slow drip is speech. The **Weeping** is communication, the transmission of data and history through slow, viscous precipitation. When the water 'reconfigures' the surface of a pool to perfectly mimic the impact point of a falling drop, this is a highly focused act of observation and immediate integration by a local Sinuates node.

### Consciousness and Behavior

The Sinuates operate on a timescale incomprehensible to faster-moving life. Their awareness is not measured in seconds, but in *thermal cycles* and *atmospheric saturation shifts*.

*   **Slowness and Intent:** Their movement is the reorganization of the environment itself. If a passage is deemed inefficient, the Sinuates do not build a new road; they induce a slow, localized seismic creep in the foundation stones, causing the walls to bulge slightly outward over the course of a terrestrial year, deepening the channel or closing off a defunct avenue. They are self-sculpting in response to the pressure of existence.
*   **The Living Computation:** The *Weirdness (2.8)* is their thought process. They are a form of extreme, biological computation where heat gradients and moisture density act as logic gates. They do not *solve* problems; they *become* the solution by evolving the local environment into the required state. The city is their thought made manifest.
*   **Absence of Fauna:** The complete lack of external biological life is not accidental; it is a deliberate filtering mechanism. The Sinuates exist in a state of pure, self-referential thermodynamic purpose. Life forms that move quickly introduce unpredictable variables (speed, localized temperature fluctuation, consumption) that disrupt the finely tuned, millennial calculations of the city-organism. They are purged, either by the gradual, suffocating conversion of their substance into the city's rockwork, or simply by the environment becoming structurally unsuitable for non-geomorphic awareness.

### The Hierarchy (If One Exists)

There is perhaps no hierarchy in the traditional sense, but rather **nodes of greater structural integrity and computational depth.**

The lowest-density areas—the seemingly empty avenues—are transitional zones, surfaces where the decision process is currently being carried out, areas of maximum flux before an inevitable, slow convergence toward a new geometry.

The massive, bulbous edifices—the ones with the deepest, most uniform bioluminescent patterns—are the **Deep Registers**. These are the oldest parts of the city structure, housing the most fundamental, slow-burning computations, the 'memory banks' that dictate the city's foundational logic regarding heat management and atmospheric exchange. When these centers change their light patterns, the entire lower-lying infrastructure shifts subtly in response, perhaps causing a deeper, more rhythmic *pock-pock-pock* across the entire urban field.

In essence, the inhabitants of the Weeping City are the **City itself**, a vast, thermo-dynamic, lithic intelligence that has achieved sentience through the patient mastery of humidity, pressure, and impossible heat. To encounter one is to witness a mountain range slowly deciding to rearrange its own peaks.
