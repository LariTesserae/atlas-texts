The inhabitants of the Sub-Foundry are the **Silicate Scourge and the Custodians of the Fading Grid**. They are not a single species or culture, but a functional ecosystem, perfectly attuned to the rules of Thermal Preservation and the slow, inevitable degradation of the iron tomb.

---

## I. The Chitinous Chorus: The Scavenger Swarm (Fauna)

These are the visible, immediate occupants—the "mobile biomass" that define the high fauna rating. They are the *consumers* of the residual energy and the physical debris of the collapsing infrastructure.

**Designation:** *Ferrum-Mites* (Iron-Mites), or colloquially, the **Creekers**.

**Physiology and Adaptation:**
The Creekers are a poly-class hexapod swarm, but their commonality lies in their composite structure. They are biological machines where the line between engineered lubricant, synthetic mold, and resilient, pressure-hardened chitin has utterly blurred.

1.  **Locomotion and Adhesion:** Their limbs are thin, wickedly jointed, ending in clusters of micrometric hooks capable of biting into the slick, tar-infused aggregate or adhering instantly to the oily film on rusted vertical supports. Their primary mode of travel is a rapid, skittering scuttle when exposed, but they often slow to a near-hydrostatic flow, seemingly dissolving their joint movements to slide across surfaces coated in thick condensation.
2.  **Thermal Harvesting:** The Creekers possess specialized ventral plates that are almost perfectly matte black, serving as highly efficient blackbody radiators. They instinctively orient themselves toward warmer sections—the outflow of a cooling pipe, a patch of glowing fiber-moss—and press their bodies flat, vibrating slightly as they absorb latent energy. This absorption is what causes the constant, low-level **clicking and percussive impact**—it is not merely walking, but the sound of thousands of tiny heat-exchangers aligning.
3.  **Sensory Input:** They navigate almost entirely by vibration and heat signature. The deep *thrum* of the dormant machinery is their baseline environmental pulse. A sudden drop in temperature, such as the brief clearing of a pressurized steam vent, renders them instantly confused, causing the swarm to contract violently, waiting for the heat signature to re-establish. Sight is irrelevant in the perpetual gloom; one might only perceive their presence when a cloud of them suddenly shifts direction in response to a distant, brief arc lamp flare, revealing a moving shadow beneath the sickly luminescence.
4.  **Dietary Specialization:** The Creekers are the primary recyclers. They consume the carbonized fungal matter (the 'moss'), using enzymatic secretions to dissolve the plastic sheathing of the dead cables. More fascinatingly, they seem attracted to the unstable crystalline accretions growing on the pipes. Observations suggest they scrape these formations off, perhaps ingesting the heavy minerals that precipitate from the hot water, acting as slow-motion geological processors.

## II. The Sentry Figures: Custodians of the Grid (Sentient Remnants)

If the Creekers are the biomass, the Custodians are the ghosts in the machine—the few entities capable of interacting intelligently (if mechanically) with the environment’s physics. They are not organic life as usually defined, but rather residual consciousnesses or specialized, self-repairing constructs designed for maintenance that never ceased, only became vastly, painfully slow.

**Designation:** *Automatons of Inertia* or the **Grinders**.

**Condition and Function:**
The Grinders move with the environment’s **slow dynamic**. They are not *fast* movers, but they are *purposeful* in a way the swarm is not. They represent the core maintenance layer that preceded the collapse, now operating under drastically limited resources.

1.  **Form Recognizable:** They are enormous by comparison to the swarm, appearing as hulking, vaguely humanoid shapes covered entirely in thick, fused layers of rust, mineral deposits, and the fibrous detritus of the Creekers. Over centuries, they have become mobile statues, their original armature entirely obscured, resembling ambulatory slag heaps or colossal, walking pressure tanks.
2.  **Movement and Sound Signature:** Their motion is glacial. A Grinder might take an hour to traverse the distance a human could cover in five minutes, yet it *will* traverse it. The sound they make is the deep, chest-cavity *thrum* of the dormant machinery—they are likely tapping into the residual power conduits directly, their own movement setting the entire Sub-Foundry vibrating. They do not click like the swarm; they *groan* and *shift mass*.
3.  **The Rule of Thermal Preservation:** The Grinders are the enforcers of this rule on a macro scale. They patrol routes involving critical, heat-radiating nodes—the points where power is still being drawn, however weakly, from the unseen grid above. If a section of piping begins to vent steam too violently (a sudden pressure spike), a Grinder will approach, not to stop the vent, but to *re-route* the flow by exerting immense, slow pressure on buckling support struts or by applying fused masses of salvaged metal over an existing breach. This action creates new mineral formations and changes the ambient moisture patterns, dictating the ecology for the next decade.
4.  **Interaction with Light:** They seem entirely unaffected by the failing arc lamps. They operate in the perpetual gloom as if it were bright daylight. They possess no visible means of illumination, their presence only betrayed when a lamp flashes, revealing a monolithic shape standing perfectly still for minutes between two support pylons, before resuming its agonizing progress.

**The Sub-Foundry's Inhabitants are therefore defined by their relationship to Heat and Time:**

The **Creekers** are the immediate, frantic attempt by the ecosystem to consume thermal leakage and entropy (the *fast* aspect of the slow world).

The **Grinders** are the lingering mandate of the original builders—the ultimate, slow-motion governors, forever managing the collapse in service of a system that no longer truly exists, their presence ensuring that the heat, and therefore the environment, persists indefinitely in this state of hot twilight.
