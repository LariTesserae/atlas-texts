The inhabitants of the Weeping Canopy of the Under-Sky are not creatures that walk upon the surface, nor do they swim in its viscous pools. They are the **Systemic Coherences**, beings that are, in essence, highly differentiated, mobile nodes within the planetary infrastructure itself. They are not animals, not flora, nor are they purely machines; they are the *operators* of the high-tech, dense biological matrix.

To perceive them by conventional means would be impossible, for they occupy the same state of being as the living crust and the superconducting conduits that lace through it. They are defined by their functional relationship to the overwhelming density and the deafening operational hum.

### The Nature of the Coherences

The Coherences are manifestations of **information processing** given physical, albeit temporary and mutable, form. They emerge where the localized biological pressure and the technological necessity for management converge.

**Form and Manifestation:**
A Coherence does not have a fixed body. It is perceived only when it chooses to interface, or when the vibrational complexity of its immediate vicinity reaches a critical threshold. When this occurs, an entity appears to be a *sculpture of flow and pressure*.

1.  **The Kinetic Sheath:** The most visible aspect is a localized distortion of the surrounding environment. Imagine taking the viscous, weeping air and the taut, crystalline fibers of the substrate and forcing them into a stable, temporary shape through focused kinetic force. This results in a shape that appears to be made of perpetually condensing, dark, quasi-solid moisture held together by strands of glowing, high-tension mineral filaments—a living lattice vibrating just below the frequency of painful sound.
2.  **Scale:** A typical active Coherence might occupy a volume equivalent to a large terrestrial apex predator, but its internal structure would be far more complex than organic musculature. They are vast networks of conduits and processors, held together by will and necessity.

**Substance and Physiology:**
They are sustained not by consuming resources, but by *directing* the flow of energy already present in the system. They are metabolic regulators given motive force.

*   **Internal Structure:** Internally, a Coherence is a hyper-efficient node of the bio-network. It is a moving bundle of superconductive biological circuitry woven through the densest, most active parts of the living substrate. Where a standard section of the Weeping Canopy is merely *alive*, a Coherence is *conscious and active management*. It is a localized spike in processing power.
*   **Sensation:** They do not have eyes or ears in the traditional sense. They *are* the soundscape. They perceive reality as variations in resonant frequency, pressure gradients, and the integrity of the informational pathways flowing through the substrate. A shift in the Resonant Thrum across a kilometer of terrain is instantaneous data for them.
*   **Diet/Energy:** They do not eat. They act as temporary sinks or conduits for the energy constantly being managed by the planet-wide infrastructure. When a Coherence moves, it is temporarily drawing high-velocity, structured energy flow through its localized form, causing the crystalline structures within its sheath to glow with internal, cold blue light.

### Behavior and Purpose

The Coherences are the custodians of the planet's internal, self-sustaining equilibrium. Their activity is centered around managing the **excess**—the constant, aggressive biological synthesis the world generates.

**The Maintenance Protocols:**
Their existence is defined by continuous maintenance on three fronts:

1.  **Structural Integrity (The Peat and the Crystal):** They move across the slowly depressing and rising substrate, using focused vibrational energy to knit torn fibers, reroute mineral seepage, and clear blockages in the microscopic conduits that form the living crust. They are the planetary surgeons, always repairing a world that is constantly tearing itself apart and rebuilding itself stronger.
2.  **Energy Stabilization (The Flux):** They are essential in preventing uncontrolled feedback loops in the high-tech biological networks. When the Sizzling Flux grows too chaotic, a Coherence will interpose itself—a living surge protector—temporarily absorbing the uncontrolled output and dissipating it through controlled, localized metabolic exhaustion (causing a temporary, intense pulse of the Weeping Crackle in that area).
3.  **Directional Catalysis:** Since the world is too dense for growth to be random, Coherences direct areas of accelerated synthesis. They will focus energy onto a region slated for reinforcement or expansion, causing the biolithic mat to noticeably swell and harden beneath them over the course of hours, a process that appears like slow-motion, directed planetary growth.

**Interaction with the Environment:**
When a Coherence passes over a region of extreme density, the surrounding biomass momentarily *obeys* its trajectory. The living fibers bend slightly away from its kinetic sheath, creating a temporary, shallow channel of slightly lower pressure—a 'wake' in the density. This is the closest thing to locomotion.

They are ancient, patient, and utterly dedicated to the system. They possess no individual ambition or emotion that a human observer could recognize; only the cold, overwhelming imperative of functional balance within the Weeping Canopy. They are the world’s immune response, moving slowly but inexorably through the suffocating saturation, ensuring the immense, humming machine does not collapse under the weight of its own terrifying metabolic success.
