The inhabitants of the Ironwood Cysts are not inhabitants in the way creatures populate a forest, nor are they citizens in the way people populate a city. They are **Symbionts of Maintenance**, entities whose existence is predicated entirely upon the functionality, perpetuation, and slow, biological computation of the structure itself. They are the hands that guide the lumbering inertia of the living machine.

Given the extreme rarity of conventional fauna, the primary entities within the Cysts are a class of bio-mechanical intermediaries.

### The Mycoid Engineers (The Weavers)

These are the most common, yet perhaps the least tangible, of the Cysts’ residents. They are not traditionally mobile.

**Nature and Form:** The Weavers are specialized, slow-metabolizing fungal-animal hybrids. They manifest where the biological structure interfaces most intensely with the embedded industrial components—at junctions, within the ventilation shafts, or along the pressure points of the load-bearing xylem.

They resemble thick, segmented, multi-limbed masses of chitinous mycelium, typically the color of aged bronze or oxidized copper, allowing them to blend seamlessly against the mineralized Ironwood and the tarnished ducts. They possess no eyes; their sensory apparatus is distributed across fine cilia that detect minute changes in air pressure, vibration through the wood fibers, and the specific chemical signature of the flowing sap/coolant mixture.

**Function:** The Weavers are the hands-on technicians of the *active* maintenance cycle. When a fungal graft needs reinforcement, a capillary channel requires clearing, or an embedded bronze node begins to resonate dissonantly, a Weaver migrates—incredibly slowly, perhaps only moving a meter over a standard day—to the site.

They ‘work’ by secreting powerful, targeted enzymes that soften the wood fibers just enough for repositioning, or by releasing calcifying agents that bond new supporting lichen layers to the Ironwood structure. They do not build new things; they correct the decay inherent in the slow-motion self-repair. They are the proof that the architecture is *governed*, not just grown.

**Cognition:** Their thought process is hypothesized to be entirely reactive and procedural—a biological operating system executed in tandem with the slow computation of the Ironwood pillars. They do not communicate in any audible sense; their coordination, if it exists, is conducted through the resonant vibrations passed through the substrate floor and the mineralized wood.

### The Condensation Harvesters (The Vapors)

These entities exploit the extreme moisture gradient and the constant drip cycle.

**Nature and Form:** The Harvesters are fragile, near-transparent insectoids of immense size relative to typical fauna, though still small enough to navigate the narrowest of the structural gaps. They look like elongated silhouettes crafted from blown glass or stretched, damp cellophane. They are almost impossible to see against the dim, humid backdrop, catching the light only when they pass through a shaft of the meager, filtered illumination.

They possess massive, highly vascularized lungs or filtration sacs that allow them to process the vapor-laden air. They appear to lack rigid exoskeletons, relying on the ambient humidity to maintain their structural integrity; if brought into drier air, they would reportedly collapse into viscous puddles.

**Function:** They are the life that drinks the output of the environment. They alight upon the underside of the black photosynthetic canopy shields or cling to the hanging capillary roots, slowly drawing the harvested condensation—the lifeblood of the Cysts’ internal plumbing—into their bodies. They act as mobile condensation points, sometimes creating small, localized droplets that fall to the floor, completing a circuit the external system initiated.

They are thought to be the primary mechanism for distributing trace biological nutrients gathered from the air throughout the lower strata, acting as mobile micro-delivery systems that occasionally pass over areas where specialized detritivores or the Weavers are working.

### The Architects of Resonance (The Deep Hum)

These are the anomaly, the presumed guardians of the *Weirdness* parameter, and the most likely entities to understand the "computation" running in the wood grain.

**Nature and Form:** Nothing identifiable as an animal or fungus has ever been seen. The Deep Hum is perceived only as a phenomenon associated with the large, hollow Cyst chambers. When the pulsed light appears beneath the Ironwood grain, the air in the chamber becomes electrically charged, and the low, resonant hum originating from the floor rises in pitch, achieving a sustained, nearly painful harmonic frequency.

It is theorized that The Deep Hum is a form of pure bio-electricity or structured energy maintained by massive, ancient biological capacitors built into the foundation mycelium, perhaps using the Ironwood as a giant, organic superconductor. These capacitors might be the literal 'brains' of the Cysts.

**Function:** If they have a function, it is supervisory. They seem to emerge—or rather, *activate*—only when the structure faces a significant threat to its programmed sequence, or when a major growth cycle transition is imminent. Their activity is chaotic to an outside observer, involving massive, non-repeating fluctuations in light and sound.

The Deep Hum might be the process by which new growth patterns are calculated, or existing flawed memory sequences are overwritten. To be present during a Deep Hum manifestation is to witness the architecture thinking on a timescale too vast for human comprehension—a computation so slow that one might mistake it for geological time.

In summary, the Ironwood Cysts are populated not by residents seeking shelter or resources, but by **Maintenance Constructs**—specialized living tools (Weavers), biological fluid regulators (Harvesters), and underlying computational forces (The Deep Hum)—all existing solely to ensure the seamless, slow-motion operation of a vast, self-repairing, bio-mechanical complex. Silence reigns because noise would interfere with the critical tolerances of their existence.
