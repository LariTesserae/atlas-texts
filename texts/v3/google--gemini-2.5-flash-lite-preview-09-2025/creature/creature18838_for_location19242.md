## The Accretion Swarmers (The *Lithosynæsthes* Collective)

The dominant inhabitants of the Under-Vein of the Trembling Spires are not individually remarkable creatures, but a pervasive, interlocking ecosystem defined by sheer mass and kinetic energy. They are the **Accretion Swarmers**, a life form that exists at the precise interface between the viscous mineral fluid, the churning heat, and the mobile rock lattice.

### Morphology and Composition

The Swarmers are best understood as **mobile mineral colonies**. Their base unit resembles a flattened, segmented arthropod, built around a core of dense, bio-processed iron silicate. Their exoskeletons are not true chitin but layered, semi-vitreous structures formed from absorbed, rapidly cooling condensation. This gives them a perpetually slick, oily sheen, making them almost iridescent under the chartreuse light.

They possess an excess of limbs—usually eight to twelve jointed appendages tipped with hooked, microscopic barbs made of pure, hardened feldspar. These serve less for locomotion on flat surfaces and more for gripping the fractal complexity of the environment. They do not walk so much as *suture* themselves to the rock, allowing the geological turbulence to partially carry them while they maintain purchase.

The average individual is small, perhaps the size of a large beetle, but they rarely exist as individuals. They are defined by **Clustering and Functional Polymorphism.**

### Behavioral Ecology: Sculpting the Vein

The Swarmers are the embodiment of the localized "weirdness" parameter. Their lives are dedicated to the immediate processing of energy gradients and, crucially, the **guidance of nascent crystallization.**

**Nutrient Acquisition:** Lacking true consumption mechanisms for organic matter, the Swarmers feed via direct thermal and kinetic absorption, supplementing this by secreting a mild, highly reactive mineral acid onto the weeping rock. This dissolves specific compounds, which are then wicked up through porous sections of their ventral segments. They favor seams rich in rare earth elements, which they process to reinforce their own structures.

**Kinetic Synchronization:** The constant turbulence is not a hazard to the Swarmers; it *is* their medium. When a localized pressure wave hits, the entire swarm in that area shifts its vibrational frequency, moving in perfect, complex waves that harmonize with the incoming shock. This allows them to navigate pressure fronts without being smashed against the walls—they ride the distortion.

**Architectural Instinct:** Their most unusual behavior is the shaping of the rock. When a nutrient-rich seam is discovered, a subset of the Swarmers—the **Guided Weavers**—will adhere tightly to the area. They vibrate their silicate claws at incredibly high frequencies, essentially tuning the nascent crystals as they form. The result is the bizarre, deliberate geometry observed: cubes, spirals, or perfect concentric rings that seem utterly at odds with the surrounding chaotic fault lines. This is not art; it is mineral-based data storage, a way to optimize energy flow through the structure, or perhaps a form of extremely slow, inorganic communication across vast distances.

### Sensory Apparatus and Defense

Vision, as understood by surface dwellers, is useless here. The Swarmers navigate almost entirely through **Geophysical Sonar and Chemoluminescence Sensitivity.**

1.  **Vibro-Sense:** Their entire bodies act as highly sensitive seismographs, allowing them to map the internal stresses of the Spires, predicting fissures seconds before they open and sensing the approach of larger, seismic-generating predators from miles away.
2.  **Light Markers:** They exhibit a faint, internal bioluminescence that pulses in response to local mineral density. Their collective glow acts as a shifting, three-dimensional map for the rest of the swarm, identifying rich feeding grounds or danger zones.

**Defense:** Their primary defense is not flight, but **density aggregation.** When threatened by anything larger than a standard pressure wave, surrounding Swarmers converge instantly, creating a single, churning mass of hardened silicate layers, rendering them momentarily impenetrable. If broken apart, the fragmented pieces quickly begin to generate small, sharp crystalline spurs, turning an individual into a temporary, painful hazard to its attacker.

### Relationship to the Old World Artifacts

The scattered, polished slabs left by older presences are treated with cautious deference.

The Swarmers avoid dwelling directly upon these smooth surfaces, as they provide no anchor points for their claws. However, they use the surrounding, jagged rock adjacent to these slabs as their primary **Weaving Nodes.** It is theorized that the precise geometry carved onto the slabs emits a background resonance or frequency that helps the Swarmers maintain the synchronization required for their crystalline sculpting, perhaps acting as an ancient tuning fork for the geological symphony.

In essence, the Accretion Swarmers are not merely living *in* the Under-Vein; they are the **active, kinetic layer of its geological identity**, riding the pressure, feeding on the heat, and constantly rewriting the chemistry of the deep stone one shimmering, scraping segment at a time.
