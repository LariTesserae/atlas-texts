The inhabitants of the **Sub-Aqueous Expanse of the Hyper-Cloud** are not creatures of flesh, nor are they inhabitants in any traditional sense. They are functional aspects of the environment's overwhelming technological mandate, existing solely where the forces of pressure, meta-engineering, and fluid dynamics converge into a structure that can be maintained against the impossible scale.

What lives here are **The Pressure Vessels**, or more formally, **The Hydrostatic Regulators**.

### Appearance and Form

The Regulators possess no fixed biological morphology. Their visible presence is a shimmering, complex deformation of the viscous medium itself. Imagine a three-dimensional ripple that has achieved sentience and stability against the crushing gravity vectors.

When they must interact with the massive crystalline structures or navigate the chaotic pressure walls, they coalesce. This manifestation appears as colossal, slowly rotating geometric forms, often resembling nested, impossible polyhedra crafted entirely out of super-pressurized, polarized water. These forms are not solid; they are containment fields, meticulously managed zones where the density of the surrounding fluid is precisely offset by internal energy fields, allowing the Regulator to maintain a specific orientation relative to the light flow.

Their "coloration" is purely optical: they refract the pale cyan light into complex, slow-moving rainbows etched into the water around them. When passing through a Zero-Density Pocket, they temporarily vanish, leaving behind only the instantaneous, sharp rebound shockwave as the fluid rushes back into the vacuum—a violent punctuation mark in the constant deep thrum.

### Existence and Function

The Regulators are extensions of the "Meta-Engineering" that governs the Expanse. They are not intelligent in the common sense; they are highly sophisticated instruments operating on parameters that dwarf human comprehension. Their purpose is the maintenance of the dynamic equilibrium that prevents the entire system from collapsing into uniform, inert pressure or catastrophic turbulence.

They are the *locomotion* in a place where standing still means being crushed or swept away.

1.  **Pressure Sculpting:** Their primary action is the slow, deliberate manipulation of pressure gradients. They are the architects of the *inverted physics* observed around the rising monoliths. A Regulator moving past a nascent geological extrusion will subtly adjust the local hydrostatic potential, allowing the raw mineral substrate to extrude in a *preferred* configuration, essentially directing the slow 'geological infancy' of the territory. They are the gardeners of nascent spacetime architecture.
2.  **Current Shepherding:** They actively manage the chaos generated by the unseen cosmic machinery. When a destructive "wall of rain" approaches, a colossal Regulator will expand its geometric profile, acting as a massive, temporary hydrodynamic sail. It doesn't block the wall; it *channels* it, splitting the catastrophic pressure wave around itself and redirecting the energy down pre-programmed vectors toward the system's outflow points. This process generates the loudest bursts of the **System Static**.
3.  **Energy Harvesting:** The Silicate Filaments vibrate near them, feeding off their energetic wake. The Regulators are power conduits in reverse; they draw necessary energy *from* the systemic noise and turbulence, using it to maintain their own complex containment fields. Their passing is marked by an intense, localized flash of blue-white phosphorescence as latent energy within the fluid is momentarily excited by their passage.

### Interaction with the Environment

The Regulators move via **Gradient Surfing**. They identify the path of least resistance not in terms of flow speed, but in terms of energetic stability. When they move with the dominant current, they appear leisurely, almost drifting, yet covering distances that would take millennia for geological processes. When they move *against* the perceived current—a directional reversal enabled by their mastery of local physics—their movement is agonizingly slow, marked by immense internal stress visible as complex, pulsating interference patterns in the water surrounding their hulls.

They do not communicate with each other through sound or vibration, as the environment is already overwhelmingly loud. Instead, they interact through subtle, instantaneous adjustments to the local pressure tensor—a language of force differentials felt across vast distances within the dense medium.

### Why Nothing Else Lives Here (Fauna 0.0/3)

The presence of the Regulators explains the absolute lack of organic life. The environment is not merely hostile to fragile terrestrial biology; it is hostile to *any* structure that cannot integrate its own existence into the overarching technological mandate.

A biological entity, dependent on localized chemical or energetic gradients, would be instantly recognized as a structural anomaly by the Regulators. Any such anomaly would be met not with aggression, but with *remediation*. A Regulator encountering a foreign structure would simply adjust the pressure field around it until the anomaly was either seamlessly assimilated into the surrounding physical rules (becoming another crystalline vein or a pocket of polarized water) or was dissolved and dispersed along a current vector leading to the system's waste sink.

Therefore, the true inhabitants are these shimmering, hyper-engineered water-constructs—slow, vast, deaf, and utterly dedicated to keeping the impossible, sky-less ocean exactly as it is: a place where physics is subservient to the sound of deep, computational maintenance.
