What lives in the Undulating Scar are the **Chitinous Scavengers** and the **Moulder-Priests**. They are an ecosystem perfectly attuned to the crucible of damp, low light, and raw geology, manifesting as both the frantic energy of life and the slow persistence of stone.

---

## The Chitinous Scavengers (The Arthropodal Tide)

These are the primary engine of the Scar’s constant, wet *skittering*. They are not warm-blooded or feathered; they are the hard, segmented response to an environment that offers abundant moisture but few conventional calories.

**Form and Adaptation:**
The Scavengers exist in a thousand subspecies, but share a common, unsettling adaptation: **mineral mimicry**. Their exoskeletons are not uniformly brown or black, but striated with pale, silicate deposits leached from the raw rock dust they ingest. An ordinary, dinner-plate-sized centipede among them might have armor resembling striated slate, making it functionally invisible against the angular jutting stones. Their claws—the source of the constant scraping sound—are tipped with naturally occurring quartz points, perfect for gripping slick, fleshy vegetation or carving purchase into packed substrate.

**Locomotion and Society:** The life cycle is short and hyper-intensive. They move in vast, non-hierarchical swarms focused entirely on processing biomass. They do not build complex structures; their only architecture is the vast, temporary **processing zone** they create around a fallen piece of fungus or a patch of saturated moss. Thousands of them will descend, and the wet scraping sound intensifies until they have reduced the organic matter to a nutrient slime that the next level of life can consume.

**The Pressure Hunters:** The most unsettling of the Scavengers are the **Pneumatic Lurkers**. These are the primary predators, adapted to hunt via atmospheric pressure fluctuation. They are relatively few, often growing to the size of a large badger, but their bodies are strange hybrids of muscle and bladder. They possess an internal network of fine, air-filled silken sacs. When moving, they regulate the pressure within these sacs with minute, rhythmic contractions. A Lurker does not *see* its prey; it detects the minuscule displacement of air created when an insect tries to pull its sticky foot from a damp surface, or the slight rush of air displaced when one of the six-winged aerial disturbances dips too low. When hunting, they drift silently, occasionally altering their gait to create a deliberate, low-frequency pressure pulse to flush out hidden prey, which manifests to others as an unseen, localized "sigh" in the otherwise constant ambient humidity.

## The Moulder-Priests (The Biotic Architects)

If the Scavengers are the frantic, ephemeral energy of the Scar, the Moulder-Priests are the slow, deliberate builders, the consciousness residing in the pale, fleshy flora and the gelatinous cements. They represent the "urban" element (1.6/3)—the attempt to organize chaos.

**Identity and Function:**
The Priests are less individual creatures and more the **focal points of the fungal/bromeliad network**. They are massive, slow-moving, sessile entities that anchor themselves to the largest, moisture-retaining rock formations. They appear as colossal, bulbous masses of pale, compacted mycelium and root structure, often mimicking the shape of the low, circular habitation ruins they settle upon.

Their defining trait is their **synthetic capability**. Using the abundant minerals and the complex sugars exuded by the fleshy bromeliads, the Priests secrete and rapidly cure the clear, high-density resin used to bind the dry-stacked stone ruins. They are not building *new* cities, but maintaining the structural integrity of the past inhabitation shells, perhaps as necessary moisture traps, or perhaps as crude shrines.

**The Bioluminescent Command:** The blue and sickly green light sources within the Scar are not random. They are the **sensory organs** of the Moulder-Priests. These fungi are extensions of the main body that extend out into the gloom. They glow brightest when the Priest is actively absorbing data—the slow analysis of mineral content in the water runoff, the detection of excessive vibrational activity from a large Scavenger swarm, or the subtle atmospheric changes indicating the presence of one of the pressure hunters. The light is a slow, visual manifestation of their deep, internal computational processes.

**Interaction with the Environment:** The Moulder-Priests are the reason for the incredible *lushness* (2.5/3). They chemically leach nutrients from the raw stone itself, effectively accelerating an impossible weathering process, creating pockets of highly enriched soil where the bromeliads can thrive. They exist in a state of symbiotic manipulation: the Scavengers process the dead material into transportable slurry, which the Priests then use to feed the support structures that maintain the dampness gradient necessary for *them* to survive.

## The Aerial Disturbance (The Static Cloud)

The six-winged insects represent the Scar’s weird defense mechanism. They are the **Atmospheric Static**.

These tiny flyers are almost entirely transparent, made of delicate chitin and extremely high surface-area wings. They feed on airborne particulate, essentially filtering the dim, mineral-heavy air. They do not appear to coordinate their movement in the traditional sense; there is no leader, no flocking behavior. Instead, they simply vibrate at a precise, ultra-high frequency determined by the ambient moisture content.

When a Pneumatic Lurker prepares to hunt, it emits a soft, rhythmic pressure pulse to stabilize its own sensing field. The collective vibration of the insect cloud acts as **acoustic white noise**. The frequency of the cloud clashes with the frequency of the Lurker’s hunting pulse, creating microscopic interference patterns that completely scramble the Lurker’s pressure map. For the Lurker, the air suddenly explodes into meaningless, chaotic bursts of static, forcing it to halt its hunt and drift until the cloud passes or recalibrates its vibration rate. They are not intelligent defenders, but rather a perfect piece of evolutionary physics—a living, shimmering shield generated by the environment itself to protect the smaller life from the apex predator.
