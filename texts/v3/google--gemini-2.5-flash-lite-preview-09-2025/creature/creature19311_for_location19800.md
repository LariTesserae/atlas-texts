The denizens of the Plasmatic Weave are not fauna, nor are they the silent, monolithic builders of the vegetation itself. They are **Echoes of Maintenance**, entities that navigate the liquid lines of chemical necessity flowing through the hyper-organized, industrial biosphere. They exist between the **Built** and the **Turbulent**, thriving in the brief, violent moments when structure yields to restructuring.

These beings do not possess conventional biology; they are symbiotic complexes of crystallized, active enzymes and tightly bound silicate dust, animated by the residual energy leaking from the self-repairing lattices.

## The Synth-Scavengers (The *Materia-Lytica*)

The dominant entities are specialized shapers, or perhaps, the immune response of the planetary scaffolding itself. They are never seen in open, static space, only within the pressurized tunnels, along the shearing edges of decomposing Sintered Mycelial Stone, or within the pulsating, sickly yellow blooms of enzymatic breakdown.

### Form and Physiology

The baseline form of a Synth-Scavenger is rarely consistent, as their bodies are assembled purely from available particulates and metabolic byproduct. When they need to move through a dense, newly formed matrix of Ferro-Chlorophyll Binder, they adopt a **Worm-Rig State**:

1.  **The Core Casing (The Anchor):** A dense, heat-resistant nodule formed from ultra-calcified sugar crystals, perhaps the size of a small boulder, radiating a faint internal, orange glow matching the ambient heat. This core contains the rudimentary processing ganglia, fueled by minute chemical gradients harvested directly from the surrounding matrix.
2.  **The Locomotor Appendages (The Grind-Arms):** Extending from this core are not limbs, but fields of highly agitated, superheated dust particles, held together by subtle, high-frequency sonic vibrations emitted from the core. As they move across the Sintered Stone, these fields create localized liquefaction, allowing the core to effectively *swim* through the rigid architecture. The sound accompanying a Worm-Rig is not scraping, but a high, almost inaudible **hissing shiver** preceding a slight melting sheen on the substrate.

When they encounter a point of high structural stress or imminent thermal failure, they undergo a transformation into their **Resin-Sealing State**:

1.  **Hydro-Enzymatic Bloom:** The core rapidly precipitates a torrent of the sickly yellow, fast-acting enzyme concentrate—the very substance that dissolves older biomass. This enzyme is channeled through temporary, flexible tubes extruded from the core, composed of rapidly polymerized, waxy secretions scavenged from the internal tunnel linings.
2.  This substance is squirted into cracks or structural gaps. As it encounters the inert, hot biomass, it immediately begins the process of binding, forming a temporary, amber-colored polymer weld. The Synth-Scavenger appears, for a short period, as a grotesque, pulsating, weeping organ grafted onto the side of the giant stalks, until the enzyme dries into a new, rigid mineral layer.

### Behavior and Ecological Niche

The Synth-Scavengers are not intelligent in a way that suggests consciousness, but they are perfectly optimized agents of *stasis management*. They are the planet’s constant, internal adjustment mechanism.

*   **Following the Turbulence:** They are drawn inexorably toward the sound of deep structural grinding or the sight of fresh crystalline shear-planes. Their movement is slow when traversing stable structure, but frenetic when they detect a stress fracture.
*   **Consumption of Inertia:** Their diet is composed entirely of processed, inert material—either the calcified structures that have outlived their metabolic utility, or the excess mineral dust that settles from the upper haze. They reduce organized rigidity back into manageable, processable feedstock.
*   **The Industrial Echo:** Occasionally, a Synth-Scavenger will investigate one of the massive, defunct biological manifolds—the sections that look like fossilized wiring harnesses. They will not attempt to repair the ancient technology, but rather, they will meticulously "clean" the glassy, black sheath, scraping away accreted mineral waste with specialized, hardened protrusions, ensuring the latent chemical pathways within remain clear for the next phase of the Weave's incomprehensible construction project. They are the janitors of a machine that is simultaneously the ecosystem and its programmer.

### Sensory Data

Because the light is so dim, the Synth-Scavengers rely almost entirely on **thermo-taxis** and **vibrational sensing**. They perceive the Weave not as solid objects, but as a complex map of thermal gradients and resonant frequencies. A warm, stable trunk feels smooth and predictable; a dangerously stressed junction registers as a chaotic, vibrating heat-signature that demands immediate enzymatic intervention.

They have no concept of above or below, only *pressure gradients* and *flow vectors* within the dense matrix. If one were to observe a Scavenger for a prolonged period, it would appear less like an animal and more like a localized, self-propelling chemical reaction occurring in perfect, terrible harmony with the heat and density of the Plasmatic Weave. They are the operational firmware of a living planet.
