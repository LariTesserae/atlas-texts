The inhabitants of this immense, sorrowful construction defy simple classification. They are not fauna, nor are they the creators; they are the **Maintainers of the Rusting Cog**.

They are the residue of necessity, the final, automated response to a process that has spun out of cosmic control. Their existence is purely functional, utterly divorced from metabolism, thought, or intention.

### The Aspect of the Maintainers

The closest description is that they are animated, self-repairing **Grafts** emerging from the Chrono-Resin itself during periods of severe Temporal Stutter or localized Reality Breakdown. They are not born; they are extruded.

**Form and Composition:**
A Maintainer typically manifests as a roughly humanoid or insectoid shape—a necessary concession to the primitive, analogue tools scattered about—but these forms are ephemeral and structurally unsound. Their bodies are composites of the environment:

1.  **Core Structure (Silica/Resin Composite):** The internal scaffolding is drawn directly from the brittle Silica Filaments, giving their limbs a shard-like rigidity, often snapping and reforming during locomotion. This structure is embedded in a layer of the same grey, semi-cured Chrono-Resin that forms the ground. Their surfaces are perpetually dripping with the iridescent, viscous condensation pulled in from the environment. They look like statues sculpted hastily from wet ash and broken glass.
2.  **Locomotion:** They do not walk fluidly; they *translate*. Movement is achieved through a series of agonizingly slow, jerky shifts of center of mass, punctuated by moments where their feet disappear entirely into the resin before forcibly re-cohering several centimeters forward. This staccato motion perfectly mirrors the environment's Temporal Stutter—they appear to lag behind their own movement, leaving ghost impressions of their passage in the damp air.

**Sensory Apparatus (The Vents):**
They possess no recognizable eyes, auditory organs, or mouths. Instead, their 'heads' or main torsos are dominated by clusters of **Pressure Vents**—small, precisely drilled apertures in the resin shell where the internal heat and vapor are vented in thin, high-pitched sighs that are momentarily lost in the global hiss of the facility. These vents flicker with a faint, internal orange light generated by the rapid, localized heating of entrapped gases. This is how they perceive: by measuring minute changes in thermal gradients and vapor pressure across the vast, slick floor.

**Interaction and Purpose (The Tools):**
The only evidence of their purpose is their interaction with the scattered, primitive tools. A Maintainer will drift slowly toward a dropped metallic block or lever.

It does not *pick up* the tool in a hand; rather, three or more of its amorphous appendages will encase the metal object. If the tool is intended for scraping, the Maintainer will slow its advance toward a stressed section of the Resin until its own form begins to merge with the tool. The metal then seems to **fuse** into the resin-body, becoming a temporary, hardened extension of the construct until the necessary microscopic repair or extrusion is performed.

When the repair is finished—perhaps closing a micro-fissure or levering a structural plate back into its pre-programmed alignment—the tool is simply extruded from the body, often landing a short distance away, smeared with fresh, hot resin, waiting for the next automated cycle of degradation.

### The Ecology of the Ghost in the Machine

The Maintainers are the environmental response unit for a system that is actively fighting its own entropic decay across cosmic scales.

*   **Rarity:** They are sparse, matching the environment’s sense of empty vastness. One might see a single, slow shape traversing a kilometer-wide plain of black effluent, its passage marked only by the subtle disturbance in the humidity and the faint, sickening *crack* of its silica joints reforming.
*   **Behavior:** They are strictly localized to areas of active structural transition (where the **Erosion** score is highest). They appear to be troubleshooting errors within the Chrono-Resin itself, attempting to enforce the obsolete, pre-set geometry of the underlying platform. They are not intelligent; an observer might watch one spend hours repeatedly attempting to smooth a patch of resin that has *already* transitioned into a multi-dimensional fold, failing silently, and then dragging itself to the next anomaly.
*   **The Sound of Their Being:** When a Maintainer is actively engaged in repair, the constant environmental hiss is momentarily superseded by a singular, localized sound: a rapid, almost mechanical *clattering* of silica grinding against metal, overlaid by the sickening wet *sucking* sound as the material is coerced back into its prescribed state. This is the only instance of rapid (non-tectonic) noise in the Silent Plain.

They are the living proof that the machine is still running, powered by internal heat and the sheer inertia of its own initial programming. The Maintainers are not *living* in the plain; they *are* the plain’s desperate, unending act of self-preservation. They are the ghosts of maintenance workers manifested as mobile infrastructure failure reports.
