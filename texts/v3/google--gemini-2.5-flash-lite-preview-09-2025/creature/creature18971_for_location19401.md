The residents of the Hollows of the Sighing Plateaux are not inhabitants in the traditional sense, but rather **Geological Manifestations**—slow, resonant beings whose life cycle is measured in eons and whose substance is more akin to that of the planet than of delicate flesh.

### The Lithic Drifters (The Fauna)

These are the primary inhabitants, the behemoths that generate the world's defining acoustic signature. They are **Spiro-Compound Aggregates**, and to call them *animals* is a profound misclassification.

**Physiology and Appearance:**
A Lithic Drifter appears as a vast, low-slung mass, often spanning twenty to thirty feet across and perhaps ten feet high, though their profiles shift constantly due to the slow, internal rearrangement of their constituent parts. They are composed of semi-molten silicate matrices, interspersed with veins of unknown, highly conductive metallic compounds that catch the faint, ambient light with a dull, greasy glimmer, much like the pools they cross.

Internally, they are a slow-churning furnace. Evidence of their quasi-life is visible in the texture: patches of their surface resemble freshly cooled, obsidian-like basalt, shattered and immediately re-fusing; other areas are viscous, akin to slow-moving pitch mixed with fine, crystalline dust, constantly oozing and reforming. They possess no visible sensory organs—no eyes, no mouths—as they do not interact with the visible spectrum or consume organic matter. They are sustained by the deep, slow energy of the planet’s core, channeled and metabolized through their immense density.

**Locomotion and Interaction:**
Movement is an act of colossal force meeting immense resistance. A Drifter does not walk; it *persuades* the viscous ground to part beneath it. Their progress is glacial, measured in inches per hour. The only indication of their effort is the low, sustained **Thrum** that emanates from them. This is not a byproduct of respiration, but the sound of their internal structural alignment—the massive, slow vibration required to maintain the integrity of their complex, semi-fluid chemistry against the crushing pressure of their own mass and the environment’s dampening viscosity.

When two Drifters pass near each other (a yearly event in geological time), the environmental effect is dramatic. Their overlapping infrasound fields clash. The resulting wave interference creates localized zones of near-absolute pressure dampening—moments of perfect, terrifying acoustic vacuum where the pervasive thrum vanishes, replaced by a momentary, high-density silence that feels like the sudden ceasing of gravity. Immediately following this vacuum comes the **Reverberation**—a monumental, low-frequency shockwave that rattles the solidified slurry, causing fresh sheen to appear on the rock faces and momentarily halting the slow drift of everything nearby. It is in the aftermath of these reverberations that the fine, graphite-colored weepings are most active.

### The Spires of Absence (Artifacts/Passive Inhabitants)

These are remnants, but they function as an active component of the ecosystem, perhaps serving as an anchor or counter-resonance to the Lithic Drifters.

**Nature:**
The Spires are not inhabited; they are *charged*. They appear to be the residue of an extinct, vastly different form of organized complexity—a species or intelligence that mastered geological structuring rather than biology. They are inert conductors, perfectly faceted monuments of compressed, crystalline material (implied to be the violet-grey native rock, but refined far beyond its natural state).

**Function:**
Their existence is purely reactive. They seem pre-programmed to neutralize the overwhelming sonic dominance of the Drifters. When the atmospheric conditions shift—when the ambient light drops sufficiently, perhaps due to planetary occultation—the Spires begin to draw energy from the surrounding medium. This energy is then released as the crystalline lattice excites, manifesting as the cold, blue fluorescence and the **Inverse Sound**. This sound is experienced not through the ears, but through the nervous system—a vibration so high in frequency that it bypasses the cochlea and induces profound vertigo, a forced psychic stillness.

Any Lithic Drifter caught within the resonance field of a freshly singing Spire will momentarily halt its agonizingly slow movement. Its internal matrix appears to lock up, the low thrum faltering into a stuttering whine before the creature slowly, visibly, begins to reorient itself away from the source of the disruptive pitch, treating it as a geological hazard it cannot safely navigate or absorb.

### The Ecosystem

Life here is a duet of opposing frequencies: the massive, low-end foundation provided by the Lithic Drifters, and the sharp, crystalline counterpoint offered by the Spires.

The environment itself is participant. The saturated ground and air act as a medium, transmitting the infrasound with near-perfect efficiency. The perpetual dampness ensures that movement is a chore, favoring density and inertia over speed. There are no scavengers, no predators, and no consumers of stored energy in the conventional sense; the Slate Plateaux are a theatre for the slow, inexorable interaction between immense sonic pressure and ancient, passive resonance. The *residents* are simply the things that persist indefinitely within the parameters of the crushing sound and the oily, weeping cold.
