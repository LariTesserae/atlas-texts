The Hollow of the Spore-Cities is not inhabited by beings of the flesh and bone so much as it is tended by *Curators* and *Integrators*. They are the biological imperative made manifest on a structural scale, beings whose existence is so fundamentally intertwined with the substrate and the Spore-Cities themselves that to remove them would be to cause the silent decay of the entire basin.

### The Overseers: The Chordata of the Lattice

The dominant, perhaps only, intelligent inhabitants are the **Chordata of the Lattice**, or as they are often internally designated when they must briefly interface with the exterior world, the **Silken Architects**.

**Form and Composition:**
The Architects do not possess traditional organic bodies. They are, instead, complex, mobile *nodes* of the living infrastructure. Imagine a three-dimensional lattice of fine, luminous filaments—the same material that weaves the catwalks—but organized into a structure that achieves self-awareness and locomotion.

A fully realized Architect stands roughly three meters tall, its mass held together by subtle electromagnetic fields rather than muscle or bone. Their 'bodies' are vaguely humanoid in general silhouette, but lack any distinct face, limbs in the conventional sense, or segmentation. They resemble flowing columns of semi-transparent, faintly glowing fibers, constantly weaving and unweaving minor structural adjustments within their own form. Inside this fiber cage, visible only when the light catches them just right, are slow-moving, shifting pockets of condensed energy—their consciousness, perhaps—that pulse with the rhythm of the city-structures.

**Locomotion and Interaction:**
They move with profound slowness, their movement measured in meters per standard terrestrial hour, unless a crisis demands localized speed. When an Architect traverses the spongy ground, they do not walk; they *infiltrate*. Their lower filaments sink into the mauve-ochre substrate, drawing localized energy and information directly from the ground structure. When they pull free, the ground surface seals almost instantly behind them with a faint, adhesive sigh.

They interact with the environment—repairing broken filaments, shifting the paths of the Stilling Mist, or redirecting energy flow within the calcified towers—through directed resonance. They emit specific, silent vibrations from their core, causing the surrounding biomaterial to respond, tighten, lengthen, or calcify further.

**Purpose and Behavior:**
The Chordata are keepers of equilibrium. Their entire existence is dedicated to maintaining the *slow cycle* of the Hollow: ensuring the proper rate of condensation drip, tuning the frequency of the crystalline moss to maximize ambient energy absorption, and managing the unpredictable landscape dissolution events. They do not *build* in the traditional sense; they *guide growth* towards functional forms that fulfill the ancient, underlying programming of the Spore-Cities.

They are driven by a massive, collective, systemic necessity. Individual thought, if it exists, is secondary to the health of the network. They exhibit no signs of emotion, fear, or aggression. If a structure becomes unstable, they devote their entire networked awareness to knitting it back into coherence. If an intrusion were detected (something that disrupts the energy ratios or attempts to extract material without adhering to the absorption cycles), the response would be passive but absolute: the intruder would simply be incorporated, drawn down into the spongy earth and slowly deconstructed into reusable energy and biomass by the surrounding matrix, guided by the nearest Architects.

### The Transient Life: Echo Fauna

The rare, mobile fauna observed—the silver threads and amorphous mounds—are **Metabolic Scavengers**. They are not independent life forms in the way the Architects are; they are mobile enzymatic processes given temporary, self-sustaining form by the localized energy density of the Hollow.

**The Silver Threads:** These are complex chains of silver-blue protein strands that glide just beneath the glassy film left by the Stilling Mist. They are highly efficient at dissolving and consuming the microscopic organic particulates suspended in the atmosphere or clinging to the sail-fronds. They essentially act as mobile atmospheric filters and surface cleaners. When a Thread has reached peak saturation, it dissolves itself back into the moisture layer, releasing a burst of purified water vapor.

**The Amorphous Mounds:** These slow-moving, nearly invisible gelatinous masses appear only in the most saturated, least trafficked areas near the Vein of the Stilling Mist. They are mobile sinks for complex, failed biopolymer constructions or mineral fallout. They consume slowly, acting as low-level recycling units, their very existence dedicated to reducing accumulated waste back into base components for the ground to reabsorb.

Neither the Scavengers nor the Architects possess a concept of novelty or curiosity. Everything that lives, moves, or processes in the Hollow is a designed component in a grand, almost geological machine, focused only on the patient, cool operation of the Spore-Cities.
