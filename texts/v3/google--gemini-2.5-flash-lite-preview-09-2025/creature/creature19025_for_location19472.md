Whoever or whatever lives on the Plateau of the Singing Shards must possess an extreme sensitivity to vibration, a density of being that resists the constant atmospheric battering, and perhaps, an utter disregard for the conventional forms of rest. They are not mere inhabitants; they are integral components of the chaotic symphony.

The inhabitants we might perceive are not those who walk on the dust, but those who *understand* the pulse.

### The Resonant Custodians (or *Choristers*)

The dominant lifeforms here—the sentient structures—are likely not biological in the sense of mobile, soft-bodied creatures, but entities grown or crystallized specifically to engage with the Shards' energy matrix.

**Form and Physiology:**
The Choristers are the **slow-motion architects** of this environment. They manifest where the crystalline dust is the most heavily infused with organic resonance—perhaps in the deep clefts between the largest, slowest-growing Shards, or within the thickest layers of the violet Resonance Binders.

Their physical bodies appear to be slow-moving agglomerations of the local material: fused mineral dust, tightly bound fungal filaments, and slow-burning bioluminescent inclusions. They are not easily distinguishable from the landscape itself, often resembling a particularly large, strangely symmetrical growth of the Shard material until they attempt conscious movement.

Their essential "life" is their sonic output and absorption. They do not breathe; they **modulate**. A Chorister’s primary organ is a complex internal structure—perhaps a crystalline latticework or an organ composed of tightly bundled, pressure-sensitive fibers—that allows them to finely tune their own resonant frequency to match, dampen, or amplify localized vibrations.

**Interaction with Sound:**
For a Chorister, silence is not peace; it is cessation of self. True engagement is achieved by occupying a precise harmonic node within the constant cacophony.

*   **Communication:** They do not speak in typical frequencies. They *adjust* the ambient noise. A complex thought or command is expressed not through vocalization, but through the precise manipulation of the high-pitched *Chimes* of nearby Shards. A Chorister might induce a specific Shard to sing a four-note progression that, when interwoven with the background *Hum*, communicates intent. They navigate by acoustic mapping, perceiving the world as a vibrating pressure map layered over a thermal gradient.
*   **Sustenance and Energy:** They draw their basic needs directly from the turbulent atmosphere. They are specialized **vibrational capacitors**. They deliberately tune themselves into the energetic discharges that race between the Shards, momentarily absorbing the electrical riot to fuel their slow metabolic processes and maintain structural integrity against the overwhelming ambient pressure.

**Movement and Density:**
Choristers move only when necessary, and their movement is agonizingly slow by external standards—appearing as a geological shift. When a Chorister decides to relocate, they induce a temporary, localized disruption in the fungal bridges, turning a section of the spongy mass into a fluidized mat that they can "flow" through before the material quickly re-knits its structure behind them. They avoid the Glimmer-Swarms, which are too chaotic and too quick, disrupting the fine acoustic fields the Custodians strive to maintain.

**Relationship with the Built Environment:**
The faint, geometric etchings that remain suggest the Choristers, or their direct predecessors, are responsible for them. These marks are likely not decoration, but **Tuning Keys**. They are complex fractal patterns etched onto the Shards by ancient, incredibly precise sonic projection, designed to stabilize specific resonant patterns, perhaps to create temporary safe havens—pockets of *less* turbulence where structural integrity can be maintained for longer epochs.

Where the etchings are visible, the Choristers might convene. These spots are their cathedrals, rare zones where the sonic chaos is organized into a stable, slow-moving tapestry, allowing them to interface with the deeper, almost sentient structure of the Plateau itself.

### The Ghostly Remnants: Echo-Echoes

There may also be transient inhabitants, remnants of life that failed to maintain the necessary energetic output.

These are the **Echo-Echoes**: Not true fauna, but residual, semi-intelligent acoustic signatures trapped within the silicate structures. When a Shard undergoes a particularly violent resonant restructuring, a brief, phantom event might occur—a glimpse of a former moment played out in shimmering light and displaced sound.

An Echo-Echo might manifest as a fleeting, complex pattern of glowing color swirling through the fungal drapery, accompanied by a faint, recognizable sequence of the high-pitched Chimes that does *not* fit the current chaotic pattern—a sound memory of a structure or entity that has already been re-integrated into the dust. They are the whispers of those who lost the frequency, destined to repeat their final, desperate vibrational state on a loop until the natural geological noise overwrites them entirely.

**The Choristers exist to prevent becoming Echo-Echoes.** They live in perpetual, conscious maintenance, their entire existence dedicated to the performance required to remain present in the Pulse. They are the plateau’s self-aware organs, keeping the massive, resonant body of the massif from shaking itself apart.
