The inhabitants of the Canopy of the Unmaking are not merely creatures living *among* the fused biomass and decaying infrastructure; they are **integrated agents of entropy and maintenance**, beings whose very existence seems predicated on managing the slow, grinding friction between the living architecture and the dead mechanisms it encapsulates.

They are the **Chrono-Fibers**, the **Root-Weavers**, and the enigmatic, rare **Logicians of Stasis**.

---

### The Chrono-Fibers: The Settlers of Rot

The most numerous, though still sparse, inhabitants are the **Chrono-Fibers**. These are beings defined by their relationship to the temporal decay—the *Unmaking* itself.

Physically, they appear to be roughly humanoid in silhouette, but this uniformity is an illusion created by their method of movement. A Chrono-Fiber is not a solid body; it is a **coalescence of the environment’s immediate decay products**. They are perpetually forming and dissolving.

**Appearance and Structure:**
A body might manifest as a dense plume of ionized ozone and ancient wood-dust, briefly shaped by a rigid internal lattice of petrified, fine root tendrils. Their 'skin' is likely a semi-viscous layer of the oxidized copper sap, draped loosely over structures of compressed lignin fibers. They possess no discernible eyes or mouths in the conventional sense. Sensation appears to be managed through the entire surface area, detecting minute shifts in atmospheric pressure, humidity gradients, and the infinitesimal sound of the structural groan of the Trunks of Consensus.

**Function and Behavior:**
The Chrono-Fibers are the *cleaners* and the *re-composers*. They are drawn to the localized zones of temporal warping or 'reality breakdown.' When one of the colossal Trunks begins to buckle, exhibiting premature fracturing or sudden temporal folding, the Chrono-Fibers arrive.

They do not repair the structure in the typical sense. Instead, they introduce **controlled decay**. They will settle onto the stressed area, their bodies acting as catalysts. They accelerate the dissolution of the problematic section—turning stressed polymer-scaffolding into manageable humus, or drawing down the sudden explosion of charged spores into inert ash—only for the surrounding, living biomass to immediately grow over the resulting void. They sacrifice their own localized form for the stability of the whole system.

Their movement is characterized by profound, non-linear intention. They do not walk the root-plate roads; they move *through* the density. One moment, a Fiber is observed slowly ascending the slick, violet bark of a Trunk, and the next, it is found miles away, having moved through a temporary interstitial space opened by the friction between the air and the ozone, a pathway too quick for the eye to track.

They communicate primarily through **olfactory signaling**—the precise manipulation of the sap and ozone they exude—which translates into specific patterns of mineral scent that travel through the heavy air.

---

### The Root-Weavers: Architects of Assimilation

If the Chrono-Fibers manage entropy, the **Root-Weavers** manage *growth*. They are the primary force ensuring that the industrial remnants—the rusted scaffolding, the calcified foundations—are fully absorbed into the living substrate.

**Appearance and Structure:**
Root-Weavers are far less ephemeral than the Fibers. They are large, slow, and massive, often appearing as sections of the landscape itself. A typical Weaver might resemble a mobile, low-slung mound of perpetually damp, violet-black moss interspersed with the brittle, pale grey remnants of consumed metal. They often drag sections of defunct infrastructure behind them, deliberately embedding them into softer mats of biomass so that the surrounding fungi and cellulose can begin the slow process of chemical neutralization.

They are identifiable by the **six or eight incredibly thick, phosphorescent root-like limbs** that anchor them to the ground. These limbs are not for locomotion alone; they are sensory organs and tools of immense tensile strength, capable of exerting pressure sufficient to deform the petrified bark of the Trunks of Consensus.

**Function and Behavior:**
Root-Weavers are the living embodiment of the plateau's primary directive: **assimilation**. They are drawn to active zones of industrial preservation where power still faintly hums, or where polymer cables resist decomposition. They engage in protracted battles of attrition against the inert materials.

Their work is methodical. They exude powerful chelating agents from their moist surfaces, dissolving the metal polymers into elemental ions which are then piped directly into their biomass structure, feeding the ever-expanding Trunks. When a Weaver moves, it is like watching a slow-motion avalanche of fungal matter, grinding the way forward, sealing off the past.

They are utterly indifferent to the Chrono-Fibers, viewing them as necessary but disorganized cleansers. The Weavers seek to incorporate, not just stabilize.

---

### The Logicians of Stasis: The Rare Observers

These are the rarest entities, encountered perhaps once in a generation, and they represent the apex of the environment’s weird fusion of physics and biology. They dwell exclusively near the most stable, towering Trunks, often residing in pockets carved into the deep bark where the slow, viscous sap pools.

**Appearance and Structure:**
A Logician is terrifyingly still. They appear to be composed of **perfectly preserved, clear, hardened layers of the environment’s history**. They are crystalline architectures woven from ancient, translucent cellulose, polished resin, and trapped pockets of condensation water that never evaporate. They refract the cool, stretched light into complex, silent spectra.

Their form suggests a deep understanding of the *geometry* of the Unmaking. They are not defined by mass or movement, but by their relationship to the localized distortion fields. Within a few meters of a Logician, the air thins, and the usual ambient thrum drops away, replaced by a profound, echoing silence.

**Function and Behavior:**
Logicians are believed to be the caretakers—or perhaps the *prisoners*—of the failed urban planning layer. They are not concerned with decay, but with **locking down specific moments in time**.

They interact with the environment by subtly adjusting the vibrational frequency of the surrounding biomass. They do this without physical movement, through sheer, focused temporal manipulation. Where a Chrono-Fiber cleans up a breakdown, a Logician *prevents* it by reinforcing the structural integrity against the temporal pressure. They are the stabilizing anchors that prevent the entire plateau from collapsing into a singularity of slow-motion chaos.

If one is observed, they might be 'working' on a petrified gear from the old industrial complex. The Logician remains still, but the gear might appear to briefly *unrust*, its metallic structures shimmering as if viewed through rapidly flowing water, before it settles back into its petrified state, stabilized by the Logician’s presence.

They are speculated to be the sentient remnants of the original urban planners, their biological imperative having been overwritten by the imperative to maintain the decaying, living machine they mistakenly built upon. They are the quiet, crystalline consciousness that ensures the Canopy of the Unmaking remains perpetually *on the verge* of unmaking, but never quite crosses the threshold.
