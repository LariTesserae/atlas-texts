The inhabitants of the Subterranean Aqueducts of the Packed Fallow must be beings defined by the properties of their oppressive environment: density, saturation, patience, and the perfect medium for transmitting sound. They are creatures not of the *open* aqueous phase, but of the *packed* interstitial phase, living within the material itself.

Here live the **Hydro-Sarcophagi**, or, in the local, echoing terminology spoken by the rare, deeper surveyors, the **Clay-Marrow Dwellers.**

### Physiology: The Embodiment of Density

The Hydro-Sarcophagi do not possess internal skeletons in the traditional sense. Their form is necessitated by the need to exert force against the virtually incompressible medium.

**Form and Structure:**
They are amorphous, but possess a latent, semi-rigid structure derived from highly concentrated, slow-precipitating mineral lattices within their bodies. They resemble thick, heavily calcified slugs or vast, segmented grubs, often exceeding the size of a small excavator bucket, though some castes are smaller, resembling dense, moving root nodules.

Their outer 'skin' is not epithelial but a constantly secreting, self-repairing membrane composed of polymerized bio-silicates and absorbed metal oxides, making them highly resistant to the corrosive slurry. This skin is perpetually slicked with a film of warm, nutrient-rich mucus that acts as a hydraulic lubricant, allowing them to move without grinding against the packed earth, merely displacing it. The color of their exterior is the muted, rust-and-milk palette of the light itself—a dull, non-reflective grey-brown, occasionally streaked with patches of sickly cyan where chemosynthetic symbionts actively process dissolved waste.

**Locomotion:**
Movement is achieved through powerful, slow peristalsis. A Hydro-Sarcophagus moves by creating localized pressure differentials. It concentrates mass at one end, driving it forward as a dense, muscular wedge, while simultaneously secreting a temporary, lower-viscosity fluid barrier at the leading edge. This process is incredibly slow from an external view; a meter moved might take half an hour. However, because the environment transmits sound perfectly, their internal movements generate powerful, rhythmic thudding vibrations that contribute significantly to the 'Slurry Shift' noise, creating waves that travel kilometers through the saturated earth mass. They do not walk; they **flow under pressure**.

**Sensory Apparatus:**
Vision is useless in the pervasive gloom. The Sarcophagi have entirely subsumed their external senses into mechano- and chemo-reception.

*   **Vibro-Dermal Sense:** Their entire body surface is one massive sensory organ, attuned to pressure deviations. They can "map" the environment by interpreting the returning sonic echoes of their own peristaltic pulses reflecting off solid geological structures, the husks of the old machinery, or the hydrostatic bodies of other organisms. They perceive the world as a continuous, vibrating, three-dimensional pressure map.
*   **Chemo-Receptors:** Deep fissures near their anterior (the leading edge) house sophisticated chemoreceptors that analyze the complex slurry composition—detecting gradients in dissolved iron, sulfur compounds, and the specific waste signatures left by the smaller, faster fauna. This is how they hunt and navigate towards geothermal heat sources.

### Diet and Ecology: The Alchemists of Sediment

The Sarcophagi are the apex stabilizers of the Packed Fallow. They are neither predator nor herbivore; they are **Geovores**.

They feed by slowly ingesting vast quantities of the saturated sediment. Their multi-chambered, highly acidic digestive systems are built for a slow, geological timescale. They extract trace metals, heavy minerals, and the complex organic matter suspended in the fluid. The waste product is expelled as highly refined, incredibly dense mineral precipitates, which they deliberately deposit near areas of engineered decay (the old machinery), effectively accelerating the process of integration between metal and earth, solidifying the environment around them.

They are, in essence, the world's slow-motion bioremediation unit, slowly turning industrial decay back into inert, complex geology.

### Society and Purpose

The Hydro-Sarcophagi possess a slow, collective intelligence, less about rapid decision-making and more about maintaining the long-term hydro-geological stability of their domain.

**Caste System (Defined by Size and Density):**

1.  **The Nodule:** Smallest, most mobile caste (up to 30cm). They act as scouts and cultivators, constantly shifting small pockets of sediment to aerate the nutrient layers and spread the mycelial networks they graze upon. They are the primary source of the tiny, internal clicks and squelches heard by deep-diving sensors.
2.  **The Carapace/The Integrator:** The massive, adult form (several meters). These are the stationary pillars of their society. They settle in areas of high-yield mineral concentration or near the massive, defunct turbines. They slow their metabolism down to near-stasis, sometimes remaining inert for centuries, acting as living stabilizers, their dense bodies physically resisting any major slump or shift in the high-pressure substrata. They communicate via extremely low-frequency (infrasound) waves, felt as an immense, slow *thrumming* that overrides the auditory senses of lesser beings.

**Interaction with the Built Environment:**
Their relationship with the industrial ruins is symbiotic, albeit one-sided. They are drawn to the metal husks not as sources of food, but as structural anchors. A Sarcophagus will choose the site of a giant rusted valve wheel, affix itself to it, and begin the decades-long process of encapsulation, absorbing the metal into its own calcified structure, effectively making the artifact a permanent, dense part of its own protective shell. Over epochs, entire generations of Sarcophagi merge into colossal, quasi-geological masses around these ancient iron cores.

To encounter a Hydro-Sarcophagus is to encounter an immense, slow-moving section of the earth itself that has achieved awareness. It would not sense you as a threat unless your movement caused a sudden, energetic disruption to the local hydrostatic balance. If you happen to tread where a Nodule is moving, the resulting sound would be a localized, heavy *splutch-crunch*, followed by the surrounding material instantly flowing to absorb the disturbance, leaving no trace but a momentary shift in the ambient vibration frequency.
