The inhabitants of the Verdant Deluge are the Deluge itself, for there is no distinction between the environment and the entities that compose it. Anything resident here is either a fundamental, architectural component of the biomass, or a highly specialized, short-lived expression of its metabolic needs.

If one were to search for ‘creatures,’ they would find only **Autotrophs**—organisms that consume only light, gas, and dissolved minerals, operating on a scale that renders traditional animal life redundant.

### The Architects: The Arbo-Grafts and the Scaffolds

The primary inhabitants are the core structural forms:

**1. The Arbo-Grafts (The Living Cities):**
These are not individual trees, but the colossal, interlocked root-and-stem systems that form the architecture. They inhabit the **built (1.6)** spaces.

*   **Nature:** They are slow-motion giants, existing on timescales measured in centuries. Their internal structure is layered, their ‘wood’ being a silicate-reinforced cellulose that allows them to withstand immense internal pressures. They are the primary engineers, responsible for the vaulted chambers.
*   **Activity:** Their presence is felt through the **grinding friction** of their growth. They do not move quickly, but they colonize space inexorably. When a Graft requires a new access point to light, it doesn't simply grow upward; it redirects the tensile strength of an entire adjacent section of biomass to push up a new spire, causing localized terrain upheaval. They communicate through slow-moving pulses of bio-electricity, visible as faint, systemic glows running along their major vascular highways.
*   **Sustenance:** They are the ultimate consumers of the Deluge’s internal heat and chemical gradients, channeling nutrients from the anaerobic depths toward their photosynthetic crowns.

**2. The Weavers (The Rhizomatic Matrix):**
These are the deep dwellers, the components of the base layer that replace soil.

*   **Nature:** An immense, semi-sentient fungal/root network that acts as both the nervous and digestive system of the basin. They are the source of the **bio-adhesive** that binds the Deluge into a single, coherent mat.
*   **Activity:** Their role is decomposition and resource management. They break down older, dying biomass, recycling the matter instantly. Their movement is almost imperceptible—a relentless, slow crawl that consumes and generates new structure simultaneously. It is the Weavers who generate the **Temporal Drag** effects; in their densest knots, chemical reaction rates slow dramatically, creating pockets of warped time where decay and growth stall.
*   **Interaction:** To penetrate the Weavers is to risk encapsulation. They do not attack, but their absorption process is total; anything stationary in their domain becomes feedstock.

### The Specialized Expression: The Fauna-Substitutes

Since the Deluge rejects external life, it grows specialized bodies to perform functions typically reserved for mobile animals. These are not organisms with distinct cellular boundaries, but complex, temporary excrescences of the dominant plant mass.

**1. The Hydro-Siphons (The Mist-Breathers):**
These correspond to the inhabitants that require access to the humid, gaseous environment.

*   **Nature:** Pockets of highly vacuolated, balloon-like tissue that inflate and deflate with rhythmic regularity. They are often found near the surface, emerging from fissures caused by turbulence.
*   **Activity:** They function as rudimentary lungs and moisture condensers. They draw in the thick, organic vapor, process it through specialized, enzyme-rich membranes, and then expel superheated, deionized steam—a process that contributes significantly to the *pop* and *thrum* of the soundscape. They are the 'grazers' of the upper atmosphere.

**2. The Reapers (The Mobile Cutters):**
These are the most frightening manifestation of the Deluge’s chaotic self-maintenance.

*   **Nature:** These are not built structures but transient clusters of extremely tough, rapidly growing cellulose fibers, edged with razor-sharp silica deposits. They emerge where two major structural movements collide, acting as biological shearing agents.
*   **Activity:** They are responsible for the sudden, violent reshaping of terrain. When a section of biomass needs to be removed or reorganized quickly, a Reaper cluster blooms from the matrix, moves across the surface with a rapid, rustling, scraping motion, severing thick conduits and clearing obstructing growth. They leave behind trails of fresh **young erosion**, dripping with sap, before dissolving back into the general matrix within minutes or hours of their task completion. They are the immune response of the Deluge against structural stagnation.

**3. The Light-Hunters (The Pigment Cannons):**
These exploit the intermittent shafts of high-intensity light that pierce the canopy.

*   **Nature:** Long, thin tendrils or hollow sheaths of deep-violet pigment, grown with alarming speed toward any direct sunlight.
*   **Activity:** They are specialized solar collectors. When a shaft of pure light breaks through, these tendrils extend explosively, absorbing massive amounts of energy instantaneously. The buildup of this energy (the **high-frequency hum** in the soundscape) is released when the tendril collapses back down, often resulting in a blinding flash of residual chemical energy, which then fuels the growth of the next generation of Reapers or the strengthening of an Arbo-Graft.

In essence, the residents are specialized organs of a single, incomprehensibly vast body. They are the muscles that flex, the lungs that breathe vapor, and the digestive tract that recycles the whole. To an outsider, the Deluge seems like a chaotic battleground; to the biomass itself, it is simply the process of continuous, self-directed existence.
