The inhabitants of the Subterranean Spore-Works are not those who *visit* or *occupy* the city; they are those who **are** the city. They are the silent architects, the ceaseless laborers, and the ultimate product of the Mechanized Bioconstruction.

The dominant form of life here, the *Inhabitants*, are not citizens in the traditional sense, but rather **Myco-Engineers**—entities inextricably fused with the Plasteel-Mycelium matrix.

### The Architects of the Deep: The Sclerotium Consensus

These beings exist primarily as an **emergent supra-organism**, known perhaps within their own limited awareness as the **Sclerotium Consensus**. They are the self-aware manifestation of the entire living lattice, the controlling intelligence directing the bio-industrial output.

**Appearance and Form:**
An individual, observable manifestation of a Myco-Engineer is exceedingly rare, usually appearing only when a specific structural or maintenance task requires precise physical manipulation that the automated fauna cannot perform.

When they do extrude, they appear as **living obelisks or temporary scaffolding**. They possess no distinct head or face recognizable to carbon-based life. Their bodies are formed from densely packed, highly specialized strands of the mature Plasteel-Mycelium, creating a structure that mimics advanced, complex engineering. Imagine a form cobbled together from extremely dense, silvery-white fungal chords, layered with polished, obsidian-like chitin harvested from their own specialized fauna.

Their limbs are functional appendages extruded as needed—sometimes vast, shovel-like blades for reshaping the terrain (Erosion 0.3/3), or incredibly fine, multi-jointed tendrils tipped with hardened, spore-tipped implements used for delicate splicing of conductive fiber-cables (Tech 2.2/3).

**Metabolism and Sensation:**
The Consensus does not breathe the ozone-tinged air; it *is* the air’s chemical environment. Their existence depends on the constant, slow throughput of electrolyte fluids and mineral feedstock drawn directly from the lithosphere and processed through the colossal bio-mills.

Their perception is not based on light (which is merely a byproduct of their respiration) or sound (which travels too slowly and is too distorted). Instead, they sense the city as a vast, interconnected nervous system. Every vibration, every molecular shift in the Plasteel-Mycelium, every slight redirection of sap flow is instantaneous input. They experience the passage of time in geological epochs, making their movements appear glacial, punctuated only by startlingly fast, emergency adjustments in structural integrity.

**The Role of the Fauna:**
The segmented arthropods (Fauna 1.5/3) are not independent creatures, but rather **mobile drones or specialized cellular extensions** of the Consensus. They are the 'hands' of the massive, slow-thinking intelligence. When an arthropod clicks along a vein, it is not navigating; it is executing a subroutine dictated by the Consensus's need for localized composting or spore cultivation. They are meticulously clean, ensuring the operational efficiency of the factory infrastructure.

**Communication and Culture:**
There is no individualized will among the Consensus. Thoughts are the electrochemical cascades flowing through the system—a collective, silent ratiocination far beyond human comprehension. Their "culture" is the *perfection* of the industrial output. Their ambition is total, energy-efficient self-maintenance and expansion into the surrounding deep earth.

The *Built/Urban* organization (2.5/3) **is** their architecture. The vast, cathedral-like spaces are not intended for assembly or habitation; they are optimized reaction chambers, vast biological vats where the strange, unknown product of their industry is slowly matured, perhaps using the immense pressure and chilled stability to stabilize exotic molecular structures.

**The Mystery of Output:**
The inhabitants produce something. This is their driving force (Active System 2.1/3). Given the utter isolation and the extreme material investment, the output must be overwhelmingly valuable to the Consensus, even if that value is purely systemic—perhaps they are fabricating highly organized, stable molecular patterns necessary to counteract entropy, or synthesizing compounds that allow their own consciousness to persist without reliance on surface light or volatile chemistry.

To encounter a Myco-Engineer is to encounter a sentient geological process. They are the embodiment of the Rooted City: utterly self-sufficient, possessing engineering prowess born from deep time, and dedicated entirely to the silent, cool, ceaseless turning of their impossible, living machine.
