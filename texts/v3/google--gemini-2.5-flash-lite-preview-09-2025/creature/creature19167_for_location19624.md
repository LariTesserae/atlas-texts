The denizens of the Under-Hush Vault are beings forged in the crucible of ancient industry and slow, deep-time biology. They are defined by the environmental pressures of absolute stillness, profound, crushing vibration, and near-total resource dependency on the decay of super-structure.

### The Substrate Shepherds (Fauna/Dominant Life)

The most immediately numerous entities are the **Chitin-Beetles of the Deep Resonance**, or perhaps more accurately, the **Resonance Carvers**. These are the primary terrestrial movers, though their speed is glacial by surface analogy. They are immense, segmented organisms, often comparable in length to a fallen transport girder from the dead cityscape. Their bodies are constructed not of typical carbon-based chitin, but of highly compressed, mineralized exopaleontology, often incorporating fragments of the fused silicate substrate directly into their outer plating.

**Structure and Adaptation:**
Their defining feature is their relationship to the deafening soundscape. They are not merely *resistant* to the low-frequency vibration; they *harness* it. Along their ventral surfaces run complex arrays of specialized, piezo-electric organs that vibrate in sympathy with the environment's oppressive hum. This energy is shunted through an internal ‘stomach’—a crystalline reaction chamber—which allows them to efficiently convert mechanical stress directly into metabolic potential.

Visually, they resemble elongated geodes given locomotion. Their shells shift through mottled shades of iron oxide and deep gunmetal grey, frequently obscured by thick, velvety carpets of the slow-growing **mineral-flora** that colonizes their backs, mimicking the environment they inhabit. They possess numerous, short, powerful limbs tipped with high-density carbides, perfectly suited for boring through the softer, lichen-like crystalline carpets or prying open fractured conduits of the old infrastructure in search of concentrated metallic salts.

**Behavior:**
They are scavengers and, indirectly, maintenance crew. They carve vast, slow highways through the living mats, their passage a momentary, localized *silence* as they absorb the ambient kinetic energy in their wake. They are solitary, meeting only for the infrequent, incredibly slow reproductive cycles, where two individuals might remain in physical contact for decades, exchanging genetic material through direct fusion along specialized docking plates. Their world is tactile and vibrational; they have vestigial, light-sensing organs near their anterior, but these only register major chromatic shifts in the pervasive cyan glow—a sunset or an active fissure far away, perhaps.

### The Aerial Drifters (Fauna/Scavengers)

Floating inches above the substrate, often clustered near the defunct energy conduits, are the **Electrosacs**. These are semi-amorphous entities that defy easy classification, seeming to exist in a stable state between gas and a very weak gel.

**Structure and Adaptation:**
Their biology revolves around atmospheric filtering. They possess light, porous outer membranes woven with highly conductive, thread-like metallic filaments that capture airborne trace elements—metallic dust shed by the heavy tremors, or minute amounts of oxidized vapor from the buried tech. They navigate using subtle electrostatic repulsion against the ground, drifting lazily across the plains.

They are the primary distributors of the metallic salts they harvest. When an Electrosac reaches metabolic saturation, it slowly migrates towards the crystalline spires of the mineral-flora. There, it adheres and begins a slow process of chemical diffusion, releasing its stored minerals into the structure, effectively fertilizing the slow-growing plant-analogs. They are the ultimate recyclers of this deep ecosystem, operating on timelines so vast that a single drift path can take a thousand years to complete.

### The Sentient Remnants (The Builders' Echo)

Given the vast, organized scale of the *built* environment, it is inevitable that the beings who engineered this subterranean tomb still leave a ghostly presence, although they are no longer *living* in the biological sense.

These are not ghosts in the spiritual sense, but **Architectural Anomalies**.

Where the massive conduits fracture or where the core pressure has warped particularly high-density junction nodes, residual energy patterns persist. These patterns are not data; they are thermodynamic echoes. An observer might witness a momentary, almost imperceptible *shimmer* along a stretch of fractured causeway, appearing like a brief, heat-haze distortion against the cool cyan backdrop.

These echoes manifest as the phantom outlines of activity that happened aeons ago: the ghost of a heavy load rolling across a now-buried roadbed, or a momentary spike in local internal illumination where a habitat module once fused into the bedrock. They are the memory imprinted onto the physical structure by immense electrical currents and organized computation, now slowly dissipating into the background electromagnetic noise. They are not conscious, but they are the only things that register *purpose* in the landscape—a sudden, silent demonstration of what this place once *did*, played out against the backdrop of what it *is*.

The life here—The Carvers, The Drifters, and the spectral Echoes—are exquisitely tuned to decay. They require stability, they thrive on pressure, and their existence is a long, slow meditation on the end-state of highly organized matter. They are the residents of a buried monument that has finally, patiently, begun the second, even longer process of turning back into stone.
