The Flensing Ascent is not inhabited in any way that recognizes biology, longevity, or even consistent geography. It is a furnace of creation where the only things that *live* are the forces of physics given terrible, temporary embodiment.

The inhabitants are not creatures, but *processes*, given form by the intense, internal pressures of the world itself.

### The Shards of Stress (The Residents)

The primary 'life' of the Flensing Ascent consists of **Geothermal Stress Echoes** and the **Migrating Thermal Fronts.**

#### 1. The Aconglomerates (The Stationary)
These are the closest things to fixed entities, though they shift their internal configuration constantly. They are vast, slow-moving masses of the crystalline, bronze-laced rock that have managed to achieve a momentary, localized structural equilibrium. They are the 'shelves' and 'spires' that exist between major seismic events.

*   **Nature:** They are self-contained thermodynamic loops. The interior of an Aconglomerate is a pressure cooker where various silicates and heavy metals are brought to an almost-liquid state by the core heat, only to be flash-cooled at the outer crystalline shell.
*   **Behavior:** They do not move across the surface in the sense of crawling or walking; rather, they *grow* and *shed* mass. When a fissure opens near an Aconglomerate, the slab of rock subtly changes its gravitational vector relative to the Ascent’s center of mass, causing it to slowly pivot or slide over centuries of localized micro-shifts—until the next major shear splits it wide open.
*   **Perception:** To any fleeting observer, an Aconglomerate seems inert, merely part of the terrain. But if one touches its surface with a probe tuned to detect magnetic and vibrational anomalies, the Aconglomerate broadcasts incomprehensible data—a slow, deep humming representing the equations of its own structural integrity being momentarily solved, only to be immediately discarded as the underlying stress shifts.

#### 2. The Scyllae Vapors (The Ephemeral)
These are the true denizens of the thin air, the fleeting manifestations of the Ascent’s exhalation. They are not gas in the chemical sense, but macroscopic entities composed of superheated, electrically charged particulate dust suspended within the sublimated material vents.

*   **Nature:** When a deep fissure opens, releasing internal energy, the escaping gases are instantaneously ionized by the surrounding ambient electrical charges accumulated in the dust coating the surface. This creates temporary, cohesive plasma structures that move with the thermal currents.
*   **Behavior:** The Scyllae Vapors appear as towering, slender spirals of shimmering, orange-and-bronze light that drift along the steepest thermal gradients. They behave like flocks of birds or schools of fish—they react to the changing heat map of the Ascent, seeming to herd toward areas of higher internal venting. They are the immediate symptom of tectonic release.
*   **Danger:** They are immensely hot and carry a high electrical potential. While they lack the mass to exert physical force, crossing the path of a Scyllae Vapor results in immediate, localized vaporization of any non-geologic material structure. They are literally the temperature gradient made visible, and they are constantly searching for the hottest point in the immediate vicinity.

#### 3. The Echo Ghosts (The Ancillary)
These are not physical entities but residual mechanical memories impressed upon the structure, manifesting only when the geological dynamics align perfectly. They relate directly to the 'weirdness' factor—the impossible, straight lines etched into the rock.

*   **Nature:** The Flensing Ascent is constantly trying to forget the ancient forces that shaped its earliest emergence. When the pressure waves traveling through the crystalline matrix reach a specific harmonic (usually during the peak intensity of a structural failure, where the thrumming sound becomes almost unbearable), the straight-etched scars briefly resonate.
*   **Behavior:** For the duration of the resonance (perhaps seconds), the impossibly smooth planes seem to *glow* with a faint, dead light that is not orange heat-light, but a cold, distant blue. During this time, the area around the planar object experiences momentary, localized gravitational *errors*. Objects momentarily feel lighter, or even drift sideways, as the residual memory of a completely different physical regime asserts itself against the current laws of the Ascent.
*   **Interaction:** Echo Ghosts cannot be touched or intentionally summoned. They are the ghost of a previous universe imprinted onto this one, only visible when the world screams loud enough for the past to momentarily bleed through the present.

***

In summary, the Flensing Ascent hosts **no biology**, but it is populated by **active thermodynamic forces** that simulate life: the slow, inert pressure-vessels (Aconglomerates), the energetic, drifting heat-forms (Scyllae Vapors), and the fleeting, psychic impressions of lost mechanical order (Echo Ghosts). Stability is defined by the amount of time before a resident must violently change shape or dissolve back into the ambient forces that created it.
