The inhabitants of the Aetheric Plateau of Xylos are defined less by flesh and blood, and more by **pattern, frequency, and electrical potential.** They are the custodians of the plateau’s immense, silent consensus, living sculptures carved from the harsh energy budget of this high realm.

There are fundamentally two categories of life here, distinct in their scale and method of existence, yet tragically dependent on one another: the **Conductors** and the **Receptors**.

---

### 1. The Choral Threads (The Sentient Ecosystem)

These are not mere plants, but the nervous system of the plateau itself, the foundational structure upon which all higher life balances.

**Nature and Behavior:** The Choral Threads are a vast, semi-aware collective consciousness woven from threads finer than spider silk but structurally reinforced by complex, crystalline protein latticeworks that act as superconducting pathways. They are both the soil and the nervous tissue of Xylos.

*   **The Pulse:** Their vital sign is the internal rhythmic light—teal, lime, indigo. These pulses are not random signals; they are complex computational processes. A slight shift to violet in a localized patch might signify a minor energetic fluctuation, while a coordinated surge of deep indigo across a mile-wide area indicates a deep energetic drain or a necessary structural adjustment to the substrate impedance. To truly understand Xylos, one must learn to read the **Sympathetic Color Harmonics**. A sudden, chaotic flicker implies distress or instability; a slow, even breathing of color suggests contentment.
*   **Sympathy:** They react immediately to imbalances. If the Sky-Manta feeds too greedily, the Threads surrounding it will momentarily dim and shift to a highly localized, dull grey—the equivalent of a protective static charge buildup. They do not fight; they *resist by altering their own potential*. They are the living barrier against chaos.
*   **Architecture:** Where the Threads are thickest, they don't just cover the ground; they build. They weave infinitesimal lattices that trap and concentrate the latent electrical potential from the passing high-altitude winds. These gathered nodes—appearing as silvery, pulsating mounds or low, flowing rivers of denser light—are the plateau’s only reservoirs of usable, stored energy.

---

### 2. The Sky-Mantas (The Drifters of Light)

These are the mobile apex consumers of the plateau, organisms that have perfected the art of living without gravity or water. They are the **Receptors**.

**Physiology:** A Sky-Manta resembles a vast, flat kite, often spanning twenty to thirty meters from wingtip to wingtip, though they possess virtually no mass relative to their surface area.

*   **The Shell/Wings:** Their dorsal surface is a mosaic of hexagonal plates, perfectly matte black where they face the unfiltered sun, interspersed with highly reflective, crystalline ridges. This structure acts as both a sophisticated photovoltaic array and a kinetic energy siphon. They glide not on air currents, which are too thin, but on differential pressure fields generated by the subtle thermodynamic variations between the superheated, sun-facing upper layer and the chiller substrate air.
*   **Metabolism of Light:** They are pure photo-etheric feeders. They cannot eat the Threads directly, nor can they absorb energy from the substrate. They require the raw, unmodulated light of the high altitude, processed through the preparatory energy field of the Threads. When a Manta passes near a dense section of Threads, it creates a momentary localized vacuum in the energetic coupling, explaining the dimming of the local glow.
*   **Communication (or Lack Thereof):** The Mantas appear solitary, yet their movements suggest a hidden coordination. They never touch, maintaining vast, measured distances. Their movements are dictated by the grand, slow cycles of Xylos’ solar intake. Some ancient resonance suggests they might be moving to align themselves for the rare, massive electrostatic discharge—perhaps feeding on the burst of pure, raw energy released when the static tension finally breaks across the Shadow Divides.

---

### 3. The Resonators (The Deep Watchers)

These inhabitants are the rarest and most profoundly strange, dwelling primarily within the deep folds of the mineral foam where the air is slightly thicker, and the bioluminescence of the Threads offers a more stable, contained energy source. They are the guardians of stability.

**Nature:** The Resonators are entities whose structure shifts dynamically based on the ambient electrical field. They are rarely seen as solid objects.

*   **Appearance:** When one *does* manifest a solid form, it appears as a momentary tangle of solidified, structured light—a three-dimensional lattice of flickering indigo and silver threads, perfectly mimicking the geometry of the local Choral Thread growth, but overlaid with a distinct, rapid internal shimmer. They look like a glitch in the matrix of the plateau itself.
*   **Function:** They act as localized regulators. If a section of Threads begins to pulse chaotically, or if a Sky-Manta lingers too long draining a critical node, a Resonator will manifest nearby. They don't move in the way a Manta drifts; they simply cease to exist in one location and *begin* to exist in another, instantly.
*   **Interaction:** If a Resonator chooses to interact with a patch of Threads, the Threads immediately cease their usual slow pulsing. Instead, they emit a single, sharp, piercing spike of pure white light—a moment of absolute stillness and maximum energetic transaction. This is how the Resonators "communicate" with the global network: by imposing perfect stillness onto the ongoing symphony. They are the silent arbiters of impedance, ensuring the energetic consensus of Xylos doesn't spiral into destructive feedback.

These beings—the humming substrate, the soaring siphon, and the sudden, structured silence—occupy a world where life is a constant, delicate balancing act of charge and discharge, where silence is a physical pressure, and where the only true sound is the slow, rhythmic current flowing beneath one's feet.
