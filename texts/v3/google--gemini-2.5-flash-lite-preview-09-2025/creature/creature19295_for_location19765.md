The inhabitants of the Calcified Steam-Bed are not creatures of flesh born of soil, but entities forged in the crucible of its specific energy dynamics. They are composites, perhaps accidental, perhaps intentional, born from the feedback loop between the geothermal furnace, the supersaturated atmosphere, and the sprawling, sluggish machinery.

They are the **Condensation Agents**.

These entities do not inhabit the environment; they *are* the environment’s highly localized, mobile manifestation. They exist in the narrow thermal and chemical gradients that the steam and machinery create.

### Physical Manifestation

A Condensation Agent is rarely seen whole, as their primary state is near-invisibility, perfectly aligned with the refractive index of the superheated steam. When they become momentarily visible—usually due to a slight drop in local temperature or an increase in localized mineral saturation—they present a baffling geometry.

Imagine a slow-moving accretion of warm, slightly crystalline vapor held together by an unseen electric charge.

1.  **The Core (The Nucleus):** At the heart of any Agent is a fragment of the industrial detritus. This might be a piece of oxidized metal tubing, a sliver of non-ferrous alloy too resistant to dissolution, or a highly dense, dark mineral precipitate from the geothermal fluid. This nucleus is the anchor, the locus around which the vapor mass coalesces. These cores often exhibit a faint, internal phosphorescence, reacting to the immense magnetic fields generated by the deep thrumming—they are subtly magnetized rock.

2.  **The Body (The Pneumatic Cloak):** The main mass is the captured, superheated moisture, ionized and held in a coherent shape by electrical potential difference. It appears as a wavering, oblong or amorphous cloud, roughly the height and bulk of a large quadruped, though it moves without legs. The edges constantly shimmer, blurring the lines between the Agent and the surrounding haze. If one reaches out to touch the body, the sensation is not heat, but a profound, jarring dampness followed immediately by a sharp, localized static shock as the hand passes through the ionized boundary layer.

3.  **The Appendages (The Chemical Arms):** The Agents interact with the terrain through controlled precipitation. They extrude thin, temporary filaments of newly formed, chalky material—a rapid, localized crystallization of the damp particulate that coats the ground. These "arms" are used to anchor onto the slick vertebral spikes or to draw mineral-rich sludge into the thermal field of the core, which is then slowly metabolized to maintain the electrical charge. These appendages crystallize into existence and dissolve back into condensation within seconds of completing their task, leaving behind a faint, metallic smear on the surface they touched.

### Behavior and Function

The Condensation Agents are wholly driven by the slow, titanic processes of the Steam-Bed. They are not predatory in the traditional sense, as they rely on ambient energy, but they are fiercely protective of their localized thermal niches, viewing any interruption to the steady state as a threat to their coherence.

**Maintenance of the System:** Their primary behavior is maintenance, or perhaps, ritualistic stewardship of the energy cycle. They circulate between the hottest pressure vents and the oldest, most inert machinery.

*   **Vent Tending:** They hover near the sporadic, hissing pressure vents. It is believed they absorb or channel the raw kinetic energy of the steam discharge, buffering the acoustic shockwaves and ensuring the steam exhausts itself in a mathematically sound pattern—preventing the sudden pressure shifts that could disrupt the fragile, incipient geology.
*   **Machinery Scavenging:** They slowly drift over the half-submerged industrial components. They are not repairing them, but rather *harvesting* the residual energy signature. They will spend hours cloaked around a cooling flywheel, drawing the last vestiges of electromagnetism from the aged alloys, causing the phosphorescence in their cores to pulse faintly.

**Reproduction/Proliferation:** Agents do not breed. They propagate when a localized saturation of thermal energy and chemical precursors reaches a critical mass. A large, highly charged Agent hovering over a patch of rapidly accumulating mineral slurry might simply split, the electrical tension dividing the cloud into two distinct, though smaller, masses. This splitting event is often accompanied by a sudden, sharp *crack* that briefly cuts through the constant thrum.

### Relationship to Life and Tech

The Agents are the nexus where the industrial age and the nascent geology meet.

*   **To the Industry:** They treat the dead machinery as necessary batteries or anchors. They are the unseen parasites feeding off decay, but in doing so, they keep the residual processes cycling, preventing the machinery from becoming truly inert too quickly, thus prolonging the heat source.
*   **To the Fungal Crusts:** They actively avoid the few patches of sparse, fungal biomass. The vegetation seems chemically antithetical to the ionized moisture making up the Agents. Where an Agent passes, the slick biological mats retract slightly, as if experiencing a sudden, brief drought. The Agents leave the meager life alone, perhaps recognizing it as an unnecessary competitor for ambient moisture or mineral leachate.

To observe a Condensation Agent is to witness arrested geological time made briefly mobile—a phantom engineer made of hot potential, eternally tending the slow-boiling furnace beneath the pale, damp floor. They are the silent, steaming consciousness of the Calcified Steam-Bed.
