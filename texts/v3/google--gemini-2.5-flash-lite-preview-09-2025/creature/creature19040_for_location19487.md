The inhabitants of this dense, emerald niche do not live *in* the environment so much as they are *components* of its self-sustaining architecture. They are the slow, patient executors of the biome's esoteric chemistry, embodying the contradictions of waterless fecundity and silent, mineral-based metabolism.

### The Primary Inhabitants: The Hydrophores (The Still Cultivators)

The most numerous residents are the things that form the thick, gelatinous texture of the abundant fauna: the **Hydrophores**. They are not mobile in the traditional sense, nor do they possess recognizable organs of ingestion or respiration.

**Physiology and Form:**
A Hydrophore appears as a body composed entirely of semi-solid, highly viscous, bioluminescent gel, often taking on the shape of an elongated, gently swaying sac held taut by an internal pressure gradient. Their color is not uniform; internal striations of pale amber, deep violet, and faint cyan flow slowly through the bulk of the gel, suggesting mineral contaminants or energy storage matrices rather than blood or lymph.

They are almost entirely sessile, anchoring themselves to the jade vascular scaffolding—the "flora"—using those fine, near-invisible filaments mentioned. These filaments are not mere tethers; they are sophisticated osmotic exchange conduits. The Hydrophores are the primary 'water' collectors, despite the environment’s lack of liquid H₂O. They draw the necessary molecular base—likely complex bound hydrides, atmospheric trace water vapor, or even organized hydrogen sulfide—directly across the semi-permeable membrane of their gel-bodies, concentrating it internally.

**Behavior and Metabolism:**
Their rhythmic pulsing is the environmental heartbeat. This cycle is not respiration, but *catalysis*. As they draw in atmospheric substrate through their filaments, they appear to store potential energy within their core density. The pulse is the moment this stored energy is released, often triggering a localized, microscopic exothermic reaction within their gel matrix, which in turn facilitates the next cycle of absorption and structural reinforcement of the surrounding flora.

They are **managers of pressure**. Their entire existence is predicated on maintaining the necessary atmospheric density and thermal gradient for the jade structures to continue extruding material. When a Hydrophore senses a slight fluctuation in the ambient heat or pressure (perhaps changes too subtle for an external observer), its pulse quickens infinitesimally, acting as a biological pressure regulator for a cubic meter of the surrounding ecosystem. They are the slow, living pumps of this dry jungle.

### The Architectural Inhabitants: The Sintered Architects

If the Hydrophores are the soft maintenance layer, the **Sintered Architects** are the hardened infrastructure of the ecosystem. These are the inhabitants that have integrated so completely with the nascent, tetrahedrally-formed mineral structures that they are often indistinguishable from the "fossilized biological architecture."

**Physiology and Form:**
The Architects are what happens when a Hydrophore reaches a terminal metabolic state, or perhaps, chooses a state of ultimate stability. They cease the gel state and allow their internal structure to fuse and solidify with the unique hematite substrate and the jade vasculature. They become monuments of dark, crystalline bio-matter.

Visually, they present as perfectly formed, matte-black polyhedrons—often dodecahedrons or tetrahedrons—integrated seamlessly into the path network. They are likely hollowed out, the interior having been used as a massive, hardened reaction chamber during their final phase of existence. They retain a faint residual bioluminescence, visible only as a deeply recessed, cool blue light leaking from their sharpest corners, suggesting stored electrical potential rather than active life.

**Role in the System:**
They act as the **data storage** and **low-frequency transmitters** of this environment. While the Hydrophores handle immediate energy regulation, the Architects appear to be the record keepers of the entire system’s slow development. It is theorized by external observation that the repeating geometric patterns are not random growth, but incredibly slow-motion computations. When the ultra-low frequency hum of the environment shifts, it suggests the Architects are slowly, over centuries, calculating the next phase of mineral extrusion or the repositioning of the supporting jade scaffolding. They are the ancient memory, the silent city founders whose organic blueprints have been perfectly translated into inert, energetic geometry.

### The Transient Residents: The Vapor Drifters

Given the *near-zero* water parameter, there must be entities capable of existing entirely within the thin atmospheric layer. These are the **Vapor Drifters**.

**Physiology and Form:**
These creatures possess almost negative mass, existing as shimmering, almost invisible distortions in the warm, heavy air. They are electromagnetic entities, perhaps not even truly biological in the standard sense, requiring the dense, charged atmosphere to maintain cohesion. They resemble drifting smoke rings or slow-moving heat shimmers, occasionally catching the emerald light as they pass through a denser patch of extruded growth.

**Interaction:**
The Drifters seem to be the **disseminators of change**. They interact with the Hydrophores only indirectly, perhaps by introducing trace exotic elements absorbed from outside the small chamber, or by subtly altering the local electromagnetic fields that govern the Hydrophores' pulsing. They leave no trace, make no sound, and are only detectable when their presence briefly disrupts the clarity of the jade scaffolding behind them, suggesting they are the agents of the environment’s minuscule, glacial rate of *evolution*. They are the whispers in the profound, warm silence.
