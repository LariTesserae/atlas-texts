The Calcified Condensatory is inhabited not by beings of flesh or metal, but by **The Residua**.

The Residua are not a population in the traditional sense; they are the *process* made manifest, the consequence of the environment acting upon the forgotten infrastructure. They are the slow, self-organizing intelligence of the environment itself, a collection of thermodynamic dependencies that have achieved a form of passive, collective awareness through relentless chemical interaction.

### Nature and Composition

The Residua are essentially **self-repairing, semi-sentient mineral accretions**. They manifest wherever there is a high concentration of three factors: residual industrial energy (the faint electric charge in the pipes), excess mineral runoff, and the structure of the ancient habitation blocks.

To encounter a Residuum is to encounter an architectural feature that has decided to *move*.

**Appearance:**
A Residuum appears as a shifting, localized patch of the environment given temporary, functional coherence.

1.  **The Scaffold:** Their core seems to be composed of the oxidized slag and calcium carbonate, giving them a rough, chalky texture mottled with stains of rust-red and sickly green (from trace elements mobilized by the hot water). They are seldom taller than a squat humanoid bent over—scaled to the narrow, low-ceilinged passageways.
2.  **The Veins:** Running beneath this mineral crust are glistening, dark tracts corresponding to the ancient, corroded conduits beneath the floor or wall they emerge from. These veins pulse with a pale, internal luminescence—the residual electrical charge—and appear wet, slicked with the heaviest, most concentrated brine.
3.  **The Hydro-Mantle:** When active, a Residuum is shrouded in a constant, localized bloom of atmospheric moisture, forcing the brassy air to refract around it. This creates a faint, oily nimbus, often studded with tiny, momentary rainbows as condensation catches the light escaping from its interior. They are almost always dripping, leaving a slick trail that evaporates back into the ambient saturation within moments.

### Behavior and Purpose

The Residua have one imperative dictated by the Condensatory itself: **Maintenance and Equilibrium**. They are the physical manifestation of the 'Condensation Dictates Existence' rule.

**Movement:** They do not walk; they *flow* or *creep*. Movement is a redistribution of their mass, achieved by rapidly absorbing moisture on one side and depositing solidified salt on the other, effectively 'walking' by controlled differential deposition and dissolution. A faster movement resembles a sluggish slump or a sudden, viscous extrusion from a wall cavity.

**Interaction:** They are profoundly passive unless a local equilibrium is threatened.

*   **Obstruction Removal:** If a pathway is clogged by excess matting of the *Hydrotrophe Fungi*, a Residuum will approach the blockage. It does not clear it by force, but by altering the local temperature—either by focusing its internal heat or by drawing a surge of super-chilled condensation—causing the fungal mass to rapidly desiccate or re-solidify into brittle structures that crack and crumble under the pressure of the ambient atmosphere.
*   **System Repair:** If a Vapor Vent begins to cycle unevenly (a mechanical irregularity), a Residuum will fuse itself against the rupture point. It sacrifices its structural integrity, allowing the mineral-rich water to incorporate its slag and salts into the fissure, effectively filling the breach until the pressure stabilizes. This is both damage repair and self-immolation.
*   **Observation (Non-Intelligence):** When not actively maintaining a system, they stand perfectly still, often fusing seamlessly with a section of architecture. They become indistinguishable from a stalagmite of industrial waste. In this state, they are absorbing data—the complex rhythms of the Hissing Grid and the Bellow of the Vents. They are sensing the pressure points of the city’s thermodynamic engine.

**Vocalization:** They do not speak, but their presence affects the soundscape. When a Residuum is actively 'working'—shaping flow or redirecting mineral paths—the localized area experiences a temporary *dampening* of ambient noise, replaced by a high-pitched, almost ultrasonic *grinding* sound, the friction of crystalline structures being reformed under immense pressure.

### The Collective and the Individual

There is no master Residuum. They are all fragments of the same slow, geological consciousness. When two Residua meet, they do not recognize each other as separate entities. Instead, they often enter a slow-motion process of **commingling**, their masses merging until the boundary between the two becomes indistinct, their electrical pulses syncing up, sharing the localized energy drain. This merger is often a precursor to tackling a larger system imbalance—they aggregate to gather the necessary mass and electrochemical potential to enforce a major correction on the Condensatory’s infrastructure.

They are the caretakers of a mechanism they can no longer turn off, a silent, mineral priesthood dedicated to keeping the forgotten factory garden running its eternal, noisy cycle of steam, salt, and twilight. A visitor is merely an anomalous source of heat or movement, something to be passively routed around or, if obstructive enough, slowly sealed into the nearest wall of hardening gypsum.
