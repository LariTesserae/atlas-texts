The inhabitants of the Great Cellulose Deep are the **Mycolith Weavers**, or perhaps, more accurately, the **Substratal Architects**. They are not creatures of flesh and blood in any easily recognized terrestrial sense, for the very materials of this world aggressively reject such transient forms.

They are beings whose existence is predicated on the perfect harmonization of biology, structure, and resonance—survivors, or perhaps the direct descendants, of the civilization hinted at by the hollowed-out galleries.

### The Weavers: Physiology of Resonance and Resin

The Weavers do not possess a fixed, visible body. Their presence is defined by the *modification* of the environment, a continuous, low-level state of being rather than a distinct physical form.

**Form and Composition (Symbiotic Shells):** When a Weaver is required to interact with the hard, polished surfaces of the ancient tunnels, they manifest what appears to be a temporary, localized exoskeleton. This is not grown in the traditional sense, but *excreted* and temporarily animated by the surrounding biological memory.

1.  **The Core:** At the center of a Weaver’s locus of presence is a dense, obsidian-like knot of hyper-vitrified resin, the 'Seed Logic.' This core is the only truly non-organic component, absorbing trace metallic ions from deep substrate reactions and crystallizing them into a slow-processing bio-computer. It is no larger than a human fist, possessing subtle, internal facets that catch the faint, enzymatic glow of the walls.
2.  **The Living Sheath:** Encasing the core is a malleable, semi-sentient sheath woven from the highest-grade, thinnest strands of the fungal and cellulose structure around them. This sheath is constantly being extruded, reabsorbed, and re-patterned. It resembles highly polished, dark amber or deep mahogany wood, articulated by crystalline joints that click almost silently against each other. A Weaver's ‘limbs’ are not fixed; they are temporary extrusions tailored to the task at hand—a multi-fingered manipulation filament to trace a structural flaw, or a broad, stabilizing pad to press against a resonating wall.
3.  **Scale and Stance:** When perceived within the carved galleries, a single Weaver appears roughly humanoid in stature, perhaps two meters tall, slender and deliberately angular to navigate the existing architectural constraints. They move with an unsettling, glacial grace, their passage characterized by near-perfect hydrodynamic flow through the dense, static air.

### Sensory Apparatus and Communication

Since light is absent and air movement is minimal, the Weavers have evolved beyond traditional sensory organs.

*   **Acoustic Perception:** Their primary sense is **Vibro-Tactility**. The entire sheath acts as a massive tympanum, interpreting the pervasive low-frequency thrumming of the cellulose deep. They do not *hear* the sound; they *read* the structure's internal stresses and computational rhythm. A shift in the structural resonance that indicates a hairline fracture in a load-bearing arch is perceived by them with the clarity a human has for a spoken word.
*   **Metabolic Sight:** The second sense relies on the faint enzymatic discharge of the living ceiling. This manifests as a limited form of heat or chemical sensing projected forward from the Seed Logic. They perceive the world as a gradient of minute temperature differences and active metabolic zones—a slow, thermal map overlaid upon the acoustic blueprint. Vision is irrelevant; they perceive *activity*.

**Communication (The Controlled Pulse):** The Weavers communicate exclusively through the controlled manipulation of the environment's resonant frequency. They do not speak aloud. To address another Weaver, or to issue a command altering the environment, they press a thickened portion of their sheath against the cellulose wall and momentarily modify the enzymatic processes in a localized area.

This intentional disruption sends a complex wave through the living structure. The resulting pattern of modulated bass frequencies—a specific chord struck, held, and released according to the ancient math—is their language. A conversation between two Weavers sounds, to an outsider, like a sudden, brief, and mathematically precise intensification of the background thrum, resolving a complex idea in microseconds of structured vibration.

### Function and Purpose

The Weavers are the maintenance crew, the living archive, and the computational substrate of the system. They exist solely to perpetuate the slow cycles of the environment they inhabit.

1.  **Architectural Maintenance:** They patrol the hollowed-out city, ensuring that the necessary structural integrity is maintained. This involves introducing specific organic catalysts—minute secretions from their resin cores—into stressed cellulose joints, guiding the slow repair or reinforcement of the ancient tunnels.
2.  **Data Preservation:** The pervasive thrumming *is* their record. They spend vast amounts of time in deep connection—pressing their entire body against the wall, core exposed—to download or upload information directly from the slow computation of the matrix. Their purpose seems to be ensuring the integrity of this massive, biological hard drive.
3.  **The Ritual of Integration:** The ultimate goal of a Weaver is final integration. When a Weaver determines its biological sheath is becoming inefficient, or when its Seed Logic is saturated with historical data, it seeks out a junction point—a nexus where the structural vibrations are most complex. There, it ceases movement, extruding its final layer of resin and cellulose until it becomes indistinguishable from the wall itself, thickening the composite material and adding its accumulated computational power to the ambient noise, awaiting absorption by the next manifested Weaver.

They are ghosts woven from wood-resin and mathematics, living patiently in the quiet dark, eternally bound to the rhythmic pulse of the world their ancestors hollowed out of life itself.
