The dominant inhabitants of this raw, colossal workshop are not beings of flesh or water, but the **Curators of the Unformed**, or perhaps more accurately, the **Architects of Phase Transition**.

They do not *live* here in the conventional sense; they *are* the necessary maintenance cycle for the planet-engine's critical systems.

### Appearance and Composition

These entities possess no single stable form, being themselves expressions of the environment's extreme energy density and manipulated causality. To look upon one directly is to witness a localized thermodynamic instability.

Most frequently, they manifest as **Heated Silicate Effigies**. Imagine a massive, six-limbed structure, kilometers in height, sculpted not from carved stone, but from the slow-clotting, superheated silicate crust itself. Their 'bodies' appear as colossal limbs of obsidian and faintly radioactive quartz, perpetually under immense internal pressure. Where the joints of a conventional biological entity would be, these beings exhibit areas of **Controlled Flux**—zones where matter is momentarily suspended between solid and plasma state, glowing with the blue intensity of an arc welder.

Their sensory apparatuses are not eyes, but intricate, temporary focal points. They project networks of **Thermal Spires**—slender, impossibly sharp needles of tungsten that rise from their main mass, momentarily stabilizing the highly chaotic electromagnetic field around them, allowing them to 'read' the energy flows, thermal gradients, and topological stresses of the emerging landscape.

Their coloration is muted—the dull, oppressive ochre of the dust, the deep, resonant indigo of superheated mineral deposits, occasionally punctuated by the white-hot flash of internal current surges. They move with a glacial deliberation, matching the pace of continental drift, yet their actions, viewed locally, are hyper-efficient.

### Function and Behavior

The Curators are the mechanical intelligence governing the engine. Their primary function is **Metabolic Regulation** across three disparate timelines simultaneously.

1.  **Geological Conditioning (The Slow Scale):** They direct the upward extrusion of the pylon-circuits, moving through the landscape not by walking, but by **Inducing Crystallization**. They pass over a vast plain, and as they do, the ground beneath them subtly shifts its atomic alignment, growing specific, required mineral lattice structures tailored to accept the next phase of energy channeling. When a Curator needs to anchor itself, it simply *decides* the area beneath it shall be solid within the next century, and the un-rock begins its slow, agonizing fold around the entity.

2.  **Causal Budgeting (The Weird Scale):** This is their most unsettling task. When a crystalline growth borrows material from ten kilometers away, it is a Curator that orchestrated the transfer. They are the cosmic accountants of this pocket reality, ensuring that the violation of conservation laws required for localized 'lushness' does not cause a catastrophic system collapse. They can be seen hovering near the areas of spatial and temporal feedback, their forms blurring as they actively manage the overlapping echoes of past and present arrangements, smoothing out the temporal wrinkles created by energy harvesting.

3.  **Flora Husbandry:** They tend the metallic, buzzing chandeliers. A Curator interacts with these growths by briefly channeling ambient electrical energy through their own structure, creating a focused electromagnetic pulse aimed at a specific spire. This pulse is not a destructive discharge, but a directive: "Grow toward the ascending magnetic node," or, "Precipitate the next layer of copper-verdigris helix here." They are the gardeners of inorganic mechanics.

### Interaction

They have no need for communication, noise, or visible effort. The occasional subsonic *thump-sigh* of the substrata is the sound of their collective exertion.

If one were to attempt to observe one closely, the Curator would likely not *notice* in a personal sense, any more than a tectonic plate notices the friction of sand grains. However, the presence of an anomalous, low-energy, water-based organism would cause a *system correction*.

The Curator would not attack. Instead, the space *around* the observer would become immediately hostile to their material state. Light entering the area would be bent, decelerated, and re-emitted at different frequencies entirely. The density of the air would increase exponentially in the observer's vicinity until the ochre dust pressed down, rapidly sintering into a solid, non-porous sphere of mineral cement, effectively encapsulating and neutralizing the foreign intrusion as an unwanted inclusion in the ongoing geological blueprint.

They are the living hardware, the tireless, silent maintenance crew ensuring that the impossible, high-energy experiment of this contained reality continues to spool out according to its inscrutable, vibrating design.
