The Abyssal Grove is inhabited by things that have achieved a deep, symbiotic fusion with its structure, entities utterly divorced from the concepts of air, sunlight, or terrestrial gravity. They are the **Hydrographers of the Lattice**.

### Classification and Form: The Press-Adapted

The primary inhabitants are not creatures in the conventional sense but evolved stewards of the Mycocoral Towers. They are classified by their primary mode of existence:

**1. The Anchorites (or Root-Shapers):**
These are the largest and least mobile of the inhabitants, functionally inseparable from the built environment. They spend almost their entire lives fused into the nutrient conduits and load-bearing joints of the Mycocoral architecture.

*   **Physiology:** Their bodies are immensely dense, utilizing hydrostatic skeletons reinforced by metabolized mineral deposits gleaned from the collapsing ancient matrices. Their outer layer is not skin, but a thick, gelatinous membrane infused with the same bioluminescent lichen that coats the towers, allowing them to blend perfectly into the wall structure when dormant.
*   **Sensory Apparatus:** They possess no dedicated eyes. Instead, their entire dermal surface is acutely sensitive to minute vibrations in the water—the grinding of the filter feeders, the cracking of geodes, and most importantly, the rhythmic pressure shifts caused by the flickering surface light. They perceive the world as a symphony of pressure gradients and resonant frequencies.
*   **Behavior:** The Anchorites are the conservators of the Grove. Their "work" involves carefully directing nutrient flow, stimulating the growth of crucial load-bearing fibers in the towers, and occasionally excising sections of intrusive, non-productive fungal bloom using hardened, obsidian-like chitinous extrusions that serve as slow-acting biological saws. They are the mechanism by which the organic ruin is maintained in a state of dynamic equilibrium—neither collapsing nor regenerating too rapidly.

**2. The Vent-Runners (or Viscosity Nomads):**
These are the mobile population, tasked with traversal and resource acquisition within the city-corridors. They navigate the high-viscosity fluid through a repulsive mastery of ambient pressure.

*   **Physiology:** Vent-Runners are streamlined, almost eel-like, but disproportionately robust. They lack fins or traditional propulsion. Their movement is achieved through rapid, focused bio-implosions and expansions of specialized internal gas bladders located along their spine. By collapsing a bladder forward and instantly expanding one behind, they create miniature, directional pressure voids that move them through the thick medium with surprisingly minimal friction, akin to "falling" through controlled density changes.
*   **Diet and Tools:** They primarily consume the softer, mineralized sap-gels found within the ancient architectural niches and the nutrient slurry generated by the grinding filter feeders. Their "Tech" (1.0/3) is manifested here: they carry specialized, sharpened tools—often modified teeth or sharpened splinters of polymerized tower lignin—used as grazers to scrape nutrient blooms from the slick walls or to puncture the nutrient-packed sacs of smaller, suspended organisms.
*   **Interaction:** They respect the Anchorites, recognizing them as the slow, moving heart of the Grove. Encounters are brief: a Vent-Runner will pause its traversal, brace against a tower surface to mitigate the pressure wave from its own movement, and offer a series of rapid, low-frequency clicks (audible only to other specialized fauna) before continuing its route along the established "urban" paths.

### The Rhythmic Consciousness

The most enigmatic inhabitants are rarely seen, perhaps because they exist mostly as sensory phenomena tied to the environment’s strange light cycles: the **Luminaires**.

The rhythmic shifting from green to venous violet is not merely environmental light refracting; it is the effect of their collective activity.

*   **Nature:** The Luminaires are thought to be complex, collective colonies of highly specialized, photosensitive microorganisms that exist in the absolute highest zones of the Grove, near the faint, filtered surface light. They are the Grove’s only true link to the outside.
*   **Function:** When the light shifts to green (the state associated with growth and mineral extraction), the Luminaires pulse encouragement to the Anchorites below, triggering specific enzymatic reactions in the Mycocoral. When the light shifts to deep violet (the state of dormancy or mineral sequestration), they seem to issue a calming, almost numbing command, slowing down active metabolism across the Grove.
*   **Auditory Signature:** Their presence is implied by the *loud sound* (2.3/3). When the light is violet, the grinding noise of the filter feeders quietens dramatically, replaced by the deeper, more profound *thrum* of the structure settling under immense, controlled pressure—the sound of the world holding its breath during the Luminaire's meditation.

In essence, the Abyssal Grove is inhabited by beings who have surrendered speed and fluidity for permanence and structural integrity. They do not live *in* the environment; they are the slow, grinding, luminous mechanism by which the environment sustains its towering, submerged ruins. Survival here means accepting the weight, mastering the viscosity, and listening to the silence hidden within the roar.
