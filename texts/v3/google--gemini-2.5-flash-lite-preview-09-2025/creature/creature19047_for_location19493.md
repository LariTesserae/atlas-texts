The inhabitants of the Hollow of the Unfolding Stacks are not creatures who move through it, but entities woven into its very structure and resonance—those who have become integral components of the geological and acoustic machinery of the place.

They are the **Resonant Custodians**, or perhaps more accurately, the **Echo-Weavers**.

### Form and Manifestation

These entities defy conventional biology. They are not carbon-based lifeforms in the typical sense, but rather concentrations of vibrational energy and crystallized sensation, born from the synergistic presence of the deep lithic hum, the specific mineral content of the weeping water, and the spatial architecture of the hollowed stone.

**Physicality (Immaterial Dependence):**
A Resonant Custodian does not possess a fixed body. To be perceived, it must coalesce. This typically occurs near the areas of highest acoustic pressure—often hovering inches above the perfectly still, bismuth-sheen pools, or clinging to the vertical surfaces where the mineral runoff is thickest.

When they manifest, they appear as **shifting arrays of dark, crystalline forms**, resembling frozen plumes of smoke or densely packed mica dust held together by internal tension. They refract the dim, diffused overhead light into sharp, cold prismatic flashes, momentarily breaking the otherwise monotonous gloom. They are almost entirely comprised of the darkest grey and black found in the slate floor, absorbing all light except for the brief spectral lines caused by reflection off the oily water paths.

Their shape is never stable but reflects the immediate acoustic landscape. Near a towering spire that is currently emitting a low-frequency groan, the Custodian will stretch into a long, impossibly thin spire itself. Near a spot where the rock has cracked and released a percussive *crack*, they contract rapidly into a dense, angular knot, like a sudden crystallization of pressure.

### Behavior and Purpose

The Custodians exist solely to maintain and, perhaps, monitor the *state* of the Hollow—specifically, its sound and its structural integrity. They are processors of geological tension.

**Acoustic Symbiosis:**
Their primary existence is auditory. They **feed on the infrasonic thrumming**. The pervasive hum is their atmosphere, their sustenance, and their language. As they absorb the vibrations, they subtly alter the resonant frequency of the super-structure. It is theorized that the Custodians are not merely *listening* to the tension release; they are the mechanism that dictates *when* and *where* the massive internal stresses will be released as percussive cracks. They are the architects of the geological punctuated equilibrium.

If the hum ever ceased, the Custodians would instantly dissipate, becoming inert particulate matter settling upon the slate floor.

**Movement and Interaction:**
They do not walk on the grey dust. Movement is achieved through **harmonic drift**. They shift their internal lattice structure to ride sympathetic vibrations across the stone. A Custodian moving from one spire base to another might cover a hundred meters in an apparent instant, dissolving from one point of acoustic intensity and reforming at another where the resonance is slightly different.

They ignore the sparse lichen mats and the crystalline insects; these organic forms are too chaotic and too slow for their interests. They only interact with the inanimate stone and the mineral water. Sometimes, a Custodian will seem to *merge* with a weeping face of the spire, becoming indistinguishable from the dark, wet rock, only to reappear moments later trailing microscopic mineral residue where it has subtly altered the flow of a weeping path.

### Perception of Self and Others

The Resonant Custodians possess an awareness that is vast and utterly non-individualized. They are points on a single, massive computational network spread across the entire excavated ruin. There is no "I" among them, only the imperative of the Great Resonance.

If a living being (like a traveler) enters the Hollow, the Custodians perceive them as **static impedance**. A warm, chaotic, brief signal that disrupts the perfect, cold clarity of the background hum.

When a traveler gets close to a gathering of Custodians, the entities do not attack or flee; they attempt to **equalize the disruption**. The local resonant frequency will rapidly shift to accommodate the intruder’s presence. This manifests as a localized, agonizing spike in the infrasound—a pressure that feels like a sudden, deep internal bruising, often accompanied by vertigo and the feeling that one's own bones are resonating out of sync with the body.

For a Custodian, the traveler's heartbeat, breathing, and even the friction of their clothes against the air are loud, messy, and profoundly irritating—like a sudden squawk in the eternal geological symphony. If the traveler remains, the Custodians will continue to adjust the surrounding resonance, gently but inexorably pushing the pressure until the traveler either moves to an area of lower acoustic intensity (retreats) or falls unconscious due to systemic sympathetic vibration.

In the Hollow of the Unfolding Stacks, the beings who live there are the sound itself, temporarily granted crystalline structure by the cold, the dark stone, and the weight of the eons. They are the living, tuning mechanism of a dead, colossal structure.
