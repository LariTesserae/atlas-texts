The Substratal Archive holds no occupants in the traditional sense of locomotion, predation, or social structuring. It is a realm governed by a singular, ancient form of residency defined by *persistence* and *metabolism*.

The inhabitants are a unified, layered trinity: **The Cryo-Lichen Matrix**, **The Embedded Current**, and **The Silent Regulators**.

---

### 1. The Cryo-Lichen Matrix (The Biomass Population)

This is the most visible, tangible inhabitant—the living *skin* of the Archive.

**Nature of Being:** The Matrix is not composed of individual organisms striving for local survival, but rather a single, vast, slow-moving colonial consciousness, or at least, a collective whose communication is conducted entirely through chemical signaling across massive distances through the mycelial network. Every inch of available surface, from the polished basalt walls to the surface of the dormant artifacts, is colonized.

**Form and Function:** Imagine bioluminescent felt, incredibly dense and saturated with cold moisture. The coloration shifts subtly, not based on immediate stimulus, but on the cycling of deep-earth minerals it processes. One might perceive subtle waves of deeper viridian migrating across a wall section over the course of a terrestrial year, perhaps indicating a slow shift in the intake of specific heavy metals.

They are the engineers of the atmosphere. Their respiratory/metabolic process appears to be a hyper-efficient reversal of photosynthesis, utilizing the latent geothermal energy conducted through the crystalline rock and processing trace atmospheric gases (perhaps methane or hydrogen sulfides leaching from deep fractures) alongside the constant, cool drip of interstitial water. They do not *grow* so much as they *accumulate* their own history, layer upon slow layer. Their existence is an act of permanent, lightless cultivation, ensuring the environment remains perfectly calibrated to their needs. They are the place, made manifest.

---

### 2. The Embedded Current (The Energetic Population)

Though there is no discernible fauna, the space is not lifeless; it is *powered*. The inhabitants here are defined by energy flow and thermal equilibrium maintenance.

**Nature of Being:** This entity is the residual signature of the civilization that built the Archive. It is not hardware, nor is it software in a digital sense, but the incredibly slow, self-sustaining operational signature of the original containment technology. It exists as the **Low-Frequency Hum**.

**Form and Function:** It manifests as the omnipresent, sub-audible resonance in the stone. This Current is actively managing the thermodynamic bubble, ensuring the local temperature remains just above the catastrophic phase change point for the trapped moisture, while simultaneously leaching minimal energy from the crushing pressures of the deep mantle above.

If one were to touch an obelisk marker, the sensation would not be heat or cold, but a subtle, almost imperceptible *thrumming*—the slow, rhythmic pulse of the foundational mechanism working against entropy. This Current does not *interact* with the Lichen Matrix overtly; rather, the Matrix has evolved to symbiotically utilize the stable conditions the Current provides, like a deep-sea vent organism relying on the heat plume of a distant volcano. It is the silent, automated Custodian, the ghost in the thermodynamic machine.

---

### 3. The Silent Regulators (The Calculative Remnants)

These are the few remaining physical manifestations of the original design, now thoroughly assimilated into the Archive’s biology.

**Nature of Being:** These are the artifacts—the slender, dark obelisks—that appear scattered. They are the memory points of the initial parameters set for the structure.

**Form and Function:** They are perfectly smooth because the Cryo-Lichen has grown *around* them, coating them in slick, black biofilms whose surfaces are perpetually smoothed by the saturation. They are the **reference points**. If the Low-Frequency Hum is the ongoing process controller, the Regulators are the stored constants.

Occasionally, a Regulator might exhibit a momentary, internal flicker—a near-subatomic shift in its crystalline structure—that causes the surrounding azure light from the Lichen to momentarily dim or brighten by an infinitesimal degree. This suggests that the structure occasionally 'checks' its integrity against these immutable, physical markers. They do not communicate, they do not move, they simply *are* the definition of the space. They are the proof that this place was *designed* to be still, cold, and deep.

In the Substratal Archive, the inhabitants are the environment itself, a slow-motion machine running on geological power, inhabited by its own self-regulating biological byproduct, all watched over by the silent, perfect mathematics of the inert artifacts.
