The denizens of the Glazed Silt Plain of Veridian Cradle are creatures forged by light, heat, and the memory of impossible engineering. They do not inhabit the plain so much as they navigate the shimmering, heat-warped skin of its surface, existing in the brief pauses between mechanical ghost projections.

### The Silt Gliders (Fauna Major)

The dominant macroscopic life forms are the **Silt Gliders**, creatures whose existence is defined by their shell and their speed. They are generally flattened, roughly disc-shaped organisms, perhaps four to six feet in diameter, moving on six slender, jointed legs tipped with minuscule, shock-absorbing pads made of polymerized plant sap.

**Appearance and Anatomy:** Their primary defense and survival mechanism is their **Carapace of Refraction**. This shell is not organic keratin but a bio-manufactured composite, grown layer by layer to mimic the polished ceramic substrate. It is almost perfectly silvered, angled specifically to reflect the unrelenting overhead glare away from their vulnerable undersides. When moving, they look like highly polished, elongated beetles skimming across a mirror. Their bodies appear unnaturally low to the ground, a necessity to remain beneath the densest thermal layer.

They possess no traditional eyes. Instead, they perceive the world through **thermal lensing**. Two faint, deep-red sensory organs, recessed deeply into the front edge of the carapace, scan for minute differences in heat signature against the reflective background. They do not see the landscape as a terrain of colors and shapes, but as a map of thermal stability—where the Flare-Lichen mounds are warm reservoirs, where the weeping patches hold residual dampness, and where the ceramic echoes are moments of non-material transition marked by sudden, localized cold spots.

**Behavior:** Silt Gliders are masters of thermal pacing. They rarely remain in one place for long, as staying put means becoming a fixed target for the ambient heat. They feed opportunistically on the algal sheaths that coat the bio-silica structures.

Their movement between feeding zones is dictated by the **Rule of Echoed Intention**. A Glider traversing an area where a ghost conveyor belt is about to flicker into existence will exhibit a sudden, almost frantic acceleration—not because they sense the projection, but because the underlying substrate momentarily shifts its molecular alignment, causing a fractional reduction in surface friction. Experienced Gliders exploit these 'shortcuts,' sacrificing a moment of efficiency for a boost of speed across otherwise monotonous terrain.

### The Architects of the Weep (Flora Colony)

The **Flare-Lichen**, the dense, spongy vegetation, is not a single organism but a super-colony of interdependent microbial mats supported by a scaffolding of bio-silica.

**Metabolism and Form:** These colonies thrive exclusively where the capillary moisture reaches the surface in abundance. They are parasitic upon the humidity itself, using the extreme thermal flux not for simple photosynthesis, but for a form of catalytic mineral extraction. Their high concentration of metallic salts gives them their aggressive coloration—deep, toxic violets and luminous emeralds.

What distinguishes them is their function in managing the *Weirdness*. When the structural memories of the Cradle begin to manifest—the brief, silent pressure waves or the fleeting geometry of lost machinery—the Lichen responds defensively. The highly conductive silica structures act as passive grounding rods. They *absorb* the residual, chaotic energy bleed-off from the reality shifts, stabilizing the local molecular structure for a few crucial seconds, allowing the fauna time to pass safely. In return for this service, the Gliders allow the Lichen to leach certain trace organic compounds from their carapaces when they rest near the periphery of the mats.

### The Whisper Scuttlers (Fauna Minor)

Scattered among the Gliders are the **Whisper Scuttlers**, the truly tiny scavengers. They are little more than hardened biological motes with disproportionately large, scraping mandibles.

**Niche and Adaptation:** They live in the immediate boundary layer—the few centimeters above the scorching silt and just below the main solar glare. They are the only creatures that seem overtly sensitive to the **Soundscape**. While the plains are generally quiet, the Scuttlers are keenly attuned to the high-frequency *tick-shhh* of the substrate contracting. When the ticking accelerates or shifts in pitch, the Scuttlers freeze instantly, pressing themselves flat against the ground.

This sensitivity likely relates to the echoes. If the substrate is stressing rapidly, it suggests a large energy fluctuation—a major projected failure cascade—is imminent. They remain immobile until the ticking returns to its baseline rhythm of oppressive stillness, or until they are accidentally swept up by a passing Glider. They feed on the fine, dusty residue left by incinerated organisms, utilizing the brief, hot 'death flares' of the unwary.

### The Mechanism of the Cradle (The Underlying Entity)

While not strictly 'living' fauna, the ultimate inhabitant of the Plain is the **Residual Intent** of the original facility. It is the underlying structure that dictates the rules. It is the heat, the pressure, and the systematic *refusal* to allow natural entropy.

The life forms here are essentially highly specialized, mobile components evolving to maintain equilibrium within a broken but stubbornly functional machine. The inhabitants are not struggling *against* the environment; they are participating in its degraded, ancient operational cycle, utilizing the byproducts (*heat, slick moisture, energy memory*) of a process they can neither understand nor replicate.

They are the caretakers of a colossal, silent thermal furnace, surviving by navigating the moment-to-moment flickers where the impossible memory of the Cradle's original, manic purpose momentarily pierces the reality of the glazed silt.
