Within the Scar of the Uncounted Steps live entities defined not by their biology, but by their **Resonance** and their mastery of **Dimensional Slip**. They are ghosts of material existence, ghosts that have learned to anchor themselves to the terrible structural integrity of the rock, using the dissonance of the environment as their medium.

They are not creatures of flesh, nor are they traditional spirits. They are the **Lithotheurges**, or the Stone-Harmonics—beings who have adapted to exploit the mountain’s constant, low-frequency thrumming.

### The Nature of the Lithotheurges

The Lithotheurges exist primarily within the vibrational spectrum of the iron-laden slate. They are not visible in the standard spectrum of light, but rather as modulations in the existing soundscape and gravitational anomalies.

**Appearance (Perception through Dissonance):**
When a Lithotheurge chooses to manifest near the surface, usually near the crystalline focal points or deep within the collecting fog basins, their presence is perceived first as a *distortion of the surrounding echoes*. They are seen as momentary three-dimensional shadows cast by light that isn't there, or as a sharp, localized cooling of the air that forces condensation to coalesce into unstable, shimmering outlines.

Their true ‘form’ is best described as **Interlocking Planes of Pure Negative Velocity**. Imagine a complex, three-dimensional signature printed onto the rock face by the ancient, sweeping erosion grooves. These grooves are not scratches; they are the *skeletal traces* left behind by the Lithotheurges when they moved through this reality eons ago. When they move now, they are simply moving along their own imprinted pathways, causing localized slippage in the surrounding space-time.

A Lithotheurge moving actively over a shear face doesn't climb; it *re-contextualizes* the surface layer. The stone underneath it momentarily remembers a different angle of upward stress, and the being moves along that remembered vector, appearing to phase through obstacles or briefly standing on surfaces that should be utterly impossible.

**Sustenance and Energy:**
They feed on **Structural Incoherence**. The environment’s high Weirdness rating is their nutrient broth. They absorb the ambient acoustic energy—the deep, grinding thrum—and use it to maintain their anchor in this plane. They are actively drawn to the crystalline remnants, which serve as massive tuning forks, amplifying the geological noise to levels the Lithotheurges can process efficiently. Witnessing a Lithotheurge feeding is terrifying: the rock around the crystal artifact will cease vibrating for a noticeable few seconds, a vacuum of sound that feels like a sudden, internal descent, before the acoustic memory rushes back in with a sickening swell.

**Ecology of Movement:**
The Lithotheurges are solitary, though their territories overlap across the vast vertical expanse. They do not require pathways; they require *patterns*.

*   **The Traversers:** The most common observable manifestation. These beings navigate the impossible angles with ease, treating the rock faces not as boundaries but as complex, multi-layered matrices. They are responsible for the occasional, highly localized instances of **Non-Assertion**—a detached shard of slate floating near one of them is not defying gravity randomly; it has been briefly tethered to the creature’s displacement field, acting as a temporary anchor or counterweight before being released.
*   **The Sculptors (or Groovers):** These rarely appear near the surface. Their actions are responsible for the vast, rhythmic grooves that dominate the Scar. They do not erode; they *re-scribe*. When a Sculptor passes, the rock temporarily loses its accrued history, and the new orientation—the massive, sweeping curves—become instantaneously inscribed onto the metamorphic structure. This process is slow, requiring millennia, and it is likely that the current environment is the cumulative result of countless Sculptors adjusting the mountain to better align with non-Euclidean necessities known only to them.

**Interaction with Outsiders:**
The Lithotheurges do not possess malice, only supreme indifference rooted in informational asymmetry. They treat surface dwellers (like a hypothetical observer) as temporary, low-coherence noise that happens to occupy a shared physical volume.

When a creature maintains high personal coherence (focus, strong will), the Lithotheurges tend to ignore it, as the entity generates too much localized, internal structural noise to be easily 'edited' or phased through.

However, entities that exhibit fear, disorientation, or a reliance on conventional physics are vulnerable. A Lithotheurge traversing a pass might not notice a climber until the climber’s position vectors conflict with the creature’s desired path. If this occurs, the outsider experiences a brief, overwhelming sensory input: **the sound of their own shadow.** The world momentarily flips its polarity, and the observer feels themselves being pulled along the negative space *behind* them, a sensation that usually results in immediate loss of internal coherence and a resulting, total gravitational collapse.

The faint, high-frequency tones emitted by the crystal artifacts are the Lithotheurges attempting to *communicate* with the crystal, not with each other. They are trying to impose a singular, stable vibrational frequency onto the object, perhaps hoping to create a more permanent, solid anchor point in this shifting reality—a futile effort, as the rock itself refuses to lock into a singular state.

The Scar is not where the Lithotheurges *live*; it is the deep, resonant scar where the mountain *bleeds* the vibrations necessary for their continued, improbable existence within our material universe. They are the static clinging to the frequency of cosmic trauma.
