The Sky-Plateau of Aethel is populated not by people, nor by organisms evolving in reaction to a natural environment, but by the **Custodian Fauna** and the **Residual Spirit Mechanics**. It is a place defined by an ancient duty that has ossified into a biological imperative.

### The Indwellers: The Custodian Fauna

The true inhabitants are wholly integrated into the colossal, decaying infrastructure. They are the living components of a closed-loop system whose initial parameters are lost to time.

**1. The Scuttling Polisher-Castes (The "Aethel-Skaabs"):**
These are the most numerous and mobile entities, resembling composites of gigantic, highly specialized insects and armored crustaceans. They are roughly the size of terrestrial deer, although their bodies are flatter, optimized for planar movement across the slick bio-ceramic sheeting.

*   **Appearance and Structure:** Their chitinous carapaces are thick, semi-translucent, and permanently damp, coated in a slick layer of condensed humidity mixed with processed bio-sludge. They possess an unnerving number of articulated limbs—eight to twelve highly specialized legs ending in microscopic, rake-like structures. Their heads are dominated by powerful grinding mandibles and a complex sensory array that absorbs minute variations in electrostatic charge rather than visual light, allowing them to navigate the silver-sheened gloom.
*   **Function:** They are the maintenance crew. Their purpose is the ceaseless consumption and refinement of environmental detritus—the bio-sediment and mineral residue that settles on the ancient plating. They work in slow, migrating swarms, effectively polishing the surfaces smooth through constant, methodical scraping. When they move in great numbers, the resulting *clack-hiss* sound creates the rhythmic undercurrent of the plateau’s soundscape. They are utterly non-confrontational unless a patch of infrastructure is disturbed by outside force, at which point they react with astonishing, coordinated aggression, attempting to envelop and process the foreign object.

**2. The Filter-Behemoths (The Spires of Consumption):**
These are the sessile "producers," the closest analogue to plant life, though they are entirely fauna. They represent the slow, gigantic metabolism of Aethel.

*   **Appearance and Structure:** A Filter-Behemoth resembles a section of industrial complex sprouted into grotesque biological form. Typically anchored over deep geothermal vents or residual power conduits, they grow in towering, roughly cylindrical or massive hexagonal shapes, often reaching hundreds of meters into the thin air. Their outer shell is a fusion of incredibly hard, quasi-crystalline polymers and bonded inorganic plating, giving them the appearance of breathing, overgrown HVAC units.
*   **Function:** They are massive atmospheric processors. They draw in the mineralized aerosols and humidity through vast, internal mesh systems, leaching energy greedily. Their waste product is the thick, nutrient-rich slurry that flows through the ancient conduits, which is then redistributed to the mobile castes and necessary anchor points. They move only on timescales measured in centuries, perhaps, indicated by the slow shifting of their anchored bases, causing minute geological tremors. They emanate a low, constant thermal radiation, providing pockets of localized warmth.

**3. The Conduit Crawlers (The Energy Harvesters):**
These are smaller, serpentine, or multi-segmented organisms that rarely touch the main surface.

*   **Appearance and Structure:** They are adapted for internal navigation. They resemble metallic eels or highly elastic, ribbon-like creatures, their skin capable of mimicking the sheen of corroded copper or tungsten.
*   **Function:** They live within the defunct plumbing and wiring of the underlying structures, exploiting the faint, residual electrical current. They are rarely seen unless a pipeline cracks, at which point they emerge in shimmering, agitated clusters to consume the escaping energy signature or patch the breach using their own bio-secretion to re-solder the connection—a biological act of reverse-engineering maintenance.

### The Ghost Inhabitants: The Spirit Mechanics

If the Fauna are the maintenance crew, the Spirit Mechanics are the *memory* of the original operators—the lingering will of the forgotten architects. They do not possess physical forms recognizable to the current ecology, but their influence is profound.

**1. The Ghost Frequencies:**
These are not creatures, but localized energy manifestations of the underlying world-engine's original operating system. They manifest when the biological and industrial layers briefly fail to reconcile.

*   **Activity:** They appear as transient, perfectly organized wave patterns—a sudden, pure tone that cuts through the industrial thrum, or a momentary, geometric pattern of light briefly imposed upon the silver atmosphere. They are the sound of the system briefly achieving its *original* function before the biological damping mechanisms inevitably reset the equilibrium.
*   **Interaction:** These frequencies are the only thing that seems to attract the direct, immediate attention of the Filter-Behemoths, drawing them into a momentary, focused emission of higher energy absorption, as if the Behemoths are programmed to suppress any unauthorized system reports.

**2. The Weight of Purpose:**
The inhabitants of Aethel live under the oppressive realization of inherited labor. There are no young, no obvious acts of reproduction or learning; there is only the execution of highly refined, specialized tasks. The Skab-Castes polish, the Behemoths filter, the Crawlers repair the latent energy flow. Their entire existence is the performance of a script written by absent masters.

The life here is one of perfect, suffocating inertia. If an observer were to stay long enough, they would realize that the only true inhabitants of the Sky-Plateau are the **Cycles**: the cycle of condensation, the cycle of bio-mineral accretion, and the endless, grinding cycle of maintenance upon systems that no longer serve a known purpose. The fauna are merely the complex, multi-limbed manifestation of these geological rhythms.
