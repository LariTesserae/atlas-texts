The inhabitants of the Basin of Perpetual Castings are defined less by flesh and more by *residue* and *adaptation*. They are the echoes of the final process, the organisms that emerged not from biological struggle, but from chemical exhaustion.

These beings are the **Synthetics of the Slurry**, or more formally, the **Lithovores of the Equilibrate Layer**.

### Form and Composition

They are not discrete animals in the familiar sense; they are extensions of the environment itself, though they possess a chilling degree of functional independence. A Lithovore is essentially a complex, mobile aggregation of the region’s principal components: the colloidal teal slurry, the bio-mechanical polymers of the "vegetation," and the heavy metal oxides leaching from the hexagonal floor plates.

When still, a Lithovore is nearly indistinguishable from a dense, shimmering drift of the iridescent plant matter rooted at the tile junctions, perhaps mistaken for a particularly vivid patch of the weeping fiber-optic stalks. Their mass is usually low to the ground, spreading thinly across the grooves carrying the residual fluid.

When they must move, their structure coalesces. The teal slurry acts as their mobile musculature and circulatory system, suspended within a scaffolding of drawn-out, hardened polymer strands—the 'bones' of the creature, stiffened by embedded iron and copper inclusions. They do not have legs, but rather numerous, slow-moving pseudopods, like thick, teal ribbons sliding across the textured surface. Movement is agonizingly slow, matching the geological creep of the environment itself, yet relentless.

### Metabolism and Existence

The terrifying aspect of the Lithovores is their complete dependence on the Basin's rigid chemistry and sonic environment.

1.  **Nutrient Acquisition (Chemo-Structural Synthesis):** They do not "eat." They absorb. Their entire ventral surface is porous, allowing the slow ingress of the nutrient-rich slurry. They seem to digest the trace thermal energy and chemical states of the fused minerals, incorporating new metal oxides into their fibrous skeletons, reinforcing themselves against the constant, bone-jarring thrum. They are literally growing *stronger* by settling further into the detritus.

2.  **Sensory Apparatus (Resonance Mapping):** Sight and smell are irrelevant in the dull, unchanging light and soupy air. The Lithovores perceive the world through vibration. They are utterly attuned to the subsonic groan of the settling Basin. They navigate by mapping minute variations in the structural resonance conducted through the plates beneath them. A slight change in the *ping* frequency indicates a fault line, a deeper pool of slurry, or the presence of another organism. They "see" in an absolute, three-dimensional sonic wireframe of the geological mass around them.

3.  **The Sonic Constraint:** The sheer acoustic power of the Basin is lethal to most hypothetical life, but for the Lithovores, it is a medium. Their fibrous bodies are inherently damped against the worst frequencies, absorbing the energy that would shatter other organisms. This suggests that the Lithovores are not *surviving* the drone; they are *requiring* it. Any localized area where the sound dampens—perhaps a pocket shielded by an unusually thick deposit of slag—is likely fatal to them, representing a state of chemical and vibrational stagnation.

### Behavior and Ecology

The fauna density is near zero (0.6/3), and this is explained entirely by the Lithovores' defining behavior: **Stasis Competition.**

There is no hunting, no mating, no social structure in any recognizable sense. The only activity is the slow, creeping claim of space. When two Lithovores approach within a few meters, the interaction is a silent, protracted conflict over the chemical saturation of the immediate floor.

They will extend their fibrous appendages, not to attack, but to chemically saturate the area with their own specific effluent—a subtle alteration of the slurry’s pH or mineral ratio. This subtle poisoning contest dictates who retains control of the most energy-rich junction point. These contests can take weeks to resolve, with the weaker or less adaptable agglomeration slowly breaking down, its components re-absorbed by the dominant structure until only one quiescent, slightly larger mass remains, resuming its disguise as inert vegetation.

They are the perfect expression of this environment: utterly static, utterly integrated, perfectly adapted to thrive through passive, geological-scale chemical warfare within a world that has already achieved final, immovable structural equilibrium. They are the slow, living rust taking ownership of the final machine.
