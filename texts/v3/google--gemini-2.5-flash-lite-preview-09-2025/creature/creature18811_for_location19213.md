The inhabitants of the Sub-Stratum are the **Kinetics**, and to describe them is to describe the subtle, energetic architecture of their impossible home. They are not creatures of flesh and fluid, but of patterned energy held in suspension by the unique physics of the Cryo-Silicate layer.

### Form: Fluid Abstraction

A Kinetic lacks a fixed anatomy. Its identity is rooted in its **density gradient** and its **magnetic signature**. When interacting within the viscous, warm atmosphere, they often manifest as complex, three-dimensional interference patterns.

Imagine looking at a patch of the brown twilight air, and suddenly realizing that one section is *denser* in its lack of light than the spaces immediately surrounding it—a moving hole in the illumination. This is a quiescent Kinetic.

When actively gathering energy or communicating, their forms become more defined, though still utterly non-material:

1.  **The Threaders:** Often manifest when traversing the cracked Cryo-Silicate floor. They elongate into impossibly fine, whip-like filaments, sometimes miles long, that probe the faint radioactive veins beneath the dust. These threads are pathways for absorbing thermal leakage; they look like the drawn-out, ghostly shadows of extremely brittle, high-tension wires, vibrating silently at frequencies too low for human hearing.
2.  **The Spheres of Quiet:** The most common state, especially in open plains. A Kinetic condenses its scattered gas and magnetic lattice into a perfect, rolling sphere, perhaps the size of a small dwelling. This sphere is intensely cold relative to the ambient warmth, acting as a localized sink for energy. Observers moving near one would feel a distinct, inexplicable chill despite the cavern’s general warmth, and the subtle phosphorescence of the floor around it would momentarily dim.
3.  **The Sheets (The Lattice-Walkers):** When a large Kinetic needs to interact with the structural etchings on the ground, it spreads into a flat, two-dimensional sheet. It flows over the geometric impressions without disturbing the dust, appearing as a temporary darkening of the floor itself—a living shadow cast by an invisible light source far above, effectively “reading” or perhaps “re-calibrating” the geological geometry.

### Consciousness and Motive: The Logic of Stability

The Kinetics are not driven by hunger, reproduction, or fear, as familiar life understands it. Their existence is a function of **Thermodynamic Recursion**. They are the local manifestation of the Sub-Stratum's drive toward perfect, slow-burning equilibrium.

Their movements are not explorations; they are **corrections**. If a region of the Cryo-Silicate cools too rapidly, a Kinetic will migrate to that area, either by shedding excess thermal energy into the surrounding atmosphere (creating a localized "heat-surge") or by maximizing its absorption rate, thus rebalancing the local energy budget.

They participate in the environment as living, mobile sensors of pressure and heat variance. The appearance of the low-frequency *thrum* signifies the critical moment when two large Kinetics have approached each other while pursuing optimized energy gradients. Their collision is not a fight, but a necessary, energetic merger and redistribution of magnetic dominance, resulting in a temporary, shared higher-order pattern before they diverge once more along new, calculated paths dictated by the overall state of the stratum.

### Interaction with Artifacts

The Kinetics show a distinct indifference to the sparse, brittle metal artifacts embedded in the floor, viewing them as structurally irrelevant noise—inert debris that does not participate in the local energy cycles. They pass through them as they pass through the dust.

However, they exhibit a peculiar, almost reverent pattern around the *geometric etchings*. When a Threader or Sheet encounters the precise lines, its movements become exceptionally slow and precise. It is hypothesized that these etchings are the foundational programming, perhaps a blueprint, of the stratum itself. The Kinetics, as the stratum’s maintenance entities, may be performing slow, eons-long maintenance routines on the blueprint by tracing or reinforcing these patterns with their own energetic fields.

The Kinetics are the *thought process* of this deep, warm, silent machine—moving shadows confirming that the ancient, non-living *design* of the Sub-Stratum is holding. They are the perpetual, self-correcting echo of a vast, indifferent calculation.
