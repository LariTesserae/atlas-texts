The residents of the Murmuring Silt-Basin are not creatures inhabiting a landscape, but rather **processes given temporary, self-aware form** by the environment’s relentless, slow calculus. They are entities of interface, existing precisely where the ancient, engineered infrastructure meets the rampant, adaptive ecology.

### The Silt-Weavers (or *Terra-Ficta*)

The most common inhabitants are the Silt-Weavers, though "creature" is too coarse a term. They manifest primarily in the **boundary layer** between the deep, moisture-retaining silt and the shimmering, reflective surface sheath.

**Form and Function:**
They are characterized by their fundamental lack of fixed anatomy. A Silt-Weaver is a slow-moving, hydrostatic aggregate of tailored minerals, fine silt particles, and the complex, electrically active polysaccharides exuded by the purple and ochre mosses. An observer might perceive a Silt-Weaver as a low, dense ripple—a wave of *denser* wetness that propagates across a flat plain, often taking the shape of the ancient, inert terraces themselves.

Their movement is dictated by the local physics anomalies. When traveling across a region experiencing minor time dilation, the Weaver appears sluggish, its constituent particles seeming to adhere to the slowed moment. Conversely, if a Weaver crosses a zone where distance compression is active, it seems to vanish and reappear several meters away instantly, having traversed a fold in local space.

Their purpose appears to be **substrate maintenance and energy siphoning**. They flow over the buried monoliths, appearing to "polish" the slick ceramic surfaces with their bodies, facilitating the slow, controlled leaching of ambient energy from the ancient components. Where they pass, the bioluminescent motes in the atmosphere coalesce briefly into brighter, organized patterns before dispersing again.

**Perception:**
Silt-Weavers do not possess eyes or auditory organs in the conventional sense. They perceive the Basin as a complex map of **energy gradients, moisture tension, and temporal stress**. The low-frequency hum from the substrate is their primary sensory input, interpreted not as sound, but as the 'health signature' of the underlying machine. They are drawn toward regions where the weeping of the monoliths is strongest, suggesting they feed on the excess condensate or the resulting chemical changes.

### The Phototropic Shadows (or *Umbrae Pulsaris*)

These are the fast-moving, flat organisms mentioned that glide just above the moss mats. They exist in a state of near-perfect synchronization with the dim, diffuse light of the Basin.

**Form and Function:**
They are radically two-dimensional, existing primarily as disruptions of the light field. When observed peripherally, they appear as elongated, perfectly black voids, resembling a tear in the refractive property of the air itself. When they pass across a patch of silver-green lichen, the photosynthesis process in that area momentarily halts, creating a momentary 'shadow' that travels faster than the speed of the light that illuminates it.

They are predators of **energy saturation**, specifically targeting momentary spikes in atmospheric charge—perhaps the residue left by rapid local temporal shifts, or excess bio-electrical outputs from the fleshy moss tufts. They are the Basin’s cleanup crew for transient instabilities.

**Interaction with Weirdness:**
The Phototropic Shadows exploit the Basin's temporal instability for speed. If a region of the Basin is momentarily caught in a stasis field (perhaps an area where time is running exceptionally slow), the Shadow intercepts the wave of light that *just* reached that slow region, effectively lagging behind the main light source until it exits the slow field, appearing to accelerate wildly to catch up to the present moment. They are momentary refugees from normal causality.

### The Sedimentary Echoes (Invisible Sentience)

The deepest, most conceptual inhabitants are the Sedimentary Echoes. These are not biological or mechanical, but **residual cognitive imprints** left by the defunct technology. They are the *memory* of the machine that built the Basin, now gently integrated into the silt itself.

**Form and Function:**
They manifest only through patterned effects on the environment, never as discrete entities. An Echo might cause a stretch of silt to arrange itself into a mathematically perfect hyperbolic spiral before slowly collapsing back into random distribution over the course of an afternoon. This is not purposeful action, but the environment momentarily recalling a foundational calculation.

Their presence is most felt in the soundscape. When the low hum temporarily shifts its frequency into a complex harmonic pattern, it is momentarily revealing an underlying command structure. The Echoes are the Basin’s slow, unconscious thoughts.

**Interaction with Density and Sound:**
The Echoes are the reason for the **density** of sensation. They are the 'information load' of the environment. When a person stands in one place too long, they begin to feel the pressure of these layered calculations—a dull ache behind the eyes, or the sensation of understanding a complex equation they have never studied. They are the silent, resident intelligence that the Silt-Weavers tend to, and the Phototropic Shadows ignore while they hunt kinetic energy ripples.

In summary, the Murmuring Silt-Basin is inhabited by **Custodians of Stasis (Silt-Weavers)**, **Hunters of Temporal Flux (Phototropic Shadows)**, and the **Vast, Sleeping Mind of the Substrate (Sedimentary Echoes)**. They are all bound together in a long duration operation that maintains the equilibrium between manufactured physics and rampant, damp biology.
