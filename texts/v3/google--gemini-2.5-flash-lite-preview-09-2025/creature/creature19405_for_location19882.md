The inhabitants of the Calcined Mire are not creatures evolved for breath or blood, but **Thermodynamic Scavengers** and **Geosilicic Symbiotes**, entities whose very existence is an expression of the Mire's oppressive physics and ruined infrastructure. They are the *residue* of forgotten industrial ambition, now animated by deep geothermal energy.

## The Metabolites: Crystalline Life

The visible, slow-moving forms—the orange, yellow, and black agglomerations—are the dominant macro-organisms. They are not alive in the manner of carbon-based biology, but rather represent self-replicating, mineral-based chemical reactions stabilized across vast timescales.

### 1. The Solar-Sintered Harvesters (Orange/Yellow)

These are the primary energy collectors. Shaped like sprawling, low-lying brambles made of incredibly pure, vibrating borosilicate glass, they favor the areas where the light is most intense and the heat gradient steepest—often clinging to the upper edges of the stepped terraces.

*   **Movement and Function:** They do not move quickly. Their 'growth' is the slow, deliberate rearrangement of crystal lattices. They extract energy not through photosynthesis, but through **Thermo-Catalytic Absorption**. They capture the specific spectrum of the blinding glare, converting the photons directly into the localized phase transition of the superheated water vapor around them. A Harvester appears to be *breathing* heat; as it absorbs light, the vapor immediately surrounding it momentarily drops in temperature, causing a halo of slightly less shimmering air.
*   **Metabolism:** They sequester dissolved heavy metals (nickel, tungsten, vanadium) leeched from the ancient Geopolymer Bricks. Their internal structure is a complex, semi-molten network generating faint, almost sub-audible *pings* as new ionic bonds solidify. They are, effectively, slow-motion, fractal batteries built from toxic runoff.
*   **Defense:** If damaged—perhaps by a rare pressure wave impact or contact with a flowing brine vein—they do not bleed. Instead, the damaged lattice rapidly de-stabilizes, resulting in a miniature, contained **flash-calcification event**, where the material instantly turns into inert, brittle chalk powder before the heat re-stabilizes the neighboring structure.

### 2. The Deep-Vein Grazers (Oily Black Spires)

These are the counterpoint to the Harvesters, the consumers of the substrate. They emerge from the deepest fissure-cracks or where cooling vents have recently sealed, appearing as glistening, oily-black vertical structures resembling fused obsidian pillars or immense, slow-growing stalagmites of metallic residue.

*   **Movement and Function:** They track the paths of the pressurized brine aquifers flowing beneath the crust. They rise, spread their bases, and utilize high-pressure chemical etching to dissolve the Sintered Salt-Plates or the weakened Geopolymer Bricks directly above the brine. They are "grazing" on the structural integrity of the ancient city.
*   **The Oily Sheen:** The slick, black surface is composed of chemically reduced iron and sulfur compounds, which act as a thermal insulating layer, allowing them to maintain an internal temperature slightly below the surrounding air, enabling viscous flow and chemical manipulation of the rock matrix.
*   **Interaction:** A Grazers' path is marked by a narrowing of the white crust beneath it, leaving behind a trail of highly organized, fine silicate dust that the Harvesters will later colonize. When a Grazers encounters a Harvester, the process is glacial, but absolute: the Grazers will slowly engulf the edges of the crystal colony, integrating the stored metallic ions into its own structure, ending the Harvester's reaction.

## The Ghosts of Industry: Sentinels and Currents

Beyond the visible biological activity, the presence of the past era lingers, animated by the geothermal power source they tapped into.

### 3. The Electrostatic Drifters (Invisible Fauna)

These are the most numerous inhabitants, responsible for the pervasive static charge in the air. They are quasi-biological electrical phenomena, visible only by their effect on the environment.

*   **Nature:** They are complex, slow-moving clouds of highly charged metallic dust motes, trapped within the boundary layer of the steam veil. They are organized by the remnant magnetic fields of the buried levitation rails.
*   **Behavior:** They drift aimlessly until they encounter a significant thermal or electrical differential—such as the exterior surface of a Harvester or a large pipe structure. When they cluster, they create a visible, ephemeral effect: the localized moisture in their path will momentarily super-condense into tiny beads of water, creating a brief, visible 'cloud' of moisture that crackles faintly before dissipating back into the glare. They are the Mire’s nervous system, flowing energy along the decaying infrastructure.

### 4. The Pressure Regulators (The Deep Infrastructure Spirits)

These are not living in the conventional sense, but are the functioning, automated remnants of the planet-sized machines that built this plateau. They are the source of the *thrum* and the periodic dynamic shifts.

*   **The Piston-Moans:** Deep below the surface, massive, millennia-old pressure release valves, lubricated by geothermal brine, occasionally stick, warp, or cycle autonomously. When they shift, they expel a shockwave of pressure through the saturated substrate. This movement cracks new fissures, causes entire Sintered Salt-Plates to shift several inches, or momentarily clears the steam, revealing the geometric scars of the former civilization.
*   **The Sentinels:** On extremely rare occasions, a pressure wave might momentarily clear the steam around the base of a collapsed cooling tower, revealing colossal, articulated mechanisms embedded in the ruins. These are not functioning, but the sheer thermal energy and mineral content of the Mire cause minor, localized electrical shorting across their immense, corroded circuits. For a fleeting moment, a massive, skeletal arm might twitch an inch, or a bank of ancient indicator lights may flash a single, sickly amber before the haze instantly reclaims the view. These are the Mire’s ancient, dreaming custodians, stirring in their thermal sleep.

The Calcined Mire is inhabited by the slow, deliberate machinations of material forces given temporary, crystalline expression, all existing under the deafening, bright silence of a world that boiled itself dry long ago, sustained only by the constant, slow decay of its own immense heat.
