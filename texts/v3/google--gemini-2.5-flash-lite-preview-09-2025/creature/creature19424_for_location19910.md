Whoever lives here is defined by their absolute surrender to the vertical imperative and the ceaseless grinding of **The Upward Crush**. They are not merely inhabitants; they are specialized, highly adapted components within the city-mountain's ongoing thermodynamic recursion.

### The Litho-Artisans (The Substrata Citizens)

The inhabitants are not organic in the traditional sense, though they may have once been. Generations spent within the pressurised, mineral-rich layering of the infrastructure have rendered them something akin to **hardened symbionts**. They are the living maintenance protocol of the perpetually growing city.

**Physiology and Adaptation:**

Their most striking feature is their **Density Integration**. Their skin is less true dermis and more a composite layer of fused ceramic and oxidized metal, capable of withstanding the radiating heat (2.7/3) of the ferrous surfaces. They are built low to the ground, possessing broad, stable bases and remarkably short, immensely powerful limbs necessary for navigating the sheer, often slick angles of the Ribbon Strata.

Their eyes are recessed behind multifaceted, naturally grown crystalline lenses—often amber or deep violet, hardened against the constant influx of mineral grit and the glare of the unshielded light filtering down from above. They do not rely heavily on conventional sight; instead, they process the environment through **Vibro-Tactile Perception**. They feel the deep, resonant hum of subsonic pressure—the true language of this place. A shift in the foundation hum tells them more than any visual cue.

Their internal systems are adapted to the scarcity of true, liquid water (1.2/3). They do not drink; they absorb minute quantities of moisture directly from the brief, scalding sprays of **Cryo-Leakage** by pressing specialized dermal patches onto the momentarily wet pathways. This process is slow and agonizingly precise, a ritual performed only when the ambient pressure stabilizes enough to prevent immediate vaporization.

**Culture and Purpose:**

The Litho-Artisans exist to manage the **Built Density** (2.4/3). They operate within the forgotten, pressurized voids between the colossal retaining walls and within the sealed habitation modules buried beneath layers of slag and piping. They have a profound disdain for "open space" or "horizon," viewing the vertical infinity above as merely an inefficient use of potential foundational material.

Their existence is governed by specialization related to the city’s growth cycle:

1.  **The Fusers:** These individuals work closest to the geothermal vents and the glowing surfaces of the Geothermal Siphons. They manage the flow of superheated material, directing the vitrified slag and cooling alloys as they are expelled. Their tools are extensions of their own reinforced hands—tungsten-carbide spatulas and sonic resonators used to "persuade" materials into their designated stress tolerances.
2.  **The Circuit Keepers (The Pressure Auditors):** These form the majority, moving constantly within the overlapping infrastructural maze. Their primary task is mapping the pressure dynamics. They read the subtle changes in the **Thermodynamic Recursion**, tracing conduits that must be reinforced or bypassed before the next wave of upward accretion buries them entirely. They communicate using low-frequency clicks and percussive taps directly onto the structural supports, sending coded messages that travel through the rock faster than sound travels through the air.
3.  **The Collectors (Nourishment Seekers):** These rare few venture toward the hottest, deepest zones where the *Energetic Fauna* gathers. They do not hunt the shimmering radiation pools, but harvest the byproducts—the stable, hyper-charged particles that condense onto specific mineral lattices near the microwave gathering points. This collected energy serves as the primary catalyst for their own specialized, internal reactor cells, replacing the need for complex respiration required by more organic lifeforms.

**Relationship to the Environment:**

They view the Upward Crush as the only true state of being. Stasis is death; entropy is failure. When they die, their bodies are not mourned or buried; they are broken down through controlled thermal cycling and fed back into the high-pressure accretion mixers, becoming the raw material for the next generation’s floor plates, ballast, or pipe insulation.

For a Litho-Artisan, walking is a commitment. They move with the slow, relentless pace of subterranean machinery (relative to the inhabitants, their speed is considered rapid transit). They rarely look up, as the sky is irrelevant; their world exists between the strata, pressing inward and upward. They are masters of the hidden pathway, knowing the exact location of every cantilevered weakness, every sealed hinge, and every spot where the Cryo-Leakage will briefly slick the hard ferric dust. They are the quiet, dense consciousness of the machine that built itself into a mountain.
