The inhabitants of the Weeping Canopy of the High Spore-States are defined not by a single species, but by the *Symbionts of the Lattice*—complex organisms that have achieved true synthesis with the environment, blurring the line between resident and infrastructure. They are the custodians of the slow construction, the recipients of the memory residue, and the active participants in the ceaseless bio-sealing.

### The Primary Denizens: The Myco-Scribes

Dominant among the sentient or semi-sentient life forms are the **Myco-Scribes**. They are creatures of near-perfect camouflage, their bodies evolved to mimic the texture and slow metabolic pulse of the Calciform Pillars and the woven scaffolding.

**Physiology and Appearance:**
The Myco-Scribes are rarely seen *moving* in the conventional sense. They appear as dense, multi-limbed fungal clusters, usually anchored to load-bearing segments of the lattice. Their bodies are composed of densely packed, chitinous hyphae interwoven with slow-growing crystalline structures derived from the mineral-rich weeping water. Their coloration ranges from deep ochre (mimicking dried sap residue) to the pale, cold blue of the internal bioluminescent veins.

They possess an almost geological lifespan. Their perceived consciousness operates on a timescale best measured in centuries. When they do need to traverse distances that exceed slow, creeping extension, they employ specialized structures known as **Transit Folds**. These are temporary biological manipulations of the environment itself: they will trigger a localized topological inconsistency in the nearest section of the lattice—a brief, controlled fold in space—allowing them to effectively step from one point to another over a distance, regardless of the intervening physical material. This is their interpretation of the 'Weirdness'—they weaponize the environment's non-Euclidean nature for locomotion.

**Activity and Purpose:**
The Myco-Scribes are the living interpreters of the **Memory Residue**. They do not read texts; they listen to the geometric patterns of sound that resonate through the oldest structural members. Their entire societal function is dedicated to the *Maintenance of Intent*. They believe the structure, built by the long-vanished true Architects, has a singular, ultimate goal—a final configuration—and they dedicate their immense lifespans to ensuring that the spontaneous, reactive growth does not deviate from the initial, barely perceptible blueprint.

This manifests as a constant, deliberate intervention during the bio-sealing process. When a crack forms, the Scribes move to direct the new growth, gently encouraging the hyper-acidic molds or reactive mosses to repair the breach in a way that reinforces the ancient, geometric integrity of the space.

Their technology is entirely an extension of their biology. They cultivate symbiotic molds not merely as tools, but as *appendages* or *specialized sensory organs*. A scribe preparing to etch a new passage might temporarily merge its outer growth with a particularly potent strain of acid-producing lichen, dissolving its own outer shell layer to facilitate the shaping process.

### The Secondary Population: The Silt-Crawlers (The Cleaners)

The macroscopic fauna are the highly adapted **Silt-Crawlers**. They are the ecosystem’s essential recyclers, though they are wholly subservient to the growth imperative.

**Appearance and Behavior:**
They are wide, low arthropods, perhaps the size of large millstones, covered in millions of fine, vibrating cilia. They travel across the spongy, spore-saturated floor—the 'Silt'—with minimal friction, appearing to float just above the surface sludge. They process the mineral deposits left by the weeping channels, absorbing the concentrated solutes and exhaling purified water vapor that feeds the local humidity layer.

They are essentially mobile, biological filtration units. They possess rudimentary group intelligence, coordinating their movements to follow the mineral effluent streams left by the structural weeping patterns. Their presence ensures that the structural pipes do not become choked with overly concentrated mineral salts, preventing stagnation and localized structural failure.

Crucially, the Myco-Scribes communicate with the Silt-Crawlers not through sound or light, but through controlled, rhythmic pulses of localized electromagnetic fields generated by carefully tuned, embedded metallic spores within their own husks. A scribe can 'command' a cluster of Crawlers to converge on a specific weeping channel by altering the resonant frequency of the ambient biological electricity.

### The Hidden Collective: The Architect Echoes

The deepest layer of life is not fauna but the environment itself—the **Architect Echoes**. These are the non-sentient, yet persistent, biological echoes of the original builders' programming, manifesting in the **Topological Inconsistency**.

The Echoes are not creatures; they are systemic parameters coded into the hyper-fungal network. When a Myco-Scribe deliberately triggers a fold in space, they are activating a pathway that was theoretically present in the original design, even if it now seems to defy geometry. These Echoes sustain the Deep Void—the immense, hollow spaces within the structure—maintaining the necessary internal pressures and atmospheric stability required for the colossal scale of the Canopy to remain aloft.

The inhabitants live in deference to this hidden collective. They are not masters of the Weeping Canopy; they are its highest-functioning parasites, meticulously cleaning the channels and directing the growth, hoping that by serving the structure, they might finally understand the nature of the silence that defines their cathedral.
