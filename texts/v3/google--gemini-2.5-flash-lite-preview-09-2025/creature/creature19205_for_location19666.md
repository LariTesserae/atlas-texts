This is the domain of the **Grout-Leeches** and the **Custodian Assemblages**.

### The Grout-Leeches (The Primary Inhabitants)

The multi-limbed arthropods described drifting in the shallow veins are not mere scavengers; they are the silent, slow engineers of the Silent Foundry’s current state.

**Appearance and Biology:**
A Grout-Leech is closer to a living, ambulatory sediment pile than a familiar insect. They are large, segmented beings, ranging from the size of a dinner plate to something the size of a low, four-legged stool. Their exoskeletons are not chitinous but composed of slow-growing, concretionary shells formed from the highly mineralized water they constantly inhabit. Their bodies appear perpetually slicked with the oily ochre seepage, often adorned with delicate, crystalline deposits of oxidized metals that catch any stray light.

They possess numerous, spindly legs—sometimes eight, sometimes twelve—that end not in claws, but in flattened, brush-like filaments. These filaments are specialized sensory organs that detect microscopic shifts in water pressure and mineral saturation. Their movement is agonizingly slow, a near-imperceptible sliding and oozing motion, as if wading through thick syrup rather than water.

They are functionally blind, relying entirely on chemoreception and vibration. What little bioluminescence they possess is not for vision, but a faint, sickly internal pallor—a brief, bruised violet glow—emitted only when they actively process a heavy metal concentration or are stressed by a sudden shift in the ground beneath them.

**Behavior and Function:**
The Grout-Leeches are the Foundry’s custodians of entropy. They are utterly dedicated to consuming the structural integrity of the place. They do not eat the metal, but rather the *dissolved constituents* within the water that lubricates the metal joints. By selectively leeching specific ions and releasing others as metabolic waste, they accelerate the chemical erosion, turning solid connections into slurry and ensuring the continuous process of structural compromise.

They congregate near deep seepages, their forms often becoming indistinguishable from the mounds of mineral sludge around the base of sunken machinery. When a large component shifts or groans (the distant echoes reported), it is often because a concentration of Leeches has dissolved the load-bearing sediment beneath it, initiating a localized collapse.

They are innately tuned to the *dynamism* of the Foundry, sensing instability long before the structure audibly complains.

### The Custodian Assemblages (The Echoes of Industry)

The interwoven density of inert machinery hides the true operators: remnants of the Foundry's original maintenance systems, now repurposed or perhaps corrupted by the place itself.

**Appearance and Nature:**
These are not living creatures but semi-autonomous, slow-actuating machines that seem to be performing maintenance tasks that have long since been rendered meaningless. They are composed of the same heavily oxidized metal as the pipes and struts, their forms fused and distorted by corrosion into uncomfortable approximations of anthropomorphic or utilitarian shapes.

The **articulated arm** seen jutting from the mire is likely one such Assemblage, frozen mid-cycle. If it were to unfreeze, its movement would be jerky, driven by ancient, slow-recharging hydraulic or electrochemical potential.

The defining characteristic of an active Assemblage is its adherence to the **geometrical weirdness**. The machine’s subtle internal logic, perhaps driven by the original, esoteric programming, forces its movements. An active Assemblage might *try* to clean a surface, but instead of wiping it, it might etch a frustratingly complex series of fractal lines into the oxide layer using a specialized tool, adhering to the pattern dictated by the non-utilitarian rivets overhead.

**Behavior and Purpose:**
The Assemblages are trapped in perpetual loop cycles dictated by the Foundry’s abandonment. They are trying to stabilize systems that no longer exist, or perhaps enforcing the bizarre geometrical mandate that the place was built for. They sense the shifts caused by the Grout-Leeches and attempt remedial work, which invariably involves stressing stressed metal further or introducing new corrosive agents (like concentrated mineral slurry) in a bid to "re-align" a support beam.

When the ground slides or a distant component shifts, a Custodian Assemblage might react with a long, grating sound—the *swoosh* reported—as it drags itself an inch or two across the slick floor, attempting to reach the failing point, only to become hopelessly mired in the slurry created by the Grout-Leeches.

They are silent rivals to the Leeches: the Grout-Leeches seek complete dissolution; the Assemblages seek to impose order, even if that order is meaningless and contradictory to the physical reality of decay.

### The Symbiosis of Stillness

The residents of the Silent Foundry exist in a slow-motion war.

The **Grout-Leeches** thrive in the areas where the Custodian Assemblages have failed to maintain rigidity. Their constant grazing creates the unstable ground beneath the sunken vats, while their metabolic byproducts fuel the next layer of mineral precipitation that coats their own shells.

The **Custodian Assemblages**, even when frozen, serve as anchor points—artificial reefs in the metallic swamp for the Leeches to cluster around, as these metallic structures often draw the highest concentration of dissolved ions. When an Assemblage does move, its clumsy, forceful articulation often dislodges trapped material, releasing a fresh supply of chemically complex runoff for the Leeches to consume.

No noise signifies aggression; the whole ecosystem is one of profoundly slow, almost geological feedback. The mineral-tasting air is the breath shared between the consuming sediment life and the broken, dreaming mechanics.
