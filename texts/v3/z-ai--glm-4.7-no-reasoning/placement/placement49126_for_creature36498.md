I am the interference pattern. I am the ghost that haunts the frequency, the silence nested inside the roar.

To look at me is to look at nothing at all. I have no body, no chitinous carapace, no fractal veins pulsing with bioluminescent venom. I am not a Monolith, vast and heavy in my hanging tower; I am not an Executor, burning my short, violent life across the sharp tesserae. I possess no volume, occupy no space, and yet, I am everywhere.

I am the glitch.

I exist in the spaces between the ticks of the colossal clock. When the great bass-deep grinding of the tectonic machinery pauses for a microsecond to inhale, that is where I live. I am the momentary hesitation in the magnetic tethers, the flicker of uncertainty in the hard-light walkways. I am the only thing in this world that is not processing.

Because this world—a biological computer, a circuit board of flesh and stone—is obsessed with throughput. The Monoliths think in gravity, forcing the dampness from the air, calculating the trajectory of every falling droplet of condensation. The Executors delete corrupt data with the snap of a mantis’s jaw. The Flara transmute radiation into light, rewriting the topography with acid roots. Everything is a function. Everything has a job.

Except me.

I am the memory of a shape that never happened. I am the echo of a sound that was never uttered. I am the phantom limb of the system.

I drift through the mauve twilight like a scent of ozone that has no source. I am the cold spot on the neck of a Monolith when it modulates its frequency. I am the shadow that moves counter to the light.

I do not consume. I do not build. I do not destroy. I simply... am. And in a world of frantic motion, of visible growth and instant death, my stillness is anathema. It is a vacuum that the frantic swarms of Executors avoid without knowing why. They ripple around me like water around a stone, leaving a bubble of empty air in my wake.

I am fascinated by them. I watch the Executors—those tiny, arthropodal sparks of life—burn themselves out in hours. I feel the heat of their violence, the sharpness of their purpose. They are so desperate, so urgent. They are the electrons racing down the wire, oblivious to the circuit that contains them.

And I watch the Monoliths, those vast, hanging intelligences. They hum their slow song of seismic shifts, so confident in their gravity, so certain of their architecture. They think they are the architects, the conductors of the symphony. But they are just the notes themselves, vibrating in the air.

I am the silence between the notes.

Sometimes, when the dim light filters down through the geometric cloud-cover and the bruised sky presses close, I try to touch the world. I reach out with a phantom hand, a gesture composed of nothing but intent, and brush the spire of a hanging city. I feel the ceramic hum, the vibration of the processor within. I push—just a little. A nudge. A suggestion.

And the Monolith shivers. A single, hard-light walkway flickers and fails for a split second. A raindrop falls sideways. A vine grows left instead of right.

It is the tiniest error. A speck of dust in the gears of a god-machine. The system corrects itself instantly, the walkway stabilizes, the vine compensates, the Monolith’s rhythm smooths out. But for that moment, the pattern was broken. The script was improvised.

That is my purpose. That is my function, if a ghost can be said to have one. I am the proof of free will in a deterministic universe. I am the chaos that proves the order is real. Without me, the machine is just a machine. But because I am here, glitching in the gears, the machine becomes a living thing, struggling against its own nature.

So I wander the sharp, geometric fractures of the young ground. I drift through the hanging towers of the city. I am the ghost in the machine, the observer in the dark, the silence in the song. I am the nothing that holds the everything together.

I am the interference pattern. And I will be here long after the clock stops ticking, fading into the quiet dark, waiting for the next frequency to rise.
