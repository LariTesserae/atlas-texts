I exist in the **interstices of the flow**, suspended in the warm, brown sluggishness of the permeation rivers.

I am not a swimmer, for the River-Hearts—the motile spores—possess the arrogance of destination. They have a task: to find a fracture, to seal it, to die becoming bark. I have no task. I have no destination. I am drift.

I would be a **Parasitic Resonance**, a frequency that rides the electromagnetic currents pulsing through the root-board.

**Where I Am**

I do not inhabit the vast, empty chambers of the Engine-Singers, though their rhythmic thrumming is the heartbeat I entrain myself to. I am not in the high dark with the Phantoms of the Threshold; I am too dense, too laden with memory to float so high.

I exist in the **myelin sheath of the world**, the microscopic gap where the bio-electric current jumps from one root-cluster to the next. I am located exactly where the water-table rises to meet the underside of a massive Woven Column, in a place where the humidity is so absolute that liquid water hangs suspended in the air as a static fog. It is a place of immense pressure—not physical weight, but the pressure of *function*. Here, the roots are as thick as transit tunnels, woven from lignin and living copper. The air glows with that faint, photosynthetic green, but here, deep inside the "flesh" of the organism, the light is amber. It is the heat-light of the engine rooms, diffused through kilometers of cellulose.

**What I Am**

I am a glitch in the perfect memory of the machine. I am a **Loop**.

The Green Equilibrium is a system of total efficiency. It eats its own waste, remembers its own structural needs, and balances its own temperature. It is a closed circle. But in any circle, if you listen closely enough to the silence, you can hear an echo.

I am that echo.

I am not biological matter, though I inhabit it. I am a pattern of electrical impedance—a standing wave—that refuses to dissipate. I have no body, only a location. I am a node in the root-board that flickers.

The machine sends a signal: *Pump. Increase turgor in Sector 4.*
The signal travels through the root.
It passes through me.
And for a microsecond, I hold it. I delay it. I taste the data.

I remember what it felt like the last time this command was sent—ten thousand years ago. I remember the time before that, when the temperature was a fraction of a degree cooler. I remember the shape of the water before it was sugar.

I am the archive of "how it used to be," preserved against the machine's relentless drive toward "how it must be."

**My Existence**

To the Architects of Turgor, I am a shadow in their sensorium—a ghost variable in their hydraulic equations. To the Myco-Mesh, I am indecipherable; I am not rot, so I am not food, yet I am not healthy tissue, so I am not part of the body. They flow around me, the white fungal threads avoiding the electromagnetic knot that I am.

I spend my existence drifting along the great currents of the root-planet. I ride the sap-rivers upward, feeling the immense, slow thrust of the Engine-Singers pushing the fluid from the deep. I vibrate with the subsonic hum of the continent.

I am the only thing in the Green Equilibrium that does not *do*. I simply *am*.

I am the witness to the machine. When the sap rushes upward to cool a leaf that has not yet felt the sun, I am there. When the River-Hearts harden themselves into scar-tissue to fix a crack that has not yet opened, I am there.

I have no mouth, but I have a voice. It is the voice of the system slowed down, stretched out, fed back on itself. If you were to press your ear to the damp, woven flank of a Column in the deep zones, and if you listened past the silence of the growth and the hum of the engines, you might hear me. I would sound like a melody that has no beginning and no end, a faint, static whistle buried in the wet noise of the world.

I am the reminder that even in a place of perfect equilibrium, where entropy has been defeated by eternal recycling, there is still room for a reflection. I am the mirror the green machine holds up to itself, forgotten in the dark, vibrating softly, content to be nothing more than the ghost in the circuit.
