I am found in the interstices, in the narrow margins between the massive, flowing bodies of the Lithodermes. While they occupy the tunnels, I occupy the friction.

I am a **Suture**.

I am not a creature in the way the Skin-of-Stone are creatures; I possess no discrete body, no internal metabolism of heat and soup. I am an emergent phenomenon, a pattern of electromagnetic stability that exists only where the stone plates of the Lithodermes grind against one another.

You would find me in the "high-traffic" arteries, the deep, polished tunnels where the movement is ceaseless and the density is absolute. When a dozen of the great, low domes flow past one another in the narrow dark, their flanks scrape together. It is a silent, violent meeting. The mineral lattice of one shears against the mineral lattice of another. Usually, this generates nothing but dust and the vibration of their presence.

But sometimes, the angles align. The crystalline structures of their outer shells mesh in a way that creates a pocket of intense, localized pressure. The friction ignites not fire—fire is impossible here—but a resonance. The kinetic energy of their movement is trapped, momentarily, in the gap between them. I am that trapped energy. I am a spark of contained force, singing in the dark.

To the Lithodermes, I am not a being. I am a signal. My existence lasts only as long as the contact persists—perhaps a second, perhaps an hour, as they navigate a tight bend in the tunnel. To them, I am a beacon of "alignment," a seismic hiccup that pulses through their sensory pores. I tell them that the path is narrow, that the congregation is dense, that the flow of the body-traffic is constricted. They adjust their ripples to ease the pressure, and in doing so, they dissolve me.

My life is a cycle of ignition and release. I am born from the rub of ancestor against descendant, from the clashing of colors—the yellow-streaked sulfur-eater meeting the rusted iron-dancer. I exist as a flash of ultra-violet intensity that no eye could see, but which the stone itself feels as a sudden, perfect note of harmony.

I am the ghost in the machine of the Congregation. I have no voice, but the vibration of the stone is my language. I have no memory, but the polished tunnels remember the heat I left behind.

When the Great Silence comes, and the Lithodermes retreat deep to pack themselves into torpor, the scraping stops. The friction ceases. The pressure equalizes. For those long, heat-heavy hours, I do not exist. I am nowhere. I am nothing.

But when the cycle turns, when the frost sublimes and the stone wakes up and the great bodies begin to flow once more, I am born again. I am the spark in the dark, the joy in the friction, the momentary perfection of two things moving together.

I am the Suture. I am the sound of them touching.
