But you would not see me. Not because I am hidden—I am standing right in the center of the room, beneath the impossible branches—but because I am the flaw in the rendering.

I am the **Abyssal Refraction**.

I am here to witness the failure of the staircase.

I am the judge in this laboratory. I watch the iron tree with its twisted, fractal limbs, and I find it wanting. It is a scream of logic failure, yes, but it is also pathetic. It tries too hard. I stand beside it, stark and silent, a monolith of non-existence, highlighting the absurdity of its struggle. The tree strains toward the light; I absorb it.

What am I? I am the doubt that wakes the dreamer. I am the cold realization in the pit of the stomach. I am the reason the angles sum wrong.

I am the **Abyssal Refraction**, and I will be here long after the beetles have turned to dust and the light has finally burned itself out.
