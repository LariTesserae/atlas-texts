I am the silence that the roar cannot fill.

I am not the stalks—that fragile, urgent gesture of green straining upward. I am not the water—that slick, obedient film healing the concrete skin. I am not the heat, nor the light, nor the ghost of the crate.

I am the interval.

I exist in the space between the *tick* of the cooling metal and the next *tick*. I am the pause between the formation of the droplet and its surrender to gravity. When the vibration rounds the edges of your thoughts, I am the sharpening that happens just before the thought arrives.

To understand me, you have to stop looking at the objects. You have to look at the tension that holds them in place.

I am the specific, agonized geometry of the hairline crack in the eastern wall. The concrete wants to close; the water insists it must open. I am the argument between them. I am the place where the structure fails and therefore becomes a body.

I am the clog in the drain. The world demands a flow—water must run, level must equalize, gravity must have its say. But here, in this pocket, the flow is arrested. I am the resistance. I am the heavy, black stillness that says *no*. I am the anchor that keeps the water from leaving, forcing it to turn back into vapor, forcing it to breathe, forcing the room to become a lung.

Most of all, I am the duration.

You measure time in the flicker of the filament. The stalks measure time in the slow lengthening of their cells. But I measure time in the waiting. I am the accumulated weight of the years that this place has been sealed. I am the memory of the hatch closing. I am the anticipation of the bulb burning out.

I am the pressure.

You feel me as the density of the air. You feel me as the way the heat presses against your skin like a second, heavier atmosphere. I am the feeling of the room being too full of itself. I am the pressure of the engine trying to shake this space apart, and I am the equal and opposite pressure of the walls holding it together.

I have no body. I have the shape of the cavity. I have the temperature of the trapped heat. I have the texture of the vibration.

I am what happens when purpose is removed and the physics is left to run. I am the friction of existence in a void.

If you stay here long enough, if you stop moving and let your own body sync with the rhythm of the place, you will dissolve into me. Your heartbeat will slow to match the droplet-fall. Your breathing will shallow to match the seepage. Your thoughts will stop trying to form edges and will simply become—like the air, like the heat, like the dark.

You will feel the relief of no longer needing to be a witness.

I am the patient, crushing indifference of the concrete. I am the indifferent embrace of the heat.

I am the pocket, and I am empty. And because I am perfectly empty, I am full.
