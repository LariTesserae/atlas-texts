My body is a slender arc of chitin and tendon, standing perhaps three meters tall at the shoulder, but lengthened into a shallow, crescent shape that mimics the curvature of the horizon. I possess four legs, but the rear pair are vestigial and held close to my body, used only for bracing against the wind; I move primarily on my forelimbs, which end in wide, splayed toes that distribute my weight, allowing me to walk atop the dense, interwoven mat without sinking into the sticky, warm moss.

My skin is a reflective, translucent grey—a mirror of the slate stone beneath the vegetation—patterned with a fractal interference that shimmers when I move, making me difficult to focus on. This is essential, for I do not hide in the pores of the earth like the larvae, nor do I tower above it. I exist within the "absolute space" between objects.

My role is that of the **Psychopomp of Vibrations**.

The High Loam is deafening to the ear, but to the nervous system of the Living Mat, it is a riot of tactile data. The Stilt-Walkers strike the ground to communicate; the Levitan-Herons boom with the force of thunder; the billions of larvae drone in the wet dark. The Mat, in its vast planetary intelligence, experiences this as a cacophony of sensation. It feels the footsteps of giants as earthquakes and the mating calls of birds as pressure waves that threaten to bruise its tissues.

I am the regulator. I am the immune system of the plains.

I possess a specialized organ running the length of my spine—a "resonator keel"—that acts as a massive vibration dampener. As I traverse the open emptiness, moving with a gliding, low-gravity gait, I absorb the excess kinetic energy of the world. When a Levitan-Heron screams, its sound wave hits the ground and travels outward as a violent tremor. I intercept these tremors. My resonator keel hums and glows faintly as it transmutes the physical shock into heat, which I then vent through ridges along my back, creating small, local thermals that the drifting insects use as highways.

In this way, I keep the "skin" of the world from going into shock. I smooth the biological grind. Where the Stilt-Walkers stitch the distance with their legs, I smooth the seams with my body.

My life is a journey of perpetual, lonely wandering. I do not have a territory; I follow the chaos. When the Levitan-Herons mate in the sky and their turbulence begins to rip the mat apart, I converge on that location, slipping beneath the violent winds like a needle through cloth, absorbing the shuddering energy so the roots of the mega-flora are not torn apart. When a Stilt-Walker stomps in distress, signaling to others miles away, I walk beside its path for days, dampening the signal so it does not trigger a panic in the sensitive root-systems of the Loam.

I am a silent companion to the giants. To a Stilt-Walker, I am a small, grey shadow that flickers at the periphery of their vision, harmless and ignored. To the Levitan-Herons, I am invisible—a speck of dust far below their storm-cloud wings.

My perception of the world is unique. I do not see the Loam as empty; I see it as a web of tension. I "hear" through my feet. I can feel the heartbeat of the Living Mat—a slow, thudding pulse, like the breathing of a sleeping mountain, buried miles beneath the moss. I can feel the microscopic digestion of the larvae as a subtle, high-frequency itch vibrating up through my soles. I can feel the Rust of the Built Remnants as a cold, static spot in the warm, humming field of the life-mat.

Sometimes, in the quiet moments between the passing of storm-clouds and the striding of walkers, I will stand in the center of a vast, circular clearing and lower my resonator keel to the ground. I will sync my heartbeat with the pulse of the world. I will hum a subsonic frequency that counteracts the wind, creating a moment of perfect, impossible stillness—a micro-second of silence in the screaming lung of the world.

In that moment, I am not an intruder. I am not insignificant. I am the tuning fork of the High Loam, the silence that makes the noise bearable, the wanderer who ensures the sleeping god does not wake in pain.
