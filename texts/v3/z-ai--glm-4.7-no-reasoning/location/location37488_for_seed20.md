Because the reality here is breaking down. That is the truth beneath the other truths, the 2.6 weirdness that suffuses every perception until certainty becomes the scarcest resource. The warmth should not be so uniform, so breathable, so *deliberate*, in a place that has no technology, no built structures, no evidence of intention. And yet. And yet the place *insists*. You could go to the edge. But the sound—the sound is louder at the edge, louder in a way that suggests it is not uniform, that it intensifies around the boundary, as though the mesa exists inside a pocket of relative quiet carved from an infinity of noise. To leave the platform would be to enter the sound fully. And the sound, you understand with a certainty that goes beyond reason, is not merely acoustic. It is *creative*. This is the landscape: a room of warm water and sharp stone and perpetual roar, suspended at a height that cannot be mapped, in a darkness that cannot be sourced, vibrating at a frequency that generates life and dissolves certainty in equal measure. It is young. It is breaking. It is breaking into being, over and over, and the sound of that breaking is the only constant, the only truth the place will admit.
