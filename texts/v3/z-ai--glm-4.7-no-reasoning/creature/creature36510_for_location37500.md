At the highest reaches, where the air is thin and the light blinds, there are the **Sky-Drifters**.

They are not birds, nor are they strictly animals in the way biology defines them. They are vast, leathery polygons of existence, shaped like manta rays but possessing a wingspan that could eclipse a town square. Their skin is pale, translucent parchment, stretched over a lattice of cartilage that mirrors the coral-like structures below. They possess no feathers; their surface is slick and hydrophobic, causing the ever-present fog to bead up and roll off them like mercury.

They do not flap. To flap would be a waste of energy in this thin, violet atmosphere. Instead, they possess a thermal symbiosis. It is a grazing that takes centuries; a single Drifter might work the face of one tower for a thousand years, moving its abrasive lips across the rock like a file, smoothing the city into perfection.

Below them, in the twilight gloom of the mid-levels where the light fractures into aquatic shards, dwell the **Weavers**.
