The inhabitants of this place are not beings in the way we understand biology—carbon and water, heartbeats and breath. They are a consequence of the geometry, a physical manifestation of the city’s architectural failure and the swamp’s psychic overflow. They are the *after-image* of a people, or perhaps the *pre-image* of a species that has not yet fully decided to exist.

They call themselves, to the extent that a thought can ripple through this humid silence, the **Drift-Logic**.

**Physiology: The Fluidity of Stone**
They appear roughly humanoid at a distance, a trick of the eye that lures observers into a false sense of familiarity. They stand upright, possessing a head, two arms, and a trunk. But the resemblance ends there. Their "skin" is not flesh, but a slow-moving aggregation of the city’s material—compacted ash, gray calcite, and pale fungal threads. Their bodies are mottled with the dark, damp stains of the porous rock, looking less like clothing and more like the map of the water table visible through their epidermis.

They do not have joints. Their limbs are single, tapering columns of semi-solid matter, bending with the viscous reluctance of warm wax. When they move, it is not with muscle contraction but with a redistribution of mass. An arm does not swing; it elongates slightly, the substance flowing up the shoulder and down the torso, pooling into the hand that needs to grasp. They are constantly losing themselves to the environment, sloughing off tiny flakes of mud and calcite that immediately begin to dissolve into the streets, only to reabsorb moisture from the air to maintain their volume.

Their faces are the most unsettling aspect. There are no eyes, no mouths, no noses—just smooth, eroded surfaces of wet clay. Occasionally, a crack will form in the clay, deep and dark, leaking a faint, bioluminescent blue vapor from within. This is how they "see." The crack acts as a sensory intake, drinking in the light and the pressure of the room. They do not look *at* things; they look *through* the humidity, tasting the shapes of objects via the distortions they make in the heavy air.

**Behavior: The Keepers of the Wet Void**
They are the custodians of the impossible geometry. You will see them standing in the centers of plazas or at the corners where streets that should be straight begin to curve back on themselves. They stand perfectly still for hours, their bodies swaying imperceptibly with the rise and fall of the water table.

They are adjusting the city.
They possess a low-frequency telekinesis, or perhaps a mastery of the local physics that allows them to manipulate the "softness" of reality. With a slow, deliberate gesture of a flowing arm, a Drift-Logic can encourage a wall to melt slightly, sealing a doorway that leads nowhere. They can straighten a curved staircase that has begun to loop, or deepen a shadow to hide a drop-off into the submerged levels.

They do not build; they *steward*. They are fighting a losing battle against the entropy of the swamp, ensuring that the city remains a labyrinth and does not simply dissolve into a featureless bog. They guide lost water back into the aquifers; they push the fog away from the bioluminescent lichen to give it room to breathe.

**The Submerged Choir**
Deep below, in the sunken portions of the city where the stairs vanish into the warm, dark glass, live the **Echo-Masons**. These are a heavier, denser variant of the species, evolved for the high-pressure depths. Their bodies are composed almost entirely of the heavy, flat bones that pave the streets above, fused together with calcite.

They do not walk; they drag themselves along the silty floor, their massive, stone-like hands carving new rooms out of the submerged mud.

They are the source of the "subsonic groan" heard in the city above. The Echo-Masons sing to the earth. It is a song of maintenance, a low, vibrating thrum that travels through the water table and into the foundations of the ziggurats. This song hardens the mud-bricks, temporarily arresting the decay. It is a sound felt in the teeth and the hollows of the bones more than heard in the ears.

**Diet and Sustenance**
They do not eat. There is no food chain here. Instead, they feed on the **Melting**.

At the edges of reality, where the geometry breaks down, there is a phenomenon of "soft time"—moments where the past and present slide into one another, creating pockets of temporal sludge. The Drift-Logic congregate around these spots. They will press their smooth, crack-less faces into these warps of reality, inhaling the vapor of disintegrating time. They consume the uncertainty, the confusion, and the structural instability of the city.

A wall that becomes a hallway, or a window that looks into yesterday, is a feast for them. By metabolizing these impossibilities, they prevent the weirdness from spreading unchecked. They are the filter that keeps the city from dissolving entirely into madness. They carry the weight of the non-linear within them, which explains their slow, heavy, pained movements.

**Reproduction: The Spawning of Angles**
They do not reproduce biologically. They are *architected*.

When a Drift-Logic becomes too full of the "impossible" energies it consumes, or when it has stood guard at a curved street for too long and absorbed the loop, it becomes heavy with potential. It will seek out a secluded courtyard—a dark, wet mouth of a room—and begin to weep.

It weeps a thick, luminous gray sludge—the excess material of its body mixed with the concentrated impossibility it has consumed. This sludge pools on the floor and seeks out the corners of the room. It forces itself into the right angles, crawling up the walls, filling the joints between the floor and the ceiling.

Over months—years perhaps—the sludge hardens, calcifies, and detaches. It forms a new, smaller geometric shape—perfectly cubic, or triangular, or spherical. This "infant" is a raw, silent geometry. It must learn to soften its edges, to grow its limbs of flowing mud, to crack its face and breathe the humid air. Until it does, it is just another block of wet stone in the endless, empty city.

**The Presence of Absence**
Ultimately, they are the answer to the question of where the people went. They are not ghosts. They are the realization that in a place where the environment absorbs everything, the people eventually absorbed the environment. They are the city’s immune system, made of mud and memory, standing in the quiet, active darkness, waiting for the rain to stop, or for the city to finally finish melting.
