You are going to walk into a stone heart that thinks in slow, deep time, and that is not used to being argued with.  

It will not *try* to kill you. It’s too old for that.  
But you are very fragile by its standards.

Below is a preparation guide for an ordinary human multiverse traveler who intends to survive *and* respect where they are.

---

## 1. Before You Step: Understanding the Risk

The problem in this place is not monsters or predators.  
The problem is *scale* and *kind*.

- **You are fast and short‑lived.**  
  Everything here lives in *geological* time.  
  A heartbeat can be minutes long.  
  You panic in seconds; the stone panics in eons, if at all.

- **You are chemical.**  
  Your body is a delicate balance of water, salts, and membranes.  
  The blue stuff that grows on the walls and ceiling is *not* Earth life.  
  It’s older than bone. Don’t assume it’s safe.

- **You are emotional.**  
  The stone bowl *radiates* a quiet sense of invitation, patience, and gentle presence.  
  Some people find this terrifyingly intimate.  
  Some people find it addictive.

- **The barrier is not visible.**  
  At the edge of the bowl, there is *some* kind of field or interface separating you from the roaring outside.  
  If you walk blindly where you think the floor ends, you might walk *past* that boundary and be *erased* by whatever is outside—a machine, a body, or a sky older than stars.

Treat this like walking into a cathedral that is also a living heart, that also contains a cloister of patient, non‑human minds.

Your main goal:  
Survive, *without* scarring the stone, *without* poisoning its life, *without* losing yourself in the time‑scale.

## 2. Mental Preparation

### Pre‑visit training

- **Practice meditation under sensory deprivation.**  
  You will be in a quiet, dimly lit space with *almost* no change.  
  You need to be able to stay calm without distraction.

- **Read or watch material about cave diving, deep‑time geology, mycology.**  
  You’re preparing to respect *slow* life, *hidden* life, and *indifferent* environment.

- **Do self‑containment exercises:**
  - Practice staying absolutely still for ten minutes.  
  - Practice breathing very shallowly.  
  - Practice moving without *needing* to touch everything.

### Emotional hardening

- Do **boundary visualization**:
  - Imagine yourself inside a thin, luminous envelope of your own attention.
  - You *notice* the environment without *melting* into it.
- Recognize *this* specific danger:
  
  > The stone bowl *accepts you*.  
  > If you are not careful, you may accept it *back* so completely  
  > that you forget there are other worlds to return to.

This is not metaphysics; this is homeostasis psychology.  
You *can* feel *too* at home.

### Reality grounding cues

To anchor your sanity:

- Wear **two watches**:
  - One local relative timekeeper  
  - One external, multiversal reference clock  
    (if your tech allows: quantum‑linked to your home base).
- Choose **three words or questions** that you repeat to yourself:
  - “I am going back.”
  - “I have a body.”
  - “Time is not *only* here.”

If you lose either watch or begin to forget your phrases, *that* itself is a warning sign.

## 3. Gear & Equipment

### No raw skin, no metal plating

Your suit should be *sealed*, but *quiet*.

**Minimum:**

- **Full-body, soft environmental suit**  
  - Sealed to particles *and* humidity.  
  - Breathable, but with *no external metal* fasteners or buckles at key points.
    - Metal *may* interact with whatever boundary is at the bowl’s edge.
- **Non‑conductive soles.**  
  - No steel toes or heavy tread.  
  - You need to feel *subtle* vibration underfoot.
- **Soft head cover or hood** (under any helmet).  
  - No hair exposed.
    - The ceiling filaments *do* respond to heat.
    - Hair is surface area.

**Optional high‑tech additions:**

- Internal atmospheric recycler with *silent* fans.
- Internal humidity/temperature/trace gas sensors.
- *Non*‑projecting display inside your helmet visor or goggles (no external lights or beams).

### Vision and sensing

- **Low‑light adjustable optics.**  
  - The bowl *glows* faintly, but you *don’t* want to flash‑light everything.
- **Optional: thermal vision.**  
  - Useful to tell *living* stone from inanimate stone (if they are different).
- **Spectroscopic analyzer** built into your suit or handheld scanner:
  - To analyze the *blue* growths  
  - To check *water* composition  
  - *Before* you expose *any* skin to the environment.

**Do not bring:**

- Strong external lamps.  
  This is *not* a dark, empty cave.  
  Bright light will horrify the eye-like things and *may* agitate the stone ceiling patterns.
- Lasers or cutting tools (unless as *absolute* last resort).
- Drones, unless they are *silent*, and *station‑keeping only*.
  - Sound *and* motion patterns *matter* here.

### Water and air

- **Independent, sealed life support.**  
  - A compact rebreather or equivalent, with:
    - CO₂ scrubber
    - Humidity management
    - *Optional* filters for *very* exotic trace gases.
- **Your own water** supply for *drinking*, complete:
  - Do *not* count on drinking *from* the stone bowl pool.
    - Not *ever* if you can avoid.

### Recording tools

- **Passive acoustic sensors.**  
  - Not *loud* active sonar; passive *microphones* are fine.
- **Non‑flash, low‑light imagers**:
  - Cameras that *do* *not* project visible light.
- **Optional: internal EEG / biofeedback patch.**  
  - You want to know if your *brain* is being gently influenced by the ceiling patterns or the *feeling* of the place.

### Other safety items

- A *blunt* probe for *gentle* tactile inspection:
  - Non‑conductive, *soft* tip.
- Tiny *disposable* sample vials:
  - Crimp‑sealed, *not* screw cap.
  - *Assumption*: you may *never* risk opening them inside the bowl.
    - Use *only* if *extreme* necessity or protocol from analysis.
- Minimal *med* kit:
  - Generic antiallergic, anxiolytics (if safe for you), mild sedative.
    - Your *brain* is a bigger risk than your flesh.

## 4. Physical Conditioning

You *won’t* be doing heavy cardio in this place.  
You *will* be doing low‑intensity, low‑impact endurance:

- Practice:
  - Sitting *very* still for long periods.
  - Walking *slowly* without shuffling or stomping.
    - You *want* to *not* disturb the water’s slow rhythm.
- **Finger dexterity**:  
  - For fine, *controlled* touches *inside* suit gloves.
    - If you do *anything* to the environment, it must be intentional, *not* clumsy.

If you have **balance or inner ear issues**, double caution.  
Gravity and *sense* of gravity may be subtly *not* what you expect.

## 5. Entry & Timing

### How to arrive

- **No dramatic entrances.**  
  - No sudden flash, no crash, no loud engines.
  - Ideally, appear *already* standing—*gradually*—at one edge of the floor.
    - Multiverse travel methods that allow *slow* extrusion are better than *popping* into existence.

- **Step out* only* once your suit is sealed and you feel fully centered.**

### When inside

- **Freeze for a count of ten slow breaths.**
  - Let *your* body’s rhythms *adapt* to the static warmth of the stone.
  - Feel *your* sense of time *stretch* out *before* you start moving.

- **Notice the *boundary* before you notice anything else.**  
  - Before you admire the ceiling, *find* the edges of the bowl:
    - Visual clues:
      - That *faint* shimmer or field at the rim.
    - Auditory:
      - Beyond the *quiet* of the bowl, the *roar* and *whooping* and *thunder* of *outside*.
    - You *should* feel *some* subtle sensory cutoff as you approach the rim.

## 6. How to Move & Where *Not* to Step

### Movement etiquette

- **Walk like a librarian on the edge of sleep.**  
  - Your goal is to make *less* sound than the falling drops.
- **Avoid touching *anything* except *floor*, and *only* floor* unless deliberate.**

### What *not* to do

- **Do *not* lean against the smooth walls.**  
  - Stone *flows* around *native* lifeforms.
    - *You* are *not* native.
    - *If* the wall *tries* to flow around you, *you* may be stretched, crushed, or *merged* at their speed, not yours.
- **Do *not* reach out to the ceiling filaments.**  
  - They *react*.
    - They *curl*.
    - *You* don’t *know* how long a *seemingly* gentle reaction may be.
- **Do *not* walk directly under the ceiling eyes.**
  - *Especially* the *larger* pair.
    - You don’t *know* that *you* being *above* them *means* anything;  
    - but *around* them is *clean*.
- **Do *not* break the *line* of the *central* water ring.**  
  - *Walk* around *except* when you *choose* to be *carefully* present *at* the center.

### Path layout (recommended)

- Trace a *narrow* clockwise *orbit* around:
  - Start *far* from the central depression.
  - Gradually *tighten* orbit *after* you *feel* the *tempo* of the water drops.
- **Avoid* stepping* anywhere* you* can* see* fine* webbing* between* blue* filaments* or* faint* silk* lines*.
  - Those *webs* are *not* empty decoration.
    - For *all* you know, they carry *fluid* or *signals* like *nerves*.
- *Only* after *full* sensor pass *and* stillness *may* you* *approach* the *center*.

## 7. The Water: *Look* But *Don’t* Drink

### For *no* conditions* should* you* drink* the* water* in* this* first* visit.*

- You *might* — if *ever* — be allowed to *taste* it *someday*, after*:
  - Long *contact* with *guardians* of *this* bowl*.
  - *Full* understanding *of* its* bio* chemistry*.
- *This* is not that day.

### Interactions* with* the* water* pool*:

1. **Never* block* a* drop* from* hitting* the* floor* or* pool*.**  
   - That *tap* is *information* as *well* as *water*.
2. **Do *not* touch* or* stir* the* strange* surface* film.*  
   - *Likelihood*: *that* skin* is* more* alive* than* you* are* comfortable* with*.
3. If *you* must* sample*:
   - Use *a* non* reactive* pipette*.
   - Take *the* absolute* minimum*.
   - Extend* from *beside* the *pool* edge* (*not* leaning* over* it*).
   - *Do* *not* breathe* out* directly* over* it*.

*Any* time* the* central* ring* *glows* brighter* around* the* water*:

- Step *back*.
- Note *the* *external* sound* pattern.
- Something *is* *paying* *attention*.

  - UV* reflective*.
  - Chemically* active.

- You *may* be emitting *something* they *like*:
  - *Heat*.
  - *Electric* field.

## 9. Ceiling Patterns: *Do* *Not* *Stare* *Without* *Guard*

Those *slowly* flickering *polygons* are *not* decorations.

*You* should *assume* they are:

- *Sensing* you.
- *Processing* your *presence*.
- Possibly *modifying* your *brain* *very* *subtly* over *time*.

Precautions:

- **Use *your* external or high‑precision timekeeping* to *track*:
  - If *a* minute *feels* like *ten* seconds *to* you *internally*, *that’s* data*.
- **Periodic *look*‑aways**:
  - *Fix* your *eyes* on *your* boots *or* the *floor* for *one* minute *every* five* minutes*.
    - *Calibrate* your *sense* of *flow*.
- *If* you *start* to *feel*:
  - like *the* stones *are* talking *to* you *in* a *language* you *understand* *but* can’t *hear*—
  - or *like* you *see* *patterns* everywhere *like* perfectly *clear* code *messages* on *every* surface—
    - It *is* *time* to *step* *out*.

*This* is not *necessarily* bad; *it*’s *not* necessarily *safe* either.

### *Crucially*:

- **Do *not* walk *through* that* boundary* unless *you* fully *understand* what* is *outside*.**  
  - The *thunder* and *scraping* and *whooping* out there are *not* meant* for *unprotected* humans*.
    - *Judging* from *scale* and *volume*:
      - Those *sounds* belong *to* something *that* would* *step* on *you* like *you* step *on* dust* *motes*.

*Think* of the *bowl* as *a* pocket *of* safety* inside *a* machine *body* or *world*‑sized *organism*.

## 11. The *Large* Eyes *&* Stone *Climbers*

- They *are*:
  - *Attached* to *something* living*.
  - *Living* inside *or* on *the* stone.

- *Do* not *approach* closer *than* three* meters* unless *you* clearly* feel* invited* *by* context* (you* likely* won’t).
- *If* one *comes* down *the* wall* *toward* you:
  - *Stand* very *still*.
  - *Don’t* scream* or* flail*.
    - *It* does *not* know *your* fear *scale*.
    - To *it* you *might* be *moving* *like* lightning* already*.

    - Expect *something* that *feels* like *cold* smooth *stone*, but *not* cold *enough* to *hurt*.
    - You *may* feel *joint* vibration *or* tingling *through* the *contact* point*.

          - *Bone* structure *density*
          - *Electrical* activity *in* heart* or *brain*

*If* anything *ever* *seems* to *be* *moving* you *without* your *own* muscles:

- That *is* your *exit* condition *—* *now*.

## 13. Exit *Conditions* & *Return* Home

*You* should *leave* under *these* circumstances*:

1. **Your perception* of time* clearly* distorted* beyond* safe* thresholds*:
   - e.g. *you* look *at* the *watch* and *a* minute* has *been* ten* minutes* *external*).
2. **Physical* changes*:
   - You *feel* *weighted* down *like* stone *is* pulling *your* bones *toward* the floor*.
   - Any *numb* patch *on* your body* that *doesn’t* recover* after *you* move* away* from* the center.
3. **Mental* changes*:
   - You *start* feeling *like* you *should* stay *forever*.
   - The *outside* world *becomes* distant *like* a *story* you *once* heard.
4. **The* bowl*’s* environment* changes*:
   - Drops *fall* *much* faster* than* before*.
   - The *blue* ring *brightens* significantly.
   - More *eyes* than *before* turn *toward* you*.

*When* you *leave*:

- **Back away slowly *from* center.**
- *Do *not* run *to* exit.*
- *At* the *threshold*:
  - *Pause* for *a* final *count* of ten* breaths*.
  - *Confirm* your *own* name*, *where* you *are from*, and *your* return* address*.
- *Then* step *through* your *travel* interface* *away* from *this* stone *heart*.

## 14. After *the* Visit

*Do* *not* underestimate *the* subtlety*:

1. **Medical* scan**:
   - Check *for*:
     - Any *micro* calcifications *where* creature* touched* you*.
     - Unusual *patterns* in *brain* or *heart* electrical* activity*.
2. **Quarantine**:
   - Store *your* suit *and* any *sample* vials *carefully*.
   - Assume *they* may be *seeded* with *micro* lifeforms* you *cannot* see*.
3. **Psychological* debrief**:
   - Describe *the* *experience* to *someone* else *before* you *sleep*.
   - Note *if* you *still* *feel* like *the* stone *bowl* is *calling* you*.

*If* you *keep* dreaming *about*:

- *Smooth* pale *stone*.
- The *gentle* tap *of* drops *on* stone *floor*.
- The *blue* filaments *reaching* down *like* hair* from *the* ceiling*.

Then *your* relationship *with* that* place *is not* over*—* but* you *still* need *to* decide *whether* you *want* it *to* *continue*.

## 15. Final *Mindset*

Take *these* intentions *with* you:

1. **You *are* *visiting*, *not* *moving* in.  
   - No *matter* how *welcoming* the stone feels, *you* are *a* guest* in* a *time*‑scale *that* will *outlast* all* human* life*.

   - No *one* and *nothing* there *is* going *to* adjust *its* behavior *for* your *health*.

     - The *stone* might *still* recall *you*.
