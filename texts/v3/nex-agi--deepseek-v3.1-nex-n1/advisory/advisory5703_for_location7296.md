Visiting Thaumos as a baseline human multiverse traveler is possible, but it requires a level of preparation unlike traveling through a normal alien environment. This place isn’t just “hot” or “weird”; the air itself is engineered as a tool, and the city literally uses heat and pressure as building materials. You need not just to avoid heatstroke but to avoid being treated as a contaminant, or food, or a structural component.

Below I’ll outline what you absolutely need to know and how I would prepare to go there, step by step.

## 1. Understand What You’re Walking Into

### 1.1. The environment is not passively “dangerous”; it’s *functionalized*

- Heat is not just weather. It’s infrastructure:
  - The air carries water vapor to the condensate-collectors.
  - Your body heat may be enough to alter local microflows.
  - Heat “pools” on the plateau because there is no engineered venting for it.
- Pressure is not a neutral fact of altitude:
  - “Pressure in your teeth” and feeling “drawn out of your head” suggests engineered pressure gradients.
  - This city literally uses differentials in air density as *tools*.
- The soundscape is at 0.1:  
  - Very low sound level, but the world is not truly silent; vibrations are instead felt through bone and skin.
  - This favors things that sense pressure, not that listen to noise.

Takeaway:  
You are entering not a landscape but a machine whose working fluids are air, heat, and water.

---

### 1.2. The world is built on water and life-form

- Roads are not stone — they are a skin over:
  - “Glass-thread” organisms in the mineral substrate.
  - Heat/water “veins” that pulse with aqueous flow.
- The plateau itself is a basalt table, but coated with:
  - “Manufactured pavement” formed by steam cycles over time.
  - A thin film of water that changes from footprint to footprint.
- The fauna use:
  - Air corridors as near-invisible aquariums.
  - Pressure gradients as biting jaws.
  - Water channels as highways.

Visually there is no “nature” you would recognize — no plants — but the system is highly alive and integrated.

### 1.3. The city is not only human

- “Thaumos itself is both city and machine.”

---

### 1.4. “Strangeness rating” 1.5/3
