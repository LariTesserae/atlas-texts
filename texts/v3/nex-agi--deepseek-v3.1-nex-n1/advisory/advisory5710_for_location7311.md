### 1. Orientation: What You’re Actually Dealing With

You’re not going to a city built on a planet.  
You are going to a city that is *almost* the geology of its place, a living organism still taking its basic shape.

Think of Jelibeth as:

- **A body:** more organism than machine, with circulation (sap), capillaries (streets/canals), and a slow metabolism.
- **Not water-based but sap-based:** Everything you think of as “water” on Earth is replaced by a sticky, translucent sap that serves as fluid, nutrient, and structural binder.
- **Pre-industrial, but not “primitive”:** There are no metals, no machines, no wheels, no locks. Things are negotiated *biologically* — between mound-beasts, plants, and the soft stone of the city.

Your “preparation” is less about gadgets and more about **re-wiring your expectations**, and then choosing gear and behavior that aligns with the reality you’ll find.

---

### 2. Mindset & Psychological Prep

You will be entering a place that is:

- **Always awake, but half-asleep**  
  It never feels fully inert. It feels like a huge body breathing slowly — shifting underfoot, walls slowly “metabolizing,” stone being reused from within.

- **Quiet**  
  There are no engines, no clanging metal, no honking traffic.  
  Sound comes from subtle organic movement: soft clicks, low murmuring, shifting fronds, the slow shuffle of mound‑beasts.

- **Non-human-centric**  
  Humans are not the obvious authors of this city. The city itself is.  
  You are not visiting “a place where humans live”; you are visiting **the interior of a living system that also happens to contain humans**.

Key mindset shifts:

- Let go of **control** and **clear causality** (“A button does B result”).  
  Instead, expect **relationship and negotiation** (“If I give this, will that accept it?”).
- Accept **slow** and **oblique**:  
  Paths are not straight lines; “getting there” is not fast. The rhythm of the place is slow and steady.
- Plan **for integration**, not intrusion.  
  You want to be accepted like one of the local creatures — unobtrusive, predictable, not threatening to the body.

If you have access to any basic training or simulation:

- Do **quietude training**: spend hours in very low‑stimulus environments, not speaking, not checking devices.  
- Practice **unhurried movement**: slow walking, meditative breathing while you walk, no “dashing.”  
  Jelibeth punishes haste with confusion and friction.

### 3. How Your Body Will Experience Jelibeth

**Humidity & air:**
- Air is “weighty without being wet” — not muggy like a rainforest, but with a subtle **pressure and warmth**.
- This is not a desert with burning dry air; it’s more like a **warm cellar** — still, but not stifling.

**Temperature:**
- Nights: warm ground from below, cooler air above — pleasant **low-level warmth** underfoot.
- Days: warmer than night but not “blazing.”  
  Expect **comfortable to warm**, not searing-hot.

**Hydration & soil-sap:**
- There is **no free water** as you know it: no rivers, no lakes, no rain that you can rely on.
- The ground and vegetation hold a **clear syrup/sap**: thin, clear, slightly bitter, root-and-metal taste.
- This sap is the **dominant fluid** of the world.

**What this means for you:**
- You cannot mentally rely on “I’ll find a stream” or “it will rain tonight.”  
- You’ll likely **absorb** this sap through skin and mouth, and your body may need some adjustment. (More on that below.)

**Light & color:**
- Light is **clear but low**, not blazing harsh sun.
- Colors: pale chalky stone, bone-white, tan, **soft greens, silvered undersides of leaves, faint yellow**.
- Even at midday, the world feels **diffused** rather than dramatic.

**Terrain & elevation:**
- Almost flat with modest **humps and low rises** — not hills so much as **“pressure blisters”** the city is slowly forming as it grows.
- Streets **emerge from the same surface** as the ground; no clear border between “city” and “nature.”
- Pavements are **warm**, even in the shade, at night.

**Physical sensations:**
- Expect a **constant background warmth** under your soles.
- Movement: **rolling, not jarring**; surfaces are often softened by plant mats and sap.
- Crowds: **dense but not loud**; body-to-body but with gentle mutual accommodation rather than pushiness.

You may feel:
- Slightly **heavy**, but not tired.
- Hypnotically **quiet** inside.
- Eventually **very thirsty**, if you don’t manage your sap intake.

### 4. Physical Health & Biological Adaptation

**Critical: you are entering a sap-based ecology.**

Your body expects **H₂O**; Jelibeth offers **something else**. It’s not poison outright, but it is not optimized for you.

**Before the jump:**

- Run **biomedical basic** checks:
  - Baseline kidney function, liver enzymes, electrolytes.
  - Any history of **renal issues**, metabolic disease, or unusual allergies — these are flags for extra caution.
- Minimum **three weeks** of heavy physical training in **low-tech** conditions:
  - Long walks without electronics, carrying **weight**, using **non-mechanical tools**.
- Practice **water fasting** (under supervision) for **24‑36 hours** to feel how your body responds when water-scarce.  
  Jelibeth will not be dry in the “empty desert” sense, but it will lack **your** water.

**If your body can be enhanced:**

- **Microbiome pre-conditioning:**  
  Implant or ingest **engineered microbial cultures** tailored to:
  - Break down **non-water sap** into more usable metabolites.
  - Handle **trace minerals** in the sap (which is likely high in calcium/alkaline salts, given all the chalk and limestone).
- **Kidney & gut protection:**  
  Anti‑crystal agents, **electrolyte buffering**, and **osmoregulation modulators** if your tech allows.
- **Basic adaptation suite:**  
  - Mild **thermoregulation** adjustment (to better tolerate persistent warmth).
  - **Metabolic versatility** mods (so you can run better on **non-standard sugars**).
- **Optional:** **slow‑oxygenation** boost or **hemoglobin modification** if the air is subtly thicker but oxygen not perfectly matched to Earth.

> **Do not arrive** assuming **“it’s just water with taste”** or **“I’ll be fine, I’m tough.”**  
> Jelibeth can **gently, quietly** break you by chemistry, not drama.

**During stay:**

- **Monitor yourself** for:
  - Headache, **concentrated urine**, **unusual thirst** beyond normal.
  - **Crystal deposits** in urine, **stomach cramps**, or **neural fog**.
- Use **urinalysis tabs** (if you have them) early and often, until you know you’re stable.
- Adjust **sap intake**:
  - Initially **small sips**, mixed **with your own carried water**, if permitted.
  - Gradually **increase** as you acclimate.

### 5. Gear: What You Actually Need (and Must Not Bring)

#### 5.1. **Water & Fluid**

You **cannot** rely on finding potable Earth‑style water. Jelibeth’s sap is usable **environment** but not guaranteed **fuel**.

Carry with you:

- **Collapsible water containers**, 2‑3 **liters** of **sterile water** from your own world:
  - For **medicine** if sap must not touch your meds.
  - For **emergency rehydration** if your body rejects the sap.
- **Ceramic or clay drinking vessels**:
  - Jelibeth is **ceramic and chalk**, not metal and machine.  
    **Do not bring** taste-imprinting metal containers; they’re culturally alien and may interact badly with the sap.
- **Strainers or cloth filters**:
  - For pre‑filtering **small** amounts of sap if you decide to try drinking it.
  - **Do not** filter the **sealed** gels in the canals; you don’t know what you’re disturbing.

**Do not bring:**
- Flashy **metal bottles**, **military canteens**, or anything **synthetic and shiny**.
- **Purification tablets** meant for **H₂O** — these are useless in a sap-based environment and may produce harmful by-products.

#### 5.2. **Clothing & Body Protection**

Principles:
- **Soft**, **breathable**, **muted**.
- Materials **friendly** to chalk, sap, and **lichen‑like animal** contact.
- No **synthetic plastics** that are obviously **not of this world**.

Recommended:
- **Natural fibers**: linen, light wool, or **woven plant‑based textiles**.
- **Loose, layered garments**:
  - A thin inner layer (to wick moisture).
  - A thicker outer layer for **cooler nights**.
- **Closed, but soft-footed shoes**:
  - **Leather or cloth** soles; **no hard rubber or metal**.
  - Surfaces are warm, sometimes **spongy** from plant mats and gel; hard soles feel **aggressive** underfoot.
- **Head covering**:
  - **Wide-brimmed hat**, light and unassuming, to protect from **low** but constant **daylight**.
- **Optional**: **Chalk-dust colored** overgarments — they will help you visually **merge** with the walls at the edges of sight, which is calming for locals.

**Colors to avoid:**
- **Bright synthetic colors**, neon, **vivid fluorescent**.
- Jelibeth is **pale**, **soft**, **muted**.  
  You don’t want to be a **sharp scream of color** in a quiet body.

#### 5.3. **Tools & Implements**

Since **there are no machines, no metalwork**, your tools should be **simple** and **organic-feeling**.

Bring:
- **Ceramic or stone knife**: plain edge, no metal.
- **Bone or wood needles** and **fibrous thread**:
  - For repair of clothing, **slight gear adjustments**.
- **Chalky tablets** or **waxed cloth** for:
  - **Writing notes**  
    - Don’t bring **plastic notebooks** or **metal pens**; they’re jarring.
- **Small container of “neutral” local earth**:  
  If you can **pretreat** your tools with **Jelibeth‑style soil/sap residue** before you go, you’ll smell and feel **a bit more like you belong**.

**Do not bring:**
- **Any powered device**: phones, electronic tablets, **flashlights**, **lasers**.
- **Anything with gears**, **clockwork**, or **complex metal mechanisms**.
- **Guns** or **explosives**.  
  Jelibeth is **non-mechanical**. Bringing **obvious** high-tech tools will **mark** you as aggressively **non-belonging**.

If you must have light:

- **Bioluminescent cultures** from **border** ecologies that can **interface** with Jelibeth’s sap:
  - Embedded in **glass or clay vessels**, **not** plastic or metal.
- **Wick** style **fat lamps** with **natural fibers** and **oil** from **organics** (not petroleum).

**Implant tech:**  
- Keep **implant** tech **subcutaneous** and **non-display**: nothing that glows visibly, nothing **exposed**.
- Your **internal** enhancements (e.g., metabolism mods) are acceptable as long as they don’t **visibly or audibly** manifest.

#### 5.4. **Medical & Survival**

**Essential medical kit:**

- **Metabolic regulators**: for electrolytes, **alkalosis** or **acidosis** adjustment.
- **Basic anti-inflammatory & analgesics**: for joint soreness, **quiet infections**.
- **Anti-fungal & anti-biofilm** treatments:
  - Jelibeth is **wet** with sap and gels; **fungus and biofilms** thrive.
- **Protective skin creams**:  
  - To protect **cuts** from being colonized too quickly by local sap-based microbes.

**Survival extras:**

- **Compact, collapsible shelter fabric**:  
  - Made from **fiber**, not plastic, if possible.
  - Design it to **not stand out**: foldable into something **small and chalk-white**.
- **Simple rope or cord**:  
  - Made from **plant fibers**, not synthetic nylon.

**What not to bring:**

- Nothing **radioactive**, **strongly acidic**, or **corrosive**.
- No **chemical pesticides** or synthetic **disinfectants** you plan to dump into the environment.
  - Jelibeth is **one body**; you don’t poison **part** of it without damaging **all**.

### 6. Navigation & Movement in Jelibeth

**The streets** don’t follow grid logic or clear hierarchy. They branch **like capillaries**: confusing from above, but **locally intuitive** if you accept **drift**.

**Expect:**

- Streets **pinching** and **widening** without obvious reason.
- **Dead-end alleys** that are not actually dead-end: they **flow into** courtyards or interior rooms **open to the street**.
- Paths that **circle** back to where you started **until** the city decides **you** are ready for a destination.

**Navigation strategy:**

- Learn **three** kinds of landmarks:
  1. **Distinctive roof mats**: unique color or **pattern of weaving**.
  2. **Salts-scars**: faint white “script” on stone walls.
  3. **Particular mound-beasts** if you can **reliably recognize** them (they move **slowly**, so you can).
- Always **give yourself extra time**.
- **Do not** rush; **accept** detours and reintroduce yourself to **humility** regarding “efficient routing.”

**When in doubt:**

- Move **with** local foot traffic:
  - The locals **flow** like fluids in channels — their **collective** path is more reliable than **your** logic.
- Observe **mound-beasts**:
  - They seem to choose paths by **pressure of the sap below**.  
  - Following **near** a mound-beast (without **blocking** or touching) often **gives** you a stable route.

**Physical posture:**

- Walk **soft**, quiet, **rolling** step.
- Don’t **stomp**; don’t **clatter**.
- Keep **hands** where **seen** but not **active** — like relaxed **vines**.

### 7. Food & Sustenance

**Do not assume** your food habits **or** digestive system match local cuisine.

**Before eating anything:**

- **Survey**: watch what locals **actually** eat.
- **Tiny** amount first:
  - Small sample; **wait** 24 hours.
- Check:
  - **Nausea**, **bloating**, **skin reactions**, **changes in urine or stool**.

Probable local foods:

- **Low, fibrous vegetation**: think **soft stems**, **rope-like fronds**, **mat-like greens**.
- **Insect-tissue** (the **chitinous** things you’ll see).
- Possibly **soft, sap-based gels** harvested from canal-linings.

**Bring:**

- A **small** supply of your own **stable** rations:
  - **Nutrient-dense** cakes or **pressed bars** from **home**—but make sure **packaging** is not **plastic**; **waxed cloth** or **hard cakes** are better.
- Use **your** food **only** when:
  - You are unsure of local food;
  - You are recovering from **illness**;
  - You **must** take **medications** that **need** your home-food baseline.

**Never**:

- Dump **your** food waste into **canals** or **onto** roofs.
- Eat **unknown** canal-gels directly **without** local guidance.

### 8. Interacting with Locals & Local Life

**Assume** locals are **deeply integrated** with the city’s body:

- They may **feel** disturbances **through** sap and **ground**.
- Loud **aggressive** behavior can **reverberate** in **unpredictable** ways.

**Approach:**

- **Calm**, **slow**, **respectful**.
- **Do not** raise your voice or **make** sudden moves.
- **Do not** hack at vegetation or **poke** into walls **without** invitation.

**Greetings:**

- Likely **subtle**: slight **pause**, **physical** slowing-down, a **hand** gesture that **imitates** the **slow** motion of **plants**.
- If you copy **anything** they **do**, do **not** overdo it — subtle **echo** is better than **exact mimic**.

**Communication:**

- Speech **may** be limited:
  - Expect **soft clicks**, low **murmurs**, **touch** and **proximity** more than **formal** language.
- If you have **translation support** (neural lace / implanted AI), **do not** broadcast **obvious** synthetic sounds:
  - Use **internal** interpretation only; **you** speak **out** using **your** voice and **body**, **slow** and **clear**.

**Regarding the creatures:**

- **Mound-beasts**:
  - **Do not** obstruct them; **do not** climb on them; **do not** treat them like **transport**.
  - They are **part of the foundation**. **Watch** and **let** them **be**; **do** not **“use”**.
- **Wall-clingers (lichen-fur creatures)**:
  - **Do not** grab or pull them.
- **“Birds” (thread‑feather** floaters)**:
  - **Do not** throw objects, **do not** shout, **do not** chase them.

**Your** job is to **move as** another **cell** in **their body**, **not** to **command** or **test**.

### 9. Housing & Shelter

**Where you sleep:**

- **Stay close** to **the** city’s **surface**, **not** high **on** roofs **unless** invited:
  - Roofs are **living mats**; **treading** heavily **disrupts** the **canopy**.
- Look for **guest-spaces** hollowed out of **existing chalk-stone** structures:
  - **Small chambers** reused from **older** homes, **simple** niches **with** minimal **decoration**.
- **Do not** insist on **“private locked room”**:
  - Locking **doors** do **not** exist **here**.
- **Expect**:
  - **Curtained** thresholds.
  - **Shared** spaces **where** several **people** sleep **near** each other **quietly**.

**Your shelter:**

- If **no** human-built space **is offered**:
  - Choose **an** alcove **out** of the **main** flow of **traffic**, where **wall** and **roof** meet **low**.
- Use **your** fibrous **shelter cloth**:
  - **Pin** or **twine** it **gently** to **vegetation**, **not** driving **sharp** stakes into **walls**.

**Sleep** rhythm:

- Jelibeth’s **day‑night** cycle seems **gentle**, not **extreme**.
- Expect **soft** transition **from** warm **night** to **warm** day.
- Align **your** sleep **to** local **rhythm** as **soon** as **possible**:
  - **Walk** by day, **rest** by night.

### 10. Long-Term Stay Considerations

If you plan to stay **more than a few weeks**:

**Integration:**

- Find **simple** ways **to** participate:
  - Moving **stones** for **mound‑beasts** **only** if **invited**.
  - **Weaving** or **mending** **plant mats** **under** guidance.
- Help **maintain** **canals**:
  - **Removing** debris **from** edges, **never** from **inside** the **gel** without **clear** instruction.

**Reputation:**

- You **will** be **remembered** **more** by **what** you **did** than **by** your **look**:
  - If **you** are **calm**, **helpful**, **quiet**, **non-destructive**, **you** become **part** of **the** city **story**.
- If **you** force **things**, **break** things, **disrupt** flows **of** sap **or** paths **of** beasts, **you** will **stick** out **like** a **toxin** in **the** bloodstream **and** that **will** be **known** **throughout** the **body**.

**Departure:**

- When **leaving**, **do** not **take** **more** than **small** tokens:
  - **Samples** only **with** local **consent**:
    - A **small** piece of **stone**, **a** few **fronds**, **not** **entire** plants **or** living **creatures**.
- **Heal** any **damage** you **made**, **restore** what **can** be restored, **leave** footprints **not** scars **in** the **flesh** **of** Jelibeth.

- **Have I** accepted **that** this **city** is **not** a **machine** **but** **a** living **body**?
- **Have** **I** adjusted **my** **pack** **to** avoid **anything** that **feels** **like** **metal**, **plastic**, **sharp** synthetic, **or** **complex** gadgetry?
- **Do I** have **sufficient** **water** from **home** **to** **buffer** **my** body **while** **adapting**?
- **Have** **I** prepared **my** body **with** microflora **and** metabolic **mods** **if** I **can** access **them**?
- **Have** **I** trained **myself** **to** **walk** **slowly**, **speak** **quietly**, **and** **accept** **circuitous** paths?
- **Am I** ready **to** **not** **be** in control **but** **instead** **flow** **with** **the** **city’s** circulatory system **as** **one** **more** **cell** **in** its **vast** body?

If you can answer **yes** to these **honestly**, you **are** as ready **as** an **ordinary** human **multiverse** traveler **can** be **for** a **city** like **Jelibeth** — a **hungry**, **half-asleep** organism **still** **forming** **its** **own** geology.
