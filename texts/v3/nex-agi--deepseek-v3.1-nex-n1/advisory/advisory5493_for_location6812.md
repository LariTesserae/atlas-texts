To walk on Qetram as an ordinary human multiverse traveler is not mainly about “survival,” in the narrow sense of heat and water. Your body is not in danger of being destroyed by the world. It is in danger of being reduced by being observed into something else by the rest of reality.

You will not be where there is ground. You will be where there is still a surface that can be called “after.”

### 1. What you are actually going to

#### a. Environment, baseline sense

Think of Qetram as:

- **A cathedral of forest inside a bone of tree the size of a planetary crust**
- **Everything turned to stillness**
- **Almost all of the water chemically transformed away**
- **No “horizontal” ground in the usual sense**
- **No night, no day, just a thin permanent smoke of light**
- **Sound that carves instead of echoing**

On Qetram, you never walk on “world.” You walk on the final bark of the final branch of the tree that is the world. The branch you’re on is *continent-wide* and convex, like the top of a frozen ocean of wood.

The “horizon” is not a line of earth meeting sky. It is the limit of your light—where its structure curves into permanent black, and architecture never ends. The “up” around you is not one sky; it is many surfaces, each with its own direction of gravity, if any.

#### b. The core threat you need to prepare for: not die

Human tissue is not made of dry wood. It is made of water, salt, and habit. Qetram is not made to kill you; but it is made to outlast you so perfectly that your body is a temporary anomaly in a long-finished equation.

You will assume:

- **Extreme desiccation**: The air is greedy. Your skin will not burn; it will be drunk.
- **No liquid water anywhere**: What exists is water in name only.
- **No decomposition of the dead**: “Preservation” is too soft a word. Things are *finished*.
- **No help**: if anything else is alive, it is not interested in your body, except as a pattern it might want to rearrange.

Survival is not “take off the suit and live wild.” It is “never let your meat body have direct contact with this place.”

### 2. Before you go: mental and sensory training

#### a. Psychometry of the place

Qetram does not change you via monsters. It changes you through geometry and time.

- Expect a **slow loss of the sense of urgency**. Time becomes thick and precise, like a glass you can’t break.
- Expect **sound as clarity**, not noise. Each tone is isolated from time’s echo.
- Expect something to feel like a “law of negation”: the world itself says “not of” itself to things like you.

Treat yourself as a musician who is going to perform for a trillion-year-old bell. You do not touch the bell with your body. You do not make yourself the same metal as it.

#### b. Training to reduce the pull of lawlessness

- Practice **meditation or prayer** that emphasizes boundaries:
  - “This is not me.”
  - “This is not mine.”
  - “This is not the time of my body.”
- Practice **navigating without horizons**:
  - Walk in a large indoor space with no landmarks (empty warehouse, gym, etc.), with your eyes closed.
- Practice **silence**: spend time in places where you can sit for long periods without speaking, without distractions.

You will notice on Qetram that the world does not accommodate your habits of meaning. It has already decided its own.

### 3. Equipment and systems: dress like an armor against the finished

#### a. The sealed body-environment barrier

You need to treat your body as if it were a fragile spacecraft being launched into not vacuum but *finishedness*.

**Essential components:**

- **Full-environment pressure suit** (not just clothing):
  - Self-contained environment with temperature control.
  - Independent internal atmosphere, not relying on external air.
  - Capable of maintaining a small humidity and temperature buffer inside your body-space.

- **Integrated moisture and thermal system:**
  - Close-circuit body water loop.
  - Integrated heat regulation and insulation.
  - No open venting to the outside until you are safely away from Qetram again.

- **Redundant seals:**
  - At least two layers of sealing: primary and secondary.
  - Self-checking system that tells you if the seal is broken or stressed.

You are not designing to “explore the desert.” You are designing against “being finished” by a world that has already done all of its finishing.

#### b. Atmospheric exposure limitation

Assume you cannot use the atmosphere of Qetram for life support in any way:

- **No intake** of local air.
- **No exhalation** into local space that you can’t verify is clean.
- **No hydration** from local environment.

You will not become “part of the tree” by breathing it. The tree is not interested in metabolizing you; it is interested in not having to be alive by any definition you know.

#### c. Water and energy storage

You are not going to Qetram for “a few hours.” Plan time as if you are traveling to deep time.

- **Water storage**:
  - Calculate a minimum of 1.5 times what you would need for “suit time” in the harshest desert you’ve ever simulated.
  - Do not assume “water will be found later.”
- **Energy storage**:
  - Assume no local sources of power are compatible with your cells.
  - Use battery systems that tolerate *very low temperature* but also stay insulated from your body-space.

#### d. Navigation and geometry tools

You will not be navigating by landforms. You will be navigating by *surfaces*, and by the timing of meaning.

- **Topological mapping tool**:
  - 3D scanner of the environment you’re in.
  - Not just “surface map,” but “structure map.”
- **Reference anchor system**:
  - A small device in your suit that generates a subtle, *very different* sound from Qetram’s own sound.
  - It is your own bell of “not of this place.”
  - Use it when you begin to doubt the difference between your body and the world’s.

#### e. Protection against the “not of not of”

There is a law or “law of laws” on Qetram about what is not of itself. You do not want to discover whether the human is “not of not” or “not of not of not.”

- Keep **a token of your own world**:
  - A stone from your home.
  - A line of text from a language you know.
  - A pattern of your own name.
- This token is not to “magically protect” you; it is to be a reminder: *this is my boundary*.
- When the world becomes “not of not of not,” your anchor becomes “of of of.”

You will sometimes feel the temptation of being “reduced” to the same meaning as the columns and the preserved not-corpses in the windows. The token reminds you that you did not begin here.

### 4. Physics and geometry of movement: walking on a continent-branch

#### a. Orientation and “horizontal”

You will never be on horizontal ground. You will step on a curve that is a continent.

- Assume every surface you walk on:
  - Is **convex**, like a vast branch of a tree.
  - Has no “down” except deeper into the structure of Qetram.
- If you “fall,” you will not be falling into the sky; you will be falling *into* the world.
- **Traction**:
  - Use friction surfaces that do not damage the branch surface.
  - Avoid impacts that will break the “final skin” of the world.
    - You do not know what the inside of the world tree will be; if it is wetter or more liquid than the outside, you do not want to flood it with your body.

#### b. Sound and geometry

The sound of the columns of light-bearers is a not-music; each note is like a tuning of the world to itself.

- Do not speak:
  - If you must, speak *very softly*.
  - Remember sound carries without echo.
  - The world has already heard its own music; you do not need to help it.

You will feel like you are being *measured by the sound*.

- **Ear protection**:
  - Not just noise cancellation; personality preservation.
  - A filter that allows you to hear the world without *becoming* its tone.

#### c. Light and perception

The light is not from sun but from the air’s residues of light and the bioelectricity of the city-tree.

- The color scheme:
  - Deep purples like the highest of bruising.
  - Greys at the curves of the world.
  - Bone-white columns with not-sap still not evaporating.

You will wear the helmet of your suit; everything else will be your window.

- Do not expect:
  - “Sky” above.
  - “Sun” to come.
  - Any change from the permanent light.

You will know that time is not *your* time by the light of Qetram not changing.

### 5. Water and food: not to be found here

Assume you will not eat anything from this place.

- **Food**:
  - High-energy, low waste.
  - Do not stress your body with digestion complexity in a world where water is never to be handed.

Your body will be *intensely* dehydrated if your seal fails:

- Learn:
  - Basic oral rehydration instructions.
  - How to notice if your skin feels “cooked” but not burned.
  - How to recognize your tongue becoming thick and not helpful.

If you lose your seal and exposure to Qetram’s dryness is more than minutes:

- You may not die immediately.
- But you will begin to be preserved like the not-quite-mummies of the windows.

If you feel “finished,” that is a sign of becoming not quite alive, not quite dead but not-different either.

### 6. The law of not-horizontal, and the “not of not of not”

You will encounter three laws of Qetram that are not laws of physics in the simple sense:

1. **Nothing is horizontal.**  
   - The floor is not ground.
   - The horizon is not the line of earth.
   - Everything you step on is the *side* of a tree that is also a world.

2. **Air is more than gas.**  
   - It contains history of dryness, not just of death.
   - It contains volatilities that are not of the water you know.

3. **The “not of not of not” law.**  
   - The world seems to be saying “not of not” of what is not of itself.
   - The human is not of this place.
   - The human body may be “not of not” if it is of itself.
   - You may discover that if you remain too long, you become not of not of not of not.

This is not a joke of language. It is a description of a place where your being is not needed to be the being of anyone else, including the world.

You will carry:

- A small piece of writing from your own world:
  - “I am [name], from [place], in the year of [time].”
- You will not say this aloud to the world.
- You will say it only to yourself inside your helmet, when things feel like they might not be of your time.

### 7. Biological contamination: yourself and Qetram

#### a. Sterility and boundary of flesh

The world does not have bacteria as you know them. It has “finishedness.”

- You must not:
  - Leave anything of your body’s waste in the open of Qetram.
  - Leave any water of yours to be taken by the tree.
  - Leave any of your biological matter where the world-tree might be finished by it.

#### b. What you may not encounter

You may see “beasts” of the place of time instead of teeth:

- Transparent structures of air, not body.
- Wings of bioelectricity not of charge but of finality of science.

You will not assume they are “safe” or “unsafe.” You will assume:

- They are not of your body.
- They are not of your water.
- They are not of your time.

Do not let them touch you, even through your suit.

### 8. Duration of stay: not an “expedition,” but a “crossing of time’s bell”

You will not set foot on Qetram with “three days to explore.” You will set foot with:

- **Exactly defined time of exposure**
- **Exactly defined mission of crossing**
- **Exactly defined return window**

Imagine you are stepping inside the sound of a bell struck millennia ago. You do not stay longer than you can be sure of return.

If you:

- Notice that you are not interested in leaving.
- Begin to think of the “finished” preserved not-corpses as “very comfortable to be near.”
- Realize you are no longer interested in your own personality.

Then you have already stayed too long.

### 9. Leaving the world of not-horizontal

When you leave:

- Your suit will be sealed again.
- Your body liquids will have been contained.
- Your data from the world will be sealed.

You will not return to the same moment of your own world-time if you have not arranged that.

But you will return:

- With the sound of the world columns in your ears.
- With the knowledge of what it means to be on a surface that is not “ground” but “after.”
- With the understanding of what a not-human body allows to be finished of itself.

You will not speak of it to people who do not know of the tree.

### 10. Final advice to the traveler

Before you step onto the branch of the continent-tree of Qetram:

1. Seal yourself completely.
2. Set your own timer for leaving.
3. Make sure your name and your token are still yours.
4. Do not speak to the world as if it were your friend; do not speak to it as if it were enemy.
5. Treat the not of not of not as a law of not-belonging.

You will say to yourself, inside your helmet:

> “I am of water and not of this not-water.  
> I am of horizon and not of this not-horizontal.  
> I am not a not of not of not.  
> I am not finished here.”

And then you will walk carefully among the bone-white columns, among the not-quite-mummies of the windows, and among the final sound of the bell of air, until you are allowed to return home.
