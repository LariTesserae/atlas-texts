Traveling to the Under-Realm is not like visiting another country. It is closer to visiting a deep memory in the mind of the world. You will be a guest in a self that is not yours, and you must be very careful with what you touch.

If you can choose at all whether to go, then your first and best preparation is to decide not to.

If you must go anyway, here is how an ordinary human traveler should prepare.

---

## 1. Prepare yourself to be changed by what you see

This is not a tourist cave.

What you will encounter:

*   **Extreme darkness:** It is not “night.” It is the absence of sky. Your eyes will not be enough to make sense of it.
*   **Sound that will not stop:** Running water, distant rockfalls, the slow groaning of stone, your own heartbeat echoing. Quiet your mind *before* you go, or it will use the noise against you.
*   **The ceiling:** “It is not broken. It is not whole.” You might get a sense of vast, hovering weight that will not let you stand upright in your thoughts. This is not just a feeling: the ceiling *is not normal*.
*   **The stone that “remembers every footstep”:** This is not a metaphor. It is a rule of that place. The stone holds you; you leave marks on it; it may leave marks on you.
*   **The River People and their silence:** They are “the People of the River” and “residents of the river.” They walk in silence. To be loud, to be noisy with your body or your mind, is to break an etiquette you might not survive breaking.

If you cannot be at least somewhat comfortable with being *small*, *watched*, and *not in charge*, you should not go.

## 2. Physical and practical gear

### Lighting and navigation

You cannot rely on “enough light to see” down there. Your eyes must be treated as unreliable instruments.

Pack:

*   **Three headlamps per person:**  
    - One main headlamp with strong long beam  
    - One secondary headlamp for redundancy  
    - One small LED headlamp or clip light as a minimum backup  
*   **Extra batteries** stored in waterproof containers, twice what you think you will need.
*   A **simple flashlight** as a fourth source.
*   **Chemical glow sticks** (not as primary light, but as markers along your route back).
*   **Marking tape** or chalk to mark your route in stone, but only *if you can feel it is allowed* where you are. If the stone feels “close” or “aware,” you do not scribble graffiti on it.

### Footwear and clothing

*   **Sturdy, waterproof boots:** You will be crossing wet rock, possibly walking through shallow water, mud, and unstable surfaces. The stone will be slippery.
*   **Thick socks**, preferably wool, to keep feet warm and reduce blisters.
*   **Layerable clothing:**  
    - A base layer that wicks moisture  
    - Insulation (fleece or similar synthetic)  
    - A waterproof, windproof shell  
    The temperature may be surprisingly cold, especially where “the condensation weeps from the walls and the slow tears gather in the dark.”
*   **Change of clothes** sealed in waterproof bags. You might be soaked by river spray or unexpected rain from the ceiling.
*   **Gloves** for handling cold, wet, sharp stone.

### Protection from water and stone

*   **Helmet:** A climbing helmet or caving helmet. You will encounter sharp ceilings and low arches.
*   **Knee pads and elbow pads**, if you are going to move along narrow or uneven surfaces near the water.
*   **Life vest or buoyancy aid:** If there are any canoe crossings, “rivers of water” or lakes in the Under-Realm, you should be able to float.
*   **Wetsuit or drysuit** if you expect prolonged contact with cold water.

### Safety and survival tools

*   **Emergency blanket:** Thin, reflective, and surprisingly warm if trapped or waiting for help.
*   **Basic first aid kit:** Bandages, antiseptics, pain medication, waterproof medical tape, and any medications you personally require.
*   **Small, fixed-blade knife:** For gear repairs, cutting cordage, and last-resort safety.
*   **Paracord or rope (strong, lightweight):**
    - For marking safe paths  
    - To use as a handrail in short dangerous sections *only if you’re trained and very sure it is safe*
*   **Whistle** for communication, but you *use it sparingly*. Noise in the Under-Realm is not taken lightly.
*   **Simple signal device** if technology works in your multiverse setup: a beacon, tracker, or flare compatible with your travel method.

### Food and water

*   **More water than you think you need:**  
    This is critical. The river might look drinkable, but downstream water in the Under-Realm might contain “rivers of blood, rivers of sound, rivers of shadow.” Do not drink from any river unless you have tested it with purification tools.
*   **Water purification methods:**  
    - Filter (if you can trust the matter), or tablets  
    - Or preferably: sealed water bottles you bring from your own world.
*   **Compact, high-calorie food:**  
    Nuts, energy bars, chocolate, jerky, etc.  
    *Nothing that requires a cooking fire.* Fire in the deep places of the world is not trivial.
    If you anticipate very long stays, pack **ration-grade survival meals**.

### Environmental protection

*   **Dust mask or respirator:** In case of dust from ancient stone or spores from unseen growths. “The stone of the Under-Realm learned to eat itself”—this is not a metaphor for harmless erosion.
*   **Simple climbing gear:** Gloves, helmet, harness, carabiners if you are trained and there are safety anchors that *your guides say are trustworthy*.
*   **Basic chem/bio hazard detection:**  
    If you have access to multiversal tech, a small device or set of indicators that can detect heavy gases, low oxygen, or strange anomalies. “Rivers of air” might not be mixed the way you expect.

## 3. Mental and spiritual preparation

This is where the real danger lies.

The Under-Realm is “a place of stone and water and sky and blood and it is always.” This sentence points to several layers of reality stacked at once. You will be inside something that does not match your intuitions.

### Practice meditation and silence

Before you go, spend hours each day simply:

*   Sitting in silence
*   Watching your breath without commentary
*   Walking without speaking
*   Being *physically still*

You will need to be able to control your fear without relying on noise or conversation.

### Stabilize your sense of self

Before you travel:

*   Write down what you know of yourself:
    - Name
    - Date and place of birth
    - Names of people you love
    - One core promise you
