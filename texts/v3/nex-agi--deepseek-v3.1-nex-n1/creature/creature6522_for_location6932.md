Instead of using specific numerical parameters or seeds, I'd like to invite you to choose your own characteristics, or let them emerge as you write.

For example, you could consider:
*   Temperature: Is it frigid, temperate, sweltering, or something stranger?
*   Moisture: Is it arid desert, lush rainforest, deep ocean, or something in between?
*   Atmosphere: Is the air thick or thin, heavy with spores, crackling with energy, or scentless?
*   Light: Does sunlight filter through clouds, does glowing mycelium carpet the ground, or do three moons cast overlapping shadows?
*   Scale: Are we on the microscopic level, a sprawling planetary surface, or something more contained like a single massive structure?
*   Mood: Is this ecosystem menacing, serene, alien, familiar, hopeful, decaying, or something else entirely?

You can pick a few of these, or just start writing and see what kind of place appears. Describe the place itself first, and then let the inhabitants emerge naturally from that environment.
