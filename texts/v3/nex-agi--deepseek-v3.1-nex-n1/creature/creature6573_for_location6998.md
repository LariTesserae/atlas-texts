Amphisov, the Liminal Circuit  

In the deep fluid of Vrth’s valve‑bay, where currents move like sentences and light sieves itself through layers of machine and flesh, the amphisov do not so much “swim” as they “change their mind about direction.”

---

## Name and Meaning

**Amphisov** is what the maintenance AI logs call them—when it logs them at all.  
**Common alias:** *Circuit‑soles*, *two‑headed tongues*, *threshold whisperers.*

“Amphisov” is cobbled from old technical roots:  
- ***amphis‑*** — “both sides,”  
- ***sov‑*** — a clipping of *sovat’*, an archaic term for “to thread,”  
- “s‑o‑v‑” itself once an acronym in some engineering dialect: *Seal‑off‑Verification*.

They are walking **or‑gates**, biological decision nodes that live between the machine and the Seam, never quite fully of either.

---

## Shape and Size

Imagine an elongated **figure‑8** of living glass, flattened as if pressed between two plates of steel.

**Length:** Two to three meters, end‑to‑end.  
**Width:** Half a meter at the widest point.  
**Thickness:** A palm’s breadth at the center; tapering to knife‑edges at the rim.

The body is not streamlined but **plate‑like**, smooth but not simple. Its outline is defined by two unequal loops:

- **Machine head** — slightly larger, faintly metallic, where internal organs gleam with engineered alloys and copper‑like lattices.  
- **Seam head** — smaller, softer, translucent where tissue merges invisibly into the dark‑blue veins of the nearest wall‑Seam.

When viewed side-on against the worklights, the amphisov seems like a **hinged blade**, suspended forever mid‑turn.

---

## External Surface

There is no skin in the dry sense. There *is*:

- **A membrane** like clear silicone oil, stretched taut over the internal frame.  
- **Subsurface tessellations** — repeating hexagonal patterns of fat‑like organelles that give the body a faintly cloudy lattice beneath the shine.  
- **No scales** and **no fins**, but the **edges** are lined with fine **fibrils**, thinner than spider silk and almost invisible: they extend and retract like the cilia of a single vast organism—the amphisov’s version of fingers.

To the touch—if you were close enough to be brushed by one—the surface would register as **cool glass** crossed with electrodes: faint sting of static, then soft, yielding pressure as the fibril field adjusts itself around you.

---

## Movement

You’ve never seen an animal fly like a child’s drawing of an airplane and **also** like a diagram of a data flow, but amphisov do.

### In the fluid

- They **bank** rather than swim.  
- By flexing their body’s internal lattice, they can **alter phase** locally—where “phase” is not about water and ice, but about **set of rules**.  
- To your eyes, one bank can be “more fluid” in behavior (flowing around obstacles), and the next “more machine” (moving along precise lines of sight between sensor cabinets like a rail carriage).

Technically, they tweak the ions of the hydraulic medium around them, causing the **local fluid to become a track**, a temporary rail of influenced pressure and polarity.

Result:

- They **drift** in wide curves when idle,  
- **Snap** along precise, straight lines when they switch to “machine logic,”  
- **Glide parallel** to Seams when tied to biological phase.

## Internal Structure

Cut open (figuratively; you *never* cut into anything alive in Vrth if you want your equipment intact), an amphisov reveals **three** dominant layers, stacked like a three‑layer motherboard:

1. **Nerve‑fiber weave**
   - Delicate web of silver‑black nerves, meshed like a lace of conductive threads.
   - Supports **lightning‑thinking** — fast decisions, pattern‑sensing in machine data.

2. **Seam‑connector roz**
   - “Roz” were once listed on AI construction diagrams as “Remote‑Oriented Zone” plates.
   - Thick, dark blue band like a **strip of Seam** itself, running the length of the body.
   - Filled with **liquid‑crystal channels** of bioluminescent fluid.
   - When the amphisov docks near a Seam, this layer **unseals** along a slit and extrudes a **connector mat** that harmonizes with the wall’s biological circuitry.

3. **Structural chassis**
   - A lattice of bones or bones‑like struts, **impregnated with metals**.
   - Gives the body rigidity but allows flex in the plane of the figure‑8.
   - In cross section it looks like: **two semi‑circles**, facing the world, and between them a meshwork hung with **soft glands** — call them “turbine‑organs” that compress the hydraulic fluid internally to generate small pressure differences.

Amphisov have **no mouths** and **no digestive tract** as such.  
They **feed** by **catching events**.

## “Feeding”: Consuming Decisions

This is where the amphisov stop resembling anything you could cleanly call “animal.”

They **swallow** the **difference‑of‑potential**.

When a machine sensor and a Seam segment disagree:

- A **tracer cloud** gathers,
- A **Censor** glides in,
- A **pressure discrepancy** appears along a weld line,

the **voltage difference**, **ion imbalance**, and **logic conflict** together form a **tension-field**.

The amphisov detects this tension **magnetically** and **chemically**, through:

- Magnetic sensors along the rim (like a diffuse **Ampullae of Lorenzini**),
- Iono‑chem receptors in their **roz** layer.

They **align** with the conflict zone:

- The **machine head** points at the sensors,
- The **Seam head** orients toward the Seam,
- Their body arches **to complete** the arc between.

Then they **snap**.

Not physically—but in **conduction**.  
The **local difference** is discharged through **them**, as:

- A short **flash of light** along their surface,  
- A **click of static** you feel through the fluid,  
- A **micro‑current** drawn through their nerve‑web.

In that moment, **they are the answer**.

So **feeding** is not about matter;  
feeding is **becoming the bridge that a decision crosses.**

Each such “fusion” leaves **growth** behind:

- A new knot of nerve fibers in the web,
- A new segment of crystal channel in the roz,
- A faint line of **metallic stain** along the outer edge, like electrochemical barnacles.

Over years, they **accumulate** decisions, **layer** after **layer**,  
and their **body** becomes **a record of conflicts resolved.**

## Relationship to Seams and Censors

### With Seams

- **Resting**: Amphisov often **hover** over a Seam line, not quite touching,  
  - One edge directly **mirroring** the contour of the blue line beneath,
  - Their **roz** layer faintly glowing in sync with the Seam’s slow heartbeat.
- **Connected**:  
  - In a serious **conflict** or **problem tide**,  
  - The **Seam head** extrudes its connector mat and  
  - **couples** with the Seam,  
  - **fusing** geometries for a time.

When the **Seam lights** roll down the wall in a SMELL‑tide, **coupled amphisov** light with **sharper**, **more precise** lines—  
like the edges of the blueprint tightened.

### With Censors

Amphisov and **Censors** **observe** each other:

- **Censors** drift **above** the amphisov when  
  - They are whispering their anomaly signals,
  - **Patterns** of **slow** color chases move along their ventral rings.
- **Amphisov** **echo** these patterns:
  - Their **edges** replicate **small** bursts of the same sequence,
  - **Accelerated** and **compressed** in time,  
    – where a censor spends a minute, the amphisov spends **five seconds**.

To **you** it might look like a **quiet** conversation  
—  
**firefly** atop  
**mathematical knife**.
