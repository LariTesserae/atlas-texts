The Water-Cathedral of Genna’s Spire thrums with a sound like rain on a deep pool—a steady, breathy rhythm. It is the breath of the ocean itself, rising through the stone throat of the spire and echoing into the hollow chamber above. But the breath is not empty. It carries with it a quiet **city** of life.

This is not the riot of a tropical reef or the teeming confusion of a jungle floor. The **fauna** here are **sparse** but **present**, woven into the **moss** and **stone** with the same careful **economy** as everything else in the chamber. Each creature is a specialist of the **vertical** world, a **high** -place dweller made to fit this narrow, **submerged** **cathedral**.

### The Swimmers

Up the center, where the **helix** of water curls itself into the **light**, **schools** of **small** fish drift like music.

They are **silver**, mostly, and **long** -bodied, with tails that taper into fine threads. These are **current-** and **moss-** specialists, their scales **pale** and almost translucent, like **water** given form. They **ride** the **vertical** river of **saltwater**, moving up and down the column of the spire like **shuttles** on a **loom**, never truly leaving it.

When they **feed**, they **filter** the **slow** flow of **water** with **fine** gills, taking from it **tiny** plant-spores, **microscopic** algae, and the **dust** of **moss** fragments that **rain** from the **walls**. You rarely see them **dash** or **bolt**; their motion is **smooth**, **patient**, and **flowing**, matching the **speed** of the **current** itself. They are **art** made **flesh**, **pure** adaptations of **line** and **light** to the **slow** storm of the **Water-**Cathedral.

Below them, working **deeper** into the **submerged** shaft, you might glimpse **lighter** shapes. **Eel-**like creatures but **not** eels—shorter, **rounder**, **thicker**. Their bodies are **marked** with **stripe** and **spot** patterns that **fade** as they move from **shadow** to **light**. They are **bottom-** and **crevice-** hunters, **rare** and **solitary**, moving through **gaps** in the **moss** and **along** the **glass** -smooth **stone** like **quiet** thoughts.

They are **not** **scary**, **not** truly predators of the larger **fauna** here; their prey are **small** things, **shell**ed creatures that **cling** to the **underside** of **moss**-**shelves**, and **worm-**like **forms** that **tunnel** through the **moss**-**walls**.

### The Wall-Makers

The **true** **builders** of this **moss**-**city** are not the **fish** but the **walkers**.

On **every** **surface** where **moss** and **stone** meet **air**, you **see** the **climbers**. They are **small**—**hand-size** or **less**—and **dark** against the **green** of the **moss**. Their bodies are **compact** but **strong**-built, with **limbs** like **climbing** claws.

**Shell-** and **bone-** are **their** **tools**. Their bodies are **covered** in a **natural** **armor** of **limestone** and **chitin**, **fused** and **grown** over **time** into **plates**. You can see the **lines** of **growth** on them, like **tree-** rings, **mark**ing **years** spent **slow**ly **moving** up and down the **cathedral**-**walls**.

These are the **architects**.

With **pre-** metal **blades** of **sharp** shell and **stone**-**chips** held in **claw**-**hands** and **mouth-**parts, they **cut**, **trim**, and **train** the **moss**-**carpets**. They **clear** **runoff**-**channels** so **water** **doesn**’t **rot** the **roots**; they **lift** and **layer** **moss**-**mat** into **terrace** **steps**, forming **neighborhoods** of **dwelling**-**platforms**.

Their **work** is **uniform**, **patterned**, **regular**—but **not** **machine-**like. Each **terrace** **shows** the **hand** of its **maker**. You can see where **one** **climber** **preferred** **gentle**-**curves** and **rounded**-**edges**, and **another** liked **straight**-**lines** and **blocky**-**shapes**.

They **live** in **shallow**-**rooms** **cut** into the **thick**-**moss**-**walls**, **tiny**-**doorways** that **open** onto **ledges**. You may see **them** **come** and **go**, **quiet**-**moving** forms **scuttling** from **terrace** to **terrace**, **always** **in** **contact** with **stone** or **plant**-**mass**.

Their **eyes** are **large**, **dark**, and **watchful**, **taking** in **light** from **all** **angles**. They **know** the **echo** of **footfall** on **stone**, **every** **change** in the **hum** of the **water**-**column**. To **them** this **submerged** **high**-**place** is **not** a **wilderness** but a **home** they **actively** **shape**.

### The Air-Dancers

Between the **water**-**helix** and the **stone**-**walls**, the **air** itself **is** **not** empty. It is **crossed** by **thin**-**trails** of **insects**.

Most **common** are **wing**-**dancers**, **translucent**-**winged** **midges** of **pale**-**gray** and **silver**. They **swarm** in **gentle**-**clouds**, **fold**ing and **unfold**ing themselves **near** the **light** of the **sky**-**well**. Their **wings** **catch** the **kelp-**green **light**, and for **one** **moment** they are **stars** in **day**-**time**.

They **feed** on **moss**-**spores** that **dust** the **surface** of the **water**-**column**, **hover**ing **just**-**above** the **slow**-**current**. You **see** **countless** of them, **constant** in **number** but **sparse**-**seeming** because they **spread** themselves **thin** across the **volume** of the **chamber**.

Larger **forms** are **rare** and **deliberate**. A **hand**-**length**-**long** **dragon**-**fly**-**like** creature, **body** **deep**-**blue** with **bands** of **white**-**green**, **cruises** the **walls** in **long**-**slow**-**glides**, **occasionally** **snatching** a **midge** or **two** from the **air** with **quiet** **precision**. Its wings **hum** with a **deep**-**note** that **almost** **matches** the **sound** of the **water**, **blending** into the **cathedral**-**song.**

### The Quiet Ones

The **most** **unusual** **thing** about the **fauna** of **Genna**’s **Spire** is **how** **little** **weirdness** they **possess**.

There are **no** **creatures** with a **dozen**-**eyes** or **floating**-**heads** or **flesh** made **of** **light**. The **life** here **feels** **recognizable**, even if **exactly**-**like**-**nothing**-**else**.

**Slender**-**bodied** **snakes**-**of-**water**, **glossy** and **black**, **move** through the **edges** of the **central**-**column** where **current** **weakens**. They **are** **not** **aggressive**. They **eat** **small**-**fish** and **nothing**-**else**, and **you** can **watch** them **for** **hours** without **ever** seeing them **strike** **quick**ly enough to **register**.

**On** the **lower**-**ledges**, **just**-**where** **stone** **meets** **water**, **pale**-**crabs** with **long**-**legs** **like** **spiders** **skitter** **sideways** across **wet**-**rock**, **picking** **moss**-**scraps** and **stranded**-**insects** from the **surface** with **fine**-**claws**.

Higher **up**, **where** **wind** **scours** the **summit**, you might **see** **birds**-**but**-**not**-**birds**.

They are **small**, **compact** **flyers**, **covered** in **fine**-**feather**-**like** **scales** that **re** -mind you of **both** **fish**-**skin** and **bird**-**plumage**. Their wings are **short**, **broad**-**and**-**strong** for **managing** the **up**-**and-**down**-**draughts** of **wind** that **howl** through **the** **notch**-**above**.

They **nest** in **deep**-**crevices** of the **stone**-**walls**, **high**-**where** **moss**-**growth** is **thinner**. Their **calls** are **sharp**, **piping**-**notes** that **slice** through the **low**-**rumble** of the **water**-**helix**, **marking** **boundaries** and **mates**.

### The Symbionts

Perhaps **one** **species** **bears** mentioning **separately**, because **without** it **none** of this **would** **work**.

Deep **within** the **moss**-**city**, **hidden** beneath **every** **carpet**, moves **a** **kind** of **tiny**-**slug**-**like** **creature**.

It is **soft**-**bodied**, **no**-**longer** than **your** **finger**, and **almost**-**colorless** except for **streaks** of **green** and **gold** that **run** along **its** **back**. These **streaks** are **not** **its** **own** **color** but **living** **algae** and **moss**-**thread**s **trapped** in **special**-**pores** in its **skin**.

The **slug**-**creatures** **eat** **dead**-**plant**-**matter**, **ex**-**creting** it **back** as **rich**‑nutrient‑paste **perfect** for **new** moss‑growth. They **are** the **hidden** **engineers** of the **soil**‑world inside **this** **stone**‑throat—**quiet**, **constant** recyclers that **transform** **everything** **dead** here **into** **something** **living**.

You **almost** **never** see them **direct**ly. **Only** when **one** **climber**-**builder** lifts **a** **section** of **moss**-**mat** do **you** glimpse **their** **pale**-**forms** **dart**ing **deeper** into the **green**.

### The Whole

The **fauna** of **Genna**’s **Spire** are **like** the **place** itself: **vertical**, **submerged**, **patient**, **established**.

They **do** **not** **overwhelm**.

The **cathedral**-**feel** comes as **much** from **what** is **absent** as **what** is **present**: **from** **the** **empty**-**spaces** between **each**-**swarm**-**of**-**midges**, **from** **the** **quiet**-**of**-**the**-**eels** in the **deep**-**shadows**, **from** **the** **slow**-**glides** of **the**-**blue**-**wing**-**hunter** above **and** **the** **steady**-**work** of **the**-**climbers** below.

You **can** stand on the **stone**-**ledge** that **rings** the **wall** and **watch** **them** all **at** **once**: 

- **fish**-**shuttles** riding **the** **vertical**-**river**  
- **climber**-**builders** **trim**ming their **moss**-**terraces**  
- **midge**-**clouds** swirling in **kelp**-**light**  
- **pale**‑crabs **skitter**ing along **wet**‑stone edges  
- **snakes**‑of‑water **moving** like **slow**‑liquid shadows  
- **bird**‑but‑not‑bird **call**‑marks **high** in the **wind**‑notch  

And **all** of it **together** feels **less** like “wildlife” and **more** like **one** **single**, **breathing** **organism**—**a** **high**‑city **woven** from **stone**, **water**, **vegetation**, and **quiet**, **patient** **animal**‑life.

This **is** **who** **lives** here.
