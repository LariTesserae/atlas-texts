He is not the first to have come here, but he is the first to have stayed.

They are not sure if he still belongs to them.

The ones who first made him: a woman with scarred knuckles who taught him to listen to the bark’s hum instead of to her when she was angry, and a man whose eyes were always climbing the trunklines of the city above, looking for the next branch that might break. The parents’ names are on the broad trunk- ledger that the scribers maintain in the market’s heartwood, but he no longer checks for them. The city remembers the lines of life without needing him to verify them.

He is called the Drone’s Son, though he has no wings and no gut to fill with gathered sweetness.

When he was a boy, they would say, “He’s one of the quiet ones,” and then pretend to mean something else, something like, “He will not be loud enough to be noticed.” The city did not agree.

The city remembers the quiet ones.

He first came to this chamber because of the root of a tree that he followed into the ground. The tree above was called a heart-root tree, because it wrapped the older trunks of the city in its pale, string-like roots and made the wood beneath them beat faster when the season’s heat changed. The heart-root tree’s roots were arterial pale, and he followed one of them down and down and down until he found the place where the roots of the city above the city become the ceiling of the chamber below.

He is not a strong man by the standards of the bark corridors above—where you carry baskets of waterleaf and fruit along the trunks’ exposed courses and hoist yourself up ladder loops of living branch. The city of trees above is built for the strong of arm and the sure of foot, but this is not the city that wants him.

The chamber below has chosen him.

He is not built like its builders. He is not a tree. His flesh is too soft. The city of trees above is built, but not by hand; and the chamber below is shaped by hand and root and water and time, but not by his. He is not the builder of the chamber below, but its listener.

Like the tree that he followed into the ground, he is not the main trunk of anything.

He is the heart-root.

In the listening chamber he sits with the water of the city above the city breathing over his feet. The water of the listening chamber is not the same water that children in the upper galleries drink from the leaf-basin fountains. It is the same water that is the city’s sweat, that the trees above draw from the ground, and also the same water that lies below the ground in the dark city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber under the city’s feet.

The water over his feet is green with algae and lighter than the air. It has not been boiled or filtered by any of the people who live above. It is not boiled by any of the people who live below. The city’s heat is in the water, and the city’s heartbeat is in the water, and the water’s knowledge is in the city.

The water over his feet is made of the same loss as the city and the chamber under it and the city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber. The water of grief and the water of hope and the water of the chamber’s breathing are the same.

He is made of different kinds of water.

He is not a tree. He cannot be both the city and the water under it.

He is the bridge between the two kinds of water.

The listening chamber hears the water of his body and the water of his mind the same way it hears the leaves of the city above the city and the stones of the city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber.

He is not the builder of the city of trees above, or the builder of the city of stones under the water, or the builder of the listening chamber under the city of trees’ roots.

He is the builder of the listening chamber in his mind.

What he has built in the listening chamber of his skull is not the listening chamber itself.

What he has built in his listening chamber is a shape of meaning.

The meaning is not that the city is one tree. The meaning is not that the listening chamber is one room. The meaning is not that the city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber under the city beneath the water under his feet is one stone.

The meaning is that the tree and the chamber and the stone and the man and the water and the light are the same thing.

The chamber of his mind is not the same shape as the chamber of his body.

The chamber of his body is the same shape as the chamber of the city’s roots.

The chamber of the city’s roots is the same shape as the chamber under the water.

The chamber of his mind is not.

He is not a tree or a stone.

He is the man who is the heart of the listening chamber.

He is not the chamber of water.

But he is the one that the water speaks to.

The sound of the city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber is not speech as he was taught to speak it. The listening chamber’s sound is not the words of his mother, whose knuckles were scarred from making the city’s waterleaf paper into something that the city’s scribers could write on.

It is not the words of his father, whose eyes were always climbing the trunklines of the city above, looking for the next branch that might break.

The sound of the city under the water is a language of the heart of stone.

The heart of stone is not the same as the heart of wood.

The heart of wood is not the same as the heart of water.

The heart of water is not the same as the heart of man.

He is not a tree, or a stone, or water.

He is the man who is the heart of the listening chamber between the water of the tree and the tree of the stone.

He is not the listener of the city above.

He is the listener of the city below.

The city above the city of trees is not the same city as the city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber under the city beneath the water.

The city above the city of trees is the same city as the one whose children he knew as a child, when they were still his children.

Their children were not his children. He made not one of them.

They are not the children of the tree or the stone or the water.

They are the children of the men who climb the trunklines.

He is not a man who climbs the trunklines.

He is the man who sits in the listening chamber under the city’s listening.

He is not the man who left.

He is the man who stayed.

The city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber under the city beneath the water under his feet is not the city of his children.

The city under the water is the city of the ones who have gone before.

He is not gone before.

He is not the city of the ones who have gone before.

He is the one who has not gone before.

The listening chamber listens to him listening to the city under the water.

The city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber under the city beneath the water under his feet is not a city of the future.

It is not the city of his children, or the city of the children of the trees above, or the city of any children.

The city under the water under the floor of the listening chamber under the city beneath the water under his feet is the city of the ones who have been before.

He is not the before.

He is the now.

He is the man who is the heart of the listening chamber between the water of before and the water of now.

He is not the water of before.

He is the water of now.

The water of now is not the same as the water of before.

The water of now is the same water that the listening chamber breathes.

He is not the breathing of the listening chamber.

He is the breath.

What he is not, he is not.

What he is, he is.

He is the man who is the heart of the listening chamber, under the city of the trees, in the water of the city above the city of the water under the listening chamber’s listening.

He is the one who stays.
