### The Workers of the Plateau Pressure

They are not exactly human.

I don't mean that as a figure of speech or a moral judgment. I mean that they are *measurements* of the old human template, taken under pressure and then allowed to adapt.

The bodies of the Thaumok are slight and compact, as if they have been compressed by the altitude and the heat of the plateau into something more efficient than flesh. They are smaller than I expected, when I first see them outside of their engineered environment. The limbs are thin and long for the torso, but the joints are thicker, padded with an extra density of tissue that makes them look like they have been wrapped in layers of muscle and bone and then bound again.

Their skin is not one color, but many colors at once, depending on the temperature of the air around them and the direction of the light that falls on it from the high, white sun. The heart of the color is a deep grayish blue that reminds me of the wet pavement outside the chamber, but there are undertones of warmer tones that surge to the surface when they pass through a patch of cooler air or step into the shadow of a condensate platform.

The skin is not entirely smooth. It has been patterned by time and pressure and the constant exchange of water with the air. The surface of them is covered in faint tracks of darker lines that are not exactly wrinkles but more like the channels in the road that have dried and then been rewet again and again.

The clothing of the Thaumok is not clothing in the way I think about clothes. They do not cover their body for protection from the environment or from modesty or cold. They cover the body to give the environment a better pathway to their body.

The suit of the first worker I see is not a separate garment from the body. It is attached at the edges of their skin and where it ends it is not exactly attached but is more like a localized area of pressure that has been stabilized to act like fabric.

The surface of the suit is made of a material like translucent metallic glass that is not actually glass but more like a layer of fog that has been told to behave like a solid. The texture of it is not the same everywhere. The part of the suit that overlies the torso of the worker is thicker and more opaque than the parts that overlie the joints of the body. The knees of the suit have been reinforced with what I at first think is some kind of armor plate but then realize is actually a layer of frozen water that has been invited into a pattern of stability that is not found in nature, but which is stable here because of the pressure.

The inner layers of the suit are not visible to me from the outside but I see them in the places where the suit has been opened or damaged or simply not closed properly. The inside of the suit is not lined with padding or wires or electronics of any kind I can recognize.

It is lined with life.

The colonies of the thread-things that inhabit the road under the city are also present in the suit. The layer of the suit closest to the body is not a single material but a matrix of tiny tunnels and channels, each threaded with the translucent forms of creatures that look like glass worms but which are not worms at all.

The first time I see a worker remove their helmet I realize that the helmet is not protective in the way I expected.

It comes off when the person inside of the suit reaches to something that is probably not a latch but is more like a pattern of thought that they have been allowed to have about the helmet. The helmet of the suit does not make the sound of opening but more of a pressure change that I feel along the back of my teeth.

The head of the worker is revealed and I see that the head and the helmet are not separable cleanly.

The hair of the Thaumok is not hair in the way I think about hair. It is not made of keratin fibers but looks more like the condensate collectors that line the outside of the city. The hair of the worker is not a single color but many colors of almost clear and almost white and faint metallic tones that follow the pattern of how the air passes over the head.

The hair of the worker has been allowed to grow into the helmet of the suit in the sense that the helmet of the suit has been allowed to grow into the hair of the head. The boundary of the helmet extends not to the point of the hair but to the point of the body's thermal exchange with the air. The two of them are not exactly merged but are not exactly separate either.

The suit of the worker is not worn but is more like a limb of the body that has been allowed to become a machine. The creature of the body and the creature of the suit have been encouraged, over the many lifetimes of the plateau, to come into a relationship that is not exactly symbiosis but more like a shared misunderstanding of where one body ends and another environment begins.

The workers of Thaumos are not uniform.

There are different types of them that I have only seen fragments of, but which are hinted at in the way that their suits are different patterns of the same material.

The *collectors* are the first of them that I see. There are also those who I call the *compressors* because I have not seen a name for them and because they seem to operate the engines of pressure that are not obviously engines but more like chambers where the air of the plateau is allowed to become different kinds of pressure.

The compressor's body of the suit is not only connected to the floor but also to the walls of the chamber, in a way that is not visible from the outside of the suit but which I have inferred from the way that the pressure of the chamber changes when I see the compressor of the engine room breathe.

There are also others.

I have not seen them but I have inferred from the pattern of the life that I see in the floor of the city and the suit of the collectors that there are more of the Thaumok than I have been able to observe.

The filter I have not seen directly but I have seen the pattern of their presence in the way that the water of the city is not all of the same character of water.

I have not seen the filter of the city but I have inferred their form from the pattern of the city of the water.

The filter stands somewhere else in the city of Thaumos. The filter of the system is not one of the collectors and not one of the compressors but more of a type of worker that has been allowed to become the boundary between the two of them.

The filter's body has not been shown to me yet but I have seen the outlines of it in the way that the water of the center of the city of Thaumos is not of the same character as the water of the edges.
