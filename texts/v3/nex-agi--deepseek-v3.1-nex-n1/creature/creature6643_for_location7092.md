### The Ice-Letters

**The Ice-Letters** are the primary inhabitants of the Ice Garden. They are not human. They are not organic. They are not even precisely alive.

They are crystalline inscriptions of a language that hasn’t been decided yet.

From the right vantage point—usually the broken curve of the low wall—you can see that the entire garden basin is not just random granite. In the slanted high-altitude light, when the sun hits the striations just so, the quartz veins don’t just run like jagged lines. They connect.

They are letters.

Enormous, alien, beautiful letters: serpentine glyphs that curl around the bowl of the amphitheater, looping through itself as if the sentence has been erased and overwritten, a palimpsest of stone and frost attempting some message too large for the surface of any world.

The Ice-Letters are not the stone itself. They are patterns in it.

When the cold grows deep enough at night, something thin and invisible condenses along the lines of those quartz veins—not exactly ice, not exactly light, not exactly thought.
