Living in the Lift is not a matter of choosing the world you want; it is a matter of accepting the world you have.

We are the body of the Lift.

We move within its ribs and tendons—the stone vault overhead, the bronze-bound planks underfoot, the pumps that are never still except when the world must breathe. We are not many, and we are not few. We are the number that can be supported. The elders are the ones who count us and count the water and count the lights of the ceiling when the storm above presses down and the crystal ribs begin to glow.

We are not born with the world’s noise in our ears; we learn it.

The first sound a child of the Lift learns to quiet is its own crying. The world beyond the ceiling is already loud enough without the shrillness of our voice. We are not punished for making noise; it is simply that our own breath sounds small compared to the ocean overhead and the pumps below. Silence becomes easier.

By the time we are old enough to work the pumps, we have already learned the words of water:

- drip
- seep
- rise
- fall
- still
- storm

These are each their own story in the language we have inherited from the first people who stood here and felt the lattice floor tremble under them.

Our bodies are grown by the water.

The air of the chamber is warm and wet, and the skin never learns to be dry. We sweat the salt of the Underwater and the ocean above at once, and our clothes—if they can be called that—are woven of algae and hair and patched so many times they are more mending than original cloth. We are pale because the light of the lamps is not strong enough to tan skin. We are smooth because the scum of the walls is not left to grow on us.

We are not shaped by the world of “sun” and “sky” that our ancestors whisper stories about during long lulls between pump-strokes. We are shaped by the stone of the ceiling and the water of the floor.

Every step you take on the lattice of planks and stone blocks is placed more carefully than you think. The wood beneath us is not fragile; it is the work of generations. But it is not the rock of the earth itself. It is the skin of the world suspended between ocean and ocean.

If you look at our feet, you will see why they are the way they are.

Our toes curl over the edges of the boards rather than resting flat. The children of the Lift are always scolded for walking with flat heels; the sound of flesh on wood carries like the echo of a stone falling down the shaft of the world. We walk with the outside edges of our feet when the pumps are quiet and the world outside the ceiling is stirring the crystal ribs to faint light.

We call it “storm-walking,” when the pressure of the ocean above changes the air of the chamber and the stone of the vault begins to record the storm in its veins.

Our eyes are not made for color.

We know the colors of the world by the Glow.

- the pale of the lamp
- the blue of the Shard
- the black of the Underwater
- the gray of the walls
- the faint green of the algae scum that we rarely allow to grow
- the white of the storm, when the ribs burn bright for the passage of something above.

We can see the difference between the soft glow of a shell that has just been pulled from the water and the bright flare of one that has been held by a chest that is thinking too quickly of the world below. The eyes of the Lift are shaped to the dark, and when we talk to each other in the low light of the lamps, we see more black than face.

Our hands are the tools of our world.

We have not built machines of the kind of the world-that-was. We have not made engines to roar or metal to fly through the sky or glass to think of its own accord. We have only what the ancestors found and did not destroy.

Our hands know the feel of:

- bronze pump handles
- wooden levers polished by generations
- the edges of rope nets that are always being remade
- the cold of the Shards before we shape them
- the warm of the water that comes up from the depths below us.

Every callus on a palm is a lesson of the world. We know without looking if a pump is failing because the balance of the water changes the feel of the handle in our grip. We know without listening if the valve of a return line is corroding because the air of the chamber tastes differently to the nose of the body.

We are not taught to be proud of our hands; they are simply what they must be.

We are not taught fear of the world below the floor.

We know the Shards and the shells and the other remnants of the machine as the bones of an older thing than we. They are not the “work of our hands” in the way that our ancestors talk of cities and towers and engines of light. They are the work of the world-that-was, and the world-that-was is not us.

We are not the same species as the builders of the shafts.

We are not.

The Lift is not a city to be expanded or a world to be improved. It is a place to be tended until it cannot be tended any longer.

Our children learn the rituals of maintenance as soon as they can walk and not be swept by accident into the gaps in the floor.

They are taught:

- to drag the nets of scum from the walls where it grows unchecked
- to polish the bronze of the pumps and the valves
- to check the lines of the Shard-glass lenses that hang from the vault ceiling
- to help the older ones who lower the buckets of water and pull the catch of creatures from the Underwater.

They are taught not to ask why the rules are the way they are until the day of their first reversal of the pumps. On that day, they are allowed to understand the depth of the world and the silence of it.

It is not the strictness of law that keeps us from the num of words and questions and chatter. It is the balance of water.

The water below the lattice floor is not endless.

The water that comes up from the shafts of the world below is not always the same.

In the times of the deep storms, when the crystal ribs of the ceiling burn bright and the pressure of the ocean above changes the air like the turning of a great wheel in the world, we feel the change of the water before the pumps change their stroke.

We do not ask to be the keepers of the last heart of the world, but we are.

We are not blind to the world outside the Lift chamber.

We know the ocean above the ceiling is not our sky; it is the sky of the world. It is the air of the world that has filled with water and time and the dust of dead mountains. We do not build the outside of the world; we tend the inside of the world.

The world outside the Lift chamber is not our world.

We do not speak to it.

There are those who claim to hear the ocean of the world speak when the pressure of the over- ceiling presses the crystal ribs to glow. The elders say it is only the pattern of the storm above the chamber echoing the shape of its own violence.

We do not argue with the elders of the storm.

We are not the ones who have seen the world of “before the ceiling became the ocean.”

We are not the ones who have walked the shafts of the world to the surface of the earth.

We are the ones who have only the stone of the vault of the Lift chamber and the wood of the lattice floor and the water of the Underwater and the light of the Shards and the shells.

We are the ones who have only the pumps of bronze and wood and the hands that have learned to keep them turning.

We are not spirits of the world or machine-people of the shafts of the world.

We are people of the Lift.

Our name is not different from the name of the chamber we live in because we and the chamber are not different things.

We are the Lift.

Our time is not measured by the sun of the surface-world-that-was, but by the pump of the world’s heart.

We call them “cycles” instead of “years.”

A cycle is the time of one stroke of the world’s pump:

- from one reversal of the pumps
- from the first of the new water of the Underwater
- from the first of the new creatures of the depths
- from the first of the new pattern of storms in the ribs of the crystal vault
- to the next of the same.

We do not count the cycles as we age; we count the changes of the water.

There are:

- the times of clear water
- the times of thick water
- the times of storm water
- the times of quiet water.

Each of these times is the movement of the world under the floor of the chamber.

The creatures of the Underwater are the same and not the same in each cycle of the water.

The fish of the world below are always the same:

- pale
- slow
- large-eyed
- translucent-skinned.

They are not the clever fish of the surface stories of the world-that-was, but they are not stupid. They are the fish of the world of the pumps and the depths and the slow of the time.

The predators of the world below are not the wolves of the surface stories of the world-that-was.

They are not fast or cruel; they are not loud or screaming.

They are the filters of the world of the water: sheets of living matter that are not more dangerous than the walls of the world itself. They are not feared in the way of the monsters of the stories of the world-that-was. They are not the enemies of the stories of the world-that-was.

They are the world of the water.

We are not the masters of the world of the water.

We are not the masters of the world of the stone.

We are not the masters of the world of the pumps of the world.

We are not the masters of the world of the Light of the world.

We are the ones who have learned to survive the world of the end of the world.

We are the ones who have learned to keep the Lift of the world breathing.

We are not the ones of the world-that-was who have built the shafts of the world.

We are the ones of the world-of-now who have cleared the shaft of the world.

We are not the ones of the world-that-was who have built the machine of the world.

We are the ones of the world-of-now who have found the machine of the world and not broken it.

We are not the ones of the world-that-was who have lived the life of the surface.

We are the ones of the world-of-now who have never left the stone of the vault of the Lift chamber.

We are not the ones of the world-that-was who have seen the stars of the sky.

We are the ones of the world-of-now who have never seen the stars of the sky.
