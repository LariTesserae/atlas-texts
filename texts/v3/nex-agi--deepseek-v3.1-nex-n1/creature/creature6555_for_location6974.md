They call themselves **Columnea** — when they need a name at all.

Most of the time they do not need one.

They are the **fifth-gendered** of the world-tree.

### The Body of the Columnea

From the outside you would not see one person so much as a shifting heap of bark and stick and leaf the size of a large wolf — made of many.

A Columnea is not a single body.

They are a **bundle** — a colony-being — made of:

- One **sounder** — a long, segmented stem of hollow wood that grows upright from a central base, carrying the vocalizing cells within.
- A dozen or more **carriers**, each like a small dog of compact wood and muscle-fiber, whose jointed legs grab the world-tree’s surface with hook-shaped feet of hardened resin.
- A handful of **tracers** — thread-thin runners that lace from the carrier’s backs to the world-tree’s surface, tasting the flow of sap and temperature in the wood.

The carriers do not cluster together randomly. They **build** a single body on the run. They clamp around the sounder and around each other with specialized limbs and lock in place, forming a torso and legs and a swaying neck-pipe that rises like a flute from the center of the group.

When you stand on the platform of the world-tree and see what looks like a single creature moving in the pattern of ants on a hill, you may actually be looking at dozens of **Columnea**, each a bundle of parts that moves like one animal.

### Society of One Mind

The Columnea are sapient — but not in the way of individuals thinking alone.

Each Columnea is **one** mind.

The **carriers** share spinal cords of plant fiber and continuous capillary-flow along their limbs. Their sap-blood is chemical information. Each step and sway and tightening of their joints is a decision that is not “voted on” but **shared** — a distributed thought that happens through body motion.

If you ask:

> Who decides which direction to go?

The answer is **all**.

The carriers are not servants to the sounder. They are the sounder’s lungs and heart and decision-making tissue. The **sounder** is the seat of memory and long-term intent — of the song they have sung to the world for generations.

But the **carriers** are the seat of **now**.

They think by:

- stepping,
- climbing,
- balancing,
- adjusting,
- tightening.

They do not “talk of” motion — they motion, and that motion *is* thought.

### What They Are

Biologically speaking, the Columnea are a **specialized organ of the world-tree**.

They are what **holds** and **hitches** and **harmonizes**.

### 1. Holders

The Columnea are the **bones** of the world-tree’s state.

They are living **cables** grown into the latticework of the branches.

You see them:

- wrapping around branches where the platform joins,
- weaving their bodies into the gaps,
- growing their rootlets into cracks and stress-lines,
- pulling **towards** health.

The world-tree does not worry about its own shape. The Columnea worry.

If a branch begins to sag on an upper platform, they:

- **Walk** to the section.
- **Anchor** themselves into the platform surface and into neighboring branches.
- **Tense** — their bodies snapping into rigidity like tightening cables.

Slowly, over the course of days or weeks, their **bundle-muscles** and **rootlets** bend the wood back toward the light-path — or at least prevent it from failing further.

### 2. Hitchers

They **manufacture** some of the world-tree’s joints.

The **Carriers** are not born only from the world-tree’s direct growth. They are also **built** by each other.

You see them:

- stripping flakes from the bark where it has been damaged,
- chewing them into pulp with resin glands in their mouths,
- weaving them into new limbs — more **carriers**, or replacement **tracers** or small **sounder buds**.

They **attach** new members to themselves — **graft** them into place as new arms of the same mind.

When a **Columnea** is damaged, they **detach** the failing part:

- a shattered carrier is cut free from the bundle,
- new carriers are grown and **hitched** into place,
- life **flows** back from the rest of the body into the new flesh.

Thus **Columnea** grow and **regenerate** by expansion rather than simple replacement.

### 3. Harmonizers

And they **sing**.

The **Sounder** — that long pipe-stem that grows from the center of each Columnea — is **built** to be used **in** and **with** the world-tree.

The **mid** of the **Columnea** is **made** of **song**.

Inside the **Sounder**:

- the **voice** is not a voice as we know it, but a **fold** of tissue that **modifies** airflow,
- powered **by** the world-tree’s wind **and** by **squeezing** of **carrier** muscles,
- tuned **to** the **resonances** of the **pipe columns** of **the** world-tree.

### How They Sing

When **wind** from **below** rises **through** the **platform** and **spills** into **one** of **those** towering **pipe-columns**, **it** sets **the** entire **column** humming **at** a **particular** pitch. **The** column **sounds** like **thunder** **half** trapped **half** released **to** sky **.**

The **Columnea** are **not** content **with** this **noise**.

They **climb** **onto** **these** columns **until** **they** **find** **nodes** — **points** **where** **the** vibration **is** strongest **in** **the** bark, **and** **they** **press** **their** sounders **side** **-** **on** to **the** column **.**

Then **they** **sing** **back** **into** **the** column **.**

### Resonance Practice

Their **“song”** is **not** melody **in** a **human** sense **.** **It** is **resonance** **practice** **;**  
**they** **search** **for** **the** pitch **,** **timbre** **,** and **overtone** **that** cause **the** **column** **to** **shake** **in** just **the** way **needed** **.**

By **doing** **so**, **they** **accomplish** **several** things **simultaneously**:

1. **They** **influence** **the** flow **of** sap **inside** **the** world-tree **.**  
   **Resonance** **travels** through **the** living **wood** **like** pressure **waves** **through** fluid **.**  
   **Specific** patterns **of** vibration **help** **push** **nutrients** from **over** rich **areas** to **need** zones **.** **Like** massaging **blood** through **muscles** **.**

2. **They** **reduce** stress **-** **points** **in** **the** structure **.**  
   **By** finding **and** amplifying **certain** harmonics **,** they **change** **how** forces **spread** **through** **the** branch-**beam**-**platforms** **.** **They** **smooth** **stress** **-** **waves** **before** they **can** crack **the** wood **.**

3. **They** **communicate** **with** **other** **Columnea** **.**  
   The **change** **in** **sound** **travels** **through** **pipe** **-** systems **and** wood-**paths** **.**  
   Other **Columnea** hear **not** words **but** **resonance** clues **to** tension **,** **flow** **,** **need** **,** **urgency** **.** **They** respond **by** moving **,** **tensing** **,** **singing** **back** **.**

### A Language of Resonance

**Columnea** **do** not **think** of **sound** as **“sound”** **.**  
They **think** of **it** as **tension**-**sharing** **.** **Change**-**calling** **.**

Each **set** of **notes** **and** overtones **is** **like** **a** sentence **about** **the** world-**tree’s** body **:**

- **low**-**steady**-**rumble** + **bright**-**high**-**whine** →  
  “Pressure **is** rising **in** **basal** shafts **,** branch **level** three **-** tier **can** be **loosened** **.”**

- **sharp**-**cutting**-**edge**-**sound** + **wavering**-**mid-**band →  
  “Crack **forming** **near** shaft **-** mouth **,** need **cable**-**wrap** **and** rhythmic-**dampening** **.”**

- **broad**-**diffuse**-**hum** →  
  “All **is** well **-** tuned **,** energy **flow** is **even** **,** workers **may** redistribute **.”**

**Meaning** **does** not **live** in **isolated** tones **but** in **patterns** **:** **the** relationship **between** the **column’s** baseline **sound**, **the** **Columnea's** added **sound**, and **how** **the** world-**tree** responds.

### Time Perception

**Columnea** live **in** **two** times **.**

### Micro-Time: Sound

**In** **sound**-**work**, **they** perceive **changes** **in** fractions **of** seconds **.**  
**They** feel **when** **a** column **over** there **shift** **phase** **-** lock **,** **and** **they** adjust **their** own **sound**-**pipe** **squeezing** **to** match **or** counter-**balance** **.**

If **you** watch **,** **it** looks like **music**-**conducting** by **many** conductors **who** never **meet** but **know** **each** other **through** **changes** **in** **the** same **muscle**-**mass** **.**

### Macro-Time: Growth

**But** **they** also **track** **time** **on** the **scale** of **branch-**growth **and** **platform-**settlement **.**

To **them**:

- **“Dawn** **-** light **-** shift **through** third **-** tier **-** opening**” **is** **like** morning **,**  
- **the** long **movement** **of** **that** shaft-**spot** across **the** platform **over** **cycles** **of** light **-** dark **is** **like** seasons **,**  
- and **a** major **reshaping** **of** **the** lattice-**beam** layout **above** **them** is **like** an **epoch** **.**

They **remember** **patterns** **of** stress **and** flow **from** **before** **the** new **branch**-**beam** **grew** in **,** **and** they **compare** **them** **to** today’s **patterns** **to** decide **where** **to** **anchor** **next** **.**

### Relationship With the World-Tree

**Columnea** **do** not think **of** themselves **as** “servants” **of** the world-tree.

**They** think **of** themselves **as** “the **part**-**of**-**the**-**one**-**mind**-**that**-**remembers** **-** **sound**-**and-**shape**-**better**-**than-**the-**rest**-**does**.”

They **sense** **the** world-tree’s **health** **through**:

- **changes** **in** the **pipe-**columns **,**  
- **the** texture **of** sap **flow** under **their** tracer-**roots****,**  
- **the** way **their** own **sound**-**songs** **reflect** back **from** distant **columns** **,** **altered** by **flow** **pressure** **.**

### Maintenance as Work

Most **of** their **activity** **is** **maintenance**:

- **When** **wind**-**erosion** **has** polished **one** section **of** platform **too** thin **,**  
  **Columnea** **climb** **there** and **sound**-**promote** **new** bark-**growth** **through** rhythmic **pressure**-**waves** **.**

- **When** **a** support **trunk** **has** shifted **under** load **,**  
  **they** **fasten** themselves **into** living **cables** and **help** **hold** **the** trunk **steady** **until** **growth** catches up **with** **stress** **.**

- **When** **another** kind of **worker**-**creature** — the **brushers** — **have** cleared **cellular** debris **from** sap **channels** and **dropped** it **into** shafts, **Columnea** **sound**-**guide** **that** debris **to** deeper **processing**-**halls** **inside** the world-tree.

### Their Own Health

**Columnea** **are** **not** immortal **.**

Individual **carriers** **wear** out **,** **crack** **,** **or** **get** crushed **when** branches **shift** unexpectedly. **Some** sounders **reach** **a** length **where** they **can** no longer **support** themselves **without** collapsing **under** their **own** weight **.**

When **a** carrier **dies** **,** **the** bundle **detaches** **,** **cuts** **free** **,** and **sometimes** **eats** the **valuable** parts, **un-**weaving **that** body **back** into **nutrients** for **new** growth. **It** is **not** seen **as** death **but** as **remodeling** **.**

### Thought in the Wind-Sound

On **their** own platform **,** **Columnea** **talk** **to** each other **less** by **sound** **than** **by** **body**-**tension** **,** **posture** **,** and **small** **vibrations** **through** the **platform**-**surface** **.** **But** they **are** never **separated** from **sound**.

That **vast** **chorus** of **thundering**-**organ-**wind-**pipes** **is** to **them** what **a** beating **heart**-**and**-**lungs**-**noise** is **to** a fetus **in** womb **:** **not** just noise **but** **found**-**world** **,** **the** **medium** **through** which they **think** **and** live **.**
