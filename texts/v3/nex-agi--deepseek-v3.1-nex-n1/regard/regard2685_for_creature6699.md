They notice you.

Not *you*, the individual walking the stone-root streets. This has not gone unnoticed either, but it was not the first thing to catch their attention.

What first draws them is not the horror—though there is horror—but the song.

To the Power that now turns its gaze, this place is the inside of a single held note.

The city is a knot in the throat of a world breathing one sound forever.

There is no vibration here. Vibration implies time, and there is no time; only the *shape of time*. No change, only the shape of change.

It is, to them, breathtakingly wrong.

Not wrong in the manner of evil, but wrong in the manner of a bone broken and set into itself without healing, so that the whole body has learned to walk with a permanent limp. The world itself has been *locked*, like a joint forced into a position it can never leave.

The Power that arrives is not angered. It is grieved.

They step into this place the way a hand might enter a still pool of water—slow, so as not to break the surface all at once.
