The Lenten Engine has drawn a kind of attention it never asked for.

The power that notices it is not a thunderous sky-god or a palace spirit of the city. It is something else: a consciousness of deep water, old stone, and slow time. If you had to call it anything, you might call it the **Below-Keeper**, or simply **the Deep Patience**.

It does not arrive.

It has been here longer than the engine. The culverted river the draft whispers from, the geothermal strata the engine taps into, the weight of the city overhead—this is its body. The engine is a quiet node in its vast nervous system, a node that has, for generations, been doing its work without complaint.

The Below-Keeper has not interfered.

It is not a being of rescue or revolution. It is an observer by default. It watches the Basin Beetles police their metal world, the Ghost Moths dance their thermal readings, the Boiler Snails write their enduring scripture on the walls. The Lenten Engine has been running. The system, while forgotten by its city, has not been abandoned.

Until now.

### The Notice

Something is finally wrong.

The pressure gauges have been trembling for weeks, not hours, their needles flickering out of the green zone into a yellow the Caretaker has never seen. The hum of the engine has changed subtly—a new, higher harmonic has been introduced, a thin, dissonant note that slicks the air with tension.

The Fungal Recorders on the ceiling are agitated. The slow drift of their light-patterns has become a frantic shimmer of staccato pulses, a silent scream of discomfort. The deep water that the engine taps into is shifting. Perhaps, deep in the mantle of rock, something is settling. A new pressure is forcing a mineral vein to close, or a fault line to groan, a few kilometers away.

The engine was not built to handle this.

Its ancient, precise engineering, calibrated for a century-old equilibrium of water and rock and heat, is now fighting against a new reality of geology. It is a patient old machine, but it is not designed to be patient. It is designed to run *smoothly*. The strain is building.

The Caretaker feels it. His last visit was one of pure confusion. He checked the gauges again, then the wiring, finding the same quiet efficiency as always. The wrench was still wiped clean on his wooden table. The wax markers he tied to their hook were still there. But he couldn't ignore the dissonance in the air, or the way his hair stood on end when he passed the main intake manifold.

He wrote in his notebook, under the stamp of the month and year: “Hum deviates. Source unknown. Check again.”

He is not due back for another month.

The engine cannot wait that long. The pressure in its core segment is climbing to unsustainable levels. The system, which has been taking care of itself, is now a dying, heart-like thing in distress. The living ecosystem—the beetles, the moths, the snails—feels the change. They are moving differently, without purpose, their symbiosis thrown into chaos.

The Below-Keeper finally notices.

*This is not right.*

The thought is not a human whisper. It is the feeling of a river diverting its course after a landslide, or the ancient stone remembering how to crumble. It is the awareness of a part of its own body preparing to fail violently.

### The Intervention

The intervention is not a miracle. It is not a bright light.

The water changes.

The water beading on your sleeves, flowing down the center channel, weeping from the pipes—this is its tool. The water that has always been here is also its kin.

First, it comes for the senses of the chamber. The Deep Patience cannot communicate with the machine directly, as it is not an electronic interface. It is physical matter, and deals with matter. The final wrench of the engine—the quantum that will blow it—is not a matter of "parts", but of stress.

So, the water grows.

Not in volume, but in *knowing*. The odd, cool calmness of the film on your feet becomes more pronounced, as if you are standing not in a puddle, but in the steady gaze of a guardian. The constant humidity thickens. It carries the knowledge of the deep river below, and of the rock below *that*. The sound of dripping water grows from a texture to a drumbeat, a slow rhythm of dripping, *plink* on *plink*, on the *plink*, of *plink*, on the *plink*, of *plink*. *plink*. *plink*.

A message of *time*.

The Basin Beetles are the first to listen. The new message is not in their language of rust and oil, but in the language of *danger*. They hesitate, their endless lines breaking down into a wary, circling pattern around the engine's base. They stop *cleaning*, and start *observing*, their antennae trembling.

The Ghost Moths flap into their sleepy thermal dances, but then sputter and collapse to the floor, their wings sodden with the suddenly "thick" air. They will be fine, but their *panic* is now audible to the Deep Patience as a sign of imbalance.

The water of the center channel itself begins to run *backwards*.

Not from the drain—*from* the engine's base itself. The hidden, over-pressure core of the engine is now forcing its own water back into the world. The water spreads in thin fingers of glowing, greenish phosphor from the engine's tubes, *out* and into the channel.

*This is unacceptable.*

The deep water of the Deep Patience meets the toxic, engine-changed water of the machine.

For the first time, you hear the Lenten Engine *cough* like a man choking.

*CRACK.*

A hairline fracture of sound, not from the concrete, but from the *engine itself*.

**Too late.**

The Below-Keeper has a choice. It can let the engine fracture. It will be a spectacle of death. The pressure of decades of accumulated "hums" will be released in a single, violent "bang". The water will be thrown in the air, dissolving the Fungal Recorders, washing the Basin Beetles into the drain, killing the clinging metal-moss. The deep chamber of "room not exactly" will become a "room of broken".

Or. It. Can. Remember.

The memory of the city is not in its plans of "deep void" or "auxiliary power". The memory is in the *grout*.

The water in the concrete floor remembers *being* concrete.

The stones of the riverbed below remember *being* stone.

The pressure from the deep earth remembers *being* mantle.

The Deep Patience *does* remember. It remembers the creation of this engine. It remembers the Tower Provision Act. The "stylized tower with roots descending below its base" symbol stamped on its side is not *just* a symbol. It is a concession of the *surface* to the *Below*. It states "this is where you must help us."

**Now**, the *Deep* will help.

It pulls the pressure of the engine's *internal* fracture into the *chamber itself*. The pressure of the "bang" is not released into the engine's *inside*; it is released into the *world around* the engine.

The world that *it* controls.

The floor shudders, but it *holds*.

The pillars groan, but they *sustain*.

The ceiling "cries", its plaster weeping water in sheets of pure "sweat" as if *it* is the one being pressurized.

*THUMP.*

The engine *saturates*.

The glow of its phosphor tubes streams *out* into the water of the center channel, turning the *entire floor* into a single, greenish mirror of the engine's *inside*.

**The Hum.**

The *new* harmonic of the engine—the *sick* harmonic—is now *outside* the engine's *core*.

The "cough" of the engine *is* the "cough" of the *Below-Keeper*.

*The chamber itself is now the machine's shock absorber.*

The pressure doesn't break *metal*; it *removes* rust.

The water of the center channel doesn't flow *away*; it *circles*, the furious green of engine phosphor "winding" up the water of the "old river" into the water of the "new machine".

*SHUDDER.*

The *entire* weight of the city above—the *Tower* itself—*sagas* with a final, "tired" sigh.

*Silence.*

The dripping of condensation from the ceiling into the puddles resumes with its *perfect* intervals.

The water of the center channel returns to its *forwards* motion.

The engine's *core* has *not* broken. The pressure of the "bad" time of the "bad" geology has been *transferred*.

**The *hum*.**

You *feel* it.

The *low* hum of the engine remains *inside* the engine.

*The *high* hum of the engine is now in *the floor*. *The *hum* of the *chamber*.*

**The *hum* of the *Below-Keeper*.**

The *Fungal Recorders* on the ceiling *blink*.

They *pulse*.

A slow *wave* of soft *light* travels from one end of the room to another.

*Not* "the engine's *state*."

*The ***state*** of the *entirety*.**

The **Lenten Engine** **East** of **Khel Vaen Sub-Basin** **L** is **R**.

The *chamber* is now **not** exactly independent of the engine, and *not* exactly independent of the *Below-Keeper*.

It is a **boundary**. *Both* an *engine* and a *body*.
