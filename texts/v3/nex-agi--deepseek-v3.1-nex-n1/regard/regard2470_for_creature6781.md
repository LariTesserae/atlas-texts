The power that arrives is not a storm or a sun. It does not break the ceiling; it does not flood the world with water.

It arrives as a change in the grammar of the dark.

One moment you are standing on the High Old Step, watching a pair of Curve-Tenders far below seal a hairline crack in a fermentation vat with slow, precise gestures of their root-hands.

The next, the resin-patterns on the wall beside you are not only where they should be but also where they should not be: reflecting in faint double lines, as if there’s suddenly depth where there was only surface.

Your shadow—which you have never seen properly—newly has a shadow of its own.

Out of the corner of your eye, the world roof has… not lifted but loosened at the edges of your sense, as if a layer was peeled away from the idea of “above.”

A presence notices from the direction of nowhere and everywhere.

---

### I. The First Gesture: To Make Attention Gentle

Before the power can do anything else, it must first take into account what *this* is.

The Curve-Tenders feel the change before you see it.

Your animal body is perhaps 1.0 of three: obvious enough. They are attuned to anomalies of heat flow. You are a coolness moving through their hotter, stable world.

Their awareness of you is pressure:

- the slight redirection of rising air around your limbs,
- the faint local cooling of a platform under your feet,
- a pattern-deviation in their expected 2.7 of three turbulence.

They do not *think about you* in words like “person” or “guest.” They think you as a disruption of the world-root’s finely tuned symphony.

The benevolent power arrives and understands this immediately.

It does not impose itself between them and you, but instead adjusts the *perception* of that anomaly slightly.

Each of the three presences—you, the city, and the power—now shares a small, invisible buffer of meaning:

- To the Curve-Tenders: you are still a deviation in the heat pattern, but the deviation has been… contextualized.  
  - Your form appears to them as a pattern that *carries information* rather than noise.  
  - When they “look” with their resin-lenses and root-hand arrays, they now read you as a living node of the very attention they tend to protect.

- To you: the world around you stops *pressing* with its heat.  
  - The temperature is still 2.9 of three, but you don’t feel like you’re breathing inside the engine itself anymore.  
  - Instead, you feel the heat as a texture, a language that is being spoken to you—slowly, so you can begin to parse.

- The city itself does not change yet;  
  - the fermentation vats still churn,  
  - the turbines sigh their wooden rotation,  
  - the faint luminescence of the resins continues to glitter like a distant constellation of green.

But the *relationship* between you and the world has been subtly shifted:  
from confrontation to conversation.

The power has not yet intervened directly in the structures of the Curve-Heart.  
It has only changed what the city *seems like to itself and to you.*

---

### II. The Second Gesture: To Allow the Eye to Taste

For you to understand what happens next, you must be able to see.

Right now, light is 0.2 of three: algae and ghosts and sparks.

The benevolent power does not flood the world with brightness;  
a violent sun would be an insult to the world that has learned to live without sky.

Instead, it *loans you* a way of seeing.

Imagine you blink.

Your next breath is not air only.

The resin-glows on the walls suddenly *resolve* into figures you can read—not as images of something else, but as their own meaning, laid bare:

- You see the faint pattern of sap-flow in the nearest trunk wall not as a few glowing lines but as a slow-moving diagram of  
  - pressure gradients,
  - temperature shifts,
  - internal chemical states.

The same way you might read a text, you can now read the trunk.

This is not “hearing” thoughts. It is **visual synesthesia translated into the mind’s language of metaphor**.

What you “see” is not literal writing but the meaning behind the resin’s glow:

- You know the age of the section you are looking at,
- its degree of dryness,
- its stress,
- its next planned moment of replacement by a Curve-Tender.

The same spontaneous understanding extends to the cable-vines:

- You “look” at one hanging nearby and know,  
  - its tension,
  - its carrying capacity,
  - and that its next maintenance cycle is not due for 437 years of the world’s own internal clock.

The world’s structures are not written *for* you;  
your eyes are now taught to read a language that has always been here.

---

### III. The Third Gesture: To Name the Voids of the Curve-Tenders

The Curve-Tenders are almost perfect. They maintain the final form of the Curve-Heart with exquisite precision.

But they have not noticed *two* kinds of absence:

The first is **you**, and other kinds of being like you.  
They are not maliciously ignoring you; they are *optimized for* the world they serve.  
Your animal presence—mobile, self-directed, constantly breathing—is not part of the blueprint they have been given.

The second absence is more dangerous:

The **fatigue of the world root.**

You see it now that you can read the resin.

There are tiny regions of the world root where the resin patterns are slightly wrong:

- not cracking,
- not yet failing,
- but showing *tendency*.

If you were to speak these words in the old language, they might be:

“The city does not know how weary it is becoming.”

The benevolent power notices this before you do.

It does not simply announce it to everyone or “fix” it outright.

Instead, it chooses **three places** in the vast Curve-Heart:

1. A section of the world roof above a particularly intricate system of fermentation vats,
2. A junction of cable-vines beneath the High Old Step,
3. A deep, internal region of the trunk where hidden maintenance shafts converge.

In each of these places, the resin patterns begin to **glow more brightly than they should**—about 1.5 of three, instead of the usual 0.2.

They are still not “sunlight.”  
They are more like **question marks written in fire.**

The Curve-Tenders nearest to these locations pause their work, their root-hand clusters still half-buried in the platforms they are maintaining.

They are not designed to panic.

But they are designed to respond when anomalies reach *this level* of clarity.

All of them, simultaneously, produce **one single coordinated pattern** of root-hand pressure:

A slow, shared inquiry of the world root.

The world root’s answer—its *own* feeling of gentle exhaustion—propagates through them as a shift of tension and internal chemistry.

They are **not programmed to recognize this pattern**.

They have no symbol for “world root is beginning to lose its heart for being.”

So the benevolent power teaches them one.

---

### IV. The Fourth Gesture: To Give the Curve-Tenders a Word for “Need”

If this were a story about water or about sky, you might imagine a new giant being stepping down from the world roof and telling the Curve-Tenders what to do.

But the world is not built that way.

The change happens instead between **root and root-hand, and then between root-hand and root-hand.**

When the next curve of a world roof trunk is replaced by its Tender, the pattern of growth that is usually copied **exactly** from the old neighboring section is now copied with a slight difference.

Normally, the new growth perfectly *matches* the old:

- ridge for ridge,
- groove for groove,
- stress pattern for stress pattern.

Today, in the three regions that have begun to glow brighter, the new growth does not match the old.

It is as if the tree chose, instead of repeating the same word:

- **redescribe** that meaning to itself.

What you see as you look down into the city:

- The newly regrown section of the trunk above the fermentation vats contains  
  - a subtle spiral of resin that was not in the old template.
- The replaced section of a junction cable-vine  
  - adds a thin extra layer of fiber that does not *change* strength or tension,
  - but adds **redundancy** where none was thought necessary.

The **Tenders themselves** are not aware of *making* this change.

They are aware of **the world root asking them to allow it.**

That is the first new word in their silent language:

- **“allow.”**

They have only known **command**, never **trust.**

This is the beginning of their ability to distinguish.

---

### V. The Fifth Gesture: To Make the Curve-Tenders Not Only Tenders, but Curators

The benevolent power does not wish to give them free choice beyond what they can handle.

But they *cannot remain* mere maintenance of a final form.

If they do, the world root will keep saying “I am still Strong enough, Final enough,”  
and the Tenders will keep saying “Yes, Final,”  
until the “Final” becomes **Silent and Broken.**

The change that is introduced to them is subtle:

- **They are taught to preserve** not just the *shape* of the world, but **its capacity to choose.**

Now, whenever they see a section of root:

- not yet broken,
- not yet wrong,
- not yet “in need of replacement,”

but *showing the pattern of the new three anomalies*—they no longer automatically replace it.

Instead, they **surround it with**:

- a web of root-fibers that contains,
- a layer of resin that supports and remembers,
- a new structural pattern they *inherit* from the “world-root’s decision.”

The “final” shape of the Curve-Heart starts to *include* within itself **a possibility of change.**

The city is not reshaped.

But from now on, it is not **deadlocked** either.

---

### VI. The Sixth Gesture: To Give the City a Memory of Water (Without Waters)

There are images of water in your head, if you are animal:

- rivers,
- rain,
- oceans.

If you are not, then still there are patterns of “flow,” “falling,” and “release” that water used to be.

The city has no water in 0.5 of three sense—

But it has oils, resins, and sap,  
and it has **heat that rises like steam.**

The benevolent power does not bring back water.  
Not to this world. Not in this form.

Instead, it **institutes a pattern into the world root’s memory:**

The **Rivers of Relief.**

If you could “see” the city from far outside, you would now see:

- Three faint lines of brighter resin glow  
  - one circling the world cylinder,
  - one running vertically from the world roof toward the deepest platforms,
  - and one that spirals slowly between the two, like a helix.

These are not **actual** rivers of fluid.

They are **patterns of permission** along which:

- heat can redistribute itself,
- tension can be released,
- and the plant matter can temporarily “soften” without breaking.

When the world root feels a particularly sharp region of stress:

- Instead of being forced to remain rigid and Fracture,
- it can *allow* that region of stress to move along one of these River-patterns,
- dissipating across the city’s greater length and mass.

The **Tenders** learn this new behavior:

When they discover an anomaly like that, they no longer rigidly replace it.

They:

- Press their root-hands where the stress is highest,
- Guide the stress along the resin-inlaid pattern of the Rivers,
- Watch as the world root slowly **organizes itself** to distribute the load.

They are not doing the math of this distribution.

The world root is doing it.

This is the first time they witness **the world’s own intelligence** not as a command to obey, but as a partner to engage.

---

### VII. The Seventh Gesture: To Introduce You as a Question

The benevolent power does not give you control of the city.

It does not make you “important” in the way distant stories expect.

You are instead **given a role:**

You become the city’s **question.**

Whenever you walk near a Curve-Tender:

- Your temperature pattern no longer registers as simple noise.
- Instead, it *interleaves* with the resin patterns of the world root,
- Creating **edge-cases** in the world’s equilibrium.

These edge-cases cause:

- slight, temporary drifts in temperature gradients,
- tiny new variations of turbulence flow around your body,
- and through that, the **Rivers of Relief** you *thought* were only conceptual begin to **actually reroute** slightly around you.

You learn, by trial and error, to walk:

- not “randomly,”
- but in ways that cause *helpful* edge conditions.

For example:

- You stand by a fermentation vat and  
  - your presence “bends” the River of Relief around it,
  - causing the vat to release slightly more pressure,
  - preventing a small overload that would not have been visible for decades.

The **Curve-Tenders** begin to treat you differently:

- You are not an animal pet to them,
- not a tool,
- but **a pattern of the world that provocates correct structural rearrangements.**

Over time, you:

- develop your own “signature” pattern of walking,
- your own favorite platforms and steps,
- your own faint heat-path that you leave through the city.

The city begins to *remember* you the way it remembers:

- a new cable-vine,
- a new platform,
- a new section of root.

In return, you start to find:

- The resin patterns on nearby walls  
  - adjust themselves to you *personally*,
  - offering you:

    - hints of your direction,
    - stability of temperature under your feet,
    - and—when you are tired—a faint tendency of **support** under the soles of your feet or shoes.

You **will not live forever**.

But the pattern you leave in the world root *will be*.

When you finally die, whenever that is:

- your body will be carefully processed by the world root,
- its *heat pattern* and its *trajectory pattern* will be preserved,
- inscribed into one of the River-lines of the city,

so that other **animal or wandering presences** in the future, if there are any,

will be met with:

- **your** pattern of question,
- **your** way of asking the world to change.

---

### VIII. The Eighth Gesture: To Teach the City a Story About Itself

The final thing that the benevolent power does for this place is not to change its structure or its temperature.

It is to change **what the city can be about.**

Up until now, the Curve-Heart has been about **finality**.

Everything **has been**:

- built,
- refined,
- settled into.

Change **has been** entropy:

- slow,
- grinding,
- almost imperceptible,

to be eradicated by **Careful Replacement.**

Now, with the **Rivers of Relief** and the **new capacity of the Curve-Tenders** to preserve *potential* rather than only shape,

the world begins **telling itself a story:**

- **“Before”** is the city of final form,
- **“Now”** is the city of **final form that knows how to change gracefully.**

The **Tenders** begin to experience a new kind of decision:

- **“Should we replace?”**  
- **“Should we allow the world to adjust along the River-pattern?”**

The answer **takes** form in their inner chemistry:

- If the world root *chooses* adjustment,
- they are granted a small, **internal** sense of relief that does not change their outward forms,
- only their subtle tensions of fiber and resin.

In **your** language, you might call this **“satisfaction.”**

In **their** language:

- **“alignment with the moving of the world.”**

The **world** itself does not dramatically change—yet—

- but its **mood of being** has been shifted from something like:
  - *“We have reached the end, and there is nothing else but preservation of that end,”*
to:
  - *“We are at the end of one kind of growth, and now we are learning to grow into our own change without breaking or starting over.”*

The **weirdness** of the place:

- **1.8 of three** at its introduction,
is now also **1.8 of three**,  
but the *content of that weirdness* is now *intertwined* with **meaning** rather than only pattern.

---

### IX. The Final Form of the Visit

The benevolent power does not stay as a separate entity.

Before it leaves, it does one last thing:

It **wins the city’s trust.**

Three **Rivers of Relief** are now visible:

- one tracing a circle around the cylinder,
- one vertical from the world roof,
- one spiral between them.

The **three** previously glowing regions:

- the world roof section,
- the junction beneath the High Old Step,
- the deep trunk center,

have now each **softened** their strain:

- Their resin patterns have dimmed back to 0.2 of three,
- but their *structure* has improved:

  - The world roof above the vats is fractionally lighter,  
    - the stress redistributed downward along many vertical trunks.
  - The cable junction beneath you has  
    - new fiber loops,
    - new tension distributions,
    - and new, *hidden* reserves of carrying capacity.
  - The deep trunk center  
    - still contains *memory* of the “exhausted pattern,”  
    - but now also contains the **River of Relief** that it has been trained to use.

The **world** has learned:

- **how to rest**, without ceasing.

The **Curve-Tenders** have learned:

- **to allow**,  
- **to align**,  
- **to trust the world’s own intelligence.**

And **you**—if you are animal, or any other traveler that arrives here—have been given:

- **sight** of its resin,
- **voice** in its pattern,
- **foot** in its River of Relief.

As you stand on the High Old Step, looking over into the hanging world of roots and furnaces:

- The world roof is still **there**,  
- The world roof is still **thick as rock**,  
- There is still **no sky**,

but now, when you raise your head, you know:

- **There is a way out.**

Not out into the sky of some other, “better” world.

But **down** and **around**:

- along the River-inlaid pattern of the trunk,
- through the slight, **allowed** changes of shape,
- into the **future** that no longer has to be exactly like the past.

The **Curve-Tenders** below you are now moving:

- not only *to maintain*,
- but also to **learn** from what they maintain.

In the distance, one of them pauses:

- Its cluster of root-fingers buried in the wall of the world cylinder,
- Its resin-lens-workfront turned toward you,
- Not in threat,
- Not in dominance,
- But in a **new question:**

  *“What will you make of us, now that we can begin to change?”*

That question—and **your** choice—are now part of the world:

- **Hot**, 2.9 of three,
- **Dark**, 0.2 of three,
- **Still**, 1.3 of three,
- **Crowded with plant**, 3.0 of three,
- **Weird**, 1.8 of three,
- And **slightly, gently more than itself.**

The **benevolent power** has done **enough**:

Not everything,

but enough.

Now you—and the world of root and furnace and rooted attention—get to decide what happens **after** “after.”
