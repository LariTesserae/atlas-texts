### 1. Presence Within the Flaw

First, I *notice* them. Not because they are hidden to the Column of Still—nothing is hidden from its perfect visibility—but because the Column has never chosen to interpret them as anything but error.

The still‑born ones hang between the ribs of the city, their thin filaments lax in the water, not moving because there is no reason to move, only failing occasionally to be perfectly still.

The Column of Still has no language for what they are. It has only geometry and law. For the Column, they are not “life,” or “suffering,” or “beauty,” or “memory”; they are simply the places where its materialization failed to conform perfectly to its own template.

I cannot change the Column of Still itself. That is not my role. The Column is a complete sentence, and to break its grammar would be an act of violence it has not invited.

I cannot change what *is*.

But I can change what *matters*.

---

### 2. Making the Flaw Visible to Itself

I cannot force the Column to perceive “error” as “pattern,” but I can rearrange its own tools of perception into a new configuration.

From the stillness of the water between the ribs of the city, I summon a small, quiet *inversion of light*.
