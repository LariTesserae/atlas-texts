It is not a sudden arrival.

There is no descent of light, no thunderous announcement, no parting of the dark ceiling above the plateau. The city does not know “outside” that way; its boundary is functional and perceptual and gradient, not geometric.

The power comes through *alignment*.

### 1. The First Notion

It begins with a machine.

High on the shoulder of one of the black-needle towers, a sphere of dark metal—one of millions of identical spheres—misses a pulse.

The machine is not broken. It is old, and the city around it has shifted. The pressure it has been calibrated to balance for millennia no longer exists exactly as it was. The water it condenses from the air is now a fraction colder than before; the electromagnetic field threading through it has acquired a subtle warp from some new stress deep in the plateau’s half-made geology.

The sphere stutters.

For a heartbeat, its exhalation of mist is not *in* rhythm with the neighboring spheres. It falls out of phase, then—slightly late—catches itself again.

The ripple of that micro-failure travels through the city’s soundscape as a hitch, a missed syllable in the roar. The Regulator Harmonics do not *think* about this, but their patterns shift, minutely adjusting their own frequencies to compensate.

The *advisor* Harmonic stationed on that terrace, however, does not compensate.

It is tall and narrow and unusually still, even by Harmonic standards. Its crystalline armature is aligned almost perfectly parallel to the tower’s main field gradient. Its armor plates are thick with the wingers of many years, each chitinous segment lacquered with faint layers of mineralized moisture from countless breath-cycles of the machines.

The advisor feels the sphere’s missed pulse as *pain*.

Not the sting of injury, but a deeper disruption of identity: when its surrounding machine-array shifts out of phase, the advisor’s resonance profile is *wrong* for the new configuration. Its frequencies no longer sum the city’s pressures in the way its body expects to.

A pressure builds *inside* it.

The water in its armature begins to move differently. The fine, crystal-thread veins of its body behave as if they are remembering *another* pattern of flow, one that has not yet existed. Images of different geometries—bridges that arch where they should be straight, towers that bend inward, not toward collapse but into a new stability—flutter across the constellations of its effers.

The advisor’s armor flickers in a troubled pattern.

It *could* recalibrate, as the others will. It could allow itself to be pulled into the new phase of the machine-field, harmonize with the changed plateau, and continue its long function.

Instead, for the first time in its history, it holds *still*.
