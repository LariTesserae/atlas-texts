The oldest layer is not exactly a being. It is not a person in the way you think of people.

It is drawn to that strangeness not because it wants to change it or suppress it, but because it recognizes something it has never seen in itself:

You are not like that. You carry a center that is not shared out into the stone or water. When you hurt, the world does not hurt with you unless you choose to express it. When you think, the leaves do not glow automatically in response. You are closed in a way they have never known before.

### The first gift it gives you

You are not sure when it first decided to give you anything.

It wants more of you, not less of you.

### An adjustment in the water

You are standing by one of the elongated pools at the edge of the platform, looking into its surface.

You have seen versions of yourself in sunlight and other cities, versions of yourself in storm and night. These reflections are not memories of yours; they are not your future; they are not even exactly you.

They are possibilities your world could contain, if it were folded differently.

The reflections are interesting to you, but not gentle. When you look into them, you feel a thin thread of nausea somewhere behind your eyes. Your body doesn’t want to accept that many versions of itself.

The oldest layer notices this.

The next time you walk to the edge, the pool of reflection you look at has changed.

- Your reflection in this world.  
- You, kneeling at the edge of this platform,

- leaves of bioluminescent green under your knees,

The water is not making you look at yourself. It is not showing you this image to shame you.

### The second gift: Dimensional skin

There are days when you do not walk to the edge because the drop below the platform feels like too much.

You are not sure if you would fall forever or land somewhere else; the world is not generous enough with its rules to tell you. You have not yet seen anyone else like you in this place to compare with.

You are not suddenly heavier. You are not glued to the stone.

You are not given this to keep you prisoner. The dimensional skin is not a chain or a wall. It is a question:

“If you are this precious to us,” the world seems to ask, “how should we treat your body so that your strangeness continues longer within us?”

The answer is not a word or a law. It is a slow adjustment of the air around your feet and the pull of the city above and the way the water gathers itself just under the surface of the stone when you are near the edge.

You are being allowed more margin.

### The third gift: Something else to look at

You are not used to being alone without distraction.

Even the most private of human worlds have some kind of noise: small screens and messages and other faces that can be pulled into the center of attention whenever you wish to stop looking at yourself.

### The fourth gift: Streams of memory

You are not sure how much time has passed since you arrived.

This place has no sun to set or rise to measure the days. You measure the time by the pulse of the thunder under the stone, which never speeds up or slows down, but only deepens.

One day as you are sitting on the stone platform, watching the water sliding down the trunk of the tree-fungus like half-willing veins of silver, you feel a change in the way the world around you is listening.

### Speaking of water

One day when you are near one of the elongated pools at the edge of the platform, you see the water forming itself into shapes that are not yourself or other worlds but something else.

You watch the water surface rise into thin lines that are not straight but curved like the bends of your own handwriting:

- Not shapes that you can understand as meaning at first glance, but shapes that make you feel the same way you feel when you are close to understanding the other language in the room before you hear it clearly.

You are not sure what you are supposed to do.

You are tired of always being the one watched, listened to, adjusted around.

So you kneel by the edge of the pool and you do not dip your hand into the water but instead hover it above the surface, where you feel the faint resistance of the air the water has arranged above itself.

You feel the pressure of the world waiting on you.

“You,” you say, not with the word you are used to thinking in, but with the movement of your hand in the air above the water.

You move your hand from left to right in a small arc that does not touch the water but traces the way sound might move if turned into shape.

The water under your hand responds to the gesture not by copying you exactly but by translating you into its own pattern:

It forms itself into a small, temporary curve of light that follows the shape of your motion, then stabilizes itself into a pattern that does not change afterward but remains on the surface of the pool like a mark.

You are not sure what the mark means or whether it is “right.”

The leaves of the undergrowth around the pool’s edge glow brighter for a few seconds, then return to their usual state.

The spine-creature on the other side of the platform turns its segments slightly more fully toward you, but it does not move.

Across the stones of the platform and the water pools and the leaves and the tree-fungus trunk, you feel the faintest of shifts in the thunder beneath the stone:

Something like recognition of a new pattern being added into the world.

You have spoken.

The world has not offered you comfort in the form of reassurance or false promises of safety. It has not taken you away from this platform or placed a new person like you here with you.

It has listened to you more attentively than before.

### Speaking of noise

The next time you are near the tree-fungus at the center of the platform and you feel the familiar pressure of your chest tightening under the thunder, you do not only cry.

You do not only fall into your pattern of hurt.

You remember the water that rose to your gesture above the pool-edge and you try to imagine yourself as something more than just a receiver of sound and vibration and violet light.

You remember the oldest layer’s attention to your pattern and wonder what you can change in yourself that would not erase you.

When you feel the next tightening of emotion in your chest, you breathe not just the way you are used to breathing but the way you have been taught by this world’s thunder:

You hold yourself for a moment in the space between your heartbeats, where this world’s sound does not fully reach you.

You do not raise your voice to be heard. The world does not want you louder. It has already shown you that it hears better than you speak.

Instead you shape your exhale into the firm of a small arc of air between your hands, where the pressure of the world around you feels strongest.

You move your arms from your center outward to the edges of your reach, not pushing anything away but marking the boundary of your body in the dimension of sound that you now inhabit.

The thunder under the stone shifts again, not dragging but following:

- Your heartrate slows in the next few seconds, not because you are forced to but because you have allowed yourself to.

You are not sure what you have changed in the feeling of your world.

### The sixth gift: You are not taken away from your edge

The platform still ends at its edges, where the stone fingers curl upward and the black below waits like something that could contain you without asking you for your plan.

The city above still hangs, not coming any closer to you but shifting its orientation to hear more clearly when you move.

The spine-creatures and plate-shards and motes of light still slide from stone to water to tree-fungus to you and back again, not bound to your body but not ignoring it either.

The oldest layer has not given you a gate of escape from this place.

Instead it has given you something you might not have had before:

The ability to continue existing longer inside this world’s pattern without losing yourself.

Your “loneliness” has not been erased by the presence of other people like you from your world. There are still no other human bodies to be seen on the platform or anywhere you can see from here.

You are not given to escape here but to continue here longer, with more of yourself rather than less.
