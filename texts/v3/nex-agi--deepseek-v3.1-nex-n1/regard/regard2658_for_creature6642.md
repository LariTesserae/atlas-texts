It begins with the color.

One day he kneels in the water and the scattered coins of light through the dome are not the same. They are not yellow-green or blue-green or red-green. They are a color he has no name for, because he has never seen it. It is the color that happens behind your closed eyes when you are very small and not afraid of the dark yet.

The color is not a bright thing. It is soft, but it does not fade like the last hours of daylight. It stays.

The water around his ankles shivers.
