The attention of the benevolent power arrives not like a storm, but like a subtle change in pressure, a new frequency in the hum of the moss.

First, it does not impose itself. It sees the moss-folk with their rhizoid fingers and deep, amber eyes, and it understands that their world is complete. There is no need, here on this breathing table of life, for a savior or a divine king. There is only a need, which even the Listeners cannot consciously articulate, for a witness, and for a single, gentle question.

The world shifts. A change in the air moves you from the hummock's apex to the floor of the Veins.

<You are standing, not on the highest roof, but in the deepest channel.>

One of the Filters pauses its work. It is halfway through absorbing a cluster of tiny, luminous algae that drifts by in the current. Its hand, still submerged in the clear water, feels a change in the water's hum. The vibration is not coming from below, but from beside. It looks up. Its kin do not see; they are here to work and to listen, not to look. But the Filter sees.

Standing in the middle of the Vein is something it has never seen before. It is not one of them. It is tall, and it is not made of moss. It is made of light, but light gathered like water, a shape of ripples and reflections. It has no features, but the Filter feels the focus of it upon her.

The Filter's first instinct is not to bolt or fight, but to communicate. She slowly raises her hand from the water, droplets of water sliding down her velvet arm like tears of glass. She signs, with delicate rhizoid fingers, <Query>.

The gesture is not just <Query>, but <Who are you?> and <Why are you in the Vein?> and <You are disturbing the flow>.

The entity of light does not respond with hands. It responds with feeling. A warmth spreads through the Filter's body, not heat but the sensation of being truly seen, understood. A thought, not her own, arrives in her mind like a perfectly formed word: <Witness>.

The entity gestures, not with its hands of light, but with the presence of its being. The Filter's gaze follows. It's pointing to the sky. The Filter's specialized eyes, which see the pressure of the rain, look at the cloud ceiling. For the first time in its existence, it sees something new. The pressure is not uniform. It is not that the whole sky will rain soon. It's that this *side* of the sky, the *other* side of the plateau, will rain soon. And rain with a force that has never been known before. It will be a rain that will not feed the Veins but tear them open, that will not quench the moss but pull it from the bone of the world.

The vision is not terrifying; it is accompanied by a profound sense of calm. The entity is not showing her this to cause fear. It is asking her a question, by showing her the answer to its premise.

The Filter's mind, which moves only a little faster than the moss itself grows, understands. The Listeners hear the heartbeat of the world. They have never heard the *breath* of it.

She leaves her channel. She does not need to tell her kin to observe; they are already unsettled by the light she is seeing so clearly in the water's reflection. She walks to the highest hummock, where the Listeners are rooted.

The Listeners do not speak to her. They are deep in their communion with the world's core. But as she approaches, leaning her body against their warm, mossy pillar, a subtle change occurs in their ancient posture. Their heads, heavy with lichen hair, tilt slightly. The rhythm of their humming shifts.

They are listening, but not to what she is saying with her hands. They are listening to what she has brought with her—the feeling of the light, the vision of the storm. It is not in her; it is *around* her.

The new frequency enters their chorus. For a moment, the world's hum falls out of tune. A discord in the drone of millennia.

Then, it re-aligns.

The Listeners have heard the storm. The Listeners have heard the Witness. The new information is now part of their world.

The entity of light does not act. It does not build a dam or raise a wall. It simply provided a new note. The moss-folk will now build their own harmony.

The Filters cease their work in the Veins. At the direction of the Listeners, whose hands now sign with a slow, deliberate purpose that has not been seen in generations, they begin to move to the far edge of the plateau. They are no longer Filters, but Builders. They use their dense bodies, not to filter water, but to divert it. They work themselves into the channels of the earth where they meet the white edge of the world, forming themselves into living banks and gentle spillways.
