They call me a traveler, but that’s never been true.

I arrive where I am needed and not where I want to be.

This roof is one of those places.

---

Nothing grew here to be a garden, but it is one now: a garden of engine and equilibrium. The mesa beneath your feet is a parasol over the inferno of a world‑factory older than empires. The heat the factories below cannot use leaks up through polished glass and has been polished by windless convection into a smooth, thin, furnace skin.

This skin is hurting you.

You don’t belong inside this temperature—not yet arranged, not yet insulated into any pattern that can carry you. You were made to walk water‑worlds, where your pores are rivers and your lungs are clouds. You’re a wrong‑architecture standing on the roof of a machine that wants to use you as mortar.

And I…

I am the one who comes to turn wrong
