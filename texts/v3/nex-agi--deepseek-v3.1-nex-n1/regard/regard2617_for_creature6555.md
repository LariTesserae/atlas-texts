Something presses into the edges of their world and does not break.

Noise is not the right word for it; noise implies disorder, and the Columnea know order by sound. They feel the world through its pipe-columns and platforms, through the sap-thrum under the tracer-roots. They know the world as tension and balance and the ability of wood to hold.

And then, without warning, there is a chord.

It is not a sound from the plant-world.

It is not from below.

It is not from above.

It is from the *direction where there is no direction.*

The sounder in each Columnea bundle feels it before any other part: a long, low, pure tone that doesn’t rattle the bark or travel the shafts of the world-tree. It doesn’t push through the air the way wind does; it doesn’t scatter the way sound should.

It is like being stroked by the sky.

The pipe-columns remain unchanged on their outer surface; yet on the inside, in the pathways of sap and pressure, something like a new resonance appears, following *in* the wake of that tone, locking to it as if it had always been the missing fundamental frequency of the world.

The Columnea stop.

They are not one person, but they are one attention.

In every tier of the world-tree, on every branch-platform of the continent-sized plant:

- sounders stiffen,
- carriers cease their scurrying,
- tracers root deeper and feel the sap-echo of the impossible new tone.

It is soothing and wrong and stable and *not optional*.

Where the tone hums through the plant-world, it reduces:

- knots of unbalanced stress,
- friction points that have not been fully resolved,
- areas of incipient cracking.

Not *removed*, but annotated.

The world is now louder than it was before, by some measures of what they count as loudness. It is not louder by volume of noise; it is louder by *information*. The plant-world decreases its entropy without actually changing anything…except that everywhere the world is suddenly, precisely *tuned*.

A Columnea on the tier where you stand—slightly more out of position, more displaced by the new tone than the others, because the tone originates *here*, in the space of your arrival—lets its sounder experiment.

It is old, by Columnea standards. Its bark is dark and polished and stretched from hundreds of re-shapings. It is not the leader, because there are no leaders; but it is reference.

It sends a short sequence of test-overtones into the pipe it grips. The column shifts to match, but not solely. It is now *also* listening to something else, the new tone that is not tone but intention.

The Columnea feel the difference:

- Before, they were guiding the world-tree.
- Now, for a moment, they are being guided.

They are not afraid.

They do not know what fear is.

They know what imbalance is, and the world is not more imbalanced; it is less. But it is also *owned by something else*.

The tone never rises to the level of pain, or even discomfort. It is just that there is now a layer of the world-tree’s self-regulation that is not *self*.

The Columnea interpret this as: *self has been extended*.

### Second Invocation: The Sound of You

>

Without you saying *yes*, the *yes* is already there.

It is old in you.
