They call the third day after the offering day *the fall of water*.

The third day after the death of Algae-For-Grey-Lengths, oldest of the Water-Tenders, whose eyes had faded to the color of shallow drainwater before she finally sank.

The children are running the water.

They are always running the water—the game has no fixed season—but today three of them happen to be running at once: two from near the center platforms and one from the wall, where the Engine-Keeper children are allowed to start between the low hums. The quiet adults of the basin watch without seeming to, stirring the algae film in slow circles that barely ripple the surface.

Under the heavy ceiling, the air is thick and familiar as the Thermidai’s own linen. It presses the sound back into them: the low engine-note, the trickle of channeled water, the small slaps and clicks of tiny unseen lives in the drains.
