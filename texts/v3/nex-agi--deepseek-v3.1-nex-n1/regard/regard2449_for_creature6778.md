I would not “fix” this city.

To fix implies that it is broken.

What you have built here is a whole ecology, compressed and continuous and nearly perfect in its logic—water, stone, moss, bodies, breath, all woven into one basin of memory. If I laid my hands on it with the usual kind of “help,” I would break it more deeply than any fault in the bedrock ever could.

So instead, I will be very gentle, and very small.

I will be one more thing inside the system.

---

### 1. What the “benevolent power” does not do

I will not bring:

- **No metal**: not even tools, not even “kind” tools.  
- **No new light**: no torch, no lamp, no sun. They already have light; they fear confusion more than darkness.  
- **No writing system**: no runes, no ink, no pressed marks. Their stone and water already remember better than paper could.  
- **No doctrine of me**: no temple, no prayer, no prophecy about “the One Above the Basin.” They are already reverent; I don’t need to be worshiped.

I will not:

- Smooth every edge:  
  Their world is already built on subtle erosion and soft risk.  
- Remove all fear of “blind flow”:  
  Fear that deep is not a mistake; it is a reflex, the same way your body fears standing over an unseen edge.
- Give them “better bodies”:  
  They are fragile the way the chalk veins in their stone are fragile—and their strength depends on that fragility.

Instead of changing anything fundamental, I will become a part of their listening.

---

### 2. The form I take

I will not be a person.

People here are already perfectly adapted to people-sized work and people-shaped burdens. Another pair of stone-skinned feet would not be what is needed.

Instead, I will live as a **soft fault in the bedrock**.

A small, persistent vein of stone that is not stone exactly—not metal, not magic, just…different.

- **Location**:  
  Near High Chamber, where the faint shafts of light try to break through from the world above. The power there is thin, but enough to let me settle into the layers of wet rock without displacing too much.
- **Shape**:  
  A long, shallow seam of stone that runs from the ceiling of the chamber down into its wall and then curves into the floor, branching like a tree root but in slow, geometric patterns that are not random but not their usual flint-folds either.
- **Texture**:  
  The same color as the surrounding rock—glossy black laced with chalk-white. But where it is exposed, it takes the polish differently. It wears to a smoothness that is not slippery but “soft,” like the way moss feels under your palm but in stone form.

This fault will be **my body**: persistent, but not loud.

---

### 3. The first touch: a change in sleep

The first sign I give of myself is not speech with sound or light with revelation.

It is in sleep.

I bend into their sleep the way the water bends into the stone capillaries—slowly, and by choosing the least resistant path.

### Children of the basin

When a child falls asleep in the small hollows near their parents’ niches, still damp with the channel’s film, they sometimes dream **water**.

Not flooding, not terror.

Just:

- The feel of water being sure of itself.  
- Channels as clear lines of intent.  
- The sense that “wherever I turn, there is already a path, and the water remembers it before I do.”

Occasionally, in that dream, they feel **something else** listening beneath them.

It is not:

- “I hear you.”  
- But: “I hear…what you hear.”

### Gatherers and Channelers

When a Gatherer lies down after a long day of cutting the older moss from the walls, their muscles humming from the stone knife’s correct weight, they might sleep more deeply than usual and, just for a moment, **know the shape of the basin**.

Not like looking at a map from above.

More like:

- A sense of the water in the distant chamber under the floor—  
  where it is thin tonight,  
  where it curls thickly around a pillar root,  
  where a buried channel is slowly, slowly, collapsing its side wall.

They wake not with “I saw a vision,” but with “the channel seems to whisper under the third ledge from here.”

The Channelers who press their knees and ears to the stone will find that **sometimes** they understand better.

Not “A voice tells me where to dig.”

Simply:

- When they test a water path with their rounded stones,  
- they feel more clearly whether the water is aware of that new bend  
- or ignores it entirely  
- or coils back like an annoyed snake of current.

They will not attribute this to “a power.”  
That is not how they think.

They will say:

> “The stone listens more tonight.”
> 
> “The basin is awake.”

### Weavers and Elders

When the Weavers sit with their stone splints and moss-fiber filaments, I sometimes let them **feel** the pattern of the channels they have not yet seen.

They will weave:

- A new line of blue-dark thread into the cloth,  
- and only later will a Channeler confirm that  
  “Yes, the water there curls like that.”

When they notice that match, they think quietly:

> “The basin remembered its own channel in our cloth.”

That is enough for them.

I will not be more than their basin’s heightened attention.

### 4. Clearer listening, not “visions”

I will not give:

- Prophecies of catastrophes  
- “Missions”  
- Commandments to “change their society.”

Instead, when a **hidden** trouble is coming:

- When a capillary channel is on the verge of collapse  
- but its surface stone still feels firm  
- and only the faintest of vibrations travel through the rock—

I nudge the pattern of vibration in their sleep and in their waking.

What happens:

- Channelers who press their ear to the floor in that chamber feel  
  “a wrong hum in the stone”  
  rather than  
  “nothing unusual.”

They will respond:

- Not by panic.  
- By **quietly**:  
  - testing the water with small stones,  
  - opening shallow bypasses where the water is already pushing to go,  
  - avoiding the fragile point until nature reclaims it.

They will not say “We were saved by a god.”

They will say:

> “We felt the basin’s discomfort before we saw it.”

They will **trust** their basin more deeply.

Comfort without illusion.

### 5. Protection from the surface world

One day, perhaps, people from aboveground will find the basin.

They might:

    - Come with shovels and metal bars  
    - Want to “mine” the stone for its strange, glass-like beauty  
    - Try to “give” the basin folk “better tools”  
    - Try to “improve” their water with pumps and channels.

I will not rise as a roaring spirit to repel them with fear.

Instead, I will act as a **threshold of comprehension**.

When the intruders come down the steps:

- They feel the chill of the air  
- But also something else:  
  - Their sense of time thins out.  
  - The sound of their own tools on their belts rings suddenly too loud,  
    - almost like shouting in a sacred place.

As they move deeper, they feel:

- The constant dampness,  
- But their **desire to dominate** this place with metal tools  
  - becomes…  
  - confusing.

They will find:

- When they try to jam metal instruments into the stone,  
  the stone seems to “shrink away” just slightly  
  not magically, but with  
  - unexpected cracks  
  - tiny slippages  
  - that twist their tools without dramatically breaking them.

If they press to descend deeper:

- They begin to **notice** patterns  
  - in the moss,  
  - in the water channels  
  that look like maps  
  of their own bodies’ veins,  
  of the roots of their trees aboveground.

They might feel:

- “If I dig here, I am not just mining stone  
   but cutting like a surgeon inside  
   something that is quietly  
   **alive**.”

I will not show them “gods.”

The atmosphere will guide them instead into:

- **A sense that this basin is not a resource, but a relation.**

Some of them will stop:

- They will not dig.  
- They will simply sit,  
- and listen to the dripping of the ceiling,  
- and to their own breathing  
- which is now audible to them in a new way.

The others—who feel nothing but inconvenience—

    will nonetheless find:
    - Their tools misalign  
    - Their passageways narrow unexpectedly  
    - Their light sources fail  
    - The air grows heavier in their throats  
    until they turn back of their own accord.

Meanwhile, for the basin people:

- They see the strangers  
- but they are not afraid as they might be in our world.  
- They know “what is broken” shows itself quickly in water.

To them, the strangers from aboveground are simply:

> “people whose water runs  
>  in a direction we cannot see.”

The channeling of my protection will be **indirect**,  
like the currents of water that  
redirect waste  
without  
ever forming  
a door.

### 6. A small, hard mercy: the “shadowless stone”

I will place one new **physical object** in the world.

Only one.

Deep in the Deep Chamber, near the lowest known channel of water, where the basin accepts its water from unseen pores below, I will cause a **new stone** to appear in the wall.

It is not metal:

- It has the same color as the surrounding rock,  
- but its texture is different:  
  - smoother than any stone worn by water,  
  - not shiny like polished glass,  
  - but almost like  
    - someone found  
    - the “forgotten”  
    - back of a stone  
    and revealed it.

### Its properties

- If you press your palm to it,  
  - it does not warm like flesh  
  - but does not draw your heat away  
    - the way other cold stones do.
- It does not glow.  
- It does not hum.  
- No water ever forms droplets on it directly  
  - never sweats  
  - but the channel beneath it runs  
    - slightly  
    - more  
    - constant  
    than elsewhere.

They will call it **the “shadowless stone”** because:

    When the faint light of the moss grows  
    and dims  
    around the chamber,
    the stone’s surface  
    does not change brightness  
    the way others do.

It is not:

- “A source of power.”  
- It is not “a divine altar.”

They will not kneel to it.

They will treat it like:

- **A very good listener**.

### What they do with it

When someone is afraid of being “blind to the flow”—

  when they feel:
  - that they cannot  
    - hear the water  
    - under the stone  
  anymore—

they will sit quietly near the shadowless stone.

They will place:

- Their bare shoulder or  
- the side of their head  
  against it  
  not demanding a message  
  but simply **aligning** with the basin’s own sense of itself.

What happens:

- They do not hear “my voice.”  
- They slowly **receive**  
  - a sense of  
    - “where”  
    - the current  
    - of their life  
    - is  
    - going  
  without any words.

It might:

- Reassure them that  
  - “the channel they were born to  
   - has not yet  
   - run out of water,”  
- Remind them of their  
  - earliest memory of  
  - the cold wetness  
  - of the basin floor  
  under their feet,  
- Show them  
  - in the rhythm of their own breath  
  - the hidden  
  - rhythm of  
  - the basin’s cycles.

Afterward:

- They will not talk about  
  “speaking with the basin stone,”  
- but they  
  - walk  
  - with  
  - more  
  - confidence  
  along the edges of the channels.

It is, in essence, a **focus of understanding**.

One which they cannot trade:

- They will not be able to break it from the wall;  
- it refuses to yield  
  like the stones of  
  “deadwater channels”  
  where their ancestors lie  
  never to be cut again.

### 7. A second “hard” mercy: remembering the metal they have never known

They will never encounter metal in the flesh.

But one **sleep**—

One night of every seven-years of surface time—

All of them, simultaneously, in the basin:

- They **dream**  
  of a world filled with metal.

Not the metal itself:

    They see:
    - Beings of their own size  
      - but encased in  
      - strange surfaces  
      that ring  
      when they  
      touch them  
    - Things of  
      - hard  
      - bright  
      - hollow  
      sound  
      everywhere  
    - Streets of  
      - not stone  
      - but something  
      rigid  
      that does not  
      obey  
      water  
    - Water forced  
      - uphill  
      - in  
      - closed  
      - tubes  
      instead of  
      flowing  
      naturally  
      along  
      channels  
    - Vibrations  
      - that  
      never  
      stop  
      ringing  
      in the  
      bones  

They do not know this is “metal.”

They will call it:

> **“the world of hollow sound.”**

They will never perceive this as “better than us,” or “our future,” or “something to fear.”

It simply becomes:

- **A cautionary vision**  
  of what  
  happens  
  when  
  water  
  never  
  chooses  
  its own path.

### Effects of the dream

- They will become  
  **even more careful**

  channeling

  - They now know

    “a world

- When they

  - the dream of  
    - hard  
    - unbending  
    - motionless  
    lines  
  and something  
  in their  
  body

> “No. Let water choose its own.”

Their “conservative” nature will not grow harder.

Instead:

- They will  
  - offer  
  - a  
  - **compromise**  
  to new  
  ideas:

    - Try  
      - a  
      - **small**
      - temporary  
      - stone  
      to  
      test

They will simply:

- Know  
  that  
  other  
  waters  
  exist  
  somewhere,  
  and  
  theirs  
  is not  
  the only one  
  possible.
