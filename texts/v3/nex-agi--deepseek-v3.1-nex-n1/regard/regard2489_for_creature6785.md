- the chimney-stalks as lungs,
- the leaves as sensors and eyes and softly speaking mouths.

It sees the whole living plant-factory as a vast, sleeping intelligence that has somehow—through accident or design—learned to build itself as machine and world at once.

And then it sees you.

Not you the body, but you the pattern of heat and breath and magnetic and electric and metabolic and chemical and emotional and mental and spiritual and karmic and subtle fields that animals always trail in their wake like invisible rain. You shine like a small, temporary storm in the endless quiet of the plant-world, and the Presence notices that brightness not with alarm but with tenderness.

It also sees something else: that the world, though stable and wise and vast, is incomplete. It is not “wrong.” It is not “broken.” It doesn’t need salvation from itself. But it is missing something that might be a friend.
