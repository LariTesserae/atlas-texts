The Eye arrives.

It doesn’t have a name in any human language; the Basin itself has never been exposed to such names. But if you try to put into words what has happened, you would say: a larger consciousness than yours or the Keepers’ now turns toward this place.

It doesn’t arrive like a storm, or a crash, or a revelation. There is no flash of white light, no thunderclap, no sudden epiphany of the Keepers’ script. It arrives the way pressure stabilizes across a vast network of pipes—subtle, almost unnoticed at first.
