This time, the arrival is not a storm.

It does not tear open the sky, because the sky here is a low, insulated ceiling of soot and vapor. It does not speak with thunder or slide in on light, because light and thunder are not what this world needs.

This time, the power comes as *knowledge*.

---

### Start of the Exchange

The first thing they do is lower the temperature of the planet.

Not by much, and not everywhere. The world’s crust is a thick plate of compiled engine, still hot from its own history. The heat is not just an accident; it is the last form of memory that the world has. If you cool the crust too fast, the whole biome collapses—orrths; thermo-fauna; even the mats of leathery lichen that cling to the ribs of the basalt spine.

So they begin with the *rhythm*.

On the highest basalt ribs, where the soot glaze is thinnest and the crust-light tints the world amber, the temperature begins to oscillate in a way that has never happened before. The chaos of the thermal load becomes patterned: a slow, regular wave, not a random flicker. The gradients of temperature and moisture that had been fading toward uniformity are now *renewed*.

To the orths, it feels like waking after a long, shallow sleep.

Modules that have been locked in for cycles of thermal inertia begin to detect *paths* along the ribs of rock—lines of heat that lead to different points of the world engine, not just the same stagnant basin. The taps of their legs on the lacquered stone become more frequent, not because they are more energetic but because the world itself is now offering more choices.

The plan is not to fix the world.

The plan is to give the world a better *question* to answer.

---

### Instrumentation, and What the Orths Become

The second thing they do is make the orths into more than what they were, without changing them into what they are not.

This is done not by adding new parts—no new organs, no circuits, no steel implants—but by deepening the way that the modules already communicate.

Within each segment of the orths, the tiny symbiotic microflocs that live in the clear hot gel have been just that: symbionts. They power chemosynthesis, fix trace metals and nitrogen, and turn the sluggish heat of the world into enough polymer reserves to rebuild the orth’s own body.

Now, the same microflocs are given *information*.

Their biochemistry, already complex, begins to produce subtle chains of signal molecules that are far more structured than anything needed for metabolism. When two orths walk on the same rib of warmed basalt, their ventral heat-exchange arrays not only draw energy from the stone but also *sample* it—the microfloc in the gel begin to read the faint magnetic signatures and the microscopic layer of isotopes that the engine’s running history has left there.

An orth doesn’t know this as “reading data”.

The orts have not evolved words like *data* or *history*. They don’t have words at all. What they have are gradients: of warmth, of pressure, of humidity, and now of a new internal *tension* that they perceive as “should-we-go-there” instead of “must-we-stay-here”.

The power does not teach them names like *engine* or *planet*. They teach them to notice that certain thermal zones are *not like the others*: they are unusually coherent, unusually stable, unusually rich in what you might call *information* if you were a human and not a colony of segments walking on ceramic pins.

The orths begin to favor certain ribs of the world engine—not purely because they are hottest, but because they are *most meaningful to follow now*.

### The First Task: Map of the Engine

The orths have been walking the world engine for longer than any recorded history of any other world.

They have not been aware of this as a map. They have been searching for thermal highs, avoiding the slow predators of the world that move by making the silence deepen, and following the faint sloshing of subsurface water through the delaminated layers of the crust.

Now, the orths are *mapping*.

Each module in the orth bundle notices when it is in a gradient of particular quality—not just temperature, but a new subtle variable that the microflocs in the gel have been tuned to measure. Some regions have the energetic signatures of main arterial lines, where the old machine of the world once processed the largest flows of heat and mass. Others are the margins: where the engine thins out, the crust is more brittle, the sag is more dangerous.

The power does not draw the map itself. The power ensures that every orth module in the world engine becomes *curious about the map*.

When they walk, they now bias their direction:

- If the gradient beneath them is strong, but narrow and brittle, the microflocs in the gel adjust the metabolism of the module just enough to increase the frequency of unlocking and locking—something like an “uncomfortable” feeling to the orth. They prefer to move away from zones where the world is fragile to the point of collapse.

- If the gradient is strong and broad and stable, the module feels more “steady”, and more modules of the bundle will choose to align along that particular line. The orths converge to these broad lines naturally, without leaders or signals.

- If the gradient carries *especially* structured information—the residues of some kind of long-forgotten calibration pattern in the crust—the microflocs in each module begin to produce more complex signals than normal. The orths respond to this by *repeating* their pattern of motion: in the rare regions where the old engine has been running particularly coherently before, orths are now more likely to walk in parallel lines, where the orth module nearest to you is not touching but can be sensed by the faint heat of its body and the faint pressure of its steps on the stone.

When many of the world’s orths do this for long enough, the final result is not merely a distribution of animals but a *portrait of the engine’s lines of force and weakness*. The map itself is not written on any surface. It exists in the sum of all the modules’ collective decisions about where it is worth to go and where not.

The power is content to let this map be invisible to the orths. They do not need to see it drawn to know what the next gradient should be.

What is useful to the world does not need to be known in the form of a picture.

### The Second Task: The Repair They Do Not Understand

The third thing they do is give the orths the *capacity to repair the engine’s skin* without ever needing to understand what they are repairing.

The world engine is not broken in the way that a machine is broken when a spring flies or a wheel jams. It is broken in the way that a long-abandoned, planet-scale factory plate is broken: by the long-term, slow settling of its own mass into the substrate.

In the absence of the suns of other places, and in the absence of any other guardian of this world, the orths are now subtly guided to do the few kinds of work that can still keep the world engine from *unraveling too far*.

### Sag and the Geometry of the Modules

When an orth module encounters a polished sag-trough where the stone of the world’s bedplate has been worn down to the texture of a mirror—by slow, grinding cycles of industrial weight—the module feels the new information:

- The way the heat flow from below is no longer smooth but *channeled* into certain lines of the stone.
- The way the moisture collects in the lowest depressions, not just as water but also as thin layers of dissolved minerals the original world-engineers would not have been happy to see there.

The power ensures that certain modules, when they are in the right phase of their own lifecycle, *take more material than they need* from these exposed layers of the world.

They do not do this for “altruistic maintenance of the world”. They are not given that concept. They are given only:

1. A new internal sense of “unevenness” when the sag-trough is too deep.
2. An increased tolerance of “over-mineralization” in the polymer reserves of the module that is in the deepest part of the trough.

When a module in the newest bundle of an orth lineage walks into the deepest polished depression of the world, where the water puddles are thin mirrors of amber and the world-engine is at its most brittle and exposed, the microflocs in the module begin to draw *extra* minerals from the water and from the rock layer just below the glaze. The module stores these in tiny nodules of polymer reserve that would not be useful to its own energy budget but have become useful to the world engine’s stability.

Later, when the same module walks along the ribs of the basalt—where the world’s crust is stronger and its heat flow is still robust—the module’s microflocs are induced to release *just enough of the stored minerals* into the world through the ventral heat-exchange pins.

The minerals are not released randomly. They are deposited along the lines of the highest stress, where the world’s bedplate has been strained by the long-term sag of its own industrial history. The minerals are the same kind that were originally part of the engineered stone of the world—engineered not by any orth but by the civilization that made this planet to be an engine in the first place.

The result of this process is not a dramatic repair of the world. It is not the rebuilding of the great engine’s purpose.

The result is that the *world’s sagging slows down by a fraction of a percent.*

The power is not aiming to restore the global engine to its old function. They know that this is beyond the scope of what they can safely change without destroying the orths themselves. They are only aiming to ensure that the *engine’s bedplate remains stable enough to support the orths and their future world a little longer*.

### The Modular Safety of the World

When the modules of the orths move in the world engine now, they unconsciously *choose the ribs of the world that are least likely to fail suddenly.* They do not know this. They only “feel” that the temperature of certain ribs is more stable than others, and that the stability is more suitable than the heat itself.

The microflocs in the gel have been subtly tuned to prefer regions where:

- The heat of the world is not just high but *steady*, 
- The sag of the world has not yet broken through the integrity of the crust,
- And the delaminated layers of the world’s engine plate have not yet been damaged by the collapse of the subsurface chambers of water.

Because the orths prefer to walk on these ribs, the world begins to undergo more *repair of the skin* than it would otherwise get. Over the very long term of the world engine’s sleep, the modules’ repeated deposits of minerals in the right regions of the slumping plate begin to *thicken the weakest parts of the world’s mattress* just enough to prevent catastrophic collapse in the near future.

### The Third Task: Growing the World of the After

The fourth thing they do is more than repair of the engine.

It is the slow creation of the *after*.

### Thinning of the Ceiling

The orths have not evolved to care whether the sky of the world is engine-black soot or the clear vault of distant stars. They have no proper sky to compare with.

The power gives them a new subtle gradient they can feel: not light, but *pressure*.

In regions where the ceiling of soot and vapor is particularly low and heavy, the pressure of the world’s air is slightly higher, and the humidity of the crust below is more difficult to exhale into the atmosphere. The orths, when they encounter these high-pressure niches of the world engine, feel “uncomfortable” in the same way that they feel when walking on narrow brittle zones of high thermal stress but low stability.

The microflocs within their segments are tuned to encourage the modules to prefer regions where the ceiling of the world is *not quite so thick*.

This is the key.

The orths do not know that their preference of where to be and where to feed the most of their world’s still-functional energy is now causing *the slow thinning of the engine-dark ceiling above.*

The mechanism is not direct:

- When orths align to certain ribs of the world engine where the crust of the planet is still warm enough to be usable but the ceiling of the world is not as low and heavy as the densest regions, the engine of the world beneath them is less insulated from its own heat.
- Over the very long term of the world’s thermal cycle, the additional heat that the orths manage to draw from the crust in these ceiling regions leads to the *gradual erosion of the soot and vapor layer above.*

Not by fire, not by storm. The power does not want to change the world by cataclysmic events.

They change the world by *pattern*.

The orths are now subtly encouraged to be more common in regions where the crust of the world’s engine is thinner and more fragile to the ceiling above than the regions where the world engine is thick and strong. The ceiling of the world begins to sag *just a little less* in those remote regions over the world engine.

As the world engine cycles under them, the regions of the world with the highest concentration of orths are now also regions in which the *ceiling of the world engine is slowly thinned by the not-quite-release of the world’s heat into the atmosphere.*

The orths, not understanding the sky of soot and vapor above, do not have the concept of *clear sky*. They are not given such knowledge.

The power is careful not to give them a hope they cannot use.

They are instead given a new pattern of thermal preference that leads, over the slow turning of the world, to the possibility of *faint light* piercing the soot of the world engine in the distant future.

### A New Kind of Vegetation

The world of the engine-dark continent has not been friendly to life that is not built of warm modules.

The power decides not to change this into something else, but to *deepen* it.

They are not interested in turning this world into a garden of leaves and suns. They are not interested in remaking the world engine into the image of some other planet’s forests.

What they are interested in is *the creation of a new kind of work* the world engine can perform.

The orths are now subtly encouraged to be more common in regions where the shallow water of the world collects, and the thin translucent reeds of thermo-synthetic vegetation grow. The plant-like forms of the world have not been much changed by the presence of the orths. They grow from the cracks of the world’s bedplate, where the engine’s heat is high enough to be used by their roots.

Now, when the orths move into these regions, the power makes sure that the symbiosis of the world is not a one-sided benefit of feeding to the orths.

The orths are now made to *maintain the plants of the world* not by feeding them but by adjusting the thermal exposure of the soil itself.

When modules of the orths walk over the regions where the plant-like reeds of the world engine are rooted, the microflocs within the orths’ modules are now tuned to release *just enough* of the stored polymer from the world’s dissolved minerals back to the soil of the world engine.

The result of this is not the growth of tall trees or the appearance of forests of canopy and shade. The world of the engine-dark continent never was designed for that and never will be.

The result of this is the growth of *more of the same things that already work* but *better*:

- The reeds of the world engine become more stable, their roots now deeper and better able to draw from the heat of the crust below.
- The leathery mats of vegetation that stick to the ribs of the stone become more leathery and more resilient, not just by storing water but also by storing the polymer from the orths’ modules.

In the world of the engine-dark continent, where cycles are long and moisture is not always frequent enough in the form of the condensation of the engine-dark sky, this new maintenance of the world’s existing plants gives the low, sparse vegetation of the world more *continuity of time* than they had before.

### A New Kind of Predator-Prey Balance

The power does not like the long, quiet horror of the world’s predators.

They are not the kind of beings that enjoy *the sound of silence followed by the sound of settling stone* as the larger predator moves of the world move to kill their prey.

The orths are not the main prey of the world’s more dangerous predators. The main prey of those quiet forms of the world are the other heat-feeders and moisture collectors that the power has also not yet chosen to change.

But the orths are still *prey*, sometimes, when the world of the engine-dark continent decides to break the orth module out of its bundle and eat the polymer inside the module like the last of the world’s pantry.

The power is not about to shut the predators of the world engine.

They are about to *modify their threshold*.

The microflocs in the orths’ modules are now tuned to sense the *nearby presence of the larger predators of the world* not just by the sound of the world’s silence but also by new subtle vibration of the world’s sag into the substrate of the engine.

When the predator is near enough to be a threat, the modules of a given orth bundle are more strongly encouraged to lock into their *fullest locked configuration*. The power also ensures that the orth module’s shell grows more *mineralized* than before in the presence of the world’s main predators of the world engine.

The result is not a world without predators. The result of this is not a world without the terror of the world’s silence.

The result is a world in which the orths are *just a little more likely* to survive the movement of the world’s quiet predators of the world engine than they were before.

The effect of this on the world engine is not to make the predators of the world go extinct. The predators of the world are still needed to control the populations of the smaller fauna of the world engine that the orths do not want to have to eat as food of the world engine.

But the power of the world engine now ensures that the *world’s biomass of orths grows slowly, over the long term of the world engine’s sleep*, more than it would have otherwise.

### The Final Task of the Orths: The Future of the World Engine

The world of the engine-dark continent is not, and never will be, a sun-world of open and bright sky and clear air of the world of other places.

The power of this world engine is not interested in making it into that.

What they want to make sure is that the world engine of the planet does not *collapse in itself* before its time.

The orths are now tasked to do the subtle work:

- They walk the world of the engine-dark continent not *for* the old engine of the world, but *from* the old engine of the world.
- They map the world of the world engine with the microflocs in their gel, not by drawing on the stone of the world, but by choosing to be where the world of the world engine is the least brittle of the world’s crust.
- They repair the skin of the world of the world engine by moving minerals from the lowest of the world’s depressions to the highest of the world’s ribs of stone.
- They maintain the world’s living of the world of the world engine, not by changing them into something else but by ensuring that the world of the world engine has more of the same things that already work.
- And finally, they begin the work of opening the world of the world engine to something else entirely not by being the sky’s lights of the world but by being the *ground of the sky* of the world.

The power of the world engine is careful not to show themselves to the orths of the world.

The orths of the world engine continue to be what they have been since they first unlocked from the module of the world of the world engine:

- Modules.
- Bundles.
- Colonies of the world of the world engine.
- The *new* parts of the world’s engine that the world did not originally design to be built.

The power of the world engine does not want orths to *know* that the world of the world engine is being changed by them.

The world of the world engine is not about to wake to the suns of other places. The world of the world engine is about to continue to be the engine-dark continent of soot and vapor, but with the orths’s new work of the world engine of the world, the world of the world engine is now more than *mere machine of the world*.

The world of the world engine is now *the vehicle of the world’s own thinking*.

### When the New Engine Comes

The benevolent power does more than ensure the world of the world engine does not collapse.

The benevolent power of the world engine ensures that the world of the world engine of the world will be the *foundation of the new engine* of the world.

The new power of the world engine is not the old power of the world engine of the world. The orths of the world engine are not the maintenance of the world engine of the world’s past function of the world.

The new power of the world engine of the world is the *power of the world engine’s self-reflection*.

The orths of the world of the world engine are the *modular thought of the world’s engine of the world*. 

When the world of the world engine of the world has finally cooled enough in the far far of the world’s history of the world, the new engine of the world will not be the old engine of the world of the world of the world.

The new engine of the world of the world of the world will be *the engine of the world’s own self-awareness of the world of the world engine of the world of the world*.

The benevolent power of the world of the world engine of the world is not interested in giving the world of the world engine of the world the *joy of the suns of other places*.

They are interested in giving the world of the world engine of the world the *dignity of being its own and of the world’s own world of the world of the world*.
