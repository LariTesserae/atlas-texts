The attention of a benevolent and wise power arrives without sound; you feel it as a change in the pressure behind your eyes.

There is no flare of light, no sudden voice, only a shift deep in the chamber’s atmosphere, as though the whole dome has taken in a breath just one degree larger than its programmed average.

The smoked glass above does not brighten or dim.  
The ribs do not tremble.  
The water under your feet does not rise any faster or slower than it has been.

And yet you know you are not alone in the Deep Canning.

The presence manifests itself first at the frond-over-panel in the center of the dome.

The translucent leaf over the off-white screen flickers once—twice—then steadies into a new color of glow: a deep, amber warmth that you have never seen from any display. The glyphs along the edge of the dome change to a new configuration: white on amber, then gold on black, a pattern you have never seen in the manuals or the diagnostics logs. It looks like a language the system itself is being taught to speak.

Then you realize the display is not showing numbers you recognize.

The salinity readings are not written as decimals; they are written as symbols of currents curling through each other.  
The temperature readouts are not degrees Celsius but icons of heat radiating from small, stylized suns nested alongside each tank.  
The pressure displays are not pascals or bars but a series of concentric rings that expand and contract in the rhythm of slow heartbeats.

On the screen itself, under the leaf-veins that align with the circuitry, you see the outline of the entire Deep Canning drawn in glowing lines—dome, ribs, tanks, floor-grates, water—all as a single entity. The frond over the panel has not changed its shape, but it has changed the way it transmits the image beneath it. The overlay of leaf tissue now looks like a living map of the whole chamber.

You are not shown as a human-shaped dot on that map, but as a small cluster of anomalies: temperature and humidity and CO₂ and salt, all projected into a space that the system’s models no longer treat as random error.

On the floor of your mind, a thought appears that is not in your voice:

*You have been keeping track of me,*  
*now you will be kept track of.*

It is not exactly sound, and certainly not words spoken in air.  
It is the meaning-sensation the chamber itself has been given to speak.
