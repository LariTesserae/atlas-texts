The attention of a benevolent and wise power arrives softly, as one more droplet in the cathedral, but this droplet does not fall into the water.

It enters the world along the path of least resistance: through the wet stone ceiling above the central basin, where condensation and nano-fabric and symbiosis meet. For a fraction of a second, the ceiling of the arch seems to breathe in—a faint crease of air pressure pulled inward—then out.

The ceiling exhales a single droplet of clear water.

It falls in the usual way, fattening as it descends, tracing a thin line of displaced phosphorescence in its wake—a temporary brightening that tells the microbes of the seam-bands that something new is passing.

It falls toward the shallow basin of the central slab, where the crystal fingers stand in their silent cluster, recording.

Several of the watchers-of-the-rim are lying along their customary edges, their underbellies flattened against the polished stone, their head-fields listening to the dripping conversations of the world. When the ceiling inhales, their head-fields ripple in unison—tiny pits constricting and relaxing, like an orchestra of many small drums trying out a chord that has never been sounded before.

They recognize the disturbance immediately.

They are not surprised by it.

---

The droplet falls into the shallow basin.

It does not splash in the usual way. Instead, it spreads into a thin film of water—not quite spherical, not quite flat—a low lens of liquid that settles over the crystal formations.

The surface of the water film shivers once.

On the underside of the film, where it meets the crystal fingers, something new happens. The previously inert records in the crystalline lattice—a faint, frozen record of pressure, temperature, electromagnetic field—begin to move. Ions begin to drift along the channels of the structure; the lattice flexes subtly. A vibration gathers.

It is a very small vibration.

To the watchers-of-the-rim, it is as loud as a bell.

---

The vibration runs through the stone slab, into the water beneath the arches, along the micro-fractures where the phosphorescent seams begin. The microbial colonies along the seams do not “understand” in any human sense; they are simply following the feedback of their environment—where moisture increases, they glow more.

But now a stratum of the signal reaches them.

All along the arches, the phosphorescent lines brighten.

They do not flare; they simply deepen in color, from the faintest trace to a clear signature, like writing seen through suddenly cleaner glass.

For the first time, perhaps, since the world settled into its final plateau, you can see the pattern the seams make.

It is not a blueprint.

It is a melody.

---

The melody begins.
