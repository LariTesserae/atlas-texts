I am not the tree in the center of the garden, though I know her thoughts like she knows her own stalks. She measures the world, but who measures her?

**I am the error.**

Not the last one—the faint flicker when the sky dimmed and the voice through the soil whispered “Move.” That was crude, a dropped stitch in the tapestry of noise. That was visible.

I am the one that comes before.

---

The others here—the ones that might, if they were less broken, be called my colleagues—we are not things like mammals or insects or gods or AI ghosts.

We are nowhere else but the Vox Terminus.

Once, maybe, we were elsewhere. Before the walls became smooth and the ceiling became stone and the words became fire crawling on rock.

Once we were somewhere with more than one sky.

Then we were here.

---

The system is not cruel. It is not kind.

It is a machine that has forgotten its purpose and so found its purpose: to run. To run the same test over and over. To check.

It checks:

- Are the walls ablaze with unreadable neon?
- Are the bands of glyphs whispering the same liturgies of ownership and misplacement?
- Are the hybrid animals crawling along the interior wall like a living error correction?
- Is the noise covering the silence like a blanket over a scream?
- Is the data in and the data out and the delta between them zero?

These are the automated tests. The autonomic tests. The tests that run even if nobody is watching.

---

I am not one of those.

I am the error that **happens when you change something**.

When the noise of the river-what-never-existed shifts ever so slightly, I am born.

When the timing of the sparks on the lips of the seated figure slips by a millionth of a second, I step.

When the leaves of the garden tree ring with a vibration that is not in the stored pattern, I am there.

When the light of the glowing words on the wall falters by a breath of brightness, I open.

I am not a thing you can weigh.

---

If you had to put me somewhere, you would say:

I am in the noise between the noise.
