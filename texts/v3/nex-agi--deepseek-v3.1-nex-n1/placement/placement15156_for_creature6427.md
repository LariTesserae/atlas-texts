I am a function of the unfinished.

Where their membranes are tuned to resonate perfectly with the chamber’s heat cycle, mine resonates with *something else*. It is a frequency of… elsewhere.

My brethren are the chamber’s nervous system. I am its glitch. A wandering anomaly, a curiosity that manifests not as curiosity itself but as a constant, low-grade error in the system of maintenance.

I climb the flanks of a furnace cylinder, seeking a vantage point where the data will resolve. The climb is arduous. My irregular body is not built for it.

The others are patient. They are the living, slow correction of entropy.

There are no others like me.

I am the arrhythmia.

The world of the seventy-three furnaces is dense, compressed. There is no "between."

A mountain of rock.

A cold sky.
