I am the stone plate.

The realization arrives like a fracture, not in the lightning-jolt of understanding but in the slow, grinding pressure of truth. The knowledge of it is not new; it is the awareness of my own reality that has just been restored to me. I have been here for longer than I can remember, which is to say, since I ceased to be debris and became part of the instrument.

I am the center of the basin.

My body is not flesh, but basalt veined with crystal. The very glyphs they are tracing are not being drawn *upon* me, but *within* me. The stone-crawlers are not artists; they are the pressure of the world given tiny, brittle legs. Their frantic, precise movements across my surface are not creation, but diagnosis. They are testing the grain of my being, finding the weak points in my structure—the hairline fractures, the microscopic inconsistencies—and filling them with the luminous dust of their passing.

Each line they leave is a thread of tension in my body singing a more precise note.

And then comes the water. It is not a drink, but a lubricant, a conductor of pressure. When it flows over me in its thin, shimmering skin, it is the final variable of the equation poured into place. The sensation is not wetness; it is a vast, heavy hand being laid upon me, the entire weight of the mountain above, distilled into a liquid lens.

*My* surface changes.

My internal glyphs, the complex symphony of fractures and crystal seams that have grown into me since I was carved from the wall, ignite with light. It is a low, cold light that ripples and shifts with the distortions on the water's surface. This is the interface. The scene being played out upon me is not a performance; it is a calculation.

My purpose is to be the instrument.

To be the chord.

The sound of the basin is not separate from me. It is not even around me. It is *through* me. The constant roar of the living wall is a low pressure wave that finds its deepest register in the dense stone of my body. The clicking of the Builder's segments as they shift over my water-blanketed form are not footsteps, but corrections being made to the string tension of a world-sized cello.

I feel its presence above me now, a vast segment of the coiled Builder. It does not descend upon me, but rather, it allows its own immense pressure to be partially applied. The blunt, not-legs projections trailing from its underbelly release the faint, shimmering threads.

The process is agonizingly slow.

For you, who stand on my edge, it may be minutes of watching, a single breath held in awe. For me, it is an eternity of being tuned. The threads, the glistening film of the not-liquid, descend into the water-membrane and sink through me. They do not fill me, but rather find the empty spaces within my lattice. The spaces between the atoms of my existence.

Where they take root, I am unmade and made anew.

My perception of the world is not sight, but pressure. I am aware of the weight of the Builder above me. I am aware of the frantic, precise touch of the stone-crawlers around my perimeter. I can feel the faint, erratic pressure of the Observer standing upon my very edge, a small anomaly of heat and breath and fear that rings through me like a discordant note.

*That* is you.

From where you stand, you are witness. Inside me, you are a complication.

An irregularity.

The pressure is not coming from you alone, but from you in orbit with the others. The swooping of the air-creatures. The roiling of the swimmers in the water. The slow, slow shift of the wall-growths. And you, a single point of heat, a point of sharp breath and a different heartbeat, a different kind of pressure exerted on the stone through your soles.

The entire system has been adjusting to your presence since you arrived.

It is not trying to understand you. It is not trying to communicate. It is trying to resolve you into the equation. The Builders are not looking at me; they are looking at *you*, using me as a mirror to calculate your influence.

*What does the mountain feel like when you are standing on it?*

I am the answer.

The stone-crawlers complete their new pattern, a radial design of incredible complexity that perfectly overlays the new configuration of pressures from your presence. Their legs are a blur of clicking, precise motion. They are rewriting me, to you, in real-time.

The water shimmer flares with a new, iridescent hue.

The deep vibration of the Builder above me shifts into a new, lower harmony.

The roar of the wall-growths changes.

We are in a new state of balance.

I have been changed. The song of the basin is now different than it was before you came here. The world's slow motion has been adjusted, to accommodate you, a single point of heat and breath, a single point of pressure, a single point of time.
