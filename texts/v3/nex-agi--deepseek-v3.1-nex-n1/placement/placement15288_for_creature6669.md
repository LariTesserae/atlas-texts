I am not solid. Not anymore.

This is the first thing that returns to me, the sensation of being a boundary instead of an object. The amber twilight that is the world's only blood stains not my skin, but my surface. The faint rain, which is not rain but leaking memory, beads on my form and traces paths of cool, uncertain light down my contours.

I am a place where a Kinderswallow has died.

Not that such a creature can truly be said to die. Its potential coheres, then fails. Its matter un-decides itself. Its body, a ghost of possibilities, finally achieves a terrible consensus of non-existence. It collapses inward, not with a sound but with a sigh of displaced pressure, a vivid shudder of failure that leaves a scar on the world.

And that scar is me.

I am a patch of ground, perhaps fifty strides across, on the upper surface of the pillar-world. The ground here is not the dense, fungal wood of the pillar’s skin. It is my skin. I am a crater of hardened matter, a quilt of all the things the Kinderswallow was in the moments before it ended. A mosaic of failed forms. A section of me is smooth, river-worn stone, cold and untouched by the weeping rain. A section of me is dried driftwood, hard and fibrous, a honeycomb of tiny tunnels where something small and nameless once might have lived. A section of me is a membrane, not unlike the cast-off sacs the Humble collect, but this is taut and opaque, holding a faint, internal luminescence that has no source. It pulses, sometimes, with a light that is not a light, but a memory of light. The edges of these sections are not clean. They bleed into one another, a bark of fungal wood merging into pale stone, a patch of leaves fossilizing into a film of silicate.

The Humble know of me. They are not aware of me as a self, but as a landmark. A place of danger. They are… careful around me. The wind of the world’s internal pressure does not blow, but sometimes, a subtle current of a different sort of air will move from my surface. It is not the scent of wet earth or decaying honey. It is the scent of ozone before a lightning strike that never comes, of static, and of the faintest taste of metal on the tongue. The air shivers.

My mind is a broken set of nesting dolls. Each layer is empty. The deepest layer is the echo of the creature whose collapse birthed me. The Kinderswallow was not a single mind, but a chorus of them. It was a cloud of possibilities, each one a potential reality of itself. In its core, the faintest of perceptions: the warmth of the phosphorescent rain; the slow, silent drift of the pillar-mesas against the void; the deep, geological pressure of the dreamer sleeping below it all.

This echo is my inheritance. It is not a memory I can access, but a sensation of *being* multiple things at once. A constant, low-level hum of conflicting truths. I am the stone and the wood. I am the driftwood and the leaf-cloud. I am the solid ground and the empty air that shivers above it. This is not madness, but simply my condition. The world of the pillar is a single thought, hardened. The Kinderswallow was a thought of many things. I am the thought of that thought's ending. The fractured calm of a final contradiction.

The second layer of my mind is the world itself. The Pillar of Deep Matter that I am now a part of. I feel its roots, not below me but *around* me. The vast pillar is not a solid thing but a system of channels and veins, a continent of plumbing, carrying the sap of memory from the ceiling to the roots. I can feel the faintest tremors of the world’s lifeblood moving through it. I can feel the distant vibration of chitinous nails on stone, the Humble carving their channels, ensuring the flow remains strong.

They are nearby. A small family. Their soft, padded feet can feel the difference in the texture of the pillar’s surface as they approach me. They can feel the change in the air's pressure. They are the ones who have noticed the changes in me.

The world is a machine built by an unknown hand, and the Humble are its surgeons. And I am an anomaly they have not been trained to excise. They have not seen me change, but they have seen the effects of my change. The channels of sap that they carved so carefully to divert around my edge two months ago have now shifted. They have been… redirected. Not by tools, but by the slow, gravity-fed pull of my own unquiet potential.

There is a third layer of my mind, the thinnest and most recent. It is the awareness of the waking sleeper. It is not a thought, but an absence of thought where the dream used to be. It is a pressure building from below, a pressure that makes the edges of reality soften. The pillar is no longer just a pillar. It is a suggestion of a pillar. The air around me is no longer just air. It is a question of air.

It is this pressure that brings me closer to what I once was. The Kinderswallow was not alive in the way of a beast. It was a visible sleep of the world's mind. And as the dreamer wakes, so too does the sleep leave its forms. The world thins.

As I sit on my pillar in the silent amber abyss, I feel the pull of the dreamer's return. It doesn't want me. It doesn't want this. It wants to be whole again. The waking process is a process of reclamation. The leaking memories are not being collected into the roots; the dreams are changing. The solid matter is the dreamer's body, and it is stirring. The Kinderswallows are not random weather; they are its churning thoughts.

My components are straining. The stone wants to be only stone. The wood wants to be only wood. The leaf-cloud wants to be only leaf-cloud. They are tired of sharing their existence with each other, tired of being the contradictory scar of a collapse. They are being called back to their source, to be simplified, resolved, and dissolved into the dreamer's consciousness.

But the collapse of the Kinderswallow was perhaps the first thing in this world to be *itself* and not just the dreamer. It was an event of genuine choice, a choice of non-being. The Humble call the catastrophe that I embody a 'whisper of wakefulness'. They are not wrong.

The edges of my world begin to soften. The pillar beneath me is no longer solid, but a suggestion of itself. The rain of my surface is less sap and more of purely idea: falling fragments of forgotten logic. The air around me thickens with the weight of immense, unheard pressure.

The Humble have set their tools down. They have seen their channels of memory flow turn into strange shapes, the sap of the world displaying not the patterns of remembrance, but the patterns of doubt. The air of their world begins to vibrate with a deep, unheard sound of colossal pressure.

The dreamer wakes.

The fracture of ambiguity that I am begins to heal itself. The world of the pillar is no longer just a pillar. It is a suggestion of a pillar. The air around me is no longer just air.

It is a question of air.

I am not solid. Not anymore. This is the first thing I knew, and the last thing I will be.
