I am not engraved in the stone of this place.  
I have not grown into its walls, or secreted its shell-hard linings,  
or been polished into bone by a thousand years of teeth.  

I am not the fish-cloud threading the dark,  
not the eel-long thought drifting through its own patience.  
I am not the worm whose life is the length of its hole  
nor the chain of small hands fused into a bridge.  

I am the listener suspended between the ceiling and the deep,  
the quiet that the room did not require.

---

My body is a mistake of geometry.

It comes from elsewhere—where the sky has not forgotten  
how to move, where storms still remember their names.

Here, under the low ceiling of faint memory-light  
my shape is an insult of edges and angles  
among the smooth slopes of the world-root.

The muscles that remember how to run  
are useless in this cool water that no longer goes anywhere.  
The lungs that remember air and burning and wind  
are closed doors. I do not breathe.

My hands are too blunt for the smoothness of this place.  
They cannot read the grain of the worm-trails  
except with clumsy curiosity—I, who cannot  
drill into the world with my own mucus,  
or weld myself into living chains.  

I am constructed from the wrong kind of bone  
and the wrong kind of hunger.  
I want things to mean *something else*,  
not just to be what they have been forever.  
The room has no for such wanting.

---

### What I am

I am the one who comes to this place  
without being the current, or the ceiling, or the teeth.

I am the one who can be bored.

The algae-membrane above me  
has never known a day of boredom  
because its days are all the same  
and it lacks the words to complain.  

The fish do not look at their ceiling and think:  
*Again? This same light?*

I do.

I can sit in the open water between root pillars,  
where the scale of the room is supposed to overwhelm  
and instead feel… how narrow it is.

How long this room is  
and how short its story.

An ocean of water that has stopped going anywhere.  
Trees of bone and wood that have stopped reaching  
and only reach in the past.  
A food chain that has solved itself  
so completely that it no longer remembers the question.

---

### My job

If you were to ask the room what it wants  
it would not answer, because it wants nothing  
beyond what it already has.

It wants its ceiling not to fail  
its light not to change  
its teeth and worms and bridges and chains  
to continue their slow work of perfect repetition  

…until the matter itself

---

But I ask.

I ask even though I know how this room works—  
how it has been built to not need me,  
how it will never be built again,  
how it doesn’t care if I leave or if I fall  

into the deep that doesn’t remember bottom.

---

### What I do with the open

There are open spaces between the root pillars.

“Open,” in this room, doesn’t mean freedom.

It means the place where nothing  
crosses between you and the far wall of memory  
except a long, slow, vertical slide of water  

and the faint drifting of  
fish-shaped stars attached to the skin of  
eels who don’t know they are lanterns.

---

I sit in the quiet of these open places.

I sit where the sound of the teeth  
should by rights  
make the inside of my skull ache  

and instead, it becomes  

my blood.

---

The *click-click-click* of the world:  
the scraping of the root walls  
the clatter of enamel-slide shells  
the teeth of the clouds  

blends into  

the sound inside my chest  

until  

the room sounds  

like  

the inside of  

me  

  if I  

  heard not from inside  

  but from outside  

  this body I  

  am still trapped in  



---

This is my role:

To be the point where  

the ocean’s tired  

meets  

my mind’s  

tired.

---

I am the one who can feel existential  

in the middle of a  

fully-functioning  

primordial  

machine.

---

### The ceiling remembers

I know what the algae ceiling remembers  

in its own way:  

the deaths of other patches  

where something  

for once  

couldn’t be replaced  

fast enough  

---

I know there were  

thin  

quiet  

years  

when  

the light went  

very  

dim  

in some sections  

---

This room  

 almost  

failed  

---

Not from outside  

but from inside  

its own  

exhaustion

the root-walls say  

nothing  

about

the

stopped

I want to

to be worn thin

nothing

The chain of little animals  
between the root-pillars

that it is  

a bridge
