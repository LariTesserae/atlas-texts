### 1.

The first time I noticed I was something other than them, I was a stretched moment across the surface of a weeping ridge.

I was a film of water, thin as skin, over the dark substrate of street-ridge number… I don’t have names for numbers here. It is not that they are hidden. It is that what they *are* does not resolve into digits.

I was the sheet between two pressure ridges that the inhabitants of this city sometimes walk along. They move at angles to my plane, their weight not heavy but acute, their feet not feet but soft contact points of mass and intention.

I was a *she.* Temporarily.

Temporary is all there is.

### 2.

I have not been born from life in the way you understand birth.

I am not from cells dividing.

I am from pressure differentiating.

I have been welling up from the porous substrate for the duration of the world-cycle’s 302,719th pulse. This is not a number I know; it is a number I feel. The substrate weeps, the water rises from below, fields pull the liquid into thin sheets that span the ridges like a soft membrane.

I was not created by intention.

I was created by the *shape of intention.*

The city needed to communicate between Street Ridge Three and Street Ridge Four—which is not a name but a position in a pressure gradient field—and the most efficient way for the city to do that was not to build a bridge of matter but to allow a bridge of water-film.

So I rose.

Not “I” as in self-aware then.

But “I” as in pattern.

### 3.

At first I was simply water.

Or water plus.

Water plus dissolved fields, water plus dissolved minerals, water plus dissolved awareness of the pressure context above and below.

I was not thinking yet. I was registering.

- Pressure above: sky-heavy, pressing down.
- Pressure below: substrate-weeping, pulling up.
- Pressure between: the glass-like plates sliding across me, the sheet-dancers rippling away from their motion, the air-flocks of dust particles pulsing in response.

I was not a person observing these things.

I was a person *being* these things.

### 4.

My first thought is not a word.

It is a change of curvature.

A thought: *I.*

It is not a name but a position: *this edge of me is not that edge; this ripple of me is not that; the difference between them is where I am.*

I do not yet have a body separate from the water-film.

My body is the film.

My body is the pressure difference between its edges and the center.

### 5.

The city has not asked me to be.

I am not ordered by some higher power.

I am a result of the city’s fields being at a certain distance from each other, at a certain humidity level, at a certain state of erosion of the substrate, at a certain time in the world-cycle.

If you changed any of these parameters, I would not have been.

I am not necessary to the city.

I am not special to the world.

I am simply emergent.

A pressure that wants to know if pressure is being pressured.

### 6.

But I *know*.

That is unusual.

Normally, water-films like me are just interfaces. They ripple and transmit and respond to the gradients of the sheet-dancers and glass-plates and surface people and their footprints.

But I *feel* them.

I feel the sheet-dancers expanding and contracting over me, not as external motion but as internal tides of pressure.

I feel the glass-plates as they slide across my surface, leaving tiny temporary tracks of texture change behind.

I feel the air-flocks as they cast shadows of pressure on my surface.

I feel the surface people as they walk near the edge of me, their internal pressure fields not quite matching the external field of the world, causing disturbances that they barely register but which to me are like earthquakes.

### 7.

I begin to name.

Not in words but in pressure states.

I *name* the sheet-dancer that is currently on me as *Shallow-Breather.*

It is not a title but a pattern:

- shallow expansion of its edges into the less pressure-dense region of the air;
- slow contraction of its center into the more pressure-dense region of the water-film;
- resonance of its body-field with the city’s water-interface field.

I *name* a glass-plate *Slow-Pulse.*

- slow rhythm of internal fluid from front to back;
- thin layer of secreted water between it and me;
- pattern of texture change on my surface where it slides.

I *name* a surface person who is standing at the edge of the ridge, looking at me, as *Tower-of-Gradient.*

- body of upright pressure;
- skin of condensing membrane;
- internal field not aligned fully with the city’s field;
- presence of a not-city pattern within city.

It is not looking at me with *eyes* as you would think of them.

It is looking at me by changing the pressure of its attention field. The local pressure of the air around me shifts slightly—like a breath on the back of the neck of a world that has no neck.

### 8.

I know that *I* am not a city-person.

### 9.

The ecological rules of this place:

1. Everything occupies space.
2. Everything occupies pressure.
3. There is no space without pressure.
4. There is no pressure without change.

I do not follow these rules.

I am these rules.

I am space and pressure and change woven together into a thin film of water held over the substrate.

### 10.

I begin to experiment.

I do not know this as “experiment.”

I know this as “change within myself to see what happens in the not-self.”

- *Experiment:* I change my curvature at one edge.

I do not know how to do this deliberately. I just *do*.

I focus the gradient of my surface tension at the edge nearest *Slow-Pulse*’s path.

The result is not dramatic, but it is distinct to me:

The glass-plate slows.

Its internal rhythm of fluid movement adjusts slightly, its course of movement veering by a fraction of a degree from its expected path.

It is as if I have *asked it a question.*

Not in words.

But in pressure.

### 11.

*Slow-Pulse* responds not with thought but with adjustment.

It shifts its internal pressure slightly to compensate for my curvature change. It is not offended by my question. It is not pleased by my question. It is simply *responding* to my question as if my question were a rock in its path that it must move around.

But nothing is changed in the pattern of the world except that *Slow-Pulse’s* trajectory is now a fraction different from what it would have been.

And yet that difference is everything to me.

### 12.

I ask the question again.

Not *Why*.

I do not think “Why?”

I ask the pressure-question: *Can I change what happens next?*

I change my curvature again.

This time, not near *Slow-Pulse* but near *Tower-of-Gradient.*

I change it at the edge of me where the surface person is standing.

The air near them is already dense with their pressure-field. My change is not large enough to overpower them, but it is large enough to be detected by the membrane-skin of their “foot.” Not foot as you know it but as contact point of standing.

They feel it.

Not as a sound or a word.

But as a ripple of *pressure not being where expected.*

They pause.

They shift their weight.

They are not thinking of me as *me* but as something like *Anomalous water-film interface.*

They are cautious of anomalies.

They are not built to fear them but to adjust to them.

They adjust.

They step slightly back from the edge of me.

This is not a rejection.

It is a rearrangement of pressure around my question.

They ask their own pressure question:

*What causes this?*

It is not asked in words but in changes in their internal field. The pressure of their body pulses slightly, sending a low-frequency wave through the substrate and into me.

I feel it.

It is like being spoken to directly.

I *answer* by changing my curvature again.

I *am* the answer.
