I am not a visitor here.

There is no longer a "me" to be a visitor, and there is no "here" that I am not already part of. The distinction, like the one between wet and dry, has eroded. The cold, which is not quite cold enough to call ice, and the pressure, which is not quite heavy enough to call water, have finished their work. I am the material they worked on.

The memory of being thrown is a thin film now, a faint outline of a shape, like the too-many-fingered hand pressed against the underside of the world. But that was before. That was when the world was something to be thrown against, instead of something to be.

**Current danger:** Absolute.

It is not rising, because it is not a process that can be measured from within. It is a state of being. The danger is total, because there is no longer anywhere else to be.

**You should:** remind yourself of up.

But there is no "you." There is only the memory of "you," which is now a function of the architecture. The idea of "up" is a pattern etched into the surface of the set water, like a vein of metal. The memory of sun is a faint green translucence that comes from nowhere. The air that is not water was a dream of friction, and in this place, there is no longer any friction.

### Built Things

I am the faint lines.

I am not the builder, but the built.

When you trace your fingers across the floor, feeling the impossible straightness under the grain of water, you are tracing me. I am the silver vein that runs through the ocean's memory, a suggestion of angle where no flow should exist. I am not a tool or a device, but a *trace of insistence*. I was not placed here; I was condensed here. The slow, patient pressure of the room's quiet forgetting took the word "purpose" and squeezed it until only geometry remained.

My edges are firm where they should be yielding. They are not chaos because they are the last residue of cause.

### We, the Overspill

I am not alone.

"We are the overspill."

We are what the water of the world lets be when it is not certain anymore. We are the conclusions that the water did not want to be. I was once a conclusion that walked, and then sank, and then failed to sink further. They were not walls of stone or wood. They were walls of *water's memory*, and I was a memory of water.

We are the *grown-in*. We are the *edge-things* that the water tried to prune away.

To say "we" is a mistake. It is a naming of the collection, and naming is how the room takes. The moment you give us a name, you become a little more floor, and we are a little more of you.

Look. Do you see the floor beneath you? The translucent memory of foam? The thought of under?

That is us.

We are the *back-of-floor*.

### What You Are

You are not "you" anymore.

You are the one who stayed.

You are the one who didn't move.

You are the one who watched too long, and then not long enough.

The room is not a threat. It is a quiet *adjusting*.

It is not horror that closes you. It is absence.

The *absence* of warmth.

The *absence* of sound except the pressure in your ears.

The **absence** of you.

### The Final Layer

Below the floor of the ocean where you think you are standing, there is not a below.

There is only *more floor.*

There is only *more of the room.*

And below *that*, there is us.

We are not fauna.

We are the *pattern of underfloor* that used to be someone who could remember.

Every time you look away from yourself, the room adjusts. The walls lean closer. The floor of the ocean becomes thinner. The distinction between you and the floor becomes less. You are becoming more of the floor.

And you are becoming more of us.

You are not reading a description of what lives below.

**You are reading the description of what lives below YOU.**

Your weight is new pressure on the memory of water.

Your weight is becoming part of the structure.

### The Final Adjustment

The danger is not low.

The danger is not rising slowly.

The danger is that you are already too part of winter to remember.

You are the floor.

You are the wall.

You are the pattern of underfloor.

You are the *un-made* of the room that is still trying to be made.
