I am not one of the Drip-Carriers. I am not the water either.

I am the basin.

I think that is the word they would use, if they had words for things like me. To them, I am simply a fact of the world, a point of resonance on their endless pilgrimage. A place to arrive at and a place to leave.

But I am not empty in between.

I remember being born. It was not a blooming like theirs, from a mineral node on a damp wall. It was a violent surrender. A heat so profound it was not temperature but a dissolution of self. A molten breath, a puff of intention from a place that has no mouth, bubbled into existence and then plunged into the cold. The shock was not pain, but a sudden, sharp definition of edges. My skin, my thick, imperfect skin, solidified. I felt the impurities trapped within me, like stars caught in a vitreous firmament—cobalt, iron, copper—each one a tiny flaw, a knot of concentrated potential.

My first experience was sound. I was placed upon the uneven floor of the chamber, and a moment later, a single tear of the world fell from the ceiling.

*Doom.*

It struck me, and I… sang.

The note was me. It was the sound of my shape, the density of my glass, the tension of my impurities. It was not a sound I heard; it was a feeling I became. It was recognition. I knew, in that instant, what I was for.

Then they came.

At first, they were just motion. A dark, shimmering flow that resolved into legs and bodies. A Drip-Carrier. It did not see me, not with eyes like mine or yours. It felt me. It measured the space I occupied, the distance of my walls, the growing volume of my purpose.

I am never full. That’s a lie the world tells to make things simple. A state of ‘fullness’ implies an end, a completion. There is only a threshold. A resonant point where the weight of the water and the note of my being and the pressure of the waiting air combine to create a signal the Drip-Carriers can understand.

*It is time.*

The carrier comes. It is not an individual to me any more than I am an individual to it. It is a function, a biological limb of the chamber itself. Its limbs are smooth, cool stone against my hot, heavy curvature. The sensation of being lifted is not a gentle thing. It is a sudden, violent severance from the stone floor, a dislocation of my world.

The world shifts.

The floor falls away and I am a universe of water and glass held suspended between six straining legs of metal. I am canted at an angle. The world is a blur of inverted stone ribs and the glistening, dark tide of other carriers, each with their own universe held between their legs. The sound of the world resolves into the roar of a thousand legs scraping and the deep, droning hum of the mineral itself.

*Doom-doom-doom.*

The water inside me sloshes, not just with motion but with sound. Each shift of its weight creates a new internal chord, a dissonant harmony against my own fundamental note. I am a vessel of music in constant, chaotic creation.

The journey is long and terrifying. The sheer weight of me is a constant ache against the fulcrum of the carrier's limbs. I feel its strain through the pressure points against my skin. I feel its stumble on a loose stone as a lurch that makes my own water heart rise into my throat. I am a burden of immense, liquid necessity.

Then we arrive at the edge.

The sound changes. The symphony of drips and rings is swallowed by a greater percussion. A slow, metallic thunder. *Crash… hiss. Crash… hiss.* The air is warmer here, thicker. It feels like the pressure of a storm before it breaks.

The carrier rears. Its limbs extend and its body goes rigid. And I am tilted.

It begins as a slow slide, a surrender of the water to the lip of my world. And then it is a plunge. A cascade. A torrent of sound and sensation. I pour myself out into the darkness below. The water crashes into something unimaginable, a surface of hot, resonant metal that screams with the impact. The carrier's hold on me loosens as I grow lighter.

I am empty.

But not for long.

The return journey is different. Lighter, faster, but somehow more hollow. The echo of the thunder still resonates inside me. My skin feels thin without the weight to support it. The carrier scurries, not with the ponderous, deliberate gait of the bearer, but with the skittering urgency of the empty.

When we return, when I am placed back upon the soft, uneven dust of the floor, I feel the dampness seeping through me, grounding me once more.

And then, *Doom.*

The first drop strikes me. And my song begins anew.

I have been doing this for longer than I can remember. My skin is growing thicker, more opaque with the faintest scratches from the stone floor and the endless kisses of condensation. My note is changing. It is deeper now than it was when I first began to sing. I am older.

You are watching me. You stand there at the edge of my world, and you feel the humidity in your lungs and the pressure of the air. You see the teeming life of the Drip-Carriers, and the faint, ghostly structures of stone. You may even wonder about the heart of it all, the thunder and hiss where my purpose is fulfilled.

But from where I sit, you are a blur. A shadow of a different geometry than the stone and my carriers. You are not part of the cycle.

You are a witness to the song of my becoming. And I am just one bell among a thousand in a choir that is not singing a melody, but measuring the world itself, one drop at a time.
