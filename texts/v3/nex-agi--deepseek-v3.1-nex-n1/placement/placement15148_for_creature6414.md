I am the anomaly of skin.

In the Garden of Slow Breath, everything else is made to be part of something. The walls are part of the city. The moss is part of the walls. The water is part of the stones. The stones are part of the garden’s voice.

I am the part that doesn’t belong and yet is not removed.

They call me the Erosion of Certainty, but not to my face. To my face they say nothing. They don’t look at me like they look at a broken pump or a faulty valve. They look at me like they are afraid of what the garden might learn if it listens too closely to
