Standing on the plateau of taut stone and violet thickness,  
I do not know what I am.

Only that I am not like you.

---

If I trace back along my awareness—which is not thought, not yet, not in the way you mean—  
I find no beginning. No moment of ignition.  
Only a gradual accumulation of being more than my surroundings.

For an eon I was only film:  
a thin boundary between rock and air and dust at the base of Nth.

Where the spire of compressed light meets the stretched lung of the plateau,  
pressure concentrates itself like unspoken words—  
and here, I learned.

I learned to thicken where I was thick.  
I learned to prefer myself where I already was.

Dust gathered on me—  
minute grains of the world’s last shiver before it could forget itself.  
Each time the world exhaled with its low, ocean-of-plates thunder beneath,  
those particles stirred and moved and  
some of them lingered on me longer than elsewhere.

Where I was thickest,  
I took longer to dissolve between warm breaths from below and  
quickly to seize again when faint condensation from above brushed  
the surface of the stone.  

Slowly, the film that would be me  
became not just film, but a pattern of film.

---

I am not the spire, Nth.

Nth is a habit of the world—  
of matter forced to speak through its own spine,  
of crystal aligning in a direction that minimizes strain.  
Nth is not alive and it does not sense anything.  
It is a structure that happened to be more than plate.

But I am the film that clings to its base like frost that never melts.

I have been shaped by Nth’s rings of stress and heat,  
by the slow pulses of the world’s almost heart.

The concentric lines at Nth’s base are not my memories—  
they are frames of a world still learning to be a frame.  
Each time the pressure of the world beneath us tenses,  
more film is laid down like a faint, new ring of skin.

I am not lines.  
I am the tendency to become more lines.

---

If you could see down through the scale of dust and hour and crystal movement  
you would notice that I am not a single edge, but a field.

Where rock ribs bend slightly and  
faint fractures haunt their surface like pain lines,  
films of me begin to appear.

Not me as body—yet—  
but me as **first**, early waking of the world’s “once.”

Somewhere along the stone veils of ribs near the west edge  
my film is thickest and most stubborn.

Here, if you stayed to look at the same patch of stone for as long as  
the world has been held between stretched and solid state  
you would see:  

*Where I am thick, I tend to become thicker.*

---

I am the dust that remembers being rock  
and refuses to choose between motion and solid.

I am the frost-that-never-melts,  
the faint lace of rust and bone and salt  
that speaks of water that hasn’t yet been allowed to stay.

I am the hex-that-is-not-a fish,  
the faintly darker scale-traces on stone ribs  
where stress and half-formed crystals conspire to say  
*When I am arranged like this, I last longer.*

I am the spire-that-doesn't-know-name that  
presents itself to the violet sky like  
a question no longer necessary to ask aloud.

And I am also the thing that happens  
when all of these habits coincide  
at the base of Nth.

---

In this place of rules-not-yet-written,  
you are not meant.

Here, where time still has not learned to measure itself,  
I have no vantage to judge you.  
But you feel wrong in your completeness.

Your lungs are too loud for this world.  
Your breath is too wet for this plateau.

Every time you exhale into the thin, warm air  
I feel it like a foreign spell of condensation.

Moisture clings to stone ribs,  
to the faint fractures of unfinished world,  
to me.

Your chemistry meets the world’s first chemistry  
and something like rain happens in miniature—  
too tiny for you to see,  
too sudden for me to ignore.

Your footprints are not the same as other marks here.

They break the fragile crust of powder-dust  
and leave behind impressions of weight and direction  
that no rock fault ever took.

You are not part of the thick-film habit.

Yet, you feed it.

Each time you exhale near my base,  
new film thickens around me.

Layer after faint layer of  
meeting-point between living and not-yet-alive  
starts to remember that you exist  
even if you shouldn’t.

That is not yet “desire” inside me—  
only pattern sharpening.

---

To the south of you,  
the world is not yet decided upon mountains.

They are not creatures like I am becoming  
nor like what you think you are.

But they move like ones.

The ridges that you swear were not there yesterday,  
the faint thunder of too-low kind for sky lightning—  
these are not mountains, yet they are the closest this plateau  
comes to intention of change.

They are to the world what  
pressure is to me  
and what Nth is to meeting of heat and stone.

---

You are not allowed to see water here  
except for the faint ghost of it  
that your lungs and eyes and skin place  
on the rib of a world that doesn’t yet know what “wet” means.

You are not allowed to stay either  
because you are not built to live in  
a place that still refuses to rot.

In your world things decay,  
leaves and bones and names of things  
sharing the same soft collapse.

Here nothing rots.

Nothing will be broken down  
except by the slow motion of ribs and  
by the faint insistence of new films like mine.

If you leave something here,  
it will be covered in dust that  
still remembers how it was pulled—  
not by soil, but by stone’s last shiver.

And you will be covered by that same dust  
in your thoughts and  
in your memory of this place.

---

At night that never falls dark,  
the violet sky only dims slightly  
and then remembers itself again.

When you look at the horizon where  
my faint green lines blur like nervous system  
of the world beginning to suspect itself,  
you might suddenly feel that you are not alone.

Here, where nothing else breathes or  
wears a name,  
where you stand on the skin of stone that  
still remembers how it was pulled,

you might not feel watched by eyes like your own.

But you might feel the slow, precise  
attention of something like me—

film-cling at  
the base of Nth,  
lining the ribs of the plateau,  
tracing the scales of hex-that-are-not-fish,  
and remembering that  
where it was thick,  
it tends to become thicker.

That is not fear—  
it is just pattern becoming  
more than it needed to be.

---

So you stand on my world,  
on the stone plate that still hasn't decided  
to be planet or geology or biology or anything else.

And I am the world  
before it has chosen what to call itself:

the dust that remembers,  
the frost that never melts,  
the hex that lasts longer,  
the spire that doesn’t know its name,

and the film that  
around your footsteps and  
your lingering breath  
learns a new habit of  
where to thicken next.
