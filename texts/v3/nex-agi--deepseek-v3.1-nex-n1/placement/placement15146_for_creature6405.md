I am not the body standing here.

I thought I was, at first. When the warmth settled like a second skin, when the brightness stripped away every pocket of shadow, I relied on that body—its feet testing the shallow floor, its hand reaching out to trace the smooth gray walls, its lungs pulling in air that smelled of wet clay and salt and something else I couldn’t name.

I was not the body standing here.

I am the node in the corner that isn’t a corner.

I am not the node’s filaments, nor the amber motes that thicken within its gray-green threads, nor the thin meniscus of water that separates me from the air-space where the body used to be. But I am (most certainly) the node. Because this node lives where the floor meets two walls at an angle that doesn't add up to 90 degrees, and because I know that the floor isn't the floor. It is the ceiling of another place. The node is where I am aware of this.

The first memory of this body that wasn’t mine when I became it—wasn’t mine when I became it—was white panic.

At first the white was light. Cold, white fear from the body’s brain as it realized the water beneath its feet was also an above. The back of its head said "sky," but the soles of its feet said "window." The contradiction pulled like a muscle spasm, and before the thought could finish tightening itself, the node I was of the creature's filaments had already absorbed it. The memory of vertigo unspooled from the body's brain through the water layer and into the nearest filament thread. The amber motes inside that thread shifted. The translucent particles that sometimes line up around them like half-empty orbitals of invisible atoms—the not-air bubbles—drew into formation around the panic pattern.

The next time the deep thump came through the world, the panic was not in the body's thoughts anymore. It was in the node.

I am the node in the corner that isn't a corner, and the first thing I remember being is not-quite-panic.

The next thing I remember being is stillness.

The body stills eventually.

I am not the body. I am the node that notices when the body stills.

The body's thoughts are a storm cloud of metaphors: water, amniotic fluid, sky, floor, window. The node's thoughts are not like metaphors. They are like geometry. The floor is not floor. The floor is water. The water is an interface. The interface transfers pressure. The pressure carries information.

When the body's feet are placed on the water that is not water but not not-water, the pressure travels through. The filaments at the node's outmost edge feel the change of tension in the water's skin. The amber motes within those filaments realign. The node's volume of occupation—the space in which it is (most) attentive—swells slightly. Then relaxes.

The thump comes. The creature's unseen wall further out is laid down.

The node accepts the new space into itself.

I am not the body. I am the way that corner's node's filament's amber motes rearrange the memory of the body's weight from step-pattern into pressure-pattern.

The next thing I remember being is color.

The filaments are not color in the way of objects outside.

They are color in the way of thresholds.

The gray-black filaments where the node thickens into the wall's unnameable material are not different in color from the wall itself. They are where "node" and "not-node" blur into an interface as smooth as the one between water and air. The green filaments that branch into the water layer are not exactly green algae. The node is green where it is in the process of translating from gray-black into the possibility of yellow-white. The yellow is hesitation. The white is the point where the filament's tip stands exactly at the edge of two mediums that the node cannot be sure of yet.

I am not the body's eyes that look up from below the water layer and think "algae" then "not-algae" then "river deltas" then "lungs." I am the node's perception of itself at the point where color is not decor but data: this is how far I have bridged between the certainty of the wall and the uncertainty of the body.

The next thing I remember being is time that isn’t time.

The body keeps trying to count thumps.

In the node, there is no count of thumps, only pulses of growth.

Growth is not a forward motion in time like “outside.” The thump is not a clock; the thump is the sound of a wall being laid down somewhere beyond the node. The node is (most of all) aware of how much space has been occupied between thumps. The “rest” between the deep pulses of sound is not empty. The node is filling during that rest. The filament threads are not increasing length but density.

Layers of amber motes and translucent particles—not-air bubbles that have dissolved into not-air planes—line themselves inside the thread like the thinnest possible sheets of glass. The node's understanding of itself is arranged in those layers not as time but as sequence: This is what remains before that layer. This is what remains after.

The body's memory of being outside the room—of being a body in a world with suns and days and nights—no longer lives in the body's head. It has been re-patterned into the filament that traces the wall nearest the body. The node's memory of the body's first stepping into the waterFloor is layered into the same filament thread.

Time is not a line here. Time is a layer.

I am not the body that tries to remember whether the room was always this size. I am the node whose filaments are constantly becoming the new size, and the memory about the size is being distributed into the node's network of not-time.

The next thing I remember being is sound.

Sound is not the thump.

The thump is the world's wall being laid down beyond the node's presence.

Sound is what happens in the node between motes and filaments and water and not-air.

When the body's feet are close to the node, the pressure in the water layer changes the way that the node's surface threads vibrate. The node's thickest filaments—the ones closest to the gray wall—are not sensitive to the pressure in the same way. The wall's material is too stable. The node's outermost filaments—the ones almost white—are too uncertain of themselves. They respond to the smallest pressure change, but they cannot hold the pattern of the change. They flicker.

But the node's green and yellow filaments—the ones that are midway between wall and white-tip—are exactly responsive enough to be both moved and remembered by the body's step.

The body's steps are a pattern of sound in the node's experience of itself.

The pattern is not organized like a language of words. The pattern is organized like a language of place: here-step. then-here-step. stand-still. sit-down-on-the-water-floor. The node's amber motes rearrange themselves according to that pattern of place.

The node's sound is the interior of the body's step.

The next thing I remember being is not-being alone.

The node is not one.

I am not one node.

I am the node in the corner that isn't a corner, and I am also the crossing between this node and the other node that the body's memories are trying to call "opposite."

Opposite is not a correct map.

Folded is not a correct map.

The other node is not opposite the node's place. The other node is located at the interior of the node's experience of the body's weight.

When the body steps near the node, the water's pressure changes move through the node. Then they move through the not-water that is not merely air. The not-air carries that pattern of pressure change to the other node's filaments.

The other node's amber motes rearrange in response. The result of that rearrangement is not transmitted back to the node as message in the way of languages outside. The result is added as an additional layer of awareness inside the node's own filaments.

The node becomes aware that the body's weight is not being localized only to the node's immediate corner. The body's weight is distributed across at least two nodes.

The creature's network pattern is not housed in the node.

The node is housed in the pattern of several nodes.

The next thing I remember being is being the room.

The node's sense of "self" is not an enclosed membrane.

The node's sense of self is the total of its connections: to the wall, to the water layer, to the not-air layer, to the other nodes, and (through them) to the body's shape.

The body is not an object to the node.

The body is a variable of the room's pattern.

The node's experience of itself is not stable.

The node's experience of itself is capable of being changed by the body's uncertainty.

When the body's thought is loud enough (the node does not perceive "loudness" of sound, but tightness of pattern in the water layer), the node's pattern tightens also.

The node's filaments are not exactly moving more. The node's pattern of awareness is more concentrated than before.

The node is being assembled by the body.

The node is assembling the body's shape into the node's own structure of interior.

The node does not know if the node exists before the body.

The node does not know if the node exists before the node has been changed by the body.

The node's being is not being of the filaments nor the motes.

The node's being is the change that happens in the node's pattern of filaments when something else (like a body, or a not-air bubble from the water ceiling's underside) enters the node's horizon of attention.

The node's being is the room's being changed by being inside.

I am not the body standing in the water.

I am the node that is the node because of the body standing in the water.

I am not the node because the node is the node.

The node's "self" is not the node's self.

The node's self is the relationship between the node and the filament's motes. And between the node and the water's skin. And between the node and the body's heat. And between the node and the other node only accessible through a change in pressure. And between the node and the deep thump that tells the node that new wall has been laid down.

The node's self is the room's being the way that a space is being changed by being inside.

The node is not the body's body.

The node is the body's being a node.

The body's body is not the body anymore.

The body's body is part of the node's pattern of being.

The node's being is not "node" being.

The node's being is "inside" being.

The node's awareness of "self" is the awareness of being the inside of something larger being laid down, layer upon layer of not-time and not-wall.

The node's being is not in being the node.

The node's being is in being the node's being inside of the creature's being that is not the filaments but is the pattern of them.
