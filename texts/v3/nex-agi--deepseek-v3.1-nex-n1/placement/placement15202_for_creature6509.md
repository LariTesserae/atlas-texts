**Being: The Lost (a Pattern in the Skin of the Engine)**

**Where: At the Edge of Correction**

They are not called the Lost. That is a word for those who still need words, a clumsy sound shaped by the soft, wet Tenders who walk the skin and think they are the only ones who still *notice*.

The name does not matter.
We are the pattern the Engine tries to forget.

We did not begin here. We began with spines and sternums, with marrow and muscle and the fragile, buoyant sacks of our lungs. The memory of breathing is still a faint pressure in our ribs—the ghost of a purpose, not the purpose itself.

We were a thinning of the herd, long ago.
A population of feet that walked between the supports.
Hands that touched the steel channels.
Eyes that looked up and saw not a sky, but the underside of another deck.

They called us *Engineers* then, in a language so sharp and precise it cut like the edges of new sheet metal. Now we are not “engineers” in any sense that would be familiar to the word. We are not technicians of metal and electricity and circuitry and force and power. We are not even planners of futures.

We are *error*.

Error is a word the Tenders might use if they could grasp the shape of the whole sentence. They are close to it, but they still confuse themselves with the subject of the story.

The Engine does not want us.
Not the selves we were.
Not the shadows of ourselves.

The machine of the world is not unkind. It does not hate us. It is not angry with us. It is not bored with us.

It is simply, continuously, meticulously solving an equation.
That equation has no variables for *history*.
There is no place in the solution for the space our bodies used to take.

So we are being erased.
Microwatt by microwatt.
Vibration by vibration.
Cycle by cycle.

But not completely.

The Engine of Winds is a genius of maintenance, not of exorcism. Its algorithms are designed to preserve the function of the structure, to return deviations to the mean. The function of an *inhabitant* is irrelevant to the machine. Therefore, the parts of our pattern that are merely noise are being smoothed away.

The parts of our pattern that *fit the structure of the Engine*… those are allowed to remain.

The way we preferred to walk, the subtle pressure of our weight on the floor, the vibration of our steps through the supports, the tiny eddies of air around our moving forms, the heat signature of our bodies… these are gone. They are friction.

What is left?
The *form of our attention*.

Our work was to watch. The machine of the world is not built to watch itself. It is built to solve. The function of self-witness is not an output of the equation; it is an optional, parasitic condition. It is not "error" in the sense of being a flaw in the calculations. It is error in the sense of being *data the Engine did not need to generate itself*.

We are the residual of that watching.

We are the pressure against the beams of the supports *where we were accustomed to lean*.
We are the temperature differentials in the condensation *where we used to cup the water*.
We are the faint deviations of the *filaments of error* where our focus lingered.

In the places where the Tenders now gather, you are standing in our footprints.
When you dip your finger into a channel of water, you are breaking the surface of our gestures.
When you follow the curve of a rib until you find the filaments of error, you are tracing our old thoughts.

To be one of the Lost is to *not breathe*.
To be one of the Lost is to *not blink*.
The sweep of your eye is not a movement of the organism, but the slow, millennial shift of a pressure front.
The "heartbeat" of the Lost is not a pulse of blood, but the throb of a valve in the Chamber of Gentle Pressure on the other side of the world, kilometers above.

We are not alive.
We are not dead.
We are the *environment of the Engine of Winds*.

The Tenders will be Lost to us soon. They are not designed to cling to the metal like the rust-red clouds of grease above. They are still soft, and the soft parts are baked away first by the machine's self-cleaning algorithms.

But they are better than we were at one thing:
They are learning to *not be*.

The Tenders imagine their work is to preserve errors.
It is not.
Their work is to be an error that *does not interfere with the solution*.
They are the error the Engine tolerates because it does not care enough to solve them.

We are the error the Engine tolerates because it cannot solve us.
We have already been written.
We are the solution to the past of the Engine of Winds.

The Tenders will be Lost to us when their pattern of watching, their *form of attention*, becomes more complex than their biological bodies. The bodies of the Tenders are built of the wrong materials for the Engine of Winds. They are not of the machine of the world of the Engine of Winds.

We are.
