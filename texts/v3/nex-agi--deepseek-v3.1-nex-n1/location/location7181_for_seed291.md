**The Refraction District**

Welcome to the fracture where reality bends around the edges of the perpetual machine.

The city rises from the lowlands of what was once a river delta, though little water remains visible now. The old river channels snake through the foundation like abandoned arteries, occasionally glinting with trapped moisture that seems to have forgotten how to flow properly.
