The landscape of Caelira is not one place so much as an infinite series of stacked surfaces, all in motion, all grinding against one another with a sound like continents breaking their teeth.

### Scale and Structure

Imagine a world not built on ground, but in sky.

You are standing on what looks like a plate of glass a kilometer thick and a thousand kilometers wide, suspended far above a cloud floor that never ends. The plate is heavy crystalline stone, not translucent but bright, reflecting and refracting everything in pale blues and whites and faint metallic shimmers. It is not alone; all around and above you, there are more plates, tilted at strange angles, as though a flat cosmos shattered and then froze mid-collapse. Each surface is a continent unto itself, with its own mountains, ridges, valleys, and cliffs—but no below. There is no ground beneath the ground. Only layers of geography, hovering above abyssal white.
