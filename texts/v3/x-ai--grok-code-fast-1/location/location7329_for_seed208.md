Frequent downpours punctuate the stillness, not savage storms but steady, primordial drizzles that last for cycles—hours or days—who can tell in this timeless haze? Distances are deceptive in the dimness, where the light filters through a canopy of eternal twilight—neither full day nor night, but a muted, diffuse glow from a shrouded sky that might be overcast with clouds or perhaps some atmospheric veil of condensed vapor. Sparsity reigns; there's space between everything, a balanced void that invites the eye to roam and wonder, the landscape never crowding itself into clutter.
