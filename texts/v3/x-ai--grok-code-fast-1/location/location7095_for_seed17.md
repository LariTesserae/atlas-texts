You arrive—though arrival feels too linear a term, too narrative in a place where direction and passage are illusions formed from static silence and unyielding depth. This is not a surface you breach, nor an ocean you dive; it is the submerged eternal, a world where water has claimed dominion absolute, a fluid mass so vast it births its own physics: gravity bent like light through curved glass, pressure a constant caress that erases edges, volume swelling to planetary scales where a single "continent" might span what would be a moon's girth elsewhere. The scale here defies measure—vast enough for storms to rage unseen in the upper abyssal flows, churning eddies that loop forever in chaotic eddies, weather systems born not from heat or air but from the slow grind of saline currents against crystalline faults. It is young in a way only primordia can be: erosion hardly begun, sharp ridges and spires rising abruptly from the deep, unweathered basalt and obsidian needles piercing upward like frozen screams, fresh-hewn from some cataclysmic forge. Yet they rise high, these peaks—elevation here is a mockery of itself, mountains towering thousands of meters beneath the waves, their summits capped by thin layers of atmoshpere trapped in underwater domes, bubbles of breathable haze clinging to the sheer cliffsides, where the sea meets not air but a perpetual membrane of stillness.

The temperature is an icy shroud, cold so profound it numbs not just flesh but thought, a chill that infiltrates atoms, slowing bonds and reactions to glacial vacua. Even the water, at these depths, hovers near freezing, its salinity engineered into strange equilibria where ice forms not in flakes but in glistening ethers—transient fractals that shimmer and dissolve without ever truly forming mass. Density here is the void incarnate: almost nothing per volume, vast hollows between presences, emptiness so total it feels like hunger, like the absence of self. The ocean is not crowded; it is a near-void, where schools of fauna—abundant yet dispersed—drift in isolation, their bodies spaced leagues apart, each creature a lone constellation in an ink-black expanse. Scatterings of built things punctuate this emptiness: industrial remnants, occasional artifacts from a scaled production era long entombed. Factories loom like sunken zeppelins, their skeletal frames of rusted iron and glazed ceramic hulls encrusted in barnacle-like accretions of frozen brine, half-buried in sediment that never shifts. A single smokestack pierces upward, venting nothing but inert gases, its bellows stilled in eternal exhale. Scattered cranes dangle from mooring struts, their gears and pistons locked in mid-cycle, industrial giants entombed in the cold slurry. Tech pulses faintly here—electrified veins in the conduits, engines that wheeze on auto-repair loops, mechanized arms plucking at phantom outputs, producing... what? Perhaps endless chains of crystalline bearings, forged in vats of molten lava bled from vents below. They operate not with workers but with automated ghosts, systems self-repairing in a frozen equilibrium, never changing, gradients erased in a world where time is invariant, stability the only rule.

Light defies the depths; it is normal yet inverted, a piercing normalcy bleeding from above and within. Sunlight, wraith-pale and unwelcome, filters down in razor-thin shafts, illuminating bioluminescent markers on scattered beacons—industrial lighthouses, their lenses cracked but glowing with inner fires. Fauna lights up the void in unnatural ways: abundant creatures, their forms a bizarre confluence of mineral and flesh, drift in loose aggregations, scales like volcanic glass refracting that diffused glow. Some are colossal, whale-like behemoths with hides of etched quartz, their eyes vast orifices pulsing with geothermal heat; others swarm in thousand-strong nebulas, fungal-creatures not truly alive but patterned energy, bio-filaments wreathed in cold plasma, pre-life echoes that feed on the static hum of undersea vents. No vegetation ties this ecology; it is mineral and simpler, a world where life mimics inert matter—beasts sculpted from obsidian, their veins traced in pyrite, humors that secrete abrasive pastes instead of blood. Evolution here bent toward the unusual, the bizarre: creatures with asymmetical forms, voids within voids, where ecology rules are skewed—predation a telepathic whisper, reproduction a fission of sound waves echoing in the deafening ambient. Fauna preys and sustains in upside-down cycles, their abundant numbers a paradox in the emptiness, each one isolated yet connected through infrasonic threads, never migrating, forever poised in dynamic arrest.

Sound reigns supreme, a deafening cacophony that drowns all else— not waves or cries, but the eternal grind of industrial decay and natural harmonics. The factories hum and roar in industrial liturgy: pistons clanking in mechanical seizures, hydraulics wheezing out pressurized fog, the shriek of metal shearing against itself in endless loops. Layered upon this are the oceanic symphonies: currents scraping over sharp, uneroded basalt ridges, a low tinnitus that builds to thunderous crescendos in the vast cavities of underwater caverns, amplifying into bass-heavy tremors that vibrate through bone. Fauna contributes—a chorus of clicks and booms, bio-sonic artillery from creatures that communicate in subharmonic bellows, their deafening calls forming interlocking chords that never resolve, suspended in the static equilibrium. It is noise without rhythm, a bombardment that echoes and reinforces itself, numbing senses in a world where silence is heresy, change unthinkable.

The atmosphere is a submerged still-life, thick with suspended particulates—microns of mineral dust, machine-oil greases emulsified in the flow, all held in perfect balance, no gradient to stir them. Pressure equalizes across elevations, high peaks feeling the same as abyss, a rule of timeless invariance where the dynamic never intrudes: storms brew but never break, mountains rise but never crumble, creatures drift but never age. Weirdness infuses the fabric—surreal geometries where space folds in on submerged gear-engines that spin perpetually yet futilely, gears meshing in dimension-defying cogs; time-looped artifices where scattered factories replay the same motion, producing chains of nothingness in kaleidoscopic fractals. The cold, the emptiness, the deafening roar—all converge into a place that feels alive in its non-change, a submerged eternity where rules are felt more than stated: permanence is the currency, void the tenant, and you, visitor or voyeur, dissolve into its unchanging gaze, part kinetic, part echo, forever adrift in the vast, high-peaked undersea.
