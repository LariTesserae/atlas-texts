No animals populate this realm—none rustle in the ferns, no insects hum in the heat, no shadows of flightless birds darken the vapors. Yet the place harbors an unusual undercurrent, a weirdness that warps the senses—a subtle strangeness in the rules of this world, where physics bends like the vapors themselves. Time feels elastic here; hours might stretch into the perception of days, but the slow dynamic cycles prevail—gradients of heat shift imperceptibly, mineral deposits form at eyeless speed across eons, the water levels rising and falling in patient tides dictated by subterranean pressures far below. This pocket is a contained anomaly, embedded in the volcano's vastness but operating by its own uncanny logic—a room where the built and the natural entwine in a slow, loud simmer, waiting for the next improbable visitor to stumble through the veil and disrupt its gradual equilibrium.
