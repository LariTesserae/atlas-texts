The air thrums with biological fury: spores popping like firecrackers, roots cracking as they burrow deeper into the substrate of themselves, a predator-prey ballet where plants both hunt and are hunted by their own kind, tendrils snapping taut in loud twangs, ensnaring wandering fungi in audible squelches that break the stillness like shattering glass.

It's a microcosm contained within the larger cthonic dryness above, a hidden intimacy where pocket ecosystems pulse and perish, continually reforming in their emptiness, where dampness gathers not just water but the weight of possibilities, and sound booms not as noise but as the rhythm of unreal becoming.
