The plateaus rise high, elevations stacking like the layered backbones of some immense, slumbering beast, pushing into a cold sky where vapors condense into perpetual mist. No violent winds tear at them yet; erosion here is a gentle insistence, a slow seep of water against nascent fissures, leaving surfaces that weep with beaded droplets, as if the rock itself weeps for the formation it has yet to achieve. These are not rugged peaks born of ages of strife, but soft, unfinished slopes—embryonic mountains where the ground feels pliable underfoot, the materials gritty and unproven, basalt melded with some iridescent ore that hints at buried energies beneath.

It's the damp of a post-rain world, though no storms rage here; instead, the moisture emerges from the land itself, drawn up from subterranean reservoirs close enough to the surface that puddles form unbidden, and marsh-fringed pools dot the plateaus infrequently but persistently. These aren't true marshes, for vegetation claims only sparse territories—scattered tufts of hardy, frost-edged grasses clinging to the cracks, their roots probing the wet stone like tentative fingers. Trees, if they can be called such, rise infrequently, their trunks twisted and low-lying, branches webbing out like veins in marble, but they leave vast expanses barren, the emptiness a deliberate choice of the world to let space breathe. Yet woven into this raw, emerging geology is a settlement on a vast urban scale, not a cramped metropolis but a sprawling, layered infrastructure that feels like an extension of the plateaus themselves. These are towns entangled with cities, their layered constructions speaking to an engineering that resculpts the world as it forms: roads that grow organically from the undersoil, buildings that emerge like crystals from the condensing fog, all spaced out with intentional emptiness. Density here is sparse, with enormous voids between edifices, allowing the cold, damp air to circulate freely, preventing any sense of overcrowding. This is a civilization that honors the immense scale of its domain, a continent-planet where human-like inhabitants (or those that walk as such) traverse the quiet expanses on low-flying drones or walking paths that whisper underfoot, their daily rhythms active and restless—markets forming and dispersing with the mist, energy flows adjusting to the emerging geothermal shifts below.
