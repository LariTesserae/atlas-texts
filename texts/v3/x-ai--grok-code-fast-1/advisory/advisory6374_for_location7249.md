   - **Cold-Weather Gear for the Damp Frigidity:** The "pervasive chill" isn't arctic, but it's a teeth-chattering, bone-seeping dampness that makes fog feel like a cold fog machine. Practice balance on uneven, wet ground to build muscle memory; this place would test your agility, with the risk of falling into canal troughs or tripping over fused machinery remnants.
