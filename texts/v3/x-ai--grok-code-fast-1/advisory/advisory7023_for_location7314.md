### Physical and Gear Preparation: Equipping for a Damp, Jagged, Reactive Environment
Physically, this cavern is a sauna of jagged rocks, uneven vegetation mats, and sudden water cascades—easy to slip, slice, or drown in if unprepared. The constant 28°C warmth and 100% humidity means dehydration lurks despite the moisture, while sharp stalactites and voids threaten falls. - **Clothing and Body Protection:** OPT for lightweight, moisture-wicking apparel that's resistive to moisture and tears. Reinforce knees, elbows, and palms with padded inserts for crawling through voids or over sharp rocks. - **Survival Essentials Pack:** Carry a lightweight backpack (under 10 lbs) with:

  - Food: Non-perishable, high-calorie items like energy bars or dehydrated meals. Avoid anything that could spoil in humidity. Intuitively, the plants might try to "fed" you with bioluminescent fruits—resist; they could drug or infect.
  - Lighting: A headlamp with red-light mode (to preserve night vision) and spare batteries. The luminescent veins pulse irregularly, so don't rely on them—bring a backup lantern that phases independently.
  - First Aid Kit: Anti-fungal/antibacterial cream (for spore exposure), bandages for cuts from jagged rocks, painkillers, antihistamines (for reactions to peculiar fauna), and a snakebite kit (adaptable for slug-like creatures). Include electrolyte packets to combat dehydration in the heat.

- **Tools for Navigation and Hazard Mitigation:** A multi-tool with knife, saw, and pliers for cutting through dense vines. Rope for rappelling into voids or climbing stalactites. A portable hammock for resting off the damp floor—hang it from rock protrusions to avoid plant mats that undulate and might swallow you. Emergency shelter: A space blanket to insulate against cold phases. GPS/Compass: Useless in a pocket realm, so rely on a landmark-etching tool (like a marker to draw arrows on rocks) and a mental map—sketch the elliptical shape and note vegetation clusters.

- **Health and Fitness Prep:** Get in peak physical condition; train for endurance hikes in humid environments (e.g., jungle trekking simulations) to handle the constant moisture and slopes. Hydrate rigorously a week before—build tolerance to the steamy air. Vaccinate or medicate against common cave microbes; consult a multiverse medic for anti-psionic drugs if phasing affects your brain.
