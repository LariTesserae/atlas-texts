Symbiotically, learn to harvest from fauna: those luminescent trails from amoeboids might exude a water-analog slurry—carry syringes or absorbent swabs to extract and purify it. Expect dehydration symptoms early: pack saline tablets, rehydration powders (mixed with synthesized fluids), and vitamin C packets to stave off scurvy-like fatigue.

- **Respiratory Protection:** The air shimmers with warmth but might carry particulates from storms or mineral vapors. Bring N95-style masks rated for extended use (weeks, not hours), plus respirators with built-in filters for ionized gasses (common near urban enclaves). If your multiverse gear includes scanners, use them to test air samples—avoid deep chasms where heat pools denser and viscous. Prepare for throat dryness: lozenges or gels that mimic saliva, and nasal strips to prevent cracking membranes.

- **Thermoregulation and Sun/Radiation Exposure:** The warmth never lets up, but at elevated plateaus or peaks, "sun" exposure (amber light refracted through hazy veils) could cause UV-like burns on exposed skin. Pack lightweight, wide-brimmed hats, long-sleeved UV-blocking clothing (silk or synthetics that breathe in heat without sweating), and full-body suits if exploring valleys where heat intensifies. For evenings/chilling periods (when light cycles dim), layered sleeping bags or thermal blankets to trap body heat. If gravity wavers at extremes, carry ankle/foot supports—boots with adaptive soles that redistribute weight, preventing sprains from sudden "shifts."

- **Medical Kit Essentials:** Stock up on broad-spectrum antibiotics, painkillers (for rare clashes with fauna), and anti-nausea meds (for disorienting physics warps). Prepare for skin issues: moisturizing creams (water-based but evaporation-resistant), bandages for cuts from sharp minerals. If time dilates conversations (lasting days), have stimulants like caffeine pills for forced alertness, but sparingly—overuse could amplify anxiety in silence. Finally, get a full physical from Earth medicine: check heart for heat stress, eyes for light distortions.
