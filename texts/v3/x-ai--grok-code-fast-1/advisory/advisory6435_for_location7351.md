I'd add ankle-high wraps or gaiters to prevent vines or tendrils from latching onto sock-lines, and maybe insect-repellent infused fabrics on the off-chance those "ghost insects" at the fringes decide to bite. Gloves—thin, tactile ones for gripping vine-ladders or reed-rungs without getting splinters, but waterproof enough to handle the constant seep. Finally, don't forget personal hygiene items: waterproof pouches for a toothbrush, soap, and biodegradable wipes, because the moisture everywhere would make it easy for mold or fungal growth to thrive on your skin. Intuitively, I'd feel most at ease in layers I can peel off quickly, as the warmth might spike near the forges; it's like prepping for a hot yoga class in a swamp, but with the added claustro-joy of being surrounded by probing plants.
