Vegetation is edible and abundant (ferns, bamboo-like stalks, flowers), thriving in isolated pockets, but gathering it might feel ethically weird in a place with no apparent fauna. The town is primitive, so expect trade or barter with smiths and artisans who speak in hushed, deliberate words (perhaps a simple language, if any). Intuitively, visiting here isn't about conquering the land; it's about joining its stasis. Fail to respect that, and you might disrupt the balance—maybe causing a rare, inexplicable ripple, like a tool corroding despite the lack of decay. Preparation starts with humility: accept that you're not here to change anything.

Prepare mentally to live like the inhabitants: patient, routine-bound, focused on equilibrium. If you break this, expect subtle consequences, like "forgotten" items fading into non-existence.

#### 3. **Physical and Health Preparation: Adapting to Wet Warmth and Sharp Terrain**
   Your body won't adapt instantly. The perpetual moisture might cause fungal infections, chafing, or dehydration misjudgments (water soothes but doesn't evaporate sweat).    - **Clothing and Gear:** Opt for moisture-wicking materials over waterproof ones; the latter might trap heat in eternal warmth. Hats with brim for constant sun, UV-protective eyewear (since light is diffused but relentless), and lightweight ponchos for any unexpected gusts. Expect everything to glisten perpetually; pack anti-chafing balms for damp skin.
   - **Health Supplies:** Stock up on antifungal creams, electrolytes (despite water availability, the humidity might dehydrate subtly), and broad-spectrum antibiotics (for cuts from sharp rocks). The terrain is uneven—spongy moss in marshes, hard stone elsewhere—so pre-condition for varied footing.    - **Diet and Hydration:** Don't fret too much—water springs and edible plants abound. But taste-test ferns or bamboo stalks cautiously; they seem nourishing, but their muted scents suggest mild flavors. Bring tastier provisions (dried fruits, nuts) to supplement, as a bland diet might worsen mental fatigue. Hydrate constantly, but avoid overfilling basins in jest—it feels disrespectful to the balance.
