Intentionally decide your exit strategy upfront (e.g., via a multiversal portal device, assuming you have one)—situationships of "maybe I'll stay forever" could spiral into fatal resignation when the unchanging roar starts scrambling your thoughts.

Aim to leave with your identity intact; this place might "rewrite" travelers who linger, turning them into more stone-veined statues in the architecture.

### 2. Physical Preparation: Enduring the Heat, Humidity, and Terrain
Physically, this is a scorcher-a fissioning marriage of jungle heat and sauna steam, where the air is so thick it feels like breathing through wet gauze. The extreme elevation (those razor-thin ridges and colossal stepped terraces) demands climbing prowess, but gravity's defiance adds unpredictability— a fall might not just kill you; it could loop you infinitely in a recursive drop. Condition your body for at least 2-3 months prior, simulating conditions where possible.

- **Fitness conditioning**: Build heat endurance and cardiovascular strength. Practice high-altitude-like exercises: hike steep trails to mimic the labyrinthine steps, focusing on balance (the terrain loops back impossibly, so slips could lead to disorienting spirals). Core and leg strength are key—carry weighted backpacks uphill for hours. Aim for dehydration-resistance training by skipping water during sessions to simulate the constant sweat-but-no-relief cycle, but rehydrate aggressively after (in the city, water seeps everywhere, but it's probably undrinkable—mixing in moss or fungal particulates—so pack purification).

- **Clothing and protection**: Lightweight, moisture-wicking, breathable layers are essential—no synthetics that trap heat. Opt for loose-fitting linen or bamboo blends covered in waterproof liners for the perpetual droplets (use seam-taped jackets like Gore-Tex but in antique styles to match the tech-absence vibe). Scarves or balaclavas to cover skin from the condensing beads—the place blurs boundaries with wetness, making everything clammy and prone to fungal growth. Pointed boots with reinforced grips for stepping up basalt terraces; the stone is ancient and worn smooth, but vines make it slippery. Gloves to protect hands from abrasive edges; hats with wide brims for shade (though the perpetual glow casts uniform shadows, the steam can obscure vision).

- **Health gear**: Sunscreen and bug repellent aren't needed (no bugs or sun variation), but pack industrial-strength antifungal creams— the symbiotic fungi binding the walls could infect cuts. Bandages for vine scratches (the plants might "bleed" sap that irritates skin). Eye protection: Steam-blurred goggles or transparents masks to counteract the humid haze obscuring the fractal horizons. Hydration system: Portable filters for the ubiquitous seepage water; assume it's contaminated by minerals (taste for saltiness). Snacks like energy gels for sustained heat—fat-based foods melt too easily. Med kit including electrolytes for constant sweating, anti-nausea meds (the disorienting geometry could induce vertigo), and sedatives if the noise overwhelms.

- **Tech adaptations**: Since the place rejects technology (no glinting metal survives), conceal your multiversal gadgetry in organic-seeming pouches (e.g., vine-woven bags). But peripherals like digital compasses are useless in recursive space; opt for analog backups like maps of recursive patterns or tide-of-blindly following pulses in the walls.

Disaster-proof: Hypothermia is impossible (it's always scorching), but overhydration from the humidity could lead to water intoxication. Monitor your weight daily; the wet air makes sweat evaporate slowly.

Humility check: This place might "test" you with its self-referential architecture, making it gaze back—prepare for identity erosion by journaling core values.

- **Mental health support**: If possible, bring a fellow traveler for accountability; talking aloud could cut through the din. Simulate isolation with solo camping trips in dense forests to practice self-reliance. Consult a therapist for exit counseling— role-play leaving a tempting yet maddening limbo.
