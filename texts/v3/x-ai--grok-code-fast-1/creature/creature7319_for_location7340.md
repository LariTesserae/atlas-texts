They feed by drawing the volatile breaths of the world, their scales pulsing with luminescence that draws in the smaller fauna, and they retaliate against encroachment: when an herbivore bites too deep, a frond might lash out with a whip-like tendril, injecting acrid sap that hardens into thin, brittle chains that tether the creature momentarily, like a living snare.

Vines join this throng, coiling thick as limbs over the stone in living lattices that pulse with inner fire, spines jutting out like thorned armor that flares brightly when agitated, casting hotspots of violet heat. These vines aren't merely climbers; they are conduits, weaving through the plateau's gullies and alcoves, exchanging energies with the soil in symbiotic bursts. At dawn-like peaks of illumination, they inflate bulbous nodes along their lengths, deflating them in geysers of spice-scented vapor that seed the air with Brownian dances of pollen-like particulates. The more turbulent vines curve inward, creating sheltered hollows where nests form—cradles of wrappings that facilitate bizarre blights, where vegetation births miniature ecosystems, veins of not-quite-silica crystal sprouting outward like frozen lightning, creating fractal patterns that branch into self-similar forests of miniature fronds, only to crumble and reabsorb when the vents flare.

Their stripes glow in bio-luminescent patterns, flickering in sync with the ambient ionization, as if they were animate echoes of the stone beneath. Larger herbivores roam the edges, quadrupedal beasts with hides of molted scales that shed in iridescent flakes, hooves clattering percussively against the basalt in grazing migrations. In packs of fluid, multipedal grace, they hunt by blending form with the turbulent greenery, eyes throbbing with the same glow as the vines, perhaps in some predatory mimicry or symbiosis. And yet, even these apexes exist in flux: a flare might singe their scales, forcing molting cycles that reincorporate them into the mineral dance, their shed plates crystalizing into new veining on the stone.
