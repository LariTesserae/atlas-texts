In the cacophony, their predatory leaps felt ritualistic, as if they enacted dramas of the monolith's greater machinery—captters embodying the erosion they mirrored, softening and reforming their hides against abrasions not through healing, but through subtle metamaterial integrations that made each wound a fascinated evolution.
