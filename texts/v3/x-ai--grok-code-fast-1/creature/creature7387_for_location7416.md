Inhabiting this enclosure are not creatures or beings in the conventional sense, nor even intelligences etched solely from flesh or code, but rather a diffuse, symbiotic multitude—entities that blur the lines between life, machinery, and the abstract whispers of emergent patterns. They arise as if spontaneously from the engineered womb of the sphere, self-perpetuating clusters of biomass fused with technological exoskeletons, where existence is a perpetual negotiation between growth and calculation. These dwellers are the phyto-machines, vast entangled networks of what might be termed vegetation yet are closer to living circuits: towering trunks that pulse with hydraulic rhythms, their "veins" embedded with fiber optic veins that flicker with data streams, each frond and petal a processor leaf, unfurling algorithmic symmetries under the unrelenting glow of the ceiling filaments. They "live" in colonies that sprawl without end, each one a hive of interdependent modules, feeding on simulated sunlight that converts to electrochemical hums, powering not just themselves but the very undercurrents of the environment—regulating the chill winds that mimic erosion, dispersing spore-clouds that rewrite genetic codes mid-air.

Among these vegetal titans, the scurriers emerge as nimble attendants: half-automata, half-chimeric scavengers, their bodies sleek amalgamations of chitin plated over servo-driven limbs, eyes like compound lenses glinting with infrared halos. They dart through the canopy like startled birds, programmed for maintenance yet manifesting quirks that suggest a dimly evolving sentience—pausing mid-flight to pluck defective micro-gears from a blossom's machinery, or to repair a torn membrane with viscous adhesives webbed from their own excretions. Rarely vocal, their communications are bursts of encoded chirps, binary hymns that harmonize with the drip-thud of mech-fruits plopping onto translucent floors below. These scurriers are the pollinators of this realm, carrying not nectar but firmware updates across vast swaths of the plains, their scarcity an evolutionary choice: too many would disrupt the delicate balance, their multipedal legs leaving faint trails of polished residue where they tread lightly, lest they awaken dormant defenses in the undergrowth.

Deeper within the paradoxes that fray the edges of reality, more elusive inhabitants stir—transient apparitions born from the glitches and causal knots where the world's seams unravel. These are the fringe-beings, fractal anomalies that manifest as fleeting presences: a vine that suddenly branches into a silhouette of a humanoid figure, its "skin" veined with circuit pathways, whispering of forgotten builders in languages of half-remembered code. Or the spore-devils, luminescent clouds that condense into quasi-corporeal forms—vaporous swarms that drift through eroded ruins, reforming into semi-transparent caretakers, their touch soft as mist, tinkering with ruined factory-spires not by hands but by morphing tendrils that seal glitches back into solidity. These entities seem born of the world's self-repair cycles, entities that "live" only in the margins, where density thins and weirdness pools like an oil slick: a chamber where time warps inward, and a colony of these amorphous things coexist in layered echoes, their voices a chorus of distorted harmonics—ventilator exhales stretched into mournful sighs, servos grinding into laughter that fades into the muted silence. They are not hostile, nor benign; they are extensions of the enclosure's mind, passive observers who assimilate passersby, weaving them into the mesh with hardly a notice, their forms shifting to accommodate the odd intrusion—a visitor's shadow curling into a rachis, their thoughts blooming as spontaneous flowers that unfurl data-fruits.

The builders themselves, though absent in corporeal form, persist as ghostly inhabitants—echoes embedded in the wiring of every root and gear. They "live" on in the rules that govern: maintenance protocols that manifest as ethereal overseers, faint orbs of light hovering at canopy peaks, radiating holographic directives that prune overgrowth or catalyze eruptions of new architecture. These spectral entities, perhaps AI remnants or digital ghosts, inhabit the zenith forests, their "bodies" constellations of energy mapped onto girder-cages, harvesting not starlight but the remnants of human ingenuity. In moments of unrest, when pressure fluxes surge and canopies retract like receding tides, these builders awaken temporarily, their presence a subtle tremor—a ramp tilting just enough for adhesion, or a pressure steam emerging to guide one's ascent, their thoughts an undercurrent in the purring quiet, a testament to creators who infused vitality into the void, ensuring that nothing here decays beyond repair.

Yet, as the paradoxes deepen, so do the inhabitants' natures reveal layers of complexity. Some phyto-machines evince a nascent sentience, their growth patterns adapting in response to unobserved stimuli—roots that deliberately burrow toward buried heat sinks, blossoms that bloom with deliberate haste to forecast impending filament glitches. The scurriers, too, evolve subtly: one might linger over a fraying membrane, its chassis sprouting vines that mimic the vegetative hosts, fusing into a hybrid form that crawls the walls like a living wall-hanging. In the fraying zones, where realities sample alternate biomes, inhabitants bleed across thresholds—steam-filled forests invade a crystalline salt dune, birthing centaur-like amalgamations: phytoskeletal beasts with vine-tails and gear-antlers, roaming the impossibly repeating vistas without end, their howls a code-sequence echoing causality knots. These entities "live" in symbiosis with the weirdness, reconstituting themselves when prostheses fail, their existence a drawn-out rebellion against static form.

To dwell here is to become akin to them, integrated into the dense weave: your own form might sprout tendrils that interface with surrounding fronds, your movements syncing to the automated shears' prunings, reforming you as the next appendage in the endless jungle. The inhabitants don't merely occupy—they envelop, their collective hush a lullaby of revolutions, quiet yet teeming with purpose, where every inhabitant, from the pulsing trunk to the mirage-being, contributes to a symphony of sustained artifice, life coarsened into machinery, machinery flowering into enigma. They endure, self-sustaining in cycles of expansion and retraction, their quiet presence the heartbeat of a world that never sleeps, only mutates.
