Parasitic vessels—tendrils woven from larger beasts' extensions—thrum deeper, harvesting essences from the greenery and water fixtures alike, injecting them back into the stalkers' orbs to sustain the osmotic balances, their symbiotic overlay ensuring that no decay unravels the whole.
