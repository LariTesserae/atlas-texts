Aeon perceives not through eyes or senses, but through the interwoven threads of essence—of what is, what could be, and what requires gentle tending. The horde, with its telepathic whispers and ritual decays, is not monstrous or flawed; it is simply a mirror of what happens when existence presses too close, too damp, too dark. Aeon's benevolence sees no need for erasure or conquest; instead, it sees opportunity—a chance to harmonize the weirdness, to alleviate the slow strangle of stagnation, and to uplift the collective being toward fuller expression. For the wise know that even in saturation, there is room for breath, for the unseen, for growth beyond ritual hoarding and swift decay.
