Conquest here is an impossibility; even the predators close in over seasons, melding rather than devouring. The realm is not a place of loss or struggle, but of endless birth, where oblivion births anew, and presence unfolds in drifts of becoming. The power, benevolent above all, feels a kinship in this—a mirror of its own nature, which favors nurturing over reshaping, enlightenment over dominion.

Yet benevolence, when wise, is not passive; it inclines toward gentle augmentation, a nudge that aligns with the realm's rhythms rather than disrupts them. The power does not arrive as a prophet or healer, imposing lessons on the slow inhabitants, for that would be a violation of their essence. Instead, it manifests through subtle emanations, harmonizing with the existing flows. In this way, the communal will that shifts corridors overnight grows wiser, not through imposed knowledge, but by revealing latent harmonies—patterns in the terraced ascents that mirror the spirals of distant galaxies, or the flutter of winged fish that now carry whispers of connection to other submerged worlds beyond this one.

The inhabitants feel this presence not as intrusion, but as an extension of their own accretion. Over epochs (for here, change measures in such spans), the inhabitants begin to adapt: their forms bud softer outgrowths, not just for camouflage, but for shared communion—tendrils lengthening to weave wider networks, allowing pods to extend their mergers across greater distances, blending rival bands in vast, slow circlets that encircle entire plateaus.
