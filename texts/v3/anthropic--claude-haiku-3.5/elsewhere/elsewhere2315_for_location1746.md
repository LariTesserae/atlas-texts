Their membranes become increasingly complex, developing crystalline structures that mirror the stone's geological memory.

It is a continuous negotiation of boundaries, where the definition of individual organisms becomes blurred. The stone breathes through the creatures. The creatures are structured by the stone. Their interactions create a third substance — neither fully mineral nor fully biological — that pulses with its own strange intelligence.
