When it passes, the crystal lattice of the ground subtly rearranges, recording and responding to these distant communications.

These membranes are points of transition, where the solid becomes liquid, where information becomes matter.

The connection between this Resonance Desert and the previous liquid realm is not physical, but informational. The Dormant Encoders of the liquid realm resonate with the information structures of the desert, each holding fragments of a larger, more complex narrative.
