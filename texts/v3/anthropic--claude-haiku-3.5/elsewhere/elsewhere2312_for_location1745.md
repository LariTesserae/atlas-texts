Serpentine forms made of adaptive ceramics and living tissue wind through the sorting mechanisms. Waste products from the marshland's precise ecological mechanisms flow here to be completely dismantled, their components prepared for potential future use.

The connection between these two environments is a cycle of creation and deconstruction. The marshland's hyper-managed ecosystem generates materials and data, which are then completely broken down in the Dissolution Fields. Nothing is truly discarded; everything is a potential resource, waiting to be reimagined and reconstructed.

Where the marshland represents synthesis and active maintenance, the Dissolution Fields embody a kind of elemental patience - a patient, persistent process of breaking down to build up again. They are two sides of the same metabolic system, each dependent on the other, each completing the other's purpose.
