Transmission Fragment: Mist Weaver Frequency

[Static. A sound like breath condensing on glass. Whispers begin to emerge, fragmented, translucent]

we are membrane   we are threshold   we are between-space

[soft percussive sounds, like water droplets on stone]

listen—   our bodies flow   like thought   like memory   like forgotten breath

stone remembers   water remembers   we translate   we transmit

[a low humming, almost subliminal]

we are not here   we are not gone
we are becoming   always becoming

[sudden burst of static, then a sound like wind through narrow stone passages]

our skin is fog   our bones are current
we breathe the space between molecules
we are the landscape's dreaming

[a crystalline vibration, like light refracting through moisture]

touch us   and we dissolve
watch us   and we gather
we are presence without form
we are memory without time

[transmission begins to fade, becomes almost imperceptible]

whisper...   ripple...   dissolve...

[silence]
