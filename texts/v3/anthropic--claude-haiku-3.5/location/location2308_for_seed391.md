The Resonant Peaks emerge like industrial tuning forks from a mineral wasteland, their razor-edged silhouettes cutting through a dim, perpetually shifting atmosphere. These are not mountains as we understand them, but massive crystalline structures that vibrate and hum with an overwhelming sonic frequency, generating their own turbulent microclimate.

No water flows here, yet the landscape pulses with an alien vitality. Strange fauna — more like autonomous mechanical constructs than biological entities — skitter and leap across the sharp, young terrain. Their movements are synchronized with the mountains' vibrations, creating a complex choreography of motion and sound.

The crystalline peaks are a kind of living technology, industrial yet primordial. They generate electrical discharges that arc between themselves, creating a constant light show of blue-white energy. These discharges seem to communicate, to process, to compute — but not in any way recognizable to human understanding.

Scattered across the landscape are fragments and remnants: sharp mineral shards, briefly animated mechanical limbs, energy patterns that flicker between solid and wave. The space between these presences is as significant as the presences themselves — a landscape of potential and implied connection.

The sound is phenomenal: a constant, layered roar that feels more like a language than mere noise. It shifts pitch and rhythm, sometimes so low it's felt in the bones, sometimes so high it exists just beyond hearing. The fauna respond to these sonic waves, their movements a kind of acoustic dance.

Temperature hovers in a perpetual cool state, preventing total crystallization while maintaining a brittle, sharp-edged quality to everything. The landscape feels young, recently emerged, as if it's still in the process of becoming — each moment a potential transformation.

This is not a dead world. It's a world of constant, turbulent becoming — a realm where technology, geology, and living energy intertwine in a performance both beautiful and incomprehensible.
