The place breathes darkness. Not an absence of light, but a living darkness that seeps and pulses, thick as membrane. 

This is a low-lying terrain of mineral-glass formations, obsidian-like surfaces that curve and intersect like frozen sound waves. The ground isn't ground, but a dense layering of translucent black structures that shift microscopically, creating a landscape of near-constant, imperceptible movement.

Fauna dominates everything. Not creatures as we understand them, but mobile energy patterns that surge and swarm in overwhelming densities. They're not quite solid, not quite liquid - more like living sound-shapes that pulse and interweave, creating complex three-dimensional choreographies. Their movements generate the deafening soundscape: a constant thrumming, clicking, vibrating chorus that seems to originate from inside the mineral structures themselves.

Occasional dampness condenses on the glass-like surfaces - not water precisely, but a silvery fluid that beads and slides, leaving momentary trails that quickly reabsorb into the terrain. The coolness isn't temperature so much as a sensation of potential energy, something waiting.

The scale extends beyond immediate perception - this isn't a small place, but a regional ecosystem of strange interactions. Each movement of the energy-creatures causes microscopic shifts in the mineral structures, which in turn subtly alter the sonic landscape.

This is a world of pure process: no construction, no technology, just relentless biological dynamism. The darkness isn't empty, but packed with potential - a living darkness that breathes, remembers, transforms.
