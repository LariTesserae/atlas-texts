In the depths of a warm, liquid realm, light dies quickly. This is an oceanic world of profound darkness, where mechanical structures drift like strange leviathans through an ancient, erosion-smoothed seascape.

The water is not merely surrounding — it is everything. Thick, almost gel-like in its density, it conducts sound in thunderous waves that reverberate through impossible distances. The acoustic environment is not just loud but overwhelming: a constant, multilayered industrial chorus of grinding, humming, clicking mechanisms that seem partially alive.

Mechanical entities — not quite machines, not quite organisms — float and pulse through this liquid space. They are sparse but abundant, scattered like strange mechanical plankton. Some are massive industrial structures with segmented, articulated forms that suggest both engineered purpose and evolutionary adaptation. Others are smaller, more intricate: spinning, filtering, processing entities that seem to metabolize the dark water itself.

No traditional plant life exists here. Instead, mineral formations — crystalline, metallic, quasi-organic — emerge from the seafloor. These are not static structures but slowly shifting landscapes that conduct energy, transmit information, respond to the mechanical entities' movements with subtle transformations.

The entire environment feels primordial yet intensely active. Predation occurs not through biological consumption but through energy transfer, information exchange, mechanical reconfiguration. The fauna — if one can call them that — are technological organisms that navigate, communicate, and interact through complex rhythmic patterns.

This is a world of constant motion, yet so ancient that its fundamental structures seem settled into their ultimate configuration. High above the dark seafloor, mechanical currents flow like blood vessels, carrying information and energy across regional scales.

Weirdness permeates everything: nothing here follows expected biological or mechanical logic. The boundary between living and constructed has dissolved completely.
