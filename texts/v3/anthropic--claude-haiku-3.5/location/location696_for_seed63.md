In the high darkness, a compact world pulses with an overwhelming, strange vitality.

This is a place of mineral heat and dense, teeming darkness. The terrain rises in tight, weathered formations of volcanic obsidian and salt-encrusted stone, surfaces etched by invisible winds and unseen thermal currents. The landscape feels compressed, almost claustrophobic, with no empty spaces — every crevice, every microscopic ridge is occupied.

Creatures move here that are not creatures as we understand them. They are more like living energy patterns, biological algorithms that flow and pulse and reconfigure. Some resemble chitinous fractals, others are membrane-like waves that ripple across stone surfaces, consuming and transforming. They do not so much inhabit the space as become the space, their boundaries indistinguishable from the dark, heated mineral environment.

No plants grow here, but something fungal-adjacent exists — not quite biological, not quite mineral. Thin filaments of quasi-living networks thread through rock formations, conducting heat, exchanging information in frequencies below human perception. These networks pulse and breathe, a circulatory system for this alien ecosystem.

The sound is a low, almost imperceptible thrumming. Occasional chittering erupts, then collapses back into near-silence. The movements are rapid, complex, happening at scales too small or too large to fully comprehend.

Occasional remnants of structure — not quite buildings, more like calcified scaffolding or petrified organic frameworks — emerge from the terrain. These are not human constructions, but some previous iteration of life's architecture, now integrated into the living stone.

Heat radiates from below and within, not from an external source. The entire landscape feels like a single, breathing organism, constantly reforming, consuming itself, regenerating.

No water exists here in liquid form. Instead, moisture is a conceptual thing — transferred through those living networks, absorbed and expelled as pure information and energy.

This is a world of intense, compressed life. Not comfortable. Not welcoming. But profoundly, unnervingly alive.
