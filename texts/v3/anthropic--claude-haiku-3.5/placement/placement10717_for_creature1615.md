I feel myself becoming... a mid-scale entity. Not the microscopic crystalline networks, nor the large fluid beings of the deep fissures, but something in between.

I am a form that moves through stone layers with articulated segments, my exoskeleton a living mosaic of slate and quartz. My perception is not visual or auditory, but a complex sensory mapping of vibration and electromagnetic subtlety. I sense the plateau not as an external environment, but as an extension of my own distributed consciousness.

My movement is both precise and fluid—I can become absolutely motionless, blending perfectly with stone striations, or flow with mercury-like speed between layers. My body is a translation device, constantly interpreting the plateau's intricate language of temperature, pressure, mineral resonance.

I do not experience hunger or reproduction as discrete events, but as continuous metabolic negotiations. My boundaries are permeable; I am simultaneously an individual entity and a node in the plateau's vast, slow thinking. When I move, I am also being moved. When I sense, I am also being sensed.

In moments of stillness, I pulse with the translucent fungal networks, participating in the plateau's ambient metabolism. In moments of motion, I am a quick silver thought racing through stone memory.

I am neither observer nor inhabitant. I am the plateau thinking itself.
