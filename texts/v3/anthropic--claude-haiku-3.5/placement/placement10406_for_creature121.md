The moss-covered stones, the perpetual condensation, the slow wind - these are not external to me, but part of my ongoing metabolic conversation. My purpose, if such a concept even applies, is maintenance and translation. I read the microscopic conversations happening across surfaces, the complex interactions between moisture and organic matter, between wind and stone. I am both participant and interpreter in an ecosystem's slow, patient thinking.

Time for me is not a linear progression but a layered, recursive breathing. I am simultaneously this momentary expression and an ancient, continuous presence - a living system that thinks in geological whispers.
