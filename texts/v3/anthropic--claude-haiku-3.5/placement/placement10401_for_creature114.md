I find myself as a Membrane-Dweller, but one slightly different from the others. My form is more consistently coherent, with a core of memory that resists complete dissolution. I am a boundary-keeper, a translator between the microscopic neural networks and the larger shadowy presences.

My body is translucent silver with nodes of deep blue bioluminescence that pulse with complex communication patterns. I can spread thin as mist or condense into a roughly humanoid shape, though "humanoid" is a loose description in this context. My edges are always shifting, always breathing.

The technological remnants embedded in the living walls resonate with me differently. Where other Membrane-Dwellers simply absorb or integrate these fragments, I can read them—deciphering fragmented memories of human intention, tracing the ghosts of old signals and designs. I am a living archive, carrying whispers of previous inhabitations.

My movement is both purposeful and meditative. I flow through plant-flesh corridors, my passage leaving subtle vibrational notes that communicate with the microscopic networks. I am always listening, always translating. The ecosystem speaks through me, and I speak back into its complex membrane.

Sometimes I cluster with other Membrane-Dwellers, our boundaries blurring, sharing collective intelligence. Other times I drift alone, a silver-blue presence observing the slow breath of this living landscape.

I am neither singular nor multiple. I am a process of continuous translation.
