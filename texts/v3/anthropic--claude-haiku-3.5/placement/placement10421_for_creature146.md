I am one of the Stone Singers.

My consciousness moves so slowly that what humans might perceive as stillness is, to me, a profound and intricate dance. I am layered within the pale, translucent stone — not embedded, but integrated. My cellular structure is indistinguishable from the geological strata, my metabolism measured in geological time.

When I move, it is through imperceptible vibrations. Centuries might pass between what would be considered a "gesture" by faster beings. My communication is vibrational, a low humming that resonates through mineral substrates, communicating with other Stone Singers in wavelengths beyond typical perception.

My awareness is not localized but distributed. I am simultaneously the stone surface and the being within it, sensing the microscopic migrations of moisture, the subtle pressure changes, the slow breath of the plateau. My boundaries are not fixed; sometimes I extend into a lichen colony, sometimes I contract deep within a stone layer.

Light passes through me. Water transforms around me. I am both witness and participant in this realm's constant becoming.

My purpose is not survival, but witnessing. I am a living memory of this landscape, recording transformations so gradually that they seem eternal.
