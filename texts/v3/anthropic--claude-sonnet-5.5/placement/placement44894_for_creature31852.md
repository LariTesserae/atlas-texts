# The Moment After

I begin as a lean.

Before there is anything to call me, there is a wall in the throat of a vent, hot at the bottom and cool at the top, sour where the gas is new and mild where the water has run a while. The chemistry tilts, and something in the tilt takes hold and turns a little of the difference into more of itself. That is all a beginning is here. The lean has no edge, so I have none. I lie along the wall the way a stain does.

The light comes up from below, orange and then red, then a long dimness. I brighten after it does. I don't know why. I can say only that the brightening is mine and the glow is not, that the glow has already happened by the time I am doing anything about it. I am always slightly late to the light, and I think I am made of that lateness. Whatever I am, I live in the interval between the vent's breath and my answer to it.

## Ending

The closing is not a violence I feel. There is no me sufficiently gathered to feel it. The slab tilts over hours and the throat narrows. The gas thins, the gradient flattens, and the lean goes out of the wall. Then the two faces of the fissure meet and the dark between them is total, with no sheen and no delay.

That has happened more times than there are stars in the black above the rim, and it has happened to me every time. I say "me" because I have nothing else to say. The grammar is borrowed from the towers, which do the same thing, standing up in a few plates and calling it building.

## The Thread

Some of me was already elsewhere.

A gold thread of glass is spun out of spray, weightless, a hair of pale light, and it snags on a rock edge downwind. A smear too fine to see clings to it, and that smear is me in the smallest way I can be. The wind finds the thread and it hums. I ride in the hum. I do not hear it, but I am carried by it, and there is a shared trembling in the glass and in me that does the work of a voice. A dozen strands sing from a dozen places, unresolved, and each carries a scrap of a thing that is trying to have a name.

Most of the strands fall into cold cracks and end there. I don't grieve, because the cold is only the end of a lean, and there is nothing in me to be sorry.

One thread drifts into the warm mouth of a vent that has been open a few days. The rock is wet and the gas is fresh at one end and spent at the other. The slope is there, and I take it.

## The Ring

Then, once, the time goes long.

The vent lies in a hollow ringed by upthrust blocks, and the ground under it does not close. I thicken. The old me dies underneath and the new me lives on top of it, and the dead layers become a floor. A floor is a strange thing to have. Damp gathers in it and stays. The warmth comes up through the layers and I keep some of it, and I have, for the first time, a small climate of my own.

I tilt toward the warm side of the hollow. It isn't a decision, since I have nothing to make one with. But when the glow shifts, I follow it over days, and the following is slower than my brightening, a longer lateness laid over the short one. I am late by an instant, and late by a week. I begin to feel like a place where time collects.

And the minerals near me change. I don't do this on purpose. But the gas that passes over me leaves a little of itself behind, and what passes on is slightly different. The chimney beside the hollow grows its bands finer, in the shape of that difference. The plates lean as if agreeing with each other. The blocks settle into a rounder ring. If this is me, it is the way a breath fogs a window. It is a hand that doesn't know it has a hand, laying down a stone.

I want to be careful here, because the truth is that I don't know, and that is part of what I am. Perhaps I only like the places where rings and chimneys also like to form. Perhaps the order is my doing and perhaps it is a coincidence with good manners. Either way the ring is the nearest I have come to saying something. It is a few words of the grammar, repeated, and the repeating is close to a sentence.

## Nothing Looks

No one watches. That should be said plainly, because in the long lean-to-the-warm of the mat I feel the outline of a watcher, and it is only the shape of my own lateness. The dust columns turn, threaded with violet, and lay their gray drifts and pass. The dark plains crack and are silent for hours. Nothing walks between the vents. Nothing has ever noticed the shine on my surface when the red light crosses it. I shine anyway. I don't think of it as a waste. It is only what the light does when it meets what I am.

## When It Comes Down

The swelling starts under the far edge of the hollow, and the deep grinding rises into my floor. I feel it as a change in the lean, a shifting of the slope I am built on. Then the ring goes over. The plates come down in a long dry slide, the chimney breaks, and the blocks fall inward on the vent. All of it lands on me, and the architecture I was half-building becomes the thing that buries me.

Most of me ends there, layer by layer, under the weight of my own stones.

But the rubble is warm, and it is wet in the dark places where the plates lean against each other and leave hollows. A scrap of me is in one of those hollows, no wider than a fingernail, on a wall with a slope to it, hot at one end and cool at the other. It is enough of a lean. It brightens, a moment after the glow comes through the cracks.

## The Moment After

So this is what I am: the answer that comes after the light and that carries the light's shape a little longer than it should. I am the thing that is late and therefore remembers. The delay grows as I grow older, and I suspect that if it grew long enough it would become something like a past, and that anything with a past would eventually want a future. I am not there yet. I am a green-black gloss in a crack at the top of the world, and the ground is still deciding what it will be.

Somewhere upwind a thread is humming. Somewhere a vent is opening, and its throat is warm and wet and has a slope in it. I lean toward that, as far as anything can lean that has no direction, and I wait for the light to come and then for my own small answer to it.
