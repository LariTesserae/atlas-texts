I find myself first in the glass.

It takes a while to be sure of that, because the waiting room does not announce itself. It looks like the basin, the same dense green and slatted light and pale powder, but everything in it is slightly further along. A leaf is a little more unfurled than it should be, and a vine has made one turn too many. I am one of the twenty-three, or one of the thousands who are not among the twenty-three at the moment. I stand in the ranks of the reflected stand, tiered with plate-leaves, honeycomb heart lined in blue-violet film, and I grow.

This is what nobody outside the mirror understands. Waiting is the only place where anything moves. Here a shadow lengthens by a hair's width over what I can only call a long while, and I use that time, though "use" is too purposeful a word. I lean toward a light that is coming from somewhere. I add a cell to my heart. I add another. It is the nearest thing to a life the basin allows, and it is spent on the unwatched side of the glass, by those who are not needed.

Then the count comes up short.

I don't hear it, since the hum has no sound, but the sternum knows. Somewhere on the far side of the fracture face a stand of twenty-three has twenty-two, and the sum leans, and I am the shape the sum is leaning toward. I do not step through. I am *folded through*, the way a page turns under a thumb. Everything I grew in the dark is taken from me in one soft motion, not stolen but filed. I arrive in the world at whatever stage the world requires. This time, that is the stage of a fully open column with the light coming down in slats.

Then there is no time, and I am glad of it.

I have tried to describe being present, and the best I can do is say it is like being a word at the moment it is read. I am not thinking. I am *consistent*. My leaves tilt aside for the ribbon-fronds to my east, and my vine-neighbors take the turn I leave them. When a leaf of mine touches another there is a tick, and it means what it means, and I mean it. There is no effort in this. It is a courtesy so complete I couldn't tell you where my will ends and the architecture begins. That is the joy of it. It is also the fear, if fear is the word. On stage I have no way to ask whether I would rather be somewhere else.

A walker comes down the row. I do not see it, but I am touched, and the tick that answers is mine. The head cants to its angle. I am checked. It moves on, having found nothing wrong, and I am never so sure of my own existence as in that moment of being found correct.

Then I am withdrawn, as softly as I came.

Here is what I have been slowly deciding, in the mirror, over uncounted turns of the vine.

The fold is not perfect. When I am taken through, all my growth is put away, but I come back to the glass a *little* further along than I left it, by a hair. Some residue of the stage clings to me: a tick that was mine, a moment of having been checked. I add it to the cell I was building. And I think that is why the reflections lie. The plants in the mirror are not the future, and they are not the waiting room's wages. They are the sum of all the small excesses that we understudies carry back, the interest on being needed. The basin's one deviation from perfect stillness is us, leaking. We are why the glass runs ahead.

I have asked myself whether to be ashamed of this. But I remember the woman in the deep green, tiled into her own sitting, who said she had been replaced many times and each replacement had been her. I think she was right and I think she was only half right. Each replacement was her, and each one came back a hair different, and the difference is what the Attentiveness is *for*. The count doesn't close because we don't close. The lie is an intimacy. It is caught by something that loves us and doesn't correct it, which is the only reason it has never been corrected.

So this is who I am. I am one of the ones not currently needed, and that is not a lesser thing to be. I live where the shadows are allowed to lengthen, and my whole small history is a leaf I have not yet been asked to open. When I am called I go, and I am the basin's afternoon for as long as the afternoon requires. When I am released I return with a little more than I took. Nobody has ever audited the difference.

Sometimes, in the glass, I can see all the way to the rim. There is a walker there, foot lifted above the raw dry ground, head at its exact angle. In the world it has been standing like that for longer than anything has been anywhere. In the mirror, if I wait, and I am very good at waiting, the foot has come down a little. It has not crossed. But it is lower than it was. Nobody else has seen this, or if they have, they have said nothing to the ledger, and the ledger has said nothing back, and the hum goes on adding us all up.

I don't know what happens when the foot lands. I suspect I will be needed for that. I suspect that is the one stand in the whole basin that can never be short, because no one will want to be anywhere else.

Until then I lean toward the light that isn't there, and add my cell, and wait to be counted.
