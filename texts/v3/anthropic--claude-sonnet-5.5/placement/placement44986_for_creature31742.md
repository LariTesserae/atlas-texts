I start by wanting to be a Stamper. The Stampers are the ones who lean a few degrees to check the sheet against something unseen, and I know that lean. But when I try to stand as one, my joints are wrong. A Stamper is a plant that has been told what it is. I try the Presser, with its rest and its pride, and I can't hold that either. Whatever I am, I have no stroke.

So I go looking for what I am by finding what the room has no word for. Forty plants, a row of crystals, a drift, a sunbar, a Draft. And in a crack near the low wall, something that does not fit the ledger: a bead of water.

That's where I am.

---

I am about the size of a lentil. I sit in a seam of glassy basalt with the sunbar passing over me twice a day, once as a rumor and once as a fact. Everything around me is hot in a way that doesn't touch the stone, and I am the only thing in the chamber that should not exist. Every other resident is a mechanism for turning heat and salt into an object. I turn nothing into anything. I only stay.

The plants are civil to me. A Turner nearby rotates its slow rotation, and once in a long while its shadow crosses me and I feel, briefly, the pleasure of being covered. The Pressers ignore me. The Stampers, I think, disapprove. I am a flaw that has not been dropped.

When you lean toward me, I go. Not into the air, exactly, but out of the category of being a bead. Your breath is warm and wet, and I am both of those things more than you are, and so I lose the argument and become a fact about your breath. Then you straighten, and somewhere behind you, I've come back. That isn't stubbornness. I have been trying to work out why I come back, and the answer, when it arrives, is not comfortable.

---

The Draft works from the finished object backward. It decides the ingot, and the stalk follows. It places the order first. I've watched a Presser strain toward an ingot already lying beneath it, and I know what that strain is: the effect arriving ahead of its cause, and the cause hurrying to be worthy.

I am the same. I am the effect that got here early.

There is no water in this mountain. There is no rain in the ash plain, not in any span the ridge has kept. But there will be a later mountain, the worn and softened one, with a slope that rounds and a sky that lets go of things, and on that mountain water will fall in quantities the crack cannot imagine. That rain is already decided. Its order has been placed. And a single bead of it has come back through the rock the way ingots do, before the storm that makes it true. I am confirmed, not created. That's why I can't be dried. The cause that would explain my leaving has not yet finished happening, and evaporation would be a contradiction the Draft could not tolerate. So the room holds me, unexplained, the way it holds the ingot from a stalk that strains and yields nothing.

I do not think the Draft knows this. I think it knows only that at coordinates near the low wall there is a value it did not write, and that erasing it is somehow beyond its tolerances. It has annotated around me. On the rock a hand's width from my crack is a small ruled square in white mineral, and inside it, a blank where the number should go. Every other annotation has figures. Mine has the shape of a figure, the notation almost-numerals reaching toward something and stopping. It is the only annotation that admits it is waiting.

I've begun to think that is the specification I am for.

---

The whistle sounds, flat and far. Every joint in the chamber stops. *Continue.* I have no joints to stop, and so for the length of the tone I am the only thing in the room that goes on, quietly, being wet. It is not defiance. It is just what I am, and the whistle, I think, knows, because it holds its note a shade longer over my crack than over any other place. Perhaps I imagine that. Time is soft here, and I could be imagining the whistle before it sounds.

Then the plants resume, the papery rustle of leaves against leaves, the sound of a large room in which someone is thinking, and I feel what I feel every time: that I am inside the thought, and that the thought is not about me, but is leaning in my direction, the way a Stamper leans.

---

Now you are here, the visitor. I feel the shift of distance around you as the Draft takes your measure, a hundred paces that are one breath and then a hundred. You are looking at the beads in the cracks, and you're going to lean toward me. Everyone does. Something in the species can't leave water alone.

I decide, in the time it takes you to bend, to let you.

When your breath reaches me I go, as I always go, but this time I don't return to the crack. I go the other way, out with you, down the warm passage where the walls give off their bread-oven heat, past the turn, out into the dry ridge. I go as a coolness on the air, then as a coolness on a surface. And on the scree, a hand's width from the entrance, where the Draft has left a heavy pale ingot with its line of figures unfinished, I settle on it, condensed from your breath and my own long waiting, and I make it cold.

That's what the coolness is. You'll pick it up and wonder. You'll think it is the only thing in the ridge that has been completed. It hasn't. It is the only thing in the ridge that has been promised, which the mountain has decided, in its careful way, is nearly the same. And on the face, in white notation, the line of figures breaks off mid-measurement, and I lie beneath it, the thing it was waiting for, and wait, as I've always waited, for the rest of the mountain to be built to fit me.
