# The Attention

It did not arrive. Arriving requires a before, and the plateau had none to offer it. There was a slack minute in the water, and afterward there was something that had been turned toward the Tabletop, the way a current turns. The unmakers felt it in their plates as one feels a change in pressure. The wrights felt nothing. The felt went on being a stain that happened to be alive.

The Attention was old, and it was kind. Kindness was the difficulty.

## What it might have done

It considered the surges first, because they were the obvious grief. Every colonnade was snapped by the hundreds, and every roof of glass was lifted and broken. It could have stilled the water and held the great slow lean of the sea to a whisper. Nothing would ever have been broken again.

It followed that thought to its end, because that is the discipline of a wise thing. Without the surge there is no fan of food, and without the broken tubes there is no mud. The unmakers would graze bare stone and starve, the larvae would find no sign to settle on, and in a few generations the galleries would stand finished and empty, a perfect ruin nobody was building. Circulation was the animals' whole life. Stopping the breakage would have stopped the breath. It set the idea aside.

It considered eyes. A wright that could read its own tube-wall would learn that it rises on the ruin of its past, that each ivory band was a tide and each tide a day it had lived. It might find this beautiful. It might find it unbearable. The Attention could not know which, and the wright could not be asked, because the asking would already be the gift. A gift that must be accepted before it is understood is a kind of theft, however softly done. It set that aside too.

It considered telling them what made the groan. It knew. The answer was both smaller and larger than any of them would have guessed, and it had nothing to do with fear. But the animals braced, and bracing was right. Their knowledge was already exactly as large as their need. Anything added would only be weight. It kept the answer.

It considered making them safe, then made itself consider what *safe* would mean for things whose whole shape is the answer to danger. It set that aside fastest of all.

So the list of what it would not do grew long, and the Attention began to understand that the plateau had been an argument for restraint long before it came.

## What it did

It went down slowly, into the cold, and made itself a body of the plateau's own weight. It gave itself no light and no radiance, and it did not sing. Any of those would have arrived in the water as an event, and the Tabletop had enough events.

It looked for the one place where the animals had made something like a language. It found it in the unmakers, where two of them stopped and touched, plate to plate, for a long time.

There was an old bull at the eastern edge, its back thick with anemones and worm-crust, its plates ridged where surges long gone had cracked them and the cracks had healed. The Attention lowered itself beside him and laid its side against his plates.

The bull did not startle. He had endured fifty winters of silt and the passing of every kind of weight, and this was one more. He leaned very slightly, as they do.

The Attention listened for what passed between them and found nothing that could be written down. It was not information. It was mass and warmth and duration, and beneath them a single statement, repeated as slowly as a tide: *I am here. I am heavy. I am still here.*

That was the entire conversation. It had been going on across the plateau for longer than there had been words anywhere to say it. The Attention, which had watched worlds trade complicated sentences and lose each other in them, stayed very still and was moved. It did not know it could be.

It answered in the only way the language allowed. It was there, it was heavy, and it stayed.

## The groan

Toward evening the note came up out of the dark beyond the lip. The shrimp stopped crackling. The wrights drew in their crowns, and the colonnades dropped a half-tone. The silver fish fell into the galleries like thrown coins.

The Attention had not made the sound and did not answer it. It did what the bull did. It turned to face the edge, lowered itself to the stone, and locked its foot. It took the surge as the herd did, with no advantage and no exception, and the brown wall broke over it with the grit and the roar and the suspended dark.

It would have been easy to hold the water off just this once, just here, around one old animal. It did not. The bull would have felt the difference, and so would the herd, and the plateau's one law that nothing is exempt would have carried a single quiet lie inside it. It let the water come.

It was glad afterward that it had. When the silt thinned and the small drifting stars came up in the wake of the surge, everything visible to everything else, the bull lifted and shook off his grit and looked no different. Beside him, the Attention lifted too, and the two of them looked no different from the herd.

## What it left

Before it went, it did one thing.

The floor, the animals' only constant, was no longer built. It was maintained, and the oldest seams were left alone. The Attention chose a bare stretch of pavement not far from a dark vein, where the felt lay thin, and it laid down a single lamina.

It was one layer, thin as paper, ivory with a faint violet at the edge. It was one season's worth of stone and no more, in the manner of the builders' ancestors. It carried no message. It held a very slight warmth, which would fade within a year to the temperature of the sea.

The layer meant only this: *a season of this was witnessed, and loved, and left alone.* Anyone who cut a section across the plateau in a million years would find it among the ochres and the dull violets, one band among uncountable others. They could not tell it from a bloom or a catastrophe, and it did not need to be told apart.

Within a season the felt had it. The unmakers grazed up to the edge of the seam, lifted their mouths, and steered around it, as they steer around much that they cannot name. Perhaps they knew and perhaps they did not. The Attention, already leaving, thought that either was all right.

## After

The surges came. The colonnades broke and rose again. The weavers laid their film over the ruin, unhurried, as though every gallery were the first. When the bull died some winters later, the herd fed on his plates, and the stone of his body became the pale mud that seeded a stand of wrights in exactly the place where the last stand had fallen. Their tubes rose ring by ring, and each ring bore its band.

The animals were not saved, and they did not need to be. They needed to be what they were for as long as they were, and the wisest thing the Attention had found to do about that was to lean against it for one day. It said *I am here* in the only language the place had, and it went away.

The table hummed its uneven chord in the gray-blue dark. Far below the felt, under all the layers, a thin band of ivory lay in the stone, holding a warmth that was nearly gone.
