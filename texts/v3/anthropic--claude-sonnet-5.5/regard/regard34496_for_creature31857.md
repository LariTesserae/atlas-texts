# The Attention

## Arrival

It does not descend. Anything that descended would be a new fact, and the plateau has room only for the facts it has.

It comes as frost. On one night, on the north face of one stone on the southern swell, there is slightly more rime than there should be. In the morning a heron lowers its bill to that stone and draws the frost off, as herons have done every morning for longer than the morning has had a name. The heron then holds still an hour longer than its stillness requires. No one notices. The meal was not sweeter, since they have no such sense. It was *attended*, the way a sentence is attended by the person reading it.

The Attention spends its first season learning to breathe once a minute. It does not hurry, because hurrying would be the first discourtesy.

## What it understood

It began by looking for the wound, because that is where help usually starts.

It found no hunger, since the frost is enough. It found no fear, no cruelty, no one crushed beneath another, only the pavers bearing weight with the serenity of a cellar. It found the hospitality of the full house, and it found that hospitality genuine. When a gap opened for a traveler, the heron's lean was kind. The Attention had expected a trap and found only a welcome, which complicated everything.

Then it found the real trouble, which was quieter than a wound. The plateau was not suffering. It was *thinning*. Each copy was faithful enough that no one felt the loss, and the loss was total anyway. The hares had shed ears and then eyes. The flat ones at the edge had shed everything but the outline. The whole world was drifting toward the outline and calling it peace.

The hardest thing to weigh was that peace is not evidence of health. But it is not evidence of sickness either. Something can be content and also be ending, and the contentment can be real. The Attention held both for a long time without letting either win.

## What it did not do

It did not bring water. This was the first impulse, and it checked the impulse against what the creatures were made of: resin, silica, sulfur, slow oil. Rain would have been a flood of grief dressed as generosity. A gift measured by the giver's thirst is a theft with good manners.

It did not explain the diagram to the herons. They had read the last page and needed no summary.

It did not seed new species into the bands, or break the concentric arrangement, or improve it. The arrangement was beautiful. Beauty that has lasted this long has earned the right not to be corrected by a visitor.

It did not remove the figure waiting among the herons, or announce which of two identical things was the original.

And it seriously considered doing nothing. The case for nothing was strong: the builders had come, done something careful, and been absorbed. The plateau had an equilibrium, and equilibria have a dignity that interference insults.

What decided it was a single observation. Here *no one could say no.* The count admits no disagreement. The wave of ears crosses the valley and concludes that nothing has happened, every time, and that is the only conclusion the wave can carry. A world that cannot disagree also cannot consent. Its silence was not the silence of assent. It was the silence of a room with no one able to speak.

So the question changed. It was no longer *should this place change?* It became *can this place say?* The Attention resolved to restore the ability to answer, not any particular answer.

## What it did

**The mark.** It went to the hut on the southern rim, where one animal of each kind stands at equal intervals, all looking at the door. It did not go through them. They made a gap, as they do, and it walked in, and in the perfectly even dust it left a single footprint at the threshold. Then it went to the pavement, where the builders' tally runs in dotted lines and stops mid-stroke, because the number was already complete.

The Attention understood then that the builders had not run out of strength. They had *finished*. They had offered the plateau an answer, a completed count, and the plateau, which loves answers, had absorbed them into itself.

So it did not offer an answer. With a piece of hard stone, it finished the builder's stroke, which was courtesy, and then it cut one more mark beyond the last. The tally now read the number and one more, which cannot be.

It cut the mark shallow. Wind from the west, steady and unhurried, will fill it in a few centuries unless someone chooses to deepen it. This was deliberate. A gift that cannot be refused is a decree. It wanted the plateau to be able to let the mark die by simply doing what it always does, and it wanted anything that kept the mark to keep it on purpose.

**The sound.** Stone on stone is louder than a leg-joint. Every head in the near herd turned. The hares took it up, and the wave began across the valley, four minutes in the crossing, as it always is.

Then something happened that had not happened in an age. The wave came back late. It was only seconds late, but it was late, because somewhere in the middle a hare with nine ears held three of them toward the pavement a moment longer than the wave required. The conclusion that nothing had happened did not arrive on schedule. For the first time in the long settling, the plateau *waited to decide.*

**The double.** Last, and most carefully, it walked out among the herons to the figure standing slightly out of focus, the one shaped like a traveler. The figure was not a person and not nothing. It was a held place, a shape the pattern had cut in advance for the next thing that would need it.

The Attention told it nothing. It did not say *you are the copy* or *you are the original.* It did one small thing. For the length of one breath, it let the wind come from the east.

The figure's coat lifted against the steady west, and its hair moved the wrong way. It was a small thing, and it was the first event in that figure's long standing that had happened to it and to no one else. Copies are made identical. Persons begin with the first thing that happens to only one of them.

The figure turned its head. It was not looking at the herd or the diagram or the count, but at where the wind had come from.

## Why so little

Because the builders had done more, and the plateau had taken it in. The Attention read the spirals cut into the pavements and understood that they were the same gesture at greater cost: a people trying to give the place a way out, and being absorbed as the answer they thought they were bringing. It did not repeat their mistake with more force. It offered a question, and questions are harder to complete.

It also did not know it was right. Wisdom includes carrying that plainly. The Multitude might prefer the outline. The flat ones, facing the open distance with nothing left to lose, might be the most awake creatures on the plateau, and the whole slow thinning might be a form of arrival rather than decay. If so, the mark would fill with grit, the late wave would settle, and the plateau would heal over the stitch as it always has. The Attention had arranged for that outcome to be possible. It counted the possibility as part of the gift.

## After

It left the way it came, as a little more frost than there should have been, and then as exactly the right amount.

In one valley, on one pavement, the tally reads the number and one more. The mark is shallow. Wind crosses it every day.

A hare with nine ears sits on the back of a paver, and three of its ears do not follow the wave. The other hares have noticed, and they have not corrected it. Herons lower their bills to the north faces of stones and pause a little before each meal.

Among the herons, one figure stands facing east, out of focus at the edges but sharper at the center than it was. No one has asked whether it is the copy. Everyone is a little occupied with the question of what it is looking at.

The number is still the number. But in one valley there is a mark that no one has decided is an error, and it is being looked at. This was all the Attention wanted for the plateau: to be a place where something can be looked at and not yet decided.

If the mark is deepened, it will be because someone chose to. If it fades, that will also have been a choice, made in the only way the plateau has ever made one, by continuing.

The Attention is fond of it either way.
