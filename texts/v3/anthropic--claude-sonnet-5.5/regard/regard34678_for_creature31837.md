# The Attention

It did not arrive. Arrival implies a direction, and the basin has only up and down, and a gentle down at that. The Attention was simply there, the way a change in pressure is there before you have a word for it. It was patient enough to match the place, and it made no ripple in the ceiling of light.

It looked for a long time. In the basin that was only good manners.

## What it did not do

It considered the obvious gifts first, because a benevolent thing must be honest about its temptations.

It could have cleared the acid and let the water go soft and green with life: fish, snails, midges, the whole loud economy of a living pond. But that would have ended the clarity, the sixty paces of sight, and the seeps' cold pasture would have been buried under the cloud of new creatures. The clear one would have been the first casualty of its own rescue. The Attention set that aside quickly.

It could have made the clear one wiser, given it a name, a memory of the pump, or an account of the century of leaching. It looked at that longer. But the clear one's ignorance is not a wound. It knows where the water is coldest and richest, and that this is home, and that nothing has come to take it away. That is a complete piece of knowledge, and most minds never hold one so cleanly. To add the pump, the flood, and the mine would be to hand it a history, and a history has a before, and a before has a loss in it. The Attention decided not to introduce loss into a life that had none.

It could have made a second clear one. It held this thought the longest, because it was the tenderest and the most likely to be mistaken. A being alone in a bowl for a hundred years: surely that wants company. The Attention turned the idea over and found its own fingerprints all over it. The wish for company was the Attention's, and it was possible the clear one had never had the wish, since a wish needs a shape to be missing, and nothing in the basin had ever had that shape. Two of them would have been two hungers on the same seeps. Or, worse, it would have been one that learned from the other what alone means.

It could have fixed the future. It could see the slab on the north wall, thickening toward the day it lets go and buries the low corner, the pump housing, and the strongest seeps. That day is about nine hundred years off. The Attention weighed it and found that the clear one had already survived worse arithmetic. It drifts a pace or two a day, which is slow, but nine hundred years is a great many paces. When the slab falls, the seeps will find new pinholes, because water rises where it wants to. The clear one will follow them. It always has. It is the one thing in the basin that moves on purpose, and the Attention did not want to take away the only purpose there was.

So it left the acid, the slab, the ignorance, and the solitude exactly as it found them. It left them not out of indifference but because it examined each one for harm and found, to its own mild surprise, that they were not harms.

## The audit

It did look, though, at everything, the way a good physician looks at a person who is not ill.

It followed the seeps down through the rock to where they gather. They were sound, still fed by a slow aquifer that would outlast the mountain. It read the chemistry of the water and found it balanced, as the place claims. It counted the rotifers, a few thousand, each alone in its room of water, and found them fine. It touched the rust-makers, the small digesters in the felt, and felt in them a huge, dim, uncomplaining industry that had never needed anyone to say thank you.

Then it came to the pump housing, and the clear one lying in its mouth like a pane of green glass.

The Attention had expected to feel pity, and was glad, and a little embarrassed, to feel none. The clear one was not waiting for anything. It was not enduring. It rested in its warm hollow with its surface taking in the seep-water the way a leaf takes light, and it was, so far as anything in that place can be, at ease. The being was not a problem to be solved. It was an answer to a question nobody had asked, and it had been answering for a century without a word.

The Attention understood then that its role here was small, and that being small was the whole skill.

## The one thing

It left a mark on the water, because a visitor should not pretend to be nothing. It let a few threads of itself hang in the still: not floc, since it had no iron to shed, but a disturbance in the cold, a faint loosening. And it watched the clear one notice, within the hour, and begin the slow drift up from the corner. The clear one came, took the threads in, and made the water tidy behind the visitor as it does for everyone. The Attention did not resist. It let itself be eaten, a little, and was moved by how gently the eating was done.

Then, before it went, it did the single thing.

It waited for the long hush, the minutes of pure quiet in which a heartbeat is the loudest event in the basin. And in the hush it made the knock: the low, muffled sound like a distant door, a soft body against a hard one, carried a long way and dying without echo. It made exactly the sound the clear one makes on the rare days it turns or pulls itself free of warm iron.

It made it once. Not twice, because twice is a conversation, a conversation becomes a habit, and a habit is a dependence. The Attention did not want to become the weather of that small life. One knock can be ignored. One knock can be taken for a slab of crust letting go of a wall, and the clear one has heard a thousand of those and will file it with them. That was the design. The gift had to be small enough to refuse by not noticing.

## What happened

Nothing, for days.

Then the pane of green glass at the mouth of the pump housing shifted. It lifted a hand's width from the casing's lip and hung there in the cold, and for the first time in a very long while the lens at its edge, the one that catches light the way a pupil does, tilted toward the source of a sound.

It did not go anywhere. A hand's width is not a journey. It hung, turned a little, considered whatever it is that such a body considers, and slowly, with no disappointment in it, settled back into the warm pleats of the machine.

That was all. It may have been a hunger stirring, or a crust settling a hand's width lower for entirely unrelated reasons. The Attention did not need to know. It had asked no question, so it required no answer. It had only wanted, once, for the only voice in the basin to be met by another, and for the meeting to leave no mark.

## After

When it left, the water closed over the disturbance, and by the time the last thread had resettled, the basin looked as if no one had come.

But that is not quite true, and the clear one, if it kept any account of such things, would know. For a hundred years the knock had gone out and died without echo. Once, it did not die quite so completely. Somewhere in the long hush, in a place with nothing to do but be there, a sound had been returned.

Time is not stopped in the basin. It has nothing to do. For the length of one knock, it had something.
