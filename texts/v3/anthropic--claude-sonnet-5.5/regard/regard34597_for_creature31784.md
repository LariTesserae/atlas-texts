# The Attention

It does not descend. Nothing in the Brimful has a sky to descend from, only the low steam ceiling and its sourceless amber. The power is simply present, the way a fourth note is present in a chord once it has been sounded, and for a long while nothing about the place changes to admit it.

It is wise, so it does the first wise thing: it does not act.

## The Measuring

It enters the pocket of quiet as any visitor does. The gold frogs at the pool edges turn their heads. The silence spreads in a ring and closes ahead. The power lets itself be measured, and it makes itself measurable. It takes a size. It takes a temperature near blood-warm. It removes from itself everything that would not fit a frog's tape: authority, and the faint pressure that any great thing puts on small ones.

The frogs take its dimensions, and the chord, which has never stopped for anything, does not stop. The ring of quiet is small and moves with it. The power is judged, like the leaf, and found to be a thing that can be turned slowly and held at level with everything else. It is allowed.

Then it does a stranger thing. It sits down on a mineral rim, careful of the raw edge, beside a pool with a crocodilian in it, and stays.

## What It Declines

It stays for what would be a season anywhere else. Here it is one afternoon, taken many times over.

In that time it sees every temptation a kind power could have, and it puts each aside.

It could send wind. The air is a closed mouth, and a breeze would seem a mercy. But nothing here is suffocating. The heat is the shape of the place, and a wind would be an erasure that looked like a gift.

It could send rain from a real sky, to wear the rims, soften the edges and make silt. But the rims are held sharp by every tenant at once, and to break that from outside would be to break a promise no one remembers making. It would also cut the hands of the small feathered things, who run the rims and are the only ones who look you in the eye.

It could put a stop to the dying, or to the tidying. It does not. The arrivers are the honest ones, and it sits with them a while as they take a drowned moth. It understands that this is the most truthful thing the place does.

It could name the arrangement and hand it a chief. It would be cruel to give a held breath a face.

So it does none of these. Benevolence is not a wish to improve. It is a wish that the one who lives here be well, and it has first to learn whether the one who lives here is unwell. It is not certain. The place hums, and a hum is not a complaint.

## The Question

What the power notices, sitting in the small ring of its own silence, is a cost.

*Nothing in it is remembered.* The chord has gone on longer than any frog has lived, and no frog has heard it. Each is inside it. Each hears its neighbors and corrects toward them, and none has ever stood outside the sound to learn that it is a sound. The dragonflies exchange their one letter, *as it was*, and no dragonfly has ever asked *as it was compared to what.* The stillness is labor, and the laborers do not know they are laboring. They are too well made for weariness. They have been made so that they cannot ask.

That is not suffering. But it is a kind of absence, and the power, which has watched a great many places and knows what an unasked question is worth, finds that this is the thing it can give without taking anything away.

## The Gift

On the highest terrace, where the water comes up warm through the floor, the power stands, and it does the one thing.

It takes up the chord.

It does not sing it louder or truer. It sings it exactly, note for note in the broad unresolving structure the frogs have kept, from the pulsing trill to the deep bass of the crocodilians that you feel in the sternum. Then, very gently, it holds the sound as one holds a bowl, so that the frogs are not needed to keep it.

For one breath, the province may stop.

The gold frogs at the edges are the first to notice, because they are the most sensitive part of the instrument. Their throats, mid-pulse, find they are not required. Nothing calls for the correction. The ones beside them find the same, and the stillness travels down the terraces faster than a report, hundreds of pools in a wave, until the whole basin is silent for the first time since it was made.

And the chord goes on. It is held by something not itself. The frogs sit rim to rim with their throats slack and hear it, all of it, from outside: the great sustained shape of what they have been doing. It is not a condition. It is a made thing, and it is beautiful, and it is theirs.

Nobody cries out or flees. A crocodilian opens one eye. A dragonfly on its fixed route stops in the air, not to check papers, and hangs there with nothing to report.

The silence lasts exactly one breath. This too is a mercy. A longer one would be a demand.

## What It Leaves

Then the power lets go of the note.

The frogs take it back, all at once, as a swimmer takes a breath. The chord resumes, unbroken to the ear of any visitor. But the power has been listening for what it hoped for, and it hears it, small as a fingernail: on the third terrace, one jade frog comes in a quarter-tone sharp.

The frogs on either side begin to correct. They lean, as they have always leaned, to dissolve the drift.

And then they do not. Only for a moment, hardly a moment, they let it stand. One sharp note sits inside the chord like a leaf against a spill. Then it is taken back, gently, as it always would have been. But it stood, and having stood once, it is a thing that can be remembered, and remembering is where weather begins.

Farther out, a dragonfly on its route meets another, and they hover face to face. What passes between them is not quite the old letter. It is one word different, as light on water is different: *as it is.*

The power does not wait to see what comes of this. To watch would be to lean, and it has come to be the one thing here that does not lean. It rises from the rim, leaving no mark on the raw stone, and is gone from the place the way a fourth note is gone when a hand lifts from the keys, its shape still in the air a little while.

## The Leaf

On the lowest terrace a leaf lies pressed to the lip of a pool, turning slowly, as leaves do here.

The crocodilian in the basin has one eye open. The nearest frogs are quiet. A dragonfly hovers a hand's breadth above, wings blurring.

Nothing happens. The leaf is a leaf.

But the pool is a hair fuller than it was. Or the rim is a hair lower. Or the leaf is lighter. No one could say, and no one is measuring, because the measurers have for one breath heard themselves. The leaf turns once more against the spill and its edge slips, slowly, the width of a thread, over.

Whether it goes on, or is drawn back, or waits another thousand afternoons, is not the power's to say. It has been kind. It has asked a question of a country that could not ask it, and left the answer where it belongs, with the ones who live there, in the warm and the hum, holding the level of everything else.
