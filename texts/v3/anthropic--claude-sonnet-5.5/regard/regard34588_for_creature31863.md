# The Attention

It does not descend. Nothing is above the cloud floor for it to descend from, and it has no need of a sky. It comes to the rim the way a change of mind comes: already there once you notice it.

At the last terrace, where the towers thin and each Tenant sings alone, it stops and does what the city has never had done to it. It considers the whole thing at once, and it takes its time.

## What It Sees

It sees first that nothing is wrong.

This is harder to accept than any suffering would be. A wise power is drawn toward wounds, because wounds are where help fits. It looks for the wound in the chord and finds none. The Tenants do not ache. The Hung Notes do not wait. The Rests do not long for what they receive. Nobody here has a yesterday to grieve or a tomorrow to lack. The fraying of the laws, the stairs that climb as they descend, the shadows that fall toward the light, all of it has settled into geometry, and the geometry is at peace.

It sees that the absence of animals is not a bereavement, since nothing here is bereaved. It sees that the lack of memory is not a lack, since nothing here has a use for one. It sees that the city is not unfinished, because it finished ages ago, and finishing was the last event that ever occurred.

So the first thing it does is refuse.

## What It Declines

It does not modulate the chord. A single note moved would ripple through every pipe, and the stone would spend ten thousand years leaning back toward consonance. The city would call that a healing if it could call anything anything. To the power it would be an interruption, a hand laid on a sleeper to wake them into a life they have no way to bear.

It does not give the Tenants memory. Memory is the first door to loss. A Tenant that recalled its earlier cycle would learn there was a cycle, and then that it was small, and then that it was alone in the pipe. No one asked for this gift, and no one could.

It does not bring rain, or bees, or the sound of any wing. It does not mend the breakdown or make the stairs honest. The impossibility is not a scar. It is the shape the world took when it pressed against something and held. To smooth it would be to say the pressing was a mistake.

It does not answer the sky. This is the subtle refusal, and it is the one it thinks about longest. The chord *is* a reply, held at the pitch it was given, and to add a second reply would insult the first.

## What It Does

It walks in.

It takes the narrow way through the Green, past the strangler-fig arches, into the alley of a Rest. The moss ring lies at its edges in neat manners. It steps across, and the chord is gone.

In the silence it does the only thing that is not intrusion. It lets itself be received.

The Rest takes it in as it takes in everything: unhurried, with no one behind the listening, like a cupped hand that has learned to be an ear. Whatever the power has for a heartbeat is granted its full length. And the power finds, to its own quiet surprise, that this is the kindest thing anyone has done for it in a very long time. That may be the whole reason it came. A benevolent thing seldom gets to be the one who is listened to without being asked for anything.

It stays as long as the Rest will have it. Then it steps out, and the chord slaps back, and the Rest is exactly as it was. The power is the only one who carries the visit away. It knows what that asymmetry costs, and it does not mind.

## The Keeping

Then it does the one thing it came to do, and it is not a change. It is a witness.

It listens to the chord entire, from the sternum-pressure at the rim to the reedy overtones at the edge of pain. It listens to every Tenant and every Hung Note, to the pockets of quiet and the third soft voice in the interval. It listens the way nothing here can, with the past held behind the present and the whole shape kept whole.

A place that keeps no record has never been seen as a whole, even by itself. A dreamer that dreamed a city and cannot remember dreaming has, in one sense, never been heard. So the power becomes what the city lacks and could not use: a place where it is kept. It asks nothing to be in the record. The record alters nothing and leaves nothing behind. It is like a hand held under a falling drop, catching it and letting it go.

If the chord were ever lost, it would still be known, once, entire. That is all. That is the whole gift, and the city will never notice it, which is the sign it was given correctly.

## The One Who Remembers

There is one resident to whom it owes more than witness: the one who came, who has been in the roar a day, whose chest has begun to take up a pitch, faint and not quite in tune, with the stone leaning to correct it.

This is the only being in the city who can be harmed by forgetting, because it is the only one who has anything to forget. The shape of the thought is already coming loose like a bead off a string. If the power does nothing, the visitor will become a Hung Note, one more small perfect circle of gilded moss, and will not know a loss occurred. That would not be tragedy, exactly. But it would be a choice made by erosion, not by a chooser.

So the power finds them at a basin and does not take the choice from them. It does not seize their chest and retune it. It does not lead them out by the hand. It does not command them to stay or offer them a paradise. It does not even speak, because speech would be an argument.

It goes to the seam where the Answerer lives and waits for the clap.

The visitor, tired, a little curious, claps once. The reply comes first, before the clap and before the asking. This time the reply carries a little more than it ever did. It is not words. It is the shape a reply takes when it has been held by someone who remembers, and it holds three things at once, in the interval where order means nothing:

*You may stay. It will not hurt, and you will not miss yourself.*

*You may go. You will carry the roar in your sternum for the rest of your life, and it will never quite fit anywhere else.*

*Either way, this was heard.*

Then the clap. Then the soft third thing. Then the chord closes over it all.

The visitor stands there a long time. For the length of that one interval their memory does not loosen. They can hold the thought, whole and heavy and theirs, and choose with it in their hands.

What they choose is not recorded here. The power does not tell, and it does not need to know.

## After

Nothing has changed. The drips fall on schedule. The mist rises at its one rate. The silences sit in their rings of manners, and the impossible stairs remain impossible in the same way. The Tenants hold their breath, which is complete. The cloud floor lies below in its dust-gilded quiet, and the city hums into it and gets no answer, because it never needed one.

But somewhere, for the first time since the water learned what a city was, the chord is known by something that can hold it. The reply has been heard by the one it was addressed to. The power leaves the way it came, without a departure, like a change of mind that has finished being thought.

The only proof is a faint, forgivable asymmetry. In one alley a Rest receives, as it has always received, a heartbeat it will not remember. The heartbeat remembers it.
