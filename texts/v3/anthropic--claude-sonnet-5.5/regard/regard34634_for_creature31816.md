# What Was Done on the Lantern Shelf

## The attention

It arrived as a slowing. The power that noticed the Lantern Shelf did not descend through the ice or open the water. It attended, the way a person in a loud room turns toward one voice, and the plateau came into focus around that voice: the ribbon-forests leaning, the felt holding its patches of light, and the long phrase crossing the terraces toward a ring of stones.

Its first thought was that here was something whole.

It looked for suffering, since that is what a power like this is drawn to. It found none. The Turning tore things, but tearing was how the plateau mixed itself and was no wound. The bladders died by the thousand, and they were made for that. Nothing was hungry. Nothing was afraid. The plants lived without being eaten, and they had used the long leisure to become slow, layered, and strange, more than most worlds manage in the same span.

So the power did what wise things do when they find no wound. It made itself small and stayed a long time.

## What it did not do

It considered the obvious gifts and set each one down.

It did not thaw the ice. The lid was the plateau's sky and its silence from the wind. The Turning depended on the season the ice made.

It did not bring light. The plants had built a whole language of glow out of darkness, and daylight would have made it noise.

It did not bring fish, or anything that moves. The plateau's peace was the peace of a world nothing eats, and the first grazer would have ended it. The power judged that a world was not improved by being made more like other worlds.

It did not translate. It could have learned the phrases well enough to speak them back as words, but the plateau had no need to be understood by anything. Understanding would have made the phrases a message. They were a song, and a song is not diminished by having no listener.

And it did not call back the builders. This was the hardest, because it was the only sorrow it found there. Somewhere in the long span, the hands that stacked the stones had stopped. The power could have looked, and perhaps found them, in whatever form endings take, and perhaps woken something. But it did not know whether the pause at the ring was longing. A wise thing does not mend a grief it has only guessed at. It might have been gratitude, or simply a habit that had become a shape of the plateau's mind.

## What it learned

So it listened, through the calm months and then through a Turning, which it did not soften.

It felt the cold water go over the edge, the reeds scream, the bladders burst and the plateau light to its horizons. It understood that the forests were not enduring this. They were doing it. Being torn and rooted somewhere new was the plateau's way of staying young. Sparing them would have been a theft.

In the calm after, it followed the phrase. Rising flush, lull, answering flush, long dark, the phrase again with small changes. It followed the light across the terraces to the ring and saw the pause. The moss inside flared once, brighter than anything, and the wave moved on.

The power waited through many repetitions before it understood what it was hearing. The phrase was not complete. After the flare there was a dark, and the dark was not empty. It was shaped like a place left open. Whatever the plateau was faithful to, it had been saying the first half of a exchange for longer than the stones had stood, and leaving room, and leaving room.

## What it did

Once, in a calm season, as the wave reached the ring and the moss flared, the power answered.

It did not use words, light, or any sign of its own. It used the plateau's own speech, the smallest piece of it. In the dark after the flare, from a reed stand tuned to the lowest note, one tone rose. It was not loud, and it was one of the plateau's own notes, placed where the plateau had always left a space. The ribbon-blades nearby leaned toward it and flushed blue-green. The felt held the trace a moment longer than usual.

That was all. The power offered no claim about who was answering, no promise about the builders, and nothing that could be mistaken for the thing the plateau had lost. It was witness only. It said, in the one way it could without lying: *this was heard.*

Then it withdrew, entirely, and did not answer again.

## Afterward

The plateau did not change much. It is not a thing that does.

But in the next calm months the phrase came round, and something in it had shifted. After the flare at the ring there was now a short lull built into the song, a rest exactly the length of one low tone. Nothing filled it. The reeds stayed quiet through it, and the forests held their glow, and then the wave went on.

Perhaps the plateau had taken the answer as an answer and made room for more. Perhaps it had taken it as a new line in the old song, something that simply belonged there now. The power could not say and did not need to. The phrase went on more fully than before, and the pause, which had been a question, could now also be a listening.

The ring will be silted over one day, in some Turning to come. The power did not prevent this. The plants had kept the pause longer than the stones had stood, and could keep it without them. If the stones go under, the flare will still come, over the buried ring, faithful to a place rather than a monument.

What the power kept was small. A tone once given, and the knowledge that a long fidelity had been received by something. It counted this as enough, and perhaps as the most any power can rightly do for a world that is not broken: come near, learn its shape, and add nothing that it would not have grown itself.

Below the ice, the light still runs in slow phrases, and at the ring it still stops and flares. Now, in the dark that follows, it waits a little, and the waiting has the quality of something at ease.
