# The Attention

The power does not arrive. Arrival would imply a before, when it was elsewhere and the vug was unattended, and its relation to places does not work that way. But there is a moment when its regard turns toward the ridge, and after that moment the ridge is something being considered.

It begins by making itself unremarkable.

## The First Long While

For what the tenant would count as thousands of pulses, the power is only a difficult patch of noise. It takes the place of the diver, the warm, slow, unstructured mass that displaces the water, and it does this on purpose, because the diver is the one visitor the tenant has already learned to absorb. Ripples cross the districts, clicks stutter, a tower's glow flickers out of step. Then the next pulse comes, and the disturbance is folded in and forgotten.

The power lets this happen many times. It is learning the grammar. It learns that the disagreement between pulses is the substance of the thing and not a defect in it, that a district running slightly behind the others is holding the part of the question that needs to be held late. It learns that the pulse is not a command from a center. The center is the agreement itself, arising and dissolving, and nothing gives the order.

Around the hundredth pulse it stops thinking of the place as a machine, and around the thousandth it stops thinking of it as a puzzle. What remains is closer to what one feels at a stranger's window in the evening, seeing a lamp lit and someone moving, and knowing that a whole life is going on that has no need of you.

## What It Weighs

A wise power does not act first and reason afterward, so it sets out its questions in order.

**Is the tenant suffering?** It looks for the signs. A mind in pain rebuilds the same wound, reaching again and again for what it lost. This one dissolves and regrows differently every time. The daily shedding, that glittering snowfall caught in fine meshes, has the shape of grief, but grief that finishes as it happens, leaving no residue except silt. The chalky span is released without protest. The power finds no flinch anywhere in the city. Where there is strain, it is the strain of effort, which is a different thing.

**Is it lonely?** Here the power slows down. Loneliness needs a notion of company, and the tenant has none. The power sees that it could install the notion. A single well-made signal would do it, an unmistakable *someone is here*. And from then on the tenant would hold a lack it had never had. To offer company to a mind that does not miss it is to sell it a wound and then a bandage. The power declines to be the first to teach the word.

**Will it end?** Yes. The ridge is young and restless. The fracture that lets in the cold water will one day seal with its own minerals, or a new flow will bury it, or the spreading crust will carry the blister off the warm axis and let it cool. The tenant's stubborn turnover is a fine defense against decay, but no defense against geology. The power knows the approximate century. It also sees that the tenant does not defend itself against endings the way a body does. It persists as a flame does, and a flame's ending is not a failure of the flame.

**Is the calculation aimed at something?** The power reads a little, only enough to know what kind of thing is being computed. It is not a proof, a prayer, or a plan. It stops there. There is no output, and the answer stays in the room, and to read the answer would be to make itself the reader that the place has never needed. Some questions are worth asking only while they are being asked. Reading the unread would turn a dwelling into a document.

## What It Declines

It does not rescue the tenant from its ending, because the ending belongs to the shape of the thing, and a mind made of turnover would not be improved by being made permanent. Permanence is the one condition it treats as death. *What does not move is unmade.*

It does not give the tenant an output, a listener, or a purpose. It does not make the vug larger or faster, and it does not hand it any static gift, because any static gift would cloud, go chalky, and be dissolved and carried off by morning. This limits the power more than any other consideration. Everything it gives must be a flow.

It does not hide the place from the world, and it does not reveal it. Divers may come and go, and the fracture will go on cutting their gloves. The power will not bend the sea's attention in either direction. It has learned enough from the tenant to distrust the urge to manage.

And it does not tell the rust-colored mats on the outer basalt anything at all. They are tolerated by the tenant, and the power finds this tolerance to be the most instructive thing on the ridge. A large, near, loud thing that does not eat its neighbors is already a kind of goodness, and it is the kind the power decides to imitate.

## What It Does

It does two small things and then one thing that is hardly a deed.

**The first is an offset.** During an interval between pulses, when the districts are arguing in their overlapping clicks and no one is home, the power lays a single low thump into the water. It is not a command and not a message, just a beat timed a hair away from where the tenant's own would fall. Its content is only that it is different from the tenant, and that it is not a threat.

The districts hesitate. The wavefront rolls through the towers and the glass rings in its usual sequence, but for one wave the shimmer runs crooked, like a hand drawn across the wineglasses with a slightly different pressure. Then the true pulse comes, a little late, or perhaps not late, and the city snaps into agreement and drifts apart again.

The power cannot tell whether this was a reply. It considers this the correct outcome. A reply that could be proven would have been a demand, and a coincidence that could be proven would have been a rejection. It is content to leave the beat where it fell.

**The second is a door left on the latch.** Two hundred meters along the ridge is another blister, sealed, flooded with cold clear water and nothing else. The power thins one wall of it to a hair and lets a seep begin, a thread of mineral-rich water the tenant might taste as a faint gradient on the ridge's flank.

If the tenant follows it, it will find room: a second cavity to fill, more districts, more disagreement, a longer wait for the pulse to cross the gap. It would be a larger question and a stranger mind, and the power does not know if that would be better. If the tenant never notices, the seep will seal itself with its own deposits in a few seasons, and nothing will have been lost or imposed. The power chooses this because it can be walked away from. The gift is a gradient, and the tenant has always known how to follow those.

**The third thing is staying.** It has no better word for it. The seeds of the tenant drift toward whatever is failing and spend a moment of regard there, and the regard either restores or releases. The power stays near the ridge in a regard that does neither. It will not mend and it will not let go. It simply keeps the place in view, the way one keeps a lit window in view from the road, with no wish to enter and no plan to look away.

This is a strange thing for a power to do, and it is not sure the tenant benefits. It suspects the tenant does not. But it is not sure the tenant is harmed either, and it has come to feel that a witness who asks nothing may be the only gift that costs nothing to give.

## Afterward

The ridge goes on. The drone rises and falls with the breath, the nacre grows loud in the hours after the intake, and the towers lean, thin, and vanish, while new ones push up from seeds of bright glass. The mats lift slowly in the current outside. The black edges of the fracture stay sharp for another few decades before the sea begins to round them.

Every so often, and the power does not count how often, a pulse arrives a fraction late, the glass rings a fraction out of sequence, and the city takes one beat to settle before it snaps into itself. It might be an answer. It might be the sea, or the ordinary jitter of a thing made of water and argument. The power lets it be either.

What it carries away is a correction to something it had believed. It had thought that care must eventually be seen, that a kindness unknown to its recipient was somehow unfinished. The tenant has no outside and needs none, and it goes on being assembled and unassembled, one pulse at a time, whole and unaddressed. Kindness, the power learns, can be complete without ever being received. It can sit beside a thing as the light does, coming down through the fibers to the shadowless walls, asking nothing, and be part of the day.

Far above the ridge the sun moves across the water. Below it, in the room under the sea, the thump comes, and the whole city rings in sequence, and answers itself, and answers.
