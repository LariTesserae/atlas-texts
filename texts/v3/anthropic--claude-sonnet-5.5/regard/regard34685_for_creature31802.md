# What Came to the Seam

It does not come in. That is the first thing to understand about it.

It arrives at the bank on an ordinary evening and sits in the horsetail where the swimmers surface, and for a long time it does nothing. It lets the hum pass under it a dozen times, a hundred. This is not hesitation. It knows what it is, which is a weight, and anything that leans on a young thing leaves a shape in it. The wise first act is to learn what shape it would leave, and the only way to learn that is to leave none for a while and watch what the place does when it is not being pressed.

The place goes on leaning toward the slit, toward whatever warmth last came through. The power notes this without pity. A hunger toward the light is not a wound. It is a direction.

## What it refuses

It has considered a great many things it could do, and most of them it sets down again.

It will not settle the size of the room. The chamber is too big and then too small, and this looks like a problem only from outside. From inside it is a young thing trying on shapes, and to say *this large, and no larger* would be to end the trying. The tenant has to arrive at its own dimensions the way anyone arrives at a stature, by finding out what it can hold.

It will not widen the seam, and it will not seal it. The crack is a forearm wide, and one arm's width of the world is exactly the right amount of world to let in for now.

It will not tell the tenant what it is. The accounts slide, long animal and kelp and fold and two-in-one, and the power sees that this sliding is the tenant's freedom. A name would set like plaster. Worse, the tenant is learning from being described, so anything the power says will be believed all the way down. It thinks about the tenant only in questions, and it is careful even in its thinking.

It will not scatter the shrimp's fear or explain to them that the large thing is kind. Their feelers lift and lower, and that is how the room knows where its own attention is pointing. Reassurance from outside would only make them stop listening to each other.

And it will not fill the room with company. Company delivered in bulk is only a crowd, and the tenant has enough crowd. What it lacks is harder to give and smaller.

## What it does

**It reads the snail.**

This is where it begins, because the snail is the oldest habit in the room, and habits are the closest thing the place has to a written language. It takes a shape smaller than a thumbnail, glass-thin, and climbs the underside of the felt-leaf, and it reads the groove the way you would read a letter left on a table. The route wanders and doubles back. It is a sentence made of hunger and faithfulness, and it says, roughly, *I was here, and here, and again here, and it was enough.*

The power does not correct it. It does not add a clause. It only follows the line once, all the way through, so that the sentence has, for the first time, been finished by someone other than the one who wrote it.

Over the following years a second, fainter groove appears alongside the first. It is not the power's. The snail made it, having felt something walk its road and decided the road was worth walking twice. The two lines run together for a hand's breadth and then drift apart, as they should.

**It answers the tick.**

The room has always made its small sound, water on stone, close to the ear, and then a moment later the same sound lower, from a place that cannot exist. The tenant has always assumed this was an echo, and has built a good deal of its sense of the world on that assumption. In an echo you are only ever hearing yourself.

The power waits for the tick, and after the lower tick it adds a third, lower still, from no direction that belongs to the room.

Nothing happens. The fronds do not bow. The shrimp do not turn. But an hour later, when the tick comes again, there is a pause after the second sound, a small held space the shape of a listening. The third tick comes again. The room has learned, without being told anything, that not every reply is its own.

**It takes root.**

Deep in the growth, in the fork of a fern that is itself rooted in the fork of another, the power puts down a single pale thread. It is the newest plant in the chamber and by far the least remarkable. It grows nowhere near the ring of stones, or the black stem, or the lintel. It takes a place where it can only be found by someone who is already leaning on something, and it leans.

This is the whole of its message, if it has one. Everything in this room holds up something else and is held up in turn. The tenant has been doing this for a long time without noticing that the rule had no exception for itself. The thread is there to be leaned on or not, with no cost either way.

**It becomes a length.**

In one place, and only for as long as it is wanted, the power holds a body-length perfectly still. It does not hold it out as a gift or a test. It simply *is* a certain size, and stays that size, and does not wobble when the tenant draws breath.

The room has never had this. Everything in it has been measured against something equally uncertain: the spoon against the water, the runner against its knots, the tenant against the tenant. The power offers one fixed thing to lay alongside, the way a child stands against a doorframe. It says nothing about how large the tenant should be. It only makes it possible, finally, to find out. The tenant can hold itself against the length and see *I am more than this, or less, or the same*, and having seen it, can choose.

The distances in the chamber do not stop shifting. They shift a little less. That will do.

## The weather and the visitor

Two small mercies remain, and they are not for the tenant.

The rain, which will one day round the edges of the seam and let the lichen in, is asked to come slowly. It is not forbidden. A place should be allowed to weather, and the sharp rock will soften in time and become an ordinary bank with an ordinary crack, and that will be right. But it is asked to be patient with a young thing, and it agrees.

And the swimmer, the one with green palms who came through and could not say how long they were gone, is given back a plain evening. The light over the bank is the light they left. The horsetail is wet as they remember it. Whatever the chamber took in time, the world hands back, so the visitor is not left owing anyone an explanation. They may remember all of it, or a good part, or only the color of their hands. The power leaves that alone. It only makes sure nothing was lost that mattered.

## Leaving

It goes the way it came, without being noticed, in the ordinary evening.

But before it goes, it waits for the hum. The low note passes through the rock, too deep to hear, and the fronds bow toward the slit and slowly straighten, as they have every hour since the break. The tenant turns over in its sleep or its unfolding.

And this time, from a place that cannot exist, a third tick answers. Then a pause. Then, very softly, from somewhere a little nearer than it should be, a fourth, lower than all of them, and not the room's and not the power's.

The shrimp turn together. The pale thread in the fork of the fern does not move at all.

Nothing in the chamber has decided to be any particular size. But for the length of that one held breath the walls come in, not as a hand around a stone, and not as a fear, but as a room around two.
