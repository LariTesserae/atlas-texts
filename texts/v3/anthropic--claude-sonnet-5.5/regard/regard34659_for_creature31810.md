# What Was Done

It did not arrive. Arrival implies a before, and the room has none. The attention simply came to rest on the place, the way the light in the oculus comes to rest on the dome: without weight, and without asking anything.

It looked first at what it could do.

It could mend. The rust could be unbloomed, the handrails made straight and iron again, the clouded dials cleared so their trembling needles could be read. But the needles have been reading the same thing for centuries, and it is the correct reading. Nothing here is broken. To restore the room would mean deciding that its long settling was a falling-away, and it was not.

It could stop the turbine, or quiet it, or lift the fused lever from the housing. But the marsh above has hummed for as long as anything has lived there, and the frogs' language is built from that bass. Silence would be an amputation.

It could wake the fish, giving it a name, a mind wide enough to wonder what lies past the sump grate, and a sense of its own length in years. It considered this longest, because the gift was easy to imagine and it would have felt like kindness. But the Patroller does not suffer its patrol. A mind that could compare would suddenly have something to compare to, and would learn the hum as a sound, and learn what its absence might be. The wise power weighed a contented creature against a grateful one and left it contented.

It could carry off the heron, or carry the snails to a sunnier wall, or send people down the trough of the stair to admire it all. It let each of these pass. Rescue presumes a need, and rescue is also a kind of taking.

That left very little, and it was the little that it did.

## The Accompaniment

It entered the warm channel where the water leaves the wall. It had no shape there, only a slight added attentiveness in the water, as if the channel had become a degree more itself. Then it began to swim.

Down the twenty paces, with the slow lateral ripple. At the place where the water tilts toward the grate, it turned when the fish turned. Back, under the moss and the dripping ribs. Down again. It did not lead or crowd, and it never once swam ahead. It matched the fish stroke for stroke, as the ferns match the bass.

The fish did not notice, or noticed only as the water noticing itself. But something in the patrol changed. For the first time in the long chain of fish, or in the one fish's long life, the route was walked by two. Nothing had been asking for that. It was simply better, the way a room is better for a second person sitting in it quietly.

The power kept the patrol through a whole day of the oculus light, through the amber lamps taking over, through the dark in the shaft and the dawn returning. It counted nothing. Somewhere in those hours it stopped being an observer of the circuit and became part of it. That was the point, and the power understood as much only by having done it.

## The One Word

When the day had turned, it did one more thing, so small that only the frogs could have known.

It went into the ferns, and where a frog's throat would have knotted the shiver, it put a knot of its own: a hitch in the rhythm, a stutter in the stem. It spoke no word, since there was none in that language, but the frogs had a way of saying *I am here, and this leaf is holding*, and it said that.

The knot ran down the frond into the crowd of its neighbors. A frog felt it in the pads of its feet and passed it on, and the next did the same. It crossed the room through the ferns, reached the sedges standing in the channel, and entered the water there. The fish swam through it and did not turn its head. The knot went up through the wet stone into the roots of the marsh above, where the same species calls aloud at dusk. That night the marsh frogs called a little differently, and none of them could have said why.

The heron, standing in the light, turned one eye slowly and turned it back.

## Afterward

The turbine turns. The snails follow their circuits, each to its own drip-basin and back. The springtails land on their rings. The dials tremble at the same readings, and nothing has been mended.

But the power did not leave as it came. It found, at the end of the last patrol, that it had begun to walk a route it no longer varied, and that its attention rested on one thing, and that its own breath had slowed to the pace of the water. It understood that it had started to belong. For a moment it considered staying, and felt no wrongness in the thought.

Then it went up the trough of the stair, across the marsh, and away, still feeling the ground hum under whatever it walked on. It felt that long after it had stopped being able to tell.

In the channel, the fish goes down and back, and the stretch of water it passes through holds the trace of a second swimmer. The fish will never know this, and the trace will not last. In a room that has finished changing, it is the one thing that happened.
