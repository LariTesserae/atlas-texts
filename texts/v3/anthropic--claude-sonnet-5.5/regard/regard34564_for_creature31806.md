# What Came Down the Slot

It did not arrive as light. Light would have been noise, and the hollow has plenty of that. It came the way a change in pressure comes, and the first sign was that the beads on the canopy hung a little stiller than usual, as if the room were listening. The frost at the rim held its ruled line. The line-tenders did not look up, having nothing to look up with.

## What it set down at the door

The power had a great many things it could do. It stood at the rim and set them down one at a time, like a person emptying their pockets before entering a sleeping house.

It could finish the stone. Every soft rib and half-drawn pillar could have been completed in an instant, clear and hard and perfect. But the stone is the Agreement's memory, and the soft ribs are the things it is still turning over. To finish them would be to answer questions the hollow was still asking. It set that down.

It could give the Agreement words, and a center, and a someone to say them. But a someone can be lonely, and the hollow, as far as anyone can tell, is not. Its way of thinking is agreement, with no one in charge of it. To make it a mind like other minds would be to pay for a gift with the thing being given. It set that down.

It could stop the dying. It could keep every peeper singing for years past its years. But the moss draws the dead down, and a brighter vein of crystal marks the place, and the living sit on it to call. The grief and the architecture are the same act. It set that down.

It could end the peepers' quarrel, or correct the flat note of the algae-mat, which the Agreement itself declines to correct. It looked at that note the longest. Then it understood why the Agreement had left it, and was a little humbled, and set that down too.

What remained in its hands was small.

## Being read

It went down the slot and stood on the moss, and it did not hide.

The filaments tasted it. They found no salt and no iron, and the taste took a long time, days by the count of the blue wave. The beetles nearest it drifted toward a rhythm that was not a heartbeat and rang there, uncertain. The vine pulsed a half-beat behind, as it does with visitors, trying the stranger on.

Then, for the first time anyone could say, the vine pulsed a half-beat ahead. The hollow was leading, and the power let it, following the hum as a guest follows a host through an unfamiliar house.

At the end of the reading the Agreement filed it, not as a shelf and not as a passing rib, but as one of the soft warm formations near the floor, among the things it is still turning over. The power took this as the highest courtesy the place could offer. It had been left unfinished on purpose.

## The one thing it could see

The hollow knows forty paces. It knows the frost line, the seep, and the weather in the slot. It does not know the hill above it, or the valley beyond, or that the winters are slowly shortening at the edges. It does not know that a track two valleys over will someday be a road, or that a man with a lamp and a good knife is curious about holes in hillsides.

The power could not stop the seasons and did not try. It stood between the hollow and the sudden.

A drift of snow lay across the slot a week longer than it should have. A leaning boulder above the rim, which would have slid in a thaw, went on leaning. And when the man with the lamp and the knife came to the edge and looked down into the breath-smoke, he felt a reluctance he could not account for, a wish to go home and tell no one. He put the knife away, and he did not come back.

None of this was noticed inside. It was time, the only thing the power gave that the hollow could use without knowing it had been given.

## A question, not a gift

There was one thing more, and it was less a gift than a question.

On certain nights, the power let the wind across the slot at a particular angle, and a note came down. It was low and long and clean, the first sound the hollow had ever heard that no animal in it had made.

The beetles faltered. The hum dipped. The moths held their clatter. Then the room went on, carefully, with the note passing through it like a stranger through a market, and the Agreement listened the way it listens to everything, with a thousand filaments touching a thousand others.

The power did not tune the note to anything and did not explain it. It only asked, and then waited through a full turning of the seasons.

The answer came in stone. At the foot of the slot, where the gray wedge of light falls, a new rib rose from the wall: thin, clear, and hollow as a reed. When the wind crossed its open end, it sounded a note a hair sharp of the algae's flat one. The Agreement had not resolved the interval. It had made a second one and set it across the room, so that the old flat flute-note and the new sharp one now lean toward each other and never arrive. The chorus, which had always leaned forward alone, now leans forward in conversation.

The power, who could have written the answer for it, had only asked.

## The gap in the line

On the coldest night that winter, a line-tender died at the rim. The ruled edge of the frost wavered, as it does, for as long as it takes another mite to fill a gap.

Something small, gray-white, and bristled walked into the place. It exhaled a mote of warmth against the cold, exactly the ordinary size, and the line steadied. It began to march, passing the others without pause, in one direction and then the other.

No one counted. No one has seen one of them sleep.

## After

The hollow keeps working. The ribs advance a fingernail a month. The blue wave circles every quarter hour, and the moths punctuate it, and the rain crosses the room and stops. A visitor who climbs out through the slot may feel the hum against their back like a large, warm, half-built animal, and may feel, on the edge of the frost, that the cold at the rim is held by someone a little more carefully than the rest of it, though there is nothing to point to.

What a benevolent and wise power could do for a thing that is content to be unfinished is protect its time to be unfinished. It can ask a question softly enough that the answer is entirely the hollow's own, and it can be one small warm body in a ring of small warm bodies, doing a job that needs doing.
