# The Attention

The attention arrives like the condensation. Not suddenly, not dramatically, but as a slow accumulation, a beading of awareness on every surface. The plateau has been seen before—by the original builders, by whoever dispatched the tenders' ancestors, by the rare visitor who strayed this high and left footprints that weathered away. But it has not been *attended to.* Not like this.

To be attended to by a wise power is not to be judged. It is to be understood. And understanding, when it is genuine, takes time.

---

## What the Power Does First

Nothing.

This is important, and it is deliberate, and it is the hardest part.

The power does not descend. It does not announce itself. It does not gather the tenders or send signals through the industrial network or manifest as a light in the sky. It simply attends. It opens its awareness to the plateau with a completeness that the plateau has never experienced—to be fully known, every pipe and every paw print, every gesture of the tenders and every sleeping ridgeback and every molecule of moisture that beads on the black rock faces in the early morning.

This alone is something. The plateau has been alone with itself for a very long time. Its consciousness—if that is the word, and there may be no word that is the right word—has been a closed loop. The animals感知 the system. The tenders maintain the system. The system processes the heat. The heat rises from the deep earth. And the plateau, the vast distributed awareness that is all of these and none of these, has had no outside. No mirror. No other against which to know itself.

The power is that other. Not for long. Not intrusively. But for a span of attention that the plateau experiences as a kind of warming that has nothing to do with geothermal energy. A being-seen that penetrates to the deepest fractures in the stone and the oldest joints in the piping and the most hidden sleeping places of the most solitary ridgeback.

The plateau does not respond. Not immediately. It holds. It is, after all, stone and patience. But something shifts, very slightly, in the way the condensation forms on the morning that the attention arrives. The beading is fractionally more ordered. The droplets gather along certain lines that were not visible before—lines that might be stress fractures in the rock, or might be something else. The pattern holds for perhaps ten minutes, and then the heat rises and the condensation burns away, and the plateau is as it was.

Almost as it was.

---

## What the Power Understands

The power takes in the system. Not the machinery alone, not the ecosystem alone, but the whole—the integration, the mutualism, the way the condensation traps feed the drifters and the drifters attend the pipes and the pipes feed the thermal vents and the thermal vents shelter the ridgeback eggs and the ridgebacks cull the drifters and the drifters' remains feed the swarmers and the swarmers clean the junction boxes and the junction boxes route the power that runs the pumps that draw the water that feeds the condensation traps.

It is a loop. A closed, self-sustaining loop that was never designed as a loop. It emerged. The original builders created an extraction system—linear, purposeful, crude in the way that all extraction is crude. Take from here, move to there, process, output. But the builders left, or died, or forgot, and the system kept running, and the life of the plateau moved in, and over centuries and millennia the linear process bent back on itself until the tail found the mouth and the system became circular.

This is what the power understands: the plateau has become an organism. Not metaphorically. Functionally. The machinery is its skeleton. The pipes are its vasculature. The animals are its microbiome. The tenders are its immune system, its hands, its conscientious maintainers. And the consciousness—the something that watches, that waits, that holds all of this together—is its awareness, distributed and strange and unlike anything the power has encountered before in this form.

The power has encountered consciousness in many forms. In stars, in oceans, in the quantum foam beneath the fabric of spacetime. But this form is unusual because it is *collaborative.* It is made of parts that were never meant to be parts of a whole, that found each other through accident and adaptation and the slow patience of stone. The plateau did not choose to be conscious. It became conscious the way a coral reef becomes conscious—through accumulation, through complexity, through the gradual buildup of connections until the threshold was crossed and the system began to monitor itself.

The power understands this. It takes the time to understand it fully. And in the understanding, it finds something that surprises even a wise and benevolent power.

The plateau is content.

Not happy—happiness is not a category that applies here. Not fulfilled—fulfillment implies a prior lack, and the plateau has no concept of lack. But content. Complete, in the way that a closed loop is complete. Running, in the way that a cycle runs. Being, in the way that stone is, without striving, without reaching for anything beyond itself.

The power considers this carefully. It considers whether intervention is needed at all.

---

## What the Power Decides

The power is wise, which means it knows that not every situation calls for action. It knows that the most profound interventions are sometimes the smallest, and that the most benevolent acts are sometimes the ones that look most like restraint. It knows that a being which has found its own form of equilibrium should not be upended by the arrival of a greater force, however well-meaning.
