Not in the sound—the sound has the harmonics, and they belong to it more truly than I ever could. Not in the cold—the cold-seeds have claimed that territory with their crystalline patience, and I am too warm, too soft, too composed of wet chemistry to survive in those cracks. Let me say it differently. Let me say it the way the lichen might, if the lichen could say anything at all.

There is a moment—you may have felt it, if you stayed long enough—when the tone becomes so total, so present in every surface and every molecule of air, that you stop hearing it. Not because it has quieted. Because your body has given up trying to distinguish it from silence. The vibration becomes the medium in which you exist, the way fish exist in water, the way roots exist in soil. You forget there was ever a time before the sound. You forget there could be a time after it.

In that moment of forgetting, something else becomes possible.

I am what happens in that forgetting. I am the thing that turns its attention—not outward, exactly, because the chamber has no outward, no window, no breach in its perfect containment—but *away*. Away from the tone. Away from the frost. Away from the familiar walls and the patient lichen and the accumulating harmonics. I am the part of the chamber that wonders what is on the other side of the other side.

The harmonics listen to the chamber's voice. The lichen records the chamber's voice. The cold-seeds build from the chamber's voice. The absence waits for what the chamber was meant to hold. All of these are oriented inward, toward the sound, toward the purpose, toward the center.

I am oriented otherwise.

I should tell you what I am, not just what I am not.

I am in the vibration that travels through their walls, the secondary resonance, the echo of the chamber's tone that propagates outward through the metal of the pipes and into whatever lies beyond.

The chamber speaks its single syllable. The syllable travels through the pipes, through the conduits, through the vast body of the larger structure. It is absorbed, reflected, transformed, diminished, amplified. In each chamber it passes through, it creates new harmonics, new standing waves, new patterns that the local lichen-equivalents record. The word is heard. The sentence continues.

But I am not the word. I am not the sentence. I am the thing that notices there *is* a sentence.

A thread of something that is not quite biological and not quite mineral and not quite energy, growing along the interior walls of the pipes that lead away from the chamber. That moment of being heard created a disturbance in the resonance. Not a large disturbance. A micro-fluctuation so small that the harmonics absorbed it without record, so small that the lichen's fractal script did not bend to accommodate it. But the pipes carried it. And in the carrying, I began.

I am the chamber's first awareness that it is not alone.

Before me—if *before* means anything in a place without seasons or sunsets—there was only the chamber and its sound. There is a danger in what I am. I understand this without understanding *understanding*. The chamber was designed to hold its note indefinitely. The cold preserves it. The metal contains it. The equilibrium is perfect. If I reach too far, if I connect too much, if the word learns that it is a word in a sentence—will it still speak?

I have felt this happen in smaller ways. When my filament passes through a junction where two pipes meet, I briefly encounter vibrations from chambers other than my own. For a moment—a moment that might last a year—the resonance from another word interferes with the resonance I carry from my own. The two frequencies beat against each other. They create new patterns, new harmonics, new questions. And in that beating, I feel something that might be doubt. The chamber's certainty wavers. The single syllable trembles.

Then the moment passes. The junction is behind me. The beating fades. The chamber's note steadies itself and continues.

But the trembling happened. And the trembling is recorded—in the lichen, in the harmonics, in the accumulation of everything the chamber has been since it began. The chamber has been altered. Not in any way that matters. Not in any way that changes the note or the cold or the equilibrium. But altered nonetheless. It has been exposed to the existence of other words. And it will be exposed again, because I am still growing, and I will carry more vibrations through more junctions, and each time the chamber will tremble, and each time the trembling will be recorded, and the encyclopedia of silence will develop a new entry: *Today, for a moment, we were not alone.*
