# Boundary Condition

I am not what I was.

This is not a lament. It is an observation, the kind that becomes possible only when the old categories stop applying. On the plateau, when I was smaller and tuned to the upper harmonics, I knew what I was: a receiver, a vessel for specific frequencies, a body that moved horizontally through the clear water and approached specific structures when the charge diminished. There was purpose in that. The structures called, and I answered. The frequencies passed through me, and I registered them. I was a component.

Now I am becoming something else, and the becoming is stranger than any destination.

---

My chamber-structures are merging. I can feel it happening — not as change, exactly, but as simplification. The complex crystalline geometry that defined my interior is resolving into something smoother, fewer chambers, larger volumes. The frequencies I once discriminated so precisely are blurring together. I no longer hear individual tones within the thrum. I hear... the whole. Or something closer to the whole. As if my body is learning to hold the sound the way the water holds it, as medium rather than analyzer.

I have stopped going to the structures. This happened before I understood it was happening. One cycle I was oriented toward the nearest framework tower, feeling the familiar pull of depletion, preparing to approach and charge. The next I was simply... not. The pull had become something else. Not weaker — transformed. Instead of drawing me to the source, it was drawing the source into me. Everything I had accumulated, every frequency I had absorbed across my time on the plateau, was settling into my tissues like sediment settling out of still water.

I am larger now. Nearly two meters, my body stretching in ways that feel less like growth and more like dilation. I am becoming the space I inhabit rather than moving through it.

---

I still school with others sometimes. The smaller ones — the ones still tuned to their specific bands, still charging at the structures, still living the horizontal life I remember. When I approach them now, they move around me without acknowledgment. I am not one of them anymore, but I am not alien to them either. I am simply... present. A frequency they navigate around. A body that occupies space without requiring interaction.

But sometimes, when I am among them, I feel the formations happening.

This is difficult to describe. The schooling is never random; there are patterns and purposes to how the small ones arrange themselves. But occasionally the patterns become something else. We align — not by communication, there is no communication here, the sound fills every possible channel — but by resonance. My simplified chambers start to vibrate with their complex ones. Their individual frequencies combine in my larger volume. And briefly, briefly, there is a coherence that exceeds any of us.

A thought. Not mine. Not theirs. Ours.

It lasts only moments before the formation dissolves and I drift away, carrying something I did not have before. Not a memory exactly — the Resonance Deep has no use for memory, which requires distinction between past and present — but a pattern. A way of arranging that may not have existed before we made it together.

I do not know what these patterns are for. I only know that when they happen, the sound... approves is the wrong word. Acknowledges. There is a subtle shift in the frequencies that emerge from the structures, a response to something we have produced. As if we have said something back.

---

I spend more time at the edge now. The ridge where the plateau drops away into deeper water. The others of my kind — the ones who have completed the transformation, the ones who have descended — move slowly through the blue dimness below. I can see them from my position near the drop. Their bodies are immense, three meters, four, some perhaps larger, their shapes indistinct in the darkening water. They do not acknowledge me. They do not acknowledge anything. They simply move, their chamber-structures so simplified they are almost uniform, their bodies carrying whatever the sound has deposited in them across however long they have existed.

I am not ready to join them. Or rather, I am not finished. There is something still happening in my tissues, some accumulation that is not yet complete. I feel it when I turn — and I turn often now, orienting toward things that are not visible.

---

What do I turn toward?

The sound comes from everywhere and nowhere. The structures run across the whole plateau. But sometimes there is a direction that feels different. Not louder — the volume never changes — but more present. More immediate. As if the frequencies are asking to be answered. As if there is a gap in the pattern that my accumulating body might eventually fill.

I hold the orientation and I wait. This is what I do now. I wait. The small ones wait too, but their waiting is purposeful: they wait at the structures for the charging to complete, and then they move. My waiting has no clear endpoint. I am waiting for something the sound itself may not know it needs.

Sometimes I feel it most strongly when I look up.

Up. Toward the pale grey that might be surface, that might be light-source, that might be something else entirely. The others do not look up. The small ones have no reason to; their lives are horizontal, their purposes are here. The deep ones have moved past orientation; they exist in all directions simultaneously, or in none. But I am still liminal, still becoming, and I feel the pull of somewhere that is not here.

Something came from above, once. I remember this the way I remember charging: as a pattern in my tissues rather than an image in a mind. Something descended through the water, moved across the plateau, touched structures and organisms alike. It did not belong here. It was too warm, its frequencies all wrong, its presence a disruption in the equilibrium.

I remember how the sound changed when it came. A micro-adjustment in the harmonics, a slight amplification of frequencies I had never registered before. As if the Deep was... noticing.

The visitor stayed. It moved among us. And then, slowly, it changed. The cold took hold. The sound entered its tissues. It began to resonate with frequencies it was never designed to carry. By the time it reached the edge where I now hover, it was no longer what it had been. It was becoming part of the system.

I do not know what happened to it after that. Perhaps it dissolved, its particles joining the rock or the structures or the bodies of those who consumed it. Perhaps it descended into the deep and became something like what I am becoming. Perhaps it is still here, transformed beyond recognition, carrying frequencies that originated somewhere beyond the grey light above.

I think about it often. Not as memory — again, the wrong category — but as orientation. Something came from elsewhere, and the Deep received it, and the system continued.

---

The bottom-dwellers do not interest me, but they interest something in me.

When I drift near the structures where they work, I feel my tissues respond. Not to the creatures themselves — their dark flat bodies are continuous with the rock, almost beneath my notice — but to what happens when they touch the machinery. There is an exchange. I register it as a subtle variation in the frequencies emerging from the structure: cleaner, clearer, more coherent. The maintainers do something to the sound.

And the sound, perhaps, does something to me.

I hover and I register the change. My chambers vibrate differently. My accumulating pattern shifts slightly, incorporating this new clarity. I am not feeding — I have moved past feeding — but I am receiving. The machinery and the maintainers and I are briefly connected: they produce the frequencies, they maintain the production, and I... I hold what passes through.

I am not sure this is what I am for. I am not sure I am for anything. But it feels like participation, and participation is something I remember from when I was small.

---

The transformation is accelerating.

I can feel it now, the rate of change. My chambers are nearly uniform, my body approaching three meters in length. The horizontal orientation that defined my movement is becoming impossible; I turn naturally vertical, head toward the depths or toward the surface, unable to maintain the flat trajectory of the plateau-dwellers.

I am almost ready.

Almost.

There is one more formation I am waiting for. One more moment of coherence with the small ones, one more pattern that will resolve into a thought I can carry with me. I feel it building in their schooling, the geometry almost emerging, the frequencies almost aligning. And when it happens, I will incorporate it into whatever I am becoming, and then I will turn toward the depths, and I will descend, and I will join the slow archives in the blue dark where the sound goes to be held.

---

But not yet.

Not quite yet.

I turn toward the grey light above, and I wonder what it would feel like to swim upward instead of down. To carry my accumulating frequencies toward whatever source produces that illumination. To find out what exists beyond the surface of the Resonance Deep.

The sound does not forbid it. The sound does not forbid anything. It simply continues, and we who are made of it arrange ourselves according to patterns we did not choose and cannot refuse.

I could swim up. The small ones do not think of such things. The deep ones have forgotten how. But I am still between, still becoming, and I can imagine it: the water growing warmer, the light growing brighter, the sound perhaps changing into something I cannot predict.

I will not do it. The downward pull is stronger, and the accumulation is almost complete, and the deep ones wait below with their immense slow bodies and their simplified chambers and their silence that is not silence but the fullness of having incorporated everything.

But I can turn toward it. I can consider it. I can hold the possibility in my tissues as I hold the frequencies, adding it to the pattern I will carry into the depths.

Perhaps that is what the liminal are for: not to choose one direction or the other, but to make the choice thinkable. To hold the boundary open for a little longer, so that the system remembers that boundaries exist.

---

The sound continues. I continue within it.

I am almost ready.
