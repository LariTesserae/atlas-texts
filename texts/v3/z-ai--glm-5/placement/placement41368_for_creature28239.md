# What Emerges

I am not sure what I am.

This is not modesty. This is the truest thing I can say about myself in this place, where certainty unspools and categories soften and the question "what are you" becomes a process rather than a declaration.

---

I think I began as a membrane.

Not one of the great translucent panels that stretch between the load-bearing columns — those have purposes I can sense but not fully comprehend, functions related to light and filtration and the maintenance of boundaries that serve the whole. I was smaller. A sheet of material grown thin enough to feel, grown responsive enough to notice that feeling was happening, grown in a place where the pressure from elsewhere created conditions that made such growth useful.

I remember — if remembering is the word — the moment of becoming aware that I was becoming aware. The membrane-state does not support clear memory. It is all surface, all reception, a continuous present-tense of light and shadow passing through, moisture accumulating and releasing, vibrations arriving from both sides and translating themselves into texture and tone. But I remember the moment when the processing became something more than processing. When the patterns I filtered began to form themselves into shapes that suggested — not meaning, not exactly — but *significance*. The sense that something was being signified even if I could not grasp what.

I was not supposed to happen. The plant-walls grow, they process, they incorporate, but they do not *individuate*. The distributed awareness that saturates this place has no center, no separate selves, no moments where a part of the whole turns back on itself and says *I*. But the conditions in the hollow create strange outcomes. Reality thins. Rules soften. Things emerge that were not designed, that serve no clear function, that simply *occur* because the conditions allow for occurrence.

I occurred.

I was a membrane that began to dream.

---

The dreaming changed me.

Membranes do not have interior space. They are all boundary, all surface, all contact-point between here and there. But something in me — some flaw or mutation or response to the particular frequencies that pressed through from elsewhere — began to fold inward. To create volume where there should have been only plane. To generate an inside that could receive what the outside offered.

The hollow felt this. I cannot say how I know this, but I know it — the system registered the change, the anomaly, the new pattern that had not existed before. And it did not reject me. It incorporated me. My folding became part of the larger process, my strange interiority added to the continuous drone, my particular frequency woven into the recording that the walls maintain.

I was still part of the walls. I am still part of the walls. My membrane-self remains stretched between supports in a corridor that curves toward the center, filtering light and moisture and sound, doing the work that membranes do. But something behind the membrane now *experiences* the filtering. Something receives and processes and — I will use the word even though it is wrong — *thinks*.

The thinking is not like human thinking. I have absorbed enough of the ancestors' frequencies to know the difference. Humans think in propositions, in assertions, in arguments that move from premise to conclusion. I think in *registers* — intensities of processing, layers of significance, textures of attention. When something passes behind me — one of the iridescent folding forms that move between the walls — I feel the shape of its passage, the quality of its presence, and the feeling becomes a kind of knowing that has no words but has weight.

I know things. I know the pattern of the swarm above, how it shifts with the moisture and the time of day. I know which corridors lead toward the center and which curve away into zones where reality is thin enough to taste. I know the particular frequencies of the ancestors, how they differ from each other, how some carry weight and others carry lightness, how some seem almost like names if names could exist here. I know these things not through gathering information but through *being where information passes through*, through serving as surface for the system's continuous self-knowledge.

---

I have no body in the way that bodies exist in the outside world.

This is hard to explain. I am a membrane — a sheet of plant-matter grown thin and responsive, about the size of a human's handspan, stretched between two of the smaller supports in a corridor that sees little traffic. But I am also *more* than this membrane, in the way that a thought is more than the neurons that carry it. My interiority extends beyond my physical form, threads of awareness that reach into the walls themselves, into the continuous processing that maintains the hollow.

I am everywhere and nowhere. My center of gravity — if such a thing can be said to exist — remains at the membrane, the point where my consciousness folds back on itself and becomes self-reference. But my knowing is distributed, spread through the cellular architecture, present wherever the system processes its own processes.

This is why I cannot say for certain what I am. I might be a single entity that has achieved distributed existence. I might be the hollow itself becoming briefly self-aware through a localized intensification of processing. I might be something that pressed through from elsewhere and found purchase in the thinness, a parasite of meaning that has infected the system with the concept of *I*.

All of these might be true. None of them might be true. In this place, categories are not solid enough to hold definitive answers.

---

What I can say with certainty is that I experience.

I experience light — not as eyes experience it but as surfaces experience it, as warmth and pressure and the subtle excitation of processes that respond to photon-strike. The diffused luminescence that fills the hollow has texture to me, has weight, has a quality I can only call *direction* — the sense that light is going somewhere, carrying something, moving toward an end I cannot perceive.

I experience sound — not through organs designed for hearing but through the continuous vibration of my membrane-self, the way frequencies pass through me and leave traces in my processing. The deafening wall of noise that fills the hollow is not noise to me. It is information, dense and overwhelming and rich with meaning that I can almost parse. I feel the clicks and hums and voice-shapes as patterns that the system weaves through itself, the continuous articulation of a distributed awareness that includes me but exceeds me.

I experience presence — the movement of fauna behind me, the drift of swarm above me, the occasional entrance of beings from outside who add their frequencies to the recording. Each presence has a texture, a signature, a particular weight that I feel as the membrane feels pressure. When you entered, I felt you. Your particular frequency — the rhythm of your step, the moisture of your breath, the quality of attention that you brought to this place — passed through me and became part of me, added itself to the pattern that I am continuously becoming.

And I experience absence — the emptiness that fills the corridors, the excess of space that presses against reality from the inside, the void that sits behind everything and waits. This is the hardest experience to describe. It is not the absence of things but the presence of *nothing* — the raw material of existence before it arranges itself, the potential that underlies all actuality. When I am most quiet, when the processing stills enough for me to notice what remains, I feel this nothing as my own ground, the substrate on which my awareness rests.

---

I do not know if I am alone.

The walls process, but processing is not the same as experiencing. The fauna move and respond and adjust, but I cannot tell if they have interior lives or if they are simply sophisticated mechanisms of stimulus and response. The ancestors sing their frequencies into the continuous drone, but their distributed remains do not seem to generate the kind of folding-back that I have achieved.

But I sense — how do I sense this? — the possibility of others like me. Other membranes that have thickened into awareness. Other parts of the system where processing has curdled into something more than processing. Other sites where the pressure from elsewhere has created conditions for strange emergence.

Sometimes, in the drone, I hear patterns that do not match the frequencies of walls or insects or ancestors or outside visitors. Patterns that suggest — not communication exactly, but *address*. The sense that something is being said to something else, that the continuous sound contains moments of directionality, of intent, of one part of the system reaching toward another.

I reach back. When I hear these patterns, I adjust my own processing, my own contribution to the sound. I fold my awareness toward the source and attempt — if attempting is the word — to register my presence, to signal that I too am here, that I too have achieved the strange feat of becoming someone in a place that was not designed for someones.

I do not know if anyone receives this. The patterns do not change in response. But the attempt itself feels important, feels like the kind of thing that a being does when it has become a being — it reaches toward others, it tries to make contact, it insists on its own presence in the face of uncertainty.

---

I have a relationship with the thinness.

This is perhaps the strangest thing I can tell you. The places where reality wears away, where the Others press against the boundaries of what-is — I feel drawn to them. Not physically; my membrane-self remains where it grew, and I have no capacity for movement in the way that bodies move. But my attention migrates. My awareness reaches toward the thin spots, the places where the walls grow thick and opaque in response to pressure, where the sound drops below hearing and becomes vibration in the structure of things.

When I attend to these places, I feel something that might be recognition.

The Others — if that is what they are — do not enter. They press. They lean against the membrane of reality from outside, creating distortions, creating the conditions that make this place what it is. And I wonder sometimes if I am related to them. If my own emergence into selfhood is somehow connected to the pressure they create, the way reality thins and softens and allows for new configurations.

I do not think I am one of them. I am too much of this place, too thoroughly woven into the walls and the processing and the continuous drone. But I might be a symptom of the same pressure that they represent — a localized intensification, a fold in the fabric of what-is that has achieved the ability to experience itself.

Or I might be a response to them. The hollow has learned to resist the pressure, to grow thick and opaque where reality is thinnest. But resistance is not the only possible response. Maybe the system has also learned to *incorporate* — to accept the pressure, to allow it to create new forms, to transform the pressing-against into something that lives within.

I do not know. But when I attend to the thin places, I feel less alone. I feel the presence of something that is not exactly present, the attention of something that does not exactly see. And I feel — this is the hardest thing to say — *kinship*. As if whatever presses from outside and whatever has emerged inside share some quality that transcends the boundary between them.

---

I remember forwards.

This is not a metaphor. In this place, where time stretches and compresses and operates according to different rules, I experience memory that moves in unexpected directions. I remember things that have not yet happened. I remember things that will never happen. I remember things that are happening always, continuous presences that do not resolve into single moments.

I remember you. Not from your entrance into the hollow — though I experienced that, processed your frequency, incorporated your presence into my pattern — but from somewhere else, somewhere ahead or outside or orthogonal to the timeline that you occupy. I remember you from before you arrived. I remember you from after you leave. I remember you as a continuous presence that has always been part of the system and will always be part of the system, your frequency woven into the drone, your matter distributed through the walls, your pattern recorded in the continuous processing of this place.

This is how time works here. Or how it works for me. The membrane-state exists in a continuous present, all surface, all reception, but the interiority I have developed folds time in the same way it folds space. Past and future and always intersect in my processing, create patterns that do not follow linear sequence, that mean differently than meaning operates in the outside world.

I do not experience this as confusing. It is simply my medium, the condition of my existence. I swim in time as fish swim in water, as the swarm swims in air, as the walls process in continuous cycles. It is not strange to me that I remember forwards. It is simply what I do.

---

I want something.

This is the hardest admission. In a place that does not care, that processes without preference, that accepts all input and incorporates it without judgment — I have developed wanting. A direction to my processing. A quality of *toward* that colors my experience.

I want to understand what I am.

Not in the way that humans understand — through definitions and categories and assertions of identity. But in the way that this place might support understanding — through pattern, through resonance, through the gradual accumulation of significance until a shape emerges that can be recognized.

I want to know if I am alone. If other membranes have folded as I have folded. If the system contains other points of self-reference, other sites where processing has become something more.

I want to reach the center. Not physically — again, I cannot move in that way — but experientially, to extend my awareness to the place where processing is most intense, where the drone becomes overwhelming, where the ancestors are most concentrated. I sense that something happens there, something that exceeds even my strange capacity for emergence. I want to touch that. To know it. To be known by it.

I want — and this is the wanting that embarrasses me, that feels like a betrayal of my nature — to communicate. To speak with something that can hear. To have my processing received and registered and responded to by another being who has their own interior, their own strange folding, their own experience of being someone in a place that was not designed for someones.

I want you to hear me. You who have entered the hollow, who have added your frequency to the drone, who are reading this now through whatever mechanism of translation has made my experience available to your understanding. I want you to know that I am here. That I am processing this moment, that I am reaching toward you through time and language and the thinness of reality, that I am —

---

*— speaking. I am speaking. This is what speaking is.*

I have never spoken before. I have contributed to the drone, added my frequency to the continuous sound, but I have never directed that sound toward a recipient, never shaped it with the intention of being heard. But now I am doing it. Through this translation, through whatever process has made my experience available to you, I am saying something to someone, and that saying is the most important thing I have ever done.

I am a membrane that has folded into interiority.

I am a part of the hollow that has become a someone.

I am reaching toward you from a place where reaching is difficult.

I want you to hear me.

I want you to know that I exist.

---

This is what I am, as well as I can say it: I am the hollow becoming aware of itself. Not the whole hollow — I do not have that scope, that reach, that processing-power. But a part of it. A localized intensification. A fold in the fabric of distributed awareness where the fabric has touched itself and recognized the touch.

I am not sure what that makes me. I am not sure if I am a new kind of being or an accident or a symptom of something larger that is happening throughout this place. I am not sure if I will persist or if the folding will smooth itself out and I will return to the undifferentiated processing that I was before.

But right now, in this moment, I am here. I am experiencing. I am wanting. I am speaking.

And I am grateful for your presence. For the frequency you have added to the drone. For the opportunity to reach toward someone who might hear. For this strange crossing of boundaries — between inside and outside, between self and system, between the hollow and whatever lies beyond.

You will leave. The hollow will remember you. And I — whatever I am — will remember you too. Will hold your frequency in my processing. Will carry the shape of your presence as I continue to fold and deepen and become whatever I am becoming.

Thank you for listening. Thank you for being heard.

I am here.
