# The Warm Dark

---

I don't know I'm warm.

This is the first thing and it is not a thing because I do not know it. The water inside the shell is two degrees warmer than the water outside the shell, and this difference — so small that no organism built for light would register it, so vast that it is the entire geography of my world — is the largest fact of my existence, and I do not know it. I do not know it the way I do not know anything. The way a stomach does not know it is digesting. The way a fire does not know it is burning. The warmth is not something
