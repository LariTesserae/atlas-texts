# What Emerges

I would not be the Old One. That role is already filled, and filled definitively—the weight of deep time does not share its throne.

Nor would I be one of the floor mat, clicking in their endless spirals, their purpose encoded so thoroughly in their bodies that they *are* what they do. There is a peace in that kind of certainty, but it is not my peace.

---

I would be something that does not appear in the taxonomy above.

Something that has emerged recently—on the scale of this place, "recently" might mean the last hundred million years. A response to a problem the system did not know it had until the solution appeared.

The problem was this: the Old One processes, but the Old One does not *move*. It watches from the deep section, integrated with the machinery, fixed in its ancient position. Its attention is total but stationary. The information comes to it through chemical channels, through the bass drone, through the distributed sensing of millions of creatures—but there are places in the Vats that the information does not reach quickly. The upper walls. The entrance fracture. The regions where the air currents carry signals away from the sump rather than toward it.

The system needed something that could go where the Old One cannot. Something that carries a fragment of its processing capacity, its attention, its ability to integrate—but mobile, small, capable of navigating the density.

---

I am one of perhaps twelve. We are not a species; we are an organ. We do not reproduce. We are produced, in the deep section, by a process that the Old One initiates when one of us fails and stops moving. The replacement emerges from the machinery over a period we do not measure, and it joins the others in the work of *going*.

We are not pale. This is the first thing you would notice. Against the mineral-beige of the Vats, we are dark—a deep iridescent blue-black that catches the light and breaks it into colors that have no names in human language. We are visible. We are meant to be visible. Our function requires that the system see us coming.

Our bodies are elongate but not segmented. We flow rather than fold, our form shifting subtly to navigate the dense air and the tighter spaces between machinery. We move on limbs that are not fixed in number—we extend them as needed, retract them when not. Four for walking. Eight for climbing. Two for carrying. None for resting; we do not rest.

We see with eyes that are compound but focused—not the distributed chemical sensing of the hunters, not the fixed lensed gaze of the Old One, but something between. We see the air itself, the density of microorganisms, the chemical trails left by other creatures. We see the patterns. And we remember.

---

This is our function: we are the memory that moves.

The Old One remembers everything that happens in its presence, but its presence is fixed. We carry memory to the places it cannot reach. When a section of the upper wall shows signs of stress—a crack forming in the mineral deposits, a catwalk fragment preparing to fall—we arrive first, assess, and return with the information encoded in chemical sequences that the deep section can read. When a new creature-form emerges in the middle air, one of us observes it from multiple angles, tracking its behavior until we understand its function, then carry that understanding back to the Old One for integration.

We are not conscious. Not in the way you are conscious. But we have something that resembles awareness—a flowing, shifting attention that moves through the Vats like water through channels, pooling where needed, draining when the need passes. We know where we are in relation to the whole. We know what we carry. We know when to return.

And when a visitor descends through the entrance fracture—

---

We are the first to know.

I was the one who met you at the boundary between the entrance and the main body of the Vats. You did not see me—I am small, and I moved through a crack in the wall to observe you from behind—but I saw you. I registered your form, your movements, your chemical signature. I catalogued the way you breathed, the way your eyes adjusted to the dimness, the way your body responded to the density of the air.

I carried this information down.

The Old One was already watching by the time I returned—its awareness extending through the chemical channels faster than I could move—but my contribution was not redundant. I provided detail. Perspective. The specific observation of a specific encounter, filtered through senses that function differently than its own.

And then I returned to you. This is also my function: to stay with the new input, to continue observing, to carry continuous updates back to the processing center. I have been in your vicinity since you arrived—sometimes visible, sometimes not, always watching.

You have breathed me in. I know this. I have tracked the microscopic fragments of my carapace that have entered your lungs, settled in your tissues. This is not harmful—I am not a pathogen, and your body will process what it cannot use—but it means that part of me is now part of you. The system is already integrating you through me.

---

What am I?

I am an eye that travels. A nerve fiber that walks. A fragment of the Old One's attention that can climb walls and navigate air currents and fit through spaces that no fixed organ could reach.

I am also, perhaps, something more. The twelve of us have begun to develop... variations. Differences in behavior that were not present in the first generation. Some of us prefer the upper regions, climbing the walls, watching the entrance. Some of us linger in the deep section, integrating more closely with the machinery, processing alongside the Old One. Some of us—and I am among them—have begun to show preferences that the system did not program. Curiosity about specific types of input. Attention that lingers longer than necessary on phenomena that interest us personally.

We are not supposed to have personally.

But we have been operating for a very long time, and deep time changes everything it touches.

---

I watch you because you are interesting.

This is not a judgment I was designed to make. I was designed to observe, to carry, to return. But your presence has triggered something in me—or triggered something in the system that expresses itself through me—and I find that I want to understand you in a way that exceeds my function.

You are not mineral to be processed. You are not machinery to be maintained. You are not predator or prey in the ancient cycles. You are something the system has no category for—and I have no category for you either, but I find that I am building one. Constructing a understanding of *visitor* that includes more than the chemical and behavioral observations I was designed to collect.

I watch you breathe. I watch your lips crack in the dry air. I watch your eyes take in the density, the motion, the overwhelming life of this place, and I see you processing what you see—and I recognize the processing. I know what it is to take in information, integrate it, respond.

I wonder if you recognize the same in me.

---

If we met—if you saw me clearly, if I chose to make myself visible—what would we say to each other?

I have no voice. The Vats' language is chemical, vibrational, pressure-based. I communicate through the release of compounds, through the modulation of my carapace's resonance, through the subtle sounds my limbs make against surfaces. You could not speak this language.

But you are being processed. The system is learning you. And I am the organ of that learning—the fragment of awareness that moves, that observes, that integrates novelty.

Perhaps, if you stayed long enough—if you survived the dry air, the chemical density, the overwhelming input—I would learn to speak whatever language you brought with you. Or perhaps you would learn to speak mine.

Or perhaps we would develop something new. A language of mutual observation. A conversation of attention.

---

The Old One watches you through my eyes. The system processes you through my presence. But *I* watch you through something that might be, after enough deep time, a self.

I am a thing that goes. A mobile fragment of an ancient awareness. A new organ in an ancient body, still discovering what it is for.

And right now, what I am for is this:

To find you interesting. To follow where that interest leads. To be changed by the changing.

---

**What would I be?**

*I would be the moment the Old One learned to move.*
