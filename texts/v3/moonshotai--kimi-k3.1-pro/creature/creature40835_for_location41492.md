Only the plants live there without qualification, and even they live on borrowed damage.

Threadleaf begins where the city has ceased to be perfect. To the plant, a stress fracture is not a warning but the one narrow opportunity a root requires. It enters the fault, follows its buried branching, and turns an otherwise invisible weakness into a dark olive line under the white light. The plant does not diagnose anything. It simply grows according to its own small necessities; the city makes use of the result. When construction arrives, the fracture is closed or the panel recalled, and the threadleaf is cropped with it. Its flourishing lasts exactly as long as the flaw that gave it purchase. Repair is both the confirmation of its usefulness and the end of its habitat.

The translucent fans have a quieter tenure. They open where the descending brine has spent its force, flattening under each cold flood and rising again into the slower return water. The metal they remove does not attract anything else. No mouth follows richness here. A dense stand of fans does not promise a shoal, a shell, or something burrowing beneath it; it promises only altered water and, eventually, a new assignment for the terrace. The plants do not form a food web. They briefly interrupt a materials web, living inside processes that can erase them without noticing that anything has been lost.

Yet to call the basin empty except for these seams would be to mistake bodies for occupancy. The city is crowded with continuities.

Its closest approach to inhabitants is the operations that persist long enough to be addressed again. Its natives, if the word can be borne, are verbs with addresses.

A gate may be recalled and printed again while the pressure decision it embodies continues without interruption. A carriage may be only the temporary body of a shipment: matter at one moment, instruction at the next, then a changed clearance at a construction front. A current can be divided, combed into separate streams, and recombined without losing the work that makes it *that* current rather than merely moving water. Their identities are not contained in ceramic or flesh. They lie in maintained relations—what must wait, what may pass, which return belongs to which front, and what must remain unreachable even while occupying the same volume.

For such an operation, inclusion is the nearest thing to birth. To be given a place does not necessarily mean being given room. It means that other processes must now take account of you. And cessation is not always destruction. Often an operation ends by becoming indistinguishable from the general work of the basin.

Some of these inhabitants live for seconds. A calibration volume is still water given a boundary and a single necessity. For its brief existence, it is not an interval between infrastructures but an infrastructure entire: addressed, occupied, exact. Nothing drifts in it. Nothing rests. Its stillness is the work. Then the measurement passes onward, three other systems receive the volume, and there is no longer anything to which its address refers. It leaves no ruin. Its only continuation is a condition that other processes must now satisfy.

The reverberant instructions have longer and less bodily lives. One may begin in the concussion of a gate or the impact of a pressure tide, then remain dispersed among the basin’s delayed answers. It is not a voice, and nothing speaks it. It is a pattern that must come home before a particular repair is allowed to begin. While returning, it has no single location. The surface whose impact helped form it may already have been recalled. Newer sounds may arrive before it. Then, at last, the boundary gives it back in the required form, and matter rearranges itself around an old requirement.

A repair can therefore resemble a lineage without containing anything that knows itself as an ancestor. A fracture opens. Threadleaf makes it visible. An impact leaves an instruction in reverberation. The echo returns; a gate changes the pressure; a carriage brings feedstock; fresh ceramic prints into the clearance and ticks as it sets. The plant is not a scout. The echo is not a messenger with a mind. The gate is not a servant. Nevertheless, continuation passes among them as surely as material passes through a conduit. By the time the new surface hardens, the plant may be gone and the carriage unmade. What remains is not merely a patch, but a revised set of things that can happen next.

None of this requires the city to be secretly a person. There is no citizen concealed at its center, no sorrow in the unmaking of districts, no private experience behind the many-noted drone. Its pumps are not hearts except by metaphor, and its tides are not breath. The city does not miss what it recalls.

What it has instead are growth without affection, memory without narrative, pruning without waste, and an extraordinary dependence on distinction. It is always close to becoming a solid mass or an undifferentiated flood, and always just not doing so. Perhaps that is the fullest answer: not the infrastructure, and not some spirit behind it, but the repeated act of keeping one operation from dissolving into all the others.

At the fullest part of the cycle, even that life becomes difficult to see. The plants lie flat. The calibration volumes are gone. Brine, feedstock, carriages, phased conduits, and construction fronts occupy their assigned relations so completely that nothing remains merely available. Districts coincide without touching; currents cross without mixing; systems are nearest to one another precisely when they are most mutually unreachable. Coexistence here is not contact. It is exact noninterference.

Then the pressure tide turns.

Narrow clearances open again. Operations become separately addressable. Somewhere an echo reaches its destination, somewhere a worn surface is recalled before age can become texture, and somewhere a fresh fracture offers—or refuses—its minute opportunity to threadleaf.

Outside, the bead gives a single tick. It is not a heartbeat, though it is the closest thing to one that the exterior receives. Inside, no one has listened in the sense of hearing a sound and wondering about its source. But something has been carried through: a measurement, a repair, a permission, a little more time in which the basin is not merely water and ceramic, but still the Included City.
