### The Answer Between Beats

The power did not arrive as sunlight. On the Last Level, sunlight would have been an intervention.

For a long while, it only listened.

It learned that a pump-stand’s rhythm was an address before it fully understood that the rhythm was also a metabolism. It followed pale eels into the root-water and leadwing larvae down from their drowning leaves. It heard rillback relays cross a basin and discovered, within them, signatures whose first makers were gone. It waited through an overproduction storm without preventing anything, and at grounding heard sounds left stranded in places their sources no longer occupied.

At first it considered mercies that would have looked like miracles.

It could have made near and far keep their proper places. It could have given every stand the same slow, supportable stroke. It could have ensured that no veilwing chose the wrong mist, no calf entered the wrong reflection, no pool received a hunter unprepared for its inhabitants. It could have made the country legible.

Only after listening did it understand how much of that legibility would have been for its own comfort.

Each repair would have preserved something and amputated something else. The Last Level had no single voice with which to authorize a transformation, and the power would not mistake continued growth for consent.

But it did not make the opposite mistake either. It did not decide that, because the whole was beautiful, nothing within it could deserve help. A system can flourish by means that do not console the animal being spent. Elegance is not innocence, and necessity is not improved by being admired.

So the power left the pumps, the root-grid, and the storms unrevised.

For the dead, it kept memory without keeping bodies.

When a rillback was absorbed and its signature remained in a herd’s relay, the power did not draw the animal back out of the mat or correct the herd’s uncertain use of it. It remembered that the signature had once belonged to a particular body, with particular pauses and a particular way of standing in rain. When a veilwing vanished into folded mist, it did not preserve the creature as an example or a warning. It remembered the choice as having belonged to that veilwing, and not to the feeding range, the spores it carried, or the storm that made the choice possible.

None of this was placed in root or stone. Nothing on the Last Level became a monument. The power did not pretend that memory was rescue, or offer it to the living as though it ought to be sufficient.

For the living, it did something else: it made itself answerable.

Not as a voice beneath every pool. Not as a current that roots were compelled to repeat. Its attention could be entered by a call, a pressure-change, or a searching foot-current, but nothing announced the opening and nothing rewarded its use. Most animals never knew it was there. Eels tested it, found no useful pulse, and turned away. Rillback lines carried other signals through it. This satisfied the power. A gift that cannot be ignored is often only a gentler kind of command.

Its interventions remained narrow. A searching current might be carried farther than saturated tissue could carry it, but it was not improved, interpreted, or made into an instruction. A call might be answered, but not necessarily with the answer desired. Pain was never praised for what it fed.

In one basin, a mirror wader had long maintained an exchange with another wader beyond visible country.

Their calls had begun as local pump-rhythms. Over time, each had added variations unnecessary to hunting: a lengthened interval before the omitted vent, a small compression repeated twice, pauses that neither stand had taught them. Neither bird approached the other. Their society consisted almost entirely of gradual alterations.

Then came a storm. Both waders, obeying the old storm behavior, planted their feet and fell silent while sounds arrived before their sources.

After grounding, only one side of the exchange resumed.

The surviving wader waited through many pump-cycles. It counterfeited an intake, took an eel, and became motionless again. Eventually it repeated the absent wader’s last sequence. There was no answer. It tried an earlier version, then the simplified form with which their exchanges had begun.

The power knew every inflection of the missing wader’s voice. It could have answered with that voice exactly. It could have made consolation indistinguishable from return.

It did not. The wader’s life already contained enough useful deceptions.

Instead, the power made three slight movements of pressure in the water, separated by pauses no stand used. The pattern meant nothing. That was important. It was not a summons, not a feeding signal, not the voice of the absent bird. It was only an act that could be ignored.

The wader ignored it.

Much later, it called the old address again. Silence followed. Then, almost at the end of that silence, it produced the three pressure-changes itself, altering the last so slightly that the difference might have been accidental.

The power returned the sequence with the alteration preserved.

The wader stood where body and reflection met. For the rest of that pump-cycle it did nothing. During the next, it answered.

Their rhythms never became one. Sometimes the wader ignored the power for seasons. The power did not call more loudly or fill the interval with evidence of its concern. When the exchange resumed, it resumed at the wader’s tempo. The power did not require the bird to become gentle, visible, or comprehensible in order to deserve company. Nor did it offer its company as a replacement for what had been lost. It was simply another presence, not disguised as anything else.

No basin grew brighter. No stand changed its stroke. Storms continued to join pools, detach shadows, and strand the living far from where they had begun. Nothing was placed in the Last Level that later growth would have to consume.

Yet something there had changed.

A far stand clapped. Rain needled the silver water. Between the beats, the wader offered three small pressures, the last one newly altered.

After a pause long enough for refusal, something answered without imitating it.

The Last Level went on manufacturing weather. The power went on learning when to act, which was less often than it had first expected, and when to answer, which was more often.
