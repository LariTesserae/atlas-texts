### The Witness

The power did nothing for a long time.

This was not indecision. It was the first form its wisdom took.

It could have made the Crown complete. It could have brought the hairline strata forward out of the air, given the gulfs dependable bottoms, and caused every journey to be exactly as long upon returning as it had been going. It could have reduced the roar to the small, local sounds of fractures actually occurring. The heights would have become legible.

They would also have ceased to nourish the gardens as they were. What appeared from outside like an intolerable suspension was the condition of the accords’ abundance. A finished Crown might have been kinder to surveyors, had there been surveyors of an ordinary kind. It would not have been kinder to the beings already there.

The power could have made the huts incorruptible. It did not. An unalterable enclosure would have preserved memory by giving the past authority over every later deliberation. An accord might then survive every age without being free to decide which of its relations deserved to survive with it.

It could have quickened their thoughts. It did not do that either. Their slowness was not a wound. The power refused to mistake its own impatience for their suffering.

So it listened.

While it listened, faint bedding lines beyond a cliff became a ledge capable of bearing dust. A garden on a distant seam released spores, and nearly all of them crossed into gulfs. Living reeds continued their almost imperceptible ascent through the sheaths of earlier reeds. Beside the blank wall, the maker’s errand did not move.

The power learned that no single gift suited every accord. Some would have experienced perfect stability as a refuge; others, as a prison. Some built toward active seams. Some entrusted nothing to stone. There were accords whose whole history consisted of preserving an imperfection in a chord, and the power could not assume that they wished to be relieved of it.

It did not offer them a common salvation.

The maker of the seventeen-step stair presented a narrower problem.

Its difficulty was not simply that the eighteenth step could not yet bear weight. Nor was it that the distance had changed. The maker had three possible responses, and each could preserve something worth preserving. What it could not always secure was the difference between recovering a choice and having that choice quietly revised before it was made.

A hut could alter while appearing to remain the same hut. A pattern could continue by becoming someone slightly different, with no occasion on which the difference could be recognized. Sometimes such revision might be precisely the work. Sometimes it might be loss. The revised pattern would not necessarily be able to distinguish the two.

The power could have settled the matter. It could have solidified the eighteenth step, fixed the interval, restored an earlier hut. Each act would have been comprehensible as help. Each would also have made one arrangement, preferred by the power, mandatory for the maker.

It chose instead to give no answer.

It gave testimony.

No new stone appeared. No mark was cut into the wall. The power simply undertook to remember the configurations the maker had actually recovered, without selecting an original among them and without preserving a copy from which some discarded version could later be imposed upon the present one. The past would be available, but it would have no power of restoration.

Nor would the power volunteer warnings. To tell an accord unasked that it was departing from itself would already privilege the self that had been.

It waited until the maker posed something close to a question in its own manner.

The cord was returned to the hut. The span of the doorway was recovered. The distance to the rear wall was recovered. The length that could be trusted only after that return was recovered also. Then, through the errand’s almost motionless preparations, those relations were carried toward the stair.

Only then did the power answer.

For the duration of one deliberation, the maker could hold its present arrangement beside earlier arrangements. The knowledge did not arrive as memory, for then it would have become part of the pattern it described. It remained other: former relations available for comparison, none of them invested with the authority of a command.

There were no words. Had there been, they would have been only approximately these:

*These arrangements have occurred. These differences have occurred. Nothing here requires you to return.*

Then the answer withdrew from immediacy. It could be sought again. It would not seek the maker.

The accord did not immediately act. For another long age, comparison remained part of its work. The power did not interpret this as ingratitude, failure, or a request for further assistance.

Eventually, the maker altered the hut.

It left the stair and the cord as they were, and accepted a revision in the enclosure through which their relations had been judged. The work was exceedingly slow. One proportion in the corbelling changed; others had to be recovered around it. The pattern that emerged was not unchanged. It could no longer use the hut to demonstrate that nothing essential had happened.

That was why the choice mattered.

The maker had not been restored to an earlier self. Neither had it been protected from becoming a later one. It had been able to recognize revision as revision, and to accept or refuse it without the acceptance being concealed inside the appearance of mere continuity.

Had it dismantled the stair instead, or restored some earlier enclosure, the power would have done nothing. It had not given the gift in order to obtain a preferred result.

No eighteenth step appeared. The roar did not soften. The cold heights continued deciding, over durations no generosity could abbreviate without destroying their character, what would be above, what would be below, and what could finally be said to exist.

That was all.

It was not all the power could have done. It was all it could do there without making the maker’s life into its own work.

Above the upper garden, in a hut that no longer had to pass for unchanged, the accord began again.
