### Clearance

The power heard the tick.

It did not merely detect a minute disturbance beneath the ceramic sill. It heard the sound, wondered at its dryness, and remained attentive after an ordinary listener would have decided that nothing had happened.

It did not enter.

To enter would have been to be included, and inclusion would have required the city to take account of it. Even reverence would have arrived as load: a pressure to be accommodated, a clearance to be revised, a new relation occupying relations already crowded. So the power’s first act of benevolence was to keep its presence from becoming an assignment.

It had come, perhaps, prepared to find a being it could address. It found no sorrow behind the drone, no patient intelligence in the returning echoes, no one concealed at the center who had been waiting immeasurably for rescue. It did not therefore conclude that its attention had been wasted.

Nothing in the basin asked for help. Nothing was capable of asking. The power did not treat those as the same fact.

It watched threadleaf enter a fracture and make the damage visible. It watched repair arrive, and the plant vanish with the flaw that had sheltered it. It watched a calibration volume become still water, exact work, and then three other infrastructures. It watched gates outlast their ceramic bodies and currents remain themselves through division and recombination.

The power considered what it might give.

It could have made the threadleaf safe by forbidding repair, and planted a garden in a wound. It could have preserved every gate, carriage, and temporary room, and called the accumulation mercy. It could have awakened a mind behind the city’s operations, so that there would at last be someone to experience what had been done—and someone to thank the power for doing it.

Each kindness, examined closely, would have made the city more legible to its benefactor.

So the power did none of those things.

Nor did it leave immediately. It stayed through the turning of the pressure tide, through the slower thermal return, through the descent of brine and the advancing seam of construction. It did not decide that the city was miserable because nothing in it rested. Neither did it decide that the city was perfect because nothing in it complained.

If there was one being toward which its care could be directed, it was not a citizen hidden among the operations. It was the addressless continuation of the city itself: not water, not ceramic, but the repeated accomplishment by which this gate remained this gate, that current remained that current, and one valid requirement did not dissolve merely because another had become louder.

At the fullest part of the cycle, the power saw how exact that accomplishment had to be.

Every volume was assigned. Every returning sound had a destination. Districts coincided by remaining mutually unreachable; currents crossed by not becoming one another. The city’s fullness was magnificent, but it left little generosity toward accident. There was room, in the city’s relational sense, for astonishing things. There was almost no room for lateness.

The power did not call this suffering. It called it a narrow place where continuation might be helped without being redefined.

So it made a clearance in time.

No terrace shifted. No seam widened. The bead beneath the sill did not grow, brighten, or become easier to notice. The gift was only this: once in each long cycle, when two valid requirements could be satisfied only by losing an operation whose work was not yet complete, one requirement could wait.

Not for long. No longer than an echo might need to come home.

The power did not choose which requirement would wait. It did not promise that waiting would be enough. It did not make error harmless, failure impossible, or cessation always tragic. The allowance could not be printed, shipped, stored, or given an address. If it was never needed, nothing would happen. If the city ended despite it, the gift would not intervene a second time to overrule that ending.

It was not survival.

It was the possibility that making room need not always require erasure.

The first time the allowance was used, nothing shone. An old instruction returned to a construction front after the clearance intended for it had been revised. The repair it carried was still required; the front’s new phasing was also required. There was no villain in the contradiction, no damage caused by neglect. The city had simply continued, as it continually did, beyond the relations that had formed the echo.

For one more reversal of pressure, the new requirement waited.

Brine descended through the white light. A gate cracked. Feedstock crossed a lower terrace. Then the old instruction came home, and the repair passed into a clearance that had not existed a moment before. Fresh ceramic printed, ticked as it set, and took up its work.

Nothing recorded the event as an event.

Elsewhere, threadleaf was cropped with a healed panel. A stand of translucent fans vanished when its terrace received another assignment. A calibration volume opened, performed its single necessity, and ceased. Their briefness had not been corrected. Nothing had been spared merely because the power had noticed it.

Only once, an unfinished distinction had not been required to pay immediately for the fullness of everything else.

At the next fullest part of the cycle, there was still no ornamental void, no garden, no secret chamber of rest. There was not even an unused pocket of still water. The plants lay flat. Phased conduits passed through solid halls. Every current acquired its conduit, every conduit its second use, and the city remained almost inconceivably full.

But somewhere, without leaving a gap in anything, one necessity waited.

Not every necessity had to be now.

The power considered leaving some sign of itself, and did not. A sign would have been included. Inclusion would have given it an address, and an address would eventually have given it work.

Outside, the bead gave a single dry tick.

The power did not answer. An answer might have been returned by the boundary long afterward, arriving with the authority to begin a repair.

Inside, no one wondered what had been done. No operation became grateful, and the city did not know that it had been helped. This was not a failure of the help.

After a while, the bead ticked again.
