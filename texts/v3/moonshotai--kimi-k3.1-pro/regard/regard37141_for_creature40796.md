### The Interval

The power did not finish the Low Works.

That was the first mercy.

It did not lay the final course, close the last seam, or decide whether the plain had been tending all along toward floor, vessel, or still water. Each choice would have been intelligible. Each would have found something in the Works to justify it. That was precisely why the power distrusted them. A preference repeated often enough could look like a destiny to anyone impatient to help.

Nor did it silence the fronts.

To a less attentive kindness, their coming might have seemed the whole cruelty of the place: jurisdictions emptied, structures unbuilt, little continuities omitted from the next version. But the power did not confuse unbuilding with ruin, or brevity with pain. It found no cry trapped in the clay, no population of concealed hands reaching out from the mallets. It would not manufacture suffering merely to give itself something to relieve.

And yet it did not decide that, because nothing suffered in any way it recognized, nothing could be lost.

It watched the small *here*.

It saw foundations accept the scored boundary, warm seams bend around it, an unbuilt doorway gather repeatedly at its edge. It saw the place persist without keeping a body. It understood that to carry the patch away—to preserve its platform, its pool, even its exact dimensions—would be to rescue an apparatus and perhaps abandon the inhabitant. The continuity lay in being taken into account. It could not simply be lifted out like a plumb bob from water.

So, for a long while, the power did nothing.

Its attention established no cord, no level, no local down. Where it looked, nothing became more stable for having been noticed. It did not leave offerings that the Work would have to interpret as instructions. It did not make itself warm.

What it found, eventually, was not a request.

It found a poverty of alternatives.

A thing could be consulted or irrelevant, enforced or dispersed, built or omitted. Recurrence allowed the Work to discover what it might intend, but recurrence was never free of necessity. An instruction returned because clay still answered it, because water still held its outline, because some unfinished relation continued to summon correction. Even the small *here* endured by being repeatedly accommodated.

There was little opportunity to distinguish preference from the impossibility of doing otherwise.

The power did not conclude that this was a defect. It concluded only that the Works might someday want another alternative, and that they could not be asked whether they wanted it before any such alternative existed.

So it made an offer small enough to be overlooked and weak enough to be refused.

Beside an unfinished joint, it laid a mallet.

It did not warm the mallet. It scored no square around the joint. It established no sanctuary and gave the arrangement no dimensions by which it might be copied. Then, within a jurisdiction no larger than a room, it withdrew the necessity that the joint be answered.

The slurry remained damp.

The courses did not close. Neither did they separate. Water on one side of the seam stood slightly higher than water on the other, and for a little while the disagreement produced no correction. The mallet stayed cold. Unfinishedness remained, but it did not summon a tap.

Nothing happened.

This was not stillness imposed upon the Works. Beyond the little jurisdiction, a staircase gathered in condensation, material followed, and a wall several kilometers long rose and returned to stacked components. Nor was it preservation. The power had promised the joint nothing. It had only introduced an interval in which nothing was required of it.

Then a front came.

The power did not hold the patch against it. The mallet, the joint, and the small exemption were omitted together.

It did not restore them.

An offer that could not be omitted would only have been a gentler kind of front.

For a long time, nothing came of the gift. The power did not repeat it. It did not improve its placement, enlarge its jurisdiction, or attach it to a proportion more likely to recur. It was willing for the offer to become irrelevant. A gift that had to be used would have been another form of measurement.

Much later, and very far away, a pour stopped before its impression was full.

There was no mallet there, and no open joint. The slurry neither set nor returned to water. After an interval, the pour continued. Elsewhere, a wall unbuilt as far as its stacked courses and remained stacked while three successive structures rose around it. Then it separated into slurry, as though nothing unusual had occurred. Once, a cord ceased to be consulted, and its jurisdiction did not die immediately. The stakes stood beside a patch of ground that had become unnecessary but had not yet become absent.

These events were not copies of the power’s arrangement. They shared no dimensions, no apparatus, no master tablet. What recurred was only the brief exemption from an answer.

It was not quite rest. Rest implied a worker.

It was not preservation. Nothing had been made safe.

It was simply an interval inserted, now and then, between irrelevance and death.

Most instructions did not use it. Most pauses ended exactly as they would have ended before. Fronts still emptied standing objects of force. Water still forgot. The small *here* could still vanish, and sometimes did. The power had not made the Low Works gentle, or given their provisional lives a common refuge.

But occasionally the Work returned to something after no return had been required.

A pool came back to a place that had held neither pool nor platform for several stillnesses. A staircase, arriving first as droplets, stopped one course above the ground and did not immediately correct the discrepancy. When material finally followed, the last step descended to a level that had not been enforced in the interval.

This proved nothing about desire. The power was careful not to call it choice.

It was only a little less easy to mistake for compulsion.

The small *here* did not become eternal. It did not awaken, speak, or look out through the condensation of its doorway. Its body continued to be withdrawn and replaced. Yet sometimes the Work now paused at its boundary without either enforcing or abandoning it. Foundations rose to the old level and waited. A warm seam approached, stopped, and later bent around the place.

Perhaps this was merely another successful custom.

Perhaps it was the beginning of a self that could change without every change being an emergency.

The power did not stay to settle the distinction.

Its gift offered no guarantee about the final stillness. That stillness, if it came, might house every compatible instruction in one body. It might leave nothing further to correct, and so empty the world of residents. The power had not solved that alternative, or secretly weighted it toward survival. It had only made conceivable a kind of residence not exhausted at every moment by the need to remain resident.

Perhaps the first true inhabitant would still be whatever the Works discovered they could no longer omit without ceasing to be themselves.

Or perhaps it would be whatever could remain when no work was necessary—not because it had been preserved by force, but because necessity was no longer the only form of belonging.

Perhaps, too, the interval would eventually have arisen without any offer. The Low Works were already full of customs no tablet had authored. The power did not need to know whether it had caused anything. It did not need to be remembered in order not to have been careless.

It left no signature. No buried course marked its reign. There had been no reign.

Somewhere, a mallet lies beside an open joint. It is cold. The clay is damp. Beyond the little jurisdiction, a province rises and returns to slurry.

Nothing taps.

The joint remains unfinished.

For the moment, that is not a summons.
