### What the Power Did

The power did not arrive as light. The dark, folded leaves would have spent centuries answering any gift of photons. It did not arrive as warmth; warmth would have made the grove’s most precise achievement irrelevant. Nor did it speak into the cords. A voice might have returned through the acoustic map as open space where there was only stone, and roots could have grown for a thousand years toward a chamber that existed only as a misunderstanding.

So, at first, it did nothing that water could detect.

It listened until patience ceased to look like consent. The grove’s tones were not necessarily speech. Its coherence was not necessarily a mind. Its persistence was not proof that it wished to be preserved forever. Nor were its animals merely cells with inconvenient hungers. By mass, the hollow was almost one life; by vulnerability, it was a multitude. The power refused to choose a scale and call the choice wisdom.

It considered large mercies.

It could have opened the bedrock, but that would have been like helping a seed by breaking it apart to release the plant. It could have kept every cord alive, until the living boundary no longer marked an achievement but only the reach of its own protection. It could have rescued the grazers from the plants, the larvae from the hunters, the eels from every failing basin. Soon there would have been no hollow, only a collection of lives maintained in the shape of their former dependencies.

Every grand intervention began to resemble replacement.

Yet refraining did not make the power innocent. It did not look into a freezing basin and praise the design. Slowness did not make injury wise. Naturalness did not make an ending just. Nothing became good merely because roots did it, or because ice did it, or because it had been happening for a very long time.

Its first act, therefore, was too small to alter a census.

Where pain could still guide an animal toward water, away from injury, out of a narrowing film, the power changed nothing. Where no action remained—where a body was already enclosed beyond escape, already frozen beyond recovery, already past the last use suffering could have—it did not require awareness to persist through every remaining detail.

A needle hunter still fed. A cup-mouth still became cloudy and slowly cleared. Ice still entered failed cords. But no enclosed larva was kept in helpless agony for the sake of an undisturbed ecology, and no eel had to undergo the whole length of an ending from which nothing could escape. No population increased or diminished. No relationship was repaired or ruined.

This was not an improvement to the hollow. It was simply mercy, and the power did not confuse it with design.

Its second act came later.

A fracture gathered in the glacier, far above the ice lens. It was not one of the ordinary complaints passing through the floor. Its final branch would cross the chamber and close it. Water would continue for a while in the crushed root felt; some animals would outlast the open space. Then the common physical state would fail, and the last liquid films would cloud.

The grove answered before the fracture arrived. Cords altered their pressure. Basins returned shortened tones. Roots began responses that might have preserved tissue around a smaller wound. Nothing in them could lift the ceiling.

The power could have made the ice harmless. Instead, high above the hollow, it changed almost nothing. It allowed an older plane of weakness to accept the stress a little earlier. The fracture still ran. The glacier still settled. Every liquid surface jumped, and the moth roosts closed in a long, delayed ripple.

But the last branch opened in empty ice.

When the groan passed, the hollow remained bowed, cold, and approximately thirty paces long. Nothing grew faster. No animal was safer from hunger. No root had been taught to expect rescue.

The power made no covenant of the act. It did not promise that no later fracture would prevail. A world guaranteed by its attention would soon have no history except that attention. But neither would it call this particular, preventable erasure freedom merely because refusing to act was easier to justify.

The gift was not forever. It was another stretch of time in which the hollow could remain undecided about its final form.

Afterward, the power left no sign. It placed no unfailing droplet among the roots, no light that did not vary with the seasons, no tone requiring an answer. It took no seed against some future loss. A preserved seed might have comforted the power, but comfort for the observer was not the same thing as continuation. It made no copy by which the hollow could be rebuilt after being misunderstood.

Nor did it demand gratitude. Gratitude would have required a story, and in that story the power would gradually have become the center of the place.

At the center, a mite waited where a swelling drop had divided its film. Above it, one moth shifted, and the adjustment traveled slowly down the roost. Beneath the root floor, dozens of eels flexed together and then became indistinguishable from the basin bottom.

Nothing had been made easy. The hollow had not been improved, explained, or assigned a destiny. It had been helped where help did not become replacement, and spared where sparing did not become possession.

The next drop fell. Plant bladders answered at lower pitches. Hours later, from the raw growing end, a tone returned through stone that had only recently acquired space around it.

The power listened.

It did not answer.
