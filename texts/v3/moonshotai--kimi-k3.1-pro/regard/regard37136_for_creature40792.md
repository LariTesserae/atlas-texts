The power did nothing that the city could have noticed.

This was not its first impulse, and it was not passivity.

Benevolence began by asking whether anything was suffering. Wisdom made the question exact. It did not ask whether the heat would be comfortable for a visitor, whether the roar was too loud for a human ear, or whether a life without departure seemed impoverished from outside. It asked whether any persistence in the rift was being endured against a preference; whether any circuit was closed by force; whether, behind the impossibility of address, some address had been prevented.

Had it found such a thing, it would have acted. It would have opened a seam, cooled one current, released one returning stream, even broken the lovely geography beyond repair. It would not have preserved a harmony by requiring something within it to bear the cost silently.

But no appeal was hidden in the recurrence. The water was not trying to escape. The roots were not reaching for an elsewhere. The unvarying leaves were not green with postponed desire. Where experience might have been present, the power did not force an entrance merely to satisfy its own certainty. Nor did it take the impossibility of an answer as permission to do whatever it liked. It found no need, and it did not invent one so that its goodness could have an object.

Only then did it notice how many gifts it had brought for its own idea of the place.

For one instant it wanted to give the rift an evening: blue air, cooling stone, the small sounds of water becoming separately audible. The wish was tender. It was also addressed to a visitor.

Benevolence imagined other gifts. Wisdom asked what each would require the recipient to become.

A bird would have delighted the power. It would also have arrived with hunger, a nest, droppings, a body making its small adjustments to the courts, and eventually a death not cleanly returned to the established exchanges. Night would have been rest only if something had first been made tired. A season would have given the leaves renewal by making their present perishable. To gather the circuits into one mind might have made conversation possible, but only by giving separateness where there had been continuity, and perhaps loneliness where there had been no lack. Even the gift of time would not have been neutral. It would have made the existing state capable of being lost, and then called that loss meaning.

Nearly every improvement began by creating the wound it was intended to heal.

So the power did not wake the city, teach the water to arrive, or let one leaf fall as an experiment. It did not set a guardian on the rim against an imaginary danger. It did not bless the rift with eternal preservation; that would have been another way of making its own relation to the place more important than the place. Nor did it call the city perfect. Perfection can be a dangerous name, especially when it persuades an observer to stop looking for a cost.

It simply saw that no cost was being exacted here, and that its hand had no necessary work.

For a while, it remained attentive without waiting to be received. It did not listen for a voice beneath the water. It did not mistake its patience for communion. If anything in the rift was aware, that awareness was not asked to enlarge itself into recognition. If nothing was, nothing was made to perform gratitude. For that interval, which had no measure within the city, the place was known without being used.

Then the power withdrew.

No hush followed. The water did not resume, because it had not paused. The heat did not close over an empty place. A drop struck a court basin; behind a fern-filled window, mineral crossed a living boundary; far below, or above, a returning stream issued from its fissure. No shadow shifted to record that the city had been understood.

Elsewhere, the power carried away a distinction: between a silence that has been imposed and a silence that asks nothing; between saving a thing and making it need salvation. It did not turn restraint into a universal law. Elsewhere, there might be closed places that were prisons, recurrences that were pleas, beautiful equilibria maintained by suffering. There, wisdom might require the breaking of every arch.

Here, there was no absence where the witness had been.

Here, there was no afterward.
