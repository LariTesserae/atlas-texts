### What the Power Did

The power did not enter.

This was not indifference, nor the fastidiousness of something unwilling to touch sap and new stone. It was the first form its care took.

A word spoken in that chamber might have altered a distance. A footstep might have become part of the plant’s next decision about where a wall was going to be. A shadow might have offered the vault another body to imitate. Even to stand in the clear center and think, *How lonely*, would have risked furnishing the hollow with a sorrow its inhabitant had not grown.

So the power remained beyond the fractured slope and waited.

At first it considered gifts.

It could have settled the space: given every wall a lasting distance from every other, made echoes follow their causes, and relieved the plant of the immense labor of finding itself again after each rain. But stability would not merely have eased the work. It would have ended a kind of work, and perhaps a kind of knowing.

It could have left the valve-opening animal one thorn that could not be softened, enclosed, or taken back. Then cleverness might have become practice, and practice inheritance. There would eventually have been a favored stone, a worn place, perhaps a cache. It would have been a generous gift to history, but the animal had not asked to become historical.

It could have made the moth a pollinator, turning a small loss of sugar into a partnership. It could have given the branching shadow roots, bark, and a destiny. It could have populated the floor with moulds and mites and all the busy inheritors of remains.

Each gift would have been good of a kind. Each would also have made the hollow easier to describe.

The power began to distrust how much that ease appealed to it.

So it watched without requiring watching to become diagnosis. It did not mistake expense for suffering, or caution for fear. It did not assume that the plant’s divided agreements were a failed unity, or that its maintained center was a wound. When a new pocket opened, perfectly empty and already costly to sustain, the power tried not to ask what it was for.

That took longer than any of the miracles it had considered.

Had there been plain harm, it would have acted. It was not so devoted to restraint that it would call a preventable crushing of the slope *respect*, or make a doctrine of leaving things untouched. But youth was not injury. Difficulty was not necessarily distress. An unusual life was not, by itself, an appeal to be made more ordinary.

Even the word *unfinished* began to seem suspect. Young was a condition. Unfinished implied an appointment.

The plant had made no promise to keep that appointment.

At last the power did something. It set down its wish to be useful there.

It left no blessing in the sap, no guardian at the pore, no command over the rain. It did not preserve the chamber at its most beautiful, or ensure that the clear center would never close. It did not make emptiness sacred in the name of protecting it. If the plant later filled the middle with roots, folded the whole chamber into a seed, opened it toward some use no visitor could yet imagine, or simply ceased, the power would not call its own restraint wasted. Care that depends on the recipient remaining worthy of one’s first impression is only another kind of possession.

It would still have turned aside the hill, had the hill been falling. It would still have answered a need, if need and not resemblance ever made one plain. But it would not confuse help with authorship.

The power took away no specimen and left no sign. It did not even keep an image of the chamber, knowing that any image would soon become false. What it kept was smaller: the memory that it had once been able to improve something, and had not been able to establish that improvement was wanted.

Before going, it watched one more shower.

The vault roared. Patches of the pale floor darkened, and water vanished into grooves without rounded corners. Somewhere a boom occurred slightly before the growth that caused it. A tendril thickened, turned, and declined the center. Then another precise reach of clear air opened where there had been no space at all.

The power felt the last temptation to call this an answer.

It let that pass, too.

Then it went, taking with it everything it had brought, including its shadow.

It had not finished the hollow. Neither had it asked the hollow to remain unfinished.
