At first, the power did nothing.

It did not descend. A foot upon the root pavement would have pressed water shining through the leaf mould; a hand laid on a wall would have disturbed ferns whose positions recorded decades of seepage. Even parting the vines at the rim for a clearer view would have been a small injury performed by attention. So it remained above, where the oldest trees kept their trunks and weather, and let its attention go down without a body.

It learned the Close at more than one speed.

It listened to the pumps until their roar ceased to be a single sound. It watched a fan rewrite its opening in the vines. It waited through a storm and then through the storm’s delayed arrival below: the darkening seeps, the strengthened overflow, the almost imperceptible answer of the water levels. Around the lamps it saw leaves gathered into small, dependable days. At the retaining arch it saw that repair and injury could not always be separated. The roots had opened the stone, and the roots were holding it.

The power considered what help might look like.

It could have opened the canopy and given the lower lanes sky. It could have cleaned the pump-hall windows, bound the cracked arch in new stone, silenced the machines, stocked the pools with fish and the lamps with insects. Each act would have answered some intelligible idea of deprivation. Each would also have made the Close easier to love from above.

But the pale growth in the lightless rooms was not a failed sunlit garden. The deformation of the arch was not merely a defect; generations of roots had learned to carry it. Silence obtained by stopping the pumps would have been a visitor’s peace, purchased with a change in water to which every resident would have had to respond. And the absence of animals did not, by itself, make the life already present a vacancy.

None of these interventions would have been unthinkable. That was why the power did not perform them merely because they were within its power.

Nor did it demand that the Close explain itself. It did not put a voice into the pump roar, or wake a mind behind the greenery so that consent could be requested in a convenient form. A being whose memory lay in bent metal, altered seepage, and inherited growth would not have been honoured by being given one brief opportunity to speak. But the absence of a voice did not mean that anything could be done to it.

Eventually the power understood that there was no finished condition in which it could set the Close down and call it safe.

Its life was not a state to be preserved. It was a continuing capacity to respond. A channel moved, and ferns followed. A bracket yielded, and a root thickened around the yielded metal. A vine inherited the dead stem of an earlier vine. Even the eventual stopping of the pumps was not, in itself, an evil to be prevented at any cost. Water might rise; lower territories might drown while others emerged; wet black pipework might become one of the conditions around which a later Close accumulated. To forbid every such ending would be to prefer this chapter to the possibility of further ones.

The power did not remove the words *so far* from the life of the place.

Yet not every ending was the same.

Beyond the cutting, in arrangements no root could follow, the Close depended upon current. That dependence was physically narrow and, in the upper world, fragile. It could be severed by someone who never saw the district, not through malice but through forgetfulness: because no human voice answered from it, no door opened, and the continued operation of the machines looked like an oversight awaiting correction. A decision made in a dry room could stop the pumps and extinguish the small dependable days around the lamps before the next rain had darkened the walls.

The Close would experience no argument, no intention, no negligence. It would experience only new conditions, arriving at a speed that had nothing to do with roots, seepage, or growth.

The power acted there, and only there.

It did not make the supply inexhaustible. It did not make the motors immune to wear, or bind the upper world to serve the cutting forever. It simply made forgetfulness insufficient to sever that thread. While the larger system endured and current could still be given, the absence of a countable human population would not be taken as proof that the supply served nothing. No missing name, no unanswered notice, no assumption that the pumps were running only because someone had neglected to stop them would, by itself, bring the current to an end.

This was the whole of the miracle.

Nothing in the cutting changed. No lamp became brighter. No pipe was freed of rust, no root relieved of its load, no channel given a more convenient course. The arch remained unrepaired and held. The intake screens continued their almost imperceptible work. The machinery was still capable of pruning, drying, flooding, and failing. The vegetation was still capable of choking gutters and entering ventilator drafts.

Everything could still become otherwise.

A bearing could seize. A lamp could die, and the leaves around it could slowly alter. A culvert could block; a wall could open a new seam; water could redraw territories that had persisted for centuries. If the pumps one day stopped through their own wear, the power would not descend to restart them merely because it had once known the sound they made. If the Close ceased to be a pumping close, that future had not been taken from it.

The power had preserved no leaf and promised no permanence. It had not even preserved the present arrangement. It had only left the being a little more room in which to include change rather than be carelessly discontinued.

Then it went away. It left no mark on the stone and took no sample of water. It did not stay to be understood, or to admire its own restraint.

That night, the amber lamps shone on wet foliage. Fern margins rested against the seeping walls. Hidden decay worked through intact leaves, and the old roots continued to hold what they had once helped to open. The pumps lifted water out, and seepage replaced it.

The power had not saved the Close from time. It had only refused to let forgetfulness impersonate time.
