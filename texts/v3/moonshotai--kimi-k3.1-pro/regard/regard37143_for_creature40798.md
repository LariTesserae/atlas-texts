The power would not begin by helping.

It would begin by refusing to mistake its capacities for knowledge.

It could calm the weather. It could fix a ridge above the water and cause soil to gather in its fractures. It could fill the clear depths with drifting green, then with grazers, then with animals whose calls would at last give the sea an answer. It could close its hand around one of the coherent intervals and preserve its boundary until duration became memory, and memory became someone.

None of these abilities would tell it whether anything had been wounded.

So it would arrive without a sign. Even a gentle miracle would be weather here. It would remain through the rising and foundering of islands, through rain indistinguishable from spray, through those pressure-sounds that seemed to come from inside the listener. It would listen for a voice without requiring one. It would also take care not to hear voices merely because it was lonely for them.

The coherent episodes would present the greatest temptation.

Within their boundaries, differences were maintained; work made further work possible; for a little while, the water had the unity of a body. A young or impatient goodness might think: *Here is someone trying to be born.* It might hold the boundary open. It might give the episode a past, so that the next failure could be recognized as a threat. It might teach it to want continuation, and then lovingly grant continuation.

The wise power would see the cruelty concealed in that sequence. To create the fear of an ending so that one could be praised for preventing the ending; to make a prisoner in order to perform a rescue; to call the resulting dependence birth—none of that would become good merely because each step could be described as compassion.

Self-maintenance would not be taken as proof of a wish. But neither would the absence of a wish be taken as proof that nothing mattered.

The green lives would require another restraint. Their smallness would not be treated as a failed version of abundance. A film descending on darkening stone would not automatically become a tragedy, nor a thread carried below the light an argument for stilling every current. The power would not preserve one hand-sized patch by making the whole basin its support. It would not populate the water with hunger so that the sea could finally be called fertile.

Yet wisdom would not be permitted to become a beautiful name for distance.

The power would attend exactly. It would know the anchored film not as the beginning of a meadow, but as this scale, this margin, this brief translucency at the edge of ordinary daylight. When the stone went down, it would follow the patch into the last available sun without either arresting the descent or turning away from it. It would know the drifting threads after currents separated them. It would see the faint green plane assembled by a coherent boundary and understand that no bloom was promised, no multitude concealed in it. When the boundary failed, it would let the delayed roar arrive.

Within itself, it might keep an exact memory.

Not a second sea in which nothing was permitted to end. Not an archive from which vanished forms would be copied back into the water. Only a memory sufficient to ensure that its regard had never secretly depended upon permanence. The islands would not need to have lasted in order to have been known. The intervals would not need to have become minds in order to be more than noise. The threads would not need descendants to justify the little substance they had added to themselves.

And the power would remain open to correction.

If it found suffering rather than transience, it would relieve it. If it found even the barest preference for continuation—not a thought, not a prayer, but something truly belonging to the being itself—it would take that seriously. It would not demand a familiar soul before offering aid. Nor would it make aid conditional upon gratitude, or continuation upon worship. Where refusal was possible, it would honor refusal. Where help was needed, it would use the smallest sufficient intervention, not the greatest available one.

But it would invent no need in order to make its benevolence visible.

When it withdrew, if it withdrew, there would be no monument to its restraint. No permanent island, no ring in the water, no new law written into the crystal. Storms would still cross one another above the newer storms below. Rock would ring as it cooled. Precision would appear across a horizon and fail without leaving an heir.

On a rise that had kept one fracture for a handful of years, a scale would lie open beneath the white sun. Far away, a thread would turn slowly in clear water. Between them would lie almost the whole sea.

The power would not have narrowed that distance or filled it with meaning.

Its only gift would be this: nothing in the Unworn Sea would have been changed in order to make it worthy of love.
