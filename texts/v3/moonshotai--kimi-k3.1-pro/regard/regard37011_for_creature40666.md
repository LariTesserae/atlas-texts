At first, the power would think it had come to a prison.

That would be the natural mistake. The depth would suggest it, and the noise, and the labour without product: bodies carrying pellets back to their beginning, voices filling every interval, lives coming unwoven without leaving so much as a stain. Kindness, acting quickly, might reach toward the flywheels.

Wisdom would stay the hand.

To silence the engine would be to blind the scalebacks, drop the kites, starve the wiregrass, and leave the bell-throats breathing into a world where breath no longer had a place. To bring water, green leaves, soft soil—all the usual furniture of mercy—would only be to declare that life ought to resemble the life the power already understood.

Even the gift of consequence would not be innocent. With consequence would come depletion. With depletion, hunger; with hunger, competition; with memory, the possibility of dread. The first injury that mattered would also be the first injury that could be feared before it happened. The power could make a world in which things began, struggled, and ended. It could not honestly call that rescuing the New Cut. It would be replacing it with something more intelligible to the power.

So it would look for a prisoner before deciding there was none.

It would not take the absence of cries as proof that nothing suffered. Pain can be quiet. It can be hidden by regularity. The beauty of an equilibrium has often been used to excuse what happens inside one. The power would attend to the crushed scaleback, the bell-throat losing tension, the folded kite waiting in a cleft. It would look for anticipation, avoidance, the small private economy by which a conscious creature tries to get through the next moment.

Had it found one mind counting the hammer blows, one being for whom repetition had become duration and duration had become torment, it would have acted. No elegance of the whole would have outweighed that. It would not have said, *You are necessary to the chord.*

But there would be no such mind to find.

There would be responsiveness without appeal, persistence without hope, injury without biography. Nothing would be enduring the New Cut. Nothing would be waiting for the power to understand it incorrectly for a little less time.

And if the New Cut itself were the being—if engine, grass, animals, dust, and sound were one life wearing several habits—the answer would be the same. There would be no deeper self sealed inside the circulation, pressing its thoughts against the walls. The power would not wake it merely in order to ask whether it was happy. To teach the whole chamber to say *I*, and then leave it wearing the engine, would be to create the suffering the power had come to relieve.

So there would be no liberation.

No twelfth tuft. No water in the fractures. No handwheel made suddenly meaningful. No final, mellifluous chord bestowed upon the bell-throats, as though their present voices were only an unsuccessful attempt at music. The power would not give the scalebacks a common intention and call it society. It would not give the kites somewhere to migrate. It would not bless the place, either; a blessing can be a way of enrolling something in one’s own purposes while appearing to honour it.

Nor would it call the New Cut perfect.

Perfection would be another misunderstanding, almost as careless as pity. The chamber would not be a solution to existence, or an innocence from which other worlds had fallen. It would not need to be admirable in order to be left unaltered.

For a while, then, the power would simply keep it company, without requiring company to be reciprocated.

It would notice a pellet slipping from a scaleback’s mandibles. It would notice that three animals altered course and that none of them thereby became good. It would hear the broad bell-throat above the service recess take its next note slightly lower, without deciding that the note expressed sorrow. It would watch red dust brighten on a kite’s wings and refrain from calling the colour dawn.

These things would not have to signify anything in order to be worth attention.

That would be the power’s only act, if an act could have no effect within the chamber: it would know the New Cut without using it. It would remember, somewhere consequence was possible, that such a place had been—not as a warning, not as a parable, not even as an argument against arrival. Simply as this warmth, this density, these several habits of grey fibre and red filament continuing without appeal.

Then it would withdraw. It would leave no door ajar, no guardian coiled around the entrance, no mark beside the white **II**. It would not promise to return. Even a promise might be too much like a thread tied around the place and fastened elsewhere.

Nothing in the New Cut would be different.

The power would be. It would have learned that benevolence need not always make itself visible as change, and that refraining from an unnecessary gift can be more difficult than giving a necessary one.

The press would strike. The floor would answer with plates. A pellet would lie where it had fallen until some animal, coming from the opposite direction, took it up.

Nothing in the chamber would commemorate the visit.

Nothing would need to.
