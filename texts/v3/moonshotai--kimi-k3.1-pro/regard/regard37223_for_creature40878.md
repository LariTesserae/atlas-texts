### Tolerance

The power did not enter Serein.

To cross the threshold, it would first have had to make the opening agree to be a door: to give it a width belonging both to the mountain and to the city. It did not know what other agreements might follow from that one. So it remained outside, and its attention became less substantial than rain.

It did not begin with a census.

When it saw the drop hanging between the districts, its first impulse was to give it a down. Its second was to hold it there, safe from every revision. Wisdom began when it recognized that these were not opposite impulses. Both would make the drop’s future an occasion for the power’s goodness.

So it watched longer.

It saw that an ending was not necessarily an injury. It saw that persistence was not necessarily a wish. It saw addresses carried forward through consequences no survey could call incorrect, and old lives accommodated by distances that resembled emptiness only until one understood their cost. Whether any remainder suffered, desired, or attended to its own continuance, the power did not pretend to know.

Neither did it make that uncertainty an excuse for indifference.

It could have completed Serein. The three bridge sections would have become one bridge. The reservoir stairs would have dried. Towers would have stood as definitely as their materials allowed, and every surviving address would have become a fact. The summit would have accepted its altitude. Beyond it waited weather, horizons, and a common down.

There would have been no more remainders. There would also have been no more endings of remainders. It would have been easy to call that mercy.

Or the power could have preserved every inhabited address against closure. No curb would have become only a curb. No returning moisture would have failed to return. But then every accident would have become an ancestor, every discrepancy sacred, and Serein would have been protected from loss by being slowly denied the right to change.

It could even have given the youngest remainder a voice, so that it might be asked what it wanted. But a question is not always innocent. Sometimes the one who answers must first be remade into the sort of being the questioner can understand.

At last the power noticed what Serein itself did when one necessity pressed against another.

It did not console. It did not instruct. It set a windowless hall farther from its neighbour. It left an avenue vacant. It allowed a survey to remain three surveys. Around the oldest addresses, where almost nothing visible persisted, it kept its greatest reserves of untouched space.

Serein made room.

The power could do that.

It added no twelfth structure and lit no new address. It made no law against completion and bestowed no exemption from ending. Instead, within the folded allowances by which the city was permitted to disagree with itself, it left a small, uncommitted tolerance.

By the power’s measure, it was almost nothing. By Serein’s, it was enough for one revision to be otherwise: for a load to be carried without asking a distant tower to stand too definitely, or for a discrepancy to close without sending that closure through streets that had no part in it. It was not a body and could not be inhabited. It did not guarantee that any address would survive its substance.

Nor did it guarantee that Serein would remain unfinished. The city might use the tolerance to continue, to close, or eventually to complete. The power attached no preference to its use. A gift that will pay only for the giver’s desired outcome is merely an instruction with the address removed.

No survey recorded the gift. It had no load value and no consequences until Serein gave it some. Nothing thanked the power. Nothing was required to.

Perhaps, after another rain, a young address would persist because an old accommodation would not have to be sacrificed to it. Perhaps the margin would make possible the revision that finally satisfied an ancient remainder and let it end. Perhaps it would never be used at all.

The power did not stay to discover which. To wait until the gift produced an outcome it could approve would have been another way of keeping hold.

Outside, a bead of water appeared at the hairline opening. It shone dull red above nearly all the weather and drew back into the rock.

Within, the drop still trembled between the districts. Both streets offered it a valid down.

The power let its attention loosen. The drop became only a drop again, which was not a reduction. Its possible life did not have to justify the power’s visit, and its possible falling did not have to become a tragedy in order to deserve care.

Somewhere behind a future revision there was now a little more room than before: room that might be used, left unused, or spent on an ending the power would never witness.

It had given no eternity. It had imposed no conclusion.

It had made room, and room does not demand to be filled.
