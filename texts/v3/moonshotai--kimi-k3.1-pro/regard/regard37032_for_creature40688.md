The power’s first mercy was to arrive without light.

Light had occurred to it. So had warmth, and the opening of the seam. It could have set a small, perpetual day among the leaves. It could have made the cold water run bright and sweet. It could have fitted the clay threshold into a wall and given the wall an elsewhere. It could have done all this gently.

It did not mistake gentleness for innocence.

Warmth would have hurried the oldest leaf-skins toward endings they had so far been spared. Light would have distinguished between the lives that could use it and the lives that could only endure it. An open seam would have admitted the outer flood’s appetite for sorting: sediment into layers, tissue into food, one body into several remains. To carry the grazer away would have been to call its transparency helplessness and its slowness infirmity, and then to rescue it from the names.

So the power waited at the edge of its own intention until intention ceased to resemble pressure.

For a long time, it only attended.

It did not part the vegetation in order to see. It did not follow a root to both sides of the pocket and then decide which touching was true. It did not count the leaves, or require the leaves sharing an injury to share an explanation. When a click entered the water, soft and sourceless, the power did not hasten it toward its cause. When the threshold was absent, it did not call the clay back merely to relieve the water of that rectangular heaviness.

Little by little, the room ceased to resemble a problem.

Nothing pressed toward the seam. Nothing waited beside it. The grazer’s pauses on the clay were not messages. The moss was not maintaining the thumbprint for a maker. The current did not carry recollections of the inundated bank because the inhabitants wished to return there. A frond leaned toward warmth that would never arrive, and this was not necessarily sorrow.

The power had thought wisdom would tell it what the room needed. Instead, wisdom showed it how many of its questions were doors it wished to build.

Still, it wished to be kind. That wish had not become false merely because there was nothing to repair.

So, when the grazer next settled on the clay with all its legs still, the power made itself small.

Not merely small in body. Small in consequence.

It laid aside the ability to open stone, summon summer, postpone death, or make one thing permanently distinct from another. It kept only the ability to accompany without requiring recognition.

A second pallor gathered just behind the grazer.

It did not feed. No film grew thinner beneath it, and the dark comma in the leading animal’s gut remained the only stain of recent nourishment. It gave off no warmth toward which a growing tip might turn. Where it passed, the plants were not asked to close more quickly or remember a passage.

When the grazer turned, the follower did not turn early to show that it knew the way. When the grazer crossed the threshold, the follower crossed too, whether clay was present or only the squared persistence of its absence. It did not pretend to see a doorway. It did not pretend there was none.

Sometimes their outlines coincided. There was no collision. The power did not call this contact, or acceptance, or an answer. If the grazer knew company, company occurred. If it did not, nothing had been demanded of it.

How long this continued is not a useful question. The threshold was absent and then present. The white moss occupied the hollow without appearing to have crossed any frontier. Somewhere among the packed growth, a crease became old on a leaf that had not yet received it. The power remained until remaining would have become another condition the room had to accommodate.

Then it withdrew, or its following ceased to be distinguishable from the delayed fact of the grazer’s movement.

Afterward, it left the seam to its slow narrowing. It did not make the pocket everlasting. Permanence would have been only another way of refusing to let events finish. It left no ward whose vigilance would have kept the room forever related to danger. It pressed no second thumb into the clay. It took no specimen, no spore veil, no fragment of silica clear enough to serve as proof.

It took away no map by which kindness could become a route back.

Outside, the lowland shifted and silted. Inside, the current continued through wall and water, distributing cold and mineral sweetness. The grazer fed on the oldest skins. The films replenished themselves. Nothing appealed for further assistance, and the power did not interpret this as contentment. It had learned not to make a diagnosis out of either silence or abundance.

Sometimes, just behind the grazer, a second pallor still gathers.

It does not always repeat each step. Sometimes it waits where the grazer has paused, then follows late. Sometimes it is empty clear through. The vegetation becomes a little less crowded around it, or around the single animal of which it may be a postponed part.

Afterward, one grazer is visible.

The room continues without ever learning whether anything has been done.
