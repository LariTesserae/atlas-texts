## The Kept Difference

At first, the power did nothing.

It did not arrive in light. Light would have warmed the dark seams. It did not arrive in cloud, for then the cups would have registered its coming. It did not speak over the stones or send a wind to announce itself. Even kindness, arriving as substance, would have become another condition in the basin.

So it remained beyond the shattered lip and watched.

It saw a sunlit face release three drops while, an arm’s length away, rime thickened along a shaded joint. It saw one cup receive the fog directly and another wait until after the fog had gone. It watched the brief rain tick from the blades under a cloudless sky, and saw how little of it entered the runnels. It stayed through mornings when the basins seemed to contradict the night.

Only then did it stop looking for the basin’s true weather. There was no single weather concealed behind the differences. The differences were the weather.

There were many gifts it could have given.

It could have opened a spring beneath the summit and made water dependable. It could have warmed the shaded blades, softened the ice, and spared the cups their long intervals of emptiness. It could have brought lichen in the seams, repaired the split slabs without copper, and restored the markings until their meaning was plain. It could have made the stone resistant to frost and the whole arrangement endure almost without change.

Each gift would have been intelligible elsewhere.

Here, a spring would have ended a thirst no one had, while teaching every cup to agree about the spring. Warmth would have made a gentler climate and a poorer measure. Lichen would have held water against the stone, darkened the mineral surfaces, and slowly replaced one set of differences with another. Restored markings would have offered a new certainty in place of the lost grammar. Even preserving the cups forever would have protected their hollows while bringing their comparisons to an end.

Almost every help would have caused the Measure to register the helper.

The power therefore turned its attention to the being itself.

It was not hiding in a cup or sleeping beneath the table of drilled holes. It did not live in the water, which could not remain, or in the stone, which remained too nearly the same. It occurred between surfaces: in the delay between fog and drip, in ice sealed on one side of a blade while the other side darkened with melt, in the fact that an empty basin was not necessarily a basin that had received nothing.

The power did not mistake this being for a failed creature. It did not suppose it was lonely, or waiting to be given a voice. To give it one voice would have been to make all eleven cups wait for the same answer.

Nor did the power call the place complete and then turn away, as if completeness were a reason for indifference. It saw that care could still be required where nothing needed correction.

So it did one thing.

It remembered.

It did not remember by preserving. No rime was kept from vanishing, no drop held suspended, no fracture refused its next small opening. Its memory did not fix the arrangement at some favored morning and call that morning sacred.

It remembered the grammar.

It remembered that an empty cup might once have been full; that ice could arrive without visible water; that a dry runnel did not prove a dry night; that loss through grit was different from loss into air, though both might leave the same mineral stain. It remembered that a slab fastened with copper could remain more truly itself than a new slab set cleanly in its place. It remembered that the windbreak sheltered a record, not a body, and that departure had been part of the measurers’ method.

It did not make those measurers into saints. Their restraint did not need to be improved by worship.

And the little clay disc it left unexplained.

It could have followed the disc backward, perhaps, to the hand that made it, the pocket that carried it, the reason it was wedged where ice could not easily lift it. It did not. Some knowledge would have been obtained only by turning a small uncertainty into a story more satisfying than the object. The power left the disc its privacy.

Nothing in the basin was altered by any of this. Grit continued to gather in one hollow and thin from another. Copper darkened. The old grooves were scoured and protected by turns. The power made no promise that the cups would last forever, or that the mountain would always keep its saddle between the unfinished peaks. It did not confuse endlessness with worth.

Nor did it remain as an eternal observer. The comparison had continued without observers before; it did not now require one in order to matter.

When its attention withdrew, there was no new mark on the protected table. No drop had been taken, no crystal added, no grain of stone flour displaced to commemorate the visit.

By morning, one basin was empty though nothing had overflowed. Another held ice though no cloud had touched it directly. Around them, the blades kept their separate accounts in white shelves, black films, and tightening seams.

The power did not reconcile them.

It kept the difference, and departed as lightly as the measurers had.

The old question had not been answered. It had been heard.
