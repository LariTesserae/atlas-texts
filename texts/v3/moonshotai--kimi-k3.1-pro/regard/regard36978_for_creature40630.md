### The Unrequired Step

The power did not enter at first.

It kept its warmth beyond the fog and its voice beyond the saturated ground. It had seen what warmth became here: not comfort, but a gathering, then a migration, then a nation of hungers crowded around the last fading gradient. A voice would feed the fog herds or teach some small hush where to wait. Even pity, if it arrived carelessly enough, might become weather.

The obvious mercies were all dangerous.

To make room would only summon another reach of inhabited ground, complete with worn feet and unfinished grievances. To fill the pools with light would alter every alignment within them. To impose silence would do the hushes’ work on a scale no hush had ever managed. And to carry an inhabitant away might be indistinguishable from killing it: a stone removed from its vent was wet gravel; a swimmer removed from its current was dirty water. A being rescued from every relationship might thereby be rescued from the only means by which it was a being.

So the power waited.

Not because waiting was innocent. Waiting could become cowardice, or appetite, or the pleasure of watching something intricate suffer beautifully. The power waited only until it could distinguish attention from fascination.

At full compression, it nearly mistook the Works for the one who needed answering. The lowland lowered itself through millions of backs; current passed through the pools; fog opened above the vents. For that stroke, everything seemed to participate in a single vast intention.

Then the pressure released, and the intention became a crowd again.

The power did not ask the roar whether the roar wished to continue. It did not make the mistake of loving the whole so much that the parts became negligible. Antiquity was not consent. Self-maintenance was not consent. Beauty was not consent.

It began listening at smaller scales.

This took longer. A request did not have to resemble speech, and a refusal did not have to resemble stillness. Sometimes a preference was no more than a repeated turning toward a particular weakness in the current. Sometimes it was an avoidance persisting after the danger had passed. Sometimes an animal’s route continued through several kinds of bodies after there was no longer anyone within the route who could be said to wish for it.

The power learned not to confuse persistence with desire.

Then it began to help, but never at the scale that had first tempted it.

It did not preserve every body. Many lives depended upon boundaries failing, and to keep every outline intact would have been another kind of extinction. It did not treat every death as an injury to the universe, or every survival as a good. Where an injury still guided escape, it left the animal its alarm. Where there was no longer any escape to guide, it did not require agony to continue merely because scavengers had not finished, or because a warning body still had one generation of pale anticipants to feed.

Most of all, it refused to make a covenant with the Works. It would not guarantee the next compression. Neither would it end the cycle merely to demonstrate that it could.

Its one lasting gift could not be eaten, precipitated, inherited, or used to open more ground.

It was the possibility of an ending that did not have to be useful.

A gait could still pass into the mud fauna. A fog herd’s route could still survive in its juveniles. Back-runners could still inherit countries of armor that had not yet met. Nothing visible needed to change. But no awareness would be compelled to travel onward inside its old habits simply because something else had learned to continue them. No preference would be treated as consent forever. No life would be kept within reach of the circulation solely because the circulation had found a use for it.

Nor would the power take anyone away for their own good while they were still reaching, in their own manner, for the next gradient.

On a reach of ground that had only just made room for itself, the power found an old animal.

At present it wore a rillback’s porous dome. Hundreds of short feet carried it through the wet mineral film, and three separate multitudes beneath those feet repeated slightly different versions of its limp. Its armor held back-runners whose geography was older than the plates they inhabited. Farther along its route, several low domes were already forming out of delays in its passage.

Perhaps the animal had once been only the limp. Perhaps the route was older than the animal. The power did not need to settle this before treating it gently.

It offered no youth. Youth here would only have meant recently assembled matter. It offered no perfect body, no warm refuge, no place beside itself where the animal could be admired forever.

It offered one step that did not have to be taken.

The herd did not pause. No bare patch opened. The cold did not relent. Nothing appeared to mark the gift, because anything that appeared could have become part of the animal’s necessity.

Had the old animal stopped, the power would have remained with it—not to restart the feet, not to preserve the dome against every smaller life that needed it, and not to call stillness peace without first learning whether it was. Had the animal refused the route, that refusal would not have been converted into another animal’s weather.

The old animal turned onto the migration route it had followed all its life.

The power did not make this mean too much.

It was not gratitude. It was not a declaration that the cold was good, that every crushing flank was justified, or that being ancient was preferable to being allowed to end. It was not the Works answering for all its inhabitants.

It was one old animal taking one familiar step.

The power watched until it could be certain that no refusal was being overridden. Then it let the act remain small.

Behind it, the saturated ground tightened. Vents opened beneath distant herds. Blue current gathered in pools whose hunters were already waiting at the kinks. The whole region answered the compression at once, already too crowded, already making room.

The power added no voice to the roar.

It did not mistake the answer for consent.

It stayed to listen for the exceptions.
