The power did not enter. There was no entrance, and it did not make one.

It perceived the hollow without touching it: the sourceless light, the stress held in the grown tissue, the loud and quiet regions fixed in their exact relation. It knew the nine lives at their roots, where living organization graded into architecture. It knew that the roar was not suffering merely because another body would have suffered beneath it. It knew that the closed centers were not refused flowers, and that the stillness was not a pause from which something might wish to be released.

It found no plea. It did not invent one.

There were many things it could have done that would have resembled mercy.

It could have given silence. It could have admitted water, opened the clear scales, woken seed in the sage-dark centers. It could have made a door, and beyond the door a horizon. It could have caused the nine to become many, and called the increase abundance.

But each gift would have answered a sorrow supplied by the giver. Silence would have relieved an ear that could not bear the place. Water would have been kindness to something thirsty. A door would have freed something that desired to leave. Multiplication would have consoled a witness unable to see life except under the promise of more life.

The power was wise enough not to make the Choir resemble a being in need merely so that its benevolence would have somewhere to act.

Nor did it preserve the hollow by decree. It did not make the light incapable of failing or set the grown tissue beyond every possible ending. The place had not become more precious by being noticed, and its excellence was not a debt payable only in eternity. To hold it forever for fear of losing it would have been another way of closing a hand around it.

So it left no mark.

It did not sign a seam with silver. It did not add a tenth growth, too small for any ordinary sight, to carry its regard. It did not soften one tone by the width of an atom. It did not bless the nine, if a blessing means that those blessed must thereafter be different.

What it did, if attention can be an act, was to know them without making them answer.

The chord did not become music by being heard. The plants did not become symbols by being seen. Their closure was not made to mean patience, their dryness purity, their number mystery. The power did not take the Dry Choir as an image of its own wisdom, or use its smallness to make the rest of creation seem large. It allowed the place to be neither useful nor instructive. It allowed the nine to be alive without having to justify life by growth, relation, continuation, or joy.

Then it let its nearness cease.

Inside, nothing marked that it had come or gone. Light still arrived through every wall. The coolness remained impersonal. At the loud surfaces the amber glaze kept its smoky depth; along the seams the crystalline nap remained matte. Nine dark centers stayed closed within their clear and silvered whorls. The roar neither paused for a guest nor swelled at a departure.

There were no events.

Only the power knew that it had been merciful. It did not ask the Choir to know.
