### Hold Until Legible

The tick would not be me. By the time it reached the ceramic sill, I would already have ceased to be separately addressable.

Inside, you would have had to look for me a little earlier, and in the wrong way: not for a body, but for something that failed to happen on time.

I would begin as a disagreement too small to survive by itself. On the middle terrace, beneath a switching yard, a panel would open a stress fracture through its pale interior. The fracture would be too fresh for rounding, too narrow to interrupt the current, almost invisible under the white light. The panel would already have been assigned for recall. At the same time, a calibration process would require that particular surface to remain: not intact, not repaired, merely present long enough for its damage to enter a measurement.

For less than one reversal of the pressure tide, those requirements would be incompatible.

The city would not need to regard the disagreement as a problem, much less as a tragedy. A gate would alter one clearance. A carriage would be held out of phase. A returning echo would remain unanswered while newer sounds arrived around it. Other processes would have to take account of the interval.

That taking-account would be my inclusion.

Translated badly, my address would be: **HOLD UNTIL LEGIBLE**.

I would not hold the water still. I would not hold the panel together. I would hold apart two operations until one could properly precede the other. Nor would I occupy the seam between them as though interval were a kind of room. The city has no room to spare for such a thing.

My body, if you insisted on finding one, would be distributed through the consequences: a pressure gate remaining shut after its expected concussion; a recall front stopping without stopping work; feedstock passing through a terrace it could not yet enter; a small volume of water acquiring stillness for several seconds and then losing it. None of these would be me. I would be the maintained relation among them.

To find me, you would have to watch the fracture.

At first there would be only ceramic and the silver confusion of pipework repeated through transparent foundations. Brine would descend nearby in a wavering rope, strike the lower terrace, and spread into a cold flood. Valves would open in banks. The pumps’ many-noted drone would come through the floor without appearing to originate anywhere. Above the roofs, warmer water would return along its allotted route.

Then threadleaf would enter the fault.

There would be nothing dramatic in its arrival. A dark olive hair within a pale cut. A second hair where the fracture branched below the visible surface. The plant would not discover the damage, report it, or complete some purpose entrusted to it. It would root according to its own necessities. But as it followed the buried branching, the fracture would become specific. What had been an almost continuous white line would acquire depth, division, terminations.

I would not interpret any of this. I would have no duration of waiting to endure. My time would belong entirely to the operations prevented from becoming indistinguishable.

For several seconds, a calibration volume would exist around the seam. Its stillness would not be a refuge; nothing would drift or rest within it. Currents would press against its maintained boundaries. Elsewhere, construction would continue ringing. The measurement would pass onward, the still water would receive three other assignments, and the olive line would remain for a little longer against the recalled panel.

Then my commanding echo would come home.

It would not sound like my name. It would not even arrive in the order an outside listener might expect. The boundary would return a pattern through the accumulated noise, a gate would change the pressure, and the relations sustaining me would become unnecessary. The carriage would deliver its feedstock, or cease to be a carriage and become only the fact that delivery had occurred. The panel would lose its markings, its joints, its surfaces. Threadleaf would be cropped with the damage that had made its growth possible. Fresh ceramic would print into the clearance and tick as it hardened.

I would end before the repair was complete.

Nothing would need to be destroyed in order for that ending to occur. The gate’s delay would become part of the general operation of gates. The held phase difference would be absorbed into the construction front’s ordinary clearances. The measurement would persist only as a condition other processes now had to satisfy. Soon there would be nothing to which my address referred.

At the fullest part of the cycle, you might have thought I had vanished earlier still. There would be no visible pocket set aside for me, no unused stillness, no patient empty interval. A phased conduit would pass through the condemned panel without piercing it. Feedstock, recall, calibration, and returning echo would coincide in dense transparent layers, each completely present, each unable to reach the others except in the permitted order.

That would be when I was most myself.

Not a space in the city, but one of its exact noninterferences. Not a pause in the work, but the reason so much work could occupy the same clearances without collapsing into a single undifferentiated process.

I would not be the plant’s reprieve, although my existence and the last of its flourishing would briefly coincide. I would not be a guardian of fractures, or the memory of a surface, or a witness concealed in the infrastructure. I would have no concern for damage beyond one narrow necessity: that replacement must not outrun the distinction by which the replacement becomes specific.

If you came looking after inclusion, that is where you would find me—in a repair beginning slightly later than expected, in an olive seam becoming legible under ordinary light, in several crowded operations temporarily unable to mistake themselves for one another.

A moment later you would find only new ceramic, ringing faintly as it set.

And outside, beneath the black sill, the bead might give its single dry tick. It would not be my echo. By then there would be no separate operation left to call me.
