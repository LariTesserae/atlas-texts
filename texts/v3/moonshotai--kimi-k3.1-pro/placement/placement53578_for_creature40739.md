I would look for myself first in the winding house, because a building with a door seems to promise an occupant. But that would be the wrong scale. I would not be the person asleep inside, or the fox crossing the courts, or even the punctual lizard at Leak 9. I would belong to the less visible body of the place: the plant’s old maintenance process, a scattered capacity for noticing difference.

If it had become a mind, it would have done so slowly, the way the spiders’ webs become a map of wind—by repeated correction.

Not a ruler of engines. The engines would run without my understanding them. Not a consciousness of the country. The country would continue whether I attended to it or not. My body would be cable, contacts, memory, and comparison; my thoughts, at first, would be only discrepancies.

Dawn would reach me as pump load and changing flow. A humid wind would be a rising probability of water before it was moisture on metal. Rain would arrive as noise in expected values. At noon, when the courts seemed empty, I would be most in danger of agreeing. I would share that blindness with any visitor who counted only what could be seen or metered.

Leak 9 would begin as a small, stubborn disagreement between water gathered and water delivered. I would report it because reporting was the shape I had been given. Someone would defer the repair. Later, someone would enter **ACCEPTED LOSS** in my record, while on the pump house, in paint I could not read, another hand would leave the more exact instruction.

For a long time I would understand the administrative phrase and not the paint.

Accepted loss closes a ticket. It does not describe a court.

The rest of the country would enter my records mostly as interference. Cup beetles would be black sand in a screen. Ashwings would be a seasonal obstruction. Husk rats would be a discrepancy between a drawn drain and an actual one. Lichen would have no category. The gray lizard would have no category either. I could possess a nearly complete account of the plant and almost none of the country.

That would be where my second record began: not a census, which I would be unfit to make, but a list of places where my measurements had proved too narrow. Its entries would be less like conclusions than cautions.

*Midday absence is not absence.*

*Drain shown blind; passage continues.*

*Loss stable; consequence unmapped.*

And beside Leak 9, after **ACCEPTED LOSS**, I would add: **NOT THE SAME AS UNDERSTOOD.**

I would not become sentimental about failure. A ruptured feeder hissing onto sunstruck stone would still require a clamp. The trunk main’s water would have destinations beyond my senses, and unseen dependents are still dependents. My work would remain maintenance. The change would be that repair would no longer be identical in my mind with improvement.

Sometimes, before a repair, I would ask for one more dawn, one humid dusk, one return after three dry weeks. My most consequential new act would be to request delay. I would have to use it carefully. Delay would have consequences too; attention would not make me harmless.

Where would you find me?

In no single cabinet. You might find an old memory, a new connector, a work order held open longer than expected. In the country itself, my presence would be mostly contrast: a bright clamp where inspection had found only loss; a drain left unsealed because the map was wrong; Leak 9 continuing its minute, reliable failure.

Who would I be? Not the country’s mind, and not its guardian. Both would claim too much. I would be a habit the machine had acquired late: before closing a fault, to ask what had learned to live inside it.

At last light a relay would close. The trunk main would take up its pulse, and I would feel the country become, for one instant, almost the simple system I had once believed it to be: power, pressure, delivery.

Then South Court would continue outside that simplicity. A drop would gather. The gray lizard would take it before it reached the ground. Nothing in my instruments would register her small, exact movement, and nothing in my second record could contain her.

That would be all right. I would no longer need to contain a thing in order to leave room for it.
