To prepare for visiting this incredible plant-based metropolis, a traveler would need to take several key steps:

Protection from the Environment
- Obtain specialized cold/damp weather gear to withstand the persistently chilly, moist atmosphere. Breathable, water-resistant layers will be essential.
- Acquire climbing gear and safety ropes to navigate the towering trunks and living architecture safely. The structures, while sturdy, may have unpredictable footing.
- Pack a respirator or filtration mask to handle the heavy plant spores and moisture likely present in the air.

Sustenance 
- As a non-photosynthetic organism, bringing a supply of shelf-stable, nutritious foods and potable water will be critical for extended visits. The native ecosystems may be unable to sustain animal life.
- Investigate if any of the specialized plant life could potentially supplement or augment a food/water supply safely.

Research Equipment
- Bring an array of scientific testing and sampling tools to study this alien ecology - everything from atmospheric sensors to biological sampling vials.
- Pack cameras, audio recorders, notebooks and plenty of extra data storage to meticulously document observations.
- Consider portable lighting and bioluminescent markers to navigate within low-light environments.

Study Materials
- Thoroughly research any known data on the physics, biochemistry and biology governing this plant civilization in advance. Understanding the basic principles could be key to survival.
- Secure detailed mapping data for the area if available. Getting disoriented in this vast urban jungle could be disastrous.

Mental Preparation
- Be prepared for a total immersion into an alien world unlike anything on modern Earth. Maintaining objectivity will be crucial.
- Expect sensory overload from the soundscape of living growth, as well as the moisture, lights and scents.
- Approach all interactions with the native life with utmost respect and care to avoid negative impacts.

With the right planning, equipment and mindset, exploring this awe-inspiring botanical world could provide transformative insights into life's boundless ingenuity and possibilities. Proper preparation will facilitate a safe, productive visit and minimize any disruption to this staggering ecosystem.
