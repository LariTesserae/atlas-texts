Here are some tips for visiting the Sunken Furnace as a multiverse traveler:

Environmental Protection
• Obtain an environmental suit that can withstand extreme heat and provides breathable air/oxygen. The thick, heated air of the valley is unbreathable for humans.
• The suit should have solid protection from the rocky terrain and hostile lifeforms, including puncture/bite resistant material.
• Pack backup air supplies and cooling units in case the environmental suit fails.

Navigation 
• Obtain powerful illumination sources that can cut through the dense gloom, such as high-lumen headlamps or flares. The darkness is oppressive.
• Map out navigable routes through the maze of rock formations using imaging scanners. Don't rely on vision alone.
• Consider equipping a small drone or probe to scout ahead, as the passages are narrow and filled with hazards.
• Have an exit strategy planned in case you get turned around in the labyrinth.

Research & Observation
• Pack scientific gear to study the bizarre geology, atmospheric conditions, and lifeforms present.
• Specimen containers rated for extreme heat to collect samples safely.
• Extendable probes and micro-cameras to examine hazardous areas up close.
• Spectroscopic analyzers and scanners to study the luminescent flora/fauna.

General Survival 
• Bring ample supplies of high-calorie rations and water, as exploration will be extremely taxing.
• Have medkits with burn treatments and supplies to treat respiratory issues from the air.
• Backup power sources and spare parts in case equipment fails due to the harsh conditions.
• Consider a tether system or guide ropes to avoid becoming lost or separated from the exit.

The key is going in prepared for a truly alien environment hostile to human life. With the right gear and precautions, you may be able to safely study and document the wonders of this "sunken furnace" ecosystem. But the utmost care and respect for the hazards is essential.
