Visiting such a lush, vibrant oasis high in the mountains would be an incredible experience demanding considerable preparation. Here are some recommendations for an ordinary multiverse traveler:

Mental Preparation:
- Embrace an open mindset to the wonders you'll encounter. This ecosystem defies conventional boundaries between nature and civilization.
- Study up on the settlement's culture and history to appreciate how they've achieved such harmonious integration with their environment.
- Prepare for sensory overload - the sights, sounds, and smells will be utterly enveloping.

Physical Preparation:
- Acclimate yourself to the high altitude and humidity through graduated exposure if possible.
- Pack breathable, moisture-wicking clothing to stay comfortable in the wet heat.
- Bring rugged, waterproof footwear with good traction for navigating the terrain.
- Consider wearing a lightweight, waterproof outer layer for sudden misty downpours.

Gear:
- A waterproof daypack to carry essentials while keeping hands free.
- Tough gloves to aid in gripping mossy surfaces and pushing through dense foliage.  
- Trekking poles for stability on uneven, vine-laden trails.
- Binoculars for spotting elusive wildlife amidst the lush growth.
- A macro camera lens to capture the incredible biodiversity up close.
- Ample water and provisions for extended exploration.

Safety:
- Avoid drinking untreated water from streams to prevent potential illness.
- Apply insect repellent regularly in this humid ecosystem.
- Orient yourself frequently and employ a guide to avoid getting lost in the labyrinth.
- Respect all wildlife - many species may be unaccustomed or dangerous if startled.

With the right preparation, an ordinary traveler can fully immerse themselves in this extraordinary place - a vibrant oasis that harmonizes the natural and the artificial into one resplendent, living panorama.
