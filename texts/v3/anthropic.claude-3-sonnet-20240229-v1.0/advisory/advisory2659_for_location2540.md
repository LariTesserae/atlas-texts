Preparing to visit such an enigmatic and alien landscape would require a great deal of care and consideration. Here are some recommendations for an ordinary human multiverse traveler:

Mental Preparation:
1) Cultivate an open and inquisitive mindset. This environment will challenge many of your fundamental assumptions about reality. Embrace the unknown and be prepared to have your perceptions altered.
2) Study any available information on this realm, though data may be limited. Try to grasp the basic principles governing its physics, geometry, and potential lifeforms.
3) Practice techniques for maintaining calm and focus in extraordinarily strange situations. Meditation, conscious breathing, and mental grounding exercises could prove invaluable.

Physical Preparation:
1) Acquire a robust environmental suit capable of protecting you from unknown elements and conditions. It should provide oxygen, regulate temperature, filter contaminants, and shield you from potential radiations or energy fields.
2) Pack equipment for sampling and analysis - collection vials, microscopes, spectrometers etc. Anything to study the anomalous materials and structures you'll encounter.
3) Bring powerful illumination sources as the dim ambient glow may not be sufficient. However, be prepared for light to behave unusually.
4) Incorporate mobility enhancements like anti-grav units or specialized footwear to navigate the shifting, semi-solid ground effectively.

Navigational Aids:
1) Equip high-resolution mapping drones and scanning arrays to construct detailed surveys of this bizarre terrain from multiple perspectives.
2) Deployphased array communication beacons to maintain contact across vast distances in case space-time distortions disrupt normal signals.
3) Implement quantum entanglement way-finders or cosmic ray vector tracking to prevent getting hopelessly lost in the featureless expanses.

Stealth & Defense:
1) You may not be alone. Bring discrete cloaking and sensor masking to avoid detection by any potential unseen inhabitants until their intent is known.  
2) Non-lethal deterrents and immobilizers may be needed in case of unforeseeable threats from enigmatic life forms.
3) Maintain constant real-time uplinks to your origin realm for emergency dematerialization if situations deteriorate.

The greatest preparation, however, may be a psychological openness to experiencing the truly ineffable. Approach this realm with humility and wonder. It may rewrite what you think is possible.
