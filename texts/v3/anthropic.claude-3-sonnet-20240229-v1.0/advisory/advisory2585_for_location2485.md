When preparing to visit this high-altitude, rugged landscape, there are several important considerations for a multiverse traveler:

1. Acclimatization: The thin air at high altitudes can cause altitude sickness, headaches, and respiratory issues for those not accustomed to it. It's crucial to allow for a gradual acclimatization process, spending a few days at lower elevations before ascending further. Medications like Diamox can also help prevent altitude sickness.

2. Clothing and Gear: The environment is harsh and unforgiving, with extreme temperature variations and rocky terrain. Sturdy, waterproof hiking boots with good ankle support are essential, as are warm, moisture-wicking layers that can be easily adjusted. A sturdy backpack, water bottles or hydration packs, and rain/wind protection are also necessary.  

3. Navigation: With the rugged and ever-changing landscape, getting lost or disoriented is a significant risk. A reliable GPS device or compass, topographic maps, and navigation skills are vital. Bringing backup power sources for electronics is also wise.

4. Shelter: Although primitive dwellings exist, it's advisable to bring a durable tent or shelter suited for extreme conditions, as well as a sleeping bag and pad rated for low temperatures.

5. Food and Water: Packing enough high-calorie, non-perishable foods to sustain oneself is crucial, as resources may be scarce. Bringing a portable water filtration system or purification tablets is also necessary, as water sources may be limited or contaminated.

6. Safety Equipment: A well-stocked first-aid kit, fire-starting tools, signaling devices (like a whistle or mirror), and a reliable communication method (satellite phone or radio) could be life-saving in an emergency.

7. Respect for Local Inhabitants: Although the settlements appear primitive, it's essential to respect the local inhabitants, their customs, and their way of life. Learning about their culture and seeking permission before exploring their territory is advisable.

8. Adaptability and Resilience: Perhaps most importantly, a traveler must possess a strong sense of adaptability and resilience. This environment demands physical and mental fortitude, resourcefulness, and the ability to remain calm and focused in challenging situations.

By properly preparing with the right gear, provisions, and mindset, a multiverse traveler can safely explore and experience this uniquely harsh and mesmerizing landscape while minimizing risk and respecting the delicate balance of the environment and its inhabitants.
