To prepare for visiting this breathtaking hidden world within the ancient mountain range, a traveler would need to take several important steps:

Physical Preparation:
- Train for strenuous hiking and climbing over rugged, rocky terrain at high altitudes with reduced oxygen levels.
- Invest in proper mountaineering gear, including warm layered clothing, hiking boots with excellent traction, helmets, headlamps, ropes, harnesses and other climbing equipment.
- Pack ample supplies of high-calorie, non-perishable foods and way more water than you think you'll need to stay hydrated in the dry mountain air.
- Bring supplemental oxygen tanks or acclimatize slowly to avoid altitude sickness in the upper reaches.

Expedition Logistics:
- Assemble an experienced team including mountaineering guides familiar with the region, as well as scientists/researchers to document discoveries.
- Obtain any required permits or permissions from local authorities to access the remote mountain area.
- Charter transportation like helicopters or rugged off-road vehicles to reach the trailheads.
- Equip the team with durable communication devices, navigation/mapping tools, and emergency beacons.

Scientific Preparation: 
- Research the geology, climate patterns, and any known history/lore about the mountain range.
- Pack an array of specimen containers, sensors, and analysis equipment to study the strange lifeforms, minerals, and energy phenomena.
- Bring cameras, audio/video recorders, lights and other documentation tools to fully capture this alien environment.

Mental/Psychological:
- Steel yourself for the overwhelming, disorienting strangeness of this metamorphic, semi-organic/inorganic ecosystem.
- Remain endlessly patient, curious and open-minded about confronting the truly unknown.
- Develop protocols for making minimal impact, preserving the integrity of ancient ruins and natural formations.

An expedition to this monumental hidden world would require embracing a sense of adventure and being prepared for literally anything. By combining rigorous physical training, extensive scientific equipment, and a philosophical mindset of humble awe, a traveler could hopefully survive to comprehend and share the marvels contained within such a place.
