Visiting a place as otherworldly and hostile as the vast liquid ocean you describe would require extensive preparation and specialized equipment for any ordinary human multiverse traveler. Here are some suggestions on how to prepare:

Protective Suit: An airtight, highly durable environmental suit would be essential for surviving the dense, viscous liquid environment. It should provide ample mobility and dexterity while protecting against the extreme pressures and preventing the corrosive liquid from entering. Include integrated life support systems with ample air/oxygen supply.

Propulsion System: Movement through the thick liquid will be extremely difficult. A powerful propulsion unit, such as hydraulic thrusters or zero-buoyancy maneuvering thrusters used in underwater exploration, would be necessary to overcome the viscosity and enable effective navigation.

Illumination: With the murky darkness, bright illumination sources would be critical for visibility and exploring. Mounted lamps on the suit as well as handheld searchlights/flares could help navigate and inspect the structures and lifeforms safely from a distance.

Sampling/Analysis Gear: Bringing equipment to obtain samples (both liquid and biological) safely and analyze them on-site could provide invaluable insights into this strange ecosystem's nature. Portable labs, specimen containers, microscopes and other analytical tools would aid study.

Communication/Tracking: Maintaining contact and relative positioning will be extremely difficult in such an environment. Robust communication relays/beacons and tracking systems resistant to interference from the liquid would be essential for coordination and navigating back.

Defensive Measures: While the larger lifeforms seem preoccupied, any sign of aggression should be treated with extreme caution. Non-lethal deterrents like sonic disruptors, flash-bangs or limited concussive weapons may be prudent for defensive purposes.

Observational Mindset: Most crucially, an open yet objective mindset primed for astounding discoveries would serve a traveler well. Meticulous observation and documentation of even the strangest phenomena in this alien world could uncover profound revelations about life itself.

With proper equipment, a balanced mix of preparation and caution, and a keen spirit of exploration - an intrepid multiverse traveler could safely study and hopefully unlock some of the mesmerizing secrets held within this primordial liquid realm.
