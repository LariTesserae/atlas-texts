To prepare for visiting this arid, desert world devoid of water, a multiverse traveler should take the following precautions and equip themselves accordingly:

1. Hydration and Water Supply: Ensuring an adequate supply of water is crucial in a desert environment. Travelers should carry multiple insulated water containers or hydration packs, filled with enough water to sustain themselves for an extended period. Electrolyte-replenishing drinks or tablets should also be included to prevent dehydration and heat exhaustion.

2. Protective Clothing: Lightweight, breathable, and loose-fitting clothing that covers as much skin as possible is essential to shield against the intense sunlight and heat. Light-colored fabrics that reflect sunlight and offer UV protection are ideal. Wide-brimmed hats, neck gaiters, and sunglasses should also be worn to protect the head, face, and eyes.

3. Footwear: Sturdy, well-ventilated hiking boots or shoes with excellent traction are necessary for navigating the rugged terrain, which may include rocky outcroppings, loose sand, and uneven surfaces. Thick soles will protect against the scorching ground temperatures.

4. Shelter and Shade: A lightweight, portable shelter or a large umbrella can provide temporary shade and respite from the relentless sun. Additionally, travelers should plan their journeys to seek natural shade provided by rock formations or outcroppings during the hottest hours of the day.

5. Navigation and Mapping: In the vast expanse of the desert, it's easy to become disoriented. A reliable GPS device, compass, and detailed maps of the area are essential for navigation. Marking or tracking one's path is also advisable to avoid getting lost.

6. Emergency Signaling and Communication: Flares, signal mirrors, or satellite communication devices should be carried to signal for help in case of emergencies or breakdowns in remote areas.

7. First Aid and Survival Kit: A comprehensive first aid kit, including supplies for treating dehydration, heat exhaustion, and other desert-related ailments, should be carried. Additionally, a survival kit with fire-starting tools, emergency blankets, and basic tools could be invaluable in case of unexpected situations.

8. Environmental Adaptation: Travelers should acclimate themselves to the extreme heat and dry conditions by gradually increasing their exposure before embarking on longer journeys. Monitoring for signs of heat-related illnesses and taking frequent breaks in shaded areas is crucial.

9. Respect for Local Wildlife and Ecosystems: While exploring this unique desert environment, it's essential to respect the local flora and fauna by minimizing disturbances and leaving no trace. Observing and appreciating the resilience of life in such harsh conditions can be a humbling and rewarding experience.

By taking these precautions and equipping themselves appropriately, multiverse travelers can safely explore and appreciate the stark beauty and resilience of life in this arid, waterless desert world.
