Visiting such an extraordinary realm would indeed be a transformative experience. As an ordinary multiverse traveler preparing for this underwater paradise, several considerations come to mind:

1. Respiration: Since this is a submerged world, the ability to breathe underwater is paramount. Procuring advanced technology like rebreathers or gill implants would be crucial to sustain extended stays. Alternatively, seeking out local lifeforms that can symbiotically provide oxygen could be an option, though this may require careful integration into the ecosystem.

2. Protection: The extreme pressures and unique environmental conditions of this realm necessitate robust protective gear. A reinforced, yet flexible exosuit capable of withstanding immense depths would be ideal. It should also offer thermal regulation, as temperatures may fluctuate between pockets of warm and cool water.

3. Mobility: Navigating this densely layered tapestry of life demands specialized modes of transportation. Propulsion systems that minimize disturbance to the delicate ecosystems, such as bio-mimetic fins or jet streams, would be preferable. Alternatively, forming symbiotic relationships with local fauna capable of transporting you could be an immersive option.

4. Sustenance: Integrating into the local food web may be necessary for extended stays. Studying the intricate relationships between organisms could reveal edible sources or facilitate the formation of mutualistic symbioses that provide nourishment. Carrying a backup supply of familiar rations would also be wise.

5. Research Tools: To fully appreciate and study this extraordinary realm, a range of scientific instruments would be invaluable. Specialized scanners, probes, and sampling equipment designed for underwater use could unlock the secrets of this living tapestry. Recording devices to document the symphony of sounds and capture the vibrant visuals would also be essential.

6. Open Mind: Perhaps most importantly, visiting such an alien world requires an open and adaptable mindset. Preconceived notions of what constitutes life, individuality, and the boundaries between species may need to be discarded. Embracing the unfamiliar and being willing to immerse oneself in the rhythms and intricacies of this realm could unlock profound insights and revelations.

Preparation is key, but ultimately, this journey promises to be one of transformation and wonder, where the lines between the traveler and the traveled blur, and the true essence of life is laid bare in all its mesmerizing complexity.
