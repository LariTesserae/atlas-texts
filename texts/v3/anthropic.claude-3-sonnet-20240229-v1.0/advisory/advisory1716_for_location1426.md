Visiting this verdant, plant-dominated realm would be a truly immersive and otherworldly experience. Here are some tips for an ordinary human multiverse traveler to prepare for this lush, overgrown world:

1. Attire: Wear lightweight, breathable clothing that covers as much skin as possible to protect against scratches and scrapes from the dense vegetation. Sturdy boots or shoes with good traction are a must to navigate the ever-shifting terrain.

2. Hydration: The oppressive heat and humid air will cause rapid dehydration. Bring ample water reserves and consider carrying a water filtration system to replenish from collected plant condensation when needed.

3. Navigation: The tangled maze of vines, thickets, and organic structures will make navigating extremely challenging. Bring a lightweight machete or cutting tool to clear paths, and consider tying markers or leaving a trail to avoid getting hopelessly lost.

4. Shelter: With no traditional buildings or clearings, you'll need to get creative for shelter. Collapsible tents or hammocks could be strung between tree trunks or within the latticed vine walls. 

5. Food: With no apparent animal life, foraging for edible plants and fungi will be crucial. Bring reference materials to identify safe specimens, and consider packing rations that are shelf-stable and lightweight.

6. First Aid: The ever-shifting terrain and dense growth increase risks of cuts, scrapes, insect bites/stings, and injuries from falls. Pack a comprehensive first aid kit.

7. Environmental Awareness: Move slowly and deliberately to avoid disrupting fragile plant systems. Stay attuned to signs of growth/decay cycles and adapt plans accordingly. Note areas of new sprouting or dying vegetation.

8. Tools: In addition to cutting tools, bring basic implements like digging tools, rope, pulleys, and sturdy poles/stakes to aid in traversing and temporary camp construction within the living habitat.

9. Documentation: Record extensive notes, take samples (carefully), and document the workings of this extraordinary ecosystem. The knowledge could be invaluable.

10. Openness: Above all, approach this realm with a profound sense of wonder and respect for the unbridled power of nature. Prepare to lose your sense of familiarity and embrace the rhythms of a world where plants are the architects of existence.

Visiting this verdant expanse will require patience, survival skills, and a deep reverence for the intricate balance and resilience of its botanical life forces. It promises to be a transformative encounter with the raw creative power of an unfettered natural world.
