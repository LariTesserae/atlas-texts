To prepare for visiting this enigmatic realm caught between worlds, a multiverse traveler would need to cultivate an open mind and adaptability like never before. Here are some key considerations:

Mental Preparation:
- Let go of preconceived notions about the nature of reality. The laws of physics and logic that govern our home universe may not apply.
- Be prepared to encounter sights, sounds, and sensory input that defies conventional explanation. Maintain objectivity when the impossible becomes normalized.
- Develop emotional detachment. Letting go of assumptions about "how things should be" will be crucial to staying grounded.
- Practice meditation and mind-calming techniques to maintain inner equilibrium when surrounded by paradoxes.

Physical Preparation:
- Pack minimal gear and supplies. With reality in flux, it's uncertain what tools or provisions may be needed or effective.
- Wear breathable, durable clothing able to withstand extremes of temperature, friction, and the effects of bizarre environs.
- Bring portable sources of nourishment and hydration, but be prepared for conventional food/water to behave unpredictably.
- Have basic tools for navigation, first aid, and self-rescue, but don't depend too heavily on technology that may malfunction.

Environmental Acclimatization:
- Be prepared for bewildering transitions between primordial landscapes and proto-civilization artifacts with no discernible pattern.
- Adjust to odd fluctuations in light, shadow, sound, and atmospheric phenomena that may strain perception.
- Develop keen environmental awareness to detect unseen forces or energy currents that could be hostile or benign.
- Observe the anomalous behavior of physics and be ready to swiftly adapt movement accordingly (e.g. altered momentum, gravity).

Ethereal Precautions:
- Remain vigilant for signs of sentient or energetic presences you cannot perceive with normal senses alone.  
- Project an aura of calm, non-threatening intent to any beings potentially observing your presence.
- If possible, research protective rituals, charms or codes of conduct for engaging higher dimensional or transdimensional entities.
- Embrace uncertainty. Shed assumptions. Expand awareness. Maintain humble curiosity about the ineffable.

Ultimately, having an intensely open yet grounded state of being will be the greatest asset in this reality-straddling realm. Rely on intuition and fluid adaptability above all else when the very fundamentals of existence are in question.
