To prepare for visiting this incredible subterranean grotto, an ordinary multiverse traveler would need to take several precautions and equip themselves properly:

Clothing & Gear:
• Wear lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing that can get damp or muddy. This grotto seems to have a humid, wet environment.
• Sturdy, waterproof boots with good traction for navigating slippery terrain.  
• A backpack to carry supplies and any samples/specimens collected.
• Ample light sources - powerful headlamps, glow sticks, infrared vision goggles may help navigate the dim environment.
• Tools like sample jars, shovels, scissors/shears for collecting plant/soil/water specimens.
• Emergency beacon or communication device in case you get disoriented or lost.
• Enough food/water to sustain yourself for an extended underground exploration.

Safety:
• Bring breathing equipment like oxygen tanks/masks in case the air quality is poor.
• Hazmat suit or protective outerwear to avoid skin contact with unknown substances.
• First aid kit stocked with supplies to treat bites, stings, cuts from unfamiliar plant/animal life.
• Anti-venom if venomous creatures are present.
• Rent a guide or hire an escort familiar with this environment, if possible.

Documentation: 
• Cameras, video recorders, audio recorders to thoroughly document findings.
• Notebooks, guides for identifying specimens.
• Mapping tools to chart your path and map out the ecosystem.

Preservation:
• Plan to collect minimal samples only what is needed for study. Avoid disrupting the environment.
• Use sterile/sealed containers to prevent cross-contamination.
• Be vigilant about containment procedures on exit to prevent introducing outside organisms.

An open mind and philosophical preparedness are also key when exploring such an alien realm operating on unfamiliar principles. One should remain awed but scientific, seeking knowledge respectfully from a place likely far more ancient than humanity itself.
