To prepare for visiting this incredible hidden oasis settlement, a multiverse traveler should take the following steps:

Gear and Supplies:
- Pack light, breathable clothing suited for hot, arid conditions as well as layers for cooler canyon depths
- Sturdy hiking boots with good traction for navigating the rocky, uneven terrain
- Plenty of water and high-calorie snacks/provisions to sustain yourself
- Basic camping/overnight gear in case you need to stay within the canyon overnight
- Headlamps or lanterns for navigating the dim, winding passages 
- Tools like rope, carabiners etc. for potentially climbing/rappelling canyon walls
- A means of water purification in case you need to replenish from subterranean streams
- Protection from the sun - wide brimmed hat, sunscreen, etc.
- Navigation tools like compass, map, GPS (if it will work underground)

Research and Preparations:
- Study the geology and geography of the region to understand canyon formations
- Learn about desert survival skills like finding water sources, cooling techniques
- Familiarize yourself with the unique canyon microclimate and ecosystems 
- Research the cultural norms and etiquette of the settlement's inhabitants
- Hire a local guide familiar with the labyrinthine passages if possible
- Obtain any necessary permits or permission to enter the protected area
- Pack minimal gear that can get wet/muddy navigating underground streams

Mental Preparation:
- Cultivate patience - the rhythm of this place moves slowly compared to the outside
- Open your senses to subtle details - sounds, smells, textures of the environment  
- Embrace an adventurous mindset for physical challenges like climbing 
- Develop an appreciation for the ingenious, sustainable way of life
- Prepare to disconnect from outside worries/urgency and sync with the unhurried pace

With the right gear, knowledge, and frame of mind, you can fully immerse yourself in the wonder of this exotic subterranean oasis and its ancient, enduring culture carved into the desert stone.
