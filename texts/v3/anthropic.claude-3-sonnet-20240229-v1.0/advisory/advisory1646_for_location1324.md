To prepare for visiting this subterranean grotto, a multiverse traveler should take several precautions and equip themselves properly for the harsh yet remarkable environment. Here are some recommendations:

Clothing & Gear:
- Wear lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing to help combat the intense humidity. Loose-fitting fabrics are preferable.
- Tough hiking boots with excellent traction are a must for navigating the slippery, uneven terrain.
- Pack a reusable water bottle and consider a hydration pack to stay properly hydrated in the sweltering heat.
- Bring a headlamp or powerful flashlight to illuminate your path through the cavern's dim recesses.
- A waterproof dry bag is wise for protecting any electronics or valuables from condensation.
- Insect repellent could help deter biting/stinging insects drawn to the profuse plant life.

Safety Precautions: 
- Research any potential hazardous lifeforms and bring appropriate antivenom if highly venomous species are present.
- Wear durable gloves to avoid cuts or irritation from abrasive rocks and caustic plants/fungi.
- Consider a breathing mask or respirator to filter out potentially harmful spores.
- Pack a simple survival kit with basics like fire starters, water purification tablets, etc.
- Tell someone your travel plans and set a return checkpoint in case you get lost or injured.

Scientific Equipment (optional):
- Bring sample vials or bags to collect unusual plant/fungal specimens for study.  
- A camera and notebook could document fascinating flora, fauna, and ancient artifacts.
- Sensitive scientific equipment like spectrometers may reveal unique subterranean mineral/chemical compositions.

Mental Preparation:
- Expect an incredibly humid, almost oppressive atmosphere. Sweat will be constant.
- Appreciate the grotto's remarkable biodiversity but maintain caution around unfamiliar lifeforms.
- Marvel at the primitive structures and enigmatic carvings - evidence of a long-lost settlement.  
- Open your senses to the cacophony of sounds and scents of life teeming all around you.
- Soak in the grotto's atmosphere of profound stillness juxtaposed with constant, subtle motions.

By coming prepared with the right gear and mindset, you can safely immerse yourself in this verdant alien world sealed away for millennia - a masterpiece of nature's resilience flourishing in total isolation.
