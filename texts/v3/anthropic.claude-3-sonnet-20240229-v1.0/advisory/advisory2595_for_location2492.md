Here are some tips for an ordinary human traveler preparing to visit this dynamic, geologically active plateau landscape where primitive settlements coexist with lush vegetation and emerging urban development:

Gear and Supplies:
- Rugged hiking boots or shoes with good traction for navigating the rocky, uneven terrain
- Lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing to handle the humid climate
- Sun protection like hats, sunglasses and sunscreen
- Hydration pack or water bottles to stay hydrated in the humid environment
- Basic camping/outdoor gear like a small tent, sleeping bag, fire-starter, etc. in case you need to camp out
- Insect repellent to ward off biting insects drawn to moist areas
- Basic medical kit with supplies for minor cuts, blisters, etc.
- Backpack or day pack to carry your supplies
- Trekking poles or staff to aid balance on rough ground
- Headlamp or flashlight for exploring crevices or caves

Research and Preparation:
- Study maps and geological surveys of the area to understand its terrain
- Learn about the local flora and fauna you may encounter
- Research the indigenous people and their culture/customs to interact respectfully
- Take a wilderness survival course covering skills like finding water, building shelters, etc.
- Get any needed travel vaccinations or medications 
- Obtain proper permits if the area has restrictions

When Visiting:
- Hire a local guide knowledgeable about the landscape to lead you safely
- Stick to marked trails when possible to avoid getting lost
- Take precautions when approaching wildlife and keep food stored properly
- Don't drink untreated water to avoid contaminants or pathogens
- Camp only in designated areas and follow fire safety protocols
- Remove any trash or waste you create from the landscape
- Respect any boundaries around settlements unless invited by locals
- Be prepared for quickly changing weather conditions

By packing the right gear, doing your research, and following wilderness protocols, you can safely experience this unique environment where nature's wild power meets burgeoning human habitation.
