To prepare for visiting this fiery, primordial volcanic world, a multiverse traveler would need to take extensive precautions and equip themselves with specialized gear. Here are some recommendations:

Environmental Protection:
- A reinforced environmental suit capable of withstanding extreme heat, noxious gases, and particulate matter is an absolute necessity. The suit should have adequate cooling and air filtration systems.
- Additional radiation shielding may be required, depending on the composition of the atmosphere and volcanic emissions.
- Sturdy boots or treads designed for traversing rough, glassy terrain with heat resistance would be crucial for mobility.

Specialized Equipment:
- Robust communications equipment capable of penetrating the dense atmosphere and potential electromagnetic interference.
- Enhanced vision/imaging systems to aid navigation through the hazy, red-tinted environment.
- Seismic monitoring devices to detect and warn of impending volcanic activity or tremors.
- Portable energy/power sources, as conventional combustibles are likely unusable on this world.
- Sampling/analysis tools for studying the unique geology, mineral compositions, and potential lifeforms (if any).

Transportation:
- A durable, heat-shielded vehicle or rover would be highly advantageous for traversing larger distances across the treacherous obsidian plains.
- Aerial drones or probes may prove useful for scouting and mapping purposes.

Resource Management:
- Ample backup supplies of essentials like air, water, and power would be critical, as resources are likely scarce.
- Redundant safety systems and contingency plans in case of equipment failure or injury.

Psychological Preparedness:
- Training to operate under extreme physical and sensory conditions of heat, aridity, and strange vistas.
- Mental fortitude to handle the stresses of an alien, harsh environment where the slightest mistake could prove catastrophic.
- Openness to potential signs of nascent industrial/technological activity from unknown entities on this volatile world.

In essence, a multiverse traveler journeying to this volcanic crucible would need to be a virtual walking fortress - equipped to endure a hellish, primordial realm where the cosmos' raw creative forces still actively churn and shape an emerging world. Extreme caution, self-sufficiency, and scientific curiosity would be vital to surviving and studying such a merciless, newborn planetary environment.
