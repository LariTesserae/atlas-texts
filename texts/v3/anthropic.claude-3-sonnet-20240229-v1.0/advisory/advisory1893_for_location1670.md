Bring a compass, GPS, or any other devices that can help you track your location and mark your path. 9. Open Mind and Respect for the Environment: Above all, approach this surreal world with a sense of wonder, curiosity, and respect for its unique ecology. Observe and interact with the environment cautiously, minimizing any potential disturbances or harm to this living, ever-evolving realm.
