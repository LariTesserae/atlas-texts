Here are some tips for preparing to visit this primordial ocean world as a multiverse traveler:

Underwater Exploration:
- Obtain a sturdy, high-tech underwater vessel or submersible craft to withstand immense pressures and explore the depths safely.
- Have specialized gear for up-close study of hydrothermal vents and their extremophile ecology.
- Bring advanced sensors, cameras, and sampling equipment to analyze unusual lifeforms.
- Pack protective suits to guard against potential biosafety threats from alien microbes.
- Be prepared for very high ambient noise levels underwater from geologic/organismic activity.

Surface Operations:  
- Have a secure, mobile surface base station able to withstand high winds/waves.
- Utilize aircraft or specialized climbing gear to study the towering rock spires safely.
- Bring environmental suits to handle little oxygen and moisture-rich air.
- Pack Night vision/thermal optics for the low-light conditions on the surface.
- Have advanced spectrographic tools to analyze vivid microbial formations.

General Preparation:
- Study the physics, chemistry and proposed evolutionary mechanisms of this world.
- Learn about the arcane biochemistries that may fuel the lifeforms.
- Prepare for decompression issues when transitioning between extreme depth/surface.
- Bring ample backup power, air supplies, and redundant critical equipment.
- Have emergency plans for geological events like underwater earthquakes/eruptions.
- Assemble a team with expertise across fields like xenobiology, geology, oceanography.

This alien ocean realm will pose unique challenges requiring specialized equipment and broad scientific expertise to properly explore and document its wonders safely. An open mind and sense of adventure will also be critical!
