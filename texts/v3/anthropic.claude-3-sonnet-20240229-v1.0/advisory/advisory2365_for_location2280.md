To prepare for visiting this enigmatic location at the fringes of reality, a multiverse traveler would need an amalgamation of practical provisions and metaphysical fortitude. Here are some recommendations:

Physical Preparations:
- Carry ample water and caching supplies, as aridity and resource scarcity seem prevalent. Hydration packs and lightweight ration bars would be ideal.
- Wear layered clothing suited for extreme temperature fluctuations - both solar protective layers and insulated warm layers. Rugged hiking boots with excellent traction are a must on the precarious terrain.
- Pack a compact tent or bivouac shelter, bedroll, and all-weather sleep system to withstand the harsh conditions during photonic deprivation cycles.
- Bring navigation equipment like GPS, compass, satellite maps/atlas - but have backup ground-truthing methods as spatial anomalies may disrupt electronics.
- A comprehensive geological field kit could prove invaluable for sampling/analyzing the enigmatic lithology and mineral compositions.
- Climbing gear, ropes, anchors etc may be needed to scale sheer rock faces and negotiate unstable areas safely.
- Carry ample illumination sources - headlamps, glowsticks, UV lights etc to counteract the absolute darkness.
- A potent atmospheric filter system or oxygen supply may help cope with the rarefied air.

Mental/Psychological Preparations:
- Undergo mindfulness and psychosomatic training to maintain cognitive focus, emotional equilibrium and differentiate subjective mental stimuli from objective phenomena in this reality-warping environment.
- Study principles of logic, rationality, empiricism - and have mnemonics to re-ground your faculties in observable fact when perception wavers.
- Bring totemic items imbued with personal anchoring significance to provide psychic ballast and stabilize your sense of self-identity amidst the murkiness.
- Develop disciplines of lucid dreaming and oneironautics to retain agency if the boundary between dreams and awake states blurs.
- Learn advanced observational skills - microscopy, spectroscopy, remote sensing etc to scientifically document inexplicable phenomena.
- Study belief systems and cosmogonies that embrace ambiguity and non-duality to navigate cognitive dissonance.
- Be prepared to adapt radical openness - suspend assumptions, release outcome dependencies and surrender to the uncertainty principle.

Perhaps most importantly, cultivate profound humility. In an antinomian plane where the foundations of experiential reality grow soft, a traveled metaphysical hitchhiker can but witness events unfold with equanimous bemusement. Embrace the liminality - understanding may or may not arrive, but the journey is treasured all the same.
