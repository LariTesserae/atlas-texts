Visiting a place as unique and disorienting as this industrial plane will require some special preparations. Here are some recommendations for an ordinary multiverse traveler:

Physical Preparations:
• Wear rugged, protective clothing like coveralls, steel-toed boots, work gloves, etc. to safeguard against potential hazards amidst all the machinery and industrial debris.
• Bring a respirator or gas mask in case of poor air quality from factory emissions.
• Pack a tough, water-resistant bag to carry supplies while trekking across the harsh terrain. 
• Thermal underwear and outerwear may be needed as temperatures could fluctuate in this unstable environment.

Survival Gear:  
• Equip yourself with a sturdy multi-tool, light source, emergency blanket and fire-starters.
• Pack ample water and non-perishable calorie-dense foods given the aridity.  
• A well-stocked first aid kit is essential for any injuries.
• Bring sample/specimen containers to safely collect unique biological/mineral finds.
• Carry robust navigational tools like a compass, GPS and detailed maps if available.

Other Essentials:  
• Invest in high-grade reality stabilization equipment to counteract the bizarre physics phenomena. 
• Recording devices like cameras and audio recorders to document the strange sights.
• Acquire a universal translation device for any encountered sentient life forms.
• Pack lightweight, compact camping/shelter gear for extended exploration periods.
• Study any arcane data about manipulating or comprehending reality distortions.

Mental Preparedness:
• Steel your mind through meditative practices to maintain calm amidst the potential chaos.  
• Keep an open but discerning perspective about distortions of truth and reality.
• Be respectful yet vigilant when interacting with unknown adapted lifeforms.

A journey through this industrial plane certainly won't be for the faint-hearted. Meticulous preparation, unshakable perseverance and a keen sense of wonder will be required to explore and hopefully comprehend the enigmas underlying its existence.
