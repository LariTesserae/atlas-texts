Visiting such an extraordinary and otherworldly location would require extensive preparation and an open mind to fully appreciate its wonders. Here are some suggestions for an ordinary human multiverse traveler preparing to visit this vibrant oasis:

Physical Preparation:
- Obtain proper desert gear - lightweight, breathable clothing to protect from the sun's relentless heat, sturdy hiking boots, and a wide-brimmed hat.
- Pack ample water and high-energy snacks to sustain yourself in the harsh desert environment surrounding the oasis.
- Bring a sturdy backpack with essential survival gear like a water filtration system, fire-starting tools, and a navigation device.

Mental/Emotional Readiness:
- Cultivate an open, curious mindset ready to embrace the extraordinary. This place defies conventional logic, so let go of preconceptions.
- Study up on the region's folklore and legends that may hint at the oasis's origins and the beings that dwell within.
- Mentally prepare for sensory overload – the cacophony of sounds, vibrant colors, movement of strange lifeforms will be overwhelming at first.

Scientific Preparation:
- Bring an array of specimen containers and preservation materials to collect samples safely (plants, insects, soil, etc.).
- Pack specialized equipment like cameras/video recorders to document the vibrant flora/fauna and any undiscovered lifeforms.  
- If possible, consult xenobiologists about potential risks of exposure to the alien ecosystem and necessary precautions.

Cultural Sensitivity:
- Research what is known about any sentient species inhabiting the oasis and their potential cultures/customs to avoid offense. 
- Bring small, harmless gift offerings that could facilitate peaceful interaction, like intricately woven textiles or simple carvings.
- Be prepared to communicate without language barriers through gestures, art, music or even psionics if warranted.

With the right mindset, supplies and scientific curiosity, an ordinary traveler can hopefully navigate this verdant alien world safely and return with wondrous tales of its unnatural beauty. An open embrace of the unknown will be key in such an extraordinary place.
