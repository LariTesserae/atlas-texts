Visiting this otherworldly crystalline desert plateau would require extensive preparation and precautions for any human traveler. Here are some key considerations:

Environmental Protection:
• A sealed environmental suit capable of providing oxygen, temperature regulation, and protection from the harsh solar radiation would be absolutely vital. The thin atmosphere and intense sun exposure would be immediately life-threatening.
• The suit should have robust insulation and heating/cooling systems to withstand the extreme temperature swings from bitterly cold nights to blazing hot days.
• Enhanced vision modes like ruby filters or photochromic lenses would help cut through the blinding glare of the crimson sun on the glassy obsidian terrain.

Mobility:
• Reinforced boots with excellent traction would be needed to traverse the jagged shards without cutting feet/legs. An alternative could be a small anti-grav sled or robotic walker to glide over the terrain.
• For longer range travel, a sealed robotic vehicle or surface rover may be preferable to withstand the environmental extremes.

Sustenance:  
• A mobile recycling system to provide water and nutrients indefinitely would be critical, as there appears to be no native food or water sources viable for human consumption.
• Emergency rations and a backup power source in case of system failures.

Scientific Study:
• Sensor arrays to study the enigmatic crystal lifeforms' photonic biology and behaviors could yield insights into entirely new branches of xenobiology.
• Geological and material analysis tools to examine the obsidian shards, ancient metal structures, and eroded industrial remnants could shed light on the mysterious predecessor civilization.
• A virtual reality rig to digitally reconstruct the landscapes and potentially decode any remaining hieroglyphs or language artifacts.

With such inhospitable conditions for human life, an initial robotic or drone survey would be highly advisable before attempting a manned expedition across this hauntingly beautiful yet hazardous glassland.  Undertaking the journey should only be considered by seasoned xenoarchaeologists and extremophile researchers, even with the most advanced life support and survival tech. The risks are immense, but the potential scientific rewards could be incalculable in unlocking the secrets of this eerily preserved world.
