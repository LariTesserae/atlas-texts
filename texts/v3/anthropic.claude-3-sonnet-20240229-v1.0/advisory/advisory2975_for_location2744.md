To prepare for visiting this breathtaking sunken metropolis, a few key considerations come to mind for a multiverse traveler:

Protection from the Environment:
The viscous, syrupy waters engulfing the city require specialized breathing apparatus and protective gear. A self-contained underwater breathing system with ample air/oxygen supply is essential. The viscosity of the liquid means normal swimming motions may be difficult, so propulsion equipment like underwater propellers or thrusters would be very helpful for mobility.

Given the lack of sunlight, illuminate the way with powerful underwater lamps or headlamps. The eerie glow from the city structures provides some light, but focused beams will be needed to navigate properly.

Body protection in the form of an insulated dive suit is recommended to maintain temperature regulation and prevent any hazardous exposure to unknown elements in the liquid environment.

Navigation:
Study any available historical maps/urban plans of the pre-submerged city to orient yourself to the layout of streets, buildings, plazas etc. Having a sonar mapping system would be tremendously useful for charting paths through this liquid labyrinth.

Waterproof recording/documentation tools like cameras and audio/video equipment preserved in watertight housings could capture incredible images and details of this strange fusion of nature and human construction.

Emergency Provisions:
Pack backup air tanks, emergency flares or signal beacons, basic tools/repair kits, high-calorie ration packs, and a way to collect and filter the liquid for hydration in case of extended stay.

Mental Preparedness:
Be prepared for a surreal, quasi-dreamlike experience traversing this time-frozen, submerged world. Its haunting, ethereal ambiance of glowing spires and liquid streets is bound to have a disorienting effect. Maintain a focal point and remain grounded.

Overall, a spirit of wonder and respect for such an amazing marvel of architecture and nature merged would serve a traveler well in this most unique of settings. Proper gear, mapping, rations and mental focus will allow you to safely explore the sunken metropolis while appreciating its strange, distinctive beauty.
