To prepare for visiting this primordial, subtropical location, a multiverse traveler would need to take several key precautions:

Environmental Protection
- Wear lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing to combat the oppressive heat and humidity. Loose, breathable fabrics are a must.
- Utilize a wide-brimmed hat, neck protection, and plenty of sunscreen/sunblock to shield against the intense sun exposure.
- Carry an ample supply of water and electrolyte drinks to stay hydrated. The air will be extremely dehydrating.
- Consider protective outerwear like lightweight rain gear to guard against sudden downpours and condensation.

Footwear & Mobility
- Invest in high-quality hiking boots with excellent traction and ankle support for navigating the rugged, eroded terrain.
- Trekking poles may prove invaluable for maintaining balance on loose scree and aiding river/ravine crossings.
- Be prepared for sections requiring climbing gear like ropes, harnesses, etc. to scale cliff faces or explore cavern systems.

Shelter & Camping
- Pack a lightweight, waterproof tent and bedding to withstand the dampness and any nighttime rain or fog.
- A hammock or cot may be preferable to keeping off the moist ground while camping.
- Fire-starting tools and ample fuel for heating/cooking in case of cool nights in higher elevations.

Safety & Survival
- Carry a full wilderness survival kit - water filters, fire starters, emergency blankets, first aid, etc.
- Navigation tools like GPS, compass, maps, and signal mirrors are critical in the maze-like landscape.
- Personal security may be a concern - have appropriate deterrents for aggressive mega-fauna.
- Comprehensive insect repellents and anti-venom treatments for protection against venomous arthropods, etc.

Scientific Study
- For research purposes: sample containers, microscopes, environment sensors, cellular sampling tools.
- Durable camera equipment like ruggedized video/photo drones to safely document hazardous areas.
- Robust data storage and power sources for long-term expeditions into the wilderness.

Overall, a flexible, comprehensive approach is key for surviving and thriving in this unforgiving, primal environment where life clings persistently in even the most extreme conditions. Being prepared for alternating periods of intense heat/dryness punctuated by drenching moisture is vital. With the right gear and mindset, the wonders of this alien, perpetual summer realm await discovery.
