Visiting such an otherworldly and alien pocket realm would require extensive preparation and precautions for any ordinary human multiverse traveler. Here are some key considerations:

1. Environmental Protection: The damp, mineral-rich atmosphere and lack of typical plant life suggest a potentially hazardous environment for human physiology. Specialized breathing apparatus or environmental suits may be necessary to protect against any toxic compounds or microscopic particulates in the air. Proper moisture and temperature regulation would also be crucial.

2. Biological Safeguards: The realm is teeming with bizarre fauna, some of which may harbor unknown pathogens or defensive mechanisms. Full-body protective gear, possibly including hazmat suits or quarantine decontamination protocols, should be employed to prevent any harmful biological contact or contamination.

3. Terrain Navigation: The rocky, crystalline terrain could be treacherous to navigate, with potential slippery surfaces and unstable mineral formations. Specialized boots or footwear with excellent traction would be essential, as well as tools for safe rock climbing or traversal over the irregular landscape.

4. Illumination and Orientation: With only dim, filtered light available, powerful and reliable illumination sources would be necessary for visibility and orientation within the realm. Mapping equipment, such as surveying tools or even drone technology, could aid in charting the unfamiliar terrain and structures.

5. Archaeological Protocols: The crumbling structures and etchings suggest the presence of an ancient, potentially intelligent civilization. Proper archaeological techniques and equipment should be employed to document and preserve any artifacts or ruins encountered, without inadvertently damaging or contaminating them.

6. Energy and Resource Management: Given the isolated nature of the pocket realm, all necessary resources (food, water, power sources) would need to be brought in and carefully rationed for the duration of the visit. Backup supplies and emergency provisions should also be accounted for.

7. Communication and Tracking: Establishing secure communication links and tracking beacons would be crucial, both for maintaining contact with any support teams outside the realm and for ensuring the safety and retrieval of exploration teams within.

8. Ethical Considerations: As with any first contact scenario, strict protocols must be observed to avoid causing undue disruption or harm to the delicate ecosystem and its inhabitants. Non-interference, scientific study, and environmental preservation should be the guiding principles.

Overall, visiting this mineral pocket realm would require a well-equipped, highly trained, and multidisciplinary team of scientists, engineers, and explorers. Extensive planning, cutting-edge technology, and a deep respect for the unknown would be paramount in ensuring a safe and responsible visit to this truly alien world.
