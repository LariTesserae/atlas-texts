To prepare for visiting this incredible liquid realm, a few key considerations come to mind:

Breathing Accommodations
Since the dense, gelatinous liquid allows for breathing as easily as on land, no specialized breathing apparatus would be required. However, it would be wise to have backup air supplies just in case there are any localized pockets or currents of different atmospheric composition.

Buoyancy Aids
The liquid's density seems to provide natural buoyancy and body support. Still, portable buoyancy controls like miniature life vests or stabilizing backpacks could allow for more precise depth/altitude adjustments and mobility assistance when needed.

Non-Disruptive Propulsion  
While swimming motions may work, quiet propulsion systems like underwater ion thrusters, bionics-inspired undulating fins, or even riding semi-domesticated local fauna could enable smooth, non-disruptive transit. Minimizing turbulence will be key to peacefully exploring this delicate engineered ecosystem.

Bioluminescence Calibration
The ambient bioluminescence suggests visibility won't be an issue, but filter visors, lamp settings, or camera adjustments may enhance perception of the pulsating light patterns, subtle hues, and your ability to appreciate the vibrant displays.

Environmental Hazard Safeguards
While the realm seems engineered for life's prosperity, having backup environmental shields, pathogen filters, etc. provides reassurance when encountering unfamiliar organisms or unpredictable phenomena.

Investigation & Analysis Resources  
Science probes, thorough scanners, sample containers, and documentation tools will be vital for carefully studying and recording observations about this new ecosystem's intricacies, from the structural materials to the alien lifeforms.

Open Perception & Patience
Above all, an attitude of reverent open-mindedness is required. This is an entirely new world, defying our conventional wisdom. Patient observation, holding back assumptions, and allowing the subtleties to reveal themselves will ultimately yield the deepest experiential connection.

With the right combination of precautions, tools, and an appreciative mindset, one can hopefully safely immerse themselves in the wonders of this engineered oceanic marvel while treading lightly as an awed traveler.
