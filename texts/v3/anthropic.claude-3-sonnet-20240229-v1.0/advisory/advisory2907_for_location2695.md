Visiting such an alien and ever-changing subterranean world would require extensive preparation and specialized equipment. Here are some recommendations for an ordinary human multiverse traveler:

1. Protective Gear: The humid, harsh environment calls for durable protective gear. You'll need a reinforced environmental suit capable of withstanding moisture, abrasions, and potential hazards from crumbling infrastructure or aggressive lifeforms. Include a built-in rebreather system and air filters to handle the thick, potentially noxious atmosphere.

2. Illumination: With limited natural light, you'll require powerful, long-lasting illumination sources. Head-mounted lamps with backup batteries are essential, and it may be wise to carry flares or chemiluminescent markers to mark your path through the maze-like tunnels.

3. Navigation and Mapping: This ever-shifting realm will be challenging to navigate. Invest in advanced mapping technology that can create real-time 3D models of your surroundings, tracking changes and allowing you to retrace your steps. Environmental sensors can also detect air quality, seismic activity, and other potential dangers.

4. Sustenance and Hydration: Pack ample supplies of nutrient-rich, compact rations and a means to purify any water sources you might encounter. The high humidity may also necessitate specialized hydration systems to prevent dehydration.

5. Geological and Biological Sampling Equipment: Bring tools to safely collect samples of the bizarre lifeforms, minerals, and any remnants of human industry you encounter. Portable analysis kits could prove invaluable in studying this alien ecosystem.

6. Communication and Tracking: Maintain contact with your team or support using ultra-low frequency transceivers capable of penetrating the dense rock. Personal locator beacons will aid in tracking and potential rescue efforts.

7. Mobility Enhancements: The rugged terrain may require specialized gear like magnetic boots or cables to navigate vertical surfaces, as well as compact, all-terrain vehicles for longer treks through the tunnels.

8. Psychological Preparation: The unsettling, primordial nature of this environment could tax one's mental fortitude. Meditation, virtual reality simulations, and rigorous training may help steel your resolve against disorientation, claustrophobia, or the unsettling sights and sounds.

Ultimately, a multidisciplinary team of experts in fields like geology, biology, engineering, and psychology would be invaluable assets in fully preparing for and exploring such an enigmatic subterranean realm. Flexibility, caution, and a spirit of discovery will be essential in unraveling the mysteries of this strange, living world beneath the surface.
