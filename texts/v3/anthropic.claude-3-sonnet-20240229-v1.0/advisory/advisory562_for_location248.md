Visiting such an alien and unforgiving environment would require extensive preparation and specialized equipment. Here are some recommendations for how a multiverse traveler should prepare:

Environmental Protection:
- A self-contained environmental suit capable of providing oxygen, pressure, and temperature regulation will be absolutely vital given the thin atmosphere and extreme conditions.
- The suit should offer maximum insulation and protection from the harsh, desiccating winds laden with abrasive particulates.
- Backup oxygen supplies, air recyclers, and self-sealing mechanisms for breaches would be critical safety features.

Sustenance:
- As there is no apparent source of water or food, all hydration and nutritional needs must be supplied from offworld sources and compact ration packs.
- Backup supply caches should be considered in case of an extended stay.

Mobility:
- Robust, all-terrain vehicles with exceptional traction and maneuverability would be needed to navigate the jagged terrain and deep chasms.
- Towing cables, pitons, and climbing gear may be necessary for accessing certain areas or precipitous slopes.
- Anti-grav packs or jetpacks could provide an aerial advantage for scouting and traversing canyons.

Scientific/Surveying Equipment:
- Durable sensor arrays to detect/analyze energies, minerals, geologic activity, atmospherics, etc.
- Mapping drones and ground-penetrating radar to chart the landscape, subsurface features, and any anomalies.
- Rugged sample collection tools to obtain rock/crystal specimens and soil for analysis.

General Provisions:
- A powerful, long-range communications suite in case of emergencies.  
- Robust shelter modules or prefab habitats for establishing a base camp.
- Backup power generators, tools, and repair kits in this harsh, abrasive environment.
- Software translators and AI assistants to interpret any information or technology remnants discovered.

From a psychological perspective, travelers must be prepared for an utterly alien world of profoundly stark, unwelcoming desolation. The pervasive silence and lack of any conventional lifeforms will be disorienting. Maintaining mental focus will be challenging in such an unsettling, stark environment that seems to defy conventional physics and geometry. Advanced training in xenogeology, geology, energy sciences, and surveying alien terrain will be invaluable.

Overall, a mission to this crystalline, tectonically volatile mountain realm would require a highly skilled team, specialized equipment, ample resources and provisions, and an unwavering psychological resilience to endure one of the most hostile environments imaginable. Adaptability and redundancy in all systems will be the key to not just surviving, but unlocking the enigmatic secrets this bizarre world may hold.
