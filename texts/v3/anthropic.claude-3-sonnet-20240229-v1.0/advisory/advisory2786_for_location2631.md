Visiting a newly formed crystalline world like this would be an incredible and surreal experience requiring extensive preparation. Here are some key considerations for an ordinary multiverse traveler:

Environmental Protection
- Specialized environmental suits would be absolutely critical to withstand the harsh conditions - extreme cold, thin atmosphere, and potentially toxic/corrosive elements. The suit would need superior insulation and temperature regulation as well as an independent air supply and filters.
- Anti-static shielding may be necessary to prevent interference from the world's ambient energy fields pulsing through the crystal structures.
- Stabilizing rig or brace system to maintain balance on the sheer crystalline surfaces which lack traditional traction.

Navigation
- Robust GPS/navigational mapping would be vital in this disorienting, ever-shifting landscape with few obvious reference points. Perhaps deploying localized mapping drones.
- Visual light filters/enhanced vision to penetrate the glare and refracted light from the crystal structures.
- Seismic/vibration sensors to detect movements and shifts in the crystal formations by reading their resonant tones.

Scientific Study  
- Extensive sensor arrays to study exotic physics, energy patterns, and analyze the natural "biocrystalline" technology.
- Spectrographic equipment to examine the auroras, crystalline lifeforms, and ambient energy fields.
- Sampling/containment units to collect crystal shards, vapors, potential microbes for detailed analysis.
- Possibly a portable stasis/sustaining environment to explore safely housing and studying lifeforms.

General Reinforcement
- Ropes, tethers, pitons to anchor and scale crystal edifices or cross potential chasms.
- Reinforced cases to protect sensitive instruments from impacts in the unpredictable terrain.  
- Reserve power sources and backup equipment in case of system failures from the intense environment.
- Emergency shelters or waypoints to seek temporary refuge if needed.

The most critical needs would be maintaining life support, navigation, traction, and scientific study capabilities in the enigmatic crystal realm's unforgiving conditions. With proper preparation, exploring this nascent crystal-infused world could yield profound revelations about the very nature of physics and life itself.
