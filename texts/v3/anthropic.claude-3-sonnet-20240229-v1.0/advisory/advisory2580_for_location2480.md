Visiting the Cave-City of Glistengut would be an otherworldly experience that requires careful preparation and an open mind. Here are some suggestions for how a multiverse traveler should get ready:

Physical Preparation:
- Pack warm, moisture-wicking clothing as the caverns are damp and chilly.
- Wear sturdy boots with good traction for navigating the uneven cave floors.
- Bring high-quality headlamps or lanterns, as the bioluminescence provides only dim lighting.
- Carry ample water and non-perishable foods, as supplies may be limited underground.
- Consider respiratory gear like a mask/respirator for the potentially earthy air.

Mental/Emotional Readiness:  
- Be prepared for the complete absence of natural light and day/night cycles.
- Expect the pervading sounds of dripping water, underground rivers, and industry to be ever-present.
- Embrace an open mindset towards the alien ecology and life forms found in this sunless realm.
- Have patience and cultural sensitivity when interacting with the cave-dwelling people.
- Don't be unsettled by the eerie blue-green glow or unusual reverberating voices.

Skills & Knowledge:
- Study up on subterranean geology, cave formations, and erosion patterns.
- Read about adapted life forms like bioluminescent fungi and troglobitic creatures.
- Learn something of the history and culture of the industrious Cave-Dweller civilization.
- Get familiar with basic mining/excavation techniques used to expand the underground city.
- A rudimentary grasp of their language could be extremely helpful for communication.

Gear & Supplies:
- Rope/harnesses may be needed for traversing bridges and drops.
- Museum specimens could help identify unusual life forms.
- Mineral/metal sampling tools for studying cave resources.
- Backup lighting, climbing gear, and other basic exploration equipment.
- Local coins/valuables to barter with residents for accommodation or goods.
- A durable waterproof journal to document this extraordinary experience.

With the proper preparation, an open mindset, and deep respect for the wonders found in Glistengut's primordial depths, any multiverse traveler can fully embrace this alien yet industrious underworld culture.
