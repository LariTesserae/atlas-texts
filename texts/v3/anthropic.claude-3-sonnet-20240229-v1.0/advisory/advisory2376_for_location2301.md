Visiting such a cavernous, subterranean realm would require careful preparation and the right mindset. Here are some recommendations for an ordinary human multiverse traveler:

Gear and Equipment:
- Durable, waterproof clothing and boots to withstand the damp environment. Quick-drying fabrics are ideal.
- Headlamps or powerful flashlights, along with backup batteries. The gloom will be pervasive.
- Supplies like water, energy-dense snacks, and basic medical kit for extended exploration.
- Ropes, carabiners, and other caving/climbing gear in case of need for vertical ascents/descents.
- Environmental monitoring equipment to track air quality, humidity, etc.  

Mental Preparation:
- Develop claustrophobia management techniques, as the enclosed spaces can induce anxiety.
- Cultivate patience and an openness to the unknown. This realm defies conventional logic.
- Study speleology (cave science) to understand cave formations and subterranean processes.
- Maintain a sense of wonder and healthy respect for the alien environment.

Physical Training: 
- Enhance cardiovascular endurance for extensive hiking/climbing in low-oxygen conditions.
- Build strength and flexibility to navigate tight squeezes and awkward positions.
- Practice cave navigation and survey skills to avoid getting disoriented.

Research and Documentation:
- Study any existing knowledge about this specific underground network's geology and hazards.
- Bring equipment to document findings through photographs, samples, survey maps, etc.
- Prepare to meticulously record observations on flora, fauna, mineral formations, etc.

Group Coordination (if not solo):
- Ensure team has diverse caving/scientific expertise to maximize discovery potential. 
- Establish clear communication protocols and fallback plans in case of emergencies.
- Designate a surface team to monitor subterranean expedition and provide backup if needed.

Perhaps most crucially, enter this realm with an open mind and profound sense of humility. Let go of conventional assumptions, for this is a domain governed by forces far older and more profound than human understanding. Be prepared to encounter mysteries that may shatter one's worldview, yet revel in the opportunity to glimpse the unseen majesty lingering beneath our feet.
