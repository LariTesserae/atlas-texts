For an ordinary human traveler preparing to visit this strange and unforgiving world, a number of precautions and preparations would be essential:

Physical Preparation:
- Acquire appropriate outdoor gear and clothing for extreme environments - durable, water-resistant, and insulated to protect against the cool, damp conditions and potential precipitation.
- Ensure you have proper footwear with excellent traction and ankle support for navigating the jagged, rocky terrain and steep inclines/declines.
- Undergo physical training to build endurance for traversing vast distances over rugged landscapes at high elevations with little infrastructure.
- Pack an ample supply of high-calorie, non-perishable foods and water purification methods, as resources may be scarce.

Gear and Supplies:
- Equip yourself with durable tents, sleeping bags, and other camping equipment rated for extreme conditions.
- Carry a comprehensive survival kit with fire-starting tools, emergency shelter, signaling devices, first aid supplies, navigation tools (maps, compass, GPS), etc.
- Consider equipment for vertical ascents/descents (ropes, harnesses, etc.) in case climbing is required over cliffs or into chasms.
- Pack appropriate tools for hiking through wilderness like trekking poles, ice axes, crampons depending on anticipated conditions.
- Bring durable communication devices and backup power sources, as conventional networks may not function reliably.

Research and Planning:
- Thoroughly study the geology, geography, climate patterns, and any potential hazards (landslides, flash floods, seismic activity) of the specific region you'll be visiting.
- Map out potential routes, water sources, areas of shelter, and contingency plans in case of emergencies or getting disoriented.
- Research the remnant structures and artifacts to understand what little is known about past human activity in the area.
- Develop contingency plans for encountering strange phenomena that may defy conventional physics/logic.

Psychological Preparation:
- Mentally steel yourself for the overwhelming scale, harshness, and weirdness of this primordial landscape unlike anything on Earth.
- Prepare for extended periods of isolation and sensory deprivation/sensory overload from the overwhelming sounds and sights.
- Maintain a mindset of adaptability, as circumstances can change rapidly in such an unpredictable, inhospitable environment.

Given the extreme nature of this world, visiting would be an immense challenge requiring peak mental and physical conditioning. An open mind and vigorous preparations would be critical for any traveler brave enough to venture into such strange, uncharted lands.
