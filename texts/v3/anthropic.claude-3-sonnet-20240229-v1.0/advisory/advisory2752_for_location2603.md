Visiting an abandoned underwater city like this would be an incredible but challenging experience for a multiverse traveler. Here are some tips on how to best prepare:

Underwater Travel: You'll need advanced diving gear or a submersible vehicle to safely reach and explore the depths where the city lies. Atmospheric diving suits or miniature personal submarines would allow you to move freely through the submerged environment.

Life Support: Plan for an extended stay by bringing sufficient air/gas supplies, water, and food provisions. The city's original life support systems may still be partially functional but cannot be relied upon after years of neglect.

Lighting: While some areas may still have operational lights, pack high-powered waterproof lamps or headlamps to illuminate your path through dimly lit or flooded sections of the city.

Navigation: Study schematics or maps of the city's layout if available. Having a clear understanding of its architecture will help avoid getting disoriented in the labyrinth of structures. Consider tying guideline markers.

Environmental Protection: The waters may contain leached industrial materials or contamination, so hazmat suits/sealed environmental suits are a necessity. Don't risk exposure.

Tool Kit: Bring assorted tools, cutting implements, and portable power sources in case you need to cut through debris or access sealed sections of the city's infrastructure.

Documentation: Record your experience thoroughly with cameras, sensors, and sampling equipment to study the marine lifeforms, geological effects, and cybernetic/technological artifacts.

Contingencies: Be prepared for emergencies like equipment failures, leaks, or structural collapses by having redundant backup systems, emergency air supply caches, and clearly mapped exit routes.

By taking the proper precautions and coming equipped with the right gear, you can safely explore this extraordinary merge of the natural and industrial worlds deep beneath the waves.
