To prepare for visiting this hidden rainforest world, here are some recommendations for the ordinary human multiverse traveler:

Gear and Supplies:
- Lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing to handle the humid conditions
- Sturdy, waterproof hiking boots with good traction for navigating the damp, uneven terrain
- A high-quality rain jacket and poncho to protect against sudden downpours
- A backpack with a built-in hydration reservoir to stay hydrated in the muggy environment
- Insect repellent to ward off any biting or stinging creatures
- A headlamp or flashlight for exploring dimly lit areas and caverns
- A compact emergency kit with basic first aid supplies, fire starter, multi-tool, etc.
- High-protein, non-perishable snacks and water purification tablets
- A waterproof journal and pen/pencil to document findings and experiences

Physical Preparation:
- Develop endurance through regular cardiovascular exercise to handle extended periods of hiking and exploration
- Build leg and core strength to navigate uneven surfaces and potential obstacles
- Practice balancing and maintaining sure footing on slippery, muddy terrain
- Acclimate to humid, tropical conditions through heat training if possible

Mental Preparation: 
- Study the local flora and fauna to identify potential hazards or points of interest
- Learn basic survival skills like fire-building, shelter construction, water procurement
- Develop an open, observant mindset to fully appreciate the unique sights and sounds
- Cultivate patience and attentiveness to notice subtle details in the environment
- Maintain a sense of wonder and humility when encountering unfamiliar life forms

By adequately equipping yourself and training your body and mind, you'll be ready to fully immerse yourself in this extraordinary subterranean rainforest realm. Open your senses to the primal rhythms pulsing through the dense vegetation, and embrace the opportunity to witness the harmonious coexistence of nature and the lingering remnants of human industrialization.
