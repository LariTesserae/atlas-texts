Visiting a submerged city realm would be an extraordinary and unique experience requiring some specialized preparation. Here are some suggestions for how an intrepid multiverse traveler should get ready:

Essential Gear:
- Professional diving equipment including tanks, regulators, buoyancy control device, depth gauge, etc. The water may be warm but you'll want full SCUBA certification and gear.
- Waterproof housing for any electronics like cameras to document the amazing sights.
- Dive lights/lamps to illuminate the buildings and structures below water.
- Guidebook or maps of the submerged city layout to help navigate the underwater streets/avoid getting lost.
- Tools like dive knives, line spools, underwater habitat modules in case you need shelter.
- Speargun or other defensive weaponry in case of aggressive larger marine creatures.

Physical/Mental Preparation:
- Extensive SCUBA and free diving training to build stamina and breath control for extended underwater exploration.
- Underwater navigation and orienteering skills to find your way around the submerged cityscape.
- Study of marine biology and underwater ecosystems to understand the flora/fauna you may encounter.
- Physical fitness training to withstand the effects of pressure, temperature changes, currents.
- Mental fortitude and calm to handle the solitude and eerie quiet of the deserted city streets.

Cultural/Historical Study:  
- Research the history and architecture of the city when it was above water to better contextualize the ruins.
- Learn about the people, culture, and events that led to its submergence under the waves.
- Study any remaining artifacts, records, or media from that past civilization.
- Reach out to any surviving offshoots of the city's population for oral histories.

With the right preparation, equipment, and knowledge, exploring this hauntingly beautiful submerged metropolis could be an unparalleled adventure into the echoes of Earth's past civilizations blended with the wonders of the natural marine world.
