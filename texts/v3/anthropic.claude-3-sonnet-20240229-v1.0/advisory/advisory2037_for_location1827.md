For an ordinary human multiverse traveler to prepare for visiting this wet, subterranean realm, several precautions and provisions would be highly advisable:

Gear & Clothing:
- Wear waterproof outerwear like a rubberized poncho or rain suit to stay dry in the perpetual drizzle and seepage.
- Knee-high waterproof boots with good traction on the slick surfaces.
- Bring backup dry clothing sealed in waterproof bags to change into if your outer layers get soaked.
- Wear a headlamp or carry a waterproof flashlight, as the tunnels are dimly lit.
- Pack a compass and mapping tools, as it's easy to get disoriented in the winding passages.

Supplies:
- Carry ample drinking water, high-calorie snacks, and water purification tablets in case potable water is scarce.
- Pack rope, grappling hooks, and basic climbing gear to navigate vertical shafts or drops.
- Bring plastic tarps or shelters in case you need tocamp in the tunnels overnight.
- Stock up on chemical hand/foot warmers, as the damp chill can cause hypothermia.
- Have a med kit with anti-fungal creams, bandages, and treatments for exposure-related illnesses.

Safety Measures:
- Travel in groups and tell others your route in case you get lost and need rescue.
- Watch your step carefully on the slippery, uneven floors and avoid deep pools.
- Be cautious around any unsupported overhangs or areas of potential rockfall.
- Stay away from any strange creatures you encounter and don't provoke them.
- Take every precaution against contaminating this ecosystem with foreign substances.

Mental Preparation:  
- Be ready for a disorienting environment with distorted sights/sounds that challenge your senses.
- Maintain an objective mindset and don't get caught up in the apparent "aliveness" of the tunnels.
- Limit your exposure time to prevent acclimating too much to the unusual conditions.

With the proper protective gear, supplies, awareness, and psychological resolve, one could carefully explore these primordial tunnels while mitigating excessive risk. However, an experienced guide would be highly recommended for any indepth expedition.
