Visiting such an extraordinary subterranean realm would require extensive preparation and precautions for any ordinary human multiverse traveler. Here are some key considerations:

1. Protective Gear: The humid, mineral-rich atmosphere and rocky terrain pose significant hazards. Sturdy, waterproof hiking boots with excellent traction are essential to navigate the treacherous, uneven ground. Protective clothing made of breathable, moisture-wicking fabrics should be worn to guard against abrasions and dampness. A helmet with a headlamp would also be necessary to illuminate the dim cavern and avoid potential falling debris.

2. Breathing Apparatus: The thick, stale air within the cavern could be low in oxygen and potentially contain harmful gases or spores from the bioluminescent fungi. A self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) or a high-quality respirator with appropriate filters should be carried to ensure a safe air supply.

3. Navigation and Communication: With the lack of natural light and the ever-shifting landscape, navigation within the cavern would be challenging. Advanced mapping and surveying equipment, such as high-precision GPS units, compasses, and laser rangefinders, could aid in charting a course and tracking one's location. Reliable communication devices, like two-way radios or satellite phones, would be crucial for maintaining contact with any support team outside the cavern.

4. Geological Monitoring: The constant geological upheavals and rumbling sounds suggest a highly active and unpredictable environment. Seismic monitoring devices and early warning systems for detecting potential cave-ins, rockfalls, or shifts in the terrain would be invaluable for ensuring safety.

5. Provisions and Survival Gear: Due to the isolated and inhospitable nature of the cavern, travelers should be well-equipped with a substantial supply of water, non-perishable food, and emergency rations. A comprehensive survival kit, including a first-aid kit, fire-starting materials, shelter supplies, and signaling devices, should also be carried in case of unforeseen circumstances.

6. Specimen Collection and Documentation: For scientific purposes or to study the remnants of the ancient civilization, proper equipment for collecting and preserving geological, biological, and archaeological samples would be necessary. This could include specimen containers, preservation materials, and documentation tools like cameras, notebooks, and digital recording devices.

7. Ethical Considerations: Despite the allure of exploration, it would be crucial to respect the delicate ecosystem and avoid any unnecessary disturbance or damage to the cavern's features or its inhabitants. Minimizing one's impact and adhering to principles of responsible multiverse tourism should be a priority.

8. Professional Guidance: Considering the extreme conditions and potential hazards, it would be advisable to seek guidance from experienced cavers, geologists, or other experts familiar with subterranean environments. Their expertise could prove invaluable in planning and executing a safe and successful expedition into this remarkable cavern.

Proper preparation, specialized equipment, and a profound respect for the natural wonders of this ever-changing realm would be essential for any ordinary human multiverse traveler seeking to explore its depths responsibly.
