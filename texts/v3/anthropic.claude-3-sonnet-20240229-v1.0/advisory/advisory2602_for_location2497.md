Visiting a world of molten expanses and searing extremes like this would require extensive preparation and specialized equipment to withstand the incredible heat and hazardous conditions. Here are some key considerations for an ordinary human multiverse traveler preparing to journey to this fiery realm:

Environmental Protection
- Obtain a highly insulated, heat-resistant environmental suit capable of withstanding extreme temperatures potentially exceeding 1,000°C. It needs excellent thermal shielding and self-contained cooling/air supply.
- The suit should allow for mobility despite the intense heat and provide protection from potential lava/magma splashes or flows.
- Pack backup oxygen tanks and cooling units in case the primary systems fail.

Transportation  
- An all-terrain vehicle impervious to heat, with heavily insulated wheels/treads to navigate the semi-solidified magma plains would be essential for surface travel.
- Thermal shielding is a must to prevent the vehicle melting or components being damaged by radiant heat.
- For aerial travel, a specialized heat-shielded aircraft or shuttle may be needed to fly over the incandescent magma seas while withstanding extreme temperatures outside.

Shelter
- Bring a deployable mobile base/habitation module rated for hellish conditions to use while exploring the rocky islands. 
- It should have backup power, air/water recycling, thermal insulation and ideally some way to create a solid foundation on shifting terrain.
- Stockpile emergency rations and plan water sources carefully as conventional hydration may prove challenging.

Scientific/Survival Gear
- Robust sensors, sampling tools, and analytical equipment built for extreme heat, to study the environment and lifeforms safely.
- High-powered lenses/filters to observe areas visually without being blinded by the searing radiance.
- Durable tethers, carabiners and other climbing/rappelling gear to scale the rocky outcrops when needed.

While the challenges of this blazing world are immense, coming prepared with the right protective gear and mindset could allow careful study of its wonders - from the pockets of hardy life, to the raging magmatic forces constantly reshaping the primordial landscape. Flexibility and constant vigilance would be key to surviving this searing celestial hell for any brave enough to attempt it.
