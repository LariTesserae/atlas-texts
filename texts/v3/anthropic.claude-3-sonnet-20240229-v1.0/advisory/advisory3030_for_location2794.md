To prepare for visiting this surreal, arid landscape where time seems suspended, a multiverse traveler would need to take several key precautions:

Water Conservation: With water being an extremely scarce resource in this parched environment, travelers must be stringent about water conservation. Pack multiple high-capacity water reservoirs/hydration packs and purification tablets or devices. Any available water sources should be treated with utmost care.

Protective Clothing: The intense, shimmering heat and lack of shade necessitate donning full-coverage, moisture-wicking clothing to prevent sunburn, dehydration, and heat exhaustion. Tightly woven, breathable fabrics in light colors are ideal. A wide-brimmed hat, UV-protective eyewear, and a moisture-resistant outer layer are also advisable.

Respiratory Protection: The thick, stifling air may contain particulate matter or minerals that could irritate the lungs. Pack high-efficiency particulate air (HEPA) filtered respirators or dust masks for travel through areas with poor air quality.

Rugged Footwear: Traversing the cracked, crystalline terrain requires sturdy, ankle-supporting boots with excellent traction and puncture resistance. The soles should provide insulation against extreme surface temperatures.

Geological Sampling Tools: For studying the unusual rock formations and mineral deposits, pack a full geology kit - hammer, chisels, sample bags, etc. The landscape's raw geology suggests invaluable scientific opportunities.

Navigation Equipment: With the hazy, contorted horizon, maintain gear like high-powered binoculars, sextants, and multiple compass types to avoid disorientation or losing one's bearings in this maze-like alien realm.

Audio Dampeners: The described cacophony of groaning structures could quickly become overwhelming without proper ear protection like noise-canceling headphones or earplugs.

Sturdy Mobility Aids: Aluminum trekking poles or a collapsible aluminum litter could prove vital for negotiating unstable, shifting terrain and towering mineral structures. 

Finally, an open yet scientifically inquisitive mindset is key when exploring such an alien, contradictory environment seemingly born anew. Observational skills and documentation methods for one's findings will be paramount.
