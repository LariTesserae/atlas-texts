To prepare for visiting this extraordinary aquatic realm, a multiverse traveler would need to take several precautions and make specific arrangements. Here are some recommendations:

1. Underwater Breathing Apparatus: Since the entire environment is submerged underwater, a reliable and long-lasting underwater breathing system is essential. This could include high-capacity oxygen tanks, rebreathers, or even a specialized water-membrane that allows for direct extraction of oxygen from the water itself.

2. Buoyancy Control: The dense, constantly shifting water currents and the lack of a defined surface or bottom could make navigation challenging. A buoyancy control device (BCD) or similar equipment that allows for precise depth and positioning control would be necessary.

3. Protection from Currents and Pressure: The powerful water currents and the immense pressure at greater depths pose significant risks. A sturdy, flexible suit or exoskeleton that can withstand high pressures and resist the forces of the swirling currents would be advisable.

4. Navigation and Mapping: With the ever-changing landscape and the absence of traditional landmarks, navigating this realm could be disorienting. Advanced mapping and positioning technology, such as sonar or echolocation systems, would be invaluable for keeping track of one's location and plotting a course.

5. Sustenance and Hydration: While the water itself may be teeming with life, it's essential to carry a reliable source of nourishment and hydration suitable for extended stays in this aquatic environment. Specialized nutrient packs or self-contained food and water systems might be necessary.

6. Communication and Emergency Signaling: Given the potential for disorientation and the unpredictable nature of the currents, a reliable communication system and emergency signaling devices would be crucial for maintaining contact and ensuring safety.

7. Adaptability and Preparedness: Above all, a traveler visiting this realm should be prepared for the unexpected. The ability to adapt to the ever-changing conditions and to think quickly in potentially hazardous situations would be paramount. Carrying a diverse array of tools, spare parts, and contingency plans would be wise.

8. Respect for the Environment: This aquatic realm is a delicate and intricate ecosystem, teeming with life and energy. It would be essential for the traveler to approach it with respect and minimize any disturbances or harmful impacts on the surroundings.

Ultimately, venturing into this liquid world would require a combination of specialized equipment, adaptability, and a deep appreciation for the unique and ever-changing nature of this aquatic realm.
