Physical Preparedness:
- Obtain rugged, waterproof clothing and boots that can withstand the wet, corrosive environment. Layers are recommended to adjust for the likely humid conditions.

- Pack tools like machetes, rope, grappling hooks etc. to navigate obstacles and overgrown areas.
- Bring ample water/provisions as the realm may lack potable resources.

Mental/Psychological Preparation:
- Study available information on the anomalous nature of this realm, as reality itself may be unstable or shifting.
- Train yourself to maintain calm detachment, as jarring sensory distortions or existential stresses may occur.  
- Meditative practice could help center your mind against potentially disorienting phenomena.
- Resign yourself to the possibility that this realm's laws defy conventional logic or physics.

Equipment:
- Deploy advanced surveying and mapping tools to chart safe paths and document the metamorphic landscape.
- Equip high-powered illumination to navigate the dim, flooded interiors and penetrate pockets of existential distortion.  
- Consider compact environmental sensors to analyze air/water quality and radiation levels.
- For extended stays, construct a fortified encampment above the contaminated waters.

Overall Mindset:
- Approach the realm with a sense of humility - you are the outsider subject to its alien rules.
- Maintain constant vigilance, as threats could emerge from any shadow, surface, oriveal space.
- Embrace an exploratory curiosity, as this plane's secrets could reshape our understanding of reality itself.
- Display extreme caution, as the price of carelessness in this unstable domain may be your very existence.
