To prepare for a journey to this otherworldly, water-dominated realm, a multiverse traveler would need to take several precautions and equip themselves with specialized gear. Here's what I would recommend:

1. Hydrophobic Suit: Invest in a high-quality, waterproof suit that can withstand the constant moisture and humidity present in the environment. This suit should be breathable yet impermeable to water, allowing you to move freely without getting soaked. Look for suits made of advanced materials like Gore-Tex or other cutting-edge fabrics.

2. Respiratory System: The dense, moisture-laden air will make breathing difficult, so a portable oxygen supply or advanced filtration system is essential. Consider a self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) or a rebreather system that can extract oxygen from the humid atmosphere while filtering out excess moisture.

3. Amphibious Transportation: Given the abundance of water and the rugged terrain, a versatile mode of transportation that can navigate both land and water is crucial. Invest in an amphibious vehicle or consider hiring a local guide with expertise in navigating the treacherous landscape.

4. Advanced Navigation Systems: The landscape's unpredictable nature and shifting terrain may render traditional navigation methods unreliable. Equip yourself with cutting-edge navigation systems that can adapt to the realm's anomalies, such as quantum-based GPS or multidimensional mapping technologies.

5. Environmental Sensors: The air, water, and soil in this realm may harbor unknown toxins or substances that could be harmful to your health. Carry advanced sensors and detection equipment to monitor the environment and alert you to any potential hazards.

6. Protective Gear: The jagged rock formations and unpredictable terrain pose a significant risk of injury. Invest in durable, lightweight protective gear, such as reinforced exosuits or impact-resistant body armor, to prevent cuts, scrapes, and falls.

7. Energy Sources: With limited natural light and the potential for energy anomalies, reliable and portable energy sources are essential. Consider carrying high-capacity batteries, solar generators, or even miniature fusion reactors to power your equipment and ensure a consistent energy supply.

8. Survival Gear: Pack a comprehensive survival kit tailored to the unique conditions of this realm. Include water purification systems, dehydrated rations, emergency shelters, and any other essentials that may aid in your survival should you become stranded or separated from your group.

9. Documentation and Research Equipment: As a multiverse traveler, you'll want to document your experiences and observations meticulously. Bring along advanced recording devices, scientific instruments, and research equipment to capture data and samples for further analysis upon your return.

10. Mental Preparation: Perhaps most importantly, mentally prepare yourself for the mind-bending strangeness and unpredictability of this realm. Cultivate a mindset of adaptability, resilience, and open-mindedness, as the laws of physics and reality itself may be distorted in ways you cannot fathom.

Remember, this realm is a place of extremes and paradoxes, where the boundaries between the natural and the extraordinary blur. Approach your journey with a sense of wonder and respect for the unknown, and be prepared to encounter phenomena that challenge your understanding of the universe itself.
