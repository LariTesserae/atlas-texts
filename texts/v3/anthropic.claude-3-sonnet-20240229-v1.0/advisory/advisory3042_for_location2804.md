Here are some tips for preparing to visit this high-altitude industrial metropolis formed from volcanic glass and metal:

Gear and Protection:
- Obtain a sealed environmental suit or respirator mask to filter out any noxious fumes or particulate matter in the air from the factories and machinery.
- Wear protective clothing like a fire-retardant coverall or thick leather apron/jacket in case of incidental burns from proximity to high heat.
- Have durable boots with good traction for navigating the angular, jagged metal landscapes.
- Carry a bright light source like a headlamp, as shadowed areas could be hazardously dim.
- Consider eye protection like goggles to shield against flying debris from the industrial activity.

Sustenance:
- As there is no natural vegetation, you'll need to bring a supply of shelf-stable, calorically-dense foods and a method to chemically treat water for drinking.
- Research if any local "food" sources from the native machine/sculpture lifeforms are viable to consume.

Technology:
- Rugged recording equipment like cameras to document this unique environment.
- Specialized tools and diagnostics to safely interface with the technological systems.
- Multi-spectrum energy sensors to monitor any unusual power sources.
- Translation modules to attempt communication with the strange entities.

Mental Preparation:
- Be prepared for constantly morphing surroundings and existential uncertainty about the nature of reality.
- Maintain objectivity in the face of bizarre sights that may challenge one's perception of life and consciousness.
- Have contingencies for becoming temporarily disoriented or experiencing a threat from the industrial chaos.
- Stay grounded through awareness of your human self, but remain open-minded.

Aboveall, approach this enigmatic cityscape with patient awe, caution, and a sense of wonder. Maintain humble flexibility as you encounter new ineffable forms of existence. An open yet measured mindset will serve you well.
