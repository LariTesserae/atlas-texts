Here are some tips for an ordinary human multiverse traveler preparing to visit the Frost Manufactory:

Gear Up
- Wear rugged, sturdy clothing that can withstand industrial environments - coveralls, work boots, etc. Layering will help adjust to temperature variations.
- Bring eye protection like goggles or a face shield to guard against sparks, debris, bright lights.
- Use ear protection like earplugs or muffs to dampen the constant industrial noise.
- A respirator or filtered mask will help filter out particulates in the air.
- Pack a durable multi-tool with various attachments to handle repairs or emergencies.

Stay Safe
- Read up on industrial safety protocols and hazard signage before arrival.
- Always give large machinery, furnaces, and robots a wide berth - don't distract workers.
- Stick to designated walkways and don't wander into restricted areas unescorted.
- In case of alarms or warnings, immediately follow instructions from personnel.  
- Pack basic supplies like water, rations, med-kit in case you get temporarily stuck somewhere.

Plan Your Visit  
- Study schematics/maps beforehand to get an overview of the massive complex's layout.
- Decide which specific factories, production lines or operations you want to observe.
- See if guided tours are available, otherwise hire an experienced local guide.
- Make a list of any specialty tools, components or machinery you need to procure.
- Arrange appropriate clearances, check dress codes for certain secure facilities.

Adjust Your Mindset
- Be prepared for a harsh, artificially-lit environment with limited natural elements.  
- Expect periods of disorienting noise punctuated by jarring sounds and flashing lights.
- Have patience for the complex's self-sustaining systems handling essentials like air/water.
- Appreciate the scale of coordinated industrial effort required to keep this urban factory running.

By coming equipped, prioritizing safety, researching thoroughly and embracing the manufactory's distinct environment, you'll be able to experience the wonders and inner workings of this remarkable industrial megastructure.
