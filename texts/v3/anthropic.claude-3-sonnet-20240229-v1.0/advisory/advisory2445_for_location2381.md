Visiting the Hollow Bloom on the airless, mantle-level world would be an extraordinary experience requiring extensive preparation and precautions. Here are some recommendations for an ordinary human multiverse traveler:

Environmental Protection
- Obtain a highly durable environmental suit capable of withstanding extreme heat and low/no atmosphere. It should have independent air supply and cooling systems.
- The suit must protect against potential toxins/corrosives in the vapor-laden cavern air from the alien lifeforms.
- Consider additional shielding from potential radiation given the mantle depth.

Transportation
- Secure a robust, all-terrain vehicle capable of traversing the winding lava tube tunnels while carrying your equipment.
- The vehicle must be thoroughly insulated against heat and outfitted with lighting to navigate the depths.
- Backup power, extra fuel/batteries, and emergency rations should be stocked.

Research Preparations
- Study any existing survey data, mapping, and imagery of the Hollow Bloom underground network to plan an expedition route.
- Consult xenobiologists and astrochemists to learn what is known about the bizarre chemoorganic life of the Bloom Fields.
- Bring an array of sensors, samplers, and containment vessels to analyze and collect specimens safely.

Security Provisions  
- Equip expedition members with protective gear/weapons in case of encounters with hazardous lifeforms lurking unseen.
- Establish communication/emergency protocols and fallback routes/shelters in case of accidents or hostile events.
- Respect the possibility that ruins could harboring deadly traps if built by an extinct alien civilization.

Above all, an open yet cautious mindset is key when facing the unknowns of such an alien environment. Preserve and study the Hollow Bloom responsibly, as it could unlock insights into alternative biochemistries and life's evolution across the multiverse.
