To visit this verdant, primordial grove, a multiverse traveler would need to prepare themselves for full immersion in an alien ecology. Here are some recommendations:

Clothing & Gear:
• Wear lightweight, moisture-wicking clothes that can get soaked from the perpetual dampness. Quick-drying fabrics are a must.
• Have durable, waterproof boots with excellent traction for navigating the spongy, uneven terrain.
• Carry a compact machete or cutting tool to clear paths through the tangled undergrowth if needed.
• Pack a water filtration system, as the omnipresent moisture should provide ample drinking water if properly treated.
• Bring ropes, carabiners, and basic climbing gear to ascend into the canopy layer if desired.
• Have a durable, waterproof pack to store supplies and any samples collected.

Survival:
• Knowledge of foraging and identifying safe/poisonous fungi and flora could be invaluable if staying long-term. 
• Study up on primordial forest ecology to better understand the cycles and interactions occurring.
• Pack ample water purification tablets and nutrient supplies in case foraged food is inadequate.
• Bring mosquito netting and insect repellents to protect against biting symbiont fauna.
• Have basic first aid supplies for treating cuts, fungal infections, or other ailments.

Observation:
• Waterproof cameras and audio recorders to document the diversity of lifeforms.
• Sample vials to collect fungi, seeds, spores or other biological specimens.
• A compact telescope or binoculars for observing high canopy dwellers.
• Tools like magnifying lenses to examine tiny, winged biota up close.
• Notebooks and writing supplies to chronicle findings.

Mindset:
• Maintain a profound respect and sense of wonder for this verdant alien realm.
• Be prepared for the ceaseless noise and activity of the teeming lifeforms all around.
• Embrace getting soaked, muddy and overwhelmed by the sheer fecundity.
• Remain vigilant, as larger predatory flora/fauna may lurk unseen in the greenery.

Overall, a visit would require immersing oneself fully in an emerald world pulsing with primordial life unlike anything on Earth. With proper preparation and an open mind, you could become a temporary part of this vibrant, endlessly renewing ecosystem.
