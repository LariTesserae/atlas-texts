Visiting this hidden urban valley would be a truly immersive and enchanting experience. Here are some tips for preparing as an ordinary multiverse traveler:

Attire - Dress for cool, damp conditions. Waterproof boots or shoes with good traction are a must for navigating the marshy terrain and wooden walkways. Layers of breathable clothing that can get wet are ideal, along with a light waterproof outer shell. Bring extras to change into if needed.

Gear - Pack minimally but be prepared. A waterproof bag or dry sack is crucial for protecting any items you bring. A sturdy walking stick or pole could be helpful for maintaining balance on slick surfaces. Bring a refillable water bottle or canteen. A headlamp or lantern allows hands-free lighting at night.

Mindset - Embrace an adventurous yet respectful attitude. This valley has sustained itself in harmony for centuries. Observe peacefully, avoid disrupting the surroundings, and be prepared to get a little muddy! Leave modern human constructs behind and open yourself to reconnecting with natural rhythms.

Senses - Take the time to fully immerse yourself through sights, sounds and smells. Let your eyes adjust to the filtered light as you absorb the lush greens, browns and the gentle glow of the lamps. Tune your ears to the omnipresent whispers of flowing water. Breathe deeply of the earthy, mossy scents.

Exploration - While tempting to simply wander wherever your feet take you, consider hiring a local guide at first. They can provide context, history and ensure you don't unwittingly trespass. Take guided waterway tours by small boat. Once oriented, respectfully experience the town at your own pace.

Accommodation - Inquire about staying at a traditional valley inn, built in the stilt-and-boardwalk style over the water. Sleep to the lulling sounds of liquid surroundings. Or camp on a dry patch using water-resistant shelter.

Provisions - Sample the local cuisine, likely revolving around what can be responsibly foraged, hunted and cultivated in this ecosystem. Obtain food from valley vendors and markets.

Overall, embrace the chance to temporarily exist autodidactically as the valley has for ages - in sync with primeval cycles, intimately tied to the land and waters, loosening the tethers of the modern human world. Prepare to let your spirit be reinvigorated.
