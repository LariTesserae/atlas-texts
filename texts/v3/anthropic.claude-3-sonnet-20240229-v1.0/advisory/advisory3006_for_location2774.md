To prepare for visiting the scorched highlands and its peculiar volcanic glass settlement, here are some recommendations for a multiverse traveler:

Protection from Environment:
- Acquire gear suited for an arid, high altitude environment with intense solar radiation like UV-resistant clothing, goggles, broad hats, etc.
- Bring ample water and supplies to stay hydrated in the thin, dry air.
- Use portable oxygen systems if needed to acclimate to the low oxygen levels.
- Apply sun protection frequently as the sun's rays will be extremely intense.

Traversing the Terrain:
- Wear rugged boots or footwear with excellent traction to navigate the rough obsidian, pumice and basalt surfaces.
- Consider acquiring tools or equipment to scale the angular architecture if needed.
- Study maps/layouts beforehand as the alleys and streets may form a confusing, winding maze.

Caution with Lifeforms: 
- Exercise extreme care around the strange alien creatures, do not approach unless deemed safe.
- Research the behaviors, potential threats, and environmental needs of the local fauna beforehand.
- Bring protective gear like thick gloves/clothing in case of venomous bites/stings.
- Do not attempt to remove or interact with anything without understanding the consequences.

Immersing in the Culture:
- Learn key phrases, body language cues or other communication methods used by the inhabitants.
- Study their architecture, crafting methods for the volcanic structures, and purpose for design choices.  
- Have an open mind to experience realities that may defy conventional logic or physics.
- Arrange a local guide if possible to provide context and avoid accidentally offending any traditions.

Maintaining Health:
- Bring ample supplies of nourishment and stay well hydrated in the harsh climate.
- Pack any needed medications along with basic medical kit for injuries.
- Have contingencies for rapid evacuation in case of emergencies or adverse reactions.

By taking thorough precautions while still maintaining openness, a multiverse traveler can hopefully have an enlightening experience in this stark yet wondrous volcanic settlement.
