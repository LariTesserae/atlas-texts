To prepare for visiting the Singing Caves, a multiverse traveler should take several precautions and equip themselves properly for this extraordinary subterranean realm:

Physical Preparedness:
- Pack ample supplies of water and non-perishable foods, as sustenance may be scarce in the caverns.
- Bring sturdy hiking/climbing gear - reinforced boots, ropes, harnesses, etc. The terrain will be rugged and demanding.
- Have a powerful, long-lasting light source like headlamps or chemical glow sticks to illuminate your path.
- Wear protective clothing suited for a warm, dusty underground environment with potential hazards.
- Pack a compact shelter like a thermal bivy sack or small tent in case you need to make camp.

Navigation & Mapping:
- Obtain detailed subterranean mapping equipment like sextants, depth gauges, and compasses (if they function normally).
- Bring marking tools like chalk or paint to mark your path and avoid getting lost in the labyrinthine tunnels.
- Study existing maps/knowledge of the caves' layout beforehand, if available.
- Have backup navigation methods like trailing lines in case instruments fail.

Scientific/Exploratory Gear:
- Pack an array of surveying and analytical tools to study the strange geology, formations, and life forms.
- Environmental sensors, spectrometers, containment units for samples, etc.
- Recording devices like cameras, audio recorders, and journals to document discoveries.
- Ample data storage for potential artifact investigation and mapping of the cave network.

Psychosomatic Preparation:
- Train mentally to maintain calm focus in the disorienting, reality-warping environment.
- Study any accounts of previous cave explorers and their psychosomatic techniques.
- Bring tools to record psi impressions and extrasensory experiences, as reality may bleed across dimensions.
- Pack psychotropic aids if necessary and permitted, to induce mind states receptive to trans-dimensional harmonics.

Overall, a spirit of extreme caution, endurance, scientific discipline, and openness to unorthodox perceptual states will be crucial for surviving and understanding the enigmatic Singing Caves. Flexibility and an absence of preconceived notions about the laws of physics/nature will be an exploratory necessity. Proper protection, navigation, documentation ability, and mental preparation are paramount when venturing into such an alien, primordial wonder.
