To prepare for visiting this eerie, primordial underwater realm, a traveler would need to take some extraordinary precautions and equip themselves with specialized gear:

Submersible Vehicle
The immense depths and crushing pressures involved necessitate the use of a state-of-the-art deep submergence vehicle or submarine. This would need to be capable of descending at least 1,000-2,000 feet and withstanding the enormous hydraulic forces. Reinforced hull materials like titanium alloys would be required.

Life Support
An ample air supply or self-contained breathing system would be vital, as surfacing for new tanks would be impossible this far down. CO2 scrubbers, emergency oxygen reserves, and redundant backups are a must.

Lighting
The inky blackness would be absolute without artificial illumination. High-intensity LED or HID lamps, ideally mounted externally on the sub, would help pierce the perpetual gloom and navigate by the eerie glow of the bioluminescent flora.

Sonar/Imaging
With visibility potentially near zero, advanced sonar and ultrasonic imaging would aid in mapping the cityscape and detecting any movement from the behemoth denizens. Side-scan and forward-looking tools would be invaluable.

Heat Shielding  
The superheated geysers spewing from the hydrothermal vents could potentially damage an ill-prepared vessel. Refractory ceramic insulation and active cooling may be required to withstand prolonged exposure to such extreme temperatures.

Reinforced Construction
While the giant deep-sea creatures seem to largely ignore their surroundings, their awesome size and crushing bulk could easily obliterate a flimsily-made submersible on inadvertent contact. A rugged hull of resilient materials would be prudent.

Finely-Tuned Buoyancy
Micro-adjustments may be needed to maintain neutral buoyancy amidst potential currents, vent eruptions, and disturbances from the colossal inhabitants' movements through the water. Dynamic ballast and trim controls would facilitate precision maneuvering.

Scientific Equipment
A myriad of sensors, samplers, and specimen containers would allow studying this bizarre biosphere in greater detail. Biological, geological, and oceanographic data could yield invaluable insights.

While an expedition to this sunken leviathan-haunted city would be extraordinarily perilous, the chance to explore such an untouched primeval realm would prove an irresistible lure for many intrepid researchers and adventure-seekers...if they could muster the specialized resources and personal fortitude to confront its unspeakable alien wonders firsthand.
