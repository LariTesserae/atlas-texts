Visiting this extraordinary fungal forest realm would be an incredible experience unlike any other. Here are some tips for preparing as a multiverse traveler:

Protective Gear
- Bring an enclosed environmental suit to protect you from the thick, viscous atmosphere that substitutes for water in this biosphere. The suit should filter air and allow independent oxygen supply.
- Wear boots with excellent traction and ankle support to navigate the spongy, uneven fungal terrain underfoot.
- Consider incorporating a mild coolant system, as the dense air may feel stifling without water to regulate body temperature.

Sustenance  
- Pack dehydrated food rations and a personal supply of potable water, as nothing in this realm will be fit for human consumption.
- Bring chemical hand-warmers or similar gear to help reconstitute food and sterilize water, if cycles of heating and cooling are needed.

Equipment
- Secure a high-quality light source, such as a headlamp, to illuminate your path under the dim, filtered light.
- Pack sample collection tools like unbreakable vials and petri dishes to secure samples of fungi, soil, atmosphere remnants for study.
- Consider a tough but compact shovel to unearth potential archaeological finds amid the primitive structures.
- For documenting purposes, rugged cameras, audio recorders, tough notebooks and chalk markers are a must.

Movement & Exploration
- Use a safety line or marking equipment when straying from established paths to avoid getting disoriented in the maze-like fungal landscape.
- Proceed cautiously and move slowly, as any lifeforms present may be sensitive to vibrations in the thick atmosphere.
- Keep a respectful distance from any living creatures you encounter for observational study from afar.

With the right preparation and exploratory care, you will be equipped for a breathtaking expedition into one of the strangest biospheres in the infinite multiverse. Studying its otherworldly ecology and forgotten civilizations promises unbridled wonder.
