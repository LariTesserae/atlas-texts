To prepare for visiting this amazing subaquatic realm, a multiverse traveler would need to take several important steps:

1. Underwater Breathing Apparatus: Given the entirely submerged nature of this environment, you will need a reliable and long-lasting method of breathing underwater. This could be a high-tech rebreather system, a supply of compressed air tanks, or perhaps some kind of magical or technological means of extracting oxygen directly from the water itself.

2. Environmental Protection: The warm, viscous water and the unknown biochemical composition of this alien ocean mean you'll need full-body protection. A durable, flexible environmental suit that can withstand the pressures and temperatures of the depths while allowing full mobility would be ideal. It should also shield you from any potential toxins or corrosive elements.

3. Illumination: While parts of this realm seem to have bioluminescent flora and fauna, you'll likely want a reliable light source of your own. A powerful underwater lamp or headlamp would be essential for navigating the darker areas and observing the incredible lifeforms up close.

4. Propulsion System: With the dense water and potentially strong currents, you'll want a way to move efficiently through this environment. Fin-like propulsion devices, portable underwater jets, or even some form of miniature submersible vehicle could be invaluable.

5. Specimen Collection: To fully study and document the wonders of this place, you'll need a way to safely collect and preserve samples of the flora, fauna, and perhaps even the structures or artifacts. Specialized containers, preservative solutions, and even miniature stasis fields could be employed.

6. Navigational Aids: This vast, alien seascape could be easy to get lost in. Underwater mapping and positioning systems, perhaps combined with some form of breadcrumb trail, would help you find your way.

7. Documentation Equipment: Waterproof cameras, audio recorders, and data storage devices would allow you to comprehensively catalogue your experiences and findings in this incredible multiverse location.

8. Sustenance: Depending on the length of your stay, you may need to bring a supply of food and water (or an alternate method of sustaining yourself) suited for an underwater environment.

9. Emergency Supplies: Backup air sources, medical supplies, emergency beacons, and any other contingency equipment that could aid in an underwater rescue or escape.

10. An Open Mind: Perhaps most importantly, you'll need a sense of wonder, respect for the unknown, and a willingness to adapt to the alien customs and conditions of this mesmerizing subaquatic realm.

With careful preparation covering all contingencies, an intrepid multiverse traveler could safely explore and study this unique ocean world, unraveling its mysteries while preserving its delicate balance.
