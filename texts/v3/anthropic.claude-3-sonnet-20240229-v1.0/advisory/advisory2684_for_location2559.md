4. Lighting: While some light filters down from above, the depths can be quite dim. Waterproof lighting equipment, such as underwater flashlights or headlamps, will be essential for exploring the nooks and crannies of this realm and appreciating the vibrant colors and textures of the marine life.

8. Decompression planning: If diving to significant depths, proper decompression procedures and equipment (e.g., decompression tables, dive computers, or a decompression chamber) will be necessary to avoid the risks of decompression sickness.

9. Respect for the environment: As with any exploration, it is crucial to have a deep respect for the delicate ecosystem and avoid any disturbance or damage to the marine life, coral formations, or ancient structures.
