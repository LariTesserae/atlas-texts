7. Psychological Preparation: Given the unsettling and alien nature of this constructed world, a traveler should undergo rigorous psychological training and assessment. Techniques for maintaining composure, managing stress, and coping with the potentially disorienting or disturbing aspects of the environment would be essential.

8. Contingency Plans and Emergency Protocols: Finally, a comprehensive set of contingency plans and emergency protocols should be in place, covering scenarios such as equipment failure, injuries, environmental hazards, and even the possibility of becoming trapped or lost within the dense structures.
