Mental and Spiritual Readiness:

• Cultivate a sense of humility, awe, and sensitivity to the intricate balance holding this extraordinary ecosystem together.

Emotional Intelligence:
• Shed expectations and assumptions - release any rigid notions of what is "normal" or "natural."

• Let go of urgency; attune yourself to the unhurried rhythm of renewal in the valley's heart.
