To prepare for visiting this vast subterranean realm beneath a frozen world, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should take several important precautions and equip themselves with the necessary gear. Here's what I would recommend:

1. Thermal Insulation: The caves are described as cold and damp, so proper thermal insulation is crucial. A well-insulated, waterproof outer layer, such as a high-quality base layer, mid-layer, and outer shell designed for extreme cold conditions, is essential. Thick socks, insulated boots, and gloves are also necessary to protect against the chill.

2. Lighting: With limited natural light filtering in from the surface, a reliable and robust lighting system is a must. Headlamps with backup batteries, as well as handheld lamps or lanterns, should be carried. It's also advisable to bring backup lighting sources, such as glow sticks or chemical light sticks, in case of equipment failure.

3. Navigation: The labyrinth of caves and tunnels poses a significant risk of getting lost. A reliable compass, maps (if available), and a GPS device (if it can function underground) should be part of your gear. Marking your trail with biodegradable markers or leaving a trail of breadcrumbs (metaphorically speaking) can also help in finding your way back.

4. Climbing and Rappelling Gear: With towering columns, weeping rocks, and potentially treacherous terrain, having the right climbing and rappelling equipment could be essential. This might include ropes, carabiners, harnesses, and anchors, as well as the knowledge and training to use them safely.

5. Water and Food: Although underground rivers and pools are mentioned, it's advisable to carry a reliable water filtration system and purification tablets to ensure safe drinking water. Non-perishable, high-energy food items should also be packed to sustain you during your exploration.

6. First Aid and Survival Kit: A well-stocked first aid kit, along with basic survival gear like a fire starter, emergency blanket, and multi-tool, could be lifesavers in case of injury or an emergency.

7. Protective Gear: Depending on the potential hazards, you may need to consider protective gear such as helmets, knee and elbow pads, and eye protection to guard against falling debris or accidents.

8. Respect for the Environment: Since this realm is home to a primitive civilization and unique lifeforms, it's essential to tread lightly and respect the delicate ecosystem. Avoid disturbing or contaminating the environment, and follow principles of leave-no-trace exploration.

9. Communication and Backup Plan: If possible, establish a way to communicate with the outside world or a support team. Additionally, have a backup plan or emergency evacuation strategy in case of unforeseen circumstances.

10. Sense of Adventure: Last but not least, embrace a sense of adventure and wonder. This otherworldly realm promises to be a unique and awe-inspiring experience, so approach it with an open mind and a willingness to immerse yourself in its strange and wondrous beauty.

Remember, proper preparation, the right gear, and a healthy respect for the environment will increase your chances of having a safe and memorable journey through this extraordinary subterranean realm.
