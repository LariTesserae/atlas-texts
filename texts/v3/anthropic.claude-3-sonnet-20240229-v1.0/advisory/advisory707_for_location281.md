To prepare for visiting this lush, overgrown world, there are several important considerations:

Gear and Supplies:
- Durable, waterproof clothing and footwear is essential given the perpetual moisture. Lightweight rain jackets and rubber boots would be ideal.
- Machetes or heavy-duty shears to cut through the dense vegetation when needed.
- Ropes/harnesses may be useful for navigating particularly tangled areas.
- Water purification tablets or filters, as standing water could be contaminated.
- Basic camping supplies like tents, sleeping bags, firestarters in case you need to make camp.

Navigation:
- GPS units or satellite communicators to avoid getting disoriented in the maze of greenery.
- Mapping equipment like compasses, sextants to chart locations.
- Mark your path clearly using trail markers or biodegradable trail blazes.

Safety:
- Despite the lack of animal life mentioned, bring insect repellent and first aid supplies.
- Have backup oxygen tanks in case the air becomes too humid/moisture-saturated.
- Research any potentially hazardous plant life and fungi that could be poisonous.
- Pack emergency flares or signaling devices in case you need rescue.

Exploration:
- Bring sample collectors (vials, bags) to gather plant/fungi specimens for study.
- A camera and audio recorders to document the strange flora and industrial remnants.
- Gloves to handle artifacts without damaging them or getting cut.
- Small tools like trowels or archaeological brushes for careful excavation.

Mental Preparation:
- Remain vigilant, as the sense of being watched could lead to unease or paranoia.  
- Study any cultural/historical information about this overgrown place beforehand.
- Have contingencies for investigating while avoiding damaging the delicate ecosystem.
- Mentally brace for the eerie, alien beauty contrasted with industrial decay.

Entering this tangled, pulsing verde mundo will require planning, proper gear, and a respectful-yet-inquisitive mindset about its mysteries. Preparedness is key to explore safely while leaving minimal impacts.
