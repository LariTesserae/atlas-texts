This suit should be able to withstand the incredible forces while allowing for mobility and dexterity. Materials like reinforced polymers, ceramics, or advanced alloys may be required.

Consider high-intensity LED lights or even bioluminescent lures to attract and observe the local lifeforms. Navigation systems, such as sonar or advanced mapping technologies, will be essential to avoid getting lost in the vast liquid expanse.

Include emergency signaling devices, flares, or locator beacons in case of unforeseen circumstances. A compact but robust shelter or habitat may also be necessary for extended stays or emergencies.
