Visiting such a primordial and untamed realm would require extensive preparation and a deep respect for the power of nature. Here are some recommendations for an ordinary human multiverse traveler:

1. Physical Conditioning: The terrain will be challenging, with dense foliage, uneven ground, and potentially long distances to traverse. Endurance training, such as hiking, running, and strength exercises, will be essential to navigate the arduous landscape.

2. Survival Skills: Mastering basic survival skills, such as fire-making, shelter construction, water purification, and foraging, will be crucial. This environment is unforgiving, and relying on modern conveniences will be impossible.

3. Navigation: With the ever-changing landscape and limited visibility, traditional navigation methods may prove ineffective. Consider studying celestial navigation, using natural landmarks, and investing in a robust GPS system (if it functions in this realm).

4. Protective Gear: Sturdy, waterproof clothing and footwear are a must. Consider investing in lightweight, breathable fabrics that offer protection from insects, thorns, and moisture. A reliable rain poncho or shelter could also be invaluable.

5. Hydration and Nutrition: Proper hydration and calorie-dense, nutrient-rich food will be essential to maintain energy levels and combat potential illnesses. Consider packing a portable water filtration system and dehydrated, high-calorie rations.

6. First Aid and Medication: A comprehensive first aid kit, including treatments for potential injuries, insect bites, and tropical diseases, should be a top priority. Consult a wilderness medicine expert for specific recommendations.

7. Documentation and Research Tools: Waterproof notebooks, cameras, and recording devices could prove invaluable for documenting this unique ecosystem and its inhabitants. Consider bringing specialized equipment, such as underwater cameras or drones, if possible.

8. Respect for the Environment: Tread lightly and leave no trace. This realm is untouched by human influence, and preserving its delicate balance should be of utmost importance. Avoid disturbing or harming the flora and fauna, and minimize your impact as much as possible.

9. Mental Preparation: The sensory overload, isolation, and primal nature of this realm may take a psychological toll. Cultivate mental resilience through meditation, mindfulness practices, or seeking guidance from experienced wilderness explorers.

10. Teamwork and Communication: Exploring such an environment alone would be extremely hazardous. Consider assembling a team of experienced, like-minded individuals with complementary skills, and establish clear communication protocols.

Remember, this realm is a living, breathing entity, ever-changing and unpredictable. Maintaining a sense of wonder, respect, and humility in the face of nature's majesty will be essential for a safe and rewarding journey.
