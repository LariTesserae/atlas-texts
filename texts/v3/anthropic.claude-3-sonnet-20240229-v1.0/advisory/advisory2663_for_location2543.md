To prepare for visiting this vast mineral world, an ordinary human multiverse traveler would need to take several precautions and equip themselves with specialized gear. Here are some recommendations:

1. Environmental Protection Suit: The arid, dusty environment with potential toxic elements in the air and soil would necessitate wearing a sealed environmental suit with an independent air supply. The suit should be resistant to abrasion from the glassy terrain and provide insulation against extreme temperature fluctuations.

2. Mobility Aids: The rough, uneven terrain composed of volcanic glass and lava flows would make traversing on foot challenging. Sturdy boots with excellent traction or even a small robotic vehicle or hovercraft might be necessary to navigate the landscape safely.

3. Hydration and Sustenance: Despite the presence of moisture pockets, the overall aridity of the world means carrying a reliable supply of water and nutrient-dense, dehydrated rations is essential. A portable water filtration system could also prove invaluable.

4. Scientific Equipment: To study the unique geology, ecology, and atmospheric conditions, a traveler should bring a variety of sensors, scanners, and sample collection devices. Equipment like ground-penetrating radar, spectrometers, and drones could reveal insights about the planet's composition and lifeforms.

5. Personal Protection: Given the presence of large, winged predators and potentially hostile multi-limbed creatures, some form of non-lethal defensive equipment like a stun baton or sonic deterrent might be wise. Sturdy shelters or temporary force field generators could also provide safe havens.

6. Communication and Navigation: Reliable long-range communication devices and advanced navigation equipment would be crucial for exploring this vast, alien world safely. Mapping drones and orbital imaging could help chart the terrain and identify points of interest.

7. Geological and Temporal Monitoring: To study the slow, tectonic processes shaping the landscape, seismic sensors, time-lapse cameras, and other long-term monitoring equipment would be required to observe the gradual shifts and crystalline growth over extended periods.

8. Psychological Preparation: The stark, desolate environment and alien ecology could be psychologically daunting. Mental training, meditation techniques, and strategies for maintaining focus and calm in an unfamiliar setting would be beneficial.

Ultimately, visiting this mineral world would require a well-equipped, multidisciplinary team of scientists and explorers prepared to operate in an extreme, highly inhospitable environment while exercising caution and respect for the strange lifeforms they may encounter.
