Traveling to this primordial botanical realm would be an experience unlike any other. Here are some tips for an ordinary human multiverse traveler to prepare for visiting this verdant wonderland:

Gear Up for Hot, Humid Conditions
The dense vegetation and misty air suggest an incredibly humid, almost rainforest-like environment. Lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing is a must to stay comfortable. Bring plenty of water and electrolyte replenishment, as you'll likely be sweating profusely. A wide-brimmed hat and neck protection can help shield you from the moisture-laden atmosphere.

Wear Sturdy, Waterproof Footwear
The spongy, waterlogged ground composed of dense root systems and mosses means proper footwear is critical. Tall waterproof boots with excellent traction are recommended to navigate the squelching terrain and shallow pools. Consider gaiters or waterproof outerwear for your lower body as well.

Pack Breathing Protection 
With the sulfurous vapor vents and dense clouds of spores/pollen, breathing protection like a respirator mask is advisable, especially for those with respiratory sensitivities. Bring extras in case filters need changing.

Bring Plenty of Light Sources
The green-tinged gloom cast by the tangled canopy means artificial illumination will be necessary. Pack multiple light sources - headlamps, lanterns, chemlight sticks. Bring backup power sources for any electronic lights.  

Equip for Getting Lost
With the landscape's confusing, undulating terrain of plant masses and rocky outcrops, getting disoriented is likely. Bring navigation tools like GPS, compass, maps (if available). Also pack supplies to start fires for warmth/rescue signaling.

Protect Against Biting/Stinging Insects  
No doubt some of the "clouds of winged creatures" will be biting insects. Bring insect repellent, netting for head/face protection, long sleeves/pants, etc.

Come Prepared for Wildlife Encounters  
While the "unseen rustling things" are hopefully just harmless scavengers, one can never be too careful. Non-lethal deterrents like bear spray, noisemakers, etc may be wise just in case.

Bring Specimen Collection Gear
For scientific study/observation, tools like sample vials, plastic bags, notebooks, cameras and the like will let you document this alien ecology.

Most importantly, maintain a profound sense of respect and wonder. This is an ancient, almost primordial domain where life and geology exist in a perpetual, finely balanced cycle. Tread lightly, observe closely, and revel in the opportunity to experience one of the universe's most archaic botanical realms.
