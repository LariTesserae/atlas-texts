As an ordinary human multiverse traveler preparing to visit this extraordinary subterranean realm, there are several crucial considerations to keep in mind:

Environmental Protection:
The air in the cavern is described as thick, damp and carrying a metallic tang - indicating potential respiratory hazards. You'll need an advanced rebreather or oxygen supply system to ensure you can breathe safely. Protective environmental suits made of heat-resistant and chemically inert materials are also a must to shield you from the superheated vapors, extreme temperatures, and potential toxins.

Illumination:
While the bioluminescent fungi provide some ambient lighting, you'll want powerful portable illumination sources to fully navigate the cavern. Headlamps, chemical glow sticks and high-intensity lamps that don't generate excessive heat would be ideal. Backup lighting is critical in case your main sources fail.

Mobility & Traversal:
The shifting volcanic glass, crystalline structures, and fissures venting vapor make for treacherous footing. Mag-soled boots with exceptional traction and heat protection are necessary. Rappelling gear, pitons, and possibly a hoversled may be needed to scale obstacles and cross hazardous terrain safely. 

Geological & Environmental Sensors:  
This is an extremely dynamic environment, with tectonic and volcanic activity reshaping it continuously. Portable geologic sensors to monitor seismic vibrations, gaseous emissions, thermal patterns and shifts in the landscape itself could provide vital early warning.

Specimen Collection:
No visit would be complete without samples of the bioluminescent fungi, mineral compositions, and perhaps even tissue samples from the subterranean lifeforms (obtained ethically). Secure sample storage containers rated for extremes are a must.

Documentation Array:
Visual and audio recording from all angles is critical to document this singular place. Rig cameras, microphones, sensors and data logs onto your environmental suit. Deploy remote drones for aerial/wide-angle perspectives.

First Aid & Survival Gear:
Despite safeguards, the hazards here could prove injurious. Carry a portable med-unit stocked with burn treatments, antitoxins, oxygenation supplies and trauma stabilizers. Pack high-calorie rations and an emergency shelter in case you get trapped.

Above all, proceed with the utmost caution, restraint and respect when entering this primordial realm. It is a frontier for existential understanding - where the universe's deepest mechanisms of creation and life are laid bare, raw and unvarnished. Approach it with an enlightened mindset of both awe and scientific detachment.
