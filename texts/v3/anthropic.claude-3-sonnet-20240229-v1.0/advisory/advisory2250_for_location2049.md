6. Respiratory Protection: The moisture-laden air and potential dust or particulates from the dry environment may irritate the lungs. Pack a respirator mask or bandana to cover your nose and mouth.

10. First Aid and Survival Kit: Assemble a comprehensive first aid kit and include essential survival tools like a multi-tool, signal mirror, and navigation equipment in case of emergencies.
