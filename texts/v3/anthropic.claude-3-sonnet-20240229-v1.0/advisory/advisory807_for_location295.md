Visiting such an ancient, desolate realm would require extensive preparation, both physical and psychological. Here are some considerations for an ordinary multiverse traveler:

Physical Preparation:
• Attire - Wear expedition-grade cold weather gear to protect against the harsh, freezing conditions. Layered insulation, waterproof/windproof outerwear, and rugged boots with excellent traction are essential. 
• Gear - Pack a durable, lightweight tent and sleeping system rated for extreme cold. Bring climbing equipment like ropes, carabiners, etc. for navigating treacherous terrain.
• Supplies - Have a reliable supply of high-calorie, dehydrated rations and plentiful water. Pack emergency supplies like fire-starters, signaling equipment, first-aid kit, etc.
• Navigation - Carry robust navigation tools like GPS, compasses, detailed maps or lunar charts to find your way on the alien landscape.
• Protection - Bring appropriate protective equipment based on potential hazards (e.g. respirators if toxic atmosphere, radiation shielding, etc.)

Psychological Preparation:
• Study the realm's history and any intelligence gathered from previous explorers to familiarize yourself with the strange environment.
• Mentally prepare for the shocking alienness and disturbing aura of the enigmatic structures which defy conventional physics.
• Develop techniques to maintain calm and focus in the face of potentially psychologically unsettling phenomena like reality distortions. Meditation could be helpful.
• Accept that you may encounter entities, energy forms, or other incomprehensible forces beyond human experience or scientific understanding.
• Be prepared to encounter landscapes and vistas of desolation on an almost unimaginable scale of ancient desolation and silence.
• Have clear objectives for your visit, but remain open-minded about what you may encounter in such an alien realm.

Overall, exploring this primordial plateau would push the limits of an ordinary human's experience and endurance. Maintaining safety, health, and mental fortitude would be paramount when confronting the unknown. Flexibility and a sense of humble wonder at the cosmos' mysteries may be your greatest assets.
