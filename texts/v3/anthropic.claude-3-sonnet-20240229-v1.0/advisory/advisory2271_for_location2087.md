To prepare for visiting this otherworldly subterranean realm, a multiverse traveler should take the following steps:

Physical Preparation:
- Obtain proper caving gear - helmets with bright headlamps, durable coveralls/clothing to withstand dampness and crawling, gloves, sturdy boots with good traction for slippery surfaces.
- Pack supplies for an extended journey - water, non-perishable foods, basic camping equipment like sleeping bags/pads.
- Bring ropes, harnesses, and climbing gear for navigating vertical shafts or traversing drops.
- Equip for cold and wet conditions - thermal layers, waterproof outerwear, extra dry clothes.
- Carry ample light sources - powerful headlamps, lanterns, glow sticks, and backup batteries.

Mental/Psychological:  
- Study maps/charts of known cave systems to familiarize yourself with potential routes and hazards.
- Take caving/spelunking courses to learn critical skills like rock climbing, navigation, and self-rescue.  
- Mentally prepare for tight squeezes, total darkness, echoing thunderous noise, and confinement.
- Practice breathing exercises and meditation to remain calm in potentially stressful situations.
- Research the unique flora/fauna to understand what you may encounter.
- Read accounts of indigenous cultures that once dwelled in these caverns.

Supernatural Precautions:
- Consult arcane tomes and mystics for rituals/charms to ward off malign influences or entities.  
- Learn protective magics and ban counter-measures for optical illusions and reality distortions.
- Undertake vision quests or trances to attune yourself to the metaphysical nature of this realm.
- Bring talismanic objects imbued with power over the primordial elements at play.
- Recruit shamans or psychics attuned to such environments as supernatural guides.

With the proper equipment, training, mental fortitude, and metaphysical safeguards, one can hopefully navigate the wonders and perils of this surreal underworld safely. However, an open mind and deep reverence for its timeless mysteries is absolutely essential.
