To prepare for visiting this immense engineered industrial pocket universe, a multiverse traveler would need to take several precautions and make certain preparations:

Safety Equipment:
- Wear sturdy, heat/fire-resistant clothing like coveralls or a protective suit to guard against sparks, molten material splashes, etc.
- Utilize appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) like steel-toed boots, hard hats, safety glasses/goggles, respirators/filtered masks to handle airborne particles/vapors.
- Carry Emergency Position Indicating Radio Beacons (EPIRBs) or similar locator devices in case you get lost in the maze-like factory areas.

Environmental Preparations:
- Acclimate yourself to the warm, dry conditions and lack of natural water sources beforehand if coming from a different environment.
- Undergo any necessary inoculations/treatments to tolerate potential engineered atmospheres, coolants, etc.
- Bring a self-contained water supply and dimensions-appropriate nutrition as natural sources may be limited.

Documentation/Scanning:
- Procure detailed maps/schematics of the complex if available to aid navigation.
- Bring sensors, cameras and robust data storage to thoroughly document this industrial marvel.  
- Study available technical literature on the manufacturing processes, engineering principles employed, etc.

Prior to arrival, multiverse travelers should receive training on:
- Protocols for interfacing with automated systems, maintenance drones, etc. without disrupting operations.
- Industrial safety procedures and hazard response measures given the prevalence of heavy machinery.
- Proper handling of any synthetic biomass present like engineered lichens/mosses.

It would also be wise to travel with technical experts/advisors well-versed in fields like:
- Mechanical/production engineering to analyze the incredible industrial facilities.
- Synthetic biology/biotechnology to study the fabricated life forms.
- Materials science to examine unique alloys, compounds, etc. used in construction.

With prudent preparation covering safety, research, and having knowledgeable personnel, one can hopefully explore and document this distinct human-made pocket universe while mitigating risks from its hazardous yet fascinating industrial nature.
