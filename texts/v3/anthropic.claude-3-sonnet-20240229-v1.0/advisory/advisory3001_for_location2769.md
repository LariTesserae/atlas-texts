As an ordinary human multiverse traveler preparing to visit this primordial, plant-dominated realm, there are several key considerations to keep in mind:

Environmental Protection
This verdant wonderland has flourished undisturbed for untold ages, and its delicate balance must be preserved. Obtain breathable atmosphere capsules to avoid introducing foreign contaminants. Use only low-impact methods of traversal, avoiding damage to the landscape and microhabitats wherever possible. Don't disturb or remove any living specimens.  

Hydration/Climate Preparedness 
The air will be hot, humid, and dense with moisture. Bring ample water reserves and electrolyte replenishment. Wear light, moisture-wicking attire suited for tropical conditions. Consider environmental body temperature regulation, either passive cooling systems or portable climate-controlled enclosures.

Innovative Pathfinding  
With the terrain completely overgrown, traditional maps and trails will be useless. You'll need advanced orientation and mobility tech - guided probots, hoverpads, climbing gear, etc. Anything to navigate the tangled vines, roots, and branches without damaging the surroundings.

Biological Study
This ecosystem displays an incredible degree of biodiversity and speciation. Bring comprehensive xenobiological sampling and analysis equipment to study the organisms safely from a distance. Look for unique methods of cellular respiration, reproduction, symbiosis, etc.

Safety Protocols
Many resident lifeforms will be entirely unknown, possibly hostile. Have defensive screening, shielding, and retreat capabilities in case of territorial threats from the realm's apex megafauna. Biological and chemical counter-agents as well, in case of virulent or invasive microbes.

Research Goals
This realm offers untold scientific insights into arboreal megafauna, biosphere reclamation dynamics, terraforming cascades, and pure, unguided ecological evolution. Determine key hypotheses and objectives beforehand - perhaps sustainable botanical engineering or astropreservation initiatives.

Multi-Sensory Documentation   
Come prepared to fully capture the experience through high-res audiovisual recordings, transcription of ambient soundscapes, atmospheric analysis logs, and more. An immense sensory archive, to share this primordial wonder upon returning.

With the right defensive, analytical and non-invasive exploratory measures, this vibrant green realm can be safely witnessed in its full, timeless majesty. An expedition here promises radical new perspectives into nature's capacity for adaptation and regeneration when left to its own inexorable devices.
