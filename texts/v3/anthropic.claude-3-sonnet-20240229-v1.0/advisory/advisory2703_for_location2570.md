For a visit to this lush, alien botanical world, there are several key considerations a multiverse traveler should take into account:

Clothing & Protection
- Lightweight, moisture-wicking layers to deal with the damp, humid air
- Sturdy boots or footwear with good traction to navigate the moss/undergrowth  
- Possibly light armor or padding for protection against sharp branches/vegetation
- Face/eye protection like goggles or a breather mask to filter potential spores/pollen
- Gloves to avoid irritants, thorns, etc when touching plants
- Insect repellent and anti-itch creams in case of bites/stings from unseen fauna

Gear & Supplies
- Rope/grappling hooks to climb over large trunks or archways if needed
- Machete or cutting tool to clear paths through particularly dense foliage  
- Wayfinding tools (compass, GPS if it works) to avoid getting lost in the maze-like growth
- Portable shelter like a hammock or lightweight tent for overnight stays
- Ample water and preserved food rations, as foraging may be difficult  
- Powerful light sources for navigating darker, tangled areas
- Sample/specimen containers to collect plant/fungi samples if permitted
- Audio/visual recording devices to document the strange sights & sounds
- Portable lab equipment if planning to study the vegetation

Preparation
- Research any known information about the biochemistry and potential hazards
- Learn to identify dangerous or toxic plant life 
- Study the historical records of the civilization that once lived there, if available
- Get inoculated against any preventable botanical illnesses or blights  
- Take antimicrobial medications as the damp could breed fungal infections
- Pack secure, waterproof cases for sensitive equipment due to moisture

Mindset
- Maintain a sense of awe and respect for the primordial nature of the realm
- Stay vigilant, as unseen fauna could be observing from concealment 
- Appreciation for the inherent strangeness and not projecting human perspectives
- Patience and care when moving to avoid damaging the surroundings
- Openness to experience the world on its own alien terms and rhythm

This richly descriptive location is equal parts captivating and unsettling. While the lush scenery would be wondrous to explore, a traveler must tempter their sense of adventure with prudence and proper preparation to experience its beauty while mitigating potential risks from its unknown hazards. Maintaining a balanced outlook of curiosity and caution would allow one to immerse themselves in this verdant dreamscape.
