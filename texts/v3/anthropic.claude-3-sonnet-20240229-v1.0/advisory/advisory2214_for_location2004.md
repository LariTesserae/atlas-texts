To prepare for visiting this volatile, alien volcanic caldera, a few key considerations would be vital for any multiverse traveler:

Protection and Safety:
- Acquire a high-quality environmental suit capable of withstanding extreme heat, noxious gases, and acidic conditions. Look for suits made from advanced, heat-resistant materials.
- The suit should have an independent air supply and filtration system to prevent inhaling the sulfurous fumes.
- Wear sturdy, reinforced boots with excellent traction to navigate the trembling, rocky terrain safely.
- Carry emergency supplies like a flare gun, medkit, rations, and backup oxygen tanks in a reinforced pack on your back.

Mobility:
- Invest in a rugged, all-terrain vehicle able to handle the caldera's harsh environment. Something akin to a reinforced lunar rover would be ideal for traversing over unstable ground and fissures.
- The vehicle should have an air-conditioned cabin to provide respite from the blistering heat when needed.

Scientific Equipment:
- Bring an array of seismic monitoring devices, thermal imaging cameras, and spectrometers to study the caldera's volcanic activity up close.
- Pack robust sample collection gear like heat-resistant containers and tools for gathering mineral and biological specimens safely.
- Equip high-resolution cameras and recording equipment to document this extraordinary environment.

Shelter:
- Establish a secure base camp a safe distance from the erupting geysers and bubbling mud pits, but close enough for expedition efficiency.  
- Look for natural overhangs or leeward areas sheltering from toxic vapors and potential projectiles.
- Construct temporary shelters able to withstand potential seismic shocks and utilize emergency shielding if violent eruptions occur.

Study of Life:
- Consult exobiologists familiar with extremophile life that may inhabit this harsh volcaniccaldra. Their expertise will prove invaluable for safe observation and sample collection of bizarre lifeforms.
- Special containment units may be required to study and potentially transport living samples for further analysis without endangering them.

Investigating the Monolith:
- Assemble a team of archaeologists, linguists, and experts in ancient alien civilizations to unravel the mysteries behind the obsidian megalith. 
- Employ sophisticated subsurface scanners and ground-penetrating radar technologies to analyze the structure's composition and any subterranean architecture non-invasively.

By taking such extensive preparations, a multiverse traveler brave enough to venture into this raw, seething volcanic hellscape would not only survive but gain unprecedented opportunities to uncover the scientific and historical marvels lying in wait.
