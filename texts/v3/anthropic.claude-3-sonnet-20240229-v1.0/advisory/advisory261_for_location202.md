Here are some tips for preparing to visit this lush, otherworldly valley:

Pack for a Hot, Humid Environment
- Lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing to stay cool and dry
- Plenty of water/electrolyte drinks to avoid dehydration
- Sunscreen and a hat for sun protection
- Waterproof gear in case of rain showers

Proper Footwear
- Sturdy, waterproof hiking boots with good traction
- The ground will be very damp and spongy underfoot

Provisions
- Pack enough food/snacks for your stay 
- The valley seems isolated, don't count on finding provisions

Gear for Exploring
- Binoculars to observe wildlife up in the canopy
- Camera/journal to document strange flora/fauna
- Sampling tools to collect specimens 
- Machete/clippers to clear vegetation if needed
- Trowel for examining soil/ground composition
- Water test kits for analyzing pools/streams

Safety Precautions
- Water purification tablets/filter
- Basic first aid kit
- Emergency blanket/shelter
- GPS/communication device (though interference possible)
- Insect repellent

Come Prepared to...
- Move slowly and observe keenly
- Remain open-minded about unexplained phenomena
- Record anything out of the ordinary
- Respect this ecosystem, disturb it minimally

Stay Alert
- The rules of nature may not fully apply
- Unseen forces/energy fields could be in effect
- Potential crossover between realities/dimensions
- Strange sights, sounds could manifest 

Most Importantly
- Maintain a sense of wonder and scientific curiosity
- Approach the valley as you would an alien world
- Study it respectfully to learn its secrets

This otherworldly place holds many mysteries to uncover for the intrepid explorer willing to linger and let its strangeness unveil itself. An open yet cautious mindset is key.
