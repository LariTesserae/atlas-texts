To prepare for visiting this verdant oasis, an ordinary human multiverse traveler would need to take several key steps:

Attire:
- Wear lightweight, breathable clothing that wicks moisture away from the body as the humidity will be high within the oasis. Loose-fitting pants and shirts made of natural fibers like linen or bamboo are ideal.
- Sturdy, waterproof boots with good traction are essential for navigating the spongy, root-covered ground. Consider aqua socks or waterproof sandals as an alternative.
- A wide-brimmed hat will provide shade and protect from moisture dripping from the canopy above.

Equipment:
- A durable backpack or satchel to carry supplies. Look for waterproof/water-resistant materials.
- Hydration pack or refillable water bottles. The air may be humid, but staying hydrated is crucial.
- High-energy, non-perishable snacks and rations in case of getting turned around in the maze-like vegetation.
- A compact multi-tool or sturdy folding knife could be useful for clearing vines or cutting makeshift paths.
- Binoculars or a small scope for observing the unique flora and fauna up close.
- A tough but lightweight hammock or sleeping pad/bag in case overnight stays are necessary.
- Environmental protection like insect repellent, sunscreen (limited UV exposure but still wise), and a basic first aid kit.
- Specimen jars or sealable bags to potentially collect small samples, if permitted.
- Powerful portable light sources like headlamps or tactical flashlights to navigate low-light areas.

Preparation:
- Research any cultural norms, taboos or hazards related to the oasis if possible. Respect for the ecosystem is crucial.
- Pack light but have backup supplies readily available. The density and moisture could make for slow going.  
- Be physically fit - navigating the interwoven terrain and thick vegetation will be taxing over long periods.
- Have a plan for marking trails, taking bearings, or using GPS to avoid becoming disoriented.
- Stay vigilant for signs of the "subtle hum of technology" - obey any warnings or advisories.
- Keep an open mind and a sense of wonder - this is an incredible, one-of-a-kind place to explore.

With the right preparation mindset, one can fully immerse themselves in the lush environs of this unique, verdant nexus while staying safe, comfortable, and minimizing impact on the delicate ecosystem. A once-in-a-lifetime experience awaits.
