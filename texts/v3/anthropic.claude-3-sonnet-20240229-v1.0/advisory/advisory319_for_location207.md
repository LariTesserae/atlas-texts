Visiting this vast subterranean complex would be an adventurous and potentially hazardous undertaking requiring proper preparation. Here are some suggestions for how an ordinary human multiverse traveler should get ready:

Gear and Equipment:
- Sturdy hiking boots with good traction for navigating the slick, uneven terrain of metal grates and wet stone
- Moisture-wicking base layers to stay warm and dry in the chilly, damp cavern environment
- Headlamp or powerful flashlight to illuminate the dim surroundings and peering into shadowy areas
- Backup light sources like glow sticks or chemical light sticks
- Hardhat or climbing helmet to protect against falling debris or low ceilings
- Gloves to get a secure grip on metal surfaces and mineral deposits  
- Waterproof outerwear like a breathable rain jacket and waterproof pants
- Hydration pack or canteens for drinking water
- High-calorie snacks and ration bars to maintain energy
- Basic camping/survival gear like fire starters, space blankets, etc
- Rope/harness/carabiners for potential vertical descents or climbs
- Gas mask or respirator in case of noxious vapors
- Tools like pickaxe, hammer, piton set for breaking trail if needed

Planning and Preparation:
- Research the cavern's history, geology, environmental conditions to know what to expect
- Obtain detailed maps or schematics if possible to plan routes and avoid getting lost
- Bring spare parts and backup power for any exploration drones/robots used
- Pack a well-stocked first aid kit including supplies to treat burns, broken bones, etc
- Make sure communications equipment will function underground to call for rescue  
- Check credentials/permits needed and file travel plans with local authorities
- Monitor hazards like potential seismic activity, groundwater levels, toxic gas pockets
- Assemble and properly train a team if exploring with others rather than going solo
- Be prepared for self-rescue and evacuation without outside assistance if needed

Overall, a flexible, safety-conscious approach is key when venturing into such an unforgiving industrial cave system. Proper gear, ample supplies, backup plans, and respecting the hazards are crucial for surviving the expedition. With the right preparation, the rewards of exploring this alien underground world could be immense.
