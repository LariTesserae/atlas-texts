To prepare for visiting this vast industrial metropolis, here are some recommendations for a multiverse traveler:

Gear Up for the Environment
- Wear rugged, protective clothing that can withstand the humid, gritty conditions. Look for moisture-wicking and abrasion-resistant fabrics.
- Have a respirator mask or air filter to handle the potentially polluted atmosphere.
- Pack a durable multi-tool and any other urban exploration gear you may need to navigate tight spaces and maintenance tunnels.

Prepare for Unseen Biology
- Since the fauna is described as unlike anything in nature, research biosafety protocols in case of encounters with unknown organisms.
- Carry specimen containers and basic field research equipment to study creatures safely if possible.
- Consider prophylactic medical measures to guard against potential pathogens or biological hazards.

Scout the Urban Landscape 
- Obtain detailed maps of the city's layout, infrastructure systems, factories, and transportation networks if possible.
- Have robust navigation tools, as the sprawling complex could be easy to get lost in.
- Learn key information about the industrial operations, energy sources, and any potential hazards.

Blend Into the Local Population
- Study the culture, rules, and social norms to avoid drawing unwanted attention as an outsider.
- Have basic prerequisites like food, water, currency, and any permits that may be required.
- Devise backstories and credible reasons for your presence that wouldn't arouse suspicion.

No matter how well you prepare, be ready to adapt. An industrial world so different from our own could hold many surprises. Maintain vigilance, keep an open mind, and be prepared for anything in this enigmatic manufactured ecosystem.
