Here are some suggestions for preparing to visit this glacially-carved valley metropolis as a multiverse traveler:

Environmental Protection
- Obtain rugged, waterproof outerwear to withstand the constant misty rain and frigid temperatures.
- Bring respirators or air filters to deal with the smoky, vapor-filled air from the industrial machinery.
- Have proper traction gear like spiked boots to navigate the potentially slick surfaces from the ever-present flowing water.

Navigation
- Study maps/layouts of the tangled, organic city infrastructure blended with natural terrain beforehand.
- Acquire a waterproof GPS system and download any available guidance data for this location.
- Be prepared for disorienting reality distortions - the laws of physics may not fully apply.

Sustenance  
- Pack dehydrated/compressed food supplies to avoid full reliance on local sources in this extreme environment.
- Bring a portable water purification system to make use of the abundant flows if needed.
- Make sure to have durable containers that can withstand jostling from constant machinery noises/vibrations.

Scientific Study
- Bring robust scanning/recording equipment to study the intermixing of industrial and biological systems.
- Have containment units to safely collect samples of the strange, mutated flora/fauna.  
- Equip sensors to analyze potential reality distortions and breaches of natural laws.

Cultural Immersion
- Research available histories/contexts for how this symbiotic city-ecosystem came to exist.
- Pack translation tools and a willingness to engage with and learn from any inhabitants encountered.
- Remain open-minded toward new perspectives that challenge one's baseline reality.

Overall, a spirit of preparedness balanced with openness will be crucial for exploring and experiencing this outlandish, metaphysically-complex location. Embracing its paradoxes while maintaining safety could unlock deep insights.
