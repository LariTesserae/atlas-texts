Here are some tips for preparing to visit this strange underwater realm as a multiverse traveler:

Attire & Equipment:
• Obtain a specialized diving suit or underwater environmental suit that can withstand extreme pressures and temperatures. The suit should have self-contained breathing apparatus.
• Equip high-intensity lights to illuminate your surroundings in the abyssal blackness.
• Carry tools and equipment to collect research samples of strange lifeforms and ancient technology.
• Bring multiple backup power sources and oxygen tanks.
• Consider a small personal submarine or deep-sea exploration vessel for mobility and protection.

Training & Skills:
• Undertake intensive training in deep sea diving, equipment operation, and survival tactics.
• Study xenobiology to better understand the seemingly bio-luminescent lifeforms you may encounter.
• Learn about the civilization that built the remnant structures to decipher their function.  
• Gain skills in underwater navigation, as orientation may be extremely challenging.
• Train in communication techniques for potential interactions with lifeforms.

Mental Preparation:
• Steel yourself for extraordinarily alien sights, sounds, and sensory experiences unlike anything on Earth.
• Prepare to encounter unusual scales, from microscopic organisms to gargantuan creatures.
• Expect the total disruption of normal frames of spatial and temporal reference.
• Remain vigilant, as you may encounter environmental or biological hazards.
• Maintain strict emotional discipline to cope with profound displacement from the familiar.

By rigorously studying this realm and methodically equipping yourself, you can hopefully negotiate its unfathomable depths as a responsible explorer while protecting yourself from its untold dangers. An open yet disciplined mindset is key when interfacing with existential unnaturalness.
