Visiting such a primordial and ancient landscape would be an experience like no other, requiring preparation both physical and mental. Here are some suggestions for how an ordinary multiverse traveler should get ready:

Physical Preparation:
• Obtain durable, waterproof clothing and footwear suited for hiking over rugged, eroded terrain. The dampness and occasional rain showers necessitate proper rain gear.
• Pack a supply of nutritious, non-perishable foods and plenty of water, as resources may be scarce in this harsh environment.
• Bring any needed medications, first-aid supplies, and survival gear like fire-starters in case of emergencies.
• Invest in high-quality optics like binoculars to better observe wildlife from a safe distance.
• Consider wearing a respirator or carrying oxygen supplies, as the air quality is unknown.

Mental/Philosophical Preparation:  
• Study up on the geology, paleontology and anthropology of this strange world to enhance your understanding and appreciation.
• Embrace an exploratory mindset - you'll be one of the first outsiders to experience this primordial realm in ages.
• Develop patience and stillness. Move slowly and quietly to avoid stressing the ecosystem's fragile balance.
• Open yourself to the profound sense of deep time this place represents - it's a living museum of natural and industrial history.
• Reflect on the impermanence of all things, even "mighty civilizations", revealed by the crumbling factories.
• Prepare to confront the unsettling experience of trekking through a world that echoes a distant, uninhabited future.

Most importantly, visit with an unwavering reverence for this ancient, slowly reviving landscape. Tread lightly as an observer, humbled by the inexorable cycles of nature outlasting even our most stubborn attempts to master it. With proper precautions, an open mind, and respect for this hauntingly beautiful world, you can gain perspectives unpurchasable elsewhere in the multiverse.
