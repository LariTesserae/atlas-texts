In this rarefied, extreme realm where the boundaries of reality blur, strange phenomena seem to manifest from the very rocks and arid air itself. Here are some possibilities for what sorts of entities or forces may dwell in such an otherworldly place:

Geomancers
Ancient beings composed of living stone and mineral deposits, their bodies constantly reshaping and reforming from the eroded landscape itself. They move with glacial slowness, their thought processes inscrutable to modern minds as they tend to the deep mysteries and geologic forces of the earth.

Vapor Wraiths  
Spectral forms that coalesce from the swirling mists and condensation, their bodies in a constant state of flux. They drift silently through the canyons and over the stony arroyos, perhaps the disembodied consciousness of the realm itself made manifest. Their motives and nature are as inscrutable as the drifting fog they comprise.

Chronovent Rifts
Fractures in the coherent flow of time and space, glitches in the matrix of reality itself. Within these distortions, moments become untethered from their linear progression, eddying and backflowing. The laws of physics hold a more tentative sway, allowing paradoxes and improbabilities to blossom into transient existence before withering away.

Mirrormaze Revenants
The lingering psychic imprints of those who became unmoored from their own subjective realities in this plane of existential uncertainty. Trapped in infinite recursions of their own unanchored perceptions, their sense-impressions and obsessive thoughts have taken on semi-autonomous existences. They flit about as chimeric illusions comprised of light, shadow and disassociated consciousness.

Echomair Paradoxes  
Strange quasi-organisms that seems to be the given auditory form of the eroded windcries and settling groans of the strata. Unfurling in fractal, Escher-like patterns, their droning harmonic resonances induce shared subjective hallucinations in any minds present. Reality itself seems to resonate in accordance with their alien song-cycles.

These are just a few imaginings of what may haunt such a liminal realm where the rational and irrational blur. Inhabitants that embody the core paradoxes and ambiguities of the place itself - at once seemingly born of the tangible geological reality yet defying those same physical laws and certainties. Entities existing in the interstitial spaces between the known and unknown.
