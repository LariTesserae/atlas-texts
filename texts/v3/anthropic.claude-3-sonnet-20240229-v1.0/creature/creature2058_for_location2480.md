The Cave-Dwellers of Glistengut are a peculiar and hardy folk, their very beings shaped by the alien environment of the vast subterranean realm they call home. 

With sallow skin the color of old mushroom caps, their bodies betray the complete lack of sunlight that has filtered down to these primordial depths over the generations. Their bulbous yellow eyes, exquisitely evolved to make use of every spare photon from the pale bioluminescent fungi, seem to glow faintly from within. When they speak, their voices carry an otherworldly reverberating quality through the dense, vaporous air - the echoes of deep ancestral memories resounding through the caverns.

Yet for all their ethereal, almost fungoid appearance, the Cave-Dwellers are an industrious and resourceful people. Their bodies are sturdy and well-muscled from laboring in the mines and workshops that drive their underground economy. Thick callouses and rock-scars adorn their calloused hands - badges of honor from a lifetime of chipping, carving and smoothing the living limestone into their city's structures.

While lacking the benefit of draft animals on the surface, teams of Cave-Dwellers perform the backbreaking work of powering the most demanding machinery - from winches and siege engines to enormous tread-mills that turn underground rivers into a ceaseless motive force. The bravest of their folk wield masterful command over the strange cave-beasts that dwell in the outermost rifts and abyssal pits, bending the fearsome creatures' strengths to the yoke of industry.

For survival and progress in this hostile, resource-starved environment depends wholly on cooperation, planning and hard labor from every able soul. There is little room for excess or luxury in Glistengut. Every object from tools to living quarters must be wrought with painstaking effort from the rock itself. Even the most mundane household item bears the unmistakable mark of a master craftsperson, ornately decorated yet ruthlessly utilitarian.

Despite the constant damp air of their cavern-home, the Cave-Dwellers swaddle themselves in layers of woven bioluminescent fungus-fiber cloaks and masks to help stave off the chill while moving freely through their winding city. Their distinctive shuffling gait has evolved over ages to navigate the omnipresent streams, rocky outcroppings and shouldering crowds that fill Glistengut's meandering thoroughfares.      

From humble cave-tenders who carefully cultivate the life-giving phosphorescent crops to the wisest of the architects and geomancers who map out the living city's expansion, all members of this tenacious subterranean society play a vital role. For in this most unlikely of realms far beneath the natural world, the Cave-Dwellers have forged a rich, if utterly bizarre, living culture steeped in tradition yet buoyed by an indefatigable drive to not just inhabit, but subjugate the primordial lightless depths to their ingenuity and industry.
