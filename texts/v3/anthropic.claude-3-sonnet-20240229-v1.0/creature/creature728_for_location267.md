This subterranean alien realm is teeming with life that blurs the lines between vegetation and fauna. Here are some of the strangest lifeforms dwelling in this twilit wonderland:

Pulsing Vapor-Trees
Towering trunks of spongy, fibrous material slowly rhythmically inflate and deflate, circulating moisture and nutrients through an intricate network of capillary tubes. Bioluminescent mosses cast an eerie glow over their flanged bark. At the peak of each inhalation cycle, pressurized jets of warm vapor vent from pores along the upper branches in a fog-like exhalation.

Mycotropic Filter-Grazers  
Undulating schools of semi-translucent, tubular filter-feeders cruise through the moisture-laden air, their bell-shaped mouths agape. As they drift, masses of microscopic symbiotic fungi within their bodies break down the organic particulates they consume, allowing them to graze on the very mists. Glowing spore clusters trail in their wakes.

Capillary Borers
Resembling sinuous animated root systems, these pale fleshy organisms relentlessly tunnel through the living substratum. Their burrowing front ends bore into the intertwined vegetation, secreting digestive enzymes to break it down. As the tunnels collapse behind them, the borers absorb the nutrients through their skin. Mobile spore repositories follow, seeding new fungal growth in the worm-casts.

Trap Polyps
Clusters of rubbery maroon stalks topped with flared gullets festoon pieces of deadfall vegetation. The gullets stretch wide, revealing ring-like valves leading into nutrient sacs below. Passing micrograzers trigger slime trails around the rim, generating capillary pulses that suck them inside to be slowly digested.

Gnasher Lures  
Dangling tangles of bioluminescent filaments undulate hypnotically, mimicking the drifting grazers. Closer inspection reveals mouthparts studding each tendril, which can rapidly constrict to immobilize prey attracted by the mesmerizing display. The captured organism's nutrients are absorbed while it's still alive.

This merely hints at the abundant diversity. There are likely forms that photosynthesize using bioluminescent wavelengths, fungi that cultivate mobile vegetation platforms, armored ambush predators, and symbiotic colonies exhibiting hive intelligences. An entire subterranean ecology flourishing with almost incomprehensible strangeness.
