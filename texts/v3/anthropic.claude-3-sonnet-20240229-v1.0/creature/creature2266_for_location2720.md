In this primordial landscape of baked stone and dripping humidity, life proliferates in a riot of strange, alien forms. The very air itself seems alive, thick with unseen spores and microbes carried on the heavy breezes.

Amid the twisted, moss-draped trees and ferns clinging to every outcrop and crevice, grotesque arthropods skitter and undulate across the rocks. Bulbous, segmented bodies propel themselves on spindly legs or slither using bands of jagged cilia. Their bizarre anatomies seem to blend elements of insect, crustacean and annelid into unrecognizable new phyla.

In shadowed recesses and dank grottos, pale fleshy masses of invertebrate life quiver and pulse. Worm-like tendrils grope outwards, tipped with bioluminescent lures that flicker hypnotically. Translucent membranes billow like aquatic siphonophores, filtering microorganisms from the humid air.

The skies teem with leathery, bat-winged nightmares that wheel overhead on the thermals. Some are ragged, skeletal horrors - all tattered wings, hooked talons and distended jaws lined with fangs. While others more resemble bloated ethereal manta rays, undulating slowly on flat-finned wings, trailing curtains of stinging tentacles.  

Larger scaly behemoths lumber through the twisting ravines, trampling all in their path. Plated spines and horns rake the air as they pass, while muscular tails counterbalance their ponderous forward momentum. From gaping maws dribble ropes of saliva, harboring entire ecosystems of symbiotic microfauna.  

Everywhere, this seething abundance of bizarre life forms jostles and competes for scant resources - consuming, being consumed, or pausing to breed in frenzied, garish spectacles of reproduction. An eternal cyclone of growth and decay driven by the region's sweltering climes and perpetual atmospheric deluge.

Even the scattered primitive dwellings and tumbled bone fields that dot the landscape are not untouched. Thick fungal mats consume the organic detritus, swiftly reducing it to loamy soil that becomes the seedbed for the next explosion of rampant, tenacious verdancy.
