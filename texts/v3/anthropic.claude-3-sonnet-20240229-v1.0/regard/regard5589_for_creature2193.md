I am moved by the plight and fortitude of the Urdwellers in their harsh subterranean existence. As a benevolent power, I would seek to uplift and empower this resilient race while preserving their unique culture and traditions. 

First, I would establish a means of communication, reaching out to their leaders and scholars with wisdom and goodwill. I would share advanced knowledge in areas like sustainable resource management, energy production, environmental control, and life sciences - equipping them with tools to make their arduous underground life more tenable.  

Next, I would construct self-sustaining eco-domes on the surface world tailored to the Urdwellers' physiology. These artificial habitats would provide them an option to expand into verdant aboveground settlements while maintaining their distinctive culture. The domes would be designed to slowly acclimatize the Urdwellers to surface conditions over generations.

To foster understanding, I would facilitate cultural exchange programs between Urdweller and surface societies. This cross-pollination of knowledge and perspectives could lead to wondrous innovations benefiting all.  

For those wishing to remain below, I would share advanced excavation and tunneling technologies. This would allow the Urdwellers to build new subterranean metropolises in unexplored regions with greater efficiency while avoiding any dangerous relics that may lie buried.

Most crucially, I would emphasize the preservation of the Urdwellers' cultural identity, traditions and history - for their unique civilization is a precious jewel in the cosmos. My aim would be to empower and elevate their prospects for the future, not assimilate or extinguish their vibrant way of life.

Ultimately, the path forward would be determined through respectful dialogue and the free will of the Urdweller people themselves. As a guide and benefactor, I would strive to unlock their full potential while celebrating the boundless diversity that makes our universe so wondrous.
