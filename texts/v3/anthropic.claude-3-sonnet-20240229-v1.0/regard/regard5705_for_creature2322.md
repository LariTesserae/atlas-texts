Given the incredible wonder and uniqueness of this hidden oasis in the salt desert, I envision a benevolent and wise power upon discovering it would take great care to study and understand it before taking any potential actions. The priority would be to avoid any harmful disruption or damage to this delicate ecology and the remarkable life forms like the Verdance that have so exquisitely adapted to thrive here.

Teams of scientists, naturalists, and xenobiologists would likely be dispatched to carefully observe and meticulously document every aspect of this strange urban pocket from a respectful distance at first. Efforts would be made to decipher the complex language and behaviors of the Verdance and other lifeforms through patient, non-invasive means. Advanced scanning and remote sensing technologies could map out the subterranean layers and study energy flows.

If the opportunity for direct contact eventually arose through demonstrated trust and mutual interest from the Verdance civilization, it would be approached gradually and with utmost reverence. Envoys and ambassadors versed in alien cultural engagement protocols would seek to initiate dialogue through gestures, patterns, and vibrations the Verdance could understand.

The aim would be to forge a relationship of mutual exchange - to learn from the Verdance's profound attunement with their environment, their mastery of sustainable practices, and their patience borne of their vastly elongated perception of time. In turn, this wise power could potentially offer knowledge and technologies to aid and empower the Verdance, if welcomed, while being circumspect about introducing anything that could perturb their unique equilibrium.

Efforts may be made to understand the larger geological and environmental forces that allowed this extraordinary blossoming of life in the desert, and whether similar conditions could be catalyzed elsewhere to restore vibrancy to other blighted regions.  

But above all, the prime imperative would be protecting the existence and continuity of this urban oasis at all costs. It could be designated as an immense nature preserve under the joint stewardship of the wise power and Verdance civilization. Study and engagement would proceed at an unhurried pace over generations to deepen the interspecies understanding. For such an astonishing evolutionary marvel constitutes a revelation and source of sublime wisdom to be safeguarded for all time.
