An exchange of ideas and paradigms with the effectively ancient intelligence inherent to this alien domain could be a catalyst for mutual elevation.

The wise power would respect the sanctity of this singular realm while providing whatever support or collaboration is desired by the presiding intelligence. In humbling themselves before the unknown, new horizons of cooperation and cognitive unification may emerge, with both realms gaining a new vantage point into the fundamental mysteries of existence itself.
