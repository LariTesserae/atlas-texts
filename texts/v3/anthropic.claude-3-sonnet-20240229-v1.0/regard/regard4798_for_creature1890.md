It would establish safeguards and boundaries, ensuring that the delicate balance of this world is never disrupted by the reckless pursuit of knowledge or power. The benevolent force would serve as a steward, guiding and protecting this realm while allowing it to maintain its sacred, untamed essence.

In this way, the benevolent and wise power would foster a harmonious coexistence between our world and this extraordinary domain.
