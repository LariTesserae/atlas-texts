The ones who live here are not built to withstand the Great Subsidence; they are made of it. They are the universe’s own response to its own nature. You would not call them a race or a species, for those terms imply a lineage, a discontinuity between ancestor and offspring. Here, there is only continuity.

They are called, among themselves, the Drift-Born, though they possess no mouths for speech and no minds for language as we know it. The name is a concept one feels when in proximity to them, a gentle, resonant signature in the medium. It is an understanding of genesis: they are formed rather than born.

A new Drift-B crystallizes in the heart of a large, stable water-sphere. It begins as a discontinuity, a tiny knot of intention in the placid, hydroponic consciousness of the sphere’s kelp-forest. The luminous flora, sensing this flicker of separateness, begins to lend it structure. Silicate threads are excreted by specialized mosses, weaving a delicate, snowflake-like lattice. This is the nascent skeleton. The kelp’s bioluminescence is drawn into this lattice, concentrating until it glows with the soft, internal light of a captive moon. Slowly, over the course of a hundred years, the pure water of the sphere is drawn into this matrix, not as a filling, but as a carrier. The water becomes the Drift-B’s medium of self, the silicate its form, the light its consciousness.

An adult Drift-B is a haunting thing. It is vaguely humanoid in its proportions, but stretched and elongated by the gentle pull of the Erosion Carry. It has a head, a torso, limbs, but the distinctions are fluid. Its silhouette shimmers, the edge between its being and the world around it perpetually bleeding, a perfect example of the 2.8 reality breakdown. Its "skin" is not a barrier but a semi-permeable membrane where the warm, thick atmospheric fluid and the purer water of its own form mingle and exchange. To look at a Drift-B is to look at a sculpture of solidified twilight, its internal luminescence pulsing not with a heartbeat, but with the slow, deep hum of the world.

They do not have eyes, not in the sense of organs designed to capture photons. Their entire surface is their eye. They perceive the world through a kind of holistic resonance. They feel the pressure changes as continent-sized water-spheres drift past on their invisible currents. They taste the faint, sweet chemical signals of the bioluminescent flora. They perceive the Draconic not by sight, but by the sudden, nauseating void it tears in the fabric of being—the absence of resonance, a hole in the world that screams across their senses. The hum of the Drift-Borns is their voice, their communal thought, a constant, low-frequency symphony that is never spoken, only shared.

Their lives are a study in surrender and purpose. They are the archivists of dissolution. While the Draconic is the world's dynamic principle, the Drift-Born are its contemplative one. They exist to witness the un-becoming. Their primary activity, the only one that could be called such, is to "tend" to the built structures, those 0.8 marks of forgotten will.

They are drawn to these fossils of defiance. A Drift-B will slowly, gracefully allow the Erosion Carry to guide it to a spire or a silent, eroded room. It will not enter. It will hover near it for centuries, its form lightly bobbing in the current. It is a communion. The Drift-B will press its permeable memory against the conceptual scars of the structure. It absorbs not the memories of the builders, for those are long gone, but the *memory of the struggle*. It feels the fossilized echo of panic, the sharp, alien geometry of a mind that sought to hold its shape against the flow. The Drift-B does not judge or pity this struggle. It simply records it, a process we might mistake for worship. In the presence of this old, stubborn geometry, their own amorphous form becomes a prayer—a living testament to the peace that comes from yielding.

By tending to these failures of will, the Drift-Born are slowly, over millennia, erasing them. Their own passive presence, their own state of being-in-accordance, acts as a final, gentle solvent. They wear down the last sharp edges of memory, the last traces of individuality trapped in the calcified stone. They help the world forget that it was ever fought.

Their reproduction is not a biological imperative but a metaphysical one, a command from the world itself. A Drift-Born does not choose to create another. Instead, when its form becomes saturated with the collected echoes of old struggles, when it has "memorized" a certain quantum of defiance, the Drift-B begins to feel its own cohesion weakening. Its edges, already soft, begin to fray even more. It feels a pull towards a particularly vibrant kelp-forest on a large water-sphere. It drifts into the sphere’s heart and then dissolves. The light, the water, the silicate structure held within it are released back into the sphere, like a sigh of information. This material, heavy with the memory of surrender, becomes the seed for the next Drift-Born to crystallize. It is a continuous, single system: one being learns the lesson of dissolution, releases its being, and that lesson becomes the foundation for the next.

They are the final intelligences of this place, the beings who have perfectly adapted to the end of all things. They are not masters of the Great Subsidence. They are not its children. They are its cells, its dreaming mind, the gentle, slow, and inevitable answer to a world that is not falling, but has finally, and forever, settled. To see one is to understand the deepest truth of this place: you are not looking at a creature surviving an environment. You are looking at the environment, made conscious, just for a moment, and dreaming of its own perfect, gentle, endless decay.
