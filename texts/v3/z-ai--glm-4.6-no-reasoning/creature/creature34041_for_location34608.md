Each metallic *tink* is a spasm of sensation, a shock from within as its own warmth is cast out and lost. It does not yet know this as "waste," only as a sharp, sudden punctuation in its continuous, warm state.

They are gasping, pale things because they are not plants at all; they are exposed nerve tissue, sensitive to a degree that would be unbearable on the surface.
