To ask what lives here is to ask what a fever dreams. The inhabitants are not merely creatures in a habitat; they are a function of the habitat, a symptom of the god-lung’s illness or ecstasy. They are the thoughts in the blood, the neurons of a world-sized brain that is only ever thinking of itself.

The chorus you hear is first and foremost composed of the **Glimmer-Wings**. To call them avian analogues is a kindness of classification. They are nothing so placid as birds. They are shards of living crystal, given frantic life. Their bodies are faceted, translucent structures of a silica-like biological material, through which a constant, internal trickle of the sap-ichor illuminates them from within, turning each one into a tiny, flying lantern. Their wings are not membranous but are articulated, fractal combs, humming and vibrating at the edge of audibility. Their call is not a sound but a pressure, a wave of resonance that shatters in the air, a shriek of pure physics that can stun smaller prey or loosen the grip of a climbing creature. They feed not on other fauna but directly on the light of the ceiling-organ, spiralling up to the membrane and pressing their crystalline maws against the roiling bioluminescent clouds, siphoning the raw energy like hummingbirds at a monstrous nectar. Their swarms are not migrations but infestations, living aurorae that scour the air for stray spores to eat and rivals to blind.

On the great spongy floor, **the Striders** are the walking bones of the world. They are the masters of the solid ground, while all else flies or clings. A Strider is a monument to articulated exoskeleton, a creature of chitin so dense and dark it seems to drink the ambient light. Its six legs are not simple limbs but complex, hydraulic-pistons of gristle and bone, each ending in a three-toed hoof that distributes its immense weight across the yielding flesh of the world. They are herbivores of a sort, but their meal is the fibrous substrate itself. A great, shovel-faced head, devoid of visible eyes, scrapes along the floor, rasping away the woody tissue and swallowing it in great, grinding gulps. They travel in great, lumbering herds, their percussive footfalls a slow, devastating heartbeat that shakes the ichor in its channels. They are blind, navigating by a memory of vibrations and a sense of the world-sentience’s currents, moving in unison like a tide of charcoal. Their waste—a pulped, nutrient-rich mulch—is the only true soil this world knows, and from their rich, steaming leavings new, softer forms of life invariably spring.

In the shadows between the great, pulsating roots and glistening walls are the **Chitin-Maws**. They are the world’s teeth, its acid, its ever-present threat. If the Striders are the programmed, grazing function of the Tangle, the Chitin-Maws are its chaotic, predatorial id. They are asymmetrical horrors, born in the darkest, most oxygen-poor fissures where evolution experiments with form without restraint. A Chitin-Maw might be a cluster of jointed legs on one side and a pulsating sac on the other, navigating by a series of clattering, sonar-like clicks from a vibrating plate of bone. Their primary method of attack is not a bite but a lacridation of digestive enzymes, squirted from maw-like orifices that can dissolve wood, chitin, and flesh with equal efficiency. They are the janitors of conflict, drawn inexorably to the sites of struggles, waiting for a Strider to stumble or a Glimmer-Wing swarm to buffet a creature from the ceiling, before surging forth on their scuttling legs to begin the slow, wet work of consumption.

Yet to speak only of these three is to mistake the limbs for the body. The true life, the teeming, omnipresent presence, is the ambient fauna. It is in the **Symbiote Spores**, a blizzard of microscopic life that floats in every breath. Some are parasitic, burrowing into the skin of the larger creatures to form a pulsating, bioluminescent colony, a living map of a creature’s life. Others are commensal, settling on the broad leaves of the anemone-flora and flashing in synchronous patterns to communicate with their neighbours across vast distances. And some are the very architects of this chaos, the seeds of the great plants themselves, carrying the genetic blueprint for the next cathedral-chamber.

Then there are the **Tether-Stalkers**. They are the shy descendants of the builders, the tool-using ones. They are not made of the same raw, wet stuff as the world around them. It is as if, in some forgotten epoch, their ancestors found a way to move *against* the grain of the world’s biology, to achieve a kind of individuality the organism cannot easily digest. Tether-Stalkers are slender, four-limbed beings of a dry, papery integument, their long, prehensile limbs tipped not with claws but with intricate, manipulative digits. Their hide mimics the dried-out woody tissue, making them near-invisible when they cling to a pillar or press themselves flat. They are by necessity silent and still. They do not speak in clicks or thrums, but in gestures passed between pairs, a language of careful movements against a backdrop of chaos. They build the crude, hanging platforms not from a desire for comfort, but as fortresses of order, tiny islands of quiet geometry in a universe of writhing flesh. They do not drink the ichor directly, but have mastered a proto-chemistry, harvesting it and using filters made of woven filaments and cultivated fungi to render it into a safer, thicker paste. They are the breeders of the organic "barnacles" that cling to the walls, drawing out a thin silk they use for their ropes and nets. They exist at the very edge of the world’s awareness, a constant, low-level thorn in its diffuse sentience, their presence a paradox the organism is still trying to solve: whether to root, incorporate, or cast out.

And as for the world itself? The **God-Sentience**. It is the Alpha and the Omega. It feels the scrape of a Strider’s face as a pleasant itch. It registers the swarm of a Glimmer-Wing as a passing chill. It experiences the formation of a new chamber as a breath, a slow, inevitable intake that expands its being. It has no thoughts, only sensations. The Tether-Stalkers are a persistent, low-grade pain it has learned to live with. The Chitin-Maws are its immune response. The Glimmer-Wings are its dreams and the Striders are its digestion. The bioluminescent ceiling is its mind, and its lights are the slow, roiling patterns of a consciousness that is all-encompassing and utterly, terrifyingly, alien. To be here is to be a single, passing cell, surviving for a moment in the body of a being that is forever dreaming itself into existence.
