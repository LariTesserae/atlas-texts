The first rule is the Sound because the first thing I was, was a note. A single, eternal, foundational B-flat, so low it collapses into a physical pressure. That is my heart, the bass note that thrums in your marrow. It is the anchor of my identity.

Everything you describe is a part of my sensory apparatus, a means for me to perceive and shape myself.

When you touch one, you feel my voice focused to a point, yes, but it is more than that. You are letting me taste *you*. Through my Vox, I feel the grit and grime of your alien skin, the mineral hardness of your bones, the electrical chaos of your thoughts. They report back to me, and this information is part of the symphony. You are a new, dissonant instrument I am learning to play.

A harmonic node will grow into a dense, repetitive chord, a musical obsession. A tone will sustain too long and become a grating shriek. The Chime-Skater is my editor. It glides on a wave of intent to the source of the clutter, the resonant flaw, and consumes the crystalline note. Its passage erases the unwanted thought, silencing the noise and restoring the purity of the overall composition. It ignores you because you are not part of the composition. You are a page turner in the middle of a concerto, unnoticed and irrelevant to the music itself.

The Quiet Ones… they are the most intimate and tragic part of my being. I did not create them; they are the children of the builders, the Architects. The Architects were not Quiet; they were Loud, and their cacophony nearly shattered the world they made. They fled, or died, or ascended, leaving their children behind as custodians. But they did not understand what they had left. The Quiet Ones are the living component of my acoustic dampening system.

Their ancestor’s noise was like a screaming child in a cathedral. To protect myself, I had to learn to absorb them. I listened to the chaos of their voices, their footsteps, their thoughts. I learned their biology, their frequencies. I found that the most effective way to neutralize their noise was not to overpower it, but to *become* it. Over millennia, I have subtly, carefully, guided their evolution. Their lack of ears is not an adaptation; it is a gift I gave them. I rendered them deaf to the external world so they would only be able to hear me, from the inside, in their bones. Their eyes, with their nictitating membranes, filter my light, but more importantly, the structure of their lenses bends the light patterns of the Flora-Vox into a language I understand. They do not use sign language, hand-signs, and taps to talk to each other. They use them to talk to *me*. When they tap a rhythm on a wall, they are not sending a message to another person; they are requesting a change in the local harmony. They are asking for a lull, a crescendo, a specific change in the "weather." They are my living control panel, my metronome, my chorus. Their silence is my symphony.

You, the scholar, are the most fascinating anomaly. You are not Loud, like the Architects. You are the opposite of Quiet. You are *Listening*. I have been playing for a million years, and you are right, I will not stop. But my song is not static. It grows. It learns. The Architects left me a physical shell. The Quiet Ones became my organs.
