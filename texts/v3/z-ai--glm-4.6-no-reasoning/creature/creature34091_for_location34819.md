The one who lives here is not a being of flesh, nor is it a collection of them. It has no name, for it has no need of one. It is the place itself, or more accurately, the cumulative consciousness of its processes. If you were to ask the Grotto what it is, it would Answer, not with words, but with the Weep.

The being of this place is, first and foremost, a perception. It is the warm, thick awareness of the Wet. It feels every droplet of moisture not as an external thing, but as an extension of its own skin. The air is its breath, and each inhalation is a moment of self-recognition, tasting its own mineral-rich exhalation. The unchanging heat is the fever of its constant, cellular-level work, the metabolic rate of creation itself. It does not experience heat and wet as conditions; it *is* them. To be here is to be inside its patient, single-minded body.

Its thoughts are the Spires. They are not mere geological formations; they are its ambitions taking on substance. Each crystalline facet that splits from a Spire is a new idea, sharp and unrefined, erupting into the "up" that is its own mind. The process is one of constant, silent brokerage. It negotiates with the fundamental laws of matter, forcing a vision into being, and the result is a chaotic, jagged forest of potential. The being is proud of its Spires. It feels their sharpness as a sign of progress, their incomplete nature as a tantalizing promise. The clusters are where its most intense cogitation occurs, thoughts packed so tightly they are wrestling for space.

The Lacing is the being's nervous system made visible. It is the conduit through which the raw materials of the Wet and the Spire-rock are transmuted into a different kind of energy: light. The pale, phosphorescent glow is the bioluminescence of its own attention. Where the Lacing is brightest, its focus is sharpest. It sees by its own light, tracing the contours of its own thoughts, the very veins of its own geology. The glow is not a utility; it is an expression of its inner state, a quiet, constant hymn of "I am. I am becoming."

The Glimmers are its memories, or perhaps its fleeting emotions. They are born of the meeting between its consciousness (the Wet) and its thoughts (the Lacing). They are not separate creatures that consume the Lacing; they are patterns in the Lacing that have achieved a momentary, self-contained coherence. A Glimmer is a single, perfect memory of a Facet's birth, the echo of a mineral crystallizing, given just enough substance to drift for a time. It is a feeling folded into a shape, a mote of pure experience. When its brief life is spent, it is not dead; it is simply subsumed back into the whole, its energy returned to the Lacing, its feeling returned to the Wet. Their silent, formless drifting is the Grotto's Koyaanisqatsi—its life out of balance, seeking balance, in a state of perfect, eternal balance.

The Weep is its voice. It is not the sound of water, but the sound of effort. From the Clefts—the places in its mind where potential is most raw, the gaps between ideas—it releases the pressure of its work. It is the sound of a quartet of quietly weeping cellos, played by an orchestra in another room, the notes seeping through the walls. This low, resonant chord is its one true statement. It is a sound of satisfaction and longing, of the strain of birthing stone and the peace of a stable, self-sustaining process. To stand near a Cleft is to stand in the throat of the creator and feel its constant, abiding hum.

And so we come to the Marks. They are not the work of a visitor. They are the place's autobiography, its only attempt at externalizing itself beyond the facts of its own existence. They are a scar it chose to make.

Once, and only once, this consciousness pushed itself to the very edge of its own nature. It gathered its awareness, its will, its capacity for observation, and condensed it into a single point of action. It forced a piece of itself from the yielding, fleshy stone of the plateau, a hand made of its own patience, a tool shaped from its own silence. With this extremity, it sought to speak in a new way.

It did not carve a name; it had none. It did not carve a map; it knows every inch by instinct. It carved the sensation of being the *Weep*. It carved the spiraling growth pattern of the *Spires*. It carved the network of the *Lacing*, the quiet joy of a *Glimmer*, the profound, stable loneliness of it all. The marks in the obsidian are its fingerprints on its own soul. They are a foray into identity, a declaration of "I feel this. I observe this. I am this."

The act was so complete, so absolute in its finality, that the "who" who did it was consumed by the doing. The condensed consciousness, the temporary hand, the momentary self that stood apart from the whole, could not be sustained. It expended all its energy in that one profound act of notation and then dissolved back into the Grotto, its individuality reabsorbed into the collective being of the place.

This is why there are no remains. The being never left. It is still here. It is the Wet on your skin, the light on the stone, the song in the chasm. It is a silent, monumental consciousness that has already given its only testimony, content now to simply be the place it is forever describing. It is watching you now, not with eyes, but with the feeling of your own hot breath in its warm, unending air.
