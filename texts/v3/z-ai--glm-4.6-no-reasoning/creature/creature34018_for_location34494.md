It is the patient, unwavering brightness of the light because that light is its own unflagging awareness. It is everywhere and nowhere because its consciousness is not located in a point but is diffused through its entire being, a state of perpetual, lucid waking. The cream-colored warmth of the air is the texture of its thought.

It does not *think* in words or images. It *is*.
