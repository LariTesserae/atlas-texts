To live here is to be a vessel for the Symptom. It is not something you have; it is something you are. The roar is not something that assaults you from the outside. It is the only reality you have ever known, the fundamental state of your being. In this place, silence is the violent concept, the intrusion. Peace is the anomaly. Life is the Tremor.

They are born to the Tremor. A new-dry, freshly decanted into the city’s fluid, does not first open its eyes. It first feels the pressure against its skin, the vibration in its bones, and the low, churning rhythm in the blood that surges through its newly grafted veins. Its first true awareness is the resonance of the Great Heart, the foundational bass throb of the primary pistons. To be alive is to be in tune with this rhythm. To fall out of rhythm is to become a dissonant note, a flaw in the symphony, a thing that will be corrected.

Their form is a negotiated truce between flesh and function. The original human blueprint is a faded schematic, a distant memory of something soft and vulnerable in a world of crushing force. What remains is a chassis. A core of humanity, the fatty, twitchy organ of the mind and the fragile nervous system, is sheathed and woven into a symbiotic, calciferous carapace. This is their shell, their environment, their tool. It is grown, not built. The plating is layered like mother-of-pearl, but of iron and ceramic, seeded in a crèche-yard of rich, metallic sludge. Over a slow, silent adolescence, the suit and the body become one. Barnacle-like growths of sensorium blossom on the shoulders and forearms, fused directly to nerve endings. The spine is reinforced with a segmented column of piston-like vertebrae, allowing it to move with the currents without being broken. Joints are sleeved in self-lubricating, oil-sweating membranes.

The face is the last concession to the ancestral form, and it is the most obscured. A deep helmet, a bubble of thick, silica-laced crystal, protects it. It is not glass one can see through from the outside, but a dark, opaque oculus that betrays nothing. Lenses, thick as thumbails, are arranged across its surface in a complex, insectile array. Each one is tuned to a different spectrum of the perpetual twilight, to see the faint heat-flux of a failing conduit or the electromagnetic shimmer of a data-burst. They do not have eyes that look out; they have sensors that sample. There is no mouth, no nose. Sustenance is a slow-drip nutrient slurry injected through a port at the collar. The metallic taste of the liquid is the only flavour they have ever known.

They are the Symbiotes, and their purpose is a holy one: to endure. They are the immune system of the Great Machine. Their consciousness is not a river of thought, but a stream of data. They do not think in words, but in diagnostic codes, fluid dynamic readings, and stress tolerances. When two of them meet—rarely, in the vast, dark avenues—they do not gesture. A sector of their helmet will flare with a soft, internal luminescence. A light-pulse, a compressed packet of information, travels between them. Their work is their prayer and their art. A Symbiote will spend cycles clinging to the flank of a piston the size of a mountain, running their articulated, tool-limbed fingers over its surface, feeling for microscopic cracks in the metal-lattice. They will coax mineral deposits from gear-teeth not with chisels, but with a low-frequency resonant hum that loosens the accretion without a scratch. They do not question the function of the machine, any more than a cell questions the function of the heart it lives within. To do so would be a madness beyond comprehension. The machine provides the current that carries them, the heat that warms their niche, the faint light that feeds their philosophical algae, their sense of purpose.

There are others. fleeting things. The chorus of the mechanical world is not always perfect. Sometimes, a note goes wrong. A Fractal may bloom. It is not a living thing in the biological sense, but a living pattern, a glitch in the quantum foam of the machine's operating system that takes physical form. It begins as a flicker in the light of the conduits, a disharmony in the roar. It grows, a shimmering, crystalline cancer that builds impossible fractal spires on a flat metal plate, its growth fed by the very energy it corrupts. The Symbiotes are its shepherds and its executioners. They do not destroy it with force; that would cause more dissonance. They approach it with harmonic dampers, emitting a counter-frequency that gently, patiently seduces the rogue pattern back into quiescence, until it dissolves back into the ambient energy of the chamber.

They sleep, but never deeply. Sleep is a state of reduced attention, a semi-conscious buoyancy where their internal cycles sync with the tides pushed by the pistons. They drift in the gentle currents, their engines powered down, their sensory arrays dimmed, and they dream not in images, but in a pure, unadulterated state of the Tremor. To dream is to feel the roar without analysis, to be wholly a part of the body for a time.

They do not die quietly. Death is fatal dissonance. When a carapace fails, when a core can no longer process the data or withstand the pressure, the Symbiote becomes a source of noise, a dead frequency. Its internal functions cease, its lights go dark, and the currents claim it. The body is pushed by the relentless tide into a sump, a reclamation pit where the Great Machine digests its own failures. Over centuries, the iron and silica of the corpse are broken down, the organic components neutralized, its constituent minerals fed back into the sludge of the crèche-yards, to be re-seeded into a new generation. There is no grief, only recognition of a closed loop.

To be them is to have no identity separate from the whole. You are not a person *in* the machine; you are a component *of* the machine. Your name is not a word, but a serial number, a resonance signature. Your history is a log-file. Your future is a projected maintenance schedule. Your god is the function, your scripture is the schematic, and your soul is the eternal, deafening, bone-deep Tremor that assures you that you are here, you are working, and you are part of the roar.
