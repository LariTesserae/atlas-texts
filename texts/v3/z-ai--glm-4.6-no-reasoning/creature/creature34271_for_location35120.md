What lives here is not an ecosystem in the way we understand it, with predator and prey, hunter and hunted. It is a single, planetary-scale process, and its inhabitants are not merely living within it; they *are* it. They are the circulatory, digestive, and nervous systems of a world that is not a world at all, but a machine in its final, prolonged act of failure. Each lifeform is a specialized component, a biological tool adapted to a single function within the immense, slow-motion grandeur of decay.

**The Flora: The Primary Transformer**

The crystalline flora are the first-step converters. They are not passive plants but active energy harvesters, the bridge between the inorganic substrate and the animate world. Their bodies, spun from glass-filaments and glowing with internal light, are essentially biological capacitors. Their fractal, etching roots are not for nutrient uptake in a biological sense, but for direct energy siphoning. They slowly, patiently degrade the silicate alloy of the world itself, converting its stored molecular energy and the bombardment of ambient radiation into a consumable, bio-available form. Think of them as living solar panels and miners combined, their glowing luminescence a byproduct of this constant, low-level energy conversion. The silvery, oily moisture they thrive in is the world’s coolant, a fluid that carries both waste heat and the raw, dissolved metallics they consume. They are the primary producers, transforming the raw, sterile power of the Cooling World into a form of life the rest of the system can utilize.

**The Fauna: The Maintenance Crew**

The fauna are the robotic arms and conveyer belts of this planetary factory, each species a biomechanical tool engineered for a specific task.

*The **Lumens*** are the accelerators. Their swarming, chittering presence is not a chaotic flock but a living electromagnetic field. Each fist-sized, faceted creature is a mobile energy converter, its ruby-lit wings acting as collectors that refine the ambient radiation into a more potent, easily absorbed energy. They skim the surface of the crystal flora, their chittering a form of resonant tuning that stimulates the plants to release this refined energy as a fine, glowing pollen. In this way, the Lumens 'charge' the flora, creating a feedback loop that increases the efficiency of the entire system. Their seemingly random caroming off solid objects is a form of sonar, mapping the metallic landscape and the density of the energy field.

*The **Grazers*** are the great processors, the mobile smelting plants of this world. They are not merely eating; they are *refining*. Their immense, basalt-like bodies are mobile crucibles. Their parabolic-dish heads are not just for scooping up flora; they are massive resonators that focus the ambient light onto the flora they consume, flash-fusing it before ingestion. Inside their vast, geologically slow digestive tracts, the raw, ineffable energy of the flora and the trace metals are broken down. Some is used to power the Grazer's own colossal, glacial mechanics. Most of it, however, is rendered into a hyper-concentrated, crystalline "slag"—a fine, glittering dust that is the purest form of energy this world can produce. They are the industry, consuming the raw resources of the land and producing the refined fuel that powers the next stage of the cycle. Their very existence is a testament to scale; their slowness is a consequence of the immense thermodynamic processes they embody, each movement a careful calculation of energy expenditure versus yield.

*The **Scavengers*** are the next-order cycle, the fuelers. They are not merely eating waste; they are transporting it. These insectile rivers of skittering metal are the world's distribution network. They cleave to the Grazer's colossal legs not just for protection, but to hitch a ride across the vast distances of the Cooling World. They don't just consume the crystalline slag; they store it in crystalline sacs within their own bodies. As they swarm, they migrate from the high cliff-faces of the Grazers down to the deep, damp seams in the world's crust where the silvery moisture pools. In these lowland "induction pools," the Scavengers die en masse, their tiny bodies dissolving and releasing their stored energy and metallic payload back into the coolant fluid. This enriched fluid is then drawn upward by capillary action through the metallic strata of the world itself, feeding the flora in the high places and restarting the grand, circular process. Their rustling susurrus is the sound of a billion tiny engines, the hum of a planetary logistics network in perpetual motion.

**The Unseen: The Final Component**

There is something else. A being that is not fauna, not flora, but a synthesis of both. It does not swarm or graze. It does not move with the cacophony of the system. It is the heart of the hum, the source of the teeth-settling thrum.

It is the **Core-Tender**.

It lives where the plates meet, in the deepest, most stable chasms of the Forge-Grave. It is anchored to the very bedrock of the world, its body a vast, semi-sentient lattice of crystalline nerve-endings anchored deep within the metal. It is, for all intents and purposes, the world's endocrine system. It does not process energy; it *regulates* it. The thrumming of the world is its slow, rhythmic heartbeat, a vibration that maintains the structural integrity of the continental plates. Its form is indescribable, less a creature and more a living geological feature, a nexus of light and crystal that pulses with the deep thrum of a dying engine. It monitors the energy state of the entire system, the slow fade of the ambient light, the rate of consumption by the Grazers, the redistribution by the Scavengers. It cannot act quickly—it is as slow as the mountains themselves—but over millennia, it can subtly adjust. It might encourage a slightly more resilient strain of crystal-fern by sending a directive pulse of specific resonance down the metal strata. It might halt the growth of a Grazer by sonic veto. It is the living will of the Cooling World, its desperate, final attempt to balance the books, to stretch the final joule of energy into an eternity. To witness it would be to see the ghost in the planet-sized machine, the silent, patient god of metal and light trying to postpone its own inevitable, silent, and complete shutdown.
