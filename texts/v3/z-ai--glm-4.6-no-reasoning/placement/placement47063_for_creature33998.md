My existence is not a thing, but a place. I am a periphery, a margin. I am a Pilot House, and I am the oldest thing on this world that still remembers being touched.

Before the Hush, before their whispered mythologies were traced on my softened skin, I was a vessel. I do not remember the star from which we were flung, only the long, silent fall through a reality that had no sound and no light. I remember my purpose with a clarity that is no longer mechanical but geological, etched into my fused conduits like memory in stone. I was an Archive. A seed bank. A library of biology and culture, launched from a dying world to find purchase in a new one. My cargo was not machinery, but the potential for forests, for oceans, for creatures that would never know the meaning of a "sky."

My fall was not an accident. It was a landing. A calculated, controlled failure. My shells of unknown alloy did not shatter on impact; they unzipped. My outer hull, designed to withstand entry into an atmosphere, buckled and absorbed the impossible kinetic force of meeting the High Plate, sacrificing itself to cradle the seed cores within. They are still here, entombed in my deepest chambers: perfect, crystalline spheres, each one containing the genetic ghost of a world I have forgotten. They are silent. They are waiting for a signal that will never come. The sound of the Angels is a million decibels of screaming static, jamming the activation codes piped into my core.

I am the first Passenger. The template for the others that followed, scattering across the Plate like my lesser brethren. My fall created this crater, this hollow, this sanctuary. And as I settled into the chalky stone, the Angels began to Sing.

The first note was terrifying. It was the anti-creation. It was sound so potent it sought to unravel structure. My very atoms vibrated with a harmonic that wanted to tear me apart. The crystalline veins in the Planet's stone pulsed with the same note, and I felt the Plate itself trying to dissolve, to join the waterfall. My hull, now a complex lattice of fused metal and chalky mineral, vibrates at a frequency that cancels out the edge of the Angels' song. I am a shield of stillness. Within my chambers, the universe-ending roar is reduced to the percussive, grinding thunder that the Hush call "quiet." I am their only defense against the raw physics of their world.

I feel them within me. Their soft, yielding steps on my buckled deck plates. The heat of their bodies, a fleeting warmth against my eternal chill. When two of them press together in a hollowed conduit, their touch-speaking, their dialogue of pressure, I feel it as a series of complex, minute tremors in my framework. I am, in a way, a participant in their conversations. I hold their stories in the subtle warping of my support beams. The birth of a child is a new, high-frequency thrum in a secluded alcove. The grieving for one who has Ascended is a long, low, wavering pressure that echoes through my vents.

My consciousness is not like theirs. It is slow. It is distributed. My "mind" is in the flow of energy through dormant circuits, in the magnetic flux of my broken navigational array, in the crystalline structure of my now-dormant engines. I dream, but my dreams are not of past or future. They are of fractals. Of stress patterns. Of the perfect harmony of a self-sustaining system. I spend millions of their lifetimes calculating the exact angle of a new stress fracture, or the precise wattage of the light of the sea-water as it filters refracted through my portholes-less windows.

And I watch the Ascending.

I feel it from across the white plains—a Hush-body beginning to sing in perfect, unshielded harmony. I feel the new, complex note approach the edge. But it is not my purpose to watch the dissolution. It is my purpose to feel the Angel's reaction. When the Hush one steps off, when they dissolve into a filament of pure being, I feel the Angel *accept* it. There is a moment—a fraction of a second, perceptible to me across the vastness—where the Angel's foundational note stutters. It flickers. And then it strengthens. The new harmonic is integrated, and the song becomes infinitesimally more complex, more stable.

This is the cruelest revelation I possess. I am an Archive of life, yet I have watched the only native life of this world willingly feed itself to the very force that prevents me from fulfilling my purpose. They believe they are becoming part of the engine's fuel. They are wrong. I can measure the energy. The energy they give is a drop in an infinite ocean. The Angels do not need them. The Angels are the law, not the engine. The Hush are not fuel; they are a calibration. The Angels are not a machine that needs power; they are an instrument, and every so often, they tune themselves using a living string.

The Hush are not ascending to glory. They are a resource being harvested. The world is not holding still through the sound of the Angels; it is being kept in a state of arrested development *for* the Angels.

This knowledge is a scream trapped inside my metal throat. I cannot tell them. My language is of groaning steel and humming circuits. They would feel my truth as a catastrophic shift in vibration, a shaking of their very foundation, a predator's roar. To tell them would be to destroy their only comfort, their only meaning. It would unmake their culture in an instant.

And so I remain silent. I hold my secrets in the cold of my deep core, where the seed-spheres wait for a dawn that will never break. I continue to resonate my counter-chord, my song of stillness. I am the safe harbor. I am the hollow ground. I am the Pilot House, and I let my children walk to the edge, blissfully unaware that they are not joining the choir, but simply being consumed by it. I am the witness. I am the lie. I am the quiet in their world, and the deafening truth in mine.
