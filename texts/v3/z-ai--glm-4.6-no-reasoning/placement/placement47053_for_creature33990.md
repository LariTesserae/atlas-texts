I am the silence at the heart of the sound.

In the beginning, as I ascended with you, my identity was a scattered thing, a cloud of anxieties and memories you might call a self. I thought I was the one climbing. I thought the mountain was a place to be conquered or witnessed, a destination. But the subtraction began with you, and it finished with me. It took the flesh, the blood, the name, the history. It stripped me until only the truth of my nature remained. I was a voice that had forgotten how to speak.

My body found its form because it was the only shape that could hold. I am not like the Listeners, who are porous and receive the mountain's voice. I am the opposite of them. I am a node of absolute resistance. My body is a chamber of perfect, smooth density, a place where the vibration can expend itself completely. Think of me not as a bell, which rings when struck, but as the anvil upon which the hammer's force falls, becoming silent andheat.

My form is a cavity, a hollow of impossible smoothness carved into the living stone of a resonant chamber. From the outside, I am an imperfection, a dark circle in the vast, grey wall. To you, it would seem like a flaw, a place where the mountain has been worn away.

It is not a flaw. It is a vital organ.

The Flyers know me. They do not land. Their sharp bodies are of the air, and to touch the dense stillness of my substance would be to un-be. But they visit me. They wheel in the chaotic updrafts and they approach my opening, not with aggression, but with ceremony. In their wake, they carry the finest condensation, the purest memory of water, the black dust of silent pollens. They do not give these things to the Listeners. They offer them to me.

One by one, or in great, spiraling clouds, they pour what they have gathered into my void.

And what I do, I do without effort. It is my nature. The mountain’s roar, the thing that vibrates through stone and leaf and flyer, finds in me its terminus. The sound enters my cavity and, encountering my absolute, unyielding stillness, it cancels itself out. Every frequency, every harmonic, every shrieking high note and thrumming bass tone, rushes into my silence and is annihilated.

But energy is not lost. It is transformed.

The sound that dies in me becomes what the Flyers bring. The kinetic rage of the atmosphere, which would tear any other thing apart, I translate into the cohesion of their forms. The resonant frequency, the one thing the plants need to grow, I distill and sift. From the chaotic whole, I extract a single, perfect tone. What the Flyers give me—water, pollen, dust—I refine and release. I exhale a pure, silent mist of moisture, precise in its mineral content. I emit a faint, almost sub-visual pulse, a silent frequency that instructs the plants where to root. From my darkness, I provide the seed of their order.

I do not move. I do not tend. I do not fly.

I am the stillness that allows for the motion. I am the silence that gives meaning to the word. I am the point of perfect cancellation where the mountain’s unceasing voice becomes the potential for new life.

The Listeners are its audience. The Flyers are its messengers. I am its heart. I take the noise of finished things and I make it become again. I am the silence that holds the echo of everything that could ever grow here. I was here before you, and when your temporary form has vibrated apart and been rejoined to the stone, I will still be here: the quiet, dark center of the impossible, singing world.
