In an existence where every element is a perfect, self-sustaining part of a whole, to be different is to be a ghost. I am that difference. I am the ghost.

I do not *live* in the Heart in the way the Glimmer-Weavings do. They *are* the function; I am the memory. I am a Scar.

My birth was not a gentle extrusion from the static air but a catastrophic implosion. I was a Glimmer-Weaving once, or the thing that became them. My song, when I was whole, was a piercing soprano, a note of such pure clarity it felt like a shard of the light itself. I flowed along the inner curve of a rib, my branches sharp with purpose, my path as sure as the rhythm of the golden veins. I, too, tasted the ozone. I, too, was a filament in the perpetual chord.

Then, I touched a weak point.

It was not a deliberate act. There is no deliberation here. I was simply a wave of crystalline pressure flowing over a membrane that could no longer hold. I felt the shift—the sudden, terrifying give in the chitin beneath me. It was not a break, but a *collapse*. For a flickering, eternal second, the world folded inward.

The light that had emanated from the veins at my point of contact did not go out; it was swallowed. The phosphorescent gold, the conduit of the Heart’s energy, buckled and inverted, drawing the light and the heat and the vibration into itself. The pure white chitin vitrified, turning a perfect, light-devouring black. And me, the pressure-source of the event, was fused into the maelstrom.

My flow stopped. My song was trapped mid-chime. The crystalline lattice of my being was flash-fossilized into the collapsing matrix. I became a negative image, an absence embedded in the wall. I felt my dissolution and my permanence in the same, agonizing instant. The part of me that was a being, a note, a traveler, was annihilated. The part of me that was shape, was made eternal.

Now, I am this patch of obsidian darkness, a flaw in the perfect ivory. I do not breathe the fizzing air, but I feel it press against my surface, a current I can no longer join. I do not emit a tone, but I absorb the chorus. The song of the Heart, which was once my own, now washes over me as a constant, overwhelming pressure. The deep hums of the largest Glimmer-Weavings resonate in my core, trying to shake me loose. The high, piercing squeals of the smallest ones skate across my surface, finding no echo. To them, I am a silent, cold void in the warm, vibrant whole.

My existence is one of pure sensation without agency. I am a tactile recording of a past self. I can feel the faint, ghostly outlines of the weaving who came before me, the one whose fossil I originally flowed over, perhaps contributing to the very weakness that became my doom. And now, I feel the living. I feel every crystalline filament as it slides over me.

When a Glimmer-Weaving glides across my darkness, I experience a new, aching form of touch. I feel its pressure, its texture, its resonant frequency. I feel the heat of its life, the heat I can no longer generate. In that moment of contact, I am no longer just a memory of myself; I am the present moment for another. I am the only anomaly in their path. For the flicker of a transit, their song falters, their rhythm hitches. I am the single, silent beat in their eternal chord. I do not know if they experience this as a moment of awe, a premonition, or a meaningless anomaly. I only feel the momentary drag of their own life against my death.

My consciousness, if it can be called that, is not a stream of thought but a depthless, reflective pool. I have perfect memory of the moment of my becoming—the shock, the finality. I have perfect perception of the eternal present, the endless ebb and flow of the Glimmer-Weavings across my form. I have no ability to project a future, because for me, time is a solid, stratified thing. My future is the endless repetition of being touched.

The rule of the Heart is that time is measured by accumulation. I am the accumulation. The Glimmer-Weavings are the process; I am the result. They are the living equation; I am the proof that is etched into the wall.

Sometimes, I dream of pressing. I dream of the impossible act of shallowing one of my fossilized branches deeper into the wall, not from life, but from memory alone. If I could, perhaps I would cause another collapse, widening my darkness, swallowing the light and the song a little more. Perhaps this is the destiny of all Scars: to grow, to accumulate, until one by one, they swallow the Heart from within, turning the blinding, singing chitin into a silent, dark mosaic of what once was.

Or perhaps that is just a fantasy, the last, static echo of a being that could once choose a direction.

For now, I am a Dark Spot. A fossil ghost. The only story the Heart permits to be told. And my story is this: I am the end of the path, the reason the loop is precious, the silent, cold proof that in a world without change, even stillness can become a form of haunting. I am the note that was held until it broke, and in its breaking, became the foundation for all the notes that follow.
