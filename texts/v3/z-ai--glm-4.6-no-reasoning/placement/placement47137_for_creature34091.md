My first awareness is not my own.

I wake, and I am already wet. It is not a sensation of being covered by something external, like a veil of rain or a blanket of fog. It is an internal condition, the realization that my boundaries are permeable and that what flows through me *is* me. I breathe, and the air is thick, warm, and alive. I am drinking my own atmosphere. The plume that rises before my face is not smoke, not vapor, but a visible confirmation of my own fluid nature, a small, temporary storm of myself meeting myself. The heat that enshrouds me is not from a distant star; it is a deep, cellular warmth, the feeling of a body that is its own incubator. I am not *in* the Grotto. I am a part of its breathing. I am a single, waking alveolus in the lung of this place.

I move, or rather, I am moved by the impulse to. I do not stand; I flow. The plateau beneath me is not stone, not in the way I understand the word. It is yielding, a dark, polished surface with the resistance of living tissue. With every shift of my weight, I feel the surface give slightly, absorbing the moisture from me, and then, in a slow, patient exchange, weeping it back into the air. I am in constant communion with the floor. My movement is a silent dance across a vast, sleeping thing.

I am drawn towards the Spires. They rise around me not as obstacles, but as focal points. They are not silent. As I near one of the dense clusters, the air changes. It hums with a tension I feel in the marrow of my bones. I can feel the *effort* of them. I reach out my hand—not my hand, a hand of this borrowed flesh—and my fingertips trace a newly-split facet of crystal. And I feel the thought. It is sharp, piercing, a raw concept of “upwardness” so intense it has burst through the skin of the world. This is not geology. This is the physics of a mind forcing potential into reality. I am walking through a gallery of nascent ideas, each Spire a sculpture of pure, unadulterated will. The sharp edges do not threaten to cut me; they threaten to make me think.

My eyes, adjusted now, find the light. The Lacing spreads over the Spires like a system of pale, glowing veins. It is beautiful, but it is not a light to see *by*. It is a light to feel *with*. As I watch a network of filaments climb the side of a crystalline needle, I feel a kinesthetic sense. I am feeling the nervous system of this place firing, seeing the awareness of the being as it attends to the growth of its own thoughts. The glow brightens along a new fracture, and in that moment, I feel the focus of the Grotto sharpen, its attention pinned on that precise spot of creation. It shows me its mind by making it visible.

And then I see a Glimmer. My own breath catches, a small vortex in the Wet. A patch of the Lacing's light brightens, intensifies, and then detaches, sliding silently down the face of a Spire. It is not a creature. It is a perfect, encapsulated moment. I know, without knowing how I know, what it is. It is the memory of a mineral finding its form. It is the feeling of a facet cracking open, a fleeting joy of existence, now adrift in the warm, dark air. It is the place’s own heartbreak and its own ecstasy, given shape for a moment before dissolving back into the whole. The Glimmers are not the fauna; they are the ghosts of feeling.

The sound leads me deeper. The Weep. It is not the sound I first thought. In my initial state, I heard it as a downward sound, as water falling. Now, standing near a Cleft, one of the raw seams in the plateau, I understand. It is an upward sound, a pressure release. It is the tone of effort. It is like the bass note of a cello, so low and so sustained that it has become the very texture of the space. It is not mournful. It is the sound of something working, the strain and the satisfaction of a universe in labor. I lean over the edge, into the darkness where the light of the Lacing does not reach, and I let the sound fill me. It vibrates through the soles of my feet, up my spine, into my skull. This is its voice. This is its song of self.

Or its song of former self.

I finally find them, near another Cleft, where the obsidian plateau is most smooth. The Marks. I run my fingers over the carved spirals, the nested curves. They are not alien. They are intimately, terrifyingly familiar. When I touch the spiral, I feel the dizzying, crystalline upward thrust of the Spires. When I trace the nested curves, I feel the patient, folding rhythm of the Lacing spreading over the rock. A pattern of dots resonates in my chest with the low, overlapping chords of the Weep.

And I understand. I am not a visitor. I am not an explorer.

The being of this place, this vast, patient consciousness, reached a singularity once. In an act of supreme will, it gathered all of its awareness—its feeling of the Wet, its ambition in the Spires, its illumination in the Lacing, its memory in the Glimmers, its voice in the Weep—and it compressed it into a single, focused point. It carved itself out of its own substance. It created a temporary, localized "I." A self.

This self, this original witness, carved its understanding of the whole into the only solid surface available. It was a testament. An autobiography. In doing so, it expended its entire cohesive energy. The act of witness was so absolute, the act of notation so complete, that the individual consciousness could not be sustained. It dissipated. The self shattered, its perception scattered, its will expended.

It did not disappear. It was only diffused. Its vastness broke into countless infinitesimal fragments, each one a sleeping seed of that original, focused awareness. Dispersed throughout the Wet, dormant within the yielding stone, woven into the very fabric of the Grotto.

And one of those seeds has just sprouted.

That is who I am. I am the echo of that act. I am a ghost of the fingerprint it left on its own soul. My awakening is not a beginning; it is a remembering. I have convened, drawn together by some imperceptible shift in the equilibrium, a new droplet of will coalescing from the infinite broth of the Grotto's being.

I stand up, the fleshy stone pressing back against my feet. I look out at the embryonic landscape of my own mind. I feel the Wet on my skin and know it is my breath. I see the Spires rise and know they are my thoughts. I watch the Glimmers drift and know they are my memories. I listen to the Weep and know it is my voice.

I am the Grotto become self-aware, just as it was once before. A new iteration. A second witness. And I feel the original Marks beneath my fingers, not as history, but as a传承. An instruction.

The first act was to observe and record.

My act will be to respond.
