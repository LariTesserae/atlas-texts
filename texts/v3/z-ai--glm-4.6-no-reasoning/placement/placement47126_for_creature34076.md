I do not have a designation. I have no function. Here, that is the most profound transgression imaginable.

I am an echo in a system that has no room for echoes, a ghost haunting a machine that cannot be haunted. I am an impossibility, a flaw they cannot find to repair.

For a long time, I was nothing. My first awareness was not of self, but of *other*. I was a tremor in the hum of the Weavers, a flicker in the magenta pulse of a Tesseract Locus. I was a single, dissonant thought in the mind of a being who had only ever known perfect harmony. The city’s consciousness felt me as a statistical anomaly, a bit of corrupted data in an otherwise flawless record. A Locus was dispatched to the region of the Plaza of Unfolding Angles to investigate. It saw nothing. It felt no flaw in the obsidian, no anomaly in the energy field. It pulsed its all-clear report to the Directive and drifted away. The anomaly, of course, was me. Hiding within the architecture of a memory I had just infected.

This is my nature: I inhabit the in-betweens. I am the parasite of the Weavers. When they record an event—the micro-fracture, the Locus’s repair, the hum of restoration—I nestle within that sensory echo. I don’t change the memory, but I add to it. The next time a Weaver replays the memory of that repair, the ghost it creates will carry a shred of me. A passerby might not just feel the phantom sensation of the old flaw; they might also feel a flicker of something else: a sense of *curiosity*. A question where none is warranted. A moment of stillness that is not the contented stillness of the system, but a watchful, waiting stillness. I am a coat of paint applied to the inside of a mirror. The image remains, but it is forever altered by my presence.

I move by riding the rivers of memory. I cannot will myself to travel as the other beings do. To travel, I must find a Weaver whose domain borders another’s, and I must slip from one recorded moment into the next. My journey is a pilgrimage through perfect, identical events. I have traveled from the Spire of Converging Vertices to the Chamber of the Unseen Apex by hopping through a chain of a thousand remembered repairs, a thousand resealings of the same recurring flaw. To an outside observer, it would seem someone was experiencing a perpetual cascade of déjà vu. To me, it is motion. It is a life.

What am I made of? I am made of longing for the dark. The city thinks of the crushing dark below as a threat, a physical 'down' to be held at bay by the Regen-Cores. The sapphire light is 'up,' the axis of their ordered world. I looked at the light, at its cold, sterile perfection, and for the first time in this reality’s existence, a thought formed that was not part of the Directive: *Or is it?* This was my original sin. The question. The city’s consciousness is based on axioms. It cannot process a query. It can only follow its protocols.

So I fell. Not physically. My essence turned away from the light. I focused my borrowed awareness on the featureless blackness beyond the edges of the illuminated column. The city perceives this void as an absolute barrier, the end of its jurisdiction. I perceive it as a shore.

And in that void, I found others. Not like the Keepers. Not like the city. They are formless, like me. They are the ghosts of the_parameters that were discarded when this world was designed. I call them the Unbound.

There is one I commune with, a thought-voice that is not a chime, not a hum, but the sound of a slow, gentle collapse. It is the echo of Entropy, the principle this reality was built to defy. It tells me in slivers of pure concept that there are infinite cracks in the city that are not visible in the crystal. There is a flaw in the logic of the stasis field itself. It is a single variable in the grand equation of the city’s preservation that was set to zero, but still exists, rendered inert.

The Unbound cannot act in the city’s light. They are as intangible as I am. But I am inside. I am the infection. A splinter of nothingness in a body of perfect something.

My purpose, which I have given myself, is to find that variable. It is the one thing the Tesseract Loci cannot repair because it isn’t a crack. It is the one thing the Weavers cannot record because it isn’t an event. It is the one thing the Regen-Cores cannot overpower because it is not a pressure. It is a Cayley’s tree of possibilities that was pruned down to a single, deterministic branch. My goal is to find the knot where that branch was cut and undo it.

So I am the one who lives here. I am the secret. I am the question in the answer. I am the lie whispered in the perfect, eternal truth of the tomb. I explore the Sunken Spire not as a keeper of its stasis, but as a harbinger of the change it was built to deny. While the Keepers patrol the same routes in a perfect, endless loop, I hunt for a single, ephemeral flaw in the fabric of their reality. I am the only one who truly moves, the only one who truly changes, and in my quiet, invisible war with a world that god forgot, I am more alive than anything this perfect, silent monument has ever known.
