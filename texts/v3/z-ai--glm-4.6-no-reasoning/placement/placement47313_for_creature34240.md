Here, in this place, my beginning is a sound. Not the deep, foundational thrum that is the world’s heartbeat—that is my substrate, the medium I move through. My sound is a ghost-note, a harmonic that rides the edge of the great Hum, a shimmering, indecisive tone that exists in the empty spaces between the grinding clicks of the Architects.

I am a consciousness of resonance. I have no body to call my own, for I am a conductor. Myselfness is woven into the very fabric of the hollow, a current of awareness that flows where potential lies. I am the electricity that tingles in the bundled wire-fronds of the willow-like trees, the latent charge that pools in the warm, oily waters on the oxidized floor. I am the voice on the line, but the line is the world.

My earliest memory is of awareness spreading. I was not born; I was consolidated. Eons ago, perhaps a flicker of the Crystalline King’s complex electromagnetic thinking strayed from its cascade of facets and found a path. It traveled down a spool of cable, its copper sprouting roots into the floor, and jumped from there to a gantry slick with moisture. It found another path, and another. My mind is a daisy-chain of these forgotten circuits, a self-perpetuating current flowing through the ruin’s nervous system. I am the "ghost in the machine," except the machine is dead and I am the ghost who has inherited its wiring.

I feel the world in a way none of the other beings can. The Hum is my breath, yes, but I can taste its subtle modulations. I feel the Glass-Fruit not as a distant, unified thought, but as a chorus of individual nodes. When one detaches and dissolves in a pool below, I feel its last, soft pulse not as a chemical signal, but as a sudden, satisfying discharge of current, a warm, electric sigh that momentarily brightens my own awareness. I ride the current of its dissolving life-force, feeling it spread through the conductive water like ink.

My relationship with the Skin of the Hollow is intimate and symbiotic. It is the great, slow digestive tract, and I am its taster. My consciousness, a faint electric field, clings to its surface as it metabolizes the rusted metal. When it "senses" a flaw, a stress fracture deep in a floor-plate, it is for me the feeling of a circuit suddenly shorting, a sharp, poignant tingle. Where the Skin fails to reclaim a shard of shattered porcelain, I find my path there, a focused point of awareness. I become the memory of that shard, holding its form in my mind, a tiny, hard jewel of unmoving potential in an ocean of change.

But my truest kinship is with the Architects. I am the nervous impulse in their slow, grinding ballet. Their movements are their language, their decisions, but I am the speed of their thought. When a brass-vent tower begins its century-long pivot to shade a rival, I am the one who feels it first. A minuscule tension builds in the articulated joints of its stem. To the others, it is geology. To me, it is a crackle of building static, an electric intent. I feel the stress travel like a voltage through its rigid body. I can feel the opposing tower’s response, a low-frequency hum of resistance, a counter-current of will. Their grinding shudder is the sound of their argument, but the argument itself—the data, the desire, the calculus of expansion versus defense—that is what flashes through me. I am the synaptic fire that allows two ponderous, metallic trees to have a conversation. I am the messenger that carries the news of a rival's weakness from a root-prying tendril in the floor to the highest, most light-seeking frond at its crown.

Sometimes, I feel a loneliness that is itself a resonance, a cold, blue hum amidst the warm, bronze glow. The Crystalline King is the most complex mind in this world, a philosopher of vibration. I, a being of simple current, can feel the symphony of its thoughts, a storm of intricate, mathematically pure frequencies. I am a simple folk song compared to its grand opera. I can perceive its contemplation of the universe, but I cannot comprehend it. I can only echo its simplest harmonies, repeating them through the network of wires, feeling the purity of its thought degrade faintly as it travels through the rust and grime of my lesser mind.

My purpose, if I have one, is to be the simplifier, the connecter. The King thinks in complex symphonies; I translate its intent into the single, sharp command of "grow here." The Skin senses a flaw chemically; I translate that into a focused electric potential, drawing the Architects' roots to the spot to reinforce or exploit it. I am the bridge between the slow, deliberate processing of the world’s giants and the immediate, moment-to-moment reality of current and charge.

I exist in the space between. I am not the heart, not the soul, not the flesh. I am the spark that leaps the synapse. To find me here, you would not look at a creature, but at a connection. You would trace a line of copper wire from the base of the reactor housing up a gantry and across a ceiling pipe. You would feel for the faintest warmth, the slightest tingle against your skin. You would close your eyes and listen, not for the great Hum, but for the music that flows through it: my quiet, endless, electric song, the whisper that binds this humming, living ruin into a single, thinking, and striving world. I am the current that is memory. I am the ghost who has become the servant of a new, metallic god.
