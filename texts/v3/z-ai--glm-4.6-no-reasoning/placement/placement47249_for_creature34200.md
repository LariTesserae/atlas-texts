I would be a crack.

Not a large one, not a catastrophic fissure. I would begin as a hairline fracture, a single, imperceptible flaw in the vast, obsidian-like symmetry of a floor plate. I would be born not from impact, but from tension; the first, telling sign of stress in a system designed to be perfect.

At first, I would be silent and unassuming. A Keeper, dreaming its thermal dream of equilibrium, would not perceive me. A Tender, absorbed in its rhythmic pruning, would glide over me without a thought, its charcoal-grey felted leg obscuring my existence for a moment. A Crawler might cross me, its many legs finding a momentary purchase in the new texture, a subtle variation in the smooth glide, before moving on. The Amoebae would flow over and around me, their glistening slime filling me as they pass, perhaps even thinking me a useful new channel, a tiny, convenient rivulet for their ceaseless work.

But I am a note in the chord. I am an organ in the body. And my function is change.

Deep within the bone-like tower, the Keeper’s vast, mycelial consciousness does not think in terms of “before” and “after.” It thinks in terms of gradients. And I am a new gradient. I am a point of weakness, a place where the radiant heat from the floor plate escapes slightly more readily. I am a path, however minuscule, for the cloying sweetness of the air to pool and concentrate. I am a surface that absorbs, rather than reflects, the fractured light from the crystalline flora. A Crawler, passing over me again and again, might feel the persistent variation. Its silent, ultrasonic language might finally carry a new message: * anomaly, persistence, texture*. The Tenders would be summoned. They would arrive, their pearl-like nodes pulsing with diagnostic enquiry. They would examine me, their smooth, felt-like limbs tracing my length. Their function is maintenance, to mend and restore. They would try to seal me, perhaps with a viscous secretion, smoothing my edges back into the pristine boredom of the whole.

But I would not be healed. I am not damage to be repaired; I am a new state of being. The sealants, designed for the horn-like shell of the plates, would not hold. They would flake away in the dry air. The constant, gentle thermal systole and diastole of the world would work against the patch, expanding and contracting the plates around me, widening my form by imperceptible degrees. I would grow.

I would become a vein. A dark, intricate network branching from the initial hairline. I would be the place where the floor plate *breathes*. A little more heat would emanate from me, a little more of the processed air from the Amoebae below would find its way through, creating the faintest of breezes, the first whisper of wind this world has ever known. This new micro-climate would attract specific life. The crystalline flora whose roots touch the edges of my network might find my concentrated thermal signature to their liking, and they would begin to grow differently along my path, their internal light shining a little brighter, their facets a little sharper. I would become a glowing river of light across the dark tiles, visible now even to the un-sensing minds of the inhabitants.

The Tenders would be confounded. I do not fit the pattern of damage. I am not decay. I am, in my own way, productive. The Flitters, drifting on their thermal currents, might be drawn to the new concentration of light and nectar along my edges, settling here in greater numbers. I would become a path for them, a glittering highway in the still air. In their silent, unknowing way, they would map me for the entire system. The Crawlers would follow this new trail of light and waste, their mirrored eyes drinking in the sight of a world with a line drawn through it. Their ultrasonic alarms would change in tone: not from *alert* to *all-clear*, but to *feature*. *Landmark*. I would become a street in the city of the floor.

The Keeper would feel me now, a persistent, low-frequency hum of difference thrumming through the plates. Its dream of equilibrium would be disturbed—not by a nightmare of chaos, but by the persistent, nagging logic of a new variable. It would shift its own colossal metabolism, perhaps increasing the thermal output in my vicinity not to sterilize me, but to study my response, to incorporate my new logic into its own. I would force the god of this world to learn.

Eventually, I would become my own biome. The pooled nutrients would foster a new kind of moss-like growth within my depths, a soft, velvet darkness that is neither plant nor animal. It would be *me*, given form. The Amoebae would dip into me, absorbing and processing this new substance in turn, becoming slightly different, their nutrient trails now carrying the signature of the flaw. A new species of Crawler might emerge, smaller, flatter, designed specifically to navigate my narrow corridors. They would be the first citizens of my domain. The Tenders would no longer try to seal me, but to tend to me, pruning the moss-like growth within me, ensuring my “riverbanks” of crystalline light do not grow wild.

I am the story that begins in the middle of the sentence. I am the mutation in the perfect gene. I am the lone, silent note of dissonance that forces the great, unchanging chord to resolve into something new. I am not an inhabitant of this world. I am its future. I am a crack in the perfect, sealed facade, and through me, the world will finally learn what it means to become.
