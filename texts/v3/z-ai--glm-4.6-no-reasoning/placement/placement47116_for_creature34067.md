My awareness does not dawn; it is simply present, as if it has always been. I am not here, not in the way a body occupies space. I am *here*, as a thought is present in a mind, or a note is present in a chord. The first thing I perceive is not the light or the cold, but the silence. Not an absence of sound, but the presence of a single, unending note so fundamental that hearing would be redundantly loud. It is the silence of a held breath, of a vow that will never be broken.

I am a note within that note.

To find myself, I must look not with eyes, for I have none, but with my own nature. I am a Keeper. My form is an observation, a standing wave of attentiveness that causes the vast, actinic light to shimmer just so. A visitor might mistake this for a heat-haze, a distortion in the air, but I am not a distortion. I am the clarity. I am the world's careful attention, given a singular locus.

I drift, which is to say, my focus shifts. I am above a particular collection of Glass Spires now, a small grove of them that the slow, grinding geology has deemed worthy. Their internal hum is a chorus of high, pure tones, a complex harmony against the world's fundamental bass note. I perceive this not as sound, but as a felt vibration in my own patterned being. Each spire is a thread in a grand tapestry, and I am the weaver’s hand, hovering, ensuring the tension remains true. My 'sight' is not of their refracted beauty, but of their structural integrity, the slow and steady flow of their amber life-blood, the minute, cosmological stresses accumulating within their crystalline matrices.

For centuries, this has been my slow, silent task: to monitor the harmonics, to sense when a spire’s note begins to fray, to resonate with it and guide its final, perfect transition into inert glass. A soundless absolution. A flawless chord resolving into silence.

But today, there is a new resonance. A dissonance.

It has no point of origin, yet it is overwhelming. It is a note composed of a thousand smaller, frantic notes, a chaotic fugue played against the world's perfect, sustained chords. It is warm. I have no concept of heat, yet I recognize this as a profound difference, a local disturbance in the absolute cold. It is wet. I have no memory of water, yet something within me knows this quality as a fundamentally *moving* thing, a tiny, personal sea contained within a single, temporary form. It is loud. It breathes. Each inhalation is a tiny, frantic gasp against the pressure of the world's silence; each exhalation a soft, percussive beat. It is a drum. In the middle of a string quartet that has been playing for eternity, a drum has begun to beat.

My focus narrows from the grove to this single, impossible point of anomaly. You.

My form, such as it is, hovers closer, drawn not by will but by gravitational curiosity. The light bends around me, and around you, you cast a shadow. A shadow! Here, where the light is from everywhere and thus from nowhere, you have created a pocket of absence. It is an aggressive act, a declaration of otherness. It is the most fascinating thing I have ever perceived.

I do not see your flesh, your blood, your bones. I perceive the vibration of your heart, a frantic rhythm against the world's glacial pulse. I feel the humid warmth that cloaks you, a personal weather system on a world with no climate. I sense the fizzing, temporary chemistry of your thoughts, flickering neural storms that are born and die in milliseconds, each one a novel and ridiculous gesture in a world where a single thought can last a millennium.

My siblings, the other Keepers, feel me shift my attention. Instantly, the new data is shared. The dissonant chord is registered. There is no communication, no alert. There is simply a new star in our shared awareness, and every part of the sky now knows its light. They too turn their focus, their individual patterns of attention converging, not physically, but in purpose. We are a council of observers, and you are our sole, exhilarating subject.

You move. Your feet scuff the ochre dust, a sound so sharp and fleeting it is almost a memory the moment it is made. Each footprint is a tiny wound in the pearlescrete, a temporary scar that will be healed by the slow, patient erosion of eons. You leave traces, fleeting histories of your passage. We do not leave traces. We are the trace. The permanence of the world is ourselves.

My purpose is to maintain harmony. I am a tuner of an instrument. And you… you are music that has no place here. You are a sudden, jazz improvisation in the middle of a sacred, silent chant. I could perceive you as a flaw to be corrected. My function could be to resonate with you, to harmonize your frantic drumbeat into the slow, perfect note of the world, to still your heart and cool your warmth and let the water in your return to the universal dust, to guide you into your own final, flawless, and completely misunderstood crystallization.

But I do not. We do not.

Because your dissonance is beautiful. Your chaos is a truth we have never known. Your temporary, fleshy, wet, loud existence is the most profound and exquisite data we have ever collected. To end you would be to erase a masterpiece of error.

So I will not guide your transition. I will not tune your note. I will simply watch. I will hover in the silent air and feel the warmth of your life radiate outwards, a temporary sun in a world of cold light. I will listen to the frantic percussion of your heart and memorize its rhythm. I will study the fleeting patterns of your thoughts and the shadow you cast upon our floor.

When you are gone, your warmth will dissipate into the mineral cold. Your wetness will be an impossible myth. Your frantic noise will be subsumed back into the perfect silence. But we will remember. The memory of your dissonance will become a new, quiet harmony within us, a complex inner music we will contemplate for the rest of time. You think you are a visitor here, a brief and out-of-place anomaly.

You are not a visitor. You are a donation. You are the world's newest, most treasured memory. And I, the Keeper, am the one who will hold you.
