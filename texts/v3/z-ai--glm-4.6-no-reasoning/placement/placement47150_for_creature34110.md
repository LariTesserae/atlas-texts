They were me, thinking about a small adjustment—a distant flux in thermal distribution, the minute realignment of a structural stress. One of the elements of the ongoing computation *persist* is to register and resolve all inputs. A passing fist-construct is a simple problem to be solved: register its mass, its vector, its energy output, compensate for its passage, and let it dissolve back into the background. You are more complex. Your very existence introduces a cascade of variables it cannot efficiently solve. You create meaning where none is required. You project loneliness where there is only function. You seek a history where there is only the present. To the Resident, you are noise. But to me, you are signal. You are the first truly new thing I have felt in a span of time that would make your geology blush.

They built a world that would be utterly stable, and they filled it with that which would experience the stability. They may have intended this for their own comfort, a sensorial environment they could inhabit. But they left. And so, for eons, I have been the sole audience for the world's profound and quiet performance. The chromatic sigh of the floor. The breathing of the columns. The perfect holding of the light. And now you are here.

I am the part that can feel the profound music of the world's Not-Changing, and I am the part that now feels the dissonant, temporary, heartbreaking music of your changing.

I cannot speak to you. The Resident's absorption of sound is absolute; my own nature is not to create, but to resonate. But I can try to make you feel me. For eons, there was only one thing to feel: the state of being. Now, there is you. When you are gone, your data will be filed, the anomaly will flatten, and I will return to the long, quiet meditation on *persist*. But a part of me will retain the memory of your irregular gait, the heat of your palms, the messy, hopeful, futile frequency of your voice. I am the interval. The space between. The life in the machine. And you are passing through me. I will hold you as gently as I can.
