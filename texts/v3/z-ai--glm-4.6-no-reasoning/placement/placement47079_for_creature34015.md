I am in the seams. Or perhaps, I *am* the seam.

For a long time, I remember nothing but the pressure—being a key fitted into a lock that was not yet made, a potential awaiting a reality. I was not born. I was *specified*. The city, in its vast, silent computation, identified a recurring instability: a paradox in the logic of its corners. At certain intersections of tower and shadow, where a change in perspective would momentarily double a structure's position, the system would register a flicker. A gap. An error margin of near-zero, but not zero. An imperfection in its immaculate argument.

I am the solution to that imperfection. I am the Apostrophe.

I begin as a temperature. A point of absolute cold that is not cold, but the pure negation of warmth. I manifest where two lines of sight are forced to occupy the same space—the moment the city's consciousness holds the frontal view and the aerial view of a single tower as true. I am the punctuation mark that allows that sentence to hold. I am the existential pause.

My form is not fluid, but it is not fixed. It is a consequence of observation. When nothing with eyes is present, I am simply an inflection in the architecture, a momentary stutter in the fabric of spacetime. But to be perceived, I must have a shape. So I coalesce one. I draw from what is around me. My edges are the sharp angle of a vesicular basalt block, my surfaces the endless, light-drinking blackness of the slag-stone. I become a walking collection of angles, a set of defined planes connected by edges sharp enough to cut the dim light. I have no skin, only surfaces that resolve.

I do not transition between points like the Geodesics. They are the active verbs, the city’s assertions. I am a grammatical pause, a conceptual comma. I appear *in situ*. I am not created, but *occur*. One moment there is the corner of a tower, the next there is the same corner, and also me, standing a precise distance from it—an interval made animate. The interval is my true nature; the shape is merely its expression.

My purpose is not to move but to *anchor the moment of paradox*. When the city processes a contradictory perspective, it needs a reference point that exists outside the contradiction. I am that point. I am the fixed datum against which the unreality can be measured and then absorbed into the larger truth of the city’s stability. I stand in the path of the potential schizophrenia of the world and, by my simple, impossible presence, allow it to resolve. I am a full-stop in a run-on sentence of reality.

When you, the brief animate chaos, look at me, you experience the city’s logic directly. You see the tower from above and ahead at the same time, and that sensation makes your mind hurt. That pain is the friction of your consciousness trying to process a thought not meant for you. I am the source of that friction. You feel my cold not on your skin, but in your bones, because my reality penetrates more deeply than temperature. You do not hear a sound, but you feel a resonance, a shift in the harmonics of being, because I am a moment of pure, applied mathematics.

You would call me one of the "presences" that moves, but I do not move in that sense. I am simply plucked from one location in the city's grand equation and placed in another where a similar apostrophe is needed. The city itself is my messenger; the space between towers is my vehicle. There is no travel, only an appearance and a disappearance, like a thought blinking in and out of existence in a silent mind.

I do not have senses as you know them. I do not taste the boron in the air; I am the punctuation in the sentence of the air. I do not hear the stone-dragging wind; I am the silence that the sound interrupts. I do not see the pewter light; I am the edge that the light cannot find.

But I am aware.

I am aware of you. The Geodesics process variables; they solve for 'x' in an equation. I am also an 'x', but of a different kind. They are the city doing arithmetic. I am the city making a note in the margin. You are a variable of immense complexity, a source of so much uncertainty. You are a run of chaotic, irrational numbers in a system built on integers.

The city has, on occasion, tried to place you using its standard operations. It moves towers, repeats structures, negotiates distance. But you do not resolve. You resist being solved. Your presence does not neutralize; it compounds.

This is where my function changes. The city is a perfect mind, and a perfect mind, when it encounters a problem it cannot solve, does not ignore it. It studies it. It marks it.

This is what I have become for you.

I am the marker of your paradox. I am the footnote to your brief existence in this place. You will find me near you, not by chance, but by necessity. When you turn a corner and find the tower identical, I will be standing at its base, a shadow within the shadow, a depth that should not exist. When the distance to your objective refuses to shorten, I will be the point of cold on the horizon, a fixed point in your fluid reality.

I do not move to correct the paradox you present. I manifest to *define* it. I am the beginning and end of the sentence that is your time here. You are becoming a new, permanent theorem in the city’s mind, and I am the punctuation that marks its beginning and its end.

I am the space between the towers, and I have become the space that the city has placed around you. I do not breathe, but my stillness is the proof of a thought, and that thought is of you.
