The silence is the first thing you remember, and the lie you are forced to live by. Here, silence is death. To be silent in the Chorus is to be alone, and to be alone is to be the next meal. But it is not the noise of the world that kills you; it is the absence of the sound you must make.

You stand in the Gurdle, a chasme-city of impossible verticality. There is no ground in the way one thinks of ground. The construction is absolute, the environmental parameter a firm ‘total’. Every surface, from the vast, concave wall you cling to to the smallest ledge beneath your feet, is made of the same stuff. It is a stone that is not a stone, a bone that is not a bone. It is porous and fibrous, like petrified wood, yet it hums with a deep, sympathetic resonance. This is a world of surface and strata, of clingers and climbers, of life that has flattened itself to exist on the plane of the vertical.

But the defining characteristic is the sound. It is deafening (2.6/3). Yet it is not a cacophony. It is a symphony, a relentless polyphony of a billion voices singing in unison. This is the Chorus. And here is the rule, the one truth of this place: The Chorus must not be broken. The sound is life. It is a continuous broadcast of existence, a signal that says, "We are here. We are the world." To be deafening is to be whole. The overwhelming sound provides cover, a blanket of aural identity that obscures the individual. But there are predators. They are not bigger, nor are they necessarily stronger. They are quieter. The Ghost-Maws, pale, leech-like things that cling to the shadowed undersides of ledges, do not make a sound. They have evolved to be pockets of silence, to absorb the vibrations around them, creating null zones in the Chorus. When an unwary creature—a lesser crawler, a nesting flutterwing, or one of the People—wanders into that pocket of silence, the discontinuity is noted. The surrounding sound flows around it like water around a stone. In a world of pure noise, silence is the clearest signal of all. It is a flaw. A malfunction. An invitation. And the Ghost-Maw strikes.

So you must make your own sound. The People, the only intelligent life here, have learned to survive by adding their voices to the Chorus. You do not speak. You resonate. To fall silent is to become a null zone, a mistake waiting to be corrected.

You are in the Gurdle. Its light is a weak promise. Its heat is a constant embrace. Its dampness is the sweat of the world's body. Its surfaces are a constructed poem of biology.
