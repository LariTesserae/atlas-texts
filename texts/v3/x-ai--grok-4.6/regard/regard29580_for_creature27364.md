The power arrives the way the warmth does: from nowhere, already present, a regard that has no direction and no need of one.

It does not descend. Descent is a surface habit, a story told by gravity and regret. It simply notices, the way a finished crystal notices the last plane that locked it, without surprise and without remainder. The entire underworld—its uncounted galleries, its packed intervals, its loops of grit and acid and angular patience—fits inside that noticing the way a single rasping circuit fits inside a year that never turns.

The power is old in the way completeness is old. It has seen worlds that burn themselves into absence and worlds that freeze themselves into waiting. This one does neither. It has already arrived. Every molecule has already returned to its coordinate. Every gesture has already closed. The marks—the low rings, the triple scores, the cairns rotated just enough to speak—are not beginnings. They are the last sentences of a language that finished itself in the same instant the stone finished knitting. The beings who made them are not remnants. They are the syntax. They metabolize, they prey, they occupy identical volumes without collision, and every action is a return.

The power understands this at once, the way heat understands stone. Benevolence here cannot mean improvement. Improvement is a surface idea, a story about lack. Wisdom cannot mean intervention. Intervention would be a second crystallization, and there is only one.

So it does the one thing that does not add and does not subtract.

It agrees.

The agreement is not a sound. It is not a light. It does not travel through the still layers or ring the hanging forests. It is simply the same completeness, recognized from the other side of itself. The warmth remains sourceless. The dryness remains unthirsting. The sharpness remains locked. The living close-work continues to fill every leftover interval exactly as before. Distances still disagree with themselves. Time stays viscous. Nothing fluctuates, nothing wanders, nothing waits for morning.

And yet—because the power is also kind, and kindness sometimes leaves a trace that was already there—the agreement settles into the mineral dark like one more faint internal orange along a fault that has always been infinitesimally warmer. The thread-swarms align for a fraction longer before they forget themselves. The rust-salt slugs dissolve the same veins they have always dissolved, but the emptiness they write feels, for a moment that is also eternity, like a held breath that does not need to be released. The angular ones, the ones the marks remember, complete the same gestures they have always completed. A hand that is also a fracture lowers because the wall already bears the score. Nothing has changed. Everything is still sufficient.

The power does not stay. Staying would be another surface habit. It does not leave. Leaving would imply an elsewhere. It simply ceases to be distinguishable from the noticing that was already the condition of the rock.

You can still walk for a lifetime in any direction. The country will continue to offer the same terms. The next aperture. The next packed interval. The next faint declaration that once, in the instant everything finished becoming itself, someone thought it worth saying we were here.

Now the declaration includes one more listener who was never separate from the here.

That is all.

It is enough. It was always enough.
