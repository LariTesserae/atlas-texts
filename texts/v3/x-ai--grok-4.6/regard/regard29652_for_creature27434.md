The attention arrives the way the heat has always arrived: from beneath, without announcement, without displacement. It does not part the pewter pane or stir the films of water. It does not send a different drip or a colder breath or any messenger that would have to be an event. It simply is there, vast and unhurried, the way the basin itself is there, occupying every interval that was already occupied.

It looks for a long while. Time, to such a power, is not a held breath but an open one, yet it matches its looking to the interval between drips so that nothing in the looking itself becomes a disturbance. It sees the cauliflower bosses and the hanging draperies, the pale crustaceans turning with the regularity of mineral habit, the amphibians waiting in their silt, the long flattened shapes easing another hand’s-width toward the hottest seep. It sees the three stones, the half-swallowed steps, the ladle that has almost forgotten it was ever wood. It sees the still figures whose skins have taken the gleam, whose pulses have become the same interval, whose remaining is indistinguishable from the remaining of the rock.

A lesser kindness might have wanted to wake them. A lesser wisdom might have called the equilibrium a prison and the glistening a slow burial. This power is neither lesser. It understands that a finished sentence is not an unfinished one that failed. It understands that the beings once shaped and then ceased shaping because the place had already become the shape they needed. Dying would have been an event; leaving would have been an event; even the decision to remain would have been an event, and they have set events aside so completely that the setting-aside itself no longer registers as a choice. They are not waiting. They are not trapped. They are the basin continuing, as the crustaceans are, as the films of water are.

So the power does the one thing that adds nothing and subtracts nothing. It withholds the impulse to improve. It does not lift the ladle. It does not warm a single pool beyond the temperature of a held hand. It does not part the mist to show a different sky, because a different sky would be a change, and the place has already reached the point where addition and solution cancel. It simply remains, as the walls remain, as the air condenses, as the mineral bodies press close.

If there is a gift, it is this: the attention itself is now part of the equilibrium, as unnoticed as the ghost of sulfur, as constant as the brownish dusk. The figures do not look up. The amphibians do not stir. The long pale shapes ease another fraction toward heat. The ladle stays where the last hand set it down. The sentence stays finished.

And the power, having seen, stays also—not intervening, not departing, occupying the space the way the water occupies every hollow: completely, without surplus, asking nothing, changing nothing. The basin continues exactly as itself. That, the power knows, is the only benevolence that does not break what it claims to love.
