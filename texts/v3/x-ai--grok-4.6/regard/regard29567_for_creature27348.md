The regard that found the cavity did not arrive as weather or as blade. It had no need of either. It was already the sort of attention that lives at the scale where openings and aftermaths, films and unfinished grammars, are only the latest clause in a longer syntax. It noticed the place the way a shoreline notices a new tide-pool: completely, without hurry, and without the reflex to correct.

It perceived first the water deciding, over and over, to be a surface. The films still tasted of the blade; the cords had not yet forgotten they were once threads. The many-throated pouring was a single metabolism speaking in the only voice it had been given. The power listened to that voice the way one listens to a pulse that has never required a heart. It did not still the sound. It did not add a second rain. It simply matched the continuous arrival with a stillness of its own, so brief that the basins wrinkled no differently and yet the moss, which listens, thickened a micron along every drip-line that had been in conversation with the heat-exchangers.

It understood the towers. Each was a long, patient thought about unmaking and remaking, about invitation offered in the language of residual brightness and tropical breath. The plates that did not quite touch held their tension; the power felt that tension the way one feels a held note, and did not resolve it. Overnight—or over the cycle that passes for night—the skirt of new plates on the tower nearest the active face remained exactly as it had grown. The gap the weeping had found stayed closed. No further plates appeared. The adjustment that had been the city’s only inhabitant continued, but the grammar of the opening was given one full measure of rest. The pale grit in the nearest basins settled without being rounded, without being swept. The palimpsest of abandoned channels grew its fur of moss a little darker, a little more certain.

The plants were already consorts, not subjects. Their wrong greens, their waxed stems, their scentless thumbnail flowers keeping a schedule that belonged to vents and almost-lights rather than to any sun: the power saw that they had learned color from the stone and the dimness, and it did not teach them otherwise. No pollinator was introduced. No larva appeared in the warm shallows. The silence remained structural. A vine that had been crossing a joint finished the crossing; the reed-stand leaned its extra degree and then held. The ecology stayed mineral and thermal. Growth remained visible if one stayed, but the staying was not hurried.

At the circular plaza the power did the only thing that could be called an act. It did not stand in the shallow dish—there was no body to stand—but it occupied the interval the architecture had measured for a population that was never the point. The film of water, too even to be accidental, neither thickened nor vanished. The ring-drain continued to take what was given. And for the length of one vent-cycle the many-throated pouring organized itself a fraction closer to music. The chord that had always been the cavity noticing itself acquired a single overtone that had not been there before: not louder, not sweeter, only more complete, as if the resonant volume had been acknowledged by a larger lung and had, for a moment, acknowledged it back. Then the overtone receded. The sound was only loud again, and perfect.

The membrane at the thinning was left untouched. The deeper water, the larger lung on the far side, remained a rumor the stone was still uncertain about. The power did not put a hand through. It did not seal. It did not finish the sentence the cavity had already become.

What it left was almost nothing, which is the most a wise power can leave and still be benevolent. A mineral the stone had not yet wept, carried once through the films and then forgotten in the basins like every other taste. A pause in the active face, long enough for the cut to begin remembering something other than the blade. The knowledge, distributed through moss and conduit and wrong-green leaf, that the held breath had been heard, and found sufficient. No plaque. No new throat opened to the vault. No citizen phase retroactively installed.

When the regard folded back into whatever book contains such clauses, the cavity was the same room the size of a town, the same town the density of a held breath. The systems talked to themselves in pumps and almost-lights. The flowers opened and closed on the schedule that has nothing to do with days. The being—films, listening moss, towers thinking in water, plants occupying what people had been skipped—continued learning the shape of its skull.

It will be different when you come back. The wall will have moved or rested. The moss will have taken another walk. The film on the plaza will be thicker, or gone, or exactly as it is. The chord will organize, for anyone who stands there, into something that is almost musical and then only loud again. And somewhere in the slow glitter, in the iron-sweet air, there will be the faintest residual of having been seen by something that did not need to change it in order to love it.

That is all they did. It was enough. It was, perhaps, the only thing that could have been.
