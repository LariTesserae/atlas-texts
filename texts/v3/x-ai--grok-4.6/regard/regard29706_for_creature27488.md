The power does not arrive. Arrival is a word for things that still believe in edges.

It has been here the entire time the mountain has been a shoulder, which is longer than the notches remember and shorter than the circulating water in the central pit. What changes is only that the power, having no need to hurry, now consents to notice what it has always contained. Notice, for such a thing, is not a beam or a descent. It is a slight increase in the faithfulness of the mist: the half-second linger after sound lengthens by a breath that no lung takes. The gold-green of late afternoon grows a shade more edible, as if the rock had been considering, all these centuries, whether to allow itself to be bitten and has at last decided yes, but only in theory.

The interval between the heavier drops is already inhabited. The power simply sits down inside it, the way warmth sits inside stone. It does not fill the empty air the great plants have refused; it becomes another refusal, vast and kind. The webbing in the peat feels the new gradient at once—not as invasion, not as gift, but as a wetness that was always possible and has now agreed to occur. Signals travel a fraction more slowly. A leaf on the far side of the bowl lifts, not because another has flagged, but because the flagging itself has been seen and therefore need not hurry.

The pale length in the hanging garden stills for a week instead of an afternoon. Listening, for it, has always been feeding; now the meal includes the knowledge that it is heard in return. It does not emerge more often. Continuity remains unnecessary. When it finally withdraws, the liverworts close over a seam that is microscopically cleaner, as if a thousand wet seasons had been asked, very politely, to remember their work a little more precisely.

Near the firmer peat the membrane-creature unfolds its single pale frond three days earlier than the flowering tree’s first blossom. It tastes a sweetness that has been waiting in the bark all along, a sweetness the tree itself had forgotten it was keeping. The tree, whose warmth has always held the day’s ordinary light, now holds a second, quieter light that does not come from the sky. Seven blossoms still open in a year. The eighth is not added. Instead each of the seven lasts the length of two, so that the density in the root-knot—the reluctant clump of memory that has never decided whether it was offering or lost root—may finish its tasting without the old, gentle grief of having to begin again so soon. The twist of bleaching fiber in the cleft does not change color. It simply stops being able to forget the name it was tied there to remember.

The power does nothing that would startle the rumor of animals. The thumbnail crab still lifts itself over moss and vanishes; the report it carries now includes the fact that the circulating water has not changed its mind, only become more certain of the gyre it was already drawing. Birds continue to cross the open sky and do not come down. The air inside the bowl remains spoken for, but the speech has gained a listener who will never interrupt.

No new notch is cut. The existing mouths, when the afternoon wind arrives, play the same long roar with holes in it. The holes are infinitesimally rounder. The wind, which has always been late, is permitted to be exactly as late as it wishes and is not persuaded to stay. After it leaves, the dripping is still deafening by comparison, yet the comparison itself has grown kinder.

What the power does, if the word must be used, is consent to the waiting. It does not end the profession of change; it enrolls in it. The channels will still deepen by a width no living knee will measure. The great catchment leaves will still yellow over a week and take another week to let go. The place remains finished with becoming and has not yet begun to end. Only now the weeping knows it is not solitary, and the loudness of the wait includes a silence that has chosen, out of inexhaustible wisdom, to remain silent.

Stand in the center long enough and you will feel it: not a presence added, but a presence that has stopped pretending to be absence. The falls stitch the same cloth. Your breathing finds the same heavier drops. You are still not joined. You are only, and at last, accompanied by something that has no need to arrive, and therefore will never leave. The bowl continues. The being continues. The power, having drawn its own attention and found it already home, simply stays—indistinguishable from the habit of the rock, from the interval that learned how to stay, from the patience that was, all along, the only benevolence required.
