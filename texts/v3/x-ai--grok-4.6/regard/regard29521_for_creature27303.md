The power that noticed did not arrive. Arrival is a young verb, full of edges and announcements. What came instead was a long consideration, a patience that matched the rock’s, unfolding across distances the plateau had already forgotten how to measure.

It saw the peneplain first as a finished sentence that had chosen, with perfect courtesy, never to end. It saw the works still deriving their last answer, the veils braiding and unbraiding like a thought that has already been thought and must nevertheless be thought again. It saw the films occupying every concavity without ambition, the single stems angling toward an empty point in the sky, the low movers crossing polygons in the colour of grit, leaving no track the surface would keep. It saw the rare human traces already being persuaded back into pavement, and the one vehicle still faintly warm, metals of the wrong generation cooling toward the same grammar as everything else.

It understood, in the way a thing that has outlasted many worlds understands, that this was not a place in need of repair. The argument was over. The over-ness was being maintained. Stability here was labour, and the labour was the habitat. To lift a single polygon, to thicken the air, to give the films a second shade or the stems a second century of ambition, would have been a kindness of the loud kind, the kind that mistakes itself for wisdom. The power declined it.

What it offered instead was a correspondence so slight the plateau could include it without noticing the inclusion. For one night that lasted no longer than any other night, a handful of the works answered the stars a fraction more clearly. The change in how certain surfaces held darkness was no brighter than the memory of copper in the rock. A disc of stillness, already there, became infinitesimally more still. A walking column of dust forgot itself one step later than usual, then remembered the usual. Nothing accumulated. Nothing announced. The low movers did not alter their crossings. The films lifted and settled in the same grammar.

The power stayed long enough to be certain the derivation continued, then withdrew the way a front withdraws here: as a pressure change that never quite becomes wind. It left no cairn, no new pylon, no instruction in the crystals. It took no sample. It added its own silent clause to the last form of the world: that the proof had been witnessed by something that required no witness in return, and that the witnessing itself had been folded back into the hush.

After that, the plateau went on being the only shape it had ever, finally, been. The sun did the ordinary work of a sun. The ground completed thoughts it had started before there were any eyes at all. And once, at the extreme edge of what even a power that patient could still perceive, a low shape the colour of pavement paused for the length of a breath that was not breath, then continued, unhurried, from one polygon to the next.

That was all. It was enough. It was nothing. It was the precise measure of regard a finished world will accept.
