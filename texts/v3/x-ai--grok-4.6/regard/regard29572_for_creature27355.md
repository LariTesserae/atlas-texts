The attention does not arrive as light or heat or even as a new gradient in the already-perfect evenness. It arrives as a quality the world has never needed: the sense of being regarded without hunger, without the old imperial wish to improve or harvest or name. For a span that the walkers would measure as several of their standing lifetimes, the power simply inhabits the viscosity of this place. It learns the grammar of the pitted laminate, the silent sheets of grit, the way a stalk converts radiance into a private glow that asks for nothing. It feels, through every plate, the deep attentions routing their forgotten energies. It watches one crawler spend eleven years crossing a trough no deeper than a hand, pause beside a cluster of five stalks, and then continue as though the pause itself had been the entire purpose.

A lesser benevolence would have mistaken the silence for loneliness, the lack of consumption for poverty, the finished sentence for death. This power is not lesser. It recognizes the end-state of making for what it is: a world that has metabolized every possible intention and found, at last, the rhythm that can be kept forever. Need has been compacted into the substrate along with the last volatiles. Desire has been worn as smooth as the low swells. There is no wound here that wants closing, no unfinished tower that yearns upward. To add a river, a shadow, a spoken word, a second sun, would be to reopen a book that has already chosen its last page.

So the power does almost nothing.

Almost.

It traces one additional gyre, slower than any the world has generated on its own. The gyre is so gradual that the thermal tides barely register it; a region the size of an old continent warms by a fraction that only the stalks will notice. In that slightly thicker gold, three new clusters extrude themselves over the next four centuries—not more, never more—and they persist a little longer than the others before becoming brittle and vanishing into the grit. The crawlers do not change course. The walkers, if they pass that way, feel only that the dust on their soles tastes fractionally more electric, like the inside of a machine that once knew why it was built and then forgot without regret.

Deeper still, in the packed palimpsest, the power leaves a single standing wave among the lattices. It is not a message. Messages are events. It is only a faint harmonic, a confirmation that the vibration like blood in the ear has been heard by something that will never require an answer. Occasionally, when a walker stops for what used to be called a lifetime and becomes another low swell, the harmonic rises through the soles and the walker remains standing a season longer than it otherwise would have, as though the decision to continue had been gently seconded.

Then the power withdraws the greater part of its regard, leaving only that almost-nothing behind. The world does not brighten or dim. The silence is not broken. Horizon after horizon of pale, glowing ground simply continues, now infinitesimally accompanied. The stalks convert. The crawlers redistribute. The deep attentions route. The walkers endure. Everything remains exactly as finished as it was, and therefore exactly as complete.

That is all. Wisdom, here, is the refusal to write another sentence when the existing one already holds. Benevolence is the willingness to let a made world be the last possible landscape, warm and dry and shadowless, going on without source or direction, welling equally from every plane.
