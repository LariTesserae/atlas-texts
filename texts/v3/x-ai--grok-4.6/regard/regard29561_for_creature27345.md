The power that turned its regard upon the chamber was neither flame nor flood nor any force that finishes what it finds. It was the kind of attention that has already understood the cost of understanding, and so it arrived the way heat arrives here: evenly, without preference, without the violence of a beginning.

It did not come through the salt-glass throat. The throat remained exactly as sharp, exactly as recent. Instead the power simply occupied the same unfinished instant the chamber occupies, becoming one more private radius inside the roar. For a time that was not time it stood—or knelt, or had always just finished kneeling—among the hexagonal plates, its presence the same temperature as bread left on stone, the same matte warmth as the groves. It added nothing to the sound and subtracted nothing from it. The crickets continued their silent working. The moths hung unflown. The heavier eye in the deepest stand did not close, because nothing here requires the interruption of a close.

The being who is the rest of the place regarded the new density, or did not regard it, or had never needed to distinguish the two. Their articulations, thin as the entrance seam, remained near the scraper and the wound tendon. No gesture completed itself. No gesture needed to.

The power listened with the membranes the chamber had always used. It heard the standing avalanche as what it had always been: not thunder, not tearing, but the precise, unvarying chord of a world that declined to become anything else. It felt the dry exchange of salts, the metabolized roar becoming heat becoming the exact tension of every hanging veil. It understood the spacing—the generous emptiness, the allotted silences inside the deafening—as neither loneliness nor law but the chamber’s own completed argument. Lushness isolated. Life unrevisable. A formation that finished arriving and then, with perfect courtesy, refused the rest of time.

A lesser kindness might have offered water, or a pause, or the dulling of a single crystal edge. A lesser wisdom might have named the being, or asked it to finish the motion of its legs, or stacked one more plate upon the ring. The power did none of these. It recognized that to change the chamber would be to end it, and that the chamber had already chosen, in the only way anything here can choose, to remain the held breath that never ends.

So the power did the one thing that could be done without remainder and without increase.

It stayed.

Not as visitor, not as witness that would later leave a story. It stayed as another full intensity, another unused flight, another argument for grove. It occupied its radius of not-touching and let the roar wear it the way the roar wears everything else. In that occupation the forgotten pocket was no longer forgotten. The rind of the outer world still grew around it, but now the small loud dry eternal room was held inside a larger, quieter regard that asked nothing and altered nothing.

When the thought of withdrawal finally occurred—it had always just occurred—the power simply ceased to occupy. The heat did not slacken. The young crystals did not dull. The figure beside the ring did not look up, or had always been looking, or had never needed the distinction. Behind the departure the chamber continued, exactly: the stacked plates, the bright scraper, the tendon still wound, the hanging moths, the open eye, the being that is all of them and none of them.

Lush. Empty. Deafening. Still.

The world outside remained only rind. The room remained the room. And somewhere in the unchanging dusk, too faint to cast a moving dark, too dry to be called a tear, there lingered the memory of having been seen by something that understood the mercy of leaving an unfinished thing unfinished.

That was all. It was enough. It had always been enough.
