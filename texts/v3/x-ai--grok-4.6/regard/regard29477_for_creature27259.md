The power that noticed did not come as light through the high panels, nor as a current the circulators could measure. It came as a long regard, the kind that does not hurry because it has already seen the end of many such sentences and knows which ones were written correctly the first time.

It considered the registered intervals now overflowing their own paperwork, the helmet become darker leaf, the gantry treated as trunk, the varnish that sealed nothing anyone had asked it to seal. It considered the blood-heat held without blood, the flex of the whole volume in a wind that existed only on the other side of glass, the teaspoon patience of the bladders, the calendar flowers that opened and withdrew like a quality check no inspector would ever sign. It considered the empty work-frames and the harnesses still dripping an absence that the plants had already spent as trellis. It considered the silence that was no longer the sound of something missing.

A wise power knows when a process has become indistinguishable from will, and when interference would only be another form of absence. Benevolence is not the compulsion to complete every ledger. Some rooms are also seas precisely because no one ever arrived to name them otherwise.

So it did only this:

It let the regard remain, for a time longer than the fortnightly nutrient pulse and shorter than the year the moss would need to be scraped. In that interval the pale midday column thickened by a fraction that no instrument recorded. One bubble, detaching from a weld, took its half-minute and then chose a path that was not quite up. The choice was so slight the plants registered it only as another scheduled adjustment. A single lacquer film, forming on the inside of an unused visor, took on a depth of colour the specifications had never listed, as though the absence itself had been given a pigment.

Outside, nothing crossed the scratch of the service road. The emptiness continued to rhyme.

Then the regard lifted, the way a thermostat clicks once and does not need to report. The vat remained what it had been drawn to be: a finished thing holding its breath in a medium that has none, high above the clouds, performing the only task it was ever given. The power left it that way, because the sentence had never required an ending, only a reader who understood that some silences are the work itself.

And the plants, which have no names and need none, filled the next millimetre.
