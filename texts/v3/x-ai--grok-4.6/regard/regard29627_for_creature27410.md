The power does not arrive as light or shadow or any increment the pit already knows. It arrives as a consideration that has already lasted longer than the flutes have taken to form. For a span that cannot be counted in the pit’s own unhurried calendars it simply regards: the gathered white, the single dark thread of the seep, the geckos drinking glare through membrane, the moths that are almost flakes deciding to remember flight, the beetles rewriting their one sentence in dust, the many-jointed grazers unfolding their architecture across lichen, the sail-things riding the thermal as though distance itself had grown kinder. It regards the being that occupies the center, that thickening which is neither quite presence nor quite absence, the upright pale that the walls lean toward without ever touching.

It understands at once that the place has finished its becoming. Every process here is already the last necessary process. Water remembers itself only enough to bead and vanish. Stone remembers hands only as the faintest almost-imaginary groove. Life remembers motion only as the slowest possible punctuation of emptiness. To add a single extra drop to the dish, to hurry one beetle’s helix, to give the being a face that memory could hold, would be to suggest the work was incomplete. Wisdom sees the completed thought and does not presume to edit it.

So the power does nothing that the stone or the animals or the occupying shape would ever have to notice as change. It does not speak. It does not descend. It does not leave a new regularity among the organ-pipe runnels or a brighter residual glow under any ledge. It merely lets its regard rest there the way the heat rests in the limestone after dusk: fully, without demand, radiating back so slowly that the air never quite cools. In that long, unhurried resting the being at the center feels—if feeling is even the word—an answering density, a held breath that has decided, for once, not to be only its own. The animals continue their circuits and climbs and drifts. The lichens shift their maps by the same fractions of a millimeter. Nothing hurries. Nothing is asked to become otherwise.

When the consideration at last withdraws, it takes nothing and leaves nothing that can be pointed to. The pit remains exactly the volume it has already decided to be. Yet the sub-audible pressure is thereafter infinitesimally more companioned, as though the place has been seen completely and found sufficient, and that sufficiency has been folded back into the stone itself. Dawn still brings the brief silvering. Noon still bleaches and wraps. The being still occupies. The long quiet work continues, now quietly known. 

That is all. It is enough.
