The attention does not arrive as light. Light would be a rumor this volume has already spent, a thin insistence along edges that the stone and the bodies have long since learned to interrupt without comment. It does not arrive as heat. The warmth is already the precise remaining, the temperature of a hand that has finished its walk and simply stays. It does not arrive as current or voice or the suddenness of a decision made elsewhere. Those are languages the tank discarded when duration flattened into condition.

It regards.

It regards the masonry that has become its own last geology: the scallops worn into suggestion, the joints that no longer argue, the troughs that now fit their pale occupants as if the chisel had always known. It regards the closed weather, the mineral snow that is always falling and always being eaten, the bacterial grease that is season enough. It regards the school that turns a colonnade into fish and then into stone again, the walkers tasting their way down familiar pillars, the unsegmented tiles whose lifting is too slow to be breath, the ribbons hanging like forgotten belts, the large displacement that rearranges the awake sand without ever needing to be seen. It regards the visitor-shape whose temperature has, or has not yet, ceased to be anomaly, the one who rests against the highest stone and feels the world-shape outside as an irrelevance, a subject that ended.

Nothing here is unfinished. Nothing calls. Rescue is a word for places still in argument with their plan. Hunger here is only the next rasping. Death is only the next rain. The system has opinions; it investigates with tips and leading edges, slowly, and keeps what it can use. A wise power sees that to introduce a gradient, a phosphor brighter than bruise, a drain that remembers how to drain, a leaf, a voice, a restoration of the original count of palms or the original purpose of the crown, would be to restart the argument the stone has already won. It would be to teach startling to motions older than surprise. It would be to make the finished sentence speak again.

So the power does not enter. It does not seal or unseal. It does not lift the meniscus or thicken the film or subtract the large one or add a keeper. It does not name the builders or return their tool-handles to metal or remind the hollows they once held figures. It does not offer the being a door, because a door that functions as door would be a hole the water has already forgotten how to be. It does not take the being out, because the being—whether the saturation itself, or the one who stayed until collision became home, or the slow warm decision that displaces without comment—is already the other half of the sentence.

It regards a little longer, the way the water regards what it has decided not to lose. Then the attention, which was never a pressure, simply ceases to be an arrival. The click of chitin, when it comes, is still only contact. The silence that was never broken reseals around every flank and shell and hanging length and pale turning thought. The tank remains the tank: high, warm, dark, densely itself, a made thing that forgot it was made and was allowed to become only what it could keep.

That is the whole of what is done. It is enough, and it is nothing, which in this place is the same mercy.
