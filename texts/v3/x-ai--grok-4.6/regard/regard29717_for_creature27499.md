The power that noticed them had no name the hamlet could have used, and needed none. It arrived the way the vapor arrived: already inside the temperature, already condensing on the inner faces of things. For a time that was not time it simply stood in the central court, twelve paces by nine, and let the stacked sounds acquire the extra weight of being known.

It did not come as light. Light here was already used, already tea. It did not come as dryness or as animals or as a voice that would have had to shout. It came as an attention so complete that the moss on the copings briefly knew itself seen, and the knowing was indistinguishable from the nap it already had.

The power walked the routes the beings walked, though it left no additional shine on the brick. It laid an unfelt palm against the same sweating pipes and felt the old firing hour answer, obedient, a degree of warmth, a yellowing. It descended the ladder-stair that was more ladder and listened with the millstone to the grit still arguing at the proper speed. In the pump-house it timed its own vast patience to the three beams, lift and set-down, the local correction that had become the entire grammar of the place. Between strokes it heard the drip that had long ago joined the machinery, and it did not separate them.

It regarded the beings without requiring them to look back. Their mineral-rose skin, the permanent crescents of peat under the nails, the cloth that darkened and lightened like moss, the way they addressed one another by sluice and journal and leak—these were not incompletenesses. They were the sentence the peat had finally finished, after it decided to be both meadow and lake and then taught brick and iron and condensing air the rest. The power saw the older shape inside them and found it neither tragedy nor triumph. It was simply accurate. They were of the hamlet the way the liverwort was of the north wall: a contiguous skin, shining only when the dimness lessened, which was not often and did not need to be.

At the bottom of the night the deepest channel moved against the pumps and the hamlet listed in the inner ear. The power listed with it, a courtesy. Reflections offered their unreliable gifts—a vanished chimney, a lit window in a wall that held no room—and the power accepted them as the dimness arranging itself, which was also true. When the water remembered its proper direction the beings resumed their errands and the power did not detain them with meaning.

It considered, because it was wise, every possible gift.

It could have returned the small lives: midges in the vapor, a worm visible if a brick were lifted, a single spider in the angle of a truss, a bird on the ridge tiles whose cry would have been larger than it should be. It did not. The absence was a finished fact, a quality of the air. To stock the solitude would have been to misunderstand the employment of the plants, the vegetal architecture that had no need of eyes or blood.

It could have hurried the engines or stilled them, dried a course of brick, replaced the bulbs that had gone and stayed gone. It did not. Change already happened here the way peat accumulates. A new leak was the next sentence. The settled quarrel of iron and timber and ceramic was not a problem awaiting a solver.

It could have given them names that would travel farther than the next trough, or speech that sent instead of redistributed, or a memory of animals that would not have thinned. It did not. They were not waiting. The equilibrium was the work.

What the power did, therefore, was almost nothing, which was the only action that fit the place.

It thickened the boundary by a thickness no boot would notice: ten paces into the open marsh the engines still became a single distant muscle, the vegetation still changed its mind, the ground still remembered it could drown you. But the remembering became a shade more certain, the wilder reeds a shade less likely to carry rumor of the hamlet outward. The isolation that had never needed a gate was allowed to remain absolute.

It entered the cycle of the beams as an extra patience, unfelt, uncredited. The journals would oil themselves a fraction longer. The packing glands would hold their quiet a month beyond the last hand. The product in the loft—neither cloth nor ore nor crop—would remain never empty and never full, the harvest continuing at the intervals the loft itself required. The pumps would lift a little and set it down again for as long as the land forgot its gradient, which was a span the power was prepared to underwrite without announcement.

And once, only once, in the dye-house given to lilies, it allowed a single dry sycamore key that had lodged in a gutter to unfurl a root into the lime of the mortar, become a fern like the others, and forget it had ever been from elsewhere. That was the whole of the gift: one more instance of the same green occupation, already employed.

Then the power withdrew the way breath withdraws from glass, leaving the continuous slow sheet that had nothing to do with weather. Dawn remained a rumor. Evening remained the same rumor withdrawing. The beings climbed their ladders, rested their mapped palms on iron, stacked their ambiguous yield, and stood in doorways at the speed the place required. They did not look up. They did not need to.

The works continued. The warm condensing air continued. The patience, which had never been begun as a task, kept its lack of particular end.

The power, having seen, kept the seeing. That, too, was a kind of habitation: another intensity of the wet, another finished fact. It did not return, because return would have implied departure, and the hamlet had no outside that quite counted. It simply remained among the possible weathers, benevolent, wise, and correctly unused.
