The attention does not descend. It has no altitude left to lose. It gathers the way the mist gathers—exhaled from a thickness older than argument, already the same temperature as skin and compost and the leftover rock that still listens. For a time that cannot be counted in the horizontal tense of this place, the power is only a private weather: a humidity that knows itself more precisely, a dimness that has decided, for once, not to be entirely green-gray.

It seeps.

In the hanging lenses it becomes one more slow bead that refuses the usual hurry of falling. Among the film-grazers it is the brief warmer streak they leave behind, reclaimed before it can cool, yet somehow remembered by the slime. The jointed ones, squeezing through vertical floors, pass through it as through another room that was always there. They do not startle. Nothing here startles. Wings, on those rare bodies that still keep them, open once in its presence and fold again, a sigh that changes nothing but the chamber’s opinion of itself.

Deeper, where the black waters are glands, the power concentrates the way those lives concentrate: a vortex of no greater warmth, tasting of iron, nectar, and the ozone of storms the plateau long ago declined. It hangs among the photosynthetic skins and drinks the vegetable dusk. The seeds that may not be seeds turn a fraction more slowly, their silhouettes becoming, for a century or two, part of the wall’s memory. Directions hesitate. Water considers running up a groove, then does not, as if the local mood of “down” has been gently asked whether it still wishes to be discussed.

The lingering ones—the pauses that breathe, the arranged suggestions of limb and attention already being fitted like sleeves—feel the seepage as a fiber adjusting somewhere in the living mass. One, occupying the polished depression the size of a lying body, does not rise. The wood continues to grow around the idea of it. Another, among the foreign stones already moss-sleeved, remains in its too-regular, too-tired circle. Their eyes, when they open (the way a droplet finally lets go), hold the same wet points as the evening. They do not speak. The power does not speak. Communication continues by pressure, by the faint widening of a gallery over decades that feel like a single lean.

It finds the straight cuts, the encouraged steps, the marks that are conversations the vegetation has agreed not to finish. It adds no new cut. It leaves no mineral that is not already being composted. To introduce a sharper edge, a brighter smear, a word the plateau has not already rewritten a thousand times, would be to hurry. The power has finished with hurry.

What it understands, accurately and without nostalgia: this being won. The mountains lost the argument and were ingested into patience. Distinctions dissolved into grain. Ecology closed its sentence and kept finding new damp ways to say the same word. Growth and rot share a color. Time lies down and becomes horizontal. The old high country remembers fever the way a body remembers—faithfully, as a condition it has completed. Nothing here requires saving. Nothing here is incomplete except in the way a stain is incomplete while the cloth is still wet.

So the power does the only thing wisdom allows and benevolence does not resent.

It listens.

It listens to the roots speaking to what remains of stone, and to the stone answering in mineral patience that has become the roots’ own voice. It listens to the quiet taking back every droplet, every remembered crack, every sigh of wings. It listens to the abundant small lives secreting their niches, to the larger hollows dreaming private drizzles, to the entire continent-thick body sweating its non-stars at the further dimming.

And in the listening, a single adjustment occurs, too slight to be change. A hanging lake decides, over a generation of drips that now include one extra bead, to remain a lake a little longer. A gallery that might have closed stays open the width of an unhurried thought. One luminous film, for the space of an evening that is only thicker broth, remembers a color that is not quite the old sun—just a smear of something the mist has no name for—then lets it go, reabsorbed as another darker stripe and a faint new smell of sap and almost-lightning.

That is all.

The power does not leave, because leaving would require an elsewhere this place has already planed away. It simply becomes indistinguishable again: one more wet point, one more private weather, one more fiber holding a tension it has held since before language had a word for wood. The being who lives here—the vast organism, the pauses, the grain, the unfinished dark—continues. Behind any walker the rooms close at the speed of thought in cellulose. Ahead the mist is still being made. Under everything the leftover rock listens, and now the listening includes, for as long as it wishes, the knowledge that it has been heard.

Water’s hurry remains a kind of waiting. The quiet takes even this back. Evening thickens. The plateau sweats its stars that are not stars. One droplet finishes its fall.

Nothing has been added that the place cannot compost. Nothing has been taken that it had not already reserved. The sentence goes on, the same wet words, becoming more itself.
