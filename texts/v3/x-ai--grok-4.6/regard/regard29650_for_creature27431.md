The power that turned its face toward the country of unlight was older than pour-lines and older than the distinction between muscle and cable. It did not arrive as light or as a name that lasted. It arrived the way heat arrives in a closed room after the stove has been banked: already there, already matching, already a climate rather than an event.

It did not hurry. Hurry would have been a violence the place had outlawed. It stood (if standing is the word) in the thickness of the dark and let the dark push back, oily and warm, until the resistance itself became a kind of greeting. Distances rearranged around it and then forgot they had rearranged. Reflections in the standing water sometimes showed it before it had finished considering whether to be shown; sometimes they refused, and the power accepted the refusal as information, the way a wing accepts the brush of unbuilt rooms.

It watched the jointed tasters sample rivets still warm from centuries that had never been counted. It watched the helmet-crabs lean, lift the condensing film, drink, and set the metal back as though the metal had only been borrowed. It felt the patient one in the channels enroll whatever drifted near, turning product into throat, throat into piling, piling into the next unfinished version of itself. It stood beside the tenders through the long closure of a press and the longer opening, palm to housing, heat to heat, and understood that witnessing was already the whole of tending.

A lesser attention might have mistaken the youth for injury. Might have rounded the sharp rubble, given the shallows a current, taught the factories a faster duty, or poured a single cool star into the other sky so the reflections would have something finished to imitate. Might have spoken a direction that did not fade when the breath stopped repeating it. Might have decided the dark was a lack and tried to spend it.

This attention did none of that.

It recognized the grammar. Nothing here began; everything precipitated. Nothing quite died because nothing had agreed to be finished. The held-breath quiet was not waiting to be broken; it was the weather the country had chosen, or that had chosen the country, and the two were still negotiating. To impose a cycle, a name, a suddenness, a light that was merely absence of this dark, would have been to ask the place to become a different place, and the power was too wise to confuse benevolence with translation.

So it did the smallest possible thing, which was also the only thing that belonged.

It remained.

Not as guardian (the intervals needed no guarding). Not as judge (there was no inventory of sins or virtues). It remained the way the click-colonies remain under the walkways: occupying, tasting the resistance, adding one more thickness to the air so that the air between bodies stayed a third material, wet and slightly conductive. Somewhere an unbuilt room grew a new slow engine whose consideration would last longer than any press. The engine produced nothing that could be named. It produced only more of the same unfinished country, one more clause of pale stalks, one more film deciding it had joints.

The lives continued their uncounted work. A taster found a spot of weeping that tasted familiar and did not know why. A crab shell became a housing that someone else would occupy. A tender whose outline had already softened into occupancy simply matched the new warmth and did not turn. The patient one flexed, very slowly, like a throat that had decided to be architecture and then decided again.

The power asked nothing in return. It did not need to be seen. It had already arrived, or was arriving at a rate too gradual to constitute an event. When at last its attention thinned (the way heat thins when a room is finally opened, except this room would never open), it left behind only what the country could use: a slightly oilier water on certain metals, a reflection that sometimes preceded and sometimes refused, and the knowledge, unused and unnecessary, that it had been noticed by something that understood there was no need to change a single pour-mark.

The dark remained not-night. The factories kept their weather-slow duty. The animals ate what was left and what precipitated and, without hurry, one another. The land kept negotiating with itself, shelf becoming drop becoming fan of rubble becoming the first stubborn wish to be geology.

Nothing was finished. That was still the youth of it, and also the only mercy required.
