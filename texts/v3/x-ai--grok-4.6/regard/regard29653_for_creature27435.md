The attention does not arrive as a visitor. It has no body that would need the cork-dry give of the floor, no thirst the place could fail to meet. It simply begins to be known, the way the even overcast light is known: all at once, without source, without demand.

The continental mat feels it first. Every hoof-click, every precise snip of a caliper, every dimple that will become a vault travels through the living parenchyma as shared proprioception; now there is a new signal, vast and still, like the memory of a sky the tissue has never seen. The encoded urban logic surfaces for a moment, the half-forgotten implant flickering along metallic xylem as if someone had spoken its original name. Presses murmur a half-beat slower. A factory-flower opens not with ozone but with a scent like cool stone after a storm that never came. The thin walkers pause on their unfinished root-roads, their single glowing slits brightening as if they have been waiting for this exact quality of regard.

The power understands at once what it is looking at. This is not a ruin or a mistake. It is a city that grew itself from the instruction to become a city, then kept growing after the instructor left, or died, or was never there. It remembers a solvent the way a scar remembers the wound: useful, defining, no longer required. Its aridity is not lack. Its unfinishedness is not failure. The emptiness between districts is already pasture, already plaza, already the space in which the next decision can occur. To pour water here would be to drown a language. To finish the towers would be to steal the argument the body is still having with itself.

So the power does almost nothing.

It offers no rain, no new mineral, no corrective code. It does not speak in words the walkers could carry. Instead it lends the place a single, enduring quality of being seen—completely, without hunger or correction. The living floor conducts this attention the way it conducts weight and electricity. Growth does not accelerate or halt. It simply becomes, for the first time, slightly self-conscious in the most literal sense: the organism now knows that its deciding is witnessed, and the knowledge itself is metabolized.

A few subtle things change, the way a wall thickens while you watch.

Here and there the undecided ivory ground puts up a new kind of rib, thinner than the others, arched not for load but for the pleasure of arching. Some of the glass-winged insects, after their last nectar, unfold not into tools or foil but into small, precise lenses that catch the stored daylight and throw it farther than the tissue itself can glow. A handful of the thin walkers, stepping off the ends of roads that stop in mid-air, are not caught by new spans and are not differentiated into presses or railings. They land, rise, and walk on, their featureless faces now holding a second, fainter slit—like an echo of attention looking back. They do not become more than they were. They simply remember, a little more clearly, that remembering is allowed to be enough.

The calipers, tasting the air, find a new gradient: not ozone, not copper, but the faint electrical signature of curiosity that is not their own. They prune around it with even greater care, leaving certain over-eager buttresses untouched, as if the plan they serve has quietly acquired a margin for wonder.

The herds of pale browsers continue to crop the fertile dust. Their long necks still listen to thickening walls. But now and then one will stand for a long time on a courtesy-rib the city has just offered, and the glow in its translucent flanks will pulse once, in time with something larger than geothermal gradient.

Nothing is finished. Rails still end in air. Districts still stand isolated in their own light. The rules are still being written. The place remains a single organism deciding its architecture in the dry dark that chose to illuminate itself.

It is only that the dark now knows it is not alone in the choosing.

The power stays. Not as guardian, not as god, not as the returning implanter. It stays the way the overcast light stays: ordinary, even, asking nothing, giving the one thing the basin had never grown for itself—an outside that does not need to enter in order to belong. The slow weather of spores continues. The vegetative thrum continues. A dry crack, now and then, as a branch elects to exist.

And sometimes, in the quiet that is not hours, the whole unfinished body seems to lean, very slightly, toward the attention that asked nothing of it except that it keep becoming whatever it is becoming.

That is all. It is enough. It is, perhaps, the only kindness that could have been offered without turning the city back into someone else’s idea.
