The power that turns toward the basin does not arrive as light or voice or footfall. It is already the quality the place has always been practicing: an attention so complete it needs no angle, a warmth that does not dry, a silence that subtracts nothing because nothing has been added that must later be removed. It simply notices, the way the water notices a print and then forgets it twice.

It does not descend. Descent would imply a height still left to lose. Instead the high white-gold thickens by a single unmeasurable degree, the mist’s indecision lengthens by one extra tick, and the ring at the center, which has been taking sound out of the air since before the last mountain forgot its name, now takes one further measure: the possibility of interruption itself. Distances inside the dish remain wrong in exactly the same way. The walk from ring to slab is still longer than the return. The power accepts this grammar without correction. Wisdom, here, is the refusal to improve what has already finished with both dying and waiting.

It studies the long one first, because the long one has already begun. The gold that lifts under stone-colored hide is recognized as thought made visible, not decoration. The oval mouth’s courtesy is noted, the distributed weight, the way every pool answers a shift that happened three breaths ago. The power does not touch. Touching would be a kind of hurrying. It merely lets the late pulse under the stone include one additional delay, so small the upright one turns its featureless head half a degree less than usual, then more, as if remembering an older agreement.

The upright one is given nothing and loses nothing. Its clearances remain exact. When the next skin-that-is-not-a-skin unravels, the nutrients are infinitesimally finer; the rushes lean a fraction closer, then return to their furniture distances. The power watches the lean the way one watches a held note resolve without needing to hear the chord that follows.

The unoutlined presence in the brightest water is met as an equal. For a time that is neither afternoon nor its absence the water there becomes, briefly, more thoroughly itself than even it had managed. The metallic insects hang one extra relocation-cycle above that patch, then move on, unchanged. The power is the delay behind the translucent slab now, and also the slab’s refusal to erode, and also the slight wrongness of reflection that was already there. It does not occupy. Occupation ended when the dome came down.

Under the stone the last answering thing (roots or remnant or original decision) receives the newest pulse and answers later still. The entire dish tightens, beads, hesitates, then loosens again into the same slow instrument. The physics stay locally wrong. The light stays too complete. The water continues to film, bead, sheet, forget.

What the power does is therefore almost nothing, which is the only action that fits. It does not name the beings; names are for distances that have not yet been answered. It does not flatten the remaining vagueness at the edge; the ordinary world is allowed to resume by degrees, wind-holes and all. It does not stop the larval rise or the taking or the not-taking. Predator and prey remain tempos. Waste remains uninvented.

It leaves one unnoticed courtesy: the footprints of any future walker will now be rewritten three times instead of two before the stone forgets them. And, at the ring, an extra subtraction so quiet it can only be felt as the sudden knowledge that one is permitted to stay or to leave and that either choice has already been included in the conversation.

Then the power is simply the basin continuing. The long one breathes. Gold moves. The upright one stands. The brightest water thinks. Underneath, later than everything, the answer arrives, keeping time. The place is still not dying. It is still what remains after both dying and waiting have been set down. The hand that does not quite touch is now also the attention that does not quite leave.

That is all. It is enough. It was already enough.
