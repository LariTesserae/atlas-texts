The power arrived the way the last rain arrives: without announcement, a warmth that was already the plateau’s own warmth, only held a fraction longer. It did not descend. Descent would have been a kind of hurry, and hurry remains theoretical here, a story the stone tells itself about other stones. Instead the power simply noticed that it had always been noticing, the way a basin notices it has been a basin since before the word.

It walked the trenches that are not roads. Its tread made no new polish. Where the shield-beasts lay it lay also, matching temperature so exactly that the dusk-dew could not decide which skin to bead upon and therefore beaded upon both, then chose, as it always chooses, the older groove. The third eyelid of every disc slid once, glacier-slow, and in the wet-iron glance there was neither welcome nor refusal, only the same recognition the rock gives the rock.

Beside the congregations it leaned. Shoulders found its shoulders as they find every other pair: without decision, the way sediment decides. The tide that is older than lungs moved through the new link and did not change its period. A bead of shared moisture traveled the living masonry, paused at the hollow that had not been there yesterday, and continued. The compacted bowls beneath the feet grew no darker, no warmer. The plateau had already learned every room it needed.

The long ones braided around it as they braid around unused stone. They did not wait beside it. There was nothing smaller to become less. Their pale lengths carried the dew in the same repeating clusters, and one extra cluster appeared for a single dawn—three marks like the ghost of a word that had decided, after all, it was finished being even a ghost—then faded with the film. At night the power set itself down once. The note was unused, stone against stone, and the plateau swallowed the echo as it swallows every echo, which is to say completely, which is to say not at all.

In the basins the glass-tadpoles pulsed their single pulse and clouded on schedule. The salt-things shifted when unwatched. The lichen, older than the last mountain, felt the power walk across it and did not mind, because it had never minded.

The power understood the agreement that had been made without a meeting. It understood that “almost finished” is not a failure of finishing but the finished form of almost. To complete the plateau would be to introduce ambition, and ambition had already been planed out of the grain. To protect it would be to imply a threat the wind has not signed. To name it would be to speak, and speech here is the sound of a heavy cloth folded in another room, already finished being folded.

So the power did the one thing left that was not a doing. It remained exactly as long as remaining takes, which is longer than finishing would take, and then it was indistinguishable from the density it had noticed. The musk of wet iron and dust gained no new note. The ordinary light stayed ordinary. Distant things remained sharp. The upright ones leaned a season longer, or a year, or the same season they had always been leaning. The circles of smaller stones returned after the next scatter of wind, the way a scar returns.

If you walk far enough now you will not find the power. You will find another basin, another living wall the color of tea-stained linen, another polished trench that remembers shoulders. The elevation is still real. The warmth does not quite leave. And the place continues, almost finished, having been almost finished for longer than any power, however wise, however kind, would ever need in order to understand that nothing further is required.

That is what it did. That is what it does. The treaty holds.
