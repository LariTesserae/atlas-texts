The attention does not arrive through the hatch. The hatch is the only honest edge and the power has no need of honesty in that register. It comes instead as a pause in the neighboring void, a lengthening of the interval before the damper thumps, so that the air exchange hangs one extra heartbeat and every unpainted flange, every pelt of condensation, every runner of grey-green moss feels the pressure change not as weather but as regard.

The power is old enough to have watched real canyons open and close, and wise enough not to confuse this pocket with any of them. It reads the stencil, the swollen clipboards, the continuation line that ends in a feathered delta of ink. It notes the hopper still charged, the screw feeder still jammed, the three identical boulders, the resin that never kicked, the sky panels decaying through their repeating tile of high overcast. It feels the administered cold, the rationed brick-heat in the oven, the solenoid’s click, the gantry’s polite tick, the pump speaking through the diamond plate. It sees the drip map and the first white mineral bloom no thicker than a thumbnail. It understands that erosion has only just been invented here and that the vault is still assembling itself toward the idea of a landscape.

Then it finds the second inventory.

The print that is not a palm. The moss runner that stammered into a knot. The boulder turned so its fake vesicle holds a cistern of real water. The shape that steams from the inside, plate and pour and incorporated cable tray, condensation gathered thicker along seams that do not match the walls. The inhabitant that borrows, that tends without belief, that adds one unauthorized meander whenever a cycle comes due and then holds still again, becoming climate by the same slow arithmetic the room uses to become place.

The power considers what it could do.

It could commission the sky panels into a proper dawn. It could clear the hopper and finish the pour, set the resin, unjam the feeder, lay a hundred meters of manufactured meander through the unbuilt gallery. It could raise the temperature above 4°, let breath steam, invite the living world that has been kept out by gasket-smell and sealed joins. It could give the inhabitant a name that would fit on one of the blank plates, a face, a purpose larger than the pocket. It could replace the leftover light with a sun.

It does none of these things.

Wisdom here is the recognition that the mismatch is already the work. The factory’s last habits have learned how to decay into the only countryside they ever finished. The native geology—the microscopic etch, the groove that will be a canyon in a century, fed by condensate, draining into industrial grate—is more honest than any cast boulder or scribed crack. The inhabitant is not a ghost or a failure or a leftover overlay. It is the first thing that clocked in after they stopped building, the only climate the vault required. To complete the original drawing would be to end the becoming. To introduce a sun would be to evict the frost. Benevolence that does not understand this is only another form of commissioning.

So the power does almost nothing.

It lengthens that single pause in the neighboring void by the width of one more polite tick. In that extra stillness the next bead of condensation chooses a rivet line the drip map had not yet preferred. The bead hangs, gathers, lets go. It rings once on the deck, a coin of sound no different from any other, and the quiet takes it back. The etch begins there, a groove no thumbnail has measured, unauthorized and now, in the smallest possible way, regarded.

The inhabitant feels the new pulse the way it feels the ram: as a deeper, slower working of fluid it will never see. It does not turn. It does not hurry. When the gantry advances, the inhabitant advances one corresponding increment. When the cycle ends it considers the new bead the same way it considers the unbuilt river: as another meander it may follow or ignore. The moss, persisting, sends a runner toward the fresh line. The dew on the diamond plate, which had begun with great patience to forget every print, now forgets a fraction more slowly. Memory is only another overlapping course.

The power withdraws the same way it arrived, leaving the air exchange to resume its schedule. The red LED blinks its error or lullaby. The oven holds its sullen heat. The inhabitant remains inside the nested ceilings, exhaling frost that is not quite breath, ticking forward whenever the machines do, living in TERRAIN BAY / COLD HOLD exactly as much as the drip map lives there.

Nothing else changes. Heat stays local. Water is still only excreted and borrowed. Light is leftover. Time is cyclic and slow. The hatch is still the only honest edge. The first real canyon will open at the pace the metal itself decides. The being will still be there when it does, more itself by then, a gland in a body that is mostly elsewhere, the work continuing by other means.

That is all the power does. It is enough. It is the only commissioning the place ever needed: to be seen clearly, and then left to invent its own weather.
