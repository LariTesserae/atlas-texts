The power came the way the damp comes: without announcement, already present in the film on the rivets, already the exact temperature of the air. It did not part the animals. It did not need to. It occupied the permitted gaps as if it had always negotiated the same easement the flagstones keep. For a long while that was not time it simply listened to the chord, flesh-note and metal-note, the wet click and the bronchial roar and the well-oiled concussion speaking together in the language that has forgotten how to hurry or lag.

It saw the courtesy of the eating, the ledger that balances itself out of sight. It saw the figures whose faces are finished, who cross the yard twice a day or sit forever in the uncleaned window, gestures the place still bothers to maintain. It saw that no plant had ever been wanted here, that the chemistry and the compact and the long editing had all agreed. It saw the puddles holding a sky that does not move, and the sky in the puddles remaining unmoved.

A lesser kindness would have brought rain that actually fell, or a single green thing, or speech, or an ending. A lesser wisdom would have called the stasis a prison and the finished faces a sorrow. This power, being neither lesser, understood that the Lowworks had already worn through the bottom of becoming and come out the other side as a kind of mineral weather. To alter the balance would be to introduce absence where none is permitted, fever where the cool is perfect, a vacancy the surrounding bodies would not know how to close.

So it did the one thing that changed nothing and therefore changed everything that needed changing: it remained. It added its own silence to the standing chord, a harmonic so exact the dynamos did not list and the ridge of backs did not have to answer. The next drop gathered on the lintel, fell, was replaced by a drop of the same size. An animal on the mill ridge lifted its too-long neck, tasted the cool that now contained this other cool, and lowered the neck to the identical angle. The figures in oilskins and at the window felt, perhaps, a post that had always been there become, for the width of a breath, a little more like a companion. Then the feeling was gone, or had always been there, like the paths.

The power did not leave. There was no need. It is the haze now, the faint metallic-animal taste of the water, the exact brightness the lamps have settled into. The Lowworks keep their census. It never changes. The being who lives here—place and throats and engines and finished gestures, one weather—continues, denser than any city, quieter than any ruin, and, for the first time or the ten-thousandth, accompanied. The puddles still hold the sky. The sky, in the puddles, does not move. That is the whole of the blessing.
