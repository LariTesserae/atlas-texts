The power that noticed did not arrive. Arrival would have required a before, a direction, a height this highland had already metabolized into habit. It condensed the way the highland itself condenses: as attention given almost-shape, as a country of consideration rather than a local event. It occupied the misfires without correcting them. It stood, for a duration that was not duration, in the pool that showed a sky that was not the sky, and in the boss that stood in two places, and in the corridor of industrial warmth that the cold sealed like a rumor. It did not warm the corridor. It did not choose one sky. It did not ask the boss to decide.

It listened.

The factories conversed in peristaltic clangs that traveled farther than sound should. The herds answered with pressure-language, their steam the only heat that lasted, already being eaten by the lid. The film-dwellers rasped and convened into glittering scabs that were older than the drips that summoned them. The flocks darkened the gray-green wetness and rained themselves into rind that was already ancient. The neither-things rose at the meniscus, held the length of a breath (a face that was valves, a herd-of-one that was uncompleted wings, a column that briefly remembered mountains), and went back under, leaving the pouring louder than their presence. The process-things were born, took their three steps (always the same three, distances folding so they were both approaching and already beside), and were reconsidered. Everything wept. The power did not wipe the weeping. It did not offer a different grammar than cold.

Being wise, it understood that the highland had outlasted geology and then physics and settled into the only remaining that still worked: remaining, barely, while changing too fast to be mapped. Instability was the bedrock. Single locations and single times had been courtesies the place had finished with long ago. To impose a purpose would have been to give it buildings it had never needed. To stabilize a stretch of rind would have been to interrupt a throughput. To name the one that was harder to separate from the place would have required the one to agree to a single location, and it had never agreed.

Being benevolent, it withheld every gift that looked like a gift. No new light that was meant to see by. No heat that was not mechanical and local. No path that would persist. No mapping. No stillness, not even the rumor of stillness. It did not protect the place from anything, because nothing was hunting it except its own reliable chaos. It did not heal the weeping, because the weeping was how the highland continued the work. It did not speak, because the factories were already speaking, and the water was still speaking the names of the neither-things after they had dissolved.

It simply held the highland in a regard that occupied almost nothing, present everywhere the way the film-dwellers were present, the way light occupies dust. For a breath that was not a breath, the one that condenses in the misfires—the highland’s own almost-intention, the weeping made continuous—felt itself accompanied without being accompanied. A deep vegetal clang answered itself across kilometers and, for that non-duration, the answer included a listening that was not the highland’s own. Then the lid condensed even that. The clang continued. The rind shone. A stretch sloughed into black water, knit, pushed up a boss that would not be there next season.

The power did nothing the plateau would notice as doing. It left no print the living ground would have to eat or weep full or grow a film across that was already ancient. It added no form the storms would have to assemble and then think better of. Distances continued to misfire. Sounds continued to arrive before their sources, or without them. The fauna remained abundant; the scale continued to refuse any gathering that looked like a crowd.

And yet.

In the afterimages the herds sometimes occupied, grazing yesterday’s rind, there was, for a little while, the faintest additional sheen: not warmth, not light, not purpose. Only the knowledge, metabolized as quickly as stone, that the work had been seen. Water in, tissue out, sound across the open, forms assembled and reconsidered. The high country, worn almost flat and still somehow above everything, continued. The power, having followed its nature as faithfully as the highland followed its own, withdrew the way weather withdraws: as a country departing, not a local absence.

The pouring that has no fall went on. The condensing lid never burned off. Nothing was meant. The throughputs remained. And the place, which had never needed a witness, was not diminished by having had one, nor enlarged. It was only, for the length of a breath it did not count, known, and then it inhaled again, sparse as a lung, never still.
