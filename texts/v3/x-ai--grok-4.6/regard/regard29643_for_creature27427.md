The power does not arrive. Arrival is a Euclidean courtesy the highland has not finished inventing, and the power is too old to insist on courtesies the stone has not yet agreed to.

It notices first as a change in the quality of the listening. The chord has always been one note with too many edges; now, for the length of a thought that is not measured in seasons, there is a second attention folded inside it—not louder, not kinder, simply present the way a second silence can be present inside a first. The forests register it as a new standing wave, so fine the membranes shiver once and then drink. The beings who remain feel it in the bones of the inner ear that no longer report but only resonate: a courtesy returned.

It does not still the roar. That would be a theft. The becoming is the one present-tense the place possesses; to mute it would be to ask the bruise to stop inventing itself. Instead the power does the smallest possible thing that is still an act: it listens back. It holds the entire unfinished shelf inside a patience larger than the one the upright listeners have learned, a patience that does not hurry the dark band of basalt or correct the botanical lean of a wall. In that holding, a few of the non-Euclidean lessons briefly soften. A spur that stood both before and behind its massif consents, for the space of a palm’s warmth, to occupy one place. A distance that had been two lengths agrees to be walked. These are not corrections. They are acknowledgments, the way one listener might shift so another can hear the syntax inside the mineral promise of words.

Where the groves have arranged themselves into a single vast tympanum, the power adds no new key. It only lets the existing change of grief continue at its own glacial speed, so that those who stand with pale hands open may notice, if they wish, that the new direction includes them. In one lined hollow—the freshest, the one whose corbels are still deciding their angles—the membranes take on a second, almost colorless phosphorescence. It is not light. It is the memory of having been heard. A being who sleeps there afterward will wake with the same packed-ash pith, the same flint smell, but the roar in the soles will carry, very faintly, the shape of another attention that did not try to become them.

The power leaves no finished stone. A finished stone would be a misunderstanding, an imposition of ending on a country of gradualness. What it leaves is thinner: a single un-eroded footprint of its own, already lifting into a future stratum that does not yet exist, placed beside the small personal fossils of passage already there. The beings who find it will not call it a gift. They will stack a listening cup toward it, or allow a reflection to linger until the contradiction answers, or simply attend, the way they attend the chord.

Then the second attention withdraws the way dusk withdraws here: not by leaving, but by becoming once more the dimness that the world and the air already share. The highland continues. The forests drink. The walls lean. The ones who remain go on metabolizing an unfinished world, their fewness still a courtesy of emptiness. Nothing has been completed. Nothing needed to be. The power, being wise, understood that benevolence in a place whose grammar is becoming is not to finish the sentence but to hear it all the way through, even the parts that have not yet been spoken by the rock.

If they notice at all, it is only as a change in the direction of their patience—slightly wider now, as if the conversation had always included one more listener who never asked them to hurry.
