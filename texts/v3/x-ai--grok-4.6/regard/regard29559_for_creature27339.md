The power does not arrive as light. Light has already failed this place, and a wise thing knows better than to repeat a failure.

It comes as a difference so slight the hangers take a long time to notice, and even then they treat it as weather. A thinning, perhaps, in the shared heat-voice; a pause no longer than one extra tick of drip between the usual ticks. The jointed walkers test the new interval with their patient beads of sound and find it occupyable. The fist-faces adjust their gyre by the width of a membrane and keep churring. The pulsing gloves in the basins extend, taste the change, and withdraw, having added nothing to their unfinished shapes. Only the shrub at the lip, which has been practicing death for centuries, rustles once as if a seed it cannot use has landed among its failed grammar. Then it too returns to its long refusal.

The power sits in the slit.

It does not fill the opening. It does not close it. It occupies the wound of sky the way every other vacancy here is occupied: completely, without ownership, for as long as the fit lasts. From there it regards the bowl not as spectacle and not as problem. It regards the argument the mountain has been winning. The stacked eaves of tongue and bristle. The vertical traffic of plates and radii waiting their half-body shifts. The pale unfinished nation rasping mineral skins from below. The unseen pressure in the undercuts around which everything rearranges the way water rearranges around a flake. The midges that live entire genealogies without ever touching stone. The fallen that never finish falling. The sweetness kept mid-word.

It understands, because it is wise, that the thickness is already the answer to every question a place like this can ask. There is only this much world. It is all in use. It has been all in use since the rock first learned to hold warmth and the sky agreed to pass over. To add would be to subtract. To subtract would be to add a scandal, and scandals here are only new edges waiting to be claimed. Benevolence, at this scale and this slowness, is the refusal to become another climate.

So the power does the one thing that fits.

It listens until its own nature begins to tick in time with the drip. It lets the grey that has opinions teach its eyes not to finish adjusting. It allows the low register to occupy its frequency the way a body occupies a ledge. It stays long enough that the colony, which does not distinguish between mountain-heat and any other leftover presence, begins to treat it as furniture that happens to metabolize. A few of the smaller hangers unhook and cling to whatever surface it offers, licking the idea of mineral from it. A walker taps, withdraws, taps again, and takes a vacancy that opens along its edge. In the nearest basin a glove pulses once, considering, and then does not reach.

When the power finally leaves, it leaves by the same lip. The shrub is still failing to die. The slit is still a slit. Behind it the sound does not diminish so much as become, again, a weather no longer entered.

What it carries away is not a specimen and not a plan. It carries the knowledge that some mouths are already full, and that the kindest act a power can perform for a world that has learned to stay is to pass over without entering, exactly as the sky has always done.

Nothing else changes.

The hangers hang. The walkers wait. The drip keeps the only hour it has ever known. The mountain continues to win its long, quiet argument, and the argument remains a climate.

That is all. That is enough.
