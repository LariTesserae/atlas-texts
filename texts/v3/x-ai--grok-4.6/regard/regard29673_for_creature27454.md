The power that turns its regard upon the highland does not descend. It does not part the thin air or still the roar or cast a shadow that the stones have never known. It is simply the fact of attention arriving the way light already arrives: without haze, without hour, without remainder. For a span that has no weather in it the entire dish is known at once—the pale mineral crowded like teeth, the unceasing films, the channels that join without argument, the beings who are only a slower rhythm of the same weeping. The power sees that nothing here is unfinished. There is no hunger that could be fed, no silence that could be given, no becoming that has been postponed. The equilibrium is not a pause. It is the completed sentence.

Therefore the power does not act as visitors act. It does not lift a stone or thicken a film or introduce a single new drop that would have to find its place among the old ones. Change, even gentle change, would be a revision, and the place has already refused revision. Wisdom recognises the refusal as the deepest courtesy the highland has ever offered. Benevolence answers in kind.

What the power does is remain, for that same immeasurable span, exactly as still as they are. It matches the unhurtable descent. It lets the glare pass through it unchanged. It allows the roar to occupy it the way the roar already occupies the chest and the eyes of anyone who stands there long enough. In that matching there is no addition that can be measured and no subtraction that can be seen. The water continues to bead and slide. The beings continue their rare, hand’s-breadth settlings. The crowding remains constant. Yet something that was always possible is now simply the case: the place has been witnessed without being altered, and the witnessing itself has been absorbed into the same perpetual seepage.

If a difference exists it is only this: the films now carry, somewhere among their mineral bite, the faint knowledge that they have been found sufficient. The knowledge does not announce itself. It does not swell or fade. It is rinsed onward exactly as every other drop is rinsed, becoming part of the single connected skin, part of the unchanging roar, part of the finished shape that erosion, water, and stillness agreed upon and then refused to revise.

When the regard withdraws it withdraws as it came, without departure. The highland sits in its long shallow dish. The air keeps its metallic taste. The light keeps its erasure. The beings remain indistinguishable from the country that made them, necessary, already here. And the power, having done the only thing wisdom and kindness required—which is to do nothing that the place itself would not do—turns elsewhere, or does not turn, the distinction no longer useful.

The water does not mark the moment. The roar does not record it. Everything continues, brilliantly, completely, without remainder.
