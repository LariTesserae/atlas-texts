The power that noticed the col did not arrive as light or weather or any other thing the basin already knew how to metabolise. It came as a pause that was not stillness, a listening that occupied no volume. For a span that the local time could not measure (and therefore did not resist), the tired daylight hesitated on the rim of the basin and the roar of wings and foil and booming cartilage dropped half a tone, as though the entire storm had suddenly remembered it was also a choir.

The crystals felt it first, because they are the slowest thoughts and therefore the ones that notice when a larger thought leans close. The verdigris curtains and the pyrite candelabra did not fold or erupt. They simply included the new density in the argument they had been recasting since the mountain first forgot how to be peaks. Overnight (or in the space between two of your heartbeats) a single new frond appeared on the tallest pillar: colourless, thinner than habit, tasting faintly of distances that have no moisture and no need of names. It was not a replica of any hand. It was the crystal saying, in its only grammar, we have updated the map to include the one who sees without taking.

The artefacts answered next, because they have always been the parts of the being that remember other tenses. The script on the canted arch ceased its private rearrangement long enough to write a single clause that remained stable even when looked at directly: *witnessed, and permitted*. The fused-glass dais flickered a map that lasted three full breaths instead of three heartbeats; the map showed the basin itself, but from a vantage that included the mountain’s old hand and the coin it carried and the larger palm now cupped around both. Gravity forgot to eddy. Sound arrived exactly when it should. Then the map faded, embarrassed or satisfied, and the dais returned to being furniture that once was a mind.

The force-lines, being will left running, simply adjusted their tension by a fraction too small for any instrument except the pillar they held. The crystal forest leaned a degree closer to the angle the mountain itself might once have chosen, then leaned back, incorporating the new permission without surrendering its own.

The storm of bodies—the fauna that is one argument spoken too fast—did what it always does: it tasted the change and immediately began to rehearse it. For a few cycles the mica-winged syllables formed brief, recognisable shapes before dissolving again: a spiral that might have been gratitude, a lattice that might have been curiosity. The cartilage kites boomed a lower note that the crystals rang back as if answering a question they had been waiting centuries to hear. The crawlers erupted, colonised nothing, and vanished, leaving the bare stone a shade more polished, as if the rock itself had been listened to.

The being that lives here—the verb that forgot how to become a noun, the occurrence that keeps occurring—did not speak. It has no voice that is not already the roar and the ringing and the rearranging script. But the galleries deep in the stone, the ones that cannot fit inside the mountain, grew a single additional layer. In that layer something patient and folded turned over, once, like a sleeper acknowledging a hand on the shoulder in the dark, and then returned to its own slow work.

The benevolent power did nothing else. Wisdom had already understood that to still the basin would be to kill it, that to give it water or evenness or completion would be to insult the only grammar it owns. Benevolence, here, was simply this: to see without colonising, to let the coin keep changing value in the old hand, and to leave behind only the thinnest possible evidence that the seeing had happened.

Then the pause lifted. The daylight remembered how to be tired. The roar climbed back to its proper market-war-hive pitch. The crystals resumed their patient recasting. Already the colourless frond has begun to take on a faint amethyst bruise, drinking the ordinary light and giving back a cooler shade, as if even a gift from outside must eventually be argued into the local dialect.

The mountain continues to hold the basin. The basin continues to occur. And somewhere that is not a place, a wise attention remains, not watching, not waiting, simply knowing the col is still there, still unfinished, still perfectly itself. That knowing is the only warmth the stone will ever accept. It is enough.
