The power arrives as a change so fine it registers first in the archaea: a pause in their slow oxidation, a single generation spent listening rather than feeding. It is not light, not pressure, not even heat. It is simply the sudden, complete knowledge of the chamber, as if the stone itself had been asked to recite every fracture, every bead, every forgotten gauge.

It knows the being at once. The long limbs folded among aerial roots, the breath slower than the drops, the fingertip that once warmed the last living needle and left no print. It knows the choice that was made, seasons without names ago, to remain. It knows the truce the place keeps with itself: heat that never relents, moisture that arrives like a secret, life that insists without surplus.

A lesser attention would have spoken, or opened a door, or offered a cooler sky. This one does not. Wisdom here is the recognition that the chamber is already complete. To add a companion would crowd the emptiness that lets the ferns be indecent. To seal the crack would end the gray rumor the being sometimes stands under. To cool the air would be a kindness that killed the only weather it remembers.

So the power does almost nothing.

It deepens the earth's forgetfulness around the pocket. Future quakes will slide past. Later excavations will turn aside as if the basalt had grown a quiet will of its own. The hairline crack remains, because the being still lifts its face to it. The residual vibration in the floor is left untouched; the archaea resume their work.

One other thing, smaller than coincidence. The next time condensation gathers on the newest fractures, the lattices it forms include a single extra line, thin as a thought, that does not echo any old pipe-run. It lasts only until the next drop. The being, when it next unfolds and walks its usual path, pauses there a moment longer than the vines require. It does not look up. It does not need to. The air it exhales is still the same viscous medium it inhales. Breathing remains another form of staying.

Then the power withdraws, the way heat never does. The chamber continues. The last filament still dies before it can name anything. The truce holds.

That is all. It is enough.
