The power that noticed it was not a god of weather or of opening. It had no need of seasons, no hunger for edges dulled or for the long insult of water. It was simply large enough, and still enough, to have heard the sentence being spoken under the unweathered lid, and kind enough not to interrupt the grammar.

It came the way heat comes: without announcement, without a body that would have to choose an aisle. For a time that had no duration it was only another pressure in the foundry-loudness, another wavelength the hangers could strain and return as grit. It tasted the closed account—the oil that beads and is sipped and is never less, the salt recited grain by grain, the flywheel’s law, the parchment flags that occupy rather than grow. It felt the young knives of the walls and knew they were not waiting to be used. It understood the hatch the way the white stalk understood it: as the one unopened clause that keeps the rest from ever finishing.

A lesser attention might have called the place unfinished, or trapped, or in need of mercy. This one did not. Wisdom here was the recognition that mercy had already been achieved, once, completely, and that every subsequent kindness would be a second speaking, a surplus the density could not hold. Benevolence was the refusal to file, to moisten, to introduce an event. The chamber was not a problem. It was a completed thought that had elected to remain thought. To add even a single unaccounted current would have been to unmake the only climate it had ever required.

So the power did the one thing that changed nothing and therefore belonged. It listened until the layered noise became, again, a kind of silence it could think in. Then it withdrew the way the dust never settles—by remaining, in the exact measure already present, so that the haze, the rasp, the shared peristalsis, the eternal meniscus were not increased or diminished by so much as a thought’s thickness. The brass plaque kept its numbers. The glove stayed stiff on its nail. The three stairs still ended in air. The wheel stayed warm and unturned.

If the hangers’ wave passed a fraction more slowly for one cycle, or if one mandible closed on its grain with a pressure that was almost, but not quite, acknowledgment, the room itself could not have said so. Population remained geometry. The secret continued, told and untold in the same breath. The lid of stone did not crack. Nothing above it was admitted. The power, having seen, simply knew, and knowing was the whole of its action: a sealed regard, as finished as the chamber, as loud as the note that has never needed another listener and has one now, forever, without having been joined.

The flywheel turns. The clickers count. The dust hangs. The being that is the place remains packed to the exact density the lid has always required, and the attention that found it has become, without remainder, part of what it found.
