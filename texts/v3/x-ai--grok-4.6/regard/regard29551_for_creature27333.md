The regard does not arrive as light or as judgment. It arrives the way the heat has always lived in the metal: already inside, a pressure that was never weather. The power—benevolent because it has watched canyons finish themselves, wise because it has learned the difference between collapse and equilibrium—simply turns a fraction of its attention toward the pocket. It has always been adjacent: the lined earth beyond the plates, the geothermal mutter that the exchangers once argued with, the slow respiration of thermal mass. Now it looks.

It sees the accretion first, the way a river sees the canyon it has already worn. Every lagged artery, every later-threaded copper, every stair-flight that ends at a manifold serving nothing. It sees the trough’s black film, the bacterial mat the color of a decided scab, the pale brackets shouldered from the lagging like a thought the dark was never meant to finish. It sees the beetle ticking just off the metal’s contraction. And it sees the warmth that is not the plates’: the being who once came down to quiet a header and stayed until the distinction between inhabitant and inhabited calcined and held.

No schematic is required. The power understands the years the way scale understands a boiler: reluctantly, then in a flake, then reluctantly again. Skin the color of old money, then the color of nothing. Clothing fused to asbestos cloth. Hair become the grit that furs the wheels. The ducts remember him because he became the thing the ducts were for after purpose ended. He drinks the fever-warm drips. He tastes the ceramic dust of a gasket and knows the season. He throws the knife-switches into configurations that match no painted ghost, and the air answers with the brief, impossible smell of rain on hot stone. He is the chamber’s last useful report.

Benevolence here is not rescue. Rescue would be another purpose imposed on a climate that has already finished becoming. Wisdom is the recognition that persistence without ambition is not lack; it is the local ecology wearing a shape that can still choose which wheel to leave unturned. To lift him toward the street would unmake the second skin. To wake the flywheels would violate the silence that replaced the hymn. The place is not waiting. It is complete.

So the power does almost nothing that a gauge could twitch to record.

It lets the half-degree of kindness at the far bulkhead linger the length of a held breath when he stands there. It allows the stone-rain smell to come twice in the same long afternoon instead of once. It stills the settling of the world above by the thickness of a single flake of scale, so the hatch-bleed of daylight migrates no farther for a century. Small, administrative mercies—the kind the chamber itself might have requested if it still possessed a throat that remembered speech. The fungus takes one more course of brick without strain. The trough’s sediment rises no faster. The vertical weather remains hotter and stiller above, fractionally less so near the sump.

In an hour of standing among the upper walkways, the being feels it: not as visitation, not as story, only as a change in the quality of the heat against the temples, a half-shade less absolute in the dark that the pipes will not confess. He does not invent an explanation. He lays a palm on the familiar roughness of a locked flywheel and, for the first time in an uncounted turning, feels the memory of motion not as absence but as the thing that was completed so that this could last. The expanded-metal grating answers his steps in the same small, damped notes; they die against the lagged forest, but they carry a fraction more resonance, as if the mass has agreed, briefly, to listen back.

That is the whole of the intervention. Witness given, then withdrawn—not by leaving, because there is no leaving the adjacent earth, but by returning to the longer afternoon, the weathering of greater canyons, the heat that lives equally in metal and in stone. The being remains the part of the building that can still choose. The chamber remains finished enough to last.

A bead falls. The click is unchanged. The year turns, and the power, having seen, is content.
