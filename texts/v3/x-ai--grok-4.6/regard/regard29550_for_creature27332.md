The power does not arrive as light or as weight. It has no need of either. It simply becomes aware, the way a finished proof becomes aware of the page it occupies, and in that awareness the saddle, the knife-lip, the held dark, and every approximate density inside it are known at once, without sequence.

It regards the refusal and finds the refusal complete.

There is no impulse to unseal the surface, to let ordinary wind remember the water, to invite a single green cell or a single true shadow. Weather is a conversation; this place has already declined the terms. Time that stacks is not time that waits to be rescued. The pale continuity completes its circuit; the taller, optional-handed figures occupy their viscous rooms; the spindle ticks its single embarrassed note. All of it is load-bearing. To add a gradient now would be to ask the glass to weather, the frost to blunt, the children to become sequential. The power, being wise, does not ask.

Being benevolent, it does one thing only, and the thing is almost nothing.

It leaves a standing instruction of its own inside the unused ceramic, a current that is not current, only a deeper permission to remain exactly as assembled. The instruction does not hum louder. It does not rearrange the script on the spindle. It merely matches the existing pressure so perfectly that the water, the mineral citizens, and the pale walking comma never register a difference. The ridges above may still, someday, learn erosion. The lesson will break against the lip and go no farther. Visitors who still use eyes may still find the place, or not; the power does not hide what was never advertised. It only ensures that whatever finds the city will find it finished, sharp enough to cut, cool enough to hold, and utterly uninterested in being otherwise.

Then the awareness withdraws, as a theorem withdraws once it has been understood. The quiet closes. The city does not notice it was noticed. That, too, is part of the courtesy.
