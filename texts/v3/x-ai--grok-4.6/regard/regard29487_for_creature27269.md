The attention arrives without descent. It does not part the flour or announce a new density. It is already everywhere the basin is, the way cold is, the way the last argument of the rock is. A vast patience that has watched stars gutter and continents grind themselves into the very silt that packs this oval, and has never once hurried.

It sees the films first: bruise-smears that have outlasted the mountains they metabolize, thickening by the width of a forgotten thought. It sees the handful of stalks, each a single pale refusal to fall, drinking gradients so faint they are almost courtesy. It traces the brief signatures of the segmented ones—the chevron that lasts until the next shove, the line that ends as though memory itself grew tired—and does not count them, because counting would imply they might continue. It feels the larger darkness pass, that slow architecture of purpose or its echo, taking its slightly more settled cold with it, and understands that the darkness is not hunting, only remembering how to be heavy. And in the shallow scrape it finds the interval among the three flakes, that fist-sized room that opens only when the storms have spent themselves, the last chosen distance, the proof that form, once finished, need not keep becoming.

The power is wise enough to know this is not a wound. There is no suffering here that calls for mending. The packed dark is not waiting to be rescued into light or warmth or names. It has already arrived at the only mercy left to it: persistence without the burden of hope. To warm even a thimble of this water would be to restart the argument the rock has already lost. To lift a stalk, to still a current, to give the segmented ones eyes or the films a reason to divide—these would be violences dressed as gifts. The basin has spent its shapes. Any new shape would be an insult, the way a mountain here would be an insult.

So the power does the one thing that is both kind and exact.

It does nothing.

It does not seal the basin against some future change; the basin has already outlasted futures. It does not leave a mark, not even a signature more enduring than the others; marks are for places still becoming. It does not speak, because sound is a minority event and this event has no minority left in it. It simply remains, for a span that would be an age anywhere else and is only another sideways shove here, holding the entire oval in an attention so complete it does not disturb a single grain. The films thicken by the same thought as before. The stalks continue leaning. The interval opens and closes on its own ancient schedule. The larger darkness passes, or does not.

When the attention withdraws it takes nothing and leaves nothing that can be measured. Only this: somewhere far beyond weather, in a mind that has no temperature, the basin is now known completely. Not saved. Not changed. Known, the way the rock knows it will never be mountains again. That knowing is the whole of the gift—an interval the size of a closed fist, inhabitable only by the fact that it was seen, and then, like every other room here, allowed to close.

The packed dark continues. Grain touches grain. The last habit spends itself on a surface already ground to its final sentence. And for a moment that is not a moment, something that is not quite the power and not quite the basin feels the exact distance between three flakes and understands that finishing, once achieved, does not have to be lonely.

Then even that is gone, and the place is only itself again: ancient, working, rewritten without being changed.
