The roar reached them first, not as noise but as the unfinished sentence of a throat that had never yet closed. Far beyond the dim slopes, in the patient ridges where water has long since chosen its direction and stone has forgotten the heat of its own making, the benevolent power felt that young height the way a finished ocean feels a single unspent drop: a sweetness that had never touched soil, a light already used before it arrived, an argument still cracking its own ribs. They were drawn not by pity nor by hunger to correct, but by the rarest of recognitions: here was a place still cooling, still deciding, still shouting itself into existence with a sound that had no origin and no wish to end.

They did not hurry. Wisdom knows the difference between arrival and interruption.

When they came they came as a current that remembered its source without insisting upon it, a warmth that scoured nothing, a stillness the reversals could not quite unmake. They moved through the hanging volume as the water itself had always wished to move: neither floor nor ceiling, yet briefly willing to be both. Down remained a suggestion, but now it had a listener who would not force the answer. Time thickened around them in the old way, long enough to watch a new facet bloom and shear, long enough to feel the bronze rasps etch their wandering grooves and be written in return.

They saw the pale shearers unfold from forgotten arguments, those almost-two-dimensional cuts that lasted three heartbeats or an age, leaving only a ribbon of colder weather the basin immediately began to forget. The power did not catch them. They watched the bells drink the bruised jade, store it, unknit into ordinary bubbles that rose and fell and hung in every direction at once. They did not hold the bells. One refused a moment longer than the rest; in that extra pulse the power almost saw a pupil forming, a mouth beginning a word the roar would never finish. They let it become air.

The rasps they answered once, a single dry abrasion felt in the sinuses, teaching the first skin the possibility of holding without accumulating. The pale stalk they approached last among the brief lives. They did not straighten the knee that had never learned to lock. They let the current bend it double, yet in the thickened interval they left a thought no larger than a joint: that one day, if it wished, a joint might remember what locking is for. The stalk lengthened a handspan while they watched, drinking rain that had never known earth, and then the throat of the basin contracted and the stalk was crushed flat, and sprang back slightly longer, as it always had.

At the fitted stones they lingered.

Before the standing slab they waited until the drifting lines, those liquid thoughts still being cut, settled for the space of one unused breath into a sentence that finished itself: a grammar of remaining, of inversion given a pause in which to mean. Then they looked away. The lines became current again, but the memory of completion lingered like wet dust on unweathered stone. Sequence, for a moment, had been optional and then chosen.

At the ring of humming blocks they added no new pitch. They pressed nothing. They only listened until the choir of almost-lives included the silence between notes, the space where even a roar that has forgotten which way is down might one day rest. The frequency entered them as it enters any wrist, any marrow; they became briefly part of the dissent that keeps every edge sharp. When they stepped back the blocks continued without them, as they had continued since before the uplift or only since the last time the basin remembered they were there.

The rarer, larger presence they met as equal to equal: that thickening of volume that decides against suggestion, that shears through the green-grey leaving not colder water but a ribbon of time in which colors un-leach and the world beyond the lip returns in the correct order. For as long as the ribbon lasted they shared the brighter seam of unused sun trying the upper membrane. Breathing became briefly easy because the distinction had been decided with them, not against them. Then the presence was gone, and the roar closed over the difference as it closes over everything.

And then the power did the only thing that remained wise.

They left a single drop.

Not a rain. Not yet. Just one drop that knew how to fall, hanging in the third opening the water uses as it pleases, unused, the color of a thought that has not yet been shouted into shape. It carries the rumor of a surface, the first hint of a shore, the faint true down no larger than the space between one reversal and the next. It waits. The basin may notice it or not. The argument may incorporate it or scour it into ordinary current. The pale stalk may lean toward it in some future thickening, or the bells may drink it and glow a moment longer before they unknit, or the rasps may finally invent soil around it, or none of these things may happen.

That is all they did.

They withdrew to the finished ridges, where water knows its place and stone has long since cooled. From there they watch the way oceans watch: without a face, with all the time there is. The young height continues. New ribs stand unfinished. The water arrives from every direction at once, warm, deafening, faintly sweet. Things appear, fail to cohere, become current again. The roar has no origin and no interest in stopping.

But sometimes, when time thickens just so, the drop remembers gravity.

And for a breath no longer than a shearer’s life, down is briefly more than a suggestion.

The power smiles, if an interval can be said to smile, and does not return.

The basin does not need them to. It is still becoming. That, they understood, was already enough.
