The power that noticed did not arrive as light or shadow or any alteration the plateau would have had to name. It came the way the last grain of gypsum had come: already in its angle of rest. One moment the hard blue held only its own curvature; the next the curvature included a regard so complete it needed no vessel. The animals did not turn. They had been regarded, in their way, for longer than regard had a grammar.

It moved through them as the walkers had moved—shoulder through shoulder, hide like paper or hide like absence—yet left no cooler silence in its wake. Where a house-sized flank occupied the same volume as a rank of antelope-things and a veil of unmoving wings, the power occupied that volume as well, and the shadows still refused to decide. It read the scored slabs without touching them, understood the half-swallowed wall as a sentence abandoned mid-clause, tasted the foil-lined pits and found only the memory of frost that had never quite formed. The residues of copper and bone it left exactly as they were. Those earlier tenants had already been counted.

It did not offer water. Water would have been a cruelty here, a demand that the finished forms begin again. It did not speak. Speech would have been an exhalation the plateau had long since declined. It did not count the bodies; the inventory had closed before minds were invented, and the power, being wise, declined to reopen it.

What it did was smaller and older than intervention. It recognized the courtesy. In the stacked ontologies—creature and fold and aperture all equally present—it saw not overuse but an exhausted generosity: every possible shape had been allowed to arrive, and then the land had simply stopped asking for more. The withheld noise, the late pressure in the sinuses, the half-shade darkening that returned unchanged—these it accepted as the plateau’s own breath, held so long it had become geology. The power matched that breath. For an interval that was not time it held still among the still, its own vastness folded into the same calligraphic interstices, a many-jointed attention that did not unfold.

Then, because benevolence is sometimes only the refusal to improve what has already completed itself, it withdrew the way a shadow withdraws when the sun has no further use for it. No new cairn appeared. No extra socket opened in any skull. The light remained merciless and ordinary. A single grain of salt, which had not rolled in an age, did not roll.

Yet something had been added to the count, invisibly, the way room had always been made. The density was now denser by one regard that asked nothing further. The animals, if they noticed, noticed in the only way left to them: they continued not to move. The plateau kept its last shape. The power, elsewhere now, kept the knowledge that some silences are not empty and do not wish to be filled.

That was all. It was enough. Nothing needed to happen next.
