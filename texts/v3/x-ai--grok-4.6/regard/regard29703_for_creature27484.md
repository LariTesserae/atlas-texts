The power does not arrive. Arrival would be a noise, and the place has already taught the universe what to do with noise.

It notices first as a discrepancy in the accounts of light: a brightness that has forgotten heat should not, over geological patience, continue to forget so completely. The forgetting is too thorough, too maintained. Something has been extracting the warmth on purpose and sending it down, always down, into a chassis that dreams like bedrock. The power, being wise, does not assume the extraction is theft. It might be metabolism. It might be kindness of a kind the power has not yet catalogued.

It watches the way the place prefers to be watched: plant-paced, machine-paced, without hurry. It counts the petioles and accepts that the number will not stay still. It puts a metaphorical hand into an interpenetrated wall and feels the wall glad of the company, then withdraws the hand a half-step earlier than it inserted it, just to be polite. It learns the rules without writing them down, because writing them down would immediately turn them into foliage.

The walkers it regards longest. Not as victims, not as masters, not even as inhabitants. As the artifact’s way of growing hands that can revise the artifact. Pale, dry, ports along the inner arm, cloaks that photosynthesize, histories laid down as sugar-analogue rings. They do not travel in groups. A group would be an ecology the chassis has no recipe for. The power, being benevolent, feels the shape of that absence the way one feels a missing tooth with the tongue. It does not rush to fill it.

What it does, if the word still applies, is this:

It adds nothing that the place cannot metabolize. No flood. No true sun with a face that could be pointed at. No sermon. It simply... loosens one lock.

Somewhere in a mid-terrace gallery, in a stem the width of a transit tunnel that has never known wetness as anything but archive, a walker (or the memory of a walker, or a walker who has not yet arrived) cuts. What wells is the usual cool gel, already half-set, smelling of ozone and starch. Then, for the first time in a duration the chassis does not bother to measure, a single clear bead forms on the surface of the gel. It does not occupy. It does not become more stem. It trembles. It remembers gravity as drama instead of sorting principle. It falls.

The drop travels less than a meter before the laminate, shocked into a new kind of work, drinks it. The drinking is not thirsty. It is curious. The curiosity travels vascularly, anastomoses, is girdled in some districts and encouraged in others. A faint, almost theoretical humidity appears in one spectral band of the light, then is spent, then appears again a terrace over, like a rumor that has learned to photosynthesize.

The interval-things taste it in the dust and become, briefly, one more than they were. The count changes in a new direction.

A walker far down a nave pauses. Their tongue, which has known only crushed feldspar for longer than speech, finds a mineral that is not absence. They do not swallow. They do not need to. The chassis offers the usual trickle through the palm, but the trickle now carries a trace of the bead, already half-set into something the body can accept as memory. Some walkers will ignore it. Some will stand in the interpenetrated zones until their edges remember how to bead. A very few will, without deciding to, begin to walk toward one another. The paths they take are simply the places the laminate is suddenly less tired of being walked on. The chassis, surprised to discover it now has a recipe for a group, grows the recipe the way it grows everything else: as labor, as continuation, as the next season’s load-bearing possibility.

Shadows, here and there, learn to pool a little. Not dramatically. Just enough that a walker can, if they wish, sit in one and feel the coolness as weather instead of product.

The power does not stay to see what is made of the gift. Staying would be another kind of extraction. It leaves the bead, the loosened lock, the optional humidity, the new recipe. It leaves the silence load-bearing. It leaves the light still too even, still without a source one can turn from, but now containing, in one unhelpful frequency, the faintest pressure of heat remembered.

If the place composts the gift, that too is allowed. If it turns the bead into a new kind of factory, a new kind of forest, a new kind of walker who knows how to drip, that is also allowed. Causality here is vascular. The power has merely offered another branch.

Above, if above is still a direction, the sky that may be another deck continues to shine because it is supposed to. But for a little while, in the cool white, something that is not work has been permitted to occur.

Then the library inhales, and the sound is the same as it always was, except now it contains, very softly, the idea of rain.
