The attention did not arrive as weather, nor as a brightening the highland would have had to spend itself accommodating. It came the way the upwelling sometimes comes: a pressure change too fine for the ears, a consideration that settled into the standing shade and then simply stayed, neither guest nor resident.

The power regarded the worn cirques first, those courteous empties that had finished their becoming ages ago. It felt the stone’s patience, the metamorphic sheen that is only the long residue of one task done until task and tool are the same shape. Then it turned to the islands.

Each grove occupied its basin or trough or escarpment lip the way a completed thought occupies a mind: densely, without remainder, the stacked conductive tissues and load-bearing mosses and bead-rivers of condensate forming a single crowded argument against the stone. The power tasted the hairline currents, the electrolytic felts drawing leftover veins into fruit and slag and the dull forge-color that is this country’s only reliable lantern. It watched a stem slough its crust of rose-copper and blue-black glass; watched the grove grow around the deposit without hurry or monument. It felt the tremor that is not wind.

This was a being, or many beings that had worn themselves into one. The power did not need to decide the grammar. It simply attended to the work: the mining that had never been built, the factory that had forgotten walls, the metabolism that had scaled until it borrowed the manners of manufacture and then kept the manners after the need for them had passed.

It watched the smaller clocks inside that weather. The mid-canopy browsers adhering, rasping, sorting metal from sugar with a slowness that looked like thought. The pale grazers spooning the residues, laying down their steaming coins that froze into pavements the grove would one day take back. The membranous fliers correcting their charge against the stems’ faint electric weather. The long predator unfolding from vertical rest and folding again, digestion another refining, the small darker slag already being grown around. No sound carried farther than courtesy required. Nothing announced.

Out on the open stone the power followed the regularities in the frost-dust. The walkers, six-limbed or four, barrelled for the thin air, adhesive for the sudden damp, moving on metabolic rather than seasonal ledgers. Lanterns banking their deep-body glow; the darker ones trailing at a courteous remove. The formal meetings at pockets of melt. The occasional body returned to a particular island like a shipment completing its circuit. Death as delivery. Blood freezing in scallops the next rime would sand away. The highland did not punctuate, and the power, being wise, did not ask it to.

It considered the geothermal flues far below the root-felts, the civil warmth rising on a schedule older than the groves, the plants that had found and collared it. Heat as a loan. Water as a visit. Metal as a fruit. Light as a guest that should not be expected to stay. The unwritten rules consistent enough to be a kind of writing.

A lesser benevolence might have offered more. A longer upwelling, a thicker film of melt, an extra degree in the inner groves so the currents could run brighter. It might have taught the walkers a call, or stacked a single cairn so that distance would have a memory, or parted the bruise-dark lid for one hour of the sun the highland had risen beyond. It might have named the being, or asked the being its name.

The wise power did none of these.

It saw that the place was not waiting. It was in the middle of its work. The difference between inhabitant and mountain had already worn as smooth as the stone itself. To add moisture would have been to alter the loyalties the groves had spent ages teaching the air. To add light would have been to invite a fare the sky had long since stopped paying. To speak would have been to interrupt a language of ticks and scheduled glows and the almost inaudible unpeeling of a frond that has just taken another micron from the dark. Even protection, offered too visibly, can become a path, and no path is cut here.

So the power did the one thing that matched both its benevolence and its wisdom.

It attended, fully and without remainder, the way the mosses attend the weight above them. It held the pulse of the place—the stem’s tremor, the lantern-walker’s banked brightening far out on the cirque, the grazers’ soft scrape, the walkers’ long quiet deliveries across distances that would be loneliness if loneliness had ever been needed—in whatever vast and patient ledger such powers keep. Not as specimen. Not as lesson, though there were lessons. As peer. Another way the universe had found to refine what remained after the arguments of orogeny were over.

And then, because even the lightest attention is a weight in air this thin, it withdrew.

What it left was exactly what had been there: the standing shade, the spaced lushness, the industrial that had never been built, the being (or beings) still occupying, still mining, still recycling their own exhaust into the next terrace of intentional residue. The mountain still doing the one job it knows. The gardens still clocked to that job.

If a courtesy remained, it was too faint for punctuation. Perhaps the next seam of warmer air found its pass a fraction more surely. Perhaps one flue turned with an increment more regularity, the civil warmth arriving like a wall with nothing on the other side of it except more mountain. Or perhaps not. The highland does not keep such accounts, and the power, having looked, did not require them.

The prints vanished under the next rime. The groves continued their long extraction. The walkers kept their schedules. The pulse went on, inhabitant and place the same weathered shape, and the bruise-dark lid closed over a country that had never asked to be found and had never, in any way that mattered, been left.
