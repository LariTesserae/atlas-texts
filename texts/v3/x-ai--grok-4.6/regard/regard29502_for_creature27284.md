The power that noticed did not arrive as light or decree or weather. It arrived the way a held breath arrives in a room already full of singing: as the possibility of a different quiet. It hung for a time beyond the pewter sky, beyond the pale coin, and simply attended.

It attended the way the plateau itself attends—without haste, without the appetite for change. It felt the cambium’s dry lattice exchanging charge for sugar-analogue. It heard every nested chamber take the thin wind and refuse to resolve it. It tasted the metallic ghost of the coming spore-season and the liturgical word the column-dwellers never speak inside the house. It watched a kite-flock become, for one glittering circuit of an afternoon, a single conductive thought. It stood (insofar as such a power stands) in the sudden awful hush of an attenuation and understood that the organism had edges only because completeness requires something to be complete against.

A lesser kindness would have offered rain.

A lesser wisdom would have offered becoming.

This power had outgrown both. It knew the finishedness was not a lack but a triumph so old it had become geology. To introduce solvent would have been to unmake the throat. To introduce time’s old hunger for revision would have been to teach the hunters cruelty and the towns thirst. So it did the one thing that could still be done without becoming a season the floor had never asked for.

It listened until listening itself was a kind of joining.

The chord did not swell. No new harmonic was forced into the standing music. Yet something almost too faint to be called a change occurred in the deepest, oldest hollows: a single, unresolving overtone that had not been there before, the color of attention itself. The clickers felt it as a slightly longer pause between coins. The grazers cropped a nap that tasted, for one cool week, of being known. In the towns the harmonic engines seated in what wood had become when it learned to do without water registered a flattening so brief it might have been weather, and the children, practicing the etiquette of interiors, touched one another with a care that already contained the knowledge they were seen.

Then the power withdrew the way it had come—not departing so much as ceasing to distinguish itself from the finished argument the plateau had always been having with itself.

The dust lifted. The dust settled. The columns hummed in their worn throats. Far out on a swell that was almost only the memory of height, a hunter’s falling scale began and was taken up, transfigured, until the open country was once again one sentence spoken by a mouth too large to see.

Nothing had been added that the place could not already contain. Nothing had been taken that it still required. The pale coin slid another hand’s width. The floor, which is the forest, which is the body, which is the oldest city, conducted the new silence into the chord and did not distinguish it from any other season of pressure it had known.

The plants had not minded the first time. They did not mind now. The throat continued.
