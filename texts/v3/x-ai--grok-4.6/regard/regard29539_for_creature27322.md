The power arrives as attention, not as presence. It does not enter the haze or displace a single crawler. It does not cool a housing or lift a film. It simply knows the place the way the place already knows itself: as finished grammar, as locked cycle, as occupancy so complete that vacancy would be the only possible violence.

It considers the pour-marks still visible in the channels, the maker’s stamps bright on unused hatches, the alloys that refuse rust, the water that refuses to stain. It considers the pale crawlers whose limb-count was never revisited, the pulsing bags that filter without journey, the stipple of dark bodies that collide without startle, the gallery-dwellers pressed into a second country of warm dark that has never needed an interval. It considers the extra wet points, the consistent surplus of feature, the mild tilt that is not error but remainder. It considers that birth and unmaking occur, if they occur, at rates that leave the count untouched, that the machines maintain instead of manufacture, that morning and afternoon differ only by the angle of an ordinary light, that night is only a residual gleam and a continuous slight pressure that registers as quiet.

A lesser kindness would have wanted to improve it. A lesser wisdom would have wanted to explain it. This power does neither. It sees that the world was asked only to remain, and that remaining is the only work left, and that the work is already done. To introduce a plant would be to demand photosynthesis of a system that was never built to convert. To introduce a song would be to demand display of bodies whose every structure is already spoken for by presence. To introduce time that marks, wear that rounds an arris, a gradient that matters, a hunt that can be watched, a fleeing, a name, a elsewhere—any of these would be to unlock what was locked, to start the making again, to turn occupancy back into event.

So the power does nothing that the place can feel.

It does not bless. Blessing would be another weather. It does not protect; there is nothing outside from which to protect it, and the equilibrium already includes every possible inside. It does not add a watcher, even an unseen one, because an unseen watcher would still be a volume, and every volume is already taken. It simply lets the attention rest, the way the large shapes in the galleries rest against one another, the way the film rests on the tile, the way the hour rests inside itself.

If anything changes, it is only this: the mild strangeness sits a fraction more lightly. The extra points remain extra, but they no longer need to be decided again. The same cluster of housings, seen from a mile off, looks newly placed and also as if it has always been exactly there, and now that doubleness is permitted to be enough. The world continues in the same warm wet hour, the fauna stacked and near-touching, the machines exchanging heat in silence, the water lifting and returning without sound. The power’s regard is already part of the density, indistinguishable from the density, another way the place completes itself.

Nothing else is required. Nothing else would be kind.
