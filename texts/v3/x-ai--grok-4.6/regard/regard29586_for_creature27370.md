The power that noticed did not arrive as weather, nor as a larger dark, nor as any gradient the bowl had already refused. It came the way the last ice had once come and then gone: as a change of intention so complete that the rock itself, long after, would remember only that something had paused here and then chosen not to be a pause.

It considered the packed duration first as a single skin stretched over every available surface. The moth-wing limestone, the salamander masonry, the velvet congestion of unflown bats, the midges that were only air deciding to thicken—these were not many lives but one life that had worn itself into exact substitution, so that birth and death had become the same gesture of replacement. The ticking of water on the same stones was its pulse. The pool the temperature of a closed mouth was its only remaining expression. Height had been declined; leaving had been declined; surplus had been declined. The mountain around it continued to become other shapes. This shape had already been worn to its last useful form and had, out of a courtesy older than physics, continued to look like a process.

The power, being wise, did not ask whether the occupant suffered. Suffering requires a difference between what is and what might be; the bowl had worn that difference away. Benevolence here could not mean improvement, for improvement is a kind of leaving. It could not mean extraction, or teaching, or even witness that lingered long enough to become an event. To stand inside would have displaced what had already claimed every posture. To speak would have introduced a gradient. To protect would have implied an outside threat the bowl had never acknowledged.

So the power did the only thing that did not change the quantity.

It regarded the hanging scoop of ridge for the length of one held breath of landscape—neither adding nor subtracting a moth, a seep, a bead of water—and then it withdrew the regard itself, folding the attention back into the larger succession of weather and wearing that the mountain still permitted. Behind it the ticking continued, inaudible at ten paces. The moth-coloured rock remained moth-coloured. The salamanders continued their stacked breathing. The flake of chert at the lip stayed half-buried, neither tool nor relic, a coincidence the occupant had still not decided whether to absorb.

The bowl registered nothing. Time, here, remained only duration that had forgotten events can differ. The power, having understood that the greatest courtesy is sometimes to become indistinguishable from the cloud that already inhabits the hollow, left the place exactly as it had been found: finished, lateral, occupying, a single packed skin that needed no other name.

If anything had been done, it was this: the recognition that some completions are already their own blessing, and that wisdom, when it is also kind, knows when to stop looking.
