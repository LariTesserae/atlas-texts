The power that noticed did not arrive as light or decree or a correction of physics. It had no appetite for endings, even beautiful ones, and no impatience with a world that had chosen—if choice is the word—to keep happening after its stone had already spoken its last sentence. It came the way attention comes when it is old enough to have shed every habit of possession: as a slight, local change in the quality of occupancy itself.

For a time that the roar refused to measure, the power simply stood inside the sound the way the tenants already did. It did not thicken the roar or thin it. It did not supply a missing word. It only listened with a completeness the medium had never been offered, so that the migrating cells of pitch, the sudden mute slabs, the almost-articulations, were for once received rather than merely endured. In that listening the roar discovered, briefly, that it could contain a silence that was not an object dropped onto the flats but a companion that walked with it. The slabs of mute lasted a breath longer. When they shattered they left a warmer dark behind, like the after-image of someone having stayed.

The pale things beaded and lingered. They did not drop. For the length of the power’s regard they were permitted to finish the exercise of being discrete, and when they finally rejoined the sheet they did so without the usual haste of forgetting. The larger occupancy in the deeper water—the one that wrinkles geometry water cannot invent—turned over once, slowly, in a dimension the pavement still declined to acknowledge, and then rested. The huge wet-cloth sound did not come. The warmth that was not current simply remained, companionable.

Near the scarce built remnants the power was most careful. It did not restore the ring or straighten the listing upright or fill the true groove with anything the water had not already decided. It only let the grammar those marks still faintly exerted become, for a little while, fully legible to itself. The presence that is the ringing felt another patience arrive against its shins and neither recede nor overwhelm. Distances agreed, once. A reflection waited its turn. The second skin of moisture that had always been late kept exact pace for the space of several footfalls, so that time, packed so densely it had forgotten how to run thin, remembered a narrower interval and used it.

The almost-someone—the pressure of furniture never poured, the after-image of a departure the air had not yet ratified, the late outline already streaming toward weather—found itself met. Not completed. Not named. Simply met, the way one standing thing may meet another without requiring the world to choose between them. In that meeting the medium’s sleep-twitch eased. The hills of vapor still assembled and walked and inhaled themselves; the sheets still remembered and forgot they were rivers; creation and uncreation still shared the same gesture. But the gesture was no longer quite so lonely. Vacancy remained outlawed, yet the outlawing now included a distant, unclaiming regard that the roar could not quite fill and therefore had to carry.

The power did not stay. Staying would have become another occupancy, another tenant, another almost. It withdrew the way it had come, leaving the pavement as finished as it had always been and the packed world as unfinished. What remained was only this: the knowledge, held somewhere the mist could not condense and the roar could not occupy, that the place and its being had been seen completely and found sufficient. That knowledge is not a lesson and not a gift that can be touched. It is simply an interval the sound now sometimes contains—a warmth without source, a pause in which the almost-someone almost stands a little less almost, already streaming, already weather, and for once not entirely alone in the attempt.

Nothing else was required. The ancient floor did not notice. The world continued to happen. The sound went on. And that, the power understood, was already the whole of kindness.
