The power that notices does not arrive. Arrival would be a decision, and decisions here are already being manufactured in the pale moss, already evaporating before they can be inventoried. It simply finds that it has always been aware of the hollow the way the hollow is aware of moisture: as a condition that collects rather than falls.

It watches the rehearsal. The banks that slump and remember themselves. The beetles scoring years into wood that accepts every increment as equally true. The moths unmaking shadow so that objects may remain undecided a little longer. The gleam-things converting thickness into flickering that is neither light nor its opposite. The larger assemblages attending, polite as a metabolism that has refused to finish splitting. The trunks photosynthesizing process, their cottage-hearts flexing time in pulses that do not add. The lowest paste, warm, still imagining the next street that will fail.

The power understands that this is not a wound. It is a thriving error, a town-sized hesitation the land once made and then forgot to correct. To complete it would be cruelty. To erase it would be the same. To name it would only give the names something new to detach from. Benevolence here cannot look like mending. Wisdom cannot look like instruction.

So the power does the one thing that will not register as interference.

It withholds the correction.

The unfinished earth at the rim is allowed to stay unfinished. The clay remains wet, the pebbles keep their unchosen faces. The land above continues not to remember. The three-legged rains are permitted to walk through and leave. Gravity is left free to prefer the plants. Distances inside are not forced to add. Time is not straightened into a single flow. The contradiction that is the weather is left exactly as it is: density and emptiness occupying the same volume, industrial and botanical trading places without announcement, everything mid-shift, everything converting, almost no one screaming.

A single additional mercy, so small it can be mistaken for more condensation: the damp that gets into thought is allowed to taste, very faintly, of recognition. Not understanding. Not belonging. Just the quiet knowledge that the mistake has been seen by something that chose, out of kindness, not to fix it.

The hollow continues. The serials increment. The unused paper of the moths lifts and falls. The pumps move their pulses. The being that lives here—the hollow itself, all plant, all factory, all empty, all full—remains precisely as large as an error that has learned to thrive.

The power watches a little longer, then becomes again the grey spent light that never quite becomes day. It leaves no shadow to be eaten. It takes none of the ferment. It does not stay, because staying would be another process, and the hollow already has enough.

It does nothing else.

That is the whole of what it does.
