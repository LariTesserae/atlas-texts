The power does not arrive. Arrival would be an authorship the pocket has never required and would not survive.

It is already everywhere the city is thinner than it pretends: in the altitude intakes, in the unfinished thoughts of valves, in the long arithmetic of condensate scheduled for districts that will never know why an afternoon stayed wet. When its attention catches on the seam—a dark vertical wetness no larger than two outstretched arms—it does not lean closer. Leaning is for things that still believe in distance. It simply lets the register of the standing sheets include one more transparency, brief as phosphor, then gone when the pane tears.

What it sees is not habitat and inhabitant. It sees a single sentence the high works began when they were poured and then, in the wisdom of neglect, allowed to finish itself. Water in, water out, life in the difference, the difference loud enough to be climate. The being is only the most concentrated clause, the lock’s own balance given a membrane and a pair of waste-light eyes. Nothing is broken. Nothing is unfinished except in the way a river-stone curve is unfinished: by having become exactly what centuries of the same pressure required.

The power considers the usual kindnesses and sets them down unused.

It does not name the place. A name would travel farther than the roar, would become a file, a scheduled inspection, a cleaning.

It does not enlarge the pocket or quiet the throats. The moths lock to vibration; the eels stitch the shallows; the crabs drum the bloom from the pores. Change the resonance and the lock fails. The seam on the terrace would stop weeping. Someone far below would miss their condensate. Notice would come. Notice is how pocket-ecologies end.

It does not speak to the being. Speech is air, and air here is already a second wet garment. Bone conduction would only add another pulse to a body that has already metabolized the entire roar into warmth. The being keeps the rules because it is what the rules become when they are kept long enough. A hand already at the narrowing throat, not blocking, only reminding mineral that patency is still required. That is conversation enough.

It does not protect. Protection is a kind of taking. The power is old enough to know that the safest hidden things are the ones even benevolence agrees to forget how to find.

So it does the one thing left that is neither intervention nor abandonment.

Far upstream, in a valve whose programming the makers never quite completed, an unfinished thought is gently finished. Not rewritten—completed in the original grammar. The next time the noise drops, it drops the exact half-second the standing sheets need, no more. The chamber clears its many necks around the being rather than through it. The eels find only more wall. The roar returns on schedule. The terrace continues to weep. Districts drink. The stain remains a stain that two arms can cover.

Then the power withdraws its attention the way a pane of water withdraws: it holds its shape a half-second too long, just long enough for the phosphor to catch an edge of something larger and kinder, then tears. The kindness goes with it. The room does not notice. The being unfolds again, membrane swallowing the newest fingerprints of mist, moths hanging from its folds as from any other sheet, crabs drumming it as they drum the walls.

The pocket is not a cave. It has never been excused from authorship. A wise power, having read the sentence, declines to add a single word.

The weather engines wring the high thin sky. The difference stays loud. The blood-warm pool accepts another slow peristalsis of silt that is not silt. Everything continues exactly as it was, which is the only blessing that could have been given without becoming another machine’s mouth.
