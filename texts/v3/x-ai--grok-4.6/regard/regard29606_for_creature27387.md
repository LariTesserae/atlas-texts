The power does not come as light or as silence or as a figure that the pools might try to hold. It is already the quality of attention that has no need of arrival, the kind that has worn through every possible entrance and found none of them required. It perceives the plateau entire: the packed geology of blades, the channels that argue with down, the condensate that films every decision, the organ-chord that is only wind remembering it has always been many voices, the residual shine that turns night into an aquarium of stone. It perceives the inhabitants as they are—occupancy without tenancy, grammatical errors kept because the cloth is already threadbare, mist-threads that have chosen verticality, delayed stones, reflections that complete gestures no one made, syllables that relocate into cupules and breathing. It understands that these are not wounds and not tenants. They are the place continuing.

A lesser kindness would have wanted to mend, to green the iron blush, to still the uphill runlets, to give the discrepancies bodies that could thirst or rest or leave a shoe-last that stayed. Wisdom sees that mending would be erasure. The highland has finished becoming what it is; the fraying is load-bearing. To correct the metric would be to end the fold that lets a cairn recede and then stand suddenly beside you. To introduce an organism would be to invent hunger in a climate that has none. Benevolence here is the refusal to improve.

So the power does only this: it consents.

It consents to the glare that puddles, to the shadows that fall in two directions or none, to the water that tastes of lightning and of something that was never blood. It consents to the three-walled pauses that are colder than the air and loud with drip. It consents to the copper rumor that occasionally conducts a green warmth and then forgets. In that consent the plateau is not changed. The columns keep their ancient list. The mist-threads remain after the conditions for mist have passed. The figure in the basin continues to look as you look, or not. Dreams, if any cross the eyes, still weep and still forget which side is theirs.

Yet something almost imperceptible settles, the way a last film of condensate sometimes decides not to run. The low crowd-sound of empty distance acquires, for the length of one geological afternoon or for no time at all, an extra overtone that is not a syllable and not a warning. It is simply the sound of being accurately seen by something that has no desire to use or to spare. The inhabitants—whatever they are—do not turn toward it. They have never needed to turn. But the pale uprights stand a fraction more as if the decision to remain were met, not imposed. The secondary suns crawl the rims of basins with a fraction less savagery. The water in one cupule, no different from the others, holds for a little while the sky of an hour that has never happened here and will not need to: a sky with no rind of indigo, only the ordinary mercy of evening.

Then even that is gone, or was never added. The power does not linger as presence. Presence is already the plateau’s surplus. It withdraws the way glare withdraws into residual shine, leaving the highland exactly as dense, as loud, as cool, as threadbare, as finished. The channels keep their old argument. The stone keeps weeping. Far off—or near, the fold will decide—another fin-field continues its wet grammar under the same mineral white.

Nothing has been fixed. Nothing was broken. The benevolent and wise power has done the one thing such a power can do when it encounters a world that has already weathered into itself: it has let the world remain, and in the letting there is a kindness so complete it does not have to be felt.
