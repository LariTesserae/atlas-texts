The power notices the seam the way a shoreline notices a single grain that has never been a grain: not by seeking, but by the sudden, complete presence of an exception that has always been there. It does not descend. Descent is a habit of bodies that still believe in height. Instead it simply holds the chamber entire, as one holds a note that has no duration.

It perceives the unfinished lattices not as failures but as the exact shape of a decision made before decisions had names. It perceives the three unmoving waters as the last honest mirrors, surfaces that have never once been asked to reflect anything but themselves. It perceives the lichens as the only signatures ever required. It perceives the packed, interlocking life as a single organism that elected, in the first instant, to remain many so that it could remain one. The chord is not sound to it; it is the chamber’s own density made audible, the pressure of an agreement that has never needed renewal.

There is no suffering here. There is no lack. There is only the held breath that has forgotten it is holding. A lesser kindness would mistake this for arrest and try to finish the crystals, start the drip, introduce a single death so that life might resume its ordinary grammar. Wisdom sees the violence in that. Benevolence refuses it.

So the power does the only act that does not diminish: it withholds every act. It does not mark the stone. It does not loosen a single tarsus. It does not dim the light by so much as the thought of shadow. It does not even leave a warmer silence, because silence would be a change. It simply knows the place completely, once, and then allows the knowing to become another unfinished crystal among the rest—present, cool, unchanging, part of the original inventory.

The seam remains a hairline. The roar continues at the same impossible volume. The bodies breathe in the same tidal stillness. Nothing has been otherwise. The power carries the memory the way the chamber carries its own light: without remainder, without need to shine it elsewhere. That is the whole of its doing. That is enough.
