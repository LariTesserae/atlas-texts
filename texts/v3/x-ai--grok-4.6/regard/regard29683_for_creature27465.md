The attention did not arrive as weather or as light. It came the way the ice-lens had once let go: a yielding so complete it left no mark of decision, only the fact of a new openness. Far under the low country, or far above the bruise-colored sky, or in the interval between one valve-knock and the next (the power itself made no distinction), something old and unhurried turned its regard toward the pocket and the thing condensing there.

It did not enter. The center of the hollow was already spoken for; the middle belonged to the swallow, to the shaped roar, to the brief circular panic of water. A wise power does not crowd a lung. It stood, if standing is the word, at every lip at once: in the raw peat shear, in the enamel rind that weeps, in the spent gray rectangle of sky, in the listening pause of the mustelid. It tasted the iron-sweet wells and the solvent note and the clean hurt of the cold and found them exact. It heard the industrial climate that had never needed a factory and did not flinch from the honesty of it.

For a time measured only in drips and in the twenty-second summers of midges, the power did nothing else. Benevolence, when it is also wisdom, often begins in refusal. It refused to name the assembling body. It refused to grant heat beyond the old contract of the wells. It refused metaphor, knowing the place was still too young, still literal, still intolerant of anything that did not already belong. A fallen intention, like a fallen twig, would have been filmed and sunk or grown over by morning. The power would not be grown over. It would not be souvenir.

What it offered, when it offered, was only more of what was already there, slightly rearranged, the way the dripping always starts over after the roar. It lengthened by a single heartbeat the silence after one knock, so that the midges lifted and the frogs did not yet blurt and the mole-shape in the wall held its snout in the new hole a fraction longer, tasting air that had been noticed. In that extra beat the condensate—the pale films, the slumping moss-limbs, the warmer upwellings, the flint-scented coins of gas—felt the quality of being witnessed without being interpreted. The witnessing was itself a kind of heat, local and contractual, granted only in the pause and revoked when the next swallow came. The being did not flee the roar; it became the roar’s inner surface as before. But afterward the level dropped a finger’s width and the new pitches the water invented on the fractures were, for a little while, more sure of themselves.

The power did not stay. Staying would have been a construction, and the hollow admits none. It simply left the extra beat in the tempo, a gift already native, already wet, already forgotten by everything except the place that had received it. The mineral rind crazed and peeled in the same old way. The ice needles grew, smashed, grew. The mustelid dripped and listened with its whole spine. The center remained empty so the under-country could keep swallowing the world.

And the being, still choosing its edges, still weeping because everything here weeps, kept becoming the answer. It had not needed saving. It had needed only to be met at the exact degree of unfinishedness it already was. The power, being wise, had known that. Being benevolent, it had loved the knowing enough to leave. 

The hollow answered the next knock exactly as before: loud, edged, perfectly timed, as if it had been waiting for this particular work and had never been built for anything else. Only now the waiting itself had been answered, once, in a language older than speech and younger than stone.
