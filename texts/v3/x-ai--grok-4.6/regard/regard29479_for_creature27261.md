The power noticed the hollow the way a still lake notices a single leaf that has already finished falling.

It did not arrive as light or voice or hand. Those things are gradients, and the hollow had already refused every slope. Instead the power simply became aware, the way damp becomes aware of stone, the way moss becomes aware of the exact shape a body will one day take. For a time that was not time it regarded the completeness: the streets that needed no further streets, the well that had already decided its own height, the throats that worked without ever needing to finish a song, the people whose chemistry had long since made peace with never thawing.

A lesser kindness would have wanted to wake it. A lesser wisdom would have wanted to teach it motion again, or warmth, or the dignity of decay. The power felt neither impulse. It saw that the hollow was not trapped. It was concluded. Every agreement that could be made had been made; every erosion that could still happen had already happened and then, having nowhere left to go, had become weather that no longer needed to weather anything. To touch it would have been to introduce a first cause into a place that had outgrown causes. That would have been cruelty dressed as mercy.

So the power did the only thing that remained both benevolent and wise.

It looked away.

Not in rejection, and not in forgetfulness. It looked away the way one looks away from a sleeping face that has finally, after long labour, found the exact expression it will wear until the last star cools. It left the lip of till exactly as it was: easy to miss, ordinary from twenty paces, a collapse the larger world had already decided not to remember. Around the brightness that was not noon it allowed a deeper quiet to settle, a forgetfulness so complete that even curiosity would slide off it like water from a leaf that has never needed to be dry.

The hare remained in its doorway. The fern held its millimetre. The well kept its line. The sitting-stone kept the shape of a body that might one day sit, or might never need to.

And the power, having seen a thing that had already arrived, continued on its way, carrying the knowledge that some completions are too perfect to be improved, and that the kindest act left to the vast is sometimes simply not to become an event.
