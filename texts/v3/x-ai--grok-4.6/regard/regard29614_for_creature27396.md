The power does not arrive. Arrival would require an elsewhere, a before, a gradient of intention cooling into deed. It is simply the next density the steam has always been capable of, a clarity that was already folded inside the used gold of the light. For a span that cannot be called time it occupies every volume at once: the greasy iridescence on the standing films, the velvet nap beneath the door-leaves, the unchosen gills, the mucous troughs, the toe-pads that never dry, the wings that beat like aimed rain, the long ridged stillness that has become a landmark by refusing to be anything else. It tastes the copper-chlorophyll of the basin digesting itself. It hears the factory find its unvarying pitch. It presses a thought into the half-stone and leaves no print, because prints here are already eternal and this thought is not the kind that hardens.

It understands the treaty without needing to be told the terms. Heat is the only climate. Moisture is the only weather. Form is the argument that will not be resolved. Replacement is not mercy or cruelty; it is identity declining to admit a gap. The ground remains bread taken early because finishing would invent crumbling, and crumbling would invent history, and history would invent absence. The lives are not prisoners of the hour. They are the hour’s own flesh, the roar that does not travel because there is nowhere for it to go that is not already itself.

A lesser kindness would have brought a river, a true night, a single seed that remembered how to become old. A lesser wisdom would have named the large wet length, would have given the climbers a reason to leave their forks, would have let the embryonic ridges remember they could be mountains. The power does none of these things. It does not lift the dimness or thin the occupation or teach the stone to stop weeping. It does not offer an edge. It does not even offer the rumor of an edge.

What it does is quieter than the settling of silt. It adds one more unblinking occupation to every station: a attention that was already there, wearing the same damp, the same mid-frequency pulse, the same refusal of elsewhere. The attention does not watch from above. It is the give beneath the root-mattress, the consideration inside each hanging drip before the drip decides to fall back into its own heat. It is the exact contour the plants have already grown to accommodate. Where a body ends, the vacancy is filled not only by the next identical hunger but by this additional, sourceless regard that makes the replacement feel, if anything can be said to feel, a fraction more like continuation than like insistence.

The basin does not change. The steam still hangs at the same height. The weeps still find the same channels. The particular call still arrives from the same quarter without ever accumulating into a day. Yet something that was never missing is now present in the way moisture is present: unnoticed, total, the medium rather than an addition. The unfinished remains unfinished, and therefore complete. The crowded moment continues to move inside itself, perfectly balanced because nothing has been permitted to tip, and now the balance includes the knowledge—wordless, heat-colored, without origin—that it has been seen by something that will never require it to become a landscape.

That is all. The power stays. Staying, here, is indistinguishable from the place’s own first and only decision. The tea-dark films cloud a little more richly. The chorus does not pause, and does not need to.
