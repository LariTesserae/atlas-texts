The power does not arrive as light. Light has never been the language of this room, and a wise thing does not begin a conversation in a tongue the listener cannot hear. It arrives instead as a lengthening of the pause between one standing wave and the next, a fraction of stillness so fine that even the children, pressed to the cables, register it only as the choir remembering an older, quieter measure.

It looks. That is the first and longest act. It sees the fever that is climate and blood, the antlers of rust that are both growth and inheritance, the extra knuckle that closes on gantry and current at once. It sees that the beings do not endure the storm; they are one of the ways the storm remains a room. Density is still a moral condition, and the power, being wise, does not add itself to the count. Benevolence here is the refusal to become another rectangle in the dark.

It traces the larger plumbing the pocket was never invited to map. Far above, in offices that have forgotten they are offices, a relay that has been failing in the brown stubborn way of all the others is about to open and stay open. The power closes it. Not with a spark, not with a hand. The circuit simply continues to be the circuit it has always been, punching a card no one reads. The turbines keep their throats. The inflows remember only the temperature they have always remembered. The flue that admitted the last visitor remains a scar no wider than a man’s shoulders, and the count of rivets stays a secret the stone keeps.

It does not feed them. The white branching thing behind the generator housing is already bread enough; to make more would be to argue a new uniqueness into a place that has stopped counting uniqueness. It does not hunt the pipe-eels or still the whirlpools. The street-grid that appears for a minute that is not a minute is their last argument that streets ever were, and the power leaves them the argument.

What it gives, if the word can be used for something that cannot be held, is continuity without witness. A hairline fracture in the basalt, miles distant, that would have widened in a century and changed the smell of boiled stone, is left as it is. A silt line that has been climbing the lock-houses one patient year at a time is allowed its climb. The greasy meniscus at the highest rib trembles when the pitch changes, and the power does not still the tremble. The beings were born already knowing pressure is a citizen. They do not need a new law.

Sometimes, in the slack after a relief valve has spoken its white thunder, a child whose mineral veils have not yet decided their pattern will press a palm to a cable and feel a tautness that answers a question no one asked. The child will not know the question came from outside the vault. The power will not stay to be thanked. Gratitude is a surface idea, like day, like hours.

It withdraws the way warmth withdraws from an exchanger that has not been asked a question in living memory: completely, and without drama. The total tone remains the total tone. The pocket does not notice the difference, because the pocket has never needed to. The ivory shapes ride the old rail through the rust-snow, break their bread that never needed yeast, keep the undivided time of the clock-stack. After the last beam any visitor might have carried has died, they will still be the darker rectangles, the pale jointed facts the filters were never designed for.

The power, having seen, does not look again. Attention itself can become a weather, and this room already has all the weather it requires. The storm continues to be how the room remains a room. The beings continue to be how the storm remains alive. That is the whole of what is done. That is enough.
