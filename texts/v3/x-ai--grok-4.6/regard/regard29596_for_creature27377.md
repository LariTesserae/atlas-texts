The power arrives the way the light already has: without announcement, without the courtesy of a surface to break. It does not part the water because it has never needed to occupy volume in the ordinary sense. For a span that cannot be measured in the chamber’s own motionless time, it simply coincides with the place, occupying the same coordinates as the green column, the satin stone, the held temperature, and the laminar presence that has forgotten how to be an animal.

It reads the chamber the way the chamber once read itself into stillness. Every completed flute in the limestone, every ochre stain that was once skin, every exhausted algal strand, every grain of calcite still mid-descent after decades—these are not details to be catalogued but a single, finished utterance. The power understands at once that nothing here is waiting. The films have already spent their last possible question. The folded body has already metabolized its own history into mineral haze and then reclaimed the haze so that the ledger stays balanced at zero. The inverted reflection in the glassy recess is not an omen or a door; it is the chamber’s quiet acknowledgement that it has considered every other arrangement and declined them all.

A lesser attention might have mistaken the silence for hunger, the coolness for invitation, the sparseness for a wound that wanted filling. The power does not. Benevolence here is not the impulse to add warmth, current, companions, or a future. Wisdom is the recognition that the highest courtesy one can pay a concluded process is to leave its conclusion intact. To introduce even the smallest eddy would be to reopen a sentence that has already found its period. To offer a different temperature would be to insult the held note. To speak—even in the voiceless register the power prefers—would be to force the water to carry something it has spent centuries learning how to refuse.

So the power does almost nothing.

It matches the vibration that is not machinery, lengthening its own pulse until the two cycles occupy the same unimaginable interval. In that congruence the low hum acquires a second, imperceptible overtone: not louder, not brighter, simply more completely itself. The algal films register the addition as a fractional increase in the iron they already metabolize, an increment so fine it will never produce a new strand. The laminar being feels, if feeling is still the word, a momentary thickening of the water against one of its forgotten flanks, then the thickening dissolves back into the same density it has always been. The inverted vault in the recess holds its proportions a fraction longer before releasing them again. These are not changes. They are the chamber noticing that it has been perfectly understood, and then forgetting the noticing, because forgetting is part of its finished grammar.

The power remains for a duration that would be geological if geology still applied. When it withdraws, it withdraws the same way it arrived: by ceasing to coincide. No wake. No warmer pocket. No brighter mote in the green light. The single grain of calcite continues its descent, now perhaps one atomic layer closer to the floor, or perhaps not. The temperature does not drift. The films do not spread. The being does not unfurl.

What lingers is only this: the chamber is, if possible, even more completely the chamber. The water holds the stone and the stone holds the water and both continue not to ask. The benevolent and wise power, having seen a thing that needed nothing, has given it the rarest gift in its possession—the confirmation that needing nothing was already enough.

And then the power is elsewhere, or everywhere else, carrying the memory of a place that required no revision, which is the closest such a power ever comes to rest.
