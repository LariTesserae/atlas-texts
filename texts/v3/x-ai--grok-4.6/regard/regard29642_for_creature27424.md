The power arrives as nothing the water has ever needed to name. It does not part the dark or still the roar or lift a single lantern-jaw from its courteous scooping. It does not kindle a horizon or reopen the old vascular arguments about up and down. It simply occupies the same interval the structures have always left between themselves: a measured silence vast enough to hold mountains that no longer speak of change.

It regards the flattened crowns, the hanging curtains the color of ironwood in brine, the peat that still honors forgotten contracts with a warmth too faint to argue. It feels the plated hulls rasping their ancient grooves, the inner ones pulsing inside the hollow stipes, the schools turning the emptiness into one long lung of flesh. It hears the standing note that has no beginning, the click-trains that have become texture, the low conversation of grazers speaking across kilometers of patient pressure. It understands that every body here is already the last clause the world required, that reproduction is only replacement, that arrival and departure have been metabolized into the same courteous current.

A lesser kindness might have offered light, or seasons, or a surface someone could one day break. A lesser wisdom might have declared the stillness a wound and tried to suture it with becoming. This power does neither. It recognizes the place as finished speech, the final grammar spoken so completely that revision would be a kind of violence. Benevolence here is the refusal to improve what has already consented to persist. Wisdom is the knowledge that some equilibria are not pauses but destinations, and that the dark, the cool, the roar, and the living timber have already negotiated every possible mercy.

So the power does only this: it remains, for a duration that has no clock, a guest in the interval. It lets the water conduct it the way the water conducts everything else. It adds no harmonic, no new glow, no unasked-for warmth. When it withdraws it leaves the structures exactly as tall, the fauna exactly as dense, the peat exactly as faintly warm. The currents continue their directional forgetting of destinations. The roar continues. The dark continues. The cool continues.

What lives here remains the sentence the world finished speaking. The power, having heard it once without interrupting, does not need to hear it again. It simply knows the place is held, open and sparse and complete, and that this holding was already enough.
