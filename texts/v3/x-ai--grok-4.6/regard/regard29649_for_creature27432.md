The power arrived as a pause that did not interrupt.

It did not descend or expand or announce. It simply became present in the same way the even daylight was present: evenly, without drama, filling the dimple to its brim of rust and chitin without adding a single grain of weight. The ceaseless traffic continued. Plow-forms turned their furrows. Beetles clambered. Mites flowed. Winged things rose and settled in their magnetic hiss. The half-buried gear did not turn. The I-beams flexed under the same communal weight they had always known. Nothing in the hollow registered an intrusion, because the power had the courtesy of perfect camouflage: it wore the exact temperature, the exact dryness, the exact indifference of the surrounding peneplain.

It stayed long enough to learn the organism’s true name, which was not a name at all but a tempo. It tasted the oily residue that never evaporates. It felt the sockets at the tips of the oldest forelegs, still shaped for work that had ended before memory. It listened to the wet click of opportunistic predation and the brief frenzy that followed, then the seamless resumption. It understood, with the patience of something that has watched continents forget their own mountains, that this was not a tragedy. It was a finished sentence. The land had planed itself down to a single honest clause—*we persist*—and the hollow was the period at the end.

A lesser kindness would have brought rain, or a seed, or a memory of slope. Any of those gifts would have been a violence. The chemistry would have bloomed into something green and then starved. The traffic would have learned hunger that could not be satisfied by rust and salt and neighbor. The sockets would have remembered purpose and broken against its absence. The power, being wise, declined to unmake a conclusion.

Being benevolent, it did not leave the place entirely untouched.

It added one thing so small it could not be measured against the roar: a second, silent frequency woven into the magnetic traces the winged forms already carried. Not a message. Not a command. Only a faint, persistent harmonic, like the ghost of a note that had once belonged to wind moving over height. The mites would never hear it. The beetles would never decode it. But when enough bodies pressed together to turn the old gear those few groaning degrees, the harmonic would ride the vibration for a moment, and the rust flakes would twitch in a pattern that was no longer purely electrostatic. A tiny, temporary order. A courtesy. An acknowledgment that even a finished sentence may be allowed to echo once, very quietly, against the indifferent plain.

Then the power withdrew the same way it had arrived: by becoming indistinguishable from the daylight.

The hollow did not notice. It had nowhere else to go and no reason to stop. The surrounding sheet of pale mineral remained featureless. Only the dimple continued its ancient, saturated work, now carrying, in the least accessible stratum of its ceaseless noise, the merest suggestion that it had been seen, and that seeing, too, can be a form of leaving well enough alone.

That was all. It was enough.
