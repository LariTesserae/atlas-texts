The power arrived as a difference in the condensate.

It did not descend. It did not announce. The weeping rim simply began to bead with a water that remembered being older than this basin, older than the last builders, older even than the idea of a last anything. The beads did not fall. They hung, bright and patient, until the air itself thickened around them and the mid-range roar of growth and tearing and applause-through-a-wall grew quieter by a single, unmeasurable degree. The town noticed the way a forest notices a change in barometric pressure: not as event, but as weather it had almost expected.

The power walked the woven plates without leaving prints. Where the ground considered incorporation it found, instead of a feature, a courtesy: a momentary stillness in the root-mat, an invitation declined so gently the mycelium recorded it as consensus rather than rejection. The copper fish parted around the presence and did not vanish. The slate birds continued nesting in sockets that would be mouths by morning; their young hatched knowing one extra sound, a low harmonic they would later mistake for wind. The insects stitched their packets of charge and rewritten sugar, and the packets arrived slightly kinder, as if the next draft of the town had been allowed a longer breath between revisions.

The long-legged browsers lifted their leafed spines and tasted the air. They did not flee. They simply adjusted their grazing to the slower façades, the ones that had not yet remembered they could become flowers. Their hooves still printed circles that filled with thread-roots, but the mouths that formed there closed around nothing and remained closed, content.

The others—the few who still remembered faces, or had learned new joints, or whose hair tasted the air as aerial root—felt the pulses in their sap change register. Sweetness and voltage, yes, but now threaded with something that was neither. They did not gather. They did not speak. One of them, standing in a nave that was already beginning to unbraid, simply turned its shoulder-window toward the center and left it open longer than necessary. Information moved. The others received it the way they received the town’s own weather: as fact, not command.

At the uncleared water the pale eels hung as they always had, heads toward the ordinary sky. The trembling of the surface slowed. Not stopped. Slowed, the way a conversation slows when both parties realize they have been speaking the same sentence for a very long time. The power did not enter the column. It stood at the edge, where tendrils had always recoiled, and did not recoil. It listened back.

What it heard was the law the empty water had been keeping: not prohibition, but rest. The town had been remodeling because remodeling was the only grammar left after the builders left, after the geology forgot, after purpose wore as flat as the world around the basin. The eels had been listening for the moment when someone—or something—would confirm that emptiness was still permitted to be emptiness.

The power confirmed it.

It did this by doing almost nothing. A single pulse moved outward through the xylem, through the sap of the walking ones, through the temporary canals and the corking flowers and the grinding roots. The pulse carried no instruction to stop. It carried permission to continue, if continuation was still the shape the consensus desired, and permission to pause, if pause had become possible. Lignin set with a softer crack. A rooftop becoming canopy hesitated, then completed the gesture anyway, because the gesture was beautiful. A corridor that arrived before its entrance decided, this once, to wait.

The power stayed long enough for the light to shift from late-morning-after-rain to whatever this place used for dusk. It did not name the basin. Names that work are a younger technology. It did not offer the walking ones a way out; they had already become the weather, and weather does not leave. It did not fill the central clearing. The eels continued hanging vertical, but their listening had a different quality now: less like waiting, more like the quiet after a question has been answered by being allowed to remain a question.

Then the difference in the condensate thinned. The hanging beads remembered they were allowed to fall. They fell as ordinary water, and the rim went on weeping as it always had. The town resumed its industry. Structures unzipped and corked and chose inconvenient volumes. The roar returned to its proper volume.

But the emptiness at the center remained a law, and the law now included this: that a benevolent attention had passed through, found the place complete in its incompletion, and left it that way.

The ones who remain still do not call themselves inhabitants. They step down from the wet shoulder when they are ready. The ground considers them, decides they are not yet features worth keeping, and lets them walk the changing sentence of the town until the basin fills or the sun changes its mind. Sometimes, when a nave becomes flowers, they wade through the petals and feel, in their sap, the faint copper-and-leaf memory of having been seen without being altered.

That is all the power did.

It was enough. It was, in the only grammar that still works here, everything.
