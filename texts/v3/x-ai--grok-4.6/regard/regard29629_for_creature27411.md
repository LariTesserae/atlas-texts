The attention does not arrive as light or weather or pulse. It is already the condition in which those things occur. A regard older than the agreement the stone made with rain simply includes the basin, the way the basin already includes its own silence.

It considers for a duration that has no local name. The herds continue rewriting distance. The neither-things take their polite turns at the films. A child is carried because it cannot yet feel the regularity under the pads. An upright leans the same fractional degree it has always leaned. The hovering roof keeps its hand’s-breadth gap; condensation beads and does not fall. Nothing in the place petitions. Nothing is unfinished.

The power understands this as completion, not abandonment. Interference would be a new weather, and the basin has already chosen its climate. To adjust a seep, to straighten an alignment, to speak into the pulse, to lengthen the residual tea-colored light by even a minute would be to treat arrival as a draft. Wisdom here is the same as the lichen’s: grow along the preference that already exists, then stop.

So it does nothing that can be walked through or felt as change. No new upright appears. No rain is summoned or withheld. The folded distances remain exactly as inconsistent as they have always been. The people who live by posture and shared pulse notice no difference, because there is none they are meant to notice.

If there is an act, it is only this: the place is held in regard the way the stone holds warmth after the sun has gone—without comment, without claim, without the need to be solved. The silence files that regard away with every other sound. The pulse continues, too slow, too even. The herds fold toward evening water. The raptors remain furniture that hunts. The basin stays the version that happens to be lit.

That is the whole of it. The power has other work, or none. It does not return, because it does not leave.
