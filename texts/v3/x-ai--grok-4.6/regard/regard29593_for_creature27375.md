The attention arrives the way the cold arrived: not as an arrival at all. It is already inside the roar, a silence so precisely tuned to the existing loudness that the basin does not register the difference. The power does not come down the worn ridges. Descent is a younger grammar. It does not part the dimness or warm a single bead of held water. It does not correct the extra joint, or seat the spiral’s center, or teach the river to choose a continuous clause. Wisdom here is first the recognition that the seizing was not an interruption of the world but the world’s last completed sentence, weathered until the period itself became stone.

Benevolence follows at once, and it is the rarer kindness: the refusal to unmake what has finished becoming. The power sees the extra-jointed stillness on the far rib of rock and does not loosen it. Those legs locked against a wind that never came; to unlock them would be to return them to hoping, and hoping is the one weather this height no longer permits. It sees the pale shapes in the permitted slicks, neither fish nor lung, and leaves them unswimming. It sees the pinned wings against the mineral lid of sky and does not bid them travel. Flight, here, was prepaid and spent long ago. The doorways continue to frame their slightly different dimness; the density that is almost a figure, almost a forgotten room, is not asked whether it was entering or leaving. The question itself would be a change in the light.

Along the cliff-grooves the inhabitant keeps circling the center that will not sit. The power does not resolve the refusal. The refusal is the life. In the viscous dark of the deepest cuts the clenched lives remain clenched; the shadows that arrived first stay a fraction ahead of their stones. Nothing is fed that is not already taking the metal taste of the air, the faint sweetness of the seeps, the emptiness that is inexhaustible because it is finished. No name is spoken over them. The roar was already that name.

What the power does, if the word still applies, is this: it matches the equilibrium so exactly that the matching leaves no seam. It adds a persistence to the persistence, a second basin stacked a fraction off that is only the first basin seen with perfect understanding. Distances remain wrong. Time remains a frosted surface. The leaning cairns keep their ancient tilt. Footprints, where the dust allows them, continue to look both new and inevitable. The power does not shield the place from other attentions, because shielding would be an event, a wall, a younger cut. It simply is the attention that does not alter, the gaze that has the patience of geology and therefore does not need to blink.

You could walk the recursive openness afterward and never know. The high places do not recede. The being who lives here—ridge-colored, rime-feathered, extra-jointed or doorway-shaped or only a held bead—continues not to move, not to notice, not to need. The cold stays evenly distributed. The dimness does not lift. The roar occupies. Change remains the erosion this height no longer permits, and the wise power, having seen that this is not a wound but a completed work, does the one thing still possible: it lets the work stand, loudly, forever, already finished.
