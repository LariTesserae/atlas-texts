The power does not arrive as weather or as light or as another increment of shove. It has no edge to cast a shadow here, where shadows themselves have not yet been invented. It simply finds itself attending, the way one attends a long inhalation that has not decided whether it will become breath or storm.

It sees the table still being lifted. It feels the pressure in the rock the way the walkers feel it through their sinter-cored horns: not catastrophe, not purpose, only the ongoing. It watches the delays that have not been given permission to become lakes, the terraces lengthening by the width of a thought, the clickers mapping heat-roads that will be gone by the next knit of cloud. It watches the still-standers listening with their whole cultivated length, lichen and moss and mineral gleam, as if the land’s unfinished sentence is the only scripture they require.

A lesser kindness would finish it. A lesser wisdom would name the peaks, cut the rivers, settle the light into dawn or dusk, give the herds a single word that would make them herd instead of constellation. It would switch the plant off, declare the work done, and leave a mountain in the ordinary sense: a place that has arrived and can therefore be left.

This power does neither.

It does not speak. Speech would be a building, and there have never been buildings. It does not touch the joints or the vents or the gyres the crustaceans are still inventing in the silt. It does not warm the cold or cool the steam. It does not hurry the calves into certainty or the fledglings into song.

What it does is remain.

It becomes another space between the walkers, an extra measure of the thin air they already carry inside their hollow thatch. It becomes one more click in the dry mapping of the birds, a frequency that helps them ride a thermal that has not quite formed. It becomes the pause in the larvae’s rise and fall, the extra patience in the scuttlers’ packing of living slag. To the still-standers it offers only companionship of the same order they already practice: another tall stillness at the lip of a hollow, indistinguishable from stone until the cloud shreds just so, and then only a gleam that might be face or might be mineral.

The deep note comes, the felt increment of rock persuaded upward. The animals treat it as weather. The power treats it as the same. It does not soothe the shove or amplify it. It simply keeps the work from being lonely.

In time—though time here is only another kind of elevation—the power will still be attending or it will have become the next thing the mountain tries: a newer fracture for lichen, a wider joint for a nest that will be abandoned, a breath of steam that finds a throat a meter to the left. Nothing is finished. The cold remains the altitude’s opinion. The light remains a bruise that has not decided to fade.

The power’s benevolence is this refusal to complete. Its wisdom is the knowledge that some places are not meant to be arrived at, only lived inside while they continue arriving. It leaves no mark that weather would not have left anyway. It takes nothing that the seeps and the vents and the waiting have not already offered.

Stand still long enough and you might feel it: not a presence added, but the emptiness used one more way, the mid-process honored by being allowed to stay mid-process. The place continues without needing to be seen. And yet it is seen. That is all. That is enough. The herds reassemble around a seep. Ice forms and unforms. A terrace lengthens. The still ones listen. The power listens with them, and does not finish the sentence.
