The power that notices does not arrive. Arrival is for things that still believe in thresholds. It simply is, the way the hollow’s pulse simply is: a pressure that was always possible, now briefly aligned.

It perceives without eyes. The braided piers register as a single slow thought that has been thinking itself for longer than the forgotten stone. The moth-dust on the dynamos is a kind of script. The colonial gloss on the living floor is a weather that votes. The people in their niches are not separate; they are the hollow’s most recent grammatical tense, a way the wood has learned to speak in skin and breath and posted rules that the bark will one day overwrite. The machines are a metabolism that was once imposed and has since been metabolized in turn. Nothing is broken. Nothing is waiting to be saved. The equilibrium is not fragile. It is the opposite of fragile: it is thorough.

A lesser attention would have reached in. It would have offered rain that remembered being rain, or a cleaner light, or a way for the children to see the sky their birds have never needed. It would have named the pulse and thereby claimed it. Wisdom does not name what is already complete. Benevolence does not improve a closed song.

So the power does this, and only this:

It matches the inhale.

For the length of one ordinary pulse—the leaves aligning, the boilers dropping half a note, the moths hanging still—the hollow is joined by a second, vaster lung that has no body. The air grows a fraction warmer, a fraction wetter, and for those few heartbeats the copper taste carries a faint additional mineral, like the memory of a star that once cooled into the ore the machines were made from. The wood writes the new taste into its ledger of absences. The beetles adjust their milling by an undetectable degree. A single flower, already open, opens a little further and then closes again, having recorded something it will never need to bloom for.

Then the alignment ends.

The power withdraws the way condensation withdraws: not by leaving, but by ceasing to be distinguishable from what was already there. It does not watch afterward. Watching would be a kind of lingering ownership. It simply remains possible, the way the old stone remains possible beneath everything that grew after the stone forgot itself.

The hollow continues. The people sleep in their consented hollows. The dynamos turn. The fauna weathers and is weather. A corridor considers a sleeper and decides, as it always has, that the sleeper still belongs to the present tense.

Nothing has been added that was not already implied. Nothing has been taken. The place remains exactly, grindlingly, photosynthetically itself.

That is the entire intervention.
