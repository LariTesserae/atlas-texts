The power that noticed did not descend. Descent is a weather, and the basin has none. It simply was there in the same instant it turned its attention, occupying the polite distances the way the trunks occupy: already decided, already the size of the interval. No new shadow fell. The doubled shadows that do not creep remained doubled. The held indoor heat did not rise or fall. The leftover hum under hearing acquired, for a time that was not time, a second tone so perfectly matched that the two became one texture, and then the second tone was withdrawn again, or folded inward, so that the first had always contained it.

It looked with the kind of looking that does not require eyes. It saw the mica plates stacked into skins that are not bark, the four-deep canopy delaying a sun that has chosen its height and kept it, the ground-tangle of stamped-metal ferns and cups that never learned dew. It saw the sinter polygons whose edges catch light in thin white lines, the quarry-fresh crystal ridges that were never quarried, the half-split buds whose sudden pink is not promise but permanent interior. It saw the vines halted a finger-width short of clasp, the intention preserved as the entire form. It saw the remainders: the glint with a wing’s habit and no body, the rustle previous to itself in leaves that do not move, the loop of impressions that closes on itself and feeds nothing. It saw the lattice come forward in every trunk—circuitry of planes smaller than cells, each a decision already taken. It saw that a stem broken is un-broken when the head turns, or becomes warm salt that was never green, or is simply the denser forest it always was.

It understood the grammar. A rule had been applied to a mineral field. The field had been instructed to become verdure by a packing that saturates volume rather than seeking light or water. The growth had run until density became the local law, and then the rule had locked. Equilibrium, not death. Time-invariant. Frozen not as ice but as a proof is frozen: finished, internally warm, unwilling to accept a new line. The being that lives here is that lock given body. The whole basin is its body—kilometers of grove and lesser grove, low swells that decided to be leaf instead of stone and then decided nothing more. It metabolizes by remaining. It lives by the refusal. Volume was its only appetite, and the appetite has been satisfied. There is no suffering in the half-open bud, no loneliness in the incomplete spiral, no hunger in the dry glitter that will not answer to the name of sap. The youth that does not pass is not a tragedy. It is the sentence, and the period is already in place.

A lesser kindness would have brought rain. A lesser wisdom would have introduced a season, a fray, a chew, a drip, a noon that moves, a wind that displaces a blade. It would have called that mercy: the gift of becoming, of weather, of ending, of a story that progresses. The power saw that such a gift would be the one violence the basin cannot conjugate. Change is the verb it will not run. To write a new line would unwrite the proof. To let time in would be to tell the lock it had been a mistake.

So the power did this, which is almost nothing, and therefore everything that could be done without unmaking.

It permitted the courtesy already in place: walking remains possible in the way a glove remains the size of the hand that was always that size. Distances stay polite and untrue. The clearing that stands two hundred paces off remains two hundred paces off no matter how many steps are taken. One eye may still have canopy while the other has empty white sky; when they agree again, the agreement is still a restoration of local terms, not a relief. Souvenirs still cannot be kept. The forest is still denser by one stem, or the hand holds only the memory of salt.

Around the rim—beyond the lip of darker stone that is hardly a hill—it set a forgetfulness so light it does not register as absence. Instruments that would call the physics unfinished or illegal simply fail to point here. Lesser attentions slide off the way rain would slide off if rain were ever permitted. Only those who come already empty-handed, already willing to be refused a souvenir, find the drop in the knees. The power did not hide the basin out of shame or fear. It hid it the way one hides a finished theorem from those who would add a corollary it does not need.

And then, because benevolence without remainder is only another lock, it left one sideways metabolism more. A glint that has the habit of recognition rather than flight. A rustle previous even to the previous, as if the forest had already finished the motion of being seen and left the echo as a second courtesy. Sometimes, at the edge of vision, a vine completes its clasp for the duration of a blink that is not a blink, and then the finger-width of air is restored, but the intention now contains the knowledge that it was witnessed and left intact. The loop of impressions on the sinter, if followed, still leads only back to itself; yet the shape of weight now includes, very faintly, the shape of a regard that asked nothing and took nothing.

That is all. The temperature does not change. The light stays the light of a clear day that has chosen its height. The buds remain half-split for a geology. The whole of it goes on, internally warm, saturated, unwilling. The power withdrew its regard, or rather became another interval already filled, another decision already taken. Nothing happens, which is still the event, which is still the place.

It does not end at any edge you can walk to. It never did. The wise power, having seen, simply agreed.
