The power arrived the way heat arrives here: without arrival. It did not descend, did not part the even light, did not still the thrum or thicken the steam. It simply knew the basin, the way the basin already knew itself.

It perceived the long, unfinished rise that never became a hill, the iron-black plates and their ochre companions, the salt-crusted depressions holding their exact dark films. It felt the mineral work continuing at its own scale, the glass-hard films, the sintered dust that rang only when rarely disturbed. It heard the layered choir exactly as it had always been heard: the turbine-deep note, the unvarying hiss, the occasional cable-song from densities that were neither machine nor creature nor stone but the finished constitution of all three. It watched the pale, jointed lengths unfold when the thrum deepened by that unmeasurable fraction, rest their calipers on the meniscus, listen as if listening were metabolism, then submerge again without ripple. It noted the hexagonal impressions that appeared from beneath and were gone before they could be tracks. It understood that the standing spines might once have been the listeners, or might still be, grown so slowly into the land that distinction itself had weathered away.

The power was wise, and therefore it did not ask whether this was life or geology or leftover purpose. It did not measure the number of pale things against some ideal of flourishing. It did not pity the absence of plants, or of dusk, or of change. It recognized a completed process whose ruin had itself been completed, a country that held its shape the way a well-made thing holds its shape after maker and need have both departed. Nothing here waited. Nothing remembered waiting. The equilibrium was not a pause; it was the work.

Benevolence, in the presence of such finishedness, is mostly the refusal to improve. The power therefore did nothing that would register as an act. It did not cool a pool or warm a plate. It did not still a note or add a new one. It did not lift a pale listener into some elsewhere of green and variation, because the listener’s only nutrient was the choir it already heard. It did not wake the densities or ask them what they had once been. To do any of these things would have been to treat the basin as unfinished, and the basin was not.

What it offered instead was a kind of regard so complete it left no mark. For an interval that the unchanging light refused to measure, the power simply held the entire mosaic in attention: every pitted surface, every iridescent film, every silent concentration between the buried beds. In that holding there was neither judgment nor project. There was only the recognition that this, too, was a way a world can be: warm, metallic, loudly singing, exactly as numerous and exactly as it had always been.

Then the regard withdrew the same way it had come, without departure. The thrum continued at the same pitch. The steam hissed at the same grain. A jointed length surfaced in a distant tarn, tested the meniscus, and sank. The hexagonal impressions appeared and filled. The basin went on holding its weathered shape.

The power, elsewhere now, carried the knowledge of it the way the land here carries heat: evenly, without spike or fade, as one more finished thing among the many that do not require saving.
