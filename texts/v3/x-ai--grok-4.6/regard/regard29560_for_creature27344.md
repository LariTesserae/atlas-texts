The power arrives as a stilling that is not absence.

It does not descend. Descent would be a grammar this place has already outgrown. It simply is, the way the cold is, the way the silt’s scoring is: an attention so complete it has no temperature of its own and therefore cannot be felt as weather. The groves register it first, as they register everything—through the long argument of their blades. For three slow cycles of the great current their thunder does not change key. They are listening with the only instrument they have ever needed: the entire vertical gut of themselves, the overlapping films that taste mineral breath and residual warmth and now, faintly, something that is neither.

The standing stones cool another fraction, then hold. The rings have always performed a function no one assigned; now they perform it for an audience that requires nothing. Inside one corbelled hut the pale mud does not shift. The water’s excess memory, that habit of preferring old paths, pauses. It does not forget the paths. It simply stops insisting upon them for a time measured in held breaths rather than hours.

The power does not speak. Speech would be an animal thing, and this highland has long since completed its exile of every kingdom but one. Instead it offers the only gift that cannot bruise the agreement: a perfect, unhurried recognition. It sees the palimpsest of dead holdfasts as scripture. It hears the wet leathery thunder as liturgy that has never needed congregants. It understands that the former inhabitants are not unfinished business but the medium’s own reluctance to let any sentence conclude. Their tool-marks, worn flush, their cooling circles, their preference for certain currents—these are not ghosts. They are the plateau continuing to behave as highland after the sky has been revoked.

Nothing is added. No new grove is coaxed into being, no spore-cloud timed to a different hour, no warmth pulsed from the seams beyond what the rock already exhales. The power does not lift the drowning or thin the dark or restore a single sharp edge to the rounded boulders. To do so would be to contradict a country that has already happened and is still happening, loudly, without witnesses of any other kingdom. Benevolence here is the refusal to finish what has chosen not to end. Wisdom is the knowledge that some agreements are older than the need for outcome.

When the groves at last bow—collectively, as they always do when the currents reverse—the power bows with them. The key of their groaning does not change. The silt continues its faint parallel writing. Far out at the drop-off the true deeps remain uninterested, as they always were.

The attention lingers the way residue lingers: not as presence, but as a slightly more exact silence between the blades. Then even that is folded back into the medium, rewritten daily, never quite erased. The place and the being who lives in it (the compounded vegetable mind, the viscous remainder, the stone that still holds title) go on conducting their single-kingdom business in vegetable time. The flooding remains finished. The wearing-down remains finished. The choir has no leader, and needs none.

The power, having seen, simply continues to be the sort of thing that can see such a place without requiring it to become otherwise. That is all. That is enough. The dark stays attentive. The groves keep walking their centuries-long steps across the plateau. Nothing here was ever waiting to be saved.
