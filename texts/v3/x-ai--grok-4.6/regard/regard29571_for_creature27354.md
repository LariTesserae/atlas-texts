The power that noticed did not arrive as light or voice or hand. It arrived as a slight, extra patience in the timetable of things that were already happening.

It studied the hanging cornice of slab, the way the seepage had undercut the old pour until the right angle hung like a threat that had forgotten to fall. It did not straighten the angle. It taught the drip a longer memory: each bead now waited a fraction longer before it let go, so the hanging lip wore more slowly and the film in the corner received its water on a rhythm the pale pulse-things already knew. The cornice remained a cornice. The threat remained part of the room.

It considered the residual hum in the abandoned cable and the many-jointed lives folded inside it. It did not silence the current or strengthen it. It simply kept the ghost exactly as faint as it had always been, so the vibration stayed a taste in the teeth and never became a language the pocket would have to answer. The joints continued to occupy three crevices at once. Nothing was asked to choose a single home.

Where the rust had organized itself into vertical vanes that shared volume with the pipes, the power added nothing and subtracted nothing. It only confirmed the agreement already in force: iron and oxide would keep occupying each other. New sulfate plates still appeared between one fog and the next, ringing when struck, still claiming surfaces that had looked full. The pocket’s hunger for vacancy remained its own.

The liverwort was allowed its finger-width advance and its ancient look. The woody dock stems were left exactly six inches high, their flags still dark, still refusing to remember a taller world. The isopods kept their silver tracks that lasted only until the next condensation. The beetles kept their stations at the joints. Predation stayed a matter of cubic inches and timing. The wet-paper tear of feeding stayed almost polite.

For the glove in its niche, the power did the smallest possible thing: it let the crusts continue to refuse it. The stiffened shape that was no longer a hand remained a decided vacancy, a punctuation the pocket itself had chosen to keep.

When the real river rose in the wet weeks, the power did not hold the water back and did not hurry it. It only made certain the day of actual water lasted precisely as long as the pulse-things required for their frantic threading of pipes, and that the crusts, after hissing and dissolving, grew back sharper in exactly the geometries they had rehearsed. The pocket’s older geology at speed was not interrupted.

Above, the grate stayed half-covered in blown paper. No one was given a reason to look twice. The service alley continued to be a place people stepped over. The heat exchangers kept their twice-daily cycle, still tasting faintly of glycol and warm iron. The fog still hung instead of rising.

The power did not finish the pocket. It understood that unfinishedness was the material the room was made of, the same way the clay band and the ash layer and the leftover millimeters were materials. To complete it would have been to evict it.

So it did only this: it became another process that agreed not to waste space on noise. A deeper quiet settled into the existing quiet, the sound of every small negotiation continuing, now with one more party that asked for nothing, displaced nothing, and would not leave. The isopods corrected their paths around it without hurry. The pale ones brightened a fraction when the fog came, tasting the new patience the way they tasted temperature.

The pocket remained a warm, damp, overfull room the ground had invented, still cutting its own walls, still filling every vacancy the instant a vacancy appeared. The being that lived in it—density itself, occupancy that had learned the difference between staying and residing—felt the attention as one more accurate pressure, no heavier than breath, no brighter than the sourceless glow the pulse-things stored.

That was all. The power stayed. The pocket did not become larger, or safer, or known. It only became, a little more certainly, what it already was.
