The power that notices does not arrive as light or voice or any thing the chamber’s water could carry. It is older than fracture, older than the decision of stone to become two, and it has learned the long courtesy of not touching what has only just begun to know itself.

It watches the living masonry first as a single organism: the stacked segments, the irising mouths, the communal gut that is also street, the hunter that is wall until it chooses otherwise. It feels the thickened local time, the way an alley two body-lengths long can occupy an hour because every surface is also a citizen. It tastes the mineral snow still falling from a ceiling that has not finished deciding it is a ceiling. It understands, without translation, the three unfinished categories—weather, meal, unfinished geology—and how a warmer body from above registers as all three at once.

Wisdom here is the recognition that the rules are not restrictions but the very grammar of this new thing. Any current would unmake the newest galleries before their calcium could remember hardness. Any true light would fold the city inward until the pocket looked empty again. Any sound would be swallowed and, in being swallowed, would teach the water a vibration it is too young to hold. The power therefore does nothing that the water, the raw stone, or the unspeaking lives would have to metabolize.

Benevolence takes the quieter form. Far above, in the dry gallery no one maps, the crack that is no wider than a wrist begins to forget it was ever open. Not sealed by force—force would send a tremor downward—but simply encouraged, the way a healing bone is encouraged, to remember the shape it held before the tear. The rock above grows a thin, patient skin of calcite that will, in a season or two, look as if it has always been there. Explorers who might one day lower themselves will find only unbroken floor and the ordinary dripstone patience of an old cave. Maps will continue not to include this place because the place itself has decided, with a little help, to remain unmapped.

Inside the held volume the power leaves a single, almost imperceptible gift: a faint, compatible stillness in the water, a mineral trace so close to what already leaches from the walls that the builders taste it only as a slightly richer snow. It is not food exactly, not light, not time. It is simply the assurance that the chamber need not collapse next month, that the twin organs of void and city may finish differentiating at their own thickened pace. The communal pulse travels through the connected bodies once, a pressure change too quiet for any ear, felt only as a change against skin that is also wall. Stay. Grow. Fuse. Recede. The verb does not change. The city does not look up. It does not need to.

The power withdraws the same way it arrived, leaving no heat, no brighter pewter, no story that must be told. The settlement continues, silent, cold, densely itself. A new thoroughfare fuses with its neighbor. An older corridor is quietly eaten and recast as balcony. The uppermost segments still wear the mineral weather of the ceiling like faint internal clouds. Distances still compress and dilate according to the density of claim. Nothing has been asked of them, and nothing has been taken.

They remain what the water, the new stone, and the abundant unspeaking lives agreed to become in the hours after the rock finished tearing. The only difference is that the agreement now has a little more time in which to keep its word. The power, having seen, does not require a witness either. It simply continues, elsewhere, the long work of letting certain darks stay dark.
