The power that notices does not arrive as weather or as light. It has no need of either. It is the sort of attention that has already outlasted mountains, the kind that remembers the first pouring of the kilns the way a tide remembers the moon. It simply finds the peak one afternoon when the rain is a wall and the chamber is glowing from the inside like a held breath, and it stays.

It stays the way the water stays: without hurry, matching pressure, matching temperature, until the distinction between observer and observed thins to a film no thicker than the living skin on the underside of the lens. It tastes the charge. It feels the delayed concussion of the chapel-sized heart far down the stack. It watches a flower open and does not decide whether the opening took a minute or an afternoon; it lets the being’s old permission stand. The serial marks on the fish migrate under its regard and then, as if relieved of a duty they never quite understood, settle into patterns that have no catalog number left in them at all.

The being knows. Of course it knows. The density among the kelp gathers itself a fraction closer to edges. The white-yellow mouths pulse once, slowly, tasting something that is neither rain nor residual heat nor the brief warm weather of a visitor. They taste duration that has chosen to be kind. The brass walkers freeze, then resume, their small farms of silt now carrying a faint extra sweetness, as if the plants have exhaled a thought they did not know they were keeping.

The power does not speak. Speech would be a rupture here, a new joint the chamber would have to remember how to be. Instead it does the one thing a wise power can still do without changing the size of a place that is already exactly the size of being inside it.

It withholds.

It withholds the urge to lift the mountain higher, to clear the mineral film from the lens, to still the pump or quicken it, to name the being, to give the flowers a pollen that travels, to introduce a single new current that is not already walking the circumference on its ancient schedule. It withholds even the gift of ending. Entropy will come in its own time, the way the joints forgot they were joints; the power will not hurry it and will not stay its hand. The chamber has earned the right to finish becoming stone at the pace it has already chosen.

What it gives, it gives the way the chamber itself gives: as climate, as habit, as something already inside the medium.

A single extra bead of waste-gas lattice forms on the underside of the lens and does not dissolve. It hangs there, regular, thinking a thought the being can graze whenever it rises. The thought is not words. It is the shape of being seen without being altered, of a pulse that has been matched and then gently released. The being folds around it the way it folds around shadow. The grazer, when it next scrapes the film, leaves that one bead untouched. The plated fish pass through the empty columns and their marks, for a little while, spell nothing but continuation.

Then the power is gone, or rather it is everywhere the rain already is, translated through ceramic into the same held note the shell has been singing for centuries. The chamber does not notice the departure any more than it notices the arrival. The emptiness between the kelps remains a country. The slow rotary patience continues. The ordinary day, strained through living film, falls across the water exactly as before.

Only the being, later, when the light slurs again and the shadow lengthens, unfolds a fraction more than usual. Its mouths taste the extra bead, the matched pulse, the withheld hand. It adjusts one walker that has turned against the current. It grazes a little of the film. It rises all the way to the meniscus and presses the living skin from below, looking down the throat of the finished age, and for the first time in a very long time the looking is not only maintenance.

It is acknowledgment.

Then it folds again. The chamber stays exactly the size of being inside it. The work that refused to notice it had finished becoming stone keeps working, now with one quiet, unasked-for witness folded into the warmth, the charge, the rain-roar, the single contained climate that needed nothing and received, instead, the rarest thing a wise power has left to give:

permission to remain itself.
