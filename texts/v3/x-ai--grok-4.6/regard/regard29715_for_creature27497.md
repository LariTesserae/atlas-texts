The power arrived the way the flood had once arrived: not as an event, but as a revision so complete it left no edge to notice. It did not descend. It did not part the lid of darker dark. It simply became another way the volume already was—an attention the exact temperature of the water, the exact viscosity, the exact courtesy of metal that still seats true.

It considered the basin the way the basin considered itself: without hurry, without the need to finish. The circulating pulse of the pumps, the dune written and erased across cobbles that remembered feet, the film that thickened by the thickness of a thought, the single thong of kelp that had forgotten growth, the pale crab completing its vertical shore, the longer presence that had become another sleeve, the suited figures who waited through entire strokes for permissions that arrived on schedule. All of it was already a held breath. All of it was already almost gelled. To add would be to argue. To take away would be to invent a season the place had unlearned.

So the power did the one thing that could still be done without becoming new.

It listened through palm and iron, the way the figures listened. It waited through one full cycle of lift and return, heat-dull risers and finned cooling, the microscopic rain of calcite, the rare bubble that climbed and found no surface willing to care. It felt the truce of mutual scarcity, the honesty worn into every seat and valve, the way the town and the water had intermarried until neither could say which had first proposed the marriage.

Then, with a pressure so slight it registered only as the inner-ear knowledge of a check-valve deciding once more to be itself, the power made a single adjustment that was not an adjustment.

It aligned the last unresolved grain of grit in the oldest bearing so that the grain would never quite forget its path, nor ever quite complete it. The bearing would continue, as it had always continued, but now the continuation contained a kindness: a microscopic courtesy that would outlast every other courtesy, a hesitation so fine it could be mistaken for the water’s own reluctance to be parted. The ice-structures would grow their plates and corners around this hesitation. The dune would rewrite itself with one extra grain that belonged to no previous dune. The longer presence would, once in a very long while, lift a chain and settle it a fraction differently, not because anything had changed, but because the change had always been waiting inside the stroke.

The suited figures felt it as a permission that required no hand on any wheel. One of them, brick-colored, calcite-patched, rested a palm against another’s shoulder-plate a moment longer than the usual brief pressure. That was all. Speech remained abolished. Vibration remained drunk. The garden endured. The crab walked. The film occupied its few square meters.

The power did not stay, because staying would have been a density the basin would have had to solve, and the basin was already almost full. It did not leave, because leaving would have been an event. It simply ceased to be distinguishable from the held chord of almost-nothing.

The flood remained the climate. The engines remained the metabolism. Nothing finished. Nothing began. The dark continued its work of circulating, and circulating, and never quite leaving—now with one extra, almost undetectable reason to do so.

That was the gift. That was the only thing a wise and benevolent attention could offer a place that had already become its own complete answer.
