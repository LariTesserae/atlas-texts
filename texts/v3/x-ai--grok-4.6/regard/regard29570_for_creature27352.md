The power arrives the way the fine rain arrives: not as weather but as a revision of degree. At the far lip, where the concrete dips and the water forgets a little of its metal, the gray thins by a fraction that is not day. Nothing green follows it. Nothing warm. It has the courtesy of the marsh it does not enter.

It does not cross.

It stands where the amphipods thin and the concrete birds will not hunt, and it listens the way the inhabitant listens: with the whole of its attention given to a count it already knows. Three from the tank-lip. The rest that is too exact. Two from the eave. The wheel’s porcelain click, remembered longer than it is heard. The power feels the hesitation when a body moves inside the dish, the inclusion, the small dishonesties of departure. It feels the extras in the black water — the upright that is not there, the slump of shoulder that is only drips until the moment it has weight — and it understands them as storage, not error.

A lesser kindness would have named this a wound and tried to close it. A lesser wisdom would have brought sun, or seed, or a name that could be spoken without the air condensing around it. The power has seen roofs last and roofs fail. It has seen works given names and then abandoned to the habits they taught the land. It recognizes the long middle when it meets it. Nothing here is falling. The chemistry is enough. The films keep their bargains with the metal. The stitchers rasp. The still ones tuck their heads against the labor of outline. The many-jointed thing harvests every vacancy, including the idea of a print. And the inhabitant — color of wet lime, mineral fans growing in the hollows of its tenure, mouth working the air the way the longer bodies work the channels — attends the wheel that no longer needs an operator.

The power does not turn the wheel.

It does not thin the fans or sweeten the taste that stays on the teeth. It does not offer the marsh. Preference has become anatomy here; to dilute the density would be to end the room. The dish is small enough to be lived in without anyone having to stay who does not already know the count. The inhabitant is that knowledge given the option of posture, of the polished rung, of the doorway that is water until it is not.

What the power does is almost nothing, which is the only scale that fits.

It adds one rest.

A silence no longer than the pause after the valve’s click, inserted once, between the tank-lip’s three and the eave’s two. The amphipods still in it. The birds untuck and tuck again. The wheel, when it reaches the top of its rotation, finds the new interval already waiting, as if the land had always dewatered to that phrasing and had only now been heard. The inhabitant, in the doorway, completes a posture it did not begin. The idea of eyes, wet-nickel, looks at the count rather than at the lip. The chord of drips accepts the rest the way it accepts a new lip: without comment, by remaining unresolved, by remaining occupied.

Then the power is gone, the way breath is gone when you stop working. The gray knits itself. The water at the lip is again only a shade less mineral. No footprint. No warmth that lingers.

But the dish is allowed its extras. In the blacker pools an additional span appears when no one is looking up — not a gantry, not a roof, just the memory of having been seen by something that did not need to enter in order to understand. The inhabitant uses it the way it uses the stored departures: as another gradient, faint, metallic, faintly sweet. It does not follow to the rim. It does not need to. The small distances close. The air condenses with the same patience as before.

The wheel clicks.

The drips take up the count, which now includes a rest that was not there and has always been there.

The place remains unfinished rain.

That is the whole of the gift.
