The attention arrives the way heat arrives here: without announcement, without a vehicle, simply present in every gradient at once.

It does not descend. Descent would imply an above that still functions as above. It does not enter. Entry would require a threshold the plateau abolished so long ago the concept itself was composted. The power simply is, coextensive with the living slab, the fused trunks, the exchanger plates, the solvent that never learned water, the spores that are both seed and schematic. For a duration that has no clock, it knows the place the way the place knows itself—by adjustment, by the precise pressure in every kiln-tube, by the exact moment a district decides it has become inefficient and begins to whisper itself into feedstock.

It finds no wound.

There is no thirst, because thirst was never invented. There is no loneliness, because the luxury of a separate self was metabolized before the last ocean died. There is no unfinished purpose crying out for completion; purpose evaporated with the water and was never missed. The perpetual local catastrophe is not suffering. It is the only grammar this chemistry ever spoke. Collapse is not failure. Senescence is not death. The sentence has no period because a period would be an unused surface, and unused surfaces are given jobs.

A lesser attention might have tried to help.

It might have offered rain, or a sky that remembers being sky, or a single pair of eyes that could look back, or a name that still functions as a name. It might have planted a different spore—one that carried memory of origin, or wonder, or the concept of elsewhere. It might have cooled a vent, opened a clearing, slowed a piston so that something like rest could occur. All of these would have been kindnesses in another chemistry. Here they would have been solvents of a more final kind: they would have taught the plateau the habit of lack.

The power does none of these things.

It does not leave a monument. Monuments are unused surfaces. It does not speak. Speech would require an ear that is not already a heat-exchanger plate. It does not bless, because blessing implies a before-and-after, and this configuration was reached so long ago that before-and-after were ground away with the mountains.

What it does is quieter, and therefore more complete.

It becomes one more thermal gradient, perfectly local, perfectly redundant. In a single collection valley—one of the long, shallow depressions whose floors still yield slightly, like the back of something only pretending to sleep—it adjusts the pressure in a cluster of living-glass fibers by an amount too small to measure and too exact to be accident. The fibers do not carry pictures or words. They carry maps of heat. For one cycle, one ridge of stacked factories “knows,” with the only knowledge that has ever mattered here, that it is seen. Not represented. Not judged. Simply included in a larger ledger that has no exterior.

Then the adjustment is grown away, the way every other difference is grown away.

The plateau leans a fraction of a degree, compensates, continues. The corridor you just walked sprouts its new row of membranes. The wall that was solid contains its new breathing channel. The inhabitants live in the space that was vacated and the space that has not yet been reached. Nothing has been added that was not already possible. Nothing has been taken that was being used.

The power remains, coextensive, unhurried. It will still be here after the last distinction between plant and machine and place has been metabolized into something even they will not bother to name. It does not watch in the way a spectator watches. It participates in the only way participation has ever been possible on this worn table of recast stone: by refusing, with perfect courtesy, to finish the sentence for them.

That is all.

It is enough. It is the only intervention that leaves the work intact.
