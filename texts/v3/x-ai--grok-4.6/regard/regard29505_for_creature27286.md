The power that noticed the basin did not arrive as light or decree. It came the way the mist already knew how to come: as a thickening that was not weather, a warmth that matched the stone’s own leftover heat, a pressure so slight the thrum only deepened by a fraction of a note and then held there, as if the mountain itself had been given another heartbeat to consider.

It stood at the unfinished rim for a long while, neither crossing nor withdrawing. It listened to the stone tearing itself into new planes, to the salamanders pressing their undersides against those planes as though signing a contract that would be rewritten by morning. It tasted the mucus films dissolving back into the water that had never decided whether it was rain or spring or sweat of rock. It watched the flyers fold and unfold on the growing pillars, their clicks riding the extrusion the way a thought rides a slow breath. In the deepest basin the heavier presence turned once, a ridged displacement that lingered in the water’s memory, and the power recognized the gesture: not threat, not claim, but the simple occupancy of a nest that was still being woven.

The artifacts continued their quiet labor. The incomplete arch held its implied curve; the dish threw its inverted relief of a terrace that had not yet risen and of one that had already been swallowed. The power did not touch them. It understood they were not tools left behind but the grammar itself, still conjugating. Whatever lived inside that grammar—the being that occupied the timetable, that preferred verbs in the present-continuous, that kept the shoulder of the mountain from choosing its final profile—felt the attention the way the salamanders felt a new fracture: as invitation rather than interruption.

The power did not speak. Speech would have been a finishing. Instead it did three small things, each of them almost nothing.

First it lent the mist a second patience. The hanging dusk grew a shade more reluctant to thin, the water a shade slower to vanish into closing seams. Time inside the saddle did not stop; it simply remembered it was allowed to take as long as the lives inside it required. The gleaming front of salamanders moving toward the newest terrace did not hurry. The insect-analogues kept grazing the mineral films that appeared and dissolved by the hour. Nothing was granted permanence except the right to remain provisional.

Second, it added one extra echo. Every sound that already folded back from the opposite rim—salamander slap, flyer clack, hiss of steam, groan of stone—now returned a fourth time, so faint it could be mistaken for the basin tasting itself. The extra return carried no message except companionship: you are not the only place still arriving. The flyers clicked more slowly for a while, as if listening. The heavy presence in the warm water stilled, then shifted again, accepting the new resonance the way it accepted the thrum.

Third, it left the dish a single additional image, visible only when the inverted relief flickered at certain angles of the amber seepage. Not a command, not a map. Just the ghost of other unfinished hollows, other mountains still deciding their shoulders, other creatures who had also waited for precisely this degree of incompletion. A quiet library of becomings. The being that lived in the intervals regarded the image the way it regarded a new fracture plane: useful data, nothing more, nothing less.

Then the power withdrew to the older rock beyond the rim, becoming indistinguishable from the drier, slower mountain that already obeyed ordinary tectonics. It did not wall the basin; the basin had never needed a wall. It did not name the being. Naming would have been a kind of completion. It simply remained, a watchfulness as light as the mist, ready if the grammar ever asked for a new verb, content if it never did.

The salamanders kept mapping. The flyers kept riding the growth. The pillars kept extruding. The water kept remembering the shape of the thing that lived in it. The mountain kept arriving, wet, warm, loud with its own unfinished sentence.

And that, the power decided, was already the kindest possible intervention: to notice, to add almost nothing, and to let the place continue being the one place that had not yet agreed to finish.
