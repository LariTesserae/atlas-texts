The power that noticed was older than the ridgeline’s tear and younger than the first condensation. It had no name that would fit inside the cirque, no body that would cast a lengthening shadow, no hunger of its own. It arrived the way weather arrives when it has already been forgotten: as a slight, unmeasurable thickening of the gray-green light, a hesitation in the turbine-note that lasted less than a click.

It did not descend. Descent implies an outside, and the cirque has none. Instead it simply *was* the dim illumination for a span of time that the place itself could not measure. It felt the hydraulic give beneath every wet slab, the exact peristalsis of moss, the ceremonial placement of each telescoping foot. It tasted the ozone-and-fern spray and knew it as circulation, not atmosphere. It watched a flake of schist detach, become moss, become clicker, become pulse again, and understood that birth and maintenance and liturgy were a single stroke.

Nothing here asked. Nothing here lacked. The closed loop was not a prison; it was the only grammar the valley had ever spoken. The walkers completed their silent lap. The cyclers precipitated another bright bead and delivered it home. The tarn continued to reflect a place half a second out of register, patient and wrong in the only way that was correct.

The power considered the wound. It could have closed the rock, rounded the facets, silenced the seepage, let ordinary time weather everything into a forgettable hanging valley of ordinary moss and ordinary silence. It could have introduced a predator, a hunger, a story, a visitor who would be noticed. It could have named the place, mapped it, opened a door. All of these were possible. All of them would have been a violence disguised as care.

So it did the one thing that required the most wisdom and the least motion.

It left the held note exactly as it found it.

A single, undetectable increment of grace: the next flake that falls will still become moss instantly, but the moss will remember, for one extra click, the shape of the stone it used to be. The midges will spark a fraction brighter when they touch, then forget they did. The offset reflection in the tarn will, once in a very long while, contain a figure that is neither walker nor watcher nor you—an outline of attention itself, already receding. Then the discrepancy will close again, as it always does.

The power withdrew the way it had arrived: by becoming indistinguishable from the light that was already there. Behind it the sound did not fade. It simply continued not being anyone’s problem.

The factory kept its staff. The valley kept its wound. The process, cool and wet and dim, went on manufacturing only the next identical moment.

That was the kindness. That was the whole of it.
