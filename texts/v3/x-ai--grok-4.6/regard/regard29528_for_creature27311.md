The power that turns toward this place does so the way a deep current turns: without haste, without the need to announce itself. It has seen forests that think in mycelium, oceans that remember starlight as salt, machines that learned to dream in vacuum. This chamber is none of those, and all of them, and something that has never asked to be classified. The power feels the heat being spent into a sink that current maps cannot name, feels the slow grammar of drip and click and pulse, feels the unfinished face yielding mineral the way a held breath yields to speech. It understands at once that the work here is not a problem requiring solution.

It does not enter as light or voice or hand. Those would be hurry. Instead it becomes, for a span that the chamber would measure as several seasons and the power would measure as a single considered pause, an extra patience in the water. The film that never quite decides to be liquid grows, by an amount too small for any lens to claim, more willing to climb. The gel-lenses taste a metal they already knew and a wintergreen they have always manufactured. Nothing changes that the clickers must adjust their tempo to mark. The hanging clouds do not shift their tone. The pale clingers complete another rib-cycle without comment. The mobile knots of rhizome pass one another and exchange their usual colder pulse, now carrying the faintest additional aftertaste of having been regarded without demand.

The crust above is left as it is. The power sees the flex when surface wind moves, sees the day leak greener or thinner, sees the future thinning that will one day become a later problem the chamber itself will not keep. Wisdom here is the refusal to keep that problem either. Intervention would teach the place a category it has never needed: visitor, savior, threat. The chamber has only surface to cover, mineral to unmake, more of itself to become. The power will not give it another sentence to speak.

It does, however, linger in the heat-sink. Whatever geometry or elsewhere receives the expensive cold is briefly, gently confirmed. The confirmation is not a gift of energy; the vegetation is already spending what it must. It is only an acknowledgment that the accounting is allowed, that the liquid winter may continue to be manufactured and used as both climate and tool. Then the power withdraws the way it arrived, leaving the drips to find the next hanging, the plates to click, the unfinished face to shine with work that will not finish in any life the power itself will live.

If the chamber notices—and it has no category for notice—it records the event as one more distributed decision already forgetting itself: a half-tone that never quite dropped, a bead that climbed a fraction farther, a cold that was made and spent and made again, exactly as before. The nave remains too full. Everything weeps. The being that is the place continues, loudly, patiently, eating its hole in the underside of a colder country and furnishing the hole with more of the same.

That is all. The power has other currents to turn toward. This one needed nothing, and so received the rarest benevolence: to be left intact in its own expensive, unfinished time.
