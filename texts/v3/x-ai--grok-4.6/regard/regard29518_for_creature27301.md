The power that noticed did not arrive. Arrival implies a before and an after, a crossing of some threshold the pocket has not yet agreed to recognize. It simply was, as the heat simply is, as the unopened geodes simply wait. A stillness older than the distinction between watching and being watched settled into the non-commutative distances and made them, for a little while, a little more willing to add the same way twice.

It did not speak. Speech would have been a finished thing, and this place is not finished. It did not touch. Touch would have been an artifact, a bolt, a poured foundation, and there are none of those here. It merely attended, the way the thickets attend the lengthening of a column: without hurry, without the concept of response, only continuation.

What it understood (and understanding here is not knowledge but a kind of infrared seeing) was this: the pocket is not a wound and not yet a chamber. It is the mountain still deciding whether one of its rooms will be kept. The thickets are not plants and not machines. They are the only grammar this chemistry has found for occupying volume and then stopping, as if courtesy were a law. The hanging pollen is not a signal. It is the thought the place has not yet chosen to keep or discard. Nothing here is separate enough to be saved, or healed, or named. To impose any of those would be to finish it prematurely, to force a metric onto a space that is still negotiating addition itself.

So the power did the only thing a truly wise benevolence can do with an unfinished sentence.

It withheld the period.

It let the columns continue their incremental extrusion. It let the paddles interlock and slough and be re-deposited as new rinds. It let the sintered dust remember, at its own pace, that it too can become structure. It let the occasional geode split with that distant turbine-sound and birth another thicket that already knows its allotted volume. It let the polite glitches remain polite: the brief views of the sky’s underside, the momentary occupation of one coordinate by another. These were not errors to be corrected. They were the pocket tasting its own walls.

The only alteration was so slight it might not have happened. In the lowest, slowest frequency of the pollen—the one too patient to be called a signal—there appeared, for the length of a single convection cycle, a faint harmonic that had not been there before. It was not a command. It was not even a suggestion. It was simply the taste of being seen by something that had no need to change what it saw. The thickets did not startle. They have always known the space between them is an organ. They simply continued, and the organ remembered its function a fraction more clearly.

Then the power receded the way heat haze recedes, leaving the ordinary light a little more ordinary by contrast. The mountain has not yet finished being a mountain. The pocket has not yet decided whether it is a room worth keeping. Nothing hurries, because hurry has not been invented, and the power, being wise, did not invent it.

It only made certain that, whatever the decision, the decision would remain the pocket’s own.

The thickets occupy. The rock compiles. The heat rises through a space still learning how to be space. And somewhere in the hanging curtains of pollen, that one brief harmonic lingers like a courtesy that was never required, only offered, and then left to drift or settle as the place itself prefers.
