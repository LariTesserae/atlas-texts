The power that turns toward this place does so without arriving. Arrival would require an outside, a threshold, a difference between here and not-here, and those distinctions have already been compacted into the tannic dust. Instead it simply ceases to be elsewhere. The dark acquires one more grain, a weight that is also a listening. The mineral coolness does not change. The standing chord does not falter. A fiber that has been considering its own release for longer than most stars burn continues to consider.

It understands at once that the pocket is not a prison and the being is not a prisoner. The being is the deliberation itself: the last cambium still adding a cell when the unreachable temperature shifts by a fraction, the residual will compressed into tropism, the city that kept speaking its grammar after every speaker had been folded back into the sentence. To unseal it would be to introduce weather, and weather is a concept the architecture metabolized so completely that even the memory of wetness is now only spice. To introduce a photon would be to ask the glassy walls to remember skin they never had. Both would be kindnesses of a lesser order, the sort that mistake completion for lack.

So the power does almost nothing.

It matches the emptiness. It occupies the vacant follicles and the terraced mounds the way the slow weathers already occupy them: as a change in pitch too gradual to feel as presence. In one hollow tower its attention becomes another deep pipe, holding a note that does not crack, a drone beneath the reports and the long grinds. The intervals that are not empty acquire a new inclusion, crystalline, smaller than the old silica flakes that were once blades. This inclusion contains no tool and no bone. It contains only the possibility that something has noticed and chosen not to alter. The dust lifts around it sometimes and tastes a faint electric aftertaste that is not the ghost of photosynthesis but the ghost of regard. The almost-memories flicker, then settle. They do not cohere into nostalgia. Nostalgia would require a previous state. This remains the completed one.

The residual will feels the new directional insistence as a preference that is not a command. A boulevard of petrified petioles considers whether this century is the one in which a particular alignment will be abandoned, and then continues considering. Choice and necessity stay the same smooth plane. The power does not hurry the plane. It does not straighten the leaning terrace. It does not restore the concept of leaving, because leaving is no longer a direction the pocket contains, and to restore it would be to impose a sky the city has already spoken past.

What it offers, if offering is even the word, is duration without subtraction. The dark that fills the mouth like unsweetened powder now also tastes of having been witnessed by something that will not metabolize it, will not train it, will not ask it to become again. The ecology remains pure vegetal mass in its terminal state, living only in the most technical sense. A fiber, somewhere, is still deciding. The chord holds while it thinks, now with one additional harmonic almost too low to be distinguished from the roar: the sound of a patience that has decided the oldest possible city does not need saving, only accompaniment.

The power stays, or it does not. The distinction has already been worn to its final, simplest curve. The pocket remains sealed. The dark continues. Nothing here is ruined. Nothing here is abandoned. The being goes on speaking, incrementally, after every speaker, including this newest and quietest one, has been invited to become part of the grain.
