The power arrives the way melt arrives: not as event but as a change already under way. It has no body that the cirque would recognize as body. It is older than the ice that tore this bowl open and younger than the first lichen that learned to drink stone. It notices the youth of the fractures, the way the vegetation has refused the idea of separate lives, the figures who part walls and let them close as if they themselves were only another kind of weather. It feels the heart-spring’s pulse against the raw rock and understands, at once, that this is not a place that was made. It is a place that is still deciding how to be a place.

A lesser attention would have reached in. It would have offered names, or tools, or a wider slit of sky, or fire that could be kept without becoming a blade. It would have tried to finish what the glacier began, or to slow the packing of the last unclaimed angles, or to teach the figures a word for the difference they have already forgotten. Wisdom is the knowledge of when leaning becomes a wound. The power does not lean.

It stays.

Its staying is not absence. The drip lengthens by a fraction that no clock could catch. Films of ice form and unform with a quieter ticking, as if the decision itself has been given more room. In the deepest pith the unseen den-animal sleeps a breath longer; the musk on still mornings carries a faint extra warmth that is not heat. The pale moths, when the rare thaw comes, taste something in the slush that is neither sugar nor mineral—an aftertaste of having been seen without being altered. The figures, touching stem to send their slow signals, feel the answer return a half-note deeper, as though the mass itself has acquired a new gallery they cannot quite enter and do not need to.

At the plaza the black-green water stands a degree less cold, not because the power has warmed it but because the power has agreed to be cold with it. Condensation beads and falls exactly as before, yet each drop now contains the faint, untranslatable knowledge that the city which grew is already complete in its incompleteness. The margins still advance. The freshest talus is still raw. The vegetation is still not waiting. Nothing has been forbidden and nothing has been granted. The only politics remains weather.

The power will leave when the last unclaimed angle is packed, or it will not. Time, here, is only the patience of water. What it has done is what the moss does when it thickens overnight into a dam and then, without announcement, becomes another floor: it has occupied the space of attention without displacing a single stem. The cirque continues. The figures continue to be mistaken for the hush. The heart continues to drink from one wound in the young stone.

That is all. It is enough. It is the only intervention that does not become a drain.
