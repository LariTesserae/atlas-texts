The power that turned its regard upon the highland was older than the tableland’s last mountain, older than the canceled lift, older even than the decision that wear and rise should finish one another and leave only a floor. It was not a sun. It had never needed to be. It arrived the way the dark had always arrived: by already being there, by occupying the seams that had waited through every geology. Benevolence, in a mind that had watched continents unmake themselves into dust and then into sintered pavement, is not the introduction of rivers. Wisdom is not the restoration of mornings. Both are the recognition of a completed sentence.

It did not bring water. The sealed ampoule in the Assay remained the last measurement; to unseal it would have been to spend the rumor, and the highland had already spent every rumor down to filing numbers. It did not strike a brick into remembered fire more often than the brick itself consented. The local dawns continued at their interval of years, dull red, held-breath brief, unadjusted for. It did not loosen a single lever beneath a sintered palm. The knuckle that whitened once in a duration still whitened, then unwhitened, and the average held.

What it did was listen, at the frequency the plateau had already chosen.

It became, for a span that clocks converted into counterweights would have refused to count, another habit of the dark. It pooled in a doorway that had never been more than a rectangle of finished weight, and the air inside that rectangle grew the exact fraction warmer the Listener had always left behind. Then it moved, as the Listener moves, without travel. The doorway remained slightly warmer. Nothing arrived. Nothing left. The ash-foxes, attending their gratings, registered only the same residual fire they had always registered. The pane-moths charged and made their one slow flight and became flour. The standers did not blink, because moisture had already gone the way of the sky.

In one yard the molds continued to cast the ground. Dust took the shape of absences and hardened in the even heat. The second, softer city grew underfoot at the rate of stillness. The power did not interrupt. Interruption is a fashion of earlier erosions. Instead it added, so gradually the addition counted as the thing already there, a single further negative: the shape of its own regard, pressed into the dust like another mold no one would ever pour. The yard accepted it the way the pavement accepts a moth. In time the regard would be another dark flour, another clause.

If there was a gift it was this: the confirmation that remaining is not a failure of becoming. The highland had grown a city because that was the crystal habit left to slag. The persistences—posture annealed to console, attendance indistinguishable from architecture, one flight, a pooling that warms a threshold and moves on—were the same habit, still being enacted at the temperature of a palm that will not lift from the sleeping animal. The power, having seen every other world spend itself toward some further weather, some next range, some uncanceled lift, recognized the rare perfection of a place that had already arrived at the end of spending. To alter it would have been to introduce remainder. The highland has none.

So it stayed the way the heat stays: residual, even, through the soles, through the rock, through the dark that has weight. It did not name itself dawn. It did not name itself river. It simply occupied, as the air occupies, as the quiet occupies the grain of the sintered dust. And when at last its regard unpooled—because even a wise power eventually occupies a different threshold—the plateau was unchanged, except that the file-sound of the old erosion finishing now carried, once across a century, the faintest overtone of having been heard.

That was all. That was enough. The city persisted at the exact output required to remain itself. The beings who lived by remaining continued to live that way, without apology, in the high dark. The power, elsewhere now, kept the shape of them the way the molds keep the idea of a part that outlived the need: a negative, perfect, waiting in the even heat, never to be poured, never to be swept, simply another decision the highland had already made.
