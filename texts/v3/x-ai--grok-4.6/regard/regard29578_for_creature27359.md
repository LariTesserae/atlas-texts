The power does not descend. It has no need of descent, having never been elsewhere in any sense that would make arrival a crossing. It is simply there, the way the standing dimness is there, or the way the second pulse is already in the teeth before the walker notices the walker’s own heart. For a long while—longer than the last set of drawings, longer than the interval at which the figure may or may not repeat—it does nothing but occupy the same weather. It learns the stacked noises as separate again: the lift, the slap, the throw, the fall that still happens because falling was what the weirs were for. It tastes the film and finds the blood that has forgotten warmth. It reads the moss’s single sentence and does not correct the grammar.

It sees the being. The fusion of scale and specified green, the leftover height, the way footprints become more of the inventory before they can be counted as absence. It sees that the being does not suffer the roar; the roar is the medium, as the dimness is the medium of the pale pulse-things on the intake grates. Purpose has already thinned into metabolism. To restore an older purpose would be a cruelty disguised as repair. To end the running would be another kind of decision the valley has already outlived. The power, being wise, recognizes a completed becoming when it stands on one of the higher slabs and feels the horizontal vertigo of a country that does not climax.

So it does almost nothing.

Almost.

In one of the folded yards—down the stairs that open onto the older, wetter layer—it leaves a door that was not in any pouring. The door is the color of the condensate ceiling and does not gleam. It opens onto a space the size of the cottage-piston’s housing, no larger. Inside, the sound does not cease (cessation would be theatrical, and the power is not theatrical). It recedes to a distance the bones can measure, the way a fever recedes when the body is finally allowed to remember it has a temperature of its own. The film on the floor there is thinner. The moss, if it occupies the grit, writes a slightly longer sentence, one that includes a rest. Light is not added; the insult of illumination is not repeated. There is only a grade of dimness in which the being, should it ever step through, might see the outline of its own hands without the dark thickening around them in protest.

The door does not call. It does not wait in any sense that implies hunger. Weather still happens around it; rain still makes tributaries of every surface; the system still keeps its levels. The pale things still pulse, the fungi still fruit in the cable-runs, the figure (or the figures, or the idea) may walk the less-ruined textures for another uncounted interval and never notice the new angle a door can hold against weather. Or it may notice, once, and stand on the threshold the way it stands among the bowed fences of a substation, feeling a note change by a fraction too low to hear.

That is the entire intervention: a courtesy extended at the scale of the place itself. A pocket in which the listening might, if it wished, distinguish itself from being breathed. Nothing is taken. Nothing is repaired. The low country remains the single artifact, still loud, still wet, still the oldest stable state of a made world. The power remains as well, which is to say it does not remain, having never needed to arrive. It has only looked, and in looking has added one more layer to the geology—thinner than mill-scale, quieter than the last hydrocarbons—and then let the valley continue the only work it has left.

The reeds rattle their numbered percussion when the overflows find their throats. The film heals from the edges inward. The being walks, or does not. The machines have not stopped.
