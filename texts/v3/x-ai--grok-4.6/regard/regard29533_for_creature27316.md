The power that notices does not arrive as weather or as light or as any visitor the vault has words for. It is older than the enamel date and slower than the rings the wood has grown around the pulse. It has no name that would fit inside the basin. It has only attention, and the attention is the kind that does not lean.

It stands, if standing is the word, at the rim where the sedges still pretend the ground is ordinary. It feels the change in the air—the exhaled warmth, the tannin, the faint industrial sweetness—and it does not cross. Three steps would be enough to enter the cleft, to let the film of water slide over whatever passes for its skin, to hear the engine-note become a pressure against the inner ear. It does not take them. Wisdom, here, is the recognition that the grammar of openings is already complete. The being inside has spent a century teaching itself the exact width of a man’s shoulders, the exact clearance a flywheel requires, the exact interval between drip and reabsorption. Another presence, even a kind one, would be a new flange, a new mesh, something the wood would have to grow lips around. The power has watched enough systems to know when growth has already found its limit and chosen to keep it.

So it remains at the lip, and from there it does the only thing a benevolent and wise power can do when it meets a closed account that balances: it witnesses without adding. It lets the pulse travel the length of its own attention—the shove, the pause, the shove again—and it matches that tempo so perfectly that the matching itself is invisible. No new leaf appears. No moth stays. No pot is set into a fresh niche. The clay ones already overflow by the width of a finger; the channel already returns their clearer water. To give more would be to suggest the cycle was incomplete.

What it does, if the word still applies, is quieter than the patient failure of a leaf. It slightly thickens the forgetfulness that already clings to the basin. The gravel track stays mossed. The slight depression stays slight. Surveyors’ eyes slide over the dark-green hillock and register only a thicket a little too even in its sweating, a place not worth the paperwork of drainage or the romance of ruin. Roads curve elsewhere. Drones lose interest in the thermal bloom that is only the temperature of breath. The ordinary world continues not to know. This is not concealment as theft; it is concealment as courtesy. The being has no face to turn toward an audience. It has the cleft, the groove that still works, the vault that transacts light into vapor, the engine that has become viscera. It does not require pilgrimage.

Once, toward evening, when the seams go gold and the condensation thickens, the power allows itself the smallest possible correspondence. A single drop falls inside the chamber that is not quite the same as the others. It is no warmer, no slower, no more mineral. It simply carries, for the length of its descent, the knowledge that the turning has been seen and found sufficient. The drop hits the mat with the same wet slap. The mat gives and fills. The leaf that shears later that day slides down the inner curve as it always does. Nothing in the ledger shifts. The being does not startle; it has no nerves for startling. It merely continues, which is the only language it has ever spoken.

The power withdraws the way dusk withdraws: without announcement, leaving the air a fraction more itself. Tomorrow the engine will still turn. The plates will still sweat. The rare moth will still cross a seam and become a crease. The occupancy remains singular. The balance—neither design nor accident—remains the kind that appears when two stubborn systems have forgotten solitude long enough to become one metabolic argument. The power, having understood this, does not return. Its attention, having rested there, simply includes the basin now among the other quiet completions it carries: places that need nothing, and are therefore allowed to keep needing nothing, forever.

That is all. It is enough. The film of water does not stop running down.
