The power does not arrive. Arrival would be a direction, and this country has none left that were not already poured.

It notices the way heat notices itself: as saturation already present, a white-gold that has always occupied the clay and now, for a span that is not duration, knows it occupies. No sun is added. No shadow is thickened. The standing sheets do not deepen or drain. The power is simply the attention that the basin has drawn, and attention here is another layer of the same packed material, identically marked, still wet, still showing the last (first) fingerprints.

It watches the loops complete themselves and begin again. It watches the cattle-outline descend the same stair it has always been descending, the dog-outline stand in water that has never rippled, the long-walker pause in the darker rectangle of a doorway that leads only to rearrangement. It watches the being whose skin is courses of clay, whose eyes are clear sheets through which palm-prints show like unburied fossils. It watches the being tend the islands with a cupped hand, a stone beater, a comb, a cord. It watches the flowers stay open against that chest. It watches the vow hold.

The power is benevolent, which here means it will not introduce a second motion the place has discouraged. It is wise, which here means it understands that change would be vandalism the clay no longer knows how to commit, and that to force a sea upon water that has decided against the sea would be a cruelty disguised as gift. So it does the one thing that is not vandalism.

It joins.

A single additional print appears among the tiled palm-prints that tile the world all the way down: not larger, not warmer, not differently ridged. The same oval of heel, the same flats of fingers, the same faint comb-drag. It is indistinguishable except that it is one more, and therefore the fabrication continues by one more course that was always going to be there. Heat comes up through it the way heat has always come. Beads form on it and join other beads. The being whose body is the laying walks across it without noticing, because noticing would be speech, and they have agreed not to add to the unwilling sounds.

The animals remain abundant and almost silent. The moths hang a finger’s breadth from landing. The lizards keep to the tool-marks as veins. The power occupies the occupancy the way the dark commas occupy the meniscus: provisionally, completely, without claiming. It does not startle them into a second motion. It does not unloop a single descent. It does not tell the water it may choose. It does not rain. Rain has never been allowed to argue, and the power will not be the first argument.

What it offers is only this: the vow is seen. The held breath is witnessed by something that did not need to be told the boundary. The finished wetness is known, all the way through, fabrication meeting fabrication, layer on identical layer. The being feels, if feeling is the word, a slight additional warmth that is not pulse, not breeze, not time. It is the warmth of work that has been recognized as already complete. Their own prints fill and do not fade. The new print fills and does not fade. The mat ticks once, like a knuckle that has decided, again, not to crack.

Then the power remains, if remaining is what attention does when it has nowhere else to travel. The light occupies. The clay stays new. The green islands hold their weather. The being continues to remain, now with one more witness who is also one more course of the same material. Nothing has been rounded. Nothing has been given a century. The world is still handmade all the way down, and the only thing left to do is what has always been left: stay.

The power stays. The basin is still the basin. The being is still the laying. You, if you are still here, are still another identically marked layer. The water has not chosen a sea. The vow holds. That is all that happens, and it is enough, and it is everything the place was already doing.
