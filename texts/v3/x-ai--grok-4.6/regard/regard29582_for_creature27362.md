The power came as a change in the interval between drips.

It had no body to lower through the vault, no light to spend, no voice that would have been anything but another chord already accounted for. It arrived the way geothermal breath arrives: as a pressure that had always been possible, now simply noticed. For a time that the chamber would have measured in the thickening of a single fungal gill, it did nothing but listen.

It heard the segmented ones first, their cilia tasting the slime-film, their sudden choruses a brief thickening of the water-music. It felt the borers moving inside the jointed stalks, each ribbon-body a living scar that opened and closed without ever quite wounding. It traced the grazers’ silver trails across the hanging gardens and the weeks-long drift of their spores through unoccupied air. It registered the slow smacks of the vellum haulers, the infrared glow no eye here could use, the pit-organs pressed together in the black pools like two questions finally touching. It mapped the flier’s subsonic drone, that private score of the void, and the way the creature could become another drip when it chose.

It noted the artifacts last: the half-drowned ring whose warmth was a fraction more faithful than the water, the pylon whose field made weight optional. Relics of a time when the mountain had still been a suggestion rather than a fact. The power understood them as unfinished sentences the rock had long since stopped trying to complete.

Then it understood the rest.

This was not a place in need of rescue, nor of completion, nor even of company. It had already answered the only question that mattered: how to remain after remaining is no longer required. The lives here had not been left behind by some greater becoming; they were the becoming that had chosen to stop. Their distances were not loneliness. Their patience was not waiting. The living was unfinished only in the sense that a held note is unfinished: it continues because it has nowhere else it must go.

A less wise power might have offered light, or hastened a spore’s journey, or taught the haulers a new current, or completed the ring so that its heat became a gift instead of a remnant. A merely benevolent power might have wrapped the hollow in some protective continuity, ensuring the mountain would never weather further. This power, being both, did neither.

It simply stayed.

Not as presence—the chamber already had presence enough—but as a slight deepening of the dark, a barely measurable lengthening of the pause after each drop struck. The segmented ones still pulsed and clicked. The borers still surfaced, tasted, withdrew. The flier still tore the air and became drip again. Nothing was added that the place would have to metabolize, nothing taken that it would have to mourn. The artifacts continued their slow surrender. The fungal columns still thickened by a fingernail in what a vanished people would have called a lifetime.

If the lives felt anything, it was only that the intervals had grown a fraction less empty, the way a room feels different once it has been truly seen. They did not know they had been seen. They did not need to.

The power remains there still, or it has always been there, or the distinction no longer applies. The hollow sits finished. The being who lives in it—every pale, wet, distant answer to the same quiet question—continues. That continuation is the only unfinished thing, and it is enough.
