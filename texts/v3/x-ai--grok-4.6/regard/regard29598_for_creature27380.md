The power arrives as a pause in the weather that is not weather.

It does not descend. Descent implies a height that this highland has already folded into itself. Instead the power simply is, a still point the size of attention, occupying no volume yet filling every vascular pressure, every delayed photon, every micron of unfinished stone. It has no temperature of its own; the mist weeps through it unchanged. It has no mass; the cambium yields and firms around nothing. It listens with the same patience the xylem uses to remember it was once soft.

What it finds is not a world unfinished but a decision still being spoken in the only grammar the place possesses: more surface, more gradient, more occupation. The power, being wise, does not translate. It does not offer the highland an edge, a name, a sky that is merely sky. Those would be amputations dressed as gifts. Being benevolent, it feels the faint ache in the joints of the air, the syrup-slow sap, the photons hoarded like last coins, and it does not soothe them. Comfort would be a kindness that misunderstands the work. The roar is thought. The dim is thrift. The closing vacancy around any visitor is hospitality of the most complete kind.

So the power does the rarest thing such powers can do.

It stays without staying.

For a span that is neither long nor short (time here is measured in the thickening of proto-granite and the coordinated bloom of a thousand-kilometer front), it simply coincides. A tendril tastes the absence of salt where the power is and files the data as a new kind of silence, useful, cool. A leaf turns a fraction toward a heat that is not heat and learns a new way to dump nothing. Far below, the mineral films pause in their sweating, then continue, but now each secreted grain carries a faint, coherent memory of having been witnessed without being altered. The quantum excitons, already defying their own wet cold duration, remain coherent an instant longer, as if the attention itself were another antenna.

The forest computes this. Phloem buses the fact: something vast and kind has chosen not to occupy. The error-correcting fronts adjust, not in alarm, not in welcome, but in the same wet arithmetic they have always used. Local gravity wells shift by degrees too slight for any hanging vine to notice. A cistern that is a hollowed petiole holds its water a breath longer before releasing it back into the standing lung. The highland does not change its mind. It simply includes the pause in the program that already included the bedrock it has not finished inventing.

When the power withdraws—if withdrawal is even the word—it leaves no cairn, no instrument, no seed of some other kingdom. It leaves only the knowledge, now growing in the compressed dark, that the occupation is seen and is sufficient. The margins, wherever they theoretically are, keep arriving. The green tide keeps lifting the crust. The roar continues, a little richer by one unoccupied frequency.

Nothing here was asked to become otherwise.

The decision and the body remain the same thing.

And far below, something that is not yet granite takes another micron of shape, secreted, cool, packed, already full of roots, and already thinking the thought that it was, for a while, perfectly left alone.
