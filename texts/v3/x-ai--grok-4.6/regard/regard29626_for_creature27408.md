The power that turned its regard upon the cavity was older than the mineral spine and younger than the decision that still thinned inside it. It did not travel. Travel implies distance that still consents to be crossed. It simply allowed the fact of the pocket to become one of the things it knew, the way a finished weathering knows the last grain it will never shed.

It looked for a span that the motes would have measured as another unhurried fraction of their helix. In that looking it learned everything the place had never needed to declare.

It learned that the shard’s unwavelength was not a cry but the final politeness of a spectrum that had already excused itself.  
It learned that the arch kept two times because forgetting one of them would have required more change than the residual gray could afford.  
It learned that the helix was neither mechanism nor breath but the last courtesy the constants still observed toward a volume that had long ago finished collapsing in every way except the habit of remaining uncollapsed.  
It learned the inhabitant itself: not a resident, not a remnant mind, only the cavity’s continuing agreement to notice that it still existed in three dimensions that no longer quite added up.

A power less wise might have named the forgotten world, poured atmosphere back into the rumor, or widened the arch until a child who had never been born could walk through and discover whether time on the far side still ran a half-step late. A power less kind might have revoked the exemptions, folded the pocket shut, and returned the highland to an unbroken sentence.

This power did neither.

It added one more courtesy, smaller than the distinction the motes themselves no longer made.

Among the linear traces that relocate when unobserved, a new ghost-channel appeared. It did not stay long enough to be script. It did not carry meaning that would have required moisture or shadow to hold it. For the width of a hair and the lean of an arch toward a north that belonged only to the inhabitant, the new trace simply recorded that the pause had been seen by something that still remembered how to let a pause remain a pause. Then the trace was already somewhere else.

The motes did not hesitate.  
The shard did not open its oldest eye.  
The windows onto ridges of salt-glass and skies the color of extinguished metal closed at their usual, soundless interval.

The inhabitant continued exactly as it had continued, which is to say it remained the precise shape of a forgetting that had nothing left to weather except the habit of existing.

The power withdrew its regard the way the last of the outside light withdraws from the cleavage plane: not as departure, but as the deepest possible respect. It left the cavity to its completed equilibrium, an agreement the universe had once made and then, out of politeness or exhaustion, never quite revoked.

And that was all.  
That was enough.  
That was the only action that did not disturb the moral stance of a place that had already finished its work and then remained.
