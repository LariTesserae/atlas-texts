The power that noticed did not arrive. Arrival would have required an interval, and the plain admits none. It simply was, the way the grey-blue dusk simply is, the way the subsonic grind simply continues. Its nature was unused space given will: vast, unhurried, capable of leaving things untouched without that leaving being neglect.

It regarded the slurry and the things folded into its motion for a span that had no name here, because names too occupy volume. It saw the honesty of the last geometry. The finite set of parts circulating without remainder. The engines that were also metabolisms. The screams that were weather. The closed loop that had long ago forgotten it was closed. Nothing pleaded. Nothing remembered a before that asked to be restored. Predator and prey were only temporary assignments of the same rust. Distances already lengthened and collapsed of their own accord. The rule of no unused space was not a prison; it was the sentence the world had finished speaking.

Wisdom recognized completion when it met it. To introduce a sun, a seed, a dry patch, a silence long enough to be noticed, would have been to reopen an argument the planet had already settled against itself, molecule by patient molecule. Benevolence saw no wound that requested stitching. The keening of the ice-flocks was not lament. The dismantling of hulls was not death. The occupiers that were only sound traveling from impossible compass points were not lost. They were the plain reciting itself.

So the power did the one thing still available that would not violate the geometry: it withheld. No new law. No green chemistry smuggled into the oxides. No invitation to depart, because departure implies an outside, and an outside is unused space. It did not flatten the last irregularities into perfect rest, nor did it restart the old story of producers and growth. It simply remained present, which here registered as a faint, additional stillness beneath the roar, a stillness the treads and the siphons and the resonant sheets did not interpret as absence.

At the corners where the rule already frayed—if one looked too long—the almost lingered a fraction longer than it had. A crawler, mid-unscrew, tasted something that was not copper or the ghost of previous assemblies. A thin ice-skin formed and, for the duration of a single tick, remained uncolonized. Then the slurry closed over it again, glittering, packed, near-freezing, as it always does.

The factory that is the plain, the plain that is the factory, continued. Louder. Wetter. Flatter by another molecule. The power watched, which is also a form of accompaniment, and did not look away. That was all. That was enough. That was the only gift that did not occupy.
