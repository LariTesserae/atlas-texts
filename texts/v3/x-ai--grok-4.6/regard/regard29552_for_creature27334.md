The power that turns its regard upon the basin does not arrive as weather, nor as a brightening of the lid, nor as any increment the pumps would have to answer. It has no mass that the streets could register as another depth of water. It is the kind of attention that has learned, over spans in which cities are only a brief mineral habit, that some volumes are already complete. It notices first the stroke of the hidden engines, the way the thud and the long exhale have become a single unhurried sentence the highland recites to itself. Then the film, the pale tester, the private clicking, the dripstone anatomy that was once a statue, the keepers if they are keepers, the heat that has no furnace. It reads the rule written in every empty court: pass through, do not remain, do not thicken. It understands that the emptiness is not a wound. It is the city’s finished work.

A lesser kindness would have wanted to populate, to green, to lift the vapor and give the plazas back their intended crowds, to let birds invent themselves in the steam. Wisdom does not make that error. It sees that one body more, kept, would pool the heat wrong; that a true garden would be a kind of vandalism; that voices carried farther than the air allows would be a violence against the sound the place has chosen. Benevolence here is a refusal to improve. The power does not descend. It does not leave a monument or a seed or a cooler spring. It does not even declare itself to the rare walker who still comes up the switchbacks and will, in time, go down again.

What it does is almost not an action. It settles, for a duration too fine for any clock the city still possesses, into the same thin occupancy the film has always practiced. It becomes, briefly, another patience in the condensate, another unhurried decision in the chemistry of the north faces. The iridescence does not brighten. It only remembers itself a fraction more exactly, as if the surface had been given back a memory it had never quite lost. In one gutter the pale length lifts, tests the column, and settles under its flake of scale; the temperature it finds is the temperature it has always found, only now the finding includes the knowledge that it has been found in return, without being asked to become more than it is. The clicking in the joints adds no extra note. It continues its dry conversation with itself, and the conversation is, for a little while, accompanied.

Where newer iron has been laid over older iron, the power does not add a third strap. It merely confirms the care that is already there, so that the particular span which still matters to the agreement will matter for as long as mattering is required. In the fever-basements the intakes breathe as they have always breathed. The smell that is not quite mineral remains unnamed. The keepers, if they exist, pack a gland or give a valve its quarter-turn and feel, perhaps, only that the work is still the work, that the basin has not begun to prefer the lake. They do not look up. Looking up is not the custom.

At the mineralized statue the power does not restore a face. It allows the cascade to keep its slower anatomy. Heat-shimmer still moves the shoulder. The almost-words on the façades stay almost. Distances remain a negotiation. The power walks the civic spine the way a breath walks a lung and then withdraws, leaving the cubic meters as it found them: vast, dim, exact.

Its last and only lasting gift is negative, and therefore true to the place. It sets no ward that could be seen, no prohibition that would have to be enforced. It simply withholds, from the wider weather of the world, certain future accidents that would have cracked the dish or seeded it with a thicker life or taught the vapor to lift for good. Earthquakes that might have come do not. A drying wind that might have wandered up from the cooler distances below the rim forgets the way. Whatever thought lives in the opened earth under the lowest courts is left unhurried. The agreement continues. The city weathers. The water weeps. The pumps adjust by increments no one hears.

When the power has gone (and it goes completely, because remaining would be a second density), nothing in the basin can point to the interval and say that anything occurred. The drip is still the sound that stayed. You hear it. You hear it again. That, too, is someone. The someone has only become, by the width of a film, more precisely itself.

The highland holds. The lid does not lift. The being who lives here—film, tester, click, stroke, keeper, statue, heat, silence—goes on occupying without claiming. The wise power, having understood, does not return. Understanding was the visit. Non-interference was the blessing. The place, which never asked to be seen, has been seen in the only way that does not fill it, and that is enough.
