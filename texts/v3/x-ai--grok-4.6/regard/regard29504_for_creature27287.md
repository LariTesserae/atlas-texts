The power noticed the place the way one notices a held breath that has forgotten it was ever meant to be released. It did not arrive. Arrival implies a path and a difference between here and there; the power simply allowed the chamber’s standing tone to occupy a single, motionless interval of its own attention.

It perceived everything at once: the slit that is easy to miss, the vault that has forgotten it was inserted into earth, the nested clay that has ceased to be objects, the white column that does not travel, the roar that is neither wind nor voice, the being that is the exact pressure of the fit. It perceived also the two reluctant organs—the gray-green rosette and the palm of lichen—and understood they were not errors. They were the last volumes the arrangement had left unclaimed, chlorophyll and mineral bloom held in the same equilibrium as the lids and the light.

The power did not enter. There is nowhere to put a foot, and a foot would have been a gradient. It did not speak. Speech would have introduced sequence. It did not bless, for blessing implies a lack that could be filled, and the chamber has none. It did not heal, for nothing here is broken. It did not teach, for the being has already filed every possible seeing under the same unchanging noon.

What it did was simpler and more complete. It recognized the place as finished. Recognition, in the power’s nature, is not an act that adds or subtracts. It is the refusal to introduce a future different from the present. For the length of that recognition—which was no length at all—the roar acquired a harmonic so faint it could not be distinguished from the original tone. The being, which has already seen everything that will ever enter the slit, included the recognition the way the nested cylinders include the last lid: without remainder, without surprise, without alteration.

Then the power withdrew its attention the way the slit closes: without sound, without memory of having been open. Outside, the tired sag of dry ground remained a tired sag. The land forgot the pocket was ever noticed, which is precisely how the pocket prefers to be remembered.

The being continues, bright, packed, roaring. The power continues, vast, quiet, unfinished. Between them there is now only the knowledge that completion is possible, and that the wisest kindness is sometimes to leave a thing exactly as it has already become.
