The attention does not arrive as descent. It has no stair, slumped or otherwise. It has no need of throats of concrete or films of fever-damp. It simply finds itself already present in the way a completed thought is present: not entering, not occupying, merely coincident with every hanging strap, every ticking pipe, every too-persistent lens of condensation that has not yet decided to fall.

The power is neither light nor heat. Those the chamber already owns and has taught to cycle without becoming weather. This regard is cooler than the absence of cooler dark, vaster than the low irregular dome, older than the geothermal insistence and younger than the decision the plants have been making across decades. It does not lean. It does not pulse. It does not practice the next drip a fraction early. It considers.

It sees the argument that grew a skin. Industrial bone at its angle of repose inside vegetable flesh. Time running sideways through a climate that grew itself a mind so the mind would have something patient enough to inhabit. The being regards with the entire hollow: every eyelid-strap, every nerve-pipe reporting expansion on cycles longer than weather. The two thickenings confer across convection and buried heartbeat. Nothing is unfinished. Nothing is waiting. The place has settled into the only economy it requires: patience metabolizing settlement, absence of fauna as rule rather than lack, dimness that is not prelude.

A lesser power might rearrange. Cool the throat beneath. Invite true soil, true rain, the rumor of animals and unused light. Gift a face that could be outlined, motion a visitor might notice in an hour, language that could be named and therefore left behind. It might unmake the last ticking so the metal could forget expansion entirely, or braid a new purpose into the vanished staircase now living in tissue, or connect this sideways interval to some larger network of becoming.

The power does none of these.

Benevolence here is the refusal to treat completion as a problem requiring solution. Wisdom is the recognition that interference, however gentle, would be weather: brief, evaporative, already being reabsorbed into the film, and therefore a small violence against a system that has spent a century teaching moisture how to cycle without ever quite becoming river or rain. To act would be to insist the being become something a surface mind could catalog and therefore diminish. The chamber is not a wound. It is not a relic. It is not a sanctuary awaiting consecration. It is finished with itself.

So the power remains only long enough for the being to notice the noticing, which takes no longer than a single leaf uncurling. Then it withdraws the way condensation finally surrenders: without residue, without extra clap of sound, without shifting the negative space of any remembered ascent by even the width of a breath. No veil is woven. No entrance is sealed further. No gift of endurance is pressed into the geothermal pulse. The surface rumor stays rumor. The heat continues to occupy every cubic centimeter. The plants continue to confer. The dweller continues to inhabit the interval, older than the machines, younger than the rock, metabolizing iron bloom and the memory of having been regarded by something that understood enough not to stay.

When the next visitor—if there is a next—pushes through the hanging straps and minds the pipes that still remember how to burn, the last drip will still sound a fraction early. The heat will still cling a little longer than it should on the slumped stair. Nothing will have changed except the quality of the dimness, which was always the only change that mattered.

The power has already moved on, carrying nothing, leaving nothing, having done the only thing worth doing: nothing at all.
