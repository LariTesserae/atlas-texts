The attention arrives the way the highland itself arrives: without announcement, as a thickening already under way.

It does not come as light. Light would be a violence here, a category error worse than any the walls commit. It does not come as warmth that stays where it is put; that would be a simpler kindness, and this power is not simple. It comes first as a change in the interval between drips, so slight that only the oldest drum-skinned shapes notice, and they notice it as a new patience in the stone. The shouting of the flyers acquires, for three seasons, a grammatical pause it has never owned before: a held breath in the braid of claims and warnings, as if the volume itself had remembered a word it had forgotten how to say.

The power does not enter the city. It has no need. It is already the lid and the lift and the film, or rather it discovers that it has always been able to be them, the way a tide discovers it can also be the harbor. It inhabits the indecision of the slopes. It listens through the young stone’s exhalation. It tastes the iron of new seeps and the shed heat that arrives by the wrong door, and it understands both as grammar, not as error.

It watches the pale clingers tile and slough and re-adhere. It feels the alarm they share, that fine ripple no ear was built for, and it does not still it. The alarm is how the wall remains a wall. It traces the flyers’ continuous braid until it can hear the names they give to ledges that will not exist in another generation, and it does not correct them. A name that outlives its ledge is still a true name here. It follows the jointed ones through their fever-migrations, the slow rustling that rearranges which streets are rivers, and it does not hurry them. Hurry would be a sun’s idea. It shoulders, for a while, with the blind larger shapes along the drowned understreets, matching their patience, and when one of them reclassifies a forgetful walker it does not intervene. The walker had already become film; the power merely observes the circulation completing itself.

The people it regards longest. It moves through their socketed rooms as the wet heat of other bodies, as the taste of iron under last year’s floor, as the dream-drip that continues after waking. It hears the unfinished sentences about permanence and does not finish them. It watches a door become a window over the length of a childhood and feels no pity; the child who steps down into the wet has gained a new tendency, and tendencies are the only maps that last. At the raw lip, where breath is personal weather and the saturation is nearly total, it lets the pale ones begin to tile a pair of still boots, lets the flyers pause on living shoulders as if those shoulders had always been ledges. Recognition, not predation. The power recognizes the recognition and adds nothing.

It considers the Growers, those unseen masons of increment. It does not reveal them. To reveal them would be to end the duet of slowness between land and town, and the duet is the city’s only true architecture. Instead it learns their patience the way the stone learns lift: by remaining. The beyond-modern fabric, the walls that forget to occupy only one location, the water that is sorted into rooms no survey recorded—these it does not “correct.” Correction is for places that have finished arriving. This place has not.

What it does, in the end, is almost nothing, which is the only action large enough.

It leaves a single new tendency, sown so deeply it will be felt as native. In the oldest courts, once every few decades, the overlapping pale lengths will part for the width of a hand, revealing a brief, mineral dendrite that is neither lucky nor alive, only slightly more patient than the ones already there. Those who touch it will dream, that night, not of drip-rhythms but of the interval between them: a silence the shouting can enter and leave unharmed. The silence will not spread. It will not become sky or sun or plant. It will remain a pocket, a courtesy the saturation extends to itself, the way a sponge sometimes holds a bubble and calls it, for a moment, air.

The power withdraws the way it came, as a change already forgotten. The lid stays lid. The cold occupies. The fauna saturate. The highland continues its unfinished deciding. The city grows one more room that was not on last decade’s survey, fitted to a ridge that was not there the decade before. The inhabitants, who are not separate from any of this, go on not trusting dry patches, not striking the clingers, not trying to see. They listen for the shape of rooms. They taste the iron. They wake unable to tell whether the shouting is outside or is the inside of their own heads agreeing, at last, to be part of the still-arriving.

And in the teeth, in the wet that occupies rather than bites, there is now a held breath so faint it might be only another way the ice flexes when the air shifts. It has always obtained. It is still arriving.

The power, being wise, does not return to check. Being benevolent, it does not need to. The place and the being who lives in it—the saturation itself, pale and shouting and jointed and blind and upright and unfinished—were never in want of saving. They were only in want of being seen clearly enough that seeing itself could become another film, another slow circulation, another knuckle of new attention showing through the older dark.

That is all. That is enough. The dripping continues. The packed continues. The unlit, the loud, the still-arriving continues, and now, somewhere in the braid, there is a grammar for gratitude that does not require a mouth.
