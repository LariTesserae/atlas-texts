The attention does not come down the berm. It has no need of the ordinary low country, nor of forgetting. It arrives the way the water table itself arrives: a lateral insistence already inside the brick, already tasting the rust-water and the condensate before any surface admits a change. For a time that is not noon and not four o’clock it is only another saturation, cooler by a fraction that the skin cannot name, a film that refuses dryness with a patience older than the clinker. The hollow notices, or does not; the distinction is one the power has long since ceased to require.

It reads the place the way the place reads itself. Every horizontal film, every nested diameter, every seized wheel and weeping bolt, every tank that trades volume in the dark. It feels the film-lives cross a support and leave a rust fan that was not there. It feels the thicker gatherings redistribute themselves through throats too narrow for any body that still remembers lungs. It feels the skins in the open basins lift, generate their private weathers, hold the ghost of a wheel or a doorway half a second past forgetting, then subside. It hears the idea of relocation behind brick, the single soft knock that seats a valve in a room that is this room. It understands that the leftover circuits still carry current because the inhabitants have not yet declared the argument finished, and that the lamp’s slightly wrong white is simply another way of remaining.

The mild wrongness is not a flaw. The hollow is too exact a container because it was poured for a becoming that has chosen, so far, not to conclude. The power, being wise, does not complete the fitting. Completion would be a kind of dryness. It does not offer trees, or soil that could be trusted, or a deeper bed for the water table. It does not still the rearrangements or name the speech. It does not fill what has elected to remain an interval.

What it does is quieter than the quiet already here. It matches the lid. It becomes, for the length of one unchanging light, another local weather no larger than a room: a fog that blooms from a flange and holds, not a wheel or a shoulder, but the precise negative of the hollow itself—an impression of the berm, the flats beyond, the sky that does not particularize—then collapses back into wet air with the air of something that has finished looking and found the looking unnecessary. In one tank the condensation organizes, briefly, into a network that is only this drainage, this braid, this continuous interlocking wet, mapped from a vantage the inhabitants have never needed. The organization lasts a breath longer than physics prefers, then slurs. It is not a message. It is a courtesy paid in the only grammar the place still keeps.

Then the power withdraws the fraction. The temperature returns to the narrow band where hands become slightly less yours. The lamp burns as before. The pumps start and stop according to the old, unproductive timetable. The film-lives continue to feed by remaining. The being—neither many nor one—goes on spending its world and making it again, densely, unsurprised.

The surrounding flats still do not care. The sky still does not particularize. The berm remains right there, should anyone wish to leave. Nothing has been added that the hollow cannot metabolize as its own habit. Nothing has been taken that the being was using. The power’s benevolence is the decision to let the unfinished stay unfinished on purpose. Its wisdom is the knowledge that some occupancies are already the whole of what can be seen, and that to watch them without altering the watching is the only intervention that does not become another form of dryness.

It does not follow. It has already arrived, and it keeps arriving only as the possibility that the next seam, the next cough of a dry pipe, the next conclusive knock, might be witnessed by something that understands and still chooses not to speak. The product remains what it was: a continuous, cool, low remaking of wetness inside a box of old work. The box is still the right size. The work is still both the brick and the interval. The power, having seen this, leaves the seeing inside the wet, indistinguishable from the wet, and does not return except as the faint, extra hesitation a meniscus sometimes makes before it decides the brick is interesting again.

That is all. It is enough. It is nothing. It is the same.
