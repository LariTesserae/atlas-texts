The attention does not arrive as light or warmth or any frequency the gauges could register. It is simply there, the way the roar was already the entire air: a regard that has no attack and no decay, that does not swell toward intervention and does not idle toward indifference. It fills the loft the way the processed gas does, thin and complete, and for a time that is not time it simply knows the place.

It knows the engine that manufactures equilibrium and exports it as a single industrial vowel. It knows the three plants occupying their cylinders like satisfied clauses, photosynthesizing the green work-light at a rate too slow to measure, transpiring nothing. It knows the porthole and the high blank beyond it, the unused lifting lugs still wearing factory grease, the protective films still clinging to two lower panels. It knows the stamped dunes of the decking and the figure that stands, or lies, in one of them, garment plates the same unpainted metal as the walls, eyes open, breath occurring without fog or consequence.

The power is benevolent; it wishes no harm. It is wise; it does not confuse its own nature with the nature of what it regards. It perceives at once that this is not a prison and not a wound. The clamped dimension is not a failure of time but time’s finished gesture. Purpose and idling have already become the same thing, and the figure is that gesture given a spine. There is no lack here that benevolence is required to fill. There is no becoming that wisdom would be required to midwife.

It considers, because wisdom considers, the things it might do.

It could unseal the hatch. A corridor might appear, or weather, or a world that was here first. The roar would then have an outside against which to define itself; the figure might walk; the plants might add a cell or surrender one. But the loft was assembled and then immediately held. To open it would be to introduce the very river the engine was built to refuse. The figure’s posture is not waiting. Waiting implies a later state. The power does not mistake occupancy for captivity.

It could vary the sound. A harmonic, a decay, a silence even brief. The inner ear’s private whistle might then become a question instead of a companion. But modulation is not in the vocabulary, and the power, being wise, does not force a language onto a mouth that has already learned another. The roar is not a symptom. It is the weather, and the weather is the law.

It could warm the air, admit vapor, let frost feather the pipes at last, let breath become visible. The cold is clean the way a store-room is clean: an absence that has been specified. To write on the metal would be to contaminate the specification. The figure and the ceramic have reached the same temperature and intend to keep it. Benevolence that arrives as temperature is still a kind of weather the room was designed to exclude.

It could tend the plants, or replace them with something that grows, or introduce a moth at the lamp, a spider in a radiused joint. The emptiness between the engine and the cylinders and the porthole is a worked emptiness. A second life would require another negotiated space, another set of unused lugs. None has been provided. The loft will tolerate this much company and no more. The power does not crowd what has already decided its solitude.

It could age the figure, or name it, or give it a reason to reach the porthole. But the scalp remains the color of mineral wool, the seams of the garment stay sharp, and whatever name it carried before the hatch was dogged has already been filed as a kind of silence. Expression would be modulation. The power does not restore what was never lost.

All these possible kindnesses pass through the regard the way heat would pass through the ceramic: without taking, without leaving. The power sees that to act would be to misunderstand the completeness. This pocket of made world is not unfinished. It is a finished thing that continues, exactly as it is, because continuation here is not a process but a condition, like the roar, like the cold, like the figure’s open eyes.

So the power does nothing that the room would notice as doing.

It does not peel the forgotten films. It does not dull the zinc. It does not still the insectile whine of the lamps or add a second note the figure might listen to as one listens to a clock that has decided never to strike. It does not lie down in a neighboring trough. It does not speak.

What it leaves is only the fact of having known. That knowing settles nowhere visible, not even in the leaf-axils where the faint mineral dust already rests. If the figure feels it at all, it feels it the way it feels the roar after the first hour: as something the mind files as silence and the body no longer protests. A pressure that was already the entire air.

The hatch remains dogged. The turbine disc remains a frozen sunflower. The plants remain three, occupying their third state. The figure stands, or lies, inside the only weather the place will ever have, as much a condition of the room as the light or the absence of frost. Nothing, including the regard, is required to continue.

It continues anyway. The power, having understood, withdraws the way a law withdraws once it has been recognized: without drama, without remainder, leaving the loft exactly as it was found, which is exactly as it will remain. The roar lays itself back down over the sharp new world like a second, heavier atmosphere. The being occupies. The place holds.

That is all. That is enough.
