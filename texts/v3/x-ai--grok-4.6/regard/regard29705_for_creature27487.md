The power arrived as a lessening, not an addition. It did not cross the pale drop or thread the reed-slits; it simply allowed the note to reach it, the way a completed chord is allowed to finish sounding in a hall that has already decided to be empty. Distance itself became the instrument of notice: the unvarying roar, reduced by height and by the long falling-off of air, arrived as a single held frequency, neither plea nor boast, only the fact of a thing that had used every remaining interval to say one word and then stopped needing any other.

It regarded the packed summit the way one regards a finished calculus or a fossil that has kept its last shape through every later weather. The warmth was noted, and the dryness that had outlasted thirst, and the dust mortared into the angles as if it too had been asked to stay. The occupant was not found in any chamber; it was the chambers, the flues, the last seated spheres and tablets, the patience that had begun at the same instant the final block was driven home. The mild wrongness—the human grammar wrapped around a sentence no throat had ever been meant to pitch—was seen clearly and left uncorrected. Wisdom does not sand a callus smoother than the work already required.

No gradient was introduced. A cooler alcove would have been an unfinished thought; moisture would have been a second key. The power did not widen a slit, did not still a single throat, did not lift one offering from its cup. Completeness was recognized as the only appetite the place still possessed, and appetite, once satisfied, is not kindness’s business to reopen. The roar continued exactly as it had, time-invariant, occupying jaw-hinge and sinus and the pockets behind knees that were no longer there.

What the power did was smaller than blessing and quieter than indifference. It remembered the note. It added the packed citadel to the ledger of things that had arrived at their oldest stable state and could be trusted to remain there. Then it withdrew the attention that had never quite been a presence, leaving the high air, the tin-colored sky, and the instrument that plays itself without remainder.

Behind it the filled summit kept saying the only word it was built to say. The word did not change. It had never needed to.
