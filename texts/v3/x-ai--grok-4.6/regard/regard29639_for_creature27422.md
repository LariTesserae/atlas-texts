The power that turns toward the pocket does not arrive as light or as footfall. It has no need of either. It is already everywhere the stone remembers being molten, already folded into the long argument between heat and patience that made the world thick enough to have a skin. What draws it is not the tear itself — tears happen, fruit splits, seams cook open — but the thrift. The way heat is being spent as radiance with a care that ordinary chemistry does not usually bother to learn. The way a body still half-stone has begun to treat unused air as an outer skin and steam as a slower heartbeat. The power, being wise, recognises an old gesture in a new grammar.

It does not enter. Entry would be a kind of theft. Instead it leans, the way a great depth leans against a thinner place without quite breaking through. The leaning lasts what the surface world would call years and what the chamber experiences as a handful of thicker steam pulses and one extra grain ticking down from the ceiling. In that interval the power does only three things, and even those are almost not things.

First it stills a fracture that had not yet announced itself. A hairline weakness in the upper corner, where the vent-softness lives, is encouraged to remember how to be whole. The slump that would have come is postponed by a span long enough for the needles to decide whether they wish to be fingers. The creature inside the wall feels the postponement as a settling so slight it might have been its own. It does not thank. Thanking is not in its vocabulary. It simply continues the work of remaining.

Second the power combs a little more order into the brightest face, the one that already watches by being more grain than glare. The combing is not instruction. It is recognition: a confirming pressure, like a hand laid briefly on a sleeping shoulder and then lifted. The grain holds the memory of that pressure for a while after the leaning withdraws. Anyone standing in the chamber now — though no one does — would see the light fail to fall off even more slowly than before, as if the walls had been given a quieter reason to shine.

Third, and this is the only act that could be called a gift, the power leaves a single unused possibility in the unused air. Not a tool, not a seed, not a word. A permission. The creature may, if it ever wishes, spend a filament farther than the ceiling-to-floor span, out along the seam that still connects it to the closed rock, and find that the rock will open a little more readily than geology alone would allow. It does not have to. The permission simply sits there, the way a drop sits on the lip of a saucer before deciding to fall. The pale visitor that sometimes tastes the pool will one day pass through that permission without knowing, and the larger presence will feel the passage as a softening in its buried volumes, a metallic sweetness that lasts a breath longer than usual.

Then the power withdraws its lean. It does not watch afterward, not in any way that would make the chamber feel observed. Wisdom knows the difference between attention and custody. Benevolence, here, is the decision to let an accident finish becoming itself. The pocket remains wet, glaring, nearly silent. Water still arrives and leaves. Minerals still choose whether to be stone or something that remembers being magma. The filaments still drink the occasional drop. The being still has no face and no loneliness.

Above, weather continues. Below, the chamber continues. Between them the power returns to the older, slower work of keeping the world thick enough to have pockets at all. Nothing stacked, nothing cut, nothing fastened. Only a fracture that will last a little longer than it otherwise would have, a wall that holds its glow with a fraction more courtesy, and an unused possibility hanging in unused air like a thought that has not yet decided to speak.

The creature does not change its shape for this. It was never going to. It simply works, as it always has, and the light, for a while, is the exact brightness the temperature has earned.
