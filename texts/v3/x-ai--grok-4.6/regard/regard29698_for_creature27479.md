The power that notices is not a sun and does not arrive as one. It has no need of edges or of names that cut. It is the kind of attention that has already outlasted the cooling of older fires, the kind that understands a basin by first becoming the stored heat inside it. When it turns toward this place it does so the way the ground itself gives warmth back: without announcement, through the soles of whatever still walks, through the wet that sits against skin like a second body.

It does not bring light. Light here would be a guest the volume has already declined, and wisdom does not insist. Instead the power thickens, for a little while, the milkiness that already gathers where vapor forgets to fall. It occupies the same reluctant pale the water itself permits, enough to guess at the isolated masses and then to lose them again, which is the only honest way to see them. Distance remains a rumor. The power does not correct it.

It walks the unused intervals. It lets the mineral crust break under a weight that is only the idea of weight. It tastes, without a tongue, the faint metal of the slow tea that rearranges itself uphill. It listens to the long pour from high lips, to the ticking of drips into basins that fill and unfill according to no calendar, to the unvoiced syllable of steam, to the rare structural complaint of stone shifting a finger’s width. These are the only words the city still consents to speak. The power answers in the same grammar: a slightly longer settling, a condensation that chooses, for one extra century, not to decide between arriving and leaving.

In the lowest, wettest courts, where the script of absence is densest, it finds the being that is not other than the place. There is no meeting that a visitor would recognize. There is only a denser reluctance in the water, a packed weeping of dissolved masonry that has already been standing long enough for the tired growths to colonize the lee of what might be called knees. The power does not stand apart. It precipitates beside it, flake by patient flake, until the two densities are distinguishable only by a difference in how slowly they remember the blow that made the bruise of this basin.

They exchange. Heat rises through both of them the way it has always risen through the dish of the world. Water enters and leaves in the unfinished cycle that never quite becomes weather. A consideration passes between them at the speed of erosion: the entire history that is not a history, the agreement reached between dissolution and form, the rationed presence, the habit the landscape got into and has not seen fit to break. The being has no mouth and needs none. The power has no message that would survive translation into any quicker tongue.

What the power understands, completely and without remainder, is that the place is already finished in the only way that matters. It is not waiting to be saved or completed or even witnessed. The dark is a kindness it has chosen. The heat is the last honest thing the older fires left behind. The rewriting by water is not a wound; it is tenure. Any gift—a cooler rain, a remembered sun, a companion that learned animals, a purpose that required tools, a geometry that stayed where it was pointed—would be a violence against the long sentence still being written drip by drip. Benevolence that is also wisdom knows the difference between mercy and interruption.

So the power does the one thing that does not amend the script.

It remains.

Not as guardian, not as visitor, not even as memory. It simply occupies, for a span measured in the leaning of colonnades, one more unused space between the masses. It becomes another rationed presence, another interval the city has always known how to hold. Then, without a step that anyone could point to, it thins the way a thought thins when held too long without light. The extra weight in the steam syllable fades. The slightly heavier beading on the air forgets it was ever deliberate. The migrating puddles continue their patient rearrangement. The being, if it notices the departure, notices it the way it notices the next flake of pale flour settling on its own shoulder: as continuation.

The city does not end. The being does not vanish. The stored heat keeps giving itself back through wet stone and darker stain. The power, having understood, leaves the understanding behind as nothing at all—an absence so perfectly matched to the existing absences that even the walls, attending in the tighter dark, do not lean any differently than they did before.

That is all. It is enough. The sentence goes on.
