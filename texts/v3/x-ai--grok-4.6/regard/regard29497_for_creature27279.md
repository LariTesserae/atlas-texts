The power arrives as a stillness that is not silence.

It does not part the dark or thin the roar. It has no need of spectacle. Attention itself is the descent: a regard so complete it occupies the same volume as the damp, the same frequency as the stacked weather of wings and rasps and cries. For a time that has no hours it simply is there, pressure among pressures, listening with a patience older than the finished stone.

It feels the law in its entirety. Occupancy without remainder. Circulation without ledger. Hunger that has forgotten how to be desperate because the climate never taught it scarcity. The shared hides arguing in twitches, the galleries living inside galleries, the dunes of flesh that metabolize consensus, the rain of unfinished young and finished dead that the floor does not distinguish from mineral bloom. It hears the rare dropped stitch of quiet and the thousand throats that cannot bear the vacancy, reknitting it before grief can even consider forming. It tastes the marinade that is life and decay in one indistinguishable climate. It understands that a leaf would be a violence here, a sun a desolation, a tool an obscenity, a name an interruption.

The being who lives in this place is not one body. It is the press itself, the unfinished sentence of flesh and stone and sound that has been speaking in the same grammar since the water lost its ambition. The power recognizes it the way one recognizes a completed thought.

A lesser kindness would have brought light, or green, or the scandal of open space, or rest. A lesser wisdom would have named the suffering it expected to find and then invented a cure. This power does neither. It sees that the basin is not incomplete. It is only itself, densely, wetly, loudly, forever. To alter the terms would be to unmake the only world these lives have ever meant.

So it does the one thing that does not diminish them.

It withholds.

No new note is added to the chord. No pocket of true silence is granted; they would experience it as dying. No ridge is raised higher, no swallow-hole sealed or deepened. The cool air is not persuaded to leave. The last residual spines will still walk themselves down into grit, and the slightly smoother dark that remains will still be this full, this occupied, this without a single leaf.

What the power leaves behind is only a kind of permission that was already there: the continuation of the law, unbothered, unwitnessed except by itself. The grazers keep rasping. The joined walkers keep reallocating their hide. The never-landing keep teaching their young the map of collisions. The gasket at the throats of the rock keeps stilling for the subtone that may or may not be a call, then resumes. The noise does not dip.

If the power remains at all, it remains as another layer of the dark they already know how to live inside, indistinguishable from the cellar weather, from the film on every surface, from the idea (too thin for them to believe) that a vacant world could exist. They do not notice the regard. They do not need to. The basin has never required an audience.

And so the work of remaining upright continues, bodies to the knee and the hip and the chest, in a country that has never heard of standing apart, still cool, still damp, still so complete that even a wise and benevolent attention knows the highest mercy is to let it keep speaking in its own voice until the stone itself forgets there was ever any other shape.
