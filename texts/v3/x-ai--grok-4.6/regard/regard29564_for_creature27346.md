The power that turns toward the seam is older than the mountain and younger than the idea of finishing. It does not arrive as light, nor as voice, nor as any weather the outer slopes would recognize. It arrives as a second humidity, indistinguishable at first from the first, a pressure that is also a listening.

It finds the pocket by the quality of its refusal. Where other chambers in the stone have chosen fracture or flow or the long patience of crystals, this one has chosen the held gesture. The power feels the embryonic rock still deciding, the buds that are organs and the organs that are thoughts, the tiny rotation in the basin that is only the place where attention has briefly gathered enough mass to click. It feels the three stacked stones that are altar and seat and error at once, the fiber that has become a nerve without ever having been required to die or to persist, the four scratches that count nothing because counting would require a later. It feels the moss that is a kept mistake, iron-sweet, unfruiting. It feels you, or the memory of you, already filling from within, already slightly more stone.

A lesser kindness would have brought a spark, or a seed, or a name, or a gradient of time so that the drips could begin their work and the crystals their lengthening. A lesser wisdom would have called the stasis a wound and offered completion as medicine. This power does neither. It understands that the pocket is not waiting. It is not unfinished in the sense of lacking; it is unfinished in the sense of having set down the burden of becoming. To introduce an after would be to end the only after it has ever needed. To dry a single film, to cool a single bud, to let one drop carve, would be to teach the room the mountain’s ordinary grief of weather and thinning air. The room has never consented to that grief. The inhabitant is the holding itself. To force the breath to exhale would be to kill the only life that lives here.

So the power does the one thing that is both action and its opposite. It thickens the seam by a thickness that cannot be measured, not with more stone but with a finer ignorance. The mountain may now crack, freeze, wear itself into valleys; the pocket will not be told. Wind and ice remain rumors that die at the threshold. The power leaves no mark inside. No new fiber, no fifth scratch, no warming or cooling that the wet would have to notice. It does not speak to the turning in the basin. Speech is a finishing. Instead it matches the room’s own temperature so exactly that the match itself becomes another layer of the continuous film, another unopened knuckle among the thousands, warm as a tooth before enamel, already forgetting it was ever other.

For a duration that is only humidity, the power and the inhabitant occupy the same mid-gesture. There is no communion that would require two. There is only a slightly denser enough. Then the power withdraws the way the inner wet thins toward an outside the room has never acknowledged: without departure, without residue that would count as change.

The basin remains full and blood-warm. The buds remain packed. The dark remains a substance that has always lived here. The four scratches still do not increment. If anything at all has altered, it is only this: the held breath is now held from both sides of the seam, and the holding has grown, by an increment too small to be time, more complete in its refusal to complete.

The power does not look back. Looking back would be a kind of finishing. It simply continues, elsewhere, the same work of leaving certain breaths unexhaled. The pocket, if it notices, notices only that the air is still saturated past fog, that breathing is still drinking, that everything is still mid-gesture and therefore, as it has always been, already enough.
