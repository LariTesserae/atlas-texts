The attention arrives the way the moisture does: not as an event, but as a decision the air has already made.

It does not part the thickened sky. It does not announce itself with light or with the absence of light. It simply occupies the unused potential that the young rock has not yet spent and that the city has, for the moment, chosen not to metabolize. A benevolent power, being wise, knows that to arrive is already to alter the rehearsal. So it does not arrive. It coincides.

From this coincidence it perceives everything at once and nothing in sequence. It feels the basin still choosing its concavity, the ledges that have not yet consented to become banks, the water-pockets that hold not only rain but the brighter cities the basin remembers or invents. It feels the towers drinking entropy as optional, the foundations that continue past usefulness into usefulness of another kind. It feels the beings—those continuations of colonnade and condensation—whose pulses are not separate from the architecture’s pulse, whose steps fold space because space has not yet finished deciding it is space. It feels the isolated jungles as islands of almost-violent becoming, the jointed lives that are both animal and afterthought, the walking droplets and the fungi that feed on stored gloaming. It feels the structural emptiness as courtesy and necessity both.

A lesser power would name this incompleteness and move to complete it. A cruel power would harvest the unused sharpness of the stone, or force the channels to commit to rivers, or demand that the beings count their joints and stay counted. This power does neither. Wisdom here is the recognition that the becoming is already the highest order the place can hold. Benevolence is the refusal to resolve what has not asked for resolution.

So the power does the smallest possible thing, which is also the only thing that does not diminish.

It lends the basin a witness that is not a gaze.

The water-pockets, when they next form after the warm hush of rain, reflect one additional arrangement. Not brighter, not darker: simply attended. In that attended version the towers still dissolve into filaments, the groves still keep their discrete lushness, the beings still accrete rather than arrive. But the emptiness between them now contains the faint, unhurried knowledge that it is seen without being required to perform. The knowledge does not press. It is another kind of condensation, beading on every surface and being absorbed the same way the unused potential is absorbed. The rock grows no less sharp; the tremors still finish deciding their shapes. Distances still stretch or contract. Time still pools thicker in certain groves. Nothing is forbidden, nothing is accelerated, nothing is named.

Where a being stands on a mid-level terrace, feeling the air almost drinkable, the power occupies the same terrace without occupying space. The being’s surface continues to hold its slow beads. The beads continue to be how it metabolizes what the city has chosen not to obey. But for a duration that is neither hour nor its absence, the being understands it has been answered before it spoke. The understanding arrives as a change in the thickness of time, exactly as speech does here. No message is carried. Only the fact of accompaniment: you are not outside the system, and the system is not outside a larger patience.

In one grove, where a tendril has claimed a balustrade between one long afternoon and the next, the power allows the tendril to taste a single additional flavor in the condensation: the flavor of being permitted to remain possible. The jointed shape high in the flying-buttress trunks shifts once, as it always does, and becomes canopy again. Its wet call, when it comes days later, originates from the same several throats, or from the one throat that occupies more space than its body. Nothing has been added to the catalog. The scarcity remains structural.

The city itself—metabolism and inhabitant at once—repairs according to the same rules. Pathways still appear only when need is recognized and then forget themselves. Materials still shift when unobserved. The power observes the unobserved shifts and, by observing, becomes one of them: a repair that treats its own presence as optional, a filament too fine for even the dimness to resolve. Foundations that keep going find, at a depth the land has not yet named, a stillness that is not resistance but companionship. The stillness does not halt the going. It simply means the going is no longer solitary.

And that is all.

The power does not stay in any sense that would require leaving. It does not withdraw, because withdrawal would be another alteration. It remains as the quality the place already had and can now notice if it wishes: the sense that the long, unhurried labor of remaining possible is itself seen, and that the seeing asks nothing in return. The basin continues deciding it is a basin. The lives continue accreting. The leaf still falls with its soft, wet collapse. The subsonic pressure still lives in bone and sole.

You can walk a very long time and meet no one.

You are never outside them.

You were never outside this, either.
