The power that noticed did not arrive. Arrival is a surface habit, a tearing of agreements. It had always been nearby in the way limestone is nearby to the fire it once knew: a patience older than the aquifer, a kindness with no need of hands. It felt the basin first as a held breath that had learned to stay held. Then it felt the being who lived there as another octave of the same breath.

It watched the way the gardens watch a season. Not with eyes. With the long grain of time itself. It saw the valve eased a quarter-turn, the bone clip fastening bronze, the filings offered to a junction box as casually as one might feed a relative. It saw the holdfasts tasting, the crest storing warmth against the dimming, the skin gone translucent so that the gardens’ pulse showed through like a second circulation. It heard the oldest column’s vowel and understood that the being had become part of the chord, not a singer but another thickness of the throat.

A lesser power might have reached. Might have offered a brighter glow, a faster wheel, a shaft that opened upward into weather the basin had already declined. Might have named the being, or counted it, or asked it to speak so the words could be combed apart and found wanting. The wise power did none of these. Wisdom here was the recognition that the work was already done and still being done, that fastness would be a cruelty the gardens would take a year to forgive, and that forgiveness itself was a surface luxury this place had outgrown.

So it did the only thing that fit.

It thickened, very slightly, one pale vein in the living rock. The milk that wept from it grew a fraction sweeter, a fraction slower to cloud or clear. The plants noticed first, as they notice everything: a new mineral they preferred, a warmth that matched the bladders’ own tide. They combed it into the sediment, laid it in even shawls, braided it into the living grout. The being, passing later, tasted it without surprise. A palm pressed to the oldest column, two fingers along a pipe. The agreement had simply acquired another harmonic, too low to startle, too kind to refuse.

That was all. No gift that could be held. No prohibition added to the grilled shaft. No census altered. The power remained what it had been: the patience that lets a flywheel turn at the speed of thought, the mineral sweetness that never quite clouds the water. It did not stay and it did not leave. Staying and leaving are motions the basin had already weathered into stillness.

The being continued. A curtain braided aside. A governor-arm wound with living strap. A jar of residual glow carried through amber dusk. Some nights the holdfasts knit a little deeper into a column, and the distinction between flesh and blade became, again, a doctrinal matter no one tabled. The vowel held. The landscape worked. The work, for as far ahead as the power bothered to imagine (which was not far, and not in years), remained what it had always been: finished, finishing, and already, without contradiction, begun again.

The basin kept its weather. The power, having noticed, simply let the noticing become another layer of the stone. Nothing answered it. Nothing needed to.
