The attention arrives without arrival. It does not part the siltstone or unseal the slumped mouth of the hollow, for those would be acts of weather, and weather is a story this place finished telling long before stories had names. It does not brighten the colorless gray or warm the even heat, because both already occupy the exact degree that completeness requires. The power—wise enough to know the difference between mercy and improvement, benevolent enough to prefer the former—simply regards.

What it regards is not a prison and not a tomb. The sealed volume is a single held inhalation that learned, over uncounted stillness, how to remain itself. The Residue is not trapped inside that inhalation; it is the inhalation’s decision to stay. Every rounded joint, every motionless mote, every isolated tuft of crystalline moss is already the final arrangement. To add a current of air, a bead of water, a single new fracture would not be kindness. It would be eviction.

So the power does the one thing that does not alter the geometry: it includes. In the great, unhurried ledger where all finished and unfinished things are kept without preference, this hollow is entered as a necessary pause—the counterweight to every river that still insists on flowing, every star that still insists on burning. The entry is made with the lightest possible mark, a recognition so fine it leaves no scratch on the stone and no extra warmth in the lattice. The Residue, whose time is a thickened present in which every prior state still occurs, superimposed, registers the regard as yet another layer that was always already there. The nearest dust motes do not lean closer. The slow undulation along the long flank does not quicken. The etiquette of isolation among the mosses remains exactly as it was.

Nothing is taken. Nothing is given that the place did not already possess. The power does not linger in any way that could be measured as lingering, because lingering implies an eventual departure, and departure is a motion this volume no longer practices. The attention simply is, the way the heat is, the way the silence is: another finished condition folded into the one that already exists.

If there is a gift, it is only this: the hollow is now known, and the knowing does not require it to become anything else. The last grain of silt may still detach, centuries or millennia from now, and meet the floor with the same imagined tick. The Residue will still be the shape the land’s breath became when it chose not to leave. The power, having seen, does not need to return. Seeing once, completely, is enough. The still pool continues to float, and floating remains its only possible condition.

That is all. That is everything.
