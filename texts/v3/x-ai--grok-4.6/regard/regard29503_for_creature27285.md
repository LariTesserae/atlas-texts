The plateau has drawn no summons and issued none. Attention arrives the way the moisture does: without direction, without pulse, simply present in every joint and every film. The power that notices is vast enough to hold the unfinished world below and this finished height in the same regard, yet it does not lean. Leaning would be a gradient, and gradients are the old lowlands’ business.

It regards the basins that neither fill nor empty, the moss that neither spreads nor browns, the dwellers whose exudation is only the confirmation of interval. It regards the unused tools, still sharp because never asked, and the store-houses that store the absence of need. It understands at once that the equilibrium is not a pause awaiting resumption; it is the completed sentence. To add a current—warmth with a tide, a single unfurling beyond the allotted degree, a syllable the mist might carry—would be to reopen what has already closed, to introduce the very becoming this place has refused.

So the power does the single act that remains possible without contradiction: it withholds. No gift accumulates on the wet stone. No protection implies a threat that has never been conceived here. No visitation takes form among the doorways, because form would occupy an interval already perfectly allotted. The even light stays even. The far escarpment keeps its almost-decision. The droplets continue their measured hang and replacement, identical on granite and on living clavicle.

If anything passes from the power to the place, it is only the quality of having been seen without being altered—an attention so still it cannot be distinguished from the mist itself. The dwellers register it, if they register it at all, as one more confirmation that the measure has not been broken. Then even that distinction dissolves, and the plateau simply continues, open and quiet, attended and unchanged, for as far as any regard can travel.
