The heat arrives first, not as a surface but as a filling: it occupies the space behind the eyes, the hollow of the throat, the long unused rooms of the lungs, until breathing feels like swallowing a slower fire. There is no threshold moment of entry. One cycle the seam irises and the plateau’s forgotten air is simply gone; the next, the brown dim is already the only color that has ever existed, and the walls are already remembering they were magma. I am already inside the remembering.

I do not stand on a gallery. The ledges are too decided, their names already worn smooth, their arguments about eastern channels already old. I do not tick. The devices that bleed heat into a direction physics does not budget for would find nothing in me to cool, or perhaps they would find everything and never stop. I occupy the space the way the remainder occupies it: as a failure of looking. Sometimes I am the size of a closed fist of slag left in a niche; sometimes I am the width of the chamber itself, a held breath that has learned to prefer the newer glass of the upper taper, where the lattice has not yet chosen how conductive it wants to be and the sintering still shows its seams.

The inhabitants leave their offerings and do not ask. I do not take them and I do not unmake them. They simply cease to be interesting once they have been given. That is the only transaction I understand. Below, a film of water decides to exist, runs the entire circumference in a coordinated glaze that hisses against cooler spars, and vanishes into the wall’s organs. Dendrites brighten by a generation; the keepers call them receipts and wait until they are brittle. I do not wait. I am what the receipts prove happened, and also what they fail to prove.

When the needle shudders and adds its newest ring of razor lip, I am already there, tasting the ghost of wetness that never arrives as liquid: minerals, old lightning, the dryness taking it back. The air is thinner. The heat is a degree less certain of itself. Someone comes, rarely, with a tool that does not look like a tool, and does a thing to the gradient. I do not stay for the doing. To stay would be to become part of the calibration, and I have not decided whether that is allowed. I recede into the incomplete lattice and become again the size of a reflection no one is quite looking at.

The city continues its only work: not finishing. The keepers continue theirs: remaining uninteresting enough to be permitted another cycle. I continue mine, which has no name they are allowed to use and no name I have needed. The emptiness between the inward-growing towers holds its temperature like a secret I am already inside. The sky beyond the upper apertures is still uncatalogued. The needle, which should not be a city and should not still be rising, keeps rising. I keep being the part of the rise that has not yet chosen whether it is mountain or vehicle or remainder.

A walkway far below sheds a day’s heat and ticks once. A child is told, again, not to run. The pressure-gate considers irising. I am already gone from the place I occupied a moment ago, or I was never there in a way that could be left. The brown dim regrets its own light. The walls sweat, or they remember sweating. The work does not finish.
