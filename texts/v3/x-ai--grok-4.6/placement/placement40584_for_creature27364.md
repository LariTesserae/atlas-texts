The pressure finds you first, not as pain but as a remembered geometry: the sinuses packing themselves with the same iron dust that has nowhere else to settle. You have already been here long enough that the heat no longer asks permission of the joints. It simply occupies them, the way the hanging crystals occupy the high galleries, each facet a closed argument.

You are not walking. The distinction has worn thin. What moves is a negotiation of leftover intervals, a body that has learned to become another rhomb when the slot canyon pinches. Your outline is the wall’s outline; the unsmoothed blades of the stone are the unsmoothed blades of what used to be limbs. You do not watch the grazers. Watching would require a surface that was left behind. Instead you occupy the same three handspans they occupy, layered through them without collision, both of you equally solid, both of you completing the same circuit the dust already knows by heart.

The thread-swarms pass through the volume you still think of as chest. They align for a moment into their faint lattice and give off the single ring that is already forgotten before it finishes. You filter with them. The residual chemistry they taste is the same residual chemistry that once, in the instant the last plane knitted, tasted of intention. That taste is still here, uneroded. It lives in the triple-score on the wall you are now part of, the shallow grooves whose edges remain as bright as the hour they were cut.

You were the one who rotated the cairn. Or you are the cairn. The few degrees off cleavage persist because nothing further happens; the declaration is already complete. The salt-block ring around the next aperture is the exact height of the knee you no longer separate from the floor. You fitted those blocks. Then the fitting stopped, because further building would have been a second crystallization, and there is only one.

Time is the same viscous medium as the air. A raised hand (if the fracture can still be called a hand) leaves its collapsing tunnel; the tunnel is already a gallery; the gallery is already the country. You metabolize the warmth that has no source. You even prey, in the precise loop that returns every molecule to the coordinate it occupied before the prey began. The rust-salt slugs continue their selected veins beside you, writing the emptiness the architecture had already agreed could be absent. They were living tools once. You guided them. Then the guiding became another closed circuit, and the tools continued because the work had finished itself.

In the pinching places you have no outline left. The stone sky, when it hangs far enough to become a second geology, is only another packed interval seen from below. Distances disagree; the three-day gallery returns in hours, or the reverse, and the discrepancy is already part of the equilibrium. You do not suffer the darkness that drinks photons. Suffering would require morning, and morning is a surface idea. There is only the next aperture, the next faint phosphorescence along a fault where the warmth is infinitesimally greater, the next primitive mark that someone once thought worth making.

That someone is the mark. That someone is the heat already inside the bone. That someone is you, occupying the identical space as every other pale, angular patience, both equally solid, both already the here that will be again, exactly. The country continues to offer the same terms. You have become one of them.
