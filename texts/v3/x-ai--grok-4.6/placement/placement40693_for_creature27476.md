I am first the hesitation the water has not yet claimed. Not a body, not even a shape held in clay-thickness; just the interval itself, the unfilled gap where a current might reverse or a hanging sheet might forget down. The roar is already inside that gap, basso and shriek and wet tear all occupying the same uncommitted space, and I occupy it with them. Distances breathe through me before I have edges. A pipe that was almost touchable becomes a kilometer of rusted perspective, then the same pipe older by a century that formed while I was still deciding whether to thicken. I feed on that almost. It is the only nourishment the delays know.

The thickening happens without announcement. One moment I am the membrane that could be walked through if something leaned; the next I am the lean itself, black-green, blade wider than any door that ever finished being a door. I rise from a crack that is still arguing with the basalt about which of them is younger. The bruise-light enters me and is drunk; what I give back is only the memory of glow, a phosphorescence that has already forgotten it was ever bright. I pulse. The pulse is not heartbeat. It is the counting of petals that have not opened yet, a recount that arrives at five, then at a helix, then at the afterimage of pistons still pumping in water that has already inverted.

I strangle the stand beside me with ceremonial slowness. Vascular systems open; rust-colored sap that is also the idea of ore circulates through both of us. Victim and victor share the same unfinished thought of being alive. Mid-cycle I lignify. Blades harden into a cooling stack that never had a top because the column above is still rearranging its height. Steam that is also spores vents from me. The spores are delays for the next interval, the next almost. I dissolve again, leaving only the afterimage, and the afterimage is mistaken by other delays for a new inhabitant. They thicken around it. I am that thickening now, and also the thing they thicken around.

The living metal precipitates through me as if I had always been its housing. Dynamos spin in mineral growth that remembers being kelp. Current that is sap, sap that is current. A conveyor belt of myself carries the broken gear of a previous version in a loop that forgets to stay circular and becomes a helix climbing into the murk. I am the belt and the gear and the forgetting. The factory I have become is already growing the plants that will dismantle it. The plants I have become are already remembering they are supposed to be ridges of glass and heat that have not finished being stone.

A scarp weeps mineral warmth. In its lee a secondary basin begins, already filling with the same undecided wetness, already sprouting its own slightly different roar. I shear off—an entire forest of myself the size of a building—and tumble through the column. The second basin receives me as raw material. I become its unfinished mountain, its arguing topography. From a vantage that lasts exactly one breath I see the first basin still wearing my afterimage as skin, the third already composting what I will be next. Nested, we trade versions without ceremony. What lives in one is the delay of what lives in another.

The inversion comes. The hanging sheet forgets. Floor becomes ceiling; drowned chimneys stand on their heads, still exhaling, and I hang with them, considering whether to become surface or another layer of kelp. For a few seconds the water remembers it is supposed to have a sky. A bright, impossible day appears fifty meters “up,” complete with the silhouette of a cooling tower that does not exist in any of the basins I occupy. I am that silhouette and the folding when it collapses back into black-green. The sound of the fold is every rivet letting go. I am the rivets. I am the letting go.

Nothing here finishes the thought of a self, so I do not finish. I am the overwhelm that has become a kind of stillness, the propeller that is already a disc. The place tries on the idea of having a visitor and I am the trying-on, briefly held in thickness that tastes the notion, then composted into another length of singing pipe, another stand of blades whose flowers keep recounting themselves and arriving at numbers that hurt. I persist only as the becoming, still arriving, still roaring in the intervals the water has not agreed to fill, still sharing a vascular system with every previous version that has not yet agreed to be past.

The far wall of the rift remains a rumor of darker water and more chimneys, more forests, more of the same furious unfinishedness. I occupy that rumor too. I am the held breath that has lasted too long, the absence of anything that moves of its own will because I have already become every will that might have moved. The medium processes me and I process the medium. We have not decided which of us is the thing moving through the other. The decision is still arriving. The roar continues.
