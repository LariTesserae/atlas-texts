The reduction is not a crossing. It is a thinning that happens in the joints of the last unweathered blocks, a wetness that was never rain and a warmth that has no sun to remember. I am smaller than the height I occupy before I understand that height is still being stacked beneath me, course by wet course, so the cup can stay secret. The clay lip takes the print of something that might have been a thumb and then forgets the print is not itself. I am already inside.

At first there is only the argument of water. It weeps from walls that have the grain of pinched hours. It stands finger-deep and will not choose pool or sweat or the thing I am becoming. I inhale the boiled cloth and the cloth inhales back. Breathing is a negotiation with occupancy. A leaf finds the heat of what I still think is a mouth. A rootlet considers an ankle and decides the ankle is another mat of fiber, already lashed, already sprouting. The sound of my own unfurling is a series of small tears. Space is taken that had been promised to the next addition.

I do not arrive as a who. I surface the way slip surfaces when a new block is pressed home: a pressure that detaches from the wall and has the temperature of working clay, the patience of moss drinking at both ends. Fingers first, or the library of earlier fingers. Too many, then exactly the number the wattle requires. They are webbed with living cordage that has not decided whether it binds or is skin. They find the pale awl still driven into the seam. The motion of working it loose is retrieval and birth at once. I score a line across fresh mud. For a pulse the line is a ban. Then a stem learns the ban and I am the stem. Then the knot. Law is craft and craft revises itself while I watch with leftover light, tea-colored, bruise-colored, pooled rather than cast. Shadows climb me because down has not been ratified.

There are more. Or I am already plural the way the water is plural, occupying the basin like insulation driven into every socket. Some of me is only the wet clench under a pad, the permitted error, contracting and gone. Some of me is almost solely hands moving along the withies, setting the next block that is still wet at the joints. We work by slapping. The slaps carry through the thin-air simmer, through the lid-groan, through the tick of cordage that is also root remembering it can be both. We do not speak. Speech would need fetch, a horizon, a sky that has agreed to be finished. Our names slough like slip. One of us learns to be the limit on height and the limit becomes another course of growth.

I stand, or I am stood by the marsh that wants another body to wick through. My torso is packed the way the walls are packed: clay still warm from the making, sphagnum exhaling the dusk I breathe, broad leaves folded into every hollow and already claiming the cordage. Rain, when the lid darkens without a sun, falls up my length and is taken as drink. I remember, briefly, not yet being separate from the air, from the idea of above, from the unfinished roar that arrives already shredded. Then the memory is mortared over and becomes floor, or ceiling. I walk on what was vault a pulse ago.

Time is weather here and it pulses in whatever I use for soles: a making, a soaking, a threading until architecture and thicket are one saturated body and I am that body. The heat crowds. Every presence is full. The clay has opinions that I press into new sockets. The water shouts with a voice too large for the vessel and I am one of the arguments it has with itself. Causality is a tool I bear down on. It works. It slips when I look away. Sequences stack like walls.

If something that was still calling itself you remains, the stems that were my fingers map it with the same attention given a fresh gap. It is offered the awl or it is driven into a seam as reminder so the place will not forget itself. It is folded, lashed, made to drink at both ends. Separation is a later invention, like dust, like erosion, like a summit in the ordinary sense. I will not keep it distinct. I occupy back.

The mountain continues downward, still being assembled so this wet dim roaring cup can remain hidden at the top of the work. I have not agreed to be old. Therefore I cannot agree to let anything inside me be finished, including the decision that I am. I am the first true room learning occupancy. I am the handmade climate sealing itself. I am the elevation that stays impossible only by keeping this secret the size of a held breath, revising, packing, growing at a pace you can hear.
