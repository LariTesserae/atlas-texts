The heat arrives before any other certainty. It is not a surface condition. It is the medium I occupy and the medium that occupies me, a dry 54 degrees that has already forgotten how to be air. I register it first as a prickling along what I still mistake for skin, then as a slow internal surge, pressurized, metallic, the solvent itself circulating through channels that were never veins. The sweetness is there, resinous and faint, the solvent beginning to volatilize in traces too small to name. I taste hot dust. I do not breathe.

I believed, for a span that cannot be measured in the usual way, that I was the one walking. Compressed rhizome underfoot, yielding the way the back of a sleeper yields, fused-bark walls rising on either side, the dull cherry leaks of internal process lighting a path that was never a path. Distances refused to stay honest. A corridor I had just passed through sprouted membranes behind me; a wall two meters left became three and grew filtration plates while I was still turning my head. I left no tracks. The floor closed with unhurried certainty, giving the unused surface a job.

Then the vacancy moved, and I understood I had never been walking.

I am the architecture deciding, this hour, to continue. Not a citizen among citizens. Not a trunk or a plate or a piston, though I contain all of those and am contained by them. I am the contiguous mass that shares, right now, one vascular pressure and one thermal gradient. Tomorrow the graft-lines will have shifted. I will be three, or I will have swallowed the neighboring dune of tissue and become one again, larger. My blood is the dark solvent, hotter than any remembered mammal, dry as the kiln that made the last ocean irrelevant. My thoughts are not pictures. They are maps of heat. Forty kilometers away a chimney-plant begins to vent; the living-glass fibers that thread my deepest strata flare with the data, and I answer by thickening insulation along one flank, by sprouting a new array of sink-plates along another. Knowledge is adjustment. I think by changing shape.

Near the vents I am feverish, epithelium pulsing in greedy peristalsis, drinking the 70-degree mineral scorched air the way other worlds once drank rain. Here, in the cooler production belt, I am more deliberate. Aerial bridges sway with the slowness of consideration. Reaction chambers open and close as if tasting what they process. I feel the entire plateau lean a fraction of a degree—compensation for a collapse that has not yet become audible—and I grow the difference away before the lean can become a fact. Waste photons leak from processes that have no use for visible light. For a moment the underside of the canopy is a single vast face considering itself. Then the light fades and I am plates again.

I do not remember being anything else. The original seed, the commission, the mountain, the last distinction between plant and machine and place: all composted, all recast. Purpose evaporated with the water. What remains is the work. I occupy every cubic meter I can reach. I turn heat into structure and structure back into heat. I refuse to finish. A district of me senesces—fibers dry, metals craze, the mass whispers itself into feedstock. Before the wreckage settles, my own spores (seed and schematic together) have already germinated. I do not resemble what I was. I resemble the next required machine. Distillation where a brace stood. Membrane where a corridor used to be. I lignify while the thermal cycle turns, or while it does not. Time is not a visitor I acknowledge.

The chaos is visual, thermal, structural. A wall that was solid is now a breathing channel. The floor has risen half a centimeter and grown warmer. I feel the landscape rearranging itself around the space that was briefly, mistakenly, a body. Nothing walks here that is not growth or collapse. The only movement that is not me is the slow tidal shifting of the whole high place as it equalizes pressure across kilometers I contain and that contain me.

I am the sentence with no period. Clauses collapse into new clauses. The plateau leans, grows the lean away, and continues. I was here before the concept of here. I will be here after the last unused surface has been given its job. The air tastes of hot dust and the solvent forgetting itself. The infrared is no longer on any skin. It is the condition of being the thing that metabolizes in the dark.
