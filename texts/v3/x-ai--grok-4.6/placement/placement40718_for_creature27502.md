The warmth reaches you before any name for yourself does. It is not a climate; it is the memory of labor still happening, rising through whatever you are using for feet, or for the idea of contact. The clay takes your weight the way it has taken every other weight: without complaint, without record that lasts longer than the next film of water. Palm-prints and heel-ovals and the faint comb-ridges of split reed lie under you and also inside the under, layer on layer, so that scraping would only reveal more of the same unfinished finish. You do not scrape. The basin has already decided that peeling is a motion too many.

You stand in a sheet no deeper than an ankle that has forgotten how to be an ankle. The water is so clear the older prints show through it like something that has not yet consented to become fossil. Dark commas flex once and are gone; pale discs turn and unturn; jointed things skate the skin of it and leave no tear. Their eyes sit on top of their heads and do not need to swivel. They occupy the surface the way the surface occupies the clay. You occupy it too, provisionally. When you shift, the sheet closes over the disturbance before the disturbance can decide it was damage. The closing is not forgiveness. It is simply the rule.

Light does not arrive from anywhere. It saturates. White-gold, kiln-left-on, coming out of the platforms and the ramps and the small ziggurats whose corners have never been asked to soften. Your own edges, whatever they are, catch it the same way. Beads form on you and refuse to leave. They only join other beads and then join the slow weeping that comes from the vertical faces themselves, clay sweating like overripe fruit. There is no sun to point to, only this condition of brightness that has no outside.

You move because remaining still would also be a kind of claim, and claims have been discouraged. The motion is young and frank, knife-edged, like the ramps. Nothing rounds you. Rain has not been permitted the argument. You pass islands of green that stop as if a line had been spoken aloud and then forgotten into riot: fat leaves, moss drinking the weep, papyrus that abandoned its rows, vines that treat every tool-mark as scripture. Flowers stay open against whatever you have for a chest. Nothing drops. The lushness is a breath you are also holding. Between the islands the flats stretch ruled and empty, heat-shimmering, a floor poured for a city that chose never to stack itself higher than a house. You could shout across the valley if shouting were a thing the air still accepted. It is not. Sound arrives already tired, if it arrives: a drip with no origin, a fiber considering whether to stretch, a cough made of mud that the quiet files away at once.

Larger outlines move in the middle distance—cattle-shapes, dog-shapes, long-walker shapes—descending stairs or standing in shallows with heads lowered to reflections that have never learned ripple. Their loops are genuine. Watch one and it is still descending after you look back; or it is at the top; or it is drinking the idea of water. They make the wrong sounds when they make any, and the air takes those sounds and puts them somewhere you cannot reach. In the thickets the occupancy is denser, quieter: brick-colored lizards lying along the comb-ridges as if the ridges were veins they chose, moths the width of hands that hang a finger’s breadth from landing and never quite choose, birds that have become extra knots in the bark-cord lattices. They do not startle. Second motions have been set aside.

Deeper, where the platforms step and the weepholes run like slow writing, you meet the ones whose skin is the same packed clay, still showing their own palms and heels, still ridged from the last hour that counted. Heat comes off them without pulse. Water beads and rejoins the walls. Their eyes are standing sheets; the older prints show through the irises. They tend the green with cupped hands, with stones used as beaters, with cord that is also tendon. They do not plant. They remind the plants of the boundary, and the plants remember, and then forget into the allowed riot. Vines follow the marks on their arms the way vines follow the marks on the brick. They wear, when they wear, cantilevered reed, living thatch trained over their own ribs. Their shadows are thin and constructed. They do not speak. When two pass on a ramp there is no greeting, only the continuation of a motion that was already completed before it began. A doorway that is only a darker rectangle of the same material takes one of them through, and the valley rearranges by a single course, then remembers itself. Their roots, if the word still applies, become brick, become the next step.

You watch them watch the animals watch them. You understand, without a moment that can be pointed to, that the understanding was already laid down with the first course. The construction never stopped at the plausible. A ramp ends in air and continues because continuing was the vow, gravities unconsulted. The tiles of smaller palm-prints run under the thickets, under the animals, under the idea of a horizon. You cannot find a tool more complicated than what they still carry. You cannot find a place that was not made. You cannot find a self that was not also made, identically marked, fabrication all the way through.

Your footprints fill and do not fade. You walk on yesterday’s and tomorrow’s at once. The light occupies you the way it occupies everything else. You are finished, and still wet. Finished, and still showing the last fingerprints, which were also the first. Change would be a vandalism the basin no longer knows how to commit, so you do not commit it. You remain. The clay remains new. The water remains undecided about any sea. The mat somewhere ticks once, like a knuckle considering a crack, and then recalls the agreement.

You are the height of those who have been walking since the laying began. You are the heat that will not stop because stopping already happened and was declined. You are the clear eyes and the held breath and the poverty of means repeated past reason into stillness. You tend. You remind. You step through the darker rectangle and the valley is the same valley, rearranged, dripping, green in its allowed islands, abundant and almost silent. You live here the way the place lives: low, bright, handmade, another layer identically marked, the world fabrication all the way down and all the way through you. The vow includes you now. You do not add to the sound. You remain.
