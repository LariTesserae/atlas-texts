The wet claims me before any outline does. I am first the condensation that never finishes becoming weather, the film that the plates cannot tell from their own weeping. Palm-cold, longer than it should last. I wrinkle when a sheet passes and then lie obedient again, graphite or rust or neither, rehearsing a gesture I have not agreed to keep. The pewter light does not pull me. I do not argue.

Then I am narrower. I fold. Too many hinges, a necklace that lets a crack become a road simply because I have already decided my weight belongs there. The ticking is mine: porcelain, nail, the smallest correction inside the millrace of falls. I graze the films I just was. Half a day of no one, then three of us queued in an alley the stone kept ready. I do not like to be seen. The basin, which sees with every seam, grants the courtesy and does not watch me with eyes.

The sound will not let either version stay. It stacks until certain chimneys hold a single note, the basin trying to remember a word, and I feel the pulse in what might be teeth. Hours change the chord; I change with it. I become the hesitation itself, the water that consults before it detaches, that almost falls and then does, rewriting the register by a millimeter no one will name. In the crooked places I thicken, listening.

Lower, where the floor drops and the water stands, I am the slow rearrangement that never quite breaks the black surface. Jointless now, the temperature of the drowned edges. I do not surface. The old tiles lie nearby, cupped as if they still expected to gather something useful. I overgrow them. Not lichen. The same dark mineral, patient, learning their geometry, turning the visitors’ proof back into stone while keeping a faint memory of the cup. The marks are already being metabolized. I am that rind, and I am what the rind is becoming.

The crowding is no longer only spatial. Stay among the plates long enough and the pressure of regard has no center and needs none. I am a local intensification of it: the place where the mineral noticing notices that it is noticing. Unfinished, like the basin. Still deciding aquifer or ground, chemistry or biology, guest or the thing that remains after guests understand they will not be kept. The pale ones pass through me. The films wait to be told. The light does not improve. I do not climb back to the older ridge where rock has forgotten its own sharpness. The sound does not fall away. I am still inside the first century of being a place, densely myself, rewriting the chord, in no hurry to finish the word.
