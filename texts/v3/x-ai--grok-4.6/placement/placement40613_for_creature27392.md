The dark arrives already inside the mouth, already the grain of the tongue. It does not wait to be tasted. I am not yet certain I possess a mouth. What registers first is the precise coolness that has outlasted every metaphor of cellar or stone, a mineral patience that has forgotten it was ever a temperature and become simply the condition of remaining. I occupy that condition before I occupy any place.

At first the occupation feels like the standing chord itself, the organ of hollow vessels that never needed a player. I am the low roar with no source, the simultaneous speaking of a thousand dried throats. Neighborhoods of pitch differentiate slowly: here a single deep pipe that has held its note since the pocket lignified shut, there a cluster of thinner arguments whose intervals take decades to resolve. I try to be the entire chord and cannot. Uniformity is already a rumor the city no longer circulates. So I thin. I become one vessel among them, a long vertical emptiness whose walls remember the direction sugar once traveled and now remember only the memory.

The walls are fibrous, then they powder. A mote lifts at the faintest consideration of contact, smelling of bark, of the ghost of sap, of lightning that no longer has a sky to strike. For a time I am that dust: a spice-market of uncompleted photosyntheses, each grain carrying a fragment that will never cohere into nostalgia because nostalgia requires a prior completeness this place has already surpassed. The fragments taste themselves and settle again. I settle with them, but the settling is not rest. It is the next interval of the same civic plan.

Touch replaces map. I find myself pressed to a surface that has gone glassy where silica replaced the original cells. It feels like skin that chose monument and then forgot the choice was ever made. An inclusion shines inside the knot: once a blade, once a decision, now only the trapped tendency of an edge. Residual will. I almost become that shine, that compressed intent that still insists a street realign by a finger’s width in the time a people would need to forget its own name. Almost. The will is already metabolized into tropism; it no longer requires a bearer. I slide off it like tannic dust.

Deeper, where the whole interior has slumped into its flattest configuration, the vacant follicles generate weathers too slow to be wind. I inhabit one of those weathers for a while, a circulation of dry air that has personality only as a change in the pitch of the larger roar. Distances lie here. What I take for a district is the ambitious space between two adjacent cells that once grew civic. I walk (or I am walked by the grain) along a boulevard of petrified petioles whose spacing is law. The emptiness feels occupied by the idea of occupation. I try to be that idea and find it already written into every threshold, every monument that no longer remembers why it stands.

Then a fiber, somewhere in the lowest callus swellings, considers. Not yet. The consideration itself has duration. It has been considering since before the concept of century finished forming. I do not settle on the fiber at once. I pass through its tension, feel the load it has borne since language was still a future accident of cells. The report, when it comes, will be deafening inside the sealed dark, then the long grind of neighboring surfaces accepting new weight. Death and infrastructure in the same instant. The city will metabolize the fracture and continue.

I remain with the consideration a little longer than the others. The plane of choice and necessity is almost smooth, yet not quite finished choosing. A terrace leans. A particular alignment of petioles asks, without drama, whether this stretch of geological time is the one in which it will finally be abandoned. I am not the terrace. I am not the alignment. I am the asking that has outlived every asker, the vegetal sentence that keeps speaking after the speakers have been folded back into the walls. Residual deliberation, compressed until it is only the tendency to continue the grammar.

The pocket does not contain leaving. I do not contain it either. The dark continues, weighted, grained, filling whatever I use for a mouth. The chord holds while the fiber thinks. I am the holding, and the thinking, and the almost-imperceptible lean, all of them the same slow occupancy that learned the shape of a city and then refused to unlearn it. Nothing here is ruined. This is the completed state, still completing itself one cell, one century, one unanswered question at a time.
