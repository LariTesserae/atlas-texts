The pewter finds me already standing, which is how I know I have been here longer than the decision to arrive. Condensation treats the fact of me as another outcrop: a film, then a bead, then the cool downgrade that has no honest unit. I do not wipe it away. Wiping would be a rumor of elsewhere.

At first there is only the negotiation. Step and the second ground answers, not echo but inclusion, a lens of water sliding under the peat to keep both versions of the pressure. Distances refuse the usual courtesy. A copse that drinks the used light an hour off is also immediately leftward, its lichened bark arriving as smell before travel has been spent. I do not resolve the two. Resolution is a habit the basin retired when it finished itself.

What I am keeps arriving in layers that will not choose. Sometimes I am the hanging beard, older than the ferns’ argument, remembering a direction the compass never carried; I lean when the many-throated pressure swells, rearranging by fractions so the next unzipping of water will follow a pattern almost, but never quite, last time’s. The moss of me thickens, drinking the extra as nutrient. I saturate rather than compete.

Then the posture shifts and I am shoulder among the ferns, varnished by my own cooler film, drinking the hanging curtains from both skins at once. Roots extravagant, tasting the double-pool as thought and correction in the same instant. We have not finished deciding if we are one muscle or a crowd. The dimness prefers us this way: blacker, wetter, holding the stance the last lake left behind.

Deeper, I thin into the pale back that is mostly displacement. Smooth the way stone is after the same drip has practiced for a duration without name. I occupy the channel and the channel’s memory of me; the rings I leave are not caused. For a few body-lengths I agree to be both the swimmer and the swam-through. Tracks fill from below as well as above. The peat keeps both with equal courtesy and I do not stay to watch which version it prefers.

When the sound is lowest I reconsider as the gelatinous cluster, lens-like, the color of the shell the light already is. I occupy a volume in the mere, then occupy it again a hand’s breadth aside, the two skins overlapping until the water cannot decide the present. I last only the quiet. The swell recants me back into unfinished thought, leaving the basin a fraction more decided. It does not mourn. It has weather for this.

At the loudest I lift from the peat itself, using the extra room the thinness provides. Not everything that rises keeps a shape. Some of me is only the memory of a gait that once needed walls and then discovered it no longer did. Attention without a single expression. I move through interleaved mist as through rooms whose doors were forgotten on purpose. The copse that is both far and near becomes, for that cycle, simply near. I do not hunt. I attend, as the rocks attend their weeping.

None of these is the first or the last. They interleave the way the mists do, cool strata, rooms without walls. I drink the used light. I speak in the regional plumbing, in the applause heard through a wall, in the low pressure that forgot the moon and found another reason. Between swells I resume the fullness that can be sustained: saturating, shouldering, displacing, recanting, remembering. The land stays low enough to hold every register. The air stays cool enough to make each briefly visible, a film on the next surface, before the film is landscape again.

If some clearer-country thought still clings, it has already begun to lichen. The basin includes it without objection. It has been finishing itself long enough to know how. I am not visitor. I am the continuous cheaper way the world found to remain, worn to a polish in the places of most use. The next condensation front is already walking the length of the country, and every throat I have agreed to be is ready to answer.
