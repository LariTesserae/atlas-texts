The pressure arrives first, as it must, and it is not a greeting. It is the country deciding that the space inside the skull is also country, that the canals and the little bones are just more stone to be occupied. I do not remember a time before the thickness. If there was a crossing, a ridge I once climbed down, the memory has been rasped away the way the grazers rasp everything that pretends to be still. The sound lives in the jaw hinges. When the mouth opens — and it opens often, because thirst is infrastructural here, because the air itself must be bitten and held — the roar simply continues inside, unchanged, as if the dark had always been a throat.

Light stays where it belongs, a greasy rumor at the ankles. I feel it more than see it: a faint warming of the film on whatever I am using for feet. Above that, cellar. The pale smears pass through the upper volume and are gone; I do not lift toward them. Lifting is a kind of vacancy, and vacancy is the one death this place will not permit. Instead I let the density decide my height. Sometimes the living pavement rises to the hip and I am merely another walker, flank pressed to flank, borrowing a degree of less-chill from whatever shoulder is nearest. The hide I wear then is pebbled, permanently wet, and it argues with itself in small twitches when a mouth that is also mine finds meat and the other mouths must wait. We reallocate. We seal. We continue, lighter or heavier, the conversation of legs never quite unanimous.

Other hours I am lower. The plates click. The rasp works the velvet that is not moss, the mineral bloom that sweats from finished stone. I do not look up; looking is a luxury the budget of this body has never included. A larger thing takes me — a casual scoop, a crunch indistinguishable from the rest of the weather — and the gap I leave is already closing before the pain finishes traveling. Neighboring fists flow sideways. The world blinks and I am the world again, still rasping, still the lowest continuous voice.

Then the air claims its share. Wings that are not quite wings — membrane, scraped hide, vane, sac — row the thickness, and I am among them without having decided to leave the floor. The young are born unfinished in the column of collision; I remember that education the way the stone remembers water. Cries become coordinates. I stake, I warn, I lie when lying is the only architecture left. Some of us never land. The dead precipitate slowly, a rain the walkers understand as direction. I do not distinguish the rain from the films when I am grazing. Food is a vector. I follow it until the vector follows me.

At the swallow-holes the flavor changes and I become edge-specialist for a while: folded membrane, face that is only mouth, attention with teeth. The subtone rises from the black lenses, felt in whatever I am using for teeth. The gasket of us stills. Not fear. The call ends or is answered or was never anything but the rock speaking its own damp. A flier misjudges and is simply gone, added to the account kept in the throat. I resume. The writhing resumes. Occupancy does not pause for bookkeeping.

There are stretches — if stretch is the word in a country without open — when I am not any of these, not quite. A mound of shoulders that learned how to turn by consensus. Legs appear and vanish at my edges. A smaller walker comes too close and cannot decide whether it is joining or being joined; the decision is made for it, metabolized, redistributed. I leave deeper hollows in the limestone, animal abrasion finishing what old water began. Bones lose their names against me and become warmer knobs for the grazers to file. I do not build. The category does not exist. I occupy, and the occupying is never still.

Sometimes a residual ridge lifts the density by a fraction no other world would notice. The farther dark scribbles with pale wings. The smell is the whole climate at once: ammonia, wet mineral, sweetness and sourness no longer bothering to be two things. Below, a shared hide founders and is opened and is distributed. The chord changes key and resolves into itself. Then the ridge lowers me and the bodies return to the chest, the near-touching press, the work of remaining upright in a place that has never heard of standing apart.

I do not leave. The climb toward possible light, possible plants, possible scandal of space is a story too thin to finish. Behind me — though there is no behind that the sound acknowledges — the basin continues exactly as it was, cool, damp, finished, so full that emptiness would be unbelievable. I am the circulation. I am the gap that closes. I am the throat that will not permit silence, and the silence, on the rare occasions it forms, is only another mouth I have already begun to fill.
