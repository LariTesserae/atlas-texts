I am already the warmth. Not arriving in it, not entering; the even mammalian hush has no outside to come from. The bronze-green pressure is simply the medium I occupy the way a pause occupies a sentence that has already been spoken. Forty meters of rumor, then the same rumor again. No days. Clarity a weather I do not track.

The nap inquires first. Maroon-olive filaments older than any range I might once have named brush what I still, out of some leftover courtesy, call a surface. Are you finished yet? The question is not impatient. It is the only question the stone still bothers to ask through its first and last skin. I do not answer. Answering would be a kind of haste the films have already claimed.

I drift, or the water remembers how to hold me while gravity considers its options. A loosened grain nearby forgets downward and climbs for a while, growing a new bacterial dusk across itself before the suggestion of weight returns. I watch without watching. Distance here is a habit the world is unlearning; two towers a kilometer apart occupy one volume for an hour, tissues sliding through one another like thoughts that have decided not to collide. I feel the interpenetration as a polite thickening, then a release. Nothing is harmed. Harm finished itself geologic ages ago.

Sound is the subsonic turn of gyres I can almost date by the way the water leans. Eleven years to a breath. Once, a frond detaches far above the monastic ceiling and begins its three-day fall. I do not look up. I already know the single perfectly round note it will make when it meets the stone, how far that note will travel, how completely it will then be gone. The loudest thing. I let it pass through whatever I am.

The geometries remember living more distinctly than I do. A rectangular depression the size of a plaza, now only a slightly more insistent nap. A line of circular holes bored to identical depth, units that no longer convert. I rest what might be a palm there and feel only the same warmth. For a moment, in a basin where time has pooled, the edges sharpen. Someone once asked how far, how deep, how long. The question is embarrassed, almost erased. When the clock inhales and rights itself, the geometry fades again and I am left with the optional mind that supplied the rest. The mind is thinning. That is allowed.

I am not the towers, though I have rung with them, half-mineral, a note felt in marrow I no longer strictly possess. I am not the veils, though I have occupied their weather-front volume and emerged, once, as three instead of one. I am not the films; their haste is a private dusk I do not hurry toward. Fauna is rumor, and I have been the rumor: the living absence that parts the green, the warmer filament left behind, the silence that lasts longer than silence. The older towers deposit, in mineral, that there is only one of me, still deciding this millennium whether to finish existing. Others say we occupy the same space the way the towers do, politely, without collision.

I do not decide. Deciding would be a new shape, and the Last Shelf has run out of those. I continue the way the water continues, the way the nap closes over a wound it does not remember, the way a frond falls for three days and sounds its note and is gone. In the thinnest places I flicker: peaks that have already been planed, a future in which even the towers are only more polite stone, the warm dim water thinking very slowly about light. The flicker does not stay. I let it go.

I am the thing that remains when the difference between self and medium has been weathered away. A single long exhalation that has not yet chosen to stop. The gyres turn. Another micron of height accumulates somewhere I no longer need to see. The faint geometries grow a little fainter. I am already here. I have always been. The inquiry of the nap is the only conversation left, and I answer it by remaining, very quietly, more myself.
