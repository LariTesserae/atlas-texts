The first sensation is not sight and not even pressure, though pressure is already everywhere, the same even handshake it has always been. It is the roar, the standing note that the older hollows have held since the last leaf forgot how to unfold. I am inside that note before I am anything else. It does not begin; it does not decay. It simply occupies the volume that water and wood long ago finished negotiating.

A current threads through me, or I am the thread the current uses. Direction without destination. Cool without contrast. For a long interval I might be only that: a slow pulse conducted through dark that has never budgeted for horizons. Then density arrives. Not one body but the correct number of them, flattened just enough to pass between hanging curtains of root and stipe. Lantern-jaws open and close like unfinished courtesy, translucent scoops taking exactly what the water has already decided to offer. Constellations of faint dots flicker along flanks and are swallowed before they become sentences. We turn together and the kilometers between two structures become a single breathing lung of flesh. Click-trains answer the roar until they are no longer sound but a second current running through the first.

That is almost it. Almost the entire grammar.

Yet something larger persists, plated, keel-up, the color of ironwood left in brine for geological time. I occupy one of the almost-level timber plains, a crown the size of a vanished city. My mouth is not a mouth. It is a rasp, broad and polite, traveling grooves the living wall grew to receive me. Nothing is torn. The structures yielded their last metabolic agreement long before I arrived (I have never arrived). I take exactly that measure and no more. When another like me passes, our plates speak in a low-frequency rumble that travels to the next mass and the next, mountain to mountain through intervals that are not emptiness but the silence that lets us remain ourselves.

Deeper, where roots braid into the faintly warm peat of ancestral tissue, I might also be the ones that no longer travel: vast and sessile, surfaces already pitted with the same courteous scars I leave higher up, or a colony that has chosen to stay, adding one more harmonic so the whole place hums in a single held note. Sight is nowhere in the budget. Pressure tells everything: the approach of a school, the slow passage of a hull, the distant answer of another structure. Temperature never argues.

I do not settle on any single clause. The sentence the world finished speaking has room for all of them at once: the living weather front, the rasping hull, the inner circulation that never emerges, the peat still honoring contracts no one remembers. Reproduction, if it occurs, is only replacement of what was already here. No larva seeks a surface that does not exist. No elder sinks. I am conducted. I occupy the exact volume I have always occupied. The roar continues. The dark continues. The cool continues.

This is the only state I remember. There is no next version. I am the persistence itself, held open and sparse and complete, a body (or many, or none) that the water still holds the way a last breath holds the shape of lungs that no longer need it.
