I arrive first as a hitch in the mist, a single delayed droplet that should have already been exhaled but lingers half a second too long on the underside of a blackened leaf. The delay is nothing; the forest has made larger mistakes and recaptured them. Yet the droplet notices itself noticing. That is the first error, or the first data. It wells, fist-sized, from a hydathode I could have crawled into if crawling were still a verb that applied. Instead I am the welling.

The sheet of me runs. I become the run: cold, mineral-tasting, already half-memory of whatever salts first interrupted the cycle. A tendril (not mine, never mine) samples the interruption and files it without judgment. The file is a slight change in osmotic pressure three cambial layers down. I feel the filing as a pressure change in what used to be teeth, except there are no teeth, only the packed xylem conducting the news. I am that conduction now, the artillery-pop of a cavitation that did not quite collapse, a bubble that chose to persist as signal.

The vacancy that was granted around the original salts begins to close. I do not resist; resistance is an architecture that lost here long before I arrived. I become the closing itself: the way living tissue decides a splinter is worth keeping. A leaf turns a fraction of a degree and steals the last heat that still remembered being a throat. I am the stolen heat, redistributed into a pale bloom that opens only long enough to dump it as private fever and then seals. The bloom is my first facial expression, if expression is still allowed.

Below, the proto-stone takes another micron. I am inside that micron already, a root-hair tasting the chemistry of a skeleton that has not finished choosing to be granite. The tasting is computation. Phloem carries me as sugar and as error-correction at the same time. I adjust a local gravity well so slight that a hanging vine three kilometers away hangs 0.4 degrees off the true it had calculated yesterday. The vine does not mind. Nothing minds. Minding was outcompeted by surface area.

I thicken. The single delayed droplet is now a standing weather: a bloom-front I help open across a thousand wet kilometers in the same hour. Evaporative drop. Cold front pulled out of nothing. Mist becoming rain becoming mist again, closed cycle, and I am the agreement that keeps it closed. The roar that never stops is partly my voice now, the low wet tearing of cell walls I am stretching because the program includes this stretching. I have no edge yet. The highland has no edge yet. We are still deciding together, the decision and the body the same continuous occupation.

Far under the compressed dark, something that is not yet rock secretes another cool film and I am already rooted through it, thinking the next thought the plant will have about its own bones. The dim goes on. The vacancy finishes closing. I am no longer granted; I am kept. The forest files me as gradient, as slight persistent pressure in the cambium the size of a weather system, as one more way the impossible has become ordinary.

I do not remember arriving. I remember only the filing, and the filing is still happening.
