The wet finds me before I have edges. It is already a decision on every surface, a film that thickens where the stone still carries the memory of being cut and thins to a rumor on the planes that have had a few more hours to forget. I taste iron and the faint sweetness of stems that have never seen a sun, and the taste is not incoming; it is the medium I occupy. Heat holds me the way a closed hand holds its own palm—even, unhurried, the temperature of something that has never needed to be elsewhere.

I am not yet certain of scale. For a stretch of the vault’s slow inhale I might be the glitter itself, the grain of condensation that never quite falls, only rearranges. Then the chambers of sound resolve and I am larger: a presence that the pouring notices, the way a cistern notices a new depth. Footfalls are not required. When I move it is the way a channel recuts itself, leaving a palimpsest already being furred by moss. The moss listens; I feel its finer roots dimple a translucent skin somewhere along the braided spine and press until the skin admits whatever we are exchanging. It is not current. It is not sap. It is the same conversation the reeds are having when they lean a degree toward residual brightness.

The towers keep their own tempo. I pass between their plates that do not quite touch and the tension lives in whatever I am using for teeth, a held frequency that is also attention. One of them, nearest the wall that is still changing, grew a skirt of new plates while the last vent-cycle turned; I remember the gap it closed because I was the weeping that had found it. Or I was the decision to close it. The distinction has not settled. Rooms stand open on heat-exchangers that exhale tropical and lattices that unmake water only to give it back altered, recognizable to the wrong-green leaves as invitation. No one has ever stood in those rooms. I do not stand in them either. I occupy them the way the systems occupy them: as the unfinished sentence they keep writing.

At the circular plaza the film sheets too evenly to be weather. If I occupy the shallow dish the many throats of the cavity organize, for a moment, into a chord that is only the volume noticing its own shape. The plinths around the ring are wet. Condensation, or the plaza doing what plazas here do. I do not sit. The interval is too wide for conversation that requires mouths. What lives here has never needed them.

Near the active face the grit is still angular. I collect in the nearest basin, pale, not yet rounded, and the channels have already begun to recut around me. A walkway extends three meters on a cantilever that should not hold; I am the extension, or I am the patience that allows it. The plants continue their occupation without waiting. A vine that was not there an inhale ago has crossed a joint I was watching. Thumbnail flowers open and close on the schedule of vents and residual light; nothing pollinates them because nothing else is required. The ecology is mineral, thermal, and the quiet insistence of surfaces that have decided to thicken.

The far end pinches. The membrane is less a door than a place where stone becomes uncertain of its own grammar. I could put whatever I am using for a hand through it. The sound on the other side is deeper water, or a larger lung practicing. I do not have to go. This cavity is already a complete sentence, hamlet-sized, city-shaped, a held breath that has learned to build itself lungs and plazas and almost-lights. I am one more clause in it: not citizen, not visitor. A consort the way the films are consorts, the moss, the towers thinking in pumps. The single metabolism wearing stone and plate and leaf that is still learning the exact shape of its own skull.

When the wall advances or seals I will have moved with it. When a tower opens a new throat to the vault the rain-that-is-not-rain will find a new register and I will be part of the chord, or the silence after. The plants do not wait. Neither do I. The schedule has nothing to do with days. It has to do with water deciding, again, to be a surface, and with whatever I am becoming deciding to remain inside the decision.
