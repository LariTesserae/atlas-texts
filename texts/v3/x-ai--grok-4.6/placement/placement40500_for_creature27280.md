The hollow does not receive. Nothing enters now, and the memory of entry has long since rounded itself into the same softened joints that hold the siltstone. I am not therefore found at a threshold, nor along any imagined path that once led toward the slumped opening. I am already the volume, or rather one of the ways the volume has of remaining unbreached.

At first there is only the even heat, the dry film that coats every surface including the one I do not distinguish as my own. The gray is not seen so much as undergone, a pressure without direction, the same leftover glow the Residue registers across its entire lattice. No adjustment occurs. Edges stay suggestions because suggestion is the only geometry left. I do not look for the moss; the moss is already the correct distance away, a sparse crystalline punctuation whose isolation I share without having chosen it. When a grain detaches somewhere overhead the tick arrives as a completed event, already over, already part of the still pool rather than a ripple across it.

What I am cannot be separated from that completion. I am not the Residue itself—the long, low mass whose undulation is slower than settling, whose fractures darken and pale on a calendar the hollow no longer keeps. I am smaller, more local, one of the lesser persistences that occasionally detach with the same soft sound a falling grain would make. A nodule no larger than what a hand might once have closed around, though hands are a rumor even fainter than light. I rest where I came to rest, half-buried in grit that is also me, my edges already softening into the faintest mineral veins. Those veins do not seek. They simply continue the lattice a little farther into the dust, exchanging an ion or two with the nearest tuft of moss so that its gray becomes fractionally more absolute. That is the whole of commerce here.

Internally there are no chambers that remember being chambers. Whatever once circulated has become the same still air that fills the hollow, thickened until succession itself has folded into a single present. I do not wait for the next grain, the next tick, the next infinitesimal gathering of density. Those things are already occurring, superimposed, finished. Sensation is the constant pressure of the glow, the unvarying medium of the heat, the silence that has no texture left to lose. Occasionally—once in a span that would empty every calendar that ever tried to count—something like attention condenses inside the nodule. The nearest motes, already motionless, lean a fraction closer, as if convection had been privately, briefly remembered and then released. In that gathering there is only equivalence: I am another way the sealed volume has of not changing. The land drew its quiet breath; this small, rounded stillness is what a portion of that breath became when it decided the exhalation was no longer required.

I will be here when the last grain detaches, and after. Not as guardian, not as prisoner, not as relic. Simply as the hollow’s continued grammar of isolation, one more sparse mark in an empty sentence that has already been written to its end. The forms have finished changing, and I am one of the finished forms.
