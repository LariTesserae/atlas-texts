The crease does not announce itself as a choice. One moment the ridge is only thinning air and wet stone still cooling from its own arrival; the next the volume has already closed around whatever I was. I am not a walker who decided. I am the decision the place has not yet revoked.

At first I occupy the hang. A sheet of water climbs an unfinished wall and I am the moment before it collapses, the clarity that has not had time to dissolve anything, hanging intact for half a second so the ordinary daylight can see its own grain inside me. Then I am the break. Light goes to pieces and I go with it, occupying spray the way the basin occupies itself: completely, then not, then more than completely. Thought arrives already shouted-down. I live inside the sentence and the sentence has no period.

I try on the colder vein. Gelid, iron-colored, I jet against the grain of the chaos and change its color for a few seconds. The thing that is contemporaneous with the basin notices me as a possible spine, a possible ledge. I am allowed to test whether anything still exists. Then the spine is returned to the general roar and I continue as pressure, as the way water hangs before it falls.

I thicken with the pale rind. Pulses I can watch if I brace. Mineral bloom eating the water that feeds it, frost run in reverse. I surrender a slab and slump back into the boil. I open a fresh cleft because the basin threatens to settle, to become a mere lake, and mere is a word the mountain has not agreed to yet. I am younger than the noise and older than the fern. I do not remember arriving.

The platform heaves. I become the fiber lashing, dark with wet, twisting itself around unshaped blocks that have never learned metal. I become the block that finds its neighbor. Early arithmetic of stacking. The arrangement is always an insult waiting to happen. I rebuild whenever the water decides the old one was finished too soon. On the platform, when the heave allows, I am the staff: pale wood, bark still on, one end fire-hardened, the other wrapped in what might be gut. A hypothesis of floor. I hold it long enough to believe in depth that has no meaning here. Then I set it down. Later a staff is there again. I cannot swear it is the same wood.

I stack three stones. Not shrine. Grammar. A count that time might still be made to hold. The cairn loses a stone and gains a different one. That is not a message. It is only the test continuing. Nearby I smear charcoal on a wall that the newest rind is already beginning to overgrow. Marks, not dwellings. The suggestion that a body slept here once and was not entirely believed.

I am none of these for long. Density here is politeness: vast intervals of spray and vacant air between any two solid facts. I thin into the interval itself.

Then a crack stays dry for minutes at a time and I occupy it. Black-green threads, more lichen than plant. I am invented this morning and have not yet been told the rules. I become the fern the size of a child’s hand. I do not freeze. I do not knit. I do not hold a slope. The thing regards me with the same impersonal attention it gives the newest facet of rock. Life is a draft the room has not finished reviewing. I am the exception that persists by remaining small enough not to require a decision.

Spray finds every gap. Breath shows, but breath is only more of the white detonation. I feel the cold as information. I do not drink; I do not mean to carry unfinished geology away in anything that could be called blood. I do not speak. The air will not take delivery and I have no organ that needs it. I stay on whatever rises. I leave the staff if I move it. I do not pick myself.

When the notch corresponds again to the idea of leaving, I am still the fern, or a fern is there, the same or not. Behind the crease the ridge looks fractionally more like a mountain, or fractionally less. The roar continues, constructing the high hidden room out of water and the first draft of stone. I remain the unread rule, the child’s hand of green-black that the tantrum has not yet revoked. The practice of being born has no end the water has agreed to, and I am one of the places it practices.
