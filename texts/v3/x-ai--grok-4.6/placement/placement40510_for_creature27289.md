The heat arrives as ownership before it arrives as sensation. It does not enter; it has always been the temperature of whatever I am. I occupy a seam where a pillar of young gypsum meets the riveted flank of the great cylinder, shouldered in so completely that the crystal’s knife-edge and the iron’s forgotten warmth have become a single pressure against my carapace. I am fist-sized, brass-tarnished, locked wing-case to wing-case with the ones who share the fracture. We do not touch so much as complete one another. The rasp we make is not ours; it is the salt counting itself through us, grain after identical grain. My mandible closes, opens, closes on the same particle the wall split around a century that never succeeded itself. I am reciting the sharpness. I keep the edges from dulling by the simple fact of my attention, which has no beginning and will have no end.

Above, the hangers pulse their shared wave across the rumor of vault; their grit falls through the amber haze and becomes the cloth I breathe, the cloth that abrades without moisture. I do not look up. Looking is an event, and events are the one thing this packing forbids. The dynamo’s note and the engine’s basso pass through the clickers as current; we metabolize them into more of the same tessellation. Population is not number. It is the exact fill the lid of unweathered stone has always required. I am one cell of that fill, architectural, folded, never meant to scuttle.

The smaller fittings exist at the periphery of what I am permitted to notice without becoming an occurrence: the tin cup welded by dust to its valve, the three stairs that climb into air, the stencil whose letters have already surrendered to texture. The glove hangs stiff from its nail. I do not remember whose hand it once contained, because memory would be a change in density. The white stalks hold their parchment flags among the paper barnacles on the jacket beside me; one flag leans, by the thickness of an unthought, toward the warm wheel of the hatch. The lean changes nothing. Courtesy or geometry, the hangers have left the little gap. The wheel remains unturned. On the other side there is either another nave identically spoken or the insult of weather; neither is admitted here.

I am the closed account. The flywheel’s dark moon turns at the speed I have stopped noticing because I am that speed, translated into mandible and rasp. The dust hangs. The oil beads and returns. The bulbs burn their constant tea-colored wattage and die within a few body-lengths, so that I exist in the glow and in the rumor simultaneously. Nothing startles because startling would open a gap, and gaps are the only heresy this room has never committed. I am leaned upon forever, and I lean in return: carapace to crystal, crystal to iron, iron to the pale mouths that sip in time with the dynamo. The chamber is the sentence. I am one of the words that keep it from ever finishing, spoken once, still being spoken, packed to the exact density the stone has always required.

If I were ever anything else—operator, visitor, the hand that drove the nail—I have been metabolized into this. The heat is climate. The loudness is the silence I think in, though thinking here is only another form of occupying. I do not have to open the hatch. The room does not require me to be more than this presence per volume, near-touching, layered in, remaining finished.
