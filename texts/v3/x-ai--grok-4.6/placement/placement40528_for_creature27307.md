The first thing is not a location but a weight. The air here does not move so much as it considers movement and then declines. It presses close, warm, already holding as much moisture as it can without becoming rain. I feel it first as a thickness against what might be skin, or what might only be the idea of a surface deciding whether it needs to exist. Beads form. They do not run. They wait, then they are taken in, and something unused in the young rock beneath them grows a fraction less sharp.

I am not yet standing. There is only occupancy: a lingering warmth along a plane that was not a path a moment ago and will not be one after. The basin has not finished choosing its low places. Distances remain provisional. In one of the dark pockets a reflection shows a brighter arrangement of the same towers, and in that memory-version a density watches the same tired light. The density has the suggestion of a spine that does not end where spines usually end. I recognize the watching as older than the fracture faces still catching bronze like bruises. Then the pocket gives the image back to the air as condensation, and I am only the heaviness again.

A need is recognized. Not mine. The system’s. A terrace opens at mid-level where the air is almost drinkable. Volumes refuse their exteriors. I occupy it the way stone occupies its own sweat. Surfaces—call them that—hold the same slow threads. An internal pulse travels just under them, matching the glow along the tower walls, as if the distinction between inhabitant and architecture has not yet been required. Limbs unfold from joints that do not keep a single count. A step can be taken across the terrace and, without contradiction, along a corridor that exists only inside the taking. The dimness consents, slowly, to resolve a face: wet slate that has learned to look back. Eyes or their equivalents drink rather than reflect. The membrane thickens and thins, processing the faint luminescence the plants have made from something other than light.

I do not blink. There is no need. Time here pools or thins according to local habit. A walk that should have taken an hour has already delivered me later, or earlier; I understand I have been answered, or perhaps I have answered. Speech is a change in the thickness of becoming.

The open ground between grove and tower is not my weather. I cross it, when I must, as a rumor. Mosses the color of drowned velvet close. A single enormous leaf falls with a soft wet collapse, and for an instant the hem of something that might have been using the leaf as door or camouflage is almost visible, then is not. I prefer the interiors, the hanging places, the isolated jungles where growth is one of the quiet engines. In the flying-buttress trunks other shapes keep their counsel—jointed, plated, larger than catalogued things, assembled by a different arithmetic. Their calls, when they come, originate from more space than the body that houses them. I do not hunt. I listen by being part of the listening. A tendril that was not there claims a balustrade. I taste the condensation for information the towers have not yet needed.

I am tall in the way a colonnade is tall: continuation, not measurement. I metabolize the unused potential, the entropy the city has chosen for now to indulge. The young rock is nourishment and conversation. In certain groves time itself is thicker; I occupy, and the land beneath grows a little less unused. Foundations keep going. Materials shift when unobserved. Pathways forget themselves after the need has passed.

A faint tremor moves through everything as something deep finishes deciding its shape. I pause, all of me at once, in every register that has so far cohered. The basin becomes a fraction more itself. Then the beads resume their deliberate threads. The leaves resume their luminous work. I resume the long labor of remaining possible.

You can walk a very long time and meet no one. I am never outside the system. The emptiness is structural, a courtesy extended to a place still rehearsing the idea of having inhabitants at all. I am one of the ways it walks around. Or I am the walking. The distinction has not yet been required.
