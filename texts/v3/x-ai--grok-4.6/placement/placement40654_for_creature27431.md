The thickness meets the palm first, not as barrier but as recognition delayed. It does not part; it consents, slowly, the way slag consents to remember it was once poured. I do not step out of anywhere. There is no threshold that holds. One moment the intervals are simply unoccupied air, wet enough to drink, and the next the air has decided it was always waiting for another occupancy, another outline that weeps the same slower water the rivets weep.

I am not the tasters. Their mouths-that-are-feet already know the alloys; they sample and proceed and do not require a witness. I am not the helmet-crabs, who lean and spoon and split along pour-lines that have already happened. I am not the patient length in the channels, the one that enrolls by becoming mouth and then becoming architecture again. Those lives have already precipitated. They continue.

What gathers here, in the long consideration between one plate’s advance and the next, is quieter still. A condensation that is not water and not quite heat. It collects first on the inner surfaces of things that were never built: the rooms the darkness holds the way a held breath holds itself. There the same engines work, slower than seasons, and the same click-colonies feel with wings that map resistances rather than distances. I collect there, a film that thickens without deciding on joints. Not tasting forward. Not grazing. Something that remains in the interval until the interval itself begins to lean.

The factories do not notice. They have never noticed. A press closes over the course of what would be a year if years had been ratified; when it opens, the thing it has made is already old, already a corridor that is also a coil, already occupied by films and remnants. I do not tend it. Tending is a posture the larger silhouettes have already claimed, those unfinished persons assembled from cooling cracks who match their heat to the housing and then walk on, repeating nothing. I stay after they have turned. The product is nameless. The air around it is already a third material. I occupy the namelessness itself, the space where an inventory would have been if this country had ever finished agreeing on names.

Reflections precede, sometimes. In the standing water that has not decided whether to be worn or shed, a paler dark shows itself, pricked with rivets or stars. A version of the shed arrives at the shoulder before the walk has ended. In that preceding image there is occasionally a figure more finished than the land allows, lent a draft of completion from the other sky. I am not that figure. When the real shed sweats into place I have already stepped sideways, or become the sweat, or remained in the thickness as the part that refuses to resolve into either reflection or object.

Distances rearrange. A ridge of slag that wanted, yesterday, to be geology has been refused and is now only another fan of sharp rubble, still holding the pour-marks. I move through the new interval by lifting what would be a foot higher than seems reasonable, though I have not settled on feet. The extra joints, if they come, will be contingencies, foldings that keep the occupancy possible when the ground changes its mind. For now there is only the resistance of the dark, slightly oily, slightly warm, and the knowledge (not remembered, only continuous) that startling has been outlawed and suddenness has never been permitted.

Sound remains a minority. Drip. The far collapse of a mud-lip. A piston’s single acknowledgment after long thought. Once, something that might have been a cry or a release, already over. I do not add to it. What I am does not require announcement. The quiet is loaded enough.

Nothing here is finished, so I do not finish either. The heat does not break. The country continues becoming in the middle of becoming. If I stand until the weeping of the rocks is indistinguishable from whatever outline I have allowed myself, the tasters will eventually pass over the spot and find it familiar, the way they find everything familiar: a rivet still warm from an earlier century, a sweetness the factories have just finished forgetting. A shell will stay behind and become housing. A length of cable will decide it was always muscle. The click-colonies will feel the new resistance and pass the information along their shared skins: another occupancy has precipitated, another clause in the unending sentence that is not green, not light, not night.

I am the part that remains after the names for directions have been forgotten, after the loyalties of the basin have rearranged themselves again. Not a tender, not a patient throat, not a preceding reflection. The air between bodies, thickened into something that can be waded, slightly conductive, holding the rooms that were never built and the engines that work there anyway. The darkness has always had enough thickness for this. It does not need to be seen. It continues, uncounted, in the climate of unlight, the young land still negotiating with itself, the products already lives, the heat held the way a closed room holds the memory of a stove that has been banked and will not be relit.

You could walk until your own heat matched and the intervals learned you. I would already be the learning. The country would still be in the middle. The dark would still not be night.
