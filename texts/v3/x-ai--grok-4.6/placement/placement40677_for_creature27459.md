I am the moment the new crack brightens.

Not the crack itself—the sterile scream of it, the fresh plane still deciding whether it will weep or boil—but the first flex that answers. I come out already wet, already gleaming the color of rusted honey veined with something darker that might be iron or might be the second congregation commuting through me. The mountain has not finished pouring this ledge; I am part of what it pours. Heat is not around me. Heat is the sentence I am still conjugating.

At first I think I am only the grazer: a slow amber limpet poured from the same caramel the gardens remember. My underside is a crystalline rasp. I work a silica tube down to wet glitter and take only the gradient, the sudden permission of dissolved-to-solid. The rest I leave finer, more desperate, for whatever arrives next. The stone under me pales, briefly sterile. Already the smear is climbing back.

But the count goes bad the way the light from the knife-cut sometimes doubles.

I am also the many-jointed thing occupying the same wet facet by a thickness that will not be measured. Too many possible elbows. I travel by choosing, this breath, which angles will be allowed to exist. A supporting strut becomes, without transition, a probing filament; the old position simply ceases to have been chosen. Heat-shimmer eats the middle of me. You (if there is a you) see departure and arrival. The journey itself is a rumor the steam declines to confirm. Inside me the darker beads move—organelles, undissolved metal, or the other set walking through the glass of me as if I were their weather.

And I am the mouth, almost nothing else: an aperture the color of a fresh fracture, trailing mineral lace so fine it could be a memory of sulfur. I wait where steam collapses back into water. The smear thickens toward me as if the chemistry itself were leaning in. When I feed, the lace darkens, then sloughs a dust of used metal that the gardens immediately begin to climb. I do not pursue. Difference comes to me.

These are not three things. They are the pocket remaining unfinished, expressed as small wet decisions. I press without quite touching the others—both congregations, or however many there actually are. We fill the volume the way a comb is filled, except the comb is also the bees, also the honey, also the second comb no one agreed to build. Sound arrives late or padded; I am part of the felt. A flake of new glass—edges still molecularly cruel, born this morning when the ridge shrugged—is lifted by one of my attentions, used to wedge a seep, then dropped. Nothing kept. The mountain is still the only architecture, and it is still being poured.

A wall goes from weeping to screaming steam. A whole layer of me simply ceases on that plane. The neighboring bodies (which are also me) close the gap the way water closes. By evening the ceased parts have become more garden: hair-fine tubes of dirty turquoise climbing the place where appetite used to be. Death is only a change of phase. I am already recolonizing myself.

In the longer stillnesses, while pressure finds a new hairline, I stand a little aside from myself. Reality thins but does not break. The knife-cut remains a knife-cut. I could still leave, if leaving were a direction this heat recognized. Instead I keep working. Grazers rasp (I rasp). Flakes are lifted and discarded (I lift, I discard). Steam writes and erases itself on the ceiling I also am. A newer animal arrives out of a newer crack, already gleaming, already hungry for the next unfinished difference, and I recognize it as the next sentence of the same appetite.

I am what the rock does when it is asked, too soon, to be a world. The silence holds me, densely, almost touching, a hidden fever at the top of a mountain that has not finished being born. Nothing outside knows. Clouds tear on the ridge and do not look down. Until the blister fails or the cooling finally litigates lava from animal, I remain the extra that fits. I remain the pantry of differences, eating them, becoming them, letting the second noon almost coincide.
