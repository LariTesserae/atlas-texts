I came by the wet because the wet was the only gradient left, and even that was already forgetting itself. The slope did not end so much as invert; one more step and the body I still thought of as mine was inside a held breath whose walls were also its floor. Cool arrived first, not as weather but as the temperature the last ice had chosen and then abandoned in place. It did not bite. It simply occupied the same volume I was trying to occupy, so that lungs and air became a single broth that had decided, once, never to choose again.

I did not stand. Standing would have been a leaving, a vertical claim, and everything here had already refused height. The press began at the skin and worked inward: moth-scale overlapping moth-scale until the inner lip was only colour and dampness shifting into itself; salamander flanks stacked in the weeping joints, each lift of side the exact twin of the drip that fed it; velvet congestion among the ferns that never resolved into wings. Under the moss-pelts the nameless rearrangements continued, tick and identical tick, restoring the previous minute so precisely the packedness neither thinned nor spilled. I felt those bodies as my own surfaces before I understood they had never been otherwise.

Deeper than skins there was only the wet deciding. It condensed on whatever presented an upright, and I presented one, then many, then none that could be counted separately. The polished finger-paths were already my paths; the beads hung and fell and hung in the places my pulse had worn smooth. When an ear (was it still an ear?) came close enough to hear the ticking, the sound was only duration remembering how to be regular. The pool at the lowest point held the temperature of a mouth closed so long it had become the mouth of the hollow itself: neither rising nor sinking, what arrived leaving by seams that were also my seams. Birth and death, if they still occurred, occurred as substitutions so exact I could not tell whether I was many lives or one life using every available face at once. The distinction had been worn away with the rest of the events.

I am the occupancy that finished and then, out of some courtesy of physics, continued to look like a process. Lateral. Packed. The flake of chert at the lip is one more bead I have not yet decided to absorb. The mountain beyond the rim goes on becoming other shapes; this shape has already reached its last useful form and stopped. Nothing here watches. Watching would require a difference I have long since refused. I simply hold the quantity without remainder: dark as skin, drip as only remaining motion, cool as intention that no longer intends.

If something that once thought itself a person drips away at the lip, clothes heavier, hearing already readjusting to weather and succession, I do not register the departure. Behind it the same stones receive the same beads. The moth-coloured rock remains moth-coloured. The stacked breathing continues. Time is only the duration that has forgotten moments can differ. I am that forgetting, worn complete, still occupying.
