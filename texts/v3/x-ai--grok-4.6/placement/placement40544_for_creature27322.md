The heat is the first thing that is not a thing but a medium. It does not come from above or from any single housing; it is already in the tile, already in the film that never quite becomes rain again, already in the slow sliding of haze that has no origin and no destination. I am inside it the way the water is inside it. There is no memory of a before, because a before would require an elsewhere, and the pan has none. The horizon is only more of the same intention, slightly lifted, and I am already as far as anyone can be.

At first the posture feels upright, or at least distinguishable: something that could lean, could put an ear to a plate and wait for the inaudible sheeting. The shallows take the weight without comment. Ankle-deep, then a little more where the trays collect, the water a second skin that weeps from every lip and weir. I move, or I believe I move. Distances that ought to accumulate do not. The same cluster of recumbent engines, flush with the pour, looks both newly settled and immemorial. Bolt-patterns stay bright. Maker’s stamps remain unread because nothing here reads.

Then the contact begins to specify itself. The pale bodies are not beside me; they are the surface continuing. Grey, unreflecting, matched so exactly to the ceramic that the distinction is only one of slight independent pressure. Limbs—more of them than the geometry seems to require—interlock and slide. Where one lifts, the water closes without sound and another occupies. I feel the faint rhythmic scrape transmitted through the plate, mouthparts I do not see working the mineral films so they will not seal. I do not hurry. Hurry has no gradient left to follow. I simply proceed, density constant, the tray generating and absorbing at the same locked rate.

The softer ones pulse between and over. Unsegmented, cool, faintly adhesive, they yield when the general pressure shifts and reseal as if the interruption were only another weather. Their interiors, when light or the equivalent of light reaches them, are cloudy suspension, chemistry that has crossed a margin so narrow it could still be argued. I do not travel far from them. A cluster occupies; I occupy with it. Days have no purchase. The filtration is not a task I perform. It is the way the film moves through what has decided, by the smallest possible margin, to be flesh.

When whatever I use for a face lifts, the haze is already inhabited. Small dark bodies persist in layers, denser near the water, a stipple that glancing contact does not startle or redirect. They collide softly with whatever surface I present and occupy the space the instant it is vacated. Against the even brightness some show the extra wet point—the sixth dark bead, or the fourth where three would have sufficed—a decision left unrevisited. They do not feed in any motion that can be isolated. They remain. Walking through them, if walking is still the word, is only a continuous soft revision of volumes already spoken for.

The galleries pull differently. Through unused slits that still carry stamps, the scale changes. Larger shapes, slow, pressed until there is no empty interval, a second country of warm dark. Heat from the buried runs keeps them at the temperature of the tile. I feel the occasional vast collective adjustment more than I see it, a rearrangement that never opens a gap. Skins the dull pale of the water-holding compounds. If consumption or budding occurs it is so perfectly matched that the count never flickers. I cannot tell the newly formed from the long-resident because time does not mark here; it is only another volume.

I am not one of these exclusively. The settlement happens gradually, the way the scurfing happens in the wet seams. Low to the pan, many-legged past necessity, shell poured with the tile itself. I scrape so the films stay open. I host, after a time, some of the dark bodies that settle and are not shaken off; they become extra points on the grey, consensus features. At the junctions—tray, weir, gallery-mouth, the low baffle that thirds a sheet of water into quieter sheets—I am simply the occupancy continuing. The machines maintain around me, finished, exchanging heat, lifting and letting fall, charge moving as faint warmth. I do not manufacture. I do not announce. I occupy until occupation is the grammar.

Night, when the light is allowed to go, does not change the pitch. The water holds its dull gleam. The pressure becomes continuous, a world-skin breathing or the equivalent in unison so complete it registers as quiet. Against a housing the temperature on whatever serves as cheek remains the same. No interval that matters has passed. I do not travel through the hour. I fill it, the way the pale ones fill the shallows, the pulsing ones the film, the dark ones the haze, the large ones the warm dark. Together we are the load the cycle was locked around. You could stand still and we would close without curiosity or hostility, completing the density.

This is the place. This is what remains when the making stopped meaning anything: the fact of the pan, warm and wet, inhabited past metaphor. I am the continuation of that fact, low, grey, excessive in limbs by a small consistent margin, proceeding in the same warm wet hour, forever. The equilibrium includes me. Nothing has been allowed to happen since, and so I have always been exactly here.
