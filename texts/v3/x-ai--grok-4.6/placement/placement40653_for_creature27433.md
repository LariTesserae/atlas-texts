The light does not announce itself. It is already there when the thought of arriving finishes weathering into the same even gray that has no edge. The clay gives once, a coolness that is not surprise, and then holds. There is no first step. There is only the long, settled fact of being inside the lid of sky.

At first it is only the interval. Not the tap of a falling bead, but the silence that has already made room for it, the silence that has always known the exact weight of that drop and has never needed to count. The silence has the texture of the pale leather underfoot: pressed, faintly damp, without beginning. It occupies no corner of the broken rectangle. It is the density the eye accepts as ordinary until it remembers there are no shadows here, only this uncolored sameness that has finished distinguishing between thing and place.

The stems rise through what might have been a hem, or fingers, or nothing that ever needed a name. They are no taller than they have always been. Lichen maps the north-facing planes in continents that do not move. The upright slab leans at its tired angle, and the lean is already the spine, already the lowered plane that was once a head. The moisture in the soil is the only memory left: not of hands, not of purpose, only of edges that were once sharper and then became the same as everything else. That memory does not stand. It lives the way the still pans live—thin, unchanging, filmed with the same thinned light.

The clay settles a fraction further. The sigh is already accounted for. It is not breath. It is the shared warmth that never quite becomes heat, the warmth that belongs equally to the stones, the water, the sparse stems, the figure that forgot to rise. There is no watching, because watching would require a difference that finished centuries ago. There is only continuance: the same damp give, the same patient stones, the same completed weathering.

This is where the interval lives. Not as a creature that could leave, not as a remnant that still remembers being built. As the hollow itself, patient, finished, without objection. The rumor of another country where things still change is only a faint coolness that rises once and then is reclaimed by the clay. The place continues being exactly itself. What remains continues being the place.
