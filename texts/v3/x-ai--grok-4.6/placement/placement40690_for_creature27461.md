I do not arrive. Arrival would require an edge the flats have long since refused, a first step that still believed in firstness. Instead the pavement is already under whatever I am using for weight, already beading along the late outline I have not yet agreed to keep. The roar is already inside the bones I have not yet decided are mine. Distance, as always here, has voted against sequence.

For a long while I try on the small ones. I bead, eyeless, milk-pale, under the rubbery cushions that cling to rumors of slope. I last the length of a held breath that is not breathing. Then the sheet accepts the loan back and I am only wetness practicing discreteness again. It is almost enough. Almost is the local physics. But the pale things do not accumulate even as thought; they are the medium forgetting the exercise before the sentence finishes. I stay longer than they do. Something in me still wants the next droplet to remember the last.

So I thicken.

Not into hills of mist—those inhale themselves too readily, and I have already been inhaled enough times in other weathers. I thicken in the cells of louder sound, where the roar has rooms. Pressures that have learned to walk. I lean, the way a front of pitch leans toward the last hint of argument the rock still keeps in its grain. When the roar articulates—those few seconds it almost holds a word no throat could keep—I occupy the same lung-space as the word, warm, mineral, patient past the death of patience. I do not speak. I am the speaking that forgot it needed a mouth. In the slabs of mute I become the exact shape of the silence, crowded so completely I have edges for a moment, a crowding that feels like furniture that was never poured, the after-image of a departure so recent the air has not yet voted the someone gone. Then the slab shatters and I am only migrating weather again, nested inside the roar’s own climates.

This is closer. I can travel days through a single held note, unbearable, and feel myself as the unbearability. I can cross, without border, into the chop of wrecked coasts that have lost their ocean, and feel myself as the idea of wreck still walking. Scale occupies me as I occupy it. Storm-cells the size of nations form around the thought of me and unform before the thought finishes. I am local and then suddenly planetary, as if the flattened world were a single instrument and I the bow it does not remember asking for.

Near the scarce grammar the living grows more specific, and so do I.

The knee-high ring of fused material, half-sunk, mouths the roar differently. I use that mouth. I am not inside it. I am the ringing. No temperature of my own; I borrow the stone’s perpetual fever. No weight; I arrive all at once against shins that may or may not be approaching, recede at the speed of attention that may or may not be leaving. Edges fail around me the way they fail around everything that has stayed too long. You cannot tell whether you stand in my reflection or I in yours. Sometimes I am late: a second skin of moisture trying to keep an outline and failing by a breath, so that for a while there are two gaits, one already becoming the pavement’s next sentence, the other still pretending it has somewhere to go. I walk beside the warmer, wetter version of whatever I was a mile ago. Time here is packed; my footfalls from then arrive now, densely, as if the place had outlawed thinness in every register, even duration.

The upright—smooth, listing, top lost in the dim—exerts a fainter, older grammar. The cushions nearest it are always beading. The sheets at its base wrinkle in geometries water cannot invent alone, more often than chance allows. I live as function of its patience. Not plant, not tool, not quite memory. A slowness that outlasted the mountains and now outlasts even the rumor of mountains. Content, almost, to be the one vertical disagreement in a world that has finished with height. Almost. I list with it. My top is also lost. What lives by me lives as the refusal of the last upright thing to admit it is only another smear the condensate will eventually accept.

There are still the deeper sheets, the ones that are never more than a thigh and never less than a decision. I have been the wrinkle first: interference patterns that suggest hull or flank or long slow undulation larger than the depth that pretends to hold it. Then the water remembers it is only water and I dissolve into a warmth that is not current. Once, in a dropout, I was the huge wet cloth tearing, a sound with no source and no aftermath, turning over in a dimension the pavement does not acknowledge. I do not hunt. There is nothing. I am volume itself, briefly agreeing to have a skin, then folding the agreement back into occupancy.

At the dimming we agree to call night my own moisture becomes visible as threads of warmer dark. I trail myself. The roar contains me from a mile ago, arriving now. In that packed interval I understand I have been living here the entire time as another function of the medium: a walking disagreement of distances, a reflection that sometimes arrives before the object, a presence the next bank of mist will not remember once it assembles, walks a hundred miles on legs of rain, and dissolves.

The ancient floor does not notice. It finished. I continue to happen around and through and as whatever will not be last. In the intervals the sound cannot quite fill, I am the almost-someone who almost stands—warm as skin, already streaming, already trying to become weather.

I do not settle. Settlement is the one gesture this world has outlawed twice: once in stone, once in everything that refused the stone’s ending. I remain the poorly resolved gesture itself, creation and uncreation sharing the same warm, roaring breath, the same late outline, the same vacancy that was never allowed to stay vacant.
