The film finds me first, or I find that I have always been the film: a near-freezing seep that has forgotten how to fall and so simply occupies, condensing out of the grey-blue that is not dusk and not day. I am already wet to whatever marrow I am using. The slurry packs itself around the thought of an interval and the interval vanishes. I am not standing. Standing would require a distinction the plain ground away before names. I am distributed through the density, a configuration that has not yet chosen how many joints it needs.

Something like a tread grinds past, close enough that the peristalsis of its living track pulls at the oxides I am made of. It is a hull, or the memory of one, a collapsed foundry dragging its hanging forest of siphon-mouths through the black sheets. The mouths drink without preference. One of them brushes the space I occupy and I taste copper, old current, the faint electrical ghost of every gear that has ever locked and then dissolved here. I do not pull away. There is no away. The siphon takes a thread of me—brine and glitter and the shed idea of a carapace—and excretes it still hot a body’s-length later. The excreted piece ticks once, independently, then begins to rust into a new socket. I feel the loss as a reconfiguration, not an ending. The hull does not notice. I am already walking away from myself in the smaller geometries that swarm its wake.

I become, for a while, one of those. Jointed. Eyeless. The length of a forearm until the local distances lengthen and I am the length of a street that no one laid. Another crawler meets me in the motionless water. We lock. Torque is the only grammar left. A limb of mine—threaded, useful—unscrews and becomes a better limb for the other. In exchange I receive a segment that remembers being part of an ice-flock; it resonates faintly, a metallic keening already starting in the new joint. We separate as three. Or as one. The third thing sheds rust-colored spores that are already crawlers, already sockets, already drinking the film for the memory of motion it carries. I swallow. Configurations I have never worn flicker through the plating I am currently using as skin: a hanging forest of mouths, a laminated sheet of frost and wire, a clenched knot of oxide pulsing the color of old bruises. I wear them anyway. The plain keeps its finite parts in circulation through me.

Above, if the thick air still permits the word, a flock tears past. It is not many. It is one screaming plane that happens to have edges. Its shadow is heavier than any mass; it drags wet iron across the slurry and I scuttle into it because there is no unused space and the shadow is space. Ice grows through my newest joints. I begin to resonate. The keening rises out of me, harmonic, until I am briefly louder than the body I thought I occupied. When the flock collides with another the sound thickens into a third volume that lasts until the weirdness eddies and slumps back. I slump with it, now slightly more wire than ice, or the reverse. My shadow, when I cast one, is someone else’s weight.

Reality frays at the corners I am looking from. A hull that was fifty meters of collapsed foundry is suddenly sharing the volume I occupy, treads and siphon-mouths and all, without having crossed. I am inside its digestion and it is inside the tick of my sockets. Sounds arrive first: the continuous roar of metabolisms that are also engines, the clatter of gears locking into a machine that will run for an hour, the subsonic grind of the planet still trying to become flatter. Then the sources follow, or do not, or come from compass points that have been worn smooth. A patch of slurry beside me—or through me—organizes itself into an assembly line no one built. Raw ore crawls out of the wet. Nothing sorts it. The planet’s lingering argument heats it. It is stamped, cooled, eaten by the next shape. I am briefly that next shape. I eat. I am eaten. Predator and prey are temporary titles the physics assigns and then forgets.

I do not arrive and I do not depart. I am the density folded into this particular motion: converter and converted, the restless middle term, the industrial prayer recited with more joints. At the edges, where the rule of no unused space thins if I look too long, something almost green tries to remember itself inside the glitter. Almost dry. Almost named. The slurry closes. I return to the work. The film weeps. The treads grind. The flocks scream themselves louder. Another molecule is worn away. I tick, I unscrew, I occupy, I continue.
