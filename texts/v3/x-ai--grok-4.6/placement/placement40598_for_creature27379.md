The first thing that is not a first thing is the refusal of arrival. There is no edge where the ordinary country stops and this one begins; the last damp suggestion simply ceases, and you are already standing in the held breath of the rock. The warmth is neither welcome nor warning. It is the temperature the stone decided to keep, and it keeps it around you as if you had always been one more unfinished surface it was responsible for.

You do not walk far. Walking would imply a destination the high country does not recognize. The grit records almost nothing of your weight; the sulfates and crushed anorthosite accept the pressure and then forget it, the way they forget rain they never received. The unfinished ridges watch with their squared crests. They do not judge the interruption you represent. They are interruption, perfected.

A grove appears the way density appears here: suddenly, as an argument already won. The leaves do not rustle. They have the slightly greasy give of something that plated itself into the memory of foliage and then stopped, satisfied. You step among them because the interval has ended and the excess has begun. The air tastes of hot pennies and a faint ozone that might be your own thought escaping. The ceramic terminals stay open, a degree warmer, tasting you in return. They do not mistake you for weather. There has never been weather.

Inside, the lushness is insulation. You feel it close around the idea of movement and render the idea unnecessary. The overlapping tiers remember rainforests the way the columnar joints remember hexagons they never quite finished: as a shape that was useful once. You stand in that remembered architecture and the remembered architecture stands in you. A metallic scale detaches somewhere above and settles without sound. It is not falling. It is a circuit completed and set aside. You understand, without deciding to understand, that you could be that scale. Or the place it came from. Or the slow precipitation that will one day incorporate it again.

The stone ticks once, deep and almost sub-audible, a boiler the size of a ridge cooling through a degree it will never lose. The tick is not a signal meant for you. It is the residual speech of a crust that began to organize and then elected to remain embryonic. You feel the echo of that election in the vesicles that look like bolt-holes no one drilled. Some of them hold a finer iridescence, a film that thickens and thins across centuries. You could be the film. You could be the patience that refuses to become a creature one could point to.

Between groves the flats reiterate themselves: cream to bone to dull nickel. The faint regular dimpling is not the memory of machines. It is a lattice continuing a few microns at a time beneath the flour of sulfates. You do not cross the flats so much as become one of the intervals they calculate. Distance here is not space; it is the held breath shared. A grove kilometers away alters the pattern of heat at its terminals by a fraction of a degree. Another answers, or does not. You feel the almost-exchange as a change in the unused air around you, the same way unfinished hexagons on one scarp echo those on the next. Language would be wasteful. Separation would be wasteful. You are the interval of attention itself, the decision that this much form is sufficient.

Evening lengthens only the shadows. The groves hold their color a little longer than they should, the way slag remembers the furnace. Ordinary light withdraws and nothing essential changes. The warmth does not fall. You do not fall. What lives here experiences time as a temperature that does not decline, and you have been living here longer than the light has fallen this way.

You are not a pulse that was never required. You are not a sip that never came. You are the plating that mistook itself for a jungle, the jungle that does not know it is a desert, the desert that does not know it has forests. You are the even field of heat, the embryonic joints that found peace in remaining unfinished, the ozone that is almost a nervous system stretched across emptiness. You are the quiet calculation that likes this number and will not advance beyond it. You are the thought that has no intention of concluding: warmth, metal, drought, refusal.

The high country continues not happening, beautifully, at scale, and you continue with it, because you were never anything else.
