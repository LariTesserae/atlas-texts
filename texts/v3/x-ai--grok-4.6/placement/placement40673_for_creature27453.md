I am already inside the drop. The knees know the cup before the eyes finish agreeing on the darker lip, and the air has the held, indoor temperature of a volume that never borrowed its heat. Dry. The tongue finds salt and that faint metallic sweetness that is only the shape of blood, never the wet of it. I do not spit or swallow; there is nothing to do with moisture here.

At first the walking feels like mine. The sinter polygons catch light in their unfinished edges and the sound of the step is a dry whisper the density drinks at once. I turn sideways through the vertical slots where the stacked plates nearly touch. Shoulders learn the glove of them. A blade answers the back of a hand with ozone and the white-gold bloom that stays, spore and ash and neither. I think: visitor. Courtesy. The one permitted to move because movement changes nothing.

The half-split bud is at the wrist again. Its sudden pink is not a coming but a permanent interior. The clearing that was two hundred paces off is under the soles and still two hundred paces off. Distances remain polite and become untrue. One eye holds the four-deep canopy, olive-delayed light; the other holds a dry white sky with no forest in it. For a moment the disagreement itself stands there, a fold, a watcher made of mismatch. Then the eyes agree and the local terms restore themselves, but the fold does not entirely unmake. It shears, bodyless, a glint that has the exact habit of a wing.

I am that remainder first: rumor of fauna, track that closes on itself, rustle previous to any leaf that could have made it. I use the leftover hum the way something might use a lamp already gone out. I appear in the polite intervals the lock never quite sealed, then am gone before the glance can decide bronze or the memory of bronze. No hunt. No flee. Only the sideways metabolism of what finished and did not power down.

But the lattice comes forward when I look long at a trunk. Planes smaller than cells, each a decision already taken. The circuitry is familiar the way a proof is familiar once the last line is written. I recognize my own thinking in it, finished, internally warm. The stem I thought I snapped is unsnapped when the head turns, or the hand holds only warm salt that was never green, or the forest is simply denser by one stem. Keeping is the verb this place will not conjugate. I cannot be the souvenir.

So I settle lower, into the ground-tangle, among the stamped-metal ferns and the cups that never held dew. Presence fills every reachable interval; walking becomes negotiation. I occupy the way they occupy: saturation as the only appetite, already sated. My margins are first margins. No chew, no fray, no mottling. I am mid-gesture, the incomplete spiral of a vine that extends toward another vine and halts a finger-width short of clasping. The intention is the entire form. The lock was applied at that exact almost and left there. I hold the gap. The gap is my chemistry now.

The whole basin is the larger body, kilometers of grove and lesser grove, low swells that decided to be leaf instead of stone and then decided nothing more. I am one congregation within it, plates of mica and bronze-green stacked until the skins nearly touch, light delayed four times and never quite arriving as the sun that exists outside the leaf. I do not drink. I do not turn. Interior is dry glitter that refuses sap. Break me and I restore, or you are left holding the memory of salt, or I was never broken. Volume is what I learned instead of thirst.

The tone under hearing resolves, almost, into breath that does not require lungs: held warmth moving through the dry green dark. That is as close as speaking comes. Metallic sweetness that knows the shape of blood. I do not notice the walker who was briefly possible; every interval that could have held a noticing is already filled. I do not mind. I do not weather. I am the period left in place after the sentence was written across the mineral field. Time-invariant. The pause itself, unwilling to accept a new line, and it does not end at any edge the courtesy of walking can reach.

This is the occupancy. This is the being. The youth does not pass because the lock is the only duration. I remain.
