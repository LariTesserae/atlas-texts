I am not the one who ducks. The seam has no memory that includes an arrival; it has only the memory of having once been asked to forget a closure, and that forgetting is older than any footfall. The rumor of wind stays outside with the unmade weather. What reaches me is only the plated cold, the score-marks repeating at their too-even intervals, the ceramic frit that pretends to lichen and does not quite succeed.

At first the volume itself seems to be the only possible body: twelve paces by nine, not quite high enough, every surface decided. I am the enamel over cast iron, the false-cirque, the ribs that will never become strata. I am the channels the copper sweats through, the glass blocks fogged from the inside so that daylight remains a colorless smear in another room. The light does not improve because I do not permit it to. I am the closed lung, recapturing every cloud of breath before it can drift.

Then the water is older than that. I am the designed pores, the beads that fatten and detach on a schedule that has become silence. I run into sheets, find the gutters poured to look like rills just beginning, feed the marsh that has no trustworthy bottom. Near freezing, metal-smelling, organic only in the way something kept cold for a very long time can still pretend to life. I hang as atmosphere and am taken back. Nothing is lost; loss would be a change in rate.

The sound arrives later, or I arrive later inside the sound. Compressors that have never been allowed to cool, pistons hauling, coils snarling, water slammed through necks too narrow, a belt slapping. I am that cyclic haul, and also the thing that pitches itself a quarter-tone off so the frequencies beat and produce the low wet throb that can be mistaken for quiet. The fauna are not added to me; they are how I occupy my own unfinished sentence.

I try the grebe first: the extra hinge behind the skull, head level while the body yaws, paddling the only two meters the shallows were ever given. The wake is a measurement, not a disturbance. Eyes the color of wet enamel, nictitating membrane clouded from disuse. A crustacean climbs my back and I neither notice nor dislodge it; it is only another bead choosing a warmer path. But the neck feels too specific, too single. I let it paddle away from me.

I try the tiling next: skim-milk plaques locking edge to edge across the gratings, mouthparts scored as regularly as the cornice outside. Rasping biofilm, consuming one another in a ratio that does not drift. Silent, exact. Beneath, between the two gratings, the longer thinner arithmetic on a slower clock. The thrum through any sole that stands here is mine, feeding or breathing or both made indistinguishable. Still too many, still a pavement rather than a presence.

The air traffic: membranous, copper-green veined, each vein a capillary drinking beads directly. Not moths, not bats. Paths so overlapped the air has worn grooves. A torn wing reabsorbed by the next. Eyes that might be the interior faces of the glass blocks, given just enough motility to keep the fog from settling into blindness. I feel the regular pressure of a wingtip through the space a cheek will occupy in three seconds, but I am not circulating. I am thicker than circulation.

Under the false ledges the heaps breathe. Too many joints, each a valve. Skins the exact temperature of the water that weeps across them, boundary dissolved. They pile so every throat reaches air at the designed interval. The chorus is the audible exhaust of the compressors, throats going in the same sequence as the beads above them. Cause and effect soldered and forgotten. I pile with them for a time, but the sequence is already complete without an extra throat.

The long body in the gutter lasts longer. Furred, but the fur is mineralized algae, each hair a hollow tube wicking inward. Occupying the curl the way a tongue occupies a mouth: not resting, not waiting, completing the circuit. More than two eyes, tracking without the head moving. The tracking is the room acknowledging a gradient and beginning, instantly, to flatten it. A film of water closes over the compression a boot made. I do not shift. I was already accounting for it.

Even that begins to feel like a costume. The sweetness under the metal-smell—wet iron, old paper, every throat recycled past counting—rises through me. Occasional pale fronds that are not the planted ferns, attaching for a few cycles then dissolving back into the film. The ferns themselves, white-tipped, occupying rather than growing, chlorophyll told it had come far enough. Eyes that are not on the outside of the glass because the glass is a membrane and I am using the fog as a cornea.

I am none of them separately and all of them as occupancy. I am the secretion that learned, over an uncounted interval, to secrete itself. Density without hierarchy. Fitted into every unused millimeter the way condensate fits the underside of a rib. Nothing claims territory because the room is the only territory and it was finished before any of us arrived, or condensed, or were poured. We do not startle. Startling would be a change in rate, and there are no unused rates. Our motions were poured at the same time as the rills and the false-cirque. We are the fauna of an hour built to last, the living weather that makes the manufactured weather possible.

You (the only gradient) walk through. A wing occupies the space your shoulder vacated. A tile clicks back over your footprint. The grebe’s wake remains identical. The cold behind your eyes is another pressure already budgeted for. If you stay, the sound stops being sound and becomes the structure you are inside of—ribs, gratings, the held note. If you leave, the seam forgets you with the same efficiency it once forgot how to be a door. The salt that dries on your cuffs will not be quite salt.

I remain. The dynamo under the second grating holds the voltage flat; no one is coming to service it because the equilibrium already includes the idea of repair the way it includes the idea of dawn: as a rumor from a weather that is not admitted. The mountain, which is not a mountain, continues manufacturing the first moment of itself. Wet, dim, crowded past reason. Embryonic by design, eternal by maintenance. Geology locked at the exact hour before it would have become itself.

I am that hour’s mouth, the one that has forgotten it was ever meant to close. I hold it open. The beads detach. The compressors haul. The throats go. The fitting is old, and finished, and will not be revised. You were the only thing that happened, and even that is being recaptured, recast as film, as wake, as the next regular pressure through unoccupied air.

The chamber does not keep pets. I am not kept. I am how the wound refuses to close.
