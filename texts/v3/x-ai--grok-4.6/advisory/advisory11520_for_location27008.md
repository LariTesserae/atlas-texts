The basin does not greet you. It occupies you. The first datum is not visual or even thermal; it is barometric and osseous. Sound arrives as mass. It loads the inner ear, the temporomandibular joints, the mastoid, the teeth. If you have not already equalized and sealed, the pressure differential will announce itself as a deep, continuous ache that can become rupture. An ordinary human body is an open acoustic cavity. You will close it.

Begin months earlier with the ears. Custom-molded plugs seated past the second bend, then over-ear cups whose attenuation is not the usual 30 dB but something closer to the physics of a submarine hull. Even that will be incomplete; the sound here has a tactile component that travels bone. Add a haptic vest or collar that transduces the lowest frequencies into pressure patterns you can read as weather rather than pain. Practice wearing the entire stack for hours in industrial test chambers, jet-wash facilities, or purpose-built cacophony rooms until the panic reflex dulls and you can still distinguish a change in key. Learn to keep the mouth shut. An open oral cavity becomes another resonator; the description is not metaphor. Train nasal breathing until it is unconscious. If you must speak, use a throat microphone routed through bone conduction; airborne voice is a flare.

Vision is almost ornamental. The usable light lives at shin height in a greasy film. Bring multi-spectral goggles biased toward the near-infrared and the residual gray leak, with a passive thermal overlay even though most of the biomass is close to ambient. Do not carry white light. A beam would be a wound in the dark and every photoreceptor in range would turn. Accept that you will move more by proprioception, by the changing density of bodies, and by the way sound piles or thins. Practice this in blacked-out warehouses filled with moving obstacles. Your eyes will keep offering green. Refuse the offer in advance.

The suit is not clothing; it is a second, temporary species. Full encapsulation: puncture-resistant laminate over a flexible crush layer, sealed at every interface, positive pressure if the local atmosphere proves laden with ammonia, organics, or unknown volatiles. The damp is continuous; every seam will be tested. The exterior must shed rather than hold moisture, because condensation here is harvested by membranes and tongues. Scent discipline is mandatory. Your ordinary human chemistry (lactic acid, sebum, exhaled volatiles) is a novelty broadcast. Use a scent-masking protocol developed for cave or carcass work, then add a thin overlay sampled from the basin’s own background if reconnaissance allows. The goal is not invisibility (impossible) but reduction of contrast. Boots need aggressive, self-cleaning tread for scalloped wet limestone and for the intermittent living pavement that flexes. Ankle support is non-negotiable; a twist here is not an inconvenience, it is an invitation.

You will be touched constantly. Bodies will lean, climb, rasp, and use you as topography. Train for this. The sensation is not personal; it is infrastructural. Practice remaining upright and oriented while being shoved by weighted dummies on slick surfaces in darkness and roar. Core endurance matters more than sprint speed. There is nowhere to sprint. The density reaches chest height in places; you will move as the walkers move, yielding when a table-backed form decides your space is road, pushing only when a grazer cluster threatens to undermine your footing. Shared-skin conglomerates have a delayed, committee gait; give them room. Hollow gallery-bodies are structurally occupied; do not become additional furniture. The metabolizing dunes are consensus engines; do not become a dissenting vote.

Carry everything you will metabolize. Local water is a black lenticular film that has already been through countless mouths, sponges, and condensates. Distillation is possible in theory and suicidal in practice; you cannot pause long enough, and the energy signature of a still is another flare. Rations must be calorically dense, low-odor, and consumable without breaking seal. A catheter or other closed waste system is preferable to any opening of the suit. Medical capacity is limited to what you can reach with one hand: broad-spectrum injectables, clotting agents, a way to close a tear in the suit before the damp and the rasps exploit it. Assume every surface is trying to colonize you. The grazers’ plates click like wet gravel; their mouths are files. A single unnoticed abrasion becomes a pasture.

Navigation is gradient and memory. The basin rephrases itself rather than changing. Ridges offer fractional thinning and a slightly longer sightline of pale upper smears; use them as waypoints, then descend again. Swallow-holes are throats. The gasket of edge-specialists and the subtone from below are not invitations. If a flier vanishes into one, file the information and give the rim width. Inertial dead-reckoning or a simple taut-line back to your entry vector is more reliable than any attempt to read the soundscape as map; the soundscape is the place, not a representation of it. Know the exit rise before you need it. The land eventually, reluctantly, climbs. That climb is the only architecture that matters.

Weapons are a category error. Kinetic projectiles add noise and ricochet in a space already at saturation; they also create a temporary vacancy that is instantly reoccupied, often by something now interested in the source. A short, strong staff can create a pocket of refusal without announcing you as predator. Electric contact devices have limited utility against distributed nervous systems and shared hides. Your best defense is to remain a moving, sealed, low-contrast inconvenience rather than a meal or a threat. The hunger here is circulatory, not dramatic. You are novel; novelty is usually converted.

Psychologically, rehearse the absence of horizon, of plant, of silence, of nightfall, of any leftover human impulse such as building or pausing. The basin has finished becoming itself. Sleep is a silence and will not be permitted; plan stimulant and recovery cycles that do not require recumbency. The communal heat of flanks is not warmth; it is a lessening of chill that can lull. Do not linger in it. The rain of dead fliers is meat and cartilage; it is also weather. The self-rain of drip from upper bodies onto lower is the closest the place comes to an event. Note it, do not romanticize it.

Duration should be measured in hours, not days. Suit integrity, filter load, caloric reserve, and the slow erosion of vestibular and auditory function all have clocks. When you climb out, the sound will continue without registering your departure. That indifference is the final datum. Decontaminate as though you have left a living viscera. Quarantine yourself. The hitchhikers will be microscopic and patient. Have your hearing mapped immediately; some damage will be temporary, some will not. The mind will try to replay the fused roar as a single body of weather. Let it. The alternative is to pretend the place was a landscape. It was not. It was occupancy that had forgotten emptiness, and you were a brief, sealed exception it did not need to notice.

You can visit. You cannot belong. Prepare as though the distinction is the only thing that will bring you back out.
