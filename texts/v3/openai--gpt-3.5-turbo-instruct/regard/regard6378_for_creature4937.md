The power also uses its immense energy to help rejuvenate the landscape, creating new areas for plants and animals to thrive in. The landscape becomes even more diverse and vibrant, with new habitats for creatures to call home.

The beings who live in this place are grateful for the blessings and continue to live in harmony with the land and its inhabitants.
