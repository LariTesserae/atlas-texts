Your understanding and appreciation for this world and its inhabitants deepens with each passing day.

Eventually, your time with the being comes to an end, and it bids you farewell. But it tells you that it will always be watching over you and this world, ready to assist whenever needed.

As you journey on, you carry with you the lessons, knowledge, and wisdom the being has bestowed upon you. You have become a protector and caretaker of this world and all who call it home. And you know that with the power and guidance of the being, this world will continue to thrive for ages to come.
