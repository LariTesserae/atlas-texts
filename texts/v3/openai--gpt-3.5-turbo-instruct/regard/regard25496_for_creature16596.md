They show me visions of how they have helped the desert people in times of need, providing guidance and assistance when needed. They have also protected them from dangers, both natural and otherwise.

They tell me that they will continue to watch over and guide this place, as long as the people remain true to their connection with the land and its spirits. They urge me to spread this message, to remind the world of the importance of harmony with nature and the power of the unseen.

And with that, they disappear, leaving behind a sense of peace and protection. As I resume my journey, I know that I am not alone in this place. The benevolent and wise presence is always watching, a silent guardian of the desert and its people.
