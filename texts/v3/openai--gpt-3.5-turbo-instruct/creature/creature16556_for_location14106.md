Closer to the ground, you might come across a herd of boulder goats, their sturdy hooves allowing them to climb the rough cliffs with ease. And if you're lucky, you might even catch a glimpse of a shadowcat, an elusive predator that moves in silence and strikes with deadly precision. Despite the harsh terrain, this landscape teems with life, each creature adapted to its unique environment. It's a world full of mystery and wonder, just waiting to be discovered.
