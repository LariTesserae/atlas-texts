The inhabitants of this place are also deeply connected to nature. They have a spiritual connection to the land and its creatures, often seeking guidance and wisdom from them.

Despite their advanced technology, the inhabitants of this place have not lost touch with their ancient roots. They still hold rituals and ceremonies to honor their ancestors and the spirits that protect their land.
