The inhabitants of this place are much like the landscape itself - raw, undeveloped, and constantly evolving. The primary source of sustenance here is the wild plants and animals that call this place home. These creatures are sturdy and resilient, honed by the harsh conditions of this landscape.

The people who live here are equally hardy, utilizing primitive technology and their own skills honed by generations of survival in this harsh environment. They are a deeply spiritual and connected people, living in harmony with the land and all its inhabitants.

Despite their rugged exterior, the people of this place are fiercely protective of their home, always looking out for any potential threats. Their unique understanding of the ecosystem allows them to navigate the ever-changing landscape with ease, adapting to any new challenges that arise.

Despite the challenges of living in this place, the people have thrived, creating a community that is deeply connected and supportive. They celebrate their uniqueness and embrace the weirdness and beauty of their existence in this strange and wondrous world.
