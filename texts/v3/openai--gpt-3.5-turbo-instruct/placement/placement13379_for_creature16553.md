As it approaches, you notice that it is carrying an intricate trident made of the same coral structures that you saw earlier. It looks powerful and intimidating, but the figure's movements are calm and purposeful.

Without saying a word, the figure gestures for you to follow. As you do, you notice a group of these creatures waiting on a nearby rock. They seem to be communicating telepathically, their thoughts echoing in your mind.

You follow the figure and the group to a large structure made entirely of coral. It is majestic and intricate, with multiple levels and chambers. The creatures move with ease and familiarity, leading you through winding halls and secret passages.

As you enter the heart of the structure, you are greeted by a being that seems to radiate wisdom and power. It is the ruler of this watery world, the one responsible for shaping and maintaining its delicate balance.

The being nods, and you can sense its approval. "We sense that you have a deep respect and curiosity for our world. We will grant you the ability to explore and learn, as long as you respect our ways and our rules."
