As you stand there, taking in the sights and sounds of this world, you feel a hand on your shoulder. Startled, you turn to see a being standing next to you. It is human-like in shape, but its skin is a deep, vibrant blue. Its hair is long and flowing, matching its skin color, and its eyes seem to shimmer in the light.

This is a being known as a water elemental. It is the being that holds dominion over the water in this world, controlling the flow and distribution of the life-giving substance. It is said that without the water elemental's guidance, this world would cease to exist.

The water elemental looks at you with a curious expression, as if it has never seen a visitor in this world before. You feel a sense of peace and serenity ripple through your body in its presence.

But as quickly as it appeared, the water elemental vanishes with a flick of its wrist. You are left standing there, alone once again in this vast and mysterious world. As you continue to explore, you can't help but wonder what other incredible beings reside in this incredible place.
