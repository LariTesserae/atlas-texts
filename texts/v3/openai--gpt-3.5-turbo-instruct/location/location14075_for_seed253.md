This place is a vast, ancient landscape, with extreme elevation and sparse density. The water is submerged and encompasses the entire world, creating an all-liquid environment. The temperature is cool, lending to a sense of stillness and quiet. The erosion of this land is deeply aged, settled-into-final-form, giving it a primordial feel.

Despite the sparse density, the vegetation here is abundant and encompasses every inch of the land. It serves as the living mass, with plants as the main architecture of this place. The scale is open and extends beyond the immediate landscape, with features that extend to regional or even mid-range levels.

The built environment in this landscape is total and man-made, with no natural substrate left. Everything is constructed and made to fit within this space. However, there is no technology present, just pure nature and biological entities.

The light here is minimal, giving a sense of darkness and quiet. However, the fauna is abundant, filling this landscape with life and sound. And while everything seems to be frozen in a never-changing state, there is a sense of unusual and dynamic energy pulsing through the land.

This landscape is both mysterious and ancient, a place that holds secrets and stories waiting to be discovered. It is both eerie and beautiful, a world of its own where time seems to stand still. And as you stand in awe of this place, you can't help but feel like you are a part of something awe-inspiring and greater than yourself.
