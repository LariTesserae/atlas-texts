As I enter this landscape, my senses are immediately overwhelmed by the sound of water. It is not a constant sound, but instead comes in waves, like intermittent moisture in the air. The ground beneath me is damp and soft, with traces of moisture in the air.

Looking around, I see that the entire landscape is covered in vegetation. It is a total overgrowth, with plants serving as both substrate and living mass. It is as if the plants have completely taken over this world, with only small gaps in their coverage for me to move through.

The temperature here is warm, but not unbearable. It is the perfect climate for the plants to thrive in. I can feel the heat radiating off their leaves and the ground, creating a warm and humid atmosphere.

The landscape seems to be at an extreme elevation, with tall mountains all around me. The soil is ancient and has settled into its final form. It almost seems as though I can see the passage of time etched into the landscape itself.

Despite the extreme elevation, there is still evidence of erosion. The mountains have been worn down over time, creating a primordial landscape that feels both ancient and wild.

The scale of this landscape is open and expansive, with vast stretches of land and towering mountains. It extends well beyond what I can see, giving me a sense of the enormity of this place.

There is a feeling of emptiness here, with almost nothing per volume. The vast space between the plants and the mountains creates a sense of void that is both beautiful and eerie.

As I continue to explore, I come across scattered remnants of structures. They are small and faint, with only traces of their presence still visible. It is clear that this landscape was once inhabited, but the structures have been reclaimed by nature.

Despite the presence of these structures, the technology level here is primitive. It is evident that the inhabitants of this landscape relied on early-tool-use, simple-fabrication, and manual-craft to survive.

The light here is normal, with the sun shining down on the landscape. However, the dense vegetation creates pockets of shade, making the landscape feel even more dynamic.

As I move through the landscape, I am overwhelmed by the sheer number of fauna present. They are active and plentiful, filling every inch of this place. Yet, there is a sense of something unusual about them, as if they have adapted to this landscape in a unique way.

The sound here is deafening, with the constant rush of water and the calls of the fauna. It is almost overwhelming, but it adds to the sense of being in a completely different world.

Overall, this landscape is unlike anything I have ever encountered. It is ruled by the plants, filled with a variety of fauna, and constantly changing with the passing of time. It is both beautiful and haunting, a true and unique wonder of nature.
