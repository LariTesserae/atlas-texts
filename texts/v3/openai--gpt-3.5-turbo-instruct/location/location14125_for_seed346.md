The landscape is a vast expanse of damp, cold land, covered in sparse vegetation. The terrain is low in elevation, with occasional pockets of water and intermittent moisture. The land is still in its early stages of formation, with emerging erosion patterns and raw geology.

Despite its size, the landscape feels small and intimate, with a scale that is best described as cathedral-like. The density is high, with many presences per volume, giving the impression that the land is almost saturated with life.

The landscape has also been heavily built upon, with urban settlements dotting the land. The construction is organized and layered, creating a dense and complex infrastructure. However, the technology used in the construction goes beyond modern, with engineering that is beyond our current understanding of physics. This technology gives a sense of otherworldliness to the landscape.

Despite the advanced technology and dense population, the landscape is mostly quiet, with minimal sounds. However, an eerie sense of reality breaking down can be felt, giving a hint of the strange and mysterious. This is reinforced by the presence of rare fauna, adding to the sense of otherness.

The landscape is constantly in a state of change, but at a slow pace. Gradual cycles and weak gradients give the impression of a patient and patient landscape, waiting for something to happen. The light here is normal, providing a sense of familiarity amidst the strange and foreign surroundings.
