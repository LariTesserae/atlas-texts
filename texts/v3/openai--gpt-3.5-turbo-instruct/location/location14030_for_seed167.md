The temperature here is warm, almost tropical. The water doesn't seem to affect it much, as if it's always the same temperature regardless of the environment.

The elevation is low, with the highest point being only a few meters above the water's surface. The water seems to have eroded everything into its own unique state, making it seem like it's always been this way. It's balanced and calm, with no signs of any recent changes.

The light here is mostly dark, with very little natural light filtering through the water. There are occasional bursts of bioluminescence, but they're quick and rare.

The fauna is abundant, with a diversity of species that are unlike anything I've ever seen. Some are clearly aquatic, while others can move on both land and water. They all seem to coexist peacefully, with no signs of any predator-prey relationships.

The weirdness of this place is ever-present, with unusual features and creatures that defy my understanding of nature. The sound is mostly quiet, with only the occasional sound of moving water breaking the stillness.
