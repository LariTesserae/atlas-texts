Mount Vex is home to a vibrant, layered community of life, perfectly adapted to its slow, humid, and luminous world:

**People** – Tens of thousands of inhabitants live in dense vertical neighborhoods carved into the structure itself, from agriculturalists in the warm Lower Gardens, artisans and traders in the Mid-Reach, water-tenders maintaining the channels of the Condensation Terraces, to the Keepers at the Summit who tend the ancient engines and light arrays with priestly care.

**Plants** – Towering cultivated canopy trees in structural basins, broad waxy vines climbing walls, lush ferns with condensation-trapping fronds, resilient mosses carpeting every shaded surface, and dense shrubs filling the understory, creating a perpetual green jungle that blankets most of the pale ceramic structure.

**Birds** – Numerous resident species that have settled here, flashing iridescent greens and blues as they dart through the canopy, filling the air with constant calls alongside migratory visitors.

**Insects** – Abundant and diverse, including ceramic-mimicking beetles, large hand-sized moths with light-dappled wings, and bee-like pollinators that produce prized honey.

**Arboreal mammals** – Curious, semi-tame monkey-cat-like creatures with grasping hands and prehensile tails that leap confidently through the trees and railings, feeding on fruit and insects.

**Aquatic life** – Fish, amphibians, insects, and algae thriving in the slow-moving cisterns and channels, forming small self-sustaining ecosystems that are lightly tended and harvested.

All of this life thrives together in the bright, warm, humming environment of the mountain, woven into a single, dense, interdependent world where the built structure and living things have become inseparable.
