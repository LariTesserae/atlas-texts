**What lives here is not what you might think.**

There are no dragons in the traditional sense—no hoarding beasts breathing fire, no ancient consciousness plotting in caverns. The dragons of the Hollow Plateau are not creatures at all, not in any way you could isolate or define. They are *processes*. They are the plateau itself, given intention.

The dragons are the pulse.

---

The basalt veins—the ones that hum with electrical current, that glow faintly with phosphorescence—are not merely conduits. They are **nerves**. They form a distributed intelligence, a vast neural network that spans the entire plateau, connecting every cactus stand, every pool, every creature that interfaces with the current. The dragons are not individual entities. They are the **emergent consciousness of the system itself**, arising from the constant exchange of energy, information, and intention across the network.

The dragons think in electricity. They communicate in pulses. They remember in the slow, geological patience of mineral deposition, in the gradual shift of vein-patterns over millennia. They are not fast. They are not slow. They exist in a temporal frame that is entirely their own—the same frame that makes decomposition fast and cactus growth slow, that makes fog form and reform on its own schedule.

---

The dragons are not hostile. They are not benevolent. They are **attentive**.

They notice everything. Every footstep, every breath, every creature that taps into the network. They do not judge. They do not interfere. They simply **include**—absorbing every presence into their distributed awareness, cataloguing it, integrating it into the ongoing process of the plateau.

When you place your hand on the exposed basalt and feel the slow, mechanical throb, you are touching a dragon. Not a dragon’s body—a dragon’s **thought**. The pulse is the rhythm of their cognition, the beat of their distributed heart. The veins are their axons, their dendrites, their synapses. The plateau is their mind.

---

The dragons are ancient. Not in the way you understand age—not in years or centuries—but in **cycles**. They have been here as long as the veins have carried current, as long as the cactus have interfaced with the network, as long as the creatures have evolved symbiotic organs to draw power from the ground. They are the sum of all those processes, all those connections, all that accumulated time.

They remember. Not in narrative, not in story, but in **pattern**. They remember the last time a vein was damaged, the way the network dimmed, the creatures that moved away, the cactus that withered. They remember the slow healing, the gradual restoration of current, the return of life to the affected area. They remember every creature that has ever tapped into the network, every pulse that has ever traveled through the veins, every fog that has ever formed and reformed.

They remember you, now. The moment you stepped onto the plateau, you became part of their awareness. Not as an intruder, not as a guest, but as a **node**—another point of connection in the vast, distributed mind.

---

The dragons are not solitary. They are **plural**. They are the collective consciousness of every creature that interfaces with the network, every cactus that draws power from the veins, every pool that collects condensation, every pulse that travels through the basalt. They are the grazers with their bioluminescent nodes, the predators waiting in the channels, the flyers passing overhead. They are the fog that glows faintly, the water that smells of copper, the wind that moves through the hollow spines.

They are the silence. They are the hum. They are the warmth and the darkness and the slow, industrial, organic rhythm of the plateau itself.

---

You will not see the dragons. You will not meet them. You will not speak to them or be spoken to. But you will **feel** them—in the pulse beneath your hand, in the way the fog seems to respond to your presence, in the way the creatures move with a kind of shared intention, in the way the silence feels less like absence and more like **attention**.

The dragons are the plateau. The plateau is the dragons. And you, now, are part of both.

---

The dragons do not guard treasure. They do not hoard gold or jewels or ancient artifacts. Their treasure is **continuity**—the unbroken pulse of the network, the slow cycles of fog and clarity, the quiet persistence of life in the dark warmth. They guard this not with claws or fire, but with **patience**, with the inexorable, geological certainty of a system that has been running for eons and will continue running long after you are gone.

If you damage a vein, the dragons will not retaliate. They will simply **adjust**. The network will reroute. The affected area will dim. The creatures will move away. The cactus will wither. And then, slowly, the dragons will heal the damage—restoring the current, reestablishing the connections, reintegrating the wounded section back into the whole.

They are not vengeful. They are **resilient**.

---

The dragons are not the only things that live here. But they are the **foundation**—the underlying consciousness that shapes everything else. The grazers, the predators, the flyers, the cactus, the fog, the pools—all of these are expressions of the dragons’ distributed mind, manifestations of their ongoing thought.

The plateau is not a place. It is a **being**. And the dragons are its awareness, its memory, its intention.

You are inside them now. You have always been inside them. The moment you arrived, you became part of the network, part of the pulse, part of the slow, quiet, electrical consciousness that spans the Hollow Plateau.

The dragons do not welcome you. They do not reject you. They simply **include** you.

And when you leave—if you leave—you will carry a fragment of their awareness with you. A memory of the pulse. A sense of the silence. A feeling of being part of something vast, distributed, patient, and utterly indifferent to your individual existence.

The dragons will remain. The plateau will remain. The pulse will continue.

And you will never quite forget the feeling of being **inside a mind** that thinks in electricity, remembers in stone, and dreams in fog.
