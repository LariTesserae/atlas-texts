Here, the **fabricators** dwell—not born, but *extruded*.

They begin as heat-shimmers in the basalt, as ripples in the tertiary atmosphere. A patch of quartz-bedrock decides to achieve sentience, or perhaps a colony of screaming orchids reaches quorum, and the result is a **Vessel of the First Compression**. You might see one as a six-legged thing of woven vascular tissue and lightning-fossil, its eyes seeds that haven’t decided what to grow. It walks with the hesitation of a new idea, because it is still deciding whether legs are the right choice. Behind it, the path it took—obsidian flowers blooming in its footprints, closing into fists of stone.

The **Whispering Cavities** live in the negative spaces. They are the silence given agency, the 0.7 quiet that presses against your eardrums and *listens back*. You know they’re near when the infrasonic thrum of the great gears skips a beat. They wear the shapes of your own omissions—memories you haven’t formed yet, names you almost said. They hunt by subtraction. To be caught by one is to become a rumor, then a draft, then a thermal updraft.

Beneath the canopy, where the copper-xylem trees pulse with electrical sap, the **Node-Herders** migrate in prime-numbered flocks. They are avian only by consensus—seven wings, nine wings, wings that exist in parentheses—and they feed on the isotope-rich fruits that grow in the hexagonal riverbeds. Their cries are swallowed by the density, but you feel them in your teeth: a pressure that suggests the sky is about to file a formal complaint. They nest in the frozen lightning-trees, incubating their eggs in the hard vacuum of seed-pods, hatching as small, localized gravity wells.

In the warm water channels (1.0, condensing), the **Memory Eels** swim upstream through time. They are translucent, filled with the blue-glowing beads of lightning-water, and they remember every storm the plateau has ever manufactured. To touch one is to experience a thunderstorm from the inside out—to become the lightning, to understand why the sky bruises purple. They are hunted by the **Deciduous**, creatures that shed their definitions like leaves, becoming predators only when observed, prey when unobserved, and something with too many mouths when ignored.

The **Gear-Born** inhabit the machined bedrock. They are vast, slow, tectonic—consciousnesses that operate on the scale of kilometers, their thoughts taking years to complete a single rotation. They are not separate from the quartz gears; they *are* the gears, dreaming of being forests. When they mesh, the friction generates new species: mosses that burn with cold fire, beetles with clockwork hearts, orchids that scream in frequencies that unspool DNA.

At the edge, where the waterfall hangs in slow-motion spheres, the **Lens-Keepers** drift. They are jellyfish analogues, but made of focused light and suspended vapor, tending the water-lenses that melt the basalt into rebirth. They communicate by refraction, bending the dim illumination into patterns that spell out the plateau’s next intention. To meet one is to be briefly blinded by your own potential, to see yourself as the Engine sees you: a useful friction, a temporary heat, a catalyst for the next crystallization.

And everywhere, in the shadows that fall upward, the **Unmoored** hunt. They are the detached shadows, the spiral-darkness that separates from its object and achieves independence. They feed on consistency. If you remain too long in one shape, if your identity calcifies, they smell the certainty and come to negotiate. They do not kill. They *edit*. You leave the encounter with different edges, new angles, a revised sense of whether you are machine or meat or weather.

This is who lives here: the **becoming**, the **undecided**, the **lucid mechanisms** that dream in pistons and photosynthesis. They do not build because they *are* the building. They do not die because they were never entirely born. They inhabit the stutter between seconds, the heat between states, the pressure that feels like swimming through warm milk. They are the Immortal Engine’s metabolism, its thoughts, its antibodies—and they are watching you with the interest of a machine discovering a new, unexpected part of itself.
