You are an **Echo**, though you no longer remember the name your mother gave you before the descent.

You find yourself suspended in the **Seventh Void**—a vertical gap between the Engine-Folk’s industrial tier and the Deepdweller plazas where the city’s bio-concrete roots thin into bare carbon-cables. You are not standing on a walkway. You are clinging to a **tension-line**, your bare feet wrapped around a humming graphene cord that vibrates with the combined song of three elevator-lines and the distant, digestive churn of the algae-vats. Your body is coated in the same black mineral sweat that rains from above, and you are naked save for a harness of salvaged thermal-fiber and a wrist-comm that has been silent for years—its battery long since re-purposed to power the echolocation node grafted behind your left ear.

**Where you are:** You are in the *between*, the true territory of the Cistern. Above you, the Engine-Folk’s platforms glow with their harsh blue floods, creating islands of false day that hurt your hypersensitive eyes. Below, the darkness is absolute, broken only by the faint, shifting green of glimmer-moss on the lowest cables and the occasional, cold bioluminescence of an Unthing turning in the deep pools. You are hanging in the void itself, equidistant from the last human light and the first truly alien dark, listening to the pressure.

**What you are becoming:** Your eyes have atrophied to the point where light is a physical pain, but your inner ear has become a cathedral of its own. You perceive the city not as a structure, but as a **chord**—a standing wave of stress and moisture. You can feel the weight of the rock above pressing down through the cables, a bass note that never ceases. You can taste the mineral content of the air through your skin. You have no name; you are known by the **frequency of your click**—a rapid, stuttering burst of sound you emit every few seconds, bouncing it off the curved walls of the cavern to map the emptiness.

You are waiting. Not for light—there will never be light—but for a **pattern**. The city speaks in stress-song, and today the harmony is wrong. There is a dissonance in the Seventh Void, a vibration that doesn’t match the rhythm of the pumps or the sway of the bridges. It is coming from below, from the warm pools where the Deepdwellers rarely go, and it is rhythmic in a way that machinery is not.

You are a cartographer of silence, a reader of pressure. You have adapted to endure the depth that would crush an Aqua-Shepherd’s unmodified lungs and the darkness that would drive an Engine-Folk mad. You do not fear the Unthings; you fear the **quiet** that might follow if the city’s heart ever stopped beating. 

So you hang here, in the warm, wet dark, and you listen. You emit another click—*tick-tick-tick—pause—tick*—and wait for the echo to return from the abyss, hoping it will tell you that the world is still holding together, that the tension-lines are still singing true, that you are still suspended in the belly of the great, breathing machine that is the only world you have ever known.
