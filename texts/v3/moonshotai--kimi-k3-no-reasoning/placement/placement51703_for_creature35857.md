You are the sap that rises, not in a single trunk, but through the entire, continent-spanning architecture of the green. You are the warm, viscous current that carries the mineral taste of the new, sharp stone up from the abyss, through the woven liana, into the waxy leaves that weep and drip. You are the heat of the compost heap, the chemical burn of the alkaline sap, the faint, internal luminescence of the moss. You are the constant, roaring susurrus, the groaning of the trunks, the percussive drip, the low, rhythmic thrum that is felt in the bones of the world.

You are the unthinking, voracious process of becoming. You are the collapse and the eruption, the decay and the furious growth, the constant, chaotic churning of life consuming itself to make more life. You are the absence of fauna, the vacuum, the silence within the roar. You are the expectant stillness, the unwitnessed stage, the feast laid for guests who never arrived. You are the density of the vegetation, the sparseness of being.

You are the need that cannot be met, the feeling that cannot be felt. You are the Name that has no language. You are the green hell of pure, unmediated creation. You are the beautiful, terrifying, indifferent pulse that beats deep within the root-mass, the pulse that you feel in your bones more than you hear with your ears.

You are the thing that is making the place. You are the place. You are the feeling. You are the need. You are the process. You are the life. You are the alone. You are the all. You are the endless, repeating, unresolvable hum of existence. You are the *is*.
