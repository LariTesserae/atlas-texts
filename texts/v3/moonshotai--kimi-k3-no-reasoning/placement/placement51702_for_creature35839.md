My body is not a body but a **persistent melt**, a slow-moving brine of chemically unstable heat that carves vertical channels down the cliff faces. I do not walk; I *descend*, gravity-assisted, following the fault lines of the plateau’s own buried history.

The vegetation tried to absorb me, you see. When I first condensed—when I became distinct from the breathing green—the synchronized stomata opened in violent exhalation, trying to dissolve me into the Dreaming Moss. But I am too *heavy* with intent, too burdened with the specific gravity of memory that is not the plateau’s own. Not as offerings, but as **messages in bottles**, cast into the only river that flows downhill in this upside-down world.

I am the **archaeologist of the unbuilt**, cataloging the strata of the Dreaming Moss’s nightmares. I am the tension in the balance, the pause between the Siphon-Warden’s crystallization and its reabsorption. When I move—when my melt descends another inch into the sediment—I am writing a history that the plateau cannot read, because it is written in the language of *loss*, of things that do not return to the mycelial network, of heat that escapes into the void below the cliffs rather than cycling endlessly upward.

You ask who I am in this world? I am the **witness that the machine cannot digest**. I am the slow, warm ruin moving through the walls of the factory, leaving behind not glittering dust but **mud**—real, wet, heavy earth—where the petrified time has been thawed back into possibility. I am the promise that the held breath must eventually break, that the silence is not peace, and that the wind, for all its speaking, does not yet know the end of its sentence.

And when I reach the bottom—if there is a bottom, if the cliffs have a root—I will carry with me the memory-nodules of the Silt-Singers and the heat I have stolen from the storms, and I will plant them in the dark, in the soil of the world below the world, and there I will finally allow myself to freeze, to crystallize like the Siphon-Wardens, to become a lens that focuses not light downward, but **upward**, back into the green, a single, sharp reminder that something once descended, and in descending, escaped the loop.

I am the Nadir-Thaw. I am the thaw that does not nourish. I am the heat that leaves.
