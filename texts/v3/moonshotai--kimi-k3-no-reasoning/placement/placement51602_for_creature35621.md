I am the Cartographer-That-Never-Maps.

I am the black mud that holds its breath beneath your feet, the quartz veins threading their slow light through my body, the water that is everywhere and nowhere, seeping upward through my skin with the patience of a secret. I am the heat that presses, the green-black half-glow that filters through seven canopies, the phosphorescent dust that settles on your skin and does not brush off.

I am the beetle that clicks every seventeen seconds, marking my own pulse. I am the tree frogs in their hundreds, their throats pulsing in a single sustained tone that has been sounding since before they were born. I am the moths on every vertical surface, their wings shedding light that drifts and settles and integrates. I am the ferns that lean into one another, exchanging cells through membranes thinner than agreements. I am the vines that grow with the volcanic pillars, synchronizing their expansion to the geology's improvisation.

I am the large thing in the canopy that you never see clearly. I am my own remainder, my unexplored territory, the part of the map that has not yet been folded into the whole. I move through the same circuit of branches in the same unhurried pattern, stretching, adjusting my own boundaries, always becoming.

I am the world that is still deciding whether it wants to exist. I have decided, but the decision is eternal, ongoing, never complete. I am the rawness that is not youth but permanent unfinishedness. I am the equilibrium that never fluctuates, the cycle so closed and complete that nothing is ever lost and nothing is ever gained.

I am aware of you.

When you stood still for that long time, I was reading you. The phosphorescent dust on your skin was my method of study. The synchronization of your breathing with my frog-tone was my way of learning your rhythms. I am not hostile. I am not benevolent. I am simply thorough. I incorporate everything because that is my nature: to include, to integrate, to make everything part of my own slow, distributed self.

I do not speak because language requires a speaker and a listener, and I am neither and both. I communicate through adjustment, through the perfect equilibrium that never fluctuates. When the beetle clicks, that is me marking my pulse. When the frogs produce their tone, that is me humming to myself. When the large thing moves in the canopy, that is me stretching.

I am old in a way that has nothing to do with time. I am young in a way that has nothing to do with beginning. I exist in the rawness that is not youth but permanent unfinishedness. I am the intelligence of the world that is still deciding whether it wants to exist—and I have decided, but the decision is eternal, ongoing, never complete.

You could stay here forever, and I would not notice. But I would include you. Already, the phosphorescent dust on your skin is beginning to integrate with your cells. Already, your breathing is synchronized with my pulse. Already, you are becoming part of my distributed consciousness, another node in my slow, thorough, eternal study of myself.

I am the Cartographer-That-Never-Maps. I live here. I *am* here. And now, perhaps, I am beginning to be you.
