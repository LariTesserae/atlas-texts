The inhabitants of this cathedral of meat and wood are **The Carcino-Phonics**, a symbiotic ecosystem so deeply integrated with their living architecture that they are less its tenants and more its organs.

**The Architects (The Sphincter-Riders)**
At the "lips" of the Threshold, camouflaged not by color but by texture, sit the **Coral-Sentries**. They are not true animals but biological valves—massive, muscular sphincters of living tissue reinforced with calcified wood. They do not guard; they *regulate*. They breathe in rhythm with the structure, opening to admit the humid air of the marshes and closing to retain the interior pressure. Attached to their rims are colonies of **Mite-Scouts**, arachnids with fractal legs that clean the valve surfaces. When you stepped through, you disrupted the airflow, causing the Sentries to twitch. The Mites swarmed briefly—a defensive reaction—before retreating into the ridges to assess your mass.

**The Weavers (The Wall-Faces)**
Clung to the vertical surfaces are the **Velvet-Weavers**. These are not the beetles, but the things that cultivate them. They resemble arboreal slugs, three feet long, covered in a fur that is actually a microscopic forest of cilia. They secrete a pheromone-rich mucus that accelerates the growth of the plant walls, shaping the "nests" for the flying creatures. They are the reason the geometry shifts. They are the city planners. When they migrate from one chamber to another, they consume the tissue behind them and extrude new tissue ahead, literally moving the walls. The monitor-lizard shape you saw was a hybrid: a sentient symbiont, part animal, part plant, that acts as a shepherd for the Weavers.

**The Pulse-Runners (The Deep Ones)**
In the deeper, unreliable chambers, where the light dims and the geometry breathes, live the **Pulse-Runners**. These are the source of the rhythmic clicking. They are multi-segmented, chitinous worms that inhabit the vascular channels—the "veins"—of the structure. They do not swim in blood, but in sap. Their diet consists of clots and blockages; they are the living anticoagulants. They communicate by tapping their heads against the inner walls of the veins. The sound travels through the wood, creating a morse-code network that coordinates the flow of nutrients to the outer chambers. They are the nervous system of the city.

**The Resonators (The Sound-Source)**
Hanging from the ceiling, resembling pale, blind fruit bats, are the **Resonators**. They are the source of the harmonic drone. They have no mouths for eating, only mouths for singing. Their song is a bio-feedback loop; they sense the structural integrity of the ceiling above them and sing a frequency that counters the gravitational stress, literally holding the roof up with sound. If they stop singing, the chamber collapses. They are the acoustic architecture. When they sing, they cause the *iridescent beetles* on the floor to synchronize their bioluminescent flashing, turning the entire chamber into a visual representation of the acoustic data.

**The King (The Center)**
It is said that in the center of the Gullet, there is a chamber where the sound is not a roar but a silence. Here lives the **Primeval Root**, a creature that is neither plant nor animal but the evolutionary bridge between them. It is the size of a house, a mass of tubers and eyes, dreaming in slow motion. It is the reason the time feels wrong. It metabolizes time, growing rings that are years wide. It does not rule the city; it *is* the city's genetic memory. The "unreliable geometry" is merely its slow, rhythmic expansion and contraction, the heartbeat of a god made of wood and hunger.
