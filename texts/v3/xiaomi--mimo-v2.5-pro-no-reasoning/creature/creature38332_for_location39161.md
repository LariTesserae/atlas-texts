The Respiration Plains breathe with life, though what lives there defies easy categorization. It is not a place of distinct, separate beings, but a symphony of interdependence, where creature, plant, and substrate blur into a single, continent-sized metabolism.

## The First Inhabitants: The Mat Itself
Most fundamental is the **Mat**. It is the land, the water table, the air conditioner, and the foundation of all life. It is not a plant, not a fungus, not an animal, but a primordial hybrid – a **clonal hyper-organism**. Its consciousness, if it can be called that, is a slow, geologic pulse of growth, repair, and exhalation. Its "thoughts" are patterns of sap-flow, its "decisions" are the orientation of growth ridges and the raising of towers. It is the ultimate inhabitant, a silent, pervasive presence whose rhythms set the tempo for all other life. Its wounds heal over decades; its breath is the weather.

## The Hidden Multitudes: The Infauna
Beneath the surface crust teems the **Infauna**, a secret civilization of billions:
*   **The Grazers:** Legions of eyeless, soft-bodied creatures, like immense, segmented grubs or burrowing mollusks without shells. They chew the dead, lower layers of the mat, recycling its bulk. Their tunnels are the mat's circulatory and nervous system in miniature.
*   **The Fluid-Siphs:** Sleek, arthropodal creatures, more crustacean than insect, that navigate the dense, pressurized vascular channels. They tap the mat's lifeblood, their bodies adapted to withstand the osmotic pressure.
*   **The Resonators:** Vibration-sensitive predators and scavengers that hunt by sound transmitted through the mat's fibers. Some are silent, ambushing grazers by their chewing vibrations. Others are themselves singers, emitting clicks and pulses to echolocate or disorient prey.
*   **The Deep Ones:** The great, slow things in the ancient, compressed chambers. Not monsters, but **leviathans of cartilage and resonant flesh**, their bodies shaped by millennia of life in darkness and pressure. They move like living seismic waves, their passage the source of the deep tremors felt on the surface. Their song is a subsonic dirge that harmonizes with the plate's own hum.

## The Visible Swarms: The Epifauna
Above the crust, the **Epifauna** takes to the hot, humid air:
*   **The Droning Skimmers:** The giant, furry insects. Their wings beat with a heavy, resonant *thrum-thrum-thrum* that vibrates in the chest. They are **mat-parasites**, using needle-like proboscises to pierce the crust. They are not mere pests; their punctures are controlled by the Mat's own defenses, and they serve as pollinators for the tower-clusters.
*   **The Cloud-Motes:** Microscopic and near-microscopic flyers that form the dense, textured swarms. They are the clean-up crew, the decomposers, the food for everything else. In certain lights, their swarms look like particulate matter, like smoke without fire.
*   **The Tube-Weavers:** Larger, quieter creatures that build intricate webs of mat-fiber between the towers, filtering the air for the cloud-motes and catching drifting spores. They are architects of intricate, fragile geometry.

## The Conductors: The Towers
The **Towers** are not inhabitants themselves, but grown organs of the Mat. Yet, they host and are shaped by life. Their hollow cores are **wind-organs**, producing the deep, chest-humming notes that resonate across the plains. Their surfaces are covered in photosynthetic blooms and are the primary perching and nesting sites for the epifauna. They are the Mat's lungs and voice-box, singing the eternal chord of the plains.

## The Shapers: The Human Tenders
Finally, the **People**, few and far between. They are not masters of this land, but **conduits and conductors**. They live in a state of negotiated symbiosis:
*   **Their Settlements** are scars that heal into ornaments, shelters that slowly rejoin the body of the world. Their walls breathe. Their floors are warm.
*   **Their Tools** are of the mat and the dead fauna—stone, bone, chitin, cured and shaped tissue. They take only what the system can spare.
*   **Their Song** is their true tool, their **ecological language**. They do not command, but suggest. A sustained note calms a swarming insect tide. A rhythmic chant encourages burrowing grazers to surface for inspection. A complex melody can guide the flow of moisture in a parched section. Their oral history is a living map of the Mat's own memory. To sing a wrong note is to trigger a cascade, a riot in the substrate. They are humble, lean, and patient, living by the Mat's 31-hour metabolic tide, not the sun.

## The Collective Breath
What lives here, ultimately, is a **single, breathing, singing entity**. The Mat is the body. The Infauna and Epifauna are its blood cells, its gut flora, its nervous impulses. The Towers are its sensory organs. The People are its memories and its voice, giving direction to the song that the whole plateau hums. To stand there is to stand inside a living thing that has devoured stone and turned it into breath, and to be one more vibration in its endless, deafening, vital sigh.
