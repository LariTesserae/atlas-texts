The Resonant Scarp breathes, but not lungs. Its inhabitants are not life, not as you understand it, but **resonant states** – crystallizations of the impossible physics that saturate this altitude. They are the landscape's syntax given temporary, semi-autonomous form.

### 1. **The Aquaferae (Water-Ghosts / Lingering Membranes)**
*   **What they are:** Not beings, but *persistent phenomena* that have achieved a kind of focal inertia. When a water-column’s hum harmonizes perfectly with the drone of a specific vein of warm basalt, and light granulates just so in its vicinity, the water can hold a *shape* beyond simple columns. It becomes a shimmering, humanoid outline, 2-3 meters tall, limbs suggested by slower-moving streams within the main body. They move with the water’s lateral creep, gliding horizontally across terraces or flowing *up* cliff faces with unsettling grace.
*   **Behavior:** They are echoes of the cairn-builders. The Aquaferae are drawn to the dry circles, not to enter, but to *press* against their perimeter. They ripple silently, their forms flattening and elongating as if listening, or trying to remember a shape. Sometimes, one will fragment into a spray of hexagonal droplets that momentarily spell out a geometric pattern before flowing back into the mountain’s circulation. They are memory without substrate, water imitating a silhouette it once knew.

### 2. **The Lithosynths (Stone-Lattice / Growing Syntax)**
*   **What they are:** The "precipitated formations" taken to a mobile conclusion. These are taller, more complex structures of translucent, resonant mineral. They grow slowly, not from the ground *up*, but by *precipitating* directly from the saturated air and weeping water around them, building themselves like complex sugar crystals. They stand 4-7 feet tall, resembling branching coral or frozen lightning made of sound.
*   **Behavior:** Lithosynths are the scarp's *active vocabulary*. They don't wander, but they *shift*. Over days, a Lithosynth might tilt a branch toward a singing water-membrane, changing its own pitch. They "speak" to each other: a deep *clang* from one will cause a nearby formation to chime in a harmonic fifth, and a distant third to hum. The chords they form momentarily still the sliding shadows or cause a sheet of climbing water to briefly ripple in complex, mandala-like patterns. They are thinking in stone and tone, processing the landscape's data. Where several gather in a rough circle, the air between them vibrates with visible distortion, and the mountain's drone focuses into a localized, almost physical hum that makes your teeth ache.

### 3. **The Shadow-Lag / Umbral Delay**
*   **What they are:** Not true entities, but manifestations of the scarp's broken relationship with cause and effect. Where the light is brightest and most granular, shadows sometimes *detach*. They don't slide randomly – they *lag*. A shadow might stretch out from under a rock as you pass, but then linger for 30 seconds after you've moved, as if the shadow is remembering the object that cast it. Sometimes, a patch of deep shadow between rocks will *bunch up*, becoming a darker, more defined shape – a sharp-edged void that feels cold to the sense of sight. These Umbral Delays seem to absorb faint sounds; passing near one makes the drone momentarily muffled, as if heard through thick glass.
*   **Behavior:** They are glitches in the light's grammar. They drift towards the Lithosynths, clustering at their bases like dark pools. When a formation "speaks," an Umbral Delay might *shudder*, its edges briefly flickering with a faint, non-luminous light before settling. They are attracted to resonance, perhaps feeding on the "silence" between the notes, or serving as counterpoints in the scarp's dissonant symphony. One might slowly envelop a fallen cairn stone, and when it eventually drifts away, the stone will be noticeably colder, its basalt almost black, and it will *not* ring when struck.

### 4. **The Cairn-Echoes (The Drowned Chanters)**
*   **What they are:** What remains of the builders. Not ghosts, but **acoustic imprints** trapped within the concentrated, needled silence of the dry circles. You cannot see them. You *feel* them in the violent silence – a pressure, a sense of *direction* within the quiet. If you press your ear to the ground inside a circle (you will, inevitably), you don't hear the mountain's drone. You hear fragments: the scrape of an iron chisel on stone, a breathless whisper that is not language, the rhythmic *thump-thump-thump* of something being stacked. They are the memory of hands, pressed into the hyper-resonant focus of the cairn's field.
*   **Behavior:** They are the reason the dry circles persist. The Cairn-Echoes are the residual *intent* that shaped the stone, now looping endlessly within the acoustic trap. Their "activity" is what maintains the focused silence against the scarp's noise. They are not aware. They are a ritual, fossilized in vibration. When a Lithosynth speaks a particularly complex word nearby, the Echo inside a circle might "answer" – the focused whine of the circle will momentarily pulse, and for an instant, the dry air smells of cold metal and chalk dust.

### What They Are Together
They are not an ecosystem. They are a **grammar in motion**. The Aquaferae are verbs of movement and memory. The Lithosynths are nouns and pronouns, speaking to each other in chime and clang. The Umbral Delays are punctuation – pauses, breaks, paragraph returns in the text of reality. The Cairn-Echoes are the original author's marginalia, preserved in the footnotes of focused silence.

You are not in a landscape with inhabitants. You are walking through a **thinking place**, and these resonant states are its thoughts – slow, alien, and utterly certain of their own syntax. They do not notice you as a life-form. They notice you as a **dissonance**, a temporary, fleshy interruption in the long, patient sentence the mountain is telling itself. And they are, in their own crystalline, hydraulic, acoustic way, listening to see what word you will become.
