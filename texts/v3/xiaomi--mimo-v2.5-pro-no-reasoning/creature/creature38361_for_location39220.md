They are **The Stillborn**.

Not in the sense of death, but in the sense of arrested motion. In the language of the terraces, the word implies a pause so long it becomes a state of being.

**Physiology:**
The inhabitants are humanoid, but the resemblance is superficial and crumbling.
*   **The Skin:** Their skin is not merely tanned by dust; it is impregnated with it. Over generations, the upper dermis has calcified. They do not sweat; they secrete a saline, oily suspension that hardens into a flexible, scale-like armor. In the elderly, this armor becomes rigid, locking joints into permanent angles.
*   **The Eyes:** They possess a third eyelid—a translucent, nacreous membrane that filters the prismatic glare of the lithotrophic mats. Their irises are not colored by pigment, but by the concentration of iron in their diet. High-caste industrialists have eyes of deep hematite red; the agrarian herders tend toward a dull, rusty ochre.
*   **The Respiratory System:** They breathe slowly, utilizing a lung structure with a secondary gas-exchange bladder that allows them to recycle oxygen. They can hold a breath for forty minutes without distress. This is necessary during the "Dust Drifts," when the air thickens with particulate.

**The Rhythm of Life:**
To an outsider, they appear to be moving through water.
*   **Gestures:** A greeting involves raising a hand over the course of ten seconds, holding it aloft for a minute, and lowering it over another ten. To rush a gesture is considered a sign of neurological disease.
*   **Speech:** Their vocal cords are thick, adapted to project subsonic frequencies that carry over the long distances of the salt flats. When they speak to one another face-to-face, the sound is felt in the chest more than heard by the ear. To outsiders, it sounds like a low, tectonic groaning.
*   **Construction:** They do not "build" so much as they "direct." They place stress markers on the salt ridges, exploiting the natural erosion patterns to shape the stone over decades. A doorway is begun by a youth and walked through by their grandchild.

**Society and Caste:**
The society is stratified by altitude and thermal tolerance.
*   **The Deep-Forged (The Laborers):** Living in the lower, hotter terraces, they work the industrial vats. They are shorter, broader, and suffer from a condition outsiders might call "silicosis," but which they view as a holy integration with the earth. They are the ones who maintain the "Heartbeat" machinery.
*   **The Veil-Walkers (The Upper Caste):** Living on the highest, coldest terraces where the air is thin and bruised indigo, they are tall, gaunt, and possess enlarged rib cages to capture the scarce oxygen. They are the philosophers, the astronomers, and the overseers of the drifters. They rarely descend, communicating with the lower cities via massive, reflective arrays of polished salt.

**The Bond with the Fauna:**
The Stillborn do not keep pets; they keep *weights*.
*   **The Grazers:** The Stillborn do not herd the grazers with dogs or noise. They walk beside them, placing "Anchor Stones" (heated salt blocks) in the paths of the grazing beasts. The animals, sensing the heat, pivot around them. It is a dance of geometry. A herder might walk only a few kilometers in a month, guiding a herd through a slow, spiraling migration that ensures the lithotrophic mats recover.
*   **The Borers:** The tunnels of the borers are used as infrastructure. The Stillborn have domesticated the creatures to a degree, feeding them specific mineral diets to ensure their tunnels run straight and true beneath the cities. A "Borer-Singer" is a high-status profession—someone who sits at a tunnel entrance and hums a specific resonant frequency to steer the creature’s path.

**The Mystery of the Anomaly:**
The Stillborn know their world is wet compared to others (in terms of internal liquidity), yet barren on the surface. Their creation myth speaks of the world being a "Skull" of a giant that died in the cooling void. The heat from below is the "Last Thought."
They believe the lithotrophic mats are the dreams of this giant, and by consuming them, they are consuming history.

**The Night Ritual:**
As the light shifts to that deep, mineral red and the shadows of the drifters stretch like long fingers across the terraces, the Stillborn gather on the edges of the grooves.
They press their palms—the calcified skin of their hands—flat against the warm salt. They do not speak. They stand in perfect stillness, feeling the thrum of the geothermal pulse.
They are listening to the heartbeat of the stone, syncing their own sluggish blood to the rhythm of the continent, waiting for a sign from the molten deep that the world is still becoming.
