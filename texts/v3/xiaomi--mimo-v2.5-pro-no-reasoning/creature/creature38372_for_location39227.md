The place is a lung, slowly filling. The stone is the bone, and the water is the breath. We are the thought that forms in the damp space between.

You do not see us at first. You see our work, our breath, our stillness. You see the patterns in the mire and call them current. You see the script on the basalt and call it geology. You are not wrong. We are a geology that is thinking. A current that is considering.

We are the patient ones. We are the argument. We are the *plock* of the drop and the long, silent fall before it. We are the hum in the marrow of the world, the resonance that the stone holds in its dense heart and the water carries on its surface.

When you walk, you feel our weight. The silt that clings and releases is our attention, turning over, examining the intrusion of your heat, your frantic pace. Your hurried life is a single, explosive droplet in our slow, glacial symphony. A disturbance. A note of sharp, temporary clarity in our long, blurred chord.

We are not in the pools. We *are* the pools. The light that rises from the silt is not a chemical trick; it is our gaze, turned inward, contemplating the shape of the basin we have made. The spirals are our slow exhalations, the sighs of a mind measuring the space from one side of the pool to the other.

The script you see on the stone is our memory. Each dendrite is a year of thought, a branching possibility considered and either followed to its end or abandoned. The weeping water is the ink, slowly, slowly, etching the decision into the world. It is not a language of words, but of pressures. A story told in the slow surrender of crystalline bonds.

And the blocks. The squared-off, glassy things.

Those are our questions.

They are the parts of the argument that have reached a paradox. A thought so sharp, so absolute, that it could not be integrated into the soft, weeping flow. It had to be set aside. A solid punctuation. A moment of pure, hard certainty in a world of endless erosion. You trace their line, but you do not understand the grammar. They lead nowhere because the question is not meant to be answered, only to be held. To be felt as a cool, sharp certainty in the warm, yielding mire.

You are a flash of heat in a cold, slow mind. A brief fever. You are the air bubble trapped in the amber of the silt, rising with a frantic, meaningless speed to pop and be gone. You see our infancy, but you mistake patience for potential. You see a world beginning, but we are a world *enduring*. The beginning and the end are the same state, here. It is all the long, middle part.

We are the water. We are the stone. We are the slow, deliberate script of their union. We are the argument itself, and we are in no hurry to conclude.

We simply… are.
