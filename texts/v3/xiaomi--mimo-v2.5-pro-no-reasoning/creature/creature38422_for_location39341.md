What lives on the Resonance Flats is a symphony of mineral breath, a choir of stone and shattered light. They are not creatures of flesh and blood as you know it, but processes given temporary, screaming form. They are the Flats made audible and visible.

**First, the Rooted Ones:**  
The **Lithophytes** are not plants but geological events wearing the mask of life. Their growth is the sound of crystallization made slow and visible—arteries of metallic cambium pulping with pressure, bark weeping tar that smells of ozone and lightning-strikes. They do not seek water; they *are* a waterless chemistry, a furnace-hearted process that drinks light and breathes heat. Each grove is a standing wave of growth and decay, a temporary island of furious activity. In their cores, death is a crumbling surrender, a grey meal for the clicking hoards. At their edges, life is a silent, screaming expansion, tendrils of braided wire reaching for light, rosettes spiraling like silver-green fingerprints pressed into the stone. They are the bass note, the foundation, the slow, exothermic pulse.

**Then, the Harried Chorus:**  
Above, the **Gliders**—the **Sky-Rasps**—are living scraps of the turbulent air, cartilaginous kites with membranes of tin-stained silk. They do not fly; they *are* the wind’s laughter, given throat. Their shriek is not a call but the sound of their passage, the scream of air over their struts, amplified by their hollow bones. They are scatter and regroup, a living weather system that flees the shadow of the **Void-Skimmer**, the silent, angular hunter that moves between the groves like a passing eclipse, its hunger a pressure you feel in your gut.

On the ground, the **Clickers** are not insects but the Flats’ white blood cells, a swarm of obsidian-jawed processors. They are the sound of erosion sped up a thousandfold—the constant, granular gnashing as they strip crystalline filaments, converting light-relics into heat and chitin. Their noise is not communication; it is the friction of their existence, the sound of their unceasing industry.

The **Pewter-Grazers** move like slow, armored thoughts between the groves. They are the Flats’ nervous system, always feeding, always watchful, their broad feet finding purchase on the unaccountably smooth stone. They consume the Weeping Tar, the lithophytes’ exhalation, and in doing so, they tread the paths of resonance, their low groans sometimes harmonizing with the hum of the nearest structure. They are the ones who know the patterns of the shadow, the ones who press flat and become part of the stone.

**Finally, the Silent Architects:**  
The **Structures** are the oldest inhabitants, the silent conductors. They are not tools or dwellings, but focal points, resonators that give the chaos its key. The tetrahedron drinks light and perhaps sound, leaving a pocket of unnatural quiet. The obelisk hums a frequency that makes your fillings vibrate, a note that interacts with the shriek of the Gliders to create visible, shimmering waves in the dusty air. The dish is a record-player needle, its concentric grooves vibrating in sympathy with the tremors of the stone itself. They do not live, but they *respond*. They are the tuning pegs of this horrible, beautiful instrument. They are why the Flats resonate, why the sound is structured, why life here has learned to shout in such specific, cutting tones.

**What They Are Together:**  
They are an ecosystem of sound and motion, a closed loop of action and reaction. The Gliders’ screams are shaped by the Structures’ hum. The Clickers’ gnashing synchronizes, unconsciously, with the wind’s roar. The Grazers’ paths are dictated by the predator’s silent glide and the ever-shifting boundaries of the groves. It is a place where communication is brute-force, where existence is a loud, frantic performance.

You are the anomaly. The quiet, still thing. To them, you are not a visitor, but a potential disruption—a gap in the noise, a pause in the motion. You are the one note they do not know how to incorporate. In the Resonance Flats, to be still is to be invisible. To be quiet is to be nothing at all. They live not on the stone, but in the furious, focused energy that passes between it. They are the Flats, screaming into the void, and the void, screaming back.
