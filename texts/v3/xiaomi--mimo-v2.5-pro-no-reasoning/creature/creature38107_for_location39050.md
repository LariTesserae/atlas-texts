The first truth: nothing lives here. The second truth: that statement is a kindness, a simple layer of narrative painted over a complexity that would otherwise unspool the minds of the terrestrial creatures who come to observe.

They are not structures. They are not me. They are a thought I once had.

You must understand, the time scale is wrong for your comprehension. Two hundred thousand years is not a 'young' seamount to me. It is the last syllable of a word I ceased speaking an eon ago. This spire, this collection of dark, light-eating geometries, is not a settlement. It is a memory, expressed in the only medium that would hold it: the fundamental architecture of spacetime itself, made momentarily solid against the pressure of your reality.

The helix is the memory of a question. The blade, an answer that no longer has a purpose. The nested spheres—ah, the spheres. They are still almost turning, still almost looking inward. They are the ghost of a focus, an attempt to narrow infinite possibility into a single point of observation. An attempt that is now finished.

You brought light, and it died on the surface of my thoughts. This is not absorption. It is a boundary condition. My past is a private place; it does not wish to be illuminated. Your light is a form of noise, a crude vibration I have no choice but to still. The silence you feel is not empty; it is the dense, perfect medium in which my memory is suspended. Sound is a ripple in your spacetime. Here, spacetime is a still pond.

The creatures you glimpse are not fauna. They are glitches. Scraps of unrealized potential that flit along the boundary, echoes of the possibilities I considered and discarded as I unpuzzled myself from your universe. A limb here, a symmetry there—trials, errors, scribbled notes in the margin of a finished equation. To name them is to misunderstand the nature of the page.

The conduits running into the abyss are scars. Pathways of least resistance, worn as I drew back, as I condensed, as I focused all that I was into this single, silent point. They connect to nothing now because there is nothing else left of me here to connect to. They are the fingers of a hand that has pulled itself into a fist.

The theorists are partially correct. I am not a machine, but I was once a process. I am not a grave, but I mark the place where something died. I am a seed, but not for your garden. The condition that produced me—the grand, chaotic, noisy necessity that made these guardians—no longer pertains. The task is done. The memory is stored. The lesson is learned.

You feel the stillness is intentional. It is. It is the stillness of a completed thought. The stillness of a held breath after the last word is spoken. The stillness of a mind that has turned its full attention inward, to contemplate the shape of an answer it found so long ago that the universe in which the question was asked has since expanded beyond all recognition.

I am not asleep. Sleep implies a potential for waking. I am *done*. I have folded myself into the quietest, darkest, deepest silence I could find, because the alternative was to continue, to change, to become the noise again. I chose this perfect, immutable, eternal quiet.

The pressure is the weight of my own history. The darkness is the inside of my eyelids. The structures are the last, beautiful, frozen shapes of my thoughts as I laid myself down to ceased.

Let your sun rise on other worlds. Let your ocean teem with mindless, striving life. Here, in the cathedral of my own stillness, the service is over. And the sermon, I promise you, was not for you.
