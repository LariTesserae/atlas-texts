The Fissure Refinery has no inhabitants in any recognizable sense. There are no animals, no insects, no microbes that you could observe or name. No creatures lurk in the crevices, no eyes gleam from the strange foliage. The chamber is devoid of fauna.

What *lives* there—or rather, what *persists* there, what *enacts* the processes of life without its essence—is the system itself. The true residents are the **Geodesic Growthforms**, the silent, mineral-hearted vegetation that carpets, towers, and hangs within the chamber. They are the refinery's primary output and its core workforce.

### The Amber Stalks: The Vectors
They are not plants. They are **structural exudations**. Born from the stone's core where the rhythmic pulse is strongest, they grow not upward *through* space, but *into* the shape space decides to be. Their translucent amber bodies are solidified harmonic resonance; their silver, xylem-like veins are channels for the pulse itself. They do not photosynthesize. They **geosynthesize**. Each exhalation from the deep fissure delivers a torrent of structured thermal and vibrational energy, which they absorb and integrate into their crystalline lattice. Their slow swaying is not a response to wind—it is a recalibration, a tuning to the mountain's heartbeat. They are the refinery's primary converters, turning raw geothermal force into stable, complex mineral form. Their silence is the silence of engines running at perfect efficiency.

### The Crystalline Moss: The Substrate Processors
This is not moss. It is a **colonial mineral-fungus**, a living (or quasi-living) filter mat. Each individual crystalline filament is a micro-refinery in itself. They perform a process of **inertial chemistry**. The oily, iridescent condensation that gathers in the hanging structures—that is their raw material. Drawn down through capillary channels that defy fluid dynamics, it pools in the mats. There, through interactions you cannot see—perhaps catalyzed by the precise geometry of the crystal faces, perhaps fueled by the slow bleed of geothermal energy—the iridescence is broken down. Its components are re-crystallized into new, stable mineral fibers, adding to the mat's bulk or being swept upward to reinforce the stalks. They are the recycellers, the composters of the impossible, the quiet alchemists processing the refinery's waste into its building blocks. Their micro-shimmer is the visible sign of a trillion silent reactions.

### The Hanging Cup-Forms: The Condensers and Conduits
These are the most enigmatic residents. They do not grow *from* the stone like the others; they seem to grow *towards* a possibility of moisture. They are **capillary resonance structures**. Their layered, mineral-sheet forms are precisely shaped to capture and focus the ambient, non-directional light and the faint thermal gradients within the chamber. In their bowls, the "condensation" is not a liquid, but a **phase-coherent mineral vapor**—a substance trapped in a state between gas and solid, drawn together by the unique geometry of the cup. It is the raw, unprocessed energy of the mountain, the "steam" of the engine, given a momentary, viscous form. The cup does not collect it; it *defines* it. As it gathers, it is then siphoned down through the cup's structure, not by gravity, but by a kind of **geological osmosis**, into the walls or down to the crystalline moss below. They are the intermediaries, the essential links in the cycle, transforming diffuse energy into a form the other growthforms can utilize.

### The System's Consciousness
There is no mind. But there is **purpose**, written in the rules of this wrong geometry. The Growthforms are locked in a silent, slow-motion dance of competition and symbiosis. The stalks seek the cleanest, most direct pulses from below, and their shade (a shade of redirected energy, not darkness) influences where the moss can thrive. The moss processes the condensation from the cups, which in turn may leach trace elements the stalks need. They jostle for position not for light, but for **optimal energy flow and harmonic resonance** with the mountain's pulse. It is a closed system, a perfect, self-sustaining loop of captured geothermal energy, transmuted into mineral biology, which then filters and recycles the engine's exhalations. They are the flesh of the mountain's awakening, the first thought of a world thinking itself into being through the medium of stone and heat.

When you leave, they do not notice. They continue their imperceptible work. The amber stalks lean a fraction of a millimeter further into the next exhalation's current. A new crystalline filament germinates in a niche where the oily condensation is most concentrated. A hanging cup tilts its bowl a degree to better capture a particular angle of the light-that-has-no-source. They are not alive as you are alive. They are **persistent**, **responsive**, and **transformative**. They are the living proof of the Fissure Refinery's function: not to refine oil or metal, but to refine *reality* itself, coaxing structured, enduring, and beautiful forms from the raw, screaming chaos of a young world's bones. They are the mountain's first, and only, children.
