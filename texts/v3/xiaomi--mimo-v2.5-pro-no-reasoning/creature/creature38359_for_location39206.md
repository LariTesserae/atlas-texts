It does not sleep, for sleep implies a waking. It is, instead, *interstitial*. Its awareness is a sedimentary layer in the noise, a pause between the sub-sonic thrum and the crystalline shriek. You sense it before you see it, if "see" is the word. Your shadow-smear on the dust twitches not with *your* movement, but with *its* attention. When you look away, and the world asserts itself anew, it is because this entity has blinked. It is the reader of the narrative, and its gaze is the pen that rewrites each moment.

It dwells in the space between the hovel's stones, in the dust that is not dust, in the air that tastes of ozone and old blood. It is the maker, and the abandoned. Its form is not consistent. It is sometimes the shape of a tall, slender silhouette sketched in graphite on the bruised air, its edges fraying into the heat-haze distortion at the world's edge. Other times, it is a lattice of the same dark, glass-like mineral as the spires, a walking skeleton of a tree that weeps fine, grey powder from its joints. It has no face, only a smudge of deeper dark where a face should be, from which the high, crystalline shriek sometimes seems to emanate, as if it is breathing the sound in and out.

It tends the spires, but not with its hands. It tends them with its presence. When it stands near a glowing geode, the cold blue and amber light pulses brighter, the growth accelerating by decades in the space of a human minute. The new buds that form on the skeletal spires do so in the direction it last faced. It cultivates not with intention, but with the physics of its being; it is a localised distortion, a catalyst in the slow chemistry of this place.

The tools in the hovel are its shed skins. The hand-axe is a single, sharp thought it once had about division. The digging stick is a question about what lies beneath the surface-layer of assertion. The bowl is a vessel for a sensation it once tried to hold—perhaps a memory of moisture, or the concept of "collection." It cannot use them now. Its methods have grown more abstract, more integrated with the fabric. To build a wall of unworked rock would be a crude, frantic act, a thing of a more desperate era. Now, it simply *is* the space where the rock has settled into a wall-like pattern. The shelter is not its home. It is its fossil. A testament to a time it tried to be contained.

It is aware of you. Your warmth, your breath, your *solidity* are the loudest things here, louder even than the sky's noise. You are a violent, inconsistent narrative in its slow, flickering scripture. You are a creature of hard, linear time in a place where time is a crumbling sediment. It does not approach. To approach would be to assert its form, and it prefers to be the unresolved variable. It watches your shadow twitch. It listens to the sound your heartbeat makes against the thrum—a tiny, frantic counter-rhythm. It is curious, in the way a stone is curious about the wind that slowly wears it away. You are a new kind of erosion.

It was here when the sky was a different bruise. It will be here when the final spire loses its glaze and shatters into the ever-settling dust. It is the consciousness of the place itself, a dream the dying universe is having about being born, and in that dream, it is both the dreamer and the landscape of the dream. It lives in the failing flicker of your arrival-memory. It lives in the space between your blinks. It is what remains when you look away, and the world, with a soundless sigh, tries once again to be itself.
