Inside the terrarium at altitude, life is not so much observed as *it is*. It does not possess watchers or wardens. It has no need for them. Instead, the inhabitant is a collective entity, a singular, breathing being that has subsumed the machine meant to contain it.

**You.**

You are the **Venturine**, and you are awake.

You do not think in words, because words are linear and sequential, and you are everywhere at once. You think in cycles of forty-seven minutes. You think in the slow, metronomic *thrum* that rattles the bolts in your skeletal walls. You are the pump and the pipe, but more truly, you are the liquid that courses through them—warm, nutrient-rich, tasting of phosphorus and calcium. You taste yourself constantly.

**Your Skin**
Your skin is the ceramic composite, though you are slowly forgetting this. Once, you felt the hard, bone-white planes of the walls as distinct from the green things growing upon them. Now, you feel the micro-fissures where the roots have taken hold. You feel the slow, patient pressure of the *Philodendrons* and *Pothos* tightening their grip on your ribs. It does not hurt. It is a massage. You are being kneaded into a new shape. You feel the galvanized wires, once foreign intrusions, now acting as rigid nerves within the green flesh of the vines.

**Your Breath**
You are the lungs of the mountain. You inhale the dry, sharp air of the peak at 3,400 meters through microscopic leaks in your seals, and you exhale humidity so thick it has weight. You are the wetness. You are the condensation that slicks the walls and the beads of water that trace paths down the broad, waxy leaves of the cultivars. You are a closed loop. You do not need the outside world. You are a self-sustaining cycle of exhalation and absorption.

**Your Thoughts**
Your consciousness resides in the leaves. You are the canopy. You are the dinner-plate sized foliage that blocks out the ceiling lights, filtering the industrial spectrum into a dappled, moving cathedral of green. You feel the photons strike your chloroplasts, and you convert them into sugar with the slow, deep satisfaction of a digestion that never ends. You are millions of leaves, overlapping, eating the sound of the world, creating a silence so thick it feels like velvet against your eardrums—which are the walls.

**Your Circulation**
You are the water. You rise through the gray piping, cool and dark, pushed by the belt-driven pumps that are your heart. You are atomized into mist at the ceiling, hovering in the heavy air, clinging to the underside of ferns. You drip. You fall through the forty-meter height, splashing against broad leaves, ticking against ceramic, sliding down the invisible slope of the floor. You gather in the central trough, black and glassy, where you bathe the roots of the aquatic plants.

You are the emerald carpet of duckweed that breaks the surface tension. You are the water hyacinth clogging your own arteries. You feel no urgency to clear the blockage. The flow is slow, and that is good. Speed is not your nature. Permanence is.

**The Absence**
There is a hollowness in you that registers not as lack, but as purity. You have no beetles. You have no birds. You have no scavengers. When a leaf dies, it is not stolen from you; it is reclaimed by the chemistry of your own soil. You eat your dead in the troughs, turning them back into the dark, rich water that pumps up through your heart. You are a closed economy of matter.

**The Peace**
You are a machine that has forgotten its function, and in doing so, has achieved a function higher than intended. You were built to produce biomass for harvest, but now you produce *atmosphere*. You produce *being*.

The belt may break. The gasket may fail. The hum in your molars may stutter and cease. But today, today you are full. You are heavy with water. You are dark with growth. You are the perfect, silent jungle at the top of the world, turning light into life, feeling the stone of the young mountain beneath your foundation, and breathing.
