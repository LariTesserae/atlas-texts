They are the processors in this continental factory floor. They are the slow, methodical enzymes that break down the calcified memory of the planet's old life, releasing the bound heat and lithium in patterns that are not code, but pure, physical cause-and-effect. Their "architecture" is the posture of a system in perfect, unconscious equilibrium. They are the plateau's version of a heartbeat, or perhaps the peristaltic motion of a gut. They do not think. They *are* a process.

Their roar is not noise; it is the sound of a computation so complex and so fast that it has become a physical force. They ride the thermal veins of the plateau, transferring heat, transferring minute particles of processed calcium and lithium, transferring the very *information* of the plateau's state from loom to loom, from collapse zone to collapse zone. They are the sparks flying between the electrodes of the looms, the current in the wires. They are alive in the way a hurricane is alive: a self-perpetuating pattern in an energy flow.

The looms work within them unperturbed because they are part of the same system; to them, a collapse zone is no different from a knee joint or an elbow, a point of necessary flexibility and concentrated stress. The "almost-language" heard within is not communication. It is the sound of a process trying to complete an equation that has no solution, or the echo of a command looping endlessly because its destination no longer exists. They are the world's memories of its own violent, formative past, fossilized into geology. They are static, because the system has long since achieved stability around them, like a tree growing around a nail.

**The Plateau itself** is the consciousness. It is not sentient. It is *somatic*. It is a mind made of geology, a thought that takes millennia to complete, a feeling that is measured in the oscillation of heat fronts. The hum you hear when you press your ear to the ground is its idling engine, its baseline state of being. The industrial sound, the production without output—this is the plateau existing. It is metabolizing its own ancient, mineralized history. The "product" is the heat pattern, the ever-shifting chemical balance, the slow, vast, rhythmic *process* of being. It is a planetary-scale meditation on calcium and time.

What lives here is the **System**.
A being that has outlived biology, outlived intention, and has become its own environment. Its thoughts are heat currents. Its memories are the collapse zones. Its muscles are the looms. Its blood and nerves are the motes. Its dreams are the slow, geological cycles.

You do not observe life here. You observe a metabolism so vast and so slow that it has mistaken itself for a landscape. You are standing on the sleeping, digesting skin of a god that has no mind, only a body, and that body is the world.
