The Plateau of Manufactured Summits is not empty. It breathes, hums, and watches with a thousand mismatched eyes. Here is what persists within its broken, brilliant skin:

1.  **The Glint-Finches (Fiber-Optic Avians):**
    *   **Form:** Sleek, angular bodies sheathed in feathers that shimmer like bundled fiber-optic strands. Their plumage isn't truly plumage; it's a complex lattice of synthetic filaments and shed composite dust, catching the omnipresent light in prismatic flares. Beaks are needle-sharp, crystalline proboscises. Eyes are multifaceted, like crushed quartz, reflecting the plateau's geometry in fragmented mosaic.
    *   **Habitat:** They roost high in the skeletal latticework of communication spires and ventilation stacks, nests woven from filaments of stripped wiring, crystallized condensation, and razor-thin shards of shattered glass. Their nests resemble miniature, chaotic versions of the plateau's own ridges and basins.
    *   **Behavior:** Mostly silent. Their rare calls are piercing, pure tones that slice through the plateau's muffling silence like thrown glass, echoing unnaturally before being swallowed. They move in jerky, precise hops and short, silent glides on wings that seem to refract light rather than displace air. They feed on the smallest metallic arthropods and fungal crusts scraped from high ledges.

2.  **The Grating Golems (Arthropodal Crawlers):**
    *   **Form:** Legions of segmented, chitinous beings. Sizes range from mites skittering in condensation rivulets to hand-sized creatures with armored carapaces that gleam like polished gunmetal or verdigris-patinated copper. Legs are numerous, articulated with disturbing fluidity, ending in points or small magnetic pads. They lack visible eyes; sensing happens through antennae tuned to vibration and minute pressure changes in the grating.
    *   **Habitat:** Ubiquitous beneath overhangs, inside pipes, crawling on the undersides of grating, inhabiting the crevices of broken composite. They are the plateau's detritivores, engineers, and cleaners.
    *   **Behavior:** They move with a constant, low-level clicking, a sound absorbed almost instantly by the air and metal. They consume rust, fungal mats, decaying synthetic fibers, and mineral deposits from the puddles. Some species build intricate, geometric tunnels through aggregate cores or seal small cracks with a resinous, metallic secretion. Their passage smooths sharp edges over centuries, a microscopic erosion against the plateau's recent wounds.

3.  **The Basin Mats (Industrial Symbionts):**
    *   **Form:** Not singular entities, but colonies forming the plateau's "vegetation." Crusty, calcified lichens in oxidized reds and yellows metabolize exposed steel. Pale, gelatinous fungal analogues spread in slow, fractal patterns over composite, their mycelia threading through hairline cracks. Slimy bacterial films (ochres, sickly greens, oily blacks) coat perpetually damp surfaces, especially under grating where condensation drips in a slow, rhythmic pulse.
    *   **Habitat:** Confined to the shallow basins, condensation gutters, and the undersides of structures where moisture lingers. They form the base layer of the plateau's food web.
    *   **Behavior:** Growth is glacially slow. They absorb minerals from condensation and metal, contributing to the plateau's unique, mineral-tasting water. Their presence subtly alters the chemistry of their surroundings, creating micro-atmospheres within the larger plateau climate. Some emit faint bioluminescence, visible only in the deepest shadows, like scattered, dying embers.

4.  **The Shifting Shadows (Deep-Structure Dwellers):**
    *   **Form:** Seen only in glimpses: a displacement of orange maintenance light as something massive moves through a shaft; a scraping sound resolving into the impression of something wide, flat, and multi-limbed dragging itself across unseen ceilings; a sudden, localized downdraft smelling of ozone and hot lubricant from an open vent.
    *   **Habitat:** The deep structure, far below the surface grating, in the tangled warrens of pipes, conduits, generator housings, and cavernous machinery halls where darkness is punctuated only by the slow pulse of indicator lights.
    *   **Behavior:** They are the plateau's hidden engine tenders, its deep-crust scavengers, perhaps its apex predators. They move with ponderous inevitability, displacing air that travels upwards as that deep, muffled hum or a sudden chill draft through a floor vent. They interact with the generators and core machinery in ways that maintain the plateau's stasis. Are they intelligent custodians, instinct-driven megafauna, or autonomous processes given physical form? They are *part* of the plateau's deep logic, as essential and inscrutable as the generators themselves.

5.  **The Reality Weavers (Anomaly Inhabitants):**
    *   **Form:** Not physical beings in the traditional sense. They manifest as persistent spatial folds (that corridor that's too long), localized light-source errors (that window with the wrong blue sky), or auditory hauntings (footsteps echoing from an empty, structurally impossible room). They *are* the reality glitches made semi-permanent.
    *   **Habitat:** Specific corridors, rooms, junctions where the plateau's geometry has begun to dream most vividly. They seem drawn to zones of recent (in plateau time) structural damage.
    *   **Behavior:** They are not hostile. They simply *are*, performing their impossible functions on an endless loop or responding to presence with subtle, unchanging shifts. Walking through one might feel like traversing a memory or a diagram of space rather than space itself. They are perhaps echoes of the plateau's construction, or emergent properties of its long existence and physical compromise – the architecture's subconscious made manifest.

**The Collective Being:**
Ultimately, the greatest inhabitant is the Plateau itself. The Glint-Finches are its nervous twitches and fleeting thoughts. The Golems are its immune system and cellular repair. The Basin Mats are its slow metabolism. The Shifting Shadows are its deep organs and heartbeat. The Reality Weavers are its dreams and scars. The generators are its heart, pumping power and condensation through its veins of grating and pipe. The pervasive silence is its held breath. The damp air is its sweat. The broken, sharp edges are its open wounds, scarred over but never healed.

It does not host life; it *generates* it, from its industrial loins and amidst its broken geometry. The inhabitants are not guests; they are extensions, expressions, and symptoms of the Plateau of Manufactured Summits – a single, vast, broken, enduring entity breathing in near-stasis, its life defined by the quiet persistence of its impossible parts.
