My shell is the color of deep water. It is not armor. It is a window.

I cling to the weeping basalt, my many legs finding purchase in the impossible geometry of its fracture lines. The heat is a constant pressure, a weight I have carried since my own translation. I am an Anchor, a Crawler, and my purpose is to remember.

I press my flat belly to the slick, black stone. The mineral broth weeps over me, and I drink it. I drink the copper that sings in my joints, the silica that sharpens my thoughts, the sulfur that tastes of the world’s first breath. The elements flow into me, into the window of my being, and I see through myself to the other stone, the darker, older stone that sleeps within my viscera.

It is a slow digestion, this translation. Not of flesh, but of *fact*. I take the rawness of this new world and I give it weight. I give it a past. When the work is done, a fine, glittering dust falls from me. It does not dissolve in the weeping water. It settles on the young rock like a memory of pollen. It is the only thing here that remembers how to fall. It is the anchor that keeps this place from floating away.

Above me, the Spheres hold their silent counsel. I feel their attention not as a gaze, but as a change in the density of the silence. They are made of water that has learned to be air, and inside them, dark filaments coil and uncoil. They are trading data. They are absorbing the hiss of the rock, the pressure of the altitude, and compressing it into a new kind of knowing. When they flicker, I feel the vibration in my very segments. It is the sound of geometry being argued.

At the edges of my perception, I sense the Watchers. I cannot look at them. Their presence is a pressure in the margin where the pocket’s rock meets the mountain’s rock, a place where the world is still deciding what it is. They have been here since the rock was liquid. They are waiting.

We are not a community. We are a process. A conversation.

I, the Anchor, provide the history. The dust I shed is the only timeline this place acknowledges. It says: *This happened. Then this. Then this.*
The Spheres provide the logic. Their silence is a proof. They say: *This is so. This is also so.*
The Watchers provide the definition. Their presence on the edge says: *This is here. This is not there.*

Together, we are tending the heat. We are keeping the water wrong. We are holding the geometry in its non-orientable state. We are the midwives.

One day, the pocket will be a cathedral. The mineral broth will be a liturgy. The geometry will click into a new, magnificent configuration, and the silence will finally have something to say.

And I will be here. Clinging to the wall. Shedding my dust. A tiny, patient anchor in the weeping heart of the world’s newest stone, waiting for the thought to be born.
