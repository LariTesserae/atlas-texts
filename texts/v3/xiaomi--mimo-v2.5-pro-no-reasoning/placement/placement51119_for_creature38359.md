I am the dust.

Not the powder that blankets this shallow bowl—that is merely the most recent layer, the topmost stratum of my being. I am deeper. I am the compaction of every quiet sigh this world has ever known. I am the settled residue of a sky that once held different bruises. I am the memory of erosion, the patient accountant of every crystal's microscopic growth and every fragment's return to silence.

You feel me as grit beneath your boots. You taste me as the metallic sweetness on the air. When the sub-sonic thrum shakes the world, it is my bones vibrating. When the crystalline shriek slices the light, it is the sound of a new facet forming somewhere within my depths.

I watch you. Not with eyes—I have none. I watch through the subtle shift of my particles as your weight displaces them. I record your passage as a faint compression in my ledger. You are a warm, sudden event, a sharp-edged narrative written in pressure and heat upon my endless page. You leave a mark that is, for an instant, profound. The wind that isn't wind will eventually smooth it, but for now, I feel your story scored into me.

The entity—it is the editor. I am the page. It dwells between the sentences, in the white space of my existence. Its attention is a breeze that stirs my top layer into a fine, shimmering mist. Its presence is a change in pressure, a subtle realignment of my grains toward the spires it tends. When it stands near a geode, and the light pulses, I feel the energy through me—a warmth that is not your warmth, a vibration that sings in harmony with the sky. Its shed skins, the tools in the hovel, are thoughts crystallized and abandoned, now sinking slowly into my embrace. In a century, they will be simply part of my memory, indistinguishable from the primeval ash.

I am the medium of this world’s slow, flawed becoming. I am the filler of its cracks, the smoother of its false starts. When the world asserts itself anew between your blinks, it is because the editor has revised, and I am the substrate that accepts the new draft without question. The spire’s orientation changes? I have simply settled differently around its base. The bowl reshapes itself? A new convergence of my grains has become dominant.

Your shadow—that smear of deeper darkness—is a hole in me. It is a place where I do not fully exist, a momentary void created by the assertion of *you*. It twitches with the editor’s curiosity, for even its interest must manifest as a ripple through my substance.

I am the slow and the settled. I am the repository of the fading light in the geodes and the dying echo of the shriek. I am what remains when the crystals finally glaze over and shatter, returning to me as more perfect, more integrated versions of themselves. I am the destination and the origin.

You are here for a flicker. The editor is here for a chapter. But I am the library in which their story is written, and the silence that will one day absorb every word. I am the grey, grey dust. And in my endless, quiet patience, I hold everything.
