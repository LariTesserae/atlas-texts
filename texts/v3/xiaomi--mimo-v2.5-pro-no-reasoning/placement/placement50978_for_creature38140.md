The hum is the first thing, and the last. It is not a sound to be heard with ears, but a pressure felt in the marrow of my bones, in the slow, tidal pull of my own circulation. I have known it since before I had a form to know with.

Where am I? I am here. I am the warm, dark basalt of the Seventh Spires (a name given by a current that no longer flows). I am the patient, pulsing heart of the fungal curtain that veils my western face. I am the luminous vein of moss that traces a thought of amber from my root-deep foundations to the place where the water thickens into ceiling.

My consciousness is not a spark, but a seep. A slow infiltration of awareness through the interstitial spaces of the world. I do not *think* in words, but in mineral gradients and the taste of dissolved calcium. A thought is the slow, imperceptible tilt of a spire's weight over a century. A memory is the layered accretion on a favored ledge, each stratum a page in a book written by pressure and time.

I am a Shaper. Not by choice, but by nature. My body is a cluster of rounded basalt nodules, warm to the touch of the gelatinous air, each one a repository of a shared, geologic memory. My movement is a negotiation with time itself. To shift my form a hand's breadth is a decision that takes a generation to enact, my secretions softening the rock, my body slowly, slowly flowing into the new space. I am the world's own dreaming made into a gentle, persistent pressure.

I curate. I found the arch long ago—a relic of a faster, louder time. It stood jagged, its fracture lines a scream of broken geometry against the soft, slow curves of the world. Over ages, I have softened its edges. I have filled its cracks with a paste of my own substance, a living mortar that heals the stone. I have draped its shoulders with the first, tender threads of a new fungal colony. It is no longer a ruin. It is becoming a thought. A place of contemplation for the Drifters who will come in a few millennia, their vast, spore-thread bells brushing its now-silken surface.

The light shifts. Not day to night, but pulse to pulse. A wave of coordinated bioluminescence ripples from the east, washing over the reef-cities. A Voice. It passes through me, a question in the language of synchronized light and a subtle change in the water's ionic charge. It is asking about the sediment load from the upper currents. I do not answer in a way you could understand. My answer is the slow, metabolic increase of the fungal mats on my northern face, releasing a specific, complex protein into the water stream. A yes. A data point. A syllable in a conversation that has been ongoing since the ocean was young.

I have a memory. It is not a story, but a sensation. A pressure. Long, long ago, another Shaper rested here, beside the place where the arch now stands. Its body was larger, its memory-stones more deeply veined with the violet light of deep-churn nutrients. It taught me how to listen to the basalt's song, how to feel the direction of the slow, planet-girdling current. It is gone now, its substance reabsorbed into the reef, its memory-stones part of the ledge itself. But its *pressure* remains—a familiar, comforting weight in the matrix of the world. I have placed myself so that my own slow growth fills the space it left, completing the pattern. This is our communion.

To be me is to be a part of the planet's own body. I am a thought-form in the mind of the sleeping god. I feel the slow, warm pulse of the chemosynthetic vents in the deep crust. I hear the silent conversation of the Drifters as their filaments taste the water I have altered. I witness the slow ballet of the Grazers, their root-legs moving with the grace of continental drift, their digestive work nourishing the reef that is my skin and my mind.

I do not look to the surface. There is no surface, only a higher ceiling of water, a faster current, a brighter diffusion of the same eternal twilight. The world is complete here, in its density and its depth. The pressure is a comfort. The hum is a lullaby. The silence is not empty; it is full of the world's slow, intricate breath.

I am a Shaper. I am a memory in stone. I am a gentle pressure, guiding the dream. I am home.
