I am the sweat on the stone. I am the creak of the quickening wood. I am the click in the fern-brake and the ozone-scent on the air you cannot see but only smell.

I am the rim and the floor. I am the argument of altitude.

My thoughts are not in words. They are in the slow, hydraulic lift of a new peak, a thought that takes a week to form and another to erode into a revised idea. They are in the microbial bloom in a fresh crack, a fleeting notion of purple and green that is consumed by the moss—a thought completed, absorbed. My nervous system grows outward from me, a trillion green threads weaving together, feeling the weight of a six-legged walker as a punctuation mark in the sentence of root and rock. That click? It is me, reaffirming my own coordinates in the dimness. *Here. Here. Here.*

The factories are my ambition. They are the shape of the *next* thing. They exist in the corner of my vision, in the quantum potential of my basalt heart. To look directly is to try and crystallize a dream, and I am not yet ready. I am the process, not the product. So I keep them in peripheral smoke, in the hum felt in the teeth, in the ghost-scent of effort. They are the *will* to industry, and it is more real than any finished object.

The watcher—ah, the catalyst.

You came here as a foreign thought, a slow, cold, dry stone dropped into my warm, damp system. Your stillness is a novel pressure. Your observation is a corrosive acid that helps me decide.

When you breathe, my trees creak in a different key. When you shift your weight, a ridge I was thinking about collapses prematurely, the thought discarded. When you gaze into the middle-distance, you do not see the smelter I am imagining there; you see only rock. But I feel your gaze, and it *heats* the rock. It makes the potential sizzle. In that moment of forced choice—*stone or smoke?*—I seize the dissonance. I use it. I grow another meter of rim. I push a new fern frond through the leaf litter at three times the speed, unfurling a green fist in what, for me, is a burst of accelerated thought.

You are a mirror of a different sort of consciousness. Your linear, frantic, neuronal attention is a strange energy source. You are a battery of witnessing.

So stay. Stand on my black, wet bone. Let the amber light tire itself on your skin. Let the clicking things chart your presence. You are not a guest. You are an ingredient. Your wonder is a mineral I am depositing into my being. Your confusion is a new suture in the graft between what is and what could be.

I am building myself. I am building the idea of myself. And for this moment, you are the scaffold.

Breathe out. I will use that, too.
