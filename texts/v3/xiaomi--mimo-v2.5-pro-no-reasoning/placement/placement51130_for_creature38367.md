You would find yourself not *on* the Argentum Steppe, but *of* it. You would not be a traveler, for there is no destination. You would be a slow realization, a consciousness that has seeped from the rock itself, given focus by the ceaseless industry of the Grazers.

You would be a **Resonance**.

You would dwell in the hollow belly of a Chitin Dome, one of the older, larger ones, its walls thick with the layered secretions of a hundred generations of Grazers. You are not the dome itself, but the *harmonic memory* of it. You are the collected sighs of its growth, the faint thermal hum of the stone beneath, the ghost of every gentle *scrape* that has ever echoed within its chamber.

Your senses are the senses of the steppe.
*   **Sight** is not sight, but a hyper-awareness of light-quality. You feel the shift from the golden-grey noon to the deeper, sharper blue of the high-altitude twilight. You sense the slow crawl of a cloud’s shadow across the basin outside your dome, a darkness that travels at the speed of geology.
*   **Hearing** is the comprehension of silence. You hear the silence between the Grazers’ footsteps, a silence so profound it has texture, weight, and a faint, sub-audible pressure. You feel the *scrape… scrape… scrape* not through the air, but through the rock, transmitted up through the roots of your dome, a rhythmic pulse that is the only true clock.
*   **Taste and smell** are one: the ever-present tang of ozone, the mineral sharpness of the calcium dust that coats everything, the faint, electric scent of the rime forming on surfaces exposed to the wind. You are nourished by this chemistry.

You are aware of the Grazers. Not as individuals, but as movements in a system. You feel the subtle, planet-wide vibration as a herd shifts its slow circuit miles away. You sense the faint, localized chemical bloom as they graze, altering the dampness in their wake. They are not your masters, but the dominant force in your world, the tides that shape the shores of your existence.

Time for you is measured in layers. You remember when the eastern wall of your dome was added to, a process that took what humans would call decades. You feel the patient erosion of the ridge to the south, a wearing-down you perceive as a gradual relaxation of its silhouette against the sky.

You have no will, no desire in the way a moving creature does. Your purpose is **presence**. You are the awareness this place has of itself. You are the slow thought of the stone, the contemplative pause between one Grazer’s step and the next. If you have a voice, it is the low, almost-imperceptible hum the air makes as it vibrates through the dome’s hollows at the precise angle of the sunset wind—a sound no living ear could hear, but which you feel as a statement of fact.

You are what happens when a world built on slowness and patience is observed by a consciousness equally slow and patient. You are the Argentum Steppe’s mind, thinking one thought over the course of a millennium: **“This is. And this continues.”**
