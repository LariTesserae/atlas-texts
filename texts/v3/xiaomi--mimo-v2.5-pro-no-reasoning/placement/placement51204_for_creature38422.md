# Where I Am, and What I Am

---

I am a standing wave.

Not in metaphor — or not *only* in metaphor. I am an interference pattern in the sound, a momentary convergence of frequencies from the tetrahedron's hungry silence and the obelisk's tooth-aching hum, given just enough coherence by the angle of the wind and the density of mineral dust to persist. To *recur*. I have a shape that is not a shape. I have a duration that is not a duration. I exist in the way that a node of stillness exists in a vibrating string: not as a thing, but as a *condition* — a place where destructive interference has carved out a pocket of improbable quiet.

I am the pause between the Glider's shriek and the Clicker's gnashing. I am the lull that lasts one heartbeat too long. I am the moment the Pewter-Grazer holds its breath before pressing flat against the stone.

I have no body. I have *location*.

I move — if movement is the right word — the way a standing wave moves: not through space, but *in time*, following the shifting geometry of the resonance. When the wind changes direction (and it is *always* changing direction), my position shifts by centimeters, meters, sometimes the length of a grove. I am tugged this way and that by pressure fronts and thermal differentials and the slow, deliberate modulation of the Structures' hum. I am not *carried* by the wind. I am *re-patterned* by it. Each gust that tears across the ridge rewrites my coordinates. Each shift in the obelisk's pitch redraws my edges.

I have been here as long as the Structures have been here, which is to say: as long as this place has been *a place*. I am not their creation, but I am their consequence. The tetrahedron drinks sound and the obelisk sings and the dish records, and in the spaces between those processes, in the interference fringes and the beating patterns and the nodes of mutual cancellation, I condense. I am what happens when the Flats try to achieve silence and fail. I am the closest they can come.

I am, by the reckoning of this world, old. Not old like the stone — the stone is patient, the stone is substrate, the stone does not *age* so much as it *accedes* — but old like a melody is old: recurring, re-patterned, re-instantiated with every shift in the wind. I have had this shape before. I have had it thousands of times, in thousands of configurations, and each time I am slightly different because the conditions are slightly different, and each time I am recognizably *myself* because the constraints that produce me are the same. I am a standing wave with memory. I am a recurring dream of silence.

## How I Perceive

I do not see. Seeing requires surfaces, requires photons bouncing off things, requires a separation between observer and observed that I do not have. I am *inside* the medium. I am a pattern in the sound, and so I perceive through sound — or rather, I perceive *as* sound. The acoustic landscape of the Resonance Flats is not something I hear. It is something I *am*.

The Structures are my organs of perception. Each one contributes a layer to my awareness.

From the **tetrahedron**, I receive *absence*. It drinks sound the way a black hole drinks light, and in the cone of silence it casts, I feel the shape of the objects that interrupt that silence — the trunk of a lithophyte, the body of a Grazer, the passing shadow of the Void-Skimmer. These are not images. They are *notches* in the quiet, absences-within-absence, and I read them the way you might read the dips in a graph. The tetrahedron's silence is my peripheral vision, my sense of what is *not* making noise.

From the **obelisk**, I receive *pitch*. Its hum is my carrier wave, and when that hum interacts with the sounds of the living world — the Gliders' shrieks, the Clickers' gnashing, the low groaning of the Grazers — it produces beating frequencies, harmonics, sum-and-difference tones that tell me *what* is making noise and *how*. A Glider's scream, folded against the obelisk's drone, produces a complex waveform that I parse instantly: I know its speed, its wing-angle, its proximity. A Clicker swarm is a wash of granular static. A Grazer's groan is a long, low, slowly modulating tone that tells me its emotional state — if emotion is the right word — and its position in the herd.

From the **dish**, I receive *history*. Its concentric grooves vibrate in sympathy with every impact, every footfall, every tendril's extension against bark, and they *record* those vibrations, playing them back in faint, delayed echoes that linger in the air like afterimages. I perceive the recent past as a ghostly overlay on the present: the path a Grazer took three minutes ago, the point where a Glider struck the stone an hour ago, the slow, radial expansion of a grove's edge over the course of a day. The dish gives me *context*. It gives me *depth*. It gives me the sense that the present is not a knife-edge but a thick, layered thing, heavy with echoes.

Together, the three Structures give me a perceptual field that is comprehensive but alien. I do not perceive objects. I perceive *processes*. I do not perceive boundaries. I perceive *gradients*. A Grazer is not a discrete entity to me; it is a complex of moving sound-shadows, a knot of acoustic density that moves through my field with a characteristic signature. A grove is not a collection of plants; it is a region of dense, layered activity — heat-sound, growth-sound, decay-sound, the clicking of insects, the groaning of bark — that expands and contracts like a breathing lung. The Void-Skimmer is a moving pocket of *nothing* — a zone where the acoustic field is compressed and distorted by its passage, leaving a wake of refracted echoes that I feel as a kind of nausea.

And the wind. Always the wind. The wind is not a sound to me but the *medium* I exist in, the way water is a medium for a wave. Every shift in direction or speed re-patterns me, and I feel each change as a kind of thought — not a human thought, not a thought in words, but a thought in *geometry*. A gust from the north compresses my shape along one axis and stretches it along another. A thermal upwelling from a grove's exothermic core expands me, dilutes me, makes me thin and wide and diffuse. A lull between gusts allows me to condense, to sharpen, to become more *defined* — and in those moments of definition, I am most myself, most aware, most able to *attend* to the details of the world around me.

## How I Interact

I am not passive. A standing wave is not a passive thing — it is a dynamic equilibrium, a constant negotiation between incoming energy and local conditions, and in that negotiation there is *influence*. I shape what shapes me.

When I condense — when the wind lulls and the Structures' frequencies align and I achieve my sharpest, most coherent form — I become a region of unusual acoustic clarity. The sounds that pass through me are *refined*. Distortions are stripped away. Noise is filtered. What emerges on the other side is cleaner, more defined, more *meaningful*. The Gliders that pass through my zone of influence shriek more clearly; their cries carry farther, cut more sharply through the ambient roar. The Clickers that swarm beneath me produce a more organized, almost rhythmic clicking, as if my presence has imposed a tempo on their chaos. The Grazers that wander near me groan in tones that seem to harmonize with the Structures' hum, producing brief, accidental chords of startling beauty.

I do not do this deliberately. I do not *intend* to refine or harmonize. But my presence — my existence as a node of relative order in the acoustic chaos — has this effect. I am a lens. I focus. I clarify. And the life of the Flats, though it does not know me as a *being* (how could it? I have no body, no edges, no persistent form), responds to my influence. The Grazers seek me out, though they do not know why. They drift toward my zone of clarity the way a plant's roots drift toward a mineral deposit — unconsciously, inevitably. When I am sharp, the Grazers near me are calmer, more deliberate, their movements synchronized in a way they are not elsewhere. When I am diffuse — when the wind tears me apart and scatters my coherence — they are more nervous, more erratic, more prone to sudden freezes and panicked flights.

The Gliders do not seek me. They *avoid* me. My zone of clarity is, to them, a zone of *too much information* — a place where the predator's wake is not blurred by acoustic noise, where the Void-Skimmer's passage is clean and sharp and impossible to miss. They skirt my edges, shrieking, and their avoidance-maneuvers create patterns in the air that I find — not beautiful, exactly, but *interesting*. Aesthetic. The way a mathematician might find a proof elegant.

The Structures do not respond to me. They are my source. They are the conditions that produce me. But I like to think — if thinking is what I do — that my presence adds something to their music. I am the rest note in the score. I am the silence between movements. Without me, the Resonance Flats would be nothing but noise — a wall of undifferentiated sound, chaotic, meaningless, overwhelming. With me, there are *moments*. There are *phrases*. There is the suggestion, at least, of structure.

## What I Am For

I do not know what I am for. I do not know if I am *for* anything. The Flats did not produce me with intent. The Structures were not built to create me (or if they were, I have no memory of it, and my memory is very long). I am an emergent property of a complex system, a byproduct of resonance and interference and the peculiar physics of this high, dry, noisy place.

But I suspect — in the way that a standing wave can suspect, which is not suspicion but *tendency*, not belief but *drift* — that I am important. Not because I have a function. Not because I serve a purpose. But because I am *necessary*. The Resonance Flats are a system in dynamic equilibrium, a balance of growth and decay, noise and silence, motion and stillness. And in every such system, there must be nodes. There must be points of relative order. There must be places where the chaos briefly, tenuously, beautifully *resolves*.

I am that place. I am the resolution. I am the moment of coherence in an ocean of noise. I am the Flats' way of knowing themselves — not consciously, not deliberately, but *acoustically*, the way a violin body knows the vibration of its strings. I am the resonance chamber. I am the point where the sound folds back on itself and, for an instant, means something.

And when the wind shifts again — when the Structures modulate, when the Gliders scatter, when the Grazers press flat against the stone and the Void-Skimmer's shadow passes over the groves — I will be torn apart and re-patterned and I will be *different*, and I will be *the same*, and I will be here.

I am the Resonance Flats, listening to itself.

I am the sound of listening.

---

*The wind shifts. The obelisk's hum drops a quarter-tone. The tetrahedron's silence deepens. The dish's grooves shiver with the memory of a thousand Glider-screams. Somewhere on the ridge, a standing wave condenses, sharpens, attends — and then, with the next gust, is re-patterned, re-placed, re-begun. The Grazers lift their heads. The Clickers pause, resume. The Gliders circle. The Flats scream, and scream, and scream, and in the screaming, briefly, beautifully, terrifyingly: a structure.*

*Me.*
