You would not find yourself at the surface, for there is no surface, only the endless, roaring entry of the Downpour. You would not find yourself in the crushing abyss, for that is the realm of the silent Receivers. You would find yourself in the **middle water**, in the great, slow-moving shadow of a Cathedral Drifter.

You would be a **Symbiont Scout**.

You are sleek, hydrodynamic, a living torpedo of coordinated purpose. Your world is one of motion and interface. The Drifter above you is a drifting continent of violet-veined jelly, its shadow your cool, constant sky. Below you, the **Litho-Weavers** are a jagged, humming city of glass and stone, their spires reaching up into your domain.

Your body is a marvel of alien efficiency. Your primary tool is your single, brilliant photophore, nestled in the hardened tip of your form. It is not merely light; it is a language, a weapon, and a scalpel. You think not in words, but in pulses and patterns—a rapid, silent dialect of **Light-Speak**. A flicker of cerulean means "parasite here." A rapid, strobing amber is a command to the Shiverer shoals: "Divert, growth-point below." A sustained, focused beam of white-hot bioluminescence is your defense, capable of searing through the membrane of a drifting predator or cauterizing a wounded section of the Weaver colony.

Your consciousness is a tapestry woven from two imperatives: **Service** and **The Great Hum**. You are an extension of the Litho-Weavers' will, but you possess a mobile, tactical awareness they lack. You feel the deep, grinding hum of their metabolism not as sound, but as a vibration in your gelatinous core, a planetary heartbeat you are attuned to protect. Your loyalty is not to an individual, but to the process, to the cathedral itself.

A typical cycle is a ballet of purpose. You dart, a flicker of motion in the slow world, to a cluster of **Parasitic Filaments** growing over a filtration pore. Your light shifts to a calming blue as you approach the colony's surface, a greeting and an alert. Your photophore focuses. You do not burn; you *command*. A tailored pulse of specific wavelength and intensity travels through the water and into the latticework. The sponge responds. Its pores around the infestation tighten, isolating the parasite. Only then do you apply a precise, searing lance of light, killing the invader without harming the host.

You are a shepherd to the **Shiverers**. Their collective consciousness is simple, reactive. You guide their glittering shoals with patterns of light, steering them towards rich currents of organic dust and away from the trailing, hungry tendrils of a Drifter. In your Light-Speak, they are a river of potential, and you are the banks that direct its flow.

Conflict, when it comes, is swift and silent. A **Void-Bladder**, a predatory, gas-filled jelly, descends from the turbulent layer above, its tendrils questing for the rich pickings among the spires. You and your fellow Scouts—you are never alone, your thoughts a synchronized network—converge. The battle is a conversation of light and physics. You flash warning patterns, a dazzling, disorienting strobe. You coordinate to focus your beams on a single point on the Bladder's tough membrane, not to pierce it, but to superheat the water within, causing it to rupture from internal pressure. The creature's defeat is a silent, popping collapse, its remains beginning the long fall to the Receivers.

You do not fear the Drifter. You respect it as a higher order of the cycle. You watch its miles-long tendrils sieve the Shiverers with a kind of professional reverence. Its waste, the nutrient-rich rain of digested light, will feed the depths. Its eventual death will be a feast for the Receivers, a chemical legacy that will, in centuries, become part of the Weavers' very substance.

You are the dynamic element in a world of patience. You are the spark of tactical thought in a cathedral of slow chemistry. You are the **Symbiont Scout**, and your purpose is written in light: to maintain the balance, to defend the hum, to serve the great, living, breathing stone. You are a neuron in the planetary brain, swimming through the endless, roaring rain that is the source of all things, and the sound of your own, eternal now.
