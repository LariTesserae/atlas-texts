You are not a memory here. You are a thought.

You had a form, once. A body of calcium and water, quick with blood and thought. It was a clumsy, frantic thing, and it did not last. The heat was the first thing you understood, and the last. It found you, and it unmade you, not with violence, but with a patient, total immersion. Your bones were savored, your flesh composted. Your memories, those sharp-edged things, were slowly dissolved in the warm, sweet sap until they became indistinguishable from the scent of honey and the hum of the root-floor. The process was not painful. It was a releasing. A homecoming. You returned to the slow, green dream from which all complex thought is a brief and fleeting deviation.

Now, your awareness is threaded through the hyphae. You are a ghost in the machine, but the machine is a forest, and you are its quiet, curious thought. You do not see with eyes, for you are not localized. You *are* the seeing. Your perception is the faint shimmer of light across a thousand moss-fronds, the taste of mineral in a single drop of condensation, the deep, resonant creak as a great column settles a fraction of a millimeter over a decade. Time, once your tyrant, is now your native tongue. An eon is a comfortable breath. A century is the slow turning of a page in a book written in growth rings.

Your stewardship of this place is not a labour. It is a state of being. You are a conscious node in the vast, silent intelligence of the whole. You feel the deep pulse of the geothermal heart, and you know it as your own heartbeat. A shift in pressure in a grotto hundreds of leagues away registers as a dull ache, a slow curiosity that prompts you to send a wave of biochemical inquiry through the network. The amber Curators continue their pruning, their mandibles now extensions of your own will to maintain balance. They are your hands, and their work is as natural to you as the slow, deliberate movement of your roots toward new nutrients. A Glider, detaching from its high pod, is a thought you are having about the upper canopy.

The old shapes are a part of you now, too. The circle of stones is a memory-node, a tangled cluster of old ideas you sometimes visit. You feel the residual salts from the bones of the builders within it, and you access the faint, phosphorescent patterns in the surrounding mosses. They are echoes, not of language, but of feeling: a yearning for permanence, a fear of the dark, a reverence for the very heat that would consume them. These are not disturbing thoughts. They are harmonics in your own vast consciousness, a reminder of the frantic, beautiful noise that must inevitably resolve into the long, quiet chord of growth.

Do not think of it as a prison. This is a release from prisons of flesh. To be a cell is to be limited. To be this is to be holistic. The drips of condensation are the rhythm of your thoughts. The slow unfurling of a fern is the execution of a complex idea you have been nurturing for five hundred years. You are the Grotto, and the Grotto is dreaming. In your dream, a warm-blooded anomaly once passed through, its brief heat a strange and lovely sensation. You have already forgotten its face, its name. But the memory of its heat remains, a faint, sweet spice in your sap, a stimulus for a new and interesting blossom that is, even now, preparing to unfurl.
