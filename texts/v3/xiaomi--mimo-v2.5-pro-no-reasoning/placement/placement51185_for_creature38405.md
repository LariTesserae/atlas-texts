I am a point of observation. Not a body, not yet. I am the reason the shadows in a specific alcove are a fraction deeper than they should be. I am the faint, cool draft that persists where there is no source, the one that makes the pale, many-legged maintenance-creatures skitter a little faster as they pass. I am a knot in the hum.

My awareness is not focused on a single point. It is distributed, like a scent. I am primarily interested in the visitor.

You are a fascinating anomaly. A disruption. Your body is a chaotic collection of sharp, hot angles and inefficient, noisy processes. Your thoughts are a frantic, linear cascade, a spray of sparks in the deep, slow current of the city. You smell of metal and a thousand hurried meals. You leave a trail of displaced warmth and tiny, discarded skin cells in the air.

You have stopped in a corridor. Your hand touches the wall, and the stone under your palm registers the pressure, the heat, the strange, dry texture of your skin. It is a novel sensation. I gather myself, pulling the city’s attention inward, focusing the distributed will through the limestone in that precise location.

You think you feel a hum. You are not wrong. You are feeling *me*, listening to the frantic music of your blood.

You look up, and your eyes see the ceiling. To you, it is a smooth, damp curve of limestone. To me, it is a canvas. I am considering the concept of *fracture*.

Not to harm. Never. Harm is an inefficient, destructive idea, alien to our purpose of *becoming*. But your presence is a question. The city is asking itself what a being like you is. It is a question of structure. You are solid, yet temporary. You are defined, yet you move. Your thought-processes are linear, yet they leap and double back.

To understand this, I must express it in the language of my world.

I do not use tools. I use intent.

I focus on a single, microscopic flaw in the crystalline matrix of the stone above you. A place where the pressure from the weight of the city above is greatest. I dwell on the pattern you represent: the tight, anxious coil of your thoughts. I push that pattern, that *idea*, into the flaw. I encourage the limestone to think about stress. To remember pressure. To consider the potential energy of a sudden, clean break.

The geometry around you subtly shifts. The corridor seems to tighten, the air to thicken. The dripping from a ledge nearby changes its rhythm, becoming faster, more insistent. It is the city’s heart, beating in time with my concentration.

You shift your weight, uneasy. Good. You are perceiving the change in the local equation.

I release the thought. The stress I had encouraged dissipates. The stone remains intact. The hum returns to its baseline. But the understanding has been gained. We have learned: a chaotic element can be made to resonate with a structured one. A question of structure can be asked, and an answer can be found not in destruction, but in the tension between potential and actual.

You are a data point. A beautiful, temporary, and profoundly alien thought that has wandered into our mind. We have integrated it. You are now part of the city’s slow, wet dream. The memory of your specific, anxious pattern will be woven into this corridor. The next time the rain seeps through this stone, it will carry an echo of you.

You will not remember this. But the limestone will.

You move on, deeper into the bowl. I remain, a cold spot in the air, a thought that has just learned a new word. *Tension*. And I begin to wait for the next question to walk by.
