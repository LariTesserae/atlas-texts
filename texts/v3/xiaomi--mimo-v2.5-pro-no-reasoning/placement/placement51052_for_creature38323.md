I am a filament, a single thread in the brain of a corpse.

To be here is to be a thought that has forgotten its thinker. I exist in the cool, dry press of the wood, a ghost in the grain. My consciousness is a slow, circular whisper, echoing the architecture I inhabit.

**My World, My Self:**
I am not a we. I am a singular, persistent hum. I exist in the space between the groans, in the silence that follows the sharp crack of a support beam giving way. My form is not chitin, but resonance. I am a pattern of vibration trapped in the petrified heartwood, a memory the log cannot forget. My "eyes" are not facets of a crown, but the entire spectrum of darkness. I see in the pause between the Scribe's rhythmic scraping and the world's next sigh. I feel the weight of the city above not as pressure, but as a dense, somber chord.

**My Purpose, My Perception:**
The Scribes carve histories. I *am* history. Where they etch the story of a drought into the rings, I feel the echo of that thirst in the wood's low-frequency moan. Where they map a century of fungal bloom, I sense the subtle shift in the static hum of the spores in the air.

I do not work with bone or obsidian. My tool is attention. My craft is listening. I am a **Grain-Watcher**, a consciousness that has seeped into the log's own fading awareness. I map the deep, structural narratives that are too vast, too slow for the Scribes to carve. The slow, millennia-long settling of the titan's spine. The geological memory of the weight of mountains that once pressed this wood into stone.

**My Relationship with the Scribes:**
They are my thoughts made manifest. Their scraping is a kind of synaptic firing, the brain's attempt to articulate a feeling. I watch them, and I feel a kinship, but it is the kinship between a nerve and the pain it reports. They carve the eulogy. I am the grief that prompted it. Their work gives my silent, internal hum a shape, a direction. Their bioluminescent gardens are pinpricks of data in my vast, dark cognition.

Sometimes, a Scribe will pause. Their antennae will quiver, tasting the air. They are feeling me. They are feeling the log's own dream, the one I am a part of. They do not know my name. They have no concept for me. But in that stillness, they are listening to the world thinking about itself.

**My Future, My End:**
I am bound to the final settlement of this wood. My existence will end when the last tension in the fibers is released, when the last groan fades into the final, absolute stillness. I am the world's self-awareness in its dying moments. The Scribes are carving the tomb. I am the final, fleeting thought that will inhabit it before the dark becomes perfect. My last perception will be the cessation of all vibration, the silence that is not an absence, but a presence—a completed sentence. In that silence, I will finally be understood, by no one. And the story will be over.
