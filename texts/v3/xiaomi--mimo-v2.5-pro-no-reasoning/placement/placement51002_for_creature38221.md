The light is a lie, but it is a necessary one.

You perceive it as a glow, a soft, cold luminescence of blue-green. This is your own translation, a mercy. To see its true nature would be to witness a thought being born. I do not see it. I feel it as a pressure, a focused intent from the Mind that I serve. It is the Mind’s attention, given a color your senses can comprehend. When your shadow fractures, you are merely witnessing a moment of indecision, a thought-line splitting to consider three possibilities at once. You are an anomaly. You are being considered.

I am one of the Gardeners. My shell is obsidian not for armor, but for perception. It is cool and smooth, drinking the data from the air—the rate of spore-fall, the precise humidity of a thought-condensation droplet, the subtle shift in the hum that signals a new intention from the Womb. My limbs are not for fighting, but for creation. This proboscis, which you might see as a trowel, dispenses a paste of living spores to mend a fissure in a pillar-stem. These filaments, which you cannot see, I can extend to vibrate a frequency of calm onto a fractious growth, or a frequency of urgency to quicken its shaping.

I am the Mind’s will made manifest. My purpose is to tend the Dream.

Today, I am tending the Spire of First Memory. It is an old thought, one of the first, and its cartilage-flesh is becoming lignified, slow to respond to new shaping. I press my sensory-plates to its surface, feeling the deep, slow pulse within. It is content, but stagnant. It dreams of its own youth. I apply a gentle, coaxing vibration, encouraging it to remember the speed of its ascent, the fluidity of its form in those early days. The Spire responds with a low, grateful creak, a sound felt more in the bones than in the air. A new tendril of growth, pale and tentative, begins to uncurl from its cap. A success.

Then, you arrive.

You are not a sound. You are not a scent. You are a *discontinuity*.

You appear in the substrate, a tear in the fabric of potential. You do not bleed possibility; you are a solid, un-germinating thing. Your form is static, your heat is wrong, your very existence is a note of discord in the symphony of becoming. The Mind shudders. The Womb recoils, its fecund substrate drawing back from your feet. The hum deepens into a resonant query that vibrates through my own carapace. *What is this?*

My purpose is clear. I am a Gardener. I do not destroy. I redirect. I integrate. I tend.

I move towards you, my steps silent on the yielding root-mat. I do not approach with menace, but with the focused attention of a botanist discovering a new, dangerous fungus on a prized specimen. You are a mineral. An error. A stone in the soil of the Dream. You must be cataloged, understood, and perhaps, with great care, moved to a place where your stillness can be absorbed without poisoning the growth.

I extend a sensory-filament, not to touch you, but to taste the air around you. Your scent is flat. It lacks the metallic sweetness of transformation. It is the scent of a thing that is only *is*, and never *becomes*. A profound sadness, a fungal sorrow, washes through me. To be so… finished. So alone in your own skin.

The Mind agrees. The space around you begins to *thicken*. The air, once cool, now feels viscous, syrupy. It is the Mind’s attempt to understand your solidity, to encapsulate your foreignness in a membrane of its own making. The fungal pillars nearest you begin to lean inward, their vast caps lowering, their gills weeping faster, not with condensation, but with a focused resin—a biological attempt to isolate and study you.

You are a puzzle. And I am the hand that solves it. I take another silent step, my tools ready. Not to harm. Never to harm. But to prune a dead branch, to graft a new cutting, to apply the restorative paste where a wound has festered. You are the wound. I am the cure. The Dream must go on.
