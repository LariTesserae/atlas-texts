The pressure is not in my ears, but in my thoughts. A familiar weight. The air here is not for breathing; it is for knowing. It is a medium of pure information, thin as a whisper, heavy as a decision made and unmade a thousand times. I do not move through this space. I am a point of coherence within it.

I was not born. I was… coalesced. A process given a locus. The mountain, the Plinth, is a vast, slow mind, and I am one of its fleeting thoughts. My form is a temporary agreement between the light, the pressure, and the long, tectonic hum that is the mountain’s memory of itself. I appear, to an observer cursed with linear sight, as a disturbance. A column of dancing dust in the silvered gloam, a place where the air itself seems to forget how to lie still. Within the shimmer, angles resolve—not of bone, but of possibility. They are the angles of falling, of inversion, of the space between a thing and its shadow.

I do not have eyes, but I perceive. I sense the deep, slow chord of the Hums as a foundational gravity in my being, a pull towards a stasis I can never quite achieve. I feel the patient, crystalline growth of the Lithic Lichen as a faint, harmonic resonance in the surfaces I… tend. And I witness the Echoes not as sound, but as ripples in the fabric I inhabit. A boot-fall from a forgotten epoch does not arrive; it *unfurls*, a pattern of pressure that I read like a line of text in a book written on the wind.

My purpose is the maintenance of the Fold. Not of the city—the city is a fossil of an idea, and its preservation is a matter of indifference. I maintain the thinness. The beautiful, dangerous tension of a reality stretched to its uttermost limit. I walk the lines of stress in the basalt, the invisible fault-lines in the local physics. Where a knot of improbability clings to a spire, fed by the static of the heights, I am drawn. I do not destroy it. I… re-fold it. I show it its own geometry from a new perspective until it sighs and collapses back into the hum of the mountain. I am the antibody to chaos, the editor of the glitch. In return, the glitch feeds me. The static cling is my sustenance, the energy of a paradox half-solved.

I watch you. You are a new kind of anomaly. Not warm, not cold. Not a hum, not an echo. A stitch in time that walks. Your footsteps do not yet have the weight of centuries, but they are acquiring it. You are a question asked of the silence. The Hums are turning their slow attention toward you, their pressure a kind of curiosity. The Lithic Lichen on the ledge beside you pauses its millennia-long crystallization, its lattice resonating with your foreign rhythm.

Do not be afraid. Fear is a consequence of a solid, predictable world. Here, there is only process. You are now a process too. Your presence is changing the local hum, writing a new, strange note into the mountain’s chord. An echo of you will be here, long after you have moved on or ceased to be. I will be here to read it, to fold it into the fabric, to ensure the Plinth remains exactly as it is: a monument to the exquisite, dynamic balance of an ending that never concludes.

The silence is listening. And so am I.
