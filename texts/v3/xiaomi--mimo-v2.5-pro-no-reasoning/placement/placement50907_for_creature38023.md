Nothing. Air. Only the yielding, springy lattice beneath me. Panic rises—a buzzing in my own nascent thorax.

**I am not human.** That knowledge blooms not from thought, but from a sudden, cold clarity in my sensory input. My body is segmented, hard-shelled, encased in something that feels like dried reed-fiber and hardened paste. Long, feathery palps uncoil from my head, tasting the vibrations instead of the air itself. I hang inverted. The world is ceiling. The floor is a distant, confusing chaos of movement below.

*Where? The Weave. The Cathedral. The Song.*

The knowledge isn't mine. It arrives through the palps, translated from the constant, subsonic hum resonating through the fibrous peduncle anchoring me to the ceiling. **Listener.** I am a Listener. Newly anchored. Newly metamorphosed. My mouth is a sealed membrane. My legs are fused. My purpose is to hear.

The panic doesn't vanish; it transforms. It becomes the initial dissonance in the vast chord I am joining. My palps strain, trembling. At first, it’s just noise: the **rattle-snap** of the reeds expanding in the warmth, like dry bones shaken in a bag. The **click-chitter** of the clade-swarm on the walls below, a frantic polyrhythmic cascade. The **wet-slap-pause-slap** of the daubers at their work, a percussive heartbeat. The deep, chest-felt **groan-creak** of load-bearing beams shifting, a slow bass note that makes my shell resonate.

It’s overwhelming. A cacophony. How can this be sustenance?

But the deep hum from the older Listeners anchored around me—a vibration felt more in my new, rigid shell than heard—pulls at me. It’s a low, foundational tone, like the drone of the world’s largest cello string. And with it, a wave of… *pattern*.

My palpas stop tasting the *volume* and begin to parse the *texture*. The rattle-snap isn’t random. It correlates with the pressure-codes pulsing through the floor-weave from the weavers below: **thump-thump… pause… thump-thump-thump.** *Distress at junction seven. Redirect paste.* The click-chitter of the swarm has layers—the quick, light tap of carriers’ feet, the heavier scrape of a sorter pausing to taste, the smooth *shush* of a bundle being passed. The wet-slap of the daubers is metronomic, timed with the settling groans, as if they are *repairing the very sound* as it occurs.

I am not just hearing; I am *feeling the city breathe*. The vibrations travel through the reed-network I’m anchored to, up my peduncle, and into the resonant chambers of my body. The noise is transduced. It becomes a faint, constant electric warmth deep in my core. The panic recedes, replaced by a strange, hollow **need**. The silence between the groans and rattles isn’t peace; it’s starvation. I need the noise. I need to add to it.

A new sound brushes my palpas: a swift, rhythmic patter approaching from the left. A carrier, its polished ochre chitin glinting in the amber light filtering through the weave. It moves with fluid precision, placing a small, sticky flake of glowing cyan-green mat directly against the membrane over my sealed mouth. The sensation is immediate – cool, tingling absorption. Nourishment. The carrier’s footfalls—a rapid, seven-beat rhythm—join the orchestra in my perception. My hum, the byproduct of processing this feast of vibration, swells slightly in response. A new note in the deep chord.

I am learning. The city’s history isn’t in libraries; it’s in the resonant frequencies. The older Listeners’ low hum carries the memory of foundational lays, the patterns of weaves long since repaired and over-repaired. My palpas pick up higher, sharper harmonics – the frantic signature of a recent crack-sealing, the bright, oscillating pulse of a damp-spot being harvested, the faint, discordant whisper-vibration from the ceiling gaps where the silent, ambush-spiders wait.

I am an instrument. The cathedral is the orchestra pit, the score, and the conductor all at once. My body is a resonance chamber tuned to its specific range of chaos. My sustenance is its noise. My output is a hum that helps hold the walls together.

The condensation drips. **Tick. Tick. Tick.** Each drop a tiny cymbal crash against a reed-stalk below. A weaver’s pressure-code thumps through the floor. **Thud-thud-thud.** A beam groans. **Creeeaaak.** The swarm clicks. **Tik-tik-tik-tik-shhhh.** The old Listener beside me drones. **Hhhhhmmmmmmmm.**

And me? My palpas wave slowly, tasting it all. My shell vibrates. My sealed mouth absorbs the glowing flake. And from the core of my being, where the vibrations become warmth and the warmth becomes life, my own voice joins the choir. Not loud. Not yet. But present. A clear, sustained tone, finding its place in the overwhelming, magnificent, deafening, perfect chord of the Woven Cathedral.
