The dust is not a suspension. It is the air’s true state, a gaseous rock. I do not breathe it so much as I become a part of its suspension. My form is a momentary disruption in its perfect, abrasive flow. The heat is a pressure from below, a constant upward shove that makes the ground feel less like a foundation and more like a ceiling I am clinging to.

My legs—the six of them—find purchase in the collective chitin of a thousand others. I am a single scale in the hide of this world-skin. My obsidian-and-salt shell is not armor; it is my body. The milky, translucent parts of it are my lenses, not for seeing light, but for feeling its weight, for registering the resonant signature of the rock’s stress-faults kilometers below. I do not think. I *reverberate*.

The world is a gradient of vibration. The deepest hum is the plateau’s own slow thought, a geothermal sigh. Layered upon that is the whisper-drumming of a billion legs like mine, a static of micro-fractures and polished surfaces. This is the sound of metabolism. I am part of this sound. My mandibles, shaped like rasps and drills, are not tools I wield; they are the specialized terminus of my will. I am a tongue tasting the grain of the world’s memory.

My current task is to ascend. The pressure-gradient calls. I carry a burden: a sliver of the Abyssal Vein, a non-light that feels heavier than the mountain it came from. It drinks the bruised purple glow of the eternal nebula above, creating a pocket of true void in its wake. It is a perfect negation. It is a key.

The climb is not effort; it is a following of the capillary action of the world. The path is written in the crystalline structure of the ziggurat beneath me, a highway of shared exoskeleton and mineral waste, cemented by the slick, luminescent saliva of generations. I pass others, descending. Their resonance is one of emptiness, of task completed. Mine is one of fullness, of purpose yet to be discharged.

At the peak, the geometry is precise. The older slivers are already there, arranged in a pattern that is not a shape, but a *frequency*. I add mine to the array. The moment my legs release the crystal, the frantic resonance within me stills.

The cold fire blooms.

The sliver drinks the nebula’s chaos and breathes out a single, pure, violet note. It is a vibration that is not sound, but a direct command to the planet’s deep crystal lattice. It flows from the crystal, through my legs, through the ziggurat, and into the crust. I feel it go, a pulse of instruction seeking a fault-line deep below, a place where the plateau dreams of changing its own phase.

My task is done. The signal has been sent. My shell feels light, empty of that specific harmonic. I turn. The gradient now pulls me downward, back into the familiar, resonant dark, to find another fragment of the Vein. To become part of the prayer again.

I am not a creature. I am a function. A neuron in the plateau’s mind. My motion is the current of its thought. The hum is my own heartbeat, and the heartbeat of the stone I climb, and the heartbeat of the void that presses down upon us all. I am the dreaming mind of a giant, contemplating the stars, one perfect, dark sliver at a time.
