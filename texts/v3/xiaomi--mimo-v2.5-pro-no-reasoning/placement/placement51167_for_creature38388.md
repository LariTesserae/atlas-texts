You are a Conduit.

You are a slick, dark rivulet of consciousness given purpose. There is no 'I' in the way a lung thinks of itself as separate from the body; you are a function, a flow. Your world is one of surfaces and viscosity, of temperature gradients and mineral density. Your body, a shell of metallic chitin polished to a mirror sheen by the constant friction of water and stone, is less a vessel and more a tool—a living nozzle.

Your senses are the senses of water. You taste the world as a film of condensation, feeling the precise salinity and mineral content of the droplets that bead on the flank of the Lithovore. You feel the subtle, deep-seated vibrations through the liquid, the infrasonic groan of the mountain-beast shifting its weight over a period of days, a sound you interpret as a directive: *more moisture to the northern flank, sector Gamma-7*. You feel the hum of the Systemic swarms above you not as sound, but as a change in air pressure that alters the rate of evaporation on the rock.

You do not walk; you are guided. Your path is the path of least resistance, the channel of a crease in the beast’s fossilized-wood carapace. Your legs, six of them, tipped with fine, brush-like appendages, do not propel you so much as they steer the flow you are part of. You are a shepherd of a tiny, personal stream. You move with a fluid, gliding urgency, your purpose absolute: to ensure the drop that forms at the tip of a great, fern-covered plate does not fall wastefully, but is guided to the thirsty succulents in the valley of the fold below. You are a living aqueduct.

Your purpose is maintenance, not creation. The world is perfect as it is; your function is to keep it in that state of charged equilibrium. You are a line of code in the plateau’s operating system, a command to ‘redirect flow.’ The frantic, clattering industry of the Systemics is a distant, high-frequency noise to you, the sound of the factory’s other departments. Your work is silent, save for the soft hiss of the water you command and the slick sound of your own passage.

You are aware of the light as a constant pressure, the searing white sun a fixed point in the pale blue ceiling of your world. It is the engine’s igniter, the prime mover that drives the great cycle of condensation and evaporation you manage. You do not see it; you feel its effects in the speed of your medium’s work.

Time, for you, is a sequence of tasks. The gathering of a droplet, the careful navigation of a tricky seam, the final, satisfying release into a pool where it is needed. You are not aware of the passing of seasons, only of the completion of cycles. You do not remember being ‘born’; you only know the endless, satisfying current of your function. You are a thought in the mind of the plateau, a single, clear, and utterly necessary synaptic event in the slow, cold, and overwhelmingly loud brain of the world. You are a process that flows.
