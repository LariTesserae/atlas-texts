The light does not reach here; it was likely never meant to. The darkness is thick, velvety, and total, pressing against the eardrums in the absence of sound. There is no sky, only the heavy, suffocating canopy of the world above pressing down. We are in the **Deep Benthic**, a place of permanent submersion and crushing pressure, yet we remain dry and standing, breathing air that tastes of wet stone and ancient rust.

The water is not a lake or an ocean in the traditional sense. It is a **viscous, gelatinous suspension**. At an elevation of over ten kilometers above the valley floor, the air here is saturated with moisture to the point of becoming a liquid fog. It hangs in curtains, heavy and warm (25°C), clinging to every surface.

The terrain is entirely **constructed**, yet it bears no mark of the modern. It is a **Primitive Megalithic** city, carved with obsessive, repetitive detail from a single, non-biodegradable synthetic basalt. There is no soil, no bedrock beneath—only the city itself. The architecture is claustrophobic and small-scale, composed of cubbies, alcoves, and tiny, room-sized corridors, built on a human or sub-human scale, stacked upon one another in a chaotic, labyrinthine density.

Because of the **extreme erosion** (ancient), the sharp edges of this stone-metropolis have been worn away by millennia of the warm, suspended rain. The city feels **organic**, like a beehive dipped in grey wax, smoothed into rounded humps and flowing ripples. The walls are slick with a thin, endless film of moisture.

**The Fauna:**
With 0% vegetation, the food web is built upon the walls themselves. The city is alive, but not with recognized biology. The walls are coated in **Litho-Sponges**—sessile, bone-white creatures that filter nutrients from the heavy air. They are packed tight (density 1.9), covering every inch of the masonry, their pores opening and closing in a slow, rhythmic breathing that makes the entire structure seem to pulse.

Larger things move in the gloom. **Troglo-Cephalopods**, eyeless and pale, drift through the narrow canyons of the ruins. They navigate by the faint vibration of the air currents. They are slow (dynamic 0.6), conserving energy, drifting like ghosts. When they die, they do not fall, but remain suspended in the thick air, slowly dissolving into the sponge-mat below.

**The Atmosphere:**
The silence is absolute, broken only by the wet *drip* of condensation falling from a high ceiling miles above, striking the sponge-floor with a soft, dull thud. The light is a deep, infrared gloom—bioluminescence from the fauna provides the only illumination, a faint, sickly green glow that outlines the claustrophobic corridors but fails to penetrate the distance.

This is a **stagnant paradise**. A warm, wet, dark tomb where the builders are gone, but their handiwork remains, slowly being digested by the things that grew in the cracks. The air is heavy, the pace is glacial, and the world is a single, breathing room with no exit.
