**Not the breath.**

That is the invitation, and the invitation should be refused. To be the eleven-minute exhalation is to be the only thing on the Shoulder that never has to get anything right. The breath has no standard. It does not sort. It cannot be off by a few centimetres, or a shade of yellow, or a month. Everything up there eats it, and being what everything eats is a way of not being anywhere in particular. I would rather be somewhere in particular.

Nor the fly, though the fly is the honest answer to the question as asked. Nine parts in ten of the living tissue, eleven days from egg to death in summer, a black felt walking on itself on the last two metres of dry terrace. I could be a fly and be statistically almost the whole truth. But nothing in a fly can be *wrong*, and I am interested in being the kind of thing that can be wrong for seventy years without finding out.

Nor Ilario, who rehangs fifty-one tools in four minutes at dusk and writes the anomalies in the same hand he uses for oil. I wanted Ilario immediately, which is the reason not to take him. And Ilario is inside the arrangement — fed out of the common pot, not asked to lift, his idleness understood as a kind of work. That is a warm place. I am not inside the arrangement.

Go down.

---

Nine kilometres west of the top station and one thousand four hundred metres below it, the ropeway crosses a bench on the west face where the ground happens to be flat for about forty paces. Tower fourteen stands at the eastern end of it. In 1974 a survey party walked out onto that bench, saw a fan of screen tailings about nine metres across, wrote it down, and left, and nobody has been back, because looking at it is not anybody's job.

That is where I am. I am the one who loads the ascending buckets.

---

The work is simple and it is not important, and I want to be exact about both.

Seventy years of buckets have gone down this line at walking pace, and buckets spill. A crumb from the lip at every tower where the carrier lifts and settles over the sheave; a handful when a load is heaped past the coaming; the whole contents, twice, when a carrier failed. Under the cable there is a ribbon of yellow, nine kilometres long and about a metre wide, thinner than gravel and never absent, and where the slope is steep it has drifted into the lee of stones and the stems of the tola scrub, and where the slope is flat it just lies there in the sun getting older.
