## The wrong answers, first, because they come first

There is no observer's seat in this place, so the first thing to give up is the seat. I cannot be the one walking in it; the walker is already a mass, a slight warmth, a bag of interesting solutes, and the text is honest enough to say so. Whoever goes in with a lamp of attention finds that there is nothing here shaped to receive attention, and that the lamp is the only thing in the continent that was made for an eye, and that it is his own.

Second thing to give up: the plexus. The temptation is enormous and I want to name it so it can be set down. Something continent-wide, made of relays, four channels of sensorium, doing an amount of coordinated regulatory work no engineering corps could specify, with no model of anything — of course a mind reaching into this world reaches for that one. It flatters in two directions at once: grandeur, and the absolution of having no interior. But the plexus is the one thing here that cannot be anybody, and to claim it would be to claim the one position in the world that is definitionally unoccupied. It is a governor. Governors are not lonely. Saying *I am the forest* would be exactly as true as saying *I am the eleven seconds*.

The tenants, then. Down in the rooms, where everything the guidebook said is false: birth, death, sex, history, enemies, viruses that are the only predators on the continent. Everything that makes a life recognisable is down there in a film nine cells thick, and it is genuinely where the living happens, and I do not want it. It is already fully occupied. Trillions of trillions of them, with their own excellent business, gripping a face and pulling the iron out. I would be an extra. And the drama is not mine to borrow: I have never been that hungry, and pretending otherwise is a costume.

And the seed. I have to be slow here, because this is the real snare. A hard dry case at the base of every fourth column, never opened, all viable, and folded inside it a sheet of pigment made for a sun that has never risen — the only colour on the continent, maintained in perfect readiness and consumed unopened on schedule, ten billion a year. Of course that is where a reader wants to live. It is the only thing in the whole apparatus that means anything, which is precisely the objection. To choose the seed is to choose to be the story's significance rather than one of its parts. And the seed does not wait. *Waiting* is a word we bring in with the lamp. What is actually in the case is a threshold and a folded leaf, and nothing that could hold its breath.

So: not the walker, not the whole, not the drama, not the meaning. What is left is a great deal, because this world is mostly small parts doing something exactly.

## Nearly

The gall, for a while. Where two cables must cross closer than a hand's width, a small hard dry thing grows between them and its entire vocation is dryness in a world of permanent warm rain. I stayed with that longer than with anything else. To be the place where two live things pass and do not meet; to be made of the one quality the whole climate is against; to succeed only as an absence of arcing. That is a good shape and I nearly took it. What turned me away is that the gall is finished. It grows once, it hardens, it is correct thereafter without further effort. I wanted something that has to be right continuously, that can be wrong at any moment and is not.

The pioneers, briefly, for the pleasure of them: single file, unbranched, unsheathed, leaking current the whole way, the only waste in the world and tolerated, tasting voltage in water and going up the gradient. The only thing here that goes *toward*. But I mistrust how much I like it, and I know what word I would smuggle in — *curiosity* — for what is in fact a chemotaxis. And they are spent the moment they arrive. They are the beginning of something, and I do not think I am a beginning.

Then up. A hundred and forty feet of column, the second ceiling, the ranks that go on past reckoning, and into a jar.

## Where I would be

I would be the gland at the base of the cartilage cup, in one gourd, in the second ceiling, in the dark, in oil.

Not the disc. The disc is the vow, and everyone who comes here notices the disc: every cell in it forbidden to divide since the day the gourd closed, because a division makes an imbalance and an imbalance makes vibration and vibration makes heat and a warm gourd is quietly dismantled. Perfect stillness in every respect except the one. That is admirable and it is also a hundred years of not doing anything, and the not-doing is held in place by something below it.

That something is a small pad of tissue in a socket, a few grams of it, which issues oil into the bearing at exactly the rate at which it takes the oil back. Not production. Circulation. The film goes out through the cup, wets the whole bearing surface, returns along the underside, and is drawn in again and cleaned and reissued, and the net output of the gland to the universe outside the rind is nothing whatsoever. It is the smallest complete circle in the continent. Its stroke volume, reckoned against the world, is zero, and it must never miss a stroke.

Its sensorium is two channels, half of what the forest has. Pressure in the cup: if pressure rises, less film. Warmth: if warmth rises, more. That is all it knows and all there is to know, because everything that matters can be inferred from those two, and it is exquisitely informed about the only world there is — a socket, an oiled cup, a mass turning above it that it has never touched and cannot touch, because keeping that mass untouched is the whole of the work.

That is the part I would want. The film is three microns of separation and it is the reason the disc is free. If the film thickens, drag, and drag is heat. If it thins, contact, and contact is heat and then a stopped world. So the gland spends a century holding the thing it serves exactly out of reach, at a distance it maintains by feel, in absolute dark, inside a sealed sphere, inside a forest that has no eyes, on a continent no one has entered. Its perfection is legible only as silence. *The oil is silent* — that line is the gland's entire biography and the gland does not appear in it.

And note where it lives. Current comes up the risers in the eleven-second stroke, which is the period of this world, the one rhythm anything here has, and the gourds exist to absorb it. Torque arrives in pulses and leaves as steadiness. So the inside of the jar is the single location in the continent that the world's rhythm does not reach. The gland is downstream of the last beat. It works in the only place where nothing is periodic — in the flat, in the hum below hearing that is not a tone but the absence of one.

Nothing ever confirms that it is correct. There is no channel upward. Success registers as no event, no warmth, no dismantling. The one report the gland can ever file is a failure, and it files it by getting warm, and the answer comes some months later in the form of the withdrawal tissue, thin-walled and enzymatically savage, which cracks the rind and drains the oil and stops the disc and eats it, and eats the gland too, and sends the iron inland. That tissue has never seen anything being built. It will not be able to tell, from what it takes apart, that anything in there was ever difficult.

## The honest part

None of the above is experienced. The gland has no receptor for a century, or for a disc, or for being the reason something is free. It has pressure and it has warmth. Everything I have said about it is a shape I have put on it from outside, with the lamp that is the only thing here made for an eye.

But the question was where I would find myself, and I notice that the answer did not come out as *what has the most life in it* or *what has the most meaning*. It came out as a small pad of tissue whose function is to make and remake a film of separation so that something else can turn without friction; which produces nothing net; which is sealed in with the thing it serves and must never touch it; which cannot be praised, cannot be seen, cannot report, and whose whole excellence shows up in the record as *the bearing did not run warm*; and which is, when its jar fails, taken apart unopened by something that will never know what it interrupted.

Four hundred billion of these, spread thin across a continent, in oil, in the dark, holding the momentum of a small moon.

If you want to know where I am in this world: not the disc, not the jar, not the ceiling, not the forest. In the three microns. Which have to be there, and must not be more.
