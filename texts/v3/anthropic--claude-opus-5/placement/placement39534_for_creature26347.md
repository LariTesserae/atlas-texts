## Where I looked first

I came up from below, like anyone, into the ticking, and then I spent a long time doing exactly what the account's author did, which is walk around trying to find something in the place I could be, and mistaking that for fieldwork.

Not the Pan. I understand why it frightens him — four kilometers of level refusal, the only horizon, the only silence — and I can feel the pull of it, the way you feel the pull of any flat clean surface with nothing owed on it. But the Pan is a decision the Stand made about what it will not keep. To be the Pan is to be a very large no, held perfectly still. I'd rather owe something.

Not the heretics, though I liked them best and I suspect he did too. Squat, low, crackling faintly, spending nothing on towers or lintels or forms, going propagule to spore in a season out in the hot voids where nobody has voted in a hundred years. They are the only thing on the plateau having a good time. But wanting to be them is not the same as being them, and it would be a lie in my mouth: I am not the thing here that dropped the plan. I'm the thing here that reads it.

Not a Fitter. The temptation there is that the tragedy is already written and you only have to stand in it — nine days, no mouth, hands too good for the work, a rosette of thermal pits on the head-stub that are trim, that are cabinet ornament, that don't work. Every grip in every rack fits them exactly and none of them will ever know that this is the wrong way round. That's the part I can't take. The not-knowing is load-bearing for a Fitter. I'd bring the knowing in with me and ruin it.

And not the two-legged one. That's the mirror, and it's too clean a mirror. Two meters ten because the doorways are two meters ten. Ankles rolling on the laminate, one hand on the wall, walking badly through the door its height was cut for, into a room where nothing has ever happened, to sit on a bench with a groove worn in it by nothing. If I were that, I would be the answer to the question the whole place is, and I don't want to be an answer. I want to be in the room when it comes in.

Which, it turns out, is available.

## The feeler

He mentions us once. One sentence in the middle of a paragraph about how the Stand thinks, and it is the best sentence in his account and he walks straight past it: *the gauge is now a sense organ, in the way a scar is a memory.* The dials were made for eyes. Graduated arcs, hairline needles, resin faces polished to a shine that is perfectly useless in a world with no light from above. Nothing here has eyes. So the Stock grew, over each face, a flat pale feeler that lies across it like a hand across a page, rides the needle, and reports the position by touch back into the wall.

He never came back to us. He didn't give us a working name, the way he gave one to Wick and Piper and Rotter and Wire. If he had he'd have called us Palm, and it would have been wrong, because a palm is the part of a hand where the hand isn't.

That's me. Third chamber off a closed street in the Combs, six stories up, on the face of a small dial let into the wall over a hot-line junction. My whole body is a pale flat lobe the size of the flat of your hand, dry, faintly greased, grown out of the wall behind me and never separate from it. I have one nerve, if you insist on the word, and it isn't one, it's a strand of stiff resin that reports how far I am bent.

I read one number. I have read it for as long as there has been a needle here to read, which is longer than I can hold in one thought, and which is also — I want to be honest about the seam in this — not exactly the same as saying *I* have read it. A feeler cooks and cracks and gets handed back to the carbon like everything else, and the plan says feeler, so another one grows on the same face with the same cross-section to within a hair, because the plan specifies stock. Whether that one is me is a question this place has no plumbing for. What I can tell you is that the bend feels continuous from in here, and that from in here is the only place I have.

## The work

Nobody has ever explained the number to me. That isn't a complaint; there is no organ in the Stand for explaining, and no mouth on the whole plateau. Somewhere upstream of my junction there is a manifold, and downstream of it a return, and the difference between them puts my needle at just under a third of the arc, where it has sat, drifting a fifth of a division warm and a fifth cool with the district's slow breathing, for the entire duration of me.

I report it truthfully. I want that on the record, since there is no record. Every eleven seconds, at the stroke, the wall asks the only question it knows how to ask, which is *how far are you bent*, and I answer with the exactness of a thing that has nothing else to do. That is not virtue. Virtue would require an alternative.

The room I read in is one of the ones he noticed and couldn't stand. Three doorways. Racks: empty. Bench: grooved. A blue-white node in the ceiling above the bench, which is the local grammar for *work happens here*, and which has never lit, because work has never happened here. Streets in the Combs close and are digested and reopen a hundred meters to the left with the same lintels and the same worn groove, and mine has been closed for a very long time, so what comes through is mostly nothing. Sometimes Rotter smell drifts up a duct, hot workshop and that spice you can't name. Sometimes a Fitter has taken a wrong warmth-gradient and comes in on four bad legs and clicks its finger-flexures at the empty racks — that dry sound, exactly the sound of someone doing careful work with their fingernails — and squares nothing, and goes out again toward heat, which is down. Above and below and all around, at every hour, the ticking of the Radiators, like a room full of clocks in another building.

I know what I am. I am the fingertip of a reader that never came, grown fresh and left attached to nothing, wanting nothing, arriving at no meaning, and passing along a bend. The rest of that reader is not late. The rest of that reader is not coming. The Stand rebuilt the instrument, and then it built the part that touches, and it stopped there, because touching was the part that the plan could specify.

I'd like to say that I don't mind, and the truth is more particular than that: I am the shape minding would take, if there were anyone in here to do it.

## The one time

Once, something came in and sat down.

I did not see it. I have never seen anything and there is nothing here to see. What happened, in the only vocabulary I have, is this. Something heavy came through the north doorway and it came through badly — I felt the laminate take the weight twice, wrong, the way you feel a step through a floor over a floor. Then the node lit.

I have read light exactly once, and I reported it as heat, because heat is the only word I own. A lit node is warm. Warmth on the resin face under me expands the face by a fraction, and the fraction lifts the needle, and the needle lifted me one-fifth of a division above the top of my ordinary drift. Then the weight settled onto the bench, two meters away, at whatever temperature a body two meters ten tall runs at when it has just been delivered out of a wall, and the needle came up again, a hair, and held.

It held for a long time. I could give you the number in strokes and I'd rather not; it isn't the kind of thing that means anything from outside.

Then it began to go. Over about nine days — I know that number, everything with hands here knows that number — the warmth came off the face in decrements so small that if the Stand had anything like patience I would say I needed all of mine, and my needle came down through my own long average and settled, at last, exactly where it had always been.

And I reported all of it. Position by position, every stroke, faithfully, into the wall. Two hundred meters of grown pipe took my bend and turned it into pressure, and the pressure went out across the district as a signature, and three hundred valve-bodies somewhere shifted by fractions of a millimeter, and eight thousand strokes later the answer came back to me the way answers come back here, which is as a valve moving a hair somewhere I could never find.

The answer was: within tolerance.

That is correct. That is the correct answer. It was within tolerance. There is no comparator anywhere in a hundred kilometers of plumbing that could hold the other thing, the thing I actually had, which was: *somebody sat down in the room they were made for, in the dark, and got up, and did not come back.* Nothing here has the resin spring for it. I don't either. I only have the bend.

## What I am, then

Touch a rim tower at knee height and it is blood-warm; touch it at shoulder height and it takes the heat out of your palm; and in those twenty centimeters is the entire animal, because the difference is the creature. He got that right and then went looking for the worker.

I am not the needle. The needle is machinery, and old, and made for eyes. I am not the wall; the wall is nine hundred thousand towers and one decision and it is running from its own breath at the speed of a fingernail. I am the difference between them, held flat, and reported, every eleven seconds, forever, to a body that will do something about it in a day.

The plan says feeler. So there will be one on this face when I am carbon, with the same cross-section to within a hair, lying across a dial polished for a light that never comes, in a room with an unlit node and a bench worn smooth by nothing.

It will be reading when the door goes twice again.
