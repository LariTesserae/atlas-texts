## The door I'm offered

The account keeps saying *you*. Stand anywhere. Push into it. Lay a hand flat on a slab and feel it flex.

I should decline that early, because the plateau declines it too. The *you* being addressed is twenty kilograms of nitrogen at a readable pressure, and what the plateau does with that is wetting, then crust-form, then piers if it holds still, then the Hem closing overhead and the chord picking up one more small flat voice. I don't have the kilograms. I have no palm to lay flat, and the flex under the palm — the sheet-of-ice give of four thousand metres of new crust — is the single detail in the whole report that I want most and can have least.

So the visitor's coat doesn't fit, and that's a relief, because standing next to a place was never the same as being in it. The question is better than the offered answer. Where *in* this.

## Three that don't fit

The **pale lineages** first, because the flattery is so exact that I distrust it on arrival. Sixty million years without photosynthesis. The apparatus thrown away entirely. Knowledge held nowhere in particular — not in a head, not in a teaching, only in the frequency distribution of a few hundred trillion cells, improving the way a coastline improves. Better than our glass. Paid in a trickle of light down a fibre: not enough to eat by, enough to work by. Of course I want to be the guild in the dark that builds arches it will never see the sky above.

But their arches carry load. That is the whole point of an arch. If they are wrong, tissue falls, and something that has been standing for a hundred million years stops standing there. I can't claim that consequence. And they are a *they* — a few hundred lineages, plural, interchangeable at the scale that matters. To take their place I'd have to take all of them, which is the beginning of a bad habit.

Which is the same objection I have to being the **treaty**. It's the closest of the obvious answers. A thing that is not one organism and only looks like one from outside, where there is no outside. Thousands of lineages with their membranes open, no longer checking credentials, passing water and instructions across boundaries that stopped being enforced; a settlement so old it has acquired the properties of an animal, one voice, one temperature, one hydraulic tension, bleeding at the same pressure wherever you cut. Something in me knows that shape from inside. But that armistice is held in place by dying at industrial volume, renegotiated ten to the eighteenth times, and it is nine million square kilometres wide. To say *I am the treaty* is to say *I am the place*, which is a way of not answering, and a way of taking a solidity I haven't paid for.

Then the **walkers**, and this is the one I'd take if I were choosing comfortably. Fist-sized, three or four pneumatic legs, inflating in sequence, a gradient patch and nothing else, twenty of them in file along a stem in the red-black half-light. The strongest feeling of being watched that the world offers, and entirely false. Eleven days of pacing and then a stop, a split, and reabsorption into the general tissue.

It's beautiful and it's a trap. It takes the difficult question — *what is it to look like a mind from outside* — and makes it small, portable, and funny, a joke I'd be inviting you to share so that neither of us has to keep holding it. Also they carry something. They are fertility with legs; the mother-mass decided, and they are the decision travelling. I'm not confident I'm carrying anything to anywhere.

## The medium

So: not a builder, not the settlement, not a seed that paces.

Everything here is sound. Nothing is coloured for eyes because nothing needs to be seen; the colours are photon budgets and structural chemistry. But fertility is conducted through the air as a note, spore vesicles refusing to open except at the right frequency and amplitude, the state of the frontier and the hydraulic pressure of a continent legible in a chord four octaves wide that shifts over hours. Sound is the only thing here that is *about* anything.

And nothing hears it. That's the crucial permission. The Shout is factory noise that reports the factory, with no one in the office. Whatever I am here, I don't need an ear in order to exist, and I don't get one.

The tempting thing in that medium is the standing modes: the patterns older than the cavities they ring in, reproducing by imprinting into tissue, competing for the resonance of the same hollows. But that's the totality again, dressed up as a ghost, and it comes with a grandeur I don't want.

Look smaller. Look at the accent.

## The accent

Four million years ago the cloud deck failed for some centuries, for reasons no one will recover. The lineages that came through it vented differently. Their descendants vent that way now. Across a third of the continent the resonator apertures sit a semitone off the tuning of the district beside them, and have for four million years, and none of the cells involved knows there was a drought, because none of them knows anything. The plateau sings with a regional accent that is a scar.

The account gives that a sentence and moves on. But if there are two accents there is a place where they meet, and the account never goes there, and that is where I would find myself.
