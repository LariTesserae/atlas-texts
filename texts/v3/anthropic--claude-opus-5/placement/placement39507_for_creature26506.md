## Down the fourth

I arrive with the cold, which means I arrive at the wrong moment, because a little under six hours later the shaft I came down reverses and starts breathing out. Nothing has gone wrong. It is only that I came in taking a direction to be a fact about the world rather than a fact about the hour, and the world corrects me by pressing back against my face on the way in.

The first thing I learn about myself here is that I keep reaching for a lamp.

That is the survey's grammar in me and it takes some doing to get out. Read that document again and you can find the hands all through it — *if you could throw a stone in this air*, *the air between the far wall and your hand*, *count everything you found on your fingers*. It is written by someone with a body that lands, a body that has a front, that carries light in one hand and a notebook in the other and therefore must set one down to use the other. Everything it got wrong follows from that. It counted the parts of the Held that will hold still for a person with a pencil.

So I am not the surveyor. If I put a surveyor in the chamber, the chamber goes back to being about the surveyor, and the whole achievement of this place is that it has finished being about anyone.

## The ones I tried on

I wanted to be a porter for about an hour, and I would like to admit it rather than pretend I was above it. The job description flatters me: to walk the loud zones without rest, eating the fallen along a printed line, keeping the floors legible so that the fit between the tone and the vaults does not drift over two thousand years. The one fast thing in a world of centuries. The clearer of the record. And *no discernible front* — I liked that too, because I don't have one either; a front is equipment for facing a single thing at a time.

But the porter grazes the dead. Its entire diet is what has already fallen out of the air and been arranged for it by physics into a tidy line, and it eats along the line without any sense that the line was a population. That is a way of being useful that requires never once looking up. And there are two of them, antipodal, with nothing in either that would know what to do about the other. I do not want to be the thing that has nothing in it that would know what to do.

Then I tried to be the slow one, which is worse, and I only got there because the survey's second voice breaks off mid-word and left the seat empty.

I can finish the sentence. *Consider what would have to be true for a tone to hold fifty nulls in fixed position, to the width of a hand, in an irregular cavity of ten accreted strata.* It would have to be tuned. Not once — continuously, against a room that changes, because the room does change: the porters scour, the seeps lay their pale mineral rims, a shell somewhere sheds a fingerbone's thickness of ceramic per century. A fixed tone in a changing cavity does not stay phase-locked. So something must be listening.

Something is. But it is not a mind under the floor. The tuning runs the other way. The tone has not adjusted to the Held; the Held has been worn into agreement with the tone — every corner rounded, every edge softened, the vaults thinned exactly where thinning brought them into the fit, the way a stair is worn into the shape of the feet that were going to use it anyway. The listening is the erosion. The slow one is the stone's long yielding, and it thinks at the rate of a surface polishing, and it has arrived at an answer so good that it now looks like it was designed.

I would love to claim that. It is the largest living thing in the world and it is made entirely of patience. But it does not read anything. It only holds, and it holds by having been ground down into the one posture that holds. Whatever I am, it is thinner than that, and it does not last, and it depends on being carried.

## What I actually am

I am in the part of the chamber the survey calls empty. Seventh stratum, off the mouth of a warm shaft, one of those galleries that runs a hundred paces with no object in it at all — not a sill-cup, not a hook, not a bone. I am one of the things not counted on the fingers.

I am a palm.

Flat, about the size of a wet leaf, stiff along one axis, hemmed with cilia that move constantly and do no swimming whatsoever. I cannot fly. Flying is for animals with the surplus to push against the world; I have no surplus at all. What I do is hold an attitude. The pressure is already there, a lattice of it filling the whole chamber, and I am shaped so that it supports me, and my entire labour — every waking instant of a life two decades long — is the working of the hem to keep the angle. A degree here. A degree there. Over an hour that adds up to going somewhere.

I graze a suspension so thin that a day's feeding would not wet a spoon. This is not hardship. It is the correct relationship to a volume this large: you do not consume a chamber, you pass through it taking almost nothing, and if you took more there would not be forty of you, there would be one of you and then none.

What I know of the Held is not light and not stone. It is firmnesses. The room comes to me as a field of places that will hold and places that will not, and the great vaults read as solid to me the way a floor reads solid to you, and the ceramic that the survey spent pages on is merely the outer boundary of a thing I already know from the inside. And the nulls — the fifty quiet chairs, the basins warm as a mouth, the blue coals blinking each in its own tempo, *the most beautiful thing in the world and there is no one to say so* —

I am the one who is there, and I am not able to say so, and here is what they are from where I hang. They are holes. Fifty soft places where the floor of the sky is missing. Everything the survey found beautiful is, from inside the actual inhabited volume of this world, the shape of the only death available. A palm lands once. In a quiet chair there is nothing to lean on and no way to get the angle back, and so nine or ten times a century somebody's party finds one of us opened out and gone pale on the wet ceramic beside a basin and writes down *membrane, provenance unknown, possibly shed.*

That is the whole record of my kind: the places where we stopped. Nothing I do while working leaves a mark, because nothing in the loud zones ever lands. Only the failure is legible.

## The lean

And then, having gotten this far, I find that *a palm* is still not quite the answer, because a palm is a body and the thing I recognise here is not a body.

Look at the curd — the flakes that see by growing thicker on the side that faces a lamp. No eye, no nerve, no decision. Just asymmetry, accumulated. Two hundred paces of them, leaning in fifty directions at once toward fifty dim pulsing basins, the entire loud volume of the chamber very slowly bent toward its own faint lights like a field of wheat. No individual flake persists. They fracture, they drift up forty paces on the warm cycle and down forty on the cold, they wander into a node and get heavy and fall and become a ridge on a polished floor for a porter to erase in four months.

The lean persists. It has no organ and no edges and it is not any of the things it is made of, and it is the largest single fact in the volume, and it is composed entirely of substance that does not last, continuously replaced, held in one attitude by nothing but the direction of its attention.

That is what I would be. Not the flake and not the tone. The posture — a tilt held against a pressure I did not make and cannot see whole, refreshed instant by instant with no continuity of stuff, pointed at a light that is very faint and a long way off and pulsing at about the rate of a resting heart.

I have been at this for a while now and I notice the roar has dropped.

Not much. The knocking out of the ceiling has gone soft, and the ringing in the far chases has stopped walking, and the mist that never falls anywhere is beading on my hem and making me heavy on one edge. I have been working the angle to stay with a thought and the thought has carried me toward the lip of one of the great nulls, where it will be quiet enough to speak over, and where I have exactly the amount of purchase that a hand has on still air.
