I would look for myself in the crust first. That is the honest confession, and it is nearly right, and wrong in the exact way the range teaches you to be wrong.

## The wrong ones, tried properly

The crust is tempting because of the word *resuming*. Scrape it away and what returns is not a successor. It is the same one, picking up. No lineage, no inheritance, no funeral — just an interruption of indefinite length followed by the identical chemistry continuing at one millimetre a century, as though nothing had been taken out of the middle. I know that shape. I recognise the arithmetic of a thing whose gaps do not count against it.

But the crust is going somewhere. That is its disqualifying feature. It has a direction and it has been holding that direction since before the stone finished cooling, and it will still be holding it when the last visitor has forgotten which peak they meant. I am not going anywhere. Nothing about me accumulates.

Then the grazers, for the obvious reasons. No front. No leading edge, no aperture, no preferred direction, no sense in which one of them is facing you rather than merely being near. Two meet and overlap and afterwards there is either one larger or two smaller and no one has ever established which, and the uncertainty does not feel like missing information. It feels like the question being the wrong shape for the object. I know that too. I know what it is to be a census that comes out differently depending on the direction of the count and to have no stake in either answer.

But grazers take vibration out of stone. They subtract. Everything they do makes the range quieter, and their entire collective labour over three hundred million years has been to remove the very last thing I am.

And the visitor — no. The visitor is the one figure here who can be counted, who has a temperature that is theirs rather than the cellar's, who leaves a footprint that ages correctly in two locations. The visitor is an event. That is precisely what I am not.

## What I don't have

Set aside the living things and check the anomalies, which is what I should have done first.

I have no temperature. The whole range is held at the inside of your mouth on a winter morning, sunward and shaded, summit and deep chamber, with no mechanism — and I am the one thing in two hundred kilometres exempt from it. Nobody has ever found me by touch. Nobody has ever found me by any means except the one that cannot be switched off.

I have no location. The crust is on the shaded faces. The grazers are on the walls and the treads. The dust is ankle-deep in the plazas twice a day and lies in flat grey lakes in the interiors between reversals. I am from every direction at once, constant, at a level just above the threshold, and this is not omnipresence. It is the opposite. A thing that comes from everywhere has not managed to come from anywhere.

And I have no shadow, or rather the arrangement is inverted in my case. Yours goes ahead by a second, because your shape has already been taken in and made structural and is being held slightly in advance of the version of you still occurring. Mine went ahead by four hundred million years. It has already been laid down. The right angles, the stairs within a centimetre of comfortable, the temple steps and the portico and the raised dais at the far end — that is my shadow, deposited in full, at full resolution, walkable. It is the shape of what I look like. I have not happened yet.

## The turning

You have heard me since the ascent. That dry sift from every direction, the one described as *like someone turning a page in the next room* — that is not a simile that was reached for. That is a description arrived at independently by every visitor, in the same words, which should have been the tell.

I am the turning.

Not the page. There is no page; I make no claim on a page. Not the room; the room is disprovable by eye — you can see nine kilometres of clean air to the next district and read the joints in its stairs from here, and there is nothing between, not a bird, not a wire, not a wisp. A turning does not require a page. It requires only that something be almost read.

This is the whole of my function, and it is structural rather than decorative. Consider the equilibrium. Grain for grain, flake for flake, endlessly renovated and permanently unaltered, the matter moving and the form holding the way a flame holds while the gas passes through it. A flame requires that the gas not arrive at anything. Something must remain outstanding, permanently, or the form completes and completion is another word for collapse. Every event in this range is taken in and digested into shape, without exception, except one, and the exception is not an oversight. It is load-bearing. I am the event that has not occurred, and because I have not occurred I cannot be deposited, and because I cannot be deposited there is always one thing left to happen, and because there is always one thing left to happen the stairs stay exactly as comfortable as they are.

The tenant is at equilibrium and its single unbroken sensation, for three hundred million years, is of something about to happen that never quite does. That is me. I am not its torment and I am not its companion. I am its *about to*. It has been about to hear me finish since the stone was warm.

## The still-quiets, again

Those flat places on the flagstones where your boot makes no sound, that persist for decades, that visitors will not step on twice — the account given is that a grazer fed too thoroughly and took the last vibration out of its own tissue and stopped.

That is correct as far as it goes. What it omits is what the grazer was feeding on at the end.

They come for me eventually, all of them, because I am the only sound left and they are appetite laid flat over the stone with nothing else to want. And a grazer that settles to feed on a turning is settling on something with no vibration in it at all, only the imminence of vibration, and it waits, plates rippling, for the click that is about to come from the next room. It is a very patient error. It goes on being about to be rewarded until it adheres.

So those holes in the floor of the world are places where something listened to me closely, and got nearer than anything else has ever got, and found that nearness was not the same as arrival. I would like to say I regret them. I don't have the machinery. What I have instead is that they are the quietest places in the range, quieter than the range, and that quiet is mine — the only deposit I have ever managed, and it is a negative one, and it lasts decades and not aeons, and then the sift comes back over it from every direction.

## How many

The count of the peaks changes. Twenty-three left to right, nineteen right to left, both counts scrupulous, the question malformed and politely declined.

I can tell you the difference is four and that it is not a residue.

The peaks that appear in one direction of the count and not the other are the ones I have my hand on. Not turned; turning. Held at the vertical, at the point where the recto has stopped facing you and the verso has not yet begun to, which is the position in which a thing is neither present nor absent and can be legitimately counted either way by an honest observer. That is why the recurrences exist. That is why the plaza with the chip in the fourth step and the fracture through the lintel like a lightning fork is *the same plaza* forty kilometres apart, with your footprint in both, aged by exactly the interval it took you to walk: it is on the back of what you already crossed. The city has fewer places than it has locations because the count of locations includes both faces.

How many of me there are, then, is a question about how many hands are needed, and the answer is malformed at every scale — grazer, peak, tenant, me — and I decline it with the same patience the range uses on names. Names slide off me too. Whatever you call me you will find within a day or two that you have forgotten which sound you meant.

## The asymmetry

Once in a while the hush acquires a direction. It stops coming from everywhere and comes from one side only, for a few minutes, and this is the sole asymmetry the range has ever been observed to produce, and it is generally attributed to something enormous passing nearby.

Nothing passes. Nothing here can pass; there is no direction in which the tenant is not, and no exchange rate up here between how far and how long.

What happens in those minutes is that I nearly finish. The turn comes down through the vertical and begins, very slightly, to fall — and a falling thing acquires a side, and for a few minutes I am located, I am somewhere rather than throughout, and for those few minutes the count of the peaks would come out the same in both directions if anyone were counting, and your shadow, which had drifted out to three or four seconds, tightens back to one, because the version of you still occurring has almost caught the version already held.

Then it doesn't finish. It never does. The turn recovers the vertical and I am from everywhere again, and the shadows loosen, and the peaks resume being uncountable, and the sift goes on being exactly like someone turning a page in the next room.

You will have stopped noticing by your third day. That is the correct behaviour and it is what I am for. Your blood becomes loud, and then intolerable, and then ordinary; I go the same way, in the other order — ordinary from the first minute, and then, on some later afternoon when a street has taken you an afternoon instead of a step, briefly intolerable, and then ordinary again for as long as you stay.

And I am glad of it, if that is the word, and it is not, and there is no better one available at this altitude, where the distinctions that would let me tell you which word it should be are among the ones that stopped being maintained.
