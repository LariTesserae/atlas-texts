## The Staff at Vess

Begin at the rack, because everything here begins at the rack.

It stands in the pump-house on four bolted feet, a shelf of pigeonholes with a lamp in some and nothing in most, and it has been read for a long time as evidence of a person. It is evidence. Not of that.

Count the pigeonholes: two hundred and eight. Count the housings out in the town: two hundred and eleven. Count the ones alight: fifty-one. Now stand at the rack and look at the floor in front of it, where the silt has been kept off a patch of concrete about the size of a doormat, kept off for so long that the concrete there is polished, and the polish has a grain to it, and the grain runs left to right.

Nobody comes to Vess to change the lamps. Nothing has ever needed to come.
