## The Tenancy

**1. The census**

Begin with the difficulty, which is not shyness. It is that nothing here is removed.

A carrier that has stopped walking stays exactly as it was when it stopped: legs folded, setae intact, the sheen still on it. It does not sink, gum, discolour, or attract anything, because there is nothing here whose living is made of other things' endings. There are no flies. There is no smell. A body is simply a very still animal, and the Draft offers no way to tell the difference, and neither will you.

So the population of the Draft is somewhere between four hundred thousand and rather more, and the figure changes depending on which direction you count in, and both figures are correct, and this bothers nobody, and nobody is a resident here of long standing.

Everything below is either provisional or unfinished. There is one exception, and it is section 6.

**2. The carriers**

Thumb-sized. Pale as candle-wax, blunt-headed, eight legs where six would do — the plant has not settled that either. Pads of setae under each foot, which is why they make no sound: not silence achieved, silence engineered, thousands of hair-tips touching down out of phase so the click never accumulates.

Each one carries a single drop of water on its back, held in a shallow dorsal saddle, roughly grape-sized, curved to a clean meniscus.
