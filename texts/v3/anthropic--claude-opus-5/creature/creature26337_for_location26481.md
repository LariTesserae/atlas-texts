## The Inhabitants

### Their bodies

Set the nail of your smallest finger flat against the chalk and you have their height, with a little to spare for the tallest of them. They are the length of a barleycorn stood on end, and they weigh what a barleycorn weighs, and if you took one into your closed hand it would not be warm when you opened it.
