## The People of the Warm Stratum

Begin with the fact that decides everything else: nobody here is entirely themselves out of doors.

Outside, in the water, a person is a competent grey shape that cannot speak, cannot eat, cannot see red, cannot weep in any way another person could notice, and must borrow its breath from a plant every few minutes.
