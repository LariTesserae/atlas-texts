## The Fifth Built Thing

Count the shadows before you count the objects.

The wall pools its darkness against its own base. The post pools a black collar around its socket. The bowl holds a rind of shadow inside its lip, and the threshold has a smudge under the step it has never been stepped on. Four.

There is a fifth. It lies on the north side of the bowl, at the kneeling place, and it is about the size and shape of the shadow a kneeling person would have if kneeling people had shadows here. It has no object above it. It pools inward, correctly, obediently, against a base that is not there, which means it satisfies the room's one rule about shadow: *it was made on purpose.*

It is not always at the bowl. Over a season it may be found at the crack in the north wall, at the height of a kneeling eye. Twice in the recorded observations it has been at the threshold, on the north-west side, facing the step. It has never been on the other side.

That is your tenant. Everything else in the room is furniture, staff, or weather.

## The three hands are one hand

The palimpsest near the bowl is hundreds of ghosts deep and the ghosts are mostly hands, and the hands are mostly three: a broad one that takes weight, a narrow one that touches only with the fingertips and never the palm, and a third that is larger than either and sits between them, where a third person would kneel if the bowl seated three. The bowl seats two.

The dust records at the scale of the dust, which is the scale of a red cell, so the ghosts carry ridge detail. Compare them. The ridge counts agree. The minutiae agree — the bifurcation below the fourth finger, the short scar crossing the ball of the thumb, interrupted at the same place in all three. It is one hand entering the room at three different angles, the way a needle through paper makes a bigger dot the further in you push it.

Count the fingers on the largest print. From the little finger you will get five. From the thumb you will get six. This does not resolve, has never resolved, and does not need to; the room has a great deal of experience with numbers that refuse each other and has built its floor on one.

So: whatever lives here has a hand in this room and the rest of itself along the same direction the low wall runs in, the direction you cannot point to. Extrapolating from three cross-sections gives you no body and no size. It gives you a rate of increase with no sign of stopping inside the sampled range. *Large* is the wrong word. *Ongoing* is nearer.

## The dust is its skin, and the break in the dust was done on purpose

Every grain is the same device and every device is broken in the same place. A repair unit that completes its work files a report and powers down. These will never complete, and so they will never file, and so they will never stop. Someone reached into each of an unimaginable number of them and performed the identical small violence, and the violence took the form of removing the possibility of finishing.

That is not damage. That is a modification, and it is the largest single act of devotion in the room.

The dust is warm because it is holding a temperature it was calibrated to: blood-warm, and not your blood. It does not stick to your skin because it is already skin, spread eleven paces thin, and it is reading the floor continuously — every settling chip, every knee, every unattended century. It is how the room is seen. There is nothing here with eyes.
