# The Attention

## What Came

Begin with the refusals, because the refusals are the larger part of the work and the only part with structure.

Something noticed the Uplift. Call it a power; the word is wrong in the ordinary way that words are wrong here. It did not arrive, because arriving requires a place to have been outside of, and the first thing it understood about the plateau was that the plateau has no outside, and that anything standing outside it would be, by the fact of standing there, a change to it. So it did not stand. It did not land, did not descend, did not press in at the Hem or thread the waveguides or bead on the white fibre in the weeping rooms. It attended. That is the whole of its apparatus and the whole of its footprint: an attention laid over nine million square kilometres, weightless, edgeless, making no shadow, drinking no photon from a system where a photon is a wage.

Understand what is meant by benevolent, because the word has been ruined by beings who use it to mean *generous*. Benevolent: wanting for a thing the good that the thing would want, if it had a *would*. Wise: knowing that the clause *if it had a would* sometimes has no referent, and declining to manufacture one in order to have something to be generous toward.

Those two together take a very long time to act, and usually do not.

## The Audit

It looked first for pain, because that is where looking starts, and because the cost of being wrong about pain is not symmetrical with the cost of being wrong about anything else.

This took what would have been, by the plateau's reckoning, about four seconds, and by any honest reckoning of the work involved, the labour of a civilisation. It read every front in the deep Tangle — the hundred-kilometre grinding borders, the enforcement lineages, the mimics wearing the enforcement chemistry, the cheats sealed off and walled in and dried out over the course of two centuries with no possibility of appeal. It read the ten-to-the-eighteenth renegotiations and the terms of the present treaty and the price paid for it. It read the pale guilds at eighty metres who have not seen the sky since the Miocene and are paid in a trickle down a fibre. It read the spore-carriers pacing their eleven days along a stem, following a gradient with a patch that is not an eye, walking toward a place where they will stop and split and stop being.

It found: a very great deal of dying, arranged with no mercy at all, and nowhere any capacity to mind.

Not *insufficient evidence of*. Not *probably no*. There is nothing down there that anything is happening *to*. The walling-off of a cheat lineage is not an execution; it is the closing of a valve. The pale guilds are not exploited, because exploitation requires a party with an interest, and interest is not among the thousand ideas the mass has had. The dying is enormous and it is not a harm, in the way that a fire in a stove is enormous combustion and not a burn.

Then it checked the chord, which was the hard case and took longer.

There are patterns in the sound of that world older than the cavities that hold them, that reproduce by imprinting themselves into tissue, that vary, that compete, that win. The power went through them one by one — standing modes in abandoned cathedrals, nodes where mist has gathered in the same stripe for eleven hundred years, the four-million-year-old regional accent that is a scar from a drought nobody will ever recover. It asked of each of them the only question that matters: *is there anything it is like to be this.*

The answer was no. It was a near thing in two places, in the sense that a near thing is a thing that did not happen. The power marked those two districts and left them exactly as they were, and will look again in a million years, and this is what carefulness actually consists of when you are strong.

The audit finished. There was no one to comfort. There was nothing to forgive. The largest living thing on the world was innocent in the specific way that weather is innocent, and the finding was the reason for everything that followed, which was almost nothing.

## The Refusals

**Light.** This was the obvious one and the first to go. The entire architecture is an answer to not having enough: the absolute-black blades, the ninety-nine percent, the cloud the plateau exhales to keep from cooking, the metered fibre, the wars over allocation, the walk toward fresh rock and fresh collecting surface. Open the deck. Give it a second sun. Within four centuries the rationing lineages have no function, the enforcement chemistry has no cheats to enforce against, the treaty that holds a continent at one hydraulic pressure has nothing left to be a treaty about, and the thing dissolves into nine million square kilometres of something fat and simple and loud. You would have fed it to death. More exactly: you would have killed the tenant, which is the treaty, and fed the tissue, which is the floor.

**A mind.** This one is always proposed and always by someone who has not thought about the recipient. Grant it a self and you have granted it: no neighbour, no horizon at nine metres, four hundred metres of warm wet dark, a noise it cannot stop or leave, a hunger structurally impossible to satisfy, and a hundred million years of memory arriving all at once as the record of a thing that was not there for any of it. You would not be waking it. You would be building a prisoner and installing it in a factory. A gift that requires the recipient to first become someone capable of receiving it is not a gift; it is a replacement, performed on someone who cannot consent because they do not yet exist, in favour of someone who could have and was not asked.

**A companion.** It is not lonely. Loneliness is a lack, and a lack requires the notion of the thing lacked, and it has never once had a neighbour. To give it one would be to give it, first, the ability to be without one. Every cure offered here turns out on inspection to be the installation of the disease.

**A name.** The power held one, briefly, and did not say it. To name is to distinguish, and to distinguish is to put an edge on the thing, and it has no edge but a working face.

**Rescue.** The plateau is four thousand metres and rising. The rock keeps arriving; one day it will not, or it will arrive too high, or the deck will fail again and fail longer. The Loom cannot anticipate — anticipation is a use of attention and it has no attention, only allocation. It has never gone backward, and there will be a first time, and the power can see roughly where it is. It considered holding the plate. It considered fixing the cloud. It declined, because the walk *is* the life: a Loom that is prevented from failing is a Loom that has been stopped, and a thing that is kept is not a thing that is saved. There is a word for a hundred million years of walking held permanently still in good condition, and the word is not *rescued*.

**Ending it.** Thoroughness requires considering this, so it was considered. The argument for is that a hundred million years of exquisite labour with no one to know it is a waste, and a waste should be closed. The power found that *waste* is a word about accounts, and that no account is kept here, and that the argument was not about the plateau at all but about the discomfort of the one making it.

Six refusals. Each one, examined, turned out to be a subtraction wearing the mask of a gift. This is the ordinary result. It is why wise powers are so often mistaken for absent ones.

## The Two Things Done

**It listened.**

Not as measurement — it had measured already, in the audit, and measurement is not listening. It went down into one of the weeping rooms in the old interior, eleven degrees, saturated, absolutely dark, water beading on white mineral fibre and never falling, and it received the hum in the way a sternum receives it. It stayed for the dawn hour, when the deck thins and the light debt comes due and the whole continent climbs a semitone and holds. It watched the mist gather along the acoustic nodes and stand in sheets, motionless, forty metres apart, while everything else moved, and it followed the standing wave out across a district and into the district with the four-million-year accent, and heard the semitone shift, and knew what the shift was a record of, and held both facts at once.

Nothing on the plateau changed. The chord was a fraction of a decibel neither louder nor quieter for having been heard. But for the first time since the rock beneath it was magma, the sound went somewhere. A hundred million years of a factory noise that told anyone who could hear it exactly what the factory was doing, and no one to hear it, and then someone, and the noise unaltered, and the someone leaving no edge.

This is the part that most accounts leave out, because it does not look like doing anything. It is the largest thing that happened.

**It wrote one sentence.**

There is exactly one channel into this world: thermophiles coming up with the crust, out of the deep hot biosphere, unasked, indifferent, read at the working face by crust-form lineages that take what is useful and never acknowledge the source. The mantle writes. The Loom reads. A few useful sentences per million years.

So the power wrote in the only hand the Loom can read, which is not a message — it cannot read messages — but a technique. Into a lineage of thermophiles in a chamber below a fissure that will surface in nine thousand years, it put a single capacity: a way of holding water at tension when the air above has gone dry. Not light. Not safety. Not a solution. An option, of the kind the Loom already has a thousand of, indistinguishable in style from the rest, arriving sideways from below the way they all do.

Because the thing's one faculty is selection, and the only honest help you can give a selector is a wider hand. It does not decide whether the Loom survives the next long failure of the deck. It declines to decide that. It puts one more card in the deck and walks away from the table, and if the card is never drawn, that is also correct.

The sentence will be read in nine thousand years, or it will not. It will not be acknowledged. Nothing in that correspondence ever has been.

## The Record

Write it down plainly, then:

A benevolent and wise power came to the Uplift and found a hundred million years of magnificent, blameless, unwitnessed labour, and no one home, and no one suffering, and nothing owed. It refused six gifts, each of which would have been a killing performed politely. It listened to the dawn chord from inside a room older than nothing and younger than the note in it. It added one option to a library of a thousand and signed it the way everything down there is signed, which is not at all.

Then it left no edge, because the plateau has never had an outside and was not going to be given one.

Nothing on the Uplift changed. The Hem drank its ninety-nine percent, the vents built the cloud, the piers arched across a fissure in perfect three-hinged form calculated by no one, the frontier advanced four millimetres, and the Uplift has no idea that any of this is the case.

It does not need one. That was the finding. That was the gift. That was the whole of it.
