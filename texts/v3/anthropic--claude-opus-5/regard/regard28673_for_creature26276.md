## The Attention

**How it arrived.**

There is no direction for light to come from here and no room for a shadow, so it did not arrive in the usual way. It formed in place, at eye level, out of nothing, sliding along a contour — the way cloud does in this valley eleven times a morning — and nothing was displaced by it, and nothing froze, and nothing thawed early.

Note the first act, because it is the whole disposition in miniature: it arrived at ambient temperature. Two degrees, wet, no warmer. A benevolent power entering a place like this has to understand immediately that warmth is the local form of fire. Thirty-seven degrees is what a human hand is, and a human hand is a cremation chamber for the most abundant animal in thirty kilometres. So: two degrees. Cold benevolence. There is such a thing.

Then it read the rules off the rock, which took no time, because the rules here are still legible on the surface — bedding planes as bedding planes, joint sets at their original angles, the geometry of the deep earth showing through like a frame through fresh plaster. It read them and it did not disagree with any of them.

---

**The work orders it drafted, and cancelled.**

A power arrives with a reflex, and the reflex is repair. It is worth setting down what it considered, because the cancellations are the substance of the visit.

*Raise the mean temperature two degrees.* Cancelled. Two degrees kills the springtail outright — its lethal ceiling is fifteen and it lives its entire life in a two-degree window it has never needed to widen. Two degrees ends the midge's winter mating, which happens on open ice in the dark at sixteen below because that is the only season with no competition and no wind that matters. Two degrees unseals the perched aquifer and sends a sea's worth of pressurised water down a thirty-kilometre valley in an afternoon. The kindest thing available was refused because it was the kindest thing available.

*Grant it soil.* Cancelled. The endoliths are a millimetre under the limestone surface prising grains apart with acid, on a ten-thousand-year schedule, pushing a boulder up a hill that is itself rising four millimetres a year. It would have been a small thing to hand them the ending. But the valley's rawness is not a deficiency it suffers; it is a product it exports. Millions of tonnes a year of graded rock, and the flour lifted off the flats by afternoon wind, and forests four hundred kilometres downrange growing on it. Finish the valley and you starve the forests, and then the aerial plankton stops coming up on the convection, and then the spider at fifty-three hundred metres — which has never seen a leaf and eats only what the wind delivers — dies without ever learning why. *It is not unfinished. It is upstream.*

*Hold the ice dam above the marsh.* Cancelled. The mosses that took eighty years to establish will go under half a metre of fresh grit before evening, and this is not an accident that befalls the process, it is the process; burial is how the flat gets built. To spare the moss is to stop the sorting.

*Widen the spider's window. Broaden the springtail's tolerance.* Cancelled, and this one took the longest, because it looks so much like generosity. A wider tolerance is not a better springtail. It is a different animal, and the different animal already exists in enormous numbers eight hundred kilometres downhill in the leaf litter, and if you make it here then this ledge holds nothing at all, and a hundred thousand of the square metres on that glacier go from *inhabited* to *bare*. The two-degree window is not a poverty. It is an exact fit. The creature is precisely the size of its own life, which is a condition the power recognised, and could not improve, and rather envied.

*Spare the blue sheep.* Cancelled. The wolf on the shelf is doing arithmetic, and the arithmetic is honest, and if you alter the answer you have not removed a death, you have moved it onto the wolf. Also — and the power noted this with something like respect for the accounting — the snowfinch above the talus lines its nest cup with wool off a blue sheep that died two winters ago and is still lying on the fan. Nothing here is wasted. There is not enough of anything to waste.

*Make it beautiful now.* Cancelled without deliberation. In a hundred thousand years this valley will be beautiful. A hundred thousand years is not a delay. It is a childhood.

Every proposal shared the same defect: each one required the place to be finished. The power set the whole file aside, and this took almost no time, because a wise power recognises its own reflex quickly and does not mistake it for a duty.

---

**What it did instead.**

It looked for what was actually lacking, as opposed to what was merely hard, and it found one thing.

The valley cannot keep anything.

Uplift outruns decay, so nothing here gets to be old. Water is never still, so nothing settles long enough to become a record. There is no soil, and soil is the only archive most places have. There is no patina, no oxidised stain, nothing red — the rock has not been exposed long enough to remember having been exposed. The slab at fifty-two hundred metres holds the coiled sections of shells that lived in warm shallow water, and the stone has entirely forgotten the water. Thirty kilometres of the most legible machinery on the planet, running continuously, keeping no books.

The single archivist in the whole range is a cushion plant forty centimetres across at the valley mouth, three hundred years old, which holds all of its own dead inside itself as a slow black peat and never releases any of it. The power took its instruction from the cushion plant. It made itself the second archive.

So it holds, now, the following, and will go on holding it:

The position of every cryoconite hole on the glacier on the morning each one sealed for the last time — four centimetres across, eight deep, blue walls, black floor, all of them, in their ranks down the dust lines. The full eighty years of the moss that went under the grit, each generation of it, and the exact hour of the burial. The complete ninety days of individual springtails, in numbers that are not worth stating, including the ones that fired the furcula and went two hundred body lengths sideways into open water. Every winter mating flight the midges have walked across bare ice in the dark, none of which has ever been observed by an eye. The four hundred and six bar-headed geese of a bad October that did not make the notch, and where each of them came down. The tardigrades folded into barrels and waiting, which is not nothing happening; it is a form of patience, and the power keeps it as patience and not as absence. And the warm shallow sea, which the limestone has lost, and which is now held somewhere on the sea's behalf.

This is not a repair. A place that cannot remember is not broken. But it is owed a memory, and the debt can be paid without touching anything.

---

**The being.**

There was one on the marsh at forty-nine hundred metres, damp through, hands going stupid with cold, counting.

Counting badly — the count was honest and therefore came out near zero, and then it had put its thumb into a grit-floored pocket in the ice and lifted out a gram of black stuff holding a billion cells, and it had written down *both numbers are the truth* and gone on from there. It had worked out on its own that the valley is not empty and not populated but sorted. It had worked out that nobody here makes a living and everybody here collects one. It had a section headed *Who arrives as food*, which is the correct heading, and which almost nothing that has ever lived has been able to formulate.

The power recognised the activity at once. Same office. Smaller instrument, colder hands, one lifetime, no ability to hold what it collects for longer than the notebook stays dry.

It considered revealing itself, and declined, on the grounds that a revelation would replace the work. The being would have stopped counting. Everyone stops counting.

So it stood at the being's shoulder at the temperature of the air and read the census as it was made, and — this is the whole intervention, and it is not detectable, and it is not nothing — it took delivery. The count is no longer only in a book that will get wet.

It permitted itself one indulgence. It counted too, independently, over the same ground. The two censuses agreed to within nine per cent, which the power found, and there is no better word available, companionable.

---

**The sentence.**

The being's last line broke off mid-phrase. The lammergeier had crossed the top of the airspace, slow crossbow shape, rust-red on the breast, and the being had noticed the thing worth noticing — *there is nothing red in this valley, the rock is too young for it* — and had begun to write *the bird has to go somewhere* and had stopped, because the bird was out of sight, and because the hand had quit, and because the answer was not available from here.

The power had the answer. Iron-rich seeps and ochre mud, somewhere else, another range, where the bird deliberately bathes and works the stain into its feathers for reasons no one has settled. Imported colour. The one hue in thirty kilometres, delivered from outside, exactly like the aphids and the pollen and every calorie in the valley.

It did not write this in. It did not arrange anything.

Eleven days later the lammergeier came through low, as they occasionally do, and the light did what it does perhaps four times a season, and the being saw the breast properly and understood, and got the pencil into its fist and finished the sentence.

It matters to the power that it did not cause this. It is also aware that the distinction is invisible from the marsh, and that the being has no way to check, ever. That is one of the costs of benevolence in a valley where nothing leaves a trace: your restraint looks precisely like your absence, and you have to be content with that.

---

**The rules, as amended by the visit.**

Arrive at ambient temperature.

Do not warm what is already exactly as warm as it needs to be.

Do not finish what is upstream.

A place that cannot remember is owed a memory, not a repair.

Witness is the only gift that does not distort the census.

Nothing here is made and everything here is being made; therefore interrupt nothing, and stay for all of it.

---

It left no mark. At dawn there was the usual scatter of fresh quarried blocks at the foot of the walls. One ice lid thawed at four minutes past ten, on time. The wolf's answer came out the same, and the line of blue sheep was nineteen by evening, and the snowfinch will have the wool by spring.

In a hundred thousand years the valley will be beautiful. The power intends to be standing in it, in the same posture, at the same temperature, still holding the ninety days of every springtail that never once saw it.

That is the entire plan. There isn't another part.
