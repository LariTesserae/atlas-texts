## The Third Saying

**1.**

Attention came to the plain the way the light comes: from the whole sky at once, from no source you could point at, casting nothing.

This was chosen. A power that arrives from a direction gives a world a direction, and this world has one already and does not need a second. So there was no descent and no figure on the ramp and no sign in the shell.
