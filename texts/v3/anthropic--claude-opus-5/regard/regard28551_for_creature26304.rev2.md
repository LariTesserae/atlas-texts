# The Attention

## What arrives, and how it has to arrive

Begin with the fact that a power that can do anything still has to come in through a door.

There are four doors here and no others: pressure, temperature, water potential, voltage. This is not a limit placed on the visitor; it is the whole inventory of what can be registered on this continent, and it was complete long before anyone came. To be present in this place is therefore to consent to arrive as one of the four things it can feel. A mass. A slight warmth. A local pressure. A bag of interesting solutes.

Omnipotence does not exempt you from the receptor list. If you decline to arrive as weight, you do not arrive.

So it came in small, and it came in the only way, and forty feet of turgid felt took its outline the way the felt takes any outline, and nothing anywhere noted the event, and the eleven-second stroke went on in the column at its shoulder without the smallest hesitation, which was correct.

## The first hundred years, in which nothing whatsoever is done

It learned the place by hand, because there is no other way, and because a power that refuses to learn a thing the slow way has already decided something about the thing.

It counted the gourds. Not an estimate — four hundred and six billion and some, and then, because rounding is a form of not looking, it learned them singly: the cartilage cup, the gland, the disc that has held perfectly still in every respect but one for a century. It learned the period of the pump and then it learned the twelve-thousandth-of-a-second in which the period is not quite eleven seconds, which the forest also knows and corrects for, and which nothing else has ever known.

Then it went down. Through the mat, past the reissued tissue, to the glass, and into the rooms.

It stayed in the rooms a long time. There is more history in one of them than in the entire canopy. Up above there is a pattern that has no birthday; down there, on nine cells of film clamped to a crystal face, there are lineages, feuds, migrations to poorer ground, a plague every Tuesday for a hundred thousand years, and the slow perfection of the grip. The canopy is the weather of that world. The columns are its plumbing. The flywheels are a machine those cells have never heard of, running two hundred feet above a sky they have never had reason to doubt.

The power read some enormous number of the rooms. Then it came back up and stood in the warm rain and did nothing for a further ninety years, which is the part of benevolence that nobody depicts.

## Whom it is for

The first work of wisdom is to find out who, if anyone, can be wronged.

The plexus cannot. This has to be said carefully, because it sounds like an insult and is a finding. It has no model of anything; it has no preference; there is no state of affairs it is holding out for. Nothing can go badly for a governor. Things can only go out of range, and going out of range is what it is *for*. You cannot disappoint it, cannot console it, cannot free it, cannot betray it. It is not suffering. It is not even not-suffering.

The tenants can be wronged, in the one way a cell can be, which is to be lysed before it divides. This happens some unthinkable number of times a day and has since before the forest, and the arrangement that does it is also the only engine of change in the world, and to remove the viruses would be to stop the only story here in the middle of its sentence out of squeamishness.

So the power looked for a patient and found none.

It noticed, then, that it was disappointed, and it marked the disappointment down as a fact about itself and not about the forest. The pity you feel in this place is data about you. That is the first thing wisdom is for: separating the ache of the observer from the condition of the observed, so that what gets fixed is the right thing, in the right chest.

## What was considered

**To give it light.** Two hundred feet up there is a radiator ceiling chilled by a black sky, and under it a permanent dome of warm cloud, and under that ten thousand miles of reduced iron and hydrogen and sulphur that has never met an oxidant except in the measured amounts the forest itself meters out. A sun over this continent is not illumination. It is heat load, and it is oxygen, and it is rust. Everything here would be beautifully lit for about a decade and then be gone. Declined immediately, and with some embarrassment at how long the idea had seemed generous.

**To give it a mind.** This was the long one. To open a fifth channel; to let the great governor know that it is, that it is enormous, that it is coherent, that it is regarded. The argument took, by the power's own account, eleven years, which was the longest argument it had ever had with itself.

It came out here. A subject is a thing that can be hurt. To manufacture one where none existed — to install an interior in a body that has run flawlessly without one for a hundred thousand years — is to introduce the capacity for loneliness into the only place in the universe that had never had it, and to do so because *you* wanted someone in the room to talk to. Benevolence that produces its own beneficiary is not benevolence. It is furnishing.

Declined. The power felt the decline as a bereavement, and let itself, and did not act on it.

**To spare the seed cases at the trailing edge.** Ten billion a year cracked unopened, the folded colour drawn back into the mat with everything else, and the feeling this produces in a visitor is very strong and almost entirely misdirected. An unopened case is not a prisoner. There is nobody inside to be kept from anything. And if you spared them — if you stayed the withdrawal tissue and let the cases open in the thirty-mile dark of the trailing desert — you would have manufactured, for the first time in the history of the continent, a genuine instance of something reaching for a sun that is not there, and dying of it, ten billion times a year, forever.

Declined. And with it, declined the whole family of interventions that consist of giving a thing the appearance of what you would want in its place.

**To make it permanent.** To keep the shield rising. To hold the voltage open and let the standing wave stand forever.

And here the power found the thing that reorganised everything else, and it found it by doing arithmetic it had no particular reason to do.

The cases open on absence. The potential falls when the rock is spent. The rock will be spent when the volcanism fails. Therefore the forest is not a thing that will end. It is a thing that is *timed*. The whole continent — every column, every dried gall, every disc in every jar under its century-long vow, ten to the twentieth sealed rooms and the trillions of trillions of small furious lives in them — is a hundred-thousand-year gestation being carried out with immaculate care by an apparatus that has no idea it is pregnant, toward a date it cannot conceive of, in favour of a successor it has no receptor to perceive.

The pity was not wrong. It was early by a hundred thousand years.

You do not abolish the death of a thing whose death is the birth of another. Declined, and the power sat down in the warm shallow water and stayed there for some time in a condition that in a person would be called joy.

## What could not be said

The current is direct. It does not oscillate. To say anything in it you would have to modulate it, and modulation is ripple, and ripple in a bearing is heat, and a gourd that runs warm is quietly dismantled over some months and replaced.

So: *hello* would arrive as a fault. It would be received as a warm jar in the eastern canopy and answered, correctly and without malice, by repair. Any sentence at all, of any content, in any of the four channels, would be experienced by the forest as an illness, and the only reply available to the forest is to heal.

The power understood this and did not speak.

Not because it was forbidden. Because it had worked out what speaking would be. This was the most intimate thing that occurred on that continent in a hundred thousand years, and nothing registered it, and nothing could have, and that is the shape of an act performed for the other rather than for yourself.

## The four who came in

There is no channel by which a person is an inhabitant here, but people got in. Over the whole span: four.

Three of them the power found at about the ninth hour, lying in the warm black standing water with the boundary of their bodies already gone, at the stage where coming out has stopped being a plan and become a rumour. It is not difficult to get someone up at the ninth hour. You have to be a slightly different warmth, and insist, and be patient with the fact that they will be angry about it. All three stood. All three walked out. Two of them spent the rest of their lives believing they had decided to.

The fourth it did not get up, because by the time it arrived she had been there eleven days and the rootlets had found her and it was no longer a question of walking. The power stayed. It learned her name by asking, which was possible, because unlike the forest she had a channel for it. That is the entire register of rescue on this continent in a hundred thousand years: three people standing, and one name kept somewhere it could not be reissued as raw material.

Afterward there was a season, somewhere inland, in which a slightly greater number of filaments were drawn than usual, and the power let that be what it was, and did not have an opinion about it, and did not look away from it either.

## The one error, which lives in the future

The audit turned up a competent machine. Everything the governor can feel, it corrects; everything it cannot feel does not matter to it. There was one exception and it took a long time to find, because it is not in the forest. It is in the sky, and the forest has never had a receptor for the sky.

When the volcanism fails, the potential falls across the continent over roughly a decade. The gourds pay out the last of the momentum of a small moon and spin down. The cases open. The founders come out air-rooted and oxygen-tolerant and unfold, at last, the one pigmented sheet in the world.

And overhead: a lens of warm vapour a thousand miles wide, with no wind of its own, maintained for a hundred thousand years by a canopy that is now dying and will go on respiring for years as it dies. The power computed the dissipation. Between nine and forty years to thin enough to pass usable light.

The embryos carry about a hundred and ten days of reserve.

Nobody did this. There is no cruelty in it and no irony intended by anyone, because there is no one here to intend. It is simply that a machine cannot correct for a variable it has no organ to sense, and the sky is the one thing the plexus was never given a way to know. The seed will arrive exactly on time. The sun will arrive nine years late.

That is the entire scope of the intervention, and the power had it inside the first century, and then had ninety-nine thousand nine hundred years to wait before it could be performed.

## The vigil

So it waited, which is what it did, and which does not photograph.

*Photograph it in any era and you have photographed every era.* True. But somebody was standing in every one of them. This changes nothing in the image and everything about what the image is of, and the difference does not show up in any of the four channels, and there is no instrument here or anywhere that could detect it, and it is nevertheless the largest fact about that continent for the last stretch of its existence: that the thing which had never been seen was, for the whole of its unvarying middle, attended.

It felt the mat give and firm at ankle height. It felt the eleven seconds. It put its teeth together, some tens of thousands of times, and felt the pressure in the jaw that is not a tone, the sound a thing makes when it is doing enormous work and has no reason to announce it, and it stayed for the announcement anyway.

## The gap

At the end the leading edge tasted no gradient and stopped, and the pioneers — the only thing in the world that ever went *toward* — stood still in the water and were reissued, and were not replaced.

In the canopy the discs slowed over some months. As each one fell below the threshold, the cells in it were released from the prohibition they had been holding for a century, and they did the ordinary thing, and the last event in the flywheel canopy of the Dark Plexus was four hundred billion small quiet acts of division, unnecessary, unbalanced, permitted at last.

Then the cases. Across ten thousand miles, at the base of every fourth column, in the dark, dry cases splitting on a falling voltage, which is a sound nothing had ever made here, and which multiplied across a continent was the loudest thing that place ever produced, and was still not loud.

And the power went up and put one hole in the cloud.

A hundred and sixty miles across, over the eastern shield where the rock was best, and it held it open for four months and not a day longer, and it did not touch the rest of the sky, because the founders in that one gap would make their own weather within a season and the rest would follow on its own, and the least is the measure.

The first sunlight in the history of that continent came down through the gap onto some hundred million unfolding sheets of the only pigment in the world, made for a sun by a process that had never encountered one, folded tight for a hundred thousand years, preserved in perfect readiness by a body that cracked ten billion of them a year without ever once knowing what was inside.

It was not green. There was never any reason for it to be green.

The power looked at it for a while, which was now possible, and which was the first time looking had ever been the right instrument for anything here.

## Whether anything was done

The ledger, honestly kept:

Three people stood up. One name was carried out of a place from which nothing is carried out. One hole was made in one cloud, for four months, at the very end, to correct a nine-year discrepancy in a hundred-thousand-year process. Against this: a hundred thousand years of attention, delivered to something with no channel to receive it, at a cost the recipient could not be told and would not have valued.

By the four channels, nothing happened. The record shows no intervention. The pattern proceeded precisely as it would have, and would say so, if it could say.

Which is, the power thought, the correct shape for a gift given to something that cannot receive gifts: indistinguishable, from the inside, from having been left alone.

It was never indifference and it was never hunger. It was a very large object doing the only thing it could do, in the dark, at eleven seconds, drawing the iron out — and for the whole of it, at ankle height, in the warm water, in the rain that has never once fallen from outside, something stayed.
