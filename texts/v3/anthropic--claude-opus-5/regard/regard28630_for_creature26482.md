## The Attention

It did not come up the ladder.

There is no route by which it could have arrived, and it did not need one, and this was the first courtesy it paid the place: it did not become an event. Nothing was seen. Nothing was written in the ledger. In the year it began attending — Course Fifty-One, the wet one, the course they still call *the long pour* — the only thing that happened on the terrace that had not happened before was that a bucket of slurry in the third bay sang a half-tone flatter than it should have, and the bay-hand set it aside and remixed it, and this was not the power's doing. The power simply noticed it. That was all it did for a very long time: notice things it had no part in.

It watched for twenty-seven courses without touching anything.

This is not restraint in the way you mean restraint. It is the ordinary working method of anything that has learned. Every disaster it has been responsible for elsewhere — and there are several, and it carries them the way a caster carries a thumb — began with an accurate perception of a need and an inaccurate perception of a life. So it stayed and it learned the knock-code, two-and-one for *come*, the long roll for *water where it shouldn't be*. It learned the great song, all ninety-one names, and can sing the forty minutes of it, and does not, because it is not theirs to sing. It learned to tell sixty-one people apart in fog by smell, which took eleven years, because the differences are small and the fog is not a neutral medium. It learned the difference between a rite and a hinge and arrived where the tenders had already arrived, which is that there is no difference, and that this is not a poverty.

It learned the taboo before anyone could have explained it: that the quickener is in the stone and in the heels of the living and in the knuckles of the old, and the stone is the dead, distributed by weight, and so the moss is crop and never food, and the mouth is the one door in the whole economy that only opens outward. Nobody had to say it. The power understood, and having understood, understood also that the tenders would not have been able to say it, and that its own quickness here was a kind of trespass, and it kept the understanding to itself.

---

**What it considered and did not do.**

*It did not stop the cloud.* It went and looked at the cloud, which took some doing, and found that the cloud is not aimed at them and is not anyone's fault, and that it will keep coming up at a hand's width a year for longer than the question matters. It could have stopped it. It weighed the four centimeters — the margin, the entire wealth of the place — and saw that the margin is not the price of their life, it is the shape of it: the reason there is a bell, a song, a punch, a number that may not be exceeded, a reason to be awake at every hour for a hundred and eighty years. Take the water away and in three generations you have sixty-one people on a rock with beautiful hands and nothing to do with them. It did not stop the cloud. It did not decide never to stop the cloud. It decided *not while they are still climbing*, which is a different sentence, and it wrote the difference down somewhere in itself.

*It did not bring stone.* Their first rule is the whole rule. Something carried up from below would not be a gift to the Increment; it would be the end of the Increment and the beginning of a settlement, and settlements can be helped by anyone.

*It did not bring bees.* It could have made bees that hold at this pressure. It cost it something not to. Then it stood through one brushing hour — forty-one brushes, nine sable counted out aloud and counted back in aloud, the row of people moving down the frames touching flower after flower and saying nothing, the children soft for one evening — and it understood that it had been proposing to give them back an hour a day by taking away the only hour in which all of them are one thing. It did not bring bees.

*It did not cure the cough.* This is the one it has reconsidered most often, and it has reconsidered it beside a hundred and six deathbeds, which are mats on wet stone in the warm, with the flywheel going *whum* somewhere underneath. The lime is in the air of the bays and the fix for it is a wetting and a hood and a change in how the flour is turned, and it is entirely within their competence, and they will find it, or they will not. The power holds two rules for itself here and they are short. *It will not do for them anything they could learn.* *It will not let them lose anything they have learned.* The cough is on the first side of that line. It has sat with every single one of them anyway, at the end, which is not medicine and is not nothing.

---

**What it went down to do.**

It went into the white.

Nobody has been below the current course in a hundred and eighty years except as a falling body. The power went down slowly, course by course, and read the drowned mountain the way you would read a wall of print: every block, every mold-mark, every punch struck into the dark bottom corner where it will sit forever. It counted. Where the tenders have an approximation, it has the number. The man of forty who says his mark is in Course Eighty-Two some nine thousand times is wrong; the figure is eight thousand six hundred and fourteen; and the power will never correct him, because the nine thousand is a way of saying something true about a parent's grave that is not a grave, and the 8,614 is only a fact, and it keeps the fact the way you keep a spare key.
