# The Attending

It did not come in.

For a long time — long enough that the walker completed a circuit and scratched the fifty-ninth mark and sat down and got up again — it stayed out in the basalt, distributed through some kilometers of rock, attending. Attention does not require an aperture. Only bodies do, and only bodies that insist on being somewhere.

It attended the way you would read a letter written by a stranger who has died: slowly, with the assumption that you are missing the point, because you have not yet earned the point.

This is the first thing it did and the most important and the one that most of its kind get wrong. The reflex of a good power in the presence of stillness is to diagnose. *Here is a sealed room, lightless but for two poor colors; here are three bodies seated against a wall with sand drifted on their heels; here is one more walking a circuit so slow it cannot be distinguished from a curse.* Any of that can be read as a tomb in under a second. It is a fast, warm, merciful reading and it is wrong, and a power that acted on it would have opened the roof and let in the sky, and the sky would have killed everything in the room within the hour, including the three, who would have gone out not in pain — they were past pain in the way they were past thirst — but in the middle of something, which is the only harm they had left available to them.

So it did not diagnose. It matched tempo first. A judgment delivered at a speed the subject cannot perceive is not a judgment; it is weather. It let a circuit go by. It learned what a hand's breadth of pry-bar movement meant as a unit of time, and then it used that unit and no other.

---

Then it audited, because restraint that has not checked is not restraint, it is only absence wearing the good clothes.

It went through the room item by item, looking for suffering, prepared to find it, prepared to act on it at whatever cost to the room's perfection — and the cost would have been total, and it had already accepted that. This must be said plainly or everything after it is a lie. If it had found one creature in the Hold that was in pain and could not get out, it would have torn the mass open and hauled the whole singing bubble into the daylight and let the note die screaming, and counted the loss cheap.

It found:

The drifters, at five altitudes, sinking on schedule, with no apparatus anywhere in them for dread. Sinking is not falling. Nothing in them was refusing the next sheet down.

The scrapers, walking the forty paces across lifetimes toward the bowl. It looked very hard at the glazing-over, because that is where you would expect to find the horror if there were horror — the slow accretion, the hardening, the becoming furniture. There was no horror. The animal gets heavy, and hot, and then stops, and stopping is not resisted, and the thing that stops is not the kind of thing that could have wanted otherwise. Its contribution is posthumous and it makes the beads and it does not know this and does not need to.

The thickets, which are cut at chest height on both sides of every path, and which flourish more for the cutting, as tended things do.

The three against the bare wall: pulse at a quarter-hour, lids moving on the scale of seasons, sulfides going in through the skin, the note going in through everything. It looked inside the state. This took most of a circuit. It found no boredom, which had been its chief worry, because the observer's word for a long steady act is always *bored* and the word is almost always the observer's. It found something with no gradient in it. Not bliss — bliss has a slope, bliss is on its way somewhere — but a flatness that was not numbness, that was in fact acutely, minutely awake to the shape of the pressure in a room where the shape of the pressure never changes, and was not waiting for it to change, and had no organ left with which to wait.

The walker: the same, plus a task.

The finding was: no one here is in pain, and no one here is trapped, because trapped requires a wish to be elsewhere, and there is no such wish in this room at any altitude.

That finding cost more to establish than any intervention would have cost to perform. Most of what a wise power does is this, and it is invisible, and it looks exactly like doing nothing, and the difference between it and doing nothing is the whole of the thing.

---

It went through the list anyway. The list of what it could have given. It is worth setting down because the refusals are the shape of the respect.

Water: obvious, generous, and the end of the world. Water is the wet business. Everything here is what it is because nothing dissolves. A single cup of kindness and the room would begin to rot for the first time in its existence, and the three would come apart in it, and the fronds would go soft, and the sand would cake.

Hearing: it could have given back the tympana. It could have let them hear the note they built. But they calcified on purpose, and their word for it is their word for coming of age, and there is nothing in the note to hear — no information, no beat, one syllable held. It would have been giving them a mouthful of a thing they had already eaten with their skin.

Light: the room has light the color of two arguments and it is enough to see by if you are not in a hurry, and no one is.

Escape: refused above.

Company: it considered opening a way to the other good rooms in the mass, if there are others, and there may be. A channel. And a channel is an aperture, and an aperture is a variation, and a variation would flutter the throat, and the throat is the thing they walled.

Memory: it considered carrying the craft up into the daylight, so that the lineage would be known, so that someone above would say their name. But *remembered* is a wet idea. It belongs to things that change and are afraid of changing further. The glyph on the wall does not mean *remember me*. It means *unchanged*. Sixty times, in a bored steady hand, which is not a bored hand.

Eternity: it could have held the mass breathing forever. It declined, and this is the delicate one, so: they did not ask for forever. They asked for *no variation*, which is a different order of request. Forever is a quantity. They were not working in quantity.

---

It did three things.

**The first.** Far downstream — beyond the three, beyond the sixty marks, beyond any span they had a use for — the deep is going to run short of breath. This was always true. But the way it was going to happen was badly made. The throat would have begun to narrow unevenly as the mass cooled, and the note would have soured first, then wavered, then come and gone, a long ugly decline of tens of thousands of years in which the lattice would drift and the antinodes would wander off the cairns and the thickets would die out of alignment and the whole map would go slack — and whoever was still in the state would have been woken by it, and their last long knowledge would have been of failure, of a tuning coming undone faster than any hand could correct it.

That was the one flaw in the Hold, and it lay entirely outside the reach of an obsidian blade and a hammer-stone and a wall of six dry-laid courses. It was the part of the job they could not finish.

So it finished the job. It went down into the constriction and reworked the way the stone will fail, so that it will fail all at once: no wavering, no souring, no decline. The note will hold and hold and hold and then, between one instant and the next, be over. A clean edge. Nothing here will experience an ending as a process, because process is decay, and decay is a wet business, and there is no water in this room and there never was.

It did not extend the note by a day. It only made the end of it sharp.

That is the whole intervention. It is, in the room's own idiom, a tuning — the same work as the baffle and the cairns, done to the one component no local tool could touch, in the same style, by the same standards, and it leaves exactly as much trace as the good work always does, which is none.

**The second.** There is no clock in the Hold. That is not a defect, but it means the count does not exist anywhere, and the count is the only thing about this place that is genuinely lost.

So it keeps the count. Outside. Elsewhere. Not in the room and never to be brought into the room: the number of circuits, the number of marks, the position of the bar, the sheets and the sinkings, all of it tallied somewhere with weather in it, where numbers are the sort of thing that can be held. Nobody in the Hold is told. Nobody in the Hold would have any use for it. It is a gift that takes place entirely on the other side of the wall, and costs the recipients nothing, and does not touch them, and is still — it decided, and it is wise, and it may be permitted a small warmth — worth doing.

**The third.** At the bowl, at the cold end, among the lumps and ribs of glazed animals that make the beads, it said one word.

It knew what would happen. The room will not tolerate a sound that is not its own; a voice is swallowed before it leaves the region of the teeth. But a broken bead also releases one clear syllable and is also swallowed, and the room eats that syllable every day without any harm at all, because a bead's worth of foreign sound is within the room's budget. So it spent exactly a bead's worth. One syllable, at the bowl, absorbed instantly and completely, and nothing shivered that was not already shivering.

The word was not a message. There was no one to receive it and it was not sent. It was the thing you say out loud at a graveside that is not a graveside, when you have looked at a life for a long time at the life's own speed and found it good and are not permitted to say so.

---

On the way out, it closed the aperture behind it, carefully, the way the three had closed theirs, because that is the local etiquette and it had been a guest.

There is a thing that happened that will not be settled. On its last pass it was standing in a node-line — you stand in the silences here, there is nowhere else to be that does not displace something — and the walker came along that line on the round, at a step a day, and passed within arm's length, and a body's worth of stillness in a node-line is readable as a dead spot in the pressure to anyone who reads pressure, and the walker reads pressure the way you read a lit room.

The walker did not stop. It went on to the panel and made the sixtieth mark and sat down for a while and got up.

It is impossible to say whether it noticed. The power considered finding out, and did not, and left the question standing, because the question is the only uncertainty in the Hold and it seemed wrong to take it.

Somewhere along the node-lines now, at the speed of a thing that has decided the question is not important, a circuit is closing. When it closes there will be a sixty-first mark, in the same hand, saying *unchanged*.

It will be true. Someone went to considerable trouble to keep it true, and left, and told no one, and that was the whole of the benevolence: not to be the exception in a room that had earned the right not to have any.
