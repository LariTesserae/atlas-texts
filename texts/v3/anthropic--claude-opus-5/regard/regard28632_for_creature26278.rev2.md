## The Attention

It found the place the way everything finds the place: by the smoke.

It came on a cold morning, when the column was leaning off downwind and dissolving, and it stood a while on the neighbouring ridge with the saxifrage and the knuckle-hard cushions, and it did what it does, which is to look at a thing until the thing has been properly looked at.

Then it went down to the rim, and the first act of a benevolent and wise power at the Warm Mouth was this: it put down its light.

Set it on the rim rock like a resin torch that will not be lit. Because light is not information here — that is the first rule of the place, and a power that arrives brighter than the room it has come to visit has already decided that the room is a mistake. So it went down the northeast wall in the dark, past the bamboo, facing the rock with its palms flat, and it learned the shaft the way the residents learn it: by hand, by smell, by listening, by the change in the sound when the wall comes closer.

This took some years. In its own accounting the delay was a courtesy of about the length of standing in a doorway, waiting to be noticed by a dog.

It was not noticed. Nothing in the Warm Mouth looked up. It found this restful. It is very tiring to be worshipped by things that would have been better off finishing their dinner.

## An Audit of Mercies

Everything it might have given, it considered, and it considered each one seriously, because a power that refuses a gift without costing it first is not wise, only fashionable.

**Sight.** It could have opened eyes down there — given the blind cricket a world of edges, given the pale snake the wingbeat as a visible thing instead of a tremor. It declined. To give eyes to the Warm Mouth is to install a sixth wall in a room that has five and needs five. These animals are not deprived of light. They are *finished with it*. The whip spider, sweeping its hair-fine antennae in the dark, is not a creature waiting for dawn; it is a creature for whom dawn would be a demotion.

**Safety.** It could have taken out the snake. Forty animals hanging from the vault, striking at the tide, missing four times out of five — it could have arranged for the fifth. And the colony would not have noticed for one generation, and would have noticed very much by the tenth, because the tide has its shape partly from being taxed, and a colony that has never been taxed will go up and up until the aerial plankton thins and the whole thing comes down in one bad year instead of one bird at a time. The strike is not a wound in the system. It is a rate. It declined.

**Rescue.** This was the hard one, and it stayed with it a long while. Every bird that comes down onto the floor is dead. A hand could catch them — a hand large enough could catch all of them, every falling nestling and every spent free-tail for a thousand years. But seven metres of floor is what the falling has made. The being's body is its own dead, compacted, fermenting, breathing out; the drought years pale, the wet centuries dark; two millimetres a year, which is not an age but a rate, which is to say the place is not old, it is *arriving*. To catch the fallen is to stop the clock, cool the fire, empty the beetles, and leave a cold stone hole with birds in it and no floor and no warmth and no calendar. It declined. It wrote the refusal down, in whatever it writes in. It did not pretend it had lost nothing.

**Sweetness.** It could have taken the ammonia out of the air. The ammonia is the breath. You do not improve a sleeping animal by removing its exhalation.

**Abundance.** It could have doubled them. Eighty thousand birds, ten million bats, a floor rising a centimetre a year. It ran the arithmetic out to the fourth decade and saw the ceiling over the lowland stripped grey in August, and the boluses coming home light, and the second egg failing, and then the first, and the whole doubled magnificence cutting itself back to something smaller than it had been. Abundance here is not a quantity. It is a relationship with forty kilometres of forest and eleven minutes of river. It declined.

**Permanence.** It could have made the place invulnerable — sealed it, fixed it, set it outside the reach of the four bad years that would empty it. And it saw what that would produce: a monument. A shaft that no longer needed the valley, no longer pumped the lowland up onto a sterile ridge, no longer had a digestive tract running south into the trees. An immortal Warm Mouth would be a Warm Mouth that had been cut off from everything that makes it warm. It declined, and this is the refusal that most powers get wrong.

**A sign.** It could have left something at the rim. It thought about this for less time than any of the others. A sign is a bright object placed at the mouth of a place that cannot survive being famous. It declined, and it declined so thoroughly that it also arranged not to be dreamt about, which is the sort of tidiness that goes unthanked.

## The Fungus

There is a thing in the dust that colonises lungs.

It wanted to take that away. It is benevolent; that is not decoration, it means what it says; there are eight generations of valley people in its count who coughed for years and then stopped, and it has all of them, by name, including the ones the valley itself has forgotten.

And it saw, looking, that the fungus is load-bearing. That the rule — *climb in the wet weeks, mouths shut, do not linger, do not talk about why* — is held in place by a fear nobody has examined in two hundred years, and that the rule is the reason the colony still boils only three days a decade instead of every dry season. Lift the danger and you lift the reticence, and then there is no reason not to bring a hundred people and a torch and a platform and a season of eggs.

So it did not take the fungus. But it refused to accept the trade as final, which is the difference between wisdom and resignation. It went looking for another way to hold the rule — something that would keep the restraint standing after the fear was gone, so that the fear could one day be allowed to go. That work is slow and unglamorous and is still running.

## What It Actually Did

Almost all of it happened somewhere else.

Forty kilometres south, a road survey found bad ground in a week when the ground had always been bad and nobody had bothered to look. A price moved a quarter of a percent in an unhelpful direction and a concession lapsed. A young official, three days before a transfer, read a file he had intended to skim, and what he read was a census — wrong, of course, nearly all of it microbial and unnamed, but accurate in the only sentence that mattered: *take the forest forty kilometres south and the dome goes cold in four years.* That census had been carried across a river in a bag that should have gone under, and did not.

Eleven consecutive Octobers rained adequately.

And a man changed his mind on a Tuesday, for reasons he would have described as entirely his own, and he would have been right. The power is scrupulous about this. It does not steal anyone's reasons. A benevolence that authors your decisions has quietly reduced you to scenery, and there is enough scenery already.

One thing happened at the mountain itself, and it was very small. In a wet season, a woman's knee went bad on the descent, and she and her granddaughter, who was nine, sat at the rim an extra night in the warm updraught. And because there was nothing else to do in the dark, the old woman told the story — not the rule, the *story*, the one about the year somebody took the occupied tier and what came of it — out loud, for the first time in three generations. The girl is old now. The pegs have been replaced twice since.

## Departure

It took everything with it except the light it had never lit, and it took that too.

It left no mark on the rim rock, no grass that had not been there, no extra warmth, no new species, nothing added to the vault, nothing subtracted from the floor. The smoke still leans off downwind on cold mornings. The tide still turns twice a day, arterial and venous. Every bird still gets home. The mothers are still right nearly every time, which is not the same as always, and it let that stand.

Within a week the mountain had closed over its absence.

What it carried away was a room — known by hand, by smell, by the gradient of warmth; forty thousand cups on a wall with no landmarks; a hundred thousand pups all the same size and the same smell, and one voice among them finding one answer; the double *tock*; the mite that lives in the left ear because the right one is needed; thirty pegs set by somebody's grandmother, and a rule nobody remembers learning.

It had come to give. It went away holding something instead, which it had not expected, and which it has kept.

Visited. Not owned.
