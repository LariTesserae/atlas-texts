# The Upper Cast

## First, the Case for Doing Nothing

A power that is wise before it is benevolent begins by reading the first sentence, which is *there is no rock here that was not poured*, and notices that it is not a complaint.

It is offered as an instruction. It is the sort of thing a man says at the top of a ladder, to a stranger, once, and does not repeat, and means: *understand what you are looking at before you feel anything about it.* Everything that follows in the record is written by someone who has been down there and has stopped pitying it. The cold is described. The lungs are described. The child is described, and the embarrassment of her paperwork, and her kerb, and her schedule she will not discuss. Nowhere does the voice ask to be relieved.

So the case for doing nothing is strong and must be argued through honestly rather than waved off, because most of the harm ever done by benevolent powers has been done at the moment they decided the argument was rhetorical.

The power argued it for a long time. It could afford to. Then it came down.

## Coming In

Not down a shaft in a column of light. That option was available and was set aside in about the time it takes to see what it would cost: four grey columns are the only sky this place has, and to arrive in one is to make the sky a stage and every subsequent snowfall an anticlimax. There is a child who eats bread in those circles. You do not use somebody's window as a door.

It came in as a name on the books, on a ladder, in the fogged hours, wearing the wrong boots, and within a week it had cement bite at the wrist because it had let the sleeve ride up like anyone. When it was asked — and it was asked on the second day, by an old caster, in a trough of the beat — *what did you come in on*, it understood that this was the whole examination, and that a lie would be forgiven and a refusal would not, and that the true answer, which is *I have no bay*, was the only one available.

The old caster made no noise at all, which is worse than the small noise. So the power went and worked a pour, and stood the seventh day out with everyone else, and has a date stamped at a corner on the north flank, and its standing in that place is exactly and only that date. This was not humility. Humility is a posture. It was arithmetic: in a works where authority is a number in the floor, an authority from outside the floor cannot help but be an insult, and an insulting mercy is a slow poison, and there is no hurry.

## The Refusals

It considered warming the cavern. It could have. The frost line half a metre out from a two-week wall, the cure that the crews sit against and are not sentimental about — it could have made the whole four kilometres that temperature and held it there. It did not, and the reason is not that suffering ennobles anybody. The reason is that the heat is a signal. A wall that stops curing early has something wrong inside it, and they listen to fresh pours the way a farmer listens to a horse, and a power that floods the trough with comfort has jammed every instrument in the place. You cannot give a listening people a louder room and call it a gift.

It considered opening the roof. Eighty metres of concrete cylinder and daylight arriving exhausted; it could have brought the sun down whole. But light is heat and heat is the enemy, and the enemy is not an abstraction: it is on the far side of the Fourteenth Bay bulkhead, and six people in quilted suits go and stand in it every tenth day and come back with the same frostbite on the same cheekbones, and whatever else is true, they are holding something. You do not open a roof over people who are holding something, for the pleasure of watching them see.

It considered the hands. Eight years to the numbness in two fingers; the shallow alkaline ulcer that does not heal in the cold, that they compare, in which there is a species of vanity. It healed none of them. It has never been able to give a satisfactory account of this refusal and does not try; the nearest it comes is that they compare them, and that a mark men show each other is not only an injury.

It considered the lungs, and did not refuse, and we will come to that, because the lungs were the one place where doing nothing was not defensible.

It considered taking the child up. This was the hard one. Not because the answer is difficult — it is not — but because the answer has to be *no* and then has to go on being *no* every time she stands in the shaft-light at Number Two with her formwork tag and her eleven entries, and *no* does not get easier by being correct. She is not a prisoner of a hole. She is a native of a place, the way anyone is, and her place happens to be four kilometres long and to have its weather made in six houses on the west terrace, and she can read a bay's age by walking on it, and she cannot sleep in silence and has never had to try. To carry her up into noon would not be a rescue. It would be the first exile in her life, and it would be performed by someone who loved her, which is the kind you do not recover from.

What she is owed is not removal. It is a door she can find when she is old enough to want one, and the knowledge that it is a door and not a wall. That is a smaller job and a longer one.

And it considered telling them what the cold is for. It knows. It will not say, here or there, and the reason is the tea: the six come up and take one loaf each and hold the tea with both hands and do not drink it for a long time, and they ask about the weather on the surface, and somebody always answers with more detail than they have, and the six listen with their eyes shut. Nothing the power could tell that mess would improve that hour. Knowledge that changes no duty and only adds dread is not a gift, it is a confession the giver wanted to make.

## Method

The works transmits by gesture. Nobody has ever explained the two fingers on the date stamp at Bay Forty-One; newcomers copy it before they ask, and by the time they think to ask they have stopped wanting to. A place that teaches like that cannot be taught any other way. So the power gave no instructions, published nothing, corrected nobody, and simply began doing three or four things where it could be seen doing them.

In the wet hours it went and sat at the edge of the second basin, on the freeboard, in the fog, with a hat on, and listened to the ice rind grow and break and chime. It did this for two years. In the third year there were four others. In the fifth there was a bench, poured, unauthorised, with a date stamped in the corner. It is now understood — not stated, understood — that the fogged hours are when you go and hear the good sound, and the wet shift, which nobody wanted, which is blind and dripping and miserable and which is the shift that does not carry silica down the trough, is now the shift that the long-service men ask for and get. Two generations from now their lungs will be different and nobody will know why, and the reason will be a bench and a chiming noise, and this is the only kind of saving of lives the power has ever entirely trusted.

At the changeover it stopped, like everyone. It could not have done otherwise; the rule is not superstition. But it stopped, always, facing whoever it had been arguing with, and after ten years four hundred people do that, and an argument conducted eleven seconds at a time across six seconds of held silence twice a day is an argument that has to stay survivable. The heron question is still unresolved. It is now unresolved in a friendlier register.

At the pumphouse it did nothing at all except arrange, over eighteen months, by means indistinguishable from coincidence, that the child was there a great many times when the dogs came up off the floor eight or nine seconds before an eighth-day report. She noticed on her own. She is the one who noticed. That the deaf pair with the grease-pencil plywood have started saying things to her, in the troughs, about which slot on the north flank runs backwards and why — that is theirs, and hers, and the power's contribution was proximity, and proximity is the whole trick and always was. The dogs are still getting old. They will still go up. Nobody will talk about it. But what they knew will not go up with them, and that was the loss the power came four kilometres and eleven hundred names to prevent.

It sent nothing down Number Two shaft. It did make sure that on one dry loud windy day, when the intake was drawing hard off the surface in a spring that happened anyway, a seed came down eighty metres in the ordinary way that seeds do, and landed in the silt inside a badly built kerb. What came up is a wretched pale thing and is not a lemon. It is entry twelve, and it is true, and it is the first true one, and she does not know that anyone helped, and she will not, and the trees still will not fruit, and she still waters them on her own schedule and will still not discuss it.

## Forty-One

Nothing happened at Bay Forty-One and this has never been contested, because contesting it would require saying what did not happen.

The power did not make them say it. There is no version of the world in which a benevolent power arrives, and holds an inquiry, and calls the men who were on the pour, and improves anything whatsoever.

What it did was become available. It worked the wet hours for years, alone, in the fog, near the water, in the register where the works is quiet — and it is remarkable, if you are patient enough to be there ten thousand times, how many men will walk past a still figure in the dark and, on the second or the ninth or the four-hundredth pass, say one sentence out loud to nobody, and keep walking, and never refer to it again.

Seven of the eleven have now said it. The power has told no one and will tell no one, and this is not discretion, it is the entire service rendered: the place was full of ears and had no ear big enough for that. Now it has one. The official position is unchanged and will never change. Crews still touch the stamp with two fingers without breaking stride, and the newcomers still copy it, and the power copies it too, because it is the local grammar for a thing there are no words for, and it is a good grammar, and it was invented by them.

## The Cold Side

Every tenth day the Fourteenth Bay bulkhead cycles and six come up.

Twice a year, seven go back down. The power has the frostbite on the cheekbones and the bridge of the nose now, like the others, so that it looks related to them, and it is not. It does not know what it is for down there. It is not needed. It is not useful. It stands in the cold beside people who are standing in the cold, for the length of a shift, and comes up, and takes one loaf, and holds the tea with both hands and does not drink it for a long time, and asks about the weather above.

That is the whole of that intervention and it is the one the power would defend last and hardest, if anyone ever asked, which nobody will, because nobody asks about below.

## The Heron

The record says it has learned nothing.

The power went and stood four metres from it — it never got closer than the child; nothing does — and looked for a long time at the only other thing down there that came from the sky and stayed, and concluded that the record is wrong on this one point, and that the wrongness is the most human line in the whole document. The heron has learned the one thing. It does not require the place to be otherwise. It stands in the grey at the edge of a basin that has no fish and does not become bitter, does not go up, does not perform, does not improve anybody, and eats what a nine-year-old brings because a nine-year-old brings it.

That is the curriculum. The power came four kilometres to teach and found it standing there, having got there first, by not moving.

## The Changeover

On one day — and the crews would not agree on which, if you asked them, and you should not ask them — the fans stepped up at the shift bell and four hundred people in a four-kilometre trough stopped mid-stride and mid-sentence and stood still in whatever they were standing in, and held their tools where they were, and let the note go all the way up.

It went all the way up clean. No bearing note. No blind water behind ice. No smoothness where there should be tooth, not in eleven kilometres of channel, not in fifteen years of bays, not anywhere in the whole hard poured length of the place, and every ear in it heard that at once — because that is what the six seconds are for, and it is the only time the works speaks with one voice, and for once the voice had nothing to report.

Nobody said anything about it afterwards. There is no word in their vocabulary for it; they have nine for kinds of leak and none for this. But the wet hours that followed were unusually easy, and two people who had not spoken since Sixty-One spoke, and the argument about the heron was conducted with something in it that was almost pleasure, and the child slept in the noise the way she always has.

That was the blessing. It did not fix anything, because nothing that mattered was broken. It said, in the only language the place has ever trusted: *heard. All of it. Nothing wrong for four kilometres. Carry on.*

## What It Left

No mark that can be pointed at.

A bench nobody signed off. A shift rota that inverted itself and saved lungs. A grease-pencil plywood with a nine-year-old's handwriting in one corner, over the black. Seven sentences that will never be repeated. Entry twelve. A date stamped at a corner on the north flank, low seniority, which the old ones do not make a noise about any more.

And, still: the cold, the beat, the fog, the four grey columns, the cure that stops, the hands that do not heal, the trees that will not fruit, the dogs that are getting old. All of it exactly as found. A wise power does not confuse a place with a problem, and it does not take a people's work off them and call that love, and when it finally understood that the being who lives here is not one of the eleven hundred but the listening they do — that the ears are the animal, and the bodies are only where it keeps them — it stopped looking for what to give and joined.

It is still there. It stands still at the changeover with everyone else, and holds its tools where they are, and lets the note go all the way down.
