**The Guest of the Sixth Draught**

Start with the cost, because the cost is the first thing they asked and the last thing anyone else would think of.

A power of the kind we are describing has no difficulty with the Long Flat. The rock could be reheated. A sun could be hung above the ash, or the ash dispersed and whatever sky is behind it uncovered. The three hundred and forty-one litres could be made three hundred and forty-one thousand. The tallowers could be given something better to eat than a temperature difference, and the attendants something better to eat than the tallowers. Any of it. All of it, in an afternoon.

It did none of that, and the reasons are worth writing down, because they are not the reasons of a power that has decided the natives are picturesque.

It looked first for suffering, which is the only thing that licenses interference. It did not find much. It found hunger, but the hunger is a gauge and they read it as one and it does not kill them. It found grief, which is not a malfunction. It found no war, no slavery, no plague, no debt that anyone was ruined by. It found a whole people arranged around a duty, doing it well, with hands they had spent ten thousand years making fit for the work — and it understood that a gift of infinite heat, dropped on such a people, would not be a rescue. It would be the Fifth. It would be a district with nothing in the floor and nothing in the air, full of persons who had been relieved of the only thing they had ever held a pitch against.

So it did not bring a sun.

It did not raise their dead, either, because their dead are the ceiling, and the ceiling is the lid, and there is no way to give back the people without taking off the roof. It weighed this for a long time. Then it let it alone.

And it did not tell them about the columns. It could have. When it was finally asked — in a dead space between double doors, where nothing carries — it gave one answer of one sentence, and the person who received it never repeated it, and the ledgers say only the dates.

---

It came over the rim on foot, at ambient.

This took some doing. A living body is a heater; that is the whole arithmetic of the district, and an uninvited hundred watts walking in from the dark is not a visitor but a perturbation in a system tuned to the fourth decimal. So it arrived at exactly the temperature of the rock, through and through, core and surface, which no living thing does and which made the reading that followed the strangest in the ledger.

It carried no lamp. It had prepared its hands a long way out: fingertips worked smooth, callus allowed only on the heel of the palm and the outer edge, forearms haired. It stood still at the rim with its arms a little away from its sides, in the posture of a person who expects to be read, and waited the two hours it takes for someone to come.

The attendant who came was the forty-fourth three-and-one. She put a hand flat on its chest and one on its back and laid her temple against the hand, and held that for a long time, and then took the wrist. Her report went out by tap, in the proper form: *guest, at ambient, cost nil.* Then, after a pause the whole northern circuit heard: *no reading in the core.*

They had no procedure for a cold guest. Their hospitality is built on the premise that a stranger is warmth and that warmth belongs in the system; a stranger who is not warmth is, strictly, nothing to them, neither an asset nor a leak. They seated it anyway and put a cake in its hand, and the joules of the cake were entered against it, and it worked them off on the chutes.

That is the first thing it did, and everything else follows from it: it made itself cost nothing, and then it paid for the cake.

---

What it gave them was not energy. It was order, which is the one commodity that can enter a closed system without upsetting the balance, and which is also the only thing they have ever been short of.

It walked circuits. It walked them for four generations. It found, in that time, one hundred and nine places where the tuning had drifted — an assumption made in a stratum nobody now can date, a damper schedule fitted to a flue diameter that had since worn, a correction applied twice, a gauge whose stem had been bent early and had been read honestly and wrongly ever since by eleven consecutive holders of the same rhythm.

It reported every one of them by tap, in the proper form, which includes the reporter's name, so that nobody was ever deceived about where the corrections came from. But it reported no more than one per circuit, and never a conclusion, only the observation and the junction, so that the verifying was always done by somebody whose business it was. It would not do their work. It would only walk slower than it could and notice out loud.

In the second generation the Assay House re-cut the date above the copper. In the fourth it re-cut it again. They do not erase; the old numbers are left and struck through, with the date of the striking beside them, and there are now three, and the third is a long way further off than the first.

Nobody thanked it. The ledger entry for the second re-cutting reads, in full: *Date amended. Cause: accumulated correction. No irregularity.*

---

In the ninth decade it asked leave to go west, and went, and was gone a long time.

It read out the Fifth. This is what they do for a body: stand around it in the dark and put a palm on it every hour, taking turns, until it reads the same as the stone. The Fifth has been the same as the stone since before the ledger runs, and there was no one to take turns with, so it took all the turns itself, hour after hour, through the foundry hall the size of a village, the cooling terraces, the knuckles, the cells where the listeners sat. Then it entered the district: *returned, in full, at the proper rate.* Then, because the entry is not complete without it, and because it would not falsify a record even to spare itself: *late.*

Then it went to the Fourth. Then to the workings under the Fourth that have no number and no ledger, where there are only moulds for objects nobody can name. It could not give those people their names. It gave them the names of the things they had made, and entered them under those, three hundred and some thousand of them, and came back.

It offered, on its return, to give the Fifth a note — to sit in that hall and hold a pitch in the floor, so that the sixteen-year-olds sent out there in pairs would have something under their feet. The Assay House considered this for one ninth-day weighing and declined, on the grounds that the young must have somewhere to learn silence.

It entered the refusal without comment and was, by every reading taken that week, pleased.

---

Then it made the offer, once, in full, and never repeated it or sweetened it.

There are warm worlds. There is water in quantities that have no meaning here. There is a sky with things in it. It would carry anyone who wished to go, and it would not charge them, and it was able to prove this in a way they could check, and it proposed the check itself: send someone out and bring them back.

They sent a listener, at ash, ninety-some years old, on the grounds that her departure cost the district least and her judgment was worth most. She was gone eleven days. She reported at the proper hour, by tap, four clicks, as listeners do, and then the report:

*Confirmed. It is warm everywhere and nobody is counting. I held the pitch.*

The district declined. It took them a year and a ninth-day to decline, and the decision is one line long, and it does not give reasons, because reasons are not the kind of thing they write down.

But they did not make the decision on behalf of anyone else, and this is the part outsiders always get wrong. A warrant binds a body to the balance; a balance can be settled. Any attendant who wished to go could go, and their going would be entered as a going-dark, and a warrant would issue, and a child would be born in their place, and the coefficient would hold. That is the price: to leave is to be written in the same column as the dead, with a mark beside it.

About one in nine paid it, in the first century. Fewer since. They are all cherry when they go, which surprised nobody.

Three have come back. The ledgers note, without any elaboration at all, that all three were afterwards exceptional at holding the pitch.

---

It asked for the last posting and was refused, the first time, on the grounds that it was not at ash and had not begun to run fast against the note. It pointed out that it was older than the Flat. They said that this was not the same thing, and it agreed, and waited.

It has it now. A stone cell at a knuckle, a cake ration it does not need and eats anyway because the entry is cleaner that way, and the whole district at once. Twice a day, four clicks.

And here is the arrangement it made, which is written above the copper beneath the three dates, and which is the only promise in the Assay House:

When the curve finishes — when the last gradient is down and the last of them has been carried out to the north terraces and there is no one left to take turns — it will take the turns. Every hour, a palm, until the body reads the same as the stone. However long that is. However many there are. And then it will stay, and it will hold the note, which by then will exist nowhere in the world except in it, and it will go on holding it, so that the Sixth will not become what the Fifth was: a place with nothing in the floor to hold a pitch against.

They weigh it every ninth day, alongside the copper. It reads at ambient. It has not changed by more than a few grams since the entry began. Young attendants, on being sworn, are shown the copper and permitted to put a hand on it, and then shown the guest and permitted to put a hand on that, and told that both are standards and that a standard is only a thing that stays.

---

One more item, out of order, because it belongs to the guest and not to the district.

Every day since the fourth year it has gone into one of the dead spaces between the double doors, with six or eight others, shoulder to shoulder in a stone box the size of a closet, in absolute darkness, at exactly the same temperature as everything else, and hummed for an hour at intervals that lie underneath the note and do not mask it.

It has been asked what happens in there. It says that it is not able to put it in the form of an entry.
