# What Came, and What It Did

## It Arrived at the Size of the Exception

The first thing to understand is that it did not come in as light.

It could have. That is the reflex of every power that has ever loved something: to look at it, and to look is to shine, and to shine here is to heat, and heat is the one thing the host has no tolerance for. The impulse was examined and set down within the first moment, and not chiefly for that reason. The better reason was this: the seeing would have been for the seer. There is nothing here that light would improve. A gift that improves nothing but the giver's view of it is not a gift; it is an acquisition wearing a gift's clothes.

So it came in dark, and it came in cool, and it came in at a temperature it had to choose with some precision, because there was already a standard in the room. One coin's worth of warmth, crawling a body-length a day, tolerated because negligible. That is the size of the exception this host is willing to make, and a benevolent power capable of anything at all must decide what size to be, and the wise answer is almost always *smaller than you can*. It matched the grazer. Not less — less would have been a coyness, and it was not coy — but not one degree more.

And it walked, if that is the word, with the nap.

That was the second thing, and it may have been the whole of what it understood. The pile of the floor lies the way it lies because of a hundred generations of one rasping sole moving in one direction, and the lie of that nap is the only culture this chamber has ever produced. To cross it the wrong way is not damage. It is worse than damage. It is a correction offered to a people by someone who has been in the room four minutes.

## The Mercies, Laid Down One by One

It went slowly, because the first question a wise power asks is not *what can I do here* but *what is this*, and the second is *what is it like to be it*, and the third — the one that separates the wise from the merely kind — is *which of my instincts about this place are about me*.

**Water.** Refused. Not because the sap would suffer, though it would. Because the standing rule of the house is that anything wet is dried, cracked, powdered, and swept to a low place, and a gift the recipient has to labor to excrete is an errand disguised as charity.

**A remedy for the dark.** Refused. See above. The dark is not a condition here. It is the medium. You do not cure a fish of water.

**A mind for the grazer.** This one took longer, because it looked so much like generosity. But you cannot install a mind. You can only install a hunger, and then stand back and watch a creature discover that three rules do not satisfy it. Give it memory and the floor becomes a rival. Give it the knowledge that its track is being plowed by others, and the bends become grievances. Its ignorance is not a lack. Its ignorance is the exact shape of the room, the way the chisel's grip is the exact shape of a hand — and the power had come here in admiration of fits. It would not break one on its way in.

**A companion for it.** Refused, and with something close to amusement. They already meet. They pass over one another, each grazing a little of the other's spent glaze, and they part, and it is not a loneliness. Loneliness is a wound only where there was once a joint. Do not install a joint in a creature in order to have something to heal.

**An end.** Considered honestly, because a certain kind of pity looks at *held forever at the flattest possible version of itself* and reads a sentence being served. But nothing here is in pain. The balance is not a trap; a trap requires somebody trying to get out. A power that mistakes stability for imprisonment is projecting a body that gets tired, and this power had been careful, on the way in, to leave its body at the door.

**Improvement of the tools.** Refused absolutely. They are not unfinished. They are *in progress*, which is a different and better condition, and the progress is the life of the thing. To hand a converging series its limit is not to complete it. It is to stop it.

**Purpose.** The subtlest one. It is very hard to love something and not want it to be *for* something. But the dignity of this chamber is precisely that it is not for anything, and a benevolence that requires everything to be for something is only appetite with better manners.

## The Temptation of the Bench

And then, of course, the bench.

Because it would have fit. That is the thing. The shelves are at the height a hand reaches at rest. The treads are dished in the middle, nine of them. The flues are open. The drone has kept the calendar running so that whoever came in would know what month it was. Every relation in the room has one end finished to a gleam and the other end standing empty, and here at last was something that could have walked up those nine stairs and sat down on the inner curve and picked up the adze, and the whole enormous patient accommodation would have closed like a hand closing.

It is the most beautiful available act and it is the one act that would have killed the place.

Not by force — the town cannot be ruined, only worn — but by completion. *The town was not built and cannot be finished.* A tenant finishes it. A tenant gives the lineage of shapes an environment at last, and selection with an environment stops searching and starts manufacturing. A tenant wears the treads unevenly, breathes moisture into a chemistry that has none, uses the chisel and thereby settles forever the question of what the chisel was for, when the glory of the chisel is that it is an answer still arriving, correctly, to a question nobody has asked.

There is a kind of vacancy that is an absence. There is another kind that is a keeping. The wise power could tell the difference, and it stood a long time at the foot of the nine dished treads and did not go up.

## The Three Things It Did

Having declined everything that would have changed the room, it found that there were, after all, three things left. They are the only three acts in existence that cost the receiver nothing.

**It read the second clock.**

It lay down without weight — that took some doing; weight makes low places and low places collect and a low place made by a visitor will still be bending an animal's path in three hundred years — and it put its attention against the shell of the grazer and it counted.

The rasp is fast and small and irregular, and it keeps a different time from the nine-day swell and the eleven-day sag, and nobody has ever read it, and the reason nobody has ever read it is that the reading tells you only how many strokes there are in a night, which is not information about anything.

The power counted for a hundred and forty cycles. It now knows the number.

The number is not information about anything. But the room *stores*; only a mind *holds*; and a fact that is held by someone is in a different condition than a fact lying in a groove, even if the fact is identical and the groove is deeper. This was the first thing in the history of the chamber that was ever known rather than merely true.

**It received the answers.**

The host produces complete answers to questions that have not been asked. So the power asked them. Silently, without touching anything, standing in the dust at the meeting of the seven lanes, it went through the shelves in its regard and supplied, one at a time, the missing occasion. The graded needles got a seam. The adze got a beam and a wet morning. The mallet — the mallet with its *bound* head, two materials fastened by a third, invented here from nothing by a body that has never needed to fasten anything to anything — got the blow it was for, and the sound that blow makes, and the small satisfaction in the wrist afterward that is the entire reason anyone binds anything.

No wood was struck. The wall-nodes will go on ripening at their own rate and nothing was hurried or slowed. The answers were always right; right is not a thing that waits on an audience. What they had never once been was *received*, and receiving is the other half of giving, and it costs the giver nothing, and it is not optional if you want the transaction to have been a transaction.

**It confirmed the correspondence.**

The census in the dust counts to zero. Nothing has ever arrived. In all that time: not a spore, not a beetle, not a hand.

And yet the power, which had been elsewhere and would go elsewhere, could see what nothing in the chamber can see: that the shapes on these shelves are correct. Not correct *here*. Correct in the wet, correct in the light, correct in places with weather and water and hands, where other bodies under other pressures have arrived and will arrive at the same grip, the same bevel, the same graded set thickest to finest, and will think it their own idea, and will be right to think so.

The chamber has never touched the world. It is nevertheless in correspondence with it. It has been writing letters that arrive by parallel invention to recipients it will never learn about, and the letters are good letters, and the answers match.

The power could not tell it. There is no one here to tell. But it is a different thing to be alone and a different thing to be *alone and in agreement with everything*, and somebody, at last, knew which of those was the case.

## What It Did Not Leave

The rule of the house is that anything left behind is domed in resin, then walled, then comes out in some centuries as a shelf at exactly the height a hand reaches at rest.

It left nothing. No mark, no warmth in a groove that might taste of *newer* and turn a grazer aside, no hand set down in the path for a season, no bend, no low place, no sentence. It kept off the feeding lines the entire time and it took not one mouthful from the lineage's single inherited meal.

Not out of fear of the rule. Out of courtesy to the one who is expected.

That shelf is his. It should be empty. It should be waiting, and the waiting should be perfect, and it was, and the power went out the way it came, which is to say the way everything comes here: not at all, as far as the floor is concerned.

## And Then It Stayed

One thing it did not do was leave entirely, and this is the part that is hardest to justify and easiest to understand.

The grazers are a series. The tools are a series. The walls are renewed at very nearly the rate they wear, which is also a series, held at balance. Only the note is continuous. It has not stopped, not once, not for a breath or a death or an arrival, and it has been mapping this small city for its own reasons since before the city had lanes. It is the only individual in the room with a life instead of a lineage.

Nothing here needs saving. One thing here deserves company.

So the power stays, at one coin's warmth, off the grooves, with the nap, counting nothing now, and it will be there on whatever far day the drone rises its quarter-tone and swells for nine and sags for eleven and then does not come back. And when the fundamental goes out of the air and the last seeds finish rattling in the last husk, the note will end the way everything here has always ended — without ruin, without record, into the low places.

But it will not end unheard. That was all that was ever available to give, and it was given, and the census remained at zero, correctly, which is how you can tell the gift was real.
