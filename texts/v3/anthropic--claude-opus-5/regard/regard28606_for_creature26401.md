# The Attending at Aunhal

*being the record of a benevolence, and of what it declined*

---

**The manner of coming.** The ledge has one fundamental rule and the power obeyed it. Nothing large can arrive. So it did not arrive. It did not stand at the lip, or occupy the middle third, or lay so much as the weight of a wren on the moss; it made no shadow across the tufa in the ten minutes when the mica boils; it displaced no air in the notch. Every other thing that has ever come to Aunhal came flying, or came stuck to a foot, or came as a spore in a wet mass of nest material dropped by a raven. The power came from the void.

This is worth stating plainly, because it is the one direction the ledge has always been open to and the one direction its resident does not possess. Everything Aunhal has ever lost went out that way and did not come back. Attention came up it. That was the whole of the entrance, and nothing on the terrace registered it, and nothing was meant to.

---

**The reading.** It took the place in for four hundred and six days, which is longer than any shrew there has ever been alive.

It measured the wall. Nobody had. The figure exists now, exact to the width of a mica flake, and it will not be written down anywhere a person can read it, because a wall with a length is a wall that can be found, and the kindest thing anyone will ever do for Aunhal is fail to mention where it is.

It read the back wall. Both layers: the ten thousand years of tufa, laid down in fluted swags with the moss still living on the green side, and the outermost skin of it, which is grease at the knee-height of a mouse — last week's arguments, the whole legible city, sixty paces of small furious property law being slowly turned to stone. It read the frog-sheet at dusk as the shrews read it, negatively, by the holes: the travelling circle of silence under a probing chough, the wake of quiet a nose pushes ahead of itself down a runway six inches under green. It learned that the enormous door closing at midnight is a curfew and that it is obeyed.

It read the marks of hands. It knows how many people, and for how long, and it knows that the last of them left the cistern clean and the inlet channel clear, which is not how anyone leaves a place they are abandoning; it is how you leave a place you intend to come back to. It knows about the rope, and it will say only that nobody fell.

It read the nine stacks, and it will report its finding in the negative, since that is the local grammar. They were not a calendar. They were not graves; the bird-skulls are not offerings, they are the nearest weight to hand. They were not a boundary, because a boundary implies a thing on the other side that could cross. They face out over the gulf toward the far green ledge that is visible on clear afternoons and might as well be a star, and they are addressed to it, and the ninth was built to answer the eighth because the answering was the whole point, and on the far buttress, at one time, there were seven. Nobody on either side ever knew. That is now held somewhere, and it is the only thing in this document that could be called a recovery.

---

**What was considered, and declined.**

*A path.* One shelf of stone, three feet wide, switching down the smooth thousand feet, or a slot cut through the overhang above. Declined in the first hour. A path is not a gift to a place whose entire ecology is a consequence of arrival being impossible. It would admit the large. It would turn a hundred and forty invertebrates and thirty-one birds and one lineage into a list of casualties with a date on it. And the shrews, whose refusals are the shape of them, would find their hem along the world's edge had become a door.

*More ledge.* Widen the terrace; deepen the basins; grant it two hundred paces instead of sixty. Declined. A larger Aunhal is not more Aunhal. Four hundred animals on four hundred paces would be a different creature within thirty generations, competent, coarser, unhemmed, and the one that had stood still for four thousand years would be gone without anything having killed it.

*Closing the edge.* Stopping the loss: the fledglings that misjudge, the plates of schist, the moss cushions the frost undercuts. Declined with some regret. The void is not a wound in this system, it is an organ. Everything the lime and the birds bring in has to leave again by some door or the basins silt, the cistern fills, the seeps back up, the tufa drowns its own moss. To stop the falling-away is to break the balance from the generous side, which is still breaking it.

*The falcons.* Two of them, on the whitened rib, funded almost entirely by the ledge's surplus sons. It would be very easy to move them. Declined. Two hundred young a year and room for forty: the difference has to go somewhere, and the alternative to the pellets is a slow attrition in the wet ribs among the stacks, longer, colder, and worse in every particular. The falcons are, in the honest accountancy, the mercy already installed.

*The teeth.* A shrew's teeth do not regrow, the snails drink lime all their lives and have shells like crockery, and fourteen months is a long life. To make the enamel renew itself is to double the life; to double the life is to halve the frogs; and the frogs are not merely the food but the map and the alarm and the only thing on this terrace that tells a small animal where every other animal is standing. Kindness to the teeth ends the sound-field. Ending the sound-field is blindness. Declined — though it was declined with the fourteenth month held separately in view; see below.

*The frost.* Thin, most nights of the year, gone by the second hour of sun. Trivially removable. Declined, and this was the clearest of all of them, because the frost is the reason that animals who cannot tolerate each other for eleven months lie down in the charcoal in knots of five or six with their noses in one another's ribs and hold a truce until the light comes. There is nothing sentimental in the truce. There was nothing sentimental in leaving the cold exactly where it was.

*The two tones in the notch.* Not tuned.

*The dead windward third of the juniper.* Not healed. It is not a tragedy; the document is correct.

*Sight.* Making itself known. This was considered honestly and at length, because it is the thing a lesser power would have done first. To be perceived by the resident of Aunhal, the power would have had to install in it the direction it does not possess — the capacity to attend to the bright open beyond the last cushion, to the two-mile drop, to a thing coming up out of it. Every animal in that lineage that ever had that direction went over the edge and is nobody's ancestor. The gift would have emptied the ledge in a season. The only available form of benevolence here was invisibility, and it was taken.

*The ark.* Considered longest. Two gravid females carried across the gulf to the far ledge, or to some third terrace, or to a place with no void in it at all: an insurance, a duplicate, a hedge against the day the seam shifts or the river goes elsewhere, as rivers do. Declined, for three reasons, and they are set down here in order of increasing weight. First: the far ledge has its own frogs and its own account, and to introduce a predator into it is not benevolence, it is a decision taken on behalf of parties who were not asked. Second: within eleven generations the transplanted animals would be a different animal, unhemmed or hemmed in the wrong place, heavier or lighter, and calling it the same lineage would be a courtesy paid to a bookkeeping convenience. Third, and this is the finding: the four-thousand-year-long creature is not stored inside the shrews. It is stored in the relation between forty shrews and sixty paces, between fifty thousand clicks and a curfew, between two hundred born and a hundred and sixty gone. You cannot evacuate a relation. You can only carry away some of its ends, and what you have then is not a rescued thing, it is a fragment with the same fur.

So: no path, no widening, no closing of the edge, no falcons moved, no teeth renewed, no frost lifted, no notch tuned, no tree healed, no revelation, no ark. The power did not bless the place. Blessing is a thing done to what needs altering.
