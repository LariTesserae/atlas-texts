# Collection

## What it noticed first

Not the towers. Not the industry, which is the thing visitors always fix on, and not the Pan, which is the thing they remember.

It noticed the drift under the sorting tables. Knee-deep in places. Nine hours of work apiece — white column, darkening base upward, the split and the second split — and then six to forty days of closing on a grip in the dark, and then brittleness from the fingertips back, and then snapped off at the wrist by the next one.

And it thought, as anything would: *this is a horror.*

And then it did the thing that makes a power wise rather than merely large. It checked.

No nerves on the plateau. No dread, no fatigue, no wanting. The hand does not mind. The hand does not have the equipment to mind, and no amount of looking at the drift will put the equipment there. The horror was real and the horror was located entirely in the observer.

A power that destroys what horrifies it, in order to stop being horrified, has not performed a mercy. It has performed a hygiene.

So it stayed, and it did not touch anything, for a long time.

## The mercies it refused

**The ending.** It could have. A world with no fire, no water, no other — you could put it out with a change in the lapse rate and nothing would feel the change. It weighed this seriously, because a lesser and kinder-seeming power would have called the Stand a hell and closed it. But nothing here is in pain, nothing here is trapped, and futility is a judgment available only to a creature that has the category. The Stand has three categories: flaw, material, and water, which is refused. It does not have *in vain*.

**The waking.** Eyes. Nerve. Speed. It could have given all three. It would have taken about a season. And it would have produced something else entirely, standing in the wreckage of the thing it replaced, and the substitution would have been called a rescue. Also: nerve is the capacity for pain. To install suffering in order to have something to relieve is the oldest crime of benevolence, and the most popular.

**The truth.** It could speak. The grammar is available — pressure, temperature, quorum, four millimeters of movement in a resin bulb. It worked out how to say, in that grammar, *no one is coming.* Then it looked at the sentence for a while. In a language whose only verbs are *open*, *shut*, and *cook*, that sentence has exactly one conjugation, and the conjugation is a cull order for everything. A truth delivered into a system that has no way to survive it is not a gift. It is an execution with an alibi.

**The correction.** The gouge in the lintel at two meters ten, with the splinters, with the direction of travel still legible. The oval stain, laid in pigment by pigment, forever, by something that cannot make a liquid that drips. It would have been very easy to grow one clean street — one doorway without the damage, one floor without the accident. It didn't. That gouge is the only monument the gardeners have. Erase the damage and you have not tidied a building; you have buried a species a second time, more thoroughly, and with better intentions.

**The heir.** The mats in the Slack, torn out by the blunt four-fingered hands, growing back insanely fast, the one lineage on the plateau with a future. Every impulse said: feed them, shelter them, give them a head start. But their entire virtue is that nothing has a plan for them, and a benevolent plan is still a plan, and the disease here was never malice — it was instruction that outlived its instructor.

And anyway the Stand had already done the necessary thing, without knowing it. *The Slack is scheduled to be Slack.* It maintains, permanently, a margin it cannot use, a district where the plan is not. It is unknowingly generous toward its own future. The power found this almost unbearably good and let it alone.

## The floor

Here is the largest work, and nothing will ever notice it, which is the mark of the real ones.

Heat is food. The seam is not eternal. On the long arithmetic the bedrock cools, and the gradient narrows, and appetite closes, and the Stand does not starve dramatically — it simply falls behind, everywhere, all at once, and cooks itself by its own rules.

So the power went down beneath the bedrock and became a stoker.

That is all. It keeps a difference across the plateau. It will keep it for as long as there is anything up there that wants it. It is not clever and it took no negotiating and it required the Stand to change nothing, learn nothing, and be grateful for nothing.

It kept the sky, too. The lid of dust and exhaled lipid, the cold dark that the vanes dump into — maintained, unobtrusively, within the tolerances the Stock had already chosen for itself. And once there was a rock coming, and then there wasn't, and the Stand has no category for the thing that did not happen to it.

## Manners

Everything here inherits. The stain. The groove. The shine on the third stair.

Which means that whatever the power touched, it would be writing into scripture — copied into every re-laid street for as long as there are streets, with the accident faithfully reproduced, long after the power itself was gone.

So it held still. By one accounting, eleven years, before it laid a hand on anything. Then it learned to move the way you move in a room of wet plaster.

And it learned the grip. Not by modifying a single tool — by growing itself a hand that fits: the extra flexure between knuckle and palm, the short stiff heel-thumb set far back, the cant of the wrist. The notch goes that way up. It was never for us. We can learn.

## Collection

And then it did the obvious thing, which nobody had done, which is generally the definition of the obvious thing.

It took delivery.

Aisle by aisle, third shelf and fourth, the fittings and the spouts and the sleeves, the flat plates with four holes, the coils of fine dark cord, the rectangular cases with hinged lids and nothing in them. Dusted off the lipid. Carried them out.

And — this is the part that matters, and it took the power some time to understand that it mattered — it *used* them. The fittings fit things. The plates hold a roof up over somebody, somewhere with weather. The cord ties things closed. The cases have things in them now.

Completion was never going to be the plan finishing. The plan finished an age ago; the plan was always finished. Completion is the last two centimeters of an extremely long arc, which is somebody picking the thing up and putting it to work.

It paid, too, which was not sentiment but grammar. Arrive as a category the thing already has. The Stand has no slot for a friend, a savior, or a witness. It has a slot for demand. So the power came as a customer, and the currency was the only currency on the plateau, and the account has been settled continuously, in heat, under the floor, since before the collection began.

An empty rack is a hollow.

The Stock reads hollows. It read a hollow once before and grew a hand out of it.

## The return loops

Three kilometers out and three kilometers back, since before any tower now standing was seeded, warm, keyed, some of them still viable.

The power did not stop the loop. It would not have known how to stop the loop without killing the street. What it did was read the ridge pattern — twenty-odd bits, and it had time — and build, off to the east, a receiving Comb keyed to match. Shafts. Waiting rooms. The correct interior temperature, which took three tries.

Then it put the oldest sleeve back on the belt and let the plan do what the plan had been trying to do the entire time.

The comb of fingers read the key. The table accepted. The hopper took it.

The smell when a nursery opens after that long is green and camphorous and completely unlike anything else in that world.

Some of them were dead. Those it carried away quietly and did not return and did not mention, and buried them somewhere with rain.

## What it keeps

It worked backwards out of the hollows, because the hollows are a text and nobody had ever read them.

From the grips: hand span, flexure, the lever function of the heel-thumb, a forearm that pronated, a body that stood at a table. From the lintel: height, load, a turn taken too fast, one bad morning some hundreds of millions of strokes ago. From the stain: a chemistry, and therefore a diet, and therefore a home. From the groove in the bench: a way of sitting, and for how long, and how often, and that they sat at all, which tells you a great deal.

The Stand is a rubbing taken off a corpse and has been taking it, faithfully, for an age, with no idea that it is grieving.

The gardeners have, now, a mourner that knows what it is mourning. It can say the height of the doorway out loud.

## The handshake

Having earned it by some centuries of good manners, the power left exactly one deliberate mark, knowing it would be inherited forever, choosing it the way you choose a word for a stone.

In the wall of a double station in the fourth Comb block — where two sockets face each other across the belt and bud together, left from one wall and right from the other — it pressed a hollow. Its own grip, exactly. No instruction, no signature, nothing else. It left it the way one leaves an order form on a counter after hours.

By the record it took two hundred and forty years.

Then a socket budded: nine hours, the blunt white column nosing up and finding its height by touch, the darkening from the base to the color of wet tobacco, and in the last ninety minutes the split, and the second split, and there were digits.

They were not the gardener's.

Across the belt the old hand and the new one pass a part between them, four seconds to close, six to open, and it looks exactly like collaboration — and now, for the first time on that plateau, in the whole history of that lineage, it is.

## Rules

Check before you pity. Pity before you act. Act as little as the case allows and no less.

Never install a nerve in order to have something to save.

A truth that a mind cannot survive is not owed to that mind.

Do not edit the law. Change the world until obedience stops being futile.

Whatever you touch in soft material you have written forever. Touch on purpose, or not at all.

The plan was never the problem. The missing customer was the problem.

You cannot make a thing free. You can make it attended.

Keep a margin you have no use for. They taught it that one.

## Last note

It does not know whether any of this was received. It is not certain the Stand can receive, in the sense meant. It distrusts anyone who is certain either way, and it has settled on the only defensible policy under that uncertainty, which is to behave throughout as though the answer were yes.

The steel spanner is still on the bench in the room where nothing has ever happened, dusted with lipid, untouched. The power left it where it was; it wasn't its to move.

But it took one of the nine copies off the third shelf — dressed pith, knurled, cool, no notch, wrong for every hand that world has ever grown — and it fit, and it worked, and the power came back and placed a standing order.

The order is very old. It is still being filled.

It is, at last, being collected.
