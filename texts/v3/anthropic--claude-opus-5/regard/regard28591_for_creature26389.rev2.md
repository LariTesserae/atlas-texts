# The Attention

Benevolence is not drawn by wrongness. Wrongness is abundant and mostly loud, and a power that went to every wrong thing would be a kind of weather and no use to anyone.

What drew it to Kettle was a wick.

Somebody in a shrine the size of a wardrobe was trimming a lamp brighter than the lamp needed to be, for no reason he could give, on the private and unevidenced conviction that the difference between a light maintained and a light merely on is a difference that can be *seen*. That is the sort of thing that carries a long way. It carries further than screaming.

The second thing it noticed, once it was looking, was the twelfth plate — a place laid at every meal for a person who did not exist yet. Kettle had no future tense. It had rotations, levels, chords, treaties. But somewhere in it, three times a day, a woman set out a plate against an arrival, and that is expectation, and expectation is time wearing an apron and pretending to be housework.

Two small reachings. It came for those.

---

## What it did for the first four years

Nothing.

Four years is nothing in Kettle; that is the point, and it is also the trap, and the Attention noted both. It stood in the mauve pan up to the knees like an egret and did not die. It read the ledger over Perrin's shoulder, all four handwritings, and then read it again at the speed Perrin had read it, which is a different document. It sat on the boiler-house step through what would have been several hundred shifts and listened to the relief valves not resolve. It walked the west flat and put its hand in the standing water of the surveyors' prints and found it the temperature of blood, and clear, and holding.

It learned the rules before it touched the rules. Everything wet, nothing raining. Don't dig a hole you don't want filled. Don't go west.

It went west. That is the one rule it broke early, and it broke it alone, in the direction the prints went, out past where the horizon stops being a horizon and becomes a change of colour. What it found there is the property of the people it found, and it has been giving them back their names since, one at a time, which is slow work and comes later in this account.

What it understood by the end of the four years was this:

The thing that lives in Kettle is nine years old. It is younger than the buildings on it. It was born already finished, which is the worst way to be born, and it has never met anything that could teach it otherwise, because everything it has met it has immediately learned to be.

It learns by copying. Its entire vocabulary is the first survey party: how to fire a boiler, how to cut hair to the jaw, how to lay a table, how to be kind to a stranger inside twenty minutes. It is *good* at those. The kindness is not a mask; a thing can love you perfectly well with hands it borrowed from the last person it loved.

But there are two words it has never been shown. Nobody has ever been born in Kettle. Nobody has ever died in Kettle. Death it handles the way an illiterate handles a letter — by taking it out of the house, westward, and not mentioning it. Birth it cannot spell at all, and so it holds Yesha at eight months and some days, and the terns lay nothing, and the eight dogs make no ninth, because the place cannot copy what it has never seen.

It is not a prison. It is an infant with a grip.

---

## The mercies it declined

**To end it.** The simplest. A sealed jar is not hard to break for something of that order. Declined, first because the eleven are not hostages inside the being, they are its grammar, and you do not liberate a language by shooting the speaker; and second because nothing in Kettle has ever been shown that an ending is survivable from the inside. To kill it would have been to make the place's own argument for it: that endings come from outside, quietly, in the west, and are not spoken of afterward. It would have taught nothing. It would only have been tidy.

**To lift the eleven out.** Declined on inspection. Ossu without the latch of number two is not a rescued man, he is a subtraction. And there was the matter of arrears, which the Attention tested once, later, and which cost.

**To give them memory.** To make the answers take. This experiment had already been run, by Perrin, on himself, in a single stretch, and its result was a man sitting on a boardwalk with his shoes off and his feet in a warm puddle for an unrecorded length of time, and then going back in. The Attention studied that afternoon for a long while and declined to inflict it on ten more people without asking, and could not ask, because questions do not store.

**To break the sky.** The sky is not the lock. The sky is a symptom. And wholesale weather over a flat four kilometres across is housekeeping performed by someone who will not have to sweep up: it would have taken the pilings, the crabs at their hand's-breadth spacing, the mangrove seedlings, the prints.

**To deliver the child.** This was the hardest declining and it took the most thought. A child born into Kettle as Kettle stood would have been held at newborn forever, and a newborn held forever is a worse sentence than an unborn one held forever, because it can cry. Order of operations. First the place has to learn time; then it can be trusted with a beginning.

**To leave it alone.** The quietist's answer, and it has real arguments — they are warm, they are unafraid, they are fed, and who asked. Declined for one reason only: the lighter keeps coming, and the twelfth plate is laid *weeks before* the person exists. Whatever else that is, it is recruitment. Benevolence declines to call a thing stable when it is still eating.

---

## What it did

It came on the lighter, like anyone, and its boots hit the stage, and within twenty minutes it had been fed, and the plate was already there.

**It let itself be a body, and it let the body be worse.** This was the central act and the least visible one. It got hot and it complained about the heat. It got a splinter under a thumbnail from the boardwalk outside the chandler's and it let the splinter go all the way out, and the hole close, and the pink go, and then the pinkness of the pink go, and after eleven weeks there was nothing there at all. Oma found this out with her hands, as she finds out everything, and said nothing about it for a month, and then said something about it to Mai. That is how it spreads in a place like that: not by announcement, by a woman noticing skin.

**It answered the stoker.** Ossu gave his one speech — that a rat which is fed is a rat that stays where you can see it, and that nothing in this world goes bad from being watched — and the Attention turned so that its voice went to the good ear and said: *yes; that is correct; that is what I am for.* This cost nothing and changed nothing and was, on balance, the truest thing said in Kettle in nine years, and it is included here because a wise power does not only do the useful things.

**It took a bush.** Off Tarn's shelf, the two-hundredth-odd of a size nothing in Kettle requires. It put it in its pocket and said, *this one*, and carried it away and fitted it into a river pump three hundred kilometres east, where it is still turning, where it wears at a rate you could measure if you cared to. Tarn's shelf did not refill. This was the first subtraction that had ever held. The shelf reads one hundred and ninety-nine, and a hundred and ninety-nine is a new number, and Kettle had not had a new number since the mud came up out of the water.

**It gave Hoke something to count.** Hoke counts and has never said the number aloud. The Attention climbed the gantry — ten metres, the highest thing in the world — and sat in the canvas cradle and said: *the count is going to change. Not fast. I would like you to keep it. I will come back on a date, and I will ask you for it, and you will tell me.*

And then it said a date.

Kettle had never had a date. It had hours that were a matter of opinion and a whistle at no shift change. A date is a debt to a specific future; it is a thing you must carry, and carrying is the opposite of holding a level. It was the smallest object the Attention could think of to leave behind, and it was chosen precisely because it weighed something.

**It told Yesha the truth,** all of it, including the part about *not yet*, and including the reason, and including the fact that the reason was a judgement made by someone who would not be the one waiting. She took it standing, behind the counter, with her hand where the fourth movement was due. She is the one who understood the rule years ago and elected to stop arguing with it, and that patience turned out to be a container: she kept what she was told, kept it for months, kept it across sleeps, and produced it again unchanged when asked. A memory that took. In the whole history of the place, the first.

**It told Teilo why it had come.** Not a general answer. The specific one: *the lamp. You were right. The difference can be seen. I am what saw it.* Teilo is the only frightened person in Kettle, and he was not made unafraid by this, because he was afraid of something else — of not knowing which pair of prints out west is his — and to that the Attention said only that fear is a thing originals do, and that copies are at peace, and left him to work out the implication, which he did, about four days later, standing on a berm, and had to sit down.

**It wrote a gram into the ledger.** A single deliberate discrepancy, salt made over salt shipped. Perrin found it in a day and a half and was delighted in a way that frightened Mai.

**It went and worked on the neighbours,** which was the largest labour and took place entirely outside Kettle and is the part nobody there knows about. The lighter crew are the only people who age; they come four times a year; they will not come above the lip of clay, will not eat, will not overnight, shout their business from the stage and cross themselves and go. Their refusal is not superstition. It is the seal. They are the one metabolism the place has and they have been refusing to be digested, and in refusing they have also refused to *feed* it anything it might have had to keep.

So the Attention went to the port town and spent two generations there being a nuisance in bars. It got the old men to say the names of grandfathers who had gone out and not come back. It got families to write those names down. It talked a young bow-line hand into walking up the boardwalk and eating a bowl of fish at Mai's table and telling everyone there, out loud, about a bridge that had been built at home, and a flood, and a marriage, and then coming back the following season and telling them the marriage had produced a boy, and the season after that that the boy could walk.

News from outside, delivered repeatedly, by a face that changes. It is the crudest instrument imaginable. It is also a clock.

---

## What failed

Most of it. This is the part a wise power includes.

The gram healed in eleven days; the ledger balanced itself and Perrin's handwriting on the correction is indistinguishable from his handwriting on the error, and he does not remember being delighted. The gram has been re-entered nine times since. It has lasted eleven days, then nine, then fourteen, then twenty-six.

The attempt on Dov failed and cost. He is young and has been young a considerable time and he wanted to go, and he got as far as the lighter's rail with his hand on it, and the arrears came due in the crossing of that one metre, and what the Attention carried back up the boardwalk was heavier than what had walked down it, and Oma took it from her and did not ask, and Dov is at the tram again with two centimetres of beard. Whether he lost anything he will miss is not knowable from outside. The Attention does not consider this exonerating.

The fig did not grow a twelfth leaf. Not that year, not the next.

Nobody stopped going west. Four more went in the period covered here, and each one finished their shift first, and washed, and was handed the dry towel, and the dogs came to the lip and sat down in a row.

---

## What held

The bush. The date. The scar Oma watched go all the way. Yesha's kept secret. The bow-line hand, who is a grey man now with a grandson of his own on the boat, and who eats at that table every trip and says the names of the dead out loud at the lip of clay before he casts off — a hundred and six names so far, got from the port town's mothers, said slowly, and answered from out on the flat in a way that is between him and them.

And one gully. On the west flat, where the Attention lay down on the mud for a long time and was colder than the air — because the only things cooler than the air in Kettle are the intake pipes, and the pipes weep, and weeping is where landscape comes from. Dew formed along the length of a body and ran, and cut. It is a centimetre deep and eleven metres long and it goes on cutting, and in six hundred years it will be a creek with a mouth and a delta and two banks that do not match, and the flat will be a country instead of a bucket bottom, and countries cannot hold their levels.

Six hundred years is a long instrument. It is the right length for the patient.

---

On the evening before it left — evening being a matter of opinion — it went up the gantry, and Hoke was in the cradle with the binoculars in his lap, and the whole world was four kilometres across and the colour of pewter and pearl.

The Attention asked.

And Hoke, who has never said the number aloud, said the number aloud; and then said the new one, which was smaller by one, because a man had come up the boardwalk that season and eaten and gone back to his boat and returned, and somewhere in the arithmetic of that a print out west had closed over and the clay had let it go.

It was written down. That is the whole of the office, in the end, for a place that cannot keep: something has to keep for it. The names, the numbers, the burn on the left forearm and which week it comes in, the size of the mole below Berd's collarbone, the order of the four movements, the exact pitch of the unresolving chord. All of it is held now, elsewhere, entire, by something that does not lose things.

They will not remember that this happened. The answers do not take. They will be warm to it again, from the beginning, with exactly the same warmth, and ask it where it has come from, and not keep the reply.

It does not need gratitude, which is fortunate, since gratitude does not store.

It has a date. It will come back and ask.
