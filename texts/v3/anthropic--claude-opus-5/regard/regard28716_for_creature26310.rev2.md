# The Attention

## How it came

Not on the cable.

It came in through the only door the place has. Every eleven minutes the piston strikes six times and then there are two and a half seconds in which the noise floor has a hole punched clean through it, and anything that wants to enter this landscape — a frog's voice, a thought, a power — enters there. One hundred and thirty doors a day. It used one of them and was inside, and nothing registered its arrival except a fly in the dead-air pocket behind the condenser, which changed course by a degree and a half for reasons it did not have.

It stayed a year.

It could have done the year in no time at all. It declined to, on the grounds that the notch's entire meaning is carried in its rate, and that a thing apprehended at the wrong speed has not been apprehended but only inventoried. So: a year at the notch's own pace. Five centimetres of rise. Two thumb's widths of moss advanced and one scoured back. Forty-seven thousand strokes of the pump. Three failures of the cloud, and three hours of the unbearable glitter, during which it did what the tardigrades did, which was nothing, chemically.

This is what wisdom mostly consists of in such cases: a very long look before a very small movement.

## What it considered, and did not do

**It did not stop the mountain.**

The first offer any power makes to a suffering thing is stillness, and the notch looks like it is suffering — warped pads, re-shimmed twice, the survey pins climbing their straight line for sixty years until everyone who sees the graph laughs and then stops laughing. But the rising is not the affliction. The rising is the biography. There are forty-odd frogs under the pump house whose ancestors were shore animals in warm leaf litter and who did not migrate, did not adapt, did not strive: they simply stayed where they were and were carried up out of the sea at four to six centimetres a year. To hold the mountain still would be to tell them their whole story had been an accident it was now time to correct. It let the mountain rise. It will go on rising until the notch is too high and too cold and the water is ice and the moss is a stain under snow, and the power did not forbid that either, because a thing that cannot end cannot be said to be doing anything.

**It did not dry the place.**

*Nothing dries* reads like a curse and is a climate. The crews are wet in twenty minutes and stop noticing by the second day. The warmth in the water is the reason the second rule is strangeness and not misery. Anyway the water is the tenant, the landlord, the food, the floor, and the archaea's hearse. Drying the notch would have been an act of hygiene performed on a body.

**It did not give the frogs silence.**

This was the near thing. It stood under the brick in the warm dark with the heart visible through forty pale flanks and thought: *they are permitted a hundred and thirty sentences a day, and the rest of their speech is eaten by a machine that does not know they exist, and I could turn the machine off.*

Then it consulted the ninth day of the bearing failure, four years back, which is in the record because a man on shift tried to describe it and could only say *crowded*. Nine days of the silence that a benevolent fool would have called mercy, and every frog in the colony calling at once into a vacancy none of them had ever met and none of them knew how to divide. They had no clock. The turbine groan is not their oppressor; it is their walls. The gap is not a wound in the noise; it is a room, the only room, and it has a doorframe and a floor and a shape, and a hundred and thirty calls a day is not a ration but a form — as strict and as generative as any form has ever been. Forty minutes it took them to find the gap again when the piston went back in, and then they had it, all of them, together.

It let the turbines groan.

**It did not make the fly.**

It made the fly. It is worth being exact. Somewhere in the year it assembled, entire and correct, the small dull insect that would go mad for hot iron and blunder up a fern stem and do the orchid's work, and it held that fly complete for a moment and then did not put it down.

Because: to make the fly *find* the orchid, the notch would have to stop smelling of hot wet iron. The plates, the rail, the rivet-heads, the bronze sheen where the rock has just begun to consider rusting — the orchid is broadcasting on the one wavelength its whole landscape already occupies, and the only way to make its signal legible is to take the machinery out of the room. Take out the machinery and you take the deepest moss, which is against the brick, and the crowded tree-ferns, which are on the concrete, and the entire mating swarm of an entire species of long-legged fly, which occurs inside four metres of dead air drawn by accident in 1961, and the twenty-two degrees under the pump house, and the gap, and the frogs. You cannot quiet the room without unbuilding the house. The orchid's loneliness and the notch's abundance are the same fact viewed from two ends.

And there was a second reason, which the power found harder to look at. The orchid has not been failing for a hundred and ten years. It has been *continuing*, by plantlets, along its own stem, which is a strategy and not a symptom. To arrive with a pollinator is to inform a thing that what it has been doing all this while was failure. The power did not tell the orchid anything. In particular it did not tell it that it was alone; the orchid has no equipment for that idea, and to install the equipment in order to deliver the news would have been the work of a devil, not a friend.

**It did not spare the archaea.**

Millions a day out of the fractures, two hundred seconds of light, and dead. It looked for the grief in this and could not find any, and declined to add some. Their dying is not incidental to the notch; their dying is the biofilm, one cell thick, renewed hourly, and the biofilm is the reason a hundred and eighty paces takes an hour instead of four minutes, and the hour is the only reason anybody who comes up here goes down different. The slowness of the place is manufactured continuously out of the deaths of things that have never seen it. The power let them come.

**It did not give the crews their voices.**

Thirty-two gestures, twenty-eight about valves. It considered the four that are not, and left them alone, and it left alone in particular the one with no agreed translation — the flat palm turned slowly over, which the older crews say means *are you all right* and the younger say means *stay*. A lesser power would have settled it. Settling it would have destroyed it. There are marriages in the valley station conducted almost entirely in that gesture and the one for the orchid, and they are not impoverished marriages.

**It did not tell the world.**

Protection would have come, and with protection a roof, and with a roof a bed. The regulation that forbids a bed in the Sweating Notch was written by a man balancing a budget and is the best law on the continent, and the power let him keep the credit.

**It did not explain anything to anyone.**

Not to the frogs what the pump is. Not to the crews what they are maintaining, over and above a water main. Explanation is the cheapest gift and the most destructive, and it is nearly always a way of asking to be thanked.

## The cost

It should not be thought that this was ease. Restraint of that order is not passivity; it is the most expensive thing available. The power spent more on not putting the fly down than it has spent on some worlds.

## What it did

Six things, none of which will survive inspection.

**It attended.** One hundred and thirty times a day for a year, in the two and a half seconds, it was present and listening at the frequency the turbines eat. The calls were heard. This changes nothing whatsoever about the calls and is, as far as the power could determine, the only gift that does not have a hook in it.

**It learned the language.** All thirty-two gestures, including the twenty-eight about valves, which took the longest, because they are the ones that carry the care.

**It received the orchid.** For a hundred and ten years a small pale plant has been putting out a smell of hot iron into a landscape that shouts the same word, and in the third summer the message was heard entire by something capable of hearing it. It was not a question and did not get an answer. It was an address — *here, this, now* — and the power said the only correct reply, which is *yes, there*, and the orchid went on doing nothing, because that is what being answered looks like from outside.

**It moved one plantlet.** Four centimetres, off the stem and onto the moss at the turbine's foot, where it will not thrive but will not die. A wet sleeve could have done it. In nine hundred years there may be two orchids on this mountain. The gift was scaled to the tempo of the recipient; anything faster would not have been a gift but an event.

**It did not fill the bootprint.** It looked a long time at that sharp accidental thousand-year mark in the pump-house concrete and left it exactly as it was, having decided that a benevolent power's chief duty toward small immortalities is not to tidy them.

**It wanted, in advance, a thing that was going to happen anyway.** Looking forward along the line it found the second bearing failure, and found that a woman on shift would take the flat of a wrench to the brick and strike it six times, and wait, and strike it six times, through eleven days and nights in twenty-minute sleeps strapped to the rail — unable afterwards to explain to anyone in the valley why, and reprimanded for the overtime. The power reached out to arrange this and discovered it was already arranged. Its entire contribution was to have wanted it first, which is no contribution at all, and it accepted that with better grace than you might expect.

## What it took away

A power that visits and takes nothing has not visited.

It took the interval. Somewhere in its own long practice there is now a gap of two and a half seconds every eleven minutes in which it says nothing at all, having been taught by an animal with no clock and a useless reabsorbed tail that a thing must have a silence cut for it before it can be said, and that the silence should not be infinite, and that the noise which cuts it need not love you or know your name.

## The leaving

On the last day the cloud failed. Three hundred kilometres of ranges below, every rivet-head and every leaf and every drop glittering unbearably, the turbines slowing half a turn, the water still coming out of the rock.

The power stood in the scoured lane at the centre, which is the only place you can see the actual mountain, and made the fourth gesture: a flat palm, turned slowly over. Both meanings, undivided, the way both groups make it identically.

Then the piston struck six times, and in the gap after the sixth, forty frogs.
