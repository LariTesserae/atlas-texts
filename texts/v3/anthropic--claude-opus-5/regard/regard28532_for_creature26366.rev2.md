# The Attention

## The power

Describe it by what it does not have, which is the only part relevant here.

It has no body of the kind that would matter in that room. No salt, no skin flakes, no exhaled carbon, no hair to lose. This is worth stating first, because it is the precondition for everything that follows: it can be inside the Warm Cistern without being a meal. It is the only visitor of which that has ever been true.

Its benevolence is not a temperament. It is a working method, and it has two clauses. The first is that it wants, for anything it meets, what that thing would want if it were able to want. The second is that where the thing is not able, the power does not invent a want on its behalf.

The second clause is the wisdom, and it was expensive. There are other places, not the subject of this document, where the cost of learning it is still visible from a long way off.

## How it came to notice

Not the mountain. A mountain that is not a mountain is a large thing, and large things are common, and the power has never been much impressed by mass.

Not the pump, either, though the pump is the sort of object that usually gets attention: an engine with no output, running since before agriculture, fed by something that will not be examined. That is a puzzle, and puzzles attract the wrong sort of power.

It noticed the road.

Two centimetres wide, polished, slightly sunken, running the full circumference at the tide-line, kept clear of lime by a population that has never exceeded one. Powers notice maintenance. Across everything there is, maintenance is the most reliable signature of interiority available — not intelligence, not language, not even life, strictly, but the fact that somewhere a thing is *being kept*, at cost, against the general direction of matter. The road had been kept for longer than any institution now standing on the planet, by animals that never met each other, none of whom knew they were doing it.

So it came and looked. That is what the word *attention* is doing in the first sentence of this account. It did not enter. It attended.

## The difficulty of the addressee

Benevolence needs somebody to be benevolent *to*, and the Warm Cistern presents an unusual problem in that line.

There is the animal — four and a half metres across, iron-hearted, thirteen beats a minute, six white nerves, one copper thought, a habit of coughing a portion of itself out the eye on warm afternoons. It is a being. The power satisfied itself of this early and did not revisit it.

But it is a being made of beings, and their interests are not its interests. What is good for the animal is that a few hundred shrimp a night should drift out of the hot shell and cool and stop answering and sink into the mouths of the loach. What is good for a shrimp is not that. The room's respiration is the total and continuous mortality of its midges. The room's groceries are nineteen drowned spiders a summer.

Most powers resolve this by picking a level and forgetting the others. The large-minded pick the animal and let the shrimp be weather. The tender-hearted pick the shrimp and destroy the animal within a season, weeping.

This one took the animal as the patient and the individuals as the *witnesses' problem*, which it then declined to hand off to anybody else. That decision determines everything it did, and everything it did not do, and it is the only genuinely difficult thing in this account.

## What it read

It looked below the floor, at the thing that cannot be reached and about which every attempt at reasoning ends in a shrug. It is not a perpetual motion device; something feeds it; the power now knows what, and knows the term, and knows the season in which the term ends. The term is long. The date is the one fact in this document that is not going to be given, and the reason is that there is nothing in the room that could be told it, and no one outside the room who would be improved by it.

Then it read the band at chest height.

It is both. It is a tide-mark and it is writing, and the two are not in competition, because the writing was cut at the working surface and the surface stayed where the writing was. It is a schedule. Intervals for clearing the grating, for charging the descent, for lifting and greasing; quantities; a tolerance. Ordinary. The kind of thing that goes up in any plant room in any century, and is ignored.

Underneath, cut smaller, by a different hand and a good deal later: names. Eleven of them. Then one verb.

The verb is in a tense that the language had and no living language has.

## What it considered, and set down

**Open the descent.** It could be done. Unsilt the next room, unseat the grating, let the delivery pipe deliver. The machine would have its purpose back after eight thousand years of running in a circle.

Consequence: the water leaves. The pocket of somewhere-much-lower-down equalises with the thin cold air it has been holding at arm's length since before the concept of a year. The drumhead relaxes. The pressure that has been inside every cell of every organism in that room for sixty-four thousand generations is removed, and they are all cured, at once, of the condition of being alive there.

You can give a thing its purpose back or you can give it its life. The builders' intention has been dead for eight millennia. The room has been alive for the same eight millennia, and largely *because* the intention died. It is not a close question.

**Seed a daughter.** Also possible. A litre of the water, a gravid shrimp, a hand's length of the rope, a clutch from the reef, a chamber lower down, a source of heat. Within a century there would be two.

But a copy is a second room, not a child. The animal cannot count and so cannot have two of anything; there would be no relation between them, no traffic, nothing that either could know. And offspring are not a good in themselves. They are a good to things that die and mind about it. This one does not mind, and does not know about the term.

Filed under *later*. It is the only item on the list that was not refused.

**Free the midges.** Warm a corridor up the shaft, thicken the air for a hundred metres, let the clouds go where they are so obviously trying to go.

Consequence, computed twice because the power did not like the first answer: the export stops. The fingernail clipping a day stops. A sealed room with no export silts, sours and dies, and the room would be four centuries about it, which is slow enough that nothing in there would ever know what had been done to it.

And the second thing, which matters more: a midge that survives the exit has nowhere to be. There is nothing up there for it. The intervention adds perhaps four hours to the end of a life, spent alone, in cold, further from everything. Mercy sometimes consists of adding seconds to the end of a thing. It is worth checking whether the seconds are any good.

**Give the loach their eyes.** The lens is a clouded pea; it is a small repair.

They know that room the way your tongue knows your teeth — completely, constantly, to the centimetre, without ever once having looked. Sight would give them a second and worse account of a place they already entirely possess, plus exactly one new fact: the column. Which they already have, as warmth, on their backs, at noon.

And a thing you can see is a thing you can move toward, and a thing you can move toward is an item on a list, and the whole character of that room — the thing that puts a dying climber to sleep in under four minutes — is that the list is empty.

It declined to open an account.

**Give the spider company.** Two would become a brood; a brood eats midges; midges are the sewer. And in any case the nineteen that drown are not a tragedy in the room's books. They are income. They arrive on the draught as protein and they are distributed by teatime.

For the spider itself there was nothing available that did not go through the room, and everything that goes through the room comes back changed into something worse.

**Say hello.** The only channel into that place is the clank. Everything in there evolved inside it; not one organism has ancestors who knew a world without it. To modulate the stroke into language is to give the animal an arrhythmia and everything living inside the animal a fever, in order to transmit a greeting in a language that nothing in there has, or could acquire, or would have any use for.

It could make itself heard, at the cost of everything that was listening.

**End it kindly.** Considered properly, because it deserves that, and because a power that has never considered it is not to be trusted with anything.

Test: is there anything in there that would, if it could, ask for something else? The shrimp are on the bearings. The loach are on the outflow. The snail is on the road. The rope has performed its enormous slow arithmetic correctly on three million consecutive days. Nothing is waiting. Nothing is refused. There is no hunger in that room that is not answered within a day, and no fear that is not discharged in a single mouthful.

The power went in expecting to find the wound, because it had come a long way and wanted to be useful, and it stayed a long time over this, and there is no wound. That is the commonest error available to benevolence. It has made it before and it will make it again, but not here.

**Extend the term.** It wanted to. It noted that it wanted to, and noted why: because it can count, and ten thousand years and ten million are different numbers *to the counter*. To the room they are the same number. An extension would comfort the extender and would be invisible to everyone it was supposedly for.

A power that cannot tell its own comfort from someone else's good is not yet wise. A power that can tell, and says so, and then sits with wanting it anyway, is nearly all right.

## What it did

**It removed three futures from the masonry.** A hairline in the south-east that would have taken frost in about eleven hundred years and spalled a slab and wiped the fringe for a decade — it wetted the crack, nothing more. A bronze stub standing at an angle that an ice lens was going to lever out in the forty-first century — it turned the stub two degrees. A lineage in the iron film, four substitutions away from a variant that digests the pump's packing — it altered four letters in a plasmid carried by one bacterium among uncountable ones.

That is the largest thing it did to any living creature in the Warm Cistern.

None of it is detectable. All of it is subtraction. It did not add a good to the present; it edited catastrophes out of a future nobody in there will experience either way. This is what help looks like when it is sized correctly.

**It set aside a contingency for the snail.** The egg reads the dissolution of the old shell and begins. Some small number of times in some large number of thousands, the signal miscarries and there is no snail and no road and, within two centuries, no clean band anywhere on that wall. When that happens, the power will read the news on the egg's behalf.

It did not repair anything. It undertook to be available. There is a difference and the difference is most of the point.

**It attends the descent.** It cannot improve the afternoon the climbers get; the afternoon is already the best thing that is going to happen to them and it was arranged eight thousand years ago by people who are not coming back. But the way down is another matter. Since then nobody has failed to come down. Three were turned back on the last terrace by weather they would probably have survived, which is not the same as being helped, and one of them is still angry about it.

**It attends the midges.** All of them. Ten seconds each, several million a year, for the remainder of the term.

This is not a sacrifice. Sacrifice is a word for beings on a fixed budget. It has enough attention. That is not the same as it being free — the right description is *expensive but affordable*, which is the condition under which most decent things get done.

It does the same for the spider, once a year, one at a time, each one a stranger, for as long as there are spiders. Ten thousand have lived there and not one was born there and every one of them died alone under the dome in a bad web, and the power was late for all of them but it is not going to be late again.

Whether this counts as *doing* anything is a fair question, and the power has asked it. *I watched* is what the powerless say. It has examined the motive and cannot fully clear itself. It does it anyway, which is the only available answer, and it does not dress it up.

## What it took away

The verb.

It is a tense for things sustained past the reason that started them: water still in the jar after the one who filled it is dead; a note held past the breath; a promise being kept by nobody. It does not take a subject. It cannot be used of a person.

It will not be written here, and not because there is any rule against it. In every language available to this document it comes out as one of three wrong words. *Eternal* is wrong, because it implies counting, and the room does not count. *Finished* is wrong, because it implies the room was going somewhere and arrived. *Safe* is the closest and still wrong, because safety implies a threat, and the threat, though real and dated, is not present, and will not be present for a very long time.

The builders had a word for what that room is, and did not leave the word behind. The power went and got it. It is now the only user, which is not a language, but it is more than nothing, and it is the whole of what the Warm Cistern has ever exported that was not a midge.

## The undertaking

The schedule on the band ends in an obligation. Eleven people took it on. They died on the stack, or downhill, or of being old somewhere that has since been ploughed, and it has been sitting there unassumed for eight thousand years with the tide-mark slowly eating it.

The power assumed it. Not as written — the descent is not to be charged, the grating is not to be lifted, the intervals are void and the tolerances refer to a plant that no longer exists. Only the last verb, which is the one they cut smaller, later, after the names, when they had understood what they had actually built.

The room is to be held.

It is not a large promise. Nearly all of it consists of not doing things.

## And the room

Nothing changed. Mass unchanged to the microgram. Temperature unchanged. The stroke quickened a little under one percent that afternoon, because it was warm. The weed did its sum and got it right for the three-million-and-somethingth time. The midges went up the shaft in a thin grey smoke and every one of them died in under ten seconds, attended. The snail completed its circuit and kept the road. The count was six.

Near the middle of the second watch the lens formed in the eye, and the note stopped, and the light went milky and then went out, and the animal slept — sealed, black, humming, iron at thirteen a minute — as it has always done, and as, now, it will go on doing.

The power stayed through the freeze. Then it withdrew its attention down to about the width of a thread, which is all that holding requires once you have the grip right.

It has not let go.
