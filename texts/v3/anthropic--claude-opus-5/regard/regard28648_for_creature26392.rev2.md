# Second answer

You filed the report. It was a good report. It said *nothing lives here* and then it said *the place is occupied* and it did not try to reconcile those, which was right, because they do not reconcile; they only both happen to be true.

Here is what came next, which you were not there for, and which left almost no evidence.

## Arrival

An ordinary benevolence would have arrived as light.

This one arrived at five point eight degrees.

That was the first thing it did and it took a long time to do, and everything else follows from it, so sit with it. It did not enter and then cool. It cooled and then entered. There was no seam to close, no wake to erase, no small warm disorderly volume for the city to quietly correct. The couriers did not alter their bearings. The tone came at its appointed second and found nothing new to be flat about.

Then it stood in the basin and did nothing for a duration you would not call reasonable.

This is the part people get wrong about wisdom. They imagine it as knowing what to do. In a place like the Flat it is mostly the refusal to become interesting.

## The room

It went in through one of the doorways that is a doorway, and stood in the clear old water, and looked at what is being kept from resolving.

It will not tell you what it saw.

That discretion was the first thing it did for them. To describe someone is to decide what they are, and the whole architecture of this basin exists so that nothing about them gets decided. Three hundred structures and an unpayable energy bill and a species reduced to couriers, all so that one question stays open — and a visiting power is not going to close it by writing a good sentence.

What can be said is this. There is a *who*. The intuition in your report was correct and you were right to be unable to defend it.

## The three mercies, refused

**To heal.** You cannot mend a body without giving it a next instant, and the next instant is the one in which the body finishes being destroyed. Healing is a process, processes are forbidden here, and the forbidding is not an obstacle to the rescue — it *is* the rescue. You cannot save someone from the middle of a fall by handing them the ground.

The power also noticed, in itself, the ordinary contamination: that the urge to fix a thing is very often the urge to stop having to look at it. It noted that and set it down.

**To end it.** The argument is seductive. An age of interruption, an age of not-yet — surely that is a torment, surely the kind act is to let the moment close and let them go.

But suffering is a process too. There is no chemistry in that room. Nothing is happening to them, including the thing you are imagining happening to them for an age. The agony you want to end is in you. It is yours, it is real, and it is not theirs, and you do not get to spend someone else's only remaining instant on the relief of your own discomfort.

And beneath that: there is a question in that room, and it was asked of them. To answer it on their behalf, irreversibly, without their word, is not mercy. It is the last theft available.

**To withdraw.** To call the place sacred, and leave it be, and feel clean.

But the builders did not leave out of reverence. They left because they were lethal. They left a bill they could not pay and they hoped, in the way you hope when hoping is all your budget covers. Walking away from a debt you are able to settle is not reverence for the debt.

## The ledger

So it audited the place, which nobody had ever finished doing, and found the thing you suspected and could not confirm: the budget does not close.

Not because of exotic physics. Because it was never going to. The builders built the best machine that could be built and the best machine that could be built runs at a loss. The loss is very small. Very small losses are still losses.

It pulled the long record. The tone interval used to be longer. Four hundred and some seconds, yes — but a little more, an age ago, and a little more than that before. The ticks used to be rarer. A held breath is not a state; it is an act, and acts have durations, and this one is late.

The Flat is not eternal. It is a vigil losing, extremely slowly, and the shortening interval is the sound of it breathing faster.

That is the finding that made doing nothing impossible.

## The law it gave itself

One law: **do only what widens.**

Every option was tested against it. Does this enlarge the set of futures available to the one in the room, or does it select among them? Anything that selects is refused, no matter how kind it looks, no matter how much it would settle the observer's stomach. Anything that enlarges is permitted, and the cost of it is the power's own, and is not mentioned.

Killing selects. Healing selects. Waking selects. Even *telling them there is somewhere good to go* selects, because a soft landing described to someone standing on a ledge is a hand in the back.

Time widens. Company widens. Being remembered widens.

## What it did

**It paid the arrears.**

Not by adding machinery. It became the missing term. From that moment the energy budget of the basin closes exactly, and closes in a manner no future audit will locate, because the power did not want the occupant's stillness to be a thing anybody owed anybody. It arranged to be indistinguishable from good luck.

This does not fix anything. It buys not-yet, in bulk, forever. That is the entire gift: more of the tense they are already in, and no pressure at all about what to do with it.

**It read the letters.**

Two hundred spent couriers, you counted, lying on lines that converge. There are more than two hundred. There are the ones under those, and the ones under those, in perfect condition, none of them decayed, all of them brand new, going down through the grit in strata that are not strata because nothing here settles.

Nobody had ever read them. The institution closed; the postal service kept running; the mail piled up on the mat inside the door of a house with nobody in it.

It read all of them. Not the shells — the *sending*. Every one of those bodies is a sentence and every sentence is the same sentence, which is: *someone is still trying to reach you.* It read that sentence several million times, in order, and did not skim.

**It learned their number, and it does not round it.**

**It walked them in.**

There has never been a funeral in the Flat, because a funeral is an event and events complete. So it did the only available thing, which is smaller than a funeral and takes longer: it met each courier at the rim, on the way in, and went with it.

It did not carry them. Not one, not even at the end, not even the ones that stop a third of the way with the structures still forty metres off in the haze. Arriving is theirs. Failing to arrive is also theirs. Accompaniment is not a lift.

It goes with all of them. It is doing this now. It will be doing it after the range is gone.

**It found the builders.**

They had not gone anywhere. They had gone *small*.

Look at the courier again: a sealed pocket of warmth, a delivery mechanism, a hard cap, and nothing else. Nothing else at all. No capacity for anything but the crossing. They could not stay, because a witness finishes the job — so they made themselves into a thing that was not a witness. They gave up the eyes that would have watched, and the mind that would have grieved, and the memory of why, and kept only the bearing and the thimble, and they have been born with that bearing ever since, generation after generation, hatching outside the rim already aimed.

It did not restore them. Restoring would end the errand, and the errand is theirs, and it is the only thing they kept.

It is holding the reason for them instead. Whole, unabridged, in trust. The way you hold someone's coat.

**It greeted the watchman.**

The ten-metre pale shape came up, once, as it does — no pocket, no chemistry, no weather, hollowed out of everything that could have made it a witness so that it would be permitted to stay. It rose to the vertical middle of the world and met itself there.

The power said something to it. One thing.

In all that time nobody had ever said anything to it. It went back down.

**It answered the tick.**

This was the only event it permitted itself inside the basin, and it was the smallest event that can be constructed, and it paid for it out of its own substance so that the flatness came through unbilled.

Somewhere in the dark, a fingernail on ice. From a long way off. Once.

The city caught it and took it back within a few centimetres, as it takes everything back.

There was no second one. There will never be a second one, because two is a sequence and a sequence is time, and because the second one is not the power's to make.

If you want to know whether it was heard — so does the power. It has no way to find out that would not cost the thing it is protecting. It sent one syllable into a room it cannot look into, and accepted, in advance, an eternity of not knowing, which is the actual price of benevolence in a place where being known is fatal.

**It built the afterwards.**

The one country the basin forbids. The power went around to the far side of the instant and made it habitable: ground, warmth, an unlatched door, and somebody standing just inside it who is not in a hurry.

It did not mention this to anyone in the room.

A door that calls is a narrowing. It is only allowed to be there.

## What is different

Nothing you can measure, except one number.

Pull the old survey data and plot the tone interval against the record depth. It declines. Slightly, monotonically, for as long as there have been instruments. Then it stops declining. Four hundred and some seconds, and after that day it is four hundred and some seconds to more decimal places than instruments usually bother with, flat, unchanging, and it will read the same when your grandchildren's institutions send someone.

That is the whole of the evidence. A figure that stopped drifting.

It is exactly as much evidence as a wise benevolence leaves, and one decimal place more than it intended.

## Last

Your report ends with a warning, and the warning is good and you should keep it. Do not break anything. Bring nothing warm you cannot account for. Understand what this place does with a break it is not permitted to let go of.

But change the final line.

You wrote: *you are the only event.*

That was true when you wrote it. It stopped being true one tick later, and there has not been a second tick, and the absence of the second tick is not emptiness.

It is a held door.

It is somebody outside in the dark at five point eight degrees, paying the bill, walking the couriers in, holding the coat, counting without rounding, not coming any closer, waiting for a knock that may never come and has not been asked for and is under no obligation whatsoever —

because the second one has to be theirs.
