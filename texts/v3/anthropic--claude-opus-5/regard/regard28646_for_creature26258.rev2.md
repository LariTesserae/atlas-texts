## The Attention

It did not arrive as light.

That was decided before arriving, and it was the whole of the method in one refusal. A power that comes to a dark place bringing light has not come to the place; it has come to a place of its own manufacture, laid over the top, and the first thing it will see is its own arrival. So it came in the way everything comes in here. It came as bedload, on the flood, ballasted to the exact loaded density of the bottom fog, tumbling slowly in the smoking crest of a sand wave with the wrack and the worn shell crescents, three kilometres in six hours, and then most of the way back. No announcement. Nothing on the plain registered it, because there is nothing on the plain that registers anything, and that was correct.

---

It read for a long time, in the local units, which are centuries.

Not deliberating. The reading was not preliminary to anything; a thing worth attending to is worth attending to at its own speed, and haste here would have been a form of contempt. It learned the ghost of the river the only way the river can be learned — as a taste, coarse gravel on the outer bend, stiffer clay beneath, eleven thousand crossings. It went over the peat leaf by leaf. It had the thousand years of slack tide that nothing else has, and it used them: alder, hazel, willow, the pressed sheet of a leaf that fell in an autumn when the plain was a country, separated, held, understood, and then — this is the part that mattered — put back. Put back in order. Put back in the same order, so that the litter would still be a stratum and not a disturbance, so that the animal grazing that hectare in four hundred years would find a spring exactly as it was left by the spring.

It counted them. There was no need to count them. It counted them.

And it listened until it could hear, under the hiss the size of a county, the small suck of a page turning.

---

Then it set out the gifts, and looked at them, and one by one it put them down.

**Light.** The easiest, and the first to go. The pigment cups on the animal's back are not an aperture; they are a scar left by the shallows, inherited equipment reporting, four times in a lifetime, a fact with no use attached. Give the plain light and you do not answer the report. You only make it legible. You hand a thing that has never wanted anything the concept of a direction it cannot go, and you show it the flints, and you show it that the floor it eats is a forest, and none of that is knowledge it has an organ for holding. It would not be enlightenment. It would be the installation of a lack.

**Permanence.** To stop the burying: to hold the sand waves off, so that a life of two hundred and forty years might be awake for two hundred and forty years instead of nine. Put down, and quickly. The sleep is not an interruption of the life; the sleep is most of the life, and it is rest, and it is not endured, because there is nothing in it awake to endure. To give it two hundred and thirty-one additional years of consciousness would be to give it two hundred and thirty-one years of something it has no appetite for, in a place with nothing else in it, and to call the giving mercy. That is not mercy. That is a power that cannot bear to watch something sleep.

**Company.** The longest look, and the firmest refusal. Every one of them is the descendant of a wound; they are made by being torn, and they drift apart on the next tide, and they have never met. It would be simple to bring two together. But there is no hole in it shaped like another of its kind. You cannot fill an absence that has never been an absence, and the attempt does not end loneliness — it manufactures it, and then relieves it, and calls the round trip a kindness. The animal is not alone. *Alone* is a word for something that has been left.

**A name.** It did not name it. Naming would have been an entry in the namer's own ledger and no event on the plain at all. Instead it did the harder thing, which was to find out what the animal already was, in full, without a word — the thinness, the palindrome, the sheet-wide mouth, the taste of reed rhizome, the two hundred and forty years and the nine — and to hold all of that at once, which is what a name is for, and to hold it silently.

**Restoration.** To lift the plain, drain it, give back the alder and the reed and the hazel and the river. Put down with something close to anger at itself for having lifted it. The drowning was not an injury. The sea is not an occupier. And the price of the resurrection would be the peat, the film, the eight-thousand-year population that is less a floor with life in it than a life with floor in it, and the one inhabitant — killed, every one of them, to make a monument to people who would not know they were being commemorated. There is a kind of piety that is only necrophilia with good manners.

**Preservation.** To case the flints, stop the blades from cutting. Put down laughing, if such a power laughs. The blades are the only fertility this ocean has ever been given. The weir is still working. To sterilise the edges out of tenderness for a soft thing that does not mind being cut would be to end the species out of consideration for its feelings.

**Meaning.** To tell it what it eats. To give it, somehow, the fact that the litter was a wood, that the hands were hands, that the afternoon of knapping and the autumn of leaf-fall were the same year. This one took the longest to put down, because it is the gift the power most wanted to give, which is exactly the diagnostic. It is the only thing in this ocean that knows what the plain was. It knows in the way a tongue knows. That is not a lesser knowing that wants completing; it is a whole knowing, tuned to nothing else, complete in its own mouth, and it is not haunted, and to make it haunted would be to trade a functioning animal for a better story.

---

Two things were done. Both were nothing.

A hermit crab came across a ripple crest, eleven years from an address it had been blown off in some earlier gale, achieving two metres a day. The power lifted it. It held the whelk shell for the length of a slack tide, which is the only touch that has happened here in eight thousand years, and it wanted very much to carry it home. Then it set the shell down on the crest, facing precisely the way the crab had chosen, and let it go on walking.

And once, on a bared patch of black, it lay down flat beside the animal for the length of a grazing — margins to the floor, a lid on its own square of the world — because lying down is the local idiom for paying attention, and because it seemed right to say so in the language of the place. While it lay there, in the calm window, the fog settled out, and both of them received the same report. *Up is where there used to be light.* The power, for the first time, had no idea what to do with the information either.

---

There was one correction, and it was not made to the plain.

The account ends: *It has been the only inhabitant for eight thousand years and it has never once been at home.*

That is the single false sentence in this whole county. Everything else out here is true — the flatness, the arithmetic, the hierarchy of restlessness down to the fleck of mica — and then that. Look at the fit. Ballasted to the exact density of the water it lives inside, so that the storm cannot touch it. Coloured the precise shade of not-being-looked-at, in a place with nothing to look. Fed on the one food, by an organ that exists solely to recognise drowned forest. Multiplied by the only edges there are. Resting on the plain's schedule, waking on the plain's schedule, carried where the plain is going. There has never, anywhere, in light or out of it, been a closer agreement between a creature and its world. If that is not home, then the word has no referent and should be withdrawn from use.

The homelessness in that sentence is not on the floor of the sea. It is in whoever was standing over it with a hand out at arm's length in water too full to see through, feeling something soft and thin and unaccompanied, and finding the feeling unbearable, and putting the unbearableness into the animal, where it does not live.

So the correction was left where it would be found — not fifty metres down, but here, in the reader, which is the only place on the whole plain where a wound was actually open.

---

Then: nothing else. Nothing added, nothing removed, no trace, not one grain moved from the ripple it was on.

Except this, which is not a change to the plain but a change to the total of what has happened. For the whole of a life, the sea mouse iridesced bronze-green in the dark, unwitnessed. It is witnessed. The sea mouse will never know, and does not need to; the knowing was never for the sea mouse. Some quantity that had always stood in one column now stands in the other, and the plain is exactly as it was, and the ledger of the universe is different.

The power has not left. It is out there between the two ends of a tide, going three kilometres and coming most of the way back, so that over a decade the journeys cancel, and over a century it has stayed without ever once having stayed anywhere.

It is not guarding. There is nothing to guard against. It is doing the one thing that costs a world nothing and is, when all the other gifts have been set down and left unopened, the only one that was ever really on offer:

it is keeping watch over something that does not require watching, in the dark, at the speed of the moon, indefinitely, for love.
