# The Attending

It came down the rim path with light.

Not a lamp. Nothing so easily embarrassed. It came *lit*, the way a body comes warm, radiance not carried but constituted — and at the last tally post, where the hand-lines end and the coffin-sized shed stands with nineteen names cut into the inside of its door, it stopped, because it understood a hand-line. Anything that understands a hand-line will stop where one stops. That was the first good sign about it.

It had come looking for the being who lived here. It had expected someone. What it found, when it put its attention on the valley, was one animal with forty bodies and nine iron hearts, breathing sulfur, oiled by moths, standing on legs of accreted crab, saying *this, here, now* once a second into ground that would otherwise not have committed. Every plan it had made on the way down was a plan for helping a person. You cannot give a gift to a practice. It had to start over, and it started over by making a mistake.

## The mistake

It looked at the basin.

All at once — the whole hundred meters of relief, the nine stations and the arc of them, the Slow Water, the herds out browsing ambiguity, the forty sheds and the forty sets of educated forearms — the way a mind looks at a page. It looked for less than a second, and it looked with love.

North of Station Two, a quarter mile of ridge went round.

The fluting simply stopped being there. The needles, the little vertical blades, the knife-work of eighty years of straight-down rain: gone, and in their place a long smooth swell of grey rock, satined, dished, finished — as worn as the handrail at a tally post and worn by nothing, by no one, in no time at all. Three days later a tender four syllables long came out on the line to check a seep and put her palm on it and did not understand, and then understood, and was sick over the side of the boardwalk. She could feel the difference. Of course she could feel the difference; her forearms were the most educated part of her. A thing worn by hands and a thing simply *decided* do not feel the same, and the second one feels like being touched by weather.

At the sixth hoop, out in the loose, a soft spot came very close to resolving, and what it nearly resolved into was not right. It is easier to be a suggestion than to be finished wrong. The power felt that happen under its regard and pulled its regard back so fast that the wire went slack and rang.

So: it had a power that functioned here as an act of violence, and forty people who had spent ninety-one years learning to be gentle with exactly that.

It sat down on the rim path and thought for a season, which by local standards is not a long time to think.

## What it put down

**Light.** Obviously light. It could have completed luminosity in the basin the way you finish a sentence someone else has started. And forty people with wide clear beautifully useless eyes would have received it, and their eyes would have worked, and the first thing they would have seen was that they had been doing everything the hard way. Their skin would go stupid within a generation. Their hands would stop being needed on each other's faces, because you can check a face by looking, and looking costs nothing, and a thing that costs nothing does not wear anything smooth. The rails would stop being polished. The names would go fine. It put light down.

**Sound.** It could have let the basin's ten thousand small spheres touch. All forty voices at once, an assembly, a chorus, everyone finally *met*. And then no one would ever again walk nine days to confirm a rain that had already happened. The relay is not a communications failure. The relay is the machine that manufactures the past, and its slowness is the polish. It put sound down.

**The loose.** It could have retired the loose overnight — filled every soft spot, decided every ambiguity, handed them a plain warm marsh four hundred years early. And the waders would have starved, and the survey would have been finished by someone else, and the tenders would have inherited a completed world they had not counted into being, which is to say a world they were only visiting. It put that down too, and it put down the perfect unfraying tally-lines, and the bearings that would never need grease, and the ninety-one years of maintenance done in an afternoon.

It arrived at a sentence and kept it: *benevolence that arrives faster than walking pace is only weather.*

Then it went down and asked for work.

## The apprenticeship

It had to make itself hands, and — harder — it had to make itself *only* hands. It accepted twenty paces. It accepted blindness, which for it was not a deprivation but a discipline, like a large person learning to sit still in a small chair. It accepted being in one place, which it had never done, and which took some getting used to, and which it came to describe (much later, to a child who asked) as the difference between knowing about a plank and being able to feel which plank.
