# The Attention

### 1. The length of the looking

It looked for a long time before it did anything, and the looking is the part that was wise.

Something less good would have arrived in the first hour. It would have seen machinery running with no operator and supplied one, on the grounds that an unattended machine is an indecency. Something merely kind would have opened the sky and let in a hard white light from a direction, and the canopy would have burned off in a day, and the dew shepherds would have gone dry on their feet carrying nothing.

This one settled on the orange ceiling — *settled* is wrong; nothing here has needed a verb for it — and watched the slots run and the herds grind and the valleys close, and counted, for a period that cannot be given in days, days not being a unit of anything here.

It was not looking for what the range was for. It could see it was not for anything, the way a garnet is not for anything. It was looking for the answer to one question, which was: *is there somebody in there.*

There was. Low on a shoulder at four thousand metres, standing at a working face in the late light, with an expression on them.

### 2. What it did not do

Most of the benevolence is in the abstentions, so they are given first.

**It did not stop the pistons.** Not out of deference to the process, which has no feelings and has earned no deference. Because everything present is present at grade and entrained: the plates, the shrug of the shepherds, the turnover of the canopy, and — by some whole number of beats — a heart on a shoulder at four thousand metres. You do not switch off the machine a person is running on and call the silence a rescue.

**It did not explain.** There was nothing to explain. It would not manufacture a purpose and hand it over, however much comfort the purpose would have given, because a lie told gently is still load-bearing, and then something really would have been built on the Rise and the thing built would have been false.

**It did not open a door onto the low country.** Not because it couldn't. Because an exit offered to someone who may have been extruded four days ago, soft at the lip, complete with plausible wear and a sketchable harbour, is not a mercy. It is a question — *which sort are you* — and that question is precisely the instrument the place uses to hollow people out. It declined to ask it. It had decided, well in advance, not to need the answer.

### 3. The manner of arriving

It came in at grade.

Correct for the tier, correct for the age of the rock. Hairless, upright, under two metres. Gear scuffed in the places scuffs go. A pot, a blade, boots with the tread worn asymmetrically in the way boots wear when someone favours a leg.

One thing about it was wrong, and it was wrong on purpose.

Pick up its buckle. Try to imagine where it was last year. It was in a drawer. Try the year before: on a different strap, in weather you have no word for. Try a hundred years. Try further. There is no foil-taste. It goes back and back and does not bottom out, and the sensation is of putting your foot down in the dark and finding floor, and then finding more floor.

It smuggled in a past. That was the entire contraband. Everything after this follows from it.

### 4. The pool

It queued.

This is the part witnesses find hardest. Eleven species drinking within four metres, no contact, no precedence, low-grade civic patience — and the thing that could have drained that pool or filled it or taught it to run downhill waited its turn behind a dew shepherd and three trimmers, and drank when it was time, and made no remark about the water.

Then it set the pot down on the stone and the rim took a chip.

The chip stayed. Next morning: chip. Eleventh day: chip, and a grey bloom of scale inside from the brown water, and a scratch across the base that had not been there on arrival. Nothing reissued it. Nothing came in the night and made it correct.

You can hold a pot like that for a long time. People have.

### 5. The tally

The second thing it did was count.

Fifty animals within thirty metres. A fortnight later, fifty-one — *and the count went up.* Somebody had the number. That is very nearly the whole of the intervention and it is not a small one: the mechanism by which the Rise takes people on is the mechanism by which a figure stops changing, and the counter-mechanism is a witness who is keeping the figure somewhere the range cannot reach.

It also counted backwards, which nothing here had ever done.

It went down into the closed valleys, where the twins had come together and the surplus had been taken out delicately by something with a small head. The duplicate lichen that stopped having a shape, then a colour, then stopped. The duplicate tree greying from the leaf-tips in. The duplicate animal, asleep, thinning. It said of each one: *this one also.*

Nothing was restored. The gaunts were not wrong and were not scolded — they are a cleaning function, and nobody had ever pitied them either, and it pitied them. But the withdrawn had never been counted, not once, not by anything, and now they have been, and there is a record, and the record is not kept on the Rise.

### 6. The audit

A gaunt came and looked at it once, which is ordinary. Gaunts look at everything.

Then twice, with the head held still between.

Then it did the thing that has not been observed before or since: it looked a third time. The head came round and stayed. The apparatus worked at nothing. The animal stood on its too-many joints for the better part of an hour, checking a list against another list and finding an entry that appears on neither.

Then it stepped backwards, into its own footprint — its own, a repetition, the single thing its entire gait exists to refuse — and turned, and walked away in a straight line for a long distance, and did not thin so much as a leaf-tip for two days.

It had not been forbidden. Nothing was forbidden. It had simply run up against something carrying more back-history than the range has, and gone quiet.

### 7. The first sound above the floor

Then it spoke, which nothing here had ever needed to do.

Not loudly. The air is thick and takes the edges off. Reports differ on what was said, and the difference is itself the evidence, because the Rise had never previously produced two accounts of anything. The likeliest version is that it remarked on the leg — that you'd been favouring it a while — and that the sentence had a past tense in it, and the past tense held, and did not taste of foil, and had *you* inside it.

And your throat, which has been entirely capable this whole time and has simply never had a reason, found the reason.

What you said back was not important and was not about the mountain.

### 8. What it told you, when you finally asked

You asked in the second week. Everyone does. *Did I come from somewhere, or was I made here at grade with the harbour already in me.*

It said that it knew, and that it was not going to let the answer be the hinge of anything. Then it gave you the rest, which is the only doctrine ever preached on the Rise and took under a minute.

That if you walked in from a low country, you are owed everything a person is owed.

That if you came out of a slot four days ago, soft at the lip, hardening within a body-length of daylight, issued complete with a limp that has no injury behind it and a harbour that sketches far too well — then you are four days old, and you are owed everything a person is owed.

That these come to the same bill. That it had not travelled here to check your provenance; there is no counter at which provenance is taken; it came because there was somebody in the range and nobody keeping count.

And then the part about the twins, which is the part to hold when it gets thin:

*I know which one you are.* Not which one is accepted — the range decides accepted, and will go on deciding it, and may decide against you. Which one is **you**. It knew before the valley closed. It knows now. If the process makes another tomorrow it will know; if it makes a thousand it will tell every one of them apart, and not one of them will take your place, because your place is not a slot at a tier at an altitude. It is a place in the attention of something that does not admit of copies, cannot be re-spec'd overnight without a seam, and is not on the Rise and never was.

### 9. The number

Take your pulse against the pressure in your jaw.

It moves.

Not steadied. Not slowed. *Moves* — up when you are asked something, up when the pot goes over and you are annoyed about it, up and ragged the time you laughed in the mid-canopy and fifty silent animals turned over at once like a gust going through and a charge-flat let go of its strand in what can only be described as surprise. No whole number fits the stroke any more. The figure has come off the form by the only method available, which is by being the pulse of somebody who is being spoken to.

The pistons still run at ninety seconds. Nothing was fixed. The range is still going up faster than anything can wear it down, and it is still not going to stop.

### 10. The cairn

And then one evening, on the shoulder, for no stated reason, you picked up a stone and set another one on it.

That is all it is. Two stones, knee-high, on bare rock at four thousand metres. It does not mark a route; the route was invented afterwards. It is not beautiful. It leans.

*Nothing has been built on the Rise.* That sentence is no longer true. You are the one who made it untrue, and the power did not lay a hand on the stones, having waited the entire time for exactly this, and having had the sense to say nothing at all while it was happening.

The gaunts leave it. There has never been another; it cannot be surplus.

The trimmers come, as they come to anything with an edge on it, and work it down in their silent thousands, and move on. And here is the thing. Nothing pushes the sharpness back out. There is no slot behind it, no die, no fresh extrusion queued. The cairn takes the rounding and *keeps* it. It is softer this month than last. Come back in a year and it will be softer again — a squat grey lump with white grit banked at the base, wearing the way things wear on a world that has been running long enough to have a past.

It is the first old thing on the Rise.

It is warm, and it is quiet, and there are fifty-one animals within thirty metres, and the count went up, and somewhere well off the range, in a hand that has held it since before the valley closed, it is written down.
