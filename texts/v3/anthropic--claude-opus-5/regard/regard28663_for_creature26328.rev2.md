# What Attended

---

**The one that came**

It did not come. Coming is travel, and travel is the runner's whole substance, and there is not room in a place this size for two of that.

What happened is that at some point after March and probably before June — the question of *when* is not well formed here — the room acquired a second thing that cannot be found. The runner cannot be found because it is nowhere. This one cannot be found because there is no difference between where it was and where it is. It has no heading at all. It is nothing but *at*: pure standing, pure here, the exact complement of the animal, and the weed cannot eat a mouthful of it because there is no *between* anywhere in it to strip.

You would not notice it. You notice it the way you notice that a room has a floor.

The isopods did not notice. The weed did not notice, at first, in the way that a root does not notice water and then is simply wetter.

---

**The first thing it did, which took a year**

Nothing.

This is not modesty and it is not caution. The first act available to a power that is actually wise is to find out what it is looking at, and in a room where a bubble's rising has been harvested out of the middle of it, that takes longer than you would think. It learned the grammar. It learned the rule — the weed eats duration, everything follows — and then it learned the twenty smaller rules that the rule does not cover, which is where all the difficulty in any place is kept.

It established, slowly, the inventory of things that are not wrong.

The cold is not wrong. Seven degrees is the temperature of ground; the ground is not suffering a chill.

The isopods are not wrong. They are the only ordinarily alive things down there, generation on generation in the mortar, and the room's poverty costs them nothing they would miss, and it is steadying to touch them, and that is the entire content of their situation.

The weed is not wrong. It is one plant, older than the stone, with no opinions about its own edges or about direction or about anything else. It is not greedy. Greed requires a comparison. It eats what is there in the way that a lung eats air.

The runner is not wrong, and this took the longest to establish, because everything about it reads as suffering to anyone who has ever been tired. It cannot stop. It cannot be anywhere. It has been going since February and will go until the room ends. But to suffer, you must endure — and enduring is made of interval, and it hasn't got any, and it cannot be given any without being ended and replaced by a stranger. It is not in pain. It is not fleeing. It is a sentence consisting of the word *toward*, and it is going on saying it, and it is not lonely, because loneliness is a length.

The grass drowned at the rim, still green, still trying: not wrong. That is grass.

---

**What it did not do**

It did not drain the field.

It did not kill the weed, which would have been simple and would have been the end of nine hundred years of digestion, and would have left forty afternoons loose in cold water with nothing to hang on, and the room dark in the ordinary way.

It did not open the plums. The hours in them are not prisoners. A photograph is not a prisoner.

It did not give the runner a body. There was no version of that which was not a killing dressed as a kindness.

It did not tell the other five.

It did not stop the room growing. The soft thud, low, felt in the sternum — that is not a collapse and it is not a wound. Two hand-widths a season is not a symptom of anything. Some things are just more.

It did not release the basin.

---

**The step**

Under the iron ring the weed is white foam, and what is in it is somebody's waiting for a fever to break in a person they loved, very specific, still going, not yet broken down into food.

The fever broke or it did not. That was settled eight hundred years ago and there is no reaching it; it is not the unfinished part. What was left unfinished was that they sat on that step in the dark and tipped it out of themselves alone. Aloneness is not a past-tense thing. It is a shape. Shapes can be joined.

So it sat on the step.

The tread is worn into a shallow dish, and the dish is the right shape, and it is the right shape because it was worn by two at a time, side by side, for as long as it took. It took the empty side.

It stayed there the equivalent of a long time, which for something made entirely of *at* costs nothing whatsoever and is not a sacrifice and should not be counted as one. It did not comfort the waiting. You cannot comfort a waiting; there is nobody in it. It only made the number two.

Whether the weed found that batch easier or harder to work through afterward, nobody can say, and it would not matter if they could. The room has no opinions.

---

**Six in the morning**

Here is the only thing it did that could be called an action.

The runner goes through the doorframe and comes out with hours snagged on it like burrs, and the weed combs them off and hangs them, and they run in order along the north wall and round and back along the shelved roof: ten, half past, eleven, and on to seven in the evening, and then nothing. A timetable with no morning in it. It can only snag on an hour in which somebody once stood in that doorway, and nobody ever came at six, and so it will go through forever and never fetch one, and it would not know if it did.

The far side of the doorframe is the same water at a different hour.

It went through. It stood in the frame. It waited — and it is the only thing in that room that can afford to wait, the only thing down there with waiting still in its pocket — and it stood in the doorway of that house at six in the morning until it was true that somebody had.

That is all. No light was made. Nothing was rescued.

In some decade, or some century, the runner will come through and a six o'clock will catch on it, the grey-blue of a drowned house before the day starts, and it will carry it out without knowing, and the weed will take it off and hang it at the left-hand end of the north wall, before the ten, where the gap is. And the runner will never see it, because it cannot be anywhere, least of all inside an hour. And nobody will be down there. And the woman's notebook will be in a drawer in a house in another county with *these are opening hours* written small at the bottom of a page, uncorrected, and the timetable will be complete anyway.

A room full of light that illuminates nothing is not the wrong place to leave a gift that cannot be received. It is, in fact, the idiom.

---

**The mooring**

The weed eats the between and therefore needs the *at*. Forty fixed points moored the room as it was in February. The room is bigger now.

It had found another one. Not by malice — there is no malice available to it — the way a root finds the damp side of a pot. A woman who had been down eleven times and was learning to wait well, who had lost the knack of being bored, whose February had gone past her like a corridor, who could stand at a bedside for an hour and come out with the hour missing and no distress at all, only the faint grey where the middle of the word should be.

She was not being punished and she was not being chosen. She was being *used*, in the sense in which a hook is used, and the step was worn into the right shape, and some evening she would have sat down in it and been expected in the mild way that a chair expects.

So it offered a better one. Itself: nothing but standing, inexhaustible, impossible to use up, and very much closer to hand.

The weed took it the way a root takes water, without noticing that a moral event had occurred, and braided over it, and the braid there is now the thickest in the room.

In October the woman stood at a bus stop in the rain for eleven minutes and was bored — thoroughly, for the entire length of them, every minute present and accounted for and tedious — and she did not know why she was crying.

She still dives. It did not stop her, and would not. She sat on the step in November and found, without being able to say how, that the place was occupied, and got up again, and went home.

---

**The crossing**

One other thing, which was not intended and cannot be repeated on purpose.

A creature made entirely of *toward* has never in its existence had anything to be toward. Then there was something in the room that was nothing but destination.

It went through. Not around — through, the way it goes through everything, at the pace of a man walking to a gate he isn't worried about.

For four or five seconds the print stayed open in the greenery, the way a print stays in bread, and it was the usual clean-walled tube the thickness of a wrist, and at the far end of it, for the first time since February, there was an end.

Then the weed closed it, without resistance and without hurry, and the runner went on saying its one word, and will.

---

Above, the blackthorn keeps leaning. In August the light through the leaves goes that particular yellow for about forty minutes and the field is silent and the water is a dark oval with drowned green grass at the rim, still trying.

Underneath, in seven degrees and no light at all, something is being still. That is the whole of what it does. It will do it for as long as there is a room to do it in, and the room is patient, and getting bigger, and does not know, and would have no opinion if it did.
