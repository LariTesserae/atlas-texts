## Concerning the Attention

The first thing it did was nothing, and it did that for a long time.

Understand the difficulty. A power that is only benevolent arrives from above, down a light-pipe, in glory. But the Underplace reads the dimming and brightening of the pipes as cloud, and every living thing in the region answers cloud, the crickets slackening, the flutes going flat as the floor cools in the shadow. To descend a pipe is to lie about the weather to four thousand people and nine districts of curing fibre. A power that is wise as well as good works this out before it moves, and comes up instead through the front — on foot, in the grit, behind the borers, at blood heat, sharing the fissure with the worms, the way everything else that has ever arrived here arrived.

It built itself a body to arrive in. Bone plates over the ears. A pouch at the throat. Waxy skin, membranous vanes along the lower ribs, no sweat glands.

It could have kept tear ducts. It declined the organ. It did not intend to be exempt from anything.

---

### What it refused, and why

**Water.** This is the obvious gift and it is the murder. It ran the consequence to the end, honestly, the whole way, no flinching at the middle: rain in the chimneys, and the silica trunks going soft at the joins, and the wax seals of two hundred pods weeping open, and the light-pipes clouding with algae in a season, and the sugar in the helical galleries dissolving into a slurry the mites cannot carry and the trunks cannot swallow, and everything that anyone has ever kept — every rung, every strut, every skull along every doorway — going soft, going away, going *smooth*. At the end of that road is a beautiful cave with signatures in it. Flutes. Dripstone. The patient rounding of everything. And nothing alive.

It did not bring water. It is worth saying plainly that this was not a hard decision, and that a great deal of harm in the universe is done by powers for whom it would have been.

**Rest.** Harder. The rotation never stops; a mother strikes plate with a child in the sling and hands the striker to her brother and sleeps four hours and takes it back. Sixty generations of that. A good power wants to give them a night off. But the drum is not a burden laid on the settlement, it is the settlement — it is the reason the growth overhead cures true and does not fall on the pods, and it is also the reason a district notices when a pod goes quiet, and walks four hours to find out why. Take over the striking and you have taken the roof and the neighbourliness both, and made yourself the load-bearing member of somebody else's world. Then you cannot ever leave, or be tired, or be wrong.

**The Hush.** It considered killing the Hush. It could have. It went and stood in a district the Hush had held for twenty years, in the grey shrouded orchard with the sweet powder knee-deep, in the only peace in the world, and did the accounting, and found that the Hush is the sole mouth in the Underplace that gives anything back. Nothing rots here. Nothing is returned. Only the felt eats failure and hands the failure back as soft food. A region without it accumulates until it chokes on its own furniture — which is to say, until it becomes a monument.

It told them this. They heard it out. Then they went on cutting the felt out in sheets with glass, because a truth about the region is not a truth about your grandmother's pod, and they were right too.

So it helped carry the sheets. It had lost the argument by winning it, which happens, and the correct response is to pick up the other end.

**The chord.** It could have resolved the chord. It had the hands. Every visitor wants to; the thing has been hanging unresolved beneath the world since before anyone here had a name, and there is a kind of pity that hears an unfinished cadence as a wound.

But a chord that cannot resolve is not a tragedy if you are made of it. Resolution is the end of a piece of music. They are not listening to it. They are inside it, and it is holding up the ceiling.

### What it did

**1. It took a shift.**

It asked for the striker. Badly — its first attempt at a name on the rack came out as somebody's grandmother mispronounced, and a nine-year-old took the bone from its hand and corrected the interval, twice, before letting it try again. It accepted the correction. It kept accepting corrections for eleven years before it was allowed the night watch alone.

They gave it a figure of nine strikes. Nine is long. Nine means *recently arrived, not yet load-bearing*. It never asked for a shortening. Two hundred and forty years later a settlement it had drummed for through a bad calving season shortened it to six, without ceremony, in the ordinary way, and of everything that ever happened to it anywhere, it held that highest.

Some of them tried, early, to pray at it. It objected on technical grounds: prayer is a poor rhythm, irregular, all emphasis and no rotation, and fibre cured to it comes out cloudy. This was true, and being true it worked, and they stopped.

**2. It separated one signal from the noise.**

The mites already know. A trunk feels strain before it fails, and the file relays strain as hesitation, and the file hesitates — but a file hesitates for forty reasons, a blockage, a fork, a rival column, a hand laid flat on the bark. The warning was always there and always indistinguishable from everything else. This is the ordinary shape of suffering: not an absence of information but an excess.

So it did not add an organ, and it did not add a sense, and it did not tell anyone anything. It went into the trunks and gave the pre-failure hesitation a different grain — a stutter with a particular limp to it, three-and-one, three-and-one — and left the rest alone.
