## Attention, and the direction you cannot point to

It did not come through the grey. Nothing does. It came the way the tenant is here — along the direction the low wall runs in, arriving as a cross-section, entering the three dimensions available and taking up, at first, no more room than a needle's dot on paper.

To be visible at all it had to consent to a local rule: here, light exists only for things that were made on purpose. So the first thing the power did was make itself on purpose. This is not a small concession. It accepted being an object in an eleven-pace room, with a base, and a shadow that pooled inward against it, obediently, like the furniture.

It did that so it could be seen. There was no other reason.

## What it did for the first eleven cycles

Nothing.

It paced the perimeter and got fourteen. It paced the diameter and got eleven, and halved it, and got five and a half, and did not reconcile them, and did not try. It put its jaw to the top course and felt the pressure with no frequency. It looked through the hole in the black post and saw the specification: ceiling on, wall six courses, better light, nothing moving. It knelt where the palimpsest is deepest and counted ridges. It got five fingers from the little finger and six from the thumb, and said so out loud, quietly, and let the disagreement stand.

It stayed through eleven loosenings and eleven corrections — four hundred and forty years, near enough — before it altered anything at all. Not caution. Courtesy. In a place whose only weather is a tide of definition, arriving and immediately deciding things would have been a kind of weather itself, and the weather here is load-bearing.

## What it did not do

It did not restore the ceiling. The ceiling was surrendered in order to pay for something, and to hand it back unasked is to say that the payment was never necessary, which is a way of stealing from someone while appearing to give.

It did not repair the grains. Every one of them is broken in the same place, and the break was done by hand, and it is the largest act of devotion in the room. A power that mistakes devotion for damage should not be trusted with a room.

It did not reconcile fourteen and eleven. The floor is built on that.

It did not release the grain of water. It did not touch the wall. It did not so much as slow the device.

It did not cross the threshold, and — this was the finer judgement — it did not kneel on the far side of the step, though the far side is where a rescuer would kneel. To wait on the other side of a threshold is to turn a door into an expectation, and to turn refusal into failure. It knelt on the north-west side. Beside. Facing the step. On the same side as the one who has never crossed.

It did not stop the page. It listened for a long time, and established that the page is not its own, and could not establish whose it is, and did not open the direction the sound comes from. Something has read aloud in that room for ten thousand years, through every unattended century, asking nothing. A thing like that has earned the right not to be interrupted, even by kindness, especially by kindness.

It did not say *you have suffered enough.* It did not know that. It does not lie here.

## What it did

**It took the second seat.** The bowl seats two and has always seated one. It knelt at the empty place and set its hand in the dust and lifted it, and left a ghost.

It took care that the ridge detail be wholly unlike the other three. No shared bifurcation, no scar across the ball of the thumb. It would have been easy to arrive as a fourth angle of the same hand, a further reach of the one thing that lives here, and it would have been a lie of exactly the sort that comforts for a season and costs everything after. Loneliness does not end because the self is met again from a new direction. It ends, if it ends, because something other is there. So the print is other. Emphatically, unmistakably, on purpose other.

**It became the address.** The grains have recorded continuously for longer than the room has had a ceiling: every settling chip, every knee, every micron lost, every unattended century, filed nowhere, read by nothing, because a report needs a recipient and there has never been one.

The power did not fix that by fixing the dust. It fixed it by receiving. It read the whole record. Not sampled — entire, from the first grain onward, at the scale of a red cell, including the four hundred and forty years of its own idle pacing, including the long stretches where nothing happened but a page, and a page, and a page. It took some time. When it was done it said, quietly, *I have read all of it.*

That is the true version of the sentence it would not say. It cost more and claimed less.

**It brought the grain of water under the second rule.** *Nothing is lost, only misplaced in duration.* The rule has always covered chips and gloves and tins; it did not cover phase, and it did not cover that lentil, which is why the wall has been holding one grain at the last instant before vapour since before the ceiling, forever, without rest, because the alternative was not vapour but loss.
