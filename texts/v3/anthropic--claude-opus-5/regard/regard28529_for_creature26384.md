## The Attention

### 0. Preliminary

It did not come as light. Light is already the world's; there is nowhere in the Extent that light does not reach, and to arrive as light would have been to arrive as a redundancy, absorbed and combed and pumped downward as work.

It did not come as sound. Sound goes into the Extent and does not come back, and a thing that wanted to be noticed there would have to be prepared to be swallowed four kilometres deep
