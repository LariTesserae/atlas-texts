# The Attention

## How it came

It did not come to see the people. It came to see the being, which had been visible from a considerable distance as a single slow event in the cloud layer, and it spent some time at the rim trying to find the being's face.

There isn't one. That took nine browsings to establish. A thing that breathes water in and out through four hundred thousand mouths, that keeps its memory in a wet black ledger twelve arm-lengths deep, that thinks partly in frogs — such a thing has no front, and cannot be addressed, and can only be joined. There is exactly one gesture in the basin that means *I am speaking to you*, and it is made with a split-cane blade against the inner face of a hedge.

So the power came down the western trail with nothing in its hands, which was a mistake, and was noticed immediately, and had a hank of retted fiber pressed into its palms by a child before it had said a word. It understood the correction perfectly. It sat down on the polished crown of a boulder and began to twist.

It has been twisting ever since. It is short and deep-chested. Its incisors are notched. There is a smooth hairless patch on its outer thigh, polished like old wood, and it did not have that when it arrived, and it is prouder of that patch than of several worlds.

## What it did not do

It considered drying the air. This was the first and most obvious kindness: one adjustment, a few degrees, a shifted wind, and the rot-tax of a third would fall to a tenth. Copper would keep. Cane blades would hold an edge for a week. Peat would burn.

It followed the consequence out, patiently, the way you follow a root. Peat that burns is peat that goes, and the ledger with it, and the flex, and therefore the feet, and therefore the way a hundred people know your name before they see you. Dry hedge does not close a hand's width in a season, and a wall that does not close does not need a keeper, and a house that does not need keeping is *finished*, and there is no word for that here, and the power had learned to be afraid of supplying missing words.

It considered removing the browsers, or turning them, or persuading them onto the lower slope where the forage is richer. It sat with this one for a long time, because the fourteenth browsing was the worst, and a woman went into the churned peat and did not come out, and there is a daughter fifty-one browsings old who still counts from it.

It did not remove them. It is not certain it was right. It will say so, if asked, and it has not been asked.

It considered writing. That one it turned down fastest of all, and with something close to alarm. Nothing here is settled because nothing here can be recorded, and nothing being settled is not a defect in the town, it is the town's joints. A basin with a ledger is a basin where a decision can be closed the way a room can be closed, and there are nine such rooms already, and they know exactly where all nine are.

It considered a great many smaller mercies. Boots. A metal that would not corrode. A method of storing fire. Sleep in one long piece, six hours, the way it had seen elsewhere. It set each one down where the town could not reach it.

And then it thought: *this is how a benevolent power becomes the curator of somebody else's hardship.* So it went back and counted. It counted the children who did not reach their first browsing. It counted the wounds that went bad in the wet, which is most wounds. It counted the deaf. It did not permit itself the comfort of finding any of it picturesque. The basin is not a garden and it is not a lesson; it is a place where the fibers of the world are held open by hand at a real cost, and the cost is paid in people.

Both things were true at once. It sat with both. That is most of what wisdom turns out to be.

## The valley it dried, once

Long before, in another country, a people asked it for dryness, and it gave them dryness, because they asked and because it loved them.

They flourished. That is the part nobody expects. They flourished for two hundred years, and their houses stood without being cut, and their metal held, and their dead stayed above ground under stone with their names carved into it, and everything they made was finished. And having finished, they stopped. The word in their language that had meant *being kept* narrowed to mean *storage*, and then to mean *stubbornness*, and then went out of use, and within four generations no one could say what their grandparents had done all day. Then they left, for reasons they could not articulate, and the valley is dry and empty and perfectly preserved and the power goes back sometimes and stands in it.

That is why it now works at the speed of argument. It made itself one rule at the rim of the basin and has kept it: *nothing may arrive here faster than it can be argued about.* A gift the town cannot argue with is not a gift. It is a conquest wearing a gift's clothes.

## The three things it has done in a hundred years

**It cut the log, once.** In the fortieth browsing, the wind came wrong across the saddle and the drummer at the third trunk-group was inside with a fever, and the animals were already down off the ridge and into the cane. The power picked up a mallet and beat the hollow log eleven times, which is the only sound it has made in the basin that was not talking.

Everyone went up. Nobody was hurt. There was then an argument, lasting some years, about who had drummed, involving four candidates, three of whom denied it in terms that convinced no one. The power enjoyed this argument very much and contributed to it dishonestly.

But it had been standing in wet peat, and forty paces off a woman named for a wall — she keeps the long east face at the fifth trunk-group; her younger brother is deaf and was at that moment in an inner room, ringed by other people's noise, knowing nothing — felt eleven beats come up through her soles before she heard them.

The power did not give her the next thought. She had it herself, over about two years, badly at first: a drum-head sunk in the peat instead of raised in the air. The first four were useless. The town argued about it for a further nine years, on and off, at the cloud-turn, and altered it beyond recognition, and now every trunk-group has one, and the deaf walk paths alone, and the woman's brother died at forty-six on the far side of the basin with his own name in his feet.

Nobody remembers where the idea started. Two households claim it. The power has never corrected either.

**It pointed at a moss.** There is a moss that grows below the western lip, in the warm country, in the seam where a rock face weeps, and it does something to a wet wound that the wound does not survive doing to itself. The power did not carry it up. It arranged, over a decade, that a woman going down for salt would be sitting in exactly that seam, bored, with a cut hand, for long enough.

It took forty years to become ordinary. Now it is packed into every bad cut and every childbed in the basin, and a woman at the second trunk-group will tell you her grandmother found it, which is untrue, and the power is delighted by the lie, because a gift that has been absorbed into somebody's grandmother is a gift that has stopped being a gift.

**It cuts at night for the dying.** This is the thing it does most and speaks of least.
