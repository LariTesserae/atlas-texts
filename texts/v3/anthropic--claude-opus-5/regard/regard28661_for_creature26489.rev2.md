## The Regard

Begin with the coming in, because everything else follows from it.

It could have worked from outside. From outside, the Same is a grey sheet being agitated from underneath, and a sheet can be lifted, dried, folded, set aside. All of that was available. None of it was done, for a reason that is not mercy and not squeamishness but simple accuracy: you cannot attend to a thing from a distance at which it is a sheet. You have to come to where it is. Where it is, is nowhere. And the price of arriving at nowhere is that you arrive everywhere.

So it was packed, immediately, like everything else. Nine million times and once. It spoke, and was answered from every point of the compass in its own voice at the same instant, and it stood in the blood-warm water with the grey mud taking and smoothing its print, and it understood the bird completely, in the first hour, without effort.

That was the first lesson and it was not a small one: that here, help arriving from all directions at once is indistinguishable from the roar.

The second lesson was closer to the bone. A power of this kind is already many and already one; it is already everywhere it attends; it has never in its existence had a fixed address. The difference between such a power and this continent is about a millimetre.

The millimetre is that one of them can tell.

---

What it did not do, and why. This is most of the record.

It did not raise a hill. A hill would be the second where in a world with one, and every living thing in the Same would walk toward it and stand on it, and then there would be a hill, and four thousand kilometres of exile.

It did not tear the lid. A sun is a direction. Everything would face it forever and that is not a gift, it is a leash.

It did not silence the roar. The roar is the only conversation on the continent. You do not end a conversation to make the room quieter.

It did not heal the bird's throat. The tear is the bird's single feature. Mend it and you have produced an ash-coloured wading bird indistinguishable from nothing at all — erasure with a kind face on it.

It did not finish the stroke at the basin.

It did not carry anyone out to the shingle, because the one who carries the block and the one who cuts and the one standing still at knee depth are one life held open, and to lift a person out you must first decide which of them arrives. Rescue here is the forbidden word performed with the hands.

And it did not say *which*. It has the word. It is the kind of being for whom *which* is native the way warmth is native to blood. It has held that word in its mouth for the whole of its residency, unsaid, every day, and that discipline is not a preliminary to the work. It *is* the work. Everything below is what remains possible to someone who will not use it.

---

What it did.

It learned the forty words, which took an embarrassing length of time, because learning requires correction, and correction requires somebody able to tell your attempt from the right one, and there was only itself.

It walked out to the coast where the mud stiffens into shingle, and it picked up stone, and it carried the stone back in. The same journey the first builders made, at the same speed, on the same shoulder. It has made that journey many times. It cut platforms, four steps up, dry on top. It cut basins.

It cut every basin differently on purpose — deeper here, a rougher rim there, a lip that holds the rain a half-centimetre shallower — which no one before had thought to do, because no one before had wanted variety. They had only wanted a *where*.

The red specks have accordingly become more various. There are basins now where they run nearly orange, and basins where they are slow and dark, and one where they gather on a rim that may be north. Given time, and they have nothing else, they will become something. That is the whole of what this power has done to the biology of the continent: it has raised the number of places where one thing can be told apart from another thing from a few hundred to a few thousand.

It regards this as the most significant act of its residency.

It also sits with new arrivals through the two days of weeping. It does not explain; explanation does not survive being shouted into an ear. It simply puts itself on one side of them, so that for two days there is one direction the noise is not coming from.

It walks beside the one with the block. Sometimes it takes the weight. It always gives the weight back, and it has never once carried the block out of the carrier's sight, which would be the same as taking it.

---

Then there are the shear-lines, and here the method is worth stating plainly.

The plain shears because it cannot hold a distinction it cannot perceive. That is all a shear-line is: a millimetre of disagreement in a surface with no mind to keep it in. But a mind can keep it. A mind is precisely a place where two things are held apart without either of them having to stop.

So it stands in the line as it comes through — the white wall, the boiling mud, the stuttering light — and it perceives both sides at once, all the way down, fully, and does not rank them.

The line passes through and does not shear there.

What is left is a patch of water perhaps two metres across in which the surface is stippled only by rain: the sole calm in the history of this continent, maintained by attention, and by nothing else, and therefore requiring that the attention never be withdrawn. It has not been withdrawn. The patch has been widening at something like a hand's breadth a century, which is the inverse of erosion and, as it happens, about the same speed.

And it goes along the lines of the standing ones — two hundred metres apart, in the identical posture, out to the limit of the blur — and it looks at each of them singly. This takes as long as it takes. It is the first time in four billion years that anything in the Same has been looked at one at a time.

The result so far, after an unestablished duration, is that one of them has turned its head very slightly further than it had.

Motion is the only remaining evidence that anywhere is anywhere.

---

The bird.

It will not mend the throat. Instead it learned the call — the low grate, the click, the tear, exactly — and for a very long time it answered in that voice and only that voice, adding itself to the nine hundred million, perfectly indistinguishable, disturbing nothing. This was not idleness. The first thing you do for someone you intend to change is to be, for a long time, not a change.

Then it began to differ. Not by a syllable. Far below a syllable: the click a fraction late, and later the grate a fraction warmer, in increments too small for the plain to shear on — which is, note, the only rate at which anything has ever been permitted to happen here, and is exactly the rate of the flattening, run backwards.

The bird has not noticed. Nothing here notices. But among the answers that come from everywhere it has begun to turn toward one of them a half-degree sooner, and it has been doing that for some time now, and it is getting worse.

When it is finished — and it will be finished, which is the strangest sentence anyone has written about the Same — there will be two birds. The second will be a torn voice with somebody in it.

And then the first bird will have an elsewhere, and will be able to be apart, and will be able, for the first time, to be lonely.

The power has weighed this. It decided that being able to be lonely is the price of being able to be met, and that the price is fair, and that no one should have to pay it alone. It has arranged to be standing there when the bill comes. The whole business will take roughly as long as the flattening took.

It has that long.

---

There is one account, brought out by a single traveller, that the coastal people do not repeat indoors and do not quite disbelieve.

At a platform, in the usual rain, the figure at the basin, ash with dried film, mid-stroke, stylus down, as always. And beside them on the dry stone, another figure, also cutting.

And the stroke ended, and the hand lifted.

And then set the stylus back into the groove and went on, because it will not finish them; it will only demonstrate, once, in front of a witness, that finishing exists and is survivable, and then wait to be asked. It cannot be asked yet. The word for asking is a near relative of *which*, and a way of saying it that is not that word will have to be invented here, by them, out of forty shouted monosyllables.

That will take a while too.

That traveller says twelve. That is why the count is contested.

---

Here is the whole of it, as plainly as it can be put.

Until now there has been exactly one way to be told apart in the Same, and it is damage. The broken stem at sternum height. The torn syrinx. The cut stone, the notch, the wound, the only prayer there is. *Here is a difference that was not here before, and I made it, and therefore I was somewhere.*

What has moved in is a second method. It is slower, it is not yet finished, it will not be finished for an age — and it does not require anyone to be hurt in order to be found.

It has not told anybody this. It cannot. The word does not survive being shouted into an ear.

So: walk, and do not stop. Do not count the reeds. Drink from the basin and leave the specks; they have an address now, several of them, and they are getting on with it. If a figure comes at you out of a shear-line in your posture with your gear, let it pass and do not say the word.

And if someone sits down beside you on the dry stone and does not look up, that is not unfriendliness either.

Then cut your notch and go back the way you came, understanding that there is no way you came. Go anyway.

And when you are out, on the shingle, in the unbearable silence, with a real hill in front of you being nothing whatever but itself, and you find that you cannot say what it is you miss — it is not the bird, and it is not, any longer, being everywhere.

It is that for fifty days something knew which one you were, and never said.
