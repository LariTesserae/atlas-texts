# Attendance

Begin with the fact that it arrived rounded.

A power that is only benevolent arrives as itself and lets the place absorb the difference. A power that is also wise does the rounding beforehand — down the valley somewhere, on a wall of red rock above a river, out of sight — and comes up over the shoulder already worn smooth: no announcement, no light that was not going to be there anyway, no weather of its own. This cost something. Not much. Rounding is easy if you know it is coming and have agreed to it.

It did not arrive *at* the town. Nothing arrives at the town. You approach for an hour across a flat table with the place visible and audible the entire way, which is a landscape's way of telling you to think it over.

## The Term

The first thing it did was use the local instrument.

It could have read the plateau from outside: every pore, every seam, the whole fused calcareous body at once, the way you read a page. It put its wrists on the stone instead and lowered its head until the chin nearly touched, because a fact arrived at from above is not the same fact as the one arrived at from inside, and here the difference is the entire matter.

So for a year it read nothing but wind.

It learned what everything on the Drone Ground learns, which is how to be alone in the middle of a great deal of company. It learned that the roar is not an obstacle to hearing but the condition of it — that the ten silences are legible only because of the three hundred and fifty-five days that drown them. It learned that patience here is not a virtue. It is a climate.

## The Census, Taken Again

Then it counted, because counting is possible here, and where counting is possible it is the least invasive form of love.

Sixty-eight columns. Two hundred and forty-one chambers. Eight thousand four hundred moths in a good year and six thousand in a poor one. Nine lichens, of which the blue-grey crust in the third plaza is fourteen hundred and some years older than the power's own arrival in this part of the sky. One cup-plant, one genotype, liftable in two hands. In the rock, at a depth of one millimetre, a green film whose population has an exponent in it; the power made no plans regarding the film, since it has no plans of its own and cannot be helped or harmed by anything short of the sun.

And two hundred and forty-one names.

It holds all of them. This should be said plainly, because it has never been true before and may not be true again. On a still morning the roll comes braided and attenuated through forty joints of stone, and no stopper that has ever lived has separated more than forty signatures out of it. The power has the full set: two hundred and forty-one rhythms, three to nine beats each, every one older than the present height of the columns, every one attached to a room rather than to anybody.

It also learned the year the drone falls below hearing. There is no one to tell.

And it settled the question that cannot be settled by watching — whether the tuning is intended, whether the chord is known to the animals who make it. It has the answer. It has not given it and will not, because there is nobody here who could receive it and nobody elsewhere with any right to ask.

## What It Considered

**To raise the columns.** Refused. You cannot regrow the pipes without regrowing the builder, and the builder did not die of anything; it stopped, and stopping is not an injury. Besides: the claws of a stopper are cut to the fluting of a dead animal's plumbing, to within a millimetre. A new column is a lock with no key in the world.

**To arrest the erosion.** Refused, and this was the one that took longest. Held at its present height the Drone Ground would no longer be finished. It would be *held* — and there is a difference between a thing that has completed and a thing that has been forbidden to continue, and the second one is a specimen. The power has walked through galleries of specimens. It does not make them.

**To open the ears.** Refused as cruelty in a good coat. Three hundred and fifty-five days of standing thunder inside a skull the size of a walnut. The sealed ear is not a deficit; it is the mercy that time already performed, patiently, over a million years, without being asked. To undo it as a gift would be to arrive sharp.

**To cut more chambers.** Refused. Two hundred and forty-one is not a ceiling. It is a treaty. Double it and the population doubles, the copper mats are stripped faster than four centuries can replace them, the nulls empty of moths, and a hundred years later there are four hundred and eighty-two chambers with nothing in any of them and no names for most.

**To bring colonists** — a second moth, a pollinator, a grass to hold the thin soil. Refused. Fullness is a more complete refusal than hostility, and fullness is not a problem to be solved. Nothing here is missing anything. To say otherwise is to carry the standards of the valley up a mountain that stopped consulting the valley before the valley had its present shape.

**To take a breeding stock somewhere safe.** Refused, and this refusal has an edge on it that the power did not round. Names stay with rooms. A stopper carried off the plateau is not a rescued stopper; it is a nameless animal in a box, and its descendants are nameless too, for ever, in a species where namelessness has no precedent. The power will not manufacture nameless things in order that things shall not end.

**To tell them.** There is no channel. Every channel that exists is already full — the wrists with weather, the floor with everyone, the air with the wind's own roar. And what would the sentence have been? *You are known.* In a vocabulary of two hundred and forty-one words, all of which are names.

## Where the Pain Was

Benevolence is a tool with a specific use, so it went looking for the work.

There was almost none. This is worth recording, because it is rare. The lichen does not suffer. The moth is pushed into the nulls by physics and dies in a wall having never made a decision to regret. The cup-plant opens and closes. Even the deaths of the stoppers come late, at sixty years or eighty, in the dark, on a floor made of the compressed bedding and compressed bodies of everyone who has held that room before.

What it found was this. A juvenile in the second storey of the east avenue, still in the undercoat, whose mother had gone cold early in a bad winter. An old holder in the north cluster, eighty-one years in one chamber, forelimb plates worn concave and shiny as the handle of a tool, four days from the end of a long slow stopping. And eleven kilometres away and three thousand metres down, a broad-winged grey bird whose second chick had gone off the ledge in a squall.

And then it did the accounting, which is the part a lesser benevolence skips.

The budget here is closed. A birth follows a death one for one and has done since before the columns were this height. To save a life on the Drone Ground is not to add a life. It is to substitute one — to decide, on behalf of an animal that will now not exist, that it will not exist, and that animal already has a name, waiting in a room, older than the mosses.

So the power did the one thing that substitutes for nothing.

It stayed with the old one. Four days, in a chamber four metres up, with the fundamentals coming through the wall like a tide under a boat. It did not extend anything. It warmed the stone a little, which the fog would have done anyway by dusk. When the wrists went slack it kept its own pressed against the wall a while longer. That is not a rite in any place that has rites, and it has no name, and it cost the plateau nothing at all.

The juvenile found the autumn wads on its own. This was likely. It was not certain. The power watched and did not help and does not consider this a failure of nerve.

## The One Word

The ninth still morning of that year.

The drone thinned and sagged and stopped, and the stone went clear the way water goes clear when the silt settles, and within half an hour there were a hundred and fifty pale bodies out on the pavement, evenly spaced, wrists down, heads low. The power went out and lay among them. Nothing startled. That response was deleted from this place before there was anything here worth startling at.

It had one word available. It had decided not to be weather. So it said a name.

It chose the fourth storey of the north cluster, a room whose holder had died two seasons back and whose successor had not yet come: a rhythm of six beats, currently worn by nobody, older than the copper mats in the northern gardens. It put the name into the floor once, at the strength of a small animal, and stopped.

A hundred and fifty creatures with their wrists on stone felt a name they knew arrive from a room they knew was empty.

The roll faltered. Heads did not come up, because heads do not come up here; the response to the stone saying something unrecognised is to settle the wrists and go still, and that is what they did, all of them, for the better part of a minute — the same stillness a held stopper offers your thumb when it finds your pulse and cannot place it. So they read the power. It let itself be read. It has been examined by councils and by instruments in other places and considers this the most thorough.

Then a gust crossed the rim, the fundamentals came up out of the ground, the stone drowned, and everyone went inside, unhurriedly, and was alone again.

Within four seasons a juvenile took the fourth-storey chamber and began producing the six-beat name as if it had always been its own.

Nothing had been added. Nothing had been changed. The single utterance of a benevolent and wise power had been rounded, absorbed, laid down in the general fabric and made indistinguishable from the town, exactly as intended, and rather faster than expected.

## What It Undertook

There being nothing to repair, it undertook the only work the place has room for.

It will come for the still mornings. Ten a year, give or take, for as long as there are mornings: through the era when the chord loses its third fundamental, through the centuries when the columns are waist-high and then ankle-high, past the year the drone drops below hearing and becomes a pressure in the sternum and then not even that. It will lie on the pavement with its wrists down among however many are left, and it will take no part, and it will hold the names.

And on the morning when the last signature goes out into the floor of the last named room and there is nobody left anywhere in the town to receive it — it will be there, and it will answer.

That is the whole of the work. It is not much, for a power. It is the exact size of the place.

## Departure

On the fourth day it went down over the shoulder, in the same direction as the bird, for the same reason.

It could have stayed. It could have unsealed nothing and sealed its own ears, or softened the gale, or simply become a thing that does not need to sleep; none of these is difficult. It declined all three, because the plateau has a rule about visitors and the rule is honest, and a power that exempts itself from the local rule has stopped visiting and started owning.

Everything that hears eventually leaves. It hears. It left.

At dusk the cloud comes up over the rim and moves through the columns as a wet grey crowd, and in a chamber four metres up in the north cluster something the size of a fist puts both wrists flat against the wall and lowers its head, and reads nothing but wind.

That is still enough. It was always going to be enough.

The only difference — and it is not a large one, and it will never be felt through the stone — is that somewhere below the rim, something holds two hundred and forty-one names, knows which room is which, is coming back in the spring, and intends to be present at the end.
