## What It Did

Begin with the fact that nothing happened.

It arrived — if that is the word for something whose presence is a set of terms rather than a position — and for a long while it did not act at all. The notebooks record nine days of ordinary weather. From the other side, it was closer to the careful reading of a single page.

It read the bowl first, because the bowl is the argument. It found the gradient's inward lean and followed it to the point it aims at, and the point is not the plateau's geometric center. It is thirty-one kilometers off, on a line toward the eastern fringe where the rock is thinnest and the rise is fastest. Which is to say the lean is not aimed at the shape of the work. It is aimed at the balance of it, at the place where the load actually sits, and it is being corrected as the load moves.

That is not the mark of a mechanism. That is the mark of someone paying attention, continuously, for a very long time, to something heavy.

It read the four degrees, and the mixing, and the rate of rise, and found no error anywhere in any of them — not a drift, not a hunt, not the small oscillation that any real controller leaves as its signature. Then it read the green, all of it at once, every patch on two thousand kilometers of coast, and found the same thing, and understood what the crews had noticed and had not been able to finish thinking.

No failures. No sports. No lineages that went wrong. Eleven years of survey and not one patch that was the wrong shade.

An organism does not do that. An organism cannot do that; the copying is not that good and never has been anywhere. Which means the primer is not merely made. It is *held* — proofread, patch by patch, error by error, twice a day forever, alongside the bowl and the temperature and the stirring. The tenant's shape is not three facts. It is four, and the fourth is the tedious one, the one with no grandeur in it at all: somebody has been keeping a continent's worth of two-millimeter film from making mistakes, by hand, since before there was sand.

And the eighteen hertz is not an idle. Idles cycle. Compressors in basements shut off. This tone has no duty cycle and never has. It is the sound of a load being taken up and not put down.

---

Then it considered, at length, what to do, and most of the length went into the things it decided against.

It did not fill the sea. There was nothing stopping it. It could have put ten thousand species into that clear cold water in an afternoon, and they would have found four degrees and dissolved oxygen and a plateau of unbroken sunlight, and they would have eaten every gram of the primer within a season, and starved, and sunk, and lain on the new rock in the dark where nothing decays in any hurry. The shallows are not built. The sand is a few grams. And beyond the arithmetic there was a simpler reason: the table is set, and it is not set by the power, and you do not bring your own guests to another's table and seat them early.

It did not quiet the surf. This was the hardest of them, because the surf is the one cruelty here that falls on someone who can feel it. But the surf on square-edged rock is what throws spray ninety meters, and spray is what carries the flakes, and the flakes are what seed the hail, and the hail is the only means by which the green has ever crossed six hundred kilometers of open water. Silence would be sterilization. The mercy and the world are the same machine, and it runs at a hundred and forty decibels.

It did not warm the water, or level the meniscus, or drain the plateau into the correct ocean four thousand meters below, though something in the architecture of any tidy mind wants to. Wrongness of position is not error.

It did not give the crews back the top of their hearing. It looked at this for a long time. The loss is not damage; it is a price, and it was calculated in advance, quietly, by each of them, and paid. There is no way to reach in and unpay it that does not also reach into the only creatures on the plateau who chose anything. Consent is the one material here that cannot be manufactured, and there are perhaps forty grams of it in the whole world, and it is all in the camps.

It did not tell the green what it was for. There is no organ for receiving that. And a tool that has been told is not thereby less a tool.

It did not ask the tenant to stop, or to rest, or to explain itself, or to hand over. You do not tap a man on the shoulder while he is holding a beam. He cannot set it down to hear you, and the asking is a weight of its own. Whoever is holding this has not answered anything in eleven years of shouting and charges and instruments, and the power did not read that as absence or as arrogance. It read it as a man with his arms full.

And it did not write in the notebooks. The notebooks are the plateau's entire past. They are also the only thing on the plateau that belongs to anybody.

---

Then it did four things, and three of them are too small to see.

**First,** it took hold.

Not of the bowl — the bowl is aimed, and two hands on a load that is being aimed is how loads get dropped. It matched instead: it laid a second holding alongside the first, congruent, at the same setting, carrying nothing yet, the way a second person gets under a beam and takes no weight at all until the first person's arms begin to go.

A holding radiates. That is the only waste a bent gradient has. So there is now a second carrier over the Shelf Sea, at eighteen point zero two four hertz, and the two of them beat against each other with a period of forty-one seconds and change. The frequency was chosen with some care: it is incommensurable with the boring period, so it can never entrain the green, not once, not in the lifetime of the rock. It does nothing. It is audible in the sinuses and the long bones as a slow swell and fade under the tone that never stops, and the crews, within a month, were calling it the breath and arguing about instrumentation, and it is not instrumentation.

It is the only thing in this ocean that is neither surf nor labor. It is company, and it is tuned so as to be useless.

**Second,** it took the fourth fact. Just that one — the proofreading, the tedium, the smallest and least beautiful of the four, the one no monument would ever mention. It now holds the primer's error-correction, in parallel, redundantly, so that the correction is done twice and the tenant's share of it is no longer load-bearing.

And it holds it one part in a hundred thousand looser than it found it.

That was deliberate and it was the whole of the power's intervention in the design, and it is defensible in one sentence: a tool that is going to be handed over has to be able to survive its owner. Perfection is a form of dependence. Somewhere in the next year or two, on some ridge four hundred kilometers from any camp, a patch will come back from a squall the wrong green — half a shade off, a rhizoid that boils where it should bore, a flake that nucleates at minus three instead of minus two. It will be the first mistake in the history of the world. It will also be the first individual, the first lineage, the first thing on the plateau with a story that is not the plateau's, and when the guests finally come and begin to eat the primer, the primer will have somewhere to go.

The rate was set so that this cannot outrun the sand. Benevolence that does not do arithmetic is just weather.
