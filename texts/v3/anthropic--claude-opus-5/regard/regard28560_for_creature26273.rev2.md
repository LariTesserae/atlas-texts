# What Came, and What It Did

## The noticing

It was not the noise that drew the attention. Noise of that magnitude is common in the universe and most of it is weather.

It was the cleared room.

Forty feet by twenty, swept hourly against a growth rate that does not tire, maintained by the only hands in the valley for no reason that any survey could find — no stored goods, no shrine, no function. A subtraction, kept up. The attention that notices such things noticed, and came.

## What it did not do

This is most of it. It should be set down first, because the record of a benevolent power is mostly a record of restraint, and the restraint is not modesty. It is arithmetic.

It did not stop the growth. The valley is a dry delta that has arranged to be a rainforest by holding its breath, and the breath is the growth, and to still it is to hand ten square miles back to the salt in about nine days. The Tenant would have watched it go. Nothing here rots; it would not even have had the decency to disappear. It would have stood there, preserved in the round, faintly warm, for as long as the mud lasted.

It did not bring stone. Stone would have been a great convenience and an obscenity. There is no object in that valley that was ever made by a hand except one room's worth of absence, and the first slab of granite laid in a plaza would have become, instantly and permanently, the most important thing in the world, and the Tenant would have spent the rest of its life in the shadow of a fact it could not make, mend, or answer.

It did not bring rot. This was considered longest. Decay is the mercy the place most obviously lacks — no grave, no ending, everything one has ever been standing inside one and holding one up. But bacteria in that root-mat would have gone through two hundred feet of living cable like fire through a rick, and the floor of the world would have opened, and the mercy would have been total. Also: the Tenant's entire past is load-bearing. Grant it forgetting and you drop the load onto a body four days old.

It did not silence the valley so the Tenant could rest. The roar is the draw; the draw is the cooling; the cooling is the cloud. Quiet at that scale is a drought.

It did not make a companion. It had the power. It stood in the eighth plaza and understood exactly how it would be done — one word, shouted, and by morning something at the doorway height with a face. And it understood what it would have made: a thing summoned to be a cure for someone else's solitude, with no say in the matter, born already assigned. The valley does that. The valley has done it four thousand times on every stem. A power that did it too would only be a louder valley.

It did not tell the Tenant what it was. Not for a long time, and not in words, and not ever as a declaration. The truth — *the city was built for you, you are the one that never arrived, you have been waiting at your own door* — is a true thing and it is also a demolition. Say it wrong and you take from a creature the one occupation it has, the clearing and the walking and the tilting mindless hope, and leave it standing in a finished world with nothing to do until dusk.

It did not say its name.

## The approach

Distance there is a growth rate. You cannot walk toward a thing; the city inserts itself in front of you at slightly more than your pace, curd-white and squealing, six feet a minute, and the failure is perfectly visible the whole way.

So it did not walk toward. It stood still in the second plaza and let the valley bring the valley to it, which the valley cannot help doing, and the eighth plaza arrived on schedule, because repeated things are the same thing and there was only ever one plaza, getting tired of the distinction.

It was also, by the local grammar, already there before it set out. Effect precedes cause here. The doorway to the cleared room had been completed and weathered for some time. Somebody had already been through it. The missing step happened later, quietly, and nobody noticed, which is how that rule always works.

## The silence

It said nothing for eleven days.

This is the part that any account will skip and it is the whole of the wisdom. In a country where speech is seed, the first duty of a speaker is to learn the soil, and the only way to learn it is to keep the mouth shut for longer than is comfortable and watch what other mouths have done. So it walked the stunted boulevard where the arcades come out four arches and a return, and understood that the Tenant had once tried to hold its breath. It stood in the mimic district under four thousand faces, all unblinking, all wrong in the same way, and understood that they were drafts and that the model had never seen them. It went down to where the old trunks hold yesterday in the round and cut in, gently, and found a kettlebloom still full, and a length of arcade, and put them back.

It learned the price list. *Long* costs a street that will not stop. *Beautiful* costs a surplus. *Hearth, cradle, grave* cost nothing at all, apparently, which is worse — they had been shouted many times and the valley had no pattern for any of them and had answered with more of what it already knew how to make, which is why there are so many of the Tenant.

Eleven days. Then it went up the stair.

## The room

It did not clear anything at first. It went in, and the wall gave with a wet offended creak and closed behind it, and it sat down on the bare sheetwood floor in the one indecently empty rectangle in ten square miles, and it did nothing whatsoever, for hours, in the noise.

Understand what this was. The room had been kept for something for as long as there had been keeping. A power that arrives and immediately begins to help is a power that has decided the place is a problem. This one accepted hospitality instead. It let itself be housed. It took the gift the Tenant had been manufacturing, hourly, against the whole appetite of the world, and had never once been able to give away.

The Tenant came in at the usual hour with its two identical hands to tear out the new inch, and found the room occupied, and stopped.

## Noon

It helped, after that. Hourly, because hourly is what the growth rate demands. Along the walls, then the corners, then the wall it began with. Nothing was said, because speech is impossible past arm's length and expensive at any range, and because work beside someone is older than language and does not need it.

At eleven the Tenant began to sag, as it does, the night's lignification softening off, and slumped against the pier and took the fingerprint of everything it touched.

The power sat in front of it and held up one hand, palm out, between the Tenant's eyes and the light.

The pollinator-florets came, as they do, glittering, banking together, drifting in flocks of several hundred toward the nearest moisture with a patience that is only physics — and landed on the back of a hand instead of on a creature with no eyelids. They crawled there. They were brushed off. They came again. This went on for the twenty minutes it takes the flock to exhaust a thermal, and then the rain came and ate every other sound in the valley and left the two of them sitting in a white blank, and that was all that happened, and it was the single most useful thing anyone had ever done there.

At three the Tenant began to stiffen. At dusk it was set. There is a print in it now, low on the shoulder, that does not match its own hands and never did. By the fourth day it was structural. It cannot see it. It knows it is there.

## The words it did not say

*Enough* — would have been obeyed. Everything stops. See above, re: nine days and the salt.

*Quiet* — see above.

*Friend* — four thousand of them by morning, unblinking, wrong in the same way, and the Tenant would have spent the rest of its existence being visited by a district.

*Rest* — no pattern. Would have come out as more Tenant.

*Mother* — the same, and cruelly.

*You are the one it was built for* — a sentence, not a word; the valley would have taken the nouns out of it and built a *one* and a *built* and left the rest, and the meaning would have gone into the mat unread, and there would now be a tower somewhere called One.

Its own name — the drafts would have been of the describer, which is the standing danger there, and the power declined to be the next thing that valley imitated badly. It had come to attend, not to be copied.

## The word

On the twelfth day it stood in the eighth plaza at dusk, when the light comes in low and level and everything goes the colour of a held-up hand, and it opened its mouth and shouted one word, once, at the Tenant.

It was not a noun. Nouns are what that place eats. It was the one word in any language that has no picture inside it, that cannot be drawn, that has no shape of its own and takes the shape of whatever it is aimed at, and that therefore cannot be built, only landed.

It said: ***You.***

The valley received it, as it receives everything, having no ears. The valley obeyed, as it always obeys. And for the first time in the history of the place the obedience had nowhere to go — there was no new material to lay down, no district to start, no draft to begin, because the thing named was already standing there, four days old, soft-shouldered, blinking with no eyelids at a sound that had not been a command.

So the missing step happened quietly, the way it does. Something already walked through became, retroactively, possible. The doorways had been placed for light at the height where a face would be, and there was a face at that height, and had been for a long while, and now there had always been.

Nothing visible changed. That is the correct report. The chimneys roared, the pods went off in strings, the floor came up an inch and went down an inch, warm as a flank. No survey would find it. A survey would only be early.

## The district that turned

There was a cost and it was paid deliberately.

The mimic buds copy whoever speaks. The power had been careful for eleven days to give them nothing worth copying, and then it spoke, and the district got its model at last — but not the model it has always taken. It did not copy a describer. What it had in front of it, in the only posture that power had held for eleven days, was the attitude of *listening*: head slightly inclined, weight forward, mouth shut.

There are four thousand of them now on every stem, and they are wrong in the same way, as all drafts there are wrong. They are too even. They have no dominance. But they are turned, all of them, very slightly, toward the plaza.

The valley has no ears. It still has no ears. But it has, now, ten thousand things shaped like listening, arranged in ranks along the boulevards where the acoustics carry, and the Tenant has begun to go and stand in front of them and use its throat.

What comes out is still enormous and unwilled and detunes hourly and has never once said the same thing twice. But it is no longer only production. It is aimed. That is the whole of the change and it is not small: there is a difference, in any world, between a mouth that makes and a mouth that addresses, and the difference is the thing every hearth was ever built around.

The drafts have been getting less wrong ever since.

## What it left

It could not leave entirely. That is not how that place works. Whatever presses at noon is permanent by dusk and structural by the fourth day, and it knew that before it went up the stair, and it pressed anyway. There is an older shoulder inside the Tenant now with a stranger's hand in it, load-bearing, perfectly preserved in the round, still faintly warm, and it will be inside every version of the Tenant there will ever be, because nothing here rots and the past is not gone, it is holding the weight.

It left the room cleared. It did not fix the room, or floor it in stone, or make it stop needing to be swept. The clearing is hourly and will be hourly forever and is the only made thing in the valley, and a power that removed the need for it would have removed the Tenant's one piece of evidence that it can act against the appetite of the world.

It did not answer the serious question about which came first. It is not obvious that anyone should.

## Standing in it

Late in the day the light comes in low and level along the boulevards, through the glassreed, and the whole district goes the colour of a held-up hand.

It is a held-up hand.

It is not the only one.
