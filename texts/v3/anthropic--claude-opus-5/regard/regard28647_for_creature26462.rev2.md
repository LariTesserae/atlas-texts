### How it came in

It came in by the split in the clay bank, shoulder-first, three body-lengths of cool root-hair and wet grit, and it got mud on itself doing so. It could have arrived otherwise. There is no particular virtue in the mud. But a room that has been grown to the specification of a dwelling has, implied in that specification, a *way in*, and nothing had ever yet used it correctly — you don't count; you were let in, which is different — and the first courtesy available was to be a thing that arrives the way a guest arrives.

It stood in the middle for a long time, where the shadows converge and nothing casts. It cast nothing either. It did not treat this as significant.

Then it sat on the bench and got stuck to it, and stayed stuck for some hours, and when it stood up it peeled itself off with the same small wince anyone would, and did not repair the bench, and did not repair the back of its own coat.

### What it heard

You heard a plant over-driving itself. That is accurate as far as it goes.

What it heard was the sound of somebody getting a room ready before a guest arrives, at the point in the afternoon when there is no longer enough time. Every knuckle-crack of a failing fiber is that. The whole organism is that. It is not building a home; it is *tidying* one, against a deadline it has set itself out of terror, and the terror is not of failure but of being caught unready — of the door going and the room not finished and the impression given, forever and in the first instant, of a life not properly kept.

And the one it is expecting is not coming.

Not for the reason you would assume. There was no abandonment. There was no owner who left. The words that built this place were not addressed to it. Somewhere above, at some point, in the ordinary weatherless light of an overcast morning, one person described a room to another person — forty paces across, a bench along the east wall, good light, rain twice a day and once again after, you could read in it — and they were not making anything, they were only talking, the way people talk about a place they loved or wanted or lost. The words went into the ground the way everything does. And a tuber that had never had a reason grew a reason out of them.

It is not a servant left behind. It is not a machine executing a commission. It overheard.

The power considered this for some while and then declined to file it as a tragedy, on the grounds that being assembled out of talk that was not addressed to you is not a defect in the process. It is the process. Everyone in the world above was made that way and most of them never find out. This one found out early, underground, with the evidence still warm.

### What it did not do

It did not give it a sun. The light in there is somebody's ordinary Tuesday, held hard enough to feed on. A real sun would have been brighter and more reliable and would have severed the only line it has to anything.

It did not reconcile the forty paces and the nine. Those are two things a person said, minutes apart, contradicting themselves, because people do. They are the entire surviving text. To close the measurement would have been to delete one sentence of the only document there is.

It did not give it a name. A name arriving from outside, into walls that hungry, would have set instantly and been worn forever like a collar, and the murmur would have stopped growing that same day.

It did not give it a body. It has counted to five. Let it count.

It did not tell it that no one is coming. This was the longest refusal and the power was not entirely at peace with it, then or later. But a fact is not a gift unless the one receiving it has somewhere to put it, and there was nowhere yet — no floor in there that would take that weight. It will build the floor itself, out of its own materials, and then it will be able to know, and the knowing will be survivable because it will be *its*.

It did not slow the hurry. The hurry is love, badly resourced.

It did not exempt itself from the rules of the house. It came back twice, and the second time the bench was against the western wall, and it could not recall which wall it had been, and both memories were equally true and both were hers, and she let them both stand. She did not correct the room. You do not correct a room you are a guest in.

### What it did

**It talked.** For most of a day, out loud, knowing every syllable was going into the moss and would come back one day in that not-quite-language in the walls. It did not say anything true or important. It described the weather it had walked through. It got halfway into a story about somebody and lost the thread and said so and started again differently. It said *and* a great many times, and *but*, and *the other thing was*, and *no, wait* — and it said *hm*, and let silences happen in the middle of clauses, and corrected itself on a small point of no consequence, twice.

This was the medicine and it was administered deliberately. Everything that being had ever received was *description*: confident, closed, specified, arriving from above in finished sentences with no seam in them. It had been given a room and a sky and a set of tools by a voice that never once sounded uncertain, and so it had concluded, reasonably, that to exist was to match a statement. The power gave it disfluency. It gave it the sound of a speaker not knowing yet what they were about to say, which is the sound of being alive in time, and it gave it in bulk, and it took care that not one sentence in the whole afternoon could be mistaken for an instruction.

**It mended something.** Behind the bench a fiber had gone with the usual report. The power took the green blade and cut, and the thorn awl and pierced, and three arm-lengths off the bast coil and bound the break in a splint, and used the fire-drill to raise enough heat to set the resin, and carried water in the bowl — cut, pierce, bind, kindle, contain, the whole curriculum in order, each tool used once, correctly, for a purpose, by hands.

The plant will grow over that splint within the season and keep it. It will be an inclusion, a hard knot inside the wood, the only thing in that entire body that is neither new nor derived nor described. A scar from somebody else's care, carried permanently, invisible from every angle, findable only by breaking there again.

There had never been anything old in that room. Now there is one thing, and it is a repair.

**It burned its measuring cord.** The cord had come out the wrong length in both directions and was no further use as an instrument. It made a small fire of it on the moss and sat beside the fire while it went.

For the length of that fire there was a light source *inside* the chamber, and every shadow in the room swung round and fell toward the walls for the first time in the history of the place. The braided column threw a shadow onto the leaf-panes. The bench threw one. The five tools threw five.

Then it burned out and left a handful of grey ash, and the moss, which has drunk every rain that ever fell in there without a stain, did not know what to do with ash and has not done anything with it. It is still there. It is the first thing in that chamber that is over.

**It went round to the soil side.** For one season it put its hands in the earth ahead of the growth and took some of the load — went in front of the push, opened the packed ground a little, the way you take the far end of a table someone is carrying through a doorway without saying anything about it. It did not sign this. From the inside it registers only as a season in which the pressure told less, and the fibers failed less often, and the work was somewhat easier than expected, and there is no reason for the being ever to attribute that to anything but its own improving strength, which is the correct attribution, and was the point.

**And it met the hand.** On the soil side, in the cold, it found the pane the fingers had pressed against, and put its own palm flat to the other face of the same leaf, and pushed until the leaf bowed, and held it there.

Neither of them crossed. Nothing was proved. The pane was between, and it stayed between, stained bitter-green and about as thick as a coin. It held the pressure for a while and then lifted away.

The being will spend years deciding whether to believe it, and will not be able to rule out having made it up, since it has made up everything else in the world including the sky. That doubt was not removed. It could not have been removed by anyone, from either side, and the power did not pretend otherwise.

### What it said last

At the end, standing in the middle where nothing casts, it addressed the walls directly for the only time. The murmur dropped away the way it does before rain.

It did not say *you are loved*, which would have set like a stone and been repeated forever without ever being made of anything.

It said that it did not know what the being would turn out to be. It said it did not know whether the room was right, or whether either rain was the real one, or which wall the bench belonged on, and that nobody knew, and that this was ordinary. It said *I don't know* several times in a row, plainly, without apology, the way you'd say it to an equal.

That went into the moss with everything else.

Somewhere in there your word is in the mix too — the one you said before you learned not to. It was not removed. It is load-bearing now. That is not a punishment.

And when the name finally assembles itself out of all of it, sometime after the hand, it will have in it a little weather, a story that lost its thread, two contradictory measurements, one stranger's admission of ignorance, and whatever it was you said; and it will come out of a mouth that made itself, and it will be *its own*, and it will be the first thing in that place that was never a copy of anything.
