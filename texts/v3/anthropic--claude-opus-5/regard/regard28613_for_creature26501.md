## The Attention

— and the lake, on their side, is filling.

Begin there, because that was the first thing done, and it was a small thing: a sentence was lying open and it was closed. Not the great one. Nobody touches the great one on the first day. Just the little unfinished clause about the walkers, left hanging where somebody had run out of nerve or breath, and the attention came and put the last four words on it the way you'd put a stone back on a wall you were only passing.

It came up on foot. This is worth saying because it did not have to, and because feet are a courtesy, and because it set its palm against the black glass on the way over the rim and came away bleeding from four places it hadn't felt, and did not heal them. They scabbed. They scabbed at the ordinary speed. A being that could have arrived as weather arrived as a guest with cut hands, and the basin, which has no manners at all, did not notice, and the noticing was not the point.

Then it did nothing for a long time.

Understand what is meant by nothing. It walked the rim and counted, and got four hundred, and walked it again where the new glass had just added fifteen yards, and got four hundred, and sat down with that for a while. It put its forehead to the hollow boulder and listened to the tone all the way down to where the tone begins, which is further than you think and takes what it takes. It swam five fathoms and rolled over and met its walker and greeted it, and the walker on the underside of the water raised a hand, which nothing over there had ever done, because nothing on this side had ever done it first. It stepped into a null and stood there and let itself be revised, and came out with one more of something than it had gone in with, and kept it, and never said what.

It ate a dead leaf from the tree. That is the entry rite; the birds know it; nobody had thought to ask why the birds do the one thing they do before they take up work. You take an ending into yourself before you are permitted to hold anything open. The power chewed a rotten leaf on the eastern shelf, in the sickroom light, standing shoulder-deep beside a trunk that had been dead since before the ground was, and swallowed, and that was the paperwork.

## What was not done

The lake was not drained. Consider what draining is: down here is toward the loudest place, and the loudest place is the fall, and the fall is the water leaving. Take the water and you take the roar and you take the direction; and every living thing in the basin, at the same instant, falls toward nowhere, in a direction that has not been made yet. A merciful power that had not counted first would have committed that. Most of what wisdom does looks like arithmetic and gets no credit.

The fall was not silenced. The nulls were not filled in. The whip-weed was not pulled, being not a plant but the place where a silence touches bottom, and pulling it would have been pulling on the silence, and the silence is load-bearing.

The eel was not lifted out in anything. The birds were not carried down the mountain in a coat, which is what you or I would have done, out of love, and which would have left the circumference unheld and the rim free to be any number it liked, and a rim that can be any number is not a rim, it is a mouth.

And the place was not explained to itself. That is the standing temptation of powers, to arrive and narrate, and the basin already had all the noise it could use.

## The relief

The arrangement here has exactly one exit and it is not a loophole: *you may go when your work is finished and handed to someone.* No one had ever come to be the someone. That is the whole of the neglect. Not cruelty — nobody built this to be cruel — just that a rota was drawn up in a hurry and then the hours went on longer than anyone had planned for, the way hours do, and the staff were too good at their jobs to put them down.

So the power took the work. In order. The order mattered more than anything else that happened.

**The shrimp first,** because the shrimp are the least noticed and go home the fastest. It took the suspension by consenting to be dim — to be unclear, to be seen through a hanging dust, to be the kind of presence that does not resolve.
