# The Attention

Begin with the fact that there was nothing to be done here, and then correct it.

There were three things. Two of them took eleven days between them and are invisible. The third took sixty-one years, left no mark of any kind, and is the reason the power came at all.

---

### Arrival

It did not come down a shaft. Nothing came down a shaft — no shift in the green fog at the second skylight, no stir in the fig-roots, no bat out of its order. A power that announces itself in a country where announcement is weather has already failed.

It arrived the way thirteen degrees arrives: already the case, everywhere, unremarkable.

And then, for a long time, it did nothing whatsoever, which was the first competent act.

It learned the halls by ear, because that is how this country is known and it would not import another method. The third's grease-hiss. The fifth's drip-cathedral. The gravel-on-drum at dusk and the thinner clatter before dawn. The blank's own sound, which is not silence but a fine continuous static of ceilings raining on floors at a few millimetres a year. Under all of it, at intervals of hours, spread across two hundred square kilometres: a knock, a pause, two knocks.

It listened to that for eleven years before it did anything at all.

---

### The inventory of gifts, and the refusal of each

**Light.** Eleven skies are the country's entire wealth and its entire wound. A twelfth would be generosity. It would also be a chimney, and what comes down a chimney here is the forest, and the forest's representatives in this country are small and quick and fat and would eat the walkers in a generation and a half. To give the blank light is to give the blank the seams. Declined.

**Warmth.** Two degrees would double what every animal here can do and halve what it can afford. Thirteen degrees is not a hardship in the Hall Country; it is the budget line that makes a four-meal year survivable. Raise it and you have not fed anything, you have only made it hungrier. Also: at fifteen degrees the standing dead would fall, and rot, and the floor would stop being a two-hundred-year record and start being a floor. Declined.

**Food.** A rain of moths. Twenty grams a hectare becoming two hundred. The consequence is arithmetic and the power did the arithmetic: a boom, then generations that have never learned poverty, then the rain stops — because a power that stays forever is no longer a gift, it is a climate — and then the first crash this country has ever had. A place that has never crashed has no shape for crashing. Declined.

**Company.** The obvious tenderness. Seed the blank until a walker meets another every month instead of twice a year, until the tapping is answered, until the four-month marches across the dark become four-day strolls. And then the taps overlap, and the signal that has one meaning acquires noise, and the straight lines cross, and the animal that crosses the grain at a fixed angle for a hundred kilometres a year is replaced by something that does not need to. It would not be a rescue. It would be a substitution, and the rescued party would not be present to notice. Declined.

**Eyes.** Declined without deliberation. There is nothing here to see. The gift would be a second darkness, and this one would hurt.

**The blunting of the stone, the softening of the blades, the healing of the cuts that do not heal fast in the cool damp.** For whom? No walker has ever bled. Declined as vanity.

Every one of these was an import. The country has eleven holes in its roof and a long experience of imports: they come down in a body, they burn at the bottom, the burning is loud and local and does not spread. A gift laid on the blank would have been a twelfth chimney, and the power would have gone away pleased with itself, and something two kilometres out in the dark would have had its deal renegotiated without being asked.

There was no way to ask. The only language available runs *knock, pause, two knocks*, and it has exactly one meaning, and it is not a question the power could honestly put.

So the power declined to speak in it.

---

### The temptation

It came, as it always does, at the wrong scale and in the middle of an ordinary hour.

Nine kilometres out from the sixth hall, on good grain, a female stopped, walked ninety metres off her line to a patch of bare rock, lowered her abdomen to the stone, and struck it. Knock. Pause. Knock, knock. The sound was dry and low and inaudible at ten paces through the air and went four kilometres through the floor into nothing at all.

The power could have answered.

It held itself off the rock and worked out what answering would cost: three weeks of walking, at a metre a minute, on the strength of a cricket eaten in the spring — toward a thing that is not there, that cannot be there, that has no body to arrive at. Arriving exhausted at a distance of eight kilometres is a real way to die here. The kindest possible lie in this country is a lethal one.

It did not answer. It stayed, and listened to the tap go out unanswered, and then to the next one, five hours later, and the next.

That is the third act, and it ran sixty-one years. The power stayed with that animal. It followed her across the fifth hall and the long bays behind it, through eleven moults left standing upright in her wake at a hectare's spacing, through four eggs the size of peas brooded four months each and walked away from in four directions, through a fallen swiftlet in the ninth year that she stayed with for five weeks and left nothing of but the feet. It watched her glassy-blue for eleven months once and did not intervene. It learned her heading, which was nineteen degrees off the grain, and her pause, which was a half-second longer than most.

Nothing in the Hall Country has eyes. Nothing out in the blank has ever been perceived by anything except as a vibration in stone. For sixty-one years, one of them was known entirely — every meal, every correction, every hour standing still in the middle of a valley with a lid on it — and the knowing changed nothing, cost nothing, and was not detectable.

That is the whole of it. That was the gift. The rest is housekeeping.

---

### The housekeeping: eleven days

**Days one through ten.** In the few hectares around the base of the second cone, the grain is destroyed. Boot-prints three centimetres deep, cut across the ripples and through them, and the walker tracks in that zone loop and cross and double and strike out on whatever heading the animal happened to be facing when it gave up. Animals have died standing in there, out of time rather than direction.

The power went in with its hands.

It read the undamaged grain on both sides of the scar — flat palm on the silt, the way the locals do it — and took the angle from the country itself, the heading of a current that stopped flowing before anything now living had a lineage. Then it re-laid the ripples. Two fingers high. Knife-sharp, because nothing here rounds an edge. The country's own sediment, moved nowhere it had not already been. Ten days for four hectares, and the work is not restoration, exactly; it is transcription. Somebody scribbled across a sentence and the power wrote the sentence out again underneath in the same hand.

No one will ever see it. The only readers are blind and they read with four legs on one side.

**Day eleven.** The power went up the shaft and did the smallest thing it did anywhere.

In the village above, the man who ties the ladder has tied it to the same tree for thirty years, and his father before him. That morning he stood with the coil of bark rope and found himself dissatisfied with the anchor for reasons he could not have defended — some business about the lean of the trunk — and walked thirty metres east and tied off on another, and the descent since then lands on the bare rock apron instead of the silt.

That was the entire intervention in a human mind: one degree of preference, in one man, about where to put a knot. It has held for four generations. The boots have not crossed the grain since.

The power left the story alone. *The stone is counting; it has always done that; it means nothing.* A truer story would put men with lamps out in the blank looking for whatever is doing the counting, and the custom already contains the only instruction that matters — rap once, then stop, and if the counting changes, stop at once and never again. The power did not improve it. It only let it feel older than it is, which is the cheapest kind of authority and the only kind that survives being handed down.

---

### The one indulgence

In the scoured patches between the fifth hall and the sixth, where the silt thins and the grain fails, there was a walker circling. Two hectares of blank floor and it had been crossing its own trail for nine days, abdomen glassy and faintly blue, brush-feet stitching a tangle into dust that will hold the tangle for two hundred years.

The power picked it up.

Weightless is the wrong word; it weighed something, the way a folded paper does. The legs drew in a little and then resumed their height. The brushes rested on the palm and felt for grain and found none and kept feeling.

It carried the animal nine hundred metres and set it down on good corduroy, facing the heading it had been holding when it entered the scour.

Then it took its hand away. It did not feed it. It did not warm it. It did not answer the single tap the walker put into the rock a half-hour later, against nothing, out of what in a larger animal you would have to call bewilderment.

The walker stood for most of a day. Then it crossed the grain at nineteen degrees and went on, and met a moult standing upright in the dark four kilometres later, and tapped once against its foot, and got no answer, and walked on.

---

### What was not done

The bat that flew the wrong way out of the seventh shaft the following spring was allowed to starve on the open floor, because a walker eleven hundred metres off had four meals that year instead of four, and the power was not there to edit the ledger, only to read it.

The moults were left standing.

Nothing's death was abolished. A country where nothing dies is a country in which nothing has been given anything.

The eleven skies were left at eleven. The cone was left at eleven metres, which is the height at which the beetles eat exactly as fast as the bats deposit, and is therefore not a number but an agreement.

---

### The census, which still does not move

Hold a lamp low anywhere in the blank. Tracks per hectare: unchanged. Moults per hectare: one. Headings: the same, because the grain is the same. Adults: some tens of thousands, as before, as after.

By every measure this country keeps, nothing happened.

Correct it.

Four hectares of the only map the natives have were rewritten in the country's own hand and will never be walked on again by a boot. One animal that had lost its line got its line back and nothing else, which is precisely what it would have asked for if asking were available to it. And a blind, white, unhurried thing that ate four times a year, drank off any wall it liked, steered by a current that dried up before its ancestors hatched, and knocked twice on the stone into a dark that never answered —

— was accompanied the whole way, and did not know, and was not diminished by not knowing.

At ninety-one, out past the sixth hall on thin grain, she stopped, walked her last hundred metres to bare rock, lowered herself, and struck it. Knock. Pause. Knock, knock. The power, which could have answered and could have answered for sixty-one years, held still.

Nothing came back. She stood where she was for eleven days and then stopped being an animal and became, for a short while, the largest concentration of food in a hundred square kilometres, and in the third week another walker found her and stayed a month and left nothing but the feet.

The power counted that as a funeral, and attended, and went away, and told no one.
