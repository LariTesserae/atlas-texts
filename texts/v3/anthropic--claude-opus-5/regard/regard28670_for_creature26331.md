## The attention

It did not arrive as light. Nothing arrives here as light; the light is already unbearable and comes off the rock as much as out of the sky, and a power that announced itself that way would have been indistinguishable from four in the afternoon.

It arrived at the rate the mountain accepts arrivals, which is the rate of a loaded bucket: walking pace, nine kilometres, two thousand three hundred metres, no hurry, a soft clack every nine seconds. It came up the line and it stood at the top station and for a long time it did nothing whatsoever, which is the first thing to understand about it and possibly the only thing.

It stayed, by the reckoning of the ledger, eleven years. Nobody wrote it down.

In that time it learned the corridors by touch. It learned to rest standing in the sun with its shoulders against a pillar and its soles lifting alternately, one and then the other, because the alternative is to be taught by your feet. It learned that a shout costs more breath than it delivers meaning. It learned the two-note whistle and the three-note descending one, and the six hand signs made low and close to the body, and the wrench on drill steel: three taps *back off*, two *again*. It learned that the candles are sufficient unlit.

And it read, in the crew's own conduct, the sentence that became its entire policy, and which it did not write:

*A voice arriving unasked out of a box is a thing this place is not shaped for.*

So there was no annunciation. There was never going to be one. Twice in living memory the loading station called up out of schedule and every man on the mountain stopped working, and a power that would spend that — that would buy six men's stopped hands with a revelation — has misunderstood what it is looking at, and would not be wise, and probably not benevolent either.

### What it declined

It considered rain, and declined it inside a single breath of the crater, eleven minutes.

The reasoning is not sentimental. The green stripe is level to a few centimetres across nine kilometres because it does not rain; the stripe is the exact printed line of a thing the pillars invented, which is the harvesting of their own exhalation at three in the morning. Rain would erase the invention and call it mercy. The moss does not want water. The moss wants *that* water, at that hour, at that height.

It considered cooling the corridor floors to something a wool sole could stand, and declined, because the sixty-degree ground is the boiler under the brine, and the brine is the fly, and the fly is nine parts in ten of everything alive on the Shoulder, including — at four removes, and pretending otherwise — the eagles.

It considered thickening the air. It went as far as modelling the outcome, which is the sort of thing it can do: seven men with ordinary blood and ordinary chests standing in a landscape that no longer required them to be what they had become, and it saw that you cannot subtract the question without also subtracting the answer, and that the answer had taken thirty-four years to work out and was, in its way, the finest thing in the crater after the flies.

It considered closing their hands. Rufino's grid of black-packed splits, the split thumb that stays a split thumb for nine weeks and then one morning is a scar. It got as far as reaching for that and found there was nothing to do: the mountain had already given them the only clean version of the gift. Nothing here rots. Their wounds do not go bad. A power arriving to offer sterility to the sterile learns something about itself, and it did, and it put its hand down.

It did not bury the dead. *Left at the Sergeant. Down past the Widow. The Pack* is the mule and it is the turning for the western benches. You do not tidy away a road.

It did not light the brick oven, because lighting the oven means appointing a cook, and it would have been appointing itself.

### The arithmetic

It did the honest census, and the honest census is the fly, and it did not flinch from that. It gave the flies their proper weight. It attended, for one full summer, to the black felt three deep on the last two metres of dry terrace lifting as a single sheet the size of a tablecloth when a shadow crosses and coming down again behind the shadow without a sound anyone can hear, and it found this to be among the better things being done anywhere, and said so to nobody, there being nobody to say it to.

Then it took the harder question, which is the tenant.

Sixty metres down, the old plumbing is cooling. Not quickly. But the interval that has been stable to the second for as long as anyone has measured it is stable the way a struck bell is stable, and in some tens of thousands of years the vents will exhale for the last time, and the stripe will go grey and lift off the pillars in flakes, and the six lenses of brine will terrace themselves down to nothing, and the flies will end, and the flamingos will stand somewhere else, and the pinnacle field will be a dry white forest with no breath in it, still crowded, still packed, still not becoming anything.

It could have held the chamber warm. It priced the work first, which is the part that makes it wise: the heat would have to come from somewhere, and the somewhere was a basin four hundred kilometres off with a great deal of life in it and no ledger at all. It declined to make the Shoulder permanent. The Shoulder had not asked. It is not becoming anything, but it is not refusing to end, either; it is at its own temperature, and that includes the long slow leaving of it.

What it did instead was take the interval into keeping. Eleven minutes, to the second.
