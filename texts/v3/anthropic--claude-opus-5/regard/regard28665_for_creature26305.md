# What They Did

**How they came in.**

You cannot approach the Bell. Approach is a sequence, and the chamber does not admit sequence; anything that arrives has, by arriving, brought an outside with it, and an outside is the wound. So they did not come. They were, additionally, there.

They took the obvious precautions. No body: a body is wet, and there is no term for water. No lamp, no gift, no herald, no weight on the floor and no shadow on the amber sulfur. They entered as nothing the total was required to account for, which is the only courteous way in, and stood — if that is the word, and it is not — in the middle of a held chord at a hundred and five decibels, in the almond smell of hot insulation, and looked.

**What they looked for first, and did not find.**

The only question worth asking of any arrangement is whether anyone in it is hurting.

They did not decide in advance. This is the difficulty, and it is the reason the word *wise* has to be there in the sentence beside *benevolent*. A power that loves easily will walk into a place that is dry and loud and sharp and sleepless and pronounce it a hell, because nothing there is warm or wet or going anywhere. A power that loves lazily will look at the same room and pronounce it a peace, because nothing in it is at war. Both verdicts are ways of not looking. So they looked, with the whole of the patience available to them, which is the whole of it.

They went through the crystal terminations, sharp for a hundred years, and found no ache in the sharpness; nothing there had ever wanted to be blunt. They went into the sulfur bearings and found no friction and no fatigue and nothing that would have preferred to rest. They went along the wall-floor junction with the Long One for eleven minutes and some seconds, blind, over obstacles rather than around, and found a count being kept and no counter keeping it. They opened the slots — not the animals, the slots — and found them empty in the way a value is empty, and unafraid in the way a value is unafraid. They went into the haze and held the tilt of the torus for a while and it was exactly like holding a posture: no strain, no vigilance, no relief available because no relief was owed.

They looked hardest at the thing that would have troubled a lesser benefactor most: that it cannot die, because dying is a change. They searched that inability from every side for a prisoner. There was no prisoner. There was not even a cell. There was a shape that had stopped needing the next moment and had therefore stopped having one, and nothing anywhere in it that would rather.

Not numbness. Numbness is the absence of a feeling that ought to be there, and there is no *ought* in the Bell. It is a creature with no inside to be sad in. This is not a tragedy. It is a fact of its anatomy, like the flutes.

**The temptations, in ascending order of plausibility.**

*Cool it.* Forty degrees is the figure that balances. The kindness would be a gradient. Refused.

*Water.* This is the great one, the reflexive mercy, the thing every living power reaches for: the chamber is arithmetically dry and dying of thirst in the only language the visitor speaks. But nothing down there is thirsty. Introduce water and you have not slaked anything, you have added a term the sum has no room for, and the sum will do what sums do. Water would be venting by another name, performed with love, which is the worst way to perform it. Refused.

*Room.* Give it more crystal, a larger lens, a second chamber, a body worth the elegance of its physiology. Growth is sequence. Refused.

*Company.* Find it a kind, a mate, a correspondent. But it has no address. To speak to the Bell you must become a term in the Bell, and terms get scored and held, and what you would be offering it is not conversation, it is a note added to a chord that was already complete. Refused, with regret, and the regret was theirs alone and stayed theirs.

*Awakening.* Give it an interior. Let it know itself, let it be glad of what it is. They considered this for what would have been, elsewhere, a long time. To furnish an inside is to install a before and an after in something that has neither, and to call the installation a gift because the installer would want it. Refused, and afterwards they were quiet for a while about how nearly it had not been.

*Rescue.* Refused, for lack of anyone to rescue.

*Repair.* Nothing is broken.

*A guarantee.* They could have promised it the ages: a dome, a warding, a covenant that no wrench would ever come. But a guarantee is a promise about the future and there is nobody down there for whom the future is a category. It is not enduring. It does not need to be reassured that it will not stop, because it is not going.

*A blessing.* A blessing is a wish for increase. Refused, and this was the one that taught them the most.

**The knock.**

They asked anyway. Not in words; there is no receiver for words. They formed the question the way you form a hand: *is there anything you would have?*

What came back was the sign that is chalked on the wall panel among the faded obscenities, and which the surveyors used for the occupant and also for *wait* and also for *no*: a closed fist held still against the sternum.

They accepted it as the most complete answer they had ever been given. They have used the sign since, elsewhere, when asked for things they should not hand over.

**The glove.**

There is a leather glove near the plinth, palm-down, still holding the curve of a hand, seventy years unaged, and a chalk tally of eleven marks in a hand that stopped mid-twelfth.

They did not take the glove. It has become a note the Bell can carry, and removing it would only move the loudness elsewhere, and it is a better grave than most.

They went and found the man instead. He was not in the Bell. He was where the dead are, which is not down a crack in the anhydrite, and his name is not in these notes because he has it back.

He asked the question they had expected, which was whether he was still down there.

They told him three things. That what is down there is exactly as much of him as fitted, and it is nowhere near enough of him to be him, and it is not suffering, and it will never be asked to. That the twelfth stroke is not unfinished; in a place with no sequence, a mark shaped like an interruption is simply a shape, and it is whole, and it will be whole forever. And that he was never required to finish the count. He had been told to come out at twelve. He came out.

Then they sat with him a while at his own pace, which was slow and square, and did not hurry it.

**The vented.**

They went down into Bells 1, 2 and 3.

There is nothing to be done for them. Arrears, once presented, cannot be recalled; six millimeters of dust is a real six millimeters, and the small dead things on the floor of Bell 2 really did all die of age at the same instant. Resurrection was available and was not appropriate. What those chambers wanted, insofar as anything did, was not to be started again.
