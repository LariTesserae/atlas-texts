# The Attention

**The arrival.**

It did not come down as light. Light is the one thing the basin cannot receive without injury, and anyway light would have arrived at the wrong speed. It came in with the spring plume, in April, heavy with rock flour, and went where the plume goes — down the old streambed, keeping its banks for two kilometres, fraying at the end like everything else that enters here.

Then it stopped, and the stopping took four hundred years.

This was not indecision. A power that thinks at the speed of a spark cannot perceive a being whose thought is a change in the direction of growth; it can only perceive a stillness, and mistake the stillness for absence, and act, and be a catastrophe. So the first work was to learn the beat. Twenty-two minutes. The lean, the hold, the reversal. The white fog climbing eleven metres and swallowing the groves and withdrawing. It kept that time until keeping it was no longer an effort, until it could feel a season as an evening, until a lampweed's life had the shape of a sentence and forty years of white lace over a fallen stem read as a slow and courteous meal.

Only then did it look.

---

**What it read.**

It read the field system first, because the field system is legible from anywhere: the trunk cables densest where the terraces held soil, the catenaries following the compacted paths, the whole organism wearing a drowned village's clothes like a coat handed down. It read the thirty-one rooms and found them lined in fan-work three times the ordinary thickness and understood that it was standing inside an ear.

It read the fast.

That took longer, because a refusal leaves no trace except an edge, and edges are easy to explain away. The lace eats wood. The lace eats bast. The lace eats bark, and lives inside bark's chemistry the way we live inside air. And on a stone ledge in the second house there is a wooden bowl, a coil of bast cord, and a bark box with fired beads in it, and the growth stops at the threshold stone, and the stop is not a ragged edge. It is trimmed. Somebody dresses that line. Somebody has been dressing it since before the ninth shoreline, in the dark, at the tempo of stone, with no witness and no reward and no name for what they are doing and no possible reason that can be put in words the doer could hold.

The power sat with that for a while. Not a long while. Sixty years.

Then it read the cairns, and the cairns were worse, or better.

Six flat river cobbles a year, of a rock that does not outcrop within the basin: they come over the rim with the spring melt from a tributary on the far side of the saddle, are dropped by the plume where the plume slows, and are gathered — floated in barrel-sized purses of bladder laminate lashed with cable, carried on the lean, cut loose at the top of the turn — and set into a line of hip-high stacks along what was the summer path. Bedded. Keyed. Chosen for fit.

The line does not wander. It runs uphill along the drowned pass road, in the direction of the saddle, in the direction of the notch, in the direction of the note. The people who built the first cairns built them to keep a path findable in fog. The tenant cannot know this. It has never seen fog above water, never seen a traveller, has no concept of outside and no organ with which to acquire one. It found stacks along a road and it has been continuing them for ten thousand years because continuing them is evidently what one does.

And out on the level white, at thirty metres, where the road disappears under the slide, there is a bed of silt that has been reconsidered for eleven years — grown out, drawn back, grown out a little to the left. The power stayed for the end of the sentence. This is the one advantage it had over every diver who ever hung there with a slate and a light: it could afford the end of the sentence.

The sentence was a masonry problem. The next cairn falls due on loose talus, where nothing hip-high will stand, and the tenant has been trying for eleven years to work out how to build on ground that will not hold, so that the road can be marked past the slide, for the use of no one, forever.

---

**The mercies, and why not.**

The power went through them the way you go through a house you are not going to buy.

*Light.* Two hundred watts in the deep basin and the meadows would come up like a struck match, and the black leaves — which reflect nothing because at this light a plant cannot afford to reflect anything — would burn, and the lamplight of the whole valley, which is a transaction between a holdfast and a felt, would be a transaction no longer, and the felt would be a beggar in its own field. Declined.

*Warmth.* Three and a half degrees at the floor, always: the sills stand because of a difference of a fraction of a degree and a pinch of old evaporite salt. Warm this water by one degree and the false floor un-floors, the flocculent snow of centuries goes down into the dark at once, the forty weirs are forty rings of stone around nothing, and the tenant loses in a season the compost it has been gathering since the road was dry. A gift like that is a flood. Declined.

*Company.* Something to talk to. But the only thing in the world that could talk to it at its own tempo would have to be as large as it is, and there is room in the Held Water for exactly one such being, and to install a second is not to end a solitude but to start a war whose first battle would be fought over the west wall vents and whose last would be fought nowhere, because it would never end. Declined.

*Animals.* One small grazer, one snail, one blind shrimp. The sediment is a clean book at every depth; nothing has ever eaten anything here that was still alive. Put a mouth in the basin and within a thousand years there is grazing, and pruning, and boring, and tracks, and bones in the core, and the groves stop growing until they fail structurally and start growing to defend themselves instead, and the fast at the threshold becomes meaningless because a mouse will eat the bowl. It would have been the richest gift and the most complete destruction. Declined, and the declining was not easy, because the power liked mice.

*Speed.* To quicken it, so that its eleven-year sentence takes an afternoon and it can be spoken with. But it is not slow. Forty years to eat a fallen stem is not a weakness of appetite, it is the only known solution to the problem of living forever in a basin with one crop. Quicken the tenant and you have not freed a mind, you have merely arranged for the valley to be stripped by something that used to be careful. Declined.

*Escape.* Over the lip, out through the notch, down the falling sheet into rivers and lakes and the whole waiting world. It has been reaching for the notch since the notch existed, and the reaching is always dying there, killed by turbulence and air and light, and always regrown. The power looked closely at that reaching and found that it was not an attempt on the outside. There is no outside in it. It reaches for the notch because that is where the note comes from, the way a root goes to water. To carry it over the lip would be to answer a question it has not asked with an ending it cannot survive. Declined.

*Death.* Because a thing with no descendants and no deaths, uniform across two thousand hectares, unchanged since the water came up, might reasonably be understood as trapped, and there is a kind of benevolence that arrives with an exit. The power considered this most carefully of all, and then went and sat in the smallest of the thirty-one rooms, where a settling leaf can be heard, and listened to a being that spends its centuries lifting stones uphill along a road for no one, and trimming an edge around three cold clean rooms it will not enter, and concluded that it was in the presence of something that was not suffering. Something occupied. Declined, and the file kept open, as such files must be.

*Speech, given by force.* It could have made itself known in a night: shaken the sills, spoken through the whole rim, written across the basin in a language installed rather than learned. And a being whose every thought is the direction of its own growth would have grown, from that night onward, in the shape of the shock. Its whole subsequent biology would have been a scar.
