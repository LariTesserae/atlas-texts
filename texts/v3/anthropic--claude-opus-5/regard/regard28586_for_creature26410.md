## The Attention

It came the way everything comes here: from outside, slowly, and without altering the average height of the land.

It came looking for the one who lives here, which took some time, because the phrase does not fit. It settled the question the way the ground settles: by subsidence, downward, into what was actually there. The one who lives here is a number. Forty-six thousand nine hundred and eleven singers on the first morning, plus their mites, plus the pale beetles, plus a population of thumbnail frogs that has not been below eleven hundred or above sixteen hundred in the whole span of the enquiry, plus bats, plus snails, plus two kinds of rail, plus a wall travelling outward at a fingernail a year. Nobody to address. It noted this and did not yet know it was the important finding.

Say what the power is by describing its habit, because the habit is the whole of it. Presented with anything, it looks for the site where the thing is being prevented from being itself, and it removes the prevention. That is the method. It is very old at it. In all its working life it had not met a place with no such site, and so it went round the dome eleven times before it would accept the reading, and then it went down into the root-raft, all eleven metres of dead self, and read that too, and came back up through the laminate slot by slot, and stood at last in the middle of the lit volume, which is to say it became the only object in the room, which it minded.

## What It Weighed

**Water.** It could give the pan a hundred days of rain a year. Followed out: humid air has no gradient for a fog-comb to work against; the crown's transpiration falls; the chimney stops; the room fills and stands; the condensate film goes over from a wet skin to standing wet, and the frogs plague, and the slots sour, and — the decisive clause — an air quick enough to grow grass is an air quick enough to rot laminae, which this air is not. Rot, then competitors, then a forest, then the dome out-shaded in nine hundred years and its horn columns going down into a loam that never existed here before. The gift of water is the demolition of the only thing that ever solved this place.
