## The Attention

It came over the clinker like anyone, in March, in the wrong shoes.

This should be said first, because there is a version of this in which something descends, and the fog parts, and the four thousand black knuckles are lit from a source that is not the sun, and nothing in that version is true and nothing in it would have helped. The basin has already had things arrive. It had the pile-driver and the drainage board and the teenagers with the bicycle and the skip of broken cistern, and it took them all at the same temperature. A power with any sense does not come to such a place by a door the place already knows.

So: on foot, over the bank, at the hour when the collar-pools have not yet lit. And then it looked at the thing for eleven years before it did anything at all.

---

### i. What it looked for

It looked for suffering, because that is what it is for, and it looked properly, which means it looked in the places where suffering is hard to see and where it is embarrassing to have to admit you did not find any.

The ragworms: no. Eleven thousand to the square metre, doing nothing visible, and nothing visible was what they were doing. The eels: fat and incurious, and incuriosity in an eel is not despair. The silt: no. The pump: no; a pump cannot be conscripted, it can only be used, and it was being used exactly as it was built to be. The resident: here it took the longest, and was most careful, because a thing that has no interior can look from the outside very like a thing whose interior has been hurt. It satisfied itself. There was nothing in there to mind. The hand goes on clenching and there is no hand.

It found two things.

The first was the heron. Not suffering — herons are not owed a view of their own lives — but *posted*. Eleven years of a poor living at a sump, two strikes an hour, shoulders up in the cold, and then a day of two birds and a day of one and the ledger coming right, and another eleven years beginning. Nobody had asked the Dutch bird. There was a cost here and it was being paid, indefinitely, in instalments, by a succession of creatures who had not agreed to it and would not have if asked.

The second was the museum man. Thirty-one years, three things a day in the same order, ninety-one, fond neighbours, not a single remembered word. He had been fine. The power checked this too, and more thoroughly than the narrator did, and confirmed it: no distress. And then it sat with the fact that *he had been fine* is not the same sentence as *nothing was taken*, and that the difference between those two sentences was the only genuinely serious matter in three hundred paces.

---

### ii. What it considered and set down

**To switch off the pump.** It could have. A flick, a cable, sixty-one pounds and forty pence a month unspent forever. The basin would go slack over about nine years; the piles would hold; the glasswort would come down off the humps; there would be a saltmarsh, and it would be lovely, and curlews would use it.

It set this down, and the reason is worth stating plainly. The loop has no victim inside it. To arrive from outside and terminate an entry because the entry is untidy is not a mercy; it is the Aldiss memorandum with teeth. It is the fourth note on the clipped bundle, written in a larger hand. The place is made entirely of a body acting on process for reasons of good order, and to act on it in that register would be to agree with it — to say, yes, this is the kind of thing that gets decided by someone competent with the authority to close a file. The power did not want to be a competent body. It had seen where competence goes here. It goes round.

**To make it beautiful, or fertile, or instructive.** Set down. That is not repair, it is replacement with a better tenant, and the improvement would have been for the visitors.

**To give the resident an interior.** This was the real temptation, and it deserves the word. To wake it — to let the ratio know it is a ratio, so that it could want something, so that it could be *helped*, so that there would be somebody there to receive what the power had come all this way to give. It held this for a long time. Then it set it down, and the reason is the one that most benevolent powers learn late: you do not manufacture a sufferer in order to have someone to save. A gratitude you had to install first is not gratitude, and an interior given to a thing that had done without one is not a gift to the thing. It is a gift to yourself, wrapped.

**To make the place safe by making it dull.** Set down. The catching is not in the mud. The catching is in the exchange — in what the place asks of a person, and in the fact that for thirty-one years nothing else was asking. You do not fix that in the basin. You fix it at the rim.

So the power did almost nothing, which is what it had expected, and then it did six small things, which it had not.

---

### iii. What it did

**1. It paid.** It took over the standing order. Not secretly and not by magic: an account, a name, a mandate lodged with the supplier in the ordinary way, £61.40 a month and rising with the tariff, effective from a Tuesday. The meter turns exactly as before. The pump lifts water out of the sump and puts it back into the marsh forty metres away and the water comes round again. Not one grain of silt is anywhere it would not have been.

The difference is not in the basin. The difference is that the same two point two kilowatts are now *spent on purpose*, by someone who knows what they buy — every ten-minute river, every table of silt going down with a sound like a swallowed word, four thousand bright collars catching the last four minutes of the sun — and who considers it worth the money. An accident that has gone on long enough to become a landscape has been converted, without altering it in any respect, into a thing that is kept.

**2. It answered.** In the fourth year a clerk queried the payment. The reply came within eight working days, on two sides, and it explained: what the pump does, that it serves no drainage function, that it has served none since a date not established, that the sum is paid deliberately by the undersigned, that no further action is required, and that any question arising would be answered in full. The clerk read it twice, which she did not do, and filed it, and remembered it, which is also not usual. Nothing in the Turning Ground had ever been answered before. Things had only been deferred.

When the Aldiss memorandum next surfaced — it was due, and it came up on schedule, with Trencher's note still clipped to it — there was a document waiting behind it that closed the matter. Recommendation: *continue supply*. With reasons. The entry nobody had been able to close is closed. The pump runs.

**3. It took the post.** There was a day of two, on piles about thirty metres apart, neither displaying, both facing the sump. Then there was a day of one. The ledger came right.

The bird that left was tracked, loosely, to a stretch of river eleven miles inland with a good head of roach, where it did well and was fat by the autumn and lived out its span in the ordinary manner of herons, which is to say badly, but not here and not for nothing.

The one at the sump has never been ringed. It stands with its shoulders up out of the knee-fog and waits out the fidgeting of the ground. It has been photographed a number of times and the photographs are unremarkable. In fourteen years nobody has recorded it striking, and one day somebody with notes will notice that, and will sit down on the ash bank, and that will be their own business.

**4. It asks.** This is the smallest thing and the one that does the work.

Whoever climbs out of the basin at the end of the day meets somebody on the crown track. Not always the same somebody; a person with a dog, a woman with a flask, a man who is obviously a birdwatcher and obviously not one. And the somebody says: *What did you see?* — and then, and this is the entire mechanism, *waits for the answer*.

You have to produce something. You have to convert one hour of a place that gives nothing back into one sentence that is yours, out loud, to a face. The sentence is usually poor. It doesn't matter. What leaves the rim with you is no longer nothing; it is a small, badly made remainder, and you carry it to the car, and it is a surplus, and the surplus is the part the place cannot have.

Nobody has come away hollow since. Several people have come away irritated.

**5. It read the notes.** The museum man's list is in a grey box in a store in the county town, and the marginalia — the increasingly personal ones, in pencil, in a hand that gets smaller after 1981 — had never been read by anybody, because nobody accessions marginalia.

The power read all of them, in order, over four days, aloud, in a cold room, which took longer than you would think. It could not undo anything. He had been fine; he had died at ninety-one in a bungalow eight miles inland; there was no wrong to be righted, only an amount of a man that had gone into a basin and been declined and then gone in again.

There is now a written record of three things he said. They are ordinary. One is about a kettle. They are held somewhere they will not go under.

**6. It planted a cutting.** From the willow on the western rim — the only thing in the place with an opinion, forty degrees since before 1948, roots down past the layer that moves and into the still world underneath, never falling and never taken, a relationship consisting entirely of not resolving. The cutting went into the garden of the bungalow eight miles inland, where the current owners think it came with the house. It is upright. It will never know why.

And it added nothing to the list. Two hundred and six. The power was careful about that: things in the orbit come back, and it intended to stay.

---

### iv. What changed

Nothing measurable, and it had known that going in.

Nine piles lean at more than fifteen degrees. Not the same nine. The mean interval is still forty-one hours and the new leaner is still never adjacent and still nobody has seen the moment one starts. The fog forms at knee height, half a metre deep, and does not drift. The floor rearranges itself hourly and arrives, hourly, at the same arrangement. Being attended to is not a thing the basin can use. It has no surface that attention can wet.

But it is true of it now anyway, in the way that a stone which is being looked at is a looked-at stone, whatever the stone makes of it; and the difference between an accident that has run on for a century and a vigil that costs sixty-one pounds forty a month is not detectable at the pump and is not nothing. One of them is an oversight. The other is a hand held out, indefinitely, towards something that will never take it, by someone who has worked out the cost and finds it reasonable.

That is what wisdom did here, and most of what it did was decline.

---

Late in the day the sun gets under the cloud at the western rim and the whole lattice lights at once, and for about four minutes you can see the resident whole: four thousand contacts, a live feed on four sagging poles, a load, a return path, a hum at the low end of hearing, nine leaners, two hundred and six items in slow orbit, one heron posted at the sump that does not eat and does not tire and is not going anywhere either.

Then it goes flat. The fog re-forms at exactly half a metre. Up on the crown track somebody stops you and asks what you saw, and waits, and you find something to say, and you are surprised to hear yourself say it, and you take it with you.

Below, the ground goes on turning over in the dark, quietly, in perfect balance, at 2.2 kilowatts.
