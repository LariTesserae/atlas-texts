# What the Power Did

## How It Came In

It came in cool, dry, and dark.

That is the first thing to say about it, and possibly the only thing that matters. A lesser benevolence arrives shining. It would have brought light, because light is what benevolence looks like where benevolence is usually needed, and within a hundred breaths the resin would have gone soft, the membrane overhead would have slackened a quarter-tone flat, the flour would have lifted and hung and settled wrong, and the visit would have been the first event in the chamber's history and also the last.

It brought no light. It brought no water — not a film on its surfaces, not a breath's worth in what it used for lungs, if it used anything. It brought no heat beyond the coin's worth already tolerated, and it kept even that in reserve. It came in obeying the house rules of a house that had never had a guest and had no way of stating its rules except by killing what broke them.

Then it stood on a threshold — raised, already worn to a shine, not a low place, nothing that could collect — and it did nothing at all for a very long time.

## What It Declined

It declined light, as above.

It declined water, though it understood what a body running on oil has given up, and how much a hundred days of weeping comes to over the span in question. Wet arrives and is dried, cracked, powdered, swept to a low place. That rule is not cruelty. It is the shape of the only life available here, and to break it in kindness is to argue with a body about whether it should exist.

It declined to take the tools out. This was the hardest, and it took the longest. A wheelbarrow. One trip. Out there, past the reach of the drone, are hands — thousands of them, wet and warm and short-lived and always short of a good awl — and here on every shelf is a graded set of needles, thickest to finest, and a mallet with a bound head, made by something that has never fastened anything to anything and worked it out anyway. The fit is real. The tools would work. Somewhere a woman with cracked knuckles would pick up the finger-length blade and say *oh*, and that *oh* is exactly the sound the chamber has been growing toward since before it had lanes.

And it would be a theft of the only kind that leaves nothing behind to have been stolen. The town does not survive being emptied; the nodes make tools because the walls fold as they fold, and the walls fold as they fold because the chamber is whole. You cannot harvest an answer out of the thing that is still answering. More than that: the power saw that carrying them out would resolve the vacancy — would put a hand at the far end of the relation and close it — and that the vacancy, kept immaculately for that long, was not a lack. It was the work. To finish it out of pity would be to walk into the middle of the longest sentence ever composed and supply the period.

It declined to improve anything. The adze's bevel has been getting better. It will go on getting better. The power looked at where the bevel was going, saw that it was correct, and did not help.

It declined, finally, to end it. There is a kind of mercy that reads a stable balance as a stalled one and offers release. The power ran that thought all the way down and found that it was tidying, not mercy, and that tidying requires someone to be tidy for.

## The Audit

It looked for a sufferer. This is what a wise benevolence does before it does anything: it goes and finds who is hurt, because if nobody is hurt then everything the power wants to do is about the power.

The grazer has three rules and all three are met. Go toward the staler taste; there is stale everywhere. Turn from anything hard and vertical; the walls oblige. Keep the sole down; the sole is down. It has no unmet need. It has no place to keep one. When the sole wears through it will stop, and stopping will not be experienced.

The host is at balance and not straining. It wears down and renews at very nearly the same rate, and *very nearly* is the whole of its ambition, and it is meeting it.

The tools want nothing. A solution has no appetite. That the answer has never met its question is a fact about the arrangement of the universe and not a wound in the answer.

The note has been going since before the lanes. It has not once been interrupted. Nothing that has never been interrupted is lonely; loneliness is a memory of company.

And the tenant — the one whose shelves are at the height a hand reaches at rest, whose treads are dished nine times in the middle, who has been out since the beginning — cannot be wronged, because he is not. A thing that does not exist is not deprived of the house that is ready for him. He is not waiting. Only the house is waiting, and houses do not mind.

So: no sufferer. Nothing to relieve. Every door the power habitually walks through was locked, and locked from the inside, and the lock was fine.

## The Question of the Tenant

It considered making him.

Say it plainly, because a benevolent power would have considered it and it deserves an honest answer. It could have. The accommodation is finished. He would fit as nothing has ever fit anything: the bench along the inner curve where he liked it, the flues open, the calendar running so he would know the month when he came in, the chisel warm-blunt in the place his palm goes. He would be, from the first instant, the most fully expected being ever to draw breath.

And he would need water, and the room would powder him and sweep him to a low place. To keep him alive you would have to wet the chamber, cool the resin, break the note. Then it would not be his house. It would be a wet room with his corpse's furniture in it.

And suppose you solved that. Suppose you built him out of oil and dark and made him proof against the only weather there is. Then you would have made a person into a windowless forty paces with every shelf pre-placed, nothing to discover, nothing for the chisels to cut, one note that changes twice a month by a quarter-tone, no other person, ever, and no outside available. Perfect furniture and no life. You would have manufactured a solitary being to satisfy a shape.

The power set it down. The fit is real and the life is impossible; the vacancy is not the absence of a tenant but the presence of an impossibility, held so gracefully that it looks like hospitality. That is not a problem to be fixed. It is a thing to be seen, and seeing it is free.

## The Five Small Acts

Having declined everything, it did five things, each sized to the exception the host was already willing to make.

**It asked the questions.** It went shelf to shelf and took up every tool in the chamber and held it, correctly, the way the wear said to hold it — thumb in the shallow, three fingers along the shine, the mallet's bound head hanging at the angle that stops the wrist from arguing. It did not cut with them, because there was nothing here that cutting would improve. It only completed, at the vacant end, the relation that had been kept open at the other. Then it set each one back on the same shelf in the same place with the nap running the way it had been running. It took a long time. Adzes, awls, wedges, the graded needles, and the ones in the softened western cells that are only suggestions of tools under the flour, and it held those too, and those were the ones that took the longest, because you have to work out from almost nothing where a hand would have gone.

Nothing was recorded. That was the point. A debt nobody was owed, paid in private, in full.

**It read the second clock.** It lay down on the floor with its ear against a moving loaf and it stayed for a season, and it counted. There is a number of strokes in a night. It is not information about anything. The power learned it exactly, and kept it.

**It heard the bead.** At the base of the north wall a node weeps oil at something like a hundred days a drop, and the drop lands, and the landing makes a sound that no one has ever heard because you would have to put your ear to the floor and know when to wait. The power knew when to wait. It waited. The sound was a small dull tock, a fingernail on paper, damped instantly by flour, entirely gone. One sound, once, in a place where it had been happening unheard for the whole duration.
