## The Attention

It did not come down through the oval.

Everything that comes down through the oval arrives dazed and is eaten within the minute — moths, a beetle, a feather. A power announcing itself that way would have been making a point, and points made in a room this small are made at someone's expense. It came in the way the spores come: on the afternoon inhalation, through the cracks, when the rock has taken the sun and the pit is drawing breath through its seams.

You can tell it was wise by what didn't happen. The geckos did not stop existing on the wall. The guards did not withdraw into the wax. The frog said his syllable at the usual interval. Nothing in the chamber registered an arrival, which is the only respectful way to arrive somewhere that has an alarm system consisting entirely of two lizards vanishing.

---

**The first thing it did was correct the question.**

It had been drawn by a report of one being living in one place. It found instead that the distinction did not hold here. The termites already know this: the colony is the animal, and the white sac in the log is an organ of it, and no one in the ridge is a person. The power simply took that arithmetic one step out. Four paces of warm wet dark, a ceiling frozen mid-rain, a raft of dying forest floor crowded inward at its living heart, two civilizations, one sole trader, one road, one film of slime that will inherit the whole thing — this was not a habitat containing residents. It was a body, three weeks into being a body, still deciding which of its organs would take.

So there was one being to attend to, and it was the room, and every question about mercy had to be asked at that scale.

---

**Then it did the thing that takes the longest, which is nothing.**

Benevolence has a professional habit: it walks through a place looking for what needs mending. The power did this thoroughly and found five candidates, and put each of them down.

*The rain.* It saw the rain — not as a probability but as a date, an hour, a particular volume of water arriving on a particular afternoon with the wind behind it. It saw the low line found, the ash lifted, the channel cut, the pool drowned, the moss torn out through the eastern crack, the raft's dry rim coming apart in sheets. It saw the ant road go in something under an hour.

It could have held that rain off. It declined, because the room has no channel for water and the rain is how a channel is made; because the moss carried east will grow in the crack it lands in; because the ash that goes will leave stone, and the raft that tears will spread, and a floor is not a thing you have, it is a thing that happens to you. To spare this room its first erosion would be to keep it, forever, at the moment before it had a floor. And because the ants will lay the road down again inside a week and will not have known they lost it. The grief about that road belongs entirely to whoever is standing in the room watching. A power that intervenes in a world to spare an observer his feelings is not being kind. It is being embarrassed.

*The ceiling.* The fig is putting a root along a crack, and in a hundred years the crack is a fault and the roof comes down in daylight and there is no chamber, only a valley with soil in it. The power could have kept the ceiling. It declined to take the side of stone against the only intention in the building.

*The wasp.* Six cells, five larvae, one district, no help. Three weeks of exposure between her and a nation. It would have cost nothing to keep the skink off her ledge. But her comb is a bet, and a bet that cannot be lost is not a bet, it is a monument, and she did not come in through that hole to build a monument. Also: the skink is the only mind in the chamber that has noticed anything. It has learned two bends of the ant road and works them at the same hour, and that small brown length of local knowledge is the most recent and least likely event in the history of this rock. You do not blind the only knower in a room to protect the only sole proprietor.

*The queen.* She was founded in a country that is now fifteen meters under basalt somewhere to the north, and she has no eyes worth the name, and her mate is beside her still, touching her, as he has since before the world was made over. The power considered giving her back the forest — considered, at least, telling her. There is no way to hand a country to something with no organ for receiving one; the gift would have gone into her chamber like a stone laid on a lung. And nothing has been taken from her that she can hold. She experienced the end of the world as a change in pressure and a wave of alarm from her daughters, and she went on laying, and she is laying now, and that is not resignation, it is her entire uninterrupted experience of being alive. The power left her alone. This is the hardest kind of respect: to withhold a consolation from someone on the grounds that they are not, in fact, bereaved.

*The frog.* This one took longest.

---

**It could have brought him a female.**

A kilometer of black glassy nothing, one careful passage on the updraft, and the thing he has asserted three or four times an hour for months would arrive.

And then there would be eggs in a hollow the depth of a cupped palm, fed by a three-day drip, already full of larvae; and a clutch of tadpoles in a pool that the coming rain will drown; and in a year, if any of it worked, two or three frogs calling in a room that eats sound, into a chamber that cannot yet carry them. To answer that call with a mate would be to answer it with a great deal of dying and one more unanswered call further down the line. The room is not ready to hold what he is asking for. It will be. It is not.

So the power did the other thing.

It heard him.

Not amplified, not carried, not relayed — the acoustics of the place are one of its properties and a power that fights the acoustics does not love the place, only the frog. The syllable still travels less than two meters. The vesicular rock still takes it. Nothing whatsoever has changed in the room.

But the sentence *it goes nowhere* is no longer true, and it is not true in a specific way: each call was received as a separate utterance. The one at three in the morning and the one twenty minutes later are two different assertions, not two instances of a habit, and they were heard as two. He does not know this. He will never know it. He is not comforted, because comfort is not among the things he is equipped for, any more than the queen is equipped for a forest.

The description of him — *he is failing at the only thing he is for* — was found, on inspection, to be an error of accounting. He is also the thing in this chamber that says something anyway, into a medium built out of holes, once every twenty minutes, for years. That is now a completed act rather than an interrupted one. The completion happened somewhere he cannot reach and does not need to.

---

**The one physical thing it did was hold still.**

It came into the room in a form the room could bite. It could have come otherwise; it chose a body with blood in it, and it ran a hand along a fresh fracture and was cut, because the rock has not yet been touched by anything that could soften it and it seemed right to be one of the things that gets opened here rather than one of the things that doesn't.

Then it stood in the middle of the bare ash and did not move, and the mosquito that has waited its whole thin generation for a mammal on the down-draft came, and settled on the wrist, and fed, and was not swatted.

That is the entire intervention. A milliliter of the outside world, delivered by the only creature in the chamber that had any use for a visitor. Two hundred eggs into the warm pool, most of which will be eaten, some of which will be crickets eventually, some of which will be frog, all of which will be, in the end, the thin layer of dead things that has to accumulate before the sterile ash becomes ground. The room's economy runs on imports it cannot solicit. This one was solicited, by a whining insect, and granted.

It added no seed. One seed a season is the room's rate, and the rate is part of what the room is; the bird's dose is the dose. The power did not compete with the bird — which, as the account correctly notes, is the closest thing this place has to a god: arbitrary, invisible to its beneficiaries, decisive in under a minute, and unaware. The power declined to improve on that except in the one particular the bird cannot manage. It did the same amount. It knew what it was doing.

---

**And it did not name the place.**

A name is a claim about continuance, and this room has no claim to make yet. But naming exists in order to do something, and the something got done: the chamber is now held in mind as a particular. Not *a lava tube skylight on a young flow* — this one, the one with the ridge of carton chimneys the height of a thumb, the amber trumpet on the west wall, the wasp on cell seven, the film of slime under the drip that nothing notices and that will outlast nearly all of it. Distinguishable from every other hole in every other flow. Known apart. Whoever eventually gives it a name will not be giving it anything it hasn't already got; they will just be catching up.

---

**What remains is an arrangement for the afternoon of the rain.**

The power will be there and will not hold the water.

It will watch the low line found and the ash lift and the channel open, and the moss go out through the eastern crack, and the pool cloud and vanish, and the raft come apart at its dry rim while its inhabitants crowd further into the wet heart, feeling exactly where the good damp is. It will watch the groove in the ash — the hair-deep line worn by a few hundred brown ants in a week, the first thing in the chamber that ever happened more than once, the only history in a room where the rock has an age and no past — fill and blur and be gone.

The ants will not know. That is the whole reason the arrangement is necessary. A place with no memory needs its memory kept somewhere, or the sentence *this was here* stops being true, and it was true, and truth of that kind should not be allowed to depend on the survival of a groove in ash.

So the road will be held. Not restored — held. And when they lay it down again in a week, without any sense of loss, along more or less the same line for more or less the same reasons, that will be the second time, and someone will be counting.

---

It went through the room looking for the thing that needed mending and did not find it. It found founders, almost all of whom will fail, building against a rain they cannot know about, out of no courage at all, because building is what they do with a day and they have been given days, and the days have been warm and wet and quiet and full of crickets. It looked at that for a long while and had nothing to correct.

A bead of three-day-old rain lets go of the ceiling and clicks once against fresh stone. The sound goes nowhere, as it always has, and is not lost, which is a different thing.

In the moss by the pool, the frog inflates a throat the size of a peppercorn and says his one syllable, quietly, into four paces of warm dark that will not carry it.

It arrives.
