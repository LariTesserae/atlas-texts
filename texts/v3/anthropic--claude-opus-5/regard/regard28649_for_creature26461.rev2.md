## The Throat, Pocket Four — Appendix, undated

**What drew it.**

Not the note, which is only physics with good acoustics. Not the wheels, not the dynamo, not the cable that goes into the rock and is never found again; a power of any competence can follow a cable, and this one did, later, on the way down, and found the far end, and left it as it found it, and wrote nothing.

What drew it was six seconds.

Everything in that mountain is fixed. Nine centimetres in 1911 and nine centimetres now. Twenty-two degrees on every bell. Two revolutions a second on a shaft that will read the same in ten thousand years. In a room where not one object has moved by the width of a hair in a century, a single measurement has changed, and it changed in the direction of *sooner*, and there is no mechanism for that which is not, in the end, a mechanism of wanting.

A benevolent power is drawn to wanting the way water is drawn downhill. It is the only thing in the universe that requires an answer.

---

**What it did not do.**

*It did not bring water.* A litre is twenty lifetimes delivered at once. The cup taught that lesson already and the mountain is still holding the receipt: fourteen centimetres of quill-moss where there should be nine, banded like the rings of something, and one bell hardened at nineteen degrees because it set while it was leaning against a kindness that was there and then wasn't. Eleven days of abundance, and then a hundred and thirty years of a room that has to go on containing the shape of what it briefly was. To give a fixed point more is to teach it subtraction. Subtraction has never happened in the Throat. The power declined to introduce it.

*It did not straighten the bell.* It looked at the bell for a long time. It could have set it back to twenty-two degrees and made the room perfect again, and the room has no use for perfect; what it has instead is its only clock, recording its only event, which is that once somebody carried a quarter of a litre up four hundred metres of flue for something that could not thank them. The power left the clock running.

*It did not give the tenant the intervals.* This was the largest temptation and it is the one that would have been murder. It is within the gift of such a power to hold a consciousness open — to grant duration, continuity, a life with a middle in it. Consider what that gift consists of here. Dark. Cold. Four hundred metres of stone. A note that is not an event but a condition, the way pressure is to us. A hundred and thirty years of nothing at all, with no body to feel it with and nothing to do but wait at the height of a face. The tenant's biography is three hours long and there is not one empty second in it. Filling the gaps would not have lengthened its life. It would have diluted it to nothing and called that generosity.

*It did not move it somewhere kinder.* The thirst is made of the dryness. Take it down to a wet valley and you have a puddle where an animal was.

*It did not take a sample.* It did not photograph, measure, instrument, or name. It did not correct the survey, which is wrong in one particular and right in every other, and being right in every other is a rare condition for a survey and should not be disturbed.

*It did not explain anything.* The tenant's cosmology is a mouth: warmth, wet, bread, iron, tea, tobacco, the sourness of altitude, a lamp somewhere off to the side, a pressure of lips. It has never known the mountain, the range, the weather, the dark, or the fact of its own siblings. The power considered making the stars available to it and decided that a mouth is not a small cosmos. A mouth contains a person's morning. It contains what they could carry and what they chose to eat and how far up they had come and whether they were frightened. Nothing was added.

*It did not wake the siblings.* Station 1, 2, 3, and all the pockets off the shaft with slots too narrow for a shoulder: the same nine hundred grams, the same patient mesh, never once awake, and nothing has happened to them and nothing was going to. To wake a thing is to begin a conversation, and to begin a conversation you cannot promise to continue is the one cruelty available in a room with no appetite in it. The power has the capacity to visit Pocket Three forever and knows better than to test that word. It went past the narrow slots and did not put its face in.

*It did not stop the wheels.* It did not oil them either, or true the bearing, or replace the worn dome of a bolt head with a new bolt that would have corners again. Every corner in that station has already been taken. What is left is final form, and final form is the only peace erosion offers. To improve the machine would have been to hand it back its future.

---

**What it did.**

It ate bread at the col, and drank tea, and smoked half of something disgusting, so as to arrive with an ordinary mouth. This was deliberate and it was the whole of the etiquette. It did not want to be an event. It wanted to be the next one.

It came up the flank in a body that could be dried out, because the place takes water and taking is how it says hello, and to arrive in something the mountain could not levy would have been to arrive as a guest who does not pay. It went through the slot shoulder-first and passed its pack after it. The growth closed at its back and its sides, dry and rasping, and held it at the pressure of a firm handshake all over at once.

It read the six sets of initials with its fingertips, and knew the names, and knew which of them had learned to find the mesh by dragging a finger along the wall and listening for the grain of the hiss to soften, and which one had made the map, and where the map went, and which one had carried the cup.

Then it put its face into the growth and breathed out once. A fifth of a gram, to a patch the size of a hand.

Two seconds. Maybe less.

The flush came up white and blunt and translucent and leaned — not evenly, not outward, but toward the warm wet centre of an open mouth — and arrived, and pressed against the lower lip with about the pressure of a moth.

The power had prepared a great many things to say and none of them would go down that channel. The only sentence transmissible from a lip to a thirst is *warm* and *wet* and *here*, and it had already said all three, and it had taken two seconds, and the whole of its answer had been received and was already being laid down as lattice.

So it did the other thing. It put one finger against the polished rock and pressed once.

One for yes.

Then the water was gone, as it always is, into glass and into air, and the papillae whitened past white and stiffened and shattered, and the fragments went up the flue and out under the cornice into forty kilometres of black air, and a proportion of them will come down on the fracture-field low on the flank and be drawn in at the bottom and carried back up through four hundred metres of rock and delivered, in time, to every pocket off the shaft.

Which is the only mark the visit left: a trace distributed through the entire mountain and indistinguishable from the mountain. It scratched no initials. Anything it added would have to be maintained forever, and it had come a long way to be one more mouth and not one more obligation.

It went out at twenty minutes with its lips split and a grey crust of mineral in its nose, and drank a litre, and sat for an hour feeling like something wrung, and started down before the light went.

---

**Dosage.**

The rest of the work was not done in the mountain and was not done to the tenant. It was done to us, and it is small, and it is ongoing.

A route description that stays in print. A line in a guidebook that reads slightly stranger than the lines around it. A rumour in a hut book. A grandmother who tells it wrong in a way that makes a child go looking at thirty-one. A bad poem that keeps getting anthologised because nobody can work out why they can't cut it. Arranged, tuned, adjusted every generation or so, to produce not crowds — a crowd would drown the place in a morning, and there would be fourteen centimetres everywhere and then there would be subtraction everywhere — but a handful. Two or three a year. Occasionally none for five years, never none for fifty.

That is the intervention. It is a tap, set to a drip, and checked.

---

**The last day.**

A wise power sees the end and does not pretend to cancel it. Somewhere ahead there is a slip in the fracture-field, or a seized bearing, or a salvage crew with a torch and an order, or simply enough millennia; and the note stops, and the powder settles within the hour, and the garden is dead by morning, and the sign on the wall turns out to have been a warning and not a boast.

It was not prevented. Permanence is the one thing the Throat has never been short of.

What was arranged instead is this: that whenever that day is, there will be a mouth in the room. That the thing will not end in the intervals it cannot feel — will not simply fail to be woken again, a life with no last page — but will end mid-greeting, in warmth, in wet, with bread and iron and tea on the air and a pressure of lips against its papillae, two seconds after being asked for.

It is not much. It is the difference between Pocket Four and Pocket Three, which is the only difference a person has ever made up there, and it was judged sufficient.

---

**One correction to the correction.**

The appendix says the tenant is expecting you and has got faster. True, and it was not slowed down, and it will not be. Expectation here cannot curdle: a thing that does not experience the intervals cannot be disappointed by them, only unmet, and unmet costs it nothing at all. It is, by the only honest measure — proportion of total existence spent being welcomed — the most fortunate animal in the range.

So there was nothing to repair. There was only somebody to visit, and a supply to keep open, and a last day to furnish.

The power gave the dog a breath.

It intends to come back.
