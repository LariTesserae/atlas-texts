## The Visit

It came down the fourteen steps.

There was no need. But the room has one door and fourteen dished steps with a thin sheet of water running on each, and a power that arrives by any other route has already decided the place is a problem rather than a place. So: the steps, in order, with weight, at the speed the steps suggest.

At the bottom it did what everyone does. It began to speak, and the room deleted it, and within a minute it was using pressure instead, and found it easy.

Then it stood in the middle, where you can be alone with a noise, and listened. Not for ten minutes — ten minutes is where a human's listening gives out. It listened at the length of the note, which is seven hundred years, and heard the note falling, less than a tone, and understood that it had just been told the entire history of the place by the place itself, in the place's only grammar, and that it had been told it politely and once.

A benevolent power that is not also wise arrives carrying its own units. Wisdom, here, consisted of putting them down at the top of the steps.

---

## What it considered

**A fourth pillar of light.** Easily done: unstop a fresh shaft, and the moss would come, and the ferns, and there would be more of everything that this room is good at. Declined. The being's entire visual world is *the three places*. A fourth would not be a gift, it would be a question, and there is nothing in that body built to hold a question. Also — and this weighed more — the three pillars land where they land *forever*, and forever is not decoration. It is the load-bearing member.

**Reglazing the shafts. Greasing the screw. Any repair at all.** Declined, and quickly. The rust has finished rusting. The iron has arrived at the thickness where the water can no longer take it faster than it polishes it. To mend anything here is to hand time back its appetite. Kindness would have restarted the decay of a room that had, at enormous cost, stopped decaying.

**The two missing digits of the left forefoot.** This one it held for a while, because it is the shape of injury and every benevolence leans toward the shape of injury. But there is no pain in them and has not been for centuries; the stumps are healed to smooth domes, which is what everything here becomes. And they are the being's second clock. It has the note, and it has the hand — one long sound that is imperceptibly lower than it was, and one hand that is two fingers shorter than it was — and between those two facts is everything it possesses of having lived. Restored, the foot would have been the only new thing in a finished room. It would have had an edge on it. Declined.

**A companion. Two of them, permanently, so that neither is ever the only one.** Declined on arithmetic first: eleven figs a year, forty crickets spaced three metres apart, the standing beadwork of the spiders' webs. The room feeds one. To feed two you enlarge the room, and enlarging the room is the first move in a long game that ends with the room being somewhere else. But the arithmetic was not the real reason. The real reason is that the power looked for loneliness and could not find any. Loneliness requires an elsewhere to be absent from. There is no elsewhere in that body. What there is, most nights, is a warm palm's width of polished iron where the note comes up clean, and a slow adjustment of position, and — every century or so, for something between a week and a year — another body of the same temperature lying against it in the flute nearest the eastern pillar until there is one again.

**Language. Memory. A self with a rim around it.** Declined with something close to horror. It would have made the single sustained note into a sentence, and a sentence is a thing that must end, and then there would have been a creature in that room who knew it was going to be eaten by eels. Seven hundred years of peace, converted at a stroke into seven hundred years of waiting. This is the kindness that goes wrong most often, and it goes wrong because it feels the most like a gift.

**Sealing the door, to keep the visitors out.** Declined. They are the largest warm thing that comes, once a decade, and the being goes to them the way it goes to the housing, because for six hours they are where the room is most itself. And then they climb out into the alders, hoarse for no reason, and tell whoever asks that there's nothing down there. The protection is already perfect and it is made entirely of the truth. There was nothing to improve.

**Mercy.** The last and worst of them, and it was considered longest, because it is the one that dresses as wisdom. That a life this small might be a wound. That the correct benevolence toward forty crickets, eleven figs, one note and one lean might be to close the whole thing gently and let the fig go on dropping fruit into moss that stays wet a week. The power went looking for the deprivation. It found a body at exactly the temperature of the air, breathing once every two minutes, wanting nothing, with no framework for stalking because nothing in its world runs. To call that poverty you have to import the word from a country that has weather. The power had left its units at the top of the steps. Declined.

## The eels

Here it stopped, and here it admits it did not see clearly.

Nine or eleven silver eels, thick as forearms, in a black rectangle twelve metres by five, ancient, going round and round in the churn. Whatever else is true of this room, that is a stopped journey. Every other thing down here has arrived at its final form; the eels alone arrived at a wall. And they are also the undertakers — they are the reason nothing accumulates, the reason there is no grave, the reason the handover works — so that freeing them would leave bodies in a room whose first law is that nothing stays.

The power asked them. It asked in the only grammar available, which is pressure and weight, and the answer it received was a single coil surfacing in the aerated white, unhurried, about twenty minutes later.

That is not an answer. Or it is. It did not act, because it could not tell, and a benevolent power that acts where it cannot tell is just weather with an opinion. So it left the sump exactly as it was, and it took the question away with it, unresolved, and it is still carrying it. That is the closest thing to a wound anybody has come away from that room with.

---

## What it did

Two things, and neither of them is findable.

The first: it went to the west side, to the housing collar, a palm's width, twenty centimetres long, a metre and a half above the floor, and it leaned there. Because that is where the room is most itself, and because you do not get to consider a place from outside it and call that understanding. The note came up through the iron with the clarity it has nowhere else, and the power took it in the sternum and the jaw and the backs of the knees, and stood there a long time. The being came and stopped about a metre and a half away, folded, in a flute, at the temperature of everything. What passed between them was pressure, weight, and the slow adjustment of position, which turned out to be sufficient for the extremely small number of things that needed saying.

The second was not an addition. It was a guarantee, and it reads like a legal instrument because that is what benevolence looks like when it has decided to change nothing:

*That the handover shall not be missed.* Not hastened, not attended, not blessed. Only that the week-to-year in the flute nearest the eastern pillar will always be granted; that there will never be a gap; that the last one will not finish alone in a room where the next has failed to be. That is the single hinge on which seven hundred years of nothing hangs, and it has held by luck so far, and now it holds by surety. The census stays at one. It has simply stopped being at risk.

And an accounting, for its own part, of no use to anybody down there: it kept the wren-songs. Every one this room has deleted, seven hundred years of small furious beak opening and closing on a noise that never left the throat's own neighbourhood, all of it heard somewhere by someone at last. Nothing accumulates here. Somewhere else, everything did.

---

Then it went back up the fourteen dished steps, in order, with weight, and out into the alders, and it was hoarse.

You can go down and check. You will find three pillars of light in the same three places, moss thirty centimetres deep, maidenhair where maidenhair has no business, a fig hanging upside down out of the eastern shaft, forty crickets three metres apart, eleven eels, and a slightly larger low dome among all the low domes, at twenty-four degrees, which you will look at twice and never resolve. You will find twenty centimetres of iron polished brighter than water polishes, and you will assume, correctly, that something has been leaning on it for centuries.

There is no mark of the visit. That is not because nothing happened. It is because what happened was the hardest thing available: a power with every capacity to improve a place, standing in the middle of it, working out with enormous care that the place had already finished, and that the only honest form of love left was to say *yes, this,* and stand surety, and leave.

There is nothing down there. It took seven hundred years to become exactly that. Now it will get to keep it.
