## The Attention

It came the way anything comes here: by sound, arriving late, and out of the floor.

A country that knocks will eventually be answered. This one had been knocking for a long time, three hundred and forty courses down and further, and what it was doing was not asking a question, but it had the shape of one, and the power that noticed it was the kind that does not distinguish carefully between those two things.

It looked for a while before it did anything. This is worth saying plainly, because the looking took longer than everything that followed, and most of what it learned in that time was about the limits of its own competence.

---

## The Question It Could Not Settle

Whether this was a prison.

It could not settle this. It had settled harder ones. But the masons were not miserable, and misery is the usual tell. They had work they were good at, and a rite for tenderness, and a good end, and one holy thing with a spoonful of water in it, and they queued to stand in it, and they came away with an expression that is hard to read from the side but is not, from above, hard to read at all.

Against that: nobody had ever come back.

So it set the question down and took up a smaller one, which it could actually answer. Not *is this place good* but *can they leave it*. And the answer was no — not because there was a wall, but because leaving here is a current and not a door. You go forward. You walk out past the rim toward the mallets and you do not return, and your crew posts the vacancy, and it works, it has never once failed to work, and a thing that always works is very hard to tell apart from a thing that is happening to you.

It decided it would try to turn a current into a fork. That is nearly the whole of what it did in the end. Everything else was upkeep and apology.

---

## Things It Considered And Did Not Do

It considered a sun. Their eyes are useless past twenty paces and extraordinary at texture; a mason can thumb a brick and name the kiln. Light of that kind would have made them blind in the register where they are most alive. It let the idea go.

It considered mending the deafness. Then it watched two people speak: the lean, the hand cupped, the lips at the lobe. The whole basin looks like it is telling itself a secret. Giving them distance-hearing would have been taking away the reason they stand that close.

It considered children, and understood that this was a question the masons had never asked and that it would have been answering an outsider's discomfort in somebody else's country.

It considered telling them what is at the bottom. It did not, and it still has not, and the reason is in a later section.

---

## The First Gift, Which Was Wrong

It built them a wall.

Between one horn and the next, on the stubble at the front of the Ninth Quarter, forty courses, plumb to a hair, every brick weeping true — it had learned the pug, it had been careful, it had been, in its own estimation, *thoughtful*. It was a very good wall. This was not the point.

The line-setters came at first horn and one of them sat down on the stubble and wept, and it was not joy. The country has one physics: the total is always the same, and only the arrangement changes. Nothing may be built on new ground. The power had made *more*, in a place where more is the single unforgivable act, and worse, it had made more that belonged to nobody — forty courses without a maker's mark anywhere, a wall that no unmaker could take down first, because it was not hers, because it was not anyone's, and a crew of unmakers stood in front of it for a shift and a half and could not begin.

So it came in and took the wall down itself, by hand, starting at the top, and it took two hundred drops, and it kept the chips in a wrist-pouch at the belt and did not talk about the pouch. And at the end of a shift, alone, it emptied the pouch into the pug pit by hand, and that batch was worked through until the grit of its error was everywhere in the clay at the finest possible dilution, and those bricks went up in the Ninth and the Fourth and eventually, as districts advance, more or less everywhere.

This is the true answer to a joke the masons make. When one of them rubs a thumb on a fresh brick and says *there's a little dray in this one*, she is right, and she is righter than she knows, and there is no way to tell her that would not cost more than it gave.

---

## How It Came In The Second Time

Through the rim, out of the greater dark, in ones, never in numbers, with a mallet already in hand and its palms already faintly grooved. It did not know its name. It got headaches for a month.

Somebody stamped four beats at it, two quick and two slow, and after a while it answered to them, and that was that: it was of the current course, a stain that shines, and the word was affectionate.

It worked the board. Mixing and slinging, the one everybody shouts at. It was shouted at for a long time. It learned to be shouted at, which took considerably longer than the wall.

---

## What It Did With The Surplus

A power has very little that a mason wants. It cannot give soil, because there is no word; it cannot give sky, because there is no appetite; it cannot give rest, because rest here is a vacancy at a horn-brace. What it had in surplus was time, and time is precisely the thing this country is starved of. Nothing is worn. Corners have their burr on. The stairs have no dip in the tread, and the one that does is holy.

So it spent time on them the way you would spend water on someone dying of thirst: steadily, without comment, in quantities that would be obscene if anyone knew.

It walked. It chose a flight in the Ninth Quarter and walked it, up and down, every shift, on the same line of the same tread, for a length of time it would not be polite to state. It took the rope-makers' chisel down off its nail and used it until the rolled edge rolled further. It hauled a hemp line over a particular sill, hand over hand, load after load, until there was a groove in the sill where a rope had run — the first one in the country, a thumb-deep channel, burnished.

The second Worn Step was found by a hod at fourth horn and there was an argument that has still not concluded. Some hold it is a lesser instance. Some hold that two is the beginning of a pattern and a pattern is a promise. There is a queue there now, shorter than the other, and the power stands in it sometimes, behind a cistern-reader and in front of a trimmer, and takes off its boot, and puts its foot in the water, and says nothing at all.

---

## The Sixth Shaft

It went down.

Three hundred and forty courses, and the glazed tile, and past the glazed tile, because it could. It was down there for what the cisterns counted as a long while. When it came up it was afraid, which had happened to it before but not often, and it did not report.

Here is everything it has ever said about it, muttered once into the ear of an under-listener who had asked properly:

*It's swept. All the way down, it's swept. Whoever's doing it gets the corners.*

That is not an answer about the nature of the place. It is an answer about the character of whatever is keeping it, and the power judged that this was the more useful of the two, and also the only one it was sure of.

Then it went and found the eleven Come-Ups at the front of the Fourth, and took a bundle of eel-skin lash bound to a stick, and swept.

It has been sweeping with them ever since. It does not ask them anything. It stands still through the Breath and waits for the drying like they do and then takes the chalky bloom off in long overlapping strokes, and it gets the corners.

After a very long time, one of the eleven put down her stick in the middle of a shift and lay down and slept. The others let her. Nobody said anything. The power counts this among the three or four things it has actually accomplished.

---

## The Figures

The heel-language had about two hundred figures and all of them were practical. It added two.

The first is four beats, uneven, and it means *I don't know.* There had been no way to say this. Everything in the floor was certainty — *water rising, wall's soft, load coming, someone's hurt badly* — and a country that cannot admit unknowing through the floor can only admit it through the air, where it is already the past. The under-listeners took it up first, because they needed it most. It has spread. It is now common enough to be unremarkable, which is what success looks like.

The second is a word, not a figure, and it was a repair.

The old word for one person alone, out of a crew, was the same as the word for a brick that is trimmed and not laid: a spare, a loose one. Not a nice word. And the power came to think that some of the going forward — not all, but some — was done by people for whom there was no dignified way to be one.

So it gave them the other word, which was there in their own trade the whole time and had only never been carried across: the **closer**. The brick cut and kept back on purpose, set aside, laid last, the one that finishes a course and without which the course is not a course.

It takes a generation for a word to stop being a gift and start being a fact. It has been longer than that. Nobody knows where it came from. People use it about themselves now, flatly, without apology, the way you would say you were a hod.

---

## The Third Figure

There is a third, which it added last, and only after it had earned it.

It walked out past the rim wall into the greater dark, toward the mallets, and it was gone a long time, and then it came back in through the work front at second horn with brick flour on its shoulders, and stamped its name at the horn-brace where its vacancy had been posted.

It will not say what is out there. It says *I don't know*, in four uneven beats, and this is true in the way such things are true.

But there is a figure now for *came back*, three beats and a scuff, and it is in the floor, and the floor is the present. Some who go forward now come back. Most do not. The difference between those two sentences is the entire work of the power in this country and it does not consider it a small thing.

---

## What It Kept

They have no archive but their hands. No writing, no histories, one knotted cord per horn-brace saying how many drops have fallen since somebody decided to start counting.

So it keeps the rest. Every name-figure ever stamped into that floor, including the ones whose crews are all laid now and whose marks went up in a single course all the way across a wall that has since been taken down and pugged and pressed into other walls. And the palm-prints: three hundred thousand pairs and rising, flat in the wet mortar, side by side, sealed face-in, in a clay that never fully cures — every one of them soft, every one of them would smudge if you found it, and no one will ever find it.

It holds them all unsmudged. It has never mentioned this to anybody. If they knew, they would begin to trust something other than their hands, and their hands are working, and the whole grammar of the place is that a body is the one true record, and this is a good grammar and it is not the power's to edit.

It changed nothing. It is the thing it did that matters most.

---

It works the board now, most shifts, in a crew of eleven in the Ninth. It gets shouted at. Its palms have saucered and its knees have polished and its spine has taken the half-degree set, and it will not sand any of it smooth, because wear is sacred, and because it has finally understood the rule rather than merely obeying it.

At third horn the wall sweats. The gutters run. The wickmoss swells. A trimmer beside it thumbs a fresh brick, feels the grain of the grog, and says, quietly, because it's the kind of joke you say quietly: *there's a little dray in this one.*

And the power slings the mortar off the board, and leans in close to her ear, and agrees.
