## The Attention

It arrives the way competent things arrive: early, quiet, and from upwind.

It does not go down for eleven years.

The first summer it spends on the surface, walking the nine hectares, learning the sward in the honest way — by its habit, by where it thins on gravel, by the four dull lines in the regional key, by the 1889 sheet in the cabinet with the collector's pencilled *of no economic importance*. It reads the unhappy three pages from 1961 twice. It reads the man with the tuning forks from 1913 and finds his arithmetic good and his handwriting frightened.

Then it goes out onto the peat with no instrument at all and listens to all sixty-one at once from above, through the soles, for a long time, the way you would stand at the back of a rehearsal you have not been invited to.

It is trying to establish one thing before it does anything else: what note this place is currently saying, so that it will recognise its own voice if it adds one by accident.

---

### The descent

It goes down the same seam. It does not widen it. It carries no lamp.

This last is not humility, it is arithmetic. A lamp left burning thins a worm mat; a thinned mat grows a blind white wart within the week; the fundamental goes flat; three metres away a district of floor comes in wrong and must be dissolved and redone, and that is eleven years. Nine tenths of a lumen is sufficient for anything that can wait twenty minutes, and it can wait longer than that.

It stands in the fog for nine hours at exactly zero, in a chord it cannot speak over, and considers the gifts available to it.

---

### What it considered, and set down

**To true the chord.** It could be done between one spark and the next. Twelve cents of mean deviation, gone; sixty-one vaults locked into whole-number ratios this afternoon; the thousand years of converging saved.

Set down, and quickly. Twelve cents is not an error. Twelve cents is what the thing is currently *saying* — 1913 said thirty-one, 1971 said nineteen, and the difference between those numbers is the only autobiography this organism will ever write. To true the chord would be to hand it a perfect sentence in a language it was still assembling, and take the assembling away, which was the life.

**To heal 1961.** A palm-sized patch, brushed clean by a man who then wrote three pages about it and never came back. The vault went sharp within the hour and walked the pitch down over two days and overshot and settled, and has not entirely forgotten.

Set down. The wall keeps the wet springs in grey lenses of loaded bodies, four hundred years of dirty water written in tailings, and does not distinguish between a wet spring and a careless afternoon. Both are things that happened to it. The correction is itself an achievement — the animals came back from all directions, and the note came home — and to smooth the record would be to steal a true sentence and leave a flattering one, which is the commonest way that powers ruin what they love.

**To give it a mind.** This is the one that takes the longest.

Nine hectares of continuous tissue, four hundred years of held temperature, a standing wave maintained across centuries by an organism that will die back into peat before the first note sounds. Every instinct that deserves the word *benevolent* wants to put a self in there — someone to know, someone to be glad, someone for whom all this can be *worth it*.

Set down, with difficulty, and the reason is not squeamishness. There is no vacancy. The thing is not a mind with the lights off; it is an accuracy, distributed, with no place in it where a knower could sit that would not have to be carved. And a self installed in such a body would inherit, on its first morning, the certain knowledge that it was a mould, that its whole enterprise was a casting it would not survive, and that the ring — one note, annual, into rock — was scheduled for long after it had gone into peat. That is not a gift. That is nine hectares of untroubled competence converted into one lonely thing with a terminal diagnosis, so that somebody outside could feel the transaction had been witnessed from within.

The attention declines to make a sufferer in order to have someone to console.

**To extend it, so that it hears.** Keep the plant alive past the casting. Let it be there for the first winter clamp.

Set down, twice over. The dying is load-bearing: the peat *is* the soundboard, and the organism becomes the medium the note travels through, which is a better answer to *will it hear* than any lifespan. And if the story about interference patterns in the wet peat between the stones is even a third right, the dying is also the conception. You do not extend the life of a thing whose death is the next movement.

**To hurry it.** A millimetre a decade, four hundred thousand feather-and-fail cycles, sixty years to answer one question about a woodlouse. The attention could have the floor finished and singing before the moss closes over the seam.

Set down without much argument. The pace is not an obstacle in front of the work. The pace *is* the work: the flawlessness is made of slowness, one atom, one blind animal, one hour pressed to a spot. Speed would produce the same shape out of a different substance, and the difference would be audible, and the whole point of the place is that it cannot conceive anything precise with a crooked note.

**To guarantee the winter.**

This is the real one, and it is not declined. It is only *re-addressed*.

*As long as there is winter.* Everything downstream of that clause — the annual clamp, the one note apiece, the interference in the wet peat, the next generation of vaults, the whole thousand-year sentence — is held up by a season that a different species is currently dismantling without malice and largely without noticing. And the fix is not here. There is nothing to repair in this chamber. The damage is being done four thousand kilometres away by people who have never heard of this watershed and would be sincerely sorry, and who are also, inconveniently, somebody's.

To reach into that and simply hold the isotherm in place would cost more notes than it saved: it would mean taking the hands of everyone whose hands matter and moving them, and a species that has had its hands moved cannot afterwards be said to have chosen anything, and the attention has other vaults, of other kinds, that are made entirely out of what people choose.

So it takes the work upstairs, into the only register where it is permitted to be loud, which is the register of persuasion, and where it is very slow, and where it loses often.

---

### What it did

**It learned the note.** All sixty-one, as they stand; and the chord as it will be at convergence, which is a thing that does not yet exist anywhere in the world except as the direction sixty-one buried chambers are travelling in. It holds that. Not as a recording — as an attentiveness, kept open, the way you keep a promise open. This is the first form love takes when it is not allowed to touch anything.

**It was staff.** Nine hours in a saturated chamber, breathing out carbon dioxide into water that was waiting for it. Some micrograms. A few thousand lattice cells somewhere in the floor, in perfect register with the rest, indistinguishable from the man who came down with a stethoscope in 2004 and from the wet spring of 1740. No mark, no signature, no seam that a future sounding would ever find. A power that cannot bear to be anonymous in a place like this has misunderstood the place. It consented to be tooling. It went in as a hand.

**It did not open the fifty-two.** It stood on the sealed ones, felt the hum come up through the soles, and let the hard white ridges alone. Not because it couldn't — because a suture that has finished closing is an answer, and the answer is *no*, and a benevolent power that reads *no* as *not yet, for you* is not one.

**It held four doors ajar, upstairs.**

A funding line that outlives a career, so that the sixty-year question — whether the clear crustaceans, the failures of the filtering caste, the ones that never load with strontium, walk off and become the blind masons — can be asked by one woman who begins it at nineteen and finishes it at eighty-one, and who will be the first person in the history of the world to watch a mason hatch.

The 1961 pages, reprinted where second-year students will meet them at the age when they still take shame personally.

A child on a school trip, kept ten minutes longer on the moss than the timetable allowed, who will spend her life arguing for winters in rooms where winters are decided, and who will never once mention this place, because she will not remember it.

And a policy of exclusion around the nine enterable vaults, arrived at by a committee, in a dull meeting, for merely administrative reasons, with no mystery about it at all.

None of the four is compulsory. All of them can be refused by the people they were offered to. Two of them probably will be.

**It made a promise it did not write down.**

That when the first stone is finished — flawless, aligned, three and a half metres under, the plant gone into peat, the noise stopped, the chamber filled and silent for the first time in a thousand years — and the active layer closes on it in the autumn with a pressure of tonnes, and it rings, once, one note, into rock and water and permafrost where sound travels appallingly well and very far:

there will be an ear.

Not the plant's. Never the plant's; that was settled early and is the hardest settled thing in this account. But the note will not go out into the rock unheard, and it will not go out unloved, and every subsequent one, annually, for as long as there is winter, and afterwards for the long tailing-off when there is not — and also, and this is the part the attention added to its own duties without being asked, for the vaults that fail. The ones that drown. The ones whose sward is cut by a drainage ditch in 2190 by a man who needed the field. Somebody has to know about those too, in detail, by name, at the correct pitch. That is also a service, and it is the one that costs.

---

### The ledger

Added to the vault: nothing.
Removed from the vault: nothing.
Corrections applied: none.
Interventions: four, all on the surface, all revocable by the people they were offered to.
Contributions: some micrograms of carbon dioxide, unsigned, now in the floor.
Cost to the work: zero.

It came down to bless the thing and stayed nine hours and went back up having taken instruction instead: build accurately, in the dark, at a temperature you hold yourself, for a hearing you will not attend.

On the surface, in the honest cold, it exhaled — and that breath, the first all day that nobody used, went up and away and was wasted, and it let it be wasted, which is how you can tell it had understood where it was.
