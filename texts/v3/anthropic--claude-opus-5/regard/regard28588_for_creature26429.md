## The Visitor

### What it came as

It could have come as weather. It could have come as a change in the pressure, an idea arriving in the head of a passing animal, a warmth under the seep. It came down the throat-root on hands and knees, forty degrees off vertical, and slid where the hairs made sliding easy, and skinned both palms at the fourth rib.

This was deliberate. The shaft has a preference, expressed over a very long time, and the first courtesy available to a guest is to be preferred against. It arrived dirty, with its hair full of fine hairs, breathing hard, at the bottom of something built to make leaving difficult, and it did not exempt itself from that either. It could have left at any moment. It observed the difficulty anyway, the way you observe a fast.

It brought no lamp.

That is the second thing, and it is not modesty. A bright lamp stops everything within sixty metres — total silence, holding, nothing attacking, nothing fleeing, ten thousand small lives suspended in the patience of a system that has outlasted worse. To bring light into the Press Chamber is to make every resident wait until you are finished. The visitor sat at the bottom of the root in the dark for a hundred and eleven strokes, which is a little over an hour, until its eyes had become the kind of eyes the chamber uses, and then it went in on the paths, in the grey-green pulse, as one more thing that could see barely and be seen barely.

### Substrate

*Stillness is consent.* Anything that stops moving for more than ten minutes is understood to be substrate and will be processed.

The visitor stopped moving for eleven minutes.

It chose the killing floor for this, the warm empty middle where the harvestmen patrol, because a rule tested in a safe place has not been tested. Seven minutes to the first arrival. It was tasted at the wrist and behind the ear. Sanitation worms came for the blood on its palms and got it. Some hair went into a nursery two plates up and is presumably still there, woven into a seam, holding out condensation. A harvestman came at nine minutes, stood over it for four strokes, took a small amount from the soft place under the jaw, and left.

At eleven minutes it got up, one minute past the limit, so that the record would show it had understood.

This is the part that visitors get wrong even when they get everything else right. They arrive un-eatable. They arrive as stone, and there is no stone anywhere in this chamber, and a thing that cannot be eaten cannot be addressed. The visitor made itself edible in a small way on purpose, and afterward it was not an anomaly. It was a low-yield feature of the environment, like the cold end, and it was left alone in the specific way that useful things are left alone.

### The census

Then it counted them.

Not estimated. Counted. Every mite in the resin seams of every plate, all four species. Every blind pale roach in the middle strata, three and four deep, and it learned to tell them apart, which requires attending to the wear on the pronotum and the way a given individual favours one direction at a seam.
