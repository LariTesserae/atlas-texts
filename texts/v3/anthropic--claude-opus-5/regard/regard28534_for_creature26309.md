## The Attention

Something noticed.

Call it a power, meaning it can do as it likes with matter and does not need to eat. Call it wise, meaning it has been wrong before and remembers exactly how. Call it benevolent, which is the hardest of the three to demonstrate and the easiest to claim.

It did not arrive in the sky, because there is no sky. It did not arrive as a voice. It came up the shelf from the low western rim, through the lukewarm sheet, in the shadowless nowhere-dusk, and it stood at the third riser to take the place in.

It stood for eleven minutes.

When it went to move, there was a white weld at each heel, a sixteenth of an inch thick, and it had to break itself free of the floor.

That was the first thing it was taught, and it was taught in eleven minutes, and it did not need to be taught again. The shelf makes no distinction between a god and a forgotten tool. Nothing may be left still.

## What It Had Come For

It had been drawn by a being, and it went looking for the being, and spent some time confused, because there were nineteen thousand and some hundreds of them, squatting at the rims with their heels braced, and not one of them was who it had felt.

Then it put its palm flat on a terrace lip and understood.
