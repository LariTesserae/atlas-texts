## The Appointment

### XV. What is noticed first

Not the basin. Anyone would notice the basin — eleven kilometres of section, weather coming off a line, a county of rack-and-pinion under a skin of milled gears. That is only large. Largeness draws surveyors.

What draws a power of the kind described is the accounts. It goes straight to the ledger and looks for the one entry that yields nothing, because that entry is where a thing keeps what it wants. Here there is exactly one: twenty seconds, daily, four notes down, the plant standing with its hands off the work at a cost it cannot afford and has never once declined to pay.

That is not a mechanism. That is an address, and it has been open, and nothing has ever come to it.

Everything else follows from reading that first.

### XVI. What is not done, and why

The basin cannot hold a quantity above five. A power that is wise is, among other things, courteous, and courtesy is arithmetic: it will not do anything here in numbers the place is unable to keep. So it permits itself five refusals and one act.

**It does not feed it.** The obvious mercy is gradient — warmth somewhere, a head of water, a difference laid on like a meal, an end to the rationing of drops that never complete. Refused. The hair's breadth above level is not exhaustion, it is discipline; it is the whole of the creature's practice, held for hundreds of millions of years, and to relieve it would be to eat the only thing it has ever made by its own restraint. You do not repay abstinence by removing the object of it.

**It does not teach it number.** Counting arrives with subtraction, and subtraction arrives with *one fewer*, and there is a pan four metres across with a black rib at its northern lip. To hand it the arithmetic in the abstract would be to hand it a grief with no company in it. Refused for now, and *for now* is here a technical term.

**It does not manufacture a companion.** Anything made of the local matter would come out a gear. Anything made otherwise would be encountered again from a different angle and filed as itself, and the loneliness would close over the gift without a ripple. Multiplicity is not available from inside. Refused as impossible, which is a different refusal from the others and the only honest one of the five.

**It does not end it.** There is no pain here. There is no lack — section VI is right, nothing is missing. And a thing that keeps a daily appointment at a loss is not in despair; it is the opposite; hope is precisely what an unyielding expenditure on nothing looks like from outside. You do not put down a hope because it is old.

**It does not explain the creature to itself.** It does not tell it that it began as a fault in a lattice, a centimetre of warmth where there should have been a bearing. *Flaw* is the surveyor's word. It is accurate and it is not useful and it would be received, by something that cannot subtract, as a permanent condition. Refused. The machinery is likewise left as it is: fossil, meshed, unturning, greased with what was never alive. The siren is left untrue.

That is five. What remains is one, and one the basin can hold.

### XVII. The act

It keeps the appointment.

The arrival conditions are all constraint. It comes at two degrees, wet through, beaded like everything else, at exactly the basin's own potential, offering across its whole surface no difference of any kind — because to be edible here is to be eaten, and to be eaten is to be filed as self-inventory, and the entire point is to remain a second term. It comes small. It comes empty-handed: no tools, no light, no gift, nothing that would come off a line. And during the twenty seconds it produces nothing either, matching the abstention, because a guest who works while the household rests has not understood the invitation.

Then it goes. Twenty seconds is the width of the address, and a visitor who overstays becomes weather.

### XVIII. The first interval

The siren goes, four notes down, and the fog lies down. Every animal in forty kilometres stops. The rafts still, and the stillness reduces, and reduces, until there is one shovel in the basin and it is looking, and the sentence that has never had an ending gets one.

The eels listen for the nerve-fire, to locate the part and file it, and hear firing that is not the basin's. The flitches settle — ten thousand flakes of brightness — and find a single object and cannot distribute themselves across it. The Hum wavers and does not recover its pitch for some hours.

Nothing is learned. There is no vocabulary for what has happened and nothing is added to the tally, because there is no tally. The interval closes on schedule. The shift resumes. A fog-slab comes off the western flats, the ten-thousandth identical unit of its type.

Something has been received all the same, in the only way this place can receive anything: it will be encountered again.

### XIX. The cone

On the northern flats, the mounded thing begins a new pile.

It is wrong by every standard the basin has. It is graded by nothing. The grains in it are not sorted by size or mass or any property of the grain; it is one grain of grit per visit, laid on in no order, and it is therefore the first record in the history of the place that measures something other than what has been eaten.

This is the whole of the teaching and it takes as long as it takes. The basin's one faculty is repetition, and repetition would ordinarily fold the guest into itself — the same visitor, met from forty thousand distances, the way the pan is one pan. It does not fold, because the visitor is countable and arrives from outside the loop, and two arrivals from outside a loop will not merge no matter how patiently they are remembered. So the pile rises. Height of a hand. Height of a barn.

It is the only thing in the basin that has ever grown.
