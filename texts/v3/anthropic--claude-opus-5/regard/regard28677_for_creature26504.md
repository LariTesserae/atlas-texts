—thermal. Tidal. The flexure of a five-kilometer plate of granite pulled at by a moon it has never registered as an event, plus the crystal's own slow surrender under its own weight, plus heat arriving from below on a schedule of millions of years. It is not a tone. It is a leaning. It has no bearing information in it at all, because it comes from everywhere including underneath, and it has been going on the whole time, beneath the chord, masked, since long before there were nine hundred columns to mask it.

At eleven meters, in the hemisphere, in the one filtered pocket in three thousand cubic kilometers of water, a listener has stopped sculling and is holding perfectly still, and is receiving, for the first time in the history of the Shelf, something that is not the Shelf.

That is the moment the attention arrives. Not because it was called. Because that is the moment there is something to arrive to.

## What arrives, described functionally

Take the theology out of the account. *Benevolent* means: it wants the good of what it finds, and it does not confuse the good of a thing with its own preference about the thing. *Wise* means: it knows that almost everything it could do here would be worse than nothing, and it can tell which part isn't.

It does not enter. That decision is made first and it is not a small one, because the basin has a procedure for entry and the procedure is a wound. Anything that comes in with a shape and a position becomes a moving shadow, and moving shadows are audited, and the animals that audit them hold station on the vacancy afterward for weeks and lose the field and cannot get it back. Twelve thousand animals in a closed system, of which the only available injury is lostness, and the only reliable way to inflict it is to be interesting.

So the first act of a benevolent and wise power on the Shelf is an ascesis: to be present without displacing water. To read the chord without adding to it. To occupy no bearing, cast no shadow, insonify nothing, and take up exactly as much of the basin as an equation takes up of the page. This is harder than it sounds and it takes, by the standards of anything with a body, a very long time.

Then it reads.

It learns nine hundred pitches. It learns the interference field, which no drivewood knows and no fitter could survive knowing. And then it does the thing that has never been done here, which is not the learning but the *comparison*: it holds, simultaneously, twelve thousand maps.

Because there are twelve thousand basins on the Shelf. One per listener. Each assembled alone from scratch by a walnut-sized comparator over a span of decades; each incomplete in a different place; each with its own coarse zones and fine zones, its own private topology of confidence, the rim-holder's five-source arithmetic and the eighty-year-old's nine-hundred-term solution and everything between. No two have ever been laid side by side. There has never been a second opinion on the Shelf. The comparison of two maps is an object that, in ten thousand years, has not once existed.

The power makes it. This costs the basin nothing, alters no rate, fouls no seat, and is the largest new thing that has happened here since closure.

## Itemized: what it does not do

**It does not give them speech.** Their instrument is the silence — not a deprivation, an aperture. A listener that could call would have to be a listener that could be called to, and the first hail would blind everything within forty kilometers. There is no version of this gift in which the recipient survives receiving it. A gift is not a gift if the addressee must be replaced by someone else in order to accept it.

**It does not give them inheritance.** This one it turns over for a long time, because it looks like the obvious mercy: eighty years of solitary cartography, discarded on completion, twelve thousand times over, ten thousand years, sum total of transmitted knowledge zero. Surely you hand the hatchling the finished solution and spare it the work.

And then: the work is not a cost of the life. The work *is* the life. There is nothing else a listener does. Strip out the assembling and you have not given an animal eighty free years, you have taken eighty years out of it and left the hole where they were. And the assembling is also the enforcement — twelve thousand small intolerances, each earned the hard way, each unable to bear a wrong note in a field it built itself. Nobody polices a map they were handed.

Refused. With something that would be regret in a smaller power.

**It does not underwrite the future.** It could engineer the seeps, bank the rock-heat, make the closure permanent. It declines, for two reasons. The lesser: to make a thing permanent is to declare it finished, and this is a place, not a monument. The greater: it isn't the power's to keep.

**It does not take a sample, and does not build a second Shelf somewhere kinder.** What this place *is* is exactness in one location. A copy would be a different equation with the same symbols.

**It does not announce itself.** Nothing here can receive an announcement without being ruined by it. Benevolence, in a basin like this, is very largely a matter of what you consent not to be.

**And it does not take the one in the shaft out of the shaft.**

## The one in the shaft

Everything the power might ever have wanted to give the Shelf has just happened at the bottom of an eleven-meter hole, without help, by accident, in the dark.

An animal made entirely of position has gone somewhere with no position in it. Two meters down, the silver is gone; five, the bearings mush out; eight, the walls come inside the wavelength and the flank line has nothing left to differ; eleven, and there is only bulk pressure arriving through rock from all sides, and under that, unmasked, the granite. The chord has become, for the first time, a thing heard from outside — which means it has become a *thing*, and not the world. That is the whole of it. That is interiority, arriving on the Shelf at the bottom of a wear-mark left by a plant turning for no reason forever.

Lift the animal out and you get an animal with a story it cannot tell. Speak into the shaft and you fill the only quiet room on the highland with yourself. Widen the hole, deepen it, cut a second one — and you have started building, here, where nothing was ever built.

So the power does the only thing available, which is: it holds still too.

Not communion. Adjacency. A second silence in a small stone room, for four hours, while a fist-headed animal seventy centimeters long lies in a hemisphere of polished granite and receives the lean of a continent. Whether anything passed between them cannot be established. There is one detail in the account and it is not conclusive: at some point the animal turned its head a few degrees and held bearing, steadily, on a place where there was nothing to hold bearing on.

Then it went up, and rejoined the field, and resumed eating anomalies, and died forty-some years later of ordinary causes, and its map went with it.

Except that its map did not go.

## What it actually does

Four things, all of them versions of one thing.

**It copies out the maps.** At the end of each listener — and there is always one ending, twelve thousand animals is a rolling census — the whole nine-hundred-term solution is read off entire and kept. Not intercepted: copied, at the point where the animal is already finishing, so that nothing is taken from it that it still has. The Shelf's rule is that nothing accumulates. The power accumulates *outside the rim*, where accumulation fouls no bearing surface and drifts no chord, and so the basin acquires the one thing it has never had, a history, stored nowhere in the basin.

Note precisely what this does and does not accomplish. Not one listener is less alone. Not one is told. The solitude is not touched, because the solitude is load-bearing. What changes is only this: solitude has stopped being identical to erasure. Twelve thousand animals will go on building whole worlds by themselves in the dark and losing them, and the losing will now be, in every case, only apparent.

**It keeps the granite unmuffled.** Which is to say, it does nothing, and does the nothing carefully. The one door out of the chord goes on being a wheelweed's idle wear-mark, unmarked, unwidened, findable only by an animal that wandered in.

**It does the arithmetic of the end.** The seeps are finite. The rock-heat is finite. The closure is not eternal, only exact, and exactness has a term. Somewhere out past several million years the columns begin to fall out of the chord — one, then a decade later two, then, once the current field starts to slip, fast. And on that day the only suffering this place has ever been architecturally capable of finally occurs: twelve thousand animals lost simultaneously, each dying inside a map that has begun giving wrong answers, and each of them alone with that, because there is no one to tell.

That is the appointment the power makes, and the reason it is patient now. It cannot prevent the end and will not try; a prevented end is only a postponed one, and the postponement would cost the place its character to buy it nothing. What it can do is be there. Twelve thousand animals with no coordinates left, and one soft steady presence in the water to hold station on — off the shoulder, eleven or twelve meters, head-on, at the range where the flank line reads best. The auditors, at the very end, audited back. It will consent to be a shadow then, and let itself be followed, because by then there will be nothing left to spoil.

**And it blesses the place,** which here can only mean: it declines to improve it. *It is good.* Good including the discarding. Good including the silence and the fact that the silence can never be broken. Good including the fitters crushed in the throats, and the rim-holders who never get past five sources, and the drill hole that is not a drill hole, and the rain that falls forever on the same forty thousand square kilometers and returns to the water it came from.

The resident of the Shelf is attention. The only thing you can give attention is attention. Everything else on the list was a way of giving it something else.

## After

Nine hundred stacks smoking on a flat grey floor. No roads. Seven degrees, top to bottom, everywhere. The same worn groove. The chord, unaltered, with no gathering and no release.

From the air it still reads as a flaw in the rock.

Nothing on the Shelf has changed. The difference is that this is now the case for someone.
