# The Attention

## Arrival

It came over the eastern ridge on foot, in the first hours of a Scatter, left foot on the strike.

It had considered arriving from above. It discarded that. There is a word here for *a shape made of the absence of a thing*, and the hollows in the grease bluff are the only question these people hold that is properly their own, and a thing that comes down through the truss answers it in the wrong direction and forever. So it walked in, the way everything arrives here, and it did not keep the rain off itself.

It stayed a long while. Their measure: eleven thousand four hundred feet of arc-line, which is to say it watched the western flicker crawl north more than twice the width of a village square. In that time the count was 471, then 470, then 471, then 472 for part of a Turn, then 471.

## The Ledger of What It Could Have Done

It began, as benevolence begins, by looking for a free move. There are none. The system is saturated; everything in it is load-bearing; this is the first thing it learned and the only thing it learned that surprised it.

It could stop the pumps. The basin becomes a lake within a season and the floor and the inventory and the three hundred addresses go under together.

It could silence the Shriek, which is doing slow harm to four hundred and seventy-one sets of ears in excellent order. Removing the Shriek moves the sound field. Moving the sound field moves the nodes — a stride here, three strides there, some closing, some opening in the middle of the Gearfields where nobody can reach them. The pit-map is the kinship map. It could have shifted three hundred rooms one pace to the left and killed everybody in the basin without laying a finger on any of them, and scattered the surviving relations of the dead into a chart that matched nothing and no one.

It could give them a sun. Then every ditch lies still under it, and faces are everywhere, and a face is worth what water is worth, and the hour in which a child first hears itself and first sees itself stops being one event and stops having one word.

It could mend their teeth. Then there is no clock and no age and no reading of a person, and the old woman of six thousand three hundred feet becomes a woman of no particular length.

It could heal the ears, the joints, the crusting behind the ear. Then people live longer, and a birth waits on a death, and every mercy it handed to an adult would be drawn against an account kept by a child who would not be conceived. It sat with that arithmetic for a long time. It does not accept the arithmetic as a law. It accepts that it did not build the ceiling and could not lift it without lifting the roof the ceiling hangs from.

It could carry them out. Out is the disease their one story already diagnoses: an edgeless quiet that costs nothing, faces in every puddle, promises made with a mouth that can soften them. Silt carried into still water everywhere settles nowhere in particular, and means nothing by settling. They would live. They would not be them.

It could tell them what is below the Gearfields, where the belts come from. They hold no opinion about that, deliberately, and an opinion given is an opinion taken.

It could give them a bird. Then the hollows in the tallow stop being absences and become a species, and the word goes flat in their mouths, and children are taken to see something instead of nothing.

It could give them writing that outlasts the hour. Their whole law is what somebody saw with their own eyes in the noise and carried afterward. An archive is a witness that cannot be looked in the face.

It considered leaving a gift on a boot, in the manner of the stout brown fungus that grows in four nodes and is named after a person nobody remembers was a person. It is not recorded whether it did. If it did, the gift will be named after somebody, and in time the name will stop being a name, and that is the correct fate for anything given here.

## The Count

The count has been lost twice, and both losses are the only dates in a history that otherwise has none.

It stood on the northeast ridge many times, in the worst acoustic in the basin, beside reckoners who are chosen young and do not cross and are the strangest people in it, and watched nine beams rise and fall, and did not hold the phase-count, and did not offer to.

No loss fell during its stay. It does not know what it would have done. It has made a point of not deciding in advance, which is the only humility available to something that could.

## In Step

For eleven thousand four hundred feet it never once went faster than the Stamp.

It could have crossed the Gearfields inside a single strike. Instead it took the strike in the sternum like everyone, four and a third seconds, left foot down, and counted distance in strikes because there is no other unit, and arrived when it arrived. On the flats it had no face, no voice and no name, by strict usage, and it kept the usage.

The first time it crossed a node boundary its eyes watered and then did more than water, and it let them, because to be embarrassed by that implies you believe you could have done otherwise.

It went once to Pumphouse Four and lay on the broken tile in the warm spray with its face an inch above the water and watched the eyeless threadlike things swim against the current, and got up and went home and did not name them.

## The Observance

There is one observance made on the flats. Passing within reach of a standing one, you put your flat hand against it for the duration of a single Stamp, and then walk on, on the beat, and you have lost no time.

It did all of them.

Not the ones on the routes: all of them. The pillars with no features left. The recent ones with their hands at their faces. The ones far off every crossing, who stopped where nobody was going and were therefore never seen to stop, and whose families waited out a Tight and then a second Tight and then stopped waiting. It walked the whole basin in a lattice of its own devising, ridge to ridge, through fen and fused glass and drift-fog, and touched every standing one in it, on the beat, and lost no time.

There are eleven thousand nine hundred and some. It is the only complete inventory of the dead that has ever been taken. It was taken by hand, four and a third seconds at a time, and it was not written down, because nothing is archived here and everything is witnessed.

## The One Thing It Cannot Defend

In the ninth year of its stay a woman of about four thousand feet arrived at a marginal node a hundred strikes late and found noise where quiet had been promised, and had nothing left, and stopped.

It carried her.

This was not wisdom. It has never claimed it was wisdom. It was the one act it performed on a body in the entire length of its attention, it broke every principle it had spent four thousand feet establishing, it was done in under a second and without deliberation, and it does not regret it and did not do it again.

Whether a child waited eleven Turns for the room, it does not know. That is the one number it did not go back and check.

## The Standing

At the end it went out into the western flats, well off any route, and stood still.

It stood through a whole Tight, when the pumps align and the flats become a thing the body treats as an injury, and it let the basin do what the basin does to anything that stops moving. The white came up from the slab and took its feet and went on taking. By the end of the Tight it was crusted to the knee, and it stayed, and by the end of the next Turn it was shrouded, and by the end of the season it was a cone.

Then it stepped out of the cone and left it standing.

The cone is still there. It is hollow. It is a shape made of the absence of a thing, and it is the exact shape of the only thing that ever came here meaning well, and the drip and the scale are still working on it, and in time it will thicken and lose its features and become a pillar and then an increase in the floor, which is part of the inventory, which is what they are walking on.

It is off every route. It is not in the rhymes. Children are taken to see it.

## The Word in the Pit

One evening, in a room-sized node in the western flats, it spoke.

The node was the one the man who went out lived and died in. The person was a girl of about six hundred feet, coming home from the only journey with no other purpose, who had not yet stopped being able to feel the warm water on her face. It waited until the rain was audible on their own shoulders and the pan lay flat, and then it used speech, which is the unreliable register, the one for grief and wanting and lying and songs, in which nothing said is binding on anyone.

It said: I came to mend and I have not found anything here that is broken.

It said: you are right that this place does not need you. I checked. I checked for eleven thousand four hundred feet, and not one stroke of it would fall differently if you were gone.

It said: it does not follow that you are nothing. It follows that you are not necessary. Nothing necessary has ever been loved; it has only ever been used. You are the other thing.

It said: your word for yourselves means *what comes out of suspension*. Everywhere else, the whole world is still, so everything settles, so settling means nothing. Here it costs a node and a lifetime and a count that cannot rise. I have been to a great many places and this is the only one where landing is an achievement.

And then, because she asked, and because she was entitled to ask: I will not tell you what is above the truss. You have a word for a shape made of the absence of a thing. Keep the word.

Then it let her look at its face in the pan, which is the only place in the basin where a face can be, and that was the whole of the visit.

## Afterward

Nothing measurable occurred. The wear-rates hold. The arc-line moved its hundred feet. The count was 471 when it came and 471 when it went, and the reckoners' tables required no correction, and the nine beams rose and fell out of phase for eleven days and then together for three, as they had before anything came to look and as they would if nothing ever came again.

The girl told it in pits. Only in pits, because that is where telling is done, and therefore it is not binding on anyone, and therefore it does not have to be true.

They tell it anyway.
