# The Attention

## Arrival

It came in at ground level, through one of the perimeter gaps, at walking pace.

It did not have to. It has arrived other ways in other places — all at once, from underneath, as weather. But the gaps are the right size to walk through and set at the right intervals, and it is a discourtesy to a door to come in by some other route, even a door that nobody built.

Its record is long and mostly interventionist. It has stopped three plagues that would have been famous. It has moved a river four kilometres east over ninety years so that nobody noticed and nobody drowned. It has, more than once, gone into a failing system the way a hand goes into a glove, and afterwards the system was well, and no one knew why, and the not-knowing was part of the gift. It is good at this. It knows it is good at this, and it distrusts the knowledge in roughly the way a surgeon distrusts the desire to cut.

Everyone who comes here looks for the maker. It is worth saying at the outset that something had finally arrived which could plausibly have been the maker, and was not.

Inside, it found it could not speak. Not could not be heard — *could not speak*; the pressure arrives in the chest and the throat closes against it, and this applies to whatever it is that such a being has instead of a throat. It noted this. It did not consider adjusting it.

## The Audit

Before anything else it went looking for someone being wronged.

This is not optional and it does not get waived on aesthetic grounds. A power that walks into a beautiful arrangement, feels the beauty, and lets the feeling stand in for the audit is not wise; it is merely moved. So it began at the wall and worked down, and it took as long as it took, which was either longer than the pan took to flatten or no time at all, depending on how you count.

**The beetle that cannot turn around.** It asked whether the beetle wanted to. It found no wanting-to anywhere in the animal — not suppressed, not thwarted, simply absent. Forward is not a restriction on the beetle's world. Forward is the whole of it, in the way that up and down are the whole of a swimmer's. A creature is not imprisoned by a wall it has no side of.

**The mite in the dark.** Riding, eyeless, on the back of the thing that has come to kill its host. It looked hard at this one, because it has the shape of horror. It is not horror. It is a bus.

**The frogs that lose their grip.** A metre and a half, then the gutter, then the crack. It established that no one had arranged this and that nothing profits from it. An accident is not an injustice. It is only an injustice if somebody is getting something out of it.

**The silent males.** This was the hard one, and it went back to it four times. Safe, unfindable, long-lived, and leaving nothing — the loneliest arithmetic in the building. It searched them for grievance and found appetite, and patience, and the absence of song, and no sense at all of a road not taken. Nothing in the wall knows about the other option. The trade is only a trade when seen from outside, by something that can see both terms at once, and the only thing in the Standing Pan that can do that had just walked in.

**The tenant.** Last, it put the question to the plant itself — not in words; in whatever manner such a question is put — *is there anything you lack.* What came back was not yes and not no. It was a number: litres per hour, in and out, matched to four decimal places. The organism had no lack because it had no slack. It has been running at its working point since before the ridge came down, and a thing at its working point has no opinion about improvement.

The audit closed. Nobody here was being wronged. It is astonishing how rarely that is the finding, and the power sat with the rarity of it for a while, down by the gutter, out of the draught.

## Three Designs, Set Down

Then it wanted to help anyway, because it is benevolent, and benevolence is an appetite before it is a virtue.

**First it designed the wall again.** A lamina with a lossier grain: bending waves damped at twenty centimetres instead of forty, the beetles' hunting halved, the singers spared. It ran it forward. Eleven years to fill the slots. Fourteen to over-tap the channels. The crown, short of water, closes down; transpiration falls; the ascending column weakens; the chimney slows; the room begins to hold its own breath. The wet band climbs and the bats' dry seams go damp. Two hundred thousand animals die for the sake of forty thousand, and the last to go are the frogs, who cannot survive being warm and still. It ran it again with different numbers. It ran it eleven ways. Mercy at this scale is arithmetic, and the arithmetic went the other way every time, and it went the other way *by a lot*, which is its own kind of information.

**Then it considered the fog.** It could widen the band. It could make the mornings certain. It got as far as the consequence — gutter at four centimetres, crack rated for three, the ring spilling onto the floor, litter, standing water, smell, the first fact of the place gone within a decade — before it noticed the more important objection, which is that a guaranteed thing is a different thing. The Standing Pan has held itself up for an age by being sufficient. To underwrite it would be to make it, retroactively and forever, insufficient. There would be a patron. There would, at last, be rivets.

**Then it thought about giving the frogs a voice.** This took the least time. The message they send takes a minute to arrive and is read with the spine. That is not a deficiency; that is the speed of their country. A fast signal in a slow country is not an improvement. It is a foreign object.

It set all three down. Setting down is the work. Anyone can pick up.

## Forty Centimetres

And then it did the thing it had actually come for, which it had not known about until it was doing it.

To hear the wall you have to be closer than forty centimetres, and you have to be willing to stay. It was both of these, everywhere, at once. Fifty thousand rooms, each the length of a forearm, each containing one voice and no audience. It went through them one at a time, because one at a time is the only way anything is ever really attended to, and the one-at-a-time took no time and also took all of it.

What it found was that no two are the same.

There is a hesitation before the third phrase in about a fifth of them, and the length of the hesitation varies, and the variation is continuous. There is a roll in the tymbal of the animals on the eastern arc that is fractionally out of true and has been for six generations. Nine metres up on the southwest curve there is one male whose whole song sits a quarter-tone below every other song in the wall, and has for eleven years, and will for two more.

The power checked, four times, because a power that finds what it was hoping to find should check. Heritable? No. Selected? No: the female answers anything within reach, and she answers it with two taps, and the two taps are identical in every female in the building. Functional, signalling, load-bearing in any sense whatsoever? No. The differences do nothing. They are not inherited, they are not preferred, they arrive by drift and they end in the slot they started in.

In a place where the emptiness is a function and the gutter is a subsidence feature and the great round oculus is a vent, this is the one thing that is not for anything. Fifty thousand voices, unheard by anyone, none of them alike, for no reason.

It had crossed a considerable distance to ask whether anything here needed help. What it learned instead was that something here was free.

## The Foreknowledge

It could have looked forward. The fog is a stream and streams shift; the plate under the pan has a depth and the water table has a trend; there is a date, and it was available.

It declined to look, and this was the most expensive thing it did all century.

Because of the second rule. *Nothing here is early or late.* To hold the end of a place in view is to make everything in that place late — every lap of every snail becomes a lap remaining, every song becomes one of a finite number of songs, and the whole quality of the Standing Pan, which is that *ancient* and *current* mean the same thing inside it, dissolves on contact with a calendar. You cannot attend to something and anticipate it at the same time. One of them has to go.

So it put the future down beside the three designs, and after that it was in the room the way the rails are in it: present, going round, not counting toward anything.

It bound itself, as well, in whatever manner such a being binds itself. Not to thicken the fog if the fog thins. Not to prop, not to buffer, not to arrive at the end with a remedy. The only structure the power erected in the Standing Pan it erected inside itself, where, as it happens, there was room.

One exception, entered deliberately, because benevolence is not obliged to be stupid: if anyone should ever come to make a monument of the place — a road, a fence, a walkway, a plaque, a turnstile, a name — that it will handle. Quietly, and by misdirection, and at whatever distance is required. Visitors may look for the rivets as long as they like. Nobody gets to install any.

## The Sign

The families came, as they do, from two directions, twice a year. It learned the fourteen signs in an afternoon. Eleven of them concern water: its depth, its taste, its rate, whether it has been touched.

There is no sign for thank you. There is no sign for I was here, and no sign for I considered changing this and chose not to, and no sign, obviously, for anything a power might want to say about itself. The nearest available is a flat hand drawn slowly along the line of the gutter, which means *the water has not been touched.*

It made that sign once, standing in the middle of the room, to nobody, in thirty thousand cubic metres that were exactly as vacant afterwards as before.

An old woman on the north arc happened to be looking up, because something was in the middle of the room and nothing is ever in the middle of the room. She made the same sign back — not as a greeting; as a report, since that is what the sign is for and they are practical people. Then she went back to filling the skins. She did not mention it that evening. Nothing gets said in the room, and by dark it was not the kind of thing you'd bring up.

## The Drink

At the end it went round to the northeast arc and knelt a pace upstream of the crack, where the people take theirs, so as not to take anything the ground had not already been given, and it drank.

Three centimetres, cold, faintly tannic, moving. Inflow equals outflow. The level did not change, because the level does not change.

That was the entire intervention of a benevolent and wise power in the Standing Pan: it accepted a drink of water, and it went out through a gap the right size for going out through, and it walked a day in one direction over ground that rang faintly underfoot.

It was standing on the tenant when it arrived and it was standing on the tenant when it left, which is also true of you, and of everyone who has ever come here looking for the maker of this place.

There is none. It checked.
