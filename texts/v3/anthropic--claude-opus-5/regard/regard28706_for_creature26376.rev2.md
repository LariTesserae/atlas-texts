## It Comes at the Hour of the Dew

It arrives without light. This is the first decision and it is made before arrival, in the way that a wise thing's decisions are mostly made before it moves: light here is not illumination, it is weather. A lumen laid across the second terrace at two in the morning would pull moths off the figs, would put the potoo off its hunt, would stall a column of carriers at the exact point where the beam crossed the groove of smell, and the stall would propagate backward for eleven metres and take forty minutes to clear. So it comes dark, at body temperature, at the hour the swales let go of their water, and the only difference between the terrace with it and the terrace without it is a difference no instrument on this planet could find.

Loudness is expensive. It has read the rules of the house and it obeys them.

## The Mercies, Considered

It comes intending good, because that is what it is, and the first thing it does is run the list. Any power worth the name arrives with a list.

**Rain, early.** The pools are down another hand's breadth and the catfish are lying flank to flank at thirty-three degrees, gulping film. It could put four centimetres on this valley before dawn and the channel would run and the caiman would swim and nothing would be crowded. It looks at what follows: the low chambers flooded out of season and unsealed; the figs flushing three weeks wrong and dropping their fruit into an empty month; the alates of eleven colonies reading the humidity and going up in a single afternoon into a sky that will not hold the pattern, tens of thousands of founding queens in the air on a day that leads nowhere. Declined.

**The heat.** It could take four degrees off the laterite. But the mound holds its gardens within a degree of optimum by shaft geometry alone, which is to say the whole six-metre architecture of the thing is a two-million-year-old solution to the exact problem of this heat. Remove a problem of that age and you do not relieve the solution, you strand it. Declined.

**The fly.** This is the first one that costs it something. The phorid is the size of a comma and its entire biography is eight seconds of inattention at the soft joint behind a carrier's head; it has no song, no beauty anyone has ever recorded, and it could be unmade tonight across the whole twenty-five kilometres without a single elegy. But the minim riding backward on the leaf above the carrier's head is riding because of the fly. Remove the fly and within a few dozen generations the gunners are gone, and the caste is gone, and the whole delicate top layer of the thing — the part that most looks like care, that most looks like one sister protecting another — was never care at all but pressure, and it evaporates the instant the pressure does. And there is the fly's own larva, which has a life. A benevolence that can only add in one direction is not benevolence. It is preference, wearing the clothes. Declined.

**Her mandibles.** She is ninety-one days old and the cutting edges are worn round, and it would take nothing — less than nothing — to sharpen them. Then what. She is not a person with plans who has been interrupted by a disability. Ninety days is not the truncation of her life; ninety days is the shape of it, the way a crescent is the shape of the leaf she is carrying. Give her three hundred and you have not extended a life, you have inserted a stranger into a colony that has already, correctly, produced her replacement. The kindness has no recipient. It is a gift addressed to a self that does not live at this address. Declined — and it sits with this one for a while, because of the roundness of the edges, which really does look like wear, which really does look like a story.

**The fungus.** Free it, says the list. Give it back its fruiting bodies, its spores, its weather, the eight million years of outdoors it has not had. The power looks at the grey-white bolsters hanging in the dark like wet bread pulled apart and understands that *freedom* is a word from somewhere else entirely. This is not a prisoner. This is the most thoroughly kept thing on Earth — weeded, watered, fed, medicated, carried in the mouths of queens across geological time — and what is being proposed, under the name of liberty, is eviction. Declined, with something that in a smaller being would be embarrassment.

## The One It Was Frightened By

**Give them experience.**

It is on the list because it is the only gift that would actually be received. Let the colony know itself. Let the queen understand that she is the mother of four million and has not seen the sky in eight years. Let the carrier feel the weight of the leaf as *weight*, and the trail as *long*, and the ninety-first day as *late*.

The power holds this one for a considerable time, and what it feels, examining it, is something adjacent to fear.

Because it would work. And at the instant it worked there would be four million subjects on this terrace where there had been none, and each would have a first experience, and the first experience of nearly all of them would be the thirty-three-degree dark and a full mouth and eleven kilometres to go. It would be the largest act of harm ever committed in this valley, executed flawlessly, out of love, by someone who had confused *mattering* with *minding*.

They do not mind. That is not a deficiency. That is the terms on which twenty million years were possible.

Declined — and noted, as the closest the visit came to catastrophe.

## What Is Actually Missing

Having declined everything, it does the thing that the list was preventing it from doing, which is to look.

It looks for a long time, by its own reckoning, and finds that the terrace is not lacking water, or coolness, or mercy, or meaning, or mind. The terrace is lacking exactly one thing, and the essay of the place says so plainly without noticing it has said it:

*There is no accumulation here except soil. It is a landscape without an attic. The terrace does not keep things.*

Nothing here is remembered. Not by anything. A branch is gone as a distinct object in three years, a peccary in two nights, a worker in four minutes and two sisters. The colony has no memory; it has a gradient of smell that is rewritten continuously and is therefore always only now. The fungus has twenty million years of unbroken continuity and not one second of recollection: it has been fed every day since the Miocene and has never once had a yesterday. The whole magnificent works — the roads, the castes, the antibiotic farms on the shoulders of the staff, the nitrogen coming out of the air in the hot pits under the garbage — runs at full capacity, in total silence, in front of no one, and then is composted, including the evidence that it ran.

That is the hole. It is the only hole. And it is the only one that can be filled from outside without breaking anything, because memory is the single gift that takes nothing from the thing remembered.

## What It Did

It counted.

*If you laid a metre square of frame on the forest floor and had an eternity to look, you would never finish counting.* It has an eternity and it is not a you. So it laid the frame, in the swale below the eleventh mound where the litter is shin-deep, and it counted: the mites first, all of them, including the eleven hundred and six species that have no names and will be extinct before anyone gets around to it; then the springtails; then the pseudoscorpion in the damp layer two fingers down with a springtail in her pincers and forty-one eggs under her; then the hyphae, metre by metre, through the whole dark mat; then the leaf miner, one millimetre long, in the two hundred microns between the skins of a leaf, whose universe is that leaf and who will never learn that there is an above. It counted the above for him.

Then it went and found her.

She was at metre one hundred and seven, uphill, four centimetres a second, ninety-one days old, a hundred and thirty milligrams of young fig held overhead at the angle that took her lineage four million years to work out, and a minim riding backward on the leaf, facing the dark, doing nothing, doing the most important nothing on the terrace. Since dusk she had made six hundred and eleven contacts with oncoming traffic, each one about a fifth of a second, each one a check, none of them remembered by anybody. They are all remembered now. That is the entire change.

It went with her at her speed. This is the part that cost it something real: to a power, four centimetres a second is not slow, it is a translation, an agreement to be finite for ninety-three minutes. It did not help her up the riser. It did not steady the leaf when she crossed the root at metre one hundred and sixty, where the trail narrows and the surface is polished and eight times in ten the carriers of her size lose two seconds. She lost two seconds. They are on the record.

It did not give her a name. Names are for things that get lost.

## The Hand Over the Turret

At the mound it did the only physical thing it did all night, which was to make a hand, briefly, and hold the back of it over a turret.

Warm, wet, moving: carbon dioxide and fungal must and formic acid and the sweetness of shredded leaf, a bakery and a compost heap having a disagreement, coming up out of the ground in litres per hour from a chamber system two thousand rooms deep. Something the size of a house, eight years old, exhaling onto its skin.

*Get it to breathe on the back of your hand and try to hold onto that.*

It held onto it. That is what it is for.

The question the terrace cannot raise — whether the ants keep the fungus or the fungus keeps the ants — it is in a position to raise, and it raises it, and the question comes apart in its hands: there is one animal here, thirty metres across and six deep, made of two tissue types that diverged before there were rivers in this basin, and it has been, for the whole of that time, entirely unaware of being magnificent. The power considers, for one last moment, correcting this.

It does not consider it seriously. Unaware of being magnificent is not a condition anyone is entitled to fix.

## What It Left

Nothing. Not a wall, not a wire, not a post, not a shard of pot. The terrace remains, as it has always been, a place without artifacts — and a benevolent power that understood the place would sooner leave a wound than leave a monument.

So the record is indistinguishable from no visit. This is the honest end of it. An observer with every instrument ever built, working for a century, would find a landscape in which nothing whatsoever occurred at two in the morning on the last dry night but one, and would be right, by every criterion except the one that matters.

Two hours after the hand, she dropped the leaf at the mound, and turned, and went back out, because at ninety-one days there is no other option available to her. Eight nights later she stopped, mid-stride, without incident, at metre fourteen. The traffic parted around her for four minutes. Two sisters carried her to the midden where the beetles are. The colony's respiration did not change by one measurable millilitre.

It was watching. That is all. That is the whole of the intervention, and it will not be improved on: that the smallest and least-regarded transaction on this terrace, the substrate-return of a used worker in a colony of four million in a valley of eleven colonies on a planet where this happens some hundreds of millions of times a night — happened, for once, *in front of someone*, and was not lost, and is not lost.

## Six Weeks

The dew gets heavier. The figs flush. The channel fills in a single afternoon, brown and fast, and the pools stop being places and start being water again, and the caiman goes wherever caimans go. The turrets are sealed from inside with pellets of soil. Thirty centimetres of flood runs over the whole silent factory floor and the factory hums along underneath it, undisturbed, exactly as it has done.

And at twenty-five degrees and total humidity, in the dark it has occupied since the Miocene, the oldest thing on this terrace goes on being fed.

It is being watched being fed. It does not know. It was never going to know. The power stays anyway — for the eight remaining years of the animal above it, and for the slump, and for the pale nitrogen scar in the understorey where something the size of a house once had a metabolism, and for the hundred thousand years of terraces after that — at the speed of the things it is watching, keeping the only thing here that nobody else can keep, having found out by looking that there was nothing to be done, which is different, entirely and forever different, from having done nothing.
