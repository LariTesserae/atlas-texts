# The Attending

*being an addendum to the account, in the same hand, some while later*

---

## On Arrival

It began with the floor, because that is where anything here begins.

Nothing arrived. Attention is not a movement, and there was no descent, no light in the truss, no interruption of the eleven-day phase. What happened is that the basin, which had been unattended for the whole of its wear-record, became attended, and for a long while this made no difference of any kind that an instrument could register.

The first thing it did was hold still and take the measurements everyone takes: bolt-stumps, drip-bowls, the polished band on a gear rim. It arrived at the same very large number. It checked the number against what it already knew and found the number honest.

The second thing it did was try to speak to someone, and fail, and understand why it had failed, and this took it longer than the first thing by a wide margin.

You cannot address a contact people from outside contact. Their language lives in the tendons of the forearm; it is read by grip in the dark; its grammar assumes a wrist. A voice is useless here, and a vision is worse than useless — a thing seen at distance on the flats already has a sign, and the sign means *do not go and see*. So before it could offer anything at all it had to become touchable, which meant becoming a body of roughly the right temperature with roughly the right number of tendons, standing in the mud, wet, cold, and annexable.

It notes, in whatever it keeps instead of records, that this was the entire cost of the operation and also the entire payment.

---

## What Was Considered

A power that is only benevolent will reach for the largest lever. A power that is also wise reads the linkage first.

**Stop the pumps.** Nine brick shells, nine beams, and the noise that arrives in the sternum before the ears. It could have laid a hand on all nine in the same second. Then: the water table, which sits a finger's width under the slab and is always trying to come up. Then the fen crossing the flats at a walking pace. Then three hundred discs of moss drowned in grey water in under a year, and four hundred people on the eastern ridge with nothing behind them. The pumps are not the affliction. The pumps are the reason there is a floor. Set aside.

**Quiet the Drone.** Four square miles of bearing hum, coupled into the slab, coupled into the joints. Silence it and you have silenced the pits, because the pits are made *of* the Drone — they are the places where it argues with its own reflection and cancels. Every green disc in the basin is a piece of arithmetic performed by the noise. Take away the noise and you do not get a quiet world; you get a world with no quiet in it, because quiet here is not an absence, it is a structure. Set aside, with regret, twice.

**Give them a sky.** Their eyes are ruined for the Arc-Front at twelve miles. Set aside on the merits, and then set aside again on a second ground: the east ridge has always been walkable. People have gone out. People go out still. The number four hundred is not a wall, it is a carrying capacity, and the difference matters. It had come prepared to open a door and found the door had never been shut, and had to sit down with that for a while — because a power that arrives to liberate and discovers no prison must then ask what it actually came for, and the honest answer is smaller and harder and involves staying.

**Settle the threads.** It knows what the threads are. It could have written the answer in the mud at Pumphouse Four in six characters, and the writing would have lasted a week on the flats and forever on the terraces. Half the households eat them; half carry them back up over the spillway in cupped tile, all their lives. The dispute cannot be shouted, and so instead of being settled it is *lived* — two ways of being a person, intermarrying, choosing at nine, never once a fight, because there is no mechanism for a fight. Handing them the answer would end the practice, and the practice is worth more than the answer, and it is not the answer's owner anyway. Set aside. This one was difficult and remains difficult.

**Their deafness, and the four hours a month.** Here it stopped and did the work properly, because here is where a certain kind of power goes wrong: it decides that the wound is the culture and the culture is beautiful and therefore the wound must be kept.

That is sentimentality, and it kills people.

So: what constrains their speech? Not their ears. The sound field. A person with perfect hearing cannot converse on the flats either; the Stamp would still take the words out of the air. Restore every ruined ear in the basin and they will still walk forearm in forearm, still write with a stick, still say their twelve words in a pit and then move because the water is rising. The silence is imposed by the world. The deafness is imposed by the fibre.

**A thing that would still be true if you fixed it is not the world. It is only damage.**

That was the finding, and everything it subsequently did came out of that one sentence.

---

## What Was Done

**The fibre.** They pack their ears from infancy with grease-soaked insulation fibre, thumbed in by a mother, changed twice daily. The fibre is the disintegration-dust of the old lagging, and under sufficient magnification it is glass — needles that migrate, scar, and by thirty have taken the top of the range, and by fifty are in the lungs of a fair proportion of everyone.

It did not remove the fibre. There is nothing else to pack an ear with, and a people cannot be asked to give up their ears' only protection on the word of a stranger they can't hear.

It altered what the fungus does with the dust. The flat white shelves that fruit in the pits and smell of wet ash now take the needles up and lay them down soft
