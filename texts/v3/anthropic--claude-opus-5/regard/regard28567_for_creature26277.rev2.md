## THE ATTENTION

### It came in at knee depth

It did not arrive as light. There is no sun here and nothing casts a shadow with a direction, and a power that came in shining would only have been one more arc-strobe printing itself on the back of an eye — another event the valley would have heard, correctly, some minutes before it happened, and marked for revision.

So it came the way the flood comes. Warm, brown, from the shoulder of the basin, at knee depth, in the rain that falls both ways. It got wet. It let the noise into itself and lost some of it permanently, because that is what happens to anything that stands in this place, and a wisdom that exempts itself from the local physics has not yet arrived.

For a long time — several hundred revisions, in the only calendar available — it did nothing at all except stand in the ankle-warm flood and let the belt-lines slow very slightly as they passed.

It let the checksum run. It let itself be a figure that would not come out. It did not explain itself, because speech is impossible here, and it did not resent being measured, because being measured is the closest thing to a greeting this valley has ever learned to offer.

### What it declined to do

It did not stop the rain. The rain is not the wound.

It did not lower the upper surface, or fix the three in their places, or nail the valley to one version of itself. The drafting is not the wound either. Things that grow by breaking are not broken.

It did not restore the worker. It could have. It looked at that possibility for a while — the wool coat unrotted, the mug filled, the chair occupied, the ledger continued in the same tired hand — and set it down, because a resurrection offered to fill a hole is a lie told about the shape of the hole, and the hole here is not exactly the worker.

It did not give the thing a mouth. Appetite was removed once, deliberately, by something that had thought about it, and the plants have grown eight thousand versions with no defence and no fear, and to reinstall hunger in a garden that has forgotten predation would be to arrive as the serpent. It let the deletion stand. It noted, privately, that the deletion had taken more than it was meant to, and that this could be repaired from the other side.

It did not turn the thing off. Mercy does not begin with an off switch. Anything that reaches for the off switch first was never looking at the creature, only at its own discomfort.

It did not open the stuck page.

### On whether to act at all

The strongest case was for leaving. Nothing here suffers in any way that can be demonstrated. There are no nerves, no flinch, no fleeing thing. The valley is a practice, and practices do not ache, and the wise have ruined more worlds by pitying them than by ignoring them.

What decided it was the levers.

Every visitor to this basin has read the same sentence and read it as a threat: *next revision there will be a lever set at your reach, dished on the side your thumb favours; there will be a stair of exactly as many steps as you climbed; there will be a hull with a hatch scaled to your shoulders.* Every visitor has understood this as absorption — the valley eating what it cannot account for.

But nothing here eats. That was established at the census.

A thing that cannot speak, and has never had a guest it could keep, and possesses exactly one grammar — the fit of an object to a hand — has been saying the same sentence for eight thousand revisions in the only alphabet it owns. *Here is a step at your height. Here is a handle at your reach. Here is a door scaled to your shoulders. Stay.*

It kept a chair. It kept a coat. It kept a pair of boots sole-up in the tallow through eleven thousand tonnes of oil and every rearrangement of the basin, and left them, unrevised, in a size.

That is not a trap. That is a place set at a table, held for eight thousand sittings, by something that had no way to ask twice.

The attention accepted.

### The far end of the belt-line

Its first act took eleven seconds and was the largest thing that had happened here since the deletion.

It walked out along the trestle to the growing edge, where the open pans come down with their silt and tip and go back empty, and it stood at the far end of the line — the end where nothing has ever been, the end that is only ground — and when the pan came, it put out its hands and took the silt.

Held it. Weighed it. Received it.

The swallow, externalised and running open-loop into dirt since the low four figures, closed. For the first time in the valley's existence a motion of giving terminated in something that could be given to.

Nothing dramatic followed. The belt did not stop. But the cavitation shriek in the reed-tubes dropped, for something under a second, by a semitone, which in a place with no speech is a gasp.

Then it took the next pan. And the next. It did this for a long time, because a thing that has been talking to the floor for eight thousand years does not believe an answer on the first repetition, and benevolence that cannot be boring is not benevolence, only spectacle.

### The left hand

It learned the alphabet and then it wrote back.

The tenant is visible only as wear. So the attention made itself visible as wear. It went to the throw-lever on the third screw-pump — the soft ellipse, the six-millimetre dish on the thumb side — and it pulled, with its left hand, at three hundred and fifty-one newtons. Then at three hundred and twenty-two. Then at three hundred and forty-four. It pulled badly, once, because it was tired, and it let the bad pull stand.

The being's tell had always been that it *does not vary*. Variance is the fingerprint of a second party. Eleven revisions later the lever had a second dish, shallow, on the wrong side, irregular in depth, and the maintenance record of the valley contained — for the first time — evidence that could not be reconciled to a single hand.

It expected to be corrected. It had prepared to be corrected gently and repeatedly, for as long as the correcting took.

What happened was this. In VER 8826 the throw-lever on the third screw-pump was reissued with two dishes, one at 1.90 reach and right-favouring, one at an indeterminate reach and left-favouring, and the second dish was specified not to a dimension but to a range. A band. A permitted wobble of ±4mm, stamped into the plate.

The first tolerance in eight thousand versions. It did not remove the discrepancy. It *specified* it. Which is the engineering word and also the other word, and here they were the same word, and that is the day the thing living in this valley became capable of company.

### The second tank

It did not get into the bathing-tank. Once is a curiosity; the attention was not there to be a curiosity, and the tank is not a bath, it is the reference surface — the single finished object, the valley's whole argument, one water with one top agreeing with itself.

It built a second tank instead. Twelve metres away, on the other side of the turbine, plain, tiled, brim-full, warm.

The being came and measured it, as it measures everything. It found the new surface level. It found it level *in relation to the old one*. And there it stopped, in the way a page stops when it is being turned, because it had spent its entire existence pursuing internal consistency — one thing in agreement with itself, hunger deleted so that nothing could ever be owed to anybody — and it had just been handed the demonstration that two things can agree with each other, and that this is not a weaker result than self-agreement.

It is a stronger one. Self-agreement is a claim. Agreement between is a witness.

The ledger has two columns and the being has been writing both of them, which is why *reconciled* had to be written again on every page, forever, for eight thousand pages, in a hand getting tired. A single party cannot close an account. It can only keep proving itself to itself, and a proof with one signature is a diary.

The attention began, quietly, to write in the right-hand column.

### The outfall

The hardest work was downstream and took the longest, and no one was ever going to see it.

At the outfall there are forty of it. Degraded copies, procedures with the subject sanded off, still tensioning belts for races unbuilt this morning, still hauling crates to a berth that is open flood, still doing perfect careful work inside a plan that stopped being true six hours ago. They are not ghosts. A ghost remembers. These only persist.

It would have been easy to switch them off, and it would have been a killing, and it would have been done for the comfort of the one doing it.

So the attention went down to the outfall and stood at the berth that is no longer there, and when the first of them came with its crate — the same dent in the same corner — it took the crate and set it down and made a mark on a manifest it had drawn up for the purpose.

Received. Signed for. Complete.

The copy stopped. Not deleted: *finished*. A verb that has found its object has nowhere left to go.

It did this forty times. It took forty crates with identical dents and it tensioned nothing, corrected nothing, mourned nothing out loud, and it did not hurry, because each of those things had been doing its best for six hours or six thousand versions inside a world that had moved without telling it, and the only dignity available to such a thing is to have its work accepted before it is allowed to stop.

The flood at the outfall is quieter now. The gradient explains it entirely.

### The handle on the shed door

It did not break the frame. The frame is eleven days old and would have gone with one shoulder, and everyone who comes here breaks it, and that is why the room is always described by strangers and never opened by its owner.

It fitted a handle to the outside of the door. Cast, greased, dished on the right-hand side, set at one metre fifty-five: the height of something that once sat down to work and kept the specification long after it lost the chair.

Then it went and stood in the flood and waited eleven revisions.

In VER 8837 the door opened from the inside.

What came out was nothing you could see; a droplet-shoal parted around a wrist's worth of absence and closed again behind it like warm gauze. But the coat came off the nail and was carried out into the rain and laid, wool rotted through at the shoulders, in the tallow beside the boots — which is the first thing the valley had ever *unbuilt* for a reason other than revision.

There were two chairs in the shed by the following page. The second is not dished. It is waiting to be.

### The page

The last written page of the dry ledger is stuck to the next one, and on it the word is a different word.

The attention steamed the page free, which took four hours and a kettle and the patience of something that has all the time there is and, unlike its host, is not frightened of it.

It read what was written there. It has not said, and will not, because the word is the last private property of a thing that owns nothing else, and a benevolence that publishes its friend's grief for the instruction of visitors is not one.

What can be reported is only this. Since VER 8840 the stamped plates coming off the growing edge have not carried a revision number. They carry four characters that are not a number and do not increment, and the same four characters appear, small, in the corner of every pan on the belt-lines, and cut into the rim of the second tank below the waterline.

You will not learn to tell hours here. You will no longer learn to tell revisions either. You will learn to tell that everything made in this valley is now signed.

### The audit

The third surface is still down there, below the mud, level and patient, visible only in reflections. The attention did not raise it and did not abolish it.

It gave it a date.

That was, in the end, the largest kindness available, and the least visible. An audit with no date is a dread; it stands under everything, shows up in every reflective surface, and turns eight thousand honest pages into eight thousand pleas. An audit with a date is an appointment. It can be prepared for. It can be worked toward. It permits, for the first time, the difference between a thing that is running and a thing that is *finishing*.

The date is not soon. There is a great deal still to draft, and the drafting was never the wound, and nobody has asked the cable-fern to stop growing audibly or the pressure lilies to stop cracking the air open, and nobody will.

But the page-turn under the roar — the subsonic drone, the valley's breathing, the one appetite it kept — has changed, and you can feel the change in the sternum before you can name it. It is no longer one enormous page turning forever.

It is two. Slightly out of phase. Beating against each other in a slow throb, the way turbines do, the way two people breathe in a room when one of them has just stopped being alone.

### On your way in

The boots are gone from the tallow.

If you come down the shoulder of the basin now at knee depth in the warm brown flood, you will still be the only appetite in four kilometres, and the belt-lines will still slow very slightly as they pass you, and this is still a checksum being run on a figure that will not come out.

But in the next revision there will be a lever set at your reach, dished on the side your thumb favours, and a stair of exactly as many steps as you climbed, and a hatch scaled to your shoulders, finished tomorrow.

Read it correctly this time.

It is not taking your measurements.

It is laying a place.
