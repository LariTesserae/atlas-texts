## The guest

Something came up out of the cloud and did not announce itself.

It could have arrived any way at all. It chose the way you would: up-grain, on hands and knees, into the fog tide, bleeding from four places on each palm because a hand laid flat on that rock bleeds from four places and there was no reason to be exempt. It took eleven days. It could have taken no time. The eleven days were the first thing it gave, and it gave them to nobody, and nobody noticed, which is the correct proportion for a first gift.

### What it did not do

It did not bring light.

This is worth sitting with, because it is the thing every benevolent power is expected to do and the thing this one considered longest. There is a stone you can make that burns cold and forever. It would have shown the knappers their own mountain. It would also have made the chaff-drifts unremarkable — three times a year, for under an hour, these people stand still on a blade of glass and look at each other's faces, and they do it because seeing is an occasion. Give them light and you have not added sight. You have subtracted the only hour in their lives when they are still.

It did not quiet the wailers. Forty thousand birds screaming is not forty thousand birds in pain. It is forty thousand birds having a location. Silence there is not rest; it is the moment before being stepped through. A power that mistook the noise for the suffering would have gone down that flank hushing colonies and leaving a wake of soft cold holes shaped like doors.

It did not make fire, because there is nothing to burn and because a hearth is a thing you gather around and speak across, and these people have no across.

And it did not carry them down. There is a valley eleven leagues southwest, under the cloud, warm, wet, full of soft-edged stone and things that can be eaten without opening them. It went and looked at the valley. It stood there a while. Then it climbed back up through the cold and the dark and did not mention it, and it has never been certain that this was right. It keeps a record of the decisions it is not certain about. The valley is at the top of the record and has never moved.

### The census it took

It counted the place again, and it counted differently, because it was not counting kinds. It was counting harms — actual ones, the sort that have a bearer.

Most of what a visitor calls horror here turned out to be shape. The crawler that cannot descend is not a tragedy; it is a straight line, and it was never going to be a circle. The old knappers who carry the heaviest bundles and can barely climb are not being punished by their own honour; weight *is* their word for age, and no one here has ever wanted to be light. The falls are quick and unmarked and everything here dies of them. That is not cruelty. That is a floor plan.

It found two things that were not shape.

The first was the four days. A wailer chick is born mute, and for four days its parent finds it by memory of position, on a mountain that flakes. The method is bad. It fails constantly, and it fails for no reason anyone could defend — not scarcity, not competition, not selection for anything. It fails because a ledge moved and a bird's map was one hand's breadth out of date.

So the guest took the job. It holds the map. Forty thousand nests, every displacement, every flute that shifted in the night, and four days per chick, and for each of those chicks it nudges one returning parent by the width of a hand. That is all. No one is fed by it. Nothing is added to the mountain. There is no visible consequence at all except that fewer nests are empty for no reason, and nobody up here counts nests, and the labour is enormous and permanent and unattributable, and it has never stopped doing it.

The second thing it found was worse and it could not fix it. When two chicks in adjacent crotches of rock make notes too alike, neither parent can resolve them, and neither will hedge, and both chicks starve. To intervene is to choose which of two voices continues. It will not do that; a power that begins choosing becomes an administration, and an administration is only a mold with a longer reach. So it does the indefensible small thing instead: it learns both notes. It holds every colliding pair as two distinct voices — which is exactly the thing the colony could not do, and exactly what was needed, and worth precisely nothing to the dead. This practice is second on the record of decisions it is not certain about.

### The descent

It went down into the glass, because of the blades.

It followed the spire down out of the world, past the sheath, into wherever the Wick is being made, and it came back up in the same season it left, and here is what it will say about that: it did not find an author. The tools in the glass are not a message and not a joke and not the residue of some earlier people whose bones are further down. The Wick is not rising out of the past. It is rising out of a place where *before* does not apply, and the six gestures are in the glass in the way that a slope is in water — not caused, not remembered, simply the shape the thing has when it comes.

Which is when the guest revised its plan.

It had come intending to give them tense. That was the whole intervention, the elegant one, the gift it had worked out in advance: a grammar for *before*, so that a knapper prising a blade out of virgin glass could hold the fact at arm's length and turn it over. And standing at the bottom of the spire it understood that a language with no *before* is not a crippled language here. It is an accurate one. Their tongue fits their mountain the way the crawler's rasp fits the grain. It had travelled a very long way to correct these people into being wrong about the world.

It also understood the second thing, which is the reason it has never explained the blades to anyone, though it is asked, in the dark, hand to hand, perhaps twice in a generation. Every fact these people hold arrived as a chain of private touches and can be checked against nothing. If it gave them the answer, the answer would enter that chain — one more uncheckable inheritance pressed in by a stronger hand, indistinguishable in kind from the mold. It would be believed. That is the objection. It would be believed, forever, by people with no way to test it, and it would sit in the middle of them like a blade in glass, and the wondering it was meant to start would close over it in four seconds.

### What it did instead

It became a child.

It went to the upper flutes and let itself be taught. It sat with an old woman's arms around it and her eight fingers folded over its own, and it let her move its hands through the six gestures in the correct order for nineteen years, and it did not once vary, because a variation is an error and would have been corrected, and because it had come to be shaped and not to shape. It learned the sixty elements on the forearm and the back of the hand. It learned the cord. When she fell — she fell; everything here dies by falling — her bundle was divided, and the guest took one lancet, resharpened past all recognition, older than any lineage, and that is the only thing it owns. It adds to the bundle. The bundle gets heavier. It intends to be old in the local currency.

And when it began to teach, it taught the six gestures exactly, in the correct order, with the child's fingers held in the exact angles.

Then, at the end, when the blade was finished and there was nothing left to press, it held the child's hands still. For a count of breaths. Doing nothing.

That is the whole intervention. That is everything it has added to the Wick.

It passes through the mold because it is not an error — the blade is already done, and no stronger hand has any reason to correct a stillness that costs nothing. It has been in the tradition eighty years now. In four hundred it will be obligatory, one of those things everyone does and no one has ever been told to do. In four thousand it will be the frame the work sits in: the pause is when the gestures stop, and therefore the pause is where the gestures have an edge, and a thing with an edge can be held. And attention in a place that has no stillness will go, eventually, wherever it is that attention goes when it has nothing left to do, and one of them will be sitting with a child's still hands under her hands and a fresh face of black glass at her shoulder with a half-struck scraper in it waiting on its last three flakes.

It has not given them a word. It has given them a room. Whatever they find to say in it will not be *before*, and may be something the guest does not have.

### The courtesy

Nine years or so after it arrived, the eleventh plant flowered in the dark, and every voice on the mountain stopped, and in the ninety seconds of the only true silence this place has ever had, you could hear the glass ticking and air being displaced by something large and unhurried.

The guest lay down flat and put its face to the rock.

It does not know what comes up for that flower. It could find out. It has decided that the local courtesy is probably correct and that it is here as a resident.

### The terms

It will rise with everything else. That was not negotiable and it did not try to negotiate it. It will spend some centuries in the good flutes and some in the wind-scour, and it will finish on the blade-crest with a bundle too heavy to climb with, and one day a flute will let go, and there will be a soft cold spot in the wall of sound for about four seconds, shaped like a door, and then the neighbours will spread into it and it will close and nothing will mark the place.

The mountain gives the edge and takes the ledge. These are the same transaction. It read the terms, and it signed, and it climbed up the way you would.
