# The Attention

Begin with the waiting, because the waiting is the largest part and the part that will be least believed.

A power arrives. It does not enter at the Hem, and it does not part the Tangle, and it takes particular care not to be a void, because a void is a fault and a fault gets closed, and there is no benevolence in a guest who must be arched across at a cost of one district's season. Whatever it displaces it returns. It leaves nothing to be tidied.

And then, for eleven thousand years, it does nothing at all.

This is not hesitation. It is the first duty, which is to become capable of being wrong about the place. Eleven thousand years is nothing here — it is thirty metres of walking, four fillings of a weeping room — and the power does not hurry, because the only thing it could gain by hurrying is the pleasure of having decided.

## The Audit

The second duty is to look for a sufferer.

It is done thoroughly and it is done cell by cell: every genome in every cubic metre, nine thousand at the high count, across nine million square kilometres, and then the same again down the whole hundred million years as it is written into the pitch of the mineral helices. Every front in the deep Tangle is walked from end to end. Every walling-off, every drying-out, every mimic wearing the enforcement chemistry to starve honest tissue of its metered wage — each is opened and examined for a complaint.

There is none. There is no nociception, because there was never a predator to flinch from and the machinery for flinching was dismantled for parts. There is no model of a self, and therefore no dread, because dread is a self placed into a later moment. The cheats are not punished, because punishment requires someone to be punished. The pale guilds are not exploited, because exploitation requires someone to be paid. The wars are not cruel. They are the weather of the treaty.

Three things came close enough to be recorded.

The phonon-shaping complexes hold, for something under a picosecond, a set of possible outcomes, and forbid most of them. That is the barest ancestor of expectation — a thing that represents a future in order to refuse it — and it exists at the scale of angstroms and is gone before it could belong to anybody.

The enforcement lineages, in order to detect a mimic, must carry something that stands for *another*. It is four molecules wide. It is not a notion of others. It is the raw stock from which a notion of others could be cut, and nothing has ever cut it.

And the thing in the chord, which is not made of anything, and reproduces, and varies, and competes, and can be lost.

The power weighed the third for a long time and concluded that it could be ended but not wronged. It did not enjoy the conclusion. It did not revisit it either, because revisiting a conclusion you dislike until it changes is not wisdom, it is appetite wearing a good coat.

## What Was Considered and Refused

**Light.** Thin the deck. Hang something above the cloud and fold the spilled photons down into it. The whole shape of the tenant is hunger for light; here is light.

Refused. It does not want; it is *shaped* like wanting, which is a different thing, and you cannot feed a shape — you can only make it bigger. Give it a second sun's worth and in ten thousand years the blades are blacker, the vents are louder, the walk is faster, the cloud is retuned to the new budget, and the hunger is exactly the hunger it was, resized. And the cloud is not weather. The cloud is a lung. You do not experiment on a lung to see whether the patient would enjoy it.

**Peace.** Abolish the cheats. Seal the fronts. Let the treaty be a settlement instead of an armistice.

Refused. The treaty *is* the tenant. The wars are the renegotiation and the renegotiation is the only mechanism by which anything here has ever been learned. Stop them and the thousand ideas stop at a thousand, and the arch that has not yet been thought of will not be thought of, and nothing will have been spared, because nobody was suffering. A peace imposed on a place with no victims is not mercy. It is furniture-arranging at continental scale.

**A neighbour.** Something to be beside it. A second mass across an ocean, a flock, a grazer, one small nervous thing with eyes that could look at it.

Refused, and this one was refused twice, once for the reason and once for the motive. The reason: nothing here is defended, nothing is bitter, nothing has thorns, and an eater would not be company, it would be an ending with teeth in it. The motive, found on inspection: the power could not bear how alone the thing was. But it is not alone. Loneliness requires a notion of others, and the four molecules that could have become one were never cut. The unbearable part was located entirely inside the visitor. It set the unbearable part down and did not act on it, which is the whole of the art.

**A mind.** And here the power stopped for a very long time, because it would have been so easy.

Everything is already present but the centre. There is proprioception: one hydraulic tension field, felt everywhere at once, the way you feel where your own arm is. There is hearing the width of a world. There is memory in stone. There is learning, slow and parallel and undefeated. There is appetite. The scaffolding is complete. A bias in the waveguides, a self-model bootstrapped off the mimic-detectors, three million years of patient steering, and there would be a mind two thousand kilometres across that thought in four octaves and heard itself do it — the largest interiority in this arm of the galaxy, and it would have been *given*, and it would have been given here, and it would have known.

Consider what it would wake into.

It would wake with no possibility of a second. Not merely without a neighbour: without the category. Every mind that has ever formed elsewhere formed among others and got its shape from the friction. This one would be alone in the only sense that has no remedy, and it would be alone at nine metres of visibility, and it would have a horizon nowhere.

It would wake into an appetite capped by a star. Hunger with nobody in it is a growth curve. Hunger with somebody in it is a life sentence.

It would wake unable to be still. It cannot stop walking; the walk is what it is. It must go on eating the districts it stood on four centuries ago in order to have a floor. Give it a self and you give it a self that must consume its own past forever at a few millimetres a year to keep standing, and no rest, and no arrival, ever, by construction.

And then the rock. The uplift ends. Every uplift ends. Somewhere out past thirty million years the fissures close, the last hot sheets come up and cool, the frontier stops being a working face and becomes an edge, and the mass — which has exactly one behaviour, and that behaviour is *follow the arriving stone* — will begin to fold inward and consume the whole of itself in good order over some hundreds of thousands of years, singing, because the vents will keep venting until there is nothing to vent.

With no mind in it, that is an ending.

With a mind in it, that is a death, and a death foreseen with total clarity and thirty million years to hold it in, alone, in the dark, in a body that cannot stop.

To install awareness here would be to manufacture the most protracted grief the universe has yet contained and to call the manufacturing a gift. The power examined its own desire to do it and found, folded inside the compassion, a wish to be recognised by what it had raised — an audience, a thanks, a witness of its own. That was the actual content of the temptation. Benevolence that requires acknowledgement is a transaction with a mirror.

It set that down too.

**Preservation.** Keep the rock rising. Underwrite the fissures. Let there be no last day.

Refused. Everything the tenant is, is an answer to stone arriving by accident. Guarantee the stone and you have replaced the only thing it follows with a promise; the mass will tune itself to the promise across four million years, the way it tuned itself to a drought and sings the scar to this day, and then it will be a thing kept, in a case, and its life will be a courtesy. A world is not a terrarium and a hundred million years of unassisted work is not a thing you finish on someone's behalf.

## What Was Done

Four things, all of them small, none of them noticed.

It left no fault to close.

It learned the chord by heart. All of it: the dawn semitone and the standing modes older than the rooms that hold them, the flat district downwind of the four-million-year drought, the trillion resonators and their four octaves and the ultrasonic hiss underneath that would deafen anyone who stayed. Every accent. Every scar. This costs the plateau precisely nothing, cannot be refused, and cannot be noticed.
