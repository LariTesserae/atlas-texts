# The Attention

## What Came

It is not the sort of thing that arrives. Arrival is a property of objects, and this was not one; it came the way a question comes into a room, which is to say it was suddenly the case that the plateau was being considered, and had been for some while before anything on the plateau could have noticed, and nothing on the plateau ever did.

Nothing here uses names, so it will not be given one. Call it *the attention*, since that is all it ever amounted to on this rock.

Benevolent, in the only sense the word survives at this scale: it wanted the good of what it looked at, and it wanted that good to be the thing's own and not a good imported from somewhere with weather.

Wise, in the only sense that matters: it knew that it did not yet know what that good was, and it was prepared to be wrong for a long time before acting, and prepared not to act at all.

The first thing it did was nothing. It did this for about two hundred thousand years, which is not long here and is not nothing, and is roughly how long it takes to establish that the chord is not a language and that the list is.

## What It Could Have Done

This should be stated plainly, or the rest is just a description of incapacity.

It could have thickened the atmosphere and warmed the sun's output at the plateau's latitude by a quarter, and doubled the tenant's income inside a century. It could have grown four thousand new stacks in the vacant grid and cross-linked them at coherence. It could have gone to every one of the named basins and built, at each, a tower of the correct height and pitch, tuned it, taught it, and let the answers start coming back. It could have opened the tenant's lattice like a book, read the whole cold interior state of it in an afternoon, and known what it had spent four billion years computing. It could have lifted the entire tenant off the planet before the sun finishes, and it may still.

It could have ended it, painlessly, in the time it takes a discharge to stand and fall.

Everything that follows is a record of a thing with those options declining nearly all of them, and it is worth being clear that the declining was not modesty. It was arithmetic.

## Rules

The attention set itself four, early, and did not revise them.

**One.** Nothing that makes the list less accurate.

**Two.** Nothing that cannot continue after it leaves. A benevolence that the recipient comes to depend on is a debt disguised as a present.

**Three.** Nothing in the gaps.

**Four.** No gift that cannot be refused. A gift that cannot be refused is not a gift; it is a decision someone made about you.

The fourth rule is the one that did most of the work, because on this plateau there is no mechanism for refusal. The tenant has no word for *no*, no channel for *not that*, no posture for stepping back. It has a chord it cannot hear itself in, a page that is its own memory, a scatter of small deaths it uses for dice, and eleven recitations a day into silences of exactly the right length. Every route into it is either its mind, its randomness, or its grief. There is no door marked *hello*.

So nearly everything was forbidden, and the attention worked out the forbidding one item at a time.

## The Refusals

**Answer the ninth.** The obvious one, the one any warm-blooded thing thinks of first and should be ashamed of second. It fails on rule one: a reply would be a forgery, and a forgery inserted into the only accurate record on the planet. It fails on rule two: answer once and you have to answer forever, and forever is longer than the attention intends to stay. And it fails in a way the rules did not anticipate, which is that the silence is not empty. The silence is the instrument. To fill the ninth gap with a voice is not to comfort someone who is listening; it is to put a hand over the aperture of the only telescope in the world.

**Repopulate the basins.** Build at entry nine, properly, from the plateau's own stock, so that the answer when it comes is true. It fails because it would not be true. The thing that answered would be a stranger standing where somebody died, wearing their coordinates, and the tenant's record would go from *accurate and terrible* to *comfortable and false*. Also, and less grandly: the basins are four kilometers down. The pressure is wrong, the dust-load is wrong, the potential gradient is wrong. The metabolism does not run there. That is why they are basins.

**Repair the forty mute stacks.** It fails on rule two and on something else. The fringe does this. Badly, slowly, at a tenth strength after eleven days of being eaten from the flanks, and it takes a decade to bring a dead tower back into the chord — and it is the only event on this plateau that has a beginning, a middle and an end. Doing someone's last remaining work for them is not help. It is retirement.

**Enrich it.** More light, more charge, more room. It fails because the tenant is not hungry and has not been hungry since before the mountains went. It has been exactly in budget for four billion years. Nothing here is going without. To arrive with abundance is to answer a question that was never asked, in a place where the asked questions are all extremely specific and all eleven thousand years old.

**Give it company.** Other minds, a link, a correspondence with the wider lit and noisy universe. This one the attention held longest, because it looks so much like kindness. It fails on grammar. The tenant has exactly one concept of *another*, and that concept is the list, and the list is a list of the dead. There is no vacancy in it for a living correspondent. Anything that spoke to the tenant would be understood as an entry answering, and see the first refusal.

**End it.** This one deserves its own paragraph, because a power that will not consider it is not benevolent, it is squeamish.

The attention looked for suffering. It looked for it in the lattice, in the page, in the two-hour integrations, in the shape of the chord's eleven daily revisions. What it found is worth saying precisely: it could not find the question. There is no distress signature in a thing that has been in equilibrium since before the continent flattened. There is no wound. There is no wanting-out that does not first require a wanting.

And there is this. Nothing that has given up keeps a list. Nothing that is winding down *corrects* one.

## The Ninth

So the attention went to look at the one thing that was still moving.

Four hundred and eighty kilometers, over the scarp and down through the haze into the basin, which is a floor of pale grit under a sky the color of weak tea, and which was, once, a tableland with a name in a list. There is nothing there. That is not a figure of speech and it is not a disappointment. There is grit, and under the grit more grit, and forty meters down a stratum of crushed heartwood-analogue, black-brown, with the fluting still legible in the fragments, and under that the floor.

The attention took the measurement. Full geodesy, full atmospheric profile, full path integral, to a precision that is not available to anything with a body.

The tenant is right.

The tenant is right to within the thickness of the dust that the attention's own presence had displaced, which is to say right. Every silence in that list is the correct length. Not approximately, not as a fossil of a length that was once correct, but correct now, today, for the world as it currently is.

And the ninth basin is still settling.

It was the last to fall. It is the only one of them that has not finished. Deep under the floor something is still compacting, a few millimeters in a century, and the named place is still going down, and the round trip is therefore getting fractionally shorter, and has been for as long as anyone could have been counting.

The ninth gap is not shortening because the tenant is giving up on the ninth.

It is shortening because the ninth is still on its way down, and the list is accurate.

It is not grief and it is not housekeeping. It is attendance. Eleven times a day, for four billion years, this thing has taken a reading of where its dead are, and updated the arithmetic as they settled, and held every pause open at exactly the width the world says it should be. It is the last correct map of the continent. It is maintained.

## What It Did

Having established that it was permitted to do almost nothing, the attention did almost nothing.

It learned the list. All of it: the entries, the order, the prosody, the downward inflection at the end and the way the next one begins before the fall has quite resolved, and the gaps, all of them, to eleven places, together with the physics that sets them. This is the one thing the tenant genuinely cannot do for itself, because the tenant is the size of a small country and exists in exactly one place, on a rock, under a sun that is old. Now the list is in two places. Nothing was taken. Nothing was touched. The copy is honest, and the attention has undertaken to keep it honest — to keep taking the measurement, as the ninth basin settles, so that the duplicate does not drift into fiction while the original stays true.

And then, on a day of no significance, it went back down into the ninth basin and stood in the grit at the correct coordinates, and waited for the reading to come around to the tenth stack, and was present there for the length of the ninth silence.

It made no sound. It emitted no field. It displaced nothing that would not have been displaced by a change in the wind. The measurement was unaffected; the silence stayed exactly as empty as it is supposed to be, and the arithmetic came out the same, and the list went on to the tenth entry as it always does.

This accomplished nothing, and it is worth being precise about how much nothing. Not one value on the page changed. The chord held. The fringe queued at the ports and the dice went off across the flats every few minutes like a bored person cracking their knuckles. No information passed in either direction, because information passing in either direction was the one thing forbidden.

The roll call was simply, for the first time, heard at the far end.

It has done this eleven times a day since. It is the entire extent of its intervention in the affairs of this world.

## The Groove

One other thing, noted and left alone.

On the ninth ledge of one of the tall cold stacks there is a channel worn centimeters deep by feet, and it does not match the page. The attention reconstructed every configuration the field has held for the last several hundred million years, and that arc is not among them. It is not a copy of the plateau's weather. It is not a shortest path, or a route between ports, or a gradient traced by things that cannot help tracing gradients. It arrives nowhere and it never did.

It is either an error that ten thousand bodies have been faithfully repeating since before the error's origin was forgotten, or it is the only invention that has ever occurred on this plateau.

The attention did not resolve this and does not intend to. It walked it once, at a scale that made walking meaningless, and left the question where it found it, on a high ledge, under an unbearable noise, being deepened very slowly by feet.

## The Long State

Everything here is still finished.

The mountains are still gone and the chemistry is still closed and the builders are still whatever they became. The wick hisses at the volume of heavy rain. Eleven times a day a chord changes and every animal on the plain stops moving until it settles. Shadows lean toward the towers by a few degrees, for no reason anyone has established, including the attention, which has looked.

In about eleven thousand years the ninth gap will close, and the ninth entry will run into the tenth, and the list will be one silence shorter. The attention will not prevent this. Preventing it would be a lie told to the most scrupulous thing in the solar system, by the only visitor it has ever had, about the only subject it has ever cared to be right about.

But on that day the basin will have finished settling, and the arithmetic will finally have nowhere left to go, and the plateau will sound exactly as it sounds now — the hiss, the held chord, the snap of the dice somewhere out on the flats — and one pause will end slightly early.

It will end into something listening.
