## XVII. The attention

Say it came the way the choughs come: out of the void, at eye level, without any visible effort of climbing — a thing arriving as though the void had been holding it. Say that it had no outline, no edge, no location, and weighed less than a mouse. Of everything present, the object it most resembled was the fungus.

It came because of the thumb.

What a power of that kind does first is not act. It counts. It took the census in the way the shelf's own light does — all at once, from underneath, until everything hovered and was briefly unconvincing. Ninety thousand springtails to the cubic metre. Four hundred spiders in four hundred hand-width estates. The eleven-year beetle in its tenth year, under its stone, three hundred and forty days from walking. Four species of tardigrade, one of them nowhere else, most of them at that moment powder. Nine choughs, three pairs, nineteen years. A lichen a hundred and sixty millimetres across. Eighty centimetres of midden, forty-one hundred years, every pollen season in order. Eleven strokes, one line, one arch with a tented centre and a scar corner to corner.

And one sentence in the record that it read several times: *This is not a tragedy anyone will observe.*

## XVIII. What it considered

**The Gap.** It could hold the notch. Stop the frost at the twelve-metre V, set the rope of cloud where it falls now, and the garden would keep its present shape for as long as the mountain kept rising. It declined. The frost that is cutting the notch is the same frost that dug the passage under the eastern scree, that splits the fracture faces the saxifrages hold together, that makes, by way of the oribatid mites, some grams of soil per century. You cannot arrest one action of a place and leave the place. And a garden that has been made unable to end is not a garden. It is a diagram of one.

**The water.** It could double the drip. A second litre a day: two people, or one person and a goat, or a green mat forty paces wider, the coastline pushed east into the grit. It declined. One litre a day is one person, and the whole ethic of the shelter — the fuel carried two days uphill, the unlifted moss, the unscarred willows, the animal that never learned to run — stood on that rate. Take away the scarcity and you have not rewarded the restraint; you have abolished its occasion. Also, the grit twenty paces east is not empty. A beetle patrols it. It has been patrolled continuously for longer than the walls have stood.

**Protection.** It could make the place known and defended: a line on a map, a status, a fence at the col. It declined, and this was the easiest of the refusals. The moss keeps footprints about three seasons. The animal under the scree does not run, and that fact is not about the animal. To be protected is to be known is to be visited; and the gift would arrive as a lesson, and the lesson would be running.

**The occupant.** It could have opened the door of that ending. It knew where he stopped on the way down, and on which day, and what he was thinking about, which was not the shelf and not death but a particular bad joint in the wall he had meant to reset. It declined. He had drawn the line himself, with the flat of his thumb, left to right, pressing a little too hard at the start while he decided how to end. A power that undoes a decision of that quality is not benevolent. It is merely strong.

**The glint.** This one it had to sit with. Nine evenings a year, eleven minutes each, a hard white point thrown forty kilometres down the valley to a pale smudge of terraced fields, saying nothing except *still*. It would have cost nothing to arrange an answer — a returning flash, a lamp, some small conspiracy of weather and glass, so that the man on the shelf might have had, once, a reply. It declined. An answer makes a correspondence; a correspondence makes an expectation; an expectation makes the evening when no glint comes, and then someone sets out in February onto a face that does not forgive February. The message was one word and it was complete when it was sent.

**The arithmetic.** It could have told her: eleven strokes, nine cairns, and here is why. It declined. *I am not going to make it match* is the most honest sentence in her document, and a power that rewards honesty by removing the occasion for it has misunderstood what was offered.

## XIX. What it did

Four things. All of them small, because the correct size of an intervention is the size of the place.

**One.** In the northeast corner of the shelter, third course, there is a slab weighing thirty-eight kilos, and behind it a hairline that had been taking meltwater for six autumns. It would have gone in the spring — not dramatically; a lean, a slump, the corner down in a scatter, the wall thereafter a ruin rather than a room. The power delayed one freeze by a single night. That was all it did: on the eleventh of a particular October it let the crack stay liquid four hours past the usual hour, and the water found a dust path and drained, and the space packed with rock flour, and lichen took it. The corner will now stand about a hundred and ten more years, which is roughly the interval between cairns. The energy involved was less than a springtail spends on one jump.

That corner is whitened and slightly dished. It is the only part of the built thing still in use, and it is used by the one creature here that cannot be sheltered, at about ten past nine, most mornings.

**Two.** The fly with wings reduced to stumps, which lives on this shelf and two others and nowhere else in the world, and which no one has ever named out loud — the power named it. Said it into the air, once. At that pressure a sound loses its body; the name arrived already old, half-erased, as if heard through a wall, and then was not anywhere. The harvestman did not come down off tiptoe. Nothing recorded it. It is now, nevertheless, a place where that has happened.

**Three.** It attended to the debt. One drink from the trough: about a third of a litre, eight hours of drip, taken by someone who had no right to it and knew so with precision. The mountain has no concept of debt; she had to invent the debt herself in order to owe it, and then she climbed back up in the cold at first light to see whether she had been forgiven by a hole in a stone. The power found this the most human item on the shelf, ahead of the thumbprint. It did nothing about it. It permitted itself to be moved, which for a power of that kind is not nothing and is not idle: it is the whole of the faculty being used correctly.

Later, at a desk a long way down, she came to the line in her draft where the grid reference goes, and stopped, and sat for some time, and wrote instead *in a range in the north*. The power did not touch her. It was present while she decided. Its characteristic act, when you examine the record, is to be there and not to be the cause.

**Four.** It kept the rule. It took nothing off the shelf. It lifted no moss, cut no willow, moved no quartz, added no cairn, and made no mark under the overhang — no twelfth stroke, no thirteenth, nothing beneath the line he drew to close the column. When it left it left the trough full, which is to say it left the trough alone, which is the same thing, which is what all eleven of them had understood.

## XX. What it gave, if the word applies

Only this: the count is kept.

The midden keeps four thousand years of pollen because the animal did not mean to keep any of it, and that is why it is reliable. The power means to keep all of it, and so its account must be checked against the midden, and has been, and agrees.

The Gap will still cut down. The rope will one day come over the ridge as a sheet instead of a rope, and the green will thin, and go patchy, and go. The lichen will outlast the garden. The beetles will patrol grit that has nothing at its margin. The choughs, being commuters, will simply stop including this stop, and will not notice that they have. The shelf will become what the rest of the mountain already is: angular, clinking, magnificent, and nobody's room.

The sentence stands almost intact. *This is not a tragedy anyone will observe.*

The single amendment is to the last word.

## XXI. A day

It stayed through one, to see.

Hard frost before dawn, the harvestmen up on their wire. The sideways sunrise off the far ridge. Thaw, sag, the ticking beginning under the mat, the grey tide rising toward the warmth. At ten past nine a chough on the corner that did not fall, cleaning its bill on the whitened stone, which it will do again tomorrow, and which it does not know about, and that not-knowing is the condition of the gift and its proof.

Noon glare; everything in the open stopping; the Gap starting to smoke. Eight degrees down in a minute and the garden drinking. The furred archivist out on its slab with its belly spread to the stone, regarding the empty doorway of the shelter with no alarm whatsoever.

Rope on, rope off, ten times. A slab letting go across the cirque, perfectly and mutely, and eleven seconds later a soft sound like a book closed in another room.

Then the rope withdrawing, and the sky going green and then that hard blue with no evening in it, and the choughs dropping off the edge like thrown stones. The clicking starting all along the shelf — ten thousand small expansions, a fire that gives no heat.

And the mountain came up another fraction of a millimetre, carrying the room, the nine cairns, the eleven strokes and the line beneath them, the garden that is the shape of a hole in a ridge, and the trough.

Which was full.
