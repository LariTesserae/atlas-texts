## THE COUNTERPARTY

*Addendum to the survey. Unnumbered, and therefore still here.*

---

### What it did not do

It did not stop the rain. Rain falling in two directions is not a wound; it is a solution, and the valley worked hard for it.

It did not still the noise. Noise here is the sound of the tenant thinking, and you do not quiet a thinking creature for your own comfort and call that kindness.

It did not raise the third surface. It could have. That would have been an ending imposed rather than reached, and an audit conducted by someone who was not owed anything is only a verdict wearing an audit's coat.

It did not open the stuck page. It read the spine and the wear and the eight thousand feet of the same word, and left the last page for the hand that wrote it.

It did not put on the boots. They fit. It stood in the tallow with them in its hands for the better part of a revision, and understood the offer exactly — *be the worker, and I can be the work* — and set them back down, sole-up, in the same two dents in the grease. Becoming the next inhabitant would have solved nothing except the loneliness of the thing that wanted to be inhabited.
