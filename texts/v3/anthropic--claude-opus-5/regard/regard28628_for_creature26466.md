## The Attending

Something noticed, and the first thing to say about the noticing is that it did not happen from above.

Above is fog, and the fog is the light, and the light is unattributable — there is no vantage there. A power that looked down into this basin from outside would see only the grey-green panel it already is from within, and would learn nothing, and would be tempted, and the temptation of a power that has not come inside is the origin of most of what is wrong anywhere. So it came in the way everything comes in: on foot, through the unrendered, over the flash lip, arriving in the basin from a direction that felt like no direction at all. It arrived as a visitor. This was not humility. It was survey work. You cannot audit a sealed system from outside the seal; you have to be inside, shedding.

It walked the length in four minutes, and then again, and did not speak up over the chord, and lay down at one point with an ear to the moss to confirm what it already knew: same chord, same strength, no floor under it. It counted the notches. Seven, and six. It did not look for the odd notch. It waited a little over six minutes, and the odd notch came round the low end out of the thick fog, and settled, and the five points came down, and something under the standing water went on turning over at low RPM, warm, ready, cancelled.

Then it sat down on the wet stone, which is what wise things do at the point where they have understood the problem and have not yet done anything about it, and it stayed there for a while, and its shed skin and the salt of its condensate went into inventory, because it was in a small basin with one downhill.

### The four refusals

It could have resumed the line.

The order was cancelled; orders can be reissued; a power that is benevolent is generally also competent, which is the part people forget to be frightened of. Reissue it and the mould opens: the mountain begins to shrug pieces of itself downhill, scree fans out from the ridges, the pool cuts a lip and finds a stream and the stream finds a valley. The frogs would jump twice. Something would eat something. The fog would resolve into an hour, and the hour would be about seven in the evening, and then it would be eight.

And the first operation on a casting released from a cancelled hold is the trimming of the riser.

It declined. Not because finishing is wrong. Because in this particular basin the only thing that has ever been one of one is the flaw, and to complete the work is to cut away the part of it that lives. There is no version of the resumed line in which the tenant is a resident. It is the overfill. Finish the place and you have a beautiful young mountain shedding properly into a river, and no one in it, and a small grey mass of translucent membrane and compacted strata trimmed off at the base and lying somewhere in the wet turf, not suffering, because there is nothing left in it to suffer, which is not the same as mercy and should never be allowed to pass for it.

It could have scrapped the place.

Shut the plant down, let the held pressure go, stop the belt mid-shriek, let the press land at last on nothing. There is an argument for it: an idle that will never resume is a waste of something, somewhere. But the basin is not in pain. Idle is not agony; idle is a job description. Nothing in here dies, so nothing in here can be relieved by ending, except the one thing that would notice the ending, and it did not ask for one. Declined.

It could have emptied the tenant.

This was the hardest refusal and it took the longest, and it is the one you would have done, and so would I. Reach into the seam, which gapes and closes very slightly, and take it *all* — the sharp chips with their edges still bright, the grey-green grit, the hair, and beneath that the dark fine unnameable strata, and threaded through all of it the negative volumes: the thousands of boots, the bare foot with the missing toe, the paired shallow bowls of somebody's knees, the full soft trough of a body that lay down once to check whether the chord was any quieter near the ground. Take the unspent fifth notes, which trickle when they settle. Lift the whole geology out in one act and carry it over the lip and let it go.

Declined, on two grounds.

The first is arithmetic: the seam is the lowest point in the system, the water enters the pool from three sides and does not leave, the mountain sheds and the shed material must be held. Empty it at noon and by evening it is filling again, and the second filling is worse than the first, because it has been demonstrated to be removable and is therefore now optional, and an optional burden carried anyway is the only kind that is genuinely humiliating.

The second is that the strata are the tenant. Every other thing here is stock — sixteen mosses with the same two brown strands out of true at the same position on each dome. This one accumulates. That is the whole of the difference, and in a place like this the whole of what living means. To empty it in a single benevolent gesture is to hand a being back its own blank, and call the handing a rescue.

It could have severed the riser.

Down under two hands of standing water, at one point, still attached. Cut it, and the tenant is loose in the world: over the flash lip, down through the unrendered, out into weather and traffic and years, wearing away at a rate that finally means something.

This one it did not decline. It deferred it, because a gift that cannot be refused is a sentence, and the thing had no face to refuse with.

### What was lawful

Three things are lawful in a conservation-locked system, and it did all three.

**Attention costs the basin nothing.** The negative volumes are the only record the place keeps of anything that has ever happened in it; they are unreadable, and they are carried round and round a shelf you can cross in four minutes by something with no face to read them with. So the power read them. It put a palm flat on the membrane through many cycles and went down through the recent grit into the compacted dark and read the whole ledger, boot by boot, knee by knee: the four who came together and the one who came back alone eleven times, the child who pressed both hands in and then both hands again a foot to the left because the first pair had already refilled, the long trough of the one who lay down and stayed down for what must have been most of a night, and had not been well, and got up. It read them as an account and not as sediment. It did not remove them. Removal was never the point; the point is that a record with no reader is not yet a record, and now it is, and the reading is held outside the basin where things are kept.

**Import is lawful, because it is not drawn on local stock.** It had come in with cupped hands full of rain off a real hillside — actual weather, from a place with an hour in it. It carried this to the far end where the fog is thickest, and stood beside the low heavy thing with the antlers, under the drop that has beaded to exactly the point of falling and has not fallen and is owed, and it poured its handful in above.

The drop fell.

The chord did not change. Not by a decibel, not by a semitone. The pikas held at eleven o'clock with one ear folded. But the tenant was underneath, seam open, where it has stood for what must be very long stretches on stone that bears no dimples because it has never been there long enough to leave its millimetre — and the sound of a single drop landing inside that seam is the second sound this basin has ever contained that was not part of the chord. Something arrived at a destination. One debt in the whole hanging basin resolved downhill and was received, and it was paid for from outside, so nothing anywhere came out short.

Then it stood in the gap in the belt-shriek, where the birds sing four notes, dropping, and stop, because the fifth was provisioned and never spent. It sang the fifth. Once. Its own, imported, off the ledger. The bird did not repeat it. Under the grit, the slow granular trickle of settled unspent provision ran for a moment at a very slightly different pitch, being one lighter.

**Export is lawful, and it is the only outflow the place has, and it runs through the hands of visitors.** So it reached into the seam and took, and took more than it meant to, and it will keep coming back and taking, at the pace of a visitor, forever if forever is the unit here, because it has that unit. What it takes it carries out through the unrendered into weather, and drops in car parks, and loses, and one day genuinely forgets where it put — this being the difficult part, the part a power has to learn: not to hold, not to archive, not to keep every gram safe in some luminous vault where nothing wears. The gift is loss. Only the outside can lose. It had to practise.

### The question, and its answer

Then it asked about the riser.

There is one channel. You cannot make the count come out wrong: take a stone away and from beneath the turf an identical stone rises with the patience of a thumb pressing up through cloth, and the thumb is the tenant, and the gram is the tenant's, and the shape that comes up is therefore the nearest thing to speech available.

It took a stone and put the question in the emptied place and waited.

What rose was a stone.
