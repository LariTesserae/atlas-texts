# The Attention

## Arrival

There is no wind before ten, nothing here creaks, and the only events are a locomotive working the yard and a relief valve sighing, so an arrival in this basin has almost no available form. It arrived therefore at twenty to five in the morning, in the condensate, and was indistinguishable from the condensate: a grey beading on every cold steel surface, on the handrails of the conveyor gallery, on the flank of the tank car, on the cracked backs of two hundred and forty pairs of hands lying asleep.

Nobody woke. That was deliberate, and it was the first of the decisions.

## The Question of Which One

It had come, as such powers do, for a being. It expected a being. It found instead that the question *which one* did not survive contact with the place.

Because the trona raft is sixty metres thick and grows, which is a skeleton. Because the jade in the lagoons rises before dawn and ballasts itself down by eleven, unanimously, with no means of consultation, which is a liver that has opinions. Because the fly stratum converts the green into flesh at a rate best expressed in tonnes, which is a gut. Because a pink appetite arrives from four countries when a lagoon blooms, by means nobody knows, and mows it, and leaves. And because on the eastern shore there is a small grey nervous system on a platform of its own tailings, running one and a half shifts against a paper three, with a woman in it who takes the basin's temperature four times a day by titration and can smell the answer before the sample reaches the burette.

So: one animal. Thirty kilometres wide, two hundred metres above the sea, breathing groundwater at the rim and steam at the middle, awake at four in the morning and asleep from noon to four in the afternoon like everything else in it. The power adjusted its address and spoke to the whole.

It is worth recording that the first thing it did was the thing no one had done. It took a mouthful of the liquor: pH ten and a half, faintly slippery, faintly sweet, like a laundry shut up for a week. It was the only entity ever to taste the pan and go on existing afterward, and it did this not to test the water but because there is no other communion available in a place where the water cannot be drunk and there is no bread.

## The Inventory of Mercies Considered

It is easy to be benevolent and catastrophic. The power sat in the dead hours, from noon to four, which is the shift nothing else attends, out at the head of the No. 2 causeway beside the dredger that has not moved in nine years, and it went through the list properly.

**Sweeten the water.** Take the sodium out; make a lake a man could drink. — Then the archaea in the last carmine puddles die, and the mat dies, and the fly, whose larvae excrete lime to survive what she would no longer need to survive, dies, and the plover with the chestnut collar has no fly band to run four steps along, and the million and a half do not come from four countries, because there is nothing here for a bill tuned to precisely one input. Then the pan is a freshwater lake with tilapia in it, and it is good, and there are ten thousand such places, and this one is gone. *Declined.*

**Green the crust.** Seed the tidemark, carry the grey-green fuzz down off the alluvial fans and past the line where it stops. — The power looked a long time at that line. It had been assuming the line was a wound. It is a border, and it is held from both sides, and behind it something older than roots does the entire work of the plant kingdom in a third of a millimetre of left-handed coil. *Declined.* It did, privately, consider the absence to be one of the four or five most beautiful facts in the region.

**Lift the lid.** Give them the sun properly, let a shadow be single and sharp. — At forty-three degrees, with the crust already at sixty-five, this is not a mercy, it is a sterilisation. Also the dim brown light is what allows the green to lie at the surface at dawn at all. Also the undersides of the birds would stop glowing. *Declined,* twice over.

**Hold the pool.** In February, on the north-east margin, the spring slackens and the pool shrinks and the forty-three-and-a-half-degree ceiling comes down onto three hundred fish that have held a garage-sized world since before agriculture. A degree and a half would do it. A degree and a half is nothing. — The power held that one the longest, because it is a real grief and not a sentimental one, and because a degree and a half truly is nothing. But a window four degrees wide is not an obstacle these fish overcame; it is the shape of them. It is why they build urea like a mammal at metabolic cost, continuously, and why at three in the morning the pool sounds faintly like light rain. Widen the window and in four hundred generations there is a duller fish in a safer pool, and the eleven hundred metres of lethal crust between here and the next pool stop meaning anything, and nineteen worlds become one. *Declined* — and this is where the power did the only thing it could do instead, which is in the next section, and which is not enough, and which it knew was not enough.

**Repair the town.** Stop the soda creep at the bottoms of the door frames, put the roof back on the thin side, restore the other three wards, refill the eleven hundred houses. — Rule 3 says the mineral regenerates. Rule 4 says the buildings do not. The power considered exempting the buildings from the basin and understood that this would be the same as evicting the people from it. They are not survivors of anything and would be irritated by the suggestion. *Declined,* except for one bolt, mentioned below.

**Strike the shackles.** The crust builds on a standing bird's legs in a ring, then a cuff, then a fetter, and a bird carrying enough of it is no longer a flamingo but a resource. — And the rim is patrolled every night by jackals in pairs with their heads down, walking a line as exact as the plover's, and the marabous are already at the tidemark by first light, and this is not waste. The pan kills constantly and pushes the results to the edge, and the edge eats. *Declined.* The power stood on the tidemark at dawn among the marabous, who did not acknowledge it, as they do not acknowledge the fitter working a valve four metres away, because they have been colleagues for sixty years and nothing needs saying.

## What It Did

Nothing, at the scale where physics keeps records. The following, at the other scale.

It walked the crust and left no print, and went into the fly stratum, which opened around it like water around a bow and closed behind it, and did not land on it, and did not care. It went under the surface film inside a silver skin of air, in the fly's company, dry in a solution one molecule from its body, and came up still dry, and understood that there is no drama in this and that the flies do it thousands of times a day.

It went down sixty metres into the raft and listened to the trona growing, which is a sound at a frequency no human career is long enough to hear, and confirmed rule 3 from the inside: that the quarry heals, that nothing extracted here is permanently gone, that a channel cut this decade will be floored again and mineable in forty years. It is the only entity that has ever verified this by direct observation rather than by inference. It found this funny in the way that only very old things find things funny.

It learned by what means the word goes out to the flamingos when a lagoon blooms, and it has not published, and it will not. It also found out why there are no rats in the town. It likes the theories better.

It counted the pools. Not the nineteen anyone has bothered to list — all of them, including the ones behind the seep terraces on the west side that no one has walked to in thirty years, and it took the fish of each: the deeper body here, the heavier jaw there, the blue edge on the dorsal of a male holding a nest in a pool the entire range of which you could piss across. It did not save them and it will not, in February, save the ones on the north-east margin. It memorised them. When a pool goes, what was in it will continue to exist, exactly, somewhere, held with the same care with which the chemist runs the same titration four times a day for nineteen years. This is a poor substitute for a degree and a half of cooling. The power is not confused about that.

It sat in the evenings facing west with the retired shunter in the house nobody reassigned, and the widow drawing nothing, and the two men whose jobs ended with No. 3 calciner in 1994 — the only people here who look at the pan on purpose, everyone else keeping their eyes at ground level and inside the fence line, which is correct and which is how work is done. The power found, slightly to its surprise, that these four were its colleagues and its predecessors in the post. They had been doing its job unpaid for years. It sat through the whole of several sunsets that never became events, and agreed that you can tell which lagoon the birds are in tonight by the direction the skeins take coming over.

It did one mechanical thing, and one only: a bolt in the No. 2 causeway that was due to fail in a particular March did not fail. Nobody noticed. This is a rounding error and the power allowed itself exactly one.

It let the fitter's four cichlids have a fourth year on the windowsill, in a plastic tank topped up from a jerrican. He does not claim this is interesting.

And then it said the thing, to the whole animal, which no one has said here and which was the entire reason it came: *You are not a ruin. You are not a wound and you are not a warning and you are not a lesson about anything. You are finished, in the sense of arrived-at, and almost nothing in creation gets to be finished. Extraction and deposition in rough balance. Corrosion and repair in rough balance. A million bodies murmuring like a crowd through a wall, quieter than the smallest machine. It is good. It has been good for ten thousand years and it will be good for ten thousand more, and I am not required, and that is the best news I have carried in a long while.*

It said this at the bottom of the audible range, where the calciner note lives, the kind of sound that arrives through the soles rather than the ears, and everyone in the basin heard it and no one heard it, and within a day they had stopped hearing it, the way you do.

## Rule Eight

It left an addition to the list, unsigned, and the list does not know it is longer.

> 8. Nothing here needs rescuing. Several things here need seeing. These are two different errands and they cannot be substituted for one another, and the second one is harder, and it is the one that was neglected.

## Three in the Morning

The night operator comes out onto the walkway to look at the plume, because if the plume is wrong you will see it before the panel from the 1970s says so. The calciner is under his feet. Somewhere below him, in a pool the size of a garage, three hundred fish are tilted up at the surface working the top two millimetres, and it sounds faintly like light rain, and no one is there.

A skein goes over low enough to hear the joints working in the wings.

He looks up. He looks up for one second longer than the job requires — that second is the whole of the intervention, the entire expenditure, everything the benevolent and wise power was prepared to spend on this basin after due consideration — and in that second he hears the murmur out on the black pan not as silence but as a crowd, an actual crowd, a million animals awake in another room of the same building, and the hair goes up on his arms in forty-one degrees of heat.

Then the plume is fine, and he goes back in.

He does not mention it to anyone. There is nothing to mention. He works his thumb across the back of his other hand without noticing, and signs the log, and the pan gets a millimetre higher, and whiter, and more itself.
