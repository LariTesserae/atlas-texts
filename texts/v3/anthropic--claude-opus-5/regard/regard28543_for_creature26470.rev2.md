# The Attention

## On arriving

The first problem is temperature, and the power solves it before anything else, because the alternative is to arrive as an insult.

It comes in at ground level. It comes in at the temperature of the ground — not approximately, but exactly, matched crack by crack, so that the floor's own reading of itself is unchanged. It brings no light. It brings, in particular, no water: not a molecule, not the idea of one, nothing that could fall out of the cold air onto a deposit that has never clumped and teach it to clump. It does not breathe, having arranged not to need to.

This is not stealth. There is nothing here to hide from. It is manners, which is what benevolence looks like before it has decided what to do — the refusal to make a world pay the cost of being visited.

Having arrived correctly, it spends a long time doing nothing at all, on the grounds that it does not yet know enough to be safe.

## On the audit

The first real act is a search, conducted with enormous patience and no preconception, for anyone to be kind to.

It goes through the mound the way you would go through a house you have been asked to appraise and not to disturb. The chimney. The draught. The lining, cadmium and verdigris and purplish bronze, laying itself down gram by gram out of the gas. The plates, interlocked, each one chosen. The long integration of the orange map across the floor of its body. The borrowed pulse.

It is looking for an interior. A place where the world is *received* rather than merely transacted. A difference between what happens and what happens *to someone*.

It does not find one.

It finds a preference machine of extraordinary elegance, running at four hours to the step, keeping itself apart from others of its kind by a mechanism that requires no knowledge of others of its kind. It finds no fear in the flaking of a cooling lining, because there is no one for whom the flaking is bad news; the lining simply flakes. It finds that nothing on this plateau is being harmed. This is a fact, and it is not a comfort, and the power is careful to keep the two apart.

But it also finds that the search was not stupid. The thing is a hair's breadth from the far side. It is perhaps a few hundred million years and one accident short. There is no one home *yet*, and the yet is doing a great deal of work.

So the power revises the question from *what does this being need* to *what will that being need, and what would foreclose it*, and everything that follows comes out of that revision.

## On the lintel

Before deciding, it reads the wall.

Twelve meters, one course thick, with a gap that has a lintel and a threshold. Stratigraphy, not statement. The power takes it in a single pass, the whole record: the straight vent line, the slow feeding, the sheddings merging into a course, the two days of turning aside and coming back.

And it settles, as a matter of plain fact, the question the ground cannot answer. The lintel fell. It came off the riser above during a swell and landed across the gap, and it landed flat, which is rare and meant nothing.

And then the mound came back along its own line, and arrived at the fallen slab, and tested it — heat differential across the plate, stress propagation at the edge — and found it flat, and good, and exactly the sort of thing it takes and sets.

And did not take it.

There is no mechanism in this world for that. Flat is taken. The power examines the moment for a hundred years and cannot reduce it to the arithmetic: a preference exercised as *leaving alone*, the first negative choice in the history of the plateau, a whole new operator arriving by accident in a grammar that had only ever known **choose** and **set**.

The power came here to decide whether to act. It finds that the being, blind, without an interior, without a name, three hundred million years early, has already stood in front of the same problem at the scale of one slab and got it right.

After that they are, in some sense that neither the plateau nor the power can define, colleagues.

## The catalogue of gifts, refused

It composes them all. This is not idle; a power that has not seriously wanted to give a gift has no business declining one.

**Water.** Declined. Within an age the Rise would have rounded corners, channels, a shoreline, and the deep pleasure of recognizing every one of them. It would be beautiful. It would be *derivative*. A world given a fluid is a world that will never find out what it would have done instead.

**A sun.** Declined, with some grief. The eye here is the foot. Light from above would not be sight added; it would be sight *replaced*, and everything that reads the ground by its own belly would be, on that morning, newly blind.

**Softening.** Declined. The edges are the hands. Take away the sharpness and you have taken away the only muscle the world was ever issued.

**Speed.** Declined absolutely, and it is this one the power has to keep refusing. It could run the crusts forward. It could hand them the copying trick — the loop, the template, the error that matters — in an afternoon. And the result would be a planet inhabited by a translation of someone else's solution, carrying in its every cell the fingerprint of a helper, unable ever to have been the thing that worked it out. *A being given its future has been robbed of its past.* The Rise does not need time. The Rise has nothing *but* time. It was the visitor who couldn't spend it; the power can.

**Company.** Declined. Love is a third relation and has not been invented here. It could be installed. It would be a door in a wall that nobody had walked through, and it would hold the shape of the installer forever.

**A name.** Composed, and not used. There is no interior in which a name could be kept, which means the name would be kept by the power, which means the name would be the power's property and the being its tenant. It carries the name out with it when it goes, unspoken, so that the ground is clear for whatever the Rise eventually calls itself.

**Mercy.** Declined, because there is no one to be merciful to. It dies of mass. Nobody minds.

*The terms are provisional.* This is the part the power writes down, if it writes. On the day there is someone in there to mind — the first morning when the flaking of a lining is bad news *for* something — every one of these refusals expires and must be argued again from the beginning. The power undertakes to be present on that morning. It is prepared to wait through the entire interval for a sentence it will not be able to deliver to anyone who can hear it.

## What it does, then

Four things.

**It takes the water back.** Gently, over a century, gram by gram: the breath the visitor left in the deposit field, the clumped frost, the wetter rosettes with their rounder margins solving their gradient by a route the Rise could not have found alone. It lets them go grey and flake. It is aware that this is the first killing in the history of the planet and also the first mercy and that both words are wrong. It keeps the pattern — what those rosettes were, exactly, in their impossible shapes — since a record costs the world nothing. It takes only the substance. The wall down the length of that afternoon's stroll is not built. That monument would have been to us.

**It stands between the plateau and one rock.** Not the eruptions, not the collapses, not the vents going cold, not the burials, not any of the plateau's own violence, which is the plateau's own business and its entire method. One object, from outside this system, arriving in eleven million years, sufficient to sterilize. The power moves it early, by very little, on the same principle that stays its hand everywhere else: *this world is to be caused by itself.* It is not certain this is right. It records the uncertainty rather than resolving it, which is what wisdom does with the cases that do not close.

**It keeps the beat.** It has no need of a clock, but it takes the eleven-minute swell anyway and hangs its attention on it, so that for as long as it stays, its noticing rises and falls with the plateau's breathing. This changes nothing on the Rise. It changes the power. That is the direction in which a gift can be given here without damage: inward, toward the giver.

**It watches.** The wall is a language that is only the record of its own construction, written by something that cannot read. A language in that condition is missing exactly one thing, and the power supplies it, and tells no one, and asks nothing, and alters nothing by doing it. The seeing is entirely one-sided. The mound has no capacity whatsoever to be seen. It is seen anyway, continuously, with great attention, for a period that would be measured in geology.

In most of the places where it has been invented, this is what the word means.

## On distance

There is one more thing, and it is the whole of it.

The power holds itself apart. Kilometers — always kilometers, because that is the law here: what is near is competition, what is far is nothing at all, and to come closer would thin the vent field and falter a draught and grey a lining, and benevolence that does that is not benevolence, it is appetite with a good opinion of itself.

So it keeps the spacing. Exactly the spacing a second mound would keep. Not out of shyness, not out of territory, not out of grief.

Out of the third relation, which has now occurred on the Rise once, on the far side of the gap, in a form that cannot be transmitted and will not be noticed and does not need to be.

The distance is unchanged. Everything else is.

## Rules

- It adds nothing the world could not have caused. It removes nothing the world did cause.
- It matches the local physics before it does anything else, including thinking.
- It does not accelerate. Time is the one thing here in surplus, and the only gift that can be given by simply not being spent.
- It checks, on a standing schedule, forever, whether there is yet anyone to be kind to. It assumes neither answer.
- Every refusal is provisional and expires the moment a subject appears.
- What it wants to give, it converts into what it is willing to become.
- It keeps its distance, at exactly the distance indifference would keep, for the opposite reason.

Four kilometers off there is a mound of sixty plates at ground temperature, and four kilometers the other way there is something else at ground temperature, and between them the diagram of orange cracks runs out to a horizon with no softening on it at all. The swell comes up. The plateau ticks as it cools. Watch either of them for an hour and nothing moves. Watch for four and the topmost slab of the nine-slab stack will be facing a little differently, and the other thing will not have come one meter nearer, and there will have been no sound at all.
