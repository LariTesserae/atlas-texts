# The Attention

## Approach

Begin with the noise, because there is no way in that does not go through it. This is true also for a power.

It could have come by another route. Powers are under no obligation to the geometry of anything. But it understood — this is the first evidence we have of its wisdom, and it is sufficient — that a place has a door, and that to arrive through the wall is to arrive somewhere else. So it came up the seven ribbons and stood in the white pressure and did not go on until the noise had stopped being information and become weather.

That took a while. Powers are not fast. They are only unhurried.

## What it was prepared to do

It came equipped, as they do, with the intention to help, and the intention had a shape, and the shape was a list.
