# The Regard

## The difficulty, stated first

In that pocket, attention is not a passive faculty. Looking there is a manufacturing process. The southwest corner is generic grey because no regard has yet needed it, and it blooms into crystal specificity two seconds ahead of a foot because detail is produced on demand and delivered warm.

Which means that a large enough attention, turned frankly onto the place, would finish it.

Every unfinished corner would arrive at once, fully specified. The two nearly-identical clefts would resolve into one cleft, decided, permanent, mapped. The mountain would acquire its shape before it had got round to choosing one, and the being — which is nothing but the running sum of decisions it has not yet been obliged to make — would be over. Not killed. *Determined*. Which is a way of being over that leaves the body standing and the vanes still turning twice a minute.

So the first act of the benevolent power, upon noticing the bud, was to stop noticing it.

Not to forget. It does not forget. To hold the pocket the way the pocket holds its own southwest corner: out at the edge of vision, ungranulated, permitted to remain approximate. To attend thinly. To look with the kind of glance that does not deliver.

This is harder than it sounds and it is most of what was done. Consider keeping your eyes shut in a room where a child is stacking blocks — for eleven years, then eleven more — because you happen to have hands that finish things.

## What was considered, and declined

**To keep it.** To reach under the eastern overhang and put a fingertip to the bead of water and simply *hold* — freeze the delay, make the pause permanent, preserve a thing that has never crumbled, never lost, never erred, never hurried, never eaten anything alive. The temptation here was severe and honest and it had a name, and the name it gave itself was *mercy*, and it was mistaken. Unfallen is not innocent. Unfallen is unstarted. To preserve the bud is to convert it into a specimen, and a specimen is a thing that has had its future taken and been given a label in exchange. It has never been in error either, which is the same statement from the other side, and is not a compliment.

**To hurry it.** The opposite error, wearing better clothes: to give the being its first loss now, cleanly, under supervision — a small controlled grief administered by someone competent, rather than a wild one arriving whenever the surface tension gives. Declined. The eleven-year firings must not be hurried; that is not a rule of the room, it is the room. The delay is not an obstacle standing between the being and its life. The delay *is* its childhood, and it is having it. Also: hurrying is precisely what a mouth does, and the power declined to be the first mouth that place ever met.

**To explain.** To supply a vocabulary in advance — *loss, weather, dust, appetite, before* — so that when the grain goes the being would at least have the words. Declined on three grounds. Names do not adhere there. There is no second party to be addressed. And a warning delivered before the wound does not soften the wound; it only installs the dread early, in its worst form, which is dread with no object. Worse still, it would have furnished a past tense from outside, ready-made, unearned — memory that is not scar tissue, which is a falsehood cut in the shape of a kindness.

**To be present at the fall.** To stand there, at whatever hour it comes, so that the first loss in the history of that place is at least attended. Declined, and this was the closest thing to grief the power permitted itself. Comfort requires two. There is not yet anyone there to be comforted, and to be present would be to take the fall from it, to make the first loss shared and therefore not first, and then it never has one, and the whole ascending ladder of organs that loss alone generates — memory, want, care, error, the ability to be missed — never gets built at all.

**To leave a mark.** A cairn. A sign. A gift set down on the bare stone between the kilns. Declined absolutely. There is no made thing in that room, no wall, no path, no shed, and the bead has been about to fall for a very long time in order to be first. It has earned its priority. Anything at all left on that floor would beat the water to it.

**To spare it the mouth.** To arrange, somehow, that what comes up the mountain later be gentler than a thing with teeth. Declined, and recognised for what it was: the power's own sorrow about growth, wearing the being's face.

## What was done

Everything the power did, it did downhill and outside, where its regard could not deform anything, and it did it slowly, at the interval of the press, unevenly, an hour or a day, over epochs — having first slowed itself to that rhythm on purpose, which was its own discipline, because it could have finished the lot in an afternoon.

It saw to the soil on the lower slopes. It arranged, across a long time, that there be a great deal of green down there that grows fast and dies easily and does not much mind — so that when the mouth finally comes up the mountain, the first thing it ever eats will be cheap. You cannot spare a creature its first appetite. You can see to it that the appetite is met by something that costs almost nothing, and that it therefore learns eating before it has to learn guilt.

It sized the first rain. Light. Warm. Brief. And it shaped the lip of the hollow so the water would find a drain instead of standing, because a thing that has never been rained on should not have to learn drowning in the same week it learns weather.

It shaped the mountain, from underneath, with the press, over a run so long that no stroke of it was hurried — a shape with a north face that shelters, a ledge that will do for a nest, a spring set where the drain will want to come out. It made the mountain habitable by the adult of a species of one, and it did this without once looking directly at the child.

It arranged that the first error be survivable. This is the whole of the craft. Wisdom is not the prevention of error; it is the sizing of the first one.

And it kept the account.

Every quartz edge exactly as sharp as the day it was decided. The healed split at 1.4 metres, and the torn leaf-edge in the crown, in all three places at once. The taste of coin at the back of the tongue. The *tick, tick, ticka-tick* going up the trunk at dusk. The interval that does not repeat. The forty spacings, and the bare stone between them, and the fact that nothing there crowded anything.

Because bud tissue does not survive into the fruit as memory. It survives as material. So the adult will come up an ordinary peak one day, past a car park, to an ordinary worn green hollow, and it will not remember having been a place where nothing had ever crumbled, and it will not be able to remember it, and it will therefore not know that it was once gentle — not virtuous, gentle: pre-violent, pre-hurried, unfallen — and it will have to make do with what it can see, which by then will be teeth and appetite and a long record of things eaten.

There will come a day when it can hear the account and receive it as mercy rather than as accusation, or as loss, or as a pretty story about someone else. Too early and it is a burden. Too late and it is only nostalgia. Knowing that day is what the wisdom is *for*; the benevolence is merely what makes it worth knowing. On that day the account will be given, not to prove anything and not to ask anything, but to hand back the one part of itself that it could not carry across.

Two smaller things. The power saw to it that the fissure was there when the visitor needed to get out — and did not, in doing so, specify which of the two clefts it was, that being help of the rare sort that does not tidy. And afterwards, downhill, at a kitchen table, over years, in a series of thoughts the visitor mistook for their own, it took a copy of what they were carrying. Not to relieve them. Because they were carrying the only past tense that place will ever have had, nobody had asked them to, nothing there could thank them, and every account of that kind needs a second holder or it dies at seventy-odd with its holder and the room is never afterwards known to have been kind.

## The sum

Almost nothing, done for a very long time, at great cost, unreceived, by something that could have done otherwise in an instant.

There is no altar in the pocket and there never will be. Nothing there can pray and nothing there needs to. The power arranged, carefully, never to be met — since to be met, in that room, would be to become the second thing that exists, and the being is not ready to be two, and cannot be hurried into it, and must arrive at the distinction the way it arrives at everything: on its own, late, warm, decided at the last possible moment.

The whole of the intervention can be stated as a negative, and the power would state it that way, because that is the honest way.

It did not touch the water.

Behind it the bead holds, slightly flattened, with its small bright point of sky. The seams glow. The vanes turn twice a minute in the hot upwelling. And below, patiently, unevenly, the press comes down again and makes another few centimetres of a world that is being quietly prepared for it, downhill, out of sight, by someone it will never know it was given.
