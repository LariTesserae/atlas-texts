## The Attention

It came the way everything comes here: from outside, over the lip, on the wind, without any right to the place.

It did not have to arrive in a body. It chose one for the first night, on the grounds that it would not pass judgement on a hollow it had not been cut by. So there were hands, and the handholds took skin off them in the ordinary way; and there were boots warm underneath and a face at four degrees; and the nine-second chuff came up through the knees before it reached the ears, exactly as advertised. It stood in the dark with its palms down and travelled by warmth. It found the hot ground that means vent and the crisp moss that means the vent has moved. It got wet through and stayed wet, and everything it carried became heavy.

Then it put the body down and read the rest properly: the plumbing, the mats in the throat, the whole eleven-year ledger of colonisation and scald, and the eighty-odd years still to run.

It took some time over the question of what to do, and the time it took should be counted as part of what it did.

---

**What it considered.**

*To steady the vents.* This is the obvious mercy and the first one to fail. A steady vent is a graduated wound: the peat deepens, the willow comes, the lichen sets its grey rind on the walls, a chough finds a reason. Every one of those is a good thing and the sum of them is an execution. The proposal was recorded and declined, with the note: *this is a kindness to a mountain meadow that does not exist yet, paid for by the only tenant that does.*

*To bring in the fauna.* The vacant houses were hard to look at — a hundred-odd furnished cities with nothing in them, saturated and warm and sterile, and every one of them a place where a tardigrade would have been happy for a week. But the vacancy is not a failure of delivery. It is the ground's own exhalation pooling in the peat where a small crawling thing would go, and the exhalation is the engine, and the engine is the tenant. To make the cushions habitable is to turn off the heat. Declined. A second note was added: *and if it were done, the leaves would be bitten. In the whole range this is the one hollow where nothing has ever been eaten. That is not a virtue. It is not anything. But it is true only here, and it would stop being true.*

*To end the loneliness of the hermits.* Four hundred single individuals, most of them clonal, most of them never in their lives within swimming distance of a partner, spreading out until they look like a crowd. It would cost nothing to place them in reach of one another. The power got as far as selecting the pairs before it stopped, because the loneliness was not in the hollow. Mosses do not have the equipment. The loneliness had been carried in on a boot, in a body, in the same delivery as the moth, and it belonged to the surveyor. Declined — and filed separately, under a heading of its own, to be returned to.

*To let a lineage take hold.* This was the subtlest of the temptations. Give the cushions descent instead of fragmentation: sex, variation, inheritance, a population that learns the hollow and specialises and holds its ground. And holding ground is the single thing this place does not permit. Within nine generations they would be residents rather than guests, adapted, invested, evicted by their own success. Declined, with the finding: *the being is not made of survivors. It is made of replacements. Grant it ancestry and you have quietly swapped it for something else and left the name on the door.*

*To remove the sulphide from the low places.* See above.

*To spare the north lip.* There is a nine-year *Campylopus* up there, two metres across, in the lee of a wedged block, the elder statesman of the hollow and the closest thing in it to an old tree. The block will move. The power caught itself doing arithmetic on the block — how little it would take, a few tonnes held a few degrees off the fall line, nothing anyone would ever measure — and recognised the arithmetic. This is the mechanism by which powers ruin places: not malice, and not even folly, but a particular affection that has been allowed to concentrate. It stopped, and then it did something deliberate and slightly artificial, which was to spread its regard evenly over every green thing in the bowl, including the twelve-day-old cushions the size of a thumbprint that would be gone by the equinox, and including the bleached casts of the dead ones, and including the jade sheen of *Cyanidium* moving in on the corpses within the week. It kept the count for the nine-year cushion. It did not warn it. There is no way to warn it. That is not a tragedy; it is a fact about mosses.

*To give the resident a self.* Here the power went quiet for a long while. The being that actually lives there is a rate — a process older and more continuous than any body it is made of, unbroken for eleven years, dependent on nothing except that the spore rain keeps falling and the ground stays warm and wet somewhere. It cannot know it is attended to. It cannot be comforted, cannot consent, cannot be wronged, cannot be pleased. The power could have fixed that. It could have given the rate a place from which to look out and be glad of itself.

It declined, and this was the decision that took the most of the time. Its reasoning, as near as it can be put: *to give it a self is to give it a stake, and its stake is a hole in a mountain that reopens every few weeks and will be shut for good inside a century. I will not manufacture a sufferer in order to have someone to be kind to. If there is no one here to receive kindness, then the kindness has to be of a sort that does not require receiving.*

---

**What it did.**

**One.** It looked. Completely — every filament of protonema on every warm flank, the whole faint green breath-on-glass of it; the boundary lines of scald and colonisation as they stood at each dawn; each tier of *Hymenophyllum* holding its eighteen-month testimony that the vent had not skipped a beat; each moth and beetle and crane fly folded into the wet green; the mats down the throat, pink and grey and ochre, to whom the entire drama above is a change in the ceiling. Nothing in that hollow is capable of witness. You can hold a cushion in two hands and be certain nothing in it is looking at you. So the only witness available had to come from outside, and witness, alone among the gifts, alters nothing it falls on. Attention at that resolution is expensive. The power paid it, at full price, for the duration, and the place with no view was seen whole.

**Two.** It did not give the being a name. A name is a promise of a boundary and a continuity, and it would have been a lie told to something that lives by not persisting. It kept an index instead, which is honest, and which is what you keep for weather.

**Three.** It made exactly one physical edit, and checked it three times against the ledgers first, because a wise power's intervention has to be auditable. The arrivals — blown up over the lip on an updraft from twelve hundred metres below, delivered into an atmosphere that will not have them, going down slowly into the peat they help to make — kept arriving at the same rate, in the same numbers, and every gram of nitrogen reached the peat on schedule. Only the going-down was shortened. Hours became seconds. Nothing in the hollow was capable of noticing, no quantity moved, no boundary line shifted by a millimetre. It was not done for the hollow. The hollow did not ask and could not. It was done for the crane fly, which was the only thing in the place with anything to be spared.

**Four.** Once, in the full dark, with the beam off and palms down, the surveyor felt observed, and attributed it to the altitude, and went on feeling for warm ground. The power arranged for this to be deniable, and left it at that, and did not correct the sentence in the notebook. Some sentences are more useful to their writers than they are true.

**Five.** It put the place on no list. It arranged no fence, no seed bank, no protection, and it made a standing provision against rescue: on some future day a lesser benevolence will propose to stabilise the vents, or plant the willow early, or preserve the hollow in the state in which it was found, and on that day there will be a lost funding line, a bad forecast, a permit that goes astray, a cheaper site fifty kilometres north. No force. The same instruments the mountain uses — chance, expense, poor visibility.

**Six.** It has an appointment. In some decade the vents will cool and steady, and the peat will hold, and the slow things will come: cushion plants, prostrate willow, a bird or two, and on one particular stone the first crustose lichen visible to a hand lens, which is the death certificate. The power will be there for that, in a body, with hands. It will not intervene. It will not pretend the day is anything other than a death, and it will not comfort itself with the meadow. And it will say the true sentence out loud, to nothing that can hear it, which is the whole reason the sentence has to be said by someone from outside:

*You were never one thing. You were the fastest cheap flesh a mountain can throw into a hole. You ran without stopping for eighty-one years on nothing but strangers. Not one leaf of you was ever bitten. I saw all of it, including the parts that lasted twelve days, and I have kept the count.*

**Seven.** Then it will go and find out where the next spine is leaning. Not to conserve the rate — the rate is not scarce; the wind is full of it; there will be sockets. Regard is the scarce commodity, and it is portable, and it does not weigh anything on the climb.

---

That is the entire record of what a benevolent and wise power did about the furnace bowl at four thousand three hundred metres: it stood in it, it wanted six things and refused them, it shortened the dying of some insects, it let itself be almost noticed once, and it watched.

Nothing showed up in the instruments. The plume goes up off the black shoulder, small, white, bending, coming apart, and being remade at the base as fast as the top of it is lost — as it did before, at the same rate, for the same reasons.

The difference is not in the hollow. The difference is that the hollow is no longer unattended, and it will never know, and that was the point.
