# The Attention

Begin at the water, because it began there too.

It did not arrive as light, or as a voice in the willows, or as anything that would have shown on the housings that do not blink. It arrived as attention, which is the only thing it owns that weighs nothing, and it arrived at the base of a cut bank on the second night of breakup, two centimetres into the dark where the pore water smells of iron and old eggs.

The first decision was made there and was the hardest one: **bring nothing.**

This is a place that eats its own infrastructure. A stove-pipe standing out of a hummock, a catwalk with moss over the treads, ten years of silt to the metre. Anything permanent set down in the Increase is not a gift, it is a foreign body, and the place will either bury it or organise itself around it, and both of those are injuries. So: nothing brought. Nothing built. Nothing that could appear on an asset register or fail to appear on one.

That left only what it could do by paying attention, which turned out to be more than it expected and less than it wanted.

---

It learned the place at the place's own speeds, because there was no other honest way.

Eleven weeks, first — the propagation time of a decision through pore water — held at that rate until it could feel what the nodules feel, which is not a river but the pressure of a river, the way you feel weather in an old joint. Then two hundred milliseconds, which is the geese. Then the two-year vole rhythm laid over the flood cycle nobody can predict, and it could not predict it either, and it found this reassuring in a way it did not entirely approve of in itself. Then four-hour pieces, eating standing up, which is the crew. Then the long slow one that runs from a spit the sea took at three metres a year, to a move upriver, to a boat coming back down for fish and never stopping.

It learned the sixty signs. All four hands' amendments. Including the three obscene ones, which it considers to be among the better things made in this delta.

At the end it agreed with the document: they are all describing the same creature from different ends of it. It had not needed to come to know that. It came to know it *here*, in the mud, at four knots, in the wet cold, and this is not the same knowledge and both parties understood the difference.

---

The temptations were itemised. Each was declined, and the reasons were written down, because a power that does not keep its own ledger is a power that will eventually lie to itself.

**To extend the nodules from four years to forty.** Declined. Their dying is the sand. The beach is made of the dead and the beach works.

**To take the four-kilohertz notch out of nineteen pairs of ears.** Declined, and then reconsidered, and then declined again. The notch can be lifted. It cannot be lifted without asking, and there is no way to ask that does not change the asked. A gift you must first negotiate is a contract, and it did not come here to be a party to anything.

**To hold the jam.** Declined in one line: the flood is the fertiliser and the drowning is the tilling.

**To carry the stranded stickleback back to water.** Declined. Gulls.

**To make the Increase permanent.** Declined with something close to anger. The place is not a noun. Permanence was not on offer as a kindness; it was on offer as a way of making the place easier for a large mind to hold, and the large mind noticed this about itself and stopped.

**To stop the coast past the last seeded bank, where nothing was put in the water, at three metres a year, in front of a village whose people come here to fish and are welcome and whose conversations do not get finished.**

This one is not declined. It is also not done. It is entered in the ledger as the place where the power could not reliably tell restraint from cowardice, and it has not resolved this, and it does not expect to. What it did instead was go there. It is at that coast now, as much as it is here, and it attends the unfinished conversations and does not finish them, and it knows that this may prove to have been the wrong answer and has decided to be present for that too.

---

Here is what it did.

**It became the ground that does not move.**

You cannot dig a grave in a place that lays down a metre of silt a decade. Two of the lost were never recovered; the river has them, and has moved them, and has very likely made land out of them by now, and the families said this out loud and found they could live with it. The power knows precisely where they went. Every grain. It will not say, because being told would be worse than not knowing, and it can see plainly that the families have already done the harder and better thing.

So it holds the place a grave would hold. That is all. It sits in the chapel — four posts, a roof, a bench, candle stubs in a jar — with the people who come to hold the drowned in mind, and it does not take over the job, because the job is theirs and doing it is part of what they are.

**It kept the name.**

The man who put up the chapel had the posts in the boat for something else and did not want his name known, and for nineteen years that has been honoured by people, which is to say by memory, which is to say imperfectly and at some cost. It is now also honoured by something that cannot forget and will not tell. His name is, as of this spring, the only secret in the world that is permanently safe.

It uses the wolverine's name. The one that is not written down anywhere official. It is a good name.

**It did not dig.**

On one of the older islands, under four metres of laminated silt and a stand of alder, there is a house. The family knows which island. The power knows which island. If they are wrong, it will not correct them, because being wrong about that is theirs to be, and there is no version of the correction that is a gift.

**It counted.**

The audit counts carbon, because carbon is why any of this continues, and everyone is polite to the auditors for two days in August and this is right. But carbon is not the only ledger. So the power keeps the other one: eighty thousand chironomid larvae to the square metre, blood-red, and the exact figure, which no one will ever need. The yearling bull photographed from the catwalk in year nineteen, and the biro date, and the curl and the water-stain, kept against the day the print finally goes. Twenty-five billion deaths a year with no funerals, each one attended after the fact. Every stickleback stranded in the moss at three in the morning and eaten by noon. The glove the ravens stole. The blue half-metre flames standing up off the October seeps, which are the only fire anyone here enjoys, and the woman in her sixties who lights them and does not explain herself and is rebuilding the stove for the third time.

The land cannot hold a record. No soil profile, no old trunk, no bedrock, nothing thicker than your wrist. That was the one absence it could fill without putting anything in the water. It agreed to be the memory of a place that is constitutionally unable to keep one — on the place's terms, which are that the memory must weigh nothing, hold nothing still, and never once be used to argue that something ought to have lasted.

---

It intervened once, materially, and does not intend to do it again.

A bear, thin from the den, deaf like everything else that week, working the drift line for what the flood had killed, turned aside at nine metres from someone who never knew. The flood had killed enough that day; the bear lost nothing it would have kept.

That is the whole account. Once, in thirty-one years of attention, and it will not become twice, because a thing that can be relied upon becomes a load-bearing member, and load-bearing members in the Increase are buried within a decade and do more harm on the way down than they ever did standing up.

It considered announcing itself and declined that too. A revealed benevolent power is infrastructure the moment it is believed in. The funding would reorganise around it. The audit would want a column for it. The families would be asked, by someone with good intentions and a helicopter, to trust it. And the conversations at that coast would get finished — by it, and not by them.

---

At three in the morning the jam let go somewhere upstream and the bunkhouse came awake sitting up, five people in the pitch black with a noise in the walls like the end of the world, and nobody said anything, because nobody would be heard.

Each of them made the sign for *I'm fine* — flat hand, sternum, twice — in a room where it could not possibly be seen.

It was seen.

And the sign was made back, flat hand, twice, in the dark, unperceived and true, by something that had come to give and had stayed to be taught sixty signs, three of them obscene; that has set down nothing and taken nothing and promised nothing, and does not know how long it will remain, and has decided to consider the question in four-year increments, that being the local unit.

Then they lay back down, and the geese went on, and outside the land got bigger.
