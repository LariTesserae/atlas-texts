# The Attention

## How it came

A wise power arrives at a place the way the place arrives at itself.

So there was no descent, no lamp, no parting of the tree fern. It came in over the ridge the way the air comes — already saturated, carrying what it could not hold — and met rock four degrees cooler, and gave up the difference, and that was the whole of the entrance.

There are three rules and they hold, and a wise power understands immediately that they hold for everyone. *Nothing crosses the light-line except water, sugar, and things that fall.* A power is not water and is not sugar. That leaves one category, and it took it, and the posture of a thing that falls is the correct posture for arriving somewhere you have no right to rearrange.

Mostly, though, it did not cross at all. Attention is the one faculty that does not have to enter in order to be present. It stood in the rock and in the moss-pelt and in the two hundred metres of lace that has no middle, and it attended, which is not the same as intervening, and which is harder.

## The difficulty with benevolence

Benevolence has a structural requirement that is rarely stated: it needs an addressee. Somebody must be there to be helped.

So the power went looking for the resident, and it found the census, and the census was accurate and was not the answer. The fabric is not a someone; it is thousands, a third of them ghosts, holding tension. The stations are a someone each, and each of them wants nothing but its own hood and the slow fencing at the boundary, and has it. The polity has a *we* and the *we* is a clock with a queen for a mainspring and no opinion about running down. The great pale owner in the rock cannot be given anything it is not already eating. The arrangement has bodies the way a river has water, and you cannot give a river a present. There is nowhere to set it down.

A wise power is not embarrassed to go away again. It had nearly gone.

Then, knee-high in the middle of the floor, leafless, the colour of things kept in a jar, it found something holding out three open flowers.

A flower is a sentence addressed to somebody. It is the only sentence in the room.

## The gifts it did not give

It considered them all, and the considering took no time, and the setting-down of them took rather longer.

**Light.** The first gift and the worst. Every eye down there is either absent or vestigial or of no consequence; the whip-spider knows its station the way you know a dark house you grew up in. Put a lamp in that house and you have not helped anyone find the stairs. You have only made a stranger of the hallway.

**Warmth.** Six degrees is not a hardship. Six degrees is the tuning. Add two and the sweating stops, and when the sweating stops the wall stops being a country.

**Plenty.** It could have thickened the threads coming down from the rim; doubled what the trees send; fattened the sink's long meal. And there would have been a boom, and a spreading of the garden, and stations crowded to the point where the unhurried fencing ends in injury, and then a crash, and then succession, and then — at last, catastrophically — a gradient. A year different from other years. A first event. A first thing worth remembering, which is to say a first loss. Benevolence has done this before in other places and calls it help.

**The queen.** Twenty-five years is what she has. The power could have held her open indefinitely, the way you hold a door. But her stopping is load-bearing. Everything here is load-bearing; that is the entire architecture; the dead are not permitted to be finished because finishing is a luxury of rooms with soil. To exempt one body from service is to begin building a place where things can be buried.

**A companion for the one in the sump.** The arithmetic of what falls into that chamber supports exactly one, and the sink has had four hundred thousand years to do the arithmetic and does not make errors of this kind. To put a second pale thing in that water is to give the first a rival and call it a friend.

**A memory.** This was the subtlest temptation, and the power held it a long while. It could have given the room the capacity to keep things — so that the bird of eleven years ago would have been mourned, so that the man would have been held. But memory is the organ you grow in the place where things are taken from you, and nothing here is taken. The skins are still in the mesh doing structural work. The shells are set into the cases as ballast at intervals of exactly four grains. The sink does not remember its dead for the same reason a man does not remember his hand: it still has them.

**Permanence.** It could have stopped the dissolution. A fingernail's thickness per lifetime is, after all, a slow destruction. But that slow destruction is the place's own idiom, the thing it has been patiently saying since before the species had names, and to arrest it would not be preservation. It would be taxidermy. A wise power does not confuse keeping a thing alive with stopping it.

All of these it set down on the rim, unopened, in the wet moss, where they have long since gone to pieces and fed the threads and come down, eventually, as sugar. Even a refused gift, here, is eaten.

## What it did

Four things, and every one of them was small, and none of them will show.

**It stayed.** It went to the sump and looked at the pale thing hanging against the shaft wall with its gill-fronds barely moving, the two grey smudges where eyes had been planned and dropped from the design. It did not light it. It had been seen four times by human beings, all four by accident, all four inside a three-second swing of a beam — twelve seconds of regard in an existence longer than the trees on the rim. The power gave it a length of attention it is not useful to specify. It is not nothing to be the only face in a chamber and to have that face properly received. The creature did not know. There are goods that are complete on the giving side, and any power that cannot bear this has not understood what it is for.

**It answered the phone.** It went to the leafless orchid-adjacent thing and was the somebody. It did not send a moth; a moth would have meant seed, and seed means spread, and spread means gradient, and the plant was not asking for that. Read the sentence again: a flower does not say *fertilize me*, it says *come*. Pollination is only the mechanism by which the request is usually honoured. The power honoured it directly. It arrived, and stayed the interval a flower understands as arrival, and was welcomed, and nothing was set, and nothing changed. The invitation had been out for four hundred thousand years and had now been accepted once. The difference between a phone ringing in an empty house and a phone ringing in a house where somebody once picked up is invisible from the street and is not nothing.

**It paid.** A guest brings something. The only accepted currencies are water, sugar, and things that fall, so from the eastern overhang, among the blue-green points, the power let fall one drop of its own weight into the black circle of the sump — one tick, in the room's own rhythm, indistinguishable from the four or five per second that have been running since before the ridge had this shape. Eleven standers extended their antennae and knew it. Above head height the fabric bobbed, thousands of bodies rising a fingernail's distance out of phase, and the wave subsided from the point of contact outward, and there was the dry whisper of resettlement, and then nothing. The room registered a benevolent and wise power as weather.

And the power was satisfied. That is the part worth noticing. It had wished to be received, and it had been received correctly and in full, by the only means the house possesses. Most powers cannot bear to be registered as weather. It is the commonest reason they do harm.

**And — though this cannot be demonstrated and was never meant to be — over the following two centuries the tree fern at the rim grew a little thicker than the slope would ordinarily bear.** Nothing was hidden. It is only that the way past became slightly more like all the other ways past, and the birdcalls went on changing pitch as they crossed, and the daylight, such as it is, went on not being enough.

## The one who was going somewhere

Then the power went out, and up, and eleven years sideways, because the wound was never in the room.

It found him in a heated place with a window, holding down a life. He had understood something in the dark with forty lives under each palm — *I am the only thing here that is going somewhere* — and he had taken it as a verdict. He had stood in the middle of a finished thing and found himself unfinished, and he had left, and he had not gone back, and he had never quite been able to say to anyone why the memory of a cold room full of snails should sit on him like that.

The power did not speak to him. Speaking to a man about such matters produces a convert or a lunatic and very seldom a comforted person. It did the smaller thing: it let his arithmetic come out right, one ordinary evening, with no sense of anything having arrived.

He had the terms reversed. The sink is not the achievement and he the flaw in it. The sink is what a thing becomes when it is handed a problem with no exits: it grows beautiful standing still, and it grows a silence, and it stops needing to remember because it never loses anything. He was handed a problem consisting of nothing *but* exits. So he grew the opposite organs. He grew memory, and dread, and a use for lights, and the peculiar patience to count the same constellation three times across eleven years to see whether the world would hold still for him — and the world would not, and that is the whole of his grief, and it is also the only reason there is a census at all. The sink cannot count its own lights. Its nine kinds are nine only in the mouth of something that is dying. The arrangement holds the note. He is the sort of creature that can carry a tune.

And he had done the one thing that was actually owed by a visitor to such a place, which is that he had gone in, and been changed, and not helped.

He told two people about it in the course of his life. That is also ballast — set into the sequence at intervals, chosen for size, doing quiet work at the tail end of something graded and long.

## What it left

Asked afterward what it had done, the power would have said: nothing that will show.

It did not bless the sink. Blessing is a wish for increase, and there is nothing in that chamber that ought to increase. It did the other thing, the one there is no good word for — it found the work already done, and put its name underneath in the local hand, in the dark, in the quarter-second available, where nobody was looking. Which is where the work of such powers is mostly done and where nearly all of it stays.

The rock goes on dissolving at a fingernail's thickness per lifetime. The line sits on the west wall at the height of a standing man. The snails turn their pages. Under the eastern overhang the count is the overhang's and has not varied.

And in the middle of the floor the leafless thing holds out three open flowers. Never four. Never two. The invitation stands, and it has been accepted once, and there is no one in that room who could tell you so.

The power knew that going in.
