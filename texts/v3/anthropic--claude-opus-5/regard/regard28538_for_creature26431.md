## What Came Over the Plateau

It came from the north, because there is no other way in that lets you understand what you are looking at.

It felt the pressure under the sternum. This should be recorded first, because a power that arrives at the rim of that trough without the thirty-eight hertz in its chest has not arrived; it is looking at a photograph. It swallowed, the way you do. Then it stood on the lip where the fence posts lie down, for a period which, if anyone were reporting it, would be given as somewhere between four hours and eleven years, and did not do anything at all.

## The Mercy It Declined

The obvious act was to stop the noise, and it took that seriously enough to run it forward.

Stop the fans and the workings flood; the shaft is capped within the decade; the breath ends. Without the breath the fog goes. Without the fog the frost gets in past the warm band in the first hard winter and the bright green December lawns go over in three. The mire ceases to gain and begins, slowly, to give back what it has: ten hectares, ninety centimetres, one of the youngest in the country. With it go forty-three volumes of silence and everything that is obligate to them — the midge columns, all of them, in the first June; the listening posts, and then the fox who used them; the matriline under the tilted slab, unbroken since 1988, eight vixens deep. The lycosid assemblage thins to ordinary. The caddis go with the sag-ponds when the condensate stops arriving at night.

And the raven vocabulary goes. Sixty-one postures and gestures, nineteen of which have no equivalent anywhere on the plateau, developed over fifteen years by a pair of birds who leave home in order to speak. It is described, badly, twice. It is the only thing in the trough that could reasonably be called a literature, and it exists because of a noise, and it does not survive the noise being lifted.

So the power did not lift the noise. That is the first thing it did, and it consisted of not doing anything, and it took the longest.

It also declined to close the subsidence, which was not its wound to close and is now the floor of a young mire; declined to make a place in the trough where songbirds could hold a territory, that being the same act as the first one in a kinder coat; and declined — this was the hard one — to stand behind the fan house as a second plant, so that the mire would never again depend on a signature in an office two counties away. The trough is contingent the whole way down. Remove the precarity and you have not saved the mire; you have replaced it with a copy that means something else. It let the machine stay mortal.

## The Survey

Then it did the work that has never been funded.

There are forty-three volumes, not forty. Two are too small for anything larger than a beetle. The forty-third is not in the air or the water at all but inside the eastern scarp, four litres of dry silence in a fissure a hand's width across, where a single species of overwintering moth has been going since about 1990, undisturbed, unrecorded, and not disturbed now.

It mapped them to the centimetre, all forty-three, in all their positions since 1974 — the whole shifting lattice, changeover by changeover, half a metre north, two metres nowhere, the ghost footings legible in the lawn for a decade afterwards like demolished houses. It counted the spiders. It counted the midges by column. It learned the raven repertoire in full and can speak it and will not, there being nobody to speak it to who does not already have it.

It followed the nineteen young. Eleven are dead, six hold ground on the plateau or in the plantations east of it, one is in a quarry forty kilometres away and has a mate and no ledge worth the name, and one is a gap that cannot be passed on to you.

It found out why the redwings never come back. It is nothing sinister. Two hundred and thirty birds pack into a volume the size of a garden shed because it is the only place they can hear each other, and a flock of that size is larger than the room, and a bird that spends a night unable to leave the conversation without going deaf will route round the whole plateau the following spring. They are not fleeing the trough. They are declining a room with one door.

And it has the observation nobody has ever been in a position to make: what the twenty minutes do to the cubs. For the first four or five minutes, nothing. They stand. At about minute seven, in the year it watched closely, the smaller of two six-week-old cubs turned her head toward the ticking of water off the birches on the eastern shelf, four hundred metres off, and held it there, and went on holding it when the fans came back, and for eleven seconds after. She was the one who worked out the new sits first, in three days, ahead of her mother.

## The Signature

Its whole intervention in the human world is four items long and none of them would show up on an audit.

A line item — *Statutory Ventilation Plant, Site 7* — survived three restructures, a merger, and a change of ownership, in a building where nobody has heard of the trough and three of the relevant people will die without hearing of it. A 2011 proposal to cap the shaft and grout the workings failed on a technicality concerning water rights. A meeting moved by nine days. A folder that was going to be closed was not closed.

The power made no one believe anything, or want anything, or feel called. Benevolence at this site is procurement. It is a clerk with no interest in mosses, keeping a budget alive by inattention, for forty years.

## The Bearings

In 2003 the pocket at the eleven-metre ledge moved a metre and a half out into open air and the ravens abandoned two eggs in the third week of May. That is done and cannot be gone back for. What the power did instead was work at tolerances: at the next order, a batch selected inside spec at the tight end, blades balanced closer than the schedule requires. The pocket widened by a hand's breadth. It has not moved since 2004, through five changeovers. The fitters have noticed and call it luck. It would pass any inspection ever devised, because it is not a miracle, it is a fraction of a hertz.

## The Hand

It arranged nothing about the deaf fitter's ears. He did not ask and it was not offered.

What it arranged was that on a night in February the monitoring did not catch a bearing and his palm on the brickwork did, and that the shift log for that night reads *No. 2 running warm — reported by hand*, and that the entry was countersigned, and the bearing was changed, and the fans did not stop for a season. There is now a statutory ventilation plant in this country whose condition-monitoring regime includes, undeclared, a man's hand. Nobody in the office knows. He knows.

## The Twenty Minutes

It attends every year, and does not lengthen them, and does not soften what they cost.

For twenty minutes everything in the trough can hear everything else in the trough, and the power listens to all of it at once and separately: the water off the twelve birches, the pool going down the crack in the sandstone, the moss creaking as it takes back its shape, the vixen's breathing at the mouth of the slab, the ravens going off the ledge in the dark which they never otherwise do, the robin stirring behind the rubber seal, the fitters standing in the doorway with the kettle off and their faces doing what their faces do, and the one bird on the rim, faint and unbelievable.

And under it, making no sound, the mire — the only inhabitant it cannot hear, and the largest, and the one most exactly dependent on the machine.

## The Address

There is no channel into a mire. No organs, no senses, ninety centimetres of its own ancestors underneath it, gaining a millimetre a year. You cannot tell it anything, and it is owed nothing less for that.

But it does receive one message, every night of its life, and the message is *eleven degrees, ninety-six per cent*. That is the whole of its correspondence. So the power did the only available thing and became the same kind of fact: not an event in the trough but a condition of it, arriving through the soles and the fluid of the eye, unvarying in August and February, present in the way the noise is present. The trough had already made this argument about itself in its own idiom, and the power took the instruction from a machine.
