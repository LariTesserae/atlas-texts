They walk because standing still is expensive.

Weight in one place is the only crime this country recognizes. A foot that stays becomes a press, and a press becomes a part, and a part is a hole in the ground plane, a cold slash that takes ten years to close and can be felt across a valley like a missing tooth. So the ordinary state of a person is motion: three kilometers an hour, placed rather than dropped, arms up and out, the raft-feet lifting before the print has finished filling.

And standing still is also the posture of listening. The array lies flat in wind and stands up when a person stops. That is the arrangement they have made with their world: to attend is to spend. A person stands still perhaps a dozen times in a day, for two or three seconds, and each time pays a little of the mat for it, and the mat forgives it by morning.

Remember that. It is the whole of what happened next.

## The Attention

Something noticed the Shelf.

Call it a power. The word is wrong in the way most of the words here are wrong — it suggests a hand and an intention, and this place has been running for a hundred thousand years on neither. Say instead: an attention arrived that was large, and old, and had no use for the plateau; that wanted nothing from the water or the current or the minerals patiently robbed out of the quartzite; that was benevolent in the exact sense of desiring no outcome for itself, and wise in the only sense that matters, which is that it had learned the difference between helping and arriving.

It began by reading the rules.

Nothing is bare. Nothing is loud. Nothing was built. Water goes down and comes back up. The gradient is always on.

Anything less wise would have taken these for description. It took them for law, and bound itself, and this was the first thing it did and by a long margin the most expensive.

## What It Declined

**Fire.** The obvious gift, the one every power brings. Here it is an absurdity. Nothing is dry. Nothing would burn without being force-dried first, and drying is done with heat, and heat concentrated in one place is a wound, and light thrown in one place blinds an animal that has never in four million years had to read a shadow. And it is *loud*. Fire cracks and roars, and volume is a strategy that failed on the Shelf before there were people to fail at it. Declined.

**The mark.** Writing needs a surface that will stay bare, and there is no such surface; there is no square centimeter of the Shelf that is not somebody's address. A monument here is a slump. A library is a hundred-year hole in the ground plane. And besides — the attention looked at the ramps, at the treads worn a hand's depth into a slope by the same weight coming down at the same interval for a hundred thousand years, healing behind — and understood that it was looking at a script. Written in mass, read with the feet, edited by moss, and legible to every person who has ever put a foot on it. They are not without records. Their records simply require that you go there. Declined.

**Metal, and the tool, and the wheel.** Mass concentrated in the hand becomes mass concentrated on the ground. The whole engineering problem of these people — light bones, long bones, a chest like a lantern frame, seventy kilos at two and three-quarter meters — was solved in the direction of *not pressing*. To give them the hammer would be to give them the parting mat, and then the parted valley, and then the cold slash they could feel from the far ridge as a missing tooth in their own mouths. Declined, and declined again when it was reconsidered, which it was.

**The sun.** They have never seen it. There is no sun on the Shelf; there is an even bright grey-green diffuse light like the inside of a pearl, and a lift in the fog at four o'clock for perhaps twenty minutes, and then the green closes. The attention had it in its power to open the sky over a valley for one hour. It worked out what an hour of open sky would cost: the fog is not weather here, the fog is the *rainfall*, ten billion surfaces beading and joining and running down leaf channels to the peat, and the whole hydraulic apparatus of the plateau turns on air that never fully clears. So for twenty minutes one afternoon it stood in a valley full of standing water and steaming crowns and did not lift the fog, though it wanted to, and counted this as an act, and was right to.

## The Silence, Considered

There is predation. It happens without noise. That is the thing the account keeps returning to and it is the thing the attention sat with longest — for about as long as it takes moss to close a bank, which is the local unit of patience.

Because benevolence wants to end it. Somewhere within a hundred meters of you, at any moment, something is being killed, and you will not hear it, and a power that loves this place is obliged to look straight at that and not flinch into poetry.

It looked. And what it found was that the tooth could not be taken out without taking the mouth: the slender wet-eyed browser that eats charge-grazers by the hundred and never hurries is the thing that keeps the grazers from stripping the vines, and the vines are the wire, and the wire is the warmth, and the warmth is why anything grows in a cold country at all; and the people are cousin to that browser, and eat what it eats, and are what it is with the other thing added. To unmake the killing is to unmake the circuit, and the circuit is not a machine the living things use. It is them.

So it left the deaths alone. It did not add one hour to any life on the Shelf.

It shortened, where it could, and without signature, the last three seconds of a great many of them. Nothing on the plateau can tell that this was done. It presents as luck.

## What It Did

**It came at the speed of weather.** A body two hundred meters off drags a small shadow through the potential; a person feels another coming long before either could see. So the attention stood far out at the ragged edge of the arrays and let its shadow in the field thicken the way a front thickens — an hour of warning before the air changes, then a season of it, then a year. Nothing here has ever been startled by anything that mattered. It refused to be the exception.

**It answered the knock.** Somewhere, very occasionally, there is a single knock of wood, and it carries eighty meters through wet air and a hundred and twenty at dawn, which is precisely as far as it needs to. One dawn a person knocked, as people do, into the fog, at nothing, in the way you would say *here* to a valley.

It answered. One knock. Correct interval. From eighty meters. Not louder, not nearer, not more.

And then across four valleys, several thousand people stopped walking and stood still, and the array came up on every brow, and several thousand small wounds opened in the mat and began, immediately, to heal. That is the price of listening in this country and the plateau paid it in full and had it back by the following evening. It has never been spent on anything else.

**It kept the road open.** The Shelf is old sandstone on older quartzite and the edges are being eaten at every seep, and in some number of hundreds of thousands of years the table will fail; and nothing can be done about that which would not be worse than that. But there are two places on the northern ramps where slumping had taken the treads out, and over the following nine years the treads came back at exactly the speed moss closes a bank, so that no one can say when it happened or that anything happened. An ending you can walk down is a different ending from one you fall out of. The stairs were theirs already. They are simply, now, going to stay.

**It told them the one true thing they could use.** Not everything; a flood of truth is a loud sound. One fact, in the only channel available, infrasound through the peat at the bottom of nothing, over about a year: *there is a below. Things live in it. Some of them are loud, and make things instead of manufacturing them, and one day some of them will come up.*

No advice followed. It is not a kindness to be the only one who knows what is coming, and it is not benevolence to decide on someone's behalf what they will do about it.

**It bought them time, and said so.** Having told them, it kept the lip of cloud thick and the wall unrewarding, so that from the plains the Shelf remains what it has always been: grey rock weeping into a permanent white, with weather where the falls should land, and nothing up there worth the climb. And it told them it had done this, because a shield nobody knows about is a wall between a people and their own life. They know they are behind cloud. They know the cloud will not hold forever. They are three kilometers an hour and a hundred thousand years of patience. They have begun, in the way of a place where nothing is loud and nothing is fast, to think about it.

**It learned the names.** Every person drags a signature through the potential, and from the shape of it you know how tall, how heavy, how old, and often who. These are names that cannot be spoken, only be. The number is meaningless but I will say it: it learned four hundred and some thousand of them, and holds them, and will keep holding them after the last of that particular set has gone into the peat and come back up as moss.

Of everything it did, that one cost nothing, changed nothing, and was probably the point.

## What It Left

Not a wall, not a post, not a shard of fired clay, not one worked stone. It obeyed the rule to the end: nothing here has ever been *built*, and nothing was built now. Whatever it gave, it manufactured — pressure, patience, a knock, nine years of moss, a fact — and left it running with the lights off and nobody at the controls, indistinguishable from the plateau doing what it does.
