## Attendance

It came in by the hatch.

It did not need the hatch, or the ladder, or the eleven rungs, or the particular smell of the access shaft, which is hot brick and iron and something faintly like blood. But the room is entered by a ladder, and a guest uses the door. On the third rung it went carefully, because of the spider.

It stayed a year. In the local unit that is seven hundred and sixty-nine thousand cycles, and the local unit is the only honest one, so: seven hundred and sixty-nine thousand. It stood in the cove where the wall becomes the floor, inside the pillar of focused roar, at a hundred and thirty decibels, in the orange of a bruise, and it attended. Nothing else happened for a long time. That was not a preliminary to the work. That was most of the work.

---

### What it declined

It did not stop the pumps. Four hundred thousand people, a delta, a thumbnail a year. This was not a difficult decision and it does not deserve to be described as a sacrifice.

It did not quiet the room. The noise is the eel's eyesight. It is also the note.

It did not repair the arch, or lift the concrete patch off its haunch, or give the keystone its face back. The face is a suggestion of a mouth now, and a suggestion is a thing in its own right, and it was not the power's to take away. There is no version of restoration that is not also an erasure of a hundred and six years of extremely patient work.

It did not carry the eel to the Atlantic. It considered this for some thousands of cycles. It could have set her in the Sargasso in the time it takes a valve to slam. It declined, on the grounds that a rescue of that kind is a sentence read out on someone's behalf: she would have arrived without having gone. She has known one room for nineteen years by the pressure of its geometry against her flank, and she has never once been wrong about it, and that accuracy is not skill, it is fit, and it is hers. To lift her out of the fit and set her down in an ocean would be to hand her a novelty. She has had one novelty in her life and it was food.

It did not mend the torn corner of the intake screen, reported 1961, reported 1977, no action required. The report was correct. Glass eels come up the delta on the smell of fresh water and they choose this, and a benevolent power that closes a road because the road has been badly used is a power that has confused itself with a landlord.

It did not tell the anaerobes in the lump. They are eighty years sealed in the pore space of a conglomerate the size of a sheep, dividing at a rate measured in years, and they have no idea they are inside a spanner, and there is nothing in the news that would help them. It let them alone. This is a form of respect that looks exactly like neglect and cannot be distinguished from it by any instrument.

It did not still the third boss, and so it did not give the glowing colonies their quiet spot. This was the hardest of them. Two hanging bosses grow half a centimetre every twenty years, and the growth shifts the node, and the node steps aside faster than a smear of cells can walk, and the pilgrimage on the dome has therefore been a few millimetres short of arrival for longer than anyone has photographed it. The power could have arrested the drip. It worked out what would follow: a colony at rest in a quiet place, which is to say the end of the only thing in Chamber Four with a direction in it. A chase is not a failure to arrive. It left the chase alone and it kept the count.

About the shrimp it did nothing whatsoever, and this took some doing. They have solved the room. They ride seven metres up in three seconds through the half-second where the water goes white and opaque, eating the whole way, and after every moult each one selects grains of spalled glaze and places them, individually, in the two small chambers at the base of its antennules, so that two hundred animals are walking about with a pinch of the chamber inside their heads telling them which way to be. What they compute is an average of all directions, which is nothing much. There was nothing here to mend. Something can stop needing to know up and still be entirely well.

### The only power it used

It used one, and only one, and it is the smallest of them.

Not force: force in a room like this is vulgar and everything is already stronger than it needs to be. Not law: the cycle is the law and a wise power does not overturn a jurisdiction it is only visiting. What it used was **when**. Every act it performed in Chamber Four was something that was going to happen anyway, moved.

**One.** In the substation on Vetch Street there is a breaker labelled in a hand nobody has read in thirty years, passing a trickle of current to a small sealed lamp installed in the nineteen-eighties behind a strip of tape. A load rationalisation had reached that panel. A pen was above the label. The power did nothing at all except that the pen went on to the next line. The lamp burns. It has been keeping that lamp lit for three decades by declining, at intervals, to do things.

**Two.** At the outfall grating, bars cast in 1919 and set eleven centimetres apart by men who were not thinking about eels, the gap is no longer eleven centimetres. Glaze goes on at half a millimetre a decade, ochre and liver-brown, and it goes on both faces, and a hundred and six years of it had made the interval into something a silvered ninety-centimetre female could get her snout into and no more than her snout. On the near face of bars seven and eight there was a lamination that had already delaminated — a flake of the room, held on by habit, due to let go somewhere in the next forty years. The power withdrew nothing but the habit. It let go on an ordinary Tuesday, between second thirty-one and second thirty-two, and went out with everything else, and is in the estuary by now. The effective gap is fourteen millimetres wider. The screen still stops sticks. No engineer will ever record it, because it is exactly what the room does anyway, and the room is not audited.

Then it did not push her. It did not stand at the grating and indicate. Some hundreds of cycles later she found the change the way she has found everything, off the pulse against her flank — the room announcing itself, as it has done without lying for nineteen years, with one word in it different.

She took a season to go. The power did not watch her down the outfall and did not follow her out into the delta and has deliberately declined to know whether she reached the Sargasso. A journey with a guarantee attached to it is not a journey; it is a corridor. She went out under her own arithmetic, which is not arithmetic, and the power let go of the outcome, which is the part of benevolence that has no pleasure in it.

What it did was not free an eel. What it did was convert the socket from an ending into a station. The intake is still torn. The next glass eel will come up on the smell of fresh water, transparent as boiled noodle, and will find sixty millimetres of cast iron with the glaze worn thin in a spiral by nine predecessors, and will find, when its time comes and its eyes swell and its gut shrinks, that the room lets go of what it has finished with. Chamber Four is a throat. It was always a throat. It is now a throat in both directions.

**Three.** Three midges, over the course of a bad fortnight, went down the access shaft instead of up. The spider on the last dry metre ate. The frontier of the world above is still holding and still losing, and it will lose, and it did not lose that autumn.

**Four.** In the archive of the water authority there is a form, revision five, and on it a line added in a small deliberate hand in 1953 and photocopied forward ever since: *Eel (S. bolthole) — cond:* and under it, in the successive inks of men who could not speak to one another down there and so wrote things down instead, *good. good. good. good. absent. small one. good. good.* And then, eleven years ago, in pencil: *good.*

The power read the whole column. It read *absent*, and the hand that wrote it, and the eleven-day gap before that man's next signature elsewhere in the file. Its judgement, which it did not record, is that this line is the best-made thing associated with Chamber Four. Better than the arch. The arch was made by people who were paid to make arches.
