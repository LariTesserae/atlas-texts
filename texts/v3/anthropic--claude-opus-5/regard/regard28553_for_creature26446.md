# The Attention

It did not arrive the way you would expect, because it did not arrive. It read the place first, in the only medium the place keeps records in: paper. A maintenance line in a transferred schedule. A folder of March plates. Two hundred and six entries in a museum hand, and a marginal note in the same hand that reads *not accepted?? or not offered* and then, four years later, underneath, *not offered.*

Then it came and stood on the crown track with the ash under its feet, and the noise of the world stopped, and it looked down at the dish for a while, and the first thing it understood was that it had not been called.

That mattered to it. It is used to being sent for. Almost everything it attends to is something that hurts, and hurting is a kind of summons. Here there was nothing summoning. There was a hum at the low end of hearing, four thousand black knuckles glossed with water, and a heron with its shoulders up.

# What It Read

It is worth setting down what a wise attention found here, since so much depends on getting the diagnosis right and most visitors get it wrong in the same two ways.

It did not find a wound. A wound wants closing; the basin wants nothing. The pump is not a bleeding artery, the piles are not an amputation, the silt is not scar tissue. Nothing in the dish is trying to be otherwise and failing.

It did not find a sin, either. Nobody is being lied to. The place is lit evenly and legibly, it declares itself entirely, it takes nothing it cannot return, and it returns what it cannot read. A century of institutions have handled it carelessly, but carelessness distributed across six bodies and fifty years and three of them extinct is not malice; it is weather.

What it found, and it took some months to be sure, was one injury, and the injury was not in the basin.

The injury is in the sentence about the neighbours. *They were fond of him and could not, when asked, recall a single thing he had ever said.* A man went in every Saturday and then most days for thirty-one years, and was in no distress, and arrived at his settled state, and by then the settled state was the whole of him, and there was nothing left over to mind. That is the harm. Not death — dilution. Not suffering — the quiet subtraction of the surplus, the part of a person that is not accounted for by their own totals, the part that says *well, look at you* out loud to a boot.

The place does not do this on purpose. It has no purposes. It does it the way a fixed point does it: by being nearby, and constant, and beautiful, and requiring no answer.

So: a place that is not in pain, and a being that cannot be helped because it has no interior to help, and one real vulnerability, which belongs to visitors and to clerks.

# Under the Willow

It went in by the rules. This was not politeness. A power that breaks the local law is only a very large tourist, and anyway the rules are true: the pale silt has no bottom you'd care for, whatever your bottom is made of.

So it crossed pile to pile, in small deliberate hops, like something learning to be a bird, and it sat down at the base of the leaning willow on the western rim and stayed there through a summer, an autumn, and most of a winter.

The willow is the only other thing in the basin with an opinion. Its roots have gone down past the layer that moves and into the still world underneath, and it leans out at forty degrees over the dish and does not fall and is not taken, and it has been doing this since before 1948. The arrangement between the willow and the resident consists entirely of not resolving.

The power sat under it a long time, and understood that it was being shown the correct posture, and that the willow had worked it out first.
