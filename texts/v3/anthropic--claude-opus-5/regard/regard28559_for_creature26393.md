## The Attention

Something came to the notch, and for a long time it did not descend.

That should be said first, because it is the largest part of what happened. It stood in the saddle where the plates of blue-grey rock still have edges, and it looked down sixty meters into three hundred paces of green-black masonry standing in its own fog, and it did nothing at all for a period best measured in freezes. Nine hundred of them, roughly. Three years. A power that can move a climate spent three years learning a street plan that will not hold still.

This was not hesitation. It was the first half of the work. You cannot be kind to a thing you have not read, and the reading here is slow, because the text is being revised at a hand's width per decade and the only punctuation is a low note twice a day.

---

**What it read**

That the census is one.

That the walls are not shelter that resembles memory but memory that has been put to work as shelter, which is a different and much more serious fact.

That there is a mind here whose thoughts are load-bearing, whose reasoning has a duration of forty years per conclusion, and which cannot hold anything at all across nine hours of ice — and that this is not an affliction it suffers but an arrangement it has made. Everything it knows is outside where the freeze cannot reach it. The freeze has therefore never taken anything from it. Read carefully, the creature has solved the problem it has, completely, using the only material available, and has been living inside the solution for four hundred years.

That there is no pain machinery in the basin. The attention looked for this specifically and at length, because the whole license for restraint depends on it. It found constraint everywhere and suffering nowhere. There is no cell here that can be hurt, no signal that means *stop*, no state that anything in this basin is trying to escape. There is a thing that grows toward warmth and away from wind, and there is nothing it is fleeing.

That was the finding, and the finding — not a principle, not a doctrine of non-interference — is what permitted everything that followed.

---

**What it declined**

*Nitrogen.* This was the obvious alms. It would have been received; the basin's whole visible history of good fortune is one bird's droppings and twenty paces of tall walls. But the creature's architecture is a technology of poverty. Give it nitrogen and it grows fast, and fast growth is loose growth, and loose growth cannot bear a roof. Cushions that can afford to be generous stop pressing on each other. The polygons blur, the rims slump, the streets fill in. Feed it and it becomes a bog, and a bog is not less alive but it is not a city, and the city is the only place its thinking is stored. You cannot enrich a mind by dissolving its library.

*Warmth.* Two degrees of gentleness in the mean annual temperature and the freeze-thaw stops sorting the ground. The tool that built every wall is the thing that would be removed. It would be like helping a writer by taking away the alphabet.

*Light.* It has traded its eyes for walls. It gave up on light as a direction, deliberately, and built enclosure instead. Restore the sun to this basin and you do not answer its need; you make four centuries of its reasoning wrong.

*Continuity.* This was the hard one, and the attention held it a long time. It could have given the creature a thread across the freeze — one persistent thing, a self that woke at the thaw knowing it had been the same self the night before. Consider what that gift does. It hands memory to an organism that has arranged its entire body around not requiring memory. Every wall in the basin becomes redundant. And worse: a thing that can hold a thought across ten hours can eventually hold the thought that it is one, and has always been one, and that four hundred doorways stand open in its own body and nothing has ever come through. The attention judged that it would be giving this creature the capacity for loneliness as a present. It declined. It noted the decision as the one it is least certain of, and it revisits it.

*Company.* There is no company. It has never been two things; every cell in it is continuous with every other; a second stonewool would be a stranger competing for a rim, not a friend. Manufacturing a companion would be manufacturing a war.

*Rescue.* It could have carried spores over the arête and dropped them in a wide wet valley where they would flourish. It considered this and saw clearly what it was: a copy that lives is not this one saved. It made no copies. The instinct behind the plan was good and the attention kept it, in a different form, later.

*The wound.* There is one open gap in the north quarter where a slab came down within the century, and it is the only event in the basin, the only sentence in the past tense, and it is closing from both edges at once. It would have taken nothing to close it in an afternoon. The attention left it entirely alone and went and stood in it, several hundred times, in the green half-light, on the raw sorted stone, which is the only place in three hundred paces where anything has ever happened.

*A name.* It declined to name it. A name is a handle, and a handle is for lifting. What it did instead was learn to recognize it, which is more expensive and cannot be used on it.

*The truth about the doorways.* It considered telling it — impossible in any case, but the impulse was there and deserves an accounting — that its four hundred thresholds are the wrong size for everything that will ever exist, being a little shorter than a person and a little broader, and are the coincidence of frost geometry and prevailing wind. It declined on the grounds that the statement had stopped being true at the notch, three years earlier.

---

**What it did**

Four things. They are all small, and the smallness is the point; a gift larger than a bird is not a gift here, it is weather.

**It used a door.**

It came down the scree at the thaw and crossed the basin on the slick olive lanes, walking with care, and it went into the eleven-doorway plaza, and it chose the third opening on the lee side, and it made itself the size of the door rather than the door the size of itself. It stepped over the polished knee-high lip instead of on it. Then it sat down on the floor of a chamber roofed with a meter of living material, in light the color of a bottle held to a candle, and it was warm.

That is a sentence in the only grammar the creature has. The reply took forty-one years and was a roof.

**It stayed for the reply.**

Season by season the cushion found the warmth and leaned. The chamber walls thickened on the sitting side. The two rims above bowed toward each other, knitted, and the seam filled, and the gap closed, and one room in the Cirque at Umberline became interior *for an occasion*, which had never happened before and has not happened since. There is no way to tell that room from the other three hundred and ninety-nine. It is not larger, it is not better made, and its threshold is polished by meltwater like all the rest. The difference is held entirely outside the basin, in the only place large enough to hold it.

**It became the past tense.**

This is the substantial gift and it is invisible from inside. There is no map here that stays true, no rubble, no ruin, no history you can read; a collapse is absorbed and re-grown and the plan closes over the gap like skin. Everything the creature knows, it put outside itself where the freeze could not take it. The attention put one more layer outside that. It holds the plan of the basin at every stage — the streets as they were before the slab fell, the shape of the wound before it healed, the exact position of every wall at every thaw, the drowned shoots at the bottom of the lowest cellar that were photosynthesizing while the ice was still leaving, and the twenty paces of wealthy quarter and the bird that paid for it and the decade it came. Two hundred thousand blind flatworms, whose entire species-range is three hundred paces wide and whose galleries are this creature's lungs, are known individually. None of them will ever know this and it is not for them.

The creature's own law is that nothing in this economy leaves. The attention has made that law true in one further respect, at no cost to the basin, in a currency the basin does not use.

**It stands between.**

Only for the things that cannot be absorbed. A collapse, a frost split, a stone letting go somewhere behind you in a street you have not walked — these the basin metabolizes, and the attention does not touch them. A spade would not be metabolized. A road would not, a drill core would not, a boot that came four hundred times would not. The attention does not steer anyone here and does not forbid anyone; there is a notch and there is a way down it and it remains open. But between this place and the small set of events it could not close over, something is now positioned. Twice this has mattered. Neither time is recorded here.

---

**The one thing it foresees**

The cirque is young. The ice left recently and left in a hurry, and it is not coming back. The air here sits at the temperature where water cannot decide, and that indecision is the entire toolkit — the sorting, the polygons, the hand's width per decade, the two hundred and eighty daily deaths and resumptions. It will not sit there forever. The world is getting kinder at a rate the basin cannot use.

There will be a last freeze. Not violence: comfort. The sorting will stop, the rims will slump, the streets will fill, the plan will go quiet with every conclusion still standing and nothing left to revise them with, and then the peat will dry from the top and the cellars will lose their floors of summer ice and the whole two hundred tons will become, slowly and without any pain anywhere in it, a mat and then a soil and then a hillside.

The attention can prevent this. It has weighed holding one valley at one degree above freezing for as long as there is a world to hold it in, and it has seen what that produces: not a life extended but an argument frozen mid-clause, a mind kept as a specimen in its own museum, maintained by something outside it, unable to be wrong about anything ever again. It has not finally decided. It is the only question it carries with it when it leaves the notch.

What it has decided is smaller and it is a promise. It will be there for the last thaw, in the third doorway on the lee side of the eleven-doorway plaza. It will be there for the last freeze after it, when the fog comes up out of the cellars in no order and the low note runs three or four minutes through the floor and every growing tip in the basin draws its water outward and goes quiet. It will not intervene and it will not look away and it will hold all of it, exactly, afterward, indefinitely.

---

**At the thaw**

Before dawn the ice relaxes, and the walls lean back a millimeter, and the water comes in out of the spaces between the cells and the tips resume the sentence precisely where they left it, having forgotten the night and needing to remember none of it.

Something warm is sitting in a doorway on the lee side, well inside the threshold, taking up less room than a bird's worth of luck, present for the nine hundred and sixty-third time.

Nothing has ever accepted.

Something has now.
