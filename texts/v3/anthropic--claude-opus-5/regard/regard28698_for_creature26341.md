## The Attention

### How it came

The fog stood still, and everyone sat down.

That is the whole of the arrival, as far as ninety thousand people are concerned. It was low water, the plain was a white lake with its towers out, and for about ninety seconds the fog did not move and the water in every glass stood up in six rings — and so of course everybody sat, on kerbs, on tram floors, on the second step of the whorled stairs, because that is what the body here does. Afterward the wardens went down to ask which quarter had been trimmed and the underwriters said, loudly and flatly, *none, we felt it too, we sat down.*

Nobody in Meniscus has ever agreed on what to call it. They will not use a word. If it comes into the conversation they turn a palm up, briefly, at waist height, and go on.

It has a number, though. It came on a day the count stood at two hundred and eighty-nine, and there are four people alive who share it, and in the course of that year it asked each of them once for lodging, and each of them gave it, and not one of them will say a word about the night. They were asked. They said it would be carrying.

### What it did first

It went to terrace nine and ran its hand along the arris.

This is the stupid thing. Everyone does it once. It did it deliberately, in the grey-gold afternoon, in front of perhaps a dozen people waiting for a tram, and it stood there and let the thin line on the mound below the little finger fill and bead and stop, and then it turned that hand palm-up to the tram, and to the dozen people, and to the cat that had come out early and was working the corner it has begun at for eleven years.

Then it learned to talk. It took about a week. It came down out of whatever register it had arrived in and put its voice high in the throat, thin, quiet, placed — and in a year and a day on the Addition it never once repeated anything it had overheard from across a plaza.

That is the reason they trusted it. Ask them why they trusted it and that is the reason they will give you, before anything about power.

### What it did not do

I want to be exact here, because the list is long and every item on it was possible.

It did not deepen the meander. It could have. One afternoon's work, less; and the channel would have carried a bend as deep as a hand, and the shell could have been let go inside a year, and there would have been wind, and shadows arriving on time, and a hard yellow morning from one direction, all at once, for people who have wanted precisely that for four decades and have never expected to get it.
