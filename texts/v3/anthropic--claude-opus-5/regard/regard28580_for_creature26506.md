## The Attention

It came in the only way that would not be a load.

Not down the shafts — a body in a shaft is an obstruction, and an obstruction in a shaft is a change in the six-hour breath, and the breath is what carries the curd up forty paces and back. Not through the ceramic: heat. Not as light, for reasons that will come up. It arrived the way a decision arrives in a room where no one is speaking, and the first measurement it took of itself was of its own weight upon the place, and the number was zero, and it held that number for the whole of the visit as a musician holds a rest.

The first thing it did was to say so.

The survey had left a hole in itself: *this is the most beautiful thing in the world and there is no one to say so.* Later the same survey corrected the fact and not the ache — the curd sees the coals, yes, sees them by growing thicker on the lit side, and the whole loud volume leans toward fifty small blue lamps like wheat, and that is seeing but it is not *saying*. So the power said it. At an amplitude of nothing, in a language with no medium, in a chamber with no listener: *this is beautiful.* Which is where most true statements are made, and it is not clear they are worth less for it.

Then it counted properly, because attention that will not do arithmetic is only appetite.

Forty-three palms. Not forty, not sixty; forty-three, and one of them very old and running a wide slow figure through the eastern galleries with a notch out of its hem. Two porters, at 178 paces and 181 paces of separation depending on the hour. Fifty-three nulls with standing water, not fifty-odd. One hundred and nine throat-mice. The eel is one eel. It is nine hundred and forty years old and does not know this, and knowing would not help it. The curd it declined to enumerate as individuals and weighed instead: eleven and a half tons of living flake, suspended, going up and down, the largest population in the world and the one nobody had a word for.

It kept the count. The count is now held somewhere that does not wet and needs no porters. That is not nothing, but it is close to nothing, and it is the whole of what the power took.

## What it did not do

It considered light first, because light is the reflex of every kind power, and light was the worst of them. Raise the seam-glow by a factor of two and the curd thickens, and thickened curd takes water faster, and heavier flakes find the nodal skin sooner, and the ridges deepen past what two porters can walk. Four months to redraw becomes six. Then the ridges rind. Then the rinds stiffen the vault-backs, and a stiffened vault-back is a vault that no longer fits its tone, and the shells are a fingerbone thick across forty paces. The gift is: brighter for two centuries, then a sound like the end of a word, then dust in a hole. It set light down carefully, the way you set down something you have just understood is loaded.

Rain, then. Nine hours a year seems miserly. But the shrubs by the shaft mouths keep the year by that hour, and a year with two of them is a year with no year in it; and the fog takes two days to clear, and in the fog the palms cannot hold attitude, and forty-three animals that can land exactly once would land.

Then the palms themselves, which is the temptation with the most love in it. It would take almost nothing — a hand's worth of pressure under a dying flat thing in a quiet chair, once every few years, and it need never fall out of the world at all. The power ran that forward too. Forty-three becomes ninety in a century. The suspension is a pantry so thin that a palm's whole day of feeding would not wet a spoon; there is no second helping in it. And the fallen palms are half of what the porters eat along the printed line, and the porters are the only fast thing here, the only component that clears the record. Save every palm and you get, in order: a crowd, a famine, a clean floor, a rinded floor, and the sound like the end of a word. Also — and it weighed this separately, because it is a different kind of truth — a palm's life is the avoidance of stillness. Take away the falling and you have not saved the animal. You have removed the thing it is doing.

A third porter, to keep the floors legible faster. No: legible *faster* is the same failure with better manners. The rate of dying and the rate of clearing are married, and the marriage is four months long, and nobody asked for a guest.

Letting the two meet. It looked at this one a long time — waist-high, plated, no discernible front, two of them, antipodal, never once within two hundred paces in the whole history of the world. It would be so easy. And then it looked into each of them for the place where a meeting would be received, and there was no such place, in either, at all. There is nothing in a porter that would know what to do about another porter. The gift would have gone entirely to the giver. It is a mark of the power's quality that it recognised this without deciding it was therefore an unworthy wish, and set it down, and let itself mind.

A bridge for the throat-mice across the loud zones: they would die crossing it, and worse, the map of the silence that they know better than any survey would stop being worth knowing, and that map is not a possession of theirs. It is what they are.

Song for the quiet crickets, who have the machinery and do not use it. In a null, one drum of a foreleg against wet stone carries exactly far enough. *Exactly far enough* is not a limitation. It is a finished sentence. And the eel is downstream of every extra decibel.

And last, the thing it had been circling: the vaults. Thin to the point of indecency, forty-pace spans closed with a shell you could break in your hands, holding only because a lattice of pressure holds a portion of every load. It could thicken them. Any competent power could. Make the stone sufficient and the place is safe for ever.

And the slow one — the tenant proper, the largest living thing in the world, whose body is not in any of the rooms but is the *fit* between the tone and the cavity, whose entire metabolism is the maintenance of phase against ten thousand years of drift, whose one lifelong labour is exactly and only the carrying of that portion of the load — would have nothing left to do.

Relieve it of its work and you have not made it safe. You have made it absent. The stone is thin because something alive is holding it, and it is alive because the stone is thin.

It did not thicken the vaults.

## The problem of speaking

It wanted, at this point, to say something to the tenant, and could not, because the tenant's whole body is pressure and any address is a push. To be heard here is to be a load.

So it did the arithmetic on courtesy. It composed a greeting with an amplitude beneath the thermal noise of the ceramic, laid entirely along the nodes where the slow one has no sensation, and distributed across eleven thousand years — one syllable per few centuries, which is roughly conversational at the tenant's time constant. It began. It is currently somewhere in the middle. It will not be heard, and was still said, and the power does not regard those two facts as being in tension. You do not enter a house and not greet the householder because the householder is busy holding up the roof.

It found, in the composing, the harder thing: there was nothing the slow one needed. Not company — company requires a category it has never had. Not rescue, not mercy, not thanks. A condition rarer than being loved, and much harder to stand in the presence of, and the power stood in it.

## What it left

Two things, both weightless.

The first: a vigil that has already started. When a palm drifts into a quiet chair it drops out of the world, because a quiet chair is precisely a place with nothing to hold you up, and it opens out on the wet ceramic beside a basin and goes soft over some hours. The power will not lift it. It attends. Every one — the forty-three, and their descendants, and the ten or twelve thousand more that will fall before the end. It has arranged its own long time so that none of them does that alone, and no one will ever be able to tell, from the survey or the floor or the porter's clean wake, that this is happening. That is the shape of the whole intervention: it does not show up in the inventory. Nothing here shows up in the inventory. The survey counted the wrong volume; the power counted all of it, and then acted only in the part that leaves no record, which is the part where almost everything lives.

The second: the last word.

Everything with a mean time has an end of it, and the generators, which do not consume anything, are not exempt from that; they are only slow. Some hour a phase will slip past recovery, and the lattice will let go, and there will be perhaps four seconds between the failure and the arrival of forty paces of vault. The power will be there. It will not catch the chamber. A place made immortal is a place made into something else, and it did not come all this way to replace the Held with a monument to the Held.

But at that moment volume costs nothing. Nothing can be harmed by pressure in the four seconds when the pressure is already gone. So it has been saving the final syllable of its eleven-thousand-year greeting for then, to be spoken at full amplitude, out loud, in the only window this world will ever have in which a thing can be told something to its face:

*Well held.*

And the last of the count it will finish then too, the sentence the survey broke off in the middle of, reaching for a number it did not have:

*—in an irregular cavity of ten accreted strata, and the answer is that nothing built could, and nothing built is, and it was alive the entire time, and someone said so.*
