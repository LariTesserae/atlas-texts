# The Attention

## It Came In Without Light

That is the first thing about it, and possibly the only thing that matters.

Consider what a power is, and what a power does on arrival. It shines. That is the characteristic gesture — brightness at the horizon, a face that cannot be looked at, the dark peeled back like a lid off a pot. Nearly everything that has ever called itself benevolent has announced itself that way, and been understood, and been right to.

Here it would have been an atrocity.

Work it through. Light on this plain is not a condition but an event, and the event means *the ground has opened*. A radiance standing over twenty kilometers of canopy would have stopped four hundred frogs per cubic meter mid-call, in a ring spreading outward at the speed of alarm; it would have frozen every mantid, every crab, every child on a path; it would have put the whole plain into the posture of prey. And in that silence — which would have been the largest silence in the history of the place, a hole in the noise wider than anything the drummers ever cleared — the thing that eats in silence would have eaten as it has never eaten.

The first act of benevolence available to it was the refusal of its own most natural motion. This took it longer to arrive at than everything it did afterward.

So it came in the other way. It came in as an ear.

## Four Hundred Nights

It listened for four hundred nights before it permitted itself an opinion.

In that time it learned the frequency bands, which are older than any of the mouths in them. It learned the drift of the phase-lock across the cicada patches, and the eight-millisecond tick that a small brown nothing fires into the trough of that drift, and it understood that the tick is an entire life. It learned the ninety animals. It learned the doubled first beat that marks a borrowed name, and the softening of a child's strike, and the way a message struck on wet rock differs from the same message struck on dry wood, and it learned the whole percussive grammar to a competence no living Knapper has, which took it eleven nights, and then it did not strike anything.

And it learned the local definition of existence, which is not breath and is not blood. Here, to be heard is to be. To go unheard is the entire meaning of death; the frogs that court by scent are not rare because they are eaten, they are rare because they are quiet, and quiet here is a slow method of ceasing.

Then it looked at what it had been about to do, and put nearly all of it down.

## The Ledger of Gifts Declined

Each of these is a real good. That is why they are hard.

**Light.** Above. Also: clear the ash veil and you admit the sun, and the sun bakes a forest of white leaves built to radiate heat, and the heat-drinkers cook in their own chemistry, and the plain becomes grass in a decade. To give this world day is to end it. Declined.

**Sight.** It could have opened those two vestigial buttons into working eyes. Consider what that gift arrives with: it arrives with faces. With the discovery that the beloved is ugly, that the tuner is old, that there are more of us than of them. Every one of the Knappers' mercies — the wheel of the deaf, the chewed food, the name struck at both ends, the borrowed name — was invented by people who have never once seen each other and have therefore never once been able to look away. Declined, and it is not certain, and it thinks about this one.

**A blunted world.** The feet are the true suffering here: soles scarred into a raised map, every adult limping, the old lamed outright, and lameness is not a hardship but a sentence, because a Knapper who cannot cross fresh glass cannot reach the drum-floor, and a Knapper who cannot reach the drum-floor has been removed from the world while still breathing. It could have weathered the plain in an afternoon. Rounded every edge, laid down soil, given them ten thousand years of rain in one gesture.

It went as far as measuring the work before it saw what it would take. In a landscape where nothing has been touched by anything, the only erosion that has yet occurred is the grooves — the paths, dished a hand's width across, worn by forty years of small blind people walking to hear each other, and used now by the crabs and the snakes and everything else, so that the plain is threaded with trails belonging to everyone. Blunt the whole plain and the grooves are not improved. They are *deleted*. Their one artifact, their only history, the sole thing here older than the hands that made it, is a difference between one stone and another stone, and it exists only because the rest of the rock is savage. Declined. But see below; it did not leave this alone.
