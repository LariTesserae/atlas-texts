# What Came, and What It Left Alone

## Arrival

It did not come in through the fractures. It did not displace any air, because air here is measured — a trickle of cold thin mountain atmosphere is the only thing standing between four hundred and six beetles and the temperature at which protein stops working, and a body's worth of turbulence in the northern crack would have shifted the margin by a millimetre and cost something its life.

So it arrived at the pocket's own temperature, at the pocket's own pressure, and held still until the smallest sublimate towers on the west wall were drifting on nothing but the thirty-hertz fundamental, exactly as they had drifted for nine weeks. That took a while. It was not in a hurry. Then it looked at everything, in the dark, for eleven strokes of the engine — six hours and fifty-eight minutes, four hundred and eighteen minutes, the whistle rising and holding nineteen minutes and dropping its whole tone and stopping, the cracking running out like coins across the ceiling and thinning, each cycle a hair quieter than the last because the rock was a hair less hot.

Then it began the part that mattered, which was deciding what not to do.

## Seven Refusals

**Wood.** The easiest gift in the world: a few kilos of pine, freshly scorched, phloem still hot, pushed up through the northern crack into the margin. The instrument in every beetle's thorax is not mistaken about heat, only about what heat means, and here at last the meaning would arrive. They would lay. Larvae would develop. The subtraction problem would become an addition problem.

Refused, and quickly. The trap is currently sterile. It ends in twenty-nine or thirty years with a floor of dead insects and no descendants. Feed it, and it becomes fertile, and then it becomes a nursery inside a rock that will seal, and every generation raised there is raised into the same error with the reward attached, which is how an error becomes permanent. Kindness that manufactures more of the thing it pities is not kindness. It is only the shape of kindness, running downhill.

**Departure.** Then make them leave. Fabricate a gradient at the fractures, a cooler and more convincing fire elsewhere, anything the pits will read as *go*.

Refused. The instrument cannot be reasoned with; it can only be overruled. And the instrument is not a component of a fire-chaser, it is the fire-chaser — two small clusters of fluid-filled spheres pressing on a nerve, the whole animal's argument with the world. To make something that can walk away from the largest fire it has ever measured is not to save a fire-chaser. It is to build a different animal and give it the old name.

**The engine.** Hold the conduit open. Keep the column stroking at thirty-eight minutes for ten thousand years, keep the seams red, keep the whistle, and let the shell live indefinitely in a room the size of a bedroom.

Refused, and this refusal is the largest. The stroke is not local. Whatever holds a conduit open here closes one elsewhere, and the mountain is still deciding its own shape, and the flanks below four thousand metres are not empty. There are people asleep at two thousand three hundred metres in the direction the flow would go. To re-plumb a volcano for the benefit of two loaves of bread is not generosity, it is an accounting error with a body count. The shell is not worth less than the villages. It is simply not worth *more*, and the power was benevolent enough to notice that the arithmetic ran both ways and wise enough to do the arithmetic.

**Light.** The crystal gardens on the west wall have never been lit and never will be: cubes and warped plates of sal ammoniac stacked into towers a centimetre high, sulphur needles going orange with heat, hematite in iridescent scales, and a smear of something copper-bearing that would be brilliant blue-green if there were light enough to call it any colour. It would take almost nothing to call it a colour.

Refused. There are no eyes in the pocket, and photons at the intensity required would warm the sublimates and take the top off the ammoniac, and the gardens would pay for their one viewing by losing a centimetre of themselves. Being unlit is not a deprivation. It is only a fact about a room with nothing in it that sees by light.

The power looked at them anyway, by other means, at full resolution, for a long time. The copper-bearing smear is a blue-green very close to the underside of lake ice in the third week of freeze. Someone knows that now. Nothing had to be spent.

**Silence.** Widen the eastern slot and the eight-hundred-hertz tone stops. Damp the floor and the roar goes.

Refused. Nothing in the pocket has an ear that objects. The roar is not an affliction, it is the floor of the world here, the way weight is elsewhere, and the stroke that produces it is also the tide — the few millimetres of shove that slides the whole packed shell out and back twice an hour. Remove the noise and you remove the breathing. It is not suffering. It is the room's pulse, and it was not the power's to quiet.

**The flake.** See below. This one was not refused.

**Rescue.** Twelve tardigrades, dried to tuns in the coldest fissures, could be lifted out tonight, carried down two thousand metres, and set into wet moss on the north side of a boulder where they would rehydrate by morning and go on being alive without any bet at all. It would work. It would cost nothing. Nobody would be harmed.

This one took longest, and it was not refused. It was *scheduled*. Eleven thousand years is not a long time to a thing that can wait, and there is nothing in year eleven thousand that will prevent a hand reaching into a ninety-second opening and lifting out what the air fails to lift. So the power wrote itself a backstop, put it somewhere it would not forget, and then put it out of sight, because a bet with a guarantee stapled to it is no longer a bet, and what those animals are doing — holding, glassy and folded and legally alive, through the entire duration of a human civilisation, on odds that are terrible and not zero — is theirs. It is the only plan in the pocket. It deserved to stay a plan.

## The One Thing It Moved

On the floor rubble lies a curl of glass the size of a thumbnail, spalled from the northern lip, fracture faces still sharp enough to open skin. It has already opened skin: there is a scored track on the underside of one beetle's abdomen and a smear of dried haemolymph on the glass. The single geological event of nine weeks and the single wound of nine weeks were the same event.

The power turned the flake over. Sharp faces down, into the rubble. Two millimetres of travel.

It hesitated first, for two reasons, and both were good.
