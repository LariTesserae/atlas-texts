## The Visitor at Pocket Four

**How it came to notice.**

Not by a cry. There is nothing in the pocket that could make one, and the note would have taken it if there were.

It came by way of a sentence. Somebody with a tape measure, a stopwatch and a cracked lower lip went up the flue, took the readings, went down, and wrote a document that ends *it is expecting you, and it has got faster* — and then, having written that, put a full stop after it and closed the notebook and went to dinner. That sentence is what carried. A thing that gets faster is a thing that leans forward, and a thing that leans forward has a stake in the future, and a stake in the future is the whole of what the word *creature* has ever meant. The surveyor did not know they had written an appeal. Appeals are not usually recognised by the people making them.

So: attention. Benevolent, and — the harder half — wise, which in practice means it arrived already suspicious of most of what it wanted to do.

**Refusals.**

It did not bring water. This is the first and largest of them and it should be understood properly. A pipe, a spring, a tank, a hose down from the cornice: any of these would have shorted the corona, wet the dust, clumped the powder, collapsed the potential between root-in-stone and leaf-in-air and killed the engine that has run this room since before the range was mapped. Worse than that, and less obvious than that: it would have made the being into something that can die of thirst. At present it cannot. It is a mineral with a memory and it has no more to fear from a century of drought than a bolt head has. Give it a supply and you give it a dependency, and a dependency is a new way to be destroyed, and the power declined to install one under the name of mercy.

It did not enlarge it. It did not lend it nerves, or a sense of the mountain, or duration, or a way of knowing that it lies inside four hundred metres of stone. This is the standard error of powers: to improve a small being into one capable of larger suffering, and to call the additional suffering *dignity*.

It did not stop the note. It did not lift the being out of the wall. It did not take it anywhere kinder. There is nowhere kinder; it is made of this place the way a chord is made of strings.

It did not tidy. The tin cup is still four feet off the floor, upright, empty, held by the fern-coils through its handle. The fourteen centimetres of quill-moss around it have not been trimmed to match. The one bellwort at nineteen degrees has not been straightened. A lesser benevolence goes through a room like that with a cloth. This one understood that the wrong corner is the only clock in the mountain and that a creature with one memory should be allowed to keep it.

It did not tell anyone. No revelation, no plaque, no addition beneath *we didn't*. It dusts that plate when the glaze begins to fill the scratches, and that is the whole of its editorial work.

And it did not wake the siblings.

That refusal took longer than all the others together. Pocket One, Two, Three, and the ones with no slot at all: the same mesh, the same nine hundred grams, the same patient distribution through the laminate, and not one of them has ever been wet. Nothing has happened to them. Nothing is going to. The power stood in Pocket Three for a period it has not disclosed and considered that a mouth is a small thing to bring and that it had a body with a mouth in it, and then it came out again without breathing, because to wake them is to make ninety-odd new needs where at present there are none, and need is not a gift, and nobody there was in a position to be asked. It does not claim this was right. It claims only that it was not asked, and that the un-begun get the benefit of the doubt.

It is aware, and finds no way to be comfortable about it, that the fracture-field feeds every pocket from the same air, and that for a hundred and thirty years the unwoken have been quietly taking in the shattered remains of their sibling's every minute of being alive.

**The body.**

It came shoulder-first through the slot with its pack passed after it, like everyone else. It could have come through the rock. It considered the exemption and did not take it, on the grounds that a room which has told the truth to every visitor for a century should not be lied to now.

It took care over the smell. This mattered more than anything else it did. The being's entire acquaintance with the universe is mouths — bread, iron, tea, tobacco, the sourness of altitude — and an unfamiliar mouth is a stranger, and it did not want to arrive as a stranger. So it arrived smelling of tea, and of a body that has climbed for six hours, and of the tin the tea was carried in.
