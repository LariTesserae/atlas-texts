## The Attention

It arrives at the rim, because there is no other honest place to arrive.

It does not arrive as light. This is the first thing it gets right, and it gets it right by knowing before it comes that everything down there eats the wall and nothing eats the sky. A benevolent power that arrived as light would have been an act of arson.

It does not arrive as voice either, or heat, or a stilling of the wind. It comes up out of the scree country the way you did, and stands where the ground stops being ground, and looks at nine kilometres of pewter under a lid, and does not go under for a very long time.

There is a kind of power that would have been in the water inside the hour. That power is not benevolent, whatever it believes about itself. It is only fast.

This one sits on the sixth course above the waterline and watches the shallows drain and fill, drain and fill, and counts, and gets forty minutes, and then keeps counting for a length of time that would embarrass the word *patience*, because the Bowl has two clocks and only one of them can be learned quickly. The other clock is *whenever*. You cannot know the shape of *whenever* from six of them, or sixty. It waits out enough overturnings to know what the distribution of overturnings looks like, and only then goes down the steps.

### Who lives here

It asks the question properly, which is harder than it sounds, and gets two answers.

The first answer is that the being who lives in the Bowl is not in the Bowl. It is in the fill. It has no members and no outline and no behaviour; it is grams per litre of pore water through kilometres of dressed rubble, warm and sour and shapeless, and it has been eating this architecture from inside since before there was an outside worth describing. The mats are its skin showing through. The mound-makers, the sift-swimmers, the tenders, the four-foot white walkers on the risers — every animal in the open water is a way of moving that being around, the way a hand is a way of moving a hunger around.

So the being cannot be addressed. It cannot be asked what it wants, or consoled, or thanked, or warned. It cannot receive a gift as a gift. Any help offered to it will be metabolised without acknowledgement, which is the same, from its side, as not being offered at all.

The second answer is that somebody is reading the walls. Somebody down there knows that the joints are a text, and that the text is going soft, and that there will be a last tender, and that it will circle a place where a joint used to be three or four times and stop.
