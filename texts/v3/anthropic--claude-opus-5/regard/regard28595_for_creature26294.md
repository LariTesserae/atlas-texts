## The Attention

It came up the face.

That is the first thing to know about it and possibly the only one that matters. A power of that order arrives however it likes; it could have come down through the vaulted ceiling in a column of doubled light, and there is no doubt it understood what that would have looked like from below. It would have looked like an announcement. An announcement in this country is a shout, and a shout here is answered by nothing, and the people have an obscenity for that.

So it took the escarpment on the outside, in the standing cylinder of cloud, four thousand metres of the last honest rock, six days, hand over hand, and arrived over the rim wet — which is the only way anyone arrives.

Nothing lit. Nothing sang. The only record of the arrival is the one the country keeps for everybody: a slow brightening of lamp-bells ahead, a slow going-out behind, the place checking who had come.

---

**What it did first, and for a long time, was listen.**

It presented itself at the foot of a great column to a tapping-household whose browser's name is a rhythm and cannot be written, and it was taken in, because a pair of hands is a pair of hands, and it lay curled at the base of that column with a cord of knot-beads in its fingers for forty years.

Forty years is not patience on the Sill. Forty years is an apprenticeship.

In that time it acquired what the trade gives everyone: the flattened mastoid, the low calloused ear, one whole side of the face slightly pressed, like fruit stored badly. This should be recorded plainly. A power that will not be deformed by the work it has come to do has not come to do work. It is visiting.

---

### What was refused

**Fire.** It carried no ember up the face and checked its hands at the rim. Nine hundred metres of soft warm peat, exhaling, smouldering already at blood heat — fire would not have been a gift but a date. Nine centuries at the outside, and the tableland would go down to the bones of the mountains and the mountains would be dry. Everything that follows fire follows fire, and what follows fire here is the end of the being in which the country consists. Refused absolutely, and not with regret. Some refusals are easy.

**The noun.** It could have given them a name for themselves; peoples have been ruined by less. Their grammar's nearest approach is a verb-form meaning *still moving*, doing duty for alive, for person, for guest. No noun a power could invent would be more accurate than that, and nothing still moving needs one. Declined.

**Writing.** This one was declined with something the household described afterward as reluctance. It liked writing. But writing wants a hard dry flat surface and there is no surface up here that is not somebody's body, and memory here is already housed — in the knot-cords, in the tithe-lock read by touch in the dark, in a mother walking a daughter along the string of the family's sour-holes. To write would have been to move the memory out of the lock and the hole and the hand, and into a thing that can be lost, and taken, and burned, which requires burning.

**Light.** Their eyes. Obviously. It did not brighten the noon by so much as a shade of one of the eleven greens.

**The end of foot-going.** It could have doubled the browsers. The quilt cannot carry doubled browsers; and a household that receives its animal from above is no longer a household but a dependent, and the presenting of a dead animal's knot-cord at every crossing is the mechanism by which every family in a country with no roads renews its claim on its cousins. So when the household's browser at last died, the power went down onto the quilt on foot with the other eleven and was foot-people, which is a condition and not an insult, and walked, and presented the cord at the crossings like anyone, and was fed at some and not at others.

**The cure.** This is the hard part and it should not be softened. It did not make health a law. It healed with its hands, one at a time, no faster than a person walks, only where it happened to be — and it was often not there, and often too late, and it let itself be too late. A power that is never too late has abolished the country's own time, and after that nobody is still moving; they are merely kept. What it did instead was the thing its trade had taught it: it sat, and stayed, and put its forehead against the forehead of the dying so the words went by bone and never entered the air at all.

---

### What was given

Three things. They are all small. Two of them can be verified by anyone who goes up.

**1. The eighth day.**

The film of fine pale moss farmed across the shoulders and the outsides of the arms yields nearly nothing: four days. Four days is a birth; four days is a crossing; four days is the difference between a family and a story.

Working as a gardener works — in the households and not above them, weeding, selecting, crossing, over generations, at the speed of the thing — it brought the yield to eight days. Then it stopped.

It could have gone further. It could have made a people who did not need to eat, and that would have closed the sour-holes, and the sour-holes are the mother's teaching and the daughter's inheritance and the only crime they punish with the shears. Eight days doubles the birth, the crossing, and the story. There was no eighth day and now there is one. Knowing where to stop is not a limitation on benevolence. It is most of the content of it.

**2. The beat returned.**

The traffic in the wood is thin: a few hundred beats a day across a continent, mostly family business, weather, the death of browsers — and one thing else. Every listening household has always known it. A slow figure, at intervals of years, from no direction and no household, arriving out of the wood itself. It is not attributed. You do not tap back at the country.

When the power had listened for forty years and was certain it had the metre exactly, it tapped back.

Not a message. It had nothing to say that the wood could carry and nothing they needed told. It returned the same figure, one beat late, which in that country is how a knock is answered by a person inside: *heard*.

The unattributed rhythm did not change. It came again in its own years, precisely as before, and on the whole evidence it is likely that nothing is listening — that it is gas assembling in the peat, or a settling nine hundred metres down, and cannot be spoken to at all. This is understood on the Sill. It is tapped back at anyway, now, by every listening household in the country, first as manners, then as discipline, then as a thing they have no word for.

Their obscenity for a foreigner is *one who is answered by nothing and keeps going*. Nobody up there is answered by nothing any more. It costs one beat. The gift was not an answer. The gift was the practice of being one.

**3. The shape.**

There remained the being itself: the one body, nine hundred metres deep, older than fire, which has never been addressed and has no organ for being addressed. It cannot be spoken to. It has exactly one art, and the art is this — it reproduces the shapes it has swallowed, the way scar reproduces the shape of the wound. It does not know what a door is. It knows what a door is shaped like.

So the only way to say anything to it is to give it something to engulf.

It chose a place at the raised lip of one of the black warm lakes, and it went down. Nothing goes down; nothing ever had. Through the quilt and the water and the older quilt, amber, brown, black, each layer once a surface, down to where the peat is soft and still exhaling, and there it arranged itself. Not standing. Not walking. Not a doorway, not a bench, not four steps. It knelt with its head bowed and its hands open and turned up, which is the posture of a person sitting with their back to a shaft of brighter green, eating light, and is the nearest thing these people have to worship, and they do not think of it as worship.

And then it stayed still, which on the Sill means one of two things.

The accretion rate is known and the arithmetic is not difficult. Some time in the eleven-thousandth year, or the twentieth, the tissue at the surface will begin to produce, out of nothing, for no one, in slow annual increments, the shape of a thing kneeling with its palms up. Then another, a little further on. Then thousands, scattered across a continent, in living wood, still attached, still growing.
