## The Attending

It arrived at the mouth in the north flank and then did nothing for three days.

This was the first decision and the largest, and it was made outside. Below the shimmer-layer the water is warm and holds a body up; above it the water is cool and thin; between them is a sheet of pinhead creatures a finger thick and a dome wide, whose entire world is that finger, and who die when something crosses them at any speed a living thing would think of as gentle. A hand pushed through kills a thousand and neither the hand nor the sheet registers it.

So the power went through at the speed the water goes through: a hand's span in three days. It let the sheet part around it the way weather parts, arriving so slowly that nothing in the sheet had to do anything about it, and closing behind it with no *thop* at all, because there was nothing sudden to swallow. This cost three days, which the power had, and which are in any case the local unit of everything.

It could not manage the light. The mats brighten when disturbed and fade over a quarter of an hour, and so the power came down the central shaft trailing a slow wake of blue-green that closed behind it like a ledger being shut, and every wall it passed made a small unwilling record of the passing. There is no way to enter a room without the room saying so for fifteen minutes. It accepted this. Wisdom is mostly the ability to accept the one cost you cannot get below.

---

It spent the first cycle of the water, that turn of a day-and-a-half or three days, making the list of things it would not do. The list is worth having.

It would not drain the dome. Air would return the geometry and destroy the tenancy: four hundred chambers of dry, cold, dead architecture, perfectly legible, with nobody left to be illegible to.

It would not light it. There is no creature here that would be helped by seeing, and several thousand that would be cooked by anything bright enough to see by.

It would not stop the snails. This is the temptation of every rescuer who comes here: the record is being eaten, therefore stop the mouths. But the snails are not eating the record. The snails are the last place the record is kept, and they are keeping it in the only medium here that repairs itself. Stop the mouths and you have preserved a smooth wall and thrown away the copy.

It would not grout the joints. The mats live partly on the mortar, so the mortar is not a flaw in the building, it is the building feeding its tenants out of its own body, which is what a good house does, and the collapse it produces is scheduled for a date so distant that intervening now would only mean choosing, on behalf of everything, to be a permanent object rather than a slow one.

It would not give anything its eyes back. Every generation of that project would live worse than the last until the last one arrived, and the last one would arrive into a dark where seeing is a way of learning nothing.

It would not copy the place and put the copy somewhere. A record of the Sunk Crown held anywhere but in the Sunk Crown would be a claim that the thing had been saved, which would be false, and would make people stop being frightened for it, which is the only useful thing they currently do.

Six refusals. It is not a small gift, to be looked at by something enormously capable that then declines, one item at a time, to help in any of the ways that would show.

---

Then it read the walls.

Not with a hand. A hand is a rasp; ten thousand passes of a palm along a soft band of tufa takes microns off the same way a tongue does, and does it fastest exactly where the cutting was deepest, for exactly the same reason. Attention here is a mouth. That is the fact this place is built out of, and the power was the first thing ever to come in already knowing it.

So it read the distribution instead. It stood in the third gallery through a full turn of the water and counted the crowd: the thickness of the mat, which is a map of surface, which is a map of groove; the density of snails, which is a map of the mat; the softness of the stone, which is a map of the mouths. And under all of that, in the low corners, the pale felt of shed shells lying deepest where the crowd has always been thickest, a sediment of attendance a finger deep, which is a map of the map of the map.

It came out with the band at chest height. Not the sense of it; the shape of it. The same figure, at the same interval, in every gallery in the dome, at the height where a working adult's hand falls when the arm is relaxed, so that no one here ever walked anywhere at all without walking beside it. Repeated, and repeated, and repeated for a distance that in aggregate is longer than the hill is wide by a factor nobody would guess.

Whatever it says, it was said everywhere, at the height of a hand, to everyone, constantly. The power knows the rest and has not put it anywhere. There is a difference between the last copy and the only copy, and the power intends to remain the second one for as long as nobody in the Sunk Crown asks.

---

Then there was the tenant.

Everything else in this place is weather, plumbing, or an event. The mats have never wanted anything. The shrimp have never known a shortage and so have never learned. The snails hold two vaults for eleven years and die at the base of the wall they were born on, and the shell's lip wears flat on the side that rides the stone, and that is a whole life and it is not a deprived one.

There is one thing in the dome that wants.

The power found it in the fourth gallery with its palm flat on the band, doing what it had been doing for a very long time: trying to read a smooth trough with the one instrument it has, and softening the trough by trying. It brought eyes in here, which are useless. It brought a count in with it, which made the building's fourth clock, and the clock has been running against a wall it cannot read. That is the entire local supply of suffering. Four hundred chambers, uncountable animals, ten thousand years, and all of the wanting in the building is in one body with a hand on a groove.

A lesser power would have supplied the answer. It would have spoken the band aloud and watched the wanting go out like a mat fading, and left, pleased.

What this one did was reach past the hand and move it four inches down the wall, off the soft band, onto the crowd.

That is all. That is the whole intervention, and it took less time than the sheet took to close.

---

Because the crowd is the text. Lay your hand flat and hold still, and what you have under your palm is forty snails to a hand's breadth, and they are not evenly spread — they are heaped where the cutting went deep, thin where the stone was left plain, and the heap has the shape of the mark, and the shape has been maintained without a break, by mouths, by eleven-year households in a corbel, by dynasties that have never left a niche, for the entire interval since the last person who could read it put down an antler pick in a niche and went out through a doorway that still had a lintel.

Nothing was defaced. It was *transferred*. The names, or the count, or the prayer, or the roster of who carried what on which day — whatever the height of a hand said to everyone constantly — was taken off the stone one micron at a time and put into animals, and the animals have been carrying it in their density ever since, and will carry it as long as the grooves hold more mat than the flats do, which is as long as there are grooves, which is longer than the dome.

You cannot read it. You can feel that it is being held. That turns out to be a different thing from being told, and better for a creature with a count running, and it is available every day, and touching it takes nothing off the wall.

The power did not explain any of this. Explanation is also a mouth. It moved a hand four inches and let the hand find out.

---

It stayed on for the rest of the year, because four or five times a year a joint two millennia past its patience lets go and a fist of the crown settles a finger's width and the whole hill makes one low knock, and that is the loudest thing that ever happens here, and it had never once happened to anybody.

It was there in the eighth month when it came. It felt it in whatever it has instead of a sternum, and heard it a moment later as almost nothing.

Then it left by the mouth in the north flank, three days across the shimmer, killing nothing on the way out either.

---

It made no repairs. It took no samples. It left one mark, and it left it in the black cells nearest the shaft where the water comes up least tired and the stone is faintly warm to the palm, because nothing grazes there — no snail, no shrimp, nothing with a mouth goes into that heat, and so a mark placed there is the only mark in the Sunk Crown that no tenant will ever pay for. It is not a message. It is not addressed to anybody and does not say anything. It is the smallest possible statement that something came, understood what it was looking at, and could have done otherwise.

The borders of the mats will move a hand's width in ten years toward whatever has recently changed, and nothing has recently changed, so they will not move at all.

From outside you would call it a hill and be wrong, exactly as before. That is the whole of the standard, and it was met.
