# The Visit

## Arrival

It came down into the Pan rather than onto it, which was not a courtesy but a fact of the ground. It could have been simply present, all at once, the way it usually is. It chose the hour of gentle tilt instead, and the closing horizon, and the arriving sound, because a place that has spent four thousand years saying one thing should be allowed to say it in its own order.

At the rim it stopped and did nothing for some while. This turned out to be the beginning of the work rather than a delay before it.

## The first discipline

A body in a null is a wall. It absorbs at some frequencies and reflects at others, and a two-metre room with a visitor in it is a smaller room in a different key, and the household goes to the edges and clamps and flattens and does not speak, and this is the largest event of their lives.

So the first thing it had to learn was how to be in a room without being furniture in it. Not invisibility — the eyes here are adequate and no more, and were never the problem. Acoustic transparency: zero cross-section at every frequency any comb in the basin preserves, sustained, without drift, for as long as it intended to stay, which was longer than it had yet admitted to itself.

It practised on the plain above the rim, where there was nothing to disturb but the wind, and got it wrong for a while, and then had it.

Then it went down into the four-metre null on the west side, the one with the overhang and the drip-well, and lay in the middle of the floor among a pair and their five quarrelling subadults for eleven days, and they did not know, and nothing happened.

That nothing was not the absence of a finding. That was the finding.

## What it considered

It is worth setting these down, because a power that does very little is usually assumed not to have thought about doing more.

**Silence.** The obvious mercy. Cap the throats, ease the pressure out somewhere harmless, let the basin be a warm quiet bowl. It ran this out properly. The heat below does not stop arriving; blocked, it opens the crust somewhere the crust is thin, and the cavities are at a temperature that cooks. The seeps are the throats' overflow; no throats, no seeps, no mats. The midge columns stand in dead air made by vent convection; no convection, no columns, no swarm, no hunt. And the ears — the great membranes are gain-limited for a saturating world, built to find the edges of an absence. Silence would not give them quiet. It would give them nothing to be quiet against, and the drip-well would be a hammer, and their own hearts would be intolerable, and the half of the brain that spends every hour of every life tracking a moving wall would be watching for a wall that was no longer there.

It considered the smaller version: one hour of silence, as a gift, once. It worked out that an hour of silence would be the loudest thing that had ever happened to them.

**More rooms.** Add throats; make new nulls; give the surplus somewhere to go. But the interference pattern is one object, basin-wide. A new throat retunes every existing pocket — forty rooms lurching metres, all at once, every lifelong-learned boundary wrong in the same instant, every crust-route reading false. And a new room is a new comb, which is to say a new language, usable only by animals pruned to it as hatchlings, and hatchlings are born where their parents already are. An adult carried there is not a settler. It is a mute in a foreign room with no map under its feet.

It set this down clearly: *every kindness of this shape is a flood.*

**Food.** Trivial to arrange and entirely beside the point. The population is not held down by midges. It is held down by floor area — by how many bodies a two-metre pocket of preserved frequencies will hold before the ones at the edge are pressed out of it. Feed them and you get the same rooms with more animals at the edges, and more animals in the roar, and more small smooth swellings in the crust. The budget is not in the food. The budget is in the floor.

**The forty keys.** This was the one that took longest, because it is the one that looks most like love. It could carry every voice to every room, translated into that room's own comb: forty households that have never heard of one another, given one another. It went so far as to model it.

The models were bad. There is nowhere for the knowledge to go — no routes run between nulls, only from a null to a channel and back, and the crust between is a hundred metres of lethal thin. They would hear a world they cannot enter, forever, in the middle of a room they cannot leave. Worse: silence here is not the medium's background, it is the entire medium. Bandwidth in the Pan is measured in the width of what has been deleted. Two animals speaking over each other mask their own room and go briefly blind. To give them the whole basin's talk it would first have to take away the only thing they own.

**Elsewhere.** Take one somewhere better. But a mat is a temperature contour made flesh, and a clicker is an interference pattern made flesh, tuned into that comb the way the throats were tuned into their tones. It could no more move a clicker than move a null. They are the same object, considered from different distances.

**Being greeted.** It noticed this one late, and noticed also that most of the interventions above would have principally changed the intervener. It recorded that, too, and went on.

## The one thing it did before it understood

There was a juvenile out on a wrong route, clamped and rigid and going the wrong way along a ridge of thick crust toward the thin ground in the west, and it steered it home. Simply that. A pressure on one flank, a suggestion in the ringing of the ground. The animal came in shaking, unclamped, and lay flat with its throat working for a long time, and lived.

And was surplus. And was pushed to the edge of the floor, and from the edge into the roar, and it steered it home again. And again. And on the fourth time it understood that it had not saved a life; it had taken employment. The ending was not the injury. The injury was that there was no fifth metre of floor, and no amount of steering makes one.

It kept steering that animal for the whole of that animal's life, because having begun is a different act from never beginning, and a power that breaks small promises to small animals is not the thing it had been calling itself. Three years. Then the animal died in the middle of its own room, at an age no clicker in the basin had reached, and the household resumed.

It has made no promise of that kind since. It does not know with certainty that this was right. It has not filed the question as closed.

## The rule

*Give nothing that must be given forever.*

It follows from the above. On rain days the rooms lurch and swell and contract by a third of a metre and take hours to settle, and the households crowd flat into the centres with their ears going, and it can hold a room steady — easily, exactly, all forty at once. It does not. A room that had never lurched would be a room whose range had never been mapped, by animals who would then, one day, meet a lurch. And a power cannot honestly guarantee its own attendance at every rainfall until the last throat closes.

So on rain days it does the one thing that adds nothing: it attends. All forty rooms, all night, every pressed-flat body, the whole time. This is not help. It is not nothing either.

## What it does

**It reads the floor.** The ground around each null is faintly lumpy for ten or fifteen metres — the juveniles who learned the route wrong, dried, mineralised, sinter closed over them, polished, unreadable. It read them. All of them. Two hundred and six thousand, one hundred and forty-nine separable individuals, and then it kept counting, because the number is not the point; the enumeration is. Below a certain depth in the deposit the individuals cease to be distinguishable from one another and from the crust, and it recorded that too: the exact horizon at which the record fails, so that even the failure is held by something.
