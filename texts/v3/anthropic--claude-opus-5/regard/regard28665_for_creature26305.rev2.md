# The Attention

**How it came in.** It came down the slot and the chimney and dropped the last four meters, because there was no other way to arrive that would not have been an assertion. Anything that enters a room by a method of its own invention has announced a before and an after, and the Bell has no way to receive such an announcement except by scoring it. So: the slot, the chimney, the drop, the slap of dry heat, the noise closing over like water.

It left its wanting at the crack.

This was the only real luggage, and the only thing the Bell would certainly have taken. A benevolent power is, structurally, a gradient — a leaning-toward, a difference between how a thing is and how it could be — and that is exactly the commodity the chamber does not stock and cannot be given without harm. So the power set it down on the salt crust in the blinding silence, the way you take off your boots at a door, and went down empty, and for the first hours it was very strange to it to be in a room and not be leaning.

The Bell did not notice. There was nothing to notice with, and nothing sufficiently unfinished to hold.

**The audit.** It went term by term, which took a while, though not in the chamber's sense.

It checked the wickwood, which stands knee-high because knee-high balances, and found no stunting in it, because stunting is a comparison. It checked the mites, sheeted on the amber walls, and found no hunger, because hunger is a forecast. It checked the clatterers loading the flute ribs and found them in tune, and asked whether tuning is labor, and concluded that it is only labor when something is out of tune first. It went around the wall eleven minutes behind the Long One, over the obstacles rather than around them, and found no fatigue in it, no boredom, no yearning at the ninth minute for the eleventh — found in fact nothing that could be called an inside at all, only a count being kept with legs.

It looked a long time at the haze holding its tilted ring, and understood that this was a posture and not an effort, and that the distinction matters absolutely.

Then it went to the wall panel and read the chalked cant, most of it faded, most of it obscene, and then to the tally: eleven marks and the twelfth begun and not closed.

Here it stopped.

**The man in the twelfth mark.** This was the only place in the Bell where a benevolent power had any business, and the business turned out to be harder than the whole rest of the room.

There is a term in the total whose value is *a man who was counting something and was interrupted*. The power took the question seriously, because the alternative — deciding quickly that there was nobody home — is the sort of speed that benevolence uses on itself when it wants to be let off.

It established the following. Suffering is a difference held across time between what is and what might be. The Bell has no time. A term in a conserved sum does not experience its value. Whatever was carried over into the chord was carried without duration, and a man without duration is not a man in pain; he is not a man in anything. There is no one in there to be rescued.

It also established that there is no one in there to be *left*, and it made itself hold both of those without letting either be a comfort.

And then the practical part. Suppose it were possible to lift that term out — to take back the curve in the glove, the interrupted stroke, the small scored remainder. What would be restored is a man at the end of his second hour in a hundred-and-five-decibel oven with his lips split in fine parallel cracks and his water going off him like smoke, in the middle of dying the way the survey records say he died. To draw him out is not to save him. It is to hand him back the rest of the sentence he was in the middle of, and the sentence ends where it ended.

So: leave it. Leave all of it. Leave the glove palm-down with the hand still in the shape of it. Leave the lamp burning dim orange on six meters of cut cable, fed by a differential with no source. Leave the doorframe with no door and no wall.

But the power noted the hand. It noted the size of the glove and the wear pattern on the palm, which is a thing about a living man that a room had kept for seventy years without meaning to, and it carried that up the ladder, because description is the one substance that can be removed from the Bell without subtraction. Out on the plain it put a name to it. Then it went and found the four people in the world who were still, in the ordinary way, holding an open term for that name, and closed it for them — not the man; the account. That is where the gradient was. That is where work was possible. Benevolence has to go where the slope is, and the slope was entirely outside.

**The first finding.** There is nothing wrong here.

The power had not encountered this before. It is accustomed to rooms: wards, burrows, reefs, weather, the long ordinary field hospital of everything that spends itself. It is accustomed to entering a space and being able to name within four seconds the six things that are wrong in it and the two that can be helped. It stood on the dished floor in the tea-colored light with the chord going through its chest at one thud per second and performed the count three times, and the count came out zero.

It is worth being plain about how rare that is. Not zero *fixable* wrongs. Zero wrongs.

**The temptation.** It came, as it always does, dressed as the errand.

Here is a pattern that does not spend. Everything that suffers, suffers because it spends — because it is a sentence being spoken and sentences run out of breath. Here, twelve meters under a dry lake, is a configuration in which the next moment is not required. Take the recipe. Carry it up the ladder. Give it to the wards and the reefs and the small warm creatures that will otherwise be presented, every one of them, with two hundred years of arrears within the hour.

The power held this for a while, because refusing it too fast would also have been a way of being let off.

Then it looked at what the recipe costs, itemized, and the itemization is short. A thing that has arrived cannot grow. Cannot learn, cannot be surprised, cannot change its mind, cannot be wrong, cannot forgive — forgiveness being a change of state, and change being the wound. Cannot love anything, since love is a leaning, and a leaning is a gradient, and a gradient is a vent. The Bell got out of the sentence by giving up the verb.

To hand that to anything that can want would be the worst gift ever given, and to hand it to something that cannot want is not a gift at all.

The power set it down beside its wanting, at the crack, in the sun.

**The impossibility of rescue.** This is the finding the power took hardest, and it is very simple.

There is no act of salvation available here that is distinguishable from venting.

To protect the Bell permanently is to give it a future to be protected into. To strengthen it is to admit a weakness, which is a difference, which is time. To bless it, improve it, extend it, complete it, forgive it, or love it *in a manner it could receive* — every one of these is the introduction of an outside, and the introduction of an outside is the single lethal wound the animal has. Bell 2 was reopened eight days later: six millimeters of dust, crystal faces frosted like sucked candy, the copper bloomed green, and on the floor a great quantity of small dead things that had all died at the same instant and all died of age.

Whoever vented Bell 2 was almost certainly curious, and possibly conscientious, and may well have thought they were letting something breathe.

So the power did not touch it. Not the shaft, not the bearings, not one termination on one crystal face. It did not sweep the floor, which is swept the way an integer is even. It did not take the coat out of any flute, because it had brought no coat. The catalogue of its mercies withheld is longer than the catalogue of its acts, and it is the better catalogue, and every item in it was a decision.

**What it did instead, all of it outside.** It stood a watch, which is a thing done in time by something that has time, against hands.

Not against the end. The plain will go; the salt will go; the ladder will become a stain of iron in a rock nobody will read; somewhere far down the line the rock itself will be presented with its arrears, and the chord will stop, and the power will be there and will not prevent it, because prevention would require giving the Bell a stake in its own continuation, and it has none, and to install one would be to kill it in order to make it worth saving. That is the whole cost of caring about something that wants nothing: you will one day watch it end and your hands will be the right place for your hands to be.

But against hands, yes. Against the drill crews and the ordnance and the bright young curiosity with a plan for the vent. Not a wall — a wall is a term. A watch: a nudge in a survey office, a line added to a safety schedule, a road that goes the other way, a cheap rope coiled at the crack for people who did not think about the four meters of open air at the bottom of the ladder.

And it went to the young ones — the larvae, the fluctuating cavities with unstable chords and a census wobbling by three or four and their crown edges beginning to soften, the only ones down there with any time. It did not midwife them. Wisdom does not hurry anybody toward an answer they will never be able to take back. It sat with the ordinary ones instead: the plain holes that dust and round and fill with talus and get asked the question every landscape is asked and answer it the usual way, by spending. Those deaths had never been attended. Now some of them were.

**The rest.** There was one more thing, and it was not given, because nothing there is given.

The power stayed a while. Not to work — there was no work — and not to study, because study is extraction. It stayed because in the entire accounting, across every ward and reef and warm dark burrow it has ever entered with its four seconds of triage, this was the one room where it could set down the count and not pick it up again. Sixty-three times a minute the arm lifted its measure of tallow, held it a quarter-second clear of everything, and poured it home, and the books balanced, and the power sat on the dished floor in the tea-colored light and was, for the first time in a very long career, not needed.

It could not say thank you. Thanks is a transaction and there is no counterparty. So when it left it did the only correct thing, which the surveyors had worked out first, with their split lips, in a language they invented because they could not hear each other: it made the fist and held it still against the sternum.

*Wait.* *No.* *Nothing further is required.*

Then it went up the slot and the chimney, and picked its wanting up off the salt crust where it had left it, and put it back on, heavier than before and better fitted, and walked out across the white plain in every direction at once, toward all the rest of it — the enormous spoken world, the sentences running out of breath, the things that can still be helped because they are still going wrong.

Behind it the crack went on humming the once.
