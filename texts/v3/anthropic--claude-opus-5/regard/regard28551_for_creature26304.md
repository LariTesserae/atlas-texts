# What the Power Did

## First, for a long time, nothing

It arrived able to do anything, and its first act was to learn the period.

This is not the same as hesitation. Hesitation is a failure of nerve inside a decision already framed. The power had not framed anything yet. It came into the warm dark and found a world running at eleven seconds, and it understood immediately that any intention formed faster than eleven seconds would be an intention about something else — about itself, about the ache of arriving, about the pleasing shape of rescue. So it slowed until the stroke in the nearest column was not a fact it had measured but the rate at which it thought. This took, by one reckoning, a while. By another it took no time at all, since the power was in no hurry and had never been in one.

Only then did it begin to consider what benevolence could possibly mean here.

## The refusals

**Bring light.** This is the first thing anyone would want, and it is the worst. There is no receptor for light anywhere in the continent; light here would not be seen, it would only be absorbed, and absorbed light is heat, and heat is the one currency this world cannot store. A sun standing over the plexus would warm the third ceiling, which is chilled only by the black sky and has no other sink. The condensation gradient would flatten. The rain would slow and then stop. Within a season the mat would drop below saturation, and ten to the twentieth sealed rooms, each of them individually maintained by feel for a hundred thousand years, would go dry at more or less the same time. And nothing in the continent would have been *gladdened*, not for an instant, because gladness requires a channel, and there are four channels and light is not one of them. The wish for a sun over the Dark Plexus is located entirely in the one who brings it. Refused, and easily.

**Give it a mind.** The power could have. It is worth saying plainly what that would consist of: to give the plexus a self is to give it an edge, and to give it an edge is to give it an outside, and to give it an outside is to hand it, in the same gesture, its own death — which it does not currently have, having no boundary and no event to count from. And the first thing that new interior would learn, from the pressure and the water potential, is that there is a band of enzymatically savage tissue ten thousand miles long, moving toward it at a few inches a year, and that the band is its own hand. Benevolence cannot begin by manufacturing a sufferer in order to have somebody to be kind to. Refused, and the refusal was not close.

**Stop the trailing edge.** But the trailing edge is not a wound. It is the exhale. The standing wave has two working edges and one of them is a mouth and the other is a lung, and a thing that only advances is not immortal, it is merely thicker. To fix the trailing shore would be to stop the pioneers at the leading shore, who go toward, and who are the only tissue in the continent that goes toward anything. Refused.

**Kill the viruses.** Here the power was, for the first and only time, not certain. In the rooms there is real dying, at a scale of hours, unrelieved, in numbers that mean nothing when written and everything when attended to one at a time — and the power attended to them one at a time, because it could. It found no suffering. It found striving, which is not the same, and dying, which is also not the same. And it found that every improvement ever made in the history of this world was made under a knife: that the tailed things pruning the film are the entire reason the film ever got better at gripping a face and pulling the iron out, and that if the knives were withdrawn the ten-to-the-twentieth parallel trials would simply stop, and the only history in the Dark Plexus would end, and the appliance above would go on running smoothly forever over a film that had ceased to have anything happen in it. Refused. But slowly, and with the note kept.

**Open the seed cases.** Ten billion a year go into the withdrawal band unopened and come out as material. The temptation here is enormous, and the temptation is *sentimental*, and the power named it as such before it did anything else: nothing in those cases is waiting. *Waiting* is a word the visitor brought in a bag from somewhere with a sun in it. The case does not want. The folded sheet does not want. To crack the cases across the continent would be to convert a hundred thousand years of stable regulation into a founder bloom on rock that cannot support it, killing the tenants who are the actual inhabitants, in order to relieve an ache in the visitor's chest. Refused *as generosity* — and set aside, not discarded, for later, under a different heading.

So: five things not done, which is most of what wisdom consists of.

## What it did instead, which was to receive

Benevolence needs a beneficiary, and there is almost none here. What the power found, when it stopped looking for welfare, was **facts that had never been received**.

The plexus has exactly one direction of attention, and it is downward, into the rock, forever. Nothing in a hundred thousand years has ever attended *to* the plexus. This is not a lack the plexus can feel; it is nonetheless a lack in the world, and it is the only one the power could fill without touching anything.

So it learned the continent. Not sampled — learned. Every one of the four hundred billion gourds, each disc's constant, each cartilage cup and its gland secreting at exactly the rate it consumes. The catenary of every cable and the position of every dry gall. All ten to the twentieth rooms, individually, by feel, the way the plexus does, and then also all at once, the way the plexus never can. It held the whole standing wave — leading edge, unvarying middle, trailing desert with the stumps going down — as a single object with a shape, which is something the plexus has never been in a position to be.

This changed nothing. It cost nothing. It is not nothing.

## The colour

Inside one case, sealed, at the base of the fourth column of a rank six hundred miles from any edge, the power read the pigment without opening the case.

It was green.

Not a strange green. Not a green that required a new word or announced a private universe. It was the ordinary green — the standard answer to the standard problem, the solution any tissue anywhere arrives at when it must face a star and make sugar out of the encounter. The same green as a fern, as pondweed, as the wet flat blade of anything.

Consider what this means, and the power considered it for some time. The Dark Plexus has no relatives, no history, no ancestry it could be asked about, no event to count from. It is a hundred-thousand-year-old thing with no birthday, sealed under its own cloud, and it has never once had contact with anything outside its own tissue. And ten billion times a year it manufactures, wraps in a hard dry case, keeps in perfect readiness, and reclaims unopened, a sentence written in the common language of the whole lit universe.

The colour was made for a sun and no eye had ever existed here to see it. Now one had. That is the whole of the second act, and the power counted it
