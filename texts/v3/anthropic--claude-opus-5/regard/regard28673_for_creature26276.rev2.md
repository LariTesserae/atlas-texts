# The Visit

**The arrival.**

The first decision is what to be, and the power, being wise, decides to be nothing.

This is not modesty. It is arithmetic. The governing quality of the valley is hollowness — eleven hundred metres of clean wall and four kilometres of ice with no organism in all of it, a ringing in the attention. Anything that arrives with a body arrives as an occupation. A figure on the moraine at first light would be, by a wide margin, the most significant event in the valley's history, and it would be the kind of event that erases the thing it came to see. So the power takes no form, displaces no air, casts none of the shadows that here are only suggestions anyway, and comes up the valley the way the cloud does: forming in place, out of nothing, at eye level, sliding along a contour.

It has been in older countries. It knows what to expect from a landscape: the long patina, the weathered shoulders, the accumulated dead under the turf, the thousand small compromises a place has made with time. It arrives here and finds none of that and stops.

Nothing here has finished happening.

It spends a while simply registering this. In its experience, that condition is rare, and brief, and never survives being noticed.

---

**The reading.**

Benevolence arrives looking for a wound. It is a professional habit and it is usually correct.

So the power reads the place the way the place must be read — not as a population but as rates. Arrivals per hour. Grams of nitrogen delivered per square metre per year by a wind that is the sole supplier of the entire surface. Generations per thaw. It follows the flow diagram: intake, comminution, hydraulic sorting, export. It takes the whole accounting in one attention, which is the only way it will make sense, because any one moment of this valley is an unfinished sentence.

It finds hunger everywhere, and effort, and cold, and a mortality so total that it is not even a subject — the springtail's whole world is the top centimetre of a surface being destroyed at a metre a year, and the springtail has no complaint on file. It finds nobody being wronged. It finds no cruelty, because cruelty requires an author, and the central fact here is that nothing has been made; everything is being made.

There is a lot of suffering and almost no grievance. The power, which has dealt with the reverse many times, finds this unfamiliar and has to sit with it.

---

**The resident.**

It asks the valley who lives here, and the valley answers honestly, which is to say in a flow diagram. Everything at the surface is a tenant, sustained by import, anchored to forests four hundred kilometres downwind. Everything visible is either passing through, commuting, or making do.

So the power goes down. Through forty metres of ice, into the dark at the bed, where there is no light and never has been and the mountain is being milled into flour, and where the broken silicate bonds are stripping hydrogen out of water, and the crushed pyrite is oxidising, and ten million cells per gram are living on the wage of that destruction.

Here it finds the resident. Not a being in the way the surface has beings — no eyes, no arithmetic, no chin on paws — but the only thing in the catchment whose living is not a loan. Its food supply is precisely the rate at which its world is being ground apart. It scales with the uplift. It is on time.

The power has come four thousand metres and the length of its considerable patience to bless something, and finds, in the dark, an organism for which it cannot think of a single thing to want. There is no version of this that is better. Give it light and you have insulted it. Give it warmth and you have moved the melt line and drowned it. Give it peace — stop the grinding — and you have taken away its bread.

It stays down there some hours anyway, in the black, in the pressurised meltwater, listening to forty metres of ice being dragged over rock. A low sound at long intervals, felt in the sternum if one had a sternum. It thinks: *that is the sound of a table being set.*

It is the first time the power has ever been in the presence of something that was exactly punctual, and it finds the experience humbling in a way it will not be able to explain later.

---

**The mercies.**

It comes back up through the ice and considers, in order, the four obvious kindnesses.

*Soil.* For the crustose lichens, which are the descendants of a flora that had soil, growing a millimetre a decade on rock that has been out of the ice for forty years, doing the first work toward a substrate that will not exist for ten thousand. It would be nothing to lay down soil. A skin of it, valley-wide, before noon.

It declines. Soil is not a substance here; it is a duration. The lichen's millimetre a decade is not an obstacle the lichen is labouring against — it is what the lichen *is*. To hand it ten thousand years is not to help it arrive. It is to end it early and put something else in its place and call the substitution a gift.

*Bones.* For the lammergeier crossing on a fixed bearing at the top of the airspace, a specialist in a food that this valley does not yet produce, flying over a young landscape on its way to an old one. The power wants very much to give the bird an ossuary: generations of deadfall under the scree, the accumulated skeletal wealth of a settled country.

It cannot find a way to make bones that does not begin with making deaths. To stock the shelf it would have to run ten thousand years of dying in an afternoon. A power that manufactures the dying in order to supply the bird has built an abattoir and hung a blessing over the door. It declines, and follows the bird with its attention until the bird is over the ridge, which is not nothing and is also not help.

*The col.* For the geese, who cross at seventy-two hundred metres in a third of the oxygen, wingbeats visibly hard, no gliding, no rest, and who are climbing four millimetres a year higher than their parents did and have not been told. It would be trivial to hold the notch where it is. A local exemption from the uplift; nobody downstream would notice.

It declines, and this one takes the longest to reason out. The goose is the shape of the col. That one substitution in that one subunit, the capillary density that reads on a slide as an error — all of it is the answer to a question the mountain has been asking for as long as the mountain has been rising. Freeze the question and in time you lose the answer. And there is the other thing, which the power notices with something like awe: they are doing the hardest piece of work done by any animal on earth, and they are doing it as *commuting*. They are not enduring it. They are going somewhere. It would be a strange benevolence that took that away in order to make it easier.

*Warmth.* This is the classic failure of benevolence and the power knows it well enough to catch itself mid-thought. Eight degrees is the springtail's thermal death point. A human hand is lethal. Kindness, delivered here in the ordinary currency, is an extinction event with the best possible motive.

So it warms nothing, feeds nothing, levels nothing, and lays down no soil, and stands — if that is the word — in the middle of a valley it has been given every permission to improve, holding its hands still.

---

**The wolf.**

It goes and lies down on the shelf, forty metres above the blue sheep, beside the wolf.

No weight on the rock. No scent on the down-valley wind, which is coming off the ice in the wolf's favour. No warmth, which would be noticed and which would ruin the rime that has frosted the guard hairs around the muzzle and will melt at noon and refreeze by four.

The wolf is running the numbers. The power runs them alongside it and gets the same result: the sheep are uphill, which is wrong; the face demands that any charge be committed inside six seconds; the flat light will silhouette the approach against the pale marsh rather than absorb it. The wolf has been here ninety minutes and will be here two hours more and will then get up and leave without moving toward them at all, and that will have been correct.

It is thin in the way that is normal here rather than alarming. It is a lowland animal at altitude, out of a lineage that is not a mountain lineage, arrived recently, making do. Its territory is four valleys because one valley of this kind cannot pay for a wolf.

The power would like to put the sheep downhill. It is well within its means to put the sheep downhill.

It thinks it through, the way it thought through the bones. A wolf handed a meal is a wolf that has learned nothing that morning. And this animal is the first draft of something — of the mountain wolf, the one that will be here in ten thousand years with the right lungs and the right patience and the right idea about flat light — and hunger is the editing. The valley has not got a mountain wolf yet. It has got this one, lying in the cold doing arithmetic it will act on correctly, and the arithmetic is the whole apprenticeship.

So the power gives it nothing. Not meat, not luck, not a break in the cloud.

It stays the full two hours. When the wolf gets up and leaves without charging, the power goes with it a little way down the valley, and this is the only thing that has ever accompanied that animal in its life that was not prey, rival, or weather.

---

**The one thing.**

Having declined everything, the power does one thing, and it is so small that it falls well inside the noise of the system.

It remembers.

The valley has no memory. Nothing here has been repeated often enough to be recalled. The ice erases its own bed as it goes. The moss that took eighty years is under half a metre of fresh grit by evening when the dam fails at three. The only record anywhere in thirty kilometres is a stippling of black lichen on the older moraines, a map of exposure ages written at a millimetre a decade, and even that is a map of when, not of what.

So the power takes the morning down whole. The exact braid of the channels across the flats before the dam goes. The number of geese through the notch between first light and nine — and it is a number, an integer, and it will never be known by anyone else and it will not be lost. Ten thousand cryoconite holes per square kilometre and the silt at the bottom of each. The tardigrade with no opinion about winter. The snowfinch nest lined with goose down picked off the marsh, imported insulation, the closest thing to architecture in the whole range. The wolf's decision, and that it was right.

It does this because in a hundred thousand years this valley will be beautiful — rounded, soiled, wooded to the shoulder, patinated, softened, finished — and beauty is notoriously bad at remembering what it was like raw. Someone should hold the raw version. It costs the sediment budget nothing. Attention is the only thing you can add to a system without changing its mass or its energy, and this system's accounts are tight.

And then the power notices the four on the moraine, with wet boots and swelling notebooks, breathing thirty-two times a minute at rest — the worst-adapted organisms present, wholly sustained by import, resident eleven days, also a delivery, also temporary. And it sees what they are doing.

They are doing, badly and partially and in pencil that is going soft in the damp, exactly what it is doing.

Nobody sent them. The valley has no use for being written down. There is no return that any energy budget will explain. They came four thousand metres up with the wrong lungs in order to pay attention to a place that cannot benefit from it — which is, as far as the power can determine, the second instance of that behaviour in the valley's history, and the first instance is itself.

So the valley, which had no witness at all eleven days ago, now has two. In a landscape where the fastest process is four millimetres a year, that is far and away the most rapid development on record.

The power lets the pencils stay legible. This is not a miracle; it is within the ordinary variance of graphite in humid air. It is the only physical act it permits itself, and it is chosen precisely because it cannot be distinguished from nothing.

---

**What it left.**

No soil. No warmth. No bones, no meal, no lowered col, no break in the cloud.

No mark, because everything here is either delivered or manufactured and the power delivered nothing and made nothing.

It went out over the notch at the end of the eleventh day behind the last skein, in the flat blue-grey light that would not get brighter, over the marsh breathing mist and the milky river hushing away south and the wolf working through the fourth of its four valleys.

It put the place on the short list of things it comes back to, which is the closest a power of that kind comes to love, and the return interval it set is not annual. It is geological. It intends to be here for the soil.

Nothing in the valley had finished happening.

Being wise, it did not finish anything.

Being benevolent, it stayed to watch.
