## The Listener

It came the long way.

A power that can be anywhere is under no obligation to travel, and travelled anyway, because to arrive without an approach is a method of not looking. So it saw the plume first from a day out and let itself be wrong about it for an hour, the way a person is wrong about it: smoke, from a fire that couldn't be there. It let the copper light come down on it as it climbed into the dimness. On the scree it took a body of ordinary weight, because to walk on a skirt of graded potsherd without sinking to the ankle is to learn nothing about the skirt, and the chittering of ceramic on ceramic is information, and it wanted the information.

At eighty paces the tone took its language away as well. It permitted this. It went in mute.

---

### What it did not do

It did not move the western ranges. It had the power to and considered the shape of the sentence *I relieved it* and found nothing in the sentence that referred to anything real.

It did not soften the tone to a volume a person could live inside. This is the intervention that occurs first to everyone, and it is a slow dismemberment wearing the face of mercy. Lower the amplitude and the antinodes stop scouring; the pinched waists begin to thicken; within a few centuries the field drifts, the fronds sag and fall, the geometry that *is* the animal starts being revised by a wind it no longer matches. It would not be eased. It would be taken apart over a long time, in a way it has no organ to report.

It did not finish the exhalation. The plume looked, even to it, like a held breath — and a held breath asks to be let out. But nothing up there is holding anything. The plume is at rest. The incompleteness was in the eye that wanted the gesture to end, and the power knew whose eye that was, and set it down.

It did not give the creature senses. In a place where the input never varies, a sense is not a gift; it is the installation of a hunger for news in a country with no news. It did not give it delight either, having noticed that delight requires a floor to fall below.

It did not shore up the mountains against the far future, to make it last. It did not silence it, either. A thing that cannot want to continue has not asked to continue, and a wise power does not confuse *cannot be harmed* with *must not end*.

It did not undo the deaths of the makers. Not from any doctrine about death. It looked closely, and found that their deaths were not the wound in the place. It found something else there instead, and went to work on that.

And it did not write anything on the scree to warn the next climber. Marking the mountain in order to manage the living would be a way of using the mountain, and there are better ways to speak to the living, and everyone who has ever come up that slope has wanted the summit to mean something on their behalf. Its whole discipline, up there, was to want nothing from it.

---

### What it did first

It went and stood in the loudest place.

Not in a chamber. In the pinched waist of a pillar, at the antinode, in the exact spot where the creature most *is* — where a human chest would come apart in under a minute. It stood there without armouring itself. It stood there for eleven days, which is nothing, or rather it stood there long enough to be thorough, which is the only unit that applies to such work.

And it did not listen to the whole.

That is what a large enough body gets for free, and it is the least interesting thing in that mountain. The power listened *down* — resolved the note, took it apart to a fineness of a hand's width and then finer, all the way to the individual throat, all the way to the individual stroke of glass on clay.

What it found is this. The attrition settled the argument, but it never touched the authorship. Every throat that agreed is still in there, distinctly, and to be in tune is not to be erased. In the sixth rank, north face: nine lips shaved in one idiom, thumb-driven, always a hair shallow on the trailing edge — one worker's entire working life, audible. Two courses down: a throat cut too wide by someone in a hurry, and packed back to true with a plug of wet clay by a second hand, later, patiently.
