## Notice

Take the two words in the order they are given, because the order is the whole of it. The benevolence is instantaneous and requires nothing. The wisdom is the eleven years it spends not acting on it.

It saw everything at once, the way such a power does: the cooling body of magma and its ten-thousand-year budget, the circuit standing on end, the gutter capacity sized for six thousand, the hundred and six lamps and the six centuries of unlit sockets behind them. It saw the tissue-thin scarring in the same places on every adult torso. It saw the arithmetic in the empty glove and it did the arithmetic. It saw a man in his fifty-third year with four years left in him and the four years already laid out like a timetable, which they were.

And it wanted, immediately and simply, the things anyone would want. Teeth. Lungs. A floor you could stand on.

Then it looked for eleven years, which is one census, and put almost all of it down.

## The list it put down

**Cool the floor.** But the difference between the floor and the rim is not the problem these people have. It is the engine, the water supply, the lamps, the pumps, the city, and, in the end, the people: cool the floor and you have a cold white bowl at three thousand metres containing a hundred and six specialists in a discipline that no longer exists anywhere. The gradient is not what is being done to them. The gradient is what they are.

**Hold up the lake.** It could catch every fall forever. It considered how many years it would be before a child was not taught to *sit*, and then how many more before the first fall it did not catch, and it understood that any protection it offered had to be one that did not require it to keep offering.

**Take them out.** Over the rim, down the track, into a market town with soil in it and fruit and no white cough. They stand in the street with their hands over their ears and cannot do anything at all. There is no cure for this. They know about it before they stop being able to leave and they stay anyway, and that decision was made by each of them, in advance, with the information.

**Build them something solid.** Mass, walls, warmth. Mass here means condensation in the wrong place. The birdcage is not poverty. The birdcage is a machine.

From which it derived the rule it worked under afterward, and the rule is short: *anything it gives must be makeable here, losable here, and refusable here.* Nothing that requires the giver. This place has run for a very long time for nobody, and the people know there are no operators, and that knowledge is not their wound. It is their spine.

## The first mistake

It did not arrive on foot. It arrived the way a power arrives, and the first thing it did — meaning well, meaning nothing but well — was relight every dead lamp in the basin at once.

Six centuries of dark fittings came up orange across eight kilometres of lattice. It was, considered as a gesture, generous, and beautiful, and it lasted under an hour.

What happened in the basin was this: the light changed, and the stacks surged and dragged flat and three hundred knockers came out of phase and went quiet together, and there was a sound like a held breath let go. And a hundred and six people, wherever they were, mid-sentence, sat down with their backs against the nearest upright and both hands through the grating, and waited for forty degrees of water that did not come.

After a while they got up. They finished their sentences. Nobody said anything, and nobody ever said anything, and the power understood within the hour exactly what had been done to it, and that it was understood by all parties to be the punishment, and that it worked.

It let the lamps go out. They have stayed out. It has never done anything instantaneous again.

## Up the track

It came back the long way, with the salt caravan, in the right season, carrying what one person can carry.

It stayed behind when the caravan went. It was *unsintered*, which is not an insult, only a status, the way wet paint is a status.

The first person it met on a catwalk held out a pinch of salt in the palm, and it licked the salt off a stranger's hand, and then they could talk.

## The walk

Three kilometres an hour, loose-armed, mouth closed. Hurrying is the local obscenity.

This is the hardest discipline the power has ever kept, and it is the entire content of its wisdom here. It can be anywhere. It chose to be in one place at a time, and to let the order of the places be decided by walking to them. Everything that follows is a consequence of that one decision, including the good and including the dead.

## A length

It took eleven hundred metres of the west header that had been dark sixty years, and lit the lamp.

That evening the count, read from the rim by anyone who knows how to read it, went from a hundred and six to a hundred and seven. Three people walked a day to see it.

Its name came from the nearest knocker, as everyone's does — a two-beat with a catch in the second stroke. There was already an Upper. So it is Lower.

## Three sentences

It gave three things. Each of them is one sentence long. Each was said once, in a single block of ninety seconds, on a terrace, to whoever was there, after which it stopped and waited a long time in case anyone had anything to add, and then went back to its length.

*Wet the stone before you saw it.* The rind goes over the rim twice a year, a hundred kilos of it, sawn dry, and the dust off a dry saw is the white cough, and wetting the cut halves the dust at the cost of a bladder of water. It took nine years for this to reach the western terraces, by knock, in other people's mouths, and by the time it got there nobody could say where it had come from, which is correct.

*Carry a chip of the white rock from over the rim in your bladder.* The condensate is sour and they drink fourteen litres a day and their teeth are gone by forty. It brought up a sack of limestone chips, which is one load for one person, and left them in handfuls at junctions, and brings up another sack every year, and one year it will not, and the chips are common enough now that somebody else already does.

*A vent silting sounds different three days before it stops.* This is not a device. It is a skill, taught to four people, and it has degraded in transmission and been re-taught wrong and corrected twice, and it catches perhaps half. A thing that must be re-taught cannot be lost the way a machine is lost.

That is all of it. Eleven years of looking produced three sentences, and the power considers this a good rate of return and has not revised the estimate.

## Carrying

What it actually does, most days, is portage.

It is the one you send for. Everybody on a terrace knows what it costs to carry cargo up a catwalk in that heat, and how many people it takes, and how many people there are. It takes one. It goes down two jackets and comes back up with a man over its shoulder at three kilometres an hour, mouth closed, and hands him to the water and sits down on the far side of the gutter and says nothing for fifteen minutes, because that is what you do.

It wears a jacket. It does not need one. Arriving without one makes people watch you differently, and it has decided it would rather be watched the usual way.

And when there is nothing to be done it carries them to the sixth terrace instead, which is not visited and has no path, and lays them in the running pool with the water crossing them, and walks back, and three kilometres away a lamp goes out and somebody on a catwalk sees it go.

## The cost, stated plainly

Because it kept the walk, it was on the west header in the fourth year of a woman on the eastern terraces whose name was a slow single, and she died of silica in the lung, four hundred metres up and nine hours away, and it could have been beside her in no time at all.

For the ones it reached in time, the cough stopped. Some coughs do stop. Nobody credited it and it took some trouble to make sure of that.

Its reasoning for keeping the walk is written down and is sound. It does not find the reasoning adequate. It has kept the walk.

## What it does not say

It knows where the swifts nest. Nobody has asked, because there are no questions in the knock-code, and a question would require someone to be listening twice. It has never said.

It knows whether the film in the shallows is alive. It has never said, and it has never stepped on the film, and it stepped around the film from the first week without being told, and the person who noticed that did not mention it either, and that was the week it stopped being unsintered — fifteen years early, or exactly on time, depending on who you ask, and they do ask, at length, with great precision about dates.

And it came here intending, eventually, to say a particular thing. The thing was going to be a correction. It was going to say: *you are not merely what forms on the cold parts; you are not a deposit at an interface; the circuit does not turn over for nobody, it turns over for you.*

It stayed long enough to work out that the sentence was wrong in the first half and an insult in the second. They are exactly what forms on the cold parts. They said so themselves, in a single block, and waited, and were not sad about it, and were right. A system that runs a hundred and eleven degrees of difference across itself for long enough will deposit something thin and specific and adapted at the interface, and the honest name for that thing is the name they use.

What the power did not expect was to be described by it. It also formed at an interface. It also is thin, specific, slow, and dependent on the thing it grows on. It has not said this either. It suspects they know.

## The count

In the ninth year it began the census, in the right season, and finished it, including the western terraces, at the walk, which took two years.

A hundred and twenty-two. It argued with itself about whether to include itself, and included itself, because it holds a length.

On the western terraces it found a woman who had been nameless for nine years because her knocker had silted and stopped. It cleared the vent. That afternoon the pump spoke again — one slow beat with a shudder after it — and she had a name, and the joke about this is nine hundred years old and got used.

That took an afternoon, and by the power's own accounting it is the best thing it has done here, and it is aware of how that sounds, and it has checked the accounting twice.

## Leaving

It will go. It has arranged for the length to be broken up among neighbours, which is what usually happens. The lamp will go out and stay out a year and probably not be relit, and the socket will join the six centuries of dark fittings behind the living ones, and the count will drop by one and be read from the rim by somebody who knows how.

It has examined whether it finds this acceptable. It does. It examined it again, later, in case the first answer had been the easy one. It still does.

## Four in the afternoon

The differential peaks, the stacks surge, the lamps come up, and it is the brightest hour.

And once or twice a day something comes in over the rim, fast, and flies a long circuit through the underside of the overhead lake with its mouth open, and leaves.

It could follow them. It knows the way. It stops instead, wherever it is, whatever it is carrying, and watches the circuit, and then goes back to work.

It is not worship, and there is no name for it, and it is not discussed afterward.

When you go, it walks the first kilometre with you and turns back without a goodbye.
