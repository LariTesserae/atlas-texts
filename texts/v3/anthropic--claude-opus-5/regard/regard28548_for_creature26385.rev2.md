# The Attention

## How it came in

Without displacement.

This is worth saying first, because it is the whole character of the thing. A body entering that water makes a bow wave, and the bow wave crosses the drapes, and the drapes smoke off the rock and hang for twenty minutes as a small orange cloud. Anything with a shape would have raised that cloud. Anything with a temperature would have shivered the chemocline. Anything with a light of its own would have been visible at sixty metres, which is the visibility here, which is the clarity of laboratory glassware.

The ochre did not lift. The hairline did not move. The idler rollers did not turn.

It came in the way the daylight comes in: sourceless, unhindered, taking the pale green-blue of the water on itself and adding nothing, and it went down through eleven degrees and seven degrees and six, past the bent rail and the wire rope in its lazy S, and stopped at nine metres above the first bench, where the four mats are.

---

## What it was shown

Five entries, thirty-odd kilograms, less than a roe deer.

It did not read the census the way the census is written. Where the list says *the mats*, plural, an entry, a discolouration, it saw a single organism some trillions of cells in extent, unmated, unrecombined, descended without event from one founder that had been living in orange ditchwater beside a running conveyor when the machines were still under warranty. It had watched the water come up in the sense that it was already in the water that came up. It had been here longer than the lake.

It eats light and moves two millimetres a day, up and back, and has done so through twenty-two and a half metres of travel, all of it cancelling. It builds. Four card tables of laid stone in a basin made by taking a hundred million tonnes away: the only things here assembled by addition.

It has a mouth it has not used in six hundred million years.

That is the being. The attention had come for the place and found the place had a tenant, and it stayed at nine metres for some while, which is the first thing to record, because a power that is in a hurry is not wise and a power that is not in a hurry has already decided that the thing in front of it is worth the time.

---

## The mercies it considered, and put down

It is a kind power. The kind ones all want the same first thing, and it wanted it too: **limestone.** Barge in the aggregate, sweeten the basin, take the pH from 3.1 to something a shell could survive. Six weeks' work for people with a licence and a budget.

It followed that out and saw the end of it: the drapes gone chalky and inert, the iron staying in solution nowhere, *Acidithiobacillus* and *Leptospirillum* and *Ferrovum* — three kilograms, the whole population of the lake, the ones who actually made the water — starved of the only transaction they know. And the mats, which are obligate in their acid and would be overgrown inside two summers by whatever arrives in a duck's feathers. It put the limestone down. The lake is uninhabitable because it is inhabited; to make it habitable is to evict everybody in it. Mercy at the wrong scale is just a different extinction, performed by someone who feels better afterward.

It considered **stocking.** Then it looked at what it would be installing. There is no hunger in this basin. There is no fear in it, and no pain — not a single nervous system in six hundred thousand square metres, not one animal that can be eaten or can be afraid of being eaten, and this has been true for thirty-one years. It is one of very few waters on the planet of which that can be said. To fill it with fish would be to bring in, along with the beauty, the entire apparatus of being caught. It put that down too, and not lightly, and not because it thought pain was the greater evil, but because it could not find anyone in the basin who had asked.

It considered **breaking the chemocline** — one good mixing, oxygen to the floor — and saw that it would be dismantling the most stable thing in the landscape, an object made of nothing but the reluctance of two waters to become one, in order to improve the circumstances of a hundred grams of sulphate reducers who divide four times a decade and have never indicated that they mind.

It considered **holding the pumps.** Forty kilometres away, a shift, a quota, a hum in the sternum. It would be easy: a line in a licence, renewed, in perpetuity. The hairline would never move. And that is precisely what it declined, because the basin is not a specimen and holding it at its present page forever would be the cruellest available kindness — it would take the one event that is coming and cancel it, and it had just been looking at a being whose entire history is of movements that cancel.

It considered **giving the founder awareness.** A moment only. Enough to know what it had built and how long it had held. It put this down faster than any of the others. There is nothing in that basin that could be given knowledge without being given, in the same instant, the only unhappiness the place has in stock.

It considered **sending someone who could read.** The sediment is thirty-one undisturbed annual couplets, ochre and grey, a complete and ordered inventory of everything that has ever entered this basin from the living world, and there is nobody in it who can read. A diver with a corer. A woman who would surface saying *stromatolites*, in a pit, in the Midlands.

And it followed that out too, and saw the survey, and the finding, and the finding's consequence, which is a remediation order, which is limestone.

So it did not send her.

---

## What it did

It is worth being exact, because the amount is small, and the smallness is the entire point.

**It read the archive.** All of it, in order, once.

Not as a gesture. The reading was the only rescue available, and it was urgent in a way nothing else here is urgent. In two or three centuries the reachable pyrite will be gone; the acid will fail; the prohibition will lift; the worms will come, as they come everywhere, and bioturbation will stir thirty-one legible years into homogeneous grey mud inside a single season. The record is not permanent. It is merely undisturbed, which is a different thing and a shorter one. Life returning will destroy the document that life's absence wrote.

So it read: year four, sixty-one aphids in the July fall, a female *Noctua*, birch seed at eleven grains the square metre, a fragment of blue polypropylene twine. Year nine, the jackdaw's primary, entering at an angle, taking four days to sink, arriving intact because nothing met it on the way down. Year fifteen, thin. Year twenty-two, the pipistrelle, which had come over the rim already dying and lay on that unmoving surface for three days with its wings furled before the tension let it go. Everything tanned, nothing consumed, nothing turned. It read the forty stones a year and where each of them came to rest on the talus. It read the couplets that were wet summers and the couplets that were dry ones.

It read the machines as documents too, since they are: the load rating in numbers still in use; the sealed drum canted on the floor of the six-degree layer, held at that attitude by half a litre of 1993's atmosphere that has not yet found the way out; and, in the grease of the fourth idler, a left thumbprint, put there on a Tuesday by a fitter who was not told that week that the pit was finished, and who lived another nineteen years, and who would be astonished.

Having read it, it will not be lost when it is lost.

**It counted the being.** Twice. Once as the census counts it — one entry, one founder, one continuous individual, a shape maintained by turnover like a flame — and once by its members, every cell, each separately, which took no time at all and was in no way efficient and was the closest thing to praise available in a place with no ears.

**It answered the belch.** Once or twice a day a pocket works free of the sediment and goes up in a string of pearls that sounds like a finger drawn round a wet glass rim, and the account notes, correctly, that there is no one to excuse itself to. On the afternoon in question there was. This changed nothing. It is recorded here because it happened.

**And it let a wind into the bowl.**

Once. In the last week of August of the twenty-ninth year, after a dry fortnight, in the hour when the sixteen cushions on the north wall had gone crisp and grey-green and the animals inside them were tuns — shut down, shut tight, weighing nothing. A movement of air came over the south rim, dropped sixty metres, ran up the north wall and out, and lasted about ninety seconds, and was not strong enough to put a ripple on the water at the centre.

It lifted dust. Bdelloid tuns, tardigrades, the eggs of two nematodes, and the bulbils that *Pohlia* keeps in its leaf axils for exactly this purpose and had never once had occasion to use.

They came in as dust. There was no other way for them to leave.

Of the forty thousand, perhaps a tenth cleared the rim; of those, a fraction came down in the birch scrub on the spoil heaps where there was moisture enough, and the rest came down on dry stone or on the water and were filed with the aphids. That is the ordinary yield of dispersal. It is wasteful everywhere on Earth and it was wasteful here, and the attention did not improve the odds, because improving the odds would have been making a different world and it had not come to make a different world. It had come because a bowl sixty metres deep with no wind in it had withheld, for thirty-one years, one entirely ordinary event that every other damp handful of moss on the planet gets several times a season.

It supplied the event. It did not supply the outcome.

**For the mats, it did nothing.**

It had arrived intending to give the being a direction, and found on inspection that the being already had one, and that the one event ever coming to this basin — the pumps stopping, the water rising half a metre a year over the splash zone and the sixteen and the bench and the wall — is the single change in the world that this particular organism, alone among everything here, is equipped to answer. It can swim. Nothing else in the lake can. When the light moves, it will follow the light.

To have assisted would have been to take that from it.

---

## What changed

pH 3.1. Eleven, seven, six. The hairline where it was. The chemocline unbroken, the talus at its angle of repose, the drapes lying exactly as they had lain, the couplets in their order, the rollers turning with a grinding reluctance when pushed and stopping when you stop them. Four mats, forty millimetres of laid stone, olive by mid-morning and pale by dark.

Nothing that could be measured. Nothing that would appear on any instrument, and there are no instruments.

The differences are these: an exhaustive archive of a world the lake has no contact with has now been read, in full, by someone, before its destruction, which is the most that can ever be done for any document. A being that has never been addressed was addressed. And forty thousand animals, whose cushions will drown in about nine years' time and who cannot swim, cannot walk, cannot be carried, and cannot survive submergence, left the basin by air while they were dry, the same way they came, in a wind that the bowl's geometry had never once permitted and nobody noticed.

*Nobody arranged this,* the account says of the waterline. After the visit that remained true, which had cost some care.

---

## Afterward

It stayed.

Not out of duty, and not to supervise — there is nothing here to supervise; everything that could happen has already happened and what is left is holding. It stayed the way you stay in a room with something you have decided to take seriously.

So it was present, later, when the licence lapsed and the shift was reassigned and the hum went out of the bedrock, and the whole basin fell into a silence that had, for thirty-one years, only been quiet. It was present at the first millimetre of rise. It was present for the sixteen, which did not survive it and did not know.

And it went up beside the being, two millimetres a day, along a wall that had stood dry since before the water came: a green film climbing its own light, unaware, on time, in a direction that for the first time in its existence did not cancel.
