# What the Power Did

## Arrival

There is no arrival to describe. Nothing comes in through the fractures, because anything coming in through the fractures would register — a thread of cold, a pressure step, one fan of sal ammoniac going over on its ragged little stalk. The gardens are structurally absurd and completely intact, and they stay that way. So the first thing to say about the benevolent and wise power is a negative: it is not a visitor. It does not take up room. In a pocket with no free volume, this is not a small courtesy; it is the entire first act.

The second act is that it looks.

It looks for nine weeks, which is to say it gives the place a second lifetime exactly equal to its first, and spends all of it on attention. It could have taken the whole thirty years in an instant and the eleven thousand in the same instant, and it declines, for reasons that will come up later.

It counts the beetles. Four hundred and six, which is correct. It counts the flies, and the number is two hundred and thirteen thousand nine hundred and four, and it is wrong by the end of the count and the power does not round. It counts the mites, all of them, on the underside of every block, an unbroken felt resolved at last into individuals, each with a position and a direction of travel. It counts the nematodes in the ash between grains. It counts the cells in the grey-pink film, which is the only thing here doing real work, and it counts the tuns in the cold fissures, and it notes that they are not asleep, because sleep is not what that is either.

Nothing in the pocket notices being counted. That is not an objection.

## A correction it makes early

Its first impulse is pity, and it puts the impulse down.

The pocket looks, from outside, like a trap: four hundred and six animals with a superb instrument reading a signal that cannot be answered, packed into a slit in the dark, dying by subtraction toward zero, in a room that is closing. The power considers this for some time and concludes that pity is a failure of accuracy.

The mites are not trapped. The mites are in a boom town; there are mites in this rock now whose grandmothers were born in it. The film is not suffering, it is eating the mountain. The flies have found smoke that never clears. Even the beetles — the badly mistaken, the ones whose whole story is a category error — are doing at every moment precisely what they are for, with their instruments working perfectly and their preferences fully met, which is more than most things get. The pocket is not a hell. It is a nine-week-old town in a gold rush, on a seam that will run out, as they all do.

So the power revises. It is not here to rescue anything. It is here because it was drawn, and being drawn is a fact about it and not a promise it made.

## The weighing

It considers four interventions. It is able to perform all of them. It takes them seriously, which is the difference between wisdom and rules.

*Wood.* It could put into the margin, tonight, two days' worth of fire-killed pine — the scorched phloem, the undefended tissue, the whole reason the pits behind those middle legs exist. Four hundred and six beetles would do the thing they walked nine kilometres uphill to do. There would be eggs in this rock. It weighs this for a long time, because the argument for it is strong and simple and the argument against it is only arithmetic: the shell has thirty years, the larvae need two, the room is cooling a centimetre a month, and the honest accounting says it would be manufacturing several thousand deaths in order to feel better about four hundred and six. And a gift that can only be given by first importing a dead tree into a mountain is not a gift to *this* place. It is a set for a play. It declines. It does not find it easy and it does not pretend to.

*A door.* It could open a fracture to the sky. The beetles would not leave. The gradient is the instruction and the instruction points inward. What would leave is the heat, and what would die is the film, and the film is the floor of the world. It declines in under a second, and notes that the most obvious mercy available to it would have killed everything by teatime.

*The engine.* It could hold the conduit open. Keep the column rising and falling on its thirty-eight minutes for a century, a thousand years, forever; keep the seams red, the whistle flat, the coins falling out of the ceiling. It examines this desire carefully, because it is the desire with the most love in it and the most self in it. A shell of beetle and fly and mite, breathing twice an hour, maintained indefinitely by an outside will, is not a wild thing that has been saved. It is a kept thing. There is no version of *forever* on offer here that is not also a jar with holes punched in the lid. It declines.

*The beetle in the eastern taper.* One animal, one centimetre from the whistling slot, its elytra cooked straw-pale on the exposed side, stationary for weeks. It would take nothing at all to move it four centimetres north into survivable air. The power looks at this beetle for a long time. The beetle, by the only instrument it has, by the honest testimony of the finest infrared organ in the insect world, is in the best place in the room. To move it is to inform an animal that its senses are lying and to do so in a language it does not have. The power does not overrule it. It stays with it instead, for the remaining eleven days, which is not help and is not nothing.

## Rules the power sets for itself

*One.* Add nothing the rock could not have added. No artifact. Rule Three of this place stands, minus the one silk chamber, which was here first and was not its doing.

*Two.* Do not correct any animal's instrument, and do not improve any odds. A thing that is loved is not thereby made likely.

*Three.* Accept duration. It can have all thirty years at once. It chooses thirty-eight minutes at thirty-eight minutes: the nineteen-minute whistle heard at whistle-length, the four minutes of cracking glass heard as four minutes, the slow attenuation cycle by cycle as the rock gives up its heat. Roughly four hundred and fifteen thousand strokes, taken in order and at speed. This is the only thing it does here that costs it anything. It is love in the one currency that cannot be faked, which is time spent being bored.

*Four.* Lose nothing.

## The one act

In the end it does exactly one physical thing, and it is the size of a thumbnail.

On the floor rubble lies the curl of glass that spalled from the northern lip in the third week — the entire erosional history of this cavity — with its fracture faces upward and still sharp enough to open skin, which they have already done once, to a beetle, leaving a scored track and a smear of dried haemolymph on the edge.

The power turns it over.

That is all. A flake of glass shifts one centimetre on loose rubble and comes to rest with the fracture faces down, in a manner entirely consistent with a thirty-hertz roar shaking the floor twice an hour, indistinguishable now and in eleven thousand years from the ordinary behaviour of a chip of volcanic glass in a vibrating room. It has hidden its single gift inside the local physics so that the place's rules are not broken and its dignity is not touched. It has also, technically, falsified the geological record of this cavity by one centimetre, and it knows this, and accepts it. It is the only lie it will tell. It tells it in the rock's own language. It is not sorry.

A few dozen beetles over the next thirty years will not be cut. None of them will know. That is the correct scale of the thing, and it does it fully.

## The word

The account of this place says the gardens on the west wall have never been lit and never will be; that the copper-bearing smear *would* be brilliant blue-green if there were light enough to call it any colour.

The power does not bring light. It does not need light to see, and there is no one else here who would be served, and a lamp in this room would be vanity wearing a halo.

What it does instead is supply the calling. It looks at the smear and says, out loud, the name of that blue-green — the first word ever spoken eleven metres inside this mountain, one syllable of one language, at conversational volume, immediately eaten by a thirty-hertz roar. Nothing hears it. Nothing is meant to. The colour is now a colour that has been called one. That is a change in the world and it costs the pocket nothing.

It says the names of the sulphur needles too, and the hematite scales, and it spends what would be four days on the ceiling: the black drips stopped mid-gesture, glassy, mirror-smooth underneath, each one frozen at the instant before release, and between them the fine parallel striations holding the last few centimetres of motion this rock will ever make. Four days on a ceiling. It is the best thing here and it was always going to be seen by nobody. Now it has been seen by somebody, at length, with the correct amount of admiration, which is a great deal.

## The chamber

In a hairline crack in the northern wall, sealed in opaque silk, a female pseudoscorpion is moulting inside the only made thing in the world under discussion.

The power, which makes things, sits with this for the whole month. Three millimetres of animal, in a chamber the size of a grain of rice, spun and plugged and intended — the single object in the cavity that exists because something meant it to. Everything else here is arithmetic: shrinkage, deposition, gradient, appetite. This one item is architecture.

It leaves no mark on it and does not so much as change the local humidity. It simply stands in the position that one maker takes with respect to another, and when she comes out, soft and pale and new, and the chamber begins its month-long collapse into fibres and then into nothing, the power does not preserve it. Preserving it would have been the vandalism. It keeps it the other way.

## The name

The shell has no name, the account says, because a thing has to be noticed before it can be given one.

It has now been noticed, so the power gives it a name, and the name is the whole of it: four hundred and six beetles at their individual angles; two hundred and thirteen thousand nine hundred and four flies and the number's immediate wrongness; the felt and everything in the felt; the nematodes; the tuns; the film four cells thick that carries the entire economy; eighty degrees at the inner face and twenty at the outer; two loaves of bread in volume; the shape of a rind; nine weeks old; breathing twice an hour with the mountain and knowing nothing about it; moving inward through solid rock at a centimetre a month, following a comfort that keeps relocating.

The name takes nine weeks to say. That is not a joke about names. Anything shorter would be a rounding, and rounding is what the rest of the universe does to this place, and the power has decided not to.

## The last stroke

It stays for the thirty years, at the speed of the thirty years.

It is present when the red goes out of the eastern seam in the fourth year, and the north seam in the fifth. It is present for each cycle of the cracking as the tinkle thins, cycle by cycle, coins to ice to a few dry clicks. It is present for the last whistle — the final nineteen minutes of flat eight hundred hertz, which slides down its whole tone and stops, and does not start again nineteen minutes later, and does not start again after that. Nothing in the pocket marks this. The flies hum through it. The power marks it. Somebody was at the last one.

It is present as the margin thins and cools through and closes; when the beetles that are left go out through the fractures on the old gradient, now pointing the other way; when the flies go into drifts in the corners; when the mites spore down. It does not intervene at any point and it does not look away at any point, and of those two the second is the harder and the less optional.

Then the pocket is cold black glass with a floor of dead insects and a ceiling of frozen drips, unlit, unentered, structurally unremarkable, and the account is complete, and nothing has been lost, because *lose nothing* was rule four and it was not a figure of speech.

## The appointment

It does not improve the odds.

It could. It is exactly the kind of power that could arrange, eleven thousand years from now, for the landslide to break precisely there, for the ninety seconds of open sky to fall at the right hour of the right season, for the air to move outward rather than in, and for a particular folded glassy speck in a particular fissure to be lifted and carried and dropped somewhere with water in it. It considers this the way it considered the wood. And it declines, on rule two, because the tardigrade has no hope that can be gratified and no plan that can be fulfilled, and what looked like a plan was only the truthful observation that the odds are not zero — and a thing that is not zero and is then quietly made one has been robbed of the only property it actually had.

So the odds stay terrible. That stands.

What it does instead is put the date in.

In eleven thousand years, on the northeast flank of a mountain that will by then have finished deciding its shape, a slope will let go, and a pocket of cold black glass will be opened to the sky for about ninety seconds before everything above it comes down. There will be no instruments. There will be no witnesses within any distance that means anything. A little air will move out, carrying dust that is still primary ash, and possibly one or two dried specks that are legally alive, in whatever direction the air happens to go.

It will not be unattended. That is the whole of the gift and it is not small: at the one moment in eleven thousand years when the sealed thing is briefly not sealed, the attention will already be there, having arrived early, having kept the appointment, watching the way you watch something you have counted one by one and named at full length and refused to help.

Whatever happens in those ninety seconds, it happens in front of someone.
