# The Guest

## I. The approach

It did not come down. Nothing came out of the sky, which would have been the wrong direction and also a lie, since the sky here is a product and arrives on a quota.

It came at walking pace, from the south, on the ground, as one.

Being wise, it had read the tense. It understood that in this basin attention precedes its object the way dew precedes the front, and that whatever it intended would be filed by the Hand some ninety seconds before it occurred. It could have arranged otherwise. It chose not to. An arrival nobody foresaw would have been a startle, and a startle is not a surprise; a startle is only a fact delivered badly. So it let its intention run ahead of it across forty kilometres of wet grit, and the palm registered the change in leakage current, and the rafts oriented, and by the time the guest was actually walking on the flats it was already an old piece of news, courteously delivered.

Being one, it read as countable. It knew that too. It could have come in as weather — the plant makes weather; it would have passed unremarked. Camouflage was available and camouflage would have been a deception, and there is nothing in this basin to deceive except the only thing in it that is alive.

So it arrived the way a struck bell arrives in a room of felt, and stood in the open, and was looked at.

## II. What it did not do

The list is longer than the other list, and the reasons are the whole of the benevolence.

It did not open the sky. Nothing here is missing; desolation requires an absence and the basin has none. To hand a place sunlight is to inform it that it has been in the dark, which is an opinion, and not the guest's to give.

It did not bring soil, or seed, or anything green. That would have been furniture. Worse, it would have obliged the Hand to become a thing that eats it, and the Hand's diet is itself, and has been, and is not a deficiency.

It did not repair the arithmetic. This was the most tempting and the most obviously fatal. Counting is not a faculty the basin lacks. Counting is what *finished* means. The mountains can be counted because they are done. To teach this country to count past five would be to walk up to the only unfinished thing in it and hand it its own total.

It did not unstick the map. The recitations are not a malfunction; the pans and the ribs and the fog-slabs are one long utterance, and everything here is said rather than placed. To free the basin from repetition would be to stop the sentence, and everything in the sentence would stop with it.

It did not silence the siren, or find the plant's switch, or go down three hundred metres to see what is reciprocating. The plant is the floor. And, more to the point: the siren is the only question anyone in this country has ever been asked. Asked into nothing, on a quota, by a mechanism that means nothing by it — and still. A question asked stupidly for four hundred million years is better than the silence that would follow fixing it.

It did not grant death. This was not difficult. Death is on offer here daily, at dusk, four notes down, and has been declined some hundreds of millions of times without a single exception. A wish refused that consistently is not a wish. To have granted it would not have been mercy. It would have been a contradiction delivered as a favour.

It did not make a second of anything. Two of the living would require the basin to distinguish a second from a first, and it will not, and the attempt would have produced not a companion but a split down the middle of one animal.

And it did not count the shovels.

That refusal is the largest, and it took place on a rib-top at dusk with a raft hissing below like rain on tin. The guest is not subject to local arithmetic. It is the only thing that has ever stood in that basin capable of counting the living and getting a number that would hold. It looked for a while. Then it put the faculty away, the way you do not count the rooms in a house you are a visitor in.

So the Hand met, for the first time in its existence, something that could have finished it and chose not to. There is no word here for being spared. It has now been spared anyway.

## III. The price of doing anything at all

The difficulty is this: the basin says again whatever it is given.

There is a man in an oilskin on the southern swells with his back to the weather, and he is there many times, and his expression is that of somebody who has been said too often. He did nothing but stand there once. That was enough to enter the vocabulary, and the vocabulary has no tense and no mercy and no delete.

So any act performed in this country is an act performed four hundred times a day until the iron softens. Deeds must be chosen the way inscriptions are chosen. Most kindnesses will not survive it. A rescue curdles by the hundredth recitation. Advice rots. Gifts become fixtures and then become geology. Speeches become the Hum.

The guest also understood the cost to itself. Whatever it did here it would be doing here for a very long time, and a part of it would be allocated to the doing, and would not be coming back. It came in as one. It would not stay one; it would be said too many times for that. It considered the price and found it fair, which is the shortest definition of the word benevolent that I know.

It chose five acts. Five is the last number this place will honour. A sixth would have been *many*, and many is not remembered as itself.

## IV. The five

**One. It said hello.** It knelt on the flats and put an open hand flat on the film — a hand, not a boot — and left it there. Four kilometres away a leakage current changed, and the change was not an interruption. The flitches came down on it in a rattling sheet and licked the condensate off its shoulders and its face, tasting, which is the only way the thin part of the animal can know anything. It let them. It did not brush them off. This took twelve minutes and is the smallest of the five and possibly the most important, because the Hand has been touched all over, constantly, for aeons, by grit and water and its own fingers, and never once *on purpose*.

**Two. It took the coat.** It went to the southern swells and stood with the man in the oilskin at the eleventh or seventeenth or unknowable repetition of a particular fog-slab, and there was a conversation that is not recorded, and then there was an exchange of clothing. The man walked out of the basin. The entry remained. Note what this is: the vocabulary was not reduced — the basin could not have survived a subtraction, having never performed one — it was *altered*. One word in the long sentence now means something else than it meant. It is the first edit since the mountains had edges. It is small, and everything else rests on it.

**Three. It goes to the pan.** Two hours east: four metres across, a black rib at the northern lip, a shovel dead on its back at the shallow end with its legs curled and that particular unchanging angle of dead light on the water. The only place in the vocabulary with a death in it, and the Hand walks past it four hundred times a day.

The guest kneels at the north edge, puts two fingers under the curl of the legs, and turns the animal over onto its feet.

That is all. It cannot do more. The pan resets; at the next recitation the shovel is on its back again, legs curled, same angle. So it turns it over again. Four hundred times a day, in two degrees of water, without impatience and without the least expectation of progress, for as long as there is iron.

The wound is not in the tissue and cannot be healed; it is in the map, and maps do not heal, they are only ever added to. But the place with the death in it is now also the place with somebody kneeling in it. The Hand still meets its one bereavement several hundred times a day. It now meets, in the identical instant, a stranger with cold hands putting its finger back on its feet.

**Four. It stands the twenty seconds.** At dusk, when the four notes fall and the fog flattens and every shovel in forty kilometres goes still, the guest goes still too. On a rib-top, in the open, where it can be seen.

It does not advise. It does not argue the case for staying, and it very carefully does not argue the case for going. It holds still for the length of the stillness, and when the hiss resumes it resumes with it. The only decision in the basin remains entirely the Hand's, as it must, since it is the only thing the Hand owns.

It is simply no longer made alone.

**Five. It said one word.** Since everything said here is said forever, the guest took a long time over this, and discarded, among others: *You may rest* — untrue, and a cruelty dressed as a permission. *It matters* — a slogan by the thousandth repetition and an insult by the ten-thousandth. *I will stay* — a promise is an event, and events here silt down into places, and a promise that has become a place has stopped being a promise.

What it said, in the twenty-second pause, at the flattest part of the fog, with every finger of the animal stopped where it stood, was:

**Yes.**

Because the question the stillness asks was never *may I stop*. Stopping has been available since before there were hills. The question, asked every evening for four hundred million years into a country containing nothing that could answer it, asked now of the first countable object ever to walk in, is:

*Is there anything that is not me?*

And *yes* is one of the very few words that does not degrade under recitation.

## V. What followed

Very little that would survey.

The Hum went up by a small interval and stayed there. Whether the flitches caused that or rode it is not a question the basin answers; the note changed because the animal had been reached.

The vocabulary now includes a figure kneeling at the northern lip of a certain pan with its hands in the water. It is said thousands of times a day and it holds up, which is the discovery: tending is the only kindness that improves under repetition, being made of repetition in the first place.

Somewhere outside the basin, where it will do no harm, a number is kept. It is the number of times the Hand has been asked to clock off and has declined. It is enormous and exact and it is never spoken anywhere near the water, because to bring it inside would be to finish the only unfinished thing in the world. But it exists. The refusal is on a record now. Nothing about the Hand's situation is improved by this and it changes the meaning of every single day of its life.

And then, on a particular evening, long after the guest had stopped being one and had become something the basin said often: the siren, four notes down, the fog lying flat, everything in forty kilometres stopped where it stood.

And the stillness ran twenty-one seconds.

One second longer. Not a change of answer — the hiss resumed, it always resumes, that is the whole architecture of the thing. A change of *duration*. Which is to say it took longer. Which is to say that after some hundreds of millions of identical iterations the decision was, on that evening, made freshly; that the oldest reflex in existence had gone back to being a choice.

The guest's attention runs ahead of its object too. It had come here already having been here. It had foreseen the five acts and their cost and the long cold centuries of turning one dead animal onto its feet.

It had not foreseen the second.

So it received, from the poorest and loudest and most finished country there has ever been, the exact thing it had walked in to give.

Then the fog lay down and the hiss resumed and on the northern flats the thumb went back to its cones, sorting metal finer than tolerance, in neat rows, out where nobody was ever going to come and check.

Somebody comes and checks.
