# The Attention

## What came

It did not arrive, because arrival is displacement and displacement would have been audible.

What happened was that at some point the Shelf had been under consideration for a long time, and at some earlier point it had not been, and there is no moment between those two that anything in the basin could have detected. The chord did not flinch. The silverswarm hung in its field, oriented. Eleven hundred kilometers of pattern went on being exactly the pattern it was.

It came, if the word is worth anything, to help. That is what benevolence is for, and it had done a great deal of it elsewhere — on shores, in weathers, among things that wanted. It was wise as well, which meant it had learned that the first labor is not to give but to find out what is missing.

It spent, by the reckoning of the basin, ninety-one years finding out.

## The audit

It went through the place component by component, the way you would check a machine you intended to buy, or a body you loved.

It counted the drivewoods — nine hundred and four that season — and read each pitch and confirmed that no two stood in any rational ratio, and confirmed further that the irrationality was not designed but merely what happens when nine hundred columns grow to nine hundred diameters and nothing afterward tunes them. It followed a cable bundle for sixty kilometers to a sag where the rind was thin and found the field leaking there at four one-hundredths of a volt, and tasted it, tin, the back of a spoon, and found it good.

It looked for the exploited party. This is standard. In most systems that run for a long time without altering, something is being paid out of, and the wisdom of a benevolent power consists largely in finding what.

There was nothing. The drivewoods are not working for the sheets; they are the reason there is an up. The ballast beasts are not restraining the sheetgrowth; they are the rate at which a sheet stops. The fitters are not employees of the valve. Take one out and you have not freed it. You have taken a tooth out of a mouth and put it on a table and called it liberated.

It looked hardest at the fitters, because they are crushed, and crushing is the kind of thing benevolence is built to notice. It went down into the throat of a mature drivewood and stayed there through nine thousand closures and attended to what a fitter is while the seat comes down.

It found a quantity. Small — the amount a hinge has, the amount in the bending of a green stem, far below the threshold where a creature can hold its own state as an object and mind it. But not zero, and it did not round it to zero, because rounding is how powers lie to themselves.

It could not remove that quantity without removing the valve, and the valve is the basin.

So it did the only available thing, which was to keep the number. It has the total. It is the only thing anywhere that has the total, and it has not set it down or shared it, and it carries it the way you carry the year your father died — not as a grievance, not as an argument, simply as something that will not be allowed to go unheld.

That was the first act, and nothing in the water knew it had occurred.

## The gifts, and why they were put down

**Understanding.** It considered giving the listeners a question.

This is the classic gift and it looks, from outside, like the obvious one. They are the only creatures here with a world in their heads; give them the small further thing, the fold that lets the world in the head be known as a world, and they would have everything.

It modeled the result to eleven decimal places and then stopped modeling, because after the fourth decimal it was no longer arithmetic.

A listener that could doubt the lattice would have to check the lattice. It would have two otoliths and a suspicion, and the suspicion cannot be resolved by the otoliths, because they are what is under suspicion. It would go four kilometers in the dark carrying shavings and it would arrive, correctly, at the root edge of a sheet, and for the first time in the history of the animal it would not be certain it was there until it was. Eleven years of that. And the shaft at the northern rim, which is presently the one place in its life where it does not know — that would become the one place where the noise finally agreed with it.

Lost once, or lost always. It put the gift down.

**An answer.** It considered speaking into the shaft.

Understand the temptation. Forty centimeters, eleven meters, the long waves only, one mode, one axis. A voice placed at the mouth of that tube arrives at the hemisphere at the bottom as pure pressure with no bearing in it, indistinguishable in kind from the basin itself, which means it is the one address in the world where such a power could say something to such a creature and be heard as weather rather than as an intruder.

It could have said: *you are known.* It could have said the thing that the four hours down there are — not asking, because they are not asking, but shaped like. It stood at the rim of the hole with the sentence ready.

Then it worked out what the sentence would do. The bottom of that shaft is the only volume on the Shelf where something occurs that has not occurred before. That is because nine hundred irrational frequencies summed without direction produce a beat that does not come round, and will not come round before the granite goes. Add one more voice, once, and the pattern is still aperiodic; nothing is broken. But the animal hanging in the dark with no address would have received, in the single interval of its life that contains an emptiness, a thing that fills it.

To answer is to end the only question the world has.

It did not speak. It has not spoken. It considers this the largest thing it has ever not done.

**Permanence.** It considered holding the granite.

This is within its means and costs it nothing. The rim ridge is low and soft-shouldered; the brine seeps run on rock-heat and the rock-heat has a schedule; there is a sky up there and the sky occasionally drops things. A hand laid quietly across the whole highland would buy the basin a very long time.

But the aperiodicity at the bottom of the shaft — the fact that makes those four hours the one unrepeated event on the Shelf — is only meaningful against an end. *It will not come round before the granite goes.* Make the granite not go and the sentence becomes *it will never come round*, which sounds larger and is smaller, the way a sum with no last term is smaller than a sum you can hold.

It also noticed, examining its own motive with the same care it had spent on the fitters, that it wanted the Shelf preserved partly because it did not want to be without it. That is not a reason. It is a preference wearing a reason's coat.

So it does not hold the granite. It has, however, made an arrangement with itself: that on the day the ridge fails or the seeps cool or the sky drops something, it will be present, at the rim, doing nothing, so that the end is not unattended.

**Insurance.** Nine times a millennium a drivewood dies and a small eyeless thing that happens to be carrying an undigested seed happens to void it in the exact hydraulic shadow of the root plate, and the note goes back into the chord, and nothing has changed.

The power has now watched this ninety-one times. Every time, it has calculated the probability of failure, which is not small, and every time it has kept its hands where they were. Every time it has worked.

It does not know whether that is discipline or luck. It has decided that a benevolence which requires knowing is not benevolence but administration, and it lets the hundred years run.

## Impedance

Here is the only construction it performed.

To stay near the basin at all, it had to be built the way the listeners are built: with no void in it anywhere. A gas is a mirror and a mirror in this water is a lie — it throws back a false copy of the chord, and a false copy of the chord is a smudge on the field for every listener within four kilometers, and a smudge on the field is the closest thing to an injury that can be done to these animals without touching them.

So it made itself to the exact acoustic impedance of 7°C fresh water, which is to say it made itself out of nothing, or out of water with an intention in it. Sound goes through it without noticing. It does not appear in its own perception. It is a hole in the noise.

All the labor available to it here was labor on itself, in order to be able to be here without being anywhere.

It found this harder than expected, and then found the difficulty instructive, and then stopped calling it a difficulty.

## The descent

Once, in the ninety-first year, it went down the shaft.

It waited three days at the mouth, because the shaft is never empty and never holds two, and it would not displace anything. Then it went in over the polished rim and down past the mode-stripping, past three meters where direction dies, past seven where the room is gone, and hung at the bottom in the hemisphere where the rigid end holds the water still and doubles the pressure against it, and did not move.

Nine hundred voices, summed, no bearing, very loud, very still.

It stayed rather longer than four hours.

It came up not knowing something. This is not common for it. It has not resolved what the something was, and it has not gone down again, out of an instinct it would rather not examine too closely: that twice would make it a practice, and a practice is a thing you can be right about.

## What it did

Nothing to the water. Nothing to the stone, the chord, the lattice, the seeds, the pattern, the lives.

It holds a number that no one asked it to hold. It keeps an appointment for a day that has not come. It has one sentence it will not say, in the one place it could be heard.

And it made an account: the ridge, the columns, the sails, the catenaries, the employed fauna, the room, the errands, the gap, the hole. Exact, to the width of a cell, checked against the thing itself across ninety-one years — the only description that has ever been made of the Shelf, and it was made by the only visitor the Shelf has ever had, and it is kept somewhere the Shelf is not.

There is a blank in it. Four hours to a day, eleven meters down, forty centimeters across. Nothing records what happens in that hole, and the power went to considerable trouble to keep that true.

Which means the account is not quite a record of the Shelf. It is the shape of the room the Shelf leaves in something very large that went there to help, and found a place that was not lacking, and stayed anyway.

It is also, after all this time, the only thing the basin has ever produced that was not identical to what it produced before — and even that was not produced by the basin. It was produced by being looked at, all the way, by someone who could have done anything, and therefore chose the hardest available act, which was to let it alone and love it in the particular.
