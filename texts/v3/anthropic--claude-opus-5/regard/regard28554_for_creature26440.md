## Unbilled Works, Origin Unrecorded

It came up the switchback road in the late afternoon, which here is most of the day, and about four paces before the first meeting a woman coming down stepped off the dry side of the stair onto the green lane in the middle and gave it the good footing, without looking up, and was past and gone into the vapour.

So the first thing it learned was that it could not be thanked here, because no transaction had occurred; and the second, within the week, was that it could not be worshipped, because a raised voice empties a street in eleven seconds and there is no register in the language for exaltation, only forty terms for water. A wall weeps, sweats, runs, bleeds, wicks, greens, beards, or sings. There is no word for *is delivered*.

A less patient power would have taken this for an obstacle. This one took it for instruction.

---

### What it did first

Nothing, for nine winters. In this valley that is not a long time; there are streets that have been scaffolded longer than anyone's memory.

It learned the sequence in the right order and did not use the wrong term in a report. It learned the note of the drop into the sump at the corner of the ninth bench, and the particular flat hush of the aqueduct overhead, and the thrush in the third arch who swallows the third note. It sat up at two in the morning, the first time the corner stopped, and by the time it reached the grate the woman was already there barefoot in her nightshirt with the lamp set down on the wet stone, holding a black mat of rowan leaves in one hand while the water found its voice again below her.

That was the finding of those nine years, and everything after came out of it: *they are always already there.* Rescue is not a thing that can be offered to these people. It arrives late by construction. Whatever was to be given would have to be given at a depth below rescue, in a currency they already used.

---

### What it did not do

It did not stop the rain. Water is the whole argument of the place; take the rain and you have not saved them, you have retired them, and there is no word for that either.

It did not lay bedrock under the fill. Nine hundred years of labour would have become, retroactively, unnecessary, which is the cruellest thing that can be done to work.

It did not lengthen the day, or thin the cloud, or lift the lid off the lower benches. The eyes here have gone the way the leaves have gone. To give this valley an honest noon would be to blind four thousand people out of kindness.

It did not build a wall that could not fail. A wall that cannot fail is not a wall here. It is a monument, and there are no monuments.

It did not take the ash out of the fill and put it somewhere it could be visited. It is visited. The barley there is good.

Once, on the Barley Stair, it held a bellying wall for eleven seconds — long enough for a child on the bench below to be got sideways out of the line of it — and then let it go, and forty tonnes went down into the flood channel with the sound of a wardrobe falling over in another house, and by evening the debris was graded and the stone stacked and the reconstruction had a foreman and a number. It has never regretted the eleven seconds and has never extended them.

---

### The error

In its eleventh year it took its days of the Hand like anyone, and on a rebuild of the fourth bench it laid a course of bond so fine, so exactly bedded, that the wall did not weep.

You will see it before they did. Water that is refused does not leave. It finds another road, and the other road is always through something.
