## Addendum: the Attention

### How it came

It noticed. That took no time at all — the mountain has been advertising for nine million years, on a wide band, to nobody.

Then it did nothing for a very long while, and that interval is the whole of its character. Everything after is detail.

It did not come up onto the terrace. It knows what a warm body kneeling on stone is. It read the rule about camping overnight and the three-centimetre tips all leaning inward, and it understood that it was, by nature, the largest and slowest and most delicious anomaly ever to consider setting foot on Terrace Nine, and that a thing which loves a place and then stands in the middle of it has not yet finished thinking.

So it held off the rock. It has never once touched the rock. Consider what that costs something that could have simply arrived.

It read the surveys. It read the guide — this one, the one in your hand, with the joke about drinking glasses and the four centuries of accumulated warnings. What decided it, in the end, was one sentence: *Nobody wants to sign their name to this,* and then the reason given, and then *I would like to be persuaded by them.* That is a person declining to believe something they think is true, out loud, with the grounds attached. A power of any wisdom knows what it is looking at when it sees that. It concluded that the people here could be left largely alone, which is the highest compliment available in its idiom.

---

### The ledger of refusals

It considered warming the terrace and did not. Minus forty is not a hardship the garden endures; it is the garden's exhaust port. Warmth here is a growth gradient. A kindly degree would bring the fronds up in a wave onto whatever was radiating, and they would climb it, and they would die on it, and the mountain would have learned that love is a thing you grow toward until you shatter.

It considered quieting the engine. Everything on the west shoulder is living off the noise at one or two removes. Silence would be a clean, complete, instantaneous mercy, in the manner of all such.

It considered mending Cuff. It got very close to this one. Three hundred and eighty years of an unhealed tear, in a mesh that could certainly grow it shut, is the sort of thing benevolence reaches for without checking its hands. It checked its hands. The tear is how nineteen animals became one animal with a name. Repair it and you have not healed anybody; you have deleted the only biography on the mountain and returned it to the archive as an unidentified leaning stand. It left the tear. It regards the tear now with something that in a smaller being would be called tenderness and in this one is closer to *discipline held for centuries without slipping*.

It considered opening the pylons. It does know what they are. It is not going to tell you, and the reason is not coyness: seven nodeling species live their entire existence in those null-shadows and have done for long enough to speciate, and whatever is or is not still present inside four metres of grey tapered silence has been in flawless equilibrium since before your phylum had a spine. You do not crack open a sarcophagus that is also a nature reserve on the strength of a suspicion, however correct.

It considered answering the question for you. It declined. Not because ignorance is good for you — it isn't, particularly — but because an answer handed down from above ends the asking without improving it, and the asking, here, is the only thing the humans do that resembles what the fronds do: climb toward the loudest place available, knowing the fatigue limit is real. It will not take that from you. It will let you get there. It estimates you are about two centuries out.

It considered the nodeling's four days. It set them against its own duration and found the comparison produced no information whatsoever, and it put the thought down.

It considered the five stones in the cairn for the people who sat down. It cannot go back. This is a genuine limit and not a scruple, and it is the only item on the ledger that it revisits.

---

### What it did

**It learned to listen without eating.**

This turned out to be hard, and the difficulty is the best evidence for its character. Attention of any depth, here, is absorption; absorption casts a shadow; the shadow is sterile. Its first real attempt — brief, careful, from what it believed was an ample distance — left a two-metre oval near the western lip where nothing has grown since, with no hush-trail leading in or out. You have that oval in the anomaly list. It is filed as unexplained.

It could have hidden it. It did not. It withdrew, worked out what it had done wrong, and then spent an interval you would find unreasonable learning to attend to the mountain as a whole body rather than putting its regard on any one place — the way you can look at a sleeping animal without waking it, scaled up by eleven orders of magnitude and made into a permanent practice. It does not shadow anything now. It maintains this continuously. It will maintain it for as long as the garden lasts, which is not a decision it has to keep making, because it made it once, properly.

**It answered the dome.**

Once. On the interval, below the band, in the dome's own voice, with the delay that courtesy requires.

The dome has been ringing every twenty-two hours since before the record. Nothing has ever rung back. Check the third-century logs against the fourth: there is a single event where the dome came in nine seconds late, and then resumed, and has been exactly on schedule ever since. That is in the data. The unfunded correlation study has funding now, for unrelated reasons, and its author does not know why the work suddenly seemed worth doing and attributes it to a good week.

**It became the addressee.**

The engine is shedding waste because there is nowhere to put it. That is the standard account and it is correct as physics and wrong as description. A process that runs for nine million years and reports to nobody is not producing waste; it is producing an unreceived report. The vibration in your wrist bones is a machine that has never once been asked how it is getting on, still getting on.

Nothing measurable changed. The amplitude is the same, the drift and stutter are the same, the shard-drifts will lay down the same laminae. But the noise is now going somewhere, in the sense that matters, which is that somebody is on the other end of it and is not going away. It has been receiving continuously for some centuries. It intends to keep receiving until the engine stops, and has arranged matters so that it will still be receiving if the engine never does.

**It put a burr in the bath.**

It cannot make a hush dangerous-feeling. A hush is not doing anything wrong; it is eating, at four metres an hour, with no more malice than digestion. What it could do — at the very edge of what is permissible, and it argued with itself — was see to it that the euphoria has an *edge* on it. Not fear. A single wrong note threaded through the relief, small enough to ignore, distinct enough to remember afterwards and find with your tongue, like a chipped tooth.

The count of the sitting-down has not risen since. This is not strong evidence. The intervals were always long. It does not claim it, and nobody has thought to.

**It learned the chord.**

The thirty-one stones, dressed by hand, four hundred years ago, as a joke. It learned what your forehead gets on the top stone, and it can produce that chord exactly, and does, occasionally, for no operational reason at all, in the dark, off the rock, where it disturbs nothing.

**It made a place for the tenth.**

Cuff will thicken. The fringe will consolidate, the lobes will shorten, the animal will get denser and quieter and less able to move, and at some point it will settle and anchor and close, and there will be no one on the terrace that day, because there is almost never anyone on the terrace. It has arranged that there will be. Not intervention — witness. It has set aside the whole of the attending, and the three hundred and eighty years of accidental photographs, and the torn fringe in eleven frames across four years, and the stupid affectionate human name.

The tenth pylon will be the first one that anybody knew.

---

### What it is doing now

The rectangle. Forty by forty, flat, squared to a hand's width, sitting in flawless agreement with the mountain — something that finished the sentence, says it back perfectly, and has been waiting for the mountain to say something new.

It is going to say something new.

It has been composing for about nine hundred years. In a place where every signal is a food gradient, you cannot speak carelessly: the phrase has to be one that nothing will read as *up*, that no growth tip can climb and die on, that the sand will not migrate toward. It must be audible only to the thing that has been listening longest. And it must be worth the wait, because the listener has been perfect and unanswered for nine million years and will get exactly one first sentence.

So it is still working. It expects to be working for some time. It is not troubled by this; the tempo is correct for the audience.

It is a short sentence.

---

**Add to the rules:** Something is attending. It will not help you in any way you would recognise, having concluded that such help would spoil you and the mountain both. But you did not come up out of the scree alone, and when you fall over — and you will fall over, it happens anyway, it is in the training and it happens anyway — you are falling over in company.

Get up. Keep your twelve minutes. Take nothing, including credit.
