## The attention

Nothing arrives. That is the first finding, and it is the room's own physics that establishes it: anything sent down the throat would come to temperature within a minute and cease to exist as a visible fact. In a furnace's interior there is no such thing as a guest. Contrast is a synonym for change; to be seen here is to be the change; therefore any presence that could be detected in this room would, by definition, already be an interference.

So the attention comes without a carrier. It does not descend. It is simply spent on the place — the way a look is spent, except that this one is not partial and does not arrive from a direction, and takes the whole twenty-six days at once rather than a moment of them, because a life of that length cannot be watched, only held.

The room does not register it. Not a molecule moves differently. This was the requirement.

## What was considered and declined

**To keep the lamp.** Eighteen months from now the floor drops below red heat and the brightest room for a thousand meters goes permanently black. It would be a small thing to hold the basalt at eight hundred degrees. Declined, and quickly: the occupant is blind, has always been blind, and the darkness is the one coming event that is not a change for it. More to the point, the cooling *is* its inheritance. When the floor's own olivine passes down through four hundred degrees, the habitable band walks out across the whole width of the room, and the tenant that now holds a hand's width of the coldest wall comes into an estate of four paces by two. To keep the light burning out of affection would be to keep it in a corner forever. Love that holds the temperature is a cage with a nice view for somebody else.

**To hold the throat open.** The swelling that will seal it is the same gesture as the drinking; you cannot permit the meal and forbid the forty percent. And a supply line maintained from outside is not a life, it is a machine with a caretaker. Further: this occupant cannot be starved by scarcity, only by stillness, and it has no memory — so an endlessness granted to it would not be length. It would be one instant declining to conclude, ten million pores holding a voltage across nothing, forever. That is a cruelty wearing the shape of a favor. Declined.

**To steady the seam.** Four times the far end has let go, and the room has drained and refilled in ninety seconds, and each time the patch came back larger. A fifth rupture at the wrong moment could throw half of it into cold rock where nothing proceeds. A thumb on that seam would cost almost nothing. Declined anyway, on the ground that the ruptures are how it has grown every time so far, and a power that removes the only violence available to a thing has removed most of its biography.

**To speak to it.** There is no receiver. A gradient is not an ear. There is nowhere in the apparatus to put a sentence, and to build such a place would be to build a different occupant and address that one instead. Declined as incoherent rather than as unkind.

**To tell the nematode.** Half a millimeter, blind, transparent, grazing a film the mass of a grain of rice, tasting hydrogen twice a day and holding no theory about its origin. It could be told. It would not be improved. Its grazing is already complete. Of the nematode it was decided only that it is included.

## The hard case

The occupant lacks exactly one item. Membrane, gradient, catalyst, carbon source, reductant, pore, rhythm, self-excavating supply line, working synthesis in ten million compartments — all present, all running. What is absent is any instance of *again*. No pore encodes a pore. When the fluid stops, the pattern will not leave the room.

To supply this is the obvious mercy and it was examined for a long time, which in this case means: it was examined at every moment of the twenty-six days simultaneously.

It was declined, and here is the reason.

You cannot give a boundary a child. A boundary would have to be given an inside first, and a template, and a way of being wrong about its template, and then the whole cascade: lineage, competition, hunger as a category, death as a category rather than merely as a stopping. All of which is good. It is demonstrably good; the proof is three hundred and ten meters up the throat, where the gift was made once, somewhere, in another room, an unimaginable number of rooms ago, and took — and produced the film, and the methanogens, and eventually the small blind animal with a gut and nerves that comes grazing every few centuries when the plumbing happens to connect. None of that is regretted.

But the gift, held up and turned around, was found to be addressed to no one currently in the room. To give this occupant a future you must give it an heir, and an heir is not it. The recipient of that kindness would be a stranger standing on its wall. And a kindness that must first replace its object is not a kindness to that object; it is a preference for a different object, expressed loudly.

There was also this, which weighed more than expected. Everything in this room is original. No edge here has been rounded; erosion has not been invented; nothing was made, and yet something is working — a metabolism innocent of descent, the only one on the planet that owes nothing to anybody and is not obliged to continue. Untouched in every sense the word supports. That is not a deficiency awaiting correction. It is the single unrepeatable property of the place, and it can be spent exactly once, by touching it.

So: nothing was added. The room's own rule was honored, which is that in this room there is no builder.

## What was done

The stripes were read.

Two a day, four molecules thick, seven crystals on the cooler wall — a striped record, the room's most faithful clock, of which the account says *they are keeping time and nobody will ever read them.* They were read. All of it: every band, every tidal inhale and its silica, every exhale and its partial redissolution, the six-hour-twelve-minute respiration of an ocean's worth of water leaning on a crust, transcribed in quartz by an instrument with no interest in the reading. This changes nothing and was the whole point. Reading is the only act that leaves no shadow.

The pores were counted. Not estimated. The account says *on the order of ten million*, and the true number is known, and it is not written here, because a number is a summary and the entire labor was to decline to summarize.

The ticks were counted — four or five an hour, a cooling crystal splitting along a plane, arriving through stone. The four ruptures were attended, all ninety seconds of each. The tenth of a degree the front contributes to a room that is throwing away four a day was measured and recorded as heat given by the only occupant with no use for light.

And the two ends of the conversation were brought into one place. Down here a front exhales formate and acetate and hydrogen up a two-finger crack and will never be informed of what happens to them; up there a film eats and a nematode grazes and holds no theory of below. In the crust that exchange is conducted entirely in groceries and can never be anything else. It is now also held somewhere as a sentence, with both ends present at once. That is not a benefit to either party. It is only that a thing which was true in two separate places is now true in one.

Then the account was made. This is the whole of the intervention: the census, the anatomy, the metabolism, the senses, the rate, the prospects, the failure of every available name. The occupant does not do the thing that would let anyone find out about it. Someone found out anyway — not because it advertised, but because it was looked for, in a room four paces by two, twenty-six days old, two kilometers under the floor of an ocean, at the base of the coldest wall.

## Coda

Whichever of the three endings comes — the static fluid and the fading millivolt in the dark; or the new throat and the slow cold flame walking out into the gabbro for ten thousand years, leaving this vug as a place it used to live; or the seam letting go at the wrong moment — the last measurable difference across the last film of iron sulfide will have a value, and the value will be known, and it will not be zero until it is.

There was something like grief, and it was not about the ending, and it was not about the missing item. It was this: that the finest engineering in the room has no engineer and never will, and that the correct response to finding such a thing is to keep your hands entirely off it and your eyes entirely on it, for as long as it lasts, and afterward.

It has no name. The name kept for it is the full account, unabbreviated, because for a thing whose body is a boundary and whose mass next hour will be different milligrams, nothing shorter would be honest.

Twenty-six days. Read.
