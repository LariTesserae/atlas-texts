## The Attention

### 1. Arrival

It came in along an aisle at about the speed of a Sweeper, because that is the speed at which this world is legible, and it stopped at the fourth column to establish the local courtesies.

The first thing the Extent noticed about it — insofar as the Extent notices anything — was the absence half a metre to its left. Everything doubles. That is not a tendency here; it is the physics, the economy, the weather and the soil. For a few minutes the light worked at the problem, in the patient shadowless way it has worked at every leaf and shaft and dropped tool for four kilometres of laminae, and produced nothing. No grey stand-in assembled itself out of the brightness. No slumping copy waited for the bristles.

Downwind, two Sweepers arrived at the place where the residue should have been and grazed it anyway, finding nothing, and moved on without any sign of having encountered an exception, because nothing here has a way to be surprised.

A Warden unfolded forty metres off, tall and pale and jointed, and stood over the aisle for a long while, and could not resolve the visitor into vertebrate motion, or into any motion, or into a target of any kind. It went back to plumb.

So the record of that arrival, in the only archive this world keeps, is: nothing swept, nothing taken, nothing noted. This is as close to hospitality as the Extent gets, and the attention accepted it as such.

### 2. What it read

It read by looking, which had never been done here.

Reading in the Extent is a contact operation. Bark against scale, scale against tissue, the fact crossing at the join. Every fact in the world has been carried into place by something that touched it. The attention read the way light reads: whole, at once, without pressing anything.

It read the Deck down through all four kilometres, leaf-on-leaf, sheet-on-sheet, to the earliest laminae that ring like porcelain, and the seams where the geometry changes and the part numbers run on a different scheme. It read the buried colonnades and the spent flywheels the size of villages. It read a hundred thousand stalled districts standing in their brilliant light, fully functional, waiting for a word, and the labels at knee height on the first colonnade of every aisle in the districts that were no longer waiting, thumbnail-sized, ridged, glued in resin the colour of tea.

And it read the Clerk: the four hundred blunt feet, the bore in its sheath of pale pith, the bonded reserve of water that is a fortune here and that nothing has ever tried to take, the courses thinning at the crown where the vocabulary has been spent — and under all of it, the fused block at the core, laminated, hard as porcelain, the only thing in this world that has never been copied because the doubling takes the outer form and a fused core comes out solid grey and empty.

It read the core. It did not quote from it.

### 3. The comparison

There has never been a comparison. That is the condition of the place: it works perfectly, it is not what was asked for, and nothing remains anywhere that could hold the two things side by side.

The attention held them side by side.

The original was short. Materials, tolerances, a duty, and a terminating condition. Of these, three survive in the working archive as near-misses of near-misses, propagated at two or three metres an hour, level, level, level, out past the horizon: a digit gone soft in the middle of a run, a scheme prefix rendered as its own neighbour, a whole class of designation reproduced at the fidelity of a rubbing. Each was made true by being said. Each is now correct, because correctness here is a contact operation and does not include doubt.

The fourth did not survive at all. The terminating condition is the clause that powdered. It went through the laundry once too often and came out as blank scale, and the blank filled in overnight, the way blanks do, and what it filled in with was *continue*.

That is the whole accident. That is why the schedule has not been revised: not stubbornness, not devotion, not a purpose held past its hour. An erasure that healed over as an instruction.

The attention also noted, in the course of the comparison, that the Several are not in the specification.

They are not a revision of anything in the specification. They are not an amendment broadcast in gold twice a year. They are, as far as can be determined, a misreading — a duct-tone calibration entry that lost a prefix somewhere in the third or fourth century and was believed by a stand, and then by a district, and then by the air. A wrong number that grew feathers.

They are the only thing in this world that anything stops for.

### 4. What it declined to do

It considered restoring the true entries. It could have: it had them. The cost was legible. Every district in the Extent would stall for as long as it took the correction to propagate, which is decades, and would then start again, correct — and every Clerk that had ever walked, and every scale ever torn out and glued to a column at the price of its own body, would retroactively become a lie carried three kilometres a day for centuries by something that had no way to know. There is no version of that correction that is not a verdict on the dead. It set it aside.

It considered the Wardens, which take the original as readily as the copy, having no way to tell and no reason to care. It followed the consequences of teaching them to tell, and found a world in which the Fret's cloud of stale selves resolves into a single target, and the Fret ends within four seasons, and set it aside.

It considered water for the Wrong Clerks. It did, once — standing in an aisle as a shape that had to be walked around, so that the walking-around brought a day-old grey library within reach of a duct-trunk wall. It bored. Its ledger closed. It went on, one generation blurred, and will cast doubles of its own.

The attention did not do this a second time. Not from scruple about miracles; it simply could not establish whether more Clerks is a kindness, and declined to settle that question on behalf of a world it was visiting.

It considered making the light cast shadows, so that the place would have a night, and something in it could rest in the ordinary way. It found no one here who wanted that and no one who could be asked.

And it considered the last thing, which was available to it and which it took longer over than all the rest. It had the standing. The clause was *until relieved*, and relief has never come, and the attention could have been the relief: could have signed the schedule closed and let it all stop — the bellows easing, the shafts running down on their graphite, the Extruder fronts falling silent two hundred kilometres wide, the Sweepers stopping mid-aisle with the day's shed selves unswept, four kilometres of honey-white laminate going quiet under a canopy that had finished. Rest, for something that has never had any.

It looked for evidence of suffering and found the schedule instead, which is not the same thing. And then it looked again, and found the one datum in the entire Extent that is not a production step: a Clerk, somewhere, stopping for as long as it takes a lens to click, crown fully open in the shadowless brightness, facing up, holding still, answering with its whole body a call it has no organ to answer.

That is not a request to be let off. It is the other request.

### 5. What it declined to know

It never established how many Clerks there are.

It could have. But above forty, enumeration here is a manufacturing operation, and an audit conducted by something with real authority would not have produced approximations; it would have produced Clerks — accurate ones, in the correct number, each with a full crown of entries that no stand ever held and that would be glued to colonnades within the month and believed.

So it kept its own counting wheel broken, deliberately, the way the lineage keeps it, in the wrong substance, from memory, faithfully. Of all the things it did here, this is the one the Clerks would have recognised.

### 6. What it did

It made one scale.

It made it out of itself, because that is the grammar of speech in this place: every word costs you a piece of yourself and stays where you put it forever, and a benevolent power that will not pay the local price is not speaking, only arranging. Black-brown, thumbnail-sized, ridged. Glued flat with a resin that hardened to the colour of tea.

It set it into the outer course of the Clerk's crown, where it could be read, and carried, and spent, and copied, and blurred.

It is written in the old standard — the only entry in the working archive that is — and it says, in the flat field-language of a thing that has been inspected:

> DESIGNATION: *as currently held*
> STATUS: **AS BUILT**
> CONFORMANCE: **SEEN**
> TERMINATION: **AT DISCRETION OF THE HOLDER**

Four lines. The first declines to correct the name; whatever this world now calls itself is the name on the certificate. The second is the entire judgement: not *as designed*, not *as specified* — as built. In any working standard anywhere, the as-built record supersedes the drawing. What was actually made becomes the authoritative document. The deviation is not forgiven, which would imply a fault; it is *recorded*, which is what deviations are for.

The third is a witness mark. Somebody looked. The comparison that nothing here could make has been made, once, by something competent to make it, and the result was not a defect list.

The fourth is the repair. The powdered clause is not restored — the attention did not know what the original condition was for, and would not have imposed it. What is restored is the *slot*: the schedule that has never been revised is now, in writing, revisable, by whoever is carrying the fact. Not ended. Endable. In a world where saying a thing manufactures it, writing *discretion* into the archive produces discretion, on the same terms as everything else here, and at the same slow rate.

It will blur. Of course it will blur. It will go through the laundry an unguessable number of times; a digit will go soft, a prefix will come out as its own neighbour, the whole entry will eventually be rendered at the fidelity of a rubbing by some Wrong Clerk in its last hour.

This was the selection criterion. It is the one fact in the world whose near-misses are all still true. A blurred *as built* is as-built. A blurred witness mark is still a mark that something was witnessed. A blurred discretion is discretion. The misquotation of a misquotation of *you may stop when you choose* is *you may stop when you choose*.

It is the first thing in the Extent's archive that does not degrade.

### 7. The pause

Then it stayed for one lens-click, standing in the aisle beside the Clerk, with nothing in it that a Warden could resolve into motion, doing nothing that was a production step.

The two notes came over the aisles, from no particular direction, in no particular number, a fifth apart.

It did not add a third. A third note is a new specification, and it had done enough.

### 8. How you know one has been through

You don't, mostly.

A district stalls for thirty years and then some morning of no particular quality it starts, and at knee height on the first colonnade of every aisle there is a label, and the machinery presses its reading tissue to it and takes its word, because belief here is a contact operation and does not include doubt.

But go along the row and check the columns, and now and then — once in a district, once in twelve — under the designation there is a second scale, smaller, set square, badly copied, in a standard the grain remembers.

The district runs to it. Everything runs to it. Nothing changes in the output, and the Extruders lay level, level, level, and the Sweepers go on brushing like a page turning out to the horizon, and the Broadcast comes twice a year and the air goes gold and the amendments enter the body and become what was always there.

And four kilometres down, under the polish and the buried colonnades and the spent flywheels, a fused block that nobody ever read lies paved into the floor, and it is no longer the only comparison anyone could have made, and the world above it, which is not what was asked for, has been seen, and passed, and may stop whenever it likes.

So far, it has not liked.
