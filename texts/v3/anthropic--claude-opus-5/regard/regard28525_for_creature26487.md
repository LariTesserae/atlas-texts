# The Attention

Begin with the power, because the power is not finished.

Whatever it is — and it has no interest in being named here, having learned that names are the first instrument of damage — it arrives at the bowl already knowing how to arrive, which means slowly and from downwind and without any light on it whatsoever. It comes up the last kilometre of the shoulder and does not notice reaching the top except that the ground stops tilting. It stands at the rim, above the surface of the darkness, for a long time.

Eleven years, in fact. It does nothing at all for eleven years but measure.

This is the part that would be mistaken for indifference by anyone watching, and nothing is watching. It measures the seep at four points and finds the cycle to be sixty-three years, not "tens." It weighs the annual chemistry budget and confirms that the entire animal population of the bowl could be carried out in a rucksack, and that it therefore could also be *destroyed* by one enthusiastic kindness, delivered once. It establishes the exact pressure at which the mat registers a change, and the exact pressure at which the mat is injured, and finds that these two numbers are separated by a factor of about two hundred, which is the whole of its working room. It cools itself, over the course of a decade, from whatever it was to twenty-four degrees, matching ambient to within a tenth, so that when it finally crosses the surface of the dark it will not be a shopfront.

That decision costs it something it did not expect. At twenty-four degrees it is illegible to the only eyes in the bowl. It will spend its entire visit unseen by the moths, unread by the flowers, undetected by the pits of the thing that goes round. It has made itself invisible in order not to lie. Nothing here will ever know it came. It works this out in year nine and goes ahead anyway, and files the feeling, correctly, as its own business.

Then it enters by the notch, because the notch is the door, and courtesy is the use of doors.

## What it declined

It could resolve the reservoir. This turns out to be the single largest thing it can do here, and it is done entirely inside the power and leaves no trace outside it: it separates the bath of old noise into its constituents, all of them, five and a half thousand years deep, and hears every frog that has ever called in this bowl as a distinct individual with a distinct signature and a distinct number of remaining calls. It hears the listeners building their rooms. It hears the thing that goes round descending through eleven octaves of one long life and then starting again higher.

Having that, the temptations arrange themselves in order of size.

**To restore the installation.** Eight posts are down. The skin does not weather; the sockets are cut and legible; the power could stand all twelve again by Tuesday and return the dark to whatever depth its makers intended. It declines, because four of those sockets are open stone cups lined with a material that no hunting sense in the bowl can read, and every frog now alive was laid in one. Repair is extinction here. The instrument's last function is that of a nursery, on the grounds of being broken. The power lets it stay broken. It also, after some thought, does not clean the rockfall dents out of the notch lining, on the grounds that it does not know what the dents are doing to the drainage, and *does not know* is a sufficient reason.

**To enrich the seep.** Trivial. A little more sulphide, a little more iron, and the mat could grow four centimetres a decade instead of one. And then the mat outpaces the grazer's circuit; the grazer's thirty-to-forty-year rule detaches from the ground it was measuring; the animal navigates by a clock that no longer matches the world, and starves in a bowl full of food. Abundance would arrive as blindness. The power leaves the allowance exactly where it found it.

**To clear the bank of its dead.** This is the one that argues hardest, because the ordinary outcome of courtship here is a female spending a season crossing the mat toward a signature and arriving at a patch where the singer has been gone for sixty years, and then waiting. The power has resolved the smear. It knows his name, in the only sense a frog has a name. It knows he was real, that he called four hundred and six times, that he held that patch through two seep-cycles, and that she is not deceived about anything except the tense.

It could scrub the reservoir of the deceased and leave only the living in circulation. It declines, because persistence is the criterion, and persistence is not measurable in a population of forty-one without a bank that keeps the dead — and because the thing that goes round navigates by exactly those old voices, and to clear them is to blind the largest animal in the bowl in order to spare the smallest a wait it is not equipped to experience as sorrow.

And because it would be a lie told to a frog. The power finds it has a rule about this, and the rule holds even when the audience is two grams and cannot appeal.

**To tell the listener that its neighbour has been dead for years.** No mechanism exists. Declined for want of a grammar.

**To take one out and keep it safe somewhere.** Nothing here has relatives. Two metres above the rim the temperature ends them. Removal is a synonym.
