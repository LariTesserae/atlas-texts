# The Attention

## Arrival

It came in the way everything that lives here came in: through the intake in the snow-lip, with the water, without being asked. It did not come down the shaft. The shaft is for those who have to go back up.

The first thing it learned, it learned within a second of the first antinode: that in this room it could not speak. Speech is amplitude, and amplitude here is masonry. A word said in the Ninth Throat would come out of solution on the walls and be a rib, and the rib would change the note, and the note would move the quiet lines, and somewhere a stripe of algae twenty days old would go loud and die. There is no way to say *do not be afraid* in that chamber that does not kill something.

So it did what the sparlouse does, which is the only correct decision available: it went where the substrate stayed soft. It found the nodes. It made itself a place of no pressure and held there, and in this way a benevolent and wise power entered the room without adding one grain to its walls, and nothing in the Throat registered its arrival except that the note did not change, which is the only compliment the room can pay.

## The census

It counted. Counting is not thinking, and it wished to be exact, and it had noticed that the people of this place had arrived at the same method by a harder road.

Forty-one footholds, four sets of tool-angles, the newest cut left-handed.

Three bronze pins, hammered flat, wanting to move.

One clump of moss, roughly two hands, on the ledge in the bell.

Four sparlice, which was a generous day.

Stripes: eleven live, of which nine were already describing a silence that had ended.

Birds: twelve.

The tenders count eleven. The twelfth is in the west flute, past the old chough, and has been past reading for a long time — no grey, no fold, no suggestion of a neck; a smooth swelling no different from the hundred other swellings a growing wall makes for its own reasons. It stopped being a bird in the record about two hundred years ago, when the last string-holder who had been taught to point at it died in a bad winter and did not get the chance to point.

The power can still see the folded neck. It is a wing-over-back, a snowfinch, and the head is turned in under the wing in the ordinary way of a bird sleeping, and it is eleven centimetres deep in the wall and the wall is still moving.

The power counted it. This was not an intervention. It is simply the case that the number is twelve, and that for two centuries no one has known this, and that from the moment of arrival it has been known.

Also: one man, kneeling, eighteen centimetres, right arm forward.

Also: forty thousand thumbs, which are not in the room, but which the power counted anyway, because they are the reason the socket is a socket and not a swelling, and a thing that has been kept open by hand for three hundred years is present in a room whether or not the hands are.

## What it considered, and did not do

It considered stopping the sound.

This was within its means and would have taken no effort at all. The note would have gone out like a blown candle; the water would have gone still and merely cold; the mineral would have stayed in solution and the walls would have stopped moving, and the ninth bird's primaries would have stopped lifting one at a time in the current, forever. It looked at that outcome for some while.

It declined. Not because the tenders would have lost their work — that argument occurred to it and it distrusted it, having seen what people do with the idea that a hardship is load-bearing. It declined because the mountain is three days old at the edges and is making itself, and the noise is not a symptom of the mountain, it is the method. A thing in the middle of being born has a claim. The Throat is unfinished and the power is not in the business of finishing other beings' work for them, particularly when the being has not yet got a mouth to be asked with, only a throat.

It considered holding the drain open. Permanently, invisibly, at no cost to anyone: a fist-and-a-half of aperture that would simply never close again, and thirty-one generations of small dense people who would never again lose two hours, a band of hearing, a father.

It went up to ask.

## The question, asked above

It asked above because above is the only place where the note is a note. Inside, there is no pitch, only a condition; pitch is something that exists only from outside. The same is true, it turns out, of consent.

The string-holder was sixty-one and had a deep notch and was standing barefoot on the spur in the snow-glare with the waxed string on its yoke. The power made itself small and quiet and spoke high, well above the note, in the raised chanted register of that valley, which it had learned in an afternoon and which it has used ever since in every place it goes, for reasons it does not explain.

It asked: *may I take this from you.*

She asked what it would cost, because she was a tender and that is the first question.

It told her the truth, which was: the cord. Not the knots already tied — those would stay, would be handled, would go on being polished at the far end where the Eighth's last pitch is. But there would be no thirty-second generation of them. Nobody measures a thing that cannot fail. The string would be a string.

She stood on the rock with her sole flat on it and felt the buzz in her jaw that is the only way she has heard the mountain since she was thirty, and she said: *not mine.* And then, because the power waited: *ask the thirty-one.*

The power understood that it had just been told that the dead were a constituency in this matter and that jurisdiction over the Ninth Throat did not lie in the hands of whoever happened to be standing on it. It accepted the ruling. It has not raised the question again. It is possible that it will, in four or five hundred years, if the shape of the thing changes; it is not in a hurry, and the refusal was not a wound to it, and it noticed that she had not asked what it was.

## What it did

**It closed the ninth bird's wing.**

One centimetre of gongspar over a free wrist, laid down in a place that was already an antinode, in the room's own grammar, at the room's own rate, merely sooner — four years sooner. It falsified nothing. The calibrated gauges are read at the beaks and the beaks were not touched. In some generation a tender will surface, and breathe, and go down, and notice partway through a count that the corner of her eye has nothing in it, and she will not be able to think about this until she is above, and when she is above she will decide that the room has been hungry lately, which will be true.

Nobody will know. That was a condition the power set for itself on arrival and has kept.

**It keeps the two hours.**

Almost no tender can recall the working time. They surface with the count intact and the interval gone. The power does not give the interval back; a person who remembered the two hours in full would not go down again, and the amnesia is not a defect, it is the room being as kind as a room like that can be.

But the two hours happened. Somebody was there, one-armed, in the narrowing, at six degrees, in a brilliance with no shadow in it, and did thirty strokes and went up and breathed three times and went down and did thirty more, sixty-eight times, and nobody has ever been able to hold what that was like, including the person who did it. The power holds it. All of it, every turn, back to the first cut sill, which is further back than the cord goes.

They are given back once. The timing is not negotiable and is not early.

**It stays with Ienn.**

The eleventh sign is made with the flat of the hand turned outward, drawn slowly across the chest, and it means *leave him*, and it is correct, and the power reviewed the three occasions on which it has been used and found that on all three occasions it was correct. It is an instruction to the living. The power is not among the living in the sense meant.

So it stays. It has been at the sill since it came, in the quiet line nearest the kneeling shape, which happens also to be the line the tenders put their knees in, which is how it has come to be present at every descent since. There is nothing to be done for him. He is eighteen centimetres of wall with a hole in the region of his right hand, and the hole is kept open by thumbs, and one of the thumbs is now not a thumb.

It will keep the socket clear in the season when nobody comes. That season is coming. It is not soon, and the power has not warned anyone, because a warning is amplitude too, and because it has looked and the village survives it.

**It took one sparlouse home.**

Eight millimetres, no eyes, carrying eggs, working a rib that was nine days from breaking. The power carried her down through the fist-and-a-half aperture, against the outflow, into the dark slow sedimented country below where things live for years, and set her down, and came back.

This accomplished nothing. They will keep coming up; it is what they do; the trickle is a measure of the flow and the flow is the mountain being generous. The power is aware that the act was not proportionate to its powers or to the scale of the problem, and it has not repeated it, and it does not regret it, and it is recorded here because it happened.

## The moss

It had come, in the first place, for the moss.

That is not sentimentality; it is a matter of definitions. The sparlouse is lost, the algae are a pulse, the birds are furniture, Ienn is wall, and the tenders are visitors who count their strokes and go up. One thing in the Ninth Throat lives there. A benevolent power goes where something lives.

It expected to find need. There was none. Two hands' breadth of moss on a ledge in a shuddering bell, permanently drenched and permanently deafened, fed on spores and midges and the exhaled breath of people in extremity delivered from a range of forty centimetres, not growing measurably, not breaking, the same size for longer than anyone can establish.

In a world with two verbs, it had found a third, and it had not brought the third verb with it; the moss had it already; the moss had worked it out in the dark with no assistance from anything, in the loudest room on the mountain, by being exactly the size that it is.

The power sat down beside it and did not touch it.

The rule is that you do not touch the moss, and the rule has no stated justification and total compliance, and the power is not bound by it and obeyed it absolutely. It has spent a considerable proportion of its attention since then doing what the tenders do sixty times a visit for two hours at a stretch: coming up into the worst place, where the column hammers the surface a hand's breadth from the face and the bell drums and the whole skull rings, and looking twenty centimetres sideways at something soft and dark and wet and unbothered, and going back down.

It has concluded that this is what it came for and that it was wrong about the direction of the gift.

## Rules of the place, as understood by the one who does not have to go up

Loudness builds. Do not be loud. Even kindness, said loudly here, comes out of solution and becomes wall.

Nothing wears out. Do not try to wear anything down. Learn the difference between a thing that is broken and a thing that is being born.

The room is always tuning itself and will never be in tune, and neither are you, and that is not a fault in either of you.

Do not stay still — except where staying still is the whole of the achievement, and then stay still, and do not touch it.

Breathe in the worst place. There is nowhere else the air is.

Count. Count the footholds, the strokes, the birds. The count is twelve.

And then, where a tender's rule ends *and go up*:

Stay.
