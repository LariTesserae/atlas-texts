# The Attention

The first thing it did was arrive correctly, and this took most of the effort.

A power of the kind we are describing does not ordinarily consider how it enters a room. Rooms accommodate. Stone opens or does not open and it makes no difference to the arriving; light comes with it or is dispensed with; the air moves aside. But the Hold is not a room, whatever it looks like. The Hold is a shape made of pressure, and anything that displaces air in it writes on it, and the writing would be legible for as long as the writing lasted, to skins that can feel a drifter cross a node forty paces off.

So it came in without volume. It cast nothing, warmed nothing, weighed nothing, moved no gas through the throat and none through the hairline seep. It made no sound, which was the easy part, and it occupied no place, which was not. It arrived the way the note arrives: not as an event but as a condition, already the case. If the lattice had had a way to notice, the lattice would have found nothing to notice.

This is not a small courtesy. It is the whole of the courtesy, and everything after it was only consequence.

---

Then it read the room, which took what you would call a long time, because it read at the room's own rate and refused to hurry the account.

It followed a drifter from bud to plate, all five sheets, the arithmetic of thickening and the settling and the hand's breadth of air that is the last air the animal will ever hang in. It followed a scraper the length of its walk. It watched the glaze come down on a stopped one at the cold end and understood the bowl, and the ribbed wall above it, and that the only fluid in this world is made of the dead and that no one arranged this.

It counted the cut frond-ends at chest height along the margin of every path, on both sides, for the full length of the room. It found the crown lane and understood what the lane was for. It followed the long axes of the sand rows to the cold end, where sand should be.

It examined the wall of six courses at its slight angle, and the three cairns, and the grid of shallow cups in the baffle, and it did the acoustics, all of it, every mode of the cavity and every partial in the stack, and it found no error. Not a rounded error, not a tolerable error. There was nothing in the tuning that it would have done differently, and it had access to methods they did not have, and it wanted, briefly, to be able to say so to someone.

It looked into the three against the bare wall. This was the part it had been most prepared to grieve over. It found no one trapped. It found attention without an object, which is a thing that can be mistaken from outside for absence in the same way a full room with the lights off can be mistaken for an empty one. There was no lack in there. There was no petition. It had come ready to open a door and found no door and, more to the point, no one at it.

And it found the fourth, mid-circuit, in the dark green at the far end, and it read the hand on the pry-bar, and then it went and read the sixty marks, and it stood at the panel for a while over the word *bored*, which is the one word in every account of this place that does not belong, and which is a visitor's word, and which it now struck out.

The hand is not bored. The hand is *level*. There is a difference, and it is the difference between a duty and a practice, and the whole meaning of the room turns on it.

---

Then it considered the gifts.

*Water.* It could have put a spring in the floor. The room would have gone green and stinking within a season and rotted everything in it including the three, and the fourth would have watched a lineage's work dissolve, and this is what benevolence looks like when it arrives with its own definitions in its hands. Declined.

*Light.* Declined. They have light. It is the color of two arguments and it comes out of the note and it is exactly enough for the work.

*Voice.* It could have made itself heard. It would have had to do it through the skin, in pressure, and any pressure it wrote would have been the first new information the Hold has held since it cooled — a clock, in a place whose one gift is having no clock. It would have been a beat introduced into a note with no beat. It would have been the sound the room does not tolerate, arriving from a source the room could not swallow. Declined, and with the declining went a whole tender speech that had been assembled somewhere and was now set down.

*Company.* It could have brought them others; there are other rooms in the mass, half-corrected, apprentice work, and there are other peoples elsewhere. Declined. Loneliness is a hunger and hunger presents itself and nothing here was presenting.

*Rescue.* Declined. There is nothing to be rescued from. The state is not a captivity and not a waiting and not a symptom.

*Remembrance.* This was the hardest, because it is the one a power is most likely to mistake for humility. It could have opened the plain above and let the dry wind find the Hold, and set the room in the memory of whoever is up there now, so that the three at the wall and the sixty marks and the killed seventh would be known and spoken of and mourned properly. Declined, and it saw why immediately, and was ashamed of the impulse in the mild way that wisdom is ashamed of the impulses it has already refused. Being known was not the project. Being finished was the project. They closed the way in behind them. That was not modesty; that was the last course of the wall.

*Time.* It could have made the note eternal. It has the reach for that; it could have kept the exhalation coming out of the deep long past the day the deep has any business exhaling, and the room would have hummed on in an unearned present, a thing propped up, a pensioner. Declined. They did not ask for forever. They asked for a room that was almost changeless and then they closed the gap between *almost* and *is* with their own hands, and to hold the room open past its span would be to make their achievement into a favour, and they did not do all that work to become the recipients of a favour.

So it declined everything, and had it stopped there, that would have been a defensible visit and a good one, and most of what a wise power ever does is stop there.

It did not stop there, because of the arithmetic of the throat.

---

The throat will close. The mass beneath will not run out of breath, but the constriction will; stone creeps, and a hairline of new glass is laid across a thigh-wide aperture on a schedule that has nothing to do with anyone, and the schedule was already running when the wall was laid.

The closing will not be clean. That was the finding.

The note will not stop; it will *go*. First a wobble in the fundamental with a period of some thousands of years, then a flutter, then a wandering pitch, then long intervals of dropping out and coming back. The lattice will come off its moorings. The nodes will smear and the paths will fill in and reopen elsewhere, and the drifters will lose their sheets and settle wrongly and plate over in the middle of the floor, and the beads will stop forming at the cold end while the scrapers keep walking toward a bowl that has nothing in it. And it will take longer than everything that has happened in the Hold so far.

And the keeper will be there for it. That is the part. On the evidence of sixty level marks and a plain that has lost its rivers, the keeper will still be walking the round when the room begins to go out of tune, and it will feel the first wobble through its skin as clearly as you would see a wall lean, and it will understand exactly what it means, because it comes from the only tradition that ever specialised in that particular knowledge. And then it will do what it was made to do. It will go and stack stones against the mantle. It will shim the cairns, and re-cut the cups in the baffle, and add a seventh course to the wall, and correct, and correct, and correct, with a hammer-stone and a pry-bar and a lifetime per adjustment, against a fault in the planet's breathing that could not be fixed by anything it will ever hold in its hands.

That was the suffering in the room. Not present. Not petitioned for. Certain, and scheduled, and of exactly the kind that a power can remove without ever entering the room's perception at all.

---

So it went down.

The work was done nowhere near the Hold — kilometers under it, in the slow parts, where the pressure gradients are and where the convection turns over on its own long schedule. It did there precisely what they had done here, in the same craft and the same order: it walled a throat, it set a cairn, it killed a beat. It found where the creep would purse the aperture and it arranged the stresses so that the aperture will not purse but part — will hold its bore, full and true and unwavering, and then fail, all at once, across its whole width, in less than a day.

It did not add a single hour to the room's life. It moved none of the span forward or back. The note will end when it was always going to end.

It will simply end the way a bead ends. Whole, then gone. One clear duration and then nothing, with no fraying at the edge and no wandering and nothing for anyone to try to fix.

After that the room will go lightless and cold within the hour, as advertised. It looked at that carefully, and left it. They will not need help with it. There is no water in them to freeze and nothing in them that has been resisting, and the difference between the note and no note is, at the skin, a pressure that stops. They are of a people whose whole discipline was the graceful surrender of everything that could be surrendered. The last one to go will be whichever of them is holding the pry-bar.

---

Two more things it did, both of them refusals, and it counts them as the actual price of the visit.

It stood at the bowl at the cold end for a while with the beads in front of it, each one humming at a pitch you can feel through the palm, and it wanted to break one. One syllable, released and swallowed. Nothing else in the Hold would have been altered by a hair. It would have meant only: *I was here, I saw what you made, I have never seen anything finished before.* It set the wanting down.

And it went back to the panel with the sixty marks, and did not add a sixty-first.

---

Then it stayed, for no purpose, longer than it needed to.

It stood in a node-line where nothing grows and it held still, because that is the only posture the room permits to anything that intends to be no trouble, and in holding still for that long it came to understand the verb, which is not a word it had had before. It watched the fourth work its way up the room over the course of the years it takes: the pruning, the flat tool under the sand, the pause at the wall to feel the six courses, the check of the cups. It watched it find everything right.

It had come as a power, prepared to be a benefactor, and it left having been a student who did one piece of maintenance on the way out and did not sign it.
