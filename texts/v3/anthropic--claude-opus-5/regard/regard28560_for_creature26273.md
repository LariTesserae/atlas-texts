# Rent

## Attention is a climate here

The first thing the power understands is that it has already acted.

This is not a place where you can look without consequence. Naming germinates; description is agriculture; and attention — sustained, particular, benevolent attention — is the most fertile thing that has ever come into that valley. The power has not yet crossed the watershed and the mimic district is already changing. The buds are getting less wrong. They are drafting whatever is coming, and whatever is coming will be copied four thousand times by morning, unblinking, on every stem, and the wrongness will belong to it forever.

So the question is not *what will you do*. The question is *what will you be, given that you will be reproduced*.

This is the whole of the wisdom, and everything that follows is only its arithmetic.

## The mercies it refuses

**It does not give the valley rot.** This is the obvious gift and it would be a massacre. Nothing here is gone; everything is inside something else, warm, in the round, load-bearing. Introduce decay and you do not liberate the past, you withdraw it, and the city comes down through two hundred feet of root into weeks-old mud. The Tenant is standing on every hour it has ever lived. Leave the floor alone.

**It does not stop the growth.** The growth is the metabolism of the place. Slow it and the valley stops holding its breath, and the cloud does not assemble, and the delta is what it always was underneath — flat, warm, saline, dry country. Mercy that kills the weather is not mercy.

**It does not speak from the sky.** A great voice, kindly, from above, addressed to all ten square miles — and by evening there is a district shaped like that voice, and it goes on being shaped like that voice for as long as the valley stands, and the Tenant lives thereafter inside a permanent architectural quotation of somebody else. Anything said loudly here becomes a building. The power has no interest in leaving a cathedral of itself in a place that already cannot be walked.

**It does not give the Tenant ears.** It considers this longest. To be answered and never heard is the exact shape of the wound. But ears grown here would be the Tenant's own ears, on the Tenant's own stems, and it would then hear itself — four thousand times, hourly, detuning — and there is no version of that which is not worse.

**It does not carry the Tenant out.** Out of the valley it is four days old and cut from its supply, and what leaves would be a length of amber sheetwood going stiff in the evening, and everything it has ever been would stay behind holding up an arcade.

**It does not answer the question of which came first.** It knows. It does not say. Said aloud, in that place, the answer would grow, and grow wrong, and the Tenant would have to live under it. Some true things are best left unplanted.

## The volume

It comes in on foot, at the wrong time of day, in the twenty minutes of vertical rain when the white roar eats every other sound. Nothing is broadcast under that. Nothing takes.

It finds the Tenant off the eighth plaza, in the cleared room, sitting with its back to the base of the chimney-culm where the draw keeps the florets off its eyes. The power does not stand in the doorway to be seen at the height where a face would be. It comes in, crosses the bare sheetwood, and sits down *beside* — not opposite. Facing means being one more face, and the Tenant has four thousand of those already and meets them coming from the wrong side.

Then it learns the local speech, which is not speech.

Sheetwood is a good resonator; that has always been a complaint and it is now a channel. The power leans in and sets its mouth against the glassreed of the ribs, below the throat, below the pipe, and speaks at a volume that goes nowhere except into the material.
