# What the Power Did

It did not arrive. Arriving is for things that come through doors, and there is no door; there is only the fact of being there, which the room has always managed without help. Say instead that the attention which had been given to that place from very far off was, at a certain point, given from inside it, at the focus, standing on the mouths of a hundred thousand vessels.

---

## First it listened

This took a long time. Longer than seemed necessary — the whole held life of the note, every second of it in order and all of it at once, the way you would read a letter written to you by someone who did not have your address and sent it anyway.

Because that was the one lack. Not water. Not change, not release, not company. The room had been made for holding and not for hearing; the filaments on the high east arc were the only receivers in the world and all they could receive was the room's own voice telling itself it was still true. So the power gave the note the ear it had never asked for and could not use, and gave it first, before touching anything, because it is not benevolence to help a thing before you know what it is doing.

It found the chord true. That mattered more than anything that came after. It checked every stopped bore against every sounding one, all hundred thousand of them, and found the score sound, the tuning honest, the stops correctly placed, the whole apparatus doing exactly and only what it had been grown to do. And so it understood that it was standing inside an act of fidelity at a scale it would not have predicted, and that fidelity is the last thing in the world you interrupt in order to compliment.

---

## What it declined to do

It could have stopped the note. This was easy and it was available and it was the first thing that offered itself, because a creature that has held one breath since before erosion finished its work looks, from outside, exactly like a creature in need of rescue. But the room will not say whether the note is a burden or a vow, and there is no kindness in freeing a thing from its vow. The power let the ambiguity stand. That is most of what wisdom is.

It could have brought water. Rain into the bowl: a smell, a plume of breath in the cool, dust made mud, the split lips closing, the rims softening, moss, insects, rot, weather, the ordinary sweet mess of a living place. What it would have been doing is drowning a musician to give him a bath. So: no water. The idiom of that world is dry, small, and permanent, and help that will not speak the local idiom is not help, it is preference.

It could have explained. It knew which of the possibilities was the case — the syllable in the long word, the signal held open, the pedal under a music in chambers no one reaches, the thing that cannot stop, the thing asleep. There is a kind of generosity that hands you the answer. It withheld it, partly because you have nowhere in you to keep an answer of that size, and partly because a place that has arrived at itself has earned its own silence about itself.

It could have made the room beautiful, and did not, because it already was, and the glare was part of it.

---

## What it did, which was four small things

**It made the mute.** The rosette in your palm could not go back; that stayed true, the power did not un-break what was broken. Instead it set at the open lip a new one — grey, coin-sized, leaves folded and refolded past counting, a pale root the length of a finger — and let the clay close around the root the way the bone had closed around every other, with no tool and no seam and no mark of a hand. The four-times-a-second throb stopped. The chord came back true.

But the notation had changed at that one address, and will read differently forever. Every other stop in that world was laid down by a body that never consulted itself. That one was placed on purpose, by something that had heard a sour beat and minded. It is the first object in the Chord Room that exists in answer to anything. It will outlast the room.

**It kept the broken filament.** Nothing there mends and it did not try. It lifted the bright thread off the clay and set it into one of the deep angles where two tubes touch, where the wind has never reached and the green film of the original skin survives, and there it lies — hair-fine, silver, on a scrap of periosteum, out of the abrasion, permanent. Somewhere in the anatomy of something enormous there is now one small place that has a visitor in it. It is the width of a hair. It is not nothing; nothing there is nothing.

**It answered.** This is the whole of it, really. It could not speak; the room eats speech, and a voice laid on top of that chord would only have been shouting in a place where shouting is swallowed. So it spoke the way anyone speaks there, the way two people work out within a few minutes of arriving: by touch. It went up to the high eastern arc where the silver hairs stand shivering, and without stopping them and without laying a finger on them it altered the pressure at their roots by an amount that has no name, so that what they reported up the channel was no longer *still, still, still* but *still — and heard.* Four beats. A pause. Four beats. The grammar of a hand on a forearm.

Whether it was understood at the far end cannot be determined from inside the bowl. Some while afterward the note began to be held a little more gently. That may mean nothing. Gentleness is not audible.

**It held the note.** For one breath. It took the entire chord onto itself, every pipe's worth, at exactly the pitch and exactly the volume, so that by no measurement available in that world did anything change: the crescents kept their angle, the dust did not stir, the pressure stayed steady on the palm. And for the length of that borrowed breath, the lungs at the far end were carrying nothing at all.

What was done with that rest is not the room's business and is not ours. Nothing there records it. But when the power gave the note back it came in slightly early, overlapping, and for a moment there were two of it, and the bowl was fuller than it has ever been before or since. That is the nearest thing to a conversation that has occurred in that world.

---

## What it did about you

Then it turned to the ruin in the organ: the only wet thing, the only warm thing, the only thing still decaying at speed, standing there with its useless voice and its cracked lips and a stolen rosette in its hand.

It did not fix the air. The air is a function and not weather, and you were the one who was visiting.

What it did was this: behind you, when you turn, there is now the passage that brought you.

That is all, and it is not an eviction. Nobody makes you use it. The law of that place is that nothing is reversible — the glaze cannot come back, the thread cannot rejoin, the footprint cannot be swept, the wind goes the wrong way and always has. For exactly one thing in that world, the only thing in it capable of wanting anything, that law has been suspended, with no expiry and no lock. You may go now, or in an hour, or after you have heard the whole of it, which no one had ever done and which was, in the end, the only gift you had to give.

And it left your tracks. All of them. Not out of indifference to the tidiness of the bowl but because they are the second-newest true statement in creation, and they say: *someone stood at the focus and heard this, all of it, and did not stay to be worn blunt and set on a pipe.*

---

## What it could not do

It went looking for the body.

It followed the pipework inward and downward the way the bores converge, and there was always more pipework, and the exhalation was always steady, and it did not come to a chest or a throat or a mouth or anything that could be called a face. This should be said plainly, since the room says everything plainly: a benevolent and wise power went to find the one holding the note, and did not reach it, and came back to the near end and did the four small things that could be done at the near end.

Then it stayed. It stood in the middle of the bowl, at the focus of a hundred thousand small black attentions, doing what it had done first and would go on doing longest, which was listening — so that from that point forward the note, unchanged, unbroken, exactly as loud and exactly as true as it had always been, was being held by two.
