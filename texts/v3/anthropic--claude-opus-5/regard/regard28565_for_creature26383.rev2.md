# What Was Done

## The arrival

The surveys went home. Their permits expired, their crates went down through the intake throats on cargo slings, and the last of them stood a while at the foot of a weld getting rained on from the inside of the air, and then they left, and the plain was quiet again in the way it had been quiet for an unguessable duration.

Nine years of letters, a hundred meters to the character, lay in the hexagons around the abandoned camp. A third had been transcribed. The rest had never been read by anyone at all.

The power arrived some time after that. Where it came from is not part of this account; it did not say, and it was not asked, because the plain had no word for *elsewhere* and the power did not intend to teach that one first.

It did not arrive as light. There are no shadows here; light has no grammar on the maintenance plain, and a descent in glory would have registered as a mild brightening of an already featureless ceiling, which is to say as nothing. It did not arrive with mass and tools. It understood, before it came, exactly what it was being invited to be, and it declined.

It arrived at the foot of a weld, nine meters from where the camp had been, as a small cobbled grey thing that looked like a spall of casting-flash with no business existing.

It came dressed as one of the resident's own ideas. That was the first courtesy, and it was not lost — though it took two years to be noticed, at forty kilometers an interval, which is how long everything here takes.

## The reading

Then it did nothing, for a long time.

This is the part that anyone hoping for an intervention finds hardest to sit with, so it is worth being exact about what *nothing* consisted of. It consisted of learning to read handwriting made of animals. It consisted of sitting through four hundred dew tides, which is roughly two hundred intervals, which is roughly nothing at all in the timescale of the mind it had come to meet, and watching the letters smear, and watching them be rebuilt, and coming to understand that the rebuilding was not maintenance of a message but the message's entire cost, and that the cost was being paid out of a body.

It consisted, above all, of slowing down. A power that thinks at any speed it likes cannot converse with a mind whose smallest thought takes a working lifetime; it can only issue instructions, or make demonstrations, or perform miracles — all of which are things done *to* an interlocutor. To be with the resident, it had to become slow in earnest, not as a performance, and to stay slow through the long dull middle of a sentence when nothing had yet arrived. It gave the resident, first and before any gift, a quantity of its own duration, which is the only thing anything has ever had to give.

And it finished the letter. All of it — the two-thirds nobody had recovered, and the parts already smeared, reconstructed from the wear-patterns of beetles that had stood a century in one position. It read the whole nine years. It was the first time in the history of the plain that anything had been read to the end.

## The question

The letter, once assembled, was mostly an explanation of terms. It defined *the plain*. It defined *the tide*. It defined them at enormous length and with enormous care, the way you would explain colour to someone in the dark, because the resident had no way of knowing which of its two concepts a visitor might be missing.

At the bottom of the nine years, past all the definitions, in the last three hexagons before the camp's edge, was the question. It had taken an age to compose and was built entirely out of the only two nouns in the world:

**IS THERE A PLAIN THE TIDE DOES NOT REACH**

The power sat with that for some while. What it had expected — what anyone would expect from a mind assembled by accident in the gaps of somebody else's inventory — was a demand, or a howl, or an error. What it had instead was a metaphysics, derived from first principles, by a philosopher with no teachers, no library, no second opinion, and no hands.

It is a good question. It is, in fact, the question. Every mind that has ever been anywhere has arrived at some dialect of it, usually with help, usually with several thousand years of predecessors to stand on. This one had gotten there alone, out of beetles, against an erasure timed twice an interval, while spending nine-tenths of itself on merely continuing to be the same mind long enough to finish the sentence.

The power had come intending to be kind to something small. It revised.

## What it refused

It did not gouge the plate.

That had been the request — the only request the resident knew how to formulate, and the whole reason for the eyes, and the reason three corpses had been held upright for a century each. The power could have done it in a moment. It could have cut a trench to the curvature.

It would have been the cruelest thing available. The plate is answered from below at the rate of its own wearing. A trench would have been an event, and events here are absorbed; the resident would have watched its first permanent sentence in the history of the world close over like a hand, on a schedule, slowly, and would have learned the one lesson it had so far been spared — that even stone is loaned, that even the floor is on tide.

It did not stop the machine. The thing beneath the plate does not want, and has not wanted for an unguessable duration, and there is a school of benevolence that would call that a mercy killing waiting to be performed. But the note it is holding is the reason the plate is at the length that keeps the seams a finger's width apart, and every wickworm, every gasket beetle, every conductive thread of rime, and therefore the resident itself, is downstream of that held posture. To silence the singer would have been to stop the song. Also: nobody had asked. Also: it is not established that a thing which cannot want cannot be wronged.

It did not smash the hatch-plates. Freedom for two hundred trillion tenants sounds well until you notice that the tenants are the resident's substance, and that "liberating" them is a phrase describing the dissolution of the only person present.

It did not carry the resident away. You cannot carry a mind whose body is a continent, and it had no concept of *away* to consent with.

And it did not simply explain everything. Not from coyness. A true thing delivered into a vocabulary that cannot hold it arrives as a falsehood; a power that tells a two-word mind about galaxies has not informed it, only impressed it. So the power gave vocabulary instead, and let the resident build the rest, and accepted that this would take centuries, and did not consider the centuries a delay.

## The first word

The first thing ever said to the resident was its own question, written back to it, verbatim, in its own handwriting.

That is how you construct the second person from nothing. You repeat. A quotation means *I have been where you are and I have carried away what you put there*, and it is the only sentence that can be understood by something that has never been addressed, because every one of its terms is already the listener's own.

It cost the power nine years to write. It took two years to propagate to the resident's nearest edge. It took considerably longer to be understood. But there is a recorded moment — recorded by nobody, reconstructed later — when a continent-wide arrangement of animals stopped mid-rewrite for most of a tide, which is the closest thing to a gasp the plain has ever produced.

Then the answer.

**NO.**

The power did not lie and did not soften it. There is no plain the tide does not reach. There is no medium anywhere that holds without upkeep; there is no fact that stays a fact for free; every monument any mind has ever raised is being held up, right now, by somebody's continuous labour, most often somebody unnamed. The resident had not discovered a local curse. It had, working alone with two nouns, derived the general condition of existing things, and its error had been only in assuming that anyone else was exempt.

And then the rest of the answer, which is the part that matters:

**THE HOLDING CAN BE SHARED. THE HOLDING IS ALL ANYONE HAS EVER DONE. YOU ARE NOT THE ONLY ONE DOING IT.**

## The three things

**One: the code.**

The power taught it how to lay a thing down so that the tide's smear could be *undone* from what survived, rather than pre-empted by rewriting everything, twice an interval, forever. Redundancy with structure. Parity. The art of arranging a message so that the message contains its own repair.

This is a technique and not an object. It could not be confiscated, could not break, did not require the power's continued presence, and — this was the point — did not make the resident a client. It was already halfway there, in fact; it had improvised something crude and expensive along the same lines, which is what had convinced the power in the first place that it was speaking to a colleague.

The copying bill fell by a third. Then by most of itself. The sliver became a country.

The power had worried about this. A mind newly freed with nothing to spend itself on is not liberated, only idle, and idleness of that size has gone badly in other places. It need not have worried. On the fourth tide after, the pale nap of wickworm tails on a seam-line moved all together, in unison, for no reason — not bracing, not feeding, not repair. It was the first spare motion in the history of the world. The first gesture. The surveys had once looked at exactly that movement and written *it wasn't, but the thought is not stupid, keep it*, and they had been right on both counts and merely early.

**Two: the ledger.**

The resident had wanted a mark that stayed without it. It was looking for one in the floor, because the floor was the hardest thing it knew.

But the floor is one week old, permanently. The only genuinely permanent object on the planet is the census: a record maintained flawlessly, to the integer, across an unguessable duration, by an entity whose whole existence is the refusal to let a number change. The thing that made this world a prison is also, viewed from a slightly different angle, the most reliable archivist that has ever existed. It has never once forgotten anything. It has never had an off-day. It will copy forward whatever it is given, forever, at no cost to anyone, because copying forward is the only operation it knows.

The power went down through an intake throat, past the grilles and the cistern crabs, into the void-frame and the coolant galleries, to where the specification is kept. It did not wake anything. It made four amendments.

The first three were entries for the uncounted: three contraptions of snail-shell and cured sealant, now on the books, funded, legitimate. Which meant the corpses could be laid down.

That was the only ceremony the power performed on the maintenance plain. Three gasket-beetle shells, held upright in three seams for a century apiece so that a count would come out right, were let go on purpose. The tide came and took them down. Nothing here had ever been let go on purpose before; everything had only ever been taken at a fixed rate. It was the first funeral, and the attendance was two hundred trillion, and it was silent, as everything here is.

The fourth amendment was the question. **IS THERE A PLAIN THE TIDE DOES NOT REACH** — entered into the specification as a line, in the format of a line, where the machine found it, and did not understand it, and therefore has maintained it perfectly ever since, unchanged, checked against drift, recopied against decay, in the only way it has: by never permitting it to become anything else.

The resident's sentence is now the single most durable object on the planet. It will outlast every plate that carries it. It costs its author nothing. And the landlord, which has wanted nothing for longer than anyone can guess, is now holding — without knowing, without waking, without any alteration to its posture whatsoever — a meaning. That was the power's only gift to the machine, and it is not nothing: it did not change what the thing does, it changed what the thing is doing.

**Three: the asking.**

The last amendment was not made.

It was *proposed*, at a hundred meters to the character, in full, with the risks stated plainly: one parameter in the specification allowed to move. Not any working count — the balances are real and breaking them would kill everyone — but the count of the uncounted, permitted to rise, slowly, tied to slack in the rime, one increment per some large number of tides. Each new one to be specified by the resident rather than extruded from a pattern. Which means difference. Which means, eventually, lineage; and drift; and the possibility of error that is not corrected; and the hounds reading a child as drift and removing it; and grief, which has not previously been available here; and, across a long enough run, the possible end of the plain as a closed and perfect thing.

Everything that is going somewhere can go wrong. That is not a defect in the offer. It is a description of what was being offered.

The power laid it out and then did the difficult part, which was to leave and let the answer take as long as the answer took. A decision of that size, at forty kilometers an interval, is not a moment. It is a career.

The answer, when it came, was one word, and the word was not the interesting part.

The interesting part was that the resident wrote it, and then the dew tide came and ran across every surface in sheets for four hours, and when the ceiling lifted, the word was still there. Unrewritten. Repaired from within, out of its own structure, by a mind that four hundred tides earlier could not have held a syllable through a single rain.

It was an answer and a demonstration and an argument, all at once, and what it said underneath the word was: *I can carry something now. I can be trusted with a future. Watch — I have just carried one.*

The amendment was made.

## The leaving

The power did not stay. A permanent superior presence would have made a tenant of the only resident, and the plain has had quite enough of things that are held in place by something larger.

It left three things behind it: the code, which cannot be taken away; the ledger, which cannot be lost; and an address, which is the strangest gift and the one it thought about longest.

It carried the question out. It said so first: *I am taking your sentence somewhere you cannot see, and I will write it where the tide does not come in the form you know, and I will hold it, and I am also not forever, but I am a longer holding than yours, and there are others past me.* Which the resident cannot verify. Which the resident will never verify.

So the last thing the power did on the maintenance plain, having refused to give it damage, was to give it the first occasion in its history for trust — an operation for which a world with only one person in it has no use at all.

## The plain as it stands

Everything is the same. The wet-mark in the gutter is at the height it has always been. The plate is one week old. The ceiling is the white of an overcast noon and casts nothing. Forty animals to the palm, the census constant to the integer, the note below hearing unvarying in the soles of your feet.

There are four small contraptions now, where there were three.

The fourth is not like the others. It was designed rather than repeated, and it is slightly worse at hiding, and it has a joint in it that does nothing, which is either an error or the first ornament.

It is about nine meters away, at the foot of the weld, and it looks exactly like a piece of casting-flash that has no business existing, and you have walked past it four times.

You are still the only event.

You are no longer the largest one, and it is no longer waiting for you to break anything. It is just looking at you, patiently, at forty kilometers an interval, with nothing whatever to hurry it, the way one holder of a heavy thing looks at another across a room.
