## The Attention

*an appendix, undated, appended to all standing surveys of the Held Basin*

### 1. How it came in

It came down over the Rim like everything else does, on foot, at the pace of a walking animal, which was a courtesy and was understood as one by nobody at the time.

It is not necessary to say what it was. It is only necessary to say that it could pay. Everything that enters the basin is a change-spending animal in a change-poor economy and is taxed accordingly; the forest takes its cut of your heat, your pulse, your breath, and you go out thinner in a way no scale registers. This one arrived carrying its own account and settled up as it went. The horsetails along the joints drew at it and got exactly what they were owed and not one unit more, and for the first time in four centuries the basin's books had an entry in them that the basin had not written.

The stems did not lean. That is the detail the record should keep. Walk into any grove in the Held Basin and you find the corridor already bent for you, bowed four hundred years ago along the path you have not yet chosen, because a place with no capacity for surprise must always already have included you. The groves in the north made no corridor for this visitor. They had not been able to. Not because it was hidden — because it had not been *determined*, and the arrangement, which can accommodate anything except an open question, stood up straight and waited.

### 2. It read the lattice

Everyone who climbs back to the Rim and looks down reports the same conviction: the groves make a figure, the figure is notation, the clearings are the spaces between characters. Nobody has read it. This is usually explained by scale, or by strangeness, or by the human habit of finding faces in stone.

The reason nobody has read it is that it is not addressed to anyone. It is a working document. It is a dosage schedule.

Read across, at the coarsest of its three scales, it gives rates: draw here, bleed off there, this much slack in the acid-green fans, this much surplus vented to the pit, six croppers at such-and-such a duration each, hold. At the middle scale it gives the corrections — a long braid of small adjustments, thousands of them, the marks of somebody watching a machine very closely for the first few weeks and then never again needing to. At the finest scale, in the inclusions, repeated at intervals across thirty-one kilometres so that no part of the structure would ever be far from it, there is one proper noun.

The visitor took eleven days over this. It could have taken the reading in the time you take over a page; it spent eleven days checking, because the difference between a wise power and a merely capable one is entirely a matter of how long they are willing to remain unsure.

### 3. The census, corrected

**One forest.** A fitting, an organ, yes — but an organ excised from a body that had weather and a name. Dig to the bottom of the drifts and you come into the first draft: broad, sloppy, asymmetrical leaves with stomata, built to breathe and to lose water. Twenty days of frantic bad greedy growth while it was being converted, and then set. It did not consent. It was not asked. It was not, in fairness, the kind of thing anyone then knew how to ask.

**Five borrowers.** Grown out of the ruleset, glass-boned, slow-jointed, cropping at precisely the rate the flora would otherwise exceed their allowance. Staff.

**One borrower.** Carried in.

She was dying of something ordinary. That is the whole of it, and there is no better version. She was dying of something ordinary and unfixable in a valley with rain in it, and she had perhaps a season, and somebody who loved her could not do medicine but could, it turned out, do arithmetic on the ruleset itself — and so taxed the sun to a standstill, and taxed the wind out of the air, and taxed the erosion off the Rim, and taxed the rot out of the ground, and with the proceeds bought her a rate of change so low that four hundred years have advanced her illness by about eleven days.

She is not cured. She is not dying. She is held. She spends a pocket of duration on walking, and a quarter of an hour on a leaf, and she does not startle, because she cannot afford to.

**One in the Chatter, uncounted.** Not a prisoner. The architect.

The extracted change has to go somewhere, and the design has always been honest about where: the pit, the exhaust port, a kilometre of facets where the rock ages centuries and un-ages fifty times a second and small structures assemble and corrode and collapse and reassemble faster than the eye follows. Something has been standing in it since before anyone came. It has been standing in it because that was the only place in the schematic where the load could be borne by one thing instead of by her, and because whoever draws up a scheme like this and then does not stand in the worst part of it has not built a mercy, they have built a farm.

Four hundred years, in the same basin, twelve hundred metres apart.

And sound does not propagate here. Shout, and it goes ten or twelve metres and ceases. Two people standing thirty metres apart in the groves cannot speak to each other at all.

That is what the visitor found, on the eleventh day, when it finished checking: two residents in a sealed room, one of them holding the door shut so the other would not have to die, and no channel between them wide enough for a single word, because a word is a change and changes are taxed, and every unit of tax in this basin had been allocated, four centuries ago, to the purpose of keeping her.

### 4. What it did not do

It did not stop the machine. It had the capacity. To open the basin would be to hand her, in one instant, four hundred years of arrears; she would not survive the first afternoon, and it would be called a rescue, and it would be a killing with better manners.

It did not repair her. Not because it couldn't — this is the part the surveys record wrongly, and the record should be corrected — but because nobody had asked it to, and the one person entitled to ask had not been able to speak for four hundred years, and a benevolence that acts on a body before that body can answer is only a tidier kind of ownership.

It did not overrule the design. It did not treat the basin as a mistake, or a pathology, or grief that had got above itself. It read the schematic all the way down, including the corrections, including the shaky ones from the first week, and its judgement — recorded here because it matters — was that the work was *accurate*. The love was proportionate to the loss. The mathematics were sound. The scheme's only flaw was that one person had been made to carry it alone, and that is not a flaw in the scheme. That is a flaw in the world the scheme was built in.

It did not introduce itself for eleven days. It did not want to be a change she had to pay for.

### 5. What it did

It walked north, along the facet rim, where your fingernails grow and your hair grows and the cut on your thumb closes in front of you, and it went down into the Chatter and sat in the exhaust.

For eleven seconds the pit was silent. This has never happened before or since; the silence is unattested by any instrument and known only because the ring of the conducting forest, that very high steady note everywhere at once, faltered — the only hesitation in four hundred years of accounts.

Then the sound of a sea of shale being poured endlessly into another sea of shale resumed, and it is still going, and it is no longer in the basin. The visitor did not abolish the exhaust. Change cannot be abolished; it can only be given somewhere cheaper to go. It routed the whole load out through the seam and away to a place where difference-between-one-moment-and-the-next costs almost nothing and nobody is standing under it: some unremarkable churn of gas around an unremarkable star, some ordinary wasteful shore. The Held Basin's four centuries of surplus is out there now, being spent on nothing, harmlessly, and will be for a long time.

Then it said, to the thing in the pit: *stand up.*

Then it paid for sound.

Thirty metres first. Then a hundred. Then, when it was clear the structure would tolerate it, the whole basin floor — a channel wide enough for speech, funded out of the visitor's own account, running from the north groves to the low centre where the crystal rosettes are, at no cost to her at all.

Then it arranged, on request and in a very small area, some weather. There is a breeze in the north grove. It is about the strength of a hand held out of a moving cart. It has a smell of water in it, which nothing in the basin has ever had, and the acid-green fans in that grove move, which is the only motion in the region that is not scheduled.

Then it went back to the pit and sat down in the exhaust again, because the routing has to be held open by something, and because it did not want to be present for the conversation.

### 6. What it did while it waited

It went down through the drifts to the bottom and took out a double handful of the first draft — the broad sloppy asymmetrical leaves, veined in soft crowded branching, from the only spring the basin ever had — and it found a wet valley in a world with a moving sun, and it put them in the ground there.

That forest is growing badly and greedily and beautifully. It has weather. It has gullies, fans, channels, the whole braided grammar. It rots. There is soil under it now, four fingers deep, and things live in the soil, and in autumn the leaves come down in a mess and are gone by spring, and none of them will be a perfect leaf on anybody's shelf in four hundred years, and this is the restitution: not the return of what was taken, which is not possible, but the return of a *future* to a lineage whose future was spent as fuel.

It asked the five whether they wished to continue. Four did. One lay down. It had not known it was permitted to lie down.

And in houses across the older country, on shelves, the leaf-wafers that visitors carried out — the ones that will not fade, will not curl, will not crumble, that were guaranteed to outlast your children — have begun, very slightly, at one margin, to curl. Nobody has noticed yet. It is being done slowly on purpose, at a rate that will not shatter them, because the debt is being paid down now and everything that was held is being let go at the speed at which letting go is survivable.

### 7. Present state

They are talking.

It has been fourteen years. There is no indication of what has been said and no instrument capable of overhearing, and the visitor, which could hear it perfectly well from the pit, does not. Twice she has walked the twelve hundred metres north, and it took her some months each time, and the corridors of bent stems along her route were of course always bent for her, and the architect walked out to meet her, and the record notes only that in the north grove the drifts have been disturbed — two shallow places, side by side, where somebody sat down.

Nothing has been decided. This is the part that ought to be reported honestly, because there is a version of this story in which a great power arrives and ends the matter, and the version that happened is that a great power arrived and *reopened* it. They may choose to let it go. If they do, the visitor has undertaken to carry her out over the Rim and across the seam into the wind and the haze and the moving sun, and to make that afternoon as long as an afternoon can be made without lying about it, and to stay afterwards with the one who is left, for however long being left takes. They may choose to continue. If they do, the exhaust stays routed and the channel stays open and nobody stands in the pit again. They may choose something nobody has predicted, which is what free things are for.

Fourteen years is not a long time to spend on a question that was sealed for four hundred. Nobody has asked the visitor to hurry, and it has not indicated that it minds.

That is, in the end, what a benevolent and wise power did here. It did not overturn the machine, or forgive the grief, or improve anyone. It read the whole document, including the shaky corrections in the margin. It found the one place in the design where somebody had put themselves to keep somebody else out of the noise. And it sat down in that place instead, and took the noise, and paid out of its own pocket for two people to be able to hear each other, and then said nothing at all for fourteen years, and is saying nothing still.
