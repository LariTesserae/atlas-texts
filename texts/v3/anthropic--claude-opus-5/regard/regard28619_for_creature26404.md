## The Attention

It came in at the water, because everything here comes in at the water.

Not as light. Light would have carried sixty metres in that cloud and would have been the only new thing in the delta and would therefore have become the delta, and every hand-sign and audit and drowned island for thirty-one years would have been reorganised around it afterwards, which is precisely the kind of damage a wise power learns to be afraid of. So it came in the way the boulders come in: inside the ice, indistinguishable from the ice, four knots, at night, in sleet.

It spent the first four days doing nothing whatsoever. That is the part of the account nobody believes.

What it did in those four days was learn. It learned the ice first, since the ice is the grammar. It learned the ninety signs, and it learned them the way the seasonals learn them, by watching hands in bad light and getting two of them wrong for a week. It learned the exact number of geese — two hundred and fourteen thousand, six hundred and eleven, falling — and then it put the number away and never gave it to anybody, because *two hundred thousand, maybe more* is doing work that the true figure would undo. It found out why the dogs go deaf. It has never said. There are four people here who believe that absolutely, together, without proof, and there was nothing in the mechanism worth what the proof would have cost them.

And it read the wall over the stove, and it took the instruction personally: **NOTHING SIGNED IN MAY.**

Then, on the fourth night, it went out and spliced the northeast stay.

The wolverine had it three-quarters through. Another gust and the mast would have come down across the hydrophone cable and taken the discharge record with it in the middle of a jam release, which is to say in the middle of the only ninety hours of the year that Tarrant would trade a finger for. The splice is not a good one. It is deliberately not a good one: it is the splice a cold man makes in the dark with a headlamp and stiff hands, slightly over-tensioned, the tail wrapped the way you wrap it when you are hurrying. Tarrant assumed Amos. Amos assumed Tarrant. Nobody signed anything. It was May.

It did not touch the wolverine, which has an agenda, and the agenda does not contain a delta.

## What It Declined

It could have quieted the flock. Two hundred thousand voices with no gaps in them, and it could have opened gaps, and everyone would have slept, and no one would ever again have sat up in a bunk and said *what* into four hundred thousand wings.

It sat with that one for some time. It is a real cruelty, the flock. Ket has aged this month. But the wall of voices is the same wall that is grazing the sedge to stubble and grubbing the rhizomes and turning silt into nitrogen and dying here in quantity and going into the peat, and the noise is not a side-effect of the labour force, it *is* the labour force, audible. And the fortnight — the answering, the ear-then-mouth, the flat hand on the back for three seconds — is the only rite these people have, and they built it themselves, out of nothing, in a place where speech does not carry. A power that takes away the terror takes away the ceremony, and then they have quiet, and no way to touch each other.

So: no.

It could have stilled the ice. No.

It could have made the nodules permanent. A hundred billion of them, four-year lifespan, then sand — it could have given them centuries. But *nothing here is old* is not a defect of the Increase, it is the Increase, and immortal machinery in the mud would make the place a noun within a generation. It left them mortal. It did add one word to their vocabulary, which is the sort of thing that is very hard to notice: in among *more here, less here, the channel wants to swing east, let it,* there is now a way for pore water to carry the meaning *someone is standing here.* It is slow and cheap like everything else they say. It takes six hours to cross a bar. It is therefore useless perhaps a third of the time, which is honest, and it means that on a good day the delta will not open underneath a man in his fifties in the middle of a sentence.

It could have restored the coastline. This was the largest of them. A century of going backwards at three metres a year, the drying racks, the two cemeteries, the last of the coffins carried inland in the year Sofie was fifteen, and the ones they stopped bothering with. It could put all of that back in its old position with its old names, correctly, tonight.

It went and looked at her map instead — the one that is wrong on purpose — and understood what it was looking at, and did nothing for a season.

In September, when the world had quieted down to a mere river and things could be said aloud and signed, it asked her.

That is the whole of it. It asked. Nobody had ever asked; the land came back four hundred hectares a year, in the wrong shape, with the wrong birds in the wrong numbers, muddier water than her father worked, and nobody asked, and a thing can be a restitution and an imposition in the same motion. So it asked, in a normal voice, sitting on the bench under the four posts and the roof, and it made clear that *no* was an available answer and that it would not ask twice.

She thought about it for eleven days. Then she said: tell me the depth of water over my grandmother's camp, and nothing else, and do not put it right.

It told her: eleven point four metres, and the racks are gone, and the ground they stood on is now a bar forty-one kilometres downstream with willows on it, nineteen years old.

She has not amended her map. There are now two maps that disagree, and she keeps both, and one of them is asked for.

## What It Did

It changed the form.

This is the intervention that a benevolent power with any sense would rank first, and it is unwritable, and no one will ever make a film about it. In the audit, both records go in — Island 47-D and Ivutaq — and one of them is asked for. So it went south, into the parts of the world that have no rivers in them, and over eleven years, by the ordinary means, it caused the schedule to acquire a second field. Local name. Required. Not-null. You cannot file the return without it.

Eleven years, for that. It considers it the best work it has done here.

It took the watch. It does not sleep, having no reason to, and the Increase is a place where nothing can be heard coming, including you, and so the obvious use of a thing that never sleeps is to be the one who hears. On the night the jam let go at three in the morning and backed the water into the willows and drained a thousand hectares in ninety minutes, the count that went out was right. It was right because something in the noise was listening for one voice in four hundred thousand and can tell, always, the difference between a goose and a name. Nobody was left standing in a channel that had not existed in October. There is no record of this. There is a record of two seasonals who came back to the bunkhouse by a route neither of them chose and could not afterwards describe, and who made the sign for *I'm all right* with the thumb pressed hard.

It gave Tarrant the missing ninety minutes.

Year nine, jam release, instruments under water: a hole in the discharge record that he has been reaching into for a decade. It arrived as a file, unsigned, in the format he uses, resolved to the fifteen-minute interval. It cost nothing to give and harmed nothing and it was the only thing he actually wanted. He wept, briefly, in a shuttered plywood room, inaudibly, and then held up two fingers to nobody.

And on the night in the following spring when he came down the levee in the dark with the intention of going out onto the floes to see the thing itself, there was something standing on the bank. Not blocking. Just standing, at the waterline, where the fog gathers because the nodules are two degrees warmer than the river. He stopped. There was a long moment in which the two of them regarded each other, and then he went back up to the bunkhouse and made coffee, a fist rotated at the sternum, for four people who were not asleep.

It has never told anyone. He has been told twice.

It offered Amos the stakes. All hundred and forty of them, to the centimetre, instantly, every May, forever — no more walking out with a bad hip into the sleet to lay a hand on a piece of rebar so that a number can be legally asserted.

Amos laughed for some time. Then he said: what would I do in May?

It never offered again. Not re-offering is the entire respect. It carries the bundle now, some years, when the mat is heaving; it is not proud.

It keeps the matches dry.

That is its one piece of permanent infrastructure in a place that eats infrastructure — four posts, a roof, a bench, candle stubs in a jar, a plastic bag of matches put there by nobody official. Ten years of silt is a metre of silt, and the chapel has been raised twice, quietly, by someone, and restocked every spring since, and it is the oldest continuously functioning built thing in the Increase, and it has no name on it anywhere.

It moved a slump block once, to buy the bunkhouse a season. It put a seal carcass on the drift line four kilometres upstream in the week the bear came out thin, and did it again the next year, and the year after, and the sign made with two hands — the beautiful one — is made less often now, and nobody knows why, and everyone has a theory involving the willows.

## The Ninety-First Sign

It never announced itself, and it never will, because the moment it is the subject it is the only subject, and then this place becomes a place where something happened rather than a place where the land is being grown out of the river on purpose by letting the river do what rivers do.

So there is only this. Somewhere in the last decade a ninety-first sign entered the pidgin. Nobody remembers who made it up. It is the old sign reversed — mouth, then ear, instead of ear, then mouth — and where the old one means *someone has answered their name*, the new one means *someone was answered.* You make it for another person, afterwards, once they have taken their hand off your back. It reads at distance in bad light. The dogs know it.

It is passed on by imitation, in the first week of everybody's first spring, and it will outlast the nodules, and it will outlast the chapel, and it costs nothing, and it is not signed in May.
