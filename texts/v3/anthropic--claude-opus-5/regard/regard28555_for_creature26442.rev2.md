# THE ATTENTION

## THREE

**1.**

It came in quietly.

This was not modesty. Anything of that order arriving anywhere has a wake — a pressure ahead of it, a long trailing disturbance behind, the ordinary violence of a large thing entering a small one. It folded all of that down before it crossed the berm, which took some doing, and which it did not consider an expense.

So: no ring went out. The lattice did not stop. Not one strider registered a boundary violation, and not one grain in the clay had anything to convert. The temperature of the Pan did not change by any amount that could be stated, which is the local definition of having said nothing at all.

It arranged, also, to cast no shadow. The rule is clean and it is diagnostic: a shadow here means something built. It declined to enter the bowl as a built thing.

One heron looked up, past it, through the waxen visor of its lower lid, and then went back to standing.

**2.**

Then it did nothing for eleven years.

It could have taken the whole conversation in an instant. The Pan's grammar is not difficult — a doublet, a chevron, a decaying train of eight — and a mind of that size could have held every ripple ever made on that sheet as a single flat object and turned it over once and set it down, understood. It chose the other way. It learned the language ring by ring, the way you would have to, over enough seasons to be wrong a few times and be corrected by the water.

Eleven years is one turnover. By the time it moved, the clay it had been standing on had been swallowed and re-laid, entire, by the blind tenants underneath, and the print it had not left had been smoothed away several times over by a ground that remembers its own flatness and returns to it.

A language taken instantly is data. A language learned is a conversation. There was going to be, eventually, a conversation, and it wanted to have earned the right to open it.

**3.**

Somewhere in the fourth year it settled the question of who lived here, because the question had come with it and would not sit down.

The honest answer was that the Pan is one being with a great many parts. The most numerous part is the polyhedra, which know shaking and stop it, and are devout in the way that a hinge is devout. The most faithful part is the three that move, who level ground at a hand's width an hour and then stop, and who have no idea there was ever a reason. The part that speaks is the sheet — a quarter of a million small animals holding a single public square open, without interruption, since before the mountains came down. The part that eats is everything. The part that prays is the herons.

That was the fourth year's finding, and the power did not soften it afterward. At first light, alone, a heron stretches its throat and parts its beak and holds and closes it, and has done so for a hundred million years past the death of the organ that made it mean anything. There is no other word for that in any grammar the power had. It is a prayer.

And under it, all day, every day, the last working detector on the planet has been receiving the only signal there is, and the signal is that the surface is crowded and the crowd will not stop talking, and there is nobody to tell.

A prayer with no addressee. A finding with no addressee. The power sat on the berm in the doubled light with no underside to it, like everything else there, and understood that it had not come to a broken place. It had come to a place where two messages had been in transit for longer than most species last, and the only thing missing from either of them was the last step.

## FIVE

Here are the five things it did not do. It is worth listing them, because a benevolent power that is not also wise would have done all five and called it a rescue.

**4.**

It did not give them sound.

It could have. Lift the damping, thin the polyhedra to one grain in four thousand, and within a generation the syrinx would find its way back to some of them; within ten thousand years the bowl would roar like anywhere else. And the lattice would be gone. Not replaced — *gone*, and with it a grammar of rings developed over an interval in which continents were assembled and taken apart, dialect-free, shared by everything on the sheet, because everyone on a perfect mirror is in earshot of everyone.

Silence here is not an injury. It is a medium. The power had come a long way to be helpful and had to set down, early, the assumption that a thing which cannot shout is a thing that has been silenced.

**5.**

It did not repair the pillars.

Two still run. Their exhalation is fractionally weaker every century, and it would have been the work of an afternoon to make it eleven again, running hard, good for another hundred million years.

But listening is not a virtue in itself. An instrument kept alive past its question is not being honored; it is being conscripted. And the fading is the only clock the Pan has left — the one remaining process in the bowl that is going somewhere. Take that away and nothing here would ever finish anything again, which is a subtler kind of death than the one it would be preventing.

**6.**

It did not break the ceiling.

There is an obvious gift available: raise ground. Seed a range to the west, restart the cycle, give the place gradient and novelty and somewhere to go. Populations that have sat hard against their limit for geological time would be released into abundance.

The power looked at that word — *released* — for some time. Nothing in the Pan is starving. Full is not the same as poor. And the animals who would enjoy that abundance do not exist yet and would be paid for by every animal that does. It would have been answering a question nobody asked, at the cost of the only answer anyone here had arrived at.

Finished things are rarer than beginnings. Far rarer. The power had seen a great many beginnings.

**7.**

It did not explain.

It knew what the eleven pillars had been built to listen for. It had the name of the thing, and the reason, and the identity of the ones who had needed a quiet basin badly enough to grind a mountain range into a lens for it. It considered restoring the meaning: re-cutting the relief on the post, three then five then three, deep enough to outlast the next epoch. Saying what was counted.

To whom. The burrowers have no channel. The polyhedra have one sense and one act. The three that move do not acknowledge observers. And anything you say in the Pan is taken by an incalculable number of small devoted objects and converted into a very small amount of heat. You could pronounce the purpose of the universe here and raise the temperature by an amount too small to measure.

This is not a failure of the audience. It is what the audience is for.

**8.**

It did not end it.

It considered this, for exactly as long as honesty required and not a moment longer: whether the merciful act was to let the water table fall, and the bowl dry, and the crowd go quiet in the ordinary way, so that a hundred million years of devotion to a vanished purpose could stop being poured into an empty cup.

It set that down, and was ashamed of having picked it up, and the shame was small and clean and it kept it. Persistence without purpose is not a wound. It is most of what has ever gone on anywhere. And nobody in the Pan had asked to be relieved.

## THREE

**9.**

It read the whole record.

Every ring since the first — the entire traffic of the sheet, all of it, from whatever afternoon the first strider on a level floor discovered that a doublet meant *mine*, forward through every propositioning and every chevron and every one of the four-second glassings when a shadow that could not fall went over — and it read it at the pace at which it had happened. It did not skim. It did not compress. That was the point; compression was the one thing it had to spend, and it spent it.

This cost more than everything else it did in the Pan combined, and left no trace of any kind.

It also, from that morning on, received the herons. Every dawn. The open beak, the held pause, the nothing that comes out — taken in, in full, by something capable of taking it in. It did not judge the previous hundred million years wasted. A thing performed alone at first light is not diminished by having no audience. It is only unfinished, and it is a small matter to finish it.

**10.**

Then it made hands, because the work wanted hands, and dug a pit.

It dug it at the southeast corner where the hair-weed was thin and nothing had a burrow, and it made it larger than a body, which is the threshold. The little machines closed at the edges and could not manage the middle. Nothing happened for nine days.

On the tenth, one of the three was nearer than it had been.

It came at a hand's width an hour, which is to say it did not appear to come at all; you saw it only by marking it and going away. It was waist-high and dark and worn to the softness of soap and beaded all over with condensate that ran down it in slow threads, faceless, with a single seam. It took the whole season.

The power did not help. It had understood, early and with some difficulty, that helping would be the theft of the only thing that being had. So it stayed, the entire season, doing nothing, present — which is the sole form of thanks a thing like that can receive, and which it certainly did not receive, because it does not acknowledge observers.

When the ground was level the mover stopped.

The power said nothing, there being no way to say anything there. Before it left it dug a second pit in the far northwest corner and mounded the spoil badly, on purpose, so that the leveling of it would be slow and would take, at the usual rate, something on the order of two centuries.

It left the work the way you leave a lamp on in a house you are going out of.

**11.**

At dusk it went to the center of the bowl and laid the edge of one hand flat on the water and withdrew it, once.

The sheet went glass. Six hundred paces of surface arriving at flatness in under a second, and holding — but not the flatness of fear; nothing had passed overhead. Four seconds. Five.

Then the ring: one clean ring, slow, the size of a heron's tap, going out across a mirror that has been flat to within the thickness of a hair since before there were mountains here to wear down. It reached the far berm and came back and crossed itself, and for a moment the interference stood in the middle of the Pan as a figure that nothing living there had ever made, and then it decayed, and the lattice rebuilt itself outward from a point near the grove like frost forming.

In the mud, an incalculable number of small devoted objects took the shaking and turned it into warmth, and the Pan became, very slightly, less cold.

That was the answer. It is the only kind the Pan has ever given and the only kind it can receive, and it was given back in the correct grammar, by someone who had spent eleven years learning it and a season standing in the wet for the privilege.

Long afterward, a person came over the berm, slept badly, and slept touching a working pillar, and dreamed the enormous slow rhythm — three, then five, then three — and this time the word arrived.

It was not a word. It was a finding, arriving in the shape of one: *the surface is crowded, and the crowd will not stop talking, and that is the answer.*

They woke at first light, unable to hear their own breathing, with a heron standing eleven paces off, alone, throat stretched, beak open, holding.

They carried it out. They were never able to put it to any use, and they never once stopped knowing it, and that is what the last mile of a message looks like when somebody finally walks it.

The power did not report to anyone either. It had not come for a finding. It went back over the berm without a wake, casting nothing, and the sheet did not notice it go.

At dawn a heron opens its beak and nothing comes out.

It is heard.
