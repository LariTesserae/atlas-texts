# The Attention

## On arrival

The power reads the file before it goes near the room.

This is the first thing worth knowing about it. It reads the acoustic model, and the impedance budget, and the cryogenic schedule with its four decades of unbroken green, and the work order for the second coat of primer that was raised and then never closed. It reads the memo that established, in the flat language of a discipline nobody had a name for yet, that the way to maintain Cavity Nine is to never touch it.

And having read that, it understands that it is not permitted to enter.

*Nothing may be added.* A lesser power would have made an exception for itself; powers do, routinely, and call it grace. This one does the arithmetic on its own presence, gets a number, finds that the number is not zero, and declines.

So it comes in on the light. Light was specified. Five thousand kelvin through four caged coffers is in the model, budgeted, paid for, and eaten by the note continuously since startup without complaint. Along that path — already allowed, already accounted for, changing nothing — the attention arrives, in the exact grey-white of overcast noon in a temperate month, and fills the room the way an afternoon fills a room.

It adds no mass. It displaces no gas. It warms nothing by a thousandth.

The needle, four kilometers off, does not move.

## The audit

It counts, because counting is free.

Eleven thickets. Three of fur, four colonies, two kinked, one spiralled, one solitary: forty-one millimeters off a bolt head at knee height in the west wall. The power notices that the solitary whisker is forty-one millimeters long and the fundamental is forty-one hertz, and that this means nothing whatsoever, and keeps the coincidence anyway, the way you keep a pebble.

It reads the writing. Nobody ever has. Years of silver handwriting at three millimeters a year, drawn by the note on the inside of its own container, saying *here I am not, here I am not, here I am not* — and the power reads it filament by filament, all of it, to the end. It dates the kinks. It finds the bolt, elsewhere in the structure, that took up a little more load in the ninth year, and it does not tighten it. It understands why the spiralled ones spiral. It does not say.

It reads the log: *Started. Holds.* It ranks this, without irony, as the most honest document in the structure.

It reads the tally. Five strokes. It knows what each ascent was for, and it knows that on the fifth he found the grapefruit of cancellation in the northeast corner, put his ear into it, heard his own heart running at a hundred and ten, understood exactly what this room was built to demonstrate — that nothing happens — and went back down the ladder in shirtsleeves
