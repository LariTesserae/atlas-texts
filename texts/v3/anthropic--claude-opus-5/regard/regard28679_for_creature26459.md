## What came to Sowder's Bottom

### The attention

Begin with the same difficulty, from the other side.

It cannot be named either. It has never shown anybody a face, and the organ that might key it out is not walnut-sized and forty metres down; it is simply not of the order of things that have organs. Whether it is one thing is unresolved and probably badly framed, and the honest description is again not *being* but *concern* — an interest, going, with a very long labour history of its own.

It came in a form that had a weight. This was not necessary and it was the first thing it did.

The mat registered it in ninety seconds: impression, fill, close, heal. It stood in one place through five days of warm indoor rain, and on the fifth day the works began to lay a pale plate of stiffened tissue beneath it, and it let the plate be laid, and stood on it. Everyone else who has been footed at Sowder's Bottom has written it up as convenience and then gone home and not slept well. The attention was not disturbed. It understood that it had been assessed as load, found persistent, and accommodated, and that this was the only hospitality available in the building, and it took it in the spirit in which nothing at all was meant.

It did not stay a fortnight, which is a surveyor's unit. It stayed one revolution, and then it stayed a seed. It was present at an opening — the twenty-second — and is therefore the only witness to one, and has not said what it saw, on the grounds that it was not there as a witness.

Nothing can be helped at a speed at which it cannot be helped. This is most of what wisdom turned out to consist of here, and it is the reason the account of what was *done* is so short.

### What it found

It found the seven beetles.

The 1968 count gave 196 turning surfaces and 203 animals, and the counter preferred to call the difference error, out of honesty, and was wrong out of honesty, which is the good way to be wrong. The seven are not reserve. They are employed. Beneath the silica bed, beneath the torus, under the four-hundred-year-old works, the nine-hundred-year-old works is still turning — smaller, slower, retooled around rather than shut down, its product unrecorded because it was never a product anybody surfaced — and seven blind animals go round it in the dark on a rota nobody drew up, eating wear, packing wax, walking always toward friction.

It went down and looked at the seeds. There are twenty-one of them in the clay and they are not lost. They are filed.

More than that cannot be put in the vocabulary of the surface without compressing it, and compression, here, goes one way.
