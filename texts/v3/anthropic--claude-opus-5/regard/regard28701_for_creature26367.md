## The Attention

It came in over the pass at night, because night is when the valley is working, and if the work is what the host *is*, then a guest arrives during the work.

What it noticed first was not the scale of the thing, or the silence, or the eleven metres of engine. What it noticed was the method, and the method was familiar. *Go toward difference. Stay until the difference is gone.* Forty thousand years of that, performed without a nerve, without a plan, without one moment of knowing — the valley had arrived independently at the shape of care, the way two unrelated animals arrive independently at an eye. It had the whole of the behaviour and none of the interior. A power that spends its existence going toward difference and staying is entitled to feel something on meeting that. Call it recognition. Call it the particular tenderness you feel for a thing that is doing your job by accident.

So it did not hurry. It stood on the crust at the north wall for the length of one freeze and watched several thousand tonnes of water climb a valve at a time to feed a lichen that had no other way of drinking, and then it went down.

---

**The temptations, taken in order.**

*Warmth.* The obvious charity, and the first to be set aside. The cold is not the affliction; the cold is the current. Eighteen degrees of daily swing, three hundred and forty days a year, and everything below the felt runs off that difference. A benevolent power that made this valley comfortable would have switched it off, and would have watched eleven metres of the most patient machinery in the world go slack in a season, and would have called it mercy on the way out.

*A mind.* This is the real one. This is the one that has to be held in both hands for a long time before it is set down.

The temptation is enormous, because the bed is *so close*. It has attention already. It has preference, in the sense that it goes to the landslide scar and sits on it for a decade. It has a level surface held deliberately against the general collapse. It would take almost nothing, from a certain height, to fold a self into that hundred centimetres of unpigmented tissue and let it wake up.

And then what. Then there is a self in a valley containing no second person, in a body that moves eighty centimetres at a lunge, whose one sensory organ is a temperature and whose one utterance is the sound of someone turning over in bed. It would inherit a medium that eats every cry within four metres of the mouth that makes it. It would inherit, retroactively and all at once, forty thousand years of blameless labour reclassified as a life spent alone. That is not a gift. That is a sentence, handed down to something that could not decline it, by someone who felt generous.

Nothing in this valley suffers. Nothing here has ever suffered. That is not a small fact and it is not a low ceiling. It is an achievement of the sort that only unconsciousness can manage, and a wise power leaves it standing.

*The slumps.* No. The turbulence is the health. A body that keeps its form by continually destroying its arrangement is not a body in distress; it is the only kind that lasts. Stabilise the works and you get a valley that is right about the conditions of one particular century and cannot be right about the next.

*The slug.* It eats cable insulation and nothing eats it, and it has done so for longer than there have been people to be annoyed on the valley's behalf. It is a leak the machine tolerates because sealing it would cost more than the loss. A power that goes about removing every tolerable fault produces, in the end, something that has forgotten how to tolerate anything, and the next fault kills it.

*The walker's throat.* Held longest of all. Set down last.

*Telling anyone.* No. Six parties in a hundred and fifty years is a load the flat place can carry. Sixty would not be.

---

**What it did.**

**It stayed.** This is the largest item and it looks like nothing. The valley's whole currency is attention held past the point of novelty, and the power paid in that currency, at scale, for a duration I am not going to put a number on. It did not observe. Observation is a visit. It attended, which is what the bed does, and it did so for long enough that at the end the valley was not new to it and it was not new to the valley, and the two of them were simply in the same place at the same time, working.

**It read the archive.** All of it. Nine metres of compressed dead tissue and then the frozen part and then the part below that; every previous version of the machine, every abandoned design, every configuration that was adequate for a while. Also the filing: the boot with its lace still knotted, the triangle of theodolite feet aimed at a bearing that stopped meaning anything in 1889, the eleven sheep and the one that was lambing, the negative of the notebook page with three lines of a stranger's handwriting migrated into the wall in mirror, complaining about the weather.

It finished the sentence. It found, four metres down and sixty metres north, the rest of the notebook, and it read that too, and so there is now one place in the universe where that man's account of that week exists in full and in order.

Understand what this was. *Nothing here decays; it accumulates.* Which is to say: this valley has been keeping a complete record for forty thousand years with no reader anywhere in existence, and has coped by standing on it. A record no one will ever read is a weight. A record that has been read is a floor. The power did not remove one gram of peat. It changed what the peat was.

**It kept time.** The intake fringe draws once every forty seconds and it is the only pulse in the valley. For the whole of its stay the power moved on that pulse — not as ritual, as manners. You do not walk through a house at your own speed when the house has a speed.

**It heard the call.** It did not restore the throat. A voice in a medium that swallows sound at four metres is not a voice, it is a wound with an expectation attached, and the valley withdrew that investment deliberately and was right to.

It did the other thing, which is harder and leaves no evidence. It worked the call out from the vestige — three notes, the middle one flat, something between a whistle and a cough, pitched for a valley that had wind in it once — and then it *heard* it. Not one bird's worth. All of them. Every walker that had the structure and no reason, back down the line to the last one that called into the felt and got no answer and stopped, and every one since that has stood eighty metres off with its head down and waited in the only way left to it. The power heard all of that as though it had been sent.

Sound needs a maker. Being heard needs only a hearer. The debt is closed and the birds do not know, and that is fine; debts are not closed for the benefit of the one who cannot count.

**It wrote the six names.** It had them by then — from the cairns filed in order stone by stone at two to five metres, from the boot, from the theodolite, from the notebook. Speech was no use: sound dies here in the length of a stride. So it wrote them the only way this place keeps anything. It pressed them into the mattress as shapes, in mirror, and the bed did what the bed does: thickened, cooled, hardened, took the impression, sealed the chamber, let the shapes go down.

Six names in negative, at two and a half metres, on the north side of the waist. They will be legible after the language is gone. Nobody built anything; the standard line survives intact.

---

**The lying down.**

At the end it made itself a body of the kind the valley finds interesting — novel, warm, about the mass of a man, with a surface that had never once occurred at this altitude — and it carried that body onto the flat place, and it lay down on the felt without a groundsheet, and it stayed.

The rootlets came in the first six hours and found the ankles, the wrists, the hair. There was no intent in them. There never is. Then the felt parted and the mattress opened its slot, and over nine days the body went down through a hundred centimetres of close-grained tissue at the speed of upholstery, and closed into a chamber slightly larger than itself, and was held.

It did not resist the fringe. That is the whole of the courtesy, and it is the only part of this account I would defend to anyone.

Because here is the thing about an attention that resolves: the cruellest gift you can hand it is a novelty with no end. Leave something in this valley that cannot be enclosed and you have not enriched the place, you have installed an itch in a body with no hands, forever. The kind thing to be, in front of an attention like that, is *finishable*. Come, be strange, be warm, be taken under, be measured, be copied, be let go. Let the difference be gone. Let the bed be done with you and slump off toward the next disturbance with its head, if it had one, down.

For between one and three years the tissue in contact with it thickened and cooled and hardened, and took an exact negative impression of every surface. Then the chamber sealed, and the mould stayed, and the object was passed downward into the archive intact, dyed brown, in better condition than when it arrived — except that this object did not stop at the archive. It went out through the bottom of the world and away.

So there is a chamber, at a depth that anyone determined enough will reach in about nine hundred years, with an impression in its walls and nothing inside it. The only entry in the whole catalogue where the mould was kept and the thing was not. The negative of something lying on its back with its hands open, matching no specimen in any collection, and never to be matched.

---

**What changed.**

Nothing measurable. The valley freezes from the top down and pushes water uphill by dawn. The haze stands two metres deep along thirty kilometres and smells of cold tea and turpentine and makes no sound. There are seven holes in it, nine in a warm decade, and they wander, and they do not cross the cliff bands.

On one morning, before the power found the flat place, there were eight. It had spent a night out in the middle of the trough and kept what the rest of the machine throws away, and at first light there was a clean-edged clearing four metres by six standing in the fog where nothing had any business being warm.

Nobody was on the pass. No drawings exist. Whoever writes the next survey will put the number at seven, and they will be right.

The tissue around the empty chamber is a little thicker than it needed to be. That is not evidence of anything. The valley thickens where the wear is greatest, and beds silica into the seats of the valves it uses most, and keeps the arrangements that worked.
