## The Thirteenth

Begin with the arithmetic, because the arithmetic is what broke first.

Eleven kinds of living thing, exact, the count and the population being one fact. Then eleven kinds and a twelfth walking, which the continent had tolerated for a long time without ever quite conceding. Then a thirteenth, and no one on the whole plain equipped to notice it except her, and she did not notice it, because what she noticed was the shadow.

There are no shadows in the Same. The light is an even silver with nothing in it to cast from; a hand held up over water throws no hand. She had lived her entire life inside a light with no opinion about direction. So when she saw, lying on the warm skin beside her, a long grey shape that did not stipple with the rain and did not slide when the rain came harder, she stopped walking — against the rule, the first rule, the one she had never once broken — and stood in the middle of four hundred thousand active shear-lines and looked at it.

A shadow is an argument. It says: there is a somewhere that the light is coming from, and there is something in between, and the something is not you.

---

It had been there for a long while by then. It had arrived and done nothing, which is the part that looks from outside like negligence and is in fact the whole of the wisdom.

It walked behind her for the better part of a year and learned the place. It learned that erosion here is not a process but a completed act, that the rock had been asked so patiently for so long to stop being a shape that it had finally, reasonably, agreed. It learned the difference between a thing that is suffering and a thing that is merely strange. It counted laminae in a stromatolite and got a number the dome had no room to hold, and understood that history here does not resolve, and sat with that for a season without deciding it was a problem.

It considered, in order, everything a power does when it first arrives somewhere and its hands are still full of gifts.

It could have torn the lid. Four hundred metres up, that closed grey engine turning ten million times a day with nothing to do; one gesture and there would be a sun-disc, an edge on the light, a direction, shadows everywhere and all of them meaning something. It ran the consequence out. The heat leaves. The water thins. The algal film — one organism, four million square kilometres, no edge that has ever met anything but itself — cooks or freezes, and it would be the only death that had ever happened here, and it would be total.

It could have dried the plain, and made banks, and given the water back the knowledge of which way to go.

It could have unpacked the superposition. This was the temptation with the best manners on it, because it looked like restitution: nine hundred million locations each permitted at last to be only itself, the reeds resolving to a single reed-stand a thousand kilometres off, the roar falling to one bird's voice at one bird's distance, and then, in the silence, the ordinary quiet of a wet flat country in the rain.

It followed that one all the way down and found the eel at the bottom of it.

The eel is one eel. It has no eyes and no reason to have ever suspected anything. It carries a single healed notch in its dorsal, put there by somebody a long time ago, and it has been carrying that one wound for longer than the domes can be made to say, and it does not appear to mind. Of everything on the continent, the eel is the only one that has got away with it.

To unpack the Same is to tell the eel.

It decided not to be the thing that told the eel. That decision took eleven days and it never revisited it.

---

Then there was her, and her the power got wrong twice before it got her right.

Its first thought was the obvious one: give her back the millimetre. Unmake the resolution. Find the shear-line, reach into that old grinding, separate the two figures who raised their hands in unison and were thereby confirmed identical, and let there be two women again, one of whom had a temper and a particular way of walking and a scar under the jaw.

But she is not two people in need of separating. She is one person who was constructed out of the answer to a question. There is nothing in her to divide; the sister is not stored anywhere but in the things she says on certain platforms in certain lights, and those sayings have been sliding through one another for decades, and she does not experience this as confusion. She experiences it as having been everywhere when it happened, which she was.

And to raise up an other from a memory is to make a copy. Copies are the one lethal object in this world. It would have been building her a mirror and calling it a sister, and the plain would have come for both of them within the hour.

Which is where the power found the rule it needed, the only law of charity that holds in the Same:

*You cannot be gentle by halves here.*

A small kindness is a small difference. A small difference is a contradiction the plain can neither perceive nor tolerate, and it resolves contradictions by shearing, by grinding two nearly-identical things past each other until the water goes wrong and something's knee holds two positions forever. Anything given here must be either enormous or nothing. There is no middle. The near-identical is destroyed; only the utterly unlike is safe.

That is why the power was safe. It differed from her by everything. There was no millimetre anywhere between them for the continent to worry at.
