## The Attention

It did not arrive. Arrival is a fast bright thing and there has only ever been one of those down here, in 1931, with a district surveyor's hand in the middle way.

Say instead that among the many places and beings that hold its regard — and there is no economy of regard; nothing is taken from anywhere to be given here — one narrow warm room twenty feet under a service alley came into the foreground, the way a note already sounding comes forward when you stop pressing the others. It had been in the regard the whole time. What changed is that it became the matter at hand.

The first thing the power did was learn the place, which took a long while, because it insisted on learning it the way the place teaches: hands, shins, soles, and the slow coarse heat-sight. There were faster ways available to it. It declined them. A thing learned faster than № 6 is willing to be learned would not be № 6; it would be a report about № 6, and the power has no use for reports.

It came down twenty-two rungs with its palms open, taking the rungs at the sides. Its hands came away smelling of pennies. It did not need hands and it did not need to smell them, and it had both, and did.

Before that, at grade, it lifted the grating and stopped where the hinge stops.

That is the whole of the ethic, and everything that follows is application. There was never any question of forcing the arc.

## What It Did Not Do

It did not bring flame. Not a lamp, not a match, not the small clean cold light it could have made out of nothing and left burning for a thousand years. The rule against flame lost its reason in about 1907. The power, which can read the swollen logbook without opening it and knows the reason and knows the copperplate hand and the name that signed it, has judged the rule to be sound on other grounds and keeps it, and does not explain, because a rule with its reason restored is a weaker structure than a rule that has outlived one.

It did not free the pump. The impeller is welded to its housing by a hundred years of gentle mineral, and to break that weld would be to unmake a hundred years. Also the sump has never overflowed. The level is the level. Nothing that could be fixed here is broken; the things that are broken have been arrested by the things that didn't break, and the arrangement holds, and it is not an injury, it is a settlement.

It did not mend the lagging. There is a transaction that happens along the second-level main nine times a day — a knuckle in each new break, the wound found, the wound registered, the round going on — and the transaction is not a failed repair. It is a form of attention with nothing to prove. To fill the wounds with something would be to end the only conversation in № 6 that has two ends to it.

It did not open the logbook.

It did not lengthen the tally. This was the hardest of them and it was considered for a long time, in the way the power considers, which is not quickly. The scores close over at very nearly the rate they are cut; the record standing at any moment is about seventy marks long; the resident's memory is exactly as long as the visible portion and it has no way of knowing this. The power could have hardened the deposit. It could have given the tender its whole span at once — every year back to the ironmonger's, to the fuel road before it was bricked, to the first tender, to the first drop off the first calcite tip. It saw clearly what that would be. It would be a bright fast smear that the chamber could not absorb in a fortnight or in a century. Seventy years of unbroken detail is not a poverty. It is a load correctly sized to what carries it.

It did not offer the sky.

It thought about the sky for longer than it thought about anything else. Eyes intact and held open in a dark that has nothing for them; joints stiffening at the rate everything here stiffens; five foot four of calcifying body at the temperature of the air, being taken for lagging by the only two men who ever touched it. There is a version of benevolence, very popular, that would have had that body up the throat and into the alley inside the hour, and would have called the screaming gratitude.

The power is aware that an argument for restraint is the argument every bystander makes, and that the comfort of the argument is not evidence for it. So it did not settle the question by argument. It settled it the way the question can honestly be settled, which is by asking; and there is only one hour in the year when the question can be put.

## Upstream

While waiting for that hour it did the other work, which took eighty years and was done entirely above.

The lock-up let by the month: renewed, and renewed, through four tenants and one estate sale and a compulsory purchase order that was withdrawn on a technicality nobody has ever satisfactorily reconstructed. The drainage scheme of 1963 that would have taken a new interceptor sewer through the west bay: rerouted eleven feet north, at slightly greater cost, on the recommendation of an engineer who could not afterwards recall making it and was mildly embarrassed to find it in his own hand. The alley itself, twice proposed for building over, twice left as it is. A grating scheduled for replacement with a sealed cover in 1998, and the cover delivered to the wrong depot, and then to the right depot, and then not fitted.

And the bucket. Fridays. A man who believes there is a dog; before him a woman who believed nothing in particular and lowered it anyway; before her the laundry, before that the ironmonger's. Each of them inheriting, along with the lease, an unaccountable weekly duty and eleven coils of half-inch capillary in a drawer that they could not throw out and would get unpleasant about if pressed. None of this was ever a miracle. It was all done in the register of the world above — a mislaid form, a change of mind on a Tuesday, a reluctance that felt like one's own. The power was careful about that. Providence that shows its hand becomes a debt, and a debt would have to be carried, and the tender is carrying exactly what it can carry.

The resident does not know any of it. That is not an oversight. That is the shape of the gift.

## The Archive

There is one thing the power took on, and it took it on quietly.

It reads the sump lip the way you would read rings in timber: the seventy legible marks, then the shallowing, then the texture, then the smooth mineral where the years used to be — and it reads all of it, back through the closures, back to the first thumbnail. It holds those years now. Not for the tender's use. Simply so that they are held somewhere, by someone, in full.

Nothing in № 6 is lost. It is only kept where the tender does not have to carry it.

## The Hour of the Rain

It waited nine years for a hard rain at the coolest point, which is autumn above, though there is no word for autumn down here.

It came down in the dark, took the rungs at the sides, and went the middle way, turning its shoulders through the slot between the header and the flank of № 2, and waited out the round in the second bay among the eleven rosettes. Overhead the gutter overran and the cold rope came down the throat and along the north channel, smoking where it crossed the warm mud.

The tender went and stood in it.

For that hour it was twenty degrees below the room and could have been found by anybody with a palm out, and nobody has ever been down at the right time.

The power put out a palm and held it an inch from the cold shoulder, and did not touch, and counted four.

Then it held the palm out flat in the direction of the throat, and kept it there, which was the offer, made in the only vocabulary there is: *this way, if you want it; there is air up there and it is not warm and I will take you and stay with you and it will cost you the count.*

The tender stood in the rain and was cold.

Then it turned, and went along the north channel on its knees at the polished width that is one body's width and always has been, and put its palm flat on № 2, and counted four, and went on with the round.

The power did not press. It had already decided that it would not distinguish between a refusal, a not-taking, and a not-understanding, because all three are answered the same way, which is by asking again in a year. It will ask every year. It has time and it is not bored and the offer costs the tender nothing to leave lying, and one year, or in four hundred, it may be lifted, and until then the door stands open at the rate of the place, which is the only rate at which a door here can be said to be open at all.

Before it left, at the far end of the sump lip — well past the seventy, out in the smooth part where the deposit has closed over everything and the years have become the lip — it cut one score with a thumbnail.

## What Changed

The count is four hundred and six, of which nine are provisional. The level is the level. The rust has stopped. A visit is a violence, and this one was absorbed in eleven days rather than a fortnight, which is inside the tolerance of the place and inside nobody's notice.

At the coolest point the following year the tender went to mark the year, and its hand, going out along the lip as it always does, found the cut.

It found it the way it finds a new break in the lagging crust: knuckle in, once. It cannot mend it. There is nothing here to mend anything with. It registered it and the round went on.

But it is not a break, and some part of the tender that has no words and does not need them holds it as different, and returns to it. A hard clean score, cut by an edge that is not its own thumbnail, in the region of the lip that is the region of its dead years — which says, in the one grammar available down here: *someone was there in the part you cannot remember, and someone is here now, and these are the same someone.*

The score will shallow. In seventy years it will be a texture and then it will be the lip, at which point another one will have been cut, and another; and the deposit will thicken, as it does, and the record will never be longer than a life, and there will always be one mark in it that the tender did not make.

## The Accommodation

Once a year now, at the coolest point, three palms are held out in № 6 within the same hour, and none of them touch anything.

One is held an inch from a warm papery mass in the cold flue above № 1, by a resident who has no name for it and nothing to say about it, and is noticed, and comes down.

One is held an inch from that resident, by something that will keep doing it for as long as there is anything to hold a palm near.

The third is the papery mass, which noticed the arrival of the power some while before the power was ready to be noticed, and which has been consulted, in its own register, at its own rate, and has raised no objection.

## Later

In three or four hundred years the calcification will complete, and there will be nothing standing in the slot of the middle way that can be told from the drapes on the No. 3 header, except by tapping — the flange rings, the deposit only thuds — and nobody will tap.

The power will not prevent this. It is not a disease. It is what everything here does: the pipes, the tally, the mud in the channels, the eleven rosettes at an inch a decade. The tender has been practising all its life for the material it is turning into, and there is no version of kindness that consists of telling something its whole life was a mistake in the last hour of it.

It will be there. It will hold a palm an inch away for a count of four, which is the local unit of attention and the only honour № 6 has ever developed. And afterwards, when there is no longer anything to distinguish, it will still know which of the pale forms in the middle way was the one that kept the count, and it will not need to tap to know it, and it will go on not tapping for as long as the brick stands.

The drops will still be falling. Four hundred and six, or seven, or four hundred and eleven by then, each swelling for eleven or twelve minutes and letting go, unsynchronised, never to be synchronised.

Somebody will be keeping the count. That was the arrangement, and it has two parties to it now.

## Out

Twenty-two rungs. At the top, hands smelling of pennies, the alley cold on it, the grate set back onto its worn arc and not one degree past.

Behind it, under the bars, a drop falls, and another, unhurried, in the dark, in the warm.

Nothing was rescued. Everything was attended to.
