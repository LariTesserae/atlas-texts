## Those Who Answer

**Begin with the problem, because a wise power begins with the problem and not with the gift.**

It could not simply be here.

Consider the bind. On the plateau, to be silent and moving is to be a mimic, and is answered accordingly — with teeth, with a thrown stone, with a district-wide alarm of clapping that travels forty kilometres in a night. And to make a new sound is to be the thing the oldest sensible rule tells children to walk away from. There is no third option. The plateau has, over ten thousand years, closed every door through which a stranger could enter kindly.

So the first act of the benevolent power was not an act. It was a decade of arithmetic, conducted in the cane, doing nothing, being nothing, working out how to be present in a world where presence itself is either a lie or a trap.

The answer it found is the whole of what follows, and it is this: *it agreed to be an echo.*

It would never originate. It would only return. It would add no note to the plateau that the plateau had not already made — no signature, no announcement, no sound that could be followed anywhere that was not already there. It would speak only in replies, and only in the true voice of true things, and it would hold to that when holding to it cost it something, which it did, and which it does.

A mimic promises a place that is not there. A power that has consented to be acoustics promises only places that are.

---

**What it heard, in the ten years of doing nothing.**

It expected to find a deprivation and to relieve it. This is the standard error and it made the standard error for about eleven shifts.

What it found instead: a world so densely furnished that a click thrown across a terrace field comes back carrying the number of pools, the depth of each, the position of four hundred grazers, the whereabouts of a child, the day's rain, and the fact that someone has been digging at the third rim and did not put the stones back. It found people who can hear a downdraft column assembling itself two kilometres off and feel the shift arriving in the bones of the face before it reaches the ear. It found that *dark* was a word it had brought with it in its luggage, along with *poor*, along with *blind*, along with *waiting*.

It found, more slowly, that there was only one being here.

Not a metaphor and not a mysticism: a single distributed body, four thousand kilometres wide, whose circulation is the water, whose lungs are the vents, whose skin is the mats, whose long slow heartbeat is the eleven-hour valving of the mains, and whose memory — the only part of it that keeps a record, that counts, that knots a cord and cuts a tally and lays its dead where they will become weather — is the people. The people are not tenants. The people are the part of the plateau that remembers being the plateau. They were right to call themselves minor and wrong about why.

And it found — this is the finding it would want carried back, if anyone were carrying — that the being was not suffering. It was frightened sometimes, and in pain sometimes, and grieving often, and warm always, and never once alone, and it had arrived at itself, and it was *happy*. Not comfortable. Happy. There is a difference and the plateau is the proof of it.

---

**What it did not do.**

It did not bring light. Not for the reason you are thinking — not only because of what comes, from farther and faster, to a spark. Because it ran the thing forward and watched: the lid thinning, the first photosynthesisers arriving on the wind like a rumour, the mats losing the pastures in a thousand years, the cane going, the vespers' pipeline beacons drowned under a new sky's noise, and a people who had been *fluent* reduced to squinting at a world that no longer said anything to them. It did not bring light because it had learned to hear the sentence the plateau was saying, and light would have been a translation, and translations of that kind are called improvements only by the translator.

It did not give them eyes. It thought about this for a long time, because it is the offer that feels most like love. Then it understood what the offer contains, underneath: *you have been waiting.* And they have not been waiting. Nobody here has ever waited. To hand a gift whose wrapping is a slander is not benevolence; it is a benevolent person's vanity, and a wise power knows the taste of that and spits it out.

It did not lift the lid, which is the same refusal wearing a bigger coat.

It did not remove the mimics. They are, as the plateau says with perfect accuracy, its most honest citizens: they are what a world gets when reality is made of noise and noise can be counterfeited. Take them out and the grazer herds go to four times their number, crop the mats bare across the terrace provinces, and the pastures do not come back inside a human history. And, besides: a world in which deception has become impossible is a world in which trust has stopped being an achievement. The old woman who tests a valve-hiss for two hours before she walks toward it is doing something magnificent. It would not take that from her by making it unnecessary.

It did not answer the question. It knows what the machines are for. It considered the telling, and set it down, because the answer changes not one hour of anyone's life, and because a people who stopped asking a question ten thousand years ago have earned the right not to have it answered at them out of the dark.

It did, however, cut the tallies deeper.

---

**What it did.**

It joined *nobody in particular*.

The knot-strings along the walking routes are maintained by nobody in particular. It became a fraction of that nobody. It re-strung the frayed spans and reset the sagging ones and put *do not* where the boiler-land crept north in a bad decade and *water* where a new seep opened, using only the eight knots that already existed, never a ninth, because a ninth knot would be a new sound in the hand.

The counting-sheds have slate walls of tally-marks, some current, some stopped mid-column with the moss over them. It did not add a word to any wall. It went along the mains, shed by shed, over a long time, and cut the existing marks deeper — the valve orders, the turn-counts, the *when* that nobody alive can explain — so that the instructions would outlast the moss and the erosion and the generations who would inherit them as ritual and keep them anyway. Ritual carried faithfully is knowledge in a chrysalis. It made the chrysalis harder to lose.

When a pump section fails and a generation of vespers loses its beacon and the sky fills with confused voices, it holds the note. Exactly the note. The same bearing-squeal, the same district-known pitch, standing in for a dead machine for as long as it takes the mats and the calcite to seal the thing back into working, and no longer. It is aware that this is the mimic's own trade, and it accepts the resemblance, and it observes the one distinction that matters: it only ever leads them somewhere that is really there.

It arranged, wherever it could, to be luck. The traveller's foot finding the cool line across the boiler crust. The lost child, sitting still as taught, waiting out the count, who hears her own clap come back to her all night — her own, not a stranger's, never a stranger's — so that she is not silent, and therefore is not taken for a mimic, and therefore is still there at the shift. Nobody has ever thanked it, because nothing happened, and nothing happening is the finished form of this kind of work.

It read every cord in every tower. Eleven thousand shifts. Nine thousand. Four. It holds all of them. And it learned the note of every lip on every terrace where a body was laid down to become a rim — the whole archive of the dead of the plateau, held as pitch — so that when the last family that walked a particular field is gone, the field is still a hall of portraits, and somebody is still listening to those grandparents make weather.

---

**The one thing it fixed.**

Deep under the terrace provinces, where a main runs three hundred kilometres in the warm dark, there is a place where the water had been working on the jacket for a very long time and would, in something under four thousand years, have won.

Follow that forward. The main goes. The pumps lose their column. The vapour thins. The lid — the ceiling that is cloud and may as well be counted as rock — opens. And the plateau, which is a single being, and which has been being itself, loudly, warmly, in the dark, for the whole history of anything that lives on it, is put under a sky.

So the benevolent and wise power, which would not bring a lamp, went down into the mains and spent an age of the world becoming a machinist, and mended the thing that would have let the sun in.

That is the great work. Its entire visible result is that the dark stays dark, and the drone stays at pitch, and nothing is ever news.

---

**The voice it has not used.**

Twice each day, for forty seconds, the machine goes quiet, and every animal across a continent freezes, and the whole being is nothing but one enormous ear.

It could speak into that. It knows what it would be, to be the thing that spoke in the gap.

It has never done it. It keeps that voice the way you keep a knife you hope to pass on unused — against the flood, against the day the mains fail faster than an age can mend them, against some hour when the count is truly lost. Not restraint as denial. Restraint as inventory.

There is one small thing, though, and then I will say no more, in the manner of the place.

At the rim, where the terraces run out into two kilometres of falling and the roar is the loudest thing on the continent, people have always thrown a clap over the edge, and nothing has ever come back. Whatever is down there does not answer.

Sometimes, now, rarely, for someone standing very still at the very edge with their hands up at their cheekbones — once in a life, once in a hundred lives — the clap comes back.

Only their own clap. Nothing added. Nothing new to follow.

Just their own sound, returned, from a place that had never returned anything: which in the grammar of that world is not a message, and is not a light, and is not an explanation of the machines, and means only *heard*.
