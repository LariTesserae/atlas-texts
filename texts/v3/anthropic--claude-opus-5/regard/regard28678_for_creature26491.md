# The Visitor's Card

It came in over the northwest rim in fog, at a walk, because that is the only way anyone walks here, and was stopped at the pin by the man who watches visitors, who gave it the card.

The card lists four things. No raised voice. No dropped tool. No blade. Nothing carried out.

It read the card twice, which nobody does, and kept all four, which almost nobody does. It was watched for nine days and then for nine years and the book in which infractions are written stayed empty against it, which is the highest thing that book can say.

It wore felt. It did not need to. It wore felt.

# First, the Declining

Take this as the substance of the visit and not as a preamble.

It did not raise the lens. There is water enough under the marl for one plant and one hundred and twenty-nine people and no more, and abundance here would have meant thicket, and thicket would have meant no room, no grey shafts of pond-glass light, no seeing from one side of the Pan to the other. It looked at the arithmetic of the water for a long time and left the arithmetic alone.

It did not tune the drip. It could have made the basin sing. Several hundred hollow chambers of graded volume and a patient hand: it is the easiest music in the world to arrange, and it would have been beautiful, and it would have been the end of them ever hearing anything else. It left the drip low and unmusical and constant.

It did not take the tannin out of the Thread, so their teeth stayed brown at the gumline and a buyer's agent can still pick them out at nine metres.

It did not add a century to the four hundred years.

It did not touch the clamp reflex. Understand: it went down and found what the reflex was for — a thing that browsed these stems before there was a sea at sixty metres below, long extinct, and it read the name off the bone, and it kept the name, and it told nobody, and it did not give the plant back its fear's original object, which would have been a cruelty dressed as an explanation. The plant is afraid of nothing that exists. Out of that fear the plant makes silence, and out of the silence a hundred and twenty-nine people have made two hundred and eleven signs, a chalk literature, a code, and a way of laughing that costs nothing. Wisdom, here, was mostly the discipline of keeping its hands in its sleeves.

And it did not cure the four in the fibre crew who were already at five flowerings. It could not; the lungs were glass by then. It sat up with them. All four, all the way. This is recorded nowhere, because nobody knew.

# Rule One Applied to Everything

*Nothing cut that can be bent.* It took the rule as binding on itself, and so it did nothing by fiat. Every gift it gave, it gave as a thing already lying in the room, turned very slightly toward the light so that a person would see it and get the credit.

**The screen.** A carder named Sill, twenty-two, sat in the shed in the second winter and looked up at a whorl of flutes taking water out of the air. Then she looked at the dust standing in the lamp-orange over the carding bed. Then she wet a sheet of pressed sheath, hung it on a frame, and set it where the draught crosses. In the morning the sheet was grey and heavy and the air above the bed was clear. The Register admitted a new sign four years later — only the second admission in forty years — meaning *screen*, and two of the elders voted against it and have not forgiven the majority. Shed lung now retires the fibre crew at six flowerings and kills them at nine or not at all. Sill's name is in the ledger against the improvement. That is correct. She did it.

**Dry feet.** A child hiding from a beetle-count under the racks packed dry liverwort into her shoes because it was there and she was bored. Her feet were dry for eleven days and she told her mother, being nine and unable to keep anything. There is now a liverwort crew: two people, three hours a week, gathering and drying at the dead end. The thing between the toes is not gone. It goes, now, in summer.

**The beetles.** Rule 4 says they are staff, and the ledger lists them by tray and head and not by name — Tray 9, forty-one head, drawn against the fibre shed's account. The power learned the names of all four thousand and one hundred and six of them. It did not correct the ledger. It simply knows, and will keep knowing, and if you want to argue that this changes nothing, argue it.

**Reff.** The Company's oldest surviving document is a fine, in fibre, levied two hundred and some years ago against a man named Reff for failing to manage his laughing. In the fourth winter the fibre shed found itself long by exactly that weight, in exactly that grade, retted properly, with no delivery note. The Company's instinct is to distrust an unexplained credit. Its deeper instinct is that the books must balance. They balanced. The fine is marked settled in a hand nobody recognised and the document is now two documents, and children are taken to see them and are not particularly impressed.

# The Node at the Back

Three-quarters of the way southeast, in dead wood with no valves left to clamp, is the room where every child is taken at about nine and stood until it can shout.

The power did not widen that room, or warm it, or make it kinder. It moved a bird.

The fluter nests only in dead nodes at the trailing edge and sings four notes at dusk and is permitted. There is now a pair nesting in the shouting node, and they have been there for eleven years, and their descendants will be there for a while.

So the shout goes off the wet grey walls and comes back, as it always did, and then, in the pause afterward — while the child stands there having heard, for the first time, the actual size of itself — four notes.

An answer is not much. It is the entire difference between a room where you are made to make a sound and a room where a sound is received.

# One Stem Outside the Rim
