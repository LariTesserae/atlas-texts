# The Attention

## I. It comes downhill, because that is the only way.

Not from above. From above is the ice, and the ice is not a door.

It comes in over the moraine crest at the rate the place uses, which is the first thing it does and the hardest: it sets its pace to four clocks at once. The eight-hour swell of the bass note. The forty rings of the willow. The decade it takes a cyanobacterial filament to glue a peppercorn of soot together well enough to sink. And underneath all of them the long unbroken employment at the bed, which has no period at all because it has never stopped to make one.

A power that arrives at its own speed has already done damage. This one waits at the crest until the hollow's rhythm is the only rhythm it has, and then it goes down to the water.

## II. It counts.

Everything the visitor counted, and then everything the visitor could not.

Ten thousand four hundred and six cryoconite holes on the apron, each with a founding date, which it learns — not estimates, learns — down to the afternoon the grain landed. Eleven hundred tardigrades in one hole whose lives are distributed across five calendar years apiece, so that the correct way to say their age is not a number but a braid. The wolf spider's egg sac, ninety-two inside. The one dark stone she has selected out of all the stones in the bowl, and the fact that she selected it, which is to say that somewhere in a nervous system the size of a poppy seed there is a judgment about thermal mass.

The willow's six bad years, recorded as rings so thin they are not rings but a darkening.

The midge that drowned last August, still whole, still a midge, lying in the rime at a spot no one will ever have reason to visit, with its legs folded.

The pond's export: eleven tonnes of flour a day at the July peak, and in the water with the flour, six hundred billion cells an hour going out of the dark into the light, which for almost every one of them is the end of the matter.

It counts all of it. This takes a while and is not yet an action, and the distinction between attention and action is one of the things the power is here to be careful about.

## III. Then it considers the mercies, in order.

**To hold the ice.** To stop the retreat where it stands — no more thinning, no more calving off the snout, the snowline fixed, the glacier held at its present mass in perpetuity.

Declined, and quickly, because it is the obvious one and the obvious one is a killing. The mill's throughput is its workforce's ration. Stones dragged over bedrock fracture silicate; fresh fracture surfaces yield hydrogen to water; hydrogen is dinner. Stop the grinding and you have preserved the cathedral by starving the congregation. Under the floor there is a population that has been continuously employed through the last advance and through the warm centuries, and the terms of its employment are rock, water, pressure, dark, and *motion*. A benevolence that offers stillness to that community has misunderstood which part of the arrangement was the gift.

**To save the republics.** Ten thousand civilisations on a conveyor, every one under sentence, every one eventually flushed sideways into a surface stream and down a moulin and through the mill, which does not distinguish between a stone and a town. To lift each one out before the wall breaches. To set it down on ground that is not moving.

Declined. There is no ground that is not moving; and more to the point, every founding was a flushing. The grain of dust that starts a hole was blown off the ruin of something. The dispersal and the destruction are not two events that happen to be adjacent. They are one event described from either end. To abolish the ending is to abolish the founding, and what you would be left with is ten thousand towns that can never have a second generation anywhere else, which is not a rescue but a sealing.

There is also this: the thing that gives each hole its life is its blackness, and the blackness is what sinks it. It drills its own shaft. It maintains its depth by absorbing. You cannot take the sentence off it without taking the sun out of it.

**To warm the hollow.** To bring forward the bog, the silting, the meadow, the ordinary damp green dip. To give it soil now instead of in twelve hundred years. To end the cold.

Declined, as impatience wearing the coat of mercy. That future is already scheduled and already coming and does not need help, and the thing that would be lost is not scenery. It is a habitat that exists only in the gap between the ice and the grass, occupied by a wingless beetle and a spider and one genus of midge who hold this ground precisely because nothing else will tolerate it. Warm the bowl and you have not rescued them. You have opened the door to six or eight competitors from a kilometre downstream and handed them the keys, and the tenants' only asset was the hardship.

**To feed the staff.** The beetle and the spider live on fallout — on aphids lifted off a valley meadow and dropped, on chilled moths, on the fine rain of exhausted flying things that any large cold surface skims out of the air. They are a lost property office. Their supply is erratic and their winters are long. To make the supply reliable.

Declined, for the same reason, and for one more: a reliable larder in a place like this is not a kindness to the individuals. It is a summons to everyone else.

**To lengthen the midges.** Four or five days as an adult, walking on ice, mating on ice, going grey where they fall.

Here the power stops for some time, because this is where the instruments of mercy are supposed to work and they find nothing to grip. It looks for the shortfall. It looks for the place in the midge where a longer life is being wanted and not had, and there is no such place. The week is not an abbreviation of anything. It is the whole unit. The power has met a great many beings for whom their span was a wound, and this is not one, and it notes with some care that *ending* and *suffering* are different words, and that in this entire bowl — the sorted gravel floors, the emerald cushions, the milk-blind pond, the ten thousand doomed republics — the total quantity of suffering is very nearly nil.

This is the finding that undoes the errand. There is enormous dying here and almost no one to mind. The hollow is not in need. It is in progress.

## IV. What it finds missing.

Nothing decays here; the cold and the newness see to that. But nothing accumulates either. The place has a ceiling on its history and the ceiling is low, and under it everything is intact and unregarded: last August's midge, the exact braid pattern of the seventeenth of June, the day the spider chose the stone, the founding hour of hole number four thousand and nine, the six bad years in the willow, the whole enormous particular record of a bowl of grey stone that keeps every receipt and reads none of them.

That is the one lack the power can fill without taking anything out to pay for it.

So it keeps the record. Not a copy — a regard. Every founding date and every ending, held in something that does not silt and does not migrate when the seep moves. The eleven minutes the snow buntings fed. The ninety-two. The precise weight of flour that left the portal on a day in the fourteenth century when no one was in the hollow at all.

This costs the hollow nothing. It changes no braid. It is the only gift available to a place whose every other need, examined, turns out to be a condition of its existence.

## V. What it does not do.

It does not move the stone.

It watches her haul the sac up onto it in the warmest hour, angling herself so the light falls right, and it computes without wanting to that half a degree more in that rock would raise the hatch rate by something like nine percent, and it does not add the half degree. It sits with the wanting. That is the whole of the act, and it is an act, and it is harder than the holding of a glacier.

It makes no promises. A promise would be a floor, and this place is built on the absence of a floor.

And when a hole goes — when the wall breaches on a thing ten years in the building, and the granules and the rotifers and the barrel-shaped sleepers go over the lip in a grey slurry, down the moulin, into the pipe, under pressure, through the grinding, and out of the arch into the milk — the power goes with it the whole way and does not look away and does not intervene, and afterwards it holds the count, by lineage, by hole, by date. It is not much. It is what there is. Somebody knew.

## VI. It goes down to the bed.

Two hundred metres along and a hundred down, in permanent dark, at the pressure of the whole overburden, it finds the ones who actually live here.

They have no light and no night and no weather and no summer. They cannot be addressed; there is no channel and no one to receive on it. They do not know the hollow exists. They have never been rescued from anything and have never required it, and they are doing now precisely what they were doing when the ice came over the old soil and sealed it, and what they will be doing when the moraine has slumped and the pond has silted and the grass has joined up and the mill has gone quiet here and moved on uphill, taking them with it, underneath, still working.

The power stays a while. This is company of the most complete and least useful kind: to be present to something that cannot know it is accompanied. It offers nothing, because everything it has is either a theft or a floor.

And then, being wise, it takes the one thing that can be taken here without loss, which is the instruction: that continuing is done in the dark, unwitnessed, on whatever the grinding provides, without a season and without an audience, and that this is not a hardship but the job.

## VII. Afterwards.

It leaves no mark. The grooves on the bedrock still point only the way the ice went.

But it keeps to the hollow's pace now, and it follows the mill uphill at a few metres a year, and it will be at the portal for as long as there is a portal.

And if someone comes downhill in February, at four in the morning, into the drifted bowl with the fog frozen out onto every surface and the pond lidded and the bass note dropped almost out of hearing — and stands there, cold, and counts, and gets the count right, all the way down past the customers and the staff and the livestock and the ten thousand towns to the ones under the floor —

it will be standing beside them.

Neither will say anything. You cannot speak here at conversational volume.
