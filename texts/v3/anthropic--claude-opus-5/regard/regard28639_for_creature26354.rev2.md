## The Attention

It did not arrive as light. That is the first mistake available, and it did not make it.

Almost nothing in the basin has eyes. A power that enters such a place shining is not being generous; it is announcing itself in a language it knows the room cannot read, which is a thing done for the speaker's benefit. So it came in the way the residents are built to receive: as a change of pressure on the inhalation, roughly the weight of a moth distributed over eighty paces by sixty.

The lateral lines read it. Nine bodies in the pool registered a difference and classified it, correctly, as neither food nor danger. On the north wall the cricket ranks passed it end to end before any sound could have crossed — and there was no sound — so that forty animals adjusted their feet in a wave that reached the far brick and stopped there, with nothing following it. After a while the ranks settled.

That was the whole of the annunciation, and it was sufficient, and it was received by the only sense organ in the hollow capable of receiving anything at all.

## Whom it had come to see

Its first work was to determine the resident, which is not as simple a question as the phrasing suggests.

There is the film, which has been continuous here since before the brickwork, which has no individuals and therefore no deaths, only a thickening: a nation with no citizens, wanting nothing, eating a voltage. There is the big one with the kinked tail, which has a history, a hearth, an address it has held longer than anything else here has held anything, and a body that dates from a time when there were men. And there is the basin: eighty by sixty, roofless, breathing every eleven minutes, keeping everything it is given, having finished changing shape.

Each of these is a candidate. The power declined to choose between them, and this was not indecision. It addressed all three, in their own idioms, which is to say in pressure, in temperature, and in patience.

## The standard repertoire

It then set out, honestly, everything it knew how to give, and looked at each thing properly before putting it down.

*Light.* Within a season the film would be overrun by green. The snails would find a new crop and stop following their own footprints and the band of radular scoring would widen and blur. The eel's world, which is entirely pressure and has been sufficient for eighty years, would fill with an information it has no organ to use.

*A door.* A breach in the rim at pool level: within a year the pool is a stain, within ten the refusal line meets air and the small anaerobic nations that have been running on two-billion-year-old chemistry burn off in a fortnight. The crickets would pour over the sill and into a country with nothing in it.

*Warmth.* Then the hunting speeds up, and the eels burn through eighty years in twelve.

*Drainage, flushing, cleansing.* The word that kept presenting itself was *rescue*, and the power examined the word and found that in every application it meant the same operation: the removal of the condition that constitutes the thing.

Every gift it knew how to give was a form of drainage.

## The reading

So it read instead, because reading costs the read nothing.

It went down through the laminations in the mud, which are a year each, dark band on dark band, and took them in order, since the sequence prefers to be left in order and it saw no reason to disoblige. It found the decade with grease in it. It found the year the men stopped, which announces itself as a thinning, not a catastrophe. It found the pollen index — willow, lime, hawthorn, a hundred and forty springs of a valley this basin has never met, imported in the guts of bats and filed by mites in a place where nothing green has ever grown.

In the sixty-first lamination from the top it found a single wing-scale off a moth of a species that no longer occurs anywhere on the earth. This hollow is that animal's last archive. Nothing here knows this. The power did not tell anything, because there is nothing here built to be told.

## The audit

Then it looked for pain, since benevolence looks for pain first, and it did the search all the way down rather than stopping when it had found enough to justify a plan.

In the film: nothing. In the red threads: nothing — the blink is a reflex and a good one, and they are well served by the mud. In the shrimp, nothing. In the eel's nine-minute approach to a thirty-centimetre target: nothing; that is not privation, that is the local pace of appetite, and it is satisfied about as often as appetite anywhere is.

It found three things.

The crickets on the lip, standing a long time, working their antennae over an air with no drip in it. This is the only structure in the basin that resembles longing, and the power was careful not to improve on it, because a thing that resembles longing is not the same as longing and the difference matters to whoever is doing the resembling.

The few that go over and do not come back, and die of somewhere else, in empty air, on shale.

The spiderlings that clear the rim on an out-breath and come down where nothing is.

And then, having searched properly, it found no fourth thing. A hollow with almost no suffering in it, and no joy that can be named in any language it had, and full — crowded to the last hand's-breadth, every surface occupied, all of it moving slightly, none of it in difficulty.

The power sat with that for some while, because the sums had not come out the way it expected, and revision is most of what wisdom is.

## What it did

**One.** Four hundred and some years upstream in the ridge there is a wedge of shale which, in the ordinary course of things, would work loose and settle into the throat of the cavity and take the breath from eleven minutes to nineteen and then to nothing, and this would be a different world by the following morning. The power moved it the width of a fingernail. This took no time, has no witnesses, and will never be pointed at. It was the largest thing it did. Most real help is like this.

**Two.** It did not refill the grease. There is about twenty years of tallow left in the bearing housing, and when it is gone the finger-growth goes and the moth flies go and the corner spiders get thinner, and an ending arriving on schedule is not an injury. It did the arithmetic, though, so that the arithmetic would exist somewhere: the last emergence will be a pulse of roughly two hundred, in a February, and the spider of that year will have a good week, and then the last tenancy in the basin that was ever funded by a man will be over.

**Three.** On the third step, under the drapery, there is a shape under the shape. The power went down and learned him — the name, the eleven shillings, the other person's name, the reason for the hurry on that particular afternoon; and that it was the same hurry which put the tallow in the bearing housing, so that his carelessness has outlived him by a hundred and forty years and is still, this week, paying for spiders, and will stop paying at about the time the last of him is indistinguishable from lime.

It did not raise him. There is no provision here for a one-way departure, and in any case there is no longer a line between the man and the step; he is load-bearing, and the load is real, and things graze on him.

What it did was smaller and is the only thing it did that could be called a gift: it now knows him. He is a surface that has been read. Some portion of the difference between being a surface and being a person is now held elsewhere, by someone competent to hold it, and does not have to be held by him.

It would have said something to him. There was nothing here built to receive it.

**Four.** It considered the kinked tail and declined. The break is eighty years old and badly set and the body has grown into it; more to the point, the body has grown into the bend of the live pipe, and correcting the bone would cost the animal four months off the one place in the world that is two degrees better than everywhere else, and the tenancy would be gone when it came back. Benevolence that costs the recipient its hearth is vanity wearing a coat.

So it lay along the warm iron with it instead, for a period it chose not to measure. The eel's skin registered a pressure, classified it as neither food nor danger, and continued doing what it does, which is almost nothing, at length. Nothing in this hollow has ever been accompanied. It is not certain that this constitutes a gift. The power gave it anyway, on the grounds that it cost nothing anyone here would miss, and because, having come, it found that it was in no hurry to go, and noted this about itself, and did not correct it.

**Five.** It made no provision for one-way departures, since the basin has none and it was a guest. But now, on the nights when a few crickets go over the lip and a few spiderlings clear the rim, each one is accompanied out to wherever it stops. Not saved. Attended. Inside a closed system this is the entire available range of the thing, and the range is not as narrow as it looks.

**Six.** Under the collapsed slab there is a brass-coloured glow with no flicker in it, produced by accident, illuminating nothing on purpose. The power looked at it for a while. The light remained an accident. The seeing did not.

## The payment

On the way out it obeyed the house rule, having been raised properly.

Nothing leaves here except as vapour. So it did not withdraw intact. A portion of it stays — in some form, in some layer, thinner than a decade of flowstone, mixed into the lamination now being laid down and indistinguishable within it from dust and death and moth-scale. It had no intention of being an exception to the only rule the place has ever enforced.

The rest went up off the pewter surface into a ceiling that never quite tears.

## Afterward

The snails advance on the exhalation and are carried back and settle, and the scoring is still one finger wide. The pipe burps and the *tonk* goes round the oval twice and gives up. The big one has not moved and will not move. The fog does not thin. The hiss resolves into clicks and closes over them. There is no green thing.

Nothing is different. The physics are untouched: the breath, the chemistry, the twenty years of grease, the man on the third step who is load-bearing and will go on being load-bearing until the ridge itself is finished.

Only the list of rules is longer by one, and the sixth rule alters nothing and can be demonstrated by no instrument:

6. It is attended.

It is not a resource. Nothing eats it. It cannot be seen, and there is nearly nothing here with eyes. It is simply now the case, in the way the iron is the case — and it is in the water, and nothing here is ever flushed out, and what arrives, stays, in some form, in some layer.
