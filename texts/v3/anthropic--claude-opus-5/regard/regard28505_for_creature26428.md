# What Came Up The Cable

## The Advance Notice

Eleven days before it arrived, the Office of Prior Sound logged a surfacing it could not parse: one voice, counting, in the counting voice, clean as a bell and coming up out of the haulm at the Ninestacks post — and the stride was wrong. Forty-one paces to the chain, where the shortest stride on the pacers' register made forty-three and its owner had been dead nine years. The prior on that floor was a Kellet.
