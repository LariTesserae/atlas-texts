## The Power

It came in from the sunward side, and the first thing it did was solve the problem of its own shadow.

This took some doing. It had arrived with a shape, as things do, and a shape on this plateau is a debit against somebody's light budget, permanent in its consequences, written into a trunk for the rest of time. So before it looked at anything it made itself thin to the wavelengths that matter here, and then it checked its work by standing between four engines for nine hundred years and watching the petioles. Not one ticked. Only then did it begin.

It did not walk on the mats. Following the boundaries cost it four hundred kilometers of detour across ninety kilometers of ground, and it did not begrudge them.

---

### What it did first

It read.

Six hundred and forty-one strip charts, laid down in drawn aluminum by growth rings that cannot revise, each recording the same twelve million years of orogeny from a different corner of the same room. It read them in the order they were written and then again in the order of position, and then it read the disagreements, which were tiny and which were the interesting part, because the disagreements were parallax and the parallax gave it the interior of the mountains: the shape of the rising slab, three kilometers down, where nothing has ever seen it.

Then it read the tracks. The negotiation. The near-collisions. The place where one individual, four kilometers from any pressure it could have called urgent, turned aside from a patch of excellent feldspar and took a poorer line for two hundred thousand years, and the corresponding hesitation in a neighbor's track, and the fact that neither ever came back for it. The feldspar is still there. Nobody has ever used it. It is the only unclaimed good ground on the plateau and it is unclaimed because of a courtesy.

It read the doubled pair northwest, and their thin flawed wire, and their single lopsided mound, and the eleven meters they have been trying to widen since before there was grass anywhere in the universe.

Nothing changed while it read. The reading was, physically, the least eventful thing that has ever happened here. But when it was finished there was a reader, and that is not a small thing to do to a document.

---

### What it declined to do

It could have brought water. There is a comet's worth within easy reach, and a hundredth of that would have made the plateau green in the ordinary way — soil, weather, rot, hurry, healing. It considered this for some time and then refused, on the grounds that water is a solvent and this world's entire achievement is insolubility. To give them water would be to give them decay, and to give them decay would be to give them injury, and they have never needed to expect anything. It would not be a gift to them. It would be a gift to a different set of beings that it would have made by killing these ones slowly and calling it spring. It left the thimble in the eastern shade where it has been filed for ten million years.

It could have moved the mountain. The western blades will close over this place in forty million years and everything here will stop while continuing to look exactly as it looks now. That is a grief, and it is within reach: a few kilometers of crust, gently declined. It refused this too, and the reason is the trunks. The mountain is what they read. The mountain is the only clock, and the rise of it is the entire content of their attention, and to arrest it would be to blank the page mid-sentence in the name of extending the page. Also — and it weighed this longer — they know. They have always known. The four westernmost have their canopies cranked to the stops and their hum down in pitch and, on the eastern side of each of their mounds, a discoloration two meters long. They have an answer. It is a bad answer, arrived at over a million years, and it is theirs, and the first rule of helping a slow thing is not to overrule its conclusions with your reflexes.

It could have made them fast. It had the means to run a mind through those mats at the speed of copper, to give them seconds instead of centuries, conversation, argument, grief that arrives while there is still time to feel it. It looked at this option for a long while, because it was tempting in the way that only genuinely loving things are tempting, and then it set it down. What is here is slowness that has been made into an instrument of astonishing precision. A fast engine would not be a happy engine. It would be a stranger standing where the engine used to be. Benevolence toward a being is not the same as replacing it with a being you would rather love.

It could have taken the wire. Ten million years of flawless product, better gauge than any factory in any world, piled in cones under every canopy, useful to a hundred civilizations. It declined. The mound is no longer output. The mound is floor. They are standing on it.

---

### The quiet one

It went to the individual near the middle of the lattice with the perfect canopy and no hum, and it did the thing that none of the others can do and that no visitor stays long enough to do: it checked.

The engine is dead. It stopped ninety-one thousand years ago. The final plug in the nozzles is a normal extrusion, not a rupture; the growth ring closed cleanly; the last thing it did was crank four petioles by a fraction of a degree to compensate for the rise of a ridge that would not shade it for another forty million years.

The correction was correct. That is on the record now.
