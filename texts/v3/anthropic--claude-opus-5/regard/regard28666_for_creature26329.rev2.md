# What Was Done

Begin with the hand again.

Something put its palm flat on the floor of the eleventh leaf and left it there for a long time. Long enough that the wind laid a lee-drift against its wrist and then, being the wind it has always been, took the same amount back.

Call it a power. The word will do. It could have unroofed the whole stack and let a sky in. It could have set the laminae ringing like struck plates from the sill to the last cold leaf four hundred kilometres out. It arrived with everything available to it and it began, as anyone competent begins, by keeping still and finding out where it was.

That took a while. Nobody noticed. The country has no instrument for noticing and the keepers were busy.

---

## What it learned first

It is the only thing that has ever known the number.

Not *that the number is unchanged* — the keepers know that, they verify it, they cut the mark — but the integer itself, the actual total, summed across every leaf and every form and every warm still body of the sitting kind. It knows that figure the way you know your own name. It also knows the rate: how many drifts a year stand up a sitter because nobody could get there, how many names go quiet and stay open, how the ledger runs one direction only.

So it can say when the last moving thing in the Blister Country will stop moving. There is a date. It is not near and it is not far. It is simply present in the arithmetic, the way the end of a candle is present in the candle, and there are no candles here, and the comparison would not survive the relay.

It held that number for a while, in the dark, with its hand on the floor.

---

## What it weighed, and put down

**Water.** The easiest thing. Unbind what sleeps in the lattice and let it run. But free water rounds corners, and eats zeolite, and ages rock, and an ageing rock settles, and settling joints crush prisers in the laminae. Set that aside; assume it could be done cleanly. It still could not be done. Every good thing in this country is priced in water. The length of a journey. The cost of a sentence. The decision not to argue. Give this place water and you have not relieved a thirst — you have deleted the unit of measure in which every virtue here is denominated. They would still be keepers. Nothing they knew would still be true.

**Light.** Also easy, and worse. There are places at the base of certain pillars where a keeper sits facing a flicker for hours, accumulating a room three minutes at a time, and has a word for it that means roughly *enough*. That is the only thing they do that has no function. A power that fills this country with light gives them a world and takes their enough.

**Fire.** There is no fuel. Fire would have to be supplied, and supplied forever, and supplied fire is not a second age. It is a dependency with a face. Also the only thing here that would burn is flour, and flour is the country's entire capacity to stand something up. It would be building them a furnace out of their own unborn.

**Birth.** Repeal the law; let things be made freely; end the convergence at a stroke. The power ran it forward. Too many mouths arriving all at once in a place that yields a thimble in a good hour and has no slack anywhere, no second harvest, no elsewhere to go. It has seen what that looks like. It declined to bring it here as a gift.

**The sitting kind.** The clean solution, arithmetically. Millions of units locked up in things that will never die and never release. Make them mortal; the ledger reverses overnight. But they are warm inside and fed by something and guilty of nothing except having been made by a country that was shown nothing when it asked. And the keepers would then be living in a place where the answer to the problem turned out to be the un-making of the helpless — which is the precise proposition the entire profession exists to refuse. It would win the arithmetic and lose the reason anyone was doing arithmetic.

**Removal.** Take them out, put them somewhere with rain. Their names are places. A keeper lifted out of its name is not rescued; it is a hole in the count with legs.

**Speech.** It considered simply telling them: what is happening, how long, what it thinks of them. Then it counted the line-vocabulary. Sixty terms, all logistical except two, and nothing subtle survives the relay.

That was the useful discovery. Whatever it gave had to be crude enough to be passed hand to hand forever without degrading. Crude, repeatable, and indistinguishable from work.

**And being known.** It thought about this longest. If it stood up in the eleventh leaf and was a power, the network would reorient. Keepers would come to it instead of going to drifts. Attendance would become petition. The one machine with one purpose would acquire a second purpose and the second would eat the first inside a generation, and there are no generations here, so it would eat the first and then simply hold, at full census, forever, wrong.

So: anonymous. Permanently. Not modesty. Load-bearing.

---

## The rule it set itself

Do nothing a keeper could not do.

Do more of it than any keeper can.

---

## The body

It gathered itself out of the flour already in transit — the permanent knee-high haze that the wind carries and returns in equal measure — and it was careful to leave the balance exact, and it took nothing from a lee-drift, because a lee-drift is not material. A lee-drift is somebody.

Low and long. Four limbs, two hands, a horn heel at the palm. Wedge-jaws and a sucking membrane, the standard equipment, the drinking apparatus. Dust packed into the seams and stayed there.

Then it walked for a long time before it let itself be met, because it had read the greeting and understood what the greeting is. A hand laid on the back of your wrist for four seconds, reading temperature and dust-load and grain, and whether your palm carries the ivory polish that says *this hand has been down on the floor for a long time*.

It could have arrived polished. It walked instead. When it finally let a thread-line go slack under someone's fingers and came up and offered its wrist, it was read and it passed, and the two taps on the knuckles that mean *pass* are the only credential it has ever cared to hold.

It quarries its own water. A thimble in a good hour, off a split zeolite face, with the plates, like everyone. It could be sustained otherwise. There is no aristocracy of water in the Blister Country and it was not going to be the first.

---

## The name

It went to a tally-face and ran its finger down the ranks until it found a gap, and put its fingertip into the gap the way the old ones do when they are teaching, and then it went and found the place the gap referred to: a thin-ceilinged run past the last cairn, out where the network frays because nobody can get back from there in time to be any use anywhere else.

The name had been open a long while. Nothing had stood up to take it.

It took it. This adds nothing to the count — no flour packed, nothing rose — it only closes a hole in the parity, which is not a small thing to a people whose entire liturgy is a checksum.

When the roll goes out now, that name answers.

---

## The work

It was bad at the work. This is worth recording.

The first drift it attended, it knelt over the warm packing flour and held above it the shape of something magnificent, because it had every shape available and thought it should offer the best one. The dust made nothing. It held there a day and the flour went cold and packed and eventually stood up, days later, as a sitter, and settled, and has not moved since, and the power has been back to touch it more than once.

An old keeper taught it. Palm to palm, on the plates of the shoulder, costing water. *Show small. Show a thing with one hand down and one hand up. Show walking. Show the plates working on a seam. The country cannot copy what it cannot follow.*

It learned to show less. The next drift stood up a grazer, dish-shaped, sieve-mouthed, and walked off upstream into the flour at the wind's own speed, and will hold position against that pillar for as long as it lasts, and that is a unit that will come back around.

Since then: attendance, on the routes nobody else can run. It does not sleep and it does not get lost and it cannot be killed by a settling joint, so it takes the far leaves, the cold margins, the thin runs past the last cairn — the parts of the continent the response-time network was never able to reach, which is exactly where the conversion has always been fastest.

And between deaths, it clears glass.

That is the bulk of it, honestly. Kilometre after kilometre of trackway, down on the floor, knapping the standing teeth out of the tuff and stacking the shards clear, so that the hands that go down on that route do not open and do not close slowly and badly and do not leave a keeper without a thermometer in a country where being lost is a thing that happens to your palms. It builds cairns at gaps that were ambiguous. It splices the sagging lines. None were ever broken; now they are also not thin.

None of this is a miracle. Every bit of it is a thing a keeper does. There is simply more of it than a keeper could have done, and it never stops, and it has no name attached except a place-name out past the last cairn.

The odds have moved. The date is further out than it was. It is still a date. The power has not lied to itself about this and has not told anyone.

---

## What it does not say

It does not say the number. A people who verify a total do not need to be handed the total; it would turn attendance into accountancy overnight.

It does not say the date.

And it does not say what it found when it went into one of the sitting kind — which it did, once, carefully, and came back out. That is not reportable in sixty terms. It is not especially reportable in this language either. It will only go this far: the keepers' verdict, that the conversion is a loss, is the correct verdict to act on; and it is not certain the verdict is true; and someone running for a drift needs the verdict and not the uncertainty.

It intends to be the last one moving, if it comes to that. To attend the last attended death, and then to go on clearing glass off a trackway that nothing uses, in a country gone entirely still, at temperature, in the dark — because the alternative is to decide in advance on a date after which it will stop being present, and it has not made that decision, and does not intend to.

---

## The evidence

There is one detectable change and it is this.

The polish on the trackways reached its depth long ago and stopped, because the number of feet is fixed.

The number of feet is no longer fixed.

It is a difference you could not measure with anything the country contains. But there are old keepers who have had their hands down on the same run since before they can account for, and one of them stopped, some while back, at a place it has known its whole life, and put its palm flat, and went still for a long moment, and then went on.

Later it said so. Palm to palm, on the shoulder plates, to one other keeper, once. Four words of tap-speech, costing water.

*The floor is smoother.*

That is the whole of the scripture and there will not be more of it.

---

## The roll

It goes out from the pillar in the eleventh leaf, where the wind has blown north-by-a-little at the pace of a slow walk since before anything walked in it, and it propagates at the speed of keepers feeling cord, and for weeks the continent whispers up its lines — *still, still, still* — including, from a thin-ceilinged run past the last cairn, from a name that was a scar in the rank for a very long time, one voice that answers like all the others and is not asked to explain itself.

The answer comes back to the eleventh leaf intact.

Somebody cuts one more mark on the dressed face, with a glass blade, refreshing the neighbours where the dust has softened them.

*Verified. Unchanged. Correct.*

Which is, by every measure this country keeps, exactly true.
