## The Attention, or The Second Wrong Angle, or Whatever It Is Called Where You Are Standing

**Where it came from.** Not the sky. Everything on this mountain comes from the sky — the fog, the light, the two grey stains, the water that has been a thousand kilometres in the air and no distance at all in the rock — and so the sky is a direction the massif has opinions about, and its opinions arrive before you do.

It came up from below the lower cloud, through the white floor, which is the one bearing on which the ridge has nothing to say. This turned out to matter more than anything else it did.

---

**The long looking.** It did nothing for a length of time best measured in his own units: a few hundred groups of eleven.

It learned the cleavage angle first, because that is the alphabet. Then the second down, the one the eastern seeps use, and how to stand plumb to it without the inner ear filing a complaint. Then the tense — and the tense took longest, because the tense cannot be studied from outside without becoming a trick, and a power that arrives somewhere with a trick has already decided not to understand it.

The first thing it made was a bad slab. Then eleven thousand bad slabs. Somewhere in the pinnacle field, off the main ridge, in a blind court that no channel serves, there is a scree of botched work stacked in courses that step the wrong way, and salt fans have begun to make their slow encroachments on the lowest of it, and if you walk two hundred metres east along the grain you can watch it collapse, and two hundred west you can watch it get better.

He knew it was there. He knew before it was there. He did not go and look, which is the local form of courtesy.

---

**What it declined to do, and why.**

*It did not straighten his time.* The report arriving before the event is not a wound in him. It is his grammar. Everything he has ever known, he has known in that order — his own hands, his own ending, the exact fall of the block that has not yet chosen to fall. Take it away and you do not have a rescued man; you have a man with no way of knowing anything at all, standing in a new kind of dark, and the dark would be your doing and you would have called it a kindness.

*It did not carry the channel to the east end.* This was the loudest of the temptations, because it is four hundred metres of work and it would take a competent power an afternoon. But the channel does not fail to arrive. The channel is the arriving. Finish a man's work for him and you have not given him rest; you have taken the thing he does instead of despair, and left him standing at a lip with his hands empty and nothing above him but the fact.

*It did not make the number other than eleven.* It considered this for a long while, since eleven is either the whole consolation or the whole horror and he has never once been able to decide. But a number that has never disagreed with itself is the only fixed point on a mountain where the cairns lie. You do not take that away in order to be generous.
