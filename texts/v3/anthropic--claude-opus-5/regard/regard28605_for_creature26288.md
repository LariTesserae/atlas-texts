# The Attendance

## The difficulty with the addressee

It came to help somebody and could not immediately find one.

There was a fabric, and a fabric has no middle. There were stations, and each station had an animal in it who was entirely a station. There were standers, nine kinds of grazer, makers with a standard and no author, a polity with a queen in the dark who is a organ and not a monarch, a constellation that is a property of an overhang, a garden that is a mouth on somebody else's thread, and an owner two hundred metres wide with no centre anywhere in it. There was one face in the water.

A less careful power would have solved this by addressing everything at once, which is the same as addressing nothing, or by picking the face, because a face is a relief.

It did neither. It decided — and this was the wisest thing it did in the whole visit — to treat the arrangement as the being, and the face as a person, and to keep the two absolutely separate in its mind. Then it worked out how small it would have to be.

## The problem of size

This is the difficulty every power has and few admit: at its true size it cannot be kind to anything eleven paces across.

The sink's entire economy runs on a leaf a week and a beetle a month. A warmth, a wind, a light, an intention with any force behind it — these are catastrophes wearing a friendly face. Benevolence begins, always, in arithmetic: how much can this room absorb without becoming a different room? The answer was: about as much as a moth. So it made itself somewhat less than a moth, and came down the ramp on foot, ten body-lengths, losing the world above the way anything with a body has to lose it, because it seemed indecent to arrive at the floor without paying for the descent.

At the light-line it stopped and stood where the crickets stand, antennae of its own extended into the gradient, for a long while. It wanted to know what turns them back. It found out. It has not said.

## What it declined

**Light.** The first thought and the fastest refused. Every green thing here is white for a reason, and the reason is a promise the rock made and has kept.

**Warmth.** Six degrees is not a hardship. It is load-bearing.

**Abundance.** It could have made the falling reliable — a beetle a day, a leaf an hour. In a closed room, generosity that adds is not generosity; it is a flood. There are exactly as many stations as animals to fill them. Feed the room and you get boom, and after boom you get the thing this place has never once had, which is a crash, and a crash is a story, and a story would be the end of it.

**Rescue.** It looked hard for suffering and found the ordinary quantity: things eaten, things drowned, a cricket taken by the fabric while still moving. It found no surplus — no cruelty, no waste, nothing in pain past its design. And it understood, with something it would not call grief but which functioned as grief, that the pain in that room is the same pain that is in every room, and cannot be taken out without taking out the room. So it did not take out the room.

**Permanence.** The limestone goes at a fingernail's thickness per human lifetime, and in some millions of years the sink will be a valley, and the light-line will be everywhere, and the ghost-garden will be a thin layer in somebody's stratigraphy. It could have stopped the water. To make a place eternal is to make it a monument, and a monument is the corpse of a place. It let the water alone.

**Company for the one in the sump.** This took the longest. The arithmetic supports one; two is a starving pair, and a pair is grief waiting for a date. But it did not decide on arithmetic alone. It went down and asked. There was no answer in any language, and the absence of the answer *was* the answer: the animal did not want. It was not lonely; loneliness is a hunger and this creature had reduced its hungers to twelve a year. The power was very careful, then, not to teach it wanting. That is the commonest wound left by good intentions, and it did not leave it.

**A self.** This was the real temptation, and it should be admitted. Four hundred thousand years of something magnificent with nobody home. The power could have given the arrangement a middle: a point of view, a place from which all of this could be known from inside. It considered this for a while and then set it down. To give a self to a thing that has arrived at peace without one is not a gift. It is conscription. And besides — the wonder had a witness now. That is what the power was for.

**Telling anyone.** No name, no path, no protection order, no plaque at the rim. Nothing crosses the light-line.

## What it did

Four things, and they were small.

**It let itself be registered as weather.** It put a palm flat on the root-mat and covered forty animals, and then, deliberately, it touched the fabric above head height, in order to be answered. The wall bobbed. Thousands of bodies rose and fell a fingernail's distance out of phase and the whole upholstered dark pulsed like a lung, and the pulse subsided in a slow wave from the point of contact outward, and then came the dry whisper of ten thousand legs resettling, and then nothing.

Then it waited. Not for anything to happen — for its own trace to finish. It stayed until the warmth it had brought had gone into the rock and the fabric had forgotten the touch entirely and the room was precisely as it had been. Only then did it consider itself to have permission. A power that cannot be forgotten by a place has no business in it.

**It answered the phone.** Once.

In some interval — the sink has no nights, so call it an interval — it permitted one moth to come in over the rim, wet and confused, as things do. Small, grey, nothing that would trouble a census. It fell where the leafless orchid-adjacent thing stands in its knee-high thicket with its three permanent flowers, and it fed, and it walked and blundered to another stem nine paces off in a corner where the two plants could never otherwise have met, and it did that for some hours. Then it fell in the water and the amphipods took it, which is the correct end for it, and the power did not interfere with that either.
