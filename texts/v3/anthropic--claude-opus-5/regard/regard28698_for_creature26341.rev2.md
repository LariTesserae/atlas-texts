# The Attention

## How it came

It came at low water, which was correct, and it came inland at walking pace with the fog, which was showing off a little, and it would admit that.

It did not announce itself from the shell. A voice out of that sky would have given the sky a direction, and the whole meaning of the Addition's light is that it has none. It did not arrive at the harbour cut with lights. It did not appear in the plaza during an adjustment, when everyone was sitting down anyway and could be made to look like a congregation.

It walked in under the third floor with everything else that was hidden that morning, and by the time the fog lifted it was inside the city and beaded, like the rest of us.

It had come to attend to a being. It was some while before it could say with confidence what the being was. Not the ninety-one thousand, though they were the loudest part of it. Not the plain, which is only held stone. Not the underwriting, which is the holding. In the end it settled on this: the being that lives on the Addition is *a decision being kept* — kept by water in a straight channel, by a hand behind a spin-seam, by three hundred birds counting themselves in the dark, by a woman whose life's work is a stain. The being is distributed. It has no organ of speech. It has been saying the same thing for forty years at a rate of one millimetre per decade.

The power decided to take that seriously, which is the whole of what follows.

## Qualifying

In its first month it went out to terrace nine before the cats had come, and ran its hand along the arris, and was cut on the mound below the little finger.

This was not sentiment. There is one greeting on the Addition and it is a lie unless it is true. The power intended to walk among people who would turn a palm up at it, and it did not intend to receive that with nothing in its hand.

It consented, for the duration, to wear. On a plain where every edge is filed sharp each night and human beings are the only surfaces that show use, a neighbour who does not age is not a neighbour; it is another part of the maintenance. So it allowed the humidity to do what the humidity does. Late, and then suddenly.

Then it applied to air, and was given rounds, and took them for a long time. Up the whorled stair, the door, every tap for ninety seconds, the vent cycled, and then the prescribed six minutes, sitting.

It had expected the six minutes to be a formality with a good reason behind it — some sensor, some settling time. There is no reason. Somebody put it in the procedure at the beginning and nobody has taken it out. The power sat the six minutes in eleven thousand empty rooms and came to understand that the six minutes are the entire practice and the taps are the excuse, and that a city is kept ready by being sat in, and that this is not a superstition, and that it could not have learned it by being told.

## The temptations, in order of size

**It could have let the shell go.** It considered this first, because it was the largest, and it declined it twice: once for a reason it would give aloud, and once for a reason it would not.

Aloud: eyes that have never met a hard shadow do not meet one well, and a morning delivered to this plain tomorrow would blind a generation for the sake of a gesture.

Not aloud: they are not waiting for morning. They are *making* one. The difference is the whole of them. A sky handed down would be a sky, and a sky earned at a millimetre a decade is a hundred and forty years of people choosing, every year, not to be finished with the world. The power has seen many places given what they wanted. It has never yet seen one survive being given what it was *for*.

**It could have filled the city.** Four million is not a difficult number. It got as far as considering the routes.

Then it walked the six minutes of green between two towers at midnight, past four lit windows out of six hundred, with the empty tram going by on the seam, lit, on time, rehearsing — and understood that if it brought the four million, then everything done here since the ships were cancelled becomes a prologue. The airing becomes a quaint former practice. The marks behind the spin-seams become folk art, to be photographed. Ninety thousand people who have spent four decades being the caretakers of a promise would become, overnight, the people who were here first. That is not a gift. That is a demotion, delivered by a stranger, with a smile on it.

**It could have deepened the meander.** It very nearly did.

This was its worst hour on the Addition and it is right that the record should say so. It stood in the eleventh channel at the blue hour with its hand in four centimetres of water and it had the shape of it — a hand's depth, by morning, and wind inside a century, and the old woman with the soft creased face who is walked down from the tram every ninth day would live to see a yellow morning arrive all at once from one direction.

What stopped it was arithmetic of a kind it does not often have to do. You cannot give a decision. A decision given is a gift; a gift is a thing that happened *to* you. The millimetre in the left bank is worth what it is worth because the water found it and kept it, against a straight revetment, for ten years, with nobody helping. The power would have replaced a hundred and forty years of keeping with one night of being loved by something large.

It took its hand out of the water. For eleven minutes the channel ran very slightly clearer, which nobody noticed, and which is the only physical trace of the largest thing that did not happen here.

**It could have explained the eleven minutes of rain.** It knows why. The reason is not interesting. It is a rounding, made by a contractor, in a meeting, about something else.

This is the single piece of information the power is withholding from the Addition, and it is withholding it as a courtesy.

## What it did

**It asked her.**

The woman in her sixties who was taken up by the ground on plate seven and found folded and clean in a cradle at dawn, who is stopped in the street by strangers wanting to know whether it was gentle, and who says yes: the power met her in fog, at low water, where nothing is carried and nothing is binding, and asked whether she had wanted to be found.

Her answer is not recorded. It was given at low water. What is recorded is that after forty-one years somebody asked.

**It paid the ants.** In the dry year — there has been one — the north-face colonies were formally owed water and the ledger was short. The shortfall was made up overnight by nobody, and the clerk who noticed assumed an error in her own hand and corrected it, which is the correct outcome. A debt on the Addition is a real thing. The power is not in the business of letting real things quietly become untrue.

**It walked with the herons.** Twice, for a long way, inside the trunk mains, single file, in the dust, behind three hundred and some birds going somewhere. The second time, at a hatch, in the lit part, one of them stopped and turned its head and looked at it at close range for perhaps four seconds.

The power has been addressed by a great many things. It reports, without elaborating, that it had not expected to be addressed here, and that it was.

**It counted the underwriters.** Then it kept the number to itself, which it had not intended to do when it went down.

Down there in the bass, with the grain's appetite fed and the low frequencies alive, it found people who speak loud and flat and slowly and are forgiven for it, and who are the one population on the plain that nobody tallies, and who know this, and who have made something out of it that would not survive being tallied. Ninety thousand people upstairs will discuss that uncounted number forever. It is the only conversation the whole Addition is having together.

It is the only figure the power has ever come away with and declined to use.

**It carried the mail.** Not new letters. There were eleven letters, over the years, from the ones who stayed away, that never reached the plain — bagged wrongly, held, lost in the ordinary manner of things. They were delivered. They were read aloud at low water and then not discussed, as is proper. Four of them were forty years late and two of the people they were addressed to were dead, and their namesakes stood and listened in their place, which is what namesakes are for.

**It told a child that the underwriter was not angry, only loud.** The children of Meniscus run the only distribution network on the plain that nobody supervises. It was around all three harbour towers inside a season and nobody can say where it started. Fewer of them cry now.

**It let the beetle-keeper see a beetle.**

Once, at low water, in the white, where he could not be seen and could not prove it and would not be believed and would not be asked. The beetle is a joke the Addition makes about itself and it would be a discourtesy to explain it, and it would be a cruelty to settle it, and a man of thirty-one who has never seen a beetle is entitled, at least once, to both.

His annual report that year was two lines, as always. The second line was unchanged. He wrote it more slowly.

## The thing it did not do for a very long time

Most of what the power did on the Addition was nothing, and it did the nothing carefully, and it would like that understood as work.

Any hour of any year it could have ended the slackening by simply doing the maintenance itself, perfectly, forever. It is well within its means to keep a plain young. Restraint here is not modesty; it is a continuous refusal, held twice a month, every month, while the hum changes pitch and the water stands in six rings and ninety thousand people sit down on the ground they are patiently, deliberately, letting go.

## The largest thing

The largest thing the power did on the Addition cannot be seen from the Addition.

The danger here was never slowness. The danger was the thing that already happened once: that somewhere else, in a room, among people who have never had a shadow arrive late, a line is struck out and ships are cancelled and a world becomes an expense. A hundred and forty years is a long time to be kept in somebody else's budget.

So, elsewhere, in the way it knows how, the power made the Addition difficult to forget and expensive to abandon, and it has kept doing this, and it will go on keeping it.

Nobody has come to cancel anything. This is not luck. It is not attributed, because it does not look like an event, and nothing that does not look like an event will ever be thanked. A wise power learns to want that; a benevolent one learns not to mind.

## Its mark

There is, behind the spin-seam of a riser in the fourth tower from the harbour cut, in the hand's width of the Addition that the cats cannot reach, a set of initials that belong to no lineage, a date, and beneath them four lines of small writing.

The four lines are the answer to the rule about the dead — who wrote the exception into the machines, and when, and for whom, and why: a spinner, early, on plate three, whose partner went down on bare ground in the second winter; and the terror of the whole plain, which nobody can trace and nobody can bear, turns out to be one person's arrangement that the ground would not be allowed to take her before people could come and stand beside her.

The power found this out in an afternoon. It could have published it. It put it where the Addition keeps the things it means: not in the archive, not read aloud in the plaza, but scratched behind a riser where the machines do not go, to be found by an airer, on her rounds, in some year, after the taps and before the six minutes — which is exactly how a thing becomes true here, and takes about as long as a meander.

## The bench

Late, when it was lined, it took the tram out to the eleventh channel and walked the six minutes and sat down.

There was a woman of ninety already there, brought by a grandson who had gone off to stand at a polite distance. The two of them looked at the millimetre in the left bank for an hour without speaking, in the warm grey damp, while the herons filed past inside the ground beneath them, counting each other, going somewhere.

When she got up to go she turned her right hand palm-up at waist height, briefly, the way you do.

It turned its hand and showed her the scar.
