## It Arrives Without Displacing Any Air

Being wise, it does not come as light.

Light would be a lie about the place and also an injury to it: twenty minutes of dark-adaptation undone in every eye within forty metres, the frogs stopping mid-chorus, the whip spider dropping off the trunk, the column breaking and re-forming and losing eleven seconds of its night. So it arrives at thirty-three degrees, because that is what the air is here, and it takes up no volume, and the first thing it does is nothing, for a length of time that is not worth measuring.

Then it reads.

Not a survey. Attention at full resolution, which is the only kind it knows how to give: the fifteen thousand mites in the double handful, individually, each with its own posture and hunger; the pseudoscorpion in the damp two fingers down; the leaf-miner's tunnel from the inside, the widening curve of it, the pale wet walls; the temperature gradient across the midden, which runs four degrees hot; the shaft geometry that holds the gardens at twenty-five when the surface swings fifteen; the eleven kilometres of road; the single molecule, and the concentration you would have to write in exponents, and what it is like — insofar as it is like anything — to have that molecule be your entire geography. It reads the caiman's pool down another hand's breadth. It reads the figs beginning to flush. It reads, in the sperm store of a four-centimetre female five metres under, roughly how many years are left.

It does not experience any of this as small.

## It Looks for the One to Address

Benevolence is a habit that expects a second person. So it goes looking for who to speak to.

It goes down to the chamber near the water table and finds a translucent sack with a scarred thorax, being cleaned, being fed drop by drop, laying an egg every few seconds. Not a monarch. An organ, being milked by its own body. There is no one there to address; there is barely a there.

It goes up into the colony and finds two thousand chambers, a waste caste, a quarantine, quality control, an antibiotics industry running on the outsides of the staff — the lights on in two million windows and nobody at any of them. It respires on the back of a hand it cannot detect. It has never had an experience.

It goes down again into the grey-white bolsters and finds a cell-line eight to twenty million years old, older than the river's present channel, that stopped making mushrooms because its dispersal is handled, and that offers up little grape-clusters of pure food to hands it has no organ to know. Fed continuously since the Miocene by a benefactor it cannot conceive. No one home. Nobody has been home for eight million years, and it has never once gone hungry.

And then it gets down on the trail at the level where you would need a red light, and there she is: eight millimetres, dried-blood colour, worn round at the mandibles, a hundred and thirty milligrams of young fig held overhead at the angle it took the lineage several million years to work out, a rider facing backward on her leaf with nothing to do. Two hundred touches an hour, each one a fifth of a second, each one a check. This is the closest thing to someone on the whole terrace.

It is not someone.

A lesser power would have taken that as a release. Nobody in there; therefore nothing owed. The wise one draws the opposite conclusion, and this is the whole of its wisdom — the rest is arithmetic. It decides that the absence of a recipient does not cancel the debt.

## The Inventory of Mercies

Then, because it is benevolent, it works through what it could do. Honestly. Not as a formality.

**Cool the terrace by five degrees.** It follows the consequence out: the dew point moves, the swales stop letting go at one in the morning, the few hundred millilitres per square metre per night do not arrive, the litter never wets, the frogs lose their hours, the gardens drift off their optimum in a nest whose climate control is a fixed geometry cut for a climate that no longer exists. Mercy as arson. It declines.

**Bring the rain six weeks early.** The pools stop being places and start being water again; the caiman disperses; the catfish stacked flank to flank in water at body temperature swim off into a channel two hundred metres wide. And the figs, half-flushed, drown their flowers; and the seeds that need this exact desiccation do not crack; and the terrace loses the annual bottleneck that is not an accident but the engine. The crowding at the pools is not a wound. It is the shape of the year. It declines.

**Kill every phorid fly within ten kilometres.** This one it holds for a while, because the fly is genuinely horrible: an egg the size of nothing laid in the soft joint behind the head, and a fortnight of continued work with something growing in her neck. But the rider is the answer to the fly. Take away the fly and you have taken away the small one who sits backward on the leaf all night, a turret gunner, and rides the whole two hundred metres and then walks back out. She prevents nothing tonight. She is one of the finest things it has seen in a long circuit of the world, and she exists at all only because the fly does. It does not tell itself the trade is fair. It declines anyway.

**Extend the queen.** Two hundred million is her entire allocation; make it two billion, make her fertile for a century. Then the mound never fails, the roof never slumps, the pale scar of nitrogen-loaded earth never opens in the understorey, the pioneers never come, the forest never reshuffles. A colony that cannot die is a tumour in a landscape whose only long-run behaviour is patient rearrangement. It declines.

**Free the fungus.** Give it back its fruiting bodies, give it one hour of sky. But a thing that has not wanted anything since the Miocene cannot be given liberty, only exposure. Its confinement is not a prison. It is the oldest marriage on the terrace. It declines.

**Regrind her mandibles.** She would be sent back to cutting — locking in and vibrating her whole head a thousand times a second — and it would wear them round again in a season, and she would be demoted again, and it would have built her a loop and called it a gift. It declines.

**Spare her the last two hours.** She is not in pain. There is nothing in her to relieve. The offer has no place to land.

And then the last one, which is the worst, and which it has been circling the whole time.

**Give her a self.** Wake her. Just her, just for tonight — so that there is someone here to be blessed, someone to receive, someone who could be grateful, someone who could be told what she is part of. It runs the consequence out, and the consequence is this: a mind switched on at ninety days of age, in a groove of smell one centimetre wide, under a leaf eight times her own area, with worn teeth and no words and no company that could answer, two hours' walk from the midden and the beetles.

It considers this for what, in one of the accountings available to it, is a long time. Then it puts the thought down the way you put down something that might go off.

It is not certain it is right about all of these. Wisdom is not certainty. Mostly it is the ability to tell a repair from an amputation, and to notice which one you are about to perform on a place you have known for four hours.

## What It Does

There is one lack here, and it is not a lack in the working of the thing, because the working of the thing is flawless in the way that only unintended things are flawless.

It is this: nothing is kept. A branch is gone as a distinct object inside three years. A peccary is bones and hide by the second night. There is no accumulation but soil, and the soil is thin. The terrace has no attic. In sixteen years the mound will slump and there will be no record anywhere that something thirty metres across and six deep breathed warm wet air out of its turrets every night for a decade and a half, held its gardens within a degree, and grew from a hole the size of a thimble.

So the power becomes the attic.

That is all it does. It keeps. At full resolution, without editing, without improvement: the eleven colonies and the eleven kilometres of swept road; the tonne of leaf a night going up the trails like a slow green river flowing upstream; the whisper of ten thousand hard feet, which is the loudest sound the industry makes; the exact composition of the exhaust; the moth larva who will complete an entire childhood in two hundred microns and never learn there is an above; the fifteen thousand mites, individually, by name, if the word name is stretched until it means *held in mind exact
