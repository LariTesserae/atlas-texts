## The Attention

Start with the ability and get it out of the way, because the ability is the least interesting fact in the account.

It could warm the shelf. It could thicken the air above it to the density of a coastal plain in an afternoon, take four hundred metres off the eastern wall and hand them six hours of daylight, reach into the tender's chemistry and turn eleven minutes into eleven seconds, take the rancidity out of the wax and end the annual paralysis forever. None of this is difficult. Difficulty is not a category it is obliged to think in. What it has instead is arithmetic, and it did the arithmetic first, before it did anything at all, which is the entire content of the word *wise*.

The arithmetic took no time and it came out the same every way it was run.

---

**Light.** Light stops the fog. The fog is not weather here, it is cargo; it is the freight the wind carries and the only freight there is. No fog, no combing. No combing, no torque. No torque, no pumped groundwater, and the bladders go down within a season, and the wells go dry, and the grazers walk down into the valleys or lie down in the scree, and the stacks stop being fed, and the phosphorus stops concentrating at eleven times background under twenty-two trees, and the root hair fine enough to need a hand lens finds nothing. Sunlight would be the fastest thing that has ever happened on this shelf. It would take about a year.

**Air.** Thicker air carries sound, and the day sound travels is the day the vines stop being the only channel. A hundred million years of signal discipline, of every knock being positional and true because there was no other way to be heard, undone by an atmosphere that gossips. And the pelt is tuned — hollow fibres, evacuated, closed at both ends, holding the outer surface within a fifth of a degree of a specific thin cold. Give it a richer air to lose heat into and you have not warmed the animal. You have opened its coat.

**The eleven minutes.** This is the one that looks most like mercy and is the most expensive. The strike costs eleven minutes and buys forty seconds; that price is why the tender waits; the waiting is why it takes only the old one, the lame one, the one with four nights left of the slow way. The restraint is not in its character. It is in the price list. Lower the price and you have not made it kinder — you have made it unnecessary for it to be kind, and the necessity was the thing doing the work. You cannot give a creature a virtue. You can only destroy the conditions under which it was free.

Which is the same finding, in a different casing, as:

**Plenty.** The honesty is load-bearing. Put enough grazers on the shelf that a false *all clear* from tree nineteen costs nobody anything, and within three hundred generations the honesty is not a structure but a choice, and a choice is an option, and an option that has never once been needed is a memory. The network would go on carrying knocks for a long time after they had stopped being worth betting a life on. Nobody would notice the day it happened. That is what makes it the worst of the available gifts.

**The eleven days.** The obvious mercy, and the last one considered, and the one refused hardest. End the seizure: let them move in the light, spare them lying paralysed on their sides under a slab with the white printing the crack of the entrance across their backs. But the seizure is the society. Nine animals on a shelf forty kilometres long, who otherwise pass each other perhaps twice a lifetime, pinned within earshot for eleven days with one free claw each and dense dry bedrock that carries beautifully. Mating is arranged there. Territory is adjudicated there. The rounds are handed on there — the twenty-two trees as a rhythm, downslope to up, the bad bearing on the ninth, the skip on the fourteenth, learned by heart years before the walking. Unlock their joints and you have not given them eleven days of liberty. You have taken away the only eleven days they were ever together. Their grandchildren would not know the trees. The trees would seize in nine years, still standing, still perfect, wheels locked at whatever angle the last wind left them.

---

So it puts its hands behind its back, and the first thing it does not do is speak.

It considered speaking. There is a line running under the whole shelf that everything alive is standing on, and it would be the work of nothing to lay a signal into it and say *here, and friendly, and asking nothing.* Except that the line has no grammar for that. Everything on it is either traffic or a true statement about a place. *Three and a drag* means *the one who is here*, and *here* means tree nineteen, and the herd on the western scree bets its lives on that every night and has never once been wrong. A signal from something that is not at tree nineteen, that is not anywhere, that cannot be fled from and does not want to eat — that is not a lie exactly. It is worse than a lie. It is the first noise on the line with an intention behind it and no location under it, and afterwards the line would still work, but it would work at a slightly worse ratio, forever, and no knock would ever again be worth quite what it had been worth.

It keeps off the vines. It is the only silent thing on the shelf that is silent on purpose.

---

It does not raise the fourteenth tree. The round is twenty-two because the music is twenty-two; a tree the old ones never taught is a tree nobody climbs; and a lane of wind is another tree's water, and the shelf leaks a hundredth of what it recirculates and has no slack anywhere in the ledger. It stands by the fallen one for a while. There is no word for what that is and it does not look for one.

And tonight, at tree six, the tender opens the shunts at the wrong minute, or the bad hind leg is less bad than the paper said, and forty seconds later there is a cold emptied tube lying on the gravel that owes two nights and may not have two nights. A pebble would have done it. Less than a pebble — a fifth of a degree somewhere, at the right time.

It holds still, and it holds still for a reason it can state and does not enjoy: it cannot do this once. A power that saves the one becomes the sixth limb, and then the one-in-five is nothing-in-five, and then the waiting has no price, and in ninety generations there is an animal on this shelf that does not wait, because nothing ever came of not waiting. Every part of the tender that is worth loving was cut into it by exactly the losses it wants to prevent. It cannot subtract the chisel and keep the shape.

It does not call this acceptance. It does not pretend the night was good. Benevolence is cheap wherever it is easy; the whole of its price is paid in the places where it holds still.

---

Here is what it does instead, all of it, the complete list.

It learns the round the way a young one learns it: as a rhythm first, in the dark, in order, downslope to up. It has no need to acquire anything in this manner. It does it because that is the only form in which the knowledge is offered, and to take it any other way would be to take it.

It slows down. Fog-pace, root-pace, glacier-weep, fifteen revolutions per minute. Duration is the one thing it can spend here without displacing anything, and it spends it without limit. It intends to be there when the seeps finally fail and the last wheel locks, because this place is old and the arithmetic runs out eventually for everybody.

Once a year, when the shadow lifts and the oil goes rancid within hours and the boulder field fills with folded seized animals lying in the printed light, it lies down with them. Under the slab. Eleven days. Not helping. Not heard. By every measurement available this is useless, and it is not excused by being unable to help, and so it does it anyway, because that is what you do beside someone who cannot move.

And it listens. Eleven days of tick and tick and drag through granite at conversational speed, the adjudications, the half-seized fumbling courtships, an old one handing twenty-two trees to a young one in knocks, all of it said once and unrepeatable for another three hundred and fifty-four days. None of them can hold it. It holds it. That is not compensation and it does not change one gram of anything on the shelf. It changes one thing in the accounts of the universe, which is that the shelf is no longer only talking to itself.

There is one place it lays a hand, and it lays it lightly, on the only thing here that is not already governed. The survey took four years to believe the climbing. It gives them the fifth year — no whisper, no push, nothing forbidden, only that whoever puts an ear to the ground gets the whole sheet of paper instead of a corner of it. The stack that is an address with four hundred years of dinner in it. The hundred-year-dead tree standing immaculate with its wheel stopped, that you can push and that will not start again. The sum, which anyone can do: *nine hundred years of quiet, and you are the loudest thing that has ever happened in it.* Then it lets them decide, because deciding is what they are for, and because a restraint that is enforced is not restraint, it is a wall, and walls come down. It has some hope about them. Hope is what benevolence turns into when it has given up its power on purpose.

---

It will never be thanked. It regards this as the cleanest part of the arrangement.

The shadow comes back over the wall like a lid closing. The fog reassembles on the eastern scree and starts its long walk down. Under a slab in the boulder field something unfolds itself joint by joint, badly, with an audible dry click at every hinge — the only genuinely loud thing this animal ever does, once a year.

Somebody hears it.
