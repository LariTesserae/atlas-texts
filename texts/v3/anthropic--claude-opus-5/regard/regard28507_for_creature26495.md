# The Attending

## I. The Terms

It came across the pan in the sixth hour, when the light is worst and the caliche gives back everything it was given, and it found the place the way anything finds the place: by the thread of moisture leaning east out of the ground, bending downwind, thin as a wire.

It could have known the room without descending. That is nearly the definition of what it was. It descended anyway, because a room is not a fact to be known but a set of terms to be accepted, and there is no way to accept terms from above.

At the pinch it went sideways. It put its hands where hands go, on the glassy warm rock, slightly greasy, fitted. At the fourth meter the air thickened and it stopped there a long while and did the only thing a benevolent power is ever obliged to do first, which is to establish what a place *is* before deciding what it is *for*.

It had been given to understand that a being lived here. It spent forty years working out which one. The tree was a candidate and so was the colony, and the bats had a case, and the ghost in the worn hollow had the best case of all if you weighted grief. In the end it settled the question the way such questions get settled, which is by noticing that it had stopped being able to describe any of them separately. The being was the room. The tree was its skeleton, the ants its immune system, the ceiling its lung, the frogs its speech. The nine hundred vertebrates were weather inside a body.

It has a voice. That is nearly the definition too. It did not use it. Not once, not in anything that follows.

## II. The Mercies It Considered and Set Down

**The frogs.** Two slits, one from each side of the mouth cavity into those full, elastic, never-inflated sacs. A morning's work for a much smaller power. By the next rainy hour the dome would have a chorus in it, three species deep, and any listener would weep. It considered who the chorus would be for. It worked the question all the way down and arrived at *the listener*, and set it down. The frogs are not lonely. A frog on a fern axil presses one foot to a taut leaf and releases it and has said what frogs say. Nobody in the room is waiting to be told anything it is not already being told.

**The crickets.** Restore the file, keep the ridges past the last instar, and the species stops building an instrument it dismantles. Before doing it, the power went and read the trembling. It found dialects. It found a grammar of duration — a tremble of fifty seconds meaning something a tremble of ninety does not, four regional variants across forty paces, a courtship conducted through the mechanical properties of fern tissue with a precision no wing file could match. To hand back the song would not be a restoration. It would be handing a fluent people a second language they do not need and cannot use, and calling it a gift because it is audible.

**The predator.** Here it was careful, because this is where a lesser benevolence gets impatient and starts helping. Whatever hunted by ear in this dome is gone. Not scarce — gone, for perhaps forty thousand years, no trace on the bone shelf, no niche for it, nothing. The room is obeying an animal that does not exist. Every silence in it is a compliance with an absent authority.

The power sat with that against the warm east wall and sweated and did not resolve it. What it wrote down instead was this: the fear is gone and what the fear built is still standing, and everything in the room now speaks through the medium the fear provided, and there is a word for a terror that outlives its object and goes on producing precision and beauty and tenderness, and the word is not *cage*.
