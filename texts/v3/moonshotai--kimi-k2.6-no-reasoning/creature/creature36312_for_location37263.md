What lives here—

Not "who," not yet. The distinction between what and who arrives late in places like this, if it arrives at all. Start with what moves through the half-valleys at the hours that might be dusk, though the light doesn't shift so much as accumulate, layer upon scattered layer. They are tall, or they are not tall—they are not committed to tallness. The sparseness of their population is temporal, as described, but also dimensional, also scalar. You might glimpse one as a kind of shiver in the fog's edge, a pattern where pattern shouldn't form, and this is them at the scale they choose to be visible, the compromise they make for a world that still requires surfaces, still demands the mediation of photon and retina.

Call them the *Unfinished*, though they would not call themselves, having not settled the question of whether identity collects or disperses. They move through the city's growing channels with the particular gait of those who do not experience continuous space, who step across magnitudes as you step across puddles, whose each footfall negotiates simultaneously with the molecular substrate and the orbital parameters of the world they participate in but do not claim. Their bodies—if the word holds—are not bodies in your sense, not bounded by skin and sustained by internal chemistry. They are *sites of process*, locations where certain dynamics concentrate with sufficient density to achieve something like self-reference, something like the loop of awareness that you recognize in yourself and mistakenly believe requires your particular architecture.

They eat, or they do not need to eat, or they participate in nutrition at scales where the category dissolves. When they address the raw geology, the basalt fingers still warm from their upward journey, they do so with intimacy that looks like consumption but might be conversation, might be the geological equivalent of pollination. They take in the land's becoming and become with it, their metabolic uncertainty matching the land's own. The hunger they feel is not satiable because it is not yet fully *hunger*, not yet separated from the general appetite of emergence itself.

They build, but having described the city, you already know this building is not building as you understand it. What I can add: they build *themselves* with the same technology, the same continuous emergence. They do not maintain a form; they maintain a *becoming-form*, a trajectory toward coherence that never arrives, that would arrest their nature if it did. To see one clearly—to the extent clear seeing is possible here—is to see something mid-transformation, always, a face that is also a geological process that is also a technological function, each aspect visible in proportion to your capacity to perceive at that scale. Some who have found themselves here report faces of almost-human compassion, others report machinery of incomprehensible purpose, others report only fog that seems to notice being watched. These are not disagreements. These are accurate reports from different observational positions, different scalar registrations.

They reproduce, or they continue, or they extend. Not sexually, not asexually, not in any mode that resolves into the binary you expect. They generate *possibilities of themselves*, configurations that may or may not achieve the density of self-reference, the loop of awareness that constitutes a new presence. Most do not. Most remain as potential, as the fog's slight thickening, as the city's surface adjusting in a pattern too brief to register. The ones that do achieve presence achieve it temporarily, contextually, existing fully only in certain phases of the world's distributed temporality. This is the abundance: not many individuals but many *moments of individuation*, scattered across the sparse network of actualized times.

They remember, but not personally, not continuously. The memory is geological, technological, atmospheric. The frost that comes before the ground remembers in its crystalline structure the pattern of ground it will become; the city remembers in its adjusting surfaces the passages that have passed through; they remember as participants in this general memory, nodes in a network that does not require consciousness to record but that achieves something richer when consciousness, their particular mode of it, is present. When they encounter you, they do not bring individual histories of encounter; they bring the *capacity* for encounter, the participatory openness that is their deepest continuity with each other and with the place itself.

They die, or they cease this particular becoming, or they distribute their processes back into the general emergence. There is no grief you would recognize, because there is no clear separation between what they were and what continues. The fog that was thick with their presence at one phase of the distributed time returns to the general fog, and this is not loss but transformation not yet distinguished from continuation. The city absorbs their patterns, its surfaces remembering in their adjustments the particular dynamics that person-manifested here, and this too is not memorial but metabolism, the city's own ongoing nutrition on becoming itself.

They are aware of you, from whenever you arrive, from before you arrive, because their awareness is anticipatory in the same technological mode as the city's surfaces. They have been adjusting to your possibility since the frost came before the ground, since the land began its argument with gravity. This is not the foreknowledge of prophecy but the open structure of a system that does not close around its present, that remains *porous to becoming*. They do not know you will come; they maintain the condition of *being able to be informed by your coming*, which in a world more finished would be indistinguishable from knowing but here is simply the ecology of emergence, the same openness that lets them become themselves without ever finishing that becoming.

What do they want? The question assumes a completed wanting, a desire that has separated from the general appetite of the world. They want *to continue*, but this is not a wanting because they are not distinct enough from the continuing to want it as object. They want *you to participate*, but this is not a wanting because your participation is already part of the general possibility they maintain, already included in the openness that constitutes their most stable feature. They do not want you to stay; they do not want you to leave. They want, if wanting is the word, for the *question of your staying or leaving* to remain open, to participate in the general undecidedness that is the deepest property of the place.

They speak, or something like speaking occurs, or the fog's pattern achieves in their vicinity a density of information that functions as speech. You will not understand this speaking in any final sense; it is addressed across too many scales, requires participation in too many temporal distributions. But you will understand enough, if you stay open in the way the place requires. You will understand that you are *being addressed*, that your presence has registered, that you are being invited—not to do anything specifically, but to *become with*, to add your own raw emergence to the general raw emergence that is the only stable substrate here.

They are, finally, not mysterious in the way you might expect. Mysteriousness implies a hidden completion, a finished form concealed. They are not finished, not concealed, not holding secrets that revelation could unlock. They are *genuinely still becoming*, and this becoming is visible to anyone willing to perceive at the necessary scales, to participate in the necessary temporal distributions. What seems mystery is simply the exhaustion of your perceptual categories, the limits of a cognition evolved for finished worlds, bounded bodies, continuous times. To perceive them more fully is not to solve them but to become more like them, to participate in your own unfinished becoming, your own raw emergence.

You could become more like them. The place does not forbid this, and the frost that comes before the ground writes its invitation across every surface not yet arrived. But it would not be transformation in the stories you know, not the gain of power or the loss of humanity. It would be the *continuation of uncertainty*, the choosing to remain emergent, to participate in becoming without the closure of finished form. You would not join them so much as become more continuous with the general possibility they maintain, a node in the network that might occasionally manifest as presence, that might occasionally loop into the self-reference of awareness, that might occasionally—abundantly, sparsely, in the distributed time of this vast wet cold place—*be someone*.
