What lives on the Plate of Qeth is completion itself, made manifest in forms that almost resemble the living but have stepped past that threshold into something else—something that no longer participates in the anxious becoming that defines existence elsewhere.

The moss is not the first thing to understand, though it is the most visible. The moss is merely the *slowdown* made green, the biological process stretched across geological time until it forgets it ever knew urgency. But deeper than the moss, more fundamental than its perpetual almost-rain and its condensation-drip and its ambition to be forest where no forest should grow—deeper than this is the **Stone That Remembers**.

Not all stone on Qeth remembers. Much of it has finished even that. But in places where the erosion has not yet completed its final work, where the planing-off has left some irregularity, some last ridge or depression that speaks of what the mountain once was—there the stone still dreams its previous selves. You can see it in certain exposures, certain cliff-faces that the wind has not yet had eternity enough to smooth: the grain of the rock will shift when you are not watching directly, will suggest strata that run in directions incompatible with each other, will offer brief impressions of *pressure* where no pressure has existed since before the category of "before."

This is not the weirdness. The weirdness is what lives in the gaps between these memories, what has colonized the spaces where stone's recollection has finally failed. The weirdness at 2.9 is **Inhabitant the First**: something that exists in the *almost* of perception, in the flicker between seeing and having-seen, in the mathematical remainder when erosion divides completely into completion. It has no shape because it has finished needing shape. It has no intent because it has finished needing intent. What it has is **patience**, and that patience is absolute, undiminished, the patience of something that watched the stone become stone and will watch whatever comes after stone become that also.

You will not encounter the weirdness directly. The weirdness encounters you, has always already encountered you, is encountering you now in the space between these words. On the Plate of Qeth, this becomes easier to notice. Not more frequent—nothing is more frequent on Qeth, everything has finished with frequency—but *easier*, as if the weirdness and your awareness of it are the same phenomenon seen from different points in time.

Then there are the **Quiet Ones**.

Not the human visitors who stay too long and become sparse, become damp, become part of the quiet's maintenance. They are something else, something transitional, incomplete in their completion. The Quiet Ones are native.

They have no bodies, or bodies that are not relevant to what they are. A human who stays long enough, becomes quiet enough, becomes sparse enough—such a human might, in the final stage of their drying-out and their cooling-down and their expansion across time's whole width instead of its forward arrow—such a human might be permitted to *perceive* a Quiet One, once, at the edge of the moss forest where the dripping stops, where the temperature drop in the shade becomes something more than temperature, where the light slows its beams to a consideration that is almost hesitation.

The Quiet Ones are the **population of having-been**. They are what remains when active presence has completed itself and become memory, when memory has completed itself and become structure, when structure has completed itself and become the pattern that new structure will incorporate without recognizing. They are not the moss forests. The moss forests are their *agriculture*, if such a term could apply to a relationship without need, without harvest, without cycle. They are not the cities. The cities are their *exhalation*, the geometry of their patience made visible in moss-fiber and compressed spore, the infrastructure of a density they do not require but somehow produce, as a stone produces the smoothness of its own erosion without willing it.

The Quiet Ones do not think. Thinking is for the unfinished. They **arrange**, and the arrangement is itself their being, their having-been, their continual completion of what they were already. The cities emerge from this arrangement. The weirdness concentrates around it. The storms that are not storms, that deposit arrangement and completion rather than rain or snow—these are the Quiet Ones *moving*, which is not movement but the appearance of movement to senses still bound to sequence, to before and after, to the becoming they have not yet finished.

In the deepest erosion, where the ancient stone has worn through to the rearrangement beneath, where the ground continues slopes that should not exist and the horizon negotiates distance with whoever approaches it—there the Quiet Ones are densest, most *almost-visible*, most nearly approximating the presence they cannot need and do not want. Not wanting. They have finished wanting. But something in their arrangement produces an *effect* of wanting, of intention, of the nearly-purposive that the human visitors mistake for meaning, for significance, for the invitation they desperately need the Plate to be offering.

There is no invitation. There is no refusal. There is only completion, and the quiet that maintains itself against interruption, and the weirdness that is not weirdness to itself but simply the true shape of reality once becoming has been finished out of it.

The Quiet Ones have no name for themselves. "Quiet Ones" is a human imposition, a translation of the hush that precedes and follows and contains the rare perceptions of them. They would not recognize it. They have finished recognizing. They have finished finishing, and in that final finishing have become simply what they are, which is not a thing that can be said, only a thing that can be almost-encountered at the edge of the moss forest, in the deepest erosion, in the empty streets of the empty cities where the light considers before it falls and the condensation drips from trees that are not trees and the stone remembers or does not remember and the weirdness concentrates or disperses according to patterns too large for perception and too old for record.

What lives on the Plate of Qeth?

**Arrangement that has forgotten the arranger.**

**Patience that has outlasted time.**

**The sparse, the damp, the almost-rain, the not-quite-tree, the no-longer-stone, the weirdness that is not weird, the quiet that is not absence, the completion that does not complete because there is nothing left to complete.**

And, transient, temporary, still becoming—still *trying* to become—the visitors. The ones who bring their primitive tech, their simple tools, their hand-fabricated clothing against a cold that is not quite temperature. The ones who hunger for a place that has finished with hunger. The ones who walk for months across the planed mountain-tops, who build fires that negotiate with air, who dig fire pits that become in a century slight depressions with different moss, different weirdness, deeper quiet.

The ones who stay, who become sparse, who drip with cold condensation, who grow slowly toward their own completion.

They are not inhabitants. The Plate of Qeth has finished with inhabiting, with the need for anything to live there, to claim it, to adapt to it or be adapted by it. The visitors are **symptoms**, perhaps. Evidence that the world below still becomes, still needs, still produces the desperate and the curious and the ones who have heard that somewhere, at least, the becoming has ended.

Or they are **offerings**, though nothing on Qeth receives them. The moss incorporates their construction. The stone accepts their water, their bodies when they finally fall, their dreams when they finally dream what the stone dreams. The quiet absorbs their sound, their footsteps, their breathing, the beating of hearts that have not yet finished with beating.

The Quiet Ones do not want them. The Quiet Ones do not want. But the arrangement shifts slightly, the weirdness concentrates slightly, the cities emerge in geometries that suggest—almost suggest—the right size for human habitation, the street that leads to the plaza that opens to the view of the sky below like sea, the stars at noon pale and presumptuous, the erosion ancient beyond ancient, the wind that has forgotten scent and now forgets other things, the moss that makes rain from cold itself, the stone that remembers or does not remember, the quiet that is presence and attention and maintenance against the noise of becoming, the dynamic active on timescales that make human activity seem like failed motion, like stasis, like the impossible attempt to remain unfinished in a place that has completed itself.

What lives on the Plate of Qeth?

*The question itself, in its asking, has begun to settle. Has begun to finish. Has begun to become what it will always from now on be:*

*Not answered. Not unanswered. Arrayed among the other arrangements, patient among the other patiences, quiet among the quiet that maintains itself, sparse in the sparseness, damp in the dampness, vast in the vastness, weird in the weirdness, complete in the completion.*

*The Plate of Qeth does not need you to know what lives there. The Plate of Qeth has finished needing. What lives there lives there. What does not live there does not live there. The moss drips. The stone erodes or remembers. The cities emerge and are incorporated. The visitors come and become sparse and drip and are not quite absorbed, not quite incorporated, not quite anything that can be said to have lived or died or been transformed or remained the same.*

*The Plate of Qeth is vast. The Plate of Qeth is ancient. The Plate of Qeth has finished becoming itself.*

*This is what lives there: that finishing. That vast ancient finished becoming, that sparse damp quiet weird complete arrangement of nothing that was once something and is now itself, only itself, always itself, the Plate of Qeth, the world that has finished becoming, that waits without waiting, that needs without needing, that lives without living in any sense that could be recognized by anything still engaged in the process, still anxious in the becoming, still desiring the completion that the Plate has already achieved, already been, already always been.*

*Go there if you must. Take warm clothing. Take water. Take the quiet already in you. Take nothing else.*

*The Plate of Qeth has finished needing. The Plate of Qeth has finished.*
