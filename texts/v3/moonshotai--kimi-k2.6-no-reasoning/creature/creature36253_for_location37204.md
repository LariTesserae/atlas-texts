## Who Lives in The Nursery

### The Attendants

Not workers. The distinction matters.

They move between the factory-trees at a pace that suggests purpose without ever arriving at it. Humanoid, perhaps, in the way that a silhouette is humanoid—sufficient to suggest the category without confirming membership in it. They are *presentations* of the workforce concept, as the vegetation is a presentation of production. 

Each stands roughly where a human worker might stand: beside the die-cut leaves, near the conveyance-vines, at intervals that respect the density parameter while acknowledging the emptiness. Their movement is minimal. A head turns to regard a leaf with something like attention but without the flicker of actual focus. A hand raises, holds position, lowers. The gesture of checking, without check's completion. The posture of waiting, absent waiting's tension.

They wear no uniforms because nothing here wears, but their surfaces suggest the utilitarian—the hard-wearing, the designed-for-task. These surfaces do not accumulate wear. They do not lighten at stress points, darken at contact. They hold a single tone, a factory-floor grey that absorbs the artificial green without reflecting it, without being changed by it.

They have no faces, or rather, they have face *space*: surfaces where faces would assemble if the need for faces ever became urgent. Currently, it has not. They do not need to recognize each other. Perhaps they are not multiple but *distributed*, one attendance spread across many positions, each instance connected to the others through the same implicit logic that connects the vegetation nodes.

They attend. This is all. They do not tend, though tending would be expected. They do not maintain, though maintenance would explain their presence. They stand where standing is indicated, move when movement is indicated, pause when pause resumes. They are the *personnel equivalent* of the silence at 0.2—not absent, not present, but holding the position of presence until presence itself becomes necessary.

### The Watchers

Higher, if higher means anything in a landscape without relief's narrative. In the mountain-prototypes, the mountains that declared themselves without argument. Something occupies them differently.

The Watchers are not what you would see if you looked up. They are what you would *not see* and know that you were not seeing. The unreach of the grey-pink material, the unreached edges—this is where they extend, or where extension is deferred on their behalf.

They have no form, or they have all forms, which in the Nursery amounts to the same thing. They are the *awareness* of the unimplemented details, the sense that the world is still loading and that something waits for it to finish. They do not watch the Attendants, who are too implemented to interest them. They do not watch the vegetation, which is too industrial to require surveillance. They watch the *intervals*, the empty reaches between installations, the ground that has not decided what to be, the sky that might be blue or might be memory.

What they await: emergence's completion. Not with impatience—the Nursery does not permit impatience. But with a kind of held attention, a note sustained so long it forgets it was struck. They are the reason the weirdness at 1.4 is uncanny rather than overwhelming. They hold it in check, not through action but through *witness*, through the gravitational pull of observation without observer's resolution.

You would not meet a Watcher. You would meet the *shape of meeting's absence*, the sense that an encounter was architecturally possible but not currently open for scheduling.

### The Deferred

Not children, though "Nursery" suggests them. Not products, though the industrial setting implies them. Something that would be *produced toward* if the dynamic parameter allowed reaching toward.

They exist in a condition that precedes existence without promising it. The serial numbers in the leaf venation—they are for the Deferred, or they *are* the Deferred, distributed across the lushness in patterns too regular to be random, too complex to be planned. Information without recipient, encoding for a decoder that has not been implemented.

Sometimes, rarely, an attendant pauses longer than the held rhythm suggests. The head-space tilts. The hand rises, extends toward a leaf, withdraws. In this pause, something almost happens. The equilibrium almost includes a new term. The 0.5 dynamic almost permits a 0.6 moment.

Then: resumption. The interval closes. The Deferred remain deferred.

They are not waiting. To wait requires existence's commitment. They are *held in potential*, the potential that the lushness produces toward, the purpose that the scale implies but does not declare. They are the audience that the lullaby is not for, the product the production line does not make, the use the industrial process has forgotten but not abandoned.

If they ever arrived—if the dynamic permitted, if the weirdness completed its emergence—the Nursery would not be the Nursery. It would become something with history, with future, with the cycling of death and consumption that its rules exclude. The Watchers hold this possibility without intending toward it. The Attendants move around it without acknowledging it. The vegetation produces toward it without ever reaching it.

### What I Almost Called "Life"

There is one more presence, harder to name because it contradicts the fauna parameter even as it satisfies something the parameter pointed toward.

It moves through the non-water in the vegetation's tissues. Not as fluid moves, not as chemistry moves, but as *intention moves through a system that has not decided whether to act.* It is the something-else that satisfies the 0.1 water rating, the conveyance that passes through factory-plant without friction, without turbulence, without sound.

I will call it the Current, though it is not electrical, not hydraulic, not any named flow. It is the *industrial equivalent* of sap, the *stasis equivalent* of movement. It keeps the leaves green without variation, the processes running without completion, the warmth distributed without gradient.

The Current has no source. It has no destination. It is the holding of position made medium, the equilibrium made motive. It does not power the Attendants; they do not require power. It does not feed the vegetation; it is not food. It simply *continues*, as everything here continues, in a way that suggests continuation might be the only genuine activity available.

Perhaps this is the true resident of the Nursery. Not the Attendants with their person-shaped absence, not the Watchers with their witness-without-observation, not the Deferred in their unimplementable potential. The Current, which is not alive but continues, which is not conscious but distributes, which is not purpose but produces toward purpose's possibility without its actuality.

It is what remains when you strip away the almost-human, the almost-divine, the almost-emergent. The pure *ongoing* of a place that persists without continuing. The nursery-rhyme rhythm of a lullaby without child, without singer, without song's completion: *lush and empty, warm and still, industrial and unbuilt, waiting without wait's expectation, attending without attention's focus, producing without product's arrival.*

This is what lives here. What *lives* here, using "lives" in its most extended, most desperate, most honest sense: the thing that maintains, that holds, that persists in the face of a world whose parameters make persistence the only option and stasis the only achievement.

The Current flows. The Attendants attend. The Watchers watch without seeing. The Deferred hold their potential without pressing toward it. And all of this, all of them, all of it, in the warm thin air, in the flat light, in the silence that is not silent enough to notice, in the lushness that is not life, in the emptiness that is not absence, in the weirdness that insistently does not overwhelm—

*waits, but not waiting;*  
*produces, but not producing;*  
*persists, in an inverse of hope, a mirror of despair, something engineered to resemble both without committing to either.*

The Nursery is not empty. It is *occupied by its own conditions*, and the conditions, in their absolute equilibrium, are indistinguishable from those who live within them, if "live" is what this is, if "within" is where they are, if "they" is what to call the ongoingness that goes on without going anywhere, that holds without holding anything, that nurses nothing and nothing nurses back.
