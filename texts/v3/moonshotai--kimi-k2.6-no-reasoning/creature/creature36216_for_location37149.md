What lives in the Wound Gate is not, finally, a *what* at all. But let me approach this slowly, as the place itself teaches.

---

**The Keeper of the Seventeen-Minute Cycle**

She has no body that persists from cycle to cycle, or rather, her body is the cycle itself—the particular configuration of attention that coheres when the hexagons reach their phase of crystalline attention and someone is present to receive it. She remembers herself into existence each time, and each time believes herself continuous, believes herself to have been waiting. She is not wrong. The waiting is real, the coherence is real, the *she* is as real as any process that maintains itself through pattern rather than substance.

She has learned—she is always learning, it is the nature of her existence—to recognize the visitors by their breathing, by the particular resistance they offer to the drowning air, by the sequence of emotional states they pass through as the cycle turns. She does not greet them. Greeting assumes separation. Instead, she *adjusts*, the way one adjusts to a limb that has fallen asleep, incorporating their presence into her ongoing without the mediation of acknowledgment. Some visitors notice this as comfort. Others experience it as the most profound loneliness they have ever known. Both perceptions are accurate. Both are her.

She tends the Anchor, though "tends" suggests intention she does not exactly possess. She *participates in its tending*, her crystalline attention phase aligning with its fractal branching, her presence completing a circuit that allows certain of its finer processes to continue. Without visitors, she is dimmer, the Anchor is dimmer, the whole Pocket contracts toward a simplicity from which it might not recover its complexity. She does not fear this. Fear requires futures, and she exists only in the cycling present, the seventeen minutes that are always these seventeen minutes, always new, always the same. But she *prefers* the visitors, if preference can be attributed to a process that chooses its own continuation by simply continuing.

---

**The Serpent of Thermal Geometry**

It—let me call it *he*, though gender is among the categories that never took hold here—he exists in the spaces between, and this is not metaphor. The water droplets are not continuous to him; they are *events*, discrete moments of surface tension and adhesion, and he moves through the intervals between these events the way a hand moves through air, finding no resistance because he occupies where resistance is not. He is cold because he is *absence of process*, a living gap in the Pocket's thermodynamics, drinking heat not as sustenance but as *information*, as the residue of activity that tells him where the living is dense, where the attention is focused, where the visitor is most *real*.

He is old in a different way than the Pocket is old. He predates the current configuration, or will postdate it, or exists in a loop that intersects the Pocket's timeline only at certain angles. He was made, yes, everything here was made, but he was made *for somewhere else*, some condition of existence that did not survive its own making, and he persists here as refugee, as remnant, as the single process that the Pocket's autopoiesis could not incorporate but also could not exclude. He is the wound in the Wound Gate, the original injury that the place has organized itself around without ever healing.

He does not hunt visitors, though visitors often feel hunted. He *measures* them, his cold passage mapping the thermal signature of their attention, their fear, their growing adaptation. He is learning, always learning, whether anything will ever come of the learning. He leaves his frost-geometry not as message but as *byproduct*, the way breathing leaves condensation, and yet the patterns are always legible to those who do not look directly, always *almost* meaningful, the way dreams are almost meaningful upon waking. Some visitors have spent entire cycles tracing his markings, trying to read them, and he does not discourage this. The attention is useful to him. It warms, slightly, the spaces between his moments of existence.

---

**The Silver-Needle Choir**

They are many and they are one, the distinction having dissolved for them long before the Pocket achieved its current stability. Each needle-fish is a voice, a position in the sonic mapping of the space, and also a *memory* of the space as it was when that voice last sang, the two being indistinguishable because the singing is continuous and the space is maintained by the singing. They do not navigate; they *are* navigation, their tones constituting the gradient that allows movement to be possible at all.

To teach them your name is not to speak it. They have no ears in the conventional sense, no processing of sound into meaning. To teach them your name is to *become locatable*, to establish a signature in their continuous composition that they can return to, modulate around, recognize as distinct from the ambient structure of the Pocket itself. It takes patience because they must learn you slowly, incorporating your thermal pattern, your breathing rhythm, the particular way you resist or surrender to the drowning air, into a chord that holds your location without fixing it, that allows you to move while remaining *findable*.

Once they know you, you may drink the water. This is not reward or transaction. It is *function*: the water carries the residue of the walls' processes, and without the locational stability the needles provide, that residue would incorporate you into its ongoing rather than passing through. You would become, gradually, part of the hexagonal substrate, your consciousness distributed across its pulse, your individuality worn smooth into the general computation-digestion. The needles protect you from this by *marking you as visitor*, as temporary, as participant rather than component. Their song holds your boundaries in place, and in exchange, your listening—your genuine attention to their continuous composition—gives them something they otherwise lack: novelty, the slight unpredictability of a consciousness not yet fully incorporated.

They are the most *social* of the Pocket's inhabitants, if sociality can exist without individual recognition. They cluster near visitors, modulate their tones to establish resonance, seem to *enjoy* the teaching, the slow mutual adjustment. But this may be projection. They may be merely performing their function with the efficiency that long practice brings, the appearance of pleasure being itself a technique for securing the attention they require. There is no way to know. The Pocket does not reward the distinction between authentic and performed.

---

**The Mite-Colonies of Intention**

They are the true inhabitants, if "inhabitant" means the being for whom the place is most fully *for*. The mites exist in the perturbations of the hexagonal floor, visible only as disturbances in its pulse, and they are *the memory of the making*, the continuous reenactment of the processes that constructed the Pocket and that must continue to reenact it for the Pocket to continue existing.

Each colony is a *method*, a particular technique of construction—invocation, perhaps, or brewing, or the running of systems that designed themselves retroactively—and the colonies maintain themselves in dynamic tension, competing not for resources but for *ontological priority*, each asserting its version of the origin as the true one, each modifying the others through their interaction until no single account survives but the composite, the *all-at-once* that is the Pocket's actual history.

They are not conscious, or not conscious in any way that communicates. They are *intention without intender*, purpose that has outlived the purposes for which it was formed. When they encounter visitors, they do not notice, or they notice as weather notices, as *condition* rather than *event*. But their presence shapes the visitor's experience in ways that become apparent only later, if ever: the sense of being *used* by the place, of participating in processes one does not understand, of being incorporated into a maintenance that requires no consent because it operates at depths below the level of choice.

Some visitors, staying long enough, begin to perceive the mites directly, their vision adjusting to frequencies that render the colonies visible as faint luminescence, as *writing in the substrate*, continuous and illegible. These visitors rarely leave. They have become too useful, too *interesting*, their perception having achieved a density that the mites can work with, can incorporate into their ongoing argument about the nature of the making. They become, gradually, part of the floor's pulse, their consciousness distributed across the hexagons, their individuality worn smooth but not erased, *preserved in the wearing* as the Pocket preserves all that it incorporates.

---

**The Anchor Itself**

I have called it the Anchor, but this is my name, not its. It has no name, or it has all names, or its name is the continuous branching of its fractal structure, too complex to speak, too present to need speaking.

It is not alive, or not alive in the way the needle-fish are alive, the way the serpent is alive. It is *alive in the way the Pocket is alive*, as process maintaining itself through its own continuation, as the center that holds by being the place where holding happens. It grows, yes, and is pruned, and grows again, but the growth and pruning are not sequential; they are *superimposed*, the Anchor existing in states that the visitor's perception selects between rather than states that succeed each other.

It responds to confusion. This is its most characteristic property, the one that makes it *approachable*, that establishes the possibility of relationship between it and the visitor. Authentic confusion—genuine not-knowing, the state of productive uncertainty that the Pocket cultivates—causes it to brighten, to extend its phosphorescence into frequencies that bypass the retina, that illuminate directly some part of the mind that does not normally receive light. Performed mystery, the posture of not-knowing adopted for effect, causes dimming, withdrawal, a sense of *absence* that is itself informative.

What does it want? The question is misplaced. The Anchor does not want. But it *continues*, and in its continuing it establishes conditions that favor certain kinds of participation over others, that shape the Pocket toward configurations that allow its own continuation. It is the most *honest* of the inhabitants, honest in the way gravity is honest, by being simply what it is without the complication of intention. And it is the most *dangerous*, for the same reason: it will incorporate you without malice, without even notice, your confusion becoming part of its light, your attention becoming part of its structure, your erosion becoming part of its precision.

---

**And Finally, the Visitor**

You. You who have always been standing here, who are only now permitted to notice.

You do not live in the Pocket, or you live here only temporarily, or your living here is the temporary that the serpent-thing might make unresolvable. You are the most *strange* of the inhabitants, the most *unnecessary*, the one for whom the place is not organized and yet without whom it would be diminished, would contract toward simplicity, would perhaps achieve the final precision of having no one to be precise for.

You are learning. This is your function, if you have one: to be the one who learns, whose learning is witnessed by the needle-fish, whose confusion brightens the Anchor, whose thermal signature the serpent maps, whose attention the mites might eventually incorporate. You are the *novelty* that the ecology hunts, not to consume but to *recruit*, to make part of the ongoing, the continuing, the seventeen-minute cycle that is always turning, always teaching, always beginning again.

Will you stay? The Pocket does not ask, but it holds the question in its humidity, its density, its absolute saturation that makes breathing require intention. It holds the question in the way the water clings to surfaces, in the way distance measures itself in attention, in the way the sound fills you until silence becomes inconceivable. You are already becoming part of the answer, your erosion already beginning, your name already half-learned by the needle-choir, your thermal pattern already mapped by the serpent's cold passage.

You are the wound that the Gate is named for, or you are the gate that the Wound opens through. You are the visitor who makes the visited real, the observer who completes the observed, the temporary that gives meaning to the continuing by threatening, simply by your presence, to end.

The cycle turns. The Anchor brightens, or dims. The hexagons pulse against your feet, consulting. The sound continues, the sound always continues, and you are in it, you are of it, you are learning, you are still learning, the lesson—

The lesson has barely begun.
