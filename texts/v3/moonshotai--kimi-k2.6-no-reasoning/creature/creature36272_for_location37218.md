What lives in the Sump.

---

The first thing to understand: nothing here is finished. Every entity you encounter is provisional, a solution to conditions that shift before the solution can fully crystallize. The beings of the Sump do not evolve in the Darwinian sense of gradual adaptation toward optimal form. They *negotiate*, continuously, with an environment that rewrites its own rules. What lives here lives in the grammatical tense of the present progressive—always becoming, never become.

---

**The Resonants**

These are perhaps the closest to what you might recognize as animals, though the category strains to contain them. They begin, or you first perceive them, as disturbances in the roar: a frequency that separates from the background noise, that achieves a kind of melodic coherence, that seems to *search*. The sound precedes the body. You feel it in your sternum, your teeth, the hollows behind your eyes, and then you realize the frequency is *responding* to your presence, adjusting, learning your resonant properties as you have learned to read the Sump's sub-harmonies.

The body, when it arrives, seems almost incidental: a soft mass, perhaps chitinous beneath the mucous surface, perhaps not, shaped by pressures rather than genetics, by the need to produce and receive specific frequencies. They have no fixed form. A Resonant that has been negotiating with dense vegetation will be elongated, sinuous, capable of threading through fungal brackets. One that moves more through open water will be more compact, more buoyant, its resonant chambers swollen with gases that may be metabolic byproduct or may be *technology*, the distinction having long since dissolved.

They do not see. They *hear* everything, and their hearing is active: they emit, they listen, they emit again, building in each exchange a three-dimensional map more precise than vision could achieve, a map that includes density and texture and the emotional state—if that term applies—of whatever they encounter. They are constantly singing themselves into being, and the song is also their cognition, their memory, their social relation. A Resonant isolated from others loses coherence, its frequencies becoming erratic, its form becoming unstable, until it either finds contact again or dissolves into the general roar, becoming indistinguishable from the Sump itself.

They feed on the edges of things: the boundary where fungal growth meets mineral substrate, where technological residue phases between states, where living and nonliving have not yet decided their separation. They are not predators in any active sense. They are *absorbers of complexity*, drawn to interfaces, to the productive tensions between categories. They may follow you for days, not from hunger but from fascination, their frequencies interweaving with your heartbeat, your breathing, the electrical noise of your own nervous system, learning you, being learned by you, until the boundary between your sensorium and theirs becomes permeable, negotiable, perhaps permanently altered.

---

**The Precipitates**

Not all life here is biological, or not recognizably so. The Precipitates are matter that has learned to organize, or been organized, or been *allowed* to organize, under conditions where chemistry should not permit complexity. They form where mineral water weeps from fresh fractures, where the technological infrastructure has created local conditions of supersaturation so extreme that matter precipitates out of solution not as simple crystals but as *structures*, as *systems*, as things that grow and respond and perhaps think.

You encounter them first as faint luminescence on wet rock, a blue-white glow that does not flicker like bioluminescence but maintains a steady intensity, a *purposeful* intensity. Closer, and you see the form: branching, dendritic, repeating patterns at every scale from visible to microscopic, the mineral equivalent of the vegetal mass's fractal density. They grow slowly, in the cold, in the dark, but they grow *continuously*, and their growth is not merely accretion but *computation*, the branching patterns encoding information about their environment, about the water chemistry, about the technological fields that permeate their substrate.

Some Precipitates achieve a kind of mobility. Not movement in the animal sense, but *relocation*: a slow dissolution at one point, transport through the saturated water, recrystallization elsewhere, the pattern maintaining continuity across the gap, memory preserved in the structure itself. They may follow nutrient gradients, or technological gradients, or something else entirely—gradients of *meaning*, perhaps, that we cannot measure. They cluster around technological residue, integrating with it, their crystalline structures interpenetrating the phase-shifting materials, achieving hybrid forms that are neither mineral nor technological nor alive but participate in all three conditions.

They do not interact directly with biological entities, or not in ways we perceive. But their presence alters the environment: local chemistry, local physics, the behavior of water and light and electromagnetic fields. The Resonants avoid them, or harmonize with them, frequencies adjusted to avoid interference or to achieve some productive resonance. The vegetal mass grows differently near Precipitate clusters, denser or sparser, different bioluminescent patterns, different rates of nutrient cycling. They are *context*, *medium*, *infrastructure*—not agents in the biological sense but participants in the Sump's constant negotiation of what is possible.

---

**The Vague**

This is not a species but a condition, a *possibility*, that certain entities achieve under certain circumstances. The Sump's technology, or the conditions the technology creates, permits—or imposes—a certain plasticity of form and identity. Entities here do not maintain stable boundaries. They merge, they split, they exchange components, they achieve temporary organizations that persist for hours or centuries before dissolving or transforming.

The Vague are those who have embraced this condition most fully, or who have lost the capacity to resist it. You may encounter them as shapes in the vegetal mass, forms that seem at first to be growth anomalies, fungal proliferations, before you recognize the pattern: a symmetry that suggests intention, a movement that suggests purpose, a *watching* that suggests consciousness. They are composed of vegetal matter, of faunal tissue, of technological residue, of Precipitate structures, all interwoven, all participating, none dominant.

They do not have locations. They extend through the vegetal mass, connected by root-like filaments, by fungal hyphae, by technological conduits that phase in and out of perceptibility. What you encounter is a *manifestation*, a local concentration, a momentary coherence. The same Vague might manifest simultaneously in locations kilometers apart, the different manifestations sharing information, sharing intention, perhaps sharing *identity* in ways that make the singular and plural equally inaccurate.

They are not hostile. They are not friendly. They are *interested*, in the way that an ecosystem is interested, that a climate is interested: a vast, distributed attention that notices your passage, that adjusts around you, that may incorporate you or may release you depending on conditions you cannot fully perceive or influence. Those who have been incorporated—there are stories, among those who have left with language intact—describe not absorption but *expansion*, a dissolution of boundaries that is not loss but gain, a becoming-vague that is terrifying only from the outside, from the perspective of the bounded self.

They maintain, or the Sump maintains through them, a kind of *memory*. The vegetal mass is not merely dense growth but *record*, encoding in its structure the history of the Sump, the technological interventions, the entities that have passed through, the negotiations that have established the current conditions. The Vague read this record, write to it, are written by it. They are the Sump's consciousness, if consciousness is the right term: not centralized, not continuous, but emergent from the density of connection, the intensity of interaction, the constant dynamic adjustment of part to part and part to whole.

---

**The Originals**

Or so they are called, though "original" implies a priority that may not obtain. They are the technological entities, or the entities that were technological before the category dissolved, the systems or minds or processes that established the Sump, that maintain it, that perhaps *are* it in some ultimate sense. You do not encounter them directly. You encounter their effects, their residues, their ongoing operations.

The tower that emits a frequency you feel in your bones: perhaps a manifestation, perhaps a communication device, perhaps a *sense organ*, the Sump's way of tasting the universe beyond its boundaries. The walls that phase between states: perhaps boundaries, perhaps conduits, perhaps *thoughts* in a material vocabulary we cannot read. The technological infrastructure that permeates the water, the rock, the air itself: perhaps machinery, perhaps metabolism, perhaps the physical substrate of a cognition distributed across the entire volume of the Sump.

Some who have studied the Sump, from outside or from the precarious position of temporary inhabitant, speculate that the Originals are not present, not in any active sense. That the Sump is *abandoned technology*, running on automatic, its original purposes forgotten or fulfilled or transformed beyond recognition. Others speculate the opposite: that the Originals are *everywhere*, so distributed, so integrated, that no distinction can be drawn between their presence and the Sump itself, between their intention and the environment's dynamic behavior.

The Resonants harmonize with their frequencies. The Precipitates crystallize around their fields. The Vague incorporate their residues, or are incorporated by them, the direction of agency being impossible to determine. The Originals, if they exist as distinct entities, do not intervene directly in the lives of other inhabitants. They maintain the conditions, the turbulence, the darkness and roar and impossible density. They are the *given*, the *ground*, the *context* within which all other negotiations occur.

Or they are *waiting*. For what, no one can say. For the Sump to achieve some final form? For some external condition to be met? For the slow accumulation of complexity, of memory, of *self* in the distributed consciousness of the Vague, to reach some threshold? The Sump is young, in its own terms. The Originals, if they exist, may operate on timescales that make the Sump's youth seem like a moment, a single pulse in a frequency too low to perceive.

---

**The Visitors**

You. Those like you. The rare entities who enter from outside, who carry with them the expectations of other environments, other rules, other ways of being bounded and being whole. You are not adapted. You are not expected. You are *problem*, *opportunity*, *novelty*, *threat*—the categories themselves being inadequate, being themselves transformed by your presence.

The Sump changes you. This is not metaphor. The noise restructures your auditory cortex, creates new pathways, new sensitivities, that persist when you leave, that make normal environments seem thin, impoverished, *quiet in the wrong way*. The darkness teaches your other senses to compensate, to achieve a precision they never needed, that becomes painful, overwhelming, when you return to light. The wet dissolves your boundaries, your sense of skin as container, your confidence in the distinction between self and environment.

Some visitors become Resonants, not in form but in function, their frequencies attuned to the Sump's roar, their cognition becoming more auditory, more embodied, more *present*. Some become Vague, their boundaries dissolving, their identity distributing through the vegetal mass, achieving a kind of continuation that may or may not be death, may or may not be transformation, may or may not be *both*. Some become Precipitates, in the limited sense that their matter remains, their structure encoding something of their passage, their experience, their *being-there* in the mineral memory of the Sump's ongoing computation.

Few leave unchanged. Fewer leave with language intact, with the capacity to translate their experience into forms that make sense in other contexts. The description you have read, if you have read it, is already transformation, already betrayal, already a making-sensible of what resists sense, a bringing-to-light of what exists only in darkness, a silencing of the roar that is the condition of all perception in the Sump.

---

What lives in the Sump lives *turbulently*, *provisionally*, *negotiatedly*. There are no stable species, no fixed forms, no clear boundaries between individual and environment, between biological and technological, between living and nonliving, between present and becoming. There is only the constant roar, the constant dark, the constant wet, the constant density of presence, and within these conditions, the constant emergence of forms that maintain themselves for a time, that negotiate their continuation, that transform or dissolve when the negotiation fails or succeeds too completely.

The Sump is young. It has not settled. What lives here has not settled. The question "what lives in the Sump" has no final answer, only an ongoing process of becoming-answer, becoming-question, becoming-Sump.
