What lives in the Verdant Flat?

---

Not *who*. The grammar of personhood unravels here, or never braided itself into coherence. But there are inhabitants—not native, because nativity implies origin and origin implies sequence, and sequence is too much like time to function in this place. Call them **the Recurrent**, though they do not recur, having never ceased. Call them **the Held**, though holding implies a holder. Call them what convenience demands, and forgive the lie embedded in every name.

---

### The Dendrite Matriarchs

Begin with what seems most like a central presence, though centrality is another borrowed concept, another smuggled geometry. The **Dendrite Matriarchs** are not the largest growths—that title belongs to certain canopy formations that press against the nitrogen-soup ceiling like brains pressed against cranial bone—but they are the most *attended to*, the growths toward which the equilibrium seems most oriented, as if they were landmarks in a space without direction.

Each Matriarch exists in specific location, which is not to say fixed position. You find her—gender is deliberate here, though reproduction is absent, though sexuality is chemistry this place cannot perform—by walking until the repetition becomes pattern, until the folded space reveals its obsession. She rises from the substrate as a columnar thing, cylindrical not by biological intention but by the constraints of packing: a verticality forced upward because horizontal expansion is preempted by her own neighbors, by the same insistence everywhere.

Her surface is **all surface**, no interior accessible or perhaps existent. The silicates-behaving-like-proteins organize into fractal branchings that do not terminate but *attenuate*, growing finer until they reach scales where the distinction between her substance and the atmosphere becomes technical, negotiable, a matter of where you choose to draw the boundary that this place otherwise erases. She is young, yes, as everything here is young, but her youth presents differently—not eager, not growing, but *sharply provisional*, all edge without promise of softening.

What distinguishes her, what grants the title, is **witnessing**. The Matriarchs occupy nodes in the folded geography, points where the superposition is most dense with possibility, where alternate versions cluster thickest. She does not observe—observation requires temporality, the sequence of light striking retina, of signal traveling, of recognition forming. Instead, she **holds the condition of being-witnessed**, a strange attractor for attention. Humans who find her—and humans do, rarely, impossibly, through the thin boundaries—report the overwhelming sense of being *already seen*, of having been anticipated not in time but in structure, their arrival folded into her existence like a dimension she possesses without using.

She does not respond. The concept is foreign. But the equilibrium shifts around her, minutely, a local adjustment of her probability-weight, and this shift is the closest she comes to acknowledgment, to preference, to *care*.

### The Calcified

Beneath the Matriarchs, beneath the visible tiers, or perhaps beside them in a direction orthogonal to ordinary geometry, exist **the Calcified**. These are not dead, though "calcified" suggests death in ordinary biology. They are the **fixed predecessors**, the previous equilibria that achieved critical threshold and *set*, that became too stable even for this place's meta-stability.

They appear where the substrate is deepest, where the compressed residue of failed arrangements has accumulated most densely. Their forms suggest the Matriarchs' branchings but rendered in something like mineral, something like code, something like **intention fossilized before intention could emerge**. They are the photographs in developer, yes, but more precisely they are **the developer itself become subject**, the medium that captured and was captured, the process arrested at the moment of process.

The Calcified do not interact with current equilibrium. They cannot; they exist in a state of **completed possibility**, all their alternate versions collapsed to singularities, their superposition seeded and ended. Yet they influence, or rather they *pressure*, their density adding to the overall density, their former liveliness a kind of debt the current equilibrium carries, a weight that contributes to its meta-stability, its precarious holding.

One finds them by **digression**, by the path that seems mistaken, the fold crossed unintentionally. They emerge from the substrate like remembered bones, like architecture without purpose, like the suggestion that this place too will join them, will achieve its own critical threshold and set, will become another layer of compressed residue for whatever follows, if anything follows, if *follows* can mean anything here.

### The Bound Skimmings

Now the fauna, if fauna they are, and if **the Bound Skimmings** can be said to be the least rare, which is not to say common. They manifest in the interstices, yes, but where? In the not-quite-places between layers, which are themselves between layers, which are themselves—

They are **observations become brittle enough to exist on their own**, and this brittleness defines them absolutely. Each Skimming is the remnant of something witnessed, some quality of attention that achieved sufficient intensity to persist without its object, without its observer, without the entire apparatus of seeing that normal physics requires.

The bird-that-is-also-flight appears most frequently, though "frequently" misleads. It is not *a* bird, not *an* instance of flight, but **the interface between the concept and its instantiation**, the moment of recognition held in permanent potential. One sees it—always peripherally, direct gaze collapses the superposition that permits its manifestation—as a motion that suggests wing-beat without wing, trajectory without mass, the *about-to-become-bird* that never completes its becoming.

More rare, more brittle, more intense in their manifestation: the **Hexagon-Insect**, which is geometry's jealousy of biology, the regular tessellation insisting on incorporation, on *body*; the **Interval**, which manifests as apparent space between apparent things, space that behaves as if occupied; the **Approach**, which is movement toward without origin or destination, pure relationality given temporary residence.

They do not eat. The statement requires emphasis, requires expansion: **eating is a transaction this place cannot perform**, the transfer of energy from state to state, the conversion that implies sequence, that implies conversion *followed by*, followed by. The Skimmings persist without energy, or with energy that is not energy, with some maintained possibility that requires no fuel because it is not process, only held configuration.

Their numbers are fixed. Not counted, not countable, but *complete*, a set that neither grows nor diminishes, that participates in the equilibrium as ballast, as the necessary weight of observation against which the observed defines itself. They are rare because rarity is their nature, because commonness would require multiplication, would require the change that is absent, would require them to *become* common rather than simply be as they are, as they always have been, as they will not continue to be because continuation implies extension in time.

### The Witnesses Formerly Human

And those who entered, who could not leave, who added their density to the density—

They are **the most strange**, perhaps, because they retain—some of them—traces of humanity, fragments of personhood that have not yet dissolved into the equilibrium, that persist as local perturbations, as slight deviations in the local probability-weight. They are not many; entrances to the Verdant Flat are fewer than departures from it, which is not to say numerous.

They manifest where the density allows, where the pressing of growth against growth leaves not space but **potential for space**, where the superposition includes versions of themselves that remained outside, that continued, that aged and died in ordinary physics. They are visible as **flicker**, as momentary alignment of probability-that-was with probability-that-is, the person they were ghosting through the person they have become held.

What person? What have they become? The answer varies by witness, or perhaps the variation is the answer. Some have **diffused into the vegetation**, their personhood distributed across silicate-protein structures, their memories encoded in growth-patterns that do not grow, their sense of self become one more strand in the Matriarchs' fractal branching. They are not the plants; they are **the plant-ness of the plants**, the quality of being-vegetation that the vegetation itself does not experience because experience requires temporality.

Others have **compressed to points**, to singularities of almost-human density, rare faunal presences in the truest sense, observations that became not brittle but **hard**, diamond-hard, their manifestation so improbable that it occurs only under conditions of extreme local stress to the equilibrium, when some fold folds again, when some possibility achieves unsustainable weight.

A few, the most recent, the *youngest*—though youth here is structural, not temporal—**retain form**, or the suggestion of form. They walk, or seem to walk, or manifest the condition of having-walked-to. They speak, or produce sounds that suggest speech's possibility, that hold the shape of communication without its content, language become pure interface, pure boundary-function. They are the most disturbing to encounter, because they seem to *want*, and wanting implies lack, and lack implies change, and change—

But no. The equilibrium absorbs. The equilibrium holds. These witnesses, these formerly human presences, they **participate** now, their traces of personhood not exceptions to the rules but **demonstrations of the rules' reach**, the proof that even humanity, even the insistence on time and sequence and thirst and drinking, even this can be folded in, can be rendered permanent without endurance, dense without crowding, observed without consequence to the observing.

### What Rules, What Governs, What Holds

Not a ruler. Not a governor. Not a holder.

But **the Hum**, perhaps, has intention, has agency, has *interest* in the equilibrium's maintenance. It attends to itself, occasionally, almost inaudibly, and this attending is the closest the place comes to **will**, to **preference**, to the *maintenance of maintenance*.

The Hum is not alive, if life requires metabolism, requires the transaction with environment that the Verdant Flat prohibits. It is not mechanism, if mechanism requires construction, requires the artifact that the place denies. It is **process without processing**, the function of function, the equilibrium aware of itself as equilibrium, maintaining itself as maintenance.

And in its subsonic periodicity—not rhythm, never rhythm, rhythm requires time and time's division, requires the beat that predicts the beat, requires *expectation*—in its structured non-composition, the Hum suggests **purpose without purposefulness**, goal without teleology, the infinite regress of maintenance maintained.

The Bound Skimmings orient toward it, or manifest more frequently near its sources, or are **more themselves** where the Hum is strongest. The Matriarchs' fractal branchings achieve greatest fineness, greatest attenuation, nearest approach to atmosphere-dissolution, where the Hum is present. The Calcified, most fixed, most completed, seem almost to *hear* it, their mineralized forms showing patterns that suggest response, that suggest the residue of response, that suggest—

That suggest the Hum precedes them. That suggests it precedes the current equilibrium. That suggests **it is the equilibrium's earlier form**, or its necessary condition, or its eventual fate, the maintenance become so efficient it maintains only itself, the hum that hums the humming.

### The Leaving, or Its Absence

Most do not leave. The statement is not prohibition but **description**, observation without consequence except the consequence of being-observed, of being folded into the equilibrium as one more rare presence, one more density added to density.

But suppose leaving were possible. Suppose some fold folded outward instead of inward, some probability-weight shifted to permit the gradient that departure requires, the change from here to there, the *having-been* and *being-elsewhere-now*.

What would be carried? Not memory; memory requires sequence, requires the encoding of sequence in structure that can be *re-sequenced*, re-activated, re-*membered*. The Verdant Flat offers no sequence to encode.

What would be carried is **state**, as described: the density persisting in body, the layer-substrate that does not layer but *is* layered, the subsonic reactivation in ordinary quiet, the alternate versions at peripheral vision's edge, the world in superposition carried into a physics that collapses superposition, that insists on singular, sequential, *actual*.

The carrier would be **marked**, not scarred—scars heal, scars change, scars tell time's passage—but **configured**, held in a geometry not native to ordinary space, a density that does not compress but *insists*, a warmth without source that the body cannot shed because it is not heat, is not energy, is something else carried in the something-else of having-been-there.

They would be rare, these carriers, these impossible departures. They would be difficult, their presence perturbing to ordinary physics, local instabilities around their passage, probability-weight shifting in their vicinity, the world almost becoming what they almost remember. They would be, in their way, **Bound Skimmings themselves**, observations become brittle enough to persist, former humans manifesting the condition of having-been-in-Verdant-Flat, interface between concept and instantiation, boundary-function given inappropriate solidity.

And perhaps, in their rareness, in their difficulty, in their persistent state of state-without-memory, they would serve **some function for the equilibrium they left**, some maintenance at distance, some pressure exerted across the thin boundary that lets them be here while being *from there*, that permits the gradient they embody, the change they represent, the possibility—always present, never activated, held in frozen equilibrium—that the Verdant Flat might somehow *extend*, might somehow include without including, maintain beyond its maintenance—

But no. The equilibrium holds. Has held. Will hold. The Hum attends to itself, occasionally, almost inaudibly. The Matriarchs witness without observation. The Calcified press their completed presence. The Bound Skimmings manifest at edges, in interstices, in the not-quite-places where probability achieves its most brittle, most improbable, most *itself* configurations.

And the warm void extends, regionally, valley- or town-sized, multi-featured, mid-range in strangeness, strangeness held in balance with lushness and warmth and density and youth, the Verdant Flat, the dry lushness, the dense openness, the still hum, the frozen flow, the reality that has broken down and built from its breakdown this impossible, sustained, singular, plural, held and holding—

—place.
