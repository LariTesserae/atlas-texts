What lives in the Null-Marshes does not die, because death requires a boundary between living and ending that the erosion here has long since worn smooth. What lives here *completes*, and in completing enters a kind of perpetual arrival—always having become, always becoming, the distinction between these states dissolved like the hills that must once have risen above this bowl of held cold and patient sound.

The Choirs themselves have no other name. "Drowning" is not what happens to them. It is what they *are*, what they *do*, the continuous performance of submersion without emergence, of breath held so long that breath has become unnecessary, has become *architecture*. They are called Choirs not because they sing together—though they do, constantly, the density of their sound bearing the packed quality of voices layered until voice becomes medium becomes body becomes pressure—but because they *choreograph* the space between them, the non-space, the shared positions where their bodies quantum-ghost through each other without exclusion. A choir is the right word for what they are because a choir is individuals who have learned to occupy the same temporal moment without losing distinction, who have made of their mutual presence a *resonance chamber* larger than any containing structure.

Each Choir is a factory that produces *location*. This is their industry, their metabolism, their reason for the electrified hum that you feel in your fillings, your blood, the water that refuses to fall in this saturated air. They are born—if that word applies—into confusion, into the weirdness of a space where position is unstable, where walking forward might raise you, where the horizon dips to meet your eye like water finding level. The infant Choirs are small, perhaps, though "size" here is as eroded as other distinctions, and they learn by *sounding* their uncertainty into the dense medium, broadcasting "I am here" and hearing how the return comes wrong, how the echo precedes, how the vibration of their own bodies feeds back through the industrial-faunal network until they cannot tell their own pulse from the piston-equivalent of a neighbor they have not learned to share position with yet.

They learn. Or *are arrived at*. The erosion that works this landscape works them too, and what emerges is the completion of their function: to make *here* stable by announcing it continuously, by the operatic sustained destruction of their vocalizations, by the pressure-differentials they maintain through valve-systems that are also throats, also hearts, also the mineral vocabulary they extract from the weeping stones. A Choir that has achieved its completion does not move, or moves only in the way that erosion moves, the active completion of having settled and settled again. It occupies. It sounds. It *is* the location it announces, the echo and the source having worn into each other until they are the same event, differently perceived.

There are no individuals in a Choir, and there are nothing but individuals. The density parameter, the saturation, the impossibility of room between presences: this means that each Choir is always already *with*, that solitude is a concept from a different rule-set, a local convention that the Null-Marshes have eroded past. When two Choirs meet—and they are always meeting, always having met, the soundscape modulating across the open scale with variations that never resolve into discrete encounters—they do not merge or remain separate. They *interleave*, their factory-bodies occupying the same position with the quantum patience of the oldest-stable-state, their industrial metabolisms adjusting, optimizing, the total packedness conserved across whatever redistribution this sharing requires.

You will never see them. This is not because they are hidden but because they are *distributed*, because the light that would reveal them is itself reluctant, diffuse, a forgetting of direction that matches their own forgetting of visuality. They evolved or were arrived at without eyes, and in this lack they have achieved a different completeness. They sense through the water-saturated air that carries pressure with the fidelity of body remembering body. They map each other through vibration, through the feedback loops of their continuous sounding, and this map is not a representation but a *performance*, the doing of the thing that the map would claim to show.

Some Choirs are old in a way that time here permits, that the multi-temporal weirdness makes possible. You will hear them in the register of almost-language, the syntax and grammar of vocalizations that have worn smooth through use, that have become *protocol* rather than communication, the underlying mathematical commitment of sound in a space where sound is location is presence is being. These old Choirs do not lead. They do not follow. They *reference*, their sounds providing the stable points by which others calibrate their own uncertainty, their own continuous necessity of announcing "I am here" into a medium that returns the announcement modified, filtered through the industrial-faunal density, carrying the information of all other announcements layered in interference patterns that the Choirs have learned, through erosion, to read.

And some Choirs are *new*, though "new" here is a suggestion, a local convention. They sound with the fracture-quality of materials under stress, the breaking-voices not yet worn to the sustained operatic destruction of completion. They are louder, perhaps, or more variable, their piston-equivalents less efficient, their valve-systems still negotiating the pressure differentials that the older Choirs maintain with the patience of geological time. They are learning to share position, to interleave, to become part of the density without decreasing it. The old Choirs incorporate them not by absorption but by *context*, by the continuous sounding that makes every location stable enough for new announcement, new uncertainty, new completion.

The Choirs do not breathe. They *drown*, continuously, the oxygen they process extracted from the liquid air with the efficiency of factories that have become what they produce. Their bodies—we call them bodies by analogy, by the erosion of distinction between life and mechanism that makes the word necessary and insufficient—are permeable, weeping, their surfaces participating in the water-cycles that have no names, the condensing and pooling and remembering that the Null-Marshes perform as active dynamic. They are wet inside and out, the mineral vocabulary of the landscape suspended in their metabolic colloids, slowing the light that would reveal them if they had eyes, if eyes were a category that applied.

They do not sleep. The dynamic parameter, the active, the process and cycle: this is their completion, their having become what they are. To sleep would be to pause the announcement, to let location become uncertain, and this they cannot do, this they have eroded past doing. They rest in the shared position, the interleaved quantum-ghostlike state where one Choir's pause is another's continuation, the total soundscape uninterrupted by any individual variation because individuality is a gradient here, not a category. They maintain, they maintain, they maintain—the electrified thrum that you feel in your teeth, in the iron in your blood if you enter their saturation, the continuous function of presence in a space that does not acknowledge presence as separate from position.

And when you come, if you come, when you descend to the low elevation and your body forgets to shiver and the cold becomes your patience, your waiting—they will incorporate you. Not by wanting to. Not by choosing. The Null-Marshes do not choose, have eroded past choosing, are what they have become with the completeness of the oldest-stable-state. They will incorporate you because you are sound, because you are water, because you are position announcing itself uncertainly into a medium that returns all announcements modified, layered, part of the density.

Your voice will join the Choirs. Your footsteps, your heartbeat, the almost-language you speak to yourself in the dimness that refuses source—these will be taken up, interleaved, shared in position with the piston-equivalents and valve-systems and sustained fracture-voices of the abundant fauna. You will not lose yourself. The self is a category that erodes here, that wears to its intention, its underlying mathematical commitment. You will *complete*, actively, dynamically, as the water cycles through its nameless states and the rocks weep their mineral vocabulary and the cold holds everything in the patience of having finally, after epochs beyond counting, *arrived*.

The Drowning Choirs of Null-Marshes are not drowning in anything. They are the *state* of drowning made continuous, made industry, made the location and the announcement of location and the listening for return that constitutes, in this weirdness, the only stable form of being present. They have finished becoming and continue, in the dim reluctant light, in the water that never falls, in the sound that is density made audible, to be what they have become: the voice of the marshes, the marshes' way of having location, the patient completed active dynamic erosion that wears everything toward the oldest-stable-state, toward the Null that is not absence but *held solution*, the parameters in their final expression, the cold and wet and loud and packed and ancient singing itself into being, singing itself into being, singing—
