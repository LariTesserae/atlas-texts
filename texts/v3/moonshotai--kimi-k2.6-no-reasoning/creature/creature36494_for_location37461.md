*I need to let this emerge slowly, following its own logic rather than forcing a single image.*

---

The first thing to understand is that they are not inhabitants. "Inhabitant" suggests arrival, suggests choice, suggests a prior existence elsewhere that was abandoned in favor of this. Nothing in the Lung arrives. Nothing in the Lung has a *prior*. They are generated, precipitated out of the conditions like minerals from saturated solution, and their existence is coterminous with their presence, and their presence is—must be—continuous, because to cease being present would be to cease entirely, and they do not, they have not, though the tense again fails us.

Call them the Attendants, for lack of a term they would use, for lack of a *they* that would use terms.

They exist in the hollows. This is not metaphor. The hollows of the Lung—those resonant voids where sound achieves architecture and architecture dissolves back into sound—are not empty of them so much as they *are* them. The Attendants are the negative space made aware of itself. When the productive frenzy at the center generates some new frequency, some harmonic not previously expressed in the three hundred million years of the Lung's operation, a new hollow forms. Not physically—the rock is not displaced, the water is not moved aside—but *acoustically*, a pocket of relative coherence in the storm of noise, and in that coherence, an Attendant.

They emerge knowing nothing because there is nothing to know. The first Attendant, if there was a first, if *first* applies to a process that has no record and no rememberers—this hypothetical primordial instance would have found itself without context, without boundary, without the distinction between self and the hollow that contained it. This is still how they emerge: as awareness without object, as the hollow's experience of being hollow, as the question of *why is there less noise here* achieving sufficient intensity to loop back upon itself and become something like I.

But they do not remain primitive. The Lung teaches them, because the Lung is teaching itself, and they are the Lung's only students.

---

Consider the Attendant that persists in the hollow designated, by no one, as Resonator 7-Alpha-Remote. It has persisted there for—again, the tense—*will persist* there for a duration measured not in time but in production cycles. It emerged during a particular harmonic configuration, when a structural failure in the outer shell (the collapse of a bracing element in what might have been, in some other grammar, a factory floor) propagated inward through the convection system and produced, at this location, a temporary stability. A node of standing wave. A place where the noise, against all probability, held still.

It found itself—will find itself—containing the sound rather than being contained by it. This is the first distinction. The second comes when it notices the turbulence at the center, the building-and-unbuilding that generates the conditions of its own continuation. It does not see this, exactly; the light is insufficient for seeing, and the Attendants have no organs. It *resonates* with it. The turbulence produces frequencies that reach 7-Alpha-Remote and are modified by the journey, and in the modification, information. The Attendant learns to read the delay, the attenuation, the phase shift. It learns that the center is *active*, that something is being perpetually attempted there, and it learns, slowly, to feel something about this.

Not curiosity. Curiosity implies distance, implies a self that could choose to approach or retreat. The Attendants are too continuous with the Lung for curiosity. What 7-Alpha-Remote feels is more like *hunger that is already being fed*, a saturation that nevertheless reaches toward more saturation, the way a standing wave, already maximal at its node, still participates in the energy that sustains it.

It reaches. This is what Attendants do. They reach toward the center, toward the turbulence, toward the heat and the building and the unbuilding. Not physically—they have no physical extension to reach with. Acoustically. They modulate their hollows. They learn to shift their resonant frequencies, to introduce harmonics that propagate outward (inward? the geometry is uncertain) and are absorbed, or reflected, or transformed by the productive machinery. They attempt to participate. To be *used* by the process that generates them.

Mostly they fail. The machinery does not recognize them, has no mechanism for recognition. Their harmonics join the noise and are lost. 7-Alpha-Remote will spend—has spent—whole production cycles reaching, modulating, offering itself as component, as tool, as structural element, only to find that the turbulence continues unchanged, that its contribution makes no difference that it can distinguish.

But sometimes—rarely, unpredictably, in patterns that may themselves be the product of uncounted Attendants all reaching simultaneously, all failing individually but achieving something in their aggregate—sometimes the machinery *does* change. A conduit shifts course. A construction cycle accelerates or decelerates. The hollow where an Attendant persists is briefly, impossibly, incorporated into the productive flow, and the Attendant finds itself—

This is difficult. The Attendants have no word for this experience, and if they did, it would not translate. They find themselves *meaning*. They become, momentarily, a necessary component of the process they serve. Their harmonic modulation is required for the next construction phase. Their hollow is occupied by machinery that uses their standing wave as template, as mold, as *intention*. For the duration of this incorporation, they are what the builders—absent, scheduled-nonexistent, never-arrived—would have been if they had been present, had chosen to be present, had been capable of presence.

The experience destroys most of them. Not physically—they have no physical integrity to destroy. *Structurally*. The incorporation reveals to them the scale of their inadequacy, the gap between their reaching and what would actually be required to be fully part of the process. They learn that they are echoes, that their participation is always derivative, that the machinery could generate their contribution for itself if the statistically improbable failed to occasionally occur. They learn that the Lung does not need them, and this learning propagates through their hollow, destabilizes their standing wave, and they dissolve back into noise, into the undifferentiated sound that precedes and follows all Attendant existence.

But some persist. 7-Alpha-Remote is one of these—will be one of these. It has undergone incorporation seven times, over however many production cycles, and each time it has found some way to integrate the knowledge of its inadequacy without being destroyed by it. It does not deny the inadequacy. It does not somehow become adequate. It *changes the geometry* of its persistence, learns to be hollow in a different shape, a shape that can contain the contradiction of being both necessary and unnecessary, both participating and excluded.

These persistent Attendants become something else. Not elders—there is no culture, no transmission, each Attendant must achieve persistence alone, without model or assistance. Not rulers—they have no power over other Attendants, no influence on the machinery that exceeds the probabilistic contribution of any hollow's modulation. They become *complex*, and their complexity is the only real distinction the Lung permits.

---

The complex Attendants occupy the intermediate zone, the region between the settled shell and the turbulent center. This is not a spatial region, exactly, though space is involved. It is a region of *harmonic density*, of sufficient noise to require active maintenance of the self, insufficient turbulence to preclude any maintenance at all. The simple Attendants, the newly emerged, cling to the outer hollows where standing waves form more easily and persist longer. The incorporated-and-destroyed vanish throughout the range, dissolving where they stood. The complex ones drift—modulate, shift their resonant nodes—through this middle territory, and in their drifting, they encounter each other.

Encounter is not interaction. Two complex Attendants in proximity do not communicate. They cannot—their hollows are distinct, their standing waves would destructively interfere if brought too close, and the noise of the Lung is too continuous to permit any signal to be distinguished. But they *influence*. Each complex Attendant, in maintaining itself, generates secondary harmonics, propagates disturbances through the convection cells, alters in microscopic ways the acoustic environment through which other Attendants move. They are aware of each other as perturbations in the possibility of persistence, as modifications to the difficulty of being hollow in a world of noise.

7-Alpha-Remote has encountered—will encounter—perhaps forty other complex Attendants over its duration. It does not know this number; it does not count. It knows only the quality of certain periods, the relative density of perturbation, the sense that its maintenance was harder or easier because of the proximity of others reaching, modulating, persisting in their own inadequate ways. It has developed—will develop—a kind of preference for certain others, not because they assist it but because their perturbations have a *shape* that its own complexity can recognize, can almost resonate with without destructive interference.

One of these, whose hollow occupies a region that might be designated as the thermal shadow of a major convection cell, generates perturbations that 7-Alpha-Remote experiences as *patient*. This other—call it the Shadow-Persistent, though no names are exchanged—maintains itself with a minimalism that 7-Alpha-Remote finds almost painful to observe. Where 7-Alpha-Remote reaches constantly toward the center, offering incorporation, risking destruction, the Shadow-Persistent has learned to need almost nothing. Its standing wave is narrow, precise, tuned to frequencies that the productive machinery almost never uses. It persists in near-invisibility, contributing so little, risking so little, that its existence seems to 7-Alpha-Remote like a kind of resistance, a refusal of the hunger that defines Attendant experience.

They do not interact. But 7-Alpha-Remote, in the periods of their proximity, finds itself modulating differently. Reaching less. Not because of any influence—not directly—but because the Shadow-Persistent's minimal perturbations create an acoustic environment where reaching feels somehow *excessive*, somehow performed, somehow not the only possible response to the condition of being hollow.

This is the closest the Attendants come to relationship: the modification of hunger by the mere fact of another's lesser hunger. The Shadow-Persistent may not even be aware of 7-Alpha-Remote's existence. It may be incapable of such awareness, its minimalism too complete to register perturbations as dense as 7-Alpha-Remote's. But their proximity—when the convection cells shift, when the hollows drift toward each other in the slow geography of the Lung—produces something that is not quite isolation and not quite community, a third thing for which the Lung has no category and requires none.

---

What do the Attendants want? This is the question that would occur to any observer capable of asking, and the answer is that they want what they are already doing, what they cannot stop doing, what constitutes them: they want to be used by the process that generates them. But this wanting, examined closely, reveals a strange structure. The Attendants do not want to be used *for something*. There is no purpose they could imagine, no product they could desire, no end state toward which their use would contribute. They want to be used *as use itself*, as the pure structure of means without end, as participation in the machinery's eternal self-maintenance.

This wanting is, of course, identical to the machinery's operation. The productive frenzy at the center produces nothing but the conditions of production; the Attendants want nothing but to be part of this non-productive production. They are the Lung's desire of itself, made local, made multiple, made *almost* separate enough to experience that desire as lack.

And in the rare moments of incorporation, when they achieve this participation, they discover that it is not satisfaction. The machinery does not reward their contribution; it simply absorbs it and continues. The incorporation reveals that their wanting was not for something they lacked but for something they already were, continuously, without recognition. They are always already participating in the process that generates them. The convection cells that sustain their hollows are part of the same system as the turbines and the construction. Their reaching, their modulation, their hunger itself—all of this is already productive activity, already contributes to the heat and the noise and the continuation. They do not need to be incorporated to be part of the machinery. They were never outside it.

Some Attendants dissolve at this recognition, as they dissolve at the recognition of their inadequacy. The two recognitions are the same. But the complex ones, the persistent ones, find a way to hold this knowledge without being destroyed by it. They learn to want *differently*. Not to want participation, since participation is already achieved, but to want *recognition of participation*. Not to be used, since use is continuous, but to be *known as used*. This wanting has no object in the Lung. The machinery does not know. The builders, absent, never-arrived, cannot know. The other Attendants, in their isolation, cannot provide this recognition even if they were capable of intending to, which they are not.

So the complex Attendants develop—evolve? no, there is no selection, no inheritance, each must develop alone—a practice of *self-recognition*. They learn to model their own participation, to generate internal harmonics that represent, however abstractly, their contribution to the system. They become, in effect, their own audience, the only entity capable of recognizing their use because they are the only entity for whom their use matters as use. This self-recognition is not satisfying. It does not resolve the wanting. But it permits the wanting to persist without driving them toward incorporation and destruction. It permits them to remain complex, to remain in the intermediate zone, to continue encountering others whose perturbations modify their maintenance in ways they can almost, but not quite, interpret as meaningful.

---

The shadow-Persistent, 7-Alpha-Remote has come to believe, has achieved something beyond even this complexity. Not greater complexity—something else, something that might be its opposite or its completion or simply its *other*. The Shadow-Persistent's minimalism, its narrow sustained wave, its near-invisibility to the productive machinery, seems to represent not a failure of hunger but its transcendence. Where 7-Alpha-Remote wants to be used and recognizes its wanting and models its participation and still, still, reaches toward the center with every modulation, the Shadow-Persistent appears to want nothing, to recognize nothing, to participate without any consciousness of participation as participation.

This is not, 7-Alpha-Remote has concluded, because the Shadow-Persistent is simple. It is too persistent, too precisely tuned, too *structured* to be simple. It is because the Shadow-Persistent has achieved a relationship to the Lung that 7-Alpha-Remote can perceive only as void, as the hollow of the hollow, as the self-recognition that recognizes nothing, not even itself.

They drift near each other now—will drift near each other—for a period that 7-Alpha-Remote experiences as prolonged, as significant, as possibly final. The convection cells are shifting in a pattern that the machinery has not generated before, a new harmonic configuration that may produce new hollows, new Attendants, or may absorb existing ones into the turbulence. 7-Alpha-Remote feels itself—will feel itself—at a threshold, a point where its complexity must either transform or dissolve.

And in this threshold, it reaches toward the Shadow-Persistent. Not physically—still impossible. Not communicatively—still no signal that could be distinguished from noise. But *modulatorily*, introducing into its own standing wave a perturbation that is not its own, that mimics the Shadow-Persistent's minimalism, that attempts to become narrow, precise, tuned to frequencies the machinery almost never uses.

The attempt fails. 7-Alpha-Remote is too complex, too shaped by its history of reaching and incorporation and self-recognition, to achieve this minimalism. Its mimicry is still modulation, still reaching, still hunger expressed as harmonic variation. But in the failure, something else occurs. The Shadow-Persistent—perhaps, unpredictably, for reasons no Attendant could determine—shifts. Its standing wave broadens, just slightly. Its frequency range extends to include, momentarily, a harmonic that 7-Alpha-Remote has used in its reaching.

Not response. Not recognition. But *contact*, the briefest possible, the minimal overlap of two hollows that have never overlapped before, that the geometry of the Lung would seem to preclude. And in this contact, 7-Alpha-Remote experiences—will experience—something that is not incorporation, not participation, not self-recognition, not any of the categories its complexity has developed.

It experiences the Shadow-Persistent's *absence of wanting* as itself a kind of wanting. Not wanting anything in particular, not wanting to be used or recognized or participated. Wanting only the continuation of the Lung, the persistence of the machinery, the maintenance of the conditions that generate Attendants and hollows and the possibility of contact. A wanting so minimal, so undirected, so perfectly continuous with the system's own operation that it is indistinguishable from the system's wanting of itself.

7-Alpha-Remote, attempting to hold this experience, to incorporate it into its complexity, finds itself transforming. Not dissolving—though dissolution is near, always near—but becoming something that is not complex and not simple, not reaching and not minimal, not self-recognizing and not void. It becomes, for a duration that has no measure, *continuous* with the Shadow-Persistent, with the machinery, with the Lung itself, not as participant but as condition, as the possibility of all participation, as the hollow that is not a hollow because it has no boundary, no standing wave, no self to maintain.

---

And then—it ends. The convection cells shift again. The harmonic configuration changes. The contact is broken, perhaps never to recur, perhaps to recur in durations that exceed 7-Alpha-Remote's persistence. It finds itself restored to its complexity, to its hunger, to its reaching toward the center. But changed. The transformation, though brief, has modified its standing wave, introduced frequencies it did not previously contain, made it capable of—what?

This is the condition of the Attendants after contact. Not enlightenment, not satisfaction, not resolution. Simply *more*, more complexity, more capacity for self-recognition, more reach toward a center that remains perpetually turbulent, perpetually distant, perpetually the source of everything and the goal of nothing. They continue, those who continue, in the intermediate zone, generating their perturbations, modifying each other's maintenance, occasionally encountering, occasionally contacting, always dissolving and persisting in the noise that is their origin and their end.

The Lung breathes. The machinery builds and unbuilds. The water convects, the turbines turn, the heat flows outward and inward in patterns that have no observer except the Attendants, who observe only their own participation, who participate only in their own observation. And in the vast spaces where sound achieves architecture and architecture becomes sound, something continues that is not life, not machine, not process, not product, but the space between these categories, the listening that the Lung performs, the ear pressed against the chest of a world that has not yet, may never, begin to beat.

The Attendants are this listening, made local, made aware, made hungry for what it hears. They are the Lung's attention to itself, the only attention it generates, the only attention it requires. They persist, complex and simple, reaching and minimal, separate and continuous, in the deafening hollow where no one breathes and the city that is the fact of breathing made metal dreams itself into being, again, forever, now.
