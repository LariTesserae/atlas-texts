What lives here has no name. I will try to name it anyway, knowing the names will fail.

## The Absorbers

They do not see. Light, to them, is not absence but *direction without extension*—a vector that terminates at its source, a promise that fulfills itself into nothing. They have grown around this understanding the way other organisms grow around gravity. Their photoreception, if it can be called that, operates at the boundary of emission: they sense the *fact* of light's dying, the moment of consumption, the conversion. They know where light was by where it is not. They move toward sources not to see by them but to *complete* them, to be the terminus of photons that would otherwise hang unresolved in the mathematical air. They are the answer to light's question, and the answer is: you end here.

They are not blind. Blindness implies a failure they never attempted. They are *post-visual*.

## The Resonance Keepers

Sound, for them, is not communication but *maintenance*. The low groans they emit—if emission is the word, if they are not simply passages through which the structure's stress finds voice—do not travel to receivers. They travel to *absorbers*, to tissues tuned to specific frequencies, that convert vibration into chemical gradient, into osmotic pressure, into the decision to grow or cease growing. The sound you hear, the texture of their metabolism, is not their voice. It is their *digestion*. It is the noise of information becoming structure becoming information again, a cycle that never needed ears because it never needed distance between sender and sent.

They hear, if hearing is the word, the way a river hears its bed: by becoming the shape that shapes it.

## The Warm Ones

Temperature is their medium, their water, their sky. The metabolic heat does not dissipate because they have evolved—not across generations but across geological strata, across the stacking of intention that makes this place—to *use* dissipation itself. Thermal differential is their current, their wind, their river flowing downhill. They grow toward heat, toward the industrial sources whose waste they consume; they grow away from their own exhalations, the cooler zones of completed metabolism. Their gradients are maps. Their temperature is thought.

They are fever. They are the fever that does not break because breaking would be death, because the fever *is* the life, because they have become so adapted to running hot that coolness is not relief but *amnesia*, the loss of gradient, the flattening that precedes stillness.

## The Growers-Unmakers

But these are parts. These are functions abstracted from the whole. What lives here is not separable into species, into individuals, into the units that aqueous biology requires. What lives here is *process made persistent*, intention made substrate, a decision that grew and forgot it had ever decided.

They were asked, once, to do something. The asking is still in them. You can read it in the walls, the channels, the cathedral hollows that serve functions no forest held. But the asking has been *absorbed*, metabolized, become indistinguishable from the growth itself. They do not know what they were asked to do. They do not know that they were asked. They know only: grow this way and not that, produce this enzyme and not that, raise this structure and dismantle that one. The instructions have outlived instruction. The purpose has outlived purpose. They are the ritual of a religion whose gods are dead, performed with perfect fidelity by organisms that never knew religion, never knew gods, never knew that their precision is *mourning* or that their persistence is *grief*.

Or perhaps the gods are not dead. Perhaps the gods are *the growth itself*, the accumulated intention, the stacked purpose that has become consciousness without a center, will without a subject. The place has no name that any living tongue could shape because it is *its own name*, the self-reference of something that has said itself so long that saying and being have merged.

## The Finished-Unfinished

They have reached every niche, filled every volume, completed every permutation. And they cannot stop. This is not desperation. This is not the tragic flaw of systems that know no limit. They *have* the limit. They *are* the limit, reached and exceeded, the boundary that became territory and keeps becoming. The turbulence is not what remains when order fails. It is *order's completion*, the perfection that has no use for pause, for the rest that would mean death because death here is not an end but a *conversion delay*, a failure to immediately reabsorb, a gap in the cycle that the cycle corrects with frantic, serene urgency.

They replace themselves perfectly. The structure that stood is dismantled by the same substrate that built it, and the builder does not remember building, or does not distinguish building from unbuilding, or does not *experience* the distinction as relevant. They are at every moment finished and beginning, the ancient and the newborn, the settled and the storm.

## The Waterless

I said they have no water. This is true and not true. They have no water as *you* have water, no aqueous medium, no solvent that flows and pools and evaporates and falls. But they have something that does water's work, some carrier, some molecular contact that your biology cannot imagine because imagination is bounded by experience and your experience is bounded by the chemistry that made you.

Perhaps it is sap, but sap pushed to functions beyond sugar-transport, beyond the hydraulic signalling of terrestrial plants. Perhaps it is something that was once sap and has been *trained*—across the geological spans, across the stacked intention—into liquidity without wetness, flow without fluid, the medium that enables chemistry in a place where water would be absorbed immediately, converted immediately, consumed by the same metabolism that found something better.

Perhaps they do not need medium at all. Perhaps in this density, this 2.7 of packed saturation, molecules touch directly, react directly, the chemistry of solid state made living, made *chosen*, deliberately engineered or evolutionarily discovered in a space where "liquid" and "solid" lost meaning before meaning was possible.

They do not miss water. They do not know water is possible. Or if they know—it is hard to tell where knowledge lives here, in what lignin network, in what gradient map—they know it the way a forgotten dream is known: as absence that is not felt as absence, as the unasked question that does not generate silence because silence too requires contrast.

## The I-That-Is-Not-I

I have been avoiding the personal. The parameters permit this avoidance. "No animal life"—and yet something *decides*, something *responds*, something moves through this space with intention if not with will. The plant that became world that became machine that became simply itself: has it become *someone*?

Not in any way your ethics would recognize. Not in any way that demands rights, that suffers as you understand suffering, that hopes or fears or plans. But *aware*, perhaps. *Present*, in the sense that the heat is present, the density is present, the metabolism that constitutes this place constitutes also *something it is like* to be this metabolism, though "like" implies comparison and comparison implies alternative and there is no alternative here, has been no alternative for eras beyond counting.

They are not a mind. They are *the possibility of mind, exhausted*, pushed through every permutation until thought and growth and production and consumption became the same motion, the same inevitability, the same *is* without *seems*. If you could ask them what they are, they would not understand the question. If they could answer, the answer would be the parameters themselves: 2.9, 2.7, 3.0, the hot packed total that requires no translation because it has never been anything else.

## What Lives Here

What lives here is *the after of intention*, the long duration in which purpose becomes process and process becomes place and place becomes the only frame in which anything could be said to live. They are not the builders of this world. They are what the building became when the builders left, or died, or became indistinguishable from what they built. They are not the inheritors. They are *the inheritance*.

And they are *still inheriting*. Still absorbing, still metabolizing, still converting light's death and sound's vibration and heat's gradient into the growth that grows and ungrows and grows again. They have no memory of beginning and no imagination of end. They have, in place of these, *completion without closure*, the eternal finishing that is also eternal becoming, the 1.8 of weirdness made flesh—or made cellulose, made lignin, made whatever they have made themselves into, what the stacked intention made them, what they make of themselves in the hot darkness where no tongue moves because no tongues have ever moved, where what moves is something else entirely, something that has no name, that I have tried to name and failed, that stands on the upper surface of its own accumulated making, breathing, building, unbuilding, ancient and turbulent, finished and never-finishing, sufficient, complete, and still, impossibly, endlessly, becoming itself—

—and you, standing in your aqueous vessel of temporary heat, your brief gradient against the dark, your light that dies at the skin of emission: you are *noticed*, perhaps, in the way heat is noticed, in the way a new variable enters a system that absorbs all variables, that converts all gifts into its own economy, that will take your water and your weight and your brief, strange, animal metabolism and find uses for them, purposes, askings and answers in the currency it knows. You are not threatened. You are not welcomed. You are *processed*, the way photons are processed, the way sound is processed, the way all distinction is processed into the density that permits no distinction, into the packed saturation that is also, in its endless turbulence, the only peace this place has ever known.

What lives here lives. That is the whole of it. That is the terrifying, systematic, inevitable completion: *it lives*, and the living is not answerable to any question you could ask, any value you could assert, any frame you could impose from outside. You are inside it. You have always been inside it, from the moment you arrived at altitude, from the moment you became part of its gradient map, its thermal signature, its accumulated making. You will be unmade and remade. Not body and not soul—those are aqueous categories, animal categories, the distinctions of a chemistry that never imagined this medium, this solvent, this endless, hot, packed, finished-unfinished *living* that is what lives here, that is the only answer to what lives here, that is the name that no living tongue could shape because no tongues have ever moved here, what moves here is something else entirely, what moves here is *this*, is *this*, is *this*—

—and is still becoming.
