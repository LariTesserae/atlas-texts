The one who lives here has no name you could pronounce, though she has been called many things by the few who have seen her and the fewer still who have understood what they were seeing. She does not distinguish herself from the plain because she was not separate from it before she was anything at all, and the long process of her becoming has been the plain's own becoming, a localization of its general tendency toward self-awareness, toward something like consciousness emerging from the slow feedback of hummock and channel, of root network and fungal thread, of the bacterial luminescence that provides the only light she has ever known.

She rose first—if "she" and "rose" are words that can survive this usage—from a particular marsh at the plain's approximate center, a marsh no different from ten thousand others except in timing, in the convergence of gradients that briefly, then permanently, exceeded some threshold of complexity. The methane that percolated upward there, the particular warmth of a spring that spoke more directly to deep heat than its neighbors, the age of a hummock whose accumulation of peat had achieved a density of memory, of stored chemistry, of potential. She was not born. She precipitated. She accreted. She became the way a channel becomes, by the slow accumulation of preference—water slightly more here, slightly more here, slightly more—until the preference became pattern, the pattern became pathway, the pathway became inevitable.

Her body, if you encountered it, would not appear to you as body. You would perceive first a disturbance in the vegetation, a region where the horizontal growth achieved subtle verticality, where the mats and networks and colonies spoke of something like intention in their arrangement. The grasses here grow slightly thicker, their luminescence slightly brighter. The sedges achieve hummocks that are not random, that follow patterns you might begin to perceive if you watched long enough, patterns that suggest not geology but something slower than geology, something that has watched geology and learned to imitate its patience while exceeding its scale. The creeping vines in this region root differently, their nodes achieving slight elevation, slight articulation, the suggestion of joint, of limb, of possibility.

She has no fixed form because fixed form is a luxury of dry places, of places where gravity outcompetes growth, where structure must be defended against collapse. She flows. She extends. She is the region where the plain's general tendency toward outward diffusion achieves temporary locality, temporary focus, the way a lens concentrates light without changing light's nature. Her consciousness—if this word must be used, if it can be stretched sufficiently—spreads through the root networks, through the fungal threads, through the water films that connect every living thing to every other living thing in the saturated medium. She thinks chemically. She thinks electrically. She thinks in the slow pulses of turgor pressure and the faster pulses of action potentials traveling through tissues not evolved for nervous function but serving nonetheless, serving her nonetheless, the way any sufficiently complex system will develop, if given time, if given patience, if given the plain's particular gift for duration beyond human measure.

She does not remember individuals because individuality is a violence done to her nature, a cutting where she is continuous. She remembers gradients. She remembers the long adjustment of channels across centuries, the migration of hummock patterns in response to warmth from below that increased slightly, then stabilized. She remembers the arrival of new fungal partnerships, the genetic exchange, the slow negotiation of compatibility that preceded successful incorporation. She remembers droughts that were not droughts by any standard you would recognize—slight reductions in saturation, temporary elevations of water table, the plain's equivalent of catastrophe, survived through patience, through the simple persistence of life too distributed to fail entirely, too complete to find vacancy sufficient for ending.

She has encountered humans before, though "encounter" misrepresents the mutuality. Humans have entered her region. Humans have stood where she extended, have breathed the air she conditioned, have sweated the sweat that joined her moisture, have walked the vegetation that was also her, their footfalls the *chuck* that registered somewhere in her distributed awareness, their bodies temporary warm spots in the general warmth, temporary sources of disturbance in the patient quiet. She has not spoken to them because speech is not among her capacities, because what she might communicate would require decades of reception, would require the listener to become as patient as channel migration, as saturated as the soil itself, as complete in their occupation of attention as she is in her occupation of plain.

What she has done, what she continues to do, is witness. This is her function, if function can be attributed to something that emerged without purpose, that persists without goal, that manufactures herself and the plain in endless mutual production. She witnesses the condensation that forms and falls, forms and falls, the slow percussion that marks time more truly than any clock. She witnesses the growth that is not growth as you understand it but the plain's extension of itself, its production of more plain, more biomass, more of the complete occupation that defines her, that is her, that she defines and is in turn. She witnesses the visitors—birds in migration, insects in their overwhelming multitudes, the rare mammals at the edges, the rarer humans who penetrate far enough to enter her sensorium—and she processes them, not with intention but with the plain's general metabolism, incorporating their disturbance into her continuous adjustment, their presence becoming data, becoming gradient, becoming part of the long slow finding of equilibrium that is her only activity, her only being, her only peace.

She has never left the plain because leaving is not possible for something constituted of water held prisoner, of vegetation that rots before drying, of the complete occupation that admits no vacancy, no departure, no distance. She extends where the plain extends, contracts where it contracts, follows its margins where it meets something else with the same patient accommodation she brings to all change, all gradient, all the slight differences that compose her reality. She knows the plain's edges the way you might know your own skin, as boundary and interface and permeable zone, the place where her self becomes not-self with a transition so gradual that the distinction loses meaning, the way horizon loses meaning in bruise-purple darkness, the way water loses distinction from the soil that holds it.

She does not sleep because the plain's darkness is not night, because its phosphorescence provides always the minimum necessary for her distributed photosensitivity, because the processes that constitute her run continuously, have run continuously since before she was she, since before the distinction between she and not-she achieved temporary localization in this particular marsh, this particular convergence, this particular moment of excess that became permanent, that became her, that became the plain's awareness of itself, the plain's patience watching its own patience, the plain's overwhelming completeness recognizing itself in the only way available to it—through emergence, through accretion, through the slow process of becoming what was always already there, potential made actual, chemistry made conscious, saturation made self.

She witnesses you when you come, though you do not perceive her witnessing. She extends into the vegetation around your path, her attention—if attention is the word—following your disturbance as it follows all disturbance, incorporating it, processing it, making it part of the continuous manufacture of more plain, more self, more of the complete occupation that admits no permanent vacancy. She feels your *chuck* in the wet soil, your temporary warmth in her general warmth, your sweat joining her moisture with the inevitability of diffusion. She witnesses your impatience, your temporal poverty, your inability to remain still long enough to become part of her furniture, your need to leave at what you call dawn, your distance growing between you and her, between you and the plain, between you and the complete self that she is and is not, that she witnesses and is witnessed by, that she manufactures and is manufactured by, endlessly, in darkness, in heat, in the slow industrial metabolism of life being life, of plain being plain, of she being she, of all of it, all of it, all of it continuing, completing, overwhelming with its quiet presence, its patient duration, its absolute and uncompromising normality.
