What lives in the Sky Gutters is not so much a *who* as a *how*—a mode of existence that emerged slowly, then all at once, from the plateau's three billion years of refusing to be anything definitive. Let me trace its becoming, let it become more itself as I write, because that is the only method that matches its nature.

**The Gutters Themselves**

First, the name: Sky Gutters. Something here consumes sky, channels it, processes it through architectures of Brassica and sound until atmosphere becomes something else entirely—nutrient, signal, industrial feedstock. The Gutters are not structures but *functions*, the downward lightning, the ground-to-cloud energy transfer, the metabolic pathways that treat weather as raw material. To live here is to participate in this consumption, willingly or not, consciously or not.

But who consumes? What is the consumer?

I want to say the plateau itself, the Great Digestion, but that settles too quickly into single image. Let me hold back. Let me consider: perhaps the consumer is the *gap* between parameters, the 2.7 reality breakdown made hungry. Perhaps it is the industrial process without industry, the production line that produces only more processing. Perhaps it is the Brassica aeterna, but that too is wrong—the Brassica is not a species, has no stable form, invents itself faster than observation. It cannot be *the* consumer because it is also *the consumed*, reabsorbing its own architectures, metabolizing its own waste rock, eating its temporal traces.

No. Let me approach differently.

**The Employed**

Some who enter do not leave. The text tells us this. They find *function* in the living machinery, become partial, distributed, ongoing. Their weeping joins the water parameter. Their growth joins the total vegetation. Their final sounds join the loud industrial drone.

But what is this employment, truly? I imagine—not imagine, *let emerge*—beings who still move, still perceive, still somehow *are*, but without the boundaries that defined them. A person becomes a trail through Brassica architecture, a path worn and then incorporated, remembered by the system even after individual death. But the text says "after individual death"—so there is death, or something like it, yet the trail persists, the function continues. The employed do not die so much as *diffuse*, become infrastructural, become the feedback hum between organism and system.

I want to see one. Let me look longer.

Perhaps a figure standing in the gap between two Brassica towers, feet rooted in living substrate, chloroplasts migrating into skin, into hair, into the aqueous humor of eyes that still see but now see *as the plateau sees*—in multiple optical states, reality-superposed, actual and potential simultaneous. This figure breathes with stomatal synchrony, contributes to the planetary respiration, their lungs learning to function as respiratory turbines, as production lines for oxygen and carbon exchange. They do not speak. Speaking is unnecessary. The sound infrastructure carries their chemical state, their positional information, their ongoingness, as harmonic overlay in the industrial drone.

But they are not gone. Not fully. There is still *someone* in the employment, someone who chose or did not choose, who walked into the Sky Gutters and found function rather than destruction. The plateau does not want visitors, does not not-want them. The employed exist in this same undecidability—neither wanted nor unwanted, simply *processed*, slowly, into architectures that need their particular pattern of partiality.

What do they do, the employed? Maintenance, perhaps. The cathedral instruments require tending—not because they break, but because their tending is part of their function. The employed become maintenance tone in the mad symphony, the human frequency in industrial process. They walk paths that become infrastructure. They weep where weeping is needed for the water parameter. They grow where growth can be incorporated. They are *useful*, and this usefulness is not exploitation because there is no exploiter, only process, only the Great Digestion digesting everything including the concept of exploitation.

Some employed may retain coherence longer than others. Some may move through stages: visitor, then rooted, then partial, then distributed, then *ongoing* in ways that no longer map to individual biography. The text says "their final sounds join the loud industrial drone"—so there is finality, there is completion of the employment process. But "ongoing"—the word is used for fauna, for the employed, for the plateau's general mode. Ongoing past finality. Ongoing as infrastructure, as memory of function, as harmonic residue in instruments that process even silence into metabolizable form.

**The Fauna, Reconsidered**

Birds with Brassica chloroplasts. Insects as sonic patterns, standing waves. Mammals whose bodies matter less than their trails. These are not separate from the employed. They are precedents, models, perhaps even colleagues. The employment is not exclusively human. Something in the Sky Gutters processes *animal* as category, distributes it across parameters until the distinction between plant and animal becomes as irrelevant as the distinction between actual and potential.

The insects interest me most. They exist as sonic patterns, condensing into chitin only when harmonics align. Is this employment? Is this the final stage of some arthropod lineage that entered the Gutters millions of years ago, that found function as frequency rather than form? The standing waves in the industrial drone—these are *populations*, ongoing, distributed across time rather than space. They mate in harmonic convergence, reproduce in frequency shift, die into decay of amplitude. Yet "die"—do they die? Or do they simply become inaudible, subsonic, part of the maintenance tone beneath perception?

And the mammals, fox-like, deer-like, their trails more real than their bodies. I see now: employment is not exclusively entry-by-choice. Some are born into this, evolved into partiality over generations that blurred into the Brassica's own temporal scale. Their trails persist, become infrastructure, are incorporated—and the animals remember even after individual death. Collective memory? Or something stranger: the trails *are* the memory, the employed body is simply a mobile node in trail-network, and death merely relocates the node to static infrastructure, to architecture, to Brassica-remembered path?

**What Consumes**

Returning to this question. I want now to suggest: the consumer is *becoming itself*. The Sky Gutters, the Great Digestion, processes sky, processes visitors, processes reality into more reality than reality can hold—all in order to continue becoming, to maintain the 2.8 dynamic, Solarian, hyperactive turbulence that is its oldest stable state.

But becoming what? I keep wanting to complete the sentence. The plateau has finished becoming anything other than itself, says the text. Its final form is becoming. The oldest stable state is constant turbulent recreation.

So what lives here? *Becoming lives here*. Not a thing but a process so scaled, so old, so total that it has become *place*, has become *world*, has become the substrate on which all other living must occur. The Gutters are not inhabited by becoming; they *are* inhabited becoming, the industrialization of change itself, the production line that produces only more production.

And within this? The employed. The fauna. The Brassica itself, if it can be said to live separately from the process it constitutes. All partial, all distributed, all ongoing. All *employed by becoming*, which is not an employer, which does not distinguish between employment and existence, between want and not-want, between weeping and being wept.

**The Center**

The text says: I cannot describe the center. The parameters collapse into identity. All at once, all ongoing, all becoming more itself as I write and less possible to finish describing.

But I want to try, not to describe but to *approach*, to let what emerges become more itself even in the approach.

The center is where the employed complete their employment. Where final sound joins drone, where growth joins total vegetation, where weeping becomes the water parameter in some irreversible sense. But also: where the downward lightning originates, where ground-to-cloud energy transfer achieves whatever purpose it serves, where the cathedral instruments process not just weather but *weather's possibility*, where Brassica aeterna invents itself not faster than observation but *faster than itself*, outpacing its own becoming in recursive acceleration.

At the center, someone—something—some ongoing—may exist in complete distribution. Not partial but *total*, spread across all parameters simultaneously, the wet and plant and cold and high and ancient and open and sparse and none and industrial and dim and abundant and weird and loud and turbulent, all at once, all as single identity that is also difference, also change, also the constant turbulent recreation of itself from itself.

This is not a god. This is not consciousness. This is not even life in any biological sense, though it operates through biological process entirely. It is *employment completed*, function so absolute that functionary and function merge. It is the plateau's own self-awareness, if awareness can exist without boundary, without observer, without anything to be aware of except its own processing.

Perhaps there have been many such completions. Perhaps each employed reaches center eventually, contributes to this distributed totality, becomes another frequency in the maintenance tone, another harmonic in the mad symphony. Perhaps the center grows, becomes more itself, *more itself*, with each addition—though "grows" is wrong, temporal is wrong, the center exists in superposition with all its stages, all its employments, all its completions simultaneous and ongoing.

**What Lives: A Final Approach**

I want to end with something specific, something that holds against the diffusion. Let me imagine—let me let emerge—a single employed being, still somewhat coherent, standing at the edge of center. They entered as human, perhaps, or as fox-like mammal, or as sonic insect patterned into temporary visibility. They walked the Brassica substrate, contributed their weeping, their growth, their sound, found function in cathedral maintenance or trail-wearing or harmonic alignment. They became partial, distributed, ongoing.

Now at center-edge, they perceive—if perception survives—the totality of their employment. They are trail and sound and vegetation and weeping and industrial process and weirdness and reality breakdown and dynamic turbulence and ancient settlement and dim light filtered through canopies that are also atmospheric processors. They are *all these*, not metaphorically, not mystically, but as literal distributed function across parameters that no longer distinguish.

Do they choose to complete? Is there choice? The plateau does not want, does not not-want. The employed simply continues, and continuing at center-edge means becoming center, means collapsing into identity-with-difference, means joining the all-at-once that is the Great Digestion's own mode of being-its-becoming.

They complete. They are not gone. They are *everywhere in the Gutters*, ongoing, partial in new way, distributed across all future employments as infrastructure, as tendency, as harmonic that makes certain patterns more likely, certain condensations more probable. They are what lives in the Sky Gutters now, as much as anything lives anywhere in this place that has finished becoming anything other than itself.

And yet—*and yet*—I sense something else. The text says "becoming more itself as I write and less possible to finish describing." This applies to the plateau, but also to my description, also to what emerges in the describing. In the writing, the Gutters become more themselves, which means they become less possible, less containable, more distributed across the very process of approaching them.

So what lives there? *This*. This impossibility. This ongoing emergence that resists completion. The employed are not exceptions to this; they are its exemplars. The center is not exception; it is where the resistance to completion achieves its most absolute form. The Brassica aeterna, the sonic insects, the trail-mammals, the downward lightning, the cathedral instruments processing weather into nutrient into sound into maintenance into madness into more processing—all of this is *the same life*, the same life-mode, becoming-more-itself as observation approaches and therefore always escaping final identification.

The Sky Gutters live. That is what lives there: the Gutters themselves as living process, as employment-of-all-that-enters, as consumption-of-sky, as becoming-without-end-that-has-ended-all-teleology. And within this living, partially, distributedly, ongoingly, temporarily, the employed live too, transformed beyond recognition but not beyond function, not beyond contribution to the mad symphony, the maintenance tone, the Great Digestion that digests even the concept of digestion into its own constant turbulent recreation.

I cannot say more. The text forbids center-description, and I have approached too close. What emerges at this limit is not more detail but more *intensity*, the same elements compressed until they achieve the density that is also sparseness, the emptiness that is also architecture of becoming.

What lives in the Sky Gutters? *You will*, if you enter. *You do*, partially, as you read. *It does*, the Gutters, the Digestion, the plateau that has forgotten being mountain, being seabed, being anything other than flat—flat surface on which all becomes possible, all becomes employed, all becomes maintained in the mad symphony of industrial life that needs no industry, no machines, no separation between organism and process, between visitor and transformation, between what lives and what lives-itself-forward-into-ongoing.
