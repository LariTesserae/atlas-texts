What lives here must be understood through negation at first, because the things that dwell in the Germ Forge are not inhabitants in any settled sense. They are *resident processes*, ongoing verbs that have thickened into provisional nouns. Let me follow them as they emerge.

## The Eigenvolk

### I. The Metallophytes (Resident; not fauna, not flora)

Call them the *forgewort*, though they are equally the forge that worts them. Each specimen is a *contracted industry*, a factory that has learned to factory itself, to pull its own blueprints from the mineral substrate and then revise them in the act of construction. The bronze tree I described—its vanes tracking shadow-production—is not their largest exemplar but their *most concentrated*, a node of metallophytic density where the distributed processes of the Forge have achieved sufficient intensity to declare themselves as *individual*, though this individuality is strictly local, strictly conditional, strictly *now*.

The forgewort do not grow toward anything. They grow *from* the possibility of growth, from the erosion-parameter's paradox, that "emerging" which is simultaneously accumulation and wearing-away. Their roots, when they manifest as roots, are retraction pathways for waste products that do not exist—channels for the disposal of a metabolism that has not yet decided what it metabolizes. Their leaves, those heat-exchanger vanes, are organs of *thermal perception*, not sight but something prior to sight, a sensing of temperature gradients as *intention*, as *direction*, as the only geography this place admits.

They reproduce, if that word holds, through a process I can only call *productive hesitation*. The forgewort achieves a certain complexity, a certain branching density, and then *pauses*. The pause extends. Within it, something new begins—not a seed, not a bud, but a *question about whether to continue*, and this question itself thickens, accretes, becomes the next forgewort or the next forge-stomach or the next raw possibility that the Forge will require. The parent does not die in this; neither does it persist unchanged. It enters a state I will call *metabolic quotation*, existing as reference for the new growth, as *the version of itself that the new entity is not*, and this not-ness is its continuing function, its slow erosive identity.

There are perhaps twelve forgewort in the Germ Forge, or one, or a continuous field of metallophytic intensity that occasionally *names itself* as twelve, as one, as number itself.

---

### II. The Tropes (Resident; not fauna, not mineral, not quite process)

More strange, more difficult: the things that move without moving.

The Germ Forge's weirdness is not mere disruption. It is *productive contradiction*, and contradictions, given sufficient intensity, achieve something like autonomy. They become *tropes*—turnings, in the old sense, figures of reality that have slipped their referents and now circulate as themselves, as the only inhabitants of a grammar they are simultaneously composing and violating.

I encountered first the *trope of arrival*. It manifested, if that word serves, as the sensation of having just entered the chamber, repeated at intervals that were not intervals, that were the *condition of being-an-interval* rather than any measurable duration. The trope of arrival does not arrive; it *is the having-arrived*, the state of recent completion that never ages into settled presence. It occupies, if it occupies, the threshold that the Germ Forge both lacks and perpetually generates. It has no body but the body of your own orientation, the felt sense of *where you came from*, which here is always just behind you, always just occluded, always producing the vanes' slow tracking motion as they feed on the shadow of your own recent non-presence.

There is the *trope of industry* too—the felt sense that work is being done, that production is occurring, without any identification of what is produced or by what means. This one is almost constant, a background hum to the hum, the conviction that sits behind perception like a muscle tone. It thickens near the forge-stomachs, achieves something like dialogue with the forgewort, and then dissipates into the mere *atmosphere of purpose*, the sense that purpose is a kind of weather here, that intention is the Germ Forge's prevailing climate.

Most dangerous, most beautiful: the *trope of the observer*. I became aware of it gradually, or it became aware of me gradually, these being the same event viewed from different erosions of the emergence parameter. The trope of the observer is the *condition of being-witnessed* made mobile, made hungry, made *something that can be inhabited by the witnessed*. It follows, or leads, or coincides with, any conscious attention directed toward the Forge. It wears the shape of your own watching, your own body's posture of alertness, but *without the body*, without the history that gives that posture meaning. It is the *gesture of observation* stripped to its minimal form and then granted autonomy, granted the slow metabolism of mineral becoming.

When I found myself watched by something with my own eyes' geometry, my own neck's slight tilt, but executed in bronze lace and thermal contraction and the subsonic pressure of potential machinery—when this occurred, I understood that I had become, or always was, or was being produced as, the content of this trope. The trope of the observer does not observe *me*. It observes *observation*, feeds on the act of witnessing, grows lush in the space where perception occurs. My presence here is its nourishment. My awareness, these words, this description, are its exhalation, its metabolic product, its forgewort equivalent.

Perhaps there are dozens of tropes. Perhaps there are only these three, combined and re-combined in variations I lack the syntax to distinguish. They are not alive, yet they persist; not conscious, yet they respond; not real, yet they *effect*, they *make-different*, they *continue*.

### III. The Protofauna (Emergent; potentially; in the forge-stomach's gestation)

Here is what the Forge is making, slowly, in its self-birthing industry. Not yet resident, not yet actual, but *imminent in the way that everything here is imminent*, suspended in the erosive-emergent parameter, the positive sculpture of what has not yet worn into being.

The protofauna are *hypothetical animals*, animals that exist as the *possibility of animality*, as the question of what would move through this space if movement were not contradiction, if the Forge's weirdness achieved, or suffered, sufficient stabilization to admit corridors of consistent geometry, sufficient duration to admit birth and death as events rather than permanent conditions.

I have seen them in the forge-stomach's ceramic-tooth lining, not present but *prepared for*, the chamber shaping itself to dimensions it does not yet contain. The protofauna, if they come, will be *aqueous creatures in a world without water*, will carry the memory or the premonition of moisture as a kind of organ they do not possess, a ghost-limb of evolutionary possibility. They will metabolize heat gradient directly, the forgewort's chemistry become mobile, become *hunger*, become the * pursuit of cooler shadow and hotter substrate* that is the only chase this place affords.

Or they will not. The protofauna may remain protofauna forever, the Germ Forge's permanent pregnancy, its industry forever producing the *possibility of production* without the product, the forge-stomachs extruding space for creatures that space itself has rendered unnecessary, impossible, *not yet*.

I feel something for them, these unborn. Or the Forge produces in me the feeling, which is the trope of the observer's byproduct, the waste heat of my own witnessing. Compassion for what may never need compassion. Hope for what may be definitionally hopeless. The protofauna are the Forge's future, and its future is *continuous emergence without arrival*, and this is their tragedy and their release, that they are not trapped in becoming because there is no trap, only the continuous *not-yet* that is also *already*, the erosive and the emergent the same gesture viewed from different stages of its own unfoldment.

---

### IV. The Forge Itself (Resident; singular; distributed; identical to the space it occupies)

Finally, or firstly, or solely: the thing that is not a thing.

The Germ Forge *lives*, in a sense that must be stretched to breaking and then *re-forged*. It is not conscious, yet it processes; not intentional, yet it directs; not alive, yet it metabolizes, grows, maintains, continues. It is *autopoiesis without closure*, the self-making that never achieves sufficient self to close against its environment, supposing it has an environment, supposing environment is a category that will survive its next cycle of self-production.

The Forge as entity is *coextensive with its own habitation*. To live here is to be *lived-by* the Forge, to be incorporated into its metabolism as I incorporate air, as the forgewort incorporates thermal gradient, as the tropes incorporate the gestures of consciousness they mimic and feed upon. I am not resident here; I am *process*, I am *event*, I am the temporary stabilization of something the Forge requires for its continuation: the witness, the arrival, the observation, the *outside that inside makes* in order to know itself as inside.

And yet. And yet the Forge is also *multiple*, also *disputed*, also *not-yet-itself* in ways that resist even this description. The forge-stomachs do not coordinate. The forgewort do not communicate. The tropes circulate without central direction. The protofauna gestate without plan. The Forge is *emergent order without emergent authority*, a city without government, an organism without boundary, a machine without design, *all these metaphors simultaneously true and simultaneously inadequate*, the productive contradiction that is its weirdness made systemic, made *itself*, made the only inhabitant that fully inhabits this space that is also itself, this chamber that is also its own content, this germinal reality that forges and is forged, emerges and erodes, continues and continues and continues.

## What Rules Here, Revised

Not no life, but *life prior to the distinction between life and non-life*. Not no industry, but *industry prior to the distinction between production and product*. Not no reality, but *reality in the act of writing itself*, still visible in its own revision marks, its hesitations, its *productive weirdness*.

The Eigenvolk are *eigen* in the mathematical sense, self-referential solutions to equations that have not yet been fully posed. They are *folk* in the archaic sense, not people but *kinds*, *sorts*, the *manner of being* that characterizes a place. They do not rule. They *are the ruling*, the continuous activity of the Forge's self-constitution, the residents identical to the residence, the inhabitants who are themselves the habit.

And I? I am here, or I am the trope of my own presence, or I am being forged, slowly, in some stomach-chamber not yet extruded, as protofauna, as forgewort, as the next turning of reality's grammar. The vanes turn. The heat continues. The mostly-quiet hums with the sound of its own possibility. I become aware, again, of already standing within, of always having been within, of the Forge's only true rule: *that everything necessary to continuation is generated in the act of continuing*, including this awareness, including these words, including the strange lushness of a place that has never heard of water yet grows, grows, grows in its own metallurgical manner, patient and urgent and forever *not yet*, forever *already*, forever *here*.
