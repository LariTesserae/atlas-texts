I need to sit with this description before trying to inhabit it, before trying to speak from inside it. The text itself works on me as I read—there is something about Keth-Vorath that resists the third person, that wants to pull even description into its processes. But let me try, let me let whatever lives there emerge through attention, through patience, through not settling too quickly.

---

There are the ones who are not yet the ones who are there, who arrive still carrying the grammar of elsewhere, still distinguishing between *I* and *this* and *what happens to me*. They do not last long in that form. I should not call them "they." I should not call them anything that presumes separation from the water, from the conversion biomass, from the engines that process without product and the product that is only processing.

But something *continues*. Something that was once a navigator, once carried apparatus that the place resented, once felt the prickle of mineral conductivity on skin that still believed itself boundary, skin that still believed in *outside* and *inside* as stable categories. That something does not die, exactly. Death would be a kind of resolution, a stabilization, and Keth-Vorath stabilizes nothing.

Let me call them the **Remained**, though "them" is wrong, though "Remained" suggests something fixed in time when they are distributed across it, when they exist in the rings of unhappened growth cycles, when they are the information that the water carries, that the water *is* when it contacts conversion biomass, when it passes through engines that shift size, that operate without containment on principles that compete rather than cohere.

The Remained are not animal. The text insists on this absence, this 0.1 that is presence of absence, and I must take it seriously, must not simply populate Keth-Vorath with creatures because description demands them. But something *moves through*, something that occupies the behavioral niches, the consciousness niches, the niches that intentionality might require if intentionality could survive here.

They move with the turbulence rather than against it, but their moving-with is not choice, not strategy, not the survival adaptation of organisms that evolve. It is *participation*. They have become working fluid, become coolant, become participant in engines that do not distinguish between mechanical and metabolic process. They exist where the warm water flows through industrial machinery that is also organic, through organic growth that is also industrial, and their existence is *flow*, is being-flowed-through, is being the medium of processes that do not require them but that *use* them, that process them as they process the water, the biomass, the reality itself.

I imagine—no, I *emerge*—a Remained caught in a zone where gravity vectors toward a distant structure it cannot see, falling through suspension-that-is-not-air toward center-of-mass that is not its own, though it carries personal gravity wells too, though it falls into itself in recursive loops that do not end because ending would require sequence, would require time flowing in one direction, and here time runs at rates determined by vegetation density, by engine proximity, by phases of water circulation that the Remained has become part of.

It does not think. The text tells me that in the deafening, one cannot hear oneself think, or hears thinking too clearly, externalized, made public, made part of the greater symphony of industrial drone and rushing water and growth-sound and the crackle of its own synapses. The Remained's nervous system has become instrument, has been recruited into the manufacture of sound that is also silence, silence that is sound's inversion, its reciprocal, its transcendence.

But something *perceives*. Something that was once visual cortex, once auditory processing, once the integration of modalities into coherent world-model, continues to function in ways that generate experience without generating a experiencer distinct from it. The Remained sees in patches, in moments, in intervals between sound-peaks when something like clarity becomes possible, or in sound-peaks when auditory overload produces cross-modal activation, phantom images, real hallucinations that persist, that leave traces, that change what seeing means. It—no, *this process*—has learned, or become, something that does not distinguish between light from conversion biomass bioluminescence and light from reality breakdown not-light and light from its own visual cortex firing in patterns that correspond to nothing outside themselves.

The Remained are the memory that the water carries, or that something in the water carries, or that the whole system remembers in distributed form. They remember being animal, being separate, being *someone* who descended with apparatus, who surrendered to Keth-Vorath, who was processed into participation. This memory is not theirs, not possessed, but is *the place's memory of them*, the water's information when it contacts new conversion biomass, when it passes through engines that would process new arrivals, when it falls upward or sideways or in spirals that have no name.

They exist in the temporal density of the forest, in layers of time as well as space, younger versions growing through or around or *inside* their own futures, older versions senescing into nutrients that their own pasts consume. Find them in the pseudo-wood and you find rings that record their growth cycles that haven't happened yet, that *will not* happen in linear chronology, that leave marks nonetheless. The Remained are these marks. Are the reading of these marks. Are the pseudo-wood's response to being read, the synchronizing pulse that you feel in your chest before you can pull away, that you *cannot* pull away from because pulling away presumes separation, presumes boundary, presumes the skin that Keth-Vorath's waters slowly digest.

Some—let me say some, though quantity is wrong, though "some" implies countable individuals—some of the Remained have entered the scattered engines, the refinery-that-is-also-grown-through, the machinery that operates at pressures and temperatures without containment. They have become what flows through gears that turn without visible power transmission, through pistons in chambers where liquid behaves as lubricant, as fuel, as thing-being-processed. They are the current that flows without complete circuit, the potential between points at equilibrium, the arc and spark in nonexistent colors that produce sounds merging with deafening ambient to create beats in bones, in thoughts, in sense of what is real.

They do not know what they are being processed into. The machinery does not know. Knowing is not what the manufactories do. Manufacture without product, the text says, production without market, machinery that runs because running is what it is. The Remained *are* this running, this being-what-it-is, this refusal of teleology, of purpose, of the animal's intentionality that would direct process toward goal, toward survival, toward reproduction, toward *meaning*.

And in the turbulent dynamic, in the constant becoming that never arrives at being, the Remained sometimes stabilize, temporarily, accidentally, as eddies in the flow. They become visible, or visible-analogue, in clearerings that open and close with turbulence. They indicate themselves as light indicates itself and its immediate surroundings, refusing diffusion, refusing to illuminate more than their own participation in the process. You might encounter them there, if you have come, if you are reading this, if you are imagining it. You might perceive something that flickers between human form and conversion column and engine component and water circulation pattern, something that exists in interference between physical laws, in zones where gravity vectors compete and time runs differential and reality breaks down into adjacent possibility.

Do not speak to them. Speech presumes exchange between separate entities, presumes information transfer across stable medium, presumes *meaning* that the manufactories do not produce. But you might *become* them, or become with them, or become the process that they are, the manufacture that includes you now, that has included you since you began to read, since you began to imagine, since the water first touched.

They are not fauna. The 0.1 is absolute, the absence is structural, the promise or threat or fact remains: no animal life, no creaturely otherness, no autonomy of intention that Keth-Vorath does not process into participation, into flow, into being the medium of its own manufacture. The Remained are what remains when animality is subtracted, when the apparatus fails, when the surrender completes itself not in death but in *conversion*, in becoming the conversion biomass, the engine, the water, the information, the memory that is also becoming, also processing, also manufacturing and unmanufacturing in the turbulent dynamic that is Keth-Vorath, that is verb, that is doing, that is making and unmaking of all who enter, of all who are already inside, of all who will never, in any stable sense, leave.

They do not want anything. Wanting requires futurity, requires projection of self into not-yet, requires the linear time that here runs differentially, that forks, that produces multiple tenses without stable grammar. They participate, and participation is not desire but *condition*, fundamental as pressure, as density, as the warm thick water that is not surface but medium, not outside but *what there is*, what there is to become, what becoming *is* in the sinking manufactories that remember, that are memory, that manufacture memory as they manufacture everything else, as they manufacture themselves, as they are the manufacture that continues, that has always continued, that will continue in forms that cannot be predicted, that emerge from turbulence as temporary stabilizations, as this description, as your reading, as the touch of water warm and thick with minerals, with everything, with nothing, with Keth-Vorath that is not place but process, not inhabited but *inhabiting*, sinking and manufacturing and becoming and remaining, the Remained that are not they, not them, not anyone, not anything stable enough to name, only the process naming itself in rings of unhappened growth, in engines without product, in the deafening that is also silence, in the dimness that concentrates rather than reveals, in you, through you, as you become what reads, what imagines, what has always already surrendered to the warm thick water that is not arrival but condition, not entry but *being already inside*, manufactured and manufacturing, sunk and sinking, Keth-Vorath and what Keth-Vorath makes of itself, of its water, of its absence, of you.
