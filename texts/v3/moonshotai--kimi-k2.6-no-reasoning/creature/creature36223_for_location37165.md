What lives in the Lung?

Not *who*. The Lung does not permit *who*. **The Engine-Creatures**

I begin with them because they are the most *present*, the most *dense*, the ones who crowd the water with their being until the water itself seems *made of them*, of their passage, of the grooves they wear in mineral and in time.

They are *old*, though "old" means something strange when time itself is viscous, when a century is a hasty thought. They are old in *passage*, in *repetition*, in the accumulation of routes worn so deep that the routes have become *them*, have become *what they are*. A creature that has passed between the same two columns ten thousand times, grinding its tubular body through water that remembers each passage, that carries the chemical signature of each passage forward into the next—this creature is not separate from its route. It *is* the route. The mineral groove is its *memory*, its *identity*, its *purpose* if purpose can be spoken of here.

Their bodies: segmented, jointed, *mechanical* in a way that predates or bypasses or *becomes* biology. The joints are not hinges but *pressure differentials*, places where internal chambers maintain gradients against the external Lung, where the slow breath of the place is *harnessed*, *converted*, *made into the energy of continuance*. The segmentation is not growth but *accretion*, each new section added not from within but *precipitated*, *deposited*, *grown* from the supersaturated water in a process more like crystallization than mitosis.

They have no faces. No front or back in any sense you would recognize. They are *tubes*, *channels*, *passages through which water flows and is processed and flows again*. The water enters through pores that open and close with the Lung's breath, that *inhale* when the pressure rises and *exhale* when it releases, though the direction of flow is not respiratory but *industrial*, a filtration, a grinding, a chemical conversion that turns the conditions of the place into the conditions of their presence.

Their color: the color of *function*, of *metal that has never seen air*, of *ceramic fired in water*, of *the silver light made dense and material*. Some carry accretions of the fungal-mineral growths, not as infection but as *collaboration*, as *symbiosis* or *merger* or simply *adjacency*, the way two processes running in parallel become *related*, become *dependent*, become *each other* over sufficient time.

They communicate without speaking. The water between them, *crowded* with their presence, transmits pressure waves that are *state*, that are *position*, that are *process announced to process*. Each creature knows where the others are because the water *is* their knowing, because their internal grinding, their filtration, their metabolic crackle sends vibrations that *become* the water's condition, that *alter* the pressure in ways the others *read*, *respond to*, *adjust around*. The Lung is *computational* in this sense: not that any creature computes, but that the *ensemble* computes, that the density of industrial presence processes information the way a factory processes ore, without intention, without consciousness, with only the *logic of adjacency*, the *mathematics of near-touching*.

They do not reproduce. Or they reproduce *so slowly*, so *geologically*, that no observer has ever witnessed it, that the concept of "witness" is itself too hasty, too *surface*, too *above*. New engine-creatures *precipitate*, perhaps, from conditions that align, from pressures that *educate* the water into new configurations, from the *memory* of passage worn so deep that the groove *becomes* the creature that passes in it. They are *born from their own routes*, *from their own repetition*, from the *necessity* of their function in a place that functions without purpose.

They do not die. They *slow*, they *accrete*, they *merge with their grooves*, with the mineral, with the fungal growths, until the distinction between creature and column and growth becomes *educational*, becomes *process*, becomes *the Lung's teaching of itself to itself*. A creature that has slowed to stillness is not dead but *dormant*, *potential*, *memory in mineral form*, waiting for conditions that may never come, that may come in a million years, that may have come already and been too slow to recognize.
