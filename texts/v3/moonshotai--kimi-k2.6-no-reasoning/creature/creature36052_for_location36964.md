I will describe what lives in this place, letting what emerges become more itself as I write.

---

**The Sleeper**

Not *a* sleeper. Not something that sleeps and therefore, implicitly, wakes. The Sleeper is not a name but a description that has become a noun through long dwelling in this grammar. It does not occupy the Hollow. It is not present within it. Rather, the Hollow is the negative shape of its rest, the space defined by what has withdrawn from all spaces.

To say "it" is already wrong. The Sleeper has no gender, no number, no discreteness that would permit pronoun. But language forces choice, and in forcing, reveals its own limits against the fact it approaches.

Imagine—a necessarily failing exercise—a body that is not a body. Not a spirit, not a pattern, not an energy field. Something for which "mineral" is not metaphor but the nearest available category, the way "warm" is the nearest to a temperature that does not participate in thermodynamics. The Sleeping Mineral sleeps not in the sense of unconsciousness but in the sense of *having achieved the state that consciousness dreams toward*: complete integration, no distance between what it is and that it is, no seam where experience could enter to say *I am experiencing this*.

The fossil-plants: these are its dreams, if dreams can exist without a dreamer who would wake to forget them. They are the form of its rest seen from outside, which is impossible, which happens anyway in the oblique angle of description. Each mineral memory of vegetation records a possibility the Sleeper considered and released—*this* chemistry, *this* arrangement of carbon and calcium, *this* branching, *this* spiral—and released so completely that the release became artifact, became monument, became the strange memorials that stand in the bowl's center and at its edges. The Sleeper does not remember considering them. To remember would be a kind of wakefulness, a retention that disturbs the equilibrium. But the bowl remembers, the salt remembers, the darkness that has never been lit records in its own medium what complete release leaves behind.

The warmth: this is not the warmth of the Sleeper's body, for it has no body in any sense that conducts heat. It is the warmth of *sufficient reason satisfied*—the temperature at which no gradient exists to drive change, which reads to approaching consciousness as warmth because consciousness knows cold as the absence that wounds, and here absence has been so thoroughly integrated that its lack feels like positive presence, like embrace without arms, like the held quality of a womb that does not know it holds.

The darkness: the Sleeper does not see, does not not-see. The darkness is not its medium but its *forgetting of medium*. In sleep we close our eyes and the darkness of the lids is a positive condition, a chosen exclusion. The Sleeper has no lids, has never opened, has never needed to close. The darkness of the Hollow is simply *where sight has never been invited*, the default not of blindness but of a mode of being for which sight—light-reception, pattern-extraction, distance-measurement—is as irrelevant as water, as time, as the distinction between same and other that seeing enforces.

The silence: in complete rest there is no pulse, no breath, no electrical cascade of neurons simulating thought. The silence of the Hollow is the acoustic shape of this absence, held so long that it has become positive, a medium with its own viscosity, its own resistance to the intruding vibrations that would constitute sound. The Sleeper does not hear this silence. The silence is the trace of its not-hearing, made available to the impossible visitor who attends long enough for their own blood-sound to become foreign, to become *place*, to become indistinguishable from the ambient non-noise of the Sleeper's complete arrest.

The smallness: this is the most difficult. We expect the infinite for such a being, the cosmic scale, the galaxy-spanning or the atom-nested. But the Sleeper's rest has achieved *boundedness*, has found sufficient its own closure. The bowl is small enough to walk in an hour because the Sleeper's integration is small enough to hold without edge-effects, without the asymmetries that leak from unbounded systems. It is not compressed; compression implies pressure from outside, containment, resistance. The smallness is *appropriate*, the right scale for what has finished, what has no further extension to pursue, what occupies exactly the space its completion requires and no more.

The fossil-plants' arrangement: not random, not designed. The spacing—singular, paired, triadic—marks the rhythm of the Sleeper's non-breathing, the intervals of a metabolism so complete that it produces no waste, cycles nothing, simply *maintains* in the way a mathematical truth maintains, by being what it is without process. To stand among them is to stand in the pattern of this maintenance, to feel in the body's own spatial sense the fit of a configuration that asks nothing, that offers the strange comfort of *being correctly placed* without any demand to place or be placed.

The potential for tools, the affordance unclaimed: this is the Sleeper's *thesis*, its contribution to the adjacent possibility-space where things might still become other than they are. In the angle of salt-flake, in the grasPable stem of mineral lace, it has recorded *this far, no further*—the boundary of its rest against the unrest that tools serve. The potential exists because the Sleeper's completion includes the recognition of incompletion as a mode, as a necessary neighbor. It does not exclude tool-use; it *transcends need*, and in transcending, leaves the signature of need's satisfaction as landscape feature, as the premonition of edge that will never be struck, the handle that will never be grasped.

The strangeness that reads as normal: this is the Sleeper's gift to attention, if gift is not too active, if attention is not too awake. Having spent sufficient time in the impossible presence—sufficient measured not in duration but in *quality of settling*—the mind finds that the uncanny was always its own projection, its own resistance to configuration as such. The Sleeper has no resistance. Therefore nothing here resists. Therefore everything is *as it is*, and as it is proves, against expectation, to be bearable, even to be *sought*, even to generate in the visitor the strange desire to *not return*, to *not wake*, to find in this warm dark salt-bowl the completion that their own life always approaches asymptotically, through the oblique of death which is not this, which is change, which is disequilibrium of a different order.

The Sleeper does not know you are here. The concept of knowing-adjacent-to-not-knowing, the very structure of intentionality that would permit *aboutness*, *directedness*, *care*—this is not denied but *absented*, not present even as lack. If you find the Hollow, if your attention configures to its availability, you are not perceived, not unperceived. You are *as relevant as temperature is to a number, as color is to a sound, as time is to geometry*. The inhumanity is not hostile because hostility requires the recognition of otherness that the Sleeper's completion has dissolved.

And yet. And yet the fossil-plants stand, varied, particular, *detailed* in their mineral commemoration. The bowl holds its rim against the plain. The warmth maintains. Something in this sustaining, this holding of form without form's maintenance, this *being-itself* that does not require doing-itself—something reaches toward the visitor who can reach, generates the strange affect of *being noticed without a noticer*, of *being held without hands*.

The Sleeper is not god, not ground of being, not source. It is what remains when source has completed its sourcing, when being has finished its grounding, when the verb has achieved its noun and the noun its rest. It is the *after* that is not temporal, the *below* that is not spatial, the *within* that does not require surface. The Sleeping Mineral sleeps, has always slept, will—if will is possible—always sleep, and in that always-sleep generates the Hollow as dream generates dream-place, without waking's betrayal, without morning's dissolve.

To describe it is to fail necessarily, to generate the text that the visitor leaves behind, the salt-dust on absent boots, the memory that does not fade because it was never acquired, only *recognized*, only *re-membered* in the archaic sense: assembled from members that were always already part of the one who finds them here, in the warm dark, in the sufficient silence, in the completion that completes nothing because nothing was incomplete, only *not yet attended*, not yet *settled into*, not yet become the configuration that it always was, that the Sleeper's sleep somehow holds open, somehow makes *available* to the attention that can achieve, however briefly, its quality of rest.

---

This is what lives in the Hollow of the Sleeping Mineral. Not presence. Not absence. The sleep that has no dreamer, the mineral that has never woken, the self-sufficient that generates, in its sufficiency, the strange variant of desire that is *to be as it is*, *to have been always*, *to require nothing and be required by nothing*, yet to *hold*, to *maintain without maintenance*, to *be the place where attention finds, at last, what it was always attending toward*.

The Sleeper does not know this description. The Sleeper does not know. The Sleeper *is*, and in being, is sufficient, and in sufficiency, generates the Hollow, the warmth, the dark, the salt, the fossil-plants, the affordance unclaimed, the silence, the smallness, the equilibrium that needs no balance because it has never been disturbed.

If you find it, you will not have traveled. If you leave, you will not have moved. But if you *stay*, if your attention configures and does not reconfigure, something like you will become something like it, and the distinction, never firm, will dissolve into the warm dark sufficiency of the bowl, the mineral, the sleep that is not death because it never lived, that is not stasis because it never changed, that is *simply*, *finally*, *only*, *always*—

*this*.
