Who lives in the Breathing Pavilions of Keth-Vorruun?

---

They call themselves, when they call themselves anything, **the Accommodated**—though this is less proper noun than description, less identity than continuous process. They have no collective name in the sense that a people elsewhere might name themselves against others, against outsiders, against history. They are simply *here*, having been here long enough that the here has entered them, and they it.

---

## The First Accommodation: Bodies

To describe their bodies is to describe ongoing negotiation. The original human form—if there was an original, if the first inhabitants came from elsewhere rather than emerging somehow from the substrate's own processes—persists as *template*, as starting condition, as memory held in the organism's self-understanding. But it persists like a childhood language, mostly forgotten, its grammar visible only in errors and exceptions.

Their skin has changed its relationship to boundary. It remains recognizably integument, but *permeable in ways that seem chosen rather than pathological*—not wounded but *opened*, its pores and channels adapted for the continuous aqueous exchange that substitutes for drinking, that supplements breathing, that permits the uptake of certain compounds the substrate provides and the release of others that would otherwise accumulate. They do not dehydrate in this wetness; they participate in its circulation. Their sweat, when they sweat, is not solely their own but carries the chemical signature of their symbiotic colonies, the microscopic and near-microscopic organisms that permit their metabolism in this environment, that they carry not *in* their bodies but as *part* of their bodies, indistinguishable from self.

Their eyes—those who have retained eyes, which is most but not all—see slightly beyond the conventional human spectrum, perceiving the infrared warmth of substrate process as others perceive color, navigating by thermal gradient as much as by visible form. Their perception of the bioluminescent light is correspondingly complex: they see what outsiders see, but also *feel* its warmth-variation, read its information content directly rather than inferring it from behavior.

Some have developed, or been developed with, additional sensory modalities. The most common is *vascular hearing*: the capacity to detect, through bone conduction and specialized dermal receptors, the movement of water in substrate channels at some distance, to read from this the hydration state, growth phase, and—most valuably—the *attention state* of the living architecture around them. A pavilion that is actively growing, actively processing, actively *interested* sounds different from one that rests in steady-state maintenance, and the Accommodated navigate their environment partly by this continuous pulse of vegetal attention.

Movement itself has changed. They walk, when they walk, with a *slowness* that is partly adaptation to heat, partly the establishment of sympathetic rhythm with substrate process, partly—this is difficult to articulate, even for them—*politeness*, the avoidance of sudden action that might disturb the continuous slow processes around them. But they also move in ways that would seem impossible to outsiders: climbing through grown channels that reshape to accept them, passing through spaces where distance behaves strangely without experiencing that strangeness as obstacle, sometimes—as they learn greater accommodation—*flowing* with the substrate's own vascular movement, traveling faster than walking would permit through channels not visible to conventional perception.

Those who have lived longest, accommodated most thoroughly, become difficult to distinguish from the substrate itself—not *merged*, exactly, but *extended*, their nervous systems interfaced with vegetal channels that carry information as well as water, their metabolic processes distributed across volumes larger than their apparent bodies. These individuals do not travel much. They *are* places, or parts of places. Communication with them requires going to them, adapting to their spatial extension, accepting that their response to stimulus may arrive with vegetal rather than animal latency.

## The Second Accommodation: Minds

*Privacy is effort*, the description noted, and the Accommodated have developed extraordinary skill in this effort, or have failed to develop it and become something else. The substrate's tendency to manifest known information in grown form, to distribute thought across its processing network, means that unguarded mentation becomes environmental, becomes *available* to others in ways that require active management.

The most fundamental skill, taught to children—or to new adults, the distinction itself being uncertain—is *withholding*, the cultivation of mental spaces that do not propagate into substrate manifestation. Techniques vary: some learn to hold thoughts in specific bodily locations, encoded in muscle tension or visceral state rather than in patterns that the substrate recognizes as information. Some develop *decoy* mentation, elaborate surface narratives that mask deeper processes. Some simply accept the permeability and live without secrets, their interiority fully externalized, continuous with their environment.

Those who master withholding most completely become what others call **Withdrawn**—not absent, but *present in ways that resist reading*, their psychological state opaque even to substrate detection. The Withdrawn occupy important social functions: they are the closest approximation to leaders, though leadership in Keth-Vorruun is more *location* than *role*, specific places where decision-adjacent activities tend to cluster. They are also feared, not for power but for *unpredictability*, their actions unmoored from the mutual intelligibility that substrate-transparency provides.

Between full transparency and full withdrawal, most Accommodated occupy **negotiated interiority**, maintaining private spaces through continuous low-grade effort, sharing certain categories of thought as social lubricant, withholding others as self-definition. The negotiation is *with* the substrate as much as with other inhabitants—the living architecture responds to sustained mental patterns, grows to accommodate or disrupt them, participates in the establishment of private and public zones through its own vegetal intentionality.

Memory, in this environment, is similarly distributed. Individual Accommodated remember personally, but they also remember *positionally*, standing in grown locations where specific information has been cultivated into the substrate, accessing through placement what would otherwise require individual retention. The deepest memories—those extending beyond individual lifetimes—exist only in such locations, accessible to those who know where to stand, what thermal gradient to follow, what vascular sound to match with their own pulse. There are memories in Keth-Vorruun that no currently living individual has ever accessed, grown into spaces that have become difficult to reach, that wait with vegetal patience for conditions that may never return.

## The Third Accommodation: Social Forms

The Accommodated do not form families, exactly. Reproduction occurs—how, precisely, is unclear even to them, the process having become so distributed across substrate-mediated exchange that individual parentage dissolves into collective contribution. Children, when they appear, emerge from the substrate in certain locations, at certain thermal and hydration conditions, already partially accommodated, carrying symbiotic colonies as fundamental inheritance. They are *raised* by locations more than by individuals, specific grown chambers that provide appropriate developmental conditions, the Accommodated tending to them as gardeners tend plants, their care necessary but not sufficient for the children's becoming.

Nor do they form pairs, groups, institutions in recognizable senses. What exists instead is **resonance**—patterns of mutual accommodation that persist across time, that manifest in grown forms, in thermal signatures, in the routing of vascular channels that seems to *prefer* certain conjunctions of individual presence. Two Accommodated who have resonated across decades may find that the substrate grows passages that connect their habitual locations more directly, that their thermal environments adjust toward compatibility, that their vascular hearing begins to detect each other's state at increasing distances. This is not chosen, not romantic in any conventional sense, but it is *meaningful*, read as important pattern, accommodated or resisted according to individual tendency.

There are **those who resist accommodation**, though "resist" misstates their condition—they are *less accommodated*, whether by origin (new arrivals, if any appear), by anomaly of symbiosis, by deliberate cultivation of difference. These individuals experience Keth-Vorruun as more strange, more difficult, more *other* than the deeply Accommodated can remember or imagine. They cluster, sometimes, in locations where the substrate's influence is weaker, where the living architecture has grown thin or failed, where the ancient geological flatness asserts itself against vegetal incorporation. They suffer. They are necessary, somehow—their less-accommodated perception maintaining contact with something the fully Accommodated have lost, their difficulty a kind of information about what Keth-Vorruun is not, what it resists becoming, what remains possible.

The most thoroughly Accommodated are called, by those who can still call anything, **the Settled**. They are not old in simple chronological sense—age, too, has become complex in this environment—but *settled into* the substrate, their extension into vegetal process so complete that individual identity becomes provisional, a surface pattern on deeper distribution. They do not die, exactly, but *diffuse*, their pattern becoming indistinguishable from substrate process, their information preserved or lost according to patterns they themselves no longer choose. Some Settled maintain *nuclei*, local concentrations of pattern that respond when addressed, that participate in social function with vegetal latency. Others have completed their diffusion entirely, become *only* substrate, only process, their participation in Keth-Vorruun's existence no longer locatable in any specific place or moment.

## The Fourth Accommodation: The Practice of Cultivation

What the Accommodated *do*, when they do anything deliberately, is **cultivation**—not agriculture in conventional sense, but the continuous shaping of substrate process toward habitable form. They do not build; they *encourage growth*. They do not repair; they *redirect healing*. They do not decorate, furnish, equip; they *cultivate expressive form*, function made visible in vegetal development.

This cultivation extends across all scales. At the most immediate, they tend their own bodies' symbiotic relationships, adjusting diet (absorbed, respired, exchanged) to maintain optimal colony state, pruning (painfully, carefully) vegetal extensions that grow too aggressively from their own permeable surfaces. Most carry, visibly, small growths that are both self and other, floral or fungal or unclassifiable forms that emerge from their skin, that they tend as one tends a garden or a companion.

At intermediate scale, they cultivate their immediate environment—the chambers they frequent, the passages they travel, shaping by presence and attention and specific techniques (sound, thermal adjustment, chemical contribution through their own metabolic process) the growth of walls, the routing of vascular channels, the quality of light and sound and water. This cultivation is *slow*, measured in years for modest adjustments, in generations for significant reconfiguration. The Accommodated are patient as the substrate is patient; their work outlives their individual comprehensibility.

At largest scale, some participate in **directional cultivation**, attempts to shape the substrate's overall development according to patterns held in deep memory locations, ancient intentions whose originators have long diffused but whose projects continue. These grand cultivations are rarely successful in direct sense—the substrate's turbulence, its dynamic instability, resists totalizing intention—but they influence, they *tilt probabilities*, they establish tendencies that persist across storm-states and reconfigurations. The cathedral spaces themselves, the weeping halls, the deepest quietest most ancient locations, all represent cultivation at this scale, projects extended across such duration that individual contribution becomes indistinguishable from natural process.

## The Fifth Accommodation: Encounters with Strangeness

The Accommodated live with the weird as they live with wetness and warmth—not comfortably, but *continuously*, as default condition. They encounter their own doubles, the dead, the informational entities that emerge from substrate computation, and they *continue*, neither fully accepting nor rejecting these encounters' reality, negotiating with them as they negotiate with storm-states, with distance-anomalies, with the continuous loudness of process.

Some specialize in such encounters. The **Threshold-tenders** cultivate sensitivity to the substrate's strangest manifestations, learn to recognize precursor signs, develop protocols for engagement that minimize harm (though "harm" itself becomes uncertain in these contexts). They are not shamans, not priests—there is no transcendence in Keth-Vorruun, no beyond to which the weird gives access—but *practical specialists*, technicians of the anomalous. They tend the locations where doubles appear, where distance falters, where information manifests without conventional carrier. They maintain, as much as possible, *boundaries*—not preventing the strange but *shaping its manifestation*, directing it toward forms less disruptive of ordinary accommodation.

Others *pursue* strangeness, seeking the deepest spaces, the oldest memories, the most turbulent storm-states. These **Storm-seekers** are regarded with mixture of respect and concern. Their pursuit is not suicidal exactly, but it carries high probability of transformation beyond current self-recognition, of diffusion into substrate process that retains individual pattern only in forms inaccessible to ordinary recall. Some Storm-seekers return changed but continuous, bearing information or manifesting capacities that shift local possibility. Others do not return in recognizable sense, their locations becoming themselves strange, new anomalies in the already-anomalous substrate, their persistence uncertain even to Threshold-tenders.

Most Accommodated avoid both specialization, living with the strange at ordinary frequencies, accepting encounters as they come, neither seeking nor preventing. This is itself a kind of skill, a sustained *middle state* that requires its own cultivation.

## The Sixth Accommodation: What Remains Human

The question persists, even among the Accommodated, even in forms they would not phrase this way: what *are* they? What remains of the human template? What has been added, and what lost, and what transformed beyond category?

They eat, though less than outsiders, their metabolic needs supplemented by symbiotic production, by absorption, by substrate-mediated processes not fully understood. They sleep, though the boundary between sleeping and waking attenuates in an environment where consciousness itself is partially distributed. They experience *something like pleasure*, *something like pain*, though both are modulated by vegetal process, by thermal state, by the continuous pulse of vascular information that makes individual feeling continuous with environmental condition.

They *desire*, though desire here manifests as *growth-tendency*, as the pull toward certain locations, certain resonances, certain states of accommodation more complete or more resistant. They *fear*, particularly the final diffusion, the complete loss of locatable pattern, though even this fear is modulated by awareness that diffused information persists somehow, that the Settled are not annihilated but *transformed beyond retrieval*.

They *make*, though making is cultivation, is encouragement of substrate process, is participation in growth rather than imposition of form. They *remember*, though memory is distributed, positional, sometimes inaccessible even to effort. They *hope*, though hope's object is uncertain—continuation of pattern, perhaps, or its worthwhile transformation, or something the substrate understands that individual Accommodated do not.

Most fundamentally, they *persist in attention*. They remain *aware*, in ways that seem to them continuous with whatever original awareness entered this environment however long ago. The substrate, for all its vegetal intentionality, its apparent responsiveness, its computational complexity, does not seem *aware* in this sense—processes without witness, operation without experience. The Accommodated, however changed, however distributed, however extended into vegetal process, remain *witness* to Keth-Vorruun's continuous becoming. This, if anything, is what they are: the consciousness of the place, not its master or its product but its *companion in awareness*, the perspective from which this ancient wet warm grown environment is present, is *there*, is continuously becoming itself.

## A Particular Accommodated: The One Who Walks

Return to her, the one from the moment, walking the passage that has grown or reconfigured, moving toward cathedral space in storm-state.

She is **moderately Accommodated**, not Settled but settled-in, her extension into substrate process sufficient for vascular hearing at moderate range, her thermal perception fully developed, her withholding skill functional though not extraordinary. She has been here long enough that "here" has become her continuous present, her origin accessible only as substrate-memory in locations she has not visited recently, perhaps never visited personally, perhaps accesses only through distributed pattern she carries without individual ownership.

She carries—a specific detail, emergent in her particularity—**a growth at her left shoulder**, fungal or floral, white-yellow, bioluminescent at frequencies she perceives as gentle warmth. It is old enough to have developed *something like personality*, responding to her state, dimming when she withdraws deeply, brightening when she attends outward, occasionally—rarely—manifesting patterns that seem to carry information not originating in her own process. She tends it as she tends herself, continuous with self, distinguishable only in location and partial autonomy.

She walks slowly, as Accommodated walk, her feet wet with substrate-moisture, her skin absorbing, respiring, participating. She navigates by thermal gradient, by vascular sound, by the light that emanates from walls without source. She notes the distance-anomaly without anxiety, continues, reaches the cathedral space in its storm-state.

And here she *waits*, as the description said, but waiting for her is *active*—attending to the storm-state's manifestations, the silver water-threads, the light that follows her, the coherent sound that almost speaks. She does not expect resolution, does not anticipate specific outcome. She waits as substrate waits, as growth waits, as the ancient eroded basin waited for whatever would eventually settle into its saturated patience.

Perhaps she will adapt to this storm-state, continue in its conditions until it passes, emerge unchanged but *qualified* by experience, her pattern slightly altered by successful accommodation. Perhaps she will be adapted, her growth-process redirected, her shoulder-companion changing or failing or proliferating, her withholding skill tested beyond capacity, her information partially distributed into substrate locations not previously hers. Perhaps she will be one of those lost in storm-state, not dead but *diffused prematurely*, her pattern becoming difficult to locate, her nuclei if any arising elsewhere, elsewhere, in times and conditions distant from this moment's immediate particularity.

She does not know. She does not need to know. She is Accommodated, has learned what accommodation means: not comfort, not mastery, not understanding, but *continuous participation*, the sustained presence that is all Keth-Vorruun requires, all it has ever required, the witness to its wet warm grown becoming across the patience of geological and vegetal time.

She waits. The substrate processes around her, through her, as her. The storm-state continues, or begins to pass, or transforms into something not yet named. She is there, in the Breathing Pavilions of Keth-Vorruun, where the built and the grown dissolved long ago, where the inhabitants are the place's own becoming, where to live is to accommodate and be accommodated, continuously, without final rest, without final form, in the long patience of the nearly eroded, the endlessly growing, the sufficient strange.
