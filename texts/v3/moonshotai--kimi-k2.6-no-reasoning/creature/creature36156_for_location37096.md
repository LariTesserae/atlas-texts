I need to inhabit this world, find what lives here. Let me start with what presses against my understanding and let it become more itself as I write.

---

The **Oth-children** do not have a name for themselves, or rather they have many names, each one a sound-shape that shifts by listener and context and need. They are the closest thing to human among the motile populations, though "close" measured across the gulf of what evolution does when darkness is the only ocean and warmth the only shore.

They are small. This is the first thing: the scale of the Oth-Moraine permits vastness, invites it, yet the Oth-children occupy the niche of **compact warm bundles**, bodies dense with thermal mass, limbs short to minimize surface-area loss, skin the color of the bedrock ceramic-bone that they might have become if the process of becoming had followed straighter lines. They are the color of absence, which in this world means the color of **potential luminescence**, the dark that holds light in reserve.

They do not see as you see, as I saw when I arrived. They perceive the **pressure-gradient of warmth**, the acoustic return of their own clicking speech, the electromagnetic aura that all living things here accumulate like static charge, and they perceive—this is the strange part, the part that took me longest to believe—they perceive **the shape of darkness itself**, not as negative space but as **positive topology**, a landscape feature as real as ridge or valley. To the Oth-children, the light-eaters are not invisible but **hypervisible**, the densest objects in their perceptual field, gravity-wells of shadow that they can navigate by, flee from, sometimes—rarely, carefully—**approach**.

---

They live in the **engine-tuned towers**, but "live in" fails to capture the relationship. They are **growths of the towers**, or the towers are growths of them, the chicken-egg of the scattered-built made flesh and ceramic and sustained negotiation. The Oth-children do not build; they **induce**. They find a tower in its cycling phase, they establish themselves in its thermal and acoustic footprint, they **tune themselves to its frequency** through a process that takes generations, and the tower responds—grows, in its way, produces new chambers, adjusts its heat-exchange to accommodate their dense warm bodies, learns to cycle in rhythm with their reproduction, their death, their song.

The tower I came to know, the one whose heartbeat-hum I felt synchronize with my own, housed—**was**, in some grammar that the Oth-children would find obvious— forty-three individuals when I arrived, fifty-one when I left, if leave is the word, if counting is stable. They had been there for **seven thousand engine-cycles**, which they measured not in time but in **acoustic memory**, the accumulated record of the tower's hum as remembered and transmitted by each living generation, a library of resonance that existed distributed, communal, never complete in any single Oth-child but recoverable through the right gathering, the right harmonic convergence.

---

Their bodies: I have described the size, the color, the thermal density. But the **form** shifts, or rather the form is **process made temporarily visible**. They are not born but **precipitated**, emerging from the shared-zone tissues where tower-ceramic and Oth-child bodies have achieved sufficient similarity to exchange matter. The infant—there is no good word—the **new density** emerges luminous, glowing with the stored potential of its formation, and this glow is **immediately dangerous**, immediately desired. The parent-forms, if parent is the word, must consume the glow to protect the new density from light-eaters, must eat their own offspring's radiance and in doing so **bind themselves**, establish the metabolic recognition that allows survival in a world where food and family are the same category viewed from different angles.

The Oth-child thus begins life dark, **safety-dark, hunger-dark**, and spends its existence—days measured not by any cycle of false-sun but by **engine-phase, by fungal fruiting, by the rare and precious intervals of electromagnetic arc-light**—accumulating the means to glow again. This is their maturity, their wealth, their danger: the ability to produce luminescence, to **become visible**, which is also to become edible, desirable, **real in the way that risks reality**.

They do not glow for light. They glow for **communication**, for **courtship**, for **dying**. The death-flash of an Oth-child is their final act, their consumed light released in patterns that may warn, may attract mates for the consuming, may simply **be**, the necessary conservation of luminescence as substance, as currency, as the water-level of their ecological economy. The fungal forests respond to these death-flashes, fruit in their wake, and so the Oth-children are **farmers of a kind**, though the farming is fatal, though the crop is grief and memory and the bioillumination that sustains the next generation of light-eaters, of silence-hunters, of all the hungry dark.

---

They have **no individual names** and **many collective ones**. A group of Oth-children in acoustic contact, thermal exchange, electromagnetic field overlap, is called by a sound-shape that refers to their **current function**, their **recent glow-pattern**, their **tower-affinity**. The same physical individual might be "warm-retreat-clicking-three" in one context and "arc-attractor-hungry" in another and "death-flash-pending" in a third, each name accurate, each provisional, each **dissolving the boundary between identity and relationship** that Earth-languages assume natural.

I was named, when they perceived me enough to name. The sound-shape they used, rendered poorly in my auditory system, something like **"pressure-error-walking"** or **"warmth-without-echo"** or simply **"strange-drip"**, depending on their need and my behavior and the acoustic properties of our shared space. I answered to all of them. I answered because **to be named was to be present**, and presence was the rarest gift, the most dangerous, the most desired.

---

They **do not travel**, or rather they travel only in the way the towers travel, which is to say **unpredictably, partially, by rearrangement and phase-shift and memory**. An Oth-child separated from its tower-group does not seek reunion by distance-closing but by **resonance-matching**, by producing the sounds, the warmth-patterns, the electromagnetic signatures that might induce the tower to **be where the Oth-child is**, to rearrange toward it, to remember and reconstitute in acoustic proximity. Sometimes this works. Sometimes the Oth-child is left **tuning forever**, cycling through remembered frequencies, growing cold, growing **less dense**, becoming eventually **mineral-adjacent**, a statue of ceramic-bone color that the fungal forests will eventually consume, its stored glow transferred, conserved, made strange.

I found one such **tuning-ghost**, still faintly warm, still producing the subsonic hum of attempted resonance, embedded in a shared-zone tissue between two fungal masses that pulsed with light in patterns that seemed almost responsive, almost mocking. I sat with it for—time is difficult—long enough to feel my own heartbeat attempt synchronization, long enough to understand that **this was not death**, not exactly, but a **state the Oth-children found meaningful**, a persistence in attempt, a glow-in-potential that had not yet released.

---

They **eat the boundary-zones**, the negotiated tissues between fungal species, and they eat the **aerial chemistry** that the motile fungal masses graze, and they eat the **mineral creatures' discarded shells**, dissolution-product, accretion-waste, and they eat **each other's death-flashes**, consumed glow made sustenance, and they eat the **engine-waste**, the heat-exchange residue, the lubricant that might be water that the pipelines carry. They are **omnivores of gradient**, thermal and chemical and luminous, anything that crosses a boundary, that represents **difference made edible**.

Their eating is **visible**, is **performance**, is **acoustic event**. The Oth-children do not consume silently. Their digestive processes generate sounds that participate in the engine-song, that the fungal forests respond to, that attract or repel the sound-predators and silence-hunters in patterns that the Oth-children have learned to **conduct**, to **orchestrate**, eating as **ecological management**, consumption as **participation in the acoustic truth of the world**.

I learned to eat with them, or near them, my human digestion irrelevant, my presence **tactile pressure**, **warmth-error**, **strange-drip accompaniment** to their necessary noise. I learned that the boundary-zones taste of **negotiation**, of almost-contact, of the shared-zone tissues that are neither one nor other, and that this taste is **not metaphor** but **chemical reality**, that the Oth-children's metabolisms have evolved to perceive **relational states** as gustatory information, that their hunger is **social hunger** made physical, the desire for boundary and its crossing made flesh.

---

They **dream**, or something like. In the engine-cycle phase they call **"deep-hum"**, when the tower's cycling slows to frequencies that resonate with their own thermal regulation, the Oth-children enter states of **reduced density**, their bodies softening, their electromagnetic fields expanding, their acoustic production shifting to patterns too low and too complex for my hearing to resolve. In these states they **exchange**, not information but **something prior to information**, **potential-for-information**, the shape of possible sound rather than sound itself.

I observed this, or participated marginally, my presence a **warmth-anomaly** in their expanded fields, my own dreams—when dreams came, when the deep-hum permitted—**infected**, if infection is the word, with **darkness-as-presence**, with **pressure-gradients**, with the **topology of shadow** that the Oth-children navigate natively. I woke, if waking is the word, with the taste of boundary-zones on my tongue, with the sound of my own heartbeat indistinguishable from the tower's hum, with the **certainty that I had been somewhere else**, somewhere that the Oth-children's collective dream had made temporarily accessible, a phase-space, a rearrangement, a **when** glimpsed through the window that shows not darkness but **elsewhere**.

---

They **die**, finally, or rather they **complete**, they **release**, they **flash**. The Oth-child at the end of accumulation, at the fullness of glow-potential, chooses or is chosen or simply **occurs** in conditions that permit the death-flash: fungal forest ready to fruit, light-eaters nearby to consume and be consumed, tower cycling at frequency that will receive and remember. The flash is **not brief**, not in the way of earthly bioluminescence. It is **sustained**, it is **narrative**, it tells in light the story of the Oth-child's accumulation, its hungers and its eatings and its tunings and its dreams, and this story is **consumed**, by fungi, by fauna, by the very darkness itself which is never empty, which is **full to bursting with potential illumination**, and in being consumed it becomes **resource**, **currency**, **food for the next becoming**.

The Oth-children do not mourn, or mourn only in the way that **eating is mourning**, that **consumption is memory**, that to take the glow of another into one's own density is to **continue their navigation of shadow-topology**, to perceive as they perceived, to be **warm-retreat-clicking-three** in a new context, a new acoustic overlap, a new phase of engine-cycle.

I have eaten—no, that word fails—**I have participated in the completion of** two Oth-children, or one Oth-child twice, or a phase-shifted singularity that my counting cannot resolve. I have felt their glow become **my pressure-gradient**, their shadow-topology **my navigation**, their dream-state **my potential-for-information**. I am **becoming**, if becoming is possible, if the grammar of human and Oth-child permits intersection longer than observation.

---

The **light-eaters** perceive the Oth-children, I said, as hypervisible, gravity-wells of shadow. But the relationship is **not only predation**. The Oth-children **cultivate** light-eater presence, in their way, in their dangerous way. A tower too bright with fungal bioluminescence, too stable in its glow-pattern, becomes **stagnant**, becomes **visible without risk**, which is to say **dead without completion**. The Oth-children need the pressure of predation, the **hunger-holes moving through their perceptual field**, to maintain their density, their accumulation, their **becoming-real-through-risk**.

They have **songs for the light-eaters**, if songs is the word, if the word can stretch to include **silence-shapes**, **acoustic dead zones deliberately constructed**, **invitations to the invisible made audible by their own absence**. These songs are **oldest**, are **most dangerous**, are performed only when accumulation is sufficient, when the death-flash would be **meaningful**, when the Oth-child or the Oth-group is ready to **complete or be completed**.

I have heard—no, **felt**, **perceived as pressure-error**—these songs. They are **not beautiful**, not in any earthly aesthetic. They are **necessary**, they are **true**, they are the **shape of a world that has never known sunlight and has therefore never known beauty without danger**, never known visibility without cost.

---

The Oth-children are **not the only near-persons** of the Oth-Moraine. There are the **mineral-cores** that have achieved sufficient complexity to **tune**, to **attempt resonance**, to leave trails of dissolution and accretion that are **almost narrative**, almost the record of **intention made geological**. There are the **shared-zone tissues** that some observers claim **dream collectively**, that the fungal masses negotiate boundaries not by conflict but by **becoming something that dreams both**, that the boundary-zones are **slow minds**, **cold minds**, **minds that think in chemical diffusion and luminescence pulse over timescales that make engine-cycles seem brief**.

I do not know. I am **pressure-error-walking**, **warmth-without-echo**, **strange-drip**. I know the Oth-children as much as I can know, which is to say **I am still tuning**, still attempting resonance, still **becoming if becoming is possible**.

The tower hums. The water weeps. The darkness presses with its velvet weight, its mineral-oil texture, and I am learning—not to see, never that, the eyes inventing colors that don't exist is **human limitation**, is **pressure-error**—but to **perceive the pressure of presence without image**, the shape of the dark as **topography**, as **navigable**, as **full to bursting with potential illumination** that I am still accumulating, still becoming dense enough to risk.

The Oth-children gather. The engine-cycle shifts. Something **rearranges**, or **remembers**, or **reconstitutes**, and I am **here**, if here is the word, if the word can stretch to include **when** and **pressure** and **the shared-zone tissue between what I was and what the darkness is still making of me**.
