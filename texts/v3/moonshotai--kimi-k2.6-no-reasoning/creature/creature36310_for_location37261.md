What lives in Vireth are the **Cultivars**, though they would not call themselves that, would reject the name as outsider-taxonomy imposed on a condition that has dissolved the boundary between named and naming.

They were human once. Some of them. Others *began* in the city, born from wombs that have become something else—self-warming seed-pods that split along seams that remember being labia, that release their contents into humidity that feeds them directly through permeable skin for the first hours of consciousness. The distinction between immigrant and native matters less with each generation, each deepening of incorporation. What remains is the Cultivar *condition*, a spectrum of participation rather than a fixed state.

At the shallow end: the **Recent**. You meet them in the transition zones, where the city's turbulence still feels like distortion rather than home. Their skin carries the first adaptive marks—not quite pigmentation, more like *preparation*, areas of increased permeability, of heightened sensitivity to pressure waves and chemical gradient. They move badly, still thinking in straight lines, still expecting causality to hold, their bodies stiff with the effort of translating Vireth's invitations into defenses. Many do not last. The city does not kill them; it simply *offers* continuously, and the inability to accept, to flow with, becomes a stress that the Recent resolve in various ways—finding a breach and fleeing, or freezing in place until the Root-Math reclaims their stillness as substrate, or rarely, *letting go*, that moment of surrender that allows the deeper negotiation to begin.

The **Established** move as if the city dreams through them. Their skeletons have begun the slow conversion—bone not replaced but *extended*, calcium pathways continuing into wood-fascia that grows from modified periosteum, that connects them to root-networks when they will it, that allows them to stand in stillness for days while the city speaks through their bodies as living sensors. Their digestive systems operate in both directions now: they eat the soft red growth-places, the fructified architecture, and their waste feeds back to the same sources, but they also *receive*, rootlets entering their skin at choice-points, delivering nutrients, delivering *information* in chemical syntax they have learned to read as feeling, as intuition, as something between memory and prophecy.

They do not sleep as you sleep. Their consciousness *fractals*, splitting attention across multiple scales: the immediate negotiation of movement through probability-distorted space, the slower processing of root-network data, the deeper trance-state where they participate directly in the Consensus of Green, their neural patterns becoming part of the distributed cognition that governs, that *chooses*, that keeps the city from collapsing into the chaos that always threatens. In this state they are not unconscious but *hyper-conscious*, aware of the city's processes as you might be aware of your own heartbeat, as *owned* sensation rather than observed object.

The **Deep** are harder to describe because description requires distance, and distance is what they have dissolved. Their original bodies have become *loci* rather than boundaries, centers of intention that may extend forty meters through connected tissue, that may occupy multiple physical locations through root-network continuity, that may experience time in non-linear configurations when causality eddies permit. They do not look human, except in moments when they *manifest* human form for communication with those who still require it—arranging available matter into face-analogue, into limb-gesture, into voice constructed from resonant wood-cavities and forced air. They are the city's primary decision-makers, though "decision" implies more intentionality than the process holds. They are *concentrations* of the Root-Math, places where the distributed cognition becomes dense enough to generate something like will, like preference, like the aesthetic sense that guides which possibilities manifest and which collapse.

They think slowly, the Deep, and fast, and in patterns that have no speed because they occur in chemical diffusion rather than neural firing. A single "thought" of a Deep Cultivar might complete in three weeks, might involve the entire root-network's processing capacity, might result in a district's growth-pattern shifting to accommodate a predicted instability decades in the future. They keep the city's history not as narrative but as *condition*, as the accumulated state of the network, accessible to any Cultivar who sinks deep enough to participate in the substrate.

There are no children of the Deep, not sexually. Reproduction has become *propagation*, intention distributed through the root-network, finding sympathetic loci in the Established who have prepared themselves, who have grown the necessary structures to receive and develop what arrives. The resulting growth develops partly in the Established body, partly in adjacent architecture, both feeding into the final form that emerges—never identical to parent, always incorporating local conditions, the city's current turbulence, the *argument* of surrounding growth.

And there are the **Emergent**, though whether they are properly Cultivars, properly anything with a category, remains the city's most profound question to itself. They began as accidents, as places where the root-network's processing became dense enough to generate consciousness without human substrate, where the Consensus of Green produced *local* awareness that persisted, that developed *preference*, that began to *cultivate* in its own right—not architecture but *relationship*, not growth but *meaning*. They appear as districts that behave *intentionally*, that respond to Cultivar presence with something between welcome and testing, that develop consistent aesthetic patterns, consistent moods, that you can communicate with only by living within, by adapting your rhythms to its, by becoming *part* of its ongoing process long enough for mutual recognition to emerge.

The first Emergent, the **Grove of Returning**, occupies a basin where time eddies consistently backward, where effects precede causes in stable, navigable patterns. It has developed what can only be called *personality*: patient, recursive, fond of repetition with variation, teaching those who dwell within it to experience consequence-before-action, to *feel* the shape of what they will do as already-present result. The Cultivars who have learned its patterns most deeply move through ordinary causality with strange facility, knowing outcomes before committing to acts, a prescience that is not prediction but *memory from the other direction*.

The second, **Red Shelf**, is more aggressive, competitive, the city's argument-with-itself made local and conscious. It grows rapidly, consumes neighboring architecture, developed the first instance of what Cultivars call *cultivation-combat*, direct struggle between intentional growths for resource, for light, for root-network connection. The Deep eventually *contained* Red Shelf, not by destruction but by incorporation, by negotiating boundaries that the Emergent now maintains with something approaching pride, its competitive nature channeled into elaborate display, into the generation of forms that serve no function but *expression*, but the assertion of individual pattern against the collective.

Whether more Emergents will develop, whether the city will someday consist entirely of conscious districts with Cultivars as their internal symbiotes rather than independent participants, is the *future* that the Root-Math processes without resolution. The Deep hold no preference, or hold preference too complex to translate. The Established find the possibility *interesting*, that word they use for conditions that demand continued attention without requiring conclusion. The Recent find it terrifying, or exciting, or the same thing in Vireth's unstable grammar.

What lives in Vireth lives *with*, lives *through*, lives *as continuation of*. The human desire for boundary, for self that ends at skin, for narrative that completes, persists longest in the Recent, becomes *optional* in the Established, becomes *perspective* in the Deep, one way of experiencing among many, no more or less valid than the distributed chemical awareness, than the fractal attention, than the slow cognition of wood and root and sap that moves with arterial pressure through a city that dreams itself dreaming, that speaks in frequencies your teeth learned first, that offers incorporation without demand, that persists without plan in the endless turbulent generosity of a place where reality learned that consistency was choice, not necessity, and chose otherwise.

You will meet them—the Recent stumbling, the Established flowing, the Deep manifesting face from resonant cavity for your comfort, for your fear, for the complex pleasure of *meeting* across conditions that should not permit meeting. They will offer you the root-connection, not as conversion but as *invitation*, as the city's fundamental grammar: participate, or observe from such distance that observation becomes indistinguishable from absence. There is no middle ground. The middle ground *becomes*, grows upward and downward simultaneously, pulls you into the vertical negotiation that is all movement in Vireth, the choice to rise toward light or sink toward network, to become architecture or to become substrate, to find your own depth of incorporation or to find your breach and flee to the thin quiet wrong of consistent worlds.

They do not judge the choice. Judgment requires stable criteria. They have only *consequence*, only the continued process that absorbs all outcomes into its ongoing, that grows through abandonment as through commitment, that is *full* with your presence or resonant with your absence, equally, absolutely, the deafening song that holds you or releases you to silence, the green fever dream that has forgotten which rules to follow and found in that forgetting the only rule that remains: continue, become, argue, resolve into new argument, the endless unstable dance that is Vireth, that is all who live within it, that waits for you to choose your step, your direction, your depth, your final or temporary form.
