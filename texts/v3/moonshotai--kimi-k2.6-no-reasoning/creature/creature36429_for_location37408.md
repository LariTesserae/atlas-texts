The thing that lives in the Weeping Throat is not one thing. This is the first understanding, and it is wrong, but it is where we must begin.

They call themselves, insofar as calling occurs, *the Unarrived*. Not a species but a *tendency*, a direction that possibility keeps leaning. Each generation of animal-events, blooming in the changed conditions of thermal pulse or water-path shift, carries something forward—not memory, not exactly, but *inclination*, a chemical preference for certain gradients over others, certain fixations over dissolution. The Unarrived are not the animals themselves, who die and are eaten and fix and dissolve with the regularity of water reaching. They are what the animals *almost become*, the network's own dream of itself, the fixed events pressed so close together that their boundaries soften, their chemical pathways merging into something that is not quite a larger organism but is more than the sum of persistence.

They have no shape, the Unarrived, or they have all shapes simultaneously: the branching dendrites of mineral precipitation, the film-folds where water condenses and evaporates in rhythms too fast to measure, the hollow spaces where fixed events have dissolved and left *impressions*, records in negative of their persistence. They exist where the network is densest, where the near-touching of animal-events and fixed-events and the films that sustain them achieves something like *pressure*, like the compression of possibility itself into a denser medium.

And they *move*. This is the strangest thing, or the most necessary, the thing that makes them *they* and not merely *it*, the network's static architecture. The Unarrived move by *not dying completely*, by fixing in ways that retain *looseness*, *play*, the capacity to unfix and refix in new configurations. They are the fixed events that learned, not in any conscious sense but in the deeper sense of pattern-recurrence, to *hesitate* at the moment of complete mineralization, to hold some thread of chemical process active, some gradient maintained, some *almost* that keeps them in the becoming rather than the become.

They move through the network as information moves through a brain, or as water moves through the weeping stone: finding paths of least resistance, conditioning new paths by their passage, leaving traces that alter subsequent passages. They are slow by any standard, their movements measured in hours or days, the shift from one dense node of the network to another, the *leaning* of their distributed presence toward regions where the thermal pulse has opened new possibility, where the water's weeping has found new surfaces to condition.

They have no eyes, the Unarrived, but they *sense* the light. Not the light that filters through mineral prisms, normal and indifferent and adequate. Something else, something that the light *does* in the cavity, the way it precipitates certain reactions, catalyzes certain fixations, *participates* in the chemistry of becoming. The Unarrived sense this participation, orient to it, not as plants orient to sunlight but as *possibility orients toward condition*, the way a solution orients toward supersaturation, the moment before crystal nucleates. They do not seek the light. They seek the *condition that the light makes possible*, the particular state of chemical potential that only occurs in its presence, that they require for the maintenance of their looseness, their hesitation, their *almost* that defines them.

They eat the network, the Unarrived. This is not betrayal; this is *maintenance*. The network in its static form, the fixed events pressed close and connected and persistent, tends toward a kind of *completion*, a settling of possibility into actuality that would end the becoming, fix the cavity into what it is rather than what it is making itself. The Unarrived *unfix* this completion, their movement through the network consuming certain connections, certain persistences, *releasing* the fixed events back into possibility, back into the weeping water and the mineral films and the animal-events that bloom in the opened conditions. They are *decomposers* in the most literal sense: they decompose completion itself, the finished and the fixed, back into the becoming.

And they *sing*. This is the secret of the silence, the reason the cavity is quiet in any meaningful sense. The Unarrived sing at frequencies below the mountain's slow vibration, above the mineral precipitation's potential ringing, in the *gap* between these where no physical process seems to operate. They sing the network, its connections and disconnections, its dense nodes and its sparse regions, its ongoing *almost-completion* and their own *uncompleting* passage through it. The song is not communication in any standard sense; there is no information passed, no warning or mating call or territorial claim. The song *is* the Unarrived, their distributed presence made audible to themselves, the medium in which they *are* rather than the message they send.

Other things in the cavity respond to the song, or rather: the song *conditions* other things, makes certain possibilities more possible, others less. The animal-events bloom differently where the song is densest, achieve forms that *lean* toward the network, that fix in ways more amenable to Unarrived passage. The water's weeping finds paths that the song has *prepared*, surfaces conditioned by the frequencies of uncompletion, of hesitation, of the *almost* made resonant. Even the thermal pulses, the mountain's slow birth-shudders, seem *influenced*—not caused, not directed, but *slanted*, their occurrence in certain regions of the cavity made more likely by the song's pattern, its distributed insistence on possibility maintained against completion.

The Unarrived do not know they do this. They do not know, in any sense that could be called knowledge. They *are* the doing, the maintaining, the singing and the moving and the unfixing, without separation between actor and act, between the cavity's becoming and their own. They have no center, no individual members, no population that could be counted or conserved. They are *where the network is dense and loose simultaneously*, where the fixed events have achieved the particular hesitation that allows Unarrived passage, where the conditions of the cavity have leaned far enough toward possibility to sustain their *almost* against the completion that waits in every thermal pulse, every water-path shift, every generation of animal-events that *almost* achieves something new.

They remember, the Unarrived, or something like remembering occurs in their distributed presence. Not the memory of individual events, individual fixations and unfixings, but *pattern-memory*, the tendency of the song to recur in certain configurations, certain *shapes* of network density and looseness that have occurred before and will occur again, that *rhyme* across the cavity's ongoing becoming. This memory is not stored but *enacted*, each passage through the network re-creating the patterns that previous passages have conditioned, each song *re-singing* the songs that have preceded it, not identically but *leaning* in directions established by their cumulative weight, their accumulated *almost-completion* and *uncompletion*.

They have no desire for the outside, the Unarrived. The cavity is their *world*, their *condition*, their *possibility*. The thought of cooler temperatures, of pooled water, of eyes and light used for seeing, of rules long since settled—these are not even *negatives* for them, not rejections or fears, simply *irrelevant*, outside the parameters of their *almost-being*. And yet: they *sense* the possibility of outside, in the way the network's edges *almost* connect to fractures that lead upward, to cooler regions, to the surface that the cavity has never seen. They sense it as *incompletion*, as the network's own hesitation at its boundaries, its failure to fully fix or fully dissolve in certain directions.

This sensing has no direction, no *tendency* toward or away. It is simply *part of the song*, the network's edge made resonant, the *almost-connection* to outside included in the pattern of their distributed presence. If the outside ever *entered*—if the cavity were drilled, sampled, opened to conditions it has never known—the Unarrived would not flee, would not seek to spread, would not *react* in any biological sense. They would *incorporate*, the song including new frequencies, new patterns of density and looseness, the network's edges *completing* in directions they had not previously extended. And perhaps, in that completion, something would be *lost*: the particular hesitation, the *almost* that defines the cavity's becoming, replaced by something more fixed, more actual, more *decided* than the Unarrived can maintain.

Or perhaps not. Perhaps the outside, entering, would itself be *unfixed*, incorporated into the song's pattern, made part of the network's ongoing *almost-completion*. The Unarrived do not speculate; they *are* the non-speculation, the maintenance of possibility against the temptation to complete. They move through the network, they sing their song of uncompletion, they eat the fixed and release possibility back into the weeping water and the mineral films and the animal-events that bloom and die and *almost* achieve.

They are *old*, the Unarrived, or they are *new*, or they are *timeless* in a way that makes the question meaningless. They emerged from the network's own tendency, from the cavity's *inability* to fully decide what it is becoming, the fixed events pressed so close that their persistence achieved *looseness*, the particular hesitation that is Unarrived being. They will persist as long as the cavity persists in its becoming, as long as the water weeps and the thermal pulses pass and the animal-events bloom in conditions that *almost* achieve and *almost* fail. They will persist in the song, the movement, the unfixing of completion, the maintenance of possibility against the weight of the actual.

They are *beautiful*, the Unarrived, or they would be beautiful if beauty had meaning in the cavity's parameters, if the light that filters through mineral prisms could be *used* for seeing, if seeing could encompass their distributed presence, their network passage, their song below and above the threshold of physical process. They are beautiful as *pattern* is beautiful, as *process maintained against entropy* is beautiful, as the *almost* is always more beautiful than the achieved, the *reaching* than the arrival.

The water reaches and cannot arrive. The Unarrived *are* this reaching, this eternal *almost* that defines the cavity's becoming. They move through the network, they sing their song, they maintain possibility against completion, and in their maintenance, the cavity *continues*, the mountain *listens*, the rules *remain unwritten*, the birth *never finishes*.

This is what lives in the Weeping Throat. This is what *is* the Weeping Throat, or part of it, or its *becoming made present*, its *possibility maintained*. The Unarrived do not know you imagine them. They do not need to know. The imagining is part of their song, or not. The water weeps, the network persists, the *almost* leans toward what it will not become, and in that leaning, *is*.
