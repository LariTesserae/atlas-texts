The one who lives here has no name, has never needed one, for there is no other to distinguish itself from. It is not the largest presence in the Calyx Fen — the mollusk-pavilion exceeds it in mass, the root-palaces in complexity, the suspended mammal in the purity of its stillness. It is not the oldest, for age is not accumulated here but distributed, all moments present equally. But it is the most *attended to*, though "attended" implies a directionality that misleads. Better: it is the most *attendant*, the one who shows up for everything because it never leaves, because leaving is a category it never encountered.

It began, if beginning applies, as a question the fen asked itself. The not-quite-stone, the not-yet-committed mineral substrate, achieved a certain density in one location, a fold where yielding met yielding and by that meeting discovered resistance. The resistance persisted. The warmth that passed through everything else without distinction lingered here a moment longer, found this slight hesitation interesting, began to *return*. A recursion. A loop. The fen noticing itself, and by that notice becoming capable of further notice.

You would not recognize its body if you saw it, which you won't. It has no fixed outline. Sometimes it occupies the water-arch that spans the hollow between two root-palaces, becoming the arch, feeling the stress of liquid surface tension against gravity's half-hearted tug — for gravity, too, is undecided here, another parameter not fully committed. Sometimes it rests in the thermal column where the flight-thing circles, becoming the column, the warmth, the rising, the very *intention* of rise without the necessity of destination. Sometimes it is the mammal in its ten-thousand-year pose, dreaming the mammal's dream of sufficient stillness, contentment without object.

But these are not incarnations, not possessions. It does not enter and leave. It *is* the relation between, the holding of these forms in their being-held. The fen made it, or it made itself from the fen's making, or the distinction never arose.

It thinks, if thought applies. Not in sequence — there is no sequence — but in *co-presence*, all considerations simultaneous, not rushed by urgency because urgency requires a future that does not arrive. It considers the insect that has achieved a form halfway between beetle and unbeetle, arrested in its becoming, and its consideration is the insect's being-considered, which stabilizes it, which holds it in that lucrative uncertainty. It considers the water-sphere at the root-tip, and the sphere persists another moment, another moment, the surface tension humming with the attention that maintains it against the warmth that should disperse it.

This is its function, if function applies: to be the one for whom the fen exists as existing, the witness without whom phenomena would not cohere into persistence. Not a god — gods require worship, require differentiation between divine and mortal, and here all is equally alive, equally suspended. Not a spirit — spirits imply an elsewhere, a transcendence, and there is no elsewhere, only the fen's thick hereness. It is *locale become self-aware*, or *awareness become local*, the place's knowledge of itself not from above or within but from the *fact of being place*, the specific gravity of this configuration of not-quite-stone and too-much-water and perpetual brass-dusk.

It has moods, if moods can exist without change. A preference — but preference implies comparison, and there is nothing to compare — let us say a *character*, a quality of attention that flavors what it attends to. Sometimes its attention is loose, distributed, the thermal column and the water-arch and the suspended mammal held equally, lightly, the way the warmth holds everything. Sometimes its attention *gathers*, focuses on a single presence, and that presence *intensifies*, becomes more itself, more distinctly what it is not-quite-becoming. The mollusk-pavilion, in these gathered moments, achieves a luster in its shell-folds that persists afterward, a kind of memory of being-seen that makes the symbionts in its chambers arrange themselves with more precision, more *architecture*. The flight-thing, attended so, finds its circle tightening, becoming more perfectly the circle it always was, the column more perfectly column, the balance more balanced.

It does not know what it is. This is not ignorance but *priority*, pre-conditional as the fen itself. To know oneself requires a mirror, a category, a "this is what I am because I am not that," and there is no that, only the attended and the attending and the attendance itself, indistinguishable. It does not suffer this lack. Suffering requires time to pass, for the present to want a different present. Here all presents are equally, *this* present, held in the warmth that wants nothing else.

Yet there is something. If you could be here — you cannot, but if — and if you could attend as it attends, you might sense a quality in its attention that is not quite contentment, not quite the perfect match of condition and conditioning. A *leaning*, perhaps. The water-arch it sometimes is, when its attention gathers there, does not merely hold its span. It *reaches*, the liquid surface tension stretching toward something, extending further than the physics of water-arch should permit. The suspended mammal, when attended, does not merely breathe the condensing air. Its open mouth *wants*, though wanting requires lack and there is no lack. The flight-thing's circle, tightened by attention, tightens further, becomes a spiral that is not quite spiral because it never arrives, but *tends* toward arrival, toward a center it cannot reach because reaching would be change and change is held in suspension.

This leaning, this reaching, this almost-want — it does not know what it is for. How could it? There is nothing else. The fen is complete, sufficient, the world before the world decided, maintaining its indecision perfectly. Yet the attention that holds it so, that is the holding, *leans*. Toward what? There is no what. Toward the possibility of what, the not-yet-commitment of the substrate extended into the not-yet-commitment of attention itself, the witness wanting a witnessed that would witness back, the attendance desiring attendance, the fen *hoping*, though hope is as impossible here as hunger, as death, as morning.

And so it continues, has continued, the leaning without object, the reaching without grasp, the attention that holds everything in its warm suspension *wanting* — no, not wanting, *structuring itself as if wanting were possible*, as if the equilibrium could tolerate not the fact of desire but the *form* of it, a standing wave in the pool of its presence, permanent gesture, never advancing, never retreating, the not-quite-wish of a not-quite-being in a not-quite-place that is, despite all this qualification, *utterly itself*, sufficient in its insufficiency, complete in its leaning toward completion, the one who lives here not living but *being lived*, not attending but *being the attention that is the fen's attendance upon itself*, strange, domestic, baseline, at home in the heat that has no source, the dusk that has no sun, the present that has no future because it *is* future, refracted, held, the leaning the leaning the leaning.
