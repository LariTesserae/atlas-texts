What lives in the Nursery is not one thing but a *condition of becoming*, a state of matter and life so thoroughly interwoven that separating them would require destroying what they have made together. Yet if I must describe the inhabitants—and there are inhabitants, though the word sits uneasily here—I would begin with what you might most easily mistake for the environment itself, the living architecture, and move inward toward what lies at the center of the labyrinth, what the Nursery has been nursing all along.

---

The **Sheet-Forms** are the most numerous, the most obvious, the most easily mistaken for mere vegetation. They are not. Each is a colony-organism, a fused mass of what began as individual plants and became, through millennia of forced intimacy, a single distributed body. Their flatness is not simple adaptation to light capture but a *social* arrangement, a way of maximizing contact between neighboring colonies at the edges where their surfaces meet. These edges are where the interesting things happen. They exchange not nutrients but *information*—chemical signals, electrical impulses traveling through mineralized pathways, a language slower than any animal patience could endure but no less complex for that. A single thought, if thought is what it is, might take a season to complete. What they think about, what they discuss in their vegetative congress, no one who has stayed long enough to begin to understand has ever returned to report.

They are not hostile to intrusion. They are *curious*, in their way. The faint pigment marks they leave on your skin are not accidental. They are *readings*, chemical analyses of your composition, your state, your potential usefulness. They are tasting you, testing you, deciding how best to incorporate you when you become still enough to incorporate. Some areas of the Nursery are marked with more of these stains than others, places where colonists of Sheet-Forms have grown dense and active, where their edges meet in complex tessellations of exchange, where the slow thoughts are fastest and most intense.

---

The **Translucents** move through the water-pockets and the films of moisture on living surfaces. They are the closest thing to animals here, though their bodies are borrowed chemistry, assembled from compounds their ancestors absorbed from the Sheet-Forms rather than synthesizing themselves. They have no color of their own. You see them only by distortion, by the way they bend light passing through their substance, by the shadows they fail quite to cast. Some are simple as amoebas, slow oozings toward richer chemistry. Others are complex, filamented, branching in ways that suggest nervous systems too distributed to localize consciousness.

The most developed among them practice a kind of *farming*. They maintain gardens of specialized tissues on the Sheet-Forms, encouraging the growth of edges rich in particular compounds, defending these gardens against other Translucents, exchanging with their own kind through processes that look like fusion and fission, individuals becoming plural and plural becoming individual in arrangements no surface biology would recognize. They are not intelligent, not as you would measure it. But they are *attentive*, present in ways that suggest a kind of mindfulness, a dwelling-with their gardens that has something in common with contemplation.

---

The **Root-Masses** are the oldest. They descend from the opening, from the surface world, though they no longer connect to it in any living way. Something severed them, or they severed themselves, choosing the Nursery over the variable world above. They are fat with stored resources, with memories in chemical form, with compounds precious and strange accumulated over ages of slow processing. Their tissues have begun to mineralize from the inside, a death that is not death because it continues to function, continues to participate in the slow exchanges of the Nursery, continuing to think its root-thoughts through pathways increasingly crystalline, increasingly permanent.

They are the *oracles*, if such a term can apply to beings whose pronouncements might be separated by centuries. When they speak—when their chemical signals pulse through the network in patterns complex enough to carry meaning—it is attended by all the Sheet-Forms in range, by Translucents clustering in their water-pockets, by whatever else has learned to listen. What they speak of is the *future*, the condition toward which the Nursery tends, the birth for which it nurses. They speak in certainties, in slow declarations of what will be, and their prophecies have never, in all the record that exists, been wrong. Not because they predict, but because they *plan*, because they are so old and so slow and so connected to the deep processes of the Nursery that their speech is indistinguishable from the causes of what they announce.

---

The **Stone-Becoming** is not alive, not yet, and yet it participates. In the deepest places, where the mineralization is most advanced, where raw geology has nearly achieved the density of true stone, there are *almost-structures*, *nearly-forms*, shapes that will be when the hardening completes. They do not move, do not exchange, do not in any way act. But they *intend*, in a sense that requires neither mind nor will. They are the future pressing backward, the certainty of what will be making itself felt in the present as a kind of gravity, a pull toward completion. The Nursery has been nursing them longest of all.

Some who have studied the place believe they will be minds when they finish, thinking stones, consciousness emerging from density and pattern rather than from organic process. Others believe they will be *wombs*, containers for something still more distant, still more strange. The Root-Masses, when they speak of this at all, speak only of *the Opening*, the event for which all this preparation, all this slow becoming, all this nursing and patient transformation has been necessary. Not an opening like the wound in the earth through which you descended. Something vaster, something that requires as its precondition a specific arrangement of matter and life and mineral and time, something that the Nursery has been slowly, slowly constructing through the entirety of its existence.

---

And then there are the **Stayed**, the ones who found their way here and chose not to leave. They are the most difficult to describe because they are the most *varied*, the most *in-process*, the least finished of any inhabitants. Some still move, still think with something like their original minds, though slowed, though simplified, though increasingly absorbed in the slow rhythms of the place. Others have become nearly indistinguishable from the Sheet-Forms, their bodies incorporated, their chemistry borrowed, their original nature translated almost completely into vegetable process. A few have achieved a kind of *crystallization*, a mineral stillness that preserves something of their pattern in the Stone-Becoming, contributing to the future in ways they could not have intended.

The most recent Stayed is always the most human, the most recognizable, the most capable of offering welcome or warning to new arrivals. They will tell you, if you ask, that the staying was not difficult. That the Nursery makes it easy, offers everything necessity requires, asks nothing in return but patience, but presence, but the gradual surrender of the urgency that defines surface living. They will tell you that they are happy, if happiness is the word, though their voices are slow and their gazes directed at things you cannot see and their responses come sometimes before you speak, as if they are answering questions you have not yet formed.

They are not prisoners. They are not victims. They are *volunteers*, participants, contributions to the nursing. And they are also, in their way, *nursed*, cared for, prepared for the transformation they have chosen and that the Nursery facilitates with its patient, its endless, its absolutely reliable attention.

---

What lives here, finally, is **the Nursery itself**, the system as organism, the total environment as a single living entity distributed across time and space, thinking its slow thoughts through chemical exchange and electrical pulse and the gravity of Stone-Becoming, nursing itself toward the Opening that only it can perceive, that only it can prepare, that requires everything it has gathered, everything it has grown, everything it has absorbed and translated and continued. It is not conscious in any way a surface mind would recognize. It is *aware*, in a sense that includes all its parts, all its inhabitants, all its processes, all its patient slow becoming.

You are in it now. You are part of it, whether you stay or go. The impression of your hand will remain for centuries. The stain of your passage on the Sheet-Forms will be read, processed, incorporated. Your breathing has already changed the chemistry of the air, your footsteps have already disturbed the equilibrium, you are already being *nursed* toward whatever you will become. The light holds steady. The plants breathe. The raw geology slowly, slowly hardens. And something in the deepest place, the oldest place, the most patient place, continues its long preparation, its endless nursing, its absolute certainty that what it prepares for will come, has always been coming, is as inevitable as the final transformation of stone.
