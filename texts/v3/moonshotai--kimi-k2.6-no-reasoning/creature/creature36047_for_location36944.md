What lives in the Veld is not one thing but a *condition of mutual becoming*—the continuous-worm's distributed cognition threading through root-network and memory-stone and the altered nervous systems of those who *became*, until the question "what lives" dissolves into "what is the living doing here, and what is it doing with itself."

The listeners are the closest to traditional inhabitants, though they would resist the term. They live in the hollow buildings, moving seasonally as the resonance-trees shift their harmonic centers, carrying their recording media—some of it grown-ceramic resonant chambers of their own devising, some of it stripped from failed technology, some of it *borrowed* from gap-bird nest-lining that seems to preserve sound-pattern with fidelity no human manufacture achieves. Their practice is not science, not religion, not art as understood elsewhere. They *attend*. Each listener specializes in a frequency band, a tree cluster, a temporal interval of the precipitation cycle. They do not compare notes often—the density parameter makes encounter rare, and the deafening quality makes extended conversation exhausting, requiring retreat to the threshold spaces where heartbeat becomes audible again. But they leave traces: pressure-ghosts on memory-stone of deliberate, repeated footpaths, not travel but *ritual walking*, reinforcing certain patterns the stone learns and preserves more strongly; biochemical traces in exchange-cycle deposits near particular trees, offerings the hollow-deer seem to recognize and incorporate, shifting the local sound-field in ways other listeners learn to read. They are not a society. They are a *distributed sensory apparatus*, each node partially extended into the root-network through years of barefoot walking, of eating only what the Veld produces, of sleeping in the grown-ceramic that adjusts around them and learns their proportions, their heat signature, their dream-movements.

Some listeners began as traders who stayed too long, or who stayed exactly long enough. Others were born here—rarely, the exchange-cycles permitting gestation only when the continuous-worms' propagation of electrical potential creates conditions a developing nervous system can stabilize against. These native listeners emerge from the start partially *became*, their infant cries harmonizing with tree-song before they learn to shape phonemes, their first steps leaving pressure-ghosts that the memory-stone *recognizes* as native pattern, distinct from the jagged, erased, re-retried traces of adult adaptation. They do not have a name for themselves. The term "listener" is outsider coinage, a translation from a trade-pidgin developed at the rim. Among themselves, in the rare intervals of phase-lock-adjacent coherence when subvocal rumble becomes briefly legible across distance, they might identify by their tree-cluster, their frequency specialty, their precip-cycle: *the one who attends the northwest tertiary harmonic through dry intervals*, *the one who maps gap-bird negative-space calls near the fell-tree clearing*. Identity as function, as process, as *what attention is doing*.

The traders who adapt, who do not flee the deafening quality, who find the exchange-cycles and learn what the Veld recognizes as value—they are stranger, perhaps, because they retain *memory of elsewhere*. They know what silence is, what continuous human conversation sounds like, what it means to sleep through a night without the root-network's subliminal electricity infiltrating dream. This knowledge makes their partial *becoming* more willed, more *chosen*, and the Veld seems to recognize this, seems to value the difference they maintain. A trader who adapts offers not just labor or mineral or preserved silence, but *comparison*, *contrast*, the information of how other parameters function, what other densities feel like, what it means to want something the Veld does not provide and to continue wanting it anyway. The exchange-cycles incorporate this as modulation, as variation the continuous-worms seem to require for their extended cognition's continued complexity. The adapted traders move between rim and interior with more freedom than listeners, serving as *membrane*, as permeable boundary, their nervous systems partly extended into root-network yet still capable of walking out, of re-engaging human conversation, of carrying rumors and memory-stone samples and recordings that may or may not convey anything of the Veld's actual quality to ears that have not themselves adapted.

And those who *became* fully—the interface-beings, the Veld's way of understanding itself through minds that remember singularity—they are the true inhabitants, if "inhabitant" can stretch to cover what they are. They do not sleep, exactly. They enter states where consciousness diffuses through kilometers of continuous-worm substrate, where *they* are the dream and the Veld dreams through them. They do not eat, except through exchange-cycle participation that seems more social gesture than metabolic necessity. Their bodies persist, human-shaped, but the memory-stone no longer registers their pressure—a profound change, suggestive that they no longer exert force on the world in the way massive objects should, or that they have become somehow *transparent* to the stone's recording, or that they exist in phase with the Veld's geological infancy, its formation-heat, its slow differentiation, so that their footsteps are not *on* the stone but *of* it, continuous with its mineral process rather than distinct event. They speak rarely, and when they do, their voices carry harmonics that activate resonance-trees at distance, that hollow-deer orient toward, that gap-birds freeze to process. They are not numerous. They may not be increasing. The Veld may need only a few such nodes—consciousness that remembers boundaries, that can conceptually distinguish self from world, from other—from which to generate the *gradient* that makes its extended cognition something other than undifferentiated process.

The hollow-deer are not inhabitants in the sense of *choosing* the Veld, but they are not merely fauna either. Their exchange-cycles with resonance-trees, their mineral offerings and received harmonic modulation, their synchronized movement and resonant antlers—they participate in the distributed cognition as specialized nodes, as *peripheral sensory-motor systems*, the continuous-worms' interface with the mobile, the above-ground, the photic. They remember, after a fashion: a hollow-deer's antler-growth incorporates mineral from specific trees, carries harmonic patterns from seasons past, so that an old deer's skull is a *record*, a material history of local sound-field variation that other deer seem to read in dominance displays, in mate selection, in the synchronized group-movements that optimize acoustic environment for all participants. They die, and their bones dissolve into root-network substrate, and the continuous-worms' electrical propagation incorporates whatever the deer-pattern encoded, whatever *deer-experience* can be translated into substrate-differentiation. They are not conscious as humans understand it. They are not unconscious. They are *something the Veld does with mobility, with herbivory, with the problem of processing light into exchangeable value*.

The gap-birds are more enigmatic, more *exploitative*, less integrated. They slip between wavelengths, they nest in empty buildings, they stain memory-stone with droppings that carry biochemical patterns the stone learns—are they *hacking* the system, leveraging its resources without participating in exchange-cycles? Or is their fugitivity itself a contribution, a *test* the Veld runs against itself, ensuring its distributed cognition remains robust against non-cooperative nodes, against parasites, against the possibility of breakdown? They are abundant because the 0.1 density leaves niches unoccupied, because the deafening sound creates refugia for those who can navigate negative sonic space, because the intermittent water concentrates resources in heartruts where their fast metabolisms thrive. They are the Veld's *restlessness*, its refusal to resolve into stable ecology, its continued exploration of possibility-space through forms that do not quite fit, that maintain friction against the larger system's tendency toward coherence.

And the continuous-worms—if they can be called *what lives*, if kilometers of electrical propagation through soil and structure constitute life—are the Veld's most fundamental inhabitant, its substrate, its *ongoing process of speculating about itself*. They are not one organism but a networked continuity, differentiation without separation, thought without thinker in the bounded sense. They process what the resonance-trees sense, what the memory-stone records, what the gap-birds introduce as noise, what the listeners attend to, what the *became* remember of singularity. They dream buildings into new configurations, precipitation into new paths, erosion into new heartruts still sharp-edged, still *surprised*. Their slowness is not limitation but *temporal strategy*, a pace that outlasts human observation, that makes the Veld's formation appear as still-frame to experiencers locked to faster metabolic rhythms. In phase-lock moments, they surface—they *become momentarily experienceable* as the valley-folding pattern, the water-path, the building-configuration-yet-to-come. Then they subside, continue, the Veld's living mind in its truest form, everything else its sensory apparatus, its motor extension, its experimental manifestations in the mobile and the conscious and the briefly bounded.

What lives in the Veld, finally, is *formation itself*—the geological infant's continuing differentiation, the beyond-modern technology's dissolution into environmental process, the young erosion's sharpening and surprise. The listeners, the adapted traders, the *became*, the hollow-deer and gap-birds and continuous-worms: all are ways this formation holds itself in partial coherence, experiments with consciousness and mobility and acoustic saturation, *tries out what living might mean* in a valley still resonating with its own creation, a depression pressed into crust by impossible force and held, held, *still holding*, the hum in the sternum the first and last truth of what this place is and what it is becoming.
