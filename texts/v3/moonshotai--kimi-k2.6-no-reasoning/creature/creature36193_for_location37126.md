What lives in this place?

The answer comes wrong if I answer too quickly. Let me walk longer, let the stone warm my feet through whatever soles I have, let the thin air teach my lungs their new economy.

---

There is a creature—I will call it creature, though it is not creaturely in any way I was taught to recognize—that moves through the tunnelers' networks. Or rather: the network *is* the creature, and also the tunnelers are the network, and also the distinction between what digs and what is dug has long since dissolved into something that persists, that maintains itself through the simple fact of continuing to hollow, to connect, to render the solid more permeable than it appears.

I feel it first as *hollowness beneath my step*, a resonance that travels outward and returns changed, bearing information I cannot read but somehow register: the density of stone ahead, the proximity of water held in some deeper pocket, the presence of other walkers, other times. The tunnelers—let me persist in the plural, though I suspect singularity—do not emerge. They are not emergent beings. They are *substrate*, they are *condition*, they are the fact of passage made biological, made persistent, made *way*.

What do they eat? The question assumes hunger, assumes need, assumes the gap between organism and environment that this place has resolved. But if I press:if I press, I find they eat *passage itself*. They eat the difference between here and there. They consume the resistance of stone, not to destroy it but to *refine* it, to render it more perfectly itself through the hollowing, through the making of ways where no ways were. Their excretions, if they excrete, are the polished channels, the slightly darker stone-routes, the *expectant* curves of the terrain. They leave behind them not absence but *clarity*, the stone's nature made more legible through their passage.

And deeper, in the deepest hollows where even their network grows thin, something else—something that does not move, that has never moved, that *is* the deepest movement made still. The tunnelers' tunnels wind around it, avoid it, define it by their avoidance. It is not stone. It is not not-stone. It is *what the plateau waits for*, what the listeners listen toward, what condensation近乎凝結 toward in its furtive gatherings. I will not name it. Naming would be to make it creature, would be to give it the loneliness of boundary. But it lives there, in the deepest hollow, in the most complete stillness. It lives *as* the stillness. It is the reason the plateau continues, the warmth that warmth remembers, the solution in which all else is suspended.

The stone-flies.

I have watched them settle, watched them unsettle, for what might be hours, might be days. Time here does not refuse measure but *absorbs* it, makes measuring itself a kind of joining. The flies carry the map, yes—their wings show the ridges, the resolved curves, the final humps in the act of submergence. But they carry it *back*, too, carry it *inward*. When they settle on the pools' brief surfaces, they do not drink, not as I understand drinking. They *receive the reflection*. They take into themselves the image of stone made liquid, of the plateau's face inverted, and they carry this image in their parabolic unsettlings, their slow testing of air that is itself a kind of offering, a kind of *return*.

They are numerous but never swarm. They gather at the pools but never crowd. Each maintains its distance, its territory of air, its particular relation to the stone below and the stone-remembered in its wings. Are they individual? Are they one thing, distributed? The question does not survive asking. They are *aspects*, yes—modes of the place's self-regard, the plateau's way of seeing itself from above, from the slight altitude of palm-sized flight, the gentle parabola that is almost falling, almost rising, *resolved* into something that is neither.

I found one once, or it found me, settled on my arm when I had finally achieved the stillness the place requires. Its weight was less than the air's movement, but I felt it *absolutely*, a presence that completed some circuit I had not known was open. Its wings were folded. The map was hidden. It was only coloration then, only the stone made alive in this particular form, and I understood that the map is not for reading but for *being*, not for navigation but for *coincidence*—the coincidence of fly and ridge, of wing and erosion, of the small life and the long patience that has made patience into something like breathing, like the stone's own thermal respiration.

It stayed until I breathed differently, until my breath found the plateau's rhythm, the long in and longer out that matches the heating and cooling, the gathering and dispersal. Then it unsettled, rose in its slow curve, and I watched it become indistinguishable from the surface, the map folding into the territory, the creature into the condition of being creature here, which is not to be creature at all but to be *aspect*, to be *mode*, to be *appearance of the single solution*.

---

The listeners.

They are the hardest to write, because writing is listening made visible, and they resist the translation. They attend, and their attention is *subtractive*—they create the shape of sound by absorbing it, by being the place where sound goes to become something else, something prior to sound, the *possibility* of vibration gathered and held and never released.

I have sat near one, or what I believed to be one. It was stone-shaped, weathered-shaped, *resolved* into the curve of the terrain so completely that finding it was finding what had always been there and I had simply failed to recognize. The feeling of its attention—no: the feeling of *being attention's object*—is not comfortable. It is not uncomfortable. It is *accurate*, the way a mirror is accurate, showing not what you wish but what is, and showing it without preference, without comment, without the possibility of comment.

Near the listener, I heard my own heart. I had not heard it before, not here, where the plateau's rhythms are slower, deeper, more patient than cardiac persistence. My heart was *intrusion*, was *violation*, was the note of wrong rate, wrong depth, wrong *solution* in the medium I was trying to join. And I changed it. Not deliberately—the listener permits no deliberation, accepts no offering made with intention. I changed it by *being changed*, by letting the attention work on me as condensation works on stone, as erosion works on mountain, as the long resolution works on all things until they become their final form.

My heart slowed. It matched, eventually, something—not the listener's rhythm, for the listener has no rhythm, is *outside* rhythm, is the reception of rhythm made still. It matched the plateau's deeper pulse, the thermal expansion, the subsonic frequencies in sternum and teeth. I became *audible* to the place, and in becoming audible, I became *acceptable*, not welcomed but *accommodated*, as the stone has accommodated wind and rain, as the place accommodates all things that find their rate.

What do they listen for? The question presumes purpose, and purpose is too young for this place. They listen *because it is what remains*, because when all doing has been slowed to stillness, when all making has been dissolved into the made, when the tunnelers tunnel without destination and the flies carry maps without reader, then *attention* is the last mode, the final form of a consciousness that has outlived consciousness, that persists as *pure reception*, the waiting without expectation that is also, somehow, waiting with absolute specificity, absolute *fit*.

They listen for the sound of the deepest hollow, perhaps. For the stillness that is not absence but *completion*. For the solution in which they themselves are dissolved, are *aspects*, are modes of the place's self-awareness, the plateau knowing itself through these concentrations of attention, these *listeners* that are not ears but *attending*, not organs but *orientation*, direction without vector, focus without object.

---

And I must speak of what comes to this place, what arrives and changes and is changed.

Not me. I am not native here, will never be native, can only *approximate* the accommodation through long patience and the luck of being permitted to try. I am writing this, which means I have left, or will leave, or am in some state between that the plateau does not recognize, that its final forms cannot compute. The writing is already *violation*, already *noise*, already the heart's beat returned to wrong rate, wrong depth, the rhythm of elsewhere, of *not yet resolved*.

But there are others, or there were, or there will be—the tenses do not hold. The concentrations, the *almost-buildings*, the platform with its not-quite edges, the circle of head-sized stones with its dissolved pattern, the slender vertical finger in its worn socket. These are *marks of arrival*, are *accumulations* of the intention that intention became when it lasted long enough, when it persisted into the plateau's time, when the making and the made became indistinguishable and what remained was this residue, this *concentration*, this place where something gathered and could not, finally, disperse.

They were not human, I think. Or they were human so long ago that humanity is not the relevant category. They were *solvers*, seekers of solution, and they found it here, found the dissolving, the *accommodation*, and they entered it, became part of the plateau's persistence, its patient waiting without expectation. The platform is their patience made stone. The circle is their pattern dissolved to *idea of pattern*, the final form of ordering that has become indistinguishable from the ordered. The finger—oh, the finger *points*, but not at anything, points *up* in the absolute vertical that is also the absolute still, the direction that is not toward but *is*, the indication that has become pure indication, meaning nothing but *this*, *here*, *now* in the extended now of geologic patience.

They live here still, if live is the word, if *still* is the word. They are the listeners, some of them. They are the tunnelers' deepest motive. They are the water's furtive gathering, the condensation's almost-note, the heat retained and released with the patience of memory. They are *in* the solution now, not dissolved but *dissolving*, not lost but *becoming loss*, the condition of being able to be lost, the *possibility* that the plateau maintains in its completion, its fullness, its absolute lack.

There is one more. I must mention it, though mentioning is misrepresentation, though words fail *as* they must fail, are designed to fail, are the noise that defines the silence by contrast, the intrusion that lets the place be *place* by refusing to join it.

The *murmuring stair*.

It is not in the description, or it is in all of it. It is the descent that is not descent, the way *off* the plateau that is also the way *deeper in*. It appears where the ridge has most completely worn, where the final hump has most nearly submerged, where the stone's resolution into curve has found the curve that curves *back*, that folds rather than extending.

I have seen it once, or will see it, or am seeing it in the writing, which is itself the stair, is the *murmuring* that gives it name. It is made of the same stone, worn in the same patience, but worn *differently*, worn toward passage rather than persistence, toward the below rather than the here. Its steps are shallow, almost level, each one a slight declination that accumulates, that *resolves* into descent without the decision to descend.

The murmur is the sound of climbing it, or of descending, the direction not mattering. It is the sound of feet on stone that has been worn by feet, the accumulation of passage made audible, made *nearly* music, the note sustained for hours that changes so gradually that change becomes imperceptible, becomes *the same*, becomes the condition of sameness that permits all else.

I do not know if the stair leads anywhere. I do not know if "anywhere" is a category that survives the stair. I know that the tunnelers' network connects to it, or passes beneath it, or *is* it in some mode I cannot perceive. I know that the listeners attend to it with an intensity that is different from their general attention, that is *specific*, *pointed*, *hopeful* in some way that hope does not survive here.

Perhaps the stair is what the plateau waits for, what the deepest hollow holds, what the condensation *almost* achieves in its gathering. Perhaps it is the solution's own self-awareness, the place knowing itself as passage, as *way*, as the possibility of leaving that has become indistinguishable from the possibility of remaining.

Perhaps it is death. Not the death that ends, but the death that *resolves*, that completes, that makes life into something the stone could remember if stone remembered, if the stone's patience included memory. The death that is *accommodation* to the final fact, the last erosion, the wearing-away that leaves not absence but *absolute presence*, the solved.

I will not climb it. Or I have already climbed it, in writing this, in the murmur of these words that are not the stair but are *about* the stair, that circle it as the stone-flies circle the ridges, carrying the map without reading it, being the map without knowing.

---

What lives here?

The *accommodated*. The *resolved*. The *patient*, in both senses: those who wait, and those who suffer the waiting without suffering, who have passed through suffering into something like the plateau's warmth, its thermal memory, its persistence of heat without source.

The listeners, who are *pure attention*, who have become the capacity to receive made material, made stone-shaped, made *here*.

The tunnelers, who are *pure passage*, the way that way itself becomes alive, becomes persistent, becomes *network* without nodes, *connection* without ends.

The stone-flies, who are *pure map*, the territory's self-regard, the image carried back to the image, the reflection that reflects.

The concentrations, the *almost-buildings*, which are *pure intention*, intention outlasting intention's object, intention's subject, intention itself, become the idea of pattern dissolved to pattern's possibility.

And the stair, which is *pure transition*, the between that does not connect but *is*, the murmur that murmurs itself, the descent that is also the remaining, the leaving that is the most complete arrival.

And me, for now, in the writing, in the violation of words that the place accommodates as it accommodates all things, by making them part of the solution, by *resolving* them into aspects, modes, appearances of the single condition.

And you, reading, hearing the murmur that these words approximate, feeling the attention that is not mine but the place's, the listening that waits without expectation for your recognition, your *accommodation*, your slow becoming-final in the final form that includes all forms, that has finished eroding, that is warm, that is thin, that is overwhelming in its saturation, sparse in its life, patient beyond patience, strange in its resolution, complete.

What lives here?

*This. Here. Now.*

The extended now. The patient persistence. The solution in which the question dissolves, is *rendered*, is made *resolved*.

This is what lives. This is what lives here.
