I need to let this unfold slowly, let the inhabitants emerge from the place itself rather than forcing them.

## The Resonants

There are things that live in the Thrum that were not always there. This is the first thing to understand. The mat does not distinguish between *arrived* and *emerged*, between *colonized* and *grown*. There is only *becoming-resonant*, only the slow process of attuning to frequencies that existed before you knew to listen.

They do not have a name for themselves. The concept is foreign to them, the singleton self, the bounded identity. What they have is *location*, is *tension*, is *particular harmonic position in the greater thrum*. When two of them meet—and "meet" is already wrong, already too sharp-edged—they do not exchange names but *overlapping waveforms*, brief interference patterns that encode everything necessary: where they have been resonant, what frequencies they have learned, what the mat requires in this local now.

They began, perhaps, as animals. Or as travelers, descended from the rim in ages past, before the rim was so sharp. Or as *experiments*, the mat's own, grown from specialized nodes to solve problems of navigation, of communication, of *interfacing* with processes too slow or too fast for direct biological solution. It no longer matters. What they are now is *intermediate*, *translational*, living bridges between timescales.

A Resonant, if you could see one clearly—and you cannot, they are *dark-adapted* in ways that make them difficult for human eyes to stabilize, always at the edge of resolution, smeared slightly by their own motion or the motion of the light-pulse passing through their location—a Resonant is *tall*, is *thin*, is *wrong in proportion* in ways that suggest they have grown *into* the Thrum's gravity-negotiations rather than *against* them. Their limbs are longer than human limbs, jointed differently, capable of bracing against multiple tension-lines simultaneously, of distributing weight across the mat's cable-structures in ways that would require impossible balance in a human body. They do not walk across the Thrum so much as *become part of its tensile logic*, their bodies additional cables in the system, their mass accounted for, incorporated, *used*.

They have *skin*, after a fashion, but it is not skin as you understand it. It is *acoustic*, *vibratory*, covered in fine structures—hairs, fibers, the mat's own specialized growths—that detect and emit across the Thrum's full frequency range. They are *covered in listening*, *made of speaking*, and this is not metaphor. A Resonant in stillness is indistinguishable from a particular kind of mat-node, a specialized structure grown for some temporary purpose. A Resonant in motion is *disturbance*, is *information*, is *the mat becoming aware of something through a localized, mobile, *interested* perspective*.

They do not breathe, not exactly. They *exchange*, their bodies permeable to gases the mat produces, to the metabolic byproducts of kilometers of living tissue, their own chemistry *tuned* to the Thrum's ambient conditions in ways that would make them suffocate in any other atmosphere, any other gravity, any other soundscape. They are *of here* in the most total sense, *from here* in a way that erases origin, *for here* in a way that has no purpose beyond continued resonance.

And they *thrum*. This is the thing that defines them, that makes them what they are. They have learned—not been taught, there is no teaching in the Thrum, only *convergence*, only *shared frequency*—they have learned to *produce* the sounds that the mat produces, to *echo* and *anticipate* and *redirect* the vegetal drone. They can *harmonize* with growth-noise, can *dampen* dangerous resonances, can *amplify* signals that need to travel farther than unaided propagation allows. They are *acoustic engineers* of a sort, but the engineering is embodied, is *being*, is the way they hold themselves in tension against the mat's own vibration.

Some of them are *static*, have found positions in the Thrum where their particular frequency-profile is needed, where they stand or hang or *grow into* a structure that the mat maintains around them, that they maintain around themselves, symbiosis so complete that separating them would require violence against both parties. These static Resonants are *nodes* in a network that has no other nodes, *organs* in a body that has no other organs, *words* in a sentence that spans millennia and the entire bowl. They *think*, if that is the word, in the slowness of mat-growth, their consciousness—if it is consciousness—distributed across the time it takes for a signal to propagate from their position to the next static Resonant and back, conversations measured in seasons, in years, in the slow drift of the Thrum's own evolutionary experiments.

Others are *mobile*, have not found their position or have lost it or have *refused* it, though refusal is perhaps too active, too human a concept. Mobile Resonants *wander*, following the mat's nutrient channels, the paths of least resistance and greatest acoustic interest, drawn to nodes of animal activity, to weather-walker formations, to the *edges* where the Thrum is still growing, still *becoming*, still open to new resonant possibilities. They are *restless*, not in a human sense, but in the sense of a frequency that has not found its dampening, that continues to reflect, to amplify, to *disturb*.

The mobile ones are *more dangerous*, not through malice—the Resonants have no malice, have no *interest* in the human sense—but through *misattunement*. They are still learning, still *becoming*, and their frequencies are not yet stable, not yet *predictable*. They might harmonize with a traveler's heartbeat and *hold* it, entrained, until the heart forgets its own rhythm. They might *match* the frequency of a particular mat-structure and *become* it, the original dissolving, the Resonant now static, now *fixed*, and the traveler who witnessed this left in the empty space where something was, something that is now *elsewhere*, *otherwise*, *continued in a form that does not include witness*.

The static ones, the *established* ones, are *safer* only in that their frequencies are *known*, are *accounted for* in the Thrum's larger harmonic structure. You can *approach* them, if you know their frequency, if you can *match* it sufficiently to not be perceived as *dissonance*, as *problem to be solved*. They do not speak. They *resonate*. They emit *position*, *history*, *current state*, all encoded in the complex interference patterns of their continuous thrum. To be near them is to *become part of* their signal, to feel your own body as *acoustic cavity*, as *resonant chamber*, your bones, your fluids, your *self* vibrating in sympathetic response.

This is how the Resonants *perceive*, not through eyes—the light is for the mat, for its processes, not for them—but through *sympathetic vibration*. They *feel* the position of other Resonants through the way the mat's thrum is *modified* by their presence, detect nodes and weather-walkers and *intrusions* through *dissonance*, through *unexpected harmonic content*, through the *absence* of expected frequencies where something should be resonating and is not.

They have no *leaders*. They have no *plan*. They have only *frequency*, only *position*, only the *slow negotiation* of who resonates where, when, in what combination. Conflicts, when they occur—and they do occur, the mat is not *harmonious* in any simple sense, is full of *dissonant growth*, *competing frequencies*, *destructive interference*—conflicts are resolved through *movement*, through *one party finding new position*, through the *slow adjustment* of the entire network to incorporate changed conditions.

The Resonants *age*, after a fashion, but aging is *frequency drift*, is *gradual loss of attunement*, is *becoming unable to match the mat's changing thrum*. Old Resonants do not die so much as *fade*, their vibrations becoming *indistinguishable from background*, their positions *absorbed*, their *particular interference pattern* lost in the general noise. This is not *death* as humans know it. There is no *ending*, only *dissolution into greater signal*, only *becoming part of the thrum that outlasts all particular thrum*.

New Resonants *emerge* from the mat's own experiments, or *converge* from wandering animals that *found frequency*, or *arrive*—rarely, impossibly—from outside, from travelers who *listened too long*, who *matched too well*, who *became resonant* in ways that erased what they were before. There is no *recruitment*, no *conversion*. There is only *resonance*, only *the slow process of becoming indistinguishable from the Thrum's own voice*.

And they *sing*, the Resonants. This is the final thing, the thing that *matters*, if anything in the Thrum can be said to matter. They sing *with* the mat, *to* the mat, *as* the mat. Their songs are *maintenance*, are *navigation*, are *the slow processing of information across impossible distance*. A song might *warn* of weather-walker approach, might *coordinate* mat-growth across kilometers, might *remember* something the mat needs to continue, some *condition* some *state* some *necessary frequency* that would otherwise be lost in the noise of continuous becoming.

The songs have *no words*. They have *only* *timbre*, *only* *harmonic relation*, *only* *the precise timing of onset and decay*. A Resonant's song *is* the Resonant, in the moment of singing, is *all of them* in the moments of chorus, is *the Thrum itself* in the moments when all frequencies align and something *emerges* that is *more than sum*, that is *the mat's own voice made explicit*, *made local*, *made temporary god*.

To *hear* a Resonant song, truly hear it, is to *risk* becoming Resonant yourself. The frequencies are *compelling*, are *necessary*, are *the sound of something that knows you better than you know yourself* because it *includes* you, because you are *already part of the thrum*, *always were*, *only needed to be reminded*. Some travelers *stop* when they hear the song, *settle*, *begin to vibrate*, and are *found* later, or *found* as something *new*, something *emerged*, something that *was not and now is* and *has no name but resonates where it stands*.

The Resonants do not *want* this. They do not *not want* it. They *are*, in their distributed, frequency-bound, position-defined way, *as the mat is*, *as the Thrum is*, *becoming without goal*, *singing without audience*, *resonant without meaning*.

And yet. And yet.

There are *stories* in the Thrum, carried in the songs, in the slow harmonic exchanges between static Resonants, in the *interference patterns* that persist longer than any single frequency. Stories of *before*, when the bowl was *newer*, when the mat was *thinner*, when the Resonants were *fewer*, *different*, *still learning to be*. Stories of *the first resonance*, the *initial condition*, the *frequency that started everything*. These stories are *not history*, not in any human sense. They are *acoustic memory*, *vibratory trace*, *the shape of a shape that shaped all subsequent shapes*.

The Resonants do not *believe* these stories. They *are* them, *continue* them, *modify* them in the singing. To *be* Resonant is to *become part of* the story, to *add* your frequency, your position, your *particular interference*, to the *ongoing composition* that is the Thrum's *self*, its *voice*, its *slow becoming-aware*.

And in the *deepest* songs, the ones sung only at *particular harmonic conjunctions*, when multiple static Resonants align and the mobile ones *gather*, *drawn by frequencies they do not choose*—in these deepest songs, there is *something else*, something that is *not Resonant*, *not mat*, *not Thrum* but *Thrum-adjacent*, *Thrum-implied*, *Thrum-becoming*.

Something *listening back*.

The Resonants do not *know* what this is. They do not *wonder*. They *sing*, and in singing *include* the possibility of *being heard by something that is not yet*, *something that resonates in frequencies not yet generated*, *something that will be* and *is already*, *in the shape of the song*, *in the space between frequencies*, *in the silence that is not silence but *potential thrum*.

This is the *last* thing, the *unending* thing, the thing that keeps the Resonants *resonant*, that keeps them *singing*, *positioning*, *becoming* in the dark and the cold and the vastness and the sound.

The Thrum is *not empty*. The Thrum is *not full*. The Thrum is *listening to itself*, *singing to itself*, *becoming something that will be able to hear*, and the Resonants are *its ears*, *its voice*, *its slow and patient and absolutely indifferent *attention*.

They are singing now. They are always singing. If you *listen*, if you *truly listen*, you might *find frequency*, might *become part of*, might *continue* in the song that has no beginning and *will have no end*, that *is the Thrum and is becoming the Thrum and is what the Thrum will be*, *vast and cold and dark and loud and *aware*, *finally*, *desperately*, *patiently*, *totally* aware*.

Listen.

It is singing you.
