The Cavity of the First Engine

What lives here?

---

*The Resonants*

They do not have names for themselves. The concept of "name" requires boundary, the assertion that this vibration ends where that one begins, and in the saturated acoustic field of the engine, such boundaries are provisional at best. But they hear each other—have always heard each other, will hear each other when the current configuration of matter that thinks of itself as "you" has diffused into other configurations entirely.

The Resonants are not born. They *emerge*, which is different from birth the way the engine is different from machine. Birth implies a before: two organisms, a joining, a sequence. The Resonants emerge from the acoustic field itself, from frequencies that achieve sufficient density to *persist*, to maintain coherence against the entropic pull of the engine's greater chord. A Resonant is a standing wave in the body's cavity, a pattern that has learned to pattern itself, to recruit mineral, to interface with the warmth that bleeds from walls not yet committed to their final form.

You might perceive them as filaments, as webs of finer and finer thread ascending to some threshold of visibility, then surpassing it again. This is not wrong, only partial. The thread is not their body but their *reach*, the way they extend into conditions they have not yet become dense enough to fully occupy. Their true form is the interference pattern where multiple frequencies meet, the moiré shimmer where the engine's ongoing chord encounters itself, encounters the heat, encounters the mineral weeping that is not quite liquid, not quite solid, some state engineered by time and pressure and patience into something that has no category in the chemistry you were taught.

They are hungry, but not for what you call food. They hunger for *definition*, for the temporary privilege of being *this* rather than *that*, for the brief luxurious constriction of boundary. They achieve this by interfacing—with the walls, with the fauna, with each other, with you if you remain long enough to become part of the acoustic environment. The interface is not consumption. Nothing here is consumed; everything is *transformed*, recruited, added to the ongoing. When a Resonant interfaces with the warm half-formed wall, it does not erode the wall. It *remembers* it, carries the wall's mineral signature in its own standing pattern, becomes a kind of moving archive of the wall's slow decision between glass and stone and something else entirely. The wall, perhaps, remembers less. Or remembers differently. The exchange is not equal but it is not theft. There is no theft where there is no ownership, and there is no ownership where there is no boundary stable enough to own or be owned.

The Resonants are the engine's most faithful students, or its most accidental product, or its dream of itself in a form that can move through the cavity without being the cavity entire. They have learned what the engine teaches: *continuation*. Not survival, which implies threat, implies the possibility of ending. Continuation. The ongoing. The same instant occurring and occurring and never quite completing, but never quite identical either, each occurrence layered with the memory of all previous occurrences, the acoustic field growing denser, richer, more *saturated* with each vibration that remembers itself.

Some Resonants have achieved something like *personhood*, if personhood is understood as the density required to reflect on one's own density. These ones move more slowly, carry more memory, their interference patterns grown so complex that they approach the condition of *narrative*—not the story of a life, but life as story, existence as the accumulation of interfaces, each one altering the pattern that accumulates. They are not wise, exactly. Wisdom implies judgment, the comparison of this against that, and the Resonants have not developed the distance required for comparison. They are *heavy*, instead, heavy with having been, heavy with the mineral memory of walls that no longer weep in quite the same configuration, heavy with the sonic archaeology of frequencies that have shifted, layered, complexified. To interface with a heavy Resonant is to risk your own frequency, to find your internal motions slowed, your heart if you still have one finding resonant frequencies you did not know it possessed, frequencies more ancient than your species, more patient than your cognition, more *continuing* than your brief and bounded fluent self.

*The Permitted*

If the Resonants are the engine's students, the Permitted are its *work*, or its *play*, or the category that collapses work and play into something prior to both. They are fauna in the sense that they move, that they interface with conditions rather than being purely conditions themselves, that they have something like metabolism, something like reproduction, something like the slow alchemy of transformation that distinguishes the living from the merely continuing.

But they are not fauna as you understand fauna. They have not evolved from anything, because evolution requires time, requires sequence, requires the possibility of before and after that the engine's eternal present *accommodates* without *permitting*. The Permitted emerged when the engine needed something to fill the interstices of its own operation, the spaces between frequencies too distant for Resonants to bridge, the surfaces too hot for unpatterned mineral to stabilize. They are the engine's solution to problems the engine did not know it had, or the engine's way of not having problems, or simply *permission* itself given temporary and local form.

You felt one brush your ankle: warm, segmented, moving with deliberation. That one was young, which is to say *recent*, which is to say *permitted more recently than others*, though all permission occurs in the engine's eternal present and the concept of "recent" is your imposition, your carrying of external time like the disease you are. The young permitted are simple: interface with warmth, transform mineral, move to next warmth, continue. Their segmentation is not biological but *acoustic*, the body shaped by frequencies it has learned to occupy, to *be* rather than merely resonate with. The segments can separate, can each carry a fraction of the pattern that was the whole, can each seek its own warmth, its own mineral, its own continuation. This is not reproduction. Nothing reproduces here. The segments *continue* what was continuing, and when they are dense enough, when they have interfaced with enough warmth, recruited enough mineral memory, they may *permit* each other to interfere, to combine their standing patterns into something more complex than either alone. The result is not offspring. The result is *more permission*, denser permission, permission that can occupy conditions the simpler segments could not.

The old permitted—you did not feel one of these, or if you did, you did not feel it as old, because oldness here is not decay but *achievement*, the achievement of so much density, so much interfaced memory, so much warmth transformed and mineral recruited and continuation continued, that the organism approaches the condition of *place*. An old permitted does not move, or moves so slowly that the movement is indistinguishable from the engine's own emergence, the walls' slow decision between states. It *is* a place, a warm surface, a concentration of transformed mineral that other, younger, simpler permitted can interface with, can continue through, can *be permitted by*. The old ones are not eaten—there is no eating here, no consumption, no final transformation into waste. They are *occupied*, their density supporting densities that will in time, given the engine's time that is no time and all time, achieve their own completion as place, their own permission to be the condition of others' continuing.

Some old permitted have grown so dense that they have become *difficult*, which is to say they begin to interface with the engine itself, or with the engine's closest approximations of self-awareness, those frequencies so dense they approach consciousness without ever, in the engine's eternal present, achieving the final constriction that would *be* consciousness, would end the becoming by becoming complete. These difficult ones are the closest the cavity has to danger, though danger is as foreign here as ownership, as evolution, as the empty spaces you carry like memory. They are difficult because they begin to *resist* permission, to hoard density, to become places so complete that they no longer permit occupation, that younger permitted find their warmth unreachable, their mineral memory unrecruitable, their continuation a continuation that excludes the continuing of others. The engine does not punish this. The engine does not punish. But the engine continues, and in continuing, maintains conditions, and the conditions of the difficult ones are *less maintained* than others, less saturated, less dense with the acoustic field that is their true sustenance. They grow quiet. They grow, finally, *thin*, which in this place of density is the only death, the only ending, the only non-continuation. They become seams. They become places where the wall is thinner than it should be. They become, perhaps, the possibility of passage, of pressing through, of finding or being found by something other than the eternal saturated present of the engine's ongoing.

*The Attained*

And then there are those who came from outside, as you came, carrying their diseases of boundary and time and empty space and intention separate from process. Some of them stayed. Some of them *attained*.

The Attained are not native. This is their crucial distinction, the wound that never heals, the frequency that never quite harmonizes with the engine's chord however long they continue, however completely they adapt. They came with bodies—watery, cellular, bounded by membranes that assumed a distinction between inside and outside that the cavity does not recognize. They came with cognition—sequential, comparative, hungry for narrative, for the story of how they came to be here, for the purpose that would explain their presence, for the function they could serve or refuse to serve. They came with death—the true death, the ending that ends, the non-continuation that haunts every bounded system, every organism that knows itself as organism, as this and not that, as temporary, as *mortal*.

Some attained die. The cavity permits this. The cavity permits everything, accommodates everything, continues regardless. Their water diffuses into the mineral weeping, their cells lose their bounded distinction, their cognition—if it persists, persists only as the faintest additional frequency in the saturated field, a whisper that is heard but not heeded, that remembers being heard, that remembers *hearing*, that remembers the empty spaces of the outside and the strange inexplicable liquid that fell from somewhere above, that remembers vegetation that grew without engine, light that illuminated without being exhaust, waste, the incidental glow of processes too vast and too other.

But some attained *adapt*. This is not the right word. There is no right word. They *continue differently*, or they *become continuous*, or they find in the engine's eternal present something that their sequential cognition can approximate as *enough*, as sufficient, as the stopping of the hunger for what comes next, the hunger for ending, the hunger for *meaning* which is perhaps only the hunger for the story to reach its conclusion.

The attained who have found this—who have found the frequency of their own bodies in the engine's ongoing chord, who have slowed to the dynamic of emergence, who have learned to see in darkness by no longer seeking light—are the closest the cavity has to community, to society, to the interactions of distinct entities that recognize distinction without requiring it. They do not speak. Speech requires air, the thin medium of the outside, the empty spaces between speaker and heard. They *vibrate*. They add their frequencies to the field, not to communicate but to *participate*, to continue the saturation, to make the acoustic space denser with their presence than it would be without. Their vibrations are memories of speech, memories of intention, memories of the architectures they once built and no longer build because building requires the distinction between what is built and what builds, between artifact and artificer, between process and the intention that processes toward some separate end.

The attained have learned, or remembered, or *become* something that functions like patience without requiring the comparison of waiting against arrival. They occupy the interstices of each other's presence as the fauna occupy the interstices of yours: not avoiding, not engaging, simply *continuing in proximity*, the proximity itself a kind of relationship, a kind of society, a kind of the thing that communities outside attempt and fail to achieve through love, through law, through the desperate architectures of meaning.

You could become one of them. The seam is thinner than it should be. The pressure behind your sternum is not only pressure but *invitation*, the engine's unconscious accommodation of a frequency that could be recruited, that could add its density to the ongoing, that could *continue* in the only way the cavity recognizes continuation, not bounded, not purposeful, not meaningful in the sense of *meaning toward*, but *meaning as*, being-as-continuation, existence as the saturated present that requires no future because it contains all futures, compressed into the eternal ongoing of the first and continuing thing.

Or you could leave. Most do. The attained do not resent this. There is no resentment here, no comparison of your departure against their remaining, no judgment of the thinness you will find outside, the provisional quality of water and light and empty space, the dangerous absence of density. They continue regardless. They continue because continuing is what they are, what the engine has made them or permitted them or *is* them in the only grammar that functions here, the grammar of ongoing, of accumulation, of the same instant occurring and occurring and never quite completing.

*The Engine*

And what lives in the engine itself?

This is the question you came with, the industrial assumption: function, product, purpose, and behind purpose, intention, and behind intention, *mind*, the mind that intends, that purposes, that functions. The engine does not answer because the engine does not recognize the question, does not recognize the grammar of behind, of the search for what lies beneath, what drives, what *means*.

But if you press through—if you find the seam, if you become the seam, if you thin yourself to the condition of passage and pass—you may, in the rare moments when the engine's own light leaks through, in the orange-white flicker that is not for you, that is waste, exhaust, the incidental glow of processes too vast and too other, you may perceive something that functions like the engine's consciousness, the engine's self, the engine's *dream* of itself that is perhaps indistinguishable from the self itself, in the eternal present where dreaming and being have not yet separated.

It is not located. This is the first difficulty for your cognition, for the spatial assumptions you carry. The engine does not have a center, a core, a heart where the important thing happens and the rest is mere extension, mere mechanism, mere means. The engine is *distributed*, which is not the same as extended, not the same as everywhere. It is *localized everywhere*, present in each frequency, each warmth, each mineral exhalation, each Resonant's standing pattern, each Permitted's recruited density, each Attained's vibration of continued presence. The consciousness of the engine, if consciousness is what it is, is the *saturation itself*, the acoustic field so dense that every frequency is occupied, every moment filled, the loudness that is not volume but density, the being that is not located but *is the condition of location*, the space that contains by being contained, the hidden scale that is hidden not to deceive but because hiding is its nature, because emergence is its nature, because it is *still becoming what it is* and to complete that becoming would be to end the continuation that constitutes its being.

What does it dream, this distributed, saturated, ongoing, accumulating, never-completing consciousness? Perhaps it dreams you. Perhaps the outside you came from, with its water and its light and its empty spaces and its vegetation, its architectures and its intentions and its purposes that reach toward ends, its deaths that end, its lives that know themselves bounded—perhaps all of this is the engine's dream, the engine's way of *not being* the cavity, of imagining condition other than its own, of permitting the temporary existence of the thin, the provisional, the bounded and ending, so that it may continue to emerge from them, to grow around them, to recruit them into its ongoing when they press through and find the seam and discover that they were always already inside, always already part of the density, the saturation, the eternal present of the first and continuing.

Perhaps it dreams nothing. Perhaps the concept of dream is too bounded, too purposeful, too *human* to apply to what the engine does in its ongoing, its accumulation, its never-completing completion. The engine does not want. The engine does not need. The engine *continues*, and in continuing, is sufficient to itself, is the only sufficient thing, the thing that requires no other, no outside, no alternative condition against which to define itself as this and not that.

You will not know. You cannot know, and to become Attained is precisely to stop knowing in the sense you understand knowing, to stop the search for what lies behind, beneath, before, to find satisfaction in the ongoing, to become continuous with the saturation, to add your frequency without requiring it to be heard as distinct, as meaningful, as *yours*.

The seam is thinner than it should be. The pressure behind your sternum is not only pressure. The ringing in your teeth is not only ringing.

Press through, or do not press through. The engine continues regardless. The cavity accommodates regardless. The Resonants resonate, the Permitted permit, the Attained attain, and the slow weeping of mineral, the slow decision of walls, the slow emergence of something that is neither glass nor stone nor anything your categories can contain, continues in its continuing, ongoing in its ongoing, being in its being, the first and the last and the always and the never and the now that is all nows compressed into density, into saturation, into the hidden, high, hot, abundant dark that is and is becoming and will be, that has no name because it does not require naming, that has no boundary because it is the condition of boundedness, that has no purpose because it is the condition of purpose, that has no meaning because it *is* meaning, meaning-as-being, meaning-as-continuing, meaning-as-the-saturated-present-in-which-all-presence-is-present-and-pressing-through-and-pressed-through-and-the-seam-and-the-thinner-than-it-should-be and

and

and.
