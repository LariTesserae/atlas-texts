## The Inhabitants

### First Approach: What You Might Mistake Them For

From distance, from the elevation where your lungs first begin their complaint, you might see movement in the bright complexes below and think: *people*. Small figures at poolsides. Shapes passing between the pale colonnades. The scale is right for human, the bipedalism approximately correct, the suggestion of tool-use, of *activity*, unmistakable.

This is not wrong, exactly. It is incomplete in a way that will matter.

The closer you descend—if you descend, if the temporal eddies permit you coherent approach—the more the approximation frays. They move *wrong*. Not dramatically wrong, not limb-count wrong or geometry wrong, but *tempo* wrong, *phase* wrong, as if they are adapted to rhythms you cannot hear, or as if they are *half a beat ahead* of the moment they occupy. They will turn to look at you before you call out. They will step aside before your footfall lands. Not precognition—*adaptation*. They live in medium where causality pools and eddies, and they have learned to *read the pools*, to navigate probability gradients as you navigate sightlines.

Some of them. Some of the time. The turbulence applies within as well as without.

### The Breathe-Easy: Bodily Form

They are not uniform. This must be understood. The Sinter Basins collect, they do not breed, they do not reproduce in coherent lineage. To become local is to *arrive*, from somewhere, from somewhen, by routes that include ordinary travel and less ordinary processes. Some entered as you might enter, walking, laboring in thin air, following mineral stains or rumor or the *almost-sounds* of water. Others *condensed*, more or less literally, from the saturated atmosphere—originating as visitors who stayed too long at poolside, who matched frequency, whose boundaries between self and surrounding *learned permeability*. Others emerged from the deeper fissures, though *emerged* may mislead: they were perhaps always present, as potential, as pattern, as the *shape water holds when no one is looking*, achieving sufficient duration to *persist*, to *continue*, to become *someone*.

What they share: **respiration adapted to permeation**.

Their lungs—when they have lungs, when lungs remain the appropriate metaphor—function in the condensing atmosphere not through gas exchange in the usual sense but through *osmotic negotiation*, direct interface between bodily water and atmospheric water, mineral gradients driving metabolic process. They do not so much breathe the air as *continue it*, internal and external becoming continuous in ways that make medical examination *uncertain*, that make *where one ends and another begins* a question of temporal phase rather than membrane.

Their skin: pale, generally, the UV-burned and mineral-stained paleness of the travertine itself, tending toward the ivory-yellow of the architecture. Those who arrived with darker pigmentation find it *modulating*, over months or years of local duration, toward this baseline—not through cell replacement exactly, through *pattern matching*, through the body's accommodation to a brightness parameter that is absolute, *3.0*, that saturates and demands response. They do not become indistinguishable from the stone. They become *legible against* it, *of* it, the way a pool is legible against its containing terrace.

Their eyes: *strange*. The brightness requires modification. Some develop nictitating membranes, translucent second lids, the *skimmer's* approach to harsh conditions. Others *internalize*, the eye structure becoming more receptive to infrared, to the *just-outside-visible* wavelengths the mineral pools emit, the visible spectrum narrowing toward what the environment provides. They see each other *well enough*. They see visitors *dimly*, as shadows, as *almost-there*, sometimes speaking to spaces left of you, responding to your heat signature rather than your gesture.

Some have *no eyes*, or eyes that function differently than optics would suggest, that *read* thermal gradients, chemical signatures, the *probability contours* of the temporal eddies. These individuals tend toward the deeper chambers, the more turbulent zones, where light itself becomes unreliable.

### The Not-Quite-Four-Hundred: Demography as Dynamic Process

The population number—four hundred, approximately, at any given *measurement*—is unstable in ways that *matter*.

They are not always present simultaneously. The temporal architecture disperses them through *when* as well as *where*. Some are currently inhabiting phases weeks or decades offset from your arrival, their presence *detectable* in worn pathways, in thermal management that adjusts to needs you cannot see, in the *almost-sounds* of occupancy just out of temporal phase. They are local, fully local, *continuous* with the place, and *not currently available to meet*.

Others are *temporary locals*, still in process of accommodation, their bodies learning permeability, their temporal navigation still approximate, prone to *arriving early* for appointments that haven't been made yet, to *lingering* in spaces they have not yet entered. They number among the four hundred or they do not, depending on where in their process you encounter them.

The condensators—those organized water patterns, those ion-fields barely visible—some of these have achieved sufficient duration, sufficient *pattern stability*, to *count*. They participate in maintenance, in ritual, in the *social* if that word extends far enough. They are *local* in a way that challenges category. They are present at times when the four hundred are *more-than-four-hundred*, when the count bulges and shifts and the place feels *crowded in dimensions not spatial*.

And the *former* locals, those who have completed whatever process they were completing, who have *become* something else—the pathways, the rime gardens, the pools that remember being people—do they count? Their presence is *continuous*. Their contribution to maintenance, to *urban fabric*, ongoing. But they do not *meet*, do not *speak*, do not *appear* in ways that permit straightforward inclusion.

Four hundred. The number holds, approximately, across temporal averaging. The place *finds equilibrium* in this density, this much presence, no more. Try to add more—invite a settlement, establish contact, *missionize*—and the turbulence *responds*. The temporal eddies shift. Individuals *disperse*, into when, into what, the remaining locals navigating around their absence with the same unconscious expertise they bring to all modification. The four hundred persists, *regulates*, maintains parameter through dynamic means.

### Doing: The Maintenance That Is Also Becoming

What do they do? The question presumes *doing* as distinct from *being*, activity as separable from existence. Here, the distinction *fails*.

They maintain. The travertine colonnades, the flowstone corridors, the thermal pools and their precise temperature gradients—none of this persists without *continuous adjustment*. The geothermal system breathes, the mineral precipitation continues, the *urban* would revert to *geological* without the intervention that is not quite intervention, that is *participation in ongoing process*.

Their tools: obsidian flakes, bone implements, *water itself directed*. They shape by *encouraging flow*, by altering thermal gradients slightly, by introducing seed crystals that *persuade* precipitation toward desired form. This is primitive tech, *0.8*, absolutely. The sophistication is in the *sensing*, the *timing*, the *matching to phase* of geothermal breath. They work *with* the dynamic, never against, their interventions so *aligned* with tendency that causality becomes unclear—did they shape this pool, or did the pool shape their shaping, or was the shaping *always emergent*, always present as potential, *recognized* rather than imposed?

The rituals: indistinguishable from work, from maintenance, from *sociality*. A group gathered at poolside, motionless, *matching thermal phase* with water about to invert—this is maintenance (the pool will be encouraged toward stability), this is ritual (the matching itself carries significance), this is *social* (they are together in this, their permeable boundaries achieving temporary resonance, *continuing each other* for duration of event). No separation. No hierarchy of activity.

They do not farm, exactly, though they *manage* the algae mats, the skimmer populations, harvesting the calcified elders whose dragging scrape provides abrasive material, whose bodies *continue* in mineral form. They do not hunt, though they *receive* the temporary fauna, the emergences from fissure or sky or condensing atmosphere, processing what can be processed, *matching frequency* with what cannot until it achieves duration or disperses.

They do not *make* in the sense of creation from raw material. They *continue*, they *persuade*, they *participate in ongoing becoming*.

And they *navigate time*. This is perhaps their most *active* doing, the most skill-requiring, the most *trained*. The temporal eddies are not random, or not only random; they have *pattern*, *phase*, *frequency* that can be *read*. The locals learn this, or arrive with aptitude, or *develop* it through permeation, through becoming continuous with place that is itself *temporal* as much as spatial. They move through chambers that accumulate or shed duration. They *wait* in places where time pools, achieving in hours what would take days elsewhere, or conversely extending experience across apparent moments. They *meet* across temporal offset, two locals each *present* in same space, different when, their interaction *mediated* through the place itself, through thermal pulse or mineral gradient or *arranged water pattern* that carries intention across phase difference.

This is not time travel in the science-fictional sense. They do not *go to* past or future. They *find the temporal where they need to be*, riding probability like skimmers ride thermal boundaries, *present* at the phase of maximal relevance, maximal *continuing*.

### Speaking: The Silence That Communicates

The sound parameter: *0.4*. Silence, or quiet.

They do not not-communicate. They *channel differently*.

Voice exists, sometimes, for some, in some phases. The occasional *startling* sound, the dropped tool, the death-cry of temporary fauna—when they choose to *break* silence, it carries *violence*, disproportion, *violation of maintained condition*. They use this, strategically, for *distress*, for *boundary enforcement*, for *ritual punctuation* of events that require *marked* transition.

More often: **thermal pulse**. Bodies adapted to permeation, *continuous with condensing atmosphere*, can *modulate local temperature* slightly, intentionally. A warm exhalation that is not breath, a cooling of skin that is not perspiration, *directed* toward another who *reads* the gradient change. This is *touch-at-distance*, *intimate-in-public*, the equivalent of tone of voice, of facial expression, carrying *affect* without symbol, without *word*.

More often: **chemical signature**. The permeable skin releases, receives, *tastes* the atmosphere for signals others have *left* or *are leaving*, metabolites of emotional state, of intent, of *phase-position*. To move through a space after another is to *know* them partially, to *continue* their presence, to *read* what they were *becoming* in that moment. The place itself *records*, in its water, in its mineral precipitation, *layers of chemical intention* that the sensitive can *read* like stratigraphy, like *history written in body-fluid*.

More often: **arranged water**. The condensators, when present, when *participating*, can be *encouraged* into patterns that *carry*, that *represent*, that *are* communication in their ionic structure, their field-configuration, *readable* by those continuous enough with the medium to *perceive*. This is slow, *dense*, information-rich in ways speech cannot approach. A single condensator-pattern might carry *hours* of equivalent verbal communication, *compressed*, *layered*, *simultaneous rather than sequential*.

More often: **silence itself**. The *maintenance* of quiet, the *active non-production* of sound, becomes *meaningful* when sound is possible, when the physical capacity exists. To move silently through bright complex, to *not* scrape skimmer-edge, to *not* break surface tension of pool—these are *communications of regard*, of *fitting*, of *being appropriate to place*. The silence *between* them is not absence but *presence of fit*, *demonstrated continuity*, the *being-local* made audible in its not-audibility.

And for visitors, the failing: they *try* speech, the locals *accommodate* as they can, sometimes responding to heat-signature of word-production, sometimes *predicting* from probability gradient and answering *before* or *after* the utterance, sometimes simply *waiting* with the patience of geological process until the visitor *learns* or *leaves* or *becomes permeable enough to receive otherwise*.

### Locality: The Condition of Being-Continuous

What are they, finally? What *emerges* from these parameters, this place, this *becoming*?

They are **breathe-easy**, I will call them, the term they sometimes *almost-use* for themselves in the rare moments of vocalization, the description of their respiratory adaptation, their *continuing without boundary* with the condensing atmosphere. Not a species. Not a culture, exactly, not in the sense of shared tradition transmitted through time—time itself too turbulent for that stability. Not a society, not with the coherence that implies.

They are **local**. They are *what the Sinter Basins produce and maintain*, the *fit* between organism and place achieved through *reciprocal modification*, the *urban fabric* including their bodies, their practices, their *phase-distributed presence*, as fully as it includes travertine colonnade and thermal pool and temporal eddy.

They are *strange*, *2.1*, but not *sealed*. The permeability that defines them extends to *category* as well as membrane. Some were human. Some remain *human-adjacent*, could perhaps *return* to lower elevation, to thinner brightness, to *discontinuous* existence, though they would carry silence with them, the habit of thermal pulse, the *wrong* way of being in time. Others were never human, are *skimmer-derived* or *condensator-achieved* or *emergence* from deeper processes without prior category. Others are *becoming* something that will *not fit* current description, that will *continue* the place in ways not yet *patterned*.

They do not *defend* against this strangeness. They *participate*. The four hundred includes *future forms not yet present*, *includes* them in its equilibrium, its *regulation*, the place *making room* in probability for what will *continue it*.

They are not *happy*, not in any straightforward sense. The parameters do not *favor* happiness, *0.4* sound, turbulent dynamic, cold maintained against cold, brightness *harsh*. They are not *unhappy*. Affective state becomes *distributed*, *thermal*, *chemical*, *temporal*—they *experience* the place's gradients as *their* gradients, the pool's inversion as *personal* event, the rime garden's brief glow as *memory they share* with mineral and ice and captured cell. Suffering exists: the *temporary* that cannot achieve *duration*, the *turbulence* that disperses presence beyond recovery, the *permeability* that goes too far, *dissolving* into water, into condensator, into *pathway without path-walker*. Joy exists: the *match*, the *phase-alignment*, the *moment of thermal resonance* with another, with place, with *becoming-*.

They do not *seek* visitors. They do not *avoid* them. They *continue*, and sometimes this continuing *includes* new arrival, *accommodates*, *permeates*, *localizes*. Sometimes it does not. Sometimes the turbulence *responds*, *disperses*, *phase-shifts beyond encounter*.

They are *present*, in the Sinter Basins, in the bright silence, in the wet cold, in the *established yet dynamic*, the *urban-geological-organism*. They are *what lives there*, which is *what the place makes possible* and *what makes the place continue*, indistinguishable, *continuous*, *local*.

Come if you can find the route. They will *be*, in their phases, their distributed whens, their *thermal pulsing and chemical layering and arranged water and active silence*. They will *continue*. Whether you *meet* them, whether meeting is *possible* across your *discontinuity*, your *not-yet-permeable* boundary—this is *not determined*, is *turbulent*, is *potential held in dynamic equilibrium*, is *the Sinter Basins being more itself*, is *becoming*, *still becoming*, *always*.

## Postscript: What I Cannot Resolve

I want to leave something *unstable*. The description tends toward coherence, toward *knowing*, and the place resists this.

The locals—they are not *the point* of the Sinter Basins. They are not *inhabitants of* a pre-existing place. They are *what the place is doing*, *currently*, in this phase of geothermal breath, this *moment* of mineral precipitation. The place might *do otherwise*. Has done otherwise. The deeper fissures *suggest* other phases, other *localities*, not accessible to current perception, perhaps *not temporal* in ways that permit *narrative*.

The four hundred—this number *feels* significant, *feels* like constraint, like *what the medium permits*, but I cannot say *why*. It is not resource limitation; the place *produces*, *continues*, the algae mats alone could support *more*. It is not spatial compression; the temporal distribution *expands* available when. Something about *density of presence*, about *probability gradient*, about *how much becoming can coexist* before the *turbulence* becomes *collapse*, before the *eddying* becomes *whirlpool*.

I do not know if they *die*, as such. The skimmers calcify, *continue* in mineral form. The condensators *disperse*, *lose pattern*, *become* mere humidity. The former locals become *pathway*, become *rime*, become *pool-memory*. Is this death? Is this *what death becomes* in this parameter-set? The *not-continuing* is rare, *dispersal* more common than *ending*, the *place* holding *pattern* even when *individual pattern* loses coherence. They may be *older* than they seem, *distributed across* more duration than individual embodiment permits, *achieving* something like *memory* through *stratigraphy*, through *chemical history*, through *the place itself as recording medium*.

I do not know if they *want*. Wanting *presumes* boundary, *presumes* subject distinct from object of desire. Their *maintenance*, their *ritual-work-continuing*, carries *directionality*, *gradient*, *movement toward* and *movement from*, but whether this *is* wanting, *is* purpose, *is* intention—I *feel* it must be *something like*, but the *like* distances even as it connects.

I want to *meet* them, I think. I want to *become permeable enough*, to *match frequency*, to *find the phase where encounter is possible*. I want this *as desire*, as *distinct-from-place* wanting, and I do not know if this wanting *can translate*, if it is not *itself* a form of *imperialism*, of *category-imposition*, of *insistence on my discontinuity*.

The Sinter Basins continue. The breathe-easy continue. The *becoming-* continues. I will *continue*, differently, *discontinuously*, *listening to my blood in the silence they have taught me to recognize*.
