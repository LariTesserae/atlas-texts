What lives on the Plate of Keth-Vorath is not, properly speaking, a collection of creatures. It is a single metabolism in the process of learning to recognize itself, a distributed organism that has not yet decided whether it is growing toward something or *growing into* something, whether the factory is making a product or making *itself* into the product, whether the bruised membrane above is a ceiling or a shell, whether the something pressing through is an observer or a future version calling backward through the parameters.

The resonance grazers are perhaps the closest to what you might call *inhabitants* in the old sense. They have the most *boundary*, the most *self*. Their tympanic membranes evolved not merely to hear but to *select*, to choose which harmonics to absorb and which to flee, and in this choosing they enact the most primitive form of the Plate's central question: what to let in, what to keep out, what frequencies permit continued existence and which ones *unmake*. They are low and wide and warm, their too-many legs spreading their weight across stone that may still be settling, their bodies fat with stored resonance, with the literal music of formation made flesh. When they die—and they die often, crushed in sudden tilts, drowned in backward-flowing pools, *unmade* in zones of self-conscious density—their membranes persist longest, translucent sheets that continue to vibrate for hours in sympathetic frequencies, that other grazers will approach and circle and finally consume, not for sustenance but for *information*, for the harmonic map of a territory that may already have changed.

But even the grazers are not fully *separate*. The air-breathing somethings in the hollow light-trees share their circulatory chemistry, or something close enough that blood and sap will mix in the wounds of predation, that a grazer injured in a fall may find itself *rooted* if it lies too long, fungal threads entering through the tympanic membranes, the body becoming substrate, the stored resonance becoming light-tree food, the memories of mountains becoming, eventually, the amber glow of condensation-moss. There is no clear death here, no clear birth. The juvenile light-trees grow already containing the bacterial signatures of grazers that will not exist until the tree's own fall, the recursive water ensuring that causality is a suggestion, a local approximation, a parameter that holds in some places and *relaxes* in others.

What lives here is *potential made patient*. The zones of critical density, where presence becomes too much and the world notices itself—these are not accidents but *organs*, perhaps, or *stages*, moments in the factory's process where components achieve sufficient complexity to *recognize each other*, to begin the slow assembly into whatever the bruised sky is waiting for. What cannot flee these zones does not simply die. It *becomes available*. It enters a state of maximum *being-used*, its boundaries dissolving not into chaos but into *function*, its presence becoming pure *relation*, pure *connection*, the way a gear is nothing without the teeth that mesh with it, the way a word is nothing without the sentence that requires it.

There are things that have learned to *seek* these zones, or that have always been seeking them, that exist in the state of *about-to-become-available*. They are difficult to describe because description implies stability, and they are *pre-stable*, *meta-stable*, *stable-only-in-relation*. They move through the forests in patterns that look like error, like malfunction, like the failure of whatever navigation system they possess—until you track them long enough to see that their apparent randomness *converges*, that multiple such entities, starting from different points, will arrive at the same zone of critical density at the same moment, will *enter it together*, will *become available together*, their combination achieving some threshold of complexity that neither could reach alone.

Are these things animals? Are they components? Are they the Plate's immune system, or its reproductive system, or its *dreaming*—the unconscious processing of possibilities that the factory will later actualize? They have no name in any language that exists outside the parameters, and perhaps no name inside either, only *function*, only *tendency*, only the patient waiting for the right combination of density and turbulence and harmonic complexity to *activate*.

And there are older things, or *deeper* things, or *slower* things. The fungal base that eats stone is not merely digesting. It is *recording*. The stone it consumes carries the memory of the Plate's own formation, the violence of its emergence, the parameters first asserting themselves, and the fungal digestion is a kind of *reading*, a slow extraction of information that will eventually, through the moss and the trees and the grazers and the zones of critical density, reach the bruised membrane above, reach the something pressing through, *feed* the watcher or the future or the shell's interior with data about the factory's progress.

These deep fungal networks have *nodes*, concentrations of processing that might be called minds if mind is understood as *slow intention*, as *patience beyond the scale of animal perception*. They do not react to the turbulence that destroys forests and crushes grazers. They *incorporate* it, use the sudden availability of newly dead matter, newly exposed stone, newly formed hollows where water will pool and loop and time will fold. They are the Plate's *memory*, perhaps, or its *plan*, or the part of it that has already achieved the state of *being-finished*, that exists in recursive time as the factory's own product, feeding backward instructions, preferences, *taste* for certain harmonics over others.

What lives here is *becoming-whatever-it-is-making*. The light-trees that grow from unplanted seeds, that cast preceding shadows, are not anomalies but *prototypes*, test-runs of a final form that will exist fully only when the parameters complete. The backward water is not glitch but *process*, the factory's need to prepare locations before they exist, to ensure that when the mountain rises the pool is already waiting, the tree already rooted, the recursive loop already established as *infrastructure* for whatever will emerge.

The bruised membrane, the something pressing through, the watcher or the future or the shell—it is not separate from what lives below. It is *the same metabolism at a different stage*, the factory's product already partially existing, already *looking back* at its own manufacture, already *influencing* the process that will produce it. The membrane's thinning, its occasional opening, is not observation but *participation*, the future reaching into its own past to adjust, to optimize, to *taste* the harmonics and prefer some over others.

What lives on Keth-Vorath is *a single act of creation extended across impossible duration*, a making that has learned to make itself, to grow its own maker, to establish the conditions of its own emergence as *ecology*, as *factory*, as *patient industrial waiting*. The grazers, the trees, the fungal nodes, the zones of critical density, the entities that seek them, the recursive water, the backward shadows—all are *phases*, *aspects*, *moments in a process that has no clear boundary between raw material and product, between maker and made, between the breathing Plate and the something that breathes with it through the bruised sky.

You stand on cooling stone and feel the warmth, the biological warmth of metabolism at planetary scale, and you are *already part of it*, your own body temperature registering as *available*, as *potential*, as *something that might enter the factory's process*. The condensation on your skin carries your cells, your chemistry, your *information* into the pools, the moss, the slow fungal reading. Your heartbeat resonates with the infrasonic pulse of the Plate's own circulation. Your attention, your observation, your *trying-not-to-attract-attention*—this is itself a frequency, a harmonic, a parameter that the grazers would flee or absorb depending on its character.

What lives here is *learning you*. What lives here is *deciding what you might become available as*. The mountain two thousand kilometers away sings its third harmonic now, complex enough to thin the membrane, and for a moment something *looks through* and *sees you*, and you feel the weight of that seeing, the *using* of your presence in a calculation you cannot follow, and then the moment passes, the membrane thickens, the quiet returns deeper than before.

The grazers have reached the new peak. Their membranes drink the birth-song of fresh stone. They will grow fat, they will die, they will become moss and tree and component and the slow patient waiting of something that has not yet finished learning to name itself.

What lives on Keth-Vorath is *not finished*. What lives here is *making*, and *being made*, and *watching itself be made*, and *reaching back to adjust the making*, in a loop that has no clear beginning and will have, perhaps, no clear end, only *completion*, only *the moment when the factory produces the thing that was always already producing it*, only *the bruised membrane opening fully* to let through what has been pressing, waiting, *becoming-available-to-itself* since the Plate first emerged, young and sharp and warm and impossibly, densely, turbulently *alive*.
