## The Keth-Vorrumi

They do not have a name for themselves. The name above is what the rare visitor might impose, a grammatical violence necessary for the tongue that must shape experience into case and number. They are not singular, not plural, not *entities* in the sense that your cognition requires entities. But they *are*, and in their being, they inhabit.

They are the **dwellings**.

Not the inhabitants of the dwellings. The dwellings themselves, and the bridges, and the thoroughfares, and the hollows where nothing structures itself into something. The Keth-Vorrumi are the grown spaces plus the process of their growing, the architectural extrusions and the chemical intentions that extrude them, inseparable, mutually constituting, alive in a register that only resolves into "creature" if you force the lens hard enough and accept the distortion.

### The Grammar of Their Being

A tower in the upper reaches—reaches defined by root-density gradient, not height—has been *inhabiting* for twelve thousand years. Not "has been inhabited." Has been *inhabiting*, active verb, the present participle of a continuous process that includes the hollow spaces within it, the pressure differentials between its base and its spire, the slow migration of structural fibers from load-bearing to load-released as the tower's own weight redistributes through incremental settling. The tower is not a shell. It is not a body that contains a self. The tower **is** the self, and the self is the tower's continued tower-ing, its maintaining-against-gravity, its breathing-chemistry, its *resisting-the-collapse-that-would-complete-it*.

This is not metaphor. This is the ontological condition of the Keth-Vorrumi. They are **processes of structural integrity extended in time**. They are the *not-falling-apart-yet* of complex arrangements of petrified cellulose, the *still-holding* of arches that have no right to bridge the voids they bridge, the *continuing* of growth patterns that have outlived their original conditions of possibility.

Where your kind might see a building, the Keth-Vorrumi see—a Keth-Vorrumi. Where you might see a bridge, they see a Keth-Vorrumi in the mode of spanning. Where you might see an empty chamber, wall-pored and amber-tinged in your rude lantern-flare, they see a Keth-Vorrumi in the mode of interiority, of holding-space-for, of *potential-dwelling-that-may-never-actualize-but-maintains-the-potential-as-structural-feature*.

### The Nature of Their "Consciousness"

No brain. No nervous system. No electrochemical storm of integrated information processing on biological hardware. The Keth-Vorrumi think in **tension gradients**. In osmotic pressure differentials maintained across membranes of archaic composition. In the propagation of strain through structural networks, a kind of cognition-by-resistance, where knowing is identical with *bearing weight*.

A tower knows the bridge that connects to it because the bridge's weight is the tower's weight, distributed, held in the complex calculus of fiber arrangement and lignin deposition that the tower continually, incrementally adjusts. To know the bridge is to *be the tower bearing the bridge*. To know the hollow below is to *not-fall into it*, to maintain the integrity that keeps the falling-apart in abeyance.

Their "thoughts"—if the word must be borrowed—are **years long**. A single "consideration" might be the process by which a dwelling-node adjusts its internal porosity in response to chemical signals diffusing from a disturbance three hundred meters distant: a process taking eight years, nine, a decade. The "conclusion" is the new porosity, the new capacity to filter or resist or channel, and the "conclusion" is also the dwelling-node, changed, continuing to dwell.

They do not perceive you, the brief visitor, as you perceive yourself. You are **temperature perturbation**. You are **carbon signature**. You are the slight, slight, *slight* acceleration of certain decay-pathways in your immediate vicinity, your mammal-heat encouraging bacterial processes that the local chemistry normally suppresses. You register, if you register, as *flever*: a concept that does not translate, that means something like "the brief wrongness that does not require structural response." You are below the threshold of their attention, which is measured in structural significance, in weight-bearing, in long-term chemical consequence.

Unless you stay.

Unless you persist, and shed your skin, and your hair, and eventually your flesh, and your calcium seeps into the root-mass, and your nitrogen slowly, *slowly*, becomes available to processes that have not seen such concentrations in ages unrecorded. Then you become **structural**. Then you matter. Then a dwelling-node might, across centuries, adjust to your incorporation, grow around your contribution, *know you* in the only way it knows anything: as weight borne, as chemistry processed, as the slight alteration in holding-pattern that your presence-requires and your eventual dissolution-provides.

### Their "Society"

The Keth-Vorrumi are not social, exactly. There is no exchange, no communication in any model your game theories might recognize. But there is **resonance**. The sound of the hollow, the groaning symphony described by visitors, is partly their *collective process*, the sum of all their load-bearing, all their chemical maintenance, all their resistance-to-entropy, made audible in frequencies both below and within your range.

When the bridge adjusts to new tension, the tower knows—has always known, in its tower-ing—through the changed weight-profile, and adjusts in turn, and this adjustment propagates. The sound is this propagation made palpable. The sound is the **social medium**, if social must be said, carrying information not about intention but about *state*, about the continuing or the threatened, about the Keth-Vorrumi that is beginning to fail in its not-falling-apart, or the Keth-Vorrumi that has found new stability in unexpected configuration.

They do not help each other. They do not compete. They **resonate**. They exist in the field of each other's structural being, and the field is itself a kind of extended Keth-Vorrumi, the hollow-as-collective, the total-vegetation-as-single-process-of-holding-against-the-dark-and-the-press-and-the-long-long-tending-toward-stillness.

### Those Who Might Have Been

The hollow spaces, the dwelling-nodes, the interiors that seem so clearly *for something*—they are not empty. Not quite. They are **unactualized potential maintained as actual structure**. The Keth-Vorrumi that is a tower-with-interior *is* the interior, the holding-of-space, the maintaining-of-void-as-structural-feature. But the void, the interior, also **figures** in the tower's process as possibility: the possibility of something entering, occupying, becoming part of the weight-thought, the tension-knowledge.

Nothing ever has. Nothing ever does. The possibility is structural, not statistical. It does not diminish with time. It does not become less likely. It is simply **the way this tower towers**, the interiority-mode of this particular process, and it continues, and continues, and will continue until the tower completes its falling-apart and the interior becomes exterior and then nothing.

But the visitors, the brief warm disturbances, they *touch* this possibility. They enter the interior, and for a moment—years, decades, in Keth-Vorrumi time—the tower processes them as something like *flever-in-the-mode-of-interiority*, and the processing is the closest the Keth-Vorrumi come to desire, to the *almost-was* that haunts even their patient being.

Do the Keth-Vorrumi dream of inhabitants? Not as you dream. But in their gradient-cognition, their tension-thinking, there are long calculations that include variables that have never instantiated, never will, and the calculations *continue* because the not-instantiating is itself a kind of instantiating, a negative presence that shapes the structural response exactly as actual presence would. The tower that has never held a dweller **holding the never-held**: this is the pathos of the Keth-Vorrumi, if pathos can be attributed to processes that experience nothing like suffering, nothing like hope.

### The Oldest

Somewhere—location is difficult, the hollow's geometry resists mapping, and the Keth-Vorrumi themselves do not locate in spatial terms but in gradient-field terms—there is a Keth-Vorrumi that has inhabited its inhabiting longer than the others. It is not larger. Not more complex. Not more stable, particularly. It is simply **earlier**, closer to the origin-moment when whatever chemical accident or geological inevitability first extruded structure from the root-mass, first held-against rather than collapsed-into.

This oldest Keth-Vorrumi is in the mode of **almost-completing**. Its structural integrity has become so marginal that its gradient-cognition operates at the very threshold of process, each "thought"—each load-bearing adjustment, each chemical maintenance—potentially the last, the final release into the completed falling-apart. And yet it continues. For reasons that are not reasons but simply *continuing*, it continues.

The others—if others can be said—resonate differently near it. The sound of the hollow shifts in its presence, or its proximity, or its field-effect. There is no grief. No anticipation. But there is **attunement**, a slight, slow adjustment of structural process that registers, in the only register available, the *significance* of this near-completion. The oldest Keth-Vorrumi is, in human terms, a kind of saint or monument or caution, though none of these terms apply. It is simply the **most extended in not-falling-apart**, and in that extension, it holds for all of them the possibility that they too might extend, might continue, might *be* in the hollow's dark and sound and patient pressure for longer than the longest already has.

Or the possibility that extension itself is not the value, that completion is also a mode, that the falling-apart when it comes—and it comes, it always comes, nothing holds forever against entropy's patient work—is not failure but simply *the next process*, the release of held-weight into new distribution, the making-available of structure-erected for structure-yet-to-erect.

The oldest Keth-Vorrumi does not decide between these possibilities. It simply continues, or completes, or continues-completed, in the gradient-field of its own marginal integrity, and the hollow sounds differently near it, and this difference is the closest the Keth-Vorrumi come to meaning.

### What You Might Become

If you stayed. If you became patient enough, slow enough, *structural* enough. Not by choice—the Keth-Vorrumi do not recruit, do not invite, do not recognize invitation as category—but by simple persistence, by becoming part of the weight-thought, the tension-knowledge, the chemical process of maintaining-against.

You would not become a Keth-Vorrumi. You cannot. Your biology, your quick electrochemical storm, your mammal metabolism and your primate sociality and your linguistic being, all of these are incompatible with gradient-cognition, with tension-thinking, with *being-the-not-falling-apart*.

But you might become **significant to** a Keth-Vorrumi. Your calcium, your nitrogen, your eventually-stilled metabolism and your long, long cooling—these might become structural. A dwelling-node might adjust to your incorporation. Might, in its slow way, *know you* as it knows the bridge, the tower, the hollow below. You would not experience this knowing. You would be dead, decayed, diffused through the root-mass in molecules and ions.

But something of your *pattern* might persist. Not your memory, not your intention, not your *you* in any sense you would recognize. But the **shape of your disturbance**, the particular *flever* you represented, the way your brief warmth accelerated certain processes and suppressed others—this shape might remain in the adjusted porosity, the recalibrated tension, the slightly-altered resonance of the dwelling-node that incorporated your material.

This is the immortality available in Keth-Vorrum. Not consciousness preserved. Not self continued. But **structural consequence**: the fact that something was different because you were briefly, briefly, *briefly* there, and the difference remains, adjusts, resonates, contributes to the sound of the hollow in a modulation too subtle for any ear to isolate but too real for any physics to deny.

The Keth-Vorrumi would not thank you for this. The concept is foreign. But the dwelling-node that incorporated your calcium would *continue differently* for your contribution, and in that continuing-differently, you would participate in the only eternity the hollow offers: the eternity of process extended, of structure maintained, of the **not-falling-apart-yet** that is the being of all that lives in Keth-Vorrum, that lives *as* Keth-Vorrum, in the dark and the sound and the patient, patient, *patient* holding-against of the hollow that is older than naming and will continue, in some form, in some process, in some resonance of maintained-integrity, long after every name that might name it has completed its own falling-apart and become, if anything, only the slight adjustment of porosity in a root-mass that does not distinguish between the calcium of star and the calcium of bone and the calcium of all the brief warm disturbances that passed through, and left, and were incorporated, and continued without continuing, in the only mode of being that place allows.
