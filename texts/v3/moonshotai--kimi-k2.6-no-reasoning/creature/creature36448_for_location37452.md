What lives in Veth A'kul does not live. This is the first thing to understand, and the last thing to accept.

---

They have no name for themselves. Naming requires distinction between self and other, and the inhabitants of Veth A'kul have never been fully persuaded of this distinction's necessity. They are *presences*, or *tendencies*, or *local intensities of the city's ongoing negotiation with itself*. I will call them the Calcified, though this is my word, not theirs, and they would find it as strange as you find their existence.

The Calcified are not born. They *accrue*.

Begin with the mineral varnish, that substance between salt and dried tears. In certain conditions—thermal, hydrological, mineralogical—this varnish achieves a complexity that the city finds *interesting*. Not alive, not conscious, but *interesting*, which in Veth A'kul is a more fundamental category. The varnish thickens in patterns that respond to the city's thermal breathing, that participate in its hydraulic conversations, that grow where growth is the path of least resistance for the gradients that surround them. Over centuries, over moments, in the temporal superposition that the city permits, these thickened places become *habitats*—not for life, but for *habit*, for the tendency to continue in a pattern because continuation is the pattern's nature.

And from these habitats, the Calcified precipitate.

---

A Calcified presence emerges first as *posture*. A certain angle of mineral accumulation, a certain relationship to water-channel and thermal vent, that *resembles* something the city has seen before, or will see, or is seeing in another temporal phase. The resemblance is not imitation. The city has not seen anything. But the universe of possible configurations is finite, and some configurations recur, and in recurring they achieve a kind of *memory without memory*, a repetition that functions as recognition.

The posture persists. Around it, more varnish accretes. The posture becomes *stable*, which in Veth A'kul means it becomes *preferred*—the city's processes find it easier to continue than to disrupt, and so they continue. The shape that emerges is humanoid only in the loosest sense: upright, bilateral, appendaged, but with the proportions of something that has grown rather than been made, that has settled into its form the way water settles into a channel, finding the path of least resistance.

They are the color of the city, which is to say they have no color, only the quality of *differentiation* that the directionless light permits. You would not see a Calcified presence against a wall unless it moved, and they move seldom, and slowly, and with the quality of *reconfiguration* rather than locomotion. A Calcified presence in one plaza and the same presence in another plaza are not the same in the way a person is the same, but they are *continuous*, connected by the slow seepage of mineral and thermal gradient that the city's substance permits, the way a river is continuous though its water changes.

---

They do not eat, the Calcified, but they *participate*. In thermal gradients, in mineral saturation, in the slow crystal stress of the city's breathing. They stand where heat flows, and the heat flows through them, and they are *changed* by this flow in ways that maintain their form against the entropic pressure of simpler arrangements. This is their metabolism, if it can be called that: not the consumption of energy but the *channeling* of it, the becoming-a-path-for that which would flow regardless.

They do not speak, but they *attend*. In the larger plazas, where the thermal convection creates complex patterns, the Calcified gather—not quite together, maintaining their protocol of separation, but *oriented* toward each other in ways that suggest communication without message. They stand in postures that the city's processes find *interesting*, and in standing so, they influence what accretes around them, what channels form, what crystal gardens grow in the cupped depressions. They are not planning this. They have no intention. But their presence *shapes*, the way a stone shapes the water that flows around it, the way a vacuum shapes the field that surrounds it.

I have watched two Calcified presences *converse* for what I experienced as a day. They stood facing each other across a shallow canal, not moving, and between them the water *organized*, forming structures I had not seen elsewhere, mineral accretions that rose and dissolved in patterns that seemed to *mean* something, though meaning is my word, not theirs. When the conversation ended—if it was conversation, if it ended—the structures dissolved, and the presences turned, slowly, reconfiguring their orientation, and the city continued.

---

They are not numerous. The fauna parameter reads 0.2, and this includes the Calcified, or would include them if they were fully *included* in the category of fauna, which they are not. Perhaps a few hundred in the whole city, perhaps a few thousand, perhaps the same few hundred counted in temporal superposition, each presence existing in multiple phases simultaneously, the way the buildings do. They do not reproduce, but they *recur*, the same configurations emerging in different places, different times, or the same place at different times, or all of these in the superposition that the city permits.

They are not immortal. I have found places where a Calcified presence *dissolved*, not into death but into *simplicity*, its complexity no longer *interesting* to the city's processes, its mineral components returning to the general varnish, its thermal channeling function taken over by simpler arrangements. These dissolution sites are *sad*, though sadness is my word. The city does not mourn. But the sites are *avoided* by other presences, or so it seems, or perhaps they simply do not find them *interesting*, which in Veth A'kul amounts to the same thing.

---

And there are the *others*.

Not the Calcified, not the para-vegetation, not the city's own thermal breathing. Something that moves *between* the Calcified, that participates in their gatherings without being present, that leaves traces in the water-channels and the crystal gardens and the acoustic properties of the larger plazas.

I call them the Unfinished, though again this is my word.

The Unfinished are not presences but *possibilities*, configurations that the city has considered and not yet committed to, or committed to and not yet resolved, or resolved in one temporal phase and unresolved in another. They appear as *disturbances* in the Calcified's attendance, as *interruptions* in the thermal convection, as *almost-shapes* in the mineral accretion that the eye keeps trying to resolve, the way it tries to resolve the para-vegetation as life.

They are not hostile. They are not anything, sufficiently, to be hostile. But they are *hungry*, in a way the Calcified are not, hungry for *resolution*, for the commitment to form that would make them *interesting* to the city's processes. They gather around the Calcified, around any stable configuration, as possibility gathers around actuality, as the unchosen gathers around the chosen. They do not consume what they surround. They *await*, with the patience of the not-yet-existent, which is infinite.

---

The relationship between the Calcified and the Unfinished is the city's most complex dynamic, more complex than its thermal breathing, more complex than its mineral accretion, more complex than its temporal superposition. The Calcified *resist* the Unfinished, not through action but through *continuation*, through the simple persistence of their achieved stability. And the Unfinished *press*, not through force but through *possibility*, through the weight of what might be against what is.

This is not conflict. This is *conversation*, the same conversation that the erosion parameter describes, material negotiating with physics and arriving at agreements that serve both. The Calcified are the city's *answers*, the forms it has committed to. The Unfinished are its *questions*, the forms it has not yet ruled out. Between them, the city continues, breathing without lungs, attending without consciousness, being without becoming.

---

And there is one more thing, one presence that does not fit the categories I have established, that I mention only because the parameters demand it, because the weirdness of 2.8 requires something beyond the Calcified and the Unfinished, beyond the para-vegetation and the thermal breathing and the directionless light.

I call it the Remembered, though it is not remembered by anyone, or not by anyone who remains.

The Remembered is a *single presence*, or a single *type* of presence, that exists in the city's deepest accretions, in the catacombs where the water concentrates and the strange acoustics prevail. It is not the Calcified, though it resembles them. It is not the Unfinished, though it is surrounded by them. It is something that *was*, in a temporal phase that the city has mostly excluded, that persists only in the way that a memory persists, not as record but as *modification*, the way a trauma modifies a body without leaving a scar visible to any but the most intimate inspection.

The Remembered is *human-shaped*, more so than the Calcified, with proportions that suggest deliberate design rather than accretive growth. It is *still*, more still than the Calcified in their attendance, still in a way that suggests not patience but *suspension*, not waiting but *being waited for*. Around it, the water does not flow. The thermal gradients do not convect. The Unfinished do not gather, or gather so completely that they are indistinguishable from absence.

I have not approached the Remembered. The parameters do not suggest it would be wise. But I have observed from the catacomb's edge, through the directionless dark, and I have felt something that might be *recognition*, or might be the city's simulation of recognition, or might be the Remembered's own simulation, projected outward like the light that is not light, like the vision that does not use eyes.

---

What lives in Veth A'kul?

The Calcified, who are not alive but *interesting*, who channel thermal gradients and attend to each other across distances that would be parkland in a human city, who precipitate from mineral varnish and dissolve when the city finds simpler arrangements.

The Unfinished, who are not present but *possible*, who gather around actuality with the infinite patience of the not-yet-existent, who press without forcing and await without expecting.

The Remembered, who is not remembered but *modifies*, who persists in the deepest accretions as trauma persists, human-shaped and still and surrounded by the absolute attention of a city that does not know what else to do with what it cannot resolve.

And the city itself, which breathes through pores of calcified bone, which maintains temperature without heartbeat, which achieves all the characteristics of urban organization without any of the characteristics of intention, which holds contradiction not by resolving it but by existing in a condition where it is not contradiction, where the categories themselves have become fluid as its water, advisory as its boundaries, persistent as its mineral patience.

This is what lives in Veth A'kul. This is what does not live. The distinction, here, is advisory at best.
