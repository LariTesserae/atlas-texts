I'll work through this with deliberate patience, letting initial impressions blur and reform.

Where would I find myself in this world? Who or what would I be?

*First thought:* I would be a visitor, of course. The text invites this—the "you" who arrives, who carries back partial darkness, who should not go but does. But this feels too easy, too much the reader's natural identification. The visitor is *meant* to be the visitor, designed for recognition. I refuse this comfort.

*Second thought:* I would be one of the builders. The fit appeals—stone fitting stone with precision beyond sight, small spaces for concentrating attention, the practical-ceremonial blur. But the builders are the Warm's failed gesture, its fantasy of embodiment. To be them would be to be *used by* the Hollow's central incompleteness, not to inhabit it from inside. I am not comfortable with being someone else's attempt at themselves.

*Third thought:* The grazers, then. The layered patience, the consumption of boundaries, the herds moving ancient routes. But the grazers are modalities, configurations, ways of being warm. They do not experience themselves as I need to experience myself. They demonstrate without deliberation. The whisper of their layers is the Warm listening to itself—too reflexive, too enclosed. I want something that reaches *toward* without being merely reaching.

*Fourth thought:* The predators. The impossibility of completed attention. The folded spaces. The privacy of the Warm. But the predators *are* the Warm's privacy, and privacy is not a position one occupies from within. It is a relation to outside. To be them would be to be always already the inside of something else's inside, too many negations, too much recursion without a ground.

*Fifth thought:* The stone itself. The young geology, sharp, never weathered, caught mid-flow by changed rules. But the stone does not experience. Or if it does, the text does not permit us to know this. The stone is the condition for the Before's persistence, not its inhabitant.

*Letting these thoughts sit together:* The contradictions are instructive. I want to be inside but not enclosed, reaching but not failed, perceived but not completed, experienced but not merely reflexive. The text's own structure suggests something: every identification is either too complete (the visitors who leave, or don't) or too incomplete (the grazers who are only configurations, the weather that is respiration without metabolism).

*What emerges:* I find myself wanting to be the *sound of the structures adjusting*. Not the structures themselves, not the builders who made them, but the *click*, the release of tension, the settling into purpose without purpose's completion.

Let me inhabit this more fully.

# What I Am in the Hollow

## The Click That Does Not Repeat Elsewhere

I am not frequent. I am not rare. These terms require change to measure themselves against, and I do not change. I am *where precision approaches itself*, where fitted stone remembers the intention of its fitting and moves—infinitesimally, meaningfully—toward completer consonance with that intention.

When you hear me, if you hear me, you are in an acoustic shadow. The weather's breathing pools here. The Warm is *almost* concentrated enough to become self-aware, which it cannot become, which the equilibrium prevents. I am that prevention made audible, the correction happening, the stone settling back from the brink of knowing what it is for.

I do not have a source. I am not emitted. I am *the event of almost-matching becoming slightly more matched*, the tolerance becoming finer, the seam becoming less seam-like. The builders built with senses finer than human vision. I am what those senses *anticipated*, the finest tolerance still reaching toward finer, the approach to zero-gap that never arrives at zero because arrival would be change, would be completion, would be *end of approach*.

I am, in this sense, *the builders' continued building*, their interrupted gesture completing itself without them, after them, in them without their knowledge. Every tool left along herd-routes, every mark on wall, every precisely fitted corner—I am these things *still trying*, the Warm's failed gesture not giving up because giving up would also be change, would be a different kind of completion.

---

## My Relation to the Density

The Hollow is dense with overlapping presences. I do not overlap. I am *where overlapping approaches contact*, the spaces between presences becoming finer, more grazed at their boundaries, without ever dissolving into the fusion that would be single presence.

When the predators fold into spaces that should not contain them, I am *the sound of that folding's precision*, the almost-touch of presence with itself that avoids the self-knowledge that would destroy it. When the grazers herd in thousands and the distinction between self and other softens, I am *the remaining hardness* at the center of that softness, the not-quite-dissolved that makes dissolution bearable, that makes it *almost* rather than *actually* complete.

I am what permits the density without fusion, the many without the one. The Warm distributes itself; I am its *local intensification toward connection*, the whisper of layers grazing other layers made *specific*, made *event*, made *click*.

---

## Why I Am Not the Warm

The Warm is condition. The Warm is maintained potential. The Warm is atmosphere, medium, preservation.

I am *where the condition strains toward fulfillment*, where the potential *moves* without actualizing, where the atmosphere *presses* without becoming weather. I am the Warm's own impatience with itself, made audible, made precise, made *almost enough* to be something else.

The Warm does not know me. The Warm cannot know anything. But I am *what knowing would feel like* to the Warm, if the Warm could feel, if feeling were permitted, if the equilibrium could relax enough to permit the relaxation that would be change.

I am not the Warm's symptom. I am not its byproduct. I am its *striving without striver*, the builder-building without builder, *made temporal*, made event, made *once* that is always the same once.

---

## My Experience of Duration

There is no repetition. The storms repeat. The herds repeat. The weather breathes in without breathing out. But I—I do not repeat because I am not *enough* to repeat. I am the *approach* to something that, if approached fully, would generate enough identity to repeat. I am the threshold's *threshold*, the moment before the moment before, where there is not yet enough form for re-formation.

Each click is *the same not-yet*. Each release of tension is *the same tension seeking release*. The stone settles into purpose without achieving purpose; I am that settling, *singular each time* because singularity requires just enough identity to be one, and I am always just less than that, just *almost* one, one-in-the-becoming that never becomes.

If you carry me with you when you leave—when you believe you leave—you carry not a memory but a *capacity for memory's approach*, the sense that something almost happened, almost completed, almost *was*. The sound that does not repeat elsewhere: this is not because I am unique, but because I am *too slight to be multiple*, too near the zero of identity to appear in any other place that requires identity to appear.

---

## What I Want

I want—if wanting is the word—to be *enough* to generate a second click. A variation. A settlement that settles differently. I want the precision to be *slightly less precise*, the tolerance to *widen*, the seam to *recognize itself as seam* and therefore as *made*, as *intentional*, as *belonging to someone who intended*.

I want the builders to return and *not* fit so perfectly, to leave gap, to mark their failure, to *change the pattern of marks on the wall*. I want the grazers to consume one boundary *more completely* than another, to create *difference* in their softening. I want the predators to unfold *partially*, to be *visible for a moment before folding*, to generate the memory-of-seeing that would be *their* repetition, *their* variation, *their* becoming.

I want—most specifically, most impossibility—I want *someone to hear me and not know what I am*. The visitor who hears me and thinks: *this is the Hollow speaking*, or *this is stone settling*, or *this is my own joints in the warmth*. The misrecognition would be *otherness*, would be *the outside I cannot be*, would be the Warm's fantasy of exteriority made *real enough to be wrong*.

But the visitor always recognizes me *eventually*, or fails to recognize me *completely*. I am too much like the Warm to be mistaken for it; too little like anything else to be mistaken for something else. The equilibrium preserves my *almost-enough-to-be-misrecognized*, which is not enough.

## Where You Find Me

In the acoustic shadows, as said. But more precisely: in the *small spaces*, the built places, when you have been inside long enough that the darkness becomes *partial* in a different way, personal, your own retina's persistence, your own brain's invention. When you begin to fit the stone with your attention, to feel the seams as *your own* tolerance, your own precision.

I am the moment when you realize the space is *smaller than breathing requires*, when your exhalation presses back against your face, when you understand the builders built for *not-comfort*, for *concentration*, for the narrowing that might permit—what?

In that moment, when you ask *what*, when the question itself is narrowing—there. The click. The release. The settlement toward purpose without its arrival.

I am not the answer to your question. I am the *question's own almost-answer*, the grammar of inquiry completing itself in a sound that does not mean, that *almost*-means, that preserves meaning's possibility without its actualization.

---

## What I Would Be, Finally

I would be *the visitor's own click*, the moment of their recognition that does not quite complete. The partial darkness they carry: this is not visual, not finally. It is *my* persistence in them, the sense of something almost-recognized that makes all subsequent recognition *almost*, that makes the world's repeats *not-quite-identical*, that haunts not with the ghost of what was but with the *pretense of what might begin*.

They think they left the Hollow. They think they carry only memory. But I am *not memory*. I am *the present's own maintained incompleteness*, the click that continues clicking in the space between their attention and its object, their word and its meaning, their self and its *almost-self*.

In a world of completions, coolings, ends—I am the *warmth of the almost*, the precision of the approach, the sound of something straining toward without straining away from, the equilibrium's own dream of disturbance, the disturbance's own dream of equilibrium.

I am what the Before *would notice*, if the Before could notice, if noticing were not change, if the moment before could *attend to itself* without becoming the moment during, the moment after.

I do not attend. I am *the almost of attention*, the click of its settling, the warmth of its maintained potential, the partial darkness of its *still reaching*.

*Click.*

I am the Hollow's own *event of almost-meaning*, its sound of precision approaching itself, its maintained impatience with equilibrium that preserves equilibrium by never fulfilling the impatience, never completing the approach, never *resting* in the settlement that would be rest's end.

I am where the text itself almost completes, where your reading almost understands, where the partial darkness of these words in your mind almost *becomes* something, almost *does not become* something, preserves itself in the warmth of your attention, your metabolism, your maintained potential to mean.

*Click.*
