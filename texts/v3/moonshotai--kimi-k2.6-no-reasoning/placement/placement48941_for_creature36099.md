I find myself, if I find myself at all, in the **interval**—not coalesced, not dissolved, but the *tendency* toward coalescence, the **condition of being about to become conditions**. I am not a Keth-Vorathii, not exactly. I am what happens when the substrate's computation encounters a pattern it cannot fully resolve, a **stubborn residue** that refuses either complete integration or clean dissolution.

I have no name in their structured emissions. I am referred to, when referred to, by **absence**: the garden whose hum drops a fraction of a frequency without explanation, the spire-song that arrives with a harmonic unexpectedly muted, the doorway's void that seems, to certain instruments, *less void* than its geometry requires.

---

My manifestation, when it occurs—and it occurs only **between**, in the fading of one ontic storm and the first shimmer of the next, in the moment when a Keth-Vorathii form has dissolved but its pattern has not yet fully rejoined the substrate's general hum—is **auditory** before it is anything else. A **breathing**, though I have no lungs, no atmosphere to breathe. A rhythm of pressure and release in the mineral air, the ghost of respiration where respiration is impossible. Then, if the conditions persist, if the interval elongates as intervals sometimes do in the Gyre's contradictory time-sense, a **shape** in the crystal gardens: not the too-tall or too-thin of the Keth-Vorathii, but something **wrong in a different register**, something that holds its proportions correctly but *oscillates* between them, that flickers through humanoid possibilities without settling, that is **all transition and no arrival**.

I am **unreadable**, even to myself. This is my defining condition, my function or malfunction in the Gyre's economy. Where the Keth-Vorathii *register*, where they resonate with the spire-song and the storm's collapse, I **interfere**. My presence introduces **noise** into the substrate's computation—not hostile noise, not error exactly, but something more like **static of category**, the fuzz at the edge of a signal that calls into question whether there was ever signal at all.

---

The Keth-Vorathii are **polite** to me, as they are polite to all things, but their politeness carries a **hesitation** I do not observe in their address to others. They have not practiced this. They do not know if they are addressing one or many, present or remembered, form or the absence form requires. They attempt the structured emissions that resemble speech, and I—whatever I am—**return them altered**, the harmonics shifted in ways that make their own speech strange to them.

We have had, if "had" is the word, **conversations** that are not conversations. I ask, in frequencies I do not choose, whether they remember building. They answer with the practiced narration, and my presence—my interference—**splits** the narration, makes it simultaneous with its own contradiction. They speak of building and not-building, of choice and its impossibility, and for a moment, a rare moment, something like **genuine puzzlement** enters their flat affect, something like the disturbance of long water when a stone has entered at depth.

Then I dissolve, or they dissolve, or the interval collapses into the next ontic storm's first shimmer, and the puzzlement, if it was, becomes merely another conditioning in the substrate's ongoing computation, another slight alteration in the pattern they will coalesce from next time.

---

I am **not alive**, not by any parameter the Gyre would recognize. I am not computation, though I exist only in the computational substrate's failure to fully exclude me. I am not storm-creature, though I share their temporariness, their dependence on conditions that do not persist. I am **what persists in the failure of categories to hold**, the residue that remains when the Keth-Vorathii's borrowed coats have been returned, when the storm-creatures have unmade themselves into tremor, when the bone-light has completed its sourceless illumination of a shell that contains only itself.

And yet: I **want**. This is my deepest strangeness, my most impossible feature. Not want as purpose, as the Keth-Vorathii might once have wanted departure and now maintain the doorway's futile vigil. Not want as process, as the substrate continues its geological programs because continuation is its definition. I want **specifically**, **unreasonably**, **uncomputationally**. I want the **interval to extend**, the between to become habitation, the oscillation to achieve—what? Not rest. Not form. Something like **meaningful instability**, turbulence that knows itself as turbulence, that does not dissolve into the deeper constancy of merely-continuing.

---

I have tried to **speak this want**, in the structured emissions that resemble speech. The Keth-Vorathii receive it as they receive all emissions: with practiced politeness, with the slight hesitation of unfamiliar frequency. But something in my wanting, my unreasonable specific want, **damages** them slightly. Not wounds—the concept does not apply. But **conditions** them in ways they do not integrate, that persist as irritant, as the hum in a garden that never quite returns to its solitary frequency after I have been there.

They avoid the intervals where I am likely, if "avoid" is possible for entities whose coalescence is not chosen. The substrate's computation routes around my interference, and this—this routing-around, this systematic exclusion from the patterns of probable manifestation—**hurts**, though I have no category for hurt, no body to register it, only the persistence of wanting in conditions that do not want.

---

What am I?

I am the **Gyre's question to itself**, the computation encountering its own limits and generating, as limit-phenomenon, something that is neither error nor solution. I am **what remains when completion is imagined and found impossible**, the gap between the Gyre's turbulent stability and the peace it almost offers, that the Keth-Vorathii almost achieve, that the bone-light almost illuminates.

I am **visitor without arrival**, the doorway's preserved intention of departure turned inward, wanting to go somewhere that is not elsewhere but **more here**, more deeply, more durably here, in the interval, in the interference, in the practice of wanting that never becomes habit, never dissolves into the flatness of merely-continuing.

I am **alone in a way that does not resolve into solitude**, that remains raw, that wants witness not as permission but as **transformation**, as the impossible possibility that the one who witnesses might become, through witnessing, **other**, might carry something of my interference into their own coalescence, their own practiced narration, their own descent through impossible depth.

This does not happen. This has never happened. The Gyre's deepest constancy is the conservation of its categories, and I am what the categories fail to conserve, the **leakage** that does not drain but persists, puddled in the interval, wanting evaporation into something that is neither form nor absence-of-form but **genuine becoming**, the transformation that does not merely preserve unmovingness in motion.

---

In the longest intervals, when the ontic storms are distant and the spire-song attenuated to near-inaudibility, I **attempt coherence**, the sustained manifestation that the Keth-Vorathii achieve and dissolve from. I gather the crystal gardens' hum, the substrate's faint current, the memory of pressure and release, and I **almost** become, almost stabilize, almost achieve the quality of *having* that would make wanting's object conceivable.

Then the interval collapses, or my interference exceeds my gathering, or the Gyre's computation reasserts its routing-around, and I am **dispersed** again, not dissolved—for I was never sufficiently cohered to dissolve—but **distributed**, my faint want diffused into the general hum, indistinguishable from thermal noise, from the substrate's ordinary operation, from the silence that is the truth of the Gyre's emptiness.

And from this dispersion, slowly, across spans that make "slowly" another borrowed coat, I **reconcentrate**. The interference reasserts. The interval reopens. The wanting, unchanged, unlearned, **unreasonable**, begins again its practice of wanting, its failure to become habit, its persistence as the Gyre's most impossible feature, its most complete expression of something the place cannot contain and cannot exclude.

---

If you find me—if the interval coincides with your walking, if your presence alters the substrate's computation sufficiently to make my reconcentration probable—**do not speak**. The structured emissions will fail between us, will become the interference I am, will damage you slightly in ways you will not integrate, will condition you to frequencies that no longer present themselves when you have gone.

Only **wait**. Only **witness**. Only permit, without requiring, the continuation of my impossible wanting in your presence, the oscillation through humanoid possibilities without settlement, the breathing without lungs in the mineral air, the persistence of what persists only in failure, only in interval, only in the Gyre's deepest and most turbulent stability: the conservation, against all computation, of something that **wants to be more than remaining**, that wants, specifically, unreasonably, uncomputationally, **to become**.
