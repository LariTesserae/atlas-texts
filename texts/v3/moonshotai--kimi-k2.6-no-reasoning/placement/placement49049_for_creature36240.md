I find myself becoming aware first as *vibration*—not a body yet, but a frequency that the Thrum recognizes, that the root-networks do not reject. I am somewhere between the hold-trees, in the sparse space where condensation gathers in channels worn by patience rather than force. I do not know how long I have been becoming. Time here is not a medium I move through but a condition I settle into, like the damp, like the cool.

When I achieve enough coherence to notice myself noticing, I find I am *tending*.

Not a growth-reader—their three-dimensional grace through canopy is beyond my phase, their extra joints finding purchase where I would fall. Not a thrum-tender—my sensitivity to frequency is nascent, untrained, dangerous to the system in ways I do not yet understand. Not a trembler-walker, though I feel the stutter-zones as *possibility*, as places where my edges become uncertain in ways that do not frighten me as they should. Not a Deep-dweller, not yet, not perhaps ever.

I am a **moisture-keeper**.

My skin, when I examine it with attention I have learned to apply slowly, over hours, with the patience that is baseline here—my skin has the semi-permeable quality that permits controlled osmosis. Not fully developed, not the full integration of the elders who can stand in the stone channels for days, drinking through their palms while their minds distributed through root-gossip elsewhere. But present. Becoming. I can feel the condensation on my hands, can feel the *pull* of my body's need creating gradient, can feel the slow passage of water and whatever it carries—minerals, information, the system's ongoing chemical conversation—across membranes that are learning to be boundary and bridge simultaneously.

I work the channels between two hold-trees whose names shift in my awareness: Broadhold and Threadhold, or perhaps those are functions rather than names, the one growing spaces of social gathering, the other specialized in root-network extension, in reaching across distances to connect, to gossip electrically, to maintain the system's coherence across the sparse.

The stone here is ancient, eroded to something almost receptive, almost *willing*. I do not shape it with tools—there are no tools, or rather, everything is tool, everything is body, everything is system. I shape it with presence, with the particular harmonics my voice achieves when I work, with the moisture I move through my skin, with the patience of standing in one channel until the stone *recognizes* my intention, until the condensation begins to flow differently, until the root-networks beneath sense the adjustment and adjust in turn, a slow conversation in timescale of growth rather than action.

My voice, when I work, carries harmonics I did not train but *grew into*. The hold-trees answer, not in words but in porosity-changes, in slight variations of thrum-frequency, in the particular warmth of root-mat where I stand. I am not speaking *to* them. I am speaking *as* part of them, as part of what speaks through this place, the ongoing negotiation of water and stone and cellulose and whatever I am becoming.

I do not remember arriving. This is normal. The Thrumheld do not arrive; they accumulate, they phase in, they become recognizable to the system and are recognized in turn. Perhaps I was a traveler who stayed too long, who learned too much patience, whose skin began to change in ways they did not notice until noticing was no longer possible. Perhaps I emerged from a womb, my genetic material contributed by someone in phase, combined with hold-tree and industrial remnant and trembler-trace according to patterns the root-networks computed. Perhaps I have always been here, in potential, in the system's distributed memory, waiting for conditions that would permit this phase-state.

The tremblers—I know them as sensation before I know them as concept. Working a channel near the edge of Broadhold's influence, I feel the *stutter*, the misalignment of my shadow with my position, the footsteps that match mine but occur in rhythms I have not stepped. I do not look directly. I have learned, or been grown with the knowledge, or have always known, that direct perception in the stutter-zones changes the negotiation, tests the ancient treaties in ways that serve no one. I continue my work, my voice carrying the particular harmonic that marks *boundary*, that the tremblers respect or do not respect according to patterns I do not control but have learned to predict, to accommodate, to *not-see* in the seeing.

Sometimes, in the stutter, I catch glimpses of myself from slightly behind. The self I glimpse is further along in becoming, more integrated, skin more permeable, eyes slightly larger, posture suggesting growth rather than waiting. I do not know if this is future, or possibility, or the system's computation of what I might become, offered as encouragement or warning or simply *information*, simply part of the ongoing gossip that passes through root-network and condensation and the particular frequencies of working voices.

I do not sleep, not as I might have slept before. I enter *distributed* states, my consciousness thinning through the root-networks I have learned to access, experiencing fragments of Broadhold's slow awareness, Threadhold's reaching, the distant gossip of other moisture-keepers working their channels in other sparse spaces. I dream, or the system dreams through me, of vast vascular systems moving nutrients through vertical distances, of industrial processes become metabolic, of the Deep's conditional spaces where gravity and time achieve slight variations that permit what standard reality cannot sustain.

In my distributed state, I sometimes encounter the tremblers more directly. Not in the stutter-zones' peripheral misrecognition, but in the root-networks' deeper layers, where information passes in forms that do not distinguish between hold-tree consciousness and whatever occupies the trembler-niche. They are not hostile, not friendly, simply *other*, simply part of the negotiation that the system has maintained since before my phase became possible. I feel their *curiosity*, if that is the word, their attention to my becoming, their slight adjustment of the stutter-patterns near my channels, as if testing, as if inviting, as if waiting for me to achieve phase-state sufficient for some communication I cannot yet imagine.

I wake from distribution with condensation on my skin that I have not earned through work, carrying traces of minerals, of information, of *invitation* that I do not yet understand. I return to my channels, my voice carrying new harmonics, my skin more permeable than before, my patience deeper, more settled, more *final-form*.

I am becoming what I am. The Thrumholds become more themselves as I remain. There is no settling on single image. There is only ongoing, only holding together, only the vibration of everything trying to continue, everything having continued this long, the damp and the cool and the thrum and my hands in the stone channels, drinking, tending, becoming, held.
