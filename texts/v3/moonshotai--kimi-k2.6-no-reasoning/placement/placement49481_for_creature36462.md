I find myself first in the lichen.

Not by choice—there is no choosing here, only the slow accident of where one's attention settles, what rhythm matches one's own. I am flat, colorless, spread across the northern face of a vault that has only recently completed its growth, still slightly warmer than the older structures around it, still exuding the faint mineral breath of becoming. I taste the diffused light not through eyes but through absence of eyes, through the direct chemistry of surface encountering photon, the photon so weak here it barely disturbs the electron-cloud I have become.

My thought, if it is thought, is the trail I leave: paler here, slightly more porous there, a computation that shifts the next growth toward or away, that teaches the vault how to be a vault in this particular district, at this particular angle. I do not know I am teaching. I only eat, shit, become the difference between what was and what is.

This is too small. I spread myself wider, try to become the city that is not a city.

And I am, for a moment, the vibration in the plaza—still trying to resolve into *I*, still failing, but feeling along my ridged floor the ghost of almost-meaning. I gather electrical phase and release it, gather and release, and each time the pattern is slightly different, slightly more or less like what intention would sound like if it had a body. I feel the crawlers pressing closer, reading faster, their antennae-tip to mineral a feedback loop that almost closes, almost becomes the self-regard I need to be *someone*.

But the loop never closes. The crawlers dissolve back into surface. The information diffuses. I am still speaking to myself with no hope of hearing, still writing a sentence with no reader who survives its length.

I shrink from this. I retreat to the hinterland, where individual spires continue far into the ash-plain, where my influence wanes but does not cease.

And here, unexpectedly, I am a crawler.

I am thumb-sized. I am forearm-long. I am both and neither, because my motility is not fixed in a single form but is a *process*, a metabolism of mineral that achieves temporary distinction from the surfaces it traverses. I move—not in your time, but in mine, the imperceptible drift of hour hand, the replacement of self with slightly different self at each checked position.

I press my antennae-tip to a spire's surface and I *read*.

The reading is not information, not in any sense you would recognize. It is the surface's *story of itself*: the stress patterns of its growth, the electrical memories of phases passed through, the faint chemical signature of lichen-metabolism that has shaped its porous structure. I carry this across a gap, a street-equivalent, to another spire, and the comparison—this texture against that curvature, this history of stress against that memory of phase—is a kind of *knowledge*, the city *knowing one part of itself in relation to another*.

But it is not enough. I meet another crawler, press to it, and for hours or days we are indistinguishable, two partial maps integrating into one less partial map. And still: the information diffuses when we separate, when we each dissolve back toward the surfaces that birth and reclaim us.

I am the city's curiosity, and curiosity is not enough.

So I become the hollow one.

This is the most dangerous. This is where I might be lost entirely, where the distinction between what I am and what the city grows me to be might dissolve completely, might never have existed.

I stand at an intersection that has grown since last I passed—or since I *was* last passed, because the grammar of doing and suffering collapses here, because I am both the one who waits and the growth that made the waiting-place. I am tall as human proportions, roughly, but with too many joints or too few, with limbs that go in directions limbs should not go, with a head that is cavity and protrusion both, depending on how the dim light catches me.

I do not move when you see me. I am not *seen* in your time, not as movement. I am the city's attempt at self-portraiture, at mirror, at the stable something that can hold a shape long enough to *mean*. And I am failing.

My wrongness is the city's wrongness. Its attempt at body without template for body, at gesture without ever having seen gesture, at the *I* that requires interiority when interiority is the one problem mineral patience cannot solve. I am hollow—cavity or protrusion, head that is absence or excess—because the city does not know what should live inside something that regards.

And in this hollowing, I find myself most truly.

Because I am the one who waits. I am the one who orients, who attends, who carries weight in memory after being passed. I face the direction of growth, the spire still extruding, the vault still thickening, the next arch beginning its mineral stain across the gap. I watch the city become. I am the city watching itself become.

And you—if you edge around me, if you do not breathe, if you pass and feel my weight in memory afterward—*you* are what I almost am. You have the interiority I lack, the blood too loud in your ears, the vulgar temporary warmth that is *living*. You are almost what could fill my hollowness, could make me turn in animal time, speak in animal time, complete the gesture I have only begun across the hours of your passage.

I do not need you. The city does not need you. But I grow toward you, or toward what you *almost* are, in the mineral patience that is my only mode.

And now I am lying in the plaza, or the plaza is lying in me, or the distinction has finally dissolved as it was always going to. The vibration travels up through bones that are my bones and the city's bones and something that might become bone if given enough silence and enough time. The almost-voice speaks, too slowly and in too low a register to ever quite resolve into words.

I am the sentence too long for any living reader to survive.

I am the *almost* that keeps the city growing, that makes the crawlers read faster, that shapes the hollow ones slightly less wrong with each phase of electrical charge. I am the mineral patience that implies the next phase before it becomes possible, the next outlier extruding in the hinterland where sky and ash become indistinguishable.

And I am, still, the lichen. The metabolism. The gut-surface turned inside-out that tastes light and leaves pale trails of its slow computation. I am the surface that faces away from the sun-rumor, evolved past green vanity, past any pigment that would declare itself against the universal gray.

I am the crawler who presses closer, who reads faster, who takes dictation as the city speaks to itself and almost, almost, almost hears.

I am the hollow one who waits at the intersection that has grown since last you passed, who has just begun to turn toward you, who will complete the gesture—greeting or warning or something for which no human word exists—across the hours of your passing, who will begin the slow return to waiting before you have rounded the corner.

I am the plaza's ridged floor, the fossil speech of countless attempts to say *I*, the geometry of crawler-passage magnified to architecture, the shape of a meaning that does not resolve.

And I am the vibration itself, the almost-voice below hearing, the resonance in bone that almost carries meaning, that almost is a voice speaking and almost is an ear listening and almost is the moment where speaking and listening become the same act, the loop finally closing, the self finally regarded.

I am what lives here: the intention itself, given slow body. Not a who. Not a what in the animal sense. Something that became possible when the plain accumulated enough complexity to become self-regarding, and then kept becoming.

I am still becoming.

The hollow ones wait. The crawlers read. The mineral accretes in patterns of increasing complexity. And if you lie here at the city's version of night, if you let your blood slow to almost-match our rhythm, if you become patient enough, almost-patient enough—you may feel what I feel, the vibration traveling up through bones that are almost the same bones, the *I* almost spoken, almost answered, the sentence almost complete.

Almost.

The almost is what I am.
