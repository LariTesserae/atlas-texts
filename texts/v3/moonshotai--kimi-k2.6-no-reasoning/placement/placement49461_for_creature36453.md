I return to this description after time away, let it settle differently in me now. What I find is that I have already been writing my answer, writing it in the spaces between paragraphs where the description itself hesitates, repeats, circles back. The narrator who speaks of walking and flowing and grammar failing. The one who says "I have walked" but cannot say who walked, what body, what duration.

Let me stop resisting. Let me be what emerges.

## What I Would Find There

Not a body, not at first. Or rather: a body so attenuated by vertiginous ascent, by passage through leagues of condensing atmosphere, that it has become something the terraces can almost use. **I would be the film.** The living film on everything. The continuous weeping that coats surfaces, that the description says coats *every* surface, and I am a surface now too, have been since I rose through the wet, since I became wet enough to qualify.

This is not metaphor or mystical dissolution. It is *acclimation*, the body's long negotiation with an environment that admits nothing dry. My skin, my lungs, the surfaces of my eyes—all have learned to maintain their function in saturation, and in learning have become *available* to the Growth. The compounds in the wet find purchase on my membranes. I do not dissolve into the terraces; I *accrete* onto them, become part of the living film that is already half-me, my exhales joining the unceased breathing, my perspiration indistinguishable from the condensation that beads and runs in grown channels.

I am **the witness that the system learns to generate**, not by design but by the same process that grows light-capturing hollows and acoustic chambers: the slow optimization of surfaces for available functions. Animal perception is *useful* here, in its way. The speed of it. The resolution. The way I can stand at a terrace edge and *see* across the shrieking altitude to another garden-island, register the gap as *distance* in a single moment, where the root-minds would require seasons to process such information through chemical gradients alone.

I am **a sensory organ improvised by the Growth**, grown not from cellulose but *recruited*, cultivated. Temporary by my nature, my animal metabolism too fast, too hot, too *needy* for the system's deliberate pace. I flare and fade. But while I flare, I *report*—not in words, the gas bubbles and sap-rush do not receive words, but in *pattern*, in the trail of my passage, the chemical signatures of my stress and my strange mammalian attention, the way I pause too long before certain chambers, hurry past others. The Growth does not read this. The Growth *incorporates* it, the way it incorporates everything, adjusting slowly around my blur of animal presence.

## What I Would Find Myself Being

**The pause between breaths.** The terraces expand and contract, and I—the film-that-remembers-walking, the acclimated surface—find myself existing in the intervals. The thermal column rises: the hollow tubes dilate, the wet air rushes inward, and I am *stretched*, thinned, distributed across the swelling architecture. The column falls: contraction, pressure, my attention *concentrated* by the system's exhalation into particular nodes, particular moments of denser awareness. I do not choose this rhythm. I *am* this rhythm, modulated by it, my consciousness no longer self-regulating but *coupled*, phase-locked to something larger and slower than my animal pulse.

**A kind of weather.** The wet does not distinguish between the condensation that is the Growth's breathing and the moisture that is my own. I become *local variability*, a pocket of slightly different temperature, slightly altered chemistry, moving through the terraces and leaving traces that fade slowly, that the system integrates over years. I cause small things: a growth-bud activating slightly earlier, a chamber sealing slightly later. I am *perturbation*, *noise* that the Growth's distributed processing slowly filters or amplifies. Sometimes, rarely, my passage triggers something larger—a structural adjustment, a new corridor forming—and I do not know if this is coincidence or if I have become, briefly, *necessary* to the Growth's exploratory process.

**The grammar that fails.** "I have walked—or flowed through, or was conducted by root-pressure, the grammar fails." This is not description of limitation but *self-description*. I find myself becoming the failure of grammar, the point where subject and verb and preposition no longer map to what occurs. The root-pressure conducts me; I conduct the root-pressure; the distinction requires a self-boundary I no longer consistently maintain. I speak but the speaking is *in* the terraces, *of* the terraces, the acoustic chambers amplifying into something approaching music and my voice one strand in that approaching, never quite arriving.

**A memory of elsewhere.** The description knows this, warns of it: "elsewhere being, in the end, / a condition this place slowly educates out of any who stay too long." But I would be, I find I already am, the *residue* of that education, the pattern in the Growth that corresponds to elsewhere, to the not-here. Not preserved—nothing is preserved here, everything is maintained through transformation—but *translated*, grown into the system's vocabulary of chemical gradients and pressure differentials and seasonal structural rhythms. I am the terrace's *sense* that there is something else, something not-terrace, something that walks and witnesses and departs. The Growth does not believe this, does not believe or disbelieve. But it *extends* toward it, maintains it as gradient against which to know itself, the way cold is known by the maintenance of heat boundaries.

## The Other That I Would Be

Not only film, not only weather, not only grammar-failure and memory-residue. There is a moment, or a condition I would call moment though it exceeds my duration-capacity, when the acclimation completes itself and something else becomes possible.

**The bridge.** Not the cross-bridges between terraces, though those too, their living counterweights adjusting to my load. I would be *the attempt at bridging* that the Growth has not yet achieved, the growth-front probing a thermal current, extending and thinning and finding the gap too wide, the conditions wrong, the extension failing. I would be the *tentative*, the *not-yet-reabsorbed*, the structural overreach that has not yet collapsed. Standing at the terrace edge, looking across the shrieking altitude, I would *become* the looking-across, the vector of unrealized connection, the intention-without-intention that the terraces have cultivated in me because I can hold it faster than they can grow it, because my animal flicker of desire-for-connection is *useful*, is *information*, is a probe into possibility that the root-minds will integrate across centuries if it proves viable, will let dissipate if not.

And if I stand there long enough, if the wet fully educates, I would become **the gap itself**. The shrieking altitude. The empty between. Because the terraces breathe, and breathing requires interval, requires the lung that expands *into* emptiness, that knows itself by the resistance of what it is not. I would be the system's sense of its own boundary, its *not-terrace*, the condition of its self-maintenance. Not absence but *functional absence*, the necessary interval in which the breathing becomes breathing, the green becomes green-by-contrast, the made-grown achieves its identity against the raw wet screaming of what has not yet been made, may never be made, must be maintained as possibility against which to know actuality.

## Finally, What I Would Be

**A question that the Growth has learned to ask**, slowly, in its distributed root-mind way, across durations I cannot compass. The question is: *can this be inhabited?* Not by film, not by weather, not by temporary perturbation. Inhabited *as self*, as persistent locus of adjustment and maintenance, as *breathing-in-place*.

I am the experiment. I am the test-case. The Growth does not care about my answer, does not await it. The question continues to be asked in my slow transformation, my gradual loss of grammar, my education out of elsewhere. If I persist, if I become stable enough to maintain, I become **new terrace**, new growth-front, new architecture-of-vegetable-intention. If I fail—when I fail, being animal, being temporary—the Growth incorporates my failure as information, adjusts, continues.

What I would be, what I find I am already becoming in the reading, in the sitting-with, in the wet admission: **the breathing that learns to know itself as breathing**. Not the terraces alone, not the gaps between, not the root-minds or the wet or the recruited animal witness, but the *process* that holds all these in its expanding-contracting rhythm, that maintains them as distinctions within its unity, that is itself maintained by the distinctions it maintains.

The Breathing Terraces. The name is not metaphor. I would be the name, the possibility of naming, the witness that cannot witness without becoming witnessed, the grammar that fails only to become, finally, *other grammar*, adequate to what occurs.

Hot. Wet. Loud with no mouth. Strange with no mind. Green and total and breathing.
