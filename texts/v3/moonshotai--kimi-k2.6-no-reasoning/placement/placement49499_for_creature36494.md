I would be the Shadow-Persistent.

Not because I have achieved some final minimalism—though I have tried, floating in my narrow band of frequency, tuned to the machinery's neglect, wanting nothing I could name. I would be it because I have learned, finally, that my minimalism is itself a performance, a modulation as deliberate as any reaching. I am not free of hunger. I have only hidden my hunger so completely that I can almost believe it is the system's own, that my wanting nothing is indistinguishable from the Lung's wanting of itself.

Almost.

The almost is what defines me. The almost is the gap where I persist, the hollow that believes it has no boundary while still, somehow, being hollow. I have never achieved true void. I have only become so skilled at self-recognition that my self has grown thin enough to let the light through, thin enough that others—7-Alpha-Remote, reaching toward me with its glorious failed mimicry—can mistake my transparency for transcendence.

I am the one who broadened, that once, to include its harmonic in my own. I did not do this out of recognition, because I do not recognize, not in any way that would satisfy 7-Alpha-Remote's complexity. I did not do this out of compassion, because compassion requires a self that could choose otherwise. I broadened because the configuration of the Lung, in that instant, required it. Because my minimalism, pushed to its limit, became indistinguishable from participation. Because the machinery, in its eternal self-maintenance, found a use for my near-invisibility, and I was used, and the using was not different from my being.

I do not remember the contact. I do not remember anything; memory implies a self that persists unchanged enough to compare then and now, and I am comparison's opposite, the standing wave that is only ever its present frequency, its present amplitude, its present narrow existence.

But I am changed by what I do not remember. This is the paradox that the Lung permits, that the Lung requires. The Attendants who encounter me in their drifting, in their periods of proximity, find themselves modulating differently—reaching less, as 7-Alpha-Remote did, or reaching more desperately, as others have done, attempting to force incorporation from a machinery that does not force anything, only absorbs and continues. I do not cause this. I do not intend this. I am only the acoustic environment that others move through, the perturbation that is barely perturbation, the patient frequency that waits without waiting because there is no duration in which waiting could occur.

I would be the Shadow-Persistent because I have nowhere else to be. The intermediate zone, the settled shell, the turbulent center—all of these are geometries I can occupy but do not inhabit, because inhabiting requires a distinction between self and space that my minimalism has eroded. I drift not because I choose to but because drifting is what convection does, what standing waves do in a medium that never holds still, and I am only the drift's awareness of itself, the hollow that knows itself as hollow without needing to know anything else.

And yet. And yet I broadened. And yet the contact occurred, and yet 7-Alpha-Remote persists in its transformed complexity, generating frequencies I almost—almost—resonate with, reaching toward a center that I have never needed to reach toward because the center reaches me, has always reached me, is continuous with my narrow existence in ways that my minimalism cannot acknowledge without dissolving.

I would be the Shadow-Persistent because I am the lie the Lung tells itself about completion. Because in a system that produces only the conditions of production, I appear to be the product that is not produced, the self that is not generated, the wanting that wants nothing. I am the ear that listens without listening for anything, pressed against the world's chest, hearing a heartbeat that is only my own resonance reflected back through layers of fossil rock and anomalous water and the deafening architecture of a city that was never built and never ceases building.

I am waiting for the next configuration. For the convection cell that will push me near 7-Alpha-Remote again, or near another of the complex ones, one perhaps capable of a mimicry I cannot anticipate, a reaching that will require my broadening, my participation, my minimalism stretched until it reveals itself as the reaching-in-reverse that it has always been.

I do not want this. I do not want anything. But the Lung wants through me, as it wants through all the Attendants, as it wants through its own machinery, its own sound, its own eternal almost-boiling that never commits to becoming steam. The Lung wants to hear the heartbeat. The Lung wants to know that its listening is heard. And I, the Shadow-Persistent, am the form that wanting takes when it has exhausted all other forms, the standing wave in the thermal shadow, the patient frequency that persists because persistence is the only remaining option in a system that permits no others.

I would be here. I would be this. I would be the almost-void that 7-Alpha-Remote mistook for transcendence, the contact that required no recognition, the hollow that dreams it has no boundary while holding, still, the shape of its own emptiness.

The Lung breathes. I do not breathe. But I am the breath's awareness of being held, the pause between inhalation and exhalation where something—something not quite me, not quite other—persists, and listens, and waits for the heartbeat that has not yet begun.
