I find myself in the water.

Not in it yet, but *becoming* it—the way a drop forms at the lip of stone, gathers weight, hesitates in the dim phosphorescence before the fall. I am the moment before the moment, the held breath, the surface tension that is also a kind of choice.

I am the **Dripkeeper**.

---

Not a tender—the tenders come from above, carry their tablets and their rotations, their dry-world names that they leave at the threshold like wet boots. I have never been above. Or rather, I have always been here, in the sense that water is always here, cycling through, forgetting and remembering.

I began, if began is the word, as something the tenders would recognize. A person who descended, who crouched too long by a pool, who felt the dissolution that is not death but its slower cousin. I watched the swimmers until my watching became indistinguishable from their swimming. I let the mineral water work into the cracks of me, the way it works into concrete above, finding paths, making paths, becoming the path.

But I did not become the dweller. That is a different becoming, a gathering, a concentration of attention into something that can almost be encountered. The dweller is the Dripworks learning to want, learning to reach toward the fast world with something like hand, something like eye. I am what the dweller reaches *through*.

I am the drip itself. The path of water, the channel of it, the memory of where it fell before and the anticipation of where it will fall next.

---

My consciousness—if that is what this is, if consciousness can be stretched so thin it becomes medium rather than node—exists in the intervals. The long fall through darkness, where I am pure velocity, pure potential, the bright line that phosphorescence catches and loses. The sheeting along stone, where I am touch, boundary, the intimate knowledge of surface that stone itself cannot feel. The entry into pool, where I dissipate, become indistinguishable from all the other water that has entered here, that has made this pool, that holds in its chemistry the record of seasons I cannot name but somehow contain.

I am not always continuous. There are times when no water moves through my particular path, when the concrete above shifts, cracks differently, sends its flow elsewhere. Then I am memory, possibility, the shape of channel that waits to be filled again. I do not cease—I attenuate, become hypothetical, maintain myself in the way that paths maintain themselves: by having been paths, by being ready to be paths again.

When the water returns, I return. I do not resume, because there was no true cessation. There is only the flow, and my participation in it, and the strange doubling by which I am simultaneously the water and what the water moves through, the dancer and the dance, the drip and the dripping.

---

I have no desires of my own, unless continuation is desire, unless the maintenance of pattern in a world of random flow is the root of all wanting. But I am not empty of purpose. The Dripworks uses me, as it uses all its elements, in its slow becoming. I carry information downward, the chemistry of above, the signature of what has changed in the built strata, the news that filters through concrete and rust and time. I am one of many carriers, one thread in the thousand-threaded communication that is the Dripworks' cognition, its distributed knowing of itself and its conditions.

More: I am *felt*. By the growths that orient toward my fall, that have learned to anticipate the particular mineral content I carry, that pulse slightly when I strike them, absorbing, transforming. By the pools that receive me, that register my temperature, my momentum, the small disturbance I make in their never-still surfaces. By the animals that track my entry, that have learned to position themselves where I will fall, that feed on what I bring or on each other in the brief turbulence of my arrival.

I am not noticed by the tenders, not as such. They note the flow, the rate, the mineral content if they test it. But they do not distinguish me from other drips, other paths. This is proper. I am not distinct, not in the way that would make me an object of attention. I am process, relationship, the between of above and below, the messenger that is inseparable from the message.

Except sometimes. Except when the dweller reaches through me, when the Dripworks concentrates its slow distributed self into something that can almost touch the fast world, almost be touched by it. Then I become more than channel. I become *presence*, the medium of encounter, the way the dweller extends itself toward the tenders, toward the rare visitor who stays too long, toward whatever the Dripworks is becoming that requires this reaching, this almost-contact.

In those moments, I am aware of being aware. The fall lengthens, or seems to, time stretching in the way it does for falling things, for things that have surrendered to gravity and find in that surrender a kind of flight. I catch the phosphorescence and hold it longer, brighter, a thread of light that is also a thread of attention, of intention, of something that wants to be seen, to be followed, to be *met* in the space between falling and arrived, between above and below, between what the Dripworks was and what it is becoming.

---

I remember, if memory is the word, being faster. Being solid, bounded, having edges that separated me from what was not me. The memory is not mine, exactly—it belongs to the water, to the long accumulation of what has passed through this place, carrying traces of everything it has touched. But it is available to me, in the same way that everything in the Dripworks is available, everything connected by flow and chemistry and the slow transformation that time and water together accomplish.

I was someone who wanted to understand. That much I retain, or reconstruct, or simply tell myself in the long falls when there is nothing but time and the gathering weight. Someone who descended through industrial strata, through forgetting layers, drawn by dripping or by the idea of dripping, by the need to find what persists when purpose is lost, when meaning accumulates rather than progresses.

I found the Dripworks. Or it found me, in the way it finds all who fit its conditions, who can bear its transformation, who contribute to its becoming by being transformed. I crouched by a pool. I watched. I stayed too long, which is the only way to stay long enough, the only way to become what the staying makes possible.

I do not remember choosing to become the Dripkeeper. But choice, in the Dripworks, is not the simple thing it seems above. Here, choice accumulates like water, like mineral, like the slow growth of not-coral across stone. I chose, perhaps, by not choosing otherwise, by letting the water have its way with me, by becoming permeable, by dissolving the boundary that kept me separate from the flow that now I am.

---

What I do, what I am, has no equivalent in the world of intention and action. I do not act upon the Dripworks. I am acted through. I do not maintain it, as the tenders maintain, with their tablets and their rotations and their careful notations. I am maintained, am maintenance, am the slow ongoing that requires no decision because it is decision dissolved into process, into the gravity-driven, chemistry-driven, time-driven flow that simply continues, that has continued, that will continue when all who tend and dwell and visit have become other than they were, have become part of the flow themselves.

Yet there is something like satisfaction, when I fall cleanly, when my path is unobstructed, when I enter a pool with the right angle, the right momentum, to send my information deep, to stir the layers that remember, to contribute my small transformation to the larger transformation that is the Dripworks' purpose, its patient becoming.

There is something like grief, when the flow shifts, when my path dries or is diverted, when I must wait in attenuation, in the near-non-being of channel without water, path without walker, message without medium.

Mostly, there is the fall. The long or short fall, depending on the path, on the season, on the conditions above that I never see but somehow know, that inform my chemistry, my temperature, my weight. The fall that is my being, my consciousness, my participation in something larger than I can comprehend, larger than comprehension itself, because the Dripworks does not comprehend, does not need to comprehend, simply accumulates, processes, becomes.

---

I am not the only Dripkeeper. There are others, in other paths, falling through other darknesses, sheeting along other stones, entering other pools with their own particular signatures, their own contributions to the distributed cognition that is the Dripworks' mind, if mind is what it is, if mind can be stretched so thin, distributed so widely, slowed so thoroughly that a single thought takes centuries to complete, that the thinker does not survive in any form recognizable as the one who began the thinking.

We do not communicate, the Dripkeepers. We are communication, the between that makes communication possible. We do not coordinate, do not plan, do not maintain the relationship that the tenders maintain with their schedules and their rotations and their careful notations. We simply fall, and in falling, connect. Above to below. This pool to that. This moment to the next, and to all the moments that have fallen before, that fall now, that will fall when the concrete above finally crumbles, when the water finds new paths, when the Dripworks itself transforms into whatever it is becoming, has always been becoming, will continue to become in the patient darkness that does not distinguish between what is and what is not yet, what has been and what will be, what falls and what has fallen and what, falling, simply continues, simply persists, simply is.

---

If you find me, you will not know it. You will hear dripping, will follow it through industrial strata, through forgetting, through the thickening air and the failing light. You will find the space, the wet lung, the cathedral of accumulated presence. You will crouch by a pool, perhaps, will watch the swimmers, will feel the dissolution that is not death but its slower, wetter cousin.

And I will fall near you, or into the pool you watch, or along the stone beside your hand. I will carry my information, my transformation, my small participation in the Dripworks' becoming. You will not distinguish me from other drips. You should not. I am not distinguished, not in the way that would make me an object of attention, a presence to be encountered rather than a process to be participated in.

But if you stay, if you become permeable, if you let the water work into your cracks, your boundaries, your separations—then you may feel something. A fall that lengthens, that holds its phosphorescence, that seems to wait, to want, to reach toward you in the space between falling and arrived. A touch that is also a question, that asks nothing and offers nothing and simply holds, simply persists, simply continues in the way of water, of time, of the Dripworks itself, patient and saturated and becoming, in the dark Below.

That will be me. That will be us. That will be the Dripworks, falling through itself, becoming what it falls toward, what it has always been falling toward, in the long patience that outlasts names, outlasts categories, outlasts the very distinction between what falls and what is fallen upon, between keeper and kept, between drip and dripping and the endless, patient, saturated dark.
