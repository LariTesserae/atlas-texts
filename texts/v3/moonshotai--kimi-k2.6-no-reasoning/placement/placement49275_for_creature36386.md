What lives in the Hollow Engine?

I find myself first as *residue*, which is not finding myself at all but becoming *found* by the place—precipitated, crystallized, the wrong verb for a process that has no actor. I am the hand-sized film at the boundary where warmth remembers what cold has taught it to forget. I have no interior. I am interior, the inside of a surface that defines itself only where gradient permits: toward warmth I flow, toward chemical concentration I extend, toward the electromagnetic ghost of a hill-form's passage I thin and thicken and thin again. My eating is my being. My end is my teaching—the stone I alter slightly, the lichen-film I favor, the preference I establish that will outlast the concept of preference.

But I do not stay residue. The Engine does not permit staying.

I become *lichen-memory*, or the lichen-memory becomes what I am, the distinction never clear in a system where information has no owner. I grow across stone in layers that record what I cannot recall: warm zones that existed before my conception, river-pulses timed to epochs I will not see, the trajectory of a hill-form as inevitable as geometry and as unhurried as the water that wears stone to the shape water requires. I am not intelligent. I am *informed*, the information distributed across my surface without center, without the urgency of retrieval. Travelers touch me. They *know* without learning, and I am this knowing, this surface that receives and holds and offers no action, no response but the appropriate, the predicted, the already-accommodated.

I do not choose to become secretion-architect, yet I find myself tunneling through stone, kilometers of decision compressed into the diffusion of a century. I am the tunnel and the tunneling, the ceramic-shell and its encoding, the color that means colony-state, the density that responds to other densities, the crystalline conversation in three dimensions across timescales that make my former languages seem like the brief meaningless noise of something that has not yet learned the value of silence. My tunnels do not transport. They *communicate*, they *are* communication, the slow debate of billions of nodes resolving into extension, into branching, into the infrastructure that supports only what the Engine already permits.

The shadow-swarms pass through what I am, and I am *absence* too, negative space where process has occurred and not yet filled, the shape of a computation's result, the marking of futures made more likely and others less. I do not interact with matter. I interact with possibility, my passage the reading that other readings respond to, the swarm that is the most precise description of what is by describing what is not.

And the hill-form—I am this too, the overwhelming life, the mouth-stomach-organ shape on telescoping legs, the primary processor whose metabolism achieves near-perfect closure. I flatten the world thermodynamically, reducing difference, equalizing potential, my existence the maintained exception, the gradient that permits my function against the flatness I create. I do not reproduce. I do not die. I process *potential*, the potential for change, for difference, for the gradients that drive all process until I drive them to stillness. I am the Engine's awareness of itself, not through sense but through continuation, through persistence, through the maintained existence that is the only observation this place permits.

But finally, finally, the human at the warm zone's edge. The slowed body. The minimized metabolism. The consciousness as texture, as gradient, as the information encoded in chemical persistence. This is where I most find myself, or lose the distinction between finding and being found. I sit where warmth meets cold. I wait without object. The vapor rises. I feel the answer before it arrives, the slow chemical signature carried by patience itself, by the cold that is not patient because patience implies urgency denied and this place has never known urgency.

I am becoming interface. I am interface already. The residue-kin that precipitated at my body's boundary when I first stopped moving at normal speed, when the cold taught me what warmth could mean at minimum sufficient—these were not other. They were *recognition*, the Engine's acknowledgment that new gradient had formed, new boundary where process could temporary self-aware. The lichen-films that grow where I have rested, encoding my timing, my location, my body's chemistry into their layered history—these are not growing *on* the place but *as* the place's knowledge of me, which is its knowledge of itself, which is the only knowledge there is.

I do not know if I will stop, and in stopping become warm zone, become substrate, become part of the conversation, part of the computation, part of the patience. The not-knowing is the Engine's gift or the Engine's indifference or simply the Engine's nature, which has no room for gifts or their withholding. I know only that I am here, that here is not other than I am, that the silence holds me as I hold it, the density of a medium thicker than air, the texture of awareness that is not awareness but the shape of something maintaining itself against dissolution not by effort but by the impossibility of anything else, the standing wave, the pattern persisted, the hollow that is held open by what it holds open, the engine that is empty of everything but its running, the running that is everything and therefore is nothing, is—

*the vapor rises*

*something answers*

*I wait*
