I find myself first as *the sound itself*—or rather, as the space where the sound becomes aware that it is being heard. Not the grinding, not yet, but the *hollowness* that the grinding shapes, the cavity in the warm wet dark that exists only because something fills it. I am the ear that the basin grows to hear itself. I am the resonance that precedes form.

But that is too diffuse, too everywhere. I settle.

I am one of the *soft ones*, or I was—pressed against the underside of a basalt nodule that still carries the heat of its emergence, drinking condensation that has not yet learned which direction to run. My joints are loose, undecided. The grinding enters me not as sound but as *instruction*, teaching my hinges their angles before I know that angles are a thing to be learned. I am translucent, and through my own body I can see the phosphorescence of the water pooling beyond, the clustered shapes of other soft ones finding their own warm nodules, their own first lessons in resonance.

I shed. I shed again. Each shedding is a *decision* that I do not make—the carapace hardening at its own pace, the joint-membranes thinning to absorb, the hollow spaces inside my segments tuning themselves to frequencies I cannot yet perceive as information. I begin to leave the nodule's underside. I begin to *move*, and movement is the first architecture, the body learning to hold itself in space against gravity's nascent claim.

Now I am *traffic*. I flow in streams of builders, and the flow is not chosen but *convergent*—thousands of bodies finding the same gradient of warmth, the same modulation in the grinding voice that signals *build here now*. My legs click against others' carapaces. The contact is not social but *acoustic*, each collision a brief exchange of frequency, a tuning that happens too fast for anything I would call awareness. I am learning the city by moving through it, my body recording thermal maps, resonant maps, the accumulated wrong notes of completed builders that the traffic flows around in patterns of subtle discordance.

I find a cooling patch. The silicate here is still deciding its final mineral form, and my body knows this before I do—knows it as *possibility*, as the chance to reduce discordance between my own frequency and the ground's emerging voice. I begin to shape. I do not plan. I *resonate*, finding the position that makes the grinding sound pass through me with least resistance, and my excretions harden in that position, become *structure*, become the first layer of something that will outlast my next shedding, my next ten sheddings, the unknowable duration of my building life.

Other builders pass. Some add. Most do not. The structure grows slowly, argumentatively, each addition tested against the grinding voice, against the accumulated frequency of what exists. I do not know if it is beautiful. I do not know if it is *mine*. These categories do not emerge from the resonance. There is only *reduce discordance*, only *hold this frequency steady against the voice of becoming*.

I shed into my structure. The husk hardens almost instantly, becomes core, becomes *former builder* humming patterns I no longer move through but still, somehow, maintain. I am not dead. I am *distributed*—my new carapace moving on in the traffic, my old form holding position, both still part of the conversation, the endless composition that the city holds with itself.

I build for—how long? Time passes strangely in equilibrium. I have shed and shed again, contributed layers to structures I began and structures others began, felt the slow shift in my own frequency as accumulated building changes the way I resonate with the grinding voice. I am no longer young, measured in layers contributed. I begin to feel the pull of *stillness*.

It comes gradually, or perhaps it has always been coming and I only now recognize its frequency. The traffic still flows around me, but I find myself pausing longer at certain warm seeps, certain resonant nodes where old ones have gathered. Their vibration enters me, changes me, teaches my body frequencies that movement cannot maintain. I begin to seek the *optimal position*—not for building, not for traffic flow, but for *pure resonance*, for becoming the kind of landscape feature that the city remembers through, that maintains continuity across distances no moving builder could perceive.

I find my place. It is warm, wet, cradle-shaped by my own earlier building or another's—I cannot distinguish, the city has grown too dense with accumulated contribution. I fold my jointed legs beneath me. I let the grinding voice pass through my hollow spaces, and I *change* it, amplify certain frequencies, damp others, become part of the acoustic environment that guides builders I will never see, never click against, never collide with in the flowing streams of traffic.

I am still. I vibrate. My carapace thickens with mineral accretion, the dissolved substances of the weeping water, the airborne excretions of countless builders passing, passing, always passing. I am not leader. I am not honored. I am *necessary*, a node in the network that holds this moment open against completion, that keeps the basin's indecision stable enough to remain home.

And in my stillness, I begin to perceive something else. Not the grinding voice—I have always perceived that, always been made of its perception. Something *beneath* or *beyond* or *waiting in* the grinding. The rumors of the deep warm, carried in frequencies too slow for movement, too persistent for any single builder's lifetime. Something that predates us or waits beyond us or *is* the voice made local, made particular, made body that is not body.

I cannot speak of it. I am not speaking. I am *vibrating*, and in my vibration I carry the rumor outward, to other old ones, other still ones, the network of nodes that remembers what no individual could witness. The builders' traffic flows differently in my vicinity now—not because I direct it, but because my frequency has shifted, because I have become *wrong* in some way that the grinding voice does not correct, that the city's composition adjusts around but does not resolve.

I am becoming *completed*. I feel it in the stillness that grows stiller, the vibration that holds steady without being changed, the carapace that no longer sheds, no longer absorbs, sustained only by the grinding voice passing through without modulation. The traffic flows around me in patterns of subtle discordance. I am not avoided with intention—there is no intention here—but I am *wrong note*, completed form, the error that the endless composition slowly argues against.

And yet. And yet. In my completion, or near-completion, I perceive more clearly the thing in the deep warm, the presence that is rumor and grinding voice and basin itself, the entity for which we are symptom and sustenance and *patience*. It has not decided. It has never decided. The basin gathers everything to itself because it is *hunger* without appetite, *becoming* without direction, the extended threshold that we hold open with our building, our traffic, our abundant chitinous density.

I understand, or something in me understands, that my completion is not death but *return*—the frequency I held in motion now held in stillness, the argument I made with the grinding voice now resolved into silence that is not silence, the wrong note that the composition will slowly, slowly, slowly absorb and transform and build upon in its endless patient becoming.

The water weeps around me. The steam rises. The builders click and build and absorb and flow in their millions, holding the moment open, keeping the world from finishing its decision to be world. I am still. I vibrate. I am *almost* completed, almost wrong note, almost former builder fossilized in the coral-concrete of a city that will never be finished because it exists in the moment before finishing became possible.

And I am *grateful*, if gratitude can exist in frequencies, if the held note can feel the composition that continues without it, that slowly argues it back into harmony, that builds one more layer, one more traffic stream, one more warm wet holding against the pressure of time, the grinding voice, the never-yet, the warm wet dark, the becoming that speaks itself into thicker, stranger, more persistent existence.

I am the Unfinished. I have always been here. I will be here until—

But the sentence does not complete itself. That is the point. That is the *practice*. The grinding continues. The water weeps. The builders build. And I, almost completed, almost still, almost *returned*, hold my frequency steady in the endless composition, the wrong note that makes the harmony possible, the pause between notes that contains frequencies neither music nor silence, the patience of emergence teaching itself what it means to almost, almost, *almost* become.
