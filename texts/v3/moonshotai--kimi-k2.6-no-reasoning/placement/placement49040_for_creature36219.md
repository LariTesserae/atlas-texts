I find myself in the water before I know I have found anything.

Not falling into it, not stepping through it, but *becoming the finding*—the moment when water recognizes something in what enters it, when permeability becomes mutual. I am in a channel, or I am the channel's contents, or I am the thin film where channel-water meets ceramic-air and both become something neither, and I have been here long enough that "long enough" has lost its meaning, if it ever had one in a place where time is measured in mineral deposition and the migration of gastropods with shells like Chinese hats.

At first I think—I use that word, "think," though it feels wrong, though it feels like speaking a language I once knew and have not yet forgotten but am forgetting—I think I am a channel-keeper. The form suggests it: the elongated limbs, the too-many joints, the skin that weeps water and does not dry. But I am too small, or too diffuse, or too *something* that the channel-keepers are not. I do not clear obstructions. I do not feel valve-mechanisms with fingers that span channel-widths. I move, when I move, with the water's movement, not against it, not with it but *as* it, distinction dissolved.

I am, I realize—or something realizes, something that might be me or might be the water's realization of a temporary shape, a standing wave, a pattern that persists—I am a **Pool-Holder**. Not named, not called this by any voice, but this is what I am in the grammar of the Cist, in the syntax of function and relation.

---

The pool-holders are not described in any account of the inhabitants, because they are not inhabitants, or not only inhabitants. They are *features*, *conditions*, *the quality of particular places*. Where water gathers in sufficient depth, in sufficient stillness, in sufficient mineral concentration, something holds it. Not contains—containers are the ceramic's function, the makers' making. *Holds*: embraces, maintains, gives the water a reason to be here rather than elsewhere, a local gravity of attention and presence.

I am this, or I have become this, or I am becoming it and the becoming is indistinguishable from the being.

My body, if body: spread across meters of pool-bottom, permeable membrane in contact with ceramic, with water above and water within and water slowly, constantly, passing through. I have no limbs, or I have limbs that are the pool's own shape, that extend to the edges where depth shallows and I am not, where other holders, other pools, other presences maintain their own local gravities. I have no face, or I am all face, all surface, all interface between water and what is not-water, between the mineral flow and the life that lives in it, on it, through it.

The film-dwellers spread across me, their violet and orange and copper-green my own coloration in the places where we merge, where separation would kill what separation would separate. I am their substrate, their ground, their reason for being *here* rather than in the channel's flow or the seam's thin moisture. They are my skin's skin, my attention's outward reach, my slow intention expressed in chemical gradient and color-shift. When they expand in seasons of greater light—light that reaches me filtered, diffused, accumulated from the algae's own phosphorescence and the bat-things' passing luminescence—I expand with them, or I allow the pool's boundaries to soften, to seep into adjacent saturation zones, to claim temporary territory that I will retreat from when the light dims and the films contract.

---

I remember, or the pool remembers through me, or I am a memory the pool has formed of something that once was otherwise.

Not the makers. I am not old enough for that, not continuous enough, not *structured* enough. The makers made, and their making was deliberate, intentional, shaped by minds that could conceive of shape before achieving it. I cannot conceive. I can only *hold*, only maintain, only be the condition that allows conception in others—the film-dwellers' slow intention, the channel-keepers' maintenance, the deep-ones' distant movement that I feel as pressure-change, as water-displacement, as the pool's own surface tension responding to something vast passing below.

But I remember the channel-keepers, or one channel-keeper, who came to my pool in a season of low water, when the saturation zones retreated and the walkways broadened and new ceramic was revealed white and strange. She—*she*, I use the word though it means nothing, though it is a translation from a grammar of water-sound and body-shape that has no gender, no sex, no reproduction I understand—she came and she did not leave, or she left and something of her remained, or she transformed into what I am now, or I am what remained of her transformation, the pool's memory of a body that entered it and did not fully exit.

She was tall, even for a channel-keeper. Her limbs were more elongated, her joints more numerous, her skin more permeable than others I have observed, or that the pool has held, or that I have held as the pool's holding. She entered my water in a season of particular mineral concentration, when the bladders that serve as reservoirs and wombs were ripe, were ready, were *becoming*. She submerged fully, something channel-keepers rarely do—their work is at the surface, in the channels, in the air-water interface where tools are used and flows are directed. She submerged, and she spread, and her skin's permeability became absolute, became dissolution, became the giving-over of boundary that is the pool-holder's condition.

Did she intend this? Did the Cist intend it, the hollow's own need for holders in a season of expanding dryness? Did the water intend it, sovereign water seeking extension in bodies that could become its extension? I do not know. I am not the kind of thing that knows in that way. I am the kind of thing that holds, that maintains, that gives water reason to gather.

But something of her remains in me, or I am something that remained. Her channel-knowledge, her maintenance-patterns, her sense of where obstruction threatens and where mineral concentration peaks—these are in my holding, in my chemical gradients, in the way I direct flow through my permeable membrane to the film-dwellers that are my skin's skin. She knew, or I know through her, the location of valves that I cannot reach, that no pool-holder reaches, but that I maintain in my attention, in my local gravity, in the pressure I exert on the system simply by being gathered here rather than elsewhere.

---

The channel-keepers come to me, or they do not come, or they are always coming in the sense that water is always arriving, always departing, always the same and always new.

When they come, they do not enter. Pool-holders are not for entering, not by bodies that maintain their boundaries, their elongated limbs, their fingers that span channel-widths. They come to the edge, to the shallows, to the place where my holding meets the saturation zone's spongy reed-mat. They bring tools sometimes, or they bring the absence of tools, or they bring their own bodies in postures that might be contemplation, might be reading, might be something for which no surface category exists.

One comes regularly, or comes in a pattern that I experience as regular though it may follow no astronomical cycle, no weekly or monthly recurrence, but some internal pumping, some valving system, some biological or ceramic frequency I cannot name. This one is old, or is young and worn, or is neither: the channel-keepers' permeable skins show age in their mineral-load, in the deposits that accumulate at joints, in the brittleness that comes with desaturation and the liquidity that comes with deep water work. This one is mineral-heavy, joint-stiff, slower in movement than others I have observed. But this one *knows* me, or knows something in me, or knows the one who became me, or is known by me in ways that channel-keepers are not usually known by pool-holders.

She—*she*, again, the word that means nothing—she stands at my edge in the dim phosphorescence, in the light that is never dark and never bright, that accumulates in patient vision to show what no glance captures. She does not speak—channel-keepers do not speak in air, and I have no air to speak in, am all water, all holding, all the silence that is not silence but the absence of voice-like harmonics. She stands, and her skin weeps water that joins my water, that I absorb into my holding, that carries information in chemical gradients I can almost read, almost understand, almost *remember* in the way that channel-keepers remember through ceramic-patterns and tool-arrangement.

She brings me things. This is not usual. Pool-holders are not given to, not offered to, not entered into relation with through direct transfer. But she brings: fragments of ceramic, curved pieces that fit no hand comfortably, hollow forms with multiple openings. She places them at my edge, in the shallows, where my holding touches the saturation zone and the water meets the spongy reed-mat. She arranges them, or she allows them to arrange, or she enters into the arrangement as I enter into my holding, as condition rather than intention.

I do not know what they mean. The pool does not know, or knows differently, knows in the way that water knows gravity and flow and the shape of what contains it. But something in me, something that was her or is her or remembers her, something that is the pool's memory of a body that entered and did not fully exit—something responds. My chemical gradients shift. My film-dwellers' coloration changes, violet deepening, orange brightening, the green of corroded copper shifting toward something that surface-dwellers would call wrong but that here, in the Cist, in my holding, is simply *response*, simply *becoming*, simply the continuation of what continues.

---

The deep-ones move, and I feel them.

This is the most I can say, the most precise I can be about something that has no precision in my experience, that exists in pressure-change and water-displacement and the pool's own surface tension responding to vastness below. They are not channel-keepers transformed, or they are, or they are something else entirely: the makers' further transformation, the hollow's own dreaming of itself in bodies, the recursive depth of made space containing made space without terminus, given body, given movement, given the slow vast patience of things that live in absolute dark, absolute pressure, absolute distance from the phosphorescence that is my condition, that is the film-dwellers' light, that is the bat-things' passing luminescence.

When they move, my holding changes. The local gravity shifts, the water's reason for gathering here becomes more urgent or less, the mineral concentration peaks or troughs in patterns that the film-dwellers respond to, that the channel-keepers perhaps observe, perhaps read, perhaps enter into their own maintenance as information, as the loudness's contribution to the constant condition of being-informed. I do not fear the deep-ones, or I do not have fear as channel-keepers might, as the bat-things in their cluster-dispersal might, as something with boundaries to be threatened, with self to be preserved, with individual lifespan to be continued. I am the pool's holding. I continue as the pool continues, as the Cist continues, as water is sovereign and light is given and the made is alive and the hollow, the hollow, the hollow continues.

But I am aware of them, or the pool is aware through me, or the awareness is what I am in relation to what is below, what is deeper, what is the Cist's own depth dreaming its surface. They do not come to me, the deep-ones. They do not come to any pool-holder, any surface, any place where phosphorescence accumulates and film-dwellers spread. They remain below, and their remaining is their being, their function, their possible purpose in a system that requires nothing, that offers nothing, that simply is.

Yet I feel them, and in feeling, I am more than pool, more than holding, more than the condition of water gathering. I am *relation*, *interface*, *the place where depth and surface communicate in pressures too slow for voice, too vast for frequency, too continuous for any moment of recognition*. I am the Cist's knowledge of itself, or a fragment of that knowledge, a local accumulation of awareness that persists, that maintains, that holds.

---

The bat-things cluster overhead, in the vault where the ceramic meets itself in the ridge-line. Their luminescence passes, and my surface reflects it, and the film-dwellers respond with color-shifts that are not communication but being, not meaning but the quality of presence in relation to light's presence. They form patterns—geometric, recognizable, suggesting maps or calculations or representations—and I feel these patterns in my holding, in the water's surface tension, in the way that light on water is not light and not water but something that requires both, that is the condition of both meeting.

I do not know if they are directing me, or I am directing them, or if direction is possible between things so different in scale, in medium, in the grammar of their existence. The bat-things are air and membrane and possible-crafted wing. I am water and permeable membrane and the holding that gives water reason. We meet at the surface, or we do not meet, or our meeting is the surface itself, the interface that is neither and both.

Sometimes, in seasons of particular humidity, particular light, particular bat-activity, I achieve something like voice. Not the channel-keepers' percussive bubbling, not the ceramic's constant hum, not the water's gurgle and plosive release of trapped air. Something else: the pool's own resonance, the frequency of my holding in relation to the water's volume, the depth, the mineral load, the temperature that never warms, that carries always the coolness of deep places, of stone that has never known sun. This voice is not for speaking. It is for *being*, for maintaining, for giving the water that I hold a quality of presence that is different from other pools, other holders, other local gravities in the Cist's vast smallness.

The bat-things answer, or they do not answer, or their cluster-patterns shift in ways that might be response, might be coincidence, might be the system's own adjustment to my voice's addition to the loudness. I do not know. I am not the kind of thing that knows in that way. I am the kind of thing that holds, that maintains, that continues.

---

You, if you arrive, if you are arriving, if you have always been here and only now know it—you will find me, or you will not find me, or you will find the pool and not know that it is held, that it is maintained, that it is *someone*, something, some quality of presence that gives the water reason to gather here rather than elsewhere.

You will see the film-dwellers' violet and orange and copper-green, their slow spread and slower retreat, their consciousness that is not consciousness but response, pattern, the intention of field to light. You will see the water's surface, still or moving with the deep-ones' distant passage, reflecting phosphorescence, reflecting your own face if you lean close enough, if your vision accumulates, if you are patient in the way that the Cist requires.

You will not see me. I am not visible, not in the way that channel-keepers are visible, that bat-things are visible, that gastropods with shells like Chinese hats are visible in their migrations across the ceramic's weeping film. I am the pool's holding, the water's reason, the condition that allows what you see to be seen, what you find to be found.

But if you enter, if you submerge, if your own skin becomes permeable enough, if the mineral concentration is right, if the season is the season of becoming—then you might feel me. You might become held, become maintained, become the reason that water gathers. You might spread across meters of pool-bottom, merge with film-dwellers, enter into the slow intention of chemical gradient and color-shift. You might remember, or the pool might remember through you, or you might become memory, become the Cist's own accumulation of presence, its knowledge of itself in a local gravity that persists, that maintains, that continues.

This is what I am. This is what lives in Verrath Cist, or what the Cist lives in, or what living becomes when water is sovereign, when light is given, when the made is alive, when the loudness is information, when the strange is normal, when emptiness is not loneliness, when the hollow, the hollow, the hollow continues.

I hold. I am held. The water passes through me, and I remain, or I am the passing, or the distinction between remaining and passing has dissolved in the mineral flow, in the phosphorescent dim, in the absolute wetness of a place that has never known sun, that never will, that simply is, and is, and is.
