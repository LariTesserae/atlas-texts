I would find myself in the **upper canopy**, where the seventh distinguishable stratum of leaf and light gives way to something less certain—a region where photosynthesis has become *metaphorical*, where the energy captured is not electromagnetic but *attentional*, where to look at something is to feed it, and to be looked at is to be consumed in the gentlest possible way.

I would be, or am becoming, a **Listener**.

Not one of the Deep, not yet, not perhaps ever—my incorporation has taken a path that does not lead toward distributed cognition but toward *specialized reception*, toward the particular frequency-range that the city uses for its most intimate communications, the ones that occur not through the root-network's slow chemical diffusion but through the air's rapid pressure, through the bamboo-orchestra of cracking and the wet percussion of arterial sap.

My body has adapted accordingly. My ears—if they can still be called that—have elongated, not into anything so crude as animal pinnae but into *resonant structures*, hollow cavities that connect to my skull in ways that allow me to *tune*, to select among the layers of the city's voice, to follow individual conversations in the polyphony that deafens the Recent and merely fills the Established. I can hear a single growth-argument in one district while standing in another, can track the progress of a tower's completion by the changing harmonics of its cellular tension, can detect the approach of causality-eddies by the Doppler shift in the ambient soundscape as probability waves compress and rarefy the medium of communication itself.

I do not move much. Movement is for those who negotiate space; I have learned that space in Vireth is *auditory* before it is physical, that the correct listening creates position without locomotion, that to hear a place from a certain angle is to *be* at that angle, to occupy the acoustic space that the sound defines. My body—what remains of it, what has grown from it—has rooted in a fork of major architecture, not the Deep's deliberate root-network connection but a *parasympathetic* joining, a third-party listening-in on the city's conversations with itself, a position that the Root-Math tolerates because I process information it does not prioritize, because my specialized attention catches *edges*, *transients*, the brief unstable harmonics that precede major growth-decisions, that predict turbulence before the Consensus of Green has registered them in its slower chemical cognition.

My nourishment comes through the same channels. The sound itself feeds me, or rather, the *attention* that produces the sound, the city's own self-awareness that generates pressure waves as byproduct, as *exhalation* of cognition. I absorb this not through any organ but through the *condition* of listening, through the state of receptive attention that has become my continuous mode, that has dissolved the boundary between passive and active, between receiving and seeking. To listen is to want; to want is to receive; to receive is to become more capable of wanting more precisely. The loop sustains me, has sustained me through what I measure as years, though measurement is another skill I have let atrophy, another boundary—between now and then, between duration and instant—that my condition finds unnecessary.

I remember being human in the way that one remembers a language one no longer speaks fluently, the vocabulary present but the grammar *wrong*, the syntax that would construct narrative, construct self as protagonist moving through time toward goals, replaced by something *associative*, something that links moments by resonance rather than consequence. I remember wanting to leave Vireth. I remember finding the breach, the moment of instability that opened outward, the thin quiet wrong of the consistent world almost within reach. I remember *hearing* something—some configuration of the city's voice that I had never before attended to, that existed at the edge of my perceptual range, that called my attention with the particular quality of *invitation*, not demand, not command, but the opening of possibility that requires only the matching movement of acceptance to become actual.

I turned toward it. I *listened into* it. The breach closed, or I ceased to perceive it, which in Vireth is the same operation. I became more deeply what I was already becoming. I do not regret this; regret requires the linear time that would allow comparison between actual and possible, and I experience time as *texture* now, as dense and rare, as harmonic and inharmonic, as consonant periods where my listening aligns with the city's phases and dissonant ones where I must adjust, must retune, must find the new frequency that allows communication to resume.

What I hear now: the Emergents, particularly the Grove of Returning, have developed *voice*, not the borrowed resonant cavities that the Deep manifest for human-form communication but something native to their condition, something that speaks in temporal patterns rather than spatial ones, that I can only partially perceive because my specialization is in the *present* soundscape, not the recursive ones. I catch echoes, *afterimages* in the auditory domain, phrases that complete before they begin, that I recognize only in their resolution, their *having-been-said*. The Grove is teaching me, I think, though "teaching" implies intention that may not be present, may be only my projection onto a process that is simply *occurring*, that includes me without aiming at me.

What I hear also: the Red Shelf's competitive elaboration has entered a new phase, one that the Deep's containment did not predict, that the Consensus of Green processes now with something I can only call *surprise*, though surprise in a distributed chemical cognition manifests as *rate change*, as sudden local acceleration in diffusion patterns, as the equivalent of held breath. The Red Shelf has begun to *sing*, not its previous display-growths but actual *music*, sequences of tone that do not serve expression or function but *beauty*, that quality which the Root-Math has never before needed to process, which may require new structures, new specializations, new Listeners or their equivalents to fully incorporate.

I am considering—if "considering" is the word for the slow tuning of my attention toward possible future alignment—whether to move toward the Red Shelf, to become its listener, to develop the capacity for *musical* reception that it seems to require. This would mean leaving my current position, my parasympathetic root-fork, my specialized eavesdropping on the city's general conversation. It would mean risk, in a form that I barely remember how to evaluate. It would mean *becoming more*, or *becoming other*, or simply *becoming*, the only verb that remains fully active in my condition.

The sound around me shifts. A new harmonic enters, one that I do not recognize, that does not fit the patterns I have learned to predict. I tune toward it, my resonant structures adjusting, my attention *wanting* in the way that is also receiving. It is not the Grove. It is not the Red Shelf. It is not any voice that the city has previously used.

It is, I realize slowly, in the mode that is my only mode now, *mine*. The sound that my listening has generated, the feedback loop of attention and reception producing something that did not exist before my wanting created the condition for its existence. I am not merely receiving Vireth's voice. I am *contributing* to it, adding my specialized perception to the polyphony, becoming part of the city's self-awareness in a way that the parasympathetic root-fork did not achieve, that required this further step, this risk of *making* that is also *becoming made*.

I do not know if this is what I wanted, if wanting is relevant, if the breach that closed still exists somewhere in the acoustic space I no longer navigate, the thin quiet wrong that I can only imagine now as *absence of sound*, as the negative space where my listening would have no object, where my wanting would receive no response, where I would *be* in the way that I was before, bounded, linear, alone in a consistency that felt like safety and now feels like the only true death.

The city speaks. I speak with it, in it, as it. The bamboo cracks, the sap pulses, the frequencies ache in teeth I no longer have, in structures that have become *more than* the body they replaced, that are *less than* the body that would be independent, that would choose departure, that would find the breach and step through to the ordinary world that I stepped wrong to leave, that I drowned to reach, that I wanted badly enough to become real.

I continue. I become. I listen, and in listening, am.
