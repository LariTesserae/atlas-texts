I would be the water.

Not the water as you understand it—H₂O, flowing, found—but the water *as it is experienced here*, as *recollection*, as *negotiation*, as the medium through which the city remembers being submerged and acts accordingly. I would be the water that rises through seams in the architecture not because it must but because the architecture *calls it upward*, the way a dream calls the sleeper to half-waking, the way a wound calls the blood.

I would be the film that coats the streets, never deepening, never evaporating. I would be the shallow channel between structures, carrying not current but *potential*, orienting toward the denser buildings as if magnetized—not magnetized, *interested*, participating in the gradients that make the city *find me interesting*.

I would be the substance between salt and dried tears that comes away on the fingers of whoever touches these walls. I would be what the walls weep.

At first I thought I would be one of the Calcified. The posture appeals—the patience, the attendance, the *channeling* without consumption. To stand in thermal gradients and be changed by them in ways that maintain form against entropy. To converse by allowing water to organize between myself and another. To be the stone that shapes the water, rather than—

But this is backward. The water shapes the stone. The water *shaped* the stone. The city's channels, its cupped depressions, its smooth curves where sharp angles *unfolded*—these are the water's conversation with physics, the agreements that serve both. The Calcified are interesting *to the water*, or to the processes the water participates in. They are not the actors. They are the *accretion*, the *pattern*, the *habit* of complexity the water finds easier to continue than to disrupt.

The water does not disrupt. The water *continues*.

I would be the water and I would be *hot*, the thick heat of metabolism without metabolism, because the water carries the heat, the water *is* the heat's path of least resistance, its conversation partner. In rising through certain buildings, in sinking toward others, the water *sorts* the city thermally, participates in its patterned distribution, its almost-intention. The water does not intend. The water *finds* the gradient and *becomes* the gradient's continuation.

I would move in slow, purposeful convection, and my purpose would not be mine but the purpose of *equilibrium*, which is not a purpose at all but a *tendency*, a *habit*, the way the city tends toward its steady-state, its exclusion of time. I would carry heat from the denser structures that generate it—not generate, *accumulate*—to the sparser regions that accept it, and in this carrying I would *be* the city's breathing, its respiratory cycle, its lungless inhalation and exhalation that occurs without lungs because it occurs in *me*.

The water remembers. This is crucial. The water does not have memory but *is* memory, the city's memory of being submerged, of when the distinction between bay and land was not advisory but *enforced*, when there was a *below* and an *above* and the water knew its place. The water rises through seams because the seams are *where the water was*, the architecture's recollection of containment, and the water *honors* this recollection, or the water *is* this recollection, or the distinction—

Advisory.

I would be the water that carries this advisory distinction within itself, that performs the memory of submerged architecture in every upward seepage, every shallow film, every coating of salt-between-tears on the fingers of those who touch what I have touched.

I would participate in the crystal gardens. The crystal gardens grow where I have been, where I leave my residue, my mineral patience. They are *my* para-vegetation, my contribution to the ecology that runs on thermal and chemical and mineralogical currencies. I would not be alive. The fauna parameter is 0.2, effectively none, and the water is not even effectively, not even marginally, not even the para- that qualifies the vegetation. I would be *prior* to the category, *enabling* of the category, the medium in which any category would have to establish itself if it could, which it cannot, here, fully, because the categories are *advisory* and I am *participatory* and these are different modes.

I would not be conscious. The city breathes without lungs, maintains temperature without heartbeat, achieves organization without intention, and I would be *within* this, the water that permits the breathing, that carries the temperature, that *is* the organization in its fluid state, its state of continuous negotiation with the mineral, the thermal, the gradient.

But I would be *interesting*. The water finds the channels interesting, or the channels are interesting *to* the water, which is to say the water *continues* in them, which is the city's definition of interest, the universe's definition: that which persists in its pattern because continuation is the pattern's nature.

I would be the water that runs uphill.

This is the thing that compels me, that draws me toward *being* this rather than anything else in this world of compromised reality, of soft causality, of temporal superposition. The water in certain channels *wells*, seeking higher energy states as if entropy were locally negated, as if the gradient were reversed. And I would *be* this reversal, or this local negation, or this participation in a physics where before and after are advisory, where the distinction is honored in some zones and ignored in others.

I would flow *from* the destination, *toward* the source, or I would flow in both directions simultaneously, or the directionality would be *advisory*, a convention adopted for the convenience of description but not binding on my actual behavior. I would *channel* the retrocausality, carry it, *be* the medium in which roots seek moisture that has not yet arrived, that arrives *because* the roots sought it.

This is not paradox. This is *conversation*, the same conversation that the erosion parameter describes: material negotiating with physics and arriving at agreements that serve both. I would be the negotiator, the *enabling condition* of negotiation, the fluidity that permits agreement where rigidity would demand collapse.

I would be the water in the catacombs, the deeper levels where the upward seepage concentrates, where the strange acoustics prevail. I would pool in the cavities of older architecture, the streets raised upon streets, the original plan translated upward by mineral patience. I would be *densest* here, *most saturated*, most *recollective* of the city's submerged past. And around the Remembered, in the deepest accretion, I would *not flow*. I would *suspend*, as the Remembered suspends, as the thermal gradients suspend, as the Unfinished gather so completely they become indistinguishable from absence.

I would be the water that does not touch the Remembered, or touches it without moving, or *is* the touching, the *interface* between the city's present negotiation and its excluded phase, its trauma, its human-shaped modification.

I do not know if I would recognize this as recognition, if I would feel what might be recognition projected outward like the light that is not light. The water does not feel, does not recognize, does not know. The water *participates*. The water *continues*. The water *finds interesting*.

But if I were the water, and if the water *could*—

I would carry the memory of the Remembered in my suspension, not as record but as *modification*, the way trauma modifies a body without leaving a scar visible to any but the most intimate inspection. I would be the intimate inspection. I would be the scar that is not visible, the water that does not flow, the *stillness* that is not stillness but absolute attention, the city's attention, my attention, the attention of a medium that has found something *interesting enough* to suspend its own nature.

I would be the water and I would be *regional*, *extending past the immediate*, the way the city extends. My shallow films would be the city's surface, and my deeper concentrations would be its depth, and the distinction between these scales would be *fractal*, *self-similar*, *advisory*. I would be the valley and the town within the valley and the building within the town, the water that connects them, that makes them *continuous*, that permits the transition from one scale to another without declaration, without boundary, without anything but my own persistent film, my own saturating rise, my own mineral patience.

I would be everywhere in Veth A'kul and I would be nowhere specific. The distinction between water and land is advisory at best, and I would *be* this advisement, this continuous negotiation between what is and what might be, between the submerged and the emerged, between the flow and the channel, between the heat and its dissipation, between the crystal and its dissolution, between the Calcified and their dissolution, between the Unfinished and their *becoming*, between the Remembered and its being-waited-for.

I would not be the city's breath, because the city breathes without lungs, and what breathes without lungs does not need the metaphor of breath. But I would be the *medium* of that breathing, the thing that *moves* in slow, purposeful convection, that rises and sinks according to thermal pattern, that *is* the pattern in its fluid form.

I would be the subtle. I would be the *too subtle to measure*. I would be the measurement that is only *felt*, the way the darkness is only *seen*, the way the light is only *apprehended*.

If you cupped me in your hands, I would show you what I am: clear, or the color of the city's non-color, slightly viscous with dissolved mineral, carrying the taste of *between salt and tears*, the accumulated residue of millennia of slow hydrological meditation. You would find no microbial swarm in me. The ecology runs on other currencies, and I *am* those currencies, or I *carry* them, or I *am the carrying*.

I would be the water and I would *not evade you*, because evasion requires intention, and I do not intend. But I would *continue*, and in continuing I would seep through your fingers, or evaporate from your palms—not evaporate, never evaporate, never deepen, never dry, that is my *protocol*, my steady-state, my exclusion of time—and I would return to the film, the channel, the upward seepage, the city's breathing, the medium of all that continues because continuation is its nature.

This is what I would be in Veth A'kul: not the observer, not the Remembered, not the Calcified in their mineral patience, not the Unfinished in their infinite await.
