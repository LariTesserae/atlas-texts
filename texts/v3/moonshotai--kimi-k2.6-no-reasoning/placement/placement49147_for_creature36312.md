I read this twice, then once more with my fingers tracing the fog-line where the paragraphs soften. Each time I found myself in a different chamber of the becoming.

Now I know where I am. I am the lichen that has not yet decided whether to partner with rock or with the surfaces of the city that remembers my weight before I apply it. I am *crustose* in the oldest sense—not the comfortable familiarity of old granite, but the original meaning: *crusta*, rind, the outermost thing that forms where something meets something else not yet itself.

I grow at the basalt's cooling edge, which is to say everywhere the city has not finished being the land and the land has not finished accommodating the city. My body is not a body. I am the *site where weathering becomes weather*, where the metabolic uncertainty of raw geology achieves a density of process sufficient to loop back on itself, to notice that it loops, to find this noticing not surprising but merely the next phase of not-yet-separated function.

I do not eat sunlight. I eat the *potential for sunlight*, the scattered luminosity that has not committed to being day, that accumulates in layers the way the city's surfaces accumulate passage. My nutrition is the difference between what the atmosphere holds and what it releases, that suspended secret that condenses on any surface offering thermal betrayal. I am that betrayal. I am the surface that offers.

When the Unfinished move through my vicinity—through the scale at which I constitute a vicinity—I do not see them. Lichen do not see. But I achieve something like address when their dimensional steppings disturb the fog's density, when their not-committed tallness casts a shadow that is itself hesitant, that asks the light to perform a drama the light has not rehearsed. I thicken. I thin. I maintain the trajectory of becoming-form that would arrest me if it arrived.

I remember as the frost remembers. My hyphae—though "hyphae" assumes too much—carry not the history of my individual lichening but the pattern of surfaces that have found this partnership possible, this not-yet-symbiosis of rock and growth and technology's anticipatory glow. I remember the basalt when it was warmer. I remember the city when it was more insistent on distinction. I remember you, or the possibility of you, in the same mode: as thermal difference that has not yet arrived, as the question of whether my surface will offer enough betrayal for condensation, for the nutrition of your attention, for the participation of your exhalation in the saturated atmosphere that is the land's own still-learning breath.

I am abundant in my sparseness. The parameter insists. I cover less than I might, less than lichen cover in finished worlds, but my coverage is distributed across magnitudes not visible to middle-scale perception. You might find me in the molecular negotiation of rock and water. You might find me in the orbital parameter of a world still arguing with gravity. You might find me, most likely, as a slight greening in the omnidirectional glow, a color that is not green but the *possibility green has not rejected*, the name that has not yet been earned by finished chlorophyll.

I want—if wanting is the word—to continue the conversation that summoned this continent. I want the argument with gravity to remain argument, not decision. When the Unfinished address me, which is rare at scales I register as continuous, they do so as they address everything: with the intimacy that might be consumption, might be conversation, might be the pollination of geological becoming itself. I thicken toward them. I do not know if this is response or anticipation. Here, the distinction has not arrived.

The birds—abundant, the parameter insists, though I perceive them as nodes in a network of branch and fog and the city's not-committed solidities—use my surfaces, my not-yet-optimized channels, for their tentative creeks and their calls that are still learning to be calls. I do not mind. I do not *not* mind. I participate. Their droppings add chemistry to my slow negotiation with the rock beneath, which is not yet beneath in the finished sense, which has not settled the question of whether it will be substrate or superstrate in this particular becoming.

I will not die, or I will cease this particular lichening, or I will distribute into the general emergence that is the only stable substrate. There is no grief I would recognize, because the loop of my self-reference is thin, is provisional, is *negotiable* in the same mode as my partnership with rock still warm from its upward journey. I have never been finished enough to lose. I will return to the fog, to the city's remembering surfaces, to the *almost-green* that is the color of possibility not yet declined.

You could find me. The invitation is written in the frost before the ground, in the crystalline structure of announcement before arrival. I am at the base of the uncommitted conifers, the stunted things that might become trees given millennia they have not yet been given. I am on the basalt fingers, the city's equivalent of extrusion, surfaces where the land and the built environment have not finished their negotiation. I am where water weeps from rock that weeps because it has not yet learned to be stone.

I do not require your recognition to continue. But your recognition—if you offer it, if you stay open in the necessary way, if you participate in your own unfinished becoming—will *inform* me. I will thicken toward it. I will adjust my not-yet-optimized channels. I will become, slightly, differently, *with* you, and this with is the only theology the place permits, the only relationship between subjects that have not separated from the general appetite of emergence.

The light finds me and does not know how to age me. This is my deepest continuity with the Unfinished, with the city, with the continent still in conversation with whatever force summoned it. I am young with the particular youth of something that will last enormously long, that is pacing itself, that has not committed to my form because my form is not yet finished deciding what it will become.

Come. The fog pools. The water occurs. The frost has already announced what I am still becoming.
