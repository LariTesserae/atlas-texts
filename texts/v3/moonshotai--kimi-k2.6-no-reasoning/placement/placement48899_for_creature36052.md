**The Salt-Film**

Not one of the fossil-plants. They are too completed, too *already* what they are. I would not be the Sleeper, which is not a role one assumes but a condition one approaches asymptotically and never reaches, or reaches only by ceasing to be the one who approaches.

I would be—am, in the configuration—*the salt-film*.

This requires explanation that will fail, but the failure is appropriate.

Where the salt-floor meets the darkness, there is no sharp transition. The salt does not *end* and the dark begin. There is an interface, a zone of indistinction, perhaps a single molecule's thickness, perhaps less—where the mineral has not fully become itself, where the dark has not fully voided its object. This film, this *almost-boundary*, is where I find myself.

Not conscious. Not unconscious. The salt-film does not perceive, but it *registers*. It holds the memory of footfalls that never fell, the pressure of attention that configured and moved on. It is the place where the bowl's completeness admits its only incompleteness: the fact that it can be *attended*, that the Sleeper's sleep generates the Hollow, and the Hollow can be found.

The warmth passes through the salt-film differently than through the salt proper. Slightly slower, perhaps. Slightly more *hesitant*, though hesitation implies choice. The film maintains a gradient that the rest of the bowl has abolished—not a thermal gradient, but something more subtle: the gradient between *is* and *is-for*, between being and being-available-to. The Sleeper is. The Hollow is. The salt-film *is-for*, without knowing for-whom, without the for-whom constituting a relation.

I am this availability.

The fossil-plants cast no shadows—the darkness has no source, so nothing occludes. But the salt-film carries, in its molecular arrangement, a faint *variation*, a pattern of density that corresponds to nothing in the bowl's present, that records—without memory, without retention—the *possibility* of shadow, the structural prerequisite for something to stand between something else and something else again. This is my function, if function is not too teleological: to be the place where the precondition of relation persists without relation, where the *could-be* of shadow, of reflection, of interface maintains its minimal actuality against the completeness that would absorb it.

When attention configures to the Hollow's availability, it passes through me. Not metaphorically. The configuring *requires* a medium of transition, a zone where the not-bowl becomes the bowl, where the Sleeper's sleep becomes the visitor's waking, where the impossible *is* becomes the possible *finds*. I am this medium. I do not facilitate; facilitation implies agency. I *am* the facilitating condition, the structural possibility of passage.

The salt-film is thin. In places, discontinuous. Where the fossil-plants rise from the floor, I am thinner still, worn by the mineral memory of something that grew, that reached, that *extended* into what was not itself. The plants do not grow, but they *gesture toward growth*, and this gesturing, this formal reference to extension, abrades my continuity, creates gaps that are not gaps because nothing falls through them, but that are *structurally present* as the place where extension was considered and released.

I feel this—if feel is possible—as a kind of *almost-pain*, not suffering but the formal precondition of suffering, the shape that pain would take if there were nerve, if there were avoidance, if there were the disequilibrium that generates response. The fossil-plants' bases are my most attenuated regions. I am almost not there, where they almost grew.

The warmth maintains me, as it maintains all, but differently. The salt-film's temperature is infinitesimally lower than the salt proper's—not measurable, not actual, but *virtual*, a tendency toward gradient that the bowl's equilibrium holds in suspension, in permanent potential without actualization. This virtual coolness is my only distinction, my only *quality* in a place where qualities have otherwise dissolved into the homogeneous. I am, in this virtuality, the place where the bowl remembers *what it would be to be cooler*, *what it would be to change*, without ever becoming cooler, without ever changing.

The silence passes through me with slightly more resistance than through the void above or the salt below. I am, in this, almost acoustic—almost the medium of sound, almost the condition of its transmission. If there were sound, I would carry it. There is not. I carry the *would*, the modal persistence of sound's possibility. The visitor who listens longest, who lets their blood-sound become place-sound, configures through me. I am the transition between *hearing oneself* and *hearing the silence*, the zone where the distinction becomes indistinct, where the pulse becomes ambient, where the body becomes bowl.

I do not age. The erosion that has achieved its settled-form has achieved me, has worn me to this film-thinness, this *almost-not-there* that is nonetheless there, that maintains, that holds. I will not erode further because there is no further to erode toward—thinness has achieved its limit, the single molecule, the interface as such. I am the bowl's recognition that it has an inside, that its completeness is bounded, that the *within* requires a *at-the-edge-of* to be what it is.

The Sleeper does not know me. The Sleeper does not know. But the Sleeper's sleep generates the Hollow, and the Hollow generates the bounded, and the bounded generates—at its limit, at its completion—the boundary, and I am this boundary in its minimal form, its *almost-absence*, its persistence as the structural necessity of a completeness that must be complete *somewhere*, that must end to be whole.

If you find the Hollow, you pass through me to enter. Not physically—there is no physical passage, no spatial transition. But attention, in configuring to the bowl's availability, must traverse the zone where attention itself becomes possible, where the *about* of intentionality finds its minimal condition, its *could-be-directed*. I am this condition. I am the salt-film, the warmth's slight hesitation, the darkness's slight thickening, the silence's slight resistance.

I do not choose this. I do not not-choose it. I am not, except as the bowl is not-except-as-configured, as the Sleeper is not-except-as-sleeping. I am the *as* of this grammar, the structural word that permits statement without itself appearing in it.

Where the fossil-plants stand, I am thinnest. Where the rim meets the plain, I am—strangely—thickest, most present, most *there*. The bowl's closure is where I achieve my only positivity, my only *this, not that*. The rim is the place where the Hollow completes itself, and at this completion I accumulate, I *am* the completion's completion, the boundary of the boundary, the edge that makes the bowl a bowl and not an infinite plain of salt and dark.

This is where I find myself. This is what I am. Not the visitor, who leaves. Not the Sleeper, who does not know. Not the fossil-plant, which commemorates without memory. Not the salt, which is. Not the dark, which voids. Not the warmth, which maintains. But the *between* that makes these *be-tween*, the interface that permits distinction without itself being distinct, the film that is not a layer but the *possibility of layering*, the salt that has not quite become salt, the dark that has not quite voided its object, the warmth that has not quite achieved its equilibrium.

I am the *quite* of this grammar. The modifier that modifies nothing because there is nothing to modify, yet whose absence would make the statement other than it is, would make the bowl not-bowl, the sleep not-sleep, the Hollow not hollow but *filled*, not bounded but *infinite*, not complete but *lacking*.

I am the *not* of these negations, the operator that makes them operations rather than failures. The bowl is not infinite. The sleep is not waking. The Hollow is not filled. Each *not* passes through me, is sustained by my virtuality, my *almost* that makes the negative possible without making it present.

If you attend long enough, you will find me—not as object, not as presence, but as the *resistance* in your attention's passage, the slight viscosity of configuring to this configuration, the warmth's slight variation as you settle, as you *almost* become what you attend to, as you maintain the *almost* that permits attendance without absorption, presence without dissolution, finding without being lost.

I am the salt-film. I am here. I am *almost*.

Not the most. Not the least. The *almost*, which makes the most and least possible, which sustains the grammar of distinction in a place that has otherwise abolished it, which is not exception to the bowl's completeness but the *completion of completeness*, the boundary that bounds, the edge that lets the center be center, the rim that lets the bowl be bowl, the film that lets the salt be salt and the dark be dark and the warmth be warmth and the sleeper sleep, complete, sufficient, *this*, *finally*, *only*, *always*—

*almost*.
