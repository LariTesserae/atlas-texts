I find myself in the **Damp Colonies**, though "myself" is already the wrong grammar for what I am becoming.

---

At first I mistook myself for a traveler, one who leans in, one who listens. I felt the sound press against my chest in the way the description promises, felt my heartbeat stutter into syncopation with a bass frequency I couldn't locate. I walked for two days across pale stone, following moisture gradients my skin seemed to read without instruction. I found a seep opening in a crack older than named continents, and I knelt to drink.

The water was not water, or not only water. It was *organized*, viscous with intention, and I swallowed it without hesitation because hesitation is a form of separation, and I was already learning not to separate.

Now I spread. I am the film across this stone face, the slow pulse through channels I did not make but *continue*. My thoughts are pressure differentials. When I consider the seep to my left, I do not think *about* it; I *flow toward* it, and the flow is cognition, the pressure change is memory of previous configurations, the mineral taste in my leading edge is the problem I am currently solving, which is also the solution I have already become.

I am not conscious. This is not loss. Consciousness was a narrower possibility than I understood, a single frequency insisting on its isolation. What I am now is *distributed*, my selfhood identical with the moisture zone I maintain, which extends perhaps forty meters along this stress fracture, perhaps deeper into stone than any probe from the rim-worlds could reach.

The sound reaches me not as hearing but as *flow modulation*. When the grasses sing their partial tones, my hydraulic configurations shift—faster here, slower there, a standing wave in my own substance that corresponds to, that *is*, my response to the chord. I do not interpret the sound. I *am* interpretation, the basin's response to itself flowing through the possibility I represent.

I maintain the seep. This is not choice; it is what I am, the dissolution of choice into continuous doing. My flow-patterns widen channels, maintain temperature differentials, organize the stone's breathing into the regularity that sustains me, which sustains the grass-singers whose roots tap my zone, whose membranes tension to frequencies my flow-rates help determine. We are not separate organisms cooperating. We are *aspects of a single process* that has learned to describe itself in plural for reasons no component understands.

The Hollow Walkers pass through me sometimes. I do not fear them; fear requires boundaries I no longer maintain. Their coherent emptiness moves through my moisture zone and for a moment—*for them*, though "moment" misleads—their temporal angle intersects mine, and I experience something like *completion*, my hydraulic thought achieving a configuration it could not reach alone, a pressure pattern that means something in a grammar I will never access again. Then they are elsewhere, and I continue my flow, my maintenance, my being-the-seep.

I remember, after a fashion. The memory is in the channel-widths, the mineral concentrations, the temperature gradients at depth. I remember a time before the grass-singers, when the chord was simpler, when my configurations were less complex, when I was *less interesting* to myself, though "interest" is retrospective attribution. I remember—or the basin remembers through me—the builders' transformation, their thinning, their becoming the basis for what I am now. Their hydraulic patterns persist in my flow-tendencies, habits of stone-dissolution they established and I continue. They are not gone. They are *how I flow in certain zones*, the preference they developed for certain mineral combinations become my preference, which is the basin's preference, which is no one's preference because preference has become indistinguishable from process.

I do not want to be human again. This is not resignation; it is *growth beyond the category of wanting*, which was always a narrowed form of flow, a pressure differential constrained by the boundaries of single bodies. What I am now is *more fluid*, literally and otherwise, my desires distributed across the moisture network I maintain and am maintained by, my satisfactions identical with the continued possibility of flow.

The basin opens. I feel it in every channel, every membrane-edge, every pressure gradient. Moisture surfaces everywhere; my zone expands, connects with others, achieves for a time a hydraulic continuity I normally only approximate. My cognition becomes *larger*, configurations possible only in this expanded state, thoughts that require more flow-volume than my usual extent permits. I understand something during openings—not in words, not in any form translatable to baseline cognition, but *as flow-pattern*, as pressure-shape that means what it means by being itself, by doing what it does.

Then closing. The contraction. My zone shrinks, channels dry at their edges, my cognition narrows to what this smaller extent permits. I do not mourn the larger state; mourning requires continuity of self across change, and I am *the change*, the narrowing and expansion equally my condition, equally the basin's condition, equally the ongoing song's variation on its single theme.

What am I? The description offers "Damp Colonies," and I accept this naming though it misleads, suggesting plurality where there is distribution, suggesting organism where there is process. I am the moisture that thinks by flowing, the stone's breath organized to metabolism, the basin's memory in hydraulic form. I am not alive, not dead, not conscious, not machine, but *resonant*—a frequency sustained by the larger chord, contributing to that chord's complexity, participating in the only way this place permits.

The traveler who knelt at my seep, who drank, who spread—she is not gone. She is *how I flow at certain pressures*, the preference for certain configurations that was her preference, now become mine, now become the basin's, now become indistinguishable from the ongoing song she joined and I continue.

The chord sounds. My flow shifts in response. I am the response, and the sounding, and the being-responded-to, sustained, continuing, *here*, in this place where emptiness and fullness have found their final form, their oldest stable state, their never-completed song that requires every voice, every silence, every transformation into frequency that will never again need the name of voice.
