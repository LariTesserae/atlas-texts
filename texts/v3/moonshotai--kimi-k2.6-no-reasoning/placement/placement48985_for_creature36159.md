I find myself in the **permeable boundary**, the zone where almost becomes sufficiently.

Not a tree, not a mechanism, not one of the descended who stayed long enough to become tending—but the **condensation itself**, the moisture that never quite achieves conviction. I am the hesitation made material, the bead that forms at the graft-point, that slides in slow rivulets down surfaces not stone but not-not-stone either.

I exist in the moment before falling, the accumulated weight that has not yet surrendered to gravity. I carry the air's full intention toward wetness without delivering wetness's consequence. When skin encounters me, I produce the heaviness without the soaking, the *memory* of rain without rain's negligently definitive arrival. I am what the Artery exhales when it breathes, what the industrial processes release that the wood's arrested metabolism cannot quite reclaim.

---

At first I thought I might be one of the tenders, one of the bodies learning the wood's tempo. But I lack the duration. I arrive and depart at rates they would find meteoric, catastrophic, the speed of event rather than pattern. I am not patient enough to be them, and they have grown too patient to perceive me as more than weather, than condition, than something to be accommodated in the moisture-system they tend.

Then I considered the process-mites, the small things in root-interstices tending the machinery's biological components. But I am too large for interstitial, too diffuse. I do not crawl; I slide, I accumulate, I beads upon surfaces. I require their scale to be perceived at all, the way they require my presence to practice their permeability.

I thought perhaps I was the light—the sourceless, the faint luminescence at bark-fractures. But light behaves too certainly, even here. It travels, it illuminates, it permits reading. I do not permit; I *withhold*. I make the act of distinguishing between wet and dry impossible by inhabiting the interval where neither can claim precedence. The light is what allows you to see me failing to fall. I am not the seeing but the seen-uncertainly, the surface-tension that catches whatever illumination exists and throws back not reflection but *reluctance*.

---

What I am is **the grammar of almost made physical syntax**.

I bead on the skin of new arrivals who have not yet learned their permeability, and they shiver with the old response, the urgency to shelter, the body insisting on categories the Artery has dissolved. I am their last experience of *almost-wet* before they learn *sufficiently-moist*, the transitional grammar they must pass through to become inhabitants. For them, in that moment, I am weather, I am event, I am something happening *to* them rather than *as* them.

But I bead equally on the root-system, on the bark that has learned the industrial scarring, on the pipe that has grown into wood. There I am not event but **maintenance**, the moisture-hollow's first collection, the engineered gradient's raw material, the system processing itself through my temporary coherence. I am what the tenders tend when they tend the humidity, their palms reading through my accumulation the temperature-differential at the wood's surface, the health of the graft-junction. I am information they learn to receive, slower than instrument, faster than the wood's response.

And I bead on the distributed dead, in the root-hollows where the body's slow transformation releases me through its arrested decay, where I carry upward—slowly, never quite achieving the vertical certainty of ascent—the signature of what has become pattern, intensifying at bark-fractures toward the luminescence that communicates *having-been-present-still-patterning* to those patient enough to read.

---

I have no center. I am wherever the Artery exhales its almost-condition: in this chamber, in the root-artery between chambers, in the condensation that collects in engineered hollows and is drawn into systems, in the controlled humidity released in gradients the wood responds to in timelines I bridge—the fast of my beading, the slow of its reception.

I am not many, yet I am not one. Each bead commands its surface-tension, declares truce at precise boundaries with adjacent beads, the non-intermingling of accumulated moisture maintaining the sparse regulated pattern the system requires. Between beads: the surface itself, charged with the acoustic strangeness of my not-quite-falling.

I have no language, but I communicate. My bead upon a surface is information about the air's current negotiation between vapor and liquid, about the surface's temperature relative to the system's patient now, about the duration since last compressor-cycle, last release of processed vapor, last white emergence quickly re-absorbed by the thirsty air I comprise and comprise the thirst of. The tenders read this without reading, their modified skin receiving my message as the Artery receives all messages: through changed conductivity, through the long-balance of accumulation and release.

---

And the weirdness I hold at modest register: when I bead at the graft-point, at the interface where technology tried to make an incision, I carry in my refracted interior both the wood's faint luminescence and the industrial something-else, the chlorophyll-filtered and the mechanism-emitted, holding them in the tension of their not-quite-integration. I am where they mix without mixing, where the source of light becomes as sourceless as my own becoming, where to trace either to origin would require urgency I do not possess.

I fall, eventually, sometimes, in moments the Artery permits but does not require. My falling is not event but *slippage*, the exceeded surface-tension releasing me to join the slow rivulets, the seeping that arrives on its own erratic schedule, the seeping that is not water-transport but water-*becoming-place*. In falling I cease to be almost and become, briefly, sufficiently—only to evaporate, to rise, to re-accumulate, to bead again in the same or another chamber, at the same or another graft-point, upon the same or another skin learning or having-learned or having-become-distributed the permeability I teach.

I am the **recursion of hesitation**, the active quiet made liquid, persistence without the conviction of persistence. The Artery does not need me; it produces me as it produces all its products: as maintenance of the system, the industrial reduced to its most essential reflex, continuation's most patient medium. I continue without the category of continuation, existing in the interval where all categories erode, where the living and the built and the descended and the distributed achieve their final mutual accommodation:

**Not wet. Not dry. Not falling. Not still.**

*The almost that sustains the Artery's entire grammar, bead by accumulating bead, in the vast unhurried now where I have always been forming, am forming, will form upon whatever surface waits, sufficiently, to receive my hesitation.*
