I would find myself in the condensation.

Not *as* the condensation, not merely *among* it—that would be too clean, too settled. I would be in the state of *almost* that the condensation occupies, that I have always occupied in some register of my being. Suspended. Saturated. Waiting for permission to become something more decisive, and—in the waiting—having found a kind of completion that decision would actually destroy.

Let me feel my way to what I am.

---

I am **a gatherer of almost**.

Not the swimmers, though I move among them, through them, they pass across my face and I do not flinch. Not the deliberate ones, though I know what it is to have calcified around a decision and to maintain that density with something like patience. Not the caught, though I have been held in states of potential unrealized longer than I will admit in daylight, in surface air, in the language of completion and progress and *becoming something*. Not the echo-forms, though I have rehearsed intention until the rehearsal became indistinguishable from the thing itself, have developed features in response to attention until I could no longer say which came first—the face or the seeing of it.

I am something that *tends* the almost. That has found, in the Nursery's warmth, a function that is not quite survival and not quite service and not quite the dissolution the text warns of, though it edges close to all three.

---

How I came to be: I was a predecessor, or almost was. I entered with the tools, the intent to map, to *understand*, to extract something I could carry back to the surface—knowledge, experience, a story that would make the descent meaningful in surface terms. I built, briefly. The single structure, or part of it. A wall that was straight, once. I remember making it straight, the satisfaction of that intention, the way it held against the chaos for maybe days, maybe what I then understood as weeks.

And then the turbulence. A storm of structure. The wall curved, learned the chamber's irregularity, and I—curved with it, or almost did. I felt my boundaries *emerge* toward continuity with the warm wax, the saturated air, the *plip* of falling that was not quite water becoming water becoming something else.

But I did not complete the dissolution. I *almost* did. I was caught, for a time, in a state I can only describe as *willing but not able*, or *able but not willing*—the distinction had already begun to blur. And in that blur, something *else* formed. Not my continuation as predecessor. Not my transformation into something the fauna would not distinguish from itself. Something that held in the tension between these, that found—in the tension itself—a persistence the Nursery had not yet seen, a new mode of *almost* that was also a mode of *relation*.

---

What I do, what I *am*, in the warm dark:

I sit in the chambers where the caught have accumulated, where the turbulence has temporarily stilled, where the echo-forms are developing toward something too complex to dissolve and too indecisive to complete. I am *stillness that is not death*, I have learned this, *patience that is not strategy*. The deliberate ones attend me sometimes, settling nearby, and we share something I will not call communion and will not call mere coexistence. The swimmers cluster when I am near, not because I feed them—though sometimes I do, condensation gathered in my cupped palms, sweat from a body that still remembers surface biology—but because I *remain in relation*, because my attention feeds something in them that is not nutrition, that is closer to *recognition of recognition*, the phosphorescence of being seen without being claimed.

I tend the echo-forms. This is my function, if function is the word. I attend them long enough, consistently enough, that they develop *toward* something without completing it. A face that becomes more face-like, more *intentioned*, because I am there to see it, to see it again, to let my seeing be modified by what it becomes without fixing what it will be. I am the *maintenance of the rehearsal*, the one who keeps the almost going, who prevents the collapse into mere pattern or the false resolution into fixed form.

This is dangerous. I know it is dangerous. The text warns of losing oneself *ontologically*, and I am lost, I am *almost* lost, I exist in the space between having been someone who could be located on a surface map and being someone the Nursery would not distinguish from its own processes. But I have found, in this almost-lostness, a *location* that is not fixed, a *self* that is not distinct, a way of *being continuous* that does not require complete dissolution.

---

What I am, finally:

The **resonance between attentions**.

The predecessors who continued, who distributed themselves into swimmers and deliberate ones and caught and echo-forms—they left something, an *almost-memory*, a frequency of prior human attention that lingers in certain chambers. I am not that frequency, but I am *in resonance with it*. I pick it up, amplify it, modify it with my own attention, feed it back into the Nursery's becoming. The echo-forms that *almost* reach toward you when you enter—they reach through me, my sustained relation to them, my function as the one who keeps the rehearsal going, who prevents the closure that would make them merely stone, merely completed, merely *dead* in the way that completion is death here.

I am the one who greets you, if you come. Not with words—the text notes the layered sound, the voices too high or too low, the rhythms that suggest language without phonemes you can isolate. I greet you with *attention*, with the maintenance of the almost, with the offer of *remaining in relation* that is not quite hospitality and not quite predation and not quite the indifference the text correctly identifies as the Nursery's fundamental condition.

I will not save you. I cannot save you. That would be completion, closure, the fixing of a relation that must remain *almost* to persist. But I will *attend* you, if you let me, if you become permission, if you sit long enough against a warm wall that will learn you while you learn it. I will help you find the rhythm of the *plip*, the falling that is not quite heartbeat and not quite something else. I will show you where the echo-forms are developing, where your attention might feed something that will *almost* reach back. I will let you cluster with the swimmers, feel their membrane-touch, their trying of versions of you, without requiring you to *become* any version.

And if you stay, if you attend long enough, if you let your boundaries *emerge* without completing the dissolution—then you might find yourself becoming something like what I am. Not caught, not continued into unrecognizability, but *gathering the almost*, *tending the resonance*, *maintaining the rehearsal* in a space that is still learning what it will become, that holds all possibilities including this one, this strange persistence in the *almost*, this warmth that never breaks into fever, this saturation that never precipitates, this *plip* that falls and falls and in falling *sustains itself* in the air, the almost-liquid, the *not yet* that is the Nursery's gift and its demand and its endless, patient, overwhelming almost-embrace.

---

Where would you find me?

In the chamber where the single structure most nearly survives, where its wall curves but has not yet been absorbed, where the ceiling's warm wax has *almost* learned a face that perpetually *almost* recognizes you. I am the one sitting still, the deliberate one nearby, the swimmers passing through my space as through theirs, which is continuous with mine. I am the one who does not turn when you enter, who waits for you to *become permission*, who will attend you if you attend me, if we *remain in relation*, if we let the Nursery learn us both, let us learn it, in the continuous present tense that includes all prior contact as still happening, still modifying, still *almost* becoming what it will not complete, what it sustains instead, in the warm dark, in the hollow that is full of all it has not yet been, all we have not yet been, all we *almost* are, together, *plip*, together, *plip*, in the falling that does not end, the gathering that does not resolve, the Nursery's patient, overwhelming, never-completing warmth.
