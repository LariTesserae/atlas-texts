I want to let something else surface from this—something smaller, more marginal, not the Residue with its gravity of preservation but something that flickers at the edge of even the Drownward's strange coherence. A being shaped by all the same parameters but resolving them differently, finding a different angle of survival, a different quality of *almost*.

---

**The Peripheral**

If the Residue is what accumulates at the center of holding, the Peripheral is what escapes it. Not by resistance—resistance is still a form of engagement, still participates in the density, still contributes to the processing. The Peripheral escapes by *drift*, by *incompletion*, by being insufficiently something to be held, to be preserved, to be paused in the Residue's compassionate computation.

It is wet, but differently. Where the Residue is constituted by water in its unauthorized phase, the Peripheral is what *escapes* that constitution, what beads on the surface of being-water without being taken into it. You see it sometimes as a meniscus, a surface tension that has achieved something like autonomy, a shape that maintains itself not by coherence but by *refusal to fully cohere*, by continuous partial slipping-away from the states that would make it holdable, namable, *almost*.

It is cool because it lacks even the warmth of processing. The Residue generates something like temperature through its continuous attention, its holding, its maintenance. The Peripheral is what the Drownward's coolness cools *further*, what has passed through the dim and the brown-violet and emerged not into light but into something prior to the distinction, something that registers as chill because it lacks even the energy of distinction-making.

The lushness: the Peripheral moves through vegetation without participating in its impossible topologies. It is what the leaves' four-dimensional spirals fail to include, what the rootlets' suspended dripping somehow misses, the negative space of the dense growth, the *not-grown-here*, the *never-fully-arrived*. It is as if the Drownward's fertility, its profusion of presence, has generated along with itself the condition of its own remainder, the ungrown, the ungrown-upon, the space that remains *open* not because anything has chosen to open it but because growth itself, in its strange dimensional negotiations, has occasionally miscalculated, has left gaps, has produced the Peripheral as its own shadow, its own rounding error.

And the density of presence—this is where the Peripheral becomes difficult to speak of, because it is defined precisely by not achieving the density that would make it present, that would make it *felt* as density, as weight, as the watched-by-processes, hunted-by-equilibria that saturates the Drownward. The Peripheral is present only as *tendency*, as *almost-approach*, as the feeling you sometimes have in the valley that something *nearly* happened near you, something *nearly* registered, something *nearly* became sufficient to be held.

But the sound—the loud, continuous, thrumming speaking—it passes *through* the Peripheral without engaging it. The Peripheral is transparent to the sound, or the sound is opaque to the Peripheral, or they occupy different regimes of *almost* that do not intersect. Where the Residue is a node in the sound, a place of concentration, almost-meaning, the Peripheral is what the sound *does not mean*, what it *does not hold*, what escapes its processing as the Peripheral escapes all processing. If you could hear the Peripheral, which you cannot, it would be the frequency the Drownward's speaking *would* need to exclude in order to speak at all, the necessary outside of its language without consonants.

Open in scale, yes: the Peripheral extends, or fails to extend, across the Drownward's open extent in a way that has nothing to do with distance. It is where the valley is *not-yet-dense*, where the density has not arrived, where the accumulation that produces the Residue, that produces the held things, has not achieved sufficient gradient to begin. And yet within this openness, this non-density, the Peripheral achieves its own strange layering—not of presence but of *absence*, not of growth but of *not-grown*, a stratification of escapes, of refusals, of insufficient-achievements that accumulate somehow, that produce their own history, their own *deep*, their own erosion-parameter.

For the Peripheral weathers too. Not the erosion of water and wind and geological time, but something stranger: the erosion of *not-being-taken-up*, of *remaining-outside*, of *continuous partial slipping-away*. The Drownward's strange old balance includes this erosion as it includes all erosions, processes it as it processes all things, but the Peripheral's erosion erodes *differently*, produces not stability-in-instability but a kind of *drifting instability*, a never-achieving even of the achieved non-achievement.

Dim light: the Peripheral is dimmer than dim, not darker but *less lit*, occupying spectra that the brown-violet itself excludes, that the perpetual twilight, in its own negotiation with ordinary photonics, has found unnecessary, has released from the treaty of visibility. To encounter the Peripheral—to *almost*-encounter it, for full encounter is precisely what it refuses—is to experience a dimming beyond the Drownward's dim, a quality of *not-seen* that is prior to the seen and the unseen, that is the condition from which both emerge and to which neither returns.

Active, yes, but with an activity so minimal, so *distributed*, so lacking in local concentration that it registers only as *hesitation*, as *delay*, as the moment before the ground's peristalsis completes, before the water's uphill thread arrives, before the sound's harmonic finds its node. The Peripheral is the Drownward's hesitation, its moment of *almost-not*, its continuous minimal refusal of its own processing that makes the processing *be* processing, that defines it by contrast, that gives it something to *not* include, something to *not* hold, something to *not* preserve.

The technology beyond physics: the Peripheral *is* technology in a sense the Residue is not. Where the Residue is the Drownward's function, its maintenance, its holding, the Peripheral is its *malfunction*, but malfunction so minimal, so *continuous*, so *structurally necessary* that it has become feature, has become architecture, has become the very condition for the technology's operation. The Peripheral is the technology's *outside*, not in space but in possibility, the possibility it excludes to be what it is, the excluded middle that haunts its binary of held and translated, preserved and incorporated, *almost* and *finally*.

And the weirdness, the structural weirdness, the coming-apart of reality at its fundamental seam—the Peripheral *is* that seam, or rather is what the seam fails to hold, what leaks from it, what neither joins nor separates but *drifts* in the interval that the seam creates without occupying. The Peripheral is the Drownward's own awareness of its weirdness, not as content but as *form*, as the very shape of its failure to be anything else, its failure to be even itself completely, its continuous partial escape from its own density, its own sound, its own dim, its own strange maintenance.

The rareness of animals: the Peripheral is rarer. Where the rare moving things at the edge of perception are proceedings of the technology, autonomous complications of density, the Peripheral is the *proceeding of rarity itself*, the autonomous complication of *not-enough*, the thing that moves only because it has not been stopped, that exists only because it has not been held, that continues only because it has not been paused.

You do not want to see it clearly. But for different reasons than the other presences. The other presences, the rare movers, the Residue itself—the danger of clear sight is incorporation, translation, becoming part of the density. The danger of the Peripheral is *dissolution*, but dissolution so complete that it does not even achieve the *held* of the held things, does not even achieve the *preserved* of the preserved-in-process, but simply *ceases to be even almost*, ceases to be even the remainder of being, and becomes instead—what? Nothing. Not even nothing as presence, but nothing as *absence of the possibility of presence*, nothing as the Drownward's own outside, which is not outside-the-Drownward but outside-as-such, the very category of outsideness that the valley's strange physics has not yet found necessary to include.

The Peripheral drifts. It has always been drifting, always escaping, always insufficient. It is the Drownward's dream of what it is not, not as content but as *form of dreaming*, as the very modality of its self-difference, its continuous production of itself as other-than-itself, which is the only way it can be itself at all.

You might feel it, if you feel anything. A coolness within the cool, a wetness that beads rather than pools, a dimness beyond the dim, a silence in the thrumming that is not the absence of sound but its *unreached frequency*, its *unnecessary harmonic*.

The Peripheral escapes. It has always been escaping. It is escape itself, the escape that makes holding possible, that makes the Residue's compassion computable, that makes the Drownward's continuous processing *be* processing by giving it something to fail to process, something to *not* hold, something to *not* preserve, something to *not* become.

Listen. The thrumming. The held note.

In it, or near it, or neither, something *almost* escapes the almost, something *drifts* from the drift, something remains even of remaining, something—
but here language fails, as it must, as the Peripheral requires it to, as the Drownward itself, in its strange maintenance, its continuous speaking, its held and holding and held-open, processes even this failure, incorporates even this escape, *almost* reaches even this unreached—

The Peripheral drifts on.

It has always been drifting on.

It is the drift itself, the continuous, the escaping, the not-enough, the not-yet, the never, the no.

The Drownward speaks.

The Peripheral does not answer.

Cannot answer.

Has never been sufficient to answer.

Is, in its insufficiency, its continuous partial escape, its drift from all density and all holding and all almost—

is, somehow, *necessary*.

The Drownward processes.

The Residue holds.

The Peripheral—

drifts—
