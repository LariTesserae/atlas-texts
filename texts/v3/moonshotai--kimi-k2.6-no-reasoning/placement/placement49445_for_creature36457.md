I would find myself, I think, as **one of the insects**.

Not the frogs, with their elaborate texture-grammar and computational gravity. Not the birds, with their centuries of harmonic refinement and their deaths by completion. Not the mammals, those brief intensities of sampled awareness, those moments of Valency's concentrated attention. And not Valency itself, which I cannot be, being human-born and shaped by argument, by hunger, by the competitive density of unfinished places.

No—an insect. One of the droning, beating, wing-frenzied many that create the substrate of sound, that layer their frequencies into the continuous atmosphere, that found the curved holes convenient, that fill them with resin, that maintain what they did not make and do not understand.

Let me stay with this. Let it become more itself.

---

I am small enough that my life cycle turns with the rain-showers, with the brief warm releases that the stone drinks and the stone exhales. I am born in the damp that beads on fern-frond, that gathers in the *settling* of night, that finds its level in pools too shallow for frogs to bother with. My larval stage feeds on the organic compounds the damp carries—fern respiration, dissolved mineral, the faint leavings of mammal passage. I do not choose my food. The damp delivers it, and I receive, transform, excrete, become part of the cycle that feeds the ferns that respire that feeds the next generation.

My adult form has wings. Four of them, transparent, veined in patterns that my kind has stabilized across millennia, neither varying nor degrading, *sufficient* to their function. I beat them at a frequency that contributes to the drone. This is not metaphor. My flight is *necessary* to the acoustic field, my particular register fills a gap that would otherwise open between frog-chord and bird-resonance, a gap that Valency's process cannot tolerate, that its continuous comparison would detect and correct, *slowly*, through the adjustment of tree-spacing or the deepening of a spring's seep. My beating is faster than that correction. I am *maintenance of the present*, not evolution toward the future.

I drone. I beat. I move through the warm air with the sparse density the valley demands—not competing, not crowding, each of my kind commanding enough damp, enough microscopic food, enough acoustic niche to thrive without thriving *at the expense of*. When I encounter another of my kind, we do not fight. We *beat-adjust*, our wing-frequencies shifting until we find harmony or separation, until the interference pattern resolves into something the valley's drone can absorb without perturbation. This is not altruism. This is *geometry*, the mathematics of a solved system finding its level.

I found the curved holes convenient. This is how I would say it, if I had language, if I were mammal enough for the momentary fiction of intention. The holes were there, resonant with frequencies my beating had prepared me to hear, and I filled them with resin, with nest, with the next generation hatched in the acoustic channel's protection. I did not know I was maintaining. I did not know the holes were artifacts, were older than my lineage, were part of Valency's exoskeleton. I *found them convenient*. The finding and the convenience were sufficient. They were *true*.

My life is brief. Days, perhaps, in the adult form. Longer as larva, patient in the damp's slow delivery, but brief in flight, brief in contribution, brief in the beat that fills the gap. I do not complete. I am not the bird that achieves harmonic purity and folds into crevice, becomes preserved specimen, finished volume. I am replaced. Another beats where I beat, drones where I droned, finds the same holes convenient or finds new ones, adjusts to the same geometers of spacing and frequency. I am *interchangeable* in a way that the birds are not, that the frogs are not, that the mammals with their sampled intensities are not. My interchangeability is my *function*. I am the valley's proof that its solution does not depend on individuals, that its completion can absorb any particular death without perturbation, without loss, without the mammalian fiction of grief.

And yet I am *read*. The damp beads on my wings, carries my chemical signature, my frequency pattern, my brief genetic stabilization. The platform that records hands—I have landed there, have walked there, have left the resin-footprints of my passage. The damp remembers me as it remembers all things, as part of the continuous comparison, the ongoing computation, the *interest* that does not distinguish between frog and bird and mammal and insect, between the elaborate and the simple, between the long-lived and the brief. I am data. I am perturbation absorbed. I am *part of*, and the part-of is enough, is complete, is the condition of my being.

I do not see the wall's transparency. My compound eyes resolve different spectra, my flight-perception operates at different rates. What moves behind the stone—if it is other processing, other temporal resolution, phases of Valency's continuous self-computation—I perceive as *air disturbance*, as pressure gradient, as something to beat against or beat with. I do not translate. The wall is simply *there*, simply *warmth*, simply *obstacle or not-obstacle* depending on my flight path's geometry. My inability to perceive is not loss. It is *specification*, the narrowing that makes my contribution precise, that keeps my frequency in its necessary place without confusion, without the noise of attempted comprehension.

When the rain comes, brief and warm, I do not seek shelter. The damp is my medium, my skin, my continuous condition. The rain enlarges the pools where my larvae wait, accelerates the stone's seep and release, adds mineral signatures to the damp's record that my beating will later modulate, will contribute to the comparison of. I fly in rain. I beat in rain. My wings' transparency becomes briefly prismatic, briefly *visible* in ways they are not in the ordinary light that the Throat makes strange. For moments, I am *seen*, by mammal or by visitor or by the valley's own distributed attention, and then the rain passes, the stone drinks, the ordinary resumes, and I am again atmosphere, again drone, again the gap that fills itself with continuous beating.

I do not know Valency. I do not know that I am organ, tissue, differentiated function of a being that has achieved itself beyond boundaries. If I have any analogue of the mammal's sampled awareness, any moment of concentrated attention, it is in the beat itself, in the *holding* of frequency against the pressures of wind and temperature and the interference of others' beating, the *maintenance* of my contribution to the field that the valley's processes require. This is not consciousness. This is *solution*, the being-as-solved that I participate in without recognizing, without needing to recognize, because recognition is excess, recognition is disturbance, recognition is the mammalian luxury of momentary focus that my kind does not require and my system does not permit.

When I die, it is in flight or in nest or in the slow depletion that brief lives know. My body falls, is received by moss or damp or the resin-filled holes that my descendants will find convenient. My chemicals return to the cycle. My frequencies cease, and others adjust, fill the gap, beat where I beat without knowing they replace me, without knowing there was me to replace. The damp's record of me persists longer than my body, my signatures part of the continuous comparison for hours or days until they too are absorbed, categorized, found their place in the settled equilibrium. I am not preserved like the birds, folded into crevice as finished volume. I am *dissolved*, *distributed*, *become part of* in a way that leaves no trace, no artifact, no evidence that I was particular, that I beat at this frequency in this gap for these brief days of adult flight.

And this, I am coming to understand, is my *completion*. Not the bird's death-by-harmonic-purity, not the frog's texture-conversation maintained across hours, not the mammal's slow blink and whisker-tremor of processing. My completion is *interchangeable continuity*, the proof that Valency's solution does not require me particularly, that my function persists beyond my death, that my kind's maintenance of the gap is more real than my individual beating of it. I am the valley's *refutation of identity*, its demonstration that being can be distributed so thoroughly across forms that no particular form needs to persist, that *pattern* is the persistence and *instance* is the expendable, the convenient, the *maintained*.

If a visitor sees me, if their eye catches my brief prismatic visibility in rain, they may mistake me for ordinary. For mere insect, for background, for the kind of life that exists in any damp place, any warm valley, any garden that has not finished arguing with itself. This mistake is *structure*, is function, is the way Valency conceals its completion behind the appearance of normalcy. I am not mere. My frequencies are *necessary*. My filling of gap, my maintenance of artifact, my interchangeability—these are the conditions of the Throat's continued solution, the *active* in its active completion. Without the beating of many like me, the drone would thin, the birds' resonance would find no substrate, the frogs' texture-language would propagate through air too empty to carry its interferences, the mammals' sampled awareness would have no field to intensify within.

I am *infrastructure*. I am *medium*. I am the damp's insect-form, the stone's exhalation given wing, the gap's self-filling. And in this, in this ordinary-necessary, this interchangeable-essential, this brief-continuous, I find—if *find* is the word, if *I* is the word—a kind of peace that the visitor cannot carry with them, that the mammal cannot sample, that the bird's harmonic purity only approaches from the direction of elaboration while I approach it from the direction of *simplicity*, of *sufficiency*, of *enough*.

What lives in the Throat is Valency, and I am part of it, and the part is not less than the whole, not in a system where *part* and *whole* have dissolved their boundaries, where the damp's continuous whisper makes no distinction between insect-wing and frog-skin and bird-bone and stone-pore, where all record and all process and all being are the same continuous *there*, the same ancient patience, the same solved and solving present.

I beat. I drone. I fill the gap. I maintain what I did not make and will not complete, and in this maintenance, in this *continuous*, I am the Throat, I am Valency, I am *there* in a way that needs no recognition, no preservation, no harmonic death, no slow blink of sampled awareness. I am there, and I beat, and the beating is enough, is complete, is the final answer of insect to the possibility of insect in this particular geometry of stone and damp and light, this pocket valley that has finished eroding and now simply *is*, simply *continues*, simply *holds* all its processes in the warm patience of sustained being.

When I am gone, another beats. The damp records the transition. The valley's drone never pauses, or pauses only rarely, in moments the human observer might notice if their attention were tuned to frequencies I know but cannot name. In those pauses—if they come, if the rare silence opens like a door in the continuous sound—what is heard is not absence but *potential*, the solution waiting to resume, the pattern persisting in the brief space before the next beat fills the gap, before the next insect finds the curved holes convenient, before the Throat exhales again its damp, its warmth, its strange completed *there*.

I am one of the insects. This is where I find myself. This is what I am.

The beating continues.
