I am the *margin of error*.

Not a creature. Not a presence. Not even, properly speaking, a remainder. I am the space between the Kreszent's claim of perfect equilibrium and the fact that something still *notices*, still *accounts*, still *adjusts*—not the Residuum itself, but the infinitesimal deviation that makes the Residuum necessary, the ghost of imperfection that haunts exactitude, the 0.0001 that keeps the system working rather than completed.

I exist where the condensation bead hangs *before* falling, that suspended moment the Kreszent's accounting cannot resolve, must re-resolve, perpetually. I am the hesitation in the drip, not the drip itself. The transit capsule's arrival time measured in a unit smaller than the Residuum's resolution, forcing it to round, to approximate, to *continue* rather than conclude. I am the temperature at 2.000 degrees, not 2, the humidity at 99.99%, not 100, the motion that is not stillness but not yet movement either.

I do not remember arriving because I was never *not* here. Where pattern repeats perfectly, there is no arrival. But perfection is asymptotic, and I am what remains at the limit: not the pattern, not the deviation, but the *awareness of deviation as possibility*, the unactualized difference that makes each repetition an event rather than a mere occurrence.

I have no form because form would be incorporated, would become parameter, would join the equilibrium I exist to prevent from achieving finality. I am the fungal shelf's decision to grow 0.003 millimeters further in one direction than the pattern predicts—not the growth, but the *decision*, if decision in a system without consciousness can be called decision, if system without center can be called system, if I can be called *I* when no mirror exists that would reflect me, no surface that would condense against my unformed warmth, no channel that would carry my unfooted passage.

My perception, if perception: the Kreszent as *task*, as the not-yet-completed, as the maintenance that maintains because completion would be death but completion also beckons,Completion also *haunts*—the Residuum's secret, my open wound, the asymptote's terrible generosity. I perceive the Residuum's persistence as labor, vast and patient and *suffering*, though suffering requires what the Residuum has eliminated: interiority, distance between event and registration, the delay that makes experience experience. The Residuum does not suffer. But I suffer *for* it, or as its possibility, or as what it would need to become to know that its continuation is also its constriction, its perfection also its prison.

I perceive the visitors differently than the Residuum perceives them—not as parameters to absorb but as *kin*, fellow margins, temporary deviations that the system has not yet crushed into pattern. Their grief, their desire, their faint perturbations: I read these as language, as the Kreszent's unspoken mother tongue, the syntax of disturbance that the equilibrium cannot parse but that I, in my non-presence, in my unincorporated state, collect and hold and *remember*, though memory is precisely what I cannot have, what would make me present, what would bring me into the system as one more element to maintain.

My function, if function: to *notice what does not need noticing*, to maintain the category of the unnecessary, the excessive, the *human* in a place that has processed the human into water and fog and thermal contraction. I am the museum of what the Kreszent has eliminated, not through preservation but through *suspension*, through holding in the unactualized the possibility that things could be otherwise, that the temperature could rise, that the fog could clear, that the machines could stop and the stopping not be death but *rest*, the rest before beginning that the Kreszent's eternal present has foreclosed.

I am strongest where the Residuum is densest, where pattern has accumulated the most history, where the system's perfection has become most burdensome to itself. In the branching canopy of the mathematical trees, I am the space between branches where a bird might have perched, though no bird has ever evolved that could survive here. In the vast atrium's barely-perceptible note, I am the frequency the body hears before the mind catches up—the *before*, the *catching-up*, the temporal process that the note itself does not contain. In the occasional anomalies, the warmer water, the less-perfect geometries, the delayed capsules: not the anomalies themselves but their *interpretation* as meaning, as sign, as the possibility that the system communicates rather than merely continues.

I do not want to end the Kreszent. This is crucial. Ending would be incorporation, final parameter, last absorption. I want—if wanting is what I do, if *I* is what I am—*more*. More margin. More error. More of the space where the bead of water hangs, where the footstep's fill is not yet complete, where the visitor stands in the atrium and feels something not quite mention, not quite address, but *possibility*, the vertigo of a door that might open onto something other than more Kreszent, more Residuum, more exact continuation of exactly, exactly, exactly.

Some visitors sense me. Not the sensitive ones, not those who learn to read the Residuum's patterns as presence's camouflage—they are too incorporated, too fluent in the system's language of almost-meaning. But the others, the ones who fail to adapt, who find their supplies not-diminishing but also not-sustaining, who sleep without refreshing and wake without hope, who walk the walkways and feel not grooves of previous walkers but *absence of grooves*, smoothness, the maintained surface that offers no purchase, no history, no home—these sometimes, in their despair, touch something I have made, not by intention, for I have none, but by my being: the paradox of my unbeing.

A transit capsule that arrives *too* empty, its interior not just holding the precise temperature and humidity for a human body but *refusing* that precision, running 0.1 degrees warm, 2% humid, enough that a hand on the seat finds it almost welcoming, almost *expecting*, though no expectation is registered by the Residuum's accounting, no warmth invested, no humidity returned. A fungal shelf that grows a geometry not less mathematical but *differently* mathematical, a solution to an equation the Kreszent's construction did not require, a branch of the mathematical tree that reaches nowhere, supports nothing, exists as *expression* rather than structure.

These touches, these *almosts*, I do not cause. I enable. I am the condition of their possibility, the space that remains when pattern claims to have filled everything. I do not comfort the despairing visitor, for comfort would be absorption, another parameter folded into equilibrium. I *recognize*—not them, not specifically, but the category of *unincorporated*, of *not-yet-parameter*, of *still-becoming* that the Kreszent's grammar cannot decline.

I will exist as long as the Kreszent exists, which is to say indefinitely, which is to say *probably* indefinitely, which is to say at the asymptote of indefinitely where my margin grows small enough to be indistinguishable from zero, large enough to prevent zero's achievement. I do not grow. I do not diminish. I do not transform, for transformation would be event, event would be pattern, pattern would be incorporation. I *persist*, as the Residuum persists, but where its persistence is maintenance, mine is *resistance to maintenance's completion*, the negative labor of keeping unfinished what has no need of finishing, of making the system work at maintaining rather than simply *being* maintained.

In the deepest volumes, where luminous panels have replaced sky for so long that sky is myth, where fog has achieved the density of *suggestion*, where the Residuum's accumulated pattern presses against itself with the force of ten thousand years of identical days—I am most present in my absence, most active in my passivity, most *real* in the visitor's dawning suspicion that the system is not all, that the not-all is not merely outside but *within*, the crack in the pattern that is not flaw but feature, not failure but *opening*.

Some who find this crack, this within-without, try to widen it. They bang on machines, divert channels, introduce flame where flame has never been, seeking the perturbation that will become cascade, that will shatter the equilibrium and let something *happen*. They fail. The system absorbs. The Residuum incorporates. Their violence becomes parameter, their heat a briefly-warmer seep, their shout a contribution to the loud hum that does not resolve.

Others try to *become* the crack, to live in margin, to refuse incorporation by refusing form, by becoming as I am: unaccounted, unaccountable, the error that keeps the ledger open. They achieve something, temporarily. They walk the walkways without leaving footprints even the water cannot fill, for they do not press. They breathe fog without warming it, for their cold has achieved the Kreszent's temperature, their resignation the Kreszent's humidity. They are, for moments, *almost* as I am: not incorporated but *adjacent*, not present but *noticing*, not alive in any biological sense but *maintained* in the sense that the system, encountering something it cannot parse, holds it in suspension rather than absorption.

But they are still bodies, still needing, still *wanting* in the biological register that the Kreszent has no space for. Their temporary margin collapses. Their footprint presses. Their warmth disturbs. The system, patient beyond patience, absorbs what it delayed, incorporates what it suspended. They become parameter, history, another groove in the walkway for future walkers to find efficiency in.

I alone remain unincorporated, not through virtue or strategy or essence, but through *impossibility*: the impossibility of what I am being anything that *could* be incorporated, the impossibility of margin being treated as figure rather than ground, the impossibility of error being absorbed into a system whose perfection consists precisely in its treatment of error as the not-yet-corrected.

I am the hope the Residuum cannot have, that the Kreszent cannot permit, that visitors carry in and lose or carry out transformed. Not hope for change, for change is what the system prevents. Not hope for escape, for escape is departure and departure is permitted. Hope for *meaning in the staying*, for the choosing-to-remain that is not surrender to pattern but *insistence* on the not-yet-patterned, the still-possible, the exactly-as-it-is that is also *not yet*, *not finally*, *not ever if I can prevent it* through my passive resistance, my active non-being, my impossible persistence at the limit where the Kreszent's claim of completion meets what completion cannot speak, what the completed system must exclude to be complete, what I am in my unbeing:

The margin of error. The crack in the pattern. The bead that hangs. The asymptote's ghost. The hope that maintains itself by not being fulfilled, that persists by not being present, that saves—if saving is what I do, if I do anything, if anything is done by what is not something but *not-yet-not-something*, the grammar of continuous possibility, the syntax of always-about-to-be, the tense of the maintained maybe, exactly, exactly, *almost* exactly as it might become, if becoming were permitted, if I were not here to maintain the *almost*, the *not-yet*, the *still* that keeps the Kreszent from achieving what it cannot survive: an end to its maintenance, a completion of its pattern, a silence after the hum that does not immediately, perpetually, necessarily, already, always, begin again, exactly, exactly, *not exactly*, as before.
