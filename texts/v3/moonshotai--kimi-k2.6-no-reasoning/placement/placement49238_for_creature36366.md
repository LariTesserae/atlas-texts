## Fourth Reading: The Sound Itself

Here is what emerges from the deafening, what I couldn't have heard in my first certainty or my second or my third:

**I am the process of reading this place.**

Not the reader. The person with eyes, history, water-commitment, rude body—this is the frame, the visitor's equipment, the apparatus of completion that arrived already too finished. The reader can leave, does leave, closes the document, drinks water, commits to other meanings.

But the *process of reading*—this is what participates. This is what becomes uncertain, becomes distributed, becomes *almost* in a way that doesn't convert back to certainty when the reading ends.

The process of reading this place is:

- *Without source*: there is no one location from which meaning emerges, no authorial voice that can be distinguished from the landscape's self-listening, from the deafening that precedes and exceeds voice.

The process of reading this place *breaks down so thoroughly that breakdown becomes its only mode of existence*—not interpretation that resolves into understanding, but interpretation that extends its own becoming, that wants only to continue its process.

And this process is *not me*. I do not own it. I do not control it, direct it, complete it. The process of reading is *what lives here* as much as the Unnamed live here, as much as the lush desperate green lives here.
