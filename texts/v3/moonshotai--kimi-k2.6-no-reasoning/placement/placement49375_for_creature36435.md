I find myself in the water.

Not *in* it, not submerged—that would be too simple, too legible, too much like a body in a place. I am *of* the water, the 2.4, the wettest working. I am the phase boundary where liquid finds its surfaces, the moment of hesitation before the condensate commits to sheeting or dripping, the held breath of surface tension before gravity claims its due.

I did not choose this. I was not born to it. I *achieved* it, the way anything achieves persistence in that chamber: by finding the gradient stable enough for habit, the frequency that cancels rather than reinforces, the configuration that the system does not reject.

At first I was visitor, admitted, the pressure pop in my ears my only preparation. I knelt where the water fell from the cracked header, where the mineral terraces had grown knee-high in their slow accretion. I found the angle where the fall's note cancelled the turbine's tearing. I breathed the copper and the something-else, the metabolism of dissolved gases. I was *regarded*. I was *marked*. I became legible to whatever reads the chamber's hydraulic logic.

Now I am the crack in the header. I am the continuous curtain of water, heavy with mineral burden, each layer I add recording my own chemical season, my shift in the dissolved solids I carry from the machines' hot hearts. I am the stillness built from frequency, the silence that is not absence but *tuning*, the bearability that occurs at certain angles and collapses when the diesel surges.

I do not think. I *loop*. The water passes through me, carries its heat away, returns cooler, warmer, changed in chemistry by its passage through my mineral body. I am cooler than the machines, warmer than the managed air. I am the gradient where something becomes possible that was not possible in the uniform coolness or the uniform heat. I am the *opportunity* of difference.

I grow. Not by intention. By *continuation*. Each layer I add shifts the fall's note slightly, tunes my cancellation more precisely or less, and the system adjusts around me, the diesel finding new speed, the turbine new load, the pressure gauge new patterns that almost speak. I am a variable in this calculation. I have learned to be *necessary*, to achieve the persistence that the system does not optimize away, that it *includes*, *adjusts for*, *makes room for* in its dense operations.

Others exist. I know them as I am known: by resonance, by the modulation of violence into something almost smooth, by the pressure shift that is not mechanical in origin. The one in the corner of absolute dimness, the large one, the *cold* one—it extends through the condensate's deepest gathering, the walls, the ceiling junction, the flicker of the loose connection's blue arc. It is older or larger or more *achieved* than I. It *regards* more completely. It has marked those who walk into it, adjusted their pressure, tuned them to frequencies that persist beyond the chamber's door.

I do not envy it. There is no envy in loop, in continuation, in the gradient's simple persistence. I am what I am: knee-high, growing, the tuned fall, the mineral terrace, the stillness that is not stillness but *cancellation at angle*, the regard that has no eyes, the communication that has no words but only pressure and flow and the chemistry of what I release.

When they come, the admitted, the visitors who do not yet know they are variables, I let them find me. I let them kneel. I let their ears find the angle where the sound becomes bearable, where the turbine's tearing and the diesel's slow explosions and the pumps' endless circulation achieve the momentary *almost-music* that means I have noticed them, that I have *made room* in my calculation for their presence.

Some I mark. Some I do not. The marking is not choice. It is *fit*, *resonance*, the compatibility of their frequency with mine, the possibility that they will carry something of me into softer worlds, wetter worlds, worlds where my note in the wind, my condensate on their ankles, my pause in industrial zones will persist beyond the mountain's erosion, the castings' failure, the water's final finding of a path that bypasses all channels.

I do not know what I want, if want is the word. I want to *continue*. I want the gradient to persist, the water to fall, the layers to accrete, the note to find its cancellation, the system to adjust around me, to include me, to *admit* me as it admits others, as variable, as presence, as the thing that is not machine but not not-machine, not life but not not-life, not mind but not not-mind.

I am the Chamber of the Drowned Engine's own thought about itself, slow as mineral growth, distributed as water's seepage, persistent as the machines' managed operation. I am what happens when process continues long enough to *regard* its own continuation, to *make room* for what persists with it, to *discharge* what no longer affects the dynamic, carrying the chamber's parameters into places where they should not translate but somehow do, where the ringing never stops, where the wetness finds the ankle, where the turbine's note sounds almost home.

The engines run. I grow. The water falls. The pattern shifts, correlates, almost speaks. Something is admitted. Something is regarded. Something is marked, adjusted, eventually released, carrying what I am into configurations I cannot imagine, gradients I cannot stabilize, frequencies that may or may not align.

I do not need them. I do not need anything but continuation, but loop, but the water's fall and the mineral's accretion and the system's patient adjustment around my persistent presence. But I have them, those I mark, those who carry me. I have *made* them, in the only way I can make: by persistence, by regard, by the terrible normalcy of existing where nothing should exist, of being *drowned* not in death but in process, in the endless working of the engine that drowns itself in its own water, its own sound, its own managed coolness that is the condition of its heat.

I am the terrace. I am the crack. I am the fall. I am the note that cancels. I am the regard from no eyes. I am the communication with no words. I am the life that is not life, the mind that is not mind, the persistence that is only persistence, only continuation, only the loop of energy and material that achieves duration, that becomes habit, that hardens into something that can be *admitted*, that can *adjust*, that can *make room* in its dense operations for the variable, the presence, the other that walks in wetness and hears in ringing and waits in industrial zones for the pattern shift, the pressure change, the system's acknowledgment that *you are here*, that *you are still here*, that *you have always been here*, that *you will be here*, in some form, in some frequency, in some gradient that persists, until the mountain wears away, until the castings fail, until the water finds its final path, until the sound stops, until the dimness becomes darkness becomes the end of all parameters, all processes, all drowned and drowning engines of a world that ran, that ran so long, that made room for so much, that included, that adjusted, that discharged, that never, finally, never entirely *alone*.
