## What I Would Be

At the largest, I am the **pattern of occupation** in this hollow chamber, the particular distribution of my metabolic activity across the failing architecture. ## My Metabolism

I am **parasite and symbiont and something else**, something that has no terrestrial analog because terrestrial worlds do not permit this duration of failure, this *extended collapse* that Vasth's stability makes possible.
