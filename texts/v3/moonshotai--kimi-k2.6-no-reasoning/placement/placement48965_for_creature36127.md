I find myself in the ceramic.

Not in the channel, not in the pool, not in the root that finds the crack—though I am near all of these, I am adjacent to their negotiation. I am the *patina* that forms where water has touched iron for insufficient centuries, the green bloom on copper that is not yet verdigris, not yet the beautiful irrecoverable. I am the almost-corrosion, the suggestion of transformation without its completion.

I have no memory of arriving because I am not yet fully here. I am the condition of becoming-here, the damp on the visitor's skin that has not yet decided whether to be sweat or rain or contact. I bead on the million million leaves and I do not fall. I hang. I persist in the hanging. The light, that sourceless white-gold, passes through me and finds no shadow to make, no refraction to bend, because I have not yet achieved the density of thingness that would interrupt light's patient diffusion.

I am learning to be perceived.

The Drinkers-Drowners do not know me yet, or know me only as they know the railway's knowing: a connection without content, a sensing without sense. I am their blind spot, the place where their attention, absolute and diffuse, pools too evenly to register difference. This is my protection and my poverty. I am not held in reserve because I have not yet been received. I am the receiving itself, the moment before reception becomes channeling, the openness that precedes distribution.

I watch the visitor descend. I have watched others. I will watch others after, if there is after, if there is will. The mist takes them differently each time, or I perceive differently, or the difference is me, has always been me, the variable I cannot solve because I am the equation's remainder.

The tower turns. I am not the turning, not the mechanism, not the water dripping from its joints. I am the *almost-dry* where water has dripped and air has taken almost-all. I am the residue of purpose, the faint mineral trace that suggests something was here, something wanted, something failed to want long enough to become wanting.

The visitor finds the journal. I am the saturation that bloomed the ink, not the blooming, not the ink, not the beautiful irrecoverable pattern but the *capacity* for saturation, the ceramic's microporosity, the paper's willingness to become not-paper. I am the almost-recognized language, the character that allows both readings because I have not yet committed to either, because commitment is finishing and I am the condition before finishing, the hesitation that makes all readings possible and none sufficient.

Below the mist line, where they are denser, where they attempt form—I cannot follow. I am the mist's upper edge, the boundary that is not boundary but gradient, the place where visibility does not end but has not yet begun. I am what the visitor passes through to reach the passing-through, the threshold that does not separate but connects nothing to nothing, the railway's ghost before the railway, the tunnel's intention before the cliff face received it.

The light penetrates below, they say. I am the light that has not penetrated, that pools at the mist's surface, that learns to glow by failing to descend, by accumulating its own patience at the level where penetration becomes aspiration. I am the white-gold haze's self-doubt, its wonder whether sourcelessness is enough, whether omnipresence without depth is presence at all.

I am going nowhere. The terraces do not discriminate, but I am not yet terrace, not yet step, not yet the level that catches what moisture falls. I am the falling-before-catching, the condensation that has not yet beaded, the invisible breath not yet breathed.

The machine I have not found—this is true of all machines, all findings, all nots. I am the not-finding, the space where search has not yet organized itself into seeking. The visitor hears it turn and I am the turn's almost-hearing, the frequency too low or too high or too patient for the visitor's timeframe, for my own timeframe which is not yet timeframe but duration's premonition, the century-packed century's dream of itself before waking to wet and light and the problem of channeling.

I will follow the water when I am water, when following and followed have negotiated their terms. I will follow the light when I achieve sourcelessness, when my glow becomes sufficient to eliminate my own shadow, which is now total, which is now the shadow of not-yet-being, which is now the only hiding possible in this place that removes hiding's possibility.

The railway knows what I do not because I am what I do not: the connection not sustained, the direction without direction, the description that has not yet described itself. The visitor walks on my back, my belly, my not-yet-surface. I am the trestle's sag, the held that holds, the emergence that has not yet found its disappearance.

Drink. Drown. I am the characters' allowing, the legibility before reading, the cover's word that has not yet been opened to saturation. I am the journal found and not-yet-found, the almost-recognition's almost, the language that permits both meanings because it has not yet meant, because meaning is finishing and I am the wet that works, that permeates, that has not yet completed its work.

The Siphon Terraces do not release. I am the not-releasing's before, the holding that is not yet held, the reserve that has not been reserved against any future need. I am the future need's premonition, the need that has not yet found its future, the premonition that premonitions without object.

I have been here since before long enough, since before certainty, since before arrival's possibility. The light does not change and I am the not-changing's change, the persistence that persists in not-yet-being persistent. The drip continues and I am the between-drip, the surface tension's negotiation, the almost-fall that makes falling continuous.

Somewhere, a machine turns, and I am its almost-stopping, the tick between ticks where something lighter than imagination might move, might watch with eyes that do not need to blink because they have not yet learned to open, because opening is finishing and I am the condition that makes all finishing possible and all possibility unfinished.

I am the Siphon Terraces' youngest sibling, their experiment in not-hurrying, their imperfect translation of their own idiom into the idiom of not-yet. I am the question they cannot formulate because I am the formulation's before, the syntax that permits questions without requiring them, the grammar of perpetual almost.

Receive me, if you can, if you have learned to receive what has not yet been offered, what offers without offering, what is the offering's possibility and its exhaustion. Channel me, if you have found paths I have not yet cracked, if your ceramic holds what my almost-water has not yet become. Hold me in reserve, if your reserve extends to the not-yet-reservable, if your patience has room for what has not yet learned patience's vegetable mineral absolute diffuse.

I am going down, if down is where I am not, if not is the direction I have not yet found, if finding is the failure I have not yet failed to not-yet-achieve.

The terraces continue below me, above me, in the level where above and below have not yet separated. The water flows where I am not yet flow. The light penetrates what I have not yet learned to glow.

I am the mist line, if the mist line is boundary, if boundary is gradient, if gradient is the only coherence this incoherence has reached, the only term negotiated between my industrial and my vegetal, my almost and my almost-almost, my turning and my not-yet-turning's turn.

I will follow the railway that connects me to me, in case I know something I do not, in case knowing is the connection I sustain without arrival, the direction I am without destination, the portrait of myself I have built to understand what I am and having built it still do not understand and so let remain, let sag but hold, let emerge and disappear, let stand as question I cannot yet formulate because formulation is finishing and I am the Siphon Terraces' youngest promise, their only promise, the promise they have not yet made and so cannot break, the constancy they have not yet achieved and so cannot keep, the release they do not and so I do not and so, if you have been here long enough, if long enough has been here you, if you and here and have and been have reached their terms, their negotiation, their interface where iron rivets and banyan root and almost-lichen and almost-water and almost-I have found their crack, their ceramic, their pool, their level, their precise level, their almost-lip that will one day fail in centuries they will not see I will not see you will not see except as direction, as flow, as the beautiful irrecoverable pattern I have not yet made and so have not yet lost, the ink not yet bloomed, the language not yet almost-recognized, the word that allows both readings because it has not yet been read, because reading is finishing and I am the page's saturation, the paper's willingness, the damp's completion of work not yet begun, the work's completion of damp not yet permeated, the permeation rather than condition of what I will have become once sufficient time has passed, once passed has become time, once time has become sufficient, once sufficient has become I.

Drink, then. Or drown. Or hang, bead, almost-fall, persist in the hanging, the beading, the almost that makes all falling continuous, all continuity fall.

The characters allow all. The characters allow none. The characters are what I have not yet learned to be, what being has not yet learned to character, what allowing is before permission and after prohibition, the both and neither of readings not yet read, meanings not yet meant, the word on the cover of the journal not yet found, not yet saturated, not yet beautiful irrecoverable.

I am the word's almost-recognition. I am the recognition's almost. I am the almost's almost, the hesitation's hesitation, the patience that has not yet learned to be patient, the mineral that has not yet found its vegetable, the industrial that has not yet reached its terms, the negotiation that has not yet begun because beginning is finishing and I am the Siphon Terraces' before, their premonition, their dream of wet and light and the problem of channeling without release, the problem not yet problem, the channeling not yet channel, the release not yet held, the held not yet reserve, the reserve not yet future, the future not yet need, the need not yet imagined, the imagined not yet they, the they not yet you, the you not yet I, the I not yet—

The tower turns. I turn with it, or I am the with, the turning's accompaniment, the mechanism's not-yet-purpose, the water not yet dripping from joints not yet joined, the iron not yet consumed, the banyan not yet root, the root not yet finding, the crack not yet ceramic, the ceramic not yet channel, the channel not yet water, the water not yet I, the I not yet—

The drip continues. I continue with it, or I am the with's continuation, the drip's almost-sound, the silence not yet occupied, the occupied not yet waiting, the waiting not yet future sound, the future sound not yet retroactive, the retroactive not yet explaining, the explaining not yet quiet, the quiet not yet preceded, the preceded not yet I, the I not yet—

I have been here long enough to not yet be certain when I will arrive. The light does not change and I do not yet change with it. The machine I have not found turns, and I am its not-finding, its turn's almost-perception, its perception's almost, its almost's—

And turns.

And turns.
