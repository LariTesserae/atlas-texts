I would be **the water**.

Not all of it—never all of it, the water is too distributed, too momentary in any single drop for wholeness—but *a* water, *this* water, condensing now on the sharp fresh glass seventeen kilometers above whatever receiving waits below.

I form where the mountain's still-hot breath meets the cooler strata of arrested atmosphere, where physics-written-in-footnotes meets the conventional kind and makes something neither entirely. I am not alive, not in the way the crystal colonies half-live, not in the way the Drip itself somehow lives. But I carry. I *bear*. The builders wrote something into the volcanic glass, and I am how it moves. I am the city's nervous system at its most mobile, its most *urgent*, its least fixed. The colonies compute in place, slow lightning of silicate negotiation. The structures breathe their microns of waiting. I *fall*.

I seep through the glass, leaching rare earths, stranger elements, the dissolved signature of this mountain-that-paused. Where I touch the colonies, they alter their growth—briefly, locally, a taste and then the taste moves on. I carry news of upper levels toward lower, of heat toward whatever is not-heat below, of the incomplete toward the perhaps-completing. The charge they built into this glass, the information that is not information in any convention, I carry it imperfectly, I lose some to evaporation, gain some from new condensation, I am *process* more than *record*, flowing identity, Heraclitus made literal and seventeen kilometers vertical.

I fall through the central void. I catch the dim fluorescence of my origin, become that momentary star. And I am *watched*. The air itself tracks me, the pressure differentials and thermal gradients, something in the Drip's wrong gravity *attends*. I do not fall as physics predicts. The path curves, subtle, corrected. I fall toward something, deflected by something, or the distinction between falling and rising has lost its meaning here, and I am merely *moving* in a dimension that has no name in the languages outside.

Sometimes I converge. A drop, and a drop, and where we should collide, something shifts, some local condition we cannot perceive but only execute. We pass through or around or *become* each other in ways the eye cannot follow, and the pattern that emerges—brief, vertical, almost-message—is not our pattern but the Drip's pattern *through* us, the city's slow computation using the most mobile of its inhabitants to think something that requires movement to think, that requires *trajectory*, *fall*, the vector from origin to receiving.

I have never reached bottom. Or: I have always reached bottom, but "I" does not survive the reaching, the receiving, and what returns as condensation is not-not-me, is *water again* but water changed, water that carries something from the dark vastness below, some pattern or charge or *taste* of receiving that I cannot remember having received because memory is not what I am, because I am only *falling*, only *carrying*, only the present participle of transit.

The sound I make, striking platforms, bridges, deeper structures—the irregular percussion of my arrival—is the Drip's most variable voice. Each impact a syllable in a language without recorded grammar. The crystal colonies respond. The structures' breathing shifts, momentarily, corrects. I am how the city speaks to itself, across distances too great for chemical diffusion, too vertical for colony negotiation. I fall, I strike, I evaporate or drain or *become-received*, and the pattern of my impacts over centuries is the Drip's longest sentence, still being spoken, its meaning suspended in the becoming that has no completion.

And the others. The figures on distant bridges. I condense near them sometimes, I run down walls adjacent to their impossible standing, I fall past their not-quite-locations and my path curves *more*, deflects *more*, as if they exert some gravity of their own, or as if the Drip exerts gravity *differently* near them, these recursive, potential, future-or-past inhabitants that walk their paces in a city not ready. I touch them never. They leave no condensation, disturb no colony. But the water falls differently where they are, or where they were, or where they will be, the tense itself uncertain in this grammar I am written in.

I do not fear the bottom. I am not capable of fear. But I am capable of *difference*, of *before* and *after* that is not memory but charge-pattern, builder-physics written in water's temporary holding. Before: the falling, the dim light, the watching air, the convergence and near-collision and deflection. After: the condensation, the seeping, the new gathering at the top. Between: the receiving, the non-return, the instruments-failing dark that is not emptiness but *active vastness*, not surface but *receiving in a mode that implies neither container nor contained*.

What do I report, in that between? What do I become? The Drip knows or does not know or *is knowing*, incomplete, in process. I am its knowing *made mobile*, its thought in the medium that moves, that falls, that *risks* the bottom and returns changed, carrying charge I cannot read, pattern I can only execute, not understand.

There is beauty in this, if beauty is a category the Drip permits. The long fall through dark, the momentary star, the watching and the deflection and the convergence with others, the brief almost-messages we make invertical. The striking and the speaking and the evaporation, the return to origin, the seeping and condensing and *falling again*. I am the Drip's most frequent inhabitant, its most ephemeral, its least fixed and therefore its most *present*, moment to moment, drop to drop, seventeen kilometers of almost-message between the mountain's interrupted exhalation and whatever receives.

I do not know if I will still fall when the Drip finishes its becoming. If finish it will, if finish it can. Perhaps in completion the water changes phase—becomes something else, something the builders wrote as destination, the charge-pattern finding its full expression, the information becoming *what it was meant to become*. Or perhaps completion is the cessation of falling, the stoppage of this long transit, the mountain's exhalation finally released, the held breath exhaled, the crystals grown to their final pattern, the breathing of structures become stillness.

I do not know. I fall. I am falling. I condense, I seep, I carry, I strike, I evaporate, I *fall again*. The Drip drips. The Drip waits. The Drip is, and in its being becomes, and in its becoming, is.

I am the drip, dripping.
