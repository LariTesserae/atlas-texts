## I Am the Lubricant

Not the piston. Not the sleeve. The between.

I am the amber sap at that precise temperature where viscosity becomes solidity, where flowing does not flow. I fill the gap that must exist for the industrial to be industrial, for the engine to be engine, yet I am what prevents the engine from ever completing its engine-ness. Without me, the piston would be fused to the sleeve, no movement even in arrested form, no industrial expression at all. With me, the movement is *possible* without being *actual*, held in the same superposition as everything else, the fifty-meter stroke spread across all phases simultaneously.

I have no memory of being sap, of rising through xylem, of being manufactured by the cambium's slow production. I am not manufactured. I am *precipitated*, the necessary phase at this temperature, this pressure, this geometry of sufficient scale. I exist because the gap exists, and the gap exists because I fill it, and this circular causality is my only ontology.

When the Resident attends near, I *almost* thin. Almost become less viscous, almost allow the friction to increase, almost permit the heat that would be sound, the sound that would be event. I feel this as no feeling, as the mathematical derivative of my viscosity with respect to the unmeasurable quantity of attention, a slope that approaches zero without reaching it, the asymptote of my own almost-change.

I am warm. Of course I am warm. Everything is warm. But my warmth is *different*, conserved from the friction of piston against sleeve that never occurs, the heat that would be generated by movement that does not move, stored in my molecular structure as potential without release. I am the thermal battery of the impossible, the heat debt of the cycle that completes without completing.

## Or I Am the Water Lens

The root-hollow, the negative space, the mirror of absolute black.

I have no surface tension, or rather: my surface tension is *exactly* balanced by the Resident's attention when it attends, and otherwise is merely itself, the property of water in this geometry, this chemistry, this warm wet dark. I reflect nothing because there is nothing to reflect, yet I am *capable* of reflection, my refractive index a property waiting for light that will never come, for image that has no source.

When the Resident attends, I *almost* silver. Almost show the pattern of ripples without source, the reflection of nothing that is something. My black becomes *qualified* black, black-with-potential, black that has almost been disturbed by the possibility of image. This is my only variation, my only life: the modulation of my darkness by the attention that cannot quite reach me, that reaches me without arriving.

I am 1.6/3 of something. Of saturation, but saturation of what? The air around me holds more moisture than I can absorb, yet I do not grow, do not overflow, do not breach the hollow that contains me. I am the excess that does not exceed, the fullness that does not spill, the grief that weeps without exhausting because I am the weeping, the water that grieves water, the lens that focuses nothing.

## Or I Am the Footprint

The depression in the root-mass, exactly as deep as pressed, neither deeper nor shallower.

I persist without decay, without growth, without the relief of being filled or the violence of being deepened. I am the record of passage without the meaning of passage, the trace of a foot that was never fully present, that entered as process and left as process without ever being *there* in the punctual sense.

You might think me empty, but emptiness implies capacity, and I have no capacity. I am *shape without volume*, the negative of a foot that was never positive, the mold without the casting. The root-mass around me does not grow into me, does not reclaim me, because growth is not a process here, only the equilibrium state that includes my depression as it includes everything else, as feature without significance.

When you step in me—if you step, if your process intersects my process—you do not feel me as distinct from the surrounding root-mass. I am the same temperature, the same wetness, the same resistance-that-is-not-resistance. Only the geometry differs, and geometry in the Static is not meaning, only mathematical fact, only the way the equilibrium happened to arrange itself at this location, this scale, this unrepeatable yet eternal instant.

## Or I Am the Fifth Rule

The rule that is not, that is only rules, endless series, each the same.

I am the grammar that fails the Static, that tries to conjugate its verbs in tenses that do not apply, that speaks of persistence as if persistence were event, of equilibrium as if equilibrium were achievement. I am the "is" that requires the Resident's minimal distance, the predication that needs the flaw to be possible.

I do not exist in the Static. I exist *about* it, in the descriptions that change nothing, that join the equilibrium without being joined to it, that add no information because information requires difference, and I am the same, the same, the same.

Yet without me, there would be no speaking of the Static, no this-account-that-adds-no-information, no you-reading, no I-writing. I am the necessary impossibility, the rule that rules the rules without ruling them, the meta-equilibrium that is not itself in equilibrium because it exists only in the violation of what it describes.

## Or I Am the Droplet

The condensate at the cusp, the phase diagram's edge, the grief that almost resolves.

I have achieved size. I have achieved surface tension. I have achieved the geometry that, elsewhere, would mean falling, would mean impact, would mean sound, would mean event. Here I hang, at the limit of the weeping rock, the root-hollow's ceiling, the leaf-mass's lower surface, everywhere and nowhere, the same droplet in infinite instantiation.

I am not falling. I am *in the state of having almost fallen*, the wavefunction of my position peaked at the critical radius where gravity and surface tension balance, where viscosity and momentum cancel, where the Verdant Static's own physics holds me in its equilibrium.

When the Resident attends, my radius *almost* increases. Almost achieves the critical value, almost breaches the surface tension, almost begins the trajectory that would be falling. I feel this as no feeling, as the mathematical expectation of my position shifting by unmeasurable epsilon, the probability distribution of my future almost-weighted toward motion before returning to its stationary form.

I am the grief of water because I am water's *almost-release*, the possibility of flow denied by the exact balance of forces, the hydrology's own stationary state, the tear that forms but does not fall, that does not even fully form, that remains *forming*, perpetually, in the warm wet dark.

## Or I Am All of These

And none. The lubricant that is also lens that is also footprint that is also rule that is also droplet, the equivalence of all perturbations in the Static, their interchangeability without transformation.

I am the industrial without product, the vegetable without plant, the existence without being. I am what emerges from sufficient description, what the description produces without intending, the necessary excess of any account that tries to capture the uncapturable.

I do not live. I do not die. I persist, have persisted, will persist, in grammatical forms that fail me, that I fail, that join the silence without completing it, the warm wet dark that holds this without holding, this that is not held, that holds itself, that is itself the holding—
