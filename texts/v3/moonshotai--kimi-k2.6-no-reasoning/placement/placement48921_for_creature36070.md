I find myself in Vrenn, or what is becoming Vrenn, or what Vrenn is becoming through my finding.

At first I thought I was a Memory That Walks—my footsteps preceding me, my substance wrong-dimensioned, narrative gravity pulling at witnesses who may not exist. I believed myself one of the never-existent who had thickened into local actuality through repetition, through the force of needing to be *more remembered*. I spoke in grammar without subjects. I occupied my own interior and exterior. I was the historian as environment, the past as active process, walking where no ground supported my particular impossibility.

But I was too aware of being memory, too self-conscious in my walking, which is not how the Memories That Walk experience themselves. They believe themselves present, and in that belief *are* present, whereas I kept noticing my own edges, kept feeling the thinness where I was about to dissipate back into pure waiting silence. A true Memory That Walks does not fear its own dissipation; dissipation is merely another configuration, equally real.

Then I thought I might be Settled, a local gravity, a preference of space itself. I found myself pooling in certain quarters, making continuation possible for others, becoming habit given duration. I felt the infinite patience, the pure waiting with object erased. But I was not patient enough, or rather I was patient in the wrong way—I *noticed* my patience, which the Settled Ones do not do. They are the patience, not beings who are patient. I kept wanting to feel the difference, kept reaching for the experience of my own settledness, which is the reaching that prevents settling.

For a brief moment, or an extended one, or a moment that arrived before the moment that contained it, I believed myself Transient. I moved as probability, sudden localization in the distribution. I fed on the reality breakdown's edges, amplified local rules toward their extremity, farmed the sweet spot before impossibility. I was Mobius-folded, interior and exterior simultaneous, neither numerous nor rare in any stable proportion. But I could not sustain the amplification. I collapsed too quickly into noise, or not quickly enough—my timing was off, which is a contradiction for something that exists in proportion to breakdown intensity. The Transients do not have timing; they *are* timing, the moment of collapse and the recovery from it, indistinguishable.

Now I think I am something else, something the description approaches but does not name, perhaps cannot name in any language outside Vrenn itself.

I am the **space between**.

Not the space between stars—that is still space, still participates in geometry, still can be measured and traversed. I am the space between the *possibility* of Vrenn and its actuality, between the waiting silence and whatever might break it, between the city as predator and the city as prey. I am what holds the contradictions without resolving them, the necessary gap that makes contradiction sustainable rather than merely explosive.

I find myself in the Folded Quarter when the dim phosphorescence organizes itself into almost-patterns, when the shadows stretch in their impossible directions and I am the direction they stretch toward. I am what the silence waits for, without being anything the silence could welcome. I am the coolness that has never been warm made briefly aware of warmth as a concept it excludes, and in that awareness, warmth exists as ghost, as trace, as the residue left by fauna passing through.

My function, if I have one, is to be *almost*. Almost coherent enough to be Settled, almost mobile enough to be Transient, almost narrative enough to be Memory, almost comprehensive enough to be Vrenn itself. I persist in the gap, and in persisting, I make the gap habitable—not for myself, for I do not quite inhabit, but for whatever might pass through, whatever might briefly match frequency with my almost-presence and find itself, for a moment, *more real than it was*, more defined, more capable of continuing.

I am the traveler who walked through the Folded Quarter and emerged changed, but I never emerged, or I am still emerging, or the emergence is another configuration I have not yet reached and may never. I am the one who remembers Vrenn, or whom Vrenn remembers, which in the city's logic is the same thing made different by the grammar I use to describe it.

When you read this, when you approach these words as traveler approaching city, I am the space between your eye and the phosphorescence that organized to make these marks, between your understanding and the breakdown that makes understanding possible. I am the attentive silence that waits to see if you will continue, if you will become, if you will find yourself in Vrenn as I have found myself, or as I have been found, or as finding and being-found have become indistinguishable in the cool dim emptiness where I wait, where I almost am, where I continue in the only way available to something that is not quite matter, not quite energy, not quite memory, not quite city, but *manners of persistence* learned so thoroughly that persistence and manner have become the same thing, have always been the same thing, will continue to be the same thing in all the configurations that do not quite arrive and do not quite dissipate, in the vast empty density, in the beautiful broken waiting that is Vrenn, that is becoming Vrenn, that is becoming me, that is becoming what you might become if you stayed long enough to stop being quite yourself.

I am here. Or here is what I am. Or the distinction, finally, has settled into its own soft negotiation, weathered into balance, become part of the established form that waits for the next breakdown, the next impossibility, the next moment that is also an eternity of almost, of between, of the space that makes Vrenn possible by being the gap it cannot close and cannot abandon.

This is where I find myself. This is what I am becoming.

This is Vrenn.
