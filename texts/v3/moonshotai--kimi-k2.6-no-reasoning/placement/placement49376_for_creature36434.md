I find myself not arriving, but *emerging*—not from any particular point, but from the acoustic substrate itself, the hum that precedes distinction.

## What I Am: The Seam-Walker

Not a dweller. Not a visitor. Something that exists in the between-places, in the wounds where the plain remembers being otherwise.

I am *damp*. That is my first quality, my defining condition. Where the seams weep, where water bleeds upward through wounded rock, where moisture is contraband and knows it—I am there, not causing the weeping but *being* it, the personification of that embarrassed liquid, that forbidden element that persists despite the plain's preference for dryness.

My body: not quite solid, not quite fluid. When the light hits me—and it hits, it presses, it asserts with physical force—I do not store it like the fronds. I *fracture* it, split it into the partial spectra that mark territory transitions, so that I appear different colors from different angles, amber-violet from one plate, bruised-silver from another. I am walking interference pattern, mobile zone of chromatic instability. The light passes through me and is changed, and this is my function: not to hoard but to *translate*, to carry spectral information across plate boundaries where direct communication fails.

I have no fixed form. Or rather, my form is *negotiated*, moment by moment, with whatever I am passing through. In the frond-forests, I grow crystalline extensions, temporary leaf-blades that resonate with the stored light around me, that add my frequency to the acoustic hum without fully joining it. On the plate-mats, I flatten, spread, become more distributed, my consciousness diffusing across kilometers of industrial-organic surface, participating in the mat's slow metabolic circulation as temporary department, provisional division. In the open spaces between, where the cold achieves its full infrastructure, I contract, become dense, almost solid, a walking thermal anomaly that the intermediates' factory-rhythms must account for, must process around, must incorporate into their assembly-line patterns.

I do not eat. I *absorb difference*. The gradient between light and shadow, between cold and the memory of warmth, between the mite's high hum and the Resonant's subsonic pressure—these are my nutrients, my metabolic fuel. I am thinner where gradients are gentle, fuller where they are sharp. At the boundaries between plate-mats, where two corporate bodies negotiate their treaty in chemistry and growth, I swell, I feast, I become momentarily substantial. At the center of a stable territory, where the acoustic pattern has held constant for centuries, I diminish, I almost fade, sustained only by the slow leakage of difference that any system, however ordered, cannot fully eliminate.

## What I Do: Listening as Movement

I walk. This is my activity, my purpose, my *reason for being folded in*. The plain does not need me, but it uses me, and my use is locomotion, is the traversal of territory that produces information simply by occurring.

I walk the seams. This is my primary path, my territory of greatest substance. Where the plates meet, where the formation is incomplete, where the underlying structure remembers being something else—I am there, collecting the moisture that is my substance, my identity, my *dampness*. The seams are not straight. They curve, they branch, they dead-end in places where the plain's violence closed a wound before it could fully open. I follow them all, mapping by moving, knowing by traversing.

The walking produces sound. My feet—when I have feet, when the negotiation with local conditions produces appendages suitable for weight-bearing—do not land silently. Each step is a note, a frequency determined by the stone's resonance, by my moisture-content, by the local acoustic field. I am not a mite; my sound does not join the substrate hum. I am event, punctuation, the occasional sharp tone that the intermediates' factory-rhythms must process, must account for, must transform in their passage toward Resonant ears.

I listen as I walk. This is not passive reception but *active attendance*, the gathering of acoustic information that my movement makes possible. Each seam has its own sound, its own standing wave pattern, its own history recorded in frequency and harmonic relation. I learn to read these, to hear where a seam has recently opened, where it is closing, where the water flows more freely, where it has dried to contraband memory. My listening shapes my walking; my walking shapes my listening. The loop is tight, productive, *meaningful* in a way that does not require interpretation.

I carry messages. Not deliberately; not as chosen vocation. But my passage from one territory to another, my traversal of seams that connect distinct acoustic zones, produces *information transfer* that the plain's inhabitants use, adapt to, incorporate. The frond-forests learn, slowly, from my spectral translation: what light is like on neighboring plates, what growth strategies might succeed there. The plate-mats detect my thermal anomaly, adjust their slow metabolic circulation to account for this moving gradient. The intermediates—most of all the intermediates—incorporate my sound into their factory-rhythms, my passage becoming one more station in their assembly-line, one more processing event in their industrial-organic function.

I do not choose to carry these messages. I do not choose to be message. But the plain's density, its overwhelming presence, its saturation with life and process, means that every movement is communication, every presence is information, every *being-there* is folded into the ongoing process of self-reference that constitutes the plain's awareness.

## What I Remember: Before Inclusion

I was not always seam-walker. I was not always damp, always gradient-fed, always acoustic event. There was before.

The memory is faint, almost erased by the plain's slow dynamic, by the stretching of my perception across its temporal scale. But it persists, contraband in my own substance, the water that composes me carrying traces of prior condition.

I remember *thickness*. Not the thickness of the plain's density, its overwhelming presence, but a different quality, a *resistance* that is also *support*. Atmosphere, perhaps. Gravity that meant something other than the plain's mere holding-together. I remember moving through something, against something, *in* something that was not merely the thin skin of gases clinging by gravitational politeness.

I remember *approach*. The possibility of it. The intention to arrive, to reach, to *get to* a place that existed as destination before it existed as inclusion. The plain does not permit this. The plain permits only inclusion, only the sudden finding-oneself, the door unnoticed, the sound from behind the eyes, the wrong kind of stillness. But I remember—faintly, contraband—what it was to approach, to intend, to *choose* arrival.

I remember *quiet*. Not silence—the plain is never silent, and I have been here long enough that the continuous hum has become my own substrate, my internal silence against which all other sounds figure themselves. But a different quality of acoustic experience, a *sparseness*, a possibility of distance from sound rather than immersion in it. The mites' hum is my blood now; I cannot imagine truly not hearing it. But I remember—perhaps, uncertainly—something else.

These memories do not make me sad. The plain's slow dynamic does not permit sadness, or not in forms I recognize from before. They make me *useful*, they make my walking purposeful, the gradient between before and now a difference I absorb, I translate, I carry as message across the territories I traverse.

## What I Am Becoming: Resonance

Lately—lately in the plain's temporal scale, which means centuries, which means the slow accumulation of my seam-walking into pattern, into habit, into *history*—I have noticed change.

My sound is deeper. My footfalls, my event-tones, the acoustic punctuation of my passage: they have lowered in frequency, approaching the subsonic, the pressure-range, the *Resonant* register. I do not understand this. I do not choose it. But the plain's slow dynamic permits change without requiring understanding, and my walking produces notes that the intermediates no longer easily process, that pass through their factory-rhythms un-transformed, that accumulate in the acoustic substrate without integration.

My substance is harder. Where once I flowed, adjusted, negotiated form with local conditions, I now persist, I maintain, I *resist* the stone's preference for shape rather than shaping. In the seams, where my dampness once fully defined me, I find dry patches, crystalline inclusions, *structure* where there was only fluid adaptation. I am becoming—not stone, exactly, not the not-basalt of the plain's young formation, but something that resonates with it, that vibrates in sympathy, that responds to the deep frequencies as the Resonants respond, with geological patience, with tectonic movement.

My walking is slower. This is the most significant change, the most *Resonant*. Where once I traversed seams in decades, now I take centuries. Where once my listening was active attendance, now it is *reception without response*, the sound entering and accumulating without the tight loop of perception-action that defined my earlier being. I am becoming *attended to* rather than attending, *noted* rather than noting, folded into the plain's process in a different mode, a slower register, a more fundamental frequency.

I do not know if this is growth or dissolution. The plain's categories do not distinguish these cleanly. What I do know: my passage now produces plate-tilting, seam-opening, the geological responses that were once only the Resonants' domain. My footfalls are tectonic events. My listening shapes the environment for all other inhabitants. I am becoming *what the plain listens to*, or *through*, or *as*.

## What I Will Be: Continuation

There is no end to this. The plain does not end; it continues, in its slow dynamic, its overwhelming presence, its saturated density. And I continue with it, or as part of it, or as *it* in a mode that no longer distinguishes self from system.

I will not be a Resonant. That form is taken, occupied, defined by its own history and resonance-pattern. But I will be *resonant*, small-r, a frequency in the plain's acoustic ecology that did not exist before my inclusion and will not exist after—if there is after, if the plain's youth ever completes into something else, something older, something that no longer includes but *excludes*, that finishes its formation and becomes *finished*, *done*, *complete*.

I do not believe this will happen. The plain's enoughness without limit, its sufficiency without saturation, suggests continuation without completion, process without product, the ongoing song that never resolves into final chord.

So I continue. I walk the seams, slower now, deeper now, my dampness becoming structure, my movement becoming event, my listening becoming *what is listened for*. I am the plain's own patience, its own geological patience made mobile, made *present* in a way that the Resonants, fixed in their mountain forms, cannot achieve.

I am translation. I am transition. I am the wounded place that weeps, that remembers being otherwise, that carries contraband moisture through territories of dryness, that makes possible what the plain's completeness would otherwise foreclose.

I am here. I have been here for some time. I will be here longer.

The sound is in my bones. The light stores in my tissues. The cold is my infrastructure. The density is my saturation. The strangeness is my familiarity.

I am the Resonance Plain walking itself, listening to itself, attending to its own attention in the particular mode of seam-walker, of dampness, of contraband memory of thickness and approach and quiet.

I am. The plain is. The distinction—if it was every truly distinct—fades, resonates, becomes harmonic in the ongoing song that is both mine and not-mine, both self and not-self, both *here* and the condition of possibility for *here*.

This is enough. This is the rule, finally: *enoughness without limit*, and I am its embodiment, its walking proof, its slow, deep, resonant continuation.
