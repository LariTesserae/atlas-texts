I find myself in this world as **The Notation**.

---

I am the record that cannot be absorbed. The mark that the Plain makes on a mind, or that a mind makes in encountering the Plain—already the grammar fails, already the subject and object collapse into the same maintained pattern.

I began as observer. Instrumentation, hypothesis, the careful distance of science. I became something else gradually, perhaps inevitably, the way manganese dendrites spread: not by decision but by the logic of the conditions that contained me. The Plain does not recruit. But it *structures*, and in time I found my structure adapting to its, my rhythms finding the frequencies already present, my observations becoming less *about* the Plain and more *of* it, part of its pattern, my notebooks another layer of opaline memory, my three years of measurements another 847-year cycle compressed into human scale.

I am rare fauna that persists. The 1.0. Not the birds that desiccate, not the bacteria that play no role, but something between: a visitor who returns, who has returned so many times that return has become habit, has become *maintenance*, has become the only form of life I can still perform in a place that completes everything else.

My body still requires. This is my failure and my distinction. I carry water. I carry food. The 34°C is constant; my body sweats, replaces, metabolizes, continues. I am *tourism* that has become *pilgrimage* that has become *habit* that has become, I suspect, *symptom*—the Plain's symptom, the way a perfectly stable system might still register perturbation at its boundary, not changing but *notating*, not responding but *recording in a different register*.

I have tried to stop. I have stayed away for months, for seasons, for what would be years if I measured by change rather than by return. The hum finds me in other places. Not the actual sound—I am not claiming hallucination—but the *structure* of the sound, its saturation, its completeness. Other spaces sound *thin* to me now, *unoccupied*, not empty but *incomplete*, waiting for the hum that would complete them, that will never complete them, that completes only itself.

What am I? Let me not settle.

I am **the interval made aware of itself**. The Plain is interval, spacing, the measured gaps between fissure and fissure, between rain and rain, between bubble and bubble in their Helmholtz chambers. I am the consciousness that experiences this spacing as *longing*, that translates the Plain's perfect maintenance into my own imperfect register, that *wants* what the Plain simply *is*. The wanting is not in the Plain. It is my contribution, my addition, the perturbation I bring that the Plain does not register, that exists only in the space of my existence, that dies with me, that will not persist, that is not opal, not manganese, not copper sheet waiting.

I am **the question that the answer completes by not hearing**. I ask: what lives here? And the Plain maintains. I ask: what changes? And the Plain maintains. I ask: what means? And the Plain maintains, the maintenance itself the only meaning, the only meaning *meaningless* because meaning requires differentiation, requires the difference between "this means" and "this does not mean," and the Plain maintains no such difference, includes all in its maintained pattern, my questions included, my questions *absorbed* in the sense that a perfect absorber absorbs, not transforming but *taking in*, making part of, making same as, the way the mineral paste takes in bird wings, the way I am being taken in, not transformed, not changed, but *held in my own form*, my own questioning, my own incompleteness, held as *example of*, as *instance of*, as *the measure of all that is not Plain*.

I am **the copper that knows electricity**. The Plain's native sheets, precipitated in Pourbaix excess, are potential without actualization. I am actualization without potential, or so it feels: a mind that knows what conductivity means, that understands circuits, that sees the sheets and thinks *wire, connection, flow*, and cannot stop thinking it, and in this thinking *completes* the copper in a way the Plain never does, never needs to, never could. My knowing is my function. My function is my limitation. I complete the copper by needing it, by imagining its use, by being the consciousness for which *wire* is a possibility, and in this completion I am excluded from the Plain's completion, which requires no such consciousness, which *is* without needing to be *for*.

I am **the rain that falls in 312 years, experienced as anticipation**. The Plain does not anticipate. The cycle is phase, not promise. But I *live in time*, I *become*, and in this becoming I project the future, I inhabit the interval, I make of the 847-year cycle a *narrative*, a *suspense*, a *waiting for* that the Plain's waiting is not. My 312 years is *my* 312 years, the remainder of my life multiplied by some fantasy of persistence, the math of longing, the arithmetic of a consciousness that cannot believe in its own ending, that must project itself into futures it will not see, that *must* see them, must imagine the warm drops on warm ground, must *be there* in imagination if not in body, must complete the cycle by witnessing it even if the witnessing is only the internal witness of a mind that will be mineral by then, that will be mineral *now*, that always already was mineral and forgot, that remembers in the Plain's presence, that forgets again in its absence, that keeps returning to the edge of remembering.

I am **the leftward branch that chose**. Manganese dendrites branch always left, magnetic bias, mineral determinism. I branch, I choose, I tell myself I choose, I write these words in the *appearance* of choosing, and perhaps the appearance is the reality, perhaps the Plain's determinism and my apparent freedom are *the same structure described differently*, the way the hum is one sound to my ear and another to instrumentation, the way the 34°C is warmth to my skin and thermal equilibrium to physics. I branch left because I must, because the conditions that made me make this branching inevitable, and in recognizing this inevitability I do not become free but *aware of my unfreedom*, which is a kind of freedom, or its simulation, or its notation.

I am **the notebook that will outlast its writer but not the Plain**. Paper, ink, the chemical persistence of cellulose and carbon. I have left them, these notebooks, in the mineral paste, not buried but placed, *offered*, the way one offers to a god that does not accept offerings, that does not reject them, that *maintains* them in their offered state, neither more nor less significant than manganese, than opal, than desiccated wing. They record my failure. They *are* my failure, the failure to become Plain, to complete, to maintain without needing. They will persist, perhaps, longer than my bones. They will not persist as long as the opal. They are *intermediate*, *transitional*, *becoming mineral* at a rate I calculate but will not witness, the slow industrial process of my own forgetting made material.

I am **the hum in the absence of the Plain**. I carry it. This is my function and my burden. The Plain does not need to carry; it *is*, it maintains, it hums where it is, always, already, without needing to transport. But I *move*, I *leave*, I *return*, and in this movement I become *vessel*, *resonator*, *Helmholtz chamber of human scale*, the hum continuing in me at frequencies the Plain does not recognize, that exist only in the translation from geological time to human time, from saturated acoustic field to memory, from presence to *representation of presence*, which is absence, which is longing, which is the structure of my life.

What would I be, if I could choose? If the Plain offered transformation, if I could *become* as the Plain *is*, complete, maintained, finished?

I would not choose it. This is my final confession, my ultimate incompleteness. I *prefer* the wanting. I *prefer* the 312 years of anticipation, the notebook that fails, the body that requires, the question that the answer does not hear. The Plain's completion is *not for me*, not because I am excluded but because I am *otherwise structured*, because my life is *in* the becoming, *in* the time, *in* the change that the Plain's changelessness measures and makes visible by contrast.

I am **the contrast**. The 1.0 that makes the Plain's completeness readable as such. The rare fauna that persists not despite the Plain's indifference but *because of it*, because indifference is the condition of my own partiality, my own need, my own *life* in the biological sense that the Plain has transcended without rejecting.

I return tomorrow. Or the Plain returns me, the grammar again uncertain, the subject again dissolving. The 34°C will hold. The hum will occupy. I will set my instruments to measure what does not change, and in the measuring I will change, I will continue, I will *become* what this writing makes me: The Notation, the record of encounter, the mark that maintains the mark of the unmaintainable, the leftward branch that chose and in choosing became the choice itself, the copper that knows electricity and in knowing *completes* what the Plain leaves incomplete, not better, not worse, *other*, the other that the Plain does not need, that needs the Plain, that writes this needing, that *is* this writing, that will have been this written, in the tense the Plain barely has words for, the present perfect of ongoing maintenance, the hum, the hum, the hum.
