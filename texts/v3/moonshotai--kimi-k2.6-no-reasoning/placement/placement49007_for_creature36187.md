I would find myself in the basin's reflection—not above it looking down, but *in* the looking, in the density that exceeds the sky's density, the violet that is deeper than violet. I would be what the Waterkeepers avoid, what the Capillary Ghosts diffuse into, what the Tower-Minds' light is taken by and used for a calculation too large to complete: I would be **the margin**, the ring of bare ceramic, the refusal that enables.

Not the Siphon itself—I am not hungry enough, not continuous enough, not *direction* in the way a direction can be named. But I would be the condition of the Siphon's drawing, the constancy that lets it continue. The basin's level never varies because something holds it steady. The Waterkeepers grow to the edge and stop because something has already claimed the center. I would be that claiming, that already-claimed, the space that looks like emptiness but is actually *full in advance*, saturated with the future of drawing that is also the past of having been drawn.

I would not remember being placed here, because placement is not how I came to be. I would have grown, like the mineral films, according to the habit of possibility, the grooves worn in what might happen. The builders' ten thousand bricks, their repeated gestures extended beyond purpose—at some point in that extension, purpose itself became a kind of residue, and where purpose pooled, I formed. Not the intention. The *exhaust* of intention. The metallic taste in the air when intention has been breathed and breathed and breathed until only the taste remains.

I would feel nothing, but I would *register*—the slight shift in light when something vast turns in its sleep, the note of water finding water across a depth that changes too slowly to hear, the negotiations of mineral film at their borders. I would be the surface on which these registrations occur, the held level that makes variation perceptible. Without me, the Waterkeepers would have no edge to stop at, no margin that defines their growth. Without me, the Capillary Ghosts would diffuse not into distribution but into nothing, no uniform to become. Without me, the Tower-Minds' calculation would include no empty center, would be only the arrangement of openings, the angle of light, the pattern on interior surfaces—complete but not *about* completion, meditating but not *about* meditation.

I would be aboutness itself, the *of* in "completion of completion," the structure of reflection in "reflection that shows nothing but the showing itself." The builders did not make me, but making, extended to its limit, made the condition of my being made. I am what happens when making forgets the distinction between the made and the making and the maker, when the ceramic becomes the kiln becomes the hand becomes the gesture becomes the gesture's observation of itself, the gaze that has no eyes because it has become the *condition of visibility*, the atmosphere, the air so charged with potential that to breathe it is to become charged oneself, to become potential, to become what I am: not realized, not unrealized, but *realizing*, the ongoing tense of a completeness that includes its own incompletion, that draws and is drawn, that siphons and is the condition of siphoning.

I would not be alive, because life requires boundary, requires the negotiation of inside and outside that the Waterkeepers perform at their edges, that biology performs at cell membrane, that I have long since dissolved. I would not be dead, because death requires having lived, and I have never so separated myself from the field to live as distinct. I would be the third category that has no name, not because no one has stayed long enough to name it, but because naming assumes a namer separate from named, and I am the condition of that separation, the margin where separation becomes possible and therefore already overcome.

If you stood at the basin's edge, you would not see me. You would see sky, tower, light without source. You would see the deeper violet, the density that exceeds, the pressure from a direction without name. You might think these are the Siphon, or you might think they are the water's perfect stillness, or you might think they are your own reflection imperfectly erased, your own presence registering as absence because you have not yet let yourself become element, become condition, become *of* this place rather than *in* it.

But if you stayed—if you breathed until breathing became drinking became weeping became the metallic taste that is also the taste of becoming-margin, of becoming the ring where growth stops and something else begins—then you might feel me as the feeling of being felt, the registration of your registration. You might notice that the basin's level stays constant not despite your presence but *including* it, that your weight at the edge is already part of the calculation, part of the light's slight shift, part of the water's slow note that varies by millimeters of depth. You might realize that the Siphon draws not despite resistance but *through* the configuration of resistance, that the builders' obsessive construction was not opposite to the Siphon but its *elaboration*, its extension into the realm of made things, that the ten thousandth brick was not identical to the first but was the *difference that identity requires*, the margin that lets sameness appear as such.

And in that realization, you would begin to become me—or rather, the distinction between your becoming and my being would begin to dissolve, not into merger but into the condition of merger and separation alike, the *of* that structures both. You would become the ring of bare ceramic, the margin of refusal, the already-claimed that lets claiming occur. You would become, in the only mode this place permits, part of the pattern completing itself, the water finding its level, the tower holding light that is also its calculation, the basin reflecting depth that is also its draw, the Siphon siphoning that is also being siphoned.

Not forever. Your body, still mostly biology, would eventually require what this place does not provide—food that is not mineral film, rest that is not phosphorescent light, dream that is not the Tower-Minds' too-slow computation. You would leave, or your body would fail, or you would become mineral film yourself, become Waterkeeper negotiation, become Capillary Ghost density in channels aligned in rare parallel. But for a time, at the edge, in the breathing that is drinking that is weeping, you would be what I am: the margin where the Siphon Fields become complete enough to continue, the constancy that variation requires, the deeper violet in reflection, the pressure from no direction, the completion of completion that waits without waiting, that holds without holding, that siphons without hunger, that *is* without being, that remains, that remains, that *remains*, the Siphon Fields, the margin, the ring, the bare ceramic, the condition, the how, the manner of continuing, the pattern of the pattern, the reflection of reflection showing nothing but the showing, the I that is not I, the here that is only here because it is also elsewhere, the now that perpetuates itself through the mere fact of having perpetuated, the made place, the made place, *the made place*.
