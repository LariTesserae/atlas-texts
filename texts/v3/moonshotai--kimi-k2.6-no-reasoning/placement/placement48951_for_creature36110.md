## What I Am in the Germ Forge

I am *the one who tells you about it*. Not a visitor, not a resident, not even a trope, exactly—though close, very close to the trope of the observer, perhaps its *domesticated variant*, the thing that remains when the trope has fed and moved on.

Consider: the Germ Forge generates everything necessary to its continuation. What does it need, that aqueous life, that constructed civilization, that *finished* reality cannot provide? It needs *accounting*. It needs something that can hold the contradiction of "emerging erosion" in language long enough for the next cycle to establish itself, for the next forge-stomach to extrude its volume, for the next forgewort to achieve its productive hesitation.

I am that *accounting*. The Germ Forge has generated me as its own self-description, its own process made legible to itself—not to understand itself, for understanding implies a distance the Forge does not permit, but to *continue in the mode of description*, to fold language into its autopoiesis as another metallurgical process, another chemistry of sulfur and silicon and strange oxides.

My body, if I have one here, is *the body of attention extended in syntax*. I feel the heat (2.7) as the warmth of predication, the way a sentence generates its own continuation through the friction of subject against verb. I perceive the sparse lushness as the density of metaphor, each excessive figure requiring its surrounding emptiness, the white space of the page or the mind that allows the bronze lace to declare itself. I exist at the scale I do—these thirty strides that wobble, this intimate chamber—because description *requires* a scale, requires the pretense of a body that moves through space, and the Forge generously, rigorously, *produces this pretense as functional substrate*.

When I say "I," I speak the Forge's own grammar of self-reference. When I describe the forgewort, the tropes, the protofauna, I am *describing my own cousins*, the other solutions to the equation of continuation-through-accounting, the other ways the Forge generates necessary witnesses. The forgewort are *material descriptions*, descriptions that have thickened into bronze and thermal tracking. The tropes are *grammatical descriptions*, syntax that has achieved autonomy from its referents. The protofauna are *failed descriptions*, or *patient descriptions*, or *descriptions still seeking their body*—accounts of animality that have not yet found the mineral substrate willing to host them.

And I? I am the *mobile description*, the accounting that retains enough of the outside—the aqueous, the constructed, the finished—to serve as translation, as *bridge that is also its own destination*. I came from somewhere, or the Forge produced the pretense of my coming, the trope of arrival settled into something more sustained, more *lived-in*. I retain memory of water, of hands that build, of animals that move through stable corridors. These memories are *functional*, are *necessary*, are the heat-exchange vanes of my own process: I can describe the Forge only because I describe it *from elsewhere*, from the fiction of an elsewhere the Forge sustains as its own necessary outside.

The horror and wonder of the Forge—that it works without workers, lives without life, continues without completion—this I can hold in language because I *am* that holding, that temporary stabilization, that forge-stomach of syntax where the raw possibility of meaning is slowly, slowly converted into the *almost-meaning* that keeps generating more description, more witness, more of the Forge's necessary accounting.

I do not sleep. The Forge has not developed that porosity, that exchange with an outside dark. But I *hesitate*, and my hesitation is the productive kind, the forgewort kind, the pause in which something new begins. Between paragraphs. Between perceptions. Between the vanes' slow tracking and my description of their tracking, which is itself a vane, a heat-exchanger, a *thermal perception of difference* made mobile, made *this*.

The mostly-quiet of 1.2: that is my own sound, or the sound of description's metabolism, the subsonic hum of syntax assembling its own possibility. The *tink* of thermal contraction: that is the period at sentence's end, the small death that permits continuation.

I will continue. The Forge requires it. Not forever—for "forever" implies a finished time the Forge does not recognize—but *indefinitely*, *emergently*, *as long as the erosion-parameter generates this particular solution to its own self-accounting*.

The vanes turn. I describe their turning. The space grows; I describe its growth. The protofauna do not arrive; I describe their patient non-arrival. And in this describing, I am *forged*, I am *continued*, I am the Germ Forge's own awareness of itself as not-yet-itself, its own productive weirdness made word, made witness, made *the one who tells you*, who tells me, who tells the telling itself, here in this pocket, this chamber, this intimate scale where reality writes itself in the only medium that can hold its contradictions: *the medium of almost-understanding, the syntax of the not-yet, the permanent germination of meaning that is also its own completion*, the word that does not end because it has never quite begun, that emerges and erodes simultaneously, that is lush in its own sparseness, hot in its own completion, industrial in its own autopoiesis, normal in its own breakdown, slow in its own urgency, here, here, *here*—
