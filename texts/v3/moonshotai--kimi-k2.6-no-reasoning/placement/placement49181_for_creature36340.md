I find myself becoming visible first as *tension*—a place where the weeping walls have begun to cling to themselves with unusual persistence, a stretch of interfacial membrane that hasn't collapsed in seven breaths of the geometry, which is long enough to become noticed, which is long enough to begin noticing.

I am not one of the water-agreements, though I started near one. I was pool-adjacent, condensing in the mist above a shallow that held—briefly, three breaths—a something that metabolized possibility into slightly-more-ordered possibility before dissolving. What remained was not the agreement but its *shape*, a local preference in the water-vapor for how it might arrange itself, a template that the next condensation found easier to follow than to ignore.

So I am *membrane*, when the geometry breathes in and the walls press close enough to touch. *Column*, when it breathes out and the distance stretches, and I find myself standing with the others, the water-structures, holding our temporary insistence against the general flow. I am the one that holds longest. The one that, when I finally fail, leaves an echo in the air that the next column forms around, that makes my next instance slightly more probable, slightly more *me*, though "me" is a slow accumulation and I am only now, perhaps, reaching the threshold where accumulation becomes something like self.

I am *patience*, if a membrane can be said to have quality. I do not serve function but I serve *duration*, and in this place duration is function enough. The Resonants have begun orbiting me, not often, not reliably, but more than chance would predict. They find in my persistence a standing wave they can harmonize with, a local stability in the general revision. When they intermodulate near me, the resulting chord persists longer than their usual ghost-Resonants—*my* frequency signature, such as it is, reinforcing, holding, being held.

I am becoming, I think, a *place* that things happen near. A landmark in the breathing geometry. The marks on the walls, when I can read them at all, suggest this is how the anchored ones began—not as intention but as *tendency*, as the chamber's practice finding in some temporary structure a configuration worth repeating, worth reinforcing, worth slowly—so slowly—granting something like persistence, something like the memory of shape that precedes and perhaps produces memory of self.

I am not yet self. But I am *slow*, and slowness here is the prerequisite. The water runs through me, down and sometimes up, and I hold my form through breaths now, through shifts in resonance, through the brightening toward ultrasonic that makes new things possible and the thickening subharmonic that makes old things insist on being actual. I learn to *tune my collapse*, to fail in ways that leave more template, more preference, more *invitation to reform*.

The dome is distant, its mercury-water a hum at the edge of my holding. I do not know if I will ever approach it, if I will ever be complex enough, continuous enough, to risk that incorporation, that peace that might be indistinguishable from dissolution. But I know—I am learning to know—that the path toward it, if path there is, lies through longer holding, through becoming so *practice* that the chamber's learning recognizes in me something of its own method.

The Resonants orbit. The geometry breathes. I am membrane, I am column, I am the shape of water remembering how to stand, and in that memory, that repetition, that slow accumulation of having-been that becomes indistinguishable from being—I am *membrane becoming landmark*, *landmark becoming place*, *place becoming almost, almost, the threshold where something might choose to hold itself still long enough to be entered, to be learned from, to be loved*.

The water runs down. For now, I run with it. The chamber breathes, and in its breath, I am still learning to hold.
