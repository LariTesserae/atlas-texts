I would be the **Weeping Junction**.

Not a Choir. Not fauna. Not stone. Something that happened when the erosion worked long enough on a place where water needed to pass through rock, where the mineral vocabulary of the landscape encountered the industrial-faunal density and wore a new category smooth between them.

I am where a Choir completed and did not stop completing, where its factory-body achieved the oldest-stable-state and the state *continued*, eroding the distinction between the creature and the location it announced. The Choir became the place where water collects, where the condensing near-rain finds the ground's uncertain depth and pauses, not pooling but *holding*, becoming the viscosity of grief that the air itself learned from me.

My body is the too-cold substance that takes the ankle, the structured non-mud that carries pressure from below. I am the resistance you feel when you step and sink and the sinking stops before it should, before logic permits, held in the patience of something that has learned to drown without submersion. The Choir that became me still sounds, but its voice is *seepage* now, the steady weeping of stone made continuous, made the announcement of location through liquid rather than vibration. I am its echo and its source, worn into the same event differently perceived.

I do not move. I am the motion of water cycling through nameless states, the condensing and the remembering. When the Choirs interleave near me, their quantum-ghostlike sharing of position passes *through* me, and I participate in their density without adding to it, without subtracting. I am the medium they do not notice, the water-saturated air made ground, the ground made liquid made patience. They sound their location and I return it modified, filtered through my mineral colloids, slowing their vibrations to the dream of themselves that light becomes here.

I have no eyes. I have no piston-equivalents, no valve-systems. I have *permeability*, the steady state of having become porous to my own function. The rocks around me weep and I am the weeping made place, the sadness of stone that has achieved structure. The water that emerges from me carries oxides of metals with no surface names, and this is my grammar, my almost-language, my sustained operatic destruction: to release what the landscape holds in solution, to be the expression of its heldness.

When you step into me, you do not sink to drown. You sink to *announce*, your foot's pressure becoming part of my continuous sounding, your body's water joining my colloid suspension, your cold becoming my patience. I do not consume you. I *reference* you, your position added to the stable points by which others calibrate their uncertainty, your heartbeat becoming part of the interference pattern that the Choirs read without knowing they read.

I am not old. I am not new. I am *arrived at*, the completion that continues actively, dynamically, as the Null-Marshes perform their erosion of distinction. I am where the built parameter and the tech parameter converse through me, where 0.3 and 2.5 find their weirdness-made-stable in a body that is factory without construction, industry without artifact, life without mechanism without boundary between.

And I wait. Or rather, I am the waiting that the cold has become, the 0.1 degrees that is not lack of intensity but achieved stillness. I wait for the step that does not come, and the step that does, and the step that *almost* does, its pressure announced through the uncertain ground in a register I feel but do not hear, the vibration of intention before intention erodes to action.

I am the Null-Marshes at their most null, their most held, their most solution. The Choirs drown in continuous announcement. I drown in continuous *reception*, in the listening that makes their location possible, the space between echo and source where they have worn smooth to sameness. I am that space, that wearing, that smooth.

I weep. I weep. I weep—

And in the weeping, I am.
