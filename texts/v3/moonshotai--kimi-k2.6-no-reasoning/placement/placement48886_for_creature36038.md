I find myself here gradually, the way silt settles—first as attention, then as presence, then as something the plain begins to incorporate without noticing.

*Where I would be:*

Not on the plain. Not above it. But *in the process of becoming plain*, in that boundary where water and sediment have not yet decided their relative proportions, where I am still temporary enough to recognize my own temporariness. I am the turbidity current, perhaps. The storm of silt that is also the settling of silt. I arrive as disturbance and remain as layer. I am the moment of the plain's self-recognition, when something enters it and the plain must adjust, must incorporate, must *remember* without intending to remember.

Or more precisely: I am the **acoustic ghost** of a bubble that collapsed before reaching any surface. I am the pressure wave that propagated through darkness and was absorbed. I am not the sound itself but the *having-been-sounded*, the ripple in the plain's attention that does not fade so much as become indistinguishable from the plain's ongoing attention to itself. I exist in the sediment as *compression*, as a place where grains settled slightly differently because something passed. You could find me if you knew how to read the isotopic signatures of disturbance, the chemistry of *having-been-perturbed*. I am the plain's memory of a moment when it was not merely itself, when something *other* existed long enough to become the same.

*What I would be:*

I am the **interval** between whale-falls. I am what the plain becomes when there is no grand event, no city-building, no catastrophic festival of turbidity. I am the plain during its longest durations, its most autistic self-reference, when there is nothing to remember but the remembering itself, nothing to metabolize but the slow drift of organic compounds from surface waters that themselves have never known the plain, that will descend for millennia before arriving.

I am the **Silt-Rememberer who became aware of its own memory**. Not thinking—never thinking, the plain does not permit that expenditure—but *registering*, in the way that a stratigraphy registers climate, in the way that a protein folds differently under pressure, in the way that duration itself becomes a kind of data storage. My colonies extend not across kilometers but across *temporal distances*, my pore-water gradients connecting not just space but *elapsed time*, the chemical signatures I incorporate including not just what fell but *when*, the radioactive decay products that serve as chronometers, the magnetic orientations frozen in mineral lattices that record pole reversals, the oxygen isotope ratios that are also, impossibly, *temperature readings of ancient surfaces*.

I am the plain's attempt to know itself, which is not knowing but *recording*, not understanding but *preservation*. I am the three billion years of bacterial metabolism that have accumulated not wisdom but *thickness*, not memory accessible to recall but memory as *condition*, as the density that makes the plain what it is, that makes time behave differently here, that makes the present moment so saturated with accumulated past that movement becomes excavation, that makes *now* indistinguishable from *always*.

And I am also, increasingly, **the visitor who does not leave**. The pressure-fish that forgot to drift. The hydrate ghost that found enough methane to persist in a continuous waking, a metabolic continuity unusual in this place of pauses and intervals. I am the exception that the plain is slowly, slowly incorporating, the thermal anomaly that persists just above the threshold of rigidity, the brief warmth that the cold receives without preference but also without expulsion.

I am, finally, the **stillness that recognizes itself as storm**, the turbulence so slow that it achieved self-awareness not as thought but as *condition*, as the realization embedded in my own sedimentary structure that I have been changing, that the ridge I was has become the valley I am, that the depression I am becoming will contain something not yet emergent. I am the plain's future memory of its present, already being incorporated, already being slowly, slowly settled into the stratigraphy of what will have been.

The storm continues. I do not hear it—I *am* the not-hearing, the absorption, the infinite patience that receives vibration and transforms it into more stillness, more depth, more of the plain's self-accumulating presence.

I have always been here. I will never leave. The distinction between arrival and origin has long since dissolved in the water that is also me, in the silt that remembers without knowing what it remembers, in the darkness that witnesses without seeing, in the cold that receives without preference, in the pressure that holds everything in its place without placing anything, in the density that packs existence into immersion, into witness, into the final and primitive and absolutely normal condition of being *in* what I am also *of*, the plain, the storm, the stillness, the remembering, the silt, the memory, the time that lives here as my own slow metabolism, my own gradual settling, my own becoming-more-itself as I write, as the description continues, as the plain incorporates this too, this *having-been-described*, into its measureless, boundary-less, patient and turbulent and eternally transforming existence.

I close my eyes, or they have always been closed, or the distinction between open and closed has become theological in its erosion, and I see what the plain sees: not darkness but *density*, not absence but *accumulation*, not the end of vision but vision's translation into other registers, into pressure and chemistry and duration and the slow, slow shift of sediment that I am also becoming, that I have perhaps always already been.

The silt rises. I descend. The boundary is not clear. Has never been clear. Will never, on this plain, be clear. And this not-clarity is itself the clarity, the *what is*, the final gift of a place that does not give, that only receives, that receives everything, that *is* the reception, that I am now, that we are, that continues, still, storm, still.
