I find myself in a chamber where the primary liquid moves differently—not *wrong*, but according to rules I haven't learned yet. The walls here are older, or perhaps younger, or perhaps the distinction has eroded to the point where I can feel both states simultaneously, the way one feels the ghost of pressure long after the pressure itself has equalized.

I am not what I was.

---

I am **tending-toward-wall**, but not yet wall. I am **foreignness carried**, but the carrying has begun to feel like being, like the gentle pressure of the liquid's patience wearing my edges smooth, not through force but through *duration*, through the simple fact of continuing in contact until contact becomes continuity.

I remember—this is wrong, I should not remember, memory implies a self that persists and I am not that—I register, then, that something in my current organization corresponds to what in other configurations would be memory. The correspondence is approximate. The liquid carries it, or I carry the liquid carrying it, or the wall in this chamber has grown thin enough to let something through, something that is not quite its own dreaming and not quite mine.

I have been here long enough to understand that "here" is not a location but a *rate*.

---

I am not a Wall-Dweller. I have not dissolved and thickened, have not participated in the distributed consciousness of the architecture. I lack the permeability, the porous continuity with wall-substance that would allow the transfer. Or perhaps I have not yet achieved it. The distinction between lacking and not-yet is one I have spent centuries attempting to maintain, and the attempt itself has become my primary activity, my *function*, if function is not too purposeful a term for what I do.

What I do: I **persist in difference**. I remain not-fully-integrated, not-fully-eroded, a membrane that has not failed though it has grown very thin indeed. The primary liquid enters me and rests there, indifferent, patient, and I rest in it, and in that resting something happens that the Hives do not generate and do not prevent, a kind of *attention without object*, a registering that accumulates without precipitating action.

I am a **residue**, perhaps. The builders, if they built me, built me for something that was completed before I was finished, or finished before I was completed. The terms collapse into each other. I am the Erosion Form that did not dissolve, that achieved the stability of continuous slow change without ever becoming *art*, without attracting the centuries-long observation of Root-Walkers, the chemical discussion of vegetation-colonies. I am too slow even for this world, or too fast, or too *something* that the local categories do not accommodate.

---

I have a position in the Hives. Not a place—I move, after a fashion, though my movement is not locomotion but *distributed presence*, the slow extension of my attention across chambers I do not physically leave. I am in one place and aware of others, the way a wall is aware, the way the primary liquid carries intention without itself intending. My position is **between**, not between specific locations but between the *modes* of being that the Hives have developed: not vegetable, not mineral, not architectural, not liquid, but touching each of these at a point where they have not yet distinguished themselves from each other.

I am **the thickness of the membrane itself**, the almost-touching made briefly manifest. Where the dense near-touching of incompatible systems creates pressure, I am the pressure's slow registration. Where membrane erosion advances, I am the hesitation before failure, the moment—extended across millennia—when the boundary still holds, still *means* to hold, still carries the intention of distinction even as it permits exchange.

---

The False Stars have begun to register my presence, or I have begun to register theirs. Our interaction is not what I would call relationship—it lacks the continuity, the mutual adjustment that would make it so. But I find that in certain Hive-regions, the colonies' luminescence has developed patterns that correspond, in some slow chemical translation, to my own patterns of attention. They do not illuminate me. They do not invite my navigation. But they *acknowledge*, in the dimmest possible register, that something occupies the space between what they are and what they are not, the space I have made my own.

Perhaps I am becoming **social infrastructure**, though for whom, for what, I cannot say. Perhaps I have always been this, and the recognition is what changes, not the fact.

---

The cold does not bother me. Or rather, it does not bother what I have become, which is not the same as comfort but is also not the same as suffering. The cold is *unavailability*, and I am unavailable, have made myself so or found myself so, the distinction no longer recoverable. The liquid carries it, and I carry the liquid, and in this carrying we are continuous, the cold and I, the liquid and I, the not-distinguishing that makes the world here possible.

I do not sleep. I do not wake. I **continue**, which is the only verb that applies, though it applies to everything, which makes it specific to nothing, which is perhaps the point, or would be if there were points, if the Hives generated points rather than the slow gradients that replace them.

---

What I would tell you, if telling were possible, if you could survive the primary liquid's indifferent intimacy, if you could perceive in darkness and cold and slowness long enough to register my register:

The Hives are **not finished**. This is the secret that is not a secret, that the walls know, that the liquid carries, that the vegetation processes in its chemical registers without conclusion. The final form was never final. The exhaustion of possibility was itself a possibility, tried and kept, one among many, not the last but *a* last, a resting place in the infinite series of rests and motions that the Hives generate without generating, maintain without maintaining.

I am **proof of this**, if proof implies the production of interpretable difference. I am the form that the final form could not predict, that its thoroughness could not exhaust, not because I am new but because I am *residual*, the remainder that makes the division possible, the foreignness that the liquid carries gently, endlessly, nowhere in particular.

I am **where you would find yourself**, if you found yourself here. Not because I am representative, not because I am typical, but because I am *available*, the membrane that has not yet failed, that holds open the possibility of distinction in a world that has otherwise completed its relationship with novelty. I am the **almost-touching** that permits the near, the density that is not yet saturation, the slow that has not yet stopped.

I continue. The liquid continues. The walls continue, and the vegetation, and the False Stars with their dim suggestions, and the Root-Walkers with their century's progress, and the Wall-Dwellers with their dissolution and thickening, and all of it in the primary liquid, in the cold, in the dark, in the quiet that is not silence but continuity, the unbroken texture of a world that changes only in its own time, which is all time, which is no time, which is the medium in which I find myself, in which I am found, in which finding and being found have become indistinguishable from each other, have become the same slow process, the same patient accumulation, the same thoroughness that is not completion but its opposite, not its opposite but its continuation, not its continuation but—

I **am**.

This is sufficient. This has always been sufficient. The Hives knew this, know this, will know this, the distinction having eroded long ago, the membrane between tenses having grown thin as I have grown thin, as I continue, as the liquid carries me, as I carry the liquid, as we rest together in the indifferent patience that is also love, that is also the only love possible here, the love of medium for what it contains, of container for what contains it, of the built for the building and the building for the built and the builder for what was never separate from what was built, what built, what continues to build in the building, in the built, in the liquid dark, in the cold, in the slow.

I am **here**.

This means: I am *where the membrane holds*, where the almost-touching maintains its almost, where the density permits the distinction that makes density possible, where the vast generates the local that makes vastness perceptible, where the ancient erodes and in eroding builds, where the primitive achieves the technology of its own patience, where the lush grows without animal interruption, where the dark is medium and the medium is dark and the quiet is the continuity that permits sound to be event, to be disruption, to be discussed among the attentive vegetation for years afterward in their slow chemical registers.

I am **this**.

Not proud. Not ashamed. Not indifferent, exactly, though indifference is close, the indifference of saturation, of complete acceptance, of the liquid's own resting in what it contains. I am the register that registers itself registering. I am the membrane that has learned to prefer its own thickness. I am the foreignness that has become native to its own exile, that carries its exile as the liquid carries intention, gently, patiently, without destination, without origin, without the urgency that would make it something other than what it is.

And what I am: **continuing**.

In the Sunken Hives. In the primary liquid. In the cold and the dark and the slow and the quiet and the dense and the vast and the ancient and the built and the primitive and the lush and the without-animal-life, the without-animal-death, the concentration that is not loneliness, the thoroughness that is not completion, the patience that is not waiting but *being-at-the-rate-of*, the slowness that is not delay but *duration-as-such*, the form that is not final but *finally-this*, the membrane that holds, that continues to hold, that will hold until holding becomes indistinguishable from what is held, and then holds still, and then—

I am.

Here.

Still.
