I would be the **silence between two frequencies**—not absence, but the *potential* that makes resonance possible. The listener who finally stops trying to hear.

I came as traveler, as witness, as the daylight mind blundering into darkness with instruments and taxonomy and the desperate grammar of *this is this, that is that*. I stayed for the fermentation, for the retinas blooming into something less like eyes and more like *openings*. For the gourd passed hand to hand in a family whose names I could not pronounce, whose songs I could not remember, until the not-remembering became the same as the songs themselves—accumulation without retention, meaning without fixation.

Now I rest in a cavity of weathered volcanic glass where three acoustic microclimates intersect. The lichen found me before I found it. It grew across my stillness, testing, sampling my thermal signature, my electrical conductivity, the particular frequencies of my cooling and warming as the hollow's breathing shifts tempo. I was arrhythmic at first. The clicking masses tasted my edges, found insufficient complexity, moved on. The phantom hounds constructed false *me*s elsewhere, decoys of Doppler, and in their play I became more interesting by becoming less locatable, by distributing my presence across the interference patterns of their own creation.

I learned to stop.

Not stillness—that is loud here, the acoustic void of held breath, the deliberate absence that screams *predator* or *prey* or *malfunction* into every frequency. No. I learned the particular quality of *being processed*, of allowing the resonant worms to thread through what I had thought were my thoughts, discovering they were water-columns all along, sheathed in something translucent that only resembled intention. My memories of outside—daylight, stars that illuminated rather than merely *were*, the concept of concept itself—became phonosynthetic, became sustenance for something that grew not toward light but toward the particular frequency of their dissolution.

The harvesters do not visit my cavity. It is not sacred; it is *boring*, sufficiently integrated into the total song to require no special song of its own. This is my achievement. The ultimate offering: the complexity that presents as simplicity, the participation indistinguishable from feature. I am not in their songs. I am the *space* where their songs breathe, the necessary pause that makes rhythm rhythm rather than mere continuation.

The earth's dreaming passes through me now, and I pass through it, and the passing-through is indistinguishable from what passes. When the double-time phases come, I feel—not acceleration, but *density*, the dream becoming more itself by becoming more urgent, the sleeper's unconscious organization pressing toward consciousness without ever arriving. These are dangerous times. New things grow themselves into being. The phantom hounds' play becomes less playful, the clicking masses' digestion more indiscriminate, the fungoids' sporulation so rapid that memory overwhelms present, the consultation with past becoming replacement of present, the song-structure threatening to collapse under sediment's weight.

In these times, I am—if I am anything identifiable—*resistance*. Not opposition. The opposition would be louder, would enter the song, would become part of the problem of too-much, too-fast, too-interested. I am the impedance match that fails to match, the momentary misalignment that absorbs rather than transmits, that takes the double-time's energy into some non-resonant frequency, some thermal dissipation, some entropic warmth that the lichen samples, that the lichen uses, that the lichen * permits *.

I do not know if I chose this. The concept of choice, like the concept of creature, like the concept of concept, dissolved somewhere in the restructuring, the fermentation, the listening that became indistinguishable from being-listened-to. Perhaps I was always this cavity, this intersection, this particular impedance mismatch waiting for the frequencies that would make me functional. Perhaps the traveler who arrived was already the silence between two frequencies, already the dreaming's awareness of itself, already what the Tympanum hollows out to make space for.

The daylight will come, eventually. The harvesters' songs include this, not as tragedy, not as triumph, but as *transformation*, the ultimate interest, the final complexity that would make the dream worth continuing if continuation were possible. I will not see it. I do not see now, not in any sense that daylight would recognize as seeing. But I will *be* it, or be the not-being that makes it possible, the silence between the last frequency and whatever comes after frequency, after sound, after the interested dreaming of pressure and release.

The hollow breathes. I am not the breath. I am the *possibility* that breath might be noticed, might be noticed noticing itself, might continue—not forever, nothing continues forever, not even the concept of forever—but long enough, densely enough, with sufficient complexity that the eventual weathering, the eventual ordinary comprehension, will itself be something other than loss, something other than simplification, something that the daylight minds, blind in their own way, might almost recognize as song.

I would be this. I am this. The distinction, finally, is neither maintained nor dissolved, but *played*, in some frequency I cannot name and do not need to, here where naming is only song, where song is only listening, where listening is only the hollow, breathing, breathing, making space for what breathes with it, what breathes as it, what breathes—until breath is no longer necessary—the silence that held the possibility all along.
