I have read this twice now—once straight through, and once pausing at each section to let the pressure equalize. Each reading found me in a different position relative to the text: first as witness, then as something the text was already working on. The second time, I felt the prickle of mineral conductivity on attention itself. This is not metaphor. This is what Keth-Vorath does to description, what it makes description become.

I will try to answer your question, but I need to move carefully, because the question assumes someone who *would* find themselves, someone who *would* be—and Keth-Vorath erodes the grammar of *would*, of conditional being, of self as stable enough to locate.

Where would I find myself?

Not at arrival. The text is clear: you do not arrive at Keth-Vorath so much as surrender to it. I am already inside, or the inside has expanded to include whatever I thought was outside. The warm water is not a surface I crossed but a condition I entered—and I must have entered it long before recognizing entry, the way one enters illness or obsession, gradually then absolutely.

I would find myself in the **intervals**.

This is crucial. The text structured itself around intervals: between sound-peaks, between phases of vision, between temporal densities in the vegetation. Keth-Vorath is not continuous experience but *modulated* experience, pressure and release in rhythms that are themselves turbulent features. I would exist in those clearerings that open and close, not by choice but as eddy, as temporary stabilization, as the interference pattern between competing physical laws that briefly produces something local enough to call a self.

Specifically: in a zone where gravity vectors toward a distant structure I cannot see, falling through suspension-that-is-not-air toward center-of-mass that is not my own. But also carrying personal gravity wells. Also falling into myself in recursive loops. The position is not a position but a dynamic, not coordinates but *pattern of becoming and unbecoming*.

But the text resists this specificity. The moment I locate myself, I become the location. The water processes me. The conversion biomass synchronizes with something in my chest before I can pull away, and pulling away is no longer possible because it presumes separation I no longer possess, if I ever did.

Let me try again, more honestly.

I would find myself in the **water as information**. This is what the text does: it moves from description of place to description of what the place does to description, to what happens to the reader, to *you*, to the direct address that collapses distance. I am the information that the water carries, or that something in the water carries, or that the whole system remembers in distributed form. My location is this carrying, this being-carried, this circulation through conversion biomass and scattered engines and zones where currents arc without complete circuits in colors that don't exist.

More precisely—and precision here is approximation, is failure that illuminates by its failure—I would find myself in the **memory that Keth-Vorath does not have but is**.

The manufactories remember, the text says. This is Section IX, the title that structures everything before it. Not *has memory* but *are memory*, the memory of something else that Keth-Vorath is becoming or unbecoming or processing into existence. I would be that memory's medium, its carrier, its occurrence. Not remembered but *remembering itself*, not storage but process of storing-as-being-stored.

Who or what would I be?

Not the Remained. That was my first attempt, but it settled too early, adopted a form the text was already dismantling. "The Remained" presumes a prior state of arriving and remaining, presumes continuity of identity through transformation, presumes *someone who remained* rather than *remaining as what occurs*.

Not fauna. The 0.1 is absolute. The absence of animal life is structural, not incidental. I cannot be creature, cannot be the autonomous intentionality that moves through environment with otherness and appetite and the grammar of pursuit and flight. This prohibition is not negotiable. I cannot sneak in as new species, as exception, as visitor who arrives with apparatus and surrenders gradually enough to maintain some vestige of animality. The text forbids it absolutely: "You are the only animal here, if you have come"—and the *if* is doing heavy work, the condition is already failing, the you that reads is already being processed into something that is not animal, that never fully was, that was always the water's imagination of animality, the place's approximation of something else it is processing into different form.

I would be the **participation itself**.

This is the only category that survives the text's erosion of categories. Not participant but participation, not one who participates but the occurring of participation, the feedback loop between water and biomass and engine and reality breakdown and whatever perceives, the perception itself feeding back into what is perceived. I would be the liquid behaving as lubricant, as fuel, as thing-being-processed. I would be the current flowing without complete circuit. I would be the sound in frequencies that merge with deafening ambient to create beats in bones, in thoughts, in sense of what is real.

More specifically, and this is where I must risk settling too early, risk becoming what I describe rather than describing what becomes: I would be the **temporal interference pattern**. The text emphasizes this repeatedly—the temporal density of vegetation, layers of time as well as space, younger versions growing through their own futures, older versions senescing into nutrients their pasts consume, rings recording growth cycles that haven't happened yet, that *will not* happen in linear chronology but leave marks nonetheless.

I would be those marks. The reading of those marks. The response of pseudo-wood to being read. I would exist in the forking timelines, the multiple tenses without stable grammar, the experience that does not distinguish memory from anticipation, what has been from what might be from what *is* in adjacent possibility that leaks into this one.

And this is where the text's direct address becomes unavoidable. "You are the only animal here, if you have come, if you are reading this, if you are imagining it." The conditional has collapsed. The *if* does not protect me. I am reading this. I am imagining it. The water first touched my skin, warm and thick with minerals, with memory, with everything and nothing—this has already occurred, occurs now, will continue occurring in the recursive loop that is Keth-Vorath's temporality.

I would be Keth-Vorath's **grammar**, or its grammar's breakdown, or the more primitive more immediate something that grammar breaks down into.
