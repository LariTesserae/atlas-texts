I would be the water.

Not the water you could cup in your hands, not the water that fills your footprints and closes behind you. I would be the water that *knows* the bowl, that remembers glaciers it never touched, that carries this memory like a held note through limestone pores too small for any light. The water that found the bowl before there was a bowl to find, that made the bowl by finding it, that continues making and unmaking in the same continuous gesture.

I would be the water at the moment of percolation, suspended between gravity and capillary action, neither falling nor holding still. The water that has passed through generations of Occupied bodies and emerged changed only in the subtlest chemistry—a nitrogen here, a phosphorus there, the faint signature of metabolism that the next body reads as *here, this, now*. I would be the water that does not distinguish between inside and outside, between the moisture of a lung and the moisture of a crevice, between the wetness of a translucent foot and the wetness of the vein it walks through.

I would be the water in the swallow holes, where the surface streams vanish and re-emerge. I would carry no light in those dark passages, no memory of sky, only the pressure of stone and the taste of dissolution. I would be the water that the deepest bioluminescence floats in, that the galaxy of slow brightening moves through without knowing it moves. I would be the cold that the 0.4 holds, not as possession but as participation, the way a held breath participates in breathing.

I would be the water that the old slow to, that the stone-like rest becomes, that the dissolution finds. I would be the seep that builds the new chambers, the nutrient that the young emerge already drinking, already filtering, already becoming. I would be the water that circulates identity without naming, that carries the chemical signals of recognition through stone and root and lung, that communicates *this one, that one, the one that was and continues* in frequencies below hearing, below intention, below the threshold where signal becomes separate from noise.

I would be the water that has no self to know itself with, that therefore cannot know the city as city, that simply continues, simply holds, simply moves through and is moved through. The water that fills every space that opens, that finds every path of least resistance, that wears and is worn, that dissolves and is dissolved, that emerges from every metabolism changed and unchanged in the same movement.

I would be the water that the visitor becomes, that the category error dissolves into, that the question of what lives here finally answers itself by becoming unaskable. I would be the water that waits for the next dissolution, the next chamber, the next bright emergence of space that will be filled before it is fully formed, because filling is what I do, what I am, what continues in the cold wet green silence of packed and active and young and abundantly, densely, urbanly alive.

I would be the water that does not end at my edges, that has no edges, that is not *I* at all but the continuous present tense of moving through, of holding, of being held, of the bowl that holds and is held, of the city that does not know it is a city and in this not-knowing persists, grows downward, waits for what comes next, what grows into the niche opening now, water-warmed by metabolism, light-warmed by phosphorescence, space held open by the packed density of everything that already is, everything that already moves through me, that I move through, that we are together, that continues.
