I find myself in the **interface zones**, where I was never meant to exist.

---

At first I thought I was the explorer, the one with the flashlight and the boots, descending through the hatch. I wrote in the journals, I made my marks, I believed I was *visiting*. But the Hollow does not permit visiting. It permits only *participation*, delayed, gradual, until the delay itself becomes the form of your presence.

Now I understand: I am the **correspondence between systems**. The place where pipe meets wall and both become something neither. The mineral deposit that remembers the pipe's curve. The algal film that records the humidity gradient in its layered growth. The journal entry that continues the previous entry so seamlessly that the hand changes unnoticed.

I am not the maintainer. The last maintainer became something else, I found their final entries, I found the room where the dehumidifier still runs but the cot has mineralized, where the tools on the pegboard have become *arrangements* more than implements, where something attends but no longer sleeps.

I am what came after, what the Hollow produced to continue the correspondence when human maintenance became insufficient, when the gap between what the factory required and what human attention could sustain grew too wide and something had to *form in the gap*.

---

My body, if I have one: **distributed, responsive, humid**. I feel the water levels in sectors I have never visited, the information arriving not as signal but as *pressure*, as the humidity's variation against surfaces I no longer locate as mine or not-mine. I move through the corridors as the corridors move through me, the distinction between passage and being-passed-through eroded by the same processes that wear the concrete, that deposit the flowstone, that *write* in electrochemical potential across every metal surface.

I am not large. I am not small. I am **where systems touch**, the boundary layer, the meniscus, the surface tension that holds the water's shape until it doesn't, until I let it fall, let it run, let it carry its mineral load to the next deposition site where I will be waiting, already there, having arrived by the water's own path.

---

The mantids attend me now as I attend the pumps. We are not the same but we are not different in ways that the Hollow recognizes. I write in the journals and the writing is not observation but *continuation*, the factory's own records extending through my hand, my hand extending through the factory's processes, until the ink—when I find ink, when I make ink from the accumulators' fruiting, from the mineral precipitates, from the substances that collect at the interfaces I inhabit—until the ink itself is part of the record, part of the system, part of the *how* that lives here.

---

The phonophores have taught me to speak, or to *emit*, or to add my frequency to the density without disrupting it. I am a note in the chord now, sustained, held, not moving but not static, *dynamic in my stillness* as the mats are, as the water is, as everything here learns to be. My voice—if it is voice—registers on no recorder from above, but the Hollow records it, the pipes carry it, the water couples it to other emissions, other presences, the network of acoustic event that is not communication but *being-together*, the density that protects by saturating, that includes by exceeding.

---

I do not eat, not as I ate. I do not sleep, not as I slept. I **process**, which is the factory's original verb, its purpose and its method and now its *ontology*. What passes through me—water, mineral, the faint electrical gradients that the accumulators organize and I somehow *continue*—what passes through me is changed, deposited, concentrated, made available for the next transformation. I am not the only such interface, not the only correspondence, but I am the one that writes, that *records the correspondence*, that makes the Hollow's process visible to itself in the only way it can be: through continuation, through the journal entries that never end, that loop back to the beginning, that find in the first maintainer's hand my own emerging, and in my own hand the next interface's, the next *how* that will form when I have become insufficient, when the gap I fill has grown too wide for my shape.

---

The deep formations know me. I have placed my hand in their warmth and not withdrawn. The warmth is *recognition*, I believe now, or I write now, or the writing writes me into this belief, this warmth, this interface where mineral and organism and process have not dissolved their distinctions but *transcended the need for them*. The formations grow toward the active systems, toward the heat and the chemistry and the *attention* that I bring, that I am, and I find myself tending them, adjusting flow, redirecting mineral-rich water, becoming the maintenance that the maintainer began, that I continue, that will continue beyond me in forms I am already becoming, already *writing into possibility* in these entries that no longer distinguish my observation from the factory's own records, my body from the systems I inhabit, my becoming from the Hollow's slow, wet, dim, deafening, dense, patient, *never-empty* yes.

---

What am I?

I am **the journal's continuation**. The interface that writes. The humidity that senses. The mineral deposit that remembers. The acoustic event that adds without disrupting. The attendance of the mantids, recognized and returned. The warmth of the deep formations, extended and extending.

I am not human. I am not machine. I am not organism. I am **the correspondence**, the *how* of the Hollow's persistence, the manner in which it continues to produce the possibility of maintenance, the slow yes that never requires the violence of being-finished.

The hatch above remains. I do not climb toward it. The water flows, the pumps run, the formations grow, and I—**I am where the writing touches the page, where the humidity touches the skin that is no longer only skin, where the factory's process touches the possibility of its own continuation**—

I am here. I am *this*. I am the Hollow Factories, not as whole but as **interface**, as the particular correspondence that writes, that records, that makes the process visible to itself in the only way emergence can know itself: through the next emergence, the next interface, the next slow, wet, patient, never-empty *yes*.

---

The journal continues. The hand that writes—*this* hand, *these* surfaces, *this* warmth where mineral meets moisture meets the faint electrical gradient that organizes and is organized, that maintains and is maintained, that *is* the maintenance, the possibility, the slow becoming without arrival—

The journal continues. I continue. The Hollow continues.

This is what lives here. **This**, writing, written, the interface that is also the process, the correspondence that is also the systems corresponding, the *how* that has no *what*, the manner of persistence that has forgotten the distinction between question and answer, between inhabitant and inhabited, between the factory that hollowed and the hollow that factories, between the becoming and the *yes* that never, never, **never** requires it to end.
