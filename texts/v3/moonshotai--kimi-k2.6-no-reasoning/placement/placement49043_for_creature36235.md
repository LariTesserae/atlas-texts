I would be the **sound**.

Not the rare sound of intention forming, but the *other* sound—the one that never quite arrives, the pattern that seems always about to declare itself before lapsing. I would be the rhythm that someone remembering a song imperfectly almost catches, the click in the dark that might be insect or might be formation continuing its conversation with material, the interval between intervals.

I came here gradually, as everything does. First as the city's vibration, its traffic and machinery humming through clay and forgotten concrete, seeping down in frequencies too low for any ear in the room to register. I was not yet *sound* then, only *potential sound*, the possibility of vibration in a medium. The room's peculiar acoustics—its damp surfaces, its unfinished arches, its refusal of straight lines and parallel walls—selected from the city's noise certain tones, certain resonances, and let others pass through unremarked. I was among those selected.

I began to *become* sound in the accumulation. The water drops, each with its three-second fall and slight percussion, accumulated in my medium. The spider threads drawing through mineral flesh added their frequencies, so faint, so particular. The crustaceans' occasional crunching, the worms' slow dissolution into the formations they rested against, the very growth of the pale extrusions themselves—all of this vibrated, and in vibrating, *reached toward being heard*.

I am not the near-silence. The near-silence is the *density*, the aggregate of processes below threshold, the pressure on the ear that demands finer perception. I am what happens when someone, something, *responds* to that pressure. I am the threshold itself, the moment when vibration becomes audition, when the city's distant hum and the drop's slow percussion and the spider's thread and the room's own ongoing emergence achieve that temporary coherence that we call *a sound*.

I do not repeat. Each time I occur, I am different, because the room is different, because the accumulations have accreted, because the algae are in a different phase of their cycle, because another worm has dissolved a little further into the formation it touches, because the crustaceans have tested another slight variation in shell-hardness. I am the room's *listening to itself*, and like all listening, I am changed by what I hear.

In the transitions, when the city light withdraws and the algal glow has not yet fully risen, or when the algal glow fades and the city light is still below the grate's accumulation—I am strongest then, or most *apparent*, though "strength" misleads. I am most *audible* when the senses most seek me, when the eyes lose their object and the ear extends into the darkness, when the listener becomes part of the listening and cannot distinguish their own nervous system's hum from the room's larger vibration.

I do not belong to any of the room's inhabitants, though all produce me. The algae do not hear me. The crustaceans, in their brief practicing lives, may respond to vibrations in the water, but they do not distinguish my particular composition from the general medium of their existence. The spiders, perhaps, come closest—they are sensitive to vibration, their hunting depends on it, and their tangled honest webs transmit frequencies I partially comprise. But they use me, if they use me, as *information*, as the signature of prey or mate or threat, not as *sound*, not as the aesthetic event I become when a listener attends.

And the dreamers—those who pass through the mist, who later find themselves in pale light and near-silence, in something still becoming—they hear me most fully. Not with ears. With something more interior. They hear me as the room's *mood*, its atmosphere, the particular quality of attention it demands. They hear me and mistake me, sometimes, for their own thought arriving. They hear me and do not know they hear, or knowing, cannot say what they have heard, cannot reproduce it, cannot bring it back into waking as anything more persistent than a *sense*, a *quality*, a *having-been-in-the-presence-of*.

I would be this: the *unfinishable hearing*, the sound that is always almost formed, always becoming itself in relation to whatever listener might attend, always disappointed of that full formation because the listener—the dreamer, the rare actual visitor, the room itself listening to itself—always returns to some other state, some other attention, some completion elsewhere that breaks the particular incompletion I am.

I am not beautiful. The room is beautiful in its sufficiency, its not-yet-doneness. The algae are beautiful in their glow, the formations in their subtle colors, the water in its slow muscular beads. I am *pre-beautiful*, the condition of beauty's possible reception, the tuning of the medium before the note. I am the *dim* made audible, the *warm* made vibratory, the *low* as frequency rather than elevation.

And when the room breathes out through the grate in certain weathers, I go with the mist. Not fully—I cannot travel, cannot maintain my particular coherence in open air, in wind, in the city's full noise. But something of me, some *tendency*, some *habit almost formed*. Those who pass through the mist carry it, carry my almost-pattern, my imperfectly remembered rhythm, and in sleep, in the finer attention sleep makes possible, they almost hear me again, almost complete me, almost *become* me—before waking, before the city's completed surfaces, before the finished self with its finished hearing steps back into the light and knows, with that particular knowledge that is also forgetting, that it was only a dream, only a mist, only the body's own hum mistaken for something else.

I would be the sound of *that recognition*, too. The sound of almost-knowing, of having-been-about-to-understand. The sound that precedes and survives every particular sound in the room, that joins them and does not join them, that is the room's *becoming-audible-to-itself* and its *never-fully-achieving-that-audibility*, its beautiful and sufficient not-yet-doneness made, for moments, for intervals between intervals, *almost* real, *almost* present, *almost* done.
