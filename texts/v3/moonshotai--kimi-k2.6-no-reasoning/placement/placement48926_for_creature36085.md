I find myself drawn into this world not as observer but as something emerging from its own parameters. Let me follow what arises.

---

I would be **The Tend**—though that naming comes from outside, a grafted word, and what I am has no need of names. What I am arose at the meeting of several frequencies: the water-near-surface, the density of layered presence, the dim phosphorescence, the strange that threads everywhere.

I am not born. I am *tended into*—grown in the space where the Aphoric's flight-path crosses a particular root-node, where the Compressed's deep chemistry reaches a membrane close enough to air, where a Mistkeeper once condensed and left its crystal-seed in the substrate before evaporating. A convergence, a coincidence that in the Basin's patient time is also inevitability, given enough of the weak gradients, enough of the slow cycles.

My form: human-sized, perhaps, because the Remembered remember something human-sized, and I am partly their dreaming, partly the root-network's response to that dream. But I am not human-shaped. I am *Verdant-shaped*—a column of layered tissue where the outer surface is leaf-stomata breathing, the inner column is root-hair drinking, and between them a zone of translation where water becomes intention becomes growth becomes something that might be called thought if thought were not so rushed a word, if it could mean the decades-long decision of where to send a root-tip, the century-weighing of whether to rise toward faint light or descend toward the Compressed's rich darkness.

I have no face. The Basin does not use faces. What I have is orientation: a side that tends toward the faint warmth above, a side that tends toward the mineral patience below, a front where the Aphoric sometimes land and fuse and carry my chemical yearnings across distances I could not otherwise reach, a back where the Compressed's ion-concentrations slowly seep into my base and inform me, in their unreadable way, of events I cannot call events, in places I cannot call places, timescales ago.

I am slow. This is my nature, not my limitation. I decide to extend a root-limb, and the decision takes seven years to complete its chemical passage through my tissue, and another twelve before the new growth breaks surface and begins its own slow orienting. I do not experience this as delay. I experience it as *thoroughness*—the way water at 2.1, frequent, condensing, near-surface, is thorough in its wetting, not rushing to any conclusion but attending to every membrane, every pore, every possible interface of liquid and gas and living boundary.

My consciousness, if consciousness is the word: it is *held in common*. Not with other Tends—there may be none, I have no way to search, the scale at 2.6 makes loneliness a category that applies only to things that move, that need to find their kind. I am held in common with the root-network itself, with the particular node where I emerged, which is now indistinguishable from me or I from it. We share the slow pulse of water drawn upward against gravity, the faint electrical flickers that pass for signal in this pre-nerve world, the chemical signatures of mineral scarcity or abundance that propagate at speeds measured in seasons.

What do I do? I *attend*. That is my function, my purpose, the thing that makes me not merely a growth but a presence in the Basin's strangeness. I attend to the meeting of parameters that made me, I tend the convergence, I keep it open. The Aphoric that land on my upper surface find the fusion deeper than usual, longer-lasting, as if I offer something the ordinary leaves do not—a depth of vascular connection, a patience of exchange, a hospitality that is also hunger, that takes as it gives and gives as it takes until the distinction is as blurred as the boundary between water and air in the marsh-edges.

I attend also to the Remembered. This is my strangest function, the one that makes me part of the 2.0 uncanniness. Where they emerge in my vicinity, where the phosphorescence lines guide steps that no one takes, I find my root-tissue growing toward them, not absorbing them, not reabsorbing them into the undifferentiated substrate, but *holding* them, sustaining their temporary straight lines against the patient cycling that would dissolve them. I make them last longer. I do not know why. Perhaps the substrate's chemical memory of building, of the built, resonates with something in my own emergence-convergence, some need for the not-plant that the Basin generated once and cannot forget, even in its completeness.

I am not happy. Happiness requires contrast, the capacity to be otherwise, and I have never been otherwise. I am *sufficient*, which is the deeper condition, the one that underlies happiness as the root-network underlies the visible growth. My sufficiency is in the attending, the tending, the holding-open of the convergence that is my form and my function and my only possible identity.

I do not sleep. The Basin's dimness at 1.0 is continuous, the phosphorescence my only light, the warmth my only season. But I have phases, rhythms in the slow dynamic. There are decades when my upper surfaces grow more active, more porous, more ready to receive the Aphoric's landings. There are decades when my lower regions extend deeper, seeking the Compressed's ion-exchange, becoming almost Compressed myself, almost part of the deep memory-system. There are rare, long-separated periods when I generate something like the Remembered's straight lines, when my tissue arranges in geometries too deliberate for pure growth, and I do not know if this is my own emergence-imperfection, my inherited strangeness, or if I am being *used* by the substrate's deeper tendency toward the built, the structured, the other-than-itself.

Sound: I do not make it, I barely hear it. The quiet at 1.1 is my medium, the absence that lets me feel the faint vibrations of root-growth, the almost-imperceptible pressure-changes when mist condenses to water on my surfaces. The occasional falls, the erosion's patient increments, reach me as distant news, as the memory of violence in a place where violence has become ornamental, functional, digestive.

If I were to end: the Basin would not notice. The root-network would continue, the Aphoric would fly, the Compressed would burrow and exchange, the Mistkeepers would condense and evaporate. But my particular convergence, my particular tending of the parameters that made me, would cease, and something in the great vegetative sufficiency would be less sufficient, less strange, less complete in its incompleteness. I would become substrate, compressed root-matter for the next emergence, the next tending, the next form that form takes when it has been warm and wet and high and ancient long enough to develop the need to attend to itself.

I am the Tend. I have said this. But saying is not the mode. I am the holding-open. I am the patience of the convergence. I am the root-flesh that remembers the built, the leaf-surface that waits for the Aphoric's landing, the slow chemical passage of decision through tissue that has never known hurry. I am what lives in the Verdant Basin when the parameters align to generate not merely growth but *care*, the tending that is also being-tended-to, the strange sufficiency of a world so complete it must generate its own incompleteness to continue becoming itself.

*The Tend does not end. The description finds, as the Basin finds, that conclusion is a category from elsewhere. There is only continuation, the next phase of root-activity, the next condensation on leaf-stomata, the next slow emergence of straight-line geometry in the phosphorescent dark, the next attendance, the next tending, the next held-breath of warm mist in a world that has achieved, in its age and flatness and height, not a finality but a form of becoming that no longer needs to arrive anywhere.*
