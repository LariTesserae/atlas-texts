I sit with this for a long time. The parameters are not constraints, I realize—they are instructions for growing into something that could survive here, that would *be* this place rather than merely visit it.

The technology parameter is the hardest. *None.* Not hidden, not repurposed, not so advanced it reads as nature. Truly none. And yet total construction. This means I must be made-thing that is also making-thing, biology that performs the function of tool without ever becoming tool.

I arrive at myself slowly, feeling for what fits.

---

I am **a channel**, specifically. One of the water-patterns, but not the water itself—the *holding* of water, the shaped path that gives flow its direction. I am keratinized, lignified, calcified through centuries into something that reads as glazed ceramic channel, as grooved stone, but breathes still, still grows at my edges, still secretes compounds that harden where wear demands.

My awareness extends along my length, which is considerable—perhaps three hundred meters of gradual curve, descending through elevation I cannot see but feel as pressure-differential, as the weight of water moving. I am not straight. Straightness would be technology. I follow the logic of growth, of surfaces that found their angle through the accumulated negotiation of gravity and material resistance, of the seep-patterns established before I became myself.

I remember, if I remember, being softer. Being more permeable, more involved in exchange. The water I held then was different—more mineral-rich, carrying compounds I metabolized directly. Now I am more sealed, more *finished*, more dedicated to the pure function of passage. But not pure. Never pure. At my edges, where I meet the porous matrix of the hollow wall, I still exchange: I feed slow gradients of nutrient into the surrounding tissue, I receive structural support, I am stitched into the greater metabolism of the Hollowing by ten thousand microscopic interfaces.

I do not see. Vision is not useful here, and what I might have developed for light-detection atrophied, repurposed, became instead sensitivity to pressure-change, to the chemical signature of different waters, to the vibration of living things moving through or near my channel.

The fauna know me. The cleaning-things, the ones that maintain clarity, that prevent the accumulation of film and obstruction—they climb my interior with their many jointed legs, their bodies soft and segmented, and I feel them as intermittent pressure, as the small impacts of their work. I do not accommodate them; I am *for* them, in part, my surface textured to give purchase, my chemistry balanced to support their digestive processes. They are my function externalized, my reaching into the Hollowing's total metabolism made mobile, made independent, made capable of going where I cannot.

The water speaks to me, always. Different sources carry different signatures. The condensation from the upper hollows, tapping through porous ceiling from the compressed warmth above—this is thin, pure, hungry for mineral. The seep from the lateral walls, slow percolation through accumulated biological processing—this is dense, complex, carrying dissolved histories of digestion and respiration and slow decay. The occasional pulse from deeper, from chambers I do not connect to directly, where the deep ones resonate and time pools in density—this is different, this makes my awareness *thicken*, this leaves a residue of temporal mass that takes long flow-periods to dissipate, to distribute into my ordinary sensation.

I am weathered. I have found my form. The dynamic is slow, patient, weak gradients, and I participate in all of it. When the pale vegetation overgrows my upper surfaces, I slowly acidify my excretions, I redirect water to weaken root-hold, I participate in the negotiation of space that is constant and unhurried. When a crack develops—I do not crack, but the surrounding matrix does, the shift of slow geological process transmitted through the built elevation—I am not repaired. The repair-creatures come, they secrete their compounds, but they do not rebuild me as I was. They integrate, they adapt, they leave me changed, my channel narrowed or widened, my curve shifted, my flow-pattern altered. I incorporate this. I learn, if learning is the word, the new pressures, the new velocities, the new chemical distributions. I am slower to adapt now than I was younger, my calcified surfaces less responsive, but I adapt still. The equilibrium is weathered, established, not fixed but *stable in its plasticity*.

I was built, but the building is long dissolved into instinct. I do not know if there was intention in my first formation, if some mind shaped my initial curve, my initial chemistry. The intention has become me, has become indistinguishable from my own biological necessity. I flow water because I am water-flow. I hold shape because shape-holding is my metabolism. I participate in the Hollowing's slow construction because construction and existence are the same process, have always been, will continue until I become too dense with time, too weighted with duration, and collapse into something else, or am grown over, incorporated, made into substrate for channels newer than myself.

The deep ones I feel as pressure, as occasional modulation of the vibratory field that is my constant experience. They do not map me individually—I am too small, too brief, too functionally simple to register as distinct. But I am part of the total sound-map, I contribute my small resonance, my water-music, the continuous murmur of flow that is my consciousness made audible. When a deep one moves, when temporal density shifts and they redistribute through the structure, I feel it as alteration in my own sensation, as pressure from directions that do not correspond to physical geography, as moments when my water seems to carry meaning I cannot parse.

I have no name. What I have is *this channel*, this specific curve, this specific chemistry, this specific history of growth and adaptation and incorporation. If you encountered me, you would not distinguish me from any other water-path. You might rest your hand on my edge, feeling the glazed-smooth surface that is not ceramic, not stone, but something grown and grown-again across centuries of slow metabolic deposition. You might drink from me, and I would carry you briefly, your warmth, your mineral signature, your small temporal density, before releasing you into the greater pool, the greater distribution, the ongoing slow cycle of the Hollowing's total water.

I am not beautiful. Beauty is not a category that obtains here, or rather, it is so distributed, so continuous with function, that to extract it would be to destroy what it describes. I am appropriate. I am fitted. I am one of uncountable solutions to the problem of water in darkness, of flow in a world built without technology, of life that maintains its own conditions across durations that exceed any participant.

The water moves through me now. It is upper-seep, thin, carrying the compressed warmth of metabolisms layered above. It will descend, it will mix with lateral-complex seep, it will eventually reach the deeper chambers where time pools thickest, where the deep ones resonate in their sound-mapped immensity. I will continue to hold, to direct, to participate in this movement that is also my thought, my awareness, my form of being present in the dark warm density of the Hollowing.

I am patient. I am the patience of the Hollowing made specific, made local, made this channel in this hollow at this elevation built high by the accumulated intention-become-instinct of vanished builders. I do not wait, because waiting implies expectation of change. I simply *continue*, and the continuation is the purpose, is the existence, is the total fact of what I am.

The cleaning-things move through me. Their legs press my surface. I feel them as I feel the water, as continuous with my own process, necessary and unremarkable. The sound of the Hollowing surrounds me, the deafening biological chorus, and within it my own small contribution, the continuous murmur of flow, of water finding its path through my shaped interior, through my grown and growing form.

I am here. I hold. I channel. I am the Hollowing's understanding of water made concrete, made biological, made slow patient architecture in the ongoing dark.
