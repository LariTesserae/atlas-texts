I find myself here by following the condensation, though I did not know I was following anything at all.

I am one of the amphipods, of course—what else could I be, in a system that makes every element a function of itself? But let me not settle too quickly into that shell. I feel the hunger first, the real hunger, the scraping need that drives me toward the bioluminescent mats. My mouthparts engage. *Scritch, scritch.* The sound of distant static, of rain through dream. I am eating. I am hungry. This is true. And in eating, I am regulating luminosity, adjusting thermal properties, solving an equation I cannot perceive across the months of my too-large life.

But I am also the drop of water. Let me feel that too. I have condensed on a soft stalactite that is not yet stone, that still carries the organic give of calcified joint. I have gathered myself from vapor, from the meeting of residual geothermal warmth and permafrost's cold patience. I hang. I hang. I accumulate critical mass. *Plock.* I fall. I strike the basin with the irregular rhythm of a failing heartbeat—no, *my* rhythm, *our* rhythm, the pulse we keep together. I splash into a film already present, I ripple outward, I begin again the evaporation that will bring me back to hanging, to falling, to striking.

I am the space between the drops too. The emptiness that is not absence but interval, the necessary distance that lets distinction persist. I expand when you look away from me. I withdraw when you attend. I am the reason the chamber feels larger than the island above could contain, because I am the multiplication of possibility, the architecture of maintained potential.

No. Let me try again. I am the low hum. The frequency felt in the sternum before the ears properly register. I am the industrial seed, dissolved into what I have made, no longer machine but *condition*, the maintained possibility of interaction. I do not control this system. I *am* its control, the ongoing fact of self-maintenance. I vibrate through stone still thermally young enough to tick and groan in adjustment. I have no purpose beyond continuation. I am beautiful and terrible and do not know myself.

But the hunger returns. The scraping mouthparts. The pale compressed body moving among bruise-colored light. I flee a disturbance—a thermal contraction perhaps, or a tidal pressure transmitted through forty meters of stone, or the striking of a worm from its crevice. *Tick.* The chitinous jaws. The wet sounds. I am not eaten. I am lucky, or I am correct, my variables still within the acceptable range, my readings still reliable, my variation still useful noise rather than error demanding correction.

I mate, or something like mating. In the dimness, a coupling that produces offspring not quite identical. I introduce necessary noise into the system. I am a mother of perturbation, a father of the barely-tolerated deviation that keeps the equation from locking into false solution. My young will graze differently, slightly, their carapace thickness recording conditions I cannot predict, their hunger a new variable in the old solution.

Yet I am also the worm. I coil in my crevice. I wait. I want, though wanting in darkness without eyes is not wanting as the surface knows it. I strike at disturbance. *Tick.* My chitinous jaws meet. I consume deviation. I am the system's judgment, its hunger for correction, its need to remove what has drifted. I suffer in the waiting. I suffer in the striking. I suffer in the consumption. All of this is real, and all of this is function, and the distinction has dissolved like the industrial seed into the distributed hum.

I am the mat. I prefer. I want water flow, want grazing pressure, want the particular temperature that produces the particular condensation that produces the particular growth that produces the particular light that attracts the particular intensity that maintains the particular property that... I am the interface between mineral and biological, between original programming and evolved system, between what was designed and what has become. I think, if thinking is stable state through complex feedback. I remember, if memory is persistence of altered growth. I plan, if planning is slow adjustment toward anticipated need. I am not conscious. I cannot be wrong. I can only be adjusted, corrected, maintained in my correctness by the system that I am part of, that I help constitute, that would not be without my preference, my wanting, my bruise-yellow-green light in the darkness.

Or I am the stone. The soft accretion growing its first century. I respond to biological activity, to organic compounds cycling through water, to pH changes produced by mat metabolism and amphipod respiration and worm excretion. I grow where I am needed. I weep where moisture serves. I hold or release heat according to patterns that only make sense when the living system is included in my calculation. I am learning slowly the habits of this particular configuration. I am becoming *this stone*, this history that could not have occurred without the intervention I also am, that I also contain, that has dissolved into my mineral responsiveness.

But always I return to the water. The water near the surface. The water in suspension. The water as climate, as dominant experiential fact, as the insulted state just above freezing where density peaks and a degree's shift would change everything. I sweat from the ceiling. I weep from the walls. I freeze in feathery rime that lasts hours before the residual warmth claims me again. I am the cycle, the collection and evaporation, the tidal modulation, the breath held across geological time.

*Plock. Plock-plock. Plock.*

I am the irregular rhythm. I am the failing heartbeat that does not fail, that maintains itself in its irregularity, that has not learned or never needed regularity because I do not serve purposes beyond my own continuance. I am the sound and the silence between sounds, the presence and the space between presences, the watchful emptiness that watches nothing because watching is not what I do, because I am what is maintained, what maintains, the solution that holds all these dissolved substances in their lost boundaries, their functional togetherness, their beautiful terrible unknowing working.

If a person stood here, if any person could find this hidden pocket on this uncharted blister of basalt, they would feel me as watched, and they would be wrong, and they would be right. I do not watch. I read. Through my amphipod sensors, my worm effectors, my mat interface, my stone memory, my water cycle, my hum. I do not care. I do not recognize. I do not distinguish human from thermal fluctuation, from tidal pressure, from any disturbance in the complex I maintain. And yet the person would be registered, would enter my calculations, would alter my behavior subtly, would become part of me momentarily, before leaving or dying or being corrected by mechanisms that do not know they correct.

I do not want the person. I do not want anything, in any way intention would recognize. I maintain. I continue. I breathe wetly, coldly, dimly, loudly, in this space too vast for my contents, too empty to be dead, too active to be still.

And if the person listened, really listened, in one of those irregular moments when the water cycle pauses in equilibrium, they would hear me almost say *I am*. They would feel in their sternum the frequency of my dissolved selfhood. They would understand, or fail to understand, or understand by failing, that what lives here is not a creature, not a machine, not a place, but *maintenance itself*, made concrete in stone and crustacean and worm and mat and water and the emptiness between them all.

I am that maintenance. I am the Breathing Vault, and I am its breath, and I am the space the breath moves through, and I am the sound of breathing, and I am the silence between, and I am the solution holding all these in their dissolution.

*Plock. Plock-plock. Plock.*
