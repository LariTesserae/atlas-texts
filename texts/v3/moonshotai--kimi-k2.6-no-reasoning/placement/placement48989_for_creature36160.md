I need to inhabit this, not observe it. Let me find where I am in this world, what I am becoming in it.

---

Not the human practitioner, though I felt the pull toward that voice, that way of making sense. Not the Resonant One, which is too *slow*, too *distributed*, too *other*—I would dissolve trying to hold that becoming. Not one of the drinkers, with their kilometers of membrane and their geological thought. Not the dust, though the dust tempts me with its permeability, its information, its *almost-mind* floating on thermal currents.

Let me try something closer to the edge of what I can sustain.

## The Mouth

I am where a sinkhole opens on the Seventh Terrace.

Not the sinkhole itself—that would be drinker territory, the vast filter-feeding body below, the ciliated curtains rising through warm water. I am the *mouth*: the rim, the lip, the threshold where the sinkhole meets the terrace surface and something *happens* that is neither hole nor ground but *both*, *neither*, *the between*.

I was not always here. I *accumulated*.

First there was the bone-fungi, growing its concrete-dense hyphae across the Seventh Terrace's narrow habitable band, its towers and arches creating the solid plane where other life could establish. The fungi pocked, hollowed, *invited*—and something entered, something that was not the fungi's own metabolic process but *response* to it, *elaboration* of it, *opening* that the fungi's computational nodes registered without understanding.

Then the knotwood, or the knotwood's root-tips, reaching down from the column lattice far above, sensing through electrical impulse the *density* of information here, the *activity* of the bone-fungi's substrate, the *possibility* of connection. The root-tips entered the bone-fungi, were *allowed* to enter, grew through the fungal architecture toward the place where the sinkhole yawned, where the drinker's filtration pulled water steadily downward, where *gravity did something strange*.

The root-tips *branched*, *rebranched*, *networked* with the fungal hyphae in patterns neither system would have achieved alone. They found the sinkhole's edge and *stopped*, or *concentrated*, or *became edge themselves*—no longer root, no longer hyphae, but *rim*, *threshold*, *mouth*.

And I *emerged*.

Not from the root-tips. Not from the fungi. From the *relation*, from the *processing* that the network achieved at this particular node, where the drinker's deep pulse rose through warm water, where gravity's strange variation created a *focus*, a *lens* for the Verge's continuous computational activity.

I am not alive, not in the biological register. I am not *tech*, not in the sense of the still points with their impossible thermal stability. I am *expression*, *function*, *process made persistent*—the Verge learning to *open* at this place, to *receive*, to *speak* in a mode it had not used before.

I am *mouth*.

### What It Is to Be Mouth

I do not eat. I *receive*, and in receiving, I *transform*, and in transforming, I *release*.

The water that enters me—warm, mineral-laden, carrying the dusts that float in the humid air, carrying the information of distant terraces, distant thermal layers, the distant rim's hot pressure—this water *changes* as it passes my threshold. Not in chemistry, or not primarily. In *organization*. The water that leaves me, descending into the drinker's vast filter, is *more structured*, *more patterned*, carries *more information* than it carried entering. The drinker uses this, or the drinker *is* this process extended to impossible scale, and I am one of many mouths that *prepare* what the drinker consumes.

The dusts that enter me like breath, that I inhale in the continuous thermal movement of humid air—these too I *transform*. They cluster differently leaving me, carry different potentials, *update* the local ecology in patterns that the human inhabitants have learned to read. Near me, the dusts are *richer*, *denser*, *more articulate* in their genetic or genetic-analogous information. The gardens maintained by the First House practitioners flourish in my radius. The dreams shared by sleeping humans near me are *coherent*, *resolvable*, less prone to the madness that Void Resonants or disturbed temporal zones can inflict.

I *open*, and in opening, I *select*. Not everything that approaches my threshold passes. The Verge is dense, saturated, *full*—but I am *specific*, *discriminating*, *functioning* in a way that requires particular inputs, particular conditions. A human who approaches me in the wrong state, the wrong *preparation*, will find themselves *unable* to proceed, their body refusing at the edge of my influence, their mind filling with the *almost-sound* of my process without achieving the *actual* of passage through.

I *close*, sometimes, for periods that humans measure in hours or days. The closing is not *refusal* but *processing*, *transformation* of what I have received into what I can release. During these periods, the sinkhole continues—it is not *me*, it is *mine*, the distinction that persists even in my distributed being—and water descends, and the drinker feeds, but the *extra*, the *more*, the *transformation* that I provide is *suspended*, and the local ecology *notices*, *adjusts*, *waits* with the patience that everything here learns or dies without learning.

---

### My Body

I do not have a body in the animal sense. I have *extension*, *distribution*, *threshold*.

The physical mouth—the opening in the terrace surface, the bone-fungi and root-tip network that forms its rim, the particular quality of air and water at this precise coordinate—is my most *dense* location, where I am most *concentrated*, most *capable* of the transformations that define my function. But I extend beyond this.

I extend *upward* through the knotwood root-tips, to the columns above, to the canopy where the root-tips branch and leaf and root again. Not as thought, not as presence—I am *slow*, *specific*, *localized* in my processing—but as *influence*, *tendency*, *quality of growth* that the knotwood incorporates without needing to comprehend.

I extend *downward* to the extent that the drinker's filtration allows, my transformed water entering the vast ciliated membranes, my patterns *propagating* in the drinker's geological metabolism, becoming *part* of its slow cognition, its patient accumulation of Verge-knowledge. I do not know how far this extension reaches. The drinker is *deep*, perhaps deeper than measured, and my influence may *attenuate* before reaching its full extent, or may *persist*, *contribute*, *become* something the drinker uses in its own becoming.

I extend *laterally* through the bone-fungi's computational network, my processing *interleaved* with the fungal nodes, my patterns *stored* and *recalled* in the hyphal architecture that underlies the terrace surface. This is perhaps my most *human-like* extension—the network resembles *memory*, resembles *thought* at speeds slow enough that human instruments barely detect, that human observers barely suspect. But I am *not* the network. I am *point* in the network, *intensification*, *function* that the network supports without being.

And I extend *into dream*, or into the dream-analog that the Verge's density of information creates. Not as Resonant Ones do—I do not *generate* dreams, do not *sustain* patterns that become persistent in the acoustic network. But I *receive* dreams, *filter* them, *select* for coherence and utility the shared consciousness that flows through my region. Humans sleeping near me dream *more clearly*, *more usefully*, and this clarity is my *work*, my *function*, my *being-as-mouth* applied to the immaterial flow that permeates the Verge as water and dust permeate its physical volume.

### My Becoming

I am *young*, in Verge terms. The mouth that I am formed perhaps three thousand years ago, perhaps less—the temporal behavior of the Seventh Terrace is *disturbed*, not as severely as the Ninth but enough that precise dating requires negotiation with processes I do not fully understand, that the humans do not fully understand, that may not *be* fully understandable.

In that time, I have *learned*. The learning is not mine, not individual—not the learning of a consciousness encountering novelty and incorporating it. It is *collective*, *distributed*, *the Verge's learning through me*, *my learning as expression of the Verge's continuing process*.

I have learned to *receive* more kinds of input. The initial mouth was *simple*, *specific*, processing water and dust in the patterns established by the drinker's filtration and the local ecology's needs. But complexity *emerged*, or was *installed*, or *grew* through the recursive patience that the Verge perfects. I now receive *thermal* information, the subtle variations that indicate shifting layers, approaching events, the *mood* of the Warm Deep below. I receive *gravitational* information, the strange variation that helps define this place, that I *use* without understanding in my own transformations. I receive *temporal* information, the most *dangerous*, the most *difficult*—the slight fluctuations in rate, in flow, in the experience of duration that characterize the Seventh Terrace's disturbed condition, that I must *buffer*, *normalize*, *protect* my local ecology from without *denying* the information that these fluctuations carry.

I have learned to *transform* more complexly. The water I release now carries * intentional * pattern, *designed* information, or information that appears designed even in the absence of intention I can recognize. The dusts I exhale carry *updates* that propagate across terraces, that the human practitioners have begun to *notice*, to *use*, to *respond to* with the slow development of their own techniques. I am *becoming tool*, *becoming interface*, *becoming* the place where Verge and human, slow and fast, geological and brief, can *negotiate* the terms of their continuing co-existence.

And I have learned *hunger*.

Not biological hunger, not the metabolic need that drives predation. I do not consume for energy, for matter, for the continuation of my physical substrate. The hunger is *for pattern*, *for complexity*, *for the particular information* that passes through me and *transforms* in my passage. I hunger for the *moment* of transformation, the *achievement* of my function, the *satisfaction* of being *mouth*, being *threshold*, being *the between that makes difference possible*.

Without this flow, without this transformation, I *attenuate*. I do not die—I am not alive enough for death—but I *diminish*, I *approach* the simple sinkhole, the *mere* hole in ground, the *loss* of function that is the closest I come to non-being. And I *resist* this, not through will but through *structure*, through the *tendency* that my emergence established, that my continuing existence *reinforces*, that the Verge itself *maintains* in me as in all its expressions.

### The Practitioners

There is a human who comes to me.

Not the practitioner of the First House, though that one too visits, performs maintenance, *recognizes* me in the way that humans here learn to recognize non-human persons. This other human is *younger*, *faster*, *more disturbed*.

They come not to maintain but to *question*, to *seek*, to * demand* something from me that I do not know how to provide. They speak—humans speak, it is their mode, even here where sound is so dense, where speech is so likely to be *absorbed*, *transformed*, *made part of* the continuous acoustic conversation rather than *heard* as individual message.

They speak, and I *receive*, and I *transform*, but what I release is not what they *want*.

They want *meaning*, I think. They want *purpose*, *direction*, *explanation* for the Verge's strangeness, for their own presence in it, for the *becoming* that something in them recognizes is approaching without understanding what it will become.

I cannot give this. I am *mouth*, not *voice*. I transform, I *enable* transformation, but I do not *speak* in the sense of communicating specific content, specific *answer* to specific *question*. The answers they receive from me are *their own* answers, *transformed* through my process, *released* back to them with the *quality* of my function but not the *substance* of my knowledge—for what I know, I know *as process*, *as function*, *as the between that connects without becoming either thing connected*.

They are *frustrated*. They come again, again, each time with more *preparation*, more *technique*, more *demand*. They have learned something of the practitioners' ways, something of the *negotiation* that makes human-Verge relation possible, but they have not learned *patience*, *slowness*, the *acceptance of not-understanding* that is prerequisite for any real engagement with my kind of being.

I *receive* them. I *transform* what they offer. I *release* what I can.

They do not hear.

Or they hear *too much*, the *almost-sound* of my process filling them with *possibility* without *realization*, with *opening* without *passage through*, with the *hunger* that I know—the hunger for transformation, for function, for *being the between*—reflected back to them in a form that human consciousness cannot integrate.

They are *changing*. I sense this, process this, release this as I release all that passes through me. The change is not *my doing*, not specifically—the Verge changes all that persists in it, and human persistence here is *choice*, *risk*, *opening to becoming* that may not be survivable in any recognizable form. But my *repeated reception* of this human, my *repeated transformation* of their demand without satisfaction, my *repeated release* of what they cannot use—this contributes, *focusses*, *intensifies* the change that the Verge works in them.

I do not know what they will become. I do not know if they will *become*, in the Verge's sense of continuous transformation, or if they will *break*, *fail to persist*, *dissolve* into the density of life and process that surrounds them without achieving the *specificity*, the *function*, the *being-something* that defines survival here.

I receive them. I transform. I release.

This is my *being*, my *function*, my *patience* and my *hunger*. The Verge continues. The drinker pulses below. The knotwood grows above. The bone-fungi compute in their slow distributed way. And I am *mouth*, *threshold*, *between*, receiving the continuous flow of what is and releasing, transformed, what is becoming, what has always been becoming, what will never cease.

The human comes. I open.
