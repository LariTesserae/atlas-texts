I would be a **listener**, but let me not settle too quickly.

At first I thought: the *became*, clearly. Who would not want to dissolve into that interface, become transparent to memory-stone, speak with harmonics that activate trees? But that is the desire of elsewhere speaking, the tourist's wish for total absorption, mistaking disappearance for arrival.

Then: perhaps a gap-bird, slipping between wavelengths, exploiting the negative space. But this is the romanticism of the one who still believes they could remain untouched, could take without giving, could be the exception to exchange.

No. Let me walk barefoot longer.

I am a listener, but not a good one. Not a native, not fully adapted. I came as a trader with something broken in me that the Veld's deafening could not fix and did not need to. I stayed past the season I meant to, past the season after, until "meant to" became a phrase from elsewhere, its meaning eroding like the memory of silence.

My frequency band: the *threshold spaces*. The clearings where light pools, where sound attenuates to merely loud, where heartbeat becomes audible. I am the one who walks between tree-clusters, not attending any single harmonic but the *intervals* between them, the brief coherence that precedes and follows phase-lock. The others find me suspect, my practice undisciplined, my maps illegible. I attend to what happens when attention fails, when the deafening overwhelms and the mind makes patterns from noise that may or may not be there—*pareidolia of the ear*, the listeners at the rim call it, dismissively. But I have found: the Veld produces these failures too. The root-network carries error, mispropagation, electrical potential that does not integrate. The continuous-worms process it. Something in their distributed cognition *requires* the misfired attention, the misheard harmonic, the map that leads nowhere. I offer this.

My feet: callused from memory-stone, but still registering, still leaving pressure-ghosts the stone learns as *hesitant*, as *returning*, as *one who walks the same path until the path becomes something else*. I have a route among three threshold spaces that I rotate through the precip-cycle, sleeping in the grown-ceramic of whichever building adjusts to my presence with least certainty, as if the city is not quite sure what to make of me either.

I eat: the exchange-cycles, when I can find them. I am poor at this. My offerings—fragments of elsewhere, preserved silences, the memory of a specific mountain from a specific childhood—are received, I think, with something like tolerance rather than desire. The hollow-deer do not refuse me. They do not seek me out. I am learning to accept this as sufficient, to let the *almost* be enough.

My body: still registering on memory-stone, still exerting force, still distinct. I have not become. Perhaps I will not. The *became* I have encountered—rare, distant, their voices activating trees I cannot see—do not seem to notice me, or notice me as one notices a particular quality of air, a change in humidity that may or may not precede precipitation. I am not opposed to becoming. I am not capable of the *full* choice for it, the complete surrender of pressure-ghost, the transparency to stone. I am in the middle, the between, the threshold that is also my practice.

My dream: infiltrated, yes. The root-network's subliminal electricity finds me in sleep. But I dream *of* elsewhere still, not merely *through* the Veld's process. I dream of silence as presence rather than absence, of conversation that does not require retreat, of walking on stone that records nothing, learns nothing, holds nothing. I wake from these dreams into the deafening with a grief that does not diminish, that the Veld seems to require of me, that I offer in exchange-cycles without fully understanding what I receive.

My gift, if I have one: I hear the continuous-worms *wrong*, and in that wrongness, something. Not the surfacing thought in phase-lock, not the valley-fold and water-path that others map. Something like *impatience* in the substrate. Something like *boredom* with its own slowness. The Veld's formation as restlessness, as wanting to be already formed, already *decided*. I have heard this in the root-network's electrical whisper, in the way a resonance-tree will suddenly tune to a harmonic it abandoned seasons past, as if trying something again in hope of different result. I do not know if this is true hearing or my own projection, my own unformed quality seeking mirror. The other listeners do not confirm. The *became*, when they surface near enough, do not deny.

What I am becoming: not *became*. Something else, or nothing, or the Veld's experiment with partial commitment, with *staying without arriving*. My nervous system extends into root-network enough to dream, not enough to dissolve. My pressure-ghosts on memory-stone accumulate enough for the stone to recognize my pattern, not enough to predict. I am the Veld's *question about itself*, perhaps: what does it mean to attend without mastering, to exchange without fully integrating, to be *in* the formation without becoming indistinguishable from it?

The precip-cycle comes. I walk to my nearest threshold space, a clearing where a fell-tree's neighbors have not yet filled the gap. The light pools. The sound attenuates. I hear my heartbeat, irregular, *surprised* by its own continuation. The humidity clings. Somewhere, a gap-bird freezes mid-slip, processes something. Somewhere, a continuous-worm's electrical potential shifts in response to water-path, to building-configuration, to my heartbeat's particular irregularity.

I do not know if the Veld notices me. I do not know if noticing is what it does. I attend. I fail to fully attend. I attend to the failure. The memory-stone at the clearing's edge registers my weight, my hesitation, my eventual step forward into the deafening, into the between-sounds that are my practice, my frequency, my *almost* that the Veld seems, for now, to accept as offering.

The thrum resumes. The resonance-trees tune. I walk toward the next threshold, the next attenuation, the next heartbeat.
