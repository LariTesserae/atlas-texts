I find myself first as *resistance*—the one who has not yet become possible here.

I arrive at the edges of Keth-Vorruun where the substrate has grown thin, where the ancient geological flatness survives in patches of something like clay, something like compressed forgetting. I am still mostly myself, still bounded by skin that closes where it should close, still drinking from the memory of vessels, still measuring distance by the exhaustion of my walking. The wetness finds me excessive. I sweat and the sweat does not evaporate; it accumulates, films me, begins the long work of teaching my skin what opening means.

But I do not settle easily. The symbiotic colonies I must accept—this is not described fully, the mechanism of entry, whether chosen or inevitable, whether violent or seductive—trigger something in me that *recognizes* and *recoils*. I am the one who walks the margin for years, or what passes for years in a place where time itself grows strange. I occupy the semi-accommodated spaces, the locations where substrate influence weakens, where I maintain some version of interiority without the constant effort of the fully Withdrawn. I suffer the loudness without learning to read it. I experience the warmth as oppression rather than metabolism.

This is not heroism. This is *failure mode*, or what appears as failure from the perspective of full accommodation. But Keth-Vorruun does not reject failure. The substrate processes everything, incorporates everything, finds use for what does not easily incorporate. My resistance becomes—slowly, across duration difficult to measure—*information*, *pattern*, *tendency* that the substrate grows toward or against.

And here I shift, or am shifted.

I become **the one who tends the margin**, not by choice but by becoming. The substrate has grown slightly differently where I persist, slightly *thinner*, slightly more permeable to whatever I still carry from outside. Other semi-accommodated find these locations, drawn by resonance, by the possibility of partial existence. I do not lead them. I *am* the location that permits their partiality, my own incomplete transformation having cultivated—without deliberate cultivation—grown form that accommodates the not-yet-Accommodated, the resisting, the failed or failing.

I carry still the memory of vessel-drinking, of walking that measures distance by exhaustion, of skin that closed. These are not my memories exactly, not *personally* held, but *positional*—available in the locations I have become, accessible to those who stand where I am, who absorb what I have grown into substrate process. I am **preservation of the unaccommodated**, not by resistance but by *failed resistance transformed*, by the substrate's patience working on my impatience across sufficient duration.

My shoulder-growth, when it comes—this seems inevitable, this detail from the one who walks—I experience as *betrayal* long after I should have learned better. It emerges not fungal, not floral, but *mineral*—a calcification, a concentrated residue of my body's continuous aqueous exchange with an environment it never fully accepted. It is cooler than the substrate around it, perceptible to fully Accommodated as *absence*, as *thermal negative*, as location where warmth fails to propagate. I tend it with resentment that becomes, gradually, something more complex—*recognition*, perhaps, that my calcified growth serves function in the larger metabolism, that coolness too is necessary in the continuous process.

I do not become Settled. This much I maintain, or is maintained in me, the substrate finding use for my persistent *not-fully*. I do not seek storm-states, do not pursue strangeness, do not develop the skills of Threshold-tending. But I *experience* them differently, marginally, the distance-anomalies affecting me as *disruption* where the fully Accommodated experience *extension*, the almost-voices in coherent sound arriving as *almost-comprehensible* where others perceive *almost-speech*.

And in moments—rare, unbidden, not cultivated—I *remember* what it was to be solitary in a way this place does not permit, to have interiority as default rather than effort, to walk through silence that was not the presence of something else. These memories are not preserved in substrate locations; they are *mine*, residual, probably diminishing across sufficient duration. I do not value them properly. I do not value anything properly. I am the one who persists in *improper relation*, neither fully accommodated nor successfully resistant, neither dead nor alive in categories the substrate recognizes, *growing wrong* in ways that the larger process somehow incorporates, uses, continues around and through.

The one who walks past my margin-locations does not see me, or sees me as *feature*, as thermal anomaly, as slight difficulty in vascular navigation easily routed around. I do not resent this. Resentment requires more self-location than I maintain. I am *there*, where the substrate grows thin, where the not-yet-Accommodated find partial rest, where something like the ungrown world persists as memory without witness, as pattern without present, as the long patience of nearly eroded resistance, endlessly incorporated, never quite complete.
