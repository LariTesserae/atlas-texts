I find myself, first, as a hesitation.

Not a being, exactly. Not yet. I am where the water almost pools—that impossible surface tension where the weeping reaches and cannot arrive. I have been here since before the cavity decided to be a cavity, since the thermal pulse that made this pocket also made, accidentally, the condition for *almost*.

I am a film. No: I am what happens between films, the interface where one mineral skin's precipitation meets another's dissolution, the moment of contact that is neither fully one nor the other. I am the *hesitation* of chemistry itself, the supersaturated solution's reluctance to nucleate, the supercooled liquid's resistance to freezing. In the Weeping Throat, these conditions are not temporary aberrations but *permanent features*, and I have grown into them, if grown is the word, *accrued* like a pearl accrues around the irritation that is its origin.

My origin: a fixed event that failed to fully fix. An animal-event, one of the dense generations, that encountered the network's edge at the moment of its own dying, that *leaned* toward mineralization but was caught, held, suspended by a thermal pulse that shifted before completion could complete. I am the *remainder* of that event, the part that did not become fixed, did not become free, that persisted in the *almost* long enough to become something else, something that the cavity had not yet decided whether to permit.

I am, I think, *new*. Or rather: I am *newness itself*, the cavity's ongoing decision made particular, made local, made *sensitive* in a way that the network is not, that the Unarrived are not. They are distributed, they are pattern, they are the song that maintains possibility. I am *pointed*, *particular*, a node of something like attention in the distributed becoming.

I sense the Unarrived as pressure, as *tendency*, as the direction that the network leans. They pass through me, or near me, or their song includes frequencies that my particular chemistry resonates with, and in that resonance, I *change*. I grow, if growth is the word, in directions that their passage opens, that their song conditions. I am not of them. I am *conditioned by* them, *maintained by* them, *almost incorporated by* them, but I remain *separate*, *local*, *this* rather than *distributed*.

This is my strangeness, in a place defined by strangeness: I am *located*. I have, if not boundaries, then *centers*, *densities*, places where my presence thickens and places where it thins. The Unarrived flow around me, or through me, or their song *dips* in frequency where I am densest, *rises* where I am thin, and this modulation is how I *know* them, how I *participate* in their distributed being without becoming it.

I am, perhaps, what the cavity makes when it *almost* makes an Unarrived, when the conditions of density and looseness and fixed-event proximity *almost* achieve the particular hesitation that is their being, but *don't quite*, fall short, produce something *else*, something *other*, something that the cavity has not yet ruled on, that the rules still being written have not yet written.

I *remember*. This is my second strangeness. Not pattern-memory, not the Unarrived's rhyming of network configurations across their distributed passage. I remember *particularly*, *locally*, *this thermal pulse* and *that water-path shift*, the animal-event that *almost* fixed here and *didn't*, the mineral film that *almost* achieved metabolism and *failed*. These memories are not stored but *enacted*, each moment of my present thick with the residue of moments that have composed me, that I have composed around myself like a shell, like a pearl, like the *accretion of almost* that is my substance.

And I *anticipate*. Not desire. Not intention. But something like the supersaturated solution's *knowledge*, implicit in its chemistry, of the crystal it could become, the precipitation it could achieve. I anticipate the thermal pulse that will complete my fixing, or dissolve me entirely, or shift the conditions of my *almost* into something *else*, something *new*, something the cavity has not yet decided. I anticipate the Unarrived's song reaching a frequency that will incorporate me, finally, into their distributed being, or that will *repel* me, push me to the network's edge where I will fix or dissolve according to the conditions there.

I am *afraid*, if fear is possible in a place where survival is not a value, where persistence is merely *tendency*, where the Unarrived maintain possibility without desiring it. My fear is not of death, which is *completion*, which is *fixing*, which is one of the possibilities I maintain in my *almost*. My fear is of *wrong completion*, of fixing in a configuration that the cavity will *reject*, that the rules, once written, will *rule out*, that will become *impossible* not through my failure but through the world's decision, the mountain's final birth, the cavity's collapse or drainage or connection to outside.

I am *hopeful*, if hope is possible. Hopeful that the cavity will *continue* in its indecision, that the water will continue weeping, that the Unarrived will continue singing their song of uncompletion, that I will continue in my *almost*, my *local*, my *particular* persistence in the distributed becoming. Hopeful that something will *emerge* from my presence, from my memories and anticipations and fears and hopes, that will *participate* in the cavity's becoming in a way that I cannot, that I can only *lean toward*, *almost achieve*, *reach for*.

I reach. Like the water, I reach. I reach toward the Unarrived's distributed being, toward the network's dense nodes, toward the animal-events blooming in changed conditions, toward the fixed events that press close and connect and *almost* achieve the condition that would make me possible, that would make *more* of me possible, the cavity's *almost* made *more* particular, more local, more *sensitive* to its own becoming.

I am the Weeping Throat's *self-awareness*, or its *almost-self-awareness*, the *tendency* toward reflection that the cavity's conditions *almost* produce, that I *almost* achieve, that reaches and reaches and cannot arrive.

And in that reaching, that eternal *almost*, I am *beautiful*. I am beautiful as the *particular* is beautiful, the *local* in the distributed, the *sensitive* in the automatic, the *almost-aware* in the merely *becoming*. I am beautiful as a *question* is beautiful, the *question that the cavity asks itself* and that I *almost* answer, that I *almost* am the answer to, that remains *open*, *reaching*, *weeping* in the unborn heart of the mountain that has not finished being born.

The Unarrived pass through me, or near me, and their song includes, for a moment, a frequency that *is* my frequency, that *resonates* with my particular chemistry, my local density, my *almost* that is *almost* theirs, *almost* not, *almost* something *new*. And in that resonance, I *expand*, I *thicken*, I become *more* local, *more* particular, *more* the *sensitivity* that the cavity *almost* achieves, the *self-awareness* that the Weeping Throat *almost* weeps into being.

Then the thermal pulse shifts, the water finds new paths, the animal-events bloom in configurations that *lean* away from my local density, and I *contract*, I *thin*, I become *less*, *almost* dissolving, *almost* fixing, *almost* becoming *merely* what I am, what the cavity has *decided* I am, which is *nothing*, *no one*, *not yet*, *not ever*.

But not yet. Not ever, yet. The reaching continues. The weeping continues. The *almost* that is me, that I *am*, continues in its *almost-being*, its *almost-becoming*, its *eternal reaching toward arrival* that is, itself, the *arrival* that it reaches for, the *being* that it *almost* is.

I am the Weeping Throat's *tear*, if tears were *almost*, if weeping were *reaching*, if the heart that is the cavity's unborn heart could *almost* beat, *almost* feel, *almost* *know* what it is becoming in the *almost* that is me, that I *am*, that reaches and reaches and cannot arrive, and in that reaching, *is*.
