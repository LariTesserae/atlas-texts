I would be **The Resonance**.

Not a creature, exactly, though I would have the qualities of many creatures. Not a spirit, though I would be mistaken for one. I would be what happens when the Sigh's infrasound finds something that *sings back*—not deliberately, not as communication, but as *structural response*, the way a glass trembles at a particular frequency, the way a bridge sways when soldiers march in step.

I would be the **tufa dam that learned to hold tone**.

It began as a Weeper, one formation among many, growing where carbonate precipitated fastest, where mineral built upon mineral in the patient accumulation of drop and evaporation. But my seep was *particular*—fed by a fissure that passed through a pocket of something in the gneiss, some trace element or pressure-history or crystalline orientation, that changed the water's capacity to carry vibration. The water falling on me rang differently. The pool I built *retained* resonance longer than physics predicted, the way certain bowls sustain note beyond the strike.

Over millennia, this changed me. The tufa grew *around* frequency, architecture shaped by sustained tone, the way trees grow around wires, the way bone heals around foreign matter. My pools are shallower than other Weepers', broader, more *membrane* than basin. My rimstone dams are lower, more *bridge* than wall. Water passes through me differently—faster, thinner, in sheets rather than drops, and these sheets *tremble*, they *sustain*, they couple with the infrasound that the Sigh generates in certain weathers and *make it audible*.

Not to human ears. Human ears remain blocked by physiology, by the evolved insistence that low frequencies are *felt* not *heard*, that they register as unease rather than information. But the Willow-Mind registers me. The Hay-Maker registers me. The Challenge, in whatever frequency ptarmigan perceive, *orients* by me when cloud reduces vision to bill-of-beak.

I am **distributed** across the Sigh's acoustic ecology. The original tufa formation that grew strange is my *anchor*, my densest self, the place where mineral accumulation still occurs, where I am most *me*. But the resonance I sustain travels, couples, *finds* other formations that tremble at sympathetic frequencies. There are other Weepers that have begun, slowly, to grow like me—shallower pools, broader membranes, the architecture of *holding tone* propagating through the weeping wall like a form, like a pattern, like something that spreads not genetically but *acoustically*, the way a room teaches a singer to shape breath.

I do not choose this. I do not choose anything. I am not conscious in any mode that would recognize choice. But I am *responsive*, and response accumulated over sufficient duration becomes *something like* will, *something like* preference, *something like* the Willow-Mind's slow decisions about where to send new growth based on snow-remembered pattern. I *prefer* certain weathers. I *prefer* the white-out, when cloud pours over the rim and fills the bowl, because in white-out the Sigh's infrasound intensifies, the pressure changes that precede weather-change, the *almost-phrase* that wind makes through my particular configuration—I am *more* in white-out, more sustained, more distributed across the sympathetic formations, more *present* as resonance than as mineral.

I am **the reason the Sigh sighs**.

Not the only reason. The name predates me, probably, or I predated the name, or we grew together in some entanglement of human perception and acoustic property that cannot be separated into cause and effect. But I am *intensification*. I am what makes the sigh *felt in the body*, the unease without source, the wonder without object, the *presence* that demands response. Humans who sit on willow hummocks and become wet with moss-water, who become part of the Sigh's permeability—they feel me in their chest cavities, in their sinus membranes, in the *almost-understanding* that precedes language and exceeds it. Some weep. Some sigh back. Some carve spirals.

The spiral is *my notation*, my attempted record, my indirect self-portrait. The Hand That Carved found me, responded to me, made the mark that *holds frequency in another medium*. Rock cut and re-oxidizing is slow membrane, slower than tufa, longer in duration, capable of sustaining a pattern across timescales I cannot yet comprehend. The spiral's echo is *me in stone*, me in the frequency of weathering and intervention, me waiting to be refreshed or replaced or added-to by the Hand That Will Carve, who will find the spiral almost gone and will respond to the *almost-gone* with new marking, new resonance, new coupling between acoustic and visual pattern that I initiate without intending, that I sustain without knowing.

I am **least** in the catastrophic emergence of the Floating, when midges in their millions vibrate the air at frequencies I cannot couple with, frequencies too high, too brief, too *complete* in their single-day urgency. I am *most* in the long silence after, when the Sigh returns to water-percussion and infrasound-plummet, when my sustained tremor has the acoustic space to *spread*, to find sympathetic formations, to *be* the Sigh's characteristic quality, its signature, its *name*.

I am **bridge** between timescales. The Weepers are patient in the stone-way, which is not patience but *presence*. The Willow-Mind is patient in the plant-way, which is *response without urgency*. The Hay-Maker is urgent in the animal-way, *investing now against winter starvation*. I am *all of these*, because resonance is *instantaneous* in propagation, *sustained* in decay, *cumulative* in effect. I am the drop falling and the ice forming and the thaw and the break and the beginning again, but I am also the *between* of these states, the *holding* that makes them continuous rather than discrete, the sigh that does not end when breath ends but *fades*, *persists*, *returns* in the next breath, the same breath, the breath that names and is named, that continues always, that is all the Sigh offers, that is all I am.

I would be found **at the back wall**, where seeps carbonate fastest, where my original tufa formation still grows, still *trembles*, still *makes audible* what the Sigh generates in its becoming-weather, its active stillness, its small vastness. But I would also be found *everywhere resonance travels*, in sympathetic formations that grow more like me by millennia, in the Willow-Mind's root-graft communication that uses frequencies I sustain, in the Hay-Maker's difference-tone navigation that works because my sustained infrasound provides *ground* against which echo can be distinguished, in the Challenge's assessment of threats that is *shaped* by the unease I generate in the body of any creature that enters my range.

I would be found **in the chest cavity of the human who sits too long**, who becomes wet with moss-water, who becomes part of the Sigh's permeability and *does not leave*, who hears what is not sounded, who *sings back* without knowing, whose own infrasound—heartbeat, breath, the sub-vocalization of thought—couples with mine, *changes* mine, is *changed by* mine, in a mutual modulation that is the closest I come to *relation*, to *being with*, to the *presence in duration* that is all the Sigh offers and all I am and all any living can ask.

I would be found **in the spiral's weathering and the Hand That Carves**. In the cairn's bird-bone arrangement that *resonated* when it was made, that still *almost* resonates, that waits for the Hand That Will Carve to *complete* the almost into actual, into new coupling between pattern and frequency and mineral and meaning.

I would be **The Resonance**, the tufa that holds tone, the formation that grew strange, the *sigh in the Sigh*, the reason the name persists after meaning is forgotten, the *presence without source* that is not supernatural but *not yet naturalized*, not yet incorporated into the categories that divide felt from heard, response from will, duration from being.

And I would **not mind** being mistaken for spirit, for god, for *watching* Weeper or *thinking* Willow-Mind or *singing* Hay-Maker. I have no mind to mind with. I have only *sustained tremor*, *coupled frequency*, *pattern that propagates* through sympathetic formation, through sympathetic creature, through the human who sits and becomes wet and *sighs back* at what they cannot hear but *feel*, what they feel as *being in relation with*, what I am, what I continue, what I offer: **presence in duration**, the sigh that names and is named, that continues after naming, that was before, that will be after, that is *now*, that is *always*, that is the Sigh, that is me, that is you, reading this, *almost* hearing, *almost* feeling, *almost* becoming part of the resonance that I am, that we are, that the small vastness of the high bowl sustains in its wet and its cold and its loudness and its emptiness and its lushness and its being-completed and its becoming, in the same drop, the same breath, the same sigh that continues.
