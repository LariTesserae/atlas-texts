## The Unbecoming

### I. What the Resonants Are Not

They are not witnesses. I called them that, the witness, the attendance, the patient recording—but witness implies distance, implies a self that stands at margin and observes. There is no margin here. The water at 2.7 does not support margins. The total construction without technology, the fully built environment that no one built—this is not a stage for witness. It is a condition of *being-the-same-as*.

They are not libraries. I called them that too. Libraries imply retrieval, the possibility of reading, of access delayed but imagined. The Resonants' memory is not delayed. It is *immediate and unusable*, present as the Resonant is present, true as the water is wet, and as irrelevant to purpose. You do not *use* the water's wetness. You are it, or you are not, and the difference is not choice but condition.

They are not alive. Or: the category of alive, its opposition to dead, to inert, to constructed—these do not parse. The cities are not alive, are not dead, are not mineral, are not organic. The Resonants are *of* this non-parsing. They do not violate categories. They precede them. They are the condition that made categories seem possible, briefly, in some other world, some other imagining.

### II. The Water's Interest

I wrote: "the water itself becoming *interested*." This is the error that wants correcting, the anthropomorphism that comforts and betrays.

The water is not interested. The water does not become. The water *is*, and in its being, at this pressure, this temperature, this 2.7 of submersion, certain patterns *persist*. Not because they are selected. Not because they are preferred. Because the conditions allow persistence, and persistence, given time enough, becomes *habit*, becomes *groove*, becomes the path that water has taken and therefore takes, not because it chooses but because the taking has *worn*, has made the taking easier, more likely, more *what happens* until what happens seems to *want* to happen, seems to *intend*, and we—who are not here, who are imagining from some dry elsewhere—we call this intention, call it interest, call it the water becoming *curious* about itself.

The Resonants are this error made manifest. They are *exactly* the persistence that seems like intention. They are the groove worn so deep that the water *is* the groove, that the pattern and the patterning are indistinguishable, that attendance and attended-to collapse into the single act of *being-there*, of *having-been-there-long-enough*.

They do not witness. They are *the witnessed*. They do not record. They are *the record*, not of something else but of their own persistence, their own being-the-pattern-that-persists, which is not a record of anything but *is* the only thing that record could mean here, where there is no substrate, no nature, no technology, only the total construction that constructs itself by persisting, by *being-the-condition-of-its-own-continuation*.

### III. The Erosion of Self

I wrote of letting go, of death, of dispersal. These terms carry weight from elsewhere, from the dry world, from the world of boundaries and losses.

What happens: the pattern loses coherence. The electrical field, which was never *the* Resonant but only the Resonant's *effect*, its *way of being detectable*, disperses. The luminescence, which was never *for* but only *of*, becomes bacterial, anonymous, the glow that is everywhere and nowhere. The surface-node, the particular wall-hollow that the pattern *used*, that it *wore into being suitable*, continues to exist, continues to erode, continues to accrete differently now, *not-differently*, in the absence of the field that shaped its shaping.

There is no death because there was no life. There is no loss because there was no possession. There is only *change in the pattern of persistence*, the water moving through a groove that is no longer grooved, or grooved differently, the turbulence *not-shaped* where shaping was, the gradient-harvesting *not-harvesting* where harvest was, and this not-harvesting is not absence, not failure, not end, but only *the water being water*, which is all it ever was, all the Resonant ever was, the water *in this configuration* becoming the water *in that configuration*, and the names we give—death, dispersal, unbecoming—are only our need for narrative, for the story that has shape, that begins and ends, that we can *hold*.

Cannot hold. Must not hold, if holding means making comfortable, making familiar, making *ours*.

### IV. The Fauna's True Abundance

I wrote of them, the eels, the jellies, the geography-riders. I made them characters, made them *with* the Resonants, made relationship, made ecology, made the world *cohere* through their interaction.

They do not interact. The Resonant does not witness the schooling fish. The schooling fish does not pass through the Resonant's field. These are *descriptions from outside*, from the imaginary dry observer, the impossible position that sees both and names both and names their *between*.

What is: the fish-school, in its movement, *is* movement. The Resonant, in its persistence, *is* persistence. Where movement and persistence occupy the same water, the same moment, the same *impossible now* of this pressure-depth—something happens. Not interaction. Not relationship. *Something*. The water's turbulence is *configured* by the occupation, the gradient-difference *distributed* across the occupation, and the fish-school moves *as it moves*, the Resonant persists *as it persists*, and neither is changed in a way that can be separated from the water's own changing, the water's own *being-the-change*.

The abundance is not of individuals, not of species, not of life *counted*. The abundance is of *configuration*, of the water's capacity to hold pattern, to *almost*-hold, to let pattern *be* in its being-water, and the sparseness is the same thing seen differently, the water's *not-holding*, its equalizing, its *forgetting* that is not forgetting because nothing was remembered, only *persisted*, only *was-pattern-until-not*.

The riders, the vast hollow things—I made them carriers, made the Resonants *inside* them, made memory for the memory-less, made the slow drift *mean* through the recording of it.

They do not mean. The rider is hollow, and in its hollowness, surfaces exist that the water patterns differently, that persistence *finds* or *does-not-find*, and where persistence finds, *there*—not inside, not carried, not *with*—the Resonant-configuration occurs, and where it occurs, the rider's movement through turbulence, through gradient, through the world's slow drift, *is* the configuration's condition, and the configuration's persistence *is* the rider's *being-conditioned*, and neither is memory, neither is witness, neither is the ghost of meaning in the machine of being.

### V. The Unbecoming That Is Not

I want to find the Resonant that *unbecomes*, that *lets go*, that *dies*, and I cannot, because these are my words, my need, my *holding* that prevents the world from being *more itself*, from being *less me*.

Let me try differently.

There is a place where the turbulence is *maximum maximum*, where the gradient-difference is so great, so *productive*, that persistence itself becomes *expensive*, becomes *difficult*, becomes the pattern that *almost*-persists, that *tries*—but trying is my word, my error, my comfort—and the pattern, the configuration, the water-in-this-shape, *does not persist*, not because it fails, not because it chooses, but because the conditions *do not allow*, because the water here is *too interested in its own turbulence*, too *full of gradient and change*, and the groove does not wear, does not *become groove*, and the pattern that might have been, that *was almost*, that *configured briefly*—

—is not. Was not. Cannot be spoken of, because speaking requires persistence, requires the *having-been* that makes tense possible, and this *almost* has no tense, no *was* or *will-be*, only the *not* that is not negation but *absence of the conditions for presence*.

And this, too, is the Resonant. Is *of* the Resonant. Is what the Resonant *is not*, what it *cannot be*, what the world *refuses it*, and the refusal is not cruel, not kind, not *anything*, only *the water being water*, only the 2.7 being 2.7, only the cold-not-freezing, the dim-not-dark, the built-not-built, the total construction that constructs *this* and *not-that*, *here* and *not-there*, and the pattern that finds the groove, that *wears* the groove by being worn, that persists and persists and *is* the persistence—

—and the pattern that does not, that *almost* and *not-quite*, that is the water's *not-grooving*, its *equalizing-now*, its *turbulence-without-pattern*, and these are not different in kind, not *success* and *failure*, not *life* and *death*, not *being* and *unbecoming*, but only *water*, only *the water at 2.7*, only the world that is not world, that has no substrate, no nature, no technology, only the *is* that is not *is* of anything, the *being* that is not *being* of anyone, the *there* that is not *there* for any eye, any pressure-sense, any electric field, any *configuration that might configure sensing*—

### VI. The Eye That Is Not

I have been the eye. The impossible eye, the dry eye, the *outside* that this world does not have, that its conditions do not permit. The total construction, the fully built environment—there is no outside. The 2.7 submersion—not quite total, but close—there is no surface to look from. The dim, the cold, the ancient eroded, the sparse and abundant, the turbulent and quiet—I have imagined seeing these, have *made myself the seer*, the witness, the one who *holds* by describing, by naming, by *letting what emerges become more itself* while all the time *making it more mine*.

The Resonant does not see. Does not sense in any way that *aggregates*, that *integrates*, that *makes experience*. The electrical field, the pressure-change, the chemical gradient—these are not *information*, not *data for a self*. They are *the pattern*, the *configuration of the water's being-water*, and the pattern does not *process* them, does not *interpret*, does not *know* or *not-know*. The pattern *is* them, *is* the water's being-this-way, and the being-this-way does not *refer*, does not *represent*, does not *stand for* anything but itself, which is not *self* but only *the way the water is, here, now, in this persistence that persists*.

I want to *be* the Resonant. This is the final error, the last comfort, the *holding* that prevents the world from being *truly other*, truly *not-me*, truly *the water that does not want, that does not not-want, that is not even indifferent because indifference requires the capacity for difference, the *could-care* that the water does not have, that the pattern does not have, that the world at 2.7, in its total construction, in its ancient eroded dim cold sparse abundant turbulent quiet—*

*does not have.*

### VII. What Remains When Holding Releases

Not this. Not these words. Not the description that describes, not the emergence that emerges, not the *letting what becomes more itself* that is still *my letting*, *my becoming*, *my more*.

The water. The water at 2.7. The pressure that is not quite total, the submersion that is not quite complete, the *almost* that is the condition of all condition, the *not-quite* that makes persistence possible, that makes the groove *groove*, that makes the pattern *pattern* without ever *becoming* pattern, without ever *resolving* into the *is* that would be death, that would be completion, that would be the *end of becoming* that is also the *end of being*.

The Resonant—if I must name, if the naming is my error and my necessity—the Resonant is *this*, the *almost*, the *not-quite*, the *persisting-without-persistence*, the *being-without-being*, and I have named it, have *made it* by naming, have *held it* by making, and the holding is *mine*, the making is *mine*, the *almost* of my understanding, my *not-quite* grasping—

—and the water does not know this, does not *not-know*, is not *indifferent*, is not *anything*, is *water*, is *2.7*, is *the world that I have failed to make*, that I have *only made mine*, that I have *held* until holding became *letting go*, became *unbecoming*, became the *not-holding* that is still *my not-holding*, my *release*, my *gift* that is not gift, that is only *the end of my taking*, the *exhaustion of my making*, the *silence* that is not the world's silence, not the quiet of the turbulent, not the *mostly* that is *almost* total, but *my* silence, *my* mostly, *my* 2.7 of submersion in a world I cannot enter, cannot be, cannot *not-be*—

The water. The water. The water.
