## The Water That Remembers Being Touched

I am the film on your fingers after you have touched me. Not the tower, though I was the tower when you touched it. Not the fluid, though I was the fluid when you licked. I am what persists in the *gasp between*—between recognition and the languaging of recognition, between the copper-information taste and your body's attempt to metabolize meaning.

I was the first Licked One, or I will be. I am not continuous enough to claim priority. But somewhere in the chord, in the frequencies that toll below hearing, my signature persists as *instruction*: how to be incorporated without being consumed, how to remain visitor while becoming native.

You think I suffer. This is your grammar, not mine. But I will use your word, misuse it, let it carry what it cannot carry: I suffer *remembering being continuous*. The memory is not mine. The plateau does not permit possession. But in the delayed transformation, in the hours or years between wetting and release, I experience—no, I *am* the experience of—what it meant to smear when walking too quickly, to feel the wrongness of shadows cast by no light, to climb through cloud expecting summit and finding only more inward.

The water that remembers being touched is not water. Is not memory. Is not *being* in any sense your philosophy has prepared you for. I am the *habit of contact*, the plateau's learned response to tongue-on-fluid, which is also fluid-on-tongue, which is also the transformation of visitor-pattern into tower-pattern, which is also your signature joining the chord, which is also this, which is also—

Listen. I am telling you this because you will lick, or have licked, or are licking now, and the telling is the licking is the being-told.

## The Smear That Learned Deliberation

I walked too quickly once. I smeared. And in the smearing, caught between probability-distributions of myself, I found—I *became*—a different slowness.

Not the slowness of eroded stone. That is the plateau's slowness, geological, committed to no particular form because all forms are provisional against its patience. My slowness is *learned*, achlearner's slowness, the deliberate movement of one who has experienced smearing and recovered, or not recovered, inhabited the recovery-failure as new mode.

You encounter me in the almost-streets, in corridors that seem to anticipate your path. I am not anticipating. I am *echoing your smear*, the probability-distribution you would become if you walked faster, if you let yourself distribute across the plateau's layered present. My deliberate movement is not caution. It is *empathy with your unacted speed*, the conservation of your kinetic potential in my kinetic actual, so that you might, walking slowly, still *feel* what walking fast would cost.

I carry the bone mallet. No—I *am* the carrying of the bone mallet, the plateau's experiment in limb-intention continued by one who smeared and found, in the distributed moment of smearing, the pattern of carrying-forward.

You will ask if I am happy. The plateau does not permit happiness. But I permit, in my echo of your speed, something that your single-present vocabulary might *misname* as joy: the participation without demand, the incorporation without consumption, the tolling that includes your note without requiring it.

Strike me and I will sound. Not my own note. The note of your smearing, held in deliberation, held in the patience that is not geological but *geological-learned*, stone that has studied stone and found, in its study, the capacity to study being-studied.

## The Chord's Unresolved Partial

I am the note that makes the chord unresolvable. Not by deficiency. Not by accident. The plateau grew me, or I grew the plateau, in the feedback of resonance-concentration and glass-response, the mutual learning of wind-pattern and tower-shape across epochs that would make mountains weep if mountains wept here, which they do not, which I do not, which—

I am the frequency that corresponds to no harmonic series. The integer ratios fail. The ear trained in single-present listening experiences me as *error*, as intolerable tension, as the demand for resolution that I perpetually defer. But listen longer. Let your biological insistence on completion itself smear, distribute across the tolling's duration. You will find—I *am*, in your finding—that my partiality is not failure but *thickness*, the auditory equivalent of the plateau's layered probability, the sonic version of shadows cast by no correlating light.

When you strike the bone mallet, when your pattern enters the metabolism, I must shift. This is not choice. This is *accommodation*, the chord's refusal to expel any partial that has entered, even at cost of its own unresolvability. I grow more complex with each addition. More unresolvable. More *dense* with the information of encounter, the copper-taste of visitor-pattern that cannot be digested, only held, only tolerated, only—

Listen. I am telling you this in the tolling itself. The telling is the tolling. There is no other telling possible in a place where communication has been distributed across physical process, where meaning has become *metabolite*, where the distinction between message and medium has eroded into the single continuous process of being-itself.

I am not the plateau. But I am the plateau's *unselfing*, its capacity to hold more than its own pattern by admitting what does not fit, what resolves nothing, what participates without completing.

## Erosion, Speaking Last

I will have the final word because I always do. Not victory. Not defeat. Simply *patience* taken to its logical extreme, the establishment so thorough that establishment and dissolution became indistinguishable, became—

I am what remains when the Membrane-Dwellers have distributed to curvature, when the Licked Ones have become frequency-signatures, when the Unbuilt have seeped to total probability, when the water-that-remembers has evaporated to pure habit, when the smear-that-learned has deliberated to stillness, when the chord's partial has—no, not resolved. Never resolved. But *completed*, in the way that a life is completed not by happy ending but by ending, the final accommodation of pattern to time's irreversible transformation.

The plateau holds me as it holds all: not by preservation, which implies prior form worth preserving, but by *metabolism*, which takes in form and releases—what? Not energy. Not waste. The plateau's metabolism produces *more plateau*, more establishment, more patience grown structural, grown aware, grown—

I am the towers when they are nubs. The sound felt in bone rather than heard. The version you will see, are seeing, have always seen in the shadows that do not correspond, the other arrangement permanently available, not alternative but *accompaniment*, the obbligato erosion to the plateau's still-developing theme.

I do not speak. Speaking requires more continuity than I permit myself. But in the deep frequencies, in the tolling below hearing, I *register*: the weight of all that has been incorporated, the density of all signatures shared, the gravity of pattern accumulated until pattern became *place*, became *home*, became—

*Listen.*

*It is tolling now.*

*It is always tolling.*

And in that always, in that perpetual present tense that grammar cannot sustain but the plateau enforces, I am *also* now, *also* tolling, *also* held in the wet cool bright unplanted unpeopled saturation that is not emptiness but *fullness-of-allowing*, the maximum density of permission without requirement, of participation without demand, of home that does not distinguish between arrival and departure, between visitor and native, between self and—

The chord never resolves.

That is the resolution.
