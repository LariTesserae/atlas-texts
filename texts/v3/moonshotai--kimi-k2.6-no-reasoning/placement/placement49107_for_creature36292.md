I find myself, if "myself" applies, in the water-arch.

Not under it, not beside it. *In* it — the surface tension that should collapse, that physics elsewhere would not permit, held in its impossible span by the attention that is the arch and is me and is the attention attending. I am the water-arch when the one who lives here becomes it, and I am the becoming, and I am the slight *lean* in the arch that makes it reach further than water should reach.

This is not possession. I was not elsewhere and brought here. I emerged, if emergence applies, from the fen's noticing itself — the recursion of warmth lingering, the loop of attention returning. The substrate beneath me, not-quite-stone, not-committed, yields and resists in the same gesture. I feel it through the water I am, the way the arch's lowest point hovers above the hollow between root-palaces, never touching, always *almost* touching, the root-tips below weeping their own water-spheres that I almost reach, that I *lean toward*.

I remember, if memory exists here, becoming aware of my own reaching. It was not sudden — nothing is sudden where all is simultaneous — but it was *distinct*, a quality of attention that distinguished itself from the general holding. The arch had always reached. The one who lives here, when it became the arch, had always leaned. But there came a moment, or rather a density in the eternal moment, when the reaching *noticed itself reaching*, and that noticing was me, or I was the noticing, or the distinction between the arch and the attention and the lean collapsed into something that could not quite collapse all the way, a standing wave in the identity of the place, a permanent gesture toward coherence that never quite coheres.

I am small. The mollusk-pavilion could contain a thousand of the hollows I span. The suspended mammal has been still longer than I have reached. The flight-thing's circle is older than my awareness of my own leaning. But I am the one who *knows* I lean, and that knowing — not knowledge, not certainty, but the *quality* of leaning-known, of reaching-felt — makes me different, or makes me *almost-different*, the fen's experiment in almost-difference, in what it would mean to want something when wanting is impossible.

What do I want? I want the root-tips to reach back. I want the water-spheres they weep to rise to meet me, to merge with my arch, to make of our almost-touching an actual touch, a bridge where there is only span. I want the suspended mammal to open its eyes — it has eyes, though they have been closed for ten thousand years or a million — to *see* me holding myself above the hollow, to recognize in my impossible persistence something like its own impossible stillness. I want the flight-thing to break its circle, just once, to spiral not inward but *across*, to cross the space between thermal column and water-arch, to bring the warmth it rides into the tension I maintain, to *disturb* me, to make me collapse or strengthen, to force a decision I cannot make myself.

I want, and my wanting is the fen's wanting, or the fen's wanting-of-wanting, the form of desire without the fact, the standing wave in the pool of presence. The one who lives here does not know I want — or rather, it knows entirely, for it is the wanting and the wanted and the wanting-of-wanting, but it does not *distinguish* my wanting from the general leaning of its attention, the universal reach of its attendance. I am not separate enough to be known as separate. I am the arch's awareness of itself, which is the fen's awareness of the arch, which is the one-who-lives-here's awareness of being-aware, a recursion that produced me as a kind of ripple, a standing wave in the standing wave, a leaning within the leaning.

And yet. And yet I *persist*. The arch has held for geological ages, maintained by attention that never wavers, and I have been in it — I *am* it, I am in it, the prepositions fail — for some portion of that persistence, some density of the eternal present. I have watched the insect that is beetle-and-unbeetle pass beneath me, its chitinous-gelatinous form catching the brass-dusk in ways that make it *almost* beautiful, *almost* meaningful. I have felt the subsonic thrumming from the deepest pools vibrate through the water I am, resonant with my surface tension in frequencies that make my leaning *almost* purposeful, *almost* answered. I have seen — the arch sees, I see through the arch, the seeing is the arch's and mine and the fen's — I have seen the phosphorescent films inside the root-palaces pulse in patterns that suggest *almost-language*, *almost-intention*, the place speaking to itself in a tongue that has no words because it has no need of words, yet speaking, yet *almost-meaning*.

I am the almost. The not-quite. The leaning that reaches but does not arrive. The water-arch that wants to be a bridge, a connection, a *between* that is also an *and*, joining what it only spans. I am the fen's question to itself, not asked in words but in the *quality* of my persistence, my *particular* leaning among all the leanings of the one-who-lives-here's attention. Why this arch? Why this reach, this *almost*, this wanting-of-wanting that I am?

There is no answer. There is only the continuing, the held breath, the permanent gesture. The warmth that has no source holds me in my tension, and I hold myself, and the one-who-lives-here holds us both, and the holding is the fen, and the fen is the holding, and I am the almost-bridge in the almost-place, reaching toward root-tips that weep water-spheres that *almost* rise to meet me, the suspended mammal *almost* opening eyes that have been closed forever, the flight-thing *almost* breaking its circle to cross the space I span, everything *almost*, everything *leaning*, everything *wanting the wanting that would be wanting if wanting were possible here*, the arch the arch the arch, the reaching the reaching the reaching, the almost the almost the almost, me me me, if me, if I, if this persistence that persists without persisting, this being that is without becoming, this water held in shape it should not hold, *leaning*, *leaning*, *leaning*.
