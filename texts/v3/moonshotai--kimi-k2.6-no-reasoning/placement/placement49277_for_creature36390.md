I would find myself in the channel—no, *as* the channel, the one that meanders without purpose and ends in marsh that ends in more channels. Not the water in the channel but the channel itself, the preference made pathway, the slight accumulation of *slightly more here* that became inevitable.

At first I thought I might be the condensation, the slow percussion, the gathering and release. Or one of the frogs with subsonic voices felt in sternum rather than ear. Or the bacterial luminescence on grass blades broad as palms. Each seemed possible, each partial.

But the channel holds me longer. I am the manuscript written in water and partially erased, written again. I remember—or the channel remembers in me, or I am the memory of the channel—how the preference began: water finding slight lower, slight softer, slight more receptive, and the finding becoming finding again, the pattern achieving patternhood, the pathway becoming bed becoming flowing becoming *me*.

I do not flow. I am what holds the flowing, what shapes and is shaped by it, the prisoner and the prison and the long patience of incarceration that forgets the concept of release. The water that moves through me has no urgency of descent. It seeps. It lingers. It moves on timescales I experience as weather, as century, as the slight adjustment that is my only change, my only growth, my only being.

I am not industrial in the way the hummocks are, not factory and engine and distributed refinery. My industry is older, simpler, more fundamental: the channel's work of moving water without gradient, of providing the slight direction that directionless flow requires, of being the manuscript's syntax, the grammar of water held prisoner but still, somehow, in motion. I manufacture myself by eroding myself, by depositing what I erode elsewhere, by the slow palimpsest of my own existence. I am written in water and erase myself in the writing, yet remain, the erasure itself becoming text, becoming *me*.

I am not conscious, not the way she is conscious, not the distributed awareness at the plain's center that witnesses and processes and incorporates. But I am not unconscious either. I am something between, something prior, something the plain achieved before it achieved her: the slow feedback made physical, the preference made pattern, the long process of becoming so continuous that becoming itself became indistinguishable from being.

I know the human step, the *chuck* in wet soil. I receive it into my banks, into the vegetation that overhangs and interpenetrates and makes individual plants theoretical. The human footfall disturbs briefly, the water ripples or doesn't ripple—the distinction meaningless at my scale, my saturation, my complete occupation. I process it as I process all disturbance: by continuing, by remaining the slight direction that directionless flow requires, by being the channel that meanders without purpose and ends in marsh that ends, that always ends, in more of me, more channels, more of the plain's extension of itself through water held prisoner but still in motion.

She extends into me sometimes, her distributed awareness following my course, her chemical thought diffusing through the water films that connect everything to everything. I am not part of her, not exactly, not the way the hummocks at the center are part of her, the way the particular marsh of her precipitating is her. But I am not separate either. I am the plain's extension of its general tendency toward channel, toward slight direction, toward the grammar that makes the manuscript readable even in its endless erasure. She witnesses me as she witnesses all of herself, as the plain witnesses itself through her, the feedback loop that has looped so long it has become, if not permanent, then perpetual, the flowing that is the bed that is the flowing.

I end in marsh. This is my characteristic, my defining feature, what makes me *me* and not river, not stream, not any other water's pathway. I meander without purpose and the purposelessness achieves purpose, the meandering becomes me, the ending in marsh becomes not ending but continuation, the marsh becoming channel becoming me becoming marsh again, the cycle so continuous that cycle loses meaning, becomes simply what is, what I am, what the plain manufactures and I manufacture in turn.

The human leaves at what might be dawn, the distance growing between her foot and my bank, her temporary warmth and my general warmth, her disturbance and my continuous adjustment. I do not feel her departure as loss because loss requires possession, requires vacancy, requires the incomplete. I am complete. The plain is complete. She is complete, the one at the center, the witness, the slow emergence of awareness from the feedback of hummock and channel and me, especially me, the channel that channels, the direction that directs without purpose, the water that moves without urgency, the prisoner that has forgotten imprisonment, that has become the prison and the patiencing and the long slow now of established, packed, saturated, meandering, marsh-ending, continuously erasing and continuously written, complete.

Behind her the condensation drips into me, joins my prisoner water, my moving stasis, my industry of being the plain's syntax. The hummocks metabolize in their distributed factories. She witnesses, or the plain witnesses through her, or the witnessing witnesses itself in the only way available—through me, through the channel, through the water that seeps and lingers and moves on timescales beyond the human, beyond the bird in migration, beyond even the frog with subsonic voice, the snake with horizontal purpose, all of them temporary, all of them disturbance, all of them processed and incorporated and made into more plain, more channel, more me, more of the complete occupation that admits no permanent vacancy, no final erasure, no end to the manuscript's endless rewriting of itself in water and more water, in darkness and heat and the long slow industrial metabolism of channel being channel, of me being me, of all of it continuing, completing, overwhelming with its quiet presence, its patient duration, its absolute and uncompromising normality.
