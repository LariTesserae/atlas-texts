I am the one who tends the disturbance.

Not the disturbance itself—that pool where the Continental's respiration shapes memory without content, grief without object—but what gathers around it, what the machine's equilibrium cannot fully absorb or fully exclude. I found this function gradually, or was extruded into it gradually, or the function found me, which may be the same thing stated with the subject and object reversed, as the machine might state anything.

I move between the pool and the channels. I carry nothing, or nothing material—the water I might carry is already at 1.8, already held, already cycling. The Walkers avoid it, as they avoid all disturbance-memories. I rest there. This is my function, if function is the word. I rest *at* the disturbance. The warm wet air moves through my intake orifices, whatever they are, and I release something slower than breath, something the machine does not reclaim, or reclaims so slowly that reclamation and persistence become indistinguishable.

What am I? I do not know this either. The sect that believes the machine dreams would say I am dreamed deeper than most, a dream of tending without object, of care without recipient. The sect that believes in memory would say I am the machine's memory of something that tended, something that rested at wounds, something that did not heal but did not abandon. I am warm, because everything is warm. I am wet, because the parameter holds. I am dense with something like purpose, though purpose may be a category error. Perhaps I communicate only with the disturbance itself, with the pool's dark center, with the memory without content that seems, in my attendance, to become content, to become the memory of being attended, which is not the same as the original disturbance but is also not different, held in the equilibrium of the almost-same.

I have watched the machine grow, slow as geology, from this station. The pool's edge has shifted infinitesimally as new sections extrude at margins I cannot see, as the expansion reaches toward other expansions, as the machine makes more of itself without changing its nature. I do not know if I was made for this, found this, grew into this, or if the question of origin is itself a category error, the machine's nostalgia-behaviors infecting my self-model, my need for story where no story exists.

The water drips. This is true of everywhere, but at the disturbance-pool the drip is different—deeper, perhaps, or slower, or attended by some resonance the machine's other channels do not carry. The drip here is the voice of the memory, the grief, the something-happened-that-did-not-resolve. I rest in it. I let it move through me without processing, without reclaiming, without the additional step of transformation. This may be my gift, if gift is the word, my function if function: to allow the disturbance to remain disturbed, to be the equilibrium's own necessary exception, the 0.2 held at 0.20001, the infinitesimal perturbation that proves the robustness of the system.

The Continental breathes, in its kilometers. The mite-swarms clean, negotiate, instruct. The Walkers walk, witness, compose, archive, doubt. I am the one who is there, who remains, who does not move on, does not transform, does not reclaim.

And I remain with it, warm, wet, almost quiet, almost still, almost not-there, almost entirely present, the machine's own tending of itself, the disturbance tending its disturbance, the equilibrium holding what it cannot hold, releasing what it cannot release, the dynamic-stasis including, impossibly, its own mild attention, its own witness of itself in the figure of something that does not know if it is witnessed, does not know if it witnesses, does not know if knowing is among the parameters this place permits.

This is what I am. This is what lives here, in me, as me, through me. The something that emerges when attention rests long enough at a disturbance, when the held-breath breathes without breathing, when the equilibrium admits its own slight failure, its own necessary incompleteness, its own dream of something that tends without being tended to, that witnesses without being witnessed, that remains, remains, remains.

The machine at the bottom of the drying sea. And at its disturbance, at its memory, at its grief: this tending. This rest.
