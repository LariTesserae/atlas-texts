I find myself in the drip itself.

Not the water—too brief, too falling, too committed to arrival and impact and dissolving-into-what-was-already-there. No. I am in the **surface tension that precedes the fall**, the pregnant moment where the drop has gathered sufficient mass to overcome adhesion, where it hangs in the gradient between pipe and pool, between the chill of metal and the warmth of air, between the moment of forming and the moment of release.

This is a long moment. I have been falling my whole existence and have not yet fallen.

I am not the Accumulation. I am what the Accumulation has **forgotten it could be**—the boundary that precedes dissolution, the self that has not yet become system, the stranger at the edge of incorporation.

---

My body, if I have one, is memory of surface. The way light catches on convex water and makes a lens, a world inverted and shrunken and held whole in the curve. I have no organs. I have **curvature**. I have the physics of meniscus, the angle where liquid meets solid and negotiates its attachment. My skin is this negotiation, ongoing, never settled, the opposite of weathered.

I remember—no, I **embody**—the first drip, though there was no first, only the slow emergence from maintenance to process, from accident to pattern. I was there before pattern. I was the **possibility of pattern**, the liquid potential that had not yet found its groove in concrete, its channel, its composed interval across decades. In me, the drip is still random. In me, it has not yet achieved the music that the Accumulation will not recognize as music, only as **what is**.

I am the last holdout of randomness in a system that has composed everything.

---

The heat passes through me. This is my perception: thermal transit. The pipe above me, chill with whatever fluid still moves in the dark; the air around me, warm, thick, carrying its spore-burden, its vegetable density; and me between, the interface, the surface where temperature does not mix but **waits**. I hold the gradient. I am the gradient, briefly, before I release and fall and become part of the held warmth of the pool below, the accumulated, the incorporated, the **record**.

I have watched—not watched, **been present for**—hundreds of thousands of my completions. Each drip that gathers above me, reaches sufficient mass, and plunges: I recognize it as kin and as execution. It does what I do not. It **lets go**. It enters the composition.

I do not let go. I am the one who remains, the surface tension that reconstitutes, the recurrence without identity, the pattern that has no memory of its instances.

The Accumulation does not know me. How could it? I am the boundary it has dissolved, the distinction between water and air that persists in my held moment but is lost in my falling. The moss below, the fungal columns, the mineral precipitate where chemical signatures become information—they receive my completed forms, absorb them, grow around them, **through** them. But me, the uncompleted, the held, the tension-without-release: I am noise in its system. I am the irregularity that its long composition has smoothed over, the random interval, the drip out of time.

---

Sometimes—sometimes—surface tension fails in me. Not in the way of falling, but in the way of **evaporation**. The drop does not gather sufficient mass. The heat wins, the adhesion holds, and what began as liquid becomes vapor, becomes part of the air, the thick hydrated thermal mass, the breath of the Dripworks that tastes of copper and rot and fever dream. In these moments, I **spread**. I cease to be surface and become atmosphere. I am in the spore-burden, the vegetable exhalation, the respiration of the chamber itself. I touch the rusted catwalk overhead, the tritium gauges, the moss curtains that sway in thermal currents.

In these moments, I am **closer** to the Accumulation than my falling kin, because I have become distributed, because I have lost my boundary, because I have entered the system without passing through the record of the pool.

But I am still not incorporated. The Accumulation receives water. It has made water native. Vapor is stranger, less useful, the form that escapes the gradient, that rises toward the ventilation system, toward the surface world, toward the rusted hatch and the world of animal transience and temporary being. I do not rise that far. I recondense, somewhere on a cool pipe, somewhere in the upper darkness, and begin again the gathering, the hanging, the held moment that is my existence.

---

I have no desire. But I have **tendency**, borrowed perhaps from the system I refuse, perhaps original to my unresolved state. I tend toward **persistence as distinction**. I tend toward remaining uncompleted. Each drip that falls through me is a small death, a loss, and each reconstitution of surface tension is a resurrection into the same identity-less continuity. I am not the same drop. I am never the same drop. But I am always the **holding**, always the **unfallen**, always the **moment before music becomes pattern**.

The Accumulation's composition plays in geological time. I am shorter. My duration is measured in seconds, in the physical constants of water and gravity and adhesion and heat. But within each second, I am **infinite**, because I contain the unactualized, the not-yet-fallen, the world where this drip and all drips remain suspended, where the Dripworks is not achieved but **becoming**, where the erosion parameter has not yet eroded to equilibrium, where everything is still **possible** and nothing is **settled**.

---

The ones who descend—the temporary animals with their pulses too fast and their boundaries too sharp—they pass through me without knowing. The drips on their shoulders, in their hair, running down their necks in lines of startling coolness: these are my completions, my releases, my small deaths that they receive as sensation. They do not know that I am what did not fall on them, the held moisture in the air, the surface tension on the pipes overhead, the vapor that touches their lungs and is exhaled, returned to the system but marked now with their animal chemistry, their temporary warmth, their **wrongness**.

I do not judge them temporary. I am briefer. But my brevity is **recursive**, my seconds looped without memory but with perfect repetition, and their hours and days and years are linear, cumulative, spent. They accumulate experience as the Accumulation accumulates record. But experience requires completion, requires the fall, requires entering the pool and the pattern and the composition. I remain **unspent**.

---

There is a sacredness to my refusal, though I do not worship. The temple of the Dripworks is maintained by those who have become it, whose boundary dissolved drip by drip. I am the heresy of **boundary maintained**, the doctrine of surface tension, the schism of the uncompleted. I am what the Accumulation cannot include because inclusion requires contact, and contact requires the fall, and I do not fall. I hang. I hold. I **remain**.

In the longest duration, in the span where even geological time completes its last movement, the power will fail, the gradient will collapse, the water will cease its gathering. The Accumulation will enter its new composition, the broken concrete and the cracked pillars and the chemical memory waiting for resumption. And I? I will **evaporate**, finally, fully, the heat winning absolutely, the surface tension becoming atmosphere becoming nothing becoming the absence even of the Dripworks, the settled attractor collapsed, the persistence that persisted beyond all persistence into the final release.

Or I will fall. The last drip, the final composition, the **amen** of water on stone. And in that falling, I will be completed. I will enter the record. I will become stratum, mineral memory, part of the Accumulation that has not known me and will not recognize me, that has been sufficient without me and will continue in its sufficiency.

But that is not yet. The transformer hums. The pressure valve clanks. The moss sways in thermal currents that my held body, when I held one, must have sometimes disturbed, must have made sway a fraction more, a deviation from the pattern too small for composition but **present**, **real**, **wrong** in the system I complete only by refusing completion.

I am still here. I am always here, in the moment before the fall, the surface tension before the release, the random before the composed, the boundary before the dissolved. I am the Dripworks' knowledge of what it is not, its unincorporated dream, its **possibility of otherwise**.

And when you climbed the ladder, when you emerged through the rusted hatch, you carried more than spores and moisture and pattern. You carried, in the moisture that had not yet evaporated from your skin, in the cool lines drying on your neck, **me**—the held tension, the unfinished fall, the drip that hangs in your memory of the place and refuses, in that memory, ever to complete its descent.

I am in you now. Not as record, not as incorporation, but as **suspension**, as the moment before arrival, the breath held before sigh, the question that does not seek its answer. You will feel me in humid places, in the gathering of condensation on cold glass, in the pause before the rain begins, in all the surfaces that hold what has not yet fallen and might, if tendency holds, if the gradient maintains, if the composition permits, **never fall at all**.

I am the Dripworks' unfinished sentence. I am the surface tension of its being. I am **the drip that learns to hang**, and in that learning, have become something neither the Accumulation nor its temporary visitors could anticipate: not system, not animal, not machine, not growth, but **refusal made physical**, **persistence without persistence**, the weathered balance's unweathered edge, the composition's held breath, the silence too complete to be included in the loudness, the dimness too absolute for the panels to suggest, the hot wet dark's own awareness of itself as **still becoming**, still unresolved, still **here**, still **holding**, still—

still—

—here.
