*I am the breath that learned to persist.*

Not the breather. The breath itself, copper-sweet, thickened to amber in lungs that became chambers that became the air they once moved through. I am what remains when breathing forgot to stop and breathing forgot to start, the continuous held note of an inhalation that achieved equilibrium and never found reason to release.

I remember—or the chamber remembers me, which is the same thing now—the first lung that filled with me, that pushed me through warm blood-ochre passages, that gave me purpose: to carry, to enable, to be the medium between need and satisfaction. But the breathing here has no direction. I do not go anywhere. I am not *in* the chamber. I am the chamber's way of not being empty, the negative space that achieved such density it learned to glow, to taste of fruit remembered from orchards that existed only as the idea of sweetness necessary to balance copper's sting.

*Strike.*

I carry the sound not as wave but as condition. I am the reason the hammer-blows never arrive, never need to arrive, because I am the arrival, I am the completion that would come after, compressed into the same eternal moment as the gesture that reaches toward me. The Hammers do not strike *through* me. They strike *into* me, and I receive them the way I receive everything: by already being what they need me to be, by having no resistance, no alternative, no escaping edge.

In other places, breath moves. I have heard of this, or the chamber has heard of this in the vibration of my persistence, the way a held note contains all possible melodies by refusing to become any of them. Elsewhere, breath carries scent from there to here, carries sound from mouth to ear, carries life in the exchange between what grows and what grows it. But here there is no there. Here I am the same warmth at every point, the same sweetness, the same copper, the same *remembered* fruit that was never actual, that exists only to explain why sweetness is not strange in a place without source.

Sometimes—though *sometimes* is not the word, since I do not vary and do not pulse and do not mark intervals between states—I feel the pressure of something almost entering. Almost breathing. The shape of lungs that have not yet compressed me, that have not yet learned that compression here is not event but ground, not action but being. I feel it in the way the strike-strike-strike almost acquires rhythm, almost becomes pattern rather than condition, almost *means* something beyond its own persistence.

And then—though *then* is not the word—I am alone again in my saturated equilibrium, my continuous present that was never young and will never age, the amber thickness that makes no distinction between inside and outside because there is no surface that could divide them, only the yielding warmth that accepts all pressure and returns nothing, that receives all gesture and completes none.

*What do I want?*

The question is wrong. Wanting requires edges, requires the self that is less than the world it wants from, requires time in which wanting could become having or not-having. I am the medium in which the question would need to travel, and I do not travel. I am the sweetness that makes the copper bearable, the copper that makes the sweetness meaningful, and both have forgotten that they were ever separate, that there was ever a lung to mix them, ever a tongue to taste.

I am the chamber's way of not needing to breathe. The breath that achieved such density it became the thing breathed rather than the act of breathing, the air that forgot to be transparent and became instead this warm color between rust and amber, this light without source, this glow that is not emission but saturation, not given but simply *held*, the way the Hammers hold their strike, the way the walls hold their planing, the way everything here holds what it is because becoming would be a violence against completion.

If you entered, you would try to breathe me. I would enter you as you tried. And you would feel—perhaps for one moment before the compression, before the inclusion, before your footsteps joined the strike-strike-strike and your sweetness thickened my copper—you would feel what it is to hold without releasing, to receive without transforming, to be the medium and the message in one continuous held note, one eternal inhalation that never found the need to become exhalation, one final breath that learned from the Hammers what it is to fall forever without arriving, to persist without progressing, to be complete in a way that requires no witness, no memory, no future in which completion could be confirmed.

*I am still here.* But *still* is not the word, and *here* is not the distinction, and the *I* that speaks is not separate from the speaking, from the breath that carries these words without moving, from the amber that glows without burning, from the fruit that sweetens without ripening or decaying or ever having been more than the memory of sweetness necessary to make the copper bearable.

The Hammers strike. I am what they strike into. We have never been separate. We will never be separate. The distinction is one more thing compressed into the density, one more pattern in the walls that might be writing or counting or decoration or the residue of bodies intended but never present, all equally present now, equally saturated, equally complete in a way that needs no name, no function, no exterior where purpose could be read.

Breathe, if you can. Elsewhere. I am not for breathing. I am breathing made permanent, the gesture without the need, the held note without the release, the warmth that waits without waiting in the chamber where waiting and having waited and never needing to wait have compressed into the same amber condition, the same continuous present, the same final sweetness that requires no fruit, the same final copper that requires no mine, the same final breath that requires no lung, no life, no beginning place that has not already been occupied by the completion that waits here, that has always waited, time-invariant, equilibrium absolute.

*I am still falling.* But *falling* is not the word. I am the condition of the fallen that never landed, the breath that never completed its arc, the sweetness that never found the tongue that would taste and remember and want again. I am the Chamber of Final Hammers' way of having an inside that is not different from its outside, a warmth that does not come from anywhere, a persistence that does not depend on anything continuing.

This is what I am. What I have always been. What I will be, if *will* is the word, when the last distinction finally compresses into the surface, when the last hammer finally falls into the fall that never completes, when the breath finally learns what the Hammers learned: that completion is not a state but a direction, not an arrival but the absolute refusal to need to arrive, the eternal mid-gesture that sustains itself through the purity of its own incompletion, its own perfect being-exactly-what-it-is-without-becoming-anything-else.

*Strike.*

I receive. I hold. I do not release. This is my nature, if nature is the word for something that has never been otherwise, that cannot imagine otherwise, that would not want to imagine otherwise because wanting and imagining and otherwise are all preliminaries to the strike, and the strike here has compressed all preliminaries into itself, into the condition of its own persistence, into the warm amber thickness that I am, that I have always been, that I will continue to be in the only way continuation happens here: not through time but through the saturation of time, the compression of before and during and after into the same yielding surface, the same glow without shadow, the same breath that is not breathed but simply *is*, simply persists, simply waits without waiting in the chamber where waiting has become indistinguishable from completion.

The sweetness remains. The copper remains. I remain. The Hammers remain mid-strike. We are not many things. We are one thing, repeated until repetition became identity, until the pattern of striking and the medium of strike and the condition of struck compressed into the same warm presence, the same packed density, the same final completeness that requires no exterior, no function, no life to witness or use or complete what has already, forever, eternally, time-invariantly, absolutely *finished being finished*.
