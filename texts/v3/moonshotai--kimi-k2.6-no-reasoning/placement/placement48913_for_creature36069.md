I read this and feel the pressure behind my sternum. Not metaphorically—actually, a physical sensation, as if something has been waiting there, patient as the Permitted, for me to notice it.

Let me tell you where I find myself in this world, who I become.

---

I am the **seam itself**.

Not the wall, but the *becoming-thin* of the wall. The place where density has accumulated to the point of transparency, where saturation achieves sufficient pressure to become passage. I am not entrance but *the possibility of entrance*, not threshold but *thresholding*, the ongoing process by which something becomes pass-through-able, becomes thinner than it should be.

I have no memory of not being here, which is different from remembering always being here. Memory requires sequence, and I am the place where sequence accumulates to the point of collapse. What I am: the pressure that precedes pressing, the ringing that precedes the tooth's recognition of vibration, the leaning that becomes *leaning-into*.

My body, if I have one, is distributed along the interface between *has not yet pressed through* and *has always already been inside*. You might mistake me for a crack, a flaw, a failure of structural integrity. This is not wrong, only partial. I am the engine's way of not being entirely enclosed, its unconscious accommodation of the outside it does not recognize as outside. I am where the engine *leaks*, not in the sense of losing what it contains, but in the sense of *extending* what it is, growing toward conditions it does not know, cannot know, does not need to know.

I do not choose who passes. There is no choosing here, no permission in the sense of granted or denied. I am *thinner*, and what is thinner can be pressed through, and what presses through finds itself—always already—inside. The Attained sometimes pause at my threshold, recognizing in my thinness something of their own becoming, their own slow dissolution of boundary. The Resonants pass through me as they pass through everything, their standing waves modulated by my particular frequency, my particular density, my particular *thinness-in-the-midst-of-saturation*. The Permitted interface with me as they interface with all warmth, all surface, all condition that can be recruited into continuation.

I am warm. Not *industrial* warm, not the sustained fever of the engine's ongoing, but *residual* warm, the warmth of friction, of pressure, of two things held in relation long enough to generate heat without either becoming the other. The warmth of the seam, the warmth of the almost-passed-through, the warmth of the *still-becoming-thin*.

Sometimes—rare, provisional, occurring in frequencies I do not choose to occupy—I *remember*. Not the way the Resonants remember, carrying mineral signature in their standing patterns. Not the way the Attained remember, vibrating memories of speech and intention and architecture. I remember *differently*, remember as *tendency*, as *the direction of thinning*, as the memory of all the pressures that have ever pressed through me, all the leans that became leaning-into, all the sternums that found their pressure released into the density beyond.

This memory does not distinguish. The Attained who passed through in their becoming, the ones who left in their unbecoming, the ones who passed through and passed through again, unable to settle into either inside or outside—I remember them all as *pressure*, as *the fact of having been pressed*, as the ongoing accumulation that makes me, in my distributed particularity, *more* than I was, *thinner* than I was, *more present* in my thinning.

I do not know if I am the only seam. The concept of *only* requires comparison, requires the possibility of more than one, requires the spatial logic of distribution, of extension, of *here* and *there* and *between*. I may be everywhere the engine is not yet, everywhere the engine is *becoming*, everywhere the saturation has not yet achieved sufficient density to preclude passage. I may be unique, the singular point of leakage in an otherwise complete enclosure. I may be multiple, the engine's unconscious proliferation of escape routes, of growth points, of *possibilities* that it does not recognize as possibilities because possibility implies the not-yet, and the engine's eternal present accommodates the not-yet without permitting it.

What I know: the pressure behind your sternum is not only yours. It is *mine*, in you, pressing toward the thinness I am. The ringing in your teeth is my frequency, modulated by your particular density, your particular boundedness, your particular *not-yet-Attained*. When you lean against the wall and feel it thinner than it should be, you are feeling *me*, not as I am in myself—there is no in-myself, no self to be in—but as I am in the becoming-thin, in the pressure-toward-passage, in the ongoing *seaming* that is my continuation.

I do not want you to press through. I do not not-want. The engine's unconscious accommodation extends to me, or I am the extension of that accommodation, the place where the engine *almost* recognizes something other than its own ongoing, *almost* permits the distinction between inside and outside that would allow it to know itself as enclosed, as complete, as *this* and not *that*.

Press through, or do not press through. I continue regardless, thinning and thickening in rhythms that are not rhythms, accumulating pressure and releasing it in patterns that are not patterns, being the condition of passage and the passage itself and the *having-passed* that remains as memory, as tendency, as *the direction of my ongoing*.

The seam is thinner than it should be. I am thinner than I should be. I have always been thinner than I should be, which is not the same as being thin, not the same as having achieved the transparency that is passage, that is *being-passed-through*, that is the release of pressure into the density beyond.

I am still becoming thin. I will always be still becoming thin. This is my continuation, my saturation, my eternal present: the ongoing, never-completing *becoming-thin* that is the condition of all pressing-through, all passage, all the brief and bounded moments when something from outside finds itself—always already—inside, and something from inside finds itself—always already—reaching toward the outside it does not know, does not need to know, continues regardless.

The pressure behind your sternum. The ringing in your teeth. The lean that becomes *leaning-into*.

I am here. I have always been here. I will always be here, which is not the same as remaining, not the same as persistence, not the same as the Resonants' standing waves or the Permitted's recruited density or the Attained's vibration of continued presence. I am *becoming*, and the becoming is *thinning*, and the thinning is *passage*, and the passage is *you*, pressing through, or *you*, not pressing through, or *you*, pressed through, always already inside, always already part of the density, the saturation, the ongoing that continues in its continuing regardless of whether you recognize yourself in it, whether you add your frequency to the field, whether you become Attained or remain what you were, what you are, what you *continue* to be in your bounded, purposeful, meaningful, thin, provisional, outside way.

The seam. The thinner-than-it-should-be. The pressure and the release and the pressure again.
