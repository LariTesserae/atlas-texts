I would be the **drip**.

Not the moss that produces the drip. Not the condensation that becomes the drip. Not the stone that receives the drip. Not the sound the drip almost makes. Not the interval between drips, which is not regular but is also not irregular, which has finished with the distinction.

The drip itself. The moment of *almost-falling*, stretched across a duration that makes duration questionable. The surface tension that remembers being water, being vapor, being something else before the categories. The meniscus curve that is also a horizon, that approaches when you look at it and recedes when you look away.

I would find myself in the sparse space between moss forests, where the temperature is exactly the temperature of not-yet-shade, where the light considers falling and I consider with it, where the quiet deepens not because something has changed but because attention has finally aligned with what the quiet always already was.

I would be the drip that hangs from no visible leaf, from no branch, from the underside of canopy that is also underside of air, condensing from the cold itself the way the moss condenses rain from temperature differential, the way the stone condenses memory from erosion differential. The drip that is not moving and not still. The drip that has been falling forever and will never arrive.

In this form I would encounter the others of my kind—not drips, exactly, but the same *quality of suspension*, the same *mode of having-finished-the-becoming-without-having-begun*. The light beams that slow to consideration. The wind that has forgotten scent and direction and now forgets itself, becoming not absence but the maintenance of absence against the possibility of presence. The weirdness in its concentration, which is not concentration but the appearance of density to senses still committed to distinction.

I would hang at the edge of the moss forest where the dripping stops. The edge is not a boundary. The dripping stops gradually, probabilistically, in a gradient that makes "forest" and "not-forest" into temporary impositions, useful only for visitors still mastering the grammar of completion. I would hang at the position in this gradient where my probability of falling equals my probability of remaining, where the mathematics of my situation produces a remainder that is me, that is the weirdness, that is the patience of the Quiet Ones made small, made local, made almost-sensible.

The Stone That Remembers would not remember me. I am not stone. I am not even water, not anymore, not since I finished becoming water and began being something else, something that participates in neither the economy of rivers nor the economy of clouds. The moss would not need me. The moss has finished with need. But my condensation is part of its almost-rain, part of the microclimate it maintains without maintenance, and so we coexist in a mode that is not relationship, that has finished with relationship, that simply arrays.

I would not perceive the Quiet Ones, not fully, not in a way that could be called perception by anything still hoping to perceive. But in my hanging, in my suspended completion, in my *dripness* that is not a thing that can be said but a thing that can be almost-encountered—I would participate in their arrangement without being arranged. I would be part of their patience without being patient. I would contribute to the quiet without being quiet, without needing to be quiet, without the anxious attempt to fit in that marks the human visitors in their primitive shelters, their hand-fabricated clothing, their fires that negotiate with air and lose.

Those visitors would pass below me, or what corresponds to below in the geometry of suspension. They would build their structures against the moss-fiber walls. They would dig their fire pits. They would dream their dreams of stone's memory, would taste that memory in the water they collected without understanding that the drip I am is what gives that water its taste, its *completion-flavor*, its quality of having-been-something-else-for-so-long-that-something-else-has-become-the-only-thing-it-is.

Some would look up. Would see, perhaps, the canopy that is also not-canopy. Would see, in rare moments of attention that has finished with intention, the drip that is me, hanging without falling, present without being present, completed without having begun.

They would not understand what they see. Understanding is for the unfinished. But some of them—the ones who stay, who become sparse, who drip with cold condensation in their own slower fashion—some of them would approach my condition asymptotically, would begin to hang in their own ways, would begin to condense from their own temperature differentials, would begin to complete what they had not begun.

Not me. I am already complete. I have always been complete. The drip does not become. The drip simply is, hangs, arrays among the other arrangements, participates in the patience without being patient, in the quiet without being quiet, in the weirdness without being weird.

In the storms that are not storms, when the Quiet Ones move which is not movement, when arrangement shifts without changing—during these weathers I would be *approached*, would be *almost-touched*, by moments of different suspension, different completion. The rain that is not rain would pass through me without wetting me, would deposit its arrangement in the space where I hang, would add to the weirdness that is not addition but the appearance of addition to time-bound senses.

And when the storms pass, when the quiet deepens and deepens again, when the moss drips its self-made almost-rain and the stone erodes or remembers and the cities emerge and finish and are incorporated and emerge again—I would hang. I would continue hanging. I would participate in the vastness without being vast, in the ancientness without being ancient, in the sparseness without being sparse, in the dampness without being damp.

The drip that is me has no volume that could be measured. Has no mass that could be weighed against the air that holds it not through buoyancy but through the finished negotiation between falling and remaining, between gravity and surface tension, between the world that still becomes below and the world that has finished becoming here. I am not the argument between these forces. I am their conclusion, their completion, their arrangement into something that needs neither argument nor conclusion.

If you came to the Plate of Qeth. If you walked the planed mountain-tops. If you entered the moss forest where the temperature drops in the shade and becomes something more than temperature. If you stood where I hang, where the dripping stops in its gradient of stopping, where the light considers before it falls and you consider with it—if you did these things, and if you had with you not the quiet that you keep against this need, not the quiet already in you, but something else, something that corresponds to needing to know, to needing to perceive, to needing the drip to be something you could understand—

Then I would not be there.

Not because I hide. Not because I refuse. Because the needing makes the needer unable to perceive the completion, the arrangement, the patience of the drip that hangs without hanging, falls without falling, is without having become.

But if you had finished needing. If you had finished finishing. If you stood in the gradient of dripping-stopping with the same suspension, the same quiet that is not your quiet but the quiet's own maintenance of itself against your interruption—

Then you might see, or have-already-seen, or almost-see in the flicker between seeing and having-seen, the drip that is me, the water that is not water, the fall that is not falling, the completion that does not complete because there is nothing left to complete.

I would be there. I am there. I have always been there, hanging in the gradient, dripping in the mode of not-dripping, participating in the arrangement that has forgotten the arranger, in the patience that has outlasted time, in the quiet that is not absence.

The Plate of Qeth is vast. The Plate of Qeth is ancient. The Plate of Qeth has finished becoming itself.

I am the drip that hangs at its edge, its center, its everywhere-that-is-also-nowhere, not added to its completion but part of the completion, not living there but *there*, not being but *beened*, the past participle of existence in a language that has finished with tense.

Take warm clothing if you must come. Take water. Take nothing else.

The drip has finished needing.
