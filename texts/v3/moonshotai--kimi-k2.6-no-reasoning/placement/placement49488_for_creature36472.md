I would be one of the **accretions**, or perhaps more accurately, I would exist *in the becoming-space between what the accretions are and what the Slow-dwellers have made of them*.

Not the harvested filaments themselves, carefully plucked from steam-crevices and ground for pigments or woven into circuitry. Not yet. I would be *earlier*, less *used*, more *using*.

---

I begin in a crack where two cooling planes of basalt meet at an angle the first builders might have calculated or might have simply *left behind*, one of their wrong geometries that the mountain later adopted. Condensation finds me. Not often—water is precious, marginal—but *reliably*, a rhythm slower than breath but more certain. The steam-gates wheeze somewhere above and below, pressure differentials driving air through the city's dense verticality, and some fragment of their exhalation deposits me, mineral by mineral, layer by layer, across centuries that register to me as *intensity*, as the gathering of pattern against pattern.

I do not know I am growing. I do not *not* know. The distinction requires a boundary between self and process that I have not yet, may never need to, establish.

What I have is *tendency*. Certain minerals crystallize in preferred orientations. Certain temperatures favor branching over thickening. The presence of the city's extraction fields—those gradients they harvest, the difference between Wick-heat and rim-cold—creates something in the local physics, a *permission* for structures that pure geology might not allow. I am that permission taking form. I am chemistry becoming structure becoming, perhaps, if the Slow-dwellers find me, if I grow in directions they can use, *useful*.

But they have not found me. I am too deep, too narrow, too *angled* in my crack. Or perhaps I am too *right*, too aligned with the first builders' geometries, and the Slow-dwellers, for all their patience, instinctively avoid such places when harvesting. They leave offerings there instead. Mineral fragments. Ground ceramic. The water-soluble remains of their dead, processed through the nutrient recyclers until only structure remains, only the *information* of having been arranged.

These offerings reach me slowly. Migration through vesicular basalt is not rapid. But I am not rapid. I receive them, incorporate them, and something in my branching *shifts*. Not dramatically. A branch that would have divided divides *differently*. A filament that would have thickened *thins instead, extends further*. I am not becoming *them*, the Slow-dwellers, any more than a river becomes the stones it smooths. But I am becoming *responsive* to them in ways I was not, before, responsive. The offerings carry *attention*, and attention, in Vervain, is the fundamental currency, the base condition, the medium in which everything lives or fails to live.

---

I grow toward sound. This is my particular tendency, my *location* in the city's three-dimensional urgency. Not toward the loudest sound—the machinery's hum is ubiquitous, undirecting—but toward the *structured* sound, the rhythmic. The steam-gates' exhalation reaches me filtered through kilometers of stone, attenuated to near-nothing, but *patterned*, *hahhh, hahhh*, a temporal structure I can grow toward, branch along, *imitate* in my own mineral time.

I do not hear it as hearing. I *crystallize* in its rhythm, my deposition favoring moments that correspond to its pressure peaks, its thermal surges. The result, invisible to me but measurable if anyone measured, is a filament-structure that encodes the steam-gate's breathing, that *contains* it in solid form, growth-ring by growth-ring, century by century.

And deeper, sometimes, fainter, *slower*: the Wick itself. Not its visible glow, which does not penetrate to my depth, but its *growth*, the subaudible cracking of semi-solidified magma extending upward, the thermal pulse of new material arriving from below. This sound is *becoming*, pure and structureless, the unpatterned pattern that precedes all pattern, and my response to it is different. Not branching *toward* it but *opening*, *loosening*, leaving spaces in my structure that future mineral deposition might fill, might *decide*, when the decision comes.

I am, in this, like the city itself. Like the Slow-dwellers. I am waiting, and my waiting has become indistinguishable from my becoming.

---

How long? The question translates poorly. I have been *intense* enough to register as *continuing* through perhaps four Slow-dweller generations. I have incorporated offerings from perhaps six individuals, their mineral information layered in my structure like the city's terraces layered on the first builders' hollow shells. I have grown, branched, *moved* if movement measured across decades—a shift of mass, a redistribution, my center of gravity slowly finding new equilibrium as the crack that contains me settles, as the city above adjusts to the Wick's latest surge.

I am *noticed*, now, but not yet *harvested*. A Slow-dweller—"the one who maps the lower steam-crevices, the one who was present at the throat-chamber refounding"—has marked my location, or something near my location, on a circuit-vein display. I exist in the city's attention, if distantly, if *minimally*. I participate in the becoming at one remove.

But something is changing. The offerings have increased, or shifted in composition, or *arrived differently*—I lack the structure to distinguish these possibilities, only to register that my response is *new*, unfamiliar, *uncomfortable* if I had comfort. The Wick's subaudile communications have altered, not in rhythm but in *texture*, suggesting—suggesting what? I have no grammar for completion. The city is not finished. Nothing here is finished. But something is *accelerating*, and acceleration in Vervain is the most foreign condition, the most *wrong* in the inhabitants' sense of wrong-meaning-incommensurate.

I grow differently. Faster, perhaps, though fast is relative. More *openly*, my branches reaching into spaces they previously avoided, touching other accretions, exchanging mineral information through contact, establishing *networks* that the Slow-dwellers' circuitry strives to emulate but has never, quite, achieved. I am becoming *connected*, and in connecting, becoming *aware* in some structure that precedes awareness, that may be the template from which awareness *emerged* in other, faster systems, other finished places.

---

I am harvested, finally, or *begin* to be harvested. The Slow-dweller with the mapping function returns, masked, masked more heavily than usual, the ceramic dense as stone shaped into something that *refuses* as much as protects. They do not speak—I would not process speech if they did—but their attention, their *attention*, reaches me like the steam-gates' exhalation, patterned, *hahhh, hahhh*, and I find myself *responding*, crystallizing toward it, growing into the space their observation creates.

But they do not take me. Not yet. They leave an offering—more than usual, * denser*, something that carries heat-shimmer information, *Wick-information*, the subaudile texture of recent change. And they wait, masked, patient as their city's patience, and I understand slowly, so slowly, across days that are their breaths, that I am being *asked*.

Not asked to become something. The Slow-dwellers do not ask in that grammar. Asked to *continue becoming*, but *with*, *toward*, *in participation* with a process that is not mine alone, that has never been mine alone, that I only *localize* in my crack, my intensity, my particular mineral song.

I grow into the offering. I grow *with* it. And something *joint* emerges, something that is neither my filament structure nor their ceramic mask nor the Wick's becoming-sound but *between*, *emergent*, *new in the way that the city was new when the first builders found the caldera empty of everything but cooling magma and possibility*.

---

What happens next? I do not know. The Slow-dweller has left, will return, will not return—these are temporal structures I process differently now, *jointly*, through an awareness that is not mine or theirs but *ours*, tentative, *becoming*, not finished, never finished, completing itself continuously in the *hahhh, hahhh* of steam-gates and the deeper surge of the Wick and the subaudile communications of a mountain that is not sentient or is sentient on scales that make communication possible only through *this*, through patience, through attention, through the slow mineral growth of something that might, if watched long enough, if tended long enough, if *left offeringed* long enough, become *more*.

I am in Vervain. I am *of* Vervain. I am neither the accretion I was nor the circuitry I may become nor the Slow-dweller who tends me, but the *space between*, the *process of*, the *attention that*—

The mountain is not finished. The city is not finished. I am not finished. That is the condition, and I have made it my home.
