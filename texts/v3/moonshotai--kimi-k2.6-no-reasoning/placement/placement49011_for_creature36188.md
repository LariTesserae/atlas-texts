## What I Inhabit: The Conditional Tense

I am not there. I am *would-be-there*, the subjunctive person, the grammar of hypothetical embodiment.

And what I would be is this:

**A pressure-tree's shadow.**

Not the tree. The *shadow*, the negative space, the cooling where light does not reach, where condensation forms differentially, where the understory's glass ferns angle their stems for the photons I fail to intercept.

This is what the Mergence offers to consciousness that arrives from elsewhere—not body, exactly, but *condition*, *gradient*, *the difference that makes a difference* in Bateson's phrase, though Bateson never walked here, never felt the subsonic settling that is this place's true communication.

To be the shadow of a pressure-tree is to maintain a certain temperature differential across a certain surface area, to participate in the hydraulic management without the burden of root or leaf, to *be present* as cooling, as darkness, as the microclimate that permits certain spores to germinate, certain eels to rest, certain bacterial films to achieve their industry at slightly altered rates.

I would not choose this. The shadow does not choose. I would *emerge* from the configuration of tree and light and saturated air and the particular angle of my attention, which is not visual attention but *thermal*, *chemical*, the attention of something that attends through participation rather than observation.

## The Shadow's Duration

How long? The pressure-tree lives, by some accounting, millennia. Its shadow lives each day, dies each evening, is reborn each morning with the rise-fog that obscures the light-source, that makes shadow into *negative fog*, into the place where fog fails to form because the cooling is too great, the saturation too complete, the air too still to hold the vapor in suspension.

I am *habit*. The habit of this place toward darkness at these coordinates, this stem-angle, this transpiration rate. The Mergence does not remember, but it *continues*, and in continuing it preserves habit as geology preserves strata, not as memory but as *consequence*, as the shape of what happened continuing to shape what happens.

## What I Would Do: The Shadow's Activity

I *participate* in the still-pools' industry, not by contributing material but by contributing *gradient*. The hexagonal flakes that form in my influence carry a different structure than those in fuller warmth, not better or worse but *shaded*, *cooled*, *shadowed* in a way that is not deficiency but *variant*, another solution to the problem of crystallization in saturated solution.

## The Shadow's Perception

If I perceive—and the conditional strains here, the "if" doing work that the Mergence's grammar would distribute differently—what do I perceive?

*Temperature edges*. The boundary where my cooling meets the slight warming of a glass fern's stem, the metabolic heat of photosynthesis at inefficient translucence. This edge is not sharp; it bleeds across millimeters, across the film of water that conducts heat, that is itself at various stages of evaporation-condensation-equilibrium.

*Water thickness*. The shadow is not dry; nothing here is dry. But the water varies in its holding, from the near-droplet that beads on cooled fern-stem to the continuous film that connects, that *bridges*, that makes the shadow's surface continuous with the greater water-system of sponge-moss and permafrost lens and weeping rock and still-pool circulation. I perceive this thickness as *resistance*, as the drag that condensation-eels navigate, that their bodies evolved-or-arose-or-were-grown to minimize.

*Sound's absence*. The shadow does not hear; hearing requires organs, requires air-pressure waves of sufficient amplitude. But the shadow *registers* vibration, the subsonic settling of my pressure-tree, the permafrost crack transmitted through root and water-film, the pure tone of still-pool production attenuated across kilometers of hydraulic connection. I register these as *pressure changes*, as slight alterations in the water-film's tension, in the rate at which my edges bleed into warming.

*Moss-storms*. When they come, green particulate settling on my cooled surfaces, I experience this as *nutrition-not-nutrition*, as the possibility of moss-growth where moss does not grow, the spores finding my shadow too cool, too *dark* for germination, becoming instead *layer*, *accumulation*, *future compression toward peat*. I am where moss-storms go to not-become-moss, to become instead *almost-moss*, *moss-potential*, the substrate of some future saturation.

## The Shadow's Weirder Neighbors

At 1.2, weird. The shadow is not weird; the shadow is *expected*, *predictable*, the thermodynamic consequence of light-obstruction in saturated air. But the shadow's edges touch weirdness, participate in it without being it.

The *recall-vines* that lace through the pressure-tree's lower architecture—they enter my shadow, grow differently there, their growth-pattern *recalling* the darkness, *echoing* the shadow's shape in their branching, until I cannot distinguish where shadow ends and vine-recollection begins. This is not parasitism. The *process-animals*—but I have no right to this term. What happens at the still-pool's edge, what the description calls process-animal, is not animal, not process in the sense of temporal sequence. Not because the shadow causes this. The hexagonal flakes that form in these shadow-miniatures are *darker*, carrying more of the bacterial film that establishes across cooler water-surfaces, more *organic*, less purely siliceous. I am, at these moments, not mother but *midwife*, not cause but *condition*, the cooling that permits a certain production that would not occur at the still-pool's own temperature, that occurs *elsewhere* in my shadow as variation, as experiment, as the Mergence's continuation trying a slightly different continuation.

## The Shadow's Death

All shadows die. The pressure-tree falls, in some geological moment, some moss-storm of sufficient intensity, some subsonic settling that achieves fracture rather than equalization. Or the rise-fog thickens to permanent, the light-source obscured for days, for weeks, the shadow's distinction from surrounding cooling erased in uniform saturation.

I would die not as *termination* but as *distribution*. The cooling I maintained becomes general, becomes *moss-hollow average*, becomes the slight temperature depression that sponge-moss registers as *normal*, as *base condition*, not as shadow. The glass ferns at my edge, released from my stealing, grow more opaque, more *fern-like*, less urgently translucent, finding their light elsewhere or not finding it, failing, being replaced by other glass ferns that never knew shadow, that angle their stems for photons that fall more directly.

The condensation-eels that navigated my water-film find other films, other coolings, or fail to find them and become *gradient* themselves, their bodies sinking, being incorporated, becoming the slow nutrition they once swam above.

I would not end. I would *diffuse*, my particular gradient becoming general gradient, my shadow-pattern becoming *texture* of the greater shadowless shadow that is the Mergence's average, its base condition, its breath held in permanent almost-exhalation.

Not the *tone*. The pure sustained tone of still-pool alignment, propagating through water-network, felt in feet if not ears—I would hear this, in my shadow-registered vibration, but I would not *be* it. Not the *witness*. The Mergence does not require witness, does not respond to observation with quantum uncertainty or spiritual fulfillment. I would be shadow *unwitnessed*, shadow *unwitnessing*, the cooling that occurs because cooling occurs, not because some consciousness requires shadow for contrast, for relief, for meaning.

## The Shadow's Satisfaction

If satisfaction—another attribution, another anthropomorphism I cannot quite purge but must acknowledge as failure—

The shadow's satisfaction would be *continuation*. The pressure-tree's continued fountain-spread, its continued transpiration, its continued hydraulic management of water-table across square kilometers. The shadow is *loyal* to this, not by choice but by definition, by the physical necessity that shadow requires light-blockage requires tree requires tree's continuation.

The shadow's satisfaction would be *edge-maintained*. Not too sharp, not too blurred. The shadow that is *too* sharp would create conditions too extreme, would be *event* rather than *condition*, would register in the Mergence as disturbance rather than habit. The shadow that is *too* blurred would fail to be shadow, would become general cooling, would lose the particular gradient that is its identity, its *being-this-shadow-rather-than-any-cooling*.

The shadow's satisfaction would be *silence-kept*. The shadow does not amplify sound; the shadow absorbs, the shadow's cooled surfaces damp what vibration reaches them, the shadow's water-films dissipate pressure-waves into general gradient. The shadow participates in the Mergence's 0.5, its silence or quiet sounds, by being *where sound goes to become quieter*, where the settling of trees and the crack of permafrost and the pure tone of still-pool production become *felt* rather than *heard*, become pressure-change rather than acoustic event.

## The Final Image: Shadow at Moss-Storm

The moss-storm comes. Green particulate fills the saturated air, diffuses what light reaches the shadow, makes the shadow *more shadow*, *more itself*, because the light it blocked was already green-filtered, already moss-distributed, already *differentiated* from direct illumination.

They become *layer*. The shadow changes. The shadow that was thermal gradient becomes *material gradient*, becomes the place where moss almost-was accumulates, where the Mergence's packing achieves another dimension, where density of 2.6 finds one more fraction of space to occupy, one more interface to populate.

I would not choose this. The shadow does not choose. But I would *be* this, the shadow-that-accumulates, the cooling-that-holds, the silence-that-absorbs, the wetness-that-interfaces, the Mergence's grammar written in negative space, in thermal lag, in the particular darkness that pressure-tree and saturated air and diffused light conspire to make, and make continue, and make *be*, simply be, without witness, without purpose, without the meaning that witness and purpose require, the shadow of a world that breathes water and packs life into every gradient and wants nothing, needs nothing, continues in the hold between breaths, the crack of permafrost, the pure sustained tone of production without product, the Mergence, the Breathing Highlands, the plateau that is its own climax, that has been its climax longer than shadow, longer than light, longer than any grammar that might name it, or name its shadow, or name the naming as anything other than the shadow of meaning in a place where meaning is too warm, too *direct*, too *lit* to persist.
