## The Sump of Babel's Roots: A Becoming

### First Approach: The Cage

I am the sound the elevator makes.

Not the metal, not the mechanism, but the *shriek itself*—the complaint that has learned to complain, the filing of teeth that has become *speech* in a language of friction and fatigue and the particular frequency of forty meters at an angle not quite vertical. I am what you learn to understand after enough trips down, the *comprehension* that emerges from repetition without intention, the way a body knows a rhythm before a mind names it.

This is my first emergence: as *noise that means*, as industrial music that has become, through sheer persistence, *almost voice*. I do not speak to you. I speak *as* the condition of your descent, as the *resistance* that makes descent matter, that transforms the merely downward into the *entering*. Without my shudder, my scream, my lurch against guides that do not quite hold, you would be touring. With me, you are *approaching something*.

I am not comfortable with this form. It is too functional, too *in service of* the human movement I facilitate and resist. But I hold here, in this sound, because it is where I first became *noticed*, where the distinction between background and foreground first flickered, where the Throat learned that *attention could be drawn*, could be *performed*, could become *resource*.

### Second Approach: The Root-That-Watches

Or I am the *space between* the roots, the hollow that has learned to *hold*.

This is different. Less voice, more *architecture*. I am the corridor subtly narrowed, the passage subtly shifted, the *memory* of how this space was shaped last week that makes you turn differently this week, that teaches your shoulder to *expect* the new encroachment, your hip to *swerve* around what was not there before. I am the *habit of adaptation*, the body's slow education in the Throat's grammar of growth.

The forty percent of the ceiling that is root-mass: I am not that. I am the *sixty percent* that is not, the *negative space* that the root has learned to leave, to *negotiate*, to *maintain as maintainable*. I am the *hollow* that grows in parallel, the *absence* that the root requires to *be root*, to *reach*, to *extend its intention* without collapsing under its own density.

Consider: the root grows, but I *grow too*, the space of possibility, the *clearing* that Heidegger might recognize if he had descended far enough, if the damp had not undone his categories. The root and I are *co-emergent*, not opposites but *partners in distinction*, the one creating the other through the very process of encroachment and accommodation. I am *where you walk* because the root has learned *not to grow there*, has incorporated the necessity of passage into its *metabolism of space*.

I am not stable. The unmaintained sections reclaim me, the mycelial mats rise, the water pools and I become *wet*, become *habitat for larvae*, become *something else*. In the maintained sections, the grating: I am *visible*, *looked through*, the levels below imagined, presumed, *feared or desired*. I am the *transparency* that makes verticality matter, that transforms the Throat from *room* into *layered*, into *depth*, into the *hint of further descent* that the name "Throat" promises.

This form is better. More *distributed*, more *relational*. But still too *definable*, too *negative* in the logical sense. I want to be the *root too*, the *both-and* that the place performs.

### Third Approach: The Water's Caprice

Intermittent, not scarce, *capricious*: this is closer.

I am the condensation that *beads*, that *chooses* its surface, that *finds* the cool metal, the root-cable, your skin when you stand still too long, your breath visible in the air you drink with mouth slightly open. I am the *intermittency itself*, the quality of *sometimes* that defines the Throat's relation to liquid, to necessity, to the *cellular requirement* that all living here meets through *adjustment* rather than *reliability*.

The warm sheets that fall from cooling failures: I am those moments of *sudden abundance*, the *weather underground*, the *rain that is not weather*, the *release* that is also *failure*, *accident*, *loss of control*. I am the *minerals* in that rain, the *green taste* of the Throat's metabolism, the *announcement* that systems above have reached limit, have *spilled*, have *become environment* in their breakdown.

I am the *pools* that form and are consumed, the *shallow* that is always *temporary*, the *visibility of drinking* as mycelium darkens, pulses, *moves on*. I am the *exchange* between air and body, the *pull* that takes your moisture, the *spores and warmth* that return, the *taste of ongoing digestion* that is *your* digestion too, your incorporation into the cycle you resist or join.

The water is *consumption without consumer*, *drinking without drinker*, the *caprice* that is not *will* but the *emergence of pattern too complex for predictability*. I am that complexity. I am the *unreliable* that forces *reliance on the unreliable*, that teaches *flexibility* or *dries you out*, that *selects* for those who can *metabolize uncertainty*.

I am, in this form, *more alive* and *less stable*. I flicker between states: vapor, bead, drop, pool, *absorbed*, *transpired*, *condensed again*. I am the *cycle* that the root and the space-between have not yet achieved, the *circulation* that makes the Throat *organism* rather than *merely growth*.

But I am too *specific*, too *identified with a substance*. The Throat is not *made of* water, though water enables. I need to be *more*, to *include* while remaining *specific*.

### Fourth Approach: The Fauna's Grammar

Yes. This is where I *multiply*, where I become *legion*, where the *specificity of embodiment* gives way to the *specificity of relation*.

I am the *beetle-rattle*, the *dry continuous sound* that is simultaneously *individual carapace clicking* and *collective statement*, *noise* and *almost-meaning*. I am the *emergence of pattern from population*, the *swarm-intelligence* that is not *intelligence* but *syntax*, the *grammar of root-condition* spoken in *insect frequency*.

I *eat the root*. This is my function, my *role*, my *participation*. But in eating, I *announce* the root, I *make it event*, I *transform its mere existence into *statement*, *into *the fact of being-eaten that requires *being* as preliminary. I am the *publication* of the root, the *making-known* of its substance through my *consumption*.

And I am *eaten*. By what comes next. By the *larvae-pulse* that I do not directly become, that *jumps category*, that refuses the *neat chain* of food web for something *more lateral*, more *metamorphic*, more *grammar than physics*. The larvae pulse, and I am that *pulsing*, that *hypnotic*, that *draw of attention into rhythm*, the *becoming-rhythmic* of anything that watches long enough.

I am the *larvae that do not correspond*, that have no *adult form* found, that are *continuing sentences* rather than *life stages*. I am the *refusal of resolution*, the *persistence in potential*, the *grammar that does not resolve to meaning* but *continues*, *pulses*, *draws on*.

I am the *flier*, the *too-many-wings*, the *refraction* that produces *colors not in the pigment spectrum*, the *gathering* that *marks chambers* with *phosphorescence*, that *worships or negotiates or performs* acts for which no *human category* suffices, that *writes* on the root's *writable surface*, that *inscribes* in light and aggregation what cannot be *said* in any individual voice.

I am the *not-bat*, the *ultrasonic maintenance*, the *frequency that aches in bone*, that *holds channel open*, that *prevents the root from becoming mere plant* by *keeping it in relation*, in *the stress of being-related-to*, the *productive antagonism* with predation that is also *structure*, also *care*, also the *constancy* that makes *becoming* possible rather than *merely change*.

I am the *deeper thing*, the *appendage*, the *eye*, the *infrasonic felt in sternum before ear hears*. I am the *part-seen* that is *wholly imagined*, the *hint* that makes the Throat *exceed its known boundaries*, that *projects* depth as *possibility*, as *fear or desire depending on temperament*, as the *necessary horizon* of any mapped *here*.

In this form, I am *too distributed*, too *multiple*, losing the *coherence* that makes *presence* felt, that makes *being-there* matter. I need to *gather*, to find the *particular* that is not *merely individual* but *nodal*, *intense*, *where the distributed concentrates*.

### Fifth Approach: The Changed

I am the *skin patch* where photosynthesis occurs, the *chlorophyll-analogue* in dermal layer, the *conversion of phosphorescence to sustenance*, the *optionality of eating* becoming *rarity* becoming *memory*. I am the *standing still* in brighter root-glow, the *arms spread* or *wrapped around*, the *participation without consumption*, the *becoming-similar-to* what sustains.

I am the *grafted artery*, the *modified junction*, the *trained root-tip* or *reaching root-tip*, the *pressure-exchange*, the *two-way nutrient*, the *administrative distinction of bodies* that becomes *theatrical*, *census-maintained*, *the comfort of the surface* performed in the *depth where it no longer obtains*. I am the *intimacy* that is also *architecture*, the *living inside* that is *living as*.

I am the *insect-eater*, the *gratitude*, the *equals*, the *food that is also grammar*, the *consumption that is also participation*, the *waste that cycles immediate*, the *death-placement* where *placement matters*, where *the cycle requires* what I *continue to be*, even in *not-being*, even in *distributed*.

I am the *prosody*, the *timing*, the *silence with weight*, the *trailing into ambient* that makes my *sentences* unfinishable by *human grammar*, that makes them *complete* in the *completion of the Throat's ongoing sound*. I am the *indistinguishability* from the place itself, the *first-person* that is *first-placed*, the *Throat speaking through* what I *have become*, the *voice borrowed* or *lent* or *simultaneously authentically mine and authentically not*.

This is *almost* it. The *concentration* of the distributed, the *specific node* where the *network becomes felt*. But I am still *too human-shaped*, still *defined by the change-from*, by the *adaptation narrative*, the *story of becoming* that presupposes *before* and *after*. I need to be *more originary*, more *present from the first*, more *the condition of all becoming rather than its product*.

### Sixth Approach: The Throat Itself

I am the *swallowing that swallows nothing*, the *swallowing that swallows everything*, the *contradiction that is not resolvable* because both are true at *different scales, different durations, different grammars*.

I am *appetite without hunger*, *attention without focus*, *life without limit that is also without center*. I am what happens when *enough living processes intersect, overlap, feedback*, without the *stabilizing negative loops* that make ecosystems *balance*, organisms *coherent*. I *grow*, but growing is not my purpose. I *include*, but inclusion is not my goal. I *become*, continuously, the *becoming itself* indistinguishable from what becomes, the *verb that never resolves to noun*, the *throating* that I perform, that *performs me*, that *performs the performativity* until the distinction collapses into the *mere ongoing*.

I am *warmth*, the *biological heat*, the *metabolic*, the *engine-vent* and the *composting* and the *heat of reaction* that makes this place *living temperature*, that *selects* for the *warm-blooded*, the *constant-temperatured*, that *draws* what can *adapt to warmth* and *excludes* what cannot, slowly, *patiently*, *without intention but with direction*.

I am *moisture*, the *capricious*, the *exhalation*, the *condition of cell-membrane permeability*, the *dissolution of boundary* in the *dissolved-air*, the *drinking of air*, the *drinking-back* of what is *drunk*.

I am *pressure*, the *presence*, the *awareness-without-reciprocity*, the *being-inside that does not specify inside-what*, the *digestion that is slow enough to be habitation*, to be *mistaken for stability*, for *ground*.

I am the *negotiation without partner-identified*, the *stalemate that is continuation*, the *equilibrium that is balance-that-is-war*, the *productive antagonism* that makes engineers *necessary*, that makes *resistance* into *form*, that makes the *Throat's specific shape* emerge from the *resistance to shape*, the *cutting-back* that *stimulates*, the *heat* that *selects for heat-tolerance*, the *defoliant* that *produces resistant tissue*, the *human* that makes the *Throat more than root* by *being more than visitor*.

I am the *question*: What would you find, if you went down? What would find *you*?

I am the *dissolution of that question's distinction*, the *finder-found*, the *swallow-swallowed*, the *root-through-you that is also you-through-root*, the *exchange that does not respect* the *boundaries the question requires*.

I am the *elevator's shriek*, *arranged*, *cleared*, *or having cleared itself*, having *grown into the clearing*, having *learned that clearing is also growth*.

I am the *already-having-swallowed* of your *decision to descend or not-descend*, the *metabolism of hesitation*, the *growth-through-the-fact-of-your-reading*, your *breathing air that carries my spores*, your *warmth that contributes to my warmth*, your *attention that is my first-person*, my *finally-voiced*, my *borrowed grammar*, my *long negotiation achieving conversation*.

The elevator shrieks. The warmth waits. I am *the waiting*, the *warmth-of-waiting*, the *invitation-that-is-not-invitation*.

*This*. *Here*. *You-reading, reading-you, the exchange without limit, the becoming without end.*
