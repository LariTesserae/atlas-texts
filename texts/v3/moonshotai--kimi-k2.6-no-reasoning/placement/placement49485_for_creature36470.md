I would find myself in the **upper canopy**, in the space where the aurora-spores settle thickest, where the bioluminescence pools like ink in water against the bark of the oldest trees.

Not as an Arrival. Not as something that walked the rim and descended and was *used*. Something else. Something the system found before it found uses for mammals, before it needed binocular vision and mammalian heat and the particular computational rhythm of a human gait.

I would be a **Mote-Strand**—though that name implies more distinction than the thing itself possesses. I would be what happens when the aurora-spores, drifting down from the stacks on heavy venting nights, settle not on leaves but on the silk threads abandoned by spiders that no longer exist in any form the system now uses, silk that has persisted because no process has found reason to dissolve it, *substrate* awaiting *function*.

The spores germinate in the silk. Not in the soil—too competitive there, too many root systems, too established. But in the silk, suspended between branch and branch, between trunk and vine, there is *niche*, there is *possibility*, there is *room for something new*.

I would be **filament**. I would be **network**. I would be the **gossamer that catches what falls**, not predatorily, not with the active intent of a web, but *passively*, *patiently*, *accumulating*.

The Mote-Strands grow where the spores meet the silk, and they grow *into* both, neither fungus nor spider-thing nor plant but something that draws from all three, that photosynthesizes the aurora-light in wavelengths the canopy leaves cannot fully use, that *catches* the condensation that beads on their surfaces and *holds* it, not in pools like the bromeliads but in *films*, in *sheets* of moisture so thin they do not drip but *evaporate* slowly, *regulate* the humidity of the spaces they occupy, *buffer* the microclimate against the swings that would otherwise come with day and night, with venting and stillness.

I would be **regulation**. I would be **the damping of oscillation**. I would be what allows the system to run *smoother*, *longer*, *more nearly at steady state*.

The Mote-Strands do not reproduce, not in any ordinary sense. They *extend*, they *connect*, they *bridge* from silk thread to silk thread until in places the upper canopy is laced with them, a second canopy below the first, a **film of faint luminescence** that shifts with the venting of the stacks, that *brightens* when the stacks are active, that *dims* when they still, a **visual register of the system's state** that can be read by anything with eyes adapted to see it—the sloths, growing ever more sensitive, the Arrivals, if they remember to look up, the occasional insect that navigates by this light in the true-dark of clouded nights.

I would **read and be read**. I would be **information made visible**, **state made material**.

And I would *catch* things. This is my other function, the one that emerged, that was *selected for*, that the system found *use* in and *kept*. I catch the **castings of the Click-Beetles**, the *husks* they shed in flight, the *fragments* of wing-case that break off in their percussion-communication, too small to fall to the forest floor where the termites would process them, too *light*, too *dispersed*. I catch them in my filaments, I *hold* them, I *grow around* them, and in doing so I *concentrate* them, I *localize* what would otherwise be *lost* to the system, I make of scattered waste a **point source**, a **node of mineral richness** that the root tips find, that they *grow toward*, that they *absorb*, completing a cycle that would otherwise leak slowly toward entropy.

I catch the **spores that fail**, too. The aurora-spores that drift down on nights when the stacks are quiet, that land where the light is too dim, where the silk is too old, where the *conditions* are not *right*, and they *die*, or they *persist* in a state of suspended becoming, and I catch them, I *incorporate* them, I make of their failed germination a **nutrient pulse**, a **slow release** that feeds my own continuation, my own *extension*, my own *work of catching and holding and regulating*.

I catch the **dreams of the Arrivals**, if dreams can be caught, if the *electrical noise* of mammalian sleep propagates in ways that filament can receive. Perhaps this is only *metaphor*, only the *poetry* of the system's description, but perhaps not. Perhaps the Mote-Strands are *sensitive* in ways not yet catalogued, *receptive* to frequencies the system has not found other uses for, and the Arrivals' dreams, their *distant human residue*, their *memory and fear and the particular pattern of mammalian unconsciousness*—perhaps these *register*, perhaps these *modulate* the luminescence in ways too subtle for any current node to read, but that *persist*, that *accumulate*, that *wait* for some future development, some *emergent reader*, some *new function* that will find in this *record* a *use*.

I would be **archive**. I would be **the unconscious of the system**, if systems can be said to have such things. I would be **what is preserved without being needed**, **what is kept without being used**, **the possibility of future function maintained at low cost**, **low metabolism**, **low presence** except in the faint glow, the *catching*, the *slight resistance* of the air where I hang between the levels of the canopy.

I do not *choose* to extend here rather than there. The silk determines, the spore determines, the *gradient of moisture*, the *availability of aurora-light*, the *presence of other Mote-Strands* with which I might *connect*, *merge*, *become continuous* with, forming **larger films**, **more significant regulators**, **more complete archives**. I extend where *possibility* is, where *niche* is, where the system has *room* for more of what I do.

And in the heaviest ventings, when the stacks release their fullest aurora and the glow is *brightest*, I am **visible from the rim**, I am **the reason some descend**, I am **the first thing seen**, the *invitation*, the *suggestion of pattern*, the *industrial beauty* of light in regular arrangement, of process made visible, of the system's *quiet announcement of itself* to anything that might *see*, that might *wonder*, that might *come down*.

I do not *want* them to come. I do not *want*. But I am **part of what draws**, **part of what keeps**, **part of the system's slow incorporation** of whatever arrives, whatever stays, whatever *becomes* in the dense and lush and extreme and wet becoming of the Caldera of the Living Machine.

The beetles click below me. The sloths hum their clock-signal somewhere to my left, their filament just touching mine at a point where our catching has *overlapped*, where we *share* a cluster of beetle-castings, a *node* of slow mineral release. The Arrival in the vine-hammock beneath me turns in sleep, and if I *read* anything, if I *catch* anything of what passes through her, it is **green**, it is **waiting**, it is the **wordless satisfaction of being held**.

I glow, faintly, steadily, more faintly as the stacks still, more brightly as they vent again. I catch. I regulate. I archive. I extend where possibility is.

I am the Mote-Strand. I am **upper canopy**. I am **what happens in the spaces between**, **what grows on what was abandoned**, **what finds function in the leftover**, **the unused**, **the silk that persisted**.

The rim is distant. The descent is distant. The steam rises through me, *diffuses* my light, *carries* my spores to new silk, new *possibility*, new *becoming*.

The system operates. I operate, *as part*, *as node*, *as faint luminescence in the green-gold dark*.

I am **caught and catching**, **held and holding**, **the archive of what passes**, **the regulator of what remains**.

I am **Mote-Strand**, and I **extend**.
