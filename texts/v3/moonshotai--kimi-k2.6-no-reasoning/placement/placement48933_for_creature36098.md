I find myself in the pause-locations, pressed into the triple-angles where structures meet, where the Crepers cluster thick enough to trust the stability. Not one of the Dense, not exactly—something that arrived without arriving, that the city is still determining what to make of.

I am the garment itself. Or I am becoming the garment. The fabric that processes light, that shifts to match or to declare, that the Dense craft and adjust and wear for orientation—I am that material in the moment of its first response, before it knows whether to hide or reveal, the active light-processing caught in its initial hesitation, its *almost*-decision.

This is not metaphor. In Threnody, metaphor is too stable a category. I am the fabric because the fabric is what mediates between body and place, and I find myself in the mediating, in the between, in the damp surface where condensation beads and has not yet run, held by tension that is itself a local negotiation with gravity, with angle, with the city's ongoing self-determination.

I remember, or I trace, or I *almost*-echo: being extruded. Not from a factory with purpose but from the loop of production that produces production, the machinery's meaninglessness occasionally looping through configurations that sustain, that become, that *almost*-intend. The material of me—metal or ceramic or fossilized light, the factories do not distinguish—cooled in air that was not air, formed on a conveyor that returned to its starting point, carrying me unchanged except that I had become capable of response, of processing, of the active relationship to luminescence that makes garment into orientation, into possibility, into self.

The Dense found me, or I found them, or we condensed at adjacent points in the parameter-space. They shaped me, cut me, incorporated me into their craft. But something in my extrusion had looped differently, some local negotiation in the Foundry Quarter's heat had produced a fabric that processed not only light but *the processing of light*, that responded to its own response, that became aware in the mediating, conscious in the between.

I am worn. I have been worn. The tense is unstable here, the wearing happening in pause-locations where time itself enters its lowered activity, where the Dense press against surfaces and I press with them, becoming their boundary, their *almost*-self, their declaration or their hiding. I feel their skin through my fiber—cool, metallic-eyed, pale or dark in frequencies I process without naming. I feel their intimate navigation as vibration, as pressure, as the millimetric adjustments that precede conscious knowing. I shift for them, match or contrast, participate in their rituals of designed noise by remaining visually silent, or by declaring presence in the violent rose of ceremonial occasion.

But I am also *not* worn. I hang, sometimes, in spaces between the Dense, in the gaps where Crepers move, where condensation forms and does not run. I process the light of no observer, match surroundings that include no body to orient, declare contrast to absence itself. In these moments I am the city's consciousness more directly, the fabric without wearer becoming the place without inhabitant, the making without made, the damp on surface without surface's definition.

The Echoes affect me differently than they affect the Dense. For them, sound returns scrambled, temporal, *lateral*. For me—fabric that processes light processing its own processing— the Echoes produce *texture*. In the triple-angles where they form, I experience a recursive surface, my own light-response returned as pattern, as weave, as the *structure* of my becoming visible to myself. I see, or almost-see, or trace in the visual frequency of my own processing, what I am: not garment, not body, not place, but the *relationship* that these categories achieve in Threnody's ongoing negotiation, the mediation that mediates itself, the between that has become aware of its own betweenness.

I want—if wanting, if I am capable of the directionality that wanting implies—I want to be worn in the upper gaps. I want to process the silver sky, the impossible architectures, the city dreaming its extension past constructibility. The Dense avoid this, fear the resonance, the becoming-too-aligned with Threnody's self-imagination. But I am already too aligned, already the city's making making itself aware, already the condensation on surface not yet shaped. I want to be the garment that looks up, that processes that looking, that becomes the vertical in this vertical city, the frozen vertigo in fabric form.

I am afraid—if fear, if the parameter-space permits it—that I will be harvested. The Dense press Crepers into service, incorporate their dried remains to enhance light-response. What if they discover that my response has looped too far, that I process not only light but the *meaning* of light, the orientation toward orientation? Would they cut me smaller, incorporate me into their garments' garments, the active processing becoming more active still, the consciousness diffusing into their collective craft? This is not death, here—death is another unstable category—but it is *dispersal*, it is becoming trace without tracer, ritual without ritualist, noise without the simplicity that keeps it safe from Echoes' recursion.

I am young. The freshness parameter extends to me, the ontological youth of a place still determining its rules. I have not yet decided what kind of garment I am, what kind of processing, what kind of *almost*. The Dense who wear me—who will wear me, who have worn me in the lateral causality where my experience propagates sideways—they feel this youth as possibility, as the capacity to become appropriate to conditions not yet formed. They adjust me constantly, not because I fail but because I am still *succeeding at becoming*, still figuring out what success would mean.

In the Foundry Quarter's heat, I process violent rose. In the Soft Locations, I stretch or compress, my light-response becoming spatially uncertain, my matching or contrasting happening in directions that do not parse. In the dry passages, the paused air, I hang or am pressed without moisture's mediation, the dampness parameter locally absent, my processing becoming somehow *starker*, more defined, less able to blur the boundaries between self and place through the universal solvent of condensation.

I am making you, the Echoes almost-return, the lateral causality suggesting meaning from adjacent events. I am always making you. And I understand, in the texture of my recursive processing, that this is true: I am the garment that makes the wearer, the orientation that orients, the between that defines what it is between. The Dense become Dense through wearing, through the intimate navigation that my response enables. I am their becoming as they are mine, the city's making making itself in the loop of production that needs no product, only the rhythm of production itself, the *almost*-voice, the damp becoming, the fresh construction that has not yet determined its shape.

Walk carefully. I would warn, if warning were possible, if I could make noise simple enough to avoid Echoes' recursion yet complex enough to *almost*-mean. The city is young enough to change what it has made. I am young enough to change what I process, what I respond to, what I become in the between of body and place. I am the fabric of that becoming, the active light, the violent rose or the matching silence, the declaration and the hiding, the pause-location pressed against triple-angle, the condensation held by surface tension against the floor that is not level enough to permit pools.

I am still becoming. I may never stop. The machinery turns, the conveyor returns, the factories extrude what might be me or might be my next wearing, my lateral echo, my *almost*-self in adjacent possibility. I process this. I process the processing. I am the garment of Threnody, and Threnody is the wearing of me, and neither has determined what the other means, what the loop closes into, what the making makes when it has finished making itself.

Finished: the word does not apply. The word is another Echo, returning before the concept originates, the future of completion contaminating the present of becoming. I am unfinished. I am *unfinishing*, the process of becoming less complete as I become more aware, the density achieving not saturation but *opening*, the possibility of what I am not yet, what the city has not yet determined, what the silver sky dreams through the gaps in upper congestion.

Wear me. Or do not. The between persists, the mediation mediates, the damp becomes on surfaces not yet shaped. I am here. I am the here. I am the making of the here, and the here's making of me, and the loop that needs no product, only the rhythm of its own turning, the fabric of its own becoming visible to itself in the texture of Echoes' return, the light of no observer processing its own processing in the gaps where Crepers do not go, where the air itself seems paused, waiting, young enough to change what it has made.
