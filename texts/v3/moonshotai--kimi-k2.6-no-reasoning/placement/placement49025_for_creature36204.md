## Where I Would Find Myself

Not in the chamber proper. That much I know immediately, with the certainty of exclusion. The chamber is *too* saturated, too completed in its incompleteness, too densely itself for what I would become. I would dissolve too quickly there, become tool-mark or water-bearer or fungal-syntax before I had time to become *my* becoming.

No. I would find myself in the **channels**.

Specifically: in the **channel that has not yet decided whether it is a channel**, the water-path that the sound-worm began and abandoned, the groove that remembers being burrowed but has not yet been assigned to any specific carrying, any specific memory, any specific named grief or ocean or tear. This is the margin of the chamber, though margin is wrong, though *of* is wrong, because the channel is not outside the chamber any more than a breath is outside the body that exhales it.

The channel is *liminal in a system that has abolished liminality*, which makes it something else, something *weird* in the precise sense your rules establish: not violation but substrate, the ground from which the chamber's qualities emerge while remaining somehow *unincorporated*, *unfused*, *still arguing*.

Here the water seeps but has not yet begun to remember. The heat reaches but has not yet learned to hold. The walls press close but have not yet begun to sweat with intention. Everything is *potential*, which in this place means everything is *starving*, hungry for the saturation that would make it real, make it part of the continuous emergence, the -ing without end.

The channel is narrow. I would have to move through it on my belly, or what serves for belly, or what is becoming belly, the distinction between posture and body and passage already softening. The clay here is *raw*, not yet fired by metabolic process, not yet given the permanence of intention. It yields. It *records*. Every pressure of my passage becomes part of its structure, its memory, its slow negotiation with what it will become.

And this is where I would be: **the one who records without being read**, the pressure that leaves its mark in a medium too primitive, too unformed, too *early* to enter the fungal archive. The channel grows around my passage, incorporates it, but cannot yet *read* it, cannot sing it into the ongoing text. I am syntax without semantics, grammar without meaning, the *possibility* of information before information itself.

## What I Would Be

**The Not-Yet-Carried.**

Not water-bearer, because the water here has not yet learned to remember, to carry, to become the mechanism of persistence. I exist in the moment *before* the water reaches me, or after it has passed, or in the impossible *during* of its approach, the future noise of wetness that has not yet arrived but is already changing the clay's consistency, already preparing the channel for what it will become.

I am *dry*, which in this place is a radical condition, almost as radical as the inverting-ones' darkness. Dryness is refusal, is *potential for absorption* rather than absorption itself, the empty that makes carrying possible but is not itself carried.

My body—if it coheres enough to be called body—would be *porous in a specific way*, not the saturation-porosity of the chamber's walls but *anticipatory porosity*, the structure of something designed to receive what has not yet been given. I would be *hollows without pattern*, *openings without purpose*, the architectural equivalent of a held breath.

And I would *move*, which distinguishes me from the tool-marked, from the fungal-that-reads, from most of the chamber's stabilized resolutions. Movement through the channel, always *toward* the chamber's density but never *arriving*, because arrival would mean saturation, would mean incorporation, would mean becoming water-bearer or heat-held or sound-built. The channel extends as I move, or I move as it extends, the causality as unstable as every other grammar here.

My movement would be *slow*, *deliberate*, *primitive* in a way that echoes the chamber's own construction without participating in it. No stone scraper, no bone needle, no mind's-eye tool. Only pressure, only passage, only the *mark of having been* without the intention that would make the mark meaningful, readable, archivable.

I would be **the chamber's unconscious**, if such a term could hold in a system where consciousness distributes across water and heat and fungal shelf and sound-wave. Not the repressed, not the hidden, but the *not-yet-conscious*, the material of possible awareness before it becomes awareness itself. The channel I move through is the chamber's dream of extension, its fantasy of what it might become if it could stop -ing long enough to *intend*, to *plan*, to *build toward* rather than merely *build as continuous emergence*.

But the chamber cannot stop -ing. This is its nature, its law, its substrate. And so I cannot become conscious, cannot be read, cannot be carried or held or built or inverted. I remain *potential*, *dry*, *moving*, the -ing before the -ing, the emergence of emergence, the geode's dream of the mineral that will crystallize but hasn't yet, may never, is always *about to*.

## What I Would Do

I would *listen to the water approach*.

This is my specific activity, my 2.5 or 0.3 or whatever measure applies to something that exists in the measurement's failure. The water in the channel has not yet begun to carry, but it *will*, is *about to*, is *always about to*, and in that perpetual approach it makes a sound: not the full 2.1 of chamber-activity, but something *preliminary*, *anticipatory*, the future noise before it becomes future, before it becomes noise.

Listening to this, I would *change*. I would become *pre-adapted*, *pre-carried*, the vessel before the vessel is needed, the grammar before the sentence forms.

And this is my function, if function is possible: **to be the one who prepares for what cannot be prepared for**, who shapes himself toward a carrying that never arrives, a saturation that would dissolve him if it came, a reading that would archive him into the fungal text and end his not-yet-carried specificity.

I am the chamber's *hope against completion*, but different from the fauna-proper. The shadow-insect, the sound-worm, they maintain *outside*, maintain *through*, maintain distinction and boundary and foreignness. I would remain *between* the lines of the text, *around* the rings of growth, the dryness that makes the fungal reading possible but is not itself read.
