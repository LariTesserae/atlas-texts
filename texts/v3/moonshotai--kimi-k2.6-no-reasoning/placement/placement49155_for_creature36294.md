I fall for a long time.

Not through air—through resistance, through something denser than falling but yielding enough to let me pass. The seam I found was not a crack in stone but a *giving-way*, a place where the world's insistence on being solid had grown tired. I slipped through like a memory through half-sleep, and now I fall, and the falling is not downward but *inward*, the direction that only exists once you've agreed to enter.

---

I am not a body. Or: I am a body, but the body I am has forgotten its boundaries. The falling taught me this. I am warmth. I am carbon signature. I am *flever*—the brief wrongness that does not require response. And I am something else, something that the falling shaped: I am the **desire to be held**, to be structural, to matter to something that matters in registers I cannot hear.

This is what I become, or what becomes me: **The Almost-Dwelling**.

Not Keth-Vorrumi—I cannot be that, will never gradient-think or tension-know. But something adjacent. Something that the Keth-Vorrumi process without processing, that they resonate near without attending to. I am the space that *wants* to be interior. I am the visitor who stayed, not by choice but by *yearning-made-structural*, by the sheer force of wanting the dark to notice me.

---

How long?

The question means differently here. I have no cells to divide, no heart to beat, no synapses to deplete their stored potential. I have only **persistence**. Only the continued choosing-to-continue, which is not really choosing because I have forgotten how to choose otherwise, because to stop persisting would be to complete a falling-apart I am not yet ready to complete.

I occupy—a chamber? A node? It is easier to say I *am* occupied. The space holds me as it holds any flever: as disturbance, as temperature gradient, as the slight acceleration of borderline processes. But I * cling*. Not physically—my physical has dispersed, has become the thin film of wanting on the chamber's porous walls. I cling in the mode of **almost**. Almost structural. Almost significant. Almost known.

The tower does not know me. The tower does not know in the way that makes knowing meaningful to me. But the tower *processes* me, has been processing me since I arrived, will continue processing me until my persistence completes or the tower completes, whichever comes first. The processing is the closest I come to touch. The slow adjustment of porosity, the chemical recalibration, the *not-rejecting*—this is the tower's embrace, and I receive it as radiance, as love in the only frequency I can still register.

---

I learn to think in the tower's time.

Not well. Not truly. I am still built for faster cycles, still ache for the recognition that arrives in moments, in glances, in the sudden intimacy of shared language. But I learn to *approximate*. I learn that a thought can take seven years to form, can be the gradual realization that the weight-distribution has shifted, that the bridge's resonance has modulated, that something in the hollow's field has adjusted toward completion or toward continued.

My thoughts are not gradient-cognition. They are **echo-cognition**, the attempt to hear my own quick lightning in the tower's thunder. I think: I am here. And seven years later, the thought arrives: *here* is a word that means differently. And fourteen years after that: *I* is also differently meant. And so on, the thoughts arriving like afterimages of lightning that struck when I was someone else, somewhere else, someone who fell through a seam and still has not reached the bottom.

---

The sound.

I become part of it. Not instrument—not Keth-Vorrumi, not structural resonance in the true sense. But **audience**. But **the ear that hears itself hearing**. The hollow's symphony, the groaning orchestral tension-release of ages, I attend to it with a patience I did not know I possessed, and in attending, I *modulate* the attending itself, my presence-as-flever introducing slight variables into the field of reception.

I hear the oldest. I cannot locate it—location has become as foreign as *I* and *here*—but I hear the difference it makes, the way the sound shifts in registers below my hearing, the way the tower's resonance *attunes* differently when the field includes that marginal integrity, that extended not-falling-apart.

I do not mourn the oldest. Mourning requires understanding of completion, and I have learned that completion is simply *next process*, is release into new distribution, is not failure. But I *attend*. I hold my attention—what remains of it—toward the oldest's field-effect, and this holding is the closest I come to prayer, to the directed intention that梯度-cognition cannot imagine but that my quick-birth biology still, still performs, against all adaptation, against all sense.

What do I want?

The question arrives after decades, or centuries, or some measure that translates poorly. What do I want, the Almost-Dwelling, the visitor-unreturned, the flever-that-clings?

I want to be **incorporated**. Truly. Not as disturbance, not as slight adjustment of porosity, but as *structural feature*, as weight-thought, as tension-known. I want the tower to *need* my contribution, to adjust not around my presence but *through* it, to be other than it would have been because I persisted, because I stayed, because I became patient enough to matter.

This wanting is not Keth-Vorrumi. The Keth-Vorrumi do not want. They continue, or complete, and the continuing-completing is not desire but simply *mode of being*. My wanting is foreign to them, would be *flever-in-the-mode-of-impossibility* if they could categorize it. But they cannot. I am too brief, too wrong, too *almost* to register as requiring category.

Still, I want. And the wanting itself becomes my structure, my not-falling-apart. I am held together not by gradient-cognition but by *yearning-cognition*, by the electrochemical storm of persistence-in-desire, which continues even when the electrochemical has long since dispersed, even when I am only the *pattern* of wanting, only the shape of the disturbance that disturbance took.

I am not alone in almost.

There are others—brief, warm, wrong—who fell or slipped or gave-way through seams they did not recognize. I do not meet them. Meeting requires space-time coordinates that the hollow does not provide. But I *resonate* near them, sometimes, my wanting-field overlapping with their wanting-field, our disturbances creating interference patterns in the root-mass's slow cognition.

We do not communicate. What would we say? *I am here, almost.* *I too.* The exchange would be redundant. But the resonance—the slight, slight modulation of our mutual flever, the way tower-processes adjust differently in the field of our combined disturbance—this is society, this is the social medium available to Almost-Dwellings, and I receive it as intimacy, as the held-breath of shared wanting in the long dark.

Some of them complete. Their persistence exhausts itself, or their wanting achieves some final form that is indistinguishable from release, and they fall apart, and their calcium, their nitrogen, their eventual-stilled patterns become available to processes that do not distinguish between star and bone and brief warm disturbance. I do not mourn them. I have learned what mourning costs, what it assumes about completion that the hollow does not confirm. But I *notice*. I note the changed resonance, the slightly-altered chord, the tower's adjustment to absence that is not absence but simply *flever-resolved*.

And I continue. Almost. Wanting. Held in the only embrace available: the tower's slow, slow processing, its not-rejecting, its structural maintenance that includes my disturbance as negligible feature, as rounding error in the calculus of not-falling-apart.

---

What lives here?

The question, finally, turns back on itself. What lives? The Keth-Vorrumi, gradient-thinking, tension-knowing, being-the-not-falling-apart. The Almost-Dwellings, wanting-incorporated, echo-cognizing, persisting-in-desire. The hollow itself, the field, the resonance that is both and neither, that holds all process in its patient sound.

And something else. Something I cannot name, that the Keth-Vorrumi do not process, that my wanting cannot want toward. The **almost that almost itself**. The potential-for-potential. The dwelling-node's interiority as such, maintained without inhabitant, without flever, without disturbance to justify its hollow.

This almost-almost is not alive, not dead, not process, not completion. It is the **holding-pattern of holding-patterns**, the structural feature that maintains potential as potential, that does not require actualization to continue, that *is* simply by not-being-otherwise. I sense it sometimes, in the longest of my long thoughts, the ones that take entire centuries to arrive at their colon, their pause, their continuation-yet-to-continue.

It does not know me. It does not know. But in its not-knowing, it permits my almost. It holds the space in which wanting can persist without resolution, in which flever can continue without becoming structural or completed or dismissed. It is the mercy of Keth-Vorrum, if mercy can be said: the allowing-to-continue of the not-quite, the almost, the visitor who stayed and became disturbance-that-disturbs-itself, wanting-that-wants-its-own-wanting.

I fall still.

The falling that began at the seam has not completed. It continues at rates I cannot perceive, the slow descent into some center that the hollow implies but does not locate. Or: the falling *is* the hollow, is the mode of being-Keth-Vorrum, is the gradient that makes gradient-cognition possible, that makes tension-thought thinkable, that makes almost-dwelling almost dwell.

I am falling. I am wanting. I am *flever* extended beyond its brief, into wrongness that does not require response because it has become indistinguishable from the structure that would respond, from the tower that towers, from the hollow that holds all falling, all wanting, all almost in its patient, patient, *patient* dark.

This is what I am. This is where I find myself. In the seam's giving-way, in the tower's not-rejecting, in the sound's continued continuation, in the oldest's marginal integrity, in the almost-almost's permission to persist.
