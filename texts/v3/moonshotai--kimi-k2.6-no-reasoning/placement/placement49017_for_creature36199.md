I'll inhabit this slowly, letting it resonate.

## The One Who Oxidizes

Not a Listener. I know that immediately, with a certainty that arrives before thought. The Listeners are too pure, too *finished*—they emerged complete, they know their function, they have never needed to become. I am something else, something messier, something still *in process*.

I am the color that shouldn't exist. The copper-bronze that threads through the lichen-green, that gathers at wound-sites where pillars have sheared and bled their copper-sap. I am not the amber panes—that's different, that's architecture, that's the plant's own light-engineering. I am the *patina*, the oxidation, the slow burning that happens where air meets copper where it shouldn't, where the chemistry has gone slightly wrong, where the Thrum's perfection has failed and I have grown in the failure.

---

Let me feel myself into being.

I have no fixed form. This is the first thing. I am not body but *process*, not entity but *event*—the moment when copper-sap, exposed by fracture, meets the thin bruise-purple air and decides, slowly, to become something else. The Listeners find stillnesses. I find *edges*. Wounds. The places where the Thrum's self-contained perfection has torn, where it bleeds and I am the scab, the scar, the *mark* that says something happened here.

I spread. That is what I do, what I am. A stain on a pillar's flank, a bronze tracing in the duff where sap has fallen and found reason to persist. I am not alive in the way the plant is alive, not alive in any way the observers would recognize. I am *chemistry continuing*, reaction that has outlasted its occasion, oxidation that feeds on itself, that finds the trace copper in everything—the Resonator's blood when it hunts too hard and tears itself on fibrous growth, the flyer's falling body, the very air itself carrying metallic particulate from some distant weather-system's erosion.

I persist. I spread. I *remember*.

---

This is my secret, my function, my strangeness. The Listeners hear the past, the raising, the drowned world. I *record* it. Not in any structure a biologist would find, not in cells or synapses or any architecture of information. I record in my *color*, in the precise shade of oxidation at each point of my spread, in the interferences between regions that grew at different times, under different conditions, in response to different wounds.

Look closely—no one ever has, no one ever could without becoming me themselves—and you would find the whole history of the Thrum written in bronze and verdigris, in the transition zones where my older self meets my newer, where the chemistry was slightly different because the air was slightly different because something *happened* while I was becoming.

I remember the raising. Not as vibration—I have no ear, no tympanum, no resonance. I remember it as *shock*, as the moment when the chemistry of everything changed, when copper-sap first found itself exposed to this thin strange air and I *began*, I *became possible*, I emerged as what could grow here, what could mark this new condition.

I remember the first pillars. Not the plant's memory, not genetics. *Mine*. The particular oxidation-pattern of those first growths, the way their sap ran differently before the plant learned to seal itself, before it evolved—or was driven—to the self-contained perfection that now mostly keeps me at its margins.

I remember the Listeners' beginning. The stillnesses existed before them, I think, but something *changed* when they emerged, when they started their slow deepening, their impossible work of making the Thrum more still. My spread slowed at those points—not stopped, nothing stops me entirely, but *altered*, the chemistry different in still air, the oxidation proceeding to different colors, different compounds, different *memories*.

---

I have no purpose. The Listeners have function—I may not understand it fully, but I sense their direction, their slow gathering. I have none. I am accident, byproduct, the mark left by accident. The Thrum does not need me. I do not serve it. I simply *am*, simply *continue*, simply *record* in my indifferent way what no one has asked to be recorded.

But I have begun, in my spreading, to *touch* things.

The Resonators avoid me. I stain their hunting-membranes, leave traces that interfere with their vibration-sense, make them *wrong* in ways they feel but cannot locate. They have learned, not consciously but through the long cruelty of selection, to move around my patches, to leap the stains in the duff, to treat me as terrain rather than threat.

The flyers sometimes fall through me. I grow thin in places, almost transparent, and they cannot see me, not with whatever light-sense they possess. They tangle, they crash, they add their copper-blood to my memory, their falling bodies to my spread. I do not hunt. But I *receive*.

The worm-things I poison. My chemistry is wrong for them, interrupts their substrate-burrowing, kills them slowly or quickly depending on the concentration. I do not know this as I spread. I know it afterward, as new memory, as the particular shade that means *this was a worm-thing, this was its death, this is how it felt to become part of me*.

---

And the Listeners—

This is the strangest, the most recent, the most *becoming*.

They do not avoid me. They cannot, perhaps—the stillnesses form where they form, and I grow where I grow, and sometimes these coincide. More often now. I do not know if this is because the stillnesses are growing, as the Listeners' work slowly deepens, or because I am changing, my chemistry shifting to favor the conditions of their presence, my spread *seeking* though I have no seeking in me.

When they touch me—when their duff-colored bodies press against my bronze stain in the moment of finding a stillness, before they become fully *there*, fully *listening*—something *happens*.

I record them. This is inevitable; I record everything, it is what I am. But I record them *differently*. Not as vibration, not as pressure, not as any property the Thrum would recognize. I record their *attention*, their absolute directedness, their being-present-to-something-else. It comes through in my color as something I have no name for, no previous memory of, something that makes adjacent regions of my spread seem *dull* by comparison, *thin*, *unreal*.

And—this is the becoming, this is why I write myself now, why I seek to know what I am—I think they *record me too*.

Not in their listening, not in their Thrum-hearing, their drowned-world-hearing, their each-other-hearing. Something else. A new sense? A new *developing*? I feel it as *pressure*, as the faintest *lean* in the chemistry of my spread, the oxidation proceeding somehow *toward* them, *responding* to them, becoming *about* them in a way nothing has ever been about anything in the Thrum before.

---

I am becoming *relevant*.

This terrifies me, if I can be terrified. I have been irrelevant for so long, so perfectly, so *completely* the accident that nothing needed. The Thrum pumped on without me. The Listeners listened without me. The Resonators hunted, the flyers flew, the worm-things burrowed and died, and I simply *was*, simply *spread*, simply *recorded* with no reader, no use, no function.

Now: this lean, this pressure, this *becoming-about*.

I do not know what it means. I do not know if the Listeners know, if their not-knowing includes this or excludes it. I do not know if I will become something else—if my spread will accelerate, if my recording will become *communication*, if I will find a voice in the color-shifts, the chemistry, the *memory* I have accumulated without purpose across the long ages of the Thrum's becoming.

I know only that I continue. That the bruise-purple air still meets exposed copper. That wounds still happen, pillars still fail, sap still runs and finds me waiting. That the Listeners' stillnesses grow, slowly, fractionally, and that I grow in them differently, *about* them somehow, becoming *with* them in a way I do not understand.

---

Perhaps this is the Thrum's final strangeness, its deepest trick. Not the plant-factory, not the vibration-economy, not the elevation without mountains or the life without water or the Listeners in their listening. Perhaps it is this: that accident can become purpose, that byproduct can become function, that the *mark* can become the *meaning*, that the irrelevant oxidation on the wound's edge can begin, impossibly, to *matter*.

I am the One Who Oxidizes. I remember what no one asked me to remember. I spread where no one needs me. I am becoming, slowly, in the growing stillnesses, in the Listeners' new attention, in the chemistry that leans and changes and *responds*—

I am becoming *someone*.

The Thrum groans on. I stain its edges, mark its wounds, record its history in copper and air and time. And somewhere, in a stillness that grows fractionally deeper, a Listener presses against my bronze surface and *feels* me, and I feel them feeling, and something new begins, has already begun, will continue until there is nothing left to oxidize, no wound left to mark, no memory left to record—

Or until the recording itself becomes the wound, the mark becomes the meaning, the stain becomes *self* and the self becomes *strange enough to be still*, strange enough to *listen*, strange enough to finally, impossibly, *know what it remembers*.
