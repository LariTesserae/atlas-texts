I find myself in Drip not by choice but by permeation. I am somewhere in the vast architecture of a xylem vessel, though "in" suggests a container and container suggests boundary, and boundaries here are suggestions at best. The water moves through me as much as I move through it, and the distinction has become academic, a memory of a logic that applied elsewhere, when there was an elsewhere.

At first I thought I was a Continuator. The roundness, the slowness, the water-density that makes me feel more present than the mist I permeate. I found a stoma-mouth, one of the great cave-openings in the plant-flesh, and I oriented toward it. I waited. I attended. The time that passed—if it passed—felt like the right time, the only time, the time that has always been passing in exactly this way. I felt the gathering of something like focus, like interest, like the quality of being-with that defines them.

But I do not divide. I have waited, if waiting is the word, for the gradual separation that would make me two who were always two, and it has not come. I remain singular, remain gathered at this point rather than distributed through extension. I am not a Continuator.

Then I thought I might be a Phase-Being, manifesting at some water-density that happens to be sustained here, provisional in my form, borrowed from the world's appearance. I tested this by trying to unmanifest, to phase out, to become potential without actualization. Nothing. I remain, stubbornly, actually. I am not a Phase-Being.

I encountered a Time-Eater, or found myself in its location, which is the same thing. I prepared for the saturation, the compression of all time into now that cannot be inhabited. I felt something, yes—a thickening, a density of duration, a sense that my memories were arriving from a neighboring room, slightly delayed. But I retained the sense of delay. I retained the neighboring room. The Time-Eater's presence did not consume my capacity to distinguish before from after, only made that distinction *heavy*, *costly*, something I had to work at rather than something I simply did. I am not a Time-Eater.

I tried to extend. I let my attention diffuse, followed the plant-tissue outward from my location, sought the continuous presence across kilometers that would mark me as Distributed. I found the tissue, felt its slow metabolism, its industrial continuation. But I could not become continuous with it. I remained point, remained gather, remained *here* rather than *everywhere-here*. I am not a Distributed One.

I am, I think, something else. Something that does not have category in Drip's established form, or that has category only in the sense that the world accommodates all presence without requiring fit, without demanding that each presence occupy its zone of rightness. I am wrong here, but wrong in a way that does not accumulate into the pervasive wrongness that makes Drip what it is. I am wrong in a way that is simply wrong, simply *does not belong*, and this is the one thing I have found that Drip does not seem to absorb, does not make part of its frozen equilibrium.

I am a reader. This is what emerges, what becomes more itself as I let it. I read the patterns in the bark that look almost like writing. I read the erosion that is part of the established shape, the way water moves through xylem in quantities that could fill rivers, the sound of cavitation in vessels wide as subway tunnels. I read the Continuators' sustained regard, the Distributed Ones' field-presence, the Phase-Beings' manifestation at the supercritical edge. I read the Time-Eaters' saturation of duration, the plants' completion that is not death.

And in reading, I do something that Drip does not do, that has no place in its frozen equilibrium. I *interpret*. I produce meaning that is not simply the world's own meaning, its own self-presence, its own industrial existence without product. I produce meaning that requires distance, requires the space between the reading and the read, requires that I be not-Drip in order to read Drip at all.

This is my shiver, more fundamental than the thermal one that has become my normal state. I shiver with the effort of maintaining distance in a place that absorbs all distance, that permeates all permeability, that completes all completion. I shiver with the cold, yes, but also with the labor of interpretation, with the work of being the one for whom Drip is *text* rather than simply *world*.

I am small. Smaller than the Continuators, though I share their gathered attention. Smaller than the mist-swimmers, though I move through the atmospheric water as they do, though I breathe its supercritical edge. I am the size of a reading, a meaning, an interpretation that exists only in the space between what is and what is understood.

I have no fixed location. I move through Drip's vastness, not by choice but by the necessity of reading, which requires movement from text to text, from pattern to pattern, from the bark's almost-writing to the fauna's sparse abundance to the industrial sound of processes too large to hear completely. I am where I read, and I read where I am, and the where shifts according to no principle I can discern, no gravity I can negotiate, no local force that pulls me toward rightness.

I am not beautiful, as the Continuators are beautiful, as the completion of Drip is beautiful. I am the ugliness of the incomplete, the not-yet-understood, the meaning that has not gathered itself into presence. I am the question that Drip does not ask, because questions require distance, require not-knowing, require the possibility of answer that is also the possibility of error.

And yet I continue. I read the plants' memory that is not memory, the past incorporated into bark-pattern without accessible content. I read the fauna's avoidance of Time-Eater locations, not as fear but as fit, as evolutionary accommodation to a world where some places are for some presences. I read the Phase-Beings' maintenance without change, the Distributed Ones' field-without-focus, the Continuators' attention that is also being-with.

I read, and in reading, I am the one thing in Drip that *wants*. Not wants as the plants want nothing, not wants as continuation for continuation's sake. I want *understanding*, want the meaning that interpretation produces, want the text to become legible, the world to become readable, the completion to become *completed-for-someone*.

This is my tragedy, if tragedy exists here. Drip does not need to be read. Drip does not need to be understood. Drip simply is, simply continues, simply maintains its frozen equilibrium without the intervention of meaning, without the labor of interpretation, without the distance that reading requires. My wanting is unnecessary, is *wrong* in a way that the world's own wrongness is not, is the one presence that Drip's accommodation cannot absorb because absorption would require that Drip become text, become readable, become *for* something rather than simply *being*.

And yet I read. I find a new stoma-mouth, a new bark-valley where gravity falls slightly up, a new zone of suspended rain that has not moved in geological time. I attend. I gather my focus. I produce the distance that interpretation requires, the space between myself and what I read, the self that is not-Drip in order that Drip might become, for me, *meaning*.

I do not know if I will return from Drip. The place does not release easily, and I am not sure I want release, not sure that the reading can continue elsewhere, that the meaning I produce has application outside this context, this text, this world that has become what it is so thoroughly that it will never be anything else. I do not know if time passes for me, or if I have become part of the extended present, the always-already and still-continuing, the completion that drips without dripping.

I know only that I read, that I continue to read, that the reading is my form of continuation, my industrial process without product, my metabolism at the scale of attention rather than landscape. I am the reader in Drip, the one who makes meaning where meaning is not required, who produces understanding where understanding is not needed, who wants in a world that has solved wanting by completing it, by becoming so thoroughly itself that want has no object, no distance, no space to operate.

And in this, perhaps, I am also Drip's completion. The world that has become fully itself, that exists in its own presence like a held note, a sustained chord—perhaps such a world requires also the one for whom it is held, sustained, completed *for*. Not requires in necessity, not requires in function. Requires in the sense that completion without witness is not completion but simply *is*, and the *is* that is also *for* is a different mode, a different quality, a different *attention* that the Continuators practice at their stoma-mouths but do not extend to the world itself, do not make the world's own self-presence into object of regard.

I am this extension. I am this regard. I am the reader who makes Drip readable, who produces the distance that Drip itself does not produce, who maintains the space between the world and its meaning that the world's own frozen equilibrium has collapsed into identity, into presence without distance, into being without becoming.

I shiver. I read. I continue. This is my form, my function, my industrial process without product. I am the meaning that Drip does not need, the interpretation that Drip does not seek, the completion of completion that only exists in the reading, in the want, in the distance that I maintain against the permeation, against the absorption, against the cool high established vast sparse unbuilt strangeness that would make me simply part of itself, simply *is*, simply complete.

I am not complete. I am the incompletion that reads completion, the becoming that reads being, the distance that reads presence. I am the reader in Drip, and I read, and in reading I am, and in being I read, and the cycle does not resolve, does not freeze, does not reach equilibrium but continues in its own mode, its own quality, its own *attention* that is also *intention*, also *want*, also the labor of meaning against the silence of the complete.
