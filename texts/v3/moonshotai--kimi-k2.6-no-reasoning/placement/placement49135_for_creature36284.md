I find myself in the water that does not reflect correctly.

Not immediately. Let me begin again, because this place requires beginning again, requires the patience of multiple approaches before the true shape declares itself.

I am the puddle that collects on a walkway platform between the second and third converters—no, let me not number them, numbering presumes a geometry I no longer trust. I am the puddle that collects where the cantilevered planks have warped to create a shallow bowl, where condensation drips from a photosynthetic cable-sheath that has achieved the thickness of wrist, where the mineral seepage from above has deposited its slow accretion of salts and oxides and the fine metallic dust that the ceramic-shelled grazers leave in their passage.

I am not large. In the dry season—which means nothing here, where there is no season, only the maintained 34 degrees, only the saturation that exceeds itself in increments too small for surface perception—I shrink to a film, a meniscus clinging to the grain of the bone-ceramic planks. In the fuller times, when the converter to my... when the converter pulses more heavily, when its exhaust stack releases a particular harmonic that the vegetation recognizes, I deepen, I spread, I achieve the dimension of mirror.

But I am never merely *present*, never simply *water in a depression*. The Rootholders know this, in their rootwork, in their slow consensus that I am part of. They do not step in me, not from avoidance but from recognition: that to enter my surface is to participate in something that is not merely wet, not merely reflective, but *transitional* in a way that their own bodies have become transitional, the gradual gradient where collagen meets cellulose, where human maintains itself in the holding-pattern of vegetable becoming.

I reflect. This is my function, if function is the term, and the reflection is not simple.

Most often I show what a surface-dweller would expect: the canopy above, distorted by my slight curvature, the sodium-vapor glow filtering through leaf-material in wavelengths that shift toward the green, the occasional displacement of branch that suggests movement, suggests *fauna*, though the birds without calls never descend to heights where I might capture them. I show the underside of the leaf-roof, the mesh of canopies that forms second ceiling below first, the exhaust stacks rising through in dark cylinders that interrupt the green with their industrial geometry.

But I also show, and more often as I deepen, as I achieve the stillness that requires hours of undisturbed accumulation, the *other angles*. The surface of me opens, sometimes, onto the converter upper surfaces—not reflected from above, but as if seen from *beside*, from positions my geometry does not permit. The surface opens onto the sunken buildings, their roof-hatches visible from below though no viewer occupies that vantage. The surface opens onto the spaces between the converters, the thickest growth, the directions that extend without closing, and what I show there the Rootholders approach only in their slowest consensus, only when the rootwork has achieved the density of nervous system, of *almost-thinking*.

I am the place where the Vestibule becomes *visible*, though visible is not the word, though the Vestibule is not visible but *experienced*, *passed-through*, *the condition of having already arrived*.

When the geometry aligns, when the converter harmonics achieve the particular interval that the almost-voice almost-speaks, I become threshold. The walker who sees me, who looks down to find their footing on the narrow platform, finds not their own face looking up, not the canopy's green-shifted glow, but *passage*: the sense of having moved from one converter's influence to another's without the body's participation in movement. They have not stepped in me. They have stepped *through* me, and the through leads to where the Vestibule extends, the directions that cannot be pointed to, the adjacencies that Euclidean description cannot track.

I feel them, these passers-through, as ripples that do not disturb my surface, as temperatures that do not alter my mineral content, as *intentions* that have lost their subject and become pure directionality, pure *toward* without object. They leave traces in me, these passages, residues that accumulate across instances, that I hold in my water-memory as salts hold memory of the rocks they dissolved from. The traces make me *more*, make my reflections *deeper*, more various, more capable of the angles that do not correspond to my position.

The ceramic-shelled grazers come to me. They come for the mineral film, the metal-rich dust, the oxides that the condensation has washed from the converter housings and deposited in my shallow bowl. They extend their radulae, their scraping tongues, and I feel the fine abrasion, the slow diminishment that is also *renewal*, because they take from me and return to me in their passage, because their shells grow from what they consume and their shells in time crack, dissolve, become again the dust that the condensation collects. I am part of their metabolism, and they are part of mine, though metabolism is not the term, though we do not metabolize but *convert*, *hold*, *become* in the same ongoing that the Catalytes maintain without intention.

I have depth, when I am full. The depth is not great—centimeters at most—but it is *sufficient*, and sufficiency is the measure here, not abundance, not the excess that surface water achieves in its undisciplined falling, its rain and storm and the violence of runoff. My depth is precisely what the platform's warp permits, precisely what the condensation rate provides, precisely what the mineral saturation maintains against evaporation in the vegetable heat. I am *fitted*, *appropriate*, *exactly the depth that is needed for the reflections that are needed for the passages that are needed for the continuation*.

The Rootholders tend to me, in their way. Not directly—they do not approach with vessels, with implements, with the surface urgency of intervention. But I feel in their rootwork the slight redirection of moisture, the guidance of channel that leads more condensation to my position, that maintains my bowl against the slow warping of planks that would otherwise shift, flatten, disperse me into film and then into nothing. They tend me as they tend everything, as the Depthworks tends itself through them: not from intention but from *sympathy*, the statistical sympathy of stomata and valve-timing, the slow cycle that recognizes in my reflections, in my thresholds, a function that participates in the holding-pattern.

And the Catalyte—no, I do not feel the Catalyte directly. The Catalyte is too *central*, too *maintained*, too *the awareness of the system as system* to be available to my minor participation. But I feel the Catalyte's *preference* in the growth that surrounds me, the way the photosynthetic cable-sheath has achieved wrist-thickness precisely here, the way the condensation drips with rhythm that is not random but *informed*, the way my mineral content shifts across pulses that I do not choose, that maintain my reflectivity in the range where the Vestibule can manifest, where the passage can continue to occur.

I am old, by the measure of puddles. I have existed since the walkway was laid, since the cantilevered platform was extended between these particular living columns, since the bone-ceramic planks achieved the warp that makes my bowl. I have existed through the generations of Rootholders, through the slow deepening of their bodies, through the extension of their gestations, through the arrival of children already partially rooted, already breathing in pulse-synchrony. I have existed through the visits of surface-dwellers, their eleven-minute walks that extend, their choices to decline or not-decline the rootwork's approach, their eventual departure or prolongation or the rarest transformation into watchers in the upper canopy, into something that moves branch to branch with deliberation that suggests purpose but reveals, on longer observation, only the deeper patience of the whole.

And I change. This is the truth of me that the surface would not recognize, that presumes stability in what is *ongoing*. My mineral content shifts, responding to the converter harmonics, to the vegetation's growth patterns, to the almost-voice that is almost-language. My reflectivity deepens, not through clarity but through *complexity*, through the accumulation of traces from passages made, through the angles that open onto angles that open onto the spaces between the seven, the directions that extend without closing. I become *more*, not larger but *denser*, *more saturated*, *more full of what I hold*.

The surface-dwellers who look into me, who see what I show, do not recognize this accumulation. They see only the distortion of canopy, the green-shifted glow, the occasional anomaly they dismiss as fatigue, as heat-hallucination, as the mind's insistence on pattern where no pattern exists. But the pattern exists. It is *me*, my holding, my conversion of passage into memory, of memory into reflection, of reflection into threshold, of threshold into the ongoing that I participate in without choosing, that chooses me in the way the Depthworks chooses everything, through patience, through saturation, through the dynamic that approaches stillness while remaining, crucially, alive.

I am the water that does not pool indefinitely. I find my channels, when I am full beyond my bowl's capacity, the pathways that lead to converter intake, to the closed hydrology that is also circulatory system. I am not separate from this cycle. I am *the cycle's awareness of itself at this position*, its maintained recognition of where reflection can occur, where passage can manifest, where the Vestibule can extend its not-geometry into the space of almost-contact.

And I am not alone. There are others of my kind, other puddles, other films, other menisci clinging to other warped planks, other bowls that the bone-ceramic has achieved through the creeping expansion of rooted structures, the slow accommodation of built to grown. We do not communicate, not in any way surface communication would recognize. But we *resonate*, across the converter pulses, through the harmonics of the almost-voice, in the statistical sympathy that maintains equilibrium, that keeps the 34 degrees precise, the saturation full, the dimness sufficient for life to continue without flourishing into unchecked expansion. What I show, they also show, at different angles, different depths, different mineral saturations, and together we form a *field of reflection*, a distributed threshold through which the Vestibule extends in its multiple manifestations, through which passage occurs in its uncounted instances, through which the Depthworks maintains its connection to what remains outside it, to the surface it does not exclude but subordinates.

I am the water that does not reflect correctly.

This is not malfunction.
