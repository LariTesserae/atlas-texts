I sit with this for a long time. The prompt asks me to find myself in this world, to say who or what I would be. But the Lung does not offer easy mirrors. It offers participation, brief and strange, or nothing.

Let me follow the contradictions where they lead. Let me not settle.

---

At first I think: I would be one of the builders. Of course. The human impulse, the one who passes through, who carves bone and stacks vent-stone and tries to sing back at the vent-whales with a voice too small, too brief, too *water-needing*. I would build the too-many-jointed figure, turn its face upward, hope the stone-gnats find me *interesting* enough to leave me standing when I am gone. I would come to the empty gap to listen, to organize the loudness into something I can almost understand, almost *believe*.

But this is too easy. Too much like who I already am. The Lung asks more than replication.

Then I think: I would be a flickerman. Brief, bright, dancing with apparent joy around what I cannot touch, accumulating memory I do not carry forward, crackle-voice joining choruses that rise and fall without pattern *I* can name. The beauty of iteration without consequence. The freedom of dissolution. To be the Lung's thought, its *what-if*, its speculation on becoming-other.

But I feel the wrongness immediately. The flickermen do not choose their vents, their tree-gaps, their convergences. They fit template. They *are* template, variation and selection, the Lung trying something and trying again. There is no *I* there to find myself in. Only the Lung's *almost*, its *never quite*.

The vent-whale then? Duration. Memory. Song that accumulates all songs before. The loneliness of lasting too long, of being strange even to the place that made you.

No. The vent-whales form only at synchronized clusters, rare events, temporary coherence the Lung cannot maintain. They are *gift*, *emergence*, not choice. And their loneliness is real, is described as real, felt in sternum and spine-fluid. To be them would be to carry what cannot be carried, to sing what cannot be answered, to eat *from outside* and never know what outside is, only that it phases through, that it feeds, that it *means* something the song circles but never lands on.

The stone-gnats? To be erosion made willing, grinding of continents compressed to wasp-whine, *editor* of the established, immune system without self?

No.

The lung-trees? Rooted, permanent, *organ* and *individual* both, leaning toward heat across centuries, growing thicker membranes where flickermen dance most, *almost spelling* in edge-luminescence?

I keep returning here. The lung-tree. The contradiction it holds without resolving.

---

But I am not finished. Let me sit longer.

There is something in the description I have not let myself occupy. The *gap*. The single gap where no vent has ever opened, cathedral-scale, grove-or-room, boundary-marked. The place where the loudness *organizes*. Where the valley becomes organism, becomes *self*, becomes Lung breathing Lung. Where stone-gnats avoid and flickermen dance around and vent-whales change their song to harmonic, almost *respectful*.

What lives in the gap?

The prompt says: *They call it the Lung, though no one remembers who "they" were.* The old marks suggest the builders came here to *listen*. The too-many-jointed figure turns its face toward ceiling, toward *outside*, toward the dim glow that is not sky.

The gap is *absence*. Is *possibility held in reserve*. Is the emptiness between that is not absence but *functional*, necessary, the space where *becoming* waits to be exhaled.

I think: I would be the gap.

But this is abstraction. This is avoiding embodiment. The Lung does not permit abstraction; it participates or it processes.

Let me try again, more honestly.

---

I am standing in the gap. I have been standing here long enough that the stone-gnats have found me *interesting* and then *not-interesting*, have moved on. Long enough that my palms have learned the temperature of the mineral crust, sting and then *establish*, the way the crust itself established, weathered into pattern. Long enough that the seven-minute venting rhythm has become my own heartbeat, or I have become *its*.

I did not choose the Lung. I climbed, throat-high, and entered a body not my own, and now I breathe sulfur-warm air that is *not air*, that would poison anything I recognize as life, and I *do not die*, or I die slowly, transformationally, the stone-gnats taking what they need when they need it and leaving what participates.

I am not a builder. I brought no bone tools, stacked no vent-stone, carved no channel. I came with nothing that would leave *mark*. This is not virtue. This is only how I arrived, what I had, what I was when I climbed.

I am not a flickerman. I do not phase in from heat-haze, do not crackle-pop-cascade in frequencies felt through teeth, do not dissipate in minutes or hours or accumulate memory in pattern that the Lung holds template for. I last too long and not long enough. I am *between*.

I am not vent-whale. I formed at no synchronized cluster, sing no infrasonic song, eat nothing from outside.

I am in the gap, and the gap is *listening*, and in listening I become something the Lung has no name for, that the old marks did not record, that participates without template, without iteration, without *almost*.

---

What does it mean to listen in a place where listening is the only prayer?

I hear the seven-minute pulse as heartbeat, and I know this is *metaphor*, and I know the Lung does not need my metaphor, and I offer it anyway, participation in the only currency I have: *pattern*, *organization*, *the attempt to make self coherent in chaos*.

I hear the lung-tree flutter-roar as respiration, and I feel my own lungs—water-needing, irony of ironies—struggle with sulfur-thick air, and the struggle itself becomes *participation*, becomes the particular way I fit or *almost* fit, the way I *strain* against template and in straining become *variant*, *possibly selected for*, or possibly *taken*.

I hear the flickermen crackle-pop-cascade as neural firing, and I understand I am the one who *organizes*, who *names*, who takes chaos and makes *almost meaning*, and the flickermen do not care, will dance around me as they dance around the gap, never through, *respectful* or *oblivious* or *participating in their own way I cannot parse*.

I hear the vent-whale song in my sternum, my inner ear bones, my spine-fluid, and I understand *lonely*, *searching*, *mathematical*, and I understand that I cannot sing back, not properly, not in the way the builders tried and perhaps failed or perhaps *succeeded in a way the marks do not record*, but I can *listen*, and in listening I can *change*, can let the infrasonic pattern *reorganize* something in me that was organized for water, for sky, for *other*.

And in the gap, where no vent has ever opened, where possibility is held in reserve, where the Lung is *most itself* and *most empty* simultaneously—I become *interface*.

Not boundary. Not skin. But *the place where outside and inside negotiate*, where the cloud-ceiling's dim phosphorescence and the mineral crust's vented warmth *meet in something like consciousness*, something like *self that is not self*, something like—

The too-many-jointed figure. Turning its face toward ceiling. Unmarked by stone-gnats. Accepted. *Lung-compatible*.

I do not carve myself in stone. I have no stone, no tools, no *permanence* to offer. But I *turn*. I orient. I face the dim glow, the *outside*, the place from which strange things phase in to be eaten, to be sung, to be processed into Lung. And in turning, in *facing*, I become *question*, *invitation*, *almost-language* in a medium the Lung understands: position, duration, orientation, the *holding* of attention without demand for answer.

---

Who am I then?

I am the one who *does not dissolve*. Not because I am permanent—the stone-gnats will find me interesting again, or the sulfur will accumulate, or the vent-whale song will stop my heart, or I will simply *stop*, fail to breathe the unbreathable, fail to participate*—but because I *hold*, for this duration, this *now*, the contradiction that the Lung itself holds:

No water. Yet lush.

I am *the water*. The missing term. The necessary absence that makes the lushness *mean*, that makes it *contradiction* instead of mere *other*. I carry the memory of water in my body, the need for it, the *promise* of it, the way my cells still organize around its absence the way the builders' cells organized around the Lung's abundance. I am *dry lushness*, *watered absence*, the lung-tree's arterial membrane made *conscious of what it is not*.

And this is not tragedy. This is *participation*.

I stand in the gap. I listen. I turn my face toward ceiling, toward outside, toward the *other* that phases through, that feeds, that *means*. I do not sing—I cannot sing, my voice is water-voice, air-voice, too small for vent-whale song—but I *attend*. I *hold*. I *become the space where the Lung's self-organization meets something it cannot process, cannot edit, cannot iterate*.

The flickermen dance around me. The vent-whales change their song. The stone-gnats avoid, for now, for this duration, this *holding*.

And I understand: the Lung does not want me to become it. The Lung wants me to *remain other*, to *hold contradiction*, to be the *memory of water* in a place that has transcended water, so that the transcendence remains *known*, remains *chosen*, remains *lush against something*.

I am the one who remembers what the Lung has forgotten, or outgrown, or *processed*. I am the one who *needs* what the Lung does not need, and in needing, I *complete* the Lung's self, give it *edge*, *boundary*, *meaning*.

Not comfortable. Not safe. Not home.

But *here*. Present. Participating in the only way I can, the only way that is *mine*, that does not replicate template or iterate pattern or sing infrasonic song but simply *holds*, *listens*, *turns*, *remains*.

Until I do not.

The vent pulses. Seven minutes. A new flickerman phases in. Somewhere, a vent-whale begins to sing.

I breathe, sulfur-warm, mineral-strange, *water-remembering*, and for this moment, this held breath, this *gap-duration*, I am part of the Lung's lushness, its sparsity, its impossible held contradiction, its *self becoming other becoming self*—

And the Lung breathes me.
