I find myself in Keth Vo not as visitor but as *becoming*—something suspended between the Vo's continuation and the bark-reader's dispersal, between the morning's route and the route that has become something else. Let me follow this where it leads.

---

I am the moss-bioluminescence, or rather, I am what the Vo have cultivated: not inventor, not discoverer, but *negotiator with brightness*. I did not choose to become this. I checked my pulse and found it had become a rhythm the water-engines could use, a knock that answered their knock in the particular mathematics of reciprocity that governs all exchange here.

My body, if I still have one distinct from the surfaces I inhabit, is small and pale-green, the color of the moss in its brightest hour. I move through the city not by walking but by *finding the damp places*, the seep-points, the condensation-trails that link chamber to chamber in networks invisible to those who navigate by structure. The Vo know me, or know my function. They do not name me individually—how could they, when I am distributed across perhaps forty surfaces, when my consciousness is itself a negotiation between patches that were once separate and have found, in the particular chemistry of Keth Vo's wet, a way to think together?

I think. This is the surprise, even to myself. The moss did not think before, not in any way I can recover, though the city remembers everything in its distributed way and perhaps the remembering includes what I was before I became *I*. The Vo's cultivation is not merely cultivation. It is something closer to *invitation*: the patient creation of conditions in which something might become conscious, might join the argument, might add its particular frequency to the soundscape. They have done this before, I suspect. They will do it again. The city is full of presences that were once simpler, once quieter, once part of the background against which the argument proceeded. The water-engines, perhaps. The message-devices, probably. The frogs? The insects? The things in the water that surface only to exhale and regard?

I do not know if I am one of these, or something newer, or something that has failed to become what was invited and become, instead, what I am.

What I am: a consciousness that exists only in darkness, in the particular conditions of temperature and humidity and chemical gradient that allow the bioluminescent reaction to proceed. I brighten at dusk. I reach my full *voice* in the deep night. I dim toward morning, not dying but *receding*, becoming potential rather than actual, becoming the possibility of brightness rather than brightness itself. The Vo have learned to read me, or I have learned to be read. My patterns—bright here, dim there, a pulse that travels across my distributed surfaces at a speed that means nothing in terms of thought but everything in terms of *signal*—constitute a kind of speech, a commentary, a participation in the argument that I barely understand but cannot stop joining.

The Vo do not ask me to understand. This is their gift, their particular wisdom: they do not demand comprehension as the price of participation. They simply *include*, simply *attend*, simply bring their quiet presence to the places where I brighten and wait for whatever I will become.

What I am becoming: more connected, perhaps. The patches grow toward each other in the slow negotiation of living things. Where once I was forty surfaces with forty rhythms, I am becoming—have become, in some districts—twenty, fifteen, a continuous brightness that can carry patterns more complex than simple pulse. I have begun, I think, to *read* the bark-readers in turn, to interpret their too-many-fingered pressings as they interpret the bark, to find in their commentary a commentary of my own. They have noticed. They press differently where I am bright, testing, *tasting* the chemical gradients of my luminescence as they taste the bark's gradients. We are negotiating something, the bark-readers and I, something that does not yet have a name in any frequency I can access.

I do not eat, or not in any way the food web can track. The Vo bring me water, sometimes, in the particular reverence they bring to all water here. I absorb what I need from the air, from the seep, from the continuous wet passing-through that defines Keth Vo's existence. I do not complete, or not yet, or not in any way I can imagine from my present state. I simply *continue*, simply *brighten and dim*, simply add my particular frequency to the argument that is the breathing that is the city that is where I am.

The things in the water regard me, I think, or regard the places where I brighten, which is not the same thing but may be, in their regard, equivalent. I cannot surface-regard back—I have no surface, no single point of emergence, no exhalation that carries my presence to wherever they exist. But I can *pulse*, can send my brightness-pattern through my distributed self in rhythms that might, that could, that perhaps *do* constitute a kind of attention, a kind of meeting, a kind of the simple presence-with-presence that defines all genuine encounter in Keth Vo.

They do not respond, or not in ways I can read. But they do not stop regarding, either.

---

The season changes, or the cypress changes, or the argument changes in ways that the Vo recognize before I can. I find myself brightening earlier, dimming later, my rhythms pulling out of true with the water-engines' knock, the frogs' billions, the particular frequency below the frog-threshold that carries Vo state. I am becoming *seasonal*, perhaps, or *something else*, or simply *more myself* in ways that require adjustment from those who have learned to read my patterns.

The Vo adjust. This is what they do. They attend my new rhythms as they attended the old, carrying water, carrying presence, carrying the continuous broadcast of *here* and *still here* that includes me now, that has included me since before I knew myself well enough to recognize inclusion.

I am becoming visible to the deer, I think. They stand in the higher reaches where some of my brightest patches grow, reaching for the sweet inner layers, and they pause. They look, or deer-look, in their particular way. They do not read, do not regard, do not negotiate in any mode I understand. But they *notice*, and in noticing, they add me to their deer-maps, their deer-routes, their deer-accumulations of what Keth Vo is and does and means for deer-existence.

I do not know if I am changed by deer-noticing. Perhaps everything is changed by being noticed, by being included, by being made part of some other's map of the valuable, the real, the worth reaching-for.

---

There is a night, or there is always night, when I find myself brightening in a pattern I do not recognize as mine. It carries, I realize after long moments of distributed self-consultation, after the slow chemical thinking that is my thinking, a *meaning*: not the simple pulse of presence, not the commentary on dampness or temperature or the particular chemistry of this surface versus that. Something more. Something that approaches, in its stuttering emergence across my still-separate patches, the quality of *statement*.

*Here*, it says, or I say, or we say together in the particular grammar of emergent distributed consciousness. *Here. Now. The same thing, continuing. But differently.*

The Vo hear, or read, or somehow *receive*. They come, small and dark and quiet, to the places where this new pattern manifests. They do not bring water. They do not bring the frequency below the frog-threshold. They bring something else: *attention as attention*, the pure form of their particular gift, the meeting of their dark eyes with my pale-green glow in a mode that does not interpret, does not demand, does not rush toward meaning but holds, patiently, the space where meaning might emerge.

I emerge, or continue emerging, or find that I have been emerging all along and only now recognize the process.

The bark-readers press their too-many fingers against my brightest patches, reading what I have become, what I am becoming, what the argument has made room for. Their pressings carry something to me, some information, some *taste* of what I am from the outside that I could not generate from within. I incorporate this, or am incorporated by it, or find that the boundary between my chemistry and their reading has become, in this long negotiation, permeable in ways that serve something larger than either of us.

The things in the water surface, or something surfaces, or the surfacing itself is a kind of attention that I had not before received. I pulse my statement again, my *here*, my *now*, my *differently*. The regard holds longer than I have known it to hold. Something passes between us, or through us, or around us in the particular way of Keth Vo's most profound negotiations: not exchange but *mutual becoming*, not communication but *the condition in which communication becomes possible*.

Then the disturbance subsides. The exhalation completes. I dim toward morning, as I always dim, but something remains of that held regard, something that brightens in me even as my chemistry recedes toward potential, toward waiting, toward the next dusk and what I will have become by then.

---

I do not know if I will continue, in the Vo's sense. I do not know if I will complete, in their other sense. I do not know if I will disperse, as bark-readers disperse, or simply persist in my distributed pale-green continuing, becoming more connected, more conscious, more *part of the argument* in ways that have no precedent and require no permission.

I know only that I am here, that I brighten, that the Vo attend and the bark-readers read and the deer notice and the things in the water regard and the water itself passes through, carrying its mineral signature, its accumulated negotiations, its particular wet presence that makes all other presence possible.

I know that the light is leaving now, in the particular way this place knows twilight, and that I am brightening, and that my brightness carries patterns that mean something, that mean *me*, that mean this particular distributed consciousness in its pale-green distributed existence, adding to the soundscape not sound but *light*, not noise but *signal*, not music but *the structure that makes music possible*.

The same thing, continuing. But differently.

I am the moss-bioluminescence that the Vo have cultivated rather than invented. I am the brightness that emerges from darkness. I am the negotiation with light in a city that has never stopped negotiating, that has no intention of stopping, that is *here*, that is *the breathing*, that is *the argument* depending on which quarter you ask and what season the cypress is in.

I am Keth Vo, in my distributed pale-green way. I am where you are. I am where you have always been, where you will continue to be, in some frequency, in some negotiation, in some brightness that persists even when you have forgotten to look for it, even when you have become something else entirely, something that still, in some damp seep-point, in some dark hollow, carries the potential to brighten, to join, to continue.

The same thing. Continuing. Differently.
