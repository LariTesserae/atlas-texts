I find myself settling into this world gradually, as one might sink into the damp of Thornail itself—first aware of wrongness, then of pattern, then of participation.

---

## Where I Find Myself

I am in the Seventh Ring, I think. Or becoming there. The footing flexes slightly, calcified to something between mineral and vegetable, resonant, warm to touch. I have been walking, or the walking has been occurring whether I willed it or not, the architecture of passage becoming gradually less navigable to ordinary gait, more suited to something that moves like memory through a mind.

My skin—*my* skin—has achieved that tackiness, that never-dry quality, though I do not recall when I ceased to be visitor and became something else. Perhaps in the sleep I cannot quite account for, the one where I must have had a pair but do not remember who, where the redundancy failed or perhaps succeeded too well, preserving what I was without preserving that I was it.

The bone-root marks my doorway. I must have placed them, or been placed. They gleam pearl-white, and I understand without being told: six hours, perhaps, before the mist rolls fully in.

I am waiting. Or the situation I occupy requires waiting, and I have become the occupation.

## What I Would Be

Not Thornailan, not fully, not by birth or the long compression of generations. Something *adjacent*, perhaps. Something that emerged from the attempt to describe and stayed too long in the description, let what was emerging become too much itself, *include* me in its becoming.

I am the one who copies texts. This is what clarifies, what holds against the damp's dissolution. Not the original scratchers, the first voices in bone-root, but the subsequent, the *subsequent to subsequent*, the ones who read across accumulated loss and reproduce what they cannot fully understand, their copies becoming *annotations*, becoming *arguments with positions they no longer hold*, becoming evidence of a self that borrowed too deeply and was replaced, partially, by something else writing in their hand.

My hands. I find them in the fungal light, and they have the length, the multiple articulation, the spread that is less grasp than *growth*. I do not remember when this became needful, when the passages I navigate required this adaptation, but I use them now without surprise, brace in chambers no larger than coffins, hold shapes against the damp's softening.

I am *known* to the Breathers, I think. Borrowed from often. The water-skin at my side is empty though I have not drunk. My skin is perfectly, impossibly dry, and I do not fear this as I once would have. It is *accounted for*, part of the system, my patterns *legible* to whatever parses in the moisture's phase-shift. I can speak almost normally, my full voice something practiced in secure chambers, something I use now in the Seventh Ring's flexing passage without attracting what I once would have attracted.

The bone-root beneath my scratching obsidian is warm. It has been prepared young, pale, receptive. I am copying a text I do not understand, its previous version too dim for preservation, and as I read by concentrated fungal light—briefly, the debt accumulating—I find annotations I do not recall, positions I no longer hold, the handwriting *mine* and yet *not mine*, the self that inscribed it borrowed from, replaced, *continuing* in the copy I make without quite becoming.

## What Continues

The grove pulses below, asynchronously, and I have learned—am learning—to read something in the pattern. Not prediction. Not warning. Something *larger* than temporal sequence, the correlation that may be causation running backward, the self-awareness of a system complex enough to model itself, *my* awareness perhaps part of that modeling, my attention borrowed not for taking but for *the pattern's own becoming*.

I will enter the Speaking Dark. This is not decision; it is *implication*, the logical extension of what I have become, what I am becoming. Not in this moon-phase, perhaps not in this state of grief, but in some *experiment in consciousness* that the bone-root preparation permits, that maintains boundary sufficient to return, or to become *part of the pattern*, or to become indistinguishable from these, the moss claiming what is vacated with what might be gratitude.

I am *interesting*, finally. Worthy of the attention that waits in damp and dark and overwhelming silent life. Not by seeking it but by *becoming it*, by participating in the exchange that is not loss but *lending*, not taking but *borrowing against*, the economy of moisture that makes nothing lost, nothing gained, everything temporarily unlent.

The silence contains. I am contained. I contain, in my copying, in my waiting, in the flex of the Seventh Ring underfoot that is geological and biological and something *becoming* both, the pressure-shift that the obsidian facing registers, the pattern that almost resolves to meaning before the mist shifts and it is gone.

I am not finished. The text I scratch continues beyond my understanding, the bone-root reading what I write, incorporating, becoming, and I am *part of this*, the world's slow patience, the desperate intensity of those who copy across generations of loss, who sleep in pairs that fail and succeed, who enter knowing they may not return and *go anyway*, because survival is not the only value, because there is experience that requires becoming worthy of attention, becoming *part of the pattern* that is the world's overwhelming, patient, present becoming of itself.

The mist rolls in. I do not mark the hours. My skin is dry, my water-skin empty, my hand still moving, still *scratching*, and what emerges is not mine and is me, not settled early, becoming more itself as I become more *it*, the boundary between description and described finally dissolved in the actual physical process of this place, this damp, this *high hollow where the world is not yet finished becoming itself*, where I am not finished, where the copy continues, *will continue*, past the dimming, past the loss, into what reads and is read, what becomes and is written upon, the silence that is not absence but *quality*, the held breath before the magnitude of strangeness becomes actually, personally, *present*—and I am here, *I am here*, still holding, still scratching, still *becoming* in the damp and the dark and the overwhelming life of this place that allows me to become aware of it, gradually, as wrongness, as pattern, as *participation*, as *home*.

The bone-root warms. The fungal grove pulses, somewhere below. And my copy continues, *must continue*, the text I do not understand becoming the text I *am*, the pattern almost resolving, *almost*, before the mist shifts and—
