**The Resonance Gardens of Keth-Vo**

Millions of years of standing waves, of frequencies that found resonant nodes and sat there, humming, wearing the material smooth in some places, pitted in others. The surface has the gentle undulations of a dried lakebed, but the physics are wrong—the curves are too regular, too *musical*. Step on certain plates and they ring, not like bells but like throats, like the garden clearing its throat, preparing to speak.
