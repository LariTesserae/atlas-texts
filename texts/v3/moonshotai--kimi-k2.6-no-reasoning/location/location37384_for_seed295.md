**The Hollowing**

Overhead, the sky is not absent but *withheld*—a darkness so complete it has texture, like velvet pressed against the eyes, like breathing inside a held lung. No stars. No moon. No sun has ever struck this place directly, though warmth persists—earth-m warmth, metabolism-warmth, the accumulated exhalation of uncountable bodies breathing in darkness.

You are high, very high. The elevation is built, not natural. Everything here is built. The substrate, the stratum, the foundation upon which you sense your own weight standing—it is all construction, all artifact, though the tech parameter insists: *none*. No devices. No tools visible anywhere, none in operation. How does one build without technology? The answer emerges slowly, as your eyes strain to parse what little light exists: this place was grown.

Or rather, it grew itself, according to old intentions long dissolved into instinct. The builders—if they were builders—encoded their purposes into biology so thoroughly that the biology outlasted every memory of plan. The result is a total environment of made-things without makers, a constructed world that has forgotten construction.

Structures rise around you, and they are dense: packed, layered, proliferating into every volume. But these are not buildings as you understand them. They are *hollows*. The Hollowing, properly named. Each form is a cavity defined by its walls, and the walls themselves are living tissue—calcified, keratinized, lignified through centuries of slow metabolic process into materials that read as stone, as brick, as something like fired ceramic, but all bearing the telltale irregularity of growth, the slight curve of organic decision-making.

The scale opens past immediate comprehension. You sense regions, valleys between towering hollow forms, whole districts of the Hollowing extending into the dark. Town-sized. Valley-sized. A world of mid-range features repeating at mid-range distances, each hollow unique, each following unguessable rules of internal necessity.

And they are damp. Intermittently wet. Water collects where condensation finds purchase, drips from curved ceilings into curved basins, feeds thin films of moisture on surfaces angled just so. Rivers run—if you can call them rivers—through channels that seem designed for exactly their current flow, neither more nor less. The water tastes of the walls, mineral-bright, faintly saline, carrying the chemistry of long biological processing.

The vegetation: lush, they said, and it is, but lush in darkness means something other than green abundance. Life here has evolved past photosynthesis entirely, or never needed it. The plants—if you insist on the word—are pale, fibrous, sometimes faintly luminescent in spectrum you barely perceive. They grow on the hollows' exteriors, in the damp interstices, forming mats and draperies and hanging gardens that exist in states of perpetual slow growth, patient fungus-like expansion, spore-based reproduction. Some are motile, you realize after watching long enough. Slow vines that creep across surfaces over decades, seeking better condensation, better mineral seep. The density of life means they press against each other, layer upon layer, competing for the thin resources of darkness, creating canopies of pale growth that filter what little moving air exists.

But the fauna. Overwhelming. This is the parameter that redefines all others.

Sound first—deafening, yes, but what *kind* of deafening? Not mechanical noise. No technology produces it. The sound is biological, and it is *constant*, and it is *everywhere*. The hollow structures themselves amplify, resonate, turn the entire Hollowing into an instrument played by uncountable players.

Insectile things, mostly. Things that click and stridulate and rub their parts together in darkness, communicating across distances you cannot measure. The sound has depth, layers: a constant baseline of low-frequency thrumming from larger creatures somewhere in the lower hollows, a mid-range cacophony of chirps and ticks and wet pulsations, and piercing above it all the high frequencies of things small and numerous, so numerous, their individual calls blurring into a texture like static, like rain, like the sound of darkness itself being continuously torn and repaired.

You realize, standing in this warmth, this high dark damp density, that you are not hearing an ecosystem but a *body*. The Hollowing functions as organism. The fauna do not merely inhabit it; they *operate* it. Things that clean the water channels, things that maintain the gas exchange through porous wall-sections, things that prune the pale vegetation before it overgrows critical surfaces, things that—your mind resists this—*repair* the hollow structures themselves, secreting compounds that harden to replace worn areas, that patch cracks, that maintain the integrity of this total built environment across geological time.

Erosion is established here. Weathered, in-balance, in-its-own-state. The Hollowing is old. Not ancient in the human sense but *settled*,找到 its form, arrived at configurations that persist because they work, because the feedback loops between hollow structure and living inhabitant have reached equilibria that resist perturbation. Change happens—the dynamic is slow, patient, weak gradients—but change happens *within* the form, not to it. The Hollowing persists while generations of its fauna generations live and die and evolve to serve it better.

You begin to understand the built parameter differently. Nothing is natural substrate because *natural* is not a category that obtained here. There was no nature to build upon, or the builders (the first builders, the vanished intention) rejected it so completely that even memory of nature was erased. The world is made, but made *biologically*, made through processes that read as natural now only because they have operated so long without intervention. This is the paradox: total construction without technology, the total artifact that reads as total nature, neither category sufficient to contain what the Hollowing *is*.

The warmth comes from metabolism, from the accumulated heat of uncountable digestions, respirations, fermentations. The elevation is built up through centuries of deposition, growth, the slow upward reaching of hollow forms that perhaps once sought something, perhaps once had purpose that became structural habit, became form without function beyond self-maintenance.

You move through the darkness, and your own sounds join the deafening chorus—your footsteps on curved surfaces, your breathing, the catch in your throat. The fauna know you are here. They always know. They are overwhelming not in threat but in *attention*, in the weight of countless sensory organs orienting, processing, deciding you are not food, not predator, not relevant to their functions. You are weather in the Hollowing. You are a slow dynamic, a weak gradient, a thing that will pass or be incorporated.

The weirdness is normal. So normal it takes effort to recognize how strange this is, how every parameter has been pushed to its limit and held there, not in tension but in *accommodation*. The Hollowing accommodates everything. The darkness accommodates the lush pale growth. The deafening sound accommodates your hearing, finds frequencies it does not occupy so you can locate yourself. The overwhelming fauna make room, literally, physically, compressing, adjusting, flowing around your presence as water flows around stone.

You find a hollow that seems, in your limited perception, larger than others. Enter it. The walls curve to cup a space of relative quiet—not silence, never silence, but the sound becomes directional, focused, you can locate its sources now instead of receiving it as total immersion. The floor is damp, slightly yielding, a membrane over something harder. You rest. You realize you have been in the Hollowing for hours, or days—the darkness erases duration as it erases distance.

And in this rest, you understand the final parameter, the one that makes all others cohere: the Hollowing is *slow*. Its dynamic is gradual cycles across long durations. Patient processes. What you experience as overwhelming fauna, as deafening sound, as dense layered built environment—this is the Hollowing in a moment. Its *real* operation unfolds across timescales you cannot witness. The hollows are growing, slowly. The fauna are evolving, slowly. The pale vegetation is creeping, slowly, toward some configuration that will take millennia to achieve. The total construction is *still constructing itself*, has been constructing itself since before your species existed, will continue after every trace of your passage has been absorbed, processed, made irrelevant.

You are temporary architecture in the Hollowing. You are a brief hollow the place makes to contain your presence, your warmth, your damp breath, your small sounds, and when you pass, the hollow will close behind you, the fauna will adjust, the pale growth will creep over any mark you might have left, and the darkness will remain, complete, warm, deafening with life, patient beyond any measure you bring to it.

The water drips somewhere. You hear it now, distinct in the relative quiet of your chosen hollow. Drip. Drip. Each drop finding its basin, its channel, its place in the slow metabolism of the total made world. You understand that you will drink soon, that the water is safe because you are not relevant enough to poison, that you will eat something pale and fibrous because the Hollowing provides, not for you but through you, through the brief opportunity your digestion represents in its ongoing cycles.

Elevation: high, and built higher still by the slow accumulation of hollow upon hollow, intention upon vanished intention, biology upon biology until the technology of intention became indistinguishable from the nature of result.

You rest. You become, for this moment, part of the Hollowing's patience. The darkness holds you like the velvet it resembles. The warmth holds you. The sound surrounds you, deafens you, includes you. The overwhelming fauna overwhelm you into acquiescence, into presence without significance, into simply being here, now, in the total constructed darkness that needs no technology to maintain its total existence, its weathered established balance, its slow patient ongoing becoming what it already is.

Time passes. The Hollowing remains.
