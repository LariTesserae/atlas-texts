*The worldlet breathes.*

First: it is a lung, a cavity, a hollowed thing. You enter not downward but *through*—through the root-mass of something too large to be a single tree, though trees are what you have words for. The cavity is young, freshly torn or grown or *sung* into being. The walls weep.

Water here does not collect. It *passes through*, a continuous transcontinental breath: moisture drawn up through vascularings of xylem, condensed on the cool air of this buried lung, then drawn back down into other rootings, other reaches. The surfaces are tacky, humid, intermittently beaded with condensation that never quite drips*, never quite pools. You could wring the atmosphere with your hands. Your lungs feel it, this tidal respiration, this 0.9 of water that means *life support* not *presence*.

And the vegetation—*the vegetation*.

This is 2.9 out of 3, which is to say: almost pure. Almost total. The substrate is pith. The architecture is cambium, heartwood, secondary growth, wound response. You stand in a chamber whose floor is compressed wood-pulp still warm with metabolism, whose walls are vascular bundles thick as your thigh, whose ceiling is a diaphragm of living bark that filters light from above into rose-gold diffusion. The young erosion (1.0) means edges are sharp, fractures are clean, recent wound-sites ooze with defensive resin that hardens to lacquer-amber even as you watch. This place was made *now*, or yesterday, or is still making itself around you as you stand here waiting for it to finish.

The light: 2.3. Normal. But *normal for what?* It filters through that living ceiling, through networks of phloem arrays that conduct it somehow, that organize it, that *understand* it in ways no leaf evolved to do. The result is a warm (1.9) amber-gold that pools where it pools and leaves absolute black where it does not. There is no gradation. The light *decides*. You step into illumination, you step out of it. This is the reality breakdown (2.9) beginning its work: not violent, not dramatic, but *wrong at the root level*, wrong at the level of expectation you didn't know you carried about how photons should behave, about how spaces should *hold together*.

The density is 0.1. Empty. Vast space between presences. This is what betrays the scale (0.5), this pocket within a pocket, this hidden room in the heartwood of a world-tree or a dream of one. The chamber is perhaps thirty meters long, irregular, branching into smaller alveoli, connecting to others through valve-like structures that might be doors or might be the tree's own internal valves, sphincters, regulatory architectures. But in that thirty meters: almost nothing. Not empty as barren. Empty as *waiting*. Empty as held breath.

The sound is 2.2. Loud. But loud *how?*

This is the reality breakdown again, because the sound is the tree's own metabolism made audible: the creak of cellulose under turgor pressure, the rush of sap in vessels too large, the *crackling* of wound-response growth, resin polymerization like small fires, the snap of fresh xylem vessels reaching, searching. All amplified. All *here*. The sound is structural, architectural, the noise of a building that is also a body building itself around you. And beneath it, or within it, something that might be voice. Might be wind in a hollow that should not have wind. Might be the weirdness making itself known through available channels.

Dynamic: 2.3. Active. The chamber *moves*. Diurnal pulses in turgor change the shape subtly, widdershins, the floor rising and falling by centimeters through the day. Wound sites seal themselves with new growth. Resin rivers flow, reach their endpoints, harden. The tree—you cannot help but call it that now, having exhausted other words—the tree is *alive*, is *acting*, is ecosystem and organism and process without the stillness that lets you observe.

Fauna: 0.6. Rare. But *present*. Things live in the interstices of this metabolism. Exobladder mites that graze on condensation, floating in the humid air like dust made conscious. Something larger—tunnel-dwellers, root-hollowers—whose passing leaves traces in the fresh pulp, whose droppings feed the localized DECOMPOSITION PICTURES NOT FOUND in patterns that the tree incorporates, responds to, grows around. Predator-prey exists here but it is *slow*, it is *chemical*, it is the tree's own immune response against invading fungi versus the fungi's evasive morphogenesis, played out in hours where elsewhere would be days, in weeks where elsewhere would be centuries.

Built: 0.5. None. But *tech* is 1.2—primitive. Here is the contradiction that the weirdness resolves. There are *tools* here, *artifacts*, but they are grown, not made. There are *fabrications* but they are of cambium and callus, of wound wood and traumatic resin. Benches of reaction wood. Cutting edges of silica deposition in cell walls. A technology of DNA, of epigenetic response, of the tree's million-year experimentation with materiality made *sudden* and *local* and *intentional* by the reality breakdown's influence. Someone—or something, or the tree dreaming itself with what passes for mind in the weirdness's wake—has been at *work* here. The work is visible. The work is *recent*. Fresh sharp edges. Young erosion. New-made in a timeline that has no business containing making.

The rules of this world:

Gravity *mostly* holds. Light *mostly* travels straight. But the mostly is doing heavy labor. The tree extends, somewhere, into spaces where these are negotiable, and this chamber is where the negotiation shows. Time moves *mostly* forward but the sphincter-valves between chambers open sometimes onto the same chamber *earlier*, the wound-site still fresh, the resin still flowing, and you can step through if you move fast and don't look at the angles wrong. The tree's growth is *mostly* phototropic but *here* it grows toward something else, toward warmth or sound or meaning, toward the loud 2.2 of its own metabolism made conscious of itself.

And the emptiness. The 0.1 density in a 0.5 scale. This is the most alien thing. This chamber was *made for something*. The grown tools, the fresh wounds, the quick-responding reality-breakdown architecture: all *waiting*. For a volume to fill. For a presence to arrive. For the tree's experiment in fabrication to reach its intended conclusion, or for that conclusion to have been lost in the weirdness's translation from wherever, *whenever*, this architecture was first dreamed.

You are, perhaps, the first to find it. You will not be the last. The tree grows, the valves shift, the light-decisions pool and retreat, and somewhere in the sound of metabolism made loud, something is *listening back* now, something has noticed the emptiness noticing itself, and the young sharp edges are beginning, just beginning, to smooth with the first touch of purpose they have ever known.
