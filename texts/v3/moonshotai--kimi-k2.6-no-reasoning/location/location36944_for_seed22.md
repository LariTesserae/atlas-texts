The sound arrives before anything else—a low-frequency thrum that lives in the sternum, that makes the fillings in your teeth ache with sympathetic vibration. You crest a low rise of fresh basalt, still warm underfoot, and look down upon the Veld.

It is a city, technically. Or was. Or is becoming something else.

The Veld occupies a sunken bowl of land, a recent geological infant: perhaps ten thousand years since some impossible force pressed a thumb into the crust and held it, creating this depression two hundred kilometers across, sharp-rimmed, the valley floor still resonating with its own creation. The erosion parameter here reads "young" because the world itself forgets slowly—the rock remembers being other than valley-floor. It hums.

---

**The Sound**

There are no open spaces in the Veld, only spaces between sounds. The deafening quality does not come from machinery, though machinery exists. It comes from growth. The vegetation—lush to the point of obscenity, rating 2.3—has evolved, or been engineered, or dreamed itself into acoustic organisms. Towering **resonance-trees** line the empty avenues, their trunks hollow, their branch-arrays tuned like pipe organs. Wind, ever-present in this active climate, activates them in sequences. But more: their root networks connect across kilometers, and something passes through them—chemical, electrical, something the technology parameter (2.9, beyond-modern) hints at without explaining. The trees *sing* to each other. Low drones, seismic rumbles, occasional shattering harmonic peaks when conditions align. The fauna, abundant and strange, have adapted to communicate in the sonic margins: click-language, subvocal rumble, bioluminescent flash-code for when the noise crests beyond hearing's utility.

---

**The City-That-Breathes**

Built parameter 1.5: urban, settlement-scale, layered. But density 0.1: empty, hollow, vast space between presences.

The Veld resolves this contradiction by threading its construction through the growth rather than clearing it. Buildings of **grown ceramic**, cream-white and faintly luminous in the dim light, rise from root-masses like mushrooms from mycelium. They are hollow forms, mostly—dormitories for a population that never arrived, or arrived and dispersed, or exists in a manner that doesn't require continuous occupation. The architecture learns. Left alone, a structure will over seasons adjust its own proportions, opening new chambers where moisture gathers, sealing others that go unused. The city is a provisional agreement between human intention and vegetable deliberation.

The streets—when you can find them beneath canopies of dripping leaf—are paved with **memory-stone**, something the tech parameter allows: surfaces that record pressure patterns and replay them as ghost-tactile sensations. Walk barefoot (and many do, the temperature warm enough to permit it) and feel the footsteps of yesterday's travelers, last year's, a decade past when the city was more populated. The stone does not discriminate. The weight of a bird lands, lingers, releases. The passage of rain.

---

**Water, Intermittent**

0.6 water: damp, intermittent, pockets in larger dryness.

The Veld does not rain so much as *remember* rain. Every forty or so hours, the resonance-trees reach a harmonic threshold and the air itself precipitates—not from cloud, but from condensation so sudden and collective it functions as localized weather. The downpour lasts minutes. Water pools in the memory-stone's slight imperfections, runs in temporary rivers down the bowl's curve, finds the **heartruts**—erosion-young channels not yet deepened by repetition, still sharp-edged, still surprised by their own existence. The water carries nutrients, messages, eggs of the stranger fauna. Then it sinks, or evaporates in the warm dim air, or is drunk by roots that resume their singing now lubricated, now harmonically enriched by the mineral content of fresh flow.

Between these events: humidity, clinging, the breathing of leaves, the slow transpiration of the city itself.

---

**The Fauna: Abundant, Strange, Communicative**

They do not behave as animals should. The **hollow-deer** move in perfect synchrony, their antlers resonant chambers that amplify and redirect the tree-song. They have no predators because nothing here predates in the conventional sense; instead, **exchange-cycles** govern energy transfer. A hollow-deer approaches a resonance-tree, offers something from its gut—processed mineral, bacterial culture, memory-stone dust—and receives in return a modulation of the local sound-field that its nervous system interprets as sustenance, as pleasure, as the completion of a metabolic loop not fully describable in terms nutritionists would recognize.

The **gap-birds** exploit the 0.1 density, the vast spaces between presences. They are small, fast, and they *slip*, somehow, between the wavelengths of deafening sound, riding pressure nodes like surfers. Their calls exist in negative sonic space, audible only in the instant between tree-drones, brief as thought. They nest in the empty buildings, the grown ceramics, their droppings slowly staining the memory-stone with biochemical patterns the stone, in turn, slowly learns.

And there are things in the root-networks. Things that may be fauna, may be the city's distributed processing, may be emergent from the 2.9 technology parameter's generous ambiguity. Call them **continuous-worms**: kilometers in length, centimeters in diameter, moving through soil and structure at the pace of a calendar year. They are thinking, if thinking is the propagation of differentiated electrical potential through extended substrate. They are the Veld's memory, perhaps, or its dream, or its immune response to intrusion.

**Light: Dim, Warm, Sufficient**

0.7 light. The sun—if this world has something so simply named—reaches the valley floor through filter of canopy, through atmospheric particulate the resonance-trees exude, through some property of the bowl's geometry that bends and attenuates. What arrives is amber, rose, the color of lamplight through old wine. The grown-ceramic buildings respond: their faint luminescence intensifies in inverse proportion to available daylight, so that the city glows at dusk, at midnight, at noon under densest cloud. The light is warm; the temperature holds steady, the valley's geology still bleeding its formation-heat through geothermal seeps, the vegetation trapping and circulating.

There are places—the heartruts, some building atriums where memory-stone failed to grow, the rare clearings where a resonance-tree fell and its neighbors have not yet filled the gap—where light pools more directly. These are dangerous or sacred, threshold spaces where the deafening sound attenuates to merely loud, where the fauna behave with uncertainty, where a traveler might hear their own heartbeat for the first time in days.

---

**Weirdness: The Rules That Emerge**

The Veld does not violate physics so much as negotiate with it at a level most realities never access. The technology—beyond-modern, nanoscale-or-cosmic—has become environmental, infrastructural, *boring*. It is the water cycle. It is the sound. It is the way a building adjusts its doorways to accommodate the deer.

But the true strangeness is temporal. The young erosion, the sharp features, the rock's memory of being other-than-valley: this extends to organism, to structure, to the city itself. The Veld is *still forming*. Not growing—forming. Differentiating. A process interrupted, or deliberately elongated, or proceeding at a pace where human observation captures only still-frames of a motion too slow to track.

A building you enter today may not have existed in that configuration yesterday. Not *changed*—reorganized, as if the city is searching through possible architectures, testing, abandoning, returning with variation. The memory-stone preserves all versions, or tries to; walk barefoot there and feel the pressure-ghosts of walls no longer present, floors that tilted differently, a population density the 0.1 parameter suggests was once, might someday again be, higher.

---

**The Occupants, Sparse**

Who lives here? The density parameter insists: almost no one. Town-or-city infrastructure, but town-or-city emptied, or never-filled, or filling on a timescale beyond survey.

There are **listeners**. Monastic, obsessive, they move through the Veld with practices of attention developed over generations—or over a single extended lifetime, time being another negotiated parameter. They map the sound. They attempt to predict the precipitation-events. They harvest gap-bird calls, recording media unknown, hoping to find pattern, meaning, the voice of the continuous-worms surfacing into audible range.

There are **traders**. Passers-through, drawn by rumor of the technology, the grown-ceramic, the memory-stone itself as commodity. They arrive at the bowl's rim, descend into the deafening thrummm, and most leave quickly, unable to sleep, unable to think in the sonic saturation. A few adapt. A fewer still find the exchange-cycles and participate, offering something the Veld recognizes as value—labor, attention, certain minerals, certain *silences* carefully preserved and presented.

And there are those who *became*. One morning a trader wakes and her footsteps no longer register on memory-stone. She moves through buildings that adjust around her presence with unusual speed, unusual precision. She no longer needs to eat, exactly, though she participates in exchange-cycles when moved to. She is still human, mostly, still conscious, but her consciousness extends slightly into the root-network, her dreams infiltrated by continuous-worm computation, her heartbeat synchronized to some sub-harmonic of the tree-song. The Veld does not have many of these. The Veld does not need many. They are its interface, perhaps, its way of understanding itself through minds that still remember being singular, bounded, *before*.

---

**A Specific Moment, Witnessed**

You stand at the bowl's rim—the low elevation making your vantage modest, you are barely above the valley floor, yet the slope behind you rises, the world outside the Veld exists at other elevations, other parameters, other rules.

The sound crests. All resonance-trees, across the 200-kilometer span, achieve momentary phase-lock. The deafening becomes *absolute*, not louder but more *coherent*, a single note sustained for seven seconds that are also, subjectively, seven hours, or seven heartbeats, or seven instances of a choice you are making and unmaking.

In that coherence, the dim light shifts. The grown-ceramic flares, not brighter but more *specific*, each building defining its edges with sudden clarity against the lush dark mass of vegetation.

Something moves in the heartrut below. A continuous-worm's thought surfacing: the valley-folds in a particular pattern, the water's next path, a building's next configuration. The gap-birds freeze mid-sip, suspended in pressure-nodes.

Then: release. Phase-lock breaks. Sound returns to its usual overwhelming complexity, the multiple processes of the active dynamic parameter resuming their asynchronous weave. Light dims to amber habit. The city exhales, adjusts, continues its slow formation.

You have not entered. You are still at the rim. The density parameter suggests you might walk for days through the Veld and encounter only the evidence of presence—adjusted building, fresh heartrut, resonance-tree tuned to new harmonic—without meeting another mind in direct address. The abundance of fauna, the lush vegetation, the deafening song of it all, makes the emptiness more pointed, more strange.

The Veld is a city. The Veld is a garden. The Veld is a single organism learning itself, or a technology so beyond-modern it has become indistinguishable from world, or a geological infant's dream of what habitation might mean, given time, given listeners, given someone to walk barefoot and feel the ghost of what was, what will be, what is still, always, forming.

You step forward. The memory-stone at the rim's edge registers your weight. It will remember, when you are elsewhere, when you are else-when, the pressure-pattern of a human foot at a particular moment of phase-lock, a particular instant of the Veld's long becoming.

The thrum resumes its sternum-residence. The humidity clings. Somewhere, kilometers distant in the dim, a resonance-tree opens new growth pores and begins, slowly, to tune.
