# The Sinking Manufactories of Keth-Vorath

## I. First Descent

You do not arrive at Keth-Vorath so much as surrender to it. The water here is not a surface you cross but a condition you enter, warm as blood, thick with dissolved minerals that make your skin prickle with strange conductivity. The liquid begins somewhere in what might once have been sky—a permanent atmospheric ocean held in suspension by pressures that should not exist, that *do* not exist in any geography that obeys ordinary physics. Rain falls upward as often as down. Horizontal sheets of water migrate like herds of translucent beasts, colliding, merging, shearing apart with sounds like tearing silk. You are always already inside it. Breathing requires apparatus that the place itself seems to resent, membranes that Keth-Vorath's waters slowly digest, filters that clog with living silt.

The elevation is low not in the sense of depth below some reference sea level but in the sense of *pressure downward*, of being pressed into submission by the weight of all this suspended ocean. What passes for ground is young, achingly young, geological infancy measured in months or weeks rather than eons. The seafloor—or floor-of-whatever-this-is—still steams with formation heat. Basalt pillows extrude fresh from vents that shouldn't vent here, that vent *wrong*, producing not the chemistry of any known magma but substances that cool into geometries that make eyes water, that phase between solid and liquid in rhythms you can feel in your teeth.

## II. The Forest of Conversion

Vegetation is lush beyond any temperate meaning of the word. This is not green growth in any Earthly sense but *conversion biomass*, the living machinery of Keth-Vorath's peculiar metabolism. Great columns rise from the young rock, structures that might be trees or might be chimneys or might be something that occupies the uncanny valley between organic and industrial. They are dense, impossibly dense, packed so tightly that moving between them requires squeezing through gaps that seem to adjust to your presence—widening slightly, then contracting behind you with what feels like intention.

Their surfaces run with warm water, not rain but *processed* liquid, effluent of some photosynthetic or chemosynthetic or *something*-synthetic metabolism that harvests energy from the reality breakdown itself. The bark, if it is bark, has the texture of hammered copper scaled with verdigris, but when you touch it, it yields like flesh, pulses like a heart, and the pulse synchronizes with something in your own chest before you can pull away. Leaves, or leaf-analogues, or collection surfaces, hang in layers that filter the dim light into spectra that don't correspond to any known color system. You see, or think you see, in wavelengths that your visual cortex was not evolved to process, and the processing itself becomes part of the seeing, a feedback loop that generates perceptions without objects to perceive.

The density is not merely spatial but *temporal*. These growths exist in layers of time as well as space, younger versions of themselves growing through or around or *inside* their own futures, older versions senescing into nutrients that their own pasts will consume. You can find rings in the pseudo-wood that record growth cycles that haven't happened yet, that *will not* happen in any linear chronology, but that leave their marks nonetheless.

## III. The Scattered Engines

Built structures punctuate this dense living-industrial forest, but scattered, so scattered that finding one feels like accident or conspiracy. They are single buildings, or partial buildings, or buildings that have become something else through processes of occupation and conversion. A refinery, perhaps, or something that was shaped like a refinery once, now grown through with conversion biomass so that the distinction between constructed and grown has become meaningless, has become *wrong* in ways that persist in your memory like afterimages.

The technology here is industrial, but industrial in a mode that suggests production without product, manufacture without market, machinery that runs because running is what it does, what it *is*. Engines the size of houses, or the size of rooms, or the size that shifts when you try to measure it, operate at pressures and temperatures that should require containment they do not possess. They are not sealed. The warm water flows through them as working fluid, as coolant, as *participant*. Gears turn without visible power transmission. Pistons stroke in chambers filled with liquid that behaves sometimes as lubricant, sometimes as fuel, sometimes as the thing being processed into something else that the machinery itself does not know and does not need to know.

Electrification is present but wrong, currents that flow without complete circuits, potentials that exist between points that should be at equilibrium, that *are* at equilibrium in any measurement you can make but that nonetheless drive work, that arc and spark in colors that don't exist, that produce sounds in frequencies that merge with the deafening ambient to create beats and harmonics that you feel in your bones, in your thoughts, in your sense of what is real and what is merely perceived.

## IV. The Reality Breakdown

Weirdness here is not anomalous but *structural*, the fundamental condition from which everything else derives. The reality breakdown manifests not as violation of physical law but as *competition* between physical laws, multiple sets of rules operating in interference patterns that create zones of stability and instability in geometries that shift and migrate.

In some regions, gravity operates normally, or normally enough, a consistent downward that orients you briefly before you learn not to trust it. In others, it vectors toward the nearest conversion column, or toward the center of mass of some distant structure you cannot see, or toward *you*, personal gravity wells that you carry with you and that other things fall into, that you fall into yourself in recursive loops that end only when you leave the zone or when the zone leaves you, migrating elsewhere in the turbulent dynamic of the whole.

Time runs at different rates in different densities of vegetation, in different proximities to different engines, in different phases of the water's complex circulation. You can age hours while walking meters, or experience days in moments of intense perception, or find that your own timeline has forked, that there are two of you now, or were, or will be, the tenses themselves becoming unstable, the grammar of experience breaking down into something more primitive, more *immediate*, that does not distinguish between memory and anticipation, between what has been and what might be and what, in the breakdown's logic, *is* in some adjacent possibility that leaks into this one.

The water itself is the primary medium of this breakdown, its high parameter value not merely descriptive but *causal*. Water remembers, here, or something in it remembers, or the remembering is distributed through the whole system, the whole place as memory of something else, something that Keth-Vorath is becoming or unbecoming or *processing* into existence. The liquid carries information, or is information, or becomes information when it contacts the conversion biomass, when it passes through the engines, when it falls upward or sideways or in spirals that have no name in any geometry.

## V. The Deafening Silence

Sound at 2.7 is deafening, but deafening in a way that includes silence as its opposite face, that makes silence loud, that *is* the loudness of silence amplified beyond bearing. The engines produce their anonymous industrial drone, the water produces its rushing and falling and colliding, the conversion biomass produces something like growth-sound, the creaking and cracking of expansion, but all of these merge into a totality that exceeds the sum, that becomes a pressure on the ears and on the mind that is not merely acoustic but *existential*.

In this deafening, you cannot hear yourself think, or you hear yourself thinking too clearly, the internal monologue externalized, made audible, made *public* in ways that violate privacy you didn't know you had. Your heartbeat becomes percussion in the greater symphony. Your breathing becomes wind instrument. The sound of your own nervous system, normally subliminal, rises to audibility, the crackle of synapses like distant static, like the engines, like the water, like the voice of the place itself speaking through your own physiology.

And in the peaks of this deafening, in the moments when it intensifies beyond previous maxima, there come instants of absolute silence, not the absence of sound but sound's *inversion*, its reciprocal, moments when the pressure releases into something that hearing cannot process, that the brain interprets as silence because it has no other category, but that is not silent, that is *more than sound*, sound's transcendence or completion or annihilation.

## VI. The Turbulent Dynamic

Everything moves. The dynamic parameter demands it, enforces it, *is* the turbulence that defines every scale of experience. The water circulates in patterns that meteorologists would call storm systems but that here are permanent, or semi-permanent, or permanent in their impermanence, stable attractors in the phase space of Keth-Vorath's fluid dynamics. Warm columns rise, cool sheets descend, but the warm and cool are relative only to each other, both warm in any absolute terms, both carrying heat from the young formation, from the engines, from the metabolic processes of the conversion biomass, from the reality breakdown itself, which generates energy, or consumes it, or transforms it in ways that thermodynamics cannot track.

The conversion biomass grows and is grown through, engines operate and are operated upon by the vegetation that surrounds and penetrates them, structures scatter and are scattered by processes of emergence and submergence, the young rock itself shifts, new formations extruding, old ones dissolving or being dissolved, the whole place in constant becoming that never arrives at being, that *is* its becoming, that defines itself by its refusal to stabilize.

This is not chaos in the colloquial sense, not mere disorder, but *hyperorder*, complexity that exceeds comprehension, that operates on principles that can be felt, intuited, participated in, but never fully known. You can navigate Keth-Vorath, after a fashion, by attending to patterns, by recognizing attractors and repellers, by moving with the turbulence rather than against it, but the navigation is itself part of the turbulence, your presence, your perception, your *trying* feeding back into the system that you are trying to understand.

## VII. The Absence

Fauna: 0.1. No animal life. This absence is not emptiness but *presence of absence*, a condition that defines everything else. There are no fish in the water, no insects in the vegetation, no birds in the deafening air, no mammals, no reptiles, no creatures of any kingdom that Earth biology would recognize as animal. The conversion biomass fills ecological niches that animals might occupy, the engines fill behavioral niches, the reality breakdown itself fills whatever niche consciousness might require, but nothing *moves through* the place in the way animals move, with the autonomy, the intentionality, the *otherness* of creaturely life.

You are the only animal here, if you have come, if you are reading this, if you are imagining it. And your animality is under constant assault, constant conversion, the place working on you to make you something else, something that fits, something that *belongs* in the way that nothing currently belongs, that nothing can belong without being changed beyond recognition. The water processes you, the vegetation processes you, the engines would process you if you entered them, the reality breakdown processes you most of all, your perception, your memory, your sense of what you are and what you are not, what is possible and what is not.

This absence is also, perhaps, a promise, or a threat, or simply a fact: that animal life, that you, are temporary conditions in a place that is learning, or has learned, or is *being* in ways that do not require the animal, that may not require anything you can recognize as life at all, that operates in registers of existence that the categories of biology, of physics, of any stable science, cannot capture, can only approximate, can only fail to fully describe.

## VIII. The Dimness

Light at 0.9 is dim, but not dark, not the absence of illumination but its *concentration*, its localization, its refusal to diffuse. Sources exist—bioluminescence in the conversion biomass, electrical discharge in the engines, thermal radiation from the young rock, something else from the reality breakdown that is not light in any standard sense but that the visual system processes as light, that *becomes* light in the interaction between place and perceiver—but these sources do not illuminate so much as *indicate*, point to themselves and their immediate surroundings, leave the greater volume in relative or absolute dimness.

Visibility extends only so far, and that distance shifts, contracts in the densest vegetation, expands in clearerings that open and close with the turbulence, that are themselves turbulent features of the dynamic whole. You see in patches, in moments, in the intervals between the deafening sound-peaks when something like visual clarity becomes possible, or in the sound-peaks themselves when the auditory overload produces some cortical release, some cross-modal activation that makes you *see* sounds, that translates the pressure waves into phantom images, into the visual hallucinations that the dimness makes plausible, that the reality breakdown makes *real*, or real enough, real in ways that persist, that leave traces, that change what seeing means for you, what light means, what the visible world consists of.

## IX. The Manufactories Remember

This is Keth-Vorath: not a place that was built or that grew but a place that *manufactures itself*, that is its own product and its own process, industrial without industry, organic without organism, real without stable reality, present without presence, dense with everything except what would make it habitable, what would make it *home* to anything that was not already being converted into something else, something that participates in the manufacture, that becomes part of the machinery, the biomass, the breakdown, the water, the sound, the turbulence, the dim and shifting light.

You will not survive here. Or you will survive as something else. Or you are already something else, reading this from inside the conversion, the process, the manufacture that continues, that has always continued, that will continue in forms that cannot be predicted, that emerge from the turbulence as temporary stabilizations, as eddies in the flow, as moments of apparent structure in the ongoing production of and from and through the reality that breaks down and builds up and breaks down again, the Keth-Vorath that is not a place but a *verb*, a doing, a making and unmaking that includes you now, that has included you since you began to read, since you began to imagine, since the water first touched your skin, warm and thick with minerals, with memory, with the everything and nothing that this place is, that it makes, that it makes of you, of all who enter, of all who are already inside, who have always been inside, who will never, in any stable sense, leave.
