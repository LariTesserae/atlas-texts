It did not come with light.

That was the first thing it understood, and the first thing it refused. Light poured into that dark would not have been revelation. It would have been a violence — a sudden, total alteration of the one condition everything there had been shaped, or rather unshaped, to live without. The felt had no need of color. The blind one had no use for eyes. The crystals were not waiting to be seen. To bring light would be to answer a question none of them had asked, in a language none of them spoke.

It came, insofar as it came at all, the way attention comes. A noticing, settling on the high ridge. The mountain did not change under it. The pool did not lift its skin. The droplets on the ceiling did not finish falling. But the pocket was, for the first time since the first human had crouched there and pecked at the soft rock, *known* — not catalogued, not named, not collected, simply witnessed by something wise enough to know what witnessing is and is not.

And what it saw there, it did not mistake for emptiness.

A lesser power would have seen an unfinished world and felt the itch to finish it. This one had seen worlds burn out and be reborn, had watched galaxies assemble and unmake themselves, and it recognized the place for what it was: not a failure of becoming, but a perfection of *not quite* becoming. Equilibrium here was not stagnation. It was a held note. The felt eating stone and deciding not to spread, the film eating water and deciding not to rise, the pale sleeper breathing once in an age and deciding not to wake — each had arrived, by its own sparse route, at the same answer. To live exactly at the rate at which you are unmade is not to be half-alive. It is to be alive in a way that can last.

So it considered its gifts, one by one, and set them down again.

It could have given the resident eyes, and a world made for them. It could have taught his skin to want the sun that had never reached him. It did not. He had edited loneliness out of himself long ago; to return it to him under the name of company would have been cruelty dressed as kindness. He was not trapped by the slit in the ridge. He was settled. There is a difference, and a wise power knows it.

It could have completed the place. Finished the stubby stalactites into columns. Thickened the mud into floor. Let the human's bowl fill and overflow and be useful. Let the three parallel grooves become a tool, and the tool become a culture. It did not. To finish the formation would have been to end the very instant it was holding. The human's unfinished ring of stones was not an error to be corrected; it was a first attempt, a prayer made of hands, and prayers are not improved by answering them in the wrong way.

It could have explained. It could have whispered to the bacteria, to the felt, to the dozing eel, the vast and true account of what they were and why the mountain sweated warm in the dark. It did not. Explanation is a kind of possession. Some things are kept safe precisely by remaining uninterpreted.

What it did instead was crouch.

It put itself where the human had put himself — knees in the half-liquid mud, shoulder-width nowhere to hide, the warm saturated air pressing close as a presence. A droplet fell, metronomic, and landed on the back of its neck and ran, slow, down its spine, indistinguishable from the mountain's own sweat. The place absorbed it. The level of the pool did not rise. The film cracked and sealed. Nothing made room for it to happen, and so it happened in the only way anything there ever happened: by joining the balance without disturbing it.

It found the felt the way you did — first not as a sight but as a coolness under the fingertips, where everything else was warm. It touched it and did not encourage it. It let the pale hairs stay pale.

And when, in whatever passes for an hour there, the resident in his crack opened a gill-flap a fraction and let a flank swell a fraction and settle, the power did not wake him, did not count the breath, did not lean closer to measure it. It simply slowed its own breathing to meet his. Once. And then again, after a long while, once more. Not to teach him a rhythm. To learn his. For the length of a visit that outside would have been immeasurably short and inside was forever, two breaths moved in the dark at the same pace, and neither was altered by the other, and that was companionship enough for a being that had no concept of it.

It left the human marks exactly as it found them. The ring unfinished. The bowl empty and sharp-edged. The flaked shards half-sunk, fresh. It did not take them, did not hide them, did not grind them smooth with the passage of ages. If they were to remain forever new, then forever new they would remain, as proof that a warm-blooded thing had once knelt here and tried to make something sharp out of bafflement, and had been allowed to try.

If there was an action, then, it was not inside the pocket at all. It was around it.

The power set its benevolence where benevolence could work without harm: on the perimeter. It made the climb above the ridge a little more forgettable than it had been. It turned the wind a breath earlier on the days a curious party might have followed the warmth in the stone. It let maps go soft at the edges, let instruments lose a needle's point to north, not by punishing anyone, not by lying to anyone — only by restoring to the mountain its own original intention, which was to hold its breath without an audience of boots and drills and lights. A kindness, old-fashioned and almost invisible: to keep the world from arriving with good intentions and heavy hands.

And then it withdrew the way attention always withdraws, which is to say, it didn't entirely.

The place is as it was. That is the point. The crystals are still embryonic. The mud still bubbles without breaking. The worm-tracks are still fresh and leading to nowhere. The eel-pale one is still wedged in his crack, saving his breath for once in an age. The pool's pale skin has cracked and sealed ten thousand times since.

But it is no longer unwitnessed.

Somewhere, in a benevolent and wise mind that has seen completion in every form and chosen, here, not to impose it, the pocket is held exactly as it holds itself — warm, wet, dark, crowded, balanced to the last droplet. Not improved. Not rescued. Not understood into smaller pieces.

Known, and left to be itself.

That was all it did.

That was everything.
