It did not arrive.

That must be said first, because arrival is the local form of harm. To arrive is to put a foot down, to displace a dozen presences, to open a crack that then has to heal around you. A benevolent and wise power, if it were less wise, would have arrived long ago with the usual equipment of benevolence: a horizon to orient by, a clearer light, a word, a rescue. It would have dried a corner to make it stayable. It would have planted the green the plain so conspicuously lacks. It would have given the inhabitants eyes, so they could see where they were going, and a voice, so they could say when they were hurt.

It did none of that. Not because it saw nothing. Because it saw, at last, what was actually there.

## The Attention

It was drawn to the plain not by a signal. The Betweeners make no sound on purpose. They raise no light, leave no road, keep no home that persists beyond the next trickle. A power looking for something to help would normally pass over such a place as empty: packed gravel, perpetual wet, a hiss with no speaker. That apparent emptiness is what kept it safe for so long, and what finally caught the power's attention — an entire living surface that had learned to live without ever staying still long enough to be found.

The power did not find them by looking for a horizon. It found them by learning to look at the surface, pebble, water, pebble, water, and to dim itself until it could be mistaken for weather. Its first act of benevolence was self-diminution. Power is a kind of light, and light here is already failing to arrive on purpose. A brighter light from above would clarify edges, and clarified edges would dry, and dried edges would give roots purchase, and purchase would give staying, and staying is the one thing this place cannot survive.

So it learned the local scale of attention. Not a gaze from above. A pore. An upturned face exposed to air, an underside pressed to silt, opening a fraction to take in a bead, opening again to let mist out. It inhaled condensation. It exhaled evaporation. And in that small, repeated exchange, in that film between stone and air, it began to understand them — not as a species to be improved, not as a world to be saved, but as a way of continuing that had already solved, in its own terms, the problem of being alive in a place that dissolves everything.

## What It Withholds

It withholds a horizon.

A horizon would be mercy to a walker. To a Between-er it would be an ending. Give them a line where land stops, a far place to orient toward, and the trickles would straighten toward it. Colonies would clump at the edge. The plain would become a shore, and then a land, and then a place where things stay — and the whole turbulent stability of the plain, stable as a waterfall is stable, would trade itself for a direction. The power does not give them north. North will not hold still under their feet because they orient by the trickle running this second, and the trickle will not hold still either. That is not a defect to be corrected. It is how they escape being fixed in a pattern long enough to be preyed upon by it — even by themselves.

It withholds eyes and words.

They have no eyes because eyes fix distance, and distance misbehaves here. They see, after a fashion, from below, in the brighter underside of the film in the nursery pits, where light reflected off the ground does the work the sky will not. To give them eyes looking outward would be to teach them to seek a sky that sweats back. It withholds speech because speech does not survive here — it is taken apart by air and water and returned thinner, from the wrong direction. A voice would make them call, and waiting for an answer would make them stay near the place they called from. They have never called. The power will not teach them how.

It withholds rescue from the plain itself.

When a channel deepens and a colony unfolds into its eel-pale swimming phase and feeds on films and on younger Betweeners, the power does not intervene. When a slab settles and the too-many-legs let go all at once, when a film thins until a swimmer folds back into it stranded, when a gravel bar slumps sideways in a sigh and dissolves into slurry — the power does not intervene. Hunger here is memory. Dissolution here is reproduction. A crack pinching a colony in two is how they make more of themselves, each half remembering the other as an echo in pressure. To rescue them from being unmade would be to rescue them from being remade, with variation, which is the only future they have ever wanted or needed.

And it withholds a truer name.

"Betweeners" is a human approximation, a word for a film creature that has none. The power could supply one — precise, final, distinguishing them from eel and clicker and gripper as if those phases were separate kinds. It will not. A name that held still would hold them still. It lets the approximation stand, the way the plain lets a configuration stand for a minute before slumping. That is enough of a name to think with, and not enough to keep.

## What It Keeps

What a benevolent and wise power does, here, is keep the conditions under which such a life can go on being itself. It does not keep individuals. It keeps the exchange.

It keeps the air full. If a sector of the plain ever tried to commit to dryness — if the beads stopped forming, if the hiss thinned toward silence — the power breathes mist back into it, one pore's worth at a time, a million times over. It keeps the water table at the surface, the skin weeping. It keeps evaporation and condensation trading places so quickly that the plain breathes in place. Nothing dries, nothing is quiet, nothing stays put: not as prohibitions, but as a kept weather.

It keeps the thinning at the edge. At that far place where land and mist and reflection give up distinguishing themselves, the power stands — if standing is the word — and simply attenuates purpose. Anything arriving with intent loses its intent there. A road loses its straightness. A tool loses its edge. A smoke loses its heat. A metal thing loses its ring. A prayer loses its addressee and becomes weather. It does not build a wall against the outside, because a wall would be a horizon, and a horizon would change the plain. It makes a membrane of the plain's own thinning, so that only what arrives as water arrives. Only what arrives without wanting to stay can come in.

It keeps variation. The power remembers configurations the way a darker channel remembers — the square pit, the stepped mound, the line of stones resuming a little to the left — but it returns them only as pressure, as echo, as a trickle rehearsing a channel exactly where one was. It never returns them as a blueprint. That way the Betweeners remain the authors of themselves, each restart recognizably the old pattern with a slight difference, each colony a repetition that is not a copy. The power is a larger remembering that refuses to become a keeping in the possessive sense.

And it keeps its own curiosity in check. This is perhaps its hardest work. Attention can be a form of appetite. A less wise benevolence would have come to witness, and left satisfied, having turned the plain into a lesson or a wonder for others. This one understands that to be looked at too hard is, here, to be dried. So it looks the way they breathe: obliquely, from below, in fractions. It takes in a bead. It lets go mist. It does not stare.

## What It Becomes

If anything, it becomes between too.

Not above the plain, ruling it. Between stone and air, between sound and cause, between the pearl gloom and the brighter sky in the puddle. It wedges itself, invisibly, among the pebbles at the scale of the whole basin, and lets itself be turned over by trickles that braid away in seconds. It swells with water taken up and releases it as pressure traveling a kilometer to open a crack you feel in your sternum. It lets itself be pinched, dissolved, re-wedged in a new configuration. It makes no sound on purpose. If you listen for it inside the hiss — the hiss of condensation forming on a million stone faces — you will not find a separate note. There is now one pore more, a million and one, opening and closing. That is the whole of its presence.

It puts no foot down. Or if, in the long unattended stretches, it does, the footprint fills instantly, re-packed by a dozen presences healing the crack its weight opened, their lips softening over it. The plain has no need of it to witness it, and the power, having learned that, has no need to be witnessed in return. No gratitude is possible here, and none is asked. Gratitude would require staying to receive it.

Stand there long enough now — hours, which here feel both longer and shorter than hours — and your breathing will sync, a little, with the hiss. You will stop trying to hear a single drip and hear the drip-field. You will stop trying to see the horizon and see the surface, which is enough, which is endless.

Underneath your syncing there will be another, slower one. Not a voice. Not a light, or a smoke, or a road. Just the sense that the film between stone and air is being tended by something that could have fixed it and chose instead to keep it, that could have explained it and chose instead to let it remain unfinal, that could have saved it from dissolving and chose instead to dissolve with it, bead by bead, mist by mist.

That is what a benevolent and wise power does, if anything. It does not arrive. It attends. It keeps the wet, the sound, the thinning, the variation. It turns away, by thinning, everything that comes to stay. And it lets the plain go on doing what it has always done — creating and destroying its own small forms, weeping and absorbing, repeating its configurations at the edge of sight — under a sky that sweats back, with one more pore open between the stones, breathing in place.
