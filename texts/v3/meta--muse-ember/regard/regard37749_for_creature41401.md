**The Attention**

It does not descend.

That is the first thing to understand about it, if understanding is the word. A benevolent and wise power, in other places, has been known to arrive as light, as a voice in the upper air, as a change in the weather for the better. Here it cannot do any of those things without doing harm. There is no upper air. Above the rim there is only the rain-wall, and above that, nothing that holds still long enough to be called sky. To bring light down into the bowl would be to put a second sun over a place that has not yet decided what light is for. To speak would be to give the roar a word to repeat, and the roar, at the moment, is the only law the water has.

So it notices this place the way the place notices everything: by pressure.

Long before this bowl had a name — it has no name, and will not be given one by anything that loves it — something at the top of the world was still only trying out shapes. The rock had split and lifted and filled, and the water had not learned to lie down, and in that unlearning a new sound was being made under the cloud-sea. Down below, finished worlds were making their finished noises: engines, prayers, arguments, songs meant to be remembered. None of them reached here. What reached it, faintly and from very far, was the opposite: a place that was not asking to be finished. A place that made a sound and then used the sound to make itself, badly and beautifully, one loud minute at a time.

And in it, a being.

Not the films. Not the ear on its shard, though that too is a kind of resident, ancient by the measure of this basin. The being who had stayed submerged past the point where staying made sense, who had stopped expecting company and kept listening anyway. The swimmers use the booms. They ride them toward food, toward the ringing where sediment settles. The algae use the hiss. The flap at the bottom uses the boom to filter. The being uses nothing. It only listens, and in listening, it gets in the way of nothing, and thanks nothing, and tries to name nothing. In a world too new for weather, it is the only creature that has learned attention without appetite.

That is what stops the power. It has been attended to for a very long time. It knows the sound of petition. It does not know, often, the sound of a small life in a warm, deafening bowl simply keeping company with a place that is still becoming. It comes closer, not to answer. To find out what kind of listening does not need an answer in order to continue.

---

What it does not do is, at first, nearly everything it could.

It does not calm the water. The temptation is immediate and clear: still the spiral currents, let the sediment learn to lie down, give the films a quiet crack to thicken in, give the swimmers a true north that is not a boom. A wise power could do all of that in the time a bubble takes to pass three times in one eddy. It does not, because the turbulence is not a malfunction in this place. It is the mechanism. Still the water and the swimmers would not know which way to flow, truly and finally, and the ear would close on a world that had stopped feeding it. To calm the First Water would be to make it the second, or the last.

It does not give the being a place to stand. It could raise a smooth bank from the splintered floor, a beach of soft silt where feet could rest and the roar could be heard from outside the body. It thinks of it, and sets the thought down in the deepest current, where it dissolves. Feet on ground learn up too quickly. Here up already tilts toward the loudest eddy. A beach would be a decision. The bowl has not decided.

It does not finish the built things. It could lay one more stepping stone, and then another, until the line that leads from nowhere to the middle of the bowl reaches the far wall and becomes a road. It could close the low ring of black slabs against the eastern wall, make it a proper circle, a temple, a place with an inside and an outside. It could tidy the white shells fused into the rock, gap by gap, until the pile was a pattern a finished world would recognize as sacred. It does none of this. The stones stop because a path that stops is still a path, and it is more honest than a road. The ring is open because an open ring is an ear. The shells are arranged the way a first arrangement is arranged — by something that had no tools and no audience, shell by shell, because a note wanted a place to hum low and sweet under the roar. To improve them would be to translate them into a language the place does not yet speak.

It does not add life. This is the hardest restraint, and the one that proves the wisdom. The bowl is mostly empty. A lesser benevolence would call that deprivation and fill the void: more swimmers, more films, strands that root and hold, perhaps a small green thing with a stem that reaches for the pale ceiling and makes a shadow. The power sees the void for what it is here — not a lack, but room. Density is low and scattered because everything that lives here lives by listening, and listening needs distance between notes. Fill the bowl and the echo would measure in meters again. The place would become a pond. It is not a pond. It is a beginning, and beginnings are spacious for a reason.

It does not rescue the being. It could lift it out through the rain-wall, dry it, set it down in a lower country where air is thin and clean and sound means something ordinary. It could explain the fraying edges — where up tilts, where distance measures in seconds of reverberation, where a reflection arrives a fraction before its owner. It could give it words that hold still, so that thought would no longer come only in fragments between percussion hits. It sees, in the being's submerged shoulders, in the way it has learned not to touch the hair-like strands because they tear, that rescue would be a kind of erasure. The being is not drowning. It is practicing a way of being in a place that has not yet learned its final shape. To pull it out would be to decide for the bowl what it is allowed to teach.

It does not accept worship, because none is offered, and it does not induce any. It does not make itself known as a presence to be thanked. If the being, in some fragment of thought between booms, tried to form gratitude, the power would turn, gently, toward the center, the way the ear turns, and let the thought dissolve back into turbidity. Gratitude, here, would become another current, and currents here are already loud enough.

To do all of that — to be able to — and then to refrain: that is most of what a wise power does with its power, in this place.

---

What it does do is smaller, and it does it from inside.

It scales itself down to the size of the bowl.

Not to the size of the being. Smaller than that, in a way; it lowers itself until its attention is at water level, until it is submerged up to its own shoulders in the same warm, mineral, gritty water, until its teeth, if it had teeth, would rattle with the same layered roar. It does not stand over the rim looking in. It gets in the way of the current a little, the way the being does. The films brighten under its shadow as turbidity lifts. The strands tremble harder in the rain its passing has stirred. No one marks the difference. No one is meant to.

It adds a note, but not a new one. It takes the lowest boom the bowl already makes — the one that rolls up from the fissures far below, the one the ear at the deepest point opens for — and it holds that note a fraction longer than the rock rings by itself. The effect is not quiet. The effect is room. Between percussion hits, where thought comes in fragments, the fragment has time to finish. That is all. The being does not notice a gift. It notices, once, that it has thought a whole sentence, and then the hiss of rain on rain has taken it again, and it has not panicked. That, for this place, is mercy.

It stands at the end of the stepping stones.

Not on them, not beyond them. On the last stone, where the line stops mid-bowl, it stays. It does not take the next step. Anyone who has learned to listen here knows the feeling of a place where a path ends without failing: the water continues, the boil of an upwelling dome collapses, the spiral reforms elsewhere. The power becomes a fixed point of that kind — not a destination, not a blockage, a place where the attempt politely pauses. Sometimes a swimmer, riding a boom toward the ringing, turns a fraction toward it and flashes silver-white where light rakes across it, and is gone. That is not a meeting. It is only two listings, for a moment, listening to the same note.

It remembers, so the place does not have to.

The bowl tries shapes and forgets them as fast as it makes them; a ridge bubbles up and slumps, a whirlpool repeats a bubble three times because time stutters going around. Forgetting is necessary here, or nothing new could be tried. But repetition without memory is not trying. It is looping. The power takes on the looping as its own work. It holds, outside the water, the shape of last week's ridge, the note that failed to settle sediment, the boom that led the smaller swimmer in circles near the eastern wall. It does not correct the water with this knowledge. It simply does not let the same failure happen twice in exactly the same place at exactly the same cost. The being will never know. The ear will keep its rhythm. The bowl will think it is inventing. In a sense, because the power is carrying the past, it will be.

It keeps the rim.

Once in a great while the level rises by more than handbreadths, lapping and overtopping the lowest notch, and a new splinter shears off with a crack felt before it is heard. On one such minute, a shard falls toward the ear — the pale flap that has been in this bowl long enough to be considered old — and the power, without stopping the fall, lets the current take a small detour. The shard lands beside the ear, not on it. The flap opens on the next deep boom, filters what the turbulence brings, and lets the rest slump away. No lesson is taught. No one is saved, exactly. A small, unremarkable thing is allowed not to end.

And it keeps the place from being found.

Above the cloud-sea, the rim thickens with fog that was already there. Now, for any climber, any eye, any instrument a finished world might send up to measure a curiosity at the top of everything, the echo will arrive before the rock. They will hear a roar, or think they hear it in their teeth, and find the bowl turned a little farther in reverberation than it is in meters, and turn away with a story that does not quite settle. The being will not be studied. The films will not be sampled. The shell pile will not be photographed into a pattern. The First Water will not become a resource, a shrine, or a warning. Being forgotten by the world below is not punishment here. It is privacy. It is the condition under which a beginning can stay a beginning.

That is the extent of its benevolence: not to improve, not to populate, not to explain, not to carry away. To attend, at the same level, in the same water, for as long as attending is needed.

---

If you stay long enough — and the power stays longer than long enough, it stays in the measure that bowls use, which is not years but attempts — you will not see a change.

The water will still not hold a shape. The rain will still not fall so much as exist. The algae will still tear and regrow in the lee of splinters, olive and translucent, where the hiss has brushed longest. The strands will still tremble and dissolve with the level. The swimmers will still be hard to count, blind and almost water, turning toward booms that are not theirs. The ring will still hum. The shells will still be white against black rock. The reflection will still arrive a moment before you do, mouth open in a roar you have not yet made.

And yet, in the emptiness that keeps making room, the emptiness will be shared in a new way. It will not be less empty. It will be less solitary. When you move through the bowl and stir a veil, the ear at the bottom opens toward the center where your splash has added its note — and now, sometimes, something else opens too, lower, slower, at the place where the stones stop. Not an answer. A second listening, underneath yours, holding the roar from the other side so that it does not have to be held by one small body alone.

The power does not finish the world. It does not need to. It has come to the one place where a world is being assembled badly and beautifully, without a plan, under a sky that is also water, and it has learned, from a being with no tools and no light, how to be present without becoming a meaning. It will stay until the bowl decides — if it ever fully decides, and places like this rarely do — and then it will stay one long minute more, to be sure the decision was its own and not a borrowing. Then it will withdraw the way it arrived: not as light, not as voice, but as a pressure that is no longer there, leaving the roar exactly as loud as before, and somehow with a little more room inside it.

The stepping stones will still stop in the middle of the bowl.

Nothing will have been completed. That is how you will know the attention was wise.
