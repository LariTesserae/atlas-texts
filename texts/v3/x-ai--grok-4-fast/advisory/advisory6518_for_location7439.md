#### 1. Physiological Preparation: Acclimating to the Heights and the Haze

- **Altitude Sickness Mitigation**: Start with pre-travel acclimation. Hydrate obsessively—the damp mists fool you into thinking you're moist, but dehydration sneaks up due to the dry cold beneath the haze.

- **Respiratory and Thermal Adaptation**: The cool damp (constant 10-15°C/50-59°F, with drizzles that bead rather than soak) demands building tolerance to persistent chill and minor hypoxia. Train in cold, misty conditions—foggy urban parks or simulated environments—to get used to the slick skin and foggy glasses. Practice deep diaphragmatic breathing to maximize oxygen intake; the air's whisper-thin quality makes shallow breaths inefficient. If you have any respiratory issues (asthma, etc.), consult a multiverse medic for a portable oxygen concentrator—Echolholm's industrial pollution (smog from forges) layers ozone and ash onto the mix, irritating lungs like a perpetual smoker's cough.

Prepare psychologically by meditating on impermanence; visualize reality as a dream you're lucid in. Journaling or mindfulness apps can help track time dilations—without them, you might lose days in a fern that blooms and wilts in a loop. If prone to vertigo or anxiety, the plateau's lip-dropping views and exposure could trigger panic; exposure therapy in open, high places beforehand is key.
