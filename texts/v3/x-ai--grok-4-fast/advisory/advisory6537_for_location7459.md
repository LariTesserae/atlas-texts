- **Head and Face Protection**: A balaclava or neck gaiter for wind and mist. The brightness and cold combo could chap skin quickly; bring lip balm and a high-SPF moisturizer. If you're sensitive to humidity, a light face mask might help filter the peaty, mineral-laden air.
