In the fevered cradle of this crest-world, where the stone dreams in half-coalesced fury, life does not bloom or scuttle—it accretes, layer by layer, from the marrow of the mountain itself. What inhabits these jagged ambiguities are not creatures of flesh or frenzy, but entities born of the rock's restless prototyping: the Lithovores, vast and infinitesimal, a spectral congress of mineral intelligences that pulse with the geothermal throb. They are not one form, not yet; they emerge as the world emerges, testing shapes in the warm voids between upheavals, their essences woven from silicates and sulfurous vapors, as mutable as the talus that shifts beneath an unseen step.

Consider first the Anchorwefts, the foundational dwellers, sprawling mycelial lattices that thread the pumice like forgotten neural maps, pale and veined with the glow of ingested quartz. They are not fungi in the damp, earthly sense, but dry symbiotes, propagating not through spores but through seismic echoes—vibrations from a distant fracture rippling through the stone, coaxing their filaments to extend in fractal precision, latching onto fresh basalt upwellings. An Anchorweft might span a saddle's length, a mile-wide web of translucent strands no thicker than a whisper, feeding on the chemical fury of mineral leachates: sulfides oxidizing in the heat, birthing ephemeral crystals that the weft absorbs like a slow inhalation. They do not move with purpose, yet they respond—when a fissure yawns nearby, their lattice tightens, drawing in the superheated gas to catalyze new growth, hardening into brittle spines that mimic the spires above. In the dim gloaming, they faintly luminesce, a bruised indigo pulse syncing with the mountain's rhythms, illuminating the etched spirals on buried monoliths as if in quiet communion. These are the elders, the patient architects, holding the world's skeleton steady amid its mania, their "life" a perpetual negotiation with erosion's absence, growing not toward light but toward the core's insistent warmth.

Drifting above and through them are the Veilswarmers, ephemeral collectives that defy the light gravity's betrayal, born from the spore-veils that illusion motion in the desiccated air. These are not insects or wisps of pollen, but volatilized mineraloids—clusters of microscopic pumice shards infused with volatile compounds, heated until they sublimate into buoyant, iridescent motes that aggregate in loose swarms. A single Veilswarmer might be a solitary fleck, drifting upward on a thermal jet from a reforming crack, its surface etched with micro-spirals that catch the phosphorescent threads and refract them into fleeting rainbows. But they congregate in the turbulent eddies, hundreds or thousands forming transient bodies: a shimmering helix that spirals around a spire's base, probing for weaknesses in the obsidian mosaic, or a diffuse cloud that veils a monolith, siphoning its latent heat to sustain their aerial dance. They "live" in cycles of dispersal and re-formation, scattering during avalanches to embed in the cascading talus, then reforming when the slope reassembles, their intelligence emergent in the swarm—a collective murmur that anticipates shifts, guiding the motes away from plummeting betrayals. In the hush between groans, you might hear their passage as a silken hiss, the air parting for their weightless procession, as if the mountain exhales them into being, only to inhale them back in the next breath.

Deeper in the shadowed ambiguities, where crevices pool darkness and time stretches like taffy in the haze, dwell the Corewakes—rarer, more singular presences, echoing the vanished builders in their deliberate forms. These are ambulatory crystalloids, humanoid in vague silhouette but forged from the worked basalt of the monoliths, animated by the subsurface fires that bubble through the veins. A Corewake stands perhaps eight feet tall when fully emergent, its body a faceted pillar of dark stone, limbs branching like incomplete fractures, head a hooded cowl of pumice that reveals glowing fissures for eyes. They do not stride the ridges with haste; instead, they migrate by submersion, sinking into a compliant outcrop during a tectonic heave, traveling through the warm stone like roots through soil, resurfacing miles away where the ground has stuttered upward anew. Their sustenance is the instability itself—absorbing the energy of snaps and rumbles, catalyzing it into crystalline lattices that extend from their forms: probing tendrils that trace the ancient spirals on their kin-artifacts, perhaps repairing or re-etching them in rituals lost to the embryonic flux. A Corewake's "voice" is a low vibration, harmonizing with the landscape's murmurs, summoning minor shifts—a talus redistribution to unearth a buried relic, or a gas jet to illuminate a shadowed escarpment. They are solitary guardians, not predators or prey, but stewards of the unfinished sculpture, their longevity measured in eons of settling stone, evolving slowly toward sentience as the world commits to its shapes. In the perpetual gloaming, one might loom suddenly close, its warm-glow fissures flickering like half-formed thoughts, inviting scrutiny before the ground tilts and reveals the illusion of distance.

Interwoven among these are the lesser echoes, the not-quite-lives that blur the line between inhabitant and environment: Symbiote Flecks, tiny quartz nodules that "grow" by thermophoretic drift, latching onto Anchorwefts to share heat gradients; or Vortex Lenses, fleeting optical anomalies in the gas jets, refractive pockets of superheated vapor that mimic eyes, watching the chaos without intent. No hierarchy binds them, no predation disrupts the bazaar of flux—Lithovores coexist in the void's intimacy, each form amplifying the others' persistence. The Veilswarmers scatter nutrients for the wefts, the Corewakes stabilize paths for the motes, and together they embody the crest-world's mania: creation without culmination, life as an ongoing prototype, half-born and wholly itself in the warm, whispering emptiness. To dwell here is to become part of the sculpture, layer by turbulent layer, as the dreaming giant stirs and refines its thoughts into something eternal.
