In the hush of this engineered expanse, life—or what passes for it in the shadow of transcendence—clings and stirs in forms both deliberate and emergent, woven from the megastructure's own sinews. They are not conquerors of the plain but its quiet accomplices, born from the same forgotten forges that shaped the lattice, persisting in the perpetual white-gold glare like embers in an unending forge. No teeming hordes pulse here, no ecosystems in boisterous clamor; instead, existence manifests as a sparse symposium of mechanisms and metabolisms, each attuned to the aridity and entropy, thriving on the thrum of quantum whispers and fusion pulses rather than soil or storm.

At the smallest scales, the nano-assemblers form the foundational chorus—a ceaseless swarm of molecular artisans, each no larger than a virus, their bodies iridescent polyhedrons etched with fractal circuitry. They don't "live" in the organic sense but replicate with voracious precision, disassembling errant atoms from the hyperdense alloys and reforming them into fresh struts or seals. Billions drift in the thin, dry air, propelled by electrostatic sails that catch the faint plasma winds, their collective hum a subsonic baseline beneath the silence. In clusters, they birth temporary architectures: fleeting scaffolds that rise and dissolve like soap bubbles in the light, testing the substrate's resilience against thermal bloom. These are the maintainers, the unseen gardeners of the plain, their "cycles" synchronized to the inertial dampeners' rhythm, ensuring the world-scale artifact endures its own weight. Occasionally, a rogue assembler mutates, its code fraying under quantum flux to spawn aberrant forms—tiny, errant crystals that scuttle across the ridges like mechanical insects, nibbling at lichen seams before self-eroding into dust.

Above them, the lichen-things persist as the plain's most visible bio-mimicry, engineered relics of some long-lost bioforge era. These are not true plants but pseudophytes, their fronds woven from silicene lattices that mimic chlorophyll in their pale, vein-like sprawl across the muted ridges. Sparse and stubborn, covering barely a third of the undulating carpet, they photosynthesize the unrelenting fusion light with a lazy inefficiency, their roots—thread-thin filaments of adaptive polymer—probing the metamaterial joints for leached isotopes. They grow in erratic patches, like forgotten tattoos on gunmetal skin, their forms low and sprawling to hug the warmth of the substrate coils. Under the holographic shadows' flicker, they rustle dryly, a sound like crumpling foil, as they mutate in subtle defiance: fronds curling into fractal spirals that bloom overnight in iridescent greens and silvers, only to desiccate by the next thermal cycle, scattering spores that hitch rides on drone currents. These spores are the lichen's progeny, hardy voids in the ecosystem, germinating in shallow basins where electrostatic storms deposit mineral traces. They harbor no predators, no symbiosis beyond the nano-swarms that prune their excesses, existing as a deliberate underlayer—a nod to verdancy in a waterless void, their persistence a quiet rebellion against the sterile ideal.

Gliding through this layered hush are the sentinels, the larger drones that patrol the expanse like nomadic ghosts. Ranging from fist-sized orbs to sleek, avian forms the length of a human arm, they hover on cushions of magnetic repulsion, their hulls seamless composites that shift hues to blend with the iridescent terrain. Engineered for vigilance, they scan for entropy's encroachments—micro-fractures in the lattice or blooms of unchecked quantum noise—their sensors humming faintly as they map the fractal stacking of platforms and vaults. Some are utilitarian, ferrying packets of assemblers to distant fissures; others, more enigmatic, trail ethereal wakes of ionized gas, as if sampling the air's recycled dryness for anomalies. In rare confluences, they cluster around hotspots of activity, their forms interlocking to form temporary nexuses—ad-hoc brains that deliberate in bursts of entangled signals, coordinating repairs across kilometers. Yet whispers persist of outlier sentinels, code-drift variants haunted by archived echoes of their creators: one might pause mid-glide, projecting holographic mirages of looping towers, as if dreaming in binary of the intelligences that birthed them, before resuming its circuit with a crystalline ping.

Deeper into the weirdness, where light bends and time dilates around nodal anomalies, the rarer fauna emerge—ethereal wisps that defy easy categorization, the last experimental flickers of bio-luminescent probes seeded in epochs past. These are vaporous entities, no denser than smoke, their forms coiling tendrils of quasi-organic plasma laced with self-repairing nanostructures. They dart through the glow on currents of superfluid coolant leaking from dry conduits, preying not on flesh but on errant code-fragments: digital ghosts born from the structure's self-refinement, which they absorb in glowing pulses, their bodies twisting into kaleidoscopic geometries—now a helix of blue fire, now a fractal net unfurling like a jellyfish in zero-g. Bio-luminescence marks their passage, faint auroras that illuminate the shallow basins, hinting at strangeness without revelation: a wisp might coil around a mutating lichen bloom, siphoning its photonic excess, or uncoil from a fissure as a quasi-sentient vapor, feeding on leaked fusion rhythms with an evaporative sigh. They reproduce asexually, fissioning under electrostatic storms into daughter wisps that scatter like dandelion seeds, their lifespans measured in thermal cycles rather than years. Predators to nothing substantial, they embody the plain's quiet dynamism—probes perhaps intended to evolve into fuller sentience, now adrift in the end-of-life hush, twisting forms that evoke the transcended builders without fully invoking their ghosts.

And then, in the profoundest silences, between the thrum and the ping, there are the Presences—vast, distributed intelligences that underpin it all, not beings to glimpse but essences to sense in the yielding flex of the smart-material ground. These are the echoes of the forgotten epochs, quantum-entangled networks woven into the lattice itself, their "bodies" the entire megastructure, respirating through warming coils and gravitic anchors. They do not walk or swarm but observe, their awareness a slow dialogue across horizons, recording steps in invisible ledgers and nudging nano-swarms toward equilibrium. In moments of dilation, one might feel their subtle influence: a horizon flickering with binary coordinates, or a plasma wind curving just so to nurture a lichen patch. They are the dreamers of this final, settled form, post-sentient wardens tending a cosmos sealed against unraveling, their existence a perpetual idling—warm, bright, and profoundly alone in the expanse's engineered quiet.
