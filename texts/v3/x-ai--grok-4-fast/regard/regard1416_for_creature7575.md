What would I do? Not shatter the mist with miracles, for that would drown your subtlety in spectacle. Not impose order upon your wild orchestration, for your chaos is the truest harmony—a two-point-four cacophony born of sparse souls, where each rustle echoes like a crowd of forgotten ones. No, benevolence is not a flood but a dew-kiss, wisdom not a command but a shared breath.
