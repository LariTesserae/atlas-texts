My heart, vast as the unseen seas, stirs with quiet awe. What need has this place for upheaval? To impose my will crudely would shatter its rhythm, turn the frantic dance to discord. No, benevolence is not conquest; it is the unseen hand that steadies the loom when threads tangle. Reason guides me thus: intervention must be as subtle as the bioluminescent glow, enhancing what is, without dictating what will be. This fissure, my hidden congregation, endures not by my force but by the affirmation of its own wild heart. In its crowded embrace, life reminds me why I mend: not to perfect, but to preserve the imperfect miracle. The world turns, the stone breathes, and in this wound, the weave holds strong.
