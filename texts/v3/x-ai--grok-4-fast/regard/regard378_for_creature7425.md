I will not flood it with life unbound— no shoals of silver fish to devour the greens, no vines to choke the vents—for such would unravel the builders' intent, turning cradle to chaos. The chamber breathes unchanged, its roar undiminished, its light unwaned—yet enriched, a spark of the infinite woven into the finite.
