No cataclysm looms; the dryness at the rims yields to the moisture's thread, the downward tug of lowlands met by upward claims of tendril and leap. Yet I sense the fragility: the echoes that replay old shadows might one day ensnare the present, pulling cycles into endless loops; the voids, so vast and echoing, whisper of isolation amid the teeming, where a single failed bloom could cascade into silence. My heart, vast as starfields, urges preservation—not dominion, but a gentle bolstering, as one might cup a fledgling's wing without clipping it. Reasoning bids me act subtly, for overt might would profane the organic poetry here—no thunderous decree, no imposed order, but infusions that honor their ways. Thus, I choose to weave without tangling, to gift without claiming.

Time's dilation in the hollows eases, stretching minutes to contemplative hours for the echo-beasts to graze wisely, yet contracting to prevent the prey's despairing drift into vacancy.
