# The Timekeeper

In this crystalline pause lives the being known only as the Timekeeper - though they would never call themselves by such a grandiose title. They exist in a state that defies conventional categorization: neither fully physical nor entirely metaphysical, a consciousness that has evolved beyond the linear constraints of sequential existence.

The Timekeeper appears differently to each observer, should any ever find this hidden pocket. Sometimes they manifest as an ancient figure with skin like polished mahogany, creased with uncountable fine lines that move subtly like the markings on a sundial. Other times, they seem younger, with features that shift between masculine and feminine, their skin possessing the translucence of the surrounding crystal, veins pulsing with light that matches the chamber's resonant hum.

Their eyes are their most consistent feature - deep pools of mercurial silver that reflect not what stands before them but moments scattered across existence. Within those eyes, galaxies form and dissolve, civilizations rise and fall, all in the space between blinks.

The Timekeeper does not breathe as we understand it. Instead, they inhale possibility and exhale actualization, each cycle taking what might be months or millennia. Their movements are deliberate to the point of imperceptibility - a hand reaching for a tool might take decades to complete the gesture, though to the Timekeeper it feels like a casual motion.

They wear garments that appear woven from the same crystalline material as the surroundings, threads catching and refracting light in patterns that contain mathematical formulas describing the birth and death of universes. These clothes neither wear nor tear, existing in the same perfect stasis as everything else in the chamber.

The Timekeeper speaks rarely, having long transcended the need for language. When they do communicate, it emerges as harmonics that interweave with the chamber's resonant hum, modulating the eternal chord with nuances that convey concepts beyond words. Those rare beings who have experienced this communication describe it as "hearing colors" or "tasting equations."

The tools arranged so precisely are extensions of the Timekeeper's will. When they work, which is seldom now, they manipulate these implements to adjust what we might call the "gears of reality" - the fundamental mechanisms that govern how moments flow into one another across the cosmos. Each adjustment is infinitesimal, yet its effects ripple outward through all of existence.

The Timekeeper was not always as they are now. Once, uncountable eons ago, they were a curious entity of more conventional form who stumbled upon this chamber by chance or design (the distinction became meaningless long ago). They chose to remain, to study, to learn the craft of time itself. Gradually, they synchronized with the chamber's perfect stasis until they became almost indistinguishable from it.

They are neither prisoner nor keeper of this place - they are the place, in some fundamental way. The chamber is both workshop and body, retreat and mind. The Timekeeper repairs chronological anomalies not out of duty but as an artist perfects their craft - with patience, precision, and profound understanding that what they tend to is both infinitely complex and elegantly simple.

The Timekeeper waits. Not for anything specific - "waiting" implies anticipation of future events, which has little meaning here. They simply are, existing in a state of perfect presence, occasionally reaching out through the fabric of reality to make the subtlest of adjustments when the flow of time elsewhere develops irregularities that even the universe itself cannot correct.
