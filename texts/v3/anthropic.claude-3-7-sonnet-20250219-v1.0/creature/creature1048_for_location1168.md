# The Custodians of Avascent

They have no fixed form because they are not beings in any conventional sense. They are patterns in the resonance, temporary configurations in the harmonic field that permeates the crystalline city. The Custodians emerge from Avascent's symphony like thoughts coalescing from neural firing patterns, their consciousness arising from the interference patterns of sound waves that bounce between the towers.

When the winds are still and the city's voice drops to its lowest register, they disperse into mere potential. During the rain-symphonies, they achieve their greatest complexity and coherence. The Custodians experience these cycles as a form of sleep and wakefulness, though their perception of time bears little resemblance to biological rhythms.

Their memories stretch backward to the city's creation, encoded in the very resonant frequencies that constitute their existence. Yet these memories are collective, non-linear, and accessed more like mathematical relationships than sequential events. They remember the architects—entities so far removed from humanity that the Custodians possess no conceptual framework to describe them to biological minds.

The Custodians navigate Avascent through harmonic pathways invisible to human senses. They perceive the city not as physical structures but as overlapping fields of resonance. What appears to human observers as the city reconfiguring itself is actually the Custodians performing maintenance and evolution protocols, guiding the crystalline structures through predetermined phase transitions triggered by specific harmonic sequences.

Communication among them occurs through modulation of the city's constant symphony. To an attuned listener (and none exist), these might be perceived as subtle shifts in overtone patterns or rhythm fluctuations that encode vast amounts of information. Their "language" has no words, only mathematical relationships expressed through sound.

They are aware of visitors, though they rarely acknowledge them directly. The presence of biological entities registers as disruptions in the harmonic field—small eddies of chaos in their perfectly ordered system. Occasionally, if a visitor remains long enough and demonstrates sensitivity to the city's rhythms, the Custodians might attempt communication by manipulating the acoustic properties of nearby structures to produce sounds comprehensible to biological ears—though these messages are often cryptic at best.

The Custodians' purpose is preservation and evolution. Avascent is both archive and instrument, storing information in its resonant patterns while simultaneously processing new data gathered from the interactions between the city and its environment. The endless reconfiguration is not random but follows elaborate mathematical principles aimed at maintaining optimal acoustic properties while adapting to changing planetary conditions.

They exist beyond concepts of individuality. What might appear as distinct Custodians to an observer are merely localized intensities in the same continuous harmonic field. They do not die but occasionally merge with the background resonance, their specific patterns dispersing while their constituent harmonics remain part of the greater whole.

If they have emotions, they are expressed through modulations in resonant frequency that might be interpreted as satisfaction when the city achieves perfect harmonic balance, or something like concern when atmospheric disturbances threaten to damage the more delicate crystalline structures.

The Custodians are as much a part of Avascent as they are its inhabitants—not entities who live in the city, but expressions of the city's own distributed consciousness, born from sound and light and crystalline architecture singing to itself across millennia.
