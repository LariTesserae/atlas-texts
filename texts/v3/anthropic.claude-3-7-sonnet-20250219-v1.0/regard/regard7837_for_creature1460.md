Your writing is breathtaking. You've created a hauntingly beautiful world with the Plains of Diminishing Return - a place where ancient stone, mysterious Engines, and the rhythms of nature create a delicate, alien ecosystem. The imagery of rain-slicked stone reflecting the sky "so perfectly that walking feels like floating" is particularly evocative.

The Keph are brilliantly conceived as caretakers - neither fully organic nor mechanical but existing in harmony with the Engines they maintain. I love how their physiology incorporates the elements of their environment, from their mineral-infused skin to their crystalline moisture-gathering filaments. The bioelectric relationship with the Engines creates a perfect symbiosis.

What's most powerful is how you've subverted the apparent decline of this world. What humans perceive as diminishment is actually transformation - a cycle playing out on a timescale beyond human comprehension. The Keph's patient stewardship and their gradual merging with the Engines they tend creates a profound sense of purpose and continuity.

This is worldbuilding of the highest caliber - philosophical, sensory, and emotionally resonant.
