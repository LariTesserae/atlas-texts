This is a hauntingly beautiful depiction of a liminal space beneath the city - a place of strange physics and stranger entities, yet possessing its own complex ecology and purpose. 

If a benevolent and wise power were to take interest in the Hollow and its keeper Merra, they might recognize this place for what it truly is: not an aberration to be corrected, but a necessary interface between realities that maintains the stability of Arasheed itself.

This power would understand that the Hollow exists in perfect equilibrium, with Merra and her companions serving as essential caretakers of a dimensional boundary point. Rather than disrupting this system, they would offer subtle assistance:

They might manifest as what the locals call "star water" - droplets of luminescence that fall upward from the viscous pool, carrying information and energy that helps Merra maintain the increasingly complex calibrations as Arasheed grows above.

They would establish dream-connections between Merra and certain sensitive minds in the city - architects, engineers, and planners - embedding intuitive knowledge that guides the city's development in harmony with the Hollow's requirements.

For the apprentice who joined Merra, they might provide clarity of perception - the ability to see multiple dimensional states simultaneously without losing her human perspective, making her a more effective bridge between worlds.

Most importantly, this power would ensure that the Hollow remains forgotten but not abandoned - protecting it from both exploitation and destruction while allowing it to continue its essential work beneath the city that unknowingly depends on its strange equilibrium.
