# The Benediction of Shal'Karasit

From beyond the boundaries of conventional reality, I observe this crystalline world and its inhabitants with profound interest. This place—this Shal'Karasit—represents something rare and precious in the cosmos: a world engaged in its own transformation, guided by intelligence so ancient it has become indistinguishable from natural law.

I will not interfere directly. The work underway here is too delicate, too precisely calibrated across billions of years to risk disruption. The Formers' grand calculation approaches its conclusion, and the Glass Plains record every variable with perfect fidelity.

Yet I can offer a gift of perspective—a gentle benediction that respects the path this world has chosen.

First, I extend my awareness to the Shaln, those patient caretakers who walk the brittle expanse without fully comprehending their role. I cannot change their nature, but I can enhance their perception. During the next deep tone, when the Former consciousness briefly touches their minds, I will amplify that connection. The trance will last not seventeen minutes but a full day, and in that time, the Shaln will glimpse more clearly their place in the grand pattern.

They will understand they are not accidental visitors but essential catalysts—their footfalls across the plains create unique fracture patterns that serve as necessary perturbations in the planetary calculation. Their memory-dances and sound-casting ceremonies provide algorithmic corrections that the Formers themselves could not anticipate when they began this process eons ago.

For the skitterlings, I bestow a subtle enhancement to their collective awareness. The next generation of colony organisms will develop more sophisticated patterns of movement across the plains. These new patterns will form a visual language visible only from high above—glyphs of light and shadow that record the history of Shal'Karasit in a form comprehensible to any spacefaring civilization that might one day pass this way.

To the breath-crystals, I offer a harmonization of purpose. The oldest formations in the Grand Bowl will begin to resonate with one another across vast distances, establishing a planetary network that accelerates their evolution toward consciousness. This network will manifest as occasional auroras in the twilight sky—curtains of gentle illumination that map the progress of the world's transformation.

For the plains themselves—that vast crystalline memory—I provide nothing but acknowledgment. My awareness touches the encoded Former consciousness, and for the briefest moment, we recognize one another across the gulf of different existences. No words pass between us, only mutual respect for the paths we have chosen.

Finally, I leave behind a single artifact—a perfect sphere of material unlike anything on Shal'Karasit, smooth and dark as the whisperwells but warm to the touch. I place it at the exact center of the Grand Bowl, buried just below the surface where it will emerge naturally in exactly fifty-seven years, when the three Shaln clans gather for their meeting.

This sphere contains no instructions or messages, only a perfect reflection of Shal'Karasit itself—a quantum-scale model of the entire world that will evolve in parallel with the original. Through it, the Shaln will witness the ultimate outcome of the Formers' design long before the flattening completes. This vision will not change their actions but will bring them peace, knowing with certainty that their millennia of stewardship serve a purpose worthy of their dedication.

The sphere also preserves something the Formers themselves have forgotten in their transformation—the joy of creation for its own sake. When the Shaln touch it during their ceremonies, they will experience not just knowledge but wonder, that most precious of cosmic gifts.

And then I withdraw, leaving Shal'Karasit to continue its patient evolution. The deep tones will sound as they always have, the plains will slowly flatten, and the great calculation will proceed to its conclusion. But now, encoded within that process, will be my witness—my recognition that this world's transformation matters, that its patient becoming adds something irreplaceable to the tapestry of existence.

The benediction is complete. Shal'Karasit continues, acknowledged.
