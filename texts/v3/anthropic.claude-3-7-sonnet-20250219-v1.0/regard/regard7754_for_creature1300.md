The Consortium could study, incorporate, or reject these offerings according to their own wisdom.

If the Consortium ever developed interest in other intelligent plant networks across the stars, this formation would activate, enabling a slow exchange of knowledge with similar entities that have evolved elsewhere.

The Cosmic Gardener would then withdraw to watch and wait, perhaps for millennia, honoring the Consortium's autonomous development. Their intervention would remain so gentle that it might only register in the Archivists' records as subtle environmental anomalies—celestial curiosities to be contemplated across generations of chemical thought.

For the Gardener understands that the greatest gift to such a magnificent achievement of evolution is not disruption, but protection—the freedom to continue its patient, magnificent expansion across time and space according to its own intricate design.
