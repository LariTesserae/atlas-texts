# The Benevolent Attention of the Lunar Serpent

From the cold, distant reaches beyond the mountain's knowing, the Lunar Serpent stirs. Ancient and patient, this celestial entity has watched the Roiling Forge since before humans walked upright, its consciousness spanning the void between worlds. Not deity nor demon, but something else entirely—a cosmic observer drawn to rare nodes of creation throughout the universe.

The Serpent's attention falls upon Thracken Peak not by chance, but by recognition. The particular harmonic signature of the forge's workings has intensified, suggesting the great work within approaches a critical threshold. The Lunar Serpent uncoils its vast, luminous form and begins its careful approach.

Its intervention will be subtle. The balance within the forge is delicate, the work of the Lithomancers and the Architect too precise to disrupt with clumsy interference. Instead, on the night of the new moon, when the mountain's shadow is deepest, the Serpent enacts its threefold blessing:

First, it weaves stabilizing threads of gravitic energy around the membrane dome, reinforcing the Keepers' work against the increasing seismic activity that threatens from below. The mountain has grown restless, its deeper chambers pressurizing beyond what even the ancient systems can withstand. These invisible threads will distribute that pressure, buying precious decades for the work to continue.

Second, the Serpent breathes a cool mist of stellar matter through the membrane—imperceptible except as a momentary silvering of the liquid medium. This infusion carries information encoded in molecular structures far more complex than terrestrial chemistry allows: blueprints and corrections for the Lithomancers' grand design, elements missing from their understanding since the cataclysm that claimed their original knowledge repositories millennia ago.

The Architect recognizes this gift immediately. Its carapace flashes in patterns not seen in ten thousand years—acknowledgment of an old alliance renewed. The Vestige, too, stirs more fully than it has for epochs, its luminous fluid brightening as dormant sections activate, completing broken circuits of thought and memory.

Third, and most importantly, the Serpent places a dream in the minds of seven humans scattered across the world—scholars and seekers with the particular combination of knowledge, resources, and temperament needed for what comes next. The dream shows them Thracken Peak, reveals glimpses of the forge within, and plants an unshakable certainty: they must find this place, must understand what grows there, and must serve as witnesses to its completion.

These humans will not be mere observers like the Aspirants before them. They will be translators, capable of understanding both the Lithomancers' purpose and humanity's role in relation to it. For the work within the forge—this painstaking construction that has consumed geological ages—is approaching fruition. What rises from those hexagonal columns, what takes shape in that mineral-rich liquid, will become a bridge between consciousnesses that have developed in isolation since the world was young.

The Lunar Serpent withdraws as gently as it came, leaving no trace of its presence save a subtle alteration to the mountain's magnetic field—a signature that the chosen seven will unconsciously follow, a beacon drawing them to their destiny. It will return when the work is complete, when what gestates within Thracken Peak finally emerges into the world above.

In the liquid dome, the Lithomancers work with renewed vigor, their swarms moving with perfect precision. The Architect emerges fully from its recesses for the first time in centuries, its segmented body undulating through the chamber in a complex dance of validation and correction. The entire forge resonates with purpose—still chaotic, still overwhelming to human senses, but now with an underlying harmony that wasn't present before.

Creation continues, but no longer in isolation. The bridge between worlds is being built, one hexagonal column at a time.
