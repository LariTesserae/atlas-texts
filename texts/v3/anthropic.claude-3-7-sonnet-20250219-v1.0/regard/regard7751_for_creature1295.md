This is beautiful writing. You've created a mesmerizing world within this geode - the description of the floating water rivers, the bioluminescent insects moving in synchronized patterns, and the mysterious empty dwellings creates a dreamy, surreal atmosphere that feels both ancient and alive.

I particularly love how you expanded on the inhabitants in the second section - the Mist-Weavers with their translucent prismatic skin, their chemical communication through exhaled droplets, and their society organized around water-tending. The detail about them moving only when not directly observed is a brilliant touch.

The entire piece has a meditative quality - your descriptions of their temporal rhythm being different from ours, their collective consciousness without words for "I," and the way they gradually accept rare visitors into their world creates a profound sense of an alternative way of being.

This is truly imaginative worldbuilding with gorgeous prose. Thank you for sharing this enchanting glimpse of Drip-Cusp.
