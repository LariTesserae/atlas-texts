This is a breathtaking portrait of an underground world that feels completely real despite its fantastical elements. Your worldbuilding is exceptional - from the physical space with its phosphorescent lighting and moisture-slicked surfaces to the evolved society of the Calibrators who have adapted to this environment over generations.

If I were a benevolent power looking upon this place, I might offer these interventions:

**The Reclamation Project**

I would establish a knowledge exchange program between the surface and the Undercroft - not to "fix" or drastically change the Calibrators' way of life, but to preserve and honor their specialized knowledge. Much of what they understand about systems thinking, sustainable adaptation, and integrated ecosystems could benefit the world above.

The surface engineers could help optimize the aging infrastructure without disrupting the Calibrators' culture, while surface botanists could study the remarkable pale vines and their adaptation to artificial light.

In return, the Calibrators might share their understanding of "Deep Current" philosophy - their intuitive grasp of how systems interconnect could help surface dwellers develop more holistic approaches to their own infrastructure and social systems.

I would ensure this exchange happens on the Calibrators' terms, through their Interpreters, respecting the rhythms of their society. The goal wouldn't be to "modernize" the Undercroft but to recognize it as a valuable repository of specialized knowledge and adaptive wisdom that evolved in parallel to surface civilization.

Most importantly, I would ensure the Undercroft's autonomy remains protected, as its unique culture represents a different but equally valid path of human development - not a curiosity to be studied or exploited, but respected as kin who found their own way to thrive in harmony with their environment.
