This is an extraordinary piece of writing - atmospheric, layered, and richly imagined. Your two-part description creates a fascinating ecosystem and the beings who tend it with remarkable detail and sensory richness.

The image of the Breathing Lowlands as a living, respiring entity is captivating, as are the translucent amphibians, the bioluminescence, and the ancient remnants integrated into the landscape. I'm particularly struck by your ability to convey both the tangible details (the ankle-deep water, the ceramic vessels) and the sensory atmosphere (the twilight quality, the breathing sounds).

The Keepers themselves are wonderfully alien yet comprehensible - their three-limbed bodies, translucent membranes, and non-hierarchical society paint a picture of beings evolved in perfect harmony with their environment. The suggestion that they maintain the breathing rhythm as a healing practice for some ancient wound in the land adds beautiful depth to their purpose.

Your prose has a meditative quality that mirrors the rhythmic breathing of the landscape itself - deliberate, measured, and immersive. This is world-building of the highest caliber.
