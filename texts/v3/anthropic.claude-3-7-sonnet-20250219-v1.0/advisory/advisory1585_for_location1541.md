# Preparation Guide for Visiting Muan'va: The Suspended Cetacean City

## Essential Overview

Muan'va presents unique challenges for human visitors. This aquatic environment exists in a liminal state between conventional physics and something more fluid. Preparation must account for both physical survival and metaphysical adaptation.

## Physical Adaptations

### Breathing Apparatus
Standard scuba equipment will fail here. The water's unusual viscosity requires specialized breathing technology:
- Procure a "viscous-medium respirator" with molecular filtration
- Ensure it has elastic pressure compensation for the thermocline zones
- Include backup systems that can function during temporal anomalies
- Consider bringing osmotic gill implants (temporary, 72-hour variety) as emergency backup

### Mobility
The thick, honey-like water will severely restrict conventional swimming:
- Train in resistance swimming techniques for at least 3 months prior
- Obtain neutrally-buoyant webbing suits that accommodate the water's unusual density
- Bring propulsion aids designed for high-viscosity environments
- Practice slow, deliberate movements; rapid motions will exhaust you quickly

### Temperature Regulation
The thermocline creates deceptive temperature gradients:
- Wear a thermally adaptive bodysuit with microscopic heat-exchange filaments
- Include insulating layers that function during time dilation events
- Monitor your core temperature constantly; the dreamy quality of movement can mask hypothermia onset

## Sensory Preparation

### Vision
Light behaves unusually in Muan'va:
- Bring adjustable-spectrum goggles that can enhance the twilight conditions
- Include anti-refraction lenses to compensate for the water's variable density
- Avoid bright artificial lights that might disturb the Deep Singers or local fauna
- Practice interpreting visual information in low-light, distorted conditions

### Hearing & Communication
Standard auditory experience will be limited:
- Obtain bone-conduction transducers to detect the subsonic communications
- Learn basic resonant-frequency sign language for communicating with locals
- Bring vibration-sensitive recording equipment to document experiences
- Practice feeling sounds rather than hearing them through pre-trip sensory deprivation

### Inner Ear & Balance
The unusual physics will challenge your sense of equilibrium:
- Take anti-vertigo medication designed for dimensional shift sickness
- Complete vestibular recalibration exercises daily for two weeks before arrival
- Prepare for moments of disorientation during time inconsistencies
- Consider temporary inner ear modifications if your multiverse medical insurance covers them

## Cognitive Preparations

### Time Perception
The inconsistent timeflow requires mental adaptation:
- Practice meditation techniques focused on temporal flexibility
- Bring a non-mechanical chronometer that responds to your biorhythms
- Prepare psychologically for the disorientation of experiencing multiple time-rates simultaneously
- Consider microdosing with prescribed chronoperception enhancers starting 10 days before travel

### Reality Anchoring
The fluid nature of reality in Muan'va can be psychologically destabilizing:
- Carry a personal anchor object from your home reality
- Establish grounding rituals to perform when reality seems most fluid
- Practice recognizing dissociation and have techniques ready to reconnect with your sense of self
- Keep a physical (not digital) journal to maintain narrative continuity of your experience

## Cultural & Social Preparation

### Etiquette
The Deep Singers have complex social protocols:
- Never disturb the phosphorescent kelp gardens
- Avoid rapid movements that create turbulence
- Learn to communicate through slow, intentional gestures
- Practice patience—interactions may take what feels like hours due to time dilation
- Bring small gifts of unusual but non-toxic minerals from your home reality

### Observation Protocols
- Never attempt to enter "the dry world" void in the central spiral
- Maintain distance from guardian thresher-eels
- Observe ceremonial shell-singing only from designated distances
- Request permission through guide-mediated vibrations before approaching any structure

Before attempting to visit Muan'va, honestly assess your:

- Comfort with altered states of consciousness

- Experience with non-Euclidean spaces
- Capacity to adapt to fluid conceptions of time

Recommended: Complete the Stanford-Byerly Multiverse Psychological Adaptation Assessment and score above 85% on the Reality Fluidity Tolerance section.

## Final Cautions

Muan'va is not merely an underwater city but a place where reality itself has different properties. Even with perfect preparation, expect to be fundamentally changed by your visit. The Deep Singers perceive existence differently than humans, and some visitors report persistent perceptual changes after returning home—seeing water move against gravity, hearing subsonic communications, or experiencing brief moments of time dilation.

Most importantly, approach Muan'va with reverence and openness. The Deep Singers respond to intention and emotional resonance more than words or actions. Those who come with curiosity and respect will find the experience transformative; those seeking to exploit or merely observe will find the city mysteriously closed to them, its wonders hidden behind veils of ordinary water.
