# Preparation Guide for Visiting the Hollow Under Arasheed

## Essential Equipment

**Light Sources:**
- Bring at least three independent light sources (headlamp, powerful flashlight, and chemical light sticks)
- Pack extra batteries sealed in waterproof containers
- Consider a hand-cranked emergency light as backup
- Avoid open flames as the atmosphere may contain unknown gases

**Protective Clothing:**
- Waterproof boots reaching at least mid-calf
- Thick-soled shoes with excellent grip (the "not-quite-water" substance may be slippery)
- Full-body waterproof coveralls or waders
- Chemical-resistant gloves
- Respirator mask with multiple filter types (the ozone/copper smell suggests unusual air composition)

**Navigation Tools:**
- Compass (though be prepared for it to malfunction)
- Physical chalk or light-colored rope to mark your path
- Analog watch (electronics may behave unpredictably)
- Small mirrors to check around corners or observe areas without direct exposure

**Safety Equipment:**
- First aid kit including antihistamines and anti-fungal treatments
- Emergency whistle (sound behaves strangely but might alert companions)
- Climbing rope and harness (30+ meters)
- Water testing kit
- Personal carbon monoxide detector
- Insulated tool kit (rubber-handled tools for any necessary interactions with equipment)

## Mental and Physical Preparation

**Physical Conditioning:**
- Practice moving in confined spaces
- Work on maintaining balance on slippery surfaces
- Build endurance for potential long periods in high humidity
- Train yourself to function in low-light conditions

**Mental Preparation:**
- Practice meditation techniques to maintain calm when experiencing spatial distortions
- Study basic mechanical and electrical systems to recognize dangerous equipment
- Learn techniques to ground yourself when experiencing perceptual anomalies
- Establish a "reality check" routine with companions

**Research:**
- Study any available maps of Arasheed's underground infrastructure
- Interview locals about folklore related to the Hollow
- Consult academic papers about non-Euclidean spaces and their effects on human perception
- Learn to identify the "hollow mice" and crustaceans from a safe distance

## Procedural Guidelines

**Entry Protocol:**
- Enter only during daylight hours above-ground
- Leave detailed plans with someone trustworthy outside
- Establish a check-in schedule and emergency extraction plan
- Never enter alone; minimum team of three recommended
- Test the passage for air quality before proceeding

**While Inside:**
- Maintain physical contact with your companions when possible
- Document your path meticulously
- Take regular compass and time readings to track anomalies
- Limit your visit to no more than two hours initially
- If you notice time inconsistencies, exit immediately
- Maintain a consistent narrative of what you're experiencing out loud
- If team members report different observations of the same area, retreat

**Control Station Protocol:**
- Do not touch any controls, switches, or levers
- Photograph gauges only from a distance
- If gauges appear to respond to your presence, increase distance
- Never step onto the metal platform
- If the red emergency lighting intensifies or dims, exit immediately

**Interaction With Fauna/Flora:**
- Do not touch or disturb the crustacean formations
- Avoid contact with the fungi
- If hollow mice approach, remain still and observe only
- Do not collect specimens on your first visit

**Exit Strategy:**
- Set a timer for departure well before your expected endurance limit
- Follow your marked path precisely
- If your marked path has changed or disappeared, follow the water channel
- If multiple channels appear where there was only one, choose the one flowing downhill
- Exit as a complete group; never leave someone behind

## Post-Visit Procedures

**Decontamination:**
- Remove and seal all clothing worn in the Hollow in airtight containers
- Shower thoroughly as soon as possible
- Monitor for unusual symptoms for at least 72 hours
- Have companion observers check you for behavioral changes

**Documentation:**
- Record your experiences immediately while fresh
- Compare notes with companions to identify perception differences
- Create maps and diagrams before seeing others' interpretations
- Note any dreams or unusual thoughts in the following week

## Psychological Warnings

The Hollow appears to exist in a state of dimensional instability. Visitors should be aware of the following potential psychological effects:

- Spatial disorientation lasting hours or days after visiting
- Temporary synesthesia (experiencing one sense through another)
- Intrusive thoughts about returning to the control station
- Dreams featuring the crustacean patterns
- Heightened sensitivity to industrial sounds
- Temporary inability to properly perceive water
- Compulsive drawing of circuit-like patterns

If any of these symptoms persist more than 72 hours after visiting, seek specialized help from those familiar with dimensional anomalies.

## Critical Cautions

- Never visit if you have existing mental health conditions involving reality perception
- Do not bring electronic recording equipment that you aren't prepared to lose
- Do not attempt to collect samples of the "not-quite-water"
- Under no circumstances attempt to operate the control station
- If you notice your shadow behaving independently, exit immediately
- If companions begin speaking in unison or in voices not their own, separate from them and exit
- Never visit more than three times in a single month
- If you begin to feel that the Hollow is "calling" to you, seek immediate counseling

The Hollow Under Arasheed represents a rare case of dimensional slippage within an urban infrastructure. Approach with scholarly caution rather than adventurous bravado. Remember that those who've mapped it extensively have reported contradictory findings—or disappeared entirely.
