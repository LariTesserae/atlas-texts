# Preparation Guide for Visiting The Drowned Court

## Essential Preparations

### Physical Conditioning
- **Cold water training:** Immerse yourself regularly in cold water (starting at 10°C/50°F and gradually reducing) for at least 3 months before visiting. Your body will need to function while partially submerged in perpetually cold water.
- **Develop leg strength:** The constant resistance of walking through water will fatigue unprepared muscles quickly.
- **Balance training:** Practice walking on slippery surfaces; the submerged stone pathways will be treacherously smooth.

### Sensory Adaptation
- **Auditory conditioning:** Spend time in environments with constant, complex sound patterns. The unending cacophony of the flying creatures will be psychologically overwhelming without preparation.
- **Sleep training with noise:** Learn to sleep with specialized noise that mimics the Court's acoustic environment—this is critical as the sound never ceases.
- **Low-light vision exercises:** Train your eyes to function in twilight conditions by gradually reducing light exposure during certain activities.

## Specialized Equipment

### Clothing
- **Thermal base layers:** Multiple sets made from modern synthetic materials that retain heat when wet.
- **Neoprene waders or dry suit:** Must be durable enough to withstand constant immersion yet flexible enough for movement.
- **Waterproof outer shell:** Choose materials that won't degrade in the mineral-rich water.
- **Specialized footwear:** Neoprene boots with aggressive treads for traction on smooth, submerged stone.
- **Thermal gloves:** With waterproof exterior and textured grips.
- **No cotton whatsoever:** It will stay wet and draw heat from your body.

### Navigation & Research
- **Waterproof light sources:** Multiple redundant options including chemical lights, as electronics may fail.
- **Non-electronic compass:** The dome structure provides a constant central reference point.
- **Waterproof notebooks and writing implements:** For documenting observations.
- **Distance measuring tools:** Laser rangefinders may work if kept dry.

### Survival Gear
- **Portable water filtration system:** While surrounded by water, you cannot drink it without treatment.
- **Concentrated food rations:** High-calorie, lightweight options to maintain energy in the cold environment.
- **Compact camp stove:** For heating food and potentially small quantities of water for washing.
- **Inflatable boat or raft:** For resting without constant immersion and exploring deeper waters.
- **First aid kit modified for aquatic environment:** Including treatments for trench foot, hypothermia, and potential allergic reactions to unknown organisms.
- **Portable shelter:** A hammock system to suspend yourself above the water for sleeping.

### Communication & Safety
- **Noise-canceling earplugs or headphones:** Critical for mental health and preventing auditory fatigue.
- **Visual signal equipment:** Laser pointers, reflective materials, and bright markers for communicating over distance.
- **Emergency extraction plan:** Establish protocols for leaving quickly if necessary.

## Cultural & Scientific Preparations

### Observation Skills
- **Practice underwater observation techniques:** The clear water provides exceptional visibility for studying submerged structures.
- **Learn basic principles of cave acoustics:** This will help understand the complex soundscape.
- **Study bioluminescence phenomena:** To better comprehend the ceiling-dwelling light sources.

### Communication Attempts
- **Develop a non-verbal communication system:** The native inhabitants likely cannot understand any human language.
- **Learn simple trade practices:** Bringing small, waterproof tokens or objects might facilitate peaceful interaction.
- **Practice illustrative drawing:** Images may bridge communication gaps better than words.

### Scientific Documentation
- **Underwater specimen collection techniques:** For studying the unique aquatic life.
- **Non-invasive observation methods:** To avoid disrupting the delicate ecosystem.
- **Sound recording equipment:** For analyzing the complex acoustic patterns of the flying creatures.

## Psychological Preparation

### Mental Conditioning
- **Sensory deprivation training:** The unchanging environment can cause temporal disorientation.
- **Meditation practices:** To manage the stress of constant noise and perpetual twilight.
- **Isolation exercises:** Practice being completely removed from familiar reference points.

### Cultural Sensitivity
- **Observe without interfering:** This ecosystem has remained in perfect equilibrium for millennia.
- **Prepare for alien concepts of time:** The civilization exists without change—your human perception of progress may be meaningless here.
- **Accept cognitive dissonance:** Many aspects of The Drowned Court defy conventional understanding.

## Approach Strategy

### Initial Entry
1. Acclimatize at the cavern entrance for 24 hours before proceeding.
2. Enter during your biological day cycle to minimize disorientation.
3. Establish a base camp near the perimeter before venturing toward the central structures.
4. Move slowly and deliberately through the water to avoid startling the aquatic life.

### Exploration Protocol
1. Always travel with a partner and maintain visual contact.
2. Document your path meticulously—the concentric layout can be disorienting.
3. Approach the inhabitants only after observing their patterns from a distance.
4. Under no circumstances attempt to access the central cube chamber without understanding the cultural significance.

## Special Warnings

- **Temporal Disorientation:** Without day/night cycles, your circadian rhythm will be disrupted. Maintain strict sleeping schedules.
- **Cold Water Immersion:** Prolonged exposure risks hypothermia despite preparation. Schedule regular warming breaks.
- **Acoustic Overload:** The never-ending sound can cause auditory hallucinations after extended exposure. Implement noise breaks.
- **Foreign Biology:** The transparent fish and other creatures may cause unknown allergic reactions. Test exposure gradually.
- **Cultural Violation:** The inhabitants have lived unchanged for millennia—your presence is an unprecedented disruption. Tread with extraordinary care.
- **The Cube:** The vibrating cube at the center represents unknown technology or power. Approach with extreme caution, if at all.

Remember that you are visiting a place of perfect equilibrium. Your very presence introduces change to a system that has known none for countless generations. The greatest respect you can show is to leave as little trace as possible of your passing through this eternal, submerged world.
