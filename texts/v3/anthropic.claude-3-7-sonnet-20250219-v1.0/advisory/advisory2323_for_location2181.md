# Preparation Guide for Visiting the Undercavern Gardens of Ithme

## Essential Preparations

### Mental and Physical Conditioning
- Spend time in enclosed spaces before your journey to acclimate to the absence of open sky
- Practice meditation techniques to manage potential claustrophobia
- Build leg strength and flexibility for navigating uneven terrain and narrow passages
- Train your balance for walking on occasionally slippery surfaces

### Vision Adaptation
- Begin dark adaptation exercises weeks before departure
- Practice navigating by low, diffuse light similar to the blue-green illumination you'll encounter
- Your eyes will need at least 30 minutes to fully adjust to the unique light conditions upon arrival

## Packing Essentials

### Clothing
- Pack lightweight, moisture-wicking layers that dry quickly in the constant humidity
- Avoid cotton, which will remain perpetually damp in Ithme's environment
- Bring fungal-resistant treatments for your clothing and gear
- Include soft-soled, grippy footwear that won't damage the delicate ecosystem
- A thin, breathable head covering will protect against occasional water droplets
- Leave space in your pack for garments crafted by Ithmians, as wearing local attire facilitates cultural integration

### Equipment
- A personal water filtration system (though local water is generally safe)
- Small LED light with red setting for emergencies (use sparingly to avoid disrupting local ecosystems)
- Compact, waterproof journal and pencils for recording observations
- Folding mat made of natural materials for sitting and sleeping
- Small gifts of practical value for your hosts (simple wooden tools, natural fiber rope, or seeds from your world may be appreciated)

### Do Not Bring
- Electronic devices with bright screens that could disturb the light balance
- Battery-powered items that could leak harmful chemicals
- Strong fragrances or chemical repellents that might disrupt the delicate ecosystem
- Anything made of metal that could inspire resource envy

## Cultural Considerations

### Communication
- Learn basic Ithmian pictographic gestures before arrival
- Speak softly; the acoustics will carry your voice further than you expect despite the dampening
- Practice communicating through movements and drawings, as shared verbal language may be limited
- Respect the natural sound levels of the environment

### Social Etiquette
- Wait for invitation before entering any dwelling
- Always accept offered food but take small portions initially
- Observe how locals interact with the living architecture before touching any plants
- Water is sacred—never waste it or use it without permission
- Be prepared to share communal sleeping areas
- Offer to help with daily maintenance tasks as a way to integrate

### Time Perception
- Abandon your timepieces and schedules
- The perpetual "now" of Ithme requires mental adjustment
- Prepare to lose track of surface-world time completely

## Health Considerations

### Biological Adaptations
- Your surface-dwelling immune system will be challenged by new fungi and microorganisms
- Begin a probiotic regimen one month before travel
- Bring mild antihistamines for possible reactions to the unique plant life
- Consider wearing a thin breathable mask during your first few days to gradually expose your lungs to the spore-rich air

### Physical Comfort
- Prepare for constant dampness—nothing will ever feel completely dry
- Your skin may develop a slight sheen from the humidity
- Ears may need to adjust to the pressure changes between chambers
- Eyes will eventually adapt to the dim conditions, but initial headaches are common

## Practical Skills Worth Learning

- Basic mycology to understand and appreciate the fungal diversity
- Cave navigation techniques and memory mapping
- Water purification methods
- How to identify phosphorescent versus bioluminescent organisms
- Reading natural air currents to navigate between chambers
- Hand carving techniques to participate in local crafting

## Final Thoughts

The Undercavern Gardens of Ithme exist in a delicate balance. As a visitor, your primary responsibility is to observe this balance and disturb it as little as possible. Enter with humility, move with deliberation, speak with care, and learn with enthusiasm. Though you may eventually leave Ithme, a piece of its timeless nature will remain with you—just as you will inevitably leave something of yourself behind in its memory-rich stone walls.
