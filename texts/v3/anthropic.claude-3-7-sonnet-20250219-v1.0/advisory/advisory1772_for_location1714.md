# Preparation Guide for the Sunken Vault of Glassfang Peak

## Essential Equipment

1. **Waterproof Everything**
   - Fully waterproof backpack with multiple sealed compartments
   - Dry bags for all electronics and documents
   - Gore-Tex or similar waterproof outerwear that breathes (you'll be in constant moisture)
   - Waterproof boots with excellent traction for slippery metal grating
   - Several changes of moisture-wicking base layers

2. **Lighting**
   - Primary light source: High-powered waterproof headlamp with at least 1000 lumens
   - Secondary: Multiple waterproof flashlights with wide-beam capabilities
   - Tertiary: Chemical light sticks (minimum 12-hour duration, at least 20)
   - Spare batteries for all electronic devices (triple what you think you'll need)
   - Consider UV and infrared light sources (the Vault's unusual properties may make certain phenomena visible only in non-standard light spectrums)

3. **Navigation & Mapping**
   - Analog compass (but be prepared for it to malfunction)
   - Mechanical altimeter (though expect unreliable readings)
   - Chalk or removable markers to leave temporary trail markers
   - Notebook with waterproof pages and pencils for mapping
   - Ball of strong twine/cord (minimum 1000 feet) to create physical guide paths
   - Small mirrors to check around corners and potentially redirect light

4. **Protection**
   - Industrial ear protection rated for high-decibel environments (adjustable to allow for communication)
   - Respirator or filtering mask (the metallic/ozone smell may indicate harmful particulates)
   - Heavy-duty gloves (protect against sharp edges and extreme temperatures)
   - Safety goggles (waterproof and fog-resistant)
   - Climbing harness and carabiners for traversing the unexpected architectural features
   - Compact first aid kit with emphasis on treatments for cuts, burns, and hypothermia

## Specialized Gear

1. **Measurement & Documentation**
   - Analog camera with mechanical operation (electronics may malfunction)
   - Digital camera in waterproof housing (for redundancy)
   - Field journal with waterproof pages
   - Portable spectrometer if available (to analyze the water's composition)
   - Small glass vials for collecting water samples from different areas
   - Thermometer with broad range (-50°F to 500°F)
   - Geiger counter (the ozone smell could indicate ionizing radiation)
   - Watch with mechanical movement (multiple if possible)

2. **Sustenance & Emergency**
   - Water purification tablets (though I'd advise against drinking any water found inside)
   - Concentrated food rations for 3x your planned visit duration
   - Thermal emergency blanket (multiple)
   - Waterproof matches and fire-starting materials
   - Chemical hand/body warmers (to combat prolonged exposure to cool, damp conditions)
   - Personal locator beacon (though signal may not penetrate the mountain)
   - Small medical kit with antibiotics (unknown microorganisms may be present)

3. **Mental Preparation Tools**
   - Audio recorder (to document observations and maintain mental clarity)
   - Small musical instrument or tone generator (to test acoustic anomalies)
   - Meditation aids (the disorienting environment may require mental grounding techniques)
   - Analog watch with second hand (to verify time anomalies)

## Psychological Preparation

1. **Before Your Visit**
   - Practice sensory deprivation exercises to acclimate to the disorienting darkness
   - Study basic principles of non-Euclidean geometry and paradoxical architecture
   - Learn meditation techniques that focus on maintaining presence in disorienting situations
   - Establish a personal mantra or grounding ritual to center yourself when reality seems to slip
   - Familiarize yourself with symptoms of hypoxia, hallucinations, and spatial disorientation
   - If possible, speak with high-altitude miners or deep cave explorers about psychological coping strategies

2. **During Your Visit**
   - Maintain regular verbal logs of your observations (speaking aloud helps anchor reality)
   - Set timers to return to entry point regardless of how compelling further exploration seems
   - Establish a buddy system if traveling with others, with regular check-ins
   - Take regular breaks to re-orient yourself to normal physics and perspective
   - Document any unusual thoughts or compulsions you experience (the environment may have subtle psychological effects)

## Approach Strategy

1. **Initial Entry**
   - Wait for dry weather to approach the entry door (less initial water release)
   - Bring tools to prop the door open securely (never let it close behind you)
   - Mark the entrance clearly with reflective material
   - Acclimate at the entrance for 30 minutes while observing water flow patterns
   - Test communications equipment to establish baseline functionality
   - Set a maximum exploration time and multiple alarm reminders

2. **Exploration Methodology**
   - Move slowly and deliberately, testing each step before committing weight
   - Use the "rule of thirds" for resources: 1/3 for entry, 1/3 for exploration, 1/3 for exit
   - Document everything through multiple media (written, photographic, audio)
   - When encountering junction points, always fully explore one path before taking another
   - Leave markers that include time information (the space may reconfigure while you're inside)
   - Stay near walls or fixed structures when possible (helps maintain orientation)

3. **Interaction Guidelines**
   - Observe but do not disrupt the mechanical moths or other functioning systems
   - Avoid direct contact with the unusually behaving water when possible
   - If entering chambers with abnormal physics, maintain physical contact with something from "normal" space
   - Do not attempt to operate controls or valves unless absolutely necessary
   - If you encounter reflections that show future events, do not attempt to change the outcome

## Emergency Protocols

1. **If Disoriented**
   - Stop moving immediately
   - Close eyes, take ten deep breaths
   - Use touch to ground yourself (feel the texture of your clothing, equipment)
   - If you brought twine/cord, follow it back toward the entrance
   - If separated from your group, stay in place and use light signals rather than shouting (sound behaves unpredictably)

2. **If Structure Begins Visibly Reconfiguring**
   - Move to the nearest stable platform or catwalk
   - Remain motionless until the transformation completes
   - Document the changes if possible
   - Do not attempt to interact with or interrupt the process

3. **If Experiencing Temporal Anomalies**
   - Focus on your mechanical watch or timepiece
   - Document the discrepancy in detail
   - Do not make sudden moves or decisions until time normalization occurs
   - If experiencing precognitive reflections, test with simple hand movements before trusting the phenomenon

## Specific Hazards to Monitor

1. **Water Behavior**
   - The water that defies gravity may have unusual properties beyond appearance
   - Avoid contact with prismatic water patterns or orbiting water spheres
   - Be prepared for sudden changes in water flow direction or volume
   - Water that has been standing in the facility for long periods may contain unknown contaminants

2. **Acoustic Dangers**
   - The described "industrial symphony" may reach damaging decibel levels
   - The tuning fork sound could potentially induce resonance in metal equipment or even bones
   - Certain sound patterns may have psychological effects or trigger changes in the facility
   - Consider using earplugs that reduce volume but don't eliminate sound entirely (to maintain awareness)

3. **Spatial/Temporal Distortions**
   - Areas where shadows fall incorrectly may indicate more significant physics anomalies
   - Chambers where sounds precede movements could create dangerous cause-effect confusion
   - The reflective pools showing future events may induce decision paralysis or reckless behavior

## Post-Visit Procedures

1. **Decontamination**
   - Remove and isolate all clothing and equipment that contacted the Vault water
   - Shower thoroughly as soon as possible with attention to eyes, ears, and any cuts
   - Monitor for unusual symptoms for at least two weeks after exposure
   - Have any collected samples analyzed in controlled conditions

2. **Documentation**
   - Transcribe notes immediately while memories are fresh
   - Compare your experience with any previous accounts
   - Create multiple backups of all documentation
   - Look for patterns in any photos or recordings that weren't apparent during the visit

3. **Psychological Aftercare**
   - Schedule a debrief with someone you trust (preferably someone with experience in altered spaces)
   - Practice regular reality-grounding exercises for several weeks
   - Be alert for dreams featuring the Vault's architecture or sounds
   - Consider the possibility that your perception of normal physics may be temporarily altered

Remember that the Sunken Vault appears to be a self-evolving system with its own internal logic. Your goal should be observation without interference, and above all, ensuring your safe return. The most dangerous aspect may not be the physical hazards, but the subtle way the place reshapes your expectations of how reality should behave.
