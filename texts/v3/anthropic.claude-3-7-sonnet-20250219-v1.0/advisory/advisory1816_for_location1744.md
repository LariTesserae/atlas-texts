# Preparation Guide for the Avian Citadel

## Essential Understanding

Before attempting to visit the Avian Citadel on the Upreach Plateau, understand that you are entering a zone of altered reality. This is not merely a strange location—it's a place where fundamental physical laws are in flux. Your very perception and existence will be challenged.

## Physical Preparations

### Oxygen and Breathing
While the air is described as "breathable," its thinness at this impossible elevation requires acclimatization. I recommend:
- Spend 2-3 weeks at progressively higher altitudes before your journey
- Bring supplemental oxygen that doesn't rely on standard pressure physics
- Practice breath control techniques used by high-altitude climbers
- Consider a face mask that humidifies the inhaled air to protect against the extreme dryness

### Temperature Regulation
The plateau features "bitter cold" that "seeps into bones" yet with light that "burns without warming":
- Layer clothing using non-conductive materials (avoid metals)
- Bring insulation that works without moisture (traditional down will be useless in this moisture-devoid environment)
- Wear specialized eye protection against the shadowless, omnidirectional light
- Your body's normal temperature regulation may fail—bring monitoring equipment

### Hydration
The absolute desiccation of the environment presents your greatest physical challenge:
- Carry triple your normal water supply in sealed, non-evaporative containers
- Use a full-face moisture-retention mask when possible
- Bring emergency rehydration solutions with electrolytes
- Apply thick occlusive ointments to exposed skin, reapplying hourly
- Your eyes will dry painfully—bring preservative-free lubricating drops

### Navigation
Standard navigation tools will likely malfunction:
- Bring multiple redundant compasses and GPS devices, but don't rely on them
- Carry physical markers (bright fabric strips) to create reference points
- Learn celestial navigation, though be prepared for the absence of visible celestial bodies
- Practice mental mapping techniques as your perception of distance will be severely compromised

## Psychological Preparations

### Sensory Distortion
The Citadel will assault your senses in ways that may trigger psychological distress:
- Practice sensory deprivation exercises before visiting, particularly silence immersion
- Train in maintaining equilibrium when visual distances suddenly shift
- Prepare for the absence of shadows, which will destroy your depth perception
- Learn meditation techniques that anchor you to your body when reality seems fluid

### Time Perception
Time inconsistencies will be disorienting:
- Wear multiple timepieces and compare them regularly
- Maintain a strict schedule of activities regardless of perceived time passage
- Keep a journal where you note observations at measured intervals
- Practice techniques for remaining present when time perception distorts

### Isolation Coping
The profound silence and alien nature of the environment will create extreme isolation:
- Undergo isolation training beforehand
- Bring a companion if possible, maintaining physical contact in areas where distance becomes unreliable
- Carry small, tactile objects from your normal reality to ground yourself
- Establish communication protocols with your companion that don't rely on sound

## Behavioral Guidelines

### Interaction with Avian Entities
- Do not attempt to interact with the birds—they appear to operate on principles beyond human comprehension
- If they approach you, remain perfectly still
- Never mimic their movements or sounds
- Avoid direct eye contact with the larger specimens
- Under no circumstances approach or touch their constructions

### Movement Protocols
- Walk with deliberate, measured steps
- When distances shift, stop immediately and reorient
- If you find yourself suddenly transported, remain in place until your perception stabilizes
- Avoid running or sudden movements which may trigger unpredictable spatial shifts

### The Crystal Dome
- Do not approach the central dome containing the egg
- If found in its vicinity, back away slowly while facing it
- Never touch any crystalline structures, especially near the center
- If the birds freeze at the sound of the "single pure note," freeze as well

## Equipment List

### Survival Gear
- Custom-designed cold weather clothing with no metallic components
- Multiple water containers with manual pressure systems
- Caloric-dense, dry foods that require minimal preparation
- Shelter materials that can function in variable gravity conditions
- Light sources that work on mechanical principles rather than electronic
- Manual heating devices (chemical heat packs)

### Documentation Tools
- Mechanical film cameras (electronics may malfunction)
- Manual audio recording devices
- Notebooks with graphite pencils (ink may freeze or behave strangely)
- Measuring tools that rely on physical properties rather than electronic sensors

### Emergency Supplies
- Signal devices that work through multiple sensory channels
- Emergency exit markers that can be deployed to create a retreat path
- First aid specifically for altitude sickness, dehydration, and sensory disorders
- Grounding tokens—small objects with personal significance to anchor your sense of self

## Final Considerations

The Avian Citadel exists at the boundary of comprehension. No preparation can be truly adequate, but maintaining these principles may help you survive:

1. Accept that understanding is not possible—observation is your only realistic goal
2. Preserve your physical body through constant attention to hydration and temperature
3. Anchor your mind through routine and physical touchstones
4. Be prepared to retreat if your perception becomes too unstable
5. Remember that time spent in the Citadel may not correlate to time in normal reality—set a strict departure deadline and adhere to it regardless of circumstances

The Citadel is not malevolent, but neither is it concerned with human limitations. It exists according to its own principles. You are, at best, an irrelevant observer in a place where reality itself has different priorities.
