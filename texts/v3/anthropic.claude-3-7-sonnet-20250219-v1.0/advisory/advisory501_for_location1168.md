# Preparation Guide for Visiting the Resonant City of Avascent

## Essential Preparation

### Physical Conditioning
Begin altitude training at least 2-3 months prior to your visit. Avascent sits on an extremely high plateau where the air is dangerously thin. Your body will need time to produce additional red blood cells. Consider sleeping in a hypoxic tent for several weeks before departure. Even with preparation, expect shortness of breath, headaches, and potential altitude sickness.

### Mental Preparation
The psychoacoustic effects of Avascent cannot be overstated. The constant, ever-changing symphony of sounds operates at both audible and infrasonic levels that affect cognition and emotional stability. Practice meditation focused specifically on sound immersion. Learn to distinguish between external sounds and internal thoughts, as the boundary between these will blur in Avascent.

## Protocol Recommendations

### Entry Approach
Enter at dawn when the condensation phase begins. The acoustics are at their mildest, allowing gradual acclimation. Establish base camp at the perimeter where architectural shifts are less dramatic. Never camp near tall structures that might reconfigure overnight.

### Movement Patterns
- Always maintain three points of contact when navigating structures
- Walk only on surfaces that have been stable for at least 10 minutes (use temporal markers)
- Never enter vertical shafts or spiral staircases without securing a tethering line
- Document your path using sound-pattern recordings, as visual landmarks will change
- Rest every 30 minutes to reorient yourself and assess cognitive function

### Sensory Management
Schedule regular "silence breaks" with earplugs fully inserted and eyes covered. The constant sensory input of Avascent can cause hallucinations after prolonged exposure. Develop a personal cognitive touchstone—a mathematical equation or poem you can recite to yourself to verify mental clarity.

### Emergency Protocols
If you experience any of the following, initiate immediate departure:
- Persistent ringing in ears that doesn't match the city's tones
- Visual hallucinations that persist during silence breaks
- Compulsion to separate from your group or remove protective gear
- Sensation of the crystal structures "calling" to you personally
- Loss of time awareness exceeding 2 hours

## Special Considerations

### Weather Timing
Plan your visit between storm systems. The acoustic properties during rainfall create sound patterns that affect neurochemistry in unpredictable ways. Some travelers report synesthetic experiences or temporary personality alterations during these events.

### Duration Limitations
No visitor should remain in Avascent for more than 72 hours continuously. The combination of altitude, acoustic stimulation, and perceptual distortion creates cumulative neurological stress that becomes dangerous beyond this threshold.

### Post-Visit Integration
Allow at least one week in a controlled, quiet environment after visiting Avascent. Many travelers report auditory flashbacks, spatial disorientation, and dreams of architectural reconfiguration for weeks afterward. Keep a sound journal to document and process these experiences.

## Scientific Collection Guidelines

If collecting data or samples:
- Use vibration-isolated containers for any crystal fragments

- Photograph structures in rapid sequence to capture movement
