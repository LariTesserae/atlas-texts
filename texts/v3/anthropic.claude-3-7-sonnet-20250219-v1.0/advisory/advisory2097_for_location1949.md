# Preparation Guide for the Undercity of Dripping Song

## Essential Preparations

### Mental Conditioning
Begin psychological preparation at least one month before your journey. The Undercity distorts reality in ways that can severely disorient unprepared minds. Practice meditation focusing specifically on accepting contradictory perceptions simultaneously. Develop a personal mantra to recite when reality shifts become overwhelming. Most importantly, understand that questioning what you experience too deeply may trigger anxiety or psychosis - acceptance is your best defense.

### Physical Training
Increase your cardiovascular endurance and practice moving in darkness. The Undercity's perpetual twilight and spatially inconsistent pathways will tax your body in unexpected ways. Train specifically for maintaining balance on slick surfaces and develop your proprioception (your sense of body position). Practice holding your breath for extended periods, as the water-saturated air can sometimes become temporarily unbreathable during reality fluctuations.

## Equipment

### Illumination
* Primary: Chemically-powered light sources (not electrical) with adjustable intensity
* Backup: Bioluminescent compounds in sealed containers
* **Avoid** standard flashlights or headlamps - electrical devices often malfunction or create dangerous visual distortions

### Navigation
* Chalk made from bone ash mixed with mineral oil (standard chalk dissolves)
* Three identical mechanical compasses (to verify when they disagree)
* Twine spun from natural fibers soaked in salt water (100+ meters)
* A small silver bell (sound mapping becomes vital when visual navigation fails)

### Protection
* Waterproof clothing made exclusively from natural materials (synthetic fabrics may undergo molecular restructuring)
* Footwear with excellent grip that rises above the ankle
* Gloves with reinforced palms (for unexpected climbing)
* Respirator with specialized mineral filters to prevent long-term lung crystallization

### Recording Equipment
* Waterproof journal with pages treated with alum
* Graphite pencils (ink behaves unpredictably, sometimes rearranging itself)
* Mechanical recording devices for sound (no digital equipment)
* Clay or wax for taking impressions of surfaces

### Sustenance
* Sealed water in glass containers (the Undercity's water is not safe to drink despite its apparent purity)
* Dry foods with minimal seasoning (strong flavors may attract unwanted attention)
* Salt tablets (crucial for maintaining electrolyte balance in the high-humidity environment)

### Specialized Items
* Small offerings of pure metals (copper, silver, iron) left at entrances seem to reduce hostile encounters
* Glass vials for collecting water samples (labeled before entry)
* A musical instrument you can play competently (sometimes necessary for communication)

## Navigational Protocols

Establish strict movement disciplines before entering:
1. Never travel alone - minimum group size is three
2. Mark passages using the bone-chalk, but expect markers to move
3. When paths diverge, send only one person to explore while others maintain position
4. If separated, remain stationary and create rhythmic sounds
5. Establish a "reality check" procedure with companions (asking questions only your group would know)

## Communication Guidelines

The entities or forces that inhabit the Undercity respond to patterns and intent rather than specific language. When addressing the space directly:
* Speak in measured, rhythmic patterns matching the water drops
* Phrase requests as observations rather than demands
* If the water-drops form words, acknowledge them but avoid direct answers
* When the building structures seem to shift, verbally express gratitude

## Time Management

Your perception of time will become unreliable. Establish strict protocols:
* Wind a mechanical watch once every 12 hours regardless of what it shows
* Eat at predetermined intervals irrespective of hunger
* Plan to exit after what you estimate to be 72 hours maximum
* If someone experiences time differently (aging faster/slower), exit immediately

## Psychological Safeguards

* Establish a rotation schedule for sleep, with at least one person awake at all times
* Create a system for reality-checking each other when perceptions differ
* Agree upon a clear abort signal if anyone's mental state deteriorates
* Memorize a shared poem or song - reciting it together can reorient the group

## Ethical Considerations

* Take nothing that appears deliberately placed
* Offer something personal before collecting specimens
* Never interrupt the water-song by damming or redirecting flows
* Avoid making permanent marks on structures or vegetation

## Post-Visit Protocols

After leaving the Undercity:
* Quarantine all collected materials for at least one lunar cycle
* Document your experiences immediately, then again after one week
* Monitor for unusual dreams, synesthesia, or heightened sensitivity to sounds
* Have companions verify your perception of time and events in the outside world

## Special Warnings

* If you hear your name spoken by the water when alone, exit the nearest way possible
* Should the fungi cease their bioluminescence simultaneously, remain absolutely still
* If you encounter a mirror-like pool showing a reflection that isn't yours, do not touch the water
* When buildings begin to rearrange themselves while being observed directly (not just when unobserved), an abnormal shift is occurring - retreat to your last safe location

Remember that the Undercity of Dripping Song exists in a state of perpetual becoming. It is not hostile, but neither is it concerned with human limitations. Approach with humility, patience, and the understanding that complete comprehension is neither possible nor the point of your journey.
