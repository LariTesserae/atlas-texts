# Preparation Guide for Visiting Neverseed

## Essential Preparation

### Physical Conditioning
Begin altitude training at least 3 months before your journey. Neverseed's high plateau environment will tax your cardiovascular system, and the unusually dry air will strain your lungs. Daily cardio and breathing exercises are mandatory, not optional.

### Hydration Technology
Standard hydration solutions are insufficient for Neverseed's extreme aridity:
- Pack a closed-loop moisture reclamation suit (military-grade if available)
- Bring at least three high-capacity water purification and storage systems
- Include electrolyte supplements formulated for extreme dehydration conditions
- Consider surgical implantation of a subcutaneous hydration reservoir if staying longer than two weeks

### Protection from the Environment
- UV-protective eyewear with polarized amber lenses to navigate the perpetual twilight
- Reinforced, heat-dissipating boots with obsidian-resistant soles
- Full-body covering with breathable, heat-reflective material
- Dust respirator with sub-micron filtration capability
- Cut-resistant gloves for handling the sharp-edged formations

## Communication and Navigation

### Translation Tools
The inhabitants communicate through vibrational patterns in the obsidian. Bring:
- Contact microphones and amplifiers
- Pattern-recognition software with machine learning capabilities
- Vibrational output devices to attempt basic communication

### Navigation Equipment
- Standard GPS will fail; bring inertial navigation systems
- Mark your path with ultraviolet trail markers
- Carry a three-dimensional mapping device to track the constantly changing landscape
- Include a static-resistant compass that won't be affected by the plateau's unusual properties

## Scientific Equipment

### Sample Collection
- Diamond-tipped collection tools for obsidian and basalt samples
- Isolation containers for the crystalline "vegetation"
- Non-reactive specimen jars with hermetic seals
- Remote collection devices to gather samples during "churns"

### Measuring Instruments
- Seismographs to monitor landscape changes
- Spectrometers to analyze the amber light
- Air composition analyzers
- Thermal imaging equipment to track heat signatures of inhabitants

## Mental Preparation

### Psychological Training
The most overlooked aspect of visiting Neverseed is mental preparation:
- Practice meditation to maintain focus during disorienting experiences
- Study geometric pattern recognition to better comprehend the landscape
- Keep a structured journal to ground yourself when reality seems fluid
- Develop a personal mantra to repeat when the sense of being "watched" becomes overwhelming

### Dream Management
The dream effects of Neverseed can be destabilizing:
- Learn lucid dreaming techniques to maintain some control
- Bring dream suppression medication for emergency use only
- Schedule "reality checks" with teammates three times daily
- Consider bringing a mundane object from home as a tactile anchor

## Social Protocol

### Interacting with Inhabitants
- Move slowly and predictably
- Avoid disrupting their swarm patterns
- Never interfere with their construction activities
- If approached, remain still and adopt a non-threatening posture
- Do not attempt to collect specimens of the inhabitants themselves

### During "Churns"
- Seek stable ground immediately
- Remain completely motionless
- Document but do not interfere with swarm movements
- Be prepared for sudden structural changes in your vicinity

## Contingency Planning

### Emergency Extraction
- Establish a communication schedule with your extraction team
- Carry signal beacons that function in Neverseed's unusual atmosphere
- Memorize at least three routes back to your entry point
- Include emergency rations for twice your planned stay

### Medical Considerations
- Pack treatments for severe dehydration and heat exhaustion
- Include remedies for respiratory irritation from the fine dust
- Bring bandages and sutures for cuts from the sharp obsidian
- Include psychotropic stabilizers in case of extreme disorientation

## Cultural Sensitivity

While not a conventional culture, Neverseed appears to be a complex system with its own internal logic:
- Approach with scientific curiosity but profound respect
- Disturb as little as possible in your observations
- Consider that what appears to be simple technology may be part of sophisticated processes beyond your understanding
- Be mindful that the entire plateau may be a single entity rather than just a location

## Final Notes

No visitor to Neverseed has ever reported feeling truly welcome or unwelcome—merely observed. The plateau's alien nature is not hostile but utterly indifferent to human needs and perceptions. Your survival depends on thorough preparation, constant vigilance, and the humility to acknowledge that you are entering a place where human intuition may be fundamentally unsuited to understanding the rules that govern existence.

Remember: In Neverseed, you are not just visiting a place—you are temporarily becoming part of a process that began long before humanity and will continue long after we are gone.
