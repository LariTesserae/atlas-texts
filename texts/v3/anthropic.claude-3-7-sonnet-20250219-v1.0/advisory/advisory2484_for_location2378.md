# Preparation Guide for Deepwood Heights

## Essential Preparations

### Mental Readiness
The Deepwood Heights requires psychological preparation more than anything else. You will be entering a living, reactive environment that may respond to your thoughts and emotions. Practice meditation and mindfulness techniques before arrival. The ability to maintain calm in disorienting circumstances will be crucial, as anxiety may cause the environment to constrict around you.

### Physical Conditioning
Focus on flexibility and balance training. The pathways shift unpredictably, and you'll need to adapt quickly to surfaces that may tilt, undulate, or transform beneath you. Develop your peripheral vision through specialized exercises, as this will help you notice the shadow-inhabitants.

## Gear Essentials

### Clothing
- Breathable, moisture-wicking fabrics that dry quickly
- Avoid synthetic materials that rustle loudly
- Multiple thin layers rather than few thick ones
- No bright colors or strong patterns that might agitate the environment
- Soft-soled, flexible footwear with excellent grip on wet surfaces
- Waterproof hat with brim to keep the perpetual moisture from dripping into your eyes

### Navigation & Safety
- Compass will likely malfunction; bring three different types of non-electronic navigation tools
- Manual altimeter to track your elevation within the Heights
- Chalk or biodegradable markers to leave temporary trail signs
- Lightweight collapsible walking staff that can test surface stability
- Silk rope (100ft minimum) - less likely to damage or provoke the plant matter
- Emergency whistle (use sparingly as sound may trigger environmental responses)
- Small mirror for signaling or checking around corners without fully exposing yourself

### Sustenance & Health
- Water purification tablets or filter straw (the pooled water may be safe but unpredictable)
- Three days' worth of dense, quiet food (nothing crunchy or strong-smelling)
- Activated charcoal for potential toxin absorption
- Antihistamines and anti-fungal medications
- Small vial of salt (can help neutralize psychoactive compounds if ingested)
- Respiratory mask with changeable filters for areas of heavy spore concentration
- Eye protection against the mist (clear, non-tinted)

### Documentation
- Waterproof notebook and pencils
- Small waterproof camera (no flash photography)
- Audio recorder (keep volume minimal)
- Sample collection vials lined with neutral material

## Behavioral Guidelines

### Movement Protocols
- Walk deliberately but not forcefully; your footsteps are conversations
- When pathways narrow, stop and wait rather than pushing through
- If a structure begins to envelop you, remain calm and still; panicked movement may accelerate absorption
- Ascend and descend gradually to allow your consciousness to acclimate to the potentially varying psychoactive concentrations at different heights
- Rest frequently but never in the same location for more than an hour

### Interaction Rules
- Never cut, break, or damage any part of the Heights
- If you must touch surfaces, do so with open palms, gently
- Whisper if you must speak; silence is preferred
- Approach the shadow-inhabitants obliquely, never directly
- If the moss-like plateaus react strongly to your presence, retreat to a different elevation
- Should you feel unusual mental effects, sit down immediately and focus on concrete memories

### Emergencies
- If passages seal behind you, find still water and wait beside it; new pathways often form near moisture collection
- If experiencing hallucinations, use your salt to ground yourself
- If the environment becomes hostile, offer a small personal item as a "gift" - this sometimes pacifies the reaction
- In case of separation from companions, do not call out; instead, find an elevated position and wait

## Time Management

Plan your exploration in four-hour increments. The Heights' transformations seem to follow loose cycles, and adapting your schedule to these rhythms improves your chances of successful navigation.

The twilight never truly transitions to night or day, but there are subtle shifts in the quality of light that locals (if you can find any) might be able to interpret. Learning these variations can help with timekeeping.

## Final Considerations

Accept that you cannot control this environment. The Deepwood Heights is not a destination but an encounter with something fundamentally other. Those who approach it with humility and wonder tend to fare better than those seeking to conquer or study it aggressively.

Some travelers report that sleeping within the Heights generates prophetic dreams or insights. Others never truly sleep there at all, entering instead a state of heightened awareness. Be prepared for either outcome.

And perhaps most importantly: if you feel an overwhelming urge to remain permanently, to merge with the Heights, give your companion clear authorization beforehand to physically remove you. The line between communion and consumption is vanishingly thin in this remarkable place.
