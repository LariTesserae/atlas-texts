# Preparation Guide for Visiting the Glass Foundries of Silon Parch

## Essential Equipment

**Respiratory Protection:**
- Bring a high-quality respirator with replaceable P100 filters specifically rated for silicate particulates
- Pack a minimum of three times the filters you expect to need
- Consider a full face shield respirator as silicate dust can irritate eyes
- Bring backup filtration systems - the microscopic dust penetrates most standard equipment

**Hearing Protection:**
- Invest in custom-molded earplugs with variable filtration (80-100 dB reduction capability)
- Pack electronic noise-canceling headphones as backup
- Learn the basics of the Silicate Guild's sign language before arrival
- Bring a handheld electronic translator/communicator that can function in high-noise environments

**Clothing:**
- Multiple sets of tightly-woven, high-collar clothing that seals at wrists and ankles
- Nothing loose or flowing that might catch in machinery or collect dust
- Specialized silicate-resistant fabric if available (standard cotton degrades rapidly)
- Head covering that fully encloses hair
- Goggles with side protection and anti-fog coating
- Boots that can be sealed at the ankle and easily cleaned
- Gloves with silicate-resistant coating
- Consider bringing a light exoskeleton support system for long-term stays, as the constant dodging of glasslizards can be physically taxing

**Environmental Adaptation:**
- Skin conditioner specifically formulated for extreme dryness
- Saline nasal spray and eye drops (used hourly)
- Lips will crack within hours - bring specialized balm
- Hydration system with built-in filtration (minimum 4 liters capacity per day)
- Electrolyte supplements to maintain balance in the dry environment
- Digital hygrometer to monitor personal space humidity

## Medical Preparations

**Pre-Visit:**
- Complete pulmonary function tests to establish baseline
- Get certified free of respiratory conditions - the environment will exacerbate any existing issues
- Vaccination against glasslizard-borne parasites (consult a specialist in xenozoological medicine)
- Consider a preventative course of anti-inflammatory medication

**Emergency Kit:**
- Silicate extraction solutions for skin and eyes
- Respiratory emergency medications
- Burn treatments (foundry accidents are common)
- Advanced first aid kit with wound sealing capabilities
- Signal device that functions in high-interference environments

## Cultural Preparation

**Etiquette:**
- Learn the basic hand signals used by workers - attempting to shout over machinery is considered both futile and rude
- Prepare to share meals communally - private dining is seen as antisocial
- Bringing a small, useful tool as a gift demonstrates respect for the community's pragmatism
- Be prepared to contribute to daily maintenance tasks - everyone works in the foundries
- Never express disgust at the presence of glasslizards - they are essential to the community

**Trade Considerations:**
- Bring practical items to trade - tools that resist silicate corrosion are highly valued
- Fresh produce is rare and precious here
- Simple pleasures like spices or preserved fruits make exceptional gifts
- Do not bring water as a gift - they have their own carefully balanced systems

## Practical Advice

- Schedule your visit to coincide with a harvest cycle if you wish to observe the most active period
- Alternatively, aim for the quieter days between cycles if you prefer more interaction with residents
- Expect to adjust your sleep cycle - work continues around the clock with reduced shifts during "night"
- The Guild maintains visitor quarters, but they will be smaller than you're accustomed to
- Privacy is a limited concept here - prepare for shared spaces and constant proximity to others
- Your skin will absorb fine silicate dust regardless of precautions - this faint marking is temporary (usually)
- The purple glow of the plains can disrupt circadian rhythms - bring a sleep mask

## Warning Signs to Watch For

- Persistent cough with glittering phlegm requires immediate medical attention
- Blurred vision with purple halos indicates dangerous silicate exposure
- If glasslizards begin avoiding a particular area, follow their example - they sense structural instabilities
- A sudden drop in ambient noise means equipment has failed and pressure may be building

Remember: The Foundries of Silon Parch exist in a delicate balance with their environment. Your presence alters that balance. Tread lightly, listen carefully, and follow the lead of those who call this remarkable place home.
