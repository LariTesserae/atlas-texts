# Preparation Guide for The Suspended Cultivation (Stratum Eleven, Hanging City of Keldai)

## Essential Preparations

### Physical Gear

* **Gravity stabilizers** - Small personal devices worn at the waist or ankles to counteract the disorienting effects of the floating platforms and potential reality "slips"
* **Respiratory supplements** - The engineered atmosphere may contain unfamiliar compounds; bring oxygen boosters and particulate filters
* **Full-spectrum adaptive eyewear** - The dim lighting conditions require vision enhancement, while the occasional spatial anomalies necessitate lenses that can adjust to sudden changes in light or perspective
* **Moisture-wicking, quick-dry clothing** - The 70-minute misting cycles will leave you regularly dampened
* **Non-slip footwear with magnetic anchoring** - Essential for navigating the thin bridges and potentially unstable surfaces
* **Temporal anchor** - A small device synchronized to your home reality's time-space coordinates; will help reorient you if caught in a "slip"
* **Recording equipment resistant to spatial anomalies** - Standard cameras often malfunction during slips; bring analog backups or specially shielded digital devices

### Biological Preparations

* **Inner ear stabilization treatment** - A week before departure, begin taking supplements to strengthen your vestibular system against the disorientation of multiple hovering platforms and occasional reality shifts
* **Chrono-immunization** - If available in your reality, this treatment helps protect cellular processes from temporal inconsistencies
* **Allergen screening** - The engineered plants and pollinators may produce proteins your immune system has never encountered

## Mental Preparation

* **Reality anchoring meditation** - Practice daily for at least two weeks before arrival
* **Spatial disorientation training** - If possible, spend time in VR simulations that replicate the sensation of being surrounded by floating platforms at varying depths
* **Time dilation awareness exercises** - Mental techniques to maintain your sense of linear time during potential slips
* **Dream journaling** - Begin recording your dreams; many travelers report that dream-logic helps them process the semi-real nature of Stratum Eleven

## Cultural Considerations

* **Worker respect protocols** - The cultivation staff face daily dangers; learn appropriate acknowledgments and avoid disrupting their routines
* **Stratum-specific terminology** - Memorize key terms like "extrusions," "slips," and platform designation numbers
* **Reality event etiquette** - If you witness a slip, follow the lead of locals; never point, shout, or try to interact with phasing objects/people
* **Harvest ceremonies** - If you're fortunate to visit during a harvest, bring a small token to leave at the collection basins as thanks

## Practical Advice

* **Movement planning** - The cable car system follows strict schedules; plan your explorations around their timetables or risk long waits
* **Rest cycles** - Synchronize your sleep schedule with the dome's opacity cycle for at least three days before arrival
* **Emergency protocols** - Memorize the locations of reality stabilization chambers placed throughout the stratum
* **Local guides** - Arrange accompaniment by someone who has experienced slips before; their guidance during an event is invaluable
* **Food and water** - Despite assurances, avoid consuming the color-changing fruits unless you've built tolerance through gradual exposure

## What to Expect During a "Slip"

Despite official denials, reality slips are a documented phenomenon in Stratum Eleven. If you experience one:

1. **Remain calm** - Panic accelerates subjective time dilation
2. **Activate your temporal anchor** immediately
3. **Stand still** rather than attempting to move through visually shifting spaces
4. **Close your eyes if necessary** - Some travelers find that relying on senses other than vision helps maintain stability
5. **Breathe slowly** and recite your home coordinates
6. **Accept the experience** - Fighting against the slip typically worsens its effects

## Ethical Considerations

* The Hanging City of Keldai exists in a precarious balance with natural laws. Consider whether your visit contributes positively to their experimental ecosystem.
* Some multiverse ethicists argue that observing the slips may actually increase their frequency. Be mindful of your observer effect.
* The engineered pollinators and other fauna are evolving in ways unforeseen by their creators - respect their adaptation process.

## Post-Visit Integration

Many travelers report lingering effects after visiting Stratum Eleven:
* Temporal afterimages
* Changed relationship to gravity
* Dreams of floating gardens
* Heightened sensitivity to atmospheric moisture

Schedule decompression time after your visit - at least three days in a reality with stable physical laws before attempting any further multiverse travel.

Remember that the Suspended Cultivation is both technological marvel and ontological experiment. Approach with wonder, but never forget that even breathing is an act of participation in this delicately balanced ecosystem.
