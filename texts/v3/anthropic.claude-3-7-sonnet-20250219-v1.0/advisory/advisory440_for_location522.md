# Visitor's Guide to The Primordial Heights

## Essential Preparation

### Physical Requirements
- **Oxygen supplementation**: Bring a reliable oxygen delivery system with redundant backups. The air is dangerously thin at these extreme altitudes, and conventional portable oxygen may behave unpredictably. Consider a closed-circuit rebreather with at least 72 hours of capacity.
- **Extreme cold protection**: Pack expedition-grade cold weather gear designed for high-altitude mountaineering. Layer systems are essential, as the temperature appears stable but your perception of it may shift with reality fluctuations.
- **Waterproof everything**: The omnipresent moisture will infiltrate conventional gear. Use vacuum-sealed containers for electronics and documents.
- **Specialized footwear**: Boots with exceptional grip for the glassy, polished stone surfaces. The ground may be unexpectedly slippery despite appearing dry.

### Navigation Tools
- **Redundant non-electronic compasses**: Bring at least three. Electronic navigation systems will likely malfunction due to reality distortions.
- **Physical markers**: Bring lightweight, brightly colored markers (flags, paint, chalk) to leave trails. The folding geography means you cannot rely on landmarks.
- **Analog timepieces**: Multiple mechanical watches to track subjective versus objective time. Wind them at different intervals to detect temporal anomalies.
- **Physical tether system**: Consider a specialized retractable cord system to anchor yourself when traversing areas where space folds unpredictably.

### Communication
- **Do not rely on electronic communication**: Any radio or digital signals will likely be absorbed, distorted, or simply vanish.
- **Consider mechanical sound amplification**: Simple acoustic devices may help establish communication over distances, though sound behaves strangely here.
- **Visual signaling equipment**: Mirrors, colored smoke, or flags may be more reliable than sound-based communication.

### Sustenance
- **Water purification**: Despite the pools appearing pristine, their connection to other realities makes their composition unpredictable. Bring comprehensive filtration.
- **Calorie-dense food**: Your body will burn energy at an accelerated rate due to the altitude and possible temporal fluctuations. Pack twice what you think you need.
- **Sealed containers**: The mist may alter exposed food in unpredictable ways.

## Mental & Cognitive Preparation

### Perceptual Training
- **Practice meditation techniques**: Specifically those that anchor you to your body's sensations. Reality distortions can cause profound disorientation.
- **Study non-Euclidean geometry**: Familiarize yourself with concepts of spatial distortion to better comprehend the architecture.
- **Sensory deprivation training**: Prepare for the unnatural quiet and the way sounds terminate abruptly.
- **Visual adaptation exercises**: The crystal forests' light refractions may cause visual hallucinations or disorientation.

### Psychological Readiness
- **Accept profound isolation**: Even if traveling with companions, the nature of the place creates an unusual psychological solitude.
- **Prepare for time dilation experiences**: Hours may feel like minutes or days; maintain routines regardless of perceived time.
- **Develop reality-grounding rituals**: Simple acts like counting breaths or reciting memorized texts can help maintain your cognitive framework when reality seems fluid.
- **Establish decision-making protocols**: In moments of confusion, having predetermined guidelines will help overcome decision paralysis.

## Interaction Guidelines

### With the Environment
- **Never drink directly from the pools**: Despite their perfect circularity and apparent purity, they connect to unknown realms.
- **Avoid prolonged contact with the crystal "vegetation"**: While visually compelling, extended exposure to their refracting patterns may alter cognition.
- **Do not attempt to take samples from the plateau itself**: The stone may respond unpredictably to extraction attempts.
- **Move deliberately and consistently**: Maintain a steady pace rather than stopping and starting, as this may help normalize spatial distortions.

### With "Inhabitants"
- **Observe from a distance**: The intention-entities appear focused on their own activities and may not recognize you as conscious.
- **Mimic rather than initiate**: If interaction becomes necessary, gentle mimicry of their movements may be safer than novel gestures.
- **Avoid interrupting their work**: The tools they use and materials they manipulate exist in multiple dimensions simultaneously.
- **Do not attempt linguistic communication**: Their "language" in crystal harmonics operates on principles beyond human vocalization.

## Emergency Protocols

### Reality Distortion Response
- If you experience severe spatial folding (distances suddenly expanding or contracting), stop moving immediately and establish physical contact with the ground.
- When time distortions occur (sun jumping position), focus on your mechanical timepiece and count breaths until stability returns.
- If you observe your companions aging rapidly or behaving inconsistently, do not touch them. Maintain verbal contact only until the anomaly passes.

### Disorientation Management
- Carry a small, heavy object with personal significance. Focusing on its weight and texture can help reorient you when reality feels thin.
- If the mist reveals "other plateaus," do not attempt to reach them regardless of how accessible they appear.
- Should you find yourself in a building that recursively folds, always choose downward paths when possible.

### Extraction
- Plan multiple extraction points and times before arrival.
- Establish objective criteria for emergency departure (oxygen levels, food supply, mental state assessments).
- Accept that departure may require passing through the same reality fluctuations as arrival; prepare accordingly.

## Final Considerations

The Primordial Heights exists at the boundary of comprehensible reality. Your experience there will be fundamentally transformative. Many travelers report a persistent sense that they've left something behind—or brought something back—that cannot be articulated. Keep a journal of your subjective experiences, but don't be surprised if the writing changes between entries or if you find notes you don't remember recording.

Remember that in a place where reality has been worn thin by eons of erosion, your certainty must remain your strongest tool. Move with purpose, observe with clarity, and maintain your human perspective as your most valuable anchor to your home reality.
