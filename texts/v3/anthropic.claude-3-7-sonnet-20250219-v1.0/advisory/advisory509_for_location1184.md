# Preparation Guide for Visiting the Undershelves

## Essential Preparations

### Physical Training
* Practice balancing on unstable surfaces - the basket platforms tilt when weight is unevenly distributed
* Develop core strength and flexibility for navigating between differently spaced baskets
* Train for moving in confined spaces where the ceiling and floor might be within arm's reach
* Practice silent, deliberate movements to minimize disturbance to the environment

### Gear

**Clothing:**
* Lightweight, breathable garments suitable for constant warm humidity
* Nothing loose or dangling that could catch on basket weaves
* Minimal layers - the temperature is consistently warm
* Consider moisture-wicking fabrics that dry quickly
* Barefoot or thin-soled footwear with excellent grip for basket surfaces

**Tools:**
* Compact LED headlamp with red-light mode (minimal disruption to ambient lighting)
* Small, waterproof notebook and pencil for observations
* Collapsible walking staff to test basket stability before committing weight
* Moisture-resistant camera with low-light capabilities
* Small mesh bags to collect any samples (only if permitted)
* Waterproof pouches for electronics and personal items

**Supplies:**
* Water purification drops (the flowing water might be safe but verify first)
* Concentrated, minimal-waste food rations
* Anti-fungal powder or cream (constant moisture may cause skin issues)
* Small first-aid kit with waterproof packaging

## Navigation and Movement

* Always maintain three points of contact when traversing between baskets
* Distribute your weight evenly when standing on a basket platform
* Move slowly and deliberately - quick movements may destabilize the structures
* Test each basket before fully committing your weight
* Observe the amphibian creatures' movement patterns - they know the safest routes
* Map your progress using the distinct weaving patterns of different basket sections
* Use the water channel patterns as navigation markers

## Ecological Etiquette

* Do not attempt to redirect water channels - they maintain the entire ecosystem
* Avoid touching the luminescent moss - oils from skin may damage it
* Never collect the amphibian creatures - they are essential system maintainers
* If you accidentally damage a basket structure, move aside to allow the creatures to repair it
* Speak softly or use hand signals - excessive noise may disrupt the delicate acoustic balance
* Carry out everything you bring in - there is no natural decay process for foreign materials
* Respect the rhythmic water dripping - do not attempt to alter the patterns

## Cultural and Scientific Considerations

* Bring recording equipment to document the mathematical patterns in the water dripping
* Set aside time for silent observation - the coordinated movements of the amphibians reveal much about this realm's organization
* Consider the possibility that the Undershelves may be a living entity or conscious system
* Practice mindfulness techniques - the unchanging nature of the environment can be psychologically disorienting
* Prepare for potential time perception distortions due to the constant dusk and unchanging conditions

## Safety Precautions

* Always travel with a companion and maintain visual contact
* Establish a "base basket" from which to explore and return regularly
* Create a system to mark your path - the similar appearance of baskets makes disorientation likely
* Be cautious about the edges of basket platforms - some may have unexpected flexibility
* Test water samples before consuming any moisture from the environment
* Move away immediately if large numbers of amphibians begin gathering near you
* Prepare for the psychological effects of constant dusk and the never-changing environment

## Time Management

* Bring a reliable timepiece - without day/night cycles, your circadian rhythm will be disrupted
* Schedule regular rest periods - the constant sameness can lead to mental fatigue
* Plan a definite departure time before entering - the timeless nature of the realm can make it easy to lose track

Remember: The Undershelves exist in perfect equilibrium. Your presence is a disruption, so minimize your impact and approach with reverence. This realm operates by rules different from our own, maintaining a perfect balance that has persisted outside normal time. Your goal should be to observe without altering, to learn without disturbing the ancient, delicate harmony of this woven world.
