To step into the Green Foundry is to enter an organ, not a room. As a traveler, you must abandon the notion of "visiting" a place; here, you are merely a transient nutrient passing through a system that is indifferent to your presence. The Foundry operates on a geological and biological timescale that makes human perception feel like a strobe light in a dark, silent chamber.

Here is how you must prepare to survive and respect the rhythm of the damp.

### 1. Physiological Preparation: The Seal
The air in the Foundry is not merely humid; it is a caustic, nutrient-rich fog. It is heavily saturated with particulate matter—spores, sap-aerosols, and the microscopic detritus of "digested" metal. 
*   **Respiratory Protection:** Do not rely on standard gas masks; the biological acidic content of the mist will eat through synthetic rubber filters within the hour. You require a high-grade, sealed rebreather (a closed-circuit system) with a humidity-resistant canister. If you inhale the mist directly, the spores will effectively attempt to graft onto the lining of your lungs, causing a rapid, suffocating growth of lichen.
*   **Dermal Protection:** Any exposed skin will, within minutes, become a site of attachment. Wear an impermeable slicker—think deep-sea gear, but reinforced with non-stick fluoropolymer coatings. You want nothing for the root-fibers to "grab." Even static electricity in your gear can attract the creeping, sensitive tendrils that feel for conductive materials.

### 2. Gear: The Burden of Dryness
The environment is a massive hydraulic machine. It will try to "process" your equipment by integrating it into the cycle.
*   **Equipment Neutrality:** Strip yourself of anything made of copper, brass, or iron. The Foundry is a "thief"—its filaments are magnetically and chemically drawn to these metals, which it interprets as nutrients to be broken down. If you must carry equipment, it should be fashioned from high-density ceramics, carbon-fiber, or inert polymers.
*   **The "Weighted" Walk:** You will be stepping on a mat of "moss-flesh." If you walk with the quick, erratic pace of an animal, you will trigger the site’s self-defense, causing the substrate to tighten around your boots like a quicksand sphincter. You must learn to walk with a **rhythmic, slow-gait**. Mimic the pace of the piston-stalks. If you match the low-frequency thrum of the ground, the system will perceive you as "part of the machine" rather than a foreign object to be dissolved.
*   **Illumination:** Do not use artificial light; the phosphorescent moss is sensitive to high-intensity spectrums and will react by bleaching or secreting a hardening wax in your direction. Use dim, red-spectrum LED cycling lamps. 

### 3. Mental Reconaissance: The "Finished" State
The most dangerous error a traveler can make is to try and "help" the Foundry or "study" it too aggressively. The Foundry is not broken; it is not a ruin. It is an optimized, self-contained organism that has reached a state of stasis. 
*   **Do not touch the valves:** When you see a bronze valve or a gear, it is tempting to see yourself as an explorer uncovering a lost laboratory. You are not. That valve is a functional part of the digestive tract. Turning it could cause a pressure surge that would result in a violent discharge of pressurized, boiling sap. Observe, but keep your hands folded behind your back.
*   **The Scent of Stasis:** The heavy, sweet-rotting smell is your warning signal. If the scent increases in potency, you are standing too close to a transition cycle. The Foundry will begin to pulse faster to clear a "clog." If you feel the vibration in your teeth, move slowly away—do not run. Movement out of rhythm identifies you as a parasite.

### 4. Psychological Conditioning
When you are inside, you will experience an intense feeling of being "watched"—not by eyes, but by the floor, the walls, and the pistons. The Foundry is a giant, slow-motion awareness. It knows you are there as much as your own body knows a stray splinter is in your skin. 

Prepare for the **"Biological Silence."** Because there is no animal life, your own heartbeat will sound like a drum solo. If you cannot regulate your pulse, you will feel panicked. Practice rhythmic breathing in the time of the piston stalks (roughly 12 beats per minute). 

### Summary Checklist:
1.  **Closed-circuit respirator:** Vital.
2.  **Fluoropolymer-coated attire:** Prevents lichen attachment.
3.  **Ceramic/Carbon-fiber tools:** No copper or iron allowed.
4.  **Red-spectrum lighting:** Low-intensity only.
5.  **A stopwatch:** To keep your pulse and step in sync with the Foundry's 5-second hydraulic cycle.

**The Golden Rule:** When you leave, assume that your boots have collected spores that are now hitchhiking on your gear. Sterilize everything in a chemical bath before exiting the transit corridor. You are bringing the Green Foundry with you, whether you intend to or not. Treat yourself as a contamination hazard; the Foundry does not want to keep you, but it doesn't mind letting you carry its seeds into the rest of the multiverse.
