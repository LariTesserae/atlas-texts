To step into a space where physics is still drafting its foundational laws is to subject your human biology to a paradox: you are the most primitive, static thing in the room. You are a coherent, legacy-software organism walking into a room where the source code is still being rewritten by a compiler that hasn't finished the boot sequence.

Preparing for this location requires more than a backpack of gear; it requires a systematic dismantling of your reliance on Newtonian constants.

### 1. Physical Preparation: The "Rigid" Self
Because the environment is a laboratory of potentiality, your body is at risk of "bleeding" into the room's fluidity. You must minimize your biological footprint.

*   **Weighted Under-Suit:** Do not wear loose clothing. Wear a compression suit lined with high-density lead or tungsten mesh. The extra weight serves as a "gravitational anchor," reminding your atoms of their original commitment to downward direction.
*   **The Anchor-Point Kit:** Carry a small collection of obsidian cairn-equivalents. If you find your own sense of "down" wavering, placing a heavy, dense object on the floor is the only way to signal to the room that you intend to occupy a specific, non-shifting space.
*   **Sensory Filtering:** The "piercing white radiance" is not just light; it is information. It will overwhelm your optic nerve if you view it directly. Wear goggles with a variable-opacity polarizing filter, specifically tuned to block ultra-violet and the rhythmic pulse frequencies. You do not want your brain to attempt to sync your internal REM cycle with the pulse of the chamber.

### 2. Cognitive Preparation: The "Negative Capability"
The greatest danger in this crystalline geometry is attempting to *understand* what you see. If you try to map the geometry-shifters to biological animals, your mind will snap under the strain of their non-Euclidean movement.

*   **Abandon Causality:** Accept that a dropped object may rise. If you reach for something and your hand is suddenly three inches to the left, **do not fight it.** Panic is a form of kinetic energy that this environment loves to consume. If you panic, you provide "state" for the environment to rearrange. Maintain a meditative, near-catatonic state of non-attachment. Treat your body as a visitor, not a participant.
*   **Acoustic Buffer:** The "compression of too many sounds" is a pressure wave that can cause localized hemorrhaging in the inner ear. Use active noise-canceling earplugs, but be warned: the silence you create is a vacuum that the room will try to fill with its own logic. Focus on a repetitive, internal mantra—perhaps a prime number sequence—to maintain the "solid" narrative of your own existence.

### 3. Safety and Survival
You are effectively visiting a construction site where the "beams" are made of logic rather than steel.

*   **The "Grounding" Protocol:** Never touch the calcified threads. They are breathing, processing, and restructuring. If you touch one, you may become part of the trade; you might find your memories replaced by a sequence of notes, or your left hand simplified into a shade of violet. 
*   **Avoid the Cairn Traps:** While the obsidian cairns are "grounding rods," they are also markers of previous travelers who failed to leave. Keep a respectful distance. If you see a cairn, treat it as a cemetery. Do not dismantle them; they are the only things keeping the local floor-logic from manifesting as a sinkhole.
*   **The "State" Exchange:** If a geometry-shifter approaches you, stand perfectly still. They are seeking to "consume state." If you are rigid, boring, and emotionally flat, they will find nothing of value in you and drift onward. If you are full of awe, fear, or profound creative electricity, you are a feast. **Be an empty vessel.**

### 4. The Exit Strategy
Before you enter, mark your entry point with a tether. Because the space is non-Euclidean, "backwards" is not always where you think it is. 

*   **Don’t look back:** When you are ready to leave, walk at a constant, moderate speed. Do not glance over your shoulder at the way the light clusters rearrange themselves the moment you vacate a space. If you perceive the change, you become anchored to the "before" and "after," which may result in a temporal stutter—you could arrive home seconds, or decades, after you left.

**Final Advice:**
Enter with the intent of a rock. Leave with the silence of a stone. Do not try to bring a souvenir home; whatever you take from that room will eventually "decide" to become something else—perhaps a glass shard in your pocket, or perhaps a realization in your mind that you can no longer explain in words. You are walking through the womb of a universe. Do not disturb the gestation.
