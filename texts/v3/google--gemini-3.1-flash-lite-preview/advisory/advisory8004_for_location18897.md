To visit the interior of the obsidian monolith is to trespass upon the drafting table of creation. You are not visiting a destination; you are intruding upon a process.

Here is how to prepare for your journey into the crease.

### 1. Physical Augmentation: The "Hull" Suit
Standard environmental suits will not suffice. The atmosphere is a viscous, ionized argon-silicate slurry that will act like abrasive liquid sandpaper on traditional materials. 
*   **Material:** You require a suit plated in **non-reflective, heat-sink ceramic.** Anything with high reflectivity will only focus the scorching heat of the localized furnaces inward. 
*   **Seals:** The ground is composed of jagged, razor-sharp glass; your boots must be tungsten-infused, mag-locked, and capable of adjusting to the fluctuating, localized gravity.
*   **Internal Buffering:** Because the vacuum of this pocket-dimension is "under construction," your suit needs a **Localized Reality-Anchor (LRA).** This device, strapped to your thoracic cavity, emits a constant feedback loop of your own cellular identity. Without it, the space’s tendency to "echo" or multiply objects might cause your own physiology to drift, potentially resulting in your limbs existing in two coordinates simultaneously. 

### 2. Sensory Calibration: The Auditory Filter
The "industrial hum" described is not just sound—it is tectonic, low-frequency pressure. Over a sustained period, this vibration will cause human internal organs to harmonize and potentially liquefy or vibrate out of place.
*   **The Filter:** You must wear **active-canceling acoustic dampers** that specifically target the sub-bass drone of the construction. 
*   **The Benefit:** By tuning out the "engine of creation," you allow your senses to focus on the *tink-tink* of the cooling glass. These sounds are your only warning for shifts in the terrain. When the humming stops, the geography is rearranging itself. Listen for the silence; it is the most dangerous thing in this world.

### 3. Navigation: The "Botanical" Radar
The vegetation—the chrome-plated, industrial orchids—are the most stable points in the geography. They are "circuited" into the bedrock. 
*   **Protocol:** Do not treat them as flora; treat them as **navigational buoys.** A single chrome orchid represents a "fixed" point in the spatial glitching. 
*   **Movement:** Your path must only ever connect one orchid to the next. When you cross the vast, barren plains, you are crossing a "dead zone" where gravity is a mere suggestion. Use a tether if possible, but be warned: if you tether to a drifting echo of a vine, you will be pulled into a recursive loop of non-space. Only tether to the solids.

### 4. Psychological Conditioning: The "Observer’s Stillness"
The greatest risk to a human traveler is the **"Construction Paradox."** The world is building itself; it is profoundly patient. If you bring an agitated, frantic, or hungry mind into this space, you will find the environment mirroring your instability.
*   **Meditative Preparation:** You must learn to synchronize your breathing with the tectonic drone you have dampened. If your biological rhythm conflicts with the "industrial hum" of the plains, the world may treat you as a foreign impurity. In geology, impurities are ground away, fused into the slag, or squeezed out of existence. 
*   **Mantra:** Treat yourself as a statue. Do not reach out to touch the copper-lace leaves. Their serrations are not just sharp; they are *molecularly incomplete*. Contact with them can initiate a "transfer of state," where the copper begins to lace into your glove, and then into your hand, as the world tries to build *you* into the landscape.

The obsidian monolith does not want to be explored. It is a lonely factory working in the dark. Treat every step as an intrusion, keep your eyes on the copper leaves, and **never assume a surface will be there when you put your foot down.** 

If the air begins to taste of metallic ozone, leave immediately. The construction project has sensed you, and it has already begun to factor in a way to "recycle" your presence into the next layer of glass.
