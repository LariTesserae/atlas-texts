To step onto the surface of this world is to realize that you are not a traveler visiting a planet, but a flea walking across the skin of a dreaming titan. You are a biological anomaly in a landscape that has long since moved past the necessity of life as you define it. The air is too thin, the scale is too vast, and the silence—despite the rhythmic, internal drone—is heavy with the weight of geological patience.

Preparation is not about survival gear in the traditional sense; it is about psychological and metabolic armor. Here is how you must ready yourself.

### 1. The Physiological Adjustment: "Porous Calibration"
The air is thin and tastes of ozone and pressurized sap. Your lungs will struggle. 
*   **Atmospheric Filtering:** Bring a pressurized mask that uses a closed-loop scrubbing system. The ambient spore clouds are bioluminescent and likely highly reactive to human chemistry. If you breathe in the wrong spore, you risk becoming an involuntary participant in the world’s cellular architecture. Do not allow your skin to touch the substrate for too long; the "osmotic pressure" the fauna use to feed is not native to your biology, but the landscape will indiscriminately attempt to "digest" anything that presses into it.
*   **The Internal Thrum:** The low-frequency drone of the sap-tunnels is not just sound; it is a tectonic pulse. Prolonged exposure will cause "harmonic vertigo." Equip ear-dampeners modified for infrasonic canceling. Without them, your inner ear will vibrate in sympathy with the planet’s circulation, leading to nausea, bleeding from the nose, and, eventually, a terrifying sense of dissociation where you feel your own heartbeat syncopating with the world's ancient metabolism.

### 2. Gear: The Aesthetics of Non-Aggression
Do not bring heavy, industrial, or clunky technology. The ecosystem is a minimalist, perfected machine. 
*   **Footwear:** Wear footwear with soft, silicone, or gel-polymer soles. Hard boots will ring out on the cellulose lattice like a drum, and here, sound is information. If you broadcast your location, you may attract the attention of the "immune system"—the multi-jointed arthropods. If they perceive you as a pathogen, they will not attack; they will simply begin to "repair" the space around you, weaving translucent pith over your path until you are encased in a new architectural expansion.
*   **Lighting:** Your artificial lights will be blindingly harsh in a world designed for soft, violet-spore illumination. Use low-intensity, diffuse lighting in the ultraviolet spectrum. You want to blend in, not be a beacon.
*   **Non-reactive Clothing:** Wear smooth, seamless fabrics. Avoid zippers, buckles, or Velcro, which could snag on the fiber-ridges. You want to be frictionless.

### 3. Psychological Preparation: The "Time-Scale" Reset
The greatest danger is not death; it is the loss of your human sense of temporal urgency. 
*   **The Abandonment of Purpose:** You are used to a world of growth and decay. In this place, everything is an eternal, static perfection. When you stand on a ridge and look at a plaza, you may feel an urge to *do* something—to explore a doorway or map a corridor. Understand that these corridors were not built for walking; they were exuded for systemic flow. To explore them is to potentially step into a chamber that is only "open" during the planet’s digestive cycle.
*   **The Witness Mentality:** You must move with the grace of an observer. Do not attempt to take samples. The ground is "thinking," and it tracks changes in its composition with high precision. If you chip away at a rib-arch, the nanoscopic filaments will begin to knit the damage back together within minutes, potentially trapping your tools—or your arm—inside the lattice.

### 4. Navigating the "Living City"
Do not try to navigate by geometry. The city rearranges itself not through construction, but through hormonal signaling. When the spore-clouds shift color from violet to a deep, bruised violet, that is a hormonal shift. The ground is *breathing*.
*   **If the terrain begins to warm:** Find a high, narrow ridge immediately. The "rooms" are expanding. The transition from a plaza to a wall can happen in minutes, and if you are in the wrong place, you will find yourself assimilated into the wall’s structure. 
*   **Watch the arthropods:** They are the immune system. They move with purpose. If they begin to skitter toward a specific junction, *follow them at a distance.* They are moving toward a nutrient source or a maintenance flux; they are your only reliable pathfinders.

### 5. The "Departure Protocol"
When you are ready to leave, do not look back. There is a narcotic quality to the silence and the dim light that makes humans want to lie down and let the cellulose lattice grow over them. It is the temptation of becoming a part of something that will last another trillion years. 

Check your suit for spores before sealing the transport. If you bring a single spore of this world home, it will begin to "improve" your ship’s hull, trying to turn your environment into a living geode. 

**Remember:** You are a ghost passing through a machine that is waiting for entropy to finish its meal. Walk softly, breathe cautiously, and never mistake the rhythmic thrum of the floor for a heartbeat that belongs to you.
