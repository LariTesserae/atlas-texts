To visit Oor is to trade the frantic, linear existence of a standard terrestrial life for the slow, saturated pulse of a living lung. It is not a place you "travel to" so much as a place you allow yourself to sink into. You are moving from a world of hard edges and fast time into a world of osmosis and velvet endurance.

Here is your guide to preparing for a descent into the bruising violet twilight of Oor.

### 1. Physical Preparation: The Physiology of Pressure
Your body’s metabolism is accustomed to thin air and brisk movement. Oor will shock you with its density.
*   **Gradual Acclimatization:** Do not arrive expecting to exert yourself. A week before your departure, begin practicing "slow-motion" exercises. In Oor, you must learn to conserve your kinetic energy. Any erratic motion creates turbulence, and in a city held together by tethered vines and fibrous tension, a sudden jump could bruise the very floor you stand on.
*   **The Weight of Dampness:** Because the air is a "teuped soup" of moisture, your skin will never feel dry again. Prepare for high-permeability clothing. Cotton is your enemy—it will become a heavy, sodden weight. You must source **bio-synthetic mesh** or **cured sea-leather garments** similar to what the locals wear. These materials allow water to wane through without soaking the garment’s integrity.
*   **Ear Care:** Because the air is thick, the sound waves will vibrate your internal organs more than your eardrums. You may experience a feeling of "thrumming" in your chest. Carry soft wax earplugs for sleep; the sound of the atmosphere compressing against the gourd-husk walls can be deafening to a newcomer, sounding like a slow, rhythmic thumping of a giant heart.

### 2. Gear: The Tools of the Drifter
Forget your electronics. Even if they are shielded, the moisture will eventually compromise them. Oor is a world of organic chemistry; your technology must be equally primitive and patient.
*   **Heat-Sources for Illumination:** Since light is attracted to heat, bring a high-quality, long-burning heat pack or a small, self-contained thermal catalyst. If you remain still for too long, the phosphorescent spores will drift away, leaving you in absolute dark. A small, regulated heat source will ensure a "cradle" of light follows you wherever you walk.
*   **Nutrient Paste:** You cannot forage here without knowing exactly which fungi are building and which are rotting. Pack high-density, amino-acid-rich gels. Expect to offer these as a gesture of goodwill; in a city that maintains its architecture through the application of nutrients, to gift paste is to gift the literal preservation of someone’s home.
*   **A "Soft-Tread" Kit:** You will need boots with soles made of permeable suction-pads—designed to grip the shifting, mossy scaffolding without cutting into it. If you puncture a fungal mat, you risk an invasive infection of the architecture. Treat the ground as if it were a living skin.

### 3. Psychological Preparation: The Myth of Stability
The hardest adjustment is the lack of "finality." In Oor, nothing is ever finished. 
*   **Abandon the "Goal":** If you enter Oor with a destination or a deadline, you will fail. The architecture ripples under your feet; the pathways shift as the fungal stalks grow. You must view navigation as a dance with the landscape. If the path you intended to take has shifted downward, accept it. The environment is "becoming" — you must become with it.
*   **The Cathedral-Hush:** There is a heavy, almost religious tranquility to Oor. Do not raise your voice. In the thick, humid air, a shout is a violent act—it creates ripples of pressure that can be felt by others miles away. Practice communicating in low, rhythmic tones. 
*   **Watch the Silk-Gliders:** If you feel disoriented, look for the Silk-Gliders. Their slow, methodical orbits are the only reliable "map" in the city. If they shift their altitude, it usually indicates a change in the moisture-density of the clouds ahead. Do not chase them; follow the current they choose.

### 4. Essential Behavioral Etiquette
*   **The Greeting of the Spores:** In Oor, people gather together specifically to create heat and summon the light. If you see a group of residents gathered in the violet gloom, approach slowly. Do not interrupt their chant. Stand on the perimeter, allow your heat to warm the local air, and watch as the spores brighten to welcome your presence into the collective glow.
*   **Maintenance as Prayer:** You will see residents massaging nutrient pastes into the walls of their gourd-homes. If invited, do not refuse to assist. It is the social currency of the planet. To help someone harden their dwelling is the Oorian equivalent of a handshake or a conversation.
*   **The "Slow-Walk":** Move as if you are moving through water. Your center of gravity should never shift rapidly. You are not walking toward a place; you are drifting as part of the atmosphere.

**A Final Warning:**
Do not look for the solid ground beneath the mist. When you stare down into the crystalline spires and the abyss far below, the "gravity-afterthought" of Oor may trick your brain into feeling you are falling. You are not falling. You are suspended. Relax your muscles, listen to the thumping of the dense air, and trust that the fungal mats are currently growing to support your weight. 

You are a guest in a monument that is still dreaming itself into being. Treat it with the quiet, breathless patience of a dreamer, and Oor will hold you.
