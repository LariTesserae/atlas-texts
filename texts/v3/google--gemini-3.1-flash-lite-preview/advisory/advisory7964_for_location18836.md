Here is how you must prepare.

### 1. Physical Augmentation: Dampening and Shielding

### 2. Optical Defense: The Anti-Glare Veil
Because the world is a multi-faceted mirror of polished metal, there are no shadows to provide visual rest. Your retinas will be assaulted by recursive reflections.

### 3. Thermal Insulation: Resisting the "Diamond Chill"
The room acts as a heat sink. It is not cold air; it is the absence of energy consumption. The environment seeks to equalize with you by pulling the thermal energy out of your blood.
*   **Active Thermal Scaffolding:** Wear a suit with a closed-loop heating system that circulates warm fluid through a mesh against your skin. You are literally fighting to keep your entropy higher than the environment's. ### 4. Psychological Preparation: The Anti-Stillness Protocol
The human brain is wired to find patterns in motion. In a space where everything is "fixed," the brain will hallucinate. It will try to convince you that the gears are turning, that the walls are closing in, or that the mineral lichen is moving.
*   **The "Variable" Counter-Measure:** Carry a chaotic object—a pouch of sand, a fluid lava lamp, or a digital metronome that does not sync with the room's hum. You need a focal point of genuine, un-patterned movement to remind your brain that you are an organic, changing entity.

You will be pinned to the wall, a permanent part of the machine's anatomy.

You are an error in this room. The machine is holding its breath. Every second you spend there, the "pressure" of the potential energy around you is pushing against your existence.
