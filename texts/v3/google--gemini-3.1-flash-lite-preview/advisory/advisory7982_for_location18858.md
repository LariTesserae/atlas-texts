To visit the High Mat is not to travel to a new continent, but to intrude upon the surface of an organism that has long ago ceased to care about the concept of "guests." Because the Mat is a planetary lung, the primary risk for a human is not physical danger—there is little aggression here—but **metabolic synchronization**. The environment is so cohesive, so rhythmic, and so saturated that it will attempt to digest your pace of life into its own lethargic, geological cadence.

Here is how you must prepare to ensure you remain a visitor and not a nutrient.

### 1. The Physiological Seal (Suit & Skin)
Do not go in light, breathable fabrics. The atmosphere is a "lukewarm emulsion," and your earthly sweat will be met with a cold rejection by the Mat.
*   **The Barrier Suit:** You require a suit constructed of hydrophobic polymers—something with the finish of a lotus leaf. If your clothing absorbs the mist, it will become heavy within the hour, dragging you down into the sponge-like floor. 
*   **The Anti-Fungal Protocol:** You are a dry, chaotic foreign object in a world of total synthesis. Your skin will be viewed as an empty void in a full world. Before arrival, you must apply a broad-spectrum, high-intensity dessicant cream to every inch of exposed flesh. If you don't, the microscopic "janitor" spores will see your skin as a prime piece of real estate to colonize. 
*   **Vibration Dampening:** The High Mat breathes. Your footfalls create a "hydraulic sink" that ripples through the root-matter. To avoid alerting the macro-flora ripples (which could cause the ground to close around your ankles like a slow-motion trap), wear boots with soft, wide-surface-area soles—like snowshoes made of aerogel. You want to distribute your weight so you do not "break the skin" of the Mat.

### 2. The Mental Calibration (The Anti-Dusk Filter)
The "bruise-colored dusk" is mentally corrosive. Without a day/night cycle, your circadian rhythm will collapse within forty-eight hours. 
*   **The Chronometer:** Carry a mechanical, high-torque watch that is not linked to the local ambient light. You must force yourself to "sleep" based on a rigid clock, not on fatigue. If you succumb to the Mat’s twilight, you will find yourself drifting into a state of "plant-thought"—a semi-conscious, meditative stupor where you become content to stand still until the lichen climbs your shins.
*   **The Stimulus Override:** Because the Mat is quiet and perfectly integrated, your mind will be desperate for patterns. You will begin to hallucinate meaning in the *drip-hiss* of the moisture. Do not look for patterns in the ruins or the rhythm. Carry a tactile, sharp-edged object—a piece of geometric, crystalline, or metallic jewelry. Keep it in your pocket. Rub it when the silence begins to feel like a lullaby. The sharp edges of man-made, cold, un-alive matter will ground your reality against the pervasive "vegetal softness."

### 3. Equipment & Hydration
*   **The "Dry" Oxygen Supply:** While the air is technically breathable, it is heavy with pheromones and plant-respiration byproducts that will make you lethargic and suggestible. Use a closed-circuit rebreather, or at the very least, a heavy-duty N95-grade filter infused with a mild, sharp stimulant like menthol or eucalyptus. This provides a "psychological bulkhead" between your consciousness and the Mat’s exhalations.
*   **Do Not Drink the Drip:** The water on the waxy surfaces is mineral-rich, nutrient-dense, and essentially the "blood" of the Mat. It is not water; it is a chemical soup designed to dissolve and reincorporate. If you are thirsty, you must have brought your own purified water from your origin point. 
*   **Navigational Aid:** Because there is no horizon and the fractal complexity repeats, you cannot rely on sight. Use a localized sonar device or magnetic beaconing. Traditional compasses will be useless against the "root-gravity" of the planet.

### 4. The Etiquette of Presence
You are walking on something that is essentially asleep. 
*   **The Silence Rule:** Do not use high-decibel equipment. The High Mat reacts to vibration. Rapid, sharp, or erratic noises are perceived as "stings" or "trauma." If you must communicate, whisper. Keep your movements fluid and slow. If you move like a predator (twitchy, fast), the Mat’s immune response may simply attempt to smooth over your path by accelerating the growth cycles around you, effectively sealing your exit.
*   **The "Artifact" Prohibition:** Do not touch the bone-white ribs rising from the ground. They are the only rigid points in a fluid world; they are the "anchors" of the organism. Interfering with them is akin to poking a sleeping whale. 

### Why you should go (and why to leave)
You are going to see a world that has reached the "terminal state of perfection." It is a rare glimpse into a reality where the conflict of biology has ended. It is profound, beautiful, and deeply, terribly lonely. 

Visit for the stillness. Feel the pulse of the subterranean circulation. But when you feel that intense, seductive urge to press your face into the violet lichen and simply *stop*—when the drip-hiss of the world starts to sound like your own heartbeat—**that is when you must leave.** 

The High Mat does not eat people; it merely invites them to join its long, dreamless sleep. Do not accept the invitation.
