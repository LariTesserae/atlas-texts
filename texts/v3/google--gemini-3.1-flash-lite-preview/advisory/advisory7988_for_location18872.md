To visit the **Spire-Labyrinth of the Vitreous Maw** is to step into an environment that does not recognize the biological mandate of "life." It is a machine that performs the architecture of a city, and you are, at best, a temporary vibration within its humming circuits. Preparation is not about survival in the traditional sense; it is about *synchronization*.

Here is how you must prepare to survive the Maw.

### 1. Physical Augmentation: The "Smoothing" Protocol
The Maw is a landscape of crystalline edges and structural obsession. Because the architecture vibrates with a low-frequency harmonic, you must minimize your own internal friction.
*   **Acoustic Damping:** Wear a hardshell suit lined with non-Newtonian visc-gel. Ordinary fabric will rasp against the synthetic quartz surfaces, and in a place where sound carries with crystalline clarity, a simple brush of your sleeve can sound like a scream. You need a suit that absorbs the city’s humming rather than echoing it.
*   **Oxygenated Filtration:** The air here is not "thin" so much as it is "sterile." It is essentially high-altitude atmospheric waste product. You require a closed-loop rebreather. If you breathe the city’s air directly, your lungs will be "polished" by the ozone—a process that is, in effect, a slow-motion scarring of your bronchial tissue.
*   **The "Weightless" Tether:** Never step onto a surface without verifying its structural continuity. Use magnetic climbing crampons. The city is a sedimentation of plates, and while the geometry seems solid, the "impossible perspective" of the corridors can cause you to lose your sense of orientation. If you feel the floor tilting or folding, you are experiencing spatial drift. You must clip yourself to a structural truss immediately; if you fall, you won't hit the ground—you will simply descend through a fold in reality until you occupy a different "hour" than the rest of the city, becoming a ghost to your own timeline.

### 2. Cognitive Preparation: The Shadow-Anchor
The lighting in the Maw is an edit-tool. Because it creates no shadows, your brain will struggle to maintain depth perception. You will reach for a handhold and find your hand passing through a "vacuum of color."
*   **The Anchor Object:** Carry a small, heavy, non-synthetic object—a smooth, dark river stone, perhaps. Keep it in your hand. When the light of the Maw threatens to "edit you out," looking at the stone provides a reference point for natural, organic density. It is your tether to the concept of *being a solid object*.
*   **Pattern Recognition Training:** You must learn to disregard the slither-cables. Do not attempt to catch or communicate with the clockwork-wasps. They are not animals; they are part of the city’s maintenance system. To the Maw, you are a foreign protein, a chaotic input. If you interfere with the wasps or cables, the city’s corrective algorithms may decide that you, too, are a structural impurity requiring removal.

### 3. Philosophical Adaptation: The "Accretion" Mindset
The most dangerous error a traveler can make is to try and move fast. The Maw operates on the scale of geological industrialization. 
*   **Temporal Calibration:** Resist the urge to rush. To move quickly through the Labyrinth is to trigger the city’s defense mechanisms—it sees rapid movement as an anomaly. Walk with a slow, deliberate pace that matches the vibration of the conduits. Match your heartbeat to the hum.
*   **The Patience Tax:** Bring supplies for ten times as long as you plan to stay. Because the city expands by accretion, the "door" you walked through may quite literally weld shut while you are inside, not out of malice, but because the structure decided that specific threshold needed an extra layer of reinforcement. 

### 4. What to Bring (The Inventory)
*   **Opaque Visors:** The harsh, blue-white light will cause retinal burnout. You need amber-tinted, polarized lenses to filter the "sterile" glow and give your eyes a chance to perceive the three-dimensional geometry of the room.
*   **Non-Metallic Prying Tools:** Carry tools made of hardened ceramics or carbon-fiber composites. Do not use metal; you do not want to become part of the city’s electrical grounding system. A copper tool in this city is a lightning rod waiting for a strike.
*   **Haptic Mapping Tape:** The geometry folds. You must mark your path with tape that reacts to light by glowing—not with the city's blue, but with a warm, dim red. This will allow you to see your "path" as a physical memory, even when the tunnel ahead of you begins to spiral into an impossible, unreachable hour.

### Final Advice: The Warning of the Hum
If you stop walking and the humming suddenly shifts pitch—dropping an octave—**begin your exit immediately.** 

The Maw has noticed that you are not a machine. Most of the time, the city is indifferent. When the hum changes, the city is recalibrating its own internal layout. If you remain caught in a folding corridor during a structural expansion, you will not die, but you will stop being an inhabitant and start being *architecture*. You will become the calcified, synthetic foundation for a future explorer, waiting, in silence, for the next million years to pass.
