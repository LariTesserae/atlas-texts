To visit the Crystallized Spire is not a journey of arrival; it is a transition into a state of permanent observation. Because this pocket universe operates on ionic equilibrium rather than biological entropy, you are not stepping into an ecosystem, but into a **living circuit**.

If you intend to visit, you must abandon the notion of "traveling." A traveler moves; you will only *be placed*. Here is how you must prepare.

### 1. The Physiological Purge
Your body is a chaotic mess of peristalsis, heartbeat, and thermal radiation—a biological machine that relies on gradients (high pressure to low, hot to cold, intake to output). This space abhor gradients. 
*   **Neutralize your core:** Before entry, you must ingest a series of ionic stabilizers. These will suppress your metabolic rate until your heartbeat is a distant, shallow thud. If you enter with a frantic pulse, the "copper-veined lichen" will interpret your circulatory system as a foreign power surge. It will attempt to "ground" you, physically rooting into your skin to pull the excess heat from your blood.
*   **Sensory Damping:** Because the acoustic environment is a "rhythmic pinging" coupled with a low-frequency drone, you must bring active noise-cancellation equipment that operates on the same frequency as the ruins. If you do not mask your internal bio-acoustics, the constant, eons-old chant of the spire will become the only thing your brain processes, leading to total cognitive synchronization with the lichen. You will *become* the moss if you listen too long.

### 2. The Suit: "The Lead-Lined Second Skin"
Standard EVA suits are useless here. They are bulky, pressurized, and designed for movement. 
*   **Material:** Wear a bodysuit composed of non-conductive, high-density weave—specifically, a weave that mimics the texture of felt. If you wear something smooth, the lichen will perceive you as an anomaly in the texture of the room and will aggressively attempt to "fill" you. You want to feel like a knot of rust, not a foreign object.
*   **Integration:** Do not carry tools. If you have pockets, the nickel-alloy scaffolding will find a way to latch into them, fusing your gear to the walls. Carry nothing that cannot be fused. Your suit should be fitted with sacrificial galvanic anodes—small metal tabs designed to corrode—to draw the "ghost-currents" away from your actual body. 

### 3. The Psychological Anchoring
The greatest danger of the Spire is the "Stillness Trap." In a place where nothing changes, your human mind will manufacture change to keep from shattering. You will start to see patterns in the lichen, then faces, then narratives. When you are squeezed into a space where there is no physical gap between you and the wall, your ego will begin to dissolve into the copper-veined tangle.
*   **The Anchor-Pulse:** Carry a mechanical device—a simple, spring-wound metronome, or better yet, a ticking analog watch. You need a vibration that is *not* attuned to the industrial chant of the cavern. Every hour, you must trigger a manual reset of this device. It is your only tether to the realization that time exists *outside* this room.
*   **The Mantra of the Finite:** You must accept before entry that you are not there to explore. You are there to witness a "finished thought." If you attempt to push the walls, to dig at the lichen, or to pull the pipes, the ionic exchange will spike. The room will defend its equilibrium by increasing the pressure. You will be crushed—not by violence, but by the room's desperate, reflexive need to be full.

### 4. Entry and Exit Protocol
*   **The Arrival:** Do not walk into the Spire. You must be lowered into it via a slender opening, as if you are a gear being placed into a watch. Gravity will not hold you; the density of the moss will. 
*   **The Exit:** You cannot simply leave. You must break the connection. The lichen will have "zipped" you into the landscape. You will need a localized de-magnetizer or a high-frequency sonic shaper to create a momentary "gap" between your suit and the copper veins. If you pull away too fast, you will leave fragments of your suit—or your skin—behind, forever integrated into the hive-mind of the lichen.

### Final Warning
Once you step into the Spire, you are contributing to the "industrial taxidermy." You are becoming part of the static art. Do not look for an exit; look for a way to maintain your own internal frequency. In a world of total equilibrium, the person who remembers how to dance—however slightly—is the only one who survives the stillness. 

**Bring nothing you aren't prepared to lose to the rust.**
