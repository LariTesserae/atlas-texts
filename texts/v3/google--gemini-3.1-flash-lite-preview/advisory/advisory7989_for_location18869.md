Visiting the Siphon-Groves is not an expedition; it is an act of extreme submission. To enter this place is to discard the human instinct for speed, volume, and individuality. Because the air is a pressurized, wet shroud and the environment is a tapestry of parasitic, slow-motion growth, your presence is an intrusion that the grove will notice with crystalline clarity.

Preparing for the Siphon-Groves requires a total recalibration of your biology and your gear. If you approach this place as a tourist, you will be "digested" by the humidity within hours. Approach it as a silent witness.

### 1. Physiological Preparation
*   **Respiratory Filtering:** The air is thick with "suspended grit"—bioluminescent spores that act as both nutrient and atmosphere. Do not use standard charcoal filters, as they will clog in minutes. You require a high-grade, moisture-wicking respirator with a wide-intake surface area that can be cleaned manually. Without it, the "sweet rot" scent will eventually trigger a respiratory fungal infection.
*   **The "Slow-Motion" Conditioning:** You must train yourself to move with deliberate, agonizing patience. If you move quickly, the flora will register you as a threat or a rupture. Practice moving in "staccato drift": move for three seconds, stop for ten. Learn to occupy space without displacing it.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** The "wet wool blanket" heat is deceptive. It is not an external flame but a constant, heavy pressure. Wear specialized thin-film cooling suits that circulate fluid to keep your core temperature stable. If you overheat, you will induce a metabolic spike that the local fauna—the slug-like, high-saturation creatures—may interpret as a thermal beacon. They are not aggressive, but they *will* crawl over you if you become the warmest object in their sector.

### 2. Tactical Provisions
*   **Non-Aggressive Footwear:** Do not wear hiking boots with deep treads. You need flat, wide-soled boots—similar to lotus-shoes or specialized moss-walkers—that distribute your weight to avoid breaking the "spongiform" ground. If you break the surface of the sediment, you release trapped gases that smell of ancient, stagnant decay; this scent acts as a pheromonal alarm that will cause the entire chamber to settle into a defensive, tightening curl around you.
*   **Bio-Luminescent Lighting:** Bring no harsh LEDs. The Siphon-Groves operates on an amber/purple spectrum. Use a light source that mimics the ambient bioluminescence—muted, pulsing, low-frequency oranges or deep ochre. A bright, white light will be blinding to you and potentially lethal to the organisms that govern the Heat-Siphon.
*   **Structural Anchors:** Because the entire grove is stacked, you will often find yourself needing to climb or traverse atop "petrified carcasses." Carry a set of soft-grip, wide-surface climbing pads. Avoid metal crampons or pitons at all costs; the grove reacts to sharp, hard surfaces by accelerating growth around the site of the puncture, which will trap your gear within hours.

### 3. The Psychological Code
*   **Surrender the Senses:** You will hear the *thwip* of ferns and the drip of moisture. Learn to categorize these sounds. If the wet *thwip* stops, it means the grove has detected your movement. Stop entirely. If you hear a series of rhythmic drips accelerating, you are in a "temperature sink" and the fauna will soon migrate toward your position. Do not panic. Simply displace yourself by a few inches, slowly.
*   **Respect the Mementos:** You will encounter the oval stone arcs and bone-white fungal towers. Do not touch them, do not lean on them, and do not treat them as landmarks. They are the "anchor-points" of the ecosystem. If you disturb a Memento, you disrupt the geometric order of the creepers and vines, which can cause a structural cascade—a collapse of a section of the grove as the interconnected vines try to "re-knot" the void you created.

### 4. Gear Packing List (The Checklist)
*   **Hydro-Gel Barrier:** Apply a translucent, permeable barrier cream to exposed skin. It prevents the moisture from causing skin maceration and masks your human scent, which the local life-forms may find confusing (or enticing).
*   **The "Still-Suit" Modification:** You likely brought a suit from another world; ensure the seals are vacuum-tested. The Siphon-Groves is humid at a molecular level. If the moisture gets into your electronics, they will not merely short—they will begin to sprout.
*   **The "Silent Journal":** Do not bring a voice recorder. The sound of your voice will disrupt the "profound silence" that keeps the grove in its state of rest. Bring paper and charcoal-based writing implements. If you must document, do it in the "stagnant pools" of time: wait until you are fully settled, and only move your hand when the creep-rate of the nearby moss provides the rhythm.

**The Golden Rule:** The Siphon-Groves is not a place you visit; it is a place you inhabit until your heartbeat slows to match the tectonic creep of the walls. If you return to the surface and your internal rhythm is still synced to the *thwip-thwip* of the ferns, stay in quarantine for three days. You have been "colonized" by the patience of the grove, and you are not yet safe for the urgency of the world above.
