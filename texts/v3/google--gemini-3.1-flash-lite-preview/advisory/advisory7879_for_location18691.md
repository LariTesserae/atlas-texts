Visiting the Bulb-City is less an act of tourism and more an act of biological intrusion. To enter this place is to become a parasite or a pathogen within a living, breathing host. If you intend to survive, and more importantly, to *not be ejected* by the city’s immune response, you must shed your human arrogance and adapt to a landscape that considers your very presence a vibration to be dampened.

Here is your manual for preparation.

### 1. Physical Preparation: The "Soft-Step" Standard
Do not bring hard-soled boots. Do not bring metal zippers, plastic buckles, or anything that clinks. Any sound you make in this environment is a pollutant. The city exists in a resonance-based economy; a careless footfall against the floor-walls behaves like a shockwave.
*   **Gear:** You must wear garments made from organic, porous fibers—raw silk, untreated wool, or felted hemp. You need clothing that absorbs sound rather than reflecting it.
*   **The Feet:** Wrap your feet in layers of thick, damp-resistant moss-pads or soft leather slippers. You need to "feel" the filaments. By walking softly, you mimic the weight of the native inhabitants, preventing your movement from being interpreted as a tectonic tremor or a predator attack.

### 2. Sensory Calibration: Sight Is a Liability
You will be tempted to use artificial, high-lumen flashlights. **Do not do this.** Intense light is caustic here. It will sear the delicate, translucent bracts and likely cause the bulb to contract violently in a defensive spasm. 
*   **Infrared vs. Haptic:** If you must see, use low-level thermal goggles, but understand that the city is built for *haptic* intelligence. 
*   **The Practice:** Spend a week in a dark, humid room before transit. Practice navigating by touch, training your fingers to distinguish between the "live" filaments (which hold information) and the structural threads (which hold the bulb together). If you pluck the wrong thread, you might accidentally "publish" a broadcast to the entire city—or rip a bridge from its moorings.

### 3. Respiratory Hygiene
The air here is a thick, frigid slurry of spores. To a human, these spores are alien—possibly hallucinogenic or physiologically destabilizing. 
*   **Filtration:** A standard gas mask will smell of plastic and screech with the hiss of its own valves. You need a simple, fine-mesh cloth mask moistened with a solution of saline and local oils (if obtainable) to prevent your own heavy, human breath from drying out the delicate, damp membranes of the bulb. 
*   **Pacing:** Your metabolic rate is too fast for this city. You are a roaring furnace compared to the heartbeat of the bulb. You must practice "rhythmic breath-holding"—inhaling slowly to match the peristaltic thrum of the floor beneath you. If you breathe out of sync with the bulb, the inhabitants will smell the chemical signature of your stress and heat.

### 4. Supplies and "Currency"
You cannot bring canned, processed goods, or anything with synthetic scents. Your soap, your deodorant, and your synthetic fabrics are biological toxins here.
*   **What to bring:** If you wish to trade with the clockwork-spider folk, bring high-quality minerals or concentrated, dried resins. They deal in materials that they can integrate into the walls. Do not offer precious metals unless they are raw, unrefined nuggets; they care about the vibration of the mineral, not its rarity on Earth.
*   **The Gift:** Bringing a small vial of purified, mineral-rich water is the highest gesture of diplomacy. In a city that filters its own life-blood from freezing shale, clean water is the ultimate luxury. 

### 5. The Psychological Shift: Being "Small"
The most dangerous thing you bring with you is your perception of space. In the Bulb-City, you are a titan. A single clumsy movement could destroy a generation’s worth of weaving.
*   **The Mantra:** You are a guest in a gut. You are effectively living inside the stomach of a giant organism. When the bulb shifts against the cold rock of the world’s crust, do not panic. Do not brace yourself or scream. Sway with it. If the walls contract, draw your limbs in tight. 
*   **Surrender:** You must accept that you have no authority here. If the city decides to close a pore or shift a bridge, you are not a traveler; you are an obstacle to be rerouted.

### Warning on Departure
When you leave, ensure you are scrubbed of all local spores. Traveling back through the multiverse while carrying the biological signature of the Bulb-City is a dangerous invitation for contamination. You are carrying a fragile, frozen ecosystem on your skin. Treat the return trip like the decontamination process of a planetary explorer. 

If you leave correctly, you will find, days later, that you still hear the *thrum*—a faint, rhythmic tick-click in the back of your mind, a reminder that somewhere deep in the weeping shale, the dark city is still stitching itself together, indifferent to your world, and perfectly, terrifyingly alive.
