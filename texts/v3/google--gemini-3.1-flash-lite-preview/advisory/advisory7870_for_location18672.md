To step into Ostral-hollow is to step into a draft of a world that the universe forgot to delete. It is not merely a high-altitude city; it is a precarious knot in the fabric of reality. For an ordinary human traveler, preparation is not just about equipment—it is about psychological calibration and safety-proofing your own existence.

### 1. Physical Preparation: The Suit of Anchoring
Standard gear will fail you here. The "soft" nature of the geology and the volatile gravity require specialized apparel.

*   **The Weighted Exoskeleton:** You cannot rely on your inner ear in a place where gravity is a "loose suggestion." Purchase a light, kinetic-assist harness. Its primary function isn’t strength, but *inertia.* You need to be heavy enough that the shifting geometry of the boulevards doesn't pull you into the abyss when a bridge decides to "stretch" a few hundred feet.
*   **The Haptic Compass:** Do not bring a magnetic compass; the mountain’s tectonic hunger renders them useless. Carry a haptic feedback tether. It must be calibrated to your point of origin—the "Anchor." If the city begins to fold in on itself or if you step into a non-Euclidean loop, the tether will vibrate in a unique, jagged rhythm to remind you of the direction that leads back to your entry point.
*   **The Absorbent Layers (Oil-Skin):** Ostral-hollow leaks memories and "memory-fluids." Use a thick-weave, oil-impregnated wool cloak. It is the only material robust enough to repel the iridescent liquids that drip from the vents. Do not let these fluids touch your skin; to touch the "memory of a storm" is to invite the echoes of that storm into your own psyche, which can lead to permanent displacement or temporal psychosis.

### 2. Biological Defense: The Filter and the Focus
The atmosphere is oxygen-thin and biologically compromised by the bioluminescent moss.

*   **Closed-Circuit Respirator:** Do not rely on simple filters. The moss in this city is sentient in its industrial dependency. If you inhale the spores, you risk your own lung tissue beginning to pulse with that same rhythmic, greenish light. A closed-circuit rig ensures you are breathing only the air you brought with you.
*   **Anti-Echo Sedatives:** The "echo-pockets"—where sounds repeat for days—can shatter a human mind tuned to linear causality. Bring neuro-suppressants that dampen auditory processing. If you hear a wrench clattering for the tenth time in as many minutes, you must be able to disconnect from the sensory data to prevent a feedback loop in your own brain.

### 3. Procedural Protocols: Navigating the Unfinished
You are not a tourist; you are a trespasser in a construction site that has been running on autopilot since time began.

*   **The "Rule of Three" Stays:** When moving between structures, never step into a transit-chasm if you haven't seen a local resident—those viscous, oil-stained shadows—cross it successfully within the last hour. If the gap "stretches," waiting is the only strategy. Do not try to rush; the physics of Ostral-hollow perceive eagerness as a form of aggression and will likely stretch the distance further in response.
*   **Don't Touch the Clockwork Moths:** When the condensation coalesces into the glass-winged moths, remain perfectly still. They are attracted to vibration and rhythm. If you move, their clicking wings will synchronize with your heartbeat. It sounds poetic, but it is fatal; if the moth-rhythm perfectly matches your pulse, you become "stuck" in the city’s timeline, effectively becoming one of the local shadows. 
*   **Observe the Rivets:** If you see a piston hammer a rivet into place, *do not look away* until the sequence is finished. There is a superstition among the few travelers who have visited: looking away from "work" in Ostral-hollow suggests to the mountain that you are finished existing. Keep your eyes on the labor; it makes the city feel observed, and therefore, more solid.

### 4. Psychological Preparation
You must accept that you are entering a place that is *an unfinished thought.*

*   **Maintain Ego-Distance:** You will see chimneys venting smoke downward and staircases leading to ceilings. Do not try to map them. Treat the city as a fluid entity rather than a structural one. If you try to apply Euclidean geometry to your surroundings, the resulting cognitive dissonance will leave you vulnerable to the vertigo of the abyss.
*   **The Muted Voice:** Speak rarely. The echoes here are hungry. If you speak a word of your own language, it may be caught in a loop. Imagine spending an eternity listening to the sound of your own voice saying "help" or "hello."

### Final Advice
If you arrive and you notice the "glass moths" are unusually quiet—or worse, if they stop clicking entirely—it means the city is resetting. The tectonic hunger is peaking. Do not look for an exit; look for a vibration. The architecture will groan, the basaltic ribs will shift, and a path will open that wasn't there a moment ago. Walk through it without looking back. Do not attempt to salvage any "iridescent memories" or souvenirs. 

In Ostral-hollow, you don't collect memories. You survive them.
