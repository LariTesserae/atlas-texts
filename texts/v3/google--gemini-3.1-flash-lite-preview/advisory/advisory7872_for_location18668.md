### 3. Kinetic Discipline (The Walk)
You cannot walk like a human here. A heavy, heel-to-toe stride puts too much pressure on the pulse-like moss.

The most dangerous aspect of the Bowl is the loss of time. Because the landscape assembles and disassembles itself, your internal clock will fail.
