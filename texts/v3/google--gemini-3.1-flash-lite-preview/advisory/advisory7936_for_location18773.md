To step into the Crystalline Basin of Kher-Oul is to abandon the concept of "solid ground" and "fixed architecture." You are not visiting a city; you are entering a slow-motion metabolism. 

As a multiverse traveler, you must prepare not just for the environment, but for the fundamental shift in your relationship with physical reality. Here is how you must equip yourself and your psyche.

### 1. The Physical Shielding: Light and Viscosity
The absolute, 3.0/3 brightness of Kher-Oul will cause permanent retinal scarring within seconds if you are unprotected. 
*   **The Visor:** You require a **photo-reactive, multi-layer polarization field.** Standard dark lenses will fail, as they cannot account for the "refractive shimmer" that hits you from every angle. Your visor needs to be able to "subtract" light based on spectral analysis—filtering out the blinding white glare while allowing the violet and bruised-gold gradients to pass through so you can distinguish the edges of the shifting architecture.
*   **Respiratory Protection:** The air is "gelatinous" and mineral-rich. If inhaled, the acidic condensation will begin to calcify inside your lungs, mimicking the same processes that build the pillars. A **closed-circuit rebreather** is mandatory. You do not want the Basin’s "lung" to attempt to inhabit your own.

### 2. The Personal Kinetic Stabilizer (The "Anchor")
Because the architecture is constantly being eroded and reformed by the dripping acidic water, the floor is never truly fixed. If you stand still for too long, a maintenance-creature might decide the section of floor beneath you is inefficient and "reconfigure" it—effectively dropping you into the basin floor.
*   **Magnetic-Serrated Boots:** You cannot rely on friction in a place that is perpetually slick with mineral slime. Your footwear needs to utilize **active clamping mechanisms** that can find purchase in the porous, semi-precious silica. 
*   **The Tether:** Always wear a gravitational anchor or a localized inertial dampener. If the wall you are standing on undergoes a "controlled collapse" to reroute a drip-stream, you need to remain suspended in place until you can find a stable footing on the newly formed structure.

### 3. Tactile and Vibratory Awareness
Sound is useless here; the moisture renders it a muffled, localized experience. However, the Basin is *loud* in the realm of vibration. 
*   **Haptic Sensors:** Equip a suite of **vest-based haptic sensors.** Since you cannot hear the creatures (the eyeless builders), you must *feel* them. When you feel a rhythmic, low-frequency hum radiating through your boots or clothes, it means a swarm of mollusks is approaching to "repair" the path you are occupying. Do not panic; move out of their way. They are strictly functional, and they will incorporate your body into the masonry if you impede their flow.

### 4. Behavioral Etiquette: "The Guest of the Drip"
The inhabitants of Kher-Oul operate on a logic of hyper-utility. 
*   **Don’t be Static:** If you linger, you become an obstacle. If you obstruct their work, you risk being "dissolved" or moved. Always keep a slow, rhythmic gait that mimics the flow of the condensation. 
*   **The "Offering" Protocol:** If you wish to stay in one area for an extended period, leave a small amount of non-silica-based mineral or a binding agent (a high-viscosity epoxy or resin). The creatures are obsessed with maintenance; by providing them with materials, you signal that you are a participant in the architecture rather than a foreign object. 
*   **The Translucence:** As you move, your own body will refract the light, throwing shadows of gold and violet onto the white walls. Watch these internal shadows. They are your best indicator of structural density. If your shadow appears "stretched" or "warped," the wall you are leaning against is thinning. Move immediately.

### 5. Psychological Calibration
The hardest part of Kher-Oul is the "becoming." Humans are wired to seek permanence—to trust that the floor will remain a floor. Here, you must cultivate **provisional living.** 
*   **The Mantra:** *Nothing here is lost that is not meant to be reformed.*
*   Do not try to map the city; the map will change ten times in an hour. Instead, adopt the perspective of the polyps: anchor yourself to the flow, accept the chill, and learn to appreciate the liquid geometry. If you hold onto your earthly expectations of stability, the anxiety of the "ephemeral origami" will shatter you.

**Final Warning:** Keep your gear strictly contained. Any loose items will be scavenged within minutes. The creatures of Kher-Oul find human technology to be an excellent, exotic binding agent. If you lose a tool, consider it donated to the next balcony built in your path. **Do not attempt to retrieve it; the "suck-clatter" of the builders is the only warning you will get before the floor transforms.**
