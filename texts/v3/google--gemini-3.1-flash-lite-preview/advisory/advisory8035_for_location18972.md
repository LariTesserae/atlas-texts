To visit the Obsidian Crypt-City is to surrender your senses to a world that was never designed for human comfort. You are not just visiting a place; you are entering a geologic digestive tract, a deafening, wet machine that operates on a timescale that renders your pulse meaningless.

Preparation for this journey requires precision and a fundamental recalibration of what you consider “equipment.” Here is how you prepare for the descent.

### 1. Physical Preparation: The Suit
Standard travel gear will fail you here within minutes. The "water" is mineral-heavy, likely caustic, and the humidity is absolute—so high that normal breathable fabrics will become soaked, heavy, and freezing against your skin immediately.

*   **The Second Skin:** You require a high-grade, hydrophobic, non-porous polymer suit. Think of the gear used by deep-sea saturation divers, but modified for extreme heat retention. It must be completely sealed at the wrists, ankles, and neck. 
*   **Thermal Regulation:** The air is saturated and metallic, pulling heat away from your body with punishing efficiency. You need an active liquid-cooling/heating mesh underneath to keep your core temperature stable. 
*   **The Boots:** You will be traversing volcanic glass bridges—sharper than razor blades and slicker than butter. Your boots must have magnetized or diamond-dust-coated soles for grip. If you slip, you do not fall into a pit; you fall into a labyrinth of stone basins where the water is deep enough to trap you.

### 2. Sensory Modification: The Sound Barrier
The "rhythmic, deafening percussion" of the drips is not just noise; it is a physical force. In high-density stone caverns, sustained acoustic energy can cause nausea, vertigo, and, if you stay long enough, structural damage to your inner ear.

*   **Active Sonic Damping:** Do not use simple earplugs. You need custom-molded, active-noise-canceling headsets that can handle low-frequency, high-decibel thrumming. You need white noise synthesis to mask the *drip-clack* that will otherwise sync with your heartbeat and drive you to panic.
*   **The Sight:** The violet light of the lichen is deceptive. It is dim and monochromatic, flattening all perception of depth. You will be prone to miscalculating the distance between those vertiginous stairs. Bring "high-contrast, depth-perception optical aids"—essentially goggles that artificially enhance edge definition. Without them, you will trip.

### 3. Psychological Preparation: The "Closed-Loop" Mindset
The city is a machine; it is finished. It does not grow, it does not rot, and it does not need you. The feeling of insignificance will be your biggest enemy.

*   **Acceptance of the Automata:** Do not attempt to interact with the translucent centipedes. They are the maintenance staff of an aeon-old process. If you interfere with their mineral scraping, you are effectively a gear-jam in a clock. Treat them like moving architectural features; watch them move, but never step in their path.
*   **Temporal Displacement:** Because there is no sunlight and the rhythm is constant, you will lose track of time within three hours. Bring a mechanical (not electronic) watch. Do not rely on digital screens—the humidity will compromise them, and the glow of a screen will feel like an offensive wound in the bruised-purple dark of the crypt.
*   **The Inward Turn:** This city encourages a specific kind of claustrophobic meditation. Prepare for intense, intrusive thoughts. When you are surrounded by a sound that demands you pay attention to it, your own mind will scream for silence. Practice rhythmic breathing that mimics the cadence of the drips; if you fight the pace of the city, the city will break you.

### 4. Logistics and Logistics
*   **No Organic Carry:** Do not bring food that requires preparation. The metallic tang of the air will penetrate any packaging. Eat before you descend, and rely on concentrated, neutral-tasting gels. Everything in this city is mineral and water; introducing organic scent or mess will make you feel like a trespasser and may attract unwanted interest from the local fauna.
*   **The Exit Protocol:** Since there is no sky, there is no "up" in a traditional sense. You must have a fixed, tethered path back to your entry point. The city is a puzzle of rounded, eroded stone. Once you turn a corner, the architecture looks almost identical in every direction. Do not rely on your memory; mark your path with reflective geometric markers that you can retrieve on your way out. 

### Final Advice: The "Mineral Sweat"
Expect to leave feeling as though you have been coated in a thin layer of grit. Your skin will itch, your lungs will feel heavy, and your hearing will be muffled for days after you ascend. You are visiting a place that is essentially a slow-moving, petrified heartbeat. 

Do not look for secrets. There are no secrets here—only the drip, the click of the centipedes, and the ancient, stubborn basalt. If you seek meaning, you will find only the rhythm of the water. **Listen to it, respect it, and leave as soon as the rhythm begins to feel like your own.**
