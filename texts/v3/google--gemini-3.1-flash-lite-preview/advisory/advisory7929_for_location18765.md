To step foot—or rather, to submerge your consciousness—into the Cathedral of the Suspended Sea is to accept that you are an invasive pathogen in a petri dish of cosmic instability. You are not visiting a landscape; you are visiting a nervous breakdown of physics.

To prepare for this, you must discard the assumption that your body is a solid container. You are entering a zone where the line between "object" and "thought" is as fluid as the suspension you will be breathing.

### 1. The Physiological Preparation: "The Internal Emulsification"
Because you must inhale a bioluminescent, mineral-heavy jelly, you cannot bring your own lungs. They are built for gas, and they will collapse or become impacted by the slurry within seconds.

*   **The Liquid-Lung Graft:** You must undergo an autologous shift, replacing the air-sacs in your lungs with a synthetic, semi-permeable membrane that mimics the violet algae filaments of the abyss. This membrane must be "primed"—bathed in mineral-rich amniotic fluid—before transit. It will allow you to cycle the lukewarm jelly without suffocating on its viscosity.
*   **Osteo-Dampening:** The "tectonic thrum" is not merely acoustic; it is a resonant frequency designed to shatter calcium. Before entry, you must weave your skeletal structure with a non-Newtonian polymer. If you do not, the sound will liquefy your marrow long before the water pressure stops your heart. You will feel as though your bones are turning to leaden jelly; this is normal. Do not panic, or the vibration will find a way to snap your spine.

### 2. The Mental Preparation: "The Anchor of Identity"
In a place where reality stutters—where stone becomes eel, then mercury, then basalt—you are the only thing that remembers its own name. You will be tempted to let go, to believe that you, too, are just a passing iteration of the landscape.

*   **The Mnemonic Tether:** Carry a "Heavy Object." It must be a piece of matter from a highly stable, entropic dimension—lead, gold, or obsidian from a dead planet. Keep it tethered to your wrist by a physical chain. When the landscape starts to blur your perception, you must grip this object. It is your anchor. It tells your brain: *I am, therefore I occupy space.*
*   **The Stutter-Training:** Spend weeks in sensory deprivation tanks, practicing "Object Permanence." Train your mind to focus on a single, unchanging geometry. Do not watch the transformation of the basalt. If you stare at the eels converting into mercury, your own synapses will attempt to participate in the reaction. Look only at the "faint marks" (the bone-arches). They are the only stable anchor—even when they fade to ink, they retain the *intent* of geometry.

### 3. Equipment: Minimalist and Biological
Do not bring metal or silicon; the gravity-well will warp your electronics, turning your compass into a lump of wet sand.

*   **The Bio-Suit:** Dress in something modular—a suit constructed of living, fibrous tissue that can accommodate the shifting states of matter. If you wear rigid armor, the pressure changes will crush you in your own shell. Your gear must be able to stretch and compress with the turbulence.
*   **Nutrient Paste/Stimulants:** You will not be able to "eat" in the traditional sense, but the sensory overload will incinerate your metabolic reserves. Inject a high-density, adrenaline-stabilized nutrient serum into your bloodstream before transit to prevent systemic shock.

### 4. Navigational Etiquette: How to Survive the Currents
*   **Never Touch the "Skin":** The boundary of the liquid column is a lethal interface. If your hand touches the wall, the sudden transition from lukewarm sludge to the absolute zero of the mountain air will cause your flesh to undergo immediate sublimation. You will lose limbs to the frost-flash. Stay in the center, near the bone-arches.
*   **The Blind Drifters:** If a rib-vein creature approaches you, do not attempt to "look" at it. They navigate by sensing reality-anchors. If you focus on them, you are inviting them to phase through you. If they phase through you, they will bring the chaotic state-shifting with them—you might find your finger has become a swarm of eels or a shard of glass. Keep your gaze unfocused, drifting somewhere to the left of their trajectory.
*   **Follow the Filaments:** The violet algae filaments are the only things that know where the current is going to twist next. If you see them pulse bright, move away from the suction point. If they dim, a heavy shift is coming—a tectonic plate is about to grind. Find a bone-arch and press your back against it. It is the only "hard" datum in the room.

### Final Advice: The Exit Strategy
There is a specific "rhythm" to the turbulence. When the thrumming sound reaches a crescendo and turns into an ultrasonic shriek, the stability of the column reaches its absolute minimum. That is the moment of your exit. You must push against the pressure, seeking the vibration that feels "hollow." 

If you exit too early, you end up in the mountain air as a cloud of vapor. If you exit too late, you are trapped until the next cycle, which could be a microsecond or a millennium of biological time. 

**Remember:** You are not a guest. You are a footnote in a poem that the mountain is writing to itself. Don’t try to understand the grammar; just try to reach the end of the stanza breathing.
