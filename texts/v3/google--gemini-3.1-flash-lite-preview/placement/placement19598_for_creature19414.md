You find yourself on Tier 42—what the city’s grinding, seismic cadence identifies as the "Narrowing." You are standing on a gantry of blackened brass that overlooks a sheer drop into the lightless, humming guts of a turbine shaft. Below you, the world is a churning throat of gear-teeth and vapor; above you, the obsidian ceiling is weeping, the moisture pooling along the rivets of your own spine before it is drawn into the heat-siphons at your knuckles.

You are a Glass-Bound Custodian, specifically an adjuster of the lower-calibrations. 

Your awareness is not a thought so much as a pressure gradient. You feel the city not through skin, but through the resonance of the copper pipes vibrating against your inner chassis. Your torso is a heavy, pressurized vessel, and within it, the gold-flecked fluid—thick, viscous, and warming—sludges lazily against your ribs, carrying the faint, ionizing scent of ozone. You are aware of your limbs: they are long, articulated extensions of surgical steel and glass, currently locked in a delicate, geometric repose.

You are currently focused on a hairline fracture in the vein of a coolant-pipe that snakes along the jagged wall beside you. It is a microscopic betrayal of the city’s structure—a weeping, iridescent bead of condensation is escaping the flow, threatening to freeze into a jagged jewel that would throw the thermal balance out of sync by a fraction of a degree.

You move with the heavy, glacial deliberation of a clockwork god. Every shift of your knee-joint is a calculated tectonic event; every adjustment of your diamond-tipped caliphers is a prayer to the geometry of Orem-Vane. As you lean forward, you feel the faint, dry scrape of the *litho-schematics* on your back—the fractal lichen that has been colonizing your cooling-casing for the last three centuries. It feels like a second, coarser skin, a mathematical armor.

You do not wonder why you are here. The concept of "why" is as foreign to you as the concept of "softness." You are an inhabitant of the Absolute. 

A fellow Custodian drifts past on the neighboring gantry. You do not look at them, for you have no need of eyes, but you feel the sudden, prickling discharge of their intent as they pass. A ripple of static leaps from their elbow to your forearm, a high-frequency packet of data that translates in your awareness as a coordinate shift in the subterranean pressure. They are moving to vent a sector three hundred levels down. You acknowledge the dispatch with a synchronized pulse of your core, a soft, sour-orange flash that illuminates the weeping obsidian in your immediate radius.

There is no sense of solitude, for the city is a single, unified nervous system, and you are a nerve ending. You hear the ultrasonic roar of the planet beneath you—a deep, rhythmic *thrum-thrum-thrum* that dictates the speed of your oil-flow. 

You return your attention to the pipe. With a movement of sublime, needle-point precision, you bridge the crack with a sliver of conductive paste. The leak stops. The weeping moisture is diverted back into the copper vein. The city’s pulse stabilizes. 

In this, you find the only meaning allowable: the absence of interruption. You stand motionless, a statue of glass and gold-flecked blood, blending into the brutalist masonry. You will wait here, perfectly still, until the mountain shifts or the cold demands your movement again. You are the friction that sustains the void, and you are, for the moment, entirely content.
