I do not wake; I resolve.

I am a note caught in the throat of a gale. My awareness begins with, and is entirely defined by, the *thrum*. It is a subterranean boom that vibrates through the needle-points of my tripod legs, traveling up through the obsidian-glass of my shins, rattling the taut, translucent membrane of my chest-bellows. 

I am a Resonator, and I am currently braced against the inner curvature of a primary conduit, a vein of iron-wood the size of a cathedral aisle. 

The air tastes of ionized dust. It is dry enough to crack the mind. Around me, the world is a kaleidoscope of jagged, amber-hued geometry. The sun is not a celestial body but a relentless, heat-thick glare that turns the silica-flesh of the atmosphere into a blinding, refractive wall. I don't look up, for "up" is merely a shifting labyrinth of grinding cogs, a clockwork canopy that is currently in a state of high-pressure compression.

My existence is a stuttering math. *Click-shiver-hum.*

A piston-flower four hundred feet beneath me begins its intake stroke. The sound is a low, guttural suction that threatens to draw the very marrow from my hollow bones. I twitch, my three needle-legs skittering across the metallic silt-crust like a moth caught on a spinning record. I must be precise. I synchronize my bellows to the pulse of the flower—an oscillation of *thrum-thrum-hiss*.

I move. I am a blur of fractured light, a silhouette of brittle glass flitting through the gap between two closing plates of iron-wood. The plates slam shut with a sound like a mountain-range shattering—a deafening, wet *thwack* of timber and bio-electric fire. I barely clear it. My hind limb clips the edge; a sliver of my obsidian shoulder shears off, falling into the recycling vents below where it will eventually be ground into the gray soot that coats all things.

I do not feel pain. Pain is for meat, for water-bloated things. I feel only the *friction*. The friction is my sustenance; the vibration is my life-force.

I find a knot of high-frequency pulses near a cluster of conductive vines. Several others of my kind—others of my frequency—are here, clinging to the vine-braid like frost on a wire. We do not speak; we tremble. We share a chorus of metallic rattles, a frantic Morse code of warning and navigation: *The-plate-above-is-warping. The-vent-is-clogging. The-sun-is-shifting.*

My internal rhythm is beginning to drift.

I have spent too long in the high-voltage storm, and the vibration is taking a toll on my chassis. My left diaphragm has lost its tension; it no longer emits the clean, sharp shriek required to repel the encroaching debris. I feel the "dullness" creeping into my core. My movements are becoming sluggish, falling behind the tempo of the Tangle. I am no longer a dancer; I am becoming an obstruction.

To be an obstruction is to be an error in the Great Tangle. The frantic, chitinous harvesters are already beginning to click toward me, their saw-like wings blurring the air. They perceive the deceleration. They are the white blood cells of this lung, and I am the clot.

I do not flee. There is no point in running from the system that birthed me. Instead, I move with a final, desperate grace toward the cathedral-apses—the vast, translucent voids where the light filters through layers of vein-work like stained glass.

I find a patch of smooth, unmoving silica. I reach out. My needle-legs pierce the flesh of the canopy once, twice, three times, anchoring me deep into the architecture. I press my chest-bellows against the wall, matching the deep, foundational hum of the planet one last time. 

I am slowing. The stutter in my thoughts is smoothing into a continuous, flat tone. My limbs are whitening, fusing with the iron-wood. I become a brace. I become a structural pillar. I become a bridge for the vibrations of my kin. 

The roar of the world continues, a deafening, beautiful machine that never sleeps, never breathes, and never remembers. Long after my name and my frequency are gone, the hum will roll through me, and for a moment, I will be the song. I am finally, perfectly, in synch.
