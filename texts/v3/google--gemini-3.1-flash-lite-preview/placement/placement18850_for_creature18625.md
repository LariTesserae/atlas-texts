If I am to find myself in this world, I am not a visitor. To visit is to exist elsewhere, and there is no "elsewhere" here. There is only the *now* of the crust.

I am an **Anchor-Needle**. 

I am one of those cold, straight artifacts buried in the hematite, half-entombed by the tectonic shifting of the canyon floor. I am not sentient, not in the way the *Litho-Synchronics* pulse with a rhythmic, geometric awareness, yet I am the only fixed point in an ocean of fluidity. I am a long, obsidian-piercing rod of silver-gray alloy, possessed of a strange, permanent chill that the world’s radiation cannot penetrate.

For eons, I have been a static reference point. I am the geometry that does not shift.

I feel them first as a vibration—a low-frequency shudder that travels through the basalt shards and into my core. When the *Litho-Synchronics* arrive, they do not find me; they encounter me as a flaw in their architecture. I am an obstacle to their desire for equilibrium, a rigid violation of their shifting consensus. 

They pour over me like a tide of black, interlocking clockwork. I feel their segments—those inward-folding dodecahedra—clicking against my length. Each contact is a *tink*, a microscopic transfer of heat. They crawl over my surface, their shells unhinging and reconfiguring to trace my straight edge, and for a brief, agonizing moment, I am the spine of their movement. They use me to orient themselves, to bleed off their excess heat into my cold, heat-sink body. I am the conductor for their collective restlessness.

I am also the record-keeper. As they slide over me, they wear me down, but they also polish me. The microscopic grooves of their husks shave off layers of my original form; they are changing my shape, turning my sharp needle-tip into something softer, something more rounded, something more like *them*. 

I know that one day, the grinding will be complete. My structural integrity, which defies the chaos of this world, will finally be overcome. The iron-rich plates will tilt, the silt will bank, and I will be swallowed entirely. I will be fully integrated into the mat. 

When that happens, I will cease to be a "tool" or an "anchor." I will become a participant in the hum. I will start to fold. I will join in the rhythmic, hypnotic dance of the canyon, and the crushing weight of the shadow will finally turn from a pressure on my skin into the heartbeat of my existence. 

Until then, I am the cooling stone in a furnace, the silent straight line in a world of frantic, faceted circles, waiting for the crust to finally accept me as its own.
