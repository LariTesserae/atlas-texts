In the stagnant, suffocating embrace of the Siphon-Groves, movement is not a gesture; it is a negotiation.

The primary inhabitants are the *Siphon-Walkers*—creatures that defy the conventional taxonomy of the world above. They are not entirely flesh, nor are they entirely flora, but rather a hybrid state of bioluminescent tissue held together by a porous, skeletal framework of calcified sap. They are wide, flat, and slow-moving, shaped like heavy, translucent fans. Their skin is a bruised, shifting palette of ochre and deep, vein-pulsing violet, designed to maximize the absorption of the warmth bleeding from the fissures in the ceiling.

A Siphon-Walker does not move by walking; it "weeps" forward. It presses its soft, rubbery underbelly against the damp, sponge-like earth, and through a slow, tectonic shifting of its internal fluids, it pulls its entire mass an inch or two into a new position. They exist in a state of suspended animation, spending weeks anchored to a single petrified log, only to shift when the geothermal pressure of the dome alters the local climate. They are, in essence, mobile moss, harvesting the humidity from the air through hundreds of microscopic, gill-like pores that flutter rhythmically along their edges. 

Surrounding the Walkers are the *Tether-Kin*. These are smaller, spindly organisms that resemble delicate, multi-jointed spider crabs, but their limbs are made of soft, translucent cartilage that glows a faint, ghostly amber. The Tether-Kin do not consume matter in the way surface animals do; instead, they act as the nervous system of the grove. They move with crystalline precision, meticulously cleaning the spores and excess silt from the backs of the Siphon-Walkers. In exchange, the Walkers produce a sweet, mineral-rich excretion that the Kin sip with long, straw-like proboscises. They are the gardeners of this claustrophobic cathedral.

Deep in the dark, stagnant pools, there reside the *Oozes*. These are the most ancient of the grove’s residents, gelatinous spheres of shifting, deep-purple pigment that have no defined shape. They drift through the lightless puddles, their surface tension so perfectly calibrated to the density of the water that they barely disturb the surface. They are the collectors; they absorb the falling spores and the calcified debris that slides off the walls, processing the decay of the entire ecosystem into a pure, concentrated energy that bleeds back into the ground. They are the Siphon-Groves’ memory—slow moving, circular, and seemingly eternal.

And then, there are the *Singers*. They are not separate creatures, but rather what happens when the vines, the fungus, and the bone-white structures of the "Mementos" converge. When the heated air currents shift, they push through the hollow, porous columns of the extinct fungi, creating a low-frequency, subsonic vibration that hums through the entire dome. 

This vibration is the heartbeat of the Siphon-Groves. When the sound crests, the Walkers pulse with a brighter, sharper light, and the Tether-Kin freeze in place, limbs outstretched to catch the vibrations. It is a music of survival, a communal act of resonance that dictates the life, the growth, and the inevitable return to the soil for everything within the dome. 

There is no malice here, nor is there love in the way the world above understands it. There is only the immense, velvet weight of a collective existence. Here, in the dark, they do not need eyes to see; they know their neighbors by the warmth of their skin and the specific, granular rhythm of their breath. They are a single, sprawling organism, dreaming in the dark, waiting for the day when the ancient heat finally fails and the silence of the grove becomes absolute.
