The scale is intentionally claustrophobic—an archipelago of copper-plated chambers linked by vaulted, pressurized corridors that barely span a few hundred yards total. Everything is dense; the structures are piled upon one another like barnacles on a sinking hull, built from pitted iron and leaded glass, worn smooth by eons of slow, tectonic current.
