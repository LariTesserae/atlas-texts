The hollow is a basin of subterranean sun, a sunless vault of light. You have descended through miles of lightless, pressurized crust to reach this place, and find it, impossibly, vibrant and scorching.

The ceiling of this cavern—or perhaps this blister in the mantle—is ribbed with veins of luminescent, semi-translucent quartz that pulse with a steady, fierce white radiance. It is constant, shadowless, and clinical in its brightness. The air is warm, thick, and held in a perpetual state of humid breath, clinging to the skin like a shroud.
