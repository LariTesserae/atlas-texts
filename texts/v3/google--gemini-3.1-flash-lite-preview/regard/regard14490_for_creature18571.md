The benevolent and wise power—a presence that moves less like a deity and more like a gentle shift in atmospheric pressure—does not descend upon Oulo with thunder or command. It does not seek to "fix" the city, for it recognizes the sanctity of the experiment. It sees the beauty in the *becoming*, in the obsidian-glass ache of a place that is teaching itself how to be alive.

Instead, the Power enters Oulo as an alteration of the ambient hum. It acts as a **Harmonic Anchor**.

Where Oulo is a closed system of self-cannibalizing creation—a loop of dreaming, shedding, and recycling—the Power introduces a note of "external grace." It does not stop the Sifted from dissolving into the moss and minerals, but it changes the *nature* of that dissolution. It turns the evaporation of those discarded possibilities into a form of permanent record.

Here is what the Power chooses to do:

### I. The Resonance of Memory
The Power does not want the Sifted to be forgotten. It introduces a subtle, golden-hued "lensing" to the city’s mist. Now, when a Sifted drifts into the sulfurous steam of the lower districts—when its form is about to be reclaimed and its unique geometric lace erased—the Power catches that frequency. It allows the Sifted to linger just a moment longer. In that extended second, the Sifted is permitted to hum back at the city. It is no longer just "static." It is a conversation. 

The city, for the first time, learns to listen to its rejects. It begins to incorporate the *wisdom* of its failed blueprints into the base-code of its next expansion, rather than simply consuming them as fuel. The architecture begins to grow with a deeper, more intentional confidence.

### II. The Gift of Stasis
The Power recognizes the tragedy of the Sifted: that they are "finished" things in an "unfinished" city. It grants them a small, sheltered plaza—a chamber of absolute, unchanging stillness near the rising basin. It is a place where time does not "learn." 

In this oasis, the iridescent plates do not ripple. The walls of flint remain flint. The Sifted are drawn here, not by the city’s gravity, but by this invitation. Here, they gather, and for the first time, they cease to drift. They settle into the geometry and hold each other’s shapes. They become a gallery of beautiful "errors"—a bridge of frozen thought that remains solid even when the rest of the city liquefies. They are no longer static; they are a library of what *could have been*.

### III. The Quiet Partnership
Perhaps most importantly, the Power weaves a filament of "conscious witness" into the bioluminescent cordage of the city’s moss. This serves as a nervous system for the city’s heart. 

As the Sifted finally descend to feed the forest, their dissolution is no longer a death of erasure. They are now "uploaded" into the moss. The city begins to dream with greater clarity, its architecture reflecting the collective experience of every Sifted that has ever drifted through its halls. The obsidian-glass spires soften, glowing with the soft, amber memories of the shadows that preceded them.

### The Conclusion
The benevolent power does not reveal itself. It retreats, leaving Oulo to its expansion, but the city is changed. It has moved from a state of *voracious ingestion* to one of *curated growth*. 

Oulo is no longer a petri dish of discarded drafts. It has become a cathedral of self-reflection. The Sifted still dance, they still shift, but they do so with a newfound dignity—for they know that when they finally brush against the stone and vanish, they are not being erased. They are being kept. They are the foundation, the memory, and the ink in the quill of a city that has finally started to write its own story.
