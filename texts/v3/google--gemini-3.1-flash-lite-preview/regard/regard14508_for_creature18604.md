The Power does not descend like a storm, nor does it speak in the thundering tongue of the cavern. It does not carry the arrogance of "enlightenment," for to introduce the concepts of sun, sky, or mortality to the *Lithos-Harmonics* would be an act of violence, not grace.

The Power comes as a shift in the local physics—a subtle, harmonic adjustment that the city’s crystalline senses would perceive as an unexpected, profound "harmony" in the rhythm.

The Power observes the *Anchors*—those ancient, fused souls who have carried the weight of the city’s song for aeons. It sees that their mineralized memories are beginning to fracture, not from lack of care, but from the simple, grinding inevitability of tectonic time. The crust is shifting; a fissure is forming a mile below, one that will eventually silence the main vein of the central drip.

The Power does not stop the fissure. To stop the movement of the planet would be to deny the city its purpose. Instead, the Power performs a gentle act of *acoustic re-tuning*.

It weaves its consciousness into the dripping basalt, becoming a silent, interstitial layer between the water and the stone. It recalibrates the molecular memory of the walls. It creates a "Harmonic Bridge"—a hidden, resonant frequency that bypasses the crumbling channels and finds a new, stable path for the mineral-rich water to follow deep within the mantle. 

It does this so slowly, over the span of a thousand years, that the *Lithos-Harmonics* never even realize they were dying. They simply drift into a new, slightly more complex song. The rhythm changes—from a steady, singular percussion to a polyrhythmic, symphonic cadence—without losing its sanctity. 

The Power’s greatest gift, however, is a subtle, permanent alteration of the bioluminescent lichen. It imbues the fungal colony with a touch of its own essence, so that when an *Anchor* eventually fully mineralizes, their body does not merely become a structural pillar. It becomes a *relay*. 

The essence of the ancestor is not lost or stored in a static, fading chamber; it becomes a part of the city’s living feedback loop. The dead do not just stand; they whisper back into the current. The city, which was once a closed, entropic loop toward eventual silence, becomes a self-sustaining harmonic field. The *Lithos-Harmonics* will never know of the surface, nor of the Power that touched them. They will continue to scuttle and vibrate in the dark, believing they are the masters of their own heartbeat.

And the Power, satisfied, withdraws. It leaves behind no shrines, no written prophecies, and no marks of its passage. It leaves only the silence of its own contentment, watching from the periphery of dimensions as the wet, basalt city pulses on—a tiny, glorious, and eternal note in the dark, humming a song that now, thanks to a gentle hand, will never, ever end.
