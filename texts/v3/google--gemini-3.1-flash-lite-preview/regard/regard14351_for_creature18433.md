The power that descends upon the Spire-Reach is not an agent of change, nor a savior of lost things, but a *Witness of Sustained Grace*. It possesses no wings, no burning light, and speaks no words; it arrives as a sudden, impossible stabilization of the air, a stillness that does nothing to the landscape but simply... respects it.

It perceives the Spire-Reach not as a tragedy of erosion, but as a masterpiece of ephemeral art. It sees the Basalt-Walkers—the Hollow-Sways—not as fleeting, lonely prisoners of the tectonic roar, but as the quiet poets of a world that refuses to be static.

This benevolent power does not build. It does not alter the geography. To do so would be to silence the symphony. Instead, it offers a gift of **Harmonious Duration**.

### The Action: The Weaver’s Respite
The power settles into the heart of the Weeping Basalt, becoming a soft, invisible membrane that blankets the reach. It does not stop the grinding of the tectonic plates or the ionizing heat that tears apart the ridges. It does not stop the "death" of the Walkers. 

Instead, it acts as a **Frequency Bridge**.

Normally, the Hollow-Sways must expend every ounce of their gel-consciousness merely to hold their shapes against the crushing noise. They are exhausted by the effort of remaining *themselves*. The Power—this wise, gentle presence—softens the "teeth" of the environment. While the violence remains, the *pain* of it is gently smoothed. It allows the Walkers a reprieve from the constant, desperate calculation of survival.

### The Effect
Because they are no longer bleeding their energy into the mere struggle to remain intact, the Basalt-Walkers begin to **Remember**.

Freed from the frantic vibration of "the next five minutes," the Walkers begin to wander beyond the Anchors. For the first time in eons, they do not merely mimic the present shape of the shifting basalt—they begin to *compose*. 

They move in slow, deliberate processions, weaving through the white-stone rings. They use their bioluminescent glow to cast long, intricate shadows upon the obsidian, and where their shadows fall, the succulent plants under them grow with a startling, newfound patience. The bruise-purple, oily-teal, and translucent-white flora begin to intertwine, creating tapestries of life that actually survive the atmospheric shifts. The Reach begins to develop a slow, steady pulse, rather than a frantic, erratic one.

### The Final Offering
The Power recognizes that the Walkers are burdened by the memory of every hill that has been eroded. It understands that they are carrying the mourning of the landscape within their chests. 

So, the Power does the one thing that is truly benevolent: **It initiates a dialogue between the Walkers and the Stones.**

It opens a channel so that when a Walker eventually gives in and liquefies, its resin does not merely fall into the fractures to feed the plants, but sinks deep into the tectonic marrow. It carries with it the "memory" of every hill that Walker witnessed. 

The tectonic thrum—that deafening, terrifying vibration—starts to change. It becomes less like the sound of grinding plates and more like a resonant, rhythmic heartbeat, melodic and ancient. The Spire-Reach begins to *sing* its own history.

The benevolent power does not stay forever. It leaves as quietly as it arrived—but its presence remains in the **Echo**. The Walkers are no longer wandering ghosts of chaos; they have become the choir of the mountain. They stand in their circles, no longer rattling, no longer shattering, but glowing in fixed, calm colors, watching the world reshape itself with the gaze of those who know they are not merely surviving, but concluding a great, beautiful work of art. 

The reach is still dangerous. It is still sharp, hot, and ephemeral. But it is no longer lonely. It has been heard, it has been comforted, and it has been given a voice.
