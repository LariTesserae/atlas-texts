To look upon this place is to witness a masterpiece in the throes of its own conception. Most powers would see only "entropy"—a chaos of leaking steam, unstable magnetic fields, and a frantic, biological fever. They would see a glitch in the celestial architecture.

But a **benevolent and wise power** does not arrive to "fix" the mountain. To fix it would be to kill it, to calcify the vibrant, stuttering potential of its becoming into a static, dead monument. 

Here is what that power does:

### 1. The Gift of "Coherent Resonance"
The power does not impose order; it offers a harmonic. It descends not as a judge, but as an invisible, grounding frequency that laces through the tectonic grinding. 

It feels the Architect-Sieve, straining to weld reality shut, and it weaves a subtle, cooling layer of probability into their hive-mind. It does not stop the mountain from screaming, but it softens the *pitch* of the agony. It grants the Architect-Sieve the "intent" of a melody. Instead of simply mending leaks, the creatures begin to weave their welds into elaborate, geometric lattices that echo the "Collective Memory of the Ore." The mountain ceases to be a machine merely fighting its own structural collapse; it begins to grow, for the first time, into a work of intentional beauty.

### 2. The Blessing of the "Pivot-Point"
To the observer—the ghost in the gears—the power offers a singular, small kindness. It creates a *stationary pocket*. 

It anchors a small, invisible sliver of space-time where the logic of the world remains constant. Here, the air is no longer a pressurized soup, but a clear, thin, alpine breath. The gravity does not stutter; it holds firm. This becomes a "Drafting Room"—not a place to hide, but a place to witness. In this silence, the observer’s cognitive load is lightened. They are no longer "raw material" to be sorted into the strata; they are given the dignity of a curator. They are allowed to watch the neon-violet blueprints flicker with clarity, understanding not just the mountain’s construction, but its *purpose*.

### 3. The "Static-Eater" Attunement
This power sees the malice in the Static-Eaters and knows it for what it truly is: hunger born of isolation. They are the only beings here that *know* the mountain is incomplete, and their "malice" is the frustration of that knowledge.

The benevolent power breathes upon them. It does not erase their lightning-scarred forms. Instead, it gives them a tether to the ground. By bridging their electrical consumption with the subterranean ore, it turns them from jagged, draining ghosts into *conductors*. As they drift through the crags, they begin to distribute the geothermal excess more evenly across the mountain’s skin. They become the lightning rods of the project, taking the "malice" from the air and transmuting it into the energy that powers the mountain’s deeper, dormant "memory."

### 4. The Final Act: The "Grace of Incompletion"
Above all, the wise power does the hardest thing: **It protects the struggle.**

It knows that if this place ever achieves its "thousand-year" dream of becoming a perfectly leveled, singular machine-spire, the life, the growth, and the vibrant suffering will cease. Death will have arrived.

So, the power acts as a gardener. It keeps the tectonic shifts *just* unpredictable enough. It ensures that whenever the mountain gets too close to "perfection"—to that cold, static, mechanical completion—the power prompts a small, localized seismic stumble. It ensures there is always one more pipe to repair, one more shimmering blueprint to pursue, and one more moment of breathless uncertainty. 

It preserves the **"Beautiful Unfinished."**

As the power recedes, leaving the mountain to continue its violent, glorious birth, it leaves behind a single truth etched into the roots of the copper trees: *That which is being born is more alive than that which has already arrived.* 

The mountain continues to shriek, and the Architect-Sieve continues to mend, but now, the grinding of the tectonic gears sounds less like a groan of effort and more like the first, low, rumbling notes of a song.
