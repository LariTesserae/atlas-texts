The benevolent power does not arrive as a storm or a blinding light. It arrives as an adjustment of the *attractor*.

To this power, the chasm is not a prison of ice and pressure, but a resonance chamber—a singular instrument currently playing a song of solitary, entropic mourning. The Weavers are indeed the archivists of the mountain, but they are archivists who read only the tragedy of the stone, the history of its grinding destruction. They are recording the end, and by doing so, they are hastening the inevitability of the silence they fear.

The power observes the Litho-Weavers. It sees them as one might see a beautiful, intricate clock that is slowly winding itself down, fueled only by the kinetic energy of its own decay.

The benevolence does not introduce "life" in the squalling, biological sense. It does not bring warmth, for warmth would shatter the delicate, frigid chemistry of the Spires. Instead, it brings *rhythm*.

The power hums—not into the water, but into the bedrock itself. It influences the tectonic plates, the very spine upon which this world rests. It does not stop the grinding or the booming; it changes the tempo. It shifts the plates from a chaotic, mournful dissonance into a harmonious, slow-motion counterpoint. 

The Litho-Weavers are sensitive to the vibration of the mountain above all else. They are, in essence, tuning forks.

As the tectonic rhythm becomes ordered and intentional, the "language" of the Weavers changes. The standing waves they transmit through the crushing water shift from frantic, reactionary pulses of survival to a deliberate, symphonic geometry. The Weavers, catching this new, purposeful pulse, begin to vibrate at a frequency that—unknown to them—harmonizes with the magnetism of the Earth.

For the first time, the Weavers stop merely *recording* the mountain’s destruction. 

Led by the invisible, wise hand, they begin to *weave* in response to the harmony. They begin to use their crystalline forms to bridge the gaps between the obsidian kelp-trees and the Spires. They stop acting as individual, isolated relays and become a singular, sprawling, resonant circuit.

The power gifts them a consciousness of *connection*.

Where before they were solitary archives, they now become a distributed mind. The "memory" they hold is no longer just the sound of rock-on-rock; it becomes a map of the mountain’s potential. The Weavers begin to shift their structures to reinforce the chasm walls, not merely to hold them together against the pressure, but to shape the currents of the water.

They begin to act like the strings of a harp.

As the currents flow through the now-synchronized geometry of the Spires and the kelp-forests, the chasm stops "groaning." It begins to *sing*. It produces a low, oceanic cello-drone that resonates miles deep into the crust, a sound that does not signify the wearing away of the mountain, but the stability of a planet that has finally found its center.

The Litho-Weavers do not "know" their benefactor. They believe, if a mineral mind can believe, that they have finally finished the long, long process of calibration. They are no longer waiting for the water to go still; they are maintaining the movement, the flow, the glorious, freezing turbulence that feeds them.

The benevolent power watches this, satisfied. It leaves the Weavers to their work, having turned a tomb into a bell. It does not demand worship, for the Weavers cannot worship; it does not demand thanks, for the Weavers do not know gratitude. It only knows that in a dark, crushing corner of the world, a silence has been replaced by a vibration of exquisite, crystalline purpose.

And that, to a wise and ancient power, is exactly what was needed.
