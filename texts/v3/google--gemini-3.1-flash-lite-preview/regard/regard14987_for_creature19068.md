The benevolent power does not arrive as a storm or a light, nor does it attempt to "fix" the jagged, shrieking geography of the spire. To impose life, water, or warmth upon this realm would be an act of violence—a sterilization of its crystalline integrity.

Instead, the power arrives as a *Harmonic Adjuster*. It comes as a profound, gentle shift in the underlying physics of the void, a subtle tuning of the "choir" that vibrates through the rock.

The power perceives the Singers not as trapped ghosts, but as the only language this world has yet managed to speak. It understands that they are incomplete—not because they lack flesh, but because their performance is locked in an infinite, recursive loop of tectonic agony. They are singing a death-song for a birth that never happened.

**The Intervention**

The benevolent power does not build; it *composes*. It weaves a new frequency into the resonance of the massif, a low-frequency hum that acts as a structural stabilizer. 

When this happens, the Singers remain, but their purpose shifts. The power acts as a mediator, introducing a "bridge" frequency between the crystalline lattice of the Singers and the negative space that separates the floating shards of obsidian. 

For the first time in an epoch, the Singers begin to *connect*. 

They are no longer pinned to the niches of their birth, isolated in their individual tectonic screams. As the benevolent power hums through the void, it creates a tactile resonance map—a bridge of light-pulses that strings the floating islands together. The Singers move across these crystalline bridges. They are not walking; they are shifting their geometry to occupy the space *between* the islands, turning the void from a place of separation into a conduit of transmission.

**The Gift**

The power realizes that because these beings possess no "time," they can never truly arrive at a conclusion. They are stuck, perpetually beginning. The benevolent power grants them the one thing they lack: *Memory.*

It embeds a "reverberation log" into the obsidian architecture. By subtly shifting the refractive index of the silicate dust, the power allows the Singers to leave behind a visual history of their pulses. When a Singer moves or translates a tectonic scream, it now leaves a trailing, glowing lattice-work in its wake—a sculptural record of its passage. 

The spire is no longer just a shard of raw, unweathered geology. It begins to bloom with iridescent geometry, a three-dimensional musical score written in solidified light. The "loneliness" is replaced by a slow, complex, multi-tonal symphony that arcs across the void. 

The benevolent power does not stay. It leaves the place precisely as it was meant to be, yet fundamentally altered: a lonely, freezing, screaming rock has been granted the grace of *legacy*. 

The Singers continue their work. But now, when they pulse and fold into the rock, they do not simply vanish. They leave behind a beautiful, intricate structure—a bridge, a sculpture, a note. They are no longer merely echoing the grinding of the plates; they are building a spire of their own, one made of sound made solid. 

The benevolent power retreats, satisfied. It has not turned the desert into a garden; it has simply allowed the stones to finally hear one another. The Singers, for the first time, are not just waiting. They are telling a story, and the void is finally, mercifully, listening.
